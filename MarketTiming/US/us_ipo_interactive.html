U.S. IPO market + size-segment indices — interactive view, calendar years 2000-2025

IPO counts and gross proceeds by issuer type (corporate operating companies, SPACs, closed-end funds / BDCs) plus year-end values and YoY % returns for the S&P 500, S&P MidCap 400, and Russell 2000. Toggle individual series via the checkboxes or the chart legend. Sources: IPO data — U.S. SEC DERA (release of March 17, 2026); index levels — Yahoo Finance daily (cross-checked vs FRED for S&P 500 over its 10-year licensing window).

Show / hide individual series

Top chart: click a legend item or a checkbox above to hide/show that line individually. Counts and proceeds share the chart but plot on separate y-axes (counts on the left, proceeds on the right). The SEC dataset includes corporate operating companies, SPAC (blank-check) IPOs, and closed-end fund / BDC IPOs — all summed in the "Total" series. Direct listings (e.g., Spotify 2018, Coinbase 2021) and Regulation A / Regulation Crowdfunding offerings are NOT counted per SEC methodology.
Bottom heatmap: trailing 12-month P/E ratios per index per year (green low → red high). Sources: S&P 500 from Robert Shiller's Yale dataset (2000-2022) + multpl.com (2023-2025). S&P MidCap 400 end-2025 snapshot only (18.9× per Yardeni Research / S&P DJI Aug 2025 reading) — historical year-end P/E for the index is paywalled. Russell 2000 P/E is intentionally left blank: the index has many constituents with negative earnings, so different sources report widely different P/Es (15× to 40× for the same date) depending on methodology. Hover any cell for the exact value.
Headline observations: 2021 was the SPAC-mania peak (1,078 total IPOs, 611 SPACs, $303B total proceeds). 2022 was the post-Fed-hike freeze (202 IPOs, $22B). S&P 500 P/E spiked in 2008 (58.98) and 2020 (39.26) reflecting GAAP earnings collapses, not pure valuation rallies.
