U.S. IPO market — interactive view, calendar years 2000-2025

IPO counts and gross proceeds by issuer type (corporate operating companies, SPACs, closed-end funds / BDCs). Toggle individual series via the checkboxes or the chart legend. Source: U.S. SEC Division of Economic and Risk Analysis (DERA), IPO Statistics dataset (release of March 17, 2026).

Show / hide individual series

Tip: click a legend item or a checkbox above to hide/show that line individually. Counts and proceeds share the chart but plot on separate y-axes (counts on the left, proceeds on the right). The SEC dataset includes corporate operating companies, SPAC (blank-check) IPOs, and closed-end fund / BDC IPOs — all summed in the "Total" series. Direct listings (e.g., Spotify 2018, Coinbase 2021) and Regulation A / Regulation Crowdfunding offerings are NOT counted per SEC methodology.
Headline observations: 2021 was the SPAC-mania peak (1,078 total IPOs, 611 SPACs, $303B total proceeds). 2022 was the post-Fed-hike freeze (202 IPOs, $22B). The structural decline in U.S. corporate IPO count post-2000 (Jay Ritter's "310 → 110/year") is visible in the green "Corporate" line.
