Indian equity issuance vs broad-market indices — interactive 2000-2025

12 series on the line chart (top) + P/E heatmap (bottom). Sources: SEBI Handbooks / SEBI Annual Reports / SEBI Bulletin Annexures / NSE archives bhavcopy.
Top chart: 12 lines on 4 y-axes (count / amount / index level / YoY %). Click a legend item to hide/show. Double-click to isolate. The YoY % series start hidden — click to enable. Drag the rangeslider below or use the year inputs above to zoom.
Bottom heatmap: trailing 12M P/E per index per year. Hover a cell for the value. Years where the raw P/E was distorted by an earnings collapse are normalized by using prior-year EPS — switch "P/E mode" above to see the raw values instead (Midcap 2020 = 419, Smallcap 2017 = 106, Smallcap 2019 = 100). Normalized cells carry a N badge in their hover tooltip.
