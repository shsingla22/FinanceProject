Show / hide individual series

Top chart: click a legend item or a checkbox above to hide/show that line individually. All series are independently controllable (12 macro + I-banking basket + 8 individual I-bank stocks). The I-banking basket is the equal-weighted YoY % across companies listed in that year — hover for the basket size (n=2 in 2006/07 → n=8 in 2025). Drag the rangeslider below or use the year inputs to zoom.
Bottom heatmap: trailing 12M P/E per index per year (green low → red high). Hover for the exact value, raw P/E, and normalization detail. "Normalized" replaces 3 cells where earnings collapsed (Midcap 2020 = 419 → 30, Smallcap 2017 = 106 → 40, Smallcap 2019 = 100 → 31) using prior-year EPS.
