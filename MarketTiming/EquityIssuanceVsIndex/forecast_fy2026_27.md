# Expected IPO Amount in India — FY 2026-27 Forecast

Prepared: 2026-05-25 (FY 2026-27 is two months in: April-May 2026).
Best-available point estimate: **₹2.5 - ₹2.6 lakh crore for CY 2026
pipeline**, translating to roughly **₹2.0 - ₹2.5 lakh crore for FY
2026-27** depending on which deals slip across the FY boundary.

This document explains exactly what numbers I'm citing, what each one
covers, and where each came from.

Notes:
- This is **calendar 2026** pipeline, not strictly FY 2026-27. Most

- Pipeline numbers move in real time as new DRHPs are filed and SEBI
  approvals are issued. The 88 / 104 split was as of end-March 2026
  per PRIME's snapshot.

  higher numbers: ~200 companies / ₹2.6 lakh crore. The ranges agree.

## 4. CY 2026 actuals so far — slow start

From January through end-March 2026, only **15 mainboard IPOs raised
~₹17,000 crore** per Uniqus India IPO Insights (May 2026). The
breakdown:
- January 2026: 3 listings, average issue size ₹1,588 cr
- February 2026: 3 listings, average issue size ₹1,336 cr
- March 2026: 9 listings, average issue size ₹895 cr

This is a much slower pace than the ₹2.5 lakh crore CY 2026 expectation
implies — front-loaded forecasts tend to deliver in H2 once issuer
sentiment and DRHP queues clear. Cumulative CY 2026 IPO amount through
end-March was ~₹17,000 cr mainboard; the implied H2 CY 2026 needs to
hit ~₹2.3 lakh crore to deliver on the PRIME estimate.

April 2026 (start of FY 2026-27) had Zepto's SEBI approval (₹11,000
cr scheduled for Jul-Sep listing) plus a few smaller listings. The
SEBI April 2026 monthly bulletin does not yet publish a separate FY
2026-27 column — that will appear in the SEBI Bulletin from May or
June 2026 onwards.

## 5. My derivation of the FY 2026-27 band

The headline ₹2.5 lakh crore PRIME estimate is calendar 2026.
Translating to FY 2026-27 (April 2026 - March 2027) requires
allocating the pipeline by listing-window expectation:

| Calendar bucket | Estimated FY mapping | Estimated amount |
|-----------------|----------------------|------------------:|
| Jan-Mar 2026 actual (already in FY 2025-26) | FY 2025-26 ← not counted | ~₹17k cr (mainboard only) |
| Apr-Dec 2026 expected | **FY 2026-27** | ~₹1.8 - ₹2.2 lakh cr |
| Jan-Mar 2027 expected | **FY 2026-27** | ~₹0.2 - ₹0.3 lakh cr |
| **FY 2026-27 estimated total** | | **₹2.0 - ₹2.5 lakh cr** |

The wide ₹0.5 lakh crore range accommodates:
- Slippage of mega-issues (Jio / NSE / Flipkart) into FY 2027-28 if
  market conditions deteriorate.
- Acceleration if Jio + NSE both list in H2 CY 2026 as planned (both
  individually could lift the total by ₹50k+ cr each).

**Central estimate: ₹2.2 - ₹2.3 lakh crore for FY 2026-27** — a step
up of ~15-20% over the FY 2025-26 actual of ₹1.89 lakh crore.

## 6. Honest caveats

1. **Pipeline estimates are not guarantees.** Companies file DRHPs
   freely; many never list, or list smaller / later than initially
   indicated. The 2016 vintage Reliance Jio listing has been
   "expected next year" for several years.
2. **Calendar-year vs fiscal-year mismatch.** PRIME quotes CY 2026;
   the user asked FY 2026-27. The two periods overlap 75% (Apr-Dec
   2026 = both). My band converts conservatively but the conversion
   itself is a judgment call.
3. **No single SEBI projection exists.** SEBI publishes actuals
   monthly via its Bulletin Annexure Tables (the most authoritative
   data source for actual numbers). It does not publish forecasts.
   All forward-looking numbers are from PRIME Database (industry
   tracker) or KPMG / EY annual reports, not from SEBI itself.
4. **Pipeline gets refreshed often.** Numbers cited here are as of
   end-March 2026. By mid-FY-2026-27 there will be revised
   estimates as new DRHPs are filed and approvals issued.
5. **Market conditions matter.** A 2018-style smallcap drawdown or
   a 2008-style external shock would slash the realized total. The
   patterns analysis in `patterns_high_probability.md` does NOT
   predict such a shock for 2026 — but absence of a predicted shock
   isn't a guarantee against one.

## 8. Bottom line

- **FY 2025-26 actual (just past): ₹1.89 lakh crore (366 IPOs).**
- **FY 2026-27 ongoing forecast: ₹2.0 - ₹2.5 lakh crore central band**,
  driven by PRIME's pipeline of 192 companies. If Reliance Jio and/or
  NSE list as expected in H2 CY 2026, the actual could approach or
  exceed ₹3.0 lakh crore.
- **Today's pace (CY 2026 to April):** 15 mainboard IPOs, ~₹17k cr —
  slow start, with the heavy listing window expected H2 CY 2026.

This central estimate represents a **+15-30% YoY growth** over the
FY 2025-26 actual — consistent with the post-2017 supply trajectory
(FY 17-18: ₹84k cr → FY 18-19: ₹16k cr → FY 19-20: ₹21k cr → FY 20-21:
₹25k cr → FY 21-22: ₹113k cr → FY 22-23: ₹55k cr → FY 23-24: ₹68k cr
→ FY 24-25: ₹172k cr → FY 25-26: ₹189k cr → FY 26-27 expected ~₹230k cr).

The IPO supply cycle is currently in its strongest phase on record.
Whether this is sustained or marks the late-cycle peak (per the
patterns in `patterns_high_probability.md`) will become clear from
the *next* fiscal year's data — at which point the smallcap segment's
return will be the cleanest read.
