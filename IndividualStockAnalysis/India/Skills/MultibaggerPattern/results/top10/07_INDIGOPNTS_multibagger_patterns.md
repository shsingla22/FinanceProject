## Friendly Middleman — STRONG FIT
*"The expert sells it for you" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** Across all 13 calls the painter/contractor/dealer influencer is the buying decision-maker, and the company has systematically bought that relationship: painter health insurance (36,000 families across 11+ states), Indigo SkillUp contractor training (1,100+ trained), Seva Utsav with local painters, and in FY26 an explicit reallocation of budget from TV to direct influencer engagement.

> "you need to focus much more on influencer engagement so that they recommend your brand to the consumer" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ✅ Operating margin of 18% moving only ±0.6 points a year — the steady, protected profitability these patterns produce.
- ⬜ Not enough margin history.

## Brand Strength — STRONG FIT
*"The name itself sells" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** 13–14 years of sustained absolute A&P (Dhoni ambassador, World Cup advertising), brand-tracking studies confirming dealers pay a premium for quality, and a successful stretch of the parent name into an adjacency — Protect Plus waterproofing, zero in 2023, ~7% of top line by FY26 — alongside share gain plus premium pricing. Heritage is the weak leg: the brand is young versus Asian Paints/Berger.

> "We have built a brand with consistent advertising at an elevated level for over 13, 14 years now" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ✅ Keeps ₹47 of every ₹100 of sales after direct input costs — room to fund brand, R&D and price leadership.
- ✅ Operating margin of 18% moving only ±0.6 points a year — the steady, protected profitability these patterns produce.

## Market Share Gainers — STRONG FIT
*"Winning customers from rivals" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** Repeated, quantified share gain over the full period: six consecutive quarters of industry-leading growth by Q2 FY25, dealers up 16,496 to 19,350 and tinting machines 8,273 to 12,200 between FY23 and FY26, and margins expanded while gaining — share was not bought with profitability until the deliberate FY27 choice to spend more on trade.

> "in the last 5 years post our going public, we have grown faster than the industry, sometimes at 2x, 2.5x" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ⬜ Insufficient data.

## Corporate Culture — STRONG FIT
*"A culture that compounds" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** Promoter-led with an unchanged team across all 13 calls (Jalan, Humane, Suresh Babu, Srihari), granular voluntary disclosure of volume-vs-value by category, prompt admission of bad news (economy emulsions underperforming, Jodhpur plant slipping to June 2026, Apple Chemie's Q1–Q2 receivable problem), the FY23 waterproofing promise of 8–9% of top line largely delivered (~7% by FY26), and a conservative balance sheet with rising dividend.

> "What has not fared well for us in the last 9 months has been the economy range of the emulsions. So we have made corrections." — *management, on an earnings call*


## Pricing Power — LIKELY FIT
*"Raises prices, keeps the customers" · Likely fit — good evidence, not yet conclusive*

**What the calls show:** Value growth consistently equals or exceeds volume growth every quarter (premiumisation in emulsions, PU enamel, waterproofing) and gross margin rose from 46.5% FY25 to 46.9% FY26 despite raw-material spikes; but the FY24 industry price cuts were absorbed and in May-2026 the Board chose to give away 2–2.5pp of gross margin to chase share, and the ~12% FY27 price rise is industry-wide cost pass-through, not independent pricing power.

> "we are prepared to accept some moderation in our gross margins if required" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ✅ Keeps ₹47 of every ₹100 of sales after direct input costs — room to fund brand, R&D and price leadership.
- ⬜ Not enough margin history.

## Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) — PARTIAL
*"Sells once, earns forever" · Partial fit — some traits present*

**What the calls show:** No licence, AMC, subscription or deferred-revenue funding is ever discussed; the only installed-base mechanic is company-placed tinting machines at dealer counters, tracked every call (8,273 in Mar-2023 to 12,200 in Mar-2026) which pull repeat emulsion offtake, plus putty as a deliberate 'entry point' into a household. That is razor-and-blade pull-through, not contractual recurring revenue.

> "putty is a very good input, sort of first entry point into any household, which starts painting" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ❌ About 55 days of cash is tied up in the trade cycle — customers are not funding the business.
- ❌ Capital spending is about 10% of sales — growth is capital-hungry.
- ⬜ Not enough cash-flow history.

## Cost to Replicate — PARTIAL
*"Could a newcomer copy this?" · Partial fit — some traits present*

**What the calls show:** Real time-based barriers exist — 19,350 dealer relationships, 12,200 placed tinting machines, 36,000 painter families in loyalty programmes and a location-advantaged in-house putty plant beside Jodhpur's white-cement sources — but the calls themselves show well-funded newcomers attacking successfully (Birla Opus outspending them on advertising, JSW, Nippon) and differentiated products being copied, so a copycat with money is not clearly locked out.

> "Others have tried to imitate perhaps not with as much success" — *management, on an earnings call*
