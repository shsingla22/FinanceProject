## Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) — STRONG FIT
*"Sells once, earns forever" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** Across all three calls, growth is repeatedly attributed to annual surveillance fees on the existing rated installed base plus renewals on subscription research and benchmarking contracts, with Apr-2026 disclosing multi-year contracts and accelerated renewals worth ~USD4.5m of pull-forward revenue — a classic billed-in-advance recurring stream.

> "the growth is supported by strong traction in both surveillance fees as well as new ratings revenues" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ❌ About 69 days of cash is tied up in the trade cycle — customers are not funding the business.
- ✅ Capital spending is about 2% of sales — growth needs little capital.
- ✅ Operating cash was positive in 12 of 12 years — the reliable cash engine recurring-revenue businesses show.

## Friendly Middleman — STRONG FIT
*"The expert sells it for you" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** The ratings franchise runs on exactly this mechanism — bond investors (who do not pay) express a preference for CRISIL, and the issuer (who pays) selects the agency accordingly; the same phrasing recurs in Apr-2024, May-2025 and Apr-2026 calls.

> "The growth in new rating revenues is aided by continued investor preference for Crisil Ratings." — *management, on an earnings call*

- ✅ Operating margin of 30% moving only ±1.6 points a year — the steady, protected profitability these patterns produce.
- ❌ Operating margin moved from about 30% (early years) to 29% (recent years) — no widening trend.

## Toll Roads — STRONG FIT
