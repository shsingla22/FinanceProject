## Friendly Middleman — STRONG FIT
*"The expert sells it for you" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** The entire model is prescription-driven: ~2,600 medical representatives detail specialist and super-specialist doctors who prescribe while patients pay, with a dedicated 46-person team built to cover 3,000 IVF centres and 19% of sales spent on KOL consulting, scientific conferences and promotion rather than end-consumer advertising.

> "our specialist and super specialist, which is the biggest chunk of IPM, we have been super focused over there" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ✅ Operating margin of 21% moving only ±3.5 points a year — the steady, protected profitability these patterns produce.
- ⬜ Not enough margin history.

## Pricing Power — STRONG FIT
*"Raises prices, keeps the customers" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** With 92-93% of the portfolio outside NLEM, management confirms it takes price under the 10% non-NLEM ceiling, reported 7.8% price growth in FY26 and called it 'absolutely sustainable' while volumes still grew 4.6x the IPM — price rises with no volume loss, alongside rising gross margins.

> "we have got 92% non- NLEM and 8% NLEM... So, we have good lever on the price hike side" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ✅ Keeps ₹81 of every ₹100 of sales after direct input costs — room to fund brand, R&D and price leadership.
- ⬜ Not enough margin history.

## Brand Strength — STRONG FIT

## Market Share Gainers — STRONG FIT
*"Winning customers from rivals" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** Share gain is quantified and repeated across all three calls: fastest-growing among India's top 30 pharma companies on MAT Sep'25, Q3FY26 and MAT Mar'26, IPM rank moving 30th to 28th to 27th (five spots in three years), growing at ~2x IPM with FY26 volume growth 4.6x the industry — and gained without credit or discounting concessions.

> "CORONA jumping up two ranks from 30th to 28th rank in IPM" — *management, on an earnings call*

## Corporate Culture — STRONG FIT
*"A culture that compounds" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** The 'Triple C' framework explicitly includes courage to acknowledge mistakes and internal promotion; management set a 15% revenue / 20% PAT guidance on its maiden call and delivered 17.3% and 33.4% for FY26, stayed net cash (explaining the INR 143cr borrowing as FD-backed overdraft unprompted), and refused to relax credit/discount hygiene to win institutional IVF business.

> "we have decided not to give any leverage onto the fundamentals of CORONA" — *management, on an earnings call*


## Innovation Dominance — LIKELY FIT
*"Out-inventing the competition" · Likely fit — good evidence, not yet conclusive*

**What the calls show:** New-introduction growth of 6.4% (2.6x IPM's 2.5%) plus FY26 biosimilar launches (Fostine R, Tricium DnaB, Wyntide, Tyvenza) and a GLP-1 injectable show a regular launch cadence, but R&D is deliberately capped below 2% of sales and an analyst flagged it as low versus peers — this is incremental line-extension innovation, not R&D-led dominance.

> "the percentage contribution to the R&D will remain less than 2%" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ✅ Keeps ₹81 of every ₹100 of sales after direct input costs — room to fund brand, R&D and price leadership.
- ✅ High retained margin (81% of sales) alongside 17% yearly growth — the profitable-innovation flywheel: margins fund the spending that drives the growth.
