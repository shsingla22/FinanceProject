**Rank 5 of 10** in the Nifty Total Market multibagger-pattern screen.

## Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) — STRONG FIT
*"Sells once, earns forever" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** CAMS's core RTA economics are annuity-like: asset-based fees accrue every quarter off an installed base of AMCs, and management repeatedly frames growth as the same installed base consuming more (KRA, CAMSPay, WealthServ, Rep cross-sell) plus lifetime SIP flows. Across all 13 calls the language is installed-base density and 'foundational' recurring metrics rather than one-off sales.

> "the installed base buying more from us, new launches of clients in the installed base coming back to us" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ✅ Only about 14 days of cash is tied up in the trade cycle.
- ❌ Capital spending is about 9% of sales — growth is capital-hungry.
- ✅ Operating cash was positive in 9 of 9 years — the reliable cash engine recurring-revenue businesses show.

## Toll Roads — STRONG FIT
*"Small cost, can't skip it" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** RTA fees are a few basis points inside an AMC's 140–200bps TER yet the platform is mission-critical (transactions, SIP registration, folios), in a rational duopoly with KFin and anchored in SEBI licences (RTA, KRA, CRA, insurance repository). Management explicitly defends price on value-to-cost ratio and expects only a marginal INR20–25 crore worst case even from the TER/exit-load reset.

> "the amount of value we sell for the price we charge: a, is very large; and secondly, it continues to scale every year" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ✅ Operating margin 45%, swings of ±3.0 points, median return on capital 48% — the fat, calm, high-return economics of a business customers cannot skip.
- ✅ Operating margin of 45% moving only ±3.0 points a year — the steady, protected profitability these patterns produce.

## Corporate Culture — STRONG FIT
*"A culture that compounds" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** Anuj Kumar and Ram Charan have run the calls consistently since FY21 with promises made and then delivered — >20% non-MF growth beaten three years running, ~1%/yr margin expansion (43.5%→46.5%), cost growth guided <11% and delivered at 9% — while bad news is disclosed plainly (price reset, loss-making pension/AA units, alternatives fall-offs), alongside ~40% ROCE and a stated 65% payout. The board-level chairman churn in the management history is the one blemish.

> "our promise has been 20%. But successively over the last 3 years, we've been able to beat that" — *management, on an earnings call*


## Cost to Replicate — STRONG FIT
*"Could a newcomer copy this?" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** A new entrant would face a SEBI-licensed duopoly serving essentially every eligible AMC, with three decades of accumulated folio/PAN data, mission-critical migration risk for a fee that is trivial in the client's cost base, and now a second-largest KRA PAN pool — advantages money alone cannot rebuild quickly, as shown by CAMS still winning nearly every new AMC logo.

> "we've already sold to everyone, who is eligible" — *management, on an earnings call*
