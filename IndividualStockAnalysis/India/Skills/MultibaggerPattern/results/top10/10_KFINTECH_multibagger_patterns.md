## Toll Roads — STRONG FIT
*"Small cost, can't skip it" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** Classic toll road: RTA/fund-accounting fees are a few basis points of an AMC's AUM yet the service is mission-critical and regulator-anchored (SEBI/AMFI, building the industry body's mobility platform, BlackRock's marquee Aladdin administrator list), in a two-player domestic oligopoly; conversions are so laborious (2-3 quarters per transition, e.g. Varun Beverages, Castrol, Punjab National Bank) that clients effectively don't switch back.

> "it takes roughly about 2 to 3 quarters depending upon the size and complexity of the contract for us to be able to transition it" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ✅ Operating margin 41%, swings of ±3.2 points, median return on capital 30% — the fat, calm, high-return economics of a business customers cannot skip.
- ✅ Operating margin of 41% moving only ±3.2 points a year — the steady, protected profitability these patterns produce.

## Market Share Gainers — STRONG FIT
*"Winning customers from rivals" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** Share numbers rise persistently and are disclosed every quarter: MF AAUM 30.3% (FY22) to 32.7% (FY26), issuer solutions 47.3% to 52% of Nifty 500 market cap, AIF 37% to 39%, NPS from 0% to 10% growing 3x the industry, Malaysia above 50%, international clients 32 to ~500 — though the recent equity-AUM and SIP share dip and the April 2025 price discounts show some of the gain was not free.

> "the trend from FY22 to FY26 , consistently from 30.3% to 32.5%" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ✅ Sales compounding at 35% while margins held or improved (40% → 43%) — growth that was WON, not bought with price cuts.

## Corporate Culture — STRONG FIT
*"A culture that compounds" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** Same CEO (Sreekanth Nadella) and CFO (Vivek Mathur) present on all 15 calls FY23-FY26, with old promises visibly delivered — Thailand/Philippines entry flagged in May 2023 and delivered by FY26, NPS promised as a hedge then broken even (Jan 2025) and at ~30% EBITDA margin (Feb 2026), non-MF revenue path from 29% to ~40% tracked openly — and bad news is disclosed promptly (mark-to-market erosion, folio exodus, Ascent margin dilution, discounting), alongside a net-cash balance sheet.

## Cost to Replicate — STRONG FIT
*"Could a newcomer copy this?" · Strong fit — confirmed by the calls and the numbers*

**What the calls show:** A funded newcomer would need SEBI registration, three decades of accreted records (350mn folios, 10,500 corporates), integration into every AMC's operations and a multi-quarter migration per client — barriers KFin itself describes when explaining why transitions are slow and why it took 18 months to be admitted to BlackRock's global administrator list; the weaker link is the newer international/wealth platform business, which is more contestable.

> "KFintech had originally started its business long back, 35 years back" — *management, on an earnings call*


## Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) — LIKELY FIT
*"Sells once, earns forever" · Likely fit — good evidence, not yet conclusive*

**What the calls show:** Across all 15 calls the model is: win a mandate once (23 of 38 new AMC launches, 60% win rate, 4 of the top-10 fastest-growing AMCs), then earn annuity fees on the installed base of folios/AUM for decades — folio maintenance, corporate actions, AMC/fund-administration contracts that get renewed (largest AMC client renewed on mPower, Jan 2025). Installed-base density is the disclosed metric (110mn folios in FY23 to 350mn by FY26); the calls are silent on advance billing/negative working capital.

> "the registry revenue is not just from IPO, but largely from the folio maintenance and the corporate actions" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ❌ About 76 days of cash is tied up in the trade cycle — customers are not funding the business.
- ❌ Capital spending is about 9% of sales — growth is capital-hungry.
- ✅ Operating cash was positive in 8 of 8 years — the reliable cash engine recurring-revenue businesses show.
