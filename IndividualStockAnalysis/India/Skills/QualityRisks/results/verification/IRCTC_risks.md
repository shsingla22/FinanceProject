## Government Dependency — HIGH RISK
*"Fortunes decided in Delhi" · High risk — the calls and the numbers both point here*

**Why this severity:** the calls show clear, repeated exposure; this risk has no numeric fingerprint to cross-check, so it is judged from the calls alone.

**What the calls show:** IRCTC is a Ministry of Railways PSU whose entire opportunity set is policy-set: catering tariffs are administered by the Railway Board, licence fees are fixed in government tender documents regardless of tariff hikes, the advance-booking window was cut from 4 months to 2 by regulation, catering growth depends on the ministry's 260 Vande Bharat train-set programme and budget capex, payment-aggregator revenue awaits RBI approval (deadline extended to Aug 2026), and even buybacks are decided by DIPAM. Across all 12 calls management repeatedly defers to the ministry rather than to its own pricing or capacity decisions.

> "it is an administered item and is decided by the Ministry of Railways. I can't comment on that." — *management, on an earnings call*

**Silver lining:** Partial diversification into tourism, e-catering and Rail Neer, but these too run on railway assets and licences


## Stakeholder Concentration — HIGH RISK
*"Too few customers, suppliers or channels" · High risk — the calls and the numbers both point here*

**Why this severity:** the calls show clear, repeated exposure; the numeric fingerprints could not be computed, so the call evidence decides alone.

**What the calls show:** Every segment — internet ticketing (89% of reserved railway tickets), catering (licence fees on railway trains), Rail Neer (sold on railway platforms/trains) and tourism (Bharat Gaurav/Maharaja/Tejas rakes) — has Indian Railways as the single counterparty, landlord and regulator; convenience fee has been flat 'for several years' because the parent decides, and receivables historically ran ~118 days waiting on railway reimbursement.

> "Secondly, this is concerning Indian Railways. They will certainly, if you send some request to them, they will consider" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ⬜ No receivables history.

## Technological Innovation — WATCH
*"Out-invented by the industry" · Worth watching — some exposure visible in the calls*

**Why this severity:** the calls show real but bounded exposure while the numbers stay quiet.

**What the calls show:** The main platform-shift question — CRIS's Swa Rail super app potentially disintermediating IRCTC's booking front-end — was raised by analysts and answered defensively ('this is just one-off'); UPI now carries >50% of payments, compressing IT-segment margins by ~Rs 8 crores, and monetising its passenger database is constrained by DPDP rules. Innovation so far is incremental rather than architecture-breaking.

> "This will have no impact on our convenience fee because this is just one of the many modes which we are doing." — *management, on an earnings call*

**Silver lining:** All online reserved bookings still route through IRCTC's engine, and the cadence is small-scale value-adding (iPay, e-catering, unified portal, UPI)

- ✅ Growth is holding up: -5% a year earlier, 46% recently — no structural stall.
- ✅ Operating margin (32%) remains near its historical peak (47%) — no collapse in the premium.

## New Entrants — WATCH
*"A new player changes the rules" · Worth watching — some exposure visible in the calls*

**Why this severity:** the calls show real but bounded exposure while the numbers stay quiet.

**What the calls show:** Management concedes its own rail passengers already buy hotels, flights and packages from private OTAs and the unorganised sector, and is building a unified portal to claw that back — it is the challenger in that arena (only ~10,000 non-rail transactions/day against 16 lakh tickets). Analysts also probed whether Swiggy/Zomato e-catering cannibalises onboard sales.

> "our rail passengers are utilizing the services related -- travel-related services from other OTA sites and from unorganized sector" — *management, on an earnings call*

**Silver lining:** IRCTC has co-opted the new-format players (Swiggy/Zomato tie-ups drove e-catering from ~2,000 to >1 lakh meals/day) and is building its own OTA platform

- ✅ Operating margin held (about 16% then 33%) — no drift down under competition.
- ✅ Growth is holding up: -5% a year earlier, 46% recently — no structural stall.

## Good Enough Goods — WATCH
*"Cheaper rivals that are good enough" · Worth watching — some exposure visible in the calls*

**Why this severity:** the calls show real but bounded exposure while the numbers stay quiet.

**What the calls show:** Rail Neer serves only 50–60% of railway bottled-water demand, with the balance taken by cheaper generic brands, and management is now 'thinking to tie up with certain other brands'; catering is a low-differentiation daal-chawal product delivered by licensees where segment EBIT fell to 6.3% in Q4 FY26 and the price premium is not the company's to defend. Group EBITDA margin has drifted from ~36% four years ago to ~30% as mix shifts toward these commoditised, 10–12% margin lines.

> "Around 50% and 60%, depending upon season is being served." — *management, on an earnings call*

**Silver lining:** Captive on-train channel and 'strong brand acceptance' for Rail Neer limit substitution inside trains

- ⬜ No input-cost data.
- ✅ Operating margin (32%) remains near its historical peak (47%) — no collapse in the premium.
- ✅ Operating margin held (about 16% then 33%) — no drift down under competition.

## Cyclicality — LOW
*"Boom-and-bust earnings" · Assessed low — the calls show real mitigation*

**Why this severity:** the judge affirmatively found the risk absent or mitigated in the calls — that grounded reading overrides the 2 numeric flags, kept visible below.

**What the calls show:** Demand is flow/consumption-linked — 13–14.6 lakh tickets booked per day, ~1 lakh meals per day, Rail Neer 12.68 lakh bottles/day with year-round demand — and revenue compounded steadily from Rs 3,541 crores (FY23) to Rs 5,215 crores (FY26) with no commodity-price or cycle language anywhere in the calls; margin moves are mix-driven, not cyclical.

> "Now the Railneer demand is throughout the year, except for the month when we have winters." — *management, on an earnings call*

**Silver lining:** Flow/consumable, operations-linked demand base (tickets, meals, water) rather than customer capex; asset-light model with continued countercyclical capacity addition (4 new Rail Neer plants)

- ⚠️ Year-to-year sales growth swings by ±52 percentage points — boom-and-bust revenue, not a steady compounder.
- ⚠️ Operating margin has ranged from 14% to 47% (swinging ±10.0 points a year) — margins fluctuate a lot, the signature of a cyclical business.
- ⬜ Insufficient capex data.

## Shifting Consumer Preferences — LOW
*"The customer is changing" · Assessed low — the calls show real mitigation*

**Why this severity:** the judge affirmatively found the risk absent or mitigated in the calls.

**What the calls show:** The dominant consumer migration in this business — from counter to online booking — runs in IRCTC's favour, taking its share of reserved tickets from ~82% in FY23 to ~89% by FY26, and the shift toward domestic and experiential travel is lifting the tourism segment (FY26 revenue +19.5%, profit +36%).

> "Nearly 89% of the reserved railway tickets in India are now booked through our online platform" — *management, on an earnings call*

**Silver lining:** Successful portfolio pivot to the new preference — digital ticketing, e-catering, domestic luxury/pilgrimage tourism

- ✅ Growth is holding up: -5% a year earlier, 46% recently — no structural stall.

## Fashion Risk — LOW

> "both our normal catering also increased and our e-catering also increased by, say, 25%" — *management, on an earnings call*
