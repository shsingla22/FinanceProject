ACME Solar’s signs PPA for 300 MW / 1,200 MWh assured
peak supply SECI FDRE Project at INR 6/unit
GURUGRAM, OCTOBER 19, 2026: ACME Solar Holdings Limited (ACME Solar)
through its subsidiary, ACME Greentech Twenty Pvt Ltd, has signed a Power
Procurement Agreement (PPA) with Solar Energy Corporation of India Limited (SECI)
for 300 MW / 1,200 MWh assured peak supply under SECI’s FDRE-IX tranche at a
tariff of INR 6 per unit, with effective date of October 06, 2026. This FDRE project
supports the growing need for reliable, dispatchable clean power during evening and
night-time peak demand, helping strengthen the grid stability as renewable energy’s
share of India’s power mix increases. Following this PPA, the under construction PPA
signed contracted portfolio stands at 4,310 MW out of total under construction
contracted capacity of 5,380 MW.
Through this FDRE project, ACME Solar will supply 4 MWh per MW of contracted
capacity for any four hours daily during the non-Solar hours, while maintaining a
minimum availability of 90% on monthly basis. The project would integrate a
combination of Solar and Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) technologies to
meet the contracted obligations and would utilize the night-time connectivity already
available with the company.
About ACME Solar: ACME Solar Holdings Limited is a leading integrated renewable energy
player with a diversified contracted portfolio of 8,370 MW spanning Solar, BESS, FDRE,
Hybrid and Wind solutions and an operational capacity of ~3,123 MW and 4.96 GWh of BESS
capacity. With an in-house EPC and O&M division, the company does end-to-end
development and O&M of the plants, thereby delivering projects in a time & cost-effective
manner while ensuring best in class operating performance evident in its industry leading CUF
and operating margins.
