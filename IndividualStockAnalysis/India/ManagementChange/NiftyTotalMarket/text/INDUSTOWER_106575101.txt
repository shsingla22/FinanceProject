Dear Sir/ Ma ’am, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Listing Regulations, this is to inform that  
Mr. Tejinder Singh Kalra, Chief Operating Officer ( ‘COO ’) of the Company, shall retire from the Company with effect 
from August 31, 2026 upon attaining superannuation.  
 
In order to ensure a smooth and seamless transition, upon recommendation of the HR, Nomination and Remuneration 
Committee, the Board of Directors of the Company on March 30, 2026, approved appointment of Mr. Venkatesh Tiwari 
as the COO  of the Company, forming part of Senior Management Personnel ( ‘SMP ’), w.e.f. April 01, 2026.                        
Mr. Kalra will continue in his capacity as COO up to June 30, 2026 . With effect from July 01, 2026, he shall cease to 
be a SMP of the Company and shall continue with the Company thereafter until his retirement to facilitate a structured 
transition process.  
Mr. Kalra has been instrumental in driving operational excellence, implementing strategic initiatives, and enhancing the 
Company’s overall growth. The Board expresses its sincere appreciation for the valuable contributions made by  
Mr. Kalra during his tenure and wishes him the very best in his future endeavors.

Brief profile of Mr. Venkatesh Tiwari  
 
Mr. Venkatesh Tiwari has nearly 28 years of leadership experience across e‑commerce, telecommunications, consumer 
electronics and logistics. Over the years, he has built a reputation for leading high -energy, customer -focused teams and 
driving transformation across complex businesses. His expertise spans P&L ownership, large -scale operations, sales and 
marketing leadership, supply chain manageme nt, multi -modal transportation networks, and customer experience excellence.  
 
Mr. Tiwari is currently associated with Amazon . At Amazon,  he has led multiple roles and created high business growth. 
As Leader for Amazon Now, Seller Fulfilment, and 3PL channels, he launched Quick commerce offering in India and led 
large, cross -functional teams across the ultra -fast delivery ecosystem, grocery operations, same -day delivery, and third -party 
logistics net works. Before this role he held senior operational roles in Middle mile and Last mile networks of Amazon India.  
He has  played a pivotal role in driving some of Amazon India’s most ambitious initiatives, including building the fastest 
quick -commerce launch in Amazon’s global network, strategic partnerships and expanding Amazon coverage to entire pin 
codes of the country. T hese initiatives helped redefine delivery speed, reliability, and accessibility for millions of customers 
and sellers.  
 
Mr. Tiwari has previously worked with Samsung, Airtel and Perfetti. At Airtel, he held a variety of leadership roles across 
Operations, Customer Service Management, and Sales & Marketing, contributing to key strategic and operational 
advancements.  
 
Mr. Tiwari holds a  B. Tech in Electronics and Communications (1996 ) from BIT Mesra and MBA in Marketing and 
International business (1998 ) from Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies, Mumbai.
