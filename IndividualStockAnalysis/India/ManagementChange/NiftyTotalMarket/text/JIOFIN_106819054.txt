Jio Financial Services Limited Q2 FY27 Cons. Total Income (ex-dividend) at

₹574 Crore, up 85% YoY
Mumbai, October 15, 2026: The Board of Directors of Jio Financial Services Limited (“JFSL”,
also referred to as the “Company”), at its meeting held in Mumbai today, approved the
unaudited financial results for the second quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026
(Q2 FY27 & H1 FY27).
Key consolidated financial highlights for the quarter ended September 30, 2026:
●​ Total Income (ex-dividend) stood at ₹1,699 crore, up 132% YoY.
●​ Pre-Provisioning Operating Profit (PPOP, ex-dividend) increased by 85% YoY to
₹574 crore.
●​ Profit Before Tax (PBT, ex-dividend) stood at ₹489 crore, up 51% YoY.
●​ Profit After Tax (PAT) stood at ₹340 crore. (PAT for Q1 FY27 and Q2 FY26 included
dividend income)
●​ NBFC PAT (Jio Credit Limited) increased 140% YoY to ₹120 crore, driven by
sustained loan book growth.
●​ Total Consolidated Shareholders' Equity stood at ₹1.3 lakh crore as of September
30, 2026.
Key consolidated financial highlights for the half year ended September 30, 2026:
●​ Total Income (ex-dividend) stood at ₹3,195 crore, up 136% YoY.
●​ PPOP (ex-dividend) increased by 60% YoY to ₹1,079 crore.
●​ PBT (ex-dividend) stood at ₹951 crore, up 33% YoY.
●​ PAT reached ₹1,171 crore, up 15% YoY.​
Key Operational Highlights for the quarter ended September 30, 2026:
●​ NBFC’s Assets Under Management (AUM) reached ₹37,320 crore, up 154% YoY.
●​ AMC’s Closing AUM expanded to ₹22,746 crore, up 57% YoY.
●​ Payment Solutions’ Total Payment Value (TPV) stood at ₹14,682 crore, up 8% YoY,
with net processing margin expanding to 14 bps.
●​ Payments Bank’s Deposits reached ₹643 crore, up 53% YoY; CASA customer base
stood at around 40 lakh accounts.
