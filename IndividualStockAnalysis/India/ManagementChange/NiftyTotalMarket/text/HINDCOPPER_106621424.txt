Profile of Shri Purshotam Das Bohra  
Shri Purshotam  Das Bohra completed his Bachelor of Engineering in Mining in 1993 from MBM 
Engineering College, Jodhpur, Rajasthan. He also obtained the Manager’s First -Class Certificate 
of Competency for Opencast and Underground Metalliferous Mines from DGMS.  
He possesses more than 31 years of rich and diversified experience in the mining sector. During 
his professional career, he has worked in limestone mining for the cement industry with Grasim 
Industries (now UltraTech Cement) at Nagaur, Rajasthan, and in ir on ore mining for the steel 
industry with Essel Mining & Industries Ltd. at Barbil, Odisha.  
In October 2011, he joined the Malanjkhand Copper Project (MCP) of Hindustan Copper Limited 
as Chief Manager (Mines). Through his dedicated service and professional competence, he was 
promoted up to the post of Deputy General Manager (Mines) and served at MCP until May 2023.  
In May 2023, Shri Bohra joined Khetri Copper Complex (KCC), Hindustan Copper Limited, as 
Deputy General Manager (Mines). He has been serving as GM (Mines) / Agent Mines at KCC. 
Considering his extensive experience, leadership qualities, and valuable contri bution to the 
organization, he has been elevated as Unit Head, KCC with effect from 13 May 2026.
