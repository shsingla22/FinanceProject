Mrs. Niyathi Naidu Madasu brings more than two decades of
People practices experience from her work globally in Talent
management and Organization Transformation. Presently
she is acting as a Director in Premier Energies Photovoltaic
Private Limited, wholly owned subsidiary of the Company.
She is proficient in a variety of methodologies including
leadership framework development and design of talent
management frameworks. She is passionate about
developing design-thinking based human resource
interventions that help create high performing teams ground
up. She has worked with global Fortune 500 organizations
across Manufacturing, Financial Services, and Technology
sectors. Niyathi holds an MBA in Human Resources from
Symbiosis Centre for Management and Human Resource
Development, Pune and an Executive certification in
organization development from ISB, Hyderabad. She is
certified in a wide variety of globally renowned psychometric
instruments, Compensation & Rewards Frameworks.
Niyathi previously served as CHRO in Premier Energies and
made a lasting contribution to our people strategy and
organizational culture. Since then, she has continued to
broaden her leadership experience, and we are confident that
her expertise will be of significant benefit as we move
forward.
Mrs. Niyathi Naidu Madasu is a daughter of Mrs. Revathi
Rohini Buragadda, a Whole-time Director of the Company.
