Company Law, SEBI Regulations, Arbitrations, 
Statutory Compliances, Mergers and 
Acquisitions, Intellectual Property, Ethics and 
Code of Conduct, Contract Management, FEMA 
and Legal Due Diligences.

shaping the company’s strategic transformation 
through mergers, demergers, acquisitions, and 
capital-raising initiatives. He was instrumental in 
strengthening governance architecture, 
enhancing board processes, managing enterprise 
risk, and navigating complex regulatory and 
litigation landscapes.
