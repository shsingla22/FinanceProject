Bombardier Transport India.  
 
He brings extensive expertise in business strategy, project development, sales and 
operations, joint venture management, and large -scale infrastructure projects. He has

businesses, including wind, solar, battery energy storage systems, and green hydrogen. He 
has also played a pivotal role in landmark railway, power, and nuclear energy i nitiatives, 
including technology transfer and localization programs.
