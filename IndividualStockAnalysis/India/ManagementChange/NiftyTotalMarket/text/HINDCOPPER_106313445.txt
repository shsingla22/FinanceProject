Annexure-I
Brief profile of Shri Abhimanue Singh, ED & Unit Head/ TCP & GCP, Hindustan Copper Ltd
Shri Abhimanue Singh is a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering. He joined Hindustan Copper Ltd as
a Graduate Engineer Trainee (Mechanical) in 1991. He was initially posted at the Indian
Copper Complex, Ghatsila, where he gained rich and diverse experience in mechanical
maintenance, as well as in erection and commissioning of turnkey projects. His work spanned
critical operations within copper smelting, electrorefining, metal recovery plants, and
sulphuric acid production units. Over the years, he has developed strong expertise in
managing mechanical systems and plant operations, ensuring the smooth and efficient
performance of essential production infrastructure.
In the year 2020, Shri Singh was entrusted with the responsibility of Unit Head for Gujarat
Copper Project and also took over as Unit Head of Taloja Copper Project in 2022, thereby
overseeing both units. Under his leadership, he has been actively involved in the Continuous
Cast Rod (CCR) production processes, contributing significantly to operational excellence and
production optimization.

Annexure-II
Brief profile of Shri Binod Kumar Gupta, ED (Mining), CO, Hindustan Copper Ltd
Shri Binod Kumar Gupta is B. Tech. in Mining Engineering from Indian School of Mines,
Dhanbad (now IIT-ISM, Dhanbad). He has also completed Executive Business Management
Programme from IIM Calcutta and PGDBM (Marketing Management) from IGNOU, New Delhi.
He is having about 28 years of working experience in metal & mining industry. He had joined
Hindustan Copper Limited (HCL) in the year 2014, initially he was posted at Malanjkhand
Copper Project of HCL and then got transferred to Corporate Office, Kolkata in the year 2020.
He is presently heading Corporate Resource Planning Department.
Before joining HCL, he worked in large corporates including MNCs like HATCH (India), TATA
Metaliks Ltd., HINDALCO Industries Ltd. and Chowgule & Company Ltd.
His working experience includes all aspects of metal & mining project i.e. from initial concept
of development to commissioning. This includes scoping study, prefeasibility & feasibility
study, statutory clearances & NOC from various regulatory agencies, project management &
execution to commence the production at rated capacity and also operation of both open cast
and underground metal mines.

Brief profile of Shri Praveen Dave, GM (Mining), MCP, Hindustan Copper Ltd
Shri Praveen Dave holds a Bachelor's degree in Mining Engineering (1992 batch) from Pt.
Ravishankar Shukla University, Raipur. He has also obtained First Class Manager’s Certificate
of Competency (restricted to opencast workings) and Second Class Manager’s Certificate of
