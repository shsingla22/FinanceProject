About RPG
A US$ 5.2 billion diversified global conglomerate, RPG Group was founded in 1979 by the legendary industrialist Dr
R.P. Goenka and has a lineage dating to the early 19th century. Today, its businesses span key sectors of
infrastructure, tyres, IT & technology, pharmaceuticals, energy products and plantations among others, with a
footprint in over 135 countries. RPG Group's prominent companies include CEAT, KEC International, Zensar
Technologies, RPG Life Sciences, Raychem RPG, Harrisons Malayalam and Spencer International Hotels. The group
is home to over 35,000 RPGians from 40 nationalities and is widely recognised for its high standards of corporate
governance and a culture of respect for people and the environment.

CORPORATE OVERVIEW

STAKEHOLDER ENGAGEMENT AND MATERIALITY ASSESSMENT

Prioritising Material Issues
through Inclusive Engagement

Stakeholder engagement remains integral to the Company’s strategic decision-making, enabling sustained value
creation through regular dialogue with identified key stakeholder groups based on influence, impact, and
dependency. The Company continues to engage with stakeholder categories, with periodic reviews, ensuring
alignment of strategy with evolving stakeholder expectations.
In FY 2024–25, the Company undertook a comprehensive double materiality assessment aligned with recognised
frameworks, including GRI, SASB, EU CSRD/EFRAG and MSCI, evaluating both impact materiality (environmental and
social impact) and financial materiality. This framework was further strengthened in FY 2025–26 through the
Stakeholder Engagement and Materiality Assessment (SEMA), incorporating stakeholder consultations, internal
evaluations and sectoral analysis to refine strategy, strengthen risk management, and guide capital allocation. The
assessment process, conducted triennially with interim reviews for evolving business and regulatory dynamics,
continues to identify fifteen material topics which remain unchanged and form the basis for disclosures,
accountability, and focused resource allocation.

STATUTORY REPORTS

FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

Approach to Double Materiality
The Company applies a double materiality framework to evaluate sustainability matters from two complementary
perspectives. Impact materiality assesses the positive and negative effects of CEAT’s operations, products, and value
chain on the environment and society, based on severity, scale, and remediability; while financial materiality
evaluates risks and opportunities that may influence financial performance, access to capital, and long-term value
creation across relevant capitals and time horizons.
A topic is considered material if it meets either criterion, with rankings validated through management deliberations,
investor inputs, and industry benchmarks. Detailed disclosures on the methodology and identified material topics are
available in the Company’s double materiality report:
https://www.ceat.com/content/dam/ceat/pdf/sustainability-page/ceat-double-materiality-report.pdf
