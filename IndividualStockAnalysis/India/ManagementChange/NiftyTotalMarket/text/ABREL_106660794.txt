 Re: Disclosure under Regulation 31(4) of Securities and Exchange Board of  
       India (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 
 
Regulation 31 (4) of the Securities & Exchange Board of India (Substantial Acquisition of 
Shares & Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 provides that the Promoter of every target company 
shall declare on an annual basis that he along with Persons Acting in Concert, has not made 
any encumbrance, directly or indirectly, other than those already disclosed during the 
financial year. 
 
Pilani Investment and Industries Corporation Limited (“ PIICL”) is the promoter of Aditya 
Birla Real Estate Limited (formerly Century Textiles & Industries Limited).  PIICL is 
making this declaration on behalf of Promoter(s) / Members of the Promoter Group/ Person 
Acting in Concert (PAC) with us (Annexure-A), as on 31st March, 2026 that we have not made 
any encumbrance, directly or indirectly, on the shares of Aditya Birla Real Estate Limited 
(formerly Century Textiles & Industries Limited) held by us, other than those already 
disclosed during the financial year ended on 31st March, 2026.
