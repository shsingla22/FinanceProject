likeness of Japanese singer and

and BS Nippon TV, and has now

newsroom ecosystem, capable of processing up to 40 concurrent
live streams, moving the platform from archive management into the
operational core of daily broadcast
● Opens news and live production, the highest volume and most time
critical category in broadcast, as a repeatable market across the
broadcaster base
