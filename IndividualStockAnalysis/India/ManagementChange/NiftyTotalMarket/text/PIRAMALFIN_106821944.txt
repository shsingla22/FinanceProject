PRESS RELEASE  
Piramal Finance Strengthens its Board with the Appointment of Former SBI 
Chairman Dinesh Kumar Khara as Independent Director  
Mumbai, October 19, 2026:  Piramal Finance Limited, a  leading retail-focused non -banking financial 
company, today announced  the appointment of Mr. Dinesh Kumar Khara, former Chairman of the State 
Bank of India (SBI), as an Independent Director for a five -year term , effective Octobe r 19, 2026.  
Mr. Khara brings nearly four decades of banking and financial services  experience. Joining SBI as a 
Probationary Officer in 1984, he held  senior roles spanning corporate and international banking, treasury, 
risk, technology  and compliance , and oversaw several of SBI’s non -banking subsidiaries , before serving as 
Chairman from October 2020 to August 2024. He also played a key role in merging  SBI’s five associate 
banks and Bharatiya Mahila Bank with SBI. 
As Chairman, Mr. Khara led a significant fin ancial and digital transformation at SBI, strengthening asset 
quality, expanding advances and scaling digital capabilities, including YONO. SBI’s market capitalisation 
rose from approximately US$20 billion to US$90 billion, representing a CAGR of ~47%. Over the same 
period,  Return on Equity increased from approximately 7.7% to over 20 %, while  the net NPA ratio declined 
from 1.50% in FY21 to 0.57% in FY24.  
Mr. Khara’s honours include Lifetime Achievement Awards at the Business Today Best Banks Awards 2025 
and Financial Express Best Banks Awards 2024 , CNN -News18 Indian of the Year 2023 in the Business 
category , and the BFSI Best CEO award in the BT -PwC India’s Best CEOs ranking 2022. During his 
chairmanship , SBI was named  Company of the Year 2023 by The Econ omic Times Awards for Corporate 
Excellence and received Global Finance’s Best Bank Award in 2022 and 2023.  
Mr. Khara currently serves as an Operating Advisor to the Abu Dhabi Investment Authority, advising on 
investment  opportunities , commercial due dilige nce and portfolio companies , and  also as an Independent 
Director on the boards of several prominent companies. 
Mr. Khara holds an MBA from the Faculty of Management Studies and a Master of Commerce from the 
University of Delhi, and is a Fellow of the India n Institute of Banking and Finance. 
Anand Piramal, Chairman, Piramal Finance Limited, said, “I am delighted to welcome Mr. Dinesh Kumar 
Khara to the Board of Piramal Finance. During his distinguished career, particularly as Chairman of SBI, he 
strengthened  one of India’s most important financial institutions and expanded access to formal banking 
and credit for millions of Indians , while nearly tripling  SBI’s annual net profit to ₹61,077 crore , growing its 
loan book by over 50% to approximately ₹38 lakh cror e and reducing its net NPA ratio by over 60%.  
His commitment to responsible,  inclusive finance strongly aligns  with our purpose.  His experience, 
judgement and deep understanding of financial services will be invaluable as Piramal Finance expands  
access to formal credit and advances its growth journey. We look forward to his counsel and guidance.”

About Piramal Finance Limited  
 
Piramal Finance Limited is a retail- led upper -layer NBFC with a pan- India presence. As on 30th September 2026, the 
company manages Assets Under Management (AUM) of over INR 1.10 lakh crore  and has served over 6 .5 million 
customers across 26 states. The company operates a distinctive phygital model combining high -touch engagement 
across 13,000+ pin codes with high- tech capabilities including machine learning models, agentic AI tools and real -
time dashboards. Pirama l Finance is rated ‘AA+’ with a ‘Stable’ Outlook by CRISIL, ICRA and CARE for its domestic 
long -term debt, ‘BB’ by S&P Global internationally, and ‘Ba2’ with a Positive outlook by Moody’s.  
In retail lending, Piramal Finance offers a diversified portfolio spanning affordable housing loans, loan against 
property, gold loans, microfinance, used car loan, personal loans, insurance solutions, and small business credit, with a presence across metro -adjacent, semi -urban, and rural markets. In wholesale lending, t he company provides asset -
backed, data -driven financing solutions across real estate and select non -real estate sectors, with a focus on mid -
income residential development and bespoke capital solutions for mid -market corporates. 
With its digital- first appr oach and AI -enabled platforms, Piramal Finance is committed to expanding access to 
affordable credit and driving inclusive growth across India.
