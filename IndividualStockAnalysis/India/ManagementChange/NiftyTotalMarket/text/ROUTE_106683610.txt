Subject:  Resignation  Letter  
Dear Tushar, 
I would  like  to formally submit  my resignation  from  my employment  with  Masivian  S.A.S.,  a 
subsidiary  of Route  Mobile  Limited,  effective  June  30, 2026 , due  to pre-occupation  and  other 
personal commitments.  
This  decision  has  not been an easy  one,  as my journey  with  Masivian  has  been  one of the most 
meaningful  and  rewarding  experiences  of my professional  career.  It has  been  a privilege  to 
contribute  to the growth  of the company,  work  alongside  exceptional  colleagues,  support  our 
customers,  and  be part  of the transformation  that led Masivian  to become part  of the Route  Mobile 
and Proximus family.  
I would like to express my sincere gratitude to Rajdip Gupta, you, the Route Mobile leadership team, 
and everyone across the organization for the trust, support, and opportunities provided throughout  
these  years.  I am especially  thankful  for the chance to continue contributing  following  the 
acquisition and to participate in the evolution of the business during such an important stage of its 
development.  
I am proud  of what we have  achieved  together  and  confident  in the future  of the organization. 
I remain fully committed to ensuring a smooth and successful transition over the coming days and 
to supporting the continuity of the business in any way that may be helpful.  
Thank you  once again for the opportunity,  the partnership,  and  the confidence  you  have placed  in 
me. I leave with great appreciation for the relationships built over the years and with my best wishes 
for the continued success of Masivian, Route Mobile, and the broader Proximus Group.  
Yours sincerely, 
Erwin Viertel   
CEO  
Masivian  S.A.S.
