 ANNEXURE – 1  
 
PRAMESH MAHESHWARI  
 
Mr. Pramesh Maheshwari  (‘Mr. Maheshwari ’) is appointed as President India 
and Southeast Asia . 
 
Mr. Maheshwari has 30+ years of experience in driving EBITDA growth, 
operational transformation, and value creation across industrial and 
technology -enabled businesses. He has proven track record of scaling P&Ls, 
executing M&A integration, organic growth, and margin expansion strategies.  
He is skilled in building high -performance teams, driving accountability, new market expansion, 
operations and execution, innovation and customer turnarounds leading large global organizations across the Americas, EMEA, Middle East, and India.    Prior to joining Timken, Mr. Maheshwari spent more than 30 years with Honeywell Process 
Automation, where he held progressively senior global leadership positions, including President of 
Honeywell Process Solutions; President of Lifecycle Solutions, Services & Cyber; and Global Vice President & General Manager. He has led businesses spanning industrial automation, services, software, cybersecurity, digital transformation, projects and engineering, with significant 
experience in customer -centric growth and operational excellence . 
 
Mr. Maheshwari holds an Engineering degree in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering 
from Rani Durgavati University, India. He later completed General Management Program from IIM 
Ahmedabad, India .
