Sub: Outcome of Board Meeting - Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Change of Company Secretary & Compliance
Officer & Key Managerial Personnel (“KMP”) of the Company
This is to inform that the Board of Directors of the Company at its Meeting held today i.e. August 29, 2025,
based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of the Company, has
approved the appointment of Ms. Reena Raje (ICSI Membership No. A21440) as Company Secretary (Key
Managerial Personnel) under the Companies Act, 2013 and Compliance Officer of the Company under the
SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (‘SEBI Listing Regulations’)
and SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, with effect from September 1, 2025.
In this regard, it may be noted that Mr. Dinesh Kalani, Company Secretary & Compliance Officer of the
Company, is due for superannuation on August 31, 2025 and hence, it was decided that he shall cease to be
Company Secretary & Compliance Officer of the Company at the close of business hours on August 31,
2025. The Board placed on record its sincere gratitude for the exceptional contributions made by Mr. Dinesh
Kalani throughout his tenure as the Company Secretary & Compliance Officer of the Company and
conveyed its best wishes to him.
The brief details of the aforesaid changes in KMP as prescribed under SEBI Listing Regulations read with
SEBI Master circular SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024, are attached as
Annexure A.
Further, in terms of Regulation 30(5) of the SEBI Listing Regulations, Ms. Reena Raje is also designated as
Key Managerial Personnel authorised for the purpose of determining materiality of an event or information
and for the purpose of making disclosures to the Stock Exchange(s) with effect from September 1, 2025.
