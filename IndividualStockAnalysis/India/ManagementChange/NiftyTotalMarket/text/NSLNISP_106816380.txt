Sub: Change in Directorate on the Board of NMDC Steel Limited – Appointment of Shri 
Vinod Kumar Tripathi, Joint Secretary, Ministry of Steel as Government Director, 
vice Shri Abhijit Narendra . 
 
Ref: Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. 
 
Ministry of Steel, Government of India vide Order No. S-14011/1/2022-BLA dated 12.10.2026 
has communicated the appointment of Shri Vinod Kumar Tripathi, Joint Secretary, Ministry of 
Steel as Government Director on the Board of Directors of NMDC Steel Limited with immediate 
effect or until further orders, whichever is earlier vice Shri Abhijit Narendra, Former Joint 
Secretary, Ministry of Steel. 
 
Accordingly, Shri Vinod Kumar Tripathi (DIN: 10711675) has been appointed as Government 
Director on the Board of Directors of NMDC Steel Limited with effect from 12.10.2026. Further, 
Shri Abhijit Narendra has ceased to be Government Director on the Board of NMDC Steel 
Limited. 
 
The brief profile of Shri Vinod Kumar Tripathi is enclosed at Annexure – A . 
 
Shri Vinod Kumar Tripathi is not related to any other Director on the Board of NMDC Steel 
Limited. He is not disqualified to act as a Director under the provisions of Section 164 of the 
Companies Act, 2013. Further, he is not debarred from holding the office of Director by virtue of 
order of Court or Tribunal or SEBI or any other such authority.  
The above is for kind information and dissemination.

Annexure - A 
 
Brief Profile of Shri Vinod Kumar Tripathi 
 
(DIN – 10711675) 
 
Shri Vinod Kumar Tripathi, Joint Secretary, Ministry of Steel, Government of 
India, belong to Indian Railway Service of Engineers (IRSE – 1995), is a Civil 
Engineering graduate from IIT Roorkee.  
 
He has worked in various capacities in Ministry of Railway, Government of India 
and has vast experience in administration, management and engineering. This 
includes management of Public Sector Enterprise, public policy, projects, 
operations, technological advancement, procurement & inventory management and 
human resource management. He has also worked in Railway Land Development 
Authority as Executive Director. Presently he is Joint Secretary in Ministry of 
Steel, Government of India.
