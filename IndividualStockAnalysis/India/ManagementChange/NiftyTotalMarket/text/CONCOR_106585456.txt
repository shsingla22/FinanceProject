Sub: Change in the Board of Directors of CONCOR 
Ref: Our Letter of even no. dated 20.03.2026. 
Kindly refer to our above letter in which pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, it was informed that Ministry 
of Railways (MOR) vide its order dated 19.03.2026 communicated that the President is 
pleased to appoint Shri Rahul Agarwal, Principal Executive Director TT(M), Railway Board 
as part-time Government Director on the Board of CONCOR, in lieu of Shri Prabhas 
Dansana, with immediate effect, till he holds the post of Principal Executive Director TT(M), 
Railway Board or further orders, whichever is earlier. 
In continuation to the above, we would like to inform that in terms of orders of the Ministry 
of Railways, the Board of Directors of CONCOR has appointed w.e.f. 19.03.2026 Shri Rahul 
Agarwal (DIN: 08970712), as Part Time Government Director of CONCOR on 09.04.2026 . Brief 
profile of the Director alongwith updated list of Board of Directors is enclosed. 
Further, it has been confirmed by appointee that he is not debarred from holding the office of 
Director by virtue of any SEBI order or any other such authority and he has no relationship 
with other Director(s) of the Company.
