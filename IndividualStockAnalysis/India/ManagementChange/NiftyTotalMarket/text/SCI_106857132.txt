Annexure-1 
Brief Profile of Shri L S Prasad 
 
Lagisetti Sreenivasa Prasad began his shipping career in 1995 as TME in SCI. After serving 
aboard different type of vessels, including Chemical Tankers, Gas Carriers, Bulk Carriers, Oil 
Tankers and General Cargo Vessel, he was promoted to the Rank of CEO in 2003.  
 
He joined shore office as a Manager in the Specialised Vessels Cell (B&T) in 2006, he was 
moved to the Coastal Passengers, Research & Containers (CRC Department) in 2021 and was 
later transferred to Off-shore Technical department in 2024.   
 
He is currently serving as DGM In-Charge (OSV & MSV) in the Technical & Offshore Division.
