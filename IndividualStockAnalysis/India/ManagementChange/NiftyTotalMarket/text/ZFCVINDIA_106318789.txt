Mr. K Inbarajan is a seasoned legal professional
with over 20 years of experience in legal drafting,
contracts, arbitration, litigation, M&A, compliance,
and corporate advisory. He currently serves as
General Manager – Legal at KONE Elevator India
Pvt. Ltd., Chennai, where he leads legal
compliance, contract management, and dispute
resolution. He has standardized contract
procedures, resolved disputes pre-litigation, and
supported global legal teams in Australia and the
Middle East. He has also presented at Global
Lawyers Meetings in Finland and participated in a
legal exchange in Dubai.

Previously, he held senior legal roles at SIVA
Industries, VGN Developers, and SAAG RR Infra,
managing international arbitrations, high-value
litigations, and complex corporate transactions. He
began his career at Surana & Surana International
Attorneys. Mr. K Inbarajan holds an LLM in
Contracts, a BL in Law, an MBA in HRD, and a
B.Sc. in Computer Science.
