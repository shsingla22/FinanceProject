Subject: Change in Directorate. 
 
Sir/ Madam, 
 
The President of India acting through Administrative Ministry, i.e., Ministry of 
Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), Government of India, vide its Office Order dated 
17.02.2026, informed that Smt. Manasi Sahay Thakur (DIN: 07647316) & Smt. Tanvi 
Garg  (DIN: 05165139) have ceased to be the Directors on the Board of Hemisphere 
Properties India Limited (HPIL) with immediate effect. 
 
Further, Shri Pradeep Kumar Jha, (DIN 07640619),  Joint Secretary, Ministry of 
Housing & Urban Affairs (MoHUA) and  Shri Shyam Lal Poonia (DIN 07554713), Land 
& Development Officer, Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs are appointed as Directors 
on the Board of Hemisphere Properties India Limited with effect from date of issue of 
abovesaid order. 
 
Pursuant to SEBI direction regarding appointment of Directors by listed companies and 
BSE Circular No. LIST/COMP/14/2018-19 and NSE Circular No. NSE/CML/2018/24, 
dated 20 June, 2018, and based on the affirmation by Director, the Company hereby 
affirm that the Director being appointed is not debarred from holding the office of 
director by virtue of any SEBI order or any other such authority. 
 
The brief profiles of Shri Pradeep Kumar Jha, IAS and Shri Shyam Lal Poonia, IAS is 
enclosed herewith. Further, the said director does not have any inter-se relationship 
with any of the Directors of Company. 
 
This is for your information and dissemination.

 BRIEF PROFILE 
 
Pradeep Kumar Jha (DIN: 07640619) Shyam Lal Poonia (DIN: 07554713) 
Shri Pradeep Kumar Jha is a 2006 batch 
Indian Administrative Service (IAS) officer of 
the Manipur Cadre. He holds Bachelor’s 
and Master’s degrees from the University of 
Delhi, along with an M.Phil. in Art. 
 
He has held key administrative assignments 
at the district and state levels, including 
serving as District Magistrate in Bihar and 
Manipur. His leadership during the 2017 
floods in Purnia Division was widely 
appreciated. As Managing Director of Bihar 
Medical Services and Infrastructure 
Corporation Ltd., he ensured uninterrupted 
medical logistics during the COVID-19 
pandemic. He has also served as 
Commissioner, Joint Secretary, and Chief 
Electoral Officer, Manipur, where he 
ensured the conduct of free and fair 
elections. 
 
Presently, he is Joint Secretary in the 
Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, 
Government of India, overseeing key 
organisations and national projects 
including Central Public Works Department 
(CPWD), Central Government Employees 
Welfare Housing Organisation (CGEWHO), 
and the Central Vista Project. He also serves 
as CEO of Kartavya Path and is a Board 
Member of Delhi Metro Rail Corporation 
(DMRC). Shri Shyam Lal Poonia is a 2010 batch 
Indian Administrative Service (IAS) officer 
of the Manipur Cadre. He holds a Bachelor 
of Science (B.Sc.) degree and a Master of 
Science (M.Sc.) in Agronomy from 
University of Rajasthan. His academic 
training has provided him with strong 
analytical aptitude, a data-driven approach 
to governance, and a disciplined 
framework for policy evaluation and 
implementation. 
Over the course of his career, Shri Poonia 
has been entrusted by the Government of 
India with several responsible and 
significant positions. His ability to navigate 
multifaceted policy issues, ensure 
procedural integrity, and coordinate 
effectively across departments has 
contributed meaningfully to institutional 
effectiveness and programme delivery. He 
is widely regarded for his methodical work 
ethic, clarity in decision-making, and 
commitment to time-bound outcomes. 
Presently, he is serving as Director (Central 
Vista) and Director, Land and Development 
Office (L&DO), in the Ministry of Housing 
and Urban Affairs, Government of India.  
Shri Poonia is recognised as a diligent, 
principled, and forward-looking civil 
servant, steadfast in his commitment to 
public service.
