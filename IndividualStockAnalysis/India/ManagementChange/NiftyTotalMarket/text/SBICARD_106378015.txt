Brief Profile of Mr. Avinash Agrawal

Mr. Avinash Agrawal is Deputy General Manager with State Bank of India. He is
appointed as Executive Vice President & Chief People Officer. Before joining SBI Card,

Solutions at the Corporate Centre in Mumbai and DGM - IT roles at GITC Belapur.

in various assignments. Mr. Agrawal has over 30 years of rich and diverse experience in
the banking sector. He holds a Postgraduate degree in Economics, MBA in Finance, and
also a Certified Associate of the Indian Institute of Bankers (CAIIB). Mr. Agrawal has
demonstrated exceptional leadership across various domains such as Retail Banking,
Branch Operations, Digital Banking, Human Resources and Information Technology.
