 Profile of Shri Subhankar Sen, Director (Marketing), BPCL – (Nominated as Chairman of IGL by BPCL) 
Shri Subhankar Sen is Director (Marketing) at Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd (BPCL), India’s Maharatna Public 
Sector  Undertaking  and  a Fortune  Global  500  Company  heading  all the marketing  functions  of BPCL 
encompassing  Retail,  LPG,  Lubricants,  Aviation,  Industrial  & Commercial,  Gas  and  Consumer  Retailing 
businesses  along  with  core entities  like Engineering  & Projects,  Pipelines,  Central  Procurement  Organization, 
Digital Business, Corporate Brand and Public Relations. 
Throughout his career spanning over three decades, Shri Subhankar has led pioneering initiatives in Fuel 
Retailing, Lubricants marketing and Energy transition. He is a transformation focused leader with an innate 
ability to build highly motivated teams with a strong belief in people empowerment, partnerships and 
continual learning. His relentless commitment in enhancing customer experience ensures focus on innovation, 
brand imagery, technology and long-term value creation. 
Shri Subhankar  has led BPCL’s  MAK  Lubricants  business  through best- in-class  R&D  and marketing  initiatives 
delivering differentiated customer value. Focused on “Atmanirbhar Bharat”, he successfully led MAK Lubricants’ 
indigenisation drive and the powerful MAK Lubricants Solutions program delivering reduced total cost of ownership 
for the customer. He grew the lubricant business in India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, UAE and Africa through 
a nation-wide  distribution  network,  partnerships  with  leading  OEMs  and a growing  network  of customers  in the 
B2C and B2B arena. 
In his role as Business Head, Retail (West), Shri Subhankar led BPCL’s automotive energy business with a 6000
strong network of Energy stations driving BPCL’s market growth through technology & digital led differentiated 
customer value propositions. His journey in BPCL through various roles & geographies has helped in his 
development as a highly effective business leader with a versatile experience. Under his leadership, BPCL led 
the Non-Fuel Retail foray in India, the launch of BeCafe, the experiential Cafe chain being set up in BPCL fuel 
stations being the latest offering. 
Shri Subhankar has been a member of BPCL’s Strategy team building pioneering brands - Pure for Sure, Speed, 
PetroBonus,  SmartFleet,  BPCL-SBI  card,  In&Out  stores,  UFill.  He built  strategic  alliances  in the QSR,  Banking, 
Retail  & Automobile  sectors.  As Oil  Industry  Coordinator,  he led the  energy  transition  journey  into EV  charging, 
working with policy makers in the Government and led BPCL’s eDrive program - “EV Fast Charging Corridors”. He 
led Oil  Industry’s major reform with the implementation of Doorstep Diesel Delivery, an efficient fuel solution for 
industrial & commercial establishments. 
Shri Subhankar  has a strong  sense  of commitment  towards  organisational  growth,  driving  value  with  effective 
cross-functional  & collaborative  skills  & a deep  understanding  of consumer  behaviour.  He is also  a passionate 
golfer having represented the country internationally. 
He has no relationship with other Directors and he is not debarred from holding the office of Director by virtue 
of any SEBI order or any such authority.  He is not holding any shares of the Company.
