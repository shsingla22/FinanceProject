Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("Listing Regulations") read with Part A of Schedule III thereto, we wish to 
inform that the Board of Directors of Mankind Pharma Limited ("the Company"), through the resolution passed 
by circulation on June 8, 2026, has approved a strategic realignment of its human resources leadership framework 
as part of an internal organizational restructuring exercise. 
Consequent to this internal restructuring, Mr. Nilesh Kulkarni will head the Human Resource Functions of the 
Group and has been designated as a Senior Management Personnel (“SMP”). Mr. Prateek Dubey will transition 
to new roles and responsibilities and, consequently, ceased to be a SMP. The above change is effective 
immediately.
