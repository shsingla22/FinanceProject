3. Brief Profile  
(in case of appointment)  
 Ms. Anushree Singh is a global HR leader with over two decades of 
experience across people strategy, organizational transformation, 
culture building, M&A integration, board management and leadership 
development across industries and geographies.  
 
Most recently, she served as Chief Human Resources Officer at JSW 
Energy, where she architected the people  framework supporting the 
Company’s “Plan 30 by 30” ambition.  
 
During this period, the organization scaled from approximately 2,000 to 
6,000 employees over two years, while she built an integration playbook, 
strengthened HR governance and onboarding systems, and developed 
leadership capability across four business ver ticals.  
 
Previously, Ms. Anushree Singh held leadership roles across Avery 
Dennison, AkzoNobel and the Aditya Birla Group, with experience 
spanning India, Europe, North America and global corporate functions. 
Her roles have included Global HR Director at Avery Denn ison, CHRO for 
the India Sub -continent at AkzoNobel, and leadership assignments across 
talent management, organizational development and HR operations at 
the Aditya Birla Group.  
 
Across her career, Ms. Anushree Singh has led large -scale culture 
transformations, HR digital transformation, organizational design and 
diversity & inclusion initiatives, while partnering closely with senior 
leadership to build high -performance organizatio ns. 
 
Ms. Anushree Singh holds a PGDM from IIPM and a Bachelor’s degree 
(Hons.) in Psychology from Delhi University. She is also a SHRM Senior 
Certified Professional.
