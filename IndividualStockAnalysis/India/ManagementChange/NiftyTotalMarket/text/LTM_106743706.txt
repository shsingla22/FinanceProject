Dear Investors,
I am pleased to share LTM's Sustainability Report for FY2025–26, titled "Outcreate Sustainable
Progress with Purpose."
Sustainability remains integral to how we create long-term value at LTM. As technological
disruption accelerates and stakeholder expectations continue to evolve, we are focused on
building a resilient, responsible, and future-ready enterprise that drives sustainable growth for
all stakeholders.
Our environmental journey continued to advance during the year. Renewable energy
accounted for 72.23% of our total energy consumption. We maintained our 2.8x water-positive
status, achieved 94.39% waste recycling, and strengthened our climate commitments through
the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) as we progress toward our Net Zero 2040 vision.
We also continued to create meaningful social impact. Our CSR initiatives positively impacted
nearly 1.2 million beneﬁciaries during the year, taking our cumulative reach to over 3.5 million
lives. At the same time, we sustained our commitment to an inclusive workforce, with women
representing 30.87% of our global talent pool, and the representation of women in leadership
roles increased to 9.53%. Our corporate governance strengthened with 67% Independent
Directors in Board.
LTM's sustainability performance continues to earn global recognition. We were ranked among
the Top 3 in the IT & Digital Services sector and Top 15 overall in Businessworld India's Most
Sustainable Companies 2025. Our FY25 Sustainability Report received multiple international
awards — a testament to the rigor and transparency of our disclosures.
The progress highlighted in this report demonstrates that we embed sustainability across our
strategy, operations, and decision-making to drive meaningful and lasting impact for all our
stakeholders. I invite you to explore the report and learn more about how LTM is Outcreating
Sustainable Progress with Purpose.
Thank you for your continued trust and conﬁdence in our journey.

About this Report
The theme ‘Outcreate Sustainable Progress
with Purpose’ reflects LTM’s commitment to
embedding sustainability at the core of its
strategy and operations.
This Sustainability Report presents LTM’s ESG
performance and initiatives, reflecting the
organization’s continued focus on responsible
growth and long-term value creation. Prepared
in accordance with the GRI Universal Standards
2021, the report outlines key environmental,
social, and governance disclosures from April 1,
2025, to March 31, 2026.
The report brings together quantitative
performance data and qualitative insights across
material ESG topics, offering stakeholders
a transparent view of how sustainability
considerations are embedded into LTM’s
strategy, governance practices, and operations.
It highlights LTM’s approach to managing
sustainability related risks and opportunities
while delivering value to clients, employees,
communities, and other stakeholders.
Aligned with LTM’s ESG vision and material
priorities, the report incorporates inputs from
stakeholder engagement processes and
draws alignment with globally recognized
frameworks, including the United Nations
Global Compact (UNGC), the Sustainable
Development Goals (SDGs), the National
