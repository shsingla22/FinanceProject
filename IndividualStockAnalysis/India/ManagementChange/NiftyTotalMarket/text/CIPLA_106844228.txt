Brief Profile  
(in case of appointment)  Mr Vikram Janakiraman brings around 30 years of experience 
across consulting leadership and pharmaceutical operations.  
 
He joins Cipla from the Boston Consulting Group (BCG) as 
Managing Director and Senior Partner, where he built BCG 
India's Pharma Operations and Automotive practices and led 
the Operations and Industrial Goods practices. He also played a key role in develop
ing the firm's Manufacturing AI and 
Industry 4.0 offerings. He has advised several Indian and MNC 
firms across topics ranging from plant operations, quality 
systems, new product development, supply chain 
improvement and cost management.  
 
Prior to joining BCG, he held leadership positions at Glenmark 
Pharmaceuticals, where in his last role he was responsible for 
Manufacturing, Supply Chain, Procurement and Capital 
Projects globally. During his tenure, he drove significant 
operational improv ements, designed and constructed new 
facilities, and supported inspections and audits by global 
regulatory authorities, including the USFDA, MHRA and 
ANVISA.
