Mr. Deepak Agarwal joined our Bank as the Head of Strategy and
transformation.
Deepak brings with him rich experience in strategy and transformation driving
strategy formulation, large scale transformation and business outcomes. He
brings with him diverse knowledge and experience gained across multiple
markets such as India, USA, South East Asia, and Middle East. In his previous
roles, he has helped financial services institutions formulate mid to long term
strategy, detail out execution plan, implement large scale transformation to
achieve their growth and profitability objectives. He last held the position of
Head of Retail Business Strategy at Axis Bank and previously worked with
Mastercard, Deloitte and KPMG.
Deepak holds an MBA from S.P. Jain Institute of Management & Research,
Mumbai (SPJIMR) and a Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering from Delhi
Technical University, Delhi (erstwhile Delhi College of Engineering).
