Annexure - I 
Brief Profile of Shri Pramod Kumar Dwibedi , ED, Bank of India  
 
Shri Pramod Kumar Dwibedi, aged 58 years, is an accomplished banking professional with over 31 
years of extensive experience in the banking industry. He holds a postgraduate degree in Economics 
and an MBA in Finance. He is also a Certified Associate of the  Indian Institute of Bankers (CAIIB) 
and possesses certifications in IT and Cyber Security from the Institute for Development and 
Research in Banking Technology (IDRBT), as well as in Fundamentals of Risk Management from 
the Institute of Risk Management, L ondon.  
He commenced his banking career in 1995 as a Probationary Officer in Bank of India and has served 
in various capacities across the country and abroad, including assignments at BOI London and New 
York. His experience spans diverse areas of banking, includin g banking operations, business 
development, wholesale banking, administrative functions and banking assurance. He has also 
developed extensive expertise in managing banking operations, strengthening customer relationships 
and overseeing diverse commercial banking functions.  
He has successfully served as General Manager, FGMO Bhopal, overseeing the states of Madhya 
Pradesh and Chhattisgarh, and subsequently as Chief General Manager, FCGMO Pune, overseeing 
Bank of India's business operations across Maharashtra, excluding Mumbai . During his tenure, he 
played a significant role in strengthening business operations and driving growth, with particular 
emphasis on MSME, Food & Agro, Agriculture Infrastructure and other priority sectors. He has also 
spearheaded various structural and cultural initiatives aimed at enhancing operational efficiency, 
business performance and organizational effectiveness.  Shri Dwibedi is currently serving as 
Executive Director on the Board of Bank of India.
