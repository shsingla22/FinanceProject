BRIEF PROFILE 
 
 
Shri. Shri. Sujit Kumar Sahoo, Chief General Manager, has 29 plus years of experience in 
Banking. He has a Master of Business Administration and diploma in Treasury, Investment and 
Risk Management. He is also a Certified Associate of Indian Institute of Bankers. He worked 
from branch level to head office level in 8 different states and across rural to metro . He has 
administrative experience of heading Ranch Circle Office and three Regional Offices at 
Madurai, Chennai and Sambalpur. At present, Shri. Sujit Kumar Sahoo is heading Compliance 
& RBS Wing, Head Office, Bengaluru.
