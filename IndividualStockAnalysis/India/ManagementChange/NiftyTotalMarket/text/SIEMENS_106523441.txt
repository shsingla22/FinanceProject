Annexure – A 
 
 
1. Mr. Sankar Balasubramanian will cease to be the Regional Compliance Officer of the 
Company , effective  close of business hours on 31st March 2026, as he will take up a new role 
within Siemens overseas.  
 
2. Mr. Amitava Sarkar is appointed as Regional Compliance Officer of the Company, effective 
1st April 2026, Brief profile of Mr. Sarkar is as under . 
 
Mr. Amitava Sarkar is a seasoned legal and compliance professional with nearly two decades 
of experience spanning governance, regulatory compliance, integrity frameworks, and legal 
risk management.  
 
He has been associated with Siemens for more than ten years, holding senior legal advisory 
roles across diverse businesses including Metals Technologies, Power & Gas, Power 
Generation Services, and Digital Industries in India. From 2015 to 2017, he served as Head 
of Legal & Compliance at Primetals Technologies before rejoining Siemens in August 2017.  
 
Throughout his career, Mr. Sarkar has advised business leadership on complex legal matters, 
contractual risk mitigation, and governance processes. Since 2019, he has been serving as 
Lead Counsel – Smart Infrastructure, partnering closely with the business on legal strategy, 
compliance programs, governance frameworks, and risk aligned decision making.  
 
Mr. Sarkar holds a Bachelor of Laws from Calcutta University and is a certified trainer in 
Contracts and Claims Management.
