Subject: DISCLOSURE UNDER REGULATION 31(4) OF SEBI (SUBSTANTIAL ACQUISITION OF SHARES &

Reg.: Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Ltd. (MRPL) 
Madam/ Sir, 
 
Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited ( ONGC) is the Promoter of Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals 
Ltd (MRPL). In terms of Regulation 31(4) of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) 
Regulations, 2011, it is hereby informed that:- 
 
1. ONGC held 125,53,54,097 equity shares in MRPL (equivalent to 71.63% of paid-up share capital); and 
2. Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Ltd. (HPCL), a subsidiary of ONGC, held 29,71,53,518 equity shares in 
MRPL (equivalent to 16.96% of paid-up share capital).  
 
Cumulatively, ONGC along with HPCL (subsidiary of ONGC) held 1,55,25,07,615 equity shares 
(representing 88.58%) in MRPL as on 31.03.2026 . 
 
It is further informed that there was no encumbrance directly or indirectly on the above-mentioned equity 
shares held by ONGC and HPCL during the financial year ended 31.03.2026.
