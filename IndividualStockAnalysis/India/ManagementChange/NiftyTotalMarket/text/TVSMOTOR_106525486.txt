TVS MOTOR STRENGTHENS ITS PRESENCE IN EGYPT
WITH TVS RONIN AND TVS NTORQ RACE EDITION
LAUNCH
Cairo, Egypt, February 07, 2026: TVS Motor Company, a global leader in two and
three-wheeler manufacturing, today unveiled two exciting new products for the
Egyptian market, the TVS Ronin Top and TVS Ntorq Race Edition . Key stakeholders,
nationwide dealers, influencers, senior representatives from TVS Motor Company and
representatives from Ezz LCV, TVS’s authorised distributor in Egypt, graced the
launch ceremony. This introduction marks a significant step in TVS Motor Company’s
long-term strategic focus to deepen its presence in Egypt, as the company continues
to expand its global portfolio.
The TVS Ronin Top launched in Nimbus Grey and Midnight Blue, blends a unique
modern-retro design philosophy combined with cutting-edge engineering, optimised
performance and everyday versatility, reflecting TVS Motor’s innovation in premium
mobility. The TVS Ntorq RE, launched in Marine Blue and Red, further enhances the
brand’s presence in the performance scooter category with its sporty design,
responsive acceleration, and advanced features, appealing to young and dynamic
urban riders seeking an engaging and connected riding experience.
Commenting on the new products, Mr. Rahul Nayak, Senior Vice President,
International Business, TVS Motor Company, said, “Egypt is a key market for us
and by launching TVS Ronin Top and TVS Ntorq Race Edition here, clearly reflects
our commitment to our continued focus on delivering well-engineered products. The
new products will further enhance our presence in Egypt by providing products that
are globally competitive, best-in-class performance and segment-ready features that
resonate with local riders. Our 6 years partnership with Ezz LCV, play a pivotal role in
expanding our footprint and delivering world-class mobility solutions to our customers
in Egypt.
Mr. Amr Ezzelarab, Ezz LCV, said, “We are proud to continue our strategic journey
with TVS Motor Company in Egypt. Our collaboration has enabled us to provide the
best products, unparalleled after-sales support, and extensive dealer network for
