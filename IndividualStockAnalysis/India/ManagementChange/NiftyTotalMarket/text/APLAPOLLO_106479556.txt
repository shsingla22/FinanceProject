practices by reducing dependence on wood, thereby

MATERIALITY ASSESSMENT
In line with the GRI Guidelines, APL Apollo has embraced the principle of materiality, focusing on factors that impact the
organization’s ability to create, sustain, or diminish economic, environmental, and social value for itself, its stakeholders,
and society at large. To gain a clearer understanding of the key ESG issues pertinent to our business and stakeholders, we
undertook a thorough materiality assessment. This process is designed to pinpoint critical issues for stakeholders across
short, medium, and long-term horizons. It serves to validate the effectiveness of our ESG strategy and implementation,
promoting positive outcomes and risk mitigation while aligning with the priorities of our stakeholders. To facilitate this, we
actively engaged a diverse range of internal and external stakeholders.
