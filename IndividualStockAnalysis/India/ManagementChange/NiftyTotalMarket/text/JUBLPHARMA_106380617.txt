Dear Sirs,
This is further to our intimation dated July 29, 2025, and pursuant to the provisions of

Brief profile of Mr. Arun Kumar Sharma
Mr. Arun Kumar Sharma is a strategic and result-driven financial leader with over three decades of
progressive experience across corporate finance, global treasury, investor relations, risk management,
financial restructuring, insurance, capex control, and business transformation. He has consistently
demonstrated excellence in driving sustainable growth, enhancing shareholder value, and upholding
governance standards in large listed companies. He has held various positions in Jubilant Bhartia
Group including CFO role in Jubilant Pharma Holdings Inc. USA for approx. 4 years, Jubilant Lifesciences
Limited (Chemical Business) for approx. 3 years and Jubilant Pharmova Limited for approx. 2 years
(April’ 2021-May’2023).
