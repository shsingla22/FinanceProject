3) Brief profile  Mr. Vinod Kumar Aggarwal is an industry veteran with an experience 
of 45 years, out of which 43 years has been with Eicher Group. In his 
long and successful career with Eicher, spanning over four decades, 
Mr. Vinod Kumar Aggarwal has held several management positions 
including being the Chief Financial Officer of the Eicher Group from 
2005-2009 and President of Eicher Trucks and Buses. 
After leading VE Commercial Vehicles Limited (“VECV”) (Company’s 
joint venture with AB Volvo)  for over 16 years, initially as CEO and 
thereafter as CEO and Managing Director, Mr. Vinod Kumar Aggarwal 
was appointed as the Non Executive Chairman of VECV (as a nominee 
of Eicher Motors Limited) w.e.f 1st April 2026. 
Mr. Vinod Kumar Aggarwal is the Past President of both the Society of 
Indian Automobile Manufacturers (SIAM) and the Automotive Skills 
Development Council (ASDC). He is on the Board of the Company since 
1st April 2019 as Non Executive Director. He was appointed as Non 
Executive Vice Chairman of the Company w.e.f 13th February 2025.  
Mr. Vinod Kumar Aggarwal is a qualified Chartered Accountant and 
Cost and Management Accountant and a commerce graduate from 
Punjab University.
