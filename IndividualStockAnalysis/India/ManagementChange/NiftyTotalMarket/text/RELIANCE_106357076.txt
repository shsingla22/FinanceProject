Brief Profile of Dr. K. R. Chandratre, Practising Company Secretary:
Dr. K. R. Chandratre, Practising Company Secretary, is a fellow member of the Institute of
Company Secretaries of India (“ICSI”) and has more than 45 years of experience in corporate
secretarial practices. Earlier to this, he worked as a Lecturer in Commerce & Accountancy at R. A.
Podar College of Commerce & Economics, Mumbai for three years. Dr. K. R. Chandratre entered
the field of practice as Company Secretary in 2003. He also holds a Master’s degree in Commerce,
Law degree and he is a Doctorate from the University of Pune. He was the President of the ICSI
in 1996 and Vice-President in 1994 and 1995. He has been a member of the Secretarial Standards
Board and Chairman of the Core Group on Secretarial Audit of the ICSI. Dr. K. R. Chandratre has
authored over 1200 articles and 26 books on various subjects in the areas of Corporate Laws and
Corporate Secretarial Practice.
