Summary:
Tata Motors (Standalone) delivered another quarter of robust performance, driven by strong revenue and profit growth.
Quarterly revenue came in at ₹23.9K Cr (+42%), EBITDA at ₹2.8K Cr (+31%) and EBITDA margin at 11.7% (-90 bps). On a
sequential basis, EBITDA margin was maintained, despite the significant impact from rising commodity costs. Strong
operating leverage, disciplined pricing actions, and rigorous cost-efficiency measures helped partially mitigate the impact
of commodity inflation. PBT (bei) for the quarter stood at ₹2.7K Cr (+51%) and Profit after tax was ₹2.1K Cr.
Free Cash Flow for the quarter stood at ₹4.6K Cr (+₹2.4K Cr) leading to a net cash position of ₹11.3K Cr as of September
30, 2026. Auto ROCE stood at 65% (68% in Q1FY27).
Consolidated financials: Consolidated revenues for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹25.3K Cr (+36%), EBITDA at ₹2.8K Cr (+31%) and
EBITDA margin came in at 10.9% (-40 bps). PBT (bei) for the quarter was ₹1.8K Cr (+423%) and Profit after tax stood at
₹1.3K Cr (+244%), led by lower mark to market loss on investments in Tata Capital Ltd. As at September 30, 2026, the
Company had a strong net cash position of ₹15.9K Cr.

Corporate Actions:
Iveco update: Following receipt of all regulatory approvals, the tender offer has commenced and will remain open for
acceptance until 26 October 2026. At Iveco’s Extraordinary General Meeting held on 16 October 2026, shareholders
approved all proposed resolutions. Subject to successful completion of the tender offer, the settlement is scheduled for
30 October 2026.
