August 25, 2025
Appointment of Mr. Rajiv Anand (DIN: 02541753) as
Managing Director & CEO of IndusInd Bank for a
period of three years, with effect from August 25, 2025
upto August 24, 2028 (both days inclusive), subject to
the approval of the Shareholders of the Bank.

Mr. Rajiv Anand was Deputy Managing Director at
Axis Bank. In this role, he led the Bank's Wholesale
Banking Business and was also responsible for driving
the digital agenda of the Bank. He has held key
management positions at leading global financial
institutions and in Capital Markets & Banking.
He joined Axis Asset Management Co. Ltd. in 2009 as
its founding Managing Director & CEO. In his next
assignment, he was appointed President of Retail
Banking at Axis Bank.
Subsequently, he was inducted into the Board of the
Axis Bank and took over as the Head of Wholesale
Banking.

Declaration

He holds a degree in Commerce and is a qualified
Chartered Accountant.
In accordance with SEBI’s instructions to Stock
Exchanges dated June 14, 2018, we confirm that Mr.
Rajiv Anand is not debarred from holding the office of
Director of the Bank, by virtue of any order of SEBI or
any other such authority.
