October 22, 2026, Bengaluru : BlueStone Jewellery and Lifestyle Limited, a leading digital
-first,
direct -to-consumer jewellery brand, today announced its results for the quarter and half year
ended September 30, 2026. EBITDA for Q2FY27 surged to INR 97.1 crores, up 127% YoY, and
BlueStone reported a profit for the fourth consecutive quarter.
Revenue grew 46.3% YoY to
INR 1,471 crores in 1HFY27, led by same -store sales growth (SSSG) of 32.9%. In Q2FY27, revenue
grew 43.9% YoY to INR 738 crores, outpacing the category even as gold prices stayed volatile and
the festive calendar moved into Q3. D
uring the quarter, BlueStone crossed one million
customers, a milestone in a category where customers decide whom to trust before they decide
what to buy.
Profitable for the fourth consecutive quarter
BlueStone has now reported a profit in each of the last four quarters, building on FY26, its first
full year of profit. EBITDA more than doubled to INR 207.
3 crores in 1HFY27, and the EBITDA
margin expanded by 422 bps YoY to 14.1%. In Q2FY27, EBITDA rose 12
7% YoY to INR 97. 1 crores,
with the margin up 48 3 bps to 13.2%.
Growing faster than the category
BlueStone’s revenue grew 43.9% YoY in Q2FY27, well ahead of the category. Two choices set it
apart. The first is omnichannel: more than 80% of its sales originate online, so its stores convert
intent that customers have already built. The second is everyda
y wear: almost all of its business
is in non -wedding jewellery, the faster -growing part of the market, as consumers move from
heavy pieces kept in lockers to lighter jewellery worn every day. Its reach is widening too, with
379 stores across 146 cities, 68 more than a year ago, and a new flagship on Park Street, Kolkata.
One million customers, in a category built on trust
Fine jewellery is bought on trust, and trust has traditionally taken generations to build.
BlueStone has built it in 15 years. Its customer base crossed one million during the quarter and
stood at 10,24,220 at the end of September, up 19.3% YoY. BlueStone counts a customer only once
they have bought, and kept, a piece of its jewellery.

Q: Can you walk us through the key highlights from the quarter?
A: Business momentum remained firm in Q2 FY27. Same
-store sales growth (SSSG)
was 28% YoY for the quarter, despite the shift in the festive season, and 32.9% YoY for
H1 FY27. Over the past year, a key focus area has been our entry
-level price points,
where gold price inflation had dislocated our offering. Our redesigned range is now
taking more shelf space in our stores, and this quarter we saw early signs of that
effort paying off: new customers contributed a larger share of revenue than in the
previous quar ter, and AOV moderated accordingly. Repeat customers contributed
56% of revenue, up from 51% a year ago. While we are not targeting a specific mix, our
focus remains on driving growth across both new and repeat customers.
Q: Were there any new investments this quarter?
A: Yes. The key investment this quarter was our new flagship store on Park Street,
Kolkata. The 3,200 sq. ft. store showcases more than 2,000 unique designs. It also
introduces a range of handcrafted and antique jewellery, a new addition to our
portfolio. Traditional elements such as Kundan setting and antique finishes give the
range its heritage character, while a modern sensibility keeps it relevant for today’s
consumer.
We continue to widen both what we offer and how consumers experience it. On the
product side, we keep adding new categories, design languages and price points so
that consumers can turn to BlueStone for a wider range of occasions and tastes. On
