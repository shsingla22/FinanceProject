7. Ei.ception Item: Litia:ation E:1:penses 
During the financial year 2020-21, Indegene Inc. (a subsidiary of the Company) was named in a class action lawsuit filed in the U.S. District Court of New Jersey, alleging non­
compliance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act of 1991 ('TCPA ') by sending unsolicited fax advertisements without the recipient's prior express invitation or pennission . 
Plaintiff initiated this matter through the filing of its Class Action Complaint against lndegene, Inc., lndegene Encima Inc., lndegene Wincere Inc., and lndegene Healthcare, LLC 
(collectively, "Indegene" or "Defendants'') seeking the Court to award actual monetary loss from the alleged TCPA violations in an amount to be proven in Court or the swn of USD 
500 for each violation, whichever is greater, and that the Court award treble damages of USD 1,500 if the violations are deemed wilful or knowing. Additionally, the Plaintiff seeks the 
Court award pre-judgment interest and costs, to be determined upon presentation of suitable eviderrtiary support. 
During the year ended 31 March 2026, the matter progressed to mediation and the Group agreed in principle to a mediator-proposed settlement framework under which the 
Defendants would make available a maximum settlement fund of approximately t417 (USD 4.72 million), calculated at USD 250 per eligible claim. The settlement is structured as a 
claims-made arrangement, whereby the actual outflow is contingent upon valid claims submitted by eligible class members, and unclaimed amounts, if any, revert to the Group in 
accordance with the settlement terms. Accordingly , the maximum settlement fund does not represent the expected cash outflow. 
Based on the status of the proceedings and the Group's assessment, supported by external legal advice, the Group concluded that a present obligation existed and that an outflow of 
economic resources was probable and can be reliably estimated. Accordingly, the Group recognised a provision of ~03 (USD 2.30 million), including estimated legal costs, 
representing its best estimate of the expenditure required to settle the obligation which has been disclosed as exceptional item in the results for the year ended 31 March 2026. During 
the period ended 30 September 2026, pursuant to the courts orders the class administrator has issued notices to class members on 10 September 2026 requesting to submit claim forms 
or opt out of the settlement wherein the Class members have until 09 December 2026 to submit the claims or opt out. The Settlement payments are expected to become payable 
following the final fairness hearing and Court approval, in acc.ordance with the settlement terms. The amount payable will depend on valid claims submitted and attorneys' fees
