Brief Profile (in case of appointment)

Shri S. C. Jain, an officer of the Indian Railway Service of
Engineers (IRSE, 1988 batch), holds a B.Tech. in Civil
Engineering from Pantnagar University and an M.Tech.
from IIT Delhi. With over 35 years of service, he has held
key assignments in construction, maintenance, bridge
research, policy, operations, and safety. He has served as
Divisional Railway Manager, Delhi, where he managed one
of the busiest divisions and oversaw critical operations
including Covid coaches and Oxygen Special trains; as
Executive Director (Bridges & Works) in the Ministry of
Railways, handling national policy matters and interministerial coordination; and as Principal Chief Engineer,
NCR Prayagraj, where he executed major infrastructure
works for Maha Kumbh. He has also contributed
significantly as Principal Chief Safety Officer, Western
Railway, and during his tenure at RDSO, advanced bridge
research and international collaborations. Widely exposed
to global best practices through visits to Japan, Finland,
Malaysia, Singapore, China, and Thailand, Shri Jain is
recognized for his expertise in railway infrastructure, safety,
and management, and continues to contribute to the growth
and modernization of Indian Railways.
