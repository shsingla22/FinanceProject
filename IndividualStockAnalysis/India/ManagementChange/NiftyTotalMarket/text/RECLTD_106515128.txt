महोदय / महोदया, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, this is to inform that Shri Harsh Baweja  
(DIN: 09769272), Director (Finance) of REC has superannuated from the services of the 
Company on January 31, 2026 and accordingly, has ceased to be the Director (Finance) and 
Chief Financial Officer of REC with effect from February 1, 2026. 
 
Further, the Ministry of Power, Government of India has entrusted the additional charge of 
the post of Director (Finance), REC to Shri Jitendra Srivastava (IAS), Chairman & Managing 
Director of REC for a period of 3 months w.e.f. February 1, 2026 or until the appointment of 
a regular incumbent to the post of Director (Finance), REC or until further order, whichever 
is earlier.
