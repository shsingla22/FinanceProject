Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), we 
hereby inform the following changes in the Senior Management Personnel (SMP) of the Company, 
effective from 1st January 2026: 
 
1. Mr. Shreyas Joshi, President – Home Textiles  (the Senior Management Personnel) , will 
transition from his existing role to oversee the Domestic Retail Business  and drive new 
strategic initiatives of the Company . He will be re -designated as President – Strategy .  
 
2. Mr. Malay Mahanti, currently working as  Senior Vice President – Commercial  (the Senior 
Management Personnel) , will transition from his existing role and assume responsibility for 
the Home Textiles Business .  He will be re -designated as  Business Head – Home Textiles.  
 
3. Mr. Neeraj Kalaskar , currently working as Vice President – Operations (Spinning)  will 
assume responsibility for Company’s procurement and supply chain function and will be 
designated as Senior Vice President – Procurement & Supply Chain . He will also be a 
member  of the Company’s Senior Management Personnel . 
 
The details of the appointment, as required under Regulation 30 of Regulations read with SEBI 
Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD -PoD1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023, are given in Annexure I 
herein.
