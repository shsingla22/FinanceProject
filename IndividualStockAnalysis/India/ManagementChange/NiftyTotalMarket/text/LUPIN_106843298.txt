Akhil Gupta Joins Lupin Board as Independent Director 
  
Mumbai, Naples, November 4, 2026: Global pharmaceutical company Lupin Limited (Lupin) today 
announced the appointment of Akhil Gupta as an Independent Director to its Board.  
 
Akhil Gupta brings more than four decades of experience in building and scaling businesses, corporate 
strategy, finance, governance, and mergers and acquisitions. As Vice Chairman of Bharti Enterprises 
Limited, he played a pivotal role in the group’s growth from inception, both organically and through 
acquisitions. A qualified Chartered Accountant, he has also completed the Advanced Management 
Program at Harvard Business School. 
 
He currently serves as Non-Executive Independent Chairman of 360 ONE WAM, Coforge and 
Valuedrive Technologies Ltd (Spinny); Independent Director of Lodha Developers and Zepto; and 
Nominee Director of Acevector Limited. He is also President of the Telecom Sector Skill Council, a 
Member of the CII National Committee on Telecom & Broadband and the CII Task Force on Insolvency 
and Bankruptcy, and Chairman of Inversion Advisory Services. In addition, he advises leading 
companies across infrastructure, renewable energy, automobiles, tourism, consulate services and 
footwear. 
 
His recognitions include the EY Entrepreneur of the Year Award (Entrepreneur CEO), CA Global 
Achiever, CA Business Achiever, CEO of the Year Award, and CNBC TV18 Best CFO Awards. 
 
Manju D Gupta, Chairperson, Lupin, said, “We are delighted to welcome Akhil to the Lupin Board. He 
brings a rare combination of strategic perspective, financial acumen, governance experience, and a 
proven track record of building businesses at scale. His counsel will be valuable as Lupin continues to 
strengthen its global position and pursue its next phase of growth.” 
 
About Lupin  
Lupin Limited is a global pharmaceutical leader headquartered in Mumbai, India, with a strong presence across 
India, the U.S., Other Developed Markets, and Emerging Markets, with products distributed in over 100 
markets. Lupin has a diversified portfolio spanning generics, complex generics, specialty medicines, and 
biosimilars, and continues to strengthen its market position through a differentiated portfolio. The company 
maintains strong leadership in the U.S. and India across core therapeutic areas, including cardiovascular, 
respiratory, diabetes, gastrointestinal, and women’s health. 15 state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities 
support sustainable operations, with 6 research centers and a dedicated workforce of over 26,000 
professionals. Lupin continues to expand the healthcare ecosystem through diagnostics, digital health, 
patient-support programs, and disease management initiatives. Sustainability continues to be a core pillar of 
the company’s business strategy.
