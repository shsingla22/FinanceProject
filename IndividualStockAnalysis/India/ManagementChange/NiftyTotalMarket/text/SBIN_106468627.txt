Brief Profile of Shri Ravi Ranjan  
 
Shri Ravi Ranjan is presently the Managing Director in the State Bank of India (SBI) with 
effect from Dec ember  15, 2025. He is responsible for Risk, Compliance, & Stressed Assets 
Resolution Group (SARG) verticals of the Bank. Prior to becoming MD, he served as Deputy 
Managing Director (DMD), Global Markets for more than a year and also served as DMD, 
Corporate Account s Group (CAG).  
 
As Chief General Manager (CGM), he headed SBI Chennai Circle (covering the State of 
Tamil Nadu and Puducherry) and also served as President and Chief Operating Officer 
(COO) at SBICAPS, Mumbai. Earlier, he headed the Retail Network as GM in Bengaluru.  
 
Shri Ranjan has done two foreign assignments, at Hong Kong (looking after Debt 
Syndications), and at Los Angeles, California. He is a career banker who joined the Bank 
as Probationary Officer in 1991. He carries a rich experience of more than 34 years of 
working in different domains of banking and finance.
