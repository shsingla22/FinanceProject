 Brief profile  Dr. Jitendra Gangwal is a pharmaceutical R&D leader with 
24+ years of progressive experience across premier research 
institutions and leading Indian pharmaceutical organizations, 
demonstrating steady growth from scientific research roles to 
executive leadership in formulation R&D.  
 
Across his career, he has developed 55+ products spanning 
injections, ophthalmics, inhalation, nasal, and oral routes, 
filed 10+ patents, led multiple 505(b)(2) and ANDA filings, 
and established state -of-the-art R&D facilities. His expertise 
spans complex parenterals, nano -crystals, liposomes, 
microspheres, depot systems, peptides, metal complexes, and 
advanced polymer -based delive ry systems.  
 
He combines deep scientific expertise with strategic business 
acumen, regulatory insight, and strong leadership capability 
to deliver high -value, commercially viable complex products 
in regulated global markets.  
 
Before joining Gland Pharma, Dr. Jitendra Gangwal is 
associated with National Chemical Laboratory (NCL); Pune, 
Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories  Limited , Glenmark 
Pharmaceuticals  Limited , Alkem Laboratories Limited and 
FDC Limited.   
 
Dr. Gangwal holds a PhD from National Chemical
