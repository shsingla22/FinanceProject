Dear Varun,
I am writing to formally tender my resignation from the position of Group ESG Head at Varun
Beverages Ltd., effective August 31, 2025, consequent to the retirement of my services.
It has been a privilege to be an integral part of the Company's journey since January 2019, when I
joined as VBL's CFO. During that tenure, I had the opportunity to lead the successful integration of
PepsiCo's West and South India territories, drive functional excellence, and spearhead
transformational initiatives that enhanced operational agility and cost efficiencies.

In 2022,1 transitioned to lead the ESG function for the Group, where I contributed to the
development of a comprehensive ESG framework across our entities, delivering tangible progress in
climate action, water positivity, renewable energy adoption, plastic recycling, packaging innovation,
and social impact—building a strong foundation for our net-zero carbon journey. Our Company also
earned multiple global ESG recognitions and accolades under PepsiCo's global Pep+ initiative.
1 sincerely appreciate and thank you and Chairman for your respective continued support and

functions for their valuable support and collaboration over the years.

over the years.
