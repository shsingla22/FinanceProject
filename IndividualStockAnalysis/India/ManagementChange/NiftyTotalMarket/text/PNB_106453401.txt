Profile of Shri Amit Kumar Srivastava, Executive Director, punjab National Bank
Shri Amit Kumar Srivastava assumed charge as Executive Director on 24th November,
2025
Shri Srivastava is a Master of Science from Banaras Hindu University and holds Post
Graduate Diploma in Treasury and Forex Management from the Institute of Chartered
Financial Analysts of India (ICFAI) and is a Certified Associate Member of Indian
Institute of Bankers (CAll B).
Shri Srivastava holds certification in Sustainability and Climate Risk (SCR) from GARP
(USA), Professional Risk Manager and Risk in Financial Services
Shri Srivastava started his banking journey in erstwhile Oriental Bank of Commerce as
Management Trainee in the year 1994 and has diversified banking experience of more
than 31 years spread across banking areas viz., Branch Banking, Treasury
Management, Risk Management, Credit Review & Monitoring.
He headed erstwhile Jodhpur Zone of Punjab National Bank as the Zonal Manager and
was Group Chief Risk Officer of Punjab National Bank before this assignment.
