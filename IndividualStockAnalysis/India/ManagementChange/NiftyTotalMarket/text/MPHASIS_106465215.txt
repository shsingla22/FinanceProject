Mphasis appoints Mr. Punit Sood  as an Independent Director on the Board  
 
Bengaluru, December  10, 202 5 
 
Mphasis , (BSE: 526299 ; NSE: MPHASIS ), a global AI -led, platform -driven technology solutions provider, has appointed  Mr. 
Punit Sood  as an Independent Director of the Company , for a period of five years , effective  11th December 2025 . This 
appointment is based on the recommendations  of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and is subject to 
shareholders' approval .  
 
Mr. Punit Sood  is currently an Independent Director on the boards of ICICI Bank and NPCI (National Payments Corporation 
of India). He brings over 36 years of leadership experience at the intersection of financial services and technology, having 
driven transformation across global enterprises.  
 
Mr. Sood’s  career spans senior roles in organizations such as Citibank, GE Capital, United Technologies, JPMorgan Chase, 
and NatWest, where he progressed to CIO positions across geographies. He has also played a key role in shaping and 
executing GCC strategies for l eading financial institutions , including NatWest, JPMorgan Chase, and Citibank. Punit chaired 
the NASSCOM GCC Council for 2021 –23 and continued as a member for the 2023 –25 term. He also serves as an External 
Expert with BCG, advising clients on g lobal capability centre  strategy.  
 
“We are delighted to have Mr. Punit Sood join the Board of Mphasis.  With an accomplished record of leading global 
technology businesses and steering transformation across markets, his experience will be a powerful asset to our 
company. His insights on leadership will enrich our strategic thinking as we continue to navigate the ever -changing 
business landscape ,” said Mr. Nitin Rakesh, CEO and Managing Director, Mphasis.  
 
“It is a privilege to join this distinguished Board and collaborate with such accomplished leaders. I look forward to serving 
as an Independent Director and contributing my experience to advance the company’s growth journey ,” said Mr. Punit 
Sood .  
 
Mr. Sood holds a Master of Business Administration  (MBA)  from IIM Ahmedabad and is a  graduate of  IIT Roorkee.
