Sub: Change in Director – Cessation & Appointment  
 
Dear Sir/ Madam,  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligation and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, it is hereby informed that the Ministry of Railways, Government of India 
vide letter No. 2022/PL/57/10 dated 10/08/2026 has conveyed the appointment of Shri Rajesh 
Gupta, PED (Signal Modernization), Railway Board as Part-Time Govt. Director on the Board 
of RailTel Corporation of India Limited, in lieu of Shri Rameshwar Meena, with immediate 
effect, till he holds the post of PED (Signal Modernization).  
 
2. Accordingly, Shri Rameshwar Meena ceased to be Part-time Govt. Nominee Director on the 
Board of the Company w.e.f. 10/08/2026 and Shri Rajesh Gupta would be the Part-time Govt. 
Nominee Director on the Board of the Company w.e.f. 10/08/2026.  
 
3. In compliance of the provisions of Companies Act, 2013 and SEBI (Listing Obligations and  
Disclosure Requirements), Regulation 2015, Company is taking necessary action to complete 
the appointment formalities of Shri Rajesh Gupta, and requisite disclosure in this regard will 
be submitted in due course.  
 
4. The requisite details as per SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-
CFDPOD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 regarding cessation of Shri Rameshwar Meena 
is enclosed as Annexure . 
 
5. Please take note of the above information on record
