Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and  
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform you of the  
following appointments  as Key Managerial Personnel (“KMP”) of Delhivery, effective im mediately:  
 
1. Mr. Arun Bagavathi has been designated as Chief Operating Officer - Network Operations. Arun has led 
Delhivery’s network operations, fleet management and control tower functions. In his elevated role, he will 
hold enterprise -wide accountability for the end -to-end performance and efficiency of Delhivery's 
nationwide logistics network. He is in full time employment of Delhivery Singapore Pte Ltd., wholly owned 
subsidiary of the Company.  
 
2. Mr. Prashant Gazipur has been designated as Chief Operating Officer - In-city Operations. Prashant has 
driven last -mile operations across Delhivery’s urban delivery network and has been instrumental in scaling 
Delhivery’s last mile reach and capacity. He w ill now lead all city -level operations strategy including building 
out last -mile franchise pickup and drop operations.  
 
3. Mr. Nikhil Ummat has been designated as Chief Operating Officer - Engineering and Automation. Nikhil has 
led Delhivery’s automation initiatives and fulfillment centre operations, and has been at the forefront of 
Delhivery’s proprietary drone and robotics p rogramme under Delhivery Robotics. As Chief of Engineering 
& Automation, he will drive Delhivery’s engineering and automation infrastructure strategy.  
 
4. Mr. Kumar Sunny Raja has been designated as Chief Procurement Officer. Sunny has led Delhivery’s 
commercials and procurement function, building scalable and cost -efficient supply -side capabilities. As 
Chief Procurement Officer, he will take on broader acco untability for effective capex strategy, partner 
management, and commercial optimisation across Delhivery.  
 
5. Mr. Varun Bakshi, who was designated as Senior Management Personnel and SVP & Head - PTL has now 
been designated as Chief Sales Officer. Varun has been instrumental in scaling Delhivery’s Part Truckload 
(PTL) business and building long -term enterprise rela tionships with leading brands across categories. As 
CSO, he will lead Delhivery’s overall direct and cross -selling strategy across all business lines and customer 
segments.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
6. Mr. Vikas Kapoor has been designated as Chief Strategy Officer. Vikas has led Delhivery’s Full Truckload 
(FTL) business and brings a strong commercial and market understanding. In his new role, he will be 
responsible for shaping Delhivery’s long -term strat egic priorities, identifying new growth opportunities, 
and driving cross -functional alignment.
