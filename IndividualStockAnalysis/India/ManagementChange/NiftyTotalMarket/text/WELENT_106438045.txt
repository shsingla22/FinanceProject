Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations, we wish to inform you that the
Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting held today i.e. on November 11, 2025, basis the
recommendation of the Nomination & Remuneration Committee, has inter alia considered and approved the
following changes in the roles and responsibilities of the Senior Management Personnel(s) as given below:a) Mr. Asim Chakraborty, from his current role of CEO - Transportation Vertical, will transition to a new role
as Executive Director, Senior Managerial Personnel (SMP), within the organization. Pursuant to this
transition, Mr. Abhishek Chaudhary, presently serving as the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and designated
as Key Managerial Personnel (KMP) of the Company, will assume the role of CEO - Transportation Vertical,
SMP, and continue to report to the Managing Director of the Company; and
b) Mr. Hardik Dhebar, currently designated as Chief Financial Officer (CFO) of Welspun Michigan Engineers
Limited (WMEL), material subsidiary of the Company, in addition to his current responsibilities, will also
lead the Investor Relations function. He will be re-designated as President - Finance & Accounts, of the
Company, alongwith the CFO of WMEL. For purposes of Investor Relations, he will report to the Managing
Director of the Company and will be considered as the SMP of the Company.
The details as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations, read along with SEBI Master
Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024, are enclosed as “Annexure-A”
and “Annexure-B” to this letter.
The Board Meeting commenced at 11:30 a.m. and concluded at 05:30 p.m.
