RATIONALE AND BENEFITS OF THE SCHEME
With the objective of simplifying the group structure, it is proposed to consolidate the assets
and liabilities of the Transferor Companies with the Transferee Company, its holding
company. The rationale and the benefits of the amalgamation of the Transferor Companies
with the Transferee Company, are as follows:
(i) consolidation of the business, leading to synergies of operations and resulting in the
expansion and long-term sustainable growth, which will enhance value for various
stakeholders of the Transferee Company;
(ii) the Transferee Company would have direct access to capital, thereby creating a
unified larger entity with greater financial strength and flexibility;
(iii) pooling of knowledge and expertise;
(iv) to achieve optimal and efficient utilization of capital, enhance operational and
management efficiencies;
(v) rationalisation and simplification of structure by reducing the number of legal entities;
and
(vi) reduction in administrative responsibilities, multiplicity of records and legal as well
as regulatory compliances.
Accordingly, the Scheme is in the interest of the Parties involved and their respective
stakeholders.
