Brief Profile (in case of appointment) Shri B. K. Jain is an Indian Railway Service of Engineers 
(IRSE), 1992 batch officer, with over three decades of 
distinguished service in Indian Railways. He holds a 
Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering from IIT Roorkee 
and an M. Tech from IIT Delhi, along with initial and 
advanced professional training from IRICEN, Pune and 
international in-service programmes in Singapore and 
Malaysia. 
He has served Indian Railways in various capacities. His 
core areas of expertise encompass new line construction, 
doubling and gauge conversion projects, permanent way, 
bridges and buildings, railway land and estate management, 
tendering, contracts and arbitration, safety management, and 
disaster preparedness. He has also played a significant role 
in coordination with State Governments, PSUs, and other 
Ministries .
