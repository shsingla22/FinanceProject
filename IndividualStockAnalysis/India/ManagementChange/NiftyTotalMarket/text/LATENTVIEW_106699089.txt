for his contributions over a decade and wish him the absolute best in his next chapter.” 
 
 2. Appointment of Ms. Sonal Ramrakhiani as the Chief Executive Officer (Senior 
Management Personnel) of the Company

the Chief Executive Officer (Senior Management Personnel) of the Company with effect from 
July 15, 2026.  
Welcoming the new CEO, Mr. A.V. Venkatraman , said, “The Board is delighted to welcome
