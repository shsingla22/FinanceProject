   Brief Profile of Mr. Kanwar Singh:  
 
 
Kanwar Singh is a seasoned technology leader with over 30 
years of experience driving large- scale IT delivery, optimizing 
enterprise technology portfolios, and leading data- driven digital 
transformations. His career reflects a deep understanding of the 
evolving technology landscape, from the early era ERP and custom solutions to cloud- first digital enterprises.  
 
Kanwar has led complex, global initiatives spanning industries 
such as retail, consumer packaged goods, manufacturing, and 
travel & transportation. His solid functional expertise includes 
digital interventions, solution architecture, automation, and global  
delivery management —reflecting a deep understanding of how 
technology can accelerate business outcomes.  
 
 
Widely respected for his strategic acumen and ability to translate technology investments into tangible business 
outcomes, Kanwar has led numerous complex, global engagements that have delivered sustained client value 
and operational excellence.  
 
Prior to joining Wipro, Kanwar spent 19 years with  Accenture, where he spearheaded global technology delivery 
and sales support for the Americas business through the Accenture  Advanced Technology Center in India. He 
also served as the global lead for Accenture’s  GCC business, driving innovation and growth across key markets.  
 
Kanwar holds a Bachelor of Engineering degree in Electronics & Communications from the Indian Institute of 
Technology (IIT), Roorkee.
