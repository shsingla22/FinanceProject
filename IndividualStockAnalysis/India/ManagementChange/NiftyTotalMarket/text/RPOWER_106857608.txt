Basis of Qualified Conclusion 
5. We draw attention to Note 5 of the Statement with reference to Rajasthan Sun Technique Energy Private 
Limited (RSTEPL), a wholly owned subsidiary company, wherein the auditors of RSTEPL have 
modified their review conclusion on its financial results for the quarter and half year ended September 
30, 2026. They have set out that RSTEPL has incurred a net loss of Rs. 11,952 lakhs during the half year 
ended September 30, 2026 respectively, as well as during the preceding financial years, and has a 
negative net worth of Rs. 2,72,954 lakhs as at September 30, 2026. Further, as at September 30, 2026, its 
current liabilities exceeded its current assets by Rs. 2,55,346 lakhs, and RSTEPL has defaulted in 
repayment of borrowings aggregating to Rs. 2,33,547 lakhs. These events and conditions indicate the 
existence of a material uncertainty that may cast significant doubt about RSTEPL' s ability to continue as 
a going concern. The appropriateness of the going concern assumption is critically dependent upon 
RSTEPL's ability to raise finance and generate cash flows in future to meet its obligations. However, the 
financial results of RSTEPL have been prepared on a going concern basis for the reasons stated in the 
aforesaid note. In the absence of necessary evidence with respect to management's assessment of going 
concern, the auditors of RSTEPL were unable to obtain adequate evidence regarding management's use 
of the going concern assumption in the preparation of the financial results of RSTEPL. 
6. We draw attention to Note 6 of the Statement wherein the auditors ofSamalkot Power Limited (SMPL) 
have set out that SMPL has incurred a net loss of Rs. 6,231 lakhs during the half year ended September 
30, 2026 respectively, as well as during the preceding financial years, and has a negative net worth of 
Rs. 2,89,415 lakhs as at September 30, 2026. Further, as at September 30, 2026, its current liabilities 
exceeded its current assets by Rs. 4,62,989 lakhs, and SMPL has defaulted in repayment of borrowings 
aggregating to Rs. 1,89,239 lakhs. The lender of SMPL has invoked the corporate guarantee against the 
Parent Company and filed an application under section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, 
and the arbitration initiated by SMPL was withdrawn on July 30, 2026 following the Arbitration 
Tribunal's partial final award on jurisdiction dated June 23, 2026. These events and conditions indicate 
the existence of a material uncertainty that may cast significant doubt about SMPL's ability to continue 
as a going concern. However, the financial results of SMPL have been prepared on a going concern basis 
for the reasons stated in the aforesaid note, including the proposed sale of equipment of Module 2 and 
Module 3, which is subject to the lender's approval. In the absence of necessary evidence with respect to 
management's assessment of going concern, the auditors of SMPL were unable to obtain adequate 
evidence regarding management's use of the going concern assumption in the preparation of the financial 
results of SMPL. 
7. We draw attention to Note 7 of the Statement wherein the auditors of Dhursar Solar Power Private 
Limited (DSPPL) have set out that the carrying value of Property, Plant and Equipment (PPE) as at 
September 30, 2026 is Rs. 32,154 lakhs, representing 38.64% of the total assets of DSPPL. The 
management of DSPPL has carried out the impairment assessment of PPE based on the report of a 
registered valuer, wherein the recoverable amount has been stated to be higher than the carrying amount 
as at March 31, 2026. However, the auditors of DSPPL were unable to obtain sufficient appropriate 
evidence regarding the assumptions and estimates used in the valuation report, including projected 
revenue, growth assumptions and the weighted average cost of capital applied in determining the 
recoverable amount. Consequently, we are unable to determine whether any adjustment is necessary to 
the carrying value of these assets and the consequential impact, if any, on the consolidated financial 
results.

Material Uncertainty relating to Going Concern 
10. We draw attention to Note 8 of the Statement regarding the Group's ability to meet its obligations, which 
is dependent on certain events or conditions. The consequential impact of the events or conditions and 
the matters stated in paragraphs 5 & 6 above indicate that a material uncertainty exists that may cast 
significant doubt on the Group's ability to continue as a going concern particularly in relation to RSTEPL 
and SMPL. However, the Group is confident of meeting its liabilities in the ordinary course of business 
and through time-bound monetisation of assets of the subsidiaries. Accordingly, the consolidated 
financial results of the Group have been prepared on a going concern basis. Our conclusion on the 
Statement is not modified in respect of this matter.

5. Rajasthan Sun Technique Energy Private Limited ("RSTEPL") has incurred continuous losses due 
to the failure of the technology implemented for the project, which has not delivered the desired 
results despite multiple improvement measures undertaken . Consequently, RSTEPL has defaulted 
in repayment dues to its lenders during the quarter ended September 30, 2026, as well as in earlier 
periods. Further, on July 26, 2022, Ld. Appellate Tribunal for Electricity ("APTEL") allowed appeal 
filed by RSTEPL granting compensation to RSTEPL towards reduction in solar radiation and steep 
Foreign Exchange Rate Variation. Procurers have challenged the said APTEL order in Hon'ble 
Supreme Court which stayed the implementation of APTEL order and is currently pending 
adjudication. Further, the management is pursuing various measures for improvement in 
operational performance and resolution of lender dues. The management is also in advanced 
discussions with lenders to achieve a resolution of the outstanding debt defaults. In view of the 
above, the financial results of RSTEPL have been prepared on a going concern basis. 
Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Parent Company remains unaffected by RSTEPL's defaults. 
The auditors of RSTEPL have drawn qualification in their review report.

6. Samalkot Power Limited ("SMPL"), a subsidiary of the Parent Company, has incurred losses during 
the quarter as well as earlier years and its current liabilities exceeds its current assets. The lender 
of SMPL raised a demand amounting to Rs. 1,89,239 lakhs (including interest), invoking the 
guarantee agreement executed by the Parent Company. Subsequently, SMPL has initiated 
arbitration proceedings against the lender and Citibank N.A. (facility agent) before the London Court 
of International Arbitration ("Arbitration Tribunal"), in accordance with the terms of the Amended 
and Restated Credit Agreement dated June 28, 2019, read with the marketing agreements 
executed among, inter alia, the lender, SMPL, and the marketing consultants . The arbitration has 
been initiated on the ground that no debt was due under the aforesaid agreements. In light of the 
above, the Parent Company has communicated to the lender and the facility agent that, since the 
debt has not fallen due and there is no default, the guarantee provided by the Parent Company 
cannot be invoked. On June 23, 2026, the Arbitration Tribunal issued its partial final award on 
jurisdiction, holding that it does not have jurisdiction in respect of SMPL's claim. The Arbitration 
Tribunal expressly confined its decision to the issue of jurisdiction and did not examine or determine 
the merits of SMPL's substantive claims. Subsequently , on July 30, 2026, the arbitration initiated 
by SMPL under the marketing agreements was withdrawn . During the previous quarter ended June 
30, 2026, the lender has initiated the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) under 
Section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 against the Parent Company and the 
management is currently evaluating the appropriate legal course of action. Further, SMPL has 
discharged all interest obligations up to June 30, 2025, and the interest for the period July 2025 to 
September 30, 2026, has been appropriately provided in the books of account. On June 6, 2026, 
SMPL entered into an Equipment Sale Agreement with a buyer for the sale of project equipment 
pertaining to Module 2 and Module 3 of the 1,508 MW (2 x 754 MW) Combined Cycle Power Plant. 
The proposed sale is subject to the lender's approval. Further during the quarter ended September 
30, 2026 SMPL has received standby letter of credit amounting to USO 10 Million from the buyer. 
Upon consummation of the proposed asset monetization transaction and receipt of requisite 
approvals, the management expects the outstanding lender obligations to be completely 
addressed. The management has considered the proposed monetization of assets and ongoing 
discussions with lenders while assessing the appropriateness of the going concern assumption. 
Accordingly, the management believes that, it will be able to realize its assets and discharge its 
liabilities in the normal course of business and therefore the financial results continue to be 
prepared on a going concern basis. The auditors of SMPL have drawn qualification in their review 
report.
