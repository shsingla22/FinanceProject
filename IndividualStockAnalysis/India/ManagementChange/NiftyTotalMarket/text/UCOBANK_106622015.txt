Madam/Dear Sir, 
 
Sub: Change in the Composition of Board of Directors of UCO Bank 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 and 51 read with Schedule III of  SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 , we inform that the 
Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub-
section (3) of section 9 of the Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of 
Undertakings) Act, 1970, has nominated Shri Hari Har Mishra as Director on the 
Board of UCO Bank in place of Shri Sudhir Shyam, with immediate effect and 
until further orders, vide Notification eF.No.6/2(iv)/2022-BO.I dated 13.05.2026.  
 
Accordingly, Shri Sudhir Shyam ceases to be Director on the Board of the Bank 
with effect from 13.05.2026. 
 
We confirm that Shri Hari Har Mishra is not debarred from holding the office of 
Director by virtue of any order passed by SEBI or any other authority. 
 
The requisite details, as prescribed in the SEBI Master Circular dated 11 July, 
2023, are enclosed herewith as Annexure I. 
 
This is for your information and record. 
 
Yours sincerely,
