Dear Sir/Madam,
In compliance with Regulation 30 read with Schedule III, Part A of the Securities and Exchange
Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI
Listing Regulations”), we hereby inform you that Mr. Dilip Pattanayak, President & Group Chief
Human Resource Officer and a Senior Management Person (“SMP”) of the Company, in terms of
Regulation 16(1)(d) of SEBI Listing Regulations, has tendered his resignation from the services of
the Company on May 20, 2025, due to personal reasons and to pursue career opportunities outside
JSW group. The Company has accepted the same and he is being relieved from the services of the
Company with effect from close of business hours on August 18, 2025 (Last working day).
Over the past six years of his association with JSW, Mr. Pattanayak has played a pivotal role in
strengthening the Human Resource function and shaping a robust people agenda. The Company
sincerely thanks him for his leadership, dedication, and significant contributions, and wish him the
very best in his future endeavors.
