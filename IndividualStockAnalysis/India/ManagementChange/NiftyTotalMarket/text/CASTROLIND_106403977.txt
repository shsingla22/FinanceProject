BRIEF PROFILE OF MR. SAUGATA BASURAY
With over 25 years of experience across sales, marketing, and
business leadership, Saugata Basuray has built a rich and
diverse career with Castrol India Limited (CIL). He joined the
Company as a Management Trainee in 1999, soon after
completing his MBA from Symbiosis Institute of Business
Management (SIBM) and has since grown though a series of
impactful roles in India and abroad.
Saugata has worked in four countries across two continents,
giving him a deep understanding of operating in diverse markets and through different
economic cycles. Early in his career, he served as Executive Assistant to the Managing
Director of CIL and Regional Vice President bp—an experience that offered him a close view
of strategic leadership and global business operations.
In 2008, Saugata moved to Castrol’s global head quarter in the UK, where he was part of
the marketing team that helped shape Castrol Edge into a global power brand. After
returning to India, he led sales for the B2B channel and later served as Head of Marketing
for Castrol India. He went on to lead Castrol Philippines from 2013 to 2017 and subsequently
took charge as Managing Director of Castrol’s joint venture in Indonesia.
Currently, Saugata heads B2C business segment at CIL, where he has driven a multi-year
transformation of the Company’s go-to-market model and significantly expanded Castrol’s
distribution network deep into rural India.
Saugata holds an Engineering degree from the University of Pune.
