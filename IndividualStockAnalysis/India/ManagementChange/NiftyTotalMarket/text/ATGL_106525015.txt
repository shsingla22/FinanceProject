downstream city gas distribution (CGD) businesses.  
 
He joined the Company as Chief Executive Officer in 
September 2018. Prior to this, he was associated with 
GAIL for about five years, followed by over 13 years 
with Mahanagar Gas Limited, a joint venture of British 
Gas (later Shell) and GAIL. He subsequently served 
with Reliance Industries Limited for more than 10 
years, where he held senior leadership positions, 
including Chief Financial Officer for the refinery and 
marketing divisions (retail petroleum business). 
 
Mr. Manglani possesses extensive experience in 
managing profit and loss responsibilities, city gas 
distribution operations, and the gas business value 
chain, as well as petroleum retail and policy advocacy. 
He is a strong proponent of digitalisation and process 
transformation, consistently driving operational 
excellence across organisations he has served.
