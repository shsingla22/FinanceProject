Annexure-“3”-Brief profile of Mr. Christoph Stoller 
 
Mr. Christoph Stoller is a highly accomplished Swiss pharmaceutical industry leader with more 
than 30 years of global experience in branded pharmaceuticals, specialty medicines, biologics, 
biosimilars, generics, OTC products, and innovative therapies. He has held CEO, President, 
Board, and Global Business Leadership roles across Europe, Emerging Markets, LATAM, and 
APAC.  
 
Educational qualification: 
 
Degree in Business Administration, Economics, Law, and Social Sciences from the University 
of St. Gallen, Switzerland, one of Europe's most respected business schools. 
 
Previous role 
 
 President & Business Head (EMEA, LATAM & APAC) at Glenmark Pharmaceuticals. 
 Member of Glenmark's Global Leadership Team. 
 Leading approximately 2,000 employees across 80 countries with P&L responsibility 
exceeding USD 750 million.  
 
Major Career Highlights 
 
 Doubled European revenue at Glenmark from approximately USD 200 million and 
significantly improved EBITDA performance. 
 Led commercialization and expansion of innovative products across international 
markets. 
 Member of Glenmark's AI Steering Committee driving business transformation 
initiatives.  
 
Previous leadership roles 
 
Teva Pharmaceuticals (2011-2021) 
 
 Senior Vice President & Cluster General Manager, Germany & Austria. 
 Senior Vice President, Generics & Commercial Operations Europe. 
 Managed businesses with up to USD 2 billion P&L and around 2,700 employees. 
 Led the European integration of Teva's acquisition of Actavis. 
 Delivered major profitability improvements, market access initiatives, and large-scale 
transformation programs.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
Helvepharm AG 
 
 CEO and member of the Executive Committee of Zur Rose Group. 
 Successfully transformed the company from a loss-making business into a highly 
profitable organization with strong value creation.  
 
Earlier Career

Industry Leadership & Governance
