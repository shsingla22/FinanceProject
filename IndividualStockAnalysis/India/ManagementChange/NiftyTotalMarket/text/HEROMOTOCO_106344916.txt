Hero MotoCorp strengthens India growth plans with appointment of
Latika Taneja as Head of Corporate Communications, Corporate
Affairs & CSR to drive Brand Narrative, Stakeholder Trust and Social
Impact
New Delhi, August 19th, 2025: Hero MotoCorp Ltd., the world’s
largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters, is pleased to
announce the appointment of Ms. Latika Taneja as the Head of
Corporate Communications, Corporate Affairs, and Corporate
Social Responsibility (CSR), effective August 19th, 2025. In her
new role, she will be responsible for shaping a cohesive, purposedriven brand narrative, forging strategic partnerships, and embedding
social impact initiatives into Hero MotoCorp’s long - term growth
strategy.
Latika’s appointment comes at a transformative moment as we shape
the future of mobility and reinforce Hero MotoCorp’s leadership in this
dynamic ecosystem,” said Dr. Pawan Munjal, Executive Chairman,
Hero MotoCorp. “Her ability to align reputation management with
business strategy will be instrumental in strengthening stakeholder
trust, advancing our global positioning, and driving our journey
towards sustainable, purpose-driven growth.
With an illustrious career spanning over 23 years, Latika brings a diverse leadership experience across
corporate communications, government relations, policy & advocacy, and CSR. She has held senior
leadership positions at globally respected organizations including Shell, Mastercard, DuPont, AlcatelLucent, and MetLife. Prior to joining Hero MotoCorp, Latika served as the Head of Corporate Relations at
Shell India, where she successfully established and scaled the function across multiple regions.
Recognized among India’s Top 100 Public Relations Professionals, Latika was also conferred the Women
Disruptor Award for Excellence in PR in 2019, acknowledging her leadership and impact in the field.
She holds a BA (Hons) in English from Lady Shri Ram College, a Post Graduate Diploma in Business
Management from Symbiosis, and has completed an Executive Program at the Tata Institute of Social
Sciences.
Latika’s appointment reinforces Hero MotoCorp’s continued focus on driving long-term creation, enhancing
governance, and deliver meaningful value to stakeholders in the evolving mobility landscape.
