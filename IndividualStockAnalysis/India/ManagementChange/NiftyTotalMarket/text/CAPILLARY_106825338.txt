The brief profile  of the Senior Management Personnel : 
Mr. Swift  joined the Capillary group in April 2023 pursuant to the acquisition of Brierley. At Brierley, 
he architected and built its original loyalty software platform, and subsequently served as its Chief 
Technology Officer and later as its Chief Executive Office r. Since joining Capillary, he has served as 
Chief Technical Architect – North America, engaging with new and existing clients, building strategic 
partnerships and supporting business development.  Mr. Swift will oversee the Company’s operations 
across the United States, Canada, Mexico, Central America and South America .
