BRIEF PROFILE 
Dr. Naushad Darius Forbes : 
Dr. Naushad Darius Forbes has Bachelor’s, Master’s and PhD Degrees from Stanford 
University. He is a Co-Chairman of Forbes Marshall, India's leading process and energy 
efficiency company. He is the Chairman of Ananta Aspen Centre and Centre for Technology, 
Innovation and Economic Research. Dr. Forbes was an occasional Lecturer and Consulting 
Professor at Stanford University from 1987 to 2004 where he developed courses on 
Technology in Newly Industrializing Countries. 
Dr. Forbes is on the board of several educational institutions and public companies. He was 
the President of Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) for 2016-17. Currently, he Chairs the 
CII National Committee on Technology, Innovation, & Research, CII National Committee on 
Intellectual Property and the CII National Higher Education Committee. He is also a founding 
member of Nayanta University, a full-service university spearheaded by CII.
