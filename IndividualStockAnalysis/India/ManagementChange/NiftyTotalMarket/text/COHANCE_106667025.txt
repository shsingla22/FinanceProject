I am writing to formally resign from my positions as Whole Time Director, Chief Financial Officer
and Key Managerial Personnel of Cohance Lifesciences Limited, as well as from the
membership of any committees of the Board.
After a deeply rewarding tenure with the Company, I have decided to accept a new professional
opportunity outside the organisation that aligns with my Jong-term career aspirations.
It has been an absolute privilege to lead the financial operations of Cohance during a period of
such significant transformation. I am deeply grateful to the Board of Directors, the leadership
team, and my colleagues in the finance department for their trust, support, and collaboration
throughout my journey here. I am proud of the strong balance sheet, corporate governance
standards and internal controls we have built together and have no doubt that the Company will
continue its growth trajectory
For the purposes of regulatory compliance and listing obligations, I hereby confirm that there
are no other material reasons for my resignation other than the career advancement opportunity
mentioned above.
My last working day will be the close of business hours on September 13, 2026, in accordance
with the notice period applicable to my role. I am fully committed to ensuring an orderly and
seamless handover of my responsibilities and to supporting the management in the smooth
onboarding of my successor during my remaining time with the Company
I request the Company to kindly accept my resignation. I wish Cohance, its Board, its leadership
team and its shareholders continued success, prosperity, and growth in the years ahead.
Yours sincerely,
Himanshu Agarwal
