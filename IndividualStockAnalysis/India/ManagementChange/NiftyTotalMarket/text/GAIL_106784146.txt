Sub: Change in Directorship – Appointment of Director (Projects)  
 
Sir/Madam,  
 
This is to inform that a letter bearing no. CA -31022/2/2025 -CA-PNG (54537) dated 17.09.2026 
has been received from the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Government of India 
(MoP&NG) nominating Shri Manoj Kumar Sharma, Executive Director, Indian Oil 
Corporation Limited as a Director (Projects) of GAIL with effect from the date of his 
assumption of charge of the post till the date of his superannuation i.e. 30.04.2031 or until 
further orders, whichever is the earlier.  
 
The Board in its meeting held on 18.09.2026 approved the appointment of Shri Manoj Kumar 
Sharma (DIN 08821234) as an Additional Director of the Company with effect from the date 
of his assumption of charge to the post of Director (Projects) of the Company.  
 
a) Brief Profile of Shri Manoj Kumar Sharma is as under : 
 
Shri Manoj Kumar Sharma  holds a Master’s Degree in Mechanical Engineering 
(Production & Industrial Engineering) from Delhi College of Engineering (DCE) and a 
Post Graduate Diploma in Business Management from Xavier Institute of Management, 
Bhubaneswar (XIMB).  
 
With over three decades of distinguished experience in the oil and gas sector, Shri 
Sharma has developed extensive expertise across the entire value chain of hydrocarbon 
infrastructure, encompassing design and engineering, project management, 
procurement, construction management, and operations and maintenance of crude oil, 
petroleum products, LPG, and natural gas pipeline networks, as well as strategically 
significant crude oil terminal projects.  
 
Shri Sharma served as Executive Director and Regional Head of Eastern Region 
Pipelines at Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL). In this role, he led the operations 
and maintenance, construction, and city gas distribution (CGD) businesses across five 
states, overseeing an extensive pipeline network exceeding 5,700 km.  
  

   
 
 
 
He also held the position of Executive Director (Projects) in IOCL’s Pipelines Division, 
where he was responsible for the engineering, procurement, and construction 
management of major oil, LPG, and natural gas pipeline projects in India as well as 
globall y. 
 
Shri Sharma has served on the Boards of GSPL India Transco Limited (GITL), GSPL 
India Gasnet Limited (GIGL), and Kochi Salem Pipeline Private Limited (KSPPL) as 
Nominee Director representing IOCL, contributing to strategic governance and sectoral 
developme nt. 
 
He brings a wealth of leadership experience and a proven track record in board -level 
governance, project execution, business development, operational excellence, and cost 
optimization. His deep understanding of energy infrastructure, coupled with his 
commi tment to innovation and emerging energy initiatives, positions him as a key 
contributor to the growth and transformation of the energy sector.
