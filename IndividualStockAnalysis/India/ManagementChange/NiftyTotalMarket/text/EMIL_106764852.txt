Built to Adapt.
Ready to Scale.
Retail is constantly changing. Consumer preferences shift,
technology moves quickly and market conditions rarely
stay predictable. At EMIL, we have built our business
around the ability to respond to that change without
compromising on execution discipline.

That adaptability shapes the choices we make
every day, from where we open our next store and
which partnerships we deepen, to how we manage
operations and serve our customers. Each decision is
made with one objective in mind: to build a stronger,
more agile and more productive business.
That approach was put to test in 2025-26. Unseasonal
weather, changing demand patterns and regulatory
developments affected consumption across several
product categories. Through these conditions, we stayed
focused on what we could control.

We maintained inventory discipline, deployed capital
carefully and worked to improve productivity across
our retail network, ensuring that every investment
strengthened the business for the long term.
At the same time, we continued to expand. We opened
29 new stores during the year, taking our network to 223
stores. Our cluster-led expansion strategy continued to
support greater operating efficiency while helping us
deepen our presence in the markets we serve.
As we look ahead, our ambition goes beyond adding scale.
We are building an organisation capable of sustaining
it, supported by trusted relationships, technology-led
operations and a customer-first approach: one that can
adapt as markets evolve, capture new opportunities and
grow profitably over time.


ABOUT EMIL

Rooted in Retail.
Built for Growth.

Electronics Mart India Limited (‘EMIL’, ‘The Company’, or ‘We’) has spent over four decades
becoming one of India’s leading multi-brand retailers of consumer durables, electronics and
home appliances. Our growth has followed a clear discipline, a customer-first approach paired
with a cluster-based expansion strategy, and it shows in a retail network built as much on local
market understanding as on scale.
Our retail footprint runs across multi-brand outlets (MBOs), Bajaj Electronics in the Southern Cluster
and Electronics Mart in the Delhi-NCR Cluster, together with exclusive brand outlets (EBOs) built through
partnerships with leading brands. This growth reflects our ability to adapt to changing customer
preferences without losing sight of the business fundamentals.
We have also diversified beyond the core retail network into specialised formats, such as Kitchen Stories,
Easy Kitchens, Audio & Beyond and The Charcoal Project (TCP), built for the growing demand in premium
home, lifestyle and integrated living. This year, we expanded our footprint further while sharpening focus
on the core multi-brand business, backed by continued premiumisation across categories, disciplined
capital allocation and technology-enabled operations.
