Sub.: Intimation for Resignation of Chief Executive Officer of the Company
Dear Sir/ Ma’am,
This is to inform you that Mr. Aseem Joshi, CEO of the Company, has submitted his resignation via email dated
August 6, 2025, in order to pursue new opportunities. The Company has accepted his resignation, and he will be
relieved of his duties effective September 26, 2025.
Following his departure, Mr. Tarak Patel, Managing Director, will assume Mr. Joshi’s responsibilities. Mr. Patel
previously led the India business and is actively involved in day-to-day operations, as well as managing key
relationships with clients and partners in the region.
GMM Pfaudler has recently launched a Global Transformation Program (GTP) aimed at unifying our operations,
speeding up decision-making, driving growth, and enhancing profitability. With these objectives in mind, the
Company will assess the requirement and scope for an India CEO role in the future as part of the next steps of
the GTP.

After nearly four enriching years of professional and personal growth at GMM, I have decided to step down from my role as CEO and as KMP of GMM, effective 26
September 2025.
It has been a truly rewarding journey to be part of an exceptional team and to contribute to the significant milestones we’ve achieved together. I am immensely grateful
for the trust placed in me and for the opportunity to lead and grow alongside such a talented group of individuals.
This decision comes after careful thought and reflection. I believe it is the right time to pursue a new opportunity that is better aligned to my professional goals and
personal aspirations.
I would like to extend my sincere thanks to the Board of Directors and the senior management team for their unwavering support, encouragement, and guidance
throughout my tenure.
