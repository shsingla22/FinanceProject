Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI Listing Regulations, we hereby 
inform that upon recommendation of Nomination and Remuneration Committee (“NRC”), the 
Board of Directors at its Meeting held today i.e. Tuesday, January 27, 2026, has inter-alia  
considered and approved the following changes in Key Managerial Personnel (KMP) and Senior 
Management Personnel (SMP) of the Company, consequent upon changes in internal role, 
designation and responsibilities in the organization structure, post acquisition of the Imperial 
Blue Business: 
  
A) Change in Key Managerial Personnel  
 
1) Mr. Abhinav Gupta, Chief Financial Officer, has relinquished his position as Chief 
Financial Officer with immediate effect, consequent upon his transition to a new role 
within the Company as Chief of Internal Audit.  Accordingly, he shall cease to be a 
Key Managerial Personnel (KMP) but shall remain as Senior Management Personnel 
(SMP) of the Company with immediate effect. The letter received from Mr. Abhinav 
Gupta is attached. 
 
2) Mr. Rajesh Choudhary, presently serving as Senior Vice President - Finance & 
Accounts is appointed as Chief Financial Officer of the Company, a Key Managerial 
Personnel (KMP) and a Senior Management Personnel (SMP), with immediate effect. 
 
3) Mr. Minuzeer Bamboat, presently serving as Company Secretary & Compliance Officer 
of the Company, a Key Managerial Personnel (KMP) and a Senior Management 
Personnel (SMP) has been assigned additional responsibilities as Head – Legal with 
immediate effect. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
                                                                                                                                          
 
B) Changes in Senior Managerial Personnel  
 
 
1) Mr. Nishant Jain, President-Sales is re-designated as Chief Sales Officer with 
immediate effect 
 
2) Mr. Ameya Deshpande, President - Corporate Development & Strategy is re-
designated as Chief Strategy Officer with immediate effect 
 
3) Mr. Tarun Behl, Vice President - Manufacturing is re-designated as Vice President 
-Manufacturing & Projects with immediate effect 
 
Mr Nishant Jain, Mr. Ameya Deshpande and Mr. Tarun Behl are Senior Management 
Personnel under Regulation 16(1)(d) of the SEBI Listing Regulations. Further, in addition, Mr. 
Sai Amrutkumar Vegisetti, Chief Information Officer has been included as a Senior 
Management Personnel and shall oversee the Information Technology function in the 
Company.

Mr. Rajesh Choudhary is a senior finance and business leader with over 29 years of 
professional experience, including more than 22 years in the alcoholic beverages (Alcobev) 
industry, primarily with Pernod Ricard India Private Limited (PRI). A Chartered Accountant 
by qualification, he brings strong expertise across P&L management, financial strategy, 
integrated business planning, transformation, risk management, fund raising, and 
governance.  
At PRI, he has held several leadership roles across finance and supply chain, including FP&A, 
pricing strategy, risk and assurance, marketing finance, and integrated business planning. 
In his role as Head – FP&A and Pricing Strategist at PRI, he was part of the leadership team 
managing a USD 3 billion P&L for the South Asia region, supporting strategic planning, 
performance management, and value creation initiatives.  
He has also led enterprise-wide transformation programs covering finance digitization, 
Anaplan-led IBP implementation, pricing optimization, and cross-functional alignment across

strengthening financial governance.
