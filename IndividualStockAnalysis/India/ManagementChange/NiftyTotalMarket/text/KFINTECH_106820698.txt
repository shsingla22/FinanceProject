The proposed role enhancement is expected to 
facilitate:   
• Centralized oversight of cybersecurity and 
information security risks across group 
entities;   
• Strengthened cyber risk management, 
cyber resilience and incident response 
capabilities;   
• Efficient utilization of cybersecurity 
expertise and resources across group 
entities;   
• Enhanced monitoring, reporting and 
management of information security 
matters at a group level; and   
• Improved compliance with applicable 
regulatory and governance requirements.   
As Group CISO, Mr. Srinivas Yadav Karri shall 
provide strategic leadership and oversight in 
relation to cybersecurity and information 
security matters across the Group and may, 
where required and subject to applicable 
approvals, discharge the responsibilit ies of an 
Effective CISO for relevant group entities in 
accordance with applicable laws, regulations and 
internal governance requirements.
