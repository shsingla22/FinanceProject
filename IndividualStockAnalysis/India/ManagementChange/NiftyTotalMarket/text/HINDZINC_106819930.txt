Hindustan Zinc has selected Rampura Agucha Mine (RAM) and
the Chanderiya Lead-Zinc Smelter (CLZS) as the priority sites
for ICMM Self‑Assessment validation as part of our first year of
ICMM‑aligned voluntary assurance, following our membership
in August 2025. Both sites represent critical strategic assets
for Hindustan Zinc. Rampura Agucha Mine (RAM) is the
world’s largest zinc mining operation and delivers 57% Metal
in Concentrate (MIC) – the highest among all Hindustan Zinc
mining units.
Chanderiya Lead-Zinc Smelter (CLZS) is Hindustan Zinc’s
largest smelting complex, with a total installed capacity of
195 kt of pyro-metallurgical (including Ausmelt) and 489.5 kt
of hydro-metallurgical processing. It contributes approximately
60% of the total metal production (zinc + lead + silver) across
Hindustan Zinc’s three smelting units.

ABOUT HINDUSTAN ZINC
Incorporated in 1966, Hindustan Zinc is
India’s largest and only integrated producer
of zinc and lead, and among the top silver
producers globally. Headquartered in Udaipur,
Rajasthan, our mining and smelting facilities are
strategically located across Rajasthan and in
Uttarakhand.
Over the years, we have established one of
the largest and most diversified portfolios,
strengthening our position as a global leader
in critical minerals and metals. We are now
striding towards Hindustan Zinc 2.0, a decisive
transformation into a larger, smarter, and more
diversified multi-metal enterprise, powered by a
2x capacity expansion programme and strategic
entry into rare earth elements, tungsten, and
potash. Through it all, we remain relentlessly
focused on driving sustainable development for
India and the world beyond.
