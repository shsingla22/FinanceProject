Notes : 1.
2a.

The Company has only one business segment namely "Cementitious Materials".
During the Year ended March 31, 2024, the Company (Holding Company), upon signing of a Share
Purchase and Shareholders Agreement (SPA) dated February 9, 2024, had acquired 85% equity stake
in Agrani Cement Private Limited (and its three wholly owned subsidiaries, together known as
"Trivikram Consortium") for ¥ 325.11 Crore. The Trivikram Consortium had earlier been awarded an
MDO Contract for three limestone mines in the State of Assam (approx. 335 MT reserves) by Assam

Mineral Development Corporation Limited (AMDCL).
During the year ended March 31, 2026, AMDCL cancelled the aforesaid MDO Contract on the ground
of non-compliance by the Trivikram Consortium for inducting the Company as an Equity Partner.

Consequently, during the Quarter/ Year ended March 31, 2026, the Company has recognized the
amount of 130 Crore paid as Claims Recoverable & written back the unpaid liability of Rs 195 Crore
as an Exceptional Item and derecognized the Investment of 325 Crore as an Exceptional Item in the
Standalone Financial Results. The Company has also de-recognized the corresponding Mining Rights
as stated above of

325 Crore in the Consolidated Financial Results of Quarter and Year ended March

31,2026.
2b.

During the Quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Arbitral Tribunal was constituted and Hon’ble

Delhi High Court directed that the application earlier filed by the Company with Delhi High Court
under Section 9 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 for securing the recovery of above
amount so paid be placed before the Arbitral Tribunal. The Company has filed its Statement of Claims
against the Seller (Respondents) before the Arbitral Tribunal for the aforesaid amount of ¥ 130 Crore
along with other claims & damages. The Arbitral Tribunal in its hearing held on September 8, 2026
has directed the Respondents

to file their Written Statement / Statement of Defence,

Replies and

Counter Claims to the Application on or before November 5, 2026 and Claimants may file their reply
to the Counter Claim and Rejoinder to the Statement of Defence on or before November 26, 2026. The

Arbitral Tribunal has listed the matter on January 6, 2027 for hearing.
Based on the Legal Opinion & Assessment of the terms of SPA, the Management is confident of
recovering the amount of T 130 Crore and the same is considered good for recovery.
The figures for the previous periods have been regrouped / rearranged wherever necessary.
The above results have been reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the Board of Directors
at their respective meetings held on November 04, 2026. The Auditors of the Company have carried
out a "Limited Review" of the same.

Industry Outlook

Cement demand is expected to improve during H2, supported by the onset of the peak construction season.
However, elevated petcoke prices and geopolitical uncertainties are likely to keep fuel, freight and packaging
costs under pressure.
While manufacturers are expected to pursue price increases, intense competition may restrict cost passthrough. Consequently, margins are likely to remain under pressure, with recovery dependent on improved
price realisations, input cost moderation and operational efficiencies.
