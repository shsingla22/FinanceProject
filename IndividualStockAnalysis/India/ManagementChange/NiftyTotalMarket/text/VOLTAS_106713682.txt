Dear Sir s, 
         
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para A of Part A of Schedule III of SEBI Listing 
Regulations, we hereby inform that Mr. Jogesh Jaitly, Head Sales, Unitary Products Business, 
who is a Senior Management Personnel of the Company, will retire from the services of the 
Company on  31st March , 2027 , upon attaining the age of superannuation.  Mr. Jaitly has had a 
decade plus long association with the Company and has made a significant contribution to the 
growth and development of the Room Air Conditioning business.  
 
As part of the Company’s leadership succession planning process,  Mr. Karan Sehgal  will join 
the Company on  3rd August , 2026 .  Following a structured transition and induction 
programme, Mr. Sehgal will assume responsibility as Head Sales , Unitary Products Business  
with effect from  1st October , 2026.  A brief profile of Mr. Sehgal is given in Annexure.  
 
Mr. Jaitly will continue in service until his retirement on 31st March , 2027 and will work 
closely with Mr. Sehgal to ensure an orderly and seamless transition of responsibilities.  
 
The information as required under Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI Listing 
Regulations read with relevant SEBI Circular s is enclosed in the Annexure.
