Dear Sir/Madam,
We wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Company, on the basis
of recommendation of Nomination and Remuneration Committee, vide its circular
resolution dated August 08, 2025, have approved the following changes in our
Senior Management Personnel ("SMP"):
(a) The appointments of Mr. Sarthak Sengupta as Vice President-Procurement

(with effect from August 18, 2025); Mr. Pankaj Bhandula as Executive, Vice
President-Land (with effect from August 18, 2025) and Mr. Harjinder
Kamboj, Vice President-Quality (with effect from August 18, 2025),
designated as Senior Management Personnels. They will be reporting to the
Chief Executive Officer.
(b) The resignations of Mr. Manish Jha (Chief Operating Officer, Renewable

Business) with effect from August 08, 2025 and Mr. Dinesh Chadha
(President, Renewable Energy) with effect from August 14, 2025. We
appreciate their significant contribution in advancing our strategic priorities
and strengthening the operations in their respective domains. Copies of
their resignation letters are enclosed as Annexure-II.

Mr. Sarthak Sengupta led negotiation and finalisation of large orders recently
placed by the Company for procurement of various key equipment including
battery energy storage system and modules. Mr. Sengupta leads Company’s
experienced procurement team. With a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering and an MBA
in Finance and Operations, Mr. Sengupta has nearly 20 years of experience in
strategic sourcing, market intelligence, procurement audit, and compliance.
Efficient procurement of critical equipment under his leadership is essential for the
timely completion of ACME’s ongoing projects. He brings with him prior experience
at Amplus Solar, TTL Ltd., Blue Star Ltd., Muniction Services, Hindustan Petroleum
Corp, and Graphite India Limited.
Mr. Pankaj Bhandula has played a key role in evaluating and ensuring timely
acquisition of land for ACME’s sizeable under-construction portfolio spanning
multiple states and covering solar, wind, FDRE, and battery projects. A Chartered
Accountant and ICWA (Int.), Mr. Bhandula brings over 30 years of experience in
strategic planning, land acquisition, profit centre management, and real estate
project management. His prior leadership roles include stints at Maruti Suzuki
India Limited, Lodha Group, Lavasa Corporation Limited, Reliance Retail Limited,
and The Wallace Flour Mills Co. Ltd.
Mr. Harjinder Kamboj has completed his PGDBA from Symbiosis and Diploma in
Mechanical Engineering. He has over 30 years of experience in Project Quality
Management,
