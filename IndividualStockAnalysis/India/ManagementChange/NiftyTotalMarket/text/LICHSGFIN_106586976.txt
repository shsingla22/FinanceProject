brief profile Born on July 15, 1969, in Srinagar, Shri Dayal hails from 
Shimla, Himachal Pradesh. He holds an MBA in Finance 
and a Ph.D. in Psychology. He is proficient in Punjabi, 
Hindi, and English. 
Shri Dayal began his professional career with Punjab 
National Bank before joining Life Insurance Corporation 
of India in 1992. Over a distinguished career spanning 
more than three decades with UC, he has held a wide 
range of leadership roles across operations, investments, 
credit, and administration. 
He has served as General Manager (Credit Appraisal) at 
UC Housing Finance Limited and as Deputy General 
Manager at UC Pension Fund. He was also on deputation 
to the National Insurance Academy. His field experience 
includes heading the Goa Division of UC as Senior 
Divisional Manager for three years, as well as leading 
multiple branch offices in Surat, Dehradun, and Mumbai 
in various managerial capacities. 
Internationally, Shri Dayal was posted as General 
Manager-in-Charge of Fiji Operations for three years, 
during which the operations received the Business 
Excellence Award (2017) from the Honourable President 
of Fiji. 
He has extensive expertise in investment management, 
having spent over 11 years in LIC's investment function 
across roles such as Chief Equity Dealer, Fund Manager, 
and Head of Project and Debt Sections.
