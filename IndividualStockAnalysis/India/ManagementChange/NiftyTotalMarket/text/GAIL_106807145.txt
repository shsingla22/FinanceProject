a) Brief Profile of Shri Rajeev Kuma r: 
 
Shri Rajeev Kumar is an Indian Statistical Service officer of 2006 batch. He is working in 
the position of Deputy Director General in the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. His 
responsibility in the Ministry inter -alia includes Project Monitoring in the Pet roleum sector, 
implementation of PM GatiShakti for Petroleum sector infrastructure, CSR related works 
of CPSEs under the Ministry, dissemination of Statistics on Petroleum and Natural Gas.  
 
Prior to joining this Ministry, he worked  in the Ministry of Statistics & Programme 
Implementation, Office of Registrar General & Census Commissioner under Ministry of 
Home Affairs and Directorate General of Commercial Intelligence & Statistics under 
Ministry of Commerce in various capacity. Amon g the previous assignments, his most 
notable contributions are in the base revision of GDP and National Accounts Statistics from 
2011 -12 to 2022 -23. He has been instrumental in the estimation of Private Final 
Consumption Expenditure, Financial sector, integration of Supply and Use Table 
framework resulting in improvement in the new series of GDP to capture the pulse of 
economy.  
 
Shri Rajeev Kumar as Director in Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation has 
also managed the implementation of Member of Parliament Local Area Development fund 
(MPLADS) scheme. In his prior assignments, he worked extensively in the collection and 
dissemination of official demographic data. He has been deeply involved in the Annual 
Health Survey (AHS) which is the pioneer exercise in bringing out estimates of vital 
statistics at district level. He has also worked on the cause of death statistics based on verbal 
autopsy. He has handled work related to dissemination of merchandise trade statistics of 
India.  
 
 
 

   
 
 
Shri Rajeev Kumar has varied experience of about two decades in the field of economic 
and social sector of the economy. He holds Master in Statistics from Delhi University.
