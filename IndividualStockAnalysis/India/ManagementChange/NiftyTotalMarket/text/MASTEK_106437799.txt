Mr. Deepak Kedia brings over two decades of
international finance leadership experience across

across APAC, EMEA, LATAM, and North America,

initiatives across P&L management, M&A, investor
relations, capital allocation, and digital finance
transformation. He has partnered closely with Boards
and senior leadership teams to drive enterprise

financial rigor in both multinational and high-growth
tech environments.
