3. Brief Profile Mr. Singhal is an EPC and infrastructure professional 
with over 38 years of experience in leadership, project 
execution, business development and P&L 
management. He has 16 years of international 
experience and has delivered projects and managed 
assets worth over US$3 billion across five countries. 
His experience spans airports, metro rail, urban 
transport, hydropower, bridges and other 
infrastructure projects. 
Mr. Singhal joins HCC from GMR Airports Ltd., 
Singapore, where he was working as CEO. He was 
responsible for global strategy, P&L management, 
business development and airport asset development 
across international markets. 
Prior to GMR Airports, Mr. Singhal held senior 
leadership positions with Tata Projects Ltd., GMR 
Group, Maldives and Alpine Bau GmbH, Singapore 
and Larsen & Toubro. At Tata Projects, he led the 
Urban Infrastructure business, growing its turnover 
from inception to over US$349 million, with an order 
backlog of over US$4 billion. At Alpine Bau, he led 
major underground metro projects in Singapore with 
a combined contract value exceeding US$1 billion.
