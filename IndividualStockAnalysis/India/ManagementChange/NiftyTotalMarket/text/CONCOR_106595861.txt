Shri Vivek Gupta
Shri Vivek Gupta is a 1997 Batch Indian Railway Accounts Service (IRAS) officer. 
He has a B.Tech. from IIT-BHU, Varanasi and MBA in Finance from BITS Pilani. 
Prior to joining Indian Railways, he has worked in ITI Ltd & Private Sector. He is 
having a distinguished experience of working in 5 Zonal Railways in various 
capacities in the field of Finance, IT, Business Development and General 
Administration including Additional DRM, lzzatnagar Division, Senior DFM at 
Morada bad, Jhansi, Agra and Kota Divisions of Indian Railways & FA&CAO in N.W. 
Railway and N.C. Railway. 
In his career spanning over 32 years, he has many achievements to his credit. To 
name a few -Implementation of TAMS -Traffic Accounts Management System 
in N.W. Railways. As BDU Head, developed First Indian Railway Freight Terminal 
as per PPP policy at Bhopatpur in 2022 through CWC. 
He has won many prestigious awards which include National Award for 
Outstanding Service (MR Award) in 2009 (the highest honour of Indian Railways) 
for integration of Crew Management System (CMS) with Payroll Accounting 
System (IPAS); GM Award for Best Innovation in 2015 for Conception and Design 
of "Railkarmi: Mobile application for W.C. Railway which was later adopted by 
CRIS as pan-India RESS platform. While working in DFCCIL, finalized refinancing 
deal of dollar-based World Bank Laon with IRFC, saving more than Rs.2700 crores 
of GOI Exchequer; 
Apart from excelling in professional responsibilities, he has been equally active 
in the field of sports like Table Tennis, Badminton, Marathon & Cycling.
