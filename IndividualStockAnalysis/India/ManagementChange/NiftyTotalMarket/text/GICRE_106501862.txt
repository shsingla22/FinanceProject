Brief Profile of Shri Sateesh Bhat  
 
 
Shri Sateesh N Bhat holds a B.  Tech degree in Electrical and Electronics from Mahatma Gandhi University,

Actuarial domains. In Actuarial, he has over 18 years of experience in retirements benefits and General
