Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we hereby 
report the following - 
 
1. Cessation of Shri Pradip Kumar Das, as Chairman & Managing Director of IREDA : 
 
Shri Pradip Kumar Das  has ceased to be Chairman & Managing Director of Indian Renewable Energy Development 
Agency Limited  w.e.f 01.07.2026 , upon attaining the age of superannuation on 30.06.2026.  
 
2. Additional Charge of Chairman & Managing Director , IREDA to Dr Bijay Kumar Mohanty, Director 
(Finance) : 
 
Pursuant to Ministry of New and Renewable Energy  Order No. 340-11/3/2019 dated 30.06.2026, Dr Bijay Kumar 
Mohanty, Director (Finance) , IREDA  has been entrusted the additional  charge of Chairman &  Managing Director, 
for a period of 3 (Three) months with effect from the date of assumption of the additional charge or till the 
appointment of a regular incumbent or until further orders, whichever is the earliest.  Accordingly, Dr Bijay Kumar 
Mohanty, Director (Finance) & Chief Financial Officer, IREDA has assume d the additional charge of Chairman & 
Managing Director , IREDA  on 01.07.2026
