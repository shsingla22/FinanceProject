Dear Sir/Madam,
With reference to the captioned subject, this is to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Company
at its Meeting held today i.e., Tuesday, November 4, 2025, based on the recommendation of the Nomination
and Remuneration Committee, inter alia, considered, noted and approved the following change in the Key
Managerial Personnel viz. Company Secretary and Compliance Officer of the Company: A. Retirement of Mr. Anil Malik (Membership No. A11197), Company Secretary and Compliance Officer of
the Company with effect from closure of business hours of November 30, 2025, pursuant to his retirement
and consequent cessation as the Company Secretary and Compliance Officer and also as the Key
Managerial Personnel and Senior Management Personnel of the Company.
Brief details of cessation as required under SEBI Master Circular are enclosed as Annexure A.
B. Appointment of Mr. Rajeev Agrawal (Membership No. A18877), as Company Secretary and Compliance
Officer and also as the Key Managerial Personnel and Senior Management Personnel of the Company with
effect from December 1, 2025.
Brief details of appointment as required under SEBI Master Circular are enclosed as Annexure B.
The Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today, placed on record its sincere gratitude to
Mr. Anil Malik for his outstanding service, exemplary professionalism, and valuable contributions during his
tenure of 32 years with the Aditya Birla Group. The Board conveyed its best wishes for his future endeavours.
This is for your information and record.
The above is being made available on the Company’s website i.e. www.abfrl.com
Thanking you,
For Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited
