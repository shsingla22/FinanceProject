Subject: Intimation pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (‘Listing Regulations’)
In compliance with Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Listing Regulations, this is to inform you that Ms.
Aruna Sundararajan, Independent Director of the Company, vide her letter dated October 12, 2025, received
through e-mail at 11:04 AM (IST), has tendered her resignation as the Independent Director of the Company, with
immediate effect, due to her recent appointment as a Government nominee on a regulatory body, and possible
consequential conflicts of interest. Consequently, she shall also ceases to be a member of the Audit Committee
and Chairperson of Stakeholders Relationship Committee of the company.
The Board of Directors of the Company place on record its appreciation for the valuable contribution and guidance
provided by Ms. Aruna Sundararajan during her association with the Company as an Independent Director.
Further, the details as required in terms of the Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular No.
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024, are enclosed as Annexure I and copy of the letter
of resignation received from Ms. Aruna Sundararajan as Annexure II.
This intimation is also being uploaded on Company’s website and can be accessed at www.infoedge.in.
You are requested to take the above on record.
