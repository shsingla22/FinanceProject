Annexure B – Brief Profile of Mr . Rajesh  Varrier  (DIN: 00105765)  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Mr. Rajesh is Cognizant’s President, Global Operations, and Chairman & Managing Director of Cognizant 
India. He leads the company’s software engineering group and operational model globally in addition to 
overseeing the company’s presence in India. Guiding  strategic direction across the company’s Software 
Engineering —along with operational strategy, processes, workforce sourcing and delivery excellence —
he defines how engineering capability is built, scaled and delivered to clients worldwide.  
  
As the CMD of Cognizant India, Rajesh provides regional leadership for Cognizant's India leadership council 
and India -based associates, with a strong emphasis on driving company transformation programs and 
representing the company as it executes its growth  plan across India.  
 
Mr. Rajesh has been an entrepreneur, chief information and digital officer and is a strong proponent of 
diversity and inclusion.   
 
Prior to joining Cognizant in September 2024, Mr. Rajesh served as EVP, Head of Global Services and 
Infosys Americas, as well as Global Head of Infosys’ Digital and Microsoft businesses. His previous roles at 
Infosys included SVP of Digital Experience and Microsoft business. Prior to that, Rajesh was CIO and Digital 
Officer for Aditya Birla Sun Life Insurance.  
 
Mr. Rajesh is a member of the NASSCOM executive council and a member of the board of directors of 
Cognizant Foundation India.  
 
Mr. Rajesh holds a bachelor’s degree in physics, and a postgraduate degree in computer engineering from 
the University of Mumbai.  
 
Mr. Rajesh Varrier is appointed as an Additional Director (Non – Executive Independent Director)  on the 
Board  of The Federal Bank Limited  for a  period of 3 consecutive  years with effect from  October 16 ,2026.
