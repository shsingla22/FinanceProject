Dear Sir/Madam,
With reference to the captioned subject, we would like to inform you that Mr. Rajesh
Choudhary, has, vide letter dated October 08, 2026, tendered his resignation from the
position of Chief Financial Officer of the Company with immediate effect in pursuit of
external career opportunities. Accordingly, he ceases to be a Key Managerial Personnel
(KMP) and Senior Management Personnel (SMP) of the Company with effect from the
close of business hours today. However, he shall continue to remain in employment of
the Company.
The Nomination & Remuneration Committee will identify and recommend to the
Board, a new Chief Financial Officer for appointment in due course of time. We further
hereby inform that upon recommendation of Nomination and Remuneration
Committee (“NRC”), the Board of Directors vide a circular resolution dated today i.e.
Thursday, October 08, 2026, has considered and approved inclusion of Mr. Anil
Singal, Deputy Chief Financial Officer as a Senior Management Personnel, to oversee
the Finance & Accounts (F&A) function with immediate effect.
The disclosures required under SEBI Listing Regulations in line with SEBI Circular
No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 are given
in "Annexure – A and Annexure – B” and the copy of the resignation letter received
from Mr. Rajesh Choudhary as “Annexure -C”.


This intimation shall also be available on the website of the Company www.tilind.com.
Kindly take the same on record.
