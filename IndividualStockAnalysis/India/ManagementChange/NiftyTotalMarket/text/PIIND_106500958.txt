ANNEXURE I  - Brief Profile of Ms. Kamali Rajesh  
 
Ms. Kamali Rajesh , age 52 years,  is a global Human Resources leader with over 25 years of 
experience across Asia, Europe, the US, and  Africa, partnering with senior business leaders 
to drive transformation, performance, and growth. Her career spans  strategic HR leadership 
roles across engineering, automotive, agritech, IT, and business services —delivering high  
impact  people strategies in complex, multi -business, and multicultural environments.   
 
Most recently, she served as Global Head of People & Organisation Development at 
Syngenta Group, a global agriscience leader . Her work contributed to meaningful 
improvements in engagement, inclusion, leadership effectiveness, and organizational agility 
at enterprise scale.  
 
Earlier in her career, Ms. Kamali Rajesh held senior HR leadership roles at Ford Motor 
Company, where she led HR across Asia Pacific and Africa. Across her career, she has been 
deeply involved in building leadership capability, shaping organization design, driving 
performance cultures, and enabli ng business -led transformation in complex, global 
environments.  
 
Having lived and worked in seven countries —India, China, Thailand, Singapore, Switzerland, 
New Zealand, and the  UAE Ms. Kamali Rajesh brings a global perspective, adaptability, and 
deep insight into how culture and leadership shape  business performance. She holds a 
Bachelor of Business Administration and a Master  of Arts in Social Work from Madras  
University . A certified executive coach and award -winning HR leader, she is passionate 
about building future  ready  organizations where people thrive and purpose drives impact.
