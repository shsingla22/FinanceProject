HCC FY26 Net Profit rises 142% to ~206 crore
Accelerated deleveraging continues with 38% YoY reduction in debt
Mumbai: May 14, 2026: HCC reported a standalone net profit of ~205.8 crore in FY26,

compared to ~84.9 crore in FY25, registering a sharp 142% YoY growth. During the year,
the company reduced its debt from ~3 . 197 crore to ~1 ,995 crore, reflecting continued
commitment to deleveraging, stronger operational performance, cost discipline and
sustained margins.

crore in 04 FY26 vs ~1 ,330.2 crore in 04 FY25.

-

increase) and net profit of ~44.6 crore in 04 FY26 vs net profit of ~228.4 crore in 04
FY25.

-

Standalone EBITDA margin at 18.2% in 04 FY26 and 16.1 % in FY26 vs 31% in 04
FY25 and 19.4% in FY25.

-

-
