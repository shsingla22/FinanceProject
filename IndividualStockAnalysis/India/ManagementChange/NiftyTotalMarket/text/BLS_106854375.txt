Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
We wish to inform you that Mr. Sarthak Behuria was reappointed for his second term as an Independent Director 
of the Company by the members of the Company in its meeting held on September 24, 2021 for a period of 5 years 
commencing from November 11, 2021 upto November 10, 2026 
 
In this regard, we hereby inform the Exchanges that pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of 
the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as 
amended, (‘Listing Regulations’), Mr. Sarthak Behuria shall cease to hold office as an Independent Director of the 
Company upon completion of his second term of five (5) consecutive years, with effect from the close of business 
hours on November 10, 2026. 
 
The disclosure pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with SEBI Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 is enclosed herewith as Annexure - A.  
 
Consequent to the Completion of the said second term of Mr. Behuria as an Independent Director of the Company, 
his membership in various committees of the Board are ceased to be exist. Composition of various statutory 
Committees of the Board, post cessation of office of Mr. Behuria are enclosed herewith as Annexure- B. 
 
The Company places on record its sincere appreciation and deep gratitude for the exceptional guidance, 
professional expertise, and valuable insights provided by Mr. Behuria during his association with the Company, 
which significantly contributed to the growth, governance, and success of the organization.
