Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)
Regulations, 2015, as amended (“SEBI Listing Regulations”) – Change in Director
This is further to our letter HZL/2025-26/SECY/60 dated July 25, 2025, and Regulation 30 read with
Schedule III, Part A, Para A of the SEBI Listing Regulations. The Company has received an Order dated
July 25, 2025 from the Ministry of Mines, Government of India informing the appointment of Mr.
Ashish Chatterjee (DIN-07688473) Additional Secretary & Financial Advisor (AS&FA), Ministry of Steel
(additional charge of M/o Coal, M/o Mines and M/o Minority Affairs) as part-time Official Director
on the Board of Hindustan Zinc Limited (HZL) in place of Ms. Nirupama Kotru (DIN: 09204338), AS&FA
(enclosed as Annexure - 1).
In view of above, and based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration
Committee, the Board of Directors of the Company through resolution passed by circulation on
Wednesday, July 30, 2025, at 04:00 PM IST have approved the appointment of Mr. Ashish Chatterjee
as Government Nominee Director (Non-Executive) with effect from July 25, 2025 and also noted the
cessation of Ms. Nirupama Kotru from the Board of the Company with effect from July 25, 2025.
Mr. Ashish Chatterjee is also inducted as a member of the Audit and Risk Management Committee
of the Board of Directors w.e.f. July 25, 2025.
The Board sincerely acknowledges and appreciates the valuable contributions made by Ms. Nirupama
Kotru during her tenure.
Mr. Ashish Chatterjee is not related with any of the Director(s) of the Company inter-se in terms of
Section 2(77) of the Companies Act, 2013 (the “Act”) read with Rule 4 of the Companies (Specification
of Definitions Details) Rules, 2014.
We hereby confirm that he is not debarred from holding the office of Director by virtue of any order
of SEBI/any other authority.
A brief profile of Mr. Ashish Chatterjee is provided below:
Mr. Ashish Chatterjee (DIN: 07688473), is a senior civil servant belonging to the 1999 batch of the
Indian Administrative Service (IAS), Tamil Nadu cadre. An alumnus of the Indian Institute of

Ministry of Steel, Government of India. Mr. Chatterjee is presently serving as Government Nominee
Director on the Boards of SAIL and NMDC.
