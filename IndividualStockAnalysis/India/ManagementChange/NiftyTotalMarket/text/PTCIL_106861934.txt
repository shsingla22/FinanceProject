MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Q2FY27

FROM THE CHAIRMAN

“Q2FY27 marked another quarter of strong execution for PTC Industries, with
healthy growth across revenue, profitability and margins.

The performance reflects the increasing contribution of our advanced materials and precision
manufacturing platform, while the progress at Aerolloy continues to strengthen our presence across
global aerospace and defence programmes.
Over the past several years, we have been building PTC ONE™ - our ‘From Melt to Mission™’ manufacturing
system with the belief that India must develop deeper, more integrated capability in critical aerospace
and defence manufacturing. The quarter and the subsequent period have advanced that journey on
three fronts. Our agreement with Airbus and the development order from Safran Aircraft Engines for the
LEAP programme take fully machined, ready-to-fit titanium castings from Aerolloy into the world’s leading
commercial aircraft and engine programmes. The ₹1,800 crore QIP, with significant participation from
global and domestic institutional investors, gives us the financial strength to pursue the next phase of
growth, both organically and through carefully considered strategic opportunities. And through STrIDE
Academy, we have begun to build the skilled workforce that manufacturing of this kind requires.
These are capabilities that take years to build, qualify and establish. The results of this half year show
them at work, with growth in income carried through to margins and to profit. Our purpose is unchanged:
to strengthen India’s sovereign capability in critical materials and components, to build a globally
relevant enterprise, and to create enduring value for all our stakeholders.”

Sachin Agarwal
CHAIRMAN & MANAGING DIRECTOR
