Sub.: Intimation regarding Change in Senior Management- (i) Appointment of Shri Raj

Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)
Regulations, 2015 [SEBI (LODR) Regulations], it is hereby informed that in terms of Ministry of
Railways Order No. 2016/E(O)II/39/4 dated 17th September, 2025 (Annexure-A), Shri Raj Kumar,
ITS (1997) has assumed the charge of Chief Vigilance Officer of IRCON, for an initial period of 03
years from the date of assumption of charge of the post i.e., 01st October, 2025 or until further
orders, whichever is the earlier. Accordingly, the abovementioned official shall be part of the Senior
Management Personnel of the Company from the date of assumption of charge of the post i.e., 01 st
October, 2025. Consequently, Shri Vivek Varshney has relinquished the additional charge of the
post of Chief Vigilance Officer w.e.f. 01st October, 2025.
The requisite details pertaining to the changes in the Senior Management Person of the Company,
pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular No.
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 11th November, 2024, are enclosed as Annexure-B.
