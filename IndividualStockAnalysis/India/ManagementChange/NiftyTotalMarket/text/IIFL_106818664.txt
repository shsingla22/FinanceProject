disclosed additional income of ₹2.3S crore in the return so furnished. The Company has received an

of the outstanding demand. Pursuant thereto, the Income-tax Department has granted stay of the demand,
subject to, inter alia, payment of 5% of the outstanding demand in instalments and compliance with other
procedural conditions. The Company has duly complied with the conditions stipulated in the stay order. The
Company has also filed an application seeking abeyance of the penalty proceedings and other applications
before the Income-tax authorities, which are presently pending. Management, after considering all available
records and facts, is of the view that there would not be any material adverse impact on the financial
position of the Company and hence no material adjustments are required to be made in the financial results
of the Company.
