PROFILE OF SHRI T.N. NATARAJAN, DIRECTOR (COMMERCIALS. SAIL
Shri T.N. Natarajan joined SAIL in 1993 as Management Trainee (Technical) at Bhilai
Steel Plant. He possesses over 32 years of diversified experience, with nearly a decade
each in Production, Logistics and Sales. His career spans multiple locations and
functions, with deep exposure to manufacturing operations, warehouse management,
marketing and customer engagement. ' -
In the year 2003, he was transferred to Central Marketing Organisation (CMC) at
Bangalore, Warehouse. In 2009, as Warehouse Manager, Chennai, he demonstrated
strong leadership by timely finalization of critical contracts, execution of developmental
infrastructure works, improving decoiling rate of TMT coils and securing ISO 14001
Environmental Certification- first ever for a SAIL Warehouse. Thereafter, as Branch
Manager in Trichy during the period 2014 to 2018, he focussed on market expansion and
servicing MSME clusters, thereby, resulting in increase in annual sales. During his tenure
as Key Account Manager in Chennai Branch of CMO from 2018 to 2022, he actively
engaged with the Key Account Customers to build long-term .strategic relationships; As
Regional Manager of Western Region from the year 2022, he demonstrated admirable
mentoring skills by placing special emphasis on enhancing the capabilities of the
Marketing Team through initiatives such as introduction of Monthly Knowledge Bytes
Presentation and quarterly In-house Journal; focussing on soft skills of his team through
communication workshops; assignment of market research projects to young executives;
promoting gender-neutral leadership development and building strong leadership pipeline
across branches in Western Region. As a result, these initiatives taken by Shfi Natarajan
in Western Region led to improvement in market share of SAIL across Chhattisgarh,
Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan; and have driven ,segmental growth in Fabrication,
Earthmoving Equipment, Pre-Engineered Buildings and Gil & Gas.
Shri .Natarajan has been instrumental in initiating a shift in SAIL's marketing approach
from commodity-based selling to solution-oriented engagement, including the
establishment of innovative customer-centric service models: Under his guidance, SAIL's
first Ready-to-Use TMT Service Centre has been established at Kalamboli. In addition to
this, he has actively contributed towards branding initiatives such as outdoor advertising;- ,
trade exhibitions; and dealer, architect & structural designer meets, while also
concentrating on marketing channel expansion through Tier-1 & Tier-2 Distributor
model.
During his tenure in Central Marketing Organisation, SAIL has achieved above-industry
growth for extended spell, strengthened SAIL's brand, expanded high-margin segments
and built high-performing teams. Apart from this,"Shri Natarajan's leadership has
contributed significantly to market share improvement, profitability -enhancement,
customer loyalty and talent development.
He is known for his strategic orientation and execution excellence, making him a key
contributor to SAIL's commercial growth and long-term value creation.
