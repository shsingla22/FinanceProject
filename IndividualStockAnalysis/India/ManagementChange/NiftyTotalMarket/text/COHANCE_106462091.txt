Profile of Mr. Gunjan 
Singh  Mr. Gunjan Singh joined the Company in May 2025 as Business Head – 
API plus. He is responsible for API and formulations portfolio. In this role, 
he drives strategic growth, customer alignment, and operational excellence 
across the platform.  
 
With over two decades of experience spanning APIs, Formulations, and 
CDMO, Gunjan brings deep expertise in business strategy, portfolio 
management, business development, and P&L leadership. Before joining 
Cohance, he was part of Dr. Reddy’s PSAI (Pharmaceu tical Services and 
Ingredients) business, where he managed operations across North America, 
South America, and Europe, and headed Product Management and 
Strategy.  His previous leadership roles include CEO of Wanbury, SBU 
Head for API Business at Jubilant P harmova, and Head of Sales & 
Marketing at Piramal Pharma for their Generic API, Nutrition, and ROW 
CDMO business. Earlier in his career, Gunjan worked as a Strategy 
Consultant with Accenture.  
 
Gunjan is an alumnus of IIT Delhi and SP Jain, Mumbai.  
  
Profile of Mr. Amrit 
Singh  Mr. Amrit Singh joined the Company in February, 2025 as Head – Specialty 
Chemicals. At Cohance Lifesciences, he is responsible for driving growth 
and scaling the Specialty Chemicals business unit.  
 
He brings over 20 years of leadership experience across leading 
organizations such as Navin Fluorine, SRF Ltd., UPL, and Unilever. In his 
most recent role as CEO of the Specialty Chemicals Business Unit at Navin 
Fluorine, he spearheaded capex -driven initia tives that delivered multifold 
growth in P&L and fostered strategic collaborations with innovators in 
AgChem and Performance Chemicals.  
 
Mr. Amrit holds a B. Tech. in Chemical Engineering (Gold Medalist) and 
an Executive MBA from IIM Calcutta. His accomplishments include being 
recognized as a High Potential Individual  at SRF and awarded the Best 
Debutant Award in 2013.  
  
Profile of Mr. Ravinatha 
Shetty  Mr. Ravinatha Shetty joined the Company in November, 2025 as Platform 
Chief Operating Officer. Mr. Ravinatha is overseeing S upply Chain 
Management , EHS, Ops Excellence, and Corporate & Industrial Affairs 
functions.  In this role, he works closely with leaders across the CDMO, 
API, and Specialty Chemicals divisions to drive synergy, alignment, and 
strategic progress.  At Cohance Lifesciences, he is responsible for 
strengthening operational excellence and accelerating organizational 
growth.  
 
With nearly 35 years of leadership experience across the pharmaceutical and specialty chemicals sectors, including Lupin, Watson Pharma, Zydus 
Cadila, Cipla, Dr. Reddy’s, and Aurobindo Pharma, Ravinatha brings deep 
expertise in operations leadership, site management, and cross- functional 
excellence.  
 
A Chemical Engineer with a degree in Strategic Management from IIM 
Kozhikode, Ravinatha combines strong technical depth with proven 
operational acumen.
