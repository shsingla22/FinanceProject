Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations

he worked for HDFC Bank and Corporation Bank. Rajesh is a commerce graduate from St. Xaviers College,
Ranchi and Post Graduate Diploma from XISS, Ranchi.

from the position of Chief Operations Officer of the Bank as he is relocating to Chennai due to his family
health issues. Given his request, the bank has agreed that his last working day will be with effect from the
close of business hours on November 17, 2025.
