4.

OBJECTS/PURPOSE OF THE TRUST '

4.1

The object and purpose for the establishment of the Trust are as follows:

(i)

To purchase or to otherwise acquire and hold investments including, in
particular, equity shares and other securities of the Settler, for the benefit
of the Beneficiaries, and such acquisition may be funded either out of the
corpus of the Trust or by way of interest free financial assistance or
provision of money in any form as maybe decided by the Settler, or in any
other manner or by way of loans availed by the Trust from other lenders
or in any combination thereof, in accordance with Applicable Law. The
Settler agrees to provide security in the form of guarantee, in accordance
with Applicable Law, in the event of loans availed of by the Trust from
other lenders;

(ii)

To operate one or more employee benefit scheme(s) for providing to the
Beneficiaries incentives/ motivation for better performance and retention
including, in particular, to operate employee benefit scheme(s), offering
right to acquire shares and other securities of the Settler held by the Trust
on such terms and conditions as are considered appropriate and in line with
the relevant scheme documents;

(iii)

To implement employee stock option(s) plans including Aditya Birla
Lifestyle Brands Limited Employee Stock Option and Performance Stock
Unit Scheme 2025, or other similar plans for providing share-based
benefits to the Beneficiaries;

(iv)

to repay the loans and other forms of financial assistance to the Settlor
from the funds available from exercise of stock options by Employees,
dividends or any other income of the trust;

(v)

To distribute the income and/or corpus amongst the Beneficiaries; and

(vi)

To sell, dispose of, transfer, assign, charge or pledge any properties
comprising the funds of the Trust, as permitted under the Applicable Law,
including to any individual or to another trust or any body corporate.
