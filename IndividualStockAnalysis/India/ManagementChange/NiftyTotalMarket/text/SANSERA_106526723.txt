On 21 November 2025, the Government of India notified the provisions of The Code on Wages, 2019, The Industrial Relations Code, 2020, The Code

on Social Security, 2020 and The Occupational Safety, Healt and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (collectively referred to as the “Labour Codes”),
which consolidate twenty-nine existing labour laws into a unified framework governing employee benetits during and after employment.
The Labour Codes, inter alia, introduce a uniform definition of wages and enhanced employee benefits, including changes relating to leave and gratuity.
The Group has assessed the financial impact of these changes, resulting in an increase in gratuity liability due to past service cost and an increase in
leave liability aggregating to Z 162.36 million.
