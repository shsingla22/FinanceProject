 Mr. Daniel Mobley (‘Dan’) was appointed as Global Corporate 
Relations Director and a s a member of Diageo Executive 
Management Team in June 2017. He is responsible for managing 
Diageo ’s reputation and positive impact on society, leading the 
government, communications and society teams across 180 
countries , where Diageo make s and sell s its brands.  
 
Dan is also a Board Member of the International Alliance for 
Responsible Drinking (IARD), which brings together the largest 
global alcohol companies to tackle misuse. He is the Grantee of 
the Royal Warrant of Appointment to Her Majesty The Queen 
held by Johnnie Walker and a Council Member of the Scotch 
Whisky Association.  
 
Prior to joining Diageo, Dan held a variety of roles in Corporate 
Relations at Standard Chartered in London, Mumbai and 
Johannesburg. He moved to private sector after eight years in UK 
Treasury, latterly as Private Secretary to the Permanent Secretary 
and had a brief spell with the Foreign Office in the UK 
Representation to the European Union in Brussels. He has also 
worked for a Trade Union, Environment and Development NGOs 
and the United Nations Association.  
 
Dan holds a degree in Philosophy and Politics and an M.Phil in 
International Relations, researching peacebuilding in Asia and 
Africa, both from the University of Oxford.
