Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 and 51 of SEBI LODR, this is to inform you that, the Board of
Directors of the Company at its meeting held on June 21,2026, has considered the following:
1.

Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved the
appointment of Mr. Ghanshyam Gupta as the Interim Chief Financial Officer, Key Managerial Personnel
(“KMP”) of the Company, with effect from June 22, 2026.

2.

Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved the
appointment of Mr. Punit Purushottam Agarwal as the Interim Chief Risk Officer, Senior Management
Personnel (“SMP”) of the Company for a period of one year, with effect from June 22, 2026.

3.

Taken note of, and accepted, the Resignation of Mr. Ghanshyam Rawat, President and Chief Financial
Officer and Mr. Ashutosh Atre, President and Chief Risk Officer from their respective positions, with
effect from September 21,2026.
