Subject: Resignation as an Independent Director of Ola Electric Mobility Limited. 
Dear Board Members, 
1 wish to inform you that, due to increasing professional commitments in my role as CEO 
and Founder at Mensa Brand Technologies Limited.l am no longer in a position to devote 
the time and level of engagement required to effectively discharge my duties as an 
Independent Director on the Board of Ola Electric Mobility Limited (“Company”). 1 believe 
itis in the bestinterest of the Company that I step aside and enablethe Board to appointan 
individual who can dedicate the requisite time and attention to the role. 
Accordingly, 1 hereby tender my resignation as an Independent Director of the Company, 
with effect from the end of the day on December 05, 202S. Consequent to this, I will also 
cease to be a member of the Stakeholders Relationship Committee of the Company. | 
hereby confirm that there are no other material reasons for my resignation other than 
those stated above. 
1 would like to express my sincere appreciation to the Board, management team, and 
colleagues for the support, collaboration, and trust extended to me during my tenure. It 
has been a privilege to serve on the Board and contribute to the Company’s journey 
during a pivotal phase of growth and transformation. 
1 remain supportive of the Company's mission and look forward to staying connected in 
other meaningful ways. I request that the Board kindly take my resignation on record and 
initiate the necessary statutory and procedural formalities. 
Wishing the Company continued success in all its future endeavours.
