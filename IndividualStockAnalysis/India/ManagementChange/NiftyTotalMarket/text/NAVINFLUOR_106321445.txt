Annexure B
Brief Profile of Mr. Amish Kamat:
Mr. Kamat brings with him a wealth of expertise with more than 25 years of experience in the field
of Environment, Health & Safety, majorly in Chemical and Pharma Industry. He was instrumental
in driving risk-based industrial hygiene systems, developing digital tools in risk assessment, and
implementing chemical exposure management and controls. He also led the Pharmaceutical
Supply Chain Initiative (PSCI) India Team, spearheading ESG capability-building initiatives for
supplier organizations.
He joins the Company from Bayer CropScience India, where he was associated for close to 10 years,
supporting global and regional business units across product supply, supply chain, and commercial
functions on occupational health and safety. Prior to this, he held key roles with Pidilite Industries,
Glenmark Generics, Cipla and Goodlass Nerolac Paints.
He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Chemical Engineering from Mumbai University and an Advanced
Diploma in Industrial Safety from the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education. He is a
Certified Industrial Hygienist (CIH) accredited by the American Board of Industrial Hygiene (ABIH).
Additionally, he is a Lead Auditor for ISO 14001, a Certified PSCI Auditor, and a certified trainer in
Behavioural safety.
