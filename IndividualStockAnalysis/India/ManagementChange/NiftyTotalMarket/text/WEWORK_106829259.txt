The bulk of our largest capex cycle is now live. We added 11.7k desks in Q2, our largest quarterly
addition, taking operational capacity to 145.3k desks across 85 centres in 8 cities, up 26.9% YoY.
First-half additions of 18.4k, with ~4.5k more desks opened in the first week of October, take us
past the 22k we set out in Q1. A further ~47k desks are secured through signed leases and LOIs,
32% beyond today’s capacity.
Demand kept pace with the largest openings in our history, across WeWork branded spaces and
managed offices. Members grew 32.5% YoY to 121.7k, ahead of 26.9% growth in capacity, and
portfolio occupancy rose 357 bps YoY to 83.7%. Mature centres reached 87.7%, up 355 bps,
and centres open less than a year are already at 64.8%, well above our 55.7% breakeven. The
114 bps QoQ dip is new capacity still filling.
Revenue grew 31.6% YoY and 10.1% QoQ to ₹768.7 Cr, and the platform is compounding on top of
the core. VAS + Digital grew 38% YoY to ₹108.8 Cr, ahead of 31.7% growth in core operations, with
Digital up 45.6%. Our order book runs well ahead of our commitments: ₹3,843 Cr of core revenue
is already locked in, up 66% YoY and 2.7 times the ₹1,421 Cr of rent committed against it.

Our mature base is absorbing the cost of growth and still widening margins. EBITDA rose 37.6%
YoY and 17.8% QoQ to ₹162.9 Cr, and margin expanded 138 bps QoQ to 21.2%, even as 25k desks
in centres open less than a year ramp up, already at 64.8% occupancy. Centre-level EBITDA grew

Growth is carrying through to the bottom line. PAT rose 67.4% YoY and 23.7% QoQ to ₹65.8 Cr,
with margin up 183 bps YoY to 8.6%, and first-half PAT more than doubled to ₹119.0 Cr. On a
reported IndAS basis, PAT was ₹20.5 Cr, from a ₹4.2 Cr loss in Q1.
Our model generates cash at scale, enough to fully fund our capex and leave a surplus. Over the
first half, we generated ₹379.8 Cr of free cash flow from operations (FCFO), up 156.6% YoY. That
paid for all ₹363.8 Cr of capex, our largest half of investment, and still left ₹16.0 Cr of free cash
flow to the firm (FCFF), from an outflow of ₹59.7 Cr a year ago. In Q2 alone, FCFO rose 149.2%
YoY to ₹237.7 Cr, 1.5× EBITDA, and we closed September with net cash of ₹23.1 Cr, from net
debt of ₹310.5 Cr a year ago.
Returns rose through our largest investment cycle. ROCE reached 34.5%, up 1,235 bps YoY and
590 bps QoQ, and 31.1% for the first half. Our credit rating moved from A− to A+ over the year,
and our cost of borrowing fell 184 bps to 8.5%. We are investing more, and earning more on
what we invest.
