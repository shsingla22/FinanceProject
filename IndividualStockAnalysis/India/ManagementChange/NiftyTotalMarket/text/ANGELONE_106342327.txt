Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015,

last working day will be October 31, 2025. Further, it is also informed that Mr. Sridhar Govardhan has been
appointed as the Group Chief Information Security Officer of the Company with effect from October 01,
2025.
