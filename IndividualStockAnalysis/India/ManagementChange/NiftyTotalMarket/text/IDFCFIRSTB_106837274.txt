3.  Brief profile  
(in case of appointment)  
  Hardik Thaker is currently with IDFC FIRST Bank as 
Deputy Chief Information Security Officer and 
leads the Bank’s cybersecurity transformation 
initiatives. He brings nearly two decades of 
experience across banking, financial services, 
regulatory institutions and global consulting, with 
expertise spanning cybersecurity transformation, AI, technology and cyber risk, cyber resilience and 
cloud security.  
 
Prior to joining IDFC FIRST Bank, he served as 
Executive Director, Enterprise Security & 
Resilience at GXS Bank, Singapore, where he 
contributed to building the security capabilities of 
its greenfield digital banks in Singapore and 
Malaysia. Previously, he  held a security leadership 
and architecture role with the Monetary Authority 
of Singapore, Singapore’s financial regulator, with 
responsibilities spanning critical information 
infrastructure protection and the design of 
security architecture for commercia l cloud 
adoption.  
 
Earlier in his career, he led IBM’s Security Strategy, 
Risk & Compliance practice across ASEAN, 
supporting organisations in strengthening their 
information security and risk capabilities. He has 
also worked with Deloitte and KPMG, advising 
clients on enhancing their cybersecurity maturity, 
including engagements associated with the 
Government of India’s National e -Governance 
Plan (NeGP).
