3 Brief Profile (in case of appointment) Mr. Varadarajan Muthuvenkataraman joined the 
Corporation in the year 1992 as an Assistant 
Administrative Officer. He is a graduate in 
Chemistry from the University of Madras and is 
also a Fellow of the Insurance Institute of India. 
He has also completed the Post Graduate 
Executive Programme conducted by Indian 
Institute of Management, Ahmedabad. 
 
In his career spanning more than three decades in 
the Corporation, he has worked in various 
capacities in both administrative and marketing 
wings at various offices in South Central Zone, 
South Zone and Central Office. 
 
He has held key leadership positions including 
Branch Manager and Senior Branch Manager in 
various branches in South Central Zone and 
South Zone and Divisional Manager (Pension 
and Group Schemes) in South Zone. He has 
worked as Manager (Sales) in Life Insurance 
Corporation (Singapore) Pte Ltd., a wholly 
owned subsidiary of the Corporation. He was 
Regional Manager of Direct Marketing and 
Micro Insurance departments and Secretary 
(Marketing / Senior Business Associate) at South 
Zone. He has also served as Secretary (Strategic 
Business Unit – International Operations) at 
Central Office. 
 
Prior to taking over charge as Additional 
Executive Director (Enterprise Risk 
Management), Central Office, Mumbai, he was 
working as Regional Manager, Southern 
Regional Office of LIC Housing Finance Ltd., 
Chennai, an Associate of the Corporation .
