PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT FOR THE ATTENTION OF THE PUBLIC SHAREHOLDERS OF APL APOLLO TUBES LIMITED UNDER
REGULATION 3(1) AND REGULATION 4 READ WITH REGULATIONS 13, 14 AND 15(1) OF THE SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD
OF INDIA (SUBSTANTIAL ACQUISITION OF SHARES AND TAKEOVERS) REGULATIONS, 2011 AND SUBSEQUENT AMENDMENTS
THERETO.
Open offer for acquisition of up to 7,28,06,387 (seven crores twenty eight lakhs six thousand three hundred and eighty seven) fully paid-up equity
shares having a face value of INR 2 (Indian Rupees Two) each (the “Equity Shares”) of APL Apollo Tubes Limited (“Target Company”), representing
26.00% (twenty six per cent.) of the Expanded Voting Share Capital (as defined below) from the Public Shareholders (as defined below) of the Target
Company, by Tata Steel Limited (“Acquirer”), pursuant to and in compliance with the requirements of the Securities and Exchange Board of India
(Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 and subsequent amendments thereto (the “SEBI (SAST) Regulations”) (the
“Offer”/ “Open Offer”). As on the date of this public announcement, no person is acting in concert with the Acquirer for the purpose of the Open
Offer.
This public announcement (“Public Announcement” or “PA”) is being issued by Kotak Mahindra Capital Company Limited, the manager to the Open Offer
(“Manager to the Open Offer”), for and on behalf of the Acquirer, to the Public Shareholders of the Target Company, as the Acquirer intends to acquire a
substantial number of equity shares, voting rights and control of the Target Company pursuant to and in compliance with Regulation 3(1) and Regulation 4 read
with Regulations 13, 14 and 15(1) and other applicable regulations of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations.

1.

Offer Details

1.1

Offer Size: The Acquirer hereby makes this Open Offer to the Public Shareholders to acquire up to 7,28,06,387 (seven crores twenty eight lakhs six
thousand three hundred and eighty seven) Equity Shares (“Offer Shares”), constituting 26.00% (twenty six per cent.) of the Expanded Voting Share
Capital (“Offer Size”), at a price of INR 2,301.40 (Indian Rupees two thousand three hundred and one and forty paise) per Offer Share aggregating to
a total consideration of INR 16755,66,19,041.80 (Indian Rupees sixteen thousand seven hundred fifty five crores sixty six lakhs nineteen thousand and
forty one and eighty paise) (assuming full acceptance), subject to, inter alia, the receipt of the Required Statutory Approvals, and the terms and
conditions mentioned in this Public Announcement and which are to be set out in the detailed public statement (“Detailed Public Statement”) and the

3


letter of offer (“Letter of Offer”) to be issued for the Open Offer in accordance with the SEBI (SAST) Regulations.
1.2

Price/ Consideration: The Equity Shares are frequently traded in terms of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations. The Open Offer is being made at a price of
INR 2,301.40 (Indian Rupees two thousand three hundred and one and forty paise) per Offer Share (the “Offer Price”), which is determined in
accordance with Regulations 8(1) and 8(2) of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations. Assuming full acceptance under the Open Offer, the total consideration
payable by the Acquirer under the Open Offer shall be INR 16755,66,19,041.80 (Indian Rupees sixteen thousand seven hundred fifty five crores sixty
six lakhs nineteen thousand and forty one and eighty paise) (“Maximum Consideration”). The Offer Price may be revised in accordance with the
SEBI (SAST) Regulations.

1.3

Mode of Payment: The Offer Price will be paid in cash by the Acquirer, in accordance with Regulation 9(1)(a) of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations, and
the terms and conditions mentioned in this Public Announcement and to be set out in the Detailed Public Statement and the Letter of Offer that are
proposed to be issued for the Open Offer in accordance with the SEBI (SAST) Regulations.

1.4

Type of Offer: This Open Offer is a mandatory open offer made by the Acquirer under Regulation 3(1) and Regulation 4 of the SEBI (SAST)
Regulations pursuant to the execution of the SPA for the acquisition of a substantial number of equity shares, voting rights, and control over the Target
Company. This Open Offer is not subject to any minimum level of acceptance in terms of Regulation 19 of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations. This Open
Offer is not a competing offer in terms of Regulation 20 of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations.

2.

Transaction which has triggered the open offer obligations (Underlying Transaction)

2.1

The Acquirer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated November 23, 2026 (the “SPA”) with S Gupta Holding Private Limited (“Seller”) and
the Target Company, pursuant to which the Acquirer has agreed to acquire from the Seller 7,38,60,062 (seven crores thirty eight lakhs sixty thousand
and sixty two) Equity Shares of the Target Company representing 26.38% (twenty six point three eight per cent.) of the Expanded Voting Share Capital,
in 3 (three) tranches in the manner set out below, subject to the terms and conditions of the SPA:
(i)

on the date of execution of the SPA, the Acquirer has agreed to acquire 2,77,38,075 (two crores seventy seven lakhs thirty eight thousand and
seventy five) Equity Shares of the Target Company representing approximately 9.91% (nine point nine one per cent.) of the Expanded Voting
Share Capital, through an on-market purchase by way of a block deal during the morning block deal window in accordance with the Block Deal
Circular (“Tranche 1 Acquisition Shares”), at a price of INR 2,100.00 (Indian Rupees two thousand one hundred) per Equity Share (“Tranche
1 Price”); and

(ii)

on the date of Tranche 2 Closing, the Acquirer has agreed to acquire 3,33,19,009 (three crores thirty three lakhs nineteen thousand and nine)
Equity Shares of the Target Company representing approximately 11.90% (eleven point nine zero per cent.) of the Expanded Voting Share
Capital by way of an off-market transfer (“Tranche 2 Acquisition Shares”) at a price of INR 2,100.00 (Indian Rupees two thousand one
hundred) per Equity Share (“Tranche 2 Price”); and

4


(iii)

on or prior to March 31, 2028, the Acquirer has agreed to acquire 1,28,02,978 (one crore twenty eight lakhs two thousand nine hundred seventy
eight) Equity Shares of the Target Company representing approximately 4.57% (four point five seven per cent.) of the Expanded Voting Share
Capital by way of an off-market transfer (“Tranche 3 Acquisition Shares”) at a price of INR 2,100.00 (Indian Rupees two thousand one
hundred) per Equity Share (“Tranche 3 Price”).

2.2

The SPA also sets forth the terms and conditions agreed amongst the Acquirer, the Seller and the Target Company, and their respective rights and
obligations in connection with the sale and purchase of the Acquisition Shares. The Tranche 2 Closing and Tranche 3 Closing are subject to the
satisfaction of certain conditions precedent under the SPA, including, but not limited to receipt of the Anti-Trust Approvals (as applicable).

2.3

The sale and purchase of the Tranche 1 Acquisition Shares shall be undertaken on the date of execution of the SPA in the manner described in paragraph
2.1 of this Public Announcement above. Upon settlement through the stock exchange settlement process, the Tranche 1 Acquisition Shares shall be
credited to a share escrow account in accordance with Regulation 22(2A) of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations, and subject to the satisfaction of the
conditions specified hereinafter, the Acquirer shall not exercise any voting rights in respect of the Tranche 1 Acquisition Shares. Such Tranche 1
Acquisition Shares shall be transferred to the Acquirer’s demat account, and the Acquirer may exercise voting rights on the Tranche 1 Acquisition
Shares, upon the earlier of: (i) the expiry of 21 (twenty one) Working Days from the date of the Detailed Public Statement, provided that the Maximum
Consideration is deposited in an escrow account in the form of cash or a bank guarantee in accordance with the requirements under Regulation 22(2)
of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations; or (ii) the expiry of the Offer Period.

2.4

The Acquirer has entered into an agreement to acquire voting rights in excess of 25% (twenty five per cent.) of the issued and paid-up equity share
capital of the Target Company as on the date of execution of the SPA. The Acquirer intends to acquire control over the Target Company pursuant to
the Underlying Transaction and the Open Offer in accordance with the SEBI (SAST) Regulations. Accordingly, this Open Offer is being made under
Regulations 3(1) and 4 of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations. Pursuant to the Open Offer and the Underlying Transaction, the Acquirer shall become a
promoter of the Target Company, including in accordance with the provisions of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended (“SEBI (LODR) Regulations”). Upon completion of acquisition of the Tranche 2
Acquisition Shares, the Seller, other promoters and other members of the promoter group shall cease to be in control of the Target Company and shall
cease to be a promoter and/ or members of the promoter group of the Target Company (as the case may be) in accordance with applicable law. Further,
the Seller, other promoters and other members of the promoter group intend to be reclassified from the ‘promoter’/ ‘promoter group’ category to the
‘public’ category of the Target Company with effect from, and immediately upon, the completion of acquisition of the Tranche 2 Acquisition Shares, in
accordance with the SEBI (LODR) Regulations.
