(7d) During the previous year ended March 31, 2026. the Company had reassessed its position in respect of recognition
of credit for its claim towards levy of Infrastructure Development Cess and Environment Cess on mineral bearing land
in the state of Chhattisgarh and the Company had filed petition in the matter before Hon'ble High Court of
Chhattisgarh since March 2007. The reassessment followed a judgment by the Hon'ble High Court of Chhattisgarh in
the similar matter, wherein the levy of these Cess was challenged and court had vide its judgement in WPT 263/2023
dated October 8. 2025, held that Cess cannot be levied or collected on mining leases as no land revenue is leviable
on mining leases. As a result. the Management supported by legal opinion, had assessed that it is entitled to get
refund of all such Cess amounts paid since inception of the levy w.e.f. May 27. 2005, pending adjudication of the
matter with Hon'ble High Court of Chhattisgarh. Pursuant to the above. the Company has outstanding recognised
receivable/ credit of z 143 Crore as at September 30, 2026 in the books, including z 134 Crore recognised during the
year ended March 31. 2026 which includes a credit of z 123 Crore disclosed under the "Exceptional item" pertaining
to the amount paid relating to earlier years up to March 31, 2025 in the standalone financials results.
Recently, the Government of India, vide Gazette notification dated August 17, 2026, enacted the Mines and Minerals
(Development and Regulation) Amendment Act, 2026 ("MMDR Amendment Act"), amending the Mines and Minerals
(Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 ("MMDR Act"), whereby refund of tax, cess or other levy already deposited
with or recovered by a State Government before commencement of the MMDR Amendment Act shall not be liable to
be refunded. However, the Management, based on the independent legal opinion obtained, has concluded that the
aforesaid amendment does not adversely affect the Company's entitlement to its claims in the pending petition and,
accordingly. continues to recognise its claim in the matter to be recoverable.
