Brief Profile of Dr. Krishnan : 
 
 
Dr. K.P. Krishnan is a former IAS officer with 37 years of distinguished service in public policy, economic 
governance, and regulatory reform across the Government of India, Government of Karnataka, and the World Bank. He has held key national leadership r oles, including Secretary, Ministry of Skill Development 
and Entrepreneurship; Additional/Special Secretary in the Ministries of Finance and Rural Development; 
and Secretary to the Prime Minister’s Economic Advisory Council. 
A respected academician, he has taught at IIM Bangalore, ISB, and Ashoka University, held the Bok 
Visiting Professorship at the University of Pennsylvania Law School, and served as IEPF Chair Professor 
at NCAER, New Delhi. He is currently a Distinguished F ellow at the Isaac Centre for Public Policy, Ashoka 
University. 
Dr. Krishnan serves on the boards and advisory councils of several leading corporates and non- profits, 
including Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Tata Consumer Products, Shriram Capital, ASREC India, Helios 
Trustee, Razorpay, and the Sanmar Group. He holds degrees in Economics (St. Stephen’s College), Law (Campus Law Centre, University of Delhi), and a Ph.D. in Economics (IIM Bangalore).
