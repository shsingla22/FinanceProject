Dear Sir(s}, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements} Regulations, 2015 [SEBI (LODR} Regulations, 2015], we would like to inform that Board of 
Directors on April 19, 2026 has approved the appointment of Mr. Vivek Shripad Joshi as Chief Executive 
Office ("CEO"}- LPS Domain (under the category of Senior Management Personnel of the Company} of the 
Company w.e.f. April 19, 2026. 
We further wish to submit that Mr. Kundan Kumar Jha who is currently the CEO-LPS Domain of the 
Company, for first 3 months after the appointment of Mr. Joshi, will front-end the business operations, 
while Mr. Joshi focuses on gaining deep understanding of LPS domain business operations & processes. 
Following this, Mr. Joshi will take over & start leading the LPS domain businesses and will have overall 
accountability for domain performance and delivery of agreed KPls, and Mr. Jha will continue to provide 
support to ensure a smooth handover & effective integration. This leadership transition between Mr. Joshi 
and Mr. Jha has been planned in a structured and collaborative manner, ensuring continuity and stability 
for the LPS Domain. 
Thereafter, Mr. Jha will continue to contribute in a strategic capacity, with his role and responsibilities 
aligned and mutually agreed, focusing on strategic areas.
