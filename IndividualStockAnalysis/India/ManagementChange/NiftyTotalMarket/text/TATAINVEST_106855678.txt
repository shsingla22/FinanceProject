Sub: Change in Directors - Outcome of the Board Meeting held today i.e. November 
11, 2026 
1. Retirement of Mr. Noel N. Tata as the Chairman: 
The Company has adopted the Tata Group Governance Guidelines, which, inter alia, 
prescribe the requirements related to Board Composition, Board Committees and the 
retirement of directors.
In accordance with the aforesaid Guidelines, Mr. Noel N. Tata (DIN: 00024713) will retire from 
the office of Chairman and Director of the Company with effect from the close of business 
hours on November 11, 2026. 
Mr. Tata has been on the Board of the Company since June 9, 2004, and was appointed as 
Chairman of the Company from June 24, 2010, and has provided exemplary leadership and 
strategic direction throughout his tenure. Under his stewardship, the Company has achieved 
significant milestones and delivered sustained growth and value for its shareholders. The 
Board of Directors places on record its deepest appreciation and profound gratitude for his 
invaluable contributions to the Company over the past 22 years. 
In recognition of his substantial and valuable contribution to the Company, the Board has 
appointed Mr. Noel N. Tata as Chairman Emeritus with effect from November 12, 2026. 
2. Appointment of Mr. Bahram Vakil as the Chairman: 
Consequent to the above, the Board has appointed Mr. Bahram Vakil (DIN: 00283980) as 
Chairman of the Company with effect from November 12, 2026. 
The details required under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, read with Schedule III 
thereto and the SEBI Master Circular dated 30th January 2026, are enclosed herewith.

Profile: 
Mr. Abizer Shabbir Diwanji 
Mr. Abizer Diwanji is a rank holder Chartered Accountant. He is the Founder of NeoStrat 
Advisors LLP, a firm focused on special situations advisory board engagements and 
learning initiatives. Prior to founding NeoStrat, he has been the head of financial services 
across KPMG & EY for 20 years. Mr. Diwanji joined EY as a head of financial services and 
later started the Restructuring business. During his professional journey, he was the COO 
of SBUT (a social cause project) with a focus on tenant management, operations, and 
government approvals. On the academic front, he teaches the Investment Banking Course 
at SP Jain Management Institute, and he is the Course Director for the PGIP course 
conducted by the Indian Institute of Corporate Affairs, Manesar. He is also a visiting faculty 
at IIM Ahmedabad and SIMSREE.
