Brief Profile (in case of appointment) Dr. Venkata Naresh Yallapragada is a seasoned Healthcare IT leader with over 23 years 
of experience at the intersection of clinical practice and technology strategy. A Clinician 
by training, he transitioned into Healthcare IT with an MSc from City University London 
and an Executive Management Program from IIM Calcutta - a rare combination that gives 
him Clinical credibility, Technology skills and expertise and business acumen. 
  
He has held Group CIO/CTO roles at 3 prominent Indian hospital groups — KIMS 
Hospitals, Rainbow Children's Medicare (both publicly listed) and latest being Maxivision 
Eye Hospitals where he lead digital transformation across 70+ ophthalmology centres. 
His track record spans HIS/EMR implementations, SAP ERP integrations, AI-driven 
CRM systems, cybersecurity infrastructure (Palo Alto, SOC/NOC), patient-facing mobile 
apps, and enterprise analytics platforms. 
  
Beyond execution, Dr. Naresh is a recognized thought leader - a published author, Past 
Chair of HL7 India, Past President of the Indian Association for Medical Informatics, and 
a HIMSS APAC member. He also holds Six Sigma Green Belt and ITIL V3 certifications.
