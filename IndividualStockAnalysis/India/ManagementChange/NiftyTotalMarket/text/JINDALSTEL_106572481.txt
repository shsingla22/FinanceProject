In terms of Regulation 30 of the S EBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (SEBI Listing Regulations), we wish to inform you that the Board of 
Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today , based on the recommendation of the 
Nomination and Remuneration Committee, has considered and approved : 
 
(a) the appointment  of Mr. Debojyoti Roy as Additional Director and Wholetime 
Director of the Company for a term of three years,  subject to the approval of the 
Shareholders ; and  
 (b) the re -appointment of Mr. Damodar Mittal as Wholetime Director of the Company, 
for a further term of three  years , subject to the approval of the Shareholders. 
 We also confirm that Mr. Debojyoti Roy and Mr. Damodar Mittal , are not debarred from 
holding the office of Director pursuant to any SEBI order or any other authority.  
 
We further wish to inform that, consequent to completion of his tenure, Mr. Sabyasachi Bandyopadhyay shall cease to be a Whole -time Director of the Company with effect from the 
close of business hours on March 27, 2026 and hence, has also stepped down from the 
Directorship of the Company.  
 The details required under the SEBI Listing Regulations, read with SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD- 2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024, with respect to the 
appointment of Mr. Debojyoti Roy and Mr. Damodar Mittal and in respect of Mr. Sabyasachi Bandyopa dhyay, are enclosed herewith as “Annexures A, B and C.” 
 
The Meeting commenced at 01:00 p.m.  and concluded at 02:15 p.m.
