Brief profile of Mr. Rohit Jawa  
                           
Mr. Rohit Jawa  has over 38 years of corporate and leadership experience in building, 
scaling, and transforming consumer businesses across India and international markets. 
He has extensive experience across India, South -East Asia, and North Asia, with 
expertise in emergi ng markets, international  growth, brand building, business 
transformation, digitalisation, premiumisation and organisational leadership.  
 
Mr. Jawa was appointed Whole -time Director of Hindustan Unilever Limited and 
President, Unilever South Asia in April 2023, and subsequently became Managing 
Director & Chief Executive Officer of Hindustan Unilever Limited in June 2023. He 
served as President, Unilever South Asia and Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer of Hindustan Unilever 
Limited until July 2025 and was also a member of the Unilever Leadership Executive, the company’s global 
executive leadership team.  
 
Immediately prior to returning to India, Mr. Jawa served as Chief of Transformation for Unilever in London, where 
he led a major end -to-end transfo rmation programme for the company. Earlier, as Executive Vice President, North 
Asia, and Chairman of Unilever China, he helped transform the China business into Unilever’s third -largest market 
globally, with a strategic focus on digitalisation, premiumisat ion and portfolio development. As Chairman and 
CEO of Unilever Philippines, he led the business as it became one of Unilever’s top ten markets globally.  
  
Mr. Jawa holds a bachelor’s degree from St. Stephen’s College and an MBA from the Faculty of Manageme nt 
Studies, both at the University of Delhi. He completed the Breakthrough Program for Senior Executives at IMD 
Business School, Lausanne, in 2018 and the Advanced Management Program at Harvard Business School in 2022.  
  
Alongside his executive responsibil ities, Mr. Jawa has held several industry and institutional roles. In India, he 
served as an Executive Member of the CII National Council and the FICCI Executive Council, and as an Executive 
Committee Member of Vikaasa – the UN SDG Accelerator in India.  
  
Earlier, he was the Founding Secretary of Food Industry Asia, Singapore, and Co -Chair of Grow Asia Philippines, 
alongside the Secretary of Agriculture of the Philippines, as part of a World Economic Forum initiative promoting 
inclusive business models in a griculture. He also served on the Consumer Goods Forum China Board, a global CEO -
led industry platform.  
  
Currently, Mr. Jawa does not hold any directorship or membership/chairmanship of Board Committees in any 
other company.
