5.  Shri Rakesh 
Sehgal  Shri Rakesh Sehgal, Executive Director (Civil) joined SJVN 
Limited on 21.01.1994 as Assistant Engineer (Civil) and presently 
serving as Head of Department, Civil and Hydromechanical 
Design, SJVN Limited, he is responsible for the design and 
engineering oversight of all under -construction and Survey & 
Investigation (S&I) stage Hydroelectric Projects (HEPs), as well as 
SJVN’s Solar HEPs in Gujarat.  
He has over 37 years of professional experience in the 
infrastructure and power sector, including more than 32 years of 
expertise in hydropower development across India and Nepal. His 
experience covers the entire lifecycle of hydropower projects from 
concept to commissioning including project planning, DPR/FSR 
studies, survey and investigation, statutory clearances, tender 
design, cost estimation, detailed engineering, construction support, 
crisis management, and commissioning. Since joining SJVN 
Limited in  1994, he has contributed to major hydropower projects 
including Nathpa Jhakri, Rampur, and Naitwar Mori in India, as 
well as Arun -3, Lower Arun, and Arun -4 HEPs in Nepal. Through 
these assignments, he possesses good experience in project 
management, design optimization, tendering, construction 
methodology, scheduling, technical due diligence, and stakeholder 
coordination with Government agencies, lenders, contractors, and 
financial institutions.  
He began SJVNs journey in Arunachal Pradesh as Head of Project 
for the 3,097 MW Etalin  HEP and later served as Regional Head 
for Arunachal Pradesh, overseeing hydropower projects with a 
cumulative capacity of approximately 5,000 MW. He successfully 
led one of the largest land acquisition processes for hydropower 
projects in the region and played a key role in securing critical 
forest and environmental clearances.  
During his tenure at SJVN, he has headed key functions including 
Civil Design, Pumped Storage Projects (PSP), Environment, 
Hydrology, Cost Engineering, Architecture and Consultancy. He 
has also served as Head of Design and Chief Executive Officer of 
SJVN Arun -3 Power Development Company (SAPDC) and SJVN 
Lower Arun Power Development Company (SLPDC) in Nepal .
