 Radico Khaitan Brings CFO to the Board; Elevates Next -Generation Leadership  
Dilip K. Banthiya appointed Whole -time Director, designated as Executive Director & CFO;  
Saket Somani named Deputy CFO  
New Delhi, October 27 , 2026: Radico Khaitan Limited, one of India’s largest homegrown alcobev  
companies, today announced the appointment of Dilip K. Banthiya, Chief Financial Officer, to its 
Board as Whole -time Director, designated as Executive Director & Chief Financial Officer, and the 
elevation of Saket Somani to Deputy Chief Financial Officer.  Both appointments are effective October 
28, 2026 . 
The appointments strengthen the Company’s governance and leadership at two levels. Bringing the 
CFO onto the Board adds his deep financial, capital allocation and alcobev  industry experience to the 
Company’s highest decision -making forum . At the management level, Saket’s elevation brings an 
expanded mandate across strategy, financial planning, investor relations, and information 
technology. This comes as Radico Khaitan enters its next phase of premium -led growth . 
Commenting on the appointments, Abhishek Khaitan, Managing Director, Radico Khaitan , said, 
“Earlier this year, we elevated leaders who have grown with Radico Khaitan. Today, we take that 
philosophy to the Board. Dilip has been central to our transformation, and his voice at the Board will 
sharpen our financial strategy and governance as we scale our premium portfolio and invest for the 
future. Saket brings a market -facing and technology -led approach that defines the next generation of 
financial leadership. Together, they give us experience at the top and depth beneath it, the foundation 
we need fo r the decade ahead .” 
The new structure creates a clear division of responsibility within the finance function. The Executive 
Director & CFO will provide Board -level oversight on the Company’s financial direction and 
governance. The Deputy CFO will lead financial planning and i nvestor engagement, and drive the 
wider digital transformation of the organisation. Together, the two roles strengthen a function that is 
central to the Company’s growth . 
Dilip K. Banthiya, Executive Director & CFO, Radico Khaitan , said, “It has been a privilege to be part 
of Radico Khaitan’s journey for nearly two decades, as it has evolved into a premium -led spirits 
company with an expanding global presence. I am grateful to the Board for its confidence. Over the 
years, we have invested s ignificantly in capacity, brands and capabilities while steadily strengthening 
our balance sheet. Going forward, our focus will be on disciplined capital allocation, robust 
governance and long -term value creation for all our stakeholders .” 
Dilip K. Banthiya brings over 35 years of experience across corporate finance, M&A, international fund 
raising, equity and equity -linked offerings, treasury and risk management, and corporate 
restructurings. A Fellow Member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, he has been 
associa ted with Radico Khaitan for over 18 years. Beyond finance, he brings a deep understanding of 
the alcobev  industry. During his tenure, he has led the Company’s finance function, strengthened 
governance frameworks, and guided the financing of each phase of its growth.  
Saket Somani, Deputy CFO, Radico Khaitan,  said, “I am grateful to the leadership for this 
opportunity. Over the past six years, I have had the privilege of working across the breadth of the 
finance function, from manufacturing finance and internal controls to operational performance and 
investor relation s. Our aim now is to make finance a true strategic partner to the business, using 
technology and data to enable better -informed decisions. I look forward to working closely with Mr. 
Banthiya and the leadership team to support Radico Khaitan’s growth and gl obal ambitions .” Annexure-B

 Saket Somani brings around 20 years of experience across corporate finance, strategy, investor
