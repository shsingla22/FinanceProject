The world our chemistry serves is changing faster than ever. Healthcare needs
are deepening, energy is becoming the new oil, semiconductors are powering a
revolution in our day-to-day lives, and climate expectations are rising across
every value chain. Chemistry for a Changing World is our story of how Acutaas
Chemicals Limited is shaping what comes next - with focus in our thinking,
discipline in our execution, and responsibility built into every molecule.
This report continues the annual GRI-aligned sustainability reporting we began with FY 2021-22 and follows our
Annual Sustainability Report for FY 2024-25. It covers our environmental, social and governance (ESG) performance for
the financial year 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026, and complements our Business Responsibility and Sustainability
Report (BRSR) for the same period. Financial performance is reported in our Annual Report 2025-26 and is not
repeated here.

Chemistry of Change

GHG emissions are calculated under the GHG Protocol Corporate
Standard using the operational control approach. Scope 2 emissions
use the Central Electricity Authority (CEA) grid emission factor for India,
and Scope 1 factors are taken from IPCC and DEFRA databases. Global
warming potentials are taken from the IPCC Sixth Assessment Report
(AR6). Energy is reported in gigajoules (GJ), water in kilolitres (KL), and
waste in metric tonnes (MT). Safety rates are calculated per one million
person-hours worked. Where actual data were not available, we used
reasonable estimates, and we say so where they appear.

We changed our name from Ami Organics Limited to Acutaas Chemicals Limited effective 15 May 2025. Indichem Inc., our
joint venture, opened an R&D centre and began building a plant in South Korea. We commissioned Phase I of the battery
chemicals plant at Jhagadia and an additional 5 MW of solar capacity. There were no other significant changes to our size,
structure, ownership or supply chain.
