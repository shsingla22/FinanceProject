BRIEF PROFILE

Shri Shailesh Kumar is a distinguished Central Secretariat Services Officer and an
academic achiever who topped his university in the undergraduate studies. With a rare
combination of technical acumen and financial expertise, he holds a Master's degree
in Physics along with an MBA in Finance, reflecting a robust interdisciplinary
foundation.
Beginning his professional journey in the Finance Division of the Ministry of Education,
Shri Kumar brought analytical precision and policy insight to the forefront of education
financing. He then transitioned to the Ministry of Civil Aviation, where he contributed
to the development and oversight of aviation security policy, playing a vital role in
national transportation safety.
His expertise was further called upon in the Ministry of Overseas Indian Affairs (now
merged with the Ministry of External Affairs), where he was involved in the
implementation of Emigration Policy, addressing the challenges and opportunities of
India's global diaspora.
Shri Kumar served as Deputy Director in the 14th Finance Commission. His tenure
there contributed to the shaping of fiscal federalism and intergovernmental financial
architecture in India.
Following this, he worked with the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) in the All
India Services (AIS) Division and the Special Cell, handling matters of national
administrative importance.
Thereafter in 2020 he joined the Ministry of Power as Deputy Secretary (Distribution).
In this capacity, he was nominated as the Government of India representative in the
Power Finance Corporation (PFC), contributing to reforms and financing strategies in
the power sector.
At present, Shri Shailesh Kumar is posted in the Ministry of Finance, Department of
Financial Services (DFS), where he oversees the Debt Recovery Division and the Legal
Cell. His work involves policy formulation and deliberation in strategy to strengthen
India's financial institutions and recovery frameworks.
A multidisciplinary thinker and experienced administrator, Shri Kumar exemplifies the
blend of intellectual rigor and practical governance, essential for public policy
leadership in India.
