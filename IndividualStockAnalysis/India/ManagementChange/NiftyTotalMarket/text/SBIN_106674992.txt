 Profile of Shri Sandeep Natwarlal Shah  
 
Shri Sandeep Natwarlal Shah is a Founder Partner of N A Shah Advisors LLP and a 
distinguished Chartered Accountant with over 40 years of professional experience. His 
expertise spans assurance, financial reporting, and corporate advisory services to a 
diverse range of industries including supporting intergenerational promoters in 
navigating succession and governance challenges.  
 
He has been engaged with Institute of Chartered Accountants of India in several 
capacity as member of Shield Panel formed by Research committee for ‘ICAI Awards 
for Excellence in Financial Reporting’, Panel of Technical Reviewer formed by Financial 
Reporti ng board, member of ICAI’s Expert Advisory Committee, Accounting Standard 
Board, Auditing  & Assurance  Standards Board, Ind AS Implementation Committee, Ind 
AS Technical Facilitation Group (ITFG) and many others. He has also contributed to 
several ICAI init iatives involving Guidance note and technical groups as also leading 
the outreach program conducted by International Accounting Standards Board (IASB) 
in India.  
 
He is committee member of Bombay Chartered Accountants’ Society’s ‘Corporate and 
Allied Laws’. He was part of a three -member committee working with SEBI on 
formulation of ‘ Operational guidelines for ‘Security and Covenant Monitoring’ using 
Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT)’ - a key initiative to enhance transparency and 
governance in the corporate debt market.  
 
He is a regular speaker at professional and industry forums, delivering lectures on a 
wide range of topics.  
 
He is currently on the executive boards of not -for-profit organizations like National Burns 
Centre, Somaiya Education Trust (affiliate trust), Independent director on board of 
Trützschler India Pvt. Ltd., a global leader in textile machinery.  
 
A voracious reader by nature and his hobbies include gardening.

 Profile of Dr. Sandhya Shekhar  
 
Dr. Sandhya Shekhar works across industry, academia and the start -up ecosystem. Her 
focus areas are business strategy, digital transformation, innovation, entrepreneurship 
and corporate governance. For over a decade, she has been serving as an independent 
director on the boards of several companies in large conglomerates and is an advisor for 
digital strategy. She is a member of the Apex Committee of BIRAC Bionest to help create 
a robust bio -tech innovation ecosystem nationally. She is on a few advisory boa rds, is a 
visiting faculty, a mentor for start -ups and women leaders  and is associated with industry 
forums.  
 
As the first CEO of IIT Madras Research Park (India’s first university research park), she 
helped create a vibrant innovation ecosystem. Earlier she worked as Director, Asia 
Pacific Consulting – Gartner Inc.; CTO, BConnectB.com; Head, Knowledge Management 
Research - Aptech; and Group Consultant – NIIT. She has worked in multiple industry 
domains, including evolving technology driven business strategies for large banks. She 
has served on Government of India policy committees and was a member of the IT 
Adviso ry Council for SBI.  
She is a recipient of the Distinguished Alumni Award from IIM Bangalore and is a 
University Rank holder in her under -graduation. Her PhD from IIT Madras won the global 
award for outstanding doctoral research from the European Foundation for Management 
Development. She is an author of several research papers and books (published by  
Springer, McGraw Hill, Macmillan etc) and is a sought -after speaker.

 Profile of Shri K.R. Ashok  
 
Shri K R Ashok is a former Executive Director of LIC of India. He joined LIC of India 
as a Direct Recruit Officer in 1989 and retired as Executive Director (Actuarial) in May 
2025. He possesses a Degree in Mathematics from the University of Madras.  
 
Over 36 years of service at LIC of India, Shri Ashok gained experience in various 
aspects of the Life Insurance business in India and abroad (UK). He worked across all 
operational areas in branch offices in India, including Sales, underwriting, accounting,  
customer service, and claims management. In the early years at the corporate office, 
he assisted the appointed actuary in valuing the life business. He also served as Head 
of the Group business actuarial team before he was selected to head the UK 
operatio ns of LIC in 2015. He held the regulatory -approved role of Head of the Third 
Country Branch in the UK, where he was responsible for implementing Solvency II in 
2016.  
 
On his return, he was promoted as Chief (Actuarial), and he headed the task force for 
the implementation of the new Actuarial IT system – Prophet before the IPO of LIC. 
Shri Ashok took charge as Executive Director (Actuarial) in April 2022, with 
responsibi lity for managing and developing the Prophet System and Shareholder 
reporting post -IPO.  
 
He was a member of the ALM committee and presented to the Board's Risk 
Management Committee on various actuarial risk -related matters.  
 
He has attended various training programs conducted by ISB and NIA. He has acquired 
a “Diploma in Actuarial Technique” from the Institute of Actuaries of India, certifications 
from the Insurance Institute of India, the Indian Institute of Quantitative Fina nce, and 
the Chartered Insurance Institute of the UK. He holds an International certificate in 
financial services risk management from the Institute of Risk Management, UK.

 Profile of Shri Khurshed Rustom Dordi  
 
Shri Khurshed Rustom Dordi is a seasoned board and enterprise leader with more 
than three decades of international experience across banking, financial services, 
governance, enterprise risk management, technology, operations and large -scale 
organisational transformation. His career spans leadership roles across India, Asia -
Pacific, Europe and the America s, working  within complex and highly regulated 
financial institutions.  
 
Shri Dordi spent over two decades with Deutsche Bank, where he held several senior 
leadership positions, including Managing Director and Group Chief Operating Officer, 
India . During his tenure, he played a key role in building and scaling India into one of 
the bank’s most  important  global franchises and contributed to the development of 
critical capabilities across corporate and investment banking, treasury, capital 
markets, risk, operations and technology.  
 
His experience encompasses enterprise leadership, governance, regulatory 
engagement, operational resilience, risk oversight, strategic execution, technology -
enabled transformation and institution building. He has worked closely with boards, 
international r egulators, global management teams and control functions on matters 
relating to growth, governance, risk management, organisational effectiveness and 
long-term value creation.  
 
Shri Dordi is recognised for his ability to bridge strategy and execution in complex 
operating environments. He has led large -scale transformation initiatives, 
strengthened governance and control frameworks, enhanced organisational 
effectiveness and helped  institutions navigate evolving regulatory, technological and 
market dynamics while maintaining a strong focus on resilience, accountability and 
stakeholder trust.  
 
In addition to his corporate and board experience, Shri Dordi is a certified executive 
coach in the Marshall Goldsmith methodology and a published author on leadership 
and organisational effectiveness.  
 
He brings to the SBI Board a distinctive combination of global financial services 
expertise, governance insight, enterprise leadership experience and transformation 
capability. His areas of expertise include financial services, enterprise risk 
management, operational resilience, technology and digital transformation, regulatory 
affairs, human capital development, succession planning and institutional 
stewardship.
