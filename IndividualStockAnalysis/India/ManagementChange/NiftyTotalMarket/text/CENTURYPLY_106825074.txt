Century Plyboards (India) Limited (“CenturyPly”,
“the Company”, “we” or “us”) presents its third
Sustainability Report, covering the period from 1
April 2025 to 31 March 2026. Prepared with reference to the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI)
Standards, the report provides an overview of our
approach, performance and progress across key
Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG)
priorities. It reﬂects our continued focus on
transparency, accountability and responsible
value creation.
The report covers all our manufacturing operations in India and presents our performance on
material ESG topics that are relevant to our business, operations and stakeholders. The disclosures consider applicable regulatory requirements, industry practices and the socio-economic
context of our operations, providing a balanced
view of our sustainability performance and areas of
continued focus.

We recognise that sustainable growth requires
responsible decisions across our operations and
value chain. Our sustainability priorities are integrated with our broader business strategy and
informed by business planning, risk management,
safety and environmental assessments, operational reviews and stakeholder engagement. Our
approach is guided by a simple principle,
reﬂecting our commitment to using resources
responsibly, strengthening our operations and
creating lasting value for our stakeholders.
As we continue to advance our sustainability
journey, we remain focused on improving our ESG
performance, strengthening responsible business
practices and building a resilient organisation that
creates long-term value for people, the environment and the business.

Reporting Boundary
The disclosures in this report are presented on a
consolidated basis, covering the Company and its
Indian subsidiaries. Overseas subsidiaries are
excluded from the reporting boundary.
The reporting boundary covers 16 national plants,
comprising manufacturing and service facilities
operated by the Company and its Indian subsidiaries, including facilities located at Joka (West
Bengal), Guwahati (Assam), Kandla (Gujarat),
Gummidipoondi (Tamil Nadu), Karnal (Haryana),
Hoshiarpur (Punjab), Roorkee (Uttarakhand), and
Badvel (Andhra Pradesh), as well as the Container
Freight Stations and Port Terminal at Kolkata (West

Bengal). The Company’s international subsidiaries
Century Gabon SUARL and Century Panels B.V. are
outside the reporting boundary.
In addition, the reporting boundary includes the
Head oﬃce in Kolkata and 26 branch oﬃces across
the country, supported by 41 warehouses and
stock points.
The entities covered within the reporting boundary
collectively account for more than 99% of the
Company’s consolidated revenue from operations
for FY 2025-26.

Century Plyboards (India) Limited is a leading
player in India’s interior infrastructure and
wood products industry, with a diverse portfolio spanning plywood, veneers, laminates,
MDF and particle board and allied solutions.
The Company has established a strong market
presence through its focus on quality, innovation, customer-centricity and product reliability, earning the trust of customers across India.
Founded by Sri Sanjay Agarwal and the late Sri
Hari Prasad Agarwal, with Sri Sajjan Bhajanka
playing a pivotal role in shaping the Company’s
journey, CenturyPly has evolved from an
entrepreneurial venture into a recognised

name in the interior wood products sector. Its
legacy is built on a culture of entrepreneurship,
disciplined execution and a commitment to
raising standards across its product portfolio.
Today, the Company is supported by a diverse
and experienced team of professionals who
bring together industry expertise, functional
knowledge and fresh perspectives. The
Company continues to invest in innovation,
technology and capabilities to address changing customer needs and contribute to the
evolution of India’s interior spaces, while
remaining anchored in the values of trust,
quality and excellence.

Our Operational Presence
The Company’s operations are supported by a
geographically diversiﬁed network that brings
manufacturing, logistics and market access
together. Headquartered in Kolkata, the
Company, at the Group level, operates 16 manufacturing and service facilities across India, with
a presence in West Bengal, Assam, Gujarat,
Tamil Nadu, Haryana, Punjab, Uttarakhand and
Andhra Pradesh. Manufacturing facilities in Joka,
Guwahati, Kandla, Gummidipoondi, Karnal and
Hoshiarpur form part of the parent entity, while
facilities at Roorkee and Badvel are operated
through subsidiaries.
