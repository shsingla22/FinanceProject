Subject: Change in Senior Management of the Company  
 
Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and 
amendments thereof, this is to inform you that  the below are the changes in the Senior 
Management of the Company: 
 
Shri Subhra Jyoti Mandal, GGM/ Civil has taken over charge as Additional Regional Head, 
East Coast Region Project Office (ECRPO), Bhubaneswar till 30.04.2026.  He will assume 
charge as Regional Head, ECRPO, Bhubaneswar w.e.f. 01.05.2026, post repatriation of 
Shri Anil Prakash, Regional Head, ECRPO, Bhubaneswar. 
 
Therefore, Shri Subhra Jyoti Mandal, GGM/ Civil, will be included in the Senior 
Management of the Company w.e.f. 01.05.2026 and Shri Anil Prakash, Regional Head, 
ECRPO, Bhubaneswar shall cease to be part of the Senior Management of the Company 
w.e.f. on 01.05.2026. 
 
In this regard, relevant disclosures pursuant to SEBI Circular are placed at Annexure-A . 
 
Kindly take this information on record and acknowledge the same.
