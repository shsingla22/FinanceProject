Dear Madam/ Sir,
In accordance with Regulation 30 and Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the Listing Regulations, we notify the following
senior management changes due to internal re-organisation. These changes will be effective from 1st January 2026, subject
to necessary approvals:
1. Mr. Vineet Singh, currently BEO - Nutrition, will lead the combined portfolio as BEO - Nutrition and Dairy. Consequently,
Mr. Manav Sahni, BEO - Dairy, will move to a new role within Nestlé.
2. Mr. Gopichandar Jagatheesan, currently BEO - Confectionery, will lead the combined portfolio as BEO - Confectionery
and Cereals. Mr. Varun Sethuraman, currently BEO - Cereals will move as Head - Marketing Communications.
Consequently, Mr. Chandan Mukherji will be stepping out as Head - Strategy and Marketing Communications.
3. Mr. Varun Gupta, currently Head - Supply Chain, will also lead Exports & Imports Business. Consequently,
Mr. Vasudevan Krishnan will be stepping out as Head - Exports & Imports Business.
4. Mr. Himmat Singh, currently Head - Corporate Affairs, will lead the combined portfolio of Corporate Affairs, Sustainability
& Societal Initiatives. Consequently, Ms. Taruna Saxena will be stepping out as Head - Sustainability and Societal
Initiatives.
5. Ms. Dhwani Rao, currently Associate General Counsel - Food, IP, HR, Digital Laws will assume as General Counsel
and Head - Legal & Compliance. Consequently, Mr. T S Venkateswaran will be stepping out as the General Counsel
and Head - Legal & Compliance.
