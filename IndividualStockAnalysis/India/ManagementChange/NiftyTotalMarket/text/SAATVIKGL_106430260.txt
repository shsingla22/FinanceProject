Mr. Vaibhav Bhardwaj (Chief Operating Officer)
Due to internal changes in role, designation and responsibilities

Designated as a Senior Management Personnel w.e.f. November 06, 2025

Mr. Vaibhav Bhardwaj brings with him over 28 years of global leadership
experience across manufacturing, engineering, and infrastructure sectors.
He has worked with leading organizations such as JSW Severfield
Structures Ltd. (JSW JV), Hero Motors Company, Havells India, AJF
(Hitachi Group), Ashok Leyland, Bajaj Auto, TVS Motor, and Asian Paints.
Throughout his career, Mr. Vaibhav Bhardwaj has successfully led largescale business operations and multi-location projects, managing teams of
over 7,000 employees. He has demonstrated excellence in P&L
management, Strategic planning, Operational transformation, and Business
expansion—turning around underperforming businesses into profitable
ventures. His key functional areas include business strategy and
development, greenfield and brownfield project execution, global
manufacturing operational excellence, ESG management, and people
development.
Mr. Vaibhav Bhardwaj is associated with Saatvik Green Energy Ltd as COO
– Solar Operation. Vaibhav holds an MTech (Mechanical Design), MS
(CMCA), and MDP (IIM Calcutta), and is a certified TPM Facilitator and
Executive Coach (Hitachi Chemical). With his strong business acumen and
people-centric leadership style, Vaibhav is driving Saatvik’s next phase of
sustainable growth and operational manufacturing excellence.

Mr. Manik Paul (Chief Information Officer)
Due to internal changes in role, designation and responsibilities

Designated as a Senior Management Personnel w.e.f. November 06, 2025

Mr. Manik Paul is a seasoned technology leader with over 30 years of global
and multi-industry experience in digital transformation, IT governance, and
enterprise automation. He has worked with reputed organizations such as
Vikram Solar, Century Plyboards, Thriveni Earth Movers, Orica Mining
Services and ICI India. He successfully lead the cross-functional teams
across India and the Asia-Pacific region.
Throughout his career, Mr. Paul has spearheaded multi-million-dollar
digital and business transformation programs that have enabled operational
excellence, innovation and sustainable growth. His areas of expertise span
digitalization strategy, ERP, cloud transformation, data analytics,
cybersecurity, IT infrastructure modernization and process automation. He
has also led global SAP and CRM rollouts, developed advanced analytics
dashboards and enhanced cybersecurity resilience through the
implementation of Zero-Trust frameworks.
Mr. Paul is an alumnus of NIT Jamshedpur and IIM Ahmedabad. He is a
recipient of the CIO 100 and Cyber Security Leadership awards. At Saatvik
Green Energy Limited, he serves as the Chief Information Officer –
Information Technology, where he continues to leverage technology as a
catalyst for innovation, efficiency and sustainable business performance
across the Saatvik Group.
