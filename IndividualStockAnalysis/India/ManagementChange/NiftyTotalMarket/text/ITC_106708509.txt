3.  Brief Profile  
 Mr. Hemant Bhargava ( Age : 6 7 years ) is an Independent Director of the 
Company since 20th December , 2021.  
 
Mr. Bhargava holds a Post Graduate Degree in Economics  from 
Lucknow University and has also completed Masters in  
Financial Management from Jamnalal Bajaj  Institute of Management 
Studies. He began his career in 1981 with the  Life Insurance Corporation 
of India (LIC)  as its youngest Direct Recruit Officer and rose through  the 
ranks to become the Chairman- in-charge and Managing Director of LIC 
in January 2019. In the same year,  he was also appointed as the  
Non-Executive Chairman of IDBI Bank Limited and LIC Housing  
Finance Limited.  During his career spanning 38 years, he has held a 
wide range of leadership roles both in India and overseas,  gaining 
extensive experience across Marketing,  International Operations and  
new business ventures.  He has successfully led teams across the 
financial  services spectrum, pioneered innovative marketing campaigns 
and launched new verticals & businesses for  LIC.  
 
Mr. Bhargava presently serves on the Boards of SMC Global  
Securities Limited, UGRO Capital Limited  and Wealth Company Asset 
Management Holdings Private Limited, amongst others.
