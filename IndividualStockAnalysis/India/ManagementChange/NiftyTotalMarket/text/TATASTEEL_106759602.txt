Annexure B  
Brief Profile  
 
Mr. Manish Garg  
 
 
 
Mr. Manish Garg joined Tata Steel as the Chief Information Officer (Designate)  in January 
2026 and has been in the role since  past 7 months. Mr. Garg  is being appointed as Chief 
Information Officer with effect from November 1, 2026.  
 
Mr. Garg has a strong track record in digital transformation, focusing on technology -driven 
innovation, data -led decisions, and sustainability. Early in his career, he lived and worked 
across the UK, Europe, and USA, leading cross -cultural global teams and  building expertise 
in delivery, product management, and consultative selling. From 2009 to 2019, he focused on 
expanding Tata Consultancy Services’s  banking and capital markets footprint in North America, 
Latin America and Europe, scaling teams and leading major integration and transformation 
programmes. Since 2020, he worked across multiple industries beyond BFSI, guiding teams , 
shaping digital strategy, and championing technology -driven growth and collaboration.  
Mr. Garg has close to 29 years of experience at Tata Consultancy Services  (‘TCS’) . He has 
partnered with clients across BFSI, manufacturing, retail, healthcare, energy, communication, 
and other industries, solving complex business challenges through technology and 
collaboration. He holds a B.E. from SGSITS Indore, an M.Tech. in Thermal  Sciences & Fluid 
Mechanics from IIT Kanpur, and an M.Tech . in Social & Behavioural Sciences from IIT Delhi. 
His work with business leaders and CIOs at leading global banks and major process industries 
has enabled him to adopt best -in-class management practices across strategy, governance, 
execution and stakehold er alignment. His holistic understanding of the evolving information 
technology landscape, combined with his leadership, planning, problem -solving and digital 
capabilities, makes him the most suitable successor for this position.
