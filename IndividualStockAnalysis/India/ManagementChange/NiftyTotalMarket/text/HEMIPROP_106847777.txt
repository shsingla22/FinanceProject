1. The above unaudited financial results for the quarter and half year ended on 30.09.2026, duly

reviewed and recommended by Audit Committee of Company and taken on record by the Board of
Directors at their meeting held on 05.11.2026.
2. The unaudited financial results for the quarter and half year ended on 30.09.2026 have been

subject to limited review by the Statutory Auditor of the Company.
3. The financial statements are prepared in compliance with Indian Accounting Standards, "IndAS" prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with relevant rules
thereunder and in terms of Regulaton 33 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (as amended) and SEBI circular dated 5th July 2016.
4. The Company issued a Request for Proposal (RFP) dated 18.02.2026 for sale of its land parcel
situated at Village Bopkhel, Pune, Maharashtra — 411031. Pursuant to the RFP, an e-auction
was successfully conducted on 12.05.2026, wherein Hypervault Al Data Center Limited

emetged as the successful bidder with a highest bid of ¥640.50 crore. Subsequent to obtaining
the requisite approval of the shareholders, the Company issued a Letter of Acceptance (LoA) to
Hypervault Al Data Center Limited.
The Company has received an amount of 2160.1250 crore (Rupees One Hundred Sixty Crore
Twelve Lakh Fifty Thousand Only) on 28.10.2026, representing 25% of the total sale

consideration, from the successful bidder, M/s HyperVault Al Data Center Limited. The
aforesaid amount has been received in accordance with the terms and conditions stipulated in
the Request for Proposal (RFP) document. The remaining sale consideration shall be received
in accordance with the stipulated terms and timelines of the RFP document and the sale deed
shall be executed on fulfilment of all applicable terms and conditions of the transaction.
5. During the year, the Company reclassified stamp duty of 10.20 crore, which had previously
been capitalised as part of the Investment Property. This amount is ateributable to the portion
of the Bopkhel land parcel that is held as Inventory. Accordingly, it has been derecognised
from Investment Property and recognised as part of the cost of Inventory (Stock-in-trade), in
accordance with Ind AS 2 - Inventories and Ind AS 40 — Investment Property

6. The Company is engaged in the business of Real Estate. In the context of Ind As 108 on
‘Segment Reporting’, the results are considered to constitute a single reportable primary/
business segment.
7. The provisions for Income Tax/ Deferred Tax is being assessed/ computed by the Company
annually and not on quarterly/ half-yearly basis in the Interim Financials (Unaudited Results).

8. Comparative figures have been regrouped/ re-casted/ rearranged wherever deemed necessary
to confirm to current period classification.
