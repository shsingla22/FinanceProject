Brief Profile:  
 
Mr. Gaurav Maheshwari is an experienced power sector professional with over 17 years 
of expertise in power markets, regulatory affairs, risk management, and policy 
advocacy. He holds a B.E. in Electrical Engineering from University of Rajasthan, MBA 
in Power M anagement from UPES (University of Petroleum and Energy Studies) 
Dehradun, Uttarakhand and LLB from Chaudhary Charan Singh University  (CCSU) 
Meerut, Uttar Pradesh.  
 
He is associated with the Company for more than 14 years. In his current role he is 
concurrently leading Regulatory Affairs, Risk & IEX Academy, navigating complex, 
multi -dimensional risks within most dynamic regulated market environments. He is 
steering Market Surveillance Committee, Risk As sessment & Management Committee 
and Enterprise Risk Management Committee since Oct 2023. He has been instrumental 
in designing and institutionalizing landmark market innovations, including the Real -
Time Market (RTM), Gr een Day Ahead Market (GDAM), Derivatives, and Cross -Border 
Power Trading, each demanding rigorous risk assessment and regulatory foresight.  
 
His earlier tenure at the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC) provided 
him with a strong foundational understanding of systemic risks across Generation, 
Transmission, and Distribution.  
 
 
 
 
Brief Profile:  
 
Mrs. Aparna Vyas Garg is a Corporate Communications and Investor Relations 
professional with over 13 years of experience across consulting, telecom, and energy 
sectors. She has worked with leading global organizations such as Deloitte, McKinsey 
& Company, and Bharti Airtel, building strong expertise at the intersection of strategy, 
communications, and capital markets.  
 
Currently, she serves as Vice President – Corporate Communications and Investor 
Relations of the Company . In this role, she leads corporate positioning and engagement 
with investors, analysts, media, regulators, and other key stakeholders. Her 
responsibilities include strategic communications, stakeholder management, marketing 
communications, crisis manageme nt, and corporate events, ensuring close alignment 
between business strategy and external perception.  
 
She holds a Bachelor’s degree in Economics from Lady Shri Ram College and also holds 
a PGDM in Finance with a minor in Marketing from IMT Ghaziabad.
