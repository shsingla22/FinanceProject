Dear Board Members,
Please accept this letter as my formal resignation from the position of Non-Executive Independent
Director of Gillette India Limited, effective
6th January 2026.
Due to personal reasons and increasing professional commitments, I find myself unable to devote the
time and attention required to discharge my duties towards the Company effectively. Therefore, I believe
it is appropriate for me to step down at this juncture.
In accordance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)
Regulations, 2015, read with Clause 7B of Part A of Schedule III of the Regulations, I hereby confirm that
there are no other material reasons for my resignation other than those provided above.

appreciation for the cooperation and support I have received from my fellow Board members and the
Management during my tenure.
I wish the Company continued success and growth in the future.
