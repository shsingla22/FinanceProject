c.   Brief profile  Shri Vinod Seshan , Joint Secretary, MoP&NG is an officer from the 2008 
batch of the Indian Administrative Service, Assam Cadre. 
He possesses over 20 years of rich work experience (17+ years in Policy 
making, implementation, Development and Infrastructure Project 
Management, District Administration and Crisis response in roles as 
Administrator, Team Leader and Project Director). He has served on the 
Boards of many government companies, the last two assignments being 
with OIL India and HPCL. Prior to being appointed to the Indian 
Administrative Service, Mr Seshan had been working in the software 
services sector in India with roles ranging from Software development, 
testing and Knowledge management. 
He possesses a Bachelor’s degree in Engineering (Electronics & 
Communication) from Visveswariah University in Karnataka. India. He 
also has Master’s degree in Public Policy as well as Infrastructure, 
Investments and Finance from the University College London. At work 
and in pursuit of knowledge, Mr Seshan has always felt his interests in 
large Infrastructure projects, their financing means and their 
sustainability.
