Pursuant to SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
read with SEBI Circular No. CIR/CFD/CMD/4/2015 dated 9th September, 2015 and in 
continuation of our intimation dated 11th February, 2026, we wish to inform that the 
Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today, i.e. 13th February, 2026, 
has approved the cessation of office of Mr. Kailash R. Lalpuria from the position of 
Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and Key Managerial Personnel (KMP) of the Company 
w.e.f. 13th February, 2026.  
 
This decision has been taken on account of his prolonged absence from office and his 
inability to discharge his duties due to health -related reasons.  
 
In the absence of Mr. Lalpuria his responsibilities have been effectively managed by 
the existing proficient team along with newly hired personnel.  
 
The Board sincerely appreciates the valuable contributions made by Mr . Kailash R. 
Lalpuria towards the growth and development of the Company during his tenure and 
conveys its best wishes to him for a speedy and complete  recovery.  
 
Further, the Board approved the appointment of Mr. Kamal Mitra, Whole -time Director, 
as a Key Managerial Personnel of the Company in terms of Section 203 of the 
Companies Act, 2013, effective 13th February, 2026.
