Brief profile  
 
Mr. Saurabh Anand Bhandari is a healthcare investor relations and strategy professional with over 
18 years of experience across investor relations, business intelligence, financial planning and 
analysis, M&A and hospital operations.  
 
Prior to joining the Company, he was Head – Investor Relations & Business Intelligence at Rainbow 
Children's Medicare Limited (Rainbow Children's Hospitals), where he worked from July 2019. He 
was part of the core team for its initial public offering, cont ributing to the industry report, the 
business section of the prospectus and investor roadshows. Post listing, he led investor 
engagement alongside the Chairman and CFO, and led annual budgeting, long -range planning and 
the evaluation and execution of acqui sitions in Warangal and Guwahati.  
 
Earlier, he worked with Fortis Healthcare Limited as Regional Business Analyst for the South and 
West regions, with Rainbow Children's Hospitals (2016 –2018), where he set up the business 
analytics function, and with CARE Hospitals, where he led operational  transformation projects. 
He began his career in technology consulting with Mahindra Satyam and Infosys.  
 
Mr. Bhandari holds a bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering  from Visvesvaraya 
Technological University and an MBA in Finance from the Institute of Management, Nirma 
University. He has also cleared Level I of the CFA Program.
