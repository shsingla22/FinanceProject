From Telecom to Banking: Wisely.ai Scales Scam Protection
After protecting over 100 million users in Indonesia, Tanla’s Wisely.ai has protected
21 million customers of one of India’s top-5 private-sector banks, blocking 91 million
scam messages in FY26.

Introduction

A leading private-sector bank, among India’s top-5 by market capitalization, serves around 55 million
customers through a pan-India retail, corporate and SME franchise. Its scale made it a prime target for
impersonation. In FY26, the bank deployed Tanla’s Wisely.ai to stop impersonation scams before they reached
customers.

The Challenge

Scammers impersonated the bank at scale, using spoofed sender IDs to send fake cashback offers, loan
approvals and reward-point redemptions by SMS. These messages routed customers to look-alike phishing
pages built to harvest banking credentials. Every scam cost customers money and trust, and eroded the
bank’s brand equity.

7


The Solution: Detect → Drop → Take Down
Wisely.ai is a real-time, AI-native anti-scam and smishing protection platform that sits at the messaging layer:

Detect: Every inbound SMS and message analyzed in under 50ms, combining
reputation signals, semantic analysis and call-to-action detection for suspicious
URLs, mobile numbers and email IDs.
Drop: Scam and impersonation messages dropped before they reach the
customer, removing the need for customer vigilance as the first line of defense.
Take down: Impersonation URLs and fraudulent sender accounts taken down
through partnerships with iZOOlogic, Google and Meta (WhatsApp), typically
within hours, 24 hours at most.

Can you summarise overall
financial performance in Q2

We delivered double-digit YoY growth across all our
key financial metrics, with revenue, gross profit and
EBITDA growing by more than 20%. More importantly, the
quarter reflects the strength of our underlying business,
with growth in existing customers continuing to drive
performance.
Revenue grew by 10.2% QoQ and 25.3% YoY to ₹13,517
Mn. Of the 10.2% QoQ revenue growth, 7.9% came from
our existing business, while the inorganic contribution
was 2.3%. The quarter also benefited from one-time
campaigns from select customers. Our OTT contribution
to overall revenue is at 36.2% in Q2 FY27.
Gross profit grew by 8.2% QoQ and 23.1% YoY to ₹3,531
Mn, with a gross margin of 26.1%. Gross profit grew by
₹269 Mn QoQ and ₹662 Mn YoY due to revenue growth,
partially offset by a 48bps contraction in gross margin
in both periods. Sequential gross margin contracted by
48 bps due to a change in revenue mix, with a higher
contribution from the Enterprise business.
EBITDA grew by 6.6% QoQ and 20.8% YoY to ₹2,143
Mn, with an EBITDA margin of 15.9%. Sequential EBITDA
increased by ₹132 Mn, driven by higher gross profit of
₹269 Mn and offset by higher indirect costs of ₹137 Mn, of
which ₹60 Mn relates to the newly acquired VF overseas
business. YoY EBITDA increased by ₹369 Mn, driven by
higher gross profit and offset by higher indirect expenses.
PAT grew 3.0% QoQ and 17.1% YoY to ₹1,464 Mn.
Sequential and YoY growth was driven by higher EBITDA,
partly offset by higher depreciation and amortisation,
including amortisation of intangible assets recognised on
acquisition, and lower other income.
We generated ₹2,316 Mn in free cash flow (158% of PAT),
during the quarter and closed with ₹14,474 Mn in cash
and zero debt.

Q6

What drove the increase in
indirect expenses, and how
did it impact EBITDA growth?

Indirect expenses increased by ₹137 Mn QoQ,
including ₹60 Mn attributable to ValueFirst’s overseas
operations. The remaining ₹77 Mn increase was
primarily driven by higher employee costs, marketing
spends, travel and other general expenses, partly
offset by lower forex costs. Marketing spends are
typically higher in Q2/Q3 each year, driven by
participation in key industry events such as Global
Fintech Festival (GFF) and India Mobile Congress
(IMC).
Indirect costs increased by 26.9% YoY, while costs for
the existing business grew by 21.3%, primarily due to
higher spends on employee cost, marketing, travel,
professional and legal spends. Employee costs
increased by 14.5% YoY, due to annual increments and
net additions. Disciplined investments in Innovation
and GTM team will continue.
EBITDA grew 6.6% QoQ and 20.8% YoY to ₹2,143 Mn.
EBITDA growth was impacted by the consolidation of
ValueFirst’s international operations, which currently
operate at lower profitability levels. We remain
focused on improving operating efficiency, business
mix and profitability as we scale.

Q8

What explains the PAT
performance in Q2 FY27?

PAT grew 3.0% QoQ and 17.1% YoY to ₹1,464 Mn in Q2
FY27. Sequential PAT growth was driven by higher
EBITDA, partly offset by higher depreciation and
amortisation, and lower other income. Other income
decreased by ₹20 Mn QoQ, as Q1 FY27 included onetime interest income of ₹34 Mn on income tax refunds.
ETR was 21.8%.
