BRIEF PROFILE OF MR. RAJESH KES WANI  
 
 
Mr. Rajesh Keswani holds a Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, a Master of Business 
Administration, and is a BEE -certified Energy Manager and Auditor. He has more than 30 years 
of experience in Cement, T hermal and Solar power plants.  
 
 
BRIEF PROFILE OF MR. RAJPAL SINGH SHEKHWAT  
 
 
Mr. Rajpal Singh Shekhawat is a seasoned cement industry professional with over 23 years of 
experience in cement manufacturing, plant operations  and project management. He holds a 
Bachelor’s degree in Chemical Engineering from MNIT Jaipur and a Master’s degree in 
Manufacturing Management from BITS Pilani. He brings in  strong expertise in process 
optimization, brownfield capacity enhancement, alternative fuel utilization (AFR)  and 
sustainability -focused initiatives. He has successfully led several high -impact projects in energy 
efficiency, AFR integration  and sustainable manufacturing, driving long -term value creation and 
advancing ESG goals in the cement sector.  
 
 
 
BRIEF PROFILE OF MR. SANJEEVA KR. JHA 
 
 
Mr. Sanjeeva Kr. Jha is a postgraduate in Economics with specialization in Rural Development 
and holds an MBA in Operations Management. With over 25 years of professional experience in 
CSR and Sustainability, he has led several high -impact projects. He is an IICA -certified ESG 
Professional (Impact Leader), a CII -certified Sustainability Assessor  and a member of the National 
Sustainability Committee of the Indian Chambers of Commerce as well as the Decarbonisation 
Committee of the Cement Manufacturers’ Association.
