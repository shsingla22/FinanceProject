Annexure – A
1. Mr. Suprakash Chaudhuri will cease to be the Head of the Company’s Digital Industries
business, effective close of business hours of 30th September 2025 as he is taking up a new
role within Siemens overseas.
2. Mr. Swapnil Deosthali is appointed as the Head of the Company’s Digital Industries business
effective 1st October 2025. Brief profile of Mr. Deosthali is as under:
Swapnil Deosthali is a seasoned professional currently serving as the Country Business Unit
Head within the Company’s Digital Industries business. He brings nearly 30 years of
experience in Sales, Business Development, and Profit & Loss (P&L) Management, having
held various leadership roles across multiple Siemens businesses.
His career includes Leadership positions across geographies such as India, ASEAN, and
Europe, reflective of his global business acumen and a strong foundation in strategic planning,
and general management.
Mr. Deosthali is a qualified Bachelor of Engineering from Government College of Engineering
Pune.
