b) Date of change and term Effective date of appointment: effective 9th April 2026. 
Term: Full-time employment 
 
c) Brief profile  
(in case of appointment) Mr. Baneswar Banerjee, a postgraduate in Sales & Marketing 
from IIM Indore and a BE in Mechanical Engineering from the 
Bhilai Institute of Technology, brings over 25 years of extensive 
experience in the automotive industry, including more than two 
decades of experience with Mahindra & Mahindra Limited 
(“Mahindra”). During his tenure, he has led high-impact roles 
across sales, customer care, customer experience, product 
launches, strategic partnerships, and business development. 
 
He has played a pivotal role in Mahindra’s auto journey, 
contributing to its emergence as the number two PV player and 
the leader in UV revenue market share. He has also driven 
innovation in digital transformation and successfully led 
cross-functional teams to deliver on revenue, NVM, and 
operational objectives. Mr. Banerjee has spearheaded several 
successful product launches for Mahindra, including BEV (9E and 
6E), Thar, XUV700, Scorpio N, 3XO, MAXX PUP, and Jeeto.
