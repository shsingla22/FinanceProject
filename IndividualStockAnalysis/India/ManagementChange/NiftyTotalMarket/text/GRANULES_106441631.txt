Brief Profile - Mr. Vinodkumar Parur
Mr. Vinodkumar Parur has over 24 years of experience in human resources leadership across
multiple sectors, including manufacturing, engineering, pharmaceuticals and_ infrastructure.
Mr. Parur has been involved in HR function setup for IPO preparation, mergers and acquisitions,
implementation of HRMS systems, leadership development and employee engagement programs.
His previous senior roles include Chief Human Resources Officer at RR Kabel Limited &
Nilkamal Limited and senior HR leadership roles at A.T.E. Group & Schindler India.
He holds a master’s degree in management studies (Human Resources) from PIMSR, Mumbai

University, and a bachelor’s degree in commerce and economics from NMIMS, Mumbai

University. He is certified in several HR assessment and training programs and is a member of
advisory and academic boards.
