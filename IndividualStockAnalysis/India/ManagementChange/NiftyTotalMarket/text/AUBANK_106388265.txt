We hereby inform that consequent to the untimely and sudden demise of Late Shri Vimal Jain,
Chief Financial Officer of AU Small Finance Bank (“the Bank”) as intimated vide our letter dated
September 10, 2025, the Board of Directors of the Bank at its meeting held today i.e.
September 26, 2025, considered and appointed Mr. Gaurav Jain as Interim Chief Financial Officer
and Key Managerial Personnel of the Bank, with effect from Friday, September 26, 2025, till
further update in this regard.
Mr. Gaurav Jain, a rank holder Chartered Accountant and MBA graduate from the Kellogg School
of Management, has over 23 years of expertise in banking and financial services. He currently
holds the position of President - Finance & Strategy and is associated with Bank since January
2021.
