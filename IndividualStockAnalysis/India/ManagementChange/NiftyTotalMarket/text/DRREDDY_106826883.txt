Brief Profile of Mr. Akhil Ravi  
 
 
 
Akhil Ravi brings nearly 19 years of experience spanning strategy, commercial leadership, business 
transformation and general management. Since joining Dr. Reddy's Laboratories in 2018, he has held leadership roles across strategy, commercial operations and business leadership, and currently serves as CEO of Aurigene Pharmaceutical Services Ltd. (APSL) , a wholly owned subsidiary of Dr. Reddy’s 
Laboratories . In this role, he has led APSL's evolution from a start -up-style business into a rapidly scaling 
CRDMO, driving significant growth, profitability and capability expansion. 
Since joining Dr. Reddy’s, Akhil has led the transformation of APSL through a multi -year agenda focused 
on strengthening operating foundations, accelerating growth, improving profitability and enhancing 
customer service. Under his leadership, the business is on track and on growth trajectory . He has also driven 
a significant improvement in service levels across the CDMO business, strengthening both operational performance and customer confidence.  
Akhil has been instrumental in advancing APSL's innovation and technology capabilities. He led the implementation of Aurigene.ai , an AI -enabled integrated drug discovery workflow that now supports 
approximately 30% of the drug discovery services business. He has also overseen the expansion of capabilities across drug discovery, antibodies, biologics, APIs and peptides, while laying the foundation for growth in the drug product CDMO business through strategic partnerships. 
  

 
 
Before joining Dr. Reddy's, Akhil held sales and transformation roles at McKinsey & Company and ITC  
and also pursued entrepreneurial ventures focused on digital sports and health solutions.  
Akhil has done Post -Graduate Programme in Management  from Indian School of Business (ISB) and 
graduated in Bachelor of Technology, Chemical Engineering from Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), 
Bombay.
