Brief Profile  Shri Rakesh Magotra  is a seasoned banking professional with over 30 
years of experience across retail banking, credit, strategy, 
operations, governance and business transformation. He has been 
appointed as the Group Compliance Officer (GCO) of J&K Bank, 
reflecting the Bank’s  commitment to strengthening its compliance 
framework and governance standards.  
 
Prior to this role, he served as General Manager & Head of Retail 
Banking Division, leading Retail Assets, Agriculture, MSME, 
Government Sponsored Schemes and Liability Management. An 
alumnus of National Institute of Technology (NIT), Jaipur and Indian 
Institute of Management (IIM), Amritsar, where he was awarded the 
Director’s Medal, he is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Economics at 
IIM Jammu.  
 
Since joining J&K Bank as a Probationary Officer in 1996, Shri 
Magotra has held several key leadership positions, including Zonal 
Head, Delhi Zone, Advisor to the Chairman, Head of Financial 
Inclusion and Head of Supervision & Control. He has led several 
strategic initiatives in MSME financing, financial inclusion, startup 
financing and institutional partnerships, and is a regular speaker at 
industry and academic forums on banking and governance.
