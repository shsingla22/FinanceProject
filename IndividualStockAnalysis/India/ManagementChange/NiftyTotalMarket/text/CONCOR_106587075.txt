Ms. Pallavi Joshi  
Director (Finance) (Additional Charge) 
 
Smt. Pallavi Joshi, a 2004 batch officer of the Indian Railwa y Accounts Service 
(IRAS) is currently serving as Executive Di rector (Finance – Commercial & PPP) in the 
Ministry of Railways, bringing over two decades of distinguished experience in financial 
management within the Indian Railways. 
 
An accomplished academic, she is a gold  medalist in B.A., a Junior Research 
Fellowship (JRF) scholar, and holds a PGDM (Exe cutive) with specialization in Financial 
Management. 
 
Over a career spanning more than 20 years,  she has held several key positions across 
Indian Railways. Her assignments include Assistant Divisional Financial Manager in the Mumbai Division of Central Railway, Division al Finance Manager in the Prayagraj Division 
of North Central Railway, Deputy Financial Ad visor in the Constructi on Unit of Northern 
Railway, and Deputy Chief Vigi lance Officer in Northern Railway. She has also served as 
Director (Finance Commercial) in the Railway Board and as General Manager (Finance) in the Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Limited (DFCCIL). 
 
She has been recognized for her exemplary service with prestigious awards, 
including the FA&CAO Award and the General Manager Award. During her tenure as 
Director in the Railway Board, she was conferred the National Rail Award in 2020. 
 
Throughout her career, she has spearhea ded several key initiatives, including 
digitization of paym ent systems for vendors and em ployees, development of IT 
infrastructure for accounts an d finance, and contributing to  the formulation of freight 
incentive schemes. At DFCCIL, she played a pivotal role in steering India’s first 
infrastructure financing through External Co mmercial Borrowing (ECB), backed by the 
Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency (MIGA), as well as refinancing of a World Bank 
loan. 
 
In her current role, she continues to provide strategic financial advice in critical 
areas such as freight and fare fixation, contributing to policy formulation and strengthening financial decision-making within the Ministry of Railways.
