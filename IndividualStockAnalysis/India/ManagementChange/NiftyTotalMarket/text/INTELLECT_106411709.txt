Vikas Misra, Executive Vice President has been
elevated as Chief Strategy Officer and
designated as Senior Management Personnel
(SMP) of the Company, effective October 16,
2025.
 Date of Appointment: October 17, 2025.
 Term of Appointment: NA

Vikas Misra joined Polaris Software Group in
April 1996 and since then played multiple roles
within the company. In 2006, he became SBU
Head with a global responsibility of establishing
and running Emerging Verticals business of the
company. In 2016 and 2017, Vikas was actively
involved in transitioning the Services Business
of Polaris and played a pivotal role as SBU
Head at Virtusa. In April 2018, Vikas took over
as CEO of Digital & Government Business of
Intellect managing Government e Marketplace.
Since January 2020, Vikas has been an integral
part of Office of Strategy working closely with
the Chairman and playing active role in
designing the mid to long term horizon
initiatives of the organisation. As a core team
member of organisation leadership, Vikas'
contribution and guidance cuts across all
functions by providing critical inputs for
execution of strategy to achieve the long term
objectives of the organisation.
