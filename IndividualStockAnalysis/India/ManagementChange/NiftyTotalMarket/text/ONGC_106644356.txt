c. Brief profile  Shri Satyan Kumar is a seasoned energy sector professional with over 36 years of extensive 
experience across the oil and gas value chain, including upstream operations, midstream, 
petrochemicals, corporate strategy, and renewable energy. An Electrical Engineering graduate 
from Dayalbagh Educational Institution, Agra, he also holds a Post Graduate Diploma in 
Management from MDI Gurgaon and has undergone advanced leadership training at ESCP-EAP, 
France. He has been a part of GOI’s flagship program for CPSE’s, capability building program 
‘DAKSH’ (Development of Aspiration, Knowledge, Succession & Harmony). 
He has been appointed as Director (Strategy & Corporate Affairs) of Oil and Natural Gas 
Corporation Limited (ONGC) by the Government of India. In this role, he will provide strategic 
leadership and oversight while continuing to drive ONGC’s expansion into renewable and 
sustainable energy, supporting the company’s broader vision of energy transition and long-term 
growth. 
He is currently Non- executive Chairman of ONGC Tripura Power Company (OTPC), Director on 
the Boards of ONGC NTPC Green Private Limited (ONGPL), OGL One Ltd. and Ayana Renewable 
Power Pvt. Ltd, and leads investments and business development initiatives across solar, wind, 
biofuels (CBG) and other emerging green energy domains.  
Over the course of his distinguished career at ONGC, Shri Kumar has held several key leadership 
positions spanning engineering, project management, operations, marketing, business 
development and corporate strategy. As Executive Director – Chief Corporate Strategy, he played 
a pivotal role in shaping ONGC’s long-term strategic roadmap, including diversification into 
petrochemicals, LNG, and green energy. He also drove innovation through venture investments in 
startups. 
He has led and delivered several large-scale and critical infrastructure / BD projects, including the 
captive power plants at Uran, ONGC Petro additions Limited(OPaL) and Hazira and the 103 km 
Hazira–Dahej naphtha pipeline. He has been instrumental in driving cost optimization while 
enhancing operational efficiency, safety, and profitability across multiple ONGC assets. He also 
played a key role in the development of ONGC’s first 15 MW utility-scale solar project from concept 
to commissioning. Notably, he has been at the forefront of ONGC’s entry into renewable energy, 
driving solar and wind initiatives in Gujarat and Rajasthan. 
Recognized for his strategic vision, execution excellence, capital discipline, digital oversight and 
leadership in the E&P value chain and energy transition, Shri Satyan Kumar continues to play a 
pivotal role in positioning ONGC as an integrated energy company aligned with India’s evolving 
energy landscape and sustainability goals, while addressing the energy trilemma of security, 
affordability, and sustainability.
