Sub: Change in Directorate on the Board of NMDC Steel Limited – Appointment of Ms. 
Sarita Chauhan, Additional Secretary & Financial Advisor, Ministry of Steel as 
Government Director 
 
Ref: Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. 
 
Ministry of Steel, Government of India vide Order No. S-14011/1/2022-BLA dated 12.10.2026 
has communicated the appointment of Ms. Sarita Chauhan, Additional Secretary & Financial 
Advisor, Ministry of Steel as Government Director on the Board of Directors of NMDC Steel 
Limited with immediate effect or until further orders, whichever is earlier. Accordingly, Ms. Sarita 
Chauhan has been appointed as Government Director on the Board of Directors of NMDC Steel 
Limited with effect from 12.10.2026. 
 
 
Brief Profile:  
 
Ms. Sarita Chauhan is a senior civil servant belonging to 1999 batch of the Indian Administrative 
Service - AGMUT Cadre. At present, she is serving as Additional Secretary & Financial Advisor 
in the Ministry of Steel, Government of India. 
 
Ms. Sarita Chauhan is not related to any other Director on the Board of NMDC Steel Limited. 
She is not disqualified to act as a Director under the provisions of Section 164 of the Companies 
Act, 2013. Further, she is not debarred from holding the office of Director by virtue of order of 
Court or Tribunal or SEBI or any other such authority.
