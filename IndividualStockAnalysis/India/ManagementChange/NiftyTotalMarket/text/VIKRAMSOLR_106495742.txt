Sub: Intimation of change in Management of the Company, under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and

Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 read with Schedule III, Part A, Para A (sub-paras 7 & 7C) of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), we hereby inform you that Mr. Arindam 
Chakraborty , Senior Vice President & Head of Global HR, designated as a Senior Management Personnel (SMP) of the 
Company, vide his letter dated December 31, 2025 has submitted his resignation. The said resignation has been accepted by 
the management of the Company today and the resignation shall be effective from the close of business hours on March 31, 
2026.  
 
The details as required under the Listing Regulations read with the SEBI Master Circular dated 11 November 2024, as 
amended from time to time, are enclosed herewith as Annexure A, along with a copy of the resignation letter. The aforesaid 
information is also available on the website of the Company www.vikramsolar.com  
 
Further, Mr. Biresh Ranjan Das has been appointed by the Company as Senior Vice President – Human Resources. Bringing 
over two decades of experience across manufacturing, telecom, hospitality, infrastructure, and global shared services, he 
brings a practical perspective on scaling organizations and aligning people strategy with business priorities in complex, 
multi-location environments. In this role, he will lead the company’s people and culture agenda, working closely with the 
management to build organizational capability aligned with Vikram Solar’s growth plans and expanding global footprint. He 
will oversee workforce strategy, organization design, productivity initiatives, and leadership development, with a focus on 
institutionalizing people processes and strengthening talent pipelines to meet the evolving demands of the clean energy 
industry. 
 
Biresh’s appointment reflects Vikram Solar’s intent to proactively strengthening talent development, expanding the 
employability pool, and creating a sustainable pipeline of future-ready professionals for the clean energy ecosystem.  
 
 
 
We request you to kindly take the aforesaid information on record.
