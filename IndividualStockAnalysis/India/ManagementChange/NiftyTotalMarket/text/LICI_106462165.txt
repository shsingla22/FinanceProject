Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, this is to inform that the Central Government, it is to inform 
that Central Government vide Notification No. eF. No. 6/2(i)/2022 -BO.I  dated December  03, 2025, has

Financial Services, Ministry of Finance , in terms of powers conferred under section 4(2)(d) of the Life Insurance 
Corporation Act, 1956 .

December 03, 2025.
