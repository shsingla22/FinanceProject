Brief Profile  
 Ms. Kalpana Singh is a veteran  business leader with 20 years 
of distinguished experience in brand building, consumer 
insights and category strategy. She has a proven track record 
of driving sustainable growth  and fostering innovation 
across the consumer goods industry.  
 
She joins the Company from Hindustan Unilever Limited 
where she most recently served as Marketing Director and 
was responsible for leading the strategic direction and 
execution of integrated marke ting campaigns  to strengthen 
brand positioning and drive customer engagement.  
 
During her 18 years of tenure with Unilever, she held several 
pivotal roles, including Personal Care Business Group 
Director for the Middle East, Turkey and North Africa region  
as well as Brand Development Director for the Middle East, 
North Africa, Russia and Turkey.  
 
Ms. Kalpana Singh holds a Master of Arts degree in Sociology 
from Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU).
