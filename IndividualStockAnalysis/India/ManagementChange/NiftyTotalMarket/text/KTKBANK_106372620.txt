Madam / Dear Sir,
Reg.: Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015:
Change in Management
Pursuant to Regulation 30 and other applicable provisions of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we inform that Mr. Vijayakumar P H has been
promoted as General Manager and appointed as the Chief Financial Officer of the Bank with
effect from September 15, 2025 in place of Mr. Abhishek Sankar Bagchi, who will cease to be
Chief Financial Officer of the Bank from the close of business hours on September 14, 2025 upon
completion of his tenure.
In terms of SEBI Master Circular No.: SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 11.11.2024
read with SEBI Circular No.: SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/2023/120 dated 11.07.2023, the
details of both Mr. Abhishek Sankar Bagchi and Mr. Vijayakumar P H are furnished in the
Annexure.
Accordingly, Mr. Abhishek Sankar Bagchi will cease to be a Key Managerial Personnel and
Senior Management Personnel (SMP) of the Bank from September 14, 2025 and Mr. Vijayakumar
P H will accordingly be a Key Managerial Personnel and Senior Management Personnel (SMP)
of the Bank with effect from September 15, 2025.
This is for your kind information and dissemination.
