Hero MotoCorp Announces Appointment of Madhuri Mehta as Chief
Human Resources Officer to Drive Business Transformation Through
People Strategy

New Delhi, September 26th, 2025: Hero MotoCorp Ltd., the
world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters today
announced the appointment of Ms. Madhuri Mehta as Chief
Human Resources Officer (CHRO) at Hero MotoCorp Ltd.,
effective October 1, 2025. Madhuri will lead the company’s global
people strategy, culture transformation, and capability-building
agenda, reinforcing Hero MotoCorp’s commitment to being a
future-ready, people-first organization.
“We are delighted to welcome Madhuri to the Hero MotoCorp
leadership team. Her rich experience and proven ability to link
people strategy with business outcomes will play a pivotal role in
shaping the next phase of our growth journey as we continue to strengthen our global footprint and future
readiness.,” said Dr. Pawan Munjal, Executive Chairman, Hero MotoCorp.
With over 24 years of experience across diverse industries, Madhuri is a seasoned HR leader known for
driving transformational change, building high-performance cultures, and aligning people strategies with
business growth. She will be joining Hero MotoCorp from Emaar India, where she served as CHRO and
Head of Communities, leading large-scale HR and organizational initiatives.
Prior to Emaar, she spent 16 years at Dalmia Bharat Group in leadership roles, including HR Head for the
Cement business, as well as Corporate HR Head and Executive Director – HR. She played a key role in
performance management, talent strategy, cultural transformation, and HR integration during mergers and
acquisitions, alongside managing industrial relations across 14 plants. She began her career with Bharti
Airtel, contributing significantly to talent acquisition, performance management, and HR digitization during
a high-growth phase.
Madhuri was recently honored as the Best HR Leader of the Year at the Economic Times Human Capital
Awards 2025. She is widely recognized as a thought leader in HR digital transformation, diversity &
inclusion, and employee engagement, with her perspectives featured in The Economic Times, Business
World, CSR Universe, and SheThePeople.
Madhuri holds a BA (Hons) in Sociology from Lady Shri Ram College and a Post Graduate Diploma in
Business Management from Fore School of Management.
With Madhuri’s appointment, Hero MotoCorp strengthens its leadership capability to align people strategy
with business goals, while reaffirming its commitment to fostering a high-performance, inclusive, and futureready workplace that empowers its people to drive the company’s vision forward.
