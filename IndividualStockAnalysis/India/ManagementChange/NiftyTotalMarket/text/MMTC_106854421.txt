holder shall be entitled to withdraw the said amount along with up-to-date accrued interest after expiry of 2 weeks
i.e. 23.05.2025. MMTC had filed SLP in Hon'ble Supreme Court, which has been dismissed by order dated

5. Basis for Qualified Conclusion
We draw attention to Note No. 3 of the Statement regarding the Anglo Coal matter. The
decree in favour of Anglo Coal has attained finality: the Hon'ble Delhi High Court, vide
order dated 09.05.2025, permitted the decree holder to withdraw the amount of Rs. 1,088.62
crore deposited by the Company along with accrued interest, and the Company's Special
Leave Petition was dismissed by the I-Ion'ble Supreme Court on 03.11.2025. An amount of
Rs. 1,000 crore was released to Anglo Coal on 17.11 .2025 as per the order of Delhi High
Court dated 10.11.2025. Based on the management's calculations, the estimated remaining
liability of the company towards Angelo coal as on 17.11.2025 amounts to Rs 170.58 crore,
including interest calculated up to 17.11.2025. Vide order dated 09.01.2026, the Hon'ble
High Court has kept pending only two issues: (i) whether tax is to be deducted at source on
the awarded amount; and (ii) the date relevant for applying the exchange rate to the awarded
amount and the interest thereon.
As per the Company's provisional calculation, the estimated present obligation of the
Company payable to Anglo Coal is Rs.170.58 crore, including interest up to 17.11.2025,
against which a provision of only Rs.87.76 crore is available in the books of accounts. The
remaining Rs.82.82 crore has not been recognised in the books of accounts and included in
contingent liabilities only, as the Management considers that interest accrued on the amount
deposited with Court, which has not been recognised in the books due to pending litigation,
is available for adjustmcnt.
As the liability is established by the decree and only its quantification is pending, the nonrecognition of the said Rs.82.82 crore, constitutes a departure from md AS 37 "Provisions,
Contingent Liabilities and Contingent Assets". Had the said Rs.82.82 crore been provided
for, provisions would have been higher, and Other Equity lower, by Rs.82.82 crore as at
30th September 2026, with a corresponding reduction in contingent liabilities.
