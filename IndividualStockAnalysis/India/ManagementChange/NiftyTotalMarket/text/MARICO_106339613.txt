Brief profile of Mr. Bhaskar Bhat

Mr. Bhaskar Bhat has over 4 decades of experience in the corporate world. Since
1983, he was associated with the Tata Watch Project (now Titan Company
Limited). At Titan, Mr. Bhat dealt with Sales & Marketing, HR, International
Business and various general managerial assignments and retired in September
2019 after serving as its Managing Director from April 2002. Under Mr. Bhat’s
leadership, Titan Company Limited enlarged its portfolio and extended its market
to Eyewear, Accessories, Fragrances and Sarees. The market cap of Titan Company
grew to 13 Billion USD in 2019 and is now the second largest in the Tata Group.
Mr. Bhat graduated in Mechanical Engineering from IIT Madras and completed his PGDBM from IIM Ahmedabad.
He has been conferred the Distinguished Alumnus Award at IIT Madras and IIM Ahmedabad.
Mr. Bhat is the Independent Chairman of the Board of Kansai Nerolac Paints Limited and Independent Director
of Metro Brands Limited, Lucas TVS Limited and Orange County Resorts & Hotels Limited. He is also a Board
Member of PANIIT Alumni Reach for Gram Udyogi Foundation and IITM Pravartak Technologies Foundation, notfor-profit Section 8 Companies.
