Mr. Anubhav Gupta, 48, brings over 25 years of
experience across design and P&L roles. He
holds a Bachelor of Architecture degree from
GGSIP University, TVB School of Habitat Studies,
a Master of Science, Architecture & Building
Engineering/Technology and Master of City
Planning degrees from MIT, USA. He is a Doctoral
Scholar (part-time M.Phil./Ph.D.) in Real Estate
Finance & Economics from University of
Cambridge, UK. He joins the Company from DLF
Ltd where he served as Chief Operating Officer,
Head of Business DLF 5 and worked on the
Camelia, Dhalia and DLF Golf Link projects,
among others. He has spent a significant part of
his career with Godrej Properties where he started
as head of design and then took on the role of
CEO Vikhroli, responsible for regeneration and
development of Godrej’s private land holding of
3000+ acres. He has also worked with RMJM,
Hongkong as Director Planning and Strategy and
RTKL Associates, UK as Principal & Country
Head, Indian Subcontinent, Emerging Markets
amongst others. He has held teaching positions
with Wentworth Institute of Technology, USA,
MIT, USA.
