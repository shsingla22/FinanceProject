4.  Shri Vikas 
Sharma  Shri Vikas Sharma has been appointed as Executive 
Director (Civil), effective 1st August 2026. Born on 6th 
January 1972, Shri Sharma hails from Urla in Tehsil 
Padhar, District Mandi, Himachal Pradesh. He holds a 
Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering from Amravati 
University, Maharashtra, and has over 30 years of rich 
experience in the power sector, primarily focusing on the 
development and execution of hydroelectric projects.  
His core competencies include Project Governance, Mega 
Hydro & Thermal Infrastructure, Tunneling (TBM & 
DBM), Stakeholder Alignment, Industrial Relations, Land 
Acquisition, Structural Risk Mitigation , Multi -Ministry 
Liaison, Statutory Clearances, and Strategic Logistics 
(Gati Shakti Cargo ) 
Shri Sharma began his career with Impregilo SPA, Italy, 
where he successfully executed the re -mining of a tunnel 
under squeezing rock conditions at the prestigious Nathpa 
Jhakri  HEP. He was also instrumental in achieving 
exceptional excavation rates in TBM tunneling during the 
execution of the Karahnjukar HEP in Iceland. Later, he 
worked with industry giants such as L&T, Gammon, and 
SN Power Norway, where he was actively involved in the 
construction of the Parbati Stage -II HEP, Purulia PSP and 
Allain Duhangan HEP.  
He joined SJVN Ltd. in December 2008 as Senior 
Manager (S&I and Infra). During his tenure, he played a 
pivotal role in securin g statutory clearances and  
completing S&I work for the Luhri HEP. For his 
exceptional field execution, structural restoration, and 
timely commissioning of the Rampur HEP, he was 
conferred the prestigious CMD Award in 2013 -14. He 
successfully oversaw completion and operation of the 
Corporate Office building, Shimla. Furthermore, he 
successfully navigated complex geological challenges in 
the Diversion Tunnel, HRT, and Dam at the Arun -3 HEP 
in Nepal, ensuring steady progress across all work fronts.  
Prior to his current role, he led the execution of the Buxar 
Thermal Power Plant as CEO, where he successfully 
achieved the Commercial Operation Date for Unit -I (660 
MW) by resolving critical challenges through innovative 
and time -bound strategies. Currently, he is heading the 
Sunni Dam HEP as Head of Project, driving steady 
progress across all operational fronts . 
With his extensive technical, managerial, and leadership 
expertise, Shri Sharma brings invaluable ex perience to his 
new role at SJVN Ltd.
