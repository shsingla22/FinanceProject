Madam/Sir,
Subject: Appointment of Shri Rohan Chand Thakur as the Government Nominee Director of the
Union Bank of India.
In terms of Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements)
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that the Central Government vide notification no. eF. No.
6/2(iv)/2022-BO.I dated July 24, 2025 appointed Shri Rohan Chand Thakur, Director, Government of
India, Ministry of Finance, Department of Financial Services as the Government Nominee Director of
the Bank with immediate effect and until further orders, vice Shri Sameer Shukla.
Reason for Change

Appointment of New Director

Date of Appointment

July 24, 2025

Tenure

From July 24, 2025 until further orders.

Brief Profile

Shri Rohan Chand Thakur is an officer of Indian Administrative Service
(IAS) belonging to 2009 batch of Himachal Pradesh Cadre. At present,
he is working as Director in Ministry of Finance, Department of
Financial Services, Government of India.
He is an economics graduate from St. Stephen’s College, Delhi and a
Postgraduate in Management from IIM Ahmedabad.
Experience in Government of Himachal Pradesh:
i. District Collector of Hamirpur and Shimla.
ii. Director of Information Technology.
iii. Managing Director of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship
Department.
iv. Managing Director of Himachal Pradesh Financial Corporation,
v. Excise and Taxation Commissioner.
vi. Managing Director of Himachal Road Transport Corporation.
He has previously worked with TAS before joining the civil services. He
is also a Chevening Gurukul Fellow from the University of Oxford and
a Master of Public Administration from Harvard Kennedy School,
Harvard University, Cambridge, Massachusetts.

Disclosure of relationship
between directors

Shri Rohan Chand Thakur doesn’t have any relationship with any of the
existing Directors of the Bank. Also, he is not debarred from holding
the office of Director by virtue of order of SEBI or any other such
authority.
