MEDIA RELEASE

GMDC Presents ₹223.55 Crore Dividend to Government of Gujarat
Final dividend of ₹9.50 per share (475%) for FY 2025-26; total payout of ₹302.10 crore to
shareholders
Ahmedabad, October 21, 2026: Gujarat Mineral Development Corporation Limited (GMDC), one of
India’s leading mining public sector enterprises, today presented a dividend cheque of
₹2,23,55,40,000 to the Hon’ble Chief Minister of Gujarat, Shri Bhupendra Patel. The cheque,
representing the State Government’s share of GMDC’s final dividend for FY 2025-26, was presented
to the Chief Minister by Shri Ajay Bhadu, IAS, Managing Director, GMDC in presence of Dr. Hasmukh
Adhia, Chairman, GMDC & Principal Advisor to the Chief Minister, Chief Secretary, Principal Secretary
to the Chief Minister and Additional Principal Secretary to the Chief Minister.
The dividend of ₹9.50 per equity share of face value ₹2 (475%) was recommended by the Board of
Directors on May 14, 2026 and approved by shareholders at the Company’s 63rd Annual General
Meeting held on September 30, 2026. It entails a total payout of ₹302.10 crore, of which ₹223.55 crore
accrues to the Government of Gujarat, which holds 74% of GMDC’s equity.
The payout follows a year in which GMDC reported a standalone profit after tax of ₹990.81 crore,
against ₹687.92 crore in FY 2024-25. The year’s profit includes an exceptional gain of ₹522.65 crore
from input tax credit following GST rate rationalisation on lignite. The total dividend corresponds to
about 30% of standalone profit after tax, in keeping with GMDC’s commitment to the Government of
Gujarat’s progressive dividend policy for State public sector enterprises.
Including this payout, GMDC’s dividends to the Government of Gujarat across the last five financial
years, FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26, total more than ₹1,050 crore.
Speaking on the occasion, Shri Ajay Bhadoo, IAS, Managing Director, GMDC, said:
“Taking charge at GMDC, I am conscious that every rupee we return to the State is a return
on Gujarat’s own mineral wealth, and it belongs to its people. This dividend reflects the
financial discipline GMDC has built over the years. Our task now is to carry that discipline
into the Company’s next phase: strengthening our lignite business, advancing our coal
and critical mineral projects, and growing responsibly, so that GMDC continues to create
lasting value for the State, our shareholders and the communities around our operations.”
Lignite remains the mainstay of GMDC’s business. Alongside it, the Company is building a diversified
portfolio spanning three coal blocks in Odisha, copper and rare earth elements projects, and 205.9
MW of wind and solar capacity, positioning GMDC as a responsible, future-ready contributor to
Gujarat’s growth and India’s resource security.


About Gujarat Mineral Development Corporation Limited
(www.gmdcltd.com | BSE: 532181 | NSE: GMDCLTD)

Established in 1963, Gujarat Mineral Development Corporation Limited is a Government of Gujarat
enterprise and one of India’s leading lignite producers. The Company operates five lignite mines across the
Kutch, South Gujarat and Bhavnagar regions, with further lignite projects under development. Its portfolio
extends to bauxite, fluorspar, manganese and limestone; three coal blocks in Odisha; a 250 MW lignitebased thermal power station at Akrimota; 205.9 MW of wind and solar capacity; and critical mineral
projects in copper and rare earth elements. GMDC is headquartered at Khanij Bhavan, Ahmedabad.
