With reference to the captioned subject, we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of the
Company at their meeting held today i.e. Friday, 7th November, 2025 have inter-alia considered and
approved the following:

1. Pursuant to Regulation 33 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, Considered and approved the Audited Financial
Results (Standalone and Consolidated) for the quarter and half year ended 30th September 2025
alongwith the Audit Report were reviewed and approved by the Audit Committee and
subsequently by the Board of Directors. Also available on the website of the Company at
www.lloydsengg.in.

2. Pursuant to Regulation 82 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue of Capital and
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, the Monitoring Agency Report for the quarter
ended 30th September 2025, in reference to allotment of Rights Issue of shares made on 5th June
2025, was considered, approved and taken on records by the Audit Committee and subsequently
by the Board of Directors. Also available on the website of the Company at www.lloydsengg.in.

3. Pursuant to Regulation 32 of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the Statement of Deviation or variation, if any, for
the quarter ended 30th September 2025, in reference to allotment of Rights Issue of shares made


on 5th June 2025, was first reviewed and approved by the Audit Committee and subsequently by
the Board of Directors of the Company. Also available on the website of the Company at
www.lloydsengg.in.

4. Considered and approved the draft Joint Venture Agreement between the Company and Techno
Industries Private Limited (“TIPL”), subsidiary of the Company and the same shall be executed
as and when the Letter of Intent has been received from the Government authorities. The
formation of a Joint Venture (the “JV”) between the Company and TIPL, subsidiary of the
Company to be constituted under the name “Techno – Lloyds”, for the purpose of jointly bidding
for and executing government tenders for elevators and escalators, by leveraging TIPL’s
specialised expertise in electrical works for vertical transportation (for 35 years) and the
Company’s vast work experience, subject to Letter of Intent received from Government
authorities.

5. The Investors Presentation for the quarter ended 30th September 2025 is also attached for your
reference.

6. Considered and approved the arrangement between the Company and Geomysore Services India
Private Limited (“GSIPL”) where, in lieu of a cash payment, GSIPL will issue its equity shares to
the Company in consideration for the services the Company will provide. GSIPL has committed
to issue 87,500 (Eighty-seven thousand five hundred) equity shares at a face value of Re. 1 (Rupee
one only) each, at an issue price of Rs. 1,599 (Rupees one thousand five hundred ninety-nine only)
per share, thereby resulting in a total consideration of Rs. 14,00,00,000 (Rupees Fourteen crores
only) for the services provided by the Company. In view of the above, the Company shall disclose
the details of the agreement entered if any at the later date in reference to Securities and Exchange
Board of India Circular dated 11th November 2024.

7. Noted the Grant of equity shares towards the Employees Stock Option Plan (ESOP) under the
employee stock option scheme 2021 to the employees of group companies i.e. 3,20,000 Equity
shares at an exercise price of Rs. 9.50 per option to the employees of Techno Industries Private
Limited, Subsidiary of the Company as approved by Nomination and Remuneration
Committee.

8. Noted the allotment of 1,05,784 equity shares at an Exercise Price of Rs. 9.50 towards the
Employees Stock Option Plan (ESOP) under the Employee Stock Option Scheme 2021 to the
Employees of Lloyds Infrastructure and Construction Limited, Associate of the Company as
approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.


Detailed disclosures as required under Regulation 30 read Schedule III of the SEBI Listing
Regulations and SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 11th November
2024 including amendments thereon, are provided in the Annexures enclosed to this Intimation.
