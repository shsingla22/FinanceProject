4. Brief Profile Mr. Neeraj is a senior legal professional with over 
three decades of experience in legal practice, 
having been actively practising as an Advocate and 
Legal Consultant since 1994. 
 
He presently serves as Additional Advocate 
General, Haryana (2024–Present) and as President 
of the Central Delhi Court Bar Association, New 
Delhi (2025–Present). He is also a Member of the 
Special Committee of the Bar Council of Delhi, a 
statutory body constituted under the Advocates Act, 
1961. 
 
Mr. Neeraj also serves as the General Secretary of 
Lawyer’s Voice, a national lawyers’ organization 
committed to the welfare, empowerment and 
upliftment of weaker and underprivileged sections of 
society. He is also serving as Senior Central 
Government Standing Counsel before the Armed 
Forces Tribunal, Principal Bench, New Delhi, 
representing the Union of India in matters 
concerning the Armed Forces. 
 
His professional and leadership journey includes 
serving as Honorary Secretary of the New Delhi Bar 
Association (NDBA) during 2009–10 and 2016–18. 
His association with institutional leadership dates 
back to his university days, when he served as 
President of the Students’ Union, Faculty of Law 
(Law Centre-I), University of Delhi.
