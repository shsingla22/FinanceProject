Brief Profile  
 
Rahul Gama is a seasoned HR leader with over 28 years of global experience across Asia, the Middle 
East, Africa, the Americas, and the Indian subcontinent, with leadership exposure across Consumer 
Goods, Chemicals, Agri, Retail, and Services.  
As CHRO at Pidilite Industries and Global CHRO at Godrej Consumer Products Limited, he led people initiatives supporting workforce transformation, international expansion, and acquisition integration 
across multiple geographies. His earlier roles at Godrej Agrovet, Godrej Chemicals, Marico, and Arvind 
International involved HR transformation, talent development, productivity improvement, and 
industrial relations across diverse businesses. Rahul has experience managing HR in complex, multi-
country environments and aligning people practices with business priorities.  
He holds a Master’s degree in Personnel Management and a Bachelor of Commerce in Economics.
