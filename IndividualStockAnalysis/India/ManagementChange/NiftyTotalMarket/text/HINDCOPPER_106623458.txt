Brief Profile of Shri Ghanshyam Das Gupta,  
Additional Director -Director (Mining), Hindustan Copper Ltd  
 
Shri Ghanshyam Das Gupta is B. Tech from premium institute NIT Surathkal  with 31 years of 
domestic and international experience. Shri Gupta is also a holder of 1st Class Mines Manager 
Certificate of Competency (FCC).  
 
Shri Gupta is a “Turnaround Specialist” in the mining industry. He has a proven track record of 
making PSU Units into profit -making  and award -winning operations.  He has vast experience and 
specialization in underground metal mining, Gold & Copper min ing. He worked in open cast 
mining in India and abroad.  
 
Shri Gupta is also a Member of Industry 4.0 Innovation Centre, a Society register ed under Society 
Registration Act, 1860 having Registered Office at Guwahati.  
 
Key Achievement  & Contributions  
• Strategic Turnaround at MCP: Revive d the Malanjkhan  Copper Project (MCP) during its 
difficult transition to underground mining. He resolved a 3 -year statutory deadlock by 
implementing an “out -of-the-box” engineering plan to connect mine outlets, securing 
essential clearances under the Mines Act.  
• Operational Scale -up: Accelerated MC P’s mine development from Zero to 1,000 
meters/month. He successfully commenced production while the underground project was 
still under construction to ensure immediate revenue.  
• Record Profitability: Led the Khetri Copper Complex (KCC) to Record Profits by expending 
the Kolihan Mine Capex Project.  
• Global Innovation: In Saudi Arabia, he redesigned Overburden (OB) waste relocation, 
significantly reducing lead distances and costs, which enhanced the reputation of Indian 
engineers globally.  
• Environmental & Safety Leadership:  
➢ Earned the 5 -Star Rating Award from Ministry of Mines and the National Safety Award  
from Ministry of Labour & Employment  
➢ Achieved Zero Water Discharge and planted 3 Lakh saplings, improving community 
relations and the PSU’s ESG profile.  
➢ Member of the Review Committee of Draft Metalliferous Mines Regulations 2025, 
framed under Occupational Safety, Health, & Working Conditions (OSH & WC) Code 
2020, Ministry of Labour & Employment.
