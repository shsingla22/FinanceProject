Brief Profile of Mr. Amit Batra 
Mr. Amit Batra possesses approximately thirty years of distinguished experience in 
a variety of leadership positions, encompassing strategy, operations, sales, 
environmental, social and governance (ESG), as well as business transformation. 
Prior to joining SBI Card, Mr. Batra served as Chief Operating Officer at TransUnion 
CIBIL India, where he led enterprise transformation initiatives with a focus on 
sustainable growth, regulatory compliance and operational resilience. His previous 
professional experience includes notable organizations such as SBI Card, GE 
Capital, Star TV, EQUUS Advertising, Modi Telstra and the Taj Group of Hotels. 
Mr. Batra is an alumnus of INSEAD, holds a Master Black Belt in Lean Six Sigma 
and is a graduate of the Institute of Hotel Management, Kolkata.
