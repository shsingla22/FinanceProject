 Deepak's journey began nearly two and a half decades ago at the Vellore Institute 
of Technology, where early encounters with software development and a deep 
fascination with Artificial Intelligence laid the foundation for a career defined by foresight and 
innovation. He holds a Bachelor of Engineering in Mechanical 
Engineering from VIT — a discipline that instilled systems thinking and problem -
solving rigour that would prove invaluable throughout his career.   
Building the Foundation: Early Career  
In the early 2000s, Deepak served as a core member of the Platform Engineering 
team at Premier Technology / EZ Data, building CRM tools for insurance advisors. 
When the business pivoted to a SaaS model, he learned a foundational lesson in 
agility — one tha t would define his leadership philosophy for years to come. His 
subsequent stint at Ebix involved significant contributions to product development in 
the insurance sector.   
Over a Decade of Leadership at Intellect  
Deepak joined Intellect Design Arena in 2011, at a pivotal moment when the company was transitioning from Polaris Financial Technology toward a product -
focused, IP- first strategy. He joined as an AVP and steadily rose through the 
organisation — leading del ivery, strategy, and innovation at Intellect SEEC — before 
being elevated to Partner and EVP – Chief Technology Officer at IntellectAI. In each 
role, he deepened Intellect's capabilities in Big Data, AI, and Machine Learning, steadily building toward a singular vision.  
The Purple Fabric Vision  
Over more than a decade of R&D under his leadership, Deepak's team created Purple Fabric — Intellect Design Arena's flagship platform designed to propel 
enterprise AI adoption for financial institutions to the next level. Today, Purple Fabric powers some of the world's most demanding financial institutions, including one of the largest sovereign wealth funds globally, supporting real -
time investment 
decisions.
