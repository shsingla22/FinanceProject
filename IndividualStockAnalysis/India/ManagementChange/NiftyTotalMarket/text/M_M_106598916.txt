 Ms. Shuchi Suri is joining Mahindra Group as Executive Vice President – Mahindra 
Experiences, effective 14th May 2026.  
 
As Head of Mahindra Experiences, Shuchi will drive strategic focus on world -class 
experiences to unlock value and drive business impact for the Group. She will take 
ownership for the Mahindra Adventure team in the Auto business and the 
Experiences team at Mahindra Holidays. She will work closely with the leaders at 
Auto and Holidays businesses and will report to Dr Anish Shah.  
 
Shuchi joins us following an illustrious 22+ year global career at Unilever, with 
leadership roles spanning India, the APAC region, and Global Headquarters in the 
UK. Most recently, as Global Vice President for Homecare, she led a multi -billion Euro 
global  category across 60+ markets, delivering consistent growth and margin 
expansion through rigorous portfolio transformation and strategic execution.  
 
She is an expert in Brand Strategy, Consumer Segmentation, Portfolio 
Transformation, Premium Innovation, Consumer lifecycle management, Digital First 
& Partnerships based Marketing, leading significant portfolios across India, UK and 
Singapore. She has bee n recognized for speed, agility, strong stakeholder influence 
and ability to build high performing purpose -based teams.  
 
Shuchi is a triple gold medallist from the School of Planning and Architecture (SPA) 
and an MBA from IIM Lucknow, before she joined Unilever.
