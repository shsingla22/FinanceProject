Regulations, 2015 - Appointment of Independent Directors and Additions to Senior Management Personnel
Dear Sir/ Ma’am,
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI
Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform that the Board of Directors at its meeting held today i.e., July 03, 2026,
has approved the following appointments, further strengthening the Company’s Board with independent
directors as the Company pursues next phase of growth and higher profitability:
1.

Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, and subject to the approval
of the members of the Company in the ensuing Annual General Meeting, as per applicable provisions of the
Companies Act, 2013 and the SEBI Listing Regulations:

a.

Appointment of industry veteran Mr. Narasinganallore Venkatesh Srinivasan (DIN: 01893686) as NonExecutive Independent Director of the Company with effect from July 05, 2026, for a term of three (3)
consecutive years. Mr. Venkatesh Srinivasan brings over four decades of experience across finance, capital
markets, banking, risk and treasury management, having held leadership positions at IDBI and the Association
of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI).

b.

Appointment of Ms. Sachee Trivedi (DIN: 11767043), Founder and Managing Partner of Trident Capital
Investments, as Non-Executive Independent Director of the Company with effect from July 05, 2026,for a
term of three (3) consecutive years. Ms. Trivedi brings over two decades of experience spanning global
equities investing and regulatory advisory in the financial services industry across the US and Europe, with a
strong background in technology. She also holds leadership roles within Luxembourg’s business organisations.

2.

Accepted the resignation tendered by Ms. Urvashi Sahai (DIN: 09521316), from the office of Whole-time
Director and Key Managerial Personnel of the Company, in order to focus on her executive responsibilities
within the Company, with effect from closure of business hours on July 05, 2026. Consequently, her position
as a member of the Stakeholders’ Relationship Committee of the Company will also conclude with effect from
the closure of business hours on July 05, 2026. She will continue to serve the Company in her existing
executive capacity as General Counsel & Senior Vice President – Legal and designated as Senior Managerial
Personnel of the Company with effect from July 06, 2026.
The Board has placed on record its deep appreciation for the valuable guidance and services rendered by Ms.
Sahai during her tenure. The necessary details and a copy of her email dated July 03, 2026, are attached
herewith as Annexure - II.

3.

Designation of Mr. Anuj Mittal (Senior Vice President – Investor Relations), Mr. Avijit Jain (Senior Vice
President & Chief Operating Officer – Loan distribution) and Mr. Vikash Jalan (Chief Operating Officer –
Consumer Payments) as additional Senior Management Personnel of the Company, based on the
recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, with effect from July 03, 2026.
