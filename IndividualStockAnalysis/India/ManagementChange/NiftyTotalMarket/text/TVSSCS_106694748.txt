Corporate Overview

TVS SCS

Orchestrating
Supply Chains
Across the World

Global commerce is powered by supply chain capabilities. Some are tangible factories, warehouses, vehicles, ports, and production lines. Others are intangible, but
equally critical - intelligence, coordination, trust, timing, and resilience. Together, they
connect businesses to markets and keep economies moving.
At TVS Supply Chain Solutions (TVS SCS), we bring these capabilities together to create
supply chain solutions that are efficient, agile, and built for a dynamic world.
Rooted in the century-strong heritage of the TVS brand, we have evolved from our
beginnings in automotive logistics into a diversified, global, integrated, asset-light, and
tech-led supply chain solutions provider, serving leading customers across industrial,
technology, consumer, and other high-growth sectors. We design, orchestrate, and
optimise supply chain solutions, delivering measurable value for customers across
industries and geographies.

Connecting businesses to markets.
Powering economies. Delivering value.

In an increasingly complex global environment shaped by geopolitical developments,
shifting trade patterns, and rapid technological change, resilient and adaptable supply
chains are essential for business success. At TVS SCS, resilience, driven by customer
centricity is embedded in our DNA, which remains at the core of everything we do.

Over the years, we have focused on building strong

Our theme, ‘Structured for Resilience, Positioned for

about stability. It is about creating the capability to adapt,
innovate, and seize new opportunities. Our evolution into
a global, tech-led, asset-light, and solutions-driven supply
chain partner reflects the deliberate choices we have made
to strengthen our business and broaden our horizons.

The bamboo spends years developing an extensive root system that
spreads wide, anchoring deep, and creating the foundation required for
sustained growth. During this phase, strength, flexibility, and endurance
are being built beneath the surface. When the bamboo rises, it does so
with remarkable speed and purpose, supported by a foundation carefully
nurtured over time. Its ability to grow, adapt, and flourish comes from the
strength it has already established.

Today, as supply chains become increasingly interconnected
and tech-led, this foundation positions us to grow with
purpose and ambition. Like the bamboo, we remain flexible
and agile, yet firmly rooted in our core values of Trust,
Value, Service and Innovation.

This principle closely mirrors our journey at TVS Supply Chain Solutions.
