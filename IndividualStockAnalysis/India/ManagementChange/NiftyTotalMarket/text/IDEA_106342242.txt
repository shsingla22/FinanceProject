Mr. Abhijit Kishore is currently the Chief Operating
Officer of the Company. He has been associated
with the Company since March 2015 and has held
multiple senior leadership roles within the

successfully launched the first 4G in India.
Abhijit holds a rich experience of over 27 years in
the Indian telecom industry across functions and
geographies.
Abhijit is an alumnus of Delhi University and FORE
School of Management, Delhi and has also
completed the Senior Leadership Programs from
IIM Ahmedabad and London Business School.
