ANNEXURE

1. Reason for change: Appointment.
2. Date & Term of Appointment: Date of Joining 20.08.2025 & tenure is 03 years.
3. Brief profile:
Shri Amit Misra joined as Chief Vigilance Officer of Container Corporation of
India Limited on 20 August 2025. He belongs to the 2001 batch of the Indian Railways
Service of Signal Engineers (IRSSE) and holds a B.E. in Electronics from Motilal
Nehru National Institute of Technology (MNNIT), Prayagraj.
He has extensive experience in the operation and maintenance of advanced
signaling and telecommunication systems, as well as in project planning, execution,
and contract management in Indian Railways. He has also held key administrative
roles as Deputy General Manager and Secretary to the General Manager, and as
Additional Divisional Railway Manager (ADRM), where he contributed to policy
formulation, strategic planning, performance reviews, and cross-departmental
coordination.
Shri Misra has served in the Research Designs and Standards Organisation
(RDSO), Lucknow, where he gained expertise in core technical areas, vendor
development, and innovations in railway signaling. He is presently Executive
Director, Vigilance (S&T), Railway Board.
