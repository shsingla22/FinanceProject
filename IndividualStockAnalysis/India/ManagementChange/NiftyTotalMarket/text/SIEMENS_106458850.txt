Annexure – A 
 
1. Dr. Shilpa Kabra Maheshwari will cease to be the Head – People & Organisation of the 
Company, effective close of business hours of 31st December 2025, as she has decided to 
leave the Company to pursue opportunities outside of Siemens. (resignation letter will be filed 
once it is effective)  
 
2. Ms. Sabine Schneider is appointed as the Head – People & Organisation of the Company, 
effective 1st January 2026. Brief profile of Ms. Schneider is as under:  
 
Ms. Sabine Schneider brings close to 30 years of rich and diverse experience in People and 
Organisation, of which 25 years plus have been with Siemens. She has held various 
leadership roles across Learning, People Development, Talent Management and Business 
Partnering in both Europe and Asia (Singapore). Her career spans global organizations, 
including Bosch and Schering in Germany, where she contributed in various                        
Human Resources capacities before joining Siemens. She currently holds  the position of 
Senior Vice President Talent Management in Siemens Mobility GmbH since 2018. Her cross -
cultural expertise and strategic HR leadership have consistently driven impactful 
organizational transformation and talent growth.  
 
She holds academic degrees in Psychology and Mathematics from Phillips University and 
Justus Liebig University in Germany. She is also a certified expert in Organizational 
Development, trained at the Hephaistos Institute in Munich.  
 
3. Mr. Rajiv Suri will cease to be the Head - Environmental Health and Safety of the Company, 
effective close of business hours of 31st December 2025 as he is superannuating from the 
Company.  
 
4. Ms. Sweta Praharaj is appointed as the Head - Environmental Health and Safety of the 
Company, effective 1st January 202 6. Brief profile of Ms. Praharaj is as under:  
 
Ms. Praharaj brings over 18 years of diverse experience at Siemens spanning project planning 
and execution, business controlling  and Environment, Health & Safety (EHS).  
 
Transitioning into EHS in 2018, Ms. Praharaj has worked on global EHS projects and has 
been instrumental in shaping governance policies and procedures, implementation of 
digitalization initiatives and has led critical crisis management initiatives - including COVID -19 
response strategy.  
 
She holds degrees in Commerce and Law, complemented by a certification in Business 
Administration and is NEBOSH IGC trained.

Dr. Subramaniyan has over three decades of experience in the electrical capital goods space , 
serving a wide range of clientele in Industry, Infrastructure and Utility sectors . During his tenure 
at Siemens, he has handled business portfolio from low voltage devices to large gas turbines 
in the South Asian markets covering India, Sri Lanka, Nepal and Bangladesh. He has also

international environmental laws and ESG reporting .  
 
He holds a degree in Electrical & Electronics Engineering (Gold Medalist), a Postgraduate

completed  executive education and leadership programs at IIM Bangalore, IIM Ahmedabad,
