Brief Profile (in case of appointment)

M/s. BNP & Associates Practicing Company Secretaries
(BNP) is one of the leading firms of Practicing Company
Secretaries. It has four Partners and one Associate Partner.
Mr. B. Narasimhan is a founder of the firm who was President
of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India New Delhi for
2024.
The other Partner includes Mr. Kalidas Ramaswami who has
written several books and articles on various topics under the
Companies Act and SEBI Act as well as SEBI Rules
Regulations. Mr. Avinash Bagul was Corporate Secretary of
large financial institution and after superannuation entered
the practice. All the Partners have 40 plus years of corporate
secretarial experience.
The firm has well equipped office in Mumbai. Its client
includes well-known Corporate and business houses from
various industries namely Pharmaceutical, Banking, NBFCs,
financial & insurance, manufacturing, construction etc. At
present BNP is Secretarial Auditor of several listed entities
including Nifty listed companies.
