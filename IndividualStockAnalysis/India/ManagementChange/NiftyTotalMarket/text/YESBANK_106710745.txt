Macroeconomic Overview
India continued its trajectory as the fastest-growing
major economy, with real GDP expanding by 7.7% in
FY 2025-26. Growth was anchored by a strong revival in private
consumption, which rose 7.7% during the year as compared
with 5.8% in FY 2024-25, aided by the next-generation GST
reforms that were rolled out in September 2025 to simplify
rates and expand consumption headroom. Headline CPI
inflation averaged 2.1% for the year, well below the RBI's
medium-term target, supported by benign food inflation and
stable core prices. Against this backdrop, the Reserve Bank of
India undertook a calibrated easing cycle, reducing the policy
repo rate cumulatively by 125 basis points since February 2025
to 5.25%, alongside liquidity infusion. This policy support
translated into strong consumer demand, improving credit
conditions, and a revival in private capital expenditure.
Despite this domestic resilience, the global environment
remained challenging with ongoing conflict in West Asia,
geopolitical fragmentation, and trade policy uncertainties
weighing on growth. The IMF projects global growth to
moderate to 3.1% in 2026. Looking ahead, India remains the
fastest-growing major economy, with the RBI projecting
GDP growth at 6.6% for FY 2026-27, providing a supportive
backdrop for your Bank's continued expansion.

Financial Performance
Your Bank reported a Net Profit of ` 3,476 crore, its highest
annual profitability since reconstruction, reflecting a 44.5%
growth over FY 2024-25 - a direct outcome of disciplined
execution and strategic clarity. Your Bank exited Q4
FY 2025-26 at an annualised Return on Assets (RoA) of
1.0%, with a full year RoA expanding to 0.8% from 0.6% in
FY 2024-25. Total deposits crossed the landmark ` 3 lakh crore
mark, with Retail and Branch Banking deposits contributing
58.4% and growing at 13.5% Y-O-Y, outpacing both the industry
(10.2%) and private sector banks (11.4%). CASA balances
crossed the milestone of ` 1 lakh crore. Your Bank's Advances
book rose to ` 273,445 crore, registering 11.1% Y-O-Y growth,
underpinned by strong disbursement momentum across
segments. Borrowings declined 9.4% Y-O-Y to ` 64,864 crore,
aided by a sustained reduction in RIDF-related balances, as
your Bank achieved another year of NIL shortfalls across all
key Priority Sector Lending (PSL) sub-categories.
