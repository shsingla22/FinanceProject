Commenting on results, the company stated:
1. Our Total Insurance Premium for the quarter was ₹9,385 Cr, up 38% YoY, led by growth in core online new
protection business at 42% YoY (new Health insurance up 46% YoY).
2. Consolidated operating revenue for the quarter grew 35% YoY to ₹2,178 Cr (Core Insurance revenue up 42%
YoY; Q2FY27 Core credit revenue, up 19% YoY).
3. Our core renewal / trail revenue on a 12-month rolling basis is at ₹1,097 Cr, up from ₹774 Cr last year same
quarter, a 42% growth (led by growth of 56% YoY in the core insurance segment). The quarterly core
insurance renewal revenue is at an ARR of ₹1153 Cr, up from ₹758 Cr in Q2 last year (growth of 52% YoY).
4. Growth in Core New Insurance Premium (net of Savings business) is holding strong at 41% YoY. Core New
Insurance Premium (Including Savings) grew 34% YoY for the quarter. Excluding Savings category, we have
been growing above 34%YoY for the last 14 consecutive quarters now.
5. We continue to improve our customer onboarding & claims support services and Insurance CSAT is consistent
above 90%.
6. Our core credit revenue for the quarter is ₹125 Cr, up 19% YoY, with core disbursal is at ₹2,791 Cr, up 22%
YoY. Core Credit disbursal is growing sequentially for the last 5 consecutive quarters.
New Initiative revenue grew 28% YoY. Quarterly adjusted EBITDA margin stood at -5%, with 6% contribution margin.
1. PB Partners, our agent aggregator platform, now supports over 570k+ advisors.
a. We have moved the business increasingly towards smaller advisors.
b. We lead the market.
c. Our Active Partner count has increased to 1.31 lacs in Q2FY27, up 55% YoY
d. Present in 19k pin codes across India, covering 99% of pin codes in India. PB Partners continues to
extend insurance access into both metro and underserved geographies - with Tier 2 and Tier 3
markets contributing 76% of overall GWP for the quarter.
e. Total Q2FY27 premium grew 36% YoY to ₹1,989 Cr (Ex GST). Revenue grew by 39% to ₹698 Cr.
2. Q2FY27 UAE Insurance premium grew 36% YoY & aligning more towards health & life insurance, similar to
our India business. We have unique value proposition of cross-border health insurance products & Claims
assurance program for motor insurance.

Note: India Insurance Premium exclude GST


Our consolidated Q2FY27 PAT for PB Fintech grew 43% YoY to ₹192 Cr (PAT margin grew from 8% in Q2FY26
to 9% in Q2FY27). This is 2% of our total insurance premium.

To summarize our performance since our public listing:
a) Revenue grew at a CAGR of 51% from ₹280 Cr in Q2FY22 to ₹2,178 Cr in Q2FY27
b) PAT margin grew from -73% in Q2FY22 to 9% in Q2FY27
