MRS. SUSAN MATHEW  
Mrs. Susan Mathew, I.A.S. (Retd.), hails from Alwaye, Kerala. She is a graduate of Women’s Christian 
College, Chennai, and has completed her post -graduation from Union Christian College, Alwaye. She 
was selected to the Indian Administrative Service in 1975 and has over 36 years of distinguished 
service with the Government.  
During her career, she held several senior and key positions, including Collector of Madras; Project 
Coordinator for the World Bank Nutrition Project (for wh ich she was awarded the prestigious Lester 
Pearson Fellowship by the Government of Canada and pursued higher studies in Public Administration 
at Carleton University and the University of Ottawa, Canada); Director of Tourism; Special Secretary 
to the Govern ment, Health & Family Welfare Department; Secretary to the Government, Energy 
Department; Chairman, Tamil Nadu Electricity Board; Secretary to the Government, Commercial Taxes 
& Registration Department; Chairman & Managing Director, Tamil Nadu Power Financ e & 
Infrastructure Development Corporation; Chairman, Tamil Nadu Women’s Development Corporation; 
Additional Chief Secretary and Vice -Chairman, Chennai Metropolitan Development Authority (CMDA); 
and Additional Chief Secretary and Chairman & Managing Direct or, Tamil Nadu Industrial 
Development Corporation (TIDCO).  
She played a pivotal role in major policy and institutional initiatives, including establishment of private 
power projects in the State, computerisation of registration services, notification of th e Second Master 
Plan for the Chennai Metropolitan Area, formulation of the Comprehensive Transport Policy for the 
State, and preparation of the State’s “Vision -2020” document for industrial development. She also 
represented the Government of India in sever al international delegations and conferences relating to 
health, nutrition, urban development, and infrastructure.  
Post retirement, M rs. Susan Mathew served as Advisor to the Union Public Service Commission, New 
Delhi, and as a member of Interview Boards f or selection to the I.A.S., I.F.S., I.P.S., Central Services and 
Para -Military Forces. She has also served on the Executive Committee and Council of the Christian 
Medical College, Vellore. She currently serves on the Council of the Christian Education Heal th and 
Development Society, as an Independent Director on the Board of a joint venture of the Holiday Inn 
Group of Hotels, as a Member of the Board of Governors of XIME, and on the Advisory Board of 
Greenfield International School, Chennai.
