Annexure-I 
Brief Profile of Shri Arvind Kumar 
 
Shri. Arvind Kumar is a qualified Company Secretary from Institute of Company Secretaries of India 
having 26 years of enriched experience. He is post graduate in commerce and Law Graduate. He 
possesses extensive experience in various areas of, Secretarial, Companies Act, SEBI 
Regulations, Restructuring, ESG and FEMA compliances. His extensive experience 
includes managing listing compliances, corporate restructuring, mergers & acquisitions, foreign 
investment regulations, corporate governance, and legal matters. Prior to joining the Company, he 
was associated with Escorts Kubota Limited as Head of Secretarial Department and has also 
worked with Reliance Industries Limited, GMR Infrastructure Limited, Omaxe Limited, DLF Group, 
Emaar MGF Group, and Kamdhenu Ispat Limited, handling key corporate compliance functions.
