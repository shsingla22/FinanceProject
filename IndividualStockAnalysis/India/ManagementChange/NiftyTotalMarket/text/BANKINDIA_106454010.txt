Dear Sir/Madam, 
Appointment of Shri Pramod Kumar Dwibedi as Executive Director, 
on the Board of the Bank 
In Compliance of Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (LODR) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of 
sub-section (3) of section 9 of The Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of 
Undertakings) Act,1970, read with paragraph 8(1) of the Nationalised Banks (Management 
and Miscellaneous Provisions) Scheme 1970 (amended vide notification dated 17.11.2022), 
the Central Government vide their notification no. eF. No. 4/4(iv)/2024-B0.1 dated 24th 
November, 2025 appointed Shri Pramod Kumar Dwibedi, Chief General Manager, Bank of 
India as Executive Director in Bank of India with effect from the date of assumption of charge 
of the office till the date of his attaining the age of superannuation i.e 30.06.2028 or until further 
orders, whichever is earlier. 
Accordingly, Shri Pramod Kumar Dwibedi has assumed office of Executive Director of 
the Bank yesterday, i.e 24.11.2025. The brief profile of Shri Pramod Kumar Dwibedi is 
attached as Annexure. 
Shri Pramod Kumar Dwibedi is not related to other directors of the Bank. Further, Shri 
Pramod Kumar Dwibedi is not debarred from holding the office of Executive Director of the 
Bank be virtue of any SEBI order or any other such authority. 
We request you to take it on record.

Annexure 
Brief profile of Shri Pramod Kumar Dwibedi, Executive Director: 
Shri Pramod Kumar Dwibedi has been appointed as Executive Director of Bank of India and 
assumed charge on 24.11.2025. 
Shri Dwibedi, aged 57 Years, is a postgraduate in Economics & MBA (Finance) started his 
career in Banking in the year 1995 as Probationary Officer. He is certified Associate Member 
of Indian Institute of Bankers (CAIIB). He also holds a certification in IT and Cyber Security 
from the Institute for Development and Research in Banking Technology (IDRBT) for Senior 
Management and Fundamentals of Risk Management from Institute of Risk Management, 
London. 
In a career spanning over 30 years, he has worked in different capacities and has worked 
across the country and abroad. He has functioned in almost all areas of banking operations 
and Administrative offices. He has widened relationship with Corporate Customers with 
special focus on Mid Corporate — MSME customers. He also ensured all-round business 
growth & all business areas worked together in an effective manner. 
He successfully handled the responsibilities such as General Manager, FGMO Bhopal, 
comprising of Madhya Pradesh & Chhattisgarh and as a Chief General Manager, FCGMO 
Pune. He had carried out significant structural and cultural upgrades bringing the Micro, Small 
& Medium Enterprises, Food & Agro, and Agriculture Infra to the forefront of business 
development model in MP & Chhattisgarh and Maharashtra. He also ensured the timely 
achievement of all core business parameters.
