Brief Profile
Mr. Ajoy Chawla became part of the Tata
Administrative Services (TAS) in 1990 and
initially joined the finance department of Titan in
1991. Over the next two decades, he was in the
Watches Division, and played several roles
spanning Commercial, Sales, Retailing, Supply
chain, SAP implementation, leading accessories
and licensed brands as SBU Head and then heading
the Titan SBU for Domestic and International.
Between 2013 and 2019, Ajoy was the Chief
Strategy Officer of Titan as well as heading
Business Incubation, scaling the Fragrances
business and seeding the Taneira business. In
October 2019, Ajoy became the CEO of Titan's
Jewellery Division. Under his leadership, the
Jewellery Division has grown approximately 3X in
sales and profits and reinforced its market and
thought leadership.
