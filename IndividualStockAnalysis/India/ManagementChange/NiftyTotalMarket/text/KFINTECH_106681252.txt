Leading India’s Ownership and Governance Infrastructure

Leader in Alternative Investments. A Formidable Platform Play

We are the largest issuer solutions provider in India, delivering end-to-end shareholder servicing and
registry solutions across the corporate lifecycle. As a trusted market infrastructure partner, we enable
issuers to manage ownership, corporate actions, and compliance with precision, scale, and reliability.
Our technology-led platforms and strong governance frameworks ensure seamless execution,
real-time visibility, and regulatory alignment, enabling corporates to efficiently manage large
shareholder bases and enhance investor engagement. We combine market leadership with digital-first
infrastructure to position ourselves as a strategic partner to India Inc., supporting sustained growth and
stakeholder trust.

We are the undisputed leader in solutions for Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs) in India, powering a
significant share of the industry through a deeply integrated, technology-led platform. Our capabilities
span end-to-end fund administration, investor onboarding, order execution, and investment lifecycle
servicing, enabling managers to operate complex strategies with speed, control, and regulatory precision.
Our platforms combine institutional-grade portfolio analytics, advanced order management systems,
and real-time intelligence, delivering superior transparency, scalability, and investor outcomes. Built for the
evolving needs of alternative and wealth managers, we provide a digitised, data-driven ecosystem that
drives efficiency, strengthens compliance, and accelerates growth. By combining category leadership
with proprietary technology and domain depth, KFintech is the partner of choice for AIFs, PMS, and wealth
platforms, enabling them to scale faster, differentiate offerings, and capture the next phase of growth in
India’s alternative investment landscape.

PENSION SCHEME
Powering India’s Digital Pension Infrastructure at Scale
As the second-largest Central Recordkeeping Agency (CRA) for the
National Pension System, we operate a critical digital infrastructure
empowering millions of subscribers and enabling end-to-end
pension lifecycle management across India. Our platform integrates
governments, corporates, pension fund managers, subscribers, and
intermediaries, delivering real-time processing, robust compliance,
and transparent governance at scale. Built on secure, scalable,
and automated architecture, we enable seamless onboarding,
transaction processing, contribution tracking, and regulatory
reporting – driving efficiency, trust, and financial inclusion. Through
technology-led innovation and process excellence, we play a pivotal
role in strengthening India’s retirement ecosystem and enabling
long-term savings outcomes.
