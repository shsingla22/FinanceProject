growth, APAR's reach now extends to over 140 countries, supported by 10 state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities across 
India and a strategic facility in the UAE. The company commands distinction among India's foremost enterprises, having 
attained the prestigious 155th rank on both the Fortune 500 India 2024 and Economic Times 500 India 2023 rankings,
