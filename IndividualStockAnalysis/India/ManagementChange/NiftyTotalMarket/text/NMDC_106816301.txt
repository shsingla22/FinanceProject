Ministry of Steel (MoS), Government of India vide Order No. S-14011/1/2022-BLA dated  
12.10.2026 has communicated the appointment of Shri Vinod Kumar Tripathi, Joint Secretary, Ministry 
of Steel as Government Director on the Board of NMDC Limited with immediate effect or until further 
orders, whichever is earlier vice Shri Abhijit Narendra, Former Joint Secretary, Ministry of Steel. 
Accordingly, Shri Vinod Kumar Tripathi (DIN: 10711675) has been appointed as Government Director 
on the Board of Directors of NMDC Limited w.e.f. 12.10.2026. Further, Shri Abhijit Narendra has 
ceased to be Government Director on the Board of NMDC Limited.  
The brief Profile of Shri Vinod Kumar Tripathi is enclosed as Annexure A.  
It is informed that Shri Tripathi is not related to any other Director on the Board of NMDC Limited. 
Further, he is not disqualified to act as a Director under the provisions of Section 164 of the Companies 
Act, 2013.  
Further, he is not debarred from holding the office of Director by virtue of order of Court or Tribunal or 
SEBI or any other such authority.  
The above is for kind information and dissemination.

Annexure A 
Brief Profile of Shri Vinod Kumar Tripathi 
Shri Vinod Kumar Tripathi, Joint Secretary, Ministry of Steel, Government of India, belong 
to Indian Railway Service of Engineers (IRSE – 1995), is a Civil Engineering graduate 
from IIT Roorkee.  
He has worked in various capacities in Ministry of Railway, Government of India and has 
vast experience in administration, management and engineering. This includes 
management of Public Sector Enterprise, public policy, projects, operations, 
technological advancement, procurement & inventory management and human resource 
management. He has also worked in Railway Land Development Authority as Executive 
Director. Presently he is Joint Secretary in Ministry of Steel, Government of India.
