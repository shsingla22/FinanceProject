Subject:

Resignation from the position of Non-Executive Director and Vice Chairman of the
Company

Dear Members of the Board,
It has been my privilege to be associated with the Company and be part of its remarkable growth.
My association with the Company has been an enriching journey, marked by significant
milestones, strategic initiatives and sustained value creation for all stakeholders.
During my tenure, I have had the opportunity to serve alongside an accomplished and highly
experienced Board, whose collective wisdom, professionalism and commitment to the highest
standards of corporate governance have played an instrumental role in shaping the Company's
success.
After a long and fulfilling association with UltraTech Cement Limited, I wish to relinquish the
position of Non-Executive Director and Vice Chairman of the Company with effect from
1st January, 2027 due to my current engagements and other personal commitments.
I would like to place on record my sincere thanks to the Chairman, my fellow Directors, the
management of the Company for their support and cooperation throughout my tenure.
I am confident that UltraTech will continue its growth trajectory and create enduring value for its
stakeholders and set new benchmarks for excellence in the years ahead and look forward to the
Company achieving new heights of success.

Yours sincerely,
