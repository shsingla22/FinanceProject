Brief Profile of Shri Ra" Kumar Sharma - Part Time Non Official Director
Mr. Raj Kumar Sharma, aged 59 years, appointed as Part-time Non Official Director in
Bank of India from 12.08.2026. He holds Law degree and has done B.Corn (Management)
from Guwahati Commerce College. He is also a Fellow Member of the Institute of
Company Secretaries of India (FCS). He has been a Practising Company Secretary since
1997 and has extensive professional experience in Company Law and related matters.
He has served as the Chairman of the North-East Chapter of the Institute of Company
Secretaries of India for two terms during 2011-2012 and is presently a member of its
Management Committee. He has also served as the Secretary of the Tax Bar Association,
Assam, in 2006.
In addition to his professional engagements, he has been associated with Marwari Yuva
Manch and served as its National Executive Member for two terms. He is presently serving
as a State Executive Member of Marwari Yuva Manch. He is also a regular speaker on
Income Tax and Company Law at various professional platforms.
