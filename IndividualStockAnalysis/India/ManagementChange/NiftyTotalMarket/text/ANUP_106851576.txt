Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR Regulations”), we wish to inform you that, the Board of
Directors of the Company at its meeting held today i.e. 6th November, 2026 has inter alia:
1. noted the resignation tendered by Mr. Nilesh Hirapara, Chief Financial Officer (Key

Managerial Personnel) of the Company with immediate effect on 6th November,
2026, in view of his transition to a new and larger role within the Group;
2. approved the appointment of Mr. Vishal Begwani as the Chief Financial Officer (Key

Managerial Personnel) of the Company, pursuant to Section 203 of the Companies
Act, 2013, with effect from 6th November, 2026 based on the recommendation of
the Nomination and Remuneration Committee & the Audit Committee of the
Company.
The transition is part of the Group’s broader organisational and succession-planning
initiatives, aimed at nurturing internal talent, providing opportunities for employees to take
on enhanced responsibilities, and strengthening leadership capabilities to support the
Group’s long-term growth objectives.
Pursuant to Regulation 30(5) of Listing Regulations Mr. Vishal Begwani, Chief Financial
Officer of the Company is authorized in place of Mr. Nilesh Hirapara for the purpose of
determining materiality of an event or information and making such disclosures to Stock
Exchanges.
