Brief Profile
Mrs Byce completed BA from Delhi University and Master’s from SCMHRD Pune. She is associated with the
Tata Group since 2025. She is currently the Chief Human Resource Officer. Prior to joining the Company, she
also worked with Sterlite Technologies, SKF Bearings, Thermax and Cummins.
Mr Choudhary completed B.Tech. in Production Engineering from BIT Sindri. He is associated with the Tata
Group since 1992. He is currently the Vice President – Operations.
Mr Patuschka completed Master’s degree in Electrical & Electronics Engineering from Technical University of
Darmstadt, Germany. He is associated with the Tata Group since 2023. He is currently the Chief Technology
Officer. Previous assignments included working with Volkswagen AG and Hyundai Motor Company.
Mr Uhlarik completed Bachelors in Transportation Design from ArtCenter College of Design, California, USA.
He is associated with the Tata Group since 2016. He is currently the Chief Design Officer. Prior to TML, he
also worked with Skoda Auto AS, Nissan Design Europe, SAIC Motor, Tata Motors Design Tech Centre.
Mr Kamat completed B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from Mumbai University and an Executive MBA from
Manipal University. He is associated with the Tata Group since 2016. He is currently the Chief Commercial
Officer for TMPV. Prior to TML, he also worked with Fiat India, Honda Motorcycle, Maruti Suzuki India Ltd.
Mr Savarkar completed B.E. from NIT Karnataka and M.E. from IIT Madras. He is associated with the Tata
Group since 1994. He is currently the Chief Product Officer for TMPV. He has also worked with Hindustan
Aeronautics Ltd, and Profiloforms.
Mr Kulkarni completed B.E. from Pune University and PGDM from SPJIMR Mumbai. He is associated with the
Tata Group since 1993. He is currently the Chief Product Officer, Head of HV Programs & Customer Service
for TPEM. He has also worked with Tata Technologies.

Mr Srivatsa completed B.Sc. From Bangalore University and PGDM from IGNOV. He is associated with the
Tata Group since 2016. He is currently the Chief Commercial Officer for TPEM. He has also worked with
Renault India, Maruti Suzuki and Philips Electricals.
Mr Chikhle completed MBA from SPJIM, Mumbai and Bachelors in Mechanical Engineering from NIT
Jalandhar. He is associated with the Tata Group since 2023. He is currently the Chief Purchase Officer.
He also worked with Ashok Leyland, Fiat, CNH and Iveco in India and overseas.
