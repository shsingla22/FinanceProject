Sir/Madam,
In terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR Regulations, read with clause 7 and clause 7C of paragraph
A of part A of Schedule III of the SEBI LODR Regulations and the master circular dated July 11, 2023
(last updated on January 30, 2026) issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India, bearing ref.
no. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 (“Master Circular”), we wish to inform you that
Mr. Amit Dixit (DIN: 01798942), Mr. Dhaval Jitendra Buch (DIN: 00106813) and Mr. Animesh Agrawal
(DIN: 08538625) have today i.e., on October 9, 2026, tendered their resignations from their
respective positions i.e. as the Non-Executive, Non-Independent Directors of the Company, with
effect from the close of business hours on October 9, 2026.
The Board of Directors of the Company (“Board”) has today i.e. on October 9, 2026, taken note of
the same.
Pursuant to the aforesaid resignations:
(a) Mr. Amit Dixit shall cease to be a Member of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of
the Board;
(b) Mr. Dhaval Jitendra Buch shall cease to be the Chairperson of the Risk Management Committee
and a Member of the Corporate Social Responsibility Committee of the Board; and
(c) Mr. Animesh Agrawal shall cease to be the Chairperson of the Stakeholders Relationship
Committee and the Security Committee, and a Member of the Audit Committee, the Corporate
Social Responsibility Committee and the Risk Management Committee of the Board.
The requisite information pertaining to the aforesaid resignations, required to be disclosed in terms
of the provisions of Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI LODR Regulations and the Master
Circular, is included in Annexure A enclosed herewith.
