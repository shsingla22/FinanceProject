Brief Profile  Mr. Govind Jaiswal is an officer of the 2007 batch of the Indian 
Administrative Service (IAS), AGMUT Cadre. He holds a Bachelor's Degree in 
Science {B.Sc. (Mathematics)} from VBSP University, Jaunpur.  
During his distinguished administrative career spanning various assignments 
in the Central Government and different State/Union Territory 
administrations, Shri Jaiswal has acquired diverse administrative and 
governance experience across several important sectors, including Higher 
Education, Health & Family Welfare, Rural Development, Panchayati Raj, 
Transport, Environment & Forests, Ports and Fisheries.  
At the Government of India level, he served as Joint Secretary in the 
Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education, from July 2023 to 
October 2026. Prior to this, he served as Director and Deputy Secretary in 
the Ministry of Health & Family Welfar e from October 2020 to July 2023, 
providing him exposure to important national -level policy and administrative 
responsibilities in the health sector.  
In his cadre assignments, Shri Jaiswal served in senior administrative 
capacities in the Governments of Arunachal Pradesh and Goa, including as 
Secretary in the Department of Rural Development & Panchayati Raj, 
Government of Arunachal Pradesh, and in vario us departments of the 
Government of Goa, including Transport, Environment & Forests, Ports and 
Fisheries. His earlier assignments also include District Magistrate, Delhi; 
Additional Commissioner, Trade & Taxes, Delhi; Deputy Commissioner, 
Zunheboto, Nagala nd; and other field -level administrative positions.  
Following his repatriation from Central Deputation in October 2026, Shri 
Jaiswal was posted as Principal Secretary to the Lieutenant Governor of 
Jammu & Kashmir. He has also been assigned the additional charge of Chief 
Executive Officer, Shri Amarnathji Sh rine Board.
