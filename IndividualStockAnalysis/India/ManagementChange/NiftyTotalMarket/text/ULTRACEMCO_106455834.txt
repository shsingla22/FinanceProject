The Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today, based on the recommendation 
of the Nomination, Remuneration and Compensation Committee, considered and approved the 
appointment of Mr. Dhiraj Kapoor (Membership No. F5454) as Company Secretary and 
Compliance Officer with effect from 1st December, 2025. He will also be a Key Managerial 
Personnel, Senior Managerial Personnel and Nodal Officer from the said date.

The Board also noted the cessation of Mr. Sanjeeb Kumar Chatterjee as Company Secretary and

smooth handover.

valuable contributions during his tenure with the Company.
