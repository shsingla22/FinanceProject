Brief Profile of Smt. Srividya Venkataraman  
 
Ms. Srividya Venkataraman is a distinguished finance professional with over three decades of  
experience in the Indian energy sector. A Chartered Accountant and Cost & Works  
Accountant, she brings extensive expertise across treasury management, taxation, ri sk 
management, investor relations, capital markets, and business finance, having served in a  
wide range of leadership positions across the petroleum industry.  
 
Prior to joining Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) as Director - Finance, she  
served  as Executive Director - Corporate Treasury at Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited  
(BPCL), where she was responsible for Treasury, Insurance, Investor Relations, Pension  
Fund Investments, Pricing and Taxation. In this role, she played a pivotal role in ma naging the  
corporation’s capital structure, investment strategy, risk management framework, and  
stakeholder engagement initiatives. Under her leadership, BPCL successfully mobilized  
project finance exceeding ₹30,000 crore for strategic growth initiatives a nd strengthened its  
financial position through innovative treasury and capital management measures.  
 
Over the course of her distinguished career, Ms. Srividya has held several key leadership  
assignments in BPCL across Treasury, Retail, and LPG businesses. A s Finance Head of the  
Retail Business, she contributed significantly to business growth, network expansion, digital  
transformation, cost optimization, and governance enhancement. During her tenure in the  
LPG business, she played an important role in the su ccessful implementation of the flagship  
Government initiatives, such as, the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scheme and the Pradhan  
Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY), facilitating efficient subsidy transfers and expanding access to  
clean cooking fuel across the c ountry.  
 
She has been instrumental in several landmark financial initiatives, including BPCL’s maiden  
international bond issuance, large -scale external commercial borrowings, pension fund  
management, strategic investment planning, insurance optimization, an d strengthening  
investor engagement. She has also led the implementation of digital finance solutions that  
enhanced operational efficiency, transparency, and governance across business functions.  
 
Ms. Srividya has served on the Boards of Kannur Internationa l Airport Limited, BPCL KIAL  
Fuel Farm Facilities Pvt. Limited, and FFPL Finserv Private Limited, contributing significantly  
to corporate governance and strategic oversight.
