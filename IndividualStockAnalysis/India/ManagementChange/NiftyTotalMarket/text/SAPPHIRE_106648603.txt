Pursuant to Regulation 30 of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform the
following changes in the Senior Management Personnel of the Company:
1. Resignation of Mr. Deepak Taluja from the position of President and Chief Executive Officer –
KFC Business (designated as Senior Management Personnel), with effect from the close of
business hours of 24th July 2026.

2. Appointment of Mr. Vikrant Vohra, presently serving as Executive Vice President & Chief
Executive Officer - Pizza Hut Business (designated as Senior Management Personnel), to the
position of President & Chief Executive Officer - KFC Business (“President and CEO – KFC”),
with effect from 25th July 2026. Mr. Vikrant Vohra shall continue to be designated as Senior
Management Personnel of the Company.

3. Appointment of Mr. Saurabh Girdhar, presently serving as Chief Operating Officer – Pizza Hut
Business (Bangalore & Kerela), to the position of Assistant Vice President and Chief Operating
Officer – Pizza Hut Business (“AVP and COO – Pizza Hut”), with effect from 25th July 2026,
designated as Senior Management Personnel of the Company.
