Tata Communications Announces Key 
Leadership Appointments to Accelerate 
Growth in Network, Cloud  and 
Cybersecurity Businesses  
 
Mumbai, I NDIA , June 30, 2026  
Tata Communications, a leading communications technology player, today announced two 
strategic leadership appointments aimed at strengthening its capabilities across network infrastructure, cloud, and cybersecurity services.  
Rupesh Chokshi has been appointed as Executive Vice President and Global Business Head –  
Network Services and Chief Technology Officer.  In addition, Vivek Srivastava has been 
appointed as Executive Vice President and Business Head –  Cloud and Cyber Security 
Services. Both leaders will be part of the company’s Global Management Committee.  
Commenting on the appointments , Ganesh Lakshminarayanan, MD & CEO, Tata 
Communications,  said, “I am delighted to welcome both Rupesh and Vivek to the team as 
we strengthen our leadership bench to drive innovation and growth. Rupesh’s expertise in next -generation networks and AI -led infrastructure, along with Vivek’s strong leadership in 
clou d and cybersecurity, will further enhance our ability to serve as the operating backbone 
for enterprise AI  needs. ”
  
Accelerating Next -Generation Network Leadership  
Rupesh Chokshi brings over 25 years of global leadership experience across networking, cloud connectivity, cybersecurity, and software -defined infrastructure. A seasoned operator, 
he has successfully driven large -scale innovation by aligning product strate gy, engineering, 
and operations across complex enterprise and carrier environments.  
Most recently, he served as Senior Vice President and General Manager of Application 
Security at Akamai Technologies. Prior to that, he held senior leadership roles at AT&T. 
Rupesh will lead Tata Communications’ Network Services business, shaping product strategy, advancing AI -led network innovation, and strengthening the company’s global 
digital infrastructure capabilities.  
Rupesh Chokshi said: “Tata Communications has built one of the world’s most advanced global networks. As enterprises accelerate AI and cloud adoption, the need for agile, secure, and high -performance connectivity has never been greater. I look forward to building on 
this str ong foundation and driving the next phase of innovation and growth.”  
Scaling Cloud and Cybersecurity Capabilities  
 
 
Vivek Srivastava brings more than 27 years of experience in cybersecurity, cloud 
infrastructure, and digital transformation. He will be joining us  from Fortinet, where he 
currently serves as Country Manager  for India & SAARC  region , leading one of the fastest -
growing cybersecurity businesses  in the region.  
Over his career, Vivek has held leadership roles at CrowdStrike, Cisco, and IBM, building high -growth businesses and delivering large -scale transformations across enterprise and 
government sectors. He is widely recognised for his expertise in Cyber, AI security, post -
quantum cryptography, and next -generation digital infrastructure.  In his new role, Vivek will 
drive the expansion of Tata Communications’ cloud and cybersecurity portfolio, delivering secure, scalable, and future -ready solutions for enterprises.  
Vivek Srivastava said : “The next era of enterprise transformation will be defined by how 
effectively organisations harness cloud, AI, and intelligent digital infrastructure while embedding security and resilience at the core. Tata Communications has built a unique strategic posit ion in enabling this transformation globally, and I am excited to help shape 
and accelerate this journey of growth and innovation.”  
With these appointments, Tata Communications continues to reinforce its strategic focus on 
building a robust, AI -enabled digital ecosystem to support enterprises in an increasingly 
connected and dynamic global environment.
