May 1, 2026.

4. Brief Profile  Shri Rajesh Kumar Agarwal, Director (Finance) of 
Power Finance Corporation Limited (PFC) has been 
appointed as Nominee Director of PFC on the Board of 
REC Limited with effect from May 1, 2026. 
 
He has a distinguished career spanning over 31 years in 
the Power and Financial sectors. He holds a Bachelor’s 
degree in Commerce (Honours) and is a Member of the 
Institute of Cost Accountants of India.  
 
In his career at PFC since 2009, he managed various 
cross-functional teams and earned diversified 
experience in the areas like Corporate Accounts, 
Taxation, Fund Management & Banking, Lending 
Policy Compliance, Restructured Assets Management, 
Fund Raisings and Loan Disbursement. He has been 
instrumental in spearheading digital reforms and 
worked extensively towards excellence in financial 
reporting. He is also a distinguished Speaker at various 
Professional forums. 
 
Prior to joining PFC, he has rich experience in other 
Power Sector Companies like NTPC, NPCIL & DTL 
handling core finance functions along with other key 
areas like Tariff Matters, PPA Negotiations & 
Implementation of Ahluwalia Committee Reforms in 
Power Sector.
