Consolidated: In Q1 FY27, TMPVL delivered revenues of ₹95.8K Cr (+9.3%), EBITDA margin of 7.4% (-130 bps YoY) and EBIT margin of
2.4% (-90 bps YoY). JLR wholesales were down 9.2% YoY on account of temporary supply constraints, including a fire at a key

will focus on revenue growth whilst remaining prudent with increased focus on cost reductions and calibrated price actions.

impact performance in Q1 FY27. We delivered a resilient quarter and are confident to drive growth through new launches, debottleneck
supply constraints, and take focused actions to deliver margin improvements.”
