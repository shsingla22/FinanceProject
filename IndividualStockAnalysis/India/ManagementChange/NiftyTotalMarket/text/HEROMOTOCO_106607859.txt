Hero MotoCorp Announces Appointment of Sachin Agrawal  as Chief 
Technology  Officer (CTO) to Lead R&D Transformation and Power 
Next -Gen Mobility  
 
New Delhi, May 4, 2026:  Hero MotoCorp Ltd., the world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and 
scooters, has announced the appointment of Mr. Sachin Agrawal as 
Chief Technology Officer (CTO), effective May 21, 2026.  
 
Sachin will lead the next phase of the company’s R&D transfor mation 
across both the Hero Centre for Innovation and Technology (CIT) and the 
Hero Tech Centre Germany (TCG). With deep technical expertise and a 
strong strategic vision, Sachin  will play a pivotal role in advancing Hero 
MotoCorp’s ambitions in next -generation mobility, electrification, and 
alternative fuels  - redefining the future of mobility and beyond.  
 
“We are delighted to welcome Sachin to our leadership team. His deep 
exper tise across mobility technologies and proven track record of driving innovation at scale make him 
uniquely suited to lead both our technology vision and R&D organization. As we accelerate our journey 
towards electrification and next -generation mobility sol utions, his leadership will be instrumental in shaping 
a future -ready, customer -centric innovation agenda,” said Dr. Pawan Munjal, Executive Chairman, Hero 
MotoCorp .  
 
Sachin has over 2 9 years of experience in automotive Research & Development (R&D) and te chnology 
leadership, with deep expertise spanning product development, powertrain engineering, electric mobility, 
and advanced digital technologies. Prior to joining Hero MotoCorp, he served as Executive Vice President 
& Head – R&D and Technology at VE Com mercial Vehicles (Joint venture between Volvo Group and Eicher 
Motors) , where he led a large global engineering organization and delivered several industry -first 
innovations. He began his career at Daewoo Motors, contributing to the design of the Matiz cha ssis system 
in South Korea.  
 
Over the course of his career, Sachin has combined deep technical expertise with strategic leadership, 
driving end -to-end development across diesel, CNG, electric, and hydrogen platforms. He has also been 
at the forefront of co nnected mobility, AI/ML integration, and software -defined vehicle ecosystems, while 
fostering a strong culture of innovation and intellectual property creation.  
 
Sachin has served as Chairperson of the SIAM Frontier Technology & Innovation Group and led th e Volvo 
Group’s Global Center of Excellence for Medium Duty Engine  Development.  
 
An alumnus of IIT Bombay (M.Tech, Mechanical Design – Gold Medalist) and a Leadership Fellow from 
Carnegie Mellon University, Sachin is widely respected for building high-performing teams and driving 
transformative technology agendas at scale.  
 
With Sachin’s appointment, Hero MotoCorp further strengthens its leadership bench as it accelerates its 
R&D transformation and builds differentiated, future -ready technologies. His leadership will be central to 
the company’s ambition to redefine mobility through innovation, sustainability, and technology.
