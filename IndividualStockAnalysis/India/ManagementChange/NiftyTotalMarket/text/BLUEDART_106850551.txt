Blue Dart Reports Strong Financial Performance in Q2FY27
Quarterly Profit After Tax Stood at fi02 Crore, Reflecting Sustained Business Momentum

Mumbai, 6th November 2026: Blue Dart Express Limited, South Asia's premier express

air and integrated transportation and distribution logistics company, today announced
its financial results for the second quarter of FY2026-27, following a meeting of its
Board of Directors in Mumbai.
The Company reported a Profit After Tax (PAT) of �101.99 Crore, compared to �79.50
crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous financial year. Revenue from
Operations stood at �1,774 Crore, compared to ,1,549 crore in Q2FY26, registering a
year-on-year growth of 14.5%.
During the quarter, the company's performance was driven by an improved product mix,
better yield realisation and continued service optimisation, supported by a focus on unit
cost efficiency and disciplined execution across the business. Blue Dart also
strengthened customer engagement through targeted outreach to MSM Es and
businesses in emerging markets, deepening relationships and supporting their growth
ambitions.
Commenting on the results, Balfour Manuel, Managing Director, Blue Dart Express,
said: "Our 02 performance marks a significant milestone, reflecting the strength of our
business fundamentals and the discipline with which we continue to manage and grow
the business. This quarter's financial performance was supported by our focus on
operational productivity and the effective utilisation of our integrated network. As India's
commerce and supply chains continue to evolve, our priorities remain firmly centred on
strengthening our infrastructure, expanding connectivity and building capabilities that
allow us to serve customers with greater efficiency and reliability. We will continue to
invest in these areas while maintaining the operational rigour and service standards that
have defined Blue Dart over the years."

Following the close of the quarter, Blue Dart entered into strategic partnerships to
explore new avenues for parcel movement and deepen the company's reach into rural
India. These initiatives reflect the company's continued focus on building a more
connected, efficient and scalable logistics network to serve businesses across India.
Looking ahead, Blue Dart will remain focused on service excellence, operational
efficiency and disciplined investment in its network and capabilities. With its extensive
reach and integrated infrastructure, the company will continue to support customers'
supply chain requirements and enable access to markets across India and overseas. _

About Blue Dart:
Blue Dart Express Ltd., South Asia's premier express air and Integrated transportation & distribution
company, offers secure and reliable delivery of consignments to over 56,400 locations in India. Blue Dart
is a provider of choice for its stakeholders due to its customer-centric approach and aims to further
strengthen this partnership. As part of OHL Group's OHL eCommerce division, Blue Dart accesses the
largest and most comprehensive express and logistics network worldwide, covering over 220 countries
and territories, and offers an entire spectrum of distribution services including air express, freight
forwarding, supply chain solutions, customs clearance and other value-added services.
The Blue Dart team drives market leadership through its motivated people, dedicated air and ground
capacity, cutting-edge technology, wide range of innovative, vertical-specific products and value-added
services to deliver unmatched standards of service quality to its customers. Blue Dart's market leadership
is further validated by its position as the nation's most innovative and awarded express logistics company,
with recognitions including 'India's Best Companies to Work For' by The Great Place to Work® Institute,
India; 'Best Multinational Workplaces in Asia' by The Great Place to Work® Institute, Asia; 'Superbrand';
and 'Reader's Digest Most Trusted Brand'.
The company has also been recognised among the Fortune 500 India's Largest Corporations and Forbes
India's Super 50 Companies, underscoring its leadership, operational excellence and trusted market
position. Blue Dart continues to strengthen its governance, quality and information security standards
through globally recognised certifications from LRQA, maintaining both the ISO 9001:2015 Quality
Management System (QMS) certification and the IS0/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management
System (ISMS) certification.
The company's Diversity and Inclusion Initiatives have led to its recognition as one of India's 'Best
Workplaces for Women' in 2021 and 'Best Organisations for Women' in 2026 for the fifth time by ET Edge.
Furthermore. Blue Dart was awarded the 'Best In-House Legal Team' award in the logistics category at
The Economic Times Global Legal Awards 2024-2025. Blue Dart fulfils its social responsibility through its
Go Programs, namely GoGreen - Climate Protection, GoHelp - Drinking Water, Livelihood, Health and
Disaster Management, and GoTeach - Education.
