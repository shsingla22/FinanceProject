ABOUT REDTAPE LIMITED

From Footwear Heritage
to Lifestyle Scale
RedTape is one of India’s
leading footwear-led lifestyle
companies, built around the
aspirations of the valueconscious Indian consumer.
Our journey began with
footwear, but over the years,
we have evolved into a
wider lifestyle proposition
across footwear, apparel and
accessories.

The RedTape brand story began in London in 1994, followed by
our launch in India in 1996. In August 2023, following the demerger
of the branded consumer business, RedTape Limited was listed
independently on BSE and NSE to unlock Shareholder Value.
This gave us a sharper platform to scale as a focussed branded
consumer business.
Today, we serve dailywear, athleisure, semi-formal and outdoor
occasions through a growing portfolio of brands and categories.
Our business combines a widening capital-light retail network,
strong e-commerce presence and a shared sourcing, merchandising
and distribution platform. This helps us scale across markets and
channels while staying focussed on style, comfort, quality and value.

CHAIRMAN’S MESSAGE

Serving a More
Aspirational India

India is moving through a powerful
consumption shift, driven by rising
incomes, a young population and rapid
digital adoption. The opportunity is no
longer limited to the largest metros. tier 2
and tier 3 cities are becoming important
consumption centres, while larger urban
markets continue to deepen.

DEAR
SHAREHOLDERS,

The footwear and apparel industries are
gaining from the same structural forces.
Organised retail is expanding, e-commerce
is widening reach and consumers are
increasingly seeking branded products
that combine style, comfort, quality and
affordability.
RedTape’s
performance
during FY 2025-26 must be seen in this
larger context. Your Company remained
steady through the early part of the year
and closed with stronger momentum,
validating the relevance of our proposition
and the discipline of our operating model.

It is my privilege to address you at the
close of FY 2025-26, a year that reinforced
our confidence in the opportunity before
RedTape and the broader Indian economy.
Despite global uncertainty and periodic
cost pressures, India remained resilient,
supported by domestic demand, public
investment and improving consumption
sentiment. For your Company, with
offerings
built
around
aspiration,
accessibility and trust, this is a deeply
encouraging context.

I would also like to welcome the GST rate
reduction on footwear priced below `2,500.
This is a constructive step for the industry
and for consumers, improving affordability
and encouraging wider participation
in organised branded consumption.
For RedTape, whose portfolio is closely
aligned with accessible price points,
the reform supports volume growth
and strengthens the market for quality,
value-led products. It also reinforces the
importance of consumption-led sectors in
formalisation, employment generation and
domestic manufacturing.

MANAGING DIRECTOR’S MESSAGE

Reinforcing Trust
with Every Step

India is among the world’s largest footwear
markets, supported by rising incomes,
urbanisation, fashion awareness and
the shift towards organised branded
consumption. Apparel is undergoing a
similar change, with organised retail,
e-commerce and demand from tier 2
and tier 3 cities creating a wider market
for accessible fashion. RedTape is well
placed in this environment because our
proposition sits at the intersection of
aspiration, value and relevance.
During the year, we strengthened
this position. RedTape remained the
anchor brand, while Ozark, Mode and
Bond Street helped us participate
in outdoor, premium, casual and
occasion-led spaces. Each brand is
being developed on the same operating
backbone, allowing us to use common
strengths in sourcing, merchandising,
distribution and retail execution without
fragmenting the business.

DEAR
SHAREHOLDERS,
FY 2025-26 marked another important
stage in RedTape’s evolution into one of
India’s leading and most trusted fashion
and lifestyle brands. From a footwearled foundation, we are creating a broader
platform across categories, channels and
consumer occasions, while retaining the
value proposition that has made RedTape
relevant to millions of customers.

Our category mix also broadened.
Apparel now accounts for 34% of revenue,
with women’s and kids’ apparel offering
further headroom. Key launches such
as the Heat Tech range, where RedTape
became the second brand in India to
introduce this technology, reflect our
focus on creating distinctive propositions.
We are also expanding our accessories
portfolio, giving consumers more reasons
to engage with RedTape.
