Dear Stakeholders,
For more than three decades, Heritage
Foods has grown alongside India’s dairy
sector, guided by a simple belief: enduring
businesses are built on the trust they earn
from the people they serve. Today, that
responsibility extends across a significant
ecosystem. Heritage Foods procures
milk from around 3,00,000 dairy farmers
across nine states, processes it through 19
manufacturing plants in 15 locations with
an installed capacity of 2.95 million litres per
day, and reaches consumers through 7,500+
distributors and 2,25,000+ retail outlets
across 13 states.
Every product that carries the Heritage
name represents more than a product. It
reflects our responsibility towards farmers,
consumers, employees, communities and
the natural resources that support our
operations.
In FY2026, our 34th year of operations,
we recorded consolidated revenue of
₹4,526 crore, representing 9.47% year-onyear growth. We continued to strengthen
our value-added products portfolio,
commissioned a state-of-the-art ice cream
facility at Shamirpet with an annual capacity
of 24 million litres, and expanded our
presence in the premium ice cream and
desserts segment through our acquisition of
Get-A-Way. These developments form part of
our broader Vision 2030 ambition to become
the most admired dairy nutrition company
in India. Achieving this ambition requires
us to pursue growth while upholding our
responsibilities across the value chain.

At Heritage Foods, sustainability is
closely connected with the resilience
of our business. It is reflected in
the way we procure, manufacture,
distribute and govern, rather than
being treated as a parallel agenda.
During FY2026, we expanded our
renewable energy capacity to 14.37
MW, comprising 8.07 MW of solar and
6.30 MW of wind, with renewable
sources accounting for 37% of our
total electricity consumption. We also
continued to strengthen awareness
of ESG responsibilities across the
organisation.
At the same time, we recognise
the areas where further progress is
required. Renewable capacity has
grown faster than our overall energy
efficiency has improved, while Scope
2 emissions increased, highlighting
the need to translate capacity
additions into broader efficiency
gains. Our share of inputs sourced
from MSMEs and small producers
declined during the year, an area we
are reviewing with due attention. We
believe responsible reporting requires
us to present both our progress and
the areas that require continued focus.

Strengthening the Dairy
Ecosystem
Our relationship with dairy farmers
remains central to our long-term
sustainability. By working with around
3,00,000 farmers, Heritage Foods
contributes to rural livelihoods while
strengthening the foundation of our

of our business is closely linked to
the resilience of the farmers and
communities with whom we work.

Nutrition

At Heritage Foods, nutrition is
central to the long-term vision of
becoming India’s most admired
dairy and nutrition company.
Through its Vision 2030 roadmap,
the Company aims to sustain an
annual revenue growth trajectory of
15%–16%, supported by continued
product innovation, premiumisation
and its commitment to delivering
“nutrition with trust” to consumers.
The Company’s approach is centred
on developing differentiated,
nutrition-focused dairy offerings
that address evolving consumer
needs. Its Nourish+ range reflects

this commitment, with Heritage
Nourish+ High Protein Paneer
being a key launch during the year.
Developed for health-conscious
consumers seeking protein-rich food
choices, the product demonstrates
the Company’s focus on expanding
its presence in the protein-rich dairy
segment.

Fortification, protein and probiotics
are important areas identified for
the Company’s nutrition-focused
portfolio. By continuing to develop
its offerings in response to changing
dietary preferences, Heritage Foods
aims to support informed food
choices and strengthen its position
in the dairy nutrition category.

The portfolio also includes Heritage
Sampurna A2 Creamy Curd, offering
a rich, homemade experience, and
Sarvaguna and Sampurna Milk,
which provide versatile solutions
for everyday consumption. These
offerings reflect Heritage Foods’
continued focus on combining
nutritional value, quality and trust
through product innovation and
portfolio development.

The encouraging market traction
of its nutrition-focused innovations
reinforces the Company’s
confidence in their potential to
support broader market expansion
in the coming years. Continued
investment in product development
and premiumisation is expected
to support the development of
a differentiated portfolio and
contribute to the Company’s Vision
2030 ambition.

steadily evolving beyond a conventional dairy business into a nutrition-first food enterprise, with sustainability
serving as a core enabler of long-term growth and operational resilience.
The Company remains focused on delivering pure, healthy and convenient products that address evolving
consumer needs while continuing to empower the farming community that forms the foundation of its
business. Through product innovation, a consumer-centric approach and responsible business practices,
Heritage Foods aims to strengthen its position in dairy nutrition and create sustainable long-term value for
consumers, farmers and other stakeholders.
Through this approach, the Company seeks to advance its nutrition-focused ambitions while contributing to
its broader Vision 2030 objectives.

that stretches across kilometres and
tens of thousands of lives. Heritage

The journey from farm gate to
breakfast table is measured in
hours, not days. At every stage, the

Company’s choices shape outcomes
far beyond the product itself.
Heritage Foods’ integrated dairy
value chain embeds sustainability
at every stage—from empowering
farmers and strengthening rural
livelihoods to ensuring food
safety, optimising resource use
and enhancing traceability.
By connecting responsible
procurement with resourceefficient operations and reliable
distribution, the Company strives
to reduce environmental impact,
build supply chain resilience and
create enduring value for farmers,
consumers, communities and future
generations.
