Sub: Resignation as statutory auditors of the Company
Dear Sir,
We were appointed as statutory auditors of!AC International Automotive India Private Limited (Formerly Known
as Lumax Integrated Ventures Private Limited) ("the Company") in Annual General Meeting ("'AGM ") held on
September 18, 2024 till the conclusion of AGM to be held in financial year 2027-28.
Further to our discussion and your request letter dated August 07 , 2025, we completed our tenure as the statutory
auditors of Lumax Auto Technologies Limited (the Company's "Holding Company") in the Annual General
Meeting held on September 27, 2024, and we understand that the Company's Management is proposing to align
the statutory auditors of the Company with the Holding Company ' s auditor. Accordingly, we hereby tender our
resignation as the statutory auditors of the Company and request you to treat this as our letter of resignation with
immediate effect. We have also discussed this matter with the Management of the Company.
Kindly take thi s on your record and do the needful. As required by Companies Act, 2013, we shall file a statement
in Fonn ADT-3 in the due course and we will forward a copy to the Company for record.
We have completed the audit of financial statements of the Company for the year ended March 31 , 2025 vide
audit report dated May 22, 2025 and have not commenced the audit of the Company as at and for the year ended
March 31 , 2026. We have performed limited review for the quarter ended June 30, 2025 vide our review report
dated August 02 , 2025 on which we have issued unmodified conclusion .
We take thi s opportunity to reiterate our appreciation for the courtesies and co-operation s extended to us by your
organization .
Should you require any assistance in the future , please feel free to contact us .
