Sub: Change in Directors
In accordance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)
Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform you that Mr. P. M. Srinivas, Executive Director has tendered
his resignation as the Director of the Company effective close of business hours of October 30,
2025, owing to other professional commitments. Letter of resignation received from him is enclosed
herewith. Mr. P. M. Srinivas will continue to hold the senior management position as Chief Human
Resources Officer of the Company.
Further, pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)
Regulations, 2015, we would like to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Company, at its
meeting held today commenced at 2:48 P.M. and concluded at 3:24 P.M., have appointed Ms.
Rohini Venkateswaran as Additional (Executive) Director of the Company, for a period of five
years, effective October 31, 2025, subject to allotment of Director Identification Number by the
Ministry of Corporate Affairs.
A brief profile of Ms. Rohini Venkateswaran is given below:
Ms. Rohini Venkateswaran is an alumnus of S.P. Jain Institute of Management, Mumbai.
She currently serves as the Head of Sales function of the Company. Prior to this role, she was Vice
President & Country manager Procter & Gamble Gulf - East Gulf & Sales Strategy Gulf, where she
was responsible for 5 countries in Gulf- Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain and Qatar as General manager and
UAE as overall sales strategy leader. She joined Procter and Gamble India in 2005 in Sales, and has
diverse experiences in distributor management, modern trade and sales strategy and planning roles
across geographies including Dubai and USA. She has worked with all global customers and
channels including e-commerce. She is passionate about Equality & Inclusion and building long
term strategic partnerships with stakeholders. She is a graduate in Mechanical Engineering from RV
College of Engineering, Bangalore.
