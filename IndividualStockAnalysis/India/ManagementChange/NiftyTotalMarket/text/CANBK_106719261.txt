BRIEF PROFILE 
 
 
Shri. Sujit Kumar Sahoo, Chief General Manager & Group Chief Compliance Officer, has 29 
plus years of experience in Banking. He has a Master of Business Administration and Diploma 
in Treasury, Investment and Risk Management. He is also a Certified Associate of Indian 
Institute of Bankers. He worked from branch level to head office level in 8 different states and 
across rural to metro . He has administrative experience of heading Ranchi Circle Office and 
three Regional Offices at Madurai, Chennai and Sambalpur. At present, Shri. Sujit Kumar 
Sahoo is heading Compliance & RBS Wing, Head Office, Bengaluru.
