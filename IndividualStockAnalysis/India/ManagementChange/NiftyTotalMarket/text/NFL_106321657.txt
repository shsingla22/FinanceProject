Brief Profile

Shri B. B. Grover is a graduate in Electronics &
Instrumentation Engineering from Thapar
Institute of Engineering & Technology, Patiala,
and holds an MBA in Human Resources. With
over 36 years of extensive experience in the
Fertilizer industry, he has made significant
contributions throughout his career. He began
his professional journey with National Fertilizers
Limited (NFL) as a Management Trainee in
1988 and went on to serve in various capacities
across the company’s production units. Shri
Grover played a pivotal role in several key
projects, including: Transition of Ammonia
feedstock from fuel oil to natural gas,
Commissioning of Gas Turbine Generator–Heat
Recovery Steam Generator (GTG-HRSG),
Implementation of Distributed Control Systems
(DCS) and Emergency Shutdown Systems
(ESD), Deployment of machine monitoring
systems, process gas analyzers, and fire & gas
safety systems. During his tenure as Unit Head
of Panipat facility, he brought about systemic
changes resulting in sustained and efficient
operations.
