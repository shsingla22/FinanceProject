Brief profile

Mr. Gurdeep Singh holds a B.Tech. degree in Information
Technology from Edexcel University, London, along with
an MCA and MBA in IT. He is a Certified SAP Professional
with nearly 21 years of extensive experience in managing
end-to-end SAP implementation, consulting, and support.
His expertise spans Project Management, IT Infrastructure
Management, and delivering technology-driven solutions
across SAP S4 HANA, mobile and web application
development/integration, PLM (Siemens Teamcenter),
SharePoint, Salesforce (CRM), and related platforms.

organizations such as SKH Metals (Krishna Maruti Group
Ltd.), OMAX Autos Ltd., BPTP Ltd., and Crystal Crop Pvt.
