     Shri Diwakar Nath Misra 
     DIN: 07464700 
     
Shri Diwakar Nath Misra (DIN 07464700) is a senior Indian Administrative Service 
officer of the 2000 batch from the Assam-Meghalaya cadre, currently serving as 
Additional Secretary in the Ministry of Power. Born on 31 July 1974, he has built a 
distinguished career across diverse domains of governance, including Finance, 
Commerce, Defence, Petroleum and Cabinet Affairs. At the state level, he held 
leadership roles as Deputy Commissioner in Dhemaji and Golaghat districts of Assam 
and later as Commissioner & Secretary in Urban Development and Panchayat & Rural 
Development. At the national level, he has served as Additional Secretary in the 
Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance, and as Joint Secretary in the Ministry of 
Commerce & Industry. His deep expertise in the energy sector comes from his tenure 
in the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas, where he also represented the 
Government on the boards of major public sector undertakings such as Oil India 
Limited, Mangalore Refinery & Petrochemicals Ltd, and Indian Strategic Petroleum 
Reserves Ltd. Combining grassroots administrative experience with board-level 
corporate governance exposure, Shri Misra brings a unique blend of policy insight and 
operational leadership to India’s power sector reforms.
