Note 1 
 
Ms. Ina Bajwa is a senior HR leader with 22 years of experience across sectors 
including retail, e-commerce, technology, banking, consulting, and infrastructure 
in Indian and global organizations. She has held CXO and global leadership roles 
within the Tata Group, including CHRO of Tata 1mg, Chief Talent Officer at Tata 
Digital, and senior HR roles at Tata Communications, partnering closely with CEOs 
and CXOs on growth, transformation, and organization effectiveness. In her last 
stint, she was the Group Head of Talent & Engagement for Landmark Group Middle 
East, responsible for CEO, Executive Succession and talent management strategy 
for 9 retail brands. Earlier in her career, she has also worked with HSBC and the 
Essar Group, giving her strong exposure to large & complex enterprises. 
 
Ms. Ina holds a PGDM in HR from KJ Somaiya Institute of Management & Research 
and a bachelor's degree in communications & economics from Panjab University. 
She is an alumni of the Tata Group Strategic Leadership Program at Harvard 
Business School and holds multiple professional certifications in talent 
management & assessments, and executive coaching.  
 
                                                                                                                                          
 
 
She brings deep expertise in human resource management, including talent and
