Appointment of Mr. Sanjeev Gupta (DIN: 11709171) as an Additional Director in
the category of Executive Director of the Company

Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Mr. Sanjeev
Gupta has been appointed as an Additional Director in the category of Executive Director of
the Company and designated as “Deputy CEO”, for a period of three years w.e.f. October
29, 2026. The appointment is subject to the approval of shareholders or any other approval,
as may be applicable.
This is in furtherance to our intimation dated October 08, 2026, wherein it was informed
that Group ADP has nominated replacement for Mr. Riols, as Executive Director of the
Company designated as “Deputy CEO”.

Mr. Sanjeev Gupta, aged about 52 years, is a graduate of the French business school HEC.
He has over 25 years of professional experience in France, Gabon, Nigeria, United Arab
Emirates, and India. He has worked on large capital projects such as the development of
the Anyala offshore oil & gas block (Niger Delta, Nigeria) and the divestment of Shell’s
partnership in the Majnoon Oil field (Southern Iraq). He has held a multitude of roles ranging


from CFO, Project Finance, Treasurer and M&A. He has worked with corporations such as
Royal Dutch Shell, SLB (formerly Schlumberger), and Groupe ADP. He joined Groupe ADP
during January 2024.
