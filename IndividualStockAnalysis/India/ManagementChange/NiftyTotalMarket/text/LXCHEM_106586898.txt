Sub: Outcome of the board meeting  and disclosure under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Listing Obligation and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  (“Listing Regulations”)   
 
We wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today, i.e. Friday, April 
10, 2026, has considered and approved / noted the following:  
 
1. Resignation of Chief Financial Officer  
 
The Board of Directors took note of the resignation of Mr. Mahadeo Karnik from the position of Chief 
Financial Officer and Key Managerial Personnel of the Company. He shall be relieved from his duties with 
effect from the close of business hours on April 1 3, 2026.  The Board placed on record its sincere 
appreciation for the valuable contributions made by Mr. Mahadeo Karnik during his tenure with the 
Company.   
 
2. Appointment of Interim Chief Financial Officer  
 
The Board of Directors also approved the appointment of Mr. Harshvardhan Goenka, Executive Director of 
the Company, to take charge as Interim Chief Financial Officer  of the Company with effect from the 
commencement of business hours on April 14, 2026, until the assumption of office by the new Chief 
Financial Officer.   
 
3. Update on appointment of new Chief Financial Officer  
 
The management has identified a successor for the position of Chief Financial Officer, who is tentatively 
expected to join the Company on June 15, 2026. The proposal for his appointment shall be placed at the 
nex t meetings of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Audit Committee and the Board of 
Directors .
