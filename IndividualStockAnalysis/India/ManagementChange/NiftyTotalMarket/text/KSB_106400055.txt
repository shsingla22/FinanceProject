KSB Limited is a leading manufacturer of pumps, valves, seals and related
systems, delivering innovative fluid handling solutions across a wide range
of industries. With a strong presence in India, the company focuses on the
manufacturing, distribution, and servicing of pumps, valves, seal and seal
systems tailored to diverse applications.
KSB's products serve critical functions across sectors such as agriculture,
domestic water supply, boiler feed and condensate extraction, fire
protection, chemical processing, hot oil circulation, refinery and
petrochemicals, water and wastewater management, building services,
solar energy, non-metallic applications, life sciences, pulp and paper,
marine, nuclear facilities, etc.
Part of the globally renowned KSB Group, which was founded in 1871, is
built on the pillars of People, Passion and Performance. These core values

Established in 1960 and headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, KSB
Limited has made significant investments in state-of-the-art
technologies and infrastructure to meet the evolving demands of a
growing India. The company's six advanced manufacturing
facilities-located in Pimpri, Chinchwad, Shirwal, Vambori, Sinnar and
Coimbatore are equipped to deliver end-to-end solutions in fluid
mechanics from advisory and design to testing, fabrication, and quality
assurance.
