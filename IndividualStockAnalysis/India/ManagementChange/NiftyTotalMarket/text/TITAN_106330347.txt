Brief Profile
Mr. Sandeep Singhal co-founded Nexus Venture Partners in
2006. Nexus manages over $1.5 billion and has an active
portfolio of over 75 companies across the technology,
enterprise, consumer services, internet and mobile, alternate
energy and agribusiness sectors. Prior to Nexus, Mr. Singhal
was co-founder & CEO of Medusind Solutions, a leading
healthcare BPO acquired by a US private equity firm, and
previously a co-founder & MD of eVentures India, where he
invested in CustomerAsset and MakeMyTrip. He has held
senior roles at McKinsey & Company in their US offices.
Mr. Singhal has an MBA (with Distinction) from The Wharton
School with a dual Major in Finance and Marketing, and a BS
in Electrical Engineering from Stanford University.
