Brief Profile Shri Amol Babulal Taori (DIN: 11890590), Director 
(Finance) in POWERGRID is a Chartered Accountant with 
nearly 30 years of rich and diverse experience across Finance, 
Treasury, International Trade, Marketing, and Compensation 
Management.  
  
Prior to his appointment as Director (Finance), 
POWERGRID, Shri Taori served as Executive Director (In-
charge) – International Trade at Hindustan Petroleum 
Corporation Limited (HPCL), where he managed crude 
procurement of approximately 700 kbd, product imports and 
exports, chartering/shipping arrangements, and oil price risk 
management. During his career, Shri Taori has managed one 
of India’s largest corporate treasuries for over a decade and 
spearheaded HPCL’s maiden USD bond issue, achieving one 
of the most competitive pricing spreads by an Indian issuer. 
Shri Taori has also served as a Director on the Board of 
HPCL Middle East FZCO, Dubai. 
  
Sh. Taori has been appointed on the Board of PTC India Ltd. 
w.e.f. from 23rd October 2026 as nominee of POWERGRID.
