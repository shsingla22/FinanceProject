4. These Unaudited Standalone Financial Results incorporate the relevant returns
of 132 branches including 4 Impaired Assets Recovery Branches, treasury
operations, credit card division and various returns of corporate office
departments reviewed by us. In the conduct of our Review, we have relied upon
the review reports in respect of non-performing assets received from the
concurrent auditors of 277 branches. These review reports cover 77.03% of the
advances portfolio of the Bank (excluding the advances of outstanding food
credit) including 54.09% advances, which have been covered by us, and 56.92%
of the non-performing assets of the bank including 52.92% which have been
covered by us, as at 30th September, 2026.

considered various returns of the remaining 611 branches including 8
Impaired Assets Recovery Branches. The returns relating to advances

portfolio, non-performing assets and provisions duly certified by various
Branch managers of the bank are also incorporated in the financial results.
The returns received from the Branches cover 22.97%of the advances portfolio
of the bank and 43.08% of non-performing assets of the bank.
