Mr. Suresh Eshwara Prabhala  
 
 
The Board of Directors has approved the appointment 
of Mr. Suresh Eshwara Prabhala as an Additional 
Director, designated as an Independent Director, on the 
recommendation of the Nomination, Remuneration and 
Compensation Committee (NRCC). His appointment is 
for a term of five years, subject to the approval of the 
shareholders through a postal ballot by way of a Special Resolution.  Suresh 
Prabhala is an experienced financial services professional with a total of 28 years of 
experience in the Indian financial services industry, and the last 22 years focused on 
leading and managing private equity investments in India.  
 
His investment focus is on privately held, mid -market companies that are family -
owned and run, with core sectors of focus being manufacturing, healthcare and 
business services. Currently, Suresh Prabhala is the CIO at 2380 Capital – a newly 
established Alternative Investment Fund. Previously, he was General Partner and Co -
Founder of ADV Partners Capital Management (ADV), an Asia -focused private equity 
firm with total assets under management of   US$ 1.25 billion.  
 
Prior to co -founding ADV, in 2009, Suresh set up the India business as Managing 
Director and Head of India for Mount Kellett Capital Management, a global private 
equity fund with US$ 8 billion under management, where he was also a member of 
the Global Investment Committee. Immediately prior to that, Suresh was Executive 
Director and India head for Principal Investments at JPMorgan. He was part of the 
group’s Asia Management Committee and also on the Management Committee of 
JPMorgan India. Suresh began his career as a credit analyst at CRISIL and worked at 
Arthur Andersen in its Corporate Finance team.  
 
Suresh has an MBA from the Indian Institute of Management (IIM) Calcutta and a 
bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering from the Delhi College of Engineering 
(DCE).  Suresh is an active angel investor and enjoys spending time with start -up 
founders and early -stage funds. He is an active member of YPO (Chennai and 
Mumbai chapters) and the Indian Venture Capital Association/ IVCA.  Outside of work, 
Suresh enjoys cycling and playing chess.
