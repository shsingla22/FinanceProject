TVS Strengthens Its Presence in Nepal with the Launches of TVS
Apache RTR 160 2V Fi and TVS Raider iGo
~ Two new products - performance-driven, youth-centric, tech laden motorcycles

Kathmandu, September 21, 2025: TVS Motor Company (TVSM), a leading global
automaker that operates in the two and three-wheeler segments, today unveiled the
TVS Apache RTR 160 2V Fi and the TVS Raider iGo, in Nepal. Both these products
bring cutting-edge technology, innovative design, and improved riding experience to
customers, further expanding the company's diverse product line in Nepal.
Part of the legendary Apache series, the TVS Apache RTR 160 2V Fi is designed with
a strong racing heritage and is aimed to offer a thrilling riding experience. The
motorcycle offers an unmatched combination of speed, performance, and safety,
considering the dual disc brakes, single-channel ABS, connected digital cluster, AHO
LED headlamp, and three ride modes (Urban, Sport, and Rain). Performance
enthusiasts who seek thrills and everyday practicalities are its target audience. TVS
Apache has been the product of choice of 6.5 million customers, globally.
The TVS Raider iGo appeals to young, tech-savvy riders in Nepal. The Raider iGo,
which operates on the same platform as its wildly popular domestic version, features
a TFT digital cluster, iGo start-stop technology, a sporty split seat, muscular styling,
and dynamic ride quality. The motorcycle redefines the commuter market, offering an
excellent option for Gen Z riders with its is an excellent option for Gen Z riders as it
redefines the commuter market by offering comfort, performance, and smart features.
The TVS Apache RTR 160 2V Fi will be available in 3 colours – Matte Blue, Racing
Red and Black. The Radier iGO will be available in 2 colours – Nardo Grey & Wicked
Black.
Commenting on the launch, Mr. Rahul Nayak, Senior Vice President – International
Business, TVS Motor Company, said, “Customers in Nepal value style, technology,
and performance. Both the products that we launch today – the TVS Raider iGo and
TVS Apache RTR 160 2V Fi - combine cutting-edge features, connected technology,
and a unique riding experience. Couple with a robust sales and service network, we
are confident that these new motorcycles will readily become the product of choice for
customers in their respective segments.”
Mr. Sahil Agrawal, Executive Director, and Mr. Akhil Gupta, Director at
Jagdamba Motors, said, “TVS Motor in Nepal begins an exciting new chapter with
the TVS Apache RTR 160 2V Fi and TVS Raider iGo. These motorcycles will offer
superior performance and design to the market in addition to reflecting the evolving
goals of our young riders. Given TVSM's robust engineering and commitment to
customer satisfaction, we believe these new models will have a significant impact on
the market in Nepal and attract both Gen Z riders and performance enthusiasts.”
