Subject: Intimation of appointment of Director (Projects) of the Company 
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, this is to inform you that Ministry of Railways vide its order No. 
2025/E(O)II/40/6 dated 19.03.2026 has approved the appointment of Shri Prem Singh Meena (DIN:  
10855590), Executive Director, RITES Limited as Director (Projects) of RITES Limited with effect 
from the date of assumption of charge of the post till the date of his superannuation i.e. 30.06.2028 
or until further orders, whichever is earlier.  
 
The Board of Directors of the Company in its meeting held today i.e. March 19, 2026 have passed 
the resolution for appointment of Shri Prem Singh Meena (DIN: 10855590) as an Additional Director 
designated as Director (Projects) from the date of his assumption of charge to the post i.e. March 19, 
2026. 
 
Further, Dr. Deepak Tripathi (DIN: 10090267), currently serving as Director (Technical) holding 
additional charge of post of Director (Projects) has relinquished the additional charge of the post of 
Director (Projects) of the Company and Shri Prem Singh Meena (DIN:  10855590) has assumed the 
charge of Director (Projects) of the Company with effect from March 19, 2026. 
 
In compliance with BSE Circular no. LIST/COMP/14/2018-19 dated June 20, 2018 and NSE Circular 
no. NSE/CML/2018/02 dated June 20, 2018, and as per the confirmation received from Shri Prem 
Singh Meena, this is to confirm that he is not debarred from holding the office of director by virtue of 
any SEBI order or any other such authority. A brief resume of Shri Prem Singh Meena and other 
details as per applicable SEBI Circular are attached as Annexure-A . 
 
The updated list of Directors of the Company after appointment of Shri Prem Singh Meena (DIN: 
10855590), Director (Projects) is placed as Annexure-B.  
 
This is for your information and records.
