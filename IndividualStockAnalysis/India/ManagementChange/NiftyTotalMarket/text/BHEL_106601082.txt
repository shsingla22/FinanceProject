BRIEF PROFILE & DISCLOSURE OF MS. NIGAR FATIMA HUSAIN  
 
Ms. Nigar  Fatima Husain, an Indian Defence Estates Service Officer of 1996 batch, has - 
been i nducted  as Part Time Official (Govt Nominee) Director in the Board of BHEL 
w.e.f. 27.04.2026.  She is presently posted as Additional Secretary & Financial Advisor, 
Ministry of Commerce & Industry and Ministry of Heavy Industries, Government of 
India. She has done her B. Sc (Hons.) in Zoology from Kolkata University, LLB from 
Delhi University, and Post-Graduation  in Sociology from Dr . Harisingh Gour 
Vishwavidyalaya, Sagar (M.P .). 
 
She has served as Chief Executive Officer of Roorkee, Jhansi, Deolali and Mhow 
Cantonments. She was also Deputy Director General, Defence Estates, looking after 
Vigilance matters of the Department and as Add l. Director General, Defence Estates, 
she was responsible for land matters pertaining to the entire Defence land in the country. 
She has also served as Director, in the Department of School Education and Literacy 
and as Joint Secretary in the Ministry of Minority Affairs, Government of India.
