BRIEF PROFILE 
 
Dr. Padmini Srinivasan is a faculty in the Finance and Accounting Area at the Indian Institute 
of Management, Bangalore (IIMB). She is also the Chairperson Centre for Corporate 
Governance and Citizenship. Previously, she was the chairperson of the PGP (MBA) 
Programme. 
 
Dr. Padmini Srinivasan has a PhD from the National Law School of India Bangalore, is a Fellow 
member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, and an Associate Member of the 
Institute of Company Secretaries of India. She also holds a bachelor’s degree in commerce. 
 
As a faculty at IIMB, her teaching and research interests are in the areas of Financial 
Accounting and Reporting, Corporate Governance, Environment Sustainability, Corporate 
Social Responsibility and Risk Management. Her teaching career includes teaching in 
institutions such as the Indian Institute of Information Technology Bangalore and the 
University of Exeter, UK. She has presented papers at conferences and published research 
papers in academic journals. Dr. Srinivasan, in the past, has been a special invitee and a 
member of the Accounting Standards Board of India and the Auditing and Assurance 
Standards Board of India. 
 
Before joining IIMB, Dr. Srinivasan worked in the industry for more than fifteen years in 
various positions in Finance, Treasury, Planning, MIS, and Accounting. Her industry stint also 
includes consulting for a multinational firm in Indonesia. She has been a consultant and a 
corporate trainer to many organizations in the areas of finance and governance both in India 
and as well as abroad. As part of the executive education, she has designed and conducted 
programmes for several organizations in various industries such as power, infrastructure, 
banks, and others both in India and abroad. She has offered board level corporate governance 
programs for independent directors as well as executive directors.
