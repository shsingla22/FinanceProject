which has resulted in the Acquirer holding 100% (one hundred percent) of the equity

for the purposes of this Open Offer and will be acquiring the Equity Shares that are
tendered by the Public Shareholders in this Open Offer.

the Public Shareholders is more than the Offer Size, then the Equity Shares validly

equitable manner.
