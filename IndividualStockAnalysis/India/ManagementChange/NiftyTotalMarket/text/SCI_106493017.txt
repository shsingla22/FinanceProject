Brief Profile of Mr. Uttam Ghard e, Dy. CVO  
 
Mr. Uttam S adashiv Gharde holds a Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering and is a 
Marine Engineer (MEO Class I).  He has over 33 years of experience in various business 
verticals in Maritime Training , Fleet Personnel , Ship Operation , Dry  dock operation and 
Repair bill settlement .  He joined SCI as a Fifth Engineer Officer in August 1992 and began 
sailing on vessels from 18th August 1992. He joined  shore cadre on 7th April 2006 and was 
promoted to the position of Deputy General Manager in April 2019.
