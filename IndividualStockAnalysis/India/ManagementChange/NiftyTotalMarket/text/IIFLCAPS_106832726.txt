Sub: Appointment of Whole -Time Director (Key Managerial Personnel) of the Company  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI Listing Regulations"), we wish to inform you that the Board of 
Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today, i.e., Wednesday, October 28, 2026, has, 
inter alia, cons idered and approved the Appointment of Mr. Vasudev Jagannath as an 
Additional Director and Whole -time Director (Key Managerial Personnel) of the Company for 
a period of three years, subject to the approval of the shareholders, regulators and such other 
approvals as may be applica ble. 
 
As part of a planned leadership succession to ensure a seamless and orderly transition, the 
Board has also approved the re -designation of Mr. Vasudev Jagannath as the Managing 
Director of the Company with effect from January 1, 2027, for a period of three years, subject 
to the approval of the shareholders, regulators and such other approvals as may be 
applicable. This transition is intended to ensure continuity of leadership and the Company's 
strategic and business priorities. Mr. R. Venkataraman will conti nue to serve as a member of 
the Board of Directors.  
 
As the Company embarks on its next phase of growth, Mr. Vasudev’s nearly two decades with 
the Company, deep institutional knowledge and broad leadership experience position him 
well to lead the Company forward.  
 
In accordance with the BSE Circular No. LIST/COMP/14/2018 -19 dated June 20, 2018 and NSE 
Circular No. NSE/CML/2018/24 dated June 20, 2018, we confirm that Mr. Vasudev Jagannath  
is not debarred from holding the office of Director by virtue of any order passed by SEBI or 
any other authority.
