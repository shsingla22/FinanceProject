Brief Profile of Smt. Aparajita Ghosh, Chief General Manager (Finance)  
  
Smt. Aparajita Ghosh joined GRSE as Deputy General Manager (E -8) on 26 Sep 2014. 
She worked in Chartered Accountants firms, private sector company & Public Sector organization for almost 22 years before joining GRSE.  
 Smt. Ghosh holds dual professional qualifications, having as a Chartered Accountant 
(CA) in 1997 and Cost & Works Accountant (ICWA) in 1990.  
 Presently she is holding the Post of General Manager (Finance) in GRSE Ltd. She has demonstrated expertise in financial reporting, budgeting, taxation,  cost optimization, 
internal controls and compliance with statutory and regulatory requirements of the 
Company.  
 
With strong analytical abilities and a deep understanding of cost structure and financial systems, she contributes significantly to strategic decision making and organizational 
growth.  
 
Due to her vast experience and leadership qualities, she has been promoted as Chief General Manager with effect from 18 Nov 2025.
