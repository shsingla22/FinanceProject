Dear Madam and Sirs,
I hereby tender my resignation from the position of Director (Finance) and as a Key Managerial
Person of the Company in order to pursue career opportunities outside the JSW Group.
My association with the JSW Group for the past 13 years has been very enriching and
professionally satisfying, and I will always be grateful to Mr. Jindal and Parth for reposing their
trust and confidence in me over all these years.
My personal journey here - starting as the Head of Group Investor Relations, then heading M&A
and Corporate Finance for JSW Steel, and finally being appointed as the Chief Financial Officer
of JSW Energy Limited 5 years ago and being elevated to the Board pf Directors subsequently,
was one of continuous growth and learning.
During my tenure of past 5 years, JSW Energy has undergone a transformational growth - from
a 4.5 GW generation company, to a 30 GW diversified and integrated platform. I was fortunate to
helm leadership and build organizational capability to deliver this considerable scale up of the
platform, and am very proud of what has been achieved collectively as a team -when operational
capacity has surged by 3x, total assets on the balance sheet are up 4x, and the Market
Capitalisation of the Company has gone up by 1 Ox.
It has been an honor and a privilege to serve on the Board with esteemed colleagues like
yourselves -and I thank each one of you for your support, guidance and generosity of sharing
your knowledge and experiences, all of which have contributed in shaping my growth as a
business leader.
I am thankful to the leadership team and all my colleagues at JSW Energy for this enriching
association and professional support over the years, making the journey truly enjoyable. I leave
the JSW Group with a deep sense of gratitude and respect for the values and the people here
who are dedicated in their mission of nation building.
I will continue as the Director (Finance) till 31 st December, 2025 and provide all the necessary
support and cooperation for an orderly transition.
I wish the Company and all my colleagues continued success in this growth journey.
