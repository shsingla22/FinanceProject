3.  Brief profile Mr. V Venkatesan is a British national and an 
accomplished automotive industry executive with 
more than 45 years of experience in the commercial 
vehicle sector. A mechanical engineering graduate 
with a postgraduate qualification in business 
management, he has extensive experience spanning 
industrial engineering, project planning, business and 
product planning, project management, performance 
monitoring, corporate strategy, business 
development, acquisitions, divestments, and 
operational leadership. 
 
Mr. V Venkatesan began his career with Ashok Leyland 
as a Graduate Trainee and subsequently held 
leadership positions across several functions. During 
his tenure, he contributed to important technology-
transfer programmes involving Hino engines and ZF 
gearboxes, as well as the introduction of the Cargo 
intermediate commercial vehicle range. 
 
He is currently President of Hinduja Automotive 
Limited and serves on its Board of Directors. During his 
association with the company, spanning more than 
two decades, he has supported Ashok Leyland in the 
evaluation and execution of several strategic 
initiatives. 
 
To strengthen Ashok Leyland’s intermediate 
commercial vehicle portfolio beyond the Cargo range, 
Mr. V Venkatesan led the acquisition of Avia Motors in 
the Czech Republic. He managed the transaction from 
target identification through execution and 
subsequently served as Chief Operating Officer during 
the initial phase, helping stabilise the company’s 
operations. He later led Avia Motors’ divestment.
