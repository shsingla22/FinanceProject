Dear Sirs,
This is to inform you that the Board of Directors of Kansai Nerolac Paints Limited has in the Board meeting
held today i.e. on Friday, 25th July, 2025, inter alia considered and approved the change in Key Managerial
Personnel of the Company as follows:
(i) Retirement of Mr. P. D. Pai as the Chief Financial Officer:
Mr. P. D. Pai, Chief Financial Officer, will retire from the services of the Company with effect from the close
of business on 31st July, 2025.
The Board placed on record its sincere appreciation and gratitude for the valuable contribution made by
Mr. Pai during his long association of around 36 years with the Company.

Key Managerial Personnel from 1st August, 2025:
