Dear valued stakeholders,
On behalf of the Board of Directors and the Management Team, I am privileged to
present Annual Report for the financial year 2025-26. This year has been a defining
chapter in Bank’s journey, a period where we did not just navigate the evolving
economic landscape, but actively reshaped the strategies to emerge stronger,
more agile, and more purposeful than ever before.








  

The Macroeconomic Landscape

On Domestic Front, India has firmly established itself as the global engine of
growth. Despite the external volatility as well as uncertainties, our domestic
economy remained a "bright spot," with GDP clocking the impressive growth of
~7.6%1. This performance was fuelled by robust domestic consumption and
sustained public-sector investment in infrastructure over the year. This favourable
domestic tailwind provided the stable foundation for the growth.
The strong GDP growth, underpinned by inclusive economic drivers,
demonstrates the nation’s ongoing transformation towards a more prosperous
and self-reliant future. The focus on infrastructure and domestic demand not only
strengthens the economy today but also sets the stage for India to realise its
aspiration of becoming a leading global powerhouse by 2047, as envisaged
under the “Viksit Bharat” mission. As the nation moves toward a $5 trillion
economy by FY 2027-28, the Bank remains at the forefront of this journey.



The global economy in FY 2025–26 has operated under a cloud of shifting trade
policies and geopolitical complexities. While the world saw a moderate expansion
with Global GDP growth estimated at approximately 3.1 per cent, the momentum
remained uneven across regions. The Emerging Market Economies (EMEs) faced
headwinds of US Tariff and fluctuating energy costs due to West Asia Crisis, yet the
global narrative was one of cautious adaptability.

31st March, 2026.
