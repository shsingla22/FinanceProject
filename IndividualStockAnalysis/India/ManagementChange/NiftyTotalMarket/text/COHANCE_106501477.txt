Profile of Mr. Sunil Kumar Singh  
 
 Mr. Sunil Kumar Singh joined the Company in December 2025 as Chief 
Quality Officer.  
 
Mr. Sunil brings over 35 years of extensive leadership experience in 
pharmaceutical quality across APIs, formulations, CDMO and external 
manufacturing ecosystems. In his most recent role, he served as Global 
Head – Quality for the PSAI Business at Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, with 
an enterprise -wide mandate as Global Head – Internal Audit & 
Compliance . 
 
His experience spans diverse areas  of pharmaceutical quality and 
compliance . He has a proven track record in building future -ready quality 
organizations, strengthening regulatory trust,  and enabling business 
growth through science -driven quality transformation, digitalization and 
proactive risk governance. He is widely trusted by global regulators and 
senior  leadership to lead quality beyond compliance and position it as a  
strategic business enabler.  
 
With a proven ability to navigate challenges and deliver impactful results, 
he has worked with leading pharmaceutical multinational companies 
including Mylan, Zydus, Glenmark and Cipla.  
 
Mr. Sunil’s holds a  Master’s degree in Management  and B.Sc. (Tech) -  
Pharmaceuticals and Fine Chemicals.
