expansion opportunity, driven by rising

for seamless connectivity are reinforcing
the need for reliable and scalable telecom
infrastructure.

5G rollout

Enabling
India’s Digital
Infrastructure
Growth

The performance for the year was complemented by
strong sourcing capabilities and operational discipline,
even amidst a dynamic global and domestic economic
environment that continued to influence supply chains.
The global economic environment during the year
remained measured, even as it navigated persistent
geopolitical tensions, evolving trade dynamics and
periodic volatility in energy markets. While inflationary
pressures moderated compared to prior periods,
uncertainty continued to shape global growth outlook.
Against this backdrop, India sustained strong growth
momentum during FY26, with GDP expanding by
~7.7% (provisional estimates), supported by resilient
consumption and continued capital investment.
Inflation eased during the year and remained within the
RBI’s target band, enabling a calibrated monetary policy
approach to support growth while maintaining price
stability. On the fiscal front, the Government maintained
consolidation discipline, while continuing its strong
emphasis on infrastructure development. Looking ahead,
India is expected to maintain its strong growth trajectory,
with RBI projecting GDP growth at ~6.6% in FY27,
reinforcing its position as one of the fastest-growing
major economies globally.
Digitialisation - a key theme
India’s infrastructure-led growth model remains
a structural driver of its economic trajectory.
Continued public investment, with capital expenditure
at over `11 Tn in FY26 is driving expansion across roads,
railways, logistics and urban infrastructure.
Urbanisation and formalisation trends are further
reinforcing this transformation driving demand for
seamless digital access.
Additionally, a key structural theme underpinning India’s
growth is the rapid expansion of its digital economy.
Digital adoption into everyday economic activity
continues to deepen across payments, commerce, public
services and enterprise operations, supported by strong
policy initiatives and the scale of India’s digital public
infrastructure. This digital shift is further complemented
by the continued rapid rise in data consumption and
rollout of 5G networks, enabling faster, more reliable and
high-speed connectivity.

The Government’s continued support for maintaining a
three-private-player market structure, including the grant

improved the long-term outlook of the sector.
Your Company witnessed healthy demand for new
towers and co-location additions, translating into robust
operational performance during the year. The growth

densification as data consumption growth was recorded at
25%. Our ability to deliver at scale, combined with a sharp

enabled us to further strengthen our position as a
preferred infrastructure partner in the telecom ecosystem.
Secular Growth Environment
Demand for data continues to remain strong with
ongoing migration from 2G to 4G and 5G, growing 5G
smartphone shipments and underlying increase in usage
for smartphone customers. The average monthly data
consumption per user for the quarter ended March 2026
touched ~26.7 GB, growing 20% YoY. In the same period,
growth in the total data consumption was 31%.
Total 5G customer base stood at 427 million as of
March 2026, with an addition of 182 million seen over the
previous year. To support this rising adoption, telecom
operators rolled out ~56,000 5G base stations during
the year compared to ~40,000 during FY25, bringing
the total count to ~531,000. The sustained deployment
of 5G equipment is a key contributor to our overall
loading revenues.

FY26 was another strong year for
your Company, underpinned by the
robustness of our business model and
our ability to consistently deliver value in
a dynamic operating environment. The
year was marked by execution excellence
and sustained momentum in network
deployments across our key customers.
