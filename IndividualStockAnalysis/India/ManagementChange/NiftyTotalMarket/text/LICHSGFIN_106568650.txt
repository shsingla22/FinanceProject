Brief Profile of Mr. ] Sangameswar 
Mr. ]. Sangameswar is the Chief Risk Officer of LIC Housing Finance Limited, bringing over three decades 
of extensive experience across risk management, credit appraisal, business development, finance, and 
operations within the housing finance sector. 
He has a proven track record of strengthening enterprise risk frameworks, enhancing governance 
standards, and ensuring regulatory compliance. As CRO, he is responsible for overseeing the Company’s 
risk management function, including credit risk, operational risk, and portfolio monitoring, with a focus 
on building a resilient and sustainable risk culture. 
Over the course of his career, Mr. Sangameswar has held key leadership roles across credit, recovery, 
project finance, and business operations, and has successfully managed large and diverse portfolios across 
multiple geographies. He has been instrumental in maintaining strong asset quality, optimizing cost of 
funds, and driving business growth through prudent risk practices. 
He has also contributed to industry-level initiatives, including participation with the National Housing 
Bank in standardization of documentation. His leadership has consistently delivered improvements in 
portfolio performance, operational efficiency, and stakeholder value. 
Mr. Sangameswar is an Associate Member of the Institute of Cost Accountants of India (AICWA). He holds 
a Master’s degree in Commerce from Osmania University and has completed an Applied Finance program 
from the Indian Institute of Management, Calcutta.

Profile of Jimit Shah 
Jimit N. Shah is an experienced Cyber Security leader with over 15 years of expertise in 
Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC), information security, and technology risk 
management. Currently serving as Chief Information Security Officer at Aditya Birla Housing 
Finance Ltd., he has a strong track record in implementing ISO 27001 standards, managing cyber 
security operations, regulatory compliance, vendor risk, and data protection frameworks. 
He has held leadership roles at reputed organizations including IndusInd Bank, HDB Financial 
Services, Reliance Industries, and PwC, with hands-on experience in audits, application security, 
cloud security, and risk assessments. Jimit is skilled in driving security strategy, managing large 
teams, handling regulatory audits, and presenting to senior management and board members. 
With an MBA in Information Systems and ISO 27001 Lead Auditor certification, he brings a strong 
blend of technical expertise and business understanding, particularly within the financial services 
domain. He is seeking senior leadership roles such as Virtual CISO, CISO, or Data Protection 
Officer where he can contribute strategically to organizational security and compliance.
