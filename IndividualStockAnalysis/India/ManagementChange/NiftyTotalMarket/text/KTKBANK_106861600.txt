Reg.:  Disclosure  under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (L isting Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirement s) Regulations, 2015: Change in Senior Management of the Bank  
 
Appointment of  Mr. Arul Raj Gomez A  as the Chief Compliance Officer  of the Bank  
 
Pursuant to  Regulation 30 and other applicable provisions of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  (Listing Regulations) , this 
is to  inform that the Board of Directors of the Bank at its meeting held today , i.e., on 
November  15, 2026  has appointed Mr. Arul Raj Gomez A , General Manager,  as the 
Chief Compliance Officer  of the Bank,  for a period of 3 (Three ) years  with effect from 
November  23, 2026.  His appointment is in terms of RBI Circular No.: RBI/DoS/2026 -
27/408  DoS.CO.PPG.2/11.01.005/2026 -27 dated July 31, 2026 . 
 
Mr. Arul Raj Gomez A is also designated as the Principal Officer of the Bank (in -charge 
of AML Cell and KYC functions) for monitoring and reporting of all transactions and 
sharing of information to Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) -India as required under the 
law (Know Your Customer (KYC) Norms  /Anti -Money Laundering (AML) Standards / 
Combating of Financing of Terrorism (CFT) / Obligation of Banks under P revention of 
Money Laundering Act (PMLA) , 2002).  
 
Further, in terms of SEBI Master Circular No.: SEBI/HO/49/14/14(7) 2025 -CFD -
POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 , the details of Mr. Arul Raj Gomez A  are 
furnished in the Annexure.  
 
Consequent to the above appointment and elevation of Mr. Vinaya Bhat P J from 
General Manager cadre to Chief General Manager cadre, Mr. Vinaya Bhat P J will take 
up other responsibilities and will hence, cease to be the Chief Compliance Officer of the 
Bank and the Principal Officer of the Bank  (as required under the law (Know Your 
Customer (KYC) Norms  /Anti -Money Laundering (AML) Standards  / Combating of 
Financing of Terrorism (CFT) / Obligation of Banks under PMLA, 2002) with effect 
from November 23, 2026.  Karnataka Bank Ltd.
