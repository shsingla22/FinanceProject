4. In light of above, their reclassification from the 'Promoter' category

The Board of Directors further considered the declarations given in the said 
request letters received from the Outgoing Promoters as: 
a. Outgoing Promoters are not associated with the business of the Company in 
any manner whatsoever. They do not exercise any control over the Company, 
directly or indirectly, and do not have any influence over the business and 
policy decisions made by the Company. Further, Outgoing Promoters are not 
involved in the day-to-day activities of the Company. 
b. Outgoing Promoters satisfy the conditions prescribed under Regulation 31A of 
SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, including below: 
i. together, do not hold more than 10 (ten) percent of the total voting rights 
of Company; 
ii. do not exercise control over the affairs of the Company, whether directly 
or indirectly; 
iii. do not have any special rights with respect to the Company through 
formal or informal arrangements , including through any shareholder 
agreements ; 
iv. are not represented in any capacity on the board of directors of the 
Company (including through any nominee director) ; 
v. are not acting as key managerial personnel in the Company; 
vi. are not 'wilful defaulters' as per the Reserve Bank of India guidelines; and 
vii. are not fugitive economic offenders. 
After consider ing the above declarations, the Board was of the view that the 
aforesaid requests meet the requirements specified under Regulation 31A of the 
SEBI (LODR) Regulations . Thereafter , the Board has approved the proposed 
reclassification of the promoters of the Company , from "Promoter/Promoter 
Group" to "Public Shareholder(s)", subject to the approval from both the Stock 
Exchanges i.e. BSE Limited & National Stock Exchange of India Limited.

Switzerland. This strategic investment in N-Dream is part of KPIT's roadmap to 
enable Automotive OEMs enhance the driver & passenger experience in the 
Cockpit of the Future. KPIT will offer complementary software integration & 
validation services to N-Dream's Automotive clients. Both parties will collaborate 
towards offering value-added data products for Automotive OEMs, thereb 
enabling them to create additional monetizable experiences & features.
