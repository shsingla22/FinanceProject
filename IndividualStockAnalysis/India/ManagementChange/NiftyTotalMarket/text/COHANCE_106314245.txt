Profile of Mr. Yann D'Herve
Yann brings strong leadership capabilities, backed by a
rich and diverse background across multiple businesses
and roles. His experience spans manufacturing, strategic
management, and commercial operations. With a career
across multiple industries, including pharmaceuticals,
healthcare, and chemicals. He has significant experience
working with global markets, including India. Yann
began his career in manufacturing, gaining hands-on
experience in production and operations. He later moved
to the commercial roles with increasing responsibilities
handling drug substance, drug products and excipients.
Yann joins us from Evonik, where he held a variety of leadership roles over the years. Most
recently, he served as Senior Vice President and General Manager of the Healthcare division,
overseeing 2,600 employees across 9 manufacturing sites. In this role, he had full P&L
responsibility, including for the CDMO business, with all teams and functions reporting directly to
him. Yann holds a degree in Chemistry and an MBA.
