Annexure-1  
 
 
Brief profile of Shri Chandran Durai Daniel 
 
Capt. Chandran Durai Daniel has assumed charge as Director (Bulk Carrier and Tanker 
Division) of The Shipping Corporation of India Ltd. (SCI) with effect from 5 May 2026. 
Capt. Chandran D. Daniel is an alumnus of NIT Trichy. A graduate Mechanical Engineer. 
After a short stint in Auto Mobile Industry, Capt. Chandran joined SCI in 1989 as Trainee 
Nautical Officer and steadily progressed through the ranks after serving all type of vessels. 
Over the course of a distinguished maritime career spanning approximately 36 years, he has 
held a wide range of key positions both at sea and ashore, demonstrating exceptional 
professional versatility and leadership. 
In 2004, he transitioned to shore-based assignments within SCI, where he undertook 
several senior management roles across diverse functional domains. During his tenure, Capt. 
Chandran also served in SCI’s all branch offices including overseas office in UK. 
In addition to his corporate responsibilities, Capt. Chandran serves as a Member of the 
Governing Council of Narottam Morarjee Institute of Shipping , an institute set up under joint 
auspices of MoPSW & Indian Shipping Industry, imparting training & advance diploma 
courses in the field of shipping & logistics.
