-------------We wish to inform regarding the outcome of the 38th Annual General Meeting held on Wednesday,
August 20, 2025, as detailed below :
1. Re-appointment of Shri Vikram Saha

Shri Vikram Saha (DIN–10597814), liable to retire by rotation has been reappointed as an
Executive Director of the Company.
2. Re-appointment of Shri Arvind Narayan Yennemadi

The Shareholders have approved the re-appointment of Shri Arvind Narayan Yennemadi
(DIN: 07402047) as an Independent Director of the Company for a 2nd term of three years
with effect from the conclusion of this 38th Annual General Meeting (i.e. August 20, 2025)
until the conclusion of the 41st Annual General Meeting for FY 2027-28.
The said re-appointment was recommended by Nomination Remuneration & HR Committee
and the Board of Directors of the Company at their respective meeting held on June 25,
2025.
The details of aforesaid appointment as per the requirement under SEBI Master Circular No.
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR /P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 are given at Annexure-1.
3. Appointment of Shri Swarupananda Mallick

The Shareholders have approved the appointment of Shri Swarupananda Mallick
(DIN:11164699) as an Independent Director of the Company for a period of three (3)
years commencing from August 21, 2025 upto August 20, 2028.
The said appointment was recommended by Nomination Remuneration & HR Committee
and the Board of Directors of the Company at their respective meeting held on June 25,
2025.
The details of aforesaid appointment as per the requirement under SEBI Master Circular No.
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR /P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 are given at Annexure-2.


4. Cessation of Office of Shri Anup Sankar Bhattacharya

The Members of the Company at their 35th AGM held on September 07, 2022, had
approved the appointment of Shri Anup Sankar Bhattacharya (DIN: 02719232), as an
Independent Director of the Company, for a tenure upto the conclusion of the Annual
General Meeting of the Company of the year 2024-25.
Accordingly, the tenure of Shri Anup Sankar Bhattacharya as an Independent Director
of the Company is completed upon the conclusion of AGM held today, i.e. August 20,
2025. Consequently, Shri Anup Sankar Bhattacharya ceased to be Independent Director
of the Company.
The details as per the requirement under SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR
/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 are given at Annexure-3.
5. Appointment of Secretarial Auditor

Pursuant to the recommendations of the Audit Committee and the Board of Directors,
the shareholders at the 38th Annual General Meeting held on August 20, 2025 have
approved the appointment of M/s. Kedarnath & Karthik, a Firm of Practicing Company
Secretaries (Firm Registration Number: P2023KR098600), Bengaluru, as the
Secretarial Auditors of the Company for a term of 5 (Five) consecutive financial years
commencing from FY 2025-26 till FY 2029-30.
The details of aforesaid appointment as per the requirement under SEBI Master
Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR /P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 are given as
Annexure-4.
