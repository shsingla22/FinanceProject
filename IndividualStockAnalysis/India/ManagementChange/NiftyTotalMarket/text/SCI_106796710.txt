Brief profi le of Shri Vipul Singhal  
 
Mr. Vipul Singhal belongs to the Indian Railways Traffic Service (IRTS) of 2008 batch. He 
has completed B.Tech . from Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Kharagpur in Ocean 
Engineering and Naval Ar chitecture. Presently, he is working as Director in Ministry of Ports, 
Shipping & Waterways.  
In addition to the current portfolio, he has also been involved in policy formulation role in the 
Ministry of Railways in the domain of passenger marketing which involved policies related to 
various issues regarding passenger fares and ticketing including digital ticketing initiatives.  
He has more than 17 years of experience in the logistics, transport & railways sector in 
various domains including passenger as wel l as freight train operations, safety in train 
operations and planning for removal of infrastructural bottlenecks for efficient transport 
solutions.  
He has attended numerous formal trainings from institutes of repute such as Indian Institute 
of Management  (IIM), Calcutta, High Speed Rail study visit conducted by Japan International 
Cooperation Agency (JICA) in Japan, and field attachments in various sectors including 
Ports, Iron Ore mines, Container Depots etc.
