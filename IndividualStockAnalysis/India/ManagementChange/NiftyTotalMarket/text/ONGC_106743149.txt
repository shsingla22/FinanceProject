c. Brief profile   Dr. Archna Thakur holds Doctorate in International Politics from Himachal Pradesh University and is a Gold 
Medalist in Master's in Political Science. She has over 20 years’ experience in Academics, Management, 
Administration and Social Services. 
  
 Prior to joining the Company, Dr. Thakur served in various roles, namely Principal of the Vocational Training 
Institute, Department of Technical Education (Polytechnic), Head of the Department of Social Science with 
a reputed International School, Marketing Director with electronic media houses in Himachal Pradesh, and 
Consultant with the National Rural Livelihood Mission (NRLM), Himachal Pradesh. 
  
 Dr. Thakur has been associated with several NGOs and has actively contributed to initiatives focused on 
women’s empowerment, social awareness, community welfare, and social development.
