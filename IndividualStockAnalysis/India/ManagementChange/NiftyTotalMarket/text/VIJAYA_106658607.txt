Subject: Resignation from the position of Chief Financia’ Officer (CFO) of the
Company
Dear All,
I hope this letter finds you well. I submit my resignation from the position of Chief
Financial Officer (CFO) at Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Limited (‘Vijaya’).
This decision was not an easy one to make, as my time at Vijaya has been an incredibly
rewarding experience both professionally and personally. Working alongside a talented
team and contributing to the growth and success of the Company has been an honor.
I am committed to ensuring a smooth transition and will assist properly in the handover of
my responsibilities and will make sure all pending projects are properly handed off to the
appropriate team members. I would request you to relieve me on or before July 24, 2026.
I want to extend my gratitude to the Board of Directors and the entire leadership team for
your support and guidance during my tenure. I am proud of what we have accomplished
together, and I am confident that Vijaya will continue to thrive in the future.
I look forward to staying in touch and wish the Company continued success. Thank you
again for the opportunity to contribute at Vijaya.

Thank you.
Sincerely,

Ankit Shah
Chief Financial Officer
