ACME Solar signs PPA with NTPC
for 400 ISTS-connected FDRE Project
GURUGRAM, OCTOBER 30, 2026: ACME Solar Holdings Limited (ACME Solar) through its subsidiary,
ACME Marigold Urja Private Limited, has signed a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) with NTPC Limited
(“NTPC”) for a 400 MW Inter-State Transmission System (ISTS)-connected Firm and Dispatchable
Renewable Energy (FDRE) project under NTPC’s FDRE-01 tranche at a tariff of INR 4.37 per unit.
The tariff for this project has been adopted by Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC). It
would be connected to Raghanesda substation on the ISTS network, for which final grant of
connectivity has been already secured. The Company has already secured a lease more than 800 acres
of land for this project and is on track for scheduled commissioning as per PPA timelines.
This FDRE project will integrate renewable energy generation with appropriate energy storage
solutions to deliver reliable and dispatchable power throughout the day, while meeting the offtakers’
power requirements during peak demand hours. It would ensure a reliable supply of renewable
power, with a minimum annual Capacity Utilisation Factor (CUF) of 40% and an obligation to meet
90% of the peak power requirement for a cumulative duration of 4.5 hours daily across the morning
and evening peak hours..
Following this PPA, the under construction PPA-locked contracted portfolio now stands at 4,710
MW out of total under construction contracted capacity of 5,380 MW.
