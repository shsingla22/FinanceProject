Brief Profile  of Smt. Madhumita Daolagupu , Part Time Non -Official (Independent) 
Director , Hindustan Copper Ltd  
 
 
Born on 19.10.1963, Smt. Madhumita Daolagupu is B.Sc. and M.Sc. with 37 years of experience 
in teaching, administration and managing financial affairs. Smt. Daolagupu has served as an 
Assistant Professor, Associate Professor and Principal of Haflong  Government College, Assam. 
Also, she served as the President of the Governing Body in Government Model College, Assam. 
During her tenure she also managed the financial affairs of the college, arranged maintenance 
and upkeep of college buildings, conducted selection for teaching & non -teaching posts.
