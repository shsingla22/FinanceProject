All Businesses deliver a broad-based performance ahead of the festive; Studded-led Jewellery growth and
robust performances across Watches, EyeCare, TEAL and International Business power overall results

Bengaluru, Sth November 2026

Titan Company Limited ('Titan'), announced its Q2FY27 results today. Key highlights {Consolidated performance)
compared to Q2FY26:

•

Financial Performance: Total Income was ~20,533 crores, up 25% for the quarter, sustaining the
healthy growth momentum of the past periods

•

Profitability: Profit before Tax was ~2,329 crores at 11.3% margin, recording a 53% increase. Adjusting
for the impact of custom duty increase in gold for the quarter, the Profit Before Tax was ~1, 793 crores
at 8.7% margin, up 18% for the period

Jewellery: Jewellery portfolio grew 24% to ~17,495 crores (exc/. Bullion and Digi-gold soles), powered by

•

'Festival of Diamonds' and brand-level promotions that drove studded growth in the early thirties,
underscoring resilient consumer demand even as the festive calendar shifted to Q3FY27

•

Watches: Led by continuing premiumization trends and enduring consumer preference for analog
timepieces, the Watches portfolio clocked one of its strongest quarterly performances, growing 27% to
~1,881 crores

•

EyeCare: EyeCare clocked a robust 28% growth to ~281 crores, driven by focused execution centered
on a multi-brand approach, an upgraded store network and a sharper merchandise portfolio enhancing
the overa ll customer experience

•

Titan strengthens watchmaking capabilities with Vetra: Marking a milestone in its horological
journey, Titan introduced Vetra, its in-house movement, advancing its expertise in movement
development and innovation

•

Tanishq marks 30 years of trust, craftsmanship and meaningful connections: Celebrating three
decades of being part of women's journeys, Tanishq honored the key milestones, aspirations and
cherished moments that have shaped its enduring legacy

About Titan:

Titan Company Limited ('Titan), a joint venture between the Tata Group and the Tamil Nadu Industrial
Development Corporation ('TIDCO'), commenced its operations in 1987 under the name Titan Watches Limited .
In 1994, Titan diversified into Jewellery ('Tanishq') and subsequently into EyeCare. Over the last three decades,
Titan has expanded into underpenetrated markets and created lifestyle brands across different product
categories including Fragrances ('Skinn'), Indian Dress Wear ('Taneira'), thoughtfully designed Women Bags
('lrth') and recently in Lab grown Diamonds ('beYon' ). Titan is widely known for transforming the watch and
jewellery industry in Ind ia and for shaping India's retail market by pioneering experiential reta il in all of its
consumer product categories. Learn more about Titan on: https://www.titancompany.in/
