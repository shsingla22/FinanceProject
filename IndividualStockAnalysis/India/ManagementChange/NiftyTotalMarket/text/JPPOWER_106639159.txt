Jaiprakash Associates Limited (the "Borrower") is a shareholder in Jaiprakash Power
Ventures Limited (the "Target Company") and had created a pledge over 1,20,05,09,431
equity shares ("Shares") and non-disposal undertaking over 10,21,88,566 shares, which
constitute 19.01% of the issued and paid-up share capital and 19.01% of the total diluted
share/voting capital of the Target Company in favour of lDBl Trusteeship Services Limited
(the "Security Trustee") acting as the Security Trustee for the lenders under the facility
provided to the Borrower.
Pursuant to the directions and requirements stipulated by the Hon'ble NCLT, Allahabad
Bench at Prayagraj, vide its order dated lVlarch 17, 2026, and in accordance with the
unanimously approved Minutes of the Joint Lenders' lVleeting ("JLIVI') dated lVay 15, 2026,
the pledge over all the Shares was released on IVay 21 ,2026.
ln terms of Regulation 29 (2) read with Regulation 29 (4) of the Takeover Code, shares that
are given upon release of encumbrance (by way of pledge) shall be treated as a disposal.
Accordingly, this disclosure is being made in respect of the release of encumbrance created
over the Shares in favour of the Onshore Security Agent for the benef it of the relevant lenders.
