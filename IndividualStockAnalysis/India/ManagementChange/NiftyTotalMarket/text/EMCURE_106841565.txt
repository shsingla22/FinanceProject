Management Commentary
Satish Mehta, Chairman, Managing Director and CEO, Emcure Pharmaceuticals Ltd., said, " Q2 was another
strong quarter for Emcure, with broad-based growth in revenue, profitability and earnings.
Revenue grew 24% year-on-year to INR 28,205 Mn, with India growing 12% and International 34.5%. EBITDA
grew 31% to INR 5,748 Mn, with margin at 20.4%, an expansion of about 100 basis points, driven by
productivity gains and operating leverage. PAT grew 29% to INR 3,251 Mn. We expect business momentum to
sustain into H2FY27.”
