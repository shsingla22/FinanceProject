Ref.: Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of SEBI (Listing Obligations and

Pursuant to the provisions as referred above, we hereby inform that, based on the 
recommendation of Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board of Directors of the 
Company at its meeting held today, has approved the following changes in the Board 
composition and senior management of the Company: 
  
1. Designating Mrs. Padma Chandrasekaran, Independent Director as Non-Executive 
Chairperson 
 
The Board has approved the designation of Mrs. Padma Chandrasekaran, Independent 
Director, as the Non-Executive Chairperson of the Board, in order to further strengthen the 
governance framework of the Company. 
 
2. Designating Mr. P. Ranjit as Executive Vice Chairman  
 
The Board has approved the designation of Mr. P Ranjit (DIN: 01952929) from the position of 
Managing Director to Executive Vice Chairman (Whole-time Director) of the Company, for a 
term of five (5) consecutive years commencing from January 22, 2026 to January 21, 2031, on 
such terms and conditions as recommended by the Nomination and Remuneration 
Committee, subject to approval of the shareholders by way of postal ballot. 
 
Mr. P Ranjit will continue to serve as a member of the Board.  
 
3. Appointment of Mr. Rampraveen Swaminathan as Managing Director  
 
The Board has approved the appointment of Mr. Rampraveen Swaminathan (DIN: 01300682) 
as an Additional Director, designated as Managing Director of the Company, for a term of five 
(5) consecutive years commencing from January 22, 2026 to January 21, 2031, on such terms 
and conditions as recommended by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, subject to 
approval of the shareholders by way of postal ballot. 
 
4. Resignation of Mr. N R Kannan, KMP (Executive Director) 
 
Mr. N. R. Kannan, Key Managerial Personnel of the Company, has tendered his resignation 
vide letter dated January 19, 2026, citing personal and family reasons, effective from the close 
of business hours on April 30, 2026.
