About Sanoﬁ Consumer Healthcare India Limited (SCHIL):
Sanoﬁ Consumer Healthcare India Limited (SCHIL) emerged as a distinct legal
entity following its demerger from Sanoﬁ India Limited on 1st June 2024. SCHIL
operates independently, with a dedicated focus on the consumer healthcare sector.
With an agile business model and legacy brands, SCHIL is one of the leading players
in India's consumer healthcare market. Leveraging its global experience, SCHIL
aims to enhance consumer wellbeing through its product portfolio encompassing
Allergy Management, Digestive Wellness, Pain Care and Multivitamins. SCHIL’s key
brands include Allegra®, DePURA®, Avil® and Combiﬂam®. These brands are
dedicated to building a healthier future by empowering people to take control of
their health, through simplifying self-care and by addressing their everyday health
concerns.
