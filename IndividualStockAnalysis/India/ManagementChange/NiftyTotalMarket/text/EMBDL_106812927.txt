Dear Sir/Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule Il and other applicable provisions of the SEBI LODR Regulations, 
we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Company (“Board”), at its meeting held on October 9, 
2026 (which commenced at 03:15 PM and concluded at 04:10 PM), has, inter alia: 
(i) noted the resignation of Mr. Sachin Shah from the position of ‘CEO & Executive Director’ of the Company, 
with effect from the close of business hours on December 31, 2026. A copy of his resignation letter, along 
with the reason stated therein, is enclosed herewith. While Mr. Shah will cease to hold the position of 
‘CEO & Executive Director’ of the Company, he shall, however, remain associated with the Company and 
Embassy Group as Senior Advisor to the Chairman and the Managing Director & CEO of the Company 
with effect from January 1, 2027; 
(i) based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and with a view to 
ensuring continuity and stability of leadership, approved the re-designation of Mr. Aditya Virwani (DIN: 
00027674), Managing Director of the Company, as “Managing Director & CEO” of the Company, with 
effect fromJanuary 1, 2027, for the remainder of his existing term of appointment, which is up to February 
24,2030, on the existing terms and conditions of his appointment. 
The Board noted that, under the leadership of Mr. Aditya Virwani, Managing Director, and the guidance of Mr. 
Jitendra Virwani, Chairman, FY 2025-26 was a year of strong execution and record performance forthe Company. 
The Company entered FY 2026-27 with a robust pipeline of 11 projects across Bengaluru, Mumbai and its initial 
pilot in the National Capital Region. Mr. Aditya Virwani has been instrumental in driving the Company’s 
performance thus far, and his passion and dedication towards achieving excellence are exemplary. He is 
presently fully focused on shaping the Company’s next phase of growth — strengthening core businesses, 
unlocking new markets and driving innovation across the platform. 
The Board was, therefore, of the view that the re-designation of Mr. Aditya Virwani as “Managing Director & CEO” 
will ensure continuity and stability of leadership, facilitate a seamless transition, and further strengthen strategic 
alignment, decision-making and execution. The Board accordingly believes that, under the capable leadership 
of Mr. Aditya Virwani as Managing Director & CEO, the Company will continue to pursue and achieve the highest 
standards of performance, execution and sustainable growth.

which has been planned taking in confidence Mr. Jitendra Virwani, Chairman, and Mr. Aditya Virwani, 
Managing Director of EDL, so a smooth transition can happen in a responsible manner. While | am 
stepping down as Executive Director and CEO of Embassy Developments Limited, my association 
with Embassy Group shall remain as senior advisor to the Chairman and MD of EDL. 
For almost two years now, | have had the privilege of working closely with Jitu and Aditya. Jitu -1am 
grateful for your friendship, guidance, support and confidence in me. | am so proud at how Aditya 
has seamlessly handled the integration of IBREL and Embassy into what stands now as Embassy 
Developments Limited, and which is on track to achieve Rs. 8000 crores in pre-sales this year. He 
has the vision, acumen and empathy to shape EDL’s next phase of growth and take it to new heights 
across its three main markets. | wholeheartedly support his proposed nomination for the added 
position of CEO and am there to externally support him as senior advisor. 
| stepped into the role as Executive Director / CEO of the erstwhile Indiabulls Real Estate Limited 
(“IBREL”) in February 2023 at the time the management change was happening and IBREL was to be 
run by an independent board. It was a different company at the time with no leader, low morale, 
projects incomplete and balance sheet issues. Over the 2 years leading up to the completion of the 
merger, we fixed these problems and got IBREL to a much better position by the time the merger was 
completed in January 2025. | would like to place on record my sincere gratitude to Mr. K. G. 
Krishnamurthy, who was the Chairman of the independent board, when | began my journey as CEO. 
Thank you for your wisdom, experience, and in guiding me and having faith in me. | also am deeply 
grateful for the trust, support, and freedom extended to me during my tenure by the other 
independent board members. They were challenging times and | am glad that these are currently 
behind the Company. 
I would be remiss if | did not take a moment to thank my colleagues, the management team, and the 
employees (past and present) for their commitment and devotion to seeing EDL succeed. You are 
the heart and soul of this business and thank you for supporting me through these past few years. | 
step down as CEO with immense pride in what we have accomplished together. 
I wish the Company, the Board, the leadership team, and the entire Embassy Group continued 
success and growth in the years ahead. While there are challenges, we are at the cusp of breaking 
out and | know you are in capable hands with Aditya at the helm and under Jitu’s overall experience. 
It has been a privilege working with EDL/IBREL and | look forward to pursuing other interests and 
spending time with my family. 
Thank you and Best wishes. 
Sachin Shah
