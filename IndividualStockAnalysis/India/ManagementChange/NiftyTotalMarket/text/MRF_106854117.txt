PRESS RELEASE
Financial Performance
MRF's consolidated total income is Rs 8791.58 Crores for the quarter ended
30th September, 2026 as compared to Rs.7489.00 Crores for the corresponding
quarter ended 30th September,2025. The consolidated profit before tax stood at Rs
521.81 Crores for the quarter ended 30th September, 2026 as compared to Rs.700.45
Crores for the corresponding quarter ended 30th September, 2025.
Provision for tax for the quarter is Rs. 134.82 Crores. After making provision for tax,
the consolidated net profit for the quarter ended 30th September, 2026 is Rs 386.99
Crores as compared to Rs. 527.43 Crores for the corresponding quarter ended 30th
September, 2025.

Operations
The Company delivered strong double digit sales growth in the 2nd Quarter. We were
able to capitalize on sustained demand for the Company's products from both OE and
replacement segments.
The steep increase in prices of raw materials due to global uncertainties adversely
affected profitability during the quarter. Price increase and cost saving measures by
the Company helped to mitigate cost pressures.
The Company anticipates continued disruptions and volatility in commodity markets.
Raw material and logistics costs are expected to remain elevated. We will continue to
take measured steps to address these challenges.
Dividend
The Board of Directors have declared an interim dividend of Rs 3/- (30%) per share of
Rs.10 each for the financial year ending 3pt March, 2027.
