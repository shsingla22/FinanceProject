Brief profile of Mr. Vijay Jerome
Shri Vijay Jerome brings over two decades of global technology leadership experience spanning
the consumer goods, retail, and digital innovation sectors. His career began with IBM, where he
built a strong foundation in enterprise technology and systems architecture. He went on to hold
senior leadership roles at Mondelēz International (formerly Kraft Foods), Pernod Ricard, and H&M
Group, leading large-scale business technology transformations and modernization initiatives
across geographies. Shri Vijay is widely recognized for his strategic foresight, sustainability-led
innovation, and ability to translate emerging technologies into measurable business value.
