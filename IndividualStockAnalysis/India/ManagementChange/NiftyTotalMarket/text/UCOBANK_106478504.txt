Madam/ Dear Sir
Re: Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015 Resignation of Shareholder Director.
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR Regulations”), we
hereby inform that Ms. Rachna Khare, Shareholder Director of the Bank, has
tendered her resignation vide letter dated 25.12.2025 from the Board of the Bank,
including membership of all Board Committees.
Ms. Rachna Khare has resigned due to her engagement in a new assignment, as
stated in her resignation letter.
In terms of sub-para 7B of Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI LODR
Regulations, the requisite details are provided in Annexure – I. The resignation
