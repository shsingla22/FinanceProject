Dear Sir,
Sub: Resignation as a Non-Executive Director of Titan Company Limited
It has been my pleasure and privilege to serve on the Board of Titan Company Ltd. under
your Chairmanship over the last few quarters.
In view of the additional time commitments that have recently arisen, I will not be able to
dedicate sufficient time to my role as a Board Member of the Company.
Therefore, I hereby submit my resignation from the position of Non-Executive Director of the
Company, effective August 20, 2025.
I would like to thank you, my fellow Directors on the Board as well as the Management of the
Company, for providing me with the opportunity and the support to perform my duties.
Thank you and I wish Titan all success in the years to come.
