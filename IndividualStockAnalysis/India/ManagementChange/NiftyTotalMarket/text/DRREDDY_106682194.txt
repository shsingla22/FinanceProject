our achievements spanning financial, operational, and sustainability metrics.

value and integration of environmental, social, and governance (ESG)
considerations into our business.

Assurance Methodology

covering research and development, manufacturing, supply
chain operations, and marketing functions. The document
incorporates Statutory Reports and Audited Financial
Statements spanning April 1, 2025, through March 31,
2026, except where otherwise indicated. Environmental and
social performance metrics for joint ventures and associate
companies that are not consolidated within our financial

Intelligent Nerve Centres and Data Democratisation

AI as a Strategic Lever

Our enterprise data platform has fundamentally redefined
how data is accessed, governed, and leveraged across
the organisation. Historically, inconsistencies in product
data across markets constrained enterprise-wide visibility
and decision-making. Today, we operate on a unified data
foundation that delivers real-time visibility across sales,
P&L, and key operational metrics, establishing a single
source of truth.

We are reimagining the role of AI as a foundational capability
to transform how work is executed, decisions are made, and
expertise is scaled across the organisation. Our approach
focuses on three strategic applications of AI in enforcing
processes, enhancing productivity, and augmenting expertise.
