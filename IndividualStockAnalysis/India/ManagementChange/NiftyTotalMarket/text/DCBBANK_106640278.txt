Annexure I- Brief profile of Mr.  Krishnan Sridhar Seshadri  
 
Mr. Krishnan Sridhar Seshadri was the Chief Risk Officer of the Bank since May 2017 and has over 40 
years of banking experience, covering various aspects of branch banking including the role of branch 
manager involving origination and management of Loans a nd Advances, system administration, 
accounting and financial control etc. amongst other aspects. Prior to joining DCB Bank as Financial 
Controller in 2009, he was with ICICI Bank, having joined it from almost its inception, and earlier with 
Syndicate Bank and State Bank of India.   
 
Mr. Seshadri holds a Master’s degree in Commerce (M.Com.) and is a Certified Associate of the Indian 
Institute of Bankers (CAIIB).  
 
He does not hold directorship in any other company or body corporate.
