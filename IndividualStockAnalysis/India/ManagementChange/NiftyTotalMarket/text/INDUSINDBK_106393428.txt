Brief Profile

September 30, 2025
Appointment of Mr. Anand Vardhan as the
General Counsel and designated as the Senior
Management Personnel of the Bank, with
effect from September 30, 2025.
Mr. Anand Vardhan is a seasoned legal
professional with around 26 years of
experience.
Prior to joining IndusInd Bank, Mr. Anand
Vardhan was Group General Counsel at
Piramal Group. He Joined Piramal Group in
2013 and has hands-on experience in dealing
with various legal matters including diverse
deals. He has a proven track record in legal
documentation, litigation/ dispute resolution,
stressed accounts management, legal policies /
procedures, regulatory compliance and
strategic partnerships.
His previous roles include General Counsel at
SUN-Apollo Ventures, Associate VP &
Head – Legal at HDFC Property Fund and
Manager - Legal at ICICI Bank and Tata
Housing.
