Ahmedabad, 22 October 2026: Adani Total Gas Limited (ATGL), one of India’s largest CGD
companies, continues its mission of transforming India’s energy landscape through infrastructure
development across existing and new geographical areas, and new-age energy businesses. Today,
ATGL announced its operational and financial performance for the second quarter and half year
ended 30th September 2026.
“ATGL delivered a strong performance in Q2FY27, with volumes growing 12% and revenue rising 34%
year-on-year, supported by sustained CNG demand, deeper PNG penetration and disciplined network
expansion. This momentum enabled ATGL to achieve strong double digit EBITDA growth up by 12%
YoY.
This performance was achieved despite a challenging operating environment marked by the
continuation of West Asia crisis, elevated Brent and spot gas prices, currency depreciation and
reduction in APM allocation. Our diversified sourcing portfolio, mix of long-term contracts and


flexible market purchases along with calibrated price mechanism helped manage cost pressures,
maintained supply reliability, and sustained customer demand.
We remain focused on discipline capital allocation, volume growth, operational excellence combined
with digital enablement across CNG, PNG, e-mobility and biomass segments to create long-term
value for customers and stakeholders.” – Sanjay Pandita, CEO, ATGL

Key Business updates
➢ The West Asia crisis continued in Q2FY27, resulting in elevated Brent and spot gas prices,
while currency depreciation further increased the gas cost.
➢ Following withdrawal of temporary pooled gas support effective 5 July 2026, the Government
introduced a revised HPHT allocation framework, improving domestic gas availability for CGD
entities through a demand and shortfall-based methodology.
➢ ATGL continued to leverage a diversified sourcing portfolio, including increased domestic
allocation, new long and mid-term contracts and market purchases, to address demand
requirements, manage supply risks and maintain operational reliability.

➢ Revenue from operations rose by 34% in Q2FY27 and 31% in H1FY27, led by sustained volume

purchases.

volume growth.

About Adani Total Gas
Given its gas distribution, ATGL is authorised in 34 Geographical Areas and plays a significant role in the
nation’s efforts to enhance the share of natural gas in its energy mix. Of the 53 GAs, 34 are owned by ATGL
and the balance 19 GAs are owned by Indian Oil-Adani Gas Private Limited (IOAGPL) – a 50:50 joint venture
between Adani Total Gas Limited and Indian Oil Corporation Limited. Further, ATGL has formed 2 wholly owned
subsidiaries namely Adani TotalEnergies E-Mobility Ltd (ATEL) and Adani TotalEnergies Biomass Ltd (ATBL)
for its E-Mobility and Biomass Business respectively. ATGL has also formed a 50:50 joint venture, namely Smart
Meter Technologies Private Limited for its gas meter manufacturing business.
