Changes in Senior Management Personnel (“SMP”)

This is to inform that based on the recommendation of Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the
Board of Directors of the Company, at their meeting held today, has inter-alia:
a) Approved appointment of Mr. Sameer Chugh as General Counsel, India (SMP), effective 1st
December 2026. Details required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations for the
above matters are provided in Annexure A.
b) Noted the resignation of Ms. Shobana Nikam, Executive Vice President and General Counsel, who
has tendered resignation from the services of the Company with effect from end of business hours
of 31st October 2026, and shall consequently cease to be an SMP of the Company, effective the
aforesaid date.
c) Noted the completion of tenure of Ms. Archana Sasan, Interim General Counsel (in a consulting
capacity), with effect from end of business hours of 31st December 2026, and shall consequently
cease to be an SMP of the Company, effective the aforesaid date.
Details required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations for point b and c are provided
in Annexure B.
Further, pursuant to provisions of Regulation 30 r/w sub-para 7C of Para A of Part A of Schedule III of
SEBI Listing Regulations, copy of resignation letter is enclosed as Annexure C.
