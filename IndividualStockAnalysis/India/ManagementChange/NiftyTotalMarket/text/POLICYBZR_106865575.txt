PRESS RELEASE  
PB Fintech co -founder Alok Bansal to transition to Non -Executive Director  
Will step down from executive role on 31 March  2027; continues on the Board  
 
Gurugram, November 18, 2026:  PB Fintech Limited (NSE: POLICYBZR; BSE: 543390), the parent company 
of Policybazaar and Paisabazaar, today announced that co -founder Alok Bansal will step down as Executive 
Vice Chairman and Whole Time Director on 31 March 2027. He will continue on the B oard as a Non -Executive 
Director, subject to the shareholder approval.  
Alok co -founded Policybazaar with Yashish Dahiya in 2008. Over nearly two decades, he has built Policybazaar 
into India's leading insurance platform, playing a central role in the comp any's strategy, fundraising, corporate 
development and investor relations, including PB Fintech's public listing in 2021.  
Yashish Dahiya, Chairman & CEO, PB Fintech, said:  
“PB Fintech and Policybazaar would not have been possible without Alok. He first s hared his wish to step back 
five years ago, and I persuaded him to stay because I needed his support. I still do, and I am glad he will remain 
on our Board, while I understand his wish to step away from the day -to-day.  
It is never easy to be the number tw o in any business. Alok always gave me his candid counsel, and when we 
disagreed, he trusted me and backed the decision fully. That is a very rare quality. I am deeply grateful to him .”  
Alok Bansal said:   
“Building Policybazaar over the past 18 years with Yashish has been the defining privilege of my professional 
life. Founders are lucky to get most of the credit and sometimes, a few brickbats as well, but the reality is that it 
takes the collective energ ies of the team to build something meaningful. PB Fintech has achieved far greater 
scale than I ever imagined, thanks entirely to the dedication of our team, the trust of our customers, and the 
support of our investors and partners. The company is in a ver y strong position, with a robust leadership pipeline. 
I look forward to continuing to support PB Fintech as a member of the Board.”  
About PB Fintech   
PB Fintech Limited is the parent company of Policybazaar, India's largest digital insurance marketplace, and 
Paisabazaar, a leading consumer credit marketplace.  
The Board and management of Policybazaar place on record their appreciation for Mr. Alok Bansal’s 
contribution to the Company over the past 18 years.  
The Company looks forward to his continued association as a Non -Executive, Non -Independent Director, subject 
to shareholders’ approval, and to benefiting from his experience and institutional knowledge as Policybazaar 
enters its next phase.
