Pursuant to the Government of India’s Notification F. No. A-11011/06/2025-Ins.I dated December 01, 2025, 
Shri Ramakrishnan Chander, Executive Director (Investment – Front Office) & Chief Investment Officer and 
Key Managerial Personnel (“KMP”), Life Insurance Corporation of India (“the Corporation”), has been 
appointed as Managing Director of the Corporation in the pay scale of Rs. 2,05,400/- to Rs. 2,24,400/- with 
effect from the date of assumption of charge of office and upto the date of his attaining the age of 
superannuation (i.e., September 30, 2027), or until further orders, whichever is earlier. 
Accordingly, Shri Ramakrishnan Chander has taken charge as Managing Director of the Corporation on 
December 01, 2025. Consequent to his taking charge as Managing Director, he ceases to be Executive Director 
(Investment – Front Office) & Chief Investment Officer of the Corporation.

as amended from time to time, is enclosed as  Annexure – A .
