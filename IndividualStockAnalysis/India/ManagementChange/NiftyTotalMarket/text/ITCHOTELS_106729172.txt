3. Brief Profile  Mr. Ramakrishnan Chander ( age: 58 years) is the Managing 
Director of LIC. He is a Science Graduate, a Fellow Member 
of the Insurance Institute of India, and also holds a Post 
Graduate Diploma in Financial Management.  
Mr. Chander  joined LIC in 1990, brings over 35 years of 
experience across marketing, administration, and 
investment management. During his illustrious career, he 
has held several senior leadership positions, including 
Executive Director (Investment – Front Office), Ex ecutive 
Director of LIC’s Strategic Business Unit for International 
Operations, Chief Investment Officer, Senior Divisional 
Manager, and Regional Manager (Marketing and  
Pension & Group Schemes ). 
Mr. Chander  is also Non -Executive Director of LIC Cards 
Services Limited , LIC Mutual Fund Trustee Private Limited , 
and LICHFL Care Homes Limited , amongst others.
