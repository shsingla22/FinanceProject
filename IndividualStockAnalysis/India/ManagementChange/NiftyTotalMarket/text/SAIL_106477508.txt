PROFILE OF SHRIPRIYA RAN JAN, DIRECTOR IN CHARGE
fBOKARO STEEL PLANT). SAIL
Shri Priya Ranjan is a distinguished professional in the steel industry, presently
serving as the Executive Director (Works), Bokaro Steel Plant, Steel Authority of
India Limited (SAIL). With an illustrious career of more than three decades in
SAIL, he has held -several senior .positions of responsibility, including Executive
Director (Operations) at the Corporate Office, New Delhi, as well as various
leadership roles in technical operations, production planning, and corporate
strategy.
An alumnus, with a B.E. in Metallurgy from BIT Sindri and a Post Graduate
Diploma in Management (Finance as specialization) from IMT Ghaziabad, Mr.
Ranjan has seamlessly blended his technical expertise with strong managerial
skills. He started his career in non-ferrous Industry and worked in Copper and
Copper alloys sector for about 2 years in Mumbai before joining SAIL in 1994 as a
Junior Manager in the Steel Melting Shop at ISP, Bumpur, West Bengal. He has
worked in areas like Steel Making, Production, Planning & Control, assisted
expansion projects in existing Steel Melting Shop as well as upcoming expansion
in ISP, Bumpur. He has-worked in ED (Works) Secretariat as Technical Advisor
arid thereafter has been associated with successive Chairman & Managing
Directors at Corporate Office, New Delhi. Subsequently, he was ED (Operations)
with additional charge of Head of Directorate of Mines and Logistics at Corporate
Office, New Delhi. Through dedication, vision, and consistent performance, he has
risen through successive positions culminating in his current leadership role.
Throughout his career, -Mr. Ranjan has made significant coritributions to
Steelmaking Processes, Plant Operations, and Organizational Efficiency. During
his 7 years stint at Chairman & Managing Director's Office, he was entmsted to
look after all Steel Plants of S AIL, Mines, Collieries, smaller Units and operations
at ICVL, Mozambique. He has contributed towards process efficiency, coal blend
optimization, raw material security plan, improving mines' performance,

rooted in advancing ■ - operational excellence, driving innovation, safety,
