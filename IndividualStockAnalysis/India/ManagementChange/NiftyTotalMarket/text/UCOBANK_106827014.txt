Madam/ Dear Sir  
 
Sub : Change in the Composition of Board of Directors of UCO Bank  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 and 51 read with Schedule III of SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we inform that the Central 
Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub section (3) 
of section 9 of  the Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) 
Act, 1970, has nominated Shri Mohamed Ashra f J S as Director on the Board of 
UCO Bank in place of Shri Hari Har Mishra , with immediate effect and until further 
orders, vide Notification eF.No.6/2 /2022 -BO.I/3 dated 23.10.2026.  
 
Accordingly, Shri Hari Har Mishra  ceases to be Director on the Board of the Bank 
with effect from 23.10 .2026. We confirm that Shri Mohamed Ashra f J S is not 
debarred from holding the office of Director by virtue of any order passe d by SEBI 
or any other authority.  
 
The requisite details, as prescribed in the SEBI Master Circular dated 11 July, 2023, 
are enclosed herewith as Annexure I.  
 
This is for your information and record.
