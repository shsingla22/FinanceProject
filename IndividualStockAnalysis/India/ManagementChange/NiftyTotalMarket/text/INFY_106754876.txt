Mitrankur (Mit) Majumdar  
Executive Vice President and Global Head, SURE ( Services Utilities Resources and E nergy) , 
Infosys  
Mitrankur (Mit) Majumdar is the Executive Vice President and Global Head of the SURE segment at 
Infosys, leading the company's business across Services, Utilities, Resources  and  Energy . He 
collaborates with C -suite leaders across Global 2000 organizations and high -growth enterprises to 
shape business strategies , deliver transformative outcomes, and create lasting value through AI, 
data, cloud, and emerging technologies.  
With over 30 years of experience , Mit has evolved from a technology practitioner to a global 
business leader, bringing a distinctive blend of technical depth, strategic thinking, and market leadership. This broad experience enables him to help enterprises accelerate transformation, drive innovation, and achieve sustainable growth in an increasingly digital and AI -driven world.  
In addition to his role as the leader of the SURE segment Mit is also spearheads efforts to build and leverage strategic AI partnerships and ecosystems that strengthen Infosys' ability to deliver and scale Enterprise AI for clients globally. He also plays a pivotal role in advancing Infosys' Global 
Capability Center (GCC) pr actice, helping enterprises establish and transform GCCs into engines 
of innovation, talent, and business value. 
Prior to leading the SURE segment, Mit served as Global Head of Services at Infosys, where he led 
growth across travel and hospitality, information services, publishing, professional services, education, engineering, construction, and real estate industrie s. His deep understanding of 
industry transformation, coupled with a strong focus on AI and innovation, has enabled clients to build more resilient, adaptive, and future- ready enterprises.  
Mit also serves on the advisory boards of East Texas A&M University's College of Business and  
University of Maryland’s  Robert H. Smith School of Business . 
Mit holds a Bachelor of Engineering degree in Electronics and Telecommunication  and an MBA from 
McCombs School of Business at UT Austin . He has also completed a Global Leadership Program 
from Stanford University Graduate School of Business and a Leadership Program from Harvard 
Business School.
