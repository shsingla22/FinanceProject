Brief Profile of Capt. Pankaj Kumar  
 
Capt. Pankaj Kumar began his shipping career in 1995 as a cadet in SCI. After serving aboard 
different type of vessels, including tankers, bulk carriers and containers, he was promoted to the 
Rank of Master in 2005.  
 
He joined shore office as a Senior Manager in the Fleet Personnel Department. In 2016, he was 
moved to the Tanker Technical Department in 2016 and was later designated  Vetting -In-
Charge.  
 
While sailing, he completed his Economics degr ee and MBA (Operation ).He also passed 
Professional Qualifying Examination (PQE) by Institute  of chartered Shipbroker (ICS). And 
completed his LLB from K C Law College in Mumbai.  
 
In 2022, he went  to WMU, where he earned a Master's Degree in Maritime Affai rs. He was 
adjudged the best student in his specialization “Shipping Management & Logistics”.  
He is currently serving as DGM in Charge (SVC Technical and Commercial) in the Bulk and 
Tanker Division. With divisional order dated 01.06.2026, he has been assig ned to assume 
charge of Repair Bill and Dry Dock Cell in addition to his existing responsibilities.
