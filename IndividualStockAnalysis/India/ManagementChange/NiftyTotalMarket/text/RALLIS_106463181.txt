Mahesh  Girdhar  
 
Mr. Mahesh Girdhar currently serves as the Managing Director & CEO at EverEnviro Resource  
Management P rivate  Limited  and Mana ging Director at Indo Enviro  Integrated  Solutions  Private  
Limited . 
Mr. Mahesh Girdhar has over 30 years of global experience in business operations, strategy, and 
general management across environment, renewable energy, and agri -business sectors. Previously, 
he served as President – Crop Nutrition Business at Deepak Fertilisers & Petrochemicals and held 
leadership roles at Bayer Crop Science and Monsanto.  
Strongly entrepreneurial and result -oriented, Mr. Girdhar is recogni sed for his positive leadership 
approach and natural ability to engage, inspire, and develop high -performing teams. He has 
consistently demonstrated success in steering organi sations through growth, transformation, and 
capability development —anchored in strong governance, innovation and measurable outcomes.  He 
has extensive expertise in asset strategy, operational management, and portfolio expansion across the 
fertili ser, seeds, and crop protection segments, both within India and in international agri -business 
markets.  
Through his regular engagements with  several  industry  platforms,  including  the Confederation  of 
Indian Industry (CII), India Federation Green Energy, Fertilizer Association of India and  International, 
CropLife, National Seeds Association, and Association of Biotech Industries, Mr. Girdhar has been 
instrumental in shaping policy dialogues, fostering collaboration, and strengthening industry 
innovation frameworks.  
Mr. Girdhar  holds  a Postgraduate  Degree  in Agriculture  from  Haryana  Agricultural  University,  Hisar, 
and has completed the Advanced Management Programme at INSEAD, France.
