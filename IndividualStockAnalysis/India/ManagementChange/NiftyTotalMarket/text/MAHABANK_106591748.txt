Sub: Appointment of Shri Sushanta Kumar Mohanty  as Executive Director (ED) on 
the Board of the Bank  
 
With reference to above, we would like to inform that  Department of Financial Services 
(DFS) vide notification no. eF. No. 4/4/2024 -BO.l dated 15th April, 2026 has notified that  in 
exercise of powers conferred by the Clause (a) of sub -section (3) of Section 9 of the 
Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970 , read with 
paragraph 8 (1) of the Nationali sed Banks (Management and Miscellaneous Provisions) 
Scheme, 1970 (amended vide notification dated 17.11.2022), the Central Government has 
appointed  Shri Sushanta Kumar Mohanty , Chief General Manager, Bank of Baroda  as 
Executive Director  in Bank of Maharashtra for a period of three years with effect from the 
date of assumption the post , or until further orders, whichever is earlier.  
 
Shri Sushanta Kumar Mohanty  has assumed the charge as Executive Director of the Bank 
on 15.04.2026 . Brief profile of Shri Sushanta Kumar Mohanty  is attached herewith.
