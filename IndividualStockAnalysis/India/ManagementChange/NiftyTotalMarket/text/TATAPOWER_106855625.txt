Brief profile of Mr. Sanjay Banga  
 
Mr. Sanjay Banga is the Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director of Tata Power Renewable Energy 
Limited and currently holds the position of President – Renewables of The Tata Power Company Limited 
(“Tata Power”).  
 Mr. Banga previously served as President of the Transmission and Distribution businesses.  His 
responsibilities included the performance of distribution companies across Odisha, Mumbai, Delhi and 
Ajmer; the Mumbai transmission business; Powerlinks  Transmission Limited; and transmission assets in 
Uttar Pradesh.  
 
Mr. Banga also served as the Chief Executive Officer of Tata Power Delhi Distribution Limited (TPDDL), a notable Public -Private Partnership (JV) between Tata Power and the Government of the National 
Capital Territory of Delhi.  
 Mr. Banga began his career as an Engineer Trainee at National Thermal Power Corporation Limited 
(NTPC), where he was involved in the operation and commissioning of super thermal power projects from 
1989 to 1995. Befor e joining TPDDL in 2003, he worked with Reliance Energy (formerly BSES Limited) 
from 1996 to 2003, with responsibilities covering operations, maintenance, planning, design and project 
engineering for thermal power projects.  
 
Mr. Banga is a power -sector professional with more than three decades of experience across the 
operational and commercial management of distribution companies, business development, digital 
transformation, project management, change management, regulatory advocacy and stakeholder 
management. His experience with public - and private -sector electricity utilities in India has provided him 
with a broad understanding of the challenges facing the electricity distribution sector. He is also a regular 
speaker at seminars, workshops and panel disc ussions in India and abroad.  
 Mr. Banga is an alumnus of the National Institute of Technology, Kurukshetra, and holds a Master of Business Administration degree from the Faculty of Management Studies, University of Delhi. He has 
also completed the Senior E xecutive Leadership Program at Harvard Business School and holds alumni 
status.
