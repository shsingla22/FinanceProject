Foundation provides teleradiology and telemedicine services to poor areas in Asia that do not have 
access to high quality medical care. Her other interests include teaching. Dr. (Mrs.) Maheshwari has 
been runn ing In dia’s e -teaching programme for postgraduates in Pediatric Cardiology for over a decade.
