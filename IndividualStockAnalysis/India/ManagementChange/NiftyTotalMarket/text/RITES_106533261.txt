Subject: Change in Senior Management of the Company  
 
Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and 
amendments thereof, this is to inform you that following changes have taken place in the 
Senior Management of the Company:  
 
1. Shri Manish Tiwari, GGM/ES&T has taken over charge as Chief Strategy Officer 
(CSO) and consequently, shall ex-officio  function as Chief Operating Officer, RITES 
EXPOCON w.e.f. February 13, 2026. Therefore, he shall be part of the Senior 
Management Personnel of the Company with effect from February 13, 2026.  
 
2. Shri Rajesh Naik, Executive Director and CSO has been relieved from the RITES, 
pursuant to approval of appointment as Director (Projects) of Ircon International 
Limited. Therefore, he ceased to be the Part of the Senior Management of the 
Company with effect from February 13, 2026.  
 
In this regard, relevant disclosures pursuant to SEBI Circular are placed at Annexure -A. 
 
Kindly take this information on record and acknowledge the same.
