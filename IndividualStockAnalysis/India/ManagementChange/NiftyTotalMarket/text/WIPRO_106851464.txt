Brief Profile of Mr. Angan Guha : 
 
 
Angan Guha  is a seasoned technology and business leader with 
over 34 years of experience driving growth, digital transformation, 
and client success across multiple geographies. He is known for his 
results -oriented leadership with  a focus on building growth- oriented 
organizations.  
 
As the Global CEO & Managing Director of Birlasoft Ltd.,  a publicly 
listed entity , Angan led the Company through significant growth and 
value creation, strengthening its financial performance,  digital 
capabilities, and customer -centric culture.   
 Prior to Birlasoft, Angan spent over 28 years with Wipro, o ver this 
tenure at Wipro, he held multiple roles, each with progressively 
greater responsibilities.  He held several senior leadership roles, 
including CEO of the Americas 2 Business Unit and member of 
Wipro’s Executive Leadership Team .  
 
Angan led a $2.9 billion business with over 45,000 employees, with P&L responsibility across key industry 
verticals, including BFSI, Energy & Utilities, Manufacturing, Hi -Tech, and Canada Operations. Angan was 
responsible for implementing large- scale digital and AI transformation programs, as well as mission- critical 
infrastructure transformation engagements . He also  spearheaded the Technology and Operations Go- To-
Market strategy, focusing on large deals and delivering consistent growth . 
 
Beyond business success, Angan is deeply committed to giving back to society, ensuring that the communities we live and serve in are positively impacted.   
 
Angan holds a degree in Electronics Engineering from the University of Pune and has also completed programs 
in management from the Booth School of Business , University of Chicago.
