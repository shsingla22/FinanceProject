Mr. Jaluka has around 15 years of experience in finance, assurance, governance and  
risk management. He has been associated with the Company  / Siemens  group for over 10 years and

statutory audits, internal controls, risk assessment and listed    company audit environments.   
 Mr. Jaluka is a Chartered Accountant and Company Secretary and holds a Bachelor of Commerce 
degree from the University of Calcutta.
