the standalone financial results. This approach is consistent with the guidance issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India. The Company had assessed the impact of these
changes and provided an amount of~ 36.61 er and recognised the same for the quarter and nine months ended on December 31, 2025, as employee benefit expenses. Consequent to the
employee compensation restructuring and on actuarial valuation, the said liability is now determined at~ 12.23 er. The excess provision of~ 24.38 er has been reversed in the results for
the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026, as a change in estimate. The Company continues to monitor the finalisation of the Central and State rules and the clarifications that may be
issued by the Government on the new labour codes and will recognise the impact of changes in the estimates in that period, as needed.
