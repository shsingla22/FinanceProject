Brief Profile (in case of appointment)  He is a finance professional and a Fellow Member of the Institute

various departments, including Internal Audit, Taxation, 
Finance, and Accounts. 
 
In addition to his responsibilities in Finance, he also headed the 
IT Department at SML for more than five years (2007–2012) and 
later led Procurement and Vendor Development for over five 
years (2019–2025). He served as the Chief Financial Officer 
(CFO) and also held the position of Chief Risk Officer (CRO) at 
SML Isuzu Limited until September 30, 2025, following a post-
retirement extension of approximately five years. From October 
2025 until May 2026, prior to superannuating from his corporate 
employment, he was involved in advisory functions at SML 
Mahindra as Advisor to the CEO.

Advisory Consortium Pvt. Ltd.”, for providing single-window 
business solutions. He has had a longstanding association with 
ASSOCHAM and currently serves as Chairman of the 
Chandigarh Council and a Member of the National Managing 
Committee of ASSOCHAM.  He is also a Life Member of the 
Institute of Materials Management (IIMM), and also served as 
Vice President (2022-23) and Central Council Member of ICMAI.
