Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform the below changes in the Senior Management Personnel of 
the Company: 
 
(i) Mr. Nilesh Pendse is appointed as Category Lead – Vaccines of the Company with effect 
from February 1, 2026. Mr. Pendse shall accordingly be a Senior Management Personnel 
of the Company effective the said date. 
(ii) Mr. Manikantan Seshadrinathan – Category Lead, Hospitals & Rare Disease will transition 
to another role within the Pfizer Group Companies effective February 1, 2026, and shall 
accordingly cease to be a Senior Management Personnel of the Company effective 
January 31, 2026.  
(iii) Mr. Prashant Mahalingam, Category Lead – Internal Medicine, will take up additional 
responsibility of the Hospitals Business of the Company and shall be redesignated as 
Category Lead – Internal Medicine & Hospitals, effective February 1, 2026.  The profile 
and other details of Mr. Mahalingam were already informed to the Stock Exchanges 
when he was appointed as Category Lead – Internal Medicine, vide our letter dated 
November 5, 2024.
