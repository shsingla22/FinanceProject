of India in the year 1991 as an Assistant Administrative
Officer. He is Post Graduate in Science. He possesses a
Bachelor of Education degree and is also a Fellow of
Insurance Institute of India.

Corporation, he has worked in various capacities both in
administrative and marketing at various offices of the

Zone. He has served the Corporation in prominent position
as Senior Divisional Manager (In-Charge) of Nasik and
Secunderabad Divisions. He also served as Faculty Member
in Zonal Training Centre, Pune-Akurdi.
