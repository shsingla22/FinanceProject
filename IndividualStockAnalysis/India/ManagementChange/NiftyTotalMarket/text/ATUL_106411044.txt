Brief résumé

Mr Shantanu Khosla has about four decades of experience in Fast-Moving
Consumer Goods industry. He has held various senior positions with the Procter
and Gamble group in India, the United Kingdom, Japan, Malaysia, Singapore and
served as the Head of Procter & Gamble, India for 15 years. He has also served as
the first Managing Director of Crompton Greaves Consumer Electricals Ltd from
2015 to 2024.
Mr Khosla is a Non-executive Vice Chairman of Crompton Greaves Consumer
Electricals Ltd and the Operating Partner at Advent, a private equity firm.
Mr Khosla holds a graduate degree in Technology (B.Tech) from Indian Institute of
Technology, Mumbai and a postgraduate diploma in management from Indian
Institute of Management, Kolkata. He is recipient of Distinguished Alumni Award
from these two institutions.
