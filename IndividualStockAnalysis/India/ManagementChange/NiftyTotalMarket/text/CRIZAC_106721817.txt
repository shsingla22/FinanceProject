step down from my executive responsibilities. Accordingly, I hereby tender my resignation from the

assuming the position of Non- Executive Director and Chairman of the Board , subject to the requisite 
approvals and completion of applicable corporate formalities.  
I hereby confirm that my resignation is solely for the reasons stated above, and there are no other 
material reasons  for my resignation from the office of Chief Executive Officer other than those 
mentioned in this letter.

committed to supporting a smooth and seamless transition.  
I request the Board to kindly accept my resignation and take the necessary steps to complete all 
applicable corporate and regulatory formalities.
