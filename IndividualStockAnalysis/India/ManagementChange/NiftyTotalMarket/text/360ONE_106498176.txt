Mumbai, January 15, 2026: 360 ONE WAM Limited, one of India’s largest wealth and alternates asset managers,
announced its financial results for the quarter ended December 31, 2025.
Business Performance
• Assets under Management for 360 ONE stood at Rs 7,11,398 Crs, consisting of ARR AUM of Rs 3,17,906 Crs and
Transactional / Brokerage AUM of Rs 3,93,492 Crs
o

Wealth Management: ARR AUM rose to Rs 2,18,957 Crs (+34.5% YoY) supported by robust growth across
segments. Our 360 ONE Plus proposition saw growth of 41.7% YoY, while Distribution and Lending businesses
grew by 30.3% YoY and 32.0% YoY respectively. Overall, it manages assets for 8,500+ relevant families and
corporates

o

Asset Management: ARR AUM increased to Rs 98,949 Crs (+16.1% YoY) mainly driven by growth in Private Equity
(+16.0% YoY), Real Assets (+72.1% YoY) and customized multi-asset (+59.8% YoY) segments

Financial Performance for the quarter ended December 31, 2025
• Total Revenue increased by 21.8% YoY to Rs 826 Crs driven by strong growth in ARR AUM
• Revenue from Operations was Rs 806 Crs - an increase of 33.2% YoY
• ARR revenue was Rs 619 Crs - an increase of 45.4% YoY
• Combined ARR retention improved to 81 basis points (bps) vis-à-vis 70 bps in Q3 FY25. Within that, Wealth
Management retention was at 79 bps, while Asset Management retention was at 85 bps
• Consolidated Profit After Tax was Rs 331 Crs - an increase of 20.3% YoY as against Rs 275 Crs for Q3 FY25
• Tangible net worth stood at Rs 6,327 Crs. Tangible Return on Equity was at 21.0% in Q3 FY26
• Q3 reflects the continued strengthening of our growth trajectory aided by robust execution across core businesses
and strategic initiatives. A constructive and forward-looking regulatory environment is strengthening transparency,
investor confidence and long-term capital formation, the outcomes of which are firmly aligned with the interests of
both investors and the broader industry. With our scaled, integrated wealth, asset management and capital markets’
platform, expanding alternates’ franchise, and growing presence in GIFT City through differentiated strategies, we
are uniquely positioned to benefit from these macro developments. The foundational levers that we have put in place
continue to gain momentum, reinforcing our confidence in delivering sustainable growth, deeper client partnerships,
and enduring value for all stakeholders
