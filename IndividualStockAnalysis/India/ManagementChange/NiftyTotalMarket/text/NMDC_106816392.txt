Sub: Change in Directorate – Appointment of Ms. Sarita Chauhan, Additional Secretary

____________________________________________________________________________ 
 
Ministry of Steel (MoS), Government of India vide Order No. S-14011/1/2022-BLA dated  
12.10.2026 has communicated the appointment of Ms. Sarita Chauhan, Additional Secretary & 
Financial Advisor, Ministry of Steel as Government Director on the Board of NMDC Limited with 
immediate effect or until further orders, whichever is earlier. 
Accordingly, Ms. Sarita Chauhan has been appointed as Government Director on the Board of 
Directors of NMDC Limited w.e.f. 12.10.2026.  
Brief Profile:  
Ms. Sarita Chauhan is a senior civil servant belonging to 1999 batch of the Indian Administrative 
Service - AGMUT Cadre. At present, she is serving as Additional Secretary & Financial Advisor 
in the Ministry of Steel, Government of India. 
It is informed that Ms. Chauhan is not related to any other Director on the Board of NMDC Limited. 
Further, she is not disqualified to act as a Director under the provisions of Section 164 of the 
Companies Act, 2013.  
Further, she is not debarred from holding the office of Director by virtue of order of Court or 
Tribunal or SEBI or any other such authority.  
The above is for kind information and dissemination.
