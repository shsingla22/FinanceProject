Appendix-IShri Anupam Kumar, IAS (2003): A Distinguished Administrator 
Shri Anupam Kumar is a senior officer of the Indian Administrative Service (IAS), belonging to 
the 2003 batch of the Bihar cadre. Over more than two decades of distinguished 
administrative service, he has earned a reputation as a competent, conscientious and forward­
looking administrator, known for his meticulous planning, effective execution of policies and 
unwavering commitment to public service. His professional journey reflects a rare 
combination of intellectual depth, administrative acumen and an empathetic approach to 
governance. 
An alumnus of the University of Lucknow, Shri Kumar holds a Bachelor's degree in Physics, 
Chemistry and Mathematics and Master's degree in Physics with specialisation in Digital 
Electronics. An academic inclination led him to pursue teaching in the Department of Physics 
at Lucknow University following his postgraduate studies. His transition from academia to the 
civil services brought with it an analytical approach, intellectual rigour and a methodical style 
of problem-solving, qualities that have remained evident throughout his administrative 
career. 
Since joining the IAS through direct recruitment in 2003, Shri Kumar has served in a wide range 
of important assignments, both at the district and state levels. His early career included 
postings as Assistant Collector in Muzaffarpur followed by assignments as Sub-Divisional 
Officer in West Champaran and Nalanda. He subsequently served as District Magistrate of 
Nalanda, Rohtas, Muzaffarpur and East Champaran, gaining extensive experience in district 
administration, law and order, development management and public service delivery. 
His tenure as District Magistrate of Rohtas was particularly noteworthy. During this period, he 
focused on addressing the challenges posed by Left-Wing Extremism through a combination 
of effective law enforcement and development-oriented interventions. Initiatives relating to 
land distribution, infrastructure development and promotion of sports helped create 
constructive opportunities for local youth and strengthen public participation in the 
development process. 
As District Magistrate of Muzaffarpur, he demonstrated his ability to combine administrative 
efficiency with developmental priorities. His efforts in facilitating the resolution of various 
bottlenecks associated with the Kanti Thermal Power Project and supporting the restoration 
of its generating units reflected his capacity to coordinate across departments and address 
complex implementation challenges. His approachable demeanour and emphasis on 
systematic public interaction further strengthened his connect with citizens. 
At the state level, Shri Kumar has held several significant positions, including Joint Secretary 
in the Finance Department, State Transport Commissioner and Secretary in the Information 
and Public Relations Department . As Transport Commissioner, he contributed to the 
modernisation and digitisation of transport services and greater transparency in the permit­
allocation system. His association with Bihar's public communication machinery also included 
serving as Managing Director of Samvad Samiti. 
One of the significant phases of his career has been his assignment in the Chief Minister's 
Secretariat as Secretary where he has been associated with policy coordination, strategic 
planning and monitoring of important governance and development initiatives. His ability to 
translate policy priorities into actionable administrative measures, institutional understanding 
and attention to detail, has made him a valued member of the state's administrative 
establishment. 
Shri Anupam Kumar's career stands out for its diversity, spanning district administration, 
finance, infrastructure, transport, public communication and public policy at the highest levels 
of state government. His subsequent elevation to the position of Joint Secretary in the 
Ministry of New and Renewable Energy, Government of India, in April 2026 marks another 
important milestone in his career. 
A soft-spoken, accessible and result-oriented officer, Shri Kumar exemplifies the spirit of 
committed public administration. His professional journey is a testament to the enduring 
value of professional integrity, institutional dedication and quiet but purposeful leadership in 
the service of the nation.
