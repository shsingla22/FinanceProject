In accordance with Regulation 30 read with Para A of Part A of Schedule III of SEBI (Listing
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, (‘Listing Regulations’) we
hereby inform you that, pursuant to the recommendations of the Nomination and
Remuneration Committee (NRC), the Board of Directors of the Company vide Circular
resolution passed on July 15, 2026, has approved the following matters:
1. Transition of Mr. Rajan Sethuraman from the position of Chief Executive Officer to
Strategic Advisor to the Chief Executive Officer
The Board accepted the resignation of Mr. Rajan Sethuraman from his position as Chief
Executive Officer (Key Managerial Personnel) of the Company, effective immediately. This
follows his formal submission of resignation via email on July 14, 2026. To ensure a smooth
and successful transition, the Board has appointed Mr. Rajan Sethuraman as Strategic Advisor
to the Chief Executive Officer, effective today.
Mr. A.V. Venkatraman, Chairperson, said, “On behalf of the entire Board, we extend our
profound thanks to Mr. Rajan Sethuraman for his exceptional stewardship, notably guiding us
through our successful IPO and the acquisition of Decision Point. He leaves behind a
remarkable legacy and a rock-solid foundation for the future. We are deeply grateful for his
contributions over a decade and wish him the absolute best in his next chapter.”


2. Appointment of Ms. Sonal Ramrakhiani as the Chief Executive Officer (Senior
Management Personnel) of the Company
Following a comprehensive evaluation by the NRC and Board members of the qualifications,
experience, and potential contributions, the Board has appointed Ms. Sonal Ramrakhiani as
the Chief Executive Officer (Senior Management Personnel) of the Company with effect from
July 15, 2026.
Welcoming the new CEO, Mr. A.V. Venkatraman, said, “The Board is delighted to welcome
Ms. Sonal Ramrakhiani as our new CEO. Sonal’s global expertise, deep customer focus, and proven
track record of scaling high-performing businesses make her uniquely qualified to drive LatentView’s
next chapter of growth in AI and analytics. As Sonal takes the helm, the Board looks forward to
partnering closely with her to deliver long-term value for our clients, employees, and shareholders.”

Dear Board members,

My journey with LatentView began in 2016 when I joined as Chief People Officer, before I took up the CEO role in 2019. This year carries special significance, as LatentView
marks 20 years since its founding, coinciding with the completion of my own decade-long journey with the organisation. Leading LatentView through this chapter has been one
of the most meaningful experiences of my professional life. Since our listing on the BSE and NSE in November 2021 - a debut that saw the issue oversubscribed many times
over and our shares list at a strong premium to the issue price - we have worked hard to build on that momentum: deepening our Al and analytics capabilities, welcoming
Decision Point into the LatentView family, forging new technology partnerships, and continuing to grow our footprint with global clients. T hrough all of this change, what I have
valued most is that we stayed true to the people-first culture that has defined this company since its founding, even as we took on the added responsibilities and complexities
of being a public company. As LatentView prepares for its next chapter, I believe it is the right time to bring in fresh leadership to take the company forward while the business
has strong momentum and stability.

Please accept this letter as formal notification of my resignation from the position of Chief Executive Officer and Key Managerial Personnel (KMP) of LatentView Analytics
Limited, effective July 15, 2026. It has been an honour to work alongside some of the smartest and most driven minds.

While I am stepping down from my role with immediate effect, I want to ensure this transition causes minimal disruption to the Company, our clients, and our people. I am glad
to make myself available, in whatever capacity the Board may require, for a period of up to six (6) months to support a smooth handover.

I am grateful to the Board for its trust and partnership over the years, and to every member of the LatentView team for their contribution to what we have built together. I wish
the Company and its people continued success and good fortune on the road ahead.

With warm regards,
Rajan Sethuraman
