Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)

Kumar Nayak (DIN: -06940432) as Chairman & Managing Director  on the Board of the Company and designated 
him as Key Managerial Personnel of the Company  w.e.f October 30, 2026 (A/N). 
 
Further, as per declaration received from Shri Jatin Kumar Nayak (DIN: -06940432) , he is not debarred from 
holding the office of Director by virtue of any SEBI Order or any other such authority.  
 Consequent upon the appointment of Shri Jatin Kumar Nayak (DIN: 06940432) as Chairman and Managing
