TIMELESS CRAFT AND A TIMELY MARKET
Dear Shareholders,
It is my privilege to present the Annual
Report of Ethos Limited for the financial
year 2025-26 - a year that, in every sense,
mattered. I share it with genuine pride:
a year in which Ethos not only navigated
a complex global landscape but turned
it to advantage, widening our lead as
India’s foremost destination for luxury and
premium watches.
By almost any measure, this was a landmark
year. Standalone total income grew by
close to 30% to J1,653 Crore; profitability
remained resilient even against a record
appreciation in the Swiss Franc and the
front-loaded costs of an accelerated
expansion; and a successful rights issue
has left our balance sheet stronger than at
almost any point in our history.
A market coming of age
India’s relationship with the fine watch
has fundamentally shifted. What was once
an occasional indulgence is fast becoming
an enduring passion - and, increasingly,
a considered investment. A swelling base
of high-net-worth individuals, a confident
and discerning younger consumer, and a
deepening appreciation for craftsmanship
have together made India one of the
fastest-growing watch markets anywhere
in the world. Swiss watch exports to India

10

to CHF 296 million in 2025 — a rise of
around 35% in just two years — and India
now ranks among the top twenty export
markets for Swiss watches worldwide. The
coming into force of the India–EFTA Trade
and Economic Partnership Agreement
adds further momentum: import duties
on Swiss watches, historically 22%, have
already been reduced to 15.71% with effect
from January 2026 and are set to reduce
progressively to zero by 2031 - opening the
door to a far wider range of brands entering
India and ultimately deepening the breadth
and quality of choice available to Indian
consumers.
Our response to this opportunity has been
deliberate in its sequencing. We built
our foundation in the luxury segment - a
business rooted in trust, relationships
and experience - and are now extending
thoughtfully into the premium and luxury
lifestyle segments beyond watches,
widening our reach across price points and
product lines while remaining true to our
core positioning. It is a strategy designed
not for a single season, but for enduring
leadership.

TURNING A TIMELY MARKET INTO LASTING SCALE
Dear Shareholders,
A year ago, much of what I will describe
was ambition. Today it is infrastructure boutiques opened, verticals carved out, a
balance sheet recapitalised. FY 2025-26 was
the year Ethos turned intent into capacity,
and capacity into results.
A landmark year, by the numbers
FY 2025-26 was, by almost any measure,
the strongest year in our history. Standalone
total income rose around 30% to J1,653
crore, driven by a deliberate shift towards
the luxury and high-luxury end of our
portfolio, a higher average selling price
of J2.07 lakh, and a fast-maturing preowned business — three forces that
converged to powerful effect. Despite a
record appreciation in the Swiss Franc and
the front-loaded costs of our accelerated
boutique expansion, profitability held firm:
consolidated EBITDA grew to J254 crore
and profit after tax stood at J95 crore. The
capital raised through our rights issue during
the year has strengthened the balance
sheet and given us the financial capacity to
keep investing with conviction.
We closed the year with 94 boutiques across
30 cities, up from 73 across 26 cities a
year earlier - and have since grown to 103
across 34 cities as on date, entering eight
new markets — Ranchi, Jodhpur, Srinagar,
Kanpur, Agra, Faridabad, Visakhapatnam
and Amritsar - each one an invitation into
a world of craftsmanship few other retailers
in the country can offer. With 64 brands
available exclusively at Ethos, our portfolio
is anchored by a differentiation that
competitors cannot replicate — exclusivity
that deepens customer loyalty, elevates
average selling prices, and positions Ethos
as the definitive gateway for the world’s
finest watchmaking in India.
The year we built for scale
The most significant milestone of the year
was the City of Time in Gurugram — a
22,000-square-foot luxury watch destination
housing nine boutiques: seven exclusive

12

mono-brand boutiques and two multi-brand
galleries showcasing over fifty independent
watch brands under one roof. It is far more
than a store; it is a statement of where
we believe Indian luxury retail is headed.
This shift towards larger, more experiential
formats is deliberate. Increasingly, our
boutiques are destinations in their own
right — our ~8,000 sq. ft. boutique in Jaipur,
the largest luxury watch store in Rajasthan,
and our two-storey, 6,000 sq. ft. Summit
boutique in Kochi are designed not simply
to display watches, but to immerse the
customer in them: private consultations,
curated brand environments, watchmaking
zones and lounges where time is meant to
slow down. It reflects a simple conviction
— that at Ethos, a watch is never merely a
purchase; it is an experience. But scale, for
us, is not measured in square footage alone
— it is about building distinct engines of
growth across the business.
Our certified pre-owned vertical continued
to gain both credibility and scale, emerging
as a sustainable, aspirational gateway into
fine watchmaking and one of our fastestgrowing businesses. Our lifestyle vertical
— anchored by Messika in fine jewellery
and Rimowa in luxury luggage — was
reorganised under a dedicated subsidiary,
Ethos Lifestyle Private Limited, to give it
sharper strategic focus; during the year
we opened India’s first Messika boutique,
extending our reach well beyond watches.
We are at an advanced stage with several
global brands not yet present in India and will partner only where we can secure
exclusivity, so we can invest behind each for
the long term.
Our people, our execution engine
None of this happens without the people
who bring it to life. In luxury retail, our
people are the experience. We have invested
in a robust training vertical so our teams
are better equipped to engage customers
and deliver a superior experience; made
our digital marketing significantly more
data-driven; and are leveraging technology

across inventory and operations to create
a seamless, omnichannel journey. What we
are building is not merely a retail network,
but a differentiated and future-ready retail
ecosystem.
The priorities ahead
The opportunity before us is substantial,
and our priorities for the year ahead are
clear. We will widen our roster of exclusive
brands across watches and adjacent luxury
categories; sharpen our omnichannel and
digital experience around the customer;
carry our presence deeper into Tier 2 and
Tier 3 India; scale our pre-owned and build
out our service network — all while holding
ourselves to the highest standards of
responsible and sustainable business.
The road to 10x
Our ambition remains unchanged and
undimmed: to grow the business tenfold
within a decade of our listing — reaching
our 10x goal by 2032. The foundation
is now firmly in place: in our brand
relationships, our retail network, our
maturing pre-owned and lifestyle verticals,
and above all in our people. The path from
here calls for ambition and discipline in
equal measure and I have every confidence
that Ethos will continue to convert this rare
market moment into durable, responsible,
long-term value.
To our brand partners, whose craftsmanship
we are privileged to represent; to my
colleagues across Ethos, whose effort turns
strategy into experience; and to you, our
shareholders, for your continued trust —
thank you. The market’s moment has come
— and we intend not merely to participate
in India’s luxury growth story, but to help
shape it.
Sincerely,
Pranav Saboo
Managing Director & Chief Executive

Where Experiences
Meet Exclusivity
We have strategically expanded into premium jewellery and high-end
travel accessories, strengthening our lifestyle-led luxury portfolio and
consolidating our presence in India’s luxury retail market. The expansion
is aligned with our broader strategic vision of making exclusive global
brands more accessible to discerning customers in India, while delivering
a more holistic and elevated luxury experience.

RIMOWA and Messika
Boutiques

Messika Jewellery

As part of its lifestyle-led expansion strategy,
the Company launched India’s first RIMOWA
boutique, introducing the brand’s iconic luxury
travel luggage portfolio, alongside a dedicated
Messika boutique showcasing contemporary high
jewellery collections. Renowned for its purposeful
design and meticulous attention to details, these
products seamlessly combine functionality and
sophistication, crafted to accompany journeys
both near and far.

Founded in Paris in 2005 by Valérie Messika, Messika has redefined modern
luxury jewellery through its contemporary interpretation of diamonds. Renowned
for blending timeless artistry with modern design sensibilities, the brand creates
elegant yet bold creations that resonate with a new generation of luxury
consumers.

22

During the year, Ethos marked a significant milestone in its luxury lifestyle
journey with the launch of Messika’s first boutique in India at The Chanakya,
New Delhi. The opening was celebrated in the presence of Valérie Messika,
Founder and Artistic Director of Messika, and acclaimed actor Ishaan Khatter,
reflecting the significance of the brand’s entry into the Indian market.

RIMOWA
Established in 1898, RIMOWA is a globally recognised luxury luggage brand celebrated
for its innovation, exceptional quality and iconic grooved aluminium design. Combining
heritage with advanced engineering, the brand offers premium travel solutions
that seamlessly balance functionality, durability and timeless appeal. The boutique
showcases the brand’s signature collection of luggage and travel essentials, offering
customers an immersive experience of RIMOWA’s design philosophy and commitment
to excellence. Complementing the retail experience is a dedicated client care area
providing expert on-site repair and after-sales services, reinforcing the brand’s focus on
longevity, reliability and lifelong ownership.

Mapping New Frontiers of Growth
We continue to broaden our retail footprint, expanding our presence to 103 boutiques
across 34 cities and reinforcing our presence in India’s luxury retail landscape. The
year was marked by several strategic additions, including the launch of our second
Haute Horology boutique in Mumbai and our second exclusive RIMOWA boutique in
New Delhi.
With a network spanning key metropolitan centres and emerging luxury markets, our
boutiques offer access to thoughtfully selected collections from over 80 of the world’s
leading luxury watch and lifestyle brands. During the year, we also entered six new
cities - Jodhpur, Ranchi, Srinagar, Kanpur, Agra and Faridabad - bringing the Ethos
experience closer to a growing community of enthusiasts and collectors.
