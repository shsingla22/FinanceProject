Sub: Resignation as Statutory Auditors of Eris Therapeutics Limited (the "Company')
We were appointed as the Statutory Auditors of the Company to hold office from the conclusion of the 1st Annual General
Meeting held on September 30, 2022, until the conclusion of the 6th Annual General Meeting, i.e., for a term of five years
commencing from April 1, 2022, and ending with the financial year March 31, 2027.
During our tenure, we have completed the statutory audit of the Company's financial statements for the year ended March
31, 2025, and issued our audit report dated May 17, 2025. Further, we have also conducted the limited review for the
quarter ended June 30, 2025, and issued our report on August 04, 2025.
In light of the change in the statutory auditors of the Holding Company, and with a view to facilitating the incoming auditors
of the Holding Company to assume audit responsibility for significant transactions and scope at the group level, we have
decided to cede the audit of the Company in favour of those auditors.
Accordingly, we hereby tender our resignation as the Statutory Auditors of the Company with immediate effect.
We sincerely thank the Management and the Board for their support, cooperation, and courtesies extended to us
throughout our tenure.
