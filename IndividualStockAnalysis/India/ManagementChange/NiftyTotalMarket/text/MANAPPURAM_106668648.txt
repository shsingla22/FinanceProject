Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, as amended (“Listing Regulations”) – Change in Senior Management 
Personnel of the Company  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, request you to note  that Mr. Ashish N Chandak 
has been appointed  as the Chief Compliance Officer  (“CCO”) of the Company with effect from June
