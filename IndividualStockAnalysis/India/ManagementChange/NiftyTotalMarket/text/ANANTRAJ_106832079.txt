1.

These unaudited consolidated financial results have been prepared in accordance
with the recognition and measurement principles of the Indian Accounting Standards
(Ind AS-34) 'Interim Financial Reporting' as notified under Section 133 of the
Companies Act, 2013, read with the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules,
2015, as amended from time to time. The said financial results represent the results
of Anant Raj Limited ("the Company''), its subsidiaries (together referred to as 'Group')
and its share in the unaudited financial results of associates and jointly controlled
entities, which have been prepared in accordance with Ind AS 110 - 'Consolidated
Financial Statements' and Ind AS 28 - 'Investment in Associates and Joint Ventures'.

2.

The unaudited consolidated financial results have been reviewed by the Audit
Comm ittee and approved by the Board of Directors at their respective meetings held
on October 28, 2026.

3.

The Statutory Auditors of the Company have carried out a Limited Review of the
unaudited consolidated financial results for the quarter and six months ended
September 30, 2026, in accordance with Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and issued an unmodified
conclusion on these unaudited consolidated financial results.

4.

The Group operates primarily in real estate development, infrastructure and related
activities, which constitute a single reportable segment: 'Real Estate Development'.
Accordingly, the disclosure requirements of Ind AS 108, 'Operating Segments', are not
applicable. As the Group's business activities are confined to India, no separate
geographical segment is reportable.

5.

In terms of the accounting policy for revenue recognition, estimates of revenues and
costs are reviewed periodically by management and the impact of any changes in
such estimates is recognised in the period in which such changes are determined.
During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, there is no change in the accounting
policy.
