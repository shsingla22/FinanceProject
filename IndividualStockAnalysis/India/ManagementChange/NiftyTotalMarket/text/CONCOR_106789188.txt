About CONCOR
CONCOR’s primary mission is to facilitate trade and industry by
developing modern logistics facilities and Multi-Modal Logistics
Parks throughout India, making it a leader in the logistics sector.
CONCOR’s trains are being operational at approximately 194
locations that includes terminals, CRTs, E-CRTs, private sidings
& gateway ports across the length & breadth of the country.
The company is focused on providing seamless end to end
logistics services serving both international and domestic trade
through strategic investment in infrastructure and technology.
