Summary of Profile  
Mr. N V Sivakumar  
 
 
Age: 63 years  
Location: Bengaluru, India  
 
Mr. N V Sivakumar is a Chartered Accountant from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India and holds a 
Bachelor of Commerce degree from Loyola Academy, Secunderabad.  
Mr. Sivakumar has over 40 years of experience with PwC across audit, transaction services, deals, governance and 
leadership roles in India, the UK and the Middle East. During his career, he served as Audit Partner, Retail & 
Consumer Sector Leader, Deals Le ader, Partner Affairs Leader, Entrepreneurial & Private Business Leader, and 
Chairman of PwC India’s Partner Oversight Committee. He retired from PwC on 31 March 2023.  
He has extensive audit, accounting and transaction advisory experience and has advised a diverse portfolio of 
domestic and international clients. His work has included deals strategy, corporate finance, valuations and 
transaction advisory, as well as engag ement with promoters, boards and senior executives on strategic and 
governance matters.  
Mr. Sivakumar currently serves on the boards of listed and unlisted companies and chairs the Audit Committees 
of Kennametal India Limited, 3M India Limited, Computer Age Management Services Limited, Schaeffler India 
Limited,  Credit Saison India and Agrata s Energy Storage Solutions Private Limited. He also serves on the Board  of 
Capital Foods Private Limited , Acko Technology & Services Pvt Ltd, Acko General Insurance Limted and TalentPro 
India HR Private Ltd. . His earlier roles include board or governance positions with PwC Private Limited, PwC India 
and  Tata Motor Finance Ltd. .
