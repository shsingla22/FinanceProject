Sub: Appointment of Shri. S. Sakthimani as Chairman and Managing Director  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR 
Regulations”), we hereby inform you that the Department of Fertilizers, Ministry of 
Chemicals & Fertilizers, Government of India has vide letter No. 86/2/2017-HR-I     
(e-21608) dated 2nd February, 2026, appointed Shri. S. Sakthimani, (DIN – 
07482308), Director (Finance), The Fertilisers and Chemicals Travancore Limited 
(FACT) as the Chairman and Managing Director, FACT, for a period of five years 
with effect from the date of his assumption of charge of the post, or till the date of his 
superannuation, or until further orders, whichever is the earliest. 
Accordingly, Shri. S. Sakthimani, has taken over charge of the post of Chairman and 
Managing Director, FACT, today, the 2nd of February, 2026 (AN). 
The details with respect to the above appointment of Director as prescribed under 
Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI LODR Regulations read with the SEBI 
circular HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, is 
enclosed as Annexure A. 
Thanking you, 
 
Yours faithfully, 
For The Fertilisers and Chemicals Travancore Limited
