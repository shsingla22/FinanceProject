Dear Sir / Madam,
In accordance with Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, read with SEBI Circular
No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30th January, 2026 ("SEBI
Circular"), we hereby inform that, as part of the Company's succession planning
process, Mr. Sunil Lalai shall step down from the position of Compliance Officer of the
Company with effect from the close of business hours on 15th November, 2026, while
continuing in his role as Company Secretary and Head – Legal of the Company. His
letter in this regard is enclosed as Annexure I.
Further, based on recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee,
the Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today, i.e., 3rd November,
2026, has approved the appointment of Mr. Mandar Velankar, Deputy Head – Company
Secretary and Legal, as the Compliance Officer of the Company with effect from 16th
November, 2026, succeeding Mr. Sunil Lalai in this role.
Consequent to his appointment as the Compliance Officer, Mr. Velankar shall also be
designated as a Key Managerial Personnel and Senior Management Personnel of the
Company. He shall further be authorised to determine the materiality of an event or
information and to disseminate the same to the stock exchanges pursuant to Regulation
30(5) of the Listing Regulations. The details required under the Listing Regulations and
the SEBI Circular are enclosed as Annexure II.
You are requested to take this intimation on record.
Thanking you.
