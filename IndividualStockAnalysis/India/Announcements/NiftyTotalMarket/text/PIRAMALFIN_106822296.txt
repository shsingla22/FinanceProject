WHEREAS: 
A. By an Indenture dated 19th November, 2009 ("the Principal Trust Deed") 
executed between Piramal Phytocare Limited (then known as Piramal Life 
Sciences Limited) ("PPL") and (1) Shri Gautam B. Doshi (2) Dr. R.A. 
Mashelkar, PPL constituted a Trust under the name "Piramal Life Sciences 
Limited Senior Employees Option Trust" (hereinafter referred to as the 
'PPL ESOP Trust'); 
B. By Supplemental Trust Deeds dated 16 October 2017, 31 January 2018, 27 
November 2019, 24 May 2024 and 13 March 2025, the Principal Trust Deed 
was amended from time to time, inter alia, to incorporate the name change 
of the PPL ESOP Trust from 'Piramal Life Sciences Limited Senior 
Employees Option Trust' to "Piramal Phytocare Limited Senior Employees 
Option Trust", record the appointment/cessation of trustees and to 
incorporate changes necessitated by the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Share Based Employee Benefits And Sweat Equity) Regulations, 
2021 ("SEBI Regulations"). 
C. Further, pursuant to the Scheme of arrangement between PPL and Piramal 
Enterprises Limited ('PEL'), as approved and sanctioned by the Hon'ble 
National Company Law Tribunal, Mumbai Bench, vide its Order dated 
12 August 2022 and made effective on 18 August 2022, PPL stood 
transferred to and vested in PEL and the PPL ESOP Trust was deemed to 
be established by PEL for the benefit of its employees. 
D. Subsequently, pursuant to the composite Scheme of arrangement amongst 
PEL, PFL and their respective shareholders and creditors under Sections 
230 to 232 of the Companies Act, 2013 and other applicable provisions of 
the Companies Act, 2013, and rules made thereunder ('Merger Scheme'), 
as approved and sanctioned by the Hon'ble National Company Law 
Tribunal, Mumbai Bench, vide its Order dated 10 September 2025 and made 
effective on 16 September 2025, PEL has stood transferred to and vested 
in PFL as a going concern and all the obligations of PEL in relation to its 
employees and the PPL ESOP Trust have been deemed to be established by 
PFL for the benefit of its employees , including employees transferred from 
PEL to PFL pursuant to the Merger Scheme. 
E. The Settlor has vide Amended and Restated Trust Deed dated 30th 
December 2025 reconstituted the PPL ESOP Trust under the name of PFL 
Employee ESOP Trust for administering and implementing the Piramal 
Finance Limited Senior Employees Stock Option 2025 in accordance with 
the ESOP Scheme provisions and SEBI Regulations. 
F. Shri Arvind Agarwal has decided to step down as Trustee with effect from 
19th October 2026. 
G. Shri Kuttikrishnan Krishna Pisharody is appointed as a Trustee with effect 
from 19th October 2026. 
NOW THIS INDENTURE WITNESSETH AND IT IS HEREBY AGREED AS 
FOLLOWS: 
The Amended and Restated Trust Deed is hereby amended as follows: 
1. Shri Arvind Agarwal has resigned as a Trustee with effect from 19th October 
2026. 
2. Shri Kuttikrishnan Krishna Pisharody is appointed as Trustee with effect from 
19th October 2026. 
3. Accordingly , Shri Suresh Tapuriah and Shri Kuttikrishnan Krishna Pisharody 
are the Trustees of the Trust with effect from 19th October 2026 
All other terms of the Amended and Restated Trust Deed shall continue to remain 
in full force and effect.
