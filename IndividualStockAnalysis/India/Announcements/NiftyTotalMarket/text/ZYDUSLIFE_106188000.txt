In our earlier intimation dated September 17, 2024, Zydus Lifesciences Limited (“the Company ”) 
intimated about execution of Business Transfer Agreement (“ BTA”) to purchase the API business 
(“the Target Business ”) of Sterling Biotech Limited (“ SBL”), subject to certain conditions 
precedent and closing date adjustments as provided in the BTA, with effect from such date, and 
in such manner and on the terms and conditions as mentioned in the BTA. In the said intimation, 
it was mentioned that the said transaction is expected to be completed by December 31, 2024.  
 
Vide our letter dated December 27, 2024, we informed that the Company and SBL had mutually 
decided to extend the date of closing upto March 31, 2025.  
 
We hereby now inform that SBL is still in the process of completing certain conditions precedents 
of the BTA and it is expected to take some more time to complete the same and as per the terms 
and conditions of the BTA, the Company and SBL had mutually decided to extend the date of 
closing upto September 30, 2025.  
 
The Company and SBL have today, i.e. March 28, 2025, executed a letter to extend the date of 
completion as above. In view of the same, the transaction is expected to be completed on or 
before September 30, 2025.
