Media Release –  April 12 , 2024 
   
 
                            
Vodafone Idea announces Further Public Offer of Rs. 18,000 crores   
 
 
The Board of Directors (“Board” ) of the Company, at its meeting held on April 11, 2024 
approved  Further Public Offering (FPO) of Equity Shares, aggregating up to ₹  18,000 crore. 
The Capital Raising Committee in its meeting held today i.e. April 12, 2024 appr oved the price 
band for the FPO issuance.  
 
The higher  end of the price band i.e.  Rs. 11  is at a  discount of ~26% compared to recently 
approved preferential issue price to the promoter entity at Rs. 1 4.87 and a discount of ~15% 
compared to last closing price  of Rs. 12.95.
