voltage transmission systems. TARIL's Shunt 
Reactors, available with variable ratings and filled 
with either mineral oil or ester, feature robust 
designs backed by rigorous quality control. Series 
Reactors are mainly used in with the purpose of 
arc stability for furnace transformers, limiting 
current, reduction of flicker in network etc.
