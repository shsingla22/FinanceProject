It may be noted that as per the Company’s Board approved Code of Practices and Procedures for Fair 
Disclosure of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (“Code”), the Company declares receipt of 
any new project, exceeding the materiality threshold, only upon receipt of Letter of Award (LoA). 
However, pursuant to the proposed corporate action being placed before the Board, as a matter of 
transparency with all our stakeholders, as a one-time measure, the Company is voluntarily disclosing 
its L1 bidder status in the following projects:- 
 
1. Construction of 6-Lane Partially Elevated Highway Corridor along with improvement of existing 
road from Km. 10+600 to Km. 64+000 (Section Pune to Shirur of NH-753F – Minimum Design 
Length 53.40 Km) in the State of Maharashtra on DFBOT (Toll) Mode, with concession period 
of 29 years. The said project is a Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (“MoRTH”) project 
where-in Maharashtra State Infrastructure Development Corporation (Client) is the implementing 
agency & Concessionaire and we are the Sub-concessionaire. The Total Project Cost is ~Rs. 
7,300 Crore with the construction period of 4 years from the appointed date. The Company 
emerged as the lowest bidder on the basis of NPV of projected toll revenue during Concession 
period on October 03, 2025; and  
 
2. Development of new 910 MLD Water Treatment Plant including Civil, Mechanical, Electrical & 
Instrumentation works at Panjrapur, Maharashtra, by Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation for 
a Design Build and Operate Contract. The Project is to be designed and constructed within a 
period of 48 months and is to be comprehensively Operated and Maintained (O&M) for a period 
of 15 years thereafter. The bid value of the project cost including GST is ~Rs. 3,145 Crore which 
includes the O&M and asset replacement during the contracted period. This project is proposed 
to be executed with Veolia Water Technologies, Malaysia, as our technology partner. The 
Company emerged as lowest bidder on October 08, 2025.  
The Company shall update the stock exchanges upon receipt of LoA in accordance with its Code.
