Subject: Outcome of the Board Meeting 
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
This is to inform you that at the meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company held today i.e. 
Wednesday, July 24, 2024, the Board inter alia has: 
 
1. Approved the Unaudited Financial Results along with Limited Review Report for the quarter ended 
June 30, 2024, which is enclosed herewith.  
 
2. Declared an interim dividend for the financial year 2024-2025 of Rs. 2/- per equity share (i.e. 200%) 
on the fully paid-up equity shares of the face value of Re. 1/- per share. 
 
3. Fixed August 1, 2024, as the Record Date towards payment of Interim Dividend. The Interim 
Dividend will be paid on or before August 16, 2024. 
 
The Board Meeting commenced at 1:00 p.m. and concluded at 1:50 p.m.
