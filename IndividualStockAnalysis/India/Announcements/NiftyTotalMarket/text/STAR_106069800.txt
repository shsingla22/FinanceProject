This has reference to our intimation dated November 19, 2024 notifying the Stock Exchanges regarding

We wish to further inform you that: 
 
1) Certified Copy of NCLT Order  - Board of Directors of the Company at their meeting held on 
November 26, 2024 have taken on record the certified true copy of the Order of the Hon’ble NCLT, 
Mumbai Bench dated November 14, 2024 received on November 26, 2024. 
 
2) Effective Date  - Participating Entities i.e., Strides, Steriscience and OneSource shall be completing 
filing of certified true copy of the NCLT Order with the Registrar of Companies, Mumbai in due course, 
thus making the Scheme effective from such date. Company shall intimate Effective Date of the Scheme 
in due course. 
 
3) Appointed Date  - Pursuant to the Scheme becoming Effective, the demerged undertakings from Strides 
and Steriscience shall vest with OneSource effective April 1, 2024. 
 
4) Record Date  - Board of Directors of the Company at their meeting held on November 26, 2024 have 
fixed Friday, December 6, 2024 as the Record Date  for determining names of the equity shareholders 
of Strides who shall be entitled for allotment of equity shares by OneSource, as consideration under the 
Scheme.   
 
Share Entitlement Ratio  for allotment of equity shares to Strides’ shareholders under the Scheme is 
1 (One) equity share of Rs. 1/- each of OneSource for every 2 (Two) equity shares of Rs. 10/- held by 
the shareholder in Strides.   
 
Equity shares so allotted by OneSource shall be listed on the BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange 
of India Limited as provided in the Scheme in due course.  
 
 
 
 
5) Suspense Escrow Account 
 
As per the provisions of SEBI Regulations, allotment of shares shall be made only in demat form. 
 
Hence, Onesource shall consolidate shares entitled by physical shareholders of Strides as unclaimed 
shares and credit them to a designated account titled “OneSource – Unclaimed Securities – Suspense 
Escrow Account”.  
 
These shares shall be transferred to the respective shareholders, once such shareholders demat their 
Strides shares, and provide requisite information relating to their demat account to OneSource/ 
Integrated (RTA of OneSource).
