EPL Limited, a global leader in packaging solutions, today announced its financial results for the second quarter 
ended September 30, 2026 (Q2 FY27). Surpassing its strong performance in earlier quarters, the Company 
delivered a standout performance with t op-line growth accelerating to 30% to reach ₹15,674 million, despite 
ongoing global market volatility and geopolitical disruptions. This demonstrates the resilience of its business 
model, strong customer partnerships and disciplined execution. Growth was b road -based, driven by exceptional 
performance in Beauty & Cosmetics which grew by 42.9%, while all regions delivered double -digit growth. On 
an underlying basis, excluding the pass -through impact of higher raw material prices, revenue growth stood at 
20.3% . 
 
EBITDA & Margins – EBITDA increased by 17.6% year -on-year to ₹2,960 million, representing EPL's 16th 
consecutive quarter of double -digit EBITDA growth. Reported EBITDA margin was at 18.9%, while underlying  
EBITDA margin stood firm at 20.4%. The company was able to fully pass on cost increases through judicious 
pricing across all regions.  
 
Profit After Tax (PAT) – PAT for the quarter stood at ₹1,101 million, representing a 5.6% year -on-year growth, 
balancing ongoing growth investments with operational profitability. Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) 
continued to be strong at 18.2%.  
 
Mr Hemant Bakshi, MD & Global CEO, EPL Limited , said, “EPL has delivered another standout quarter, achieving 
30% revenue growth, on an underlying basis revenue growth was 20% and margins were 20.4%. What makes 
this performance particularly meaningful is that our strategy is delivering in full across every geo graphy and core 
category. Beauty & Cosmetics delivered exceptional growth of over 40%, while Oral Care continued its strong 
momentum with 20% growth. In the month of September, B&C category overtook Oral care to become our 
largest category, a significant m ilestone towards building a fully diversified, high growth portfolio. Despite 
continued cost pressures, we successfully recovered the full impact of cost increases through pricing actions, 
enabling us to sustain underlying EBITDA margins above 20% while co ntinuing to invest in our growth priorities. 
Looking ahead, we remain confident in our momentum and confidently reaffirm our near -term guidance of high -
teens revenue growth and 20% underlying EBITDA margins”.  
 
 
Strategic & Operational Highlights  
Geographic Diversification:  All regions delivered strong double -digit revenue growth during the quarter. East 
Asia Pacific (EAP) grew 40.2%, Europe 30.4%, AMESA 28.0% (with India growing at a solid 30.5%) and the 
Americas 25.3%.  
Category Transformation:  Beauty & Cosmetics delivered exceptional performance growing 42.9% year -on-year, 
Oral Care maintained steady market leadership with growth of 19.2%. Personal Care & Beyond now accounts 
for 55% of the total portfolio.  
Sustainability:  Sustainable tube formats contributed 41% of total sales year -to-date. EPL remains EcoVadis 
Platinum certified (Top 1% globally out of 150,000+ companies assessed), reinforcing its vision to be the most 
sustainable packaging company in the world.  
Recognition & Culture:  EPL's focus on innovation and quality was recognized at the IFCA Star Awards 2026 and 
Print Week 2026. EPL operations across seven countries (India, Poland, Egypt, Philippines, Colombia, China, 
Brazil) remain certified as a Great Place to Work® for FY25 -26. 
Strategic Corporate Merger:  As part of its long -term vision to build a leading consumer packaging business in 
emerging markets, EPL's proposed merger with Indovida —creating a $1 billion revenue entity with a $2 billion 
valuation —advanced significantly. Following approvals from the C ompetition Commission of India (CCI), stock 
exchanges (BSE/NSE observation letters dated Oct 19, 2026), and foreign anti -trust authorities across 10 
jurisdictions, EPL officially filed its application with the Hon'ble NCLT Mumb ai on October 23, 2026. EPL remains 
confident of completing the merger within the expected timelines.  
Outlook  
EPL continues to navigate the volatile global operating environment with operational discipline and focus. The 
Company retains its near -term guidance of revenue growth in the high -teens for the next few quarters, while 
maintaining its underlying EBITDA mar gin target at 20%. EPL remains focused on strengthening its market 
leadership in Beauty & Cosmetics, accelerating growth in high -potential emerging markets , driving capital 
efficiency, and expanding beyond tubes into broader consumer packaging platforms.  
About EPL Limited:  
EPL Limited is the world’s leading speciality packaging company, reaching one in every three toothpaste tubes 
globally. Headquartered in Mumbai and listed on the BSE and NSE, EPL has a presence across all five continents, 
with 21 state -of-the-art facilitie s across 11 countries and a workforce of more than 6,000 employees 
representing 23 nationalities. The company produces more than 9 billion tubes annually and serves over 1,200 
customers across Oral Care, Beauty & Cosmetics, Pharmaceuticals & Health, Food &  Nutrition, and Home Care. 
Its portfolio spans laminates, laminated tubes, extruded tubes, caps, closures and dispensing systems. EPL 
reported revenue of ₹47,631 million in FY26, marking a 13% year -on-year increase. With more than 150 granted 
patents, the company continues to drive innovation in packaging technologies, while its sustainability -led 
solutions support the transition towards circular packaging. EPL is evolving beyond tubes to become a trusted 
global partner for next -generation consumer packagin g solutions.
