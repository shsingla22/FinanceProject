Key Business & Strategic Updates  
 
Commenting on the results and performance, Mr. Naresh Jalan, Managing Director, 
Ramkrishna Forgings Limited said:  
"Q2 was another quarter of steady progress, building on the strong start we made to FY27. 
Domestic demand remained healthy, supported by firm commercial vehicle production 
schedules, and customer activity across our key export markets has been resilient. We  remain 
watchful of geopolitical developments in West Asia and evolving global tariff policies.  
We have reported consolidated revenues of ₹ 1,405 crore in Q2, higher by 55% on YoY basis. 
Profit After Tax stood at ₹ 71 crore, as against ₹47 crore in the previous quarter and a loss of 
₹10 crore in Q2 last year. For the first half, revenues stood at ₹ 2,622  crore with Profit After 
Tax of ₹ 117 crore.  
Our forged wheels facility near Chennai, set up through our joint venture with Titagarh Rail 
Systems, is near to  commercial production. This project will take us into a strategically 
important product for Indian Railways and add a long -term growth driver. Alongside, the 
ramp -up of our casting operations and the addition of new capacities and value -added 
products are  enabling us to address a broader range of customer requirements across 
passenger vehicles, electric vehicles and industrial applications.  

During the quarter, the promoters completed the conversion of warrants, infusing ₹150 crore 
into the Company. This reflects our confidence in the business and supports our ongoing 
efforts to strengthen the balance sheet.  
Looking ahead, we are building our presence in non -ferrous forgings, including aluminium, 
titanium, Inconel and Nimonic grades, for high -technology applications across Aerospace, 
Robotics and Semiconductors. Our focus remains on disciplined execution, bett er utilisation 
of the capacities we have built and a stronger balance sheet, positioning the Company for 
sustainable growth and long -term value creation for all stakeholders. " 
 
About Ramkrishna Forgings Limited  
Ramkrishna Forgings Limited (BSE: 532527, NSE: RKFORGE), incorporated in 1981 and 
headquartered in Kolkata, is one of India's leading integrated manufacturers of rolled, 
forged, machined and cast products. The Company operates 20 manufacturing facilities 
across India and Mexico, with an installed capacity of 3,95,800 MT as of September  30, 2026, 
spanning ring rolling, forging, press, and casting operations. Its capabilities span the full 
manufacturing value chain — hot, warm and cold forging, ring rolling, gear grinding, 
machining, fabrication, aluminium forging and casting; supported b y a global network of 
sales, support and warehousing facilities across the USA, Mexico, Brazil, Turkey, Italy, 
Sweden and Belgium. The Company is certified in terms of TS 16949, OSHAS 18001 and ISO 
14001.  
Ramkrishna Forgings serves a diversified set of end markets, including Commercial and 
Passenger Vehicles, Electric Vehicles, Railways, Farm Equipment, Mining & Earth Moving, Oil 
& Gas, Power and Construction, across both domestic and export markets. It is a preferred 
supplier to leading OEMs such as TATA Motors, Ashok Leyland, VE Commercial and Daimler 
in India, and to Volvo, Mack Trucks, Iveco, DAF, Scania, MAN, UD Trucks and Ford Otosan 
overseas, and supplies globally to Tier 1 axle manufacturers includin g Dana, Meritor and 
American Axles.
