Tata Motors registers  17% growth YoY with total sales of 32,850  commercial vehicle units in May  2026 
Mumbai, 1 June  2026 : Tata Motors Limited sales in the domestic & international markets for May  2026 stood at 3 2,850  
units, compared to 28,147 units during May  2025, up 17% YoY .
