Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
The Company, vide its intimation dated July 18, 2025, informed the exchanges about 
sanctioning of the Scheme of Amalgamation of Adani Cementation Limited (“ Transferor 
Company ”) with Ambuja Cements Limited (“ Transferee Company ”) (“Scheme ”) by the 
Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Ahmedabad Bench (NCLT Ahmedabad) vide its 
order dated July 18, 2025.  
 
Further to our aforesaid letter, we wish to inform you that all the conditions to make the 
Scheme effective, as specified under the Scheme, have been fulfilled. Accordingly, the 
Scheme has become effective today, i.e., August 1, 2025 (“ Effective Date ”) and 
consequently, Transferor Company stands amalgamated with Ambuja Cements Limited 
and dissolved without being wound up. In accordance with the Scheme, the Appointed 
Date of the Scheme is April 1, 2024. 
 
This intimation will also be uploaded on the Company’s website at 
www.ambujacement.com.
