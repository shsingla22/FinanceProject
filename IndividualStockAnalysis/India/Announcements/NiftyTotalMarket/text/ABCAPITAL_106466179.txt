Sub:  Receipt of Certificate of Registration as Non -Banking Financial Company -Investment and Credit

This refers to our letter dated 01 April 2025, wherein we informed the Exchanges about the effectiveness of the 
Scheme of Amalgamation of Aditya Birla Finance Limited (“Amalgamating Company”) with the Company, which 
became effective on 01 April 2025.  
 
We wish to inform you that, consequent to the Scheme becoming effective and in accordance with the no -objection 
letter received from the Reserve Bank of India (“RBI”), the Company had applied to RBI for obtaining a Certificate 
of Registration as an NBFC – Investment and Credit Company (NBFC -ICC), thereby effecting the conversion of 
the Company from an NBFC – Core Investment Company (NBFC -CIC) to an NBFC -ICC. 
 
We wish to inform you that t he Company has today received the Certificate of Registration dated  
09 December 2025 from RBI as an NBFC -ICC. 
 
 
Further details as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations, read with SEBI Master Circular
