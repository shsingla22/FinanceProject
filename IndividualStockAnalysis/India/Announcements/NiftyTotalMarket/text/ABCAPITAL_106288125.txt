Particulars  Remarks  
a) Reason for change viz. 
appointment.  
 
 As per the RBI Guidelines, the statutory audit of a Non-Banking 
Financial Compan y (NBFC ) with an asset  size of Rs. 15,000 crore and 
above as at the end of  previous year, should be  conducted under joint 
audit of a minimum of two  audit firms.  
 
Further, the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Ahmedabad 
Bench vide its order dated 24 March 2025 has approved the Scheme 
of Amalgamation of Aditya Birla Finance Limited  with Aditya Birla 
Capital Limited (“the company”) and their respective shareholders and 
creditors. The said scheme has become effective from 1  April 2025 . 
 
Pursuant to said amalgamation, the asset size of the  Company has 
exceeded ₹ 15,000 crore.  Consequently, the Statutory Audit of the 
Company for Financial Year 2025 -26 and onwards is required to be 
conducted under the joint audit, by a minimum of two Joint Statutory 
Auditor.  
 
In view of above, t he Board of Directors, based on the recommendation 
of the Audit Committee considered and recommended the appointment 
of M/s. K K C & Associates LLP, Chartered Accountants (ICAI Firm 
Registration No. 105146W/W100621), as one of the Joint Statutory 
Auditors of the Company, for a term of 3 (three) consecutive years from 
the conclusion of 18th (Eighteenth) Annual General Meeting (AGM) till 
the conclusion of the 21st (Twenty -First) AGM , for approval of members 
in the ensuing Annual General Mee ting of the Company.  b) Date of appointment & term 
of appointment;

conglomerate, etc. and therefore it perfectly commensurate with the
