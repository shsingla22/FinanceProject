applicable reserves within equity.

Revenue remained flat due to the ongoing normalization of demand in Digital Industries and due to 
normal project delivery schedules  in the Mobility business. The decline in Profit from Operations  was 
due to  under absorption  of fixed costs and higher  cost of material  in the Digital Industries  business . 
Additionally, the Profit was impacted by an extraordinary gain of INR 192 crore from the sale of 
property in Q2  FY 2024 and demerger expenses of INR 63 crore in the current quarter.

Infrastructure businesses where we see continued public Capex spending on infrastructure. Our short
