Mphasis appoints Ajay Arya  as Head of Mergers & Acquisitions  
New York, September 1 7, 2024  
Mphasis , (BSE:526299; NSE:  MPHASIS), a leading Information Technology (IT) solutions provider specialising in cloud and 
cognitive services, today announced the appointment of Ajay Arya as Head of Mergers & Acquisitions  (M&A) . Ajay will 
be based in New York and lead the entire process, including strategy, evaluation, deal sourcing, negotiation , diligence 
management  and post -merger integration . 
“I am absolutely delighted that Ajay will be heading up our M&A department , as we continue to build out a world -class 
M&A practice. This appointment  reinforce s Mphasis’ commitment to investing in top level talent and  further 
accelerating the strong momentum in our inorganic business . Ajay’s strategic thinking combined with his extensive 
transaction execution experience and proven leadership qualities, will be important assets in carrying out our strategic 
roadmap;  making  him a tremendous addition to the existing M&A team ,” said Nitin Rakesh, CEO and Managing 
Director, Mphasis.   
“I am looking forward to the challenge of driving M&A forward in this momentous time  of technological revolution.  
Bringing Mphasis’ strength in technology with my experience in mergers and acquisitions, and financial engineering will 
help capture more value and in  succeeding to deliver  to our  clients  and to our financial objectives . I am excited to be 
working with the Mphasis leadership as well as the M&A team to accelerate our brand promise of ‘Applying the Next ,” 
said Ajay Arya, Head of Mergers & Acquisitions, Mphasis.  
Following his successful career over 20 years  in Corporate Development, Strategy, and M&A across technology sectors, 
including Aerospace, Defense, Biotechnology, and Healthcare , Ajay’s strategic vision and leadership were instrumental in 
driving growth and success in these areas. He has spearheaded numerous M&A transactions, most recently leading a 
transformative merger and financing for a major health intelligence company. Previously, he oversaw acquisitions, 
divestitures, ventures, and investments for a $35B+ publicly traded Aero space & Defense firm, closing 30+ deals valued 
at nearly $40B since 2011. His notable transactions span healthcare technology, commercial aviation, and advanced 
defense sectors, including a $35B merger of equals.  
The existing M&A organization will roll up into Ajay.   Ajay holds a Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Accounting and Finance from 
The College of New Jersey. Before joining Mphasis, he gained extensive leadership experience across several reputable 
organizations. His career includes key leadership roles at prominent com panies such as Carrier, GeneDX, and L3Harris 
Technologies.
