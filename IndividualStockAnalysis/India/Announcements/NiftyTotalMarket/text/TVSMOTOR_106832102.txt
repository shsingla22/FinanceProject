2. Composite Scheme of Arrangement amongst TVS Motor Services Limited (“Transferor 
Company”) , TVS Motor Company Limited (“Company” or “Transferee Company” 
or “Demerged Company”) and Harita ARC Limited (“Resulting Company / TVSM 
WOS ”) and their respective shareholders and creditors under Sections 230 to 232 and 
other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 (“Act”) (“Scheme”) . The 
Transferor Company and the Resulting Company are wholly owned subsidiaries of the 
Company.  
 
The Scheme, inter alia, provides for: (i) the amalgamation of the Transferor Company with the 
Company; and (ii) the d emerger of the Demerged Undertaking (as defined in the Scheme)  from 
the Company into the Resulting Company on a going concern basis, in consideration for which 
the Resulting Company shall issue its shares to the shareholders of the Company in proportion 
to their shareholding in the Company.  
 
The Scheme is, inter alia, subject to the receipt of statutory, regulatory and customary 
approvals, including approvals from BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (collectively, the “Stock Exchanges”) , the Securities and Exchange Board of India, 
the jurisdictional National Company Law Tribunal and the shareholders and creditors
