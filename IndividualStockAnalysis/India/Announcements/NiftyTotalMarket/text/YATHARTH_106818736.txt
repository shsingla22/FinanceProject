Summary of Proceedings of the 1st/2026-27 Extra-Ordinary General Meeting of  
Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Limited 
 
The 1st/2026-27 Extra-Ordinary General Meeting (“EGM”)  of the Members of Yatharth Hospital & 
Trauma Care Services Limited (the “Company” ) held today i.e. Thursday, October 15, 2026 at 
11:00 A.M. (IST) through video conferencing /  Other Audio-Visual Means ( “VC/OAVM” ) in 
accordance with the applicable provisions of Companies Act, 2013 read with the Rules framed  
thereunder and the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure  
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and the Circulars issued by Ministry of Corporate Affairs  (“MCA”) 
and the Securities and Exchange Board of India (“SEBI”). 
 
Chairman of the Meeting 
 
Dr. Ajay Kumar Tyagi, Chairman & Whole-time Director of the Company, took the Chair and 
presided over the Meeting. 
 
Quorum 
 
The number of members as on cut-off date i.e. Thursday, October 08, 2026 were 101829.

was present and accordingly commenced the proceedings of the meeting.

EGM through VC / OAVM and also the process of voting through remote e-voting as well as e-

votes electronically.

He further informed that Dr. Sanjeev Upadhyaya, Independent Director of the Company was 
unable to attend the Meeting due to unavoidable circumstances. 
The Chairperson of the Audit Committee and Stakeholders Relationship Committee were present 
and the Chairperson of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Dr. Sanjeev Upadhyaya, 
could not attend the EGM. 
The Company Secretary requested Mr. Yatharth Tyagi, Whole-time Director to address the 
members. Thereafter, Mr. Yatharth Tyagi welcomed the Members and apprised them of the 
Company’s rationale and key terms of the proposed strategic investment by Rasmalai Limited, an 
affiliate of Advent International, through a preferential issue of equity shares and warrants, 
highlighting its potential to accelerate the Company’s growth, strengthen its financial position 
and create sustainable long-term value for the shareholders. He further briefed the Members on 
the proposed utilisation of proceeds, governance-related matters, etc. He emphasized that the 
proposed transaction is in the best interests of the Company and its shareholders and it can 
provide the capital, capability and flexibility required to accelerate responsible growth, maintain 
balance sheet strength and create sustainable long-term value for the Company. 
 
 
The Company Secretary informed the members that since this meeting was convened through 
VC/OAVM, the notice convening EGM and the corrigendum to the Notice of EGM dated October 
06, 2026 have already been circulated to the members, and the resolutions provided in the notice 
have already been put to vote through e-voting, there will be no proposing and seconding for the 
resolutions. He also apprised the members that all documents relating to the proposed issue are 
available on the Company’s website at www.yatharthhospitals.com . Thereafter, he briefly read 
out the resolutions set out in the EGM notice. 
 The Company Secretary confirmed that the Notice of the EGM and the corrigendum to the 
Notice of EGM, was sent electronically to all eligible shareholders on Tuesday, 
September 22, 2026 and Tuesday, October 06, 2026 respectively. 
 E-voting facility was also provided during the EGM through CDSL. 
 Remote e-voting was conducted from  Monday, October 12, 2026 (09:00 AM)  to 
Wednesday,  October 14, 2026 (05:00 PM) . 
 It was further informed that members who had not cast their votes through remote e-
voting could vote during the EGM. 
 Members who had registered themselves as speakers were invited to raise their queries, 
which were addressed suitably.
