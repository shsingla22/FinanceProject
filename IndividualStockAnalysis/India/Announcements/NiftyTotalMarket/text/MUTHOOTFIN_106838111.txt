Consolidated  Results  of Muthoot  Finance  Ltd 
Muthoot Finance Ltd ’s Consolidated Loan Assets Under Management grew 42% YoY to Rs. 2,09,317  crores in H1 
FY2 7 as against Rs. 1,47,673  crores in H1 FY2 6. During the half year, Consolidated Loan Assets Under Management 
grew by Rs. 27,401  crores, growth of  15%.  Consolidated Profit after tax for  H1 FY2 7 increased to  Rs. 6,103  crores 
as against Rs. 4,386  crores last year, an increase of 39% YoY.

Commenting on the Company’s performance, Mr. Alexander George Muthoot, Managing Director, said , “We are pleased 
to report another strong quarter of performance, with our Standalone Loan Assets Under Management reaching Rs. 
1,87,810 crore, marking a 42% year -on-year and 9% quarter -on-quarter growth. Our Standalone Profit After Tax for H1 
FY27 rose b y 26% YoY to Rs.5,527 crore, reflecting improved operational efficiency, prudent cost management, and a 
disciplined approach to growth. This consistent upward trajectory underscores the resilience of our business model and the 
continued trust our customers  place in us. We are also revising our gold loan growth guidance for FY27 upward by an 
additional 10%. The RBI's updated gold lending guidelines, effective 1st April 2026, involves a learning curve for us as well  
as customers, which will get stabilized ove r few quarters irrespective of which growth trajectory will continue.  
 
During the quarter Muthoot Finance added 201 branches further expanding the reach across the length and breadth of 
India.  India’s gold loan market continues to evolve, with new entrant s and rising demand creating opportunities for 
responsible growth. At Muthoot Finance, our focus remains on strengthening our services through customer trust, deeper 
reach and technology -led service delivery, while maintaining prudent lending practices and  sustaining our growth 
momentum through FY27.”  
 
Standalone  Results  of Muthoot  Finance  Ltd and  its subsidiaries  
Muthoot Finance Ltd (MFIN),  India’s largest gold loan NBFC in terms of loan portfolio, registered profit after tax of 
Rs. 5,527 crores in H1 FY2 7 as against Rs. 4,391  crores in H1 FY2 6, an increase of 26% YoY. The profit after tax for 
Q2FY27 stood at Rs. 2,977  crores as against Rs. 2,345  crores in Q2 FY26, an increase of 27% YoY. Loan AUM stood at 
Rs. 1,87,810  crores in H1 FY2 7 as compared to Rs. 1,32,305  crores in H1 FY2 6, registering a growth of 42% YoY. 
During H1 FY27, Loan AUM increased by Rs. 24,984  crores, registering a growth of 15% and Gold Loan AUM 
increased by Rs. 24,872  crores, registering a growth of 16%. During Q2 FY27, Gold Loan AUM increased by Rs. 15,658 
crores . 
Muthoot Homefin (India) Ltd (M HIL),  the wholly owned subsidiary ’s loan AUM stood at Rs. 3,460 crores in H1 FY27 
as against Rs. 3,247  crores in H1 FY2 6, an increase of 7% YoY. The loan disbursement for H1 FY27 stood at Rs. 277  
crores. Total revenue for H1 FY2 7 stood at Rs. 218  crores as against Rs. 214 crores in H1 FY2 6, registering a growth 
of 2% YoY. Profit after tax stood at Rs. 11 crores in H1 FY2 7 as against Rs. 10 crores in H1 FY2 6, an increase of 9% 
YoY.  Stage III Loan Asset s stood at 2.50% as of September  30, 202 6. 
M/s. Belstar Microfinance Limited (BML)  is an RBI registered micro finance NBFC, and a subsidiary company where  
Muthoot Finance holds 66.13% stake. Loan AUM for H1 FY2 7 stood at Rs. 7,876 crores  as against Rs. 7,715  crores in 
H1 FY26,  an increase of 2%. Total revenue for H1 FY27 stood at Rs. 906 crores as against Rs.  840  crores in H1 FY26, 
registering a growth of 8% YoY.  It achieved a Profit after tax of Rs. 144 crores in H1 FY27 as against loss of Rs. 160  
crores in H1 FY26.  Stage III L oan assets  stood at  3.26% in H1 FY27 , as compared to 4.58% last year.  Consequent to 
RBI allowing micro finance companies to have 40% non -microfinance loan portfolio, it has opened 163  gold loan 
branches as at  H1 FY2 7 to diversify the loan product portfolio.  The gold loan portfolio stood at Rs. 484  crores as at H1 
FY27.  
Muth oot Insurance Brokers Pvt. Limited (MIBPL),  an IRDA registered Direct Broker in insurance products and a 
wholly owned subsidiary company generated a total premium collection amounting to Rs. 144  crores in H1 FY2 7. Total 
revenue for H1 FY2 7 stood at Rs. 56 crores. It achieved a Profit after tax of Rs. 30 crores in H1 FY2 7 as against Rs.  23 
crores in H1 FY 26, an increase of 34%  YoY .

About  Muthoot  Homefin  (India)  Limited:  
MHIL is a Housing  Finance  Company  registered  with National  Housing  Bank (NHB). It is a wholly owned subsidiary  of 
Muthoot  Finance Limited. MHIL’s prime goal is to contribute towards financial inclusion of LMI families by opening doors 
of formal housing finance  to them.  Its focus is on exte nding  Affordable  Housing  Finance.  MHIL would  be concentrating  
primarily  on retail  housing  loans  in the  initial  stages.  It operates  on a ‘Hub  and Spoke’  model,  with the centralized  
processing based out of corporate office at Mumbai. MHIL has operations  in 15 states and 3 Union territories  - 
Maharashtra (including Mumbai), Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Kerala, Karnataka, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, 
Haryana, Chandigarh,  Uttar Pradesh,  Chhattisgarh,  Punjab,  Tamil  Nadu,  Delhi, Pondicherry, Ut tarakhand and Himachal 
Pradesh.

safety regarding timely payment of financial obligations and carry lowest credit risk” from ICRA Ltd. MHIL has long term 
debt rating of AA+/(stable) for its Bank Limits and Non -Convertible Debentures which indicates  “High degree of safety 
with regard to timely servicing of financial obligations and carries very low credit risk” from CRISIL Ltd. It also has short  
term debt rating of ‘A1+’ for its Commercial Paper programme which indicates, “Very strong degree of safet y regarding 
timely payment of financial obligations and carry lowest credit risk” and long -term debt rating of AA+/(stable) for its 
Non -Convertible Debentures which indicates “High degree of safety regarding timely servicing of financial obligations 
and ca rries very low credit risk ” from CARE Ratings Ltd.

About  Belstar  Microfinance  Limited  (BML):  
BML  was incorporated  in January  1988  at Bangalore,  and the company  was registered  with  the RBI in March  2001 
as a Non - Banking Finance Company. The company was reclassified  as “NBFC -MFI” by RBI effective from 11th December 
2013. Muthoot Finance holds 66.13% of equity share capital in BML. BML was acquired by the ‘Hand in Hand’ group in 
September  2008 to provide scalab le microfinance services to entrepreneurs  nurtured by ‘Hand in Hand’s’ Self Help 
Group (SHG) program. The company commenced its first lending operations at Haveri District of Karnataka  in March  
2009  to 3 SHGs,  22 members  for INR 0.2  million.  
 
As of September  30, 202 6, BML operations  are spread over 19 states and 2 UT  (Tamil Nadu, Andhra  Pradesh, Karnataka, 
Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra,  Kerala, Odisha, Chhattisgarh,  Gujarat, Rajasthan, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West 
Bengal, Pondicherry, Telang ana, Jharkhand, Haryana, Punjab, Tripura and Delhi). It  has 1,362  branches,  with  261 
controlling regional offices  and employing  10,417  staff.  Loan AUM has stood at Rs. 7,876 crores as of  September  30, 
202 6. Total Revenue for H1 FY2 7 stood at Rs. 906  crores , and Net  worth stood  at Rs. 1,939  crores  as of September  30, 
202 6.
