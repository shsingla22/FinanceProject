Subject: Completion of Acquisition of FMCG business of Triology Solutions Private Limited 
(TSPL)  
 
In continuation to our earlier communication dated October 31, 2025, wherein Godrej 
Consumer Products Limited had informed that it had entered into an agreement to acquire 
FMCG business of Triology Solutions Private Limited (TSPL)  by way of a slump sale on a going 
concern basis, we wish to inform you that the said acquisition has been completed today.  
 
TSPL is an Indian FMCG company operating primarily in the male grooming category under 
the Muuchstac  brand.
