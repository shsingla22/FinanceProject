Commenting on Q4 FY25 and FY25 performance  
 
Dr. Anish Shah, Group CEO & Managing Director , M&M Ltd . said, “We have delivered  strong 
growth on the back of stellar execution  in F25 . Auto and Farm continue to gain market share and

strengthening client positioning and margin expansion . MMFSL  has maintained GS3 under 4% as 
committed , remains focused on controls and has delivered 33% growth in profits . Our Growth 
Gems are scaling up well . We continue to build strong businesses which will deliver significant 
value to our stakeholders .”

our highest -ever Q4 market share of 41.2%, gaining 180 bps YoY . In F25, our Auto Standalone 
PBIT margin improved by 110 bps and core tractor PBIT margins improved by 200 bps ”
