 PRESS  RELEASE  
Pricol and Warburg Pincus Jointly Announce Agreement for 
Promoter Stake Sale in  Pricol Autotech Limited  
 
• Pricol announced the proposed demerger of its Driver Information and Connected Vehicle 
Solutions (DICVS) business into Pricol Autotech Limited through a scheme of arrangement  
on 27th June 2026.  
• Warburg  Pincus to  acquire the  Pricol promoter group’s entire  38.51% stake  in Pricol 
Autotech Limited,  following the proposed demerger and  listing  of Pricol Autotech Limited.  
• Warburg  Pincus  to launch  a mandatory open  offer  to Pricol  Autotech Limited’s  public 
shareholders following completion of the demerger and listing on NSE and BSE, in 
accordance with applicable SEBI regulations.  
• This transaction will build on Pricol’s strong legacy and accelerate Pricol Autotech Limited’s 
next  phase  of growth, expanding  its capabilities  in instrument  clusters,  connected  vehicle  
and e -cockpit solutions, with the ambition to build a broader automotive electronics 
business.  
• Following the proposed demerger, the Actuation, Control and Fluid Management Systems 
(ACFMS) business will continue to operate under  Pricol Limited , while  the Polymer business 
will continue to operate through  Pricol’s  wholly owned subsidiary Pricol Precision Product s 
Private Limited.  Both businesses will remain under the ownership and management of the 
current promoter group, the Mohan family, with  no change  in their shareholding.  
 
 
Coimbatore / Mumbai, India, 13 November 2026 — Pricol Limited (“Pricol”), a leading Indian 
automotive  technology and  precision  components  company,  and  Warburg  Pincus,  the pioneer  of 
global  growth investing, today jointly announced that Warburg Pincus has entered into definitive 
agreements with Pricol’s promoters to acquire their entire 38.51% shareholding in Pricol 
Autotech Limited (“Pricol Autotech” or the “Company”) following the proposed demerger of 
Pricol’s Driver Information & Connected Vehicle Solutions (“DICVS”) business into Pricol 
Autotech. The proposed acquisition is subject to the demerger becoming effective and requisite 
regulatory and other approvals.  
The proposed transaction represents an important next chapter for a business built by Pricol’s 
promoters  and  management  over five  decades.  With  a strong  foundation  in product  innovation, 
engineering capabilities and trusted relationships with leading vehicle manufacturers, Pricol 
Autotech is expected to benefit from Warburg Pincus long -term investment approach, deep 
sector expertise and global network as it explores opportuniti es in India n and international 
markets.  
Pricol Autotech will house the DICVS business, one of India’s leading providers of driver 
information systems  and  automotive electronics,  including  instrument clusters,  telematics,  
sensors and e -cockpit solutions. It serves original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) across 
two -wheelers, three -wheelers, four -wheelers, commercial vehicles and off -highway segments. 
 
 The DICVS business reported approximately  INR  2,400 crore in  revenue  in FY26, representing  
around  60%  of Pricol’s  consolidated revenue.  
 
Vikram Mohan, Chairman and Managing Director of Pricol Limited, said, “Over five decades, 
Pricol has grown from a small Coimbatore factory into a trusted technology partner to vehicle  
manufacturers, with  our driver  information  and  connected  vehicle  business  at the heart of  that 
journey. The proposed demerger will give Pricol Autotech the focus and independence to realise 
its full potential. We believe Warburg Pincus, with its long and storied history both in India and 
globally, extensive  resources, global connections and deep automotive  sector  expertise,  is the  
right custodian to  take  the business forward into  the next phase of  its growth  story.  We are proud  
of what  our people have built and confident that we are placing Pricol Autotech in capable hands 
for its next chapter of growth . We will continue to focus on growing the businesses of Pricol 
Limited to achieve greater success .” 
 
Anish  Saraf, Managing  Director  at Warburg  Pincus,  said, “We  are grateful  to Vikram  Mohan  and 
the Pricol  promoters  for the trust they  have  placed  in us. They  and  the management  team  have  
built an  exceptional  business  with  deep  OEM  relationships,  strong  engineering  and  product  
development capabilities,  and  a respected  position  in India’s automotive  industry.  We recognise  
the responsibility that comes with building on this legacy. We look forward to partnering with

Swapnil Sinha, Managing Director at Warburg Pincus, said,  “The automotive components

Transaction overview  
On 27 June 2026, Pricol’s Board of Directors approved a Scheme of Arrangement for the demerger 
of its DICVS business into Pricol Autotech. Under the Scheme, eligible Pricol shareholders are 
expected to receive one equity share of Pricol Autotech for each e quity share held in Pricol Limited, 
and Pricol Autotech is proposed to be listed on the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) and 
BSE Limited (BSE). Following the demerger, Pricol  Limited  will continue to operate its Actuation, 
Control & Fluid Management Systems and Precision Products businesses.  
 
Following the Scheme becoming effective, Warburg Pincus will make a mandatory open offer to 
public shareholders and complete the acquisition of promoter’s stake to become Pricol 
Autotech’s  largest shareholder. Per applicable SEBI regulations, the open offer will be to acquire 
up to an additional 26% of the Company’s share capital. The Scheme and proposed transaction 
remain subject to applicable approvals and conditions, including, as releva nt, approvals from 
shareholders, creditors, stock exchanges, SEBI, the National Company Law Tribunal and the 
Competition Commission of India.
