of up to INR 5763,76,63,332 /- (Indian Rupees Five Thousand Seven Hundred Sixty Three Crores Seventy Six Lakhs Sixty Three Thousand Three

Announcement and in the detailed public statement (“ DPS ”) and the letter of offer (“ LoF”) that are proposed to be issued in accordance with the SEBI 
(SAST) Regulations.

compliance with Regulation s 3 and 4 of the SEBI (SAST)  Regulations pursuant to the execution of the SSA and SHA . This Offer is not subject to any 
minimum level of acceptance.
