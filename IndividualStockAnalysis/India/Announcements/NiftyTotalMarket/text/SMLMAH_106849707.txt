Sub: Outcome of the Meeting of the Board of Directors of SML Mahindra Limited (“the Company”) 
held on 6th November, 2026 pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  
 
In furtherance to our intimation dated 2nd November, 2026, we wish to inform that the Board of Directors 
of the Company at its meeting held today i.e. 6th November, 2026 has inter-alia, considered and approved 
raising of funds through the issuance of fully paid-up equity shares of the Company of the face value of 
Rs. 10/- each by way of a rights issue (the " Rights Issue ") for an amount not exceeding Rs. 1450 Crore 
(Rupees One Thousand Four Hundred and Fifty Crore) to the eligible equity shareholders of the Company 
as on the record date (to be determined and notified subsequently), subject to receipt of regulatory and 
statutory approvals, as may be applicable in accordance with the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, and other applicable laws.  
 
For the purposes of giving effect to the rights issue, the Board or the Rights Issue Committee shall, decide 
and approve the detailed terms and conditions of the Rights issue, including but not limited to 
determination of issue price, rights entitlement ratio, record date, rights issue period, timing and terms of 
payment and any such other matters as may be necessary and incidental thereto.  
 
The meeting of the Board commenced at 11:30 AM and concluded at 12:03 PM.
