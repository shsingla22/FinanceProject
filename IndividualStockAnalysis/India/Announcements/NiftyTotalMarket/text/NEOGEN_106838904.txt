Q2 FY27 PRESS RELEASE                         Nov 1, 2026  
 
Neogen Chemicals Limited (Neogen) reported strong  financial performance for the second  
quarter and half -year ended 3 0th September , 2026. In Q 2 FY27 (consolidated), revenues 
stood at INR 296 crore, higher by 42% Y-o-Y. The Company delivered robust growth , despite 
challenges arising from geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, supply chain disruptions, and 
rising feedstock costs, driven by sustained volume growth across its core segments and 
anchored by Organolithium expansion into high -growth emerging applications . 
This was bolstered by accelerated scale up in Neogen Ionics (NIL)  with Q2 FY27 revenue 
reach ing INR 37 crore (vs. INR 5.4 crore in Q 2 FY26).  
EBITDA for Q 2 FY27 (consolidated) stood at INR 45 crore, higher by 51% Y-o-Y. Achieved 
strong EBITDA performance despite higher cost base related to Dahej plant rebuild, tolling 
arrangements, NIL capital expenditure, and elevated input costs . Consolidated EBITDA 
reflects the initial fixed -cost absorption at NIL, while revenue scale -up and capacity utilization 
are still ramping up, setting the foundation for future operating leverage . 
Neogen’s profit after tax for Q 2 FY27 (consolidated) stood at INR 8 crore , higher by 144%           
Y-o-Y. PAT reflected core operational performance, impacted temporarily by higher interest  
and depreciation costs from ongoing CAPEX initiatives . Interest expenses are expected to 
decline moving ahead, supported by term debt reduction using QIP proceeds and lower 
borrowing costs following credit rating upgrade . 
Consolidated Earnings Per Share (EPS) for Q 2 FY27 stood at INR 2.96  per share (not 
annualized).

Commenting on the Q 2 FY27 performance, Dr. Harin Kanani, Managing Director, Neogen 
Chemicals said:   
“We have delivered a robust all -round performance, demonstrating remarkable agility despite 
a challenging macroeconomic backdrop marked by geopolitical conflicts, supply chain friction, 
feedstock cost inflation, and the temporary unavailability of our Dahe j facility. This 
performance underscores the resilience of our business model, as we swiftly pivoted toward 
applications with better demand visibility and higher volumes. This momentum was further 
reinforced by accelerated ramp -up in our Battery Chemicals business.  
In our Battery Chemicals business, we are transitioning from capital investment to execution 
and ramp -up phase. We continue to deepen our engagement with leading domestic and 
global cell manufacturers for supply of electrolyte and lithium salts. With upcom ing ACC 
battery manufacturing plants coming online in India and a progressive shift by US electrolyte 
manufacturers toward sourcing from non -FEOC supplier s from India, we expect a substantial 
scale -up in Q4 FY27 and beyond. Our dual facilities at Dahej SEZ and Pakhajan are uniquely 
positioned to cater to these accelerated requirements, establishing Neogen as one of the 
largest Indian battery component manufa cturers.  
A key strategic milestone during the period was the successful completion of our maiden QIP, 
raising INR 600 crore, which was oversubscribed 6.5x, drawing strong participation from 
marquee domestic and global institutional investors. This overwhelming resp onse serves as a 
strong endorsement of market confidence in our long -term growth story. The proceeds are 
being strategically deployed toward de -leveraging our balance sheet, preparing for funding 
incremental business requirements and growth initiatives.  
Regarding our Dahej facility insurance claims, we are pleased to share that the majority of the 
recognized claim amount has already been received through successive on -account payouts. 
Discussions with the insurers for the remaining balance are progressing  constructively, and we 
expect further settlements in due course as the final assessments conclude.  
Looking ahead, the outlook for both Neogen Standalone and our Battery Chemicals business 
remains encouraging as we enter the next phase of transformational growth, supporting 
India’s vision for self -reliance in the energy storage and advanced battery mater ials 
ecosystem. Guided by a clear execution roadmap and supported by a strengthened balance 
sheet, our full -year consolidated guidance remains on track as we stay focused on driving 
sustainable growth and delivering long -term value for our stakeholders.”

About Neogen Chemicals Limited  
Incorporated in 1989, Neogen Chemicals Ltd. (NSE Code: NEOGEN; BSE Code: 542665) is India’s 
one of the leading manufacturers of Bromine -based and Lithium -based specialty chemicals.  
Products  
Its specialty chemicals product offerings comprise Organic as well as Inorganic chemicals. Its 
products are used in pharmaceutical and agrochemical intermediates, engineering fluids, 
electronic chemicals, polymer additives, water treatment, construction ch emicals, and aroma 
chemicals, flavours and fragrances, specialty polymers, Chemicals and Vapour Absorption Chillers 
– original -equipment manufacturers and with new upcoming usage in lithium -ion battery 
materials for energy storage and Electric Vehicles (EV ) application. Over the years, Neogen  has 
expanded its range of products and at present, manufactures an extensive range of specialty 
chemicals which find application across various industries in India and the world. It has a product 
portfolio of over 313 products.  
In addition to manufacturing specialty chemicals, Neogen also undertakes custom synthesis and 
contract manufacturing where the product is developed and customised primarily for a specific 
customer, but process know -how and technical specifications are deve loped in -house.  
The Company has announced plans to utilise its three decades of experience in Lithium Chemistry 
to manufacture Lithium -Ion battery materials with an initial investment plan to manufacture 
electrolytes and Lithium electrolyte salts.  
Facilities  
The Company operates out of its four manufacturing facilities located in Mahape, Navi Mumbai 
in Maharashtra, Dahej SEZ, Bharuch, Karakhadi, Vadodara in Gujarat and Patancheru, Hyderabad 
(in January 2025, Buli Chemicals India Private Limited was merged with  Neogen Chemicals, their 
facility was located in Patancheru).  
Neogen Chemicals Limited also has a wholly owned subsidiary – Neogen Ionics Limited, dedicated 
to projects related to battery materials. Neogen Ionics Limited has one of the earliest LIB 
electrolyte facility at its Dahej SEZ site since April 2024.
