Dear Shareholder(s),  
 
Sub: Request for  Dematerialis ation of  Physical Shares  
 
As you may be aware, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) ha s vide Circular dated                   
September 7, 2020,  has prohibited transfer of shares held in physical form effective April 1, 2021.  Further, 
SEBI vide its notification dated January 24, 2022  and Circular dated January 25, 2022, has also prohibited 
issuance of physical share certificate(s) whilst processing any requests such as issuance of duplicate share 
certificate(s), transmission, transpo sition  etc.  
 
Pursuant to the SEBI Circular dated 30th January 2026, the Registrar and Share Transfer Agent ( RTA ) / 
Company shall verify and  process the demat service requests and  upon satisfactory verification , credit the 
shares  directly to the shareholder's demat account within 30 days from the receipt of the request . 
 
Post confirmation of the demat erialisation  request, the Depositor y / RTA / Compan y shall intimate  the 
shareholder  regarding the successful credit and  dematerialisation of shares . 
 
In view of the aforesaid requirement and also on account of the following benefits, we request you to kindly 
dematerialise your physical shares at the earliest  by contac ting your Depository Participant . 
 
There are several benefits of  holding shares in dematerialised form , such as:  
 
• Convenient mode of holding securities, especially in case you are holding shares of many companies.  
• Immediate transfer of securities.  
• Reduction in paperwork  and transaction cost.  
• Saving of time - Change in address  / bank account particulars etc . recorded with Depository Participant 
gets registered electronically with all companies in which you hold securities.  
• Facilitates direct credit of shares in case of allotment under Initial Public Offer, Rights Issue, Bonus Issue,  
Stock Split etc.  
• Eases portfolio management and provides on -line access through internet . 
 
It is in your interest to get your shares dematerialised at the earliest.
