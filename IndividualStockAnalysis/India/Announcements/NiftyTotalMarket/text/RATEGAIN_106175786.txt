 3. Area of business of the 
entity(ies) BCV Social LLC - Transferor Company, specializes in 
delivering Data as a Service (DaaS), Distribution, and 
Market Technologies (MarTech) services tailored for

RateGain Adara Inc., Transferee Company specializes in

insights, audience segmentation, and advertising 
solutions, empowering travel brands to make informed

enhancing customer engagement throughout the travel

customer retention.  4. Rationale for

scale of operations, optimize operational and

management.
