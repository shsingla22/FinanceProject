In terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform you that the Board of Directors of 
the Company has approved sale of 100% equity shares of Welspun Home Solutions 
Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company to Welspun Advanced Materials 
(India) Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company. 
On completion of the aforesaid sale, Welspun Home Solutions Limited shall cease to be 
a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company and shall become a stepdown wholly 
owned subsidiary of the Company.
