CA 96/2025:- This company application has been filed seeking correction crept in the 
order on account of typographical error at para 18 of the order dated 27.02.2025, 
wherein, the appointed date has been mentioned as 14.12.2024. Learned counsel for the 
Applicant submits that at two other places at para no. 4 as also in the RDs Report which 
has been reproduced in the order, the appointed date at para 2(f) has been mentioned as 
14.12.2023, which is the correct appointed date. 
Learned counsel further brings to our knowledge part 1 of the Scheme, where 
definitions and share capital are detailed and more specific to point no. 1.4 where the 
definition of appointed date has been given which means 14.12.2023. learned counsel 
submits that apart from Rule 11, Rule 154 of the NCLT Rules also enables such 
rectification sought.

than the erstwhile members who have passed the order can direct such rectification, 
learned counsel for the applicant has cited the provisions contained at150, 151 and
