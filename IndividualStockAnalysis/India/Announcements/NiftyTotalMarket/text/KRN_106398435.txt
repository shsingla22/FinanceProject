SUB: BUSINESS UPDATE – ACQUISITION OF THE BUSINESS OF BUS AIR-CONDITIONING DIVISION FROM SPHERE 
REFRIGERATION SYSTEMS PRIVATE LIMITED (“SRSPL”) 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
We would like to inform that the Board of Directors of KRN HVAC Products Private Limited, (“KRN HVAC”), a wholly 
owned subsidiary of the company at their meeting held today has approved the signing of a Business Transfer 
Agreement (“BTA”) with Sphere Refrigeration Systems Private Limited (“SRSPL”) under which KRN HVAC will acquire 
the business of the Bus Air-Conditioning division carried out by SRSPL.  
 
Consequent to this and after signing of the BTA, the business of the Bus Air-Conditioning division so far carried out 
by SRSPL will be transferred to and carried out by KRN HVAC. The above-mentioned business acquisition is expected 
to enhance revenues and profitability.  
 
Details as per Master circular Ref No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 11th November 2024 is provided in 
the Annexure A attached herewith.
