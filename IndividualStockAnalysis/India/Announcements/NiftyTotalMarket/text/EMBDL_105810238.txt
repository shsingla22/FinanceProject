Dear Sir/Ma’am, 
 
This is with reference to our letter dated April 5, 2024 with the subject ‘ Outcome of the meeting of the 
Board of Directors of Indiabulls Real Estate Limited held today i.e. April 5, 2024 ’ (the “April 5 
Letter”) in relation to the matters approved by the Board which includes, inter alia , fund-raise via 
preferential issue of equity shares and convertible warrants, on a private placement basis, for a cash 
consideration, aggregating to approximately INR 3,910.93 crore.

typographical errors were included, in the April 5 Letter. Accordingly, Annexure A, Annexure – B(i)

Eden as ‘~31.3 acre’ instead of ‘~31.7 acre’ at point (b) of the cover letter and Annexure-C to the April 
5 Letter. 
 
All other contents of the April 5 Letter, save and except as amended and replaced by this corrigendum, 
shall remain unchanged, and the April 5 Letter shall stand modified to the extent of, and be read together
