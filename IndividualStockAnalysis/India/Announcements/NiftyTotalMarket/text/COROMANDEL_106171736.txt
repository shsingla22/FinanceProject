of Voting Share Capital) held by the Tag Holder Seller 2 (“ Tag Holder 2 Sale Shares ”) will be purchased by Acquirer concurrently with the

(SAST) Regulations and in accordance with the terms of the  share purchase agreement dated March 12, 2025 (the “ Tag Holder  Share Purchase 
Agreement 2 ” or “ Tag Holder  2 SPA ”) among Acquirer  and Tag Holder Seller 2.
