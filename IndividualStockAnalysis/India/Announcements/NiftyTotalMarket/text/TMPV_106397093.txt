Sub: Demerger of Commercial Vehicles business  of Tata Motors Limited and transfer

We had, vide our earlier letter  bearing reference no. 18764 dated October 1, 2025, intimated 
about the effectiveness of the Composite Scheme of Arrangement amongst  
Tata Motors Limited (“the Company” or “TML” or “Demerged Company” or “Amalgamated 
Company”), TML Commercial Vehicles Limited (“TMLCV” or “Resulting Company”), and Tata 
Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited (“TMPV” or “Amalgamating Company”) and their 
respectiv e shareholders, under Sections 230 -232 and other applicable provisions of the 
Companies Act, 2013 and the Rules framed thereunder (“Scheme”), sanctioned by the Hon’ble 
National Company Law Tribunal, Mumbai Bench (“NCLT”), vide its Order dated  
August 25, 2025 and Rectification Order dated September 10, 2025 .  
 
Consequent  upon demerger of the Commercial Vehicles business  of the Company and  the 
concurrent transfer of its Group Companies to TMLCV , we hereby wish to inform the 
Exchanges that the entire shareholding held by the Company in the following entities will stand 
transferred to TMLCV, w.e.f ., the effective date of the Scheme .
