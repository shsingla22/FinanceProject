Pursuant  to the Composite  Scheme  of Arrangement  (“the  Scheme”)  sanctioned  by the Hon'ble  National  Company  Law Tribunal, 
Mumbai and Bengaluru Benches, the Board of Directors of Quess Corp Limited (“the Company”), on December 09, 2019, allotted 
7,14,56,240 equity shares of face value ₹10 each, fully paid -up, to the eligible equity shareholders of Thomas Cook (India) Limited 
(“TCIL”) as on December 06, 2019 (“Record Date”), in the ratio of 1,889 equity shares of the Company for every 10,000 equity shares 
of ₹1  each held in TCIL.

proceeds  were  distributed  to the  eligible  shareholders  in proportion  to their  fractional  entitlements.  The  amount  remaining 
unpaid/unclaimed is held in a separate bank account of the Company titled "Quess Corp Limited Demerger Scheme" .

amalgamation  that  remain  unpaid/unclaimed  for seven  years  or more  are required  to be credited  to the Investor  Education  and
