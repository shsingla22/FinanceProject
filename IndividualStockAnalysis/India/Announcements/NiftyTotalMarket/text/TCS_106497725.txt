(ii) TCS North America Corporation (a wholly owned subsidiary of ListEngage) acquired  
the remaining  14% of the equity interest in Coastal Cloud through acquisition of 100% 
of the equity interest in SCP V -B Blocker III LLC (“SCP Blocker”).  
  
SCP Blocker was formed on May 13, 2020. It does not have any operations or assets, other than holding the 14% equity interest in Coastal Cloud. The  transaction does not fall 
within related party transaction(s)  and the promoter/promoter group/group companies do 
not have any interest  in SCP Blocker .
