PRESS  RELEASE                                                                                                                                                             
 
 
Piramal Finance reports PAT of ₹  545 crore  in Q2 FY2027, up 6 7% yoy 
 
Successfully raises ₹  3,850 crore to strengthen capital base and support retail -led growth  
 
 
 
 
Mumbai, India | October  19, 2026 : Piramal Finance Limited (‘Piramal Fin’), one of India’s leading Non -
Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), reported Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₹  545 Cr, up 67% year-on-year 
(yoy), for the second  quarter ended September  30, 2026.  With this , H1 FY 2027 PAT s tands at ₹ 1,006  Cr, 
up 6 7% year-on-year ( yoy).  
 
Assets Under Management (AUM) increased 2 4% yoy to ₹ 1,13,112 Cr, driven by 3 0% yoy increase  in 
Growth  AUM , which exclude s the discontinued legacy business . Retail disbursements increased 34% yoy 
to ₹  14,646 Cr, reflecting sustained  customer demand and continued  business momentum.  The 
Company's nationwide footprint expanded to 838 branches across 621 cities in 2 5 states, while its 
customer franchise grew 26% yoy to 6.5 million, further strengthening its presence across India.  
 
The Company completed an equity capital raise of  ₹ 3,850 crore , comprising  a QIP of ₹ 2,100 crore  and a 
preferential allotment of ₹ 1,750 crore  of warrants to the promoter group , strengthen ing its capital base 
to support future retail -led growth.  
 
Today, the company also announce d the appointment of Mr. Dinesh Kumar Khara, former Chairman of 
the State Bank of India (SBI), as an Independent Director for a five -year term . With  a distinguished career  
of nearly four decades , his experience, judgement and deep understanding of financial services will be 
invaluable, and the company look s forward to his counsel and guidance.  
 
 
Speaking on the performance,  Anand Piramal, Chairman, Piramal Finance Limited , said,  "Q2 marks 
another quarter of consistent performance across growth, profitability and predictability , with our 
Growth  AUM increasing 30 % yoy. We are encouraged by the confidence shown in us through our first 
capital raise as Piramal Finance, including strong participation from domestic and global investors.  
 
This quarter also marks five  years since  the DHFL merger, an important milestone  in our transformation 
into a diversified, retail -led financial institution  serving around 6.5 million customers  across Bharat. We 
are grateful for their trust and  recognise the responsibility that comes with this scale. As we grow, our  
focus remains on expanding responsible access to formal credit , combining our physical network with  
technology and AI to serve customers better . We remain committed to delivering sustained, profitable 
growth and building a strong  and sustainable institution for the long term.”

Piramal.ai  – Delivering me asurable impact at scale  
Piramal Finance continues to scale GenAI adoption through Piramal.ai, embedding AI across core business 
operations  and moving beyond pilots to day -to-day integration. This is enhancing productivity, customer 
experience and decision -making across the organisation.  
AI adoption and engineering productivity strengthened . GenAI usage grew over 5x yoy, with token consumption 
rising to 476 bn in Q2 FY27 from 91 bn in Q2 FY26 . AI now generates 57% of the overall code , reflecting deeper 
integration into engineering workflows.  
Operating leverage and frontline productivity strengthened . Disbursement per branch increased 17% yoy and 
disbursement per employee rose 10% yoy. AI-enabled hiring supported interviews of over 2,600 relationship 
managers, helping accelerate recruitment and frontline expansion.  
Business growth and stakeholder engagement advanced . Voice AI enabled ₹  102 crore in cross -sell 
disbursements, while ARYA, the AI -enabled work assistant, re solved  ~5.6 lakh queries, up 45% yoy. The Company 
also launched PIA (Piramal Investor Assistant), an AI -powered investor relations agent that enables investors to 
analyse financial performance across quarter s and access management commentary from past earnings calls.  
Underwriting and fraud risk management enhanced . AI analysed ~1.94 lakh bank statements and processed 2.13 
lakh images to support credit decisions. Document fraud intelligence scanned over 6 lakh documents, while AI 
agents generated fraud alerts across ~20.9 lakh cases, strengthening data -driven risk assessment.  
Collections efficiency improved . Monthly hands -free collections increased over 2.5x yoy to ₹ 754 crore in Q2 FY27 
from ₹  300 crore in Q2 FY26, reflecting the growing application of AI in collections operations.  
Compliance and customer engagement became more automated . Over 20.8 lakh documents were identified for 
masking/Aadhaar masking, up 59% yoy from 13.1 lakh in Q2 FY26. Customer resolutions handled through 
WhatsApp, Mobile app and email automation increased to 74%. Live voice and chat bots increased to 49 from 12 
and accounted for 41% of customer onboardings.  
Overall, Piramal.ai is delivering measurable gains across productivity, business growth, risk management, 
collections , customer and stakeholder engagement.

About Piramal Finance Limited:  
Piramal Finance Limited is a retail -led upper -layer NBFC with a pan -India presence. As o n 30th September  2026, the 
company manages Assets Under Management (AUM) of over INR 1.10 lakh crore and has served over 6 .5 million 
customers across 2 5 states. The company operates a distinctive phygital model combining high -touch engagement 
across 13,000+  pin codes as on 30th September , 2026 with high -tech capabilities including machine learning 
models, agentic AI tools and real -time dashboards. Piramal Finance is rated ‘AA+’ with a ‘Stable’ Outlook  by CRISIL, 
ICRA and CARE for its domestic long -term debt, ‘BB’ by S&P Global internationally, and ‘Ba2’ with a Stable  outlook 
by Moody’s.  
In Retail Lending, Piramal Finance offers a diversified portfolio spanning affordable housing loans, loan against 
property, gold loans, microfinance, used car loan, personal loans, insurance solutions, and small business credit, with 
a presence across metro -adjacent,  semi -urban, and rural markets. In Wholesale Lending, the company provides 
asset -backed, data -driven financing solutions across real estate and select non -real estate sectors, with a focus on 
mid-income residential development and bespoke capital solutions for mid -market corporates.  
With its digital -first approach and AI -enabled platforms, Piramal Finance is committed to expanding access to 
affordable credit and driving inclusive growth across India.
