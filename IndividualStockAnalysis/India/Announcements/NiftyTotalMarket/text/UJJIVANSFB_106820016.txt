The Bank delivered highest quarterly PAT of ₹365 Cr,  with RoA and RoE at 2.4% and 19.7 %, respectively . This performance is 
supported by robust credit expansion, a growin g deposit franchise , an increasing share of secured assets  and all-round financial 
discipline . The Bank remains on track to achieve the upper end of our guidance at 2.0% RoA . Bank will continue to invest in 
distribution, technology and customer engagement.   
On the key operational performance t otal deposits increased by 29.2% year -on-year to ₹50,643 crore, crossing the ₹50,000 
crore milestone, with CASA constituting 26.0% of deposits. The Bank maintained its cost of funds at 6.9%, supported by deposi t 
repricing, although funding costs are expected to increase gradually. During the quarter, Ujjivan als o introduced the ASBA

Growth across secured businesses rema ined healthy  at 47% YoY . Affordable Housing reached ₹10,056 crore, while Micro

About Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Limited:  
Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Limited is a Scheduled Commercial Bank serving ~1 Cr customers through 880 branches across 352 districts in 26 
states and Union Territories , supported  by robust digital channels. The Bank offers a diversified portfolio spanning ac ross Affordable Housing, 
MSME, Agri, Vehicle, Gold, Micro -Mortgage, FIG, and Microfinance (group and individual) loans.  As of Sep 30, 2026, Ujjivan’s gross loan book 
stood at ₹ 45,699 Cr, with d eposits of ₹ 50,643 Cr and a Net  worth of ₹ 7,532 Cr. The Bank is rated AA - (Stable) for long -term facilities and A1+ 
for short -term instruments by CARE Ratings and ICRA , respectively , reflecting the sustained strength of the banks performance and balance 
sheet.
