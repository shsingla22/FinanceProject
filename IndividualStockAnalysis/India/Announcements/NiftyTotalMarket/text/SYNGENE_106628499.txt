Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para A of Part A of Schedule III of Securities and Exchange Board of

• the proposal for  Voluntary liquidation of Syngene Manufacturing Solutions Limited  (SMSL) , a Wholly 
Owned Subsidiary (which is not a material subsidiary) of the Company as per the applicable provisions
