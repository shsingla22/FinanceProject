Ref:   Our letter dated 14th March 2025 – Notice of the adjourned meeting of the Secured 
Creditors 
 
 
This is in continuation to our letter cited above on the subject. We would like to inform that as 
per the directions of the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Hyderabad Bench (NCLT) 
the meeting of the Secured Creditors of the Company, was held on 30th January 2025 and was 
adjourned at the request of the participants thereat. The adjourned meeting of the Secured 
Creditors was held today i.e. 16th April 2025 at 11.30 a.m for considering and approving the 
proposed Scheme of Arrangement between NCC Infrastructure Holdings Limited (Transferor 
Company) and NCC Limited (Transferee Company), and their respective Shareholders. 
 
Mr. Md. Hidayathullah Shareef, who was appointed as the Chairman of the meeting of the 
secured creditors by the Hon'ble NCLT conducted the proceedings of the Meeting. 14 secured 
creditors representing 70.55% in value of the total outstanding debt to the secured creditors 
were present at the meeting. Working capital consortium lenders representing 59.14% in value 
of the outstanding debt due to the Secured Creditors requested for an adjournment of the 
meeting on the reasoning that the proposed scheme is pending approval before their respective 
committees. 
 
In view of the representation made at the meeting by the majority of the Secured Creditors and 
with no objection from the other secured creditors present at the meeting, the Chairman 
informed that any adjournment can only be granted subject to the directions of the Hon’ble 
NCLT. Accordingly, the Chairman adjourned the aforesaid meeting to Monday, 16th June 2025 
at 11.30 a.m., subject to any directions from NCLT for the said adjournment. 
 
 
We request you to take the aforesaid intimation on record.  
 
Thanking you.
