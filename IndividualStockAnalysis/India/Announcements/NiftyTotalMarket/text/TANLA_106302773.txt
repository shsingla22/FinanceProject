(i) The Buyback will help the Company to return surplus cash to its shareholders holding Equity 
Shares broadly in proportion to their shareholding, thereby, enhancing the overall return to 
shareholders;

(iii) The Buyback would help in improving its earning per share and return on equity, by reduction

past, thereby leading to long term increase in shareholders’ value; an d
