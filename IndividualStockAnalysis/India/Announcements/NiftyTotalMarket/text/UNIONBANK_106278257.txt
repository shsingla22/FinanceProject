This is in furtherance to our aforementioned letter  and pursuant to Regulation 30 read with 
point no. 4(d) of Para A of Part A of Schedule III of SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors in its 
meeting held on 25th June, 202 5, inter-alia, considered & approved:  
 
Capital Plan of the Bank to raise capital by an amount not exceeding ₹ 6,000 crore, subject 
to: 
a) Raising of equity capital not exceeding ₹ 3,000 Crore  in tranche(s) within the overall 
limit of ₹ 6,000 Crore , through Public Issue (i.e. F urther Public Offer) and/or Rights 
Issue and/or Private Placements including Qualified Institutions Placements and/or 
Preferential Allotment or a combination(s) thereof to any eligible institutions and/or 
through any other mode(s)  subject to the approval of Government of India, other 
regulatory authorities and approval of Shareholders  of the Bank .  
b) Raising of Basel III compliant Additional Tier 1 ( AT 1 ) Bonds  not exceeding   
₹ 2,000 Crore  and Tier 2 Bonds  not exceeding  ₹ 1,000 Crore  (including foreign currency 
denominated AT1/Tier 2 Bonds) within the overall limit of ₹ 6,000 Crore . 
 
The Board meeting commenced at 11:00 A.M. and concluded at 3.40 P.M.
