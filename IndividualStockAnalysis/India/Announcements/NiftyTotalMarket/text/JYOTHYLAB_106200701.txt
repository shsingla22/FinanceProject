Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (Listing Regulations), we wish to inform you 
that the Company has today i.e. April 11, 2025, received an intimation from Western Railway-
Ahmedabad Division (Western Railway) whereby the Company has been informed to hand 
over its Laundry Service Operations located at Coaching Depot- Kankaria, Ahmedabad, 
Gujarat by April 30, 2025 as its Build-Own-Operate-Transfer (BOOT) contract with Western 
Railway has concluded.  
The above-mentioned contract was earlier awarded to erstwhile Jyothy Fabricare Services 
Limited (since amalgamated into Jyothy Labs Limited) on November 15, 2011, for a period of 
ten years which was further extended. Subsequently, by virtue of the aforesaid intimation from 
Western Railway, the said contract shall now stand concluded on April 30, 2025.  
Accordingly, the operations of one of the Dry Cleaning and Laundry Service Units of the 
Company situated at Kankaria, Ahmedabad, Gujarat (Ahmedabad Unit), operating on BOOT 
model shall cease with effect from April 30, 2025 at 18:00 Hours.  
Further, the disclosure as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with 
SEBI Master circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 is 
enclosed herewith as Annexure A . 
 
Kindly take the above on your record and disseminate the same for the information of 
investors.
