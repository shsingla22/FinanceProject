Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015  we wish to inform you that: 
1. Issuance of Debentures by Subsidiary – Apollo Defence Industries Private Limited:  
 Our subsidiary, Apollo Defence Industries Private Limited, has approved the issuance of 
Unlisted , Secured, Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures on preferential basis by way 
of private placement aggregating to ₹50 crores. 
 The debentures will be issued on a private placement basis to institutional 
investors/financial institutions for a tenure of three years, with the proceeds intended for 
expanding business operations through acquisition of entities. 
2. Corporate Guarantee by Apollo Micro Systems Limited:  
To support the fund-raising of its subsidiary, the Board of Directors of Apollo Micro Systems 
Limited has approved extending a Corporate Guarantee of ₹50 crores in favour of the 
Debenture Holders .
