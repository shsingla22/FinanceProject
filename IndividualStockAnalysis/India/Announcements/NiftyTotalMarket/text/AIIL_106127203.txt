National Company Law Tribunal  with effect from the Appointed date i.e. October 1, 2023  
 
Pursuant to the above, we wish to inform you that  today i.e. January 28, 2025 Reliance Commercial 
Finance Limited, wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, has received the Cancellation Order 
dated January 24, 2025 from Reserve Bank of India approving the Company’s application for 
surrender of its Certificate of Registration on account of voluntary exit from non -banking 
finance business.
