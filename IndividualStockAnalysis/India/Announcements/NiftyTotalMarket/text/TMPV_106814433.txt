PRESS RELEASE  
Stellantis and Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Broaden Strategic Partnership 
to include Advanced Technology and Product Collaboration  
Sign definitive agreement bringing together Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles' technology and 
vehicle architecture capabilities with Stellantis' global product development expertise.  
Agreement reinforces India's growing global role in vehicle development, manufacturing and exports  
Mumbai/Pune, India | 12 October 2026: Stellantis India, Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited (TMPV) 
and its subsidiary Tata Passenger Electric Mobility Limited (TPEM) today announced the signing of a 
Definitive Agreement that expands the scope of their long -standing partnership further into advanced 
technology and product collaboration. It builds on the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) signed in February 2026 and marks an important expansion of the companies' long- standing strategic partnership.  
 Under the Agreement, TMPV will provide Stellantis access to its advanced product technology and vehicle 
architecture, which Stellantis will build upon through its product development and engineering 
capabilities in India to address the requirements of more  than 50 global markets, adapting to diverse 
customer, regulatory and operating requirements. The  Vehicles developed under th is Agreement will be 
manufactured in India at Fiat India Automobiles Private Limited (FIAPL), the 50:50 joint venture between 
TMPV and Stellantis.  
 This Definitive Agreement follows the MoU signed in February 2026 to explore collaboration across 
manufacturing, engineering and supply chain in India and overseas. It turns that intent into a specific 
product technology and architecture collaboration. Utilising  India’s competitive engineering strengths and 
manufacturing scale, the collaboration aims to accelerate the export ambitions and further anchor India 
as a key node in the global production network.  
 
Shailesh Chandra, Managing Director & CEO, Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles, said: "Our partnership 
with Stellantis has grown steadily over more than two decades, built on mutual trust and complementary strengths. This Agreement marks an important next step, extending our relationship into advanced vehicle technology and architecture, reflecting the strength and competitiveness of TMPV’s capabilities in these areas.  We are delighted that Stellantis will leverage them to develop products for global markets. 
Together, we have an exciting opportunity to combine our respective strengths to create compelling vehicles engineered and made in India for customers in India and around the world.”  
 
Tianshu Xin, Chief Operating Officer, Stellantis Asia -Pacific Region,  said: "Partnerships are central to how 
Stellantis creates value across Asia -Pacific. By combining Tata Motors' strong capabilities in India with 
Stellantis' global product development and market reach, this Agreement will help us develop competitive 
vehicles in India and expand our export potential across international markets. It also reinforces India's growing role as a strategic manufacturing and development hub for Stellantis."  
 Stellantis brings a portfolio of 14 iconic automotive brands, a strong presence across Europe, North America, South America, the Middle East, Africa and Asia- Pacific, and significant global product 
engineering and market expertise. Combined with Tata Motors' technology capabilities, this creates a 
strong foundation to develop and manufacture vehicles in India for markets worldwide.
