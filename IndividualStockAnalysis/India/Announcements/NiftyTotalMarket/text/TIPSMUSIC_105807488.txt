Sub: Declaration of Results of Postal Ballot 
 
Pursuant to the provisions of Section 110 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with rules made thereunder, the 
Company had conducted the process of Postal Ballot by remote e-voting for seeking the consent of the

2015, please find attached the result of the voting by Postal Ballot through remote e-voting along with the
