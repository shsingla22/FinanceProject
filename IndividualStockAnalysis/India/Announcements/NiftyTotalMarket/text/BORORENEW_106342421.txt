A. Date, time and venue of the Extra -Ordinary  General Meeting  
 
The Extra -Ordinary  General Meeting (“Meeting”) of Borosil Renewables Limited (the 
“Company”) was held today i.e.,  Thursday , August  14, 202 5 through Video Conferencing 
(“VC”) facility in compliance with the circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs and 
Securities and Exchange Board of India. The Meeting commenced at 5:00 p.m. (IST) and 
concluded at 05.30 p.m. (IST).  
 B. Summary of proceedings  
 
1. Mr. Ashok Jain, Director was elected as a Chairman of the meeting .  
 
2. The requisite quorum being present, the Chairman called the Meeting to order .   
 
3. It was informed that t he Notice of the Meeting along with Corrigendum issued thereto  
was circulated to shareholders in due compliance with applicable laws.  
 
4. The Shareholders were informed about the remote e- voting facility which was provided 
to them from Monday , August  11, 202 5 [09:00 a.m. (IST)] up to Wednesday , August  13, 
2025 [05:00 p.m. (IST)]. They were also informed that facility of e- voting was open during 
the Meeting for those equity shareholders who had not cast their votes through remote e-voting.   
 5. The Chairman briefed the shareholders on the proposal to approve i ssuance of e quity 
shares on a preferential basis  by way of a special resolution.  He also apprised the m of 
object s for which the issue proceeds will be utilised.  
 
6. During the Q&A session, speaker  shareholders expressed their views and asked a few 
questions , which were duly and satisfactorily  addressed.  
 
7. Towards the conclusion, the Chairman thanked the shareholders for attending the Meeting. It was informed that e- voting results along with the scrutiniser’s report  will be 
placed on the website of the Company, the Stock Exchanges and National Securities Depository Limited, the ag ency appointed by the Company for e- voting facility.  
 
8. The facility of e -voting was thereafter kept open for another 15 minutes for the 
shareholders to exercise their voting rights. The Meeting concluded after expiry of the said 15 minutes . 
 
The e -voting results and Scrutiniser’s Report  are being submitted separately.
