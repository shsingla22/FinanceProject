Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  
- Merger of Indirect Wholly Owned Subsidiaries.  
 
Pursuant to provisions of Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we hereby inform that the merger of 
Indegene Healthcare Germany GmbH with Trilogy Writing & Consulting GmbH, both indirect wholly owned 
subsidiaries of Indegene Limited in Germany, was registered with the Commercial Register of the Local Court 
(Amtsgericht) of Frankfurt am Main  on September 9, 2026.  
 
Accordingly, the merger became effective on September 9, 2026. Consequent to the effectiveness of the merger, 
Indegene Healthcare Germany GmbH ceased to exist as a separate legal entity and Trilogy Writing & Consulting 
GmbH became the surviving entity.  
 
Further, the change in the name of Trilogy Writing & Consulting GmbH to Indegene Healthcare Germany GmbH 
was also registered with the Commercial Register on September 9, 2026. Accordingly, the surviving entity shall 
hereafter operate under the name Indegen e Healthcare Germany GmbH.  
 
The aforesaid merger forms part of an internal reorganization of the Company's indirect wholly owned 
subsidiaries in Germany and does not have any material impact on the business operations, shareholding pattern, 
or financial position of Indegene Limited.  
 
The detailed particulars required under  Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with the SEBI Master Circular 
No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025 -CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026  are annexed to this letter.  
 
The above information is being made available on the website of the Company https://www.indegene.com/
