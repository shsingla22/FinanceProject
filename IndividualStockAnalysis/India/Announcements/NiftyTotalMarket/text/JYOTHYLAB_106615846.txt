Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure

This is in reference to License Agreements and Technology License Agreements entered by 
the Company with Henkel AG & Co. KGaA (“Henkel”) on May 31, 2011, for manufacturing, 
distribution, marketing and sale of the brands Pril and Fa (“Business”), which had automatic 
renewal provisions until May 31, 2026 (“Agreements”). The said Agreements contain the 
process for Business valuation and transfer mechanism in the event of discontinuation of 
existing Business.  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform that Henkel has decided not to renew the Pril license 
beyond May 31, 2026. The Company was in discussions with Henkel on renewal and various 
other options, however, basis the latest discussions, it has been concluded that there is no 
further reasonable possibility regarding renewal of the said Agreements beyond May 31, 2026.  
 
In view of the aforesaid, Company held its meeting of the board of directors earlier today and 
based on the outcome, this disclosure is being made under applicable Regulations. We are 
currently evaluating all options available with the Company and any material developments 
will be promptly disclosed.  
 
Further, the disclosure as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with 
SEBI Master circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 
2026 is enclosed herewith as Annexure A .
