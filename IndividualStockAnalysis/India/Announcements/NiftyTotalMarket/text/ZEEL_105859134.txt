Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
This is with reference to our disclosure letters dated January 22, 2024, January 24, 2024 and 
April 16, 2024 with respect  to the termination of the Merger Cooperation agreement dated 
December 22, 2021 (“ MCA”) between Zee Entertainment Enterprises Limited (“ Company ”) 
and Culver Max Entertainment Private Limited (“ Culver Max ”) and Bangla Entertainment 
Private Limited (“ BEPL”) by Culver Max and BEPL and the Company’s application before the 
Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Mumbai seeking directions to implement the 
Composite Scheme of Arrangement and the withdrawal of the said application.  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 and Part A of Schedule III of the Listing Regulations, we hereby 
wish to inform you that the Company has, on account of Culver Max’s and BEPL’s breaches 
under the MCA, terminated the MCA by issuing a letter dated May 23, 2024, and sought a 
termination fee from Culver Max and BEPL in accordance with the provisions of the MCA.  
 
The details as required under the SEBI Listing Regulations read with the SEBI circular no. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 are set out in Annexure A . 
 
Thanking you,
