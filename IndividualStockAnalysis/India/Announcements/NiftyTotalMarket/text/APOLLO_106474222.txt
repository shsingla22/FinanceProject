This is in furtherance to the intimation given by the Company on 2nd June, 2025 w.r.t. allotment

each convertible into one equity share of face value of Re. 1/- each  on preferential basis to 30 
allottees (“warrant holders”), as per the list forming part of the said letter. 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), this to inform you that upon receipt of an 
amount aggregating to Rs. 10,00,008/- (Rupees Ten Lakhs Eight Only ) as “Warrant Exercise 
Price” and duly signed application form from Mr. Piyush Bhupendra Gala, as per the terms of 
issue of Warrants, the Securities Allotment Committee of Board of Directors of the Company, 
has on 22nd  December, 2025, considered and approved the allotment of 11,696 Equity Shares 
of Re. 1/- each, upon conversion/ exchange of/for such number of Warrants, as per the details 
given in Annexure-1 Consequently, the issued and paid up capital of the Company stands 
increased to Rs. 35,72,92,440/- consisting of 35,72,92,440 equity shares of Re. 1/- each. The 
new equity shares so allotted, shall rank pari passu with the existing equity shares of the 
Company.
