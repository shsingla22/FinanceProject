Date, Time and Venue: 
 
The 21st Annual General Meeting (“ AGM”) of PC Jeweller Limited (the “ Company ”) was held on

CAHIRMAN’S SPEECH 
 
Dear Members, 
 
I am pleased to present you Annual Report of your Company for the financial year 2025-26. 
 
First and Foremost, I am very happy to inform all of you that the company has successfully discharged and cleared 
all its debt repayment obligations of all the banks and has become Debt Free. Achievement of a Debt Free status is 
one of the biggest milestones for the company in its turnaround journey. This will significantly strengthen its 
balance sheet and financial position going forward. 
 
The Company’s fund raising through preferential issue of Fully Convertible Warrants was completed successfully 
on April 10, 2026 with realization of ₹ 2,512.77 crores which included ₹ 716.55 crores contribution by the Promoter 
Group entities.  
 
Also, the Company’s funds raising aggregating upto ₹ 500 crore by preferential issue of equity shares as well as 
Warrants has also been completed successfully on September 24, 2026 including ₹ 175 crores contribution by the 
Promoter. The Promoter has been contributing to the company’s growth in the past as well and now these 
contribution in the Preferential issues has further shown continued confidence of the Promoter in the Company’s 
growth prospects. 
 
In India, the jewellery market is mainly ruled by unorganised sector. The organized retail accounts for about 35% 
only whereas the remaining 65% is ruled by unorganized segment. There are over 6.5 lakh villages in India, yet 
only 4% of rural towns have access to branded or quality-tested jewellery.  
 
There is a huge potential for organised jewellery brands in these untapped markets. Being an established national 
brand, the company seeks to explore the opportunity to tap these untapped rural and semi-urban markets located in 
Tier 4 and below cities. Therefore, during the financial year 2025-26, the Company has executed MoU’s with 
NSDC under the Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship, Government of India and with the 
Government of Uttar Pradesh under the CM YUVA scheme for on boarding of entrepreneurs under the PC Jeweller 
brand as PCJ Lifestyle Franchisees.  
 
Both the initiatives are a low-investment, high-impact retail model backed with financial assistance from the 
Government and Banks. The company will provide application support, training, marketing support and digital 
jewellery catalogue to the franchisee. Further, this initiative will also help entrepreneurship development, local 
economic growth and employment generation in the local region. Even the customers will benefit from this 
partnership as they will take advantage of the customer friendly policies and schemes of the company, and will get 
trust and goodwill of a nationally established brand. For the company, it will enable us to increase our retail 
footprint in the untapped markets with no heavy investment.

In addition to this, the company also has a separate model for establishing Franchisee in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. 
The company already has 4 operational franchisees under this model. We plan to further expand in this model as 
there is less Capex investment by the company with rapid expansion opportunities and higher ROE. The response 
received by the Company from potential business partners for establishing franchisee showrooms in this model has 
been very encouraging. The company plans to establish upto 100 new showrooms, own as well as franchise, in the 
next 12 to 18 months. These shall reap long term benefits for the company. 
 
We understand that with changing times, we need to upgrade as well. Therefore, we are focusing on developing 
technology based applications, such as, try-it-on jewellery for our franchisee model. This reduces the investment 
required by the franchisee and increases the customer experience as well.  
 
Also, I am pleased to inform you that soon, the company is also planning to launch a new jewellery brand which 
will mainly focus on jewellery for youngsters. The jewellery under this new brand will be light weight, budget 
friendly, daily wear and most importantly, will be as per the trends and fashion that is appealing to the young 
generation. We believe that is approach will act as a booster for the company’s growth in the future. 
 
One of the Company’s subsidiary has incorporated PCJ Mining SARL in the Republic of Chad to undertake the 
extraction of precious metal ores. PCJ Mining SARL has been granted license for semi-mechanized artisanal 
mining of gold by the Ministry of Petroleum, Mining and Oil Geology, Republic of Chad. This strategic initiative 
provides the PCJ Group with an opportunity to explore mining operations and possibly establish vertical integration 
across its value chain. 
 
During the financial year 2025-26, the Company recorded strong consolidated revenue from operations amounting 
to ₹ 3,353 crore (as compared ₹ 2,245 crore in previous year) and consolidated net profit of ₹ 722 crore (as 
compared to ₹ 577 crore in previous year). 
 
Further, during the debt repayment period, the company had incurred and paid interest amounting to approximately 
₹ 175 crores to the banks. With no banking debt outstanding, the company will be saving on its finance cost in 
future, which will have a positive impact on its bottomline. 
 
With rich experience from the past, going forward, the Company is committed to expansion of its retail footprint. 
Initiatives like MoU’s with the Government and focus on the franchise model will help the demographic expansion. 
The company already caters to different market segments through its jewellery design. With launch of a new brand 
for youngsters, the company will further expand segment wise. This will help the company to reach a broader 
customer base and enhance its market presence and is aligned with the Company’s future roadmap for growth and 
creating value for its stakeholders. 
 
I am thankful to all our employees, management, lenders, shareholders as well as my Board colleagues for their 
continued support and commitment towards the Company. 
 
Thank You.
