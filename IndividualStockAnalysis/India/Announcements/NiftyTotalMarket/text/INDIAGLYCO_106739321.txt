has allowed the application inter-alia, vide Order dated 15th January, 2026 read with Order dated 16th February 2026, has directed the Company to convened separate meetings of Equity Shareholders and Unsecured 
Creditor on 24th March, 2026 at which the Scheme was duly approved.

2026, and the matter was scheduled for further hearing on 21st May 2026, however the same was not taken up for hearing due to paucity of time. On 2nd July, 2026, the NCLT heard the matter and reserved its order, 
and the NCLT pronounced its decision vide Order dated 17th July, 2026 for sanctioning the Scheme. The Company has applied for the certified true copy of the NCLT Order and the same is awaited.
