This is in furtherance to the intimation given by the Company on 2nd June, 2025 w.r.t. allotment

allottees (“warrant holders”), as per the list forming part of the said letter. 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), this to inform you that upon receipt of an 
amount aggregating to Rs. 30,00,024/- (Rupees Thirty Lakhs Twenty Four Rupees only) as 
“Warrant Exercise Price” and duly signed application form from Mr. Piyush Bhupendra Gala 
, as per the terms of issue of Warrants, the Securities Allotment Committee of the Board of 
Directors of the Company, has on 12th November, 2025, considered and approved the allotment 
of 35,088 (Thirty Five Lakhs Eighty Eight Only)  Equity Shares of Re. 1/- each, upon 
conversion/ exchange of/for such number of Warrants, as per the details given in Annexure-1 
Consequently, the issued and paid up capital of the Company stands increased to Rs 
33,56,74,736/- consisting of 33,56,74,736  equity shares of Re. 1/- each. The new equity shares 
so allotted, shall rank pari passu  with the existing equity shares of the Company.

APPROVAL FOR ALLOTMENT OF EQUITY SHARES UPON EXERCISE OF 
WARRANTS: 
The Chairman informed the Securities Allotment  Committee that the Company had allotted 
3,80,67,058 convertible equity warrants, on preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters 
with an option to convert the same into equal number of equity shares at a price of Rs.114/- per 
warrant, including premium of Rs. 113/- per share on face value of Rs.1/- per share, within a 
period of 6 months from the date of allotment of warrants i.e. 2nd June 2025, as per terms and 
conditions approved in EGM held on 4th February, 2025. The Company had also received in-
principle approval from the BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited on 
19th May 2025 for this matter.   
He further informed that the Company had already received upfront payment of Rs. 
108,49,11,153 (Rupees One Hundred and Eight Crores Forty Nine Lakhs Eleven Thousand 
One Hundred and Fifty Three only), 25% i.e. Rs.28.50 per warrant, at the time of subscription 
of the warrants, from promoter group & non promoters. As per terms of warrants, the warrant 
holders shall deposit the remaining portion of 75% per warrant for conversion of warrant into 
equity share. 
He further informed the Company has received Rs. 30,00,024 (Rupees Thirty Lakhs Twenty 
Four Rupees only), 75% i.e. Rs. 85.50 per warrant  from the warrant holders as detailed below 
for exercise of 35,088 warrants into equity shares. He placed necessary details/statements 
including bank statement confirming deposit of Rs. 30,00,024/- on table of the Committee.
