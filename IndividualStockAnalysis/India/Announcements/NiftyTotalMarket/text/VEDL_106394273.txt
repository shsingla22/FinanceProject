Sensitivity: Public (C4)  As set out in the disclosure dated March 28, 2025, the Board of the Company and the Resulting Companies had 
extended the timeline for fulﬁlment of the conditions precedent as set out in the Scheme from March 31, 2025 
to September 30, 2025.

September 30, 2025 to March 31, 2026 .
