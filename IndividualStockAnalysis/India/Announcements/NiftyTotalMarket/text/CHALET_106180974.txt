Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations  and further to  the Outcome of the Board Meeting  dated  
October 24 , 2024 and January 9 , 2025 , please be informed that the Finance Committee of the Board of Directors 
of the Company has today i.e., on  March 25 , 2025 approved  allotment of  7,500 Listed, Rated, Secured, 
Redeemable,  Transferable, Taxable, Non-Cumulative,  Non-Convertible Debentures  (‘NCDs’) aggregating to 
Rs.75.08 crore  on a Private Placement basis  as per the terms and cond ition s as sta ted in the Key Information 
Document dated March 13, 2025  and other relevant Transaction Documents executed in relation to the NCDs. 
 
The issue price of the NCDs was discovered on the basis of multiple yield allotment method through the 
Electronic Book Building Platform (EBP) of BSE Limited on  March 24 , 2025 and accordingly the  Company has 
received a consideration of Rs.75 ,08,40,000/ - (Indian Rupees  Seventy Five Crore  Eight Lakh  and Forty Thousand) 
against the aggregate principal amount of upto Rs.75 ,00,00,000 (Indian Rupees  Seventy Five Crore)  resulting 
into a total premium amount of Rs.8,40,000/ - (Indian Rupees  Eight Lakh  Forty Thousand) . 
 
The issuance is rated CRISIL AA - (Stable  Outlook)  by CRISIL Ratings . 
 
The aforesaid NCDs are proposed to be listed on the Wholesale Debt Market (WDM) Segment of BSE Limited.  
 
The details with respect to the said issue of NCDs as required pursuant to Regulation 30 of  the Listing Regulations 
read with Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155  dated  November 11, 2024  are set out in Annexure 
to this letter.
