Pursuant  to Regulation  30 read with Para  A, Part A of Schedule  III of SEBI  (Listing  Obligation 
and Disclosure Requirement),  2015,  we wish to inform  that Akzo Nobel India Limited (“ the 
Company”) has received disclosure/s from Imperial Chemical Industries Limited (“ICIL”), one 
of the promoters of the Company, informing that ICIL has sold 22,77,015 equity shares of the 
Company for an aggregate consideration of Rs. 764.80 crore  (rounded off) through Block Deal 
on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited on 24th September 2025.  The number of 
shares comprising the aforesaid sale constitutes ~5%  (rounded off) of  the total paid-  up capital 
of the Company.  
 
After this Block Deal, ICIL continues to be one of the promoters of the Company.  
 
Post this transaction, the promoters’/promoter group’s shareholding in the Company stands 
reduced to 69.76% from the pre -transaction shareholding of 74.76% of the 
promoters/promoters group.
