Sub:    Outcome of the Board Meeting held on August 10, 2026  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (the Listing Regulations), we hereby inform 
you that the Board of Directors of the Company has at their meeting held today i.e. on 
Monday, August 10, 2026, which commenced at 5 :24 p.m. and concluded at 6:00 p.m., inter 
alia, approved  the acquisition of portfolio management services (PMS) business from ICICI 
Securities Limited (I CICI Securities ) by way of a slump sale, as a going concern, together 
with the assets and liabilities pertaining thereto and the execution of the business transfer 
agreement between the Company and ICICI Securities  (“BTA”) in relation to the aforesaid .  
 
The acquisition of the PMS business is subject to approvals as required to be obtained under 
applicable laws. The proposed acquisition would enable the Company to broaden the 
Company’s existing portfolio management services offerings.
