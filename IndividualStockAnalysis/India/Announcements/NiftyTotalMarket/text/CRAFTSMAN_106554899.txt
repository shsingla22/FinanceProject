Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“ SEBI LODR Regulations ”), read with Schedule III thereof, we wish to inform 
that the Board of Directors (“ Board”) of Craftsman Automation Limited (“ Company ”), at its 
meeting held today, i.e., on Wednesday, the 11th March, 2026, which commenced at 3:55 P.M. 
and concluded at 4.15 P.M. has considered and approved the following matters:  
 
1. Upon evaluation of the options/proposals for internal restructuring/consolidation of the 
Aluminium Products business, the Board has decided that any such 
restructuring/consolidation should be undertaken in a phased manner.  
 
2. The Board further took note of the commencement of the first phase, whereby, inter alia , 
the Boards of Directors of the Material Subsidiaries of the Company, namely, DR Axion 
India Limited ( formerly DR Axion India Private Limited ) (“DRA”), and Sunbeam 
Lightweighting Solutions Limited ( formerly Sunbeam Lightweighting Solutions Private 
Limited) (“Sunbeam ”)  have, in their respective meetings held today, i.e., on Wednesday, 
the 11th March, 2026, considered and approved a draft Composite Scheme of Arrangement 
(“Scheme ”) pursuant to the provisions of Sections 230 to 232 and other applicable 
provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, involving, inter alia, the following: 
 
(a) Amalgamation of one or more Step-Down Wholly Owned Subsidiaries of the 
Company into and with DRA subject to compliance with certain conditions precedent;  
 
(b) Amalgamation of DRA (with or without completion of (a)) into and with Sunbeam; 
and 
 
(c) Other matters connected therewith, including, re-organization of the equity share 
capital of Sunbeam. 
 
The implementation of the above mentioned draft Scheme shall be subject to the receipt of all 
necessary or desirable approvals, consents and no-objections, including without limitation, the 
approvals of the Shareholders and Creditors of the relevant Companies, Regulatory Approvals 
and approval of National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) or other appropriate authority.

(d)  Rationale for 
amalgamation/ merger The Management of the Subsidiary Companies are of the view 
that the proposed Scheme will be in the best interest of their 
respective Shareholders, Creditors, Employees, and other 
Stakeholders and not be against public interest, inter alia, for the 
following reasons: 
 
(i) DRA and Sunbeam are both engaged in manufacturing of 
Aluminium Components and related activities. At present, 
the Aluminium business and the land and infrastructure 
required for its expansion are housed in different entities, 
resulting in the fragmentation of operations and asset 
ownership. 
 
(ii) The Management of the Companies are of the view that the 
Aluminium Components Industry is poised for substantial 
growth. This opportunity can be better capitalized by 
consolidating the Aluminium business under a single 
focused operating entity having a stronger balance sheet, 
a broader product and manufacturing base, unified asset 
ownership, and a streamlined organizational structure. 
 
(iii) The Step-Down Subsidiary Companies have been recently 
acquired by DRA are engaged in holding land parcels 
which have been leased to DRA for the development and 
expansion of its Aluminium manufacturing facilities. The 
Board of DRA has approved the setting up of a new 
Aluminium facility on the land parcels leased from the Step 
Down Subsidiaries. It is expected that consolidation of the 
Step-Down Subsidiaries with DRA will lead to efficient 
implementation of the approved expansion plans, seamless 
development of manufacturing facilities, and align
