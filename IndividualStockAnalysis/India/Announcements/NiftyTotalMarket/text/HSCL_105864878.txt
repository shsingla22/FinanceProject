1. We, M/s Singhi & Co., Chartered Accountants are the statutory auditors of M/s Himadri Speciality Chemical 
Limited (the "Company") . This Statutory Auditor's certificate is issued in accordance with the requirements of 
Regulation 169(5) of Chapter V of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue of Capital and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2018 ('ICDR Regulations') and is issued for onward submission to the National Stock 
Exchange of India Limited ('NSE') and BSE Limited ('BSE') in connection with receipt of 25% of the issue price per 
warrant as upfront p·ayment ("Warrant Subscription Price") for issue of 1,08,17,000 (One Crore Eight Lakh 
Seventeen Thousand) Warrants (the "Warrants") on preferential basis each convertible into, or exchangeable for, 
one equity share of face value of Re. 1 each at an issue price (including the Warrant Subscription Price and the 
Warrant Exercise Price) of Rs 316 each by the Company to the Promoters of the Company and certain identified 
non-promoter persons ("Allottees") .
