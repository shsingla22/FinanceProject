About  Raymond

business  and  Real  Estate.  After  demerging  its Lifestyle  Business  into  a separate  listed  entity  in the year  2024,  Raymond  Limited  now  has  two  core  
businesses,  Real  Estate  and  Engineering.  Raymond  Realty  has  carved  its position  in Real  Estate  sector  by leaps  and  bounds  and  today  is amongst

leadership  position  in manufacturing  files  and  hand  tools  and  has  a significant  presence  in national  and  international  markets.  With  the acquisition

Aerospace  & Defense  components,  distinctly  positioned  to target  high‐growth  precision  engineering  products  with  a significant  presence  across
