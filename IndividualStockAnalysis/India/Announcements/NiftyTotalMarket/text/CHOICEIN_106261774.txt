Dear Sir/ Madam, 
In reference to our earlier intimation dated June 06, 2024, the Securities Allotment committee of the 
Company had allotted 2,31,21,000 (Two Crore Thirty-One Lakh Twenty-One Thousand) Warrants at a 
Price of INR. 300/- per warrant convertible in to Equivalent number of Equity Shares of Face Value of 
INR. 10/- each on Preferential basis to the Person’s belonging to Promoter as well as Non — Promoter 
Group after obtaining requisite approvals from the Members of the Company & the Stock Exchange’s 
and on receipt of the 25 % of the Consideration amount. 
In furtherance to our above mentioned intimation and pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule 
1l and other applicable provisions of the SEBI LODR Regulations, we are pleased to inform that on 
receipt of the balance 75% of the Consideration amount aggregating to INR. 135 Crores for the 
exercise of 60,00,000 Warrants convertible in to Equity out of the Total 2,00,00,000 Warrants held by 
the Warrant Holder M/s. Plutus Wealth Management LLP, the Securities Allotment Committee of the 
Company on June 04, 2025 has allotted 60,00,000 Equity Shares of Face value of INR. 10/- each to the 
Warrant Holder. 
The Equity Shares, arising upon conversion of the warrants, shall rank pari passu with the existing 
Equity Shares of the Company. Consequent to the said allotment, the paid-up equity share capital of 
the Company stands increased to INR. 205,65,55,000 consisting of 20,56,55,500 Equity Shares of INR. 
10 each. 
The details as required under Listing Regulations read with SEBI Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 are given in Annexure — A to this letter.
