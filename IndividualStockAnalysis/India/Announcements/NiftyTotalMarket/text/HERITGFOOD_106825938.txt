4 Rationale for 
amalgamation/ 
merger;  The following rationale for the proposed amalgamation of 
Heritage Novandie Foods Limited with Heritage Foods Limited 
pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation:  
• Greater integration of the businesses and financial resources, 
resulting in enhanced financial and operational efficiency;  
• Efficient utilisation and management of cash flows and other 
resources of the combined business;  
• Simplification of the Group structure by elimination of an 
additional legal entity and reduction of duplicative statutory, 
regulatory and administrative compliances;  
• Streamlined management, unified control and faster 
decision -making through consolidation of the businesses;  
• Achievement of synergies and economies of scale through 
optimization of resources, production capabilities and 
business processes; and  
• Rationalization of administrative and operational costs and 
improved overall efficiency.
