laid down in Indian Accounting Standards ("Ind AS") as prescribed under Section 133 of the 
Companies Act 2013, as amended, read with relevant rules thereunder and in terms of Regulation

4. During the quarter ended 30 September 2026, the Company has granted 16,166 Employee Stock

:). The Company had entered into a lease agreement with Ruby Infotech Private Limited ("the Lessor")

consent award. Due to certain infrastructural issues in addition to safety issues relating to the leased 
premises, the Company terminated the Lease Deed in March 2024, during the lock-in period, 
pursuant to which disputes arose between the parties. The disputes were referred to arbitration 
before the learned Sole Arbitrator against the Lessor, claiming an amount oft 65.10 million. The 
Lessor also filed counterclaims against the Company. 
During the quarter ended 30 September 2026, the Sole Arbitrator had passed his award in favor of 
the lessor determining an amount oft 82.66 million towards the rent for the lock-in period (net of 
the security deposit), along with interest at 18% per annum and other costs of 1 million. 
The Company does not agree with the award and is in the process of filing a petition before the 
Hon'ble High Court of Delhi seeking to set it aside. Based on the legal opinion obtained from 
independent legal counsel, management believes that the Company has reasonable grounds to 
challenge the award and that no liability is likely to arise against the Company. Accordingly, no 
provision has been recognised in the standalone financial results.

Explanatory notes to the statement of unaudited consolidated financial results for the quarter and six

1. The above unaudited consolidated financial results of the Awfis Space Solutions Limited ("the Holding 
Company") and its subsidiaries (collectively the 'Group') have been prepared in accordance with the 
recognition and measurement principle laid down in Indian Accounting Standards ("Ind AS") as 
prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act 2013, as amended, read with relevant rules 
thereunder and in terms of Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligation and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations 2015 ("the Regulations"). 
2. These unaudited consolidated financial results have been reviewed by the Audit Committee at its 
meeting held on 3 November 2026 and have been approved by the Board of Directors at its meeting held 
on 3 November 2026. 
3. During the quarter ended 30 September 2026, the Holding Company has granted 16,166 Employee Stock 
Option to the eligible employees under the Awfis Space Solutions Employee Stock Option Scheme -
2024, as approved by the Board of Directors. 
4. The Holding Company had entered into a lease agreement with Ruby Infotech Private Limited ("the 
Lessor") for commercial premises situated at Noida, which was revised in September 2023 pursuant to a 
consent award. Due to certain infrastructural issues in addition to safety issues relating to the leased 
premises, the Holding Company terminated the Lease Deed in March 2024, during the lock-in period, 
pursuant to which disputes arose between the parties. The disputes were referred to arbitration before 
the learned Sole Arbitrator against the Lessor, claiming an amount of~ 65.10 million. The Lessor also 
filed counterclaims against the Holding Company. 
During the quarter ended 30 September 2026, the Sole Arbitrator had passed his award in favor of the 
lessor determining an amount of ~ 82.66 million towards the rent for the lock-in period (net of the 
security deposit), along with interest at 18 % per annum and other costs of 1 million. 
The Holding Company does not agree with the award and is in the process of filing a petition before the 
Hon'ble High Court of Delhi seeking to set it aside. Based on the legal opinion obtained from 
independent legal counsel, management believes that the Holding Company has reasonable grounds to 
challenge the award and that no liability is likely to arise against the Holding Company. Accordingly, 
no provision has been recognised in the consolidated financial results.
