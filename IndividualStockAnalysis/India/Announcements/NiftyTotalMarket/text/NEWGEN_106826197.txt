d.  Rationale for 
amalgamation/merger:  
 The Transferor Company is a wholly owned subsidiary of the 
Transferee Company and both the Companies are under common 
management. The proposed amalgamation is intended to simplify 
the existing group structure by consolidating the Transferor 
Company with th e Transferee Company into a single legal entity. 
The amalgamation would also rationalise the administrative, 
operational, legal and regulatory compliances and the associated 
costs.  
 
In summary, the Scheme is expected to facilitate a more 
streamlined corporate structure and efficient utilisation of 
resources, thereby creating and preserving long -term value for all 
the stakeholders.  
e.  In case of cash 
consideration - amount or 
otherwise share exchange 
ratio:  
 Pursuant to the Scheme, no shares shall be issued, nor shall any 
consideration be paid in cash or otherwise by the Transferee 
Company, as the Transferor Company is a wholly owned 
subsidiary of the Transferee Company.  
 
Considering the Scheme does not envisage the issuance of any 
fresh shares or payment of any consideration to the shareholders 
of the Transferor Company, the requirement for determination of 
a Share Exchange Ratio and preparation of a Valuation Report for 
such purpose is not applicable.
