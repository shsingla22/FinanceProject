Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
This is in continuation of our letters dated July 28, 2025 and December 30, 2025 regarding the transfer of the 
Company’s Portfolio Management Services (“PMS”) business (“Identified Business”) to IIFL Capital Asset 
Management Limited , a wholly owned subsidiary  of the Company , by way of a slump sale on a going concern 
basis (“Transaction”). Consequent to the transfer of the said business, the Company had applied to the 
Securities and Exchange Board of India (“SEBI”) for surrender of its registration as a Portfolio Manager.  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
(“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform you that SEBI, vide its letter dated May 20, 2026, has accepted 
the said application and the certifica te of registration  of the Company  as a Portfolio Manager bearing 
Registration No. INP000002213 has been cancelled with effect from May 20, 2026.  
 
The disclosure required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular
