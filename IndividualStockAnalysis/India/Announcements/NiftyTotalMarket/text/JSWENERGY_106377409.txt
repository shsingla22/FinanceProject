enhancing cost efficiencies, and improving contr ol over 
critical component requirements of thermal power asset 
enabling increased production capacity to support future 
thermal projects of the Company aligned with the long-term 
vision of expanding into energy portfolio and thereby 
creating value for all its shareholders .
