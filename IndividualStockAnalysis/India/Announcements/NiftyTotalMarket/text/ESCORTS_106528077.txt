Profit before tax from continuing operations, excluding exceptional items of z 52.5 crore on account 
of one-time impact of the new labour code, came at z 522.7 crore, up by 37.5%, as against z 380.2 
crore in corresponding quarter and up by 21.2% as against z 431.1 crore in sequential quarter. 
Adjusted Net profit after tax came at z 401.6 crore (adjusted for impact of new labour code), up by 
38.3% as against z 290.5 Crore in corresponding quarter and up by 25.0% as against z 321.2 crore 
in the Sequential quarter. 
Standalone Net Profit after tax including exceptional items and discontinued operations came at z 
362.4 crore up by 12.1 % as against z 323.2 crore in corresponding quarter and up by 12.8% as 
against z 321.2 Crore in sequential quarter. Earnings per share (EPS) for the quarter ended in 
December 2025 is z 32.9 as against z 29.39 in corresponding quarter and z 29.19 in sequential 
quarter. 
For the nine months ended December 2025 for Standalone continuing operations, Revenue came at z 
8,522.1 crore up by 9.9% as against z 7,756.7 crore in corresponding period last year. EBIDTA 
came at z 1,127.0 crore up by 27.3% as against z 885.0 crore in corresponding period last year. 
Profit before tax and exceptional items came at z 1,371.7 crore up 36.0% as against z 1,008.2 crore 
in corresponding period last year. 
During the nine months ended December 2025, the Railway business divestment was successfully 
concluded in accordance with the conditions outlined in the Business Transfer Agreement (BTA) 
and the income of z 1,601.7 Crore (net of transaction cost) from the divestment has been accounted 
for in the financial results under discontinued operations. Standalone PBT, including discontinued 
operations for nine months ended December 2025, came at z 2,598.3 crore as against z 1,134.3 
crore in corresponding period last year. Standalone Net Profit after tax including discontinued 
operations came at ~ 2,083.8 crore as against z 953.4 crore in corresponding period last year. 
Earnings per share (EPS) for nine months ended December 2025 is z 189.4 as against z 86.72 in 
corresponding period last year. The Board has declared a one-time special dividend of z 18.0 per 
equity share of face value z 10, pursuant to the completion of the Railway business divestment. 
On Consolidated basis, for the quarter ended December 2025, Revenue from Continuing 
operations came at z 3,280.5 crore up 11.3%, as against z 2,948.0 crore in corresponding quarter 
and up 17.5% as against z 2,791.6 crore in sequential quarter. Adjusted Consolidated Net Profit 
After Tax from continuing operations came at z 397 .6 crore (adjusted for impact of new labour code) 
up by 38.1 % as against z 287.9 Crore in corresponding quarter and up by 25.0% as against z 318.1 
crore in the Sequential quarter.

SEGMENT WISE PERFORMANCE 
Agri Machinery Products 
For the quarter ended December 2025, Tractors volumes came at 36,955 units up by 13.5% as 
against 32,556 units in corresponding quarter and up by 9.1 % as against 33,877 units in 
sequential quarter. Segment revenue came at t 2,769.6 crore in quarter ended December 2025 up 
by 14.6% as against t 2,416.6 crore in corresponding quarter and up by 13.8% as against t 2,432.9 
crore in sequential quarter. EBIT margin for the quarter ended December 2025 came at 13.5% up 
by 310 bps as against 10.4% in corresponding quarter and up by 72 bps as against 12.8% in 
sequential quarter. 
For the nine months ended December 2025, Tractors volume came at 1,01,413 units up by 14.0% 
as against 88,921 units in corresponding period last year. Segment revenue came at t 7,383.9 crore 
up by 14.1% as against t 6,472.5 crore in the previous year. The EBIT margin for nine months 
ended December 2025 came at 13.0% as against 10.5% in corresponding period last year. 
Construction Equipment 
For the quarter ended December 2025, Construction Equipmen t's sales volume was at 1,716 units 
as against 1,989 units in corresponding quarter and as against 1,146 units in sequential quarter. 
Segment revenue came at t 489.9 crore as against z 515.7 crore in corresponding quarter and as 
against z 338.1 crore in sequential quarter. EBIT margin for the quarter came at 6.6% as against 
11.0% in. corresponding quarter and 3.8% in sequential quarter. 
For the nine months ended December 2025, construction equipment volumes came at 3,917 units 
as against 4,765 units in corresponding period last year. Segment revenue came at z 1,129.5 crore 
as against z 1,276.2 crore in corresponding period last year. EBIT margin for the nine months 
ended December 2025 came at 5.5% as against 10.3% in corresponding period last year.
