Dear Sir/Madam,  
Re: Qualified institutions placement of equity shares of face value of ₹ 5 each (the 
“Equity Shares”) to qualified institutional buyers (“QIB”) by Wockhardt Limited 
under the provision of Chapter VI of Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, and Sections  
42 and 62  (1) (c)  of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 14 of the Companies 
(Prospectus and Allotment of Securities) Rules, 2014, each as amended, (the 
“Issue”)  
Sub:  Outcome of the meeting of the Capital  Raising Committee  
In furtherance to our earlier intimation bearing reference no. WOCK/SEC/SE/2024 -25/069 
dated 6th November , 2024 , and WOCK/SEC/SE/2024 -25/071 dated 11th November , 2024 , with 
respect to the Issue  which opened on 6th November , 2024  and closed on 11th November , 2024 , 
we wish to inform you that the Capital  Raising Committee  of the Company at its meeting held 
today, i.e. 11th November , 2024 , approved the issue  and allotment of 90,49,773  Equity Shares , 
to eligible QIBs at the issue  price of ₹ 1,105 /- per Equity Share (including a premium of ₹ 1,100 /- 
per Equity Share ), which includes a discount of ₹ 57.25 /- per Equity Share  being equivalent to  
4.93% of the floor price, as determined in terms of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Issue of Capita l and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, as amended  (the “ SEBI 
ICDR Regulations ”), aggregating to ₹ 999,99,99,165  (Rupees Nine Hundred Ninety Nine 
Crores  Ninety Nine Lakhs Ninety Nine Thousand One Hundred Sixty Five Only) , pursuant to 
the Issue .  
Pursuant to  the allotment of Equity Shares  under  the Issue , the paid -up equity share capital 
of the Company stands increased from ₹76,71,12,730  consisting of 15,34,22,546  Equity 
Shares  of ₹5 each  to ₹81,23,61,595  consisting of 16,24,72,319  Equity Shares  of face value 
of ₹5 each. The shareholding pattern of the Company after the Issue , will be submitted along 
with listing application in the format specified in Regulation 31 of the Securities and Exchange 
Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure R equirements) Regulations, 2015 , as 
amended (“ SEBI Listing Regulations ”). 
The Capital  Raising Committee  meeting commenced at 11:35 p.m. and the meeting was 
concluded at 11:45  p.m. 
Further , please  find attached herewith the list of allottees who hav e been allotted more than 
five percent (5%)  of the Equity Shares of the Issue  size marked as Annexure A . 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The details, as required to be disclosed under Regulation 30 of SEBI Listing Regulations read 
with SEBI Circular dated  13th July, 2023 are enclosed as Annexure B . 
We request you to take the above on record pursuant to compliance under SEBI Listing 
Regulations .
