Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
In terms of Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, we hereby inform that as a part of the 
Company’s overall efforts for simplification of corporate structure, Liva Investment Limited 
(“Liva”), a non-operating wholly owned subsidiary of the Company has been dissolved with 
effect from August 20, 2025, pursuant to the provisions of section 59 of the Insolvency and 
Bankruptcy Code, 2016 read with regulations made thereunder. 
 
The dissolution of Liva has been approved by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal, 
Division Bench Court-1, Ahmedabad on August 20, 2025. The Order of dissolution dated                
August 20, 2025 was downloaded from the website of NCLT on August 21, 2025 at 4:08 p.m. 
 
 
 
 
It may be noted that Liva was not a material subsidiary of the Company and the dissolution will 
have no material impact on the Consolidated Financial Statements of the Company. 
 
The details as required under clause 1 of Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the Listing 
Regulations read with the SEBI Circulars are provided in Annexure-“A” .
