Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
We, Embassy Developments Limited (the “Company”), enclose herewith the disclosures received by 
the Company, from Embassy Property Developments Private Limited (“EPDPL”) and OMR Investments 
LLP (“OMR”), both Promoter Group entities of the Company, under Reg ulation 7(2)(a) of the SEBI PIT 
Regulations in respect of the release of pledge over 9,79,05,440 equity shares of the Company 
held by EPDPL and the re -creation of pledge over an equivalent number of equity shares of the 
Company held by EPDPL and OMR, during the period from November 06, 2026 to November 11, 
2026 (the “ Transaction ”). 
 
Based on the disclosures received from the Promoter Group, we wish to place on record that:  
 
(a) No additional pledge: the aggregate number of equity shares of the Company under 
pledge by the Promoter Group remains unchanged before and after the Transaction. No 
additional encumbrance has been created;  
 
(b) No additional borrowing: the Promoter Group has not availed any new, additional or 
enhanced borrowing or facility. There is no change in the underlying financing arrangement;  
  
(c) Sole purpose – regulatory compliance: the Transaction has been undertaken solely to 
comply with the lock -in requirements under Chapter V of the ICDR Regulations (defined 
below).  
 
The Company had, on October 12, 2026, allotted 3,25,18,900 convertible warrants (“Warrants”), 
on a preferential basis to EPDPL, a member of the Promoter Group, under Chapter V of the 
Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue of Capital and Disclosure R equirements) 
Regulations, 2018 (“ICDR Regulations”). In terms of the ICDR Regulations, the entire pre -
preferential shareholding of the allottee is required to be locked -in from the relevant date up to 90 
(ninety) trading days from the date of allotment of such Warrants . As these 9,79,05,440 shares were 
under pledge, the pledge was released to enable the lock -in, and thereafter an equivalent number 
of shares was re -pledged.  
                                                                                                                                       
 
 
The Promoter Group has converted all the Warrants at ₹111.51 per equity share, well ahead of 
their tenure. The Promoter Group has thereby infused an aggregate amount of approximately 
₹362.62 crore into the Company.  
 
The full and early conversion of the warrants at ₹ 111.51 per share reflects the Promoter Group’s 
continued commitment to, and confidence in, the long -term growth prospects of the 
Company. Pursuant to the conversion, the shareholding of the Promoter Group in the 
Company has increased from 42.68% to 43.99% of the paid -up equity share capital.  
 
We reiterate that there has been no additional pledge and no additional borrowing by the 
Promoter Group.  
 
We request you to kindly take the above on record.
