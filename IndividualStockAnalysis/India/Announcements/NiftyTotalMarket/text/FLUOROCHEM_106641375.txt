5. During the year ended 31 March 2025, pursuant to the approval of the Board of Directors of the Company in their meeting held on 26 December, 2024, the Company had sold its Energy

and loss and included in 'other income'. 
Subsequently on 11 February 2025, IGREL Mahidad Limited has allotted additional equity shares to the Company and also to external investors and accordingly the Company's holding in 
IGREL Mahidad Limited is reduced to 26.25% and it has ceased to be a subsidiary from that date. 
The 'tax expense' for the quarter and the year ended 31 March 2025 is lower by Rs. 29 crores on account of (a) utilisation of brought forward capital losses on which deferred tax asset was 
not recognised, and (b) lower tax rate in respect of capital gains on above slump-sale.
