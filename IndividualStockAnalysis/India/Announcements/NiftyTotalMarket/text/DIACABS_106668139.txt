EXPLANATORY STATEMENT PURSUANT TO SECTION 102 OF THE COMPANIES ACT, 2013  
 
Item No. 1 :  
 
The Members may recall that the Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held on November 10, 
2025, and subsequently the shareholders through Postal Ballot  on December 17, 2025, had approved and 
authorized the Company to raise further capital by way of issuance of equity shares and/or other eligible 
securities through a Qualified Institutions Placement ("QIP") for an aggregate amount not exceeding ₹1,000 
Crore, in one or more tranches.  
 
The Members are aware that, pursuant to the completion of the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process 
("CIRP"), the Company is required to achieve and maintain the minimum public shareholding ("MPS") 
requirements prescribed under the applicable provisions of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 ("SEBI ICDR Regulations"), and the 
Securities Contracts (Regulation) Rules, 1957.  
 
Considering the prevailing market conditions , improving geopolitical scenario  and the current shareholding 
structure of the Company, the existing authorization limit of ₹1,000 Crore may not be adequate to enable 
the Company to undertake a QIP of such size as may be necessary to achieve compliance with the 
prescribed MPS requirement s. 
 
Accordingly, the Board considers it prudent and in the best interests of the Company to seek Members' 
approval for enhancement of the existing fund -raising limit through QIP from ₹1,000 Crore to ₹ 2,000 Crore, 
which would provide the Company with greater flexibility to determine the appropriate issue size and 
structure, depending upon market conditions and regulatory requirements prevailing at the relevant time.  
    
The Company, with a view to supporting its future growth plans, strengthening its capital base and 
enhancing financial flexibility, proposes to raise capital through a Qualified Institutions Placement ("QIP"). 
The proceeds of the proposed issue are intende d to be utilized, inter alia, for the following purposes:  
 
(a) making investments in subsidiaries, joint ventures and/or associates by way of equity, preference 
shares, debt or any other permissible instruments, and funding the growth of existing businesses, including 
expansion of product offerings, entry into new  markets and territories, and pursuing strategic acquisitions 
and inorganic growth opportunities, if any;  
 
(b) capital expenditure towards procurement of plant & machinery, office equipment, furniture & fixtures, 
civil works and related infrastructure including R&D and technical know -how for expansion, modernization 
and strengthening of existing operations;  
 
(c) prepayment, repayment and/or reduction, in full or in part, of existing secured and/or unsecured 
borrowings and other indebtedness of the Company and/or its subsidiaries, including borrowings availed 
from banks, financial institutions, related parties and/o r other lenders ; and  
 
(d) meeting working capital requirements, general corporate purposes and such other purposes as may be 
permitted under applicable laws.  
 
The exact amount to be utilized for each of the aforesaid purposes shall be determined by the Board of 
Directors of the Company (‘‘the Board’’, which term shall include the Management Committee of the 
Board or any other committee) , which term shall be deemed to include any committee thereof, including 
the Management Committee, as may be authorized by the Board in this regard, based on business 
requirements and prevailing circumstances.
