Media Release   
 
Heritage Foods Advances Strategic Integration of Livo Yogurt Business, 
Strengthening Its Value -Added Dairy Platform  
 
Amalgamation of Heritage Novandie Foods to simplify operations and support the next phase 
of branded dairy growth  
Livo ranks among India’s #5 yogurt brands   
October 23, 2026:  Heritage Foods Limited (BSE: 519552; NSE: HERITGFOOD) has approved the 
amalgamation of its wholly owned subsidiary, Heritage Novandie Foods Limited, with Heritage Foods 
Limited. Heritage Novandie  Foods operates the Company’s yogurt production  under the Livo brand.  
Strategic Highlights  
 
Strengthening Livo’s Platform for Growth  
 
Livo has established a presence in India’s yogurt market. Its integration with Heritage Foods’ wider dairy 
platform is intended to support coordinated brand development and closer alignment with the Company’s 
consumer and distribution capabilities.  
 
Growing Value -Added Dairy and Building a More Resilient Mix  
 
Value -added products are becoming an increasingly important part of Heritage Foods’ portfolio. Excluding 
ghee and butter, VAP contribution to dairy revenue reached 35.4% in Q2 FY27 and 39.6% in H1 FY27, 
compared with 31.3% and 33.7%, respectively, in the c orresponding periods last year.  
 
A greater contribution from branded, differentiated products can diversify the revenue mix and reduce 
relative dependence on liqui d milk, which is more exposed to seasonal demand patterns and commodity -
linked fluctuations. Heritage Foods aims to increase VAP contribution to approximately 50% over the next 
three years , supporting a more balanced and resilient business mix.  
 
Simplifying the Group Structure and Improving Efficiency  
 
The proposed amalgamation is intended to integrate the businesses and financial resources more 
effectively, enable efficient utilisation and management of cash flows, and simplify the Group structure by 
eliminating an additional legal entity and reducing d uplicative statutory, regulatory and administrative 
requirements.  
 
The consolidation is also intended to support unified management, faster decision -making and synergies 
through optimisation  of resources, production capabilities and business processes, alongside 
rationalisation of administrative and operational costs.  
Media Release   
 
Management Commentary  
Commenting on the Results , Mrs. Brahmani Nara - Executive Director , said: Our focus remains on building 
a stronger, more integrated and future -ready dairy business. The proposed amalgamation of Heritage 
Novandie  Foods is an important step in strengthening our value -added dairy platform, while simplifying 
the overall structure and enabling greater alignment across the business. As we continue to scale 
differentiated and branded dairy products, we believe this inte gration will further strengthen our platform 
for sustainable growth and enhance operational efficiency over the long term.”  
About Heritage Foods Limited  
Heritage Foods Limited (NSE: HERITGFOOD; BSE: 519552) is one of India’s leading dairy companies, 
founded in 1992 with a vision to provide high quality, nutritious milk and dairy products to families across 
the nation and to empower farmers. Over the years,  Heritage has grown into a trusted brand, known for 
its commitment to purity, freshness, and sustainability. With operations spanning  13 states, Heritage 
Foods serves millions of consumers, offering a wide range of milk, curd, butter, paneer, and other value -
added dairy products.  
Heritage Foods stands out for its farm -to-home approach, ensuring every product is produced with the 
highest standards of quality and care. The company works closely with its network of dairy farmers, 
empowering them with modern farming practices, training , and a fair partnership model, vetted by their 
500+ Quality Experts who perform more than 25 stringent quality tests daily which in turn guarantees a 
consistent supply of fresh, high -quality milk.  
Driven by a passion for excellence and a deep understanding of consumer needs, Heritage Foods remains 
at the forefront of India’s dairy industry, constantly striving to provide products that nourish, comfort, and 
support families across the country.
