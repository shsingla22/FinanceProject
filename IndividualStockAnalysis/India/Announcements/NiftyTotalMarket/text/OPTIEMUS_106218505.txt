j) Brief Background about the Entity whose shares being acquired : 
 
Bharat Innovative Glass Technologies Private Limited (“BIGTech”) is a company incorporated as a 
subsidiary of the Company, under the Companies Act, 2013 on 4th October, 2023 having its registered 
office at K-20, Second Floor, Lajpat Nagar-II, New Delhi – 110 024. It is also a Joint venture of Optiemus 
Infracom Limited and Corning International Corporation, a company incorporated under the laws of 
Delaware, United States of America. 
 
BIGTech is setting up its manufacturing facility in Tamil Nadu for manufacturing of  finished cover glass for 
use in mobile consumer electronic devices, and other cover glass applications, to meet the needs of next-
generation mobile consumer electronic devices.
