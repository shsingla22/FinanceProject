Regulations, 2015 – Change in status of Statutory Auditor of Material Subsidiary of Share 
India Securities Limited (‘the Company’) 
 
Dear Sir,  
 
We wish to inform you that M/s M O Mehta & Associates (FRN: 135696W), the Statutory Auditor of Share 
India AlgoPlus Private Limited, a wholly owned and material subsidiary of the Company (“SIAPL”), has 
undergone a merger. Pursuant to the merger, M/s M O Mehta & Associates has transitioned from a 
proprietorship to a partnership and Mr. Manish Mehta, sole proprietor of the said firm, will henceforth 
operate under the name M/s K L Parmar & Co. (FRN: 120112W), effective from March 21, 2025. 
 
Accordingly, to give effect to the above transition in the records of various regulatory authorities, M/s. M 
O Mehta & Associates, has ceased to be the Statutory Auditors of SIAPL and subsequently, M/s K L Parmar 
& Co., wherein Mr. Manish Mehta, erstwhile auditor, has become a Signing Partner pursuant to merger, 
has been appointed as Statutory Auditors at the Extra-Ordinary General Meeting of SIAPL held on March 
27, 2025.  
 
M/s K L Parmar & Co. is a Partnership Firm specializing in financial and business advisory, internal audit, 
statutory audit, corporate governance and corporate tax and regulatory services. Adept at executing multi-
locational work for its clients, the firm caters to diverse industries and supports them in their growth. The 
Firm’s clientele ranges from Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) to Listed Companies. 
 
Though the change in constitution of the audit firm is not material to the Company or SIAPL, this disclosure 
under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 is being made voluntarily. 
 
The date and time of receipt of such information by the Company is March 27, 2025 at 01:21 p.m. 
 
You are requested to take the same on your records.
