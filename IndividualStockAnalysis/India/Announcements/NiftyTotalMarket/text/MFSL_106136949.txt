This is to inform you that Axis Max Life Insurance Limited ( Axis Max Life ), the material subsidiary 
of the Company was directed by the IRDAI to submit its board approved public listing roadmap. 
The Board of Directors of Axis Max Life in its meeting concluded on February 4, 2025, discussed 
and approved the said listing roadmap.  
 
As the said listing roadmap was a significant agenda item which was proposed to be considered at 
the Board meeting of Axis Max Life which included the matters concerning the interest of the 
Company as 80.98% shareholder of Axis Max Life, the said listing roadmap was also placed before 
the Board of Directors of the Company for its consideration.

of Axis Max Life, the Board of the Company has approved a roadmap which envisages the listing 
of Axis Max Life through the merger of MFSL into Axis Max Life, in order to achieve the status of 
listed company for Axis Max Life. 
 
The Board also discussed that it shall initiate the process for listing of Axis Max Life once there is 
clarity on the applicable law allowing such merger.   
 
The Company will keep the investors informed on the developments in this regard.  A copy of the 
disclosure made by Axis Max Life in this regard is enclosed.

Sub: Disclosure under the extant provisions of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (SEBI Listing Regulations) by Axis Max Life Insurance Limited (the Company or Axis Max Life)  
 
This is to inform you that, basis a direction received from the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India to 
submit a Board approved public listing roadmap of the Company, the Board of Directors of ( Board) of the Company, in its 
meeting concluded on February 4, 2025, discussed and approved a listing roadmap of the Company. Amongst the options 
discussed as a part of the said public listing roadmap, the Board has approved a roadmap which envisages the listing of the 
Company through the merger of Max Financial Services Limited, the holding company of Axis Max Life, having its equity shares 
listed on the stock exchanges in India and holding 80.98% of the shareholding of the Company, into Axis Max Life, in order to 
achieve the status of listed company for Axis Max Life. 
 
The Board discussed that it shall initiate the process for listing of the Company once there is clarity on the applicable law 
allowing such merger.   
 
The Company will keep the investors informed on the developments in this regard. 
 
You are requested to kindly take the same on record.
