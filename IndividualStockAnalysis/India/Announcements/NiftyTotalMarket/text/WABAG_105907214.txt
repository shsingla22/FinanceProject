Ref.: Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements) Regulations

As part of the business strategy to optimize the Group's structure and in line with our strategic 
focus to reduce our exposure to European Geographies, the Board of Directors ("Board") of VA 
Tech Wabag Limited ("the Company") at their meeting held today i.e., Monday, July 08, 2024, 
have approved the sale of 100% stake in Wabag Water Services S.R.L., Romania ("Wabag 
Romania"). 
Wabag Romania, a company limited by shares existing under the laws of Romania having its 
registered office at Dimitrie Pompeiu Blv, Nr. 6E, Etaj 13, Sector 2, 020337 Bucuresti, Romania is 
wholly-owned Subsidiary of VA Tech Wabag GmbH, Austria ("Wabag Austria"), step down 
Subsidiary of the Company. 
The share transfer process and other related formalities between Wabag Austria ("the Seller") 
and Circular Waters Solutions S.R.L., Romania ("the Buyer") will be completed upon receipt of 
necessary approvals as per the applicable laws and upon satisfaction of Conditions Precedents, 
as per the agreement. 
The details required to be disclosed pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR read with SEBI 
circular no. SEBI/ HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/ P/CIR/ 2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 are provided as 
Annexure-A below.
