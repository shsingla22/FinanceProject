The Board of Directors of IHH wishes to announce that on 12 February 2026, NTK has 
submitted to the Tokyo District Court (“the Court ”) a petition to further amend  NTK’s claim 
against Daiichi Sankyo (“ Petition to Amend  Claim s”) and the copy of the Petition to Amend  
Claim s was delivered to Daiichi Sankyo by the Court on 17 February 2026 .  
 
As a background, o n 14 November 2023 and 20 May 2025 , the Company had announced that 
NTK had sought the following reliefs from Daiichi Sankyo, in accordance with the applicable 
substantive laws:  
 
(i) INR109,299,359,054 (equivalent to RM5,708,268,325.95 and 
JPY199,785,395,700.48 )1  for losses arising from NTK’s tortious claim (other than 
defamation) against Daiichi Sankyo; JPY5,000,000 (equivalent to RM142,860.00 and 
INR2,735,419.14)1 for losses arising from NTK’s defamation claim against Daiichi 
Sankyo; and the accrued interest on the damages claimed;  
 
(ii) by way of injunction, to refrain Daiichi Sankyo from obstructing the Open Offers  by 
making defamatory statements to the Security and Exchanges Board of India (“SEBI ”) 
and to any other third parties, including the public at large  (“the Injunctive Claim ”); 
and 
 
(iii) to publish a statement on Daiichi Sankyo’s website and issue a statement to SEBI to 
vindicate NTK’s reputation.  
 
Pursuant to the completion of the Open Offers on 10 November 2025,  the Petition to Amend 
Claims has been filed  with the Court to amend the Injunctive Claim to prevent Daiichi Sankyo 
from obstructing the acquisition of shares and any other investment and corporate exercises 
in respect of  Fortis and Malar by NTK, by making defamatory statements to SEBI and to any 
other third parties, including the public at large , in accordance with applicable substantive 
laws. Apart from this, there is no change to the other reliefs sought by NTK from Daiichi  
Sankyo .  
 
NTK has reserved its rights to further amend the amount of damages in the Petition to Amend 
Claims.  
 
The Company will make further announcements in the event of any material developments on 
the above matter.  
  
This announcement is dated 19 February 2026.
