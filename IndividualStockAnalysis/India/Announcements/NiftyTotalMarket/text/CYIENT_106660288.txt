e-voting process:

22056; CP No. 8068) as the scrutinizer (Scrutinizer) for conducting the Postal Ballot (remote e-
voting process) in a fair and transparent manner.  
 
The Company had engaged the services of KFin Technologies Limited (KFin), the registrar and 
share transfer agent, for the purpose of providing evoting facility and technical services relating to 
the Postal Ballot to all its members.  
The equity shareholders as on the cut-off date i.e. 08 May 2026 were entitled to vote on the 
resolution stated in the Postal Ballot Notice.

the remote e-voting commenced at 9 AM IST on Tuesday, 12 May 2026 and ended at 5 PM IST 
on Wednesday, 10 June 2026. The newspaper advertisements required under the Act were 
published in Business Standard (all editions) on 12 May 2026 and Mana Telangana newspapers on 
12 and 13 May 2026.
