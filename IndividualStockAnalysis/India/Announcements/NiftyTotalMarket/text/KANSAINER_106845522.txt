Kansai Nerolac Paints Ltd Announces Q 2 Results FY 202 6-202 7 
 
 Mumbai, November 4, 2026: Kansai Nerolac Paints Limited, one of India’s leading paint companies and a leader in industrial coatings, today announced its unaudited financial results for the quarter and half-year ended September 30, 2026.  
 For the quarter, revenue from operations stood at ₹ 2268.77 crore, representing year -on-year 
growth of  18.9%. EBITDA was ₹  223.23 crore, an increase of  4.9% over the corresponding quarter 
of the previous year, while profit before tax was ₹ 191.88  crore, representing growth of 4.3%.  
 For the half year, revenue from operations stood at ₹ 4601.60 crore, representing year -on-year 
growth of  14.2%. EBITDA was ₹  553.11 crore, an increase of  5.8%, while  profit before tax was      ₹ 
510.41 crore, representing growth of 4.0 % over the corresponding period of the previous year.   
  Commenting on the results, Mr. Pravin Chaudhari , Managing Director, Kansai Nerolac Paints 
Ltd said, “We delivered healthy revenue growth during the quarter, supported by good momentum 
across both Decorative and Industrial businesses. The Industrial business maintained strong momentum across key segments, benefiting from our established customer relations hips, 
technology capabilities and diversified portfolio.  
 In Decorative, our focus remains on pursuing growth with profitability. We continued to strengthen our position in attractive adjacencies, including construction chemicals, waterproofing, wood finishes and projects, while maintaining disciplined market execution.  
 Raw- material and foreign- exchange volatility continued to exert pressure on input costs. We have 
taken calibrated pricing actions to partly mitigate the impact and are intensifying our efforts on product mix, formulation optimisation, sourcing efficiencies  and cost management. These initiatives 
remain important for progressively improving the quality and sustainability of earnings.  
 Looking ahead, we expect demand conditions to remain supportive across both Decorative and Industrial businesses, aided by the festive season and underlying activity in automotive, infrastructure, real estate and core industrial sectors. At the same time, we remain watchful of geopolitical uncertainty, commodity prices and currency movements.  
 Our priorities remain clear: deliver sustainable growth, strengthen margins through disciplined execution, expand in attractive segments and continue investing in innovation, brand, distribution and customer service.”
