Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
Sub: Intimation regarding the closure of Buyback of fully paid-up equity shares of ₹ 2/- 
each (Rupees Two only) (“Equity Share(s)”) of Advanced Enzyme Technologies 
Limited (“Company”) from the Open Market through stock exchange mechanism 
pursuant to the provisions of the SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018 
as amended (“Buyback Regulations”) and the Companies Act, 2013 and Rules 
made thereunder, as amended (“Act”).  
 
Re:  Intimation under Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III, and other 
 applicable provisions of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
 Obligations and Disclosure Requirements), Regulations, 2015, as amended. 
 
This is further to (i) our letter dated August 08, 2026 informing the outcome of the meeting 
of our Board of Directors (" Board") approving the Buyback and (ii) our letter dated August 
11, 2026 regarding publication of the Public Announcement dated August 10, 2026 for the 
Buyback. 
 
Pursuant to the resolution passed by the Board on August 08, 2026, the Company approved 
the Buyback of Company’s fully paid-up Equity Shares, at a buyback price not exceeding ₹ 
500/- (Rupees Five Hundred only) per Equity Share (“ Maximum Buyback Price ”) from the 
shareholders/ beneficial owners of the Company (other than those who are Promoters, 
members of the Promoter Group and persons in control of the Company) (“ Eligible 
Shareholders ”) excluding Transaction Costs payable in cash for an aggregate amount not 
exceeding ₹ 697,000,000 (Rupees Six Hundred and Ninety Seven Million only) (hereinafter 
referred to as the “ Maximum Buyback Size ”), being 9.99% and 5.09% of the aggregate of the 
total paid-up Equity Share capital and free reserves of the Company based on the latest 
standalone and consolidated audited financial statements of the Company respectively, as at 
March 31, 2026 and which is not more than 10% of the total paid-up Equity Share capital and 
free reserves of the Company, whichever sets out a lower amount, in accordance with the 
proviso to Regulation 5(i)(b) of the Buyback Regulations and in accordance with the 
provisions contained in the Buyback Regulations and the Act (hereinafter referred to as the 
“Buyback”). 
 
 
 
The Company commenced the Buyback on Friday, August 14, 2026, from the Eligible 
Shareholders from the Open Market through the Stock Exchanges mechanism under the 
Buyback Regulations. 
 
In this regard, please be informed that as on October 30, 2026, the Company has bought back 
2,410,906 (Two Million Four Hundred Ten Thousand Nine Hundred and Six)) Equity Shares 
at an average price of ₹ 289.09/- (Rupees Two Hundred and Eighty Nine and Nine Paisa 
only) per Equity Share and utilized ₹ 696,962,962.22 (Rupees Six Hundred Ninety-Six 
Million Nine Hundred Sixty-Two Thousand Nine Hundred Sixty-Two and Twenty-Two 
Paise), which is 99.99% of the Maximum Buyback Size (excluding Transaction Costs). 
Accordingly, the Buyback Committee in its meeting held today approved the closure of the 
Buyback, pursuant to the terms of the Public Announcement, with effect from the end of 
market hours today i.e., Friday, October 30, 2026, prior to the expiry of the Sixty-Six 
Working Days from the commencement date of the Buyback. 
 
The Company has already extinguished 1,713,324 (One Million Seven Hundred Thirteen 
Thousand Three Hundred and Twenty-Four) Equity Shares bought back till September 2026 
and will extinguish remaining 697,582 (Six Hundred Ninety-Seven Thousand Five Hundred 
and Eighty-Two) Equity Shares bought back during October 2026 within the timelines 
prescribed under the Buyback Regulations. The details of the Pre Buyback and Post Buyback 
shareholding pattern of the Company is enclosed herewith as Annexure-I . 
 
The post Buyback public announcement (“Post Buyback Public Announcement”) will be 
released in due course in accordance with the provisions of the Buyback Regulations.  
 
The aforesaid intimation is also being uploaded on the website of the Company i.e. 
www.advancedenzymes.com.
