Sub: Disclosure under Reg. 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 – 
Amalgamation of Capitalgate Investment Advisors Private Limited with 
Sundaram Alternate Assets Limited.   
 
Ref :  Our letter SEC:138:2025-26/MAA dated November 3, 2025 
 
We wish to inform you that the Office of the Regional Director, Southern Region, Chennai, 
has, vide Order dated 30th March 2026, sanctioned the Scheme of Amalgamation of 
Capitalgate Investment Advisors Private Limited (CGIA), a Wholly Owned Subsidiary of  
Sundaram Alternate Assets Limited(SAAL), with SAAL, a wholly-owned subsidiary of 
Sundaram Asset Management Company Limited (SAMCL), which is a wholly-owned 
subsidiary of Sundaram Finance Limited (SFL).  The Order of the Regional Director has 
been filed by SAAL with the Registrar of Companies on 17th April 2026.  The Appointed Date 
and Effective Date of the merger are 1st January 2026 and 17th April 2026 respectively. 
 
Details as required in terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, read 
with SEBI Master Circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024, 
are annexed to this letter. Further, a copy of the order issued by the office of the Regional 
Director together with the scheme of amalgamation is enclosed for your records. 
 
Thanking you,
