Devyani International ’s Q2 FY2027 Financial Results  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Gurugram , November 02 , 2026 : Devyani International Limited (DIL), one of the leading global Quick Service 
Restaurant (QSR) operators , has announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. 
Financial Performance Review:  
• Store network at 2,325. 70 new stores were added  during the quarter, with 50 in KFC.  
• Q2 Revenues reached Rs. 1 5,194 million, up 1 0.4% YoY  
• KFC SSSG accelerates to +4.4%. Costa at + 9.9%. BBK delivered + 8.1% SSSG . 
• Margin improvement continues . Brand Contribution margins  up +1.3% for the quarter and + 1.2% YTD .  
• Operating  EBITDA grew by +34% reaching INR 1, 251 Mn with 8.2% margin.  
• Delivered INR 57 Mn in Profit before Tax during the quarter – against a loss last year .  
Commenting on the performance for Q2 FY2027 Mr. Ravi Jaipuria, Chairman , Devyani  International Limited said , 
“Our growth momentum carried on firmly into the second quarter. We have had a good quarter with positive SSSGs 
ranging from 4% to 9% across our brand portfolio in India except PH which saw sequentially better numbers. We added 
70 net new stores during the quarter. KFC led the store additions, with 50 net new units added in India. This takes our 
store footprint to 1,923 stores in India and 2,325 globally. We are on track t o meet our full -year guidance of 200 –220 net 
new additions, with the same discipline: the right locations and a clear line of sight to profitability.  Building on the positive 
SSSG performance, we delivered consolidated sales of INR 1,519 Cr, growing 10.4% yoy. Despite steep inflation in raw 
materials and wages, the leverage from higher same -store sales and tight cost control helped us lift the EBITDA by  nearly 
18%, to INR 229 Cr. This is one of the best Q2 operating EBITDA numbers that we have seen and the same helped us 
post Profit before Taxes of INR 5.7 Cr for the quarter, against a loss last year.  Your company delivered a PBT of Rs 29 
Crs in H1 vs a loss of Rs 24 Crs in the previous year . 
Q2 FY27 Performance Highlights   
Delivered strong Operating EBITDA growth of 34% YOY. Store expansion pace picks up.  
- Positive SSSG growth accelerates across most of the portfolio including KFC.  
- Quarterly reported EBITDA at INR 229 Cr.  
- Positive PBT for the quarter against loss last year  
- Store expansion on track to add  200-220 new stores during the year.  
   
Equally encouraging is the quality of growth that we are observing. Across brands, we have witnessed transaction led 
growth – and growth in our on-premises  channel sales. Both these trends are very heartening and are key to a long -term 
sustainable growth trajectory . 
Our new management team is settling in, and we are confident that our plans with respect to pre -merger preparation are 
progressing well. We are expecting that the merger should get approvals in line with our initial plans.  You will be pleased 
to know that  we have received CCI approval for the merger from the authorities .” 
 
About Devyani International Limited:  
 
Devyani International Limited is one of India’s largest chain quick service restaurant  (QSR) operators, with a network of 
over 2, 200 stores across more than 350 cities in India, Thailand, Nigeria, and Nepal. The Company’s portfolio represents 
a compelling blend of iconic global brands and successful homegrown concepts.  
DIL holds the distinction of being the largest franchisee of Yum! Brands in India and Nepal. In addition, DIL is the sole 
franchisee in India for several international brands, including Costa Coffee, New York Fries, and Sanook Kitchen.  
Complementing its global portfolio, DIL has developed strong indigenous brands such as Vaango, a South Indian 
vegetarian cuisine concept, and The Food Street, a food court format that brings multiple brands together under one roof 
to enhance consumer exper ience. DIL has also strengthened its Indian cuisine offerings through the acquisition of Sky 
Gate Hospitality, which owns popular brands such as Biryani By Kilo and Goila Butter Chicken.
