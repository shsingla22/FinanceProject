Over the last three fiscal years, the Bank expanded its gross loan book from Rs. 24,085 crores in FY23 to Rs. 
40,655 crores in FY26 at a healthy compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 19% that further grew to  Rs. 45,699 
crores as of Sep 30, 2026. This expansion was highlighted by a significant strategy shift to grow secured loans to 
shield the bank from microfinance sector volatility. Driven by a strong 45.2% CAGR, the Bank's secured loan 
book grew significantly ov er three years. It rose from Rs. 6,555 crore s in FY23 to Rs. 20,079 crores by March 
2026. Secured loan  book share  as of Sep  30, 2026, was at 52.1%. Deposit grew from Rs.  25,538 crores in FY23 
to Rs. 45,668 crores in FY26 at a healthy CAGR of 21.4% that further grew to Rs. 50,643 crore s as of Sep 30, 
2026 crossing the Rs. 50,000 crore milestone for the first time. CASA grew from Rs. 6,744 crore in FY23 to Rs. 
13,062 crore in FY26 with a healthy CAGR of 24.7%. CASA ratio grew from 26.4% to 28.6% in the same 
duration.   
 
At its meeting on May 8, 2026, the Board of the Bank commended the Bank’s consistent growth trajectory. Since 
its last equity infusion via a QIP in FY2022 –23, the Bank has self -funded its strategic expansion entirely through 
internal accruals and retained earnings. This capability underscores the robust financial health and operational 
self-sufficiency  of the Bank . To support the Bank’s future growth phases and fortify the solid foundation, the 
Board approved a capital raise of up to Rs. 2,000 crores through one or more permissible routes. This was 
subsequently  approved by the shareholders at the 10th AGM on July 24, 2026.
