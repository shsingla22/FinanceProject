Pursuant to the requirements of Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Listing Regulations, we wish to inform 
you that the Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting held today i.e. on Wednesday, October 28, 2026, 
have, inter alia, considered and approved the following: 
 
a) Proposal to divest its entire holding (i.e. 14.18%) held in Medcords Healthcare Solutions Private Limited 
(‘Medcords’) on fully converted & diluted basis, through its wholly-owned subsidiary, Startup Investments 
(Holding) Limited (SIHL). A provision for impairment of full amount against said investment was booked in 
FY 2023-24.  
 
Post this divestment, Medcords will cease to be Joint Venture of the Company. 
 
b) Proposal to divest its entire holding (i.e. 31.64%) held in Unnati Online Private Limited (‘Unnati Online’) on 
fully converted & diluted basis (excluding ESOP Pool), through its wholly-owned subsidiary, Startup 
Investments (Holding) Limited (SIHL). A provision for impairment of full amount against said investment 
was booked in FY 2019-20.  
 
Post this divestment, Unnati Online will cease to be Associate Company of the Company. 
 
c) Investment of an amount of about Rs. 150 crores in Redstart Labs (India) Limited, wholly owned subsidiary 
of the Company. 
 
d) Basis the recommendation of the Nomination & Remuneration Committee, reconstitution of the Audit 
Committee (AC), by inducting Mr. Naresh Chand Gupta, Additional Director (Independent Category), as a 
member of the Committee, with effect from October 29, 2026. Post reconstitution, the composition of AC 
with effect from October 29, 2026 shall be as under:
