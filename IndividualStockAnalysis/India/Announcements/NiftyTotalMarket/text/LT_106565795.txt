Sub:   Amalgamation of SiliConch Systems Private Limited, a step -down subsidiary 
of the Company with L&T Semiconductor Technologies Limited a wholly 
owned subsidiary.  
 
We wish to inform you that SiliConch Systems Private Limited (“SSPL”), a wholly -owned 
subsidiary of L&T Semiconductor Technologies Limited (“LTSCT”) and a step -down 
subsidiary of the Company, has been amalgamated with and into L&T Semiconductor 
Technologies Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of the Company, pursuant to a Scheme of 
Amalgamation approved by the Regional Director, Western Region, Ministry of Corporate 
Affairs, vide order dated March 13, 2026. The Appointed Date for the Scheme is August 10, 
2025.  
 
The certified copy of the aforesaid order approving the amalgamation of SiliConch Systems 
Private Limited with and into L&T Semiconductor Technologies Limited, has been filed with 
the Registrar of Companies today, i.e. March 24, 2026, which is the effective date of the 
amalgamation in terms of the Scheme.  
 
Accordingly, SiliConch Systems Private Limited has ceased to be a step down subsidiary of 
the Company with effect from March 24, 2026.  
 
We request you to take the same on record.
