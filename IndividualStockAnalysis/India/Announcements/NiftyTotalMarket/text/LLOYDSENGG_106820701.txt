Annexure -A  
SUMMARY OF THE PROCEEDINGS OF THE MEETING OF THE UNSECURED CREDITORS 
OF LLOYDS ENGINEERING WORKS LIMITED (‘TRANSFEREE COMPANY’ OR ‘THE 
COMPANY’) HELD AS PER THE DIRECTIONS ISSUED BY THE HON’BLE NATIONAL 
COMPANY LAW TRIBUNAL, MUMBAI BENCH (‘HON’BLE TRIBUNAL’) VIDE ITS ORDER 
DATED AUGUST 19, 2026 (‘ORDER’)  
  
The meeting of the unsecured creditors of the Company was held on Friday, October 16, 2026 
at 2.45 P.M. (IST) through Video Conferencing or Other Audio Video Means (‘VC’/’OAVM’). 
 
The meeting was chaired by Mr. Pranay Luniya, Practicing Chartered Accountant, Chairperson 
appointed by the Hon’ble Tribunal. Mr. Harshvardhan Tarkas, Practicing Company Secretary, 
scrutinizer, was appointed by the Hon’ble Tribunal to scrutinize the e-voting conducted at the 
meeting of the unsecured creditors of the Company.  
 
The aforesaid Meeting was attended by the following Personnel 
1. Mr. Pranay Luniya, Practicing Chartered

The Chairman of the meeting informed the unsecured creditors that physical attendance at the 
meeting is not required and hence, the facility for the appointment of the proxies is not available 
for this meeting. The Chairman informed the unsecured creditors that the Company had 
provided the facility to cast their votes electronically through NSDL on the resolution set forth in 
the Notice. The Chairman also informed that voting by electronic means was also available 
during the meeting to those unsecured creditors who had not already voted by means of remote 
e-voting.   
 
 
 
 
 
 
The Notice convening unsecured creditors was taken as read as the same was already circulated 
to the unsecured creditors whose names appeared in the books of the Company as on March 31, 
2026. 
Chairman informed the unsecured creditors that the Scheme, if approved by the unsecured 
creditors of the Company, will be subject to subsequent approval of the Hon'ble Tribunal and 
such other approvals, permissions and sanctions from any other regulatory or statutory 
authorities, as may be deemed necessary.  
 
The Chairman requested the scrutinizer to confirm the quorum. On confirmation by the 
scrutinizer on the requisite quorum being present, the Chairman called the meeting to order. 
 
The Chairman thereafter briefed the unsecured creditors on the salient features and rationale of 
the proposed Scheme. 
 
The resolution set out in the Notice, as reproduced below, was recommended for consideration by 
Chairman and approval of the unsecured creditors through the e-voting system. 
 
Approval of Scheme of Merger by Absorption of Scheme of Merger by Absorption of Lloyds 
Infrastructure & Construction Limited (‘First Transferor Company’), Metalfab Hightech Private 
Limited (‘Second Transferor Company’) and Techno Industries Private Limited (‘Third Transferor 
Company’) with Lloyds Engineering Works Limited (‘Transferee Company’) and their respective 
shareholders (‘Scheme’) under Section 230 to Section 232 of the Companies Act, 2013 (‘the Act’) 
and rules made thereunder. 
 
The Chairman informed the unsecured creditors that the Scheme shall be considered approved if 
the resolution is approved by a majority in number representing three-fourths in value of the 
unsecured creditors present and voting, in terms of Section 230(6) of the Act. 
 
In compliance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the applicable provisions of 
the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“LODR 
Regulations”), the facility of remote e-voting prior to the meeting, as well as e-voting during 
the meeting, was provided to all unsecured creditors who were entitled to vote as on the cut-
off date, i.e., Tuesday, March 31, 2026. The remote e-voting facility commenced on Monday, 
October 12, 2026, at 9:00 A.M. (IST) and ended on Thursday, October 15, 2026, at 5:00 P.M. (IST). 
 
 
 
The Chairman informed the unsecured creditors that the e-voting results along with the 
Scrutinizer’s Report would be communicated to the Stock Exchanges within two working days 
from the conclusion of the Meeting and would also be placed on the websites of the Company, 
NSDL, and the stock exchanges. 
 
The Chairman, thereafter, thanked the unsecured creditors for attending and participating at the 
meeting and requested the unsecured creditors who had not voted earlier, to complete e-voting in 
the next 15 minutes. Thereafter, the meeting concluded at 3.25 PM (IST) after being open for 15 
minutes for e-voting to be completed by the unsecured creditors.
