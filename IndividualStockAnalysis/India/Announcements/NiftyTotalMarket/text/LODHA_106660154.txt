Sub:  Intimation under Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”) – Cessation of a 
Wholly Owned Subsidiary  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we wish to inform you that Thane Commercial Tower A Management Private Limited  (“Thane 
Commercial ”) has ceased to be a Wholly Owned Subsidiary of the Company  with effect  from June 10, 
2026 . 
 
Thane Commercial was incorporated solely  for the purpose of providing infrastructure, facility 
management and allied services in relation to the commercial  building known as “Lodha iThink -Tower A’ ” 
situated  at Eastern Express Highway, Chirak Nagar,  Thane ( W), Taluka & Dist . Thane  – 400607  (the 
“Project ”). In accordance with the contractual arrangements entered into with the respective unit holders  
of the Project and pursuant to the provision(s) of Maharashtra Ownership Flats Act , 1963  (the “ MOFA ”), 
the Company has transferred /shall transfer  proportionate equity shares of Thane Commercial to each 
unit holder corresponding to their ownership in the Project . 
 
The details required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with the relevant SEBI 
circular(s) is enclosed as Annexure -A.  
 
We request you to take the same on record.  
 
The above information is also available on the Company’s website at www.lodhagroup.com . 
 
Thanking you,
