Tata Motors registered 49% growth YoY with total sales of 44 ,411 commercial vehicle units in 
August 2026  
Mumbai, 1 September  2026 : Tata Motors Limited sales in the domestic & international markets for August  2026 stood 
at 44,411 units, compared to 29, 863 units during August  2025, up 49% YoY.  
 
Category  August ’26 August ’25 Growth (Y -o-Y) 
HCV Trucks  10,612 7,451 42%  
ILMCV Trucks  6,859 5,711 20%  
Passenger Carriers  4,701 3,577 31%  
SCV cargo and pickup  14,447 10,742 34% 
Total CV Domestic  36,619 27,481 33%  
International Business  7,792 2,382 227%  
Total CV  44,411 29,863 49%  
 
• Domestic sales of MH&ICV in August 2026, 17,531 units vs 13, 405 units in August 2025 (up 31% YoY)  
• Domestic & International sales for MH&ICV in August  2026, was 18 ,920 units vs 14, 667 units in August 2025 (up 29% 
YoY)
