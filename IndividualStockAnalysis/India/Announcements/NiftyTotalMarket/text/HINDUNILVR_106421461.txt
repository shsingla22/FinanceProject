In furtherance to our Letter dated 18th September 2025, and pursuant to Regulation 30 of the 
Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)

following the month in which the conditions and matters  referred to in Clause 20 of the Scheme 
have occurred or have been fulfilled , obtained or waived, as applicable, in accordance with the 
Scheme and the Appointed Date is the same as the Effective Date . The Company will intimate 
the Effective Date to the Stock Exchanges in due course.
