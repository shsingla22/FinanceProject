In continuation of the same, we wish to inform that each of the Transferor Companies and the

Arrangement. Accordingly, in term of Clause 1.7 of Part I of the Scheme, the Effective Date

Companies stands amalgamated into the Company and be dissolved without being wound up 
w.e.f. March 29, 2025. Further, pursuant to clause 3 and 4 of Part II of the Scheme, all the 
Assets and Liabilities of the Transferor Companies stands transferred to the Transferee 
Company.
