Sub: Disclosure regarding  change in shareholding of the Promoter and Promoter Group of

 Dear Sir/Madam,  
We wish  to inform you that the Company has received an intimation from Dr. Krishna Prasad 
Chigurupati, Promoter of the Company , regarding the sale of  1,72,00,000  equity shares of Granules 
India Limited on September 11 , 202 6, through  a block deal  & open market . The transaction saw  
participation from marquee institutional investors, including Capital Group, Kotak Mahindra Life 
Insurance Company,  ChrysCapital, Allspring,  and other domestic and global investor s. The 
transaction was undertaken to fund  the second tranche of the Company’s ongoing preferential issue  
and support  the next phase of growth initiatives in line with its stated  objects . 
 
The Company has deployed  the funds raised through the preferential issue in February 2026 and 
executed  the identified growth initiatives at a pace faster than originally envisaged . Accordingly , 
the funds ha ve been utilised significantly earlier than anticipated, reflecting the Company’s  focused 
execution of its strategic growth plans. The utilisation remains fully aligned with the objects 
disclosed at the time of the preferential issue.  
 
Following the transaction, the aggregate shareholding of the Promoter and Promoter Group in the 
Company has changed from 38.02% to 31.08% of the paid-up equity share capital of the Company.  
On a diluted basis, the aggregate shareholding of the Promoter and Promoter Group after the 
transaction stands at 37.39%. Dr. Krishna Prasad Chigurupati’s individual shareholding,  as a 
Promoter of the Company , has changed from 31.00% to 24.06% of the paid-up equity share capital 
of the Company.
