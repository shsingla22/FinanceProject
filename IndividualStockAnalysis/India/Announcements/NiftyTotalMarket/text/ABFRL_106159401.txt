Committee of the Board of Directors  (“Committee”) at its meeting held today i.e. February  24, 2025,

(1,22,02,49,942 fully paid- up equity shares of ₹ 10/ - each).  
 The aforesaid e quity shares shall rank pari -passu, in all respects with existing e quity shares of the Company.  
 The Company has received in -principle  approval from Stock Exchanges for issue of the aforesaid e quity shares 
on preferential basis on February 12, 2025 . 
 The meeting of the Preferential Issue Committee commenced at 3:40 p.m. and concluded at 3: 55 p.m.
