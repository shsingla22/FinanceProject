appointed as the Scrutiniser by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal, Ahmedabad Bench

1. The notice and explanatory statement dated March 27, 2026, was sent to the Equity 
Shareholders of the Company through electronic mode to those Equity Shareholders 
whose e-mail id are registered with Company, Registrar and Share Transfer Agent and 
concerned depositories as on March 20, 2026. 
2. On March 28, 2026, the Company has also published the notice of the Meeting of 
Equity Shareholders of the Company in Indian Express in English language (All India 
Editions) and Sandesh in Gujarati Language (Gujarat Edition). 
3. The Company had availed services of Central Depository Services (India) Limited 
("CDSL") for the purpose of providing facility of VC / OAVM, voting by remote e-voting 
and e-voting during the Meeting.
