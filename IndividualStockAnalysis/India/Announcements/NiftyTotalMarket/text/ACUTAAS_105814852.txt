with the total borrowing limits of upto Rs. 3000 million only  (Rupees Three Thousand million only) apart

creation of securities for such borrowings by mortgage and/or charge/hypothecation on movable and 
immovable assets of the Company in terms of provisions of section 180(1)(a) of the Companies Act,
