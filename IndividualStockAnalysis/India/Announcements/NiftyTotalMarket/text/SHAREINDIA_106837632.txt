Recent Developments:  
Proposed Restructuring with Ace Alpha Tech : 
Share India Securities Limited has received in -principle approval from its Board of Directors  to 
evaluate the proposed  amalgamation  of Ace Alpha Tech Limited   with the Company , subject to 
applicable regulatory and statutory approvals.  
Ace Alpha's capabilities in customised front -end development, API integration, analytics, risk and 
user management, product development and end -to-end client solutions  are expected to 
complement the Group's existing capabilities across algorithmic trading, quantitative research and 
execution infrastructure.  
The proposed acquisition  is expected to create opportunities for technology integration, faster 
product development, enhanced client experience, cross -selling and broader deployment of 
technology solutions  across the Group and external clients. The proposal remains subject to 
applicable approvals and completion of the restructuring process.  
Amalgamation of Silverleaf Capital Services  
The amalgamation of Silverleaf Capital Services  is another step towards strengthening technology 
integration, enhancing the algorithmic trading ecosystem  and creating opportunities for business 
growth and global expansion.

About the Company  
Since its inception in 1994, Share India Securities Limited has steadily evolved into a prominent financial services 
conglomerate, primarily focusing on algo -trading solutions. While initially serving High Networth  Individuals (HNIs), 
Share India is now committed to expanding its services to cater to the burgeoning retail investor market. The Company 
has broadened its service offerings, assisting clients in growing and multiplying their investments. Share India's ap proach 
is grounded in transparency, clarity, honesty, and a customer -centric philosophy, which has garnered trust within the 
Indian market. Share India has channelled its expertise towards better serving India's financial sector. Share India is 
having rapi d growth as a fintech brokerage with a strong retail presence, strong market share in algo -trading, consistent 
top rankings in the Indian Derivatives Market, and a robust financial position. Share India proudly boasts an impressive 
client base, including 4 8,877 clients in broking and 36,668  NBFC clients, 20,563  MF Clients, 2 30 Institutional clients and 
1,05,784  Lives covered under Insurance. Share India's extensive network encompasses 2 42 broking branches and 
franchisees, along with 70 NBFC branches. The Company's journey within the financial sector reflects their dedication 
to growth, innovation, and building trust with customers, positioning them as a leader in the ever -evolving landscape 
of Indian finance.
