Dear Sir/Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), read with Part A of Schedule III of the Listing 
Regulations, we wish to inform you of the sale/disposal of 04 (four) “IQ” retail Stores (IQ 
Attapur, IQ Madhapur, Telangana, IQ Rajahmundry, and IQ Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh) of the 
Company on a going concern basis. 
The Company is engaged in the retail business through multiple branded stores. One such 
format is the brand 1Q’, under which the Company undertakes the retail sale of Apple 
products. 
The decision of the Company to sell has been taken after due consideration of the following 
factors: 
1. Strategic realignment to focus on strengthening the Company’s own retail brands and 
innovative retail concepts. 
2. Optimization of resources and Return on Investment, considering the relatively lesser 
contributions of the “IQ” brand stores, to the operating profits. 
3. Long-term focus on strengthening the proprietary retail formats such as “Audio & 
Beyond,” “Easy Kitchen,” and “Kitchen Stories.”
