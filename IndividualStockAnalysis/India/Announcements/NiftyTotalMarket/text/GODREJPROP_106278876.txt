Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), we hereby inform 
you of the execution of the Share Purchase Agreement dated June 25, 2025, entered into by the 
following parties: 
 
1. Godrej Properties Limited (“GPL”), Godrej Ventures and Investment Advisers Private 
Limited (“GVAIAPL”) and Madhuvan Enterprises Private Limited (“MEPL”), whereby 
GPL has sold 2.5% stake held by it in equity share capital of MEPL, to GVAIAPL at a 
consideration of Rs. 3.25 crore. 
 
2. Godrej Projects Development Limited (“GPDL”), GVAIAPL, Munjal Hospitality Private 
Limited (“MHPL”) and GPL, whereby, GPDL has sold 2.5% stake held by it in equity 
share capital of MHPL, to GVAIAPL at a consideration of Rs. 33.44 crore. 
 
3. GPDL, GVAIAPL and Vagishwari Land Developers Private Limited (“VLDPL”) 
whereby, GPDL has sold 2.5% stake held by it in equity share capital of VLDPL, to 
GVAIAPL at a consideration of Rs. 17.27 crore. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The details required under Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 are enclosed herewith as 
Annexures I, II and III respectively. 
 
Request you to take the same on record.
