“Public Announcement ”) and the letter of offer dated August 6, 2024 (the “ Letter of Offer ”)

August 16, 2024.

Shares. A copy of the certificate dated September 6, 2024 by Link Intime India Private Limited

dated September 02, 2024 of National Securities Depository Limited confirming 
extinguishment of 1,26,55,970 Equity Shares in dematerialized form is enclosed herewith as 
Annexure B .
