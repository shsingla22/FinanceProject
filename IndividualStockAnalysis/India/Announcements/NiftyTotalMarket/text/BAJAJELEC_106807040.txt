proﬁts  FY 2025 -26 was a year of considerable pressure for the Company , however, it is equally 
important to recognise that a signiﬁcant portion of the reported Loss Before Tax of ₹75 
crore was attributable to restructuring and business -strengthening initiatives 
undertaken during the year. These included exceptional charges of ₹91 cr ore, 
comprising costs associated with the impairment charges relating to our Sambhajinagar facility, the impairment of moulds, tooling, and capitalised product 
development costs, and implementation of the new Labour Codes.  
 
During the year, the Company’s total revenue from operations on a standalone basis 
stood at ₹4,462 crore, compared with ₹4,828 crore in the previous year, reﬂecting a 
decline of 7.6%. Revenue from the Lighting Solutions segment increased by 9.5% to 
₹1,120 crore compared with the previous year while revenue from the Consumer 
Products segment decreased by 12.2% to ₹3,343 crore.
