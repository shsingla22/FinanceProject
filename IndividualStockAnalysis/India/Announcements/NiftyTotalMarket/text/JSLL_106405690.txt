1. Approval and Execution of Exclusive Diagnostic Collaboration Agreement with Chandan 
Healthcare Limited : 
 
The Board considered and approved entering into an Exclusive Diagnostic Collaboration 
Agreement  (“Agreement ”) with Chandan Healthcare Limited , a company incorporated 
under the Companies Act, 1956, having its registered office at Biotech Park, Sector G, 
Jankipuram, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh – 226021 (CIN: U85110UP2003PLC193493).  
 
Under the Agreement:  
 
 Chandan Healthcare Limited (“Chandan ”) has been appointed as the exclusive diagnostic 
partner for all diagnostic, pathological, radiological, and allied healthcare services across all 
existing and future hospitals, clinics, and wellness centers operated un der the “Jeena Sikho ” 
brand.  
 The collaboration shall remain valid for a lock -in period of five (5) years . 
 Chandan shall establish, operate, and maintain fully functional Diagnostic Centers and 
Collection Centers within Jeena Sikho ’s facilities, investing i n all necessary infrastructure, 
equipment, and manpower for providing advanced diagnostic and imaging services.  
 Jeena Sikho shall provide suitable space, utilities, and access within its hospitals and clinics, 
and shall recognize Chandan as its “Official D iagnostic Partner ” in all communications, 
branding, and marketing materials.  
 Jeena Sikho  will also provide Chandan Healthcare cards to its patients, enabling them to avail 
preferential or concessional diagnostic services from Chandan as per the terms of the 
Agreement.  
 The collaboration aims to ensure high -quality, timely, and standardized dia gnostic support for 
all Jeena Sikho patients.  
 
The Agreement was accordingly approved and executed by the Company  on October 13, 2025 . 
 
 
 
 
  
 
 
 
2. Subscription of Convertible Warrants under the proposed Preferential Issue of Chandan 
Healthcare Limited  
 
The Board considered the proposal for Company ’s participation in the proposed preferential 
issue of convertible warrants  by Chandan Healthcare Limited  (“Issuer Company ”). In this 
regard, the Board considered and approved for subscription of upto 18,00,000 (Eighteen Lakh)  
warrants, each convertible into one equity share of a face value Rs. 10/ - (Rupees Ten only)  of 
the Issuer Company at an issue price of Rs. 234 .00 each , as determined under the provisions 
of the as per the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue of Capital and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2018,  aggregating to Rs. 42.12 crores, 25% of which shall be 
payable at the time of allotment of warrants and balance 75%  will be payable at the time of 
conversion of warrants into equity sh ares of the Issuer Company, within a maximum period of 
18 months  from the date of allotment.
