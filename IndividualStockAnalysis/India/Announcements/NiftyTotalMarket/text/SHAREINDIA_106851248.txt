done at “arm’s length”;  The proposed Scheme of Amalgamation does not constitute a Related Party Transaction, as 
Ace Alpha Tech Limited is not a related party of t he Company as on the date of entering 
into the proposed transaction.  
 
However, certain members from the Promoter and Promoter Group of the Company hold

Promoter Group do not have any interest in the Transferor Company.  
Area of Business of 
entity(ies) ; Ace Alpha Tech Limited  (the “Transferor Company”) is primarily engaged in developing, 
customising and providing technology -driven solutions for financial markets, particularly 
automated trading systems and trading solutions for institutional investors, high -net-worth 
individuals (HNIs) and retail traders. Its activities include the development of customised 
trading software incorporating advanced algorithms , which interfaces with existing order 
management systems (OMS) and risk management systems (RMS), together with other 
technology -related services and trading, dealing and investment in securities and other 
financial instruments on its own account.  
 
Share India Securities Limited  (the “Transferee Company”) is a technology -driven 
financial services company with a principal focus on capital markets and securities trading. 
Its business activities include share and stock broking, commodity derivatives broking, 
equity and currency derivatives broking, proprietary trading in securities and other financial 
instruments, depository participant services, portfolio management, research and analysis, 
distribution of mutual funds and other investment products, and other allied financial 
services. The Transferee Company is also engaged in technology -driven financial solutions, 
including automated and algorithmic trading systems, trading platforms and related 
technology services.
