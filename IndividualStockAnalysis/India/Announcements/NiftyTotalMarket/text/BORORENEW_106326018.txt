Dear Sirs, 
 
This is to inform that the Company has received on August 4, 2025, an Advisory letter from 
National Stock Exchange of India Ltd.  (NSE)  in connection with its application for  listing  
equity shares arising upon conversion of warrants issued by the Company. The matter 
pertains  to lock-in of pre-preferential holding of 870 shares of one allottee. The Company 
could not lock in the said shares due to the allottee bought and sold the shares during two 
trading days by oversight.  
 The Company has undertaken all the possible measures for complying with SEBI ICDR 
Regulations including weekly monitoring of trades until date of allotment. However, due to 
unavailability of any medium to monitor the trades on real time basis, the aforesaid trades 
could not be tracked by the Company and hence the same were not locked in. The Stock 
Exchanges has granted their approval for listing of the equity shares. The NSE has advised 
the Company to exercise due diligence while submitting further applications .   
 
Being  advisory in nature , the said letter has no impact on the financial, operation or other 
activities of the Company .  
 
You are requested to take the above on record.  
Yours faithfully,
