 In furtherance to the above and pursuant to Regulations 30 and 42 of the SEBI Listing Regulations , 
we wish to inform you that  the Company has fixed Tuesday , October 14, 2025 as the “R ecord Date” 
for the purpose of ascertaining the shareholders of the Company who shall be issued and allotted  
1 (One) share of TMLCV (face value of INR 2/ - each fully paid up), for every 1 (One) share of the 
Company (face value of INR 2/ - each fully paid up) of the same class of shares outstanding and as 
held by such shareholder in the Company on the “Record Date”.  
 
The said equity shares to be allotted by TMLCV are proposed to be listed with BSE Limited and the 
National Stock Exchange of India Limited, subject to necessary regulatory approvals.
