Annexure B 
Brief Profile of Mr. Sanjay Abhyankar 
Mr. Sanjay Abhyankar started his career in LIC of India, in the year 1993 and has been in YES 
BANK since September 2020 and currently designated as President, Vertical Head, Company 
Secretary Operations. During his tenure in the Bank, he was responsible for ensuring 
regulatory compliances with the applicable corporate and securities laws, secretarial 
standards and has handled the resp onsibility in the key areas including reconstruction of the 
Bank, formation of alternate board of the Bank, preferential issue, etc.  
Mr. Abhyankar possesses 27 years of work expe rience in the domains like Corporate Law, 
Legal, Compliance, Capital Market, Banking and Finance. He worked with the listed 
companies like Uttam Steel Limited, BSE Limite d, Shrenuj & Company Limited.  Prior to 
joining YES BANK he was Vice President & Co mpliance Officer of St ate Bank of India, 
wherein he has successfully handled the merger  of 5 Associate Banks of SBI and Bharatiya 
Mahila Bank Ltd. with SBI and handled the largest QIP of Asia.  
Mr. Abhyankar has received Gold Medal and Hall of Fame Award from Welingkar Institute 
of Management while studying MBA in Finance, an appreciation letter from National Stock 
Exchange of India for assisting them in their journey of continuous improvement and Business 
Excellence and BSE Ltd. for handling various bond issues through EBP Platform.  
Mr. Abhyankar is also a visiting faculty to  the Welingkar Institut e of Management, and 
various other Management Institutes, University  of Mumbai and Knowledge Centres of ICSI. 
Mr. Abhyankar is an Associate Member of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India and 
also holds B.Com, L.L.B, DCM and MBA degree in Finance.
