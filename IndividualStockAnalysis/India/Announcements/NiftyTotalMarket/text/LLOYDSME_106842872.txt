and Renewable Energy Projects .

Maharashtra

Thermal power plant at Konsari, Maharashtra, with an aggregate installed capacity of 360 
MW. The proposed power plant is primarily intended to meet the power requirements of the 
Company’s integrated operations at Hedri and Konsari.  
 
The details required pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with Clause B 
(3)(3.1) of Annexure 18 of Disclosure Circular, are set out under Annexure -B. 
 
b) Approval for Investment of  upto  30% Stake in Renewable Energy Projects for 
Procurement of 168 MW Power through the Captive Route .

for undertaking investments in renewable energy projects for captive consumption, in line 
with the Company's commitment towards sustainable and renewable sources of ene rgy and to 
meet its long -term operational and energy requirements.  
 
In this regard, the Company is evaluating potential investment opportunities and associated 
power procurement arrangements for securing renewable energy capacity, envisaged to be 
implemented in one or more tranches.  
 
The details as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with Part A Clause 
A(1)(1.1) of Annexure 18 of the Disclosure Circular  will be provided upon further approval
