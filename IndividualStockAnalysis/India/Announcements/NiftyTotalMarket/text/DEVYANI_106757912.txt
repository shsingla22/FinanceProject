effectiveness of the Scheme is conditional on the transfer by sale of 5,94,55,837 (Five

18.5% of the fully paid -up equity share capital of the Transferor Company  (‘Secondary 
Sale Transaction ’). 
 
  

 
 
 
Update s: 
 
4. We would now like to intimate that Arctic has informed the Transferee Company that the share 
purchase agreement between SFML and Arctic ( ‘SPA ’) has been terminated  by mutual agreement

of the Transferee Company in accordance with the Scheme, similar to the other shareholders of 
the Transferor Company.  Arctic ha s informed the Company that Arctic and SFML may continue 
exploring a secondary transaction, which if agreed, may be undertaken at a later date, in 
compliance with all applicable law s. It is clarified that the share exchange ratio and other terms 
and condit ions of the Scheme, as approved by the Board on January 1, 2026, remain unchanged 
pursuant to the approval of the revised Scheme.

has inter-alia:
