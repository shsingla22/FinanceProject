to prioritise transaction scale and merchant relationships through deliberate competitive pricing, while building additional

Building Transaction Scale Through Competitive Pricing  
The Company has deliberately priced competitively to deepen merchant relationships and grow payment volumes. Q2 
payments TPV grew 19% quarter-on-quarter, while NTR moderated 4% to 5.06 basis points. The Company sees payment 
scale as a platform for future merchant and enterprise monetisation. 
The Next Phase of AvenuesAI: Three Strategic Priorities for FY27  
1. Unlocking the value of Rediff  
The Company continues to pursue the integration of Rediff’s consumer and enterprise digital ecosystem with AvenuesAI’s 
payments and AI capabilities. RediffOne, digital commerce and communication services offer potential to deepen enterprise 
relationships, while RediffPay is intended to broaden consumer payment engagement. The objective is to develop additional 
revenue opportunities across a connected digital ecosystem. 
2. Expanding CCAvenue payments internationally  
AvenuesAI remains focused on building a scalable international payments franchise, including its previously stated US 
expansion priority. The Company intends to leverage its payment technology, enterprise relationships and regulated 
infrastructure while applying disciplined investment and execution standards. 
3. Building AI-led transaction intelligence (TISco)  
AvenuesAI is developing the AvenuesAI Transaction Intelligence Score (TISco) to apply permitted transaction and 
merchant-behaviour signals to risk assessment, fraud prevention, merchant intelligence and financial-services workflows.

About AvenuesAI Limited 
 
 
 
AvenuesAI Limited (formerly Infibeam Avenues Ltd) (“Company”) is one of the leading global financial technology (fintech) companies offering 
comprehensive digital payment and artificial intelligence (AI) solutions across industry verticals. The company’s payment infrastructure suite 
includes acquiring and issuing solutions, as well as core payment infrastructure for banks. Company’s flagship brand CCAvenue powers one of 
India’s largest Payment Gateway (PG) platforms, offering over 200 payment options that enable merchants to accept payments through websites 
and mobile devices in 27 international currencies. As part of its diversified digital ecosystem, Rediff.com, a subsidiary of AvenuesAI Limited 
(formerly Infibeam Avenues Ltd), strengthens the Company’s position in the AI-driven commerce and content platform space. Rediff’s offerings, 
combined with AvenuesAI’s advanced fintech and AI capabilities, enable a unified ecosystem of payments, platforms, and digital services that 
drive value across consumer and enterprise segments. The company processed transactions worth INR 5.04 trillion (US$ 53.4 billion) in FY26 
across payments and platforms and serves over 10 million clients across its digital payments and platform businesses, catering to merchants, 
enterprises, corporations, governments, and financial institutions in both domestic and international markets. AvenuesAI’s international operations 
span the United Arab Emirates, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Australia, and the United States of America, with an additional presence in Oman, 
where it partners with three of the country’s largest banks.
