Sub: Outcome of Board Meeting under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (‘Listing Regulations’) 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Listing Regulations, we wish to inform you that the Board 
of Directors of Quality Power Electrical Equipments Limited, at its meeting held today, Saturday, 31st October 
2026, inter alia, considered and approved the following: 
 
1. Approval of Unaudited Financial Results 
 
The Board approved the Unaudited Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results of the Company for the 
quarter and half year ended 30th September 2026, as reviewed and recommended by the Audit Committee, 
together with the Limited Review Reports issued by M/s. Kishor Gujar & Associates, Chartered Accountants, 
Statutory Auditors of the Company (Firm Registration No. 116747W). 
 
The Financial Results and Limited Review Reports are enclosed as Annexure A. 
 
2. Review of Business Performance and Order Book 
 
The Board reviewed the operational and financial performance of the Company for the half year ended 30th 
September 2026 and noted the growth achieved despite continued volatility in raw material prices. 
 
The Board noted with satisfaction that the Company's consolidated order book has surpassed ₹2,000 crore , 
driven by sustained demand for reactors, instrument transformers, Power Conversion Systems (PCS) and FACTS 
solutions. The Board also reviewed the enquiry pipeline, execution schedules and capacity readiness across the 
Group's manufacturing operations. 
 
The Board further acknowledged the combined efforts of the sales teams of Quality Power and Winwin Speciality 
Insulators Limited (WSIL) in securing an 800 kV HVDC porcelain insulator order, along with multiple orders for 
765 kV and 400 kV applications . These orders have not been included in the Company's reported consolidated 
order book, pending completion of the proposed acquisition. 
 
The Board also commended the technical and manufacturing teams of WSIL for successfully completing the 
applicable type tests in accordance with European and American standards for porcelain insulators up to 400 kV 
during the preceding two months. These developments strengthen the commercial and technical positioning of 
the proposed insulator business in domestic and international markets. 
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
3. Capital Expenditure and Manufacturing Expansion 
 
Global Coil Factory, Sangli  
The Board took note of the commencement of manufacturing operations at the Company's new Global Coil 
Factory in Sangli on 20th October 2026. 
The Board further approved an additional capital expenditure of ₹25 crore for establishing manufacturing 
capabilities for oil filled shunt reactors  at the Global Coil Factory. This investment will extend the Company's 
manufacturing portfolio and address additional opportunities in the high voltage transmission equipment 
market. 
 
HVDC CTC Magnet Wire Facility  
The Board reviewed the implementation status of the HVDC CTC Magnet Wire manufacturing facility, including 
machinery readiness and the remaining installation and commissioning activities. 
 
Endoks PCS Facility, Niğde, Turkey  
The Board noted the commencement of manufacturing operations on 16th October 2026 at the new Power 
Conversion Systems (PCS) facility of the Company's subsidiary, Endoks Energy Systems, in Niğde, Turkey. 
 
The Board congratulated the management and employees of the Group on the successful inauguration and 
commencement of production at both manufacturing facilities during October 2026. These investments expand 
the Group's production capabilities and support its long-term growth strategy. 
 
4. Proposed Acquisition of Winwin Speciality Insulators Limited 
 
The Board reviewed the status of the proposed acquisition of up to 100% of Winwin Speciality Insulators Limited 
for an aggregate consideration of up to ₹272.34 crore, comprising cash consideration of up to ₹123.84 crore and 
issuance of up to 10,17,123 equity shares at ₹1,460 per share on a preferential basis. 
 
The Board noted that the requisite shareholder approval was obtained through a Special Resolution passed at 
the Extraordinary General Meeting held on 19th October 2026. The acquisition remains subject to fulfilment of 
the conditions precedent under the Share Purchase Agreement and receipt of applicable regulatory approvals. 
 
The Board further noted that the listing of the equity shares proposed to be issued on a preferential basis is 
required to be completed prior to the opening of the Company's proposed Qualified Institutions Placement 
(QIP). The management will undertake the necessary regulatory and procedural formalities accordingly. 
 
The meeting of the Board of Directors commenced at 04:35 P.M. and concluded at 05.40 P.M. 
 
The above information is also being made available on the Company's website at www.qualitypower.com .
