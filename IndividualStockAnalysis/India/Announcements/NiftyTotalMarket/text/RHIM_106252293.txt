Dear Sir/Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing 
Regulations”), we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of RHI Magnesita India Limited (“the Company”), at its 
meeting held today, i.e., 28 May 2025, has, inter alia, approved an investment in Intermetal Engineers (India) Private Limited, 
a 100 % wholly owned subsidiary of the Company. 
The investment involves subscription up to 150 equity shares at an issue price of Rs. 1,66,475 per share, which includes a 
premium of Rs. 1,66,375 per share, by way of arights issue. The total consideration for said investment amounts is 
approximately Rs. 2.5 crore.
