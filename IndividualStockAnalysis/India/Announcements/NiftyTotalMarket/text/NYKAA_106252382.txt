Nykaa Receives NCLT Approval for Merger of Iluminar Media Limited (LBB ) 
into Nykaa Fashion Limited , both wholly owned subsidiaries of FSN E-
Commerce Ventures Limited  
 
Mumbai, May 28, 2025: FSN E -Commerce Ventures Limited ( “Nykaa” ) has received 
approval from the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai Bench, for the 
Merger of Iluminar Media Limited (LBB) into Nykaa Fashion Limited on May 27, 2025 , 
both wholly owned subsidiaries of FSN E -Commerce Ventures Limited.  
 
The merger proposal was announced in the Company’s Q4 FY 2024 earnings in May 2024 
and was subject to statutory and regulatory  approvals, which have now been completed.   
 
FSN E -Commerce Ventures Limited had acquired  100% stake LBB in August 2022. The 
lifestyle discovery and content platform LBB has a discerning and engaged user base, robust 
content creation expertise, and a strong curation mindset across all the lifestyle categories. Its content and consumer engagement capabilities complement Nykaa Fashion’s mission to be 
the destination for discovery, expression, and style.  
This integration will consolidate Nykaa’s  content creation and events business within a single 
entity, reinforcing the Group’s commitment to offering consumers highly relevant, inspiring, 
and engaging content -led experiences that deepen consumer connections across 
touchpoints. This will drive synergies in technology infrastructure, collaboration with brand 
partners as well as the content creation/marketing for all our businesses.
