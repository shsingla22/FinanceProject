Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
This is in continuation to our earlier intimations: 
i. Outcome of the meeting of the Board of Directors (“ Board”) of Lloyds Enterprises Limited (“ Company ”) 
held on Monday, 21st July, 2025, approving the offer and issuance of Equity Shares to the Existing 
Shareholders of the Company by way of a Rights Issue (“ Rights Issue ”) and  
ii. Outcome of meeting of the Rights Issue Committee held on Saturday, 09th August, 2025 intimating 
about Record Date, Issue Price, Rights Entitlements Ratio and other terms & conditions of the Rights 
Issue. 
 
In furtherance of the above, the Company had announced the Rights Issue of 25,44,25,324 Equity Shares 
of face value of ₹ 1 each at an issue price of ₹ 39 per Equity Share (including a premium of ₹ 38 per Equity 
Share), aggregating up to ₹ 992,25,87,636/- (Rupees Nine Hundred Ninety-Two Crores Twenty-Five Lakhs 
Eighty-Seven Thousand Six Hundred Thirty-Six), in accordance with the Letter of Offer dated Monday, 
11th August, 2025. 
The Rights Issue offer opened on Monday, 25th August 2025 and closed on Tuesday, 09th September, 2025. 
Pursuant to the finalisation of the basis of allotment of the Rights Issue, in consultation with BIGSHARE 
SERVICES PRIVATE LIMITED (“ Registrar to the Issue ”) and as approved by National Stock Exchange of India 
Limited (“ NSE”), being the Designated Stock Exchange  for the Issue, the Rights Issue Committee, at its 
meeting held today, i.e., on Wednesday, 10th September 2025, have inter-alia considered and approved 
the allotment of 25,44,25,324 partly paid-up Equity Shares of Face Value of Re. 1/- each on Rights Basis 
to the eligible shareholders and/or renouncee(s) in terms of the Letter of Offer dated Monday, 11th 
August, 2025 of which ₹ 19.50/- per Equity Share (including a premium of ₹ 19/- per Equity Share) has 
been paid on application (“ Allotment ”) and the balance amount shall be payable on not more than two 
Calls, both to be completed on or before March 31, 2027, and the terms and conditions, such as the timing 
and quantum, of each Call, as may be decided by the Rights issue Committee from time to time.
