(“Transferor Company”) and Coforge Limited (“Transferee Company” or the “Company”) have filed the 
certified copy of the NCLT Order with the Registrar of Companies, Haryana. With this filing, the Transferor

issued in accordance with the swap ratio set out in the Scheme.
