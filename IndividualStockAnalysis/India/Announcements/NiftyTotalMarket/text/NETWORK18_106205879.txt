Mumbai , 18th April , 202 5 – Network18 Media & Investments Limited today announced its 
results for the quarter  and year ended March 31, 2025.

audience reach  and product portfolio

driven by  the strength in regional markets

markets while News18 Kannada became a strong #2 channel; The network
