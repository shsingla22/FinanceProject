1. PURPOSE AND REGULATORY FRAMEWORK 
 
1.1 J. K. Cement Limited (the “Company”) is comm itted to maintaining the highest standards of 
integrity, transparency and fairness in its dealings in securities of the Company and in the 
handling and dissemination of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (“UPSI”). 
 
1.2 This Internal Code of Conduct to Regulate, Mo nitor and Report Trading by Designated Persons 
and Code of Practices and Procedures for Fair  Disclosure of Unpublished Price Sensitive 
Information (the “Code”) has been formulated by the Managing Director and approved by the 
Board of Directors of the Company in accordance with the requirements of the Securities and 
Exchange Board of India (Prohibition of Inside r Trading) Regulations, 2015, as amended from 
time to time (the “SEBI PIT Regulations”), and comprises (a) the code of conduct to regulate, 
monitor and report trading by Designated Person s and their Immediate Relatives, framed under 
Regulation 9 read with Schedule B and Regulation 9A of the SEBI PIT Regulations, and (b) the 
code of practices and procedures for fair disclo sure of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information, 
framed under Regulation 8 read with Schedule A of the SEBI PIT Regulations.  
1.3 The Code is intended to: 
 
(a) regulate, monitor and report trading in securiti es of the Company by Designated Persons and 
their Immediate Relatives; 
(b) prevent trading in securities of the Co mpany by persons in possession of UPSI; 
(c) establish principles and procedures for id entification, handling, communication and 
protection of UPSI; 
(d) prescribe appropriate controls for preven tion of misuse or leakage of UPSI; 
(e) establish procedures for pre-clearance, tradin g window closure, disclosures and monitoring 
of trades, wherever applicable; 
(f) provide a framework for maintaining the Structured Digital Database and other records 
required under the SEBI PIT Regulations; 
(g) establish a framework for inquiry into actual or suspected leakage of UPSI; and 
(h) ensure fair, timely, adequate and uniform disclo sure of UPSI in accord ance with applicable 
law. 
 
1.4 This Code shall be read together with the SEBI PIT Regulations and other applicable laws, 
regulations, circulars, notifications, FAQs, directio ns and clarifications issued by the Securities 
and Exchange Board of India (“SEBI”) and the recognised stock exchanges from time to time.

5. HANDLING, COMMUNICATION AN D PROTECTION OF UPSI 
 5.1 All UPSI shall be handled on a need-to-know basis. No UPSI shall be communicated, provided or 
accessed except in furtherance of a Legitimate Pu rpose, performance of duties or discharge of 
legal obligations.  
5.2 In accordance with Regulation 3 of the SEBI PIT Regulations, no Insider shall communicate, provide 
or allow access to UPSI to any person, including other Insiders, and no person shall procure UPSI 
from, or cause its communication by, any Insider, ex cept as stated in Clau se 5.1 and Clause 5.3. 
 
5.3 UPSI may be communicated, provided, allowed to be accessed or procured in connection with a 
transaction that would:

(a) entail an obligation to make an open offer under the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and 
Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, where the Board of the Company is of the informed opinion that 
sharing such information is in the best interests of the Company; or 
 
(b) not attract an obligation to make an open offe r under the aforesaid Regulations, where the Board 
of the Company is of the informed opinion that shar ing of such information is in the best interests 
of the Company and the information constituting  UPSI is disseminated to be made generally 
available at least two Trading Days prior to the proposed transaction being effected, in such form 
as the Board may determine to be adequate and fair to cover all relevant and material facts. 
 In either case, the Board shall require the parties to execute agreements to contract confidentiality 
and non-disclosure obligations. Such parties shall ke ep the information receiv ed confidential, except 
for the limited purpose for which it was provided, an d shall not otherwise trade in securities of the 
Company while in possession of UPSI.  5.4 The Company’s policy for determination of  Legitimate Purposes is as follows: 
 
(a) The sharing of UPSI by an Insider in the ordinary course of business with the categories of persons 
mentioned in Clause 3.11 shall be a Legitimate Pu rpose, provided it is on a need-to-know basis 
and is not intended to evade or circumvent the SEBI PIT Regulations.  
(b) The following are examples of Legitimate Purp oses, each subject to the conditions in (a): 
(i) giving information to a court, tribunal, or  governmental or regulatory authority; 
(ii) sharing financial information with the statutor y auditors for limited reviews, audit reports 
and certificates; 
(iii) sharing with legal advisors, merchant bankers, lenders, secretarial auditors, practising 
professionals, the registrar and share transfer  agent, depositories, custodians, IT system 
providers and other advisors or service providers the Company engages; 
(iv) due diligence for a transaction in the ordinary course of business; 
(v) performance of a contract or arrangement by which the Company is bound; and 
(vi) a statutory consolidation or similar reportin g requirement of the Promoter or another 
group entity, to the extent necessary.  
(c) The person sharing UPSI shall satisfy himself or herself in good faith that the purpose is legitimate 
and, in case of doubt, shall consult th e Compliance Officer before sharing.  
 
(d) Any person receiving UPSI for a Legitimate Purpose shall be an Insider and shall be given due

(e) A confidentiality or non-disclosure agreement shall be executed with, or a notice served on, every

5.5 Every person in possession of UPSI shall keep it confidential, use it only for the purpose for which 
it was shared, and shall not trade in co ntravention of the SEBI PIT Regulations.

7. PROHIBITION ON TRADING WHILE IN POSSESSION OF UPSI 
 
7.1 No Insider shall trade in securities of the Compan y, directly or indirectly, while in possession of 
UPSI. This includes trading in the name of an Immediate Relative, through a nominee or any other person, or through any entity.  
The prohibition applies at all times, whether or not the Trading Window is open, whether or not 
the Insider is a Designated Person, and even  where pre-clearance has been obtained. 
 
The prohibition depends on possession of UPSI, not on whether the Insider actually used it. Under the SEBI PIT Regulations, an Insider who trades while in possession of UPSI is presumed to have 
been influenced by it. The limited circumstances in which the SEBI PIT Regulations recognise such 
trading as permissible are set out in Regulation 4( 1), and the principal ones are listed in Clause 
9.7. 
  
7.2 No Insider shall recommend, advise or encourage any other person to trade in securities of the 
Company while the Insider is in possession of UPSI , whether or not the UPSI itself is disclosed. 
Communication of UPSI is govern ed separately by Clause 5.2. 
 7.3 As an additional safeguard under this Code, no Designated Person shall enter into any derivative 
transaction, such as futures or options, in the securities of the Company at any time. 
 
8. TRADING PLANS  
 8.1 An Insider may formulate a Trading Plan setting out the trades to be carried out on his or her 
behalf in future and submit it to the Compliance Officer for approval and public disclosure. Every 
Trading Plan shall comply with Regulation 5 of  the SEBI PIT Regulations and this Clause 8. 
 
8.2 A Trading Plan shall: 
9 of 24  
  
(a) not allow any trade to commence earlier than  one hundred and twenty (120) calendar days 
after the plan is publicly disclosed; 
(b) not overlap with the period of any other Trading Plan of the Insider that is already in existence; 
(c) specify for each trade the value of the trade or the number of securities to be traded, whether 
it is a purchase or a sale, and either the date or a period of not more than five (5) consecutive Trading Days on which it is to be executed; and 
(d) not involve trading for the purpose of market abuse. 
 A Trading Plan may also, at the In sider’s option, specify a price limi t for each trade: an upper limit 
for a purchase or a lower limit for a sale. The limit shall lie between the closing price of the securities on the day before the plan is submitted and a price twenty per cent above (for a 
purchase) or twenty per cent below (for a sale) that closing price, rounded off to the nearest 
numeral. 
 8.3 The Compliance Officer shall review each proposed  Trading Plan to assess whether it could lead 
to a violation of the SEBI PIT Regulations, and may ask the Insider for any undertaking or 
information needed for the purpose. The Complia nce Officer shall approve or reject the plan 
within two Trading Days of receiving it and, if a pproved, shall notify it to the stock exchanges on 
which the securities of the Company are listed on the day of approval.  
8.4 An approved Trading Plan is irrevocable. The Insi der shall implement it in full and shall not trade 
in securities of the Company outside the plan, except in the cases of permanent incapacity, 
bankruptcy or operation of law recognised by the SEBI PIT Regulations. The death of the Insider shall be treated as operation of law for this purpose, and the Trading Plan lapses on the date of death.

 9.1 The Compliance Officer shall administer the Tradin g Window. Except as provided in Clause 9.7,

9.3 The Compliance Officer shall also close the Tradi ng Window whenever he or she determines that

from within the Company, but no person sh all trade while in possession of such UPSI.

10. PRE-CLEARANCE OF TRADES 
 10.1 When the Trading Window is open, a Designated Person shall obtain pre-clearance from the

 (a) a trade executed under an approved Trading Plan; or

11. CONTRA-TRADE RESTRICTION 
 
11.1 A Designated Person and his or her Immediate Re latives who buy or sell securities of the 
Company shall not enter into an opposite transa ction (a sale after a purchase, or a purchase 
after a sale) in securities of the Company with in six months of the ea rlier transaction. The 
restriction applies to the Designa ted Person and his or her Immediate Relatives collectively, to 
any number of securities, and to every trade wh ether or not it required pre-clearance. A gift 
or transfer is treated as a trade for this purpose.  
11.2 The Compliance Officer is empowered to grant relaxation from this restriction, before the 
trade is executed, for reasons recorded in writi ng, provided the relaxation does not violate the 
SEBI PIT Regulations. A relaxation for the Co mpliance Officer, or for his or her Immediate 
Relatives, shall be granted by the Board, acting through the Managing Director as authorised under Clause 10.6.  
11.3 If a contra trade is executed in violation of this  restriction, whether inadvertently or otherwise, 
the profits from it shall be liable to be disgorged and remitted to SEBI for credit to the Investor 
Protection and Education Fund administered by SEBI under the Act. 
 
11.4 The restriction does not apply to transmission of  securities. It does apply to trades under an 
approved Trading Plan, including trades under two different Trading Plans.
