4.  Rationale for  
amalgamation / merger  The proposed amalgamation of Sproutlife with ITC will,  
inter alia, have the following benefits:  
(a) Enable  seamless integration of business operations of both the 
companies, thereby unlocking the full potential of the combined 
business operations and maximi sing value creation 
opportunities through reali sation of synergies;  
(b) Achieve optimal deployment of financial, managerial and 
operational resources across the combined business operations 
by accessing the shared talent pool and common resources;  
(c) Enhanc e efficiency of capital allocation by consolidating and 
optimi sing the cash flows and financial resources of both the 
companies;  and 
(d) Result in reduction of the number of operating entities to 
simplify the corporate holding structure.
