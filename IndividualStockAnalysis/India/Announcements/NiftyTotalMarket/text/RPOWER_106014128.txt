CORRIGENDUM TO THE NOTICE OF POSTAL BALLOT DATED  SEPTEMBER  23, 2024  
 
This is with reference to the Notice of Postal Ballot dated September 23, 2024 issued to the

A.M. (IST) on Tuesday, September 24, 2024 to 5.00 P.M. (IST) on Wednesday, October 23, 2024.
