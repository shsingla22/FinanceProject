Upon allotment of the Warrants, the Warrant Holder shall pay INR 38.95/- (Indian 
Rupees Thirty Eight and Ninety Five Paisa Only) per Warrant, being at least 25% of 
the issue price, as the warrant subscription price (“Warrant Subscription Price ”). The 
balance amount of INR 116.85/- (Indian Rupees One Hundred Sixteen and Eighty 
Five Paisa Only) per Warrant (“Warrant Exercise Price ”) shall be payable at the time 
of exercise of the Warrants.  
 
In the event the Warrants are not exercised within the stipulated period of 18 
(eighteen) months, the Warrants shall lapse and the Warrant Subscription Price  paid 
shall stand forfeited in accordance with applicable law.

Section 62(1)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013, read with applicable rules thereto, as

Requirements) Regulations, 2018,  as amended (“SEBI ICDR Regulations ”), the SEBI 
Listing Regulations and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations, as amended 
from time to time, and shall be subject  to the approval of the members of the Company 
and such other regulatory / statutory authorities as may be required, including from 
Registrar of Companies, Ahmedabad, BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of 
India Limited.
