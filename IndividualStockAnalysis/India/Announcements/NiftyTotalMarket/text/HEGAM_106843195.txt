Profile of Shri Hiren Pravin Shah 
Shri Hiren Pravin Shah is a visionary entrepreneur and energy industry leader with over 25 years of 
experience in energy storage, lithium-ion battery technologies, renewable energy and e-mobility. As 
the founder of Replus Engitech, he is driving innovation in Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and 
electric mobility, with a vision to accelerate the global transition towards clean energy. 
Before establishing Replus, he spent 17 years with Panasonic, where he led key business initiatives 
and spearheaded the deployment of lithium-ion batteries for Reliance Jio's 4G network. His 
contributions earned him the Panasonic Corporation President's Rising Star Award. He subsequently 
served as Head of Energy Storage Solutions at Delta Power Solutions, driving product innovation, 
business growth and India-centric engineering capabilities. 
An Industrial Electronics graduate specialising in Power Electronics from Pune University, Mr. Shah 
brings strong technical expertise and entrepreneurial leadership to the evolving energy landscape.

company profile.

first fully solar-powered village, and containerised BESS installations at Capgemini campuses in Noida 
and Airoli.
