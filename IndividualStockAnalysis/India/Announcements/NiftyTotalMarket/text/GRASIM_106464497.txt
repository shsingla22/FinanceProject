This is to inform that the Board of Directors of Grasim Industries Limited (“ the Company ”) has, 
inter alia , approved the  proposal for  infusion of fund in Aditya Birla Renewables Limited 
(“ABRe n”), a wholly owned subsidiary  of the  Company , by Essel Mining & Industries Limited 
(“EMIL ”), by way of a preferential issue on private placement basis , amounting to Rs.  500 
crore ( Rupees Five Hundred crore) , vide equity shares  within the current financial year  2026  
for meeting urgent business needs of ABRen . EMIL  is a part of the Aditya Birla Group (ABG) 
with business interests in mining and renewable energy.  
 
Pursuant to this, ABRen  will cease to be a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company but  
will continue to be a Subsidiary of the Company . 
 
The Board meeting commenced at 6:00 p.m. (IST) and concluded at 6:56 p.m. (IST).
