Fortis acquires 110-bedded Suguna Hospital, Rajajinagar, Bengaluru for  
approx. INR 277 Crs; facility provides further potential to scale up to 150 beds  
Acquisition comprises business operations along with hospital land and building;  
Consolidates Fortis’ position in the micromarket post the People Tree Hospital 
acquisition in January this year  
Deal augments Company’s cluster-focused growth strategy in Bengaluru with an 
operational bed capacity of ~1000 beds at present 
Gurugram, October 28, 2026:  Fortis Healthcare Limited (“FHL” or “Fortis” or the “Company”), 
amongst India’s leading healthcare delivery companies, today announced its acquisition of 
Suguna Hospital in Bengaluru from inter alia  Suguna Ramaiah Hospitals Private Limited by 
way of a slump sale. The said acquisition has been undertaken by FHL’s wholly-owned 
subsidiary International Hospital Limited (IHL) with the transaction being consummated today.  
As part of the transaction, IHL would acquire the entire business operations of Suguna 
Hospital, along with the underlying hospital land and building. The transaction, structured as 
an all-cash deal is in line with the Company’s cluster-focused inorganic growth strategy and 
further bolsters Fortis’ network in the city of Bengaluru.  
Commissioned in 2006, Suguna Hospital is a 110-bedded facility located on Dr Rajkumar 
Road, Rajajinagar in North-West Bengaluru and has a potential to expand to ~150 beds. The 
hospital is strategically located in an affluent and densely populated catchment with strong 
connectivity. The acquisition positions Fortis as amongst the leading healthcare providers in 
the North-West Bengaluru corridor with approx. 500 potential beds across its existing facility 
in Rajajinagar, the People Tree facility in Yeshwanthpur and the Suguna Hospital.  
The facility recorded revenues of approximately INR 50 Crores in FY26. The above acquisition 
and the planned investments for upgrading medical infrastructure, expanding clinical 
programs and adding bed capacity in Suguna Hospitals provide an opportunity for Fortis to 
leverage its existing brand presence and enhance the facility’s performance in future.  
Dr Ashutosh Raghuvanshi, MD & CEO of the Company, said , “This acquisition expands 
our presence in the city and specifically in the North West Bengaluru corridor which we believe 
is an underserved catchment. With Suguna hospitals in our portfolio, we now have ~1000 
operational beds in the city including our O&M facilities; reinforcing our strategy of expanding 
and deepening our presence in our focused clusters. We foresee significant opportunities to 
leverage our existing network and drawing from scale and synergy efficiencies to optimize 
operations, while further strengthening our clinical capabilities and brand presence” 
Dr. R. Ravindra and Ms Sujatha Ravindra, Promoters of Suguna Hospital , said, “Over the 
years, Suguna Hospital has grown with a clear focus on delivering accessible and quality 
healthcare. The agreement with Fortis Healthcare represents an important step in the 
hospital’s next phase of growth. Fortis brings significant clinical expertise, operational 
capabilities and resources that will enable the hospital to expand its offerings and strengthen 
the care it provides to patients. We are confident that the transition will create new
