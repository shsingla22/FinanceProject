Pursuant to our disclosures dated February 3, 2026 and April 7, 2026, we would like to inform you that  
the Board of Directors of Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited (“ ABHFL ”), a material subsidiary of the 
Company, in its meeting held today i.e., April 17, 2026, approved the allotment of 12,32,52,061 (Twelve 
Crore Thirty Two Lakh Fifty Two Thousand and Sixty One)  equity shares of ABHFL (“ Subscription 
Shares ”), each of face value of INR 10 (Indian Rupees Ten) at a price of INR 223.12 (Indian Rupees 
Two Hund red and Twenty Three Point One Two Only) per equity share to Indriya Limited (“ Investor ”), 
one of the entities of Advent International, L.P. by way of preferential issue on a private placement basis 
(“Preferential Issue ”).  
 
Pursuant to the abovementioned allotment:  
 
(i) the Investor now holds 14.286% of the paid -up equity share capital of ABHFL on a fully diluted 
basis.  
(ii) ABHFL has ceased to be a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, and the Company now holds 
85.505% of the paid -up equity share capital of ABHFL on a fully diluted basis.
