Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
The Company, vide its intimation dated March 30, 2026 informed the exchanges about 
sanctioning of the Scheme of Arrangement between Penna Cement Industries Limited 
(“Transferor Company ”) and Ambuja Cements Limited (“ Transferee Company ” or “Company ”) and 
their respective shareholders (“ Scheme ”) by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, 
Ahmedabad Bench vide its order dated March 30, 2026 (“ NCLT Order ”). 
 
Further to our aforesaid letter, we wish to inform you that the Transferor Company and the 
Company have filed the certified copy of the NCLT Order with the Registrar of Companies, 
Ahmedabad, Gujarat today, i.e. on April 10, 2026. With this filing, the Company and the 
Transferor Company have now complied with all the conditions specified in Clause 3.3 of the 
Scheme. 
 
Accordingly, the Scheme has become effective today, i.e. April 10, 2026 (“ Effective Date ”) and 
consequently, Transferor Company stands amalgamated with the Company and dissolved 
without being wound up. In accordance with the Scheme, the Appointed Date of the Scheme is 
August 16, 2024. 
 
Further, in accordance with the Scheme and pursuant to its effectiveness, the equity 
shareholders of the Transferor Company whose name appears in the Register of Members of the 
Transferor Company as on the Effective Date i.e. April 10, 2026 will be paid cash consideration 
as per the Clause 2.3.2 of the Scheme. 
 
This disclosure is being made in terms of Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board 
of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
