Outcome of Board Meeting  
Capital Raising Plan for the FY 2024 -25 
 
In terms of Regulation 30 and other applicable regulations of SEBI (LODR) Regulations 
2015, we hereby inform that t he Board of Directors of the Bank in its meeting held on 
April 22, 2024 at Chennai  has inter alia  approved the following:    
a) To raise  Paid -up equity capital  to a maximum extent of Rs.5000 crore to the 
public, by way of Follow -on Public Offer / Rights Issue /Qualified Institutional 
Placement  / ESPS/  Preferential  Issue  or any other mode or combination thereof , 
in one or more tranches  within a period of 12 months from the date of approval , 
after obtaining the necessary approval of the Government of India and the 
approval of shareholders through Annual General Meeting (AGM)/ Extraordinary 
General Meeting (EGM) . 
 
b) To raise Tier II capital by issue of BASEL III Compliant Tier II Bonds up  to a maximum 
extent of Rs.1000 crore  depending upon the requirement, with or without green 
shoe option, in one or more tranche s within a period of 12 months from the date 
of approval . 
 
The Meeting of the Board of Directors concluded at 5.00 p.m.
