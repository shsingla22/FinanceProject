2 Exceptional items: 
(i) The Group has availed customs duty exemptions on imported capital goods under the Export Promotion Capital Goods (EPCG) scheme, which are subject to the fulfillment of specified export 
obligations . During the quarter ended 30 September 2026, the Group evaluated the likelihood of meeting these export obligations and estimated a shortfall. Consequently, a provision of Rs. 
1,159 lakhs has been recognized towards the estimated customs duty liability and applicable interest thereon. 
(ii) During the previous year ended 31 March 2026, the management committed to a plan to divest the Company's entire equity interest in BACC Healthcare Private Limited ("BACC") , a wholly 
owned subsidiary engaged in the management of infertility treatment hospitals. Accordingly , the assets and liabilities of BACC were classified as a disposal group held for sale as at 31 March 
2026. Along with the classification as held for sale, the carrying amount of goodwill allocated to the cash-generating unit (i.e., BACC) was assessed for impairment in accordance with Ind AS 36 
and an impairment loss of Rs. 3,191 lakhs was recognised as an exceptional item during the previous year ended 3 I March 2026 based on the fair value of the consideration receivable, net of 
related expenses. 
During the preceding quarter ended 30 June 2026, based on the recommendation of the Audit Committee, and pursuant to the approval of the Board of Directors at its meeting held on 19 May 
2026, the Company completed the divestment of its entire equity interest in BACC to lnviga Healthcare Fund I and its nominee, a related party, for a consideration of Rs. 3,764 lakhs on 29 June 
2026 and recognised resulting gain of Rs. 296 lakhs in Other income. Of the said consideration, Rs. 2,823 lakhs has been received upfront and the balance Rs. 941 lakhs is receivable within 18 
months from the date of sale. Consequent to completion of the transaction, BACC ceased to be a subsidiary of the Company with effect from 30 June 2026. 
(iii) During the previous year ended 31 March 2026, pursuant to the notification of new Labour Codes by the Government oflndia on 21 November 2025 resulting in change in wage definition , 
the incrementa l impact on the employee benefit obligations aggregating Rs. 1,267 lakhs was recognised as an exceptional item, consistent with the guidance provided by the Institute of Chartered 
Accountants oflndia.
