The Board of Directors (“Board”) of Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited  (“Company ”) at its meeting held on August 
8, 2026 , based on  the recommendation of Nomination and Remuneration Committee approved adoption of the 
‘Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited Employee Stock Option Scheme 2026  (“ABFRL ESOP Scheme 2026 ”) for the 
benefit of eligible employees of the Company, including subsidiary  Company(ies) , in compliance with the Securities 
and Exchange Board of India (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021 (“SEBI SBEB & SE 
Regulations”) and the Companies Act, 2013 (“Act”), which entitles them to the equity shares of the Company, subject to the approval of the Members of the Company by way of a Special Resolution being sought via this Notice .  
The Board has nominated the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (the “Committee”/ “NRC”) to be designated 
as the Compensation Committee for the implementation, administration and superintendence of the ABFRL ESOP 
Scheme 2026  in accordance with the SEBI SBEB & SE Regulations. Approval of the members is being sought for grant 
of employee stock options (“Options”) and/or performance stock units (“PSUs”) (collectively “Stock Options”) to the 
eligible employees as may be determined by the NRC. The ABFRL ESOP S cheme 2026  shall be implemented and 
administered by the NRC.  
The Company had earlier implemented the Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited Employee Stock Option and Performance Stock Unit Scheme 2025 (“ABFRL ESOP Scheme 2025”), which was approved by the shareholders of the 
Company at the Annual General Meeting of the Company on September 23, 2025. The ABFRL ESOP Scheme 2026  is 
proposed to be adopted, subject to the approval of the Members of the Company by way of a Special Resolution being sought via this Notice , on the same key terms as the ABFRL ESOP  Scheme 2025  and the Company has not proposed 
any key changes to the terms of the ABFRL ESOP Scheme 2026 , except for the change in maximum number of Stock 
Options to be granted under the ABFRL ESOP Scheme 2026  and the maximum vesting period for Stock Options to be 
granted under the ABFRL ESOP Scheme 2026 , which is proposed to be increased to 5  (five) years from 4  (four)  years,  
as was provided for under the AB FRL ESOP  Scheme 2025.  
 In accordance with Regulation 6 of the SEBI SBEB & SE Regulations, approval of Members by way of Special Resolution 
is required to be passed, for the adoption of the ABFRL ESOP Scheme 2026  and extending the benefits of the ABFRL 
ESOP Scheme 2026  to eligible employees of the subsidiary  company(ies). 
 
Accordingly, separate Resolutions are being passed for the above.
