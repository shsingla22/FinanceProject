PFC Consulting Limited (a wholly owned subsidiary of Power Finance Corporation Limited) has
been nominated as the 'Bid Process Coordinator' (BPC) for selection of developer through tariff
based competitive bidding for the Independent Transmission Projects (ITPs) by Ministry of Power,
Gol.
As per the provisions oftariffbased competitive bidding Guidelines issued by the Ministry of Power,
the BPC has to prepare the project profile and initiate the process of land acquisition and forest
clearance, if required. For these activities, One Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) is required to be
incorporated which will take up various preparatory activities of the transmission project e.g. survey
and preparation of report, initialization of the process of acquisition of land and also initiate the
process of seeking forest clearance, if required. 
The SPV has to be transferred to the Successful Bidder selected through International Competitive
Bidding Process under Tariff Based Competitive Bidding Guidelines. 
Jammu and Kashmir Power Transmission Corporation Limited (JKPTCL) vide Letter of Award
(LoA) dated July 23, 2024, had appointed PFC Consulting Limited (PFCCL) as the Bid Process
Coordinator (BPC) for selection of the bidder as the Transmission Service Provider (TSP) through
the Tariff-Based Competitive Bidding (TBCB) route for the transmission scheme, "Execution of
System Strengthening/up-gradation Works and Intra-State Transmission System Projects."
Accordingly, PFCCL incorporated a SPV namely "WAHIPORA AND SALLAR TRANSMISSION
