each exchangeable into one equity share of face value of Re. 1/ - each, (“Warrants”)  at an issue price 
(“Warrant Issue Price ”) of Rs. 316/ - (Rupees Three Hundred Sixteen only) [including the warrant

preferential basis to 28 allottees (“ Warr ant Holders ”), as per the list forming part of the said letter.  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), this is to inform you that upon receipt of an amount ag gregating to 
Rs. 56,88,000/ - (Rupees Fifty -Six Lakhs Eighty -Eight Thousand only)  at the rate of Rs. 237/ - (Rupees 
Two Hundred Thirty -Seven Only) per warrant, being 75% of the Warrant Issue Price (“ Warrant 
Exercise Price ”), and application f orm from 2 (Two ) warrant holder, as per the terms of issue of 
Warrants, the Share Issue & Allotment Committee of the Board of Directors of the Company , at its 
meeting held today i.e. on 10 November 2025,  has considered and approved the allotment of  24,000 
(Twenty Four  Thousand)  Equity Shares of face value of Re. 1/ - each, upon conversion of equal number 
of Warrants, as per the details given in ‘Annexure -I’. 
 
Consequently, the issued and paid up capital of the Company stands increased to Rs. 50,45,18, 950/- 
consisting of  50,45,18, 950 equity shares of Re. 1/ - each.
