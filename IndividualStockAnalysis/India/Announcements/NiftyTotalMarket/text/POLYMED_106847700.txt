POLICY ON MATERIAL SUBSIDIARIES  
[Pursuant to Regulation 16(1)(c) and Regulation 24 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015]  
1. PREAMBLE:  
 
The Board of Directors of Poly Medicure Limited (“Company”) in compliance with the 
provisions of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), updated the policy for 
determining ‘material’ subsidiaries (“Policy”) vide its Board meeting held on November 05, 
2026.  
 
2. OBJECTIVE:  
 
The primary object of this Policy is to lay down the criteria for identification of Material 
Subsidiary(ies) (as defined below) and disclosure and governance thereof as required under the 
SEBI Listing Regulations, as amended from time to time.  
 
3. EFFECTIVE FROM:  
 
The Policy shall come into force with effect from the date the SEBI Listing Regulations is 
effective with respect to the Company.  
 
4. DEFINITIONS:  
 
“Act” means the Companies Act, 2013 & rules made thereunder, including any amendments or 
modifications thereof.  
 
“Insolvency Code”  means Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, as amended;  
 
“Holding company” , means a holding company as defined under Section 2(46) of the Act.  
 
“Subsidiary”  shall mean a subsidiary as defined under Section 2(87) of the Companies Act, 
2013. 
 
“Unlisted Subsidiary”  means a Subsidiary of the Company whose shares are not listed on any 
stock exchange.  
 
“Material Subsidiary” means a material subsidiary of the Company as defined under 
Regulation 16(1)(c) of the SEBI Listing Regulations, or as may be amended from time to time.  
 
 
 
4 | Pa ge 
 
“Significant Transaction or Arrangement”  shall mean any individual transaction or 
arrangement that exceeds or is likely to exceed 10% of the total revenues or total expenses or 
total assets or total liabilities, as the case may be, of the unlisted subsidiary for the immediately 
preceding accounting year.  
 
“Net Worth”  means net worth as defined under section 2(57) of the Companies Act, 2013.  
 
“Turnover”  means turnover as defined under section 2(91) of the Companies Act, 2013.  
 
“Board of Directors”  or “Board” means the “Board of Directors” of Company, as constituted 
from time to time.  
 
“Audit Committee”  means Audit Committee constituted by the Board of Directors of the 
Company, from time to time, under Section 177 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with Regulation 
18 of the SEBI Listing Regulations.  
 
“Independent Director”  or “ID” means an Independent Director as defined under the Act and 
the SEBI Listing Regulations from time to time.  
 
(Note: Any other term not defined herein shall have the same meaning as defined in the 
Companies Act, 2013, the SEBI Listing Regulations, Securities Contract (Regulation) Act, 1956 
or any other applicable regulations or Act.)  
 
5. IDENTIFICATION OF MATERIAL SUBSIDIARIES  
 
A Subsidiary of the Company shall be consider as Material Subsidiary, if it’s turnover or net 
worth exceeds 10% of the consolidated turnover or net worth respectively of the listed entity & 
its subsidiaries in the immediately preceding accounting year of the Company, as defined under 
Regulation 16(1)(c) of the SEBI Listing Regulations.  
 
The Audit Committee shall review such details / information as may be required to determine 
the ‘Material Subsidiary(ies)’ from time to time.  
 
6. GOVERNANCE OF MATERIAL SUBSIDIARIES  
 
In terms of Regulation 24 of SEBI Listing Regulations, the Company shall make the following 
compliances in connection with the Material Subsidiary:  
 
 
 
 
 
 
5 | Pa ge 
 
a) Appointment of Independent Director  
 
 At least one Independent Director on the Board of the Company shall be appointed as a 
director on the board of unlisted Material Subsidiary(ies), whether incorporated in India or 
not.   
 
Notwithstanding anything contrary contained in this Policy, for the purpose of this Clause, 
the term “Material Subsidiary” shall mean a subsidiary, whose turnover or net worth exceeds 
20% of the consolidated turnover or net worth respectively, of the Company and its 
Subsidiaries in the immediately preceding accounting year.  
 
b) Matter to be reviewed by the Audit Committee  
 
 The audit committee of the listed entity shall also review the financial statements, particular 
the investments made by the unlisted subsidiary.  
 
 Reviewing the utilization of loans and/ or advances from/investment by the holding 
company in the subsidiary exceeding rupees 100 crore or 10% of the asset size of the 
subsidiary, whichever is lower including existing loans / advances / investments.  
 
c) Matter to be reviewed by the Board  
 
 The minutes of the meetings of the board of directors of the Unlisted Subsidiary shall be 
placed at the meeting of the Board of Directors of the listed entity.  
 
 The management of the unlisted subsidiary shall periodically bring to the notice of the board 
of directors of the listed entity, a statement of all “significant transactions and 
arrangements”  entered into by the unlisted subsidiary.  
 
d) Restriction on Disposal of shares of Material Subsidiary by the company  
 
 The company shall not dispose of shares in its material subsidiary resulting in reduction of 
its shareholding (either on its own or together with other subsidiaries) to less than or equal 
to fifty percent or cease the exercise of control over the subsidiary without passing a 
special resolution in its General Meeting except in cases where such divestment is made 
under a scheme of arrangement duly approved by a Court/Tribunal or under a resolution 
plan duly approved under section 31 of the Insolvency Code and such an event is disclosed 
to the recognized stock exchanges within one day of the resolution plan being approved.  
 
 
 
6 | Pa ge 
 
 
e) Restriction on Disposal of Assets of Material Subsidiary  
 
 Selling, disposing and leasing of assets amounting to more than twenty percent of the assets 
of the material subsidiary on an aggregate basis during a financial year shall require prior 
approval of shareholders by way of special resolution, unless the sale/disposal/lease is made 
under a scheme of arrangement duly approved by a Court/Tribunal or under a resolution 
plan duly approved under section 31 of the Insolvency Code and such an event is disclosed 
to the recognized stock exchanges within one day of the resolution plan being approved.  
 
7. SECRETARIAL AUDIT  
 
 Every Material Unlisted Subsidiary of the Company incorporated in India shall undertake 
secretarial audit and shall annex a secretarial audit report, given by a company secretary in 
practice, in such form as may be specified with the annual report of the listed entity.  
 
8. DISCLOSURE  
 
 The Company shall disclose all events or information with respect to its Subsidiaries which 
are material for the Company, in accordance with Regulation 30(9) of the SEBI Listing 
Regulations.  
 
 The Policy shall be disclosed on the Company’s website and a web link thereto shall be 
provided in the annual report of the Company.  
 
9. COMPLIANCE RESPONSIBILITY  
 
 Company Secretary shall be responsible to ensure the compliance with this Policy. It shall 
have the power to ask for any information or clarification from the management in this 
regard. 
 
10. SCOPE AND LIMITATION  
 
 In the event of any conflict between the provisions of this Policy and the Act or SEBI Listing 
Regulations or any other statutory enactments, modification or rules, the provisions of SEBI 
Listing Regulations /Act or statutory modification, enactments, rules shall prevail over this 
Policy and the part(s) so repugnant shall be deemed to be severed from the Policy and the 
rest of the Policy shall remain in force.

 The Board may review this Policy as and when it deems necessary or with the amendments 
in the Listing Regulation or Companies Act, 2013 or with the enactment of any applicable 
law. The Board may also establish further rules and procedures, from time to time, to give 
effect to the intent of this Policy.

1. PREAMBLE  
Poly Medicure Limited ("Company") is committed to maintaining high standards of corporate 
governance, transparency and timely disclosure of material information to its stakeholders.  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended from time to time ("SEBI LODR 
Regulations"), the Company is required to formulate a policy for determination of materiality of 
events and information requiring disclosure to the Stock Exchange(s). Accordingly, the Board of 
Directors has adopted this Policy for Determination of Materiality of Events or Information 
("Policy") to establish a framework for identification, assessment and timely disclosure of material 
events and information in compliance with applicable laws.  
This Policy provides guidance to the directors, Key Managerial Personnel and employees of the 
Company for reporting potentially material events or information to the persons authorized under 
Regulation 30(5) of the SEBI LODR Regulations for determination of materiality and making 
appropriate disclosures to the Stock Exchange(s). Disclosures made pursuant to this Policy shall be 
hosted on the website of the Company and preserved in accordance with applicable laws and the 
Company's Archival Policy.  
2. OBJECTIVE  
The objective of this Policy is to:  
a) establish a framework for identification and determination of material events and information in 
accordance with the requirements of Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR Regulations;  
b) ensure timely, adequate, accurate, explicit, consistent and transparent disclosure of material 
events and information to the Stock Exchange(s);  
c) provide guidance to directors, Key Managerial Personnel and employees of the Company for 
reporting and escalation of events or information that may be material in nature;  
d) facilitate compliance with the disclosure obligations prescribed under the SEBI LODR 
Regulations and other applicable laws and regulations;  
e) promote uniformity, transparency and fairness in dissemination of information to shareholders, 
investors, regulators and other stakeholders;  
f) enable the authorised Key Managerial Personnel to effectively determine the materiality of 
events or information and ensure timely disclosures to the Stock Exchange(s); and  
g) provide for hosting and preservation of disclosures on the Company's website in accordance with 
applicable regulatory requirements and the Company's Archival Policy.  
 
3. Adoption and Effective Date  
This policy was originally approved by the Board of Directors on January 6, 2016  and became 
effective from December 1, 2015 . The Policy was subsequently amended with effect from August 
7, 2023. In view of the recent regulatory changes and the ongoing review of the Company’s

will be effective from November 5, 2026 .

5. DISCLOSURE OF MATERIAL EVENTS AND INFORMATION  
5.1 Events Deemed Material  
The Company shall disclose all events specified under Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI 
Listing Regulations, as amended from time to time, without applying any test of materiality.  
5.2 Events Requiring Materiality Assessment  
The Company shall disclose events specified under Para B of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI 
Listing Regulations upon determination of materiality in accordance with this Policy.  
5.3 Other Material Events and Information  
      The Company shall also disclose:  
a) Any major development likely to materially affect the business, operations, financial position, 
performance or reputation of the Company;  
b) Events or information necessary to enable investors to appraise the Company's position and 
avoid the creation of a false market in its securities;  
c) Material events or information relating to subsidiaries that are material to the Company;  
d) Events or information not specifically prescribed under Schedule III but determined to be 
material by the Authorized Persons or the Board;  
 
6 
 
e) Communications received from any regulatory, statutory, enforcement or judicial authority 
requiring disclosure, unless disclosure is prohibited by such authority; and  
f) Such other events or information as may be specified by SEBI or any other regulatory authority 
from time to time.  
5.4 Continuous Disclosure  
The Company shall disclose all material developments relating to an event or information previously 
disclosed until such event is resolved or closed, with appropriate updates and explanations.  
6. DETERMINATION OF MATERIALITY  
Materiality shall be determined based on both qualitative and quantitative criteria.  
6.1 Qualitative Criteria  
An event or information shall be considered material if:  
a) Its omission is likely to result in discontinuity, alteration or misrepresentation of information 
already available in the public domain;  
b) Its omission is likely to result in significant market reaction if such omission subsequently 
becomes known; or  
c) Considering its nature, impact, strategic significance or circumstances, it is otherwise considered 
material. 
6.2 Quantitative Criteria  
An event or information shall ordinarily be considered material if its value or expected impact 
exceeds the lower of the following thresholds:  
a) Two percent (2%) of turnover as per the last audited consolidated financial statements;  
b) Two percent (2%) of net worth as per the last audited consolidated financial statements, except 
where net worth is negative; or  
c) Five percent (5%) of the average of the absolute value of profit or loss after tax as per the last 
three audited consolidated financial statements.  
For the purpose of calculating the above threshold, the average profit or loss shall be computed by 
disregarding the positive or negative sign.  
6.3 Residual Materiality  
Where the above criteria are not directly applicable, materiality shall be determined by the Authorized 
Persons or the Board based on the facts and circumstances of the particular case.  
7. OCCURRENCE OF EVENTS OR INFORMATION  
For determining disclosure timelines, an event or information shall be considered to have occurred:  
 
7 
 
7.1 Events Requiring Approval  
Upon receipt of final approval from the Board, shareholders or any other authority, as applicable.  
However, where an event is price sensitive in nature, disclosure may be made upon approval of the 
Board, even if subsequent approvals are pending.  
7.2 Events Arising Otherwise  
When the Company becomes aware of the event or information or when an officer of the Company, 
in the ordinary course of duties, ought reasonably to have become aware of it.  
For this purpose, the term "officer" shall have the meaning assigned under the Companies Act, 2013 
and shall include promoters of the Company.  
8. TIMELINES FOR DISCLOSURE  
The Company shall disclose material events and information within the timelines prescribed under 
the SEBI Listing Regulations.  
Unless otherwise specified, disclosures shall be made as follows:  
a) Within 30 minutes from the closure of the Board Meeting where a decision has been taken;  
b) Within 12 hours from the occurrence of an event or information emanating from within the 
Company;  
c) Within 24 hours from the occurrence of an event or information emanating from outside the 
Company.  
d) Where disclosure is made beyond the prescribed timelines, the Company shall provide an 
explanation for such delay.  
 
9. VERIFICATION OF MARKET RUMOURS  
Where applicable under Regulation 30(11) of the SEBI Listing Regulations, the Company shall 
confirm, deny or clarify any reported event or information appearing in the mainstream media which 
indicates circulation of rumours relating to a specific material event or information.  
Such clarification shall be provided as soon as reasonably possible and not later than twenty-four 
(24) hours from the reporting of the event or information.  
The Company shall also comply with its Code of Practices and Procedures for Fair Disclosure under 
the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.  
10. AUTHORIZED PERSONS  
The following Key Managerial Personnel shall be authorized to determine materiality of events and 
information and to make disclosures to Stock Exchanges ("Authorized Persons"):  
 
8 
 
a) Managing Director;  
b) Whole-time Director;  
c) Chief Financial Officer; and  
d) Company Secretary & Compliance Officer.  
The Authorized Persons may act jointly or severally unless otherwise determined by the Board.  
 
11. RESPONSIBILITIES OF AUTHORIZED PERSONS  
The Authorized Persons shall be responsible for:  
a) Determining the materiality of events and information;  
b) Reviewing and approving disclosures to Stock Exchanges;  
c) Determining the timing and content of disclosures;  
d) Monitoring and disclosing material developments on a continuing basis;  
e) Seeking internal or external professional advice where considered necessary;  
f) Formulating operational procedures for implementation of this Policy; and  
g) Ensuring compliance with applicable laws, regulations and regulatory guidance.  
 
12. DISCLOSURE PROCESS  
12.1 Reporting of Events  
All employees and functional heads shall promptly report potentially material events or information 
to the Authorized Persons.  
12.2 Stock Exchange Disclosure  
The Company shall first disclose material events and information to the Stock Exchanges through 
the prescribed electronic platform(s).  
12.3 Website Disclosure  
All material disclosures made to Stock Exchanges shall also be hosted on the Company's website 
within the timelines prescribed under applicable regulations and shall remain available for the period 
prescribed under law.  
12.4 Regulatory Queries  
The Authorized Persons shall respond promptly to any clarification sought by Stock Exchanges or 
other regulatory authorities concerning disclosures made under this Policy.  
13. ROLES OF FUNCTIONAL HEADS  
Heads of departments and business functions shall:  
 
9 
 
a) Monitor events and information relating to their respective functions;  
b) Promptly notify the Authorized Persons of any event or information that may require disclosure;  
c) Provide complete and accurate information necessary for disclosure; and  
d) Extend all assistance required for implementation of this Policy.  
 
14. WEBSITE DISCLOSURE AND DISSEMINATION  
This Policy shall be hosted on the website of the Company and shall be made available to all relevant 
stakeholders.  
The contact details of the Authorized Persons shall also be disclosed to the Stock Exchanges and 
placed on the Company's website in accordance with applicable regulations.  
15. INTERPRETATION  
Any questions concerning interpretation of this Policy shall be determined by the Board.  
In the event of any inconsistency between this Policy and the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, 
SEBI Listing Regulations or any other applicable law, the provisions of such law shall prevail.  
16. REVIEW AND AMENDMENT  
The Board of Directors may amend, modify or revise this Policy from time to time in accordance 
with applicable laws and regulatory requirements. The Company Secretary & Compliance Officer 
may make necessary changes arising from statutory or regulatory amendments, subject to approval 
or noting by the Board, wherever required.  
Any subsequent amendment, clarification, circular, notification, guideline or direction issued by any 
regulatory or statutory authority and applicable to this Policy shall be deemed to form part of this 
Policy and shall apply automatically, even if not specifically incorporated herein.

POLICY ON RELATED PARTY TRANSACTION POLICY  
[Pursuant to Regulation 23 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 and Section 188 of the Companies Act, 2013]  
1. PREAMBLE  
In pursuance of Regulation 23 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing 
Regulations”) and Section 188 and 177 of the Companies Act, 2013 and other 
applicable provision (including any statutory enactment / amendments thereof),  
The Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Poly Medicure Limited (the “Company” or 
“POLYMED”), has adopted the following policy and procedures with regard to 
Related Party Transactions as defined below. The Board of Directors will review and 
may amend this policy from time to time on recommendation of Audit Committee. 
Related Party Policy is to regulate the transaction(s) between the Company and its 
Related Parties based on the applicable laws and regulations applicable on the 
Company.  
2. PURPOSE  
In pursuance of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 requires a company to formulate a policy on materiality of related 
party transactions and on dealing with related party transactions including clear 
threshold limits duly approved by the board of directors and such policy shall be 
reviewed by the board of directors at least once every three years and updated 
accordingly. In line with the requirements of Listing Regulations and provisions of 
the Companies Act, 2013, the Board of Directors has formulated this policy for 
identification and proper conduct and approval, reporting of related party 
transactions.  
3. DEFINITIONS  
“Act” means the Companies Act, 2013 including any statutory modifications or re-
enactments thereof for the time being in force.  
“Audit Committee”  means the committee of Board of Directors constituted in 
pursuance of Section 177 of the Act and Regulation 18 of Listing Regulations.  
“Board” means the Board of Directors of the Company.  
“Key Managerial Personnel”  means the person(s) appointed as such in pursuance of 
Section 203 read with Section 2(51) of the Act.  
 
4 
 
“Company Secretary”  means Company Secretary of the Company appointed under 
the Act. 
 “SEBI Listing Regulations”  or “Listing Regulation”  means Securities and Exchange 
Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
including any statutory modifications or reenactments thereof for the time being in 
force. 
“Material Related Party Transaction”  means a transaction with a related party shall 
be considered material, if the transaction(s) to be entered into individually or taken 
together with previous transactions during a financial year, exceeds the thresholds 
specified in Schedule XII of the Listing regulations as per the last audited financial 
statements of the Company, whichever is lower threshold as may be prescribed under 
the provisions of the Act and Listing Regulations from time to time.  
Notwithstanding the above, a transaction involving payments made to a related party 
with respect to brand usage or royalty shall be considered material if the transaction 
to be entered into, either individually or taken together with previous transactions 
during a financial year, exceeds 5% or such higher/lower percentage of the Annual 
Consolidated Turnover of the Company as per the last audited financial statements of 
the Company as may be prescribed under the provisions of the Act and Listing 
Regulations from time to time.  
“SCHEDULE XII OF LISTING REGULATION: RELATED PARTY 
TRANSACTIONS REGULATION 23(1)”

 “Related Party”  means an entity which is a related party under Section 2(76) of the

“related party”, with reference to a company, means—  
(i) a director or his relative;  
(ii) key managerial personnel or his relative;  
(iii) a firm, in which a director, manager or his relative is a partner;  
(iv) a private company in which a director or manager or his relative  is a member or 
director;  
(v) a public company in which a director or manager is a director and  holds along with 
his relatives, more than two per cent of its paid-up share capital;  
(vi) any body corporate whose Board of Directors, managing director or manager is

director or manager;  
(vii) any person on whose advice, directions or instructions a director or manager is 
accustomed to act:  
Provided that nothing in sub-clauses ( vi) and (vii) shall apply to the advice, directions 
or instructions given in a professional capacity;  
(viii) anybody corporate which is—  
(A) a holding, subsidiary or an  associate company of such company;  
(B) a subsidiary of a holding company to which it is also a subsidiary; or  
(C) an investing company or the venturer of a company;  
‘‘Relative’’ means a relative as defined under Section 2(77) of the Companies Act read 
with SEBI Listing Regulations.  
“Related Party Transaction”  shall mean a transaction, as defined under the provisions 
of Section 188 of the Act and Regulation 2(1) (zc) SEBI Listing Regulations, means 
involving a transfer of resources, services or obligations between:  
(i) the Company or any of its subsidiaries on one hand and a related party of 
the Company or any of its subsidiaries on the other hand; or

related party of the Company or any of its subsidiaries;

party shall be construed to include a single transaction or a group of 
transactions in a contract, and includes transactions as defined as a “related 
party transaction” under the relevant provisions of the Companies Act, the 
Listing Regulations or any other related law, regulation, standard, each as

Provided that the following shall not be a related party transaction:  
 
(a)  The issue of specified securities on a preferential basis, subject to compliance

(b) The following corporate actions by the Company which are uniformly

ii. subdivision or consolidation of securities;  
iii. issuance of securities by way of a rights issue or a bonus issue; and

(c) retail purchases from any company or its subsidiary by the directors or key

employees, directors, key managerial personnel and relatives of directors or 
key managerial personnel.

related party transaction having variance of 20% of the existing limit as sanctioned by

4. IDENTIFICATION OF RELATED PARTIES  
 
On the basis of the disclosures made by Directors and Key Managerial Personnel 
and considering the applicable provisions under the Relevant Laws, the Company 
shall maintain an updated list of Related Parties.  
 
5. APPROVAL PROCESS OF RELATED PARTY TRANSACTION  
 
1. Audit Committee  
 
i. All Related Party Transactions except the exempted transactions under the Act 
and/or the SEBI Listing Regulations and subsequent material modification 
thereto shall require prior approval of the Audit Committee in accordance with 
the provisions of the Act and the Listing Regulations for the time being in force.  
 
ii. Further only those members of the Audit Committee, who are independent 
directors, shall approve related party transactions. Any member of the Audit 
Committee who has a potential interest in any RPT will abstain from discussion 
and voting on the approval of such RPT.  
 
iii. Subject to the provisions of the applicable laws, the Audit Committee will have 
the discretion to approve /modify /recommend/ refer the proposed Related 
Party Transaction for the approval of the Board or shareholders.  
 
iv. In the event such a transaction, contract or arrangement is not in the ordinary 
course of business or is not at arm's length, the Company shall comply with the 
applicable laws including provisions of the Companies Act and the Rules 
 
8 
 
framed thereunder and obtain approval of the Audit Committee or the Board 
or its shareholders, as applicable, for such contract or arrangement.  
 
v. The remuneration and sitting fees paid by the Company or its subsidiary(ies) 
to its directors, key managerial personnel or senior management, except who is 
a promoter or part of the promoter group, shall not require approval of the 
audit committee provided that the same is not material in terms of the 
definition of “ Material Related Party Transaction”.  
 
2. Transaction involving Subsidiaries  
 
A related party transaction above rupees one crore, whether entered into 
individually or taken together with previous transactions during a financial 
year, to which the subsidiary of a Company is a party but the Company is not 
a party, shall require prior approval of the audit committee of the Company if 
the value of such transaction, exceeds the lower of the following:  
 
(i) ten percent of the annual standalone turnover of the subsidiary as 
per the last audited financial statements of the subsidiary; or  
 
(ii) the threshold for material related party transactions of Company as 
specified in Schedule XII of the Listing regulations.  
 
In the event of a related party transaction above rupees one crore, whether 
entered into individually or taken together with previous transactions during 
a financial year, to which the subsidiary of a Company is a party but the 
Company is not a party and such subsidiary does not have audited financial 
statements for a period of at least one year, prior approval of the audit 
committee of the Company shall be obtained if the value of such transaction 
exceeds the lower of the following:  
 
(i) ten percent of the aggregate value of paid-up share capital and securities 
premium account of the subsidiary; or  
 
(ii) the threshold for material related party transactions of Company as 
specified in Schedule XII of the Listing Regulation:  
 
9 
 
Provided that the aggregate value of paid-up share capital and securities 
premium account of the subsidiary shall be taken as on a date, not older than 
three months prior to the date of seeking approval of the audit committee.  
Further in case of prior approval of the audit committee of the Company shall 
not be required for a related party transaction to which the listed subsidiary of 
company is a party but the Company is not a party, if regulation 23 and sub-
regulation (2) of regulation 15 of the SEBI Listing Regulation are applicable to 
such listed subsidiary.  
The remuneration and sitting fees paid by the Company or its Subsidiary to its 
Director, Key Managerial Personnel or Senior Management, except who is part 
of promoter or promoter group, shall not require approval of the Audit 
Committee provided that the same is not material in terms of the provisions of 
SEBI Regulations or the Act.  
6. RATIFICATION  
The members of the audit committee, who are independent directors, may 
ratify related party transactions within three months from the date of the 
transaction or in the immediate next meeting of the audit committee, whichever 
is earlier, subject to the following conditions:  
(i) the value of the ratified transaction(s) with a related party, whether entered 
into individually or taken together, during a financial year shall not exceed 
rupees one crore;  
 
(ii) the transaction is not material in terms of the provisions of sub-regulation 
(1) of the regulation 23 of SEBI Listing Regulation;  
 
(iii) rationale for inability to seek prior approval for the transaction shall be 
placed before the audit committee at the time of seeking ratification;  
 
(iv) the details of ratification shall be disclosed along with the disclosures of 
related party transactions in terms of the provisions of sub-regulation (9) of 
this regulation;  
 
(v) any other condition as specified by the audit committee:  
Provided that failure to seek ratification of the audit committee shall render the 
transaction voidable at the option of the audit committee and if the transaction 
 
10 
 
is with a related party to any director, or is authorised by any other director, 
the director(s) concerned shall indemnify the company against any loss 
incurred by it.  
7. OMNIBUS APPROVAL  
Audit committee may grant omnibus approval for related party transactions 
proposed to be entered into by the Company or its subsidiary subject to the 
following conditions, namely-  
(a)the audit committee shall lay down the criteria for granting the omnibus 
approval in line with the policy on related party transactions and such approval 
shall be applicable in respect of transactions which are repetitive in nature;  
(b)the audit committee shall satisfy itself regarding the need for such omnibus 
approval and that such approval is in the interest of the Company;  
(c)the omnibus approval shall specify:  
(i) the name(s) of the related party, nature of transaction, period of transaction, 
maximum amount of transactions that shall be entered into,  
(ii) the indicative base price / current contracted price and the formula for 
variation in the price if any; and  
(iii) such other conditions as the audit committee may deem fit:  
Provided that where the need for related party transaction cannot be foreseen 
and aforesaid details are not available, audit committee may grant omnibus 
approval for such transactions subject to their value not exceeding rupees one 
crore per transaction.  
(d) the audit committee shall review, atleast on a quarterly basis, the details of 
related party transactions entered into by the Company of the Company or its 
subsidiary pursuant to each of the omnibus approvals given.  
(e) Such omnibus approvals shall be valid for a period not exceeding one year 
and shall require fresh approvals after the expiry of one year:  
(f) Any other conditions as the Audit Committee may deem fit.  
 
8. BOARD OF DIRECTORS  
 
The following Related Party Transaction(s) shall be approved by the Board by 
passing a resolution in this regard at a meeting of the Board of Directors:  
 
11 
 
i. Transactions in which any of the Directors or the KMPs of the Company are

ii. Transactions specified in Section 188(1) of the Act which are not in the ordinary

iii. Material Related Party Transactions which are proposed to be placed before the

iv. Related Party Transactions where Audit Committee of the Company is of the opinion

v. Related Party Transactions for which approval of the Board of Directors is

vii. Any other transaction as may be prescribed by the Shareholders from time to

All material related party transactions and subsequent material modifications 
as defined by the audit committee, shall require prior approval of the

transaction or not.  
Further Transactions specified in Section 188(1) of the Act which:  
a) are not in the ordinary course of business and/or not on arm’s length terms;  
and 
b) exceed the threshold specified in Rule 15(3) of Companies (Meetings of Board

Provided that  prior approval of the shareholders of a Company shall not be

regulation 15 of these regulations are applicable to such listed subsidiary.

Provided further that the requirements specified under this sub-regulation

exchanges within one day of the resolution plan being approved;  
Provided further that the omnibus approval granted by the shareholders for

till the date of the next annual general meeting held within the timelines

Provided further that in case of omnibus approvals for material related party

1. Transactions entered into between a holding company and its wholly owned

2. Transactions entered into between two wholly-owned subsidiaries of the 
company, whose accounts are consolidated with company and placed before

3. Transactions which are in the nature of payment of statutory dues, statutory 
fees or statutory charges entered into between an entity on one hand and the 
Central Government or any State Government or any combination thereof on 
the other hand.  
  
4. Any other transaction for which approval of the Audit Committee or Board or 
Shareholders, is not required or exempted under the Act, SEBI Listing 
Regulations or any other applicable law.

members for their approval of the respective Related Party Transaction.

POLICY FOR DETERMINATION OF LEGITIMATE PURPOSES  
I. INTRODUCTION  
This Policy for Determination of Legitimate Purposes ("Policy") is framed in accordance with 
Regulation 3(2A) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Prohibition of Insider Trading) 
Regulations, 2015, as amended from time to time ("SEBI PIT Regulations").  
II. OBJECTIVE  
The objective of this Policy is to identify legitimate purposes for sharing Unpublished Price 
Sensitive Information ("UPSI") in the ordinary course of business, for performance of duties, 
discharge of legal or regulatory obligations, or for genuine business purposes, in compliance with 
the SEBI PIT Regulations.  
The determination of whether sharing of UPSI constitutes a legitimate purpose shall be assessed 
based on the facts and circumstances of each case, including whether:  
 such sharing is in the ordinary course of business;  
 such sharing is in furtherance of a genuine business, commercial, legal, regulatory, or 
statutory purpose;  
 the sharing is not intended to evade or circumvent the provisions of the SEBI PIT 
Regulations;  
 the information is required for performance of duties, discharge of obligations, or execution 
of legitimate business transactions; and  
 the nature and extent of information shared is commensurate with the purpose for which 
access is provided.

b. is a common commercial practice or  
 
c.  meets any other parameters/criteria as decided by the Board/ Audit Committee.  
 
 
b) “UPSI” stands for Unpublished Price Sensitive Information as defined under Regulation 2(1)(n) 
of the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.

with

1. INTRODUCTION  
The Securities and Exchange Board of India ("SEBI") has notified the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, as amended from time to time ("PIT Regulations"), to establish 
a regulatory framework for preventing insider trading and safeguarding the integrity of the securities market. The 
PIT Regulations prohibit communication, procurement and misuse of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information 
("UPSI") and regulate trading in securities by persons who may have access to such information.  
Pursuant to Regulation 9 of the PIT Regulations, the Board of Directors of Poly Medicure Limited ("Company"), 
in its meeting held on November 05, 2026, has adopted this Code of Conduct to Regulate, Monitor and Report 
Trading by Designated Persons ("Code"). The Code sets out the framework for dealing in the securities of the 
Company and for handling UPSI in a lawful, ethical and transparent manner.  
This Code has been formulated in compliance with the PIT Regulations and incorporates the minimum standards 
prescribed under Schedule B thereto. The Code shall be read in conjunction with the PIT Regulations and other 
applicable laws, rules and regulations in force from time to time. In the event of any inconsistency between the 
provisions of this Code and any applicable law, the provisions of the applicable law shall prevail.  
This Code shall come into effect from November 05, 2026 and shall be applicable as amended from time to time 
with the approval of the Board of Directors and/or such authority as may be authorized under applicable law.  
2. OBJECTIVE  
The objective of this Code is to regulate, monitor and report trading in the securities of the Company by Designated 
Persons and other persons covered under this Code, and to ensure compliance with the provisions of the PIT 
Regulations.  
Specifically, this Code aims to:  
a) prevent insider trading and the misuse or unlawful communication of UPSI;  
b) establish a framework for preserving the confidentiality of UPSI and ensuring its legitimate use only for lawful 
purposes;  
c) provide guidance to Designated Persons and their Immediate Relatives regarding their obligations, 
responsibilities and restrictions under the PIT Regulations;  
d) prescribe procedures relating to trading in the Company's securities, including pre-clearance requirements, 
trading window restrictions, disclosures and reporting obligations;  
e) promote ethical conduct, transparency, fairness and investor confidence in dealings involving the securities of 
the Company; and  
f) define the roles and responsibilities of Designated Persons, Insiders, Connected Persons and the Compliance 
Officer in ensuring effective implementation of and compliance with the PIT Regulations.  
3. APPLICABILITY  
This Code shall apply to:  
a) all Designated Persons of the Company;  
b) Immediate Relatives of Designated Persons;  
c) Insiders and Connected Persons, as defined under the PIT Regulations;  
d) Directors, Key managerial personnel, employees, and such other persons who may be designated by the Company 
from time to time for the purpose of compliance with the PIT Regulations; and  
 
 
 
e) any other person who is in possession of, has access to, or is deemed to be in possession of Unpublished Price 
Sensitive Information relating to the Company or its securities.  
All persons covered under this Code shall comply with the provisions contained herein and with the requirements 
of the PIT Regulations, as amended from time to time. The obligations under this Code shall be in addition to, and 
not in derogation of, any obligations imposed under applicable laws.  
This Code shall further extend to trading in the securities of the Company undertaken directly or indirectly by such 
persons, including trades executed by their Immediate Relatives and persons for whom trading decisions are taken 
by or on behalf of such persons.

m) promptly inform the Stock Exchange(s) and other regulatory authorities, wherever required under applicable 
law, regarding any violation of the PIT Regulations or this Code;  
n) establish, maintain, update and monitor the Structured Digital Database ("SDD") in accordance with the PIT 
Regulations, containing details of persons with whom UPSI is shared or who have access to UPSI for legitimate 
purposes;  
o) ensure that the Structured Digital Database contains details of the nature of UPSI, the persons sharing such 
information, the persons receiving such information and their Permanent Account Number or any other identifier 
authorized by law, where PAN is not available;  
p) ensure that the Structured Digital Database is maintained with adequate internal controls, audit trails and time-
stamping mechanisms to preserve accuracy, completeness and non-tampering of records;  
q) ensure that information received from external sources and required to be recorded in the Structured Digital 
Database is entered within the timelines prescribed under applicable laws and regulations;  
r) undertake such actions as may be necessary for freezing or restricting trading by Designated Persons, including 
freezing of Permanent Account Numbers (PAN), in accordance with applicable SEBI regulations, circulars and 
system-driven disclosure mechanisms; and  
s) perform such other duties and responsibilities as may be prescribed under the PIT Regulations, this Code or 
assigned by the Board from time to time.  
5.3 Powers of the Compliance Officer  
For the purpose of discharging duties under this Code, the Compliance Officer shall have the authority to:  
a) access all records, information, documents, data and communications relating to the Company and its Securities, 
as may be necessary for ensuring compliance with the PIT Regulations and this Code;  
b) seek information, records, confirmations, declarations and explanations from any Insider, Designated Person, 
Employee, director, consultant, advisor or other person covered under this Code; and  
c) require full co-operation from all persons covered under this Code in connection with any compliance review, 
monitoring activity, inquiry or investigation undertaken for the purposes of this Code.  
5.4 Reporting  
The Compliance Officer shall report to the Board and shall provide periodic reports on compliance with the PIT 
Regulations and this Code to the Chairman of the Audit Committee or such other authority as may be prescribed 
by the Board. Such reporting shall be made at such intervals as may be determined by the Board, but not less than 
once in a financial year.  
6. PRESERVATION OF UNPUBLISHED PRICE SENSITIVE INFORMATION (UPSI) AND 
RESTRICTIONS ON COMMUNICATION, PROCUREMENT AND TRADING  
6.1 Preservation of UPSI  
All UPSI shall be handled on a "Need-to-Know"  basis. UPSI shall be disclosed only to those persons within the 
Company who require such information for the discharge of their duties and whose possession of such information 
will not give rise to a conflict of interest or likelihood of misuse.  
 
 
 
 
 
To prevent the misuse of confidential information, the Company shall maintain a "Chinese Wall"  mechanism 
separating departments or functional areas that routinely have access to UPSI ("Inside Areas") from departments 
engaged in sales, marketing, investor relations, business development or other functions not requiring access to 
UPSI ("Public Areas").  
Employees in Inside Areas shall not communicate or provide access to UPSI to persons in Public Areas except on  
a Need-to-Know basis and in furtherance of legitimate purposes, performance of duties or discharge of legal 
obligations. The identification of Inside Areas and Public Areas shall be determined by the Compliance Officer in 
consultation with the management of the Company.  
In exceptional circumstances, any Designated Person may be permitted to "cross the wall" and share UPSI on a 
Need-to-Know basis for legitimate purposes, performance of duties or discharge of legal obligations, subject to 
prior intimation to or approval of the Compliance Officer, as may be applicable.  
All files, records and documents containing UPSI shall be maintained in a secure manner with appropriate physical 
and electronic safeguards. Electronic records shall be protected through suitable access controls, passwords, 
encryption and other security measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, modification or misuse.  
6.2 Restriction on Communication and Procurement of UPSI  
No Insider shall communicate, provide, share or allow access to any UPSI relating to the Company or its Securities 
to any person, including another Insider, except where such communication is in furtherance of legitimate purposes, 
performance of duties or discharge of legal obligations.  
No person shall procure or cause the communication of UPSI from any Insider relating to the Company or its 
Securities except in furtherance of legitimate purposes, performance of duties or discharge of legal obligations.  
Any person receiving UPSI pursuant to a legitimate purpose shall be considered an Insider for the purposes of the 
PIT Regulations and this Code and shall be required to maintain the confidentiality of such UPSI and comply with 
all applicable restrictions relating to trading and handling of UPSI.  
6.3 Sharing of UPSI in Connection with Transactions  
Notwithstanding anything contained in this Code, UPSI may be communicated, provided, allowed access to or 
procured in connection with a transaction that:  
a) entails an obligation to make an open offer under the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) 
Regulations, 2011, where the Board is of the informed opinion that such sharing is in the best interests of the 
Company; or  
b) does not attract an open offer obligation under the Takeover Regulations, but where the Board is of the informed 
opinion that the proposed transaction is in the best interests of the Company and the information constituting UPSI 
is disseminated and made Generally Available Information at least two Trading Days prior to the proposed 
transaction being effected, in such manner as the Board may determine to be adequate and fair for disclosure of all 
relevant and material facts.  
For the above purposes, the Board shall ensure that the concerned parties execute appropriate confidentiality and 
non-disclosure agreements and shall require such parties to maintain strict confidentiality of the information 
received and not trade in the Securities of the Company while in possession of UPSI.  
 
 
 
 
 
6.5 Structured Digital Database  
The Company shall maintain a Structured Digital Database ("SDD") containing:  
a) the nature of UPSI shared;  
b) the names of persons who have shared the UPSI;  
c) the names of persons with whom the UPSI has been shared; and  
d) the Permanent Account Number (PAN) or any other identifier authorized by law of such persons, where PAN is 
not available.  
The Structured Digital Database shall be maintained internally and shall not be outsourced. Adequate internal 
controls, audit trails, time-stamping mechanisms and other checks shall be maintained to ensure integrity, 
completeness and non-tampering of records.  
The Structured Digital Database shall be preserved for a period of not less than eight years  after completion of the 
relevant transaction. Where any investigation or enforcement proceeding is initiated by SEBI or any other 
regulatory authority, the relevant information shall be preserved until completion of such proceeding.  
Entries in the Structured Digital Database shall be made contemporaneously with the sharing, communication or 
receipt of UPSI or as soon as practicable thereafter in accordance with the PIT Regulations and SEBI requirements.  
6.6 Restriction on Trading While in Possession of UPSI  
No Insider shall trade in the Securities of the Company while in possession of UPSI, except as permitted under the 
PIT Regulations and this Code.  
Any person who has traded in the Securities of the Company while in possession of UPSI shall be presumed to 
have been motivated by the knowledge and awareness of such UPSI unless such person establishes otherwise in 
accordance with the PIT Regulations.  
Without prejudice to the foregoing, an Insider may demonstrate innocence where the trade is undertaken:  
a) pursuant to an off-market inter-se transfer between Insiders possessing the same UPSI and undertaken without 
breach of this Code;  
b) through a block deal mechanism between persons possessing the same UPSI and undertaken without breach of 
this Code;  
c) pursuant to a statutory or regulatory obligation;  
d) pursuant to the exercise of stock options where the exercise price was pre-determined in accordance with 
applicable regulations;  
e) by a non-individual Insider that has established appropriate arrangements, controls and segregation of decision-
making functions such that the persons making trading decisions were not in possession of UPSI; or  
f) pursuant to an approved Trading Plan in accordance with the PIT Regulations.  
6.7 Confidentiality Obligations  
All Insiders, Designated Persons, Connected Persons and persons receiving UPSI for a legitimate purpose shall 
preserve the confidentiality of UPSI and shall not:  
a) trade in the Securities of the Company on the basis of UPSI;  
b) communicate, share, discuss or disclose UPSI except as permitted under this Code;  
 
 
 
 
c) recommend or induce any person to trade in the Securities of the Company based on UPSI; or  
d) misuse UPSI for personal gain or for the benefit of any other person.  
In the case of Connected Persons, the burden of establishing that they were not in possession of UPSI at the time 
of trading shall lie upon such Connected Persons.  
7. PREVENTION OF MISUSE OF UNPUBLISHED PRICE SENSITIVE INFORMATION  
7.1 Trading Plan  
7.1.1 Formulation of Trading Plan  
An Insider shall be entitled to formulate a Trading Plan and present it to the Compliance Officer for approval and 
public disclosure, pursuant to which Trades may be carried out on his/her behalf in accordance with such Trading 
Plan. 
The Trading Plan provides an option to an Insider who may be perpetually in possession of Unpublished Price 
Sensitive Information ("UPSI"), enabling such Insider to trade in the Securities of the Company in a compliant 
manner. 
The intimation regarding formulation of a Trading Plan shall be submitted to the Compliance Officer in the 
prescribed format. Upon approval of the Trading Plan, the Compliance Officer shall notify the same to the Stock 
Exchange(s) where the Securities of the Company are listed.  
7.1.2 Conditions Applicable to Trading Plan  
A Trading Plan shall:  
a) not entail commencement of Trading on behalf of the Insider earlier than one hundred and twenty (120) calendar 
days from the public disclosure of the Trading Plan;  
b) not entail overlap of any period for which another Trading Plan is already in existence;  
c) not entail Trading in Securities for market abuse;  
d) specify for each proposed Trade:  
i. either the value of Trades to be effected or the number of Securities to be traded;  
ii. nature of the Trade;  
iii. either specific date or a time period not exceeding five consecutive Trading Days; and  
iv. price limit, being:  
• an upper price limit in case of a buy Trade; and  
• a lower price limit in case of a sell Trade.  
For a buy Trade, the upper price limit shall be between the closing price on the day prior to submission of the 
Trading Plan and up to twenty percent (20%) higher than such closing price.  
For a sell Trade, the lower price limit shall be between the closing price on the day prior to submission of the 
Trading Plan and up to twenty percent (20%) lower than such closing price.  
Where a price limit has been specified, the Trade shall be executed only if the execution price falls within such 
limit. If the market price falls outside such limit, the Trade shall not be executed.  
 
 
 
Explanation:  
i. The parameters relating to value/quantity, nature of Trade and specific date/time period shall be mandatory.  
ii. Specification of a price limit shall be optional.  
iii. The price limit shall be rounded off to the nearest numeral.  
7.1.3 Adjustment for Corporate Actions  
An Insider may, with the approval of the Compliance Officer, make adjustments in the number of Securities and/or 
price limits specified under the Trading Plan in the event of corporate actions including bonus issue, stock split or 
similar corporate actions occurring after approval of the Trading Plan.  
Any such adjustment shall be notified to the Stock Exchange(s).  
7.1.4 Review and Approval of Trading Plan  
The Compliance Officer shall review the Trading Plan to assess whether the Trading Plan has any potential for 
violation of the PIT Regulations or this Code.  
For this purpose, the Compliance Officer shall be entitled to seek such declarations, confirmations and undertakings 
as may be considered necessary.  
The Compliance Officer shall approve or reject the Trading Plan within two (2) Trading Days of receipt of the 
Trading Plan and shall notify the approved Trading Plan to the Stock Exchange(s) on the date of approval.  
7.1.5 Implementation and Irrevocability  
An approved Trading Plan shall be irrevocable and shall be mandatorily implemented by the Insider.  
The Insider shall not:  
a) execute any Trade outside the scope of the approved Trading Plan; or  
b) deviate from the approved Trading Plan,  
except due to permanent incapacity, bankruptcy or operation of law.  
Provided that implementation of the Trading Plan shall not commence if any UPSI in possession of the Insider at 
the time of formulation of the Trading Plan has not become Generally Available Information at the time of 
commencement of implementation.  
In such cases, the Compliance Officer shall confirm that commencement of the Trading Plan shall stand deferred 
until such UPSI becomes Generally Available Information.  
7.1.6 Non-Implementation of Trading Plan  
In case of non-implementation, whether fully or partially, of a Trading Plan due to:  
a) reasons permitted under applicable law;  
b) permanent incapacity;  
c) bankruptcy;  
d) operation of law; or  
e) failure of execution due to inadequate liquidity in the scrip,  
 
 
 
 
 
the Insider shall intimate the Compliance Officer within two (2) Trading Days from expiry of the tenure of the 
Trading Plan, together with reasons and supporting documents.  
Upon receipt of such information, the Compliance Officer shall place the same along with his/her recommendations 
before the Audit Committee at its immediate next meeting.  
The Audit Committee shall determine whether the non-implementation was bona fide.  
The decision of the Audit Committee shall be communicated to the Stock Exchange(s) on the same day.  
Where the Audit Committee does not accept the submissions made by the Insider, action shall be initiated in 
accordance with this Code.  
7.1.7 Exemption for Approved Trading Plans  
Pre-clearance requirements, Trading Window restrictions and Contra Trade restrictions shall not apply to Trades 
executed pursuant to an approved Trading Plan.  
7.2 Trading Window  
7.2.1 General Restriction  
Designated Persons and their Immediate Relatives shall execute Trades in the Securities of the Company only 
during a valid Trading Window.  
No Designated Person shall execute any Trade in his/her own name or in the name of any Immediate Relative during 
the period when the Trading Window is closed.  
The responsibility for communicating Trading Window restrictions to Immediate Relatives shall rest with the 
concerned Designated Person.  
7.2.2 Closure of Trading Window  
The Trading Window shall be closed for, inter alia:  
a) declaration of quarterly and annual financial results (standalone and consolidated);  
b) declaration of interim or final dividend;  
c) issue of Securities, rights issue, bonus issue, buy-back, fundraising and changes in capital structure;  
d) change in Key Managerial Personnel;  
e) mergers, demergers, amalgamations, acquisitions, delisting, disposals, restructuring, expansion of business and 
similar transactions;  
f) material events under the SEBI Listing Regulations; and  
g) any other matter which, in the opinion of the Compliance Officer, constitutes UPSI.  
7.2.3 Additional Closure by Compliance Officer  
The Trading Window may also be closed where the Compliance Officer determines that a Designated Person or 
class of Designated Persons can reasonably be expected to be in possession of UPSI.  
For UPSI not emanating from within the Company, the Compliance Officer may determine that closure of the 
Trading Window is not required.  
 
 
 
 
 
7.2.4 Closure for Financial Results  
The Trading Window shall remain closed from the end of every financial quarter or financial period for which 
financial results are required to be announced and shall reopen forty-eight (48) hours after public disclosure of such 
results. 
The gap between clearance of accounts by the Audit Committee and consideration by the Board shall be as narrow 
as possible and preferably on the same day.  
7.2.5 Silent Period  
The Company shall observe a Silent Period to prevent any inadvertent disclosure of Unpublished Price Sensitive 
Information ("UPSI"), selective disclosure, or the appearance of providing insider information to select investors 
or analysts.  
For the purpose of financial results, the Silent Period shall commence from the end of each financial quarter or 
financial period for which financial results are required to be announced and shall continue for such period until 
the financial results for such quarter or period are made publicly available by the Company through an intimation 
to the Stock Exchanges.  
During the Silent Period:  
a) the Company shall ordinarily not initiate meetings, conference calls, email communications, telephone 
discussions, investor conferences, group meetings or one-on-one interactions with analysts or investors;  
b) the Company may respond to unsolicited queries relating to factual matters, previously disclosed information, 
historical data available in the public domain, or general information regarding the Company's business, provided 
that no UPSI is disclosed;  
c) if the Company is invited to participate in investor meetings, conferences or similar events organised by third 
parties during the Silent Period, the Chief Investor Relations Officer ("CIRO") shall determine, on a case-by-case 
basis, whether participation is appropriate, having regard to the nature of the event and the participants involved. 
Any Company representative attending such events shall ensure that no UPSI is shared or discussed;  
d) social interactions not involving discussions on Company affairs shall not be considered a violation of the Silent 
Period; and  
e) notwithstanding the above, the Company may interact with analysts and investors during the Silent Period where 
a material event has occurred and has already been publicly disclosed by the Company, provided that discussions 
are limited to information available in the public domain.  
7.2.6 Re-opening of Trading Window  
The Compliance Officer shall determine the timing of re-opening of the Trading Window after considering:  
a) whether the UPSI has become Generally Available Information; and  
b) whether sufficient time has elapsed for assimilation by the market.  
In no event shall the Trading Window reopen earlier than forty-eight (48) hours after UPSI becomes Generally 
Available Information.  
7.2.7 Intimation of Closure  
Notice of closure and reopening of the Trading Window shall be communicated to all relevant persons through e-
mail, compliance software, electronic communication systems or such other mode as may be determined by the  
 
 
 
 
Compliance Officer. Where required under applicable law, such information shall also be communicated to the 
Stock Exchange(s).  
7.2.8 Exempt Transactions  
Trading Window restrictions shall not apply to:  
a) off-market inter-se transfers between Insiders possessing the same UPSI and having taken a conscious and 
informed trading decision;  
b) transactions through the block deal window mechanism between persons possessing the same UPSI and having 
taken a conscious and informed trading decision;  
c) transactions carried out pursuant to a statutory or regulatory obligation;  
d) exercise of stock options with a predetermined exercise price;  
e) Trades executed pursuant to an approved Trading Plan;  
f) pledge of Securities for bona fide purposes such as raising funds, subject to pre-clearance and applicable 
regulations;  
g) acquisition pursuant to conversion of warrants or debentures, rights issue, preferential allotment, public issue, 
buy-back, open offer, delisting offer or such other SEBI-approved mechanisms; and  
h) any other transaction exempted under the PIT Regulations or SEBI circulars from time to time.  
 
7.3 PRE-CLEARANCE OF TRADES  
7.3.1 Applicability  
During an open Trading Window, Designated Persons and/or their Immediate Relatives intending to Trade in the 
Securities of the Company shall comply with the pre-clearance requirements specified under this Code.  
All Designated Persons and/or their Immediate Relatives proposing to Trade in the Securities of the Company shall 
obtain prior approval from the Compliance Officer in accordance with the provisions of this Code and such 
procedures as may be prescribed by the Company from time to time.  
7.3.2 Threshold for Pre-Clearance  
Pre-clearance shall be mandatory where the aggregate value of Securities proposed to be traded, whether through a 
single transaction or a series of transactions during a calendar quarter, exceeds ₹10,00,000 (Rupees Ten Lakh) or 
such other threshold as may be prescribed by the Company or applicable law from time to time.  
Provided that the Company may require all Designated Persons and/or their Immediate Relatives to seek pre-
clearance irrespective of the value or number of Securities proposed to be traded, through an online compliance 
platform or such other mechanism as may be specified by the Compliance Officer.  
7.3.3 Application for Pre-Clearance  
An application for pre-clearance of Trade shall be made to the Compliance Officer in the prescribed form (Annexure 
B / Form E) or through such online software or digital platform as may be implemented by the Company from time 
to time. 
The application shall contain, inter alia:  
 
 
 
 
a) estimated number and/or value of Securities proposed to be traded;  
b) nature of the proposed transaction;  
c) details of the Depository Participant and demat account;  
d) particulars of the Securities proposed to be traded; and  
e) such other information, declarations and undertakings as may be prescribed by the Compliance Officer.  
7.3.4 Undertaking by Applicant  
Every application for pre-clearance shall be accompanied by an undertaking in the prescribed format (Annexure C) 
confirming, inter alia, that:  
a) the applicant is not in possession of any UPSI;  
b) the applicant has not received UPSI from any source;  
c) if the applicant receives or comes into possession of UPSI after obtaining pre-clearance but before execution of 
the Trade, he/she shall immediately inform the Compliance Officer and shall refrain from Trading until such UPSI 
becomes Generally Available Information; and  
d) the proposed Trade shall be undertaken in compliance with the PIT Regulations and this Code.  
7.3.5 Processing of Applications  
Upon receipt of a complete application, the Compliance Officer shall:  
a) acknowledge receipt of the application in physical or electronic form;  
b) review the application and seek such additional information, clarifications, declarations or undertakings as may 
be considered necessary;  
c) satisfy himself/herself that the applicant is not in possession of UPSI; and  
d) grant or reject approval within two (2) Trading Days from the date of receipt of complete information.  
Approval may be granted in the prescribed format (Annexure D) or through the digital platform implemented by 
the Company.  
The Compliance Officer shall maintain copies of all applications, acknowledgements, approvals, rejections and 
related documents in physical and/or electronic form.  
7.3.6 Pre-Clearance for Trades by Compliance Officer  
Where the Compliance Officer and/or his/her Immediate Relative proposes to Trade in the Securities of the 
Company, prior approval shall be obtained from the Managing Director, Deputy Managing Director, Whole-Time 
Director, Chief Financial Officer or such other authority as may be authorized by the Board.  
In the absence of such authority, approval shall be obtained from the Chairman of the Board.  
7.3.7 Refusal of Pre-Clearance  
The Compliance Officer may refuse pre-clearance if, in his/her opinion:  
a) the proposed Trade may be based on possession of UPSI;  
b) the proposed Trade may result in a violation of the PIT Regulations or this Code; or  
 
 
 
 
c) the declarations furnished by the applicant are incomplete, inaccurate or unreliable.  
The Compliance Officer shall not be under any obligation to assign reasons for withholding approval.  
7.3.8 Restrictions While in Possession of UPSI  
No Designated Person or Immediate Relative shall:  
a) seek pre-clearance while in possession of UPSI; or  
b) execute a Trade pursuant to an approval already obtained after subsequently coming into possession of UPSI.  
Where a Designated Person gains access to UPSI after obtaining pre-clearance but before execution of the proposed 
Trade, he/she shall immediately inform the Compliance Officer and shall refrain from Trading until such UPSI 
becomes Generally Available Information.  
7.3.9 Trading During Closure of Trading Window  
No Designated Person or Immediate Relative shall Trade in the Securities of the Company when the Trading 
Window is closed.  
Any pre-clearance granted while the Trading Window was open shall automatically lapse if the Trading Window 
closes before the approved transaction is executed and fresh approval shall be required after reopening of the 
Trading Window.  
7.4 VALIDITY OF PRE-CLEARANCE  
7.4.1 Validity Period  
All Trades approved pursuant to the pre-clearance process shall be executed within seven (7) Trading Days from 
the date of approval.  
Where the approved Trade is not executed within the prescribed period, fresh pre-clearance shall be obtained prior 
to execution of such Trade.  
7.5 REPORTING OF PRE-CLEARED TRADES  
7.5.1 Reporting of Executed Trades  
Every Designated Person who executes a Trade pursuant to pre-clearance shall report the details of such Trade to 
the Compliance Officer within two (2) Trading Days of execution of the Trade in the prescribed format (Annexure 
E / Form D) or through the online compliance platform specified by the Company.  
7.5.2 Reporting of Non-Execution of Trades  
Where a Trade approved pursuant to the pre-clearance process is not executed, the Designated Person shall submit 
a NIL report together with the reasons for non-execution within two (2) Trading Days from expiry of the validity 
period or prior to seeking fresh pre-clearance, whichever is earlier.  
7.5.3 Trades by Immediate Relatives  
The disclosures required under this Clause shall include Trades undertaken by:  
a) Immediate Relatives; and  
b) any other person for whom the Designated Person takes Trading decisions.  
 
 
 
 
The concerned Designated Person shall be responsible for ensuring compliance with the requirements of this Code 
in respect of such persons . 
7.5.4 Derivative Transactions  
For the purposes of disclosures under this Code, Trading in derivatives of Securities shall also be reported and the 
traded value of such derivatives shall be taken into account wherever applicable.  
7.6 EXEMPTIONS FROM PRE-CLEARANCE  
Pre-clearance shall not be required for:  
a) Trades executed pursuant to an approved Trading Plan;  
b) transmission or inheritance of Securities;  
c) exercise of employee stock options where the exercise price is pre-determined in accordance with applicable 
regulations;  
d) transactions carried out pursuant to statutory or regulatory obligations;  
e) acquisitions pursuant to rights issues, bonus issues, preferential allotments, conversion of warrants or debentures, 
further public issues, open offers, buy-back offers, delisting offers or such other transactions as may be specified 
by SEBI from time to time; and  
f) such other transactions as may be exempted under the PIT Regulations or applicable law.  
7.7 CONTRA TRADES AND RELATED RESTRICTIONS  
7.7.1 Restriction on Contra Trades  
No Designated Person who is permitted to Trade in the Securities of the Company shall execute a Contra Trade 
within six (6) months from the date of the immediately preceding Trade.  
For the purpose of this Clause, a Contra Trade shall mean an opposite transaction involving purchase followed by 
sale or sale followed by purchase of the Securities of the Company.  
7.7.2 Waiver in Case of Personal Emergency  
In exceptional circumstances involving personal emergency, the Compliance Officer may waive the restriction on 
Contra Trades after recording reasons in writing, provided such waiver does not result in violation of the PIT 
Regulations or this Code.  
Where the Compliance Officer seeks such waiver, approval shall be obtained from the Managing Director, Deputy 
Managing Director, Whole-Time Director, Chief Financial Officer or Chairman of the Board, as applicable.  
Applications for waiver shall be made in the prescribed format (Annexure F / Form F).  
7.7.3 Disgorgement of Profits  
Where a Contra Trade is executed in violation of this Code, whether inadvertently or otherwise, any profit earned 
from such transaction shall be liable to be disgorged and remitted to SEBI for credit to the Investor Protection and 
Education Fund administered by SEBI.  
7.7.4 Exemptions from Contra Trade Restrictions  
The restrictions contained in this Clause shall not apply to:  
a) Trades executed pursuant to an approved Trading Plan;  
 
 
 
 
b) exercise of employee stock options;  
c) acquisition of Securities pursuant to rights issues, bonus issues, follow-on public offers, offer for sale, stock split, 
merger, amalgamation, demerger or similar corporate actions, provided the original transaction was undertaken in 
compliance with applicable law;  
d) disposal of Securities pursuant to buy-back offers, open offers, exit offers, delisting offers, merger, amalgamation 
or similar corporate actions, provided the original acquisition was undertaken in compliance with applicable law; 
and 
e) such other transactions as may be exempted under the PIT Regulations or applicable law from time to time.  
8. DISCLOSURES AND REPORTING REQUIREMENTS  
8.1 General Provisions  
All disclosures required to be made pursuant to the PIT Regulations and this Code shall be made in the forms and 
manner specified under the PIT Regulations, applicable circulars and this Code from time to time.  
The disclosures to be made by any person under this Code shall include disclosures relating to:  
a) Trading by such person's Immediate Relatives; and  
b) Trading by any other person for whom such person takes Trading decisions.  
The disclosure requirements under this Code shall also apply to Trading in derivatives of Securities, to the extent 
permitted under applicable law, and the traded value of such derivatives shall be taken into account for the purpose 
of determining disclosure thresholds.  
8.2 Initial Disclosures  
8.2.1 
Every person on appointment as:  
a) a Director of the Company;  
b) a Key Managerial Personnel of the Company; or  
c) upon becoming a Promoter or member of the Promoter Group,  
shall disclose his/her holding of Securities of the Company as on the date of such appointment or becoming a 
Promoter or member of the Promoter Group, within seven (7) days of such appointment or becoming a Promoter 
or member of the Promoter Group, in the prescribed format ( Annexure H / Form A ). 
8.2.2 
Promoters, members of the Promoter Group, Directors and Key Managerial Personnel shall disclose their holding 
of Securities of the Company in accordance with the requirements prescribed under the PIT Regulations and in the 
format specified under Annexure G , wherever applicable.  
8.3 Continual Disclosures  
8.3.1 
Every Promoter, member of the Promoter Group, Director and Designated Person shall disclose to the Company 
the number of Securities acquired or disposed of within two (2) Trading Days of such transaction if the value of the 
Securities traded, whether in one transaction or a series of transactions over any calendar quarter, aggregates to a  
 
 
 
 
traded value in excess of ₹10,00,000 (Rupees Ten Lakh) or such other threshold as may be prescribed under 
applicable law from time to time.  
Such disclosure shall be made in the prescribed format ( Annexure I / Form B ) through:  
a) the online compliance software implemented by the Company; or  
b) such other mode as may be specified by the Compliance Officer.  
8.3.2 
The disclosure shall be made within two (2) Trading Days of:  
a) receipt of intimation of allotment of Securities; or  
b) acquisition, disposal or transfer of Securities or voting rights, as the case may be.  
8.3.3 
The disclosure of incremental transactions after any continual disclosure shall be required only when the 
transactions undertaken after the previous disclosure cross the threshold prescribed under Clause 8.3.1.  
8.3.4 
The Company shall disclose the particulars of Trading received under this Clause to the Stock Exchange(s), 
wherever required under applicable law, within the timelines prescribed under the PIT Regulations and SEBI 
circulars. 
8.3.5 
Where any SEBI circular, amendment or regulatory clarification modifies the disclosure requirements or Stock 
Exchange filing obligations, such revised requirements shall automatically apply and prevail over the provisions 
contained in this Code.  
8.4 Disclosure by Other Connected Persons  
8.4.1 
The Compliance Officer or the Company may, at its discretion, require any Connected Person or class of Connected 
Persons to disclose:  
a) holdings in Securities of the Company; and/or  
b) Trading in Securities of the Company,  
in the prescribed format ( Annexure J / Form C ) and at such frequency as may be determined by the Compliance 
Officer for monitoring compliance with the PIT Regulations.  
8.5 Disclosures by Designated Persons  
8.5.1 One-Time Disclosure  
Every Designated Person shall furnish the following information to the Company within fifteen (15) days from the 
date on which this Code becomes applicable to such Designated Person or within such period as may be prescribed 
by the Company:  
a) Permanent Account Number (PAN) or such other identifier authorized by law;  
b) phone number, mobile number and contact details;  
 
 
 
 
c) names of educational institutions from which the Designated Person has graduated; and  
d) names of past employers.  
8.5.2 Initial Declaration  
Every Designated Person shall submit an initial declaration in the format prescribed by the Company containing, 
inter alia, the following details:  
a) name of educational institutions from which the Designated Person has graduated;  
b) names of all past employers;  
c) PAN or any other identifier authorized by law and mobile numbers of the Designated Person;  
d) details of Immediate Relatives;  
e) PAN or any other identifier authorized by law and mobile numbers of Immediate Relatives; and  
f) details of persons with whom such Designated Person shares a Material Financial Relationship.  
8.5.3 Material Financial Relationship  
For the purpose of this Code, "Material Financial Relationship" shall mean a relationship in which one person is a 
recipient of any kind of payment, including by way of loan or gift, from a Designated Person during the immediately 
preceding twelve (12) months, equivalent to at least twenty-five percent (25%) of the annual income of such 
Designated Person.  
Provided that transactions undertaken on an arm's length basis shall not be regarded as a Material Financial 
Relationship.  
8.6 Annual Disclosures  
8.6.1 Annual Holding Disclosure  
Every Designated Person shall submit to the Compliance Officer an annual statement of holdings in Securities of 
the Company as at the close of each financial year.  
Such disclosure shall be submitted within thirty (30) days from the end of the financial year in the prescribed format 
(Annexure K / Form G ) or through the online compliance platform designated by the Company.  
8.6.2 Annual Confirmation of Information  
Every Designated Person shall annually confirm and update the information provided under Clause 8.5, including:  
a) Immediate Relatives;  
b) Material Financial Relationships;  
c) educational qualifications;  
d) employment details;  
e) PAN and other identifiers; and  
f) contact details.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
The annual confirmation as of 31 March shall be submitted on or before 30 April of each financial year or within 
such period as may be specified by the Compliance Officer, who may, at his/her discretion, extend or curtail such 
period. 
8.6.3 Continual Update of Information  
Any change in the information furnished by a Designated Person under this Code shall be reported to the 
Compliance Officer within seven (7) days of such change or within such period as may be prescribed by the 
Company.  
8.7 Preservation of Disclosures and Records  
All undertakings, disclosures, declarations, applications, approvals, reports, forms and records submitted or 
maintained under the PIT Regulations and this Code shall be preserved by the Compliance Officer in physical 
and/or electronic form for a minimum period of five (5) years or such longer period as may be prescribed under 
applicable law.  
9. INSTITUTIONAL MECHANISM FOR PREVENTION OF INSIDER TRADING  
9.1 Internal Control Framework  
The Chief Executive Officer, Managing Director, Deputy Managing Director or such analogous person as may be 
determined by the Board shall establish, implement and maintain adequate and effective systems of internal controls 
to ensure compliance with the PIT Regulations and this Code and to prevent insider trading.  
9.2 Internal Controls  
The internal control framework shall include, inter alia, the following measures:  
a) identification of all Employees having access to UPSI and classification of such persons as Designated Persons, 
wherever applicable;  
b) identification of UPSI and maintenance of confidentiality thereof in accordance with the PIT Regulations and 
this Code;  
c) implementation of adequate restrictions on communication, sharing or procurement of UPSI;  
d) maintenance of a list of Employees and other persons with whom UPSI is shared;  
e) execution of confidentiality agreements and/or issuance of confidentiality notices to persons receiving UPSI;  
f) compliance with all requirements prescribed under the PIT Regulations and this Code; and  
g) periodic review and assessment of the effectiveness of internal controls and compliance procedures.  
9.3 Oversight by the Board  
The Board shall ensure that the Chief Executive Officer, Managing Director, Deputy Managing Director or such 
other authorized person establishes and maintains an adequate framework for compliance with the PIT Regulations 
and this Code.  
9.4 Review by Audit Committee  
The Audit Committee shall review compliance with the PIT Regulations and this Code at least once in every 
financial year and shall verify that adequate internal control systems have been established and are operating 
effectively.  
 
 
 
 
Such review shall include, inter alia:  
a) adequacy of controls for identification and handling of UPSI;  
b) maintenance and effectiveness of the Structured Digital Database;  
c) compliance with Trading Window restrictions, Trading Plans and disclosure requirements; and  
d) effectiveness of measures adopted for prevention of insider trading.  
9.5 Inquiry in Case of Leak of UPSI  
The Company shall maintain a Policy and Procedure for Inquiry in Case of Leak or Suspected Leak of UPSI, which 
shall form an integral part of this Code.  
9.6 Whistle Blower Mechanism  
The Company shall maintain a Whistle Blower Policy and shall take appropriate measures to make Employees 
aware of the mechanism available for reporting:  
a) actual leak of UPSI;  
b) suspected leak of UPSI; and  
c) violations of the PIT Regulations or this Code.  
9.7 Cooperation During Investigations  
Where an inquiry is initiated in relation to an actual or suspected leak of UPSI, all relevant intermediaries, 
fiduciaries, consultants, advisors and other concerned persons shall extend full cooperation to the Company in 
connection with such inquiry or investigation.  
9.8 Obligations of Intermediaries and Fiduciaries  
Every intermediary and fiduciary engaged by the Company shall establish adequate and effective systems of internal 
controls to ensure compliance with the PIT Regulations and prevention of insider trading.  
9.9 Engagement of Compliant Intermediaries and Fiduciaries  
The Company shall engage such intermediary or fiduciary who has:  
a) formulated a Code of Conduct in accordance with the PIT Regulations; and  
b) established appropriate controls for handling UPSI and monitoring Trading by its designated persons.  
10. COMPLIANCE WITH THE CODE  
10.1 Compliance Reporting  
The Compliance Officer shall monitor compliance with this Code and the PIT Regulations and shall submit a 
compliance report to the Audit Committee on a half-yearly basis or at such intervals as may be prescribed under 
applicable law.  
10.2 Responsibility for Compliance  
Every Designated Person shall be responsible for compliance with this Code and the PIT Regulations, including 
compliance by his/her Immediate Relatives and any person for whom such Designated Person takes Trading 
decisions.  
 
 
 
 
10.3 Presumption of Possession of UPSI  
Where a person has Traded in the Securities of the Company while in possession of UPSI, such Trade shall be 
presumed to have been motivated by knowledge and awareness of such UPSI. The burden of proving otherwise 
shall lie on such person.  
10.4 Disclosure by Designated Persons  
The disclosure requirements applicable to Designated Persons, including disclosures relating to Immediate 
Relatives and persons for whom Trading decisions are taken, shall be governed by Clause 8.5 and Clause 8.6 of this 
Code. All Designated Persons shall comply with the disclosure, reporting and update requirements specified therein 
within the prescribed timelines.  
10.5 Sharing of UPSI for Legitimate Purposes  
Sharing of UPSI for Legitimate Purpose shall be governed by Clause 6.3 of this Code.  
10.6 Obligations of Recipients of UPSI  
Recipients of UPSI shall comply with the requirements relating to communication, sharing, confidentiality and use 
of UPSI set out under Clause 6 of this Code and the applicable provisions of the PIT Regulations.  
10.7 Policy on Legitimate Purpose  
The Policy for Determination of Legitimate Purpose forms part of the Company's Code of Practices and Procedures 
for Fair Disclosure of UPSI and is incorporated by reference into this Code.  
10.8 Reporting of Violations  
Any violation of the PIT Regulations or this Code shall be reported by the Compliance Officer to the Stock 
Exchange(s) in accordance with applicable law.  
11. CONSEQUENCES OF CONTRAVENTION  
Any Insider, Designated Person or other person covered by this Code who Trades in Securities or communicates, 
procures, provides or permits access to UPSI in contravention of this Code or the PIT Regulations shall be subject 
to disciplinary action.  
Without prejudice to any action by SEBI or any regulatory authority, the Company may impose one or more of the 
following actions:  
a) warning or reprimand;  
b) prohibition from Trading in the Securities of the Company;  
c) wage freeze;  
d) withholding of increments and/or bonus;  
e) suspension;  
f) recovery or clawback of gains or incentives;  
g) ineligibility to participate in Employee Stock Option Schemes;  
h) cancellation or lapse of outstanding stock options;  
i) termination of employment or engagement; and/or  
 
 
 
 
j) disgorgement of gains arising from transactions undertaken in violation of this Code.  
The Compliance Officer shall report all violations of this Code to the Audit Committee.  
Where a violation of the PIT Regulations is observed, the Company shall promptly inform the Stock Exchange(s) 
in the manner prescribed by SEBI.  
The actions specified in this Code shall be without prejudice to any action that may be initiated by SEBI under the 
SEBI Act, the PIT Regulations or any other applicable law.  
Any amount recovered or disgorged pursuant to this Code shall be remitted to SEBI for credit to the Investor 
Protection and Education Fund, where required under applicable law.  
12. PROTECTION OF INFORMANTS  
Any employee of the Company, whether regular or contractual, or any Director ("Informant"), who becomes aware 
of an actual, suspected or likely violation of insider trading laws may submit a Voluntary Information Disclosure 
Form ("VIDF") to SEBI.  
The Company shall not discharge, terminate, demote, suspend, threaten, harass or discriminate against an Informant 
on account of:  
a) filing a VIDF;  
b) assisting, participating or testifying in any investigation, inquiry, audit, examination or proceeding conducted by 
SEBI; or 
c) cooperating with SEBI in relation to any alleged violation of insider trading laws.  
An Informant shall not be required to establish that:  
a) SEBI has initiated any enforcement action based on the information provided; or  
b) the information constitutes original information under the PIT Regulations.  
Any Informant who believes that he/she has been subjected to retaliation or victimisation may approach the 
Compliance Officer, SEBI or any competent court or tribunal for appropriate relief.  
The protection provided under this Clause shall not apply where the disclosure is made:  
a) with mala fide intent;  
b) to harass the Company or any person; or  
c) to extort money or secure undue advantage.  
13. SENSITIVE TRANSACTIONS  
The handling, sharing and recording of UPSI in connection with sensitive transactions shall be governed by Clause 
6 of this Code and the applicable provisions of the PIT Regulations.  
14. AMENDMENTS AND REVIEW  
The Board may amend, modify, substitute or review any provision of this Code from time to time in accordance 
with applicable law.  
Any amendment, clarification, circular, notification or guidance issued by SEBI or any other regulatory authority 
shall prevail over the provisions of this Code to the extent of any inconsistency.  
 
 
 
 
Any amendment to the PIT Regulations shall be deemed incorporated into this Code, notwithstanding that a formal 
amendment to this Code has not been carried out.  
The decision of the Board regarding interpretation, implementation and administration of this Code shall be final 
and binding.  
15. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL MECHANISM  
Any person seeking clarification or wishing to raise a grievance under this Code may contact the Compliance 
Officer at the details notified by the Company from time to time.  
The Compliance Officer shall examine and address such grievance in accordance with this Code and applicable 
law.

POLICY AND PROCEDURE FOR INQUIRY IN CASE OF LEAK OF UNPUBLISHED PRICE 
SENSITIVE INFORMATION OR SUSPECTED LEAK OF UNPUBLISHED PRICE SENSITIVE 
INFORMATION  
Preamble  
This Policy is framed with an aim to implement a structured procedure for investigation in case of leak or 
suspected Leak of UPSI.  
Applicability  
This policy shall apply to all Insiders and any other persons as assigned by law from time to time.  
Process of inquiry in case of Leak of UPSI or suspected Leak of UPSI  
1. Complaint (written or oral or electronic) regarding a leak or suspected Leak of UPSI may be received by 
the Company from the following sources:  
a. Internal:  
I. Whistle blower vide the whistle blower process as illustrated in the Company Whistle blower Policy;  
II. Any leak or suspected leak of UPSI detected through the internal controls implemented by the Company.  
b. External:  
Registrar and Share Transfer Agent, Depository, Stock Exchange, Regional Director, Registrar of Companies, 
regulatory / statutory authority or any other department of Central or State Government based on the 
complaint received from a whistleblower  
(above shall be collectively referred to as “Complaint(s)”  
2. The Chief Compliance Officer shall report the Complaint to the Audit Committee within a reasonable time 
from the date of receipt of the Complaint;  
3. The Audit Committee shall review the Complaint and shall discuss with the Chief Compliance Officer and 
Company Secretary on potential next steps including but not limited to seek additional information to 
consider an investigation, disclosure requirements to the regulatory authorities, appointment of an 
investigation panel consisting of internal employees or external agencies. If the Complaint implicates the 
Chief Compliance Officer and/or Company Secretary, then they shall recuse themselves from the said inquiry 
process; 
4. If the Audit Committee mandates an investigation, then the identified panel of investigators shall conduct 
the investigation into the Complaint(s) and present their findings to the Chief Compliance Officer. The 
executive summary of the investigation shall be reported to the Audit Committee by the Chief Compliance 
Officer; 
5. Based on the update provided by the Chief Compliance Officer, the Audit Committee shall put forward its 
recommendation to the Board. The Board, on receipt of such recommendation and after due 
review/deliberations, shall decide on the next steps;  
 
23 
 
6. The Board shall have the power to amend any of the provisions of this Policy, substitute any of the 
provisions with a new provision and also replace this Policy entirely with a new Policy;  
7. Any words used in this Policy but not defined herein shall have the same meaning as described to it in the 
Companies Act, 2013 or Rules made thereunder, Securities & Exchange Board of India Act or Rules and 
Regulations made thereunder, SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, 
SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 or any other relevant legislation/ law applicable to 
the Company, as amended from time to time.
