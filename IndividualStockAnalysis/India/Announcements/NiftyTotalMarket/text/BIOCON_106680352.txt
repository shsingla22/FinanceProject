Subject: Acquisition of equity shares of Biocon Biologics Limited and allotment  of equity shares of Biocon 
Limited (the “Company”) on preferential basis  
 
This is to inform you that pursuant to the approval of the shareholders of the Company  vide resolution 
passed through postal ballot on June 07, 2026 , for preferential issue of equity shares of the Company , 
towards discharge  of the full consideration payable  for acquisition of equity shares of Biocon Biologics 
Limited (“ BBL”), a material subsidiary of the Company, held by 127 selling shareholders  of BBL (swap of 
equity shares) , the Company has today i.e. on June 29, 2026 :   
 
(i) completed acquisition of 1,15,36,956 equity shares of BBL from  127 selling shareholders of BBL , and   
(ii) pursuant  to the in -principle approval issued by the National Stock Exchange  of India Limited and BSE 
Limited dated June 24, 2026, issued and allotted 87,86,362  equity shares of the Company of the face 
value of Rs. 5 /- each, fully paid -up, on preferential basis in demateriali sed form to the 127 selling 
shareholders  (“Allottees ”), as consideration for the aforesaid acquisition  of equity shares of BBL , at 
an issue price of Rs. 376.41  per equity share pursuant to the approval of the Preferential Issue & 
Allotment Committee  of the Company  in accordance with the terms of the share purchase 
agreements entered into with the Allottees , as per details enclosed  in Annexure I . 
 
The equity shares allotted shall rank pari -passu, in all respects, with the existing equity shares of the 
Company.  
  
Consequent to the aforesaid allotment, the issued, subscribed and paid -up equity share capital of the 
Company stand s increased from Rs. 8,10,45,38,865 /- comprising of 1,62,09,07,773 fully paid -up equity 
shares of the Company, having face value of Rs. 5/- each to Rs. 8,14,84,70,675 /- comprising of 
1,62,96,94,135 fully paid -up equity shares of the Company, having face value of Rs. 5/- each.   
 
  
 
 
 
Post the aforesaid acquisition of 1,15,36,956 equity shares of BBL by the Company, BBL has become a 
wholly -owned subsidiary of the Company w.e.f. June 29, 2026.  
 
The meeting of the Preferential Issue & Allotment Committee  commenced at 10:00  PM and concluded at 
10:10  PM. 
 
The above information will also be available on the website of the Company at www.biocon.com .
