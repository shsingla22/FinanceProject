The following Explanatory Statement sets out the material facts relating to the Special Business at Item No. 1 of 
the accompanying Notice.  
 
Article 7 of the Articles presently permits the Company to undertake an issue of either  equity share s or preference 
share s. As part of its continuing efforts to keep the Company’s constitutional documents current, relevant and 
aligned with the evolving regulatory framework, the Board has reviewed the Articles and noted that the Articles 
should also expressly empower the Company to issue warrants and other securities convertible into or 
exchangeable for equity shares, so as to allow the Company additional flexibility with respect to the nature and 
type of instrument that it may choose to issue at the time of fund raising, as permitted by the Act. Accordingly, the 
definition of “Securities” is proposed to be inserted in Article 3, and amendments to Article s 7 and 11 are proposed 
so as to permit the issue of such Securities and to place them under the control of the Board, in each case subject 
to applicable law as to the terms and manner of issue, pricing, tenure, consideration, conversion, lock -in and 
listing.

Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, as amended. Accordingly, a clarificatory amendment is 
proposed to be made to Article 11(1) to align the pricing requirements of any issuance of Securities by the

accordance with the applicable provisions of the Act  and SEBI Listing Regulations . The Board recommends the
