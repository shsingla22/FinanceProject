 Amazon Collaborates with Tata Motors to Deploy 3,000 Electric Trucks in Its Transportation 
Network in India  
• Deployment of Tata Ultra E.7 begins during the festive season, with electric trucks supporting within -city 
freight movement as volumes rise  
• The plan also includes electrifying the 2,800 -km Delhi –Bengaluru –Chennai corridor along NH48, spanning 
seven states  
 
NEW DELHI, October 27, 2026 — Amazon and Tata Motors today announced a collaboration to deploy 3,000 electric 
trucks across Amazon's transportation network in India. The largest electric -truck deployment by an e -commerce 
company in India marks Amazon’s most significant move yet to exte nd fleet electrification beyond last -mile delivery. The 
trucks will support the movement of customer shipments between facilities, across cities and along national freight 
corridors.  
The first batch of Tata electric trucks was flagged in Delhi by Shri H.D. Kumaraswamy, Hon’ble Minister of Heavy Industries 
& Steel, Government of India; Dr. Pankaj Kumar Singh, Hon’ble Health & Family Welfare, Transport & IT, Government of 
Delhi ;  Mr. Abhinav Singh, Vice President, Operations, Amazon, APAC, Middle East, Africa and Turkey; Mr Kishore Lanka, 
Director, Operations, Amazon India; Mr. Sushant Naik, Global Head – Government & Corporate Affairs and Chief 
Corporate Affairs Officer, Tata Motors and Mr. Rajesh Kaul, Vice President and Business Head – Trucks, Tata Motors, 
among other dignitaries . 
The planned deployment of 3,000 electric trucks represents a substantial addition to India’s electric truck fleet, marking 
a major step in scaling electric freight transportation across the country. As per the arrangement, Tata Motors will supply 
electric trucks for Amazon’s transportation requirements, while Amazon’s carrier partners will own and operate the 
vehicles. The collaboration brings t ogether  Amazon’s logistics network and long -term freight demand with the 
engineering, manufacturing and service capabilities of Tata Motors —India’s largest commercial -vehicle manufacturer. 
Together, the companies aim to address key barriers to adoption and demons trate how zero -exhaust -emission trucks 
can operate at scale.  
Scaling Electric Trucks Across Cities and Highway Corridors  
The initial rollout will begin in Delhi during India’s festive season, when freight volumes across e -commerce and retail 
supply chains reach their annual peak. Amazon will deploy light - and medium -duty electric trucks from Tata Motors across 
its middle -mile transportation network. The next phase will see the deployment expand to major national highways, with 
the ambition of electrifying the 2,800 -km Delhi –Bengaluru –Chennai corridor along NH48 — India’s longest freight 
corridor — spanning seven states and co nnecting six of the country’s largest e -commerce regions.   Dedicated charging 
infrastructure, including hubs being developed with Yahhvi, a charging infrastructure provider, will support this 
expansion. The rollout follows the introduction of Delhi ’s 2026 EV Policy, which gives  a significant incentive to early 
adopters: the first 1,000 privately owned medium -duty electric trucks registered in the three months after the policy 
launches will be allowed to enter the city at any time of day — bypassing the usual "No Entry" hours — for a full decade. 
It also supports India’s broader push towards cleaner mobility and freight electrification, alongside initiatives such as the  
PM Electric Drive Revolution in Innovative Vehicle Enhancement (PM E -DRIVE) and PARIVARTAN.  
Shri H. D. Kumaraswamy, Hon’ble Union Minister for Heavy Industries and Steel, Government of India, commented on 
the announcement,  “India under the able leadership of Hon’ble Prime minister Shri Narendra Modiji, is accelerating 
towards electric mobility and the realisation of Viksit Bharat 2047. The collaboration between Tata Motors and Amazon 
shows: sustained demand from a nationwide  logistics network, met by electric trucks manufactured in India.  Through 
scheme likes PM E -DRIVE, PLI Auto and PLI ACC the Government will continue to support this transition, and I look forward 
to more such efforts in powering green logistics."  
Dr. Pankaj Kumar Singh, Hon’ble  Minister  Health & Family Welfare , Transport & IT , Government of Delhi, said,  “Delhi 
has led the nation’s transition to electric mobility, from expanding our zero -emission mass -mobility to building robust 
charging infrastructure across the capital. Decarbonising logistics is an important next step in our efforts to reduce

 transport -related emissions and contribute to improving air quality in Delhi. This collaboration between Amazon and Tata

Abhinav Singh, Vice President, Operations, Amazon, APAC, Middle East, Africa and Turkey said,  “Electrifying our

We have made significant progress in India, with more than 15,000 electric vehicles operating across 500 -plus cities.  To

easier for our carrier partners and drivers. We are now setting a bold vision to scale electric trucking, addressing one of 
the toughes t challenges in decarbonising transportation. Our collaboration with Tata Motors marks an important step 
towards realising this vision. By combining our strengths, we aim to accelerate the deployment of electric trucks across 
more routes, starting with int racity operations and expanding to India’s major freight corridors .” 
Sharing his views at the flag off ceremony,  Rajesh Kaul, Vice President and Business Head – Trucks, Tata Motors Ltd. , 
added,  “Electric trucking in India is moving beyond the pilot stage and entering a phase of wider adoption. With deep 
localisation driving affordability and a compelling total cost of ownership, zero -emission trucks are becoming a practical 
business proposition fo r fleet operators across segments. Working with Amazon India, we are going beyond vehicle 
deployment to provide charging infrastructure, servicing and operational support. This collaboration will demonstrate 
how electric trucks can be integrated into large -scale logistics operations, with the potential to help reduce transportation -
related emissions. We believe this effort will contribute to the broader adoption of electric commercial vehicles in the 
country ." 
Built around an integrated e -axle, the Tata Ultra E.7 features CCS2 fast charging, regenerative braking and a new 
generation crash -tested cabin equipped with high -voltage safety systems. The truck is designed to deliver lower operating 
costs, higher uptime  and a lower -emission alternative for freight transportation.  
The collaboration builds on Amazon's ongoing work to electrify its transportation network in India supported by 10 
charging yards across the country developed by Yahhvi. As a co -founder of The Climate Pledge, Amazon has a goal to 
reach net -zero carbon emis sions by 2040 and is supporting initiatives to accelerate commercial fleet electrification in 
India.   These include Laneshift, a programme with C40 Cities that piloted electric trucks on the Bengaluru –Chennai 
corridor and contributed to India ’s first evidence -based roadmap  for zero -exhaust -emission trucks. More recently, The 
Climate Pledge launched SUPEREV, a unified digital platform designed to simplify commercial EV adoption by bringing 
together access to charging, vehicles, financing and renewable energy.
