PRESS RELEASE  
Brigade Group’s Q2 FY25 Real Estate Sales stands at ₹ 1,821 Crores, a growth of 46% over Q2 FY24  
Achieved highest ever quarterly collections of ₹ 1,936 Crores, up by 35% from Q2 FY24 
Bengaluru, November 13, 2024 : Brigade Group reported a total revenue of ₹ 1,138 Crores and PAT of 
₹ 115 Crores in Q2 FY25. The company achieved pre-sales volume of ~1.68 million sqft in Q2 FY25. 
Average sales realization stood at ₹10,832 per sqft in the quarter under review, an increase of 45% 
over Q2 FY24. Brigade has 25 mn sqft of ongoing projects as of September 2024.  
Commenting on the results, Pavitra Shankar, MD, Brigade Enterprises Ltd. said, "We attribute this 
growth to our commitment to delivering high quality residential spaces, coupled with a strong 
financial position and the robust demand for quality residential spaces. In the real estate space, we 
have upcoming projects of approximately 13 mn sqft over the next four quarters including eight in 
Bengaluru, four in Chennai, one in Hyderabad and two in Mysuru. We continue to have zero residential 
debt, primarily due to robust sales and collections.  With a healthy pipeline, increasing demand and 
steady interest rates, we are confident to carry forward momentum in future quarters.”  
REAL ESTATE  
During the quarter, revenue from real estate stood at ₹ 727 Crores with an EBITDA of ₹ 107 Crores. 
Achieved presales volume of 1.68 mn sqft valued at ₹ 1,821 Crores. Real estate collections stood at 
₹1,470 Crores, a highest ever quarterly collections. 
LEASING 
During the quarter under review, Revenue stood at ₹ 292 Crores with an EBITDA of ₹ 209 Crores. The 
Company’s portfolio occupancy stood at 97% with overall leasing of 8.46 mn sqft, including hard 
option.  BuzzWorks – the flexible office venture arm of Brigade launched its first space in Hyderabad. 
BuzzWorks now has locations in Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kochi and GIFT city, Gujarat. 
   
HOSPITALITY 
 
Brigade Hotel Ventures Limited, wholly owned subsidiary is proposing, subject to receipt of requisite 
approvals, market conditions and other considerations, an initial public offering of its equity shares 
and has filed a draft red herring prospectus dated October 30th 2024, with the Securities and Exchange 
Board of India and the relevant stock exchanges. 
 
 Q2 FY25 CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS  
During the quarter under review, total income stood at ₹ 1,138 Crores while EBITDA was ₹ 358 Crores. 
PAT stood at ₹ 115 Crores. Total collections for the Q2 FY25 stood at ₹ 1,936 crores. New launches of 
16 mn sqft has been planned for rolling 4 quarters and would include approximately 13 mn sqft in the 
residential sector.
