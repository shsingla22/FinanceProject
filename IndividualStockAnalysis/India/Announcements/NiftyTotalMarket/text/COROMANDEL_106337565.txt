MLI” ) as ratified by India with the respective country of which the said shareholder is tax resident; (b) 
non-applicability of General Anti -Avoidance Rule (“GAAR”)  and (c) providing and maintaining

4.2. Period of Holding

Depending on the period for which the shares are held, the gains would be taxable as ‘short term capital 
gain’ or ‘long -term capital gain’. Such classification would be determined as under:
