Sub:  Request for reclassification from ‘promoter’ to ‘public’ category in Indus Towers Limited

Dear Sir/Ma’am,  
1. We are writing this letter to inform you of our intention to be reclassified as ‘public’ category in 
Indus in accordance with Regulation 31A of the SEBI Listing Regulations.   
2. Al-Amin Investments Limited, Asian Telecommunication Investments (Mauritius) Limited, Trans 
Crystal Ltd, Vodafone Telecommunications (India) Limited, CCII (Mauritius), Inc., Euro Pacific 
Securities Ltd, Prime Metals Ltd and Mobilvest, entities incorporated in Mauritius (collectively, 
the “ Vodafone Mauritius Promoters ”),together with Omega Telecom Holdings Private Limited 
and Usha Martin Telematics Limited, entities incorporated in India (collectively, the “ Vodafone
Indian Promoters ” and together with the Vodafone Mauritius Promoters, the “ Vodafone 
Promoters ”) are promoters of Indus.
3. Pursuant to: (i) the on-market sale of 484,680,977 equity shares of Indus held by the Vodafone 
Mauritius Promoters on 19 June 2024; (ii) the buyback of 82,483,058 equity shares of Indus held 
by the Vodafone Indian Promoters on 27 August 2024; and (iii) the on-market sale of 79,211,086 
equity shares of Indus held by the Vodafone Indian Promoters on 5 December 2024, the Vodafone 
Promoters have disposed of their entire shareholding in Indus. Consequently, the Vodafone 
Promoters do not hold any equity shares in Indus as of 5 December 2024.  
4. Accordingly, we hereby request Indus to reclassify the Vodafone Promoters from ‘promoter’ 
category to ‘public’ category in accordance with Regulation 31A and other applicable provisions 
of SEBI Listing Regulations by obtaining the necessary approvals/authorisations and making the 
necessary applications to BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India for such 
reclassification.  
5. In connection with our request for reclassification, we hereby confirm and certify that: 
(a) We, along with the persons related to us: 
(i) do not hold any equity shares or voting rights in Indus; 
(ii) do not exercise control over the affairs of Indus, directly or indirectly; 
(iii) do not have any special rights in Indus through formal or informal arrangements, 
including through any shareholders agreements; 
2 (iv) do not have any representative on the board of directors (including not having a 
nominee director) of Indus;  
(v) do not act as a key managerial personnel in Indus; 
(vi) are not “wilful defaulters” as per the guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of 
India; and  
(vii) are not fugitive economic offender(s). 
6. There is no regulatory action pending against us with respect to compliance with securities laws. 
7. We will comply with the conditions set out in Regulation 31A(4) of the SEBI Listing Regulations. 
8. We request you to take the above on record.
