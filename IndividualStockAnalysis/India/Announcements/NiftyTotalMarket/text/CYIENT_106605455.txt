Sub: Clarification regarding the outcome filed on 23 April 2026 -Proposal for buyback of equity shares 
 
In continuation of our letter dated 23 April 2026 ( Post Board Meeting Announcement ), we inform you 
that, in the outcome disclosed by the Company in relation to the proposed buyback, it was stated that the 
Company has a class of securities "registered" under Section 12 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 in 
the United States. While the securities are not registered securities, the requirement for the approval of 
U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission ( SEC) as mentioned in the Post Board Meeting Announcement, 
continues and remains unchanged.  Accordingly, the relevant portion of the intimation, as corrected, is 
reproduced below: 
 
The Board also noted that, given the shareholding of US residents in the Company’s Equity Shares, it is 
necessary for the Company to seek and obtain exemptive relief from the U.S. Securities and Exchange 
Commission ( SEC) on certain aspects of the tender offer procedures, due to conflicting regulatory 
requirements between Indian and U.S. laws for tender offer buybacks and the Company will be making 
application in this regard.
