income deemed to be received in India).

the equity shares on which STT is paid were acquired prior to January 31, 2018, the cost of 
acquisition of such shares should be higher of: (a) actual cos t of acquisition and (b) lower of (i)

Category I or Category II Alternative Investment Fund and is regulated under the Securities and 
Exchange Board of India (Alternate Investment Fund) Regulations, 2012.
