Q2 and H1 FY2027 BUSINESS HIGHLIGHTS 
 
▪  Revenue growth of 34.1% year on year to Rs. 294 Cr, driven by continued demand across the Group's high-
voltage equipment and power quality portfolios. 
▪  EBITDA growth of 70.3% year on year  to Rs. 84 Cr , with EBITDA margin expanding to 28.6% from 22.5% 
in Q2 FY26 
▪  Strong H1 FY2027 performance, with revenue increasing 33.2% year on year to Rs. 550 Cr, while EBITDA 
and PAT grew 52.2% and 52.4% to Rs. 149 Cr and Rs. 110 Cr, respectively 
▪  Order book of approx. Rs. 2,010 Cr as on date,  providing visibility across FY2027 and beyond 
▪  The Company completed confirmatory due diligence on Winwin Speciality Insulators Limited , with 
no adverse findings reported, and subsequently executed a Share Purchase Agreement on 23 September 2026 for 
the proposed acquisition of up to 100% stake 
▪  Winwin Speciality Insulators business making operational progress,  with type testing completed for 
porcelain insulators up to 400 kV and qualifications and orders secured for 800 kV HVDC, 765 kV and 400 kV 
applications, strengthening its capabilities across high voltage transmission applications 
▪  Global Coil Factory at Sangli commenced manufacturing operations on 20 October 2026 , 
strengthening the Group's manufacturing capabilities in high voltage air core dry type reactors and specialised coil 
products for HVAC, HVDC, FACTS and renewable energy applications  
▪  Power Conversion Systems (PCS) manufacturing facility in Türkiye commenced operations on 16 
October 2026 , focusing on power electronic converters and inverters for utility scale Battery Energy Storage 
Systems (BESS) and other grid connected applications, supporting the execution of the Group's international 
BESS order book

The Group has a consolidated order book of Rs. 2,010 Cr approx. The book is well diversified across geographies and 
technology segments and is anchored by long cycle, high engineering content projects awarded by global utilities, 
transmission system operators, original equipment manufacturers and engineering, procurement and construction 
contractors. 
The Group continued to strengthen its order pipeline across key technology segments and geographies, with notable 
wins in HVDC, FACTS and high voltage equipment. These include progress across both line commutated converter 
and voltage source converter technologies, new FACTS project and equipment wins across the Americas, Europe, the 
Middle East and Asia Pacific, and initial orders for high voltage interconnect equipment catering to hyperscale and AI 
data centre campuses in the United States. The Group has also made progress in grid scale battery energy storage 
opportunities in Europe, supported by customer qualifications and tender conversions. These developments reflect 
the growing demand for grid modernisation, renewable energy integration, transmission capacity and grid stability 
solutions. 
OUTLOOK  
The outlook for Quality Power remains encouraging, supported by sustained investments in grid modernisation, high-
voltage transmission, renewable energy integration and energy storage. Rising electricity demand from data centres, 
industrial expansion and electrification is creating additional requirements for reliable grid connectivity and power 
quality. These trends, together with planned expansion of transmission networks across India and international 
markets, are expected to support demand for the Group's high voltage equipment, FACTS and power conversion 
solutions. 
Timely execution and capacity ramp up will remain key priorities as the Group addresses a growing opportunity 
pipeline. Availability and pricing of key raw materials, particularly electrical grade steel, copper and specialised 
insulation systems, continue to require close attention. The commencement of operations at the new Sangli and 
Türkiye facilities is expected to strengthen manufacturing capabilities, while the Group remains focused on improving 
throughput, completing customer qualifications, managing supply chains and maintaining quality standards. 
MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY  
Mr. P. T. Pandyan , Chairman and Managing Director, said: 
 
“The Group has delivered a strong performance in the second quarter of FY27, supported by business activity 
across its key segments. Consolidated revenue increased by 38.7% to Rs. 285.45 crore, while profit after tax 
grew by 80.1% to Rs. 63.33 crore. For the first half of FY27, revenue was Rs. 518.11 crore, registering growth of 
35.5%, while profit after tax increased by 52.4% to Rs. 110.05 crore. This performance reflects the strength of 
our business portfolio, the continued trust of our customers and the efforts of our teams across the Group.  
During the quarter, we continued to strengthen our presence across the power transmission and grid 
infrastructure value chain. Our businesses secured important orders across different product categories, 
including a significant international order for FACTS equipment through our subsidiary Endoks Enerji in 
Japan and multiple orders for 400 kV instrument transformers through Mehru from Hitachi Energy India. 
These orders reflect the technical capabilities of our businesses and their ability to serve the requirements of 
utilities and leading industry participants across domestic and international markets. 
We have also taken important steps to expand our manufacturing capabilities and broaden our technology 
offering. The commencement of operations at the Global Coil Factory in Sangli and the PCS manufacturing 
facility in Niğde, Türkiye, marks meaningful progress in our capacity expansion programme. The Sangli facility 
is designed to manufacture high voltage air core dry type reactors and specialised coil products, supported by 
dedicated high voltage testing and specialised conductor processing capabilities. The Türkiye facility will 
manufacture power electronic converters and inverters for utility  scale battery energy storage systems and 
other grid connected applications. Both facilities will be progressively scaled up in line with customer 
requirements and operational plans. 
Our proposed acquisition of Winwin Speciality Insulators Limited is another important step towards expanding 
the Group's product portfolio. We have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement for the proposed acquisition 
of up to 100% of the company, with the transaction remaining subject to the fulfilment of applicable conditions 
and approvals. Winwin brings capabilities in high  voltage porcelain insulators, an important product category 
within the power transmission ecosystem. Its recent completion of type testing for porcelain insulators up to 
400 kV, along with qualifications and orders for 800 kV HVDC, 765 kV and 400 kV applications, represents 
encouraging progress. We believe that, upon completion of the transaction, the business can complement our 
existing capabilities and strengthen our participation in a wider range of transmission projects. 
The long  term industry outlook remains encouraging, supported by increasing investments in transmission 
networks, HVDC interconnections, renewable energy integration, grid stability and energy storage. Growing 
power requirements from data centres are also creating additional demand for reliable grid infrastructure and 
specialised power equipment. Our presence across multiple technology segments positions us to participate in 
these evolving opportunities. 
Our immediate priorities remain focused on improving manufacturing throughput, strengthening supply 
chains, completing customer qualifications and progressively ramping up the new facilities. We will continue 
to focus on disciplined execution, timely delivery and operational efficiency as we work towards building a 
stronger, more integrated and globally competitive power equipment business. We remain committed to 
pursuing sustainable growth and creating long  term value for all our stakeholders.” 
ABOUT QUALITY POWER  
Quality Power Electrical Equipments Limited is an India headquartered company serving global customers in critical 
energy transition equipment and power technologies. The Company designs and manufactures high voltage electrical 
equipment and solutions for grid connectivity, renewable integration and energy transition applications, operating 
seven manufacturing facilities across India and Turkey. 
Quality Power is among the few global manufacturers of critical high voltage equipment for High Voltage Direct 
Current and Flexible AC Transmission Systems networks, and serves a customer base spanning utilities, transmission 
system operators, original equipment manufacturers, engineering contractors and hyperscale data centre developers 
across more than 100 countries.
