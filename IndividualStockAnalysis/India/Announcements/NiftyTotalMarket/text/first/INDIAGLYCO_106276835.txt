HIGHEST SAlES OF A COUNTRY LIQUOR BRAND IN A YEAR 
The record for the highest sales of a country liquor brand (Bunty Bubbly) in 
a year was set by India Glycols Limited of Gorakhpur , Uttar Pradesh. In the 
fiscal year 2024-25, the country liquor Brand, 'Bunty Bubbly,' achieved the 
highest sales of 18,304,086 cases, containing 823,683,870 pieces, as 
officially ratified by the Uttar Pradesh Excise Department. Despite being a 
single unit, it has produced the country liquor, Bunty Bubbly, adhering to 
the highest quality standards, as confirmed on June 14, 2025.
