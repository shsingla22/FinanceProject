Re: Outcome of the Board Meeting  
 
The Board of Directors of Tejas Networks Limited (‘the Company’) at its meeting held today i.e.

2. Grant of Restricted Stock Units  
 
Approved the grant of 8,790  Restricted Stock Units at face value of Rs. 10/ - each under Tejas 
Restricted Stock Unit Plans to the eligible employees  of the Company.  
 
The Meeting started at 6.00 P.M. (IST) and ended at 6.35 P.M. (IST) .
