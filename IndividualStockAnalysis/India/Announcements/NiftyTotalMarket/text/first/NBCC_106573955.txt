Subject: - Intimation of engagement of NBCC (India) Limited as Project 
Management Consultant in Republic of Seychelles for execution of 
Housing and associated infrastructure works  
 
Sir, 
 
It is informed that  Govt. of Republic of Seychelles has issued a Letter of Intent 
and in -principle agreed to engage the NBCC (India) Limited as the Project 
Management Consultant to render services such as planning, design, procurement 
of 1008 affordable housing units and the associated infrastructure requirements 
including sewage treatment plant (STP).  
The said project of USD 75 Mn is to be funded by EXIM bank under Line of Credit 
(LoC) scheme.  
 
The above information is also available on the Company’s website at:  
https://www.nbccindia.in/webEnglish/announcementNotices  
 
This is for your information and record.
