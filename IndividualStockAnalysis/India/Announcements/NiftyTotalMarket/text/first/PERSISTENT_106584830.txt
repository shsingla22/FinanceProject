News Summary  
Persistent Systems  (BSE: 533179 and NSE: PERSISTENT), a global Digital Engineering and 
Enterprise Modernization leader,  today announced the launch  of its Merchant Risk Management 
and Fraud Detection solution  powered by  Databricks Data Intelligence platform . The solution  
helps financial institutions reduce fraud losses, improve detection accuracy and lower manual 
review effort through real -time, intelligence -driven decisions . 
 
As digital payments scale , financial institutions , payment service providers, digital platforms and 
their end customers face rising fraud, regulatory scrutiny and reputational risk . Traditional 
approaches rely  on static rules  and post -transaction analysis , limiting early detection . Persistent 
shifts merchant risk management upstream to enable early detection, continuous monitoring 
and AI -driven action before  losses  occur . 
Built on the  Databricks Data Intelligence platform, the solution uses Agentic AI to  perform multi -
signal merchant vetting during onboarding, analyzing business profiles, compliance history, 
transaction patterns and external indicators to assess risk before transactions begin. Once live, 
it continuously monitors transactions, chargebacks and third -party signals in real time to detect 
emerging fraud or compliance risks.  
When risk signals are detected, the system triggers configurable actions , such as enhanced 
monitoring, watch listing  or transaction restrictions  with full  auditability and governance . 
Designed as a Databricks accelerator, it unifies  batch and streaming data, merchant profiles
