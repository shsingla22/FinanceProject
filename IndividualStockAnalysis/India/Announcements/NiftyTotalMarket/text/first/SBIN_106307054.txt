Disclosure of the details of Allotment of equity shares  in accordance with the SEBI  (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosures Requirements), 2015, (“SEBI Listing Regulations”)   
 
Re: Qualified Institutions Placement (QIP) of fully paid -up equity shares  of face value ₹1 each 
(the “ Issue ”) by State Bank of India ( ‘the Bank ’) under the provisions of Chapter VI of the SEBI  
(Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, and the State Bank of India 
Act 1955, read with the State Bank of India General Regulations, 1955  
 
1. With reference to our intimation vide letter no. CC/S&B/AND/2025 -26/264 dated 21.07.2025, we  
submit that  the Committee of Directors, at its meeting held today i.e. , July 21, 2025  approved 
the issue and allotment of 30,59,97,552  fully paid -up equity shares  to eligible Qualified 
Institutional Buyers (QIBs) at an Issue Price of  ₹817.00  per equity share of face value of ₹1 each  
(including a premium of  ₹816.00  per equity share), aggregating to ₹24,999,99,99,984 (Rupees 
Twenty -Four Thousand Nine Hundred Ninety -Nine Crore , Ninety -Nine Lakh , Ninety -Nine 
Thousand , Nine Hundred Eighty -Four only) . 
 
2. Pursuant to the allotment of equity shares  in the Issue, the issued Equity Share Capital of the 
Bank increased from ₹892.54 Crore, comprising of 892 ,54,05,164 equity shares  of face value 
₹1 each to ₹923.14 Crore, comprising of 923,14,02,716 equity shares  of face value ₹1 each  and 
the Paid-up Equity Share Capital of the Bank increased from ₹ 892.46 Crore, comprising of 
892,46,20,034 equity shares  of face value ₹1 each to ₹ 923.06 Crore, comprising of 
923,06,17,586 equity shares  of face value ₹1 each . 
 
3. The Issue opened on July 16, 2025  and closed on July 21, 2025 and the same was intimated to 
you vide our letter  no. CC/S&B/AND/ 2025 -26/257 dated 16.07.2025  and CC/S&B/AND/2025 -
26/264 dated 21.07.2025  respectively.  
 
4. The shareholding pattern of the Bank, before and after the Issue, will be submitted along with 
the listing application in the format specified in Regulation 31 of the SEBI Listing Regulations.  
 
5. The list of allottees who have been allotted more than 5%  (five percent ) of the equity shares  in 
the Issue, is attached  as Annexure A .
