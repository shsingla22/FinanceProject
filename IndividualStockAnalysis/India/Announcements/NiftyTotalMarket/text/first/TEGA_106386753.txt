Dear Madam/Sir, 
 
This is further to our intimation dated September 18, 2025, and pursuant to Regulation 30 read with 
Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended from time to time (“ SEBI Listing Regulations ”), we 
would like to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Company (“ Board”), at their meeting held 
today, September 26, 2025, have inter alia approved the following matters: 
 
1. Change in the name of proposed allottee in Sl. No. 18 i.e. 360 One Mutual Fund (Application 
size INR 50 Crores) and replacing it with the names of entities/funds from the same group as 
Sl. No. 18(a) Turnaround Opportunities Fund 18(b) 360 One High Growth Companies Fund 
18(c) High Conviction Fund - Series 1, and 18(d) 360 One Equity Opportunity Fund,  keeping 
all the other terms and conditions, and Application size for 360 One Group Funds as INR 50 
crores in aggregate and the Total Issue offer as INR 2000.60 crores, same as approved in their 
meeting held on September 18, 2025. The revised and updated list of allottees is enclosed as 
Table - A. Further, the Board has also approved the issuance of corrigendum to the notice and 
explanatory statement dated September 18, 2025, to the shareholders of the Company for 
updating the changes. 
 
2. Pursuant to the General Circular No. 03/2025 as issued by Ministry of Corporate Affairs 
(“MCA”) dated September 22, 2025, extending the time for holding of Annual General 
Meeting (“AGM”) and Extraordinary General Meeting (“EGM”) through Video Conferencing 
(VC) or Other Audio Visual Means (OAVM) beyond September 30,2025, the Board has 
approved change in the mode of conducting the forthcoming EGM of the Company scheduled 
on Friday, October 10, 2025 at 11:00 A.M. (IST) from physical mode to virtual mode (via video 
 
 
conferencing (VC)and/or other audio-visual means (OAVM)). A corrigendum in this 
regard shall also be issued to the shareholders in due course.  
 
This disclosure will also be hosted on the Company’s website viz. www.tegaindustries.com . 
 
The meeting of the Board commenced at 15:00 Hrs and concluded at 15:30 Hrs.
