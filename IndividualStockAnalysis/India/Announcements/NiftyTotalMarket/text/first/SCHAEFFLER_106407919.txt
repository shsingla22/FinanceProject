Pune , India  | 15 October  2025 | Schaeffler India, a leading motion technology 
company, will showcase its latest portfolio of advanced bearing solutions and 
industrial innovations for the railway sector at the 15th International Railway 
Equipment Exhibition (IREE) 2025.  The exhibition will be held from October 15 –17, 
2025 at Pragati Maidan, New Delhi. Visitors can experience Schaeffler’s solutions at 
Hall – 3, Booth – 3.30 . 
Through its participation,  Schaeffler  India as a trusted technology partner , aims to 
strengthen its presence in the Indian railway market , highlight ing its localization 
efforts in alignment with the Government of India’s “ Atmanirbhar Bharat ” vision, 
and engage with key stakeholders to support the nation’s expanding rail 
infrastructure.  
The booth will feature Schaeffler’s wide range of cutting -edge railway and industrial 
solutions, including:  
• TAROL Bearings : Compact, ready -to-mount tapered roller bearing units for 
railway axle boxes, ensuring durability and reduced maintenance.  
• CRU Bearings : Cylindrical Roller Bearing Unit for Locomotive Axle offering 
high reliability and simplified installation.  
• Current Insulated Bearings : Bearings with special coatings to prevent 
electrical damage, widely used in motors and generators.  
• Housings : Robust bearing housings to support the bearings adequately and 
offering longer service life.  
• Lubrication Greases : Specialized greases that reduce friction, protect 
against wea r and extend service intervals.  
• Class C/D/K Seals : Advanced sealing solutions for effective contamination 
protection.  
• Condition Monitoring Display Kit : Advanced condition monitoring 
solutions for  bearing s, aiding predictive maintenance.  Schaeffler India showcases innovative industrial solutions at 
International Railway Equipment Exhibition (IREE) 2025

Schaeffler India has been continuously investing in strengthening its capabilities for 
the railway sector. Recently, the company commissioned a state -of-the-art TAROL 
heat treatment facility at its Maneja Plant, developed to meet the evolving 
requirements of the rail industry and contribute to India’s infrastructure ambitions .
