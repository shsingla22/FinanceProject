Press Release  
 
Nuvoco celebrates ‘Dus Saal Bemis aal’ as it marks  a decade of purposeful growth 
 
Mumbai, October  12, 2026 –  Nuvoco Vistas Corp. Ltd. (“Nuvoco”), India’s trusted building materials C ompany, 
is commemorating a remarkable decade of transformation, growth and institution- building, a journey defined by 
bold choices, enduring conviction and above all its people.  
 
Marking a decade of growth, Nuvoco is celebrating under the theme 'Dus Saal Bemisaal' , a tribute to the 
milestones that shaped its mission of becoming a trusted building materials company creating value for its 
stakeholders. Each step of this journey has brought Nuvoco closer to its vision of Building a Safer, Smarter, and 
Sustainable worl d. 
 
The roots of this journey go back to 2014, when Nirma Group, its parent organisation, commenced operations 
with its greenfield cement plant at Nimbol, Rajasthan  with a capacity of 2 MMTPA. What began as a single 
manufacturing operation evolved over the years into a diversified building materials Organisation spanning 
Cement, Ready -Mix Concrete,  and Modern Building Materials  businesses . 
  
A defining inflection point came in  October  2016, with the completion of the acquisition of Lafarge India.  
The milestone significantly expanded the Organisation’s scale and laid the foundation for the Nuvoco of today. 
More importantly, it marked the beginning of a larger ambition to build an institution for the long term, guided by purpose, performance and the confidence to continually move forward.  
 
This ambition found its own identity with the emergence of Nuvoco Vistas Corporation Limited. It marked the 
creation of an Organisation with its own aspirations and purpose, and the beginning of a brand that employees, 
customers,  and partners could increasingly call their own.  Furthermore, in 2020, even amid global uncertainty 
of COVID -19, Nuvoco acquired Emami Cement  (now NU Vista Limited) , reflecting its long- term confidence in 
India's building materials sector and its willingness to invest through challenging times. In 2021, Nuvoco became 
a publicly listed company, opening the Organisation to a wider set of stakeholders and ushering in a new phase 
of growth.  
 The ambition continues even today. In April 2025, Nuvoco added Vadraj Cement in its portfolio, representing 
another milestone in its growth story, strengthening its presence in western India and underscoring the 
Company’s continued willingness to invest for the long term.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The journey has never been just about becoming bigger; it has been about becoming better, advancing innovation 
and operational excellence, building talent and deeper customer relationships, and growing responsibly and 
sustainably to create enduring value f or every stakeholder.  
 
Today, the Company has 27 MMTPA of operating cement capacity and 14 manufacturing units  (including 
Kutch and Sachana facilities in Gujarat in the advanced stage of completion) . The Company also has 58 
ready -mix concrete plants across India and a wide portfolio of products and solutions under its Modern 
Building Materials business.  The Company is poised to reach 35 MMTPA of cement capacity by  
FY 2027– 28, representing approximately 15x growth over the past decade.  
 Reflecting on the milestone, Shri. Hiren Patel, Chairman, Nuvoco Vistas Corp. Ltd., said, “As we celebrate 
‘Dus Saal Bemisaal’, we look back with immense pride and gratitude at a decade shaped by courageous  
decisions, long- term conviction and the collective strength of our people. From our beginnings at Nimbol to the 
Organisation we are today, every milestone has added a new chapter to Nuvoco’s journey and strengthened our 
belief in building for the long term. ” 
  
He further added,  “As we step into the next decade, our ambition is to build on this strong foundation and create 
an institution that is innovative, sustainable , high -performing and an Organisation where people are proud to 
contribute, grow and build meaningful careers. Dus Saal Bemisaal celebrates how far we have come and 
reminds us  how much more we can achieve together. The legacy of Nuvoco belongs to all of us , and I believe our 
most exciting chapters are still ahead.”  
 
 
About Nuvoco Vistas:  
Nuvoco Vistas  Corporation  Limited  (“Nuvoco”) is a building  materials  company with  a vision  to build  a safer,  smarter,  and sustainable  world  and among  
the leading  players  in East India  with  strong  presence  in North  and West  India.  Nuvoco  started  operations  in 2014  with  a greenfield  cement  plant  in 
Nimbol,  Rajasthan,  and subsequently  acquired  Lafarge  India  Limited,  which  entered  India  in 1999,  and Emami  Cement  Ltd. in 2020  and Vadraj  Cement  
Limited  in April  2025.  Furthermore,  the Company  announced expansion in the East through  a new  grinding  mill at the Arasmeta  Cement  Plant  
complemented  by a series  of debottlenecking  projects,  including  equipment  upgrades,  process  improvements,  and internal debottlenecking  initiatives.  
With  this,  Nuvoco is on track  to achieve  a total  cement  capacity  of 35 MMTPA.  The Company  reported  total  income of Rs. 11,362  crore  in FY 2025 -26, 
underscoring  its strong  and sustainable  growth  trajectory.  
 
Nuvoco offers  a diversified  business portfolio  in three  business  segments:  Cement,  Ready -Mix Concrete  (RMX),  and Modern  Building  Materials  (MBM).  
Nuvoco’s  Cement  product  portfolio  includes - Concreto,  Duraguard,  Double  Bull,  PSC,  Nirmax  and Infracem  brands that offer  a complete  spectrum  of 
Ordinary  Portland  Cement  (OPC),  Portland  Slag Cement  (PSC),  Portland  Pozzolana  Cement  (PPC)  and Portland  Composite  Cement  (PCC).  Nuvoco’s  RMX  
business possesses a pan- India  presence and offers  value -added products under  Concreto (Performance concrete),  Artiste  (Decorative  concrete),  
InstaMix  (ready- to-use bagged  concrete - the first- of-its-kind  in the industry),  X-Con (M20  to M60)  and Ecodure (Special green  concrete) brands.  It is 
also a proud  contributor  to landmar k projects  like the Mumbai -Ahmedabad Bullet  Train;  Birsa  Munda  Hockey Stadium  (Rourkela),  Aquatic  Gallery  
Science City (Ahmedabad),  Metro Railway  (Delhi,  Jaipur,  Noida  and Mumbai),  and among  many  others.  Nuvoco’s  MBM  product  portfolio,  under  the 
‘Zero M’ brands,  comprises  construction  chemicals,  tile adhesives,  wall putty  and cover  blocks.  Through  the NABL -accredited  Construction  Development  
and Innovation  Centre  (CDIC)  based in Mumbai,  Nuvoco identifies  gaps in the marketplace  and offers  innovative  product s to meet  customer  
requirements.
