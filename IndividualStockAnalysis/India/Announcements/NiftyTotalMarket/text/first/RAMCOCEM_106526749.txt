PRESS  RELEASE  ON STANDALONE  UN-AUDITED  RESULTS  FOR Q3FY26
...2During  Q3 FY26, the cement  sale volume  is 4.43 million  tons, compared  to 4.28 million  
tons in the Q3 FY25 with a growth  of 4%. Construction  chemicals  business  has registered  
sale volume  of 1.54 lac tons for Q3FY26  as against  0.86 lac tons during  Q3FY25  with a 
growth  of 79%. The overall  sale volume  for the current  quarter  has increased  by 5% when  
compared  to Q3FY25.  The cement  capacity  utilisation  for the Q3FY26  is at 73% as against  
75% during  Q3FY25.
Net revenue  for Q3FY26  is Rs. 2,119  crores  as against  Rs. 1,988  crores  during  Q3FY25  with 
a growth  of 7% YoY. The net revenue  include  revenue  from construction  chemicals  
amounting  to Rs.90 Crores  for Q3FY26  as against  Rs.52 Crores  during  Q3FY25  with a 
growth  of 74%. The company  continues  to focus  on the strategy  of right products  for right 
applications  to make its brands  stronger.  EBIDTA  for Q3FY26  is Rs. 298 crores  as against  
Rs. 291 crores  during  Q3FY25  with growth  of 3%. Blended  EBIDTA  per ton for the Q3FY26  
is Rs. 651/-  as against  Rs. 666/-  during  Q3FY25.  Operating  profit  ratio for Q3FY26  is 14% as 
against  15% during  Q3FY25.
Cost of raw materials  per ton has increased  by 4% YoY from Rs. 969 to Rs. 1012 for the 
current  period,  due to levy of mineral  bearing  land tax (MBIT)  of Rs.160  per ton of 
limestone  in TN from Apr-25,  which  translate  into impact  in variable  cost of Rs.47  Crores  
for Q3FY26  for the company.  It may be noted that Tamil Nadu is the only state where  
such a huge levy is imposed.  The company  along with other cement  companies  have 
represented  to the TN Government  to reduce  the levy, which  is pending.
The power & fuel cost per ton of cement  for Q3FY26  has increased  to Rs. 1,088/-  
compared  to Rs. 1,061/-  in Q3FY25.  The usage  of green  energy  has increased  by 8% from  
39% in Q3FY25  to 47% in Q3FY26  on account  of record  high generation  of wind power,  
which has helped  to manage  the overall  power  & fuel cost for Q3FY26.  All green  power  
assets  are 100% owned  by the Company  / wholly  owned  subsidiary  company,  with no 
dependence  on group  captive  or third party  ownership.
