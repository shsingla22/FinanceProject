TVS Motor Achieves Highest ever Revenue  
with 44% Growth in Q2 2026 -27  
 
• Record  Revenue of Rs. 17,103  Crores  in Q2 2026- 27 
• Highest Ever Sales of 1.9 Million U nits        
• Profit After Tax Grew by 5 2% at Rs. 1,3 77 Crores    
 
 Bengaluru, October  28, 2026:   TVS Motor Company , part of TVS VENU, posted revenue 
for the second  quarter of financial year 2026 -27 at Rs. 17,103  Crores , registering a growth of 
44% as against  Rs. 11,905  Crores recorded in the second  quarter of 202 5-26.  
 The Company posted its highest EBITDA of Rs. 2,2 19 Crores with a growth of 4 7% for the 
second  quarter of 202 6-27 as against EBITDA of Rs. 1,507 Crores in the same period of 
2025-26. The Company’s EBITDA margin improved by 30 bps at 13.0% during the quarter 
ended September  2026 as against 12.7% in the quarter ended September  2025.  
During the current quarter, the Company’s Profit After Tax grew by 52% at Rs. 1,377 Crores 
as against Rs. 904  Crores during the second  quarter of 202 5-26. 
 SALES Q2 FY 2026 -27: 
 During the second  quarter of the financial year 2026 -27, the C ompany registered the highest 
ever quarterly sales . The overall tw o and three- wheeler sales grew by 27% at 1.9 Million  units 
as against 1.5  Million  units registered in the quarter ended September  2025. Motorcycles 
sales during the quarter under review  grew by 28% recording 0.9 Million  units as against 0.7 
Million  units in the quarter  ended September  2025. Scooter s sales during the quarter under 
review  grew by 29% at 0.8 Million  units as against  0.6 Million  units in the quarter  ended 
September  2025. International Business reported a growth of 35% with sales of 0.5  Million  
units in Q 2 2026- 27 as against 0.4 Million  units in the second  quarter of 2025- 26.  
Two-wheeler electric vehicles grew by 133 % registering sales of 186,186 units in the second  
quarter  of  2026-27 as against 80,009  units during second  quarter of 202 5-26.  
 Three- wheeler s sales for the quarter under review grew by 47 % to 78,475  units in the second  
quarter  ended September  2026 as against 53,449  units in the quarter  ended September  
2025.  
Half-year results:   
The total two- wheeler sales of the Company for the half -year ended September 2026  grew 
by 27% registering 3.4 Million  units as against 2.7 Million  units recorded in the half -year ended 
September 2025 . The total three- wheeler sales for the half -year ended September 202 6 grew 
by 47% at 145,172 units as against 98,427  units registered in the half -year ended September 
2025. The total sales in I nternational Business of two and three- wheelers for the half -year 
ended September 202 6 grew by 34% at 1.0 Million  units as against 0.8 Million  units in the 
half-year ended September 2025 .

About TVS Motor Company  
 
TVS Motor Company (BSE:532343 and NSE: TVSMOTOR) , part of TVS V ENU,  is a reputed 
two and three- wheeler manufacturer globally, championing progress through sustainable 
mobility with four state- of-the-art manufacturing facilities located in India and Indonesia. 
Rooted in our 100- year legacy of trust, value, and passion for customers, it takes pride in 
making internationally accepted products of the highest quality through innovative and 
sustainable processes. TVS Motor is the only two- wheeler company to have won the 
prestigious Deming Prize. Our products lead in their respec tive categories in the J.D. Power 
IQS and APEAL surveys. We have been ranked No. 1 Company in the J.D. Power Customer 
Service Satisfaction Survey for four consecutive years. Our group company Norton 
Motorcycles, based in the United Kingdom, is one of the m ost emotive motorcycle brands in 
the world. TVS Motor Company endeavours to deliver the most superior customer experience across 90 countries in which we operate. For more information, please visit 
www.tvsmotor.com.
