KIMS Secunderabad 
• KIMS Secunderabad achieved a rare milestone of comp leting 10,000 brain tumour surgeries 
successfully: A rare case of Weber- Christian Disea se patient saved without Liver transplant. 
Weber- Christian disease is a rare autoimmune disor der characterised by inflammation of fatty 
tissues beneath the skin. A 26-year-old woman suffe ring from an extremely rare and life-
threatening complication caused by Weber-Christian disease was treated and saved without 
performing a liver transplant because of her other complications. Severe liver involvement in 
Weber-Christian Disease is highly fatal and has bee n documented in only a handful of cases 
globally. The patient was treated with pulse steroi ds and advanced immune-modulatory therapy. 
At a critical stage, doctors administered Anakunra,  an IL-1 receptor Antagonist which was 
specially imported from abroad. This marked the fir st ever reported use of Anakunra in the world 
to treat Weber- Christian Disease with fulminant He patic failure.   Specialists from 
Rheumatology, Hepatology, Liver transplant intensiv e care and critical care departments treated 
her successfully and patient was discharged. She le ads a normal life now. 
 
• Needle-based Cryoablation relieves chronic Testicul ar pain without surgery: A 51-year-old 
patient was suffering from severe scrotal pain for over 8 months making normal life difficult. 
Despite consulting various hospitals and undergoing  various treatments, he found no relief. The 
doctors performed the procedure of Cryoablation the rapy by which the pain causing nerves are 
disabled providing immediate pain relief.

• The child regains Smile after Jaw surgery: The doct ors performed a rare and complex jaw 
reconstruction surgery on a 5-year-old boy restorin g his ability to open his mouth and 
significantly improving his quality of life. The ch ild had been suffering from an inability to open

• As 65-year-old heart attack patient was successfull y treated using intravascular lithotripsy (IVL)

• A medical milestone was achieved by performing the region’s first Transcatheter Aortic Valve 
Replacement (TAVR) – a heart valve replacement cond ucted without any surgical incision. The 
patient, a 75-year-old suffered from severe aortic valve narrowing and complex calcium deposits. 
Given his advanced age traditional open-heart surge ry posed extreme risks. Hence the doctors 
opted for the minimally invasive TAVR approach usin g the advanced Meril Myval system. In this 
knife-less procedure the new valve was delivered vi a a catheter through the femoral artery in the 
leg. The procedure was a success, and the patient w as discharged within 72 hours. 
 
 
 
KIMS Kurnool 
• The doctors saved the life of a critically ill Kidn ey failure patient using Continuous Renal 
Replacement Therapy (CRRT), a highly advanced dialy sis technique. The case is notable as the 
patient’s blood pressure has dropped to dangerously  low levels, making conventional treatment 
extremely risky. The CRRT is particularly beneficia l for ICU patients with septic shocks and kidney 
failure, where conventional dialysis may pose high risks. Until recently this advanced facility was 
largely available only in metro cities. 
 
• In a rare and life-saving medical intervention, the  doctors successfully performed a complex 
surgery on a newborn baby on the 7 th  day after birth. The baby was diagnosed with 
Hirschsprung’s disease, a congenital condition in w hich nerve cells responsible for bowel 
movements are absent in a portion of intestine. The  doctors adopted a advanced Trans Anal 
Endorectal Pull Through (TERPT) procedure, which al lows surgeons to correct the defect in a 
single stage without any external incisions.    
 
• The doctors have successfully performed a complex k idney stone removal procedure on a 
pregnant woman ensuring the safety of both the moth er and the foetus. The woman was 
admitted with severe flank pain and fever. Investig ations revealed a 16mm stone in the renal

fragmented and removed using laser technology. The case highlights the hospital’s expertise in 
handling complex, high-risk urological cases during  pregnancy.

• A new lease of life with Bone Graft from Brain-Dead  Donor: In a rare medical achievement, the 
doctors have successfully restored shoulder functio n in a patient using a bone graft harvested 
from a brain-dead donor. The patient, a 40-year-old  man had been suffering from recurrent 
shoulder dislocations due to epilepsy, which caused  frequent falls. Despite multiples surgeries in 
the past, the condition persisted. The medical team  performed an open rotator cuff repair along 
with allograft bone grafting. The donor bone was us ed to reconstruct the damaged portion of the 
shoulder, thereby restoring both structure and stab ility. This is the first time such an allograft 
bone grafting procedure has been successfully perfo rmed in the state, marking a significant 
advancement in orthopaedic care.
