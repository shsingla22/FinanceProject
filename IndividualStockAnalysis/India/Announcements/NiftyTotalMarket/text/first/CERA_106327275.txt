audited figures in respect of full financial year ended 31st March, 2025 and the unaudited 
year to date figures up to the third quarter ended 3P1 December, 2024. 
4) The Company operates mainly in manufacturing of "Building Products" and all other 
activities are incidental thereto which have similar risk and return. Further, the sales are 
substantially in the domestic market. Accordingly, there are no separate reportable 
segments as required under Ind AS 108 "Operating Segment".

adjusting against the impairment loss provided (Rs. 655.57 Lakhs upto FY 2023-24 and 
remaining amount of Rs.150.43 Lakhs in March 2025 quarter) and disclosed as an 
exceptional item in the respective periods.
