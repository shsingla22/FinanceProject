PRESS RELEASE  
PNG Jewellers to Incorporate New Subsidiary , Strengthening its 
Supply Chain and E ntering Diamond Jewellery M anufacturing  
 
Pune, November 16, 2026: P N Gadgil Jewellers Limited (PNG/ PNG Jewellers) , one of India's leading 
jewellery retailers, today announced its plans to incorporate a new subsidiary marking a significant step 
towards strengthening its supply chain through backward integration and entering into manufacturing of diamond and gold jewel lery.  
Deal Overview  
The initial investment towards the project is estimated upto  ₹10 crore, which will primarily be utilized for 
acquiring the relevant business assets and inventory. The investment will be funded through internal 
accruals.  
The new entity will be incorporated with PNG Jewellers holding a 76% equity stake and the operational 
partners holding the remaining 24%.  
Strategic Rationale & Synergies  
1. Backward Integration and Manufacturing Control  
This move will provide PNG with a direct presence in diamond and gold jewellery manufacturing, enabling greater control over product quality, design development, procurement and delivery timelines. It will strengthen the company's ability to respond to evolving consumer preferences while improving operational efficiencies.  
2. Established Manufacturing Expertise and Technical Capabilities  
The operational partners bring over 27 years of experience in diamond and gold jewellery manufacturing, including diamond cutting and polishing capabilities. Their technical expertise, operational knowledge and manufacturing experience will provide a stron g foundation for establishing and scaling the new entity.  
3. Strengthening Studded Jewellery Business and North India Expansion  
Over the past couple of years, PNG has significantly improved its studded jewellery sales, and with the ongoing expansion in the northern region, this growth is expected to accelerate further. Studded jewellery contributes a relatively higher share of the overall jewellery market in several northern Indian states, which provides a strong rationale for entering into this new venture. This new venture  will also support 
the company’s broader geographical expansion strategy by ensuring access to a wider range of studded jewellery designs and products.  
4. Complementary Strengths and Experienced Management  
The venture brings together PNG’s established retail presence and the operational partners’ specialised 
experience in diamond and gold jewellery manufacturing. This combination will give the new entity strong technical expertise from the outset, supported by PNG’s customer reach and brand strength.  
 
Financial Outlook  
The subsidiary  is expected to achieve a PAT margin of approximately 6 –8% upon stabilization. The 
company intends to scale operations in a phased manner . 
The subsidiary  is expected to contribute to improved supply chain efficiencies, stronger control over 
product quality and costs, and enhanced long -term profitability.

Management Commentary 
Dr. Saurabh Gadgil, Chairman and Managing Director of P N Gadgil Jewellers, said:  
“This venture is an important step in our backward integration strategy, marking PNG’s entry into diamond and studded jewellery manufacturing. By combining our established retail network with in -
house manufacturing, diamond processing and design capabilities, we will gain greater control over our supply chain, from sourcing to the showroom.  
This will strengthen product quality and operational efficiency, and help us meet the growing demand for studded jewellery.”  
Way Forward 
The new entity will commence operations in a phased manner, with an initial focus on acquiring and integrating the relevant business assets, establishing manufacturing operations . 
The immediate priorities will include strengthening diamond cutting and polishing capabilities and utilizing PNG's retail network to create additional business opportunities.
