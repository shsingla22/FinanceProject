 c. A brief on types of cu stomers
   Jyothy Labs is an Indian consumer goods company that manufactures and markets a variety of products 
such as Fabric Care, Household Insecticides, Personal Care, and Dishwashing products catering to diverse 
range of consumer needs and preferences. Through its “Fabric Spa” brand, the Company also provides 
laundry and dry cleaning services. The Company has a strong presence in India and recognized for 
quality and consumer trust. The Company’s products are available through a network of retail outlets and 
supermarkets. The Company also has a significant digital presence, with items sold online on E-commerce 
platforms.
