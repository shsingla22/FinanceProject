This is with reference to the clarification sought on the news item which appeared i n the “ website - 
www.economictimes.com ” dated  March 30, 2026 captioned “ SFIO summons former top executives of 
IndusInd Bank in accounting probe .” 
In this regard, we wish to inform that the Bank had earlier made the following disclosures to the stock 
exchanges :  
1) On December 18, 2025, the Bank had disclosed that matters relating to accounting of internal 
derivative trades, certain unsubstantiated balances in “other assets” and “other liabilities” accounts 
of the Bank and micro finance interest income/ fee income had been reported to the Serious Fraud 
Investigation Office (“SFIO”), Ministry of Corporate Affairs, on June 2, 2025. It was also disclosed 
that the SFIO had engaged in telephonic discussions with the Bank official and that written 
communication in this rega rd was awaited.  
2) Subsequently, on December 24, 2025, the Bank had disclosed that it had received a letter dated 
December 23, 2025 from the SFIO, initiating an investigation into the affairs of IndusInd Bank 
Limited u nder section  212 of the Companies Act, 2013 and seeking relevant information.  
We now wish to clarify that, as on date, the Bank has not received any communication from the SFIO 
regarding the summoning of employees, as stated in the aforesaid news item.  However, as part of its 
ongoing investigation, the SFIO may be issuing summons/notices directly to individuals, without routing the same through the Bank.  
We remain conscious of our disclosure obligations under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations and 
shall continue to comply with the same.
