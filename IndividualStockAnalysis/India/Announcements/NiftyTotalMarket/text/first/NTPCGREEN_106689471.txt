Sub:  Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 - Issue of unsecured non-convertible debentures 
   
  Dear Sir/Ma’am, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we wish to inform you that NTPC Green Energy Limited has decided to issue unsecured non-
convertible debentures of ₹2,500 crore on 09.07.2026, through private placement at a coupon of 7.27% 
p.a. for a tenor of 10 years, maturing on 09.07.2036, as per the approval granted by the Board of Directors 
on 22.05.2026.  
 
This is the first issue of debentures under the above mentioned approval. 
Proceeds of the issue shall be utilized for, inter alia,  financing capital expenditure, including refinancing 
and recoupment of capital expenditure already incurred by the Company, as well as for extending such 
financing for capital expenditure to its subsidiaries and joint ventures through inter-corporate loans, and 
for other general corporate purposes. 
 
The debentures are proposed to be listed on NSE.  
 
Debenture Trust Deed will be duly executed as per the requirements of and within the period of time 
prescribed under the applicable provisions.
