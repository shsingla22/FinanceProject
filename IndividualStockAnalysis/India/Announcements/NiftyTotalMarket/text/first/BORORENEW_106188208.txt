 Borosil Renewables Limited (the “Company” ) has outstanding standby letter of credit ( SBLC)  
from HDFC Bank Limited  (“HDFC”)  of EUR 10. 8 million . This SBLC  was arranged by the 
Company from the HDFC, Mumbai Branch ( “HDFC SBLC Lender ”) in favour of GIFT City 
Branch of HDFC (“HDFC Lender Bank”) , for EUR 12 million , as a security for the loan of 
corresponding amount to GMB Glasmanufaktur  Brandenburg GmbH ( “GMB ”), a step- down 
subsidiary of the Company, incorporated in Germany. T he Company through its wholly owned 
subsidiary, Geosphere Glassworks GmbH , indirectly holds 86% stake in GMB . 
 
The Company vide its disclosure dated March 13, 2025 , and attached as Annexure,  informed 
about the payment of EUR 10.2 million made by it under  similar SBLC issued by The Hongkong 
and Shangai Banking Corporation Limited , consequent to the request made by GMB in view of 
its immediate weak business outlook.   
 Similarly, GMB has also requested the HDFC Lender Bank to prepone the repayment schedule 
and draw upon the SBLC issued by the HDFC SBLC Lender towards the repayment of the 
principal outstanding amount to close the loan.  In view of this  request by GMB, the HDFC Lender 
Bank has preponed the repayment schedule and demanded the payment from GMB who has 
requested the Company to pay under SBLC. The HDFC SBLC Lender has called upon the 
Company to make the payment.  
 Accordingly, pursuant to the e- mail communication received from the HDFC SBLC Lender, the 
Company has honoured its obligations under SBLC by funding the cash credit account 
maintained with the HDFC SBLC Lender which was utilised towards settlement of the 
outstanding loan amount on behalf of GMB, amounting to EUR 10.8 million to HDFC Lender 
Bank. The said payment was processed today .     
        
Consequent to making such payment under the SBLC towards  the settlement of the debt of the 
HDFC Lender Bank, the Company stands subrogated in place of the HDFC Lender Bank and 
has become the creditor to GMB to the extent of the aforesaid amount of EUR 10. 8 million paid 
under the SBLC.  
 
The aforementioned infor mation is also being made available on the Company’s website at
