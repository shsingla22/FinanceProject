Revolt Motors Launches ‘Azadi From Petrol’ Offer 
with Benefits Worth ₹20,000 This Independence Day  
Customers to get free insurance and additional savings on Revolt electric motorcycles till 
August 1 6 
New Delhi, 12th August  2025: In celebration of India’s 78th Independence Day, Revolt Motors, 
India’s No.1 electric motorcycle brand, has launched its special “Azadi From Petrol”  offer, empowering 
riders to break free from rising fuel costs and shift to smarter, sustainable mobility. As part of this 
limited -period offer, customers can avail benefits worth up to ₹20,000  on the purchase of any Revolt 
electric motorcycle.  
The offer includes a Zero Insurance Fee , giving customers free insurance coverage  (valued up 
to ₹7,000), along with attractive cash savings  of up to ₹13,000. The campaign underscores 
Revolt’s commitment to making electric mobility more accessible and financially rewarding 
for Indian consumers during the festive period.  
 
Speaking about the offer, Mrs. Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises  said, 
“Azadi From Petrol is not just an offer , it’s a call to action for riders to choose freedom from 
rising fuel prices, frequent maintenance, and outdated technology. This Independence Day, we 

   
invite every Indian to make the switch to electric with Revolt and experience a smarter, cleaner, 
and more cost -effective way to ride.”  
Revolt’s product lineup includes the feature -rich RV400 , the all -new RV400 BRZ , RV1, RV1+ , 
and the recently launched RV BlazeX . Revolt Motorcycles are known for their AI -enabled 
features, zero fuel dependency, low running costs, and unmatched riding experience.  
The “Azadi From Petrol” offer is available across all authorized Revolt dealerships in India from 
August 11 to August 1 6, 2025, subject to stock availability. Customers can book their ride 
online at www.revoltmotors.com  or visit the nearest Revolt Hub to avail the offer.  
This Independence Day, let your ride reflect your freedom —from petrol, from noise, and from 
compromise.
