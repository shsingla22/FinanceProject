Resident  Shareholders  - Please  Note  that:  
 
1. Recording of the valid PAN for the registered Folio/DP id -Client Id is mandatory. In absence 
of valid PAN, tax will be deducted at  a higher  rate of  20% as per Section 397(2) of the Act.  
 
2. As per Section 262 of the Income Tax Act 2025, every person eligible to obtain an Aadhaar and has PAN must link their Aadhaar with their PAN. TDS will be deducted at 20% if PAN 
is not linked to Aadhaar. For the purpose of identifying non- linking of PAN wit h Aadhaar, 
CBDT is providing an online utility via the reporting portal, the company is using the same 
to identify inoperative PAN. 
 
3.  Shareholders holding shares under multiple accounts under different status / category and 
single PAN, may note that higher of the tax as applicable to the status in which shares held 
under a PAN will be considered on their entire holding in different accounts.
