1FY 2025-26 Key Highlights Consolidated financial results

Quarter/half year ended September 30, 2026, at their meeting held in Mumbai on Friday, October 23, 2026.

Inclusion Limited (BFIL), a business correspondent (BC) of the Bank involved in originating small ticket

the Bank.

ASSET QUALITY  
Gross NPA were at 2.73% of gross advances as on September 30, 2026, as against 3.25% as on June 30, 
2026. Net NPA were 0.83% of net advances as on September 30, 2026, as compared to 0.95% as on June 
30, 2026. 
 
The Provision Coverage Ratio was at 70.19 % as at September 30, 2026. Provisions and contingencies (other 
than tax) for the Quater ended September 30, 2026, were ₹1,223 crores as compared to ₹ 2,631 crores for 
the corresponding quarter of previous year.  
  
 
CAPITAL ADEQUACY 
   
The Bank’s Total Capital Adequacy Ratio as per Basel III guidelines stands at 16.59 % as on September 30, 
2026, as compared to 17.10% as on September 30, 2025. Tier 1 CRAR was at 15.55% as on September 30, 
2026, compared to 15.88% as on September 30, 2025. Risk-Weighted Assets were at ₹4,25,746 crores as 
against ₹ 3,98,256 crores a year ago. 
 
 
 
 
NETWORK 
 
As of September 30, 2026, the Bank’s distribution network included 3,139 branches/ Banking outlets and 
2,773 onsite and offsite ATMs, as against 3,116 branches/banking outlets and 3,054 onsite and offsite ATMs 
as of September 30, 2025. The client base stood at approx. 42 million as on September, 2026.

Commenting on the performance, Mr. Rajiv Anand, the MD and CEO, IndusInd Bank  said: 
“Despite a challenging global environment, India’s macroeconomic fundamentals remained resilient 
supported prudent policy decisions, creating a constructive backdrop for the banking sector. Against this 
backdrop, Q2 reflects the growing strength of the IndusInd Bank franchise and the progress we have made 
in rebuilding momentum across the business. Our Q2 results demonstrate that the growth has strengthened, 
profitability has improved, asset quality trends remain encouraging, and our funding profile is becoming 
more granular and resilient. It is also encouraging to see increasing traction across our retail businesses, 
alongside continued improvement in our liability franchise. These outcomes reflect the work undertaken 
over the past year to strengthen our leadership team, enhance governance and risk standards, and invest in 
technology and operating capabilities. With healthy capital and liquidity, improving momentum across 
businesses, and a clear focus on disciplined execution, we are entering the second half with confidence and 
remain committed to delivering sustainable growth, stronger profitability and improved returns over time”. 
 
ABOUT INDUSIND BANK 
 
IndusInd Bank Limited has been redefining banking for the past 32 years and has been a force for progression 
and innovation, offering an elevated banking experience for its diverse range of stakeholders, including 
government entities, PSUs, retail customers, and large corporations. The Bank’s product offerings include 
microfinance, personal loans, debit/credit cards, SME loans, advanced digital banking facilities, affluent 
and NRI banking services, vehicle financing, and innovative ESG-linked financial products. 
 
The Bank also caters to the growing Indian diaspora with representative offices in Dubai, and Abu Dhabi. 
As of September 30, 2026, IndusInd Bank serves around 42 million customers through 3,139 
branches/banking outlets and 2,773 ATMs, reaching 1.54 lakh villages across India. IndusInd Bank 
leverages technology through its 'Digital 2.0' strategy, ensuring multi-channel delivery and a robust digital 
infrastructure. In each of its unique offerings that include ‘INDIE’ – the one-stop-shop for all things digital 
banking; innovation and customer centricity remain at the core. IndusInd Bank holds clearing bank status 
for major stock exchanges BSE and NSE, settlement bank status for NCDEX, and is an empanelled banker 
for MCX.
