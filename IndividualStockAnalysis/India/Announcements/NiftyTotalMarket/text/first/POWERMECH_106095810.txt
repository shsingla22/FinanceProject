We are delighted to announce that our Company has secured a ₹186 Crore (excluding GST) order from 
Jaiprakash Power Ventures Limited (JPVL). This order entails providing Field Operation and Maintenance services 
for the 2 x 660MW Jaypee Nigrie Super Thermal Power Plant located in Nigrie , Madhya Pradesh, over a five-year 
period starting from January 1, 2025, to December 31, 2029. 
 The contract value/ month for the first year would be ₹3.10 crores per month and thereafter, a monthly price 
escalation, based on the Wholesale Pric e Index (WPI), shall be applicable for the remaining period of 48 months 
from 01.01.2026 to 31.12.2029.  
The details as required under the SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/ CIR/P/ 2023/120 dated July 11, 
2023, are given in the enclosed as Annexure-I.
