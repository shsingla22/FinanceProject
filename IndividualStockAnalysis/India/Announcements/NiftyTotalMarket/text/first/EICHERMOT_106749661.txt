Pursuant to SEBI Circular HO/38/13/11(2)2026-MIRSD-POD/ 
1/3750/2026 dated January 30, 2026, this public advertisement is issued 
to inform that a Special Window has been made available for a period of 
one year from February 05, 2026 to February 04, 2027 to facilitate the 
transfer and dematerialization of physical shares of the Company 
purchased or sold prior to April 01, 2019, which were either not lodged 
previously with the Company/ Registrar and Share Transfer Agent (RTA)

furnish necessary documents, duly complete in all respects, to the RTA.

issued in dematerialization mode and shall be locked in for one year from 
the date of registration of transfer, in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

from this facility.
