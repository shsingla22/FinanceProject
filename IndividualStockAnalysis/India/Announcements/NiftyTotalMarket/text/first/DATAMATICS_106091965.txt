Datamatics and IIT Bombay Empower 
Future Innovators through Hackathon at 
Techfest 2024 -25  
23rd December 2024 , Mumbai:  Datamatics, a global leader in Digital Technologies, 
Operations, and Experien ces, successfully concluded Datamatics Hackathon 2024/25 
in collaboration with IIT Bombay at Techfest, Asia's largest technology festival, for the 
second consecutive year. This year over 500 tech enthusiasts from  well-known 
educational institutes across In dia came  together to showcase their creativity, solve 
real-world challenges, and present innovative solutions.  
The Hackathon provided participants with practical experience using Datamatics’ AI -
powered tools, TruCap+ for Intelligent Document Processing an d TruBot for Intelligent 
Automation, empowering  them with industry -ready skills in Artificial Intelligence, Data 
Analytics, and Digital Transformation.   
The competition showcased remarkable talent, and amongst over 500 young 
innovators, top 10 teams were chosen for displaying Creat ivity and effort s in the Grand 
Finale . Among the winners, Team SIMON from Keshav Memorial Institute of 
Technology, Hyderabad , impressed with an innovative project that summarized 
emails and extracted key details using Datamatics’  advanced automation tools, TruBot 
and TruCap+. Team SOPPY MAIL from the National Institute of Technology, Goa,  
developed a cutting -edge solution for automating customer email feedback analysis 
by leveraging TruBot Designer and TruCap+, making customer ins ights faster, smarter, 
and more actionable. Team INVALID from Cummins College of Engineering for 
Women, Pune,  created an impactful project focused on automating real -time email 
feedback processing, extracting valuable insights, and delivering instant, acti onable 
results to decision -makers. Their innovative use of Datamatics TruBot and TruCap+ 
demonstrated their ingenuity and technical expertise. The top three teams will be 
awarded cash prizes and internship opportunities to further enhance their journey in 
innovation.  
Mitul Meht a, EVP and CMO, Datamatics, expressed, “Partnering with IIT Bombay 
has been a great opportunity to help nurture future tech leaders. By engaging with 
young minds and introducing them to cutting -edge intelligent automation products, 
we have empowered them to work on real -life projects using TruBot and TruCap+. This 
Hackathon offers an excellent platform for young innovators t o turn their ideas into 
reality and bridge  the gap betwee n academia and industry. We extend our sincere 
thanks to  IIT Bombay for making this happen through the Techfest platform. ” 
 
Additionally, Shashi Bhargava, EVP & Head of Intelligent Automation Products  at 
Datamatics, participated in an exclusive panel discussion on Data Privacy & Security 
with AI at Techfest. During the session, he shared valuable insights on the responsible 
use of technology and explored how AI -powered solutions can help address 
organizational concerns related to data privacy and security.  
Datamatics is committed to fostering innovation and co nnecting with the next 
generation of technologists. For more details about the Datamatics Hackathon 
2024/25 : https://techfest.org/competitions/datamatics%20hackathon
