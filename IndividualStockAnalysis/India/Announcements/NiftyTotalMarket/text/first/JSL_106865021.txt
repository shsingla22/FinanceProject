Jindal Stainless secures three recognitions at ISA Steel Conclave 2026 for gender 
diversity and process safety  
• Two of Jindal Stainless’ employees were recognised with Gender Diversity awards while Mundra 
plant won for p revention of fire and explosion in Pickling - Electrolysis section . 
National, November 18, 2026:  Jindal Stainless, India's largest stainless steel manufacturer, has received 
three recognitions at the Indian Steel Association (ISA) Steel Conclave 2026, including two honours for 
the contributions of its women employees in core manufacturing and engineering roles . In a first for the 
company, two employees have been honoured in the same edition . Ms Laxmi Hembram received a Jury 
Special Mention – Gender Diversity Icon Award while Ms Nidhi Gupta won in the ISA Gender Diversity 
Award s category . The company’s Mundra unit also won the ISA Safety Award in Process Safety category 
for prevention of fire and explosion in Pickling - Electrolysis section.  
The recognitions reflect two important aspects of Jindal Stainless' manufacturing operations  – its focus 
on developing a skilled and diverse workforce, and its emphasis on continuously strengthening process 
safety across its facilities.  
 
Commenting on the recognition, Managing Director, Jindal Stainless, Mr Abhyuday Jindal, said , “The 
steel industry needs to draw on the full range of talent available to it, including more women in technical, 
operational and engineering roles. Laxmi's work in operating specialised industrial machinery and Nidhi's 
contribution to engineering and automation projects show the expertise and perspective women bring to

functions that have traditionally seen limited representation  of women . At Jindal Stainless, we encourage  
women to acquire industry -relevant technical skills, gain hands -on experience with industrial processes 
and equipment, and build careers across the manufacturing value chain.  At the same time, safety remains 
fundamental to our operations. The recognition for our Mundra plant reflects the importance we place on 
identifying process risks and strengthening safeguards to prevent incidents.  These are both essential 
aspects to building a capable and responsible manufacturing organisation. ” 
The gender diversity  recognition s highlight the expanding scope of roles women can take on in the steel 
industry. At Jajpur, Dulevo Sweeping Machine Operator Laxmi , operates specialised industrial machinery 
essential to plant operations. At Hisar, Nidhi , Associate Manager  – Hot Rolling Mills , works on electrical 
and automation systems in the Hot Rolling Mills, contributing to critical engineering projects. Both have 
built their careers in functions where women have traditionally been under -represented, taking on 
responsibilities with technical skills, and the confidence to work in demanding industrial environments.  
Ms Hembram's  career at Jindal Stainless illustrates how access to training can open up opportunities 
beyond conventional support functions. She joined the company in 2017 as a housekeeping staff member 
at its Jajpur Power Plant. She was subsequently selected under the  company's Women Empowerment 
Driving Scheme and trained to operate heavy industrial vehicles. After obtaining her driving licence, she 
took on the role of Assistant Driver and gained hands -on experience operating the Dulevo  sweeping 
machine alongside its senior operator. Thereafter, she volunteered to take independent charge of the 
machine, showcasing her confidence and it marked a significant shift in the responsibilities she handles 
and the skills she has developed.  
Ms Gupta has built a 13 -year career in electrical and automation engineering in Hot Rolling Mills at Hisar. 
She joined Jindal Stainless in 2013 as an Executive Engineer Trainee and was the only woman engineer on 
the shop floor at the time. Over the years, sh e has worked on several key technical projects, including the 
Hot Strip Mill Modification, implementation of JBS rolling with Laminar Cooling, Fuel Automation using 
TIA Portal and in -house HMI commissioning. The Fuel Automation project involved automating fuel 
transfer across key furnaces, helping improve process control, operational safety and energy efficiency. 
In addition to her technical responsibilities, Ms Gupta serves as Chairperson of the Internal Complaints 
Committee at the Hisar facility.  
The ISA Safety Award recognises t he Mundra plant's interventions to prevent fire and explosion risks in 
the Pickling –Electrolysis section. These include real -time temperature monitoring of anodes and rectifiers, 
interlocking of rectifiers with bus bar temperature monitoring, and real -time monitoring of oil and winding 
temperatures in transformers. The plant also replaced PPH tank covers at the Neolite  tank with SS304 
stainless steel covers.  Together, these measures strengthen process safeguards and help mitigate 
potential fire and explosion hazards in the section.  
These recognitions are part of Jindal Stainless' broader focus on strengthening its manufacturing 
capabilities through people development and operational improvements. Initiatives such as the Women 
Empowerment Driving Scheme support women's participation i n plant operations by providing access to 
specialised  training and new career opportunities. Across its facilities, the company also continues to 
strengthen process safety through targeted interventions and improvements to plant systems and 
infrastructure.

About Jindal Stainless  
India’s leading stainless steel  manufacturer, Jindal Stainless, had an annual turnover of INR 42,955 crore (USD 4.86 
billion) in FY26 and an annual melt capacity of 4.2 million tonnes. It has 16 stainless steel manufacturing and 
processing facilities in India and abroad, including in Sp ain and Indonesia, and a worldwide network in 12 countries, 
as of March 2026. In India, there are ten sales offices and six service centres, as of March 2026. The company’s 
product range includes stainless steel slabs, blooms, coils, plates, sheets, precis ion strips, wire rods, rebars, blade 
steel, and coin blanks.  
 
Jindal Stainless relies on its integrated operations to enhance its cost competitiveness and operational efficiency. 
Founded in 1970, Jindal Stainless continues to be inspired by a vision for innovation and enriching lives and is 
committed to social respon sibility.  
 
Jindal Stainless remains focused towards achieving net -zero  greenhouse gas emissions by 2050 . The company is 
advancing initiatives in renewable energy, green hydrogen and increased scrap utilisation to support its transition 
towards more sustainable manufacturing.
