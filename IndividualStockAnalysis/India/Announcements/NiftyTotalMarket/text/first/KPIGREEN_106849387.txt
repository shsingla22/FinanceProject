Apropos the captioned subject and pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that KPI Green Energy 
Limited (“the Company”) has energized further capacity of 49.7 MWp  under its 250 MW / 350 
MWp Solar Power Project (IPP) and 121.8 MWp  under its 370 MW / 677 MWp Hybrid Power Project 
(IPP) at Bharuch, Gujarat.  
 
With the energizing of the aforesaid capacity, the Company has cumulatively energize d 300.8 
MWp out of 350 MWp  capacity under the said Solar IPP Project and 510.7 MWp out of 677 MWp  
capacity under the said Hybrid IPP Project, comprising 449.2 MWp of solar  and 61.5 MW of wind  
capacity. These projects are being developed by the Company as an Independent Power 
Producer (IPP)  under long -term Power Purchase Agreements with Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam 
Limited (GUVNL) . 
 
The Company continues to steadily expand its Independent Power Producer (IPP) portfolio, as it 
simultaneously scales its EPC business. The progressive energization of capacity under long -
term PPAs further strengthens the Company's annuity -based, recurring revenue streams, 
enhancing earnings visibility and creating sustained long -term value for its stakeholders.
