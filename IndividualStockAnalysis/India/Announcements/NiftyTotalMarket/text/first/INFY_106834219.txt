PRESS RELEASE 
Winners of Qorus- Infosys Finacle Banking Innovation Awards 2026 Announced  
The 13th edition of awards recognized 37 financial institutions across ten categories for next-gen 
banking innovations  
Bengaluru, India and Paris, France – October 29, 2026:  Infosys Finacle, part of EdgeVerve Systems , a 
wholly -owned subsidiary of Infosys  (NSE, BSE, NYSE: INFY) and Qorus , a global association for retail 
financial institutions, announced the winners of the 13th edition of the Qorus -Infosys Finacle Banking 
Innovation Awards . Winners were felicitated in an award ceremony, conducted as part of the Infosys Finacle 
Conclave  2026, the company’s annual leadership conference for the banking and financial services 
industry, in Rome, Italy.  
  
In this year’s edition of the awards, 538 projects were submitted for evaluation by 206 financial institutions 
from 68 countries. Winners were selected across ten categories: Business Model Innovation, Product and 
Service Innovation, Business Banking Innov ation, Predictive, AI -led Value Creation, Operational 
Transformation, Customer Experience Innovation, Social, Sustainable & Responsible Banking Innovation, Modern Tech Innovation of the Year, Neobank and Fintech Innovation, and Transformative Innovator of the 
Year. The selection process involved voting by a community of industry peers combined with evaluation by an expert jury panel of 81 senior global banking executives.  
  
The complete list of the recognized banks and details of their award -winning banking innovations is 
available here.  
 
John Berry, Chairman, Qorus  said,  “This year’s edition was an inspiring demonstration of innovation 
across the banking industry, bringing together institutions from around the world to showcase initiatives that 
are shaping the future of financial services. The evening was filled wit h valuable insights and clearly 
demonstrated that we are at the dawn of a new era, where AI is becoming an integral part of nearly every initiative —transforming them and pushing the boundaries of what is possible.”  
  
Sajit Vijayakumar, Chief Executive Officer, Infosys Finacle,  said, “Across the world, banking is evolving 
for an era where intelligence is pervasive, engagements are real time, and value is increasingly created 
across connected ecosystems. In response, leading institutions are transforming how they engage, 
operate,  and grow.  At Infosys Finacle, we inspire better banking by helping turn these ambitions into 
technology -led innovation that delivers meaningful outcomes for people, businesses, and communities. The 
2026 Qorus –Infosys Finacle Banking Innovation Awards showcase how banks are moving beyond 
experimentation and applying AI, data, digital platforms, and new business models to generate measurable 
  
  
impact at scale. We are proud to celebrate this year's winners at Finacle Conclave 2026 as they set new 
benchmarks for the industry and help define the next era of financial services.”  
 
About Infosys Finacle  
Finacle is an industry leader in digital banking solutions. We are a unit of EdgeVerve Systems, a wholly -
owned product subsidiary of Infosys (NSE, BSE, NYSE: INFY). We partner with emerging and established 
financial institutions to help inspire better banking. Our cloud- native solution suite and SaaS services help 
banks engage, innovate, operate, and transform better to scale digital transformation with confidence. 
Finacle solutions address the core banking, lending, digital engagement, payments, cash management, 
wealth management, treasury, analytics, AI, and blockchain requirements of financial institutions. Today, 
banks in over 100 countries rely on Finacle to help more than a billion people and millions of businesses to 
save, pay, borrow, and invest better.  For more information, visit  www.finacle.com .
