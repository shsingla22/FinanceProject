Vitreous Alliance Chooses Aurionpro to Launch Secure 
Omnichannel Payments at Scale  
 
Cloud -native, API -first payments infrastructure reinforces Aurionpro’s  position as the 
platform of choice for U.S. fintechs  
 
Mumbai / Austin – January 23 , 2026 : Aurionpro, an enterprise technology leader, today 
announced that Vitreous Alliance, LLC, a leading provider of retail POS and payment services 
in the U .S., has selected Aurionpro to expand beyond traditional in -store transactions and 
enable faster, more transparent, and secure payments across multiple channels.   
  
  
The Aurionpro Payments suite  is purpose -built for banks, fintechs, and PSPs. With a cloud -
native  foundation, modular API -first design, and buil t-in support for surcharging and dual 
pricing, it simplifies compliance and integration while unifying multiple payment methods and 
payment rails in a single environment.  
  
Key c apabilities of the solution  
 High -Volume, Cloud -Native Performance:  Engineered for enterprise -scale 
transaction processing, ensuring reliability and speed during peak demand.  
 Fully White -Labeled Solutions:  Enables partners to maintain brand control with 
private label hosted forms, emb eddable payment widgets, and comprehensive mobile 
SDKs.  
 Simplified Compliance & Security:  Reduces risk and st reamlines audits with a PCI 
DSS –certified framework, network tokenization, and comprehensive security features 
built-in. 
 Advanced Pricing and Reven ue Controls:  Provides the flexibility to implement 
sophisticated pricing models, including surcharging and dual pricing, to optimize 
revenue and navigate complex regulations.  
 Open API for Seamless Integration:  An API -first architecture designed to connect 
effortlessly with CRMs, ERPs, and other third -party systems, creating a unified 
commercial ecosystem.   
 
For Vitreous, this means faster merchant onboarding, stronger payments control, and a
