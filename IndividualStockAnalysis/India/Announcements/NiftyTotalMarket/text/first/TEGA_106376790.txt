Dear Madam/Sir, 
 
In continuation to our letter dated September 15, 2025, and pursuant to Regulation 30 read with 
Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended from time to time (“ SEBI Listing Regulations ”), we 
would like to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Company (“ Board”) has, at its meeting held 
today, September 18, 2025, have inter alia approved  the following: 
 
1. The creation, issue, and offer of 100,33,090 equity shares of the Company, of face value of Rs. 
10/- (Rupees Ten) each fully paid up (“ Equity Shares ”), through preferential issue on private 
placement basis, for cash consideration, to certain identified investors (hereinafter individually 
or collectively referred to as “ Proposed Allottees ”), as mentioned in Table A, at an issue price 
of Rs. 1,994 /- (including premium of Rs. 1,984/-) per Equity Share, aggregating up to Rs. 
2000.60 Crores. In accordance with Chapter V of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 ( “SEBI ICDR Regulations” ), 
as amended and other applicable laws, rules and regulations, and subject to the approval of 
the regulatory/ statutory authorities and the shareholders of the Company (“ Preferential 
Issue”). In this regard, the Board has also noted and taken on record the Expression of Interest 
(EOI) Letters provided by the Proposed Allottees to the Company; and  
 
2. Convening an Extra-Ordinary General Meeting (EGM) of the Company on Friday, October 10, 
2025, at 11:00 a.m. at Mini Auditorium, Science City, Kolkata, J.B.S Haldane Avenue, Kolkata, 
West Bengal 700046, to seek necessary approval of the shareholders of the Company, for the 
Preferential Issue.
