Sub:  Intimation of Temporary Shutdown of 60 MW Power Plant at Odisha to carry out  
routine maintenance / overhauling  
 
Ref:  Reg. 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations  
 
We wish to inform you that the Company’s energy operations in the State of Odisha comprise 
three generating units with an aggregate capacity of 150 MW, consisting of Two 60 MW  IPP Units 
and One 30 MW CPP Unit. 
 
In accordance with the recommendations of the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) and 
prevailing industry best practices , the Company has planned a temporary shutdown of “One of its 
60 MW IPP Units”  commencing from September 06, 2026, to  undertake routine inspection, 
maintenance and overhauling activities in compliance with applicable safety and regulatory 
requirements.  
 
The shutdown is expected to continue for about  55 to 60 days and is scheduled during a period of 
relatively lower thermal power production requirements due to greater availability of hydel power 
and consequent lower tariffs.  Accordingly, the Company does not expect any material adverse 
impact on its business operations or overall performance  on account of th is shutdown.  
 
The Company will keep the Stock Exchanges informed of any material developments and will 
promptly intimate the Stock Exchanges upon resumption of operations.  
 
This is for your information and records.
