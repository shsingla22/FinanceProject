PRESS RELEASE  
Hyderabad, India –  27th October,  2026  
MIDHANI signs MoU with HWB and BARC  for recovery of Nickel and Cobalt from plant reverts  
In a significant step towards promoting resource recovery, sustainable manufacturing, and the 
circular economy, MISHRA DHATU NIGAM LIMITED (MIDHANI),  Hyderabad under Ministry of 
Defence, HEAVY WATER BOARD (HWB), and BHABHA ATOMIC RESEARCH CENTRE (BARC), 
Mumbai both under Department of Atomic Energy, have signed a Memorandum of 
Understanding (MoU) to collaborate on the production and recovery of cobalt and nickel from 
plant reverts on 27th October 2026.  
This MoU establishes a framework for cooperation among the three organizations, leveraging 
their respective expertise, technological capabilities, and industrial resources to explore and 
implement efficient processes for recovering valuable metals from plant reverts. This initiative 
aims to maximise resource utilisation, reduce dependence on primary mineral resources, and 
promote environmentally responsible metal production.  
Cobalt and nickel are critical metals widely used in advanced battery technologies,  electric 
vehicles, stainless steels, superalloys, and other strategic industrial applications. Recovering 
these metals from plant reverts offers significant potential to strengthen domestic supply chains, 
improve resource efficiency, and support the growing demand for critical metals. By recovering 
cobalt and nickel from secondary sources, this collaboration seeks to unlock value from plant 
reverts that might otherwise remain under utilised and contribute to a more resilient and 
sustainable supply of criti cal metals.  
Speaking on the occasion, Dr. S V S Narayana Murty, Chairman & Managing Director, MIDHANI 
said, “The signing of this MoU marks an important milestone in our efforts to promote the 
recovery of critical metals from secondary resources. Through th is collaboration, we aim to 
combine our strengths and develop viable, sustainable, and commercially competitive solutions 
for the production of cobalt and nickel from plant reverts.” The MoU was signed by Dr. S V S 
Narayana Murty, Chairman & Managing Direc tor, MIDHANI, Shri V.V.S.A. Prasad, Chairman and 
Chief Executive Heavy Water Board and Dr. Alok Awasthi, Associate Director, Materials Group, 
BARC. This event was also attended by Smt. K Madhubala, Director (Finance), MIDHANI and Shri 
Sandeep Wadhwa, Execu tive Director, HWB.  
These three organizations expressed confidence that the collaboration would create new 
opportunities in metal recovery and recycling, support the circular economy, and contribute to 
the long -term availability of critical raw materials f or India's growing industrial and clean -energy 
sectors.
