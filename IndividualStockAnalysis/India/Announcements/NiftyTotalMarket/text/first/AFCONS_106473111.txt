Afcons adjudged India’s `Most Innovative Company’ for 2025 
Mumbai, December 19, 2025:  Afcons Infrastructure Limited has emerged as India’s most 
innovative company , clinching two top honours at the CII Industrial Innovation Awards 2025 . 
It received the Grand Award for Top Innovative Company  (across all sectors and amongst all 
categories) and Top Innovative Company in the Service Category (Large) for its Incredible 
Engineering.  
These accolades reaffirm Afcon’s reputation as a pioneer in engineering excellence and 
innovation-driven infrastructure solutions , setting new benchmarks for the industry. 
The recognition comes after a rigorous three-stage evaluation  wherein over 900 leading 
organisations and multinational corporations participated for the award . The process 
included an online application, assessor-led evaluation, and a final review by a Grand Jury 
comprising Directors of IITs & IIMs, industry stalwarts, and international experts . Afcon’s 
cutting-edge innovations stood out during intense deliberations at this prestigious forum. 
In addition to this recognition Afcons has the distinction of achieving `Global’ recognition as the 
Most Innovative Knowledge Enterprise  (MIKE Award)  for 8 years in a row, awarded by a 
distinguished global jury.  Both, this recent CII Industrial Innovation recognition and MIKE award in 
2025, are a testament to the company’s emphasis on innovation in every project it undertakes.
