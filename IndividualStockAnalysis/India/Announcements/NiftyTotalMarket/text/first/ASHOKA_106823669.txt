Resurgent ESG Services Assigns ‘A+’ ESG Rating (Score 79.44) to Ashoka 
Buildcon, Recognising Strength of Its Sustainability and Governance 
Practices  
Press Release  
Delhi/NCR, October 21, 2026:  Resurgent ESG Services Private Limited (Resurgent ESG), a 
wholly owned subsidiary of Resurgent India Limited and a SEBI -registered Category -II ESG 
Rating Provider (ERP), has assigned an ESG score of 79.44 to Ashoka Buildcon Limited 
(ABL) based on data for  FY 2025 -26. The score corresponds to an ‘A+’ ESG Rating, placing 
Ashoka Buildcon within the ‘Leader’ category, recognising its ongoing efforts towards 
strengthening environmental, social and governance practices.  
Ashoka Buildcon Limited, one of India’s leading highway developers and infrastructure 
companies, was evaluated through Resurgent ESG’s comprehensive ESG assessment 
framework. The assessment considered the company’s environmental and social impacts 
arising from its infrastructure development activities, alongside the strength of its 
governance practices and their role in supporting responsible business conduct and 
sustainable value creation. ABL undertakes infrastructure development through 
Engineering, Proc urement and Construction (EPC), Build -Operate -Transfer (BOT), and 
Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM) projects.  
Commenting on the rating, Mr. Sanjeet Kumar, Director, Resurgent ESG Services Pvt. Ltd., 
said, “For infrastructure companies such as Ashoka Buildcon Limited, ESG performance is 
increasingly becoming an important indicator of how responsibly and resiliently  a business 
operates. A strong ESG framework brings together governance, responsible resource 
management, occupational health and safety, environmental stewardship and meaningful 
stakeholder engagement. As infrastructure projects have a direct and lasting impact on 
communities and the environment, integrating ESG considerations into planning and 
execution is critical to managing risks and creating sustainable long -term value. The 
assessment also provides companies with an opportunity to identify areas for 
improvement and strengthen their sustainability practices and disclosures in line with 
evolving stakeholder and regulatory expectations, including the Securities and Exchange 
Board of India’s Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting framework.”  
A key feature of Resurgent ESG’s assessment methodology is its combination of data -
driven analysis and direct stakeholder engagement. The framework goes beyond publicly 
available disclosures by incorporating direct engagement with key stakeholders within t he 
rated entity. This approach enables the assessment to consider both quantitative and 
qualitative factors while developing a comprehensive view of an organisation’s 
sustainability profile.  

The 79.44 ESG score and A+ rating provide a structured assessment of ABL’s current ESG 
profile while highlighting the importance of continued progress in sustainability, 
responsible business practices and governance. As ESG considerations become 
increasing ly relevant to investors, lenders, regulators and other stakeholders, such 
assessments can help organisations better understand their sustainability performance 
and identify opportunities for further improvement.
