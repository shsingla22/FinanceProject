PRESS RELEASE 
NLCIL'S BIGGEST MINES -MACHHAKATA COAL BLOCK 
VESTING ORDER ISSUED 
NLCIL's Second Commercial Coal Block and the Biggest among the NLCIL's 
Mining Projects, Machhakata Coal Block's Vesting Order was issued today 
(05.09.2024) by Addi. Secretary (Coal) & the Nominated Authority, Ministry of Coal 
(MoC), Govt. of India to Dr. Suresh Chandra Suman, Director (Mines) and Director 
(P&P A/C), NLCIL at Shastribhavan, MOC, New Delhi. 
This Coal Mine is having reserves of 1.4 BT, with average Grade of G10 -G11, 
and capacity of 30 MTPA, which is expected to be one among the Top Five Biggest 
Mines in India. Today marks the zero date for development of this Mine as per MoC 
timeline. NLCIL aims to commence the mining ahead of the schedule. This marks 
another milestone towards realising NLCIL's Corporate Plan 2030. 
Shri. Prasanna Kumar Motupalli, Chairman and Managing Director, NLC India 
Limited congratulated Dr. Suresh Chandra Suman, Director (Mines) and Director 
(P&P A/C) and Team NLCIL for these outstanding achievements and advised to 
take all necessary steps to bring the Mine into operations at the earliest.
