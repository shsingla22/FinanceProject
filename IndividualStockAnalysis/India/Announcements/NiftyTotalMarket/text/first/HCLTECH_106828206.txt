Rapidus and  HCLTech  announce partnership for next -generation  semiconductor 
technologies  
 
TOKYO and NOIDA, India, Oct. 26, 2026:  HCL Tech , a leading global technology company, and 
Rapidus Corporation , a Japanese company developing  and manufacturing advanced logic 
semiconductors, today officially inaugurated two HCL Tech development center s in India 
dedicated to supporting Rapidus ’ evolving semiconductor engineering requirements.  
 
The center s, located in Bengal uru and Hubli , will leverage HCL Tech’s  global leadership in  core 
engineering to offer Rapidus  a range of semiconductor engineering capabilities, including 
mask design, design infrastructure, process design kit development , design technology co-
optimization  and layout design.  
 
HCL Tech will also collaborate with Rapidus to build a pipeline of Indian engineering talent to 
meet Rapidus’ evolving talent requirements in Japan.  
 
“Building a strong engineering ecosystem is essential as Rapidus advances our mission to 
establish next -generation semiconductor  manufacturing  capabilities in Japan, ” said Dr. 
Atsuyoshi Koike, Representative Director  and CEO, Rapidus Corporation. “Through this 
collaboration with HCL Tech, we expect to access India’s growing pool of semiconductor 
engineering talent and establish dedicated development capabilities that complement our 
operations in Japan. We look forward to working together to develop the techn ical expertise 
and resources needed to support our long -term objectives.”  
 
"Advanced semiconductors will define the technologies of the next decade," said C 
Vijayakumar, CEO and Managing Director , HCL Tech. "This partnership reflects the growing 
strategic collaboration between Japan and India, bringing together Rapidus' next -generation 
semiconductor ambitions with HCL Tech's engineering expertise to accelerate chip adoption, 
compress time -to-market an d lay the foundations for the next era of semiconductor 
innovation."
