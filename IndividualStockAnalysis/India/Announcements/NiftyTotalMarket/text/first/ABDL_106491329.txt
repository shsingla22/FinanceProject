Scotch Whisky Veteran  Martin Leonard Appointed to Advise Allied Blenders and 
Distillers on Single Malt Foray  
 
Mumbai, 8th January  2026  – Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD), India’s largest domestic spirits 
company by volume, today announced the appointment of globally renowned Scotch whisky specialist Dr. 
Martin Leonard as Consultant . In this role, he is advis ing the company on commissioning of the Malt 
Distillery  and on its single malt whisky initiative, further strengthening ABD’s technical expertise as it 
advances its  premium whisky portfolio . 
Martin brings over three decades of experience in the global Scotch whisky industry, covering distillation, 
maturation, blending, bottling, and supply chain operations. He most recently served as Managing Director 
of Inver House Distillers, where he led five sing le malt distilleries in Scotland and oversaw large -scale 
whisky  operations , including inventories of more than 600,000 casks.  
Martin  has also served as a Non -Executive Director  and Chairman  of the Scotch Whisky Research Institute 
and is a recipient of the Master of the Quaich. He holds a PhD and a BSc (Hons) in Agricultural Chemistry 
from the University of Glasgow and has previously held senior roles with United Distillers ( now  Diageo) 
and BAT plc. As Consultant, he will work with ABD’s leadership and technical teams to support the 
development and long -term direction of its single malt business, with a focus on strengthening 
manuf acturing capability  and process quality.  

 
Currently, the construction of the single malt distillery is in progress and is expected to be commissioned 
by Q4 FY26. This is part of the already announced backward integration investment programs, including 
PET bottle manufacturing, which has already been commissioned in Telangana in September 2025, ENA 
distillation capacity expans ion in Maharashtra, and India’s first single malt distillery in Rangapur , Telangana . 
Strategically located within the largest integrated alcobev facility in Telangana which also houses an ENA 
distillery and an IMFL bottling unit, the Rangapur -based single malt distillery is being developed with an 
investment of ~₹75 Cr and a ~4.0 MLPA capacity to meet the current requirement of ~2.0 MLPA for 
blending and future single malt whiskies.  
On the appointment , Alok Gupta, Managing Director, ABD,  said, “Allied Blenders and Distillers has taken 
important steps in building its presence in India’s premium whisky segment. As we move into the next 
phase of our single malt journey, Martin’s role will be critical in strengthening our technical depth and 
operatio nal capabilities. His extensive experience across malt whisky production, maturation, and large -
scale operations brings valuable expertise to our team. We believe his guidance will play an impo rtant role 
in shaping a robust, high -quality single malt business aligned with our long -term growth ambitions.”  
Sharing his thoughts on the appointment , Martin Leonard , Consultant, ABD , said , “I am pleased to be 
associated with Allied Blenders and Distillers at this key stage of its growth  strategy . I look forward to 
working closely with the leadership and technical teams to contribute my experience in malt whisky 
production and operations. I see a strong opportunity to support the development of  a high -quality and 
sustainable single malt business at ABD.”  
The appointment of  Martin  marks an important step as ABD progresses its single malt plans and prepares 
to advance this business through its next phase of development.  
 
About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited ( www.abdindia.com ) 
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD) is the largest domestic Spirits company in India, in terms of annual 
sales volumes. ABD has a presence in five main flavors, i.e., whisky, brandy, rum, vodka, and gin, with 
‘millionaire’ brands like Officer’s Ch oice Whisky, Officer’s Choice Blue Whisky, Sterling Reserve Premium 
Whiskies and ICONiQ White Whisky. Currently, its manufacturing network comprises 37 units, of which 9 are 
owned bottling units, 2 owned distilleries, and 26 non -owned manufacturing units.
