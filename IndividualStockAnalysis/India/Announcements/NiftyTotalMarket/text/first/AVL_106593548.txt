Dear Sir  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we hereby  inform you that the Company has appointed Mr. Pankaj Tripathi 
as its Brand Ambassador for a period of one (1) year.  
 
This marks the first instance of the Company onboarding a brand ambassador, with the 
objective of strengthening customer engagement and bringing its offerings closer to 
consumers.  Mr. Tripathi, known for his strong connect with audiences of Hindi Heartland and 
credible persona, is expected to enhance the visibility of the Company and its products and 
support its outreach across key markets.  
 
The said association aligns with the Company’s strategic focus on deepening customer 
relationships and is expected to support its marketing and communication initiatives during 
the tenure of the engagement.
