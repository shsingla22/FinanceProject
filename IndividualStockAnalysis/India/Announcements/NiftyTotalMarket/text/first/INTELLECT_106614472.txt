FY26 represents a structural shift in how financial institutions are deploying AI. The centerpiece of Intellect’s competitive  
moat is the compounding synergy between its two innovation engines:  
 
● eMACH.ai:  The world's most comprehensive, composable, and intelligent Open Finance platform, featuring 700

● Purple Fabric: The world’s first Open Business Impact AI platform powered by 550+ domain -aware AI agents,

enterprise AI platforms designed to accelerate intelligent automation, contextual decisioning, and enterprise -scale AI 
adoption.  
 
● eMACH.ai AI -First Banking:  Unveiled the next wave of autonomous, context -aware banking powered by 550+ 
domain -aware AI agents spanning lending, payments, treasury, wealth, and servicing.
