TCS and Google Cloud launch Gemini Experience Center in 
Mexico to help drive AI adoption  
Integrated with agentic AI capabilities and Google Cloud use cases, the center will help 
accelerate business transformation across industries and enterprise AI adoption  
The facility marks TCS’ ninth Gemini Experience Center worldwide and second in Latin 
America  
MEXICO CITY | MUMBAI, August  11, 2026:  Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS), a global leader 
in IT services, consulting, and business solutions and Google Cloud have set up a new Gemini 
Experience Center (GEC) in Mexico City, TCS’ second in Latin America and the ninth 
worldwide. Located in TCS’ Mexico City office, the new f acility will empower organizations 
in the region with advanced AI capabilities and help  accelerate the development of 
solutions that drive innovation, productivity, and data -driven decision -making.  
The newly launched GEC is equipped with over 3,000 industry - and context -aware AI agents 
built by TCS with Gemini Enterprise that integrate seamlessly into customer environments. 
Some of these AI -led solutions were demonstrated during the launch, including  intelligent 
process assessment, fraud investigation for financial institutions, automated insurance 
claims processing, and generative AI -powered data acceleration.  
Javier Carrique, Head of Partners & Alliances for Latin America , Google Cloud , said, “As 
artificial intelligence reshapes how organizations compete and create value, unlocking its 
full potential depends on giving businesses the right environment to adopt and apply AI with 
confidence. The Gemini Experience Center in Mexico is a powerful exa mple of how our 
partner ecosystem is helping turn that vision into reality.”  
Marcelo Wurmann, CEO, TCS Latin America , said , “The Gemini Experience Center, launched 
by TCS, brings advanced AI capabilities closer to enterprises in Mexico, helping them turn 
ideas into practical, scalable solutions. Together with Google Cloud, we are creating a space 
for organizations to explore how  technology can transform their operations and add greater 
value. ” 
TCS will combine its consulting, engineering, and innovation capabilities with Google 
Cloud’s technology ecosystem at the new GEC. It will help organizations bridge the gap 
between experimentation and the implementation of AI solutions, reducing the time 
 
 
required to achieve business outcomes. The center will support the development of 
industry -specific solutions across financial services, retail and consumer goods, 
manufacturing, healthcare and life sciences, and energy and utilities, tailored to the regio n's 
unique business needs.  
The launch of the GEC in Mexico City is part of TCS’ global strategy to expand its network of 
innovation centers. It reaffirms TCS’ commitment to advancing responsible AI adoption by 
fostering collaboration among businesses, specialized talent, and technol ogy to scale digital 
transformation in the country. In 2025, TCS launched its first GEC in Latin America in São 
Paulo, Brazil.  
TCS has been present in Mexico since 2003 and today serves more than 260 clients through 
a workforce of over 12,000 associates. This new center further strengthens Mexico’s 
position as an innovation hub for Latin America. With operations in Mexico City, 
Guadalajara, Querétaro, and Monterrey, Mexico is a strategic market for TCS in Latin 
America.
