15,733 crores pertaining to periods up to 31 March 2025, as an exceptional item and 1703 crores pertaining to the nine months ended 31 
December 2025, as a reduction from revenue from operations, with a corresponding adjustment in the wholly owned subsidiary . 
The above expense has resulted in a tax benefit of 11,531 crores during the quarter ended 31 March 2026 which includes 11,317 crores for the 
period upto 31 March 2025 and 1214 crores for the nine months ended 31 December 2025.

14,416 crores for the period upto 31 March 2025 and 1489 crores for the nine months ended 31 December 2025.
