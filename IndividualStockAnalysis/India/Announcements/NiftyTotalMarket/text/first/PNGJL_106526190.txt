“The quarter witnessed strong momentum, supported byhealthy Festive and wedding -leddemand .Revenue
from operations increased 35.6%YoY to₹33,026 Mn inQ3FY26,despite gold price volatility .Demand
remained broad -based across core markets, ledbygold jewellery, new designs and anincreasing preference
forlightweight and studded jewellery, supported bystrong brand recall and customer trust .
Profitability improved significantly during thequarter, with PAT rising 98.6%YoY to₹1,709 Mn, supported by
afavorable product mix, higher contribution from Studded jewellery, along with disciplined cost
management, resulted inmeaningful margin expansion .Retail continued tobetheprimary growth driver,
complemented bystrong growth ine-commerce and steady performance inthefranchise segment .
The Company continued toexecute itsexpansion strategy with theaddition ofthree new company -owned

About  P N Gadgil  Jewellers  Limited
Established in1832 ,PNGadgil Jewellers Limited isaleading jewellery retailer inIndia with around 193 years of
heritage .The company offers awide range ofgold, silver, diamond and platinum jewellery forweddings, festivals,
and daily wear .AsofDecember 31st,2025 ,theCompany operates 66retail stores -65inIndia and 1intheU.S.A.
The portfolio also includes adedicated sub-brand, LiteStyle byPNG, which caters tothe growing demand for
stylish and lightweight jewellery .
Under Chairman &Managing Director Dr.Saurabh Gadgil, asixth -generation entrepreneur with 25+years of
experience, PNGadgil Jewellers hasevolved into acontemporary brand with strong customer trust and loyalty .
