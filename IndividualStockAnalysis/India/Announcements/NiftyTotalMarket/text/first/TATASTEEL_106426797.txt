Tata Steel Limited sign s an Asset Transfer Agreement with Indian Metals & Ferro 
Alloys Ltd. (IMFA) for the sale of its Ferro Alloy Plant at Jajpur , Odisha  
 
Mumbai, November 4, 2025: Tata Steel Limited has signed an Asset Transfer Agreement with Indian  
Metals & Ferro Alloys Ltd. (IMFA) for the sale of its Ferro Alloy Plant at Jajpur, Odisha for a base consideration 
of Rs. 610 crore.  
 
The transaction is expected to close within 3 months, after receipt of requisite approvals.
