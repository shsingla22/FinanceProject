Happiest Minds Run 2025: A United Effort with 1000+ Runners across 4 
Locations to Support Girls’ Education in STEM   
 
 
 
Bengaluru, January 22, 2025:  Happiest Minds Technologies Limited  (NSE: HAPPSTMNDS), a 
‘Born Digital . Born Agile’, Mindful IT Company, organized the fifth edition of the Happiest Minds 
Run at the Kanteerava Stadium, Cubbon Park , Bengaluru, on Sunday, 19th January, 2025.  
The Run, with over 1000 participants, was also held at three other locations (Bhubaneswar, Noida 
and Pune) on the same day. This year, an amount of ₹10 lacs was contributed to Nanhi Kali , an 
NGO supporting girls’ education in STEM. Aon, ICICI Bank, and RBL Bank  were the proud 
sponsors of the run. Beverage Partner – Hurricane and Wellness Partner – Fortis also contributed to 
the event's success.  
The event began with an energizing dance fitness session by Happiest Health that set the tone. 
Joseph officially flagged off the run at 7:00 a.m., initiating the event at Kanteerava Stadium. The 
runners ran through the 3-mile and 6-mile courses at beautiful Cubbon Park. All participants 
received medals and a tasty breakfast. 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
Joseph Anantharaju, Executive Vice Chairman, Happiest Minds , said, “As someone who values 
fitness as a cornerstone of well-being, I am excited to be part of this inspiring initiative. The 1000 
runners who have come together to support girls' education in STEM demonstrate the power of 
community and a strong commitment to this important cause.” 
 
Sachin Khurana, Chief People Officer, Happiest Minds , said, “We are happy that we saw 
participation from a large and diverse group who came together to support a united cause. This 
collaboration not only strengthens the cause itself but also fosters a sense of belonging and 
empowerment, showing that when we come together, we can create meaningful change.” 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
The collective efforts, attention to detail, and collaborative spirit of the run committee comprising 
Sachin Khurana, Dr. Kiran Veigas, Paul Jacob, Hemanta Lenka, Payal Jhatakia, Anchal Shetty, Sachi 
Jagirdar, Reshika E, Saloni Jain, and Deeksha Priya were instrumental in making this event a 
resounding success. The organizers expressed gratitude to the volunteers, sponsors, and behind-
the-scenes contributors who worked tirelessly to ensure the seamless execution of the run. 
 
Happiest Minds Run 2025 reflected the organization's belief in the importance of mindfulness, 
happiness and well-being, turning fitness into a shared celebration of personal and collective 
achievement.
