PRESS RELEASE
Aerolloy Technologies Selected by Safran
Aircraft Engines for Critical Titanium
Castings for the LEAP Engine Programme
Programme to Leverage Aerolloy’s Integrated Titanium Manufacturing
Capability under PTC ONE™ - from Melt to Mission™
Key Highlights
Development order from Safran Aircraft Engines. Aerolloy Technologies Limited, a wholly owned
subsidiary of PTC Industries Limited, has received a development order for critical Titanium cast part
numbers, fully machined, ready-to-fit supply for the LEAP engine programme.
Long-term production framework established. Aerolloy Technologies and Safran Aircraft Engines have
signed a Memorandum of Understanding establishing the commercial framework for these components.
Upon successful completion of development and qualification, the programme is expected to transition
into long-term serial production.
Bengaluru, India - October, 2026: Aerolloy Technologies Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of PTC Industries
Limited (NSE: PTCIL | BSE: 539006), today announced that it has been selected by Safran Aircraft Engines,
one of the world’s leading commercial aircraft engine manufacturers, for the development and manufacture of
critical Titanium cast components, fully machined, ready-to-fit supply for the LEAP engine programme.
This represents the second set of orders received by Aerolloy Technologies from Safran Aircraft Engines for
titanium castings under the LEAP engine programme, further strengthening and expanding the scope of the
ongoing relationship.
Aerolloy Technologies and Safran Aircraft Engines have also signed a Memorandum of Understanding
establishing the framework for long-term production supply following successful development
and qualification.
The LEAP engine is among the world’s most important commercial aero-engine programmes, powering the
Airbus A320neo, Boeing 737 MAX and COMAC C919 aircraft families. The components under this programme
comprise complex structural Titanium castings and will be supplied in a fully machined, ready-to-fit condition.

PTC ONE™ - from Melt to Mission™
The LEAP programme will be manufactured through PTC ONE™ - from Melt to Mission™, the Group’s
integrated manufacturing system for transforming advanced aerospace materials into mission-
critical components.
For this programme, Aerolloy’s role will combine Titanium material capability, precision investment casting, HIP,
vacuum heat treatment, special processes, precision machining, assembly and inspection under one
manufacturing organisation.
This integrated approach provides process continuity, quality control, traceability and supply-chain resilience
- all of which are critical for global aerospace programmes where performance, reliability and repeatability
are essential.
Chairman and Managing Director’s Statement
Mr. Sachin Agarwal, Chairman & Managing Director, PTC Industries Limited, said:
“We are grateful to Safran Aircraft Engines for the trust placed in Aerolloy Technologies and PTC Industries
through this important programme. Being selected for critical Titanium components for the LEAP engine is a
strong validation of the integrated aerospace manufacturing capability that we have been building over
several years.
The significance of this programme lies in the depth of responsibility - from Titanium material capability to
precision casting, special processes, machining, assembly and inspection - with components supplied in a
fully machined, ready-to-fit condition.”
