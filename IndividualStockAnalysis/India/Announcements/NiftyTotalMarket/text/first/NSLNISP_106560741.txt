Sub: Receipt of communication from Ministry of Steel, Government of India 
regarding appointment of Shri Anurag Kapil as Director (Finance) on the Board 
– Reg. 
 
Ref: Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. 
 
This is to inform that Ministry of Steel (MoS), Government of India vide Order No. 
14013/1/2024-BLA dated 18.03.2026 has communicated the appointment of Shri Anurag 
Kapil, IRAS, ED (Finance) – Expenditure, Railway Board to the post of Director (Finance) 
on the Board of NMDC Limited, on immediate absorption basis, for a period of 5 years 
from the date of his assumption of charge of the post, or until further orders, whichever is 
earlier.  
 
Further, MoS vide earlier Order dated 20.03.2023 had intimated that the CMD, Functional 
Directors and Government Directors of NMDC Limited will also be the CMD, Functional 
Directors and Government Directors of NMDC Steel Limited respectively on co-terminus 
basis, till further communication in this regard. 
 
Accordingly, Shri Anurag Kapil will also be Director (Finance) on the Board of NMDC Steel 
Limited, subject to assumption of the charge of the post in terms of Order dated 
18.03.2026 as referred above. 
 
The above is for kind information and dissemination.
