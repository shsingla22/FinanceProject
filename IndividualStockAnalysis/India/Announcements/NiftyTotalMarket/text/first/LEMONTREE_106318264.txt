Lemon Tree Hotels signs three new properties, across Gujarat , 
Uttarakhand  and Himachal Pradesh  
 
New Delhi and Surat , 30th July 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels announces its three 
latest signings – Lemon Tree Hotel  Surat , Lemon Tree Hotel  Rudrapur, Uttarakhand  
and Lemon Tree Resort , Dalhousie . These properties shall be managed by Carnation 
Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
 
Lemon Tree Hotel, Surat  will feature 85 well -appointed rooms, a restaurant, a 
banquet, a meeting room and recreational facilities like a swimming pool , a spa  and 
other public areas. The Surat International Airport is approximately 12 kms away 
from the property, while the Surat Railway Station is about 15 kms away. The hotel 
is well connected by roadways for both public and private transport.  
 
Lemon Tree Hotel, Rudrapur, Uttarakhand  will feature 84 well -appointed rooms, 
a restaurant, a conference hall and recreational facilities like a swimming pool, a 
fitness center  and other public areas. The Pantnagar Airport, Uttarakhand, is 
approximately 15 kms away from the property, while the Rudrapur City Railway 
Station is just 4 kms away. The hotel is well connected by roadways for both public 
and private transport.  
 
Lemon Tree Resort, Dalhousie  will feature 75 well -appointed rooms, a restaurant, 
a banquet, and recreational facilities like a swimming pool, a spa and other public 
areas. The Sri Guru Ram Das Jee International Airport, Amritsar, is approximately 
200 kms away from the property, while the Pathankot Railway Junction is about 79 
kms away. The hotel is well connected by roadways for both public and private 
transport.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Kapil Sharma, Executive Vice President - Finance & 
Chief Financial Officer, Lemon Tree Hotels, commented, "We are delighted at the 
three -pronged expansion. The launch of these new properties not only reinforces our 
presence in  these states but also aligns with our broader strategy for sustained 
growth across key markets like  Gujarat , Uttarakhand  and Himachal Pradesh ." 
  

 
 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of the largest hotel chains in India, and 
owns/leases/operates/franchises hotels across the upscale, upper -midscale, midscale 
and economy segments. It delivers differentiated yet superior service offerings, with 
a compelling value proposition. The group offers seven brands to meet guests’ needs 
across all levels, viz. Aurika Hotels & Resorts, Lemon Tree Premier, Lemon Tree 
Hotels, Red Fox Hotels by Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotels, 
Keys Select by Lem on Tree Hotels and Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels.  
 
LTH opened its first hotel with 49 rooms in May 2004. Today, t he company has a 
portfolio of 23 0+ hotels, which includes over 110 ope rational hotels and more than 
110 hotels set to open in India and internationally. Lemon Tree Hotels are located 
across India, in key metro regions including the NCR, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, 
Hyderabad and Chennai, as well as numerous tier I, II and III cities such as Pune, 
Ahmedabad,  Chandigarh, Jaipur, Indore, Aurangabad, Udaipur, Visakhapatnam, 
Kochi, Ludhiana, Thiruvanan thapuram, Vijayawada, etc. The company has also 
expanded internationally to Dubai, Bhutan and Nepal.
