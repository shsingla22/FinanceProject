PRESS RELEASE 
27th October, 2026 Vadodara, India  
 
Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited receives USFDA Final Approval for Valganciclovir for Oral 
Solution, 50 mg/mL. 
 
Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited (Alembic) today announced that it has received final approval 
from the US Food & Drug Administration (USFDA) for its Abbreviated New Drug Application 
(ANDA) for Valganciclovir for Oral Solution, 50 mg/mL. The approved ANDA is therapeutically 
equivalent to the reference listed drug product (RLD), Valcyte for Oral Solution 50 mg/mL of 
CHEPLAPHARM Arzneimittel GmbH (CHEPLAPHARM). Valganciclovir for oral solution is indicated 
for: 1) treatment of cytomegalovirus (CMV) retinitis in patients with acquired immunodeficiency 
syndrome (AIDS), and 2) prevention of CMV disease in kidney, heart, or kidney-pancreas 
transplant patients at high risk. Refer label for a detailed indication and usage. 
 
Alembic has a cumulative total of 247 ANDA approvals (227 final approvals and 20 tentative 
approvals) from the USFDA.
