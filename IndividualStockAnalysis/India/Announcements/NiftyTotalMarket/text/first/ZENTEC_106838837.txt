Q2 & H1FY27 PRESS & MEDIA RELEASE
Hyderabad, 01 November 2026
Zen Technologies, India’s leading anti -drone technology and defense training solutions 
provider announced its financial results for Q2 & H1FY27
Order  inflow  during  the quarter  was ₹481.81 crore,  taking  the consolidated  order  book  to 
₹1,375.70 crore  as at 30 September  2026 . The order  book  continued  to grow  as 
execution  accelerated  during  the quarter,  reflecting  sustained  demand  across  Zen’s  
product  categories . 
This demand  is underpinned  by a supportive  policy  environment . The Defence  
Acquisition  Council  recently  accorded  Acceptance  of Necessity  to capital  acquisition  
proposals,  of which  around  82% is earmarked  for sourcing  from  the domestic  defence  
industry,  including  categories  in which  Zen has fielded  products . As indigenisation  
becomes  central  to defence  procurement  in India,  Zen’s  ownership  of its core technology  
positions  it as a natural  beneficiary  of this shift.
Commenting  on the results , Mr. Ashok  Atluri,  Chairman  and Managing  Director,  
said: 
“This  quarter  marks  a defining  milestone  in Zen’s  growth  trajectory . Zen reported  its 
highest -ever consolidated  quarterly  revenue  of ₹345.13 crore,  a growth  of 98.8% year-on-
year,  at a healthy  Operational  EBITDA  margin  of 38.2%. These  results  reflect  the scale  of 
the execution  capability  we have  built over the years .
Zen has begun  delivering  the Fast Track  Procurement  orders  received  last year,  ahead  of 
the contractual  timelines . That is possible  because  the technology  is ours - there  is no 
foreign  licensor  to go back to when  the Army  changes  a requirement,  so the product  
changes  in weeks,  not procurement  cycles . This translates  directly  into a significantly  faster  
turnaround,  enabling  us to respond  to evolving  operational  needs  at the speed  the Armed  
Forces  require . With  the sector  moving  decisively  towards  indigenisation , this ownership  of 
our core technology  places  Zen in a strong  position  to capitalise  on the opportunities  
ahead .”PR ES S  & MED IA R ELEAS E 1/2Consolidated Financial Highlights Q2FY27
