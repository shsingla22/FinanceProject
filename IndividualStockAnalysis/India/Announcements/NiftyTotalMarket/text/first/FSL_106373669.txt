Firstsource Appoints Kumaran Shanmuhan as C hief Strategy Officer 
Mumbai, India – September 15, 2025 : Firstsource Solutions Limited  (NSE:FSL, BSE:532809), a leading global 
provider of business process management  services and an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company, today announced the 
appointment of Kumaran Shanmuhan as their Chief Strategy Officer , effective September 15, 2025 . 
His appointment is a strategic step in advancing Firstsource’s bold UnBPO ™ vision  - a transformative reimagining of 
outsourcing driven by expertise, technology, and outcome -oriented solutions.  As Chief Strategy Officer, Kumaran will 
lead the development and execution of Firstsource’s long -term strategic agenda. His mandate includes driving practical 
alignment between evolving market trends and business priorities, identifying high -impact growth opportunities, and 
embedding innovation. Collaborating closely with the CEO and executive leadership team, Kumaran will ensure that 
strategy transl ates into measurable outcomes, empowering Firstsource to deliver greater value to clients and sustain its 
competitive edge in a fast -evolving global market.  
“As the industry undergoes a fundamental shift from labor arbitrage to technology -driven outcome -focused solutions, 
having a clear and bold strategy is critical to staying ahead of disruption,” said Ritesh Idnani, Managing Director & 
CEO, Firstsource . “Kumaran’s deep expertise in enterprise AI strategy, platform commercialization, and business 
transformation uniquely positions him to lead our strategic agenda. His ability to connect technology innovation with 
real-world business outcomes and go -to-market ac celeration will be instrumental in advancing our UnBPO ™ vision  -
where deep domain expertise, agile partnerships, and AI -powered solutions come together to deliver next -generation 
value for our clients. I am confident his leadership will accelerate our journey to becoming a true orchestrator of tech -
led business transfo rmation.”  
Kumaran brings over two decades of strategic leadership in technology, operations, and business transformation, with 
deep expertise in building and scaling consulting, product, platform, and go -to-market functions for high -growth SaaS 
and AI companies. He has a proven track record of driving high -impact growth, accelerating enterprise AI adoption, and 
generating tangible business outcomes.  In his last role at Uniphore, Kumaran built and scaled the first AI consulting and 
industry solutions teams, fueling 70 % year -over-year revenue growth and delivering significant  new business.  His ability 
to turn advanced AI technologies into scalable, high -value solutions aligns directly with Firstsource’s UnBPO ™ vision 
of technology -led, outcome -driven transformation.  
“I am excited to join Firstsource at such a pivotal moment in its journey . The UnBPO ™ approach is about challenging 
legacy thinking and delivering agile, tech -led solutions that create disproportionate value. I look forward to collaborating 
with our talented teams and partners to drive sustained growth and meaningful impact for our clients ,” said Kumaran 
Shanmuhan, Chief Strategy Officer, Firstsource . 
Kumaran’s appointment reflects Firstsource’s strategic intent to lead in a future where relevance, agility, deep domain 
and technology -driven outcomes matter more than scale. His leadership will play a critical role in formalizing Firstsource 
as a trailblazer in reimagining outsourcing,  ensuring the company  remains at the forefront of the industry’s 
transformation.
