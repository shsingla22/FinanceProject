We refer to your letter Ref. No. NSE/CM/Surveillance/17278 dated July 17, 2026 seeking

In this regard, we wish to submit that Premarin Vaginal Cream, which is an imported product,

the Company’s ﬁnancial performance, business operations or proﬁtability.
