HCLTech and UWA deepen strategic partnership to shape next-generation talent 
architecture 
CHENNAI, India, Mar 06, 2026:  HCLTech , a leading global technology company, in partnership 
with the Government of Tamil Nadu and the University of Western Australia  (UWA), convened 
senior leaders from government, academia and industry at the Capability Summit 2026, hosted 
at its Sholinganallur campus in Chennai.  
The summit, themed “From Complexity to Capability – A Leadership Imperative,” focused on how 
sustainable competitive advantage for nations and enterprises will increasingly depend not just 
on technology adoption, but on building scalable, resilient talent and leadership architectures to 
support long term digital growth. 
The leadership exchange brought together Dr. Palanivel Thiaga Rajan, Hon’ble Minister for 
Information Technology, Government of Tamil Nadu (virtual address), Dr Ian Martinus, Investment 
and Trade Commissioner for India-Gulf, Invest and Trade Western Australia, Professor Amit 
Chakma, Vice-Chancellor of UWA, Srimathi Shivashankar, Corporate Vice President and Global 
Head, EdTech Business at HCLTech, and Swapan Johri, President, Growth Markets at HCLTech. 
The collaboration between HCLTech and the University of Western Australia brings together 
industry and academia to advance future-ready leadership and digital capabilities through joint 
dialogue, research, executive education and innovation exchange. It reﬂects UWA’s expanding 
presence in India, including its forthcoming Chennai campus, and HCLTech’s commitment to 
building sustainable talent ecosystems that strengthen leadership pipelines, embed continuous 
learning and align workforce development with evolving global digital priorities. 
Addressing the gathering, Dr. Palanivel Thiaga Rajan, Hon’ble Minister for Information Technology 
said, “We are at a clear inﬂection point, where change is happening across multiple dimensions. 
Preparing for the future requires a fundamental rethink of talent development and education. As 
technology reshapes work in healthcare, government and society at large, our focus must be on 
responsible automation that strengthens human judgment, supported by unbiased, accurate 
and veriﬁable data. This is how we secure long-term trust and progress.” 
Professor Amit Chakma, Vice-Chancellor of UWA,  said, “In an era deﬁned by rapid technological 
transformation, long-term success will depend on our ability to design leadership systems that 
are adaptive, globally connected and innovation-driven. Our partnership with HCLTech reﬂects a 
shared commitment to building talent ecosystems that prepare individuals and institutions for 
complexity, not just change.” 
Srimathi Shivashankar, Corporate Vice President and Global Head, EdTech Business at HCLTech,  
said, “Sustainable advantage is built through vision and intentional investment, not reactive 
responses. As talent models evolve, learnability must be embedded alongside strong data, 
decision frameworks and a culture of curiosity. Our collaboration with the University of Western 
Australia reﬂects HCLTech’s commitment to building future -ready leadership through industry–
academia partnerships.”
