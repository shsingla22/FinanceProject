TVS Motor Launches TVS Jupiter Dual Tone 
Spectral Range 
• TVS Jupiter becomes  the first in the 110cc segment to offer the distinctive 
spectral colour s in dual tone styling  
• Features segment -first Sequential Turn Signal Lamps (TSL), premium comfort 
enhancements and USB Type- C charging  
 
Bengaluru, August 27, 2026:  TVS Motor Company, part of TVS VENU, and a global 
leader in two and three- wheeler manufacturing, today launched the TVS Jupiter d ual tone 
spectral range, the latest addition to India's most progressive family scooter . The new 
range brings two new dual -tone spectral colour s – Spectral Blue  and Spectral Copper  to 
the 110cc scooter segment, making TVS Jupiter the first scooter in the segment to offer 
these distinctive colour s, alongside thoughtful enhancements across styling, comfort and 
technology.  
The distinctive colour treatment is 
complemented by colour -coordinated 
inner panels, a new seat colour, and partial 
chrome mirrors, creating a refined and 
contemporary road presence.  
The new variant further enhances 
everyday comfort and convenience with a 
premium long seat featuring an integrated 
backrest for the rider and pillion. It also 
introduces segment -first Sequential Turn 
Signal Lamps (TSL), offering a distinctive 
lighting signature while enhancing  
visibility, and a USB Type -C charging port 
for convenient charging on the move. The 
scooter continues to offer its best -in-class 
33-litre under -seat storage, capable of 
accommodating two helmets, alongside 
SmartXonnect ™ connected technology.  
Commenting on the new edition, Aniruddha Haldar, Senior Vice President — Head 
Commuter & EV Business and Head Corporate Brand & Media, TVS Motor Company said, “Young and Urban Indian families  are increasingly exposed to new experiences, design 
and technology, shaping their aspirations f or everyday mobility. TVS Jupiter has played a 
pivotal role in India’s scooterisation journey, winning the love and trust of millions of families  
and earning its place as the ‘Scooter of India’. Over the years, it  has evolved , bringing 
meaningful innovations while staying true  to what families value . Building on its progressive 
design and feature -rich offering, the new TVS Jupiter dual -tone takes this journey forward with 
a more contemporary  offering, featuring first -in-segment spectral colours , complemented by 
first-in-segment Sequential Turn Signal Lamps  and much more. Together,  these 
enhancements reaffirm TVS Jupiter’s commitment to being the preferred choice  of mobility for 
today’s new -age, modern families. ” TVS Jupiter Dual Tone Spectral Range
