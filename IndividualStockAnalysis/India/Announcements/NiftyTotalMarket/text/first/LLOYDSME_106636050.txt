Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (“ SEBI”) (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“ Listing Regulations ”), we regret to inform the sudden and 
untimely demise of Mr. Mahendra Singh Mehta (DIN: 00019566), Independent Director of the Company, 
on 22nd May, 2026. 
 
Mr. Mahendra Singh Mehta had been serving as an Independent Director of the Company since 23rd October, 
2023 and was an integral part of the Company’s leadership. During his tenure, he provided invaluable 
guidance and played a significant role in the strategic growth and governance of the Company. His wisdom, 
experience, and contributions were deeply valued by the Board and the management. 
 
The passing away of Mr. Mahendra Singh Mehta is an irreparable loss to the Company. The Board of 
Directors and all employees of the Company express their deepest condolences and heartfelt sympathies to 
the bereaved family. 
 
Mr. Mahendra Singh Mehta was a Member of Audit Committee, Stakeholders’ Relationship Committee, 
Nomination and Remuneration Committee and Committee of Board of Directors of the Company.  
Consequent to his demise, Mr. Mahendra Singh Mehta ceased to be a Member of the aforementioned 
Committees of the Board of the Company with effect from 22nd May, 2026.
