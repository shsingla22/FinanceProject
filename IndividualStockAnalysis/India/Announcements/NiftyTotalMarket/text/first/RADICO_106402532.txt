Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations, we wish to inform you that M/s. Piccadily 
Agro Industries Limited filed a commercial suit bearing Commercial Suit No. 8 of 2025 before the 
District Court, Karnal against the Company  inter alia  seeking to restrain the Company from using its 
trademarks “The Spirit of Kashmyr Indian Natural Vodka” and “The Spirit of Kashmyr Indian Saffron 
Vodka.” 
 
In the said matter, the Learned District Court, Karnal, vide its order dated September 23, 2025, 
temporarily restrained the Company from using the mark “Kashmyr”, whether in isolation or in 
conjunction with any prefix or suffix, till the disposal of the said suit. 
 
Aggrieved by the above order, the Company preferred an appeal before the Hon’ble Punjab and Haryana 
High Court at Chandigarh, registered as FAO (COM) No. 17 of 2025. The Hon’ble High Court, after 
hearing the parties, has vide its order dated October 9, 2025, directed that the effect and operation of 
the order dated September 23, 2025, passed by the Learned District Court, Karnal, shall be kept in 
abeyance till the next date of hearing.  
 
The brief details required under Regulation 30 is enclosed herewith. 
 
The matter is presently sub judice before the Hon’ble High Court, and we shall keep the Stock 
Exchanges duly informed of any further material developments in this regard, in compliance with the

3. Brief Details of Dispute 
Litigation Radico Khaitan Limited (“Radico”) is the applicant of the trademarks 
“The Spirit of Kashmyr Indian Saffron Vodka” and “The Spirit of 
Kashmyr Indian Natural Vodka.” The Company launched its luxury 
vodka and made its first sale on May 21, 2025, in the State of Uttar 
Pradesh after obtaining all requisite statutory approvals. 
 
Subsequent to Radico’s launch and first sale, Piccadily Agro Industries 
Limited (“Piccadily”) announced its own vodka product under the 
unregistered mark “Cashmir Vodka” on May 23, 2025, and effected its 
first sale on May 26, 2025. 
 
Despite its later use and absence of prior commercial exploitation of 
its registered mark “Cashmere” (under rectification), Piccadily 
instituted a commercial suit bearing Commercial Suit No. 8 of 2025 
before the District Court, Karnal, seeking an injunction to restrain 
Radico from using its applied trademarks, alleging deceptive similarity 
with its “Cashmere” mark—which has not been used by Piccadily for 
any of its products to date. 
 
The Learned District Court, Karnal, vide its order dated September 23, 
2025, granted an interim injunction temporarily restraining Radico 
from using the mark “Kashmyr” whether in isolation or in conjunction 
with any prefix or suffix, till the disposal of the said suit. 
 
Aggrieved by the said interim order, Radico preferred an appeal before 
the Hon’ble Punjab and Haryana High Court, registered as FAO 
(COM) No. 17 of 2025. The Hon’ble High Court, vide its order dated 
October 9, 2025, has been pleased to keep the operation and effect of 
the District Court’s order dated September 23, 2025, in abeyance till 
the next date of hearing.
