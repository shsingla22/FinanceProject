Subject – Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations 2015 – Update on cyber security incident. 
 
Further to our intimation dated April 10, 2025, regarding the cyber security incident, we wish to 
provide an update as below. 
 
Pursuant to the incident, the company had shutdown and isolated all its systems. Thereafter, it began 
restoration activity with the assistance of cyber security experts. The company fully restored the core 
operating systems for management of funds by the next working day itself. Thereafter, the restoration 
of mobile application and website was taken up. 
 
We would like to inform that the company's mobile application and website are now fully functional 
and operating normally. During the downtime, the customers were requested to transact through 
alternate channels which remained completely unaffected through this period.   
 
Overall, there was no material impact on the operations of the Company due to the said incident.
