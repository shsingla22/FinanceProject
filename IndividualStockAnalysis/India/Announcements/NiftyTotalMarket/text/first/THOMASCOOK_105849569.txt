Sterling Resort in Ooty shines with Prestigious Agoda Gold Circle Award  
Chennai, Tamilnadu - May 16, 2024 - Sterling – Ooty Elk Hill Resort has been selected as a 
recipient of the 2023 Gold Circle Award by Agoda. This award recognizes a select group of 
property partners who have demonstrated exceptional value for customers, provided world -
class service, and maintained a strong partnership with Agoda over the past year.  
"We are thrilled to receive the Agoda Gold Circle Award, which underscores the hard work and dedication of our team in delivering an unparalleled experience to our guests. This award 
reaffirms ou r relentless pursuit of excellence and our commitment to continuously raising the 
bar in the hospitality industry,” said Harinath M, Senior Vice President -  Marketing, Sterling 
Holiday Resorts.  
The Agoda Gold Circle Awards, now in their 15th year, have exp anded significantly, honoring 
more than 2,300 properties across 130 markets worldwide. The award is a testament to 
Sterling’s unwavering dedication to guest satisfaction and its ability to adapt to the evolving needs of the travel industry.  
Sterling Ooty E lk Hill awarded “Best Family Resort” is spread over 4.5 acres with an 
unobstructed view of the Ooty valley and the pristine Nilgiri Hills. Ideal for a family holiday, 
with large rooms and refurbished suites including 2 -Bedroom family suites up to 520 sq.ft . 
the resort also offers fun games and activities for children. The resort is themed on the 
UNESCO World Heritage site –  the Nilgiri Railway. With a working model train in the lobby, 
and the Nilgiri Express restaurant, providing authentic and local cuisine s. One can indulge in 
a celebratory dining experience at the resort’s observatory or relax at the Subuthi Spa with signature ayurvedic treatments and beauty regimens.
