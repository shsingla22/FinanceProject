        Subject:  Clarification on news item appearing in  https://www.moneycontrol.com  
 
1. With reference to the news item which appeared in the "https://www.moneycontrol.com" dated 
27th May, 2025 captioned "Govt readies Rs 2,000 crore-3,000 crore order to buy Invar missiles 
from Bharat Dynamics”, BDL response to stock exchanges queries are as follows: 
 
a) Whether such negotiations were taking place? If do, you are advised to provide the said 
information along with sequence of events in chronological order from the start of 
negotiations till date. 
BDL’s reply:  There is no requirement of any negotiation. BDL, being a PSU under Ministry 
of Defence, its contracts are confidential & classified in nature. Divulging information on the 
same may be detrimental to national security. 
 
b) Whether you/company are aware of any information that has not been announced to the 
exchanges which could explain the aforesaid movement in the trading? 
BDL’s reply:  In our opinion, no such information is there which affected the movement in 
the trading. However, regarding contracts, refer response to para (a) above. 
 
c) In case of regulatory / legal proceedings please provide the information on initiation / 
outcome of the proceeding.  
BDL’s reply: There are no legal proceedings in this regard as on date of this response.
