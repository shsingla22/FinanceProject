Garware Hi -Tech Films Introduces Four New Products in Automotive 
Care Segment  
• Garware Ceramic & Graphene Coating – Next-generation surface protection engineered for superior 
durability and gloss  
• Garware Car Care Kit – Professional -grade care solutions for comprehensive vehicle maintenance  
• Window Film Kits – Premium (Gold), Mid (Silver), and Economy (Bronze) categories, offering high 
product quality, competitive pricing, and warranty support across the range.  
• Garware Wind -Shield Pro – Advanced windshield protection for enhanced clarity, safety, and 
longevity  
Mumbai, 06th February 2026  – Garware Hi -Tech Films (GHFL), a global leader in Paint Protection and 
Sun Control Films, today reaffirmed its presence in automotive care with the launch of four new 
innovations at ACMA Automechanika New Delhi 2026. The new offerings highlight the Company’ s 
continued focus on product performance, practical usability, and solutions designed around customer 
needs.  
At the expo, GHFL introduced the All New Garware Ceramic & Graphene Coating, Garware 
WindShield Pro, Garware Detailing Kit, and the All -New Window Film Kits , a curated portfolio 
created to improve vehicle protection, simplify maintenance, and enhance the overall ownership 
experience for customers.  
Commenting on the launch, Ms. Monika Garware,  Vice Chairperson & Joint Managing  Director,  
Garware Hi -Tech Films Limited  added, “This launch marks an important step in expanding our 
automotive care portfolio with products that combine advanced technology and everyday usability. 
These new products are designed to improve vehicle protection, simplify regular care, and provide 
reliable performance in daily use .” 
The Garware Ceramic & Graphene Coating  represents a meaningful advancement in surface 
protection technology. It is designed to provide strong resistance against environmental exposure, UV 
rays, and regular wear while maintaining long -lasting gloss and surface quality.  
The Garware Car Care Kit  brings professional -grade solutions to vehicle owners through a well -
rounded range of products for effective cleaning, protection, and routine care.  
The Garware Window Film Kits  are available across Premium (Gold), Mid (Silver), and Economy 
(Bronze) categories, offering dependable heat rejection, visual comfort, and durability, supported by 
consistent quality and warranty coverage across the range.  
The Garware WindShield Pro  introduces advanced windshield protection that helps preserve optical 
clarity while safeguarding glass surfaces from scratches, stone chipping , and everyday wear, supporting 
safer and more comfortable driving.  
With these introductions, Garware Hi -Tech Films continues to strengthen its automotive solutions 
portfolio and deepen engagement with customers seeking dependable, high -quality protection and care 
for their vehicles.
