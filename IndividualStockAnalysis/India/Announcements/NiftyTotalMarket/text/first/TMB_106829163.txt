Unaudited Financial Results of the Bank for the Quarter Ended / Half Year Ended 
September 30, 2026 
 
Tamilnad Mercantile Bank Ltd (TMB), one of the renowned Old Private Sector Banks, 
having its headquarters in Thoothukudi has a long-cherished history of 105 years of 
eventful existence with strong fundamentals and an enviable track record of continuous 
profit making in the industry. 
 
TMB is having pan India presence with 631 branches and 13 Regional Offices across 17 
states and 4 Union Territories and serves more than 5.3 million satisfied customers.    
 
The Board of Directors of Tamilnad Mercantile Bank Ltd has approved the Unaudited 
Financial Results of the Quarter Ended / Half Year Ended September 30,2026 in their 
meeting held at Thoothukudi on 26.10.2026. Shri. Salee S Nair, Managing Director and 
CEO of the Bank declared the results. The Chief Financial Officer, Executive Vice 
Presidents and other senior officials of the Bank were also present in the event. 
 
Shri. Salee S Nair, MD & CEO, Tamilnad Mercantile Bank Ltd said, “Our performance in 
Q2 FY2027 reflects the strength of our core banking business, with retail banking 
continuing to be a key contributor and business gaining momentum overall. As we enter 
the festive season, we see opportunities to support increased business activity across the 
markets we serve, particularly among traders and MSMEs preparing for higher inventory 
requirements and working capital needs. With our deep understanding of local businesses 
and customer relationships built over decades, we are well positioned to support these 
evolving credit requirements while continuing to serve the financial needs of households. 
Going forward, our focus will be on building this business momentum, expanding our reach 
and ensuring that growth is supported by sound underwriting and a sustained commitment 
to customer service.”  
 
The Bank continues to post strong performance in all fundamental parameters.
