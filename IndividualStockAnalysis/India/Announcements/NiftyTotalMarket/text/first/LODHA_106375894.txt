Sub: Update on resignation of Mr Rajendra Lodha  
 
 In continuation of our intimation dated August 18, 2025 about resignation of Mr Rajendra Lodha as a

related to his role with an independent  external firm and their  recommendations are being implemented 
with immediate effect.
