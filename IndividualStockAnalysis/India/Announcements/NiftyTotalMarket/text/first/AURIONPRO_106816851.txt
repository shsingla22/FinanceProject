Matkahuolto selects Aurionpro to advance seamless, 
sustainable public transport payments across Finland  
 
Aurionpro’s contactless validation and payment technology will support Matkahuolto’s 
cEMV project, enabling acceptance of EMV and local contactless card standards 
across Finland  
 
Mumbai, India / Finland, October  14, 2026: Aurionpro Solutions, a global technology 
solutions provider serving the banking, payments, mobility , and transit sectors, today 
announced that Matkahuolto, Finland’s long -standing provider of parcel and digital 
transport services, has selected Aurionpro as the device vendor for its contactless 
EMV (cEMV) project following a competitive RFP process.  
     
 
 
 
 
Under a Master Service Agreement, Aurionpro will provide its SCV 500 Validator and 
ECR-one contactless card reader, together with integration to Matkahuolto’s Transport 
Management System (TMS) and APIs for payment -application connectivity. The 
solution w ill enable the acceptance of EMV payment cards alongside Finland’s local 
contactless card standards, delivering a seamless and reliable fare -payment 
experience across the public transport network.

About Matkahuloto : Matkahuolto is a modern service company that has been bringing 
people and goods together seamlessly for more than 90 years. The company’s 
business is divided into Parcel Services and Digital Transport Services. Parcel 
Services transport 20 million parcels a year in Finland and abroad, and Matkahuolto’s 
customers are served by a nationwide network of 2,550 service points. Digital 
Transport Services focus on sustainable mobility services, timetables and tickets, 
enabling over 7 million journeys annually. For transport operators, the company 
provides digital tools for business development. Matkahuolto is committed to science -
based climate targets, and the company aims to halve all its emissions by 2030. In 
2025, Matkahuolto’s turnover was EUR 98.5 million. www.matkahuolto.fi
