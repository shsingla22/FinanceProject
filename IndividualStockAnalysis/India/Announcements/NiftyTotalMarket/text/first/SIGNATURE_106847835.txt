Dear Sir/Madam, 
We would like you to take note of our Key Financial updates for Q2FY27 and H1FY27: 
a) Revenue from operation: The Company delivered a strong revenue performance in Q2FY27, driving robust growth for the 
half year. Revenue for Q2FY27 surged 148% YoY to INR 8.2 bn, compared to INR 3.3 bn in Q2FY26, reflecting a significant 
acceleration in business momentum during the quarter. Supported by this strong quarterly performance, H1FY27 revenue 
increased 14% YoY to INR 13.7 bn, up from INR 12.0 bn in H1FY26. 
b) Profit after tax (PAT): Driven by strong business performance and operational execution, the Company reported a profit after 
tax of INR 0.7 bn in Q2FY27, marking a significant turnaround from a loss after tax of INR 0.5 bn in Q2FY26. Sustaining this 
momentum, the Company delivered a profit after tax of INR 0.5 bn in H1FY27, compared to a loss after tax of INR 0.1 bn in H1FY26. 
c) Adjusted gross profit margin: The Company's adjusted gross profit margin expanded to 36% in Q2FY27 and 31% in H1FY27, 
from 35% and 29%, respectively, in the corresponding periods of the previous year, demonstrating continued strength in project 
execution and profitability. 
d) Adjusted EBITDA margin: The Company delivered a substantial improvement in operating profitability, with adjusted EBITDA 
margins rising to 18% in Q2FY27 and 13% in H1FY27, compared to negative 7% and positive 6%, respectively, in the corresponding 
periods last year, driven by higher revenue recognition, margin expansion, and operating efficiencies. 
Key Operational updates for Q2FY27 and H1FY27: 
a) Pre-Sales: The Company recorded pre-sales of INR 18.3 bn in Q2FY27 as compared with INR 20.2 bn in Q2FY26 and INR 19.7 bn in 
Q1FY27, respectively. For H1FY27, the Company achieved pre-sales of INR 38.0 bn, as against INR 46.6 bn in H1FY26. Further, the 
Company remains focused on sustaining sales momentum through strategic launches, disciplined execution, and a diversified 
product portfolio. 
b) Sales realization: Average sales realization increased to INR 17,122 per sq. ft. in Q2FY27, compared to INR 15,250 per sq. ft. in FY26, 
reflecting an increase of 12.3%. The improvement was primarily driven by the successful launch and strong response to the premium 
residential project, Tonino Lamborghini Residences, located on Southern Peripheral Road (SPR), Sector 71, Gurugram. 
c) Collections: Collections demonstrated strong momentum during Q2FY27, rising to INR 10.1 bn and registering a robust 51% QoQ 
growth from INR 6.7 bn in Q1FY27. On a YoY basis, collections grew by 9% from INR 9.3 bn in Q2FY26, reflecting healthy customer 
demand, efficient collections management, and sustained execution progress across the portfolio. For H1FY27, collections stood at 
INR 16.8 bn, compared to INR 18.6 bn in H1FY26.
