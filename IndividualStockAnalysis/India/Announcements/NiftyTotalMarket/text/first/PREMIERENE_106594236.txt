Sub: - Allotment of equity shares to the PEL ESOP Trust (‘ESOP Trust’) pursuant to 
Premier Energies Limited - Employee Stock Option Plan, 2025 (ESOP Scheme). 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform that the Nomination and 
Remuneration Committee of the Company, at its meeting held today, i.e., April 20, 2026, has 
approved the allotment of 9,57,142 (Nine Lakh Fifty-Seven Thousand One Hundred Forty-Two) 
equity shares of face value of ₹1/- each, fully paid-up, to the PEL ESOP Trust under the PEL 
ESOP Scheme, 2025. 
 
The equity shares so allotted to the ESOP Trust shall be transferred to, or for the benefit of, 
eligible employees upon exercise of stock options to be granted under the said Scheme. 
 
The Nomination and Remuneration Committee had previously approved the issuance of up to 
45,00,000 (Forty-Five Lakh) equity shares, in the first phase, at a price of ₹700 per share, out of 
the total 90,15,487 equity shares approved by the shareholders, to the PEL ESOP Trust. Such 
issuance is to be made in one or more tranches for the implementation of the PEL ESOP Scheme 
2025. 
  
Pursuant to the aforesaid approval, the present allotment of 9,57,142 equity shares has been carried 
out in compliance with the limits prescribed under Section 67(3) of the Companies Act, 2013. 
 
Consequent to the aforesaid allotment, the paid-up share capital of the Company has increased 
from 45,29,94,368 equity shares of ₹1/- each, aggregating to ₹45,29,94,368, to 45,39,51,510 equity 
shares of ₹1/- each, aggregating to ₹45,39,51,510. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Requisite disclosure under Regulation 10(c) of the SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits and 
Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021 is enclosed in Annexure A  and Disclosure pursuant to Regulation 
30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 as Annexure B . 
 
The NRC Meeting commenced at 03:15 PM (IST) and concluded at 03:29 PM (IST)
