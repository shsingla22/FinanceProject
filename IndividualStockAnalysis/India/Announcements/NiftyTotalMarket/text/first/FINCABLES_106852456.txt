1. PREAMBLE AND APPLICABILITY  
1.1 This Code of Conduct to Regulate, Monitor and Report Trading by Insiders (the “ Code ”) is 
framed by Finolex Cables Limited (the “ Company ”) to regulate, monitor and report Trading 
by Insiders. 
1.2 This Code is intended to prevent misuse of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information 
(“UPSI ”) by Insiders and Connected Persons and to ensure that the Directors and Designated 
Persons of the Company and their Immediate Relatives do not derive any benefit, or assist 
others to derive any benefit, from access to and possession of UPSI. 
1.3 The Code, approved by the Board of Directors on 12th May 2015, was effective from 15th 
May 2015 and was amended on 26th May 2023, 10th August 2024 and 11th November 2025. 
The Code has been further amended by the Bo ard on 7th November 2026 and the amended 
Code shall be effective from 7th November 2026. 
1.4 The Code shall apply to and be binding on all Designated Persons and their Relative and 
Immediate Relatives and, to the extent provided herein, on all Insiders and Connected 
Persons, including persons having a contractual or fiduciary relationship with the Company. 
2. LEGAL FRAMEWORK  
2.1 The Securities and Exchange Board of India (“ SEBI ”), in exercise of the powers conferred on 
it under the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992, has notified the SEBI 
(Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, effective from 15th May 2015, as amended 
from time to time (the “ Regulations ”). 
2.2 In terms of Regulation 9(1) of the Regulations, the board of directors of every listed company 
shall ensure that the Managing Director / Chief Executive Officer formulates, with the 
approval of the board, a code of conduct to  regulate, monitor and report trading by its 
designated persons and their immediate relati ves towards achieving compliance with the 
Regulations, adopting the minimum standards set out in Schedule B to the Regulations. 
2.3 Accordingly, the Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting held on 12th May 2015 
approved and adopted the Code, which was amended on 26th May 2023, 10th August 2024 
and 11th November 2025 and further amended on 7th November 2026.
