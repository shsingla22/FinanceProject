Sensitivity: Internal (C3)  Hindustan Zinc’s Chanderiya Lead Zinc Smelter  Becomes India’s First Zinc 
Mark  Site 
• The Company’s  Chanderiya Lead Zinc Smelter ( CLZS) become s the first site in India to 
achieve Zinc Mark validation  under the Copper Mark Assurance Process  
• The Zinc Mark assessment validates C LZS’s strong performance against rigorous 
environmental, social, and governance (ESG) criteria  
Udaipur, 29th April ’26: Hindustan Zinc Limited (NSE: HINDZINC), the world’s largest integrated 
zinc producer and among the top ten global silver producers, announced a key milestone . 
Chanderiya Lead Zinc Smelter (C LZS) achieved Zinc Mark validation, becoming the first site in 
India to be assessed as meeting the Zinc Mark Criteria under the Copper Mark Assurance 
Process. This recognition follows a comprehensive review of C LZS’s Independent Assessment 
Report by The Copper Mark, which confirmed the competence of the independent assessors 
and validated that the assessment process and findings were conducted in line with the 
Copper Mark Assurance Process and Criteria Guide.  
The outcome reflects C LZS’s consistent implementation of robust policies and responsible 
operating practices aligned with globally benchmarked environmental, social, and 
governance standards.  
The Zinc Mark , governed by The Copper Mark , is an internationally recognized  assurance 
framework that independently assesses site -level performance against robust environmental, 
social, and governance (ESG) criteria.  These include benchmarks across emissions, energy and 
water efficiency, waste management, bio-diversity management , worker safety, human 
rights,  stakeholder engagements  and ethical business practices. The achievement comes 
amid growing global emphasis on responsible sourcing and traceability. As demand for 
responsibly produced metals continues to rise, driven by the  global energy transition and 
infrastructure development, Zinc Mark validation  reinforces C LZS’s credibility as a responsible 
contributor to the manufacturing ecosystem.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Arun Misra, CEO, Hindustan Zinc, said:  
“Achieving Zinc Mark conformance at our Chanderiya Lead Zinc Smelter marks more than a 
milestone, it reflects the direction of the future we are building. Assured under the Copper 
Mark framework, this recognition reinforces our commitment to redefining met al production 
through transparency, accountability, and globally benchmarked ESG excellence. In alignment 
with the principles of the International Council on Mining and Metals, we are embedding 
responsible mining practices across our operations, strengthen ing trust across global value 
chains and steadily positioning India as a credible supplier of sustainably produced metals.”  
 
 
 
Sensitivity: Internal (C3)  The Copper Mark and its metal -specific Zinc Mark framework follow a multi -stakeholder 
governance approach aimed at promoting responsible production of critical materials.  As part 
of this, certified sites demonstrate strong performance not only in operational efficiency but 
also in positively impacting communities and respecting human rights. These certifications go 
beyond ESG ratings by validating performance at the operati onal level and promoting 
continuous progress.  
The Chanderiya Lead Zinc Smelter ’s achievement reflects Hindustan Zinc’s broader and deeply 
embedded sustainability commitment, underpinned by ambitious targets across 
decarbonisation, water positivity, and biodiversity conservation.  
This sustainability leadership has received global recognition, with Hindustan Zinc being 
named the world’s most sustainable metals and mining company for the third consecutive 
year in the S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment 2025. As India’s fir st mining 
company to join the International Council on Mining & Metals (ICMM), Hindustan Zinc 
continues to align with globally benchmarked best practices while strengthening India’s 
position as a credible and responsible producer in the global metals value  chain.  
 
About Hindustan Zinc  
Hindustan Zinc Limited (BSE: 500188 and NSE: HINDZINC), a Vedanta Group company, is the world’s largest integrated zinc 
producer and is amongst the top10 silver producers globally. The company supplies to more than 40 countries and holds a 
market share of about 74% of the primary zinc market in India. Hindustan Zinc has been recognized as the world’s most 
sustainable company in the metals and mining category for the third consecutive year by the S&P Global Corporate 
Sustainability Assessment 2025, reflectin g its operational excellence, innovation, and leading ESG practices. The company 
also became the first Indian company to join the prestigious International Council on Mining & Metals (ICMM) in 2025. The 
company also launched EcoZen, Asia’s first low carbon  ‘green’ zinc brand, produced using renewable energy. Hindustan Zinc 
is also a certified 3.32 times Water -Positive company and is committed to achieving Net Zero emissions by 2050 or sooner. 
The company is also transforming the lives of 2.6 million people through its focused social welfare initiatives. As an energy 
transition metals company, Hindustan Zinc is pivotal in the journey of sourcing critical metals essential for a sustainable 
future.
