case, we are also seeing that the thrust of the government on the government spending,

opinion. Secondly, consecutive good monsoon which is what has been predicted will 
further add to this. So, from a consumer demand point of view, we remain quite

a month or two max, then I think the year would perhaps play out to the fundamental 
consumer demand. If this is extended, then the impact of this is something which we 
have not factored in and will be very different in our opinion.
