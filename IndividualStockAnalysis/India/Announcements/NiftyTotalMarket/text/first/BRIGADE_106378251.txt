PRESS RELEASE  
  
First -Ever Brigade Showcase to be held in Chennai on September 20 and 21, 2025  
 
Brigade Group’s flagship event to showcase residential and commercial projects in Chennai, along with upcoming 
launches and exclusive event -only offers.  
 
Chennai, September 19 , 2025:  Brigade Group, having successfully conducted 18 editions of its flagship 
property exhibition in Bengaluru, will hold the first -ever ‘Brigade Showcase Chennai 2025’ at hotel Leela 
Palace, Chennai on September 20 and 21. The event will present Brigade’s por tfolio of developments in the 
residential and commercial real estate segments across the cities of Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and 
Mysuru under one roof. Attendees will have access to the complete range of Brigade properties , including 
new launches and completed projects, as well as rental, resale, and interior offerings. The event will also 
feature limited time offers, including additional booking benefits.  
 
“Since debuting in Bengaluru in 2009, Brigade Showcase has grown into a trusted platform, with 18 successful 
editions to date. With the launch of the Chennai edition, we are reinforcing our presence in this market and 
our intention to continue expanding ou r portfolio here. Brigade Showcase – Chennai 2025 is designed to give 
buyers and investors direct access to our projects, and we look forward to engaging with the community 
through this platform. By bringing the legacy of Brigade Showcase to Chennai for th e very first time, we are 
reiterating our long -term commitment to this city. With a planned investment of ₹8,000 crore s over the next 
six to seven years, and a strong pipeline of developments, we see significant opportunity to contribute 
meaningfully to Chennai ’s evolving urban landscape. ” said Pavitra Shankar, Managing Director, Brigade 
Enterprises Limited.  
 
There are various initiatives undertaken to reach out to the Chennai audience through a 360 -degree integrated 
marketing campaign spanning mainline print media, hoardings, radio, digital, hyper -local quick commerce, 
influencer partnerships, corporate, mall and society activations.  
 
About Brigade Group:  Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers with 
close to four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and winning their 
customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark bu ildings and transformed skylines across India in 
the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, and GIFT City with 
developments across Residential, Office, Retail, Hospitality and Education Sectors.
