Key highlights for the quarter and half-year FY27: 
 
Info Edge Standalone: 
 Info Edge Standalone billings grew 12.8% YoY in Q2FY27 . Adjusting for the renewal timing differences in 
the Recruitment business described below, management estimates underlying standalone billings growth in Q2 
at approximately  14% YoY . 
 
Recruitment Solutions: 
 Recruitment billings can vary from quarter to quarter with the timing of customer renewals, as some clients renew 
ahead of schedule and others defer to a subsequent quarter. 
 Adjusting for renewal timing differences, management estimates underlying growth in Q2FY27 at 
approximately 14-15% YoY , against reported growth of 12.6%. Underlying growth was also approximately 
15% in Q1FY27, when timing effects lifted reported growth to 17.5%. 
 H1FY27 billings grew 14.9% YoY . Over a longer period, timing effects partly even out, providing a more 
normalized view of growth. 
 Adoption of our B2B AI products continued to increase . AI-Rex and Talent Pulse, in particular, saw growth 
in both new customer sign-ups and revenue contribution. 
Real Estate: 
 99acres billings grew 21.2% YoY in Q2FY27 , compared with 16.5% YoY in Q1FY27. The platform's share of 
category traffic remained stable, with web time-share of 49%  and app time-share of 59%  for July and August 
2026 (source: SimilarWeb). 
 
Matchmaking & Education: 
 In Q2FY27, Jeevansathi billings grew 18.4% YoY  and Aisle billings grew 40.0% YoY , taking combined 
matchmaking billings growth to 23.1% YoY . 
 Shiksha's traffic and billings have been under pressure, as AI-driven search increasingly answers user queries 
directly and reduces search referrals to the platform. The business is adapting its model and introducing new 
offerings to reduce its dependence on search. We expect this transition to take place over the medium term as 
these initiatives scale, and billings growth is likely to remain under pressure in the interim.
