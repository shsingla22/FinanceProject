Hindustan Zinc Expands Value -Added Zinc  Product  Offerings with 
Next -Gen Alloys ‘VedAZ’  and ‘VedAyush ’ for the Galvani zing 
Industry  
 
• Two specialised alloys designed to enhance process  efficiency  and deliver consistent 
coating quality for galvanizers  
• Developed to address evolving industry requirements and support India’s 
infrastructure and manufacturing sectors  
 
Jaipur, 3rd November 2026:  Hindustan Zinc Limited (BSE: 500188; NSE: HINDZINC), the 
world's largest integrated zinc producer and one of the top 10 silver producers globally, today 
announced the launch of  VedAZ and VedAyush , two next -generation zinc alloys. Developed 
to address diverse requirements across the galvanizing value chain, the alloys are designed 
to enhance process efficiency, improve coating consistency and strengthen long- term 
performance of galvanized steel.  
The launch comes  at a time when corrosion management is gaining increasing importance in 
safeguarding industrial assists and public infrastructure. According to a  2026 CII –Nomura 
Research Institute report , corrosion related losses in India are estimated at approximately 
4.3% of the country’s GDP annually. In this context, consistent coating quality, effective 
corrosion protection and efficient manufacturing processes are critical to industries that rely 
on galvanized stell. Advanced zinc alloy solutions can play an important role in improving asset 
durability and supporting the long- term performance of infrastructure.  
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Amarendu Prakash, CEO and Whole- time Director, 
Hindustan Zinc, said,  "We see significant scope to grow our value- added zinc business 
across infrastructure, automotive, engineering and consumer products. Customers in these 
sectors need specialised grades tailored to their manufacturing processes and performance 
requirements.  VedAZ and VedAyush broaden the range of needs we can address and create 
opportunities to build closer customer relationships. Our integrated operations and alloy manufacturing capabilities provide a strong base to develop customised products and scale 
this business. We want value- added products to become a larger part of our portfolio, helping 
us reach new customers and capture more value from the zinc we produce"  
VedAZ, a specialised zinc -aluminium master alloy developed for the galvanising industry, is 
designed to bring greater precision, consistency and efficiency to coating processes. By 
enabling better control of aluminium levels in galvanising baths, the alloy  supports stable bath 
chemistry, uniform coating thickness and improved surface finish. Its optimised composition is 
also designed to reduce dross formation and zinc losses, helping manufacturers enhance 
operational efficiency, optimise material utilisation and deliver high -quality galvanised 
products.  
VedAyush, a specialised zinc -aluminium -magnesium alloy, is engineered to enhance 
corrosion protection, durability and coating performance across demanding industrial 
environments. The addition of magnesium is designed to improve corrosion resistance, 
coati ng adhesion and surface stability, particularly in applications exposed to high humidity,

Through its wholly owned subsidiary, Hindustan Zinc Alloys Private Limited (HZAPL), the 
company operates a 30,000- tonne -per-annum zinc alloy melting and casting facility at Rajpura 
Dariba, Rajasthan.  Supported by Hindustan Zinc's integrated operations, the facility 
strengthens domestic availability of application- specific zinc alloys and supports the 
development of a more resilient manufacturing supply chain.  
Hindustan Zinc's specialised alloy portfolio includes zinc die- casting grades such as HZDA -3 
and HZDA -5, developed for applications requiring superior castability, dimensional stability, 
mechanical strength, electrical conductivity and corrosion resistance . These alloys cater to 
precision components across automotive, electrical, industrial hardware and consumer products. The company's HZDA -5 grade has also received Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) 
certification, complementing its HZDA -3 certification and r einforcing its commitment to product 
quality and standardisation.  
The launch of VedAZ and VedAyush marks another step in Hindustan Zinc's efforts to build a diversified portfolio of specialised zinc products. By working closely with galvanisers and 
downstream industries, the company aims to support India's growing infras tructure and 
manufacturing needs with solutions shaped around real application requirements.
