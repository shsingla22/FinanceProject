 Dear Sirs,  
 
Re: Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015  - Update  
 
This is to inform you that the Company is modernising its tile manufacturing plant 
located in Vijaywada, Andhra Pradesh. The new machinery will be more fuel efficient 
and will produce tiles that are in line with current market trends.  
The Company is replacing all the existing three production lines at the plant with one 
large modern production line for GVT. This will lead to change in annual tile production 
capacity of the Vijaywada plant from around 9 million m2 currently to around 6 million 
m2 of higher value product. The total capex for the modernisation project will be 
approximately ₹ 60 Crores and is expected to be complete d by September 2024.  
The reduction in annual tile production capacity would not have any adverse impact on 
overall sales of HRJ Division of the Company. With this, the overall capacity of HRJ 
Division, along-with its joint ventures and subsidiaries shall be 64 MSM per annum.
