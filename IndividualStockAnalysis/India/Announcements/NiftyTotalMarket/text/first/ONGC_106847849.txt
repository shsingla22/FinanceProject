Madam/ Sir, 
 
The Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today i.e. 05.11.2026, has inter-alia considered the following 
proposals: 
 
1. Unaudited Financial Results (Standalone and Consolidated) for the Quarter and Half year ended 30th 
September, 2026 
 
The Board of Directors has approved the Unaudited Financial Results (Standalone and Consolidated) along with 
limited review report of the Auditors thereon for the Quarter and Half year ended 30th September, 2026. 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 33 & 52 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, a Statement of Unaudited Financial Results 
(Standalone and Consolidated) along with limited review report of the Auditors thereon for the Quarter and Half year 
ended 30th September, 2026 are enclosed as “ Annexure-A ”. 
 
2. Declaration of 1st Interim Dividend for the Financial Year 2026-27  
 
The Board of Directors has declared 1st Interim dividend at the rate ₹8 per equity share of face value of ₹5/- each i.e.  
@160% for the Financial Year 2026-27.  
 
As informed vide letter dated 30.10.2026, Thursday, the 12th November, 2026 has been fixed  as “Record Date ” for 
determining eligibility of shareholders for payment of 1st Interim Dividend. Dividend would be paid within 30 days from 
the date of declaration. 
 
3. Inter Corporate Loan to ONGC Videsh Limited 
 
The Board of Directors has approved the proposal for providing inter-corporate loan to ONGC Videsh Limited valid for 
5 years for a value not exceeding ₹10,000 Crore outstanding at any point of time on such terms and conditions as 
may be mutually agreed between the Company as lender and ONGC Videsh Limited as borrower. 
       
ONGC Videsh Limited is a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company. 
 
4. Disclosure under Regulation 52(7) & (2A) and Regulation 54(3) of SEBI Listing Regulations, 2015 
 
The Company had ₹1,000 Crore unsecured Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) as on 30.09.2026.  
 
Security Cover certificates are not applicable under Regulation 54 of SEBI Listing Regulations, 2015, as these are 
Unsecured NCDs.
