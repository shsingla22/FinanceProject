PRESS RELEASE CIN: L23209KA1988GOI008959 
MRPL Results New Delhi: 22nd July, 2024 
The Board of Directors of Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals 
Limited (MRPL), a subsidiary company of ONGC and Schedule "A" Mini 
Ratna Category I Company during its 264th Meeting held on July 22, 
2024, approved it's limited reviewed Standalone and Consolidated 
financial results for the first quarter (Q1 FY 2024-25) ended June 30, 
2024. 
Major highlights for the first quarter (Q1 FY 2024-25): 
❖ Ever highest crude processed in any month is recorded in the month 
of May-2024 i.e. 1,593.2 TMT (Previous highest was 1,557.3 TMT in 
Jan-2016). 
❖ Ever highest ATF dispatch recorded in the month of May-2024 i.e. 
230.416 TMT (Previous highest was 227.515 TMT in Feb-24). 
❖ VARANDEY crude from Russia, (API— 37.63) was processed for the 
first time in the month of June 2024. 
❖ EOCENE crude from Saudi - Kuwait Neutral Zone, (API— 18.07) was 
processed for the first time in the month of June 2024. 
❖ KALININGRAD crude from Russia, (API— 39.43) was processed for the 
first time in the month of April 2024. 
❖ June month crude processed - ever highest 1,474.34 TMT (Previous 
highest was 1,463.3 TMT in Jun-2015).
