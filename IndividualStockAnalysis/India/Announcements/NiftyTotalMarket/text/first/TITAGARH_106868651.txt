Titagarh Naval  Systems Limited delivers  DSC A21 to the Indian Navy at Naval Dockyard, Mumbai  
 
Kolkata, 2 3 November 2026: Titagarh Naval Systems Limited (TNSL), a wholly owned subsidiary of Titagarh Rail 
Systems Limited (TRSL), has delivered DSC A21  on 20th Nov, the second of five Diving Support Craft being built for 
the Indian Navy under the Ministry of Defence's Make in India initiative. The vessel was handed over at Naval 
Dockyard, Mumbai, following the delivery of DSC A20 last year.  
Built with a catamaran -type hull, DSC A21 is designed to support specialised diving operations, underwater 
inspections and salvage assistance. Measuring 32.9 metres in length and 13 metres in breadth, the vessel can 
accommodate 18 personnel and has an endu rance of 72 hours at economical speed.  
The vessel has been indigenously designed and built under the relevant Naval Rules and Regulations of the Indian 
Register of Shipping. Hydrodynamic analysis and model testing were undertaken by the Naval Science and 
Technological Laboratory (NSTL), Visakha patnam.  
We take pride in TNSL’s remarkable journey of building its capabilities, infrastructure and expertise over the years. 
Through consistent execution and sustained order wins, TNSL has successfully delivered over 35 vessels to various 
customers, with its vess els today serving across the Indian Ocean.  
Looking ahead, the company is advancing its focus on technology -led shipbuilding, including advanced ship design, 
analytics -driven project management to reduce execution timelines and anticipate supply -chain disruptions, and 
the development of specialised vessels. Artificial intelligence and emerging maritime technologies  and floating 
data centres  are also becoming integral to its future plans.  
Speaking on the occasion, Shri Umesh Chowdhary, Vice -Chairman and Managing Director, Titagarh Rail Systems 
Limited, said:  
"We are grateful to the Indian Navy for its continued trust and to the Government of India for creating an enabling 
environment for indigenous shipbuilding. Our journey in this sector has been one of steadily building capabilities 
alongside our core busine ss, and the delivery of DSC A21 is another important milestone.  
As we look ahead, our focus is on developing next -generation capabilities in green vessels, autonomous and 
underwater vessels, along with further investments in specialised shipbuilding and emerging technologies. We 
aspire to take the Make in India vision to the world by delivering ships that meet global standards. With our 
upcoming greenfield shipyard at Falta, we look forward to contributing further to India's maritime ambitions and 
the evolution of its shipbuilding ecosystem."  
With a growing order book, an expanding manufacturing footprint and an increasing focus on advanced maritime 
technologies, TNSL is positioning itself for the next phase of growth in India's shipbuilding sector.
