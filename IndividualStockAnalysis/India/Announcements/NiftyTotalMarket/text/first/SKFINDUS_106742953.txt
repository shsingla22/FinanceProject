SKF India (Industrial) appoints Sujeeth Pai as its new Managing
Director
Pune, August XX, 2026: SKF India (Industrial) Limited (NSE: SKFINDUS | BSE: 544572) ,
India’s leading technology and solutions provider of bearings and units, condition
monitoring, and services, today announced the appointment of Sujeeth Pai asitsManaging
Director, effective September 1, 2026, subject to shareholder approval. The appointment
was approved by the Nomination Committee and the Board of Directors on August 11,
2026.
In his new role, Sujeeth will lead SKF India (Industrial)’s operations and drive the
company’s profitable growth agenda.
Sujeeth succeeds Mukund Vasudevan , who will assume the role of President for the
Industrial Region covering India ,Southeast Asia & Middle East within the SKF Group . This
governance structure creates a clear distinction between regional strategic oversight and
India -focused operational leadership.
Sujeeth brings over four years of leadership experience within SKF, having held key sales
and operations roles across the organization. Prior to joining SKF, he led the Sales and
Service business for Danfoss Drives India and held senior positions at Tektro nix and Fluke,
managing diverse markets across India, Southeast Asia, Australia, and New Zealand.
He holds a degree in Mechanical Engineering from P.E.S.I.T., Bangalore, and an MBA from
the Indian School of Business (ISB), Hyderabad.
About SKF
Since 1907, SKF has been making some of the world’s most innovative bearings, seals, lubrication systems,
condition monitoring solutions, and services to reduce friction. Less friction means more energy saved and
by reducing it, we make industry smarter, m ore competitive, and more energy efficient, building a more
sustainable future where we can all do more with less. SKF is represented in approximately 130 countries
and has around 17,000 distributor locations worldwide. Annual sales in 2025 were SEK 91,583 million and
the number of employees was 37,271.
