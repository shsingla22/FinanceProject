Opinion 
We have audited the accompanying Statement of Standalone Financial Results of Asian

In our opinion and to the best of our information and according to the explanations given 
to us, these Standalone Financial Results for the quarter and nine months ended December 
31, 2024: 
a. is presented in accordance with the requirements of Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended; and 
b. gives a true and fair view in conformity with the recognition and measurement

this Standalone Financial Results.
