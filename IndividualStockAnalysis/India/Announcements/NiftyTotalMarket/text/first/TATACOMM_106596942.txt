Data Services includes Core connectivity services andDigital platforms & connected services.(Refer note 6a).
Payment Solutions includes end-to-end ATM deployment, end-to-end POS enablement, hosted core banking, end to end

owned subsidiary Tata Communications Payment Solutions Limited. (Refer note 6b).

Limited and its subsidiaries.
