Tata Elxsi delivers healthy  revenue growth of 3. 4% QoQ and 15% YoY in Q2 FY’2 7 
Reports EBIT DA margin growth of 7.1% QoQ and 19.7 % YoY  
 
Bangalore, October  13, 202 6: Tata Elxsi (BSE: 500408 | NSE: TATAELXSI), amongst the world’s leading providers 
of design led technology services, announced its second quarter results for the period ending 30th September 
2026. 
For the second quarter of financial year 202 6-27, the company reported operating revenue of Rs. 1,055.7 Cr. 
growing 3.4% QoQ and 15.0% YoY.  
Highlights of the Quarter Ended 30th September 2026: 
• Revenue  from operations at Rs. 1,055.7  Cr; grew 3.4% QoQ  and 2.3% QoQ in cc terms  
• EBITDA at Rs. 231.4  Cr, grew  7.1% QoQ ; EBITDA Margin at 2 1.9% 
• Revenue from Transportation vertical gr ew 2.0% QoQ in cc terms  
• Revenue from Media and Communications vertical grew 3.4% QoQ  in cc terms  
• Revenue from Healthcare & Life Sciences vertical grew 3.5% QoQ in cc terms  
 
Mr. Manoj Raghavan, CEO and Managing Director, Tata Elxsi , commenting on the company’s performance 
in the second quarter of FY ’27, said:  
“During the second quarter of FY’2 7, Tata Elxsi reported operating revenue of Rs. 1,055.7 crores , growing 
3.4% QoQ in rupee terms and 2.3% QoQ in constant currency . This growth was broad -based across all three 
key verticals . 
The Transportation vertical , which accounts for more than 55% of our SDS segment , posted growth of 2.0% 
QoQ in constant currency . Amongst new deal wins, w e won a strategic multi -year deal from a leading 
automotive OEM for a next -generation cloud -based telematics and connected vehicle ecosystem . 
We are seeing continued traction in adjacencies, esp ecially for the aerospace segment. I am delighted with 
the strategic collaboration with Sarla Aviation to support the development of Shunya, India’s first indigenous 
electric vertical take -off and landing (eVTOL) aircraft.  
Our Media & Communications vertical , which accounted for 33.6% of our SDS segment , registered continued  
growth at 3.4% QoQ  in constant currency . This growth was led by continued deal ramp -ups across  our 
leading customers  and new deal wins . In the quarter, we won a s trategic AI deal from a global technology 
leader for Gen  AI based virtual assistan t focused on multilingual intelligence and global -scale digital 
experiences, reinforcing Tata Elxsi's capabilities in the rapidly expanding GenAI ecosystem.  
During the quarter, o ur healthcare vertical also recorded a healthy growth of 3.5% QoQ in constant currency.  
Tata Elxsi has won a comprehensive product engineering mandate from a global leader in renal care to 
redesign a next -generation home dialysis system . The scope includes full -stack product development and re -
engineering across UX, mechanical engineering, electronics, embedded systems, and regulatory compliance.  
 
Our focus  on operational  efficiencies across the organization  has resulted in improved EBITDA margins, with 
EBITDA expanding 70 bps QoQ , despite salary revisions implemented across the e ntire employee base  in the 
quarter.  
We are continuing to invest ahead in applying Gen AI and AI thoughtfully to the innovation and product 
engineering context in our chosen verticals and domains. This is reflected in the new deals we are winning 
across verticals . In September, we also made a strategic early -stage investment in Silicon Valley -based KAVIA 
AI, expanding a partnership that first began in July 2025 . While most AI tools focus on accelerating coding, 
this collaboration enables enterprises to manage, modernise, and transform complex software 
environments at scale across the AI-Driven Development Lifecycle (AIDLC)  by combining persistent 
knowledge, governed engineering artifacts, collaboration, traceability, and deployment through a single 
platform , backed by the deep domain and process knowledge from Tata Elxsi.  
We have delivered strong operational excellence and resilient growth across customer s and  industry 
verticals in a volatile  macro  environment . We look forward to sustaining growth in the  second half of the 
financial year, even as we continue to invest in differentiated AI -first offerings and services  and focus on 
continued  operational excellence.”
