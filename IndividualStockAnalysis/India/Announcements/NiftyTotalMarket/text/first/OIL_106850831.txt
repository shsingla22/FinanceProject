PRESS STATEMENT  
Reported Fire incident at Indirect Bath Heater (IDBH) at Intermediate Tank Farm 
(ITF)-Tengakhat on 6th November, 2026  
Duliajan, 6 November 2026:  At about 9:05 AM a fire incident took place at IDBH during 
maintenance activities at ITF -Tengakhat near Duliajan of O il India Limited at Dibrugarh District, 
Assam. The incident occurred at indirect bath heater area which was taken for maintenance and 
was in isolation since 4th November 2026.  
Seven persons of the O&M Contractor sustained burn injuries in the fire incident. While six 
persons were rescued and taken  for medical treatment, one person succumbed to the injuries 
at the incident site. Of the six injured persons, five have been taken  to Assam Medical College 
and Hospital (AMCH), Dibrugarh, Assam, while one has been taken  to OIL Hospital, Duliajan. 
Medical treatment is being provided to all the injured persons.  
Fire fighting started through fixed firefighting system of the installation and OIL’s Fire service 
department immediately mobilised fire tender & Dry Chemical Powder (DCP) tender to douse 
the fire, and the fire was extinguished at 12:30 PM and situation is  under control. Total eleven 
fire tenders were deployed at site.  
An internal inquiry committee has been constituted to investigate the incident for immediate and 
underlying cause leading to the incident, damage assessment and suggest proactive measures 
to avoid such incidents in future.  
OIL express its deepest condolence to the bereaved family.
