This is to inform that the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of the Board of Directors 
of the Company vide circular resolution passed today i.e. May 10, 2024, has allotted 46,698 
equity shares of face value of Re. 1/- each of the Company to the eligible employees who have 
exercised their options pursuant to Employees Stock Option Plan 2022 (“ESOP 2022”). 
 
These shares shall rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the Company. Consequent 
to the aforesaid allotment, the paid-up share capital of the Company has increased from 
40,05,88,440 Equity Shares to 40,06,35,138 Equity Shares having face value of Re. 1/- each. 
 
In terms of Regulation 10(c) of the SEBI ESOP Regulations, the statutory details are enclosed 
as “Annexure - I”.
