PRESS RELEASE  
 
Jupiter Wagons secures order for 2,583 BOXNHL and BOXNS 
wagons worth INR 1,140 crore from the Ministry of Railways  
• Combined order for 2,583 wagons across two types, BOXNHL and BOXNS, valued at INR 
1,140 crore  
• Private sector order book remains strong across freight operators, leasing companies 
and core sector customers  
• Company expects incremental order inflows of approximately INR 2,000 crore in Q4 FY27  
 
National, 29th October 2026:  Jupiter Wagons Limited (JWL), one of India’s leading rolling stock and 
mobility solutions companies, has received order from the Ministry of Railways (Railway Board) for the 
manufacture and supply of 2,583 wagons. The order is valued at INR 1,140 crore (incl. of taxes) and 
reflects the Company’s sustained execution record on Indian Railways’ wagon procurement 
programme.  
Alongside its Indian Railways business, JWL’s private sector order book remains strong, supported by 
steady demand from private freight operators, wagon leasing companies and core sector customers. 
This balance between government and private demand continues to give the Company a diversified 
and resilient order pipeline.  
Building on the present award, JWL expects incremental order inflows of approximately INR 2,000 
crore in Q4 FY27.  
The sector’s near-term demand outlook is further strengthened by the Ministry of Railways’ recently 
released tender for the procurement of 5,800 BOBRN wagons.  
Commenting on the order, Mr Vivek Lohia, Managing Director, Jupiter Wagons Limited, said:  
“Indian Railways is adding freight capacity at a scale and pace this country has not seen before, and 
this order reflects the Ministry’s continued confidence in our ability to deliver. Over the years, we have 
integrated backward across the wagon value chain, from castings and bogies to couplers and brake 
systems, and we are now extending this to wheelsets through our facility in Odisha. That gives us 
greater control over cost, quality and delivery timelines. Our private sector order book remains strong, 
and we expect to add around INR 2,000 crore of incremental orders next quarter, giving us healthy 
visibility. We are ready to execute with the discipline this programme demands as India moves a 
greater share of its freight by rail on the path to Viksit Bharat 2047.”  
The order supports the Government of India’s objective of raising the railways’ share of freight 
movement under the National Rail Plan. Large-scale wagon procurement, together with the Dedicated 
Freight Corridors and PM Gati Shakti, is central to building a faster, more efficient and self-reliant 
freight network for the country.
