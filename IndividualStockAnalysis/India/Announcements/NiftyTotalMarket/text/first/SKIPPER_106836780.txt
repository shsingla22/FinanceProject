Q2, FY 27 HIGHLIGHTS  
 
Skipper Limited Records Highest-Ever Q2 Revenue; PAT Rises 56% 
Margin Expansion Continues with Record Order Book and Strong Export Growth 
National, 30th October 2026: Skipper Limited  (BSE: 538562) and NSE (Symbol: SKIPPER) is one of the 
world's leading manufacturers for Power Transmission & Distribution structures. It is a major EPC player 
in 765 kV transmission lines and substation, and a prominent manufacturer of Telecom and Railway 
structures. Skipper, also a significant player in Polymer Pipes & Fittings industry, announced its results for 
the second quarter of FY 27, ended 30th September 2026.

Director Speaks  
Commenting on the results, Mr. Sharan Bansal, Director, Skipper Limited, said: “Q2 FY’27 reflects our 
continued focus on profitable growth, with revenue reaching a record Rs. 13,840 million for the quarter, up 
10% year-on-year, and PAT growing 56% to Rs. 575 million. For H1 FY’27, we recorded our highest-ever 
half-yearly revenue of Rs. 26,938 million and PAT of Rs. 1,139 million, up 40% year-on-year. Our EBITDA 
margins also improved, reflecting operating leverage and disciplined execution. Despite geopolitical 
developments in West Asia impacting exports during the period, our business fundamentals remain 
strong, supported by a record closing order book of over Rs. 100,657 million and an all-time high bidding 
pipeline exceeding Rs. 350,000 million. We remain focused on sustaining our growth momentum, 
strengthening profitability and expanding our presence across domestic and international markets.”  
Speaking on the results, Mr. Devesh Bansal, Director, Skipper Limited, said: “Our performance in Q2 
and H1 FY’27 reflects the strength of our execution capabilities and continued demand for our Engineering 
business. During the period, we secured our highest-ever quarterly export order inflows of Rs. 10,540 
million and a multi-million-dollar contract in North America, our largest order to date in the region, further 
strengthening our international presence. The ongoing 75,000 MTPA capacity expansion is on track and 
likely to be commissioned and operational by H2’27, taking our total installed capacity to 450,000 MTPA 
from existing 375,000 MTPA. With the establishment of our US subsidiary, complementing our presence in 
Brazil and the UAE, we are well positioned to deepen our global reach and capitalise on emerging 
opportunities across key markets.” 
ABOUT SKIPPER LIMITED  
Established in 1981, Skipper Limited is one of the leading companies in the Power Transmission & 
Distribution and Polymer segments. With over 45 years of domain knowledge, it is the largest in India and 
fifth largest globally based on manufacturing capacity. Skipper differentiates its offerings with high-quality 
yet cost-effective solutions for infrastructure providers and telecom operators. Its international footprint 
spans continents such as Latin America, Europe, and Africa, and covers 65+ countries, with a presence 
across sub-segments such as Towers, EPC, Monopoles, Poles, and Railway Electrification Structures. 
Skipper Limited is a national powerhouse in the polymer pipe business. The company manufactures 
premium-quality polymer pipes and fittings under the 'Skipper’ brand for the agricultural and plumbing 
sectors. Skipper Limited is listed on the BSE (538562) and NSE (Symbol: SKIPPER), since 2014 and 2015 
respectively.
