In compliance with Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, this is to inform you that Optiemus Unmanned Systems Private Limited (“OUS”), a Wholly 
Owned Subsidiary of Optiemus Infracom Limited, has appointed  Lieutenant General Anil Chandra Chait , as a 
Consultant , for availing his services on retainer basis in respect of providing and developing roadmaps, strategies 
pertaining to marketing and selling of OUS’s unmanned systems for the Defence and Home Land Security business of 
the Company within the Indian Territory. 
 
This would result in providing strategic direction for the sustainable growth of Optiemus Group and would boost its 
brand and credibility. 
 
Brief Profile of Lt General Anil Chandra Chait is given below: 
 
Lieutenant General Anil Chandra Chait is having more than 40 years of experience in serving Indian Defence/ Armed 
forces and a former General Officer of the Indian Army. Lt Gen Chait played a pivotal role in organizing the 2007 Military 
World Games and was instrumental in the Indian Army's response to the 2013 North India floods, leading operations 
such as Operation Ganga Prahar and Operation Surya Hope.  
 
Throughout his service, Lt Gen Chait was recognized for his contributions and was awarded the Param Vishisht Seva 
Medal (PVSM), Ati Vishisht Seva Medal (AVSM), and Vishisht Seva Medal (VSM ) and was appointed  ADC . He was 
also awarded the Yash Bharti Samman by the UP Government for his services to the State and the Nation. His 
contributions have had a lasting impact on the Indian Army's operational effectiveness and have established 
benchmarks in humanitarian response and disaster management within military contexts.
