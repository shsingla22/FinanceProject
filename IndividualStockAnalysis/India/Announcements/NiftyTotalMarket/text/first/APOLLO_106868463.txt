Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, Apollo Micro Systems 
Limited ("the Company" / "AMSL") informs that its subsidiary, Apollo Defence Industries 
Private Limited ("ADIPL"), has on 23rd November 2026 issued a Binding Expression of 
Interest ("EOI") for the acquisition of 100% ownership of the following entities (together, 
"the IEC Group"): 
 
1. Integrated Electric Co Private Limited  – 100% of the equity share capital; 
2. IE Power Technologies Private Limited  – 100% of the equity share capital; and 
3. M/s Integrated Services  (partnership firm) – 100% of the partnership interest and 
business. 
The proposed acquisition marks the entry of the Apollo Micro Systems Limited group into 
indigenous design and manufacture of electrical rotating machines, generators and drives. 
These are core building blocks for electric propulsion, onboard power generation and 
actuation across land, air and naval defence platforms. The IEC Group's product portfolio 
complements the Company's existing capabilities in DRDO programmes, enriching the 
value the Company delivers and captures in each programme. 
 
Profile of the IEC Group 
The IEC Group is a Bengaluru-based electrical engineering group with over four decades 
in designing and making electrical rotating machines and drives. It started in 1982 in the 
Peenya Industrial Area, one of India's largest engineering and aerospace manufacturing 
clusters. 
 
Business 
IEC designs and manufactures electrical rotating machines and drives for industrial use. 
Its products are developed in-house and built to international standards. The group serves 
steel, paper, sugar, cement, plastics, railways and telecom customers, and exports to Asia, 
Africa, Australia, Europe and the Americas.

Manufacturing footprint 
The group runs multi-unit manufacturing in Bengaluru, including Units 2 and 3 and a 
leasehold land parcel at Somapura Village. As part of the transaction, these land assets 
will be consolidated in the name of the target companies, so the business transfers with its 
full operating footprint. Its customer reach is backed by a sales and service presence 
across major Indian industrial centres. 
 
The acquisition gives Apollo Micro Systems Limited a proven, in-house capability in electric 
machines and drives, the power and motion layer under its electronics, guidance and 
weapon-system programmes. 
 
The EOI carries binding obligations on confidentiality, exclusivity and standstill. Completion 
is subject to satisfactory due diligence, execution of definitive agreements, approval of the 
Boards of ADIPL and the Company, and requisite statutory approvals. The consideration 
and other commercial terms will be disclosed upon execution of the definitive agreements. 
 
The Company will keep the Exchanges informed of material developments.
