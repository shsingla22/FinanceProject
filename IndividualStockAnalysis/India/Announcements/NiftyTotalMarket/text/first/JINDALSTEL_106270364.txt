Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)

 The Board , based on the recommendations of the Nomination & Remuneration Committee,  has 
approved certain amendments to the Scheme, subject to the approval of the shareholders of the 
Company, in compliance with the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, read with rules thereunder and the SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021.

 The Board has approved the change in the name of the Company from ‘Jindal Steel & Power Limited’ to ‘Jindal Steel Limited’, along with the consequential amendments to the 
Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association, in order to align the Company’s 
brand identity with its core business and strategic focus.  
 
Please note that this change is subject to the approval of the Shareholders of the Company, the Ministry of Corporate Affairs and other applicable statutory authorities, as required.
