We wish to inform you that Archean Chemical Industries Limited ("ACIL" / "the Company"), 
along with its Wholly Owned Subsidiary Neun Infra Private Limited ("Neun Infra"), and its 
step-down subsidiary SiCSem Private Limited ("SiCSem"), has executed the Fiscal Support 
Agreement ("FSA") with the India Semiconductor Mission ("ISM") , on May 11, 2026, 
pursuant to the Semicon India Programme of the Government of India. 
 
The signing of the FSA marks an important procedural milestone in the implementation of 
the eligible fiscal support as notified under the scheme guidelines of the Semicon India 
Programme of the Government of India. The FSA formalises the fiscal incentive framework 
under which the Government of India has committed capital expenditure (CapEx) support 
to SiCSem for the establishment of India's first approved Silicon Carbide (SiC) based 
Compound Semiconductor Fab and ATMP facility. 
 
The compound semiconductor manufacturing facility is proposed to be established at Info 
Valley, Bhubaneswar, Odisha. The project envisages an investment of approximately Rs. 
2,067 crore and will integrate end-to-end Wafer Fabrication and Packaging capabilities, 
targeting an annual capacity of ~60,000 SiC wafers and ~96 million SiC MOSFETs and 
diodes. The facility will cater to critical sectors including Electric Vehicles (EVs), Renewable 
Energy, Smart Grids, 5G, Industrial Automation, Data Centres etc. 
 
This execution is in continuation of the earlier intimations made by the Company, including 
the ISM approval received by SiCSem on August 12, 2025, and the subsequent inter-
corporate funding extended by the Company to its subsidiary, Neun Infra Private Limited to 
advance procurement of long-lead machinery and equipment, pending the execution of 
the FSA.
