Pursuant to Regulation 30 and other applicable regulations of the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing

considered and approved: 
Raising of funds by way of issuance of equity shares of the Company through a Qualified 
Institutions Placement (“QIP”), in accordance with the applicable provisions of the 
Companies Act, 2013, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue of Capital and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, as amended, and other applicable acts, rules 
and regulations, subject to the approval of the shareholders, the stock exchanges and receipt 
of such regulatory, statutory and other approvals as may be applicable, with a view to 
achieving and maintaining the minimum public shareholding requirements of the 
Company. 
In order to give effect to the above, the Board has constituted and authorised the “QIP 
Committee” of the Board, comprising the Directors, to, inter alia, determine the size, timing, 
number and price of the issue, identify eligible Qualified Institutional Buyers (‘QIBs’), 
approve allocation and allotment, appoint necessary intermediaries and advisors, finalise the 
placement and other transaction documents, make requisite filings and applications with the 
concemed authorities, obtain listing and trading approvals, and undertake all other acts, 
deeds and matters necessary or incidental to the proposed fund raising. 
The Company will seek approval from the shareholders in relation to the matter mentioned 
above through postal ballot, subject to applicable provisions/ circulars issued by the Ministry 
of Corporate Affairs from time to time. The postal ballot notice will be circulated to the 
shareholders in due course, in accordance with applicable laws.
