EXTRACT OF THE MINUTES OF THE MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF 
ALKEM LABORATORIES LTD. HELD ON 09m FEBRUARY, 2024 AT THE REGISTERED 
OFFICE OF THE COMPANY. 
Consider and review the requests received from certain members of Promoter Group for 
reclassification from "Promoter Group" category to "Public" category of the Company . 
The Board was informed that Ms. Jayanti Sinha, member of the Promoter Group of the Company had 
transferred 22,00,000 equity shares (1.84% of the equity share capital of the Company) by way of gift to 
her grandchildren namely Mr. Shikhar Raj, Ms. Sadhika Raj, Ms. Sakshi Sinha and Mr. Satyam Sinha 
("Members ") in equal proportion i.e. 5,50,000 equity shares each (0.46%). Accordingly, the said 
Members had been reclassified as the members of Promoter Group pursuant to Regulation 31A(6) of 
SEBI (LODR) Regulations. The above-mentioned persons seeking reclassification would not have been 
classified as members of the promoter group if it were not for the gift of equity shares received from an 
existing member of the promoter group. 
The Board was further informed that the Company had received reque st in the form of applications dated 
30th January, 2024 from the said Members seeking reclassification from 'Promoter Group' to 'Public' 
Category in terms of the Regulation 31A of SEBI (LODR) Regulation s, 2015 ("Regulation "). 
Accordingly , the Company had on 30th January, 2024 intimated to the Stock Exchanges about the receipt 
of the reclassification request from the Members of the 'Promoter Group' to 'Public' category. A copy 
of the said disclosure was tabled before the Board for its perusal. 
In terms of the said Regulation, the request for re-classification of any person to a Promoter / Promoter 
Group or Public category shall be permitted by the Stock Exchange(s) where the Company is listed, 
subject to fulfillment of conditions as provided therein. The relevant provisions of the said Regulation 
were tabled before the Board for its perusal. 
Pursuant to the provisions of the said Regulation, the Board was required to consider and analyze the 
reclassification request received from the Members of the Promoter Group and place the same before the 
shareholders for their approval along with its views on the same.
