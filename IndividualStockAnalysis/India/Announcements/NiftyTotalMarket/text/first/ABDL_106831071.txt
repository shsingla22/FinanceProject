Reported highest -ever quarterly sales volumes of 10.0 m illion  cases in Q2FY27  driven by growth 
across all 4 millionaire brands on q -o-q basis

expect the topline growth momentum to continue. We  remain focused on driving operational

 Performance Review for Q2FY27  and Recent Developments : 
1) Growth across the Portfolio and Region s 
• Delivered 10.0  million cases in Q 2FY27, compared to 9.0 million cases in Q 2FY26, reflecting  
overall volume growth of 11.1 %, with g rowth across Mass Premium & Others and Prestige & 
Above (P&A) categories .  
• This strong volume growth momentum has been visible through H1FY27 , during which we 
achieved record monthly sales on three occasions, culminating in our highest -ever monthly 
volume of over 3.5 million cases in September 2026 . 
• Premiumization momentum continue d, with the P&A value segment salience at 58% in 
H1FY27, compared to 56% in H1FY26 while P&A volume sal ience  is 46% in H1FY27, compared 
to 47% in H1FY26.   
• Growth across key domestic and international markets driven by millionaire brands . 
• ICONiQ White has been  to be the world’s fastest -growing Millionaire Whisky Brand across  three

to exceed  1 million cases .  
 
2) Premium Whisky Portfolio: Launch of The Indian Edit  
• Launched in September 2026, The Indian Edit strengthens ABD’s premium whisky portfolio, 
blending Indian malt and grain spirits with fine Scotch malts and featuring packaging inspired 
by Indian heritage.  
• Available in Maharashtra, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Assam, Tripura, Rajasthan and Goa, with 
further expansion to other markets, the brand has received an encouraging response from 
trade partners and consumers .  
• Offered in 180 ml, 500 ml and 750 ml  SKUs , the 750 ml pack is priced at ₹1,550 in Maharashtra.  
 
3) Officer’s Choice Blue: Refreshed Identity with Premium Packaging  
• Officer’s Choice Blue unveiled a refreshed identity and premium packaging, featuring a sleeker 
bottle, a transparent label with matte gold foiling and an embossed “Trusted Since 1988” 
marker, celebrating its legacy while retaining its promise of quality an d consistency.  
• Rolled out from September 2026 across Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Haryana and West 
Bengal, the refreshed packaging has received an encouraging customer response, with more 
markets to follow in phases.  
 
4) Commissioning of Bottling Unit  in Moradabad : Strengthening Manufacturing Presence in Uttar 
Pradesh  
• In October 2026, ABD commenced commercial operations at its bottling facility in Moradabad, 
Uttar Pradesh, with an initial capacity of 6 million cases per annum. The facility was part of 
the assets acquired in January 2026 for ~ ₹70 crore and was subsequently upgraded with an 
investment of ~ ₹40 crore.  
• The facility strengthens ABD’s in -house bottling capabilities in one of its key markets and is 
expected to improve service efficiency while delivering cost benefits through increased in -
house bottling and savings on franchise fees.  
 
  
 
5) Commissioning of Malt Distillery in Rangapur : Strengthening Captive Malt Capabilities  
• In October 2026, ABD commissioned its captive malt distillery at Rangapur, Telangana, with an 
investment of ~ ₹75 crore and an annual capacity of ~ 4.0 million bulk litres . The facility is 
integrated with ABD’s existing manufacturing complex, which also houses an ENA distillery, an 
IMFL bottling unit and a PET bottle manufacturing plant.  
• The distillery is expected to support the captive malt requirements of ABD’s growing P&A  
portfolio, reduce dependence on external sourcing and support the development of its future 
single malt whisky portfolio.  
 
6) International Markets : Expanding Presence across Geographies  
• 1st International manufacturing facility:  In August 2026, ABD announced its first overseas 
local production arrangement for Officer’s Choice Blue in Malaysia through an asset -light co -
bottling model with an established local partner. The initiative marks an important step in 
ABD’s expansion in Sou theast Asia, enabling faster market entry with limited capital 
deployment while maintaining oversight of product quality and brand standards.  
• Continued Expansion:  ABD expanded its international footprint to 42 countries in Q2FY27 , 
compared to 36 countries in Q4FY26 . During H1FY27, it has expanded in Europ ean and 
African markets and in Australia.   
• ABD Maestro’s super -premium and luxury portfolio  continued to expand internationally 
and is  now present in Australia, Canada, Ivory Coast, New Zealand, Rwanda, Sierra Leone and 
the UAE.  
 
7) Key Awards and Recognitions:  Spiritz Conclave & Achievers’ Awards 2026  
• ABD Maestro Private Limited was named ‘Company of the Year 2026 (Domestic) ’ and received 
Gold, recognising  the strength of its super -premium and luxury spirits portfolio.  
• RANGEELA Contemporary Indian Vodka won the competition’s most coveted  ‘Grand Gold’ 
Medal  in the blind tasting category.  YELLO Designer Whisky and AODH Irish Whiskey each

 About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (www.abdindia.com ) 
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD) is one of India’s  leading spirits companies and the country’s largest 
spirits exporter by volume , with a presence in 42 international markets . The Company  operates across whisky, 
brandy, rum, vodka and gin. Its portfolio  is anchored by "millionaire" brands, including the flagship Officer’s 
Choice, which remains among the largest -selling whiskies globally. This is complemented by Sterling Reserve 
and the rapidly scaling ICONiQ White, which has been  the world’s fastest -growing millionaire whisky brand  for 
3 consecutive calendar years – CY2023, CY2024 and CY2025 . As part of its premiumisation  journey, ABD has 
expanded into the super -premium and luxury segments through its subsidiary - ABD Maestro Pvt Ltd. The 
company maintains a robust manufacturing footprint of 4 1 units including 10 owned bottling facilities, 2 owned 
ENA and 1 malt distilleries, 1 owned PET bottle plant and 2 7 non-owned manufacturing units  (including 1 in 
Malaysia ), supported by a pan -India distribution network that ensures scale and supply chain flexibility.
