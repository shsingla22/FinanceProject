Sterling Forays Into the Leopard Sanctuary of Rajasthan with  
Sterling Ratan Villas Jawai . 
 
• Sterling’s 8th Resort in Rajasthan, with more in pipeline  
• Marks  13 Resorts  in Premier Wildlife Destinations of India  
• Upscale Boutique Resort with Focus on Exclusive and Indulgent Experiences  
 
10th February 2025  – Sterling Holiday Resorts unveils Sterling Ratan Villas Jawai, an 
elegant jungle retreat set amidst the dramatic landscapes of Rajasthan’s premier 
leopard country. This marks Sterling’s eighth property in Rajasthan, further deepening 
its presence in a st ate known for its wildlife, heritage, and distinctive landscapes.  
Strategically located near Jawai Band h, the region’s prime leopard -sighting zone, the 
resort offers a seamless blend of raw nature and refined opulence . Situated  amidst  30 
acres of unspoiled farmland, it caters to those who seek both adventure and serenity —
whether tracking Jawai’s elusive leopards or unwinding in its pristine wilderness.  
“Sterling Ratan Villas Jawai is a key addition to our Rajasthan portfolio. The state’s 
unique mix of wildlife and cultural tourism makes it an undeniable choice for discerning 
travelers. This also brings to itinerary a one -of-a-kind circuit between our Sterling 
resorts in a Hill-station like Mount Abu , Heritage destination like Udaipur and 
Wilderness escape like Jawai .” said Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director & CEO, 
Sterling Holiday Resorts.  
Designed to blend seamlessly with the terrain, the resort features thoughtfully curated 
accommodations, ranging from intimate Jawai Rooms to expansive Pool Villas with 
private courtyards. Select suites also offer private plunge pools, creating an exclusive  
retreat amid st the wilderness , and for large families, the 2  and 4 -Bedroom Suites with 
additional large sized bunker beds offer more avenues to relax.  
The resort’s culinary experiences celebrate Rajasthan’s rich flavors, with Chul ha, an all -
day dining restaurant, serving authentic local delicacies like Godwadi Maans and Pather 
Kutta Gosht, crafted with fresh, locally sourced ingredients. Soviet  – the bar and 
alfresco  inspired by 1970s Soviet graffiti, offers a bold selection of regional spirits and 
inventive cocktails, while  Robin , the coffee lounge and library, provides a laid -back 
escape with artisanal brews and fusion snacks.  
  
 
 
“Sterling Ratan Villas Jawai is designed to deliver a promise of luxury to our travellers. 
Our commitment to the  product is unmatched , it is carefully crafted  to present  the 
charm  of Jawai in its  décor and  facilities. Aligning with the product is our association 
with Sterling Holidays – India’s leading leisure hospitality brand . We are confident that 
together we will bring more attention and much needed boost to the destination. ” said 
Sawai Singh Chaudhary  and Siddharth Singh Chaudhary  Owner s, Ratan  Villas 
Hospitality.  
The resort  goes beyond opulent accommodations and gourmet dining —it is designed as 
a gateway to extraordinary experiences. Guests can embark on day/night jeep safaris, 
led by expert trackers . For those seeking cultural immersion, heritage treks offer a 
glimpse into the timeless traditions of the Rabari community, whose nomadic lifestyle 
has remained unchanged for centuries , and a guided visit to Ranakpur Temple, and 
Kumbhalgarh fort.  Guests can also experience private B ush Dinners by the crocodile 
pond, a surreal setting where nature takes center stage under a canopy of stars.  
The resort also caters to wellness seekers with bespoke experiences designed for 
relaxation and rejuvenation. From sundowner with high tea  by the Jawai River  to 
curated nature treks  that reveal the region’s diverse flora and fauna, every aspect of the 
stay is crafted to provide a deeper connection with the land . Whether it’s an expedition 
into the wild or a star gazing experience , this retreat offers a rare chance to connect 
with Rajasthan in its most authentic form
