Shri S.K. Sinha assumes charge as Director (Finance) of GAIL (India) Limited  
  
New Delhi, July 1, 2026:  Shri S.K. Sinha today assumed the charge as Director (Finance) 
of GAIL (India) Limited, India’s integrated energy major.   
 
Prior to joining the Board of Directors, he has served as Executive Director (Finance & 
Accounts).  
  
A qualified Cost and Management Accountant with an illustrious career spanning over three 
decades in oil and gas sector, Shri Sinha possesses extensive expertise in Corporate Finance, 
Corporate Accounts, Management Accounting, Investor Relations, Taxation, Treasury & 
Banking Operations, Costing, Budgeting, Project Evaluation, and Mergers & Acquisitions. 
Throughout his career, he has played a pivotal role in strengthening GAIL’s financial 
systems, enhancing investor engagement, and driving strategic financial decision -making.  
 
Shri Sinha joined GAIL (India) Limited as a Management Trainee (Finance & Accounts) in 
1994 and has since held several key positions across core finance functions. He has 
contributed significantly to value creation through governance, compliance, and cost 
optimization initiatives, large -scale investment appraisals, and effective  treasury 
management. He has been instrumental in driving digital transformation initiatives in 
finance, including SAP implementation, automation, and centralized financial processes.  
He has also served on the Boards of GAIL Global Singapore Pte Limited, and GAIL 
Mangalore Petrochemicals Limited, contributing to key strategic and operational milestones.  
 
He has received multiple professional accolades, including securing 2nd Rank in Asia for IR 
Professional in the Extel Asia Survey 2026, along with recognitions from ICAI and ICPA 
for excellence in cost management and finance leadership. Shri Sinha brings a strong blend 
of financial acumen and strategic perspective that will fu rther reinforce GAIL’s financial 
strength and support its evolution as an integrated energy company.  
 
Under his leadership, GAIL has achieved notable milestones in financial reporting 
excellence, investor relations recognition, and sustained shareholder value creation.
