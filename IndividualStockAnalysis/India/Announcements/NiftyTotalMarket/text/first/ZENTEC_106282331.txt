Zen Technologies Secures Patent for Innovation in Laser -Based Combat Training
Zen Technologies  Limited  (“Zen”)  is pleased  to announce  the grant  of its 54th Indian  
patent,  titled  “Single  ILU Long  Pass Filter”,  marking  a significant  breakthrough  in laser -
based  military  training  systems . This patent  also represents  the company’s  82nd patent  
globally,  reinforcing  Zen’s  leadership  in defence  innovation  through  indigenous  research  
and development .
The newly  patented  long-pass optical  filter  enables  the seamless  integration  of visible  
and infrared  laser  beams  into a single,  stable  output . This innovation  enhances  the 
accuracy,  realism,  and responsiveness  of combat  training  simulators . Unlike  traditional  
systems  that rely on separate  beams  and often  face issues  related  to alignment  and 
durability,  Zen’s  compact  and robust  solution  delivers  superior  optical  performance  
under  rugged  field conditions,  while  significantly  reducing  the need  for recalibration  and 
maintenance .
In addition  to bolstering  domestic  capabilities,  this technological  leap opens  significant  
export  opportunities  for Zen. With  several  nations  actively  seeking  to modernize  their 
military  training  infrastructure,  Zen’s  high-performance,  cost-effective,  and low-
footprint  solution  is well-positioned  to address  international  demand . The patented  filter  
system’s  compact  design  and modularity  make  it ideal  not only for full-scale  simulator  
environments  but also for mobile  deployment  and export -oriented  models .
This development  further  demonstrates  India's  growing  capability  to design  and deliver  
advanced  defence  technologies  through  homegrown  innovation . By reducing  reliance  on 
foreign  components  and emphasizing  domestic  R&D,  Zen contributes  to building  a more  
self-sufficient  and globally  competitive  defence  manufacturing  ecosystem . The patent  
adds to Zen’s  expanding  intellectual  property  portfolio  and highlights  the strategic  
importance  of sustained  investment  in indigenous  technological  advancement .PR ES S  & MED IA R ELEAS E
