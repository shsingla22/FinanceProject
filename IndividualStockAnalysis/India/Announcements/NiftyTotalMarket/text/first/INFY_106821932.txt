PRESS RELEASE 
Infosys and Truist Strengthen Strategic Collaboration to Build a Global Capability 
Center in India  
Bengaluru, India –  October 19, 2026: Infosys  (NSE, BSE, NYSE:INFY), a global leader in AI -first 
business consulting and technology services, recently announced the expansion of its collaboration with 
Truist , one of the largest US -based financial services companies, to support the bank’s continued build- out 
of its AI -first Global Capability Center in Hyderabad. Together with Infosys, Truist is strengthening India’s 
role as a key global hub for innovation, tal ent, and digital capability.  
 
Through this expanded collaboration, Infosys will utilize its deep financial services expertise and AI -first 
capabilities, to help accelerate scalable transformation and drive enterprise- wide value creation. As part of 
this effort, Truist is consolidating capabilities currently delivered through multiple supplier relationships in 
India into a more unified, integrated operating model. Infosys and Truist will celebrate the successful 
onboarding of more than 1,000 colleagues later this month, marking a signifi cant milestone in the continued 
growth and expansion of the GCC.  
 
The GCC represents  Truist’s continued investment in building a scaled, future -ready innovation and 
operations hub designed to enhance client experiences, empower teammates, and help deliver a more 
digitally enabled and relationship- driven banking experience. Infosys will enable Truist to build, scale, and 
transition a self -sustaining center of excellence, integrating AI, technology, data, and operations into a 
single cohesive delivery framework. This model is designed to reduce third- party dependency, manage 
global delivery complexity, and cultivate deep, future -ready technology talent.  
 
India represents a critical part of Truist’s evolving operating model, with a strong base of Truist contract 
professionals already contributing across engineering, data, and operations. Building on its long- standing 
relationship with Truist, Infosys will s upport the continued scaling of Truist’s GCC, helping to enable 
seamless cross -geography collaboration and faster delivery in an increasingly digital and AI -driven industry.  
 
Steve Hagerman, Chief Information Officer, Truist, said,  “Our Global Capability Center in India is a 
strategic investment in Truist’s future. As we accelerate our transformation into a more agile, AI -enabled 
enterprise, we are intentionally building global capabilities that allow us to move faster, innovat e at scale, 
and better serve our clients. This center will play a critical role in helping Truist build and scale strategic 
capabilities through a more integrated operating model improving client experience and empowering 
 
 
teammates. Through this collaboration with Infosys, we are building not just capacity, but enduring expertise 
that strengthens how we operate as a global organization.”  
 
Dennis Gada, EVP and Global Head of Banking and Financial Services, Infosys, said, "We are proud 
to deepen our long -standing relationship with Truist as they evolve their presence in India into a strategic 
engine for AI -led transformation and reimagination. Financial institutions today must move decisively from 
modernizing legacy  infrastructure to deploying AI at scale across every function. By leveraging Infosys 
Topaz, our proven Build, Operate and Transfer enablement model and deep financial services expertise, 
we are committed to helping Truist build a resilient, scalable digit al core - and unlock AI value at scale - 
with trust and governance- built in.”
