Key businesses continue to deliver strong  operating performance : 
▪ Aluminium  
o Highest ever annual aluminium cast metal production at 2,370  kt, up 3% YoY.

o Stands in the first quartile  of the global cost curve.

▪ Zinc India  
o Hindustan Zinc becomes the 3rd largest producer of Silver globally . 
o Highest -ever annual mined metal, refined metal,  and silver production at 1,079 kt, 1,033 kt and 
746 mt respectively.  
o Strong foothold  in the first decile of the global zinc mines cost curve.  
o Consecutive 5 quarters of cost optimisation with lowest zinc cost of production in last 3 years , at 
$1,051/t  in 4QFY24 .

~35% in last 5 years ( net of 65.1 million tonnes of ore  production )
