Press Release 
Mumbai,  30th September  2025
Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Limited,  a developer  of luxury  and  ultra  luxury  residential  and  
commercial  properties  in Mumbai,  with  a focus  in redevelopment  & joint  development  
projects  announced  the successful  launch  of two new  projects   The Arcadian  (Juhu)  & Amalfi  
(Versova)  ✓Launches The Arcadian (Juhu) & Amalfi (Versova) with strong 
initial bookings worth ₹130 crore; 
✓Set to launch Project Varun (Bandra) in Q3 FY26
KEY  UPDATE  ON LAUNCH  
1❖ The  Arcadian  (Juhu) : Launched  in September  2025  and construction  is progressing  in full 
swing . The project  has achieved  bookings  worth  ₹92 crore  within  the first week  of launch . 
The project  has an estimated  GDV  of ₹ 700 crore .
❖ Amalfi  (Versova) : Launched  in September  2025  and construction  is progressing  in full 
swing . The project  has achieved  bookings  worth  ₹38 crore,  within  the first week  of launch . 
The project  has an estimated  GDV  of ₹300 crore . 
❖ Both  projects  continue  to witness  strong  enquiries,  with  demand  driven  by their  prime  
locations,  the distinct  B&G  architectural  theme,  world -class  amenities,  and Lotus’  proven  
record  of quality  construction  and timely  delivery . 
❖ Project  Varun  (Bandra) : Construction  is progressing  in full swing  at Project  Varun  in Bandra,  
and the company  expects  to launch  the project  in Q3FY26. Positioned  in one of Mumbai’s  
most  sought -after  addresses,  this ultra -luxury  development  will further  strengthen  Lotus  
Developers’  presence  in the premium  segment .
Mr. Anand  K Pandit,  Chairman  & Managing  Director,  Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Limited,  said,  
“We have  received  strong  response  to the launch  of our marquee  projects  – The Arcadian  in Juhu  
and Amalfi  in Versova  – a testimony  to the continuing  demand  uptick  we are witnessing  in the 
luxury  real estate  segment  and our chosen  micro  markets .  Achieving  such  healthy  bookings  in the 
1st week  of launch  reflects  the trust  homebuyers  place  in the Lotus  brand,  our deep  understanding  
of Mumbai’s  micro -markets,  and our proven  expertise  in luxury  redevelopment . With  Project  Varun  
in Bandra  scheduled  for launch  in Q3FY26, we are continuing  the momentum  of FY25 in FY26 which  
we expect  to be a strong  growth  year  in line with  our guidance  of Pre-Sales  of Rs 1,100-1,300 Crs, 
Revenue  growth  of 75-85% YoY and PAT growth  of 30-35% YoY.”

About  Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Limited
Incorporated  in 2015  (formerly  AKP Holdings  Limited),  Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Ltd. is 
a Mumbai -based  real estate  developer  specializing  in luxury  and ultra -luxury  residential  
and premium  commercial  projects,  with  a strong  focus  on redevelopment  in the western  
suburbs  of Mumbai . The company  has completed  4 projects  aggregating  over  4.2 lakh  
sq. ft. of carpet  area,  with  5 ongoing  and 11 upcoming  developments  covering  ~25.5 lakh  
sq. ft. of carpet  area  and ~18.7 lakh  sq. ft. of saleable  area
With  a strong  presence  in Juhu  and Andheri  West  and the company  plans  to expand  into 
South  and Central  Mumbai,  Bandra,  and Versova . Lotus  Developers  manages  end-to-
end execution —from  design  and construction  to sales  and customer  engagement —
ensuring  consistent  quality,  superior  materials,  and customer  satisfaction . Guided  by its 
philosophy  of creating  inclusive  and high -quality  living  spaces,  the company  is shaping  
some  of the most  coveted  addresses  in Mumbai’s  real estate  landscape
