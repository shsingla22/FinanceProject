Firstsource named a Leader in three ISG Provider Lens® 2026 
quadrants across Generative AI and Agentic AI Services  
 
Second consecutive year as a Leader in both GenAI midsize quadrants, and a first -time 
Leader in Agentic AI  
 
Mumbai, India | November  05, 2026:  Firstsource Solutions Limited  (NSE: FSL, BSE: 532809), a global intelligence partner and 
an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company, has been named a Leader in three midsize quadrants across two ISG Provider Lens® 2026 
reports. In Generative AI Services, Firstsource is a Leader in Strategy and  Consulting Services and in Development and Deployment 
Services, the second consecutive year it has led both. In Agentic AI Services, it is a Leader in Agentic AI Development and 
Deployment Services.  
 
Together, the three quadrants cover the full path of Kairos, Firstsource’s operating system for AI -native operations. Its Transform, 
Implement and Operate model takes clients from deciding where AI should change work, to engineering that change into produc tion, 
to running agents within governed workflows.  
 
Hasit Trivedi, Chief Digital & AI Officer, Firstsource, said, “Enterprises don’t need more AI pilots . They need to rethink how work 
gets done: redesigning processes, using GenAI where it adds value and giving agents clearly defined responsibilities, with hu man 
judgment at the right points. Ultimately, t hey want outcome s delivered. At Firstsource, Kairos is how we make that real : Intelligence 
That Operates . ISG’s recognition across strategy, engineering and agentic execution reflects that full-stack Transform, Implement 
and Operate  approach .” 
 
Gowtham Kumar Sampath, Assistant Director & Principal Analyst, ISG, said, “ Firstsource combines domain -led GenAI with 
governed agentic execution, embedding contextual intelligence into enterprise workflows and progressively extending AI from 
assistance to coordinated action. Its Kairos, Intelligent Context Framework and Agentic A I Studio connect process knowledge, 
orchestration, human oversight and runtime governance, creating a foundation for scalable, controlled AI -driven operations. ”  
 
What ISG highlighted  
Across the two reports, ISG highlights Firstsource’s operations -led approach that keeps AI strategy tied to how work will actually run . 
It also cites reusable domain engineering that lets clients start from proven, productized components rather than bespoke bui lds. In 
agentic AI, ISG points to agent design grounded in domain playbooks, orchestration through Agentic AI Studio, and the Int elligent 
Context Framework (ICF). The ICF captures the exceptions and decisions that standard procedures miss, so each deployment 
strengthens the next.  
 
ISG also emphasizes that as agents act across enterprise systems, permissions, escalation paths and human oversight matter as  
much as the agents themselves. Firstsource’s aim is not maximum autonomy but reliable execution within clear boundaries, with  
autonomy extended as trust is earned.
