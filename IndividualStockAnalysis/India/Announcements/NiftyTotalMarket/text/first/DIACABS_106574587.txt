Receipt of Major Order for EHV Cables from Gujarat Energy Transmission Company Limited   
Ahmedabad, India  
Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (“DPIL” or “the Company”), one of India’s oldest and most trusted 
manufacturers of power cables and transmission conductors under the brand DICABS , is pleased to announce 
the receipt of a significant order for supply of 66 kV Extra High Voltage (EHV) Cables  from Gujarat Energy 
Transmission Company Limited.  
This order, aggregating approximately 270 kilometers of 66 kV EHV cables , marks a major milestone  for 
the Company and reinforces DPIL’s strong positioning in the high -voltage cable segment.  
Key Highlights of the Order  
• Client:  Gujarat Energy Transmission Company Limited  
• Product:  66 kV EHV Power Cables  
• Order Size:  ~270 kms  
• Segment:  Transmission Infrastructure  
• Execution Timeline:  4 months from day of Purchase Order  
Strategic Significance  
• This is one of the largest single EHV cable orders  received by the Company and represents a 
breakthrough achievement  in the transmission segment.  
• The order is expected to significantly contribute to revenue visibility and profitability  over the 
execution period.  
• It further strengthens DPIL’s presence with state transmission utilities , a critical segment in India’s 
power infrastructure growth  
Strong Execution Track Record  
DPIL has demonstrated robust capabilities in the EHV cable segment, having:  
• Received and successfully executed over 90 kms of 66 kV and 110 kV EHV cable orders  in the last 
six months  
• Supplied to leading private sector developers and infrastructure players  
• Built a strong reputation for quality, reliability, and timely delivery

The Extra High Voltage (EHV) cable industry in India operates as a highly specialized and restricted 
segment , characterized by:  
• Limited number of qualified manufacturers  with proven design, manufacturing, and testing 
capabilities  
• Requirement of advanced CCV (Catenary Continuous Vulcanization) lines  and precision 
manufacturing  
• Mandatory compliance with stringent type testing and pre -qualification standards  
• Long approval cycles by utilities and EPC players  
As a result, the EHV cable space is often regarded as a “closed club” of technically qualified and approved 
manufacturers , where entry barriers are significantly high.  
DPIL, with its state -of-the-art manufacturing infrastructure and NABL -approved testing facilities capable 
of testing up to 700 kV , is well positioned among this select group of manufacturers.  
Order Book Position  
With this order, the Company’s total outstanding order book as on 30th March 2026 stands at over ₹3,500 
Crores , providing strong revenue visibility for the coming periods.
