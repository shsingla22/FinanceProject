Amsterdam , the Netherlands , 6 November 2026 – With reference to the voluntary totalitarian tender 
offer (the " Offer ") promoted  by TML CV Holdings Pte. Ltd.  ("TML CV HS "), through its wholly -owned  
company TML CV Holdings B.V. (the " Offeror "), pursuant to Articles 102 et seq. of the Italian 
Legislative Decree No. 58 of 24 February 1998, as further amended and supplemented (the " CFA"), 
on all the common  shares (the " Common Shares ") of Iveco Group N.V.  ("IVG" or the "Issuer "), the 
Offeror hereby announces the following . 
Capitalised terms used in this press release, unless otherwise defined, shall have the meanings 
ascribed to them in the offer document relating to the Offer, approved by CONSOB by resolution No. 
24119 of 3 September 2026 and published on 4 Sept ember 2026 (the " Offer Document "), as well 
as in the Amendment Notice  (as defined below), both available, inter alia , on the Issuer 's website 
(www.ivecogroup.com ) and on the Offeror's website ( www.tatamotors.com ). 
It is recalled that the Offer was amended, pursuant to Articles 36 and 43 of the Issuers ' Regulation, 
on 9 October 2026 by means of an increase of the Price  through the recognition of an additional 
cash component equal to EUR  0.30 for each Common Share tendered to the Offer, as announced 
to the market by means of a press release published on the same date (the "Amendment Notice "). 
1. Provisional results of the Reopening of the Terms  
Based on the provisional results communicated by  BNP Paribas,  Italian Branch,  acting as  
Intermediar y Responsible for  Coordinat ing the Collection of the Acceptances, during the Reopening 
of the Terms , which ended on the date hereof , No. 18,309,029  Common S hares have been tendered 
in acceptance of the Offer, representing approximately 6.75% of the Issuer 's common share capital 
and of the Offer  Shares  (i.e., No. 271,215,400) , for an aggregate  countervalue (calculated on the 
basis of the Price, as amended by the Amendment Notice ) equal to EUR  263,650,017.60. 
 
2 In light of the above, should the provisional results of the Reopening of the Terms  be confirmed , 
taking into account  (i) the No. 238,157,212  Common  Shares already tendered to the Offer during 
the Acceptance Period (equal to  approximately  87.81% of the Issuer 's common share capital), (ii) 
the No. 18,309,029  Common Shares tendered to the Offer during the Reopening of the Terms (equal 
to approximately  6.75% of the Issuer 's common share capital) , (iii) the No. 4,868,597  Treasury 
Shares  (equal to approximately 1.80% of the Issuer 's common share capital), and ( iv) that in the 
period between the Date of the Offer Document (i.e., 4 September 2026) and the date  hereof,  the 
Offeror has not purchased Common  Shares outside the Offer , the Offeror will hold No. 256,466,241 
Common  Shares, equal to approximately 96.29% of the Issuer 's common share capital , net of 
Treasury Shares . 
The final results of the Reopening of the Terms will be announced in a separate press release 
pursuant to Article 41, paragraph 6, of the Issuers' Regulation, which will be issued by the Offeror by 
7:29 a.m. ( CET) on the Trading Day prior to the Payment Date upon Completion of the Reopening 
of the Terms (i.e., by 7:29 a.m. ( CET) on 12 November 2026)  (the "Press Release on the Final  
Results  of the Reopening of the Terms ").  
2. Price and Payment Date  upon Completion of the Reopening of the Terms  
The Price for each Common Share tendered in acceptance of the Offer consists of the initial Price 
indicated in the Offer Document equal to EUR 14.10 ( cum dividend ), increased by the Additional 
Price  of EUR 0.30 ( cum dividend ), and therefore is equal to an aggregate amount of EUR 14.40 
(cum dividend ) per Common Share  (the " Price "). 
The payment of the Price  with respect to the Common Shares tendered to the Offer during the 
Reopening of the Terms – against the simultaneous transfer to the Offeror of the ownership of the 
Common Shares  tendered to the Offer  – will take place on 13 November 2026. 
3. Dutch Legal Squeeze -Out 
Based on the provisional results (if confirmed), in light of the Offeror having reached, together with 
its Affiliates , an aggregate shareholding at least equal to 95% of the Issuer's issued and outstanding 
share capital represented by Common Shares (calculated in accordance with the DCC, in any case 
not taking into account the Treasury Shares), and having declared in the Offer Document its intention 
not to restore a free float sufficient to ensure the regular trading of the Issuer's Common Shares, the 
Offeror hereby announces that it will commence the Dutch Legal Squeeze -Out in respect  of the 
remaining Common Shares  currently outstanding , and the Dutch Legal Squeeze -Out may be 
preceded, at the Offeror's election, by the implementation of the Demerger and the Share Sale . 
The timing, modalities and terms  of the Dutch Legal Squeeze -Out and, if implemented at the 
Offeror's election, the Demerger  and the Share Sale will be communicated to the market in due time 
by means of a separate press release in accordance with applicable laws and regulati ons. 
Borsa Italiana will order the suspension of trading in the Common Shares and/or the Delisting 
pursuant to Article 2.5.1 of the Stock Exchange Regulation, taking into account the time required to 
complete the Demerger and the Share Sale and/or the Dutch Leg al Squeeze -Out.
