Dear Sirs, 
 
Pursuant to regulation 30 of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended (“SEBI LODR Regulations”) , we wish to inform that 
Indiabulls Constructions Limited, a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company, has completed the 
acquisition of 100% stake, on a fully diluted basis, of Sky Forest Projects Private Limited, which owns 
a residential project ‘Sky Forest’, situated in Lowel Parel, Mumbai, from certain entities controlled by 
funds managed by Blackstone, Inc., for an enterprise value of approximately INR 646.71 crore.  
  
The disclosure as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR Regulations read with SEBI Circular
