complex diseases.

arrangement or slump sale or business transfer or any other appropriate structure or 
mode, as may be evaluated, subject to requisite regulatory and statutory approvals 
("Proposed Restructuring").

the Stock Exchanges and other regulatory/statutory authorities, as applicable. 
 
The Company will ensure compliance with all applicable regulatory requirements, 
including under the Companies Act, 2013 and the Listing Regulations, at each stage of the
