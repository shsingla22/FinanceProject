        Britannia’s Consolidated Sales for the quarter  grew 16.6% while Net profit grew 1 0.8% 
 
 
Mumbai , November 5th, 2026:   
 
Britannia’s Consolidated Sales for the Quarter ended 30th September  2026 stands at Rs 5,542 Crores, growing 16.6% led 
by Volume growth  of 13%, while the Net Profit stands at Rs 726 Crores, growing 10.8% over the same period last year . 
 
For the six -month period ended 30th September 2026, the Consolidated Sales stands at Rs 10,506 Crores , growing 13.1%  
led by Volume growth of  11%, while the Net Profit stands at Rs 1,319 Crores, growing 12.3% over the same period last 
year. 
 Commenting on the performance, Mr. Rakshit Hargave , Chief Executive Officer & Managing Director , said :  
 
“We sustained strong top- line momentum during the quarter, delivering a healthy double -digit volume and value  growth 
that was broad -based across channels  and key states. Our Bakery and adjacency  portfolio  – alongside the International 
business, registered strong acceleration  complementing the Biscuit  growth.  Sequentially, we witnessed strong quarter -on-
quarter momentum, clocking a 12 % top -line and 22% bottom -line growth . 
Our brand investments remained focused on driving consumer relevance through cultural and digital -first campaigns . Our 
festive -led activations  — such as  the Bourbon collaboration for Ganesh Chaturthi and the 50- 50 Cheeze Dipped campaign 
for Onam  — unlocked deep regional affinity, ably supported by our efficien t on-ground execution across “Many Indias” , 
while targeted moment marketing and platform -specific activations on brands like Little Hearts, Winkin’ Cow, and 
NutriChoice deepened connections across diverse consumer cohorts.  
We remain watchful of the inflation ary pressures  while  staying agile in our actions to drive sustainable, profitable growth  
accentuated by strong execution, innovations and disciplined cost efficiency .”
