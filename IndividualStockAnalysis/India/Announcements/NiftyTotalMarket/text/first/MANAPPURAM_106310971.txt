EXPLANATORY STATEMENT 
The following explanatory statement sets out the reasons for the withdrawal of Agenda Item No. 6 
from the Notice of 33rd Annual General Meeting of the Company dated July 11, 2025 . 
Manappuram Finance Limited ( the “ Company ”) issued a notice on July 11, 2025 (‘AGM Notice’) for 
convening the 33rd Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the Members  of the Company , scheduled to be 
held on Thursday, August 14, 2025, at 11.00 a.m. (IST) at Latha Convention Centre (formerly known as 
Anugraha Auditorium), Valapad, Thrissur, Kerala - 680 567.  The said Notice was duly dispatched to all 
members of the company  on July 21, 202 5, in due compliance with the provisions of the Companies 
Act, 2013 (the “Act”) read with the Rules  made thereunder and applicable SEB I Regulations.  
The Board of Directors at its meeting held on July 11, 2025, had approved resolution for increasing the 
ceiling limit on total holdings of FPIs through primary or secondary market route, up to an aggregate 
limit of 74% of the paid -up share capital of the Company subject to the shareholders’ approval at the 
ensuing the AGM.  
However, it is to be noted that as per the provisions of the Foreign Exchange Management (Non -debt 
Instruments) Rules, 2019 read along with  the Consolidated FDI policy issued eﬀective from October 
15, 2020, the aggregate limit of investment by foreign portfolio investors in the Company is the 
applicable sectoral cap, i.e., 100%.  
For the reason mentioned above , Item no. 06 of the  AGM notice pertaining to increasing the ceiling 
limit on total holdings of FPIs through primary or secondary market route, up to an aggregate limit of 74% of the paid -up share capital of the Company  is not required, and accordingly , stands withdrawn 
and shall not be transacted at the ensuing AGM . The Board of Directors of the Company, via circular 
resolution dated July 23, 2025, has consented  to this withdrawal, and the matter will not be transacted 
at the forthcoming AGM . Accordingly, the remaining agenda items i.e., Nos. 7 and 8 of the AGM 
Notice shall stand renumbered as Item Nos. 6 and 7, respectively. All references in the AGM Notice 
with  respect to the Agenda Item s, including the Explanatory Statement, shall be renumbered 
respectively.  
Please note that there are no other modiﬁcations to the AGM Notice and this Addendum should be read in continuation of and in conjunction with the AGM Notice , and shall be deemed to be an integral 
part thereof.
