MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY 
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Ashish Jakhanwala, MD & CEO, SAMHI Hotels Ltd., said: 
Strong Operating Environment 
We are pleased to report our performance for the quarter. Occupancy held close to 80% and RevPAR grew by about 14%, 
making this a reassuring period for the business. Revenue growth remained ahead of our long-term guidance. Domestic 
travellers accounted for about 80% of the room nights sold during the quarter. This cohort has proven more resilient 
amid ongoing geopolitical instability, reinforcing our confidence in the resilience of the Indian economy and the 
opportunity it represents. 
Building for the Future 
Against this strong backdrop, we have been steadily building for the future. For several quarters, our reported growth 
has been largely driven by same-store performance, as no new inventory came online. This is set to change. We look 
forward to opening the W Hotel in Hyderabad next fiscal year and have secured a pipeline of approximately 1,700 new 
rooms through FY2031. These initiatives put us on a path to strong growth, and our focus now is on delivering them on 
schedule. 
We are equally proud of having built this pipeline without compromising our discipline: we add assets only in core 
markets and at a discount to replacement cost. We are not buying growth through expensive acquisitions; instead, we 
are building it through the efforts that conversions and turnarounds demand, as demonstrated in Bangalore, and by 
securing long-term leases, as in Noida and Hyderabad. 
Our modest investment in RARE, together with ongoing discussions on a partnership with Marriott, will help RARE build 
a strong platform in experience-led leisure across the region, with limited capital exposure. 
Outlook 
We expect H2 FY27 to be operationally stronger. Going forward, incremental EBITDA is expected to translate into free 
cash flow, with interest outflows remaining stable. This should enable us to increasingly fund future growth investments 
through internal cash generation, supporting a disproportionate improvement in free cash flow generation. 
Our Focus on Doing What Is Right 
We released our first Sustainability Report for FY2025-26, covering 31 hotels and 4,899 rooms across 11 states. The 
report highlights improvements in energy efficiency, waste reduction and renewable energy usage. Building on our FY30 
commitments, we will establish quantified targets across key sustainability parameters, with clear baselines to track and 
benchmark our progress.

ABOUT SAMHI HOTELS LTD. 
SAMHI is a prominent branded hotel ownership and asset management platform in India with an institutional 
ownership model, experienced leadership and a professional management team. SAMHI has long-term management 
arrangements with three established and well-recognized global hotel operators — Marriott, IHG and Hyatt. The 
company has a portfolio of 31 operating hotels comprising 4,899 keys, with a diverse geographic presence across 13 
cities in India, including the National Capital Region (NCR), Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Pune.
