 Financial Performance Q2 FY 27 / H1 FY 27: 
 
1. Net Interest Income 
 
The Bank earned Net Interest Income of Rs.873 cr for Q2 FY27 and Rs.1,693 Cr for H1 
FY 27 as against Rs.667 cr and Rs.1,292 Cr respectively in Q2 FY 26 and H1 FY 26. The 
NII had grown by 31% for both Q2 FY 27 and H1 FY 27.  
 
2. Non Interest Income 
 
Non Interest Income of the Bank for Q2 FY 27 was at Rs.350 Cr as against Rs.259 Cr for 
Q2 FY 26 with 35% growth. For H1 FY 27, it had grown by 18% and stood at Rs. 594 Cr 
as against Rs. 503 Cr for the same period last financial year. 
 
3. Operating Expenses 
 
The operating expense is Rs.562 Cr for Q2 FY27 as compared to Rs. 455 Cr in Q2 FY 26 
and for H1 FY 27 the same was at Rs. 1,045 Cr as against Rs. 873 Cr for the corresponding 
period last year. 
 
4. Operating Profit 
 
The Gross Profit for Q2 FY 27 stood at Rs.661 Cr as against Rs.471 Cr in Q2 FY 26 with 
a growth of 40%. For the half year ended FY 27 the operating profit was at Rs.1,241 Cr 
compared to Rs.922 Cr in the corresponding period last year.  
 
5. Provisions 
 
The Bank made a provision for bad debts for Rs.120 cr as of 30.09.2026 as against           
Rs.110 cr in the corresponding period last year. The Bank has provided Rs. 27 Cr towards 
Standard Assets and a contingent provision of Rs. 50 Cr has been made during this 
quarter. 
 
6. Net Profit 
 
The PAT had increased by 27% and improved to Rs.416 Cr in the current quarter from 
Rs.329 Cr in Q2 FY 26. For the half year ended PAT had registered a growth of 26% and 
stood at Rs.798 Cr as compared to Rs.635 Cr for H1 FY 26.

 7. Deposits 
 
Total Deposits of the Bank had increased by 21% and increased to Rs.83,893 Cr in Q2 
FY 27 from Rs. 69,486 Cr in corresponding period last year. CASA portion stood at 27% 
to total deposits. Cost of Deposits reduced to 5.56% in Q2 FY 27 from 5.71 % in Q2 FY 
26. Though we had anticipated higher cost, easing of liquidity by regulatory measures 
such as FCNR(B) has helped to reduce cost of deposits in this year. 
 
 
8. Advances 
 
Total Advances increased by 26% for Q2 FY 27 to Rs.72,442 Cr from Rs. 57,561 Cr in Q2 
FY 26. Average Credit Deposit ratio stood at 86%. The yield on Advances for H1 FY 27 
marginally improved to 9.82% compared to 9.73% for H1 FY 26.  
 
 
9. Asset Quality 
 
The Gross NPA for Q2 FY 27 was reduced to 1.44% from 2.42% in the corresponding 
quarter last financial year which is 98 bps reduction. Net NPA was decreased by 42 bps 
to 0.48% from 0.90% for the same period. The provision coverage ratio (PCR) as of 
30.09.2026 is at 82% (including T/W) and 67% (excluding T/W).  
 
10. Net Interest Margin 
 
Net Interest Margin is at 3.87% for Q2 FY 27 as against 3.63% in Q2 FY 26. For H1 FY27 
it stood at 3.83% as against 3.59% in the same period last year. Marginal reduction in 
cost of deposits has helped in maintaining the improved NIM. 
 
11. Return on Assets 
 
Return on assets for Q2 FY 27 is 1.62% as against 1.59% in same quarter last year. ROA 
is over and above our long term level of 1.50 since Q1 FY 25.  
 
12. Return on Equity 
 
Return on Equity was at 14.78 % in H1 FY 27 as compared to 13.15% in H1 FY 26

 13. Cost to Income Ratio (CIR) 
 
Our cost to income ratio for Q2 FY 27 was at 45.96% as compared to 49.16% in Q2 FY 
26 and we expect CIR in the range of 48% to 50% for the current financial year. 
 
14. Capital Adequacy 
 
The Bank’s capital adequacy as on 30.09.2026 as per Reserve Bank of India (RBI) 
guidelines on Basel III norms is 21.26% and Tier-1 capital adequacy was 20.43%, well 
above regulatory requirements.       
 
About the Bank: 
 
City Union Bank is an old sector private sector bank with 1002 branches across 19 states 
and 4 Union territories. It is a scheduled commercial Bank regulated by the Reserve Bank 
of India. It is professionally managed and governed. City Union Bank has contemporary 
technology and infrastructure including ATMs, Net Banking, Mobile Banking, E-Wallet, 
Social Media Banking for personal as well as business banking customers. The Bank 
positioned itself as a banker to SMEs, Agri and Traders apart from all other banking 
activities.
