For nine months ended December 2024, Standalone revenue from continuing operations came 
at< 7,756.7 crore up by 4.2% as against< 7,441.1 crore in corresponding period last year. The

crore up by 22.8% as against< 699.6 Crore in corresponding period last year. 
Net Profit after tax including discontinued operations came at< 953.4 crore in nine months ended 
December 2024, up by 18.2% as against< 806.6 Crore in corresponding period last year. 
On Consolidated basis, revenue from continuing operations for the nine months ended 
December 2024, at< 7,799.0 crore up by 3.9% as against< 7,502.9 crore in corresponding period 
last year. Consolidated Net Profit After tax from continuing operations came at< 852.4 Crore up 
by 21.8% as against< 699.6 Crore in corresponding period last year. 
Consolidated Net Profit after tax including discontinued operations came at< 946.5 crore in nine 
months ended December 2024, up by 17.3% as against< 806.6 Crore in corresponding period 
last year. Earnings per share (EPS) reported for nine months ended December 2024 at< 86.09 
as against< 73.51 in corresponding period last year.
