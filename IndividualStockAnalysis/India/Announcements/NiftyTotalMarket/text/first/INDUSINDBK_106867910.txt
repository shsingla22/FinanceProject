This is in continuation of the Bank’s disclosures dated April 29, 2025 and May 21, 2025, and the 
disclosures made in the Bank’s Annual Reports for FY 2024- 25 and FY 2025- 26 in relation to Mr. 
Sumant Kathpalia, former Managing Director & CEO and Key Managerial Personnel of the Bank. Mr. Sumant Kathpalia had, vide his letter dated April 29, 2025, submitted his resignation from the 
services of the Bank with effect from the close of working hours on April 29, 2025. At its meeting 
held on April 29, 2025, the Board took note of the aforesaid resignation and made the requisite 
disclosures in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements. While taking note of his 
resignation as Managing Director & CEO and as a Director from the Board of the Bank, the Bank had 
communicated to him that he would continue to remain in employment with the Bank until further 
intimation. Subsequently, in accordance with the Bank’s internal code of conduct, disciplinary 
proceedings were initiated against Mr. Sumant Kathpalia and, with effect from June 18, 2025, he was 
placed under suspension pending completion of fu rther proceedings.  
 
Further to the above, we hereby inform that the Enquiry Officer  (Former Judge -Bombay High Court) 
appointed in the matter submitted his report dated November 18, 2026. Based on the findings recorded in the Enquiry Report, the Board of Directors of the Bank (Disciplinary Authority), at its 
meeting held today, November 20, 2026, decided  as under : 
 
i. Termination of Mr. Sumant Kathpalia’s employment from services of the Bank, with 
immediate effect; and  
 
ii. Invocation of the applicable malus and claw -back provisions with effect from the date of his 
initial appointment as Managing Director & CEO of the Bank i.e. March 24, 2020. 
 
The Board Meeting commenced at  5:31 p.m. (IST) and concluded at 7: 50 p.m. (IST).  
 
This intimation is also being uploaded on the website of the Bank at www.indusind.bank.in.
