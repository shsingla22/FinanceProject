Sir/ महोदय , 
 
  In continuation to our earlier letter dated 11.06.2024  and i n compliance to Regulation 30 of SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, it is to inform that the issue regarding 
classification of Investment  in NHPTL, after transfer of partial stake to PGCIL was reviewed as per the 
requirement of Ind  AS and found to meet t he definition of an Associate as per Ind AS -28.  
 
Hence, for the purpos e of preparation of Financial Statements / Financial results , the investment in 
NHPTL will be classified as “Investment in Associates ” and accounted for using the Equity method in 
consolidated financial  statements  of the Company . 
 
  This is for your information and record.
