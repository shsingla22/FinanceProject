Annexure-A 
 
Sunjay Kapur 
Chairman Emeritus 
 
Visionary Leader | Industry Statesman | Champion of Innovation | Compassionate 
Philanthropist 
 Sunjay Kapur (1971–2025) served as the Chairman  of Sona Comstar, one of the global leaders 
in mobility technology, and was a transformative  figure in India’s automotive landscape. Mr. 
Kapur’s leadership was marked by strategic foresight, global expansion, and an unwavering 
commitment to innovation and sustainability. 
 He joined Sona BLW Precision Forgings Limited as Director in 2006. In 2015, Mr. Kapur assumed 
the role of Managing Director following the passing of his father and the Company's founder, 
Dr. Surinder Kapur. At a time when the Company was navigating significant financial 
challenges, he led its turnaround by stabiliz ing finances, expanding export business, and 
transforming its product portfolio from conventional differential gears to integrated electric vehicle driveline solutions. His strategic partnersh ip with Blackstone enabled the acquisition of 
Comstar’s motor capabilities, laying the foundation for electrification and giving rise to the 
Company’s new identity – Sona Comstar. Mr. Kapur was instrumental in pivoting the company 
toward electric vehicle components, positioning Sona Comstar as a forward-looking player in the global auto components industry.  
 As Chairman of Sona Comstar from 2019  onward, Mr. Kapur continued to guide the 
company’s strategic direction. Under his chairmanship, Sona Comstar expanded its global footprint across India, the USA, Mexico, China, and Serbia, successfully listed on BSE Ltd. and 
the National Stock Exchange of India Ltd., and entered future mobility domains such as sensors and software through the acquisition of a majority stake in Novelic. He also supported the 
Company’s diversification into sustainable mobility beyond automotive with the acquisition of 
the railway business from Escorts Kubota Limited. His emphasis on purposeful growth, sustainability, technological excellence, an d exemplary governance helped the Company 
navigate its journey towards its vision of becoming one of the world’s most respected and 
valuable mobility technology companies for its customers, employees, and shareholders. 
 Mr. Kapur believed deeply in the power of business to uplift communities. Under his chairmanship, Sona Comstar actively contributed to society through a wide range of CSR programs focused on environmental stewardshi p, inclusive education, healthcare access, 
gender equality, and skill development. 
   
 
 
 
Beyond corporate leadership, Mr. Kapur was a respected voice in shaping India’s 
manufacturing and automotive policies. He held numerous influential positions, including President of Automotive Component Manufactur ers Association of India (ACMA), Chairman 
of CII Northern Region, Co-Chair of CII National  Council of Manufacturing, Chair of CII Europe 
Committee and Member of CII Trade Policy Coun cil, CII-VLFM Governing Board, and several 
other strategic bodies.  His contributions extended to education and en trepreneurship. He served on the Board of 
Governors of The Doon School and the Advisory Council of the Munjal Institute for Global 
Manufacturing. As Global Chairman of Entrepre neurs’ Organization (2007–08), he was the first 
Indian to hold this prestigious position.   
A sports enthusiast and accomplished polo player, Mr. Kapur founded and captained the 
Aureus Polo team, which won championships acro ss India, the USA, and the UK. His love for 
sports and mentorship reflected his belief in holistic leadership and personal excellence.  Mr. Kapur’s legacy is one of bold vision, deep relationships, and enduring impact. As Chairman 
Emeritus, his spirit will continue to inspire the Sona Comstar family and the broader industry he 
helped shape.
