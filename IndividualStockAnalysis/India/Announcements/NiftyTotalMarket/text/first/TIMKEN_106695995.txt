Timken India Receives Four BIS licenses for t heir Bearings  
 
Timken India  received BIS licenses for two product categories across two 
manufacturing facilities.  
 
Bangalore, Karnataka, July 13, 2026 —Timken India Limited (the “Company”) 
today announces that it has been awarded four Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) 
licenses for its bearings.  
 This achievement confirms Timken India’s leadership in manufacturing excellence, 
quality assurance and regulatory compliance across multiple bearing categories and production sites.  
 Key highlights:  
 
• The Company has received four BIS licenses for bearing products.  
• The Company  has received BIS License for Cylindrical Roller Bearing 
(CRB) Roller production at its Bharuch (new) plant.  
• The Company  has received BIS License for CRB  production at its Bharuch 
(new) plant.  
• The Company  has received BIS License for TRB ( Tapered Roller Bearing) 
Roller production at its Bharuch (new) and Jamshedpur plant.  
 “Our teams have worked tirelessly to achieve this landmark recognition,” said 
Sanjay Koul, Chairman & Managing Director, Timken India Limited. “Securing four 
BIS licenses and being the first in India to obtain CRB and TRB roller licenses across multiple plants underscores our commitment to the highest standards of quality, engineering and customer service.  These certifications enable our customers and 
partners to source bearings that meet stringent national standards, simplify 
procurement for regulated proj ects, and deliver enhanced operational reliability.”  
 
 
Customer and market impact  
 
• Enhanced confidence: BIS -certified bearings reduce technical qualification barriers 
for OEMs, infrastructure projects and regulated sectors.  
• Stronger supply assurance: Certified manufacturing across plants shortens lead 
times and improves regional availability.  
• Tender readiness: Distributors and customers will benefit from ready 
documentation and qualification support for tenders requiring BIS -certified 
components.  
• Continued investment: The Company will continue to invest in testing, process 
controls and capacity to meet growing certified demand.  
 
About the BIS licenses - The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) licenses were issued 
after rigorous evaluation of product design, manufacturing processes, and quality -control 
systems. Each license confirms conformity to the relevant Indian national standards  for 
bearing performance and safety.
