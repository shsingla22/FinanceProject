Targeting the surge in demand for spiritual tourism 
Thomas Cook and SOTC pa rtner with Uttarakhand Tourism Development Board 
Launch unique aerial yatras to Dev Bhoomi (Adi Kailash and Om Parvath) 
 
 5 days tour covering the second abode of Lord Shiva and Parvati  
 Two helicopter darshans to Adi Kailash and Om Parvath 
 ATV (All-Terrain Vehicle) for convenient journey to Parvati Sarovar (mandir) 
 All meals included – vegetarian 
 Exclusive discount in partnership with Uttarakhand Tourism Development Board 
 
Mumbai, April 10, 2024:  Thomas Cook (India) Limited - India’s leading omnichannel travel services company 
and its Group Company, SOTC Travel , have partnered with the Uttarakhand Tourism Development Board to 
launch special aerial darshans to Adi Kailash & Om Parvat (Dev Bhoomi). This unique 5 day spiritual 
helicopter tour has been launched at an attractive price of Rs. 90,000. 00 – subsidized by Uttarakhand 
Tourism Development Board (UTDB) .  
 
Thomas Cook and SOTC’s data indicates a significant demand of 100% YoY for Spiritual Tourism. While, 
pilgrimage tours to Adi Kailash/Vyas Valley have always been of strong appeal to Indians, the limited 
seasonality and tough terrain have posed numerous challenges. Hence, the Companies’ unique aerial 
darshans via helicopter will usher in increased accessibility and open up the spiritual sector in the country.  
 
This spiritual soujorn promises travellers a once-in-a-lifetime experience to offer darshans at the sacred Dev 
Bhoomi and from an unparalleled aerial perspective. The journey covers transportation via helicopter and 
ATV (All-Terrain Vehicle) to ensure convenience and comfort of pilgrims.  
 
Mr. Rajeev Kale, President & Country Head – Holiday s, MICE, Visa, at Thomas Cook (India) Limited  
said, “We are honoured to partner with Uttarakhand Tourism Development Board to introduce exceptional 
aerial tours to Adi Kailash & Om Parvath, offering our customers an enriching spiritual experience. Our aim is 
to redefine spiritual tourism in India, by unveiling hidden gems /sacred sites while also ensuring accessibility." 
 
Daniel D'Souza, President & Country Head - Holidays, SOTC Travel, said, "Over our history of 75 years, 
we are delighted to witness a resurgence in spiritual tourism - not just from India seniors, but also from 
families, Young India’s millennial and Gen Z. Our partnership with the Uttarakhand Tourism Development 
Board to launch aerial tours to Dev Bhoomi (Vyas Valley) offers both - helicopter and temple darshans, also 
ATV transportation across mountainous terrain. The pricing has been subsidized in partnership with UTDB to 
ensure affordability for our Indian consumers."
