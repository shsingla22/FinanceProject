Press Release  
Signature Global Emerges as Successful Bidder for 475 Acres of Land in MMR Through 
a Consortium Led by Its Subsidiary 
● Land parcels are located in MMR in Raigad district, close to the Navi Mumbai Airport area 
● Development marks a strategic milestone reinforcing Signature Global’s presence in the 
Mumbai Metropolitan Region 
New Delhi, 17th November 2026:  Signature Global, one of India’s leading real estate 
developers, today announced that, a consortium led by its wholly owned subsidiary Maa 
Vaishno Net-Tech Limited, has emerged as the successful bidder for 474.66 acres of land 
in Raigad, Maharashtra, a part of MMR, with a bid valued at INR 194.19 Crores.  
As per the arrangement between consortium partners, 35% of the total equity in Maa 
Vaishno Net-Tech Limited will be diluted post necessary approvals, with other consortium 
members acquiring the said stake. Signatureglobal (India) Limited will continue to retain 
a 65% majority shareholding.  
The land parcels are strategically located across Raigad district including in talukas of 
Karjat, Khalapur, Sudhagad, and adjoining areas, benefiting from the region's evolving 
infrastructure landscape. The broader catchment is supported by major connectivity 
developments such as the Navi Mumbai International Airport, Atal Setu (Mumbai Trans 
Harbour Link), Mumbai–Pune Expressway and the upcoming Panvel–Karjat suburban 
railway corridor.  
The bid was won through an online e-auction under the asset monetisation process of 
PACL limited, overseen by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee; a body constituted 
by SEBI pursuant to the orders of Supreme Court of India.  
The successful bid gives the company access to a strategically located land parcels in one 
of Maharashtra’s emerging growth corridors. The Company is currently evaluating the 
right product opportunity for the acquired land parcels. 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Pradeep Kumar Aggarwal, Founder and 
Chairman, Signature Global (India) Limited, said,  “We are pleased to have emerged as 
the successful bidder for Raigad in MMR area. Over the years, we have built a strong and 
trusted name in Gurugram and the wider NCR, and this bid reflects our confidence in our 
business model, our execution capabilities and the people behind them. Securing this land 
through an auction is a meaningful step, and we have approached it with the same 
discipline that has always guided us: acquiring well and planning thoughtfully. 
“Growth for us has never been only about adding land; it is about building a pipeline that 
creates lasting value for our homebuyers, our investors and the communities we serve.

About Signature Global (India) Limited:  
Signature Global, India’s leading real estate development company, is reshaping the housing 
market in northern India. While it initially made its mark in the affordable housing segment, the 
Company now focuses on the mid and premium housing categories. This strategic evolution is 
driven by a strong emphasis on quality execution, value creation, reliability, and adherence to 
global standards.  
Backed by marquee institutional investors, Signature Global upholds high standards of corporate 
governance. The Company follows a disciplined and efficient land acquisition strategy, typically 
launching projects within 18 months of acquisition, enabling faster go-to-market timelines and 
enhanced project viability. Within the INR 20 million to INR 50 million price range, Signature Global 
holds a 13% market share in the National Capital Region (NCR) and a 20% share in Gurugram, 
reflecting its strong position in its target micro-markets. 
As of Q2FY27, the Company has delivered 19.9 million sq. ft. of real estate. Its project pipeline 
remains robust, comprising 23.2 million sq. ft. of recently launched projects, 27.3 million sq. ft. of 
forthcoming developments, and 9.2 million sq. ft. of ongoing construction (5.1 million sq. ft. under 
construction and 4.1 million sq. ft. with OC received), all slated for execution over the next 2-3 
years.
