 About Astral :

manufactures pro- India plumbing and drainage systems in CPVC, PVC and DWC

piping solutions, Astral Pipes t oday offers  the widest range of pipes and fittings for 
various applications including specialized products  for infrastructure development. 
Astral entered the Adhesives category in 2014 by acquiring UK  based Seal It Services
