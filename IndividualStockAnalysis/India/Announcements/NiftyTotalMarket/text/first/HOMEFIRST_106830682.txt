Home  First Finance  Company  India Limited“HomeFirst delivered astrong operating performance inQ2FY27,sustaining healthy business momentum alongside
profitability and stable asset quality .The quarter was marked byhighest ever disbursements, continued expansion of
our distribution franchise and disciplined execution across our markets .Asour business scales, our focus remains on
translating portfolio growth into sustainable earnings while maintaining underwriting standards and operating
efficiency .
AUM increased by27.0%year -on-year to₹18,010 crore, while quarterly disbursements reached ahigh of₹1,705 crore .
Growth was supported byhealthy customer demand across our target segments, deeper penetration inexisting
markets and continued investments inourdistribution network .Importantly, business momentum was accompanied by
strong earnings growth, reflecting ourfocus onscaling thefranchise while maintaining profitability and portfolio quality .
Profit After Taxincreased by29.1%year -on-year and 6.5%sequentially to₹170 crore, outpacing AUM growth of27.0%.
Net Total Income grew by23.9%year -on-year and 3.3%quarter -on-quarter to₹343 crore .Return onAssets remained
healthy at4.2%,while Return onEquity stood at14.9%.Our focus remains onsustaining this relationship between
portfolio and earnings growth through disciplined pricing, cost management and efficient capital deployment, while
continuing toinvest inthefranchise .Ourcost-to-income ratio stood at30.8%,compared with 32.7%inthepreceding
quarter, reflecting ourcontinued focus onoperating productivity asthebusiness scales .
Spreads, excluding co-lending, moderated by10basis points during thequarter .Wecontinue toactively manage asset
pricing, funding costs and liability mix, supported byourgranular retail portfolio and diversified funding relationships .
During thequarter, weadded sixbranches, taking ournetwork to181 branches and 379 touchpoints .Wealso added 26
employees onanetbasis, primarily incustomer -facing roles, bringing ourtotal employee base to2,014.
Asset quality remained stable, reflecting our continued emphasis onprudent underwriting and proactive collections .
Early -stage delinquency indicators remained contained, with 1+DPD at4.7%and 30+DPD at3.2%,while Gross Stage 3
reduced by10basis points sequentially to1.7%.Credit cost remained contained at40basis points during thequarter .
Asthe portfolio grows, our emphasis remains onprudent risk selection, early identification ofpotential stress and
consistent collection discipline topreserve theunderlying quality oftheloan book .
Our Green Homes initiative also progressed during the quarter, with anadditional 100 homes certified, taking the
cumulative total to650 asofSeptember 2026 .Through this initiative, wecontinue topromote environmentally
responsible housing practices and progressively integrate sustainability considerations into ourcore business activities .
This commitment was recognised, with HomeFirst winning the'ESG &Financial Inclusion' award intheNBFC category at
ASSOCHAM's 21stAnnual Summit &Awards onBanking &Financial Sector Lending Companies, reaffirming our focus
onfinancial inclusion, responsible business practices and sustainable, long -term value creation .
The long -term opportunity inaffordable housing finance remains substantial, supported byunmet housing demand,
rising homeownership aspirations, urbanisation and increasing formalisation ofincomes .Our presence across key
markets, expanding distribution network and technology -enabled operating platform provide afoundation todeepen
customer penetration and progressively enhance operating productivity .
Our priorities forthe remainder ofFY27are clear :sustain growth momentum, preserve portfolio quality, protect
profitability and improve operating efficiency .Asourfranchise continues toscale, ouremphasis remains onconverting
business growth into consistent earnings, healthy returns and long -term shareholder value .”Commenting on the performance

About Home First Finance Company India Limited
Home  First Finance  Company  India LimitedHome  First  Finance  Company  India  Limited  (HomeFirst)  is a technology  driven  affordable  housing   finance  company  that 
targets  first time  home  buyers  in low and middle -income  groups . It primarily  offers   housing  loans  for the purchase  or 
construction  of homes . The Company  has deep  penetration  in the large  housing  finance  markets  with  a network  of 181 
branches  with  presence  in 13 States  / UT in  India,  with  a significant  presence  in emerging  urban  regions  in the states of 
Gujarat,  Maharashtra,  Andhra  Pradesh,  Telangana,  Karnataka  and Tamil  Nadu  and increasing  presence  in emerging  
affordable  housing  finance  markets  of Uttar  Pradesh,  Uttarakhand, Madhya  Pradesh ,Rajasthan ,Chhattisgarh, Haryana and
NCR . The company  has diversified  lead  generating  channels  with  a wide  network  of connectors .
