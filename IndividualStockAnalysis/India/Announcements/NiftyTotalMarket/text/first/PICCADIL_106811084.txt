PRESS RELEASE  
 
THE WORLD’S BEST WHISKY IS BACK: INDRI UNVEILS ITS HIGHLY ANTICIPATED 
DIWALI COLLECTOR’S EDITION 2026 
 
A Rare Ruby Port Cask Expression Created To Redefine The Next Chapter Of Indri’s 
Globally Coveted Collector’s Edition Series 
 
 
8th October’26, New Delhi  - Following its international debut in London, Indri, India’s 
largest-selling luxury single malt whisky by Piccadily Distilleries, has unveiled the much-
anticipated Diwali Collector’s Edition 2026 in India — a rare Ruby Port Cask expression 
crafted by Master Blender Surrinder Kumar. 
 
Indri Diwali Collector’s Edition 2026, one of the most anticipated whisky releases globally, 
made its international debut in London, marking the next chapter in Indri’s celebrated 
annual Collector’s Edition series. The expression was subsequently showcased at The 
Whisky Show , London where it received strong admiration and recognition from reputed 
whisky journalists, critics, connoisseurs, collectors and industry experts, reinforcing Indri’s 
position among the world’s most talked-about and celebrated single malts. 
 
A celebration of India’s rich cultural heritage and the artistry of contemporary Indian whisky-
making, the Diwali Collector’s Edition 2026 brings together the spirit of Diwali with the 
depth, character and vinous elegance of a Ruby Port Cask-matured single malt. The fourth 
release in Indri’s annual festive series, this limited-edition expression is bottled at 50% ABV 
and restricted to just 600 individually numbered bottles for the Indian market. Crafted from

The result is an indulgent profile of black cherries, wild berries and ripe plums on the nose,

The expression is as much a celebration of India’s artistic heritage as it is of whisky. The 
packaging is designed by celebrated designer Krsnaa Mehta, Founder of India Circus. 
Inspired by Indian miniature art and royal heritage, the design combines jewel tones, 
intricate florals and ornate detailing around a Marwari horse and its foal, symbolising 
nobility, lineage and continuity. 
 
“The Diwali Collector’s Edition has played a defining role in Indri’s journey and in the story of 
Indian single malts globally. When the 2023 edition was named the ‘Best Whisky in the 
World’, it was a watershed moment — not just for Indri, but for the belief that Indian single 
malts could stand confidently alongside the world’s finest. The Diwali Collector’s Edition 
has become an important expression of what Indri stands for — a distinctly Indian whisky 
with the ambition and craftsmanship to command attention on the world stage. With the 
2026 Ruby Port Cask, we wanted to push that story further, bringing together the richness of 
Indian single malt, the elegance of exceptional Ruby Port casks and the artistry of India’s 
cultural heritage. Limited to just 600 individually numbered bottles, this is a release created 
not only to celebrate Diwali, but to celebrate how far Indian whisky has come. The 
recognition it has already received globally is a testament to the craftsmanship behind Indri 
and our belief that the finest expressions of whisky can proudly come from India.”  said 
Surrinder Kumar, Master Blender, Piccadily Distilleries.  
 
The Indri Diwali Collector’s Edition has become one of the most anticipated expressions in 
Indian whisky. The 2023 edition marked a defining moment , when Indri’s Diwali Collector’s 
Edition was recognised as the “Best Whisky in the World” at the Whiskies of the World 
Awards 2023 , helping place Indian single malt whisky firmly on the global whisky map. 
 
The Indri Diwali Collector’s Edition 2026  carries forward the legacy of its acclaimed 
predecessors, making a strong mark on the global spirits stage even before its official India 
launch. The expression has secured Double Gold with 96 points at the USA Spirits Ratings 
2026, where it was also named Indian Single Malt Whisky of the Year  and Spirit of the Year 
– India. It has further earned Gold at the World Whisky Masters 2026 and the New York 
World Spirits Competition 2026 , alongside Gold at the Asia World Spirits Competition 
2026 in Singapore . Collectively, the series has secured 29 international awards , 
reinforcing the growing recognition of Indian single malt on the global stage.

With just 600 individually numbered bottles, the Ruby Port Cask expression brings together

The Indri Diwali Collector’s Edition 2026 Ruby Port Cask will be available in India from mid-

About Indri Single Malt Whisky: 
Launched in 2022, Indri Single Malt Whisky, crafted by Piccadily, has quickly become the fastest-
growing single malt in the world, earning over 40 prestigious accolades, including 'Best Indian 
Single Malt' and 'Best Whisky in The World.' Produced in Indri, a small village in Haryana, using 
premium 6-row Indian barley from Rajasthan, Indri single malt whisky showcases Indian 
excellence and innovation. 
 
In a market once dominated by imported labels, Indri stands tall as a beacon of Indian excellence. 
It's not just a brand, it has become a symbol of national pride, elevating the status of Indian spirits 
to unparalleled heights. Indri isn't just leading the charge; it's leading a revolution. 
 
As Indri continues to redefine the standards of Indian single malts, Piccadily remains dedicated to 
pushing boundaries, exploring new horizons, and delivering exceptional experiences to whisky 
enthusiasts worldwide.
