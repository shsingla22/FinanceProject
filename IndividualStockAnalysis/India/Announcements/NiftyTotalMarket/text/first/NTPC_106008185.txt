Press Release  
NGEL Signs MoU with Govt of Rajasthan for development of 25 GW of RE Projects 
New Delhi, Sept 30th 2024:   On the occasion of Rising Rajasthan Investor Meet at New Delhi, NTPC Green 
Energy Limited (NGEL), a wholly-owned subsidiary of NTPC Ltd, signed a Memorandum of Understanding 
(MoU) with Govt of Rajasthan on 30th September 2024 for development of 25 GW of Renewable Energy 
Projects in the State of Rajasthan.  
The MoU was exchanged between Shri K S Sundaram, Director (Projects), NGEL and Shri Alok, (IAS) ACS 
(Energy)  in the  presence  of Hon’ble Chief  Minister of Rajasthan.  Senior  officials  from  Government  of 
Rajasthan and NGEL were also present on the occasion.
