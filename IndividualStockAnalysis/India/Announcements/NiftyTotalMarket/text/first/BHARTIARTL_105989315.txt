Airtel expands its Home Wi-Fi service across all 14 districts in Kerala  
 
 Offers a whole host of leading Malayalam OTTs and TV channels including Asianet, 
Manorama News, Surya TV, SunNXT, Manorama Max, etc. 
 Home Wi-Fi service now reaches additional 5.7 million new households in Kerala 
 
Kochi/Trivandrum/Calicut, September 16, 2024 : Bharti Airtel (“Airtel”), one of India’s leading 
telecommunications services providers, today, announced that it has expanded its Home Wi-Fi 
service across all the 14 districts of Kerala. This expansion will enable to cover an additional 5.7 
million new households in the state.  
  
With Airtel Wi-Fi, a customer not only gets high-speed reliable wireless internet service but a 
whole host of other benefits including access to unlimited streaming, 22+ OTTs services and over 
350+ TV channels. Customers can book Airtel Wi-Fi by placing an order using the Airtel Thanks 
App or by calling 8130181301. 
 
Amit Gupta – COO, Bharti Airtel, Kerala , said, “I am happy to announce that Airtel Wi-Fi has now 
reached every nook and corner of Kerala. With Airtel Wi-Fi, customers can unlock a wide range 
of entertainment options including access to 22+ OTTs, 350+ television channels and a reliable 
high-speed wireless Wi-Fi service at an affordable tariff starting at Rs. 599 a month. We hope 
customers make the most of this and enjoy endless entertainment.”
