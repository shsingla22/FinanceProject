Press Release
Revenue up by 21% and PAT up by 18%
(4thQuarter FY 2026 Consolidated Results)
Mumbai, 5thMay, 2026: Ajanta Pharma Ltd., a specialty pharmaceutical formulation
company today reported its excellent performance for 4thquarter and year ended 31stMarch 
2026. 
Q4 FY 2026 performance highlights (compared to Q4 FY 2025):
xRevenue from operations at Rs. 1,422 cr. against Rs. 1,170 cr.; up 21% . 
xEBITDA at Rs. 333 cr. against Rs. 297 cr.; EBITDA at 23%; up 12% . 
xThe mark-to-market forex loss stood at Rs. 42 cr. Excluding this impact, EBITDA stood at 
Rs. 375 cr. , reflecting a 26% growth, with an EBITDA margin of 26% . 
xProfit after tax at Rs. 267 cr. against Rs. 225 cr.; PAT at 19%; up 18% . 
FY 2026 performance highlights (compared to FY 2025):
xRevenue from operations at Rs. 5,453 cr. against Rs. 4,648 cr.; up 17% . 
xEBITDA at Rs. 1,395 cr. against Rs. 1,260 cr.; EBITDA at 26%; up 11% . 
xThe mark-to-market forex loss stood at Rs. 103 cr. Excluding this impact, EBITDA stood 
at Rs. 1,498 cr. , reflecting a 18% growth, with an EBITDA margin of 27% . 
xProfit after tax at Rs. 1,056 cr. against Rs. 920 cr.; PAT at 19%; up 15% . 
xROCE stood at a healthy level of 33% and RONW at 25%.

About Ajanta Pharma Limited
Ajanta Pharma is a speciality pharmaceutical formulation company having major focus on
branded generic business across India, Asia & Africa. Company has ground presence in
each of these 30+ countries. Many of company’s products are 1stto market and are leading
in their sub-therapeutic segments. Company also has presence in USA in generic businessand institution business in Africa.
Company’s state of the art R&D centre is in Mumbai. Company has 7 world class
manufacturing facilities located in India.
For the last 3 financial years, company has posted healthy Revenue and PAT CAGR of 
14%.
