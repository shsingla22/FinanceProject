Press Information Note  
 
TTBS Celebrates MSME Day with “Zidd  Hai to Jeet Ha i” Campaign  
A Tribute to the Indomitable Spirit of Indian MSMEs  
New Delhi, June 27, 2025:  As India celebrates  MSME Day 2025, Tata Tele Business Services 
(TTBS)  launche d its powerful new campaign  - ”Zidd  Hai To Jeet Hai” – a compelling tribute to 
the determined  spirit that drives India' s MSMEs to overcome every obstacle and emerge 
victorious.  The campaign celebrates the relentless grit that has made MSMEs the true 
architects of India's economic transformation, contributing significantly to the nation's GDP 
and generating employment for innumerable people.  
Brand Film : https://youtu.be/eJZun2bcltI?si=NdsafRDoKw2a2Ubq  
 
 
Beyond Ambition: The Power of 'Zidd'  
While ambition sets the goal, it is ‘Zidd’ —the unshakeable will to persevere —that transforms 
challenges into milestones . TTBS recognizes that Indian MSMEs don’t just dream big —they act with 
courage and consistency, often navigating uncertainty with limited resources but limitless 
determination.  
“In boardrooms and on shop floors, in small towns and big cities, there’s one common thread: the 
‘Zidd ’ to succeed,” said Vishal Rally, Senior Vice -President – Product and Marketing, Tata 
Teleservices . “MSMEs are the key drivers of India’s economy, and at TTBS, we’re proud to fuel their 
growth with smart digital solutions that empower them to scale with confidence and thrive in today’s 
digital world.”  
Three Stories, One Unbreakable Spirit  
The 'Zidd Hai to Jeet Hai'  campaign showcases three compelling brand films that capture 
the essence of MSME resilience across critical sectors:  

 
• Tech Pioneers:  IT MSMEs coding India's future from modest setups, proving that innovation 
knows no boundaries when fuelled  by determination .  
• Financial Inclusivity Champions:  Banking  and Fintech MSMEs expanding access to banking in 
underserved regions, bridging critical gaps . 
• Manufacturing Mavericks:  Homegrown Manufacturing MSMEs delivering the world class 
products under the 'Made in India'  to global markets, competing with established 
international players.  
These narratives underscore a simple but powerful truth: when MSMEs have the 'Zidd' to succeed, no 
challenge is undefeatable.  The campaign reflects India’s vision of building a digitally empowered, 
resilient MSME sector capable of driving sustainable, inclusive growth.  
TTBS has been instrumental in bridging the digital gap for MSMEs  across diverse sectors  by providing 
affordable, scalable solutions purpose built for their business needs. From Smartflo UCaaS  to secured 
cloud connectivity  and cybersecurity , TTBS empowers businesses to streamline operations, cut costs, 
and grow confidently in a digital -first economy.
