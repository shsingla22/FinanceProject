Sub: Intimation of Bhoomi Pujan for upcoming Robotics and UAV Manufacturing Facility 
  
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to applicable provisions of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we are pleased to inform that Solar Defence and Aerospace Limited, the wholly 
owned subsidiary of Solar Industries India Limited conducted the Bhoomi Pujan ceremony for its 
upcoming Robotics and Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) manufacturing facility at the MIHAN Special 
Economic Zone (SEZ) in Nagpur on March 7, 2026. 
 
The Bhoomi Pujan ceremony was performed by Shri Devendra Ji Fadnavis , Hon’ble Chief Minister of 
Maharashtra, in the august presence of Shri Nitin Ji Gadkari , Hon’ble Union Minister of Road 
Transport and Highways. 
 
This facility represents a significant step in the Company’s strategic expansion into advanced defence 
technologies, autonomous systems, and robotics-enabled solutions. The proposed facility will focus 
on the development and manufacturing of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), robotics platforms, and 
next-generation defence technologies aimed at strengthening indigenous capabilities. 
 
We remain committed to supporting the nation’s vision for self -reliance in defence manufacturing 
and technological advancements.
