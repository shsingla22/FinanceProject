Sterling Unveils the Reimagined Sterling Palavelli Godavari in the Heart of Konaseema  
 
19th December 2024  – Sterling Holiday Resorts, India’s leading hospitality brand, proudly announces 
the reimagined Sterling Palavelli Godavari . Nestled across 13 acres of lush greenery on the serene 
banks of the Godavari River, this riverside retreat invites trave llers to rediscover nature, culture, and 
luxury in the heart of Konaseema.  
Surrounded by coconut plantations & a vibrant biodiversity hotspot with  23 exotic species of flora, 
the resort offers guests a sensory escape. Guests wake up to the mesmerizing sight of Amazonica 
lilies & thousand -petal lotuses blooming in the central pond, setting the stage for tranquil mornings.  
A Blend of Heritage & Comfort  
The reimagined resort celebrates regional artistry and architecture, offering an eclectic mix of 
accommodations: Zamindar -style Houses  and Mud Houses  for rustic charm, Manduva Logili rooms  
showcasing local craftsmanship, and spacious, newly upgraded rooms and suites (up to 750 sq. ft.) 
with modern comforts and elegant aesthetics, perfect for families and discerning travellers.  
“We are delighted to unveil the reimagined Sterling Palavelli Godavari, reflecting our commitment to 
offering unique and immersive travel experiences. With its rich cultural tapestry, stunning riverside 
location, and thoughtfully upgraded amenities, this p roperty exemplifies our dedication to 
preserving the essence of each region while delivering unparalleled comfort and memorable 
experiences,” said Mr. Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director & CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts.  
A Culinary Journey with ‘Konaseema’  
The resort’s all -day dining restaurant, Konaseema , features a thoughtfully curated menu that revives 
native flavo urs. Guests can indulge in local delicacies like the famed Pulasa fish and the delightful 
Pootharekulu, alongside popular Indian and international cuisines.  
Endless Experiences & Thrills  
From laidback luxury to adrenaline -pumping adventures, Sterling Palavelli Godavari is a complete 
getaway offering:  an Infinity Pool  for relaxation , Subuthi Spa  for rejuvenation , speed -boat rides  on 
the Godavari , On-site adventure activities , Mini -island celebrations  and water games . 
A Venue for Every Occasion  
Sterling Palavelli Godavari caters to all occasions with  2 acres of outdoor spaces for grand weddings  
& events with the river as a stunning backdrop , a Houseboat for exclusive CXO meetings  and high -
tea cruises , traditional courtyards offering multiple Instagram -able venues for unforgettable 
celebrations . 
Celebrate Konaseema’s Culture  
The resort brings the vibrant culture of Konaseema to life through curated activities:  Folk 
performances  such as Burra Katha , Regional festivities like ‘Sankranti Sandharbaralu’  (Sankranti 
Celebrations) in January , Temple trails , paddy cultivation tours, and jaggery -making workshops  for 
curious travel lers. From serene sunsets on the river to culturally immersive adventures, Sterling 
Palavelli Godavari promises an unforgettable getaway.  
 
For bookings and more details, visit:  www.sterlingholidays.com  
 
 
 
About Sterling Holiday Resorts Ltd.  
Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited is a leading hospitality company in India that owns & manages more 
than 3000 keys, across 5 7 resorts, hotels and retreats, at 4 8 unique destinations. The company caters

across hills, beaches,  jungles, waterfront, adventure, heritage, pilgrimage and drive -to locations.
