Committee’s comments is not applicable.

We, M/s. BRAHMAYYA & CO, Chartered Accountants, having our office at D.no: 33-25-33B, Govindarajulunaidu 
Street, Suryaraopet, Vijayawada were appointed as Statutory auditors of Amara Raja Advanced Cell 
Technologies Private Limited (Wholly Owned Subsidiary of Amara Raja Energy & Mobility Limited) pursuant 
to the resolution passed by the shareholders of Amara Raja Advanced Cell Technologies Private Limited at 
their 1't AGM held on 29th August 2023 for a term of 5 years in accordance with the provisions of Section 139(1) 
of the Companies Act, 2013. 
We have completed our Statutory audit till31't March, 2025 and issued our Audit report on May 26,2025. We 
also completed limited review of financial statements for 01 ended June 30, 2025 and issued our report on 
August 13, 2025. 
We completed our tenure as the statutory auditors of Amara Raja Energy & Mobility Limited (the Company's 
"Holding Company ") in the Annual General Meeting held on August 14, 2025. As per the discussions we had 
with the management , pursuant to the change in the Statutory auditors of the holding company, for the 
reasons ofthe company's operational efficiency and need for bringing in synergies with the holding company 
audit firm, as well as for maintaining consistency in the audit process, the company intends to align its 
statutory auditor with that of the Holding company . Accordingly, we hereby tender our resignation as the 
statutory auditors of the Amara Raja Advanced Cell Technologies Private Limited and request you to treat this 
as our letter of resignation with immediate effect. 
We confirm that there are no circumstances connected with our resignation which we consider should be 
brought to the attention of the members or creditors of the Company, other than the reason stated above.
