AXISCADES  and  Aldoria  Forge  Strategic  Partnership   
to Strengthen  India’s  Space  Capabilities  
 
Paris  / Bengaluru,  June  19, 2025  – AXISCADES Technologies Ltd., a premier Indian technology 
solutions provider in defense, aerospace, a nd space electronics and software, has signed a strategic 
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Aldoria, a European leader in Space Surveillance and 
Situational Awareness (SSA), as part of their shared vision to strengthen India’s burgeoning space 
capabili ties.  
The announcement was made at the Paris Air Show 2025 , Le Bourget , marking a significant milestone 
in international space collaboration aimed at bolstering India’s ability to monitor, manage, and 
secure its space assets amidst growing global activity in orbit.  
Under the MoU, both organizations will evaluate opportunities for high -level data and system 
alignment to provide cutting -edge SSA solutions that contribute to India’s space safety, security, and 
sovereignty. This collaboration also includes prospects for sharing select SSA insights and technical 
know -how to ensure alignment with the evolving requirements of the Indian space ecosystem.  
AXISCADES and Aldori a engaged in discussions with ISRO and ISTRAC for the pa st 5 months and 
decided to go with the partnership to address the potential.  
Furthermore, AXISCADES and Aldoria will together explore the development of software and system 
integration concepts to enhance situational awareness, with a focus on creating scalable, end -to-
end solutions. This collaboration reflects a forward -looking vi sion to equip India with advanced tools 
for orbital monitoring and collision avoidance.  
Commenting on the partnership, Dr. Sampath Ravinarayanan, Chairman, AXISCADES Technologies 
Ltd., said: “India’s growing constellation of space assets demands robust, indigenous SSA systems. 
Our collaboration with Aldoria marks an important milestone in our  ongoing commitment to 
providing India's space ambitions with the best technology solutions available. We aspire to 
contribute and create a safe and sustainable space environment by combining our engineering 
expertise with Aldoria's pioneering role in spac e surveillance. Strategic space capabilities are 
essential for national security and long -term space exploration, and this alliance is a step toward 
enabling them.”  
Romain Lucken, CEO, Aldoria, stated: “This agreement reflects Aldoria’s mission to democratize 
SSA data and making it accessible to nations. By combining our high -precision telescope technology 
with AXISCADES’ software expertise, we are poised to deliver h igh-fidelity solutions tailored to 
India’s strategic requirements.”

About Aldoria   
Aldoria is a French company founded in 2017 to tackle the critital challenges of space 
safety and security.  From designing and assembling optical sensors to operating a global 
sensor network, processing and  fusing data, managing space object catalogs, and 
delivering high -value services like collision avoidance  and maneuver detection, Aldoria 
covers the entire spectrum of space surveillance for both civilian and  military 
applications.  
To power these services, Aldoria is deploying a proprietary optical sensor network across 
four continents,  with 15 fully operational telescopes across six stations in January 2025 , 
with key contracts from EU SST and CNES, Aldoria is scaling rapidly to  secure of space 
operations.
