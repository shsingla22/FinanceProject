CPCL’S FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE FOR THE QUARTER ENDED 31.03.2026  
SUSTAINED OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE  
 
CPCL delivered outstanding operational results during Q 4 FY2025 –26, achieving a crude 
throughput of 2.93 million metric tonnes  (MMT), compared to 2.97 MMT in the corresponding 
quarter of the previous financial year. This translates to a capacity utilisation of 112%, 
underscoring efficient plant operations and high reliability.  
For the year ended March  31, 202 6, crude throughput stood at 11.71  MMT, as against 10.45  
MMT in the same period last year, maintaining a capacity utilisation of 11 2%. The Company 
continued to maintain its best -ever distillate yield of about 80% achieved during the year, 
underscoring its operational excellence and sustained focus on energy efficiency.  
FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE  
The Company’s financial results for the quarter reflect ed the robust physical performance and 
improved refining margin s. 
For the quarter ended March  31, 202 6, Revenue from Operations stood at ₹  20,455  crore, 
compared to ₹  20,581 crore in the corresponding period last year. The Company reported a 
Profit Before Tax (PBT) of ₹  1890 crore and a Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₹ 1400  crore, as against 
a Profit Before Tax of ₹ 582 crore and Profit  After Tax of ₹ 450 crore during the same quarter 
last year.  
For the year ended March  31, 202 6, Revenue from Operations stood at ₹  78,611 crore, 
compared to ₹  71,050 crore in the previous year. The Company reported a Profit Before Tax 
of ₹ 4122  crore and a Profit After Tax of ₹  3062  crore, as against Profit  Before Tax of ₹ 208 
crore and Profit After Tax of ₹174 crore in the corresponding period of the previous financial 
year.  
The Gross Refining Margin (GRM) for the quarter improved to US$ 13.75  per barrel, as against 
a US$ 6.22 per barrel in the same period last year. For the year ended , the GRM stood at US$ 
9.28 per barrel, compared to a US$ 4.22 per barrel in the previous year.                                          
        
On a consolidated basis, for the year ended  March  31, 202 6, CPCL recorded a Profit After Tax 
of ₹ 3103  crore, and for the quarter  ended March  31, 202 6, CPCL recorded a Profit After Tax 
of ₹ 1422  crore.  
The board has recommended a final dividend of ₹ 54 per share with face value of ₹ 10 per share 
in addition to an Interim dividend of ₹ 8 per share declared during the year.
