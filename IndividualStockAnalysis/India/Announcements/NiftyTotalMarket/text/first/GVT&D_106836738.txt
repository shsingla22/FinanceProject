GE Vernova T&D India Limited Announces Second  Quarter FY 2026-27 Results  
 
New Delhi,  October  30, 202 6: GE Vernova T&D India Limited  (NSE:GVT&D) reported on October 30, 
2026 its financial results for the second  quarter ended September  30, 202 6. 
For the second  quarter ( July 1, 202 6 – September  30, 202 6)  
  
• Revenue was INR 20.3 billion, against INR 15.4 billion in Quarter Ended Sep 2025, up 32% YoY  
• EBITDA was at INR 5.5 billion (27.1%), against INR 4.0 billion (25.8%) in Quarter Ended Sep 2025, 
up 39% YoY  
• Profit After Tax was at INR 4.3 billion (21.3%), against INR 3.0 billion (19.5%) in Quarter Ended 
Sep 2025, up 44% YoY  
• Order bookings were INR 24.4 billion, against INR 16.1 billion in Quarter Ended Sep 2025, up by 
52% YoY  
 
Key Wins i n second  quarter (July 1, 2026 – September 30, 2026)  
 
• Secured order for supply of 400 kV Generator Transformers and   420 kV GIS package for a major 
1,500 MW pumped storage hydro project in the state of Maharashtra . 
• Secured order for s upply of 800 kV and 420 kV GIS packages for a transmission project in the 
state of Andhra Pradesh.  
• Secured order for supply of 400 kV Reactors and 420/220 kV GIS package for a TBCB 
transmission project in the state of Himachal Pradesh . 
• Secured order for supply of 765 kV shunt reactors (110 MVAR & 80 MVAR) for a TBCB 
transmission project in state of Andhra Pradesh . 
• Secured m ultiple orders for export of AIS/GIS equipment to North America, Europe, Middle East 
and Africa.  
• Secured m ultiple orders for supply of Grid Automation packages from state utilities, EPCs for 
transmission and   data center projects.

About GE  Vernova  T&D India  
GE Vernova T&D India Ltd is the listed entity of GE Vernova’s Electrification segment in India. With over 100 
years of presence in the country, GE Vernova T&D India is a leading player in the power transmission and 
distribution business. The company provid es a versatile and robust range of solutions for connecting and 
evacuating power from generations sources onto the grid, and a wide range of products including power 
transformers, circuit breakers, gas -insulated switchgear, instrument transformers, substation automation, 
digital software solutions, turnkey substation solutions, FACTS, HVDC, and maintenance support. With five 
manufacturing sites, GE Vernova T&D India is future ready to meet the industry’s growing demand for grid 
equipment and services.   GE Vernova is committed to invent, deploy, and service technology to help 
decarbonize and electrify the Indian grid,  and catalyse access to more secure, sustainable, reliable, and
