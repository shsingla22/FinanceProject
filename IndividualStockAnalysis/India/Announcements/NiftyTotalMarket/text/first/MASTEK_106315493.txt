About Great Place to Work Certification™  
Great Place To Work® Certification ™ is the most definitive “employer -of-choice” recognition that 
companies aspire to achieve. It is the only recognition based entirely on what employees report 
about their workplace experience –  specifically, how consistently they experience a high -trust 
workplace. Great Place to Work Certification is recognized worldwide by employees and employers 
alike and is the global benchmark for identifying and recognizing outstanding employee experience. 
Every year, more than 10,000 companies across 60 countries apply to get Great Place To Work -
Certified.
