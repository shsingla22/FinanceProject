JBM Electric Vehicles launches Electric City Bus — ECOLIFE e12 
at Singapore Urban Mobility Week 2026 
Nov 4, 2026| Singapore: JBM Electric Vehicles (JBM EV), a global leading electric bus manufacturer and EV 
Ecosystem player launched its all-electric city bus - ECOLIFE e12 today at the Singapore Urban Mobility Week 
(SUMW) 2026. The bus was launched by Dr. Shilpak Ambule, High Commissioner of India to the Republic of 
Singapore in the presence of Mr. Nishant Arya, Chairman, JBM Electric Vehicles. JBM Electric Vehicles is the 
flagship company of the $4.0 billion global Indian conglomerate, JBM Group. 
JBM EV brings in the agility and innovation of an established global leader with the proven scale, 
manufacturing muscle and international expertise. The company offers its customers a low-risk, high-reward 
propositioni.e. cutting-edge e-bus technology with thousands of units of proven real-world reliability, backed 
by one of the largest dedicated manufacturing capacity of 20,000 buses per annum and a commitment to 
exceptional local support all at a highly competitive value. 
The ECOLIFE citybus comes with a light weight monocoque full aluminium body structure and equipped with 
state-of-the-art features including Intelligent Transport System as per all Singapore regulations, advanced 
telematics giving 24x7 access to vehicle’s state of health amongst host of other features. 
JBM ECOLIFE is equipped with the Advanced Driver-Assistance System (ADAS) technology that assists the 
driver detect obstacles and checks driver errors for utmost safe transit. A host of other additional safety 
features include, Forward vehicle collision warning systems (FVCWS), Lane Departure Warning System, 
Pedestrian and cyclist collision warning (MOIS), Blind spot detection, protection of vehicle against cyber- 
attack, driver drowsiness, attention warning and advance driver distraction warning and Fire detection & 
suppression system FDSS, 360 degrees camera, etc. 
Speaking at the occasion, Mr. Nishant Arya, Chairman, JBM Electric Vehicles (P) Ltd. said, " Our mission is to 
decarbonize public transport by creating new and sustainable global benchmarks. At JBM EV, we believe that 
the future of green mobility lies in creating vehicles that seamlessly blend innovation, sustainability and user- 
centric design. Our electric mobility solutions are not just about reducing carbon footprints, they are about 
elevating the quality of life for individuals and communities by making transportation smarter, safer, and more 
accessible”. 
“We believe in the power of partnerships and collaboration to continuously improve, listening to the needs of 
our customers to develop solutions to address their pain points. We constantly evolve to provide new ideas 
and solutions in the e-mobility world. We invest to better support our customers by thinking global and acting 
local in meeting the challenges of today and tomorrow”. 
In a strategic move to strengthen its operations, JBM EV has established its APAC headquarters in Singapore. 
This hub will serve as the company’s central base for marketing & sales, aftersales, operations and 
partnerships across the APAC region. 
JBM Electric Vehicles brings to the table a robust orderbook of 10,000 electric buses to be deployed over next 
2years across the globe. The company has set up one of the world's largest dedicated integrated electric bus 
manufacturing facility, located in India, with an annual production capacity of 20,000 electric buses. With 
over 4,000+ electric buses on road across various geographies, JBM electric buses have clocked 500+ million 
e-kms having catered to over 1.5 billion passengers and having saved 650+ billion kgs CO2 emissions till 
date.
