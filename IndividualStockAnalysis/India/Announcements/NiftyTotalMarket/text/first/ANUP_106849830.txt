Ahmedabad, November  06, 2026: The Anup Engineering Limited (ANUP), one of India’s leading manufacturers 
of static process equipment, today announced its financial results for the quarter and half year ended 
September 30, 2026.  
The Company serves core industries such as Oil & Gas and  Petrochemicals, transitory and new -age energy 
segments including LNG/LPG and Hydrogen, and high -technology sectors such as Defence, Nuclear and 
Aerospace.  
The quarter reflects a meaningful sequential  improvement in our financial performance. The subdued order 
environment caused by geopolitical disruptions over the past year resulted in lower revenue levels, but also 
enabled us to proactively realign and debottleneck our operations. These initiatives h ave strengthened our 
execution platform and position s us well to capitalize on the opportunities reflected in our growing order book.

PERFORMANCE HIGHLIGHTS  – Q2 and H1 FY 27  
■ Sequential Recovery . Consistent with our commentary during first quarter call , we delivered a strong 
sequential recovery, with revenue in Q2 growing 46% QoQ and improved EBITDA margins.  
■ Under -absorption of Fixed cost . During Q2 and H1 FY27, sub -optimal revenue levels impacted EBITDA 
margins due to the under -absorption of fixed cost s. Nevertheless, gross margins remained resilient, with 
only a modest impact from higher raw material costs.
