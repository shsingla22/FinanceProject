marketing representations

/labelling representations  may fall 
within the scope of diseases and 
conditions specified in the Schedule to 
the DMR Act. Accordingly, the authority 
has sought the Company's response in 
relation to the applicability of Sections 3 
and 4 of the DMR Act.  
 
Pending examination of the Company's 
response, the communication, inter alia, 
calls upon the Company to take certain 
measures, including recall of the 
product, discontinuation of its 
distribution, sale and supply until the

communication are addressed,

promotional /labelling representations ,

is in the process of formulating an 
appropriate response. The Company 
will take all necessary actions in 
accordance with applicable law and 
regulatory requirements and does not 
presently anticipate any material impact 
on its business operations arising solely 
from the receipt of the notice.
