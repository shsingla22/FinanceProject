About Edelweiss Financial Services  
Edelweiss is a diversified financial services company with seven independent and well -governed businesses. The businesses 
include  Alternative Asset Management , Mutual Fund, Asset Reconstruction, Corporate Lending, Ho using  Finance, General 
Insurance and Life Insurance. The businesses have robust operating platforms, dedicated management teams and strong 
boards that ensure the highest standards of governance. Edelweiss employs nearly  6,000 people, serves around 1.1 Cr 
customers, and manages  around  INR 2,30,000 Cr worth of assets.
