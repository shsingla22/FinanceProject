Noida, 5th September 2025 : RateGain  Travel Technologies Limited (NSE: RATEGAIN), a global 
provider of AI -powered SaaS solutions for travel and hospitality,  today announced that its AI -
powered voice agent, UNO VIVA, has been honored as the Winner for Excellence in Best AI Solution 
Showcase at the CII National Artificial Intelligence Awards 2025, held during the Global AI Summit in 
New Delhi.  
The CII National AI Awards and Summit 2025 spotlighted innovations driving India’s digital 
transformation, with participation from leaders across government, industry, and the travel and 
hospitality sector. The IndiaAI Mission, under the leadership of the Ministry of Electronics and 
Information Technology, is enabling India’s rise as a global AI hub. RateGain is proud to contribute to 
this vision with AI -first solutions like UNO VIVA that are solving real -world problems from India for 
the world.  
UNO VIVA is the industry’s first CRS -integrated AI voice agent for hotel revenue teams. Beyond 
reducing revenue leakage from voice calls, it uses adaptive intelligence to understand booking intent, 
convert more opportunities, and grow direct revenue. With real -time responses, end -to-end 
reservation handling, multilingual support in 30+ languages, and rich call analytics, UNO VIVA 
empowers hotels to maximize revenue while delivering seamless guest experiences. This award 
underscores RateGain’s commitment to building AI that not only automates but transforms how travel 
businesses sell, serve, and scale globally.  
Bhanu Chopra, Founder and Managing Director, RateGain , said, “We are proud to be at the 
forefront of AI innovation in travel. This award validates our belief that AI built in India can solve 
global challenges. With our UNO platform at the core, we are reimagining every product to be AI -
native —designed for in telligence, speed, and personalization at scale. This is not just a milestone for 
UNO VIVA but a motivation to keep pushing boundaries and build the next generation of travel 
technology.”
