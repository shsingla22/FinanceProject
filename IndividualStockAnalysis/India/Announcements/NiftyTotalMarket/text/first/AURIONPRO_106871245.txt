Aurionpro Named  Among Forbes Asia’s Best Under A Billion 
Companies for 2026  
The recognition places Aurionpro among 200 of the Asia -Pacific region’s top -
performing publicly listed companies  
Mumbai, November 26, 2026:  Aurionpro Solutions Limited, a global 
technology solutions provider, has been named to  Forbes Asia’s Best Under A 
Billion 2026 , which  honours 200 of the top -performing publicly listed 
companies across the Asia -Pacific region with annual revenues of under US$1 
billion.  
 
 
 
 
© 2026, Forbes Media Asia Pte Ltd. Used with permission.  
Featured in the August 2026 issue of Forbes Asia, the list evaluates more than 
19,000 small and midsized publicly traded enterprises across the region using 
quantitative and qualitative metrics , including debt, sales, earnings per share 
growth, and corporate governance , to identify companies with sustained 
performance.
