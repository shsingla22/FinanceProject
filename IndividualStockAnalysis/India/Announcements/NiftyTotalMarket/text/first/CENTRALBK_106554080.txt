Sub: Intimation under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 – Co-Lending Partnership with M/s. Capri Global Capital Ltd . 
 
We would like to inform that Central Bank of India has renewed  Co-Lending Partnership with 
M/s. Capri Global Capital Limited  to offer Loans at competitive rates, subject to compliance 
with the applicable law(s) including the revised Co -Lending Arrangements (CLA)  guidelines 
dated 28.11.2025 issued by Reserve Bank of India (RBI). The participation by both the entities in 
this Co -Lending arrangement will result in greater expansion of portfolio by Central Bank of India 
and M/s. Capri Global Capital Ltd . 
 
M/s. Capri Global Capital Ltd is registered NBFC with Reserve Bank of India.  The NBFC is 
engaged in providing Secured LAP  and Gold Loans.  
  
The company is headquartered in Mumbai and the network of branches across India enables it to 
source and service clients with a network of 1331 branches spread across 19  States  / Union 
territories  in India . The AUM of the company  as on 31st December 2025  was Rs.23,916.00 Crore. 
Under the arrangement, M/s. Capri Global Capital Ltd  will originate loan proposals, which will be 
processed by both lenders as per jointly formulated credit parameters and eligibility criteria . The 
borrower will benefit from the blended interest rate charged.  M/s. Capri Global Capital Ltd  will 
service the loan account throughout the life cycle of the loan. The Co -Lending arrangement  is 
expected to help both the entities and the end borrower  and will also help to expand customer 
outreach across India .
