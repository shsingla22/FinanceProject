Coforge Celebrat es 20 Years in Peru, Reinforces Commitment to 
Move Enterprises from AI Ambition to Execution  
 
Lima to serve as  regional hub for expansion in Latin America  
 
Princeton, NJ & Lima, Peru | August 2 7, 2026  — Coforge Limited (NSE: COFORGE), a global 
AI-native engineering services leader , is marking its 20th year of operations in Peru and announc ing 
the country’s expanded role as the foundation of the company’s  AI-led engineering expansion  in Latin 
America . Building on  two decades of local  presence , Lima will serve as Coforge’s principal delivery, 
innovation, and client engagement hub for the region.   
 
The firm’s Peru operations began two decades ago as a local engineering unit , since evolving into a 
strategic growth hub employing more than 1,300  employees and serving banking, financial services, 
retail, and consumer clients . Coforge's delivery model for Latin America n enterprises is built around 
execution intensity, expertise in AI, digital engineering, cloud, and data, and deep industry and 
regional market knowledge .  
 
As Coforge continues to strengthen its presence in Latin America, this milestone underscores the 
company's long -term commitment to the region and marks an important step in its growth and 
expansion strategy.  The strengthened Latin America operations  will help local, regional and global 
clients move faster from technology ambition to execution with greater proximity, agility, and access 
to specialized engineering and AI talent.  
 
In advance of  a celebratory  event  in Lima  for Coforge’s  Latin American clients, partners , and 
employees , Sudhir Singh, Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director, Coforge , said, “We 
are delighted to celebrate 20 years of business in Peru, and to demonstrate  our ongoing commitment 
to deliver AI-led engineering services to enterprises , and contribute to the region’s technological and 
economic advancement .” 
 
“Peru is among Latin America ’s leaders in AI adoption , and w e believe that Coforge’s  ability to 
operationaliz e AI and embed intelligence into enterprise decisions, workflows, and operating models, 
will help our clients  move from AI adoption to enterprise autonomy .” 
 
Alvaro Lang, Executive Vice President, Banking & Financial Services and Head of Latin 
America, Coforge , said, “ This milestone  is a testament to  the trust our customers place in us, the 
strength of our partnerships, and the remarkable talent of our teams across Latin America. Together, 
we have built a thriving business that is now a key contributor to Coforge’s growth. Peru has been a 
cornerstone of our engineering excellence, and it will continue to be a strategic hub as we accelerate 
our expansion across the region.”
