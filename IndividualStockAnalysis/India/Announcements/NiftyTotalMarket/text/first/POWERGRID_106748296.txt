 Shri Amol Babulal Taori (DIN: 11890590) is a Chartered 
Accountant with nearly 30 years of rich and diverse 
experience across Finance, Treasury, International Trade, 
Marketing, and Compensation Management.  
 
Prior to his appointment as Director (Finance), 
POWERGRID, Shri Taori served as Executive Director (In -
charge) – International Trade at Hindustan Petroleum 
Corporation Limited (HPCL), where he managed crude 
procurement of approximately 700 kbd, product imp orts and 
exports, chartering/shipping arrangements, and oil price risk 
management.  
 
During his career, Shri Taori has managed one of India’s 
largest corporate treasuries for over a decade and 
spearheaded HPCL’s maiden USD bond issue, achieving 
one of the most competitive pricing spreads by an Indian 
issuer. He also conceptualised the Oil Bond Swap, which 
helped reduce borrowing costs, and facilitated HPCL’s first 
foreign currency swaps and revolving line of credit. He 
represented HPCL at global equity and debt roadshows, 
contributing significantly to strengthening investor 
confidence. He h as also been a Trustee of all the employee 
benefits trusts of HPCL.   
 
Shri Taori has also served as a Director on the Board of 
HPCL Middle East FZCO, Dubai.
