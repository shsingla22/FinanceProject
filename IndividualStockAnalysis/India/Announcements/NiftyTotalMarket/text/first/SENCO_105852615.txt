Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“LODR Regulations”), this is to inform you that the Company has received 
an intimation under Section 143(1) of the Income Tax Act, 1961 from Centralized Processing 
Center, Income Tax Department bearing an Income Tax demand Rs. 10,46,49,620/- (Ten crores 
forty-six lacs forty-nine thousand six hundred twenty only) for the Assessment Year 2023-24.  
 
The Income Tax demand arose on account of non-grant of credit of self-assessment tax paid 
aggregating to INR 10 crore, but incidentally wrong challan no & BSR code stated in ITR and 
consequentially, interest has been levied under sections 234B and 234C of the Act, resulting 
into the aggregate demand of Rs. 10,46,49,620/-. 
 
Based on our assessment, we believe that the Demand Notice does not have any material 
impact on the financials, operations, or other activities of the Company 
 
Additional details required to be disclosed pursuant to the Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing
