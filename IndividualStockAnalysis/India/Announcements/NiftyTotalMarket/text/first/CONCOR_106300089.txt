Press Release 
Container Corporation of India Ltd (CONCOR) and M/ s. Rais Hassan Saadi Group 
(RHS), Dubai, signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on 14th July 2025 to 
explore collaborative opportunities in the overseas shipping and logistics sector. The 
partnership is envisioned to deliver integrated, seamless, and cost-effective end-to­
end logistics solutions, with a special focus on enhancing international multimodal 
connectivity. 
The event was graced by Shri Sanjay Swarup, Chairman and Managing Director of 
CONCOR, and Mr. Saadi Al Rais, Chairman of RHS Group, along with Directors and 
senior officials from both organizations. This strategic collaboration brings together 
CONCOR's vast experience in inland logistics, terminal operations, and supply chain 
solutions with RHS Group's strong global presence in shipping, freight forwarding, 
and maritime services. 
This MoU marks a significant step toward creating synergies that will contribute to 
the growth of global trade and position both organizations as key players in the 
evolving logistics and shipping landscape 
On this occasion, CMD CONCOR Shri Swarup mentioned that this MoU facilitates 
achieving our aim to provide seamless end to end Logistics solutions for the customers 
beyond Indian territory. 
Mr. Saadi Al Rais, Chairman of RHS Group expressed pleasure in entering into an 
alliance with CONCOR and assured best of the services on behalf of RHS Group.
