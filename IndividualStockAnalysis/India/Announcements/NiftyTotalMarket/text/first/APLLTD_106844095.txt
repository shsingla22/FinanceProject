Press Release 
Alembic Pharmaceuticals reports strong Q2 FY27 performance 
Revenue up 36%; PAT up 81% YoY 
 
 
Vadodara, November 4th, 2026: Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited reported its 
consolidated financial results for the second quarter and half year ended 30th September, 
2026. 
 
Financial Highlights for Q2 FY27 
 Revenue from operations increased by 36% YoY to Rs.2603 Cr. 
 EBITDA up by 78% YoY to Rs. 578 Cr, margin at 22% 
 Profit After Tax at Rs. 335 Cr, up 81% YoY  
Commenting on the results, Mr. Pranav Amin, Managing Director, Alembic 
Pharmaceuticals Limited, said,  
“We delivered another quarter of strong, broad-based growth and improved profitability, 
reflecting consistent execution across markets. Our US business delivered an exceptional 
performance, driven by strong volume growth and new launches, while India regained 
momentum as Ex-US and API continued their growth trajectories. In India Branded, 
improving momentum in chronic therapies is strengthening the quality of growth, while in 
US Branded, we are encouraged by the traction building across our existing portfolio. 
Following a strong first half, we are well positioned to sustain growth and create long-term 
value for all our stakeholders” 
Operational Highlights  
India Branded Business 
 India Branded Business delivered 14% YoY growth, reaching Rs 725 Cr. in revenue 
for the quarter. 
 Gynecology, Cardiology, Gastrology and Ophthalmology registered robust growth. 
 Animal Health continued its growth momentum with 23% growth for the quarter.

About Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited 
Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited, a vertically integrated research and development 
pharmaceutical company, has been at the forefront of healthcare since 1907. 
Headquartered in India, Alembic is a publicly listed company that manufactures and 
markets generic pharmaceutical products all over the world. Alembic's state of the art 
research and manufacturing facilities are approved by regulatory authorities of many 
developed countries including the US FDA. Alembic is one of the leaders in branded 
generics in India.
