Sub : Notice of Annual General Meeting of the Bank 
 
 
Pursuant to SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, we inform that 23rd Annual General 
Meeting (AGM) of the Bank is scheduled to be held on Friday,12th June, 2026 at 11:00

1 Agenda  
Item no. 1 : To discuss, approve and adopt the Audited Balance

period covered by the Accounts and the Auditors’ Report on the 
Balance Sheet and Accounts. 
 
 
Item no. 2 : To declare dividend on equity shares of the Bank for 
the financial year 2025-26. 
 
Item no. 3: To approve the Equity Capital Raising Plan for the 
financial year 2026-27.

Director on the Board of UCO Bank w.e.f. 13.05.2026.
