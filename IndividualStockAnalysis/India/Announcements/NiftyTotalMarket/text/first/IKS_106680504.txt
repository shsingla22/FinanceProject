Investor FAQ: WWMG  
 
1. What is this investment going into the MSO? What are the uses of cash?  
• A total equity infusion of $15 million has been approved  until March 31, 2029, which will result in 
IKS becoming the majority owner with an immediate investment of $3 million upto  3rd July 2026.  
• The strategic allocation of this capital is for strategic growth initiatives, including physician hiring, 
Value -Based Care (VBC) expansion, and general working capital support.  
• This capital injection is designed to facilitate the expansion of the physician practice. Currently, 13 
recently acquired physicians are in their initial ramp -up stage, with recruitment for an additional 
nine doctors slated for the coming 6 months. The org anization anticipates growth as these clinical 
practices reach maturity, leading to increased operating leverage and a projected rise in IKS 
platform services revenue.  
 
2. How is the implementation of the platform progressing?  
• Platform adoption is nearly complete following the resolution of initial Epic access challenges.  
• As of June 2026, 95% clinicians have been successfully onboarded onto the Scribble platform. Key 
technologies such as the Virtual Clinic Assistant, Referral Management, and My Care Hub are 
operational. Ongoing technical specification updates are in progres s to resolve remaining data 
compatibility issues for EVE - Prior Authorization.  
 
3. What is the current quantum of investment?  
• The total investment by IKS  which stands at $17 million today is expected to reach $32 million by 
2029. The immediate near term investment is ~5mn.  
 
4. How is the investment in WWMG playing out compared to your expectations?  
• While the platform implementation was delayed due to an initial Epic access issue, the investment 
is panning out as on expected lines, with visible green shoots on various parameters. The 
organisation  has acquired 13 new providers in the last six months and plans to acquire 9 providers 
in the next 6 months. On the existing providers, we are able to see improvement in the 
performance as the platform is being implemented.  
• Operational Efficiency: Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) optimization has successfully reduced 
Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) from 46.1 days to 41.7 days.  
 
• Cost Management: The organization has taken actions that will result in $2.5 million dollars of 
annualised savings in the upcoming year.  
• Performance Growth: Visit counts increased by 3% from April to May 2026 as platform 
implementation accelerated. Additionally, all VBC agreements have been renegotiated to 
contribute to future revenue growth.  
• Our return on investment is along expected lines and in line with our overall business ROE. WWMG 
continues to be a top 10 customer for IKS.  
 
5. What are the intangible and strategic benefits of the WWMG partnership?  
• WWMG serves as a powerful brand ambassador and reference site, enhancing market credibility.  
• WWMG’s endorsement directly facilitated securing full platform deals with similar physician -led 
multi -speciality groups, helping validate the unified solution model.  
• Acting as a premier partner, WWMG has enabled the organization to obtain a license as an 
Accountable Care Organization (ACO) convenor.
