“Target Company”) was incorporated in India on 
April 25, 2024 . It is a special purpose vehicle 
engaged in the development, construction, 
ownership and operation of solar power generation 
projects. The Target Company is developing an 
aggregated solar power portfolio of approximately 
990 MW (AC) with an approximate project cost of 
around Rs. 3,900 Crore, in Maharashtra under 
Component C of the Government of India’s  PM – 
KUSUM scheme. The project is intended to support 
the solarisation of agricultural feeders and improve 
the availability of daytime electricity for irrigation  
The portfolio is backed by a 25- year Power Purchase 
Agreement with Maharashtra State Electricity 
Distribution Company Limited (MSEDCL) at a 
fixed tariff of ₹3.10 per unit. The indicative total 
project cost is approximately ₹3,900 crore, and debt 
financing has been sanctioned by reputed financial 
institution, subject to applicable financing and 
disbursement conditions.  
As the Target Company has not yet commenced 
commercial operations, it has not reported any 
revenue from operations in FY 2024- 25 or FY 2025-
26. The Target Company’s presence is limited to 
India.
