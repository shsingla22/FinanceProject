Delhi, 05th November 2026:  Enviro Infra Engineers Limited, a leading infrastructure company specializing 
in the design, construction, operation, and maintenance of Water and Wastewater Treatment Plants 
(WWTPs) and Water Supply Scheme Projects (WSSPs) for government authorities acro ss India and 
rapidly scaling its presence across renewable energy and battery storage infrastructure, today announced 
its unaudited consolidated financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.  
With an established presence in water and wastewater infrastructure, the Company provides integrated 
engineering solutions across sewage treatment, sewerage networks, industrial effluent treatment, water 
supply and treated -water reuse. Supported by its in -house engineering capabilities, advanced treatment 
technologies and long -term operations and maintenance expertise, Enviro Infra continues to strengthen 
its core water infrastructure business while expanding its presence across renewable energy and battery  
storage infrastructure.

For the Half Year Ended September 30, 2026 (H1 FY27):  
• Revenue from Operations stood at ₹7,230 million , registering a 54.39% YoY growth, supported 
by execution across the Company's core water and wastewater infrastructure business and 
increasing contribution from renewable energy operations.  
• EBITDA stood at ₹1,505 million , registering a 16.56% YoY growth, with an EBITDA margin of 
20.82%.  
• PAT stood at ₹847 million , with a PAT margin of 11. 49%. 
• The core water and wastewater infrastructure business, including O&M and annuity revenue, 
accounted for approximately 71% of consolidated revenue , reflecting the continued significance 
of the Company's established operations.  

• Renewable Energy contributed approximately 29% of consolidated revenue , demonstrating the 
increasing scale of the Company's diversified business portfolio.  
• The Company's consolidated order book stood at ₹67,782 million , providing healthy execution 
visibility across its infrastructure businesses.  
For the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026 (Q2 FY27):  
• Revenue from Operations stood at ₹3,638 million , registering a 60.01% YoY growth, supported by 
continued execution across the Company's diversified infrastructure portfolio.  
• EBITDA stood at ₹749 million , registering a 15.27% YoY growth, with an EBITDA margin of 
20.58%.  
• PAT stood at ₹395 million , with a PAT margin of 10.6 2%. 
Key Business Developments  
• Water & Wastewater Infrastructure:  The Company continues to strengthen its core water and 
wastewater business through the execution of EPC, HAM and long -term O&M projects. During H1 
FY27, it secured two HAM projects in Varanasi under the Namami Gange Programme, with a 
combined value of app roximately ₹257 crore (excluding GST), including 15 years of O&M.  
• Wind EPC Order – Tata Power Renewable Energy:  The Company's step -down subsidiary, 
Suyog Urja Limited, received an LOI worth ₹224.19 crore (including GST) for EPC works relating 
to the 180 MW NTPC Wind Power Project in Maharashtra.  
• Wind EPC Order – ARIE Energy:  In October 2026, Suyog Urja received an LOI worth ₹228 crore 
(excluding GST) for the 100 MW Ajani Wind Power Project in Maharashtra, further strengthening 
the Company's wind EPC portfolio.  
• Renewable Energy Diversification:  Renewable Energy contributed approximately 29% of H1 
FY27 revenue, compared with 11% in FY26, reflecting the growing contribution from solar, wind 
and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).  
Order Book  
As of September 30, 2026, the Company's diversified order book stood at ₹67,782 million (₹6,778.2 
crore) , providing healthy execution visibility across its businesses.  
• Water & Wastewater:  ₹34,387 million, comprising EPC orders of ₹21,056 million, HAM projects 
of ₹3,463 million and long -term O&M contracts of ₹9,867 million.  
• Renewable Energy:  ₹33,395 million across Solar EPC, Wind EPC, BESS EPC, IPP and O&M 
projects.  
Commenting on the overall performance of the Company, Mr. Sanjay Jain, Chairman, Enviro Infra 
Engineers Limited, said  
“Q2 FY27 reflects strong growth in our operations, supported by continued execution across our core water 
and wastewater infrastructure business and the increasing contribution from our renewable energy 
portfolio. During the quarter, revenue grew by 60% ye ar-on-year, while EBITDA increased by 15.3%, 
reflecting the growing scale and diversification of our business.  
The core Water and Wastewater segment contributes around 71% of consolidated revenue, underscoring 
its significance to the overall business.  Continuing on the back of our strong execution capabilities, the 
company is in advanced stages of completion of 3 STP projects, namely 60 MLD STP at Mathura, 77 MLD 
STP at Yamuna Nagar and 50 MLD STP at Amritsar, being delivered well ahead of time.  
 Our renewable energy vertical continues to gain momentum across solar, wind and Battery Energy 
Storage Systems (BESS), contributing approximately 29% of our revenues during H1 FY27. A key 
development during the quarter was the ₹224.19 crore wind EPC order  secured through our step -down 
subsidiary, Suyog Urja, from Tata Power Renewable Energy Limited, further strengthening our capabilities 
and presence in the renewable energy sector.  
 
 
Our diversified order book of approximately ₹6,778 crore across water infrastructure and renewable energy 
provides healthy revenue visibility. While profitability has been impacted by changes in project mix and 
elevated costs, we remain focused on discipli ned execution, operational efficiencies and maintaining 
sustainable margins as our businesses scale.  
Looking ahead, with increasing investments in water infrastructure, renewable energy and energy storage, 
we remain confident in our long -term growth opportunities. Our focus continues to be on strengthening 
execution capabilities, expanding our presence ac ross sustainable infrastructure segments and creating 
long-term value for our stakeholders."  
About Enviro Infra Engineers Limited:  
Enviro Infra Engineers Limited, incorporated in 2009 and headquartered in New Delhi, is an infrastructure 
company specializing in the design, construction, operation and maintenance of Water and Wastewater 
Treatment Plants (WWTPs) and Water Supply Scheme P rojects (WSSPs) for government authorities 
across India. The Company's expertise spans Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs), Sewerage Schemes 
(SS), Common Effluent Treatment Plants (CETPs), Water Treatment Plants (WTPs), pumping stations 
and water supply pipelin es, supported by advanced treatment technologies, including Zero Liquid 
Discharge (ZLD) solutions.  
Building on its strong foundation in water and wastewater infrastructure, the Company has diversified into 
renewable energy through its subsidiary, EIE Renewables, with capabilities across solar, wind and Battery 
Energy Storage Systems (BESS), further stre ngthening its presence in sustainable infrastructure.  
Backed by an experienced in -house engineering team and strategic joint venture partnerships, Enviro Infra 
Engineers delivers complex infrastructure projects while adhering to the highest quality, environmental 
and regulatory standards. The Company's projec ts are supported by key government initiatives, including 
the Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation (AMRUT), the National Mission for Clean 
Ganga (NMCG) and the Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM), reinforcing its commitment to strengthening India's 
water and environmental infrastructure.
