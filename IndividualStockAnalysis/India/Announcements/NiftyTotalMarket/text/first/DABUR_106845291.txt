New Delhi, November 4th, 2026: Dabur India Limited, one of India's leading FMCG companies, 
announced its results for the quarter ended 30th September 2026. 
Key Highlights: 
• Consolidated Revenue: Q2 Revenue Grew 11.6% to ,3,563 crore -2nd straight quarter 
of double-digit growth; H1 Revenue at ,7,327 crore, 11.1% growth. 
• Net Profit: Q2 PAT rose 11.7% to '505 crore -4th consecutive quarter of double-digit 
profit growth; H1 PAT at ,1,096 Crore, up 13.4%. 
• India FMCG Business: 10.7% growth, with 5.5% underlying volume growth. 
• Premiumisation: Premium brands grew at 4X the pace of regular brands and accounts 
for 20% share of the domestic business. 
• International Business: Grew 20% in INR terms in Q2, with strong performances across 
key markets. H1 International business up 19.4%. 
Dabur's performance reflected the underlying strength of its core brands, continued 
momentum in the premium portfolio, and focused execution across categories. 
Commenting on the business performance , Dabur India Limited Chief Executive Officer-Indi a 
Business Mr. Herjit S. Bhalla said: 
"Our performance in Q2 reflects the strength of our brands and the sharper execution of our 
strategy. We delivered profitable growth despite operating in a challenging external 
environment marked by geopolitical uncertainty and elevated input-cost pressures. What gives 
me confidence is that the growth has been broad-based and backed by healthy underlying 
volumes." 
"The evolving channel landscape continues to present significant opportunities for us. This 
quarter saw a marked resurgence in General Trade with the channel growing by mid to high 
single digit, supported by our continued efforts under Project Saksham. We remain focused on 
strengthening our brands and expanding our presence across channels to sustain growth 
momentum," Mr. Bhalla said.
