Thomas Cook India reiterates its focus on retail expansion 
 Strengthens foothold in Delhi -NCR with inauguration of its outlet in 
Greater Noida  
 
Increases consumer access in NCR to 13 locations 
 
 The new Greater Noida outlet intends to leverage the strong tr avel demand from the region  
 Key growth drivers : group tours ; increased demand for premium holidays, luxury cruises , experiential travel  
 
 
Mumbai,  October  18, 2024:  Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel services company, has 
inaugurated i ts new franchise outlet in Gr eater Noida, to leverage the high- growth market of the Delhi -NCR 
region and s urrounding areas . This new store, further strengthens Thomas Cook ’s network in the Delhi -NCR 
region to 13 locations (6 owned stores and 7 Gold Circle Partner outlets), reaffirming its strategic commitment to 
expansion in viable growth markets.  
 
The location has been carefully selected: the vibrant  residential and business hubs of Greater Noida, offering  
Thomas Cook  a diverse customer base of  families, professionals /business travellers  and senior citizens.  The 
location also provides  access to NCR’s HNI -UHNI segment, offering expertise for their personalised travel 
requirements . 
 
Thomas Cook’s Greater Noida outlet will provide end-to-end travel services: International & Domestic Holidays 
(Group Tours, Personalised holidays , Cruises, etc.), Value Added Services like Travel Insurance, etc . In addition 
to the holiday experts at the outlets,  Thomas Cook’s omnichannel strategy equips customers with digital tool s 
like its AI platform to assist customers plan and book their personalized vacations in real- time.
