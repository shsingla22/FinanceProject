City Civil Court Bengaluru, Injuncts BTV a Kannada news channel of Bengaluru 
from telecasting defamatory news/message against the Karnataka Bank Ltd. 
BTV, a Kannada News Channel has published defamatory news/message on 29th 
and 30th of June 2025 without any basis. An attempt was made by the said channel 
to create confusion in the minds of the stakeholders of the bank including the 
shareholders and depositors. In order to restrain the said TV from publishing such 
baseless and defamatory news in its channel and to restrain it from creating panic 
and confusion amongst the stakeholders, the bank has filed a Civil Suit before the 
City Civil Court at Bengaluru on 30.06.2025 in O.S. No.4614/2025. 
The Learned 29th City Civil and Sessions Judge has restrained the defendant BTV 
from telecasting defamatory news/message against the plaintiff bank till next date 
vide order dated 01.07.2025. The operative portion of the order is as under: 
"Issue an ex-parte order of temporary injunction restraining the defendant from telecasting 
the defamatory news/message against the plaintiff bank, till next date". 
The bank has obtained a certified copy of the above order dated 01.07.2025. In the 
preamble of the order the Court has referred to the various baseless and defamatory 
allegations made by BTV against the bank. In order ·to avoid panic/ confusion 
amongst the stakeholders of the bank, the bank had to take legal recourse by filing a 
Civil Suit for awarding damages to the bank because of the defamatory news 
telecasted by BTV and for permanent injunction against BTV from telecasting any

sort of damage to the reputation of this 100 years old Bank. It is reiterated that the 
depositors, account holders, shareholders etc., need not have any concern about the 
reliability and the strength of the bank.
