 India’s No.1 Vodka Brand Magic Moments Crosses 8 Million Cases, Riding High 
on Strong Consumer Trends and Bold Flavour Innovations  
Flavour innovation under the ‘Flavours of India’ range driving the momentum further  
New Delhi, March 18, 2026  – Radico Khaitan Limited, one of India’s largest IMFL companies, has 
announced another significant milestone for its flagship brand Magic Moments Vodka, which has 
crossed 8 million cases in sales this year, fortifying its position as India’s No.1 vodka brand  and one 
of the fastest -growing vodka brands globally. Accelerating from 7.1 million cases last year to crossing 
the 8 million mark already this year, the brand’s sharp growth trajectory underscores its rising 
dominance and deepening consumer connect acros s markets.  
Radico Khaitan started its premiumization journey in 2006 with the launch of Magic Moments Vodka. 
Since then, it has steadily strengthened its position in the market through a strong combination of 
product innovation, consumer -centric experiences and a dee p understanding of evolving taste 
preferences. Today, Magic Moments is India’s largest premium vodka brand with around 60% market 
share and ranked as the 6th largest vodka brand in the world last year. The brand continues to 
command a leading position in t he Indian vodka market and has consistently climbed the global 
ranking ladder.  
A key driver of the brand’s recent momentum has been its innovative flavour portfolio, particularly the 
much -loved Magic Moments ‘Flavours of India’ range, which celebrates India’s iconic tastes through 
contemporary vodka expressions. The latest addition t o this portfolio, Jamun SpicyMint, has emerged 
as a runaway success since its launch, creating significant momentum among consumers and 
witnessing strong offtake across markets. Earlier variants such as Alphonso Mango and Thandaai, 
along with other flavour s have continued to receive strong consumer traction.  
Speaking on the milestone, Abhishek Khaitan, Managing Director, Radico Khaitan  said: “Crossing 
the 8 million case mark is a proud milestone for Magic Moments. It indicates a defining shift in India’s 
evolving spirit’s landscape. While vodka accounts for over 20 –25% of the global spirits market, in India 
it remains under -indexed at less tha n 4%, presenting a significant headroom for growth. What we are 
witnessing today is a clear inflection point, with vodka emerging as one of the fastest -growing 
categories in the country. With a commanding 60% share of the Indian vodka market, we are strong ly 
positioned to lead and shape this transformation. Our growth is being fuelled by a deep understanding 
of evolving consumer preferences, with innovations like Jamun SpicyMint setting new benchmarks by 
bringing bold, distinctly Indian flavours to the fore front of the category. With the changing consumer 
trends, our ambition is clear and unwavering, making Magic Moments as one of the leading vodka 
brands in the world.”  
Magic Moments Vodka today offers an extensive portfolio across Semi -Premium and Premium Vodka 
categories, including Magic Moments Remix, Verve and Dazzle, along with a growing lineup of 
innovative flavours and experiences that resonate with evolving consum er preferences. From 
pioneering flavour -led innovations to engaging consumers through music, culture and lifestyle 
platforms, the brand continues to strengthen its position among India’s young and aspirational 
consumers. With innovation -led growth and a st eadily expanding consumer base, Radico Khaitan 
continues to redefine the vodka experience in India while steadily firming its presence on the global 
stage .

 About Radico Khaitan  
Radico Khaitan Limited ( “Radico Khaitan” or the Company) is among the oldest and one of the largest 
manufacturers of IMFL in India. Earlier known as Rampur Distillery Company,  Radico Khaitan 
commenced its operations in 1943 and over the years emerged as a major bulk spirits supplier and 
bottler to other spirit manufacturers. In 1998, the Company started its own brands with the 
introduction of 8 PM Whisky.  Radico Khaitan is one of the few companies in India to have developed 
its entire brand portfolio organi cally.  
 The Company’s brand portfolio includes Rampur Indian Single Malt Whiskies, Sangam World Malt 
Whisky, Kohinoor Reserve Indian Dark Rum, Spirit of Victory 1999 Pure Malt Whisky, Jaisalmer Indian 
Craft Gin, Royal Ranthambore Heritage Collection Royal Crafted  Whisky, Morpheus Rare Luxury 
Whisky Morpheus and Morpheus Blue Brandy, The Spirit of Kashmyr, Magic Moments Vodka, Magic 
Moments Remix Pink Vodka, Magic Moments Verve Vodka, Magic Moments Dazzle Vodka (Gold & 
Silver), 1965 The Spirit of Victory Premium XX X Rum and Lemon Dash Premium Flavored Rum, After 
Dark Whisky, 8PM Premium Black Whisky, 8PM Whisky, Contessa Rum and Old Admiral Brandy.  
Radico Khaitan is also one of the largest providers of branded IMFL to the Canteen Stores Department 
(CSD), which has significant business barriers to entry. The Company has distilleries situated in 
Rampur, Sitapur and Aurangabad, Maharashtra which is a 36% joint  venture. The Company has a total 
owned capacity of 321 million litres and operates 44 bottling units (5 owned, 39 contract and royalty 
bottling units). It is also one of the largest exporters of Alcoholic beverages from India, with brands 
available  in over 100 countries .
