Performance Highlights:  
• Assets under Management  (AuM) of the company registered a growth of 1 6.1% to reach Rs. 248 bn as 
on 3 0th Sep  26.  
• During H1FY27 , we disbursed loans worth to Rs. 35.1 bn , delivering robust growth of 30% YoY , while 
maintaining our strong focus on quality origination and prudent underwriting. In Q2FY27 , disbursements  
grew by  21% YoY at Rs. 18.9 bn . 
• Our Net profit  for H1FY2 7 grew by 16.4% YoY to Rs 3.5 bn led by robust 17% YoY growth in NI I and 
18% YoY growth in Operating Profit .  
• During H1FY27, Net Total Income  (NIM) grew by 14% YoY in absolute terms . NIM  as a percentage of 
average total assets remained broadly stable at 7.76% , moderating marginally by 5 bps YoY.  Whereas in 
Q2FY27  NIM improved by 11 bps QoQ to 7.81% . 
• Despite the volatile interest rate environment, borrowing costs remained stable at 7.64% sequentially.  
We strengthened and diversified our funding franchise  with the addition of two new large 
relationships .  
• Our cost -to-income ratio improved by 195 bps YoY in H1FY27 to 4 2.9%, driven by better cost 
efficiency. As a result, our operating expense -to-assets ratio improved by 18 bps YoY to 3.3 3%.  
• Our asset quality remains pristine , with our 1+ DPD improv ed by 18 bps YoY to 3.76% in Q 2 FY27, 
remaining comfortably below 5%. Gross NPA  improved by 1 3 bps YoY to 1.11%, while Net NPA  
improved by 1 6 bps YoY to 0.7 0% 
• Supported by our disciplined underwriting and strong collection framework, credit costs for Q 2 FY27 
remained stable at 24 bps , remaining within our guided range . 
 
 
 
• Our Net Worth  continues to compound steadily, growing at 15.4% YoY , with the strength of our capital 
position driven by consistently  compounding internal accruals.  
• We continued to deliver a steady improvement in profitability during H1FY27 , with RoA expanding by 4 
bps YoY  and RoE improving by 5 bps YoY . The momentum strengthened further in Q2FY27 , as RoA 
improved by 9 bps QoQ  to 3.28%, while RoE increased by 28 bps QoQ to 13.6 3%. 
 
Commenting on the performance,  Mr. Manu Singh , Chief Executive Officer, said :  
“Dear All,   
 
It gives me immense pleasure and pride to share that Aavas will  cross Rs. 250 bn in AUM  this month , 
having served nearly  4.5 lakh customers . As we embark on the next phase of our journey, the 
significant opportunity in affordable housing, the depth of the underserved customer segment and our 
expanding distribution network give s us strong confidence in the long -term potential of the Aavas 
franchise and provide a credible path toward our next milestone of Rs. 500 bn in AUM . As we pursue 
this ambition, the quality of growth  will remain as important as its pace , with sustainable 
profitability , prudent underwriting, strong governance  and disciplined liability management at the 
core  of our strategy. Technology  will serve as a critical force multiplier  in this journey, enabling us to 
scale with  greater speed, efficiency  and control, while building a more productive , resilient and 
future -ready Aavas that delivers lasting value to all our stakeholders.  
 
During H1FY27 , we disbursed Rs. 35.1 bn , achieving robust growth of 30% YoY , primarily driven by 
higher volumes. The momentum continued in Q2FY27 , with disbursements close to Rs. 19 bn, 
growing by 21% YoY , supported by improving productivity  and healthy demand across our markets.  
 
Our AUM grew by 16.1% YoY to Rs. 248 bn , while the repayment rate normali zed to around 17% 
and will remain in similar range . These trends reinforce our confidence in accelerating our growth  
trajectory and delivering more than 20%  growth over the long term.  
 
During the quarter, we expanded our branch network to 44 3 across 15 states . We will continue to 
invest in branch expansion to further strengthen and diversify our presence.  In H1FY27, we added 
21,500+ new customers  to the Aavas’ franchise.  
 
Our diversified liability franchise enabled us to maintain the cost of borrowing at 7.64% and spread 
above 5%,  despite the volatile interest -rate environment. While the recent 25 -bps repo -rate increase, 
we expect appropriate repricing of incremental lending  should progressively support disbursement 
and AUM yields . During the quarter, NIM expanded by 11 bps QoQ to 7.81% , supported by stable 
borrowing costs and disciplined risk -adjusted pricing, giving us confidence in sustaining healthy 
spreads and margins .  
 
We delivered 16.4% YoY growth  in Net profit for H1FY27 , driven by a healthy 17% YoY  growth  in 
Net Interest Income  and 18% YoY growth in Operating Profit , supported by robust business growth. 
In addition, improved operating efficiencies, reflected in a 153 -bps QoQ improvement in our cost -
to-income ratio , further strengthened profitability during the quarter.  
 
We continue to deliver industry -leading asset quality , with all key indicators trending positively and 
remaining well within our guided range. Our 1+ DPD improved by 18 bps YoY to 3. 81% in Q 2FY27, 
remaining comfortably below 5%. Gross NPA  improved to 1.11%, while Net NPA  to 0.7 0%, 
underscoring the resilience of our portfolio and the effectiveness of our credit -first approach.  
 
We expect the business momentum and strong performance to continue, as we aim to reach 20% 
AuM growth, while embedding greater execution discipline  and rigor  at every layer  of the business, 
underpinned by a firm  commitment to strong governance . Our collective ambition is anchored in a 
simple philosophy: People. Performance. Perseverance.  
 
 
 
 
  
  
  
  
 
  
About Aavas Financiers  Limited  
Aavas Financiers Limited, incorporated in 2011 in Jaipur is a retail, affordable housing finance company, 
primarily serving low - and middle -income  self -employed customers in semi -urban and rural areas in India. A 
majority of our customers have limited access to formal banking credit. The Company’s product offering 
consists of home loans for the purchase or construction of residential properties, and for the extension and 
repair of existing housing units , Loan against property and MSME loans . The Company  has in -house execution  
model leading to superior business outcomes.
