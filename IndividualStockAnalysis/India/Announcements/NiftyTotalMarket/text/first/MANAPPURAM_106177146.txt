5. Acquisition of Manappuram Asset Finance Limited

2025 (“ MAFL SPA ”) inter alia  amongst the Company, V.P . Nandakumar and Manappuram 
Asset Finance Limited (“ MAFL ”) for acquisition of 99.917% (ninety nine point nine one
