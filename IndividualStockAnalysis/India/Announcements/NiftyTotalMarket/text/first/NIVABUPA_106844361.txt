2  Background & Purpose 
 
Part – A 
 
Regulation 8(1) read with Schedule A of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations 2015 (“SEBI PIT Regulations”) requires the 
Company to formulate a Code of practices and procedures for fair disclosure of unpublished 
price sensitive information (“Code”), forms as Part A of this document.  
 
This Code sets out the principles and practices the Company shall follow to ensure uniform, 
transparent, and timely disclosure of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (“ UPSI”) to 
prevent selective disclosure. The Code incorporates the principles laid down in Schedule A 
of the SEBI PIT Regulations and reinforces the Company’s commitment to transparent and 
fair disclosure practices. 
 
The Company shall maintain adequate and effective internal controls and institutional 
mechanisms to ensure compliance with SEBI PIT Regulations, including identification of 
UPSI, restriction of access on a need-to-know basis, and periodic review of compliance 
controls by the Compliance Officer. 
The internal controls shall include the controls specified under Regulation 9A(2), including 
identification of employees having access to UPSI as Designated Persons, identification and 
protection of UPSI, restrictions on its communication or procurement, maintenance of lists 
of persons with whom UPSI is shared, service of confidentiality notices or execution of 
confidentiality agreements, and periodic review of the effectiveness of such controls. 
 
Part -B 
 
Regulation 9A (5) of the SEBI PIT Regulations requires Board of Directors of a listed company 
to formulate written Policy and procedures (“Policy”), for inquiry in case of leak or 
suspected leak of unpublished price sensitive information and to initiate appropriate 
inquiries upon becoming aware of such leak or suspected leak  
 
Part B of this document has been formulated in accordance with the said requirement. 
 
The purpose of Part B is to lay down a structured mechanism for investigating, inquiring 
into, and taking appropriate disciplinary action against any actual or suspected leak of UPSI, 
thereby safeguarding confidentiality and ensuring regulatory compliance. 
 
Definitions and Interpretations 
 
Words and expressions used but not defined in this Code and Policy shall have the same 
meaning assigned to them in the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, the 
Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992, the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 
1956, the Depositories Act, 1996 or the Companies Act, 2013 and the rules and regulations 
made thereunder, as the case may be or in any amendment thereto. 
 
Inquiry Committee  
 
There shall be a Committee called the Inquiry Committee ("Committee") comprising of: 
- Chief Financial Officer; 
- Chief Compliance Officer  
3  - Chief Risk Officer;  
- Chief Human Resource Officer; or 
- Any other person nominated by Managing Director & CEO. 
A member who is the subject of the inquiry, is connected with the matter, has reported the 
matter, or may reasonably be perceived to have a conflict of interest shall recuse from the 
inquiry. The Inquiry Committee may co-opt an alternate member or direct that the inquiry 
be conducted by an independent external professional. 
 
The quorum shall be three members. Decisions shall be taken by majority and the reasons 
for material decisions shall be recorded. 
 
The Company Secretary & Compliance Officer of the Company shall act as Secretary to the 
Committee.  
 
The Committee shall also decide the suitable actions under Clause 12 of Code of Conduct 
to regulate, monitor and report trading by its Designated Persons and immediate relatives 
of designated persons. This Committee shall report the matter of breach or violation under 
this Code to the Audit Committee/ Board, in accordance with this Policy. Further, such 
Committee will also concern itself with any leak / suspected leak of unpublished price 
sensitive information and inquiries as laid out in the Policy on procedures to be conducted 
while conducting an inquiry in the event of leak or suspected leak of unpublished price 
sensitive information. 
  
4   
 
Part A - Code of Practices and Procedures for Fair Disclosure of Unpublished Price 
Sensitive Information (“Code”) 
 
Designation and Role of Chief Investor Relations Officer (“CIRO”) 
 
The Company Secretary & Compliance Officer of the Company shall act as the Chief Investor 
Relations Officer (“CIRO”) for the purpose of this Code or in absence of CIRO, such other 
Official as may be nominated by the Board. The CIRO may be contacted by e-mail at 
investor@nivabupa.com .   
 
The CIRO shall be responsible for ensuring dissemination of information and disclosure of 
UPSI at appropriate times, so as to avoid selective disclosure.  
 
Disclosures under the Code 
 
1. The CIRO shall ensure:  
 prompt public disclosure of UPSI that would impact price discovery no sooner than 
credible and concrete information comes into being in order to make such 
information generally available. 
 uniform and universal dissemination of UPSI to avoid selective disclosure; and  
 prompt dissemination of UPSI that gets disclosed inadvertently or selectively or 
otherwise to ensure that the information is made generally available. 
2. It is clarified that information to be termed UPSI should be specific and intended to be 
generally made available at a point of time to ensure it does not lead to creation of a 
false market in securities. For the purpose of disclosure, the CIRO may consult such 
officials within the Company to ensure the correctness and credibility of UPSI. 
3. The CIRO shall disclose or disseminate UPSI (i) by way of intimation to the stock 
exchanges; (ii) on the official website to ensure official confirmation and documentation; 
and (iii) in any other manner as may be decided by the Chief Investor Relations Officer 
to facilitate uniform and universal dissemination of UPSI.  
4. All communications of UPSI with the stock exchange shall be approved by the Chief 
Investor Relations Officer and communicated through appropriate personnel under 
his/her direction. 
5. The Chief Investor Relations Officer shall also be responsible for overseeing the contents 
of UPSI to be posted on the website of the Company for the purposes of this Code and 
shall give appropriate directions for the publication of the same. No other person shall 
be authorised to post any UPSI in the absence of any directions from the Chief Investor 
Relations Officer. 
6. To facilitate timely disclosure of UPSI, designated persons as identified by the Company 
will be required to communicate any UPSI to the Chief Investor Relations Officer as soon 
as credible and concrete information comes into being. 
 
Information to be Shared on a Need-to-Know Basis 
 
All employees, directors, Insiders, and other persons who is bestowed with the information 
or UPSI shall be required to ensure that all information or UPSI is handled within the 
Company on a need-to-know basis and no Unpublished Price Sensitive Information shall be 
communicated to any person except in furtherance of Legitimate Purpose, performance of 
duties or discharge of legal obligations. 
 
5  Information Shared with Specific Persons 
 
1. CIRO shall ensure that any information shared with analysts, investors, research 
personnel and other industry professionals is not UPSI and is generally available.  
2. The only persons authorized to speak on behalf of the Company with analysts, research 
personnel and other industry professionals are the Managing Director & Chief Executive 
Officer, Executive Directors, Chief Financial Officer, CIRO, employees forming part of 
Investor Relations team and any other employees specifically authorised by the 
Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer / CIRO. 
3. Any disclosures made to analysts, research personnel and other industry professionals 
must be complete and specific. Selective disclosures are strictly prohibited. 
4. Inquiries from analysts, research personnel and other industry professionals must be 
handled only by any of the above specified officials. Under no circumstances an attempt 
shall be made by any person other than specified employee to handle these queries 
without prior authorization from the Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer / CIRO. 
All press releases to be issued must be reviewed by the CIRO before being shared with 
the media. In case the CIRO determines that the press release requires disclosure on the 
Stock Exchanges, such press release must be shared with media only after disclosure on 
the Stock Exchanges. For follow-up inquiries on press releases, the CIRO may designate 
any person within the Corporate Communications team to respond to such inquiries. 
5. The CIRO shall ensure that transcripts or recordings of proceedings of post earning or 
quarterly call(s)s with analysts and other investors are made publicly available in 
accordance with applicable law, including the SEBI LODR Regulations. 
 
Sharing of UPSI   
 
1. UPSI may be shared with any person only in furtherance of a ‘legitimate purpose’, 
‘performance of duties’ or ‘discharge of legal obligations’ in accordance with the SEBI 
PIT Regulations and execution of confidentiality agreement with the person to whom 
such information is being shared.  
2. UPSI shall be shared only after satisfaction that (i) sharing is necessary, (ii) recipient is 
subject to confidentiality obligations, and (iii) corresponding entries have been made 
in the Structured Digital Database. 
3. UPSI may be shared only after the recipient is made subject to appropriate 
confidentiality obligations through a confidentiality agreement or service of a 
confidentiality notice, as applicable. 
4. UPSI in relation to the Company and its Securities may be communicated, provided, 
allowed access to or procured, in connection with a transaction in compliance with 
Regulation 3(3) of the Regulations.  
5. The Compliance Officer shall ensure that a structured digital database is maintained 
containing the nature of UPSI and the names of such persons who have shared the 
information and also the names of such persons with whom information is shared along 
with the Permanent Account Number or any other identifier authorized by law where 
Permanent Account Number is not available. Irrespective of whether UPSI is shared 
internally or externally, necessary recording should be made in SDD. The SDD shall not 
be outsourced and shall be maintained internally with adequate internal controls and 
checks such as time stamping and audit trails to ensure non-tampering of the database. 
UPSI which are not emanating from within the Company may be entered in Structured 
Digital Database, not later than two (2) calendar days from the receipt of such 
information.
