Media release  
Adani’s Kutch Copper Becomes First Indian Producer to 
Receive the Prestigious ‘Copper Mark’    
• Global recognition reinforces KCL's commitment to world -class ESG standards and 
responsible production  
• The Veramark Assurance or Copper Mark framework promotes transparency, 
accountability, and continuous improvement through periodic reassessments.  
• The certification advances KCL’s goals to adhere to the UN’s Sustainable Development Goals - 12 on responsible consumption and production.  
Ahmedabad, 11 November 2026:
 Kutch Copper Ltd (KCL), a unit of Adani Enterprises Ltd and 
operator of one of the world's largest single -location copper smelters, today announced that it has 
received the prestigious Veramark Assurance, formerly Copper Mark, certification, becoming the 
first Indian producer to be recognised under the global framework for responsible copper 
production.  
Veramark Assurance , formerly Copper Mark, is a leading independent assurance framework that 
promotes responsible practices across the copper, nickel, and zinc value chains. The certification 
is awarded to facilities that comply with rigorous, independent third -party audits of their 
environmental, social, and governance (ESG) performance.  
"Achieving the Copper Mark, a first for any Indian producer, underscores our unwavering 
commitment to sustainable and responsible production. This milestone positions Kutch Copper 
among an elite group of leading global producers adhering to internationally recognised standards. 
As copper becomes increasingly vital to global electrification, renewable energy, modern mobility, and infrastructure, Kutch Copper is committed to delivering the high -quality metal India needs to 
power its future while adhering to th e highest global standards of environmental and social 
stewardship ," said Dr. Vinay Prakash, CEO, Adani Natural Resources and Managing Director, KCL.  
The certification enables producers to demonstrate alignment with leading international standards for responsible production, while actively advancing the United Nations Sustainable 
Development Goals (SDGs) —particularly SDG 12 on responsible consumption an d production.  
 
The Veramark  Assurance or Copper Mark framework has been adopted by several of the world's 
leading mining and metals companies, including BHP, Rio Tinto, Freeport -McMoRan, Antofagasta, 
Aurubis, among others. It promotes transparency, accountability, and continuous imp rovement 
through periodic reassessments, ensuring that certified facilities continue to meet evolving global 
expectations for responsible production.  
Driven by rapid expansions in renewable energy, electric mobility, power transmission, digital 
infrastructure, and advanced manufacturing, the demand for copper has accelerated. Across the 
globe, customers, investors, and supply chain partners are actively  prioritising responsible sourcing 
and seeking manufacturers adhering to stringent sustainable production benchmarks.  
In this evolving market, the Copper Mark recognition firmly establishes KCL as a trusted supplier 
of responsibly produced copper. It significantly advances the company’s objective to become the 
partner of choice for global businesses building sustainable, resilient, and future -ready supply 
chains.
