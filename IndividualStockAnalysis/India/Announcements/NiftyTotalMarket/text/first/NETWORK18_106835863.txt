EARNINGS RELEASE: Q 2 FY202 6-27 
  
 
Mumbai, 30th October , 202 6 – Network18 Media & Investments Limited today announced its 
results for the quarter  ended September  30, 2026. 
 
• Consolidated  operating revenue grew 9 .4% to Rs. 545 crores  during the quarter ; 
advertising inventory demand for the TV news industry declined by ~13% YoY  
• H1 consolidated revenue up by ~10% to Rs. 1,061 crores  
• Network18 continued to be the #1 digital news/information network in India with a 
monthly reach of 330mn+ 
• Network18 maintains a clear dominance as the biggest news network on Youtube 
with ~2x video views of the nearest competitor   
• Moneycontrol Pro continue s to be India’s #1 subscription -based financial 
intelligence platform with over 1 million paid subscribers; Other digital products – 
Firstpost , MC Super Pro, CNBCTV18, News18  continue to see strong traction  
• TV ratings continue to be suspended over the issue of BARC ’s license renewal 
under the Television Ratings Policy, 2026  
• Merger of News18 Marathi  with Network18 approved  by NCLT ; to become effective 
post regulatory approval

Viewership ratings data continues to be suspended for the television industry   
• Television rating s data has been suspended for over 6 months for the news genre and for 
more than 3 months for other genres.  Ministry of Information and Broadcasting (MIB) ha d 
directed the Broadcast Audience Research Council (BARC) to pause weekly viewership 
data for news channels in early March as West Asia crisis began . In early July, ratings of 
non-news genres were also suspended over the issue of BARC’s license renew al under the 
Television Ratings Policy, 2026 .
