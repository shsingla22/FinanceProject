EXPLANATORY STATEMENT  
 
Pursuant to Section 102 of the Companies Act, 2013 (Act), Regulation 36 of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (Listing Regulations) and the applicable 
Secretarial Standards, the following explanatory statement sets  out all the material facts relating to the 
businesses mentioned under Item No. 1 of this Postal Ballot Notice  
 
Item 1: Appointment of Mr. Biren Chandrakant Gabhawala as Independent Director of the 
Company  
 
The Nomination and Remuneration Committee (“NRC”), at its meeting held on October 29, 2026, 
considered the candidature of Mr. Biren Chandrakant Gabhawala and recommended his appointment as 
an Independent Director of the Company for a first term of five consecutive years commencing from 
November 1, 2026. Based on the recommendation of the NRC, the Board of Directors (“Board”), at its meeting held on the same day, approved his appointment, subject to approval of the Shareholders . 
 
Accordingly, pursuant to the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 (“Act”) and the SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), approval 
of the Shareholders is being sought by way of a Special Resolution through Postal Ballot.  
 
The relevant particulars regarding Mr. Biren Gabhawala, together with the rationale and 
recommendations of the NRC and the Board, are set out below for the consideration of the Members.  
 
About Biren  
 Mr. Biren Gabhawala, aged about 60 years, is a Chartered Accountant and holds a Bachelor of Commerce degree. He has over three decades of professional experience spanning accounting, audit, taxation, 
financial reporting, corporate finance, mergers and acqu isitions, FEMA, business consulting, 
international taxation, valuations, forensic accounting and insolvency- related matters.  
 
Biren has been associated with M/s. C M Gabhawala & Co., a Mumbai -based firm of Chartered 
Accountants, since 1987 and currently serves as its Senior Partner.  In his professional capacity, he has 
advised organisations across diverse industries and stages of business development on matters relating to 
financial reporting, taxation, audit, cross -border transactions, deal structuring, corporate finance, 
valuations and regulatory matters.  
 Biren also brings extensive listed -company Board and Committee experience. He served as an 
Independent Director of eClerx Services Limited from May 2011 to March 2024 and of 3M India Limited 
from August 2014 to August 2024. During his tenure with both listed entities, he chaired their respective 
Audit Committees and also served on various Board Committees, including the Stakeholders’ 
Relationship, Corporate Social Responsibility and Nomination and Remuneration Committees.
