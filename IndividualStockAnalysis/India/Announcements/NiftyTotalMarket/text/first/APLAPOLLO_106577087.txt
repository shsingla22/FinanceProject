Noida , April 1, 202 6: APL Apollo Tubes Limited (APL Apollo), the World's largest branded structural 
steel tube company, today announced its all-time high sales volume for Q 4FY26. The Company 
registered the sales volume of 924,881 Ton in Q 4FY26 compared to  850,447  Ton in Q 4FY25. For 
FY26 , the company reported sales volume of 3,491,243  Ton, an increase of 11% YoY .

an array of industry applications such as urban inf rastructure and real estate, rural housing, commercial construction, greenhouse structures and
