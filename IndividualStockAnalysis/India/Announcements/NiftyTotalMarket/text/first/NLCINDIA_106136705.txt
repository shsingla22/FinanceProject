"NLCIL bags its Third Commercial Coal Mine - New Patrapara South at 
Angul , Odisha - Vesting Order Issued by GoI"  
 
NLC India Limited, has emerged as Successful bidder for New Patrapara South Coal Mine 
in Angul District of Odisha subsequent to the commercial coal block E -auction held on 
21.11.2024 by Ministry of Coal. Coal Mine D evelopment and Production Agreement 
(CMDPA) was signed by NLCIL with Nominated Authority/ MoC on 05.12.2024.  
 
Subsequently, Vesting Order issued by Additional Secretary/ Coal (Nominated Authority), 
GoI, to NLC India Limited in the Ceremony conducted on 04. 02.2025 at Shastri Bhavan, 
MoC, New Delhi.  
 
The Coal Mine Block is having a total geological reserve of 720.87 Million Tonnes 
(Approximately) with a peak rated capacity of 12 Million Tonnes Per Annum.  
 
This is the third commercial coal mine for NLCIL and t he Company has crossed              
100 Million Tonnes of Mining Capacity commitment in line with its Corporate Plan 2030.
