Pursuant to the provisions of SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform you that in the Meeting of the Board of Directors 
of the Company held on June 23, 2025, which commenced at 06:30 p.m. and 
concluded at 8:15 p.m., the Board of Directors considered and approved inter-alia, the 
following matters: 
1.Annual General Meeting
The Board approved the convening of the 34th Annual General Meeting (the
"AGM") of the Company on Wednesday, August 13, 2025, through Video
Conference/ Other Audio-Visual Means.
2.Intimation of Record Date for payment of Dividend
Pursuant to Regulation 42 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the Company has 
fixed Wednesday, August 06, 2025, as the Record Date for determining 
entitlement of Members to Dividend for the Financial Year ended March 31, 2025. 
The aforesaid Dividend, if approved by the Shareholders at the forthcoming 34th
Annual General Meeting of the Company, will be paid on or after Wednesday, 
August 20, 2025, to the Members whose names appear on the Register of 
Members and to the Beneficial Owners of the Shares as on the Record Date, as 
per the details furnished by the Depositories for this purpose.

has approved the appointment of Mis. Makarand M. Joshi & Co., Practising
