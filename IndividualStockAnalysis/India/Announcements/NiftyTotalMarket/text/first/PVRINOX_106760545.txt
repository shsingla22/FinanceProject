PVR INOX EXPANDS BENGALURU FOOTPRINT WITH NEW SIX -SCREEN CINEMA 
IN WHITEFIELD  
 
~ Strengthening its presence in the city with a premium entertainment destination at Brigade 
Cornerstone Utopia  ~ 
National , 28 August 2026:  PVR INOX Limited has opened a new six -screen cinema with a seating capacity 
of 949 at Brigade Cornerstone Utopia on Varthur Road, near Whitefield, marking its 28th cinema in Bengaluru.  
The new property brings the company ’s contemporary cinema experience to  a fast -evolving part of the city, 
with a strong focus on convenience, comfort and entertainment.  
Located within Arcadia at Brigade Cornerstone Utopia, the cinema is part of an integrated residential and 
lifestyle development, serving both the township and  the wider Whitefield– Varthur Road catchment. With 
residential communities, retail and leisure destinations increasingly shaping the corridor, the property is positioned as a natural entertainment anchor for the surrounding community. It brings together fi lms, food 
and social experiences in one setting, offering audiences a convenient destination to spend time before, 
during and after a movie.  
Opening with Toxic: A Fairy Tale for Grown- Ups, starring Yash, the cinema gives audiences in Whitefield the 
opportunity to experience one of the year ’s most anticipated films in a new, premium setting.  
Across its six screens, the cinema features all -laser projection, Dolby 7.1 surround sound and Next -Gen 3D. 
Two auditoriums are equipped with D TSX immersive sound, creating a more expansive sound experience 
for major releases and premium content.  
The auditorium experience is complemented by Celebrity Recliners and Lounger Seats, offering audiences 
greater choice and elevated comfort. Contemporary interiors, refined d etailing and a hospitality -led design 
approach create a polished environment that begins at arrival and extends beyond the screening.  
The cinema also introduces PVR INOX ’s new Cinema Food Court concept, bringing food, beverage and 
hospitality into a more c entral part of the moviegoing experience. Designed for audiences to meet, dine, relax 
and spend time together, the concept adds a more social and experiential dimension to a visit to the cinema.  
Mr. Ajay Bijli, Managing Director, PVR INOX Limited , said, “Bengaluru has always been an important 
market for PVR INOX, and its continued evolution makes it one of the most exciting cities for entertainment 
and lifestyle experiences. The Whitefield region, in particular, reflects the new energy of the city , where 
ambitious residential communities, workplaces, retail and leisure are coming together.  Our 28th cinema in 
Bengaluru strengthens our presence in this important growth corridor while bringing audiences a high- quality 
moviegoing experience closer to home. We re main focused on creating destinations that are easy to access, 
relevant to their communities and distinctive in the experience they offer. ” 
Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli, Executive Director, PVR INOX Limited,  added , “A cinema is more than a place 
to watch a film . It is a destination where people come together, discover new stories and create shared 
memories. At Brigade Cornerstone Utopia, we have designed a property that reflects this broader vision of entertainment.  From advanced projection and sound to premium seating and our new Cinema Food Court 
concept, every element has been considered to make the experience more seamless, comfortable and 
engaging. We look forward to making this cinema a natural part of life in the Varthur Road –Whitefield corridor. ” 
 
 
 
 
The launch  reflects PVR INOX ’s continued focus on high- growth urban markets and carefully selected 
locations. As audiences seek more complete and engaging leisure experiences, the company continues to 
evolve the cinema environment through a considered mix of t echnology, design, comfort, food and hospitality .

About PVR INOX Limited  
 
PVR INOX Limited is India’ s largest film exhibition company, operating 1,786  screens across 35 6 properties 
in 113 cities in India and Sri Lanka. Formed through the merger, in 2023, of two of the country ’s most 
established and widely recognized cinema brands, PVR Limited and INOX Leisure Limited, the company 
represents the convergence of scale, operational excellence, and long- term creative vision. Operating as a 
unified platform, PVR INOX brings together deep industry expertise and a strong consumer -centric 
approach to deliver world- class cinema experiences.  
PVR INOX continues to invest in continuous innovation, next -generation formats, and immersive 
experiences, enabling the seamless introduction of global content and storytelling to audiences across the region. Guided by international benchmarks and evolving audience preferences, the company remains 
committed to shaping the future of out -of-home entertainment in South Asia. Its portfolio spans thoughtfully 
designed child- friendly auditoriums, state- of-the-art projection and immersive sound technologies, a diverse 
and curated food and beverage offering, film and non- film programming, and an expansive suite of premium 
large- format screens.
