Proceeding of 83rd Annual General Meeting held on 27th June. 2025 
83rd Annual General Meeting of the Members of the Company was held through Video 
Conference ("VC") I Other Audio Visual Means ("OAVM") on Friday, the 2th June, 2025 at 4.00 
p.m. and concluded at about 5.18 p.m. 
-Shri R.J. Saboo, VP (Corporate Affairs) & Company Secretary welcomed all the 
Shareholders and Directors. 
-The Annual General Meeting was attended by all the Directors, except Shri B.L. Taparia 
and Shri Pulak Prasad. 
-Directors present selected Shri Sarthak Behuria as Chairman of 83rd Annual General 
Meeting. Shri Sarthak Behuria, Chairman of 83rd Annual General Meeting occupied 
chair. 
-Shri Sarthak Behuria welcomed all the shareholders. He informed that the AGM was 
held through Video Conferencing I Other Audio-Visual means in compliance with the 
Circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs and Securities and Exchange Board 
of India. He informed that meeting was in order as more than 30 members are present. 
He requested the Directors to introduce themselves one by one. He further informed that 
apart from Directors, Shri P C Somani, CFO, also participated in the meeting. 
-Shri Sarthak Behuria, Chairman also informed the shareholders that since, this AGM is 
being held through VC, the facility for appointment of proxies by the Members was not 
available for this AGM. 
-Shri Sarthak Behuria informed that representatives of M/s Lodha & Company, the 
Statutory Auditors, and representatives of M/s Kishore Bhatia & Associates, the Cost 
Accountants and Mr. V. Laxman, Proprietor of M/s V Laxman & Company, the 
Secretarial Auditor of the Company also participated in the meeting. He further informed 
that the Register of Directors and Key Managerial Personnel and their shareholding 
maintained under section 170 of Companies Act, 2013, the Register of Contracts or 
arrangements in which directors are interested maintained under section 189 of the 
Companies Act, 2013 were made available for inspection in electronic mode to every 
member who had made specific request for inspection by sending an email to the 
Company. 
-Shri Sarthak Behuria sought the permission of the shareholders to take the Notice dated 
24th April, 2025, as being read. He informed the shareholders that the Auditor's Report 
did not have any qualification, observation or comments on the financial transactions of 
the Company. He sought the permission of the shareholders to take the Auditor's Report 
as being read. He further informed the shareholders that the Secretarial Audit report also 
does not contain any qualifications, observations or comments on financial transactions 
or matters, which have adverse effect on the functioning of the Company, hence the 
same need not be read at this Annual General Meeting.
