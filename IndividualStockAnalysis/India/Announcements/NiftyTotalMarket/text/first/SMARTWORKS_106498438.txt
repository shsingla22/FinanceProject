Smartworks Enters Compounding Phase in Q3 FY26 with Record EBITDA and Strong 
Cash Conversion  
Record normalised EBITDA  with ~150 bps margin expansion to ~18% ; operating cash flow exceeds 
EBITDA; ROCE expands to ~21%; net -debt negative balance sheet reinforces self -funded growth  
Gurugram, 15 January 2026 : Smartworks Coworking Spaces Limited (“Smartworks” or “the 
Company”), India’s largest managed office platform by area under management, reported its strongest 
quarterly performance in Q3 FY26, marking a structural inflection  in the Company’s growth trajectory.  
Performance Overview  
Q3 FY26 marked a decisive milestone for Smartworks, with growth, profitability, cash flows, and returns 
on capital improving simultaneously . The quarter reflects rising portfolio maturity, a higher share of large -
format enterprise contracts, and operating leverage inherent in Smartworks’ managed campus model.  
Record normalised EBITDA during the quarter was supported by an expanding base of mature centres, 
while operating cash flow exceeded EBITDA, reinforcing the Company’s cash -compounding and self -
funded growth model . The performance underscores Smartworks’ evolution into an enterprise 
workspace infrastructure platform , rather than a cyclical or transactional flex operator . 
Q3 FY26 – Key Highlights  
• Revenue of ~₹472 crore, up 34% YoY and 11% QoQ  
• Normalised EBITDA at an all -time high of ~₹85 crore; margin expanded to ~18% 
• Normalised operating cash flow of ~₹101 crore; OCF -to-EBITDA at ~ 1.2 
• Annualised ROCE  improved to ~ 21%, continuing its upward trajectory  
• Net-debt negative balance sheet sustained at ~₹42 crore  
• Committed rental revenue exceeded ₹ 4,700 crore, providing strong forward visibility  
• PAT profitability achieved under Ind AS  
• Committed occupancy for mature centres  at ~93% 
Together, these metrics reflect improving earnings durability, capital efficiency, and downside resilience.  
Q3 FY26 represents Smartworks’ transition into a compounding phase, where scale increasingly 
translates into margin expansion, stronger cash conversion, and rising returns on capital. With a 
growing share of the portfolio entering maturity, secured supply visibility through FY27 (with sourcing 
underway for FY28), and enterprise -led, long -tenure demand, Smartworks’ growth is now increasingly 
visible, and capital -efficient.  
Operational Performance  
• Monthly revenue run -rate (MRR) reached ~₹150 crore  
• Total footprint expanded to ~ 15.3 million sq. ft.  
• 1.7 million sq. ft. LOIs  added during Q3 FY26  
• Presence across 15 cities, serving ~770+  clients  
• Committed occupied seats crossed ~1,92,000 
  
 
 
   
 
Demand and Supply Developments  
Demand momentum during Q3 FY26 was driven by execution on large -format supply additions and 
deepening enterprise relationships, rather than short -term or transactional occupancy.  During the 
quarter, Smartworks:  
• LOI’s signed for  new large campuses across core growth markets, strengthening supply 
visibility and scale  
• Closed multiple marquee enterprise deals, including large deployments from BFSI, consulting, 
technology, and global services clients  
• Continued to see expansions from existing enterprise clients, reflecting repeatability and 
embedded relationships  
Multi -city enterprise clients remained a key growth driver, contributing approximately ~30% of rental 
revenue, as enterprises increasingly consolidate their real estate requirements with Smartworks across 
locations. This trend improves revenue visibility, reduces concentration risk, and reinforces Smartworks’ 
positioning as a pan -India infrastructure partner rather than a city -specific workspace provider.  
Large -format contracts typically carry longer lock -ins and tenures, improving revenue predictability and 
reducing volatility. The 1,000+ seat cohort now contributes ~3 5% of rental  revenue, reinforcing the 
annuity -like nature of the Company’s income profile and reducing dependence on short -tenure, small -
ticket demand.  
Management Commentary  
Commenting on the performance, Neetish Sarda, Founder & Managing Director, Smartworks,  said:  
“Q3 FY26 represents Smartworks’ strongest quarter to date and confirms that the business has entered 
a compounding phase. We delivered record normalised EBITDA alongside strong revenue growth, 
driven by rising portfolio maturity and sustained enterprise dem and. 
Growth during the quarter was anchored in large, long -tenure enterprise contracts and continued 
expansions from existing clients, improving the quality, predictability, and durability of our revenues.  
As operating leverage play s out across a larger base of mature centres, profitability, cash generation, 
and returns on capital will continue to improve. With operating cash flow exceeding EBITDA and a net -
debt-negative balance sheet, we are scaling through a disciplined, self -funded expansion model.  
With secured supply visibility and enterprise -led demand, Smartworks is well positioned to sustain 
momentum and continue improving returns over the coming quarters .” 
 
About Smartworks Coworking Spaces Ltd  
Smartworks is India’s largest managed office platform by total area under management, with a footprint 
of ~15.3 million sq. ft. across 63 centres in 15 cities in India and Singapore. The Company partners with 
developers to transform large, bare -shell assets into fully managed, enterprise -grade campuses.  
Smartworks primarily serves mid -to-large enterprises and counts 770+ clients, including Forbes 2000 
companies, GCCs, MNCs, and high -growth startups. The Company also offers SmartVantage , a GCC -
  
 
 
   
 
focused solution combining scalable campuses with a curated partner ecosystem to enable rapid and 
compliant expansion in India.
