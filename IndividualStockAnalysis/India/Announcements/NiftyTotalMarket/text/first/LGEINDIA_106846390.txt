New Delhi, November 5, 2026  - LG Electronics India Limited (LGE India), India's leading 
consumer electronics brand, today announced results for the second quarter of FY27, delivering its 
highest year-on-year growth since listing and a second consecutive quarter  of double-digit growth.

LGE India continues its growth momentum by delivering strong revenue from operations of ₹73.80 
billion in Q2 FY27, up 19.5% over ₹61.74 billion in Q2 FY26. EBITDA rose 2 8.0% to ₹7.01 
billion, with margin expanding 63 basis points to 9.5%, while profit after tax (PAT) grew 25.7% 
to ₹4.89 billion. Increased profitability  was achieved despite elevated commodity prices, rupee 
depreciation and go-to-market investments to capture the upcoming festive season demand.  
 
Looking at H1FY27, revenue grew 17.5% to ₹146.13 billion , PAT rose 26.5% to ₹11.42 billion , 
EBITDA grew 27.9% to ₹16.16 billion with EBITDA margin of 11.1% (up 0.9% YoY).  
 
 
 
 
 
  
   
 
What distinguished the quarter was the quality of the growth. It was balanced across volume and 
value, and across both mass and premium segments. Both businesses contributed strongly - Home 
Appliances & Air Solution recorded its highest growth since listin g, while Home Entertainment 
extended its run of double -digit growth to a third consecutive quarter on a high base. Television 
value market share reached a record high, and a fast -scaling export business complemented healthy 
domestic demand, reflecting grow th that is broad-based and built to last.  
 
LGE India also announced its entry into a new B2B segment – Vehicle Solutions, In-Vehicle 
Infotainment (IVI). This initiative will bring LG Korea's key global B2B automotive business, 
including automotive infotainment and telematics solutions, to India. These products will be 
manufactured and supplied from LGE India’s 3rd plant at Sri City, Andhra Pradesh.  
 
Highlighting the results, Mr. Hong Ju Jeon, Managing Director, LG E India Limited, said, “We 
are proud to announce our second consecutive strong quarter results, culminating in a robust 
H1FY27. This performance reflects the broader strength of India’s consumer durables industry, 
which continues to benefit from rising disposable incomes, growing  aspiration for premium 
products and deepening penetration across metro and emerging markets. As LGE India completes 
its first year as a listed company, this performan ce reflects the trust our shareholders have placed 
in us and our commitment to creating long -term value for them.  Moving beyond consumer 
electronics, we are expanding our footprint in the rapidly evolving B2B sector, by entering a new 
B2B vertical, Vehicle Solutions . Our foundational three-pillar strategy—Make-in-India, Make-for-
India, and Make-India-Global—will continue driving our ambitious growth trajectory across 
domestic and export markets.”  
 
Category Performance  
 
Home Appliances & Air Solution (H&A)  delivered its highest  quarterly growth since listing, 
with revenue of ₹47.72 billion, up 20.9% YoY. Strong double -digit momentum ran across major 
categories, with washing machines, refrigerators and room air conditioners leading the charge, 
while dishwashers delivered breakout growth. T he company's two-track strategy delivered growth 
across both mass and premium segments, and the expanding LG Essential Series range, together 
with the newly launched Chest Freezers  and French Door Refrigerators  further widened its reach , 
strengthening market leadership across key categories.  
 
Segment EBIT grew 28.4% YoY to ₹4.16 billion, with EBIT margin improving to 8.7% from 8.2% 
in Q2 FY26. Operating leverage on strong volume growth more than offset elevated commodity 
prices, currency impact on imports and go -to-market investments made ahead of the festive season.  
 
Home Entertainment (HE)  delivered revenue of ₹26.08 billion, up 17.2% YoY on a  high base, 
marking the segment's third consecutive quarter of double -digit growth. Televisions led the 
momentum as consumers upgraded to larger screens and offline market share scaled to an all -time 
high, widening LG's lead in the category. The B2B Information Display business delivered strong 
growth, adding further depth to the segment's growth engines.  
 
Segment EBIT rose 19.9% to ₹3.37 billion, with EBIT margin improving to 12.9% from 12.6% in 
Q2 FY26. Elevated panel costs, currency impact on imports and incremental festive go -to-market 
investments were offset by operating leverage from strong revenue gro wth and improved 
profitability in the B2B business, which has emerged as a second profit engine alongside 
televisions. 
 
 
 
 
 
  
   
 
 
 
FY27 Outlook 
• Strong festive and wedding season positioning, with go -to-market investments made in Q2 
ensuring channel readiness across key markets  
• Expanded television line -up: Mini LED TVs join the large -screen, QNED and OLED 
range, building on record -high television market share  
• Energy-efficient ACs to continue driving growth momentum  
• Premium momentum: French Door refrigerators and dishwashers expected to sustain 
growth as consumers shift towards premium, energy -efficient and larger -capacity 
appliances, while B2B continues to add resilience to margins  
• Export scale-up: India positioned as LG's preferred export hub, with over 20 new countries 
entered since the IPO  
• Sri City plant expected to commence operations in Q3 FY27 with compressor 
manufacturing, enhancing localisation and set to increase existing capacity for domestic 
and export demand 
 
Backed by a strong H1 FY27 performance, LGE India reaffirms its FY27 targets of mid -teen 
revenue growth and an early double -digit EBITDA margin. While commodity and currency trends 
remain key monitorables, these are being actively managed through localisat ion, calibrated pric-
ing and operational efficiency, and overall industry sentiment remains positive heading into the 
peak demand season.
