Corporate Snapshot 
The Varroc Advantage
We are a global automotive technology company delivering 
engineering-led mobility solutions backed by advanced manufacturing capabilities. Our portfolio spans electrical and electronic systems, lighting solutions, polymer and metallic components, precision-engineered powertrain products and intelligent mobility technologies for leading OEMs in the two-wheeler, three-wheeler, passenger vehicle, commercial vehicle and off-highway segments.
As mobility transitions towards electrified, connected and intelligent platforms, we are shaping the 
next generation of automotive innovation. By combining engineering excellence, manufacturing expertise and investments in emerging technologies, we develop safer, smarter and more sustainable mobility solutions while expanding our capabilities in EV components, advanced lighting, telematics and driver assistance systems.
