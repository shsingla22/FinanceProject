Trident Celebrates the Festive Season with Shareholders Through Exclusive Gifts and Heartfelt 
Appreciation  
Barnala , India, September 0 9, 2025: As we welcome the Festive Season - 2025 with great pleasure , Trident 
Limited extends warm festive greetings to its valued shareholders, acknowledging their unwavering trust 
and support over the years.  
In the true spirit of gratitude and togetherness, Trident is pleased to offer a Special Festive Season Gift —
an exclusive 40% discount voucher  redeemable on its premium e -commerce platform, myTrident.  This 
gesture is a token of appreciation for the enduring partnership that has helped shape Trident’s journey.  
Shareholders can enjoy this festive benefit by using the unique coupon code shared via their registered 
email addresses. The voucher unlocks access to Trident’s exquisite range of bed and bath linen, home 
decor, and lifestyle products, crafted to bring comfort and elegance into every home.  
myTrident , the flagship brand of Trident Group, is renowned for its luxurious and sustainable home 
furnishings. From everyday essentials to premium indulgences, the brand offers a thoughtfully curated 
collection including bedsheets, towels, bathrobes, rugs, and mor e. Each product reflects Trident’s 
commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.  
Trusted by leading hospitality chains across India, myTrident continues to set benchmarks in the home 
textile industry. With a seamless online shopping experience at www.mytrident.com  customers can 
explore and enjoy the comfort and craftsmanship that define the brand.  
This festive season, Trident celebrates not just tradition and joy, but also the meaningful relationships that 
fuel its success. Here's to a season filled with warmth, celebration, and shared prosperity.  
About Trident:  
Trident Limited is the flagship company of Trident Group, an Indian business conglomerate and global 
player. Headquartered in Ludhiana, Punjab, Trident Limited is a vertically integrated textile (Yarn, Bath & 
Bed Linen) Paper (Wheat Straw -based) and Chemic al manufacturer.  
Trident’s yarn, bath & bedlinen, and paper businesses have earned global recognition and are delighting 
millions of customers across India and the world. Trident is one of the largest players in home textiles in 
India.  
Supplying national, captive, and retailer -owned brands,  the organization is highly decorated with awards  
and accolades from its customers, vendors, and various government entities in recognition of advancing 
the highest standards in product quality, social responsibility, and environmental stewardship.  
The company operates in three major business segments: Textile (Yarn, Bath & Bed Linen) , Paper  (Wheat 
Straw -based) and Chemical, with its manufacturing facilities in Punjab and Madhya Pradesh.
