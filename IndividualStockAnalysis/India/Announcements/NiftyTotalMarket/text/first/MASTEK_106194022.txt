Mastek  Continues Its  Commitment to Sustainability and Social Impact through 
Philanthropy and CSR Initiatives  
Mastek Foundation proudly aligns with the UN Sust ainable  Development Goals , actively contributing to  
eight  key sustainability goals . 
Mumbai, India, 04th April  2025 : Mastek (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted digital engineering and 
cloud transformation partner, reinforces its commitment to social good and environmental 
sustainability through purpose -driven philanthropy and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives. 
Furthering human itarian impact and ecological harmony, Mastek Foundation celebrates another 
milestone —the grand success of ‘ Inspired 2025 ,’ the annual  musical fundraiser that  pledged INR 1 crore  
to support  social well- being and quality education . 
 
Mastek Foundation continues to drive impact across communities, wildlife, and the environment, 
focusing on economic inclusion, workforce equality, and climate resilience. Through its initiatives and 25 
charity partners , it has positively impacted over 38 5,516 human lives . Aligned with the UN Sustainable 
Development Goal s, the foundation contributes to Zero Hunger, Good Health and  Well- Being, Quality 
Education, and Clean Water and Sanitation . Additionally, other initiatives include  several blood donation  
drives,  and digital literacy using technology  to reduce carbon impact.  
Mastek has made significant progress in environmental conservation and community well- being, 
achieving:  
• A reduction of 3,155 KL in water consumption and 3.7 tons of e -waste  
• Plan ting over 28,405 trees  globally  
• Recognition  in the S&P Global  Sustainability Yearbook 2025  
• Support  for cancer patients  
• Expanding and deepening seven water dams, each with increa sed capacity of ~50 lakh liters  
The ‘Inspired’ musical fundraiser will support three charity partners —Open Links Foundation (Pune), 
SEWA Rural (Jhagadia, Gujarat) , and Samata Vidya Mandir . The proceeds from the event will be 
directed towards providing  quality education for underserved children, along with healthcare and 
vocational training for rural communities. Mastek’s flagship donation drives —including Gratitude is 
Attitude, Payroll Giving, Daan Utsav , and the Christmas Fundraiser —have been instrumental in 
identifying and supporting the right charity partner s, ensuring effective fund utili zation.  
In an AI -driven era, bridging the digital divide is crucial for inclusive growth. Mastek empowers and 
recogniz es the need for  essential tech skills through digital boot camps, laptop distribution, and career 
fairs. With an early career approach, it equips students and underprivileged individuals with digital 
readiness, ensuring future -ready opportunities.  
 
 
 
 
Ashank Desai, Founder & Chairman, Mastek  commented, “At Mastek, we are committed to  giving back 
to society by creating meaningful and lasting impact. Through Mastek Foundation, we are not only 
addressing critical social and environmental challenges but also bridging gaps in education, healthcare, 
and digital i nclusion. Our commitment goes  beyond philanthropy —we empower communities with 
equity and dignity, drive sustainable progress, and create opportunities that transform lives. ”
