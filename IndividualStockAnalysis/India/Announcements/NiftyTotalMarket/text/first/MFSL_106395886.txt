geared for the future. Her team of HR professionals at Axis Group is focused on nurturing distinctive career 
experiences that matter to the people and wherein they thrive. Axis Bank has been recognized externally as an 
employer of choice for its Diversity, Equity & Inclusion efforts and its Giga Opportunities program has been widely 
lauded. She is also the Co- chairperson of FICCI HR Committee. She graduated in Science from Himachal Pradesh
