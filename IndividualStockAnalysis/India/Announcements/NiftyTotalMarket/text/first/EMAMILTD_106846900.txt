Emami delivers robust Q2FY27 performance with 36% 
revenue growth; Domestic Business surges 49%  
 
• Consolidated revenues grew 36% to ₹1,088 crore; like -to-like growth of 2 4% 
• Domestic Business grew 49% with volume growth of 4 3%;  
 Like-to-like domestic growth of 3 2% with volume growth of 26%  
• Strategic Investments portfolio grew 73% on a like -to-like basis  
 Contributed ~26% of domestic business in Q2FY27  
• EBITDA grew 25% to ₹22 2 crore and PBT grew 2 9% to ₹19 4 crore  
• Completed share buyback of ₹281.24 crore  
• Board approves first interim dividend of ₹5 per share  
 
Kolkata, 5th November, 2026 :   The Board of Directors of Emami Limited met on Thursday, 
5th November 2026 to consider the unaudited financial results of the Company for the 
second quarter and half year ended 30th September 2026.  
 
Building on the momentum of Q1, the Company delivered another quarter of broad -based 
double -digit growth, driven by the strong performance of both its core brands and 
Strategic Investments. The results reflect the Company's continued progress in building a  
more diversified and future- ready consumer products portfolio. The operating 
environment was not an easy one, marked by renewed geopolitical tensions in West Asia, 
floods in Nepal, persistent inflationary pressure across a range of commodities, and 
defici ent rainfall in several parts of the country. Even so, demand conditions remained 
steady, and the Company’s diversified portfolio, sharper execution and the rapid scale- up 
of its new -age growth engines enabled it to deliver a strong set of numbers.  
 
On a consolidated basis, Revenue from Operations grew by 36% to ₹1,088 crore during 
Q2FY27. Domestic Business grew by 49%, driven by volume growth of 4 3%. On a like -to-
like basis, after considering the previous period’s numbers of Axiom Ayurveda and IncNut 
Digital, consolidated revenues grew by 2 4% and Domestic Business grew by 3 2%, with 
volume growth of 26%.  
 Skin Care grew by 31%, led by the BoroPlus and Talc portfolios. Hair & Scalp Care grew by 
30%, with strong growth in Navratna Cool Oils and 7 Oils in One. Health Care grew by 11%, 
led by the Pain Management range. The Strategic Investments portfolio grew b y 73% on 
a like -to-like basis and now contributes 26% of domestic business, reinforcing its role as 
an important growth engine and broadening the Company's presence across emerging 
consumer segments and categories.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
The quarter also witnessed a series of launches aimed at expanding the Company's consumer reach and strengthening its relevance across emerging needs and consumption 
occasions. The Company entered the baby care category with the “FromMaa” range under 
the B oroPlus brand, and introduced the Kesh King Scalp First range in modern trade and 
e-commerce. The Strategic Investments portfolio also launched several innovative, new -
age products during the quarter.  
 
The Company’s channel transformation continued at pace, reflecting its increasing 
alignment with evolving shopping behaviours and rapidly expanding digital and organised 
retail ecosystems. Organised channels contributed 37% of domestic business and Quick 
Commerce accounted for ~15%  of total digital  sales.  
 
Despite significant external disruptions across certain international markets, the 
underlying International Business remained resilient during Q2FY27. While reported 
revenues declined by 2%, the business grew 6% excluding Nepal and Iraq, supported by 
healthy momentum across key markets. The Company has implemented multiple 
mitigation measures across logistics, pricing and cost management to navigate the 
prevailing supply -chain challenges. With secondary sales trends improving and supply 
conditions gradually stabilizing, the Company expects the International Business growth 
trajectory to strengthen progressively in the coming quarters.  
 
On profitability, input costs remained elevated through the quarter; however, EBITDA 
grew by 25% to ₹222  crore and Profit Before Tax grew by 2 9% to ₹19 4 crore. Profit After 
Tax stood at ₹14 4 crore, lower by 3 %, largely due to the normalisation of the effective tax 
rate following the phase -out of fiscal benefits. For the first half of FY27, revenues stood at 
₹2,127 crore, up 25%. EBITDA was ₹449  crore, up 1 4%, and Profit Before Tax was ₹38 8 
crore, up 15%.  
 
Mr Harsha V Agarwal, Vice Chairman and Managing Director, Emami Limited said:  
 "We delivered another quarter of strong growth, with revenues increasing 36% and Domestic Business growing 49%. The strong performance of our core brands, coupled with 
the rapid scale -up of our Strategic Investments portfolio, which now contributes 
approxi mately 26% of domestic business, reinforces our strategy of building multiple 
growth engines for the future and expanding our presence across evolving consumer segments. As we continue to invest behind innovation, AI -enabled capabilities and new-
age consum er brands, we are creating a more diversified, agile and future -ready business. 
While inflationary and geopolitical headwinds persist, our robust portfolio, execution strength and productivity- led approach give us confidence of navigating these challenges 
effectively”.   
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Mr Mohan Goenka, Vice Chairman and Whole -Time Director, Emami Limited said:  
 
"Despite elevated input costs, we delivered EBITDA growth of 25% to ₹222  crore through 
disciplined execution, calibrated pricing actions and sustained productivity improvements. 
Our continued focus on efficiencies across procurement, manufacturing, supply chain and 
revenue management is helping support healthy margins while enabling investments for 
future growth. Transformation initiatives such as Project SETU and SalesCode.AI are 
enhancing planning, execution and decision- making across the sales ecosystem, 
strengthening our operational agility and scalability. Reflecting our c onfidence in the 
business and commitment to shareholder value, we successfully completed a share 
buyback of over ₹281 crore and declared an interim dividend of ₹5 per share."  
 
 
 
About Emami Ltd  
Emami Limited  (NSE: EMAMILTD, BSE: 531162), founded in 1974, is the flagship company of the Emami Group 
and one of India’s leading FMCG companies in the personal care and healthcare space. With a portfolio of over 
1000 products, Emami’s iconic brands include Navratna, BoroPlus, Zandu, Kesh King, Dermicool, Smart & 
Handsome, Mentho Plus, 7 Oils in One, Creme 21 among others.  
 The Company has consistently strengthened and diversified its portfolio through strategic acquisitions and 
investments across high -growth consumer segments. In addition to acquisitions such as Zandu, Kesh King, Creme 
21 and Dermicool, Emami has strategical ly invested in a new generation of consumer brands, including The Man 
Company, Brillare, Alofrut and IncNut, which have since become subsidiaries of the Company. Other strategic investments include The Furball Story and TruNative , reflecting Emami’s focus on identifying and nurturing 
emerging consumer businesses.  
 
Emami’s products reach over 5.4 million retail outlets in India through a network of 4000+ distributors, while its 
international presence spans 70+ countries. Known for its strong consumer connect, innovative marketing and 
distinctive brand -building capabi lities, Emami continues to leverage its deep consumer insights, brand -building 
expertise and strategic investment approach to create enduring brands and new avenues for growth in India and international markets.
