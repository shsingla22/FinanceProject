Lemon Tree Hotels launches its 2nd property in  Bihar  with  
Lemon Tree Hotel , Motihari  
 
The hotel features 32 well -appointed rooms and suites, two restaurants, 
banqueting spaces, and recreational amenities  
 
New Delhi, 1st November 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -
growing mid -scale and premium hotel chain, launched its latest managed  
property , Lemon Tree Hotel, Motihari , on October 31, 2025 . This launch marks 
another milestone in the brand’s expansion across Eastern India and further 
reinforces its presence in Bihar , where it has one operational property . 
 
Located in the historic city of Motihari in East Champaran district, the hotel offers 
guests an opportunity to experience the region’s rich heritage and culture. Motihari 
holds immense historical significance as the birthplace of author George Orwell 
and a s the starting point of Mahatma Gandhi’s Champaran Satyagraha. The city 
also serves as a gateway to Nepal and is well -connected to major towns such as 
Patna and Darbhanga, making it an ideal destination for both business and leisure 
travellers.  
 
The hotel features 50 well -appointed rooms and suites across various categories, 
designed for comfort and functionality. Of these 32 rooms, along with Citrus Café, 
the multi -cuisine restaurant and banquet spaces, including Tangerine Grand 1, 
Tangerine Gran d 2, a Boardroom, as well as a spa, a fitness center and a 
swimming pool will open in the first phase.  
 
The remaining 18 rooms, along with Kebab Theater, a modern Indian speciality  
restaurant serving authentic kebabs, tikkas, and regional delicacies as well as the 
remaining banquet, Tangerine, will open subsequently in the second phase.  
 
Commenting on the opening , Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, Lemon 
Tree Hotels , said, “ Motihari stands as a city of deep historical and cultural 
relevance, marking an important chapter in India’s independence movement. With 
the opening of Lemon Tree Hotel, Motihari, we are pleased to extend our signature 
comfort and warmth to travellers visi ting this iconic destination, blending modern 
hospitality with the city’s timeless charm .”
