Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and in furtherance to our letters dated February 
08, 2025 and March 06, 2025 in this regards, we wish to inform you about an order 
dated 16th July 2025 passed by the Hon’ble Bombay High Court as per the details 
mentioned in Annexure 1. 
 
We would like to further inform that the Company (TI) has successfully established 
its proprietary rights in the trademarks MANSION HOUSE & SAVOY CLUB and the 
Company’s rights to continue the usage of the concerned trademarks as had been 
upheld in the order passed on 22nd December 2011 by the Single Bench of Bombay 
High Court has now been upheld by the Division Bench of Bombay High Court, which 
has deemed it fit to dismiss the appeal filed by UTO Nederland B V (UTO)/ Herman 
Jansen B V, against the said order.  
 
Further, the concerned Division Bench has also allowed the appeals bearing no.  
6617/2025 & 6622/2025 filed by the Company, while setting aside the order dated 
7th February 2025, which had rejected the interim application that was filed by the 
Company to restrain UTO (Herman Jansen B V) and Allied Blenders and Distillers 
Ltd. (ABD), from launching the brand MANSION HOUSE & SAVOY CLUB in the 
markets in India. The Division Bench has deemed it fit to also order that until the 
final order in the suit, UTO (Herman Jansen B V) and Allied Blenders and Distillers 
Ltd. will not launch the brands MANSION HOUSE & SAVOY CLUB in India. 
 
  
 
 
 
 
The above disclosure is available on the website of the Company www.tilind.com . 
 
We request you to kindly take the above on your record.

3. Brief Description of 
Litigation Vide a composite order dated 16th July 2025, in the 
above-mentioned appeals, the Division Bench of 
Bombay High Court has: 
 
Dismissed the Appeal no. 66/2012  filed by UTO 
Nederland BV., challenging the order dated 22nd 
December, 2011, passed by the Single Bench of 
Bombay High Court, dismissing the Notice of 
Motion bearing no. 993 of 2009 in suit no. 632 of 
2009, filed by UTO Nederland B V to restrain the 
Company from using the brands MANSION HOUSE 
& SAVOY CLUB, and as a result has upheld the 
order dated 22nd November, 2011 passed by the 
Single Bench of Bombay High Court and in turn 
 
 
 
 
has upheld the rights and ownership of the 
Company in the trademarks MANSION HOUSE & 
SAVOY CLUB while allowing the Company to 
continue its usage of the concerned trademarks in 
assertion of its rights in the same. 
 
In Appeal no. 6617/2025 & Appeal no. 
6622/2025 , the Division Bench has allowed both 
these appeals filed by TI and  has set aside the order 
dated 7th February 2025, passed by the Single 
Bench of the Hon’ble Bombay High Court which 
had dismissed Tilaknagar Industries’ (TI’s) Notice 
of Motion No. 1287 of 2010 in Counter Claim No. 6 
of 2010 in Commercial IP Suit No. 2 of 2009 against 
Herman Jansen Beverages Nederland B.V. & Ors. 
inter alia seeking injunction against them from 
manufacturing and/or bottling and/or marketing 
and/or trading in and/or otherwise dealing in 
alcoholic products bearing the trademarks 
‘MANSION HOUSE’ and/or ‘SAVOY CLUB’ so as to 
pass off their business and goods as and for those 
of TI.  
 
Under the order dated 7th February 2025, the 
Single Bench of Hon’ble Bombay High Court had 
also allowed ABD’s Interim Application (L) No. 
16999 of 2023 in the above Counter Claim granting 
leave to ABD to introduce products bearing the 
mark ‘MANSION HOUSE’ in the State of West 
Bengal.  
 
However, vide order dated 16th July 2025, the 
Division Bench set aside the order in Interim 
Application No. 16999 of 2023 and ordered that 
UTO and ABD shall continue the current status of 
non-introduction of their products in India under 
the impugned marks MANSION HOUSE and 
SAVOY CLUB till decision of the Suit. 
 
To summarize, the operative part of the order dated 
16th July 2025 is as below:  
 
(i) Judgement and order dated 22nd December 
2011 passed in Notice of Motion No. 993/2009 
filed in Suit No. 632/2009 is upheld and 
consequently Appeal No. 66/2012 filed by UTO 
 
 
 
 
is dismissed – thereby in favour of 
Tilaknagar Industries Ltd .  
 
(ii) Judgment and order dated 7th February 2025 
passed in Notice of Motion No. 1287/2010 filed 
in Counterclaim No.6/2010 and passed in 
Interim Application (Lodg.) No. 16999/2023 is 
set aside and consequently Commercial Appeal 
(Lodg.) No. 6617/2025 and Commercial 
Appeal (Lodg.) No.6622/2025 filed by 
Tilaknagar are allowed – thereby both in 
favour of Tilaknagar Industries Ltd . 
 
(iii) UTO and ABD shall continue the current 
status of non-introduction of their products in 
India under the impugned marks MANSION 
HOUSE and SAVOY CLUB till decision of the 
Suit.  
 
(iv) The learned Trial Judge is requested to 
expedite hearing of Commercial IP Suit 
No.2/2009 and to make an endeavour to 
decide the same in an expeditious manner.  
 
(v) It is clarified that the findings recorded in the 
judgement are prima facie and the learned 
Single Judge shall not be influenced by the 
same while deciding the suit finally. 
 
4. Expected Financial 
Implication These orders have not only upheld the ownership 
and proprietary rights of TI under the trademarks 
MANSION HOUSE & SAVOY CLUB, while allowing 
TI to continue its uninterrupted, exclusive use and 
sale of the brands MANSION HOUSE & SAVOY 
CLUB, but have also restrained other entities such 
as UTO & ABD from using and/or launching the 
brand MANSION HOUSE & SAVOY CLUB in all of 
India by protecting exclusive usage rights in favour 
of TI.
