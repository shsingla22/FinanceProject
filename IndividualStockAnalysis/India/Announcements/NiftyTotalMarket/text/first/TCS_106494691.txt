AI-led Growth and Strong Order  Book highlight  TCS’ Q3  
 
 
MUMBAI, January 12 , 202 6: Tata Consultancy Services (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS) reported its consolidated 
financial results according to Ind AS and IFRS, for the quarter ending December  31, 202 5. 
 
Highlights of the Quarter Ended December 31 , 2025  
 
• Revenue at ₹67,087  crore , up 2.0% QoQ , Sequential growth: 0.8% in Constant Currency  
• Annualized AI Services Revenue at $1.8 billion ; up 17.3% QoQ  in Constant  Currency  
• Operating Margin at 25.2 %; stable sequentially   
• Net Income at ₹13,438 crore , +8.5% YoY | Net Margin at 20.0 %; up 40 bps QoQ  
• Cash flow from Operations at 130.4 % of Net Income  
• Q3 Total Contract Value (TCV):  US$9.3 billion  
• Dividend per share: ₹57 including  ₹46 per share as special dividend | Record date 17/01/2026 | 
Payment date 03/02/2026
