LEMON TREE HOTELS SIGNS NEW PROPERTY IN BAROG, 
HIMACHAL PRADESH  
Marks the company’s continued expansion into high -potential leisure 
destinations in North India  
 
New Delhi , 5th March 2026 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -growing 
mid-scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the signing of a new hotel 
in Barog , Himachal Pradesh , marking its 10th property in the State. With four 
operational hotels already in Himachal Pradesh and six additional properties under 
development, including this signing, the Company continues to strengthen its 
footprint in one of India’s most dynamic travel markets.  
The hotel will be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
Nestled in the serene hills of Himachal Pradesh, Barog is a picturesque town known 
for its lush landscapes, colonial heritage and tranquil environment. Located along 
the historic Kalka –Shimla route, the destination has steadily gained popularity 
among leis ure travellers seeking weekend getaways and experiential stays within 
driving distance of North India’s major cities.  
The upcoming Lemon Tree Hotel, Barog, Himachal Pradesh will feature 49 well -
appointed rooms, a restaurant , banquet hall, meeting room,  spa, swimming pool 
and fitness center . The property is designed to cater to leisure travellers, 
destination events and intimate corporate offsites.  
Commenting on the signing, Mr. Neelendra Singh , Managing Director , Lemon 
Tree Hotels  Limited , said,  “Himachal Pradesh continues to be a strong leisure 
market with growing demand for branded hospitality in scenic destinations. 
Barog’s proximity to Chandigarh and its appeal as a peaceful hill retreat make s it 
an attractive addition to our portfolio. This signing aligns with our strategy of 
expanding thoughtfully into emerging leisure micro -markets while delivering 
comfort able and quality experiences .” 
The hotel is conveniently accessible, located approximately 64 km from Shaheed 
Bhagat Singh International Airport, Chandigarh, about 53 km from Chandigarh 
Railway Station, and nearly 32 km from Kalka Railway Station, ensuring seamless 
connectivity for both  regional and interstate guests.
