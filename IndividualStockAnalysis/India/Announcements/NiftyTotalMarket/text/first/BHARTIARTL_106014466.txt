Airtel becomes the first service provider to offer seamless connectivity along Pune's 
newly constructed Metro route  
  
Commuters will have uninterrupted connectivity along the entire route from Kasba Peth to Swargate, 
including stops at Civil Court and Shivaji Nagar  
  
Pune, October 07, 2024: Bharti Airtel (“Airtel”), one of India’s leading telecommunications service 
providers, announced that its commuters can enjoy uninterrupted and seamless connectivity on the newly 
inaugurated North-South Corridor of the Pune metro. This makes Airtel the first operator to offer 
connectivity across the complete stretch, spanning from PCMC to Swargate, including new stations Shivaji 
Nagar, District court, Kasba Peth, Mandai and Swargate.  
  
Airtel has upgraded significant number of sites along the 17.4 km route to enhance 5G capabilities. 
Furthermore, the company has deployed dedicated in-building solutions in the metro stations for 
consistent coverage within the 6 km underground metro stretch. This will ensure that commuters can 
enjoy blazing 5G speeds, uninterrupted voice calls and data transmission allowing them to stay connected 
and productive throughout their travel.  
  
George Mathen, CEO- Maharashtra and Goa, Bharti Airtel, said, “ We are committed to offering our 
customers in Pune the best. Airtel has deployed additional nodes and sites across the new metro route 
that will allow commuters to access high speed mobile internet on the go. We will do what needs to be 
done to ensure that our network remains reliable making it an indispensable companion for all our 
customers”  
  
With this new phase, Pune metro will now have a total stretch of 33 kms across the city with over 30 
stations. Airtel is the only operator to the ensure connectivity on the entire metro route in the city.
