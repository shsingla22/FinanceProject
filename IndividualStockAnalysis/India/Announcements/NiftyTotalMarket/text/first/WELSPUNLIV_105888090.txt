3  EXPLANATORY STATEMENT PURSUANT TO SECTION 102          OF THE COMPANIES ACT, 
2013 
ITEM NO. 1 
 
Pursuant to the recommendation of Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board of 
Directors appointed Ms. Naiyya Saggi (DIN: 06755099) as an Additional Independent Director of 
the Company.   
 
Ms. Saggi, aged 40 years, is a Harvard Business School Graduate, B.A, LLB (Hons) from National 
Law School (Bangalore) where she was elected University President. She was a Fulbright and TATA 
Scholar at Harvard Business School. Ms. Saggi has 16 years of experience with specialization in 
Consumer/FMCG, Consumer Tech, Go to Market, Fundraising, Team Building, Diversity & 
Inclusion and also has expertise in Digital Healthcare.  
 
In 2015, Ms. Saggi founded BabyChakra to support young parents with relevant information via 
smart communities, personalized content and access to doctors. Twenty Four million mothers and 
10,000 doctors have used BabyChakra. Backed by the world’s leading healthtech funds & angels, 
BabyChakra is a Top Consumer Tech product recognised by Global Tech leaders. 
 
In July 2021, BabyChakra was successfully merged with MyGlamm and Ms. Saggi joined 
MyGlamm as Co-founder, President. As Co-founder, she helped grow the merged entity to the 
evolution of The Good Glamm Group which is today India’s First Insurgent Brands Unicorn, 
valued at over $1.2 Billion. GoodGlamm has a portfolio of 6 market leading brands in makeup, 
skincare, babycare and personal care and is backed by top funds such as Warburg Pincus, Amazon, 
Prosus & Bessemer. Naiyya has also led equity investments in the group by top Indian and Global 
celebrities & production houses. Ms. Saggi is the subject of a Harvard Business School Case study 
featuring her decision to merge her company BabyChakra and join the acquiring company as a co-
founder. She has been featured in The Economic Times Top Young Entrepreneurs, Entrepreneur 
India’s Top Women Entrepreneurs, Business World’s Young Entrepreneurs Awards, ET Now 
Leaders of Tomorrow, CNBC Young Turks, Beauty Matters US, The Guardian and by the Indian 
Government under its Startup India program. She is a prominent voice of Women & Diversity in 
India. She was instrumental in orchestrating a partnership with Mint & Startup India to publish a 
seminal report on ‘100 Women Entrepreneurs Building India’. She was formerly a Management 
Consultant at McKinsey & Co. 
 
She is not a director or member / chairman of any board or board committee. Also she has not 
resigned /ceased to be a director from any listed entity during the past 3 years. 
 
The Company has received declaration from the Ms. Saggi confirming that she meets the criteria 
for independence as provided under Section 149(6) of the Act and Regulation 16(1)(b) of the SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ( “Listing Regulations” ) and 
that she is eligible to be appointed as an independent director of the Company and that she is not 
disqualified from being appointed as an independent director. The Company has also received 
notice from a shareholder under Section 160 of the Act proposing her appointment as an 
independent director.
