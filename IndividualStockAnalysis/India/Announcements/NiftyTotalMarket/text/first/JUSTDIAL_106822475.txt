JUST DIAL ANNOUNCES RESULTS FOR QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 
 
Mumbai, India – October 19, 2026: Just Dial Limited (BSE: 535648, NSE: JUSTDIAL), India’s leading 
local search engine company. 
 
Justdial Builds on Strong Start to FY27, Accelerating AI-Led Transformation and Expanding 
Merchant Offerings.  
The company reported Revenue of ₹342.8 Crores for Q2 FY27, registering a YoY growth of 13.1%  
and a QoQ growth of 4.7% . Operating EBITDA  stood at ₹90.7 Crores  with EBITDA Margin at 
26.4%. Net Profit  stood at ₹107.8 Crores , reflecting a YoY decline of 9.7% , primarily due to lower 
other income amid elevated yield rates. 
As part of its ongoing transformation, Justdial continued to invest in AI-led capabilities, automation and 
B2B expansion, with these strategic priorities accounting for approximately one-third of incremental 
expenditure during the quarter. The investments are intended to enhance platform capabilities, deliver 
greater value to merchants and unlock opportunities for long-term growth. 
Justdial continues to strengthen its position as a leading platform for local business discovery in India, 
with Paid Campaigns growing 5.2% YoY and 2.7% QoQ to 656,520  during the quarter. The platform 
recorded 198.4 million unique visitors , while total business listings stood at 57.4 million  as of 
September 30, 2026. 
 
 
 
Financial Highlights for Fiscal 2027 Second Quarter (2Q-FY27) 
 Operating Revenue: Operating Revenue  stood at ₹342.8 Crores, up 4.7% QoQ and 13.1% 
YoY. 
 Operating EBITDA & Margin:  Operating EBITDA stood at ₹90.7 Crores, up 3.7% QoQ. 
EBITDA Margin at 26.4%. Advertising spends stood at ~ ₹11.6 Crores during the quarter.

 Other Income  stood at ₹54.6 Crores, down 58.5% QoQ and 25.6% YoY. Bond yields 
increased ~57 bps QoQ Basis as compared to Q1 FY27 where it had gone down ~44 bps 
resulting in higher MTM Gains during Q1 FY27. 
 Profit Before Taxes stood at ₹134.6 Crores, down 34.9% QoQ. 
 Net Profit  stood at ₹107.8 Crores, down 35.1% QoQ and 9.7% YoY. ETR stood at a 
normalised level of 19.9% for the quarter. 
 Collections (incl. Monthly ECS)  stood at ₹328.8 Crores, up 5.3% QoQ and 11.7% YoY.  
 Deferred Revenue stood at ₹ 526.1 Crores, down 2.6% QoQ and flattish YoY. 
 Cash and Investments stood at ₹ 6,113.9 Crores as on September 30, 2026 compared to ₹ 
5,569.7 Crores as on September 30, 2025 and ₹ 6,022.1 Crores as on June 30, 2026. 
 
 
Business Highlights for Fiscal 2027 Second Quarter (2Q-FY27) 
 Active Paid Campaigns at the end of quarter stood at 656,520 up 5.2% YoY and 2.7% QoQ.  
 Total Traffic (Unique Visitors) for the quarter  stood at 198.4 million, up 0.4% YoY and 2.8% 
QoQ. 85.1% traffic originated on Mobile platforms, 12.4% on Desktop/ PC and 2.5% on our 
Voice platform.  
 Total Active Listings stood at 57.4 million as on September 30, 2026, an increase of 12.2% 
YoY and 2.3% QoQ. 1,309,647 listings were added (net) to the database during the quarter. 
Out of total listings, 43.0 million listings were geocoded as on September 30, 2026, up 19.8% 
YoY. Total Images in listings stood at 269.5 million, up 12.8% YoY and 2.5% QoQ.  
 Total Ratings & Reviews stood at 164.0 million at the end of quarter, up 6.0% YoY.

Mr. Dinkar Ayilavarapu, Chief Executive Officer, Just Dial, commented:   
“The first half of FY27 has given us something more valuable than a couple of good quarters: clarity on 
where Justdial goes next, and early evidence that we are on the right path. Over three decades, Justdial 
has built one of India’s deepest and most trusted relationships with small businesses. Historically, we have 
served that relationship mainly through discovery. Our focus now is to make every merchant on Justdial 
more successful, and to become the platform they rely on to be found, win customers, and run their 
business. 
We are executing on three clear priorities. First, strengthening our core by improving outcomes for 
merchants and using AI to make our sales engine sharper and more productive. Second, helping merchants 
manage their digital presence and customer conversations wherever their customers are. Third, building 
new products and services that make every merchant relationship more valuable over time. Early adoption 
of our new merchant tools has been encouraging. With a profitable core business and a strong balance 
sheet, we are investing from a position of strength, with discipline and patience.” 
 
Building Justdial’s Next Phase of Growth 
Justdial’s Immediate strategy is focused on building on its established strengths while expanding the value 
it delivers to merchants. The company continued to advance strategic initiatives across B2B offerings and 
AI capabilities, while sharpening its focus on merchant success, using proprietary data and insights to 
improve demand-supply matching, make customer acquisition more efficient, and deepen its presence in 
priority business categories. 
Justdial is also seeing positive adoption of its AI-powered CRM and lead management tools, designed to 
address the needs of Indian businesses. 
Looking further ahead, Justdial sees opportunities to extend its platform beyond discovery and lead 
generation by addressing a broader range of merchant needs. Potential areas include connecting 
businesses with relevant services through a marketplace, developing AI-powered tools to support business 
operations, and applying its merchant intelligence and context to enable new digital services. 
The company’s approach is centred on disciplined execution, with a focus on strengthening its core 
business, scaling initiatives that demonstrate sustainable economics, and developing new capabilities as 
opportunities mature.

About Just Dial Limited 
Just Dial Limited provides local search related services to users in India through multiple platforms such as desktop 
and mobile website ( https://www.justdial.com ), mobile apps (Android & iOS), and over the telephone (Voice, pan 
India number 88888-88888). Justdial’s latest version of JD App, is an All-in-One App, replete with features like Map-
aided Search, Live TV, Videos, Stock quotes, etc to make the life of the consumer infinitely smoother & more 
engaging. 
The Company has launched its B2B marketplace platform, JD Mart. JD Mart platform, available at 
https://www.jdmart.com  and via apps on Play Store and App Store, is aimed at enabling millions of India’s 
manufacturers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers to become internet-ready in post-COVID era, get new customers 
and sell their products online. The platform offers digital product catalogues to businesses and aims at digitalising 
India’s businesses, especially MSMEs, across categories. Buyers can discover quality vendors offering a wide 
selection of products to choose from, spread across millions of categories to suit all B2B needs. 
Justdial has also initiated transaction-oriented services for its users. These services aim at making several day-to-
day tasks conveniently actionable and accessible to users from one App. With this step, Justdial is transitioning from 
being purely a provider of local search and related information to being a direct/ indirect enabler of such transactions. 
Justdial has also recently launched an end-to-end business management solution for SMEs, through which it intends 
to transition thousands of SMEs to efficiently run business online and have their adequate online presence via their 
own website, mobile site. Apart from this, Justdial has also launched JD Pay, a unique solution for quick digital 
payments for its users and vendors.
