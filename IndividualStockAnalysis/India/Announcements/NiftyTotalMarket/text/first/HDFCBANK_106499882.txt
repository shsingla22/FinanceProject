B. Retirement of Mr. Bhavesh Zaveri as an Executive Director of the Bank  
 
This is with reference to our earlier intimations made on April 20, 2023 and April 27, 2023 with regard to 
appointment of Mr. Bhavesh Zaveri as an Executive Director of the Bank for a period of 3 years 
commencing April 19, 2023 till April 18, 2026.

the banking sector, including in the group companies, if possible. Accordingly, he will retire as an Executive 
Director of the Bank with effect from the close of business hours on April 18, 2026.

the Bank and wishes him all the success in his future endeavours.
