Datamatics expands operations in the Philippines 
with the inauguration of its fourth delivery center, 
now in Cebu City  
 
19th December 2024, Cebu City, Philippines  – Datamatics, a global Digital 
Technologies , Operations , and Experiences company, has announced the inauguration 
of its new delivery centre  in Cebu, Philippines. Strategically located at Samar Loop, 
Ayala Business Park, the centre  demonstrates Datamatics’ continued commitment to 
support ing clients across industries, including financial services, healthcare, insurance, 
education, e -commerce, technical support, and customer management.  
 
From left to right: Wilson Ng, Praveer Chadhha, Sandeep Arora, Larry, Dr. Rima Ghose  Chowdhury, Darwin 
John Moises, Dileep Tiwari, Buddy Villasis.  
 
This is Datamatics’  4th delivery center in the Philippines, following two centers in 
Manila and one in Clark.  The Cebu delivery center is a registered site under the 
Philippines Economic Zone Authority (PEZA) equipped with modern infrastructure and 
advanced technology to deliver exceptional service and foster innovation.  

 
H.E. Mr. Harsh Kumar Jain, Ambassador of India to the Philippines,  conveyed his 
best wishes and congratulated Datamatics on t he occasion, stating, “The inauguration 
of Datamatics’ new office in Cebu on December 10, 2024, marks another significant 
milestone in the company’s journey in the Philippines. This expansion reflects 
Datamatics’ continued growth and its strong commitment to fostering economic and 
technological collaboration with Cebu City. It also highlights the strengthening ties 
between India and the Philippines.”  
Rahul Kanodia, Vice Chairman and CEO, Datamatics, said,  “The new Cebu facility, 
our fourth delivery center i n the Philippines in just five years, is a significant milestone 
in strengthening our global operations. This facility is designed to deliver superior 
customer experiences, leveraging Cebu’s strategic location and skilled workforce to 
create exceptional va lue and impactful outcomes for our clients.”  
The event was graced by esteemed leaders and professionals, including Dileep Tiwari,  
President of the Indian Business Forum; Darwin John Moises , Vice President of the 
Cebu IT -BPM Organization; Buddy Villasis , Executive Director of CIB.O - Cebu IT -BPM 
Organization; Cherry Lyn C . Sta. Romana, Dean of the College of Computer Studies at 
the Cebu Institute of Technology – University; and Wilson Ng,  Trustee of the Cebu IT -
BPM Organization. Their presence and insigh ts added immense value to the event, 
fostering meaningful discussions and collaboration.  
Datamatics has a global presence across 6 Countries and Global Delivery centers in 4 
Regions. With its enhanced capacity and advanced infrastructure, the new facility 
strengthens Datamatics’ ability to provide agile and scalable solutions. It also 
highlights the company’s focus on fostering local talent and supporting sustainable 
regional growth.
