Sir/Madam,  
 
Housing and Urban Development Corporation Limited (HUDCO) has signed a Memorandum of 
Understanding (MoU) with National Institute of Urban Affairs (NIUA) , on 2 4th November, 2025. Th rough this 
non-binding MoU , parties intend to collaborate for develop ing programmes of mutual interest on themes 
related to urban infrastructure & development including infrastructure  investment and HUDCO’s Urban Invest 
Window (UiWIN), organising capacity building programmes, seminars/ conferences/ workshops  and 
conducting research, monitoring, evaluation, and impact studies. The MoU also aims to explore collaborations 
with multi -lateral funding agencies and other suitable financing opportunities.  
 
The MoU was signed by Shri M Nagaraj, Director (Corporate Planning), HUDCO and Dr. Debolina Kundu , 
Director  (AC), NIUA .  
 
Copy of the signed MoU  is enclosed  for information and record.
