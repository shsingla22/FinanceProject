This quarter, Axis Bank continued to build its all -weather franchise through investments in digital trust, payments innovation, AI 
capabilities, network growth and talent.  
 
Reinforcing its leadership in digital payments and secure banking, Axis Bank became the first Indian bank to bring Apple Pay to its credit 
card customers across Mastercard and Visa networks, making it a milestone moment for the industry.  
 
The Bank continued to focus on smart partnerships, expanding its travel -focused offerings with Scapia and MakeMyTrip. It also advanced 
its technology transformation agenda through the launch of Express Banking: Digital Banking Point 2.0 and AI Soundbox in part nership 
with Hitachi Payment Services, while implementing the next phase of its AMS 2.0 pro gramme with Cognizant to further drive efficiency 
and productivity.  
 
Axis Bank rolled out two campaigns during the quarter: “OPEN ki aadat pad jaayegi ”, showcasing the convenience, personalisation and 
security offered through its OPEN app, and “Far Away But Never Apart ”, celebrating the connection NRIs maintain with India.  
 
Customer engagement and talent development remained key priorities during the quarter. The Bank launched Sparsh Week 2026, it s 
flagship customer engagement initiative; introduced ARISE Homecoming for globally educated Indians looking to build careers i n India; 
and concluded MOVES 2026, engaging over 43,000 students from more than 110 institutions to reimagine the future of banking an d 
organisations.  
 
The quarter also saw Axis Bank deepen its contribution to Indian sports through the launch of Axis Bank -GoSports Foundation 
Performance Institute and the development of Axis Bank Sanjeeva Singh Olympic Archery Centre, supporting and nurturing the co untry’s  
budding sports talent.  
 
The Bank received several industry recognitions during the quarter, including India's Best Digital Bank at the Euromoney  Awards for 
Excellence 2026, Brandon Hall Awards 2026 for learning and leadership development, ICC Emerging Asia Awards 2026 for AI/ML 
implementation and MSME acceleration, multiple honours at the ABF Retail and Wholesale Banking Awards 2026, and the CEO o f the 
Year Award at the SABRE South Asia Awards 2026.  
 
Amitabh Chaudhry, MD & CEO, Axis Bank, said : “As customer expectations continue to evolve, our focus remains on building a 
franchise that combines innovation, trust and execution at scale. This quarter, we built on our leadership in digital payment s and secure 
banking while advancing our technology  agenda through AI -driven innovation and strategic partnerships. We expanded our reach, 
enhanced customer propositions, invested in talent, and deepened our contribution to communities. Together, these efforts ref lect our 
commitment to creating long -term v alue for our customers, stakeholders and the broader economy.”  
 
The Board of Directors of Axis Bank Limited approved the financial results for the quarter and half year ended 30th September 2026  at its 
meeting held in Mumbai on Saturday , 17th October 2026.

Net Interest Income an d Net Interest Margin  
The Bank’s Net Interest Income (NII) for Q2FY27 stood at `15,371 crores up 5% QOQ and 12% YOY basis. Net Interest 
Margin (NIM) for Q2FY27 stood at 3.36%. 
Other Income  
Fee income for Q2FY27 grew 6% YOY to `6,381  crores. Retail fees grew 7% QOQ  and 4% YOY  and constituted 70% of 
the Bank’s total fee income. The Corporate & Commercial banking fees together grew 10% YOY  to `1,943  crores. The 
trading income for the quarter stood at `85 crores; miscellaneous income in Q2FY27 stood at `65 crores. Overall, non -
interest income (comprising of fee, trading and miscellaneous income) for Q2FY27 stood at `6,531 crores.  
Operating Profit and Net Profit  
The Bank’s operating profit for the quarter stood at `11,083 crores  up 6% YOY . Core operatin g profit stood at `10,998 
crores  up 11% YOY . Operating cost grew 9% YOY in Q2FY27. Net profit up 38% YOY to `7,003 crores in Q2FY27.   
Provisions and contingencies                       
Provision and contingencies for Q2FY27 stood at `1,694 crores. Specific loan loss provisions for Q2FY27 stood at `1,564  
crores. The Bank holds cumulative provisions (standard + additional other than NPA) of `15,860 crores as on September 
30, 2026. It is pertinent to note that this is over and above the NPA provisioning included in the Bank’s PCR calculations. 
These cumulative provisions translate to a standard asset coverage of 1.16% as on 30th September 2026. On an aggregated 
basis, the provision coverage ratio (including specific + standard + additional) stands at 164% of GNPA as on 30th 
September 2026. Credit cost (annualized) for the quarter ended 30th September 2026 stood at 0.45%.

Profit for H1FY27 stood at `14,117 crores, up 30% YOY from `10,896 crores in H1FY26.

Capital Adequacy and Shareholders’ Funds  
The shareholders’ funds of the Bank grew 15% YOY and stood at `2,18, 749 crores as on 30th September 2026. The Capital 
Adequacy Ratio (CAR) and CET1 r atio stood at 16.27% and 14.61 % respectively at the end of 30th September 2026. 
Additionally, `8,006  crores of other provisions  and one -time additional standard asset provision, are over and above the 
reported CAR, providing cushion of ~ 49 bps over the reported CAR. The Book value per equity share increased to `703 as 
on 30th September 2026 from `612 as on 30th September 2025.  
Asset Quality  
As on 30th September 2026, the Bank’s reported Gross NPA and Net NPA levels were 1.16% and 0.35% respectively, as 
against 1.46% and 0.44% as on 30th September 2025. Recov eries from written off accounts for the quarter were `971 
crores. Reported net slippages in the quarter adjusted for recoveries from written off pool was `1,412  crores, of which retail 
was `1,313  crores, CBG was `98 crores and Wholesale was `1 crore.   
Gross slippages during the quarter were `4,762  crores, compared to `5,566 crores in Q1FY27 and `5,696 crores in 
Q2FY26. Recoveries and upgrades from NPAs during the quarter were `2,380 crores. The Bank in the quarter wrote off 
NPAs aggregating `2,729  crores . 
As on 30th September 2026, the Bank’s provision coverage, as a proportion of Gross NPAs stood at 70%, as compared to 
70% as at 30th June 2026 and 70% as at 30th September 2025.  The Bank’s provision coverage ratio including prudential 
write -offs stands at 92% as at 30th September 2026.  
The fund based outstanding of standard restructured loans implemented under resolution framework for COVID -19 related 
stress (Covid 1.0 and Covid 2.0) declined during the quarter and as at 30th September 2026 stood at `897 crores that 
translates to 0.06% of the gross customer assets. The Bank carries a provision of ~ 16% on restructured loans, which is in 
excess of regulatory limits.  
Network  
The Bank’s overall distribution network stands at 6,414 domestic branches and extension counters along with  353 Business 
Correspondent Banking Outlets (BCBOs) situated across 3,404  centers as at 30th September 2026 compared to 5,976 
domestic branches and extension counters, and 259 BCBO’s situated in 3,238 centers as at 30th September 2025. As on 
30th September 2026, the Bank had 12,533  ATMs and cash recyclers spread across the country. The Bank’s Axis Virtual 
Centre is present across 8 centers with 1,669  Virtual Relationship Managers as on 30th September 2026.
