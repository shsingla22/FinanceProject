PRESS RELEASE  
Blue Dart Connects Srinagar to India and the World Through Its Express Network  
Srinagar, 20 November 2026 - Blue Dart Express Limited, South Asia’s premier express 
air & integrated transportation and distribution logistics company, has launched next -
day delivery from Srinagar to five major metropolitan cities. The development marks a 
significant milestone in the company’s continued expansion across India. Blue Dart’s first Service Centre in Jammu & Kashmir supports the launch, giving businesses and 
residents direct access to the company’s express logistics expertise, fleet and 
nationwide network.  
Delivery from Srinagar to five major metropolitan cities in India is available within 24 
hours, subject to applicable terms and conditions. The launch strengthens Srinagar’s 
role as a gateway to key domestic markets and lays the groundwork for extending express connectivity to more locations across the region.  
Commenting on the launch, Balfour Manuel, Managing Director, Blue Dart Express Limited , said, “ “For more than four decades, Blue Dart’s promise has been to connect 
businesses across India through dependable express delivery. We are delivering on that promise in Srinagar by making it easier for local enterprises to reach customers across 
the country and internationally, with our global network extending to 220 countries. 
When businesses can serve a wider market reliably, they have more reason to invest, 
expand production and create jobs. That is how stronger logistics connectivity can 
support long -term economic growth in Jammu & Kashmir.”  
Highlighting the opportunity for local businesses, Dipanjan Banerjee, Chief 
Commercial Officer , Blue Dart Express said, “At Blue Dart, our priority is to democratise 
access to express logistics, giving small businesses across geographies the ability to 
compete in larger markets.  With industrial investment in Jammu & Kashmir up about 
40% YoY  in FY2025–26, we see an opportunity to help local enterprises meet growing 
demand. Faster express connectivity from Srinagar helps them fulfil orders sooner, 
replenish stock for retail partners and take on new business.”  
The enhanced connectivity is expected to benefit businesses across the Kashmir Valley, 
including Pashmina weavers, handicraft and carpet artisans, cricket bat manufacturers, apple growers, and saffron and dry fruit producers. The new centre will also support e -
commerce sellers, retailers and other MSMEs with shipping needs ranging from 
individual customer orders to larger commercial consignments.
