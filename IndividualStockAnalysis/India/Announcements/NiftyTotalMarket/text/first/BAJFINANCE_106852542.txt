Half Yearly Report 2026-27 01Key highlights of your Company’s performance in H1 FY27The global economy has remained resilient despite elevated geopolitical and trade uncertainties. India continues to 
perform strongly, with real GDP growing by 7.8% in Q1 FY27. The Reserve Bank of India expects GDP growth of 7.1% 
in FY27. While inﬂationary pressures have increased, India’s medium- to long-term growth outlook remains strong, 
supported by domestic consumption and investment.
Your Company is a strong believer in India opportunity. As India advances towards Viksit Bharat 2047, opportunities 
across credit, ﬁnancial inclusion, MSMEs, digital infrastructure, technology and sustainable ﬁnancing will expand 
signiﬁcantly. Your Company is committed to growing with India and contributing meaningfully to this journey.
With its diversiﬁed business model, large customer franchise, extensive distribution network, omnipresence 
strategy and FINAI transformation, your Company is well positioned to seize the India opportunity and deliver 
sustainable long-term growth.
Your Company delivered a 28% growth in consolidated proﬁt after tax to ₹ 12,447 crore in H1 FY27, while assets 
under management grew 26% to ₹ 5,84,624 crore as at 30 September 2026, reinforcing its position as one of 
India’s largest and most diversiﬁed NBFCs. Both the subsidiaries , Bajaj Housing Finance Limited (BHFL) and Bajaj 
Financial Securities Limited (BFinsec) have also performed very well.
Your Company continued to scale its omnipresence strategy, with a presence across 4,091 locations and 94.0 MM 
net installs on the Bajaj Finserv App as of 30 September 2026.
Number of new loans booked during H1 FY27 grew by 15% to 29.58 MM from 25.66 MM in H1 FY26.
Customer franchise grew by 16% to 128.85 MM as of 30 September 2026 from 110.64 MM as of 30 September 
2025. Your Company estimates new customer addition at 18-20 MM for FY27.
Assets under management (AUM) grew by 26% to ₹ 584,624 crore as of 30 September 2026 from ₹ 462,261 
crore as of 30 September 2025.
Deposits book stood at ₹ 69,754 crore as of 30 September 2026 as compared to ₹ 69,766 crore as of 30 
September 2025. Deposits constitute approximately 14% of consolidated borrowings as of 30 September 2026.
Net interest income for H1 FY27 increased by 23% to ₹ 25,905 crore from ₹ 21,013 crore in H1 FY26. 
Net total income for H1 FY27 increased by 23% to ₹ 31,198 crore from ₹ 25,441 crore in H1 FY26.
Total operating expenses to net total income was 33.2% in H1 FY27 as against 33.1% in H1 FY26. Your Company 
continues to expand its branch network while optimising its operating expenses and rapidly implement AI 
capabilities to improve productivity.
Loan losses and provisions for H1 FY27 were ₹ 4,160 crore as against ₹ 4,049 crore in H1 FY26. H1 FY27 includes 
a prudent management and macro-economic provisions of ₹ 633 crore. Excluding this, loan losses and 
provisions was ₹ 3,527 crore, a decline of 13% as against H1 FY26.
Annualised loan loss and provision to average asset under ﬁnance for H1 FY27 was 1.55%. Excluding the 
prudent management and macro-economic provision, it was 1.31%.
Proﬁt before tax (PBT) for H1 FY27 increased by 29% to ₹ 16,686 crore from ₹ 12,976 crore in H1 FY26. PBT 
before prudent management and macro-economic provision grew by 33% to ₹ 17,319 crore in H1 FY27.
Proﬁt after tax (PAT) for H1 FY27 increased by 28% to ₹ 12,447 crore from ₹ 9,713 crore in H1 FY26. PAT before 
prudent management and macro-economic provision grew by 33% to ₹ 12,921 crore in H1 FY27.
Gross NPA and Net NPA stood at 1.06% and 0.42%, respectively, as of 30 September 2026, compared to 1.24% 
and 0.60% as of 30 September 2025. Provisioning coverage ratio stood at 60% on stage 3 asset and 143 bps on 
stage 1 and 2 assets as of 30 September 2026. 
Capital adequacy ratio (including Tier-II capital) stood at 20.33% as of 30 September 2026, including Tier-I 
capital of 19.43%.
Your Company maintained a healthy liquidity surplus of ₹ 19,995 crore as of 30 September 2026.Consolidated Financial Performance Highlights of H1 FY27
