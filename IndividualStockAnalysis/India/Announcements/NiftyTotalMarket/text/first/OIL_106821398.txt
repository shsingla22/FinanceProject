OIL Achieves a Landmark Milestone with Signing of Special CBM Block RSC  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Oil India Limited (OIL) has achieved a landmark milestone in its strategic energy diversification journey 
with the signing of the Revenue Sharing Contract (RSC) for Special Coal Bed Methane (CBM) Block SR -
ONHP -(CBM) -2026/1 on 15 -10-2026. The historic signi ng ceremony was graced by Shri Hardeep Singh 
Puri, Hon’ble Minister of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Dr. Neeraj Mittal, Secretary, MoP&NG, and Shri V. 
Ponnuraj, Director General, DGH. From OIL, Director (Operation), Shri Trailukya Borgohain and his team 
represented the distinguished occasion.  
Spread across 272.14 sq. km in the Singrauli Coalfield region, the Special Coal Bed Methane (CBM) Block 
SR-ONHP -(CBM) -2026/1 exhibits promising geological prospects, with expected coal seam depths of 600 -
800 metres and an average seam thickness of around 4 0 metres.  
OIL celebrates this milestone as a landmark return to Coal Bed Methane (CBM) exploration, underscoring 
its commitment to unlocking India's unconventional hydrocarbon potential and strengthening the nation's 
energy security.
