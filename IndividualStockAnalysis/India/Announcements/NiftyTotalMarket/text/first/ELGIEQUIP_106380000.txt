ELGi Unveils Demand=Match                       
Saves like VFD. Costs like Fixed.  
Up to 17% energy savings, stable operations, and extended equipment life - a capital -light route to better 
efficiency and sustainability.  
Chennai, India, 19 September 2025: Elgi Equipments (BSE: 522074 NSE: ELGIEQUIP), one of the world's 
leading air compressor manufacturers, today, unveiled the Demand=Match  system, a breakthrough 
innovation that transforms how fixed -speed compressors operate under fluctuating demand. This intelligent 
technology automatically adjusts compressor delivery to real -time plant requirements by smartly 
recirculating airflow inside the compressor, ensuring delivery always equals demand. The result: VFD -like 
energy savings with the simplicity and robustness of fixed -speed machines.  
“Compressed air is the fourth utility in every factory, yet demand is never constant. Most factories rely on 
fixed -speed compressors that continue to deliver constant output even when demand drops. ELGi’s 
Demand=Match system brings dynamic intelligence to these machines, automatically adjusting delivery to 
real-time demand, helping customers save energy, reduce wear, and improve uptime without heavy capital 
investment”  - Dr Jairam Varadaraj, Managing Director, Elgi Equipments Limited.  
The problem: Constant Output Vs Varying Demand  
In most plants, air demand fluctuates minute by minute and shift by shift as the demand at the point of use 
changes constantly. Fixed -speed compressors respond to these fluctuations by cycling between cut -in and 
cut-out pressure bands, leading to:  
• energy losses  
• pressure overshoots  
• frequent cycling and stress critical components  
These inefficiencies increase operating costs and reduce equipment life.  
The solution: Demand=Match System  
ELGi’s Demand=Match technology is a breakthrough system that automatically adjusts compressor delivery 
to match plant demand. It works by smartly recirculating airflow inside the compressor, ensuring delivery 
always equals demand. This eliminates unnecessary cycling of the machine and maintains stable pressure 
even under fluctuating demand.  
Key customer benefits  
• Energy savings: Up to 17% energy reduction in typical operating profiles (field test results — confirm 
with Product/Testing).  
• Improved reliability: Fewer cut -in and cut -out cycles reduce mechanical stress and component wear.  
• Cost efficiency: Capital -light implementation ensures equipment stays lifetime young.
