Dear Sir/Ma’am,  
 
We would like to inform you that Eternal Limited (“ the Company ”) has received the following 
communications today, that:  
 
a. Zomato Media (Private) Limited,  (“ZMPL ”) wholly owned subsidiary of the Company , located in 
Sri Lanka will be struck off from the register of companies and dissolved at the expiration of 
three months from the date of gazette notification i.e. November 21, 2025; and  
 
b. Zomato Internet Hizmetleri Ticaret Anonim Sirketi (“ ZIHTAS ”), step down subsidiary of the 
Company, located in Turkey has been liquidated w.e.f. December 9, 2025.  
 
Further, as disclosed in our red herring prospectus dated July 6, 2021 and prospectus dated July 
19, 2021, ZMPL  and ZIHTAS do not have any active business operations and are under the process 
of liquidation.  
 
It may be further noted that ZMPL  and ZIHTAS  are not material subsidiaries of the Company and 
the dissolution of ZMPL  and ZIHTAS  will not affect the turnover/ revenue of the Company. The 
details as required under Listing Regulations read with SEBI master circular no. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024, for ZMPL and ZIHTAS are given in 
Annexure A and  Annexure B  respectively . 
 
The above information will also be available on the website of the Company www.eternal.com .
