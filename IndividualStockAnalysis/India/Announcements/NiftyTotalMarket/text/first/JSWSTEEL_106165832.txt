Press	Release	
	
JSW	STEEL	RECEIVES 	ResponsibleSteelTM	CERTIFICATION 	
SIMULTANEOUSLY 	FOR	FOUR	MANUFACTURING 	SITES			
 
COMPANY’S 	MANUFACTURING 	PLANTS	AT	VIJAYNAGAR, 	DOLVI,	TARAPUR 	&	SALEM	
RECEIVE 	THE	GLOBAL	CERTIFICATION 		
 
Mumbai, 	India	–	March	05,	2025:  In a coveted global recognitio n of its commitment to lead 
sustainable business practices, JSW Steel – Indi a’s largest steel producer – has received the 
prestigious ResponsibleSteelTM Certification simultaneously for four of its manufacturing 
sites.  
 
In keeping with the Company’s philosophy of ‘B etter Every Day’, the significant accolade has 
been achieved by the manufacturing plants of JSW Steel situated at Vijaynagar (Karnataka), 
Dolvi and Tarapur (both in Maharash tra) and Salem (Tamil Nadu).  
  With this momentous milestone, JSW Steel (Indian Operations) now has over 80% of its 
primary steel production from ResponsibleSteel
TM certified sites, with Tarapur being the 
first downstream rolling mill in India to achieve this milestone.  
 
ResponsibleSteelTM is the steel industry’s first global multi-stak eholder standard and 
certification initiative that works with stee l producers, consumers, and intermediaries 
towards building a sustainabl e steel industry by addressi ng key challenges including 
responsible sourcing, climate ch ange, diversity, human rights  and environment stewardship, 
among a host of ot her initiatives.  
 
Jayant	Acharya, 	Joint	Managing 	Director 	and	CEO	of	JSW	Steel,	said,	“The 
ResponsibleSteel Certification for our four sites in ‘one-go’ is a testament to our unwavering 
resolve to future-proof our bu sinesses by addressing the emerging global challenges while 
we transition to su stainable world.  We	have	built	a	strong	foundation 	for	responsible 	growth	
and	take	pride	in	our	consistent 	commitment 	to	sustainable 	business	practices. 	I thank the team 
from the ResponsibleSteel, the assurance panel and the certification body (BV) for their 
support and congratulate the enti re family of JSW Steel for achieving this milestone. Being 
the largest steel producer from India, we are co mmitted for a holistic grow th of the sector by 
taking care of the need of the society, en vironment and people to support the country’s 
aspiration of being a developed na tion or “Vikshit Bharat” by 2047 .”	
	
Annie	Heaton,	CEO	of	ResponsibleSteel  commented “Congratulations to JSW for achieving 
these four ResponsibleSteel certifications. By  choosing to adopt an international standard 
which addresses not only emissions but a wide breadth of environmenta l and social issues, 
JSW is demonstrating ambition, foresight and le adership as a global industry player. This 
means being open to external monitoring, be ing ready to engage on opportunities for 
improvement with local stakeholders and laying  the foundations for a responsible transition 

to near zero.  As the second largest steel producing country globally, India has a real 
opportunity to emerge at the helm of the indu stry’s shift to sustai nable practices. JSW's 
certifications mean that today we’re proud to  have some 29% of India’s steel capacity 
certified against the ResponsibleSteel Internat ional Production Standard. We look forward 
to their next steps in deepening this journey” 
 
"We	are	honored	to	have	played	a	key	role	in	this	remarkable 	accomplishment 	by	JSW	Steel"

	About	JSW	Steel  
JSW	Steel	is	the	flagship	business	of	the	US$	24	billion
	diversified 	JSW	Group,	one	of	India’s		
fastest‐growing	conglomerates 	with	leading	businesses 	spanning 	across	multiple	sectors,	
including 	energy,	infrastructure, 	cement,	paints,	realty,	B2B	e‐commerce 	,	automobiles, 	defense,	
sports,	and	venture	capital.	Over	the	past	three	decades,	JSW	Steel	has	grown	from	a	single	
manufacturing 	unit	to	India’s	leading	integrated 	steel	producer 	with	a	total	capacity	of	35.7	
MTPA	in	India	and	the	USA,	including 	5	MTPA	currently 	under	commissioning 	in	India.	The	
company’s 	capacity	is	set	to	grow	further	to	43.5	MTPA	by	September 	2027.	Its	Vijayanagar 	
facility	in	Karnataka, 	with	a	current	capacity	of	17.5	MTPA	(including 	5	MTPA	under	
commissioning), 	is	the	largest	single‐location	steel‐producing 	plant	in	India.	
	
With	innovation 	and	technology 	at	its	core,		JSW	Steel	is	globally	recognized 	for	its	business	
excellence 	and	sustainability 	practices. 	It	has	been	a	Steel	Sustainability 	Champion 	by	the	
World	Steel	Association 	(2019–2024), 	secured	Leadership 	Ratings	(A‐)	in	CDP	Climate	Change	
and	an	A	in	CDP	Water	Disclosures 	(2023),	and	won	the	prestigious 	Deming	Prize	for	TQM	at	
its	Vijayanagar 	(2018)	and	Salem	(2019)	facilities.		Apart	of	the	Dow	Jones	Sustainability 	
Indices	(World	and	Emerging 	Markets), 	the	Company 	has	featured	in	S&P	Global’s	
Sustainability 	Yearbook 	(2020–2023). 	Recently,	its	SEED	project	was	awarded	Energy	
Transition 	Changemakers 	at	COP28.	
	
Ranked	8th	in	the	World‐Class	Steelmaker 	Rankings 	by	World	Steel	Dynamics, 	JSW	Steel	is	
committed 	to	reducing	CO₂	emissions 	by	42%	by	2030	and	achieving 	net‐zero	carbon	emissions 	
by	2050.	The	company 	also	aims	to	power	its	operations 	entirely	with	renewable 	energy	by	
2030	and	achieve	no	net	loss	in	biodiversity, 	along	with	improving 	air	quality	and	water	
conservation 	across	its	sites.	JSW	Steel	is	equally	recognized 	for	its	workplace 	excellence, 	
earning	certification 	from	Great	Place	to	Work	(2021–2023) 	and	accolades 	as	one	of	India’s	
Best	Employers 	among	Nation	Builders	(2023–2024) 	and	Best	Workplaces 	in	Health	&	Wellness	
(2023).
