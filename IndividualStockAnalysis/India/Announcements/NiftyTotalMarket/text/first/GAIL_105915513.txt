Sub.: Clarification/Confirmation on news item appearing in "Mainstream Media" 
Dear Sir/ Madam, 
With reference to the news published in Mainstream Media on 15.07.2024 regarding 'Proposed 
GAIL's investment of around Rs. 700 crore in Yamuna City', we would like to submit as follows: 
Considering the strategic advantage of having an in-house R&D centre, GAIL has in-principally 
approved the setting -up of R&D Centre. However, no specific investment approval has yet been 
accorded. Request letter(s) has been sent to various competent authorities of NCR region 
(authorised to allot land for the purpose) in January 2024 for allocation of suitable Institutional 
Land area for setting -up of GAIL 's R&D Centre. However , so far none of the authorities have 
communicated on the allocation of the Institutional land to GAIL. 
This was not intimated to Stock Exchanges as the same was not required to be intimated under 
Regulation 30 of SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015. 
This disclosure is in compliance of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015.
