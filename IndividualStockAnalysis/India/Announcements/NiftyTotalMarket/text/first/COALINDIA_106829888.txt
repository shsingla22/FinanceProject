Coal India Logs 27% Growth in Capex During H1 FY’27;  
Achieves 109% of Planned Outlay  
 
Kolkata, October 26, 2026: Coal India Limited (CIL) incurred capital expenditure (capex) of 
₹7,966 crore during April –September (H1) of FY 2026 –27, registering a growth of 27% over 
₹6,283 crore spent during the corresponding period of FY 2025 –26. The expenditure also 
surpassed the H 1 target of ₹7,286 crore, achieving 109% of the planned outlay for the period 
and over 48% of the annual capex target of ₹16,500 crore.  
 
The robust capex performance underscores the company’s continued focus on strengthening 
mining infrastructure, acquiring land, procuring heavy earth moving machinery (HEMM) and 
advancing diversification projects.  
 
Mine infrastructure development emerged as the largest area of investment during H1 FY 
2026 –27, attracting ₹2,152 crore. This included investments in railway sidings and corridors, 
coal handling plants (CHPs), silos and weighbridges to strengthen the coal evacuation and 
handling infrastructure.  
 
Land acquisition was another major component of expenditure, accounting for ₹2,033 crore 
during the period. Additionally, over ₹1,000 crore was spent on mine development to support 
the expansion and operational readiness of mining projects.  
 
Capital expenditure under the Plant and Machinery (P&M) segment stood at ₹1,394 crore. This 
included investments in HEMMs, washeries and other plant and machinery. Of this, ₹452 crore 
was spent on procuring HEMMs to strengthen mining operations and enhance  operational 
efficiency.  
 
CIL continued to advance its diversification initiatives, investing over ₹739 crore in projects 
including solar energy, critical minerals, coal gasification and thermal power generation.  
 
Mine infrastructure development, plant and machinery, land acquisition and diversification 
remained the principal components of CIL’s capital expenditure, together accounting for over 
79% of the total capex during H1 FY 2026 –27. These strategic investments  are expected to 
strengthen the company’s operational capabilities and support the achievement of its 
production and supply targets for the ongoing financial year.
