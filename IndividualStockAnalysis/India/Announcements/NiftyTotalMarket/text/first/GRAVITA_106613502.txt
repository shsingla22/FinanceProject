Press Release 
 
Gravita India Limited to Set up Copper Recycling Plant at Mandvi, Gujarat 
 
Jaipur, May 7, 2026:  Gravita India Limited, a leading recycling and manufacturing company 
with a strong presence across metals, plastics, and rubber recycling, has announced plans to 
establish a Copper Recycling plant in Mandvi, Gujarat. This initiative aligns with the company’s 
strategy to expand value-added recycling capacities and strengthen its contribution to the circular 
economy. 
 
Plant Overview 
 
 Capital Expenditure: Approximately ₹160 crore  
 Location: Mandvi, Gujarat (strategically chosen for strong infrastructure and logistics 
access)  
 Installed Capacity: ~29,400 MTPA  
 
The facility will deploy advanced and efficient recycling technologies to ensure high metal 
recovery, consistent product quality, and compliance with environmental standards. Commercial 
operations are expected to commence within the next 12 months. The commissioning of plant 
will be funded through internal accruals. 
 
 
Strategic Rationale 
 
The copper recycling plant is aimed at: 
 
 Expanding Gravita’s non-ferrous metal recycling portfolio  
 Enhancing value addition and vertical integration in the metals segment  
 Meeting growing domestic and export demand for recycled copper  
 Supporting sustainable and environmentally responsible manufacturing  
 
The proposed copper recycling plant at Mandvi is a strategic step aligned with our long-term 
vision of expanding recycling capacities. The plant will strengthen our non-ferrous portfolio 
while reinforcing our commitment to sustainable resource utilization and long-term stakeholder 
value creation. 
 
Expected Impact 
 
Upon commissioning, the plant is expected to: 
 
 Contribute positively to revenue and profitability  
 Improve product mix and margins through value-added copper products  
 Generate direct and indirect employment in the region  
 Reduce carbon footprint via increased use of recycled copper  
 Deliver operational synergies across procurement, logistics, and sales 
 
 
 
This initiative further reinforces Gravita India Limited’s position as a global leader in sustainable 
recycling solutions, aligned with India’s focus on circular economy and resource efficiency. 
 
About Gravita India Limited 
 
Gravita Group is among well-known recycling companies across the globe and establishment of 
this recycling plant will help company to further strengthen its financial position coupled with 
diversification in copper recycling and value added products.
