Sub: Update on investment in Offgrid Energy Labs Inc. – Inauguration of first Zinc 
Bromide Based Battery pilot manufacturing facility in the United Kingdom 
 
Reference: Voluntary disclosure pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
 
In continuation of our intimation dated October 27, 2024 and January 22, 2025 regarding 
the Company’s investment of USD 12 million in Offgrid Energy Labs Inc., Delaware, USA 
(“Offgrid”), and our subsequent disclosures in the investor presentations and in the notes to 
the financial results regarding the subscription of A1 Preferred Stock on May 14, 2025 
securing an 18.14% stake in Offgrid (with the balance commitment to be made in due 
course), we wish to voluntarily inform you of the following development. 
 
Offgrid has inaugurated its first Zinc Bromide based battery (ZincGel®) pilot manufacturing 
facility – an initial 10 MWh demonstration line – at Hook, Hampshire, United Kingdom, on 
July 07, 2026. This marks the transition of the technology from laboratory innovation to 
commercial pilot-scale production. The establishment of a pilot manufacturing facility in the 
UK was one of the stated objectives of the Company’s investment in Offgrid, as disclosed in 
our earlier intimation. 
 
ZincGel® is a proprietary zinc-bromide battery platform developed by Offgrid Energy 
designed for long-duration stationary energy storage applications, including renewable 
energy integration, grid balancing, peak-load management, microgrids and industrial 
backup power. Long-Duration Energy Storage ("LDES") refers to systems capable of storing 
and dispatching energy for extended periods ranging from 10 hours to several days or 
seasons and is increasingly considered critical for bridging intermittencies in renewable 
energy generation and ensuring grid stability. The Company’s Bromine business synergizes 
directly with the zinc-bromide chemistry underlying batteries, and the facility establishes a 
foundation for future large-scale battery manufacturing, including the intended giga-
factory in India. 
 
There is no additional financial commitment by the Company arising from this 
development.
