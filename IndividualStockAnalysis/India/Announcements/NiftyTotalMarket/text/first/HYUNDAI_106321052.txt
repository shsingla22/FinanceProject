Hyundai Motor India Reports Total Sales of 60,073 Units  
in July 2025  
• HMIL achieved total monthly domestic sales of 43,973 units and exports 
of 16,100 units in July 2025  
• SUVs contributed 71.8% to HMIL monthly domestic sales in July 2025, 
highest ever since inception  
• Hyundai CRETA complet es 10 years of being India’s favourite SUV  with 
over 1.2 million delighted customers cumulatively since its launch in 2015  
 
Gurugram | August  01, 2025:  Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) has achieved 
total monthly sales of 60,073 units (domestic: 4 3,973 units + exports: 16,100 units) , 
in July 2025.  
  
Commenting on HMIL sales, Mr. Tarun Garg, Whole -time Director and Chief 
Operating Officer, HMIL  said,  “As we celebrate  the incredible 10 -year journey of 
Hyundai CRETA in India, we are proud to see it setting new benchmarks in comfort, 
convenience and performance  thereby holding on to the best -selling SUV crown in 
the Mid -SUV segment, every completed year since its launch in 2015. In the 
January -July 2025 period  too, Hyundai CRETA has further fortified its leadership 
position amongst SUVs. While the overall auto industry has seen some softness in 
recent months, we remain optimistic with the onset of the festive season and a re 
fully geared up with robust supply and exciting product offerings. At HMIL , we 
continue to stay focused on delivering enhanced value and innovation to our 
customers and with  the upcoming state of the art facility at Talegaon, we are well 
positioned to adapt to market movements .”
