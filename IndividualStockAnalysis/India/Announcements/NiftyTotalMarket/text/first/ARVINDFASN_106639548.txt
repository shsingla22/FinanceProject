Media Release  
Arvind Fashions Limited Appoints Lakshmi Krishnan as Chief 
Human Resources Officer  
Bengaluru, 25th May 2026:  Arvind Fashions Limited (AFL), India’s leading casual and denim 
wear company, today announced the appointment of Ms. Lakshmi Krishnan as its Chief 
Human Resources Officer (CHRO).  
Lakshmi brings over 25 years of global experience in human resources, with a strong track 
record of leading transformation, building high-performing teams, and driving strategic 
business outcomes across geographies. Her career spans leadership roles across India, 
Southeast Asia, and the United Kingdom, spanning the consumer goods, financial services, 
and technology sectors.  
Most recently, she was Head of HR for Corporate Functions at Unilever's global headquarters 
in London, where she partnered with the Global CFO and Chief Legal Officer to drive 
organisation-wide transformation across a 5,000+ employee base. Prior to this, she served as 
Head of HR for Unilever's Global Procurement business in Singapore, a €31 billion operation, 
where she led a best-in-class inclusion programme while significantly enhancing 
organisational effectiveness and performance.  
Commenting on the appointment, Ms. Amisha Jain, Managing Director & CEO, Arvind 
Fashions Limited, said, "We are delighted to welcome Lakshmi to AFL at an important moment 
in our growth journey. Her global HR leadership across consumer, financial services, and 
technology businesses and her track record of driving transformation at scale will be invaluable 
as we strengthen our organisational capabilities and build the talent and culture needed for 
our next phase of growth."  
Commenting on her appointment, Ms. Lakshmi Krishnan, Chief Human Resources Officer, 
Arvind Fashions Limited, said, "I am excited to join Arvind Fashions at this important phase of 
its growth journey. The company's strong portfolio of brands and its focus on building future-
ready capabilities present a compelling opportunity. I look forward to working closely with the 
leadership team to further strengthen the organisation, nurture talent, and build a high-
performance culture."  
Lakshmi holds a Master's degree in Social Work (Gold Medallist) from the University of Madras 
and is pursuing the CHRO Program at Wharton.  
About AFL  
Arvind Fashions Ltd is India's no. 1 casual and denim player, a lifestyle powerhouse with a 
strong portfolio of fashion brands catering to consumers across the sub-categories and price
