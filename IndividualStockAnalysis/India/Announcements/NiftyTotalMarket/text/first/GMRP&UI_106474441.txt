subsidiary of GMR Power and Urban Infra Limited (“Company”), has successfully

cost.

reduced GKEL’s average cost of borrowing from ~ 12.15% p.a. to 9.50% p.a. with a 
provision to further reduce the same to 9.25% p.a. subject to a credit rating upgrade. 
Post refinancing, the estimated savings in interest cost during the first full year of 
operations will be ~  ₹72-75 Cr ore.

strengthen profitability and enhance shareholders’ value."
