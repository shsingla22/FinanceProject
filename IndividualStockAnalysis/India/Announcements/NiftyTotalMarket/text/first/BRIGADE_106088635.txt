PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Group Launches Brigade Citrine , India’s First Net -Zero Residential Development  
The project has an estimated GDV of about ₹500 Crores  
 
Bengaluru , December 18, 2024 : Brigade Group  has announce d the launch of Brigade Citrine, a 
residential project that sets new benchmarks in sustainable living. This high -rise township, located 
in Budigere Cross, Whitefield , Bengaluru  is India's first truly net -zero carbon , residential 
development.  The project has a n estimated GDV of about ₹500 Crores . The total size of the 
project site is around 4.3 acres and includes  420 thoughtfully designed 1,2,3 and 4 BHK  homes . 
  
To achieve Brigade Citrine’s net-zero carbon goal, several innovative strategie s have been 
implemented  including  the use of eco -friendly materials, energy -efficient designs, water 
conservation measures, and carbon offsetting initiatives. Among other benefits , the community 
will save  around 8900 tankers of water annually and us e renewable energy for 100% of the common 
area light ing, not only reduc ing the community’s environmental impact but also enhanc ing the 
quality of life for its residents.   
 
“Our commitment to net -zero for this project focuses on  mitigating,  reusing and offsetting to 
reduce the Brigade Citrine community’s environmental impact over the next 25 years. Innovative 
designs le ads to operational carbon efficiency and embodied carbon reduction upto 40%. The 
project is in line with Brigade Group’s journey of achieving net zero emissions by 2045 . From site 
selection to design and development and operations, we ensure our projects are aligned to our 
sustainability goals. In  fact, Brigade Group ha d always incorporated environmentally friendly  
measures in our projects . Every new Brigade home is IGBC Certified  and we have around 14.8 
million square feet of green building certifications  in various stages .” said Pavitra Shankar, 
Managing Director, Brigade Enterprises Ltd.  
 
Brigade Citrine offers a unique blend of luxury, sustainability, and convenience. Being a low-density 
development, residents can enjoy spacious and serene living  with ample outdoor and indoor 
amenities .
