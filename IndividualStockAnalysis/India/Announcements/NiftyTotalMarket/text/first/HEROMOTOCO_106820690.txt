HERO MO TOCORP ACCELERATES ITS PREMIUMIS ATION JOURNEY  
LAUNCHES ALL -NEW XTREME 160R 4V AND REFRESHED XTREME 250R  
 
Hero MotoCorp , the world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters , is accelerating its 
premium growth ambitions with the launch of the all -new Xtreme 160R 4V and refreshed Xtreme 
250R. The twin launch marks a significant step in strengthening the Company’s premium motorcycle 
portfolio, bringing together sharper streetfighter styling, exhilarating performan ce and advanced 
rider -focused technology across two key segments.  
 
While the all -new Xtreme 160R 4V expands the appeal of the Xtreme family with a comprehensive 
redesign and new technology, the refreshed Xtreme 250R builds on its performance credentials wi th 
enhanced equipment, refined detailing and greater riding versatility. Together, the two motorcycles 
reinforce Hero MotoCorp’s commitment to delivering aspirational, technology -led product s and 
accelerating its premiumis ation journey.  
 
Speaking on the la unch, Anuj Dua, Chief Business Officer – Premium Segment, Hero MotoCorp  
said, “Premiumis ation remains one of the biggest growth opportunities ahead of us and we continue to 
strengthen our position in this segment through sharper product differentiation and  enhanced riding 
experiences. The Xtreme family is a key pillar of this strategy.  
 
The all -new Xtreme 160R 4V brings the Xtreme DNA to a larger community of young riders as the fastest 
motorcycle in its segment, with a complete redesign and segment -first cruise control and Panic Brake 
Alert, while the newly launched Xtreme 250R raises the bar in its segment with Ride -by-wire, four ride 
modes, traction control, a quickshifter, segment -first cruise control, Tyre Pressure Monitoring System, 
and the industry's  first Frequency selective damping suspension. Backed by our Hero Premia network of 
more than 130 stores, these launches reflect our commitment to creating world -class, aspirational 
products and further strengthening our premium portfolio.”  
 
THE ALL -NEW XT REME 160R 4V  
Since its debut in 2023, the Xtreme 160R 4V has been a key pillar of Hero MotoCorp's premium 
motorcycle portfolio. The all -new model is its most comprehensive transformation yet: a complete 
redesign carrying the Xtreme family DNA, segment -lead ing performance and a host of innovative 
technology. Conceived as a sporty naked motorcycle , the all -new Xtreme 160R 4V is y outhful, 
muscular and aggressive , with a big -bike presence designed for riders who want thrill, individuality 
and technology from their motorcycle.  
 
Xtreme DNA, Sharper Stance  
The all -new Xtreme 160R 4V features a “Cybersharp” design language that is aggressive, with sharper 
tank shrouds, a muscular stance, and revised bodywork for increased road presence. The 
ergonomically contoured  fuel tank enhances knee grip and rider comfort, with easy ground reach. Its 
Advanced Lighting Package features an auto -illuminating Class D full LED headlamp with ambient 
light sensor, projector high beam, and signature “H”-shaped DRLs complemented by an “H”-shaped 
LED tail lamp and full LED winkers. Red and black front wheel rim options add a personalised touch.

Fastest In I ts Segment  
The all -new Xtreme 160R 4V is powered by Hero MotoCorp's 163cc, 4 -valve, oil -cooled engine , 
producing 17.1 PS @ 8,750  rpm  of peak power and 14.6 Nm @ 6500 rpm of peak torque. Its short -
stroke design revs quickly to deliver high peak power, while a finned oil cooler keeps the engine 
thermally stable through extended, spirited riding. The result is the fas test motorcycle in it s segment 
with a top speed of 0 to 60 km/h in 4.4 seconds. It also delivers the best rear -wheel power  in its 
segment . Ownership is easier too, with extended service intervals: no oil change and no tappet setting 
is required until 6,000 km.  
 
Ride -by-Wire, R ide Modes &  Segment -First Cruise Control  
Electronic throttle control (ride -by-wire) delivers precise, smooth and predictable throttle response, 
and enables three ride modes. Rain mode offers a softer throttle response for slippery conditions, 
Road mode pro vides smooth, balanced power delivery for everyday riding, and Sport mode 
unleashes the motorcycle's full potential with sharp, direct throttle response. The Xtreme 160R 4V is 
also the first motorcycle in its segment to offer cruise control, making every d ay and long distance 
riding effortless.  
 
Confidence O n Every Ride  
Dual -channel ABS, tuned for the best balance between braking stability and deceleration on all 
surfaces, works with a 276 mm front and 220 mm rear petal disc. The motorcycle also introduces a 
segment -first Panic Brake Alert, which enhances safety notifying surrounding traffic during sudden, 
hard braking. Suspension duties are handled by 37 mm KYB upside -down front forks and a 7 -step 
preload -adjustable rear monoshock, delivering a planted ride  with balanced comfort and body 
control. Radial tubeless tyres, including a wide 130/70 R17 rear, provide grip and confidence through 
corners.  
 
All-New TFT Console &  Rider -Focused Comfort  
The new TFT console displays ride modes, gear position with shift advisory, cruise control status, 
distance to empty, and real -time mileage, with auto -adjusting brightness. Bluetooth connectivity 
enables call and message alerts, music, phone battery status, and turn -by-turn navigation. 
Illuminated soft -touch switchgear, an integrated kill -start switch, hazard lights, cruise control 
controls, and a Type -C fast charger enhance convenience. 5 -step adjustable brake and clutch levers 
allow personalised reach, while a revised rider triangle, ergonomically contoured seat, and im proved 
ground reach enhance comfort. The seat is 16 mm longer at 642.9 mm, with single and split -seat 
options.  
 
THE REFRESHED XTREME 250R  
The refreshed Xtreme 250R raises the bar in the 250cc performance segment with enhanced 
performance, advanced technolo gy, sharper dynamics and premium design and finish. Combining 
track -inspired technology and confidence -inspiring handling with everyday usability, it delivers an 
exhilarating, well -rounded riding experience.  
 
Bolder Style, Sharper Finish  
The product sharpe ns its aggressive streetfighter stance with gold anodised front forks, carbon -fibre 
hydro -dipped panels, coloured rims with rim tapes, evolved graphics, and embossed detailing. The 
FSD branding on the rear suspension adds a distinctive performance cue. Wit h 50+ fit and finish

Dynamic Performance  
At the heart of the new Xtreme 250R is the proven 250cc liquid -cooled, 4 -valve DOHC engi ne,

Effortless Riding  
The new Xtreme 250R introduces segment -first cruise control. It allows riders to automatically 
maintain a set speed without constantly holding the throttle, enhancing riding comfort and reducing 
fatigue. Adding to  the experience is Hero MotoCorp's first sensorless bi -directional quickshifter. It 
allows clutchless upshifts and downshifts without closing the throttle, delivering faster shifts on the 
track and less fatigue on long rides.  
 
Advanced Rider Aids for Total  Control  
The motorcycle now offers four ride modes, making the Xtreme 250R the best -equipped motorcycle 
in its segment for riding versatility. The modes are tuned to suit different riding conditions and 
preferences, with Rain mode offering smoother power d elivery for wet and slippery surfaces, Road 
mode delivering linear performance for everyday riding, Sport mode sharpening the motorcycle's 
response for spirited riding, and Track mode unlocking its full performance potential for the race 
track. A Tyre Pres sure Monitoring System (TPMS) displays real -time tyre pressure and temperature, 
with colour -coded alerts when pressure is too high or too low.  
 
Frequency Selective Damping Suspension: Comfort Meets Control  
It is equipped with Frequency Selective Damping (F SD) rear suspension, a first in the two -wheeler 
industry. An additional FSD piston provides two distinct rebound damping profiles that adapt to the 
frequency of road inputs. The result is a ride that smooths out broken roads while keeping the tyre 
planted for precise line selection through corners. The rigid trellis frame and 43mm USD front forks 
continue to deliver agile and precise handling.  
 
Smart TFT Console & Connectivity  
A new full -colour TFT instrument cluster displays all information in an easily re adable interface that 
also looks good.  Riders can also use the built -in lap timer and drag timer. Bluetooth connectivity 
brings call and message alerts, phone battery status and turn -by-turn navigation to the console. New 
soft-touch switchgear puts every f unction at the rider's fingertips, including a dedicated mode 
selector, an integrated kill -start switch, hazard lights and cruise control switches.

About Hero MotoCorp  
Hero MotoCorp, headquartered in New Delhi, India, is the world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters for 25 
consecutive years with ~$5 billion revenue. The  Company’s global footprint spans 53 countries across Asia, Africa, Europe, 
Central and Latin America with a customer base of over 130 million. The Company operates eight manufacturing facilities 
- six in India and one each in Colombia and Bangladesh - along with two Research and Development centres: Hero Centre 
for Innovation and Technology (CIT) in India and Hero Tech Centre Germany (TCG). Hero MotoCorp is driving the transition 
to electric mobility through VIDA. Strategic alliances with Harley -Davidson a nd Zero Motorcycles support the design and 
development of premium and electric motorcycles, respectively.  
Further demonstrating its commitment to the EV future, the Company has made strategic investments in two - and three -
wheeler EV manufacturers, Ather En ergy and Euler Motors. Hero MotoCorp is the only Indian two -wheeler manufacturer 
listed on the prestigious Dow Jones Sustainability Index (DJSI).  
Hero MotoCorp’s Executive Chairman, Dr. Pawan Munjal, was named  to the 2025 TIME100 Climate List of the 100 mo st 
influential leaders driving business climate action.  
Beyond mobility, Hero MotoCorp is a major global promoter of sports, including golf, football, field hockey, and cricket. 
Hero MotoSports Team Rally is India's flag bearer in global rally racing.
