Madam/ Sir, 
 
It is hereby informed that a Company - “MAHAGENCO NTPC GREEN ENERGY 
PRIVATE LIMITED [MNGEPL]”, has been incorporated on 25.11.2024 as 50:50 joint 
venture of NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL), a wholly owned subsidiary of NTPC 
Limited and Maharashtra State Power Generation Company Limited (MAHAGENCO), 
a Government of Maharashtra (GOM) owned company.  
 
MNGEPL will be engaged in the business of developing, operating and maintaining 
Renewable Energy Park(s) in Maharashtra under UMREPP and allot Park(s) for 
development of Renewable Energy Project(s).  
 
Additional information in terms of SEBI circular dated 13.07.2023 is enclosed .
