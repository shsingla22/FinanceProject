Sub: Conclusion of US FDA Inspection  at Company’s JNPC  Facility, Visakhapatnam  
 
This is to inform you that the United States Food and Drug Administration (US FDA) has conducted 
a Pre-approval inspection (PAI) for Sterile APIs  at the Company’s Facility at JNPC, Visakhapatnam  
between  February 19, 2025  and February 25 , 2025.   
 
The said inspection  was concluded with THREE  (3) Form 483 Observations . These observations are 
procedural in nature . The corrective and preventive actions for these observations will be submitted 
to the US FDA within the stipulated period. The observations issued are neither repeated observations 
nor related to data integrity.  
 
The information required as per Regulation 30 read with Schedule III Para A Sub Para 20 of the SEBI  
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  are provided in Annexure A  
enclosed herewith.
