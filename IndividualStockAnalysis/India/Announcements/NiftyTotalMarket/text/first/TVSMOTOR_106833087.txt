TVS MOTOR COMPANY ANNOUNCES DEMERGER OF 
CONSUMER FINANCE AND RELATED BUSINESS 
UNDERTAKING  
 
• Proposed demerger will unlock shareholder value through a focused platform for the 
Group's consumer finance and related businesses  
• Provides greater strategic autonomy, sharper capital allocation and enhanced 
flexibility to pursue sector opportunities  
• Shareholders to participate in the future growth of both mobility and financial services 
businesses  
 
Bengaluru, October 28, 2026:  The Board of Directors of TVS Motor Company , part 
of TVS VENU,  today approved a Composite Scheme of Arrangement  (Scheme) to 
demerge its consumer finance and related business  undertaking into a separate 
holding company. Under the proposed Scheme, shareholders of the c ompany will 
receive equal number of shares in the resulting company  as in TVS Motor , enabling 
them to participate in the future growth of both the mobility and consumer finance  
businesses.    
 
The consumer finance and related business  have expanded in scale and scope  and 
a dedicated st ructure will provide greater strategic focus, flexibility in capital 
allocation and the ability to pursue opportunities across the consumer finance and 
related  sector s. The proposed structure will better align shareholders with the future 
growth of these businesses while supporting their continued expansion.  
 In August 2026, the NBFC Companies  within the Group have approved a proposal to 
merge and consolidate the Group’s consumer finance businesses into a single 
operating platform. The proposed demerger is  the next step in this strategic process 
and is intended to segregate the Consumer financ e and related business es from 
TVS Motor into a dedicated platform  and reflects the Group's long- term conviction in 
India's financial services sector and its role in supporting economic growth, financial inclusion and rising aspirations across the country.  
The proposed structure will also enable TVS Motor to sharpen its focus on its continuing automotive and mobility businesses. The Group remains committed to the growth and development of both the dedicated consumer  finance platform and TVS 
Motor’s continuing business.  
 Sudarshan Venu, Chairman, TVS Motor Company, said:  "India's  rapidly 
expanding  financial services sector offer s tremendous opportunities for long- term 
growth. Driven by digitisation, expanding credit access and the aspirations of India's growing middle class, financial services are  playing an increasingly important role in 
one of the world's fastest -growing large economies. A t TVS VENU , we have built 
meaningful presence and capabilities  in this sector through sustained investments 
over 15 years. Our NBFC TVS Credit, along with Home Credit India, serves more
