Coforge Named  a Leader in Everest Group Quality Engineering (QE) 
Services for AI Applications and Systems PEAK Matrix® Assessment 
2026  
 
GREATER NOIDA, India, October 26, 2026 – Coforge Limited (NSE: COFORGE), a global AI -
native engineering services leader, today  announced it has been positioned as a Leader in 
Everest Group’s Quality Engineering (QE) Services for AI Applications and Systems PEAK 
Matrix® Assessment 2026.  
 
The recognition reflects Coforge's ability to  help enterprises validate, secure, and govern 
production AI systems as AI adoption accelerate s across customer experiences, business 
processes, and core technology environments.  
 
The assessment evaluated 40 service providers based on market impact , overall vision , and 
capability. The evaluation criteria included the breadth of capabilities in AI model validation, 
generative AI and retrieval -augmented generation (RAG) evaluation, agentic AI assurance, 
responsible AI, security testing, AI observability, specialized talent, partner ecosystems, and 
demonstrated client outcomes . 
 
As AI moves from experimentation to production, enterprises require assurance  frameworks 
that deliver  reliability, governance, and operational resilience across the AI lifecycle. Coforge's 
AI-led QE portfolio helps organizations reduce deployment risk, accelerate innovation, and 
scale AI responsibly.  
 
"AI is fundamentally changing how organizations  build, operate, and scale. As enterprise  
systems become more autonomous, quality engineering cannot stop  at validating 
functionality; it m ust establish trust across the entire AI lifecycle," said Raghu Krovvidy, 
Executive Vice President and Head of Quality Engineering, Coforge . "This recognition 
validates our investments in AI-native quality engineering, combining deep domain expertise, 
proprietary accelerators, and governance -led assurance frameworks that help clients 
confidently deploy AI at scale ." 
 
Coforge 's QE portfolio spans  advisory , trans formation, digital assurance, regulatory 
compliance, and a suite of AI -powered solutions  that transform quality engineering into a 
strategic business enabler that supports greater enterprise autonomy.
