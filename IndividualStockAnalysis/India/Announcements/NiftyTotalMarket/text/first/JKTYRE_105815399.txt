a) Name(s) of the opposing party, court/tribunal/agency where
litigation is filed: The Commercial Tax Department, Bhilwara (“CTO
Bhilwara”), State of Rajasthan, Supreme Court of India
b) Brief detaIls of dispute/litigation; The Company received notices in
the year 2004, 2010 and 2013 from the Commercial Taxes Department
for recovery of Rs. 9.94 crore claimed as remission by the Company for
the period between 1999 and 2008. The CTO Bhilwara passed an order
against the Company. Resultantly, the Company filed a writ petition
before the High Court of Rajasthan challenging the order passed by the
CTO Bhilwara, The High Court passed an order in favour of the Company
against which the Government of Rajasthan and CTO Bhilwara filed a
special leave petition before the Supreme Court of India.
The matter was heard by the Hon’ble Supreme Court on 15th February
2024. The Hon'ble Supreme Court set aside the Order of the Hon’ble
High Court and remanded the matter back to Hon’ble High Court,
Jodhpur, leaving all questions open
c) Expected financial implications, if any, due to compensation,
penalty etc.: Rs. 9.94 Crore, as commercial tax plus interest and
penalty, if any.

in relation to such proceedings:
The matter was listed before the Hon’ble High Court on 5th April 2024
and on the said date, the Company prayed for withdrawal of the matter

withdrawn by the Hon’ble High Court vide order dated 21 st March 2024.
