Thomas Cook India strengthens foothold in Delhi- NCR with inauguration 
of its new outlet in Gurugram  
 
Extends consumer access in Delhi-NCR to 14 locations 
 
 The new Gurugram outlet intends to leverage the strong travel demand from the region  
 Customers from Delhi -NCR  are displaying year -round appetite for travel, including peak seasons of 
summer vacations and winter/year -end holiday  
 Key growth drivers  include  group tours , experiential travel , increased demand for premium programs and  
luxury cruises  
 
 
Mumbai,  December 17, 2024:  Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel services company, has 
inaugurated its new franchise outlet in Gurugram, to tap into the high- growth market of the Delhi -NCR region  and 
surrounding areas . This new store, further strengthens Thomas Cook’s network in the Delhi -NCR region to 14 
locations (6 owned stores and 8 Gold Circle Partner outlets ) and aims to cater to the significant and growing 
demand for year -round travel.  
 
The new outlet is in the heart of Gurugram, providing Thomas Cook a viable catchment for a range of 
consumers: families, professionals/business travellers, senior citizens and NRIs.  Located near some of the most 
expensive real estate in Gurugram, the outlet also provides access to NCR’s HNI and UHNI segments, offering expertise tailored to their personalized travel requirements.  
  
Thomas Cook’s Gurugram  outlet will provide end-to-end travel services: International & Domestic Holidays 
(Group Tours, Personalised holidays , Cruises, etc.), Value Added Services like Travel Insurance, etc . In addition 
to the holiday experts at the outlets,  Thomas Cook’s omnichannel strategy equips customers with digital tools 
like its AI platform to assist customers plan and book their personalized vacations in real- time.
