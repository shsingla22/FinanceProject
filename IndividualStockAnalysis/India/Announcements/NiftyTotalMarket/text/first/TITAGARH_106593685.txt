Titagarh Naval Systems Limited  (a wholly owned subsidiary of Titagarh Rail Systems Limited)  Launches 
Fourth Diving Support Craft  A23 (YARD 328)  for Indian Navy  
 
Kolkata, 19 April 2026:  Titagarh Naval Systems Limited (TNSL) , a wholly owned subsidiary of Titagarh Rail 
Systems Limited,  is proud to achieve  another milestone in its shipbuilding journey with the launch of the fourth 
Diving Support Craft  A23 (YARD 328)  for the Indian Navy. The vessel is part of an ongoing series of five DSCs 
being constructed under the Government of India’s Make in India initiative.  
The launch ceremony was graced by Mrs. Deepa Sivakumar, wife of Vice Admiral B Sivakumar, AVSM, VSM, COM 
who performed the traditional naming and launch of the vessel. The craft was successfully side launched  into 
the Hooghly River  at 1455 Hrs on 19th April 2026 . Around 150 employees were present to witness this important 
occasion.  
Designed as catamaran -type vessels, these Diving Support Crafts are equipped with advanced, indigenously 
sourced systems to support the Indian Navy’s Command Clearance Diving Teams (CCDTs). They will play a critical 
role in underwater operations, including  inspection, repair, maintenance, and salvage activities within harbour 
and near -shore environments.  
Speaking on the occasion, Shri Umesh Chowdhary, Vice Chairman & Managing Director, Titagarh Rail Systems 
Limited, said: “The launch of DSC  A23 (YARD 328) marks steady progress in our commitment to delivering 
specialised vessels for the Indian Navy. It reflects our continued focus on strengthening indigenous capabilities in 
defence manufacturing and shipbuilding, also underscores our steadfast efforts to fur ther enhance our 
shipbuilding capabilities and contribute consistently to the delivery of critical vessels in service of the nation. We 
are proud to contribute to enhancing the Navy’s operational preparedness through these advanced platforms.”  
Speaking on the occasion, Vice Admiral B Sivakumar, AVSM, VSM, COM, Indian Navy, said: “It was a privilege to 
mark the launch of DSC A23 (YARD 328), a key milestone reflecting dedicated teamwork, engineering excellence, 
and India’s growing self -reliance in defence manufacturing. Launched in Kolkata, a city with a rich maritime 
legacy, the indi genously built 30 -metre catamaran will enhance diving and underwater operations in coastal 
waters. He appreciated the strong collaboration between the Indian Navy, the  shipyard, and industry partners, 
and expressed confidence that such indigenous efforts will further strengthen India’s maritime power. ” 
In addition to operational deployment, the DSCs will serve as training platforms for the Navy’s diving personnel, 
supporting capability development and readiness.  
This launch further underscores the evolving role of Titagarh Naval Systems Limited as the dedicated arm for 
shipbuilding and maritime systems, following the strategic restructuring of Titagarh Rail Systems Limited. The 
move enables a sharper focus on mari time growth while continuing to build on the TRSL’ s engineering strengths , 
reaffirming our commitment to the nation and towards a Viksit Bharat.  
With a legacy of delivering complex vessels across defence and commercial segments, TNSL continues to 
contribute to India’s self -reliance in shipbuilding. Its portfolio includes specialised vessels delivered to key 
institutions and strategic international collaborations, reinforcing its position as a trusted partner in the maritime 
ecosystem.  
 
 
 
 
This launch marks the growing role of Titagarh Naval Systems Limited as a dedicated shipbuilding and maritime 
systems company, formed after the strategic restructuring of Titagarh Rail Systems Limited. This move helps us 
focus more clearly on maritime grow th and also strengthens our new expansion at Falta, West Bengal. The 
Falta facility is being developed into a modern shipyard spread over approximately 50 acres, aimed at meeting 
both national and international shipbuilding requirements. This initiative re flects our strong commitment to 
the nation and supports the vision of a Viksit Bharat.  
 
About TNSL:  
 
Titagarh Naval Systems Limited is poised to mark a significant milestone in India’s maritime sector with the 
establishment of a state -of-the-art shipyard at Falta, south of Kolkata. To drive this growth, the Board of Directors 
has, in principle, approved t he transfer of the shipbuilding and maritime systems business into a wholly owned 
subsidiary, Titagarh Naval Systems Limited, with an approximate order book of above 500 crores (including 
taxes).  
 
Designed with advanced automation and fully aligned with Industry 4.0 standards, the new facility builds on 
TRSL’s strong legacy of delivering more than 35 vessels —including coastal research ships for NIOT, the Indian 
Navy, Coast Guard, and GRSE —and is eng ineered to handle ships up to 180 meters in length, with an annual 
capacity of 12 -16 vessels.  
 
The establishment of Titagarh Naval Systems Limited will enable TRSL to concentrate more sharply on its core 
railway systems business, while empowering the new  company to pursue aggressive growth in shipbuilding by 
securing contracts for specialized vessels that contribute to the nation’s maritime excellence. Titagarh Naval 
Systems Limited will also carry forward existing projects, including the Diving Support C raft project. To drive 
further expansion, Titagarh Naval Systems Limited is actively exploring partnerships with strategic collaborators 
and investors to accelerate scaling and enhance its capabilities under various Government of India maritime 
initiatives .
