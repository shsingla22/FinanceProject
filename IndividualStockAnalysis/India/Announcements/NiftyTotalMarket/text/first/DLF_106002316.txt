 SERVICE PROVIDER 
 
The Company has availed the services of National Securities Depository Limited NSDL) for 
facilitating remote e-voting to enable the Members to cast their votes electronically using remote 
e-voting system on special business sought to be transacted through Postal Ballot. 
 
 
 
The Management of the Company is responsible to ensure the compliances with the requirements 
of the Act read with Rules made thereunder, Listing Regulations, MCA Circulars or any other 
provisions, as applicable for conducting postal ballot through remote e-voting. The management 
of the Company is responsible for ensuring a secured framework and robustness of the electronic 
voting system. 
 
SIBILITY 
 
Our responsibility as Scrutinizers of the voting process is restricted to scrutinize the e-voting 
process, in a fair and transparent manner and to prepare a 
For or Against the Resolution stated in the Notice of Postal Ballot, based on the report generated 
from the e-voting system provided by the NSDL.
 
CUT OFF DATE 
 
The Members of the Company holding equity shares either in physical form or in dematerialized 
form, Cut-off Date Friday, 23 August 2024 were entitled to cast their votes 
electronically through remote e-voting on the Resolution as set out in the Notice of Postal Ballot. 
 
REMOTE E-VOTING PROCESS 
 
The remote e-voting period commenced from Wednesday,  28 August 2024 at 09:00 a.m. (IST) 
and ended on  Thursday, 26 September 2024 at 5:00 p.m. (IST) on the designated website 
https://www.evoting.nsdl.com  of NSDL.   
 
 
NOTICE IN ELECTRONIC MODE 
 
The Notice for Postal Ballot was sent to all the Members/Beneficiaries electronically on Tuesday, 
27 August 2024, whose E-mail IDs were registered with the Company or  National Securities 
Depository Limited/ Central Depository Services (India) Limited (collectively referred to as 
Depositories) and whose names appear in the Register of Members maintained by the Company/ 
list of Beneficial Owners as received from the Depositories as on Cut-off Date in accordance with 
the provisions of the Act read with Rules made thereunder together with the MCA Circulars.
