Subject: Udupi -CSL, a wholly owned subsidiary of CSL, secured a Notable* order from 
a Domestic Client for construction of four nos. of 70 T Bollard Pull ASD Tugs  
1. We would like to inform that Udupi Cochin Shipyard Limited (U dupi-CSL), a wholly 
owned subsidiary of CSL, has bagged a Notable * order from Ocean Sparkle Limited , an Adani 
Group  Company , for the construction of four nos. of ASD (Azimuthing Stern Drive) Tugs of 70 
T Bollard Pull Power . These tugs will be constructed in accordance with the Approved 
Standard Tug Design and Specifications (ASTDS) promulgated by the Ministry of Ports, 
Shipping & Waterways (MoPSW) , Government of India. The Tug deliveries will commence 
from November 2028 and will be completed by June  2029.  
2. None of the promoter/ promoter group/ group companies have any interest in the entity 
that awarded the order. Further, the said order also does not fall under the purview of related 
party transactions .  
3. The above is for your information and record please .
