 Thomas Cook India & SOTC Travel Partner with DCT Abu Dhabi to offer 
complimentary UAE entry visas for eligible Indian travellers   
The partnership reinforces Abu Dhabi's commitment to the Indian market while making holidays 
to the destination more accessible through a limited- period Visa Subsidy Programme  
 
Mumbai, August 14, 2026: Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India's leading omnichannel travel services 
company, and its Group Company, SOTC Travel, have partnered with the Department of Culture and Tourism 
– Abu Dhabi (DCT Abu Dhabi) under the destination's Visa Subsidy Programme to make holidays to Abu Dhabi 
more accessible for Indian travellers.  
 
As select travel partners for the programme, under which DCT Abu Dhabi covers the full cost of UAE entry visas for eligible bookings, the companies will enable eligible customers booking leisure holiday packages with a minimum continuous three- night hotel stay in Abu Dhabi to avail of a complimentary UAE entry visa at no 
additional cost. The offer is applicable for eligible bookings made between 1 August and 31 October 2026, for travel until 31 October 2026, and is subject to applicable terms and conditions . 
 
The initiative reinforces the long- standing partnership between DCT Abu Dhabi, Thomas Cook India and SOTC 
Travel, reflecting a shared commitment to creating greater value for Indian travellers. By combining destination -
led initiatives with the companies' extensive omnichannel reach and holiday expertise, the collaboration aims 
to drive awareness and encourage more Indian travellers to experience Abu Dhabi's unique blend of cultural 
heritage, world- class attractions, family entertainment, adventure, luxury hospitality and immersive 
experiences.  
 
As one of India's largest source markets for international travel, the country continues to play a key role in Abu Dhabi's tourism growth strategy. Through strategic collaborations with leading travel partners such as Thomas 
Cook India and SOTC Travel, Abu Dhabi continues to strengthen its engagement with the Indian market by 
making travel planning more seamless, accessible and rewarding.  
 
Mr. Rajeev Kale, President & Country Head –  Holidays, MICE, Visa, Thomas Cook (India) Limited,  said, 
“Our long- standing partnership with Abu Dhabi has enabled us to consistently curate enriching holiday 
experiences for Indian travellers. We are delighted to be among the select travel partners for Abu Dhabi's Visa Subsidy Programme, which further enhances t he value proposition for our customers. By offering a 
complimentary UAE entry visa
  for eligible leisure holiday bookings, we are making it even more compelling for 
Indian travellers to explore Abu Dhabi's diverse experiences —from iconic landmarks and cultural attractions to 
family entertainment, adventure and luxury."  
 
Mr. SD Nandakumar, President & Country Head –  Holidays & Corporate Tours, SOTC Travel, said,  "Indian 
travellers today seek holidays that combine enriching experiences with exceptional value. Abu Dhabi continues 
to enjoy strong appeal across families, couples, young professionals and groups, thanks to its diverse tourism 
offerings and year -round at tractions. We are delighted to partner with DCT Abu Dhabi on this initiative, enabling 
eligible leisure travellers to enjoy the added value of a complimentary UAE entry visa  . We look forward to 
inspiring more Indian travellers to choose Abu Dhabi for their next international holiday."
