2. Re-appointment of Mr.  Desh Deepak Verma as an Independent Director  
 
Recommended the re -appointment of Mr. Desh Deepak Verma (DIN: 09393549 ) as a n 
Independent Director of the Company for  a second term of 5 consecutive years with effect 
from 2 1st July, 2025 , to the Members of the Company . 
 
Mr. Desh Deepak Verma, aged about 71 years, is a post graduate in Physics from 
Allahabad University and an MBA from Australia in International Business,  is a retired 1978 
batch IAS Officer. He retired from the post of Secretary, Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs, 
Government of India, New Delhi in June, 2013. Post retirement, he was appointed as the 
Chairman of U.P. Electricity Regulatory Commission. He co ntinued in this post for 4 years 
and subsequently served as the Secretary General, Rajya Sabha from September, 2017 to 
August, 2021.  
 
Mr. Verma is not debarred from holding the office of Director by virtue of any SEBI Order or 
any such authority.
