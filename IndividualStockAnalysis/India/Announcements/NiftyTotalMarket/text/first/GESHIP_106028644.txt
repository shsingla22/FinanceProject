PRESS RELEASE
G E Shipping delivers its 2011 built Supramax Dry Bulk Carrier 
“Jag Rani” 
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (G E Shipping) delivered its 2011 built Supramax Dry Bulk 
Carrier “Jag Rani” to the buyers. The vessel was contracted for sale in August 2024. 
Excluding Jag Rani, company’s current fleet stands at 41 vessels, comprising 28 tankers (6 Crude carriers, 
18 Product carriers, 4 LPG carriers) and 13 dry bulk carriers (2 Capesize, 8 Kamsarmax, 3 Supramax) 
aggregating 3.31 mn dwt. 
Additionally, the company has contracted to sell one Suezmax crude tanker in Sep 2024, which is due for 
delivery by Q3 FY25.
