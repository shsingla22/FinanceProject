3 Brief profile (in case of

 Dr. Ashutosh Raghuvanshi, a highly accomplished 
healthcare leader with over 35+ years of experience, 
currently serves as the Managing Director & Chief 
Executive Officer of the Company.  
 
Dr. Ashutosh Raghuvanshi's journey reflects his 
multifaceted role as a distinguished cardiac surgeon, 
seasoned healthcare administrator, and visionary 
leader. His journey from a successful clinician to a 
dynamic management professional has significantly 
contributed to the success and growth of major 
healthcare institutions in India. Before joining Fortis 
Healthcare, Dr. Raghuvanshi spent over 18 years 
(2000 - 2018) with Narayana Health, a leading 
healthcare chain operating over 28 hospitals. His 
journey also includes significant roles at esteemed 
institutions such as Bombay Hospital, Apollo 
Hospitals, Vijaya Heart Foundation, and Manipal 
Heart Foundation. 
 
Dr. Ashutosh Raghuvanshi actively engages in 
industry forums such as CII, FICCI, Healthcare 
Sector Skill Council and NATHEALTH. Dr. 
Raghuvanshi has contributed significantly to the 
healthcare sector's growth. He is also a past President 
of NATHEALTH – an apex body representing the 
entire ecosystem of the Indian healthcare industry.
