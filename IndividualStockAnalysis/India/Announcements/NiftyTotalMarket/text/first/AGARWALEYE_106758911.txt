PRESS RELEASE  
 
Hon’ble Chief Minister  of Tamil Nadu,  Thiru C. Joseph Vijay Inaugurates 
the World’s First  Pinhole Pupilloplasty Centre at Dr Agarwals Eye 
Hospital, TTK road, Alwarpet, Chennai  
 
➢ The advanced procedure was developed by Prof. Dr. Amar Agarwal, which has 
provided vision to over 5000 patients so far  
 
➢ New centre  launched during the 41st National Eye Donation Fortnight,  to advance 
tissue -preserving treatment for selected patients with corneal scars, irregular 
corneas and complex optical aberrations  
 
Chennai, August 27, 2026:  The Hon’ble Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, Thiru C. Joseph 
Vijay , today inaugurated the  World’s First  Pinhole Pupilloplasty Centre at Dr Agarwals 
Eye Hospital,  TTK Road,  Alwarpet, Chennai , in the presence of Dr Amar Agarwal, Dr 
Manoj Khatri  and many other important dignitaries, marking an important step in 
advancing access to innovative, tissue -preserving solutions for selected patients 
affecte d by complex corneal conditions. The initiative also complements the ongoing 41st 
National Eye Donation Fortnight , reinforcing the importance of exploring innovative 
approaches to restoring vision while preserving precious donor tissue.  
 
The dedicated centre will focus on Pinhole Pupilloplasty (PPP), a microsurgical 
technique, developed by Dr Amar Agarwal , designed to improve the quality of vision in 
appropriately selected patients with conditions such as corneal scars, irregular corneas, 
high corneal aberrations and certain post -surgical optical distortions. The inauguration 
places renewed focus on an imp ortant challenge in ophthalmology , restoring useful 
vision to patients with complex corneal optics while preserving the natural cornea 
whenever clinically possible.  
 
Instead of replacing the cornea, PPP modifies the eye's optical system by creating a 
small, precisely centred pupillary aperture. The smaller aperture helps block scattered 
and distorted light rays a nd allows better -focused  rays to reach the retina. This pinhole 
effect can reduce the impact of higher -order optical aberrations and thereby improve 
functional vision and visual quality.  
 
Importantly, the procedure does not require donor corneal tissue, making it particularly 
relevant in the context of the global shortage of donor corneas. A landmark international 
survey on corneal transplantation and eye banking estimated that approximatel y 12.7 
million people worldwide were awaiting corneal transplantation, while only one donor 
cornea was available for every 70 people in need. The survey further reported that 
 
approximately 185,000 corneal transplants were performed annually across 116 
countries, highlighting the significant gap between global demand and donor tissue 
availability.  
 
Prof. Dr. Amar Agarwal, Chairman, Dr Agarwals Eye Hospital , said: "We are 
immensely thankful to the Honourable Chief Minister, Thiru. C. Joseph Vijay, for 
inaugurating  the PPP Centre. The inauguration of a dedicated Pinhole Pupilloplasty 
Centre is an important milestone because it will enhance the access and wide 
adaptation of this path -breaking surgical innovation, thus providing vision to many”  He 
further added, "The PPP Centre enables  us to create a highly structured pathway, from 
detailed corneal diagnostics and optical assessment to surgery and long -term outcome 
monitoring. It will also become a platform for teaching ophthalmologists how to identify 
the right patient for the right pr ocedure."  
 
Dr. Manoj Khatri, Director, Vitreo -Retinal Services, Dr Agarwals Eye Hospital , said, 
"Innovation becomes truly meaningful when it moves from a scientific idea to a solution 
that changes a patient's everyday life. We firmly believe that the Honourable Chief 
Minister’s presence here at the inauguration will further this cause, and take a 
remarkable innovation to benefit millions of patients worldwide”  
 
The hospital has also been working to expand knowledge of the technique among 
ophthalmologists, with more than 50 ophthalmologists undergoing training through 
workshops and academic programmes every year. The Pinhole Pupilloplasty Centre at 
Alwarpet will s erve not merely as a surgical facility but as a comprehensive programme 
encompassing advanced diagnostics, patient selection, surgery, postoperative 
assessment, long -term follow -up, research and ophthalmic training.  
 
The inauguration also builds on Dr Agarwals Eye Hospital's recent community eye -health 
initiatives in Tamil Nadu. Earlier, to mark the birthday of Hon’ble Chief Minister Thiru C. 
Joseph Vijay, Dr Agarwals Eye Hospital conducted a large -scale, one -day eye -screening 
initiative for schoolchildren across Tamil Nadu. More than 16,000 students were 
screened in a single day, underscoring the importance of early detection and timely eye 
care among children.  
 
The launch of the Pinhole Pupilloplasty Centre at Dr Agarwals Eye Hospital, Alwarpet, by 
the Hon’ble Chief Minister, Thiru. C. Joseph Vijay, therefore marks more than the opening 
of a specialised clinical facility. It represents a step towards a future in which advanced 
optics, microsurgery, tissue preservat ion and precision patient selection work together 
to help more people see.
