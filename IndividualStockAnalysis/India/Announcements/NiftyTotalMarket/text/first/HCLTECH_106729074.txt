HCLTech named OpenAI Advanced Partner  
  
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, Aug 6, 2026 —HCLTech , a leading global technology 
company, today  announced that it has achieved OpenAI Advanced Partner  status  within 
the OpenAI Partner Network .  
 
The OpenAI Partner Network is a global program for partners to build, sell, and deliver AI 
solutions with OpenAI. It brings together partners with deep industry expertise, delivery 
capabilities and customer relationships while equipping them with resources , enablement 
and support to help enterprises adopt OpenAI frontier models and products and turn 
them into measurable impact.  
 
As an OpenAI Advanced Partner, HCLTech will continue working with OpenAI to help 
organizations build, deploy and scale AI -native Solutions responsibly, securely and at 
enterprise scale. HCLTech brings deep AI expertise across industries through its portfol io 
of sector -specific Industry AI Solutions , helping enterprises use GPT -5.6 and ChatGPT 
Work to accelerate adoption and drive measurable business value.  
 
HCLTech’s AI -powered service transformation platform, AI Force , is also integrated with 
OpenAI models to help enterprises accelerate productivity, enhance service experiences 
and drive greater value from their AI investments.  
 As one of the Global Systems Integrators selected by OpenAI as a partner, HCLTech 
continues to collaborate closely with OpenAI to help enterprises accelerate adoption and 
maximize business value from their AI investments.  
 
“We’re pleased to welcome HCLTech as an OpenAI Advanced Partner. The HCLTech team 
brings practical enterprise AI delivery experience across strategy, implementation and 
adoption,” said Colleen Kapase , Vice President of Strategic Global Partnerships and  
Ecosystems, OpenAI . “Through the OpenAI Partner Network, HCLTech can help more 
enterprises move from AI pilots to measurable business impact.”  
 
“Being recognized as an OpenAI Advanced Partner reflects the strength of our strategic 
collaboration with OpenAI. Together, we are helping clients transition into new Agentic 
Operating model across values streams by building, deploying and scaling industrialized 
Enterprise AI operating systems and control towers to unlock measurable business value 
with AI,” said Raghu Kidambi, Corporate Vice President, AI Partnerships .
