We are pleased to inform you, in accordance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, that Apollo Micro Systems Limited is shortlisted keeping in 
view the capability and was awarded Make II project by the Indian Army.  
 
This project is for the procurement of a Vehicle Mounted Counter Swarm Drone System (VMCSDS) 
(Version I) under the Make II category of DAP-2020.  
 
This project is very prestigious and first Make II project for the Company. The company is happy to share 
that the systems developed out of it are ‘state of the art’ and highly futuristic in nature. 
 
As a Make II project, there will be no cost obligation involved.
