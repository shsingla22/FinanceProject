assemblies, friction stir welding and surface 
treatments.

manufacturing site of the Company.  
3. Proposed capacity  installation 900 MT castings parts per month.  
4. Period within which the 
proposed capacity is to be 
added The start of production is envisaged in Q1 of 
financial year 2025-26.

is expected to be completed in stages by 
March, 2028.

5. Investment required ~Rs. 4,009 million.  
6. Mode of financing Internal accruals. 
7. Rationale To meet increased demand for the products
