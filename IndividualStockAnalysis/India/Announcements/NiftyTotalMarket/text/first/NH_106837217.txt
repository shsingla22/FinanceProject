Q2 FY27 AT A GLANCE  
■ Strong earnings growth:  Consolidated EBITDA rose 42.0% YoY and 19.8% QoQ to ₹ 6,054 Mn, and PAT rose 16.6% 
YoY and 45.3% QoQ to ₹ 3,012 Mn.  
■ Robust underlying business:  Excluding the UK, revenue grew 22.6% YoY to ₹ 20,156 Mn and EBITDA grew 27.8% to 
₹ 5,452 Mn, with the margin expanding to 27.0% from 25.9%.  
■ India – volume, mix and margin expansion:  India revenue grew 16.6% YoY, driven by higher patient volumes, 
complex procedures and a 75.7% rise in international patient revenue; India hospitals EBITDA margin expanded to 
26.8% from 23.8% a year ago.  
■ Cayman – sharp profitability improvement:  Cayman EBITDA (hospital and insurance) rose to US$ 22.1 Mn from 
US$ 17.9 Mn a year ago, as the insurance loss narrowed to US$ 1.1 Mn from US$ 3.7 Mn in Q1 FY27.  
■ Expanding capacity in core clusters:  The Banashankari hospital in Bengaluru commenced operations and a 120 -bed 
hospital at Park Street, Kolkata was added, taking the pipeline to about 1,555 beds and the bed capacity target to 
over 7,800.  
■ Strong balance sheet:  Net debt stood at ₹ 18,830 Mn as on 30 September 2026, a net debt to equity ratio of 0.38, 
down from 0.42 at 30 June 2026.

■ Consolidated:  Operating revenue was ₹ 28,440 Mn, up 73.0% YoY (largely due to the UK acquisition) and 6.0% QoQ. 
EBITDA was ₹ 6,054 Mn, up 42.0% YoY and 19.8% QoQ, and PAT was ₹ 3,012 Mn (10.6% margin), up 16.6% YoY and 
45.3% QoQ.  
■ Excluding the UK:  Operating revenue  was ₹ 20,156 Mn, up 8.5% QoQ, EBITDA was ₹ 5,452 Mn, up 26.1% QoQ, and 
PAT was ₹ 3,359 Mn (16.7% margin), up 42.6% QoQ.  
■ India:  Operating revenue was ₹ 14,402 Mn, up 16.6% YoY and 8.7% QoQ. EBITDA was ₹ 3,548 Mn, up 30.8% YoY 
and 22.3% QoQ, and PAT was ₹ 1,899 Mn (13.2% margin), up 36.2% YoY and 44.2% QoQ.  
■ Cayman Islands:  Operating revenue was US$ 63.4 Mn, up 28.2% YoY and 8.9% QoQ. EBITDA was US$ 22.1 Mn, up 
23.8% YoY and 46.4% QoQ, and PAT was US$ 16.6 Mn (26.2% margin), up 20.9% YoY and 62.9% QoQ.  
■ United Kingdom:  Operating revenue was GBP 64.4 Mn, down 0.9% QoQ. EBITDA was GBP 4.7 Mn, down 18.5% 
QoQ, and the net loss was GBP 2.7 Mn ( -4.2% PAT margin), compared with a loss of GBP 2.2 Mn in Q1 FY27.  
MANAGEMENT COMMENT  
Commenting on the performance, Dr. Emmanuel Rupert, Managing Director and Group CEO, Narayana 
Hrudayalaya Limited , said:  
“Q2 FY27 has been a strong quarter for the Group, with consolidated EBITDA growing 42% YoY and margins 
improving sequentially in India and Cayman. India delivered robust growth, driven by higher patient volumes, a 
richer mix of high -end and complex procedu res which also translated into healthy margin expansion. During the 
quarter, we commissioned our Banashankari hospital in Bengaluru and signed an asset -light agreement for a 120 -
bed hospital at Park Street, Kolkata, further strengthening our presence in ou r core clusters. Our Integrated Care 
ecosystem continues to deepen patient engagement, with clinic footfalls up over 25% YoY and improving claims 
experience in our insurance business.  
Our Cayman business continued its strong performance, with healthy growth in the hospitals business and the One 
Health insurance platform significantly narrowing its losses, resulting in a marked improvement in overall 
profitability. Our UK business delive red stable revenues, with margins reflecting near -term separation -related costs 
that we expect to normalize over time. We remain focused on driving operational efficiencies and growing our self -
pay and insured patient business to strengthen margins. With a  strong first half behind us, we thank the investor 
community for their continued faith and remain confident of delivering on our expectations for the year.”  
BUSINESS UNIT PERFORMANCE  
India Hospitals  
India revenue (excluding the ATHMA and Medha businesses, net of inter -company eliminations) was ₹ 14,388 Mn, up 
16.7% YoY and 9.0% QoQ. Domestic revenue was ₹ 13,442 Mn, including ₹ 186 Mn from clinics, and international patient 
revenue rose to ₹ 901 Mn, u p 75.7% YoY and 41.2% QoQ.  
By specialty, cardiac sciences remained the largest at about 33% of India hospital revenue, followed by oncology (15%), 
medicine and gastro sciences (14%), renal sciences (9%), neurosciences (8%) and orthopaedics (5%). By payor, self  
paying domestic patients contributed about 43%, insured and corporate patients 33%, government schemes (CGHS, 
ESIS and state schemes) 18% and international patients 7%.

Bengaluru (NICS, MSMC, HSR) is the largest cluster at 37% of India hospital revenue, growing 18% YoY, followed by 
Kolkata (RTIICS, Barasat, NMH, NSH) at 26% and 16% growth. North (Gurugram, New Delhi, Jaipur) contributed 13% 
with 12% growth, Eastern Periph eral (Jamshedpur, Guwahati, Raipur) 10% with 16% growth, Southern Peripheral 
(Mysore, Shimoga, Davangere, Dharwad, Kolar) 9% with 10% growth, and West (Mumbai, Ahmedabad) 5% with 12% 
growth.  
India hospitals EBITDA rose to ₹ 3,799 Mn at a 26.8% margin, from ₹ 2,903 Mn (23.8%) in Q2 FY26 and ₹ 3,229 Mn 
(24.8%) in Q1 FY27; including clinics, the margin was 26.0%. EBITDA margin excluding other income improved to 23.6% 
from 22.2% a year ago, helped  by lower doctor and nurse costs (25.9% of revenue vs 26.7%) and consumables (22.2% 
vs 23.4%), partly offset by higher other expenses (18.3% vs 17.1%).  
India Clinics (Integrated Care)  
Narayana Health operated 12 integrated care clinics as on 30 September 2026, with the Nagarabhavi Clinic in Bengaluru 
opening in the last week of September. Clinic outpatient footfalls rose 27% YoY to about 81,000, and clinic revenue rose 
19% YoY to ₹ 186 Mn. 
Insurance  
India:  NH Insurance wrote ₹ 78 Mn of gross premium in Q2 FY27, up from ₹ 70 Mn a year ago. Net incurred claims of ₹ 
211 Mn against net earned premium of ₹ 133 Mn gave a claims ratio of 159%; the loss after tax was ₹ 151 Mn (₹ 42 Mn 
in Q2 FY26) and the solvency r atio improved to 162% from 155%.  
Cayman (CIHL):  Insurance revenue was US$ 16.8 Mn, up 80.2% YoY and 8.8% QoQ, and EBITDA was a loss of US$ 1.1 
Mn, narrowing from a loss of US$ 3.7 Mn in Q1 FY27.  
Cayman Islands  
Cayman hospital revenue was US$ 49.3 Mn, up 18.4% YoY and 9.9% QoQ. Combined hospital and insurance EBITDA rose 
to US$ 22.1 Mn from US$ 17.9 Mn a year ago and US$ 15.1 Mn last quarter; outpatient visits were 12,979 (up 5.5% YoY) 
and discharges were 823 (up  4.8% YoY and 5.5% QoQ). Reported Cayman revenue is shown after removing US$ 2.7 Mn 
of inter -company transactions.  
United Kingdom  
UK revenue was GBP 64.4 Mn, broadly flat QoQ (down 0.9%). EBITDA after IFRS 16 was GBP 4.7 Mn (7.2% margin) 
against GBP 5.7 Mn (8.8%) in Q1 FY27, and EBITDA before IFRS 16 was GBP 1.9 Mn; these figures exclude one -time 
acquisition costs and include GBP 0.8  Mn of losses at the new centre.  
CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE  
Higher depreciation and amortisation (₹ 1,629 Mn) and finance costs (₹ 967 Mn), mainly from the UK acquisition, meant 
profit before tax grew 16.2% YoY to ₹ 3,433 Mn and PAT grew 16.6% to ₹ 3,012 Mn. Total comprehensive income for 
the quarter was ₹ 3,632 Mn .

ABOUT NARAYANA HRUDAYALAYA LIMITED  
Founded in 2000 by Dr. Devi Prasad Shetty and headquartered in Bengaluru, Narayana Hrudayalaya Limited is one of 
the leading healthcare service providers, operating a chain of multispecialty, tertiary, and primary healthcare facilities. 
As of 30 September 2026, the Group operates 56 healthcare facilities across India, the Cayman Islands and the United 
Kingdom, with 6,119 operational beds, a total capacity of 6,374 beds and about 21,150 employees. In India it is present 
across Bengaluru, Mysore, Shimoga, Dav angere, Kolkata, Jamshedpur, Guwahati, Raipur, Delhi NCR, Jaipur, Ahmedabad 
and Mumbai; in the Cayman Islands it runs Health City Cayman Islands and Health City Camana Bay; and in the UK it 
runs hospitals, surgical centres and urgent treatment centres acro ss England.
