HCLTech recognized as a Leader in four ISG Provider Lens® SAP Ecosystem 2026 
quadrants  
 
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, June 23, 2026 — HCLTech, a leading global technology company,  
has been recognized as a Leader in four quadrants of the ISG Provider Lens® SAP Ecosystem 2026 
assessment for the U .S. region. 
 
The recognition highlights  HCLTech’s  comprehensive service portfolio, strong market presence, 
innovative strength and competitive stability  in SAP S/4HANA system transformation (large 
accounts), SAP application managed services (AMS), SAP Business AI and Business Technology 
Platform (BTP) services and managed cloud services for SAP ERP . 
 
This positioning reflects HCLTech’s ability to support the growing enterprise shift toward AI -first, 
clean-core transformation focused on measurable outcomes  — accelerating S/4HANA adoption 
with lower risk, improving efficiency through automation and enabling AI -led business operations 
through platforms such as AI Force. Its strength in managing secure, resilient SAP environments 
across hybrid and cloud landscapes helps sustain these gains, allowing enterprises to derive 
consistent value from their SAP investments.  
 
“HCLTech  helps enterprises realize the full value of their SAP transformations , driving long ‑term 
outcomes and building more adaptable, data ‑driven organizations. By combining deep  industry SAP 
expertise with AI capabilities  and clean-core strategies , , we enable clients to navigate change with 
confidence and deliver sustained business value, ” said Praveen Bhat , EVP, Global Head, SAP,  
HCLTech .
