Dear Sir/Madam, 
We wish to inform you that Dalmia Cement (Bharat) Limited (“ DCBL”), a wholly owned subsidiary 
of the Company, has received orders on February 5, 2025 under Section 74 of the Central Goods 
and Services Tax Act, 2017 from the Assistant Commissioner of CGST, New Delhi, and the 
Assistant Commissioner, Central Tax and Central Excise, Belagavi, Karnataka, confirming demand 
of GST under Reverse Charge Mechanism and denial of Input Tax Credit, respectively, for the 
financial years 2017-21. The authority has confirmed the demand of tax along with interest and 
penalty. 
DCBL has a good case on merits to defend the matters before the Appellate Authorities and will 
file appeal/writ petition against the said orders.
