Engineer (Er.) Shailendra Shukla  
 
A distinguished engineering professional with over 
42 years of experience, Er. Shailendra Shukla has 
held leadership roles for 24 years including as a 
Chairman, CEO, MD, and Director in the Power, 
Renewable Energy, and Biofuel sectors. An 
alumnus of Jawaharlal Nehru University (1982 
batch), he has been instrumental in shaping energy 
policies an d projects at national and international 
levels.  
 
Er. Shukla has chaired major energy companies, 
including the Western Regional Power Company 
and Chhattisgarh State Power Companies, 
overseeing power generation, transmission, and 
distribution. His contribut ions to biofuels and solar 
energy have led to joint ventures with Indian Oil 
(IOCL) and Hindustan Petroleum (HPCL). He has 
also played a pivotal role in developing India's 
National Coal Index and contributed to high -impact 
research and technical papers in renewable energy.  
 
Recognised globally, Er. Shukla has been awarded 
the Zayed Future Energy Prize, India Pride Award, 
and numerous honours for his pioneering work in 
renewable energy, biofuels, and solar technology. 
His expertise has been acknowledged by D r. A.P.J. 
Abdul Kalam, and he has been featured in 
international media, including BBC, CNN, and 
Discovery Channel.  
 
Currently, Er. Shukla is actively involved in Project 
Management Consultancy (PMC) for MW -scale 
solar projects. Additionally, he has contrib uted to the 
field of solar energy by authoring several technical 
papers, further cementing his thought leadership in 
the industry.
