Sub: Declaration of COD of third part capacity of 32.90 MW out of 300 MW Shambu ki Burj -2 Solar PV 
Project of NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL) 
 
Consequent upon successful commissioning and due approvals, third part capacity of 32.90 MW out of 300 MW 
Shambu ki Burj-2 (Kolayat) Solar PV Project at Bikaner, Rajasthan of NTPC Green Energy Limited (a wholly-
owned subsidiary of NTPC Limited), is declared on Commercial Operation w.e.f. 00:00 Hrs. of 31.10.2024.  
 
The first part capacity of 150 MW and second part capacity of 98.78 MW has already been declared on 
commercial operation w.e.f. 00:00 Hrs. of 29.09.2022 and 23:00 Hrs. of 30.09.2024 respectively.   
 
With this, the total installed and commercial capacity of NTPC group has now become 76475.68 MW.
