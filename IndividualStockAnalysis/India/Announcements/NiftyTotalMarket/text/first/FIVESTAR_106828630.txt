Commenting on the results, Mr Lakshmipathy Deenadayalan, Chairman & Managing Director, said,  
 
I am extremely happy to present to all of you, the details about another quarter of strong performance of Five -Star 
Business Finance Limited (“Five Star” or “Company”). The quarter ended September 30, 2026 , saw Five Star 
delivering very strong numbers across various parameters.  
 
Our upward trajectory in disbursements continued during the current quarter as well, where we recorded our 
historically best disbursements at INR 2,078 Cr, up 39% over the previous quarter and 74% year -on-year. The loan 
book also saw robust growth with our  AUM at INR 14,640 Cr, a growth of 7% over the previous quarter and 14% 
year -on-year. Importantly, this growth has come alongside improving collections , stable asset quality and 
profitability.  
 
Collections were encouraging across buckets during the quarter, and the following metrics bring out the 
improvement:  
 
1. Our unique customer collection efficiency (excluding NPAs) improv ed to 98.2% in the current quarter 
from 97.9% in Q1FY27 . 
 

2. X-bucket collections improved to 99.4% from 99.2% in Q1FY27, which has helped contain forward flows. 
As a result, the current bucket proportion of our AUM rose sharply from 83.30% to 84.78%, and 30+ DPD 
came down from 12.38% to 11.50%.  
3. Our slippage ratio (defined as increase in NPAs + write offs as a % of the opening standard AUM) eased 
marginally to 0.68% from 0.70% in Q1FY27. Gross Stage 3 assets were broadly stable at 3.48%, despite 
macroeconomic headwinds being witnessed across the industry.  
4. Credit cost improved to 1.80% of average AUM from 1.85% in Q1FY27. We expect flattish credit 
slippages and credit cost for the rest of the year, despite the industry headwinds.  
 
With our collection framework delivering better than expected results, we continue to focus on bringing back the 
growth momentum Five Star is known for.  Disbursements for H1FY27 stood at ₹3,574 Cr, up 44% year -on-year. 
AUM grew 7% sequentially, a step -up from the 4% growth in Q1, and 14% year -on-year to ₹14,640 Cr. With strong 
collections, controlled slippages and credit cost  and  no material impact of the macroeconomic scenario, we are 
well poised to continue our strong traction in disbursements in the coming quarters, which will help us cross our 
growth guidance for this year very comfortably.  
 
We availed incremental debt of ₹825 Cr during the quarter at an all -inclusive cost of 8.43%, and our cost of funds 
on the book eased marginally to 8.78% from 8.80% in Q1FY27. Net Interest Margin remained broadly stable at 
19.87% as against 19.97% in Q1FY27  and was 16 bps higher year -on-year, showing that our spreads have held up 
despite the drop in yields. With the tightening rate cycle, we expect to see some inch -up in our cost of funds, both 
on incremental debt and on the book, which would have some impac t on the spread and NIM. However, we expect 
to cushion this impact through yield increases on our incremental disbursements, which have been made effective 
from October 2026.  
 
Further, through the operating leverage that we will be able to achieve in the coming years resulting from increased 
productivity, higher growth, tighter control over operational and credit cost, we are confident of largely offsetting 
the hawkish stance of  the RBI expected for the next few quarters. This would ensure our RoA remains flattish 
except for the impact of leverage.  
 
We achieved a PAT of ₹299 Cr for the quarter, up 10% sequentially and 5% year -on-year. This includes a one -time 
tax benefit of ₹1 9 Cr pertaining to prior years. RoAUM improved by 28 bps to 8.39% and RoE by 79 bps to 15.25% 
over the previous quarter. PAT for H1FY27 was ₹571 Cr, up 3% year -on-year. As is our philosophy, we will continue 
to pursue strong yet sustainable growth, quality and profitability through robus t credit underwriting, strong 
collections and proactive risk management, backed by appropriate technology and AI and supported by a 
diversified and cost -effective funding profile.  
 
After a year beset with challenges across multiple fronts, we have bounced back stronger than before. Some of 
these numbers across disbursements and collections are our historical best and clearly validate the actions we 
have taken over the last few quarte rs; they also strengthen our confidence that we are moving in the direction of 
“Onwards and Upwards”, well poised to achieve many more milestones in the quarters to come.

About  Five -Star Business Finance Limited:  (www.fivestargroup.in ; BSE:  543663;  NSE: FIVESTAR)  
 
Five -Star Business Finance Limited is a Non -Banking Finance Company (NBFC -ND-SI) registered with Reserve 
Bank of India with a network of 875 branches catering to around 5 10,000 active live accounts in Tamil Nadu 
(including the Union Territory of Puducherry), Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, 
Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Rajasthan and Gujarat. The Company primarily offers small loans for 
business purposes, asset creation and other economic purposes to small business customers and self -employed 
individuals after due underwriting of their cashflows and backed by the collateral of their house prope rty.
