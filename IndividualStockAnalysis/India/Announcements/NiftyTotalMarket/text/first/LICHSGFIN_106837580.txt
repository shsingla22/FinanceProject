30, 2026, following its approval by the board of directors in the meeting held in Mumbai on October 
30, 2026. 
 
The results  are as per the  Indian  Accounting  Standards  i.e., IndAS  notifications  issued  by the   
Ministry  of Corporate  Affairs  and National  Housing  Bank in April 2018.

Performance highlights for the quarter ended September 30 , 202 6 
 
Total  disbursements  were  at Rs. 18127  crs in Q2 FY202 7, as against  Rs. 16313 crs for the 
corresponding  period  in Q2 FY202 6 up by 11%. Out of this, disbursements  in the  Retail  Loan  
segment  were  at Rs 17563  crs against Rs 15935  crs in Q2  FY202 6, a growth of 10%. Project  loans  
were  at Rs. 564 crs as compared  with Rs 378 crs for the same quarter in the previous year , up by 49% . 
 
The company’s total income  for the Q2 FY202 7 quarter was Rs. 7183 crs as compared to Rs  7080 
crs during the same quarter in the previous year . The company's  revenue  from operations  grew to 
Rs. 7175 crs as against Rs. 7074 crs in Q2 FY202 6. 
 
 
 
 
  
 
Net Interest  Income  (NII)  stood at  Rs. 2088 crs, as against  Rs 2038 crs for the same  period  in the 
previous  year. Net   interest  margin (NIM)  for the quarter  stood  at 2.58 % as against  2.62%  for Q2 FY 
2026 and 2. 58 % for Q1 FY202 7. 
 
Profit  Before  Tax for the quarter  was Rs. 1918.55  crs as against  Rs 1704.71 crs in Q2 FY202 6, a growth 
of 13 %. Net profit after tax stood at Rs. 1508.78  crs, as compared with Rs 1353.87 crs during the same 
period in the previous year, show ing a growth of 11%. 
 
The Loan  Portfolio  stood at Rs. 326173  crs as against Rs 311816 crs on September 30, 202 5, a 
growth of 5 %. Out of which Retail  Loans  grew by 4% from Rs 303084  crs to Rs. 316433  crs as on 
September 30, 202 6, whereas t he Project loan portfolio  stood at Rs. 9740 crs as on September  30, 
2026, as against Rs 8732 crs as on September  30, 2025, up by 12%. 
 
Under  IndAS  109, asset classification  and provisioning  changes  for future  credit  loss are reported  on an  
Expected  Credit Loss (ECL)  basis.  As per the same methodology, the  provisions for ECL stood at Rs. 
4292 crs with a coverage of 49% on Stage 3  as on September 30, 202 6, as against Rs 5074 crs as on 
September 30, 202 5. 
 
The Stage 3 exposure on default as on September 30, 202 6 stood at 2.05% as against  2.51 % as on  
September 30, 2025 and 2.14 % as on June 30, 202 6.
