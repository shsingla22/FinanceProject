Gland Pharma Reports  Yet Another Quarter with Strong Revenue  Growth  
Revenue Growth @ 23% Y-o-Y | PAT Growth @  58% Y-o-Y 
 
Hyderabad, November  6th, 202 6: Gland Pharma Limited (BSE: 543245 | NSE: GLAND), a n injectable -focused 
pharmaceutical company, announced its financial results for the second  quarter  ended September  30th, 202 6. 
 
Commenting on the results , Mr. Srinivas Sadu, Executive Chairman of Gland Pharma , stated,  “We delivered 
another quarter of strong growth, with revenue increasing 23% year -on-year and profit after tax growing 58% 
year-on-year, driven by strong execution across our businesses. Our CDMO business grew 31% year -on-
year, supported by expanding custome r engagements, strategic partnerships and a robust opportunity 
pipeline. With continued investments in manufacturing capabilities, innovation and capacity expansion, we 
remain well positioned to capitalize on growing global outsourcing opportunities and cr eate sustainable 
long -term value for shareholders. ”

Business Highlights:  
 
• R&D Expenses: Total R&D expenses were ₹1, 077 million in Q2 FY27, representing 6% of consolidated 
revenue , as against ₹817 million in Q2 FY2 6. The R&D expenditure was mainly focused on complex 
product development and the number of filings.  
• New Launches: The company launched 4 molecules in the USA this quarter, including Sugammadex, 
Erythromycin, Fluorouracil and Atropine sulfate.  
• Filings and Approvals: Eleven ANDAs were filed, and ten were approved in Q2 FY27, contributing to a 
cumulative total of 399 ANDA filings in the U.S. (350 approved, 49 pending).  
• In-house Complex Pipeline: Six products have already been launched, and three more are in line for 
approval. Complex injectables are expected to remain a central pillar of long -term growth, with more 
products being added to the pipeline.  
• Co-development Partnerships: Fifteen products are in co -development (seven 505(b)(2) and eight 
ANDAs), with commercialization anticipated to begin in FY29.  
• Ready -to-Use (RTU) Bags: Filed 24 Ready -to-Use infusion bag products and received approval for 18 so 
far. An additional 8 are currently under development. The total RTU bag portfolio addresses a market 
opportunity of approximately $640 million in the US.  
• CDMO partnership with A Global Pharmaceutical Company: Technology transfer activities are 
advancing according to plan. Further expanded the partnership by adding a few more technology transfer 
dossiers to the list.  
• Strategic CDMO collaborations: Secured manufacturing contracts for microspheres and respiratory 
products.  
• Quality and Compliance: Two of the Gland’s facilities were audited by USFDA. VSEZ had zero 
observations , and Dundigal  concluded  (in October 2026)  with four procedural observations.

About Gland Pharma Limited (BSE: 543245, NSE: GLAND)  
Gland Pharma was established in 1978 in Hyderabad and has grown over the years from a contract manufacturer 
of small -volume liquid parenteral products to become one of the largest and fastest -growing  injectable -focused 
companies, with a global footprint across 60 countries, including the United States, Europe, Canada, Australia, India, 
and other markets. It operates primarily under a Contract Development & Manufacturing Operations (CDMO), 
Business -to-Business (B2B) model and has an excellent track record in the development, manufacturing, and 
marketing of sterile injectables. It has a wide range of injectables, including vials, ampoules, pre -filled syringes, 
lyophilized vials, dry powders, infusions, on cology, and ophthalmic solutions, and also enjoys the distinction of 
having pioneered Heparin technology in India. For more information, log on to: www.glandpharma.com
