For immediate release  
KPI Green  Energy Limited  energises 35 MWp to reach 589 MWp energised capacity out of 
2.17 GWp IPP Portfolio  
KPI Green Energy Limited (“KPI Green” or “the Company”) has recently energised 
an additional  35 MWp of solar capacity, taking its  total operational IPP capacity to 589 MWp . 
With projects currently in hand, the Company  remains  well on track to commission 
an additional  1,582 MWp  in the near term, which will expand its  total IPP portfolio to 2.17  GWp . 
This continued capacity addition further strengthens KPI Green’s position as a leading 
Independent Power Producer (IPP).   
This portfolio underscores a  robust and sustainable annuity revenue  stream from long -term 
contracted projects under Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs), many with tenures of up to 25 
years. It further reinforces the Company’s strategic transition toward an asset -backed 
Independent Power Producer (IPP) model, complemented by its well -established EPC  expertise.   
Key  IPP Project  Developments:   
200 MW/240 MWp  Khavda  Solar IPP Project   
The Company successfully commissioned  and started injecting power from  its 200 MW  (240 
MWp)  solar IPP project at  Khavda  Solar Park (South Block)  in January 2026, making it among 
the early projects operational within the  1.8 GW solar park developed by GSECL . The project is 
connected under a long -term  Power Purchase Agreement with Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam 
Limited (GUVNL)   
50 MW/92.15 MWp Hybrid Renewable Energy IPP   
KPI Green achieved  grid synchronisation and power injection ahead of the scheduled July 
2026 commercial operation date  for its  92.15 MWp hybrid project , comprising  16.95 MW wind 
capacity and 75.2 MWp solar capacity . The early synchronisation  demonstrates  the 
Company’s strong execution capability in complex hybrid renewable projects.   
620 MW/917  MWp Hybrid Renewable Energy IPP   
In October 2024, the Company executed  two long -term PPAs with GUVNL  of renewable 
capacity  comprising:   
• 250 MW/275 MWp  grid -connected solar IPP project   
The company has commissioned  48.4 MW / 69.41 MWp capacity  on a phased basis under 
its 250 MW/350 MWp solar IPP project . The remaining capacity is currently under active 
execution with full completion targeted within the  October 2026 timeline .   
• 370 MW/642 MWp  grid -connected hybrid IPP project   
The  project  capacity is currently under active execution with full completion targeted within 
the October 2026 timeline .  
 
 
 
445 MW / 890 MWh Standalone Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)   
Sun Drops Energia Limited  (Formerly Known as Sun Drops Energia Private Limited) , a KPI Green 
subsidiary focused on energy storage solutions, executed a  Battery Energy Storage Purchase 
Agreement (BESPA) with GUVNL  for 445 MW / 890 MWh of standalone battery energy storage 
systems .  
This marks KPI Green’s entry into  utility -scale energy storage , strengthening the Company’s 
capability to integrate renewable generation with advanced grid -stability and energy -
management solutions.
