As per the provisions of the Act, read with relevant rules thereunder and Regulation 44 of SEBI 
Listing Regulations and applicable Secretarial Standards issued by the Institute of Company 
Secretaries of India (“ICSI”), the Company had provided the facility of e-voting to the Shareholders to

Thursday, August 7, 2025 (5:00 pm). The Company had also provided the e-voting facility at the 
AGM (insta-poll) to those members who did not cast their vote through remote e-voting. 
 
M/s. Ashish Kapoor & Associates, Practicing Company Secretaries was appointed as the Scrutinizer 
for conducting remote e-voting and voting through electronic voting system at AGM in a fair and 
transparent manner. The Scrutinizer have carried out the scrutiny of all the electronic votes received 
through remote e-voting up to 5:00 P.M. on August 7, 2025 and votes received through e-voting till 15 
minutes after the conclusion of the AGM and submitted his report on August 9, 2025.
