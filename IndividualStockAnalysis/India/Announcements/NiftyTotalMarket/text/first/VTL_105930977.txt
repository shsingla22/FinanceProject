In continuation to our earlier intimation dated 9" May, 2024, under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, regarding approval by the Board of 
enhancement and modernization of existing Spinning and Fabric Business capacity of the Company 
and also generation of Green Power with a total capital outlay of Rs. 2,000/- crore (approx.). In this

meeting held on 30" July, 2024 has revised one of the expansion plan of its Spinning Capacity by

56,744 equivalent spindles instead of 50,000 equivalent spindles.

based Boiler Projects. This project is an initiative taken by the Company towards its ESG responsibilities 
and also to cater to requirements of its customers. It will be financed through internal accruals/ debt 
and is expected to be completed within next 2 years. 
This Biomass based Boiler Projects will not have any impact on the Spinning and Fabric capacity of the 
Company which has already been informed vide our above referred letter dated 9" May, 2024. 
The above projects will have additional capital outlay of about Rs. 500 crore. Accordingly, the total 
Capex of the Company will stand enhanced to Rs. 2,500 crore (approx.) instead of Rs. 2,000 crore 
(approx.), as informed earlier.
