PRESS RELEASE 
 
Cyient’s Founder Chairman Dr. B.V.R. Mohan Reddy 
Conferred the “Living Legend Award” by Geospatial World 
Honoured for shaping India’s geospatial trajectory and placing India  
on the global R&D map through geospatial innovation 
 
Hyderabad, India, January 14, 2026:  Cyient, a global Intelligent Engineering 
solutions company , proudly announced that its Founder Chairman, Dr. BVR Mohan 
Reddy, has been conferred the Living Legend Award  by Geospatial World , one of 
the world’s leading geospatial knowledge organisations. The award recognises Dr. 
Reddy’s pioneering leadership and lifetime contribution to advancing the geospatial 
industry in India and globally. 
 
The Living Legend Award  is bestowed upon distinguished visionaries who have 
significantly shaped the global geospatial sector through transformative innovation, 
sustained leadership, and enduring impact. Dr. Reddy was honoured during the 25th 
edition of GeoSmart India , a milestone event that celebrates the evolution and 
maturity of India’s geospatial ecosystem and acknowledges the individuals who have 
played foundational roles in strengthening it. 
 
Over the decades, Dr. Reddy has built Cyient from modest beginnings into one of 
India’s largest and most globally respected geospatial and engineering organisations. 
His entrepreneurial vision and consistent focus on innovation have significantly 
contributed to India's growth in geospatial capabilities, positioning the country as a 
key global hub for engineering and R&D services. 
 
Beyond business, Dr. Reddy has also been widely recognised for nurturing next-
generation leadership at Cyient and for his active mentoring across Indian education 
institutions, start-up ecosystems, and industry platforms. He has proactively 
engaged with government and national initiatives spanning education, the IT and 
engineering industries, and societal development—contributing to India’s broader 
development agenda. 
 
Speaking on the occasion, Dr. BVR Mohan Reddy , Founder Chairman, Cyient, said: 
“I am deeply honoured to receive the Living Legend Award from Geospatial World. 
The geospatial industry has played a transformative role in shaping our 
understanding of and building a better world, and I am proud to have contributed to 
India’s journey in this space. This recognition belongs as much to the many talented 
teams and collaborators I have worked with over the years, and it strengthens my 
commitment to mentoring the next-generation and enabling innovation that benefits 
society.” 
 
Sanjay Kumar , CEO, Geospatial World, said, “Dr BVR Mohan Reddy has been a 
founding pillar of the geospatial industry in India. His entrepreneurial instincts, 
 
resilient determination, methodological approach, collaborative partnership, and 
enabling leadership have been the hallmarks of his professional journey. Dr Reddy’s 
exemplary commitment to national development continues to reflect in his efforts 
towards education, innovation, societal development, and public policy initiatives, 
nurturing the next-generation for a better tomorrow.” 
 
A knowledge organisation with a global presence, Geospatial World  has been 
instrumental in strengthening geospatial ecosystems at the business, national, 
regional, and global levels for over 25 years. Pursuing its vision of “Making a 
Difference through Geospatial Knowledge in the World Economy and Society,”  
Geospatial World continues to advance sustainability and growth through its 
Consulting and Events divisions. 
 
Cyient's Utilities & Spatial Intelligence (USI) business combines 35+ years of 
geospatial expertise with deep utility operations knowledge to transform complex 
spatial data into real-time, decision-ready intelligence. Through advanced GIS, 
AI/ML, and digital platforms, it enables enterprises and governments to modernize 
asset and network management.
