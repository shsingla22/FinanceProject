Consolidated Results for the quarter and year ended 31st March, 2025:

amounting to~ 343 crores from~ 174 crores. Net Profit (after tax) also saw a substantial increase, climbing

leading compani es in the consumer durables within the Tata group and is the undisput ed market leader in 
room air conditioners in India, with a footprint of over 30,000 touch points. Voltas has also launched a wide

with Beko, a leader in Home Appliances in Europe.
