The Phoenix  Mills  Limited  elevates  Shishir  Shrivastava  as Non -executive  Vice  Chairman  
 
Mumbai, India (September  29, 2025): The Phoenix  Mills Limited (“PML” or “the Company”) 
today  announced  the elevation  of Mr. Shishir  Shrivastava  as Non -Executive  Vice  Chairman  of 
the Company, with effect from October 1, 2025, to September 30, 20 27 (both days inclusive) , 
subject to shareholder approval.  
 
Mr. Atul  Ruia,  Chairman,  The Phoenix  Mills  Limited,  said:  
"Shishir has been an integral part of PML’s journey since 1999, and his story is deeply 
intertwined with the company’s growth and evolution. Over more than two decades, his 
leadership,  vision  and unwavering  commitment  have  steered  PML  through  milestone  phases, 
including the resilience he demonstrated during the COVID -19 crisis. He has also been 
instrumental in building trusted partnerships and fostering a culture of excellence. This 
elevation is both a recognition of his outstanding contributions and an affirmation of the 
continuity  of his role in guiding  us forward. As  he transitions  to the role of Non-Executive  Vice 
Chairman, we look forward to his wisdom and perspective remaining a cornerstone of 
Phoenix’s journey ahead. "  
 
Mr. Shishir  Shrivastava,  Non -Executive  Vice  Chairman,  The Phoenix  Mills  Limited,  said: 
"I am deeply grateful to  the Board, to Atul, and to every member  of the Phoenix Mills family: 
our employees,  partners,  investors  and stakeholders:  for the trust,  support,  and collaboration 
that have defined my journey. To have spent more than half my life  with PML has been both a  
privilege  and a source  of immense  pride.  I am especially  thankful  to Atul for the opportunity he 
extended to me and for his mentorship, which has profoundly shaped my journey as a leader.  
Together,  we have  transformed  a bold  vision  into iconic  destinations,  forged enduring 
partnerships, and built a culture of excellence that  defines PML  today. As I transition into the 
role of Non-Executive  Vice Chairman,  I do so with gratitude  for all that  we have  achieved  and 
great confidence in the leadership that will carry the company forward. While stepping back 
from day -to-day management, I  look forward to  continuing to guide, mentor, and contribute 
strategically to the PML’s next chapter of growth."  
 
Mr. Shrivastava has been associated with PML since 1999, playing a defining role in its 
transformation into India’s leading retail -led mixed -use developer. He began as Project 
Manager for the early phases of High Street Phoenix (now Phoenix Palladium) and went on to 
lead landmark projects including The St. Regis, Mumbai, the Phoenix MarketCity portfolio, 
and significant project launches between 2020 -2023 across asset classes that expanded PML’s 
national footprint. He also spearheaded the Company’s property management, development 
management, and leasing advisory businesses. Over the years, he has overseen strategy, 
capital raising, business development, land acquisition, legal affairs, and investor relations. 
Under his leadership, PML has successfully naviga ted inflection points such as the COVID -19 
pandemic, forged long -term partnerships with leading global investors, executed 
transformative private equity transactions, and integrated sustainability as a growth pillar. His 
disciplined approach to capital all ocation has strengthened PML’s balance sheet, while his 
leadership has built a resilient organization with strong governance and a capable pipeline of 
future leaders.  
 
This elevation  marks  an important  milestone  in PML’s  journey  as it continues  to scale  greater 
heights. Backed  by an experienced  leadership  team,  PML continues  to focus  on executing  its 
development  pipeline,  driving  operational  excellence,  and delivering value to all stakeholders.  
 
About  The Phoenix  Mills  Limited (PML)  
PML (BSE: 503100 | NSE: PHOENIXLTD) is India’s leading owner, operator and developer of 
retail -led mixed -use destinations. PML Group’s developments are spread across retail, 
hospitality, commercial offices, and residential asset classes.  
 
PML and its subsidiaries have an operational retail portfolio of over 11 msft of chargeable 
retail area across 8 major cities of India and are further developing approximately 7 msft of 
chargeable retail space across 5 new malls, and expansion of the exist ing retail destinations.  
 
PML’s mixed -use destinations also include Grade A offices with a completed office portfolio 
GLA of ~4.2 msft in Mumbai, Pune and Bengaluru. PML recently received completion 
certificate  for its offices  in Chennai  of ~0.6  msft,  taking  the completed  office  portfolio  to ~4.8 
msft. The Group has further ~2 msft currently under planning at existing mixed -use 
destinations.  
 
In the hospitality segment, PML owns two premium hotels totaling  588 keys, including the 
flagship The St. Regis, Mumbai. The Company is currently constructing the Grand Hyatt 
Bangalore (~400 keys) and plans to soon commence construction of ~700 keys across two new 
hotels. A further two hotels  with approximately 500 keys are  under  planning at existing mixed -
use destinations.  
 
Till date, PML has successfully delivered >3.5 msft of premium residential space in Chennai 
and Bengaluru. The Company is currently executing a ~1 msft  premium residential project in 
Alipore,  Kolkata,  and has an under -planning  residential  pipeline  of ~2.5  msft  across  key metro 
cities at existing locations.  
 
With  a pan-India  presence  and a proven  track  record  of creating  landmark  destinations,  PML 
continues to redefine urban living by integrating shopping, dining, entertainment, 
workspaces, and hospitality into vibrant, city -shaping environments.
