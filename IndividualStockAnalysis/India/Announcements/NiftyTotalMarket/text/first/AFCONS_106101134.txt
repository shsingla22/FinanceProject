 A demand notice received from the Tax Authority of 
Mozambique raise d a total tax demand of 51. 09 
million MZN and a penalty of 5 .00 million MZN for the 
financial years 2019, 2020  and 2021. The demand and 
penalty, amounting to 56. 09 million MZN are on 
account of taxes on salaries earned by Indian 
employees working in Mozambique. In INR terms, this 
equates to approximately ₹7.5 0 crores, including a 
penalty of ₹6 7 lakhs  
Impact on financial, operation or other 
activities of the listed entity, 
quantifiable in monetary terms to the 
extent possible   
   The Company believes that the said demand is not 
feasible as per the law. The Company will file suitable 
appeal against the said order  
There is no material impact on the Company’s 
financials or operations of the Company . 
Remark  The Contract between Afcons and ANE (client -
Ministry of Roads) specifically exempts Afcons from 
all direct and indirect taxes in Mozambique. It may be 
noted that as per the guidelines issued by the 
Government of India vide letter no 21/3/2015 - IDEAS 
dated December 07, 2015 the goods and services 
provided under this contract to be funded under a Line 
of Credit from Export -Import Bank of India shall be free 
from all kinds of taxes and duties of any nature levied 
in the borrower country and no tax is liable to be paid 
from the Line of Credit. The same was also confirmed 
by Tax Commissioner, Directorate of General Taxes, 
Government of Mozambique, vide their Note No. 
C/DGI-DNT/132/2020 dated - February 2, 2020.
