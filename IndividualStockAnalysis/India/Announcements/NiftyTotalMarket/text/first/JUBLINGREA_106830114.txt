PRESS RELEASE  
NOIDA , Tuesday , October  27th, 2026  
JUBILANT  INGREVIA LIMITED  – Q2’FY27 RESULTS  
                  
               
                 
1. All figures are in Rs Crore unless otherwise stated  
The Board of Jubilant Ingrevia Limited met t oday to approve financial results for the quarter and half year ended 
September  30th, 2026.   
Commenting on the Company’s performance, Mr. Shyam S Bhartia, Chairman and Mr. Hari S Bhartia, Co-
Chairman, Jubilant Ingrevia Limited  said:  
“We are pleased to report sustained strong growth momentum in Q2  FY27 , with revenue growing by 24% YoY, 
and EBITDA growing by 51% YoY, reflecting disciplined execution across our businesses. Our quarterly EBITDA has more than doubled in three years, reflecting the successful  execution of our Pinnacle strategy and disciplined 
cost management. This momentum was also reflected in our strong H1 performance, with revenue increasing by 25% and EBITDA by 44%.  
We are delighted to have signed an agreement to acquire a 40% stake in Zet taOne Technologies Limited, 
marking our entry into the EDMS space, which will help us in offering an integrated value proposition to our 
customers in Electronics/Semicon sectors ”. 
 
Markets Update:  
Amid continued geopolitical uncertainties, volumes remained stable, while firmer crude prices supported improved 
realizations. The quarter also witnessed higher input and utility costs, which partially offset the benefits of 
improved pricing. 
The Pharmaceutical sector  continued to grow on the back of steady volumes driven by sustained demand for 
intermediates.  
The Agrochemicals segment  continued to be under pressure with steady volumes and muted pricing, driven by 
China overcapacity  
In Personal Care , we are witnessing strong volume growth and increasing traction with new customers seeking 
outsourcing partnerships.  
The Nutrition segment  maintained steady volumes, despite softening B3 pricing in the feed segment in last few 
weeks.  
In Semiconductors and Electronics , we are witnessing a strong growth in the new -molecule pipeline, with 
encouraging customer traction, particularly from Japan.

Future Outlook:  
Our Pinnacle journey continues to progress well, reflected in strong revenue and EBITDA growth, and a robust 
opportunity pipeline. We remain confident of sustaining this momentum, with growth expected across all three business segments in FY27, particularl y in Acetyls, where we hope to get higher volumes supported by plant

our CDMO and Fine Chemicals growth roadmap. ” 
 Commenting on the Company’s per formance, Mr. Deepak Jain, Chief Executive Officer and M anaging Director , 
Jubilant Ingrevia Limited  said:
 
“We continued to execute well against our strategic priorities despite ongoing global uncertainties. Our diversified 
sourcing capabilities, strong customer partnerships, and agile operations enabled us to effectively navigate these disruptions and pricing volatility, while managing cost pass -through.  
This execution strength is clearly reflected in our strong performance during both Q2 and H1 FY27.  
We delivered another strong quarter in Q2, reinforcing our performance for H1 FY27. Revenue reached an all -time 
high of ₹1,394 crore  in Q2 FY27 , up 24% YoY, supported by stronger realizations and stable volumes. EBITDA rose 
51% YoY to ₹221 crore  in Q2 FY27 , the highest in the last three years, while PAT increased 60% YoY to ₹111 crore.   
Let me share Our Segmental Business update for Q 2’FY27:  
Specialty Chemicals  
1) The business delivered a strong performance, with revenue reaching ₹560 crore, up 16% year -on-year and 5% 
sequentially, led by healthy volume growth across Fine Chemicals and CDMO.  
2) We delivered EBITDA of ₹140 crore, translating into a strong EBITDA margin of 25%, driven by improved pricing 
across our value -added Fine Chemicals and CDMO portfolio.  
a) In Pyridine & Picolines, volumes remained stable despite continued softness in pricing. Our ongoing cost 
optimization and capacity debottlenecking initiatives are  expected to strengthen performance going 
forward.  
b) Our Fine Chemicals business delivered broad- based growth across Pyridine and Diketene Derivatives, 
supported by improved pricing, with particularly strong momentum in the Diketene portfolio.  
c) In Personal Care, we have multiple products under development and are seeing increasing traction across 
key personal care segments.  
d) Our CDMO business continued to build strong momentum across Agro, Pharma/Personal Care and 
Semiconductors.  
i) Agro  delivered healthy volume growth, led by higher Innovator CDMO volumes  
ii) While we are seeing strong customer engagement in Pharma with our US and Europe roadshows with 
innovators . 
iii) In Semiconductors, we continue to see growing inquiries and an expanding appli cation pipeline, 
supported by our upcoming R&D and clean- room facility in Greater Noida.  
iv) Overall, we have 25+ confirmed molecules in our pipeline, which are expected to drive our future 
growth in coming years.  
3) The Nutrition business segment delivered anot her strong quarter, with revenue reaching ₹270 crore, up 50% 
year -on-year and 11% sequentially. EBITDA increased to ₹39 crore, representing growth of 87% year -on-year 
and 9% sequentially, while margins remained healthy at 15%.  
  
 
 
a) Within Human Nutrition, Vitamin B3 delivered strong volume growth both year -on-year and sequentially, 
led by double -digit growth across Food and Cosmetics applications. CC/CBT also witnessed improved 
volumes and pricing, supported by strong customer traction across the EU, US and  India. In Premixes, the 
Remidex integration continues to strengthen engagement and sales with Tier -1 customers in India.  
b) Our Animal Nutrition business also continued to show healthy momentum. Vitamin B3 delivered strong 
double -digit volume growth both year -on-year and sequentially, while pricing remained marginally soft. 
Choline maintained its domestic market position, with continued traction and sequential improvement in 
volumes in Europe. Premixes also delivered strong pricing improvement, supporting healthy margin performance.  
4) Chemical intermediates business delivered another strong quarter, with steady volumes and improved realizations, amid higher input costs arising from elevated crude prices.  
a) Segment revenue grew 24% year -on-year and 8% sequential ly to ₹563 crore. EBITDA rose significantly to 
₹64 crore, up 226% year -on-year and 13% sequentially, driven by improved realizations across the product 
portfolio. 
b) Acetic Anhydride delivered healthy sequential volume growth, supported by resilient pharma demand and market share gains in Europe, while maintaining a stable domestic market position. We also witnessed strong double -digit pricing improvement year -on-year, and with the competitor closure in Europe, we 
expect to further strengthen demand and pricing, going forward.  
c) Ethyl Acetate continued to see steady sequential volume growth, while realizations improved significantly both year -on-year and quarter -on-quarter, reflecting the elevated input -cost environment.  
Update o n the progress made on the M&A front : 
1) I am delighted to share that we have recently signed an agreement to acquire a 40% stake in ZettaOne with an investment of ₹189.2 crore. The transaction is progressing well, and the shareholders’ agreement will also be concluded soon.  
2) ZettaOne operates in the Electronics Development and Manufacturing Services space, providing us access to the broader electronics and semiconductor ecosystem. This transaction will enable us to deliver an integrated value proposition in electronics and semi -conductor space, leading to long- term and 
sustainable value creation.  
3) We are already witnessing encouraging customer traction, with several discussions underway with mid-sized and large customers. ZettaOne will also broaden our access to new customers and deepen 
relationships with OEMs and technology companies across semiconductors, aerospace & defence, 
industrial and related electronics segments.  
4) ZettaOne has delivered a strong growth over the last three years, and based on the current momentum, 
we expect the busines s to grow by at least 60 –70% this year. The company is also establishing a new 
manufacturing facility near Bengaluru, which will further support its growth journey.  
With the strong momentum achieved in the first half of FY27, we are increasingly confident  that our Pinnacle 
strategy is delivering the desired outcomes. We believe FY27 will be a pivotal year in our journey, providing a strong foundation to accelerate growth and create greater value in the quarters ahead. ”
