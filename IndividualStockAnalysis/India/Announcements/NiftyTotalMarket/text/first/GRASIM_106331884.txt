3. Brief Profile  Mr. Neelabja Chakrabarty is an experienced 
Governance and Legal Professional with over 24 years 
of experience and has a proven track record of 
governance and legal matters including Schemes of 
Arrangements for demerger and amalgamation, internal 
restructuring, fund raising and acquisitions.  
 
He is a Commerce graduate and holds a law degree 
(LLM) from ISBM University, Cost & Management 
Accountant from ICMAI, along with being a qualified 
Company Secretary. 
 
Mr. Neelabja is currently the Senior Vice President and 
Company Secretary of FSN E-Commerce Ventures 
Limited (Nykaa) where he had been instrumental in 
restructuring projects within the group companies and 
fund raising.  
 
Prior to Nykaa, he had worked as Senior Vice President 
and Company Secretary of Tata Consumer Products 
Limited handling varied M & A projects. He had been 
associated with Tata Group for about 12 years. 
 
Earlier in his career, he had worked in Secretarial, legal 
and compliance roles in various reputed companies like 
CRISIL Limited, The Indian Hotels Company Limited and 
The Phoenix Mills Limited.
