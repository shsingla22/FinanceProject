Noida , January 1, 202 6: APL Apollo Tubes Limited (APL Apollo), the World's largest branded 
structural steel tube company, today announced its all-time high sales volume for Q 3FY26. The 
Company registered the sales volume of 916,976  Ton in Q3FY26 compared to 855,037  Ton in 
Q2FY26 and 828,200 Ton in Q 3FY25. For 9MFY26 , the company reported sales volume of 2,566,363  
Ton, an increase of 11% YoY .

About APL Apollo Tubes Limited  
APL Apollo Tubes Limited (APL Apollo) BSE: 533758, NSE: APLAPOLLO is India’s leading structural steel tube manufacturer. Head quartered at Delhi NCR, 
the Company operates 11 manufacturing facilities with a total capacity of 4.5 Mn Ton. It has a pan -India pr esence with 11 units strategically located 
in Hyderabad, 3 plants at Sikandarabad (UP), Bangalore, Hosur (Tamil Nadu), 2 plants in Raipur (Chhattisgarh), Malur (Karnata ka), Murbad 
(Maharashtra) and Umm AI Quwain (UAE). APL Apollo’s multi -product offerings include over 3,000 varieties for multiple building material structural steel 
applications. With state -of-the-art-manufacturing facilities, APL Apollo serves as a ‘one -stop shop’ for a wide spectrum of products, catering to an 
array of industry applications  such as urban infrastructure and real estate, rural housing, commercial construction, greenhouse structures and 
engineering applications. The Company’s vast 3 -tier distribution network of over 800 Distributors is spread across India, with presence in over  300 towns 
and cities
