VESTING ORDER ISSUED BY MINISTRY OF COAL, GOVT. OF INDIA, IN 
RESPECT OF MAHAN COAL BLOCK IN MADHYA PRADESH 
JK Cement Ltd. (JKC) had emerged as the successful bidder for the Mahan Coal Mine in 
Singrauli District of Madhya Pradesh under the 2" attempt of 17% tranche of CM(SP) Act, 
2015 commercial coal block e-auction held by the Ministry of Coal, Gol. The Coal Mine 
Block has a total geological coal reserve of 107.4 million tonnes (approximately) with a 
peak-rated capacity of 1.2 million tonnes per annum. This is the second commercial coal 
mine block won by JKC after the West of Shahdol (South) Coal Block. 
JKC is committed to become self-reliant for its existing cement Plants and upcoming 
projects. Since the mine is commercial one, the surplus coal produced from the coal mine 
may be gainfully disposed off. 
Today JK Cement has received from Nominated Authority, Ministry of Coal, Govt. of 
India the Vesting Order in the ceremony conducted at Shastri Bhawan, Ministry of Coal, 
New Delhi. This is key date for adhering to the time schedule for commencing the mining 
operation.
