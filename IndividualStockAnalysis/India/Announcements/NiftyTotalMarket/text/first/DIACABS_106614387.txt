Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited Discharged in CBI & ED / PMLA Matters; Major Milestone in 
release of company’s assets worth approximate to Rs 1000+ Cr. & receivables approximate to  Rs 
900+ Cr. paving way for Companies Revival and Future Growth  
 
Ahmedabad, Gujarat | 08 May 2026  
 
This is with reference to a significant legal and corporate milestone with the discharge of the Company 
in the matters initiated by the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) and the Enforcement Directorate 
(ED) in 2018 under which company was arrayed as pa rty accused and its assets were under embargo, 
approximate value comprising of Fixed Assets of Rs 1000+ Cr and Receivables aggregating to Rs 900+ cr 
along with the assets of Subsidiary Diamond Power Transformers L imited.  
 
The Company has been discharged and given clean chit in PMLA Case Nos. 2/2024 and yet another legal 
battle will be resolved under the provisions of IBC . 
 
by the Judgement and Order from Hon’ble Court of the Principal District & Sessions Judge, Ahmedabad, 
being the designated Special Court under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (“PMLA”), 
pertaining to proceedings under Section 3 and punishable un der Section 4 of the PMLA Act, 2002 vide 
order pronounced on 6th May, 2026 -Wednesday and received by company on 7th May 2026 on Thursday.  
 
As a consequence of the aforesaid proceedings initiated in 2018 under criminal as well as civil 
jurisprudence, substantial fixed assets and current assets of the Company had been attached by the 
Enforcement Directorate, which had materially impacted the op erational and financial flexibility of the 
Company for several years.  
 
Subsequently, pursuant to the takeover and revival of the Company by the new management under the 
Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (“IBC”), the Company approached various judicial forums for 
appropriate legal remedies, including discharge and release of asse ts.  
 
Earlier, the Hon’ble Gujarat High Court had quashed and set aside the relevant proceedings and actions, 
and pleased to allow discharge of company from prosecution initiate under CBI/ED proceedings. Further, 
with the present order of the Trial Court dischar ging the Company as an accused, the Company believes 
that a significant overhang on its operations and financial position now stands resolved and -materialized 
to achieve next level milestone.  
 
The Company expects that the discharge and consequential legal clarity will enable:  
 
1) Leveraging of the Company’s very large fixed asset base for raising substantial working capital 
and long -term growth capital;  
2) Realisation  of outstanding pre -NCLT receivables and recoveries from debtors;  
At Present , the company has made a remarkable turnaround supported by the fund infusion of the 
Promoters, these developments will result in Acceleration of operational expansion and execution 
capabilities; enhanced confidence amongst lenders, customers, vendors, and institutional stakeholders.  
The Company further expects that the anticipated infusion of Fund based and Non -Fund based working 
capital limits and improved financial flexibility will significantly accelerate the utilisation of its presently 
highly under -utilised manufacturing capacities across various plants and business divisions. This will also 
pave way for Ban k guarantee limits to participate in various business Opportunities with National, State 
and International Utilities.
