 PRESS RELEASE  
 
 
 
Information Classification: Public  Redington Partners with Salicru to Expand Access to  
Power Protection Solutions across the Middle East  & Africa  
The partnership combines Salicru’s power electronics expertise with Redington’s channel 
reach to help businesses protect critical technology and maintain operational continuity.  
Dubai, UAE, 13th November 2026 : Redington, the technology ecosystem orchestrator shaping 
how technology is brought to market, scaled and adopted across geographies, has announced 
a distribution partnership with Salicru , a Spanish manufacturer of power electronics and 
uninterruptible power supply (UPS) systems, for the Middle East  and Africa  region . The 
collaboration will broaden access to power protection solutions and create opportunities for 
channel partners to address business continuity requirements across the country.  
Reliable power is fundamental to the technology businesses depend on every day. From 
servers and networking equipment to critical operational systems, interruptions and electrical 
disturbances can damage equipment, disrupt services and put data at risk. Th e partnership 
brings together Salicru’s engineering expertise and Redington’s local market capabilities to 
help partners address these requirements as part of a broader infrastructure offering.  
Founded in 1965, Salicru  develops and manufactures solutions that protect professional, 
industrial and household systems against power supply disturbances. Its UPS range spans 
smaller IT environments, online single -phase and three -phase solutions  in mega -watts , and 
modular configurations for applications requiring scalability and high availability.  Multi -Mega 
watt deployments in the region, places Salicru as one of the unique power electronics 
manufacturer to adapt customer needs by its customs tailormade solutions.  
These capabilities are particularly relevant to data centres, where power continuity underpins 
critical workloads and service availability. Salicru’s UPS solutions help keep servers, storage 
and networking systems operational during power interruptions, wh ile its modular systems 
allow protection capacity to grow alongside infrastructure. This gives partners an opportunity 
to integrate power protection into data centre expansion and modernisation projects.  
Through its channel network, market knowledge and distribution capabilities, Redington will 
connect Salicru with resellers and systems integrators across the region . The collaboration 
aims to help partners identify power protection opportunities alongside their existing 
technology deployments, strengthening their ability to deliver more complete solutions.  
Sukhil Nair, President, UAE, Redington, said, “As businesses deepen their reliance on digital 
infrastructure, power continuity becomes a strategic consideration in how they invest, operate 
and grow. Our partnership with Salicru strengthens our ability to help partners address this 
requirement as part of a complete technology offering. As an ecosystem orchestrator,
