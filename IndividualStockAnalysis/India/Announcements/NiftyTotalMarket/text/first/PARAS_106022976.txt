Subject: Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015  – Grant of License  under Arms Act, 1959:  
 
With reference to the captioned subject matter, we are pleased to  inform you that M/s. Paras Defence and Space 
Technologies Limited  (“the Company”)  has been  granted  a License under the Arms Act, 1959.   
 
This license authorizes the Company to engage in the design, development, manufacturing, assembly and upgrad ing 
of cannons for  naval gun systems, air defence gun system, airborne cannon systems (helicopter, fighter jet), armoured 
fighting vehicles based on cannon systems, mounted cannon systems  of caliber - 20X102 MM, 20X110 MM, 20X128 
MM, 20X138 MM, 20X139 MM, 23X152 MM, 30X173 MM, 30X165 MM, 30X113 MM, 35X228 MM and 40X365 MM  
with a proposed an annual capacity of 100 nos.
