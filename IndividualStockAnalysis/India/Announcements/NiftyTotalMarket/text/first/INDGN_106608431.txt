Indegene Identified as a Leader in ISG Provider Lens ™ for Life Sciences Digital 
Services in Commercial Operations – Digital Evolution (Service Providers) Quadrant 
2026 
 
Princeton, US, May 4, 2026: Information Services Group (ISG), a leading global technology research and 
advisory firm, has identified Indegene as a Leader in its Provider Lens ™ for Life Sciences Digital Services 
in Commercial Operations – Digital Evolution (Service Providers) Quadrant 2026.  The assessment 
highlights Indegene’s strong domain expertise, proprietary technology platforms, and growing use of AI and GenAI to enable more integrated, data -driven commercial operations for life sciences organizations.  
 
 
 
The ISG Provider Lens ™ report evaluates service providers based on their ability to help 
biopharmaceutical, biotech, and medical technology companies modernize commercial operations across 
planning, content, engagement, analytics, and execution through digital, data, and AI -enabled models.  
“Indegene shows a focused and capability -driven approach to life sciences commercial operations, 
supported by its proprietary platforms such as the NEXT Technology Platform and Cortex. These assets enable structured content management, workflow automation and more targeted HCP engagement 
through data -driven insights. The company’s long -standing domain experience is reflected in its ability to 
deliver across the commercial value chain, from planning to execution, with an emphasis on standardized 
processes and measurable outcomes. Its use of AI and GenAI in areas such as engagement planning, 
forecasting and analytics, along with a broad partner ecosystem, indicates a steady shift toward more integrated and data -led commercial models,”  
— Rohan Sinha, Principal Analyst – Healthcare and Life Sciences, ISG  
Indegene’s offerings in commercial operations focus on helping life sciences enterprises move beyond fragmented digital initiatives toward orchestrated, intelligence -led execution. Through its NEXT 
Technology Platform and Cortex, Indegene enables clients t o connect content, data, workflows, and 

 
engagement across channels, supporting consistent execution, improved operational efficiency, and more 
meaningful HCP interactions.  
“ISG’s recognition as a Leader affirms our belief that the future of life sciences commercial operations will 
be intelligence- led and AI -native by design.” said Vivek Ghai, EVP, Indegene. “As the industry accelerates 
toward fully connected, data -driven ecosystems, we are shaping this transformation - enabling our 
clients to operationalize data and analytics into real -world outcomes at scale, with speed, precision, and 
consistency.”  
ISG’s recognition – for the 3rd consecutive year - further reinforces Indegene’s position as a trusted partner 
for life sciences companies seeking to modernize commercial operations, improve engagement effectiveness, and deliver sustained business value through digital, data, and AI -led transformation.
