REVISION IN COMPLETION SCHEDULE AND ADDITIONAL COST FOR MUMBAI -
NAGPUR -JHARSUGUDA PIPELINE (MNJPL) PROJECT  
a Pipeline  MUMBAI -NAGPUR -JHARSUGUDA PIPELINE PROJECT  
b Length of Pipeline  1702 Km  
• Mumbai – Nagpur Pipeline (Part -A): 693 Km  
• Nagpur - Jharsuguda Pipeline (Part -B): 692 Km  
• Nagpur - Jabalpur Pipeline (Part -C): 317 Km  
c Revision of Completion 
Schedule  From 30th June 2025 to 30th September 2025 progressively  
d Investment required  Additional Financial implication of Rs. 411.12 Crore    
e Rationale  Mumbai -Nagpur Pipeline (Part -A): Pipeline mechanically completed 
except 1 km stretch in multiple patches.  Delay was due to various 
hindrances in MSRDC Utility Corridor in multiple patches coupled with 
other technical challenges.  
 
Nagpur -Jharsuguda Pipeline (Part -B): Pipeline ~98% mechan ically 
completed. The pipeline laying work is affected due to delay in obtaining 
Forest Permission, NBWL Clearance and MoRTH permission for BT 
cutting along NH -44 in Maharashtra state.  
 
Nagpur - Jabalpur Pipeline (Part -C): Pipeline ~97 % mechanically 
comp leted.  The pipeline laying work is affected due to delay in 

acquisition of RoU, permanent Land parcels for valve stations and local 
resistance.  
The entire Mumbai -Nagpur -Jharsuguda pipeline is expected to be 
completed progressively by Sept.’25  
Additiona l Cost : The project cost is anticipated to be increased by Rs. 
411.12 Crore and therefore, the revised project cost for MNJPL is Rs. 
8,255.37 Crore  which is 5.24%  higher than originally approved project 
cost of Rs. 7,844.25 Crore
