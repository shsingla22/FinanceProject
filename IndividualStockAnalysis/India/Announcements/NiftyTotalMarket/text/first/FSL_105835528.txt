Firstsource Solutions announces Hasit Trivedi as  
President & Chief Digital and AI Officer   
  
Mumbai, India | May  06, 2024:  Firstsource Solutions Limited  (NSE: FSL, BSE:532809), a leading global 
provider of Business Process Management (BPM) services and an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company, has 
announced the appointment of  Hasit Trivedi  as President & Chief Digital and AI Officer , effective March 
19, 2024. Hasit will oversee the scaling and deployment of Firstsource’s digital capabilities including elevating 
its platforms, data & analytics, automation , and AI ecosystem.   
  
"Over the last couple of quarters , Firstsource has been setting the foundation for sustained upward 
momentum. Bringing onboard Hasit’s depth of experience in the digital and AI space is aligned with that 
vision. With him leading our digital and technology initiatives, we anticipate an extr aordinary future 
characterized by innovative solutions and enhanced value for our clients. His extensive experience combined 
with our comprehensive range of services forms a formidable partnership, positioning us to elevat e our 
performance and the success of our clients to new heights ,” shared  Ritesh Idnani, Managing Director & Chief 
Executive Officer at Firstsource Solutions .   
  
“I am excited about synergizing my expertise in digital technologies with the unique and unparalleled domain 
proficiency within Firstsource.  The combination can significantly serve the digital transformation agenda of 
an organization and  add value to our clients in a disruptive manner.”, said  Hasit Trivedi, President & Chief 
Digital,  and AI Officer at Firstsource Solutions .   
  
With nearly three decades of experience in technology, Hasit brings a wealth of knowledge and expertise to 
Firstsource Solutions. He is widely recognized for his contributions to the field of artificial intelligence and 
has played a key role in helping lea ding Indian SIs establish and operate their AI, automation, and platform 
practices.    
  
Hasit's appointment marks a significant milestone in Firstsource Solutions’ journey towards harnessing the 
power of disruptive technology to drive transformative change.
