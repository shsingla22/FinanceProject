Clinical achievements during the quarter 
KIMS KURNOOL 
 Our doctors at KIMS Kurnool successfully conducted a transplant - a first-of-its-kind pediatric 
incompatible blood group kidney transplant in Andhra Pradesh. In order to save her 12 years old 
daughter’s life, the mother came forward to donate her kidney. However, the blood group did not 
match. Yet the doctors conducted a successful transplant which is rare particularly at district levels.  
 The doctors successfully performed a rare airway stenting procedure on a 75-year-old woman with 
esophageal cancer. Whose windpipe was over 75% blocked causing severe respiratory distress. The 
case is a life-Saving success demonstrating effectiveness of advanced interventional pulmonology 
in critical conditions.  
 Chronic Calcific Pancreatitis is very rare condition in children characterised by formation of stones 
in the pancreas over a prolonged period. In south India it is seen only in about 126 children per 
lakh. A 10-year-old boy suffering from such a condition underwent a successful surgery at our 
hospital. 
KIMS SAVEERA  ANANTAPUR   
 In a rare and complex medical case, a young man with diagnosis of congenital diaphragmatic 
hernia, a condition that affects only 5 in every 10,000 individuals was treated by performing a 
lifesaving surgery. The team noted early signs of tissue gangrene in the abdominal region 
prompting an emergency surgical intervention.  
 A woman suffering from an extremely rare form of thyroid Cancer was successfully diagnosed and 
treated, bringing her much needed relief. The tests revealed that the thyroid Cancer had 
significantly spread into the right side of her neck. After thorough mapping, the Cancerous tissue in 
the thyroid was carefully removed while ensuring preservation of vital structures in the neck. The 
patient has since fully recovered. Thyroid cancer is generally considered a rare disease, occurring in 
only 5.4 people per 1,00,000. Among Women, the rate is even lower, just 3.5 per 1,00,000. 
 
KIMS SECUNDERABAD 
 A Woman suffering from severe Stress urinary Incontinence (SUI) causing urine leakage during 
laughing or bending finally found relief at KIMS Cuddles. Her condition, worsened by Chronic Pelvic 
pain and a prior hysterectomy, ruled out the use of synthetic mesh, the standard treatment for SUI. 
The medical team opted for an innovative autologous rectus fascial sling surgery, using the 
patient's own tissue to create a supportive sling. She made a full recovery, with no recurrence of 
leakage- even during a flu episode. 
 PREMATURE BABY SAVED AFTER 1300 KM ROAD JOURNEY- A WORLD RECORD:  Born in the 
seventh month and weighing just 1.1Kg, the baby suffered from severe sepsis, respiratory distress 
and multi- organ failure. A 31-member expert team from KIMS transported the ventilator- 
dependent baby over 14-16 hours, using two ambulances- one for the baby and the other for 
Oxygen support. On arrival, he received intensive care for two months, gaining weight and 
recovering fully. This 1300 Km journey surpasses the previous global neonatal transport record of 
723 Kms. 
KIMS SUNSHINE 
 The spine surgery team successfully performed a complex corrective procedure on a young boy 
suffering from Congenital Scoliosis. The doctors utilised state-of-the-art technologies like Spine 
navigation, ultrasonic bone scalpel, and intraoperative neuro-monitoring to ensure a safe and 
precise correction. The severity of the deformity required a multidisciplinary approach and 
meticulous planning. The procedure was completed successfully, and the child is now on the path 
to a full recovery. 
 
 
 
KIMS GACHIBOWLI 
 Heart procedure on a 600 - gram Preterm baby: The baby was born at just seven months of 
gestation and had to be kept in NICU for 97 days. The baby developed a condition called PDA - 
PATENT DUCTUS ARTERIOSUS which combined with respiratory issues, required ventilator support. 
Earlier, surgery was the only option to close the PDA by operating the chest. However, in this case, 
due to the baby's extremely low weight and other complications, surgery was considered risky. The 
team decided to use a minimally invasive, state-of-the-art device to close the PDA through the leg 
artery. Following the procedure, the baby recovered well and now holds the distinction of being 
the lightest baby in the country to undergo this type of procedure.  
 A BRITISH WOMAN TRAVELS TO HYDERABAD FOR SURGERY: A British woman travelled all the way 
to Hyderabad to undergo bariatric surgery. She weighed 102 kgs and suffered from hypertension, 
Kidney failure, and thyroid issues. The procedure performed was a Sleeve Gastrectomy, in which 
two- thirds of the stomach is removed. She recovered within 24 hours and was discharged the very 
next day. 
 
KIMS CUDDLES, KONDAPUR 
 A boy was diagnosed with severe aplastic anaemia, a rare and life- threatening condition where the 
bone marrow fails to produce sufficient blood cells. Upon admission, he had dangerously low 
haemoglobin, platelets and neutrophils. His sister's stem cells were successfully transplanted 
offering him a new lease of life.  
 
KIMS ICON, VIZAG 
 FIRST SUCCESSFUL ECPR IN ANDHRAPRADESH: The team of doctors successfully performed the 
first-ever Extracorporeal Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (ECPR) in the state saving a patient who 
suffered a cardiac arrest following a severe case of poisoning, triggered by accidental inhalation of 
Isobutyl nitrite. The highly advanced procedure involves a machine that takes over the function of 
the heart and lungs, providing vital circulatory support. The case sets a new benchmark for treating 
similar cases. 
 
KIMS SEETAMNADHARA, VIZAG. 
 A 4.5 Kg tumour was removed from the liver of a 16-year-old boy in a long and complex surgery. It 
was challenging as the tumour had occupied almost half of the right side of the liver. The boy was 
suffering from an extremely rare form of liver Cancer, known as malignant hepatic 
angiomyelolipoma. After the surgery, the boy recovered rapidly and was discharged on the fifth 
day. 
 FIRST- EVER ARTIFICIAL LIGAMENT SURGERY IN ANDHRAPRADESH: A 28-year-old woman suffered a 
severe knee injury after a fall. She has a small physical frame and was obese which made her a less 
ideal candidate for conventional arthroscopic surgery with autografts. The team decided to use an 
artificial ligament. With the cutting- edge technology, the hospital was able to provide complete 
relief and restore mobility for the young woman. 80% of our units are located in urban centres 
thereby making high-end treatment with latest equipment accessible and available to the people 
there.

About KIMS Hospitals: 
KIMS Hospitals is one of the largest corporate healthcare groups in India with hospitals in Telangana, 
Andhra Pradesh, and Maharashtra, providing multi-disciplinary integrated healthcare services, with a focus 
on tertiary and quaternary healthcare at an affordable cost. Our network of 25 hospitals and over 8000 
beds is spread across Telangana (Secunderabad, Kondapur, Gachibowli, Begumpet, Asian Transcare and 
Kompally), Andhra Pradesh (Nellore, Rajahmundry, Srikakulam, Ongole, Vizag - 2 units, Anantapur, Guntur 
– 2 units and Kurnool), Maharashtra (Nagpur, Nashik – 2 units, Thane and Sangli), Kerala (Kannur and 
Kollam) and Bangalore (Mahadevpura and Electronic City). We offer a bouquet of comprehensive 
healthcare services across 25 specialties including cardiac sciences, oncology, neurosciences, gastric 
sciences, orthopaedics, organ transplantation, renal sciences, and mother & child care.
