Principal Agreements:  
 
(a) Addendum to the Agreement to Transfer Leasehold Rights dated 14.11.2026; and  
(b) Addendum to the Plant and Machinery Transfer Agreement dated 14.11.2026.  
Except for the aforesaid extension, all other terms and conditions of the Principal Agreements
