Mumbai, March  25, 2025: L&T-Cloudfiniti, a leading technology solutions provider, is proud

in Europe.  
The collaborations will focus  on groundbreaking developments in healthcare, life sciences, 
vertical AI, and conversational technologies  in India and across the globe  by harness ing

in India. By integrating AI -driven insights into healthcare practices, Hanooman  is poised to

time,  personalised communication and AI -enabled attentiveness to t he forefront of 
businesses in India.
