Karnataka Bank celebrated World Environment Day on 05.06.2026 with a plantation 
drive organised at the Bank's Head Office premises in Mangaluru. The event was led 
by the Bank's Managing Director & CEO, Shri Raghavendra S. Bhat along with 
senior management officials, who planted saplings as part of the initiative 
promoting environmental conservation and sustainability. 
... 
I 
Speaking on the occasion, Shri Raghavendra S. Bhat emphasized the importance of 
protecting nature and adopting environmentally responsible practices in daily life. 
He stated that every individual has a role to play in preserving the environment for 
future generations. He encouraged people to reduce the usage of vehicles wherever 
possible, adopt car pooling practices, and gradually shift towards electric vehicles to 
, reduce carbon emissions and pollution. 
He further noted that small and conscious efforts by individuals and institutions can 
collectively create a significant positive impact on the environment. He also urged 
employees and customers to actively participate in green initiatives and contribute 
towards building a cleaner and healthier planet. 
Karnataka Bank has consistently been undertaking various environmentally 
sustainable initiatives under its Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) activities, 
reaffirming its commitment towards environmental conservation and a greener 
future. 
Top management and senior officials of the Bank were present on the occasion.
