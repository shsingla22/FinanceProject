Sir/Madam,  
In line with the Corporate Disclosure requirements under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that:    
HUDCO has a potential for extending loan assistance in the range of Rs. 75,000 Crore to    
Rs. 1,00,000 Crore under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana -Urban 2.0 (PMAY -U 2.0) scheme 
guidelines  recently released by the Govt. of India. The expected loan assistance under PMAY -
U 2.0 has been arrived at considering loan amount of more than Rs. 42,000 Crore disbursed 
by HUDCO under earlier phase of PMAY. Under PMAY- U 2.0, HUDCO has also been 
designated as one of the Central Nodal Agency (CNA) for channelizing interest subsidy  and 
one of the technical institutions  to carry out Desk -cum-Site Scrutiny of the projects . 
Additionally, PMAY- U 2.0 scheme guidelines will open substantial opportunity for HUDCO in 
consultancy and capacity building areas.
