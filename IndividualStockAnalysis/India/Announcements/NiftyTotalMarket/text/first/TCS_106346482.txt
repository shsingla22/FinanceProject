Finnish Retailer Kesko Partners with TCS to Drive AI -Powered 
Retail Transformation  
 
Tata Consultancy Services will deliver SAP S4 Transformation, digital development, application managed 
service s and intelligent automation to help the Finnish retail group scale operations , launch next gen digital 
services  and drive growth  
 
HELSINKI | MUMBAI, August 20, 2025:  Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS), a global 
leader in IT services, consulting, and business solutions has been selected by Kesko, a leading Finnish retail 
group, as its strategic IT partner to spearhead the transformation of its core technology landscape across  the 
Nordics and Baltics. This partnership is set to play a pivotal role in Kesko’s journey to modernize operations, 
enhance business agility, and support its long -term growth and expansion ambitions in grocery, car , building 
and technical trades across multiple geographies and sectors.  
The new multi-year partnership is designed to help Kesko to accelerate development of next generation of 
digital services, increase operational efficiency, go to market faster, and stay competitive by modernizing its 
IT landscape . This partnership will also help Kesko  leverage  AI and intelligent automation, improve quality, 
and sav e costs across Kesko’s operations in Finland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Poland, Estonia, Latvia and 
Lithuania.  As part of the collaboration, TCS will provide comprehensive, AI -enab led application managed 
services that will leverage machine learning and AI capabilities to understand the health of the application 
and take proactive measures to resolve incidents within Kesko’s SAP and non -SAP landscapes.  
Arto Hiltunen, Chief Information Officer, Kesko  said, “As the biggest trading sector operator in Finland and 
one of the biggest in Northern Europe, we are always working to stay competitive and bring our large and 
diverse customer bases more value. That is why we are partnering with TCS to continuously improv e our 
quality, efficiency and the multichannel customer experience, while staying innovative and resilient amidst 
uncertainties in the operating environment.”  
The engagement covers hosting and modernization of SAP environments,  and end-to-end support for digital 
applications. These services will be delivered through a unified operations model —powered by TCS ignio ™ 
AIOps and the TCS RiO ™ (Rigor in Operations) framework —to enable full -stack observability, and deliver 
predictive, proactive incident management at scale. TCS’ ignio AIOps is built on an agentic SaaS (Software -
as-a-Service) architecture, and it autonomously resolves IT issues a cross multi -cloud and hybrid 
environments . 
Subhadipta Samantray, Country Head, TCS Finland,  said, “We are excited to partner with Kesko to accelerate 
their digital transformation with our AI -driven solutions and deep industry expertise. This collaboration sets 
a benchmark for the retail sector, demonstrating how advanced technology and innovation can em power 
businesses to adapt, enhance customer experiences, and lead in an ever -evolving market.”  
TCS partners with  the top 10 global retailers and many others around the world to build profitable and 
sustainable businesses and offer immersive, unified, and hyper -personalized omnichannel experiences for 
their customers. TCS’ deep industry expertise, backed by decades of ex perience and relevant partnerships in 
retail, spans across store operations, merchandising, supply chain, marketing, pricing strategies, and business 
model reinvention.

TCS has had a strong presence in Finland for the past 30 years. TCS Finland has ranked among top in customer 
satisfaction by an independent survey and has also been recognized as a Top Employer by the Top Employers 
Institute.  To accelerate innovation and digital transformation in the Nordics, TCS recently launched The TCS 
Pace Studio in Stockholm, its first in the region. This hub joins TCS’ global Pace ™ innovation network, which 
fosters rapid prototyping and business solutions across key locations.  
 
Kesko Oyj  
Kesko Oyj is one of the largest retail companies in Northern Europe, operating in grocery trade, building 
and technical trade, and car trade. Based in Helsinki, the company serves over 3.5 million customers and 
has a strong footprint across Finland, Sweden , Norway, Denmark, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, and Poland.
