This is with reference to clarification sought on recent news item captioned Mumbai CNG 
Crisis Ends: Damaged CNG Pipeline Restored, Supply to Resume Shortly.  
 In this respect, we would like to submit the following:  
 
Gas pipeline of GAIL (India ) Limited  (‘GAIL ’) was damaged by a third party. The damaged 
pipeline is a feeder pipeline for receiving gas at MGL’s City Gate Station ( ‘CGS ’) at Wadala. 
Due to this incident gas supply to CGS at Wadala was interrupted. This resulted in interruption of gas supply to Mumbai city and suburbs. It may be noted that pipeline network of Mumbai city and suburbs is inter -connected with other CGS of MGL situated at Mahape, 
Ambernath, Taloja, Usarghar and Savaroli. MGL immediately restored the gas supply to the maximum extent possible through other CGS to Mumbai city and suburbs continuously throughout this interruption.  Thus, MGL maintained 100% supply to all domestic household consumers and all small / medium commercial establishments. Large Industrial customers were asked to stop their consumption unless critical.  Since gas supply to Mumbai city and suburbs was through distant CGSs, to maintain pressure at CNG station in whole region it was ensured that all large CNG station s remain operational 
in all pockets of the city. Thus, 60% of the CNG stations including CNG station at State Transport Undertakings were fully operational most of the time in the affected region.  
 The Company confirms that gas supply by GAIL is fully restored and all operations of MGL back to normalcy from afternoon of Tuesday, November 18, 2025.
