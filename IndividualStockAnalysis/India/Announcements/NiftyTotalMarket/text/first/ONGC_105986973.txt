Reg.: Investment in ONGC Petro additions Limited (OPaL)  
Madam/ Sir,  
 
This is further to our filing dated 23.08.2024 on the subject.  
The Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting held on 21.08.2024 had, inter-alia, approved buy-back of 
Compulsorily Convertible Debentures (CCDs) issued by OPaL of ₹7,778 crore in 3 tranches from Financial 
Institutions/ Banks/ Mutual Funds and other holders and conversion of said CCDs into equity shares. 
It is informed that OPaL has allotted 61,070 lakh equity shares of ₹10/- each to the Company on 12.09.2024 against 
conversion of 2 tranches of CCDs (I & III tranches of ₹5,615 crore and ₹492 crore; respectively) of ₹6,107 crore.  
Upon issuance of 61,070 lakh shares, the shareholding of the Company in OPaL increased from 81.28% to 91.16% 
equity shares.
