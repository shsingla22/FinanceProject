Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA). The operative part of order dated 28th November, 2024 is as
follows
“11. We have considered the submissions made by both the parties and find
that the appellant herein who are accused persons for the offence under
Money Laundering Act, 2002 have been discharged thus nothing would
survive against the appellants. In the light of discharge of the appellants
(accused persons) the impugned order does not survive and accordingly the
Provisional Attachment Order confirmed by the Ld. Adjudicating Authority are
set aside. The appeals are allowed.”
