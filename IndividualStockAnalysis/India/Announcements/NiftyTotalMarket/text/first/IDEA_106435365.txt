1. The above unaudited consolidated financial results of Vodafone Idea Limited, its subsidiaries (the Group), joint venture 
and associate, as reviewed by the Audit Committee of the Board, were approved and taken on record by the Board of 
Directors at their meeting held on 10"' November , 2025. 
2. The AGR demands till FY 2019 are part of the four-year moratorium period. Settlement of the AGR liability is subject to 
disposal of the representations made/to be made by the Company in line with the orders pronounced by the Hon'ble 
Supreme Court in this regard. 
In September 2025, the Company filed a writ petition before the Hon'ble Supreme Court seeking quashing of the 
additional AGR demands for the period up to FY 2017 raised by DoT and for comprehensive 
reassessment/reconciliation of all AGR dues including interest and penalty upto said period. The Hon'ble Supreme 
qourt, keeping in view the change in circumstances and the larger public interest. vide its orders dated 
27th October, 2025 and 3'' November, 2025, has held that the matter falls within the policy domain of the Government 
of India (Gol) and DoT could reconsider the issue. The Hon'ble Supreme Court has also clarified that DoT could re­
assess and reconcile all AGR dues including interest and penalty up to the said period. Pending the outcome of such 
reconsideration/re-assessment by DoT, no effect has been given in these consolidated financial results. 
3. The Group has incurred a loss of Rs. 121,323 Mn for the six months ended 30th September, 2025 and net worth stands 
at negative Rs. 824,602 Mn at that date. 
II As at 30th September , 2025, the Group's outstanding debt from banks (including interest accrued but not due) is 
Rs. 15.421 Mn and Deferred payment obligation (including interest accrued but not due) towards Spectrum which 
is payable over the years till FY 2044 and AGR which is payable over the years till FY 2031 aggregates to 
Rs. 2,014,090 Mn. 
The AGR instalment on which moratorium was availed as per the Telecom Reforms Package 2021, falling due 
during FY 2026 is Rs.164,280 Mn (subject to reconsideration/ re-assessment of AGR dues including Interest and 
Penalty up to FY 2016 -17 by DoT discussed in Note 2 above). Instalments related to deferred payment obligations 
towards spectrum payable by September 2026 is Rs. 25,581 Mn. 
Out of the total debt from banks (including interest accrued but not due) of Rs 15.421 Mn, an amount of Rs 14.402 
Mn is payable in the next 12 months i.e. by September 2026 with applicable interest. Further an amount of Rs 858 
Mn has been reclassified from long-term borrowings to short-term borrowings for not meeting certain covenant 
clauses under the financial agreements . The Group has exchanged correspondences and continues to be in 
discussion with the lenders for next steps/waivers . 
As of date, the Group has met all its debt obligations payable to its lenders/ banks and financial institutions along 
with applicable interest. The Company is in discussion with banks to raise additional funds as required. 
The Group's ability to settle the above liabilities is dependent on reconsiderat ion / re-assessment of AGR dues 
including Interest and Penalty up to FY 2016 -17 by DoT, fund raise through equity and debt and generation of cash 
flow from operations. Based on current efforts and recent directions by the Hon'ble Supreme Court, the Group believes 
that it would be able to get DoT support, successfully arrange funds and generate cash flow from operations . 
Accordingly, these consolidated financial results have been prepared on a going concern basis. 
4. As at 30th September , 2025, assets include amounts recorded as recoverable from the promoters of erstwhile Vodafone 
India Limited ('VlnL") under the Implementation agreement (IA) executed on 20"' March, 2017. Under the IA. the 
Company can claim the amounts should it discharge part of the AGR dues by 30"' June, 2025 ("Last Settlement Date"). 
Considering the moratorium by the Gol, the AGR dues have not been paid by the Company . The parties have extended 
the Last Settlement Date till 31 ~ December, 2025. The Company will take necessary steps as required in this regard to 
realise this amount.
