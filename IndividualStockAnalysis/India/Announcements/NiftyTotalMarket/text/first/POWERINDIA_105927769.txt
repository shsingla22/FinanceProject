BG of INR 8.40 Crores. Hence, the Company had to approach 
Tuticorin Court for stay on BG encashment and the stay was 
granted in the Company’s favour. Therefore, the arbitration 
proceedings were commenced.  
 
Arbitration Award dated 31st December 2022 was received on 
January 4, 2022 as per which NTPL was entitled to encash the 
BG for INR 8.40 crores furnished by the Company, NLC is 
entitled to interest on the amount @ 12% per annum from the 
date of commencement of arbitration and NTPL is entitled to recover INR 10 Lakhs as costs of arbitration. On 6
th January 
2023, the Company filed a petition u/s 34 of the Arbitration and 
Conciliation Act, 1996, before the Principal District Court in 
Tuticorin to set aside this  award and the Hon’ble Court was 
pleased to order ‘status quo’ to be maintained.  
 
Further , under the Vivad se Vishwas scheme (“Scheme”) 
introduced by Ministry of Finance, Government of India , 
settlement was arrived  between Hitachi Energy India Limited 
(“the Company”) and M/s. NLC Tamil Nadu Power Limited (NTPL), having its Registered Office at “Neyveli House” 135, 
Periyar EVR High Road, Kilpauk, Chennai – 600 010 . 
Consequently,  the Arb O.P No. 8 of 2023 filed by the Company 
before the Hon’ble Principal District Court at Tuticorin  was 
withdrawn on June 03, 2024. As per the Settlement Agreement 
dated June 13, 2024  between the Parties, the Company has 
paid Rs. 6,93,93,772/ - to NTPL as full and final payment. 
Thereafter,  Bank Guarantees worth 8,40,10,898/ - were 
returned by NTPL to the Company.  After receipt of the said 
Bank guarantees, a no dues Certificate dated July 26 , 2024
