PRESS RELEASE  
Mumbai, November 2, 2026 
 
Financial  Results for Q 2 FY27 
Strong revenue  growth  at 15.0% YoY; Adj. EBITDA margin at 13.6%, increase 
of 43 basis points  YoY; PAT growth at 19. 1% YoY  
 
Eureka Forbes Limited, one of India’s largest health and hygiene companies, today 
announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. 
   
Highlights of the quarter ended September 30, 202 6 (on a standalone basis) :  
▪ Revenue from operations grew by 15.0% YoY  to Rs. 889.6 Cr  on a strong base quarter  
from last year.  
▪ Product business revenue grew in high teens in Q2 FY27 , led by strong growth in Water 
Purifiers and Emerging Categories.  
▪ Water Purifiers saw high-teens growth  that was well ahead of category growth leading 
to significant market share gains.  
▪ Emerging categories of Robotics, Softeners and Air Purif iers continued their high growth 
trajectory in Q2 FY27 on the back of new launches and category investments.  
▪ Adj. EBITDA1 margins expanded by 43bps YoY to a lifetime time margin of 13.6%; Adj. 
EBITDA grew 18. 8% YoY to Rs 120.7 Cr. 
▪ Adj. PB T2 grew by 17.8% YoY to Rs 104.7 Cr.  
▪ Reported Profit After Tax (PAT) grew by 19.1% YoY to Rs 73.3 Cr; Reported PAT margin 
came in at 8.2%. 
 
Highlights of the six months ended September 30, 202 6 (on a standalone basis) :  
▪ Revenue from operations growth stepped up to 15.1% YoY, driven by high-teens growth 
in products business . 
▪ Adj. EBITDA increased by 15.5% YoY to Rs 1 94.6 Cr from Rs 1 68.5 Cr during H1 FY2 6, 
despite elevated commodity costs and continued investments behind growth .  
▪ Adj. PBT increased by 13 .6% YoY to Rs 1 66.2 Cr.  
▪ PAT (pre-exceptional items) grew by 14.1% YoY to Rs 114.2 Cr; Reported PAT increased 
by 28.6% YoY to Rs 1 28.8 Cr from Rs 100.1 Cr during H1 FY2 6. 
 
                                                
1 Adjusted (Adj.) EBITDA is defined as PBT (before exceptional items) + Finance cost + Depreciation + Amortization + ESOP 
charge less other non -operating income  
2 Adj. PBT is P rofit Before Tax excluding exceptional items and ESOP charges  

 
 Commenting on the performance, Mr. Pratik Pota, MD, and CEO, Eureka Forbes Limited said, 
“We delivered another strong quarter in Q2 FY27 with a revenue growth of 15.0% YoY.  
Growth was broad -based across all channels and categories. Water Purifiers continued their 
strong performance with high -teens growth and healthy market share gains, while our 
emerging categories sustained their growth momentum.  
Our focus on stepping up growth is translating into results. Revenues grew 15.1% YoY in H1 
FY27 accelerating from 12.7% in H1 FY26.   
Importantly, we delivered this strong growth along with continued improvement in 
profitability. Despite significant inflationary pressures, EBITDA margins reached a lifetime 
high of 13.6%, expanding by 43 bps YoY. This reinforces the strength of our operat ing model 
and our ability to balance investments for growth with disciplined execution and operating 
leverage.   
Our strong H1 performance strengthens our confidence in delivering a meaningful step -up 
in growth in FY27. More importantly, the broad -based mo mentum across our core business, 
emerging categories and channels gives us increasing confidence in our ability to sustain a 
higher growth trajectory over the medium term. With continued discipline on execution, we 
remain focused on delivering sustainable profitable growth over the long term. ” 
 
About Eureka Forbes:  
Eureka Forbes Limited is India’s leading health and hygiene brand. With over four decades 
of existence, it is today a multi -product and an omni -channel organization. Eureka Forbes’ 
product portfo lio encompasses water purification, vacuum cleaning and air purification. It 
has direct, retail, e -commerce and institutional sales channels, an inventive business 
partner network and one of the most expansive service networks across India.
