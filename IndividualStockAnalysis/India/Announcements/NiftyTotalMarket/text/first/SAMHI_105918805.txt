SAMHI Executes Agreement with Marriott for Rebranding Two Hotels 
Gurugram, 18t July 2024 — SAMHI Hotels Ltd, a prominent hotel ownership and asset 
management platform in India, has informed exchanges about entering into agreements for the 
rebranding of two of its hotels acquired as part of ACIC portfolio. This follows SAMHI’s core 
strength and proven experience of acquisition and turn-around of hotel assets. 
Pursuant to the above, SAMHI has entered into a management agreement with Marriott for the 
rebranding of its 217 rooms hotel in Pune to a Courtyard by Marriott branded hotel. The 
conversion and rebranding will occur post a renovation set to conclude by the end of FY2026. 
The renovation and rebranding will allow the hotel to reposition itself in the Pune hotel market, 
which has performed well over past few years. This will be the second Courtyard by Marriott 
owned by SAMHI Hotels, the other being its highly successful Courtyard by Marriott at Outer Ring 
Road, Bengaluru. 
It has also entered into a management agreement with Marriott for the rebranding of its 114 
rooms hotel in Jaipur to a Tribute Portfolio branded hotel. The conversion and rebranding will 
occur post a renovation set to conclude by the end of FY2026. Jaipur is a key tourist and business 
destination in India and has over the years drawn strong interest from both domestic and 
international visitors. The hotel, which is located in the heart of the city, will be redeveloped to 
be a part of the Tribute Portfolio, which is a collection of characterful, independent hotels drawn 
together by their passion for captivating design and their drive to create vibrant social scenes for 
guests and locals alike. The rebranding will allow the hotel to significantly improve its positioning 
in the market. 
In addition, SAMHI has also signed agreements with Marriott for the other four ACIC hotels to be 
converted from franchise to management agreements. 
Mr. Ashish Jakhanwala, Chairman & Managing Director of SAMHI Hotels Ltd. says, “We are 
pleased to announce a significant milestone in the integration process for the ACIC portfolio. 
Over the years, we have demonstrated our ability to drive performance & growth through 
turnarounds. With the proposed rebranding and conversion to management agreements, these 
hotels will be repositioned for better performance and improve their contribution to SAMHI”". 
About SAMHI Hotels Ltd. 
SAMHI is a prominent branded hotel ownership and asset management platform in India with an 
institutional ownership model, experienced leadership and professional management team. SAMHI has 
long-term management arrangements with three of the established and well recognized global hotel 
operators, namely, Marriott, IHG and Hyatt. SAMHI has a portfolio of 31 operating hotels comprising 4,801 
keys and has a diverse geographic presence in 13 cities across India, including National Capital Region 
(NCR), Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Pune. SAMHI has 2 hotels under development with a total of 
461 keys in Kolkata and Navi Mumbai.
