Press Release No. 23: Quarter 3 ended on 30 September 2026  
Major Project s and Export Orders Drive Continued Business Growth for KSB Limited in Q3 FY 2026 
 
• Quarterly sales reached INR 772.70 crore, reflecting an 18.95% year- on-year growth over the corresponding 
quarter last year.  
 
• Sales for the first nine months of 2026 amounted to INR 2,064.70 crore, representing an 8.00% increase 
compared with the same period in the previous year.  
 
 
 Major Highlights:  
 
1. Petrochemicals Projects  
Orders were received for multiple projects in the refinery and fertilizers sectors from various customers , 
domestic as well as international. The notable orders were received from Dangote for the upcoming fertilizer 
project and from ONGC for the replacement pumps.  
 
2. Nuclear Project –  Kaiga Units 5 & 6  
 Major order received for the Kaiga Nuclear Power Plant Units 5 & 6.  
 
3. Energy  Project  
In the energy sector, we received orders for boiler feed pumps and condensate extraction pumps from various 
OEMs and contractors , across both domestic and export markets.   
 
4. Major Valve Orders –  Multiple Projects  
Valve orders received for the NPP Recovery Boiler Project , Andritz Technologies, BPCL Bina Refinery, and  our 
major OEMs.  
 
5. Major Spare Parts Orders – Multiple Customers  
Spare parts orders were received for descaling pumps from the eastern region of India. We also received an 
order from Adani Power for the servicing and repair of boiler circulation pumps.

Summarizing the Q 3 2026 business performance, Mr. Prashant Kumar, Vice President - Sales and Marketing, KSB Limited, said , 
“The diverse mix of orders secured this quarter highlights KSB's strong positioning across process industries, energy, 
infrastructure and specialised project businesses. From petrochemical and LNG projects to refinery expansion and nuclear 
power application s, these orders reflect both the strength of our offerings and the confidence customers place in KSB. We 
remain focused on delivering the value to our customers through reliable products, technical excellence and responsive 
service.”  
 
Mr. Mahesh Bhave, Chief Financial Officer at KSB Limited, commented:  “Business momentum remained positive, with sales 
growth continuing during the first nine months of 2026. While margins remained under pressure, we continue to focus on 
operational efficiency, cost discipline and execution. Recent order s won in the energy sector, across both domestic and export 
markets, support a positive business outlook. ” 
 
About KSB Limited  
KSB Limited, founded in 1960 in India, is a part of KSB SE & Co. KGaA, one of the world’s leading suppliers of pumps, valves,  and 
systems. Combining innovative technology with excellent service to provide intelligent solutions, the company offers robust a nd 
reliable solutions catering to the diverse needs of customers in India and overseas across sectors such as power, oil, buildi ng 
services, process engineering, water treatment, and water transport. KSB specializes in Centrifugal End Suction Pumps, High -
Pressure Multistage Pumps, Industrial Gate, Globe and Check Valves, Submersible Motor Pumps, Monobloc and Mini Monobloc 
Pumps, Hydro pneumatic Systems, and Control Valves.  
Today, the KSB Group has a presence across all continents through its own sales and marketing companies, manufacturing 
facilities, and service operations. In 2025, the Group generated consolidated annual sales revenue exceeding €3 billion.
