RateGain Powers Sunlight Air’s Growth Journey with Smarter Pricing Decisions  
 
India , 15th January 2026 : RateGain Travel Technologies Limited (RateGain), a global provider of AI -powered 
SaaS solutions for travel and hospitality,  today announced that Sunlight Air, a boutique leisure airline based in the 
Philippines, has selected AirGain to gain deeper market visibility and strengthen its competitive edge across Southeast 
Asia . 
 
Known for offering curated travel experiences and seamless connectivity to the Philippines’ most stunning island 
destinations, Sunlight Air is expanding its presence in a market where travelers increasingly seek affordable yet 
personalized journeys. To kee p pace with growing demand and evolving traveler expectations, the airline will leverage 
real-time rate intelligence using AirGain’s unified platform, designed to deliver both rate intelligence and rate parity 
insights.  
 
The platform provides  competitive insights, market movements, and pricing anomalies into a single, intuitive 
dashboard. With AirGain, Sunlight Air can track fares across OTAs, airline sites, in real time, helping its teams anticipate 
demand shifts, protect yields, and craft str ategies that resonate with digitally savvy travelers. This capability enables 
Sunlight Air to balance affordability with premium leisure experiences, marking a major step in its regional growth 
journey.  
 
Commenting on the partnership, Carina G. Jayme,  Manager – Revenue Management  at Sunlight Air , said , “Our 
travelers choose Sunlight Air because we combine comfort and style with access to the Philippines’ most stunning 
destinations. As we expand across Southeast Asia, we need sharper visibility into pricing trends so we can continue to 
offer journeys that  are both accessible and rewarding. AirGain gives us the intelligence to anticipate shifts in demand 
and shape fares that keep our promise of affordable, experience -driven travel.”  
 
Vinay Varma, Senior Vice President and General Manager at AirGain , added, “ Competing in Southeast Asia 
requires real -time intelligence to strike that balance between affordability and quality. With AirGain’s AI-powered 
platform , Sunlight Air’s teams can respond faster to market changes, protect yields, and continue building their 
reputation as the leisure airline of choice for travelers seeking both comfort and value.”  
 
With this partnership, AirGain reinforces its commitment to helping regional and boutique airlines compete more 
effectively. As part of this mission, AirGain is introducing its AI -powered Route Performance Digest  to select airline 
partners. This daily automated insight highlights pricing anomalies and route -level performance gaps, enabling nimble 
carriers like Sunlight Air to respond quickly to market shifts while protecting both value and profitability .
