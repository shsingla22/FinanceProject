colleagues who worked tirelessly to stabilize operations —your dedication and ‘service from the heart’ 
enabled us to return swiftly to norma l operations . We are grateful  to the Government , Aviation Authorities  
and all other partners  in the Indian aviation ecosystem  for their support in helping restore normalcy.

customer experience .”
