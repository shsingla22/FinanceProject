Sterling  Holidays  Leads the Way for Destination Weddings . 
• 45 Resorts across hill stations, beaches, heritage and pilgrimage locations  
• Large resorts for Grand Weddings, Boutique resorts for Intimate Weddings  
At the recently concluded ET MICE and Wedding Tourism Awards 2024, Mumbai, Sterling  Puri emerged 
as the Best Destination Wedding Venue (East). This coveted award celebrates brands that excel in 
catering to Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, Events and Soci als / Wedding segment.  
Nestled in a picturesque estuary where the Dhaudia River meets the sea, Sterling Puri spread over a 
sprawling 15  acre s oﬀers the perfect setting for a destination wedding. With a breath -taking location, 
multiple venues, large lawns , spacious and well -appointed rooms featuring local Pattachitra artwork 
oﬀering  stunning views of the Bay of Bengal, this resort is an ideal destination for couples seeking to 
make their iconic day into an unforgettable memor y. 
With 45 destinations Sterlin g oﬀers a range of locations to choose from – hill stations, beaches, 
waterfronts, heritage and pilgrimage locations. Some of our l arge resorts in Alleppey, Ooty, Kodaikanal, 
Munnar, Darjeeling, Mount Abu, Puri etc. that are spread over multiple acres len d themselves to Grand 
Destination Weddings  and featur e multiple banquet halls of varying sizes and lush lawns for pre/post 
wedding ceremonies. Similarly , exuding the charm of the destinations, boutique resorts  like Anaikatti, 
Balicha, Kalimpong, Karwar,  Godavari, Guruvayur etc.  are ideal for couples wanting to have an intimate 
wedding with close friends and family – where they can take up the entire resort for the occasion – 
giving them total privacy and ﬂexibility of use of the resort and its fa cilities.  
The resorts provide creative fun games and activities to engage the guests in between the ceremonies. Live streaming facilities ensure that the celebrations can be shared with extended family, real -time.  
A dedicated wedding manager at the resort  ensures that every detail, from the planning stage to the 
wedding day rituals, is meticulously taken care of, allowing families to enjoy the event stress -free. 
Tailor -made selection of services for couples including gourmet specials, thematic setups, choi ces in 
transportation, and bridal services like pre -wedding bridal treatments at the signature Subuthi Spa are 
also provided.   
"We are thrilled with the recognition that Sterling Puri has received. It’s a testament to the trust of our 
many wedding guests and partners who look to celebrate their momentous day with us , and to our  
dedicated teams who help make their celebrations into memorable occasions. Sterling  caters to an 
entire range of events – from wedding proposals to pre -wedding shoots to wedding eve nts from ‘haldi’ 
to ‘bidaii’ to honeymoons and anniversaries!” said Harinath Mohanasundaram, Senior Vice President
