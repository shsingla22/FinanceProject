(C&P ) of OIL in presence of the Chairman & Managing Director  of OIL  along with 
Functional  Director s and senior official s of OIL and Dolphin Drilling Limited. The 
Blackford Dolphin will be deployed for drilling in Andaman Offshore Blocks to
