Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in  
Karnataka  
 
 
New Delhi, 21st October  2024: Lemon Tree Hotels announced its latest 
signing – Lemon Tree Hotel, Kalaburagi , Karnataka.  The property, which shall 
be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, is expected to open in FY 2027.  
 
Kalaburagi, a historical city in northern Karnataka, is renowned for its rich 
cultural heritage. With its stunning architectural landmarks like the Gulbarga 
Fort and the Khwaja Bande Nawaz Dargah, the city attracts visitors from 
everywhere. Kalaburagi's unique blend of Kannada and Urdu is evident in its 
language, cuisine, and festiv als. Other religious places of importance include 
Sharana Basaveshwara Temple, a shrine dedicated to a Lingayat religious 
teacher and philosopher, Shri Sharana Basaveshwara, a  saint of the 18th 
century  as well as the Buddha Vihara, a  Buddhist temple  and sp iritual center 
that blends architectural features of eminent Buddhist centers 
of Sanchi,  Sarnath, Ajanta and Nagpur. The city's thriving educational 
institutions and picturesque rural landscapes make it a captivating destination 
for those seeking to explor e the heart of Karnataka.  
 
Lemon Tree Hotel, Kalaburagi, Karnataka will feature 72 well -appointed rooms, a 
restaurant, a bar, a banquet hall, a meeting room, a swimming pool, a fitness 
center, a spa and other public areas. Kalaburagi Airport is approximate ly 14 kms 
while the Kalaburagi Railway Station is 6 kms from the property. The hotel is 
also well -connected by road, making it accessible by both public and private 
transport.  
 
Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO — Managed & Franchise Business, Lemon Tree Hotels said, 
"We are delighted to announce our further expansion in Karnataka, the land of 
Sandalwood. This new addition will complement our eight existing and one 
upcoming properties in the state, further enhancing our commitment to 
providing exceptional hospitality and  experiences in this beautiful region.”
