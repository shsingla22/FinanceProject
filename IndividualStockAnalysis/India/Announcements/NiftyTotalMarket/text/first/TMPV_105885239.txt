Tata Motors announces price increase of its commercial vehicles from July 2024  
Mumbai, 19th June  2024: Tata Motors announced that it will increase the price of its commercial vehicles effective 1st July 
2024, up to 2%. The price increase is to offset the impact of rising commodity prices. It will be applicable across the entir e 
range of commercial vehicles, and will vary as per individual model and variant .
