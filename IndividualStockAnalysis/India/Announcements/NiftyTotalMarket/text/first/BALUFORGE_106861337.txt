Q2& H1 FY27 Earnings Press Release  
 
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Jaspal Singh Chandock, Chairman & Managing Director of BFIL stated:  
“H1 FY27 has been a record half for Balu Forge , reflecting the strength of our business and the progress we are 
making towards the next phase of growth. Revenue grew 17% to Rs.625 crore, while PAT increased 17% to 
Rs.143 crore. EBITDA stood at Rs.179 crore, up 15%, with a margin of 28.6%.  Q2 EBITDA margin further improved 
by 130 bps to 28.9% . Equally encouraging is the strong cash generation, with operating cash flow reaching 
Rs.245 crore, nearly 7x the previous year. Receivables reduced by Rs.31.2 crore , while net debt -to-equity 
remained low at 0.0 4x, reflecting our focus on disciplined execution and a stronger balance sheet.  
Building on our 37-year engineering legacy , we are entering the next phase of growth by creating a globally 
competitive, integrated precision manufacturing platform.  Q2 provides the base before our new capacities 
begin contributing to growth.  We are scaling forging capacity from 100,000 MTPA to 168,000 MTPA  and 
precision machining capacity from 58,000 MTPA to 80,000 MTPA . Investments in advanced machining, 
automated forging and specialised manufacturing will enable us to bring more complex, higher -value 
capabilities in -house and deepen our partnerships with global OEMs.  
Our business mix is steadily shifting towards higher -value applications , with Defence, Aerospace and Railways 
contributing nearly 50% of our Rs.2,400 crore order book . The new 6.7 -metre ring rolling capability  further 
expands the range and complexity of components we can manufacture. With defence dispatches underway  
and expanded capacities progressively coming on stream, we remain confident of delivering 25–30% revenue 
growth in FY27.  
The promoter group's commitment of up to Rs.162 crore through  investments  reflects its confidence in our 
vision and growth plans. As we move ahead, our focus remains on technology -led expansion, operational 
excellence, prudent capital allocation and stronger customer relationships . Our ambition is not just to grow 
larger, but to build a globally trusted precision engineering business that creates sustainable, long -term value 
for all stakeholders.”

About Balu Forge Industries:  
Balu Forge Industries Limited, founded by Mr. Prehlad Singh Chandock, is a leading Indian precision engineering 
company delivering forged and machined components across multiple global industries. The company offers a 
comprehensive product portfolio rangin g from 1 kg to 1, 500 kg and up to 3 meters in length, supporting diverse 
applications in automotive, industrial vehicles, earthmoving equipment, wind energy, aerospace, defence, oil 
and gas, railways, marine, and agriculture. Its operations include fully integrated forging and machining 
capabilities, with advanced manufacturing facilities in Belgaum, Karnataka, spread over a 46+ acre campus. 
Equipped with high -capacity hydraulic hammers and forging presses, and supported by a dedicated inhouse 
tool room, metallurgical labs, and CNC machining units, Balu Forge ensures consistent precision and quality. The 
company’s strategy is driven by innovation, with a specialized R&D division focusing on new materials and rapid 
prototyping. Strategic initiatives emphasize expanding defence  production, enhancing automation, and 
strengthening global partnerships. With a strong focus on operational scalability, customer diversification, and 
ESG commitments, Balu Forge continues to strengthen its global footprint and industry positioning. For m ore 
details, please visit: https:/ /www.baluindustries.com/
