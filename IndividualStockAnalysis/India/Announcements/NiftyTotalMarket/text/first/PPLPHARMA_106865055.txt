Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 as amended (‘SEBI Listing Regulations’) – exercise 
of call option to acquire the balance 26% equity stake in Yapan Bio Private Limited  
 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that the Administrative Committee of the Board of Directors 
of Piramal Pharma Limited (‘the Company’), acting under the authority delegated by the Board, has 
approved the exercise of the Company's call option to acquire the balance 26% equity stake in Yapan 
Bio Private Limited (‘Yapan’), a subsidiary of the Company, from its existing shareholders (‘the 
Proposed Transaction’).  
 
Consequent to the exercise of the call option, the Company is engaged in discussions with the 
shareholders of Yapan in relation to matters necessary for consummation of the Proposed 
Transaction.  
 
The final terms of the acquisition, including the acquisition consideration and the effective date of 
transfer of shares, are yet to be finalized. The Company will make appropriate disclosures including 
the details as required under Regulation 30 of the Securities Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with Securities Exchange of 
Board of India Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 
2026, upon execution of definitive documentation and completion of the Proposed Transaction. 
 
Yapan is a Contract Development and Manufacturing Organization that specializes in process 
development, characterization and Phase I/II GMP manufacturing services for vaccines and 
biologics.  
 
Upon completion of the proposed transaction the Company’s shareholding in Yapan is expected to 
increase from the existing 74% to 100%, and Yapan will become a wholly owned subsidiary of the 
Company.
