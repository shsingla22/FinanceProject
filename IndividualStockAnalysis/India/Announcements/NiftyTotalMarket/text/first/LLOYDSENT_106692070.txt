I am pleased to share the Companies group investment position.
1. Lloyds Realty Developers Limited
Our real estate business continues to perform strongly, expanding its presence across the
Mumbai Metropolitan Region and nearby areas including Panvel, Khopoli, Dadar, and Thane.
With nearly 14 million square feet of upcoming projects under development, the Company
has strong growth visibility for the future. Importantly, as a result of the merger and demerger
scheme approved during the year, our real estate business will be listed, unlocking its true
potential and delivering greater value to shareholders.
2. Lloyds Engineering Works Limited
Our material subsidiary, Lloyds Engineering Works Limited, continued its stro ng growth
trajectory, reporting its highest-ever revenue and profit after tax in FY26. Together with
subsidiaries and associates, the Group maintained a robust order book of approximately
₹8,000 crores and achieved profit before tax growth of 44% during the year.
 3. Geomysore Services (India) Pvt. Ltd.
Lloyds Enterprises holds around 31% stake in Geomysore Services, which operates India’s first
private gold mine post-independence. The mine has already commenced production and is
expected to produce approximately 600 kilograms of gold in FY27, supported by substantial
reserves for long-term operations. This investment is poised to become a steady and valuable
contributor to our growth in the coming years.
4. Lloyds Metals and Energy Limited
Our flagship mining and metals company continues to strengthen its position as an
integrated mining-to-metal enterprise. Lloyds Enterprises Limited holds around 4.13% stake
directly and indirectly, and during the year subscribed to 75,00,002 share warra nts, which
were converted into equity shares. Lloyds Metals has also diversified into the copper sector
through an acquisition in the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC), reflecting our confidence
in its long-term growth potential and creating significant value for shareholders
