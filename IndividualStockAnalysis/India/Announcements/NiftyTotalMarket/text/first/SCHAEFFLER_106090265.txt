Schaeffler India appoints Mr. Sharad Bhatia as Head of Vehicle Lifetime 
Solutions Business Division in India  
Pune, India | 20th December  2024  – Schaeffler India  has announced  the appointment of 
Mr. Sharad Bhatia  as Head of Vehicle Lifetime Solutions (VLS), effective 2 December 2024. 
In his new role, Mr. Bhatia will spearhead Schaeffler India’s efforts to strengthen its 
presence in the mobility ecosystem,  reinforcing the company's commitment to providing 
sustainable, advanced solutions in motion technology.  
Mr. Bhatia brings over 24 years of extensive experience in the automotive parts industry. 
He has a proven track record in establishing robust business platforms and launching 
innovative product lines tailored to meet evolving customer needs.  His expertise in the 
aftermarket service parts sector is a valuable asset  for Schaeffler India, particularly as the 
company expands its VLS business segment aimed at enhancing vehicle longevity and 
sustainability through advanced technological solutions.  
In addition to his experience in India, Mr. Bhatia has held positions in  various multinational 
organizations across international markets, including Indonesia, China, and Singapore  and 
Italy.  
Commenting on the appointment, Mr. Harsha Kadam, MD & CEO of Schaeffler India , said : 
“We are delighted to welcome Mr. Sharad Bhatia to Schaeffler India as Head of Vehicle 
Lifetime Solutions. The Vehicle Lifetime Solutions business is an important business division 
for the Strategic growth of Schaeffler in India. His extensive background in the aftermarket 
business, combined with his experience in multinational markets, aligns well with our 
strategic goals. Sharad’s leadership will be pivotal in strengthenin g and enhancing the 
agility of our VLS business, further reinforcing our commitment to sustainable motion 
technology solutions in India. ” 
Expressing his enthusiasm for the new role, Mr. Sharad Bhatia  shared : “I am excited to 
join Schaeffler India at a time when the automotive industry is rapidly transforming. My 
focus will be on expanding our aftermarket solutions portfolio by leveraging advanced 
motion technology to deliver value and innovation. I look forward to working closely with 
the talented team at Schaeffler India to strengthen our Vehicle  Lifetime Solutions division 
and provide our customers with sustainable, reliable, and forward -looking solutions. ” 
Mr. Bhatia holds a bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering from Pune University  
(1998) and an Executive MBA from the Indian Institute of Management (IIM), Calcutta 
(2009). His certification in advanced technology for E -mobility  from Indian Institute of 
Technology Delhi  in 2022 further underscores his forward -looking vision in the automotive 
sector, particularly as India’s mobility landscape continues to transition towards greener 
solutions.
