HCLTech ’s network solution  wins 2025 Product of the Year a ward  at NAB Show  
 
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, May  07, 2025 —HCLTech,  a leading global technology company, 
announced that its ATSC 3.0 Broadcast Core Network solution won  the 2025 National 
Association of Broadcasters (NAB)  Show’s Product of the Year a ward  in the  Cloud Computing 
and Storage category . This marks the third year in a row that HCLTech has received this 
accolade.   
 
The solution leverages an Agentic AI framework to help broadcasters monetize their excess 
spectrum and offer various non- core services such as datacasting and AI -based media 
management. This facilitates the efficient deployment and management of TV channels and 
datacas ting services from a single interface, supporting a multi -vendor environment.  
 
"Congratulations to HCLTech on winning the 2025 NAB Show Product of the Year Award for 
ATSC 3.0 Broadcast Core Network —  a breakthrough solution transforming a pivotal stage of 
the content lifecycle and empowering storytellers to meet today's  challenges while shaping 
the future of media ," said Eric Trabb, Senior Vice President and Chief Customer Success 
Officer , NAB.  
 
"We are delighted to be recognized by NAB as an industry leader, helping broadcasters adopt 
new business models and transform  with the latest digital technology to modernize and 
monetize their assets," said Anil Ganjoo, Chief Growth Officer, Americas, TMT and RCPG Industries, HCLTech. "Incorporating AI into our solutions has further enhanced our ability to 
deliver cutting -edge technology that meets the evolving needs of our clients and supports 
industry growth ." 
For more information about the 2025 NAB Show Product of the Year Awards , visit:
