Capillary  Strengthens  US Leadership,  Names  Bill Swift  as President,  Americas  
 
Longtime  Brierley+Partners  leader  and former  Brierley  CEO,  who helped  build  the company 
alongside founder Hal Brierley, to lead Capillary's growth across the Americas  
 
 
Dallas, TX, October 29, 2026 — Capillary Technologies, a leading provider of AI -powered loyalty  
and customer  engagement  solutions,  today  announced  the appointment  of Bill Swift  as President, 
Americas. The move deepens Capillary’s commitment across the Americas as brands 
increasingly see loyalty not as just a program, but as a catalyst for growth, built on lasting, data -
driven customer relat ionships.  
Swift has spent nearly 30 years helping the world’s leading brands build lasting customer 
relationships and measurable business results. He joined Brierley+Partners in 2001 to spearhead  
its loyalty platform and partnered closely with Hal Brierley , the founder of Brierley+Partners and 
widely known as the founder of loyalty, as the company grew into a leading loyalty partner to 
global brands. He was named CEO of Brierley in 2022, completing the company’s evolution into 
a technology -enabled loyalty p rovider that pairs industry -leading consultative  depth  with the 
execution  needed  to deliver  those  results.  Since  April 2023,  following Brierley’s acquisition by 
Capillary, he has been part of the Brierley – Capillary leadership team  

as Chief  Integration  Officer,  leading  the integration  of acquisitions  and joint ventures  and playing 
a central role in building Capillary into the company it is today.  
In his new role, Swift  will lead Capillary’s  business  across  the Americas,  including  sales,  client 
success and delivery. He will drive its growth with enterprise brands across retail, hospitality, 
travel, airlines and all major focus verticals, and will help shape Capillary’s loyalty strategy for 
clients across the region. He brings deep experience he lping enterprise brands use loyalty to 
drive revenue, retention and growth, along with a firsthand understanding of how to bring 
organizations and teams together.  
“Loyalty  has always  been  about  earning  the right to a lasting  relationship  with the customer,  and 
that has not changed. What has changed is its role. The most successful brands no longer see 
loyalty as a program. They see it as a catalyst for growth. That happens when marketing and 
technology leaders work as one, with technology as the enabler tha t delivers powerful customer 
experiences and real business results. The Americas are home to some of the most sophisticated 
loyalty leaders in the world, and they are ready to put loyalty at the center of how they  grow.  With 
Capillary’s  AI-powered  platform  and the loyalty  expertise  our team  has built over decades, we can 
help brands build relationships that are more personal, more predictive and more profitable,” said 
Bill Swift, President, Americas, Capillary Technologies.  
“Bill’s leadership was an essenti al part of Brierley+Partners’ success, creating the platform that 
allowed us to drive some of the nation’s most successful loyalty programs. For over twenty years, 
he has been a trusted partner in shaping the strategies that drive customer loyalty and deli ver 
measurable  results.  Capillary  is fortunate  to have  Bill delivering  the technology  solutions that its 
clients demand,” said Hal Brierley, Founder, Brierley+Partners.  
“Bill has spent more than two decades shaping how leading brands think about and run lo yalty at  
scale,  and since  joining  us he has been  instrumental  in building  Capillary  into what  it is today. His 
strategic depth and his understanding of what truly drives customer relationships make him the  
right leader  to accelerate  our growth  across  the Americas,”  said Anant  Choubey,  co-founder & 
COO, Capillary Technologies.
