improve efficiency, reduce costs and enhance transparency.

direct subsidiary of MHL and in turn of the Company; and PSL would become direct subsidiary  of 
MHL and in turn of the  Company  as per the Companies Act, 2013 . 
  
Consequent to MMCL and PSL becoming subsidiaries of the Company, New Democratic Electoral 
Trust (‘NDET’)  and Kota Farms Services Limited (‘KFSL’)  would also become subsidiaries of the 
Company  as per the Companies Act, 2013 .
