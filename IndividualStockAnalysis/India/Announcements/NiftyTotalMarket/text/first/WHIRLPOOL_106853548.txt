WHIRLPOOL OF INDIA DELIVERS DOUBLE DIGIT REVENUE GROWTH IN Q2  
 
Q2 2026-27 KEY HIGHLIGHTS  
  
● Consolidated Revenue from operations: Rs 1873 Cr  (14% increase YoY)  
● Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 40 Cr, 2.1%, lower by 140 bps vs LY  
● Consolidated PBT (before exceptional items): Rs. 34 Cr, 1.8%, lower by 110 bps vs LY  
 
 
For the quarter concluding September 30, 2026, Whirlpool of India recorded strong top line 
momentum and delivered Consolidated Revenue from Operations of Rs 1,873 Cr, a 14% increase over 
the previous year.  Revenue growth vs LY was driven by industry volume growth, strategic price actions 
& continued premiumisation of our product portfolio.  
Further accelerating our premiumisation journey, we proudly launched "Luxuriem"  during the 
quarter. This premium 4-door, 654-litre refrigerator range is meticulously designed for the discerning, 
modern Indian consumer seeking the perfect balance of sophisticated aesthetics, intelligent capability, 
and intuitive everyday usability. 
Consolidated EBITDA and PBT stood at Rs 40 Cr and Rs 34 Cr respectively. Profits for Q2 2026-27 faced 
headwinds from commodity inflation fueled by geopolitical instability, increase in crude oil prices and 
currency depreciation. Additionally, margins were impacted by cost upcharges stemming from new 
energy regulations in the refrigerator & air conditioner categories and investments in Luxuriem launch. 
 
These cost pressures were partially mitigated through strategic price increases and robust internal 
cost-productivity initiatives, primarily the company's P4G program, which continues to deliver cost 
efficiencies across all lines of the P&L. To further safeguard margins against ongoing inflationary 
pressures, we implemented an additional price increase of ~5%, effective November 1, 2026.   
 
Elica India, the company’s subsidiary, sustained its robust growth trajectory, delivering a 12% increase 
in revenue and a 22% increase in PBT for the second quarter of FY 26-27, continuing to add significant 
value to the consolidated entity.  
 
About Whirlpool of India Limited 
Whirlpool of India Limited (WOIL), headquartered in Gurugram, is one of the leading manufacturers 
and marketers of major home appliances in the country. It operates three state-of-the-art 
manufacturing facilities at Faridabad, Puducherry and Pune. Each facility's infrastructure attests to 
WOIL's commitment to providing its consumers with the best in class solutions. Additional company 
information is available at www.india.whirlpool.in
