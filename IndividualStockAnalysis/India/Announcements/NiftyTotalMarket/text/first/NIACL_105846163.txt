Sub: Entrustment of financial and administrative powers and functions of the Chairman-
cum-Managing Director to Executive Directors 
Subsequent to the superannuation of Mrs Neerja Kapur, Chairman cum Managing Director on 
30th April 2024, the Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance has issued a 
communication bearing ref no. F. No. A- 11011/07/2023-1ns.1 dated 13th May 2024 (copy 
enclosed), which states as under: 
"In view of the vacancy of Chairman-cum-Managing Director (CMD) which has arisen on 
1.5.2024 consequent upon superannuation of Smt. Neerja Kapur, the Board of Directors and the 
Management Committee, as the case may be, of The New India Assurance Company Limited 
(NIACL), may entrust to look after the financial and administrative powers and functions of the 
post of CMD, NIACL to respective Executive Directors till such time appointment to the post of 
CMD, NIACL is made by the Government" 
Hence, Board in its Board meeting dated 14th May, 2024 approved the proposal to entrust the 
financial and administrative powers and functions of the post of Chairman cum Managing 
Director, to respective Executive Directors (Mr. Titus Francis and Smt. Smita Srivastava) till 
such time, the appointment to the post of CMD is made by the Government. 
Kindly take the same on record.
