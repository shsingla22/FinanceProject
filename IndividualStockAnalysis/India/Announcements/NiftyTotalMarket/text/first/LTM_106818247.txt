LTM Secures Multi-million Dollar Managed Services Transformation 
Deal 
To Build and Deploy an AI-led Managed Services Operating Model  
 
Mumbai, India – October 15, 2026: LTM , the Business Creativity partner to the world's largest 
enterprises, today announced a multi-million-dollar agreement with a leading North American 
specialty engineering and construction company. The engagement consolidates 
infrastructure, cybersecurity and application administration managed services from multiple 
providers to LTM, with an initial three-year term.    
 
As part of the agreement, LTM will build an AI-led managed services operating model designed 
to deliver outcome-driven services, incorporating observability and automation to ensure 
resilient technology services.    
 
The engagement marks a significant step in the client's technology modernization journey, 
consolidating critical IT operations under a unified governance framework designed to 
enhance operational efficiency, improve service quality, and reduce management complexity. 
Leveraging its deep domain expertise and AI-centric managed services capabilities, LTM will 
consolidate infrastructure, cybersecurity, and application services into a streamlined, 
outcome-driven operating model that accelerates modernization while strengthening 
operational resilience. 
 
"As enterprises modernize their technology landscapes, they are increasingly seeking 
strategic partners that can combine operational excellence with AI-driven innovation," said 
Rajesh Sundaram , Chief Business Officer, Manufacturing and Retail CPG, LTM. "This 
engagement reflects our ability to help clients unlock greater value from their technology 
investments, enhance business agility, and create a foundation for sustained growth through 
AI-enabled operations."
