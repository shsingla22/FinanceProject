Prestige Clocks ₹1,38,043 Million in H1 FY27 Pre -sales; Ropes in CPP Investments for Hospitality, 
₹4,00,000 Million Launch Pipeline Lined Up for H2  
 
Bengaluru, 13th October, 2026:  Prestige Estates Projects Limited ("Prestige", “Company”), one of 
India's leading real estate developers, announced its operational performance for the quarter and 
half-year ended September 30, 2026. The Company sustained healthy m omentum through the first 
half of FY27, with all business verticals, viz. Residential, Commercial, Retail, Hospitality and 
Property Management, continuing to perform well, reflecting the strength of the Company's 
diversified platform and its ability to del iver consistent operational performance across market 
cycles.  
 
During Q2 FY27, the Company launched / commenced construction on 9 projects  across its 
residential, commercial, retail and hospitality segments, with a combined developable area of 10.09  
million square feet , taking total for H1 FY27 to 30.25 million square feet . The Company also 
completed 5.07 million square feet  during the quarter, taking total completions for H1 FY27 to 9.44 
million square feet . A segment -wise break -up of launches and completions is provided in the 
respective sections below.  
 
 
Operational Highlights – Residential  
 
• The residential business recorded Pre-sales of ₹72,250 million  (up 20% year -on-year) during 
Q2 FY27, with sales volume of 5.84 million square feet  and 3,910 units sold . 
• For H1 FY27, the Company recorded Pre-sales of ₹ 1,38,043 million , with sales volume of 
11.88 million square feet  and 7,247 units sold . 
• Bengaluru  was the largest contributor to half -yearly sales, accounting for 41%  of total sales, 
followed by Hyderabad (32%) , Mumbai (12%) , Chennai (8%) , and 7% from other markets, 
demonstrating the Company's well -diversified geographical presence.  
• During Q2 FY27, the Company achieved an average realization of ₹12,375 per square foot , 
while the half-yearly average realization stood at ₹11,618 per square foot .  
• Collections remained robust at ₹52,941 million  during the quarter, taking H1 FY27 
collections to ₹1,00,963 million , reflecting healthy customer demand and strong execution 
across projects.  
• During the quarter, the Company launched 3 residential projects  with a combined 
developable area of 5.91 million square feet , and a GDV of approximately ₹40,200 million . 
Residential Launches for H1 FY27 aggregated 23.14 million square feet  with a GDV of over

Commenting on the operational performance, Mr. Irfan Razack, Chairman and Managing 
Director, Prestige Group said:  
 
“We have seen a good performance across our business verticals, with each of our asset classes contributing to 
the overall performance during the quarter. This reflects the strength and diversity of our portfolio.  
Our residential business continues to see healthy demand. What is particularly encouraging is the response to 
our new launches, a couple  of which were introduced towards the end of the quarter but nevertheless saw good 
sales traction. Sustenance sales have also remained strong, reinforcing our view that underlying demand for 
housing continues to be healthy.  
 
We have a substantial launch pipeline of over ₹4,00,000 million planned across Bengaluru, Mumbai, NCR, 
Chennai and Hyderabad over the next two quarters. This gives us a strong platform to carry the momentum 
forward, and there is a great deal to look forward to in the coming months.  
 
We have also taken an important step in our hospitality business through our partnership with CPP 
Investments in Prestige Hospitality Ventures. This investment will support the next phase of growth in our 
hospitality portfolio as we focus on executing our development pipeline and pursuing opportunities to expand 
our presence in the sector.”  
 
About Prestige  
 
Prestige Group is one of India's leading and most diversified real estate developers, with a legacy

integrated township projects across major cities. As o f June 2026, the Group has delivered 319 
projects spanning 216 million sq. ft. and currently has a pipeline of 137 projects across 229 million 
sq. ft. For more information, visit www.prestigeconstructions.com
