Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015  – Signing of Teaming Agreement with High Performance Space Structure Systems GmBH, Germany  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, w e are 
pleased to inform that M/s. Paras Defence and Space Technologies Limited (“PARAS”) has signed a Teaming 
Agreement with High Performance Space Structure Systems GmBH, Germany (“HPS”) with an intention to working  
together  for projects  related  to Defence  & Space  in the Indian  Region.  
 
HPS has experience and expertise in  the area of Design,  Manufacturing,  Integration and Test  of 
Unfurlable/Deployable  Antenna Reflector Subsystems  for Space Applications and in other areas such as solid reflector 
antennas, lightweight structures, thermal hardware, nanosatellite equipment.  
 
PARAS and HPS will complement the unique capabilities of each  other and will collaborate exclusively with each 
other for the  cutting -edge  identified Product i.e. “Unfurlable/Deployable Antenna Reflector Subsystems (including 
reflector and arm assembly, HDRM, Thermal Hardware, Deployment Electronics)” in the Indian region.  
 
Further, the details as required under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
read with SEBI circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024  is attached herewith as 
Annexure A . 
 
Kindly take the above information on record and acknowledge the receipt.
