results for the period April- June, FY 202 5-26 (Quarter 1).

n Quarter 1, the domestic passenger vehicle industry continued to witness  a sluggish  demand environment .

total of 527,861 vehicles during the Quarter, comprising domestic sales of 430,889 units and exports of 
96,972 units.
