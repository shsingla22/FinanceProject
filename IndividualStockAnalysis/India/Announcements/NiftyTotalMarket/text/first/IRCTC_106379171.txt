Sub:  Intimation regarding revision in Maximum Retail Price (MRP) of Rail Neer 
(1000 ml and 500 ml) bottles pursuant to reduction in GST on Packaged 
Drinking Water (PDW) from 18% to 5%. 
Sir/ Madam,  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, this is to inform that 
consequent to the reduction in Goods and Services Tax (GST) on Packaged Drinking 
Water (PDW) from 18% to 5%, the Ministry of Railways, Government of India, has 
approved the revision in the Maximum Retail Price (MRP) of Rail Neer bottles, as tabled 
below: 
Pack Size  Existing MRP ( ₹) Revised MRP ( ₹) Effective Date  
1000 ml ₹15 ₹14 22nd September, 2025 
500 ml ₹10 ₹9 22nd September, 2025 
 
The above revision is in line with the directives of the Government and to pass on the 
benefit of the reduced tax burden to railway passengers.  
This is for your information and record please.
