Headline Results 
▪Gross Transaction Value (GTV) stood at ₹5,304.47 Cr in Q2 FY27, registering 22% YoY growth compared
to ₹4,347.50 Cr in Q2 FY26.
▪Revenue from Operations grew 13% YoY to ₹320.96 Cr in Q2 FY27 from ₹284.23 Cr in Q2 FY26.
▪Contribution Margin (CM) increased 14% YoY to ₹124.86 Cr in Q2 FY27 from ₹109.58 Cr in Q2 FY26.
▪Adjusted EBITDA (EBITDA plus ESOP Expenses less Other Income) stood at ₹23.58 Cr in Q2 FY27, down 17% YoY 
from ₹28.48 Cr in Q2 FY26.
▪Proﬁt before share of loss of an associate, exceptional items and tax, stood at ₹33.53 Cr in Q2 FY27
▪Q2 FY27 also recorded an all-time high Proﬁt After Tax at ₹63.42 Cr Key Performance Highlights - Q2 FY27Note:
1. GTV (Gross Transaction Value) refers to the total amount paid (including taxes, fees and service charges,
gross of all discounts) by users for the OTA services and products booked through us in the relevant period/year. 
2.Contribution Margin is deﬁned as net ticketing revenue plus other operating revenue less direct expenses. 
3.Adjusted EBITDA is calculated as the restated proﬁt for the period or year plus tax expense, ﬁnance cost, depreciation,
amortization expenses, Employee Stock Option Scheme less other income, exceptional items, share of proﬁt/loss of associate.
