Sub.:  Update on intimation dated 30th December, 2025 regarding GST Demand 
Order  
 
Ref.: Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III to  the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing 
Regulations”)  
 
Dear Sir / Madam,  
 
This is in continuation  to the Company’s intimation dated 30th December, 2025 , 
regarding an Order passed by the Deputy Commissioner, Rudrapur  - I, Uttarakhand  
(“GST Order”) . The GST Order pertained  to the financial year 2021 -22 and  involved 
a tax demand of Rs. 12.39 crore , and a penalty of Rs. 1.13 crore . This order was 
based , inter alia , on alleged discrepancies including mismatch of input tax credit 
(“ITC”)  compared to  details stated in GSTR -2A, reversal of  ITC by customers, sundry 
creditors  and eligibility of ITC.  
 
In accordance with  Regulation 30(7) of the Listing Regulations, we further inform 
that the Company had filed a Writ Petition  before the Hon’ble H igh Court of 
Uttarakhand  (“High Court”)  challeng ing the GST Order . The petition sought,  inter 
alia, to set aside the GST Order  that was issued without providing an opportunity 
for a personal hearing.  
 
The matter was listed for hearing on 25th March, 2026 and the order copy was 
received on 29th March, 2026 . The Hon’ble High Court has quashed  the GST Order 
and remanded the matter back to the relevant authority for reassessment , after 
considering submissions made by the Company .  
 
Request you to take the above information on record.  
 
Thanking you .
