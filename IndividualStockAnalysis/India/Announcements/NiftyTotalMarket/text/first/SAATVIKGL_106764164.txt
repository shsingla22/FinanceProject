Saatvik Solar Industries Applies for ALMM List -II Enlistment for Its 2.4 GW 
Odisha Solar Cell Facility  
 
• Facility to manufacture high- efficiency, advanced cutting- edge technology  N-TOPCon G12R 
solar  cells under DCR category, strengthening Saatvik's domestic solar manufacturing 
capabilities  
 
National, 1st September  2026:  Saatvik Solar Industries Private Limited, material subsidiary of Saatvik 
Green Energy Limited, is advancing its 2.4 GW solar cell manufacturing facility in Odisha towards 
enlistment under ALMM List -II, marking an important step in the company’s plans to scale up high -
efficiency, advanced  cutting- edge technology solar cell manufacturing and strengthen India's 
domestic solar PV value chain.  
 
Located at Ganjam, Odisha, the facility has an applied manufacturing capacity of 2,400 MW and is 
being developed to manufacture Saatvik's own portfolio of high -efficiency, high -wattage solar cell 
manufacturing with the latest N -TOPCon technology . The facility's ALMM List -II submission covers 
Monocrystalline Bifacial N -TOPCon G12R  solar cell technology under the Domestic Content 
Requirement (DCR) category, with Model No. SS -NTP-210R -16BB -BF. 
 The development positions Saatvik to expand its manufacturing capabilities further upstream in the 
solar PV value chain, as India continues to build domestic capacity in advanced solar technologies. 
Subject to MNRE's approval and enlistment, the facility w ill be eligible for deployment in projects 
requiring compliance with applicable domestic sourcing requirements.  
 
Commenting on the development , Mr Prashant Mathur, CEO, Saatvik Green Energy  said, "The 
advancement of our Odisha cell manufacturing facility towards ALMM List -II enlistment is an 
important milestone in our ambition to build advanced and competitive solar manufacturing 
capabilities in India. With a planned capacity of 2.4 GW and a focus on high- efficiency N -TOPCon 
technology, the facility will strengthen our ability to offer domestically manufactured solar cells while 
supporting the industry's broader localisation journey. We remain focused on investing in technology, scale and manufactur ing capabilities that can contribute to India's long- term renewable energy 
ambitions."  
 The Odisha facility forms part of Saatvik's broader manufacturing expansion strategy, with the company increasing its focus on high -efficiency cell and module technologies to address the evolving 
requirements of India's rapidly growing solar market. The fa cility will manufacture N -TOPCon G12R 
cells, a technology designed to deliver higher conversion efficiency and enhanced power output, 
supporting the industry's continued shift towards higher -performing PV products.  The development 
also strengthens Saatvik's manufacturing presence in Odisha and is expected to create opportunities 
across the regional industrial and supplier ecosystem.  
 
Saatvik Green Energy has been expanding its presence across the renewable energy value chain, with 
capabilities spanning utility -scale solar power generation, solar PV module manufacturing and energy 
storage solutions. The company currently has 4.86 GW of module manufacturing capacity and is 
pursuing further expansion across solar cells and modules as part of its long -term manufacturing 
roadmap.  

 
The company's focus on domestic cell manufacturing comes at a time when India is accelerating efforts to develop a resilient and self- reliant solar manufacturing ecosystem. The combination of 
domestic manufacturing capacity, advanced cell technology and DC R-compliant production is 
expected to play an increasingly important role in meeting the country's growing renewable energy requirements while strengthening supply -chain resilience.  
 With the Odisha facility, Saatvik aims to deepen its manufacturing capabilities and contribute to the development of a technology -led, locally anchored and globally competitive solar manufacturing 
ecosystem in India.  
 
  
About Saatvik Green Energy Limited  
 
Saatvik Green Energy Limited is one of India’s leading solar photovoltaic module manufacturers, with 
a strong presence across utility -scale, commercial & industrial, EPC, and distributed solar segments. 
The Company currently operates a 4.8 6 GW module manufacturing facility in Ambala, Haryana, and 
is developing a Greenfield integrated manufacturing facility in Odisha with 4 GW of module capacity and 6 GW of solar cell capacity. With a focus on latest technology , quality, and execution, Saatvik 
aims to play a meaningful role in India’s renewable energy transition.
