The Share Allotment and Transfer Committee of the Board of Directors of the Company viz. Havells 
India Limited, in its meeting held today on 16th May, 2025, has allotted 2,64,689 (Two Lakhs Sixty 
Four Thousand Six Hundred and Eighty Nine Only) Equity Shares of Re.1/- each to the Havells 
Employees Welfare Trust (‘the Trust’) under the prevailing ESPS Schemes of the Company, as 
follows:- 
- Havells Employees Stock Purchase Plan 2014 (ESPS 2014) – 73,082 Shares;  
- Havells Employees Stock Purchase Scheme 2015 (ESPS 2015) – 1,50,000  Shares; and  
- Havells Employees Stock Purchase Scheme 2016 (ESPS 2016) – 41,607 Shares, 
which will be transferred to eligible employees against the Exercise of equivalent no. of shares. 
 
The Meeting of the Share Allotment and Transfer Committee commenced at 4:30 pm and 
concluded at 5:00 pm.
