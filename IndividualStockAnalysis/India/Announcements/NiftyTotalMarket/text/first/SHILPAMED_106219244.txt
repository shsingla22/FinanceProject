Shilpa Medicare’s Biologics arm Shilpa Biologics  site at Dharwad, Karnataka , has received its European GMP 
certification for manufacturing of test batches based on the inspection between 18 Feb to  20 Feb 2025, 
conducted by the competent authority of Austria.  The inspection covering the sterile drug product filling and 
packaging line, quality systems and quality control laboratories concluded compliance with the principles and 
guidelines of Good Ma nufacturing Practice laid down in Directive (EU) 2017/1572.   
  
Dr. Sridevi Khambhampaty, CEO of Shilpa Biologics commented “This inspection outcome affirms our     
commitment to global quality standards and assures reliable outcomes for our customers .”
