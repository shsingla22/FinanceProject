Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 [“SEBI (LODR) Regulations”], it is hereby informed that the Ministry of 
Railways vide its order no. 2026/PL/57/16 dated 13th August, 2026 (copy enclosed) conveyed

whichever is earlier. 
 
The Company is taking necessary actions to complete the appointment formalities in compliance 
with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the SEBI (LODR) Regulations and requisite 
disclosures in this regard will be submitted in due course.
