PRESS RELEASE 
Karnataka Bank Empaneled as Arranger for HUDCO Capital Gain Bonds 
Karnataka Bank is pleased to announce its empanelment as an Arranger & Collector for 
HUDCO Capital Gain Bonds, further strengthening the Bank's commitment to offering 
a comprehensive range of investment solutions to its customers. 
With this empanelment, Karnataka Bank joins a select group of arrangers facilitating 
investments in Government-backed Capital Gain Bonds issued by Housing and Urban 
Development Corporation Limited (HUDCO), Rural Electrification Corporation (REC), 
Power Finance Corporation (PFC), and Indian Railway Finance Corporation (IRFC), 
under Section 85 of the Income Tax Ad, 202S (Iu1rneily SediuH G4EC ui Lhe lucume Tax 
Act, 1961). 
Capital Gain Bonds offer eligible taxpayers an opportunity to save tax on long-term 
capital gains while investing in secure, Government-backed instruments. Through this 
arrangement, Karnataka Bank customers can conveniently subscribe to HUDCO 
Capital Gain Bonds across the Bank's extensive branch network, backed by dedicated 
relationship management and seamless customer service. 
The addition of HUDCO Capital Gain Bonds further strengthens Karnataka Bank's 
investment offerings, reflecting its continued focus on innovative financial products 
tailored to the evolving needs of retail and institutional customers. 
Customers may visit their nearest Karnataka Bank branch for detailed information on 
eligibility, investment limits, subscription process, and applicable terms and conditions.
