c. A brief on types of customers
  Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (BPCL) is a leading Oil and Gas company, providing services to both retail and 
bulk customers. Through its extensive network of retail outlets and LPG distributorships, BPCL ensures a consistent 
and reliable supply of fuel and related services. In addition to serving retail customers, BPCL also caters to the energy 
needs of bulk customers, which include the Defense Forces, Indian Railways, State government organizations, State 
transport undertakings, power producers, etc. This comprehensive approach allows BPCL to play a crucial role in 
meeting the energy demands of multiple sectors, industries and retail consumers across the country.
