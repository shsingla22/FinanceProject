LG INTRODUCES NEW RANGE OF WASH ING MACHINES WITH AI DD 2.0 
TECHNOLOGY 
 
New Delhi, 9th December, 2025 – LG Electronics India Ltd. today announced th e launch of its 
latest Washing Machine line up, engineered with  premium design and cutting edge technology. 
The new range includes 10 models ranging from Wash er Dryer, Front Load Washing Machines to 
Top Load Washing Machines equipped with advan ced AI for superior fabric care, intelligent 
washing, and enhanced convenience. The new ra nge blends innovation with contemporary design 
to simplify everyday laundry routines for Indian households.  
LG’s consumer campaign slogan for the new line-up, “AI Detection. Washing Perfection”, highlights 
how AI DD 2.0 reads fabric type and wash requirements with precision to deliver optimal results with 
minimal effort. 
 
Sanjay Chitkara, Co-Chief Sales & Marketing Officer,  LG Electronics India Ltd. said, “At LG, 
we are committed to making life comfortable for our customers by simplifying their daily tasks 
through smart technology. The new AIDD 2.0 Washing machine is a testament to that commitment. 
With smart technology, powerful performance, and thoughtful design, this range is crafted to 
deliver convenience, care, and energy efficiency. We are confident that this series will redefine the 
washing experience and emerge as a prefer red choice for customer’s daily needs.”  
  
Front Load Washing Machine 
The Front Load Washing machine delivers precision washing with modern aesthetics, ensuring 
both exceptional cleaning and energy efficiency. Powered by AI DD 2.0 technology , it detects 
fabric weight, type, and soil level to automatically choose the most suitable wash cycle for optimal fabric protection and care. The Steam+ technology  further enhances washing performance by 
reducing wrinkles and eliminating allergens, ke eping clothes fresh, hygienic and smooth. The 
TurboWash® 360° feature , with its multi-directional water jets , thoroughly cleans clothes in just 
39 minutes without compromising on quality. The eZDispense ™ provides seamless auto-dosing 
for nearly 30 washes, adding effortless convenience.  With LG ThinQ™ smart connectivity , users can remotely control and monitor the machine, 
adding convenience to daily laundry management. The AIDD 2.0 Series also includes thoughtful 
programs such as Allergy Care  and Baby Steam Care , designed to cater to delicate fabrics and 
sensitive skin. Additionally, the Micro plastic Care cycle  helps reduce micro plastic emissions 
by up to 60%, reinforcing LG’s focus on sustaina bility and protecting ocean. The new range of 
Front Load Washing Machines with minimalist desi gn features Digital Dial LCD, which adds an 
elegant modern touch while offering smoother,  more intuitive control for everyday use.  The range 
with Flat tempered glass door  with chrome finish , comes in premium colours  making them a 
stylish addition to modern homes. Designed for energy and water efficiency, the new models 
combine performance, care and aesthetics in one sophisticated package.  The new range of Washer Dryer models  launched in larger capacity, 20/10 KG & 15/8 KG, 
designed specifically for households  seeking both washing and dryi ng in single machine. These 
models offer high-performance wash cycles with  efficient drying-ideal for monsoon seasons, high-
rise living or homes with limited drying space. With advanced technology , these models ensure 
faster, convenient and complete  laundry care, All-in-One Wash-Dry in one machine. 
 
 
Top load Washing Machine 
The machine combines powerful performance with in telligent fabric care to offer an elevated 
washing experience. The advanced AI DD technology  intelligently detects the weight and type 
 of fabrics and automatically se lects the optimal wash pattern from 20,000 possible combinations, 
ensuring effective cleaning with gentle care. The TurboWash® feature enables a full load to be 
washed in just 29 minutes, offering both speed  and efficiency for busy households. To ensure

and schedule wash cycles remotely, offering co mplete control through a smartphone. The machine 
also features a water resistant IPX4-rated control panel  for durability and a premium Jog Dial
