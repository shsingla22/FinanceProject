Colgate Visible White Purple and Global Korean Icon IU Partner to 
Unveil the Secret to a Dazzling White Smile 
India, 14 August, 2025: In a move that merges global beauty trends with oral 
care innovation, Colgate-Palmolive announced an exclusive partnership with 
renowned Korean icon, IU(Lee Ji-eun), to introduce her secret for a radiant, white 
smile to millions of Indians. 
While millions of Indian consumers have embraced Korean beauty trends for skincare, 
this collaboration marks the first time a major Korean personality is sharing oral care 
as an integral part of her beauty regimen. 
IU, celebrated globally for her flawless aesthetic, revealed Colgate Visible White Purple 
as the  secret  behind  her dazzling  smile,  a cornerstone  of her  iconic  look.  This 
partnership introduces a new era of oral beauty to the Indian market, connecting the 
aspirational standards of Korean beauty with Colgate's trusted, world-class products. 
Gunjit  Jain,  Executive  Vice  President  of Marketing  at Colgate-Palmolive  (India) , 
added,  “K-culture  has  evolved  from  a niche  trend  to a mainstream  phenomenon  in 
India,  from  K-dramas  and K-pop  right  through  to K-beauty.  We’re  thrilled  to partner 
with IU, a global icon of K-culture. While many K-stars have spoken about routines and 
hacks  to beautiful  skin,  for the  first  time  ever  IU is revealing  the  secret  behind  her 
beautiful smile - Colgate Visible White Purple.”  
The campaign, launched  on 8th August, features  IU across various  platforms,  including 
digital, television, and social media. It reveals how Colgate Visible White with its purple 
optic brighteners color corrects  yellow tones to give a  whiter smile.  Users can purchase 
this IU-certified  beauty  essential  from  quick  commerce,  e-commerce  platforms,  or 
from major retail outlets.
