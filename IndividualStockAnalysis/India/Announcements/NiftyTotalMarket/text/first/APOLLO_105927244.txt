With reference to the Outcome of Meeting of Board of Directors of the Company held on 23rd July 
2024, we are writing to inform you of an update related to the Board Meeting. 
We have considered 17th July, 2024 as the relevant date. However, pursuant to Regulation 161 of 
SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, if the relevant date falls on a weekend or a holiday, the day preceding 
the weekend or the holiday will be reckoned as the relevant date. Since 17th July, 2024 is a holiday, 
we need to consider 16th July, 2024 as the relevant date. 
Due to this change in the relevant date, the issue price and the total issue size have been adjusted 
accordingly. The revised details are as follows: 
1. The issue price is Rs. 108.15 (Indian Rupees One Hundred and Eight and Paise Fifteen 
Only) 
2. The total issue size is Rs. 2,64,97,50,705/- (Indian Rupees Two Hundred and Sixty-Four 
Crores Ninety-Seven Lakhs Fifty Thousand Seven Hundred and Five Only) 
We regret any inconvenience caused appreciate your understanding.
