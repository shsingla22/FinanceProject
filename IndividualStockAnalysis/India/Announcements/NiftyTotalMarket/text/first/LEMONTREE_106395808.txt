Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in Uttarakhand  
 
New Delhi , 1st October 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -growing 
mid-scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the signing of its latest 
property , Keys Select  by Lemon Tree Hotels,  Haridwar.   
 
This property shall be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned 
subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited , and further strengthens the company’s 
presence in  Uttarakhand , while catering to both  religious  and leisure travellers for a 
fresh, vibrant, and value -driven experience.  
 
Property Highlights   
Keys Select  by Lemon Tree Hotels, Haridwar  is in one of the seven holiest cities 
in India  - a sacred gateway to the Himalayas where the Ganga River enters the plains. 
Known for the grand Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri, the city draws pilgrims and travel lers 
seeking spiritual solace. The city is dotted with sacred temples like Mansa Devi 
Temple, Chandi Devi Temple, and Daksha Mahadev Temple, along with peaceful 
ghats for ritual baths. Nearby, the Rajaji National Park offers a touch of wilderness, 
making Haridwar a unique blend of spirituality, culture, and nature.  
 
The key features of the hotel will include:  
• 52 well-appointed rooms  
• A rest aurant  
• A conference hall  
• Recreational facilit y includes a fitness center  
 
The Jolly Grant Airport , Dehradun  is approximately 32 kms away from the property, 
while the Haridwar  Railway Station is about 9 kms away. The hotel is well connected 
by roadways for both public and private transport.   
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels , commented , "With this  signing , we will expan d our leisure 
portfolio in Uttarakhand , a state where we have eight operational and nine upcoming 
properties in addition to this one . While expanding its footprint across the country in 
cities of all tiers, this signing by Lemon Tree Hotels perfectly aligns with the 
company’s vision of offering refreshing and comfortable stays across religious and 
spiritual destinations ."
