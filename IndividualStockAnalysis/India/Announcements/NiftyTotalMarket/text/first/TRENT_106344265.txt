Burnt Toast Enters Surat with the Launch of Its First-Ever Store in the City  
Vibrant new fashion brand from Trent Ltd. brings its new store to Gujarat  
Surat, August 2025  – Burnt Toast , the newest youth-focused fashion label from Trent Ltd., has 
officially made its debut in Gujarat with the launch of its first-ever store in Surat. Located at Unity 
Corner, Plot No. 1, Umra South, City Light, the store is poised to redefine affordable fashion by 
offering youthful and fun apparel for the city’s vibrant audience. 
Nestled in the heart of Surat’s bustling City Light area, the new store delivers a high-energy retail 
experience. Offering a wide range of apparel, accessories, and beauty products, Burnt Toast 
invites Surat’s youth to embrace personal style and set their own trends. 
“Surat is a city that beautifully blends tradition with a rapidly growing appetite for modern, edgy 
fashion,” said Mr. P. Venkatesalu, Managing Director , Trent Ltd . “We’re excited to bring Burnt 
Toast to a city full of young, expressive voices.” 
Burnt Toast caters to a wide spectrum of preferences and budgets with trend-forward, affordable 
pieces crafted with care. With elevated comfort, trendy silhouettes, and a refined aesthetic, the 
collection reflects the global aspirations of today’s youth ensuring there’s something for every 
individual and every style. 
From curated capsules of best-selling styles to dynamic seasonal collections, the store has been 
thoughtfully designed to inspire discovery and capture the ever-evolving spirit of youth culture.  
As Burnt Toast continues to grow, more cities are on the horizon. The brand is set to fuel a new 
wave of fashion one that celebrates energy, individuality, and creativity in every corner of the 
country. 
About the brand- 
Burnt Toast is inspired by the idea that sometimes the most extraordinary things emerge from 
the unexpected. Blending contemporary trends with effortless ease, we have created pieces that 
move seamlessly from casual hangouts to more curated moments, always with a sense of fun and 
connection. More than just fashion, Burnt Toast is a lifestyle proposition a celebration of self-
expression and community, where style becomes a shared experience. From bold apparel to 
statement accessories, our brand inspires the young, dynamic youth to live authentically, with 
style that transcends fashion.
