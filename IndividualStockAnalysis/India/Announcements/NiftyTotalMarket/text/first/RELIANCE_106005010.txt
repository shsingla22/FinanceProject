Liquidation of Reliance Industries Uruguay Petroquimica S.A  
 
 
This is to inform that  Reliance Industries Uruguay Petroquimica S.A, (Reliance Uruguay), a 
wholly -owned subsidiary of the Company , has been voluntary liquidated and has  ceased to 
be a wholly -owned subsidiary of the Company  with effect from September 26, 2024 .

turnover and networth of the Company for the financial year 2023 -24 was NIL.  The Company 
had invested an amount equivalent to Rs. 0.65 crore in Reliance Uruguay.  
 
The Company received certificate of liquidation of  Reliance Uruguay  on September 26, 2024  
at 8:59 p.m. (IST).
