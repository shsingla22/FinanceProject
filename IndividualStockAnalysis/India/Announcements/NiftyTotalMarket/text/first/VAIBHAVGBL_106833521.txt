 VGL Completes Platform Transition,                                                                    
Focuses on Restoring Digital Momentum and Improving Operating Leverage  
 
JAIPUR, India, 28 October 2026  -- Vaibhav Global Limited ("VGL") (NSE: VAIBHAVGBL; BSE: 532156), a vertically 
integrated electronic retailer of fashion jewellery, accessories and lifestyle products, today reported results for the 
second quarter and half year ended 30 September 2026. 
Mr. Sunil Agarwal, Managing Director, Vaibhav Global Limited, said:" Q2 FY27 was a quarter of transition as we 
completed the migration of our channel websites from Salesforce Commerce Cloud to Shopify Enterprise. During 
the stabilisation phase, temporary integration -related challenges, including customer account reactiva tion, 
optimisation of search and merchandising functionality, and SEO re -indexing, impacted digital conversion and 
revenue. In the UK, we also consolidated four warehouses and two studios into a single, larger headquarters. While 
the transition was complet ed smoothly, some inventory remains temporarily offline, limiting product availability and 
customer choice.  
Despite these near -term challenges, our European business delivered growth in constant currency, our B2B business 
recorded strong growth, and total revenue for the first half grew close to 10%.  
With the transition substantially behind us, we now have a unified, cloud -based and scalable platform supporting 
our brands and geographies. Our focus is on restoring digital momentum, strengthening customer acquisition and 
improving operating leverage as we enter our seasonally strongest quarters. We believe the new platform provides 
a stronger foundation for accelerating digital growth and driving sustainable, profitable growth in the periods 
ahead .,”  
 
• Revenue Growth:  Revenue from operations increased 7.0% to ₹938.4 crore in Q2 FY27. H1 FY27 revenue 
grew 9.7% to ₹1,855.4 crore.  
• EBITDA Performance:  Q2 FY27 EBITDA stood at ₹7 6.2 crore, with an EBITDA margin of 8.1%. H1 FY27 EBITDA 
increased 9.6% to ₹17 8.3 crore, with the margin of 9.6%. 
• United States:  Q2 FY27 Revenue stood at ₹ 500.0 crore, increased by 4.3% in rupee terms and declined 
4.1% in constant currency.  
• United Kingdom:  Q2 FY27 Revenue stood at ₹257.2  crore  increased by 2.3% in rupee terms and declined 
7.0% in constant currency.  
• Europe (excluding UK):  Q2 FY27 Revenue stood at ₹110.0 increased by 18.3% in rupee terms and 8.0% in 
constant currency.  
• B2B Business:  Q2 FY27 Revenue stood at ₹70.9 crore grew 32 .4%, while EBITDA  stood at ₹ 42.4  crore  
increased 1 8.5% YoY 
• In-house Brands:  In-house brands contributed 5 7% of B2C revenue.  
• Digital Business:  eCommerce contributed 42.4 % of B2C revenue, compared with 42. 8% in Q2 FY26 and 
45.1% in Q1 FY27. All retail businesses have now migrated to Shopify Enterprise.

Group Performance  
Revenue from operations increased 7.0% to ₹938.4 crore in Q2 FY27, supported by 5.3% growth in retail (B2C) 
revenue and 32.4% growth in B2B revenue. Reported revenue growth benefited from favorable  foreign exchange 
movements, with the Indian Rupee weakening by approximately 9% against the US Dollar and 10% against both the 
British Pound and the Euro. Accordingly, constant currency  growth is also presented for the overseas retail segments 
to provide a clearer view of underlying business performance.  
EBITDA stood at ₹ 76.2 crore, with the EBITDA margin at 8. 1%. The year -on-year decline primarily reflected sales 
deleverage in the US and UK amid the transition to Shopify Enterprise, continued investment in digital customer 
acquisition and the non -recurrence of a foreign exchange gain recognized  in Europe in Q2 FY26. These factors were 
partially offset by higher B2B EBITDA. For H1 FY27, EBITDA increased 9.6 % to ₹178 .3 crore, with the EBITDA margin 
of 9.6%.

About Vaibhav Global  Limited  
Vaibhav Global Limited (VGL) is listed on stock exchanges in India (BSE: 532156, NSE: VAIBHAVGBL, ISIN - 
INE884A01027 ). VGL is an omni -channel E -tailer of fashion jewellery, accessories, and lifestyle products in developed 
markets with direct access to 127 million households (FTE) through its TV home shopping networks – Shop LC in US , 
Shop TJC & Ideal World in UK  and Shop LC in Germany. The Company’s ecommerce websites www.shoplc.com  & 
https://mindfulsouls.com/  in the US, www.tjc.co.uk  ,www.idealworld.tv/  and www.rachelgalley.com  in the UK and 
www.shoplc.de  in Germany , complement TV coverage and diversify customer engagement. The Company is 
committed to ‘Delivering Joy’ to all the stakeholders. Through its flagship midday meal  program , ‘your purchase 
feeds… ’ where a meal is provided for every piece sold at the retail channels, the Company has provided over 119 
million meals in US, UK, Germany , and India at a run rate of ~56,000 meals being donated every school day since 
program ’s inception .
