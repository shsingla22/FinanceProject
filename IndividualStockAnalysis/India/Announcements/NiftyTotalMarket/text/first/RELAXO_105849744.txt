4. The existing ‘Clause IV’ i.e., ‘The Liability of the members is limited” be and is hereby stands deleted and replaced by 
new ‘Clause IV’ as under:
 ‘The liability of the member(s) is limited and this liability is limited to the amount unpaid, if any, on the shares held by
