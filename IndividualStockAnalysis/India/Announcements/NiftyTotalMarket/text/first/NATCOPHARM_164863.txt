Board and approved by the Board of Directors of the Company at their meetings held on 12 August 2024.

auditors and they have issued an unmodified reviewed report on the same. The review report of the statutory auditors is being 
filed with the National Stock Exchange of India Limited ('NSE') and BSE Limited ('BSE') and is also available on the Company 's 
website.

to date consolidated figures up to the third quarter of the previous financial year. Also, the consolidated figures upto the end of the 
third quarter of the previous financial year had only been reviewed and not subjected to audit.
