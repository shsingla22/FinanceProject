Press Release  
  
RITES & DGNP-Mumbai sign  
MoU for consultancy services  
 
Gurugram, November 6, 2025: RITES Limited, the leading Transport Infrastructure Consultancy and Engineering 
firm, today signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Director General of Naval Projects (DGNP), 
Mumbai, Indian Navy. The partnership aims at providing consultancy services for naval infrastructure works in 
the Mumbai region.  
 
 
Officials of Director General of Naval Projects (DGNP), Mumbai, and RITES at the MoU-signing ceremony in Mumbai  
 
Under the MoU, RITES will extend its expertise in soil investigation, feasibility studies, structural and 
architectural designs, preparation of Detailed Project Reports (DPRs), project management consultancy, bid 
management, third-party inspection services, and other preparatory technical consultancy activities. The 
collaboration is designed to enable efficient planning and execution of naval infrastructure projects under the 
DGNP, Mumbai.  
 
 
About RITES Limited:  
RITES Limited, a Navratna Public Sector Enterprise, is a leading player in the transport consultancy and engineering sector 
in India, having diversified services and geographical reach. The company has an experience spanning more than 5 decades 
and undertaken projects in over 55 countries in Asia, Africa, South America, and Middle East region.
