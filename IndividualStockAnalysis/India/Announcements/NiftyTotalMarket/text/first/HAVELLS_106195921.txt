Lloyd Unveils Luxuria Collection, Expands Manufacturing to Meet 
Rising Demand  
 Lloyd introduces its Luxuria series with range of air conditioner products under the StunnAir 
Stylus, Stellar, and the all-new categories 
 The series also features launches of the Lloyd refrigerator (KOLOR), Lloyd Mini LED TV, and 
Novante Washing Machine 
 To keep up with the growing demand for cooling products, Lloyd has increased its 
manufacturing capacity to 3 million ACs. 
National, 7th April, 2025:  Lloyd, a leading consumer durables brand from Havells India, is 
elevating its product offering with a new line-up of luxury cooling solutions with the launch of the Lloyd 
Luxuria Collection. This exciting expansion includes the introduction of the innovative StunnAir , and 
the expansion of the already popular Stellar & Stylus models . With this, Lloyd is enhancing its 
premium air conditioner offerings, responding to the growing demand for high-end products. In addition, 
to meet the anticipated rise in consumer demand and build on last year’s successful launch, Lloyd has 
ramped up the combined manufacturing capacity of its Ghiloth and Sri City plants to 3 million Acs 
per annum.  
 
The Lloyd Luxuria Collection brings together top-notch craftsmanship, innovative technology, energy 
efficiency, and elegant design ACs. This expansion is driven by a keen understanding of consumer 
demand for premium air conditioning solutions. By increasing its focus on this segment, Lloyd is not just 
meeting expectations—it's raising the bar for comfort and innovation, delivering on the needs of 
customers who prioritize quality, performance, and style. 
 
On the launch, Yogesh Kumar Gupta, Executive Director, Havells India Limited,  said, "Lloyd has 
always been at the forefront of innovation, and last year’s lineup was received exceptionally well, really 
solidifying our position in the premium AC market. Launching the Luxuria collection is our way of building 
on that success and responding to the increasing demand for thoughtfully curated cooling solutions. 
The StunnAir, with its features like AI-powered cooling and voice commands, sets our portfolio apart. 
The move toward luxury in air conditioning isn’t just about adding high-end features; it’s about creating 
a complete experience. With the StunnAir, Stellar and Stylus, we are reshaping what comfort means. 
Expanding our manufacturing capacity at Sri City reflects our confidence in this vision and our 
commitment to delivering outstanding value, all while staying true to our high standards of quality." 
 
Speaking on the occasion, Alok Tickoo, Executive Vice President, Lloyd,  shared, "The launch of the 
Lloyd Luxuria Collection  is a big moment for us. With the addition of the StunnAir to our lineup, which 
already includes the Stellar & Stylus models, we’re raising the bar in the premium air conditioning 
market. What really excites us is how these units not only perform exceptionally well but also fit 
beautifully into modern homes. The Luxuria Collection  is part of our larger effort to enhance the way 
our customers live, creating environments where comfort and style can coexist." 
 
The launch of the Luxuria Collection highlights a significant shift in consumer priorities toward products 
that marry innovation with design. Today, homes are an extension of one’s personality, a reflection of 
taste. As homeowners increasingly seek modern environments, there is a growing demand for air 
conditioners that not only perform exceptionally well but also enhance interior aesthetics. The Luxuria 
Collection is Lloyd’s answer to this evolving mindset for the customers who refuse to compromise on 
luxury. 
  
 
  
 
 
“At Havells, we believe great appliances should do more than just perform—they should enhance the

StunnAir Air Conditioner  
The StunnAir air conditioner redefines intelligent cooling with cutting-edge AI and sleek design. Its AI-
Powered Cooling adapts to user preferences, while INDRI (Individual Detection and Range Imaging)  
can differentiate between a human & non-human i.e. objects and adjusts its airflow for maximum 
comfort and efficiency and even shuts down & starts automatically basis human presence. The 
intelligent Find Me  and Miss Me  modes directs air towards or away from the user, and 3D Airflow 
ensures even distribution of cool air throughout the space.  
 
This 6 in 1 Expandable AC  is capable to cool even at 60°C , for instant relief, Rapid Cooling  drops 
temperature to 18°C in just 30 seconds, powered by 20% enhanced air suction and 10% increased 
airflow. AI based Direct Voice Command offers hands-free control as it supports all important 
commands right from turning ON / OFF or changing temperature & Fan Speed with no requirement of 
Wi-Fi, Data, Smart Phones or Devices, just say Hello Lloyd !! While the Interactive LED Fascia and 
Ribbed Glass-Inspired Design adds a premium & modern touch, making it a perfect blend of intelligence 
& style and then the Sliding Fascia  further enhances its beauty when the AC is turned on. 
 
This power packed AC also offers In-Built Air Purifier  which not just indicates Real Time IAQ of the 
room but also ensures healthy environment for its users. To further enhance the beauty of the product 
Mood Lighting  plays the best role as users can customize 7 mood lighting colour options as per their 
liking and needs, even when the AC is in stand-by mode. 
 
Stellar, Stellar Duo, Stellar Art, and Stellar Grace Air Conditioner 
The Stellar range has been enhanced with the Stellar Duo, Stellar Art, and Stellar Grace, all part of the 
premium Lloyd Luxuria series. Each of these models features subtle yet impactful design elements with 
a top up of customizable lighting effects. The Stellar Duo  is a heat pump AC, while the Stellar Art  and 
Stellar Grace  features unique solutions to suit modern interiors. The addition of Mood-based Ambi 
lighting in these models allows users to set their ideal atmosphere with ease. 
Lloyd Stylus Air Conditioner 
The Lloyd Stylus air conditioner blends innovation with style, offering a DIY changeable fascia  that lets 
users customize panels in coloured, marble, and wood finishes to match their décor. More than just an 
AC, it is a design statement that adapts to the environment. Equipped with built-in Wi-Fi , the Stylus 
enables seamless smart control, while its 6-in-1 Convertible feature  adjusts cooling capacity for 
maximum efficiency. Automatic Humidity Control  ensures better air quality, making Stylus an ideal 
fusion of aesthetics, comfort, and advanced technology. 
  
 
  
 
Masterpiece Air Conditioner 
Lloyd Masterpiece Air Conditioners are designed for efficient, seamless cooling in any space. Its Opti-
Breeze Technology  ensures super quite performance with effective wind free cooling and its Top-
Throw Air Discharge  offers uniform airflow, making it perfect for mid-sized rooms. Inverter Technology 
of this 6 in 1 Convertible AC  and its 5-Star Energy Rating, delivers powerful cooling even at 52°C while 
keeping energy consumption in check. For added convenience, the Automatic Temperature Control  
maintains the ideal climate without fluctuations, while Feather Touch Controls  offers effortless 
operations for its users. It’s whisper-quiet performance and sleek & compact design make it a true 
Masterpiece and perfect fit for apartments, offices, and modern living spaces. 
KOLOR Refrigerator 
The Lloyd Direct Cool Refrigerator, KOLOR, combines smart technology with stylish design for a 
seamless cooling experience. Its Anto-Defrost Technology  automatically detects and initiates 
defrosting, eliminating manual effort. Advanced sensors ensure better temperature control and energy 
efficiency, optimizing cooling while reducing power consumption. 
 
For faster performance, the Express Freeze Mode  delivers instant freshness, while Faster Ice Making  
ensures quick ice formation. The Digital Temperature Control  allows precise adjustments for 
customized cooling. Designed for modern homes, the refrigerator is available in stunning pastel shades, 
Sunkissed Yellow and Minty Green  with a designer handle, blending aesthetics with functionality. 
The KOLOR refrigerator is available in three capacities – 195L, 216L, and 240L . 
 
Lloyd Mini LED TV 
Lloyd continues to push the boundaries of home entertainment with its latest Mini-LED TV lineup. 
Building on its legacy of cutting-edge panel technology, including WCG Vision Pro and QLED Google 
TVs with Far field voice technology , Lloyd is now introducing Mini-LED technology which employs 
smaller lights arrange in a grid formation, which contributes to improved color accuracy and brightness 
levels for enhanced picture quality. With features like 1400 nits peak brightness, 100% NTSC color 
gamut, a 120Hz panel, Dolby Vision & Atmos, Far-Field Voice Technology, and an in-built 
subwoofer , these TVs deliver stunning visuals and immersive sound. Designed for a premium viewing 
experience, the new Mini-LED TVs will be available in 55”, 65”, and 75” screen sizes.  
 
Lloyd Novante Washing Machine 
The Lloyd Novante washing machine range is designed to combine style and advanced technology, 
featuring an IoT Intelli Inverter Motor and an In-Built Heater for enhanced efficiency. Available in 
Copper Blush, Satin Steel, and Ebony Black , and in 8kg, 8.5kg, and 9kg capacities, this range offers 
a seamless blend of performance and aesthetics. Equipped with 5D Ultra Wash Technology , 12 i-
Sensing, Insta Wash, and Shower Rinse, the washing process is optimized to save 50% time and water. 
The Dual Lint Filter  enhances lint collection by 50%, ensuring better fabric care. As India’s first fully 
automatic top-load washing machine with an in-built heater, it is capable of removing 120 different types 
of stains, even those up to 4 days old. 
 
With IoT-enabled Wash Genie , users can download additional wash programs, receive cycle updates, 
and manage laundry conveniently through a smartphone, offering a smarter and more efficient washing 
experience. 
 
 
 
 
  
 
 
Lloyd Manufacturing Facility Expansion 
In line with the growing demand for its premium AC products, Lloyd has ramped up the combined 
manufacturing capacity at its Sri City and Ghiloth plants , increasing output to 3 million units . This 
move will ensure the brand can meet the increasing market demand for its stylish and innovative cooling 
solutions, while also maintaining its commitment to quality and sustainability.
