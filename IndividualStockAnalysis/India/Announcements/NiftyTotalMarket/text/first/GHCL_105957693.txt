Sub: Annual maintenance activities of Boiler  at Soda Ash Plant - Intimation under 
Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements,

In reference to the above captioned subject, we would like to inform you that annual 
maintenance activities of Boiler  at Company's Soda Ash plant is scheduled to take place from 
September 16 , 2024  for a period of two weeks. This is expected to result in a total production 
shortfall of approximately 14,000 tons.  The purpose of the activities is to carry out essential 
maintenance tasks to ensure the efficient and safe operations of our Soda Ash plant.  
 
During this period, we want to assure our stakeholders t hat we have a sufficient reserve stock 
of Soda Ash to meet our customers' requirements. Therefore, there will be no adverse impact 
on our supply commitments during the aforementioned period.  
 We kindly request you to please take note of this information and take appropriate measures 
to disseminate it to all concerned parties as per the SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
