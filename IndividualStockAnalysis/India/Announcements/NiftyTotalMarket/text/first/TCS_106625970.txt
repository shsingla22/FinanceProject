 Corporate Overview
The Company integrates purpose driven leadership with digital expertise and innovation to address its material environmental, social and 
economic impacts, while supporting sustainable value creation for customers, business partners and stakeholders across the value chain. The Company’s environmental stewardship is guided by four core pillars-carbon emission mitigation, water conservation & recycling, waste reduction & recycling and biodiversity preservation. The Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR), aligned with the National Guidelines for Responsible Business Conduct (NGRBC), presents transparent disclosures on the Company’s policies, processes and ESG performance.
