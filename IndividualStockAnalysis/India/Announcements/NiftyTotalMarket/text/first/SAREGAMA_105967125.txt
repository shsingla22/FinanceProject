A. Date, time and venue of the AGM:  
 
The 77th AGM of the Company was held on Tuesday, 27th August, 2024 through VC/OAVM

B. Proceedings brief:  
 
i.) Dr. Sanjiv Goenka, Chair ed the meeting in accordance with Article  81 of the Articles of 
Association of the Company.

VC/ OAVM facility.  
 
ii.) The requisite quorum b eing present, the Chairman declared the Meeting open and

basic rules relating to the AG M. The Company Secretary, inter -alia, stated the following:

qualification(s), observation(s) or comments.

5 Items of business set forth in the Notice through Remote e -Voting prior to the 
Meeting and through e -voting system during the AGM using the platform provided 
by National Securities Depository Limited (‘NSDL ’).

accessible electronically for inspection during the continuance of the Meeting.  
 
d. Questions & Answers session for the registered speaker members commenced after 
all the Resolutions in the Notice were tabled.  
 
iv.) The Notice convening the 77th AGM was taken as read with the consent of the 
Members present.
