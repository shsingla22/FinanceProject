Coforge Launches CXNova , an Agentic CX Orchestration 
Platform that Deliver s Intelligent, Real -Time Customer 
Experiences   
 
Greater Noida, India / Princeton, NJ, USA – September 30, 2026  - Coforge, an AI -
native engineering services leader, today announced the launch of CXNova , an AI -native 
customer experience ( CX) platform designed to help enterprises deliver personalization, 
engagement, and continuous optimization across critical customer touchpoints. Built on a 
multi-agent architecture, CXNova  enables organizations to transform disconnected AI, 
content, and channel systems into a unified experience execution layer.  
Enterprises have made significant investments in AI, digital experience platforms, and data 
ecosystems, yet many struggle to operationalize AI a cross the customer journey . CXNova 
addresses this gap with an agentic CX orchestration layer that enables systems to turn 
strategic intent into scalable, autonomous customer journeys that learn, adapt, and deliver 
business value.  
At its foundation, CXNova  employs  domain -specific ontologies and enterprise knowledge 
graphs that connect customer, content, product, journey, and operational intelligence, giving 
every agent a shared understanding of business context. Building on this intelligence layer, 
specialized content, context, and canvas agents work together to understand, decide, create, 
and adapt experiences in real time.  
Content agents dynamically generate, adapt, and optimize content using enterprise 
knowledge, structured data, and domain -trained models to ensure personalization and brand 
compliance at scale. Context agents interpret identity, intent, behavior, and real -time signals 
to drive next -best-action decisioning across the customer journey. Canvas  agents dynamically 
assemble and deliver experiences across digital and physical touchpoints like web, mobile, 
conversational, and emerging interaction channels.  
These capabilities are enhanced by industry -specific agents with embedded language, 
knowledge, experience patterns, and decision models , as well as a centralized agent 
orchestration and experience operations (XOps) control  plane that coordinates autonomous 
agents, enterprise systems, and human expertise.  
“At Coforge, we believe the next phase of digital transformation is not about adding more AI 
features . It’s about orchestrating experiences that can act in the moment ,” said  Kapil Tyagi, 
Senior Vice President and Head of Experience, Coforge . “CXNova brings together five 
integrated capability areas to power an autonomous, experience -led enterprise  with governed 
decision -making, continuous learning, and scalable experiences that become more intelligent 
with every interaction .” 
The CXNova platform is ready for enterprise -scale deployment  across industries including 
travel, airlines , banking and financial services, insurance, and healthcare . It integrat es with 
existing enterprise ecosystems to enable rapid adoption and scalable CX transformation.
