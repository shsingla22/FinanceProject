Change in Chief Financial Officer and Key Managerial Personnel:

Annexure-B 
 
Closure of manufacturing facility at Hyderabad 
 
The Board of Directors of GMM Pfaudler Limited in their meeting dated December 1 3, 2024, approved the 
closure of the manufacturing facility at Hyderabad. 
  
The decision to discontinue glass-lined manufacturing operations in Hyd erabad and consolidate them with 
the operations in Karamsad aims to improve capacity utilization and reduce costs. 
  
It is to be noted that the above closure of the Hyderabad facility will not hav e any material or adverse impact 
on the operations of the company.
