Biocon Secures Market Entry for Yesintek ™, a Proposed 
Biosimilar to Stelara®, in Additional Global Markets  
 
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, October 26, 2026  
 
Biocon Limited (BSE code: 532523, NSE: BIOCON) has signed a license agreement with  
Janssen Sciences Ireland clearing the way for the commercialization of Yesintek ™, its  
proposed biosimilar to Stelara® (Ustekinumab), in Australia, Brazil, China, Hong Kong,  
Israel, Mexico, New Zealand, South Korea and Kazakhstan with immediate effect . 
 
This milestone builds on the Company’s previously announced agreements with Janssen  
that secured market entry dates for Yesintek ™ in the United States , and in Europe, the 
UK,  Canada and Japan . 
 
These additional market entry rights for biosimilar Ustekinumab will allow Biocon to offer 
an affordable and effective treatment option for patients impacted by autoimmune 
diseases in  these markets.  
 
Yesintek ™ is a human interleukin -12 and -23 antagonist that disrupts IL -12 and IL -23 
mediated signalling in immune mediated diseases and has been approved for the 
treatment  of Crohn’s disease, ulcerative colitis, plaque psoriasis and psoriatic arthritis. 
The reference  brand, Stelara®, had worldwide sales of $6.08 billion in 2025^.
