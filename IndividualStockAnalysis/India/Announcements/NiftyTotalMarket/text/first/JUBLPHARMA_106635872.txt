   Brief Profile of Mr. Ashish Mukkirwar 
 
Ashish Mukkirwar is a strategic, results-driven  financial leader with over two decades of 
progressive experience across corporate finance,  business finance, capital markets, investor 
relations, M&A, and strategy. He joins Jubilant Pharmova Ltd. from Glenmark 
Pharmaceuticals, where he most recently served as Group Vice President & CFO – Global Formulations Business. Prior to that, he was the Group Vice President – Strategy, M&A, and 
Investor Relations at Glenmark for four years. Earlier in his career, Ashish held senior roles at Moelis & Company and UBS Investment Banking, gaining extensive exposure to global capital 
markets and advisory.  
Ashish holds an MBA from the Indian Institute of Management, Kolkata, and brings a strong 
track record of driving financial performance,  enabling strategic growth, and creating long 
term stakeholder value.
