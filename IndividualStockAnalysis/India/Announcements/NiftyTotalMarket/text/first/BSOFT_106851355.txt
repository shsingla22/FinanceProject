Birlasoft Appoints Madhavan Hariharan as Chief Executive Officer and Managing 
Director  
Seasoned business leader with deep knowledge of Birlasoft and extensive transformation 
experience to lead the company's next phase of growth and value creation  
 
Noida, Pune (India) , November  6, 2026 : Birlasoft Ltd  [BSE: 532400, NSE: BSOFT] , part of the multibillion -dollar 
CKA Birla Group  today announced the appointment of Madhavan Hariharan  as its Chief Executive Officer and 
Managing Director, effective 8th December 2026.  
Madhavan assumes leadership of Birlasoft at a pivotal moment for the technology industry, as artificial 
intelligence, automation and digital technologies continue to reshape how enterprises operate, innovate  and 
create value. He has a strong track record of driving business transformation, strategic growth  and operational 
excellence.  
Madhavan succeeds Angan Guha , who led the company through an important phase of its evolution and 
growth.  
Madhavan has been closely associated with Birlasoft for several years through his leadership roles within the 
CKA Birla Group. As Group CFO, he worked extensively with the Birlasoft leadership team and Chairpersons to 
help shape strategic priorities, stren gthen organisational performance , and  drive long -term value creation.  
Over the course of his career, Madhavan has led several business transformations, growth initiatives  and 
strategic transactions across the Group's portfolio of companies. Widely recognized for his strategic acumen, 
sound judgement  and execution focus, he has consistently helped businesses navigate change, accelerate 
growth  and create sustainable value.  
Commenting on the appointment, Amita Birla, Chairman, Birlasoft , said:  
"Technology is undergoing one of the most significant transformations in its history. As clients rethink their 
businesses in an AI -driven world, Birlasoft has a unique opportunity to deepen its relevance, strengthen client 
partnerships  and accelerate growth.  
Madhavan brings a unique combination of strategic vision, operational discipline  and deep familiarity with 
Birlasoft. Having worked closely with the company over several years, he has played an important role in 
shaping strategic priorities, strengthening performance and supporting Birlasoft's transformation journey. His 
wide  leadership experience across the CKA Birla Group, coupled with his proven ability to drive business 
transformation and long -term value creation, makes him ideally positioned to lead Birlasoft into its next chapter.  
On behalf of the entire Birlasoft family, I would also like to thank Angan for his contributions and leadership and 
wish him the very best for the future."

About Birlasoft  
Birlasoft is a global technology company enabling “next -generation” digital transformation through expertise in 
Cloud, AI, Data  and enterprise solutions. Combining industry proficiency with advanced digital capabilities, it 
helps businesses accelerate change with speed, scale  and purpose, delivering “future -ready” solutions that 
enhance agility, resilience  and customer experience.  
Part of the CKA Birla Group and led by Chairman Amita Birla, Birlasoft’s over 11,000 professionals drive 
innovation while building a diverse, inclusive  and learning -oriented culture. With a strong focus on 
sustainability and long -term value creation, Birlasoft transforms enterprises and communities, earning its 
reputation as a trusted partner and one of the best places to work. Explore the innovation at 
www.birlasoft.com .  
About The CKA Birla Group  
The CKA Birla Group, is an Indian multinational conglomerate with a multibillion -dollar revenue. With over 
35,000 employees, the group operates more than 50 manufacturing facilities across India and the world, with 
a presence in diverse sectors including t echnology, automotive, home and building and healthcare.  
The CKA Birla Group continuously adapts to stay ahead in a changing world. By harnessing technology and 
investing in people and digital transformation, the Group consistently remains agile and delivers profitable 
growth. Viewing value creation through a gl obal lens, our companies operate without borders.  
The CKA Birla Group companies include Birlasoft, GMMCO, National Engineering Industries (manufacturer of 
NBC Bearings), BirlaNu (formerly HIL), Orient Electric, CK Birla Healthcare (CK Birla Hospitals and Birla Fertility 
& IVF), Orient Paper, AVTEC and Neo sym.  
Our companies share a common purpose of serving customers, partners and communities to create long term 
value through trust -based relationships.

The CKA Birla Group is also known for its deep -rooted commitment to community with institutions like BIT
