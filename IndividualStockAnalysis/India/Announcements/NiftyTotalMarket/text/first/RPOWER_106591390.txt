 About Reliance Group  
 
Reliance Infrastructure Limited and Reliance Power Limited, part of the Reliance Group have a combined investor base of approximately  50 lakh shareholders  and serves millions of 
customers /consumers.  
 Reliance Infrastructure Limited is one of the largest  infrastructure companies, with projects 
through various Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) in several high growth sectors such as Power, 
Roads, and Metro Rail in the Infrastructure space, and the Defence sector. Reliance Infrastructure 
is a major player in p roviding Engineering and Construction (E&C) services for developing power, 
infrastructure, metro and road projects.  
 
Reliance Power Limited is one of India’s leading power generation companies, with a total 
installed capacity of 5,305 MW, including ~ 4,000  MW Sasan Ultra Mega Power Project in Madhya 
Pradesh, the largest integrated thermal power plant in the world.
