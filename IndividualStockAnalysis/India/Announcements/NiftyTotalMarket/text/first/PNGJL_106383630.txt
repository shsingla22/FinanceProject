LiteStyle by PNG Jewellers Enters Solapur with Modern 
Everyday Jewellery 
 
Solapur, 25 September 2025:  PNG Jewellers further strengthened its presence of LiteStyle 
their lightweight fine jewellery sub-brand with the launch of a new shop-in-shop counter in 
their Solapur store. Designed to resonate with young, style-conscious women, LiteStyle offers 
a curated range of jewellery crafted in gold and diamonds, striking the perfect balance 
between affordability and elegance. 
The new shop in shop store reflects PNG’s ongoing commitment to expanding access to 
versatile fine jewellery that women can wear beyond traditional occasions. The designs are 
minimalist, pocket-friendly, and ideal for daily wear. 
At the inauguration, actress Meera Jagannath  said, “LiteStyle is jewellery that reflects today’s 
woman who is independent, confident, and graceful. It is not just about occasion wear, but 
about celebrating yourself every single day. I am thrilled that Solapur women now have direct 
access to such designs.”  
To mark the launch, PNG Jewellers is offering up to 50% off on LiteStyle jewellery making 
charges, giving customers a great reason to begin their festive shopping early.
