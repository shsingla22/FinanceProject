Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, the Board of Directors at its meeting held 
today i.e. Tuesday, March 24, 2026  has, inter alia , considered the following:  
 
1. Board Comments on fines levied by the Exchanges for the quarter ended 31st December, 2025:  
 
In reference to NSE’s Letter and BSE’s e -mail dated 27th February, 2026 imposing fine of ₹5,42,200/ - 
each on the Company for non -compliance of the provisions of  Regulation 17(1) (Board Composition, 
including failure to appoint woman director)  of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for the quarter ended 
on 31st December, 2025. The same was duly intimated to NSE and BSE on 28th February, 2026.  
 
Further, as advised by the Exchanges, the aforesaid matter was placed before the Board, wherein the 
Board deliberated and noted that “the non -compliance observed by BSE & NS E is beyond control of the 
Company and the Company has taken pro -active steps by informing and requesting Administrative 
Ministry i.e., Ministry of Railways well in advance, about the potential non -compliance. The Board also 
expressed its concern on the no tice received from BSE & NSE and advised that Administrative Ministry 
be requested on regular basis to expedite the process to appoint requisite number of independent 
directors including woman independent director on IRCTC’s Board to avoid non -compliance o f SEBI 
(LODR) Regulations,2015 Companies Act, 2013 and other applicable laws.”  
 
2. Change in the nomination of Chief Investor Relations Officer (CIRO):  
 
Pursuant to the applicable provisions of the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, and 
the Company’s Code of Conduct for Regulating & Reporting Trading by Designated Persons and their 
Immediate Relatives, the Board of Directors has appro ved the change in the nomination of Chief Investor 
Relations Officer (CIRO) of the Company. Further, the updated details of CIRO are being uploaded to 
the Company’s official website at www.irctc.com .
