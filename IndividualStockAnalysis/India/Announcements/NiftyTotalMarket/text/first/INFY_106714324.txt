growing at double-digit Q-on-Q  for the last several quarters. With this momentum, we see long-term 
relevance of our services for our clients, which are all driven through AI. From our delivery team, 
over 80,000 employees are working on coding tools such as Claude Code or Codex or several others 
for our clients, for our work inside. We see strong traction across the six  areas of new growth that

using coding tools. We are building a team of frontier engineers to support our client work. Our plan is to have 6,000 Frontier Engineers over the next few years.
