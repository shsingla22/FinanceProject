AI-enablement PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2025 
 
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, April 27, 2026 — HCLTech , a leading global technology company, 
today announced that it has been positioned as a Leader in the Everest Group Application 
Transformation Services for AI-enablement PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2025.  
 The assessment highlights HCLTech’s strong market impact , vision and capability, driven by  
investments in AI -focused accelerators, deep cloud ecosystem partnerships, expanding data 
engineering and AI -led transformation capabilities. HCLTech enables enterprises to modernize 
applications and scale automation with AI through solutions such as AI Force  and PRIZM , built 
with AWS, Azure and Google Cloud . These solutions enable automate d code transformation, 
effort estimation and architecture recommendations. The report also notes HCLTech’s continued focus on strengthening its AI and data capabilities through targeted acquisitions, 
including Starschema and Confidante, further enhancing its ability to deliver industry -specific, 
AI-powered transformation programs for global enterprises.  
 
“HCLTech emerges as a Leader in Everest Group’s Application Transformation Services for AI -
enablement PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2025, supported by its engineering -led transformation 
approach, repeatable frameworks and AI -powered assets that help enterprises modernize 
legacy estates, strengthen AI -ready data foundations and scale transformation initiatives. Its 
strong ecosystem partnerships further reinforce its position as a strong partner for AI -enabled 
modernization ,” says Yugal Joshi , Partner  at Everest Gr oup.  
 “This recognition as a Leader reflects our continued focus on helping clients modernize with speed, confidence and measurable impact,” said Pawan Vadapalli, Corporate Vice President 
and Global Head, Digital Business Services, HCLTech. “At a time when AI -led  engineering, 
platform operating models and cloud native transformation are reshaping enterprise 
technology, we remain committed to enabling organizations to build resilient, future-ready 
application landscapes that drive long -term growth.”
