 Sub : Intimation under Regulation 30 read with Regulation 31(A)(8) of Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended (“SEBI 
Listing Regulations”) following receipt of request for re-classification from the promoter category 
The Company has received today (September 17, 2025), a letter dated September 16, 2025 (“ Request Letter ”) from, 
Yanmar Coromandel Agrisolutions Private Limited (“YCAS”), seeking re-classification from the ‘promoter and promoter 
group’ of the Company under Regulation 31A of the SEBI Listing Regulations. 
The above mentioned request is pursuant to change in the shareholding of Coromandel International Limited (CIL) in YCAS 
from 40% to 10.60%, on account of a fund infusion of Rs.149.72 Crores by Yanmar Asia (Singapore) Corporation Pte Ltd 
(Yanmar Singapore) on September 27, 2024. Further to the allotment of 11,09,00,741 equity shares of face value Rs.10 
each by YCAS to Yanmar Singapore, YCAS does not fall under the definition of Promoter group specified in 2(pp) of SEBI 
(Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 (by virtue of CIL holding less than 20% of the equity 
share capital of YCAS) and has now requested their name to be removed from the Company’s Promoter/promoter Group 
list. 
As on the date of the Request Letter, YCAS does not hold any shares in the Company: 
The above request letter will be placed before the Board of Directors as stipulated under Regulation 31A (3) of the SEBI 
Listing Regulations and post receipt of the Board’s approval, we would submit an application seeking no-objection from 
National Stock Exchange of India Limited and BSE Limited.  
We kindly request you to take the above submission on record.
