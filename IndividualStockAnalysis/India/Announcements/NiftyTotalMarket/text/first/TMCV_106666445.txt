Tata Motors announces price increase for commercial vehicles from July 2026  
Mumbai, 18 June 2026:  Tata Motors, today, announced a price increase of up to 2.5% across its commercial 
vehicle range, effective 1 July 2026.  
The price increase is being undertaken to partially offset the impact of rising commodity prices and other input 
costs. The increase will vary depending on the model and variant.
