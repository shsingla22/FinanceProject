RESS RELEASE  
Blue Star elevates Mr P Venkat Rao to the Board designated as  
Executive Director - Projects, Solutions & International  
T
he Board of Directors of Blue Star Limited in its meeting held on August 6, 
2024,  has appointed Mr P Venkat Rao (P V Rao) as a Working Director 
designated as Executive Director - Projects, Solutions & International with 
effect from August 7, 2024,  up to Ju ly 31, 2028, subject to the approval of the 
shareholders.   

Mr P V Rao will be the third Working Director joining the Board in addition to 
Mr Vir Advani, Chairman & Managing Director and Mr B Thiagarajan, 
Managing Director.  
 
P V Rao  holds a degree in Mechanical Engineering with an MBA in Marketing 
and a Post Graduate diploma in Industrial Relations and Personnel 
Management. He has also completed the ‘Senior Executive Program’ from 
London Business School. 
  
P V Rao joined Blue Star in 1985, and in his long and successful career 
spanning over 36 years in the Company, he has held various positions and currently, as the President & Chief Operating Officer, he is responsible for the 
Electro -Mechanical Projects and Commercial Air Conditioni ng Systems 
segment.   
  
P V Rao currently represents Blue Star Limited in CII Western Regional Council 
as an elected member . He is a Life Member of t he Indian Society of Heating, 
Refrigerating and Air Conditioning Engineers (ISHRAE) . 
 Vir S Advani, Chairman & Manging Director adds, “P V Rao is a customer -
oriented professional with rich experience in running B2B businesses. He  
played a significant role in the turnaround of the Electro -Mechanical Projects 
business and making it a profitab le segment with a differentiated value 
proposition of Superior Project s Delivery. He is a welcome addition to the 
Board , and in his new role as an  Executive Director, P V Rao will be  responsible 
for the expansion of the air conditioning and refrigeration p roducts business 
in the Middle East & Africa markets.”
