PRESS RELEASE  
 
 
DLF and Everstone Group to form a strategic alliance to invest in IREO group  
 
 New Delhi, November  06, 2026  
 DLF Limited ( DLF) and Everstone Group ( Everstone ), a Singapore -headquartered global investment manager , 
have entered into a binding and exclusive term sheet , dated November 06, 2026 to strategically collaborate 
and form a joint venture  (JV) to invest in IREO Private Limited and other group companies in India ( IREO India 
Companies ) held by the IREO group of FDI funds ( IREO Funds ), with investments from reputed global 
investors .  
 
Through the proposed arrangement, DLF intends to invest approximately Rs 3,750  crore  for a majority stake,  
in the JV , which will hold economic interest in the IREO  India Companies . Under a separate binding and 
exclusive term sheet executed on the same date by the parties with the IREO Funds, t he proposed JV intend s 
to invest th ese funds to acquire a  stake of at least 20% in the IREO India Companies, together with certain 
governance -related rights. Through the proposed transaction, the JV’s aggregate economic interest 
(contractual and otherwise) in the IREO India Companies will be approximately 40%, with the  IREO Funds 
holding the balance.  
 With its size, scale and strategic locations, the proposed transaction presents a compelling opportunity to 
deliver high -quality, valuable developments across Delhi, Gurugram and other regions.  
 
On completion , the IREO India Companies  will be operated by a strong, independent management team 
executing an agreed business plan within a robust governance framework, under the guidance of DLF and 
Everstone. The alliance combines DLF’s proven development expertise with the strong capabilitie s of a global 
investment manager, with the aim of restoring momentum, unlocking embedded growth and building a stronger, more resilient business.  
 
The proposed transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including satisfactory completion of due 
diligence, receipt of all applicable corporate, regulatory and third -party approvals, and execution of deﬁnitive 
documents. Closing is expected t o occur during  2027.  
 “We are pleased to partner with Everstone  Group on this strategic investment. Bringing together strong 
industry experience and global investment capabilities, we look forward to unlocking the growth potential of the IREO India Companies, ” said Ashok Kumar Tyagi –  Managing Director , DLF.  
 “It’s our privilege to partner with DLF, especially given their incredible capabilities in a market they understand 
extremely well.  Consistent with our ﬁduciary responsibilities we look forward to working hard and creating 
signiﬁcant value for our IREO Funds  investors, ” said Sameer Sain  - Co-Founder & CEO, Everstone  Group
