CORRIGENDUM TO THE NOTICE OF THE EXTRAORDINARY GENERAL 
MEETING TO BE HELD ON OCTOBER 19, 2026  
The Company has issued the Notice of the Extraordinary General Meeting (“EGM”) scheduled on 
Monday, October 19, 2026 (“Notice”), which has been dispatched to the shareholders on September 
26, 2026. This corrigendum is issued pursuant to the observations of the National Stock Exchange 
of India Limited vide letter dated October 07, 2026, to clarify and correct the following: 
1. Date of the Notice  
The Notice was approved by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on September 23, 2026 and 
was issued and dispatched to the shareholders on September 26, 2026. The date of the Notice shall 
be read as September 26, 2026 , being the date of its dispatch. The applications for in principle 
approval were filed with BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited on the same 
day, in terms of Regulation 160(f) of the SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018.

3. Maximum number of Equity Shares in the Explanatory Statement  
The reference to “10,19,635” Equity Shares at point d(ii) of the Explanatory Statement shall be 
read as “10,17,123”  Equity Shares, consistent with the resolution at Item No. 1. 
4. Footnotes “#” and “*” on page 25 of the Notice  
The footnotes shall be read with the following clarification: the Equity Shares will be allotted to 
the selling shareholders of Winwin Speciality Insulators Limited against transfer of their shares to 
the Company in the manner set out in the Share Purchase Agreement dated September 23, 2026. 
The allotment shall be completed within 15 days from the date of passing of the special 
resolution, or where any regulatory approval is pending, within 15 days from receipt of the 
last such approval, as required under Regulation 170 of the SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018.  
This corrigendum shall form an integral part of the Notice and shall be read together with it. All
