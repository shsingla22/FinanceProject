PRESS RELEASE 
KARNATAKA BANK HITS AN ALL-TIME HIGH OF Rs. 2,07,132.41 CRORES IN 
AGGREGATE BUSINESS AND Rs. 435.89 CRORES IN NET PROFIT 
In the Meeting of the Board of Directors held today at Mangaluru, the Board approved the 
financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. 
Bank's Aggregate Deposits stood at Rs. 1,15,117.15 crores as on 30th September 2026, registering 
a YoY growth of 12% from Rs.1,02,817.19 crores as on 30th September 2025, while Retail Term 
Deposits (RTD) was Rs. 72,197.32 crores as on 30th September 2026, as against Rs. 65,531.80 
crores, with a 10% Yo Y increase from 30th September 2025. 
Gross Advances stood at Rs. 92,015.26 crores registering YoY growth of 25%, as against Rs. 
73,644.14 crores as on 30th September 2025. Bank's NIMs have improved from 2.72% for the 
quarter ended 30th September 2025, to 3.17% for the quarter ended 30th September 2026, thereby 
registering a 45bps Yo Y increase. 
Bank's Net Profit for the quarter ended 30th September 2026 was Rs.435.89 crores as against Rs. 
319.12 crores for the quarter ended 30th September 2025, which is a 37% YoY improvement. 
Consequently, Bank's ROE has improved from 10.14% for the quarter ended 30th September 
2025, to 12.51 % for the quarter ended 30th September 2026. Furthermore, for the half year 
ended September 2026, the net profit stood at Rs. 854.84 crores, as against Rs. 611.52 crores for 
half year ended September 2025. 
Asset quality has improved significantly, with GNPA % improved by 106 bps to 2.27% from 
3.33% as compared to September 2025. Similarly, the NNPA% also declined by 61 bps to 0.74% 
from 1.35% as compared to September 2025. The PCR (excluding TWO) improved to 68.05% in 
September 2026 from 60.22% in September 2025. 
1 
Announcing the results at the Bank's Head Quarters at Mangaluru, Shri Raghavendra S. Bhat, 
Managing Director & CEO, said, 
"The Bank has reached a historic milestone, with its aggregate business climbing to a record Rs. 
2,07,132.41 crore and net profit hitting Rs. 435.89 crore. This performance underscores our deep 
dedication to steady growth, sound fiscal oversight, and a customer-first approach to banking. 
Notably, our RAM (Retail, Agriculture, and MSME) segment expanded by a robust 19% YoY, 
surging from Rs. 47,958.40 crore on September 30, 2025, to Rs. 56,968.79 crore by September 30, 
2026. This upward trajectory has been fueled entirely by the enduring trust of our customers, 
the reliance of our shareholders, and the tireless dedication of our workforce. 
Driven by disciplined underwriting, robust recovery strategies, and the continuous monitoring 
of our credit portfolios, the bank's asset quality has strengthened remarkably. This is 
demonstrated by a YoY reduction in Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) by 106 basis points 
to 2.27%, while Net Non-Performing Assets (NNPA) lowered by 61 basis points to 0.74%. 
Our evolution into the digital space is picking up speed as we upgrade our technological 
infrastructure to offer secure, novel, and customer-focused financial platforms. We are 
leveraging data insights and adopting technological shifts to streamline consumer journeys, 
optimize internal productivity, and shape a modern institution capable of answering our 
clients' shifting demands. 
Concurrently, we intend to draw on our core advantages, scale our operations safely, and 
generate enduring value for everyone connected to us. Backed by a well-defined strategic 
blueprint and a firm focus on leadership, innovation, and exceptional care, we expect to 
maintain this development pace and cement our status as a reliable financial ally. 
Looking ahead, our priority remains to optimize our core capabilities, grow our footprint 
ethically, and deliver enduring worth to our stakeholders. Guided by a distinct strategic 
framework, focused implementation, and a steadfast dedication to reliability, progress, and 
service quality, we are poised to carry forward this expansion and solidify Karnataka Bank's 
standing as a dependable financial partner."
