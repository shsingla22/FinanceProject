ABD Maestro unveils YELLO Designer Whisky,  
a bold new fusion of Scotch and Indian Malts 
 
Powerhouse Ranveer Singh and ABD Maestro quickly follow the launch of 
Rangeela Vodka with a Whisky custom-made for modern India. 
 
Mumbai, 01st December 2025:  ABD Maestro Pvt. Ltd., the super-premium and luxury spirits 
brand company and a subsidiary of Allied Blenders & Distillers (ABD), tod ay announced the 
launch of YELLO Designer Whisky , a unique fusion of Scotch Malts and Indian Malt whiskies. 
 
Following the launch of Rangeela Vodka from Ranveer Singh, Co-Founder and Creative 
Partner of ABD Maestro , the company ’s YELLO Designer Whisky, introduces a breakthrough 
packaging that mirrors the pulse of modern India — expressive, youthful, and born to stand 
out. It grabs the spotlight for its colour, sleek lines, and the ‘whisky window ’. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
For the flamboyant whisky, YELLO fuses the best of Speyside and Highland Scotches with the 
richness of Indian Malts , which is now making news globally . The result is an exceptional spirit 
that partners the distinctive characteristics of both worlds. The Scot ch malts, matured in ex-
bourbon oak, infuse YELLO with notes of vanilla caramel and gentle oak, while the Indian malts 
add warmth, richness, and a bold, lingering finish. 
 
Bikram Basu, Managing Director of ABD Maestro Pvt. Ltd., commented , “YELLO is designed 
for an innovative, daring and youthful brand construct fo r product, packaging and 
communication. It ’s born to stand out , with outstanding whisky  sitting in the bottle. At ABD 
Maestro, we build with quality, creativity and consumers at it s heart. ” 
 
As India rapidly progresses to a major global economy, the super-premium and luxury  spirits 
segment is seeing strong growth. New-age consumers increasingly value self-exp ression and 
strong identity with the brands that they choose. YELLO Designer Whisky, bold  in packaging, 
positioning and taste, talks to this new generation of confident, culture-fo rward consumers. 
 
YELLO Designer Whisky  has debuted in Maharashtra at a MRP of Rs. 2,700/- for a 750ML. Goa, 
West Bengal and markets of North India will follow in quick succession .
