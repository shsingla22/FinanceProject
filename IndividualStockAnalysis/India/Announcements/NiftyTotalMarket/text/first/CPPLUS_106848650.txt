FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE 
CP PLUS owner Aditya Infotech to acquire 30% stake in AI video -compression 
firm BLUE  
₹45.4 crore investment brings proprietary technology that can reduce  video storage and 
bandwidth requirements by up to 90%, while strengthening CP PLUS's AI- led surveillance 
capabilities  
Noida, November 5, 2026:  Aditya Infotech Limited (BSE: 544466 | NSE: CPPLUS), one of India's 
leading providers of video surveillance and security technology solutions and owner of the 
flagship CP PLUS brand, has received Board approval to invest  ₹45.4 crore in KGraph AI Solutions 
Private Limited (BLUE), an AI -powered video compression technology company, for a 30% stake 
on a fully diluted basis. The investment will strengthen the technology capabilities behind CP PLUS products and advance its nex t-generation surveillance solutions.  
The transaction will comprise  a combination of fresh equity infusion and purchase of shares from 
existing shareholders. It implies  a post -money valuation of approximately ₹151 crore and is 
expected to be completed by November 5, 2026, subject to customary closing conditions. The deal marks another step in CP PLUS 2.0, the brand's evolution from a hardware -led surveillance 
business in to an integrated security solutions provider. BLUE's technology addresses a growing 
requirement of modern surveillance infrastructure: managing increasing volumes of video data efficiently while preserving essential visual information.  
BLUE is led by six co -founders who bring 15 to over 20 years of experience, with academic 
backgrounds from IIT Bombay and IIM Bangalore. The company has a 10- patent pipeline, with 
three applications filed and seven in progress.  
BLUE's proprietary Semantic Codec technology uses a perception- aware compression layer over 
the standard H.264 video codec to identify redundant visual information and reduce data volumes while preserving essential details. Based on its deployment data, the technology can 
reduce  video storage and bandwidth requirements by up to 90%. It operates on cost- efficient 
edge hardware without requiring GPU infrastructure  and is compatible with existing cameras 
and recording systems. This can help  customers extend video retention and optimise deployment 
costs without replacing their  existing infrastructure.  
As surveillance expands across smart cities, government infrastructure, enterprises and critical infrastructure, the industry is moving towards more intelligent, connected and insight -led 
security solutions. This shift calls for stronger capabilities in da ta management, edge computing, 
AI-powered analytics and cloud integration. BLUE's technology complements the CP PLUS 
roadmap by strengthening this critical layer and creating opportunities to enhance existing solutions and develop more scalable AI -enabled monitoring, video analytics and cloud -based 
services.  
 
BLUE will continue to operate independently while collaborating with Aditya Infotech's R&D and 
engineering teams across Noida, Ahmedabad and Taiwan. The partnership will combine BLUE's expertise in computer vision, embedded systems and video compression with Aditya Infotech's engineering, manufacturing and market capabilities to advance the development of intelligent security solutions.  
Commenting on the investment, Aditya Khemka, Managing Director, Aditya Infotech Limited, said:  "Video compression is foundational to the next phase of surveillance. As cameras multiply 
and resolutions rise, the cost of storing and moving video becomes the real constraint. BLUE addresses this challenge on cameras already in the field and brings a highly experienced team with expertise  in computer vision and embedded systems. This investment is an important step 
in our CP PLUS 2.0 journey towards intelligent, connected security for a safer India."  
The partnership provides BLUE with an opportunity to scale its technology by leveraging Aditya Infotech's established surveillance ecosystem, engineering capabilities and customer reach. It also brings together complementary expertise to explore emerging a pplications of intelligent 
video technology across enterprise, industrial and infrastructure environments. 
Kunal Kislay, Co -founder and CEO, BLUE, said:  "Our vision at BLUE has been to make video 
intelligence more efficient and accessible by addressing the growing challenges of video storage 
and bandwidth. Partnering with Aditya Infotech gives us the opportunity to take our technology to a wider market, combining our specialised capabilities with CP PLUS's established surveillance ecosystem, engineering strengths and customer reach. We look forward to contributing to the next phase of intelligent and connected surveillance solutions."  
About Aditya Infotech Limited:  
Aditya Infotech Limited (BSE: 544466 | NSE: CPPLUS) is one of India’s leading video security and

Bharat initiatives.  
About BLUE:  
BLUE is an Indian deep -tech company pioneering a new category of AI -native video 
infrastructure through its  Semantic Video Data Lake  and proprietary  Semantic 
Codec technologies. The platform transforms continuous video streams into intelligent, 
searchable and actionable enterprise data, enabling organisations to store, manage, analyse 
and derive insights from video at scale. Its proprietary Semantic Codec significantly reduces video bandwidth and storage requirements while accelerating AI inference, making large -scale 
video intelligence more efficient and economically viable. Designed for enterprises across manufacturing, infrastructure, energy, logistics, retail and public safety, BLUE enables 
 
organisations to extend their video surveillance into an AI -powered video data 
infrastructure.  BLUE is redefining how machines comprehend, manage and reason over visual 
information.
