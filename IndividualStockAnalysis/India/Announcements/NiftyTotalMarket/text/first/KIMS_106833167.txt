Further to the earlier intimation dated December 21, 2025, regarding the Company’s 
execution of a Leave and Licence Agreement with the Andhra Mahila Sabha Trust (a 
public charitable trust) [“Trust” hereinafter] for taking on a long-term lease land 
admeasuring approximately 1.168 acres (equivalent to 21.19 Grounds), located at Dr. 
Durgabai Deshmukh Road, Raja Annamalai Puram, Chennai, Tamil Nadu-600028, for 
the establishment of a Super Speciality Hospital, the following update is being 
provided for the information of the general public. 
 
Mrs. Lakshmi Murthy and Ms. Vidya Gajapathi Raju Singh, as plaintiffs seeking to 
institute a representative suit in the interest of the public, initiated proceedings under 
Section 92 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, questioning the administration and 
functioning of the Trust, including the manner in which the aforesaid lease transaction 
was undertaken wherein the Company was arrayed as a party being the Defendant 
No. 12. On July 29, 2026, the learned Single Judge granted leave to institute the suit 
against the Defendants therein. The Trust’s appeal against the said order was 
dismissed by the Division Bench on September 15, 2026, and the interim stay was 
vacated, thereby permitting the suit proceedings to continue. Thereafter, the Company 
approached the Hon’ble Supreme Court by way of a Special Leave Petition, which was 
dismissed on October 27, 2026. 
 
 
 
 
In this regard, it is clarified that the aforesaid proceedings relate to issues concerning 
the administration of the Trust. The proceedings remain pending adjudication on their 
merits, and no final determination has been made in respect of the allegations raised.  
The aforesaid proceedings remain pending adjudication on their merits. However, no 
order of injunction or stay restraining the Company from proceeding with the project 
has been passed in the said proceedings. Since the Company’s rights and interests 
under the Leave and Licence Agreement subject to its terms and applicable law, 
continuous to prevail, the project’s commercial viability and underlying value remain 
intact.
