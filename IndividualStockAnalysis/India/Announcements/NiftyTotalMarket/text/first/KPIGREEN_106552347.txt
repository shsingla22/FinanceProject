Sub.: Execution of Battery Energy Storage Purchase Agreement  for Development of 445 MW / 890 
MWh IPP Standalone BESS  Projects with GUVNL  
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Apropos the captioned subject and pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and further to our earlier disclosure dated January 3, 
2026  regarding the receipt of a Letter of Intent (“LOI”)  from Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited 
(“GUVNL”)  for the development of self -owned IPP Standalone Battery Energy Storage System 
(“BESS”) Projects  with an aggregate capacity of 445 MW / 890 MWh,  we wish to inform you that Sun 
Drops Energia Limited,  a subsidiary of KPI Green Energy Limited (“the Company”),  has executed the 
Battery Energy Storage Purchase Agreement (“BESPA”) with GUVNL.  
 
The BESPA has been executed pursuant to the tariff -based competitive bidding process conducted 
by GUVNL for procurement of Standalone BESS capacity under Phase -VII, supported by Viability 
Gap Funding (VGF) through the Power System Development Fund (PSDF) . 
 
Under the terms of the BESPA, Sun Drops Energia Limited will develop, own and operate Standalone 
BESS Projects with an aggregate capacity of 445 MW / 890 MWh at multiple locations in the State 
of Gujarat  and make the contracted battery energy storage capacity available to GUVNL on a long -term 
basis.  
 
The execution of BESPA marks a significant milestone for the Group as it formally progresses into the 
implementation phase of its first utility -scale IPP Battery Energy Storage System project , further 
strengthening its presence across emerging segments of the renewable energy ecosystem.
