MapmyIndia Mappls integrates with Arattai  
Mappls App to enable Aratt ai users to seamlessly navigate to chat -shared locations  
 
• Arattai users can now open locations shared in their chats and groups directly in the Mappls App and get 
turn- by-turn directions to their destination  
 
• The integration brings together two homegrown Indian platforms – MapmyIndia’s indigenous maps & 
navigation app Mappls and Zoho’s indigenous messaging app Arattai  – making the journey from a 
conversation to a destination simpler and more seamless.  
 
New Delhi, November 17 , 2026:  MapmyIndia Mappls, India’s leading digital mapping and deep -tech company, 
today announced the integration of its flagship Mappls App with Arattai, an instant messaging and VoIP calling app from global technology company Zoho Corporation. With this integra tion, locations shared in Arattai chats 
and groups can now be opened directly in the Mappls  App, allowing users to get directions and navigate to their 
destination without copying addresses or switching between multiple apps.
 
 
Simple to use -  Open a chat or group on Arattai, tap on the shared location, select ‘Get directions’ and select  
Mappls Maps. The Mappls App instantly displays  the route to the shared location along with the estimated travel 
time, distance and trip cost . Users can start turn -by-turn navigation right away. Whether you are  meeting friends 
at a cafe, reaching a family gathering, picking up someone up or finding a new address  - the integration makes 
the journey from a conversation to navigating to a destination s eamless . The feature is available on both Android 
and iOS  devices . 
 
 
1. Open the location in Mappls   
2. View route, travel time & trip cost   
3. Start turn -by-turn navigation

The integration brings together two platforms that have been built from the ground up in India, uniting Arattai’s  secure messaging infrastructure with MapmyIndia’s indigenous location technology. As both 
platforms prioritise technological sovereignty and data protection, users can share locations and navigate without worrying about their data leaving a highly secure local ecosystem. Arattai users who open shared locations in the Mappls  App also benefit from its India -first navigation features, including door- level 
guidance, 3D junction views, and safety alerts and speed camera warnings, helping them reach their destinations safely and with confidence. Mappls App is downloaded by over 45  million users across India.
