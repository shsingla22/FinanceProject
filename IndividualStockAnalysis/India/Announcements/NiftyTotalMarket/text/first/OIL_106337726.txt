Oil India Limited and Oil & Natural Gas Corporation Limited 
Signed Joint Operating Agreement for Three OALP -IX 
Blocks  
 
Oil India Limited (OIL) and Oil & Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC)  have 
entered into a Joint Operating Agreement (JOA)  for Exploration Block s MN-UDWHP -
2023/1,  CB-ONHP -2022/2 , and AS -ONHP -2022/2 , awarded under OALP Bid Round -
IX, covering an area of around 10,965 sq. km . The Block MN-UDWHP -2023/1  in 
Mahanadi Basin is located in the Ultradeep waters of Bay of Bengal with bathymetry 
beyond 2500 m isobath, whereas block AS -ONHP -2022/2 is located in the south -west 
of Meghalaya, one of the most challenging region of Assam Shelf Basin  and shall open 
up new exploration opportunities in the hydrocarbon landscape.

This collaboration endeavour marks a momentous landmark, reflecting a strong 
synergy between the two National Oil Companies to drive  the collective  pursuit of 
hydrocarbon exploration  and sustainable growth  to meet the energy security of the 
country.
