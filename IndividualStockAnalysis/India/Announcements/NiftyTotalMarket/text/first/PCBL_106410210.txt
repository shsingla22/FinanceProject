This is further to our stock exchange intimation dated 1st May, 2024 intimating about an order being issued 
by the Assistant Commissioner, GST (Central Tax), Sangareddy Division confirming a demand of Rs. 
27,94,167/ - (Indian Rupees Twenty seven lakhs ninety four thousand one hundred and sixty seven) as tax 
with applicable interest & penalty of Rs. 2,79,41 6/- (Indian Rupees Two lakhs seventy nine thousand four 
hundred and sixteen) during the period April 2018 to March 2019 & April 2020 to March 2021.    
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 (7) of the SEBI Listing Regulations , this is to inform that pursuant to the Company 
preferring an appeal against the said order, the Additional Commissioner of Appeals II, Office  of the 
Commissioner Appeals of Customs & Central Tax Appeals II Commissionerate, Hyderabad has issued an 
order  with revised demand of Rs. 2,35,741  (IGST - INR 53147, CGST -INR 91297 , SGST - INR 91297) 
along with applicable interest and penalty under section 73 of the CGST Act, 2017 , thereby granting partial 
relief to the Company . 
 
Details required as per SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 1 1th November, 2024 ,
