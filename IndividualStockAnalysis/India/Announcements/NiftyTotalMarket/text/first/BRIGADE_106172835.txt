PRESS RELEASE 
 
Brigade Group Announces the Launch of Brigade Eternia in Yelahanka, Bangalore 
 
 
Revenue potential of over ₹2700 crores; Project to be completed by March 31, 2030 
 
Bangalore, March 14, 2025  – Brigade Group has launched its latest residential project, Brigade 
Eternia, in Yelahanka, Bangalore. This expansive development, spanning 14.65 acres will feature 1124 
residential units with a total built-up area of approximately 2 million square feet across 12 towers 
of 2 Basement + Ground + 13/14 floors each. The project has a revenue potential of over ₹2700 
crores. Brigade Eternia, set to be completed by March 31, 2030, is designed to provide a luxurious 
and comfortable living experience, with a focus on community and convenience.  Brigade Eternia 
offers a range of 3 BHK, 3 BHK with Study, and 4 BHK spacious homes, catering to diverse needs. 
Each home is thoughtfully designed to provide comfort and luxury, with expansive decks and flexible 
home office/study spaces. 
 
Commenting on the launch, Pavitra Shankar, Managing Director, of Brigade Enterprises Limited, said, 
“North Bangalore, particularly Yelahanka, is emerging as a key micro market with immense growth 
potential. We are thrilled to be launching Brigade Eternia on the last major land parcel in the heart 
of Yelahanka, and this is one of the best locations for a residential project of this scale, especially 
in recent years. By providing a combination of luxury, convenience, and community-focused amenities, 
we offer an excellent option for potential homebuyers in the region.” 
 
Brigade Eternia’s strategic location allows easy access to schools, hospitals, and office spaces. The 
project boasts a variety of amenities designed for all age groups, including a grand central courtyard, 
a scenic lakeside park, and a green entry tunnel with a Ficus court that provides a beautiful, shaded 
walkway enhancing aesthetic appeal of the project. Additionally, the project features an iconic 
expansive urban window façade and a 35,000 sq. ft. clubhouse with premium amenities. 
 
About Brigade Group:  Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property 
developers with close to four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their 
stakeholders and winning their customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark buildings and 
transformed skylines across South India in the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, 
Kochi, Trivandrum and Gift City, Gujarat with developments across Residential, Office, Retail and 
Hotels.
