PRESS RELEASE  
GRSE signs MoU with DNV - Strengthening India’s Maritime & Defence 
Shipbuilding Capabilities  
 
Kolkata/Hamburg (02 September, 2026):  Expanding its technological frontier 
and global competitiveness on the international stage, Garden Reach Shipbuilders & 
Engineers (GRSE) Ltd., a Navratna Defence Public Sector Undertaking under the 
Ministry of Defence, Government of India, signed a Memora ndum of Understanding 
(MoU) with global maritime organisation DNV (Det Norske Veritas) , on 02 Sep 26 
during the SMM Hamburg Maritime  Trade Fair in Germany.   
 
The MoU brings together GRSE’s established expertise in warship design, 
shipbuilding and marine engineering with DNV’s global capabilities in classification, 
certification, technical assurance, digitalisation, sustainability and maritime advisory 
services.  
 
The collaboration shall focus on key areas including:  
◼  Advanced warships and specialised vessels  
◼  Green shipping and sustainable technologies  
◼  Digital shipbuilding, AI and Industry 4.0  
◼  Skill development, training and knowledge exchange

GRSE signs MoU with M/s Damen - Advancing India ’s Dredging &  
Maritime Infrastructure  
Kolkata/Hamburg (02 September, 2026):  Expanding its technological frontier 
and global competitiveness on the international stage, Garden Reach Shipbuilders & 
Engineers (GRSE) Ltd., a Navratna De fence Public Sector Undertaking under the 
Ministry of Defence, Government of India, signed a Memorandum of Understanding 
(MoU) with global maritime organisation  Damen Technical Cooperation B.V., 
Netherlands , on 02 Sep 26 during the SMM Hamburg Maritime  Trade Fair in Germany . 
The MoU intends to establish a technical and business collaboration framework to 
explore the design and construction of advanced dredgers in India.  
 
The MoU combines GRSE ’s shipbuilding capabilities with Damen ’s globally 
prove n expertise in Trailing Suction Hopper Dredgers (TSHD) and Water 
Injection Dredgers (WID) . 
 
The Key areas of cooperation include:  
◼ Advanced dredger design and technology  
◼ Technical know -how transfer  
◼ Joint design and construction proposals  
◼ Development of indi genous dredger -building capabilities  
◼ Long -term cooperation for future dredging requirements
