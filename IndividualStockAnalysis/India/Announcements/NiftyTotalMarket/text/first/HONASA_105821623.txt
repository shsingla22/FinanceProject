10.1 ACCOUNTING TREATMENT IN THE BOOKS OF THE TRANSFEREE COMPANY FOR THE MERGER 
OF TRANSFEROR COMPANY 1 AND TRANSFEROR COMPANY 2 WITH THE TRANSFEREE 
COMPANY  
 
Upon the Scheme becoming effective, the amalgamation of the Transferor Company -1 and 
Transferor Company -2 with the Transferee Company will be accounted for in the following 
manner:  
 
10.1.1  Notwithstanding anything else contained in the Scheme, the Transferee Company shall 
account for the amalgamation of the Transferor Company 1 and Transferor Company 2 in its 
books of accounts in accordance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS) notified under 
Section 133 of the Compa nies Act, 2013, under the Companies (Indian Accounting Standard) 
Rules, 2015, as may be amended from time to time, and the date of such accounting treatment 
would be in accordance with the applicable Ind AS:  
 
10.1.2  The accounting treatment will be as under:  
 
a. The Transferee Company shall record the assets and liabilities, if any, of the Transferor 
Company 1 and Transferor Company 2 vested in it pursuant to this Scheme, at the carrying 
values as appearing in the consolidated financial statements of the Transferee C ompany;  
 
b. The identity of the reserves pertaining to the Transferor Company 1 and Transferor Company 
2 shall be preserved and the Transferee Company shall record the reserves of the Transferor 
Company 1 and Transferor Company 2,  in the same form and at th e carrying values as 
appearing in the consolidated financial statements of the Transferee Company;
