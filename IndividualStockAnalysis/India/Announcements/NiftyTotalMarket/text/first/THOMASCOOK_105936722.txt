Sterling unveils the newly refurbished Sterling Karwar , a tranquil alternative to Goa and a  
Picture -Perfect Destination Wedding on the West Coast  
 
• Located on the estuary where the River  Kali merges with  the Arabian Sea  
• Selection of Renewed and Upgraded rooms , for an i mmersive and comfortable e xperience 
• Banquet Hall and Expansive Lawns repurposed for Intimate Weddings

Date: 02 August , 2024  
Sterling Holiday Resorts has unveiled the newly refurbished Sterling Karwar, a serene retreat located in the 
picturesque coastal town of Karwar, Karnataka. Nestled near the estuary where the Kali River enters the Arabian 
Sea and just a 1 5 kms drive from Goa, this resort offers a pristine alternative to the bustling beaches of its 
neighbouring  state.  
The resort features 8 4 newly refurbished rooms with views of the estuary and lush coconut groves. Sterling 
Karwar also boasts excellent banquet facilitie s, making it an ideal choice for corporates looking for an alternative 
to Goa and for celebrations, kitty parties, anniversaries  and intimate weddings –  where one can take up the 
entire resort exclusively for the event .  
The banquet venue can comfortably s eat 150 guests for indoor conferences, while the outdoor lawn can 
accommodate up to 1000 guests in weddings and socials, providing a picturesque setting for memorable events.  
Guests can enjoy a variety of amenities and activities within the resort, includi ng the multi -cuisine rasa  
restaurant, where visitors can savour  authentic dishes from the Konkan coast. Guests can unwind at the LOCAL 
resto -bar, which offers an authentic  fusion of  cocktails and exotic regional flavours . Additionally, visitors can 
partake in  activities such as cycling, table tennis, and carrom board, or simply relax by the pool while soaking in 
the sea breeze.  
Sterling Karwar  is also a pet -friendly resort  and offers custom designed rooms for pets, a cura ted pet menu and 
earmarked pet zones for playtime, ensuring that every family member feels at home.  
"We are thrilled to unveil Sterling Karwar  in a new avatar and present the resort in its full potential for the 
discerning traveller. Karwar as a destinatio n has lots to offer for our guests and its far more pristine compared 
to Goa. As a leading brand in the region , we are looking forward to making Karwar a more experiential getaway. 
Also , with a Sterling resort In Varca Goa, it’s a perfect circuit or can be  an enroute stopover for people driving 
into Goa. The resort can host  multiple events, pre/post wedding ceremonies, thematic offsites, and leisure 
getaways .” said Vikram Lalvani, CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts .  
For those looking to explore, Sterling Karwar serves as an ideal base to discover nearby attractions such as 
Dandeli, Rabindranath Tagore Beach, and Majali Island. Guests can also embark on day trips to popular 
destinations like Om Beach and Gokarna, exper iencing the best of both  the seren ity of  Karwar and the vibrant 
atmosphere of Goa.

About Sterling Holiday Resorts Ltd.  
Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited is a leading hospitality company in India that owns & manages 50 resorts, hotels
