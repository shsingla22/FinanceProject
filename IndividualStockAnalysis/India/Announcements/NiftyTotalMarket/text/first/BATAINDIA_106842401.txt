PRESS RELEASE  
 
BATA INDIA DECLARES Q2 RESULTS - FOURTH CONSECUTIVE QUARTER OF ACCELERATING GROWTH  
~Strong double digit profit growth | Premiumisation and SSSG drive growth | Improved cash flow~  
 
Gurugram, November 3, 2026: Bata India, India’s most trusted footwear today announced its financial 
results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, reporting revenue of Rs. 8,618 million, reflecting a growth of 
over 7.5% vs. Rs. 8,013 million in Q2FY26, despite the festive calendar largely deferring to Q3 this year. Profit after tax for the quarter sto od at Rs. 180 million, compared with Rs. 138 million in the corresponding quarter last 
year, a growth of over 31%, supported by higher ASP.  
 One- off’s for the quarter includes non -cash forex loss on license fees amounting to Rs. 30 million owing to 
continued currency devaluation and Rs. 39 million towards  one time asset write -off. Other income was lower by 
Rs. 46 milion  due to distribution of surplus funds of Rs. 3,213 million during the quarter as special dividend. 
 Commenting on the results, Mr. Sanjay S. Rao, Managing Director and CEO, Bata India, who took over from 
October 1, 2026, said, “On a quarterly basis, the growth momentum continued for the  fourth consecutive 
quarter, with a topline growth of over 7.5% in Q2FY27 , robust LFL & ASP growth in Retail, and double -digit 
growth in Franchise and I&D channels.”   
 “Gross margin improved marginally, and operating performance continued to strengthen. Inventory improved in both quantity and quality, with  freshness at 92%  and  net stock 7% lower  than on September 30, 2025. We now 
have 2,000+ stores  (including franchise) and 3,800+ KROs, and are optimising the network to drive footfall. Zero 
Base Merchandising, now in 850 stores, has improved consumer experience and revenue per sq. ft. Despite 
higher input costs and wage revisions, freshness and prudent sourcing delivered our highest ful l-price sales and 
lower markdowns. We remain focused on  profitable growth  through premiumisation, network expansion, 
sharper assortment, volume growth, consumer  engagement, operational excellence and disciplined capital 
allocation. With festive business shifting to Q3, we  expect the growth momentum to continue ,” he added. 
 
ABOUT BATA INDIA : 
 
For close to a century, Bata India has been a symbol of trust and quality for Indian consumers, serving over 250,000 
customers every day. Throughout this journey, Bata has continuously evolved to cater to the diverse needs and 
preferences of its customers to become the largest footwear retailer and manufacturer in the country. Its expansive 
retail network consist s of 2000 + stores (Company Owned and Franchise), supported by thousands of Multi Brand 
Outlets and a robust omni -channel presence across D2C and marketplaces.  
 
Bata India sells close to 50 million pairs annually . Bata India is on a mission to make global trends and premium fashion 
accessible to all consumers through its extensive retail network. It is redefining the intersection of fashion and comfort 
through brands including Bata Red Label, Bata Comfit, Power, N orthStar, Floatz, Bubblegummers and Hush Puppies 
among others.
