Coforge Partners with Driving Forward to Expand Career Pathways for 
First -Generation and Under -Resourced College Students  in Chicago  
 
Coforge supports their mentorship, paves their practical learning  roadmap  and provide 
professional connections that help these students move from college to career  
 
Princeton, NJ, USA | Greater Noida, India | October 27, 2026  – Coforge Limited (NSE: 
COFORGE), a global AI -native engineering services leader, supported  Driving Forward, a U.S. -based 
nonprofit that helps first -generation and under -resourced college students successfully transition from 
college to career. Coforge participated as an Opportunity Sponsor at the organization’s 2026 Annual 
Celebration, held in Chicago.  
 
Driving Forward helps students build practical skills, confidence and professional networks through 
programs that include paid internships, one -on-one mentorship and coaching, project -based learning, 
career -readiness training and networking opportunities. Since 2020, the organization has positively 
impacted nearly 280 students.  
 
Coforge’s sponsorship reflects its commitment to expanding access to career development 
opportunities and supporting the next generation of professionals. The Coforge team attended the 
celebration, joining students, mentors, volunteers and industry leaders  to recognize the impact of the 
program. Several Coforge employees also volunteered and some of them have been selected to serve 
as a Driving Forward mentor in 2027.  
 
“Talent is everywhere, but access to mentorship, practical experience and professional networks is 
not always equal,” said  Preeti Singh, EVP & Head of US Geo, Coforge.  “Through our support of Driving 
Forward, we are proud to help students build the skills and connections that can open doors as they 
begin their careers. We look forward to contributing to this work and to the community of mentors 
and leaders investing in the next generation.”  
 
“We’re very grateful to Coforge for investing in Driving Forward and our work with first -generation 
college students and students from financially disadvantaged backgrounds. Their sponsorship helps 
us provide the mentorship, practical experience, and conne ctions that can make a lasting difference 
as students begin their careers,” said Marvin Richardson, Board Chair and Co -Founder, Driving 
Forward.  
 
The 2026 Annual Celebration brought together approximately 100 attendees, including students, 
volunteer mentors and executives from across the healthcare and business communities. Students 
shared how the organization’s programs have supported their career journeys, while mentors and 
coaches spoke about the value of helping students prepare for professional opportunities.
