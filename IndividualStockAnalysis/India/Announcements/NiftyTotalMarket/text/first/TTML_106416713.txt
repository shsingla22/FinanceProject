Pursuant to the provisions of Section(s) 110, 108 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act,

and notification/circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India, for holding 
general meeting/conducting postal ballot process through e-Voting, the Company has transacted special 
business by way of Postal Ballot through remote e-Voting process as set out in the notice of Postal Ballot 
dated September 19, 2025. 
 
The remote e-Voting period started on Saturday, September 27, 2025, at 9.00 a.m. (IST) and concluded on 
Sunday, October 26, 2025, at 5.00 p.m. (IST).  
 
The Scrutiniser has on Monday, October 27, 2025, submitted the Scrutiniser’s report on the results of the 
Postal Ballot through remote e-Voting. Based on the report of the Scrutiniser, we hereby inform that, the 
Members of the Company have duly passed by requisite majority the above-mentioned ordinary resolution 
as set out in the Posal Ballot Notice.  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 44(3) of the Listing Regulations, we are submitting herewith the details of the voting 
results and the Report of the Scrutiniser.
