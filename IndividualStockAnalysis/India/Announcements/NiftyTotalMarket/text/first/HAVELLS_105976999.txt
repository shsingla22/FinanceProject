Havells Studio Unveils Meditate Air Purifier Ra nge with Revolutionary SpaceTech Technology 
 Powered by the international space station inspired Advanced SpaceTech Technology 
 Equipped with smart features, seamles sly integrating into Any living space 
National, September 4, 2024: Havells, a leading Fast Moving Electrical Goods (FMEG) company, 
proudly announces the launch of its latest innova tion—the Studio Meditate AP400 and AP250 air 
purifiers. Building on the success of the previously  launched Studio Meditate Air Purifier, which was 
praised for its SpaceTech technology, these new mo dels introduce advanced features and enhanced 
performance, making them the ultima te solution for air purification. 
 
With innovation and cutting-edge technology at its core, the Havells Studio Meditate Air Purifiers 
offers a host of advanced features designed to ensure optimal air quality and enhance the living experience. Below is an exploration of the key elem ents that make this air purifier a revolutionary 
addition to any home. 
 
 Revolutionary SpaceTech Air Purification Technology  
At the core of the Havells Studio Meditate is the revolutionary SpaceTech Air Purification technology. 
Inspired by the technology used on the International Space Station, this system purifies air at a molecular level through photo-catalytic oxidation. In  this process, air is passed through channels 
coated with titanium dioxide and exposed to UV-AC and UV-A light, effectively eliminating 
microscopic impurities such as dus t, bacteria, and fungal spores. 
 
 Mindfulness Beyond PM 2.5 
The Havells Studio Meditate addr esses a broader spectrum of indo or air pollution beyond just 
filtering particulate matter like PM2.5. It tackles volatile organic compounds (VOCs), dust, dirt, and 
aerosol particles released by everyd ay household activities such as cooking, cleaning, and even from 
furniture and carpets. 
 
 Pure Air All-Year-Round 
Understanding that air pollution is  a year-round concern, the Havells Studio Meditate ensures deeply 
cleansed indoor air every day. It effectively comb ats pollution from various sources, including paint 
odors, formaldehyde emissions, pet hair and dander,  and kitchen fumes, making it an indispensable 
appliance for maintaining a healthy home environment. 
 
 Innovative Features and Smart Interaction 
The Havells Studio Meditate is equipped with Ai r Quality Indicator (AQI). The AQI Indicator 
automatically monitors the air quality in the vicinity  and adjusts the purifier’s fan speed to maintain 
optimal air quality. It operates in three modes: 
 
 Active Mode: Functions as a remote and air quality i ndicator, displaying AQI and controlling 
the purifier. 
 Sleep Mode: Displays AQI using an LED indicator with  the display off to conserve battery. 
 Explore Mode: It allows users to check the AQI in diff erent areas of their home and displays 
air quality without controlling the purifier.
