Brief Profile  
 Chaturvedi & Co LLP was established in 1963 having 
ICAI registration number 302137E/E300286. The firm is 
empanelled with several key regulatory and financial 
institutions, such as Comptroller & Auditor General of 
India (C&AG), Reserve Bank of India, Serious Fraud 
Investigation Office, and various other government and 
non-government bodies. The firm comprises 13 partners, 
more than 10 Chartered Accountants (CAs) and a team of 
over 110 professional staffs, operating from offices 
located Head Office in Kolkata and branches in Mumbai, 
Delhi and Chennai. The Firm has expertise across a wide 
range of domains, including audit and assurance services, 
financial reporting, taxation, corporate advisory, 
restructuring, valuation, due diligence, risk consulting, 
forensic audits, and IPO advisory etc. The firm also 
provides consultancy services in areas such as 
accounting, mergers and acquisitions, due diligence, 
strategic investment advisory, income tax (including 
international taxation), Goods and Services Tax (GST), 
company law matters, SEBI regulations, FEMA, and 
FCRA compliance. Its clientele includes large listed and 
unlisted companies, banks and financial institutions, 
government enterprises, multinational corporations 
(MNCs), and start-ups. The firm maintains a strong 
presence across diverse sectors—manufacturing, 
commercial, services, financial.
