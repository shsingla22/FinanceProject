PRESS RELEASE 
 
Cyient Foundation & SIDBI Swavalamban Foundation 
Launch AI & Future Skills Center of Excellence in 
Nagaland 
Dimapur, November 15, 2025: Cyient Foundation, the CSR arm of Cyient, in 
collaboration with SIDBI Swavalamban Foundation,  announced the establishment 
of an AI & Future Skills Center of Excellence (CoE)  at the Nagaland Tool Room & 
Training Centre (NTTC), Dimapur . The initiative aims to accelerate AI-driven 
skilling and employability  in India’s North-East region, empowering local youth with 
future-ready digital and technological skills . 
The Center of Excellence , which was inaugurated by Smt. Nirmala Sitharaman , 
Honorable Union Minister of Finance and Corporate Affairs , Government of India, 
in the presence of Y.Patton , Hon’ble Deputy Chief Minister of Nagaland, and Dr. 
BVR Mohan Reddy, Founder Chairman, Cyient Limited,  marks a significant 
milestone in India’s mission to democratize access to technology and promote 
inclusive growth. 
As the Program Management and Implementation Partner  of AI & Future Skills 
Center of Excellence (CoE) at the Nagaland Tool Room & Training Centre (NTTC), 
Cyient Foundation  conceptualized, designed, and executed the project. The CoE 
will offer AI-integrated skilling programs  across domains such as Handloom & 
Handicraft ; AI, Software Coding & Robotics , aimed at bridging employability gaps, 
promoting entrepreneurship, and fostering digital inclusion in the region. 
The initiative has been supported through a CSR partnership with the SIDBI 
Swavalamban Foundation, which has extended funding support towards setting 
up infrastructure, AI simulation labs, and digital classrooms at NTTC. 
Dr. BVR Mohan Reddy, Founder Chairman, Cyient Limited , said, “India’s future 
depends on how effectively we empower our youth with digital and technical skills. 
At Cyient Foundation, we are deeply committed to making technology a force for 
social good. The AI & Future Skills Center of Excellence in Nagaland exemplifies our 
vision to bridge the digital divide, create opportunities in emerging industries, and 
enable every youth regardless of geography to contribute to India’s innovation story. 
We are proud to collaborate with NITI Aayog in identifying need of integrating AI and 
emerging technologies into skilling unemployed youth in the developing states of 
India, we are also glad to get associated with SIDBI Swavalamban Foundation as an 
implementation partners on this impactful national initiative.” 
Mr. Sudatta Mandal, Deputy Managing Director, SIDBI , said, “This collaboration 
reflects our shared commitment to building a digitally skilled and self-reliant India. 
By supporting the AI & Future Skills Center of Excellence in Nagaland, SIDBI 
Swavalamban Foundation is advancing inclusive skill development and 
entrepreneurship in one of India’s most promising regions. Together with Cyient  
 
 
Foundation and NITI Aayog, we aim to create a scalable model for technology-led 
livelihood generation.” 
The CoE is expected to train over 200–240 youth annually , with 75% of graduates  
projected to find employment or become self-employed within three months of 
course completion. The model includes faculty training, AI-based curriculum 
integration , and market linkage opportunities  for sustained impact. 
This initiative supports national missions including Digital India , Skill India , and 
Viksit Bharat 2047 , strengthening India’s efforts to equip its workforce with 
advanced technology capabilities while fostering regional economic resilience. 
This initiative reflects Cyient Foundation’s larger vision of “Empowering 
Tomorrow Together” —equipping India’s youth with future-ready skills and creating 
pathways to inclusive growth. Building on its strong track record, the Foundation has 
digitized over 120 government schools , reaching more than 35,000 students  with 
digital literacy programs. Through its Adopt-a-School initiative , over 21,000 
children  are currently receiving quality education. Beyond education, the 
Foundation has also championed women’s empowerment and rural development . 
Each year, more than 2,000 rural women  are trained for sustainable employment.   
 
About Cyient Foundation 
Cyient Foundation, the CSR arm of Cyient Ltd., is dedicated to driving sustainable 
social impact through initiatives in education, digital literacy, healthcare, and skill 
development. Since its inception, the Foundation has partnered with communities, 
governments, and NGOs to create inclusive and scalable models for change.
