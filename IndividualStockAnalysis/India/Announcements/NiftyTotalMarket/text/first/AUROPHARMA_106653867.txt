Hyderabad, India, June  03, 202 6 
 
 
 
 
 
In the presence of Shri D. Sridhar Babu and Shri Damodar Raja Narasimha, Ministers of the Government of 
Telangana, Aurobindo Pharma unveiled a landmark CMO that sets a new standard for biologics manufacturing in 
India — with MSD joining as its anchor customer.  
 
 
Hyderabad, India — June  03, 2026 — Aurobindo Pharma Limited today marked a significant milestone with the 
inauguration of TheraNym, one of India's largest dedicated biologics contract manufacturing organisations (CMO). 
Ahead of the ceremony, the Directors of Aurobindo Pharma and the senior leadership team of MSD called upon 
Hon'ble Chief Minister Shri Anumula Revanth Reddy, briefing him on the project and expressing their gratitude for 
the Government's steadfast support. The TheraNym Biologics facility was then inaugurated by Shri D. Sridhar Babu, 
Hon'ble Minister for IT, Industries & Commerce and Legislative Affairs of Telangana, and Shri Damodar Raja  
Narasimha, Minister for Science and Technology of Telangana — a gesture that reaffirms the Government's 
unwavering commitment to positioning Telangana as a global centre of excellence in pharmaceuticals and 
biotechnology.  
 
TheraNym represents Aurobindo Pharma’s bold and strategic foray into biologics contract manufacturing — a 
segment that is growing worldwide and is increasingly vital to meeting global demand for biologic therapies.  
 
“TheraNym represents a defining moment in large -scale biologics manufacturing in India and a proud milestone for 
Aurobindo Pharma. Designed and built to the highest international standards, the facility is positioned to serve the 
supply chains of leading global pharmaceutical companies. We are privileged to welcome MSD as TheraNym's 
anchor customer — their confidence in our capabilities is both an honour and a mandate to deliver excellence. As 
the global biologics market continues its remarkable expansion, TheraNym stands ready to help bring biologic 
therapies to patients worldwide, reliably and more affordably than before."  — Dr. Satakarni Makkapati, CEO 
Biologics and Director, Aurobindo Pharma Limited  
 
The facility is strategically located in Borapatla approximately 45 minutes from the Hyderabad’s bustling Financial 
District and is equipped with 15 KL bioreactors, advanced downstream processing suites, aseptic fill -finish 
capabilities, and quality control laboratories. TheraNym has been designed with international regulatory 
expectations in mind. The facility is intended to support MSD supply chain, both domestic and export markets, 
subject to applicable approvals and customer requirements.  
 
“This innovative partnership reflects a shared commitment to strengthening global access to critical biologic 
medicines. TheraNym adds meaningful capacity and capability to the MSD  supply chain, helping enable compliant, 
and reliable supply of products to the patients who need them most.”  - Dr. Dave Maraldo, MSD Executive Vice 
President and President, MSD Manufacturing Division

HYDERABAD: INDIA’S BIOTECH CAPITAL  
Hyderabad’s stature as India’s premier pharmaceutical and biotechnology hub is the product of decades of 
deliberate ecosystem building. The city is home to over 800 pharmaceutical companies, employs more than 
300,000 life sciences professionals, and generates pharmaceutical exports exceeding USD 8 billion annually. 
Genome Valley alone has attracted marquee global and Indian companies and represents one of Asia’s densest 
concentrations of biologics R&D and manufacturing capability.  
Key strengths of the Hyderabad ecosystem include:  
•  World -class scientific talent drawn from IITs, BITS, University of Hyderabad, and the Centre for Cellular and 
Molecular Biology (CCMB)  
•  A mature vendor and supplier network for bioprocess equipment, raw materials, and analytical services  
•  Proximity to international airport enabling rapid logistics for temperature -sensitive biologic products  
•  A collaborative innovation culture linking industry, academia, and Government through institutions like BIRAC, 
DBT, and the Telangana Academy for Skill and Knowledge  
 
TheraNym is proud to be a part of this ecosystem and commits to contributing to its further growth through job 
creation, technology transfer, and supplier development.  
 
INDIA’S POLICY MOMENTUM: A FOUNDATION FOR THE FUTURE  
Aurobindo Pharma acknowledges the transformative impact of India’s policy landscape in catalysing the growth of 
domestic biomanufacturing. The Government of India’s Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme for 
pharmaceuticals, the National Biopharma Mission, and the Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council 
(BIRAC) are expected to collectively mobilise significant capital and talent into the biologics sector.  
Aurobindo Pharma is confident that with sustained Government commitment and support, India can capture a 
dominant share of the global biologics CDMO market and deliver enormous economic and public health dividends 
for the nation.
