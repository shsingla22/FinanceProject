ABD Records Highest Ever Monthly Sales in Festive Season  
Highest ever monthly sales volumes reported  in two consecutive months , with over 3.5 million 
cases in  September 2026 and over 3.6  million cases in October 2026  
Officer's Choice Brandy and Sumo Whisky achieve millionaire brand status, bringing ABD ’s 
tally to 6 millionaire brands  
Mumb ai, 4th November, 2026: Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD), one of the leading 
spirits companies in India, today  announced that the strong volume momentum seen  through 
H1FY27 has continued  in October 2026 . 
Demonstrating sustained market demand and execution excellence, the Company recorded 
its highest -ever monthly sales on four occasions within seven months in current financial  
year, including consecutive record -breaking months in September and October 2026 . 
Monthly sales volume exceeded 3.5 million cases in September and rose further to over 3.6 
million cases in October, driving combined volumes for the two months beyond the 7 million 
case s mark . 
The performance reflects consistent  demand across the portfolio, with festive season -led 
demand providing additional momentum in both months. It builds on the strength of flagship  
millionaires  brands  – Officer ’s Choice franchise and ICONiQ White , continued premiumisation 
and wider market reach. The  growth was broad -based, with contributions  from  across  regions  
and defence  market . Additionally , international markets  played important role in maintaining 
this momentum.  
Underpinning this growth is the enduring strength of the company ’s core brands, highlighted 
by Officer's Choice Brandy  joining the prestigious millionaire brands club. Driven by strong 
consumer response, the brand has achieved near double -digit market shar e in the key 
southern states within the Mass Premium category.  
Sumo Whisky , the Company ’s latest brand in the Mass Premium  and Others  segment, also 
joined the millionaire brands club in October 2026 . Led by strong consumer demand  across 
key southern markets, the brand further strengthens  ABD ’s whisky portfolio.  
With this milestone, ABD now boasts six millionaire brands in its portfolio, further reinforcing 
its market presence and product depth across key categories.  
  

About Allied  Blenders and Distillers Limited ( www.abdindia.com ) 
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD) is one of India ’s leading spirits companies and 
the country ’s largest spirits exporter by volume, with a presence in 42 international markets. 
The Company operates across whisky, brandy, rum, vodka and gin. Its portfolio is anchored 
by "millionaire" brands, including the flagship Officer ’s Choice, which remains among the 
largest -selling whiskies globally. This is complemented by Sterling Reserve and the rapidly 
scaling ICONiQ White, which has been the world ’s fas test-growing millionaire whisky brand 
for 3 consecutive calendar years – CY2023, CY2024 and CY2025. As part of its 
premiumisation journey, ABD has expanded into the super -premium and luxury segments 
through its subsidiary - ABD Maestro Pvt Ltd. The company maintains a robust 
manufacturing footprint of 41 units including 10 owned bottling facilities, 2 owned ENA and 
1 malt distilleries, 1 owned PET bottle plant and 27 non -owned manufacturing units 
(including 1 in Malaysia), supported by a pan -India distributi on network that ensures scale 
and supply chain flexibility.
