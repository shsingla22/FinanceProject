Mastek Partners with Innovaccer to Transform Value -Based Care and 
Improve the Patient Experience  
By utilizing advanced customer experience (CX) solutions and analytics, Mastek will enhance its 
healthcare service offerings and deliver substantial value through a value -based care model.  
 
Mumbai, India , 21st October , 2024 : Mastek (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted digital 
engineering and cloud transformation partner, is pleased to announce its strategic partnership 
with Innovaccer, a leading healthcare AI company to drive better outcomes at lower costs and 
meet contract ed performance targets. This collaboration will help recalibrate Mastek’s 
extensive healthcare focus by integrating Innovaccer’s expertise in data, customer experience, 
AI, and analytics, enhancing service offerings, and de livering significant impact through a 
focused value -based care model.  
 
With healthcare as a key strategic focus, Mastek and Innovaccer aim to deliver holistic solutions to healthcare organizations. This partnership will enable healthcare organizations to 
provide best -in-class patient/consumer experiences, improve health outcomes, and improve the 
overall quality of care, all while ensuring affordability for patients.  
 
Innovaccer’s AI- powered Health Experience Platform (HXP) unifies data from diverse sources,  
including electronic health records (EHRs), customer relationship management (CRM) systems, and enterprise data warehouses (EDWs). By integrating this platform with Mastek’s Healthcloud 
capabilities, healthcare organizations will gain access to predictive  and actionable insights, 
helping them enhance decision -making and patient outcomes. Omnichannel communication, 
marketing intelligence, and propensity scoring organizations will significantly enhance patient engagement across multiple touchpoints, deliveri ng a more personalized and efficient 
healthcare experience.  
Raman Sapra , President  &  Chief Growth Officer at Mastek  said , "We are thrilled to partner with 
Innovaccer  as Mastek further solidifies its role as a trusted system integrator, magnifying digital 
excellence and innovation within the healthcare industry. This partnership aligns with Mastek's 
mission to decomplexify the complex healthcare landscape, address crit ical market dynamics, and 
drive meaningful impacts in patient lives. We look forward to seeing our combined capabilities 
deliver a multitude of improvements in patient experience and achieve greater efficiency in our 
service offerings. " 
The partnership offers several key benefits for healthcare organizations:  
● Healthcare AI Platform:  This suite of AI -powered applications will help automate trivial 
tasks, analyze clinical data, deliver insights, and ease data accessibility.  
● Data Integration/APIs for Interoperability:  Advanced CX and analytics capabilities will 
help centralize data from diverse sources on a secure healthcare platform, streamlining 
 
document and record exchange through data modules and APIs for enhanced 
interoperability.  
● Population Health Management:  Accelerate value -based care transformation by 
improving clinical, financial, operational, and experiential outcomes with advanced analytics and workflows.  
● Risk Adjustment and Care Management:  Leveraging data -driven insights and AI -
powered risk analytics will help customers identify gaps and format information from structured and unstructured data, providing a comprehensive 360 -degree view to 
improve patient interactions and outcomes.  
● Patient Experience: Enterprise CRM will empower its healthcare clients to attract new 
consumers and guide patients throughout their entire care journey.  
● Seamless Integration and Digital Transformation:  Mastek's expertise in digital 
engineering, cloud transformation, and healthcare IT integration will ensure smooth implementation of Innovaccer's solutions into existing healthcare ecosystems. This will 
also empower healthcare organizations to rapidly mode rnize their IT infrastructure, 
optimize workflows, and leverage advanced technologies while maintaining focus on 
core healthcare delivery.  
"We’re excited to partner with Mastek to bring our AI -powered Healthcare Experience Platform to a 
wider audience, " said Abhinav Shashank, co -founder and CEO of Innovaccer . "This collaboration 
aligns perfectly with our mission to accelerate care innovation and empower healthcare 
organizations with scalable, modern applications to help them focus on what truly matters: 
delivering high -quality care. By combining Mastek's digital engineering expertise with our advanced 
healthcare tech nology, we're set to deliver personalized, connected, and convenient healthcare 
experiences that will not only improve patient outcomes but also drive growth and efficiency for healthcare providers. Together, we're taking a significant step towards realizi ng our vision of a 
more intelligent, patient -centric healthcare ecosystem. "
