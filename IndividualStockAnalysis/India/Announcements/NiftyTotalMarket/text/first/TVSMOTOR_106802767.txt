TVS MOTOSOUL 6.0: THE ULTIMATE CELEBRATION OF 
MOTORCYCLING RETURNS THIS DECEMBER  
• The 6th edition of TVS MotoSoul will be held in Goa on December  11-12, 2026  and is 
set to be the biggest and most immersive one.  
Bengaluru, September 30, 2026 : TVS Motor Company, part of TVS VENU and a leading 
global two- and three- wheeler manufacturer, today announced the sixth edition of its flagship 
motorcycling festival, TVS MotoSoul 6.0, to be held on December 11  and 12, 2026, at , Goa.  
Through the years, TVS MotoSoul has consistently served as a platform for showcasing new 
TVS products , custom unveils, merchandise and accessories, customer programmes  and 
international brand collaborations . Some of the notable showcases include:  
Product & Technology Innovation:  
• 2023: Launched TVS Apache RTR 160 4V  with Dual -Channel ABS , Voice Assist  
• 2024 : TVS RT -XD4 300 engine platform  – TVS' 1st indigenously  designed, developed 
and engineered performance- engine platform  
• 2025: Launched TVS Ronin Agonda Edition

Building on the success of its previous editions, TVS MotoSoul 6.0  is set to bring together 
riders, racers, creators, musicians and motorcycle enthusiasts across two immersive, 
high- energy days. With new firsts, experiences and formats added to the festival every year, 
MotoSoul goes beyond a celebration of motorcycling to create a powerful convergence of 
performance, technology, youth culture, creativity and community. The festival brings 
these diverse worlds together to showcase what TVS stands for today, while celebrating the 
evolving spirit of motorcycling and the possibilities it is shaping for tomorrow.  
The festival  brings the world of motorcycling to life to create an experience beyond the 
motorcycle through three defining experiences:

of racing and riding experiences  
 Engage:  Connecting with Apache Owners Group (AOG), TVS RONIN C uLT riders , 
creators  & Motorcycle enthusiasts  
 Celebrate:  Immerse  into a vibrant festival of music, entertainment, food and lifestyle, 
featuring international DJs, L ive performances  and A rtist Collaborations

About TVS Motor Company  
TVS Motor Company (BSE: 532343 and NSE: TVSMOTOR), part of TVS VENU, is a reputed 
two and three -wheeler manufacturer globally, championing progress through sustainable 
mobility with four state -of-the-art manufacturing facilities located in India and Indonesia. 
Rooted in a 100- year legacy of trust, value and passion for customers, the company takes 
pride in making internationally accepted products of the highest quality through innovative and sustainable processes. TVS Motor is the only two- wheeler company t o have won the 
prestigious Deming Prize. Its products lead in their respective categories in the J.D. Power 
IQS and APEAL surveys. The company has been ranked No. 1 in the J.D. Power Customer 
Service Satisfaction Survey for four consecutive years. Its group company, Norton 
Motorcycles, based in the United Kingdom, is one of the most emotive motorcycle brands in 
the world. TVS Motor Company endeavours to deliver the most superior customer experience
