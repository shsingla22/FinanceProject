Sub: Outcome of the meeting of Board’s constituted committee of Embassy Developments Limited  
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Further to the intimation dated November 18, 2026 and pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III and other 
applicable provisions of the SEBI LODR Regulations, please be informed that the Board’s constituted committee of 
Embassy Developments Limited (the “Company”) has, at its meeting held today i.e. November 21, 2026 (which 
commenced at 11:30 a.m. and concluded at 12:25 p.m.), inter alia, considered and approved an enabling authorisation 
for raising funds aggregating up to INR 250 crores through the issuance, on a private placement basis, of up to 25,000 
Non-Convertible Debentures, each having a face value of INR 1,00,000/- (“NCDs” or “Debentures”), in one or more 
tranches and/or series, from time to time, on such terms and conditions as may be approved by the Board or the 
Board’s constituted committee, in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 (the “Act”), 
the rules framed thereunder and other applicable laws. 
 
The aforesaid approval is purely enabling in nature and is intended to provide the Company with financial flexibility to 
access debt capital, as and when required, to meet the phased funding requirements of its ongoing and planned 
projects. The issuance of NCDs may or may not be undertaken, depending on the Company's actual funding 
requirements and other relevant considerations. If raised, the proceeds would be primarily utilised towards the 
development and construction of projects of the Company and/or its subsidiaries, thereby supporting business growth 
and creating long-term value for shareholders and other stakeholders. 
 
The information required to be disclosed pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR Regulations, read with the SEBI 
Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, is set out in Annexure A 
enclosed herewith. The Company shall make the applicable requisite disclosures at the time of each issuance of 
Debentures, including the amount raised and the material terms thereof. 
 
Please take the above information on record.  
 
Yours truly,
