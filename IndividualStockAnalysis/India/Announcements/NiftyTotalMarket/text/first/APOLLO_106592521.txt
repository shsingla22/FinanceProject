 PRESS RELEASE  
 
 
Hyderabad, 17 April 2026      |     For Immediate Release  
 
 
Apollo Micro Systems Limited Receives Arms Manufacturing License 
from Government of India  
Lifetime license under the Arms Act, 1959 authorises manufacture of Missiles, ATGMs, Torpedoes, 
Aerial Bombs and Loitering Munitions;   
Apollo Micro Systems Limited  (BSE: 540879 | NSE: APOLLO), a Hyderabad-based defence 
technology company, today announced that the Government of India, Department for Promotion of 
Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce & Industry, has granted the Company a 
license to manufacture and proof test defence weapon systems under Section 43(1) of the Arms Act, 
1959. 
Issuing Authority  DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce & Industry, Government of India  
Issue Date  10 April 2026  
Validity Lifetime — No Expiry  
Annual Production Capacity  1,000 Units per Category  
 
Scope of License 
The license authorises manufacture of the following systems across two categories of Arms of 
Caliber above 12.7mm, at the Company’s facilities in Hyderabad:  
Category I — Guided Weapons, Underwater Systems & Countermeasures: 
 
 Missiles :-   Short, medium and long-range guided munitions for land, air and naval platforms  
 Anti-Tank Guided Missiles (ATGM) :-  Wire-guided, laser-guided and fire-and-forget variants  
 Torpedoes :-  Light weight and heavy weight variants for ship and submarine launch  
 Underwater Mines :-   Naval mines for underwater denial  
 Safety & Arming Mechanisms :-   Fuzing and arming systems for munitions  
 Chaffs, Flares & Decoys :-  Countermeasure systems for platform protection

IDL Explosives Limited — Capability Building  
 
IDL Explosives Limited, is a Step-Down Subsidiary of Apollo Micro Systems Limited, brings established 
expertise in the manufacture of industrial and defence-grade explosives, propellants, pyrotechnics, 
detonators, and initiating systems. This capability is directly relevant to the weapon systems that 
Apollo Micro Systems Limited is now licensed to manufacture.  
IDL Explosives Limited’s capabilities in the areas relevant to the licensed weapon systems include:  
 Solid propellants for missiles, rockets and torpedo propulsion systems  
 Explosive fill compositions for warheads, aerial bombs and mine systems  
 Pyrotechnic compositions for chaffs, flares and decoy systems  
 Precision detonators and initiating systems for fuzing applications  
 Warhead design and explosive fill from development through to production  
 
The combination of IDL Explosives Limited’s energetics capabilities with Apollo Micro Systems 
Limited’s competence in guidance electronics, embedded systems, and sensor integration is 
expected to support the end-to-end development and manufacture of the weapon systems covered 
under the license.

 About Apollo Micro Systems Limited  
Apollo Micro Systems Limited(BSE: 540879 | NSE: APOLLO | CIN: L72200TG1997PLC026556) is a 
Hyderabad-based, publicly listed, multi-disciplinary defence technology company and a Tier-I 
Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) with end-to-end capabilities spanning the design, 
development, manufacture, integration and life-cycle support of advanced weapon systems and 
defence platforms. The Company's competencies encompass defence electronics, embedded 
systems, electronic warfare, electro-optic systems, guidance & control, seekers, fuzing electronics, 
and complete weapon system integration. An established Tier-I supplier to DRDO, HAL, BEL and the 
Ministry of Defence, Apollo Micro Systems Limited is now additionally authorised to manufacture 
missile-class weapon systems, torpedoes, aerial bombs and loitering munitions under the Arms Act, 
1959 reinforcing its position as a full-spectrum, sovereign-capable defence manufacturer committed 
to the Aatmanirbhar Bharat mission.
