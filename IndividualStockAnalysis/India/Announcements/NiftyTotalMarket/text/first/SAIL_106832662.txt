Profile of Ms. Sarita Chauhan. Additional Secretary & Financial Advison
Ministry of Steel and Director, SAIL
Ms. Sarita Chauhan is a senior civil servant belonging to 1999 batch of the Indian
Administrative Service, AGMUT Cadre. She holds a Bachelors' degree in Textile
Engineering and brings over 27 years of extensive experience spanning both State
and Central Government roles. At present, she is serving as Additional Secretary &
Financial Advisor in the Ministry of Steel, Government of India.
Over the years, Ms. Sarita Chauhan has handled a wide range of portfolios
covering personnel administration, administrative reforms, e-govemance,
education, environment, labour, social welfare, law and justice, tourism, industries,
agriculture, fisheries, animal husbandry, power, urban development and district
administration. Her diverse administrative exposure has placed her in several key
policy and implementation roles at both the Central and Cadre levels.
In the State of Jammu & Kashmir, she served as District Magistrate and Deputy
Commissioner, Kathua, where she led district administration in land revenue
management and delivery of development programmes. As Secretary of various
departments for over 10 years in the State, Ms. Sarita has experience in the areas
of policy, planning and institution building.
In the Government of India, as Joint Secretary and Additional Secretaiy,
Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances, Ms.. Sarita has
worked in Digitalization of Governance, through e-Govemance reforms for citizen
empowerment and citizen centric governance. The assignment gave her exposure
to national-level policy and programme implementation.
Since September 2026, Ms Sarita Chauhan has assumed the position of Additional
Secretary & Financial Advisor, Ministry of Steel, where she oversees financial
administration and advises on fiscal matters relating to the steel sector.
She is also serving as Government Director on the Board of NMDC Steel Limited.

PROFILE OF SHRI VINOD KUMAR TRIPATHY, JOINT SECRETARY,
MINISTRY OF STEEL & DIRECTOR, SAIL
Shri Vinod Kumar Tripathi, Joint Secretary, Ministry of Steel, Government
of India, belongs to Indian Railway Service of Engineers (IRSE-1995). He is a
Civil Engineering graduate from IIT Roorkee. He has accomplished Advance
Management courses from NAIR Baroda, INSEAD Singapore and Leadership and
Governance Centre (Iclif) Kuala Lumpur. He has also got training in Strategic
Management from ISB Hyderabad.
Shri Tripathy has versatile experience in Administration, Management and
Engineering. This includes management of Public Sector Enterprises, Public
Policy, Projects, Operations, Technological Advancement, Procurement &
Inventory Management and Human Resource Management.
