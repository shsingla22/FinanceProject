PRESS RELEASE  
 
Double Digit Revenue and Profit Growth  
 
Mumbai, October  29, 202 6: Godrej Consumer Products Limited (GCPL), a leading emerging 
markets FMCG company, today announced its financial results for the quarter ended  
September  30, 2026 . 
 
 
FINANCIAL OVERVIEW  
Q2 FY 2027  FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE SUMMARY:  
• Q2 FY 2027  consolidated sales grew by 19% year -on-year  on the back of underlying volume

Press Release – October  29, 2026  MANAGING DIRECTOR AND CEO’S COMMENTS  
 
Commenting on the business performance, Aasif Malbari , Managing Director, and CEO, 
GCPL, said:  
 
Q2 FY2 7 has been a quarter of strong, broad -based performance for Godrej Consumer Products Limited, 
fully aligned with our expectations and strategic priorities. We delivered  strong  broad -based  underlying 
volume  growth across India, Indonesia and Africa , despite subdued  monsoon conditions in India while  
supported by tailwinds in Indonesia.  
At a consolidated level, revenues grew 19% year -on-year (15% in CC terms), with underlying volume 
growth of 8%. EBITDA grew 12%, Net profit grew 1 4% to ₹5 23 crore. This marks our second consecutive 
quarter of high teens revenue growth with h igh-single -digit underlying volume growth and double -digit  
profit growth .  
Our Standalone business delivered strong 13% sales growth performance driven by 7% underlying volume 
growth . This is despite an estimated ~150 basis point s impact from trade inventory correction in the 
quarter . While t here  has been a modest near -term impact on growth, we believe that maintaining healthy 
channel  stock levels  is important for  operational excellence and sales team to remain focused purely on 
offtakes.  
In Home Care, we delivered 19% value growth led by strong performance in Air Fresheners and Home c are 
liquids , alongside  we gained market share in Household insecticides. This is encouraging and indicates that 
the actions we have taken across brands are beginning to deliver . Our electrics portfolio delivered 
sequentially improving performance, supported by the new “6 -minute” campaign, while incense sticks 
continued to scale strongly. Godrej Fab maintained its  strong  double -digit growth trajectory and 
continued to strengthen its market position.  Godrej Spic, which was scaled nationally during Q1, continues 
to witness healthy consumer repeats and sequential growth, with significant distribution headroom still 
available.  During the quarter, we launched Godrej Rizz in select markets across West and South India. We 
also launched Godrej Zap, our entry into stain removers  online . Both these new launches have been well 
received . 
Personal Care delivered value growth of 8%. Soaps remained stable despite significant price increases and 
grammage reductions, although with a favorable comparator. Our premium liquid formats  continue to do 
well . Cinthol Bodywash continue s to scale sequentially, while Muuchstac face wash progressed in line with 
our business plan. Hair Colours maintained its growth momentum and continued to gain market share 
across our key bets. Perfumes and Deodorants delivered double -digit growth, supported by healthy 
underlying volume growth in deodorants and contin ued momentum in perfumes and EDPs . 
Our international portfolio delivered strong growth. In Indonesia, sales grew 20%, wi th underlying volume 
growth of 8%. EBITDA grew 1 2%, even with LPG prices doubling . However, we have benefited from the 
EL-Nino tailwind leading to an  increased mosquito infestation. With our future portfolio of LV, Pocket etc. 
we have seen continued tremendous success with strong double -digit underlying volume growth.  
GAUM delivered another outstanding quarter. Sales grew 40% in INR terms and 23% in constant -
currency terms, supported by underlying volume growth of 16%. EBITDA grew 4 3%. This performance 
was broad -based across categories and geographies. More importantly, GAUM has now delivered seven 
consecutive quarters of double -digit top-line growth and eleven consecutive quarters of  double -digit  
bottom -line growth. This reinforces our confidence that the improvement in the business is structura l. We 
continue to be focused on the larger opportunity that the continent offers. The  launch of HI incense stick 
has received good early traction . 
The input cost environment remained significantly challenging during the quarter. Inflation intensified 
across crude -linked derivatives, palm oils and other commodity inputs. This has led to one of the highest
