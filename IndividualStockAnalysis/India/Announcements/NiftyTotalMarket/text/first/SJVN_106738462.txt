 PRESS RELEASE  
 
SJVN Achieves COD of 75 MW Jamui Solar Power Project in Bihar  
 
Sh. Bhupender Gupta, Chairman & Managing Director, SJVN has informed that 
SJVN has successfully achieved Commercial Operation Date (COD) of the 75 MW 
Jamui Solar Power Project in District Jamui,  Bihar.  The project has been executed 
through wholly owned subsidiary, SJVN Green Energy Limited (SGEL) marking 
another significant milestone in company’s renewable energy journey.  
 
Sh. Ajay Sharma, Director (Personnel), SJVN, informed that the 75 MW project is 
expected to generate approximately 90 million units of electricity during its first year 
of operation and around 3,522 million units cumulatively over a period of 25 years. 
The project has been developed over approximately 300 acres of outright -purchased 
land near Village Hadhadiya, Kakanchaur, in Jamui District of Bihar, at an estimated 
project cost of ₹  342 crore.  
 
Sh. Parthajit  De, Director (Finance), SJVN, stated that the tariff for the power 
generated from the project has been fixed at ₹2.96 per unit. The competitive tariff will 
facilitate the availability of affordable and clean green power to consumers. The 
electricity generated from the project will be supplied to the State of Bihar.  
 
Sh. Rajesh Chandel, Director (Projects), SJVN, informed that the project has also 
contributed to employment generation and local economic developmen t. It has 
created approximately 15 –20 direct employ ment opportunities and around 50 -100 
indirect employment opportunities, thereby contributing to the creation of green jobs 
and providing an economic impetus to the local community.  
 
The commissioning of the project is also a significant contribution towards the 
Government of India’s target of achieving 500 GW of renewable energy capacity by 
2030. Besides supplying clean energy to the grid, the project is estimated to reduce 
approximately 82,250.74 tonnes of carbon emissions, thereby supporting the 
country’s decarbonisation objectives and climate commitments.  
 
The 75 MW Jamui  Solar Power Project has been developed pursuant to the tender 
floated by the Bihar Renewable Energy Development Agency (BREDA), Government 
of Bihar. The successful commissioning of the project further reinforces SGEL’s 
commitment to developing renewable energy projects across the country and 
advancing SJVN’s vision of becoming a leading renewable energy company.  
 
With the commissioning of the Jamui  Solar Power Project, SJVN continues to 
expand its renewable energy footprint and contribute towards building a cleaner, 
greener and more sustainable energy future for India.
