2. Necessity of the Buy Back
The Board, after undertaking a comprehensive review of the Company's financial and cash flow position,
business outlook, growth strategy, future funding requi rements and long-term va lue creation opportunities,
believes that the prevailing market price of the Equity Shares does not adequately reflect the Company's
underlying business fundamentals, intrinsic value and future growth prospects.
The Buy Back provides an opportunity to enhance shareholder value by enabling Eligible Shareholders who
wish to participate to tender their Equity Shares at a premium to the prevailing market price. The Board
believes that the Buy Back reflects its confidence in the Company's business strategy, operational strengths
and long-term prospects, while reinforcing its commitment to creating sustainable value for shareholders.
The Board is of the view that the Buy Back is in the best interests of the Company and its shareholders and
represents an efficient means of rewarding shareholders while demonstrating confidence in the Company's
future growth trajectory.
The Board remains confident in the Company's ability to continue executing its strategic priorities, strengthen
its core businesses, accelerate digital initiatives, leverage technology-led innovation and pursue future
growth opportunities to build a stronger, future-ready entertainment and media enterprise while maintaining
a disciplined approach towards value creation for all stakeholders.
