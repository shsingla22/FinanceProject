Media Release  
Arvind Limited Announces New Leadership Chapter  
in Textiles & Apparel  
 
Ahmedabad, 18th December, 2025 : Arvind Limited, India’s leading textile, apparel 
and technical textile manufacturing company today announced an important 
leadership change. Punit Lalbhai, Vice Chairman, Arvind Limited is stepping into an 
executive role to lead the textiles and apparel business as a part of the company’s 
long term succession plan. The change will be in effect from April 1st, 2026. 
 
Susheel Kaul, MD and President of the textiles and apparel business has led Arvind 
with distinction. He will continue in his role as MD and will take up the key strategic 
projects, working closely with the management to shape the future growth initiatives 
of the group. 
 
Susheel has expressed his gratitude, commenting on the development, he said " years 
I have spent in leadership roles, have been the most satisfying part of the long relation 
I have had with the group. Arvind Limited has been an agile and innovative 
organization and I foresee it rising to newer heights. I will remain thankful to Mr. Sanjay 
Lalbhai and Mr. Punit Lalbhai for their trust and support, and I am looking forward to 
the next chapter in my personal and professional life"  
 
Punit Lalbhai, Vice Chairman, Arvind Ltd said "S usheel has been central to Arvind’s 
success, driving strong growth, innovation, and expansion. We have together built a 
brand that speaks for itself. We sincerely appreciate his contributions to the 
organization ." 
 
Arvind expresses heartfelt appreciation for Mr. Kaul’s service and looks ahead to 
continued growth under its refreshed leadership structure. 
 
About Arvind Ltd: 
 
Arvind is a textile to retail conglomerate with focus on textiles, apparels, advanced 
materials, environmental solutions, telecom and Omni-channel commerce. Arvind
