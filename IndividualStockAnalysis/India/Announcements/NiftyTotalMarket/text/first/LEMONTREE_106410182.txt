Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in  Maharashtra  
 
Mumbai , 16th October 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -growing 
mid-scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the signing of its latest 
property , Lemon Tre e Resort, Saj by the Mountain, Mahabaleshwar.  
 
This property shall be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned 
subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited . It will further strengthen  the company’s 
presence in Maharashtra , while catering to both  business  and leisure travellers for a 
fresh, vibrant, and value -driven experience.  
 
Property Highlights   
Lemon Tree Resort, Saj by the Mountain, Mahabaleshwar  is in the charming 
hill station known for its lush green valleys, strawberry farms, and breathtaking 
viewpoints. Famous for attractions like Arthur’s Seat, Kate's Point, and Elephant's 
Head Point . Visitors can go boating at the Venna Lake , visit the Pratapgadh Fort, built 
on a hilltop in the 1650s and head to the Lingmala Waterfall  for scenic views . The 
town offers a perfect mix of natural beauty and colonial charm. With its pleasant 
climate, serene landscapes, and winding roads lined with dense forests, 
Mahabaleshwar is as an ideal getaway for nature lovers, honeymooners, and families 
looking for a peaceful retreat.  
 
The key features of the hotel will include:  
• 78 well-appointed rooms  
• A rest aurant  and a bar  
• A banquet hall  
• Recreational facilit y includes a swimming pool and a spa  
 
The Pune International Airport  is approximately 147 kms away from the property, 
while the Pune Railway Station is about 160 kms away. The hotel is well connected 
by roadways for both public and private transport.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels, commented , "With this  signing , we will expan d our leisure 
portfolio in Maharashtra , a state where we have 15 operational and 11 upcoming 
properties in addition to this one . While expanding its footprint across the country 
across cities of all tiers, this signing by Lemon Tree Hotels perfectly aligns with the 
company’s vision of offering refreshing and comfortable stays across all destinations ."
