SUB.:  Approval of the Amended and Restated  Deposit Agreement  
 
We would like to inform you that the Board of Directors of Axis Bank Limited (the “Bank”) at its meeting 
held today, i.e., October  15, 2025, has approved the Amended and Restated  Deposit Agreement  
which includes certain amendments to the Deposit Agreement governing the Global Depositary 
Receipts (“ GDRs ”) issued by the Bank , subject to approval of Reserve Bank of India . 
 
The amendments inter alia  pertain to t he grant  of voting rights by the Bank to the holder s of the GDRs , 
which  would be subject to such holders  demonstrating that their  holdings are in conformity with  
Section 12B of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 read with applicable master  direction s and 
guidelines, each as amended from time to time . 
 
The Board meeting commenced at 11:30 am (IST) and concluded at 6:50 pm (IST).
