RateGain’s  UNO Channel Manager Wins “World’s Best Hotel Channel Manager 2025” for Redefining Speed, 
Reliability, and Control in Hotel Distribution  
India, 11th December 2025:  RateGain  Travel Technologies Limited (RateGain), a leading provider of AI -
powered SaaS solutions for travel and  hospitality , today announced that  it has been awarded “World’s Best Hotel 
Channel Manager 2025” at the World Travel Tech Awards , one of the highest global honors in hospitality technology.  
The win marks a defining milestone in UNO’s journey toward building a modern, intelligent CRS that gives hotels 
unprecedented control, transparency, and confidence across their distribution ecosystem.  
Over the years, UNO has reimagined the channel management experience by replacing legacy, fragmented workflows 
with a highly reliable and globally scalable distribution backbone. Its next -generation CRS architecture powers 
thousands of hotels worldwide, en suring consistency in operations, lightning -fast and error -free ARI distribution, and 
enterprise -grade performance suited for large hotel groups and complex multi -property environments. With deep, 
high -quality channel connectivity and industry -leading stab ility, UNO has emerged as the distribution -first CRS that 
modern hotels rely on for revenue accuracy and operational resilience.  
Bhanu Chopra, Founder and Managing Director , RateGain, said , “This award validates our belief that a strong, 
reliable channel manager is the heart of any modern CRS. Hotels today need precision, speed, and stability in their ARI 
distribution  - and UNO delivers exactly that. This recognition is a tribute to the hotels that trust us, the channel partners 
who collaborate with us, and the teams who have built UNO into one of the world’s most dependable distribution 
platforms.”  
UNO continues to expand its global presence with strong adoption across leading hotel chains, soft brands, 
independents, and regional groups in NORAM, LATAM, Europe, APMEA, and India. Its momentum aligns with 
RateGain’s commitment to shaping the future of hotel distribution, advancing channel connectivity, strengthening rate 
and inventory governance, accelerating ARI workflows, and driving greater automation. Co -built closely with hotel 
partners, UNO’s roadmap remains focused on delivering unmatched reliabi lity, speed, and distribution intelligence for 
a rapidly evolving hospitality landscape .
