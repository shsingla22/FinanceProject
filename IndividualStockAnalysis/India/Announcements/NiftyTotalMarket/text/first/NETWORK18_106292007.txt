Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Cessation of Eenadu Television Private Limited as an 
associate of the Company 
 
In terms of the undertaking given by the Company and pursuant to the order dated August 27, 
2024 of Competition Commission of India approving the combination of the entertainment 
businesses of Viacom18 Media Private Limited (name changed to Studio18 Media Private 
Limited) and Star India Private Limited (name changed to JioStar India Private Limited), the 
Company has assigned the voting rights on the 24.5% equity holding in Eenadu Television 
Private Limited (ETPL) to the promoter of ETPL. The Company continues to hold the economic 
interest in these 24.5% equity shares.  
 
ETPL has thus ceased to be an associate of the Company. 
 
The promoter of ETPL does not belong to the promoter, promoter group of the Company and 
the transaction is not a related party transaction. 
 
As on March 31, 2025, ETPL contributed to 32.61% of the consolidated net worth of the 
Company. However, there will be no reduction in the consolidated net worth of the Company 
since in accordance with the Accounting Standards, the Company will be fair valuing its 
investment in ETPL.  
 
The assignment agreement was executed at 2:06 p.m. today.
