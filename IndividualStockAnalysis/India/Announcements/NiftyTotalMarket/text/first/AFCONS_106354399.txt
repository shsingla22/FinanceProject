Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("Listing Regulations"), we 
wish to inform you that, the Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting held today 
i.e., Thursday, August 28, 2025, inter-alia approved/ took note of the following matter(s):  
 
1. Resignation of Mr. Shapoorji Pallonji Mistry (DIN: 00010114) as Non-Executive               
Non-Independent Director and Chairman of the Company and his appointment as 
Chairman Emeritus , a honorary non-board position  
 
Mr. Shapoorji Pallonji Mistry (DIN: 00010114) steps-down as “Non-Executive                      
Non-Independent Director” and Chairman of the Company w.e.f. the conclusion of the 
Board meeting held on August 28, 2025. A copy of the resignation letter is enclosed as 
Annexure 1.  
 
Considering the rich experience, leadership, vision and long association of Mr. Shapoorji 
Pallonji Mistry with the Company, the Board has appointed Mr. Shapoorji Mistry as 
Chairman Emeritus of the Company with effect from 29th August 2025 to continue to 
provide guidance, mentorship, and support to the Board and the management of Afcons 
as and when necessary. It is an honorary non-board position without any remuneration/ 
fees. 
 
2. Elevation of Mr. Subramanian Krishnamurthy (DIN: 00047592) as Executive 
Chairman of the Company  
 
Mr. Subramanian Krishnamurthy (DIN: 00047592) has been elevated to the position of 
Executive Chairman of the Company w.e.f. the conclusion of the Board Meeting held on 
August 28, 2025.
