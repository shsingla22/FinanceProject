We wish to inform that the Board of Directors today, on the recommendation of Risk Management

Mr. Padhye is a Chartered Accountant and holds a bachelor’s degree in commerce from University of 
Mumbai. He brings over 23 years of experience in risk management across leading banking and 
consulting organizations, with expertise in establ ishing and strengthening risk management 
functions, market and liquidity risk oversight, risk technology transformation.  
 
He has held leadership roles at HDFC Bank, Ernst & Young LLP and BNP Paribas, and has a proven 
track record of building high -performing teams. He is also a recognized thought leader and speaker, 
regularly contributing at premier institutions and industry f orums on risk management, Responsible 
AI, and Generative AI risk.
