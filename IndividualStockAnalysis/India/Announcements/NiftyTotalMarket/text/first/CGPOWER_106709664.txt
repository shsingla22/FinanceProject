Limited ("TII" or "Holding Company") as of date, and they will demit office as auditors after completion of

August 14, 2026. 
Consequent to the above, we understand that management of the Company proposes to align its statutory 
auditors with those affiliated to the same network as appointed by the Holding Company, consistent with the 
practice adopted by various groups, to enable better coordination with the Holding Company's auditors and 
bring efficiency and synergy to the audit process, given the relative material size of the Company together 
with its subsidiaries (more than 50%) to the Holding Company's consolidated financial statements.
