 
 
 Clarification regarding the likely public listing of ONGC Green Limited New Delhi | 14 December 2024  ONGC would like to clarify regarding the news article on likely public listing of ONGC Green Limited (OGL). The question was asked during recent interview of Director (Exploration) with the correspondent from Financial Express. As part of conversation about exploration activities, growth plans and future energy thrust of the company, Director did mention that the company is in the process of building a green energy portfolio. However, any decision regarding a public listing remains for the future as and when decided by ONGC Board. At present, no concrete plans have been made for a listing. Issued By  Corporate Communication ONGC New Delhi
