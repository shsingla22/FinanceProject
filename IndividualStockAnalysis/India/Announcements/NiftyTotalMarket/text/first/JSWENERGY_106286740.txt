JSW Energy Signs BESPA for 250 MW / 500 MWh Standalone BESS with RVUNL  
 
Mumbai, Ind ia — July 01, 2025  – JSW Renew Energy Thirty Seven Limited , a step -down  subsidiary 
of JSW Energy Limited (“ the Company”), has signed Battery Energy Storage Purchase Agreement s 
(“BESPA ”) with  Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Limited  (“RVUNL”)  for 250 MW / 500 
MWh Standalone Battery Energy Storage System (“BESS”).  
These project s will be developed on a Build, Own and Operate model in the state of Rajasthan . 
The BESPA is signed for a duration of 12 years, with a tariff of ₹ 2,24,000  per MW per month, and 
is supported by viability gap funding.  
The Company's total locked -in energy st orage capacity stands at 29.3  GWh, including 2. 9 GWh of 
BESS  and 26.4 GWh of Pumped Hydro Storage . With this, the Company is well -positioned to 
achieve its target of 40 GWh of energy storage by 2030 .
