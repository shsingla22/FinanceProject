Queensland Tourism  
Mumbai, August  31, 2025 : In a strategic initiative to boost travel to Queensland  - Australia from the high -
potential India market, Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading  omni channel travel services company , 
and its group company, SOTC Travel, h ave signed a lo ng-term, multi -pronged MOU  with Queensland
