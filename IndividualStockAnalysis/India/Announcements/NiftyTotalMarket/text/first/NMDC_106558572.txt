With reference to the above-mentioned subject, we wish to inform you that NMDC Limited has 
executed a Non-binding Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on 16.03.2026 with Gujarat 
Mineral Development Corporation Limited (GMDC) , a Government of Gujarat Enterprise to 
explore collaboration in Rare Earth Elements (REE) and associated minerals. The MoU aims to 
strengthen cooperation towards securing India’s critical mineral supply chain and developing an 
integrated rare earth value chain in India covering exploration, mining, processing, separation 
and downstream manufacturing.
