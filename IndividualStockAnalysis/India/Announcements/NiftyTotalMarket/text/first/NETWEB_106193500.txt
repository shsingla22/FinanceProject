Netweb Technologies Receives PLI Incentive Under IT Hardware Scheme 2.0  
 
 
Delhi - NCR, 3rd  April  2025  - Netweb Technologies is pleased to announce that it has received an incentive 
of   ₹ 5,93,97,758  under the  Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme 2.0 for IT Hardware  for the period 
from  July 1, 2023, to March 31, 2024 .  
 
This approval underscores Netweb Technologies’ contribution in advancing India’s high -end computing 
manufacturing capabilities by providing world -class in -house design, SMT and production facilities , which 
are already in use for producing some of the world’s most powerful systems attached with comprehensive 
solutions’ ecosystem, aligning with the government’s vision of ‘Atmanirbhar Bharat’ (Self -Reliant India). The 
incentive reflects the Government of  India’s recognition of our capabilities in delivering la test generation 
enterprise grade systems, scalable computing solutions and our successful adherence to the PLI Scheme’s 
objectives —driving domestic innovation, self -sufficiency, and global competitiveness in high -performance 
computing infrastructure.  
 
The incentive, granted under the  Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)’s PLI Scheme , 
recognizes Netweb Technologies’ contributions to domestic IT hardware production and innovation. 
Netweb’s clear roadmap for manufacturing top bin technology platforms for coming years too shows our 
unwavering focus and commitment  towards the “ Make -in-India ” mission . The company extends its sincere 
gratitude to the  Government of India, MeitY, and all relevant authorities  for their unwavering support and 
for fostering a conducive environment for local manufacturing.  
Mr. Sanjay Lodha , Chairman & MD  of Netweb Technologies , said,  “We are honoured to receive this 
incentive, which reinforces our dedication to high -quality IT hardware manufacturing in India. This 
achievement reflects the success of the PLI scheme in boosting domestic production and creating employment 
opportunities. W e remain committed to supporting the ‘Make in India’ initiative and contributing to India’s 
growth as a global manufacturing hub.”  
 
About Netweb Technologies   
 
Netweb is India’s leading high -end computing solutions (HCS) provider, with fully integrated design and manufacturing 
capabilities. Netweb’s HCS offering comprises HPC, Private cloud and HCI, AI systems and enterprise workstations, High 
performance storage  (HPS) and Data Centre Servers. Founded in 1999, and  headed by Mr. Sanjay Lodha, Chairman and 
Managing Director, Netweb has helped  many  companies globally by following a customer centric approach.  Netweb 
has manufacturing facility  in Delhi - NCR  and 1 8 offices across India. Netweb got listed at NSE and BSE in July 2023 .
