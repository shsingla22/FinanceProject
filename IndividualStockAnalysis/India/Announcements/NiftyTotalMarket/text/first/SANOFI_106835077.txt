Press  Release   
 
Sanofi India delivers strong Q3 2026 performance, led by 9% growth in 
Diabetes Portfolio resulting in robust profit momentum  
 
Mumbai, October  29th, 2026   
Sanofi India Limited  announced its financial results for the quarter ended on September  
30th, 2026, reporting domestic net sales  growth of +2% and total net sales growth 
of 4% compared to the third quarter of 2025.  
 
The Insulin portfolio  extended its winning run, delivering high single -digit growth of 
9% in Q3 ’26 cementing Sanofi's leadership in the Diabetes Injectable segment with a 
55% market share . Lantus®, Toujeo®, and Apidra® led the charge, joined by the 
strong performance of Soliqua®, the premium offering in the premix diabetes space.  
 
The public sector  business surged by 85%, driven  by new accounts opened for 
innovative analogue segments , further deepening Sanofi's footprint in the diabetes 
injectable space.  
 
Export sales  delivered good double -digit growth  on the back of a winning strategy, 
underscoring the resilience of Sanofi India's international operations amid global 
headwinds.  
 
Strategic partnerships in the Cardiovascular, Oral anti -Diabetes and Central Nervous 
System segments are progressing and have contributed to incremental market reach. 
Partnership revenues were broadly stable in Q3 2026, with the expanded commercial 
footpri nt showing an acceptable return on investment, while continued focus is needed 
to strengthen performance.  
 
Profits before tax and exceptional items  rose 2 2% to INR 1 58 crores, compared 
to INR 130 crores in the third quarter of 2025. Profit before tax as a percentage of net 
sales improved to 34%  in Q3 2026 , up from 29% in Q3 2025.  
 
This gross margin improvement reflects a favorable product mix, a one -off reversal of 
provisions, and disciplined cost management through operational efficiencies, with 
operating expenses  (employee cost + other expenses) declining 8% compared to 

  the third quarter of 2025.

Sanofi in India  
 
Present in India for nearly seven decades, Sanofi has earned the trust of customers and 
stakeholders for its commitment to promoting health. As Sanofi chases the miracles of science 
to improve people’s lives, it continues to engage across the entire health  spectrum from 
prevention with vaccines to wellness, treatment, patient support & capacity building.  
 
Sanofi’s India Charitable Access Program (InCAP) is the country’s longest running humanitarian 
program providing free treatment to people afflicted with Lysosomal Storage Disorders. Sanofi 
conducts clinical trials in India, so that the country can have qui cker access to the latest from 
the company’s global pipeline. Sanofi India’s world -class manufacturing site in Goa produces for 
people in India and 24+ other countries. Sanofi has located one of its Global Capability Center 
in Hyderabad, India, which serve s as a global talent hub, providing a broad range of services 
across Sanofi’s worldwide operations.  
 
Sanofi (India) is a certified ‘Great Place to Work’ (2026 -2027) and is recognized by the ‘Top® 
Employers Institute’ – a global authority that honours excellence in people practices from 2019 -
2024. Sanofi’s local entities include Sanofi India Limited (SIL - listed entity), and Sanofi 
Healthcare India Pvt. Ltd. (SHIPL).
