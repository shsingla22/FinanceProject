P N Gadgil Jewellers Limited - Quarterly Update  Q2FY27 
 
Revenue  Update  
The Company delivered a healthy  performance in Q 2FY27, reporting  revenue  growth  of 22.4% YoY , despite the 
shift in the Navratri festive period to Q3 this year . 
The Retail segment grew 31.1% YoY , with robust same -store sales growth (SSSG) of 25.5% YoY as the primary 
driver , indicating that the growth was substantially organic in nature, driven by deeper customer engagement at 
existing stores. As a result, the Retail segment contributed around 77.3% YoY of the total revenue , which is 
meaningfully higher on a YoY basis.  
Franchise business grew 34.7% YoY , while E-commerce declined 83.6% YoY , primarily due to a strategic 
recalibration of the product mix, as the Company deliberately reduced the share of low -margin coin sales and 
shifted focus toward higher -margin jewellery products to enhance the quality and profitability of the channel.  
The share of gold coin & bar  sales in retail revenue normalised to ~15.8% , compared with ~2 3.3% in the same  
quarter  previous year . The continued moderation in the lower -margin gold coin  business, coupled with the 
increasing contribution from retail and studded jewellery , is driving a sustained improvement in the overall quality 
and profitability of the revenue mix . 
During the  festive period of  Raksha  Bandhan,  Rakhi Sales witnessed strong growth of 1 14% in terms of pieces 
sold and 1 42% in terms of value, compared with the same  quarter  previous year . 
Sales  Mix  
The Company recorded strong growth across categories during the quarter, with gold revenue growing by 23.5% , 
diamond by 21.0%,  and silver by 12.0%  YoY. The growth in diamond sales reflects the growing consumer preference  
for studded jewellery across our existing and new markets. As a result, the studded jewellery mix improved, with 
the retail stud ratio rising to 11.8%  for the quarter  compared to 9 .0% in the same quarter previous year , while the 
northern region delivered a notably stronger performance, achieving a stud ratio of 17.4%.  
The share of old gold exchange as a percentage of sales also increased to  38% in Q2 FY27 , compared with 29% in 
Q2 FY26, reflecting growing consumer participation in gold exchange and recycling as more customers choose to 
bring in their existing gold to upgrade to new jewellery. This rising share of old gold supports the broader objective 
of putting idle household gold back into circulation, thereby helping reduce dependence on fresh gold imports.  
Operational Highlights  
The Company has transitioned LiteStyle to YOOU , marking a strategic evolution towards a more distinctive and 
contemporary brand identity. As consumers increasingly shift towards self-expression, everyday wear and 
lifestyle -led jewellery , YOOU is positioned to address these evolving aspirations through a differentiated product 
and brand proposition, while leveraging the trust, heritage and credibility of PNG Jewellers . 
During the quarter, we opened 2 new stores, comprising one Legacy store  in Gurugram  and one YOOU store  in 
Amanora Mall, Pune . Our focus remained on strengthening the performance of the existing store network, 
accelerating site identification and franchise partner onboarding for upcoming launches, and enhancing 
operational readiness to ensure the effective execution of our expan sion pipeline over the remainder of the year.

The total store count as of Sept  30th, 2026 , stood at 80 (79 in India and 1 in the U.S.A.),  with the store rollout for

Outlook for FY2 7 
We remain on track to open further ~ 23 new stores during FY27 , taking the total store count to around ~103 stores  
by year -end. The rollout will be phased over the remaining two quarters, with launches accelerating, driven 
primarily by a franchise -led expansion strategy across both Legacy and YOOU format stores.  With the Ayodhya 
store expected to open by mid -October  and the Prayagraj store by early November , the Company continues to 
make steady progress on its expansion plans. In addition, 10 store commitments , comprising 6 Legacy and 4 YOOU 
stores , have already been secured, with several more opportunities in the pipeline, predominantly across the  
Northern and Central regions of India . 
While continuing to strengthen our operational excellence in Maharashtra , we are strategically expanding our 
presence across Northern &  Central Region of India , with a focus on building a strong and scalable footprint across 
these high -potential markets.

About Us:  
Established in 1832, P N Gadgil Jewellers Limited is a leading jewellery retailer in India with around 19 4 years of 
heritage. The Company offers a wide range of gold, silver, diamond, and platinum jewellery for weddings, festivals, 
and daily wear. The portfolio also includes a dedicated sub -brand, YOOU by PNG, catering to the growing demand 
for stylish and lightweight jewellery.  
As of  September  30th, 202 6, the Company operates 80 retail stores ( 79 in India and 1 in the U.S.A.).  Under the 
leadership of Chairman & Managing Director Dr. Saurabh Gadgil, a sixth -generation entrepreneur with over 2 6 
years of experience, P N Gadgil Jewellers has evolved into a contemporary brand with strong customer trust and 
loyalty.
