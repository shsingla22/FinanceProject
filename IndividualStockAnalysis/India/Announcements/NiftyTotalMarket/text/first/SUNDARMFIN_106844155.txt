PRESS RELEASE  
Unaudited standalone & consolidated financial results for the  half year  ended  
September  30, 202 6 
 
Sundaram Finance disbursements grew by 2 5% to  Rs. 19,302 crores  in H1FY27; 
Q2FY27 grew by 28% to Rs. 10,355 crores   
AUM grows 1 9% to Rs. 65,992 crores  
Profit after tax closed at Rs. 997 crores, up by 21% over H1FY26 
Gross Stage 3 assets stood at 1.84% (2.03% as of Sep 30, 2025) and Net Stage 3 assets at 0.91% 
(1.13% as of Sep 30, 202 5) 
ROA at 2.86% for H1FY27 (2.72% for H1FY26) 
Capital Adequacy Ratio at  18.5% (19.3% for H1FY26) 
The Board of Directors of Sundaram Finance Ltd. (SFL) approved the unaudited standalone and 
consolidated financial results for the half year  ended  September  30, 202 6, at its meeting held on 
November  4, 202 6, in Chennai.  
“Domestic economic momentum remained strong in Q2FY27 despite an increasingly challenging 
global environment. Renewed tensions in West Asia, crude oil price volatility and tighter global 
financial conditions increased inflationary and external -sector risks.  Against  this backdrop , Team 
Sundaram delivered 1 9% AUM growth to Rs. 6 5,992 crores, asset quality with net stage 3 assets at 
0.91% against  1.13% last year , and 2 1% year -on-year  growth in profit after tax.  Our Group 
companies in asset management, general i nsurance and home finance continued to report  strong 
results. We remain committed  to our time -tested approach of steady , sustainable growth , best -in-
class asset quality and consistent profitability ,” said Harsha Viji, Executive Vice Chairman.  
AUM for H1FY27 grew 19% to Rs. 65,992 crores. Disbursements for H1FY27 recorded a growth of 
25% over H1FY26 and for Q2FY27 , disbursements grew 28% Y -o-Y. Gross stage 3 assets as on  
September  30, 202 6, stood at 1.84% with provision cover of 51% as against 2.03% as on 
September  30, 202 5, with provision cover of 45%. Profit s from operations performed strongly , 
growing by 23% in H1FY27 and 11% in Q2FY27 . Profit after tax registered a 21% rise in H1FY27, 
with net profit at Rs. 997 crores  and for Q2, PAT grew 21% to Rs. 475 crores . Return on assets 
closed at 2.86% in H1FY27 as against 2.72% for H1FY26 and capital adequacy at 18.5% remained  
quite comfortable.  
 
  
 
 
Rajiv Lochan, Managing Director  stated, “ Our outlook for H2 is constructive on growth coupled  
with a need for mo nitoring external factors around geopolitic al tensions , monetary policy direction 
and the impact to sentiment of the below normal monsoon . We have entered H2 with  strong  
momentum and a superior  asset -quality position than last year. As the rate cycle turns , our focus is 
to sustain growth, improve asset quality further and build resilience in profitability through 
disciplined pricing, optimal  asset mix and early intervention on collections. As always, we will take 
a long -term, through -cycle view in delivering the Sundaram experience to our customers, partners 
and employees .” 
 
STANDALONE PERFORMANCE HIGHLIGHTS FOR H1FY27  
 Disbursements for H1FY27 grew by 25% to Rs. 19,302 crores as compared to Rs. 15,423 
crores registered in H1FY26. Disbursements for Q2FY27 grew by 2 8% to Rs. 10,355 crores 
as compared to Rs. 8,113 crores registered in Q2FY26.  Disbursement s for H1FY27 includes 
acquisition of loans amounting to Rs. 726 crores from Sundaram Home Finance Limited.  
 The assets under management grew by 19% to Rs. 65,992 crores as on 3 0th September  
2026 as against Rs.  55,419 crores as on 3 0th September  2025. 
 Net interest income  (NII)  grew by 18% to Rs.  1,888  crores in H1FY27 from Rs.  1,603  crores 
in H1FY26. Q2FY2 7 growth in NII was 17% to Rs. 963 crores . 
 Gross stage 3  assets  as on 3 0th September  2026 stood at 1.84% with 51% provision cover as 
against 2.03% with provision cover of 45% as on 3 0th September  2025. Net stage 3 assets as 
on 3 0th September  2026 closed at 0.91% as against 1.13% as on 3 0th September  2025. 
 The Gross and Net NPA , as per RBI’s asset classification norms for NBFCs,  are 2.38% and 
1.35% respectively as against 2.80% and 1.79% as of 3 0th September  2025. 
 Cost  to income ratio  was at 29.40% in H1FY27 as against 29.60% in H1FY26. 
 Profits from operations grew 23% to Rs. 1,154  crores in H1FY27 as against Rs. 937 crores in 
H1FY26. For the quarter, profits from operations grew 1 1% to Rs. 5 57 crores . 
 Profit after tax register ed a growth of 2 1% in H1FY27, with net profit at Rs. 997 crores  as 
against Rs. 823 crores in H1FY26. For the quarter, profit after tax grew 21% to Rs. 475 
crores.  
 Return on assets (ROA) for H1FY27 closed at 2.86% as  against 2. 72% for H1FY26. Return on 
equity (ROE) was at 16.75% for H1FY27 as against 1 5.91% for H1FY26. 
 Capital Adequacy Ratio stood at  18.5% (Tier I –17.0%) as of 3 0th September  2026 
compared to 19.3% (Tier I – 16.9%) as of 3 0th September  2025.

ABOUT SUNDARAM FINANCE  
Sundaram Finance was established in 1954 and the company has today grown into one of the 
most trusted and diversified financial services groups in India providing financing for 
commercial vehicles, cars & utility vehicles, tractors and farm equipment, cons truction 
equipment, SME finance and a range of working capital products for financing diesel, tyres , 
insurance as well as working capital for SMEs. Through its subsidiaries and group companies, 
the company offers home finance, loans against property, mutua l funds and investment 
management solutions and the full range of general insurance products and services. It has a 
nationwide  presence of over 800  branches, nearly  1 lakh depositors and over  5.80 lakh lending 
customers . 
Sundaram Finance’s vision is to be the most respected NBFC in the country and its mission is to 
deliver the Sundaram experience to all customers, big and small, in keeping with the ethos of 
the Company. Sundaram Finance embraces a philosophy that balances Growth  with  Quality 
and Profitabili ty and remains rooted in its ideal of protecting and enhancing shareholder value. 
The founding philosophy of the company is that everything begins with the customer. Our 
founder, Late Sri T S Santhanam, enshrined in the company its core values - The Sundar am 
Way - that have been the company’s guiding light over the decades. The company is deeply 
rooted in its values and proud of its heritage, also constantly innovating in terms of technology 
and processes to deliver the unique Sundaram experience to its cus tomers and stakeholders.  
ABOUT TSF GROUP  
With a legacy spanning a century, the TSF Group’s interests cover the automotive and financial 
services sectors. Companies promoted by the TSF Group have combined revenue of more than 
Rs. 36,800 crores, 42,000 employees, 1,200 branches, and 36 factories. In  the automotive 
industry, the TSF group operates across segments from component manufacturing, parts 
distribution, vehicle dealership and vehicle financing. The Group serves marquee customers 
across the globe and is known for high quality design‐led manufacturing. The TSF Group 
companies are market leaders in their segments and include Brakes India, Wheels India, Axles 
India, Turbo Energy, IMPAL, Madras Auto Service, and Sundaram Motors . In financial services, 
the TSF Group promoted Sundaram Finance (founded 1954), one of the most respected names 
in the NBFC sector, with interests in automotive lending, general insurance, housing loans, and 
asset management. The financial services business has more than Rs. 97,500 crores in assets 
and a further Rs.  90,500 crores in assets managed. The TSF Group comprises the T.S. 
Santhanam branch of the erstwhile TVS group and continues the tradition of Trust, Value and 
Service that the group has been known for this past century.  For more information, please
