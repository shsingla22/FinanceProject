We are proud to announce that Company has been awarded a significant order for the supply of 
industrial packaging products from a Government of Gujarat Undertaking and a leading name in the Chlor 
Alkali industry. 
This landmark order, valued at approximately INR 65.85 Crores (inclusive of GST), spans a two-year 
contract period. The collaboration underscores Company's unwavering commitment to delivering 
premium-quality products and innovative solutions within the industrial packaging sector. 
This order highlights the trust and confidence our clients place in Company’s capabilities. It marks 
another milestone in our journey to reinforce our position as a leader in the industrial packaging industry. 
The contract is expected to have a positive impact on Company’s business operations, further enhancing 
its market presence and creating new growth opportunities. 
The Company's total consolidated revenue is composed of 76% from the Industrial Packaging segment, 
which includes Polymer Drums, Jerry Cans, Pails and Intermediate Bulk Containers (IBC). The remaining 
24% of the revenue comes from a diversified portfolio, including, Composite Products (LPG, CNG & 
Oxygen), Infrastructure, MOX Film (Techpaulin) as well as Technical and Lifestyle products. This revenue 
breakdown highlights the Company’s strong position in both established and value-added products.
