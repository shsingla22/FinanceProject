Press Release NTPC Renewable Energy Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL) Signed Green Ammonia Purchase Agreement (GAPA) with Solar Energy Corporation Limited (SECI) for supply of Green Ammonia. 
                                           NTPC Renewable Energy Limited (NTPC REL), a wholly owned subsidiary of NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL), has executed a Green Ammonia Purchase Agreement (GAPA) with Solar Energy Corporation of India Limited (SECI) on 30.03.2026 for supply of Green Ammonia under the SIGHT Scheme of the National Green Hydrogen Mission, Government of India. The agreement entails the supply of 70,000 MTPA of Green Ammonia by NTPC REL to M/s Krishna Phoschem Limited, located at Meghnagar, Madhya Pradesh for which SECI is the intermediary procurer. The execution of this agreement marks a significant milestone in NGEL’s Green Hydrogen and Green Ammonia portfolio and reinforces its commitment towards India’s decarbonization goals.
