CPCL Financial Performance for the quarter ended 30.09.2026  
 
Physical Performance  
 
CPCL delivered outstanding operational results during Q2 FY2026 -27, achieving a crude 
throughput of 2.590 million metric tonnes (MMT) considering planned turnaround 
maintenance of one of the crude units during the current quarter. This translates to a 
capa city utilisation of 98%, highlighting the company’s operational efficiency and superior 
plant reliability.  
For the half year ended September 30, 2026 , crude throughput stood at 5.438 MMT 
maintaining a capacity utilisation of 103%.  
Financial Performance  
The Company’s financial results for the quarter reflects outstanding operations and 
improved refining margins.  
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, Revenue from Operations stood at ₹22,109 
crore, compared to ₹20,069 crore in the corresponding period of last year. The Company 
reported a Profit Before Tax (PBT) of ₹3,032 crore and a Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₹22 71 
crore, as against a Profit  Before Tax of ₹994 crore and Profit After Tax of ₹732 crore during 
the same quarter last year.  
For the half year ended September 30, 2026, Revenue from Operations stood at ₹51,486 
crore, compared to ₹38,762 crore in the previous year. The Company reported a Profit 
Before Tax of ₹4397 crore and a Profit After Tax of ₹3288 crore, as compared to Profit  
Before Tax of ₹914 crore and Profit After Tax of ₹675 crore in the corresponding period 
of the previous financial year.  
The Gross Refining Margin (GRM) for the quarter showed a substantial improvement, 
rising to US$ 20.41 per barrel compared to US$ 9.04 per barrel in the corresponding 
quarter of the previous year. For the half year ended September 30, 2026, the GRM stood 
at US$ 14.34 per barrel, as compared to US$ 6.17 per barrel recorded during the same 
period last year.  
The Company declared an interim dividend of ₹25 per equity share of face value of Rs.10/ - 
each for the financial year 2026 –27, reflecting its strong financial performance and 
commitment to delivering sustained value to shareholders.  
On a consolidated basis, for the half year ended September 30, 2026, CPCL recorded a 
Profit After Tax of ₹3278 crore, as against a consolidated Profit After Tax of ₹694 crore in 
the corresponding period of the previous year.
