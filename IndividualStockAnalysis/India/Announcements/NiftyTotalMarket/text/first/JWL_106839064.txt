PRESS RELEASE 
Jupiter Wagons secures orders worth over INR 324 crore from 
GATX India and Chetak International 
• Combined private sector business of over INR 324 crore from GATX India and Chetak 
International, both for ACT2 automobile carrier wagons  
• Letter of Intent from GATX India for 10 rakes of ACT2 wagons; order received from 
Chetak International for 5 rakes of ACT2 wagons  
• Wins underscore strong private sector momentum alongside continued Indian 
Railways order flow, including Jupiter Wagons’ recent INR 1,140 crore Railway Board 
order for 2,583 wagons  
National, 2 November 2026: Jupiter Wagons Limited (JWL), one of India’s leading rolling stock 
and mobility solutions companies, has secured fresh private sector business worth over INR 324 
crore (incl. GST), comprising a Letter of Intent from GATX India Private Limited and an order from 
Chetak International Private Limited, both for its new-design ACT2 wagon platform. 
Jupiter Wagons has received a Letter of Intent from GATX India Private Limited, a leading rail-
leasing company, for the manufacture and supply of 10 rakes of ACT2 wagons, comprising 270 
ACT2 wagons. The Letter of Intent is valued at INR 214.94 crore. 
Separately, the Company has received an order from Chetak International Private Limited for the 
manufacture and supply of 5 rakes of ACT2 wagons, comprising 135 ACT2. The order is valued 
at INR 109.10 crore. 
These wins reinforce the growing traction for Jupiter Wagons’ new-design, ACT2 platform among 
private wagon-leasing and logistics operators. They come alongside strong momentum in the 
Company’s Indian Railways business, including its INR 1,140 crore order from the Ministry of 
Railways for 2,583 wagons, giving Jupiter Wagons a diversified and resilient order book balanced 
across government and private sector demand. 
Commenting on the development, Mr Vivek Lohia, Managing Director, Jupiter Wagons 
Limited, said: 
“These wins reflect the confidence that private wagon-leasing and logistics operators place in our 
new-design ACT2 platform and in our ability to deliver at scale. As India moves a greater share of 
its freight to rail, we are seeing strong, broad-based demand across both the Indian Railways 
programme and the private sector. A balanced order book across government and private 
customers gives us healthy visibility and a resilient foundation for growth, and we remain focused 
on executing with the discipline and quality our customers expect.”  
These orders demonstrate Jupiter Wagons’ deepening traction with private sector customers and 
reinforce its position as a preferred manufacturing partner for specialised, high-capacity wagon 
platforms. Backed by its integrated manufacturing footprint and broad product portfolio, the 
 
Company remains focused on scaling capacity and execution to meet the accelerating demand for 
rolling stock across India’s rail freight and logistics landscape. 
About Jupiter Wagons Limited (JWL) (BSE: 533272; NSE: JWL) 
 
Jupiter Wagons Limited (JWL) is a provider of comprehensive mobility solutions, with offerings across freight 
wagons, locomotives, commercial vehicle load bodies, ISO marine containers, and components including 
couplers, draft gears, bogies, CMS crossings, brake discs, brake systems, wheels, axles and wheelsets. JWL 
operates 14 facilities across India, including manufacturing locations in Kolkata, Jamshedpur, Indore, Jabalpur, 
Bengaluru and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, with full backward integration into foundry operations. The group 
works with leading global companies, including Tatravagonka a.s. (Slovakia) as offtake partner, DAKO-CZ 
(Czech Republic), Kovis d.o.o. (Slovenia) and Talleres Alegria S.A. (Spain). 
 
With a legacy of over four decades, the Company has leveraged its technological capabilities and financial strength 
to emerge as a one-stop shop for mobility solutions and one of the fastest growing companies in its industry. 
Serving sectors including railways, automobiles, transportation, logistics, construction equipment, energy, mining 
and infrastructure, JWL’s client base includes Indian Railways, the Indian Ministry of Defence, BEML, Alstom,
