Sub: Clarification with regards to Postal Ballot Notice dated October 18, 2024 for reclassification of certain 
members from promoter and promoter group category to public shareholder category  
 
We would like to state that the Company, in its intimation dated October 08, 2024, had intimated the Stock 
Exchanges about the receipt of letters by members of the promoter / promoter group seeking reclassification, 
wherein each of the Promoters have confirmed that:  
 
The members of the promoter / promoter group seeking re -classification, along with the person related to them, 
do not / shall not:  
 
(i) together, hold more than ten percent of the total voting rights in the listed entity;  
(ii) exercise control over the affairs of the listed entity directly or indirectly;  
(iii) have any special rights with respect to the listed entity through formal or informal arrangements including 
through any shareholder agreements;  
(iv) be represented on the board of directors (including not having a nominee director) of the listed entity;  
(v) act as a key managerial personnel in the listed entity;  
(vi) be a ‘wilful defaulter’ as per the Reserve Bank of India Guidelines;  
(vii) be a fugitive economic offender.  
 
We would like to clarify that Regulation 31A(1)(b) of SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“ SEBI LODR Regulations ”) clearly mentions as to which related parties are to be considered as 
“persons related to promoters seeking re -classification” which includes relationships covered under sub -clauses 
(ii), (iii) and (iv) of Regulation 2(1)(pp) of SEBI ICDR Regulations.  
 
It is noteworthy that the quoted definition of Regulation 2(1)(pp) of SEBI ICDR Regulations states that “promoter 
group” includes the promoter and the an immediate relative of the promoter (i.e. any spouse of that person, or any 
parent, brother, sister or child of the person or of the spouse).  
 
Mr. Tarachand Thakkar, who is the brother of Mr. Dinesh Thakkar, falls under the 'Promoter group'  solely by virtue of 
the definition prescribed under Regulation 2(1)(pp) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue of Capital 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018. Further, he does not exercise  control over the affairs of the 
Company,  directly or indirectly or have any special rights with respect to the Company  through formal or informal 
arrangements including through any shareholder agreeme nts.  
 
Mr. Lalit Thakkar and Mr. Deepak Thakkar, sons of Mr. Tarachand Thakkar (Member of the Promoter Group), can 
seek reclassification with reference to Regulation 31A(1) of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements ) Regulation 2015, since all the members are part of the Promoter Group.  
 
The other persons who have applied for reclassification does not exercise  control over the affairs of the Company,  
directly or indirectly or have any special rights with respect to the Company  through formal or informal 
arrangements including through any shareholder agreements .
