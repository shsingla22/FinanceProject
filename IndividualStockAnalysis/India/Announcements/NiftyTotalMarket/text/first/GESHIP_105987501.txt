PRESS RELEASE
G E Shipping contracts to sell its 2005 built Suezmax Crude Tanker 
“Jag Lalit”  
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (G E Shipping) has contracted to sell its 2005 built Suezmax 
Crude Tanker, Jag Lalit of about 158,344 dwt on September 13, 2024 to an unaffiliated third party. The 
vessel will be delivered to the new buyer by Q3 FY25. 
Including Jag Lalit, company’s current fleet stands at 43 vessels, comprising 29 tankers (6 crude carriers, 
19 product tankers, 4 LPG carrier) and 14 dry bulk carriers (2 Capesize, 8 Kamsarmax, 4 Supramax) 
aggregating 3.41 mn dwt. 
Additionally, we had contracted to sell one MR product tanker in July 2024 and one Supramax drybulk 
carrier in Aug 2024, which are due for deliveries by Q3 FY25.
