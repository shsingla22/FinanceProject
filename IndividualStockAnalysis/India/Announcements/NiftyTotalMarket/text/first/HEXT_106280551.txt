Hexaware Achieves Guidewire Migration Acceleration Specialization  
Iselin, New Jersey, U.S., 26th June, 2025 : Hexaware Technologies  [NSE: HEXT] , today 
announced that it has been awarded the Guidewire Migration Acceleration specialization  as 
part of the Guidewire PartnerConnect program. Hexaware is a Guidewire PartnerConnect Consulting 
partner at the Advantage level and works with Guidewire in North America, EMEA, and APAC.   
To achieve the Migration Acceleration specialization, partners must demonstrate the ability to lower 
customers’ costs of transitioning to the cloud, accelerate implementation timeframes, and improve 
delivery quality and predictability. They also need to ad here to cloud standards and align with 
Guidewire's Delivery Services and Sales organizations. This specialization is a global badge and is 
awarded by invitation only.  Specializations are both regionally and globally based and require 
partners to demonstrate skills, knowledge, and competency in a particular Guidewire product or 
solution area.  The achievement of specializations gives insurers more clarity and insight into which 
partners have proven capabilities in a region. Additionally, partners with specializations can better 
promote their capabilities across Guidewire products and solutions.   
“We congratulate Hexaware on achieving the  Migration Acceleration specialization. We are pleased 
to recognize their expertise,  proven capabilities, and commitment to drive down customers’ costs 
and implementation timeframes, and we look forward to continuing our work together to contribute 
to the success of our shared  customers,” said Molly Black, Senior Director, Partner Programs 
and Enablement, Alliances, Guidewire Software.   
“We are delighted to achieve the  Migration Acceleration specialization. This recognition highlights 
our dedication to leveraging Guidewire's platform to deliver seamless and efficient migration solutions for our customers. Our continued investment in building accelerators ensures certaint y of 
outcomes for customers looking to migrate to Guidewire Cloud. We greatly value our partnership with Guidewire and are excited to continue driving success for our shared clients,” said Shreyas 
Vasanth Kumar, Senior Vice President, Hexaware Technologies .  
Please find more information about Guidewire specializations on the Guidewire website .
