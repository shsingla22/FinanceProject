Honasa Consumer Limited Elevates Vipul Maheshwari to Chief Product & Technology Officer 
Gurugram, October 23, 2026 : Honasa Consumer Limited, one of India’s leading beauty and personal care 
companies, today announced the elevation of Vipul Maheshwari to Chief Product & Technology Officer 
(CPTO). He will lead the company’s product, technology, engineering and analytics functions, with a 
mandate to advance its AI-led transformation and agentic AI vision. 
Over the past six years, Vipul has played a pivotal role in building Honasa’s data and product capabilities, 
bringing together business understanding, data intelligence and technology to solve complex challenges. 
This intersection of data and product expertise has been a key strength in shaping Honasa’s AI and 
agentic vision, helping identify opportunities to embed intelligence into business processes and build 
solutions that go beyond conventional automation. In his new role, he will drive the evolution of these 
capabilities into scalable, AI-powered systems that enable smarter decision-making, greater operational 
efficiency and new ways of working. 
As Honasa scales its diverse portfolio of beauty and personal care brands, AI is emerging as a strategic 
enabler of business transformation. The company is building AI-powered tools and intelligent workflows 
to simplify complex processes, automate tasks and enable faster decision-making, while exploring 
agentic AI systems that can execute multi-step workflows and coordinate tasks with appropriate human 
oversight. 
Vipul’s combined experience in data and product will be instrumental in translating this vision into 
scalable business solutions that enhance productivity, strengthen decision-making and enable more 
intelligent ways of working across the organisation. 
Commenting on the appointment,  Varun Alagh, Co-founder and CEO of Honasa Consumer Limited , said, 
“Technology has always been an important enabler of our growth, and AI presents an opportunity to  

fundamentally rethink how we build and operate our business. As we move towards a more intelligent 
and connected organisation, our ambition is to go beyond isolated use cases and build AI-powered 
workflows and agentic capabilities that help our teams operate with greater speed, precision and 
effectiveness. Vipul’s strong foundation in data and product, along with his deep understanding of our 
business, makes him well positioned to shape and lead this journey. His elevation reflects our 
commitment to building leadership from within and investing in capabilities that will define our next 
phase of growth.” 
Speaking on his new role, Vipul Maheshwari, Chief Product & Technology Officer, Honasa Consumer 
Limited, said, “AI and agentic systems represent a fundamental shift in how businesses will operate, 
moving from tools that provide information to intelligent systems that can execute workflows, connect 
decisions and drive outcomes. At Honasa, our strong foundation in data and product gives us the 
opportunity to build an AI-first approach to solving business problems. We are focused on developing 
intelligent workflows, enabling AI agents to support multi-step processes with the right guardrails, and 
embedding intelligence into everyday operations. My priority will be to translate this vision into scalable 
capabilities that deliver measurable business impact, strengthen organisational agility and create lasting 
value.” 
Vipul holds a master’s degree in Mathematics and Computer Science from the Indian Institute of 
Technology Delhi. Prior to joining Honasa, he held key positions at Delhivery and Global Analytics India 
Pvt. Ltd., where he worked on data-driven business solutions and analytics-led problem-solving.
