FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE  
Emmvee Photovoltaic Power Limited Reports Stellar Q2 & H1 FY26 Results; H1 
Revenue Surges 193% YoY to ₹2,159 Crore  
• Robust Financial Performance:  Consolidated Revenue from Operations for H1 
FY26 grew 193% YoY to ₹2,159 Crore; H1 PAT jumped nearly 7x to ₹426 Crore.  
• Operational Scale:  Photovoltaic (PV) Module segment continues to drive growth 
with substantial order execution.  
• Market Debut:  Successfully completed IPO raising ₹2,900 Crore; listed on BSE and 
NSE on November 18, 2025.  
BENGALURU, India – December 1, 2025  – Emmvee Photovoltaic Power Limited  (NSE: 
EMMVEE; BSE: 544608), a leading Indian renewable energy company and reliable partner 
for solar energy since 2007 , today announced its un -audited consolidated financial results for 
the second quarter (Q2 FY26) and half -year (H1 FY26) ended September 30, 2025.  
The Board of Directors approved the results at its meeting held today in Bengaluru. This marks 
the Company’s first earnings announcement following its successful listing on the stock 
exchanges.

About Emmvee Photovoltaic Power Limited  
Emmvee Photovoltaic Power Limited is a leading renewable energy company based in 
Bengaluru, India. Incorporated in 2007 , Emmvee has evolved into a large -scale manufacturer 
of Solar PV modules and systems. The Company operates state -of-the-art manufacturing 
facilities with a focus on high -efficiency technologies. Emmvee is committed to providing 
reliable, high -quality solar  energy solutions to customers worldwide, contributing to a 
sustainable and green future.
