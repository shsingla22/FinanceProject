Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulation, 
2015, as amended, (“ SEBI LODR Regulations ”), we wish to inform you that the United States 
Department of Commerce, International Trade Administr ation (“ US DoC ”), in the course of its 
administrative review of the countervailing duty (“ CVD ”) order on organic soybean meal from India for 
the period from January  1, 2023 to December 31, 2023 (“ Administrative Review ”), has applied the 
‘adverse facts avai lable’ (“ AFA”) methodology to Ecopure Specialities Limited (“ Ecopure ”), a step -down 
subsidiary of the Company. As a result, the highest possible CVD rate of 340.27% has been imposed 
on Ecopure’s exports of organic soybean meal to the United States. . 
 
Ecopure is presently evaluating all available legal remedies in response to the aforementioned 
imposition, in accordance with applicable law. As of the date of this communication, the impact of the 
Administrative Review on the Company has not yet been determined . The Company will continue to 
keep you informed of any further developments in this matter.  
 
The relevant details, as per the requirement of Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR Regulations, read with 
SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD -PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023  and SEBI  Circular 
SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD -PoD-2/CIR/P/2024/185 dated December 31, 2024, are given in the enclosed 
‘Annexure - A’. 
 
We request you to take the same on record.  
 
Thanking you.
