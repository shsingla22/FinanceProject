Thyrocare Reports Strong  Q2FY27 Results – Revenue Up 25.1%, 
PAT before Exceptional I tems Up 25.7% 
The Company delivered a robust performance in Q2FY27, with consolidated revenue of ₹270.8 crore , 
up 25.1% year -on-year, led by continued momentum in the Pathology segment, which grew 26.7% 
YoY. EBITDA rose  24.9% YoY to ₹89.3 crore . PAT before  exceptional items  surged 25.7% YoY  to 
₹60.2 crore , underscoring the Company ’s focus on profitable growth.  
Exceptional items comprise a one-time, non -cash impairment charge of ₹45 crore  relating to NHL , 
recognised in the consolidated financial statements  in connection with the definitive term sheet for the 
proposed sale of the NHL business. This charge has no impact on the Company’s operational 
performance . Thyrocare has issued a postal ballot seeking shareholders’ approval for the proposed 
divestment of its Nuclear Imaging business, enabling the Company to sharpen its focus and allocate 
capital towards its Pathology  business, which offers a stronger growth trajectory, higher margins and 
better return on capital employed (ROCE).  
Thyrocare, India’s largest diagnostic test volume processor, further strengthened its leadership 
position during the quarter, processing 61.6 million tests,  up 27.4% year -on-year. The Pathology 
business maintained strong traction , with franchise revenue up 26.9% and partnership revenue up 
27.9% year-on-year.  
Thyrocare’s  commitment to quality continued to deliver  Six Sigma -level performance, with  complaints 
reduced to 2.9 per million  (down 24% YoY)  and an average turnaround time ( ATAT ) of 3.49 hours  
from sample receipt.  
Thyrocare continued its expansion into specialty diagnostics , marking the next phase of its growth 
journey. Having commenced with allergy and genomics, the Company is progressively expanding  its 
specialty test portfolio, with a focus on building clinical trust through scientific rigour, uncompromising 
quality and industry -leading turnaround times.  
Thyrocare also launched its  Pregnancy Test Kit , a rapid home -testing solution that delivers results in 
just five minutes  and is the first kit in India backed by a confirmatory lab test . The Company has  
onboarded actress Yami Gautam  as brand ambassador to strengthen awareness and consumer 
outreach for this product. We have launched this with the help of our API Holdings group company 
Ascent and are already present in more than 10,000 chemists .

About Thyrocare  
Thyrocare Technologies Limited (NSE: THYROCARE, BSE: 539871) is India’s  first fully automated 
diagnostic laboratory chain, with a strong pan -India presence . Thyrocare focuses on providing quality 
and affordable diagnostic services to patients, healthtech and wellness providers, insurance 
companies, pharmaceutical companies, franchisees, laboratories , hospitals  and the government . Many 
leading healthcare product and services brands all over India use the comprehensive test profile menu 
offered by Thyrocare.  
Thyrocare has an extensive network of lab oratories  across India , with  12,911 active franchisees during  
the quarter . In Q2FY27 , it processed 61.6 million  diagnostic and screening investigations.  
Thyrocare is one of the first Indian diagnostic laboratories to obtain internationally renowned quality 
accreditations including ISO 9001 :2000 certification  as early as 2001  (since  upgraded to ISO 
9001:2015 ), as well as  National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories (NABL) 
and College of American Pathologists (CAP) accreditations in 2005.
