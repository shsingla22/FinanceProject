Update  
 
Park Group announces strategic divestment  
 
Park Medi World  Limited  (NSE:  PARKHOSPS , BSE : 544645 ), North India’s 2nd largest chain 
of hospitals  hereby informs its investors and stakeholders that, as part of its ongoing 
restructuring and strategic realignment initiatives, its wholly owned subsidiary, Aggarwal 
Hospital and Research Services Private Limited, has approved the divestment of its entire 55% 
shareholding in Devina Derma Private Limited, a subsidiary of Aggarwal Hospital and 
Research Services Private Limited , amounting INR 0. 06 crore . 
Upon successful completion of the transaction, Devina Derma Private Limited will cease to be 
a subsidiary of Aggarwal Hospital and Research Services Private Limited, and consequently, 
of the Company. This divestment has been undertaken following the failur e of the sellers to 
fulfil certain pre -requisite conditions stipulated under the transactional documents — non-
compliance with which had the potential to adversely impact the operational efficiency and 
financial flexibility of the Group.  
The Board and Management are of the considered view that this divestment represents a 
prudent and value -accretive decision, enabling the Group to streamline its corporate structure, 
eliminate operational uncertainties, and redeploy its focus and resources towards core 
strategic priorities.  
Park Group of Hospitals remains steadfast in its commitment to building a robust and resilient 
healthcare delivery network. The Group  will continue to evaluate and execute prudent 
strategic initiatives and restructuring measures aimed at enhancing long -term stakeholder 
value, strengthening its competitive positioning, and sustaining its growth momentum across 
its operational footprint.
