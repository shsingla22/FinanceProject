Karur Vysya Bank inaugurate s 4 new branches  today    
The Bank reaches a milestone by inaugurating  its 888th branch.   
Chennai , 28th March 2025 : Karur Vysya Bank (KVB) inaugurated 4 new branches  - 3 in Tamil 
Nadu, 1 in Andhra Pradesh today. By inaugurating these branches, the total branch network 
of the Bank increased to 888. 
Mr. Shailesh Kumar Yadav, IPS, Chairman & Managing Director, Tamil Nadu Police Housing 
Corporation inaugurated Karur Vysya Bank’s 888th branch in Chennai today. This branch is 
located  at No: 337/1B2C, Alapakkam Main Road, Alapakkam, Chennai - 600 095.   
During th e financial year 2024 -25, the bank added 46 branches across India.  
The new branches that were opened  today : 
1. 885th Branch  - Kumbakonam East - inaugurated by Dr. Prabhakar, Chief Civil Surgeon, 
Govt. District Head Quarters Hospital, Kumbakonam  
2. 886th Branch  - Visakhapatnam (Poorna Market ) - inaugurated by  Dr. Sandhya Devi, 
Principal, Andhra Medical College , Visakhapatnam   
3. 887th Branch - Kannampalayam  (Coimbatore ) - inaugurated  by Mr. Sathish Kumar, 
Executive Officer, Kannampalayam Town Panchayat  
4. 888th Branch  - Alapakkam (Chennai ) - inaugurated by  Mr. Shailesh Kumar Yadav, IPS, 
Chairman & Managing Director, Tamil Nadu Police Housing Corporation , Chennai   
 
The new branches shall cater to all basic banking transactions and also specific needs of 
customers, offering the entire spectrum of banking products and financial services covering 
Retail, Institutional and Consumer lending.  
KVB offers Internet Banking and Mobile Banking facilities.  KVB DLite, the Mobile Banking app 
of the Bank offers convenience of financial and non -financial services through 150+ features.  
The app has been recently upgraded with several customer friendly features.   
About Karur Vysya Bank:   
Karur Vysya Bank has touch points in the form of 8 88 branches and 2200+ ATMs & cash 
recyclers. The Bank continues to grow with its strong financials.  The bank’s total business was 
Rs. 1,81,993 crores as on 3 1.12.2024 with a deposit base of Rs. 99,155 cr. and advances at Rs. 
82,838 cr. The bank had posted its highest ever net profit of Rs. 1,605 cr. during the last 
financial year. The Net Profit for the nine months of the current fiscal was Rs. 1,428  crores.  
Net NPA of the Bank was 0.2 0%.
