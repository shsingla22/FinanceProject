PRESS RELEASE  
Arvind sustain ed growth through strategic agility and operational resilience  
Highlights for Q 2 FY26 (Comparison on a YoY basis ): 
• Highest ever quarterly revenue of ₹2,371 Cr, a growth of 8.4% , aided by higher volume  
• Reported EBITDA of ₹262 Cr, a growth of 13%  & EBITDA margin crossed  11%  
• Garmenting division delivered 17% growth & volume crosse d 10.7 Mn pieces  
 
Ahmedabad, 07th Nov 2025: Arvind Ltd, India's leading textile and apparel compan y, today announced its 
financial results for the second  quarter and half year ended 30 September 2025 . The result reflects  strong trends 
in both volumes and revenues  with stable margins , underscoring steady demand and improved operational 
efficiency across segments, while absorbing  the heightened global uncertainty and tariff -related headwinds.

Q2 FY26  was an eventful quarter  for the company . During the first half of the year, the global economy continued 
to grapple with multiple trade disruptions and geopolitical conflicts. Amid this challenging environment, Arvind 
demonstrated resilience and agility, supported by its diversified product por tfolio, balanced supply chain , and 
prudent risk -reward approach.  
Business highlights for Q 2 FY26 (Comparison on YoY basis)  
The quarter witnessed robust volume growth across core business segments, underscoring strong market 
demand and the company’s strategic  agility . 
• Denim fabric achieved  a volume of 15.2 Mn meters, a growth  of 16% , supported by higher 
verticalization and stable realizations . 
• Woven fabric registered a volume of 35.1 Mn meters, a growth of 8%  at 100% capacity utilization . 
• Garmenting division delivered a record 10.7 Mn pieces, a n increase  of 17% , driven by higher capacity 
utilization, new customer additions, and enhanced vertical integration.  
Above  said volume growth in our textile business is attributable to our continued efforts to increase wallet share 
with marquee clients, increasing diversification of our  product categ ories —reflecting the success of our 
verticalization strategy  and the customer centric business model where higher volume s were  received as a trade -
off against  tariff absorbed . 
• AMD division  regains momentum supported by improved realizations, favourable product mix and 
registers  strong growth in the Industrial segment and seen resurgence in Pultrusion demand .  
• Protective gear business witnessed resumption of defence orders . 
Arvind has adopted a multi -pronged strategy to navigate the evolving global trade environment —realigning its 
supply chain, expanding into non -US markets, optimizing costs, and maintaining strong customer relationships —
to sustain competitiveness and profitability amid the ongoi ng tariff regime.
