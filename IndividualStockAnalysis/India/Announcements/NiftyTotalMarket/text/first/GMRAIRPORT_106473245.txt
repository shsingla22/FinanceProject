Dear Sir/  Madam,  
 
This is to inform that  the Company has acquired  3,04,15,621 equity shares, 
representing 49.9 0% of the issued and paid -up share capital of its subsidiary 
company i.e . Delhi Duty Free Services Private Limited (“ DDFS ”) from Delhi 
International Airport Limited  (“DIAL”) , another subsidiary of the Company , at 
an aggregate purchase  consideration of Rs. 1,83,22,37,010 . 
  
Following the said acquisit ion, the Company now holds 66.93% of the paid -
up equity share capital of DDFS.  
DDFS  was incorporated on July 07, 2009  under the Companies Act, 1956 and 
was primarily engaged in develop ment, operation , manage ment and 
maintain ing the Duty -Free Operations at the Indira Gandhi International 
Airport, New Delhi . Revenue from operations of DDFS  for FY 2025, 2024 and 
2023 was Rs. 2,177.32 Crore, Rs. 1,940.98 Crore and Rs. 1,541.83 Crore 
respectively.    
Since the Company was appointed as  the new concessionaire for the Delhi 
Duty Free business  and since the Company would be exploring duty free 
business opportunities in India and abroad , it would be in the best interest of 
the Company to acquire DIAL’s stake in DDFS.   
 
The said acquisition of shares by the Company being a related party 
transaction is done on an arm ’s length basis and was duly approved by the 
relevant authorities .  
 
  
 
  
Promoter/promoter group /group companies  of the Company do not hold any 
interest in the said trans action  other than any direct/indirect shareholding . 
 
There were no governmental or regulatory approvals required for the above 
acquisition.
