Ref.: Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements) Regulations

Please refer to the outcome of the Board meeting intimation dated July 08, 2024, informing the 
approval granted by the Board of Directors of VA Tech Wabag Limited ("the Company") for sale 
of 100% stake in Wabag Water Services S.R.L., Romania ("Wabag Romania") which was a wholly-
owned Subsidiary of VA Tech Wabag GmbH, Austria ("Wabag Austria"), a step down Subsidiary 
of the Company. 
In this regard, we hereby inform that the Company has received confirmation from Wabag 
Austria on August 05, 2024 regarding completion of the share transfer process and other related 
formalities between Wabag Austria ("the Seller") and Circular Waters Solutions S.R.L., Romania 
("the Buyer") post satisfaction of Conditions Precedents, as per the agreement. 
By virtue of this transaction, Wabag Austria realized a Sale Consideration of EUR 1.20 Million 
towards transfer of 100% of its shareholding in Wabag Romania to the Buyer. This is in addition 
to the accumulated Net Worth of Wabag Romania as on June 30 2024 (date of deconsolidation of the 
step down subsidiary), which was realized through Dividends to the tune of EUR 8.46 Million 
declared by Wabag Romania to Wabag Austria. 
Wabag Romania shall consequently cease to be a step down Subsidiary of the Company. 
The details required to be disclosed pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR read with SEBI 
circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/ P/CIR/ 2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 were already 
provided in our earlier intimation dated July 08, 2024.
