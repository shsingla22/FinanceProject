Sub.: Business Update for the Quarter ended 30th September 2026 
 
Dear Sir / Ma’am, 
 
The Company is pleased to submit its business update for the quarter ended 30th September 2026. 
 
Consolidated Revenue growth of approximately 28% YoY – 
 
Backed with a healthy consumer demand, the company continued to deliver a strong performance, concluding Q2FY2027 
with a consolidated revenue growth of approximately 28% YoY reflecting a strong business momentum. 
 
Realization of outstanding export receivables – 
 
The company has received remittances of approximately Rs 142 crores during the quarter from its outstanding export debtors. 
The company had been putting in its best efforts for amicably resolving the issue of its outstanding export receivables. The 
efforts and negotiations have yielded positive results for the company. The issue between the company and its export debtors 
have been resolved and the realization of outstanding export receivables has commenced with this remittance. 
 
Debt-Free status achieved –  
 
During the quarter ended 30th September 2026, the company achieved a Debt Free status after successful repayment of 
outstanding debt of all the banks with all repayments completed ahead of the scheduled due dates. With no bank debts 
outstanding, the company will be saving on the interest cost in the future, which will positively impact its bottomline and 
strengthen its balance sheet. 
 
Completion of fund raising through Preferential issue of Equity Shares and Warrants – 
 
The Company’s funds raising aggregating upto ₹ 500 crore by preferential issue of equity shares and Warrants has been 
completed successfully on 24th September, 2026 upon completion of conversion of all the Warrants allotted to Shri Balram 
Garg, Promoter.  
 
The financial details provided above are provisional and subject to Limited Review by Statutory Auditor.
