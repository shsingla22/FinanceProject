` Dear Shareholders,  
“Affle continued its established growth trajectory in H1 FY2027. We achieved our 15th consecutive quarter of 
sequential topline growth, while registering our highest -ever quarterly revenue run -rate, EBITDA, PAT and 
consumer conversions. This performance reflects the strength of our differentiated CPCU business Model in 
sustaining ROI for advertisers globally, despite prevailing geopolitical tensions, and regulatory and 
macroeconomic headwinds.  
As we progress towards our vision of 10X, we continue to advance on our ambition of building a globally scaled, 
AI-powered full funnel Consumer Platform Stack that delivers measurable value for advertisers through premium 
consumer conversions. Our focus remains on strengthening our competitive differentiation, deepening our 
presence across Developed and Emerging Markets, and expanding the capabilities of our platform across mobile, 
Connected TV and other Agentic/Autonomous Intelligent Connected Devices.”  
Anuj Khanna Sohum,  
Chairperson, MD and CEO  
 
 
In the letter  below,  we address  the key questions  that we think  investors  might  have.  
 
Q1. How did Affle  perform  during  Q2 and H1 FY2027?  
Our performance in H1 FY2027 underscores the continued strength and scalability of Affle’s business model , with 
performance  for the first half surpassing H1  of the previous  financial  year.  Q2 FY2027  marked  our 15th 
consecutive  quarter  of sequential  topline  growth, with the business delivering its highest -ever quarterly 
revenue, EBITDA, profit after tax (PAT) and consumer conversions.  
During  Q2 FY2027,  on a consolidated  basis,  revenue  stood  at INR 7.84 billion,  growing  by 21.2%  year-on-year and 
4.9% sequentially; EBITDA  grew by 24.2% year -on-year to INR 1.81 billion, with EBITDA  margin expanding by 56 
basis points  year-on-year to 23.2%.  Profit  before  tax from operations,  excluding  other  income,  stood  at INR 1.40 
billion,  representing  growth  of 23.3%  year-on-year,  while  PAT stood  at INR 1.30 billion,  growing  by 17.4% 
year-on-year with 16.2% PAT Margin.  
For H1 FY2027,  on a consolidated  basis,  revenue  stood  at INR 15.31  billion,  representing  growth  of 20.8% 
year-on-year;  EBITDA  stood  at INR 3.49 billion,  registering  a growth  of 22.1%  year-on-year,  while  PAT stood  at INR 
2.58 billion, growing by 19.5% year -on-year. This performance reflects the continued scale -up of our organic  
business across markets and our consistent execution against the growth opportunities ahead.  
 
Q2. What  drove  growth  in our CPCU  business?  
Affle’s differentiated Cost Per Converted User (“CPCU”) business model continued to deliver healthy growth 
during Q2 FY2027, with consumer conversions, on a consolidated basis, increasing by approximately 18.2%  year-
on-year to 128.93 million, while CPCU revenue grew by approximately 23.7% to INR 7.8 2 billion.
