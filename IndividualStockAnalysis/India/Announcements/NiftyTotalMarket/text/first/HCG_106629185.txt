HCG Launches Cancer Hospital  in North Bengaluru,  
Introduces Karnataka’s First MR -Linac  
 
India’s largest dedicated cancer hospital network strengthens advanced 
oncology access in North Bengaluru  
 
BENGALURU, May 19, 2026:  HealthCare Global Enterprises Limited (HCG), India’s largest 
dedicated cancer hospital  network,  today announced the launch of its new comprehensive 
cancer hospital in Byatarayanapura (Hebbal) , strengthening access to advanced oncology care 
in North Bengaluru. The new facility marks a major advancement  in the region’s oncology 
landscape, bringing together clinical excellence, compassionate care and advanced 
technology to make world -class cancer treatment more accessible to people acr oss 
Karnataka.  
Marking a significant milestone in precision oncology, the new facility with up to 132 -beds 
introduces Karnataka’s first Elekta Unity MR -Linac , one of the world’s most advanced 
precision radiation therapy platforms. The technology combines high -quality MRI imaging 
with a linear accelerator to enable highly precise, adaptive cancer treatment.  Designed as a 
full-spectrum oncology centre, the hospital brings together diagnosis, treatment, recovery 
support, and patient- centric care under one roof, making world -class cancer care more 
accessible to patients across North Bengaluru.  
Abhinaya Chakravarthy Baad ashah Kichcha Sudeep, Renowned Actor & Director 
(Sandalwood) , who inaugurated the facility said, "Cancer is a journey that affects not just 
the patient, but the entire family. What makes a real difference is timely diagnosis, awareness, 
and access to the right treatment at the right time. HCG’s new Hebbal facility is a powerful 
step in that direction, bringing advanced technology and expert care closer to people. It is heartening to see such world- class cancer care becoming more ac cessible for families in 
Bengaluru and across Karnataka.”  
Speaking on the launch, Dr. B. S. Ajaikumar, Founder & Chairman of HealthCare Global 
Enterprises Limited (HCG), said:  “Cancer care today has become increasingly data- driven, 
technology -oriented and focused on improving long -term outcomes. Our goal is not only to 
prevent recurrences, but also to make even advanced and metastatic cancers manageable through personalised, mult idisciplinary care.  
We are very happy to launch our new centre at Hebbal, North Bengaluru, bringing state -of-
the-art infrastructure and Karnataka’s first MR -Linac technology to the region. This marks an 
 
 
important step forward in precision -led cancer treatment and reinforces HCG’s commitment 
to making advanced oncology care more accessible.  
 
As a founder, I have always believed in providing the right treatment to the patient the first 
time, because that is when we achieve the best outcomes. The launch of our Hebbal centre 
is another important milestone in HCG’s pursuit of excellence in cancer care. ” 
 
At the heart of the facility is the Elekta Unity MR -Linac , a breakthrough radiation oncology 
platform being introduced for the first time in Karnataka . This advanced technology 
integrates high -quality MRI imaging with a linear accelerator, enabling real -time visualisation 
of the tumour during treatment. The result is highly precise, adaptive radiation therapy that 
allows clinicians to treat cancer with exceptional accuracy while helping protect surrounding 
healthy tissue.  
Dr. Manish Mattoo, Executive Director & CEO, HealthCare Global Enterprises Limited (HCG), said,  “HCG Hebbal brings together everything we stand for, people -centric care, 
multidisciplinary expertise, preventive focus and advanced technology. With MR -Linac, we are 
introducing a major upgrade to our oncology services and making technology -first cancer c are 
more accessible, especially for people  of Karnataka. HCG Hebbal reflects our 35 -year legacy 
of ‘adding life to years’, bringing together clinical excellence , compassionate care, and cutting-
edge technology to improve cancer outcomes .” 
The hospital  is equipped with Accuray TomoTherapy with Radixact , further strengthening its 
radiation oncology capabilities, along with an 80-slice Digital PET -CT scanner  for precise, high -
resolution diagnostic imaging. In addition to advanced imaging and radiation therapy, HCG Hebbal features five robotic -assisted modular operation theatres , 19 chemotherapy 
daycare beds , 14 premium treatment bays , and a 15- bed ICU and HDU . The facility is also 
supported by a comprehensive range of diagnostic and clinical services, including endoscopy, 
bronchoscopy, ultrasound, X -ray, mammography, advanced laboratories, histopathology, 
frozen section services and blood storage . 
“The launch of our Hebbal cancer hospital  marks an important milestone in HCG’s continued 
commitment to expanding access to advanced cancer care in Bengaluru. This new facility 
brings together our legacy of clinical excellence, compassionate care, and cutting- edge 
technology in a thoughtfully designed environment. Said Ms. Manisha Kumar , COO, 
Karnataka, HCG Cancer Hospital  
“The launch of HCG Hebbal marks a significant expansion of our Bengaluru footprint, s
trengthening our network in the city while extending advanced oncology services to emerging 
neighbourhoods.  The hospital has been thoughtfully designed to move away from a 
conventional clinical environment and instead create spaces that inspire comfort, confidence 
and hope . With the introduction of first -in-Karnataka innovations like the MR -Linac, we are 
 
 
strengthening our ability to deliver precise, personali sed treatment . “ Said Sachin  S.E. , COO, 
HCG Cancer Hospital Hebbal.  
Designed with patient comfort and convenience in mind, the centre also offers an 
international lounge, cafeteria and valet parking , reinforcing HCG’s commitment to creating 
a healing environment that balances advanced care with thoughtful hospitality.  The total built 
up area of this facility is 1,75,086  Sqft. The new facility is structured to function as a 
comprehensive cancer management hub, supported by HCG’s multidisciplinary care model 
and expert- led treatment planning. With specialists across medical oncology, surgical 
oncology and radiation oncology, patients will benefit from personalised protocols reviewed collaboratively by experts across disciplines.
