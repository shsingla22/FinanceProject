Media Release  
 
CMS Info Systems announces leadership update  
Anush Raghavan appointed as  Chief Business Officer  
 
 
Mumbai, 7th July 2025: CMS Info Systems Limited, India’s leading business services company , today 
announced that Anush Raghavan ’s role will expand from President of Cash Logistics business to Chief 
Business Officer of the company.  
 
In this expanded role, Anush will drive  sales across all business lines, aligning with CMS’s integrated 
growth strategy.  
 
Anush has held  key leadership  roles at CMS  over the last 16 years and has been instrumental in 
growing the cash logistics business to a strong market leadership position. He also serves as the 
President  - Cash Logistics Association and the Currency  Cycle Association.  These industry bodies have 
worked closely with RBI and Banks to drive new industry standards and compliance norms.  
 
Rajiv Kaul, Exec Vice Chairman, C EO, and Whole -Time Director, CMS said, “Anush has played a pivotal 
role in CMS ’ transformation and growth journey. His leadership experience in growing our cash 
logistics business , increasing our market positioning , leading our industry engagement  will be strong 
foundation s for his new role and driving growth with customer centricity for our Cash Logistics as 
well as Managed Services business .” 
 
Anush  holds a post -graduate diploma in management from the IIM, Ahmedabad.  
 
About CMS Info Systems Limited (CMSINFO):  
 
CMS Info Systems Limited (BSE: CMSINFO | 543441, NSE: CMSINFO) is India’s leading business services company providing logistics and 
technology solutions to banks, financial institutions, organized retail, and e -commerce companies with a presence across Cash 
Logistics, Managed Services, and Techno logy Solutions.  
CMSINFO businesses include ATM and Retail Cash Management, Banking Automation, ATM -as-a-service, AIoT Remote Monitoring, 
Software Solutions and Card Issuance, Management & Personalization.  
CMSINFO is committed to enable financial inclusion by providing access to formal banking services and driving seamless physical 
payments in India .
