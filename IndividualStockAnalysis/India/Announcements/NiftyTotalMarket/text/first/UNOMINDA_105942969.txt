Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. The auditors have expressed an unmodified 
conclusion on above results.

April 20, 2024. The Company is yet to receive the approval of NCL T on the Scheme, accordingly, the accounting treatment of 
aforesaid amalgamation will be implemented upon receipt of requisite approval of the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal, 
New Delhi.
