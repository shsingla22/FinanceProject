Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Receipt of Notice of Strike  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, we inform you  that a strike 
notice has been issued by the Workers’ Union to take effect on or after September 3, 2025, 
demanding reinstatement of four dismissed workmen and a few who are under suspension.  
 
The dismissals were carried out after the charges of serious misconduct were proved through 
extensive enquiry processes and those under suspension are undergoing lawful enquiry 
processes for serious misconduct.  As such, the call for a strike is considered inappropriate. The 
Company is in the process of applying for an injunction order in this regard  and is taking all 
necessary measures to resolv e the matter  and ensure uninterrupted operations. Further updates 
will be provided in due course.  
 
Impact on business is insignificant as the strike is only by a section of workers which represent 
approx. 9.8% of the Company’s total workforce including contract workmen . 
 
The details required to be furnished pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with 
the SEBI SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD PoD - 1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 are enclosed as 
Annexure -A.  
Kindly take the above on record.
