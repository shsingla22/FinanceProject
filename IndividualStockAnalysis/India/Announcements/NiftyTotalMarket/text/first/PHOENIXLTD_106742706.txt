Dear Member,  
We are pleased to inform you that the Board of Directors at its Meeting held on April 27, 202 6, 
have recommended a Final Dividend of Rs. 2.50 per equity share (125%) of face value of Rs.2/ - 
each for the ﬁnancial year ended March 31, 202 6. The  dividend, as recommended by the 
Board, if declared at the ensuing 12 1st Annual General Meeting (“AGM”), will be paid to 
eligible shareholders within 30 days of the date of its declaration.  
Pursuant to the provisions of Income Tax Act, 2025 (“IT Act, 2025”), dividend income is taxable 
in the hands of shareholders and the company is required to deduct tax at source (TDS) at the rates applicable prescribed in the IT Act, 2025 or Tax treaty at t he time of making payment of 
the dividend.
