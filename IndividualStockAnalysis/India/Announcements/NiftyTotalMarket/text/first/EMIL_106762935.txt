Pursuant to Regulation 30 (4) of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, this is to inform you 
that Electronics Mart India Limited has decided to close the operations of one of its Multi-
Brand Outlet (MBO) operating under the brand name “Audio & Beyond” situated at Unit No. 
TF 313, Third Floor, Nexus Mall, Hyderabad, effective from 31st August 2026. 
 
The decision to close the operations of the aforesaid MBO has been taken after evaluating the 
business performance and in line with the Company's strategic business plans. This closure is 
part of the Company’s ongoing efforts to optimize its retail network and strengthen and expand 
its presence through strategically located and better performing Multi-Brand Outlets (MBOs).  
 
We would also like to assure all stakeholders that this transition will not adversely affect our 
employees. All the employees from these outlets will be redeployed to nearby outlets, ensuring 
continuity of employment and uninterrupted customer service. 
 
We assure you that the Company is committed to supporting its employees and customers 
through this transition. 
 
We request that you kindly take the above information on record.
