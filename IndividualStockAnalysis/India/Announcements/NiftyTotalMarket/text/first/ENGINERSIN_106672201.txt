HCL and Ell Sign Memorandum of Agreement 
New Delhi June 24, 2026: Building on their long-standing partnership, Hindustan Copper Limited 
(HCl) and Engineers India Limited (Ell) signed a Memorandum of Agreement (MeA) on June 22, 
2026. To support its expansive, vertically integrated operations-spanning from mineral 
exploration and end-to-end mining to smelting, refining, and downstream extraction-HCl will 
engage Ell under this agreement for consultancy, engineering, project management, safety and 
integrity studies, sustainability, and advanced technical solutions focused on copper, critical 
minerals, and rare earth elements .. 
The MoAwas formalized at Ell's Corporate Office in New Delhi in the presence of HCl Chairman 
and Managing Director Shri Sanjiv Kumar Singh, alongside Ell's management and senior officials 
from both organizations.
