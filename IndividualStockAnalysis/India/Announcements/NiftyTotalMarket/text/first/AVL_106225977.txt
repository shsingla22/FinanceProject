Brief Profile: - 
 
Ravinder Zutshi -  
 
Mr. Ravinder Zutshi has an illustrious experience of sale/marketing/operations of over 
forty  five years in the Indian consumer electronics and consumer durables industry. He is a 
respected consumer durable veteran and has worked with Samsung India for 19 years  as 
Deputy Managing Director where he played a vital role in setting up the brand in India. He 
has also worked with LG Electronics India Pvt. Ltd. as Sr. Director - India Head for Enterprise 
Business till 2020 and his last assignment was with Havells India Limited as President - 
Business Development and Corporate Affairs from 2021 -2023.  
 
He has served as the President of the Consumer Electronics and Appliance Manufacturers 
Association (CEAMA) from 2007 -2009. Additionally, he held various other significant 
positions such as  Chairman of the ICT Committee at FICCI (2014) and Vice Chairman Sports 
Committee ASSOCHAAM (2014).  
 
Vikash Kumar - 
 
Mr. Vikash Kumar is a seasoned finance professional and qualified Chartered Accountant, 
complemented by management certifications, with over 15 years of multi -industry 
experience in steering finance teams and driving financial efficiencies. He brings expertise  
in financial stewardship, strategic planning, and decision support at the executive level, 
making him a key contributor to organizational growth and value creation.  Throughout his 
career, Vikash has demonstrated exceptional proficiency in financial re porting and analysis, 
risk-based audits, internal controls, compliance and business valuations.  
 
His professional journey includes key roles at reputed listed and unlisted companies, where 
he has led finance transformations and advised on complex financial and operational 
matters. In addition, he has also rendered consulting experience across diverse sectors and 
companies which further amplifies his ability to navigate dynamic business landscapes and 
deliver sustainable financial outcomes.
