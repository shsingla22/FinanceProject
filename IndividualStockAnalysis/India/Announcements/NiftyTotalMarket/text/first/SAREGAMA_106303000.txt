Saregama Acquires the biggest and most popular Haryanvi Music Catalogue  
6,500+ tracks across Punjabi, Haryanvi, Ghazals, Devotional, Indie Pop & more  
 
Mumbai, 17/07/2025:  Saregama, India’s leading Entertainment IP company, has entered into a deal with 
NAV Records Pvt. Ltd., the lead player in the Haryanvi music space. This significant move will see Saregama 
acquiring NAV’s 6,500 tracks across Haryanvi, Punjabi, Ghazals, De votional and Indie Pop. The acquisition 
also includes  super popular Youtube channels like NAV Haryanvi, Nupur Audio etc having over 24M 
subscriber base, further boosting Saregama’s digital footprint. Some of the biggest songs that are part of this 
catalogu e are : 
• Coco cola https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9UaG7UL5yS4&pp=0gcJCc0JAYcqIYzv   with 900+ 
million views  
• Parvati Boli Shakar Se https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DkNCgUXbig  with 500+ million 
views  
• Loot Liya https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AMQIiEea12A  with 400+ million views  
• Dabya Ni Karde https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3oOGF8P1BJo  with 140+ million views  
• Gunehgar   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_zXf6bhgTo  with 140+ million views  
This cements Saregama’s No.1 position in the only language where the Company was not dominant.  Going 
forward, Saregama and NAV will jointly work towards new Haryanvi and Punjabi content creation.  
Saregama is committed to building IP across genres and languages for sustained growth and value creation.
