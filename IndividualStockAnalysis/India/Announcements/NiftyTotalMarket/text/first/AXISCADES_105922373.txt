AXISCADES’ Subsidiary Mistral Solutions Wins Defennovation Award  
by ELCINA for Excellence in Advanced R&D  
 
Bengaluru, 23 July 2024:  Mistral Solutions Pvt. Ltd., an AXISCADES company and a globally recognized 
technology design and systems engineering company, has been awarded the prestigious 
Defennovation Award 2024 , for Excellence in Research and Development (SME) by ELCINA. This 
accolade recognized Mistral's relentless efforts in advancing R&D activities within the defence sector 
and contributing significantly towards self -reliance for India in this critical field.  
 
The ELCINA Defennovation Awards, established in 2018, aims to honour outstanding contributions in 
strategic electronics, including R&D, design, and manufacturing. This year several outstanding 
organizations were nominated by a committee comprising of indus try stalwarts, which then debated 
their merits before reaching a unanimous decision. Mistral was nominated and awarded for its 
contribution to the ARUDHRA RADAR designed by DRDO, a homegrown rotating, active, phased array 
multi -function 4D RADAR that can a utomatically detect and track various aerial targets from slow 
moving objects to combat aircrafts.  
 
Commenting on this achievement, Mr. Arun Krishnamurthi, the CEO and MD of AXISCADES, said 
“We are elated with Mistral's remarkable achievement in winning the  Defennovation Award 2024. 
Leveraging Mistral's advanced capabilities in R&D and integrating them with our strategic vision and 
operational prowess in advanced defence  technology has significantly strengthened our position in the 
defence vertical. Th e award is a nother  testament to our leadership in the defense sector, and the huge 
market potential for u s. Our integrated efforts will continue to propel India's self -reliance in defence 
technology forward, ensuring a robust and secure future for the nation."  
 
Mr. Mujahid Alam, the CEO of Mistral Solutions Pvt Ltd, added "The team at Mistral is honoured to 
receive this prestigious recognition. Being bestowed with the Defennovation Award 2024 is a testament 
to our dedication to advancing innovation and excellence in the domestic defence sector. This key 
project involved des ign and development of Radar Processing Systems for the Arudhra Medium 
Powered Radar (MPR). We also recently received a production order worth ₹90 Crore for the delivery of 
these systems. This a ward underscores the significant impact Mistral Solutions has made over the 
years to enhance its R&D capabilities and support self -reliance in defence technology. This 
achievement reflects the persistent efforts and commitment of our team members who opera te with a 
common goal to enhance our defence technology and strengthen it every single day. Together, we will 
continue to strive for even greater success for the country. "  
 
With this accolade, AXISCADES is positioned even stronger to further  enhance  the landscape of India's 
defence technology , through its integrated solutions  in the strategic electronics and defence sectors .

About Mistral:  
Mistral, an AXISCADES Company, is a globally recognized technology design and systems engineering company 
delivering high -performance products and services across various industries. Mistral is focused in three business 
domains: Product Engineering Service s, Aerospace & Defence and Homeland Security. Mistral provides total 
solutions for a given requirement, which may include hardware design, embedded software development, 
systems integration and customized turnkey solutions.  
Mistral’s Aerospace & Defense domain offers end -to-end system engineering of sub -systems that meet the 
complex requirements of Defense Electronics application for Radar, Sonar, Electronic Warfare, Airborne 
Systems, Naval Systems and Airborne Telemetry. As part of its offerings in this domain, Mistral offers complex 
system engineering solutions that include complex hardware design, system software development, Radar & 
Signal Processing Apps, GUI applications, integration of multi -vendor COTS products, system integration, testing 
and validation, deployment and field trails.
