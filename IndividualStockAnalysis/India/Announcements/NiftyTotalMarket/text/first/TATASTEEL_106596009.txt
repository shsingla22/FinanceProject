Sub: Acquisition of 26% equity stake in TP Adarsh Limited   
 
This has reference to the outcome of the Board Meeting  of Tata Steel Limited (‘Company ’) 
disclosed on  July 30 , 202 5 vide letter reference no.  SEC/652/2025 -26. The Board of 
Directors of the Company had approved the infusion of an amount up to ₹6 crore, in one or 
more tranches, in TP Adarsh Limited (‘TPAL ’), wholly owned subsidiary of Tata Power 
Renewable Energy Limited  (‘TPREL ’) and to execute a Share Purchase and Shareholders ’ 
Agreement (‘SPSA ’) between the Company, TPREL  and TPAL, to acquire  (including by way 
of subscription)  26% equity shareholding in TP AL 
 
In this connection, we wish to inform you that the Company has today , i.e. April 21, 2026 , 
executed the Share Subscription and Shareholders ’ Agreement  (‘SSSA ’) with TPREL and 
TPAL and completed the acquisition , by way of subscription,  of 59,00,000 equity shares of 
face value of ₹10/- each of TP AL (26% equity shareholding ) for an aggregate consideration 
of ₹5,90,00,000/-. Post this transaction, TPAL has become an indirect associate company of 
Tata Steel Limited.  
 
This disclosure is being made in compliance with Regulation 30 , 51 and other applicable 
provisions  of the Securi ties and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.
