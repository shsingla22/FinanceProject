Tata Power Renewables Commissions 100.8 MW Wind Project in  
Ausa, Maharashtra 
 
 
 
 
Tata Power Renewables commissions its 100.8 MW Wind Project in  Ausa , Maharashtra,  
supplying renewable power to TPC -D 
 
• Project to generate 308 million units of clean electricity  annually  
• Project expected to reduce approximately 2.52 lakh tonnes of CO ₂ emissions every year  
• First-of-its -kind Monopole Extra High Voltage (EHV ) line installation undertaken by TPREL’s 
Wind Project Execution Division  
 
 
National, November 16, 2026: Tata Power Renewable Energy Limited (TPREL), a leading 
player in India’s renewable energy sector and a subsidiary of The Tata Power Company Limited (‘Tata Power’) has commissioned its 100.8 MW Wind Project in Ausa, Latur, Maharashtra. The project will su pply renewable power to TPC -D (Tata Power Mumbai 
Distribution) and is expected to generate approximately 308 million units (MU) of electricity 
annually, contributing to the supply of clean and reliable power.  
  
The project comprises 28 Horizontal Axis Wind Turbine Generators (WTGs), using the SG 
3.6-145 model, with each turbine designed to support efficient renewable power generation. 
With an annual generation potential of 308 million units, the project is expected to help reduce 
approximately 252,560 tonnes of CO ₂ emissions every year, strengthening the clean energy 
footprint of TPREL and supporting the transition towards a lower -carbon power ecosystem.  
  
One of the significant challenges emerged midway through the project, when the grid 
connectivity plan w as revised and the EHV line configuration was changed from UG cable to 
Monopole. The revised configuration involved substantial design, procurement and execution complexities and marked the first -of-its-kind Monopole installation undertaken by TPREL’s 
Wind  Project Execution Division. Despite the change being introduced during project 

 
execution, the team successfully incorporated the revised configuration, ensuring that the 
overall project schedule remained unaffected.  
  
With the commissioning of the 100.8 M W Ausa Wind Project, TPREL’s total renewable utility 
capacity has reached 12.1 GW. Currently, 7.3  GW of this capacity is operational, comprising 
5.9 GW of solar and 1.4 GW of wind energy. Additionally, the remaining 4.8 GW is under 
various stages of implem entation.  
 
 
About Tata Power:  
The Tata Power Company Limited, a vertically integrated power company and part of the Tata Group , India’s largest 
multinational business conglomerate, has built a diversified portfolio across the entire power value chain, with its total 
operational and under -construction capacity surpassing 27  GW. This includes approximately 18.3 GW of clean and green 
energy capacity (including projects under construction) and around 8.8 GW of thermal generation capacity, ensuring a 
balanced and resilient energy mix. The Company also has a strong transmission and distribution footprint, with over 7,800  
circuit kilometres of transmission lines, including pipeline projects, and serves over 13 million customers across its 
distribution businesses, making it one of India’s largest private power distribution companies.  Further strengthening its 
leadership in future-ready energy solutions, Tata Power has established 4.9 GW of integrated solar cell and module 
manufacturing capacity, signed 2.8 GW of pumped hydro storage projects, and expanded its EV charging network to over 
7,000 public charging points across 793 cities and towns in India.  As a pioneer in I ndia’s clean energy transition, Tata 
Power remains committed to achieving Net Zero by 2045 and continues to partner with public and private stakeholders to deliver reliable, sustainable, and technology -led energy solutions across the country.
