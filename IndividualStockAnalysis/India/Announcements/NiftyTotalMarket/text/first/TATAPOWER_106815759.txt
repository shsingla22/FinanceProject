Tata Power and Imperial College London Collaborate to 
Advance Next-Generation Energy Systems and Accelerate 
India –UK Net- Zero Ambitions  
Partnership will explore research and knowledge exchange across grid 
flexibility, energy storage, digital energy systems, AI and industrial 
decarbonisation  
 
Dr. Praveer Sinha, CEO& M anaging Director , Tata Power (second from left- black suit) and Professor Mary 
Ryan, Vice Provost (Research and Enterprise) at Imperial College, London during the signing of a 
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to explore collaboration in research, technology and knowledge 
exchange across key areas of the energy transition.  
National, 1 3 October 2026:  Tata Power, one of India’s largest integrated power companies  and 
Imperial College London, a world- leading UK university, have signed a Memorandum of Understanding 
(MoU) to explore opportunities for collaboration in research, technology and knowledge exc hange 
across critical areas of the energy transition.  
 
The collaboration will focus on areas including grid flexibility, energy storage, AI and ML applications in energy, and advanced energy systems. The partnership will seek to connect Tata Power’s experience 
of developing and operating energy infrastructure at scale with Imperial’s research expertise across 
digital technologies and decarbonisation.  
 The partnership comes as India and the UK deepen cooperation on clean energy and climate action. Under the India –UK Vision 2035, the two countries have identified energy storage and grid 
transformation as areas for greater cooperation, alongside joint work on AI, renewables, energy storage, 

 
hydrogen and carbon capture. The Vision also highlights the Net Zero Innovation Partnership as a 
vehicle for supporting deep -tech solutions and new markets for climate innovation.  
 
Commenting on the partnership, Business and Trade Secretary, UK Government Jonathan 
Reynolds  said: “When great British research teams up with the ambition and expertise of Indian 
business, there is huge potential to make a real difference.  This partnership is a great example of our 
fantastic new trade deal bringing opportunities to life, and will help turn ideas into cleaner, smarter 
energy solutions – supporting good jobs and growth in both our countries.”  
 
Harjinder Kang, His Majesty’s Trade Commissioner for South Asia and British Deputy High Commissioner for Western India , said:  “The partnership between Imperial College London and Tata 
Power is a great example of the UK and India combining their strengths to turn innovation into impact.  
Bringing together world- class UK research and Indian industrial expertise will help accelerate next -
generation energy solutions, unlock new commercial opportunities and  support sustainable growth in 
both countries.”  
 Dr. Praveer Sinha, CEO & M anaging Director , Tata Power , said: “India’s energy transition is entering 
a phase where scale, flexibility and reliability will be as important as the pace of renewable energy 
growth. The next generation of grids, storage and digital energy systems will need to work together to 
meet a rapidly evolving demand landscape. Our collaboration with Imperial College will help solve real -
world industry challenges with deep research and new thinking and explore solutions that can be relevant not only to India’s energy journey, but also to the wider global transition.”  
 The partnership reflects a shared interest in translating research and innovation into practical 
applications. Tata Power brings extensive experience across power generation, transmission, 
distribution and renewable energy, while Imperial contributes research capabilities spanning energy systems, engineering, digital technologies, economics and decarbonisation.  
 Professor Mary Ryan, Vice Provost ( Research and Enterprise) at Imperial College London, said: 
“As one of the largest energy companies in the world’s most populous country, Tata Power has 
expertise across a range of energy technologies and the scale needed to deliver the cleaner and more 
resilient energy systems we urgently need. This makes the company a fantastic partner for Imperial, 
which brings not only world -leading frontier research capabilities in science, engineering and business, 
but a model of convergence science that looks beyond individual disciplines to tackle challenges too large for standard approaches to innovation.”  
 
By bringing these capabilities together, the partnership aims to support the development and 
demonstration of solutions that can contribute to more flexible, resilient and lower -carbon energy 
systems, while creating opportunities for knowledge exchange and innovation between India and the UK. 
 
About Tata Power  
 
The Tata Power Company Limited, a vertically integrated power company and part of the Tata Group, India’s 
largest multinational business conglomerate, has built a diversified portfolio across the entire power value chain, with its total operational and under -construction capacity surpassing 26 GW. This includes 
approximately 17.7 GW of clean and green energy capacity (including projects under construction) and around 8.8 GW of thermal generation capacity, ensuring a balanced and resilient energy mix. The Company also has a strong transmission and distribution footprint, with over 7,800 circuit kilometres of transmission 
lines, including pipeline projects, and serves over 13 million customers across its distribution businesses, making it one of India’s largest private power distribution companies.  Further strengthening its leadership in 
future- ready energy solutions, Tata Power has established 4.9 GW of integrated solar cell and module 
manufacturing capacity, signed 2.8 GW of pumped hydro storage projects, and expanded its EV chargi ng 
network to over 7,000 public charging points across 717 cities and towns in India.  
As a pioneer in India’s 
clean energy transition, Tata Power remains committed to achieving Net Zero by 2045 and continues 
to partner with public and private stakeholders to deliver reliable, sustainable, and technology -led 
energy solutions across the country.  
 
 
About Imperial College London  
 
Imperial College London is a world- leading university for science, technology, engineering, medicine 
and business (STEMB), where scientific imagination leads to world- changing impact.  
 As a global top- ten university based in London, it uses science to better understand the universe and 
improve the lives of people around the world. Across its 10 campuses and throughout its global netw ork, 
Imperial’s students, staff and partners work together on scientific discovery, innovation and entrepreneurship. Their work addresses some of the world’s toughest challenges in global health, climate change, AI, business leadership and more.  
 Founded in 1907, Imperial builds on a distinguished past, having pioneered advances in penicillin, 
holography and fibre optics. Today, Imperial combines exceptional teaching, world- class facilities and 
an interdisciplinary approach to unlock scientific imagination.
