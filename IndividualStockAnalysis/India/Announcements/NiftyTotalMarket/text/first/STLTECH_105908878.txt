c. brief details of dispute/litigation  Prysmian  Cables and Systems USA, LLC (the 
“Plaintiff”) filed a complaint in the U.S. District 
Court for the District of South Carolina, Columbia 
Division, against Stephen Szymanski, (“Szymanski”), 
an employee of STL’s U.S. subsidiary, Sterlite 
Technologies Inc. (“ STI”), as well as against STI, 
alleging inter alia  that Szymanski violated certain 
non-compete and confidentiality agreements with 
the Plaintiff and subsequently divulged such  
confidential information to STI, which Plaintiff 
further alleges provided STI w ith an unjust 
competitive advantage. Szymanski and STI have 
asserted affirmative and meritorious defenses to 
the allegations.  
On July 5, 2024 and  at the pre -
trial hearing  conference on July  8, 2024,  the Court 
granted in part STI and Szymanski’s Motion for 
Summary Judgment  whereby the Court held 
that as Szymanski's non -compete agreement with 
Plaintiff is not supported by consideration and, 
consequently, is unenforceable  and entirely 
dismissed certain of Plaintiff’s claims and dismissed 
in part portions of t he remaining claims. As to the 
documents at issue which the Plaintiff alleges were 
misappropriated, the Court also reduced the 
number of such documents from over 50 to only 
10. The case is currently scheduled to commence 
trial from July 22, 2024.
