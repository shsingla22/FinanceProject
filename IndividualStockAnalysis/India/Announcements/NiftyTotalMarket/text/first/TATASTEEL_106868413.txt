Tata Steel and Promoters of APL Apollo Enter into a Comprehensive 
Agreement to Build an Indian Steel Tubes and Colour Coated Product s 
Powerhouse Targeting 15.5 Million Tonnes of Capacity  
Mumbai, November 23, 2026  
Strategic Context  
Tata Steel today announced big strides in the Value -Added downstream play in India for Steel Tubes and 
Colour Coated Sheets. The Company has executed agreements for a comprehensive strategic future covering 
the sale of its Steel Tubes and Colour  Coated business to APL Apollo, the acquisition of the entire promoter 
stake of 26. 6% in APL Apollo, an MOU with SG Tech Engineering Private Ltd.  (SG Spectra) , a company 
associated with the Promoter Group of APL Apollo to set up exclusive contract manufacturing facilities of  5 
MTPA  of Colour Coated products on a modular and pan India basis to leverage the growth in the Indian Colour 
Coated products market. APL Apollo is a key Indian Steel Tubes company with 5 MTPA  of existing capacity 
and significant ongoing expansion projects across these businesses.  
Upon the completion of the transaction after regulatory approvals and after the close of the open offer by Tata 
Steel, the consolidated APL Apollo will comprise a 7.5 MTPA Steel Tubes and Colour  Coated capacity with a 
very rich and diversified product mix, market agility, pan -India presence, benchmark production costs and 
efficiency of capital allocation. This sets the basis for both superior profitability and high growth potential.  
The vision for this company is to grow the Steel Tubes business further by 3 MTPA  and to also build Colour 
Coated capacity of 5 MTPA  in the coming few years  to emerge as one of the leading  global Steel Tubes makers 
and a prominent Colour  Coated products company. Tata Steel and APL Apollo will also realise synergies in 
terms of higher overall capacity utilisation  and de -bottlenecking, development of projects, purchase of 
equipment and materials, and distribution channels and capabilities. This will ensure further improvement in 
the already robust EBITDA margins of APL Apollo Tubes. With a mix of conventional and a sset light growth 
and premium product mix, the combined business in APL Apollo will be focused on growth, value addition and 
high Return on Capital Employed. The asset -light growth will be driven through the agreement with SG Tech 
Engineering  which  will build the 5 MTPA  of Colour Coated capacity in the coming few years  on a modular and 
geographically dispersed basis across India, through  an exclusive contract manufacturing  arrangement . The 
first million tonne of capacity is expected to be built by SG Tech Engineering in the next 24 months.  
 
                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
  
 
 
The Transaction framework  
Tata Steel yesterday had executed agreements to sell all of  its shareholding in Tata Steel Colors Pvt. Ltd. a 
wholly owned subsidiary, as well as the whole of Tata Steel’s Steel Tubes business to APL Apollo Tubes Ltd. 
This will consolidate Tata Steel’s surface coated coil capacity of 800 KTPA, as well as its 1.7 MTPA  Steel 
Tubes business in APL Apollo Pvt. Ltd for a combined consideration of Rs.28,000 crores. APL Apollo will 
initially fund the acquisition through a mix of acquisition financing and subsequently replace the debt with long 
term capitalization to de -risk the Balance Sheet.  
This morning, Tata Steel has executed agreements to acquire the 26.6% promoter stake in APL Apollo Tubes 
Ltd. at a price of Rs.2,100 per share for a consideration of Rs.15,510 crores. Tata Steel will also make an Open 
Offer for an additional 26% stake in A PL Apollo Tubes Ltd at Rs.2,301.40 per share for a consideration of 
Rs.16, 756 crores. All of  the announced transactions are subject to regulatory approvals, and Tata Steel expects 
to obtain these approvals in the coming months and close the composite set of transactions within FY2027.  
For Tata Steel, APL Apollo will now be the vehicle to drive downstream growth in Steel Tubes and Colour 
Coated products. The new APL Apollo Tubes Pvt Ltd. will build upto a 15.5 MTPA  capacity by 2035, including 
~9 MTPA  of Steel Tubes and ~6.5 MTPA  of Colour Coated products. The combination with APL Apollo Tubes 
will unleash the combined strengths of the companies , optimise capacity utilisation , unlock value and enhance 
profitability.  
Reacting to the above transaction Mr T V Narendran CEO and MD Tata Steel said “We are very pleased to 
have entered into a comprehensive agreement with APL Apollo and the Promoters as we create a downstream 
powerhouse for the future characterized by its inherent strengths of agility in the market, strong growth 
trajectory and benchmark cape x-opex  profile to serve the growing Indian market. This strategic move helps to 
consolidate the Steel Tubes and the Colour Coated business under Tata Steel’s ownership while unlocking 
multiple synergies between the two companies. This will create value for all s takeholders with enhanced 
customer centricity, pan -India reach, and a full product portfolio. We look forward to work with our partners 
through this exciting journey.”  
Mr Koushik Chatterjee ED & CFO of Tata Steel said that “We have over the last year emphasised our strategic 
intent to grow aggressively in the value -added space and expand our downstream footprint both organically 
and inorganically. While we have multiple projects under construction in Value -Added segments like tinplate 
and Hot Rolled Pickled & Galvanised lines, APL Apollo presented a unique opportunity for us. This is not just 
an acquisition of a very profitable an d efficient company, but the best foundation to build a future business in 
the Colour  Coated space with an exclusive contract manufacturing agreement with SG Tech Engineering, an 
entity run by the promoters of APL Apollo. Mr Sanjay Gupta and his team have demonstrated experience in 
building downstream capacity with agility, speed and effic iency and this partnership will be very strategic for 
Tata Steel and APL Apollo in the future. The opportunity to grow is immense and APL Apollo not only adds 
growth on the first day with the transfer of Tata Steel’s business but has the definite potential  to be a 1 5.5 
MTPA  very profitable downstream company  in the future. "
