Persistent Wins CII Award for Excellence in 
Disability Inclusion  
Recognized for embedding accessibility by design across 
workplaces, digital platforms and employee experiences   
 
June 19, 2026  
Pune, India  
 
News Summary  
Persistent Systems  (BSE: 533179 and NSE: PERSISTENT),  a global Digital Engineering and 
Enterprise Modernization leader , has been recognized as the winner of the CII Award for 
Excellence in Disability Inclusion 2026 in the category “Best Employer – Physical Accessibility.”  
The recognition acknowledges the Company’s approach to embedding accessibility across 
workplaces, digital platforms and talent programs through governance, leadership accountability 
and the implementation of global accessibility standards . 
The CII jury recognized Persistent ’s comprehensive  approach to accessibility, validated through 
independent audits  and extending across workplace infrastructure, digital platforms, enterprise 
applications and organizational culture. While the evaluation centered on Persistent’s Pune 
facility, its accessibility framework is being implemented across workplace s and the digital 
ecosystem globally.  
The Company’s Pune facility served as the benchmark site, where accessibility was 
incorporated  at the architectural design  stage and implement ed in line with India’s Harmonized 
Guidelines & Standards for Universal Accessibility (2021 ). Persistent ’s digital platforms and 
applications are also aligned with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) , 
demonstrating  its integrated approach to physical and digital accessibility .  
Across the employee lifecycle, the Company drives equitable hiring, career development and 
initiatives that foster awareness , allyship  and inclusion . These efforts help employees, 
candidates and visitors, including people  with disabilities, navigate workplaces and digital 
systems independently and confidently. Accessibility and inclusive design checks are integrated 
into Persistent’s engineering model, helping ensure digital solutions are more inclusive, usable 
and effective for a broader range of users .
