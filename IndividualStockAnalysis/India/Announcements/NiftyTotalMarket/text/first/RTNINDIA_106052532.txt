RattanIndia Enterprises Ltd. Launches Neomate – A New 
Standard in Affordable, Premium Stationery  
New Delhi, 11th November  2024 : RattanIndia Enterprises Ltd. is pleased to announce the launch of its 
newest brand, Neomate , through its 100% wholly owned subsidiary Neobrands Limited. Neomate 
introduces fresh and innovative stationery designed to merge premium quality with accessible pricing. 
The new lineup spans a variety of workspace essentials and creative tools, with offe rings meticulously 
crafted to inspire productivity, creativity, and organization for individuals and professionals alike.  
 
Neomate products are available on Amazon.in, with prices starting just from INR 160, making premium 
stationery just a click away for consumers across India. Explore our collection and find the perfect 
stationery items to elevate your workspace at  www.Amazon.in/neomate . 
 
As a brand dedicated to providing the best -in-class quality stationary products without the high -end 
price tag, Neomate’s product range has a wide selection of stationery, including well -designed desk 
organizers, high -performance markers, and more. Neomate  aims to be the go -to choice for anyone 
seeking functionality and aesthetic appeal in their workspace and creative projects.  
 

                                                                                             
Neomate introduces a practical, stylish range of stationery essentials crafted to enhance both 
organization and creativity. From sleek desk organizers designed for efficient storage to reusable 
whiteboard stickers suited for any workspace, Neomate’s collec tion is all about functionality and ease. 
The upcoming lineup also includes quick -dry, triangular grip dual -tip markers in varied colour  packs 
and acrylic paint markers in sets of 12 and 24 colours , catering to students, artists, and professionals 
alike. N eomate offers reliable, thoughtfully designed tools that make daily tasks and creative projects 
both enjoyable and efficient.  
         
Ms. Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises  Ltd., commented on the launch, “ We’re 
excited to bring Neomate to the market —a brand designed to elevate everyday tasks through premium 
yet affordable stationery. Neomate is all about creating possibilities for people to work, create, and 
organize better. We look forward to expanding Neomate’s range and suppor ting our customers’ 
evolving needs with quality products that inspire and enable. ”
