PRESS RELEASE  
 
BuzzWorks Further Expands in Hyderabad with State -Of-The-Art-Workspaces  
 
Expansion in Hyderabad, adding 50,000 sqft. to BuzzWorks ’ Existing Portfolio  
 
Hyderabad | July 1 5, 2025 :  BuzzWorks, the managed and flexible  offices arm of Brigade Group has announced 
its latest pre -eminent centre at Mindspace Business Park, HITEC City, Hyderabad, spanning over 50,000 square 
feet. This launch, which adds  1000 seats to its existing desk ban k, marks yet another strategic expansion beyond 
Brigade -owned developments. It also underlines the brand’s growing footprint and commitment to delivering 
premium managed workspaces in high -demand commercial locations.  
 
Located in the thriving IT corridor of Cyberabad, the centre will be equipped with a full spectrum of amenities 
including a cafeteria, a wellness room, collaborative zones, recreational areas, phone booths and a common 
boardroom along with multiple meeting  rooms. Following the success of its first Hyderabad centre at                      
Auro Orbit – HITEC City, designed around the  Urban Forest  theme, BuzzWorks is now set to launch its second 
workspace in the city. Staying true to its commitment to wellbein g-led design, the new space is themed 
around  ModZen  or Modern Zen  – blending the calm of Zen principles with the pace of modern -day work life. 
The design reflects Hyderabad’s unique duality as a thriving tech hub and a serene, liveable city, creating a 
workspace that inspires both momentum and mindfulness.  
 
Connectivity is a major strength of the new BuzzWorks centre, with direct skywalk access to Raidurg Metro 
Station just 650 meters away, in addition to easy access via TSRTC buses, cabs and ample in -campus parking. 
It is also easily accessible from the Raji v Gandhi International Airport (34 km), Secunderabad Railway Station 
(18 km) and key business districts such as Gachibowli (4.3 km).  
 
“This expansion reflects BuzzWorks’ deliberate and demand -led approach to growth, rooted in market insight, 
operational agility and strategic location selection. Our entry into Mindspace Business Park, HITEC City aligns 
with our larger vision of delivering  high -performance workspaces in prime business districts. Backed by the 
trusted quality and legacy of the Brigade Group, this new centre is designed to meet enterprise -grade 
expectations while reinforcing our commitment to flexibility, speed to market and infrastructure that enables 
business continuity and scale.” said Siddharth Verma, General Manager, BuzzWorks.  
 
About BuzzWorks:  
 
Brigade Group  entered the flexible office space segment with the launch of  BuzzWorks  in 2019. The brand offers 
managed, customisable workspaces through two key formats:  Built -to-Suit solutions  for enterprises with 
specific design and operational needs, and  Ready -to-Occupy offices  for businesses seeking high -quality, 
immediately functional  spaces. BuzzWorks primarily caters to large enterprises and growth -focused 
companies. Managed offices are increasingly seen as enablers of productivity , culture, and scale in today’s 
dynamic business landscape. With a sharp focus on experience, flexibility and scalability, the brand goes 
beyond workspaces; curating events, activations, and collaborative touchpoints that foster meaningful 
connections and enable professional and cultural exchange among its members.
