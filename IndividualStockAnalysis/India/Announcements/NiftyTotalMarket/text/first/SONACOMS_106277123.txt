Dear Sir / Madam,  
 
In terms of the provisions of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we wish to inform you that the Nomination and Remuneration Committee 
(NRC/Compensation Committee) of the Board of Directors of the Company on 24th June, 2025, 
approved the allotment of 3,000 (Three thousand only) equity shares having face value of Rs. 10/

under the Sona Employee Stock Option Plan - 2023 (“ESOP Plan”) of the Company. The equity

Company in every aspect, including dividend entitlement.

Company stands increased from Rs. 6,21,71,79,750 (Rupees six hundred twenty one crores, 
seventy one lakhs, seventy nine thousand and seven hundred fifty only) consisting of 62,17,17,975 
(Sixty two crores, seventeen lakhs, seventeen thousands, nine hundred seventy five only) equity 
shares having face value of Rs. 10/- each fully paid-up to Rs. 6,21,72,09,750 (Rupees six hundred 
twenty one crores, seventy two lakhs, nine thousand seven hundred and fifty only) consisting of 
62,17,20,975 (Sixty two crores, seventeen lakhs, twenty thousands, nine hundred and seventy five 
only) equity shares having face value of Rs. 10/- each fully paid-up.
