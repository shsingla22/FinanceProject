LT Foods makes Basmati rice the hero of every meal with 
  
Delight ’s new campaign  
 
Delhi (India), 17th November  2026 : LT Foods Ltd., a global FMCG company in the consumer food 
space, today unveiled a new campaign for 
  Delight Basmati, featuring Celebr ity Chef  
Ranveer Brar . The latest campaign puts the spotlight  on the rice , showcasing how the rig ht Basmati 
can absorb the flavo rs of the dish it is served with and make the entire food experience more 
enjoyable.  
 
Set in an office during lunchtime, the film captures a light -hearted exchange among three colleagues 
over Rajma -Chawal. Two colleagues are having the sam e Rajma but with different rice, and when a 
third colleague tastes both, she immediately notices the difference. The moment sparks a playful 
conversation around how the Rajma can be the same, yet the overall taste experience can change 
with the rice. Chef Ranveer Brar takes the conversation forward, bringing alive 
  Delight ’s 
Superior Flavour Absorption and demonstrating how the right basmati rice absorbs the flavours of the 
accompanying dish to make the complete meal more enjoyable.  
 
Conceptualised by BBDO India, the film uses this simple, everyday insight to land the campaign 
thought, “Har Dish Ko Banaye Very Very Nice. ” 
 
The first campaign of 
  Delight was launched in August 2026, which marked the beginning 
of the brand ’s association with Chef Ranveer Brar. Since its launch, 
  Delight has been 
seeing an encouraging consumer response, providing a strong foundation for LT Foods to further 
build the propo sition and deepen consumer engagement with the brand.
