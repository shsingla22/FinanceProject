About the Company  
 
We aim to make insurance simple. Through innovation and transparency, we believe in delivering a 
seamless customer experience journey in a significant financial product an individual would purchase in 
their lifetime. We are one of the leading digital full stack insurance companies, leveraging our technology 
to power what we believe to be an innovative approach to product design, distribution and customer 
experience for non -life insurance products.  
We offer motor insurance, health insurance, travel insurance, property insurance, marine insurance, 
liability insurance and other insurance products, which the customers can customize to meet their  needs.  
 
For more details visit https://www.godigit.com/ .
