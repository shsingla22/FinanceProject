Re: Offer for Sale by Greaves Cotton Limited (“Company”) in proposed Initial Public 
Offering (“IPO”) of equity shares of Greaves Electric Mobility Limited  (“GEML ”), a 
material subsidiary of the Company . 
 
We refer to our earlier intimation dated  01st December, 2024 , in relation to the proposed IPO 
of equity shares of GEML, a material subsidiary of the Company.  
 
In this connection, we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Company at its 
meeting held today , i.e., on 11th December, 2024 , inter alia , considered and approved the 
Offer for Sale of certain equity shares of GEML held by the Company in the proposed IPO  
(“OFS”), subject to any revisions to such quantum as may be permissible under applicable law 
and subject to market conditions, receipt of necessary approvals/ regulatory clearances and 
other considerations.  
 
Please note that , post the proposed IPO, while the Company would continue to be a significant 
shareholder of GEML , whether GEML would continue to be a subsidiary of the Company  
would be contingent on the final quantum of the OFS and the size of the IPO , which along with 
the price and other details of the proposed IPO, will be determined in due course by the 
competent body  based on provisions of applicable law, market conditions and other 
considerations (such as the valuation of GEML ). 
 
We request you to kindly take this matter on record .
