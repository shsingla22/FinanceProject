It may be noted that Delhi International Airports Limited (“DIAL”), a subsidiary of the 
Company, had invoked arbitration against AAI with respect to the scope and meaning 
of the term “Revenue” under th e Operation, Management and Development 
Agreement dated April 04, 2006 (“OMDA”) that set out the process for computation 
of the Annual Fee which is payable to AAI along with a corresponding claim for 
consequential recovery of certain amounts of Annual Fee  paid to AAI.  
  
The Arbitral Tribunal had passed the Award on July 16, 2022, the key aspects of the 
Award being the following:  
   
1. For the purpose of computing the Annual Fee payable by DIAL to AAI, following 
amounts shall be excluded from the shareable re venue of DIAL:  
a. Amounts representing the costs relating to aeronautical assets, i.e., 
amounts spent from the borrowed capital including interest thereon 
and form the equity capital;  
b. Charges for various utilities including power, water, sewage or 
analogous utilities, the property tax paid to Relevant Authorities and 
payments towards security maintenance cost.;  
c. Proceeds accruing from sale of any capital assets; and  
d. Other Income  
  
2. DIAL is entitled f or refund of excess Annual Fee paid from June 21, 2015 in 
line with the terms of Award, the actual amount whereof shall be determined 
by the Independent Auditor.  
 
The said arbitration award of July 16, 2022 was challenged by the AAI in Delhi High 
Court and the same has now been dismissed by the Delhi High Court vide the 
Judgement and order dated October 18, 2024, upholding the arbitration award of July 
16, 2022. This order is subject to appeal, if any preferred,  before the Division  Bench 
of the Delhi High Court.
