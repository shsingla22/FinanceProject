Sanofi India Limited appoints  Rahul Bhatnagar as the 
Chairman of its Board of Directors  
 
Rahul Bhatnagar succeeds Aditya Narayan, who complete d a decade -long distinguished 
tenure 
 
Mumbai , India: 30th April 2026 . Sanofi India Limited (SIL) has appointed Mr. Rahul 
Bhatnagar as the Chairman of its  Board of Directors with effect from Thursday, 30th April 
2026.  
 
Since joining SIL's Board in 2020, Mr. Bhatnagar has actively contributed across multiple 
Committees, including Audit, Nomination and Remuneration, Corporate Social Responsibility, 
and Risk Management . He brings extensive expertise in finance, strategy and governance.  
 
A Chartered Accountant and Wharton MBA, he has held leadership positions  across leading global 
and Indian organizations, including Nestlé, PepsiCo, and the Bharti Group. He currently serves 
as an Independent Director on the Boards of Whirlpool India Limited , Rossell India Limited, TBO 
TEK Limited & Tasty Bite Eatables  Limited.  
 The Board extends its sincere gratitude to  Independent Director  Mr. Aditya Narayan  for his 
exemplary stewardship  over the past decade, which has significantly strengthened SIL's 
corporate  governance framework.  
 
The Board also placed on record its deep appreciation for Independent Director Mrs. Usha 
Thorat , whose tenure ended  at the close of business hours on 29
th April 2026. We thank her for 
her invaluable contributions, guidance and services during her association with the Company.  
 
The Board warmly welcomes Mr. Bhatnagar to his new role and is confident that his strategic 
vision  will drive continued growth and excellence , as SIL advances its mission to chase the 
miracles of science .
