Sub:  Intimation under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
– Partnership with KredX  Platform Pvt Ltd. (KPPL) for TReDS Business.  
 
This is to inform that Central Bank of India has announced a strategic partnership with KredX, India’s 
leading supply chain finance platform, to facilitate Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS) 
services  for MSMEs across the country. This collaboration marks a significant step toward improving 
liquidity and financial inclusion for small and medium enterprises.  
 
KredX is India’s leading integrated supply chain finance platform, and among the very few entit ies licensed 
by both the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and the International Financial Services Centers Authority 
(IFSCA).  
 
Under this partnership, Central Bank of India will leverage KredX’s newly licensed TReDS platform, 
branded as DTX (Domestic Trade Exch ange), to offer seamless invoice discounting services. The move 
aligns with the Reserve Bank of India’s push to expand digital financing avenues for MSMEs and promote 
transparent, efficient trade receivables management.
