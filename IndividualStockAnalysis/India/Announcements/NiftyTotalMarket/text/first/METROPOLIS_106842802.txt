PRESS RELEASE  
Metropolis Healthcare reports strong Q2  FY27 performance, 
with revenue up 16% to ₹497 crore and PAT up 43% to ₹7 5 crore . 
 
Mumbai, India, 3rd November 2026:  Ameera Shah -led Metropolis Healthcare Limited (NSE: 
METROPOLIS | BSE: 542650), one of India’s leading diagnostic chains, today announced its unaudited 
consolidated financial results for the quarter ended 30th September 2026.  
 
REVENUE  
INR 497 Cr  
▲ 16% YoY  EBITDA  
INR 13 5 Cr 
▲ 24% YoY  PAT  
INR 7 5 Cr 
▲ 43% YoY  
 
 
• Patient volume grew 10% YoY to 4.1 million , while test volume increased 11% YoY to 8.7 
million , reflecting sustained demand and the continued shift towards organised diagnostic 
providers.  
 
• B2C revenue grew 17% YoY to INR 289 crore,  driven by lifecycle -led customer engagement, 
targeted micro -marketing initiatives and strong brand recall. B2B revenue rose 15% YoY to INR 
208 crore,  driven by the addition of new clients and increased uptake of specialised tests among 
existing clients.  
 
• TruHealth revenue grew 20% YoY to INR 86 crore , driven by premiumisation and more 
customers opting for preventive care. Specialty revenue rose 20% YoY to INR 198 crore , led by 
advanced genomic testing and accelerated growth in cancer testing.  
 
• Revenue per Patient (RPP) increased 6% YoY to INR 1,221 d espite no price increase benefit, 
while Revenue per Test (RPT) improved 5% YoY to INR 572, supported by a richer test mix and 
improved realisations.  
 
• Tier III cities remained the fastest -growing markets , with revenue rising around 20% YoY . 
Metropolis now reaches around 750 towns through 5,119 service points.  
 
• The Board has dec lared an interim dividend of ₹1 per share.

About Metropolis Healthcare Limited:  Established in 1981, Metropolis Healthcare Limited is one of India’s  leading 
diagnostic chains , led by visionary leader Ms. Ameera Shah. With over four decades of excellence, Metropolis has been a 
pioneer in delivering best -in-class diagnostic services to patients, healthcare providers, and corporates across India and 
Africa. The company’s extensive  footprint spans 28 states, 7 Union Territories, and over 750+ towns in India, supported by a 
robust network of more than 2 13 laboratories, 5 119 plus service network, and over 10,000 touchpoints. Each year, 
Metropolis serves millions of individuals by providing actionable health insights that empower better clinical outcomes.  
 
Metropolis offers a comprehensive range of more than 4,500 tests and profiles, including advanced tests for diagnosing 
cancer, neurological disorders, infectious diseases, and various genetic abnormalities. The company’s commitment to 
quality and accuracy is reinforced by its consistent CAP proficiency score of over 99 .99% for the past decade, placing it 
among the top 1% of laboratories worldwide for quality assurance. The Metropolis philosophy is built on the pillars of 
technological superiority, a warm, patient -centric approach, and reliable diagnostic reports. For more information, please 
visit www.metropolisindia.com or click on Twitter , Facebook , or LinkedIn
