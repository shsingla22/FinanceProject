Mastek Partners with OpenAna to Accelerate AI -Driven Innovation  
 
Mumbai, India, 10th February 2025 : Mastek (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted digital engineering 
and cloud transformation partner, today announced its strategic partnership with OpenAna, an 
innovative AI-driven platform modernizing software development lifecycle (SDLC) processes. This 
partnership marks a significant milestone in Mastek’s journey to enhance its Generative AI capabilities, 
enabling businesses to innovate faster, optimize development workflows, and accelerate digital 
transformation.  
OpenAna  is transforming  software engineering with Ana, an  AI-powered Autonomous Software Engineer 
revolutionizing how technology teams deliver software. As an integral engineering teammate, Ana 
enables enterprises to accelerate delivery, scale effortlessly, and achieve unparalleled efficiency —all 
while empowering teams to focus on innovation and strategic growth . 
The collaboration will focus on integrating Gen AI into SDLC processes by  harnessing OpenAna’s next -gen 
AI capabilities and Mastek’s deep expertise in digital transformation. Together, the partnership aims to empower organizations to navigate complex digital challenges and seize new growth opportunities. This will streamline de velopment cycles, enhance operational efficiency, and accelerate time- to-market.  
“We are excited to partner with OpenAna, as it aligns with our shared vision of transforming Software 
Development  through Gen AI. By leveraging the Ana platform, we aim to empower our customers by 
developing and maintaining software applications more efficiently and effectively. This collaboration further strengthens Mastek’s commitment to helping our customers unlock greater value through AI ,” 
said Raman Sapra, President &  Chief Growth Officer  at Mastek . 
“With Ana as an Autonomous Engineer, our partnership with Mastek aligns with our mission to make 
software development better, faster, and more secure.  This partnership accelerates vision into reality by 
empowering engineering teams and enabling enterprises to ship faster, innovate smarter, and operate with unparalleled confidence. ” said Rajiv Sondhi, CEO OpenAna .

About Open Ana 
OpenAna  is the creator of Ana, an advanced AI -powered Autonomous Software Engineer designed to 
revolutionize software development. Ana operates independently, managing the entire software 
development lifecycle— from design and coding to testing and deployment.  
By automating end -to-end workflows, Ana enables enterprises to ship software faster, innovate at scale, 
and optimize costs without compromising on quality or security. Trusted by engineering teams 
worldwide, Ana empowers organizations to achieve their digital ambitions with confidence, 
transforming how software is built and delivered.
