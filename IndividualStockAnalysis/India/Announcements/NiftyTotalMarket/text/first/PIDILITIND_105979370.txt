Communication / Media Release from CollTech Group 
CollTech Group, a Company dedicated to the manufacture and sale of high-
performance adhesives and thermal management solutions is pleased to announce an 
exclusive distribution agreement with Pidilite Industries Limited, a well-respected brand 
name in the industrial adhesives and sealants industry in India. 
 
Under this partnership, Pidilite will serve as the exclusive distributor of CollTech’s 
extensive range of products in India.  This collaboration is aimed to enhance CollTech’s 
market presence and further expand Pidilite’s position in the electronics industry in 
India.  
  
The exclusive agreement underscores CollTech’s commitment to delivering exceptional 
products and solutions through a strategic partnership with Pidilite Industries Ltd.  By 
leveraging extensive distribution network and market knowledge of Pidilite Industries 
Ltd., CollTech aims to provide even greater value to its customers, ensuring consistent 
product availability and superior service in India.
