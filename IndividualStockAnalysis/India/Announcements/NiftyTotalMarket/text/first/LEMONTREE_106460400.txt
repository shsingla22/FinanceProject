Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in  Rajasthan  
 
New Delhi , 3rd December 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -
growing mid -scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the signing of its latest 
property , “Lemon Tre e Hotel”, Pacific Mall, Jaipur.  
 
This property shall be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned 
subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited . It will further strengthen  the company’s 
presence in Rajasthan , while catering to both  business  and leisure travellers for a 
fresh, vibrant, and value -driven experience.  
 
Property Highlights   
“Lemon Tre e Hotel ”, Pacific Mall, Jaipur  is in the vibrant capital of Rajasthan, 
which is a beautiful blend of royal heritage and colo urful culture. Fondly called the 
Pink City , it is known for its stunning palaces, grand forts, bustling bazaars, and warm 
hospitality. From the majestic Amber Fort and Hawa Mahal to the lively streets filled 
with handicrafts, jewel lery, and traditional Rajasthani flavo urs, Jaipur offers a 
timeless charm that captures the essence of India’s regal past while embracing 
modern energy.  
 
The key features of the hotel will include:  
 
• 124 well-appointed rooms  
• A rest aurant  and a lounge  
• A meeting room   
• Recreational facility includes a swimming pool and a fitness center  
 
The Jaipur International Airport  is approximately 8 kms aw ay from the property, while 
the Jaipur Railway Station is about 8.4 kms away. The hotel is well connected by 
roadways for both public and private transport.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO — Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels, commented , "With this  signing , we will expan d our leisure  and 
business portfolio in Rajasthan , where we have 12 operational  and 9 upcoming 
properties  in addition to this one . The state, with its  booming  commerce,  distinct

ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India’s leading hospitality companies, 
catering to a wide range of customers – from value -conscious travellers to premium 
business and leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels & Resorts, 
Lemon T ree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys Select, and Keys 
Lite – the group offers experiences across upper upscale, upscale, upper midscale, 
midscale, leisure, wildlife, and spiritual segments.  
 
LTHL operates 120+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing 
pipeline of 120+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, 
Bengaluru, and Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, and 
Indore – and with an international presence in Dubai, Bhutan, and Nepal – Lemon 
Tree Hotels delivers exceptional comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, refreshing 
experience.  
 
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in  2004, the group has grown to 25 0+ properties 
(operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for both business 
and leisure travellers.
