HERO MOTOCORP UNVEILS SUPER SPLENDOR XTEC 2.0  
INDIA ’S MOST TRUSTED 125 CC MOTORCYCLE , GETS AN UPGRADE!  
 
 
The ultimate commuter motorcycle  just got a major upgrade.  Hero MotoCorp , the world’s largest 
manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters , today launch ed the all -new Super Splendor XTEC 2.0  –
India’s most trusted and widely loved commuter motorcycle. Driven by the  Company ’s aggressive 
product revitalization strategy , the all -new Super Splendor XTEC 2.0  is engineered to elevate 
everyd ay commuting experience  through  meaningful suite of technology upgrades, a significant leap 
in fuel efficiency, and a bold , contemporary design tailored for today’s progressive riders.   
 
Built on the trusted legacy of the Splendor that has provided mobility to millions of Indian riders 
since 1994,  the all-new  Super Splendor XTEC 2.0  is designed for riders who seek dependable 
performance with modern -day convenience  and next -gen product tech features . 
 
Speaking at the launch, Ashutosh Varma, Chief Business Office r, India Business Unit, Hero 
MotoCorp  said, “ Hero MotoCorp’s Splendor has built a strong legacy  of delivering trust, performance 
and everyday reliability to millions of customers for over three  decades . The new Super Splendor XTEC 
2.0 carries this promise  forward with a bold and contemporary design, advanced technology and 
enhanced conven ience features . With its trusted performance  & reliability , everyday practicality and an 
elevated riding experience, the Super Splendor  XTEC 2.0 furthe r strengthens Hero MotoCorp’s 
leadership  in the segment while reaffirming its commitment to delivering superior value to its 
customers.”  
  
Modern Styling and Refreshed Identity  
Boasting a refreshed visual identity, the all -new Super Splendor XTEC 2.0 brings a sharp, 
contemporary appeal to a classic silhouette.  Dynamic dual -tone body graphics paired with  a vibrant 
color palette breathe s fresh energy into the  motorcycle design . This new variant comes with  
signature  XTEC 2.0 badging , a distinct and premium 3D emblem, a stylish stitched dual -textured seat 
and eye-catching rim tapes  -creating a commanding and premium road presence.  
 
Enhanced Performance & Best -in-Class Mileage  
At the heart of the all -new Super Splendor XTEC 2.0 is a proven and highly efficient 124.7cc single -
cylinder engine producing 10.7 bhp at 7500 rpm and a peak torque of 10.6 Nm at 6000 rpm. Tuned 
for refined everyday commuting, this motorcycle delivers smooth acceleration, dependable riding 
experience and re sponsive performance across varied riding conditions.  
 
The highlight  of the XTEC 2.0 is its best -in-class  mileage of 72 kmpl , enabled through the combined 
efficiency of Advanced Programmed Fuel Injection  (APFI) and Hero MotoCorp’s trusted i3S (Idle 
Stop -Start System). The system optimizes fuel consumption while enhancing overall riding 
experience. Additionally, the  Integrated Starter Generator  (ISG), enables a near silent engine start 
experience along with seamless restart during idle -stop conditions – delivering enhanced comfort 
and convenience in dense traffic conditions.

Safety Enabled by Smart Technology  
Elevating everyday safety and rider confidence, the all -new Super Splendor XTEC 2.0 introduces  
segment -first Hazard Lights in the Super Splendor series . This critical safety feature allows  for the 
simultaneous activation of all four indicators,  ensuring maximum visibility during unexpected 
emergencies and breakdown situations. The motorcycle also comes equipped with a Front Disc Brake 
for assured braking performance and enhanced control across varied road conditions. Adding to the 
rider convenience is a handlebar -mounted engine kill switch, e nabling quick ignition cut -off for 
stress -free everyday usability.  
 
Smart Connectivity and Everyday Convenience  
The all-new Super Splendor XTEC 2.0 keeps riders seamlessly connected through a Fully Digital 
Instrument Cluster with Bluetooth connectivity. Designed to enhance convenience during daily 
commute, the console displays incoming call alerts, SMS notifications, mi ssed call alerts, caller ID and 
phone battery status . 
 
The motorcycle also features Hero MotoCorp’s Real Time Mileage Indicator (RTMi) and Low Fuel 
Indicator, helping riders stay informed and in control of fuel consumption at all times. Further 
enhancing p racticality for today’s connected riders is the addition of USB Type -C charging port of 2 
Amp to ensure smartphone remain powered for communication and navigation through the ride.  
 
Superior Comfort for Everyday Mobility  
Engineered to tackle  the demanding  realities of long daily commutes, the Super Splendor XTEC 2.0 
offers superior ergonomics with a wide , upright handlebar and a relaxed riding posture that 
significantly minimizes rider fatigue on both  city streets and open highway s. The long comfortable 
seat provides ample space for both rider and pillion, while the refined telescopic front suspension 
deliver s a balanced and comfortable ride across unpredictable terrains . Additionally, a wide rear grab 
rail provides excellent stabil ity to the Pillon  rider  for added support during bumpy rides.  
 
Colo rs and Pricing  
The all -new Super Splendor XTEC 2.0 is available in five distinctive color  options – Glossy Black, 
Candy Blazing Red, Matt Axis Grey, Matt Nexus Blue, and Matt Chestnut Brown - each crafted to 
compliment the motorcycle’s bold and refreshed identity. The new variant is available at Hero 
MotoCorp dealerships across the country at an attractive price of Rs. 86500 /- (Ex-Showroom, Delhi).
