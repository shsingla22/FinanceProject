Dear Sir / Madam,  
This is to inform you that the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Ahmedabad  Bench  (‘NCLT’) has, vide its 
order dated 4th November 2026 ( order copy received through email  on the same day i.e., 04th November  2026  
at 6: 10 PM ), approved the reduction , cancellation and writing off the securities premium account of Arvind 
Lifestyle Brands Limited  (‘ALBL ’), wholly owned material subsidiary of Arvind Fashions Limited , to the extent of 
Rs. 1438.79 crores against the negative balance of accumulated losses to the extent of Rs. 1438.79 crores . 
ALBL shall take necessary steps as per the regulatory requirements to implement the aforesaid transaction  as 
per the said order of Hon’ble NCLT.  
The said reduction is purely an internal capital restructuring of ALBL, involving only an adjustment between the 
securities premium account and the accumulated losses within its other equity. It does not affect the net worth 
of ALBL and has no implication on the consolidated financial numbers of Arvind Fashions Limited.  
The said disclosure shall be available on the website of the company on the link given below.
