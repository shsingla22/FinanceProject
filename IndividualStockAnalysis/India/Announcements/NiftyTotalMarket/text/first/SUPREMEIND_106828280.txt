 The Board of Directors has declared an interim dividend @ 600 %  i.e. Rs. 12 per share of Rs. 2 each 
(FV), for the financial year 2026 -27. The Dividend will be paid to those shareholders whose names 
stand on the Register of Members as on the Record date i.e. 30th October  2026.

 The overall turnover of value- added products increased to Rs. 1 476 crores during the current 2nd 
quarter as compared to Rs. 1072 crores in the corresponding quarter of previous year achieving 
growth of 38%.  
 
Business Outlook  
 
Commenting on the business environment, Mr. M. P. Taparia, Chairman & Managing Director, said:  
 
The operating environment during the second quarter of FY27 has shown encouraging signs of stabilization after the unusual volatility witnessed in polymer prices during the first quarter. Although prevailing geopolitical situation in west Asia has its own impact on Polymer prices volatility but measures taken by the Government including implementation of Minimum Import Price (MIP) on 
Suspension Grade PVC Resin and withdrawal of customs duty exemption have supported the market 
environment towards reduced vol atility.  
 India continues to remain one of the fastest- growing major economies, supported by sustained 
investments in infrastructure, housing, urban development, water management projects, irrigation networks and expansion of city gas distribution infrastructure. Th ese structural growth drivers 
provide long -term demand visibility across all major business segments of the Company.  
 
The Plastic Piping Systems business continues to strengthen its market leadership through an 
expanding portfolio of value- added products, enhanced manufacturing capabilities and deeper market 
penetration. The successful integration of the former Wavin business and access to advanced technologies are enabling the Company to offer differentiated solutions for building and infrastructure 
applications. The Company remains focused on increasing the share of Value- Added products 
including PERT piping systems, Gas  piping system, Electrofusion fittings, premium range of Bath 
Fittings, industrial valves and PP Silent Pipe Systems.  
 
The Company had a growth of about 2% in volume during first half of the year over the corresponding 
period of previous year in Plastic Piping Division. In such an uncertain geopolitical situation, precise 
growth forecast is not feasible but encouraged by about 2 4% volume growth achieved in seco nd 
quarter, Company aims to continue the momentum and achieve better growth in full year.  
 
The Company's newly launched uPVC  Windows & Doors business has received encouraging 
acceptance in the marketplace. The business is steadily expanding its dealer network and is expected to emerge as an important growth driver in the building products segment over next couple of years.  
 
The Composite Cylinder business is witnessing gradual improvement in capacity utilization with 
procurement activities now initiated by all three Oil Marketing Companies. The division continues to explore various export markets and is also expanding its par ticipation in CNG cascade applications, 
creating additional avenues for growth.  
 
 
 
The Industrial Component, Material Handling and Packaging Product segment businesses continue to 
perform satisfactorily. The introduction of PP Bubble Guard Sheets and sustained focus on customized solutions are helping widen application areas and enhance customer engagement. The Company's consumer products portfolio continues to struggle for growth amid high volatility of base polymer 
prices.  
 
The Company remains committed to its growth path and continues to invest in capacity expansion across businesses. Development of new manufacturing facilities at Bihar, Jammu, second site at Erode, another site at Puducherry, Malanpur and brown field expans ion at other strategic locations is 
progressing as planned. With healthy cash flows and robust internal accruals, the Company is well positioned to fund its expansion plans while maintaining financial discipline.  
 Looking ahead, the Company remains optimistic about the business outlook for the remaining half of 
FY27. With encouraging outlook of Plastic Piping Division and other businesses in the second quarter, 
Company expects to grow by about 10% in volume for the full year despite big degrowth in first quarter. Diversified product portfolio, strong brand, extensive distribution network and continued focus on operational excellence will enable the Company to deliver sustainable long -term growth .

About Supreme Industries Limited  
Supreme Industries Limited is India’s leading plastics product manufacturer, offering a wide and comprehensive range of plastic products in India. The company operates in various product categories 
viz. Plastic Piping System, Cross Laminated Films & Produc ts, Protective Packaging Products, Industrial 
Moulded Components, Moulded Furniture, Storage & Material Handling Products, Performance 
Packaging Films, Composite LPG Cylinders and Window & Doors.  
 Supreme Industries has 35 technologically advanced manufacturing facilities located at various places 
spread across the country. The company has built up an excellent relationship with its distributors and 
is also providing orientation & training to them, in order to ensure proper service to ultimate customers.
