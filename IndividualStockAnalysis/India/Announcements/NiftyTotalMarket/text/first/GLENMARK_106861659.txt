Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Inc., USA Launches 
Authorized  Generic  of INLYTA® (axitinib)  Tablets,  1 mg and 5 mg 
 
 
Elmwood Park, New Jersey, November 16, 2026 – Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Inc., USA 
(Glenmark),  today  announced  the launch  of Axitinib  Tablets,  1 mg and 5 mg. This product  is an 
Authorized Generic version of INLYTA®1 (axitinib) tablets.  
 
According to IQVIA® sales data for the 12 -month period ending September 2026, U.S.  annual 
sales of INLYTA® Tablets were approximately $538.4 million*.  
 
“The launch of Axitinib Tablets reinforces Glenmark’s commitment to expanding access to 
quality, affordable medicines for patients in the United States,” said Marc Kikuchi, President 
& Business Head, North America . By adding this important product to our portfolio, we 
continue to strengthen our focus on addressing the evolving needs of patients and healthcare 
providers.”  
 
1INLYTA® is a registered  trademark  of Pfizer  Inc. 
 
*IQVIA® National  Sales  Perspectives:  Retail  & Non-Retail,  September  2026
