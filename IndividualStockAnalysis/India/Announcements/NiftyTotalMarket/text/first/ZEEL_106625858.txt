Dear Punit, 
  
After 21 years at ZEEL, it is with utmost sadness and a heavy heart that I hereby tender my resignation from 
its services on account of personal reasons. 
  
My sincere thanks to you for entrusting me with innumerable opportunities and your invaluable support over 
the last 21 years. I truly appreciate it and I shall fondly remember the many innumerable breakthroughs and 
successes we registered, under your leadership. 
  
For a holistic growth and learning experience that I take away from my tenure here, I am comforted by the 
fact that I have – and in no small measure – also been able to give back to the organization both, tangibly 
and intangibly, through my sincere efforts & passion. I also take this opportunity to express my heartfelt 
gratitude to all colleagues who have not only made this journey successful but also a memorable one. 
  
Lastly, I shall be serving my notice period as per company norms (i.e. 180 days) and so my last working day 
would be 9th Nov. 2026. While I have already completed the handover to Sandeep - as advised by 
management, I assure you that, during this notice period, I shall fulfil any further assistance required of me 
regarding the same. 
   
Thanking you, 
Yours Truly, 
Laxmi Shetty
