Tax Deduction at Source (TDS) on Dividend  
  
Pursuant to the amendments introduced by the Finance Act, 2020, dividend income is taxable 
in the hands of shareholders. Accordingly, the Company is required to deduct tax at source 
(TDS) at the time of payment of dividend, as per the provisions of the Income  Tax Act, 1961  
(“Act”).
