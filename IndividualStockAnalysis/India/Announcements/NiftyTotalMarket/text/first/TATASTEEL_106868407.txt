Sub: Outcome of Board Meeting  of Tata Steel Limited  
 
The Board of Directors (‘ Board ’) of Tata Steel Limited (‘ Company ’) at its meeting held today, i.e. 
November 23, 2026, inter alia, transacted the following business:  
 
1. Acquisition of equity stake in APL Apollo Tubes Limited  
 
The Board has considered and approved entering into a share purchase agreement dated 
November 23, 2026 ( ‘SPA’) with S Gupta Holding Private Limited (‘Seller ’), a member of the 
promoter/ promoter group of  APL Apollo Tubes Limited (‘Target Company ’), for acquiring  of up to 
7,38,60,062  (seven crore thirty eight lakh sixty thousand  and sixty two ) fully paid -up equity shares 
of face value INR 2 (Indian Rupees two)  each (‘Equity Shares’ ) of the Target Company  for a cash 
consideration aggregating up to approximately INR 15510,61,30,200  (Indian Rupees Fifteen 
Thousand Five Hundred and Ten Crores Sixty One Lakhs Thirty Thousand Two Hundred), 
(‘Proposed Acquisition ’).  
 
This Proposed Acquisition  represent s approximately 26.38% of Target Company’s  total voting 
share capital on a fully diluted basis expected as of the 10th (tenth) working day from the closure 
of the tendering period for the Open Offer  (as defined hereinbelow ) which includes 23,66,159 
(twenty three lakhs sixty six thousand one hundred and fifty nine) Equity Shares, being the 
maximum number of Equity Shares that can be allotted against the outstanding stock appreciation 
rights of the Target Company (‘Expanded Voting Share Capital ’). The Proposed Acquisition will 
be conducted  in three tranches,  in the manner set out below, subject to the terms and conditions 
of the SPA :  
 
(i) on the date of execution of the SPA for the Proposed Acquisition , 2,77,38,075 (two crore 
seventy seven lakh thirty eight thousand and seventy five) Equity Shares representing 
approximately 9.91% of the Expanded Voting Share Capital, through an on -market 
purchase by way of a block deal during the morning block deal window, at a price of  
INR 2,100 /- (Indian Rupees two thousand one hundred) per Equity Share (‘ Tranche 1 
Acquisition Shares ’);  
 
(ii) on the date of Tranche 2 closing under the Share Purchase Agreement, 3,33,19,009 (three 
crore thirty three lakh nineteen thousand and nine ) Equity Shares representing 
approximately 11.90% of the Expanded Voting Share Capital, by way of an off -market 
transfer, at a price of INR 2,100 /- (Indian Rupees two thousand one hundred) per Equity 
 
 Share , subject to adjustment for dividends declared between execution and closing in 
accordance with the Share Purchase Agreement (‘Tranche 2 Acquisition Shares ’), 
subject to the satisfaction (or waiver, where permitted) of the conditions precedent under 
the Share Purchase Agreement, including receipt of necessary approvals from regulatory 
authorities ; and 
 
(iii) on or prior to March 31, 2028, 1,28,02,978 (one crore twenty eight lakh two thousand nine 
hundred and seventy eight) Equity Shares representing approximately 4.57% of the 
Expanded Voting Share Capital, by way of an off -market transfer, at a price of INR 2, 100/- 
(Indian Rupees two thousand one hundred) per Equity Share (‘ Tranche 3 Acquisition 
Shares ’), subject to the satisfaction (or waiver, where permitted) of the conditions 
precedent under the Share Purchase Agreement, including receipt of necessary approvals 
from regulatory authorities . 
 
Pursuant to the approval of the Board, the Company has today executed  the Share Purchase 
Agreement  for the Proposed Acquisition . 
 
2. Open Offer  
 
Considered and approved m aking a mandatory open offer under Regulation 3(1) and Regulation 
4 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) 
Regulations, 2011, as amended ( ‘SEBI (SAST) Regulations ’), for acquisition of up to 7,28,06,387  
(seven crore twenty eight lakh six thousand three hundred and eighty seven) Equity Shares 
representing 26% of the Expanded Voting Share Capital, from the public shareholders of the Target 
Company, at a price of INR 2,301.40 (Indian Rupees two thousand three hundred and one and 
forty paise) per Equity Share, for a total consideration of up to INR 16755 ,66,19,041.80  (Indian 
Rupees sixteen thousand seven hundred fifty five crores sixty six lakhs nineteen thousand and 
forty one and eighty paise)  (assuming full acceptance), payable in cash ( ‘Open Offer ’), subject to 
the terms and conditions set out in the public announcement and to be set out in the detailed public 
statement and the letter of offer to be issued in respect of the Open Offer in accordance with the 
SEBI (SAST) Regulations. For further details on this transaction, please refer Annexure 1.
