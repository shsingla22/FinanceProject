PRESS RELEASE 
Strategic in-house development of Al-based Education Technology Platform 
Kolkata, 30t»March, 2026: Crizac Limited, a Kolkata-headquartered B2B education platform, 
serves over 135 global higher education institutions across the United Kingdom, United States 
of America, Canada, the Republic of Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, United Arab Emirates 
and through its network of more than 10,000 registered agents — identified EduMentor as a 
strategic complement to its existing technology-led infrastructure. 
The Company understands that AI is the future and in view of that it has committed $2.5 
million towards inhouse development of technology led infrastructure through EduMentor, 
structured across a five-year strategic integration roadmap. This is represents a deliberate 
strategic alignment between Crizac’s model and EduMentor’s mentor-driven intelligence 
layer. 
Dr. Vikash Agarwal, Chairman and Managing Director of Crizac Limited, who himself is a 
leading force behind technological innovation and the growth of Crizac, has taken a personal 
interest in steering the project’s integration. He states that this Al-led innovation defines 
Crizac’s mission and future goals to be the leader in innovating and dominating the Edtech 
industry.” 
EduMentor is not a review site. It is a structured, Al-driven mentorship marketplace that 
connects prospective students with verified current students and recent graduates at 
universities worldwide. The platform uses AI and machine learning to automate every layer 
of the process. From onboarding and credentialing mentors, to intelligently matching them 
with incoming enquiries based on programme relevance, location, language, and career 
interest. 
This AI project shall be developed under the guidance and leadership of Mr. Dishant 
Kharbanda, an IIM Calcutta, Alumni. He brought nearly two decades of experience in edtech 
entrepreneurship to the programme. His active help shaped the platform’s architecture and 
go-to-market strategy. Before this project, he had already built and exited a significant venture 
in the same sector, which was recognised as Most Innovative Edtech Enterprise by M.M. 
Pallam Raju India’s Union Minister for Human Resource Development in 2013.
