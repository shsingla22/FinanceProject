LEMON TREE HOTELS EXPANDS PRESENCE IN TELANGANA WITH 
SIGNING OF LEMON TREE HOTEL, KOMPALLY                
 
The signing takes the Company's portfolio in Telangana to nine hotels, including 
five in Hyderabad.  
New Delhi, 24th November  2026 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, one of India’s 
leading hospitality companies, has announced the signing of Lemon Tree Hotel, 
Kompally. The hotel will be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a 
wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
Located in the northern part of Hyderabad, Kompally is an emerging residential 
and commercial hub, benefiting from the city's expanding urban landscape. Its 
growing prominence makes it an attractive micro -market for business and leisure 
travellers seeking comfortable accommodation with convenient access to the 
wider Hyderabad region.  
The hotel will feature 96 well -appointed rooms, a restaurant, banquet, meeting/ 
conference room, swimming pool and fitness centre, along with other public areas, 
offering guests a comfortable and convenient stay.  
Commenting on the signing, Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, 
Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd.,  said, "Hyderabad's continued momentum as a business 
and commercial centre is creating opportunities beyond its established hubs. Our 
micro -market -led strategy enables us to identify these growth avenues and build 
a presence in locations with distinct demand driv ers. Our operational hotels in 
corporate hubs such as HITEC City and Gachibowli, the established residential and 
commercial neighbourhood of Banjara Hills and now the addition of a hotel in the 
fast-growing residential suburb of Kompally  reflect this approach. With this 
signing, our portfolio in Telangana will comprise nine hotels, including four 
operational and five upcoming properties."  
The hotel will be conveniently connected to key transport hubs. Gundla 
Pochampally Railway Station is approximately 7 km from the property, while Rajiv 
Gandhi International Airport, Hyderabad, is approximately 48 km away.
