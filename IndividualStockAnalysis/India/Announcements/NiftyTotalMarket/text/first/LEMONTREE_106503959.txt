Lemon Tree Hotels announces the signing of its new property in Madhya  
Pradesh  
 
New Delhi, 21st January  2026: Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -growing mid -
scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the signing of its latest property – 
Lemon Tree Premier , Omkareshwar , Madhya  Pradesh.  
 
The property shall be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited. This signing will further strengthen the 
company’s presence in Madhya  Pradesh , while catering to both business and leisure 
travellers for a fresh, vibrant, and value -driven experience .   
 
Property Highlights  
 
Lemon Tree Premier , Omkareshwar , Madhya Pradesh is located at the sacred town 
situated on an island in the Narmada River in Madhya Pradesh, home to one of the 12 
Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. Known for its spiritual energy, ancient temples, and serene river 
ghats, it is a major pilgrimage destination that attracts devotees seeking divine blessings 
and inner peace.   
 
The key features of the hotel will include:  
 
• 85 well-appointed rooms   
• A restaurant  
• A banquet hall and a meeting room  
• Recreational facilit ies includ ing a fitness center, a swimming pool  and a spa  
 
The Devi Ahilya Bai Holkar International Airport  is at a distance of 85 kms from the 
property  while the Indore Junction is about 81 kms from the property. The hotel is well 
connected by both public and private transport.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, Lemon 
Tree Hotels, commented, “With this signing, we are pleased to extend our business and 
leisure portfolio in Madhya Pradesh . Omkareshwar  is an ideal destination for pilgrims and 
travellers seeking divine blessings, spiritual reflection, and a deep connection with India’s 
ancient heritage . The state has four operational and 10 upcoming hotels ."
