NEWS RELEASE  
 
TILAKNAGAR INDUSTRIES TO ADD AMARA ARTISANAL PINK 
VODKA TO DISTRIBUTION  PORTFOLIO  
 
Mumbai, March 24, 2025: Leading India Made Foreign Liquor ( IMFL) maker Tilaknagar  
Industries (TI) (BSE: 507205) (NSE: TI) will under take the distribution of AMARA  Artisanal 
Pink Vodka, an innovative spirit being launched by Spaceman Spirits Lab Pvt Ltd 
(Spaceman) , the makers of SAMSARA Gin. The distribution by TI will be in the form of a 
royalty arrangement with Spaceman for distribution of Spaceman brands in certain states 
in India and abroad.  
The addition of AMARA  to TI’s distribution portfolio will further enrich the TI product 
bouquet that includes homegrown premium brands such as Monarch Legacy Edition 
Brandy, Mansion House Flandy and Courrier Napoleon Brandy. Spaceman brands, namely, 
SAMSARA Gin , SITARA Rum and AMARA Vodka will start leveraging  TI’s robust distribution 
network under the royalty arrangement  from Q1 FY26 . This collaboration will bolster sales 
of Spaceman’s premium craft spirits across markets in India and abroad , as well as expand 
TI’s luxury and super -premium portfolio which will now straddle across Brandy, Gin, Rum 
and Vodka , contributing additional business for TI.  
According to IWSR Drinks Market Analysis, India’s vodka consumption surged by 16.5  per 
cent in 2023, significantly outpacing historical trends. While value vodka still holds a 94  
per cent market share, the premium and ultra -premium categories are witnessing 
exponential growth, with super -premium vodka expanding at an annual rate of 19.6  per 
cent from 2022 to 2023. “Globally, craft spirits are experiencing a surge in popularity with 
flavored craft spirit s leading premium market innovations. India is fast catching up , with 
younger consumers and high -energy urban bars embracing colorful, Instagrammable 
cocktails. The global shift toward premiumization and flavored vodkas bodes well for the 
launch of India’s first artisanal luxury pink vodka AMARA ,” said Mr Amit Dahanukar, 
Chairman & Managing Director, Tilaknagar Industries Limited.  
Positioned in the super -premium category , AMARA  will compete with global vodka brands  
known for their smoothness and exceptional quality .  
“India is experiencing a post -COVID drinking renaissance. Consumers are seeking elevated 
experiences, occasion -driven consumption, unique flavors, and ultra -premium -quality 
spirits,” said Mr Aditya Aggarwal, Founder and MD of Spaceman Spirits Lab. “With AM ARA, 
we’re bringing craftsmanship and innovation into a category that is ripe for disruption”, he 
added.  
AMARA  is an opulent blend of five -times distilled spirits, crafted from handpicked 
Bangalore Blue Grapes and rice grains from India’s Deccan Plateau for an exceptionally 
smooth profile. It is infused with strawberries, passion fruit, peaches, citrus, rose petal s, 
lotus, and cherry blossoms, resulting in a delicate, fruit -forward profile and a signature 
pink hue. A first of its kind pink ruby fi ltration, enhances AMARA’s  brilliance, purity, and 
silky-smooth texture.

Priced between INR 2,500 and INR 4,500 (depending on local state taxes) for a 750 ml 
decanter, AMARA  will be available in select premium retail stores, high -end bars, and 
luxury hotels across Goa, Maharashtra and Karnataka, Haryana, Delhi, Rajasthan in a 
phased roll -out. Supplies to international markets such as Global Travel Retail, UAE, UK 
and Singapore will commence  in the second half of FY26.  
Founded by Mr Aditya Aggarwal, Ms Riddhi Aggarwal, Ms Sapna Aggarwal and Mr Jai 
Chopra, Spaceman Spirits Lab is renowned for pushing boundaries in the craft spirits 
industry. Following the massive success of SAMSARA  gin, the company has built a 
reputation for innovation, craftsmanship, and luxury experiences, particularly in the 
flavored spirits segment.  
In view of Spaceman Spirits’ potential and to accelerate it's expansion, TI had, in 
September 2024, announced a follow -on investment of Rs 13.15  crore in Spaceman, in 
multiple tranches, that would increase TI’s shareholding in Spaceman to 20 per cent on a 
fully diluted  basis.  
About Tilaknagar Industries:  
Tilaknagar Industries (TI) is one of India’s leading alcoholic beverage companies , with a 
rich legacy spanning over 90 years. Founded in 1933 by Shri Mahadev L. Dahanukar as 
Maharashtra Sugar Mills, TI has, over the years, transformed in to a major player in the 
India Made Foreign Liquor (IMFL) industry having a manufacturing footprint  of 19 units 
across 12 states. TI’s brand portfolio straddles multiple categories, featuring two 
‘Millionaire’ brandy brands —Mansion House and Courrier Napoleon —along with a strong 
presence in whisky, rum, and gin through Mansion House Whisky, Madiraa Rum and Blue 
Lagoon Gin. Recently, TI has expanded into the luxury segment with Monarch Legacy 
Edition Brandy .
