Coforge and Chippenham RFC Partner to Support Rugby, Cricket and 
Community Development  
 
Partnership will help widen participation, nurture local talent and create opportunities for people 
across the community  
 
London, UK | Greater Noida, India | October 28, 2026  – Coforge Limited  (NSE: COFORGE), 
a global AI -native engineering services leader, and Chippenham Rugby Football Club  (RFC) 
announced a community partnership to support local talent and participation in rugby and cricket, 
while contributing to the wider community.  The partnership was formally launched at Chippenham 
RFC’s home at Allington Fields  with representatives from Coforge, Chippenham RFC and invited 
stakeholders.  
 
Under the theme “Raising Our Game. Together.” , the partnership brings together two 
organisations committed to investing in people and enabling long -term development. Coforge will 
support Chippenham RFC’s community role across rugby and cricket, including its work to encourage 
participation, nurture t alent and create opportunities for people of different ages and abilities.  
 
Founded in 1898, Chippenham RFC is a member -run community club with more than 1,000 members 
and the support of over 100 volunteers. Its programmes span senior men’s and women’s rugby, 
junior, girls’ and mini rugby, as well as mixed ability  touch and walking rugby. The club also supports 
cricket at its 22 -acre Allington Fields site, which includes a cricket ground and pavilion alongside rugby 
pitches, training areas, an all -weather multi -sports pitch and clubhouse facilities.  
 
Beyond sport, Chippenham RFC works with local schools and community organisations to encourage 
participation and support development. Its long -running activities include the Chippenham Schools 
Tag Rugby Festival, which has previously brought together more than 200 local pupils. The club has 
also helped develop ed over 60  players who have represented their countries at full international, 
student or schools level.  
 
“At Coforge, we believe that lasting progress begins with investing in people and creating opportunities 
for them to grow. Chippenham RFC has built a strong community platform that nurtures local talent, 
encourages participation and helps individuals devel op confidence, teamwork and life skills,” said John 
Speight, Executive Director  & President  and UK and Europe Geo Business Leader, Coforge. “Through 
this partnership, we look forward to supporting the club’s work across rugby and cricket and 
contributing to the continued development of the local community, both on and off the field.”  
 
“We are delighted to welcome Coforge as our Community Partner. Their commitment to developing 
people and creating meaningful opportunities closely reflects the values and philosophy of our club,” 
said Kevin Thornton, Chairman, Chippenham RFC. “This partners hip will support our continued efforts 
to widen participation, nurture local talent and strengthen the positive contribution we make across 
the community.”
