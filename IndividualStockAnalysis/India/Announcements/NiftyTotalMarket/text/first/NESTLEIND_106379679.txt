Nestlé India rolls -out GST rate reduction benefits for consumers  
 
 
Nestlé India welcomes the recent amendments in the Goods and Services Tax (GST) rates  announced by the 
Government of India . It will pass on the benefits directly to consumers, from September 22, 2025  onwards.  
 
Mr. Manish Tiwary, Chairman and Managing Director of Nestlé India, stated , "The GST rate reduction is a positive 
step for consumers in India, as it will stimulate consumption and  contribute to the overall growth of the economy . Nestlé 
has been an integral part of India for over 113 years  and c onsumers are at the heart of our business . We remain 
committed to the single purpose of serving consumers in all possible ways.”  
 
To ensure transparency, Nestlé India has communicated the benefits through announcements  in national and regional 
newspapers.  These c hanges have also been communicated to wholesalers, distribut ors, and retail ers.
