(IST) through Video Conferencing or Other Audio Video Means (‘VC’/’OAVM’).

appointed by the Hon’ble Tribunal to scrutinize the e-voting conducted at the meeting of the equity 
shareholders of the Company.

The Chairman of the meeting informed the equity shareholders that physical attendance at the meeting is 
not required and hence, the facility for the appointment of the proxies is not available for this meeting. The 
Chairman informed the equity shareholders that the Company had provided the facility to cast their votes 
electronically through NSDL on the resolution set forth in the Notice. The Chairman also informed that 
voting by electronic means was also available during the meeting to those equity shareholders who had not 
already voted by means of remote e-voting.   
 
 
 
 
 
 
The Notice convening equity shareholders was taken as read as the same was already circulated to the 
equity shareholders whose names appeared in the Register of Members, i.e. September 04, 2026.  
 
Chairman informed the equity shareholders that the Scheme, if approved by the equity shareholders of the 
Company, will be subject to subsequent approval of the Hon'ble Tribunal and such other approvals, 
permissions and sanctions from any other regulatory or statutory authorities, as may be deemed necessary.  
 
The Chairman requested the scrutinizer to confirm the quorum. On confirmation by the scrutinizer on the 
requisite quorum being present, the Chairman called the meeting to order.  
 
The Chairman thereafter briefed the equity shareholders on the salient features and rationale of the proposed 
Scheme. 
 
The resolution set out in the Notice, as reproduced below, was recommended for consideration by Chairman 
and approval of the Equity Shareholders through the e-voting system.  
 
Approval of Scheme of Merger by Absorption of Scheme of Merger by Absorption of Lloyds Infrastructure 
& Construction Limited (‘First Transferor Company’), Metalfab Hightech Private Limited (‘Second 
Transferor Company’) and Techno Industries Private Limited (‘Third Transferor Company’) with Lloyds 
Engineering Works Limited (‘Transferee Company’) and their respective shareholders (‘Scheme’) under 
Section 230 to Section 232 of the Companies Act, 2013 (‘the Act’) and rules made thereunder.  
 
The members who had registered in advance with the Company as speakers were then invited to ask 
questions. The Company, responded to the queries raised by the equity shareholders and provided 
clarifications.  
 
The Chairman informed the equity shareholders that the Scheme shall be considered approved if two 
conditions are met:  
1. The resolution must be approved by a majority in number representing three-fourths in value of the 
equity shareholders present and voting, in terms of Section 230(6) of the Act; and  
 
2. In accordance with the SEBI Master Circular dated June 20, 2023, the votes cast by public shareholders 
in favour of the resolution must exceed the votes cast by public shareholders against it.  
In compliance with the provisions of the Act and SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (‘LODR Regulations’), the facility of remote e-voting as well as e-voting at this meeting 
was provided to all the equity shareholders in proportion to their voting rights as on the cut-off date of i.e. 
Friday, October 09, 2026. The remote e-voting facility commenced on Monday, October 12, 2026 from 
9:00 A.M. (IST) and ended on Thursday, October 15, 2026 at 5:00 P.M. (IST).   
 
 
 
 
The Chairman informed the equity shareholders that the e-voting results along with the Scrutinizer’s Report 
would be communicated to the Stock Exchanges within two working days from the conclusion of the 
Meeting and would also be placed on the websites of the Company, NSDL, and the stock exchanges.  
The Chairman, thereafter, thanked the equity shareholders for attending and participating at the meeting 
and requested the equity shareholders who had not voted earlier, to complete e-voting in the next 15 minutes. 
Thereafter, the meeting concluded at 12.25 PM (IST) after being open for 15 minutes for e-voting to be 
completed by the equity shareholders.
