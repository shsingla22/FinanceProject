 Prestige Group Expands MMR Footprint with ₹6,000 Crore Thane Residential 
Development  
 
Mumbai, July  29, 2026:  Prestige Group, one of India's leading real estate developers, has 
entered into a strategic partnership to develop a landmark residential project in Thane. Spread 
across approximately 14.6 acres with an estimated GDV of ₹6,000 crore, the project will have 
a developable potential of over 5 million square feet, making it one of the largest residential 
developments in the micro -market.  
 
The project will offer a thoughtfully planned mix of residences designed to cater to a diverse 
range of homebuyers, including young professionals, first -time buyers and growing families. 
Complementing the residential offering will be a vibrant retail offer ing, creating a convenient 
live-work -shop environment for residents and the surrounding community.  
 
Strategically located near Kolshet –Balkum Road in Thane, the development enjoys excellent 
connectivity to key business districts, major road networks, educational institutions, 
healthcare facilities and established social infrastructure. Backed by signific ant infrastructure 
investments and strong end -user demand, the location has emerged as one of the Mumbai 
Metropolitan Region's most sought -after residential destinations.  
 
Designed in line with Prestige Group's commitment to quality, innovation and customer -
centric development, the project will feature contemporary architecture, expansive 
landscaped open spaces, world -class lifestyle amenities and sustainable design principl es. The 
development aims to set new benchmarks for integrated urban living while delivering an 
exceptional residential experience.  
  
Commenting on the partnership, Mr. Irfan Razack, Chairman & Managing Director, 
Prestige Group , said: "This project marks another significant milestone in Prestige Group's 
continued expansion in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region. Thane has emerged as one of the region's 
most dynamic residential markets, driven by robust infrastructure development and sustaine d housing 
demand. We are delighted to create an integrated community that reflects our commitment to quality, 
thoughtful design, timely delivery and customer satisfaction. We look forward to bringing this 
landmark development to market in the near future  and are confident it will be well received by 
discerning homebuyers in the region."  
 
The collaboration combines Prestige Group's extensive experience in delivering large -scale 
residential and mixed -use developments across India with strong local presence and market 
knowledge of the local developer partner. Together, the partners are commit ted to creating a 
landmark destination that will contribute meaningfully to the evolving urban landscape of 
Thane.  
 
The project is expected to be developed in phases, subject to the requisite statutory and 
regulatory approvals.
