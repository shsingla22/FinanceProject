bp signs strategic partnership with Wipro to build best -in-class 
finance operations  
 
LONDON |  BENGALURU, India | E. BRUNSWICK, N.J.  – Nov. 25, 2026 - bp has today 
announced a strategic supplier partnership with Wipro Limited (NYSE: WIT, BSE: 507685, NSE: 
WIPRO), a leading AI -powered technology services and consulting company, to transform its core 
finance operations as it continues to strengthen perform ance and improve long -term cost 
competitiveness.  
 
The collaboration will bring together Wipro’s expertise in AI, automation, and transformation to 
drive greater efficiency in bp’s finance operations and improve processes. Under the agreement, 
around 3,000 bp employees supporting finance operations in Indi a, Hungary and Malaysia will 
move to Wipro.  
 
Kate Thomson, Chief Financial Officer, bp,  said, “We believe this partnership with Wipro is the 
best path forward for bp and our people as we build a simpler, stronger, more valuable bp. It 
moves us closer to best -in-class finance operations while giving our people opportunities to 
continue to dev elop their skills. Wipro brings specialist digital capabilities and investment that will 
help us make better use of finance operations technology, deliver value for the business more 
quickly and simplify how we work.”  
 
Srini Pallia, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director, Wipro Limited  said, “This 
landmark engagement with bp underscores our commitment to leveraging our deep domain 
expertise and Wipro Intelligence to drive large -scale technology transformation. Through this 
collaboration, we aim to unlock measurable business outcomes for  bp in the AI -first world. I also 
welcome the bp employees joining the Wipro family and look forward to integrating their 
specialized skills with our consulting -led, AI -powered strategy.”  
 
The new operating model is expected to take effect in 2027, subject to applicable conditions. bp 
will retain overall accountability for standards, controls, data and technology governance, with 
PwC providing controls oversight.
