Lemon Tree Hotels launches its 12th property in  Rajasthan with  
Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels, Jaipur  
 
The hotel features 50 well-appointed rooms , a restaurant , a fitness center  and 
other public areas . 
 
New Delhi, 9th October 2025:  Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -
growing mid -scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the launch of its 
latest property  – Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels, Jaipur . This marks the brand’s 
third property in the city, further strengthening its presence in Rajasthan’s capital.  
 
This managed  hotel features 50 well -appointed rooms, complemented by Keys 
Café – a multi -cuisine  restaurant , and  a well -equipped fitness center , offering 
guests an efficient, comfortable, and value -driven stay experience.  
 
Jaipur, popularly known as the Pink City, is the capital of Rajasthan and one of 
India’s most vibrant and culturally rich destinations. The city earned its iconic 
nickname in 1876 when its buildings were painted pink to welcome the Prince of 
Wales, to symbolise hospitality. Renowned for its stunning architecture, Jaipur is 
home to iconic landmarks such as Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Amber Fort, and Jantar 
Mantar – a UNESCO World Heritage Site.  
 
Recently, Jaipur was ranked 5th among the world’s best cities for travellers in the 
Travel + Leisure “World’s Best Awards 2025”, surpassing celebrated global 
destinations like Florence. The city’s lively markets, traditional handicrafts, 
jewellery, colourf ul textiles  and regional cuisine  further add to its charm.  
 
Commenting on the opening Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, Lemon Tree 
Hotels said, “ Jaipur continues to be one of India’s most sought -after destinations, 
attracting both leisure and business travellers. With this new opening, our third in 
the city, we are delighted to strengthen our presence in Rajasthan and offer guests 
the brand’s signa ture combination of comfort, warmth, a nd value in a city that 
epitomis es culture and hospitality .”
