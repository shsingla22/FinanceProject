VIDA INTRODUCES NEW VARIANT – VIDA VX2 GO FB, EXPANDING 
EVOOTER PORTFOLIO TO OFFER MULTIPLE CHARGING OPTIONS  
  
 Expanding the VIDA EVOOTER VX2 lineup to give every rider the power to choose between direct -plug 
convenience and removable -battery flexibility.  
 Available at an attractive price of INR 1,13,000/ -* in New Delhi  
  
VIDA , powered by Hero,  today expanded choices within its electric mobility portfolio with the 
introduction of the  VIDA EVOOTER VX2 Go FB (3.1 kWh)  featuring direct -plug charging. Joining 
VIDA ’s existing lineup of removable battery variants, this new option advances the brand’s vision of 
'GHAR GHAR  EVOOTER' by offering customers complete freedom to choose how they power their 
ride. Whether riders prefer the ease of plugging straight into a standard wall socket or also need  the 
versatility of a removable -battery, VIDA now delivers a tailored solution  for every Indian household. 
Priced at INR 1,13,000/ -* (ex -showroom, New Delhi) , the direct -plug VIDA EVOOTER VX2 Go FB 
delivers an effortless blend of practical range, everyday performance, and seamless charging 
flexibility.  
The VIDA EVOOTER VX2 portfolio  includes:  
 
VIDA VX2 Variant  Range**  
VIDA VX2 Go 2.2 kWh  IDC-certified range of 93 km 
VIDA VX2 Go FB (3.1 kWh)  IDC-certified range of 128 km  
VIDA VX2 Go 3.4 kWh  IDC-certified range of 146 km 
VIDA VX2 Plus 3.4 kWh  IDC-certified range of 146 km 
VIDA VX2  Plus 4.4 kWh  IDC-certified range of 187 km  
 
 
Powered by a  3.1 kWh fixed battery , the VIDA EVOOTER VX2 Go FB offers an IDC -certified range of  128 km . 
It is driven by an advanced 6 kW swing -arm motor delivering a top speed of  70 km/h . Designed for everyday 
comfort and convenience, it features a long, comfortable seat, 27.2 litres of under -seat storage, a 4.3 -inch LCD 
with smartphone connectivity and turn -by-turn navigation, along with DC fast -charging that powers the 
battery from  0–80% in 65 minutes .   
  
Complementing its comprehensive product line -up, VIDA continues to strengthen its ownership ecosystem 
with an extensive network of  over 5,900 fast -charging stations  and 700+ service touchpoints  across the 
country. Alongside a flexible  Battery -as-a-Service (BaaS)  offering,  the VIDA EVOOTER VX2 Go FB  will be 
available across authorized dealerships nationwide  starting early August.
