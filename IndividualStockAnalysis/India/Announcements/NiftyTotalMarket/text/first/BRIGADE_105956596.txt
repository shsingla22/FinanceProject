PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Group Partners with abCoffee  
to Enhance Tenant Experience in its Bengaluru Office Spaces  
 
Bengaluru, August  16, 2024:  Brigade Group, one of India’s leading  property developers, has partnered  
with abCoffee , grab -and-go specialty coffee,  to establish outlets across its commercial office spaces in 
Bengaluru. Through this partnership, abCoffee will open 6 outlets within Brigade properties, enhancing the 
café experience for tenants and visitors alike.   
 
"We partnered with abCoffee in order to enhance the F&B offerings at our office parks. abCoffee is able 
to retro fit into operational  buildings without requiring additional water or gas points. We find the brand 
youthful, and relevant, providing high quality F&B at reasonable prices.  We are excited to  take another step 
towards enhancing the  experience across our campuses .” said Arvind Ra o, Vice Presiden t – Commercial 
Business , Brigade  Enterprises Limited . 
 
"We are thrilled to partner with Brigade Group . This partnership between abCoffee, and one of India's 
most reputed developers, Brigade Group, aims to enhance the premium beverage choices for India's evolving 
lifestyle. Brigade has been instrumental in developing neighbourhoods, and abCoffee is the neigh bourhood 
coffee spot. Together, we look forward to being a part of the daily lives of Bangalore's residents, whether 
at home, work, or leisure." said Abhijeet Anand, Founder, abCoffee . 
 
abCoffee will introduce technology enabled, novel brewing methods to guarantee a combination of consistent 
quality and quick service, specifically designed for busy professionals and visitors in our premises . With a 
shared vision of adding value to the business atmosphere, Brigade Group and abCoffee are confident of a 
significant impact on the coffee culture in business and beyond.  
 
About Brigade Group:  Established in 1986, Brigade Group with close to four  decades of expertise in 
building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and winning their customers’ trust. Brigade has 
developed many landmark buildings and transformed skylines across the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, 
Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, and Gift City, Gujarat with developments across 
Residential, Office, Retail, Hospitality and Education Sectors.
