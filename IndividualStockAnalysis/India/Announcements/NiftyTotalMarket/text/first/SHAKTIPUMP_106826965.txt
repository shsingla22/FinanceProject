Mr. Dinesh Patidar  
Chairman  Shakti Pumps (India) Limited Chairman, Mr. Dinesh Patidar, commented on the 
company’s performance:  
“We are pleased to report a healthy H1 FY27 performance, with revenue from operations 
growing 24.5% year -on-year to ₹ 16,047 Mn, driven by strong execution on the ground. Solar 
pump installations rose 29.3% year -on-year to 51,543 units during H1 FY27, reflecting our 
continued role in expanding access to clean, reliable irrigation for farmers across the country.  
Margins during the period moderated due to lower realisations, higher raw material costs and 
elevated logistics expenses linked to prevailing geopolitical conditions. However, we are 
addressing these pressures through cost optimisation, operating efficienc ies and disciplined 
execution. Despite this, we delivered a PAT of ₹ 901 Mn and cash profit of ₹ 1,058 Mn in H1 
FY27, underscoring the resilience of our business.  
Our order book stood at ₹ 7,510 Mn as of 2 3rd October 2026. We see significant opportunities 
under PM -KUSUM 2.0, which is expected to be announced during the ongoing quarter, along 
with continuous order inflow from Magel Tyala and other state programmes. Beyond India, 
we see a strong growth opportunit y in our exports business, as demand for energy -efficient 
and solar pumping solutions continues to rise across international markets, where we hold a 
strong presence. At the same time, we are investing around ₹ 17,000 Mn to strengthen our 
manufacturing base and backward integration. I am happy to share that 0.5 GW of our 
planned 2.2 GW solar module capacity has commenced trial production in October 2026, with 
the 2.2 GW DCR solar cell facility to be commissioned  by FY28.  
Along with our diversification in solar rooftop and EV motors, controllers & chargers, these 
investments position Shakti as an integrated renewable energy solutions company. Our 
priorities remain clear: execute our order book efficiently, improve margins, strengthen cash 
conversion and bring our new capacities online on schedule.”

Financial Highlights  
● Consolidated Revenue from Operations for H1 FY27 grew 24.5% YoY to ₹ 16,047 Mn; Q2 FY27 Revenue from 
Operations stood at ₹ 7,460 Mn, up 12.0% YoY  
● EBITDA for H1 FY27 stood at ₹ 1,469 Mn, with an EBITDA margin of 9.2%, while Q2 FY27 EBITDA was ₹ 640 Mn, 
translating into a margin of 8. 6%. Profitability during the quarter was impacted by softer realisations, higher raw 
material costs and elevated logistics expenses amid prevailing geopolitical uncertainties. Despite these near -term 
headwinds, margins have sustained  on a sequential basis. The Company continues to focus on cost optimisation, 
operating efficiencies and disciplined execution to mitigate these pressures and support margin improvement.

About Shakti Pumps (India) Limited  
SPIL founded in 1982 as a partnership firm and later converted to a public limited company in 1995, manufactures solar pumps,  energy -
efficient stainless -steel submersible pumps, pressure booster pumps, pump -motors, and other products. SPIL is the only comp any that 
manufactures a wide range of products for solar pump installation in -house, including Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs), Structures, Motors, 
Invertors, and so on. Pithampur, Madhya Pradesh, is home to four manufacturing facilities with a combined a nnual capacity of 500,000 
pumps & motors, 400,000 VFDs & Inverters, and 200,000 structures. Shakti Pumps is at the forefront of sustainable innovation and reliability 
in solar pumping solutions, while also being environmentally responsible. The company has  been at the forefront of transforming the 
agriculture sector through solar pump technology. All Shakti submersible pumps are based on Stainless Steel (SS), which is a testimony to 
the latest technology and quality in manufacturing. Notably, Shakti Pumps h as the distinction of being India’s first 5 -star rated pump 
manufacturer, supplying its products to more than 100 countries across the globe and manufacturing its own solar pumps, motor s, 
structures, controllers & VFDs. Shakti Pumps is committed to helping  India meet its energy goals.
