This is to inform that the Company has been granted another patent in India. This strengthens the overall 
IP portfolio of the Company.  
 
The patent subject area is “System for switching and handover between one or more intelligent 
conversational agents”. 
 
This patent is aimed at enabling seamless switching and handover between different intelligent systems 
or services (such as chatbots, conversational agents, AR/VR agents), ensuring uninterrupted user 
experience across digital platforms. It intelligently determines which system or agent is better suited to 
handle a specific task based on multiple factors such as the intent / complexity / preference of a user 
query, past performance and cost of engagement of agents, etc. and ensures a smooth switching between 
multiple AI systems, without losing context of the conversation.  
  
This is our 12th patent grant out of a total IP portfolio of 36 Patents and is one of the multiple patents 
filed by Affle in the areas of Artificial Intelligence and/or Intelligent Agents. This would further 
enhance our AI-driven capabilities to power next-gen customer-centric technologies, allowing ads to 
adapt dynamically based on user behaviour and various device transitions with multi-channel 
attribution. 
 
The inventors of this patent include Anuj Khanna Sohum, Charles Yong and Madhusudana 
Ramakrishna.
