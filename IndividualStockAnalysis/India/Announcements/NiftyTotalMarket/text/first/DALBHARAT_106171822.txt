Re: Approval of Scheme of Arrangement between Vinay Cement Limited and Dalmia 
Cement (North East) Limited, subsidiaries of Dalmia Bharat Limited (“the Company”) 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
We would like to inform that the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal, Guwahati Bench 
(“NCLT”) vide its Order dated February 21, 2025 (“Order”), has approved the Scheme of 
Arrangement between Dalmia Cement (North East) Limited (“Transferee Company”, a material 
subsidiary of the Company), Vinay Cement Limited (“Transferor Company”, a subsidiary of the 
Company) and their respective shareholders and creditors (“Scheme”), involving demerger and 
transfer of the cement and mining business operation undertaking of the Transferor Company to 
the Transferee Company, with effect from the Appointed Date i.e. March 31, 2023 under Sections 
230 to 232 of the Companies Act, 2013. The Company is not a party to the Scheme and no shares 
are proposed to be issued by/ to the Company. Both subsidiaries shall continue to remain 
subsidiaries of the Company post effectiveness of the Scheme. The certified copy of the Order has 
been received by Dalmia Cement (North East) Limited today and the same has been intimated to 
the Company on March 12, 2025 at 4:45 PM. 
 
The Scheme will be effective upon completion of legal formalities including filing of the certified 
true copy of the Order by Transferor & Transferee companies with the Registrar of Companies, 
Guwahati. 
 
You are requested to kindly take the same on record.
