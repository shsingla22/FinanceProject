Gokul Agro reports  strong profitability in  Q2FY27  & H1FY27  
    Revenue up 13% to ₹7,481  crore; EBITDA up 41% YoY and PAT up 40% YoY  in Q2FY27  
Revenue up 10% to ₹ 12,763  crore; EBITDA up 4 6% YoY and PAT up 54% YoY in H1FY27  
 
Ahmedabad, India | 2 8 October  2026: Gokul Agro Resources Limited (NSE: GOKULAGRO; BSE: 
539725; ISIN: INE314T01033), one of India’s leading integrated edible oil, castor oil and agri -
processing companies, announced its financial results for the quarter & half year ended 30 Sep 2026. 
The Company delivered a strong  quarter with profitability growth outpacing revenue growth, 
supported by product diversification, improved operating efficiencies, value -added product launches 
and strong  contributions  from exports and non -edible businesses.

Mr. Kanubhai J. Thakkar, Chairman & Managing Director, said:  
“We are pleased with our performance in Q2FY27 and H1FY27, reflecting the strength of our integrated business model and disci plined 
execution. In H1FY27, our revenue grew 10% YoY to ₹12,763 crore, while EBITDA and PAT grew significantly by 46% and 54%, 
respectively.  
 During the quarter, we continued to strengthen our operating platform through higher volumes, expansion of our branded B2C pr esence, 
a growing export footprint and contribution from newer businesses such as biodiesel. Our diversified manufacturing footprin t and 
sourcing capabilities continue to provide us with the flexibility to efficiently serve domestic and international markets.  
Going ahead, our focus will remain on disciplined capacity expansion, strengthening our consumer franchise, increasing value -added 
products and improving operating efficiencies. With planned capacity additions across our manufacturing locations, renewable -energy 
investments and continued investments in our growth platforms, we remain confident in building a stronger and more diversifie d business 
over the long term.”  
About Gokul Agro Resources Limited  
One of India’s leading integrated agro -processing and edible oil companies with a diversified presence across edible oils, 
castor oil & derivatives, specialty fats, vanaspati, feed meals and biodiesel. The Company operates an integrated value 
chain spanning sourcing, crushing, refining, storag e, branded retail products and exports, serving customers across India 
and international markets. With 4 strategically located manufacturing facilities, robust logistics infrastructure and a 
diversified product portfolio, Gokul Agro remains focused on quality, scale, sustainability and long -term value creation.
