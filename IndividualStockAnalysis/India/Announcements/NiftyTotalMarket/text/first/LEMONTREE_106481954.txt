Lemon Tree Hotels announces execution of HOA for a new property in Andhra 
Pradesh  
 
New Delhi, 29th December 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels announces execution of Hotel 
Operation Agreement (‘HOA’) in respect of upcoming hotel at Tirupati , Andhra Pradesh  by 
Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited. 
Carnations Hotels Private Limited shall operate the hotel under brand name “Lemon Tree 
Suites” subject to execution of licence agreement by Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
 
Property Highlights  
Lemon Tree Suites, Tirupati is located in one of India’s most revered spiritual destinations, 
best known for the Sri Venkateswara Temple, which draws millions of devotees every year. 
Nestled in the Eastern Ghats of Andhra Pradesh, the town beautifully blends deep -rooted 
tradition with natural sere nity, offering a sense of calm, devotion, and cultural richness. 
Tirupati is also known for its well -organized pilgrim infrastructure, lush hill landscapes, 
and sacred rituals that create a powerful, immersive spiritual experience for visitors from 
across the world.  
 
The key features of the hotel will include:  
 
• 228 well-appointed rooms  and suites  
• A restaurant  
• A banquet and a meeting room  
• Recreational facilities include a swimming pool  and a spa  
 
The T irupati  International Airport, is approximately 1 0 kms away from the property, while 
the Tirupati Railway Station  is about 6.3 kms away. The hotel is well connected by both 
public and private transport.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, Lemon 
Tree Hotels, commented, “With this signing, we are pleased to extend our pilgrimage 
portfolio in a place that holds deep spiritual resonance. Moreover, the vibrant tourism, rich 
cuisine, and growing hotel infrastructure make Andhra Pradesh  a magnet for tourism 
travellers seeking unforgettable experiences. The state has six operational and three  
upcoming hotels ."
