Sub:  Recognition of Jana Small Finance Bank's CSR Initiatives by the Government of Haryana  
Ref: Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing 
Regulations”)  
We wish to inform you that Jana Small Finance Bank Limited ("th e Bank") has been specially acknowledged by            
Shri Nayab Singh Saini, Hon'ble Chief Minister of Haryana, for its contribution towards strengthening healthcare 
infrastructure across district hospitals in the State through its Corporate Social Responsibility ( "CSR") initiatives.  
The recognition was conferred during the inauguration and launch of key healthcare initiatives undertaken by the 
Government of Haryana on 29th May 2026 at the PWD Rest House, Sector 1, Panchkula, Haryana. The event was 
graced by Shri Naya b Singh Saini, Hon'ble Chief Minister of Haryana, and presided over by Smt. Arti Singh Rao, 
Hon'ble Minister of Health, Medical Education & Research, Haryana.  
During the event, the Government of Haryana recognised both HDFC Bank and Jana Small Finance Bank  for 
undertaking meaningful and impactful CSR projects aimed at strengthening district hospitals and advancing healthcare services across the State.  
The token of appreciation on behalf of the Bank was received by Mr. Shrinivas Murty, President & Head –  Branch 
Banking & Marketing.  The recognition specifically acknowledges the Bank's healthcare -focused CSR interventions 
implemented during FY 2025- 26, including:  
• Installation of a 500 MA Digital X -Ray Machine at Civil Hospital, Panipat;  
• Provision of 20 Syringe  Infusion Pumps for the ICU and HDU at Civil Hospital, Karnal; and  
• Installation of a GLUQUANT A1C HPLC Machine at Lok Nayak Jaiprakash (LNJP) Hospital, Kurukshetra.  
This recognition from the Hon'ble Chief Minister and the Government of Haryana reflects t he positive social impact 
created through the Bank's CSR programmes and reinforces the Bank's commitment to supporting community development through sustainable healthcare interventions. The acknowledgement also underscores the 
importance of collaborative engagement with State Governments and other stakeholders to improve healthcare 
access and infrastructure for underserved communities.
