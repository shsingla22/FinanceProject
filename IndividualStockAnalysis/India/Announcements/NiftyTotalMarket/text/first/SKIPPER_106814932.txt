PRESS RELEASE  
 
 
 
Skipper Limited appoints Mr. R. N. Nayak, former CMD 
of Power Grid Corporation of India, as Non-Executive 
Independent Director  
 
Adds four decades of power-sector leadership to the Board as Skipper scales its transmission and 
distribution business 
 
National, 12 August 2026: Skipper Limited (BSE: 538562 | NSE: SKIPPER) today announced the 
appointment of Mr. R. N. Nayak as a Non- Executive Independent Director on its Board, e Ưective 
12 October 2026. 
Mr. Nayak is the former Chairman and Managing Director of Power Grid Corporation of India 
Limited (PGCIL). He has over 40 years of experience in senior roles at premier Central Public 
Sector Undertakings in the power sector, including PGCIL and NTPC. At PGCIL, he led a team of 
about 10,000 employees through an extraordinary turnaround, more than doubling the 
company’s assets, revenue and proﬁt in just over three years. 
Mr. Nayak currently serves on the boards of several companies, including Odisha Power 
Transmission Corporation Limited, TP Northern Odisha Distribution Limited, TP Western Odisha 
Distribution Limited, GRIDCO Limited, Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited, Yash High Voltage 
Limited, IMP Power, OHPC, GEDCOL, Powerica Limited and Future Smartec (OPC) Private 
Limited. He is also a consultant to the World Bank Group in Washington, advising on international 
cross-border projects, and provides services to leading national and international consulting 
companies. He holds a Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering from IIT Kharagpur and a 
Bachelor’s degree from NIT Rourkela. 
Mr. Sharan Bansal, Director, Skipper Limited , said: “We are delighted to welcome Mr. Nayak to 
the Board. Mr. Nayak has shaped national transmission build-out from the inside. His experience 
across every facet of the sector, from technology adoption to large-scale execution and grid 
operations, will strengthen the Board’s oversight as Skipper pursues growth in India and abroad. 
His perspective will be invaluable to Skipper and its Board, and we look forward to working closely 
with him to create long-term, sustainable value for our shareholders and wider stakeholders.”
