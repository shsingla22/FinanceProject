TCS and Rezolve Ai forge first -of-its-kind partnership to scale 
Agentic Commerce  globally  
Together, they will help retailers embed agentic -AI into core commerce workflows and drive enterprise -wide 
transformation at scale  
NEW YORK | MUMBAI , May 12 , 2026:  Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS), a global 
leader in IT services, consulting, and business solutions has announced a global strategic partnership with 
Rezolve Ai, a leading AI -native commerce infrastructure provider. This partnership marks TCS’ entry into the 
agentic AI commerce market. Al ong with Rezolve Ai, TCS will  help retailers worldwide embed A I into core 
commerce workflows at enterprise scale.  
Under the partnership, TCS will enable retail enterprises to leverage Rezolve Ai’s proprietary intelligent 
commerce platform, brainpowa ™. These specialized agentic solutions will facilitate retailers to create AI -led 
experiences across conversational commerce, intelligent discovery, and agentic checkout.  As part of the 
collaboration, TCS and Rezolve  Ai will jointly enable global retail enterprises to integrate production -grade AI 
solutions within their core commerce workflows.  
Daniel M. Wagner, Chief Executive Officer, Rezolve Ai, said , “This partnership is a major commercial 
milestone for Rezolve Ai. TCS brings extraordinary reach, deep enterprise trust, and global execution 
capability. Together, we are creating a clear pathway to take agentic commerce from platform innovation to 
enterpr ise‑scale deployment. We believe, this collaboration will significantly accelerate enterprise adoption 
of our technology and further strengthen Rezolve Ai’s position as a category leader in AI -powered commerce.”  
Shekar Krishnan, Head of  Retail , UK and Europe , TCS, said,  “Enterprises are increasingly seeking AI solutions 
that deliver measurable business outcomes at scale. Our partnership with Rezolve Ai, combines TCS’ global 
transformation capabilities with a specialized AI -powered commerce platform built for real -world ent erprise 
deployment. Together, we aim to help customers modernize digital commerce in ways that improve customer 
experience and commercial performance. This partnership is a key component of TCS’ s trategy to create an 
integrated AI ecosystem bringing together in -house innovation with strategic partnerships to industrialize AI 
and deliver scalable, real -world outcomes for enterprises.”  
This partnership combines TCS’ deep enterprise relationships, global delivery network, scale, and 
go-to-market capabilities with Rezolve Ai’s native technology for the retail sector. TCS will help organizations 
scale AI‑led commerce solutions reliably and responsibly and achieve enterprise ‑grade deployment 
capabilities by leveraging Rezolve Ai’s platform innovation across global markets. Global retailers can also 
experience this unique agentic commerce solution hands -on across TCS’ global network of Pace Port ™ 
innovation centers.  
TCS is helping businesses across various industries realize the advantages of AI by incorporating Human+AI 
decision -making systems into their operations. Collaborating with AI -focused firms like Resolve AI are central 
to TCS’ mission of making AI practical  for organisations and ensuring they gain tangible, large -scale benefits 
from AI technology.
