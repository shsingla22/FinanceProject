Brief Profile of Mr. Krunal Bhatt: 
Mr. Krunal Bhatt is a qualified Company Secretary and legal professional wi th over 19 years of 
extensive experience in corporate governance, regulatory complianc e, and legal advisory 
functions across leading listed companies.  
He possesses strong expertise in SEBI regulations, Companies Act compliance, capital market 
transactions, mergers and acquisitions, and corporate restructuring. He  also brings significant 
experience in overseeing RBI regulatory compliances, ESG initiatives , legal and IPR matters as 
part of overall professional experience. 
He is presently associated with KPI Green Energy Limited (part of KP Group) in a leadership role, 
overseeing corporate governance, secretarial compliances, and board processes at the Group 
level. Prior to joining KPI Green Energy Limited, he was associated wi th Arvind Limited as 
Company Secretary. Earlier in his career, he has worked with reputed listed companies such as 
Astral Limited and Adani Power Limited, where he made significant contributions in the areas of 
corporate governance, Mergers & Acquisitions, capital market transacti ons, and regulatory 
compliance. 
Mr. Bhatt is a commerce & law graduate and a member of the Institute of Company Secretaries 
of India (ICSI) holding membership No. A20162.
