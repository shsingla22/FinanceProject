Dear Sir/Madam, 
Under regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that a fire accident took place at midnight, i.e., 
early hours of Thursday, 29% May 2025, at a Godown located at Commercial property bearing 
No. 211-240, Survey No. 175, 184, 185, Block No. 22H, Phase — IV, Auto Nagar, Guntur 
District, Andhra Pradesh — 522001 
The cause of the fire is currently under investigation. Although the fire has been controlled, 
operations at the affected godown have been disrupted. We are relieved to report that there 
have been no injuries or casualties. The godown is adequately insured, and the insurance 
provider has been duly notified of the incident. While the company is determining the exact 
extent of the loss, preliminary estimates suggest that the damage may be Rs. 6 to 7 crores. 
Any further developments related to this incident will be communicated in due course.
