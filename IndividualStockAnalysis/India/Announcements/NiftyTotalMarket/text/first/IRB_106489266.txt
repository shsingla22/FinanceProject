IRB Infrastructure Trust receives Letter of Award (LoA) from NHAI for TOT-
18 Bundle  - Chandikhole Bhadrak NH16 section in the State of O disha  
Mumbai, January 6, 202 6: IRB Infrastructure Trust, Private InvIT sponsored by  India’s leading and 
the largest toll road  operator,  IRB Infrastructure Developers Ltd., has received a Letter of Award (LoA) 
from the NHAI for TOT -18 bundle  encompassing Chandikhole - Bhadrak NH-16 section in the State 
of Odisha.  
Commenting on the award,  Mr. Virendra D. Mhaiskar, Chairman & Managing Director, IRB 
Infrastructure Developers Ltd.  Said, “Receiving the LoA for our first -ever project in 
Odisha, i.e., TOT -18, enables us to proceed with the necessary steps, including 
execution of the Concession Agreement and achievement of Financial Closure within 
the stipulated timeframe, and formally onboarding the asset in to our portfolio. This award places us on a fast track to building an asset portfolio of Rs. 1 lakh crore in near 
future , the largest by any private toll road developer in India. ” He further said,  “The 
addition of TOT- 17 and TOT- 18 together will enhance the toll revenues by approx. 
Rs.1,000Crs in FY27 ”. 
Key Highlights: TOT -18 Concession:  
• Group’s Footprint:  Group’s foray in Odisha, 13th Indian State  for Company ; 2nd in Eastern 
India, after West Bengal (Palsit Dankuni NH -19 BOT Project ) 
• Project Length:  74.5 K ms (approx. 450 Lane Kms)  Chandikhole to Bhadrak NH -16 Corridor  
• Part of India’s prestigious Golden Quadrilateral Project  
• Scope:  Tolling and Operations & Maintenance (TOT)  
• Concession Period:  Revenue Linked Concession Period of 20 -years  
• Upfront Payment to the NHAI: Rs.3,087Crs   
IRB Group , post addition of this project : 
• Toll revenue  of the Portfolio: Expected substantial increase   
• Group ’s Asset Base:  To reach approx. Rs. 94,000Cr s 
• Market Share  in TOT segment :  Rises to  approx. 44% 
• O&M Order Book:  Expected to grow by  around Rs.1,600Crs   
• Share in the Golden Quadrilateral project:  Enhances to approx.16%   
About IRB Infrastructure Developers Ltd:  
 
At present, IRB Infrastructure Developers Ltd (IRB) is India’s first Integrated Multi -National 
Transport Infrastructure Developer in Roads & Highways segment. The Company has acquired 
ISO Certification in Quality (ISO 9001); Environment Management (ISO 14001), Occupational 
Health and Safety (ISO 45001) and IT Security (ISO 27001) from the ISOQAR, United Kingdom.

As the largest integrated private toll roads and highways infrastructure developer in India, IRB has 
an asset base of approx. Rs.94,000Crs in 13 States across the parent company and two InvITs.  
 
The Company has strong track record of constructing, tolling, operating, and maintaining around 
20,500 Lane Kms pan India in its existence of more than 25 years in India.    
 
The group has approx. 16% share (including TOT -18) in India’s prestigious Golden Quadrilateral 
Project and 12% share in India’s North South highway connectivity.  
 
After adding the TOT 18 project to IRB Group’s project portfolio (including Private and Public InvIT) 
will have 28 highway projects that include 18 BOT, 6 TOT, and 4 HAM projects.   
 
IRB Infrastructure Trust (“Private InvIT”):  
 
IRB Infrastructure Trust is an Infrastructure Investment Trust sponsored by IRB Infrastructure Developers Limited under the SEBI’s Infrastructure Investment Trusts Regulations 2014. Private 
InvIT, pursuant to the Concessions granted by the respective Nodal  Agencies, operates and manages 
11 highways assets having 8,763 Lane Kms and Enterprise Value of approx. Rs.62,000Crs in the States of Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Goa, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Telangana, and 
Madhya Pradesh. The Weighted Average Life of these Assets is 20 Years plus. Private InvIT is listed 
on NSE since 2023 under ticker “IRBIT”. For more information on Private InvIT, please visit
