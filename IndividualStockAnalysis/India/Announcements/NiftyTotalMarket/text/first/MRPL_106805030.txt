CHTU Fire Incident: MRPL Announces Support for Bereaved Family 
Mangaluru, 1 October 2026 
The Management of Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL) 
expresses its profound grief over the loss of Shri Manish Karkada in the unfortunate 
incident that occurred on 30 September 2026 in the Coker Hydrotreater Unit (CHTU) 
of MRPL. MRPL extends its heartfelt condolences to the bereaved family and to his 
near and dear ones. The Company stands in solidarity with the family during this 
difficult time. 
While no financial assistance can compensate for the loss of a precious life, MRPL has 
decided to extend financial support of ₹90 lakh to the family of the deceased. In 
addition, the family will be eligible for benefits of approximately ₹10 lakh under the 
Group Term Life Insurance (GTLI) and ₹20 lakh under the Workmen Compensation 
Policy (WCP), taking the overall financial support and insurance benefits to 
approximately ₹1.20 crore. 
Regarding Mediclaim insurance for the secondary workforce, the proposal for the 
same is being drafted and under discussion. 
The same will be finalized after discussing the matter with the Hon'ble Member of 
Parliament, Dakshina Kannada, and the respected DC Dakshina Kannada. MRPL 
expects the process relating to the proposed Mediclaim arrangement to be 
completed within approximately four weeks. 
MRPL reiterates its commitment to the safety, health and welfare of all personnel 
associated with its operations and will continue to extend all possible support to the 
affected family.
