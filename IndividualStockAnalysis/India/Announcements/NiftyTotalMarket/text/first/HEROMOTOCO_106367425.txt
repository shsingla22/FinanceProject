New Delhi, September 08 , 2025  
 
HERO MOTOCORP COMMENCES SALES OF THE ALL -NEW  XOOM 160   
 
USHERS A NEW ERA OF ‘SUPER SCOOTER ’ THAT MAKES EVERY DAY  
RIDE A NEW ADVENTURE  
 
Hero MotoCorp, the world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters, today 
announced the sales commencement of its much -awaited Xoom 160 across markets in India . 
Unveiled at the Bharat Mobility Show earlier this year , Xoom 160 instantly captured t he 
imagination of young riders and emerged  as one of  the most anticipated launches - ushering 
a new era of ‘Super Scooters ’.  
 
Crafted with power, precision and a bold design, Xoom 160 is built to reimagine everyday 
rides. With unmatched performance  and control  that go beyond a conventional scooter , this 
versatile ‘Super Scooter ’ is designed to dominate city rides, conquer challenging terr ains and 
transform everyday commute into adventure . Engineered with advanced technology, 
distinctive  design and a commanding presence on the road, Xoom 160 is meant for every 
rider who seeks not just mobility but a ride that’s truly liberating.  
 
Ashutosh Varma, Chief Business Officer, India Business Unit – Hero MotoCorp,  said, 
“Xoom 160 represents a new  era of category definin g ‘Super Scooters’ that combine  power, 
precision and adventure. Tailored for young riders who crave  performance, style and an 
experience that goes beyond  commuting, Xoom 160  is crafted f or seamless city rides and 
weekend getaways alike. The addition of Xoom 160 builds on our strong presence in the scooter 
segment as we continue to scale  the 'Xoom' brand with differentiated models such as  Xoom 
125cc and Xoom 110 cc. We are confident that Xoom 160  will redefine urban mobility , setting a 
new  benchmark for ‘Super Scooters’ in India. ” 
 
Hero Xoom 160 will be available soon across all Hero Premia dealerships in India. Customers 
can also book the scooter through the company’s online platform 
(https://www.heromotocorp.com/en -in/scooters/xoom -160.html ).

About Xoom 160  
The Xoom 160 sets a new benchmark  in the ‘Super -Scooter ’ category, bringing together 
innovative design, advanced technology and exceptional performance. Crafted to transform 
every day ride into a n ew adventure, Xoom 160 delivers a performance that goes beyond 
conve ntional scooter - A ‘Super Scooter ’ that turns everyday ride into a new adventure!  
 
Equipped with a 156cc liquid -cooled engine , Xoom 160 delivers  10.9kW  @ 8,000  rpm and 
14 Nm torque @ 6 ,250 rpm, ensuring strong acceleration and refined efficiency . It features 
i3s silent start and 4 -valve technology  for optimal fuel economy and high -speed capability. 
Xoom 160’ s bold design includes a raised stance, 14” large wheels  with block -pattern tires, 
and a wide, cushioned seat for superior comfort. Premium  features like smart key with 
remote seat access , dual -chamber LED headlamp, Front disc brake with ABS , and 
Bluetooth -enabled digital speedometer with Turn -by-Turn navigation.  
 
With its bold performance and advanced technology, Xoom 160 paves the way for a new 
generation of ‘Super Scooters ’ that will redefine everyday urban mobility.
