Reduction in Operational Emissions Intensity Since FY13  
The Company also record ed a 29.95% reduction in Scope 3 GHG emissions intensity against its 
FY19 baseline  
 
Mumbai, October 2 2, 2026: Marico Limited, one of India’s leading FMCG companies, today 
highlighted significant progress in its climate action journey on the occasion of the International Day 
of Climate Action. Reinforcing its commitment to building a low -carbon future, the Company has 
achieved an 89.3% reduction in Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG emissions intensity compared to its FY13 
baseline and a 29.95% reduction in Scope 3 GHG emissions intensity against its FY19 baseline.  
 
Marico’s climate strategy is guided by its ESG 2030 Roadmap  and its ambition to achieve Net Zero 
emissions across Indian operations (owned manufacturing units)  in India by 2030 and across its 
global operations by 2040. The Company continues to embed climate considerations across 
business operations, supply chain m anagement, innovation, circular packaging, resource allocation 
and long -term growth planning.  
 
The progress achieved to date has been driven by a combination of increased renewable energy 
adoption, energy -efficiency measures, process optimisation and the t ransition to lower -carbon 
energy sources across operations. In FY2025 -26, renewable energy accounted for 77.3% of Marico’s 
operational energy consumption, while its Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions intensity stood at 0.84 
tCO₂e per crore of revenue.  
 
As part of its decarbonisation roadmap, Marico is targeting a 93% reduction in Scope 1 and Scope 2 
emissions by 2030, compared to its FY13 baseline. Residual emissions will be addressed through 
carbon sequestration initiatives and verified carbon offsets.  
 
Comment ing on the occasion, Amit Bhasin, Chief Legal Officer, Group General Counsel and 
Secretary, CSR Committee of Marico Limited , said , “Climate action is an integral part of our 
business strategy and long -term growth agenda. Building a future -ready organisatio n requires 
sustainability to be embedded across operations, supply chains and decision -making processes. 
The progress we have made in reducing emissions across our operations and value chain reflects 
the collective efforts of our teams, partners and stakeh olders. As we advance towards our climate 
ambitions, we will continue to scale renewable energy adoption, improve energy efficiency, 
strengthen responsible sourcing practices, drive packaging circularity and collaborate across our 
value chain to deliver on  our long -term Net Zero commitments. ” 
 
Marico’s Climate Transition Plan brings together the company’s climate initiatives within a unified 
enterprise -wide framework, linking climate ambitions with implementation roadmaps, governance 
mechanisms, resource allocation and measurable outcomes. Throu gh this integrated approach, the
