Press Release

Puravankara Signs JDA  for 12.76 -acre Land Parcel in Sathanur, Bengaluru, with 
Potential GDV of Rs 2,500 Crore  
Bengaluru:  Puravankara Limited (NSE: PURVA | BSE: 532891), one of India's most trusted and 
admired real estate developers, has signed a Joint Development Agreement (JDA)  for a 12.76 -acre land 
parcel in Sathanur, North Bengaluru, with an estimated Gross Development Value (GDV) of Rs 2,500 
crore.  
The proposed development, with a saleable area of approximately 1.90 million square feet (msft), will 
strengthen the company’s residential development pipeline in Bengaluru and reinforce its focus on high -
potential micro -markets and strategic land addition s. 
Commenting on the development, Ashish Puravankara, Managing Director, Puravankara 
Limited , said, “Our focus remains on strengthening Puravankara’s development pipeline through 
strategic land additions that offer scale, strong market potential and visibility for future growth. The 
Sathanur JDA , with an estimated GDV of Rs 2,500 crore, is another step in this direction. We continue 
to evaluate opportunities across key markets with a disciplined approach to capital allocation, ensuring 
that each investment contributes to building a stronger and m ore sustainable portfolio.”  
This JDA adds to Puravankara’s development pipeline in Bengaluru . This  aligns  with the company’s  
focus on expanding its portfolio while maintaining capital efficiency and ensuring timely project 
execution.  
Mallanna Sasalu, CEO -South , Puravankara Limited , added, “The 12.76 -acre land parcel at 
Sathanur offers us an opportunity to develop a sizeable residential project with approximately 1.90 
million square feet of saleable area. Bengaluru continues to see healthy end -user demand. Our priority 
will be to translate t his opportunity into a well -planned development that meets evolving homebuyer 
expectations, with a clear focus on product quality, timely approvals and efficient execution.”  
FY27 Business Development Highlights  
With the proposed Sathanur development, Puravankara's business development additions for FY27 so 
far comprise eight projects with a combined development potential of around 12.64 msft across 
Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi -NCR, and a total potential GDV of ₹16 ,600 crore.  
● Bengaluru:  Six land additions, including Sathanur, Mandur, Doddagubbi, Sanna Ammanikere, 
Sarjapur and South -East Bengaluru, representing a development potential of approximately 
7.02 msft and a combined estimated GDV of Rs 8,800 crore.  
● Mumbai:  A redevelopment project in Goregaon West, with an estimated GDV of Rs 2,600 
crore and approximately 1.05 msft of saleable area.  
● Delhi -NCR:  A land parcel in Greater Noida, with development potential of 4.57 msft and an 
estimated GDV potential of Rs 5,200 crore.  
These additions strengthen Puravankara's development pipeline across three key markets, supporting 
its long -term growth plans.

About Puravankara Limited  
The Puravankara Group is one of India's most trusted realty majors, headquartered in Bengaluru with a pan -India 
presence. Over the past five decades, the company has catered to the full spectrum of housing and plotted 
development needs. The group has also forayed into developing Grade A commercial real estate, with a presence 
of approximately 3 msft, and is rapidly expanding its footprint. Additionally, its interior design arm, Purva Streaks, 
serves customers seeking an integrated interior design solution.  
As of June 30, 2026, Puravankara has completed 97 projects totalling approximately 59 msft across nine cities: 
Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Coimbatore, Mangaluru, Kochi, Mumbai, Pune, and Goa. The company's total land 
bank is approximately 40 msft, and o ngoing projects total 35.14 msft.
