Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
We would like to inform you that the Finance Committee of the Board of Directors at 
its Meeting held today i,e on September 19, 2024, has considered and approved  an 
investment of Rs. 8.03 Crore s (Rupees Eight  Crore s and three lakhs  only) in Round 
the Cocktails Private Limited (“Bartisans”) . 
 
Bartisans is a 'ready to pour' beverage company , engaged in the business of 
developing, producing, marketing and selling non -alcoholic beverages which can be 
mixed with alcohol to cre ate cocktails, and can also be consumed on their own as 
mocktails.  
 
Further, the Company has entered into three agreements dated September 19, 2024 
as follows:  
 
1. Share Subscription Agreement  with Bartisans and their Promoters;  
2. Share Purchase Agreement  with Bartisans  and their  Promoters and Early 
Investors  and; 
3. Shareholder s’ Agreement  with Bartisans and their Promoters . 
 
 
 
 
 
 
  
  
 
The Company shall invest Rs. 8.03 Crore s (Rupees Eight  Crore s and Three lakhs only) 
as given below :  
 
(a) Subscribing to 2,352 Compul sory Convertible Preference Shares ("CCPS") and 1 
equity share of Bartisans equivalent to 13.52% of share capital on a fully diluted basis 
for INR 3 crore s; and 
 
(b) Purchasing from existing shareholders, 163 equity shares and 3,781 CCPS of 
Bartisans equiv alent to 22.65% of share capital on a fully diluted basis for INR 5.03 
crore s. 
 
In aggregate, the Company will own 36.17% of the share capital of Bartisans on a fully 
diluted basis post this investment.
