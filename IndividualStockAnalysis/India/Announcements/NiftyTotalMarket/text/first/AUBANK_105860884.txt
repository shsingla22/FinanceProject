Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, this is to inform that the Bank has received a communication from the Reserve Bank of India 
informing that penalty of Rs. 5,000 has been imposed on account of deficiency observed dur ing RBI's 
regular incognito visit in awareness among official of Bank’s Chandigarh branch regarding the rules of 
exchange of mutilated notes.  
  
The Bank has imparted necessary training among all the branch officials to create awareness on RBI 
guidelines on the subject matter.
