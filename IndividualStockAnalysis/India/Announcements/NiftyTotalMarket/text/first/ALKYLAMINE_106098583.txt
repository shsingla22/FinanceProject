Dear Sir/ Madam,  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations and as per the information received from the plant’s 
official at around 2.15 PM yesterday, we hereby inform you that on Saturday, December 28, 2024, at around 6: 
15 AM, a residue tank in Derivatives plant ruptured resulting into serious burn injuries to the attending officer 
due to hot content of residue tank, at Kurkumbh site at Plot No. D -6/1 & D -6/2, MIDC Kurkumbh Industrial 
Area, Pune - 413802, Maharashtra. The said officer succumbed to the injuries today morning in the Surya burns 
hospital, Pune where he was being treated.  
Impact on Operations  
There could be a temporary disruption in the operations of the affected plant. We are currently assessing the 
full extent of the damage and its potential impact on production. We are taking all necessary steps to restore 
normal operations at the earliest.  
Insurance Coverage  
We would like to state that the assets at the affected plant are adequately insured, and we are in the process 
of filing insurance claim to cover the damage and losses.
