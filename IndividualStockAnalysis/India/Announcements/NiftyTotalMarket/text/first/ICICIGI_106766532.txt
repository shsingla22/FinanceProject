Subject: Update on Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  
 
This is with reference to our letter dated April 11, 2024, wherein the Company had 
informed the stock exchanges regarding receipt of an Order, from the Additional 
Commissioner, Customs & Central Tax, Hyderabad GST Commissionerate, 
Hyderabad raising a GST demand of ₹ 1,47,86,059/- and penalty of ₹ 14,78,606/- 
for FY2018-19 under Section 73 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 
(“the Act”). The Company had subsequently filed an appeal against the said Order. 
The Department also filed an appeal against the relief of tax demand of                                   
₹ 1,81,97,223/- granted to the Company vide the said Order.  
 
In this regard, please note that the Company has received Order from the 
Commissioner of Customs & Central Tax (Appeals-I), Hyderabad (Commissioner 
Appreals-I) on September 1, 2026 at 6:32 p.m., wherein the appeal filed by the 
Department was allowed. The Company has also received Order from the 
Commissioner Appreals-I on September 2, 2026 at 12:52 p.m., wherein the appeal 
filed by the Company was rejected. This resulted in aggregate GST demand of                   
₹ 3,29,83,282/- and penalty of ₹ 14,78,606/- for FY2018-19. The Company would 
pursue an appeal / evaluate any other legal options against the aforesaid Orders, 
including filing of writ petition. The requisite details of the aforesaid Orders are 
enclosed herewith as Annexure 1.
