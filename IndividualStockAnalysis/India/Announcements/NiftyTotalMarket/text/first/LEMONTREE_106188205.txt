Lemon Tree Hotel s signs a new property in Maharashtra  
 
 
New Delhi, 28th March 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels announces its  latest signing — 
Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels, Pali, Maharashtra . The property, which shall 
be franchised under Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, is expected to start operations in 
FY-26.  
 
Raigad district  of Maharashtra  is home to the small town of Pali. Known for its 
historical significance and natural beauty, it is located c lose to the Western Ghats. 
Palis is known for t he Ballaleshwar Ganapati Temple, one of the Ashtavinayak 
temples devoted to Lord Ganesha . The town is popular with nature enthusiasts 
and trave llers who like trekking and outdoor pursuits  as there are are several 
trekking routes around Pali. One trek leads to the Sarasgad  Fort and the other to 
Sudhagad  Fort which is about 10  km away. The Sudhagad trek ends at a temple 
dedicated to a local goddess called 'Bhora i'.  
 
Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels, Pali, Maharashtra will feature 54  well-appointed 
rooms, a restaurant, a banquet , a bar, a swimming pool,  and other public areas.  
Th Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport , the nearest airport, is 
approximately 98 kms  from the property. Mumbai Central Railway Station is about 
97 kms away from the property. The hotel is also well -connected by road, offering 
easy access to both public and private transport.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels c ommented, “We are thrilled to expand our presence in 
Maharashtra , complementi ng our portfolio of 12  existing hotel s and seven  
upcoming properties . This signing  marks our  milestone  of signing 50th hotel in FY 
2025, significantly boosting our visibility and reinforcing our commitment to 
growth. ”  
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of the largest hotel chains in India, and 
owns/leases/operates/franchises hotels across the upscale, upper -midscale, 
midscale and economy segments. It delivers differentiated yet superior service 
offerings, with a co mpelling value proposition. The group offers seven brands to 
meet guests’ needs across all levels, viz. Aurika Hotels & Resorts, Lemon Tree 
Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox Hotels by Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Prima 
by Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Select by Lem on Tree Hotels and Keys Lite by Lemon 
Tree Hotels.  
 
LTH opened its first hotel with 49 rooms in May 2004. Today, the company has a 
portfolio of 210+ hotels, which includes over 110 operational hotels and more than 
100 hotels set to open in India and intern ationally. Lemon Tree Hotels are located 

   
across India, in key metro regions including the NCR, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, 
Hyderabad and Chennai, as well as numerous tier I, II and III cities such as Pune, 
Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, Jaipur, Indore, Aurangabad,  Udaipur, Visakhapatnam, 
Kochi, Ludhiana, Thiruvananthapuram, Vijayawada, etc. The company has also 
expanded internationally to Dubai, Bhutan and Nepal.
