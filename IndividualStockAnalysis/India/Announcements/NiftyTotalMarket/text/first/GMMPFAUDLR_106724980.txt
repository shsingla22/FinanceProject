Summary of the proceedings of 63rd Annual General Meeting of the Company  
 
The 63rd Annual General Meeting (“ AGM ”) of the Members of GMM Pfaudler Limited (“ Company ”) was held 
on August 4, 2026,  through Video Conferencing (“ VC”)/ Other Audio Visual Means (“ OAVM ”) organized by the 
Company at 12:00 noon (IST) to transact business mentioned in the Notice of the AGM dated May 21, 2026 
(“AGM Notice ”). Mr. Prakash Apte, Non -executive Independent Director and Chairperson of the Company 
chaired the AGM. The AGM concluded at 12:51  p.m. (IST).
