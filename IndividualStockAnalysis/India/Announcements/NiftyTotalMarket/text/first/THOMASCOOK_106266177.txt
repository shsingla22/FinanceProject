Thomas Cook  forays into Punjab’s high -growth market  
Opens its first Gold Circle Partner outlet in Mohali  
 
Expands  the Company's retail network to 10 outlets across Punjab  
 
Mumbai,  June  10, 2025: In line with its strat egic expansion into high -potential growth markets, Thomas Cook 
(India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel  travel  services company, has strengthened its presence in 
Punjab with the launch of its first franchise outlet in Mohali.  A key part of the Tricity  region, Mohali is known 
for its rapidly growing residential  and commercial development.  
Thomas Cook India’s  India Holiday Report 2025 (Mohali /Punjab’s data) highlights that 60% of 
respondents intend to increase the frequency of their holidays, while 59 % plan to boost their holiday 
budgets by 20– 50% – a clear indicator of the city/state’s  strong travel appetite. The launch of Thomas 
Cook’s  outlet is thus strategically timed to tap into this  strong demand, by  providing  access and 
convenience for customers in Mohali and neighbouring areas including Banur, Rajpura, and Kharar. With 
this addition, Thomas Cook India expands its footprint in Punjab to a total of 10 outlets across the state.  
 
Thomas C ook’s Mohali  outlet offers  end-to-end travel services: International & Domestic Holidays (Group 
Tours, Personalised Holidays, Cruises, etc.), Value Added Ser vices like Travel Insurance etc. Additionally, 
Thomas Cook’s AI booking platform assist s customers in planning their personalized vacations in real time  
by checking availability, and making reservations in a matter of minutes . This is coupled with the expertise 
of the Company’s in- store holiday experts to help customers’ co- curate memorable vacations.  
 
India Holiday Report 2025: Travel Trends from Mohali  
 
• 55% of Mohali travellers plan to increase their holidays from 2 to 4- 6 trips annually  
• 54% plan to increase travel spends  by 20 –50% in 2025; reflecting a willingness to invest in deeper, 
more enriching experiences  
• 60% of respondents show a strong preference for experience -led holidays ; 55% (higher than the 
national average of  45%) are drawn to phenomenon -based travel  such as the Northern Lights, Cherry 
Blossom; 40% express interest in safaris, self -drives, and outdoor adventures  
• New travel formats in demand: 35%  of travellers plan to opt for cruises; 25% for self-drives and 20% for 
scenic train journeys  
• 55% of travellers prefer a phygital approach,  combining digital convenience with trusted offline expertise 
for travel planning  
• Top destinations:  
o International : Europe (60%)  continues to be a top choice (higher than the national average of  50%) for 
Mohali customers with Switzerland, Austria, and France ranking high;  followed by Japan, South Korea 
(40%) ; Southeast Asia (30%)  (Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, and Singapore); Australia -New Zealand 
(25%) ; Middle East (25%) (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Oman); Central Asia (20%) (Uzbekistan, Kyr gyzstan, 
Kazakhstan and others) ; Island locales  (15%) (Mauritius, Maldives, Bali, Sri Lanka)  
o Domestic: Kashmir, Ladakh, Himachal, Andaman Islands , North -East India  
• Simplified visa processes  (e-visa, visa -on-arrival, visa -free access) strongly influencing destination 
choices,  with countries like Thailand, Malaysia, Sri Lanka in high demand  
• Continued  demand for Pilgrimage Tourism : Char Dham, Kashi, Ayodhya, Rameswaram, Tirupati,  
Amritsar, Varanasi, Prayagraj
