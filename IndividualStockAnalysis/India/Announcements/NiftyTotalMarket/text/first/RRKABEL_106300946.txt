Sub:  Intimation under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – 
Update relating to Exercise Price for ESOPs. 
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
We wish to inform you that the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of the Company, at its 
meeting held today, i.e. Tuesday, 15 July 2025, has approved that the maximum discount to be 
considered while determining the Exercise Price for any Options to be granted under the RR Kabel 
Employee Stock Option Plan 2023 (the “RRKL ESOP 2023”) (subject to the RRKL ESOP 2023 being 
ratified by the shareholders at the ensuing Annual General Meeting) shall not exceed 20% of the 
Market Price as prevailing on the date of issuance of grants (“Grant Date”) to any eligible employee(s). 
 
Accordingly, the Exercise Price for any Options to be granted under the RRKL ESOP 2023 shall not be 
less than 80% of the Market Price prevailing on the Grant Date, subject to the minimum Exercise Price 
of ₹ 270.10 per equity share, as provided under the RRKL ESOP 2023. 
 
The Committee meeting commenced at 8:30 p.m. (IST) and concluded at 8:46 p.m. (IST) 
 
This intimation is also being made available on the Company’s website at https://www.rrkabel.com .  
 
We request you to kindly take the above on record.
