Press Release            
 
Transrail Lighting Secures New Orders Worth ₹ 574 Crore   
 
Strengthens Domestic T&D order book and reinforces growth momentum  
with major contribution from HTLS reconductoring jobs .  
 
Additional L1 position of ₹694 crore.  
 
Mumbai, Sept  30, 202 6: Transrail  Lighting Limited (BSE: 544317, NSE: TRANSRAILL), one of the leading EPC 
players in the Transmission & Distribution (T&D) sector, has secured new orders worth more than  ₹574 crore 
mainly in the core Domestic Transmission & Distribution (T&D) business  with major contribution from the 
reconductoring of Transmission Lines  with its new age carbon core HTLS conductors manufactured in its 
Silvassa plant . 
The order wins comprise projects including EPC of Transmission Lines  and Transmission line reconductoring 
projects with supply of HTLS Conductors  from its factories and also  supply of Poles  etc. These wins further 
demonstrate the Company's capabilities in the HTLS Conductor manufacturing and stringing and the 
importance of its recent increase in conductor manufacturing capacity by 70%.  It also includes an EPC 
contract for construction of Transmission line from a very large private sector Indian developer.  
With this the order intake for the year grows to ₹1,609  crore  and there are additional ₹ 694 crore  worth of 
orders where the Company is L1 . 
The Company continues to enjoy a healthy bidding pipeline across both domestic and international markets. 
A robust order book, coupled with a strong bidding pipeline, and increased capacities reinforces the 
Company's long -term growth outlook.  It is notable that in order to cater to the growing orderbook of the 
Company, it has already doubled its Tower manufacturing capacity to 172,400 MTPA and increased the conductor capacity by 70% to 40,800 KMPA.   
Mr. Randeep Narang, MD & CEO, stated,  
"The latest order wins reflect the continued confidence our marque customers place in our engineering, 
manufacturing and execution capabilities in the T&D sector . I am particularly thrilled for the HTLS 
reconductoring jobs we have won as it is a testimony to not just our construction excellence but also to our advanced product range . With a strong order book and a healthy bidding pipeline  coupled with increased 
manufacturing capacities across towers and conductors , we continue to see significant opportunities for 
sustainable growth for Transrail ."
