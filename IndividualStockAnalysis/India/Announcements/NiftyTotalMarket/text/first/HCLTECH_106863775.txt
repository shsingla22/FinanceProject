HCLTech and Cohere partner to build and deliver sovereign AI  
 
NOIDA, India, Nov. 17, 2026 — HCLTech, a leading global technology company, and Cohere , a leading 
provider of sovereign AI for governments and regulated industries, today announced a partnership to build and deliver sovereign AI solutions for enterprises and governments.  
 
With this agreement, HCLTech becomes one of Cohere’s preferred global systems integrator partners in Canada and Germany, two of HCLTech’s key global focus markets. Sovereignty is shaping how 
enterprises and governments adopt AI in both countries, and HCLTech brings a strong local presence and the capabilities to apply its advanced AI solutions, backed by its AI labs, established client base 
and execution strength. The two companies aim to extend the partnership worldwide.  
 
The partnership brings together Cohere’s sovereign AI platform and models, including North , its 
flagship agentic AI platform and HCLTech’s professional services expertise and end -to-end 
advanced  AI capabilities spanning Physical AI and Industry AI solutions.  
 The partnership will focus on industry -specific AI solutions that combine precise, risk -aware retrieval 
and reasoning with domain- adapted models. These solutions will be deployed in each customer’s 
preferred environment to meet data residency, security and  regulatory requirements. Development 
will begin in insurance, financial services and manufacturing, with use cases such as jurisdiction- aware 
claims assessment, secure analysis of sensitive financial data and faster access to technical 
documentation.  
 “Organizations shouldn’t have to choose between the benefits of AI and control over their data. Our 
partnership with HCLTech will help customers tackle complex, industry -specific challenges while 
meeting their security, governance and data sovereignty requirements,” said Phil Blunsom, Chief 
Technology Officer at Cohere. “By combining Cohere’s sovereign AI technology with HCLTech’s industry expertise and global delivery capabilities, we’re making it easier for enterprises and 
governments to move beyond exper imentation and solve real operational challenges at scale.”  
  
“As AI adoption accelerates, the ability to deploy it responsibly with built -in control, trust and 
sovereignty has become a strategic imperative for enterprises and governments,” said Vijay Guntur, 
Chief Technology Officer and Head of Ecosystems  at HCLTech. “Together with Cohere, we will build 
and deliver sovereign AI solutions that bring industry -leading AI innovation and enterprise -scale 
execution to clients who need AI capabilities aligned to their data, domain and operational needs, and we will e mbed AI across the enterprise to drive sustainable business outcomes.”
