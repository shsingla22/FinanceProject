Bajaj Electricals Limited Expands  Its Portfolio with the 
Launch of Bajaj Secura Wires  
Bajaj Electricals Limited today announced the launch of Bajaj Secura Wires , marking its 
entry into the wires segment and further strengthening its diversified electrical solutions 
portfolio. Introduced under the company’s Lighting Solutions  business, the new range 
expands Bajaj Electricals’ footprint across allied electrical categories, addressing the growing demand for safe, reliable, and high-performance wiring solutions for residential applications.  The demand outlook for wires continues to be robust therefore provid ing an 
attractive opportunity for a new trusted player in the electrical i ndustry . 
With a legacy spanning over 80 years , Bajaj Electricals has powered homes and businesses 
across generations. The launch of Bajaj Secura Wires extends this legacy beyond illumination to the core of electrical safety. Secura Wires  comes in 4 offerings, Secura (FR) , 
Secura Plus (HR -FR) , Secura Green (FR -LSH) , and Secura Green Plus (ZH -FR) . 
Annexure B
Engineered to set new benchmarks in performance and reliability, Bajaj Secura Wires 
feature over 99.9% copper purity , ensuring superior conductivity and long -term durability. 
Built with advanced insulation technology, the range offers flame -retardant  and halogen -
free  properties, delivering enhanced safety across a wide range of applications.  
Designed for demanding environments, the wires are moisture -resistant  and offer anti -
termite and anti -rodent protection . With 101% conductivity and a 100% length 
guarantee , Bajaj Secura Wires reinforce trust, precision, and consistency at every 
connection. The entire range is RoHS and REACH compliant , underlining Bajaj Electricals’ 
commitment to quality, safety, and sustainability.  
Available in multiple colour options , Bajaj Secura Wires are offered in sizes ranging from 
0.75 sqmm to 6.00 sqmm , and in 90 -metre and 180 -metre  pack sizes to suit diverse 
installation requirements.  
Thoughtfully engineered for both electricians and end users, Bajaj Secura Wires support easy installations and reliable  performance. This launch aligns with Bajaj Electricals’ 
strategy to build an integrated  and comprehensive  electrical solutions portfolio while 
continuing to power progress with safety, reliability, and trust . 
“The launch of Bajaj Secura Wires marks a strategic expansion of our electrical solutions 
portfolio. As we enter the wires segment, our focus is on delivering products that combine safety, performance, and reliability  - backed by Bajaj Electricals’ legacy of trust. This foray 
reinforces our commitment to building an integrated, end -to-end electrical ecosystem for 
homes and businesses.”  said Rajesh Naik, COO – Lighting Solutions, Bajaj Electricals 
Ltd.
