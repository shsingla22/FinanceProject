Press Release:  
Jupiter Tatravagonka Railwheel Factory (JTR WF) a subsidiary of Jupiter  Wagons 
Ltd. (JWL) Sign s a Landmark Deﬁnitive Long- Term Supply Agreement with 
Tatravagonka a.s.; Positions India on Global Railwheel Map  
Become s India’s First Rail Wheel Manufacturer To Export  Wheelsets Globally  – designed to 
export approx. 50,000 wheelsets  annually  
 
National, 27th May  2026 : Jupiter Tatravagonka Railwheel Factory Pvt. Ltd. (JTR WF), has entered into a 
deﬁnitive long -term supply agreement with the leading European rail wagon manufacturer Tatravagonka  
a.s. for the supply of rail wheels, axles,  and wheelsets.  Through its greenﬁeld plant at Odisha, the company 
has planned annual capacity of One Lakh wheelsets. And expects to export approximately 50,000 
wheelsets annually to the European market  placing India squarely on the global rail supply chain map.  
The agreement is structured for 10 years, with commercial supplies expected to commence by the end of 
calendar year 2027. The deal establishes a required procurement framework under which 
TATRAVAGÓNKA a.s. and its affiliates will procure railway wheels, axles, and wheelsets from JTRWF. 
TATRAVAGÓNKA a.s., which manufactures and maintains freight wagons for customers across Europe and 
beyond, currently alone consumes approximately 20,000 to 30,000 wheelsets annually to sustain its 
operations .  
JTRWF’s greenfield Odisha plant, will cater to this demand and has been purpose -built with a state -of-the-
art design and engineering capabilities.  W ith an estimated investment of ₹ 3,000 crore , the facility is being 
designed with a planned annual production capacity of 100,000 wheelset  and will cater to both domestic 
international demands . The company has previously indicated that nearly 40 -50% of the Odisha plant’s 
production capacity is expected to cater to export markets over the long term.  
 
 

Mr. Vivek Lohia, Managing Director, Jupiter Wagons Limited, commented: “This agreement represents 
an important milestone not only for Jupiter Tatravagonka Railwheel Factory , but also for India’s rail 
manufacturing ecosystem. Through this agreement, India- that  was a net importer of wheelsets, will now 
become major exporter to the world. It validates our technology, manufacturing processes and quality 
standards for global markets while strengthening our long -term export ambitions. We are expecting to 
add 1000- 1500 Cr.  of revenue through the exports  in the coming years.  
This agreement is a powerful validation of India’s Make in India and Aatmanirbhar Bharat initiatives. It 
demonstrates that Indian manufacturing, when backed by the right investment, technology, and quality 
systems, can cater to the requirements of the world’s most  competitive markets of the world.  The railway 
sector has historically been dominated by European, Chinese, and American manufacturers. JTRWF’s 
breakthrough into this space as the ONLY exporter signals a structural shift in India’s industrial 
compe titiveness.”  
Mr. Pawan Kumar Agrawal, CEO & Whole Time Director, Jupiter Tatravagonka Railwheel Factory Private 
Limited, said: “The long- term agreement with Tatravagonk a a.s.  provides strategic visibility for our 
upcoming Odisha facility and reinforces confidence in India’s ability to emerge as a competitive supplier of 
high- quality  forged wheelsets for international markets. This partnership is an important step towards 
building a globally integrated rail manufacturing ecosystem from India to the world. ”
