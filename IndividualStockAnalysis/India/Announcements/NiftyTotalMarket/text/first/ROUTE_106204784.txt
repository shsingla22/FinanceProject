Route Mobile announces changes to its Board of Directors  
17th Apr 2025, Mumbai, India  – Route Mobile Limited (“Route Mobile”), (NSE: ROUTE, BSE: 543228), 
a leading global CPaaS (Communications Platform as a Service) provider to enterprises, over -the-top 
(OTT) players, and mobile network operators, announced the following changes to its Board of 
Directors.  
Mr. Guillaume Antoine Boutin , Chairman (Non -Executive, Non -Independent ) Director of Route Mobile 
Limited, has tendered  his resignation with effect from April 17th, 2025, due to his pre -occupation and 
other personal commitments. The Board has noted the resignation and places on record its sincere 
appreciation for his remarkable contribution during his tenure with the Company and wishes him the 
very best in his futur e endeavors.  
The Board is pleased to announce the appointment of Mr. Mark James Reid, (Non -Executive, Non -
Independent ) Director of the Company  as the new Chairman of the Board and Company with effect from 
April 17th 2025. Mr. Reid currently serves as the Financial Lead  of the Proximus Group and as CEO ad 
interim of Proximus Global , and he is acting as Board member of BICS and of Proximus Global. He 
brings a wealth of experience in global telecommunications and enterprise solutions. His leadership is 
expected to further strengthen Route Mobile’s strategic direction and governance framework.  
Finally, the Board has appointed Mr. Jan Van Acoleyen as an Additional (Non -Executive, Non -
Independent ) Director on the Board of Route Mobile with effect from April 16th 2025. Mr. Van Ac oleyen 
is the Human Capital Lead and CEO ad interim of the Proximus Group , and a Board member of BICS, 
Telesign, MWingz, Proximus Pension Fund and Proximus Global. He also serves as the Chairman of the 
Remuneration Committee of BICS and as Chairman of the Board of Be -Mobile. A seasoned executive with 
deep expertise in digital transformation and human capital strategy, Mr. Van Acoleyen will offer valuable 
perspectives as Route Mobile continues to expand its global footprint.  
These appointments mark a new cha pter in Route Mobile’s journey, reinforcing its commitment to 
strong leadership, innovation, and sustainable growth.  
About Route Mobile Limited ( www.routemobile.com ) (BSE: 543228; NSE: ROUTE)  
Established in 2004, Route Mobile Limited (“RML”) is a cloud communications platform service provider 
catering to enterprises, over -the-top (OTT) players, and mobile network operators (MNO). RML’s 
portfolio comprises solutions in messaging, voice, email, SMS filtering, a nalytics, and monetization. RML 
has a diverse enterprise client base across various industries, including social media companies, banks 
and financial institutions, e -commerce entities, and travel aggregators. RML is headquartered in 
Mumbai, India, with a g lobal presence in Asia Pacific, the Middle East, Africa, Europe, and the Americas. 
Route Mobile is now a part of Proximus Global, a provider of digital services and communication 
solutions operating in Belgium and international markets.
