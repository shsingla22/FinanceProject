TVS MOTOR COMPANY UNVEILS FESTIVE EDITION OF TVS RONIN 
WITH A STRIKING NEW COLOUR VARIANT  
 
• The top variant of TVS RONIN now comes in an exclusive Midnight Blue with 
vibrant fluorescent green graphics . 
• Special pricing for the base variant  – adds to the excitement of the season . 
 
Bangalore, September 24, 2024:  TVS Motor Company, a leading global automaker operating 
in the two and three -wheeler segments , is adding to the sparkle of  the festive season with an 
exciting new addition to its popular TVS RONIN  series. Known for its 'modern -retro' appeal, 
the motorcycle  will now feature a striking  Midnight  Blue  colour scheme with vibrant 
fluorescent green colour graphics , available exclusively on the top variant. This bold new 
look enhances the motorcycle’s  modern -retro appeal and stylish character.  
 
As part of th e festivities, TVS RONIN  is also introduc ing a special pricing for its base variant . 
The range will now start at INR 1,35,000 (ex-showroom New Delhi) .  
 
Speaking on the occasion , Vimal Sumbly, Head of Business – Premium, TVS Motor 
Company , said, “TVS RONIN has set benchmarks with its  modern -retro design and advanced 
features. Our #Unscripted mantra  inspired  riders to explore new horizons, embrace limitless 
adventures, and create their own stories . While this  festive edition adds  excitement to the 
range, our special pricing  for the bas e variant  makes the motorcycle  more accessible  for 
customers  who seek the unique design, cutting edge technology and connected features  of 
the TVS RONIN. ”
