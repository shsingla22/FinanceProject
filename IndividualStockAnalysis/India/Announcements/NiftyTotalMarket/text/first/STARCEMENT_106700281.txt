In this regard, you may draw your  attention to Regulation 31A(3)( a) of SEBI  LODR Regulations , 2015  which 
states that:  
“31A. Conditions for re -classification of any person as promoter / public   
(3) Reclassification of status of a promoter including promoter group, shall be subject to the following 
conditions:  
(a) Fulfilment of following requirements : 
(i) the promoter(s) seeking reclassification shall make a request for reclassification to the listed entity along 
with a rationale for the request and a description as to how the conditions specified in clause (b) of this sub -
regulation (3) are satisfied;  
(ii) the board of directors of the listed entity shall analyze such request which is compliant with the conditions 
specified in clause (b) of sub -regulation (3) and provide their views in the immediate next board meeting or 
within two months from the date of r eceipt of the request from its promoter(s), whichever is earlier; ”  
 
The application for reclassification submitted by the listed entity was not accompanie d by request letter s from 
the above -mentioned  promoter(s)/promoter group(s)  entities  for seeking reclassification , as required under 
regulation 31A(3)(a) of the SEBI LODR . The application was based on the  Board’s independent satisfaction 
that the conditions specified under Regulation 3 1A(3)(b) were duly fulfilled.  
 
In light of the above , the application of promoters for seeking reclassification  cannot be considered  under 
Regulation 31A  of the SEBI LODR Regulations . The application for reclassification  is disposed off accordingly .
