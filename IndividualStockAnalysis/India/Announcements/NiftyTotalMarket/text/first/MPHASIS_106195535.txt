Mphasis Recognized for Excellence in Identity and Access Management at the 2025 Cybersecurity 
Excellence Awards  
  
New York, April 0 7, 2025  
  
Mphasis , (BSE: 526299; NSE: MPHASIS), an Information Technology (IT) solutions provider specializing in cloud  and 
cognitive  services,  has been honored with the 2025 Cybersecurity Excellence Award  in the Identity and Access 
Management category. This award highlights Mphasis' dedication to advancing security innovations and delivering 
impactful solutions to enterprises worldwide.  
 
As organizations undergo digital transformation, securing identities has become a critical challenge, with traditional 
methods often proving inadequate against modern threats. Mphasis identified this gap and responded by developing 
Identity Security Services  – Comprehensive Identity Access Management, an innovative solution designed to provide 
enterprises with secure, seamless, and scalable identity protection.  
 
Mphasis ’ solution combines AI -driven security, biometric aut hentication, and zero -trust principles to help organizations 
verify identities with greater accuracy. By reducing fraud risks, ensuring regulatory compliance, and streamlining user 
access, the solution enhances both security and user experience.  
 
“At Mphas is, we are committed to helping businesses navigate the complexities of cybersecurity with cutting -edge identity 
security solutions. This recognition is a testament to our relentless focus on providing secure, seamless, and scalable 
identity access managem ent for enterprises worldwide, ” said Nitin Rakesh, C hief Executive Officer and Managing Director , 
Mphasis . “We take pride in our ability to anticipate industry needs and deliver solutions that empower organizations to stay 
resilient in an evolving digital world. ” We remain dedicated to pushing the boundaries of identity and access management, 
ensuring organizations stay ahead of evolving threats while delivering seamless user experiences.  
 
“As we celebrate a decade of recognizing cybersecurity excellence, M phasis stands out in the Identity and Access 
Management category for its pioneering work in identity security, ” said Holger Schulze, founder of Cybersecurity Insiders 
and organizer of the Cybersecurity Excellence Awards.  “Mphasis ’ commitment to security in novation continues to raise the 
bar for the industry. ” 
 
The Cybersecurity Excellence Awards, organized by Cybersecurity Insiders, honor organizations, products, and professionals 
who demonstrate outstanding achievements in the field of security. With this recognition, Mphasis strengthens its position 
as a leading provider of cybersecurity solutions. To learn more about the award visit  https://cybersecurity -excellence -
awards.com .
