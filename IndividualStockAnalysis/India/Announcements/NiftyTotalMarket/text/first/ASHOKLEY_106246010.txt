Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Subject:  Outcome of the Board Meeting 
 
Further to the intimations made earlier in the day, we wish to inform that the Board of Directors of 
the Company, at their meeting held today, have approved the following: 
 
(a) Annual General Meeting (AGM)  
 
The 76th AGM of the shareholders of the Company is scheduled to be held on Thursday, 14th August 
2025 through Video Conferencing / Other Audio-Visual means. 
 
(b) Appointment of Secretarial Auditor 
 
The Board has approved the appointment of Messrs. B Chandra & Associates, Company Secretaries, 
as Secretarial Auditors of the Company for a period of five consecutive years commencing from FY 
2025-26 till FY 2029-30, subject to approval of the shareholders of the Company at the ensuing AGM. 
Additional information as required under Regulation 30 of Listing Regulations is enclosed as Annexure . 
 
(c) Dividend  
 
Further to the communication made on May 8, 2025, we wish to intimate that t he Company has paid 
/ declared two interim dividends, viz. first interim dividend of Rs. 2 per share in November 2024, and 
subsequently the second interim dividend of Rs. 4.25 per share in May 2025, aggregating to Rs. 6.25 
per share of face value Rs. 1 (625%). Having regard to the facts and circumstances, the second interim 
dividend may be considered as final dividend.  
 
The meeting commenced at 1:00 p.m. IST (8.30 A.M. UK Time) and the meeting concluded at 4:05 p.m. 
IST.
