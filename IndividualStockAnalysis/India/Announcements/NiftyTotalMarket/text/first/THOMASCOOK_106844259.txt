Thomas Cook India strengthens  footprint in Uttar Pradesh  
Opens first outlet in Gorakhpur’s  high -potential market  
Expands consumer access to 12 locations across the  State  
 
Mumbai,  November 4 , 2026 : In line with its strategic expansion into viable emerging markets, Thomas Cook 
(India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel services company , has strengthened its network with the 
opening of its first outlet in the city of Gorakhpur . This newly launched outlet broadens the Company’s 
access to  12 outlets across the state of Uttar Pradesh.  
 
Located in Taramandal, South- East Gorakhpur, the outlet is situated in an established commercial and 
residential neighbourhood with a growing base of affluent households, business professionals and outbound travellers. Its proximity to key commercial and l ifestyle landmarks, coupled with good 
connectivity to surrounding areas, gives it access to a wider customer catchment. The outlet is well -
positioned to cater to diverse customer segments, including multi -generational families, corporate 
professionals, couples, Millennials and Gen Z, as well as GenS (seniors).  
 
The outlet will  offer a comprehensive portfolio spanning International and Domestic Holidays, Group Tours, 
Personalised Holidays, Cruises and value- added services such as Travel Insurance. Customers can also 
access Thomas Cook’s AI -powered booking platform, which enables personalised vacation planning by 
checking availability and making reservations in real time.  
 
Gorakhpur/ Uttar Pradesh Travel Trends  
 
• Year -round travel:  Travellers from Gorakhpur are increasingly leveraging public holidays and weekends 
to take multiple short breaks and mini -cations throughout the year, extending travel beyond traditional 
holiday seasons.  
• Preference for experiential holidays:  Travellers are increasingly looking for holidays that offer distinctive 
experiences and greater personalisation, moving beyond conventional destination- based itineraries.  
• Diverse travel formats:  Cruise holidays and charter -led journeys are gaining traction among travellers, 
with Thomas Cook India launching exclusive charter flights to Lakshadweep and Bhutan for the year -end 
holiday season.  
• Growing demand for pilgrimage and spiritual tourism: Pilgrimage and spiritual travel continue to gain 
momentum, with Thomas Cook India strengthening its Spiritual Tourism portfolio ‘Bhakti’  to cater to this 
growing segment. Key spiritual hubs  include Kailash Mansarovar, Char Dham, Vaishnodevi, Ujjain, Puri, 
Kashi  and Ayodhya.  
• Top destinations:  
o International:  Europe’s Switzerland, France, Austria, Italy, Spain; the Scandinavian countries of 
Finland, Norway and Sweden; Eastern European destinations such as the Czech Republic and Hungary; 
Australia and New Zealand; South America; Vietnam, Indonesia, China, Japan and South Korea; as 
well as Thailand, Malaysia and Singapore.  
o Domestic and Indian subcontinent : Rajasthan, Kerala, Andamans, Lakshadweep, Northeast India, 
and Sri Lanka.
