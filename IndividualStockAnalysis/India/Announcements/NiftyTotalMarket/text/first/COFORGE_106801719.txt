Coforge Highlights the Shift to Agentic Travel and 
Modern Airline Retailing  
 
AI-native travelers, autonomous agents, and modern retailing will 
redefine how journeys are discovered, purchased, serviced, and 
managed  
 
Princeton, NJ, USA | Greater Noida, India | September 30, 2026  – Coforge Limited 
(NSE: COFORGE), a global AI -native engineering services leader,  today highlighted a 
fundamental shift underway across the travel industry as airlines transition toward Modern 
Airline Retailing (MAR) and agentic travel, where intelligent agents orchestrate travel 
discovery, transactions, and journey management. Based on extensive work with global 
airlines and travel organizations, Coforge expect s this transformation  to reshape how airlines 
engage travelers, distribute content, manage operations, and drive revenue growth.  
 
The shift is being driven by a new generation of travelers who expect seamless, personalized 
experiences and are increasingly comfortable interacting through AI -powered interfaces 
rather than traditional search and booking journeys. While AI pilots have become 
commonplace across the travel industry, Coforge  believes that  the next wave of 
transformation will be driven not by isolated AI deployments, but by connected systems of 
intelligence that orchestrate decisions across pricing, offers, inventory, servicing, and 
operations.   
 
"The future of travel will be defined by intelligent experiences , not individual transactions," 
said Erika Moore, Chief Officer, Strategy & Growth, Travel, Transportation and 
Hospitality , Coforge.  "Unlike previous technology shifts that took decades to mature, 
advances in AI are accelerating the industry ’s move toward a world where travelers express 
intent, and AI -powered systems dynamically create, optimize, and manage outcomes across 
the entire journey."   
 
Success will depend on an organization's ability to move beyond traditional transaction -based 
systems and connect business processes, operational decisions, and personalized customer 
experiences through a common intelligence layer.  
 
Coforge’s Aeronova.AI  helps travel enterprises turn Offer and Order ambition into execution. 
Through agentic AI and orchestrated agents, it simplifies business transformation, aligns 
teams around a common blueprint, de -risks execution, and manages coexistence across 
legacy and modern retailing platforms , laying the foundations for AI -driven decision making 
at scale.
