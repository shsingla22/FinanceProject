Annexure-1 
1. Brief background: 
MSEZ is a special purpose vehicle incorporated on 24th February, 2006. It is a Joint Venture 
of ONGC (26%), IL&FS (50%), Karnataka Industrial Area Development Board (KIADB) 
(23%), Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Ltd (0.96%) and Kanara Chamber of 
Commerce & Industry (KCCI) (0.04%). 
The Zone is spread over an area of 1,607 acres and the Zone is operational from 2014 with around 
85% area leased out. The Zone has 10 operational units such as MRPL (Aromatic Complex), 
Syngene, ISPRL, Catsynth speciality chemicals and other food processing units, MSEZL has 
emerged as one of the most vibrant operational multi-products SEZs in India. It is one of India's 
successful SEZs with investments exceeding USD 2 billion so far and exports of over USD 3.2 
billion worth of goods from its units. 
2. Products/line of business: 
MSEZ is a Multi-product SEZ and its main business is leasing of the land to the industries and 
providing utility services such as water supply, power supply, Lease rental, Zone 
Maintenance, CETP, Marine outfall and pipe line corridor.
