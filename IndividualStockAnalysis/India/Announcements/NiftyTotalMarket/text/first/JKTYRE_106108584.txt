I, Ronak Jhuthawat of M/s Ronak Jhuthawat & Co., Company Secretary in practice (Certificate of Practice 
No. 12094), have been appointed by the Board of Directors of JK Tyre & Industries Limited 
(the Company) as a Scrutinizer for the purpose of scrutinizing the Postal Ballot (remote e-voting process) 
for passing the resolution set out in the notice of Postal Ballot dated 5th November, 2024. 
The Management of the Company is responsible to ensure the compliance with the requirements of the 
Companies Act, 2013 and the Rules framed thereunder relating to voting through electronic means and 
Postal Ballot on the resolution as stated in the Postal Ballot Notice. My responsibility as Scrutinizer is to 
ensure that the Postal Ballot through remote e-voting process is conducted in a fair and transparent 
manner and to issue the Scrutinizer's Report on the votes cast "FOR" or "AGAINST" the resolution stated 
above, based on the Reports generated from the e-voting system provided by the Central Depository 
Services (India) Limited ("CDSL"), the Authorized Agency to provide e-voting facilities, engaged by the 
Company. 
I hereby submit my report as under:-
A. As per the General Circular No. 09/2024 dated September 19, 2024 including any other circular in 
this regard issued by Ministry of Corporate Affairs ("MCA Circulars"), the said MCA Circulars permit 
Postal Ballot through remote e-voting process. 
8. In compliance with the aforesaid MCA Circulars, the Postal Ballot Notice was sent only through 
electronic mode to those Members, whose names appeared in the Register of Members/Beneficial 
Owners maintained by the Depositories as on 4th December, 2024. The Postal Ballot Notice was also 
made available on the website of the Company at www.jktyre.com. The Physical Copies of this 
Postal Ballot notice along with Postal Ballot form and pre-paid business envelope were not sent to 
the members for the Postal Ballot in accordance with the requirements specified under the MCA 
Circulars. 
C. The Company has appointed Central Depository Services (India) Limited (CDSL) to provide remote 
e-voting facility to the members of the Company to cast their votes through a secured electronic 
mode on the resolution to be transacted as mentioned in the Postal Ballot Notice and therefore, this 
Report contains the details of remote e-voting only. 
D. The Company completed the dispatch of Postal Ballot Notice by e-mail only on 
6th December, 2024 in accordance with the MCA Circulars.
