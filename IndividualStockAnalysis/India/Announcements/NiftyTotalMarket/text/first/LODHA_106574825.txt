In accordance with Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, we hereby inform that the Board of Directors 
of Lodha Developers Limited (‘the Company’) at its meeting held today, i.e., March 30, 2026 has, inter-
alia approved the following: -   
 
Appointment of M/s Walker Chandiok & Co., LLP as the Statutory Auditor of the Company  
 The Statutory Auditors of the Company, M/s. MSKA & Associates LLP , Chartered Accountants, will 
complete their second term of five years and are due to retire at the forthcoming 31
st Annual General 
Meeting of the Company.   
 Based on recommendation of Audit Committee, the Board has approved the appointment of M/s. Walker 
Chandiok & Co., LLP (Firm Registration No. 001076N/N500013) as the Statutory Auditors of the Company 
to hold office for first term of 5 consecutive years effective from FY 2026- 27 i.e. from the conclusion of 
31
st Annual General Meeting to the conclusion of 36th Annual General Meeting, subject to approval of the 
shareholders at the ensuing Annual General Meeting.  
 
The details required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with the relevant SEBI circular(s) 
is enclosed as Annexure- A.
