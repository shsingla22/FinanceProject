Subject: - Allotment of Equity Shares under ESOP Plan 2023 by Nomination and Remuneration Committee.  
 
Dear Sir / Madam,  
 
In terms of the provisions of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
(“Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform you that the Nomination and Remuneration Committee 
(NRC/Compensation Committee) of the Board of Directors of the Company on Tuesday, November 17, 
2026, approved the allotment of 4,20,435 (Four Lakh Twenty Thousand Four Hundred and Thirty-five) equity 
shares having face value of Rs. 10/ - each fully paid-up to the Eligible Employees of the Company, who 
exercised the vested Options, under the Sona Employee Stock Option Plan - 2023 (“ ESOP Plan 2023 ”) of the 
Company. The equity shares so allotted under the ESOP Plan shall rank pari passu with the existing equity 
shares of the Company in every aspect, including dividend entitlement.  
 
Consequent to the aforesaid allotment, the issued and paid-up equity share capital of the Company 
stands increased from Rs. 6,251,773,950 (Rupees Six Hundred Twenty-Five Crore Seventeen Lakh Seventy-
Three Thousand Nine Hundred and Fifty only) consisting of 62,51,77,395 (Sixty-Two Crore Fifty-One Lakh 
Seventy-Seven Thousand Three Hundred and Ninety-Five only) equity shares having face value of Rs. 10/- 
each fully paid up to Rs 6,255,978,300 (Rupees Six Hundred Twenty-Five Crore Fifty-Nine Lakh Seventy-Eight 
Thousand and Three Hundred  only) consisting of 62,55,97,830 (Sixty-Two Crore Fifty-Five Lakh Ninety-Seven 
Thousand Eight Hundred Thirty only) equity shares having face value of Rs. 10/- each fully paid-up.  
 
The disclosures as required under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 read with SEBI Master Circular dated November 11, 2024, are given in the “Annexure-1” 
and the details as required under Regulation 10(c) of SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat 
Equity) Regulations, 2021 – Notification for issue of shares are given in the “Annexure-2”. 
 
This is for your information and further dissemination.  
 
Thanking you,
