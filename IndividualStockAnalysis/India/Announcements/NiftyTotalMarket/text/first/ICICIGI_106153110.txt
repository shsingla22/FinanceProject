Subject: Update - Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  
 
We refer to our intimation dated September 13, 2024, wherein the Company had 
informed the stock exchanges regarding receipt of form of appeal filed by the Income 
Tax Department before the Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (ITAT) against the two 
favourable orders passed by Commissioner of Income Tax (Appeals) – [CIT(A)] 54 
under Section 250 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, setting aside the income-tax demand 
for Assessment Year 2013-14 and 2014-15. 
 
In this regard, the Company has received a common order passed by the ITAT, 
Mumbai for AY2013-14 and AY2014-15 on February 14, 2025 at 1:30 p.m., wherein 
the appeals filed by the Income Tax Department against the Company have been 
dismissed.  
 
The brief details of the said Order as required under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with SEBI Master 
Circular dated November 11, 2024 are enclosed herewith as an Annexure 1 . 
 
The above information will also be made available on the Company's website at 
www.icicilombard.com .
