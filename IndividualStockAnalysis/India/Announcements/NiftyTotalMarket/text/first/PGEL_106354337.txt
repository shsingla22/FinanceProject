Brief profile (in case of appointment)  Mr. Girish Chander  is a seasoned manufacturing and 
operations leader with 29 years of industry 
experience, including 21 years of deep domain 
expertise in the air-conditioning sector. He holds a 
B.E. in Production Engineering from PEC Chandigarh 
(1995) and completed a Senior Executive 
Development Program in Transformational Leadership 
in 2023.  
 
He started his career with Vardhman Spinning Mills, 
post which he served in Whirlpool India Limited, LG 
Electronics India Private Limited, Carrier Midea India 
Private Limited and Havells India Limited for a time 
span of more than 29 years in various roles across 
production, purchase, and customer service, launch of 
VRF systems and led two major greenfield factory 
setups. Most recently, he successfully stabilized 
operations at the Sricity plant of Havells India Limited. 
His leadership spans demand planning, 
manufacturing, logistics, and cross-functional 
collaboration with sales and service teams. He is 
known for his execution excellence, strategic acumen 
and people development skills.            Mr. Chander 
now looks forward to leveraging his extensive 
experience to lead the organization toward sustainable 
and scalable manufacturing excellence. 
 
His leadership will be instrumental in formulating and 
executing corporate strategies, enhancing operational 
efficiency, and ensuring organizational excellence 
across all functions. The Board believes that Mr. 
Chander’s extensive experience and proven track 
record make him well-suited to lead the Company into 
its next phase of growth.
