Subject: Update on Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  
 
We refer to our intimation dated January 30, 2025, wherein the Company had 
informed stock exchanges regarding receipt of an order from the Additional 
Commissioner of CGST & Central Excise, Palghar Commissionerate (‘the impugned 
order’), raising a GST demand of ₹ 31,18,41,716/- under Section 74 of the Central 
Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (‘the Act’) along with interest under Section 50 
and levying a penalty of ₹ 31,18,41,716/- under Section 74 read with Section 122 
of the Act for the period July 2017 to March 2024 covering various states. 
Subsequently, the Company had filed a writ petition before Hon’ble Bombay High 
Court (“Hon’ble Court”) against the impugned order. The impugned order refers to 
matters, which are relating to industry wide issues and pertains to applicability of 
GST on supplies of Group Health Insurance/ Group Personal Accident policies to 
units located within a Special Economic Zone. 
 
In this regard, we wish to inform you that the Company has received an order dated 
March 26, 2026, from its tax advisors on March 28, 2026 at 10:46 p.m., passed by 
the Hon’ble Court, granting a stay on the impugned order till the final disposal of 
the Writ Petition. 
 
The requisite details of the said Order are enclosed herewith as an Annexure 1 . 
 
We further state and declare that the information and details provided in      
Annexure 1, in compliance with Regulation 30(13) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with the Industry Standards 
Note on Regulation 30 are true, correct and complete to the best of our knowledge 
and belief.
