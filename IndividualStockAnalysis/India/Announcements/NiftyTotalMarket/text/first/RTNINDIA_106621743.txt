PM Modi’s Vision, Already on Road: Revolt Riders 
Cross 72 Crore Electric Kilometres  
As the Prime Minister calls on India to reduce fuel dependence and embrace EVs, Revolt’s growing 
community of 57,654 + riders have  already been building that future , one electric kilometre at a time.  
New Delhi, 14th May 2026: Revolt Motors, India’s No.1 electric motorcycle brand, welcomes Prime 
Minister Narendra Modi ’s recent call encouraging citizens to reduce dependence on petrol and 
diesel and adopt more sustainable mobility choices as part of a nationally responsible lifestyle.  
For Revolt riders across India, this transition is not a future ambition. It is already a lived reality.  
Every day, thousands of Revolt riders choose electric mobility not only for convenience and savings, 
but as a conscious step towards a cleaner and more self -reliant India. Long before sustainability 
became a national conversation, this growing community had  already begun redefining the way India 
rides.  Today, that collective shift is translating into measurable impact.  
Revolt Riders Across India Have Collectively:  
• Covered over 72 crore +  (72,00,61,295)  electric kilometres  
• Helped avoid an estimated  93+ Lacs  (9,398,694)  litres of petrol consumption  
• Enabled cumulative rider savings of over ₹89.28  crore  since inception  
These are not projections or future targets. These are real journeys completed by Indians who chose 
electric mobility because they believed in a smarter and more sustainable way forward. Today, the 
Prime Minister’s words give that belief a national voice.  
Ms Anjali Rattan, Chairperson, RattanIndia Enterprises Limited, said , “Prime Minister Modi has put 
into words what our riders have lived every single day. When he speaks of reducing India’s dependence 
on imported fuel and choosing a more responsible way of life, our  riders across the country ha ve 
already been contributing to that vision quietly and consistently. We are proud that Revolt Motors is 
part of building the India the Prime Minister envisions. ” 
As India accelerates towards a cleaner mobility future, Revolt Motors remains committed to making 
electric motorcycles accessible, practical, and relevant for everyday riders. Through continuous 
innovation, expanding accessibility, and a growing nationwide  ecosystem, the company continues to 
strengthen its mission of accelerating EV adoption across the country.  
Revolt Motors is available across 200+ cities  through its expanding dealership network . For every 
Indian ready to answer the Prime Minister’s call, the switch to electric mobility has never been more 
accessible.  
For more information, customers  can visit the official Revolt Motors website at 
https://www.revoltmotors.com  or to have the hand -on experience of Revolt electric motorcycles, 
customers can visit their nearest authorized dealership.
