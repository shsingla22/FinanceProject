Key Features ofthe Refinancing Plan
1. Lead Consortium: Kotak Mahindra Bank, which led a consortium comprising prominent institutions
such as Nomura Capital, Aditya Birla Capital, and STCI. This collaboration has been pivotal in securing
{1,000 crores at highly favorable terms, reflecting the confidence of these institutions in TARC's
