About  Transrail  Lighting  Limited:  
Transrail  is a leading  turnkey  engineering,  procurement  and construction  (EPC)  company with  primary  focus  on 
power  transmission  and distribution  business  with  4 decades  of experience.  With its headquarters  in India,  it is a 
global  enterprise  with  a footprint  in 64 countries  across  6 continents.  It provides turnkey  solutions  from  Designing,  
Engineering,  Supply,  Manufacture,  Construction,  Testing  services  across  all its business verticals which include  
Transmission Lines, Substations, Civil Construction, Railways, Solar and Pole & Lighting.  It has more  than  2,800 
employees  on its roll.  As part  of Power  T&D  business, Transrail  has large -scale manufacturing  facilities  in India  for 
Galvanized  Lattice  Towers,  Overhead  Conductors  and Galvanized  Monopoles  in addition to a well -accredited 
Tower testing facility.
