4. Details of violation/contravention 
committed or alleged to be committed  The Employees’ Provident Fund 
Organisation (EPFO) has levied certain 
damages due to a delay in remittance of PF 
contributions pertaining to a few 
employees.  
 
The delay was attributable to technical 
issues arising from non -seeding and/or 
mismatch of employees’ Aadhaar details 
with their respective Universal Account 
Numbers (UANs) in the EPFO portal. As 
per EPFO’s regulatory framework, 
Aadhaar –UAN verification is  mandatory 
 
 
for processing PF remittances, and the 
system restriction temporarily prevented 
timely deposit of contributions for the 
affected employees.  
 
Upon identification of the issue, Company 
undertook corrective measures to complete 
the Aadhaar verification and subsequently 
deposited the pending contributions and 
then the damages have started reflecting on 
the EPFO portal due to delay in deposition 
of PF contributions pertaining to thes e few 
employees.  
 
The delay was purely technical and 
inadvertent in nature, with no intent to 
withhold or defer statutory dues.  
5. Impact on financial, operations or other 
activities of the listed entity quantifiable 
in monetary terms to the extent possible.  Except the amount mentioned above, there 
is no material impact on financials, 
operation or other activities of the Company 
due to this intimation of damages  being 
payable. The impact is limited to the extent 
of damages  payable.
