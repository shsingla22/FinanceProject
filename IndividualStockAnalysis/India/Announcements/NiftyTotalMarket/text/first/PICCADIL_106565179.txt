PRESS RELEASE  
 
IN AN EXCLUSIVE COLLABORATION, INDRI AND THE DRAM CLUB UNVEIL INDRI RUDHIRA — 
INDIA’S FIRST SINGLE MALT MATURED IN PINEAU DES CHARENTES CASK  
 
Indri Rudhira introduces a new cask maturation style to Indian single malt, bringing together 
innovation, rarity and craftsmanship  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
24th March’26, New Delhi – Indri Single Malt Whisky , India’s fastest -growing single malt, in an 
exclusive first -ever collaboration with The Dram Club , India’s largest & most vibrant Whisky 
community, proudly unveils Indri Rudhira — India’s first -ever single malt matured in Pineau 
des Charentes casks.  
 
With  only 252 individually numbered bottles , Indri Rudhira stands as one of the rarest 
expressions ever released by any Indian brand . Created in collaboration with The Dram Club, 
Rudhira is crafted for collectors and enthusiasts seeking something truly excepti onal.  
 
A style never before explored in the country’s single malt landscape, Rudhira is a lightly 
peated whisky matured in Pineau des Charentes casks. This pioneering expression reflects 
Indri’s spirit of innovation and its commitment to pushing the bounda ries of Indian single 
malt. Crafted in the village of Indri in Haryana, Rudhira brings together smoke, fruit and oak in 
bold harmony. The result is a whisky that is complex yet harmoniously balanced — nuanced, 
layered and distinctly refined.

distillery in India to create something truly unique in this space, we have stepped into an

Tasting Notes  
Nose:  Elegant and layered, with notes of ripe orchard fruits and stone fruits interwoven with 
honeyed sweetness, dried apricot and subtle floral tones, gently lifted by soft peat smoke and 
toasted oak.  
 
Palate:  Rich and full -bodied, revealing flavours of carame lised fruits, vanilla and warm spice, 
balanced by delicate peat smoke and oak complexity, with a rounded sweetness derived from 
Pineau des Charentes cask maturation.  
 
Finish:  Long, warming and refined, with lingering fruit sweetness, gentle spice and a sof t 
smoky trail, leaving a balanced and memorable impression.

About The Dram Club  
Founded in 2019, The Dram Club is India’s leading whisky and spirits community platform, built on the 
mission of making whisky culture more accessible, inclusive, and engaging. Inspired by the founders’ 
extensive explorations of Scottish distilleries — nearly 50 to date — the platform was created to bridge 
the knowledge gap among Indian whisky consumers and move beyond the elitist  conventions 
traditionally associated with the category.  
 
Over six years, The Dram Club has built a highly engaged community of over 150,000 followers across 
Instagram and YouTube, curated more than 250 experiential events across eight Indian cities, and 
connected with upwards of 15,000 spirits enthusiasts offline. The platform is now expanding its 
portfolio beyond whisky and scaling towards a global community of one million spirits enthusiasts.  
 
The Dram Club is co -founded by Swati Sharma, a marketing pro fessional with 15+ years of experience 
across FMCG brands including Hindustan Unilever, Pidilite, and AkzoNobel India; and Vinayak Singh, a 
capital markets and wealth management specialist with 15 years of experience across Indian and 
European financial in stitutions.
