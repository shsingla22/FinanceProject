Key  Subsidiaries  – ‘Stable Performance Across Subsidiaries’  
 
Belstar  Microfinance  
• 82 new Gold Loan B ranches opened  in H1 FY 27 taking the total gold loan branches to  163, further 
strengthening portfolio resilience and supporting a balanced asset mix. Total branches as on H1 FY27 are 
1,362 vs 1,287 branches in H1 FY 26.  
• The branch expansion has led to Gold Loan portfolio reaching ₹484 crores as at H1 FY27.  
• Collection efficiency improved to 99.61% in H1 FY27 , versus 99.25% in H1 FY26 , indicating continued 
operating improvement  in the core micro -finance loan portfolio .  
• Loan AUM at Rs. 7,8 76 crores in H1 FY27, with a marginal increase versus Rs. 7,715 crores in H1 FY26, as 
industry -wide stress moderated disbursement growth.  
• Profit  After  Tax in H1 FY27  at Rs. 144  crores , compared  to a loss of Rs.160 crores in H1 FY2 6. 
• GNPA improved to 3.26% in H1 FY27, versus 4.58% in H1 FY26, while NNPA improved to 0.07% from 0.26% 
over the same period.
