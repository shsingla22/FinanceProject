Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
This is in continuation to our earlier intimations dated August 12, 2023,                                  
September 12, 2023 and December 14, 2024 wherein we had informed that ZWPL, had filed 
a Writ Petition against Union of India and Others (“ Respondent ”) in the Hon’ble High Court 
of Sikkim (“ High Court ”) in the matter of arbitrarily and unilaterally denying the budgetary 
support upon conversion of Zydus Wellness (a partnership firm) into ZWPL (a company form), 
under the Scheme of budgetary support in Goods and Service Tax Regime issued by the 
Department of Industrial Policy and Promotion, The Ministry of Commerce and Industry, vide 
its notification dated October 5, 2017. The High Court had pronounced the judgement on 
September 12, 2023 dismissing the Writ Petition against ZWPL and in favour of Respondent. 
 
 
 
 
In this regard, ZWPL had filed a Writ Appeal before the Division Bench of the High Court. The 
said Division Bench on December 13, 2024 has set aside the earlier Order passed on                        
September 12, 2023 by the Learned Single Judge, with a direction to the concerned authority 
to adjudicate the claims of ZWPL after affording an opportunity of hearing to ZWPL, within a 
period of 12 (twelve) weeks from the date of the Order. 
 
Against the aforesaid Order of the Division Bench of the High Court, an SLP has been filed by 
Union of India and Others in the Supreme Court against ZWPL on June 4, 2025, which was 
intimated to ZWPL on July 3, 2025. ZWPL believes that it has a strong case and it shall take 
necessary action in due course against the aforesaid SLP. 
 
We request you to kindly take this on record.
