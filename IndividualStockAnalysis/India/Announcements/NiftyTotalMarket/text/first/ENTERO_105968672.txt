Mr. Jayant Prakash informed the Members as under:  
 
a. The Notice convening the 6th AGM and the Annual Report of the Company for the financial

e-voting at AGM and for participation of members in AGM.

open until 1 5 minutes after closure of the M eeting.

members  were provided an opportunity to participate in this AGM and vote.  
 
f. The detailed instructions for speakers and participants had been provided in the notice of 
AGM and were informed briefly to the members .  
 
g. The necessary registers and documents referred to in the Notice dated 2nd August, 2024

circulars.

on the website of the Company and will also be submitted to the Stock Exchanges where
