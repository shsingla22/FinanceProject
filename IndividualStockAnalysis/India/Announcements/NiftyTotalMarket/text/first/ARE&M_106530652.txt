Profile of Mr. Annush Ramasamy 
 
Mr. Annush Ramasamy is a Mechanical Engineering graduate from PSG 
College of Technology, Coimbatore, and holds an MBA in Strategy and 
Manufacturing Management from Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT), New 
York. 
 
He serves as the President & Managing Director of Sri KumaraGuru Mill Limited 
(SKG), a flagship company of the KG Group, which has a strong presence in 
the textiles sector along with private equity investments in technology, 
infrastructure, and real estate development. 
 
Mr. Ramasamy provides strategic leadership to the SKG Group with a focus on new business 
initiatives, product innovation, and adoption of advanced technologies. He is passionate about 
building high-performance organizations and fostering a culture of excellence, talent development, 
and employee empowerment. 
 
He is actively involved in the Group’s social initiatives and serves as the Managing Trustee of Katya 
Janani Charitable Trust, which works towards improving the lives of underprivileged girl children. 
The Group also manages multiple educational and healthcare institutions through its charitable 
trusts. 
 
Mr. Ramasamy is a prominent member of leading entrepreneurial and industry forums including 
Entrepreneurs’ Organization (EO) and Young Indians (Yi). He has served on the Global Board of 
EO, heading the Global Student Entrepreneur Awards (GSEA) sub-committee, and was the National 
Chairman of CII’s Young Indians.
