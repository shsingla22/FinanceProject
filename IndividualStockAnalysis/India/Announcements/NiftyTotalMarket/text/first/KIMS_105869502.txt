KIMS HOSPITALS announces expansion by setting up of  350 bed Super 
Specialty Hospital in Bengaluru, Karnataka 
 
30 th  May, 2024, Hyderabad 
  
PES is a university of repute and conducts several educational courses in various 
parts of the country. PES is in the process of sett ing up a medical college along 
with a teaching hospital at its campus at Electroni cs City, Bengaluru. PES is 
undertaking the construction and development of the  building to house an 
approximately 350 bedded hospital, comprising of Gr ound Floor and 11 Upper 
Floors, totally measuring 3,50,000 square feet of b uilt-up area, along with  
utility area and bunker for radiation oncology. KIM S Hospital Bengaluru Pvt. 
Ltd, a subsidiary of Krishna Institute of Medical S ciences Limited (KIMS) 
entered into a lease deed of 25 years and availed o n lease the land and hospital 
building, for the purposes of setting up the Super Specialty Hospital from PES. 
 
KIMS Hospital Bengaluru Private Limited (“the KIMS” ), a wholly-owned 
subsidiary of Krishna Institute of Medical Sciences  Limited also entered into a 
management agreement with PES to exclusively manage  the proposed 
under-construction hospital unit at Electronics Cit y, Bengaluru. The Super 
Specialty Hospital will be under the exclusive supe rvision and control of KIMS 
which shall be solely responsible for the proper an d efficient management.  
KIMS shall bill and collect all revenues of the Sup er Specialty Hospital and 
incur expenses for running the hospital. KIMS shall  be solely entitled to the Net 
Profits of the Super Specialty Hospital in each Fin ancial Year. 
 
Dr. B. Bhaskara Rao, CMD, KIMS Hospitals said “Our tie-up with PES for their 
upcoming 350 beds hospital is our second foray into  Bangalore and it 
underscores the strategic importance that we assign  to this city. Our first foray in 
Bangalore (415 bed hospital being built in Mahadeva pura) will be operational in 
second half of FY 25. We believe there is tremendou s opportunity for affordable 
and accessible clinical care brand like KIMS in Ban galore. We are confident that 
we will be able to replicate the KIMS model in Karn ataka too.”
