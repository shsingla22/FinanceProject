Appointment of Additional Directors on the Board of the Bank
This is to inform that the Board of Directors of IDBI Bank Ltd. at its meeting held

Brief Profile of Shri Abhijit Chakravorty
Shri Abhijit Chakravorty is a postgraduate in applied Chemistry and is a Certified
Associate of the Indian Institute of Bankers (CAIIB).
He is a seasoned banker and has a rich experience of more than 3 decades in SBI.
He superannuated in the rank of Dy. Managing Director from SBI and his last assignment
was as MD&CEO, SBI Cards & Payment Systems Ltd, the publicly listed credit card
subsidiary of SBI.
Apart from handling assignments in Retail, International Banking and IT verticals
of SBI, had exposure of 1 1 years in high value Corporate Credit involving Infrastructure,
Power, Steel, Edible Oil etc, in the Commercial and Mid Corporate Credit vertical of the
Bank. He also managed the operations of the SBI Global IT Centre in the capacity of
Chief General Manager and at other senior levels for a period of four years.
Shri Chakravorty has led the operations ofSBI in Bangladesh as CEO and Country
Head. He has also served at SBI’s Hongkong branch. He is presently an Independent
Director at BOBCard Ltd

Brief Profile of Shri Ketan Vikamsey
Shri Ketan Vikamsey is a Senior Partner of KKC & Associates LLP (formerly Khimji
Kunverji & Co LLP) since 1990. He is a Chartered Accountant with advanced professional
qualifications, including IFRS certification, a Diploma in Information System Audit (DISA),
certifications in derivatives, internal audit, IT and cyber security for board members and has
completed AICA Level-1 Al course.
He heads the firm’s Assurance Practice with wide-ranging experience in audits of large
corporates, banks, and financial institutions, including mutual funds, NBFCs, and asset
reconstruction companies, as well as regulatory inspections and investigations. He is also a
regular speaker at professional forums of ICAI, BC AS, RBI and CAG, and is actively
associated with charitable institutions and the Chamber of Tax Consultants.
. He has held senior leadership and technical roles across the profession, including
Chairman of the HLB India Federation, member of several IC AI board, committees, and
expert study groups, and contributor to authoritative publications on auditing and banking
standards. He has convened and participated in multiple IC AI working groups on audit
standards, bank audits, IT auditing, and accounting guidance, led award-winning professional
study circles, and served as a director on the boards of the Metropolitan Stock Exchange of
India Limited, Atlantis Investment Advisors Private Limited, mLB Technologies (Mumbai)
Private Limited, Miloni Consultants Private Limited and few other private limited companies.
He is presently an Independent Director in State Bank of India (SBI) and his term in
SBI will end on June 25, 2026.
