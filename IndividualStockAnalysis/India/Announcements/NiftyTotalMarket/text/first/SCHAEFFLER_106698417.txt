Schaeffler India Social Innovation Fellowship 2026 –27 to 
Empower Innovators Addressing India’s Social Challenges  
 
 
Pune , India  | July 15, 20 26 | Schaeffler India Limited (BSE: 505790, NSE: 
SCHAEFFLER),  a leading motion technology company,  today announced the launch 
of the Schaeffler India Social Innovation Fellowship 2026 -27, a future forward 
initiative  aimed at recogni zing and empowering individuals who are developing 
innovative, sustainable solutions that have the potential to drive meaningful social 
and environmental impact.  The initiative, now in its fifth  year , represents the 
company’s long -standing dedication to driving inclusive progress through 
innovation and technology . 
The fellowship is designed to identify, support and accelerate 10 high -potential 
social innovators  from across India, with a deliberate focus on entrepreneurs from 
Tier II, Tier III and rural geographies. Through financial grant, structured 
mentorship, industry exposure and access to leading innovation ecosystems, the 
program enables innovators to stre ngthen and scale solutions addressing some of 
India's most pressing social and environmental challenges. The 24-week cohort 
program delivered by IIMA Ventures  at IIM Ahmedabad , equip s fellows with 
business, impact and investor readiness capabilities.  
“At Schaeffler, we believe that technology and innovation have the power to create 
meaningful social change. The Schaeffler India Social Innovator Fellowship is more 
than a fellowship ; it is a platform that nurtures purpose -driven innovators, enabling 
them to transform bold ideas into scalable solutions that address some of India's 
most pressing development challenges ,” said Mr. Harsha Kadam , Managing 
Director and Chief Executive Officer,  Schaeffler India  Limited.  
Since its inception in 2021, the fellowship has supported over 4 1 early -stage social 
innovators across India, helping them strengthen their ventures through financial 
assistance, mentorship and access to industry networks. Several fellows have gone 
on to receive national recognition, attract investments and successfully  scale their 
innovations.  
Applications for the 2026 –27 cohort are open from July 15, 2026, and close on 
August 31, 2026  to individual innovators, startups and organisations (for -profit and 
non-profit) between 18 and 35 years of age who have developed functional 
prototypes and are working to solve critical development challenges  across 
healthcare, agriculture, education, sanitation & WASH, clean energy, livelihoods, 
manufacturing safety, mobility, environment and sustainability .

PUBLIC  Selected s tartups  will receive mentorship,  learning opportunities with IIMA 
Ventures, ecosystem access , along with fellowship grants  of Rs 2 Lakhs each ,

The selection process includes application screening, document verification, multi -
stage evaluation, interactions with subject matter experts, and a final jury 
presentation before industry leaders.  
The Schaeffler India Social Innovator Fellowship Program 2026 is implemented by 
IHFC (I -Hub Foundation  for Cobotics ), in partnership with India Accelerator serving 
as the program management and evaluation partner, and IIMA Ventures delivering 
the cohort -based mentorship and venture development program.
