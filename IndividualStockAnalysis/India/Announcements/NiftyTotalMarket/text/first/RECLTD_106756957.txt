Sub: Intimation regarding imposition of fines by Stock Exchanges.  
 
महोदय / महोदया, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), this is to inform that REC Limited 
(“REC” / “the Company”) has received notices dated August 25, 2026 from National Stock 
Exchange of India Limited (“NSE”) and BSE Limited (“BSE”) regarding non-compliance during 
the quarter ended June 30, 2026, with certain provisions of the Listing Regulations pertaining to 
composition of Board of Directors and Committees thereof and have imposed a fine of 
₹10,77,340/- each by NSE & BSE for such non-compliance. 
 
In this regard, it is submitted that, REC being a Government Company and as per provisions of 
Article 91 of its Articles of Association, the power to appoint Directors on the Board of the 
Company is vested with the President of India acting through its administrative Ministry i.e. 
Ministry of Power, Government of India and the Company has no role in the appointment of 
Directors.  However, the Company has been requesting its administrative Ministry 
for the appointment of requisite number of Independent Directors on the Board of the 
Company, from time to time.
