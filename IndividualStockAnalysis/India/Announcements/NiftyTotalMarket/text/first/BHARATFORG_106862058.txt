Hon’ble Union Home Minister Shri Amit Shah Visits Bharat Forge's Pune Facility  
- Inaugurates Milestone Plaque to Mark the Company's 60th Anniversary - 
- Flags off consignment of 5.56 x 45 mm rifles for the Uttar Pradesh Special Task Force - 
 
Pune | November 16, 2026: Hon'ble Union Minister of Home Affairs and Minister of Cooperation, 
Government of India , Shri Amit Shah, visited the Kalyani - Bharat Forge facility in Pune, the world's largest 
forging facility , on November 15. He was received  by Padma Bhushan  Baba Kalyani, Chairman and Managing 
Director, Bharat Forge Ltd . 
The Hon’ble Home Minister inaugurated a milestone plaque to commemorate Bharat Forge's 60th 
Anniversary . The plaque marks six decades of the Company’s journey in engineering excellence, innovation 
and nation building.  
During the visit, he toured the forging lines and interacted with the leadership on the Company’s growth over 
the years. The discussion covered Bharat Forge’s role in strengthening India’s manufacturing and defence 
capabilities, in line with the vision of Aatmanirbhar Bharat  and Sashakt Bharat , Surakshit Bharat . 
The Hon’ble Home Minister was also shown the range of indigenously designed and manufactured products, 
including small arms , artillery guns, ammunition systems, unmanned systems in air, land and water  - by 
Kalyani Strategic Systems Limited (KSSL), the wholly -owned defence subsidiary of Bharat Forge. These 
products show the company’s contribution to self -reliance in defence manufacturing.  
Hon’ble Home Minister flagged off the first consignment  of 5.56 x 45 mm rifles  for the Uttar Pradesh Special 
Task Force (UPSTF) .

About Bharat Forge  
Bharat Forge Limited (BFL), a Pune -based multinational company, is a technology -driven global leader in providing 
innovative, high -performance and safety -critical components and solutions across automotive, aerospace, defence, rail, 
marine, energy and industrial sectors. With a global manufacturing footprint and world -class engineering capabilities, 
BFL is at the forefront of supporting India’s strategic industrial and defence ambitions.  
About Kalyani Strategic Systems Limited  
Kalyani Strategic Systems Limited (KSSL), a wholly owned subsidiary of Bharat Forge Limited, drives the defence business 
of the Kalyani Group, one of the world’s foremost engineering and metallurgical enterprises. With more than six decades 
of expertise in  design, engineering, metallurgy and advanced manufacturing, KSSL delivers sovereign defence capability 
across the land, marine and unmanned domains - spanning artillery systems, armoured and protected vehicles, small 
arms, ammunition, and marine and unman ned platforms. Beyond supplying battle -ready systems, KSSL enables partner 
nations to build their own defence industrial base through technology partnership, local industrialisation and assured 
through -life support, backed by proven production at scale and  security of supply. Trusted by both Indian and global 
armed forces and governments seeking resilient, self -reliant capability, KSSL is a trusted global defence partner of 
choice.
