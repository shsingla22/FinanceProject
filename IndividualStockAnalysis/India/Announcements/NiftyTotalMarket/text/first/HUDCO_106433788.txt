Sir/Madam, 
In line with the Corporate Disclosure requirements under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, this is to inform that HUDCO has 
launched dedicated ‘Urban Invest Window’ (UiWIN) i.e. one-stop, end-to-end support platform 
for Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) in developing bankable infrastructure projects and to offer 
solutions such as: 
 
i. Capacity Building to ULBs 
ii. Project Formulation and Asset Monetization 
iii. Access to Financial and Capital Markets 
iv. Management of existing Assets 
 
The weblink of the launch event is given below for ready reference. 
 
https://www.hudco.org.in/writereaddata/National-Urban-Conclave.mp4  
 
The above initiative is aligned with planned business growth of HUDCO. 
 
This is for your kind information.
