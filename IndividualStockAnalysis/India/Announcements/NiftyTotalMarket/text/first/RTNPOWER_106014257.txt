Dear Sirs, 
 
This has reference to our earlier intimation dated June 23, 2023, whereby the Exchange had been informed 
about the issuance and allotment of Series I , Series II and Series III unlisted, secured Non-Convertible 
Debentures ( NCDs) to certain external entities/ lenders. The NCDs were for an aggregate amount of INR 
1125,00,00,000/- (Rupees One thousand one hundred and twenty ﬁve crores), with a portion thereof 
interchangeable into a Rupee Term Loan of INR 100,00,00,000/- (Rupees One hundred crores). The NCDs

point of time.

the Rupee Term Loan Agreement), the Company has been regularly servicing the aforementioned debts. 
 
Pursuant to and in compliance with the requirements of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with the SEBI Circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD-
1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023, the Company is pleased to inform that now,  consequent to the 
aforementioned  repayments so made by the Company from time to time,  the external indebtedness of the 
Company aggregating to INR 510,00,00,000/-( Rupees Five hundred and ten crore), as mentioned below, 
stands repaid in full, i.e. the principal amount as well as the interest accrued thereon: 
 
(a) An aggregate of INR 410,00,00,000/- (Rupees Four hundred ten crores) represented by 41,000 (Forty 
one thousand) Series -1, rated, unlisted secured, Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of face value 
INR 1,00,000/- (Rupees One lakh) each. 
(b) a Rupee Term Loan of INR 100,00,00,000/- (Rupees One hundred crores) 
 
The Company continues to service its principal and interest repayments of Series II and III, Non-Convertible 
Debentures as per the Debenture Trust Deed.
