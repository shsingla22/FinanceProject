This is in continuation of our previous (i) intimation dated 8th April, 2024, wherein we had informed the stock 
exchange that the Company has executed (a) Share Purchase Agreement dated 8th April, 2024 (“ SPA”) with Ismartu

approved the Proposed Transaction.

consideration of INR 275,98,73,643/- on 13th August, 2024 (subject to post closing adjustment in terms of the SPA). 
Accordingly, the Target Company has become a subsidiary of the Company.
