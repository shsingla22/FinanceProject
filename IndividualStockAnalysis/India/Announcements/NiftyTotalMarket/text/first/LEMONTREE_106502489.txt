Lemon Tree Hotels announces the signing of its new property in Uttar  Pradesh  
 
New Delhi, 20th January  2026: Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -growing mid -
scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the signing of its latest property – Keys 
Select by Lemon Tree Hotels, Chitrakoot , Uttar  Pradesh.  
 
The property shall be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited. This signing will further strengthen the 
company’s presence in Uttar  Pradesh , while catering to both religious and leisure travellers 
for a fresh, vibrant, and value -driven experience .   
 
Property Highlights  
 
Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels, Chitrakoot , Uttar  Pradesh  is located at  the 
spiritually significant town nestled on the banks of the Mandakini River, revered as a place 
where Lord Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana spent years of their exile. Dotted with sacred 
ghats, forests, and temples, it beautifully blends mythological heritage with t ranquil 
natural surroundings.  
 
The key features of the hotel will include:  
 
• 48 well-appointed rooms  
• A restaurant  
• A banquet hall and a meeting room  
• Recreational facilit ies includ ing a fitness centre and a swimming pool  
 
The Chitrakoot Airport is at a distance of 10 kms from the property  while the Prayagraj 
Airport, is approximately 105 kms away. The Chitrakutdham Karwi Railway Station  is just 
5kms from the property. The hotel is well connected by both public and private transport.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, Lemon 
Tree Hotels, commented, “With this signing, we are pleased to extend our religious and 
leisure  portfolio at a place ideal for pilgrims and travellers seeking spiritual reflection, quiet 
contemplation, and a meaningful connection with India’s timeless heritage . The state has 
eight operational and fourteen  upcoming hotels ."
