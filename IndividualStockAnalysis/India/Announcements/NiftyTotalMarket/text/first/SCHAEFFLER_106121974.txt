Schaeffler India showcases smart & innovative motion 
technology solutions at IMTEX 2025, Bengaluru  
Pune, India | January 23, 2025  | Schaeffler India, a leading motion technology company, is 
presenting its cutting -edge solutions for machine tools, robotics, and industrial automation 
at IMTEX 2025 in Bengaluru , with a focus on  empower ing the  industry with precision, 
efficiency, and high -performance technologies . 
As a trusted partner in the machine tool industry and a key enabler of automation, 
Schaeffler India combines its expertise in precision engineering and advanced motion 
technology with its deep understanding of customer challenges. This approach allows the 
company to provide customized solutions that enable its customers and partners to achieve 
operational excellence and address the dynamic needs of modern manufacturing.  
Mr. Seshan Iyer, President of Bearings & Industrial Solutions , Schaeffler India stated, 
“IMTEX Bengaluru is the perfect platform to reaffirm our dedication to supporting the 
leading OEMs in the machine tool and manufacturing sectors with advanced solutions 
designed for precision, reliability, and sustainability. This platform allows us to con nect with 
industry stakeholders to drive innovation and strengthen India’s position in the global 
industrial ecosystem . Visitors to our booth will have a first -hand experience to our solutions 
that aims to address the ch allenges of precision and efficiency in industrial applications.”  
Schaeffler India ’s booth at the IMTEX 2025  will highlight:  
• Comprehensive Products & Services that Guide, Generate and Sustain Motion : 
Schaeffler India will showcase its high -performance spindle bearings, linear 
guidance systems, rotary tables, and mechatronic components for machine tools. 
These solutions are designed to maximize productivity and reliability in high -
precision application s. 
• Robotics and Industrial Automation Innovations:  Visitors will experience 
Schaeffler’s state -of-the-art motion systems for robotics, including precision 
components for robotic arms, collaborative robots (co -bots), and automated 
guided vehicles (AGVs).  
• Keeping Machines Rolling with Conditions Monitoring S olutions : Schaeffler India 
will highlight its sensor -integrated condition monitoring and predictive 
maintenance  products  for the machine lifetime , ensuring seamless operations and 
reduced downtime.  
• Ultra  High -Performance  Products That Unlock New Possibilities : Characterized by 
higher basic dynamic load ratings compared to conventional products , Schaeffler’s 
X-life portfolio  of products have  heightened endurance, resilience under stress, 
and enormous flexibility , that can overcome even the most challenging of 
obstacles and environments . 
 
2 Schaeffler is driving India’s industrial transformation with products tailored to local needs. 
As a key partner in the industrial and machine tools sector , Schaeffler India is committed to 
delivering higher value to its stakeholders . Visitors are invited to explore these cutting -edge 
solutions at  The IMTEX Expo 2025  Hall – 3B, Booth – B 103, located at the Bangalore 
International Exhibition Centre (BIEC)  from 23rd to 29th January 2025.
