This is to inform that 49th Annual General Meeting (AGM) of members of NHPC Limited is scheduled

registered with the Company/RTA/Depositories.

a) The Company has fixed Thursday, August 14, 2025  as “Record Date ” for determining

Companies Act, 2013.
