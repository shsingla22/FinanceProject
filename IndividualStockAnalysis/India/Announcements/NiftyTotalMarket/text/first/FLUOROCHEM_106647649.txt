We were appointed as statutory auditors of GFCL EV Products Limited ("the Company") by the 
shareholders in the First Annual General Meeting held on 26 September 2022, for a period of five 
consecutive years from the conclusion of the First Annual General Meeting till the conclusion of Sixth 
Annual General Meeting to be held in FY 2027-28. We have completed the statutory audit for FY 2025-
26 and submitted unmodified audit report dated 25th May, 2026. 
As per the terms of the recent Shareholders Agreement ("SHA") between the Company and 
International Finance Corporation, the Company is required to appoint a member of Big Five 
Accountir:ig Firm (as defined in the SHA) as the statutory auditor of the Company. 
In view of above, to facilitate this transition and enable the Company to comply with the SHA, we 
tender our resignation as statutory auditors of the Company effective from the conclusion of the 
forthcoming Annual General Meeting {AGM) of the Company. We further confirm that there is no 
other reason for our resignation. 
We thank the management and staff for their cooperation during our tenure. Please file the necessary 
forms/intimation with the Registrar of Companies, SEBI, Stock Exchanges and other regulatory
