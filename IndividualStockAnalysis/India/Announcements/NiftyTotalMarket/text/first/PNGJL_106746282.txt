PNG Jewellers Enters NCR with New Store in Gurugram 
Gurugram, August 15, 2026: PNG Jewellers, one of India's leading legacy jewellery brands, 
has strengthened its presence in the Delhi NCR region with the launch of a new company-
owned, company-o perated store in R2, Ground Floor, M3M Route 65, Sector -65, Gurugram, 
Haryana, on August 15, 2026. Spread across 3,500 sq. ft., the new showroom has been launched 
in response to growing customer demand and forms part of the brand's continued retail 
expansion across key markets. 
The store was inaugurated by actress Chitrangada Singh in the presence of Dr. Saurabh Gadgil, 
Chairman and Managing Director, PNG Jewellers, employees and well -wishers. The new showroom 
offers customers PNG Jewellers' comprehensive portfolio across gold, diamond and silver jewellery, 
bringing together all the brand's collections across categories, under one roof. The store has 
been designed to cater to the diverse jewellery requirements of customers in Gurugram, including 
everyday wear, festive purchases, weddings, gifting and milestone occasion purchases. 
Commenting on the launch, Chitrangada said,  "PNG Jewellers brings with it a legacy built on trust, 
craftsmanship and an enduring understanding of what customers value. As the brand enters this new 
market, customers here will have the opportunity to experience that legacy first-h and through 
its thoughtfully designed jewellery and commitment to quality. I am delighted to welcome PNG 
Jewellers to the region and wish the team every success in this new chapter.” 
Gurugram is one of India’s most affluent and lifestyle-d riven urban centres, with a strong 
concentration of multinational corporations, technology, consulting, finance and corporate 
services. Its high-i ncome, globally exposed consumer base has developed a strong affinity for fashion, 
premium experiences and contemporary jewellery.  
To mark the opening, customers can avail of up to 50% off on gold jewellery making charges and up 
to 100% off on diamond jewellery making charges from August 15 to August 31, 2026, subject to 
applicable terms and conditions. The introductory offers are intended to provide customers 
with added value while exploring the brand's extensive jewellery portfolio. 
“Gurugram is an important market in our national expansion journey, and the launch of our new 
store in Sector 65 is a direct response to the growing demand we have seen from customers in the 
region. With a comprehensive portfolio across gold, diamond and silver jewellery, this store will 
allow us to serve customers more closely while delivering the trust, craftsmanship and experience 
associated with PNG Jewellers. We look forward to becoming a part of the local community and 
building lasting relationships with PNG Jewellers’ distinctive designs, tailored to preferences of 
Gurugram’s discerning customers,” said Dr. Saurabh Gadgil, Chairman and Managing Director, PNG 
Jewellers. 
 
 
The Gurugram launch further strengthens PNG Jewellers' retail network as the brand continues to 
expand into markets where customer demand for its products and services is growing. The company -
owned, company -operated format enables PNG Jewellers to maintain  consistency in its product 
assortment, customer experience and service standards while building a closer connection with local 
consumers.  
With its growing presence across India, PNG Jewellers continues to combine its longstanding heritage 
with contemporary designs and evolving retail formats, strengthening its accessibility to customers across established and emerging jewellery markets.
