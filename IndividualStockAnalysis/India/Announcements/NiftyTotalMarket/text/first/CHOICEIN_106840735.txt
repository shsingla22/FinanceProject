Commenting on the performance, Mr. Kamal Poddar, Managing Director, Choice 
International Limited, said:  

“Our strongest -ever quarterly performance reinforces our strategy of building complementary 
businesses around India ’s growing investment, financing and development needs. As these 
businesses scale, our priority is to deepen customer relationships and make greater use of the 
Group ’s shared distribution, technology and expertise to drive sustainable earnings.  
The ₹900 crore investment by NH Group is a significant step in this journey, bringing capital and 
international expertise to support our next phase of growth. We intend to build on this partnership 
to expand beyond retail broking and strengthen our instit utional, wealth and asset management 
capabilities. Alongside our advisory businesses, these capabilities position Choice to serve 
customers through more stages of their financial journey, with disciplined capital allocation and 
execution guiding our expans ion.” 
Key business developments  
Broking and Distribution:   
Choice added 1.73 lakh demat accounts, taking the total to 13.78 lakh, representing 14% year -
on-year growth. Its distribution network comprised 218 branches, client assets under custody 
stood at ₹66,300 crore and the MTF book reached ₹463 crore.  
Following  the quarter -end, Choice Equity Broking received ₹900 crore in its bank account, 
completing the strategic investment by NH Investment & Securities of South Korea. The funds will 
primarily support MTF expansion, while the partnership creates opportunities t o strengthen 
capabilities across other financial services businesses.  
Wealth Distribution: Total AUM stood at approximately ₹ 6,047 crore, comprising mutual fund AUM 
of ₹4,108 crore and PMS and AIF AUM of ₹291 crore. Within mutual funds, equity AUM grew 49%  
year-on-year to ₹3,013 crore from ₹2,023 crore, while PMS and AIF AUM increased 127% from 
₹128 crore. Structured and fixed -income product distribution reached ₹1,636 crore in H1 FY27, 
compared with ₹512 crore a year earlier  and surpassing the full -year FY 26 volume of ₹745 crore.  
Our partnership with India Post Payments Bank, launched on a pilot basis in a few selected cities, 
has onboarded over 25,000 customers and generated AUM of ₹1.12 crore.  
Insurance: The business sold 75,173 policies and generated pre miums of ₹81 crore during the 
quarter.  
Government Advisory:   
The business closed with an order book of ₹710 crore. The proposed acquisition of Feedback 
Infra advanced with receipt of a Letter of Intent from the Resolution Professional, confirming the 
Commi ttee of Creditors ’ approval of the resolution plan. Completion remains subject to NCLT 
approval and other applicable requirements.  
Investment Banking:  
Choice handled five IPOs that collectively raised approximately ₹680 crore across the mainboard 
and SME platforms during the quarter, with one offering subscribed approximately 177 times. 
Execution momentum continued into October, while the IPO pipeline comprises 24 mandates with 
estimated fundraising of ₹6,210 crore.  
NBFC:  
The total loan book stood at ₹935  crore. Our focus remains on growing the retail lending business, 
supported by disciplined underwriting and prudent risk management.  
Asset Management:   
The NFO for our first actively managed scheme, the Choice Large & Mid Cap Fund, closes 
tomorrow. We plan  to launch additional products to broaden our offering and address a wider 
range of investor needs.  
About Choice International Limited  
Choice International Limited is a diversified financial services and consulting group headquartered 
in Mumbai and listed on BSE and NSE. Serving over 1.7 million clients through a network of 
70,000+ business associates, the Group offers technology -driven solutions across broking, 
wealth management, lending, insurance, investment banking, asset management, and 
government and infrastructure consultancy. Recognized as a Great Place to Work, Choice 
combines its pan -India distribution network with digital capabilities to expand access to financial 
services and support long -term wealth creation. The Group remains committed to advan cing 
financial inclusion and creating lasting value for its clients, shareholders and communities.
