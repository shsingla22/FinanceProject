“As the Transferor Company is a wholly owned  subsidiary of the Transferee

certificates, if any, and/or the shares in electronic form representing the shares in 
the Transferor Company shall be deemed to be cancelled without any further act 
or deed for cancellation thereof and shall cease to be in existence accordingly.”
