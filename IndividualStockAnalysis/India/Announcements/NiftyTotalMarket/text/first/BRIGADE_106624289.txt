PRESS RELEASE  
 
BuzzWorks Marks Strategic Entry into  Mysuru; Unveils Multi -City Expansion Blueprint for 2026  
 
Briga de Group’s managed office brand targets rising demand from GCCs and startups with new Grade -A centres 
across  Mysuru, Bengaluru, and Chennai  
 
Bengaluru, May 1 5, 2026: BuzzWorks, the premium managed office and co -working arm of the Brigade 
Group, has announced its strategic foray into  Mysuru  with the launch of Brigade Vantage on Vinoba Road. 
This milestone marks Buzzworks’ entry into a fast maturing technology ecosystem,  with a superior offering 
that is designed to address a critical gap where the supply of Grade -A managed facilities remains relatively 
limited. The new centre, which offers 500+seats is located within the 1.3 lakh sq. ft. Brigade Vantage 
commercial  campus which integrates modern, enterprise -ready infrastructure with design elements inspired 
by Mysuru’s rich cultural heritage, ensuring the workspace feels deeply rooted in its local environment.  
 
Commenting on the initiative, Siddharth Verma, Business Head, BuzzWorks, said, " Our entry into  Mysuru  is 
a testament to BuzzWorks’ strategic growth and our commitment to leading the expansion into Tier -2 cities 
across India. BuzzWorks would be the first Grade A managed workspace in the city, and we believe the first -
mover advantage, c oupled with the market demand, will be a great fit for the brand and the business. By 
delivering institutional -grade managed workspaces, we are enabling corporates and GCCs to seamlessly 
expand their operations into emerging hubs that were previously under served. This launch is one of the 
components of a larger multi -city expansion blueprint that we will implement during this financial year. 
Enterprises today are looking for the same infrastructure, compliance, and experience standards in Tier -2 
cities that  they exist in major metros. What we are offering is a plug -and-play environment that bridges the 
quality gap and empowers businesses to scale with confidence and speed.”  
 
BuzzWorks will shortly be expanding in Brigade Tech Gardens in Whitefield, Bengaluru as well as a new 
facility at Brigade Elysium in Varthur. Also, sustained demand has prompted the brand to expand 
BuzzWorks’ within the World Trade Center (WTC) campus at B rigade Gateway Complex in Rajajinagar, 
Bengaluru. BuzzWorks is focused on driving its expansion strategy across markets, with growth at over a 
100% compared to the last financial year and are on their way to double the portfolio this FY.  
 
Beyond Karnataka, BuzzWorks is also making its debut in Chennai at Brigade Tech Boulevard. This move 
capitalizes on Chennai’s 25% growth in GCC activity and its status as a national leader in BFSI and 
automotive engineering leasing. Across all these new lo cations, the strategy is to provide a distributed 
portfolio that meets the needs of an evolving corporate ecosystem.  
 
About BuzzWorks: BuzzWorks is a managed workspace provider offering strategic office solutions 
designed for today’s evolving workforce. Part of Brigade Group, BuzzWorks creates environments where 
energy, collaboration, and innovation come together naturally.
