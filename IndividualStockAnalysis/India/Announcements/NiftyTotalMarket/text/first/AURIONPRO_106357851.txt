Aurionpro and DFCC Bank Announce Strategic 
Collaboration in AI  
Partnership set to redefine digital banking experiences at 
DFCC Bank through Deep Learning and Gen AI -powered solutions  
Mumbai/Singapore – 1st September 2025:   Aurionpro Solutions 
Limited (BSE: 532668 | NSE: AURIONPRO ), an enterprise 
technology leader, announces a strategic partnership with DFCC 
Bank PLC , a leading bank in Sri Lanka, to collaborate on the 
development and im plementation of Predictive Artificial 
Intelligence (AI) and Generative AI (Gen AI) use cases aimed at 
enhancing the Bank’s digital capabilities and competitive edge.  
 
The partnership marks the beginning of a long -term, future -
focused commitment to co -create innovative AI -driven solutions 
that will reshape banking operations, elevate customer 
engagement, and enable data -driven decision -making. Aurionpro 
will leverage its deep domain expertise in advanced technologies 
to develop scalable and customized solut ions for DFCC Bank’s 
digital transformation journey.  
Commenting on the partnership, Deekshith Marla, 
Co-founder of Arya.ai – an Aurionpro Company , 
said: "We are delighted to partner with DFCC Bank 
as they embark on an AI -led transformation. Our 
combined strengths will enable the delivery of 
intelligent and impactful solutions that align 
with the Bank ’s vision for the future of       
financial servi ces." 
 

              
Thimal Perera, CEO of DFCC Bank PLC,  said, “We see 
artificial intelligence as a means to bring us 
closer to our customers. As we integrate AI across  
our operations, the purpose is clear - to reduce 
friction, sharpen our service delivery, and 
create banking experiences that feel intuitive, 
relevant, and human in an increasingly digital 
world. We are happy to be taking this step as part of our broader 
strategy to build a more responsive, insight -driven, and 
customer -centric bank. ” 
This partnership un derscores Aurionpro’s role as a trusted 
innovation partner to the BFSI sector globally, helping 
institutions like DFCC Bank stay ahead in an increasingly digital 
and competitive market landscape. It also reaffirms Aurionpro’s  
commitment to expanding its AI -led innovation, leveraging its 
growing portfolio of enterprise AI solutions and deep domain 
experience.
