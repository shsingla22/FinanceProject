Sub.: Investigation into the suspected insider trading activities of certain entities in the 
scrip of Gujarat Mineral Development Corporation Limited -Administrative Warning SEBIWHO/VD-1/|D15/\/6329/2026 
March 06, 2026 
1. SEBI has conducted an investigation in the matter of suspected insider trading activities of 
certain entities in the scrip of Gujarat Mineral Development Corporation Limited (GMDCL) 
during the period December 01, 2023-January 31 2024 (Investigation Period). In this regard 
inter-alia, following was observed: 
1.1. GMDCL vide press release dated January 08, 2024 has disclosed receipt of 
Environmental Clearance for Capacity Expansion from 3 MTPA to 5 MTPA of Lignite for 
Sukrha(N) Lignite Mine, by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change 
(MOEFCC) 
1.2. GMDCL vide its response dated November 26, 2025 has submitted MOEFCC letter dated 
February 16, 2024 granting approval for Environmental Clearance to GMDCL for Capacity 
Expansion from 3 MTPA to 5 MTPA of Lignite for Sukrha(N) Lignite Mine. 
2. Based on above, it is observed that the Press Release dated January 08, 2024 ought to have 
reflected the factual position having regard to the principles governing disclosures and 
obligations prescribed in the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015. 
3. You are, therefore, advised to be careful in future and improve your compliance standards to 
avoid recurrence of such instances, failing which action as deemed fit, will be initiated against 
you in accordance with the provisions of SEBI Act, 1992 and Rules & Regulations framed 
thereunder.
