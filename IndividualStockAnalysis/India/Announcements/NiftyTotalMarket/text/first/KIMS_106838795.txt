Sub: Expansion of KIMS Saveera Hospital, Anantapur, from 250 to 500 Beds. 
 
With reference to the aforesaid matter, we wish to inform you that Saveera Institute of Medical 
Sciences Private Limited, Subsidiary of the Company operating under the brand name “KIMS Saveera 
Hospital” Anantapur, has expanded its bed capacity from 250 to 500 beds, marking a significant 
milestone in strengthening the Company's healthcare infrastructure and enhancing access to advanced 
tertiary and quaternary healthcare services across the Rayalaseema region of Andhra Pradesh. 
 
The newly added facilities, which are being inaugurated today, i.e., November 1, 2026, include a 
comprehensive Oncology Block equipped with advanced medical infrastructure such as a High 
Definition Linear Accelerator (HD-LINAC), Digital PET-CT, Brachytherapy and other state-of-the-
art diagnostic and treatment facilities. The expansion also includes a dedicated Mother and Child 
Block, supported by specialised neonatal intensive care facilities, to provide comprehensive maternal, 
newborn and paediatric healthcare services in the region. 
 
This expansion represents a significant step towards enhancing the hospital's clinical capabilities, 
broadening its range of specialised healthcare services and reinforcing its position as a leading 
healthcare provider in the Rayalaseema region.
