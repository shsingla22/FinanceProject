Happiest Minds  Launches  Rel(AI)Build , an Agentic AI Platform to 
Transform Enterprise Software Delivery    
Bengaluru, Seattle, and London,  June  11, 2026 ,  Happiest Minds Technologies Limited 
(NSE:HAPPSTMNDS), an AI First, customer -centric digital engineering company,  today announced 
the launch of  Rel(AI)Build , its proprietary Agentic AI development platform, along with the Agentic 
Development Lifecycle (ADLC) , a structured, governed methodology designed to integrate AI across 
the entire software development lifecycle.  
.Rel(AI)Build : A Unified Agentic Platform   
 
Rel(AI)Build is an enterprise -grade, agentic platform designed to transform how organizations build, 
modernize, and operate software systems. The platform brings together AI -driven automation, 
governance, and human oversight into a single, unified software delivery fra mework.  
Built on an Agentic Development Lifecycle (ADLC), Rel(AI)Build orchestrates specialized, context -aware 
AI agents across a structured lifecycle , from planning through operations. This enables parallel 
execution, intelligent task routing, and continuous learning through shared contextual memory, 
significantly improving delivery speed and consistency.  
At the core of Rel (AI)Build is a composable, registry -driven Agent Core that integrates seamlessly with 
existing enterprise ecosystems. The platform is designed with governance by default, embedding 
policy controls, auditability, and human -in-the-loop mechanisms to ensure responsible  and 
transparent AI adoption. A key differentiator of Rel(AI)Build is its token -sensitive architecture, which 
optimizes how AI models are invoked and utilized across workflows. By intelligently managing token 
consumption, contex t usage, and model interactions, the platform helps enterprises control costs, 
improve efficiency, and ensure predictable performance , which is critical for scaling AI adoption in
