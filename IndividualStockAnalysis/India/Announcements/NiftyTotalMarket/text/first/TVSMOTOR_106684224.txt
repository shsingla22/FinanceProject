TVS LAUNCHES THE ALL -NEW CALLISTO 110 IN 
JAKARTA, A MODERN SCOOTER DELIVERING 
EXCEPTIONAL COMFORT FOR URBAN COMMUTERS  
● Built on the philosophy of “Built for Real,” the scooter comes equipped with a range 
of standout features, including an extra-large storage compartment, a long and 
comfortable seat, a metal body, Front Fuel Filling, an Emergency Brake Signal, Auto-
Off Turn Signal, and other modern features designed to appeal to consumers in 
Indonesia’s 110cc scooter market. 
● The All -New TVS Callisto 110 is available in four attractive color options: Mint Green, 
Moon Silver, Solid White, and Peach Perfect. 
 
Jakarta, July 2, 2026 – TVS Motor Company (TVSM), part of TVS VENU, and a reputed two and three-
wheeler manufacturer globally, today launched the revolutionary scooter, TVS The All New Callisto 
110, in Jakarta. Embodying the spirit of #BuiltForReal , the new model is designed to meet the needs 
of dynamic urban commuters who seek a scooter capable of supporting their diverse everyday mobility 
needs.  
 
Recognizing that modern vehicles must offer more than just a means of transportation, TVS The All 
New Callisto 110 is designed to elevate the daily riding experience through superior comfort, smart 
features that enhance everyday commute convenience, and a relevant modern design. As a result, 
every journey becomes more comfortable, practical, efficient, and enjoyable; while also addressing the 
real-world mobility needs of Indonesian riders.  
 
This launch marks an important strategic milestone for TVS in Indonesia. According to data from the 
Asosiasi Industri Sepeda Motor Indonesia (AISI), the 110cc automatic scooter segment remains the 
backbone of the market, accounting for approximately 5 0% of total two -wheeler sales in Indonesia. 
Through the TVS The All New Callisto 110, TVS is poised to strengthen its position and capture a larger 
share of this key segment by offering a product that delivers superior technology and comfort.  
 
Speaking about the launch,  Mr. Peyman Kargar, President, International Business, TVS Motor 
Company , said, “Indonesia is a key market for TVS, and we are excited to introduce TVS The All New 
Callisto 110 —a scooter that truly embodies the ‘Built for Real’ philosophy and is designed to meet the 
lifestyle needs of Indonesian consumers. ” 
 
“For us, ‘Built for Real’ means enhancing the everyday riding experience through maximum comfort, 
intuitive technology, and a style riders can take pride in. With a range of distinctive features, including 
Auto-off Turn Signal and Emergency Brake Warning, we are not only delivering greater convenience 
but also introducing something new to the 110cc scooter segment,”  said Anthony Ho, Regional 
Business Head – Indonesia & Singapore TVS Motor Company.  
 
On the same occasion, TVS also hosted a fireside chat  featuring Nex Carlos (food vlogger), Fina 
Philipe  (sport & automotive enthusiast), and Bilal Indrajaya  — an emerging singer- songwriter who has 
gained popularity among Gen Z and millennial audiences. They shared their perspectives on how the 
 
   
 
 
 
   
 
TVS Callisto 110 supports their urban lifestyles, from discovering new culinary spots and maintaining 
active training routines to navigating their daily creative journeys. They also discussed what a truly 
#BuiltForReal vehicle means in the context of their  everyday lives.  
The All New TVS Callisto 110 comes packed with premium features, including several first -in-class 
technologies:  
 
Next -Generation Performance & Efficiency  
● More Powerful New Engine, equipped with an Integrated Starter Generator (ISG) - for a 
smoother, quieter, and more comfortable engine start.  
● Next -Gen Stop- Go Technology - Automatically shuts off the engine during brief stops, 
improving fuel efficiency by up to 10%.  
 
Outstanding Comfort & Convenience  
● Super Spacious 33 -Liter Storage, A massive under -seat compartment  capable of fitting two 
half-face helmets.  
● Front Fuel Filling, A front -facing fuel inlet that allows riders and passengers to refuel easily 
without having to get off the scooter.  
● Smart All -in-One Lock, Turn on the ignition, open the storage compartment, and unlock the 
fuel port all from a single key slot. It also features a glove box equipped with a USB port for 
mobile charging.  
● Features a long and wide seat, 3-step adjustable rear shocks, and Body Balance 
Technology to deliver a softer, more stable, and highly comfortable riding experience.  
 
Above- Average Style, Quality, and Safety  
● Tough Metal Body: Built to be robust, durable, and long- lasting.  
● Emergency Brake Signal: A safety feature designed to alert riders behind you. When braking 
suddenly at speeds of at least 50 km/h, the rear emergency brake lights will flash automatically.  
● Infinity Light Bar: A modern, infinity -designed headlight that creates a distinct and premium 
visual impression.  
 
The launch event concluded with a performance by Bilal Indrajaya,  who represents the "Built for Real" 
spirit through his authentic character, music, and personality.  
 
TVS The All New Callisto 110 is available in four attractive color options, starting from Mint Green, 
Moon Silver, Solid White,  and Peach Perfect . This scooter is marketed at a price of IDR20,900,000 
(On-The-Road Jakarta). During a limited period, TVS The All New Callisto 110 is offered at a special 
launching price of IDR19,900,000 (On-The- Road Jakarta), only for the first 150 buyers. TVS The All 
New Callisto 110 can be ordered at all official TVS dealer networks in Indonesia starting July 2, 2026.
