Performance Review of Q2 FY2 7 & H1 FY27  
 
Strong Q2 performance with Revenue growth of 22.5% YoY (12.2% in constant currency)  
Adjusted EBITDA at 26.3% with 23.0% growth YoY  
Adjusted PAT at 1 6.0% with 8.1% growth YoY  
 
Bengaluru, India | Oct  22, 202 6: Sagility Limited (NSE: SAGILITY, BSE: 544282), a leading global healthcare 
operations partner that helps U.S . payers, providers, and pharmacy benefit managers reduce cost s, improve 
quality, and enhance member and provider experiences, reported its consolidated financial results for the 
quarter Q2 FY27 ended September  30, 2026 , according to IndAS.  
 
Financial highlights for the quarter ended Sep  30, 202 6: 
 
• Revenue at ₹ 20,321 million (US$ 2 12.6 million), YoY growth of 2 2.5% (1 2.2% in CC terms)  
• Organic YoY growth of 2 2.2% (1 1.9% in CC terms)  
• Adjusted EBITDA at ₹ 5,353 million (US$ 56.0 million) at 2 6.3% of revenue, YoY growth of 2 3.0% 
• Adjusted PAT at ₹ 3,254 million (US$ 34.0 million) at 1 6.0% of revenue, YoY growth of 8.1%  
• Basic Earnings per share (EPS) at ₹ 0.61, YoY growth of 13.3% 
• Adjusted Basic Earnings per share (EPS) at ₹ 0.70, YoY growth of 8.1% 
 
Financial highlights for the half year  ended Sep  30, 2026:  
 
• Revenue at ₹ 39,956 million (US$ 420.4  million), YoY growth of 2 5.0% (1 3.7% in CC terms)  
• Organic YoY growth of 2 4.7% (1 3.4% in CC terms)  
• Adjusted EBITDA at ₹ 10,069  million (US$ 105.9 million) at 2 5.2% of revenue, YoY growth of 2 5.2% 
• Adjusted PAT at ₹ 5,951 million (US$ 62.6 million) at 1 4.9% of revenue, YoY growth of 18.9% 
• Basic Earnings per share (EPS) at ₹ 1.07, YoY growth of 25.4% 
• Adjusted Basic Earnings per share (EPS) at ₹ 1.27, YoY growth of 18.9% 
 
Business Highlights:  
• Sagility secured $ 40.2 million of ACV from 31 existing clients and 1 new logo.  
• Added 28 client groups  in H1 FY27  (including clients acquired  through the CareSeed  acquisition ), bringing

opportunities.  
• Employees: At the end of Q 2, Sagility had 51,486 employees  
• Geo Presence: As of Sep  30, 2026, Sagility had a presence in 5 countries with 28 delivery centers  
• Interim Dividend of ₹ 0.15 per share

Commenting on the results announcement, Ramesh Gopalan, Managing Director and Group CEO said ,  
“Our performance in the first half of FY27 reflects  broad -based momentum across the business.  Our pipeline is 
healthy  with larger  deals  and a growing set of opportunities with Blues and  mid -market plans.   Our AI solutions 
are gaining traction, with new SOW wins and more  solutions  moving into production.  CareSeed is now 
integrated into our Clinical Practice, adding depth to our healthcare quality capabilities  and our recent  
acquisition of Chirok will accelerate our growth in the provider segment, strengthening  our end -to-end 
provider RCM capabilities . As we enter the second half , our priority is disciplined execution through the AEP and 
Open Enrolment  season, while  investing in the capabilities that will drive our next phase of growth .” 
 
Srinivas Mattapal li, Group Chief Financial Officer added , “Q2 FY27 was a strong quarter with healthy growth, 
robust cash generation and our highest adjusted EBITDA margin in six quarters, reflecting disciplined execution, 
operating leverage and delivery efficiencies. Our revenue trajectory demonstrates the resilience  of the operating 
model as we enter the seasonally stronger H2. Underscoring our confidence in the business and our commitment 
to shareholder returns , the Board has declared an interim dividend of ₹0.15 per share, up from ₹0.05 in the 
previous interim . We will continue to invest selectively in technology, AI and domain -led growth to create long -
term value for our stakeholders .” 
 
About Sagility Limited  
Sagility is a leading healthcare operations partner that helps payers, providers, and pharmacy benefit 
managers reduce costs , improve quality, and enhance member and provider experiences. With more than 25 
years of exclusive focus in healthcare, Sagility combines deep domain expertise with technology -led 
transformation - embedding analytics, automation, and AI directly into ope rations while ensuring compliance 
with complex regulatory and clinical requirements. Serving over 100 healthcare clients, including 7 of the top 
U.S. health plans, Sagility delivers end -to-end operational transformation at scale through a model built on 
accountability, transparency, and measurable outcomes. To learn more, visit: https://sagility.com/
