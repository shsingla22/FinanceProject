 Press Release  
Signature Global’s GRESB Score Rises to 97.8, Securing 12th Position Globally 
Among Residential Peers  
● GRESB score rises from 84 to 97.8, bringing a 4-Star rating and 12th place globally among 
residential/listed peers 
● Achieves a perfect 100% in Reporting, including full marks for reporting coverage, 
reflecting a high standard of transparency and disclosure 
● Scores 99.8% in Energy, including full marks for on-site renewable energy plans 
New Delhi, 23 November 2026: Signature Global (India) Ltd., one of India’s leading real 
estate developers, has achieved a notable milestone in the Global Real Estate 
Sustainability Benchmark (GRESB)  Real Estate Assessment, scoring 97.8 out of 100. This 
is a significant leap from the score of 84 recorded in its debut assessment last year. The 
Company has been awarded a 4-Star GRESB Rating and ranked 12th in the 
Residential/Listed peer group, reinforcing its growing leadership in sustainability and ESG 
excellence. 
Reporting and Energy emerged as the standout categories in the assessment. Signature 
Global scored a perfect 100% in Reporting, backed by comprehensive disclosures, wider 
coverage and strong assurance mechanisms. In Energy, the Company achieved an 
exceptional 99.8%, led by proactive energy management and a strategic focus on on-site 
renewable energy integration, which earned full marks.  
FY26 also saw the Company strengthen its sustainability commitments across the 
business. Nearly all its projects are IGBC or EDGE certified, and all new developments are 
being targeted for green building certification. Its near-term and net-zero emission-
reduction targets have been validated by the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi), and 
over 9,700 trees were planted during the year. 
Commenting on the achievement, Mr. Pradeep Aggarwal, Founder & Chairman, 
Signature Global (India) Ltd., said, “This recognition from GRESB, one of the world’s most 
respected sustainability benchmarks for real estate, reflects sustained effort across our 
teams, and I am grateful to every colleague who contributed. Over the past year, we have 
strengthened how we collect, verify and disclose our sustainability data, and we have paid 
closer attention to energy use across our projects, including on-site renewable generation. 
The improvement in our score and ranking confirms that these measures are working. We 
recognize, however, that sustainability is a continuing responsibility, and we will keep 
refining our practices to meet a higher standard of environmental and governance 
performance.”

About Signature Global (India) Limited:  
Signature Global, India’s leading real estate development company, is reshaping the housing 
market in northern India. While it initially made its mark in the affordable housing segment, the 
Company now focuses on the mid and premium housing categories. This strategic evolution is 
driven by a strong emphasis on quality execution, value creation, reliability, and adherence to 
global standards.  
Backed by marquee institutional investors, Signature Global upholds high standards of corporate 
governance. The Company follows a disciplined and efficient land acquisition strategy, typically 
launching projects within 18 months of acquisition, enabling faster go-to-market timelines and 
enhanced project viability. Within the INR 20 million to INR 50 million price range, Signature Global 
holds a 13% market share in the National Capital Region (NCR) and a 20% share in Gurugram, 
reflecting its strong position in its target micro-markets. 
As of Q2FY27, the Company has delivered 19.9 million sq. ft. of real estate. Its project pipeline 
remains robust, comprising 23.2 million sq. ft. of recently launched projects, 27.3 million sq. ft. of 
forthcoming developments, and 9.2 million sq. ft. of ongoing construction (5.1 million sq. ft. under 
construction and 4.1 million sq. ft. with OC received), all slated for execution over the next 2-3 
years.
