Press Release  
 
RITES, DVC collaborate for  
innovation in rail infra works  
 
Gurugram, June 14, 2024: RITES Ltd., a prime transport infrastructure consultancy, today signed an 
MoU with Damodar Valley Corporation (DVC), a statutory body under the Ministry of Power, to 
collaborate for the comprehensive maintenance and operational management of railway sidings, 
signalling and telecommunication (S&T) systems, and 25KV Overhead Electrification (OHE) 
isolators across DVC’s private railway network in West Bengal and Jharkhand.  
 
 
Sh. S B R K Murthy, Sr GM, DVC, and Sh. S K Singhal, GM, RITES, with senior DVC officials at the MoU-signing ceremony  
 
The maintenance work entrusted to RITES will include the AMC for railway sidings, O&M of S&T 
systems, and operation of 25KV OHE isolators.  
 
About RITES Limited:  
RITES Limited is a Navratna Public Sector Enterprise and a leading player in the transport consultancy and 
engineering sector in India, having diversified services and geographical reach. The company has an 
experience spanning 50 years and undertaken projects in over 55 countries in Asia, Africa, Latin America, 
South America, and Middle East region.
