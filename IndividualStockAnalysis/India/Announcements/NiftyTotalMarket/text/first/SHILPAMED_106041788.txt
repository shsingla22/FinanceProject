Conclusion of USFDA Inspection of Shilpa Medicare Ltd, Unit VI, Bengaluru, Karnataka. 
This is to inform you that Shilpa Medicare Limited, Unit VI, Dabaspet, Bengaluru, India was inspected by USFDA on 
24, 25 and 28, 29, 30 October 2024. The inspection was the onsite GMP inspection of this Site.  
The inspection concluded with the issuance of Form 483 with four observations. All observations are procedural in 
nature. The Company shall respond to the observations within the time frame.  
This Unit of Shilpa Medicare Ltd. is engaged in the manufacturing, testing and release of Oral Mouth Dissolving 
Films and Transdermal Systems.   
The Site has already been approved by MHRA-UK and TGA Australia and has USFDA approval for supplying 
nutraceutical Oral Film products into US and other markets. 
About Shilpa:  
Shilpa is an integrated pharmaceutical group with business interests in niche Oncology & Non-oncology APIs, 
Peptides, Polymers, differentiated finished dosage formulations including orally dispersible films & transdermal 
patches along with carefully crafted biological portfolio. Shilpa also provides end-to-end CDMO services to global 
pharmaceutical companies across all its business segments based on its strong R&D and manufacturing capabilities 
backed by four R&D units and seven manufacturing facilities.
