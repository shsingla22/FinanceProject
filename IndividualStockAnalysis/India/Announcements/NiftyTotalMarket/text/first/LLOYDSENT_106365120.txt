Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
In furtherance to our Board Meeting intimation dated Monday, 11th August, 2025 and pursuant to Regulation 
30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations an d Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 ( “SEBI Listing Regulations ”), the Board of Directors ( “the Board ”) of Lloyds Enterprises Limited ( “the 
Company ”) at their meeting held today i.e. Thursday, 14th August, 2025  has considered and inter-alia approved 
the following: 
 
1. Approval of the Unaudited Financial Results (Standalone and Conso lidated) of the Company for the 
quarter ended 30th June, 2025 along with Limited Review Reports: 
 
The Unaudited Financial Results (Standalone and Consolidated) of the Company for the quarter ended 
30th June, 2025 of the financial year 2025-26 along with the Limited Review Report issued by the Statutory 
Auditors, which is enclosed herewith pursuant to Regulation 33(3) of the SEBI List ing Regulations.  
 
The aforesaid financial results have been duly reviewed by the Audit Committee and subsequently 
approved by the Board of Directors of the Company at their respective meetings  held today i.e. Thursday, 
14th August, 2025. 
 
2. Approval of the declaration of an interim dividend to the equity shareholders: 
 
In accordance with Regulation 30 read with Schedule III Pa rt A of the SEBI Listing Regulations, the Board 
declared an interim dividend of Re. 0.10 paisa (i.e. 10%) per Equity Shares of face value of Re. 1/- for the 
Financial Year 2025-26. The interim dividend shall be paid to all eligibl e shareholders whose names appear 
in the Register of Members of the Company as on the Record Date i.e Friday, 05th September, 2025 . The 
dividend shall be disbursed to eligible equity shareholders on or before Friday, 12th September, 2025.
