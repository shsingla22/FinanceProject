This is to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting held today 
i.e. Friday, March 21, 2025 inter alia, considered and approved the proposal for Voluntary 
Delisting of its Equity Shares from the Calcutta Stock Exchange Limited (CSE) pursuant to 
Regulation 6 of the SEBI (Delisting of Equity Sharers) regulations, 2021 as amended from 
time to time where no exit opportunity is required to be provided to the shareholders of the 
Company; since the CSE does not have any active platform for trading in equity shares, the 
Shareholders of the Company are not affected in any manner from delisting.   
 
The Company will continue to remain listed on the stock exchanges with nationwide trading 
terminals viz BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited.
