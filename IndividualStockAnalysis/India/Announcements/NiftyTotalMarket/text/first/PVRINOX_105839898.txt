PVR INOX REIMAGINES CINEMA EXPERIENCE WITH CINEMAGIC IN GURUGRAM  
~4 screen multiplex at Ambience Mall comprises theme based auditoriums, 4DX and Playhouse ~ 
National, May 9, 2024: PVR INOX, the largest and the most premium cinema exhibitor in India  has 
announced the opening of a new cinema, PVR  Cinemagic  at Ambience Mall, Gurugram. The 4 -screen 
multiplex introduces two unique theme -based  auditoriums , “Space and Fores t” along with the multi -
sensory 4DX and the Kid’s auditorium  Playhouse  each catering to diverse  customer  preferences.  
With the addition of PVR Cinemagic, PVR INOX continues its commitment to provid e the most experiential 
out-of-home entertainment experiences across India. This marks the 14th cinema in Gurugram , bringing 
the total screen count  to 64 in the city . In Haryana, PVR INOX now operates 2 2 properties with 9 3 screens , 
and in North India, it boasts 101 properties and 4 59 screens.  
The distinctive theme -based auditoria - "Forest" transports viewers to a serene environment with its 
green hues and nature -inspired design, while the "Space" auditorium creates an intergalactic adventure 
with its blue and black color scheme, include a themed rainforest and out of space audio track that 
immerses the audience and takes their experience to new hei ghts upon settling in their seat, before the 
on screen feature begins . With a total seating capacity of 397 guests , the new cinema is equipped with 
the best -in-class theatrical technology  with  Dolby 7.1 sound, 2K laser projection and 3D  enabled screens.  
PVR Cine magic features  a 4DX auditorium  that incorporates on -screen visuals with synchronized motion 
seats and environmental effects such as water, wind, fog, scent, snow and more, to enhance the action 
on screen  for an immersive multi -sensory cinematic ex perience. Further, the Playhouse auditorium is a 
one-of-a-kind movie theatre designed to captivate younger audiences with vibrant colors, playful 
elements, and a specially curated seating arrangement  accompanied by slides.  
Commenting on the announcement, Mr. Ajay Bijli, Managing Director, PVR INOX  Limited  said, “In the 
past few years, there has been a notable shift in the theatrical landscape, with audiences showing a 
growing interest in distinctive out -of-home entertainment venues. Cinemagic represents ou r endeavor to 
make  cinemas more experiential , blending diverse themes and sensations. It embodies our commitment 
to innovate  and appealing to every facet of our clientele, promising a memorable cinematic encounter for 
all.” 
The foyer of PVR Cinemagic is a testament to sophistication and modernity, featuring opulent finishes and 
modern design elements. LED columns create a dynamic atmosphere, while cozy seating spaces and 
framed artworks celebrating legendary actors and actresses add to the ambiance. The cin ema promises 
culinary delight with a gourmet menu curated by celebrity chefs , featuring cinema classics and seasonal 
favorites  
"PVR Cinemagic embodies our imaginative spirit and underscores our commitment to offering more than 
just movies; it's about craft ing unforgettable experiences. Whether it's the tranquil setting of the Forest or 
the cosmic journey through Space, each auditorium guarantees an unparalleled experience. Through 
cutting -edge technology and distinct thematic designs, our goal is to fashion  an enchanting hub where 
cinema enthusiasts can delve into spellbinding narratives like never before,"  remarked Mr. Sanjeev Kumar 
Bijli, Executive Director , PVR INOX Limited.
