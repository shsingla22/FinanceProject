Regulations, 2015 - Subsidiary Updates
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015,
it is hereby informed that NRF Holding B.V. (formerly known as Nederlandse Radiateuren Fabriek B.V.), a
wholly owned subsidiary of Banco Products (India) Limited, has issued an official statement regarding an
incident of fire at the warehouse of its wholly owned subsidiary, NRF France S.A.S. – France.
We hereby report that everyone is safe, and the situation is coming under control. To ensure continuity of
operations, all current orders and pending shipments will be delivered through other NRF warehouses.
The local operations at the affected warehouse have been impacted. The preliminary assessments indicate
that there will be no material impact on the Group performance from this incident.
Further/ we would like to inform you that the Company has insurance coverage and have informed the
Insurance Company about the incident and coordinating with them for ascertaining the loss/damage caused
due to the fire.
A copy of the official statement issued by NRF Holding B.V. is enclosed herewith for your reference.
Any further updates/ if any, will be updated promptly in accordance with the applicable provisions of
Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
We request you to kindly take the same on record.
