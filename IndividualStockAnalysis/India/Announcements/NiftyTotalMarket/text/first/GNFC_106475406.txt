PRESS RELEASE 
 
Subsequent to the Board approval in its meeting hel d on November 12, 2025 for installation 
of Ammonium Nitrate – II plant of 480 MTPD capacity , i.e. 1,63,200 MTPA, GNFC had 
entered in to Contract with Toyo Engineering India Private Limited (Toyo) for supply of 
Ammonium Nitrate – II plant on LEPC basis. Toyo had  tie-up with INCRO, S. A., Spain for 
supply of Process knowhow and License for Ammonium Nitrate – II plant. 
 
We are delighted to further strengthen our market p resence for Ammonium Nitrate in India 
by installation of new plant based on INCRO technol ogy. At present, we are also operating 
Ammonium Nitrate plant. This plant will enhance the  capacity of GNFC by ~94% and fulfill 
the downstream requirement to support Make in India  Campaign with commitment to 
environment conservations.
