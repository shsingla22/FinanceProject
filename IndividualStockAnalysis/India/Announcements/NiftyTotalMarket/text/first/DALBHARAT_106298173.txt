Sub:  Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”) – Update on ongoing litigation 
 
Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
This is in continuation of our earlier disclosures dated December 31, 2023 and February 2, 2024, made 
pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, regarding writ petition filed by Dalmia Cement (Bharat) 
Limited (DCBL), a wholly owned subsidiary, before the Madurai Bench of the Madras High Court, challenging 
the assessment order dated December 29, 2023 of the State Tax Officer, Lalgudi Assessment Circle, Lalgudi, 
for FY 2017-18. The Hon’ble Court had granted interim stay of the impugned order/ demand vide Order 
dated January 30, 2024. 
 
In this regard we would like to update that the Hon’ble Madras High Court, in a batch of petitions filed in 
similar grounds, has disposed of the petitions by remanding to the assessing authorities with directions to 
pass fresh Orders after considering submissions by the petitioners. The Hon’ble Court had pronounced the 
order on June 12, 2025. However, the Order copy was uploaded in portal in late evening on the portal on 
July 11 2025.  
 
DCBL has good case on merit and shall take all reasonable steps pursuant to the aforesaid Order to defend 
the matter before the adjudicating authorities and pursue the remedies available to the Company. 
 
The details as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with SEBI Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 are provided in the Annexure hereto.
