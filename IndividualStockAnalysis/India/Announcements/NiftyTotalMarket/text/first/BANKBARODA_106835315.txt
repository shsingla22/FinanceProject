Madam / Dear Sir,  
 
Re: Interim Applications by Dr. BR Shetty in Hon’ble Bombay High Court  
 
We wish to inform that  Bank of Baroda has received inquiries from the media regarding certain interim 
applications filed by Dr. BR Shetty against the Bank in his suit before the Hon’ble Bombay High 
Court. As the Bank clarified in its earlier filing with the stock exchanges on 14  October 2026, the 
Bank has not been served with such a suit. The Bank has also not been served with the interim 
applications that were subject of today’s press inquiries. In both cases, the Bank maintains that it 
cannot comment on the contents of pleading s or interim applications that it has not been served with 
and that have not been heard before the court.  
 
As a matter of principle, it would be improper for the Bank to respond to untested allegations in 
relation to court proceedings in public media. It appears from the website of the Hon’ble Bombay 
High Court that both the suit and the interim applications fi led by Dr. BR Shetty are either yet to be 
scrutinized or registered by the Hon’ble Bombay High Court. As such, the allegations remain 
untested until such time that a Court considers them in accordance with law.  
 
Contents of the suit and interim applications appear to have been selectively disseminated to the 
news media on the eve of the announcement of the Bank’s quarterly results with an improper 
intention. The Bank will take appropriate steps in relation to such  dissemination.  
 
We request you to take note of the above pursuant to SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 and  upload 
the information on your website.
