Dear Sir /Madam , 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Regulations”), we wish to inform you that with reference to our earlier 
announcements dated May  1, 2024 and March  10, 2025 regarding the Solar Power Project at 
Dalpura, Banaskantha, Gujarat, the Company has change the location of the said project for 
technical reasons and avoiding further regulatory delays identified with the original site land 
location.  
 
The Company has now acquired project land by way of lease at Village Bamanva, Taluka Vijapur, 
District Mehsana, Gujarat and has received approval from Gujarat Energy Transmission Corporation 
Limited (GETCO) on December 30, 2025  for grid connectivity and evacuation of 11.84 MW Solar 
Power Project at 66KV Bamanva Substation of GETCO for captive use.  
 
The installation work for the project is expected to be completed within the next 6 –8 months . 
 
You are requested to take the above on your records.
