Bhopal, November 12, 2026:  Dilip Buildcon Limited (“DBL” or “the Company”), a multi -
asset infrastructure platform, today announced its reviewed financial results for the 
second quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026.  
The Company recorded fresh order inflow of approximately ₹7,321 crore during the 
quarter across BOT, EPC and HAM, taking H1FY27 inflow to approximately ₹7,589 crore. 
During the quarter, it signed definitive agreements with Alpha Alternatives  Fund 
Advisors LLP and its associates (“Alpha Group”)  for the Mekhali tr ansmission project in 
Karnataka and the 1,363 MW AC solar portfolio in Madhya Pradesh. Alpha Alternatives 
will co -invest 49% of the equity during construction, funding approximately ₹820  crore 
of the combined equity requirement of about ₹1,680 crore, and will acquire DBL’s 
 
remaining 51% stake after commissioning. EPC work of over ₹ 6,612  crore will remain 
with DBL. The Company also received the Grant of Authorisation from PNGRB for the 
Paradip –Raipur LPG pipeline, a 25 -year regulated -tariff concession i n which  DBL will 
hold 100% of the SPV and execute the approximately ₹1, 558 crore EPC component.  
Q2FY27 in a nutshell : 
Earnings now come from operations, not one -offs.  Q2FY26 consolidated profit 
included ₹122 crore of exceptional items; this quarter’s were ₹2 crore. On a like -for-like 
basis, consolidated profit before exceptional items and tax rose 24% and the standalone 
profit before exceptional item and tax  nearly tripled.  
Margins are rising quarter on quarter.  Consolidated EBITDA margin has improved for 
two consecutive quarters, from 17.1% in Q4FY26 to 18.1% in Q1FY27 and 19.5% in 
Q2FY27. The standalone margin was 10.4%, compared with 10.3% in Q1FY27 and 
10.75% in Q2FY26 , despite higher input costs amid the sit uation in West Asia.  
The order book has turned.  After moderating in Q1 as execution outpaced intake, the 
order book rose 19% in Q2 to a record ₹33,081 crore. Roads now account for 15.6% of 
the book, down from 17.1% in June, as the Company bids selectively and grows in 
transmission, renewables, mining and irrigation.  H1 order inflow of ₹7,589 crore already 
covers 76% of the lower end of FY27 order inflow guidance . 
Lighter group balance sheet:  Consolidated finance costs fell 39% in H1, and the FY28 
standalone net -debt -free target is reaffirmed.  
 
Financial Performance – Q2FY27 (Consolidated Basis)  
• Revenue from Operations:  ₹2,250 crore (up 17% YoY)  
• EBITDA:  ₹439 crore  
• EBITDA Margin:  19.5% (18.1% in Q1FY27)  
• Profit before Exceptional Items and Tax:  ₹176 c rore (up 24% YoY)  
• Profit After Tax (PAT):  ₹101 crore  
 
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported consolidated revenue 
from operations of ₹2,250 crore, compared with ₹1,926 crore in Q2FY26. EBITDA 
(excluding other income) was ₹439 crore, with the margin improving to 19.5% from 
18.1% in Q1FY27. Finance costs fell 22% year -on-year to ₹251 crore, and profit before 
exceptional items and tax rose 24% to ₹176 crore from ₹142 crore. PAT was ₹101 crore, 
compared with ₹214 crore in Q2FY26, w hich included exceptional items of ₹122 crore.  
For H1FY27, consolidated revenue stood at ₹4,628 crore, EBITDA (excluding other 
income) at ₹868 crore (margin 18.8%), profit before exceptional items and tax at ₹334 
crore (up 10% from ₹304 crore) and PAT at ₹229 crore. Finance costs for the half fell 
39% to ₹496 crore from ₹818 crore.  
 
Financial Performance – Q2FY27 (Standalone Basis)  
• Revenue from Operations:  ₹1,686 crore (up 19% YoY)  
• EBITDA:  ₹175 crore (up 15% YoY)  
• EBITDA Margin:  10.4%  
• Profit before Exception al Items and Tax:  ₹71 crore (₹2 3 crore in Q2FY26)  
• Profit After Tax (PAT):  ₹52 crore (up 28% YoY)  
 
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported standalone revenue 
from operations of ₹1,686 crore, up 19% from ₹1,417 crore in Q2FY26. EBITDA rose 15% 
to ₹175 crore (margin 10.4%) from ₹152 crore. Profit before exceptional items and tax 
rose to ₹71 crore from ₹2 3 crore, and PAT rose 2 8% to ₹52 crore from ₹41 crore in 
Q2FY26, which included exceptional gains of ₹42 crore.  
For H1FY27, stand alone revenue was ₹3,615 crore, EBITDA ₹374 crore (margin 10.3%) 
and profit before exceptional items and tax ₹143 crore, up 77% from ₹8 1 crore in 
H1FY26.  
Order Book Position:  
As of September 30, 2026, Dilip Buildcon’s  order book stood at a record ₹33,081 crore, 
up 19% from ₹27,691 crore as of June 30, 2026 and 15% from ₹28,830 crore as of March 
31, 2026. The order book is spread across 12 business verticals, with irrigation and water 
at approximately 20.7%, mining at 1 6.9%, roads and highways at 15.6%, and other 
verticals (renewable energy, power transmission, petroleum and gas, tunnels, special 
bridges and urban development, metro and railways, and optical fibre) making up the 
balance of 46.8%.  
Renewable energy and pow er transmission together now account for 27% of the order 
book, well ahead of roads and highways at 15.6%, reflecting the Company’s shift towards 
energy and long -duration infrastructure . 
The mining MDO order book reflects a three -year rolling snapshot of a pproximately 
₹3,998 crore, while the balance contract value of the mining portfolio is approximately 
₹98,618 crore at current prices, providing visibility well beyond the reported order book. 
H1 order inflow of ₹7,589 crore already covers 76% of the lower end of FY27 order inflow 
guidance  of ₹10,000 –12,000 crore , which it reiterates.  
Segmental Revenue:  
The large projects awarded in Q4FY26, worth approximately ₹15,000 crore, are in their 
mobilisation phase and are expected to start contributing from Q3FY27, lifting EPC 
execution in the second half. Mining continued to ramp up: coal production rose 27% 
sequentially to 6.1 million tonnes in Q2FY27 from 4.79 million tonnes in Q1FY27, taking 
H1FY27 production to 10.89 million tonnes. The Company reiterated its target of 
approximately 57 million tonnes of annual coal production by FY29, at which level the 
minin g business alone is expected to generate around ₹1,000 crore of annual operating 
 
cash flow.  
A detailed segment -wise revenue breakup will be provided in the Company’s financial 
statements filed with the stock exchanges.  
• Gross Revenue from EPC business stood at ₹ 1499 crore in  Q2FY27  
• Gross Revenue from Mining stood at ₹433  crore in  Q2FY27; mining SPVs recorded 
revenue of ₹795 crore in H1FY27 at an EBITDA margin of 25%  
• Income from InvITs stood at ₹37 crore in Q2FY27 on consolidated basis  
 
Debt Position:  
As of September 30, 2026, Dilip Buildcon’s  standalone net debt stood at ₹2,146 crore, 
compared with ₹2,106 crore as of June 30, 2026, ₹1,880 crore as of March 31, 2026 and 
₹2,094 crore  as of September 30, 2025. The increase in Q2 was limited to ₹40 crore, 
against ₹226  crore in Q1FY27, and standalone net debt -to-equity stood at 0.31x. 
Consolidated net debt stood at ₹8,950 crore, while consolidated finance costs fell 39% 
year -on-year to ₹496 crore in H1FY27.  
The Company remains committed to becom e net debt -free on a stan dalone basis by FY28, 
supported by the transfer of the remaining HAM assets to Anantam Highways Trust, 
Alpha Alternatives’ equity co -investment and improved collections. Working capital 
requirements are typically higher in the first half, and the Company e xpects them to ease 
in the second half as execution improves and collections progress, with a target of 
approximately 120 working capital days by the end of FY27.

Dilip Buildcon Limited’s CEO, Mr. Devendra Jain, said,  “This quarter’s profit is built on

this quar ter had almost none. On a like -for-like basis, consolidated pre -tax profit rose 
24%, standalone pre -tax profit nearly tripled, and our consolidated EBITDA margin has 
improved for two consecutive quarters. Our businesses beyond standalone EPC, mining

About Dilip Buildcon Limited  
Established in 1987, Dilip Buildcon Limited (“DBL”) is a diversified infrastructure 
Company with over three decades of execution experience across large -scale 
infrastructure development projects in India. The Company is present acros s 20 states 
and 1 Union Territory, supported by a workforce of 19,104  employees and a fleet of over 
8,697 equipment units.  
Through DBL 2.0, the Company is strategically transitioning from a pure -play EPC 
contractor into a diversified multi -asset infrastruc ture platform focused on building 
long -duration contracted businesses and sustainable cash -flow -generating assets.  
DBL largely operates across multiple verticals, including roads and highways, irrigation 
and water, mining, renewable energy, power transmission, petroleum and gas, tunnels, 
special bridges and urban development, metro and railways, and optical fibre.
