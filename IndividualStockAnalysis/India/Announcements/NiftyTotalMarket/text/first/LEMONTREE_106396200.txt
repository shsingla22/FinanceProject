Lemon Tree Hotels launches its 6th property in  Andhra Pradesh  with  
Lemon Tree Premier,  Tirupati  
 
The hotel features 111 well-appointed rooms  & suites,  restaurants and a bar 
along  with event spaces, recreational facilities and other public areas . 
 
New Delhi, 2nd October 2025: Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -
growing mid -scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the launch of its 
latest property  – Lemon Tree Premier, Tirupati.  
 
This managed  hotel features 111 well-appointed rooms & suites, complemented 
by Citrus Café – a multi -cuisine restaurant, Slounge – the hip recreational bar, the 
Pool Bar & Grill, banquet spaces, a spa, a fitness center and the swimming pool.  
 
Of this, 54  rooms & suites along with Citrus Café open ed today  in the first phase. 
The remaining 57 rooms along with Slounge, the Pool Bar & Grill, banquet spaces, 
a spa, a fitness center and the swimming pool are set to open in the second phase.  
 
Tirupati, in Andhra Pradesh, is a significant pilgrimage centre best known for the 
Sri Venkateswara Temple situated atop the Tirumala Hills, dedicated to Lord 
Venkateswara, an incarnation of Lord Vishnu. This sacred temple attracts millions 
of devotees fro m across the globe each year. Apart from its religious prominence, 
Tirupati has evolved into a well -connected urban centre with modern 
infrastructure, a growing hospitality industry, and easy accessibility via Tirupati 
International Airport and Renigunta R ailway Station.  
 
Commenting on the opening Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, Lemon Tree 
Hotels said, “ Religious and spiritual destinations have become major driver s of 
tourism. This vibrant hotel in one of the most significant pilgrimage centers in our 
country, is tapping into the demand to offer contemporary facilities to our guests .”
