Dear Stakeholders,
It is with immense pleasure that we 
present to you our Sustainability Report 
for FY 2026. As we marked another year 
of strong business performance, we 
remained equally focused on advancing

priorities. This balanced approach 
enabled us to create lasting value for 
our stakeholders while building long-term 
business resilience.
Responsible growth requires continuous 
investment in the fundamentals that sustain 
our business over the long term. During 
the year, we continued to strengthen 
our environmental stewardship, improve 
operational resilience, foster an inclusive 
workplace and reinforce the governance 
practices that guide every aspect of 
our operations.

clean energy portfolio by commissioning 
additional solar power installation, 
taking our total renewable capacity to 
16.6 MW. We also replaced fossil fuels 
with biomass -fired boiler systems, scaling 
the use of renewable biofuels across our 
operations. Alongside we continued efforts 
to optimise energy use, reduce waste

review. Six of our major sites are certified

our emphasis on occupational health

In terms of water management, 
we enhanced our effluent treatment 
capability to increase water reuse and 
extended our conservation efforts to 
neighbouring communities. Additionally, 
we conducted life-cycle assessments of 
three key products and expanded the 
adoption of aqueous coating technology 
that eliminates the usage of solvents. 
Our people are our greatest strength, 
and their safety and well-being remain

Introduction

FY 2026 Performance 
SnapshotThis Sustainability Report outlines our approach to managing the environmental, 
social and governance aspects of our business, along with the progress made 
during FY 2026. It complements the Annual Report by providing greater insight 
into the ESG initiatives, practices and outcomes that support our long-term 
growth. The two reports present a holistic view of our performance and our 
continued commitment to creating value responsibly.
Reporting Scope and Boundaries
This Sustainability Report presents 
Ajanta Pharma's environmental, 
social and governance (ESG) 
performance for FY 2026 (1 April 
2025 to 31 March 2026). Prepared 
as part of our annual reporting 
cycle, it builds on the previous year's 
report and provides a consistent 
basis for tracking our sustainability 
performance over time.
The disclosures cover our 
manufacturing units, research and 
development facilities and corporate 
offices in India, which together 
account for the substantial majority 
of our operational footprint. Where 
relevant, we also include information 
pertaining to our international 
operations across more than 33 
countries in which we operate. Unless 
stated otherwise, all data relates to 
our India operations for the reporting 
period, and any figures restated 
from the prior year are identified in 
the relevant sections. The financial 
information presented in this report is 
consistent with our audited financial 
statements, while the non-financial 
information has been compiled from 
our internal management systems.
External Assurance
To strengthen the credibility of 
our disclosures, Ajanta Pharma 
Limited’s Business Responsibility 
and Sustainability Report (BRSR) 
for FY 2025–26 has undergone 
independent Reasonable Assurance 
by TÜV India Private Limited in 
accordance with ISAE 3000 (Revised). 
The assurance engagement covered 
disclosures relating to all nine BRSR Feedback and Contact

better and improve the way we operate.

The data in this report has been 
compiled from a combination of
