Note 1 : Prag had undertaken a capex project in 2010 towards expansion of its bottling 
capacity to reduce the dependency of TI group on third party bottling tie-up arrangements. The 
expansion LOI was received as late as 2016 subject to a licence fee payment of approx Rs 20 
crores. Meanwhile, TI had become Non-Performing Asset and was facing liquidity crunch. 
Hence, Prag could not fulfil the financial obligation for commencing the aforesaid project.  
 
While the Holding Company was in talks with the Banks and Financial Institutions for 
settlement, Prag was admitted under Hon National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) in June 
2017. The process of settlement of financial creditors of Prag took around six years. This 
further stalled the commissioning of the expansion project.  
 
Prag came out of liquidation in the financial year 2023-2024. In the several years that elapsed, 
the business dynamics had changed and the external bottling capacities available in Andhra 
Pradesh had become sufficient to meet TI’s growing demand. The Management evaluated that 
since the project got inordinately delayed, it was no longer financially prudent to further 
increase its own exposure for the capex project. Accordingly, the management provided for the 
impairment of the capex project of Rs. 10,021.69 lacs which was disclosed under exceptional 
items in the financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2023. The same was 
subsequently written off in the financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2024. 
 
Prag is in discussion with the Andhra Pradesh authorities to examine the veracity of the claim 
and is examining various alternatives. We are in the process of responding to the authorities 
within the stipulated time.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
  
 
 
Considering the inordinate delay in providing the approval of LOI, and the fact that Prag was 
under NCLT proceedings for around six years, the Company is confident that it has a strong 
case to challenge the order. We will keep the exchange updated on any material developments 
in the above matter.
