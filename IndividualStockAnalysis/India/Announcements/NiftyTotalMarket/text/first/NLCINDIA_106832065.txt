In terms of Regulation 30 of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we write to inform that: 
 
“NLC India Renewables Limited (NIRL), a whol ly owned subsidiary of NLC India Limited, 
participated in the tender floated by Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited (GUVNL) for 
procurement of power through a competit ive bidding process from 250 MW Grid-
Connected Wind Power Projects (Phase XI), with  a Greenshoe option of additional capacity 
of up to 250 MW.

accepted the Greenshoe and additional Greenshoe capacities of 100 MW each, aggregating 
to a total capacity of 300 MW. 
 
In this regard, NIRL has received the Letter of Intent (LoI) from GUVNL for the 
development of the aforesaid 300 MW Wind Power Projects.”
