Hyderabad, November 13, 2026 : Viyash Scientific Limited (formerly SeQuent Scientific Limited) an integrated 
pharmaceutical company with businesses across Formulations, APIs and CDMO services for Human and 
Animal Health, today announced its financial results for the quarter and half y ear ended September 30, 2026.  
Viyash Scientific H1 FY27: Revenue up 1 7.8% to ₹ 19,343 Mn; H1 PAT up 49% to ₹ 1,639 Mn  
* EBITDA before ESOP costs 
**Assuming a normalised annual ESOP cost of INR 25 Cr  
 
Commenting on the Company’s performance, Dr Hari Babu Bodepudi, Managing Director and Group CEO 
stated that 
 
“Viyash Scientific has delivered yet another stellar quarter with H1 revenue up 17.8% and H1 profit 
after tax (PAT) at INR 164 Cr, up ~50% from last year.  H1 adjusted PAT with normalized ESOP cost  is 
INR 204 Cr**. The completion of acquisition of BioForLife in Italy strengthens our companion animal 
platform. We are also investing in new oncology capabilities, in -house companion animal 
manufacturing facility and expanding our API capacity to meet customer demand.  
Our focus remains on disciplined execution, R&D -led growth and sustainable value creation for 
customers and stakeholders. ” 
Dr Hari Babu Bodepudi , Managing Director and Group CEO

About Viyash Scientific Limited  
Viyash Scientific Limited (formerly SeQuent Scientific Limited), headquartered in Hyderabad, India, is an 
integrated pharmaceutical platform across animal and human health, backed by science and IP, and is 
among the top-25 animal health companies globally.  Its portfolio spans 110+ APIs, 30+ human health and 
140+ animal health formulations, serving customers in 150+ countries.  
The Company operates 16 manufacturing facilities and R&D, manufacturing and distribution operations 
across India, Spain, Brazil, Turkey, and the USA with approvals from international regulators including 
USFDA, EU GMP, WHO and TGA. It has 3, 300+ employees, 335+ scientists, 190+ patents filed and 1,000+ 
registrations.
