ACME Solar operationalizes additional  cumulative BESS  
capacity of 107.36 MW / 437.49 MWh in Rajasthan  
 
Gurugram – October 1, 2026 : ACME Solar Holdings Ltd (ACME Solar) through its subsidiaries 
has commissioned additional cumulative  BESS capacity of 107.36 MW / 437.49 MWh across 
two key projects  in Rajasthan  as mentioned below . The commissioning of both these projects 
was successfully achieved on September 30, 2026 and t he Commercial Operation Date (COD) 
for both projects shall be October 02, 2026. Following these milestones, the total operational 
BESS capacity of ACME Solar now  stands at  4.96 GWh.  
   
Commissioning of BESS capacity at FDRE Project  
ACME Solar has commissioned first phase of  BESS capacity of 53.73 MW / 222.969 MWh  FDRE 
Project .  This FDRE Project is  located at Neemri village in  Tehsil Rawatbhata District 
Chittorgarh, Rajasthan  and connected to Neemuch substation. The Project has a 25 -year 
power purchase  agreement  with a National REIA . The complete BESS capacity  of this project  
is expected to be commissioned within  the current financial year.  
 
Commissioning of Phase III BESS capacity of FDRE  (Assured Peak Power ) Project  
ACME Solar has commissioned another  53.63 MW / 214.52 MWh  of Assured Peak Power 
Project.  With this commissioning, the total BESS capacity of 159.58 MW / 638.32 MWh has 
been achieved under this Project.  Located at Kelan village in Bikaner, Rajasthan , it is 
connected to Bikaner III substation. Th is Project has a 25 -year power purchase agreement  
with a National REIA . The BESS capacity for full 300 MW Project is expected to be 
commissioned within  the current financial year.  
 
About ACME Solar: ACME Solar Holdings Limited is a leading integrated renewable energy player with
