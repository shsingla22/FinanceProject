Bombay High Court Sets Aside MahaREAT Order, Restores Project De-Registration 
and Promoter Change in Turf View Project in Mahalaxmi 
Mumbai, 1 Oct 2026 :  In light of the order dated 29 September 2026 passed by the 
Hon'ble High Court of Judicature at Bombay, the Order dated 2 September 2022 de-
registering the Turf View Project in Mahalaxmi, together with the Order/Note dated 29 
October 2021 approving the change of promoter, stands restored. 
Consequent to the Hon'ble High Court's order, all concerned parties have duly settled 
their respective disputes and claims relating to the matter. 
The resolution brings closure to the proceedings and restores the regulatory position as 
it stood under the aforementioned orders.
