Mumbai, 	18	July,	2025:	Route Mobile, a leading global cloud communication platform service 
provider, announced today that the Board of Direct ors has accepted Mr. Gautam Badalia’s resignation 
from his role as CEO, ef fective 17 July 2025.  
 
Mr. Badalia informed the Board on the occasion of th e scheduled Board of Directors meeting on 17 July 
2025, citing personal reasons for his decision.  	Route Mobile has named senior leader Mr. Rajdip kumar Gupta as CEO. Mr. Gupta is Founder and 
Managing Director of the company, and has been a key leadership figure in the organization’s evolution 
since its beginnings in 2004. He will continue in his role  as Managing Director in addition to this latest 
appointment. 
 
Commenting 	on	the	announcement, 	Mr.	Gupta	said , “This	is	a	pivotal	time	for	Route	Mobile,	and	I’m	
looking	forward	to	driving	performance 	and	delivering 	exceptional 	value	for	our	customers. 	My	focus	will	
be	on	execution, 	operational 	excellence, 	and	
positioning 	the	company 	for	sustained 	success.”	
	
The Board also appointed Mr. Sammy Mamdani, Ex ecutive Vice President – Group Head – Global 
Operations, as Chief Operating Officer, effective 17 July 2025. Mr. Mamdani leads Route Mobile’s global 
operations, overseeing daily management and coordination across international offices. With 18+ 
years of experience, he has held senior roles at Protiviti (formerly Andersen Consulting), a Mumbai-based investment firm, and has served as COO at Celle nt Technologies (India) Pr ivate Limited, acquired 
by Route Mobile in 2016. He holds an MBA from the University of Central Florida. 
 
About	Route	Mobile	Limited		
	
Established in 2004, Route Mobile Limited (“RML” ) is a cloud communications platform service 
provider catering to enterprises, over-the-top (O TT) players, and mobile network operators (MNOs). 
RML’s portfolio comprises solutions in Business Messaging, Voice, Email, SMS filtering, analytics, and 
monetization. RML has a diverse enterprise client base across various industries, including social 
media companies, banks and financial institutions, e-commerce entities, and travel aggregators. RML 
is headquartered in Mumbai, India, with a global presence in Asia Pacific, the Middle East, Africa, 
Europe, and the Americas. Route Mobile is now a part of Proximus Group, a provider of digital services 
and communication solutions operating in Belgium and international markets.
