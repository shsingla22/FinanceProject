THANE  
The Thane Municipal  
Corporation (TMC) on  
Thursday announced finan -
cial assistance of `10 lakh each  
to the families of Sagar  
Suryakant Shinde,  42, the  
fire station officer, and Kalu  
Shankar Gadekar, 55, a TMC  
security guard, who lost their  
lives in the fire at the  
Gaondevi vegetable market.  
The civic body also  
announced `1 lakh each for  
injured personnel Sujit  
Pashte, 45, and Samir Jadhav,  
40, and assured compassion -
ate employment to the  
families of the deceased.  
The last rites of Shinde  
were performed with full  
honours. Deputy Chief  
Minister Eknath Shinde,  
Thane Municipal  
Commissioner Saurabh Rao,  
Mayor Sharmila Pimpolkar  
and MLA Jitendra Awhad  
were present to pay tributes.  
Contingents from the Thane,  
MCGM, Navi Mumbai, Panvel  
and Kulgaon-Badlapur fire  brigades also attended the  
ceremony and offered final  
salutes.  
Leader of the TMC House  
Hanmant Jagdale, Leader of  
the Opposition Ashraf Shanu  
Pathan and several corpor -ators were also present.  
Shinde hailed from Thane,  
while Gadekar belonged to  
Gadekarwadi village in  
Ambegaon taluka of Pune  
district. Shinde is survived by  
his mother, wife and a two-month-old daughter, while  
Gadekar is survived by his  
wife and two sons.  
During the joint meeting  
following the fire, the civic  
administration also finalised  
long-term rehabilitation  plans for those affected by  
the fire. Officials said the 155  
stall owners affected by the  
blaze would be rehabilitated  
at the same location by  
June 1.  
The civic body also  
decided to undertake debris  
removal and deep cleaning of  
the market premises  
immediately. Officials said  
the building would undergo  
mandatory structural stabil -
ity and comprehensive fire  
safety audits before rewiring,  
painting and restoration  
work are completed.  
The TMC further resolved  
to increase insurance cover  
payout limits for emergency  
and firefighting personnel.  
Following recommenda -
tions made by Leader of the  
House Hanmant Jagdale, the  
TMC is also considering tem -
porarily stationing two fully  
equipped fire tenders at  
Dadoji Konddev Stadium to  
improve emergency  
response time. A final deci -
sion on the proposal will be  
taken within the next three  
days.mumbai
