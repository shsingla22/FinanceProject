PRESS RELEASE  
 
Motilal Oswal Financial Services Announces Leadership 
Strengthening & Transitions  
 
Mumbai, 03 November,2026:  Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited (MOFSL), one of India's leading 
diversified financial services groups, today announced a series of leadership transitions, marking an 
important milestone in its leadership journey.  
 
Strengthening of Leadership in M O Alternates:  
 
Mr. Vishal Tulsyan,  founder of the MO Alternates platform has led the platform admirably over the 
last 20 years. From being an MD & CEO he was elevated to the role of   Executive Chairman in 2024. 
Along with his home grown top leadership team , Vishal in the last 20 years has been instrumental in 
making MO Alts amongst the largest & most successful domestic platforms in India. Vishal is being 
inducted into the MOFSL board, will continue to play a key role in MOFSL strategy and guide the MO 
Alts leadership in its next build up phase as Executive Chairman. Given MOFS’s ambitious growth plans 
for the Alts platform, we are now strengthening the leadership capabilities by appointing a MD & CEO 
of Motilal Oswal Alternates.  
 
Mr. Padmanabh Sinha  will be  joining MO Alternates, as the MD & CEO and will lead the platform . 
Padmanabh is a seasoned private equity and alternatives leader with nearly 30 years of investing and 
leadership experience across India and global markets, spanning top global sovereign fu nds and Indian 
corporations as well as institutions. He is a former ED & CIO at National Investment and Infrastructure 
Funds (NIIF),  India’s sovereign -linked alternative asset manager with $ 5 billion AUM. His previous 
stints have been with Tata Capital w here he grew from CIO to Managing Partner of the Tata 
Opportunities Fund. He co -founded the India office and also worked as MD – India at Temasek, where 
he was responsible for ~USD 1 Bn of investments. He is an alumnus of IIM Kolkatta and BITS Pilani.  
 
Strengthening of Leadership in Retail Broking & Private Wealth Businesses:    
 
Mr. Ajay Menon, currently MD & CEO of the Wealth Management (Retail Broking) Business and Mr. 
Ashish Shankar, who was co -leading the Private Wealth Business will be moving on from their 
respective roles to jointly pursue their start up journey outside the group. In their multi -decadal 
journey with MOFS, both leaders have made significant contri butions to the growth and evolution of 
the organisation. The Group places on record its sincere appreciation for their invaluable contributions 
and wishes them the very best for the next phase of their journey.  
 
MOFS Group has been a leadership factory an d is proud to see many of its homegrown  leaders 
leading businesses across the financial services industry over the past 30 years. The strong 
leadership pipeline of 150 identified high -potential  leaders at MOFS Group and focus on succession 
planning is driv ing the new opportunities, appointments and elevations.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
Wealth Management:  
 
Suresh Shukla, currently ED & CBO of the Wealth Management Business will be taking over as the 
MD & CEO of the business. Suresh is a veteran in the Broking & Wealth Management industry and 
brings with him nearly 30 years of professional expertise spanning across Retail broking, Digital 
transformation, Sales and Distribution. Prior to joining MOFS, Suresh served a 25 year stint at Kotak 
Securities and a 3 year stint at SBI Securities with end -to-end P&L responsibility across business 
verticals and led a work force of over 16,000 professionals across 180+ branches.  
 
Private Wealth Management:   
 
The Private Wealth business of MOFS Group is currently co -led by Anupam Guha and Ashish Shankar. 
Going forward, Anupam Guha & Akash Hariani will co -lead the PWM busines s as MD & CEO’s driving 
the private wealth franchise to the next level. Anupam Guha and Akash Hariani are industry veterans, 
each with nearly 30 years of experience, and have been a part of the MOFS leadership team. Both of 
them have been instrumental in s caling up the MOPWM franchise. Pratik Oswal, co -promoter & board 
member, is being designated as Chairman - MOPWM and will work with both Anupam & Akash to 
grow this business manifold.  
 
These appointments reflect the Group's continued focus on building a st rong leadership pipeline, 
nurturing internal talent and bringing in leadership capabilities. Every leadership change has 
unleashed the next big phase of growth in the last 30 years and we are confident that this is one 
more such important milestone in MOFS  Group’s journey.  
 
Commenting on the leadership transitions, Mr. Motilal Oswal, Co -Founder, Motilal Oswal Financial 
Services , said:  
 
"We thank Ajay & Ashish for their invaluable contribution, commitment and role in building the 
organisation over the year s. We also welcome Padmanabh into the MOFSL family. We are known and 
recognised as Leadership Factory in the industry. We also congratulate our internal leaders, Suresh 
Shukla, Anupam Guha, Akash Hariani and Pratik Oswal on their extremely well -deserved el evation and 
wish them the very best in building the next phase of growth. As we transition in this journey, we 
remain focused on strengthening our leadership depth, building institutional capabilities and creating 
sustainable long -term value for all our st akeholders. Our belief in nurturing talent, empowering leaders 
and building an enduring institution continues to guide us."  
 
About Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited  
Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited (MOFSL) is India's largest integrated capit al market player , 
with strong and rising rankings across its businesses, each of which still offers a long growth runway. 
Rising wealth, to over USD 100 Tn, and the financialisation of savings are powerful tailwinds, and 
MOFSL is widening its presence by e ntering promising adjacencies in each of its businesses.
