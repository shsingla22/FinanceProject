Press Release  
Date: 13th Aug -2024  
 
Discover the Ancient Heritage of Badami  at Sterling ’s new resort in Karnataka  
• Spacious 10 -acre property for a tranquil holiday  
• 23 spacious cottages styled rooms  with sit -outs , largest in the region  
• Vatapi – Multi cuisine restaurant offering a uthentic local Saoji cuisine  
• Close to numerous heritage caves , temples, rock climbing and trekking trails  
 
Sterling proudly announces the opening of its latest resor t Sterling Banashree Badami, a 
quaint getaway nestled in the ancient heritage city of Badami , Karnataka. Th e new 
property in this hidden gem, marks a significant addition to Sterling ’s portfolio  of 50 plus 
resorts across India  and offer s a unique blend of historical charm, adventure and natural 
beauty.  
Badami, nestled in the heart of Karnataka, is a treasure trove of ancient heritage and 
architectural marvels. Known for its stunning rock -cut cave temples  with  intricate 
carvings, Badami offers a glimpse into the grandeur of the Chalukya dynasty. Pattadakal, 
nearby is a UNESCO World Heritage site  and Aihole is considered the “Cradle of Indian 
Temple Architecture ” – the designs created here have inspired various large & famous 
temples in the country.  The area’s unique sandstone formations make it a hotspot for rock 
climbing enthusiasts.  
Sterling Banashree Badami set within 10 acres of lush mango, coconut, and neem groves  
is a treasure trove featuring vast expanses of natural vistas, designed for laid -back 
holidays for some, and as a base for immersive adventure -holidays for others.   
The resort features Garden View Cottages and Premier Rooms, all designed as villa -styled 
cottage s featuring  spacious sit -outs  allowing guests to enjoy the tranquil surroundings. 
Starting  at 558 square feet  the cottages are the largest of their  kind in the region and come 
equipped with modern amenities, including a bathtub for relaxation  after a day of 
exploring the destination . Notably, Sterling Banashree Badami is pet -friendly, welcoming 
furry companions to join their owners on vacation.  
"We are delighted to launch Sterling Banashree  Badami  to increase our footprint in 
Karnataka and to further strengthen our presence in the Heritage circuit  thus  cater ing to 
the growing interest towards Heritage destinations amongst the new -age travellers .” said 
Mr. Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director and CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited.  
Dining at the resort is an experience, with  Vatapi, the multi -cuisine restaurant, serving a 
delightful array of global and Indian dishes. Guests are encouraged to indulge in the 
exquisite Saoji cuisine, a local specialty that showcases the flavo urs of Karnataka.  
Sterling Banashree Badami is not just a retreat for leisure trave llers; it also caters to 
corporate events and weddings. The property boasts a 4,000 square foot pillarless hall 
 
that can accommodate up to 300 guests, along with three expansive lawns and a private 
gazebo for outdoor events. The resort promises comprehensive planning services and 
exceptional hospitality to ensure a seamless experience for all occasions.  
“We are excited to be associated with Sterling Holidays. Sterling Banashree Badami is a 
unique resort that is designed for large families  and adventure enthusiast s and is ready 
to host intimate weddings  too. With Sterling’s expertise in developing destination s, we  
hope to make our resort a landmark for all kinds of domestic and international travellers ," 
said Mr. Ashok Kattimani, owner, Sri Ranganath Hotels & Resorts Pvt. Ltd.   
 
 
  
 
About Sterling Holiday Resorts Ltd.  
Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited is a leading hospitality company in India that owns & 
manages 50 -plus  resorts, hotels and retreats, across 45 -plus  iconic destinations. The 
company caters to leisure stays, Meetings and Conferences (MICE), Destination Weddings,

company offers a network of hotels across hills, beaches,  jungles, waterfront, adventure,
