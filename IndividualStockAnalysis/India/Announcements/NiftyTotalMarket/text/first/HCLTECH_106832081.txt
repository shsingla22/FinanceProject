HCLTech Launches AI -Powered Nano Global Capability Centers on AWS to Help Enterprises 
Scale AI Beyond the Pilot  
 
NOIDA, India and S YDNEY, Oct  28, 2026 — HCLTech , a leading global technology company, 
today announced the launch of an AI -powered nano  Global Capability Center (GCC) offering, 
built on  Amazon Web Services (AWS), to help enterprises move from AI experimentation to high -
value business outcomes.   
  
The nano GCC model is part of HCLTech's GCC 360 solution suite  and combines HCLTech’s 
GCC advisory capabilities and its AI platforms including AI Force . 
 
The model is designed for two enterprise archetypes:  
• Established GCCs already operating in -country on largely conventional work that need a 
non- disruptive vehicle to drive AI -led transformation through a hub -and-spoke model 
anchored from HCLTech’s Lucknow campus . 
• Mid-market enterprises without an in -country presence that are cautious about entering a 
new geography and want a low -risk platform to accelerate their AI journey .  
In both use cases, it enables organizations to begin with a targeted scope, establish AI -native 
operating models and reduce setup cycles, while demonstrating value and scaling progressively.  
 
Unlike traditional GCC models built around a broad scope and scale, the nano GCC model is 
designed around compact, product -centric squads, AI -enabled workflows and a land- and-
expand approach. The offering spans AI -native engineering, autonomous IT operatio ns, 
advanced data and analytics, and intelligent business operations, with responsible AI governance 
built in. HCLTech leads the operating model design and implementation, while AWS provides 
joint go -to-market support and access to agentic AI services including Amazon Bedrock , Kiro , 
and AWS Transform , to accelerate delivery.  
 
The nano GCC offering forms part of HCLTech’s broader global strategy, with the Asia Pacific  
(APAC) serving as the first phase of the rollout. It will be  delivered through HCLTech’s India -
based delivery organization  and supported by the Center  of Excellence in Lucknow,  providing 
access to India’s deep pool of technology and engineering talent.  
 "The nano GCC model is at an inflection point. What began as a cost and talent strategy is 
becoming the primary engine for enterprise AI capability building. The question for most 
organizations is no longer whether to build AI -native operations, but how to  do it without the 
drag of legacy setup models. The nano GCC is our answer to that urgency," said Kiran Cherukuri, 
Executive Vice President & Global GCC Practice Leader, HCLTech.
