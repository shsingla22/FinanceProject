MTR Expands its Sweets Portfolio with New Formats and Innovations  
Building on the iconic legacy of Mysore Pak and Gulab Jamun, MTR is taking its traditional favourites 
to a wider audience across India through differentiated offerings and new occasions for indulgence  
Bengaluru , 8th October  2026:  MTR Foods, one of India’s most trusted food brands with a legacy spanning 
over 100 years, is strengthening its presence in the sweets category with the launch of 8 new products, 
taking its portfolio to a total of 27 offerings spanning traditional favourites, ingredient -led innovations and 
differentiated formats.  
Building on its strong equity in the sweets segment, MTR is deepening its play in the category at a time 
when consumer preferences are evolving. While traditional sweets continue to hold a n integra l place in 
celebrations and culture, consumers are increasingly seeking greater variety, new experiences, mindful 
ingredients and formats suited to different lifestyles and occasions. Within this evolving landscape, MTR 
is focused not just on participating  in the category, but on expanding its possibilities by taking  its iconic 
products  into newer formats and consumption occasions, while staying rooted in familiar tastes and 
traditions.  
At the heart of this expansion are MTR’s timeless favourites, Mysore Pak and Gulab Jamun. Building on 
Mysore Pak’s strong equity, MTR has expanded the portfolio with variants including Milk Mysore Pak, Kaju 
Mysore Pak  and now the  Ghee Jaggery range, which includes Jaggery Ghee Mysore Pak, Jaggery Ghee 
Coconut Burfi and Ghee Dry Fruit Burfi . Its latest innovation, MTR Mysore Pak Date Bites, combines 
Mysore Pak with dates and dry fruits in a distinctive two -layered format, bridging traditional indulgence 
and everyday snacking. The Gulab Jamun portfolio has also evolved beyond its iconic mixes  and ready -to-
eat tins to include Dry Jamun, Kala Jamun and Kesar Badam Gulab Jamun, offering greater variety while 
retaining its familiar taste.  
Commenting on the launch, Sunay Bhasin, CEO, MTR , said:  “At MTR, innovation begins with 
understanding how consumer preferences are evolving. We are seeing strong momentum in the sweets 
category, supported by a growing demand for products that combine tradition with contemporary 
relevance . Sweets are becoming an increasingly important pillar of our business, and our focus is on 
creating new ways for consumers to enjoy familiar favourites.  Through new formats, ingredient -led 
innovations and differentiated consumption occasions, we are bringing  our iconic sweets such as Mysore 
Pak and Gulab Jamun to a wider audience across the country , while staying true to the taste, quality and 
convenience that define MTR. ” 
MTR’s  new sweets range is  available across the top metro cities through quick -commerce platforms and 
leading general trade  and modern trade  outlets . 
 
Beyond portfolio expansion, MTR is fostering a larger cultural conversation around sweets. Through its 
'India's Sweetest Language'  campaign, the brand brings the category to life through storytelling, books, 
live experiences and immersive activations, celebrating the role sweets play in expressing emotions, 
marking occasions and bringing people together.
