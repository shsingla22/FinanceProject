Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
This is to inform that the Company has received an order imposing penalty on the Company, the 
details of which are as follows:  
 
1. Name of the authority – Adjudicating Officer & Joint Commissioner (Amravati Division), Food 
and Drug Administration, Amravati . 
 
2. Nature and details of the action(s) taken, initiated or order(s) passed  – Penalty has been 
imposed on the Company and Manufacturer of the product. The Company has been directed to 
pay penalty of Rs.15,000 (Rupees fifteen thousand). 
 
3. Date of receipt of direction or order, including any ad-interim or interim orders, or any other 
communication from the authority   - June 21, 2024 
 
4. Details of the violation(s)/contravention(s) committed or alleged to be committed – The order 
has been issued in respect of violation of certain provisions of Food Safety and Standards (Food 
Products Standards and Food Additives) Regulation 2011 read with Food Safety and Standards 
(Packaging and Labelling) Regulation 2011 for misbranding of a food product produced by the 
manufacturer and sold in the DMart store located at Amravati Camp, Amravati, Maharashtra.  
 
5. Impact on financial, operation or other activities of the listed entity, quantifiable in monetary 
terms to the extent possible - The financial impact of the aforesaid order is to the extent of the 
penalty imposed. There is no material impact on the operations or other activities of the 
Company due to the said order. 
 
Request you to kindly take the above information on record.
