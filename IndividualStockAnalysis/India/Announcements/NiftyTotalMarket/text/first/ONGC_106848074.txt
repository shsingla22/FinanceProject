ONGC maintained broadly stable production during Q2FY’27  compared to Q1FY’27. The year on year moderation in quarterly production was primarily attributable to reservoir behaviour complexities in KG-98/2 Asset in Eastern Offshore Basin, coupled with  monsoon related operational challenges and associated delays in PRP-8 and PRP-9 (Pipeline Replacement Projects). Gas production from onshore fields was also affected by lower customer offtake during the quarter, following disruptions at certain major consumer facilities that temporarily reduced demand. ONGC has scaled up engagement of  M/s bp as Technical Service Provider(TSP) for entire Western Offshore (TSP-1 & TSP-2), as part of it’s strategy to enhance production from its mature fields through focused interventions to arrest natural decline and accelerate development activities. M/s bp mobilised its team against TSP-2 (for entire Western Offshore except MH field) in Aug’2026.  5. New Projects Major development and Infrastructure projects in Western Offshore (i) ONGC awarded two major offshore development projects, MBOSN-2005-1, SD-4 & BS-17 and the additional development of Ratna-1 & NLM-14, involving a combined capital investment of approximately ₹11,800 crore. These projects are expected to unlock an estimated 22 MMTOE of hydrocarbon potential, strengthening future production from the Western Offshore region. (ii) ONGC also awarded the Pipeline Replacement Project-X (PRP-X), with an estimated project cost of over ₹4,300 crore, for the installation of new subsea pipelines and replacement of ageing pipeline infrastructure in the Western Offshore. The project is aimed at enhancing asset integrity, operational reliability, and long-term production sustainability. 6. Exploration Performance: (i) Samudra Manthan: ONGC drilled its first exploratory well MNDWO181HDB-1 in OALP Block MN-DWHP-2018/1 in the Mahanadi deepwater. The well has been proved gas-bearing, marking an important outcome of ONGC's exploration efforts under the National Offshore Exploration Scheme.  (iii) Discoveries: ONGC has notified two hydrocarbon discoveries both onland during Q2 FY’27, comprising one new prospect discovery, Amraghat-1, and one new pool discovery, Vadatal-48.  The details of the latest discoveries notified since last press release in this regard on 04.08.2026 are as under.  
 
   
 
Amraghat-1: Exploratory well Amraghat-1 was drilled in Cachar District PML in Assam-Arakan fold Belt with the objective to explore Upper and Middle Bhuban Formations. The successful gas flow in the well led to notification as new prospect discovery. Vadatal-48: Well, Vadatal-48 was drilled in Vadatal Ext-I PML (NELP-VI block CB- ONN-2004/2) in Cambay basin to produce oil from Chhatral Pay. New sand was encountered in the well below Chhatral Pay. The find creates new exploration opportunities below active Chhatral Pay and is notified as new pool discovery in Chhatral Lower Pay.  7. Other Highlights/ Awards/Recognitions (i) Takeover of CB-OS/2 block: ONGC was designated as the Government’s nominee to continue petroleum operations in the CB-OS/2 block and assumed control and possession of all associated assets, data, facilities, infrastructure, and operations with effect from 22 July 2026.  (ii) Global Decarbonization Award 2026 in Platinum Category from Energy & Environment Foundation: ONGC has been honored with the Platinum Category Award at the 16th Annual EEF Global Decarbonization Award 2026 held in New Delhi on 2 July 2026, one of the most prestigious recognitions conferred by the Energy and Environment Foundation (EEF) for outstanding achievements in Decarbonization, Net Zero Transition and Sustainable Energy Leadership.  (iii) Best PSU Partner at PMIS Recognition of Partner Company Awards 2026: ONGC has been recognized as the Best PSU Partner at the Prime Minister Internship Scheme (PMIS) Recognition of Partner Company Awards 2026, organized by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India, in New Delhi on 14 August 2026. (iv) Global Sustainability Champion Award 2026 in Platinum Category: ONGC has been conferred with the prestigious Global Sustainability Champion Award 2026 by the Global Energy & Environment Foundation (GEEF) in recognition of its commitment to sustainability, ESG excellence, and climate action in New Delhi on 21 August 2026.  (v)  IPEOT wins double honors at 16th Annual EEF Awards 2026: ONGC’s Institute of Production Engineering and Ocean Technology (IPEOT) bagged double recognition at the 16th Annual Energy & Environment Foundation Awards 2026, held on 2 July 2026 in New Delhi. The prestigious honors acknowledge ONGC's leadership in both digital innovation and environmental sustainability, reaffirming its commitment to advancing intelligent and sustainable energy operations 
 
   
 
(vi) NH&BS Asset wins Corporate EHS Excellence Award 2026 : NH&BS Asset  has been conferred with the Corporate EHS Excellence Award at the Greentech 3rd EHS Summit & Awards 2026, recognizing the Asset’s commitment to Environment, Health and Safety excellence and sustainable offshore operations.  (vii) IPEOT secures 35th patent for developing ‘Single Phase Retarded Acid System’ for enhancing offshore oil production: IPEOT was granted its 35th patent for developing an in-house stimulation formulation, the ‘Single-Phase Retarded Acid system (SPRAS)’, on 20 August 2026.  (viii) IDWE receives 12th Patent for innovative Water-Based Drilling-Fluid Technology: The Institute of Drilling and Well Engineering (IDWE), Dehradun, has achieved a significant milestone in drilling-fluid innovation with the grant of an Indian patent by the Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, Government of India, on 8 September 2026.
