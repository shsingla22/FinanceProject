Thomas Cook India  strengthens West Bengal  footprint  
Opens outlet in Kolkata  
Increases consumer access to 10 locations in the city  
 
 The new outlet intends to leverage the growing travel demand from the region  
 Growth drivers include regional Bengali group tours, featuring experienced Bengali -speaking Tour 
Managers  and the comfort of travelling with co- travellers from the region  
 
Mumbai,  November  24, 2026 : With a strategic focus on capitalising on the strong and growing travel demand from 
Kolkata  – one of its  key source markets, Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel travel 
services company, has inaugurated its new outlet in Kolkata . The new outlet expands Thomas Cook India’s 
network to 10  locations in the city and 1 1 across the State, enhancing access to its comprehensive range of 
travel services.  
 
Baranagar is an important residential and commercial catchment in North Kolkata, supported by its strong 
connectivity and proximity to key residential neighbourhoods and emerging growth hubs. Its s trategic location 
makes it well suited to cater to the travel needs of a diverse and growing base of travellers across North Kolkata 
and adjoining areas.  
 The new outlet will cater to travellers from Baranagar, Dunlop and the BT Road corridor, along with neighbouring 
catchments of Dakshineswar, Barasat and Hooghly district across diverse customer segments including families, 
couples, Young India's Millennials and Gen Z, business travellers and GenS (seniors). Thomas Cook’s 
Baranagar  outlet offers end- to-end travel services: International & Domestic Holidays (Group Tours, 
Personalised holidays , Cruises, etc.), Value Added Services like Travel Insurance, etc . Additionally, Thomas 
Cook’s AI booking platform assist s customers in planning their personalized vacations in real time by checking 
availability  and making reservations in a matter of minutes . 
 
Kolkata /West Bengal  Trav el Trends   
 
• Year -round travel: Travellers from Kolkata are increasingly leveraging public holidays and weekends to 
take multiple short break s and mini -cations throughout the year, extending travel beyond traditional 
holiday seasons.  
• Preference for experiential holidays: Travellers are increasingly looking for holidays that offer distinctive experiences and greater personalisation, moving beyond conventional destination- based 
itineraries.  
• Diverse travel formats:  Cruise holidays and charter -led journeys are gaining traction among travellers, 
with Thomas Cook India launching exclusive charter flights to Lakshadweep and Bhutan for the year -end 
holiday season.  
• Growing demand for pilgrimage and spiritual tourism: Pilgrimage and spiritual travel continue to gain 
momentum, with Thomas Cook India strengthening its Spiritual Tourism portfolio ‘Bhakti’ to cater to this 
growing segment. Key spiritual hubs include Kailash Mansarovar, Char Dham, Vaishnodevi, Ujjain, Puri, 
Kashi and Ayodhya.
