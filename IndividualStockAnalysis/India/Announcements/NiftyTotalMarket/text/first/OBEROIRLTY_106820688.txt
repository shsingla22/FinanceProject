OBEROI REALTY ANNOUNCES H1FY2 7 AND Q2FY2 7 RESULTS  
 
HIGHLIGHTS OF CONSOLIDATED FINANCIALS FOR H1FY2 7 AND Q2FY2 7 
 
• Booking value for H1FY2 7 is Rs. 10,199.57 crores and for Q2FY2 7 is Rs. 9,149.69  crores  
 
• Revenue for H1FY2 7 is Rs. 3,098.06  crores  and Q2FY2 7 is Rs. 1,736.37  crores . EBITDA 
for H1FY2 7 is Rs. 1,879.53 crores  and for Q2FY2 7 is Rs. 1,084.59  crores.  
 
• Profit After Tax (PAT) for H1FY2 7 is Rs. 1,303.72  crores  and for Q2FY2 7 is Rs. 759.01  
crores  
 
Mumbai, October 16th, 202 6: Oberoi Realty Limited, headquartered in Mumbai, today announced 
its results for the first half of FY2 7 and the second quarter of FY2 7. The Company has recorded 
booking value of Rs. 10,199.57 crores for H1FY2 7 as against Rs . 2,937.73 crores for H1FY2 6. 
 
The Consolidated Revenue of Rs. 3,098.06  crores  for H1FY2 7 as against Rs. 2,918.82 crores  for 
H1FY2 6. Further , the Consolidated Revenue for Q2FY2 7 is Rs. 1,736.37  crores  as against Rs. 
1,361.69  crores  for Q 1FY27. 
 
The Consolidated Profit After Tax is Rs. 1,303.72  crores  for H1FY2 7 as against Rs. 1,180.46  crores  
for H1FY2 6. Further , the Consolidated Profit After Tax for Q2FY2 7 is Rs. 759.01  crores  as against 
Rs. 544.71  crores  for Q 1FY27.  
 
Commenting on the Q2FY2 7 results, Mr. Vikas Oberoi, Chairman & Managing Director, Oberoi 
Realty , said, “The demand in India for real estate continues to be driven by a preference for quality, 
brand credibility and execution, with established developers and proven delivery track records 
continuing to attract sustained interest. In the last quarter, our reside ntial, commercial, retail and 
hospitality businesses continued to deliver steady performance, reflecting the strength of our 
diversified portfolio.  
 
Our entry into NCR with our project Three Sixty North marked an important milestone in Oberoi 
Realty’s journey. The Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR) market continues to demonstrate its 
depth and resilience, and we remain encouraged by demand trends as we e nter the festive period. 
We continue to take a strategic approach to growth, with a focus on executing our existing 
developments while pursuing opportunities that are aligned with our long -term vision.”

About Oberoi Realty Limited  
Oberoi Realty Ltd. is one of India’s leading real estate development company, headquartered in 
Mumbai. It is focused on premium developments in the residential, office space, retail, hospitality 
and social infrastructure verticals . In the real estate space, Oberoi Realty is an established brand 
with an impeccable track record. Its primary aim is to build aspirational developments for its 
customers with distinctive designs, functional aesthetics and quality finishes that translate i nto 
landmark projects through its mixed -use and single -segment developments. This mix of innovative 
design, planning initiatives and use of cutting -edge technologies has enabled the company to
