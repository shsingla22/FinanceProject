Backed by a robust 28.7% H1 margin, GHFL raises its FY27 EBITDA guidance by 200 bps (to 
27% ±2%) while reaffirming its INR 2,500+ Cr Revenue target.

Solar Control Films, Paint Protection Films and other Specialty Films, announced its 
unaudited FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER AND HALF YEAR ENDED SEPTEMBER 30,

Financial Highlights  
 
Strong Operating Performance Drives Peak 27.2% Q2 EBITDA Margin, Despite Geopolitical 
Headwinds  
• Record -Breaking Revenue Performance : Revenue from operations reached a record 
₹696 crore, registering 22% YoY and 10% QoQ growth, driven by robust demand and 
improved realizations across product segments, despite  slowdown in the Middle East 
amid the ongoing conflict.  
• Strong Margin Expansion:  EBITDA increased 42% YoY to INR 189 crore, with EBITDA 
margin expanding by 37 7 basis points to 27.2% from 23.4% in Q2 FY26. The 
improvement was driven by operating leverage and a favou rable product mix, which 
helped offset higher raw material and logistics costs . 
 
• Robust Earnings Growth:  PAT increased 42% YoY to INR 129 crore, with PAT margin 
expanding to 18.6% from 16.0% in Q2 FY26, reflecting improved operational 
performance and profitability.  
 
Media Release                                                                                                                          
 
Strong Cash Generation  and Optimised Working Ca pital   
• Strong Liquidity Position:  Cash and cash equivalents stood at INR 933 crore as of 
September 30, 2026.  
 
• Strong WC Discipline: Working capital days stood at 29 days as of September 30, 
2026, remaining broadly stable against 28 days in FY26 reflect ing prudent inventory 
management and disciplined control over receivables and payables  as revenue  
scales.  
Strategic & Operational Highlights  
Capacity Expansion & Strategic Partnerships  
• Capex -Led Growth Momentum:  TPU line commissioning remains on track for Q3 
FY27. The INR 191 crore SCF line, equipped with advanced robotics and automation, 
is expected to commence commercial production in H1 FY28, adding ~1,200 LSF of 
fungible capacity.  
 
• Strategic Collaboration with Lubrizol:  Signed an MoU with Lubrizol to develop 
advanced TPU -based film solutions in India, combining Lubrizol’s technology 
expertise with GHFL’s manufacturing capabilities and strengthening the premium PPF 
growth opportunity.  
Product Innovation & New Growth Segments  
• PDLC - Entry into Smart Architecture:  Launched state of the art facility for switchable 
privacy film, enabling instant switching between transparent and opaque states.  
 
• Consumer Product Launches:  Introduced car -care products and Ultra Series WF kits 
with a 1 -year warranty.  
 
• Digital Services:  Launched the e -Warranty portal and Pine Labs No Cost EMI facility.  
D2C Expansion & Market Penetration  
 
• Global Expansion:  Opened two new Global Application Studios (GAS) in the USA 
during Q2 FY27, taking the total global network to 14 studios, comprising 11 in the 
USA and 3 in the Middle East.  
 
• Domestic Expansion:  Scaled to 14 Garware Home Solutions (GHS) studios, up from 9 
in Q1  FY27  
 
• GHS Growth Roadmap:  Plans to add 12 studios in Q3  with launches in Delhi NCR and 
Mumbai, targeting 50 studios by Dec’27.

About Garware Hi -Tech Films Limited (GHFL):  
GHFL ( BSE:  500655 | NSE:  GRWRHITECH), is one of the largest manufacturers of Sun Control 
window films for architectural & automotive applications, paint protection films and high -end 
BOPET films for label and industrial applications. The Company has been recognized as a world 
class brand and leverages its state -of-the-art nano -dispersion and other cutting -edge 
technologies to maintain its market leadership. Being vertically integrated, it tightly controls 
product quality and provides a great value proposition to its customers. It has a strong 
presence in 90+ countries.
