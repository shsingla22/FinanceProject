Dear Shareholder, 
 
We are pleased to inform you that the Board of Directors of InterGlobe Aviation 
Limited (' the Company '), at its meeting held on May 21, 2025 has recommended a 
Final Dividend of Rs. 10 (Rupees Ten only) per equity share (face value of Rs. 10/- each) 
for the Financial Year ('FY') ended March 31, 2025, subject to the approval of the 
shareholders at the ensuing Annual General Meeting (‘AGM’). The said dividend, if 
approved by the shareholders at the AGM, shall be paid/ dispatched within 30 days of 
approval. 
 
In accordance with the provisions of the Income Tax Act, 1961, as amended by the 
Finance Act, 2020 ('the IT Act') dividend paid by the Company on or after April 1, 2020, 
is taxable in hands of shareholders. The Company shall, therefore, be required to 
deduct Tax at Source ('TDS') at the applicable rates [prescribed under the IT Act, read 
with Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (wherever applicable)] on dividend 
payable to its shareholders. The rate of TDS would vary depending on the residential 
status of the shareholder and the documents submitted and duly accepted by the 
Company. Accordingly, the above referred Final Dividend will be paid after deducting
