Schaeffler India recognizes  the winners of its flagship Social Innovator 
Fellowship Program  
Pune , India  | 22 December  2025 | Schaeffler India, a leading motion technology 
company  has announce d the  winners of its 4th Edition  of the ‘ Schaeffler India Social 
Innovator Fellowship Program’.  The program is designed to recognize and empower 
young social innovators who are creating impactful and scalable solutions to some 
of society’s most pressing environmental and societal challenges. By nurturing 
ideas that combine innovation with sustainabil ity, it seeks to transform grassroots 
initiatives into long -term change drivers that can improve communities.  
Marking its fourth  edition, this year’s fellowship  program  invited applications from 
individuals, groups, institutions, and organizations , both for -profit and non -profit , 
across India. The program was open to applicants aged 18 –35 who are addressing 
pressing challenges in Environmental sustainability, Renewable energy, Carbon 
neutrality, Circular economy, Natural resource management, and the application of 
technology in the social sector.   
After  a rigorous screening process, which encompasses stringent evaluations and 
prototype demonstrations , the top 10 exceptional projects were awarded a grant 
of INR 1.75 lakh and access to an intensive 24 -week hybrid mentorship program at 
the renowned IIM  ventures  at IIM Ahmedabad , designed to accelerate their ideas. 
The program will provide tools,  framework, access to industry expert mentoring,  
and resources to be market ready and scale their solutions to amplify their impact.  
Since  its enrolment  opened  in July 202 5, the program has received over 460 
registrations from over 21 states  and final submission of 172 eligible applications , 
including the state of  Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, Orrissa, Madhya 
Pradesh, UP, Gujarat, Assam, Tamilnadu, Jammu and Kashmir, West Bengal, AP  and 
Delhi, P unjab and more  with 103 shortlisted applicants  for assessment .  
Harsha Kadam, MD and CEO of Schaeffler India  stated that “Change is the only 
constant . In a dynamic world where environment and technology play a 
fundamental role to meet the evolving needs of the world’s populus, the Social 
Innovator Fellowship Program speaks volumes of Schaeffler’s commitment to ESG 
and building not just a technologically advanced business model but be sensitive to 
the environm ent we operate in. It is our commitment to building a sustainable 
future that drives us to keep supporting this p rogram year on year. With the 
response rates from participants growing over the years it’s overwhelming to see

build a better world.    
We are happy that  program is designed to identify and empower young 
changemakers who are developing scalable, impactful, and technology -driven 
solutions. In doing so, Schaeffler aims to harness entrepreneurial spirit and provide 
a platform where innovators can translate th eir ideas into transformative actions 
that make a sustainable impact on society. ” 
Lastly, he congratulated all the Innovators and thanked them for their participation 
and wished them success in commercializing their projects so the world can be a 
little better place for future generations.  
The award ceremony was graced by Professor Gurudas Nulkar , Director Centre for 
Sustainable Development, Gokhale Institute of Politics and Economics , Mr. Munish 
Bhatia , Co-Founder , India Accelerator & CEO, Bharat Ventures  , Dr Bharat Kakade  
-President & Managing Trustee, BAIF Development Research Foundation and Ms. 
Ankur Sohanpal , AVP IIMA Ventures . 
Beyond this fellowship, Schaeffler India continues to drive social development 
through targeted initiatives such as promoting STEM education in underserved 
areas, advancing water conservation, encouraging agroforestry and climate -
resilient agriculture, and  supporting community health projects. Through these 
initiatives, the company envisions creating an ecosystem where innovation and 
sustainability converge, ensuring equitable progress and positive transformation in 
society.

Schaeffler India Honours Amravati Innovator Swapnil Gawande with ‘Social 
Innovator Fellowship’  
Mumbai, India | 22 December 2025  | Schaeffler India, a leading motion technology 
company has announced the winners of its 2025 edition of the ‘Schaeffler India 
Social Innovator Fellowship Program’. The program is designed to recognize and 
empower young social innovators who are creating impactful and scalable solutions 
to some of society’s most pressing environmental and societal challenges. By 
nurturing ideas that combine innovation with sustainability, it seeks to transform 
grassroots initiatives into lon g-term change drivers that can improve communities.  
Among the top ten winners, Swapnil Gawande from  Amravati  has been recognized 
for his innovation, a unified digital platform  that connects all Eye Banks, Eye 
Retrieval Centres, and Eye Transplant Centres to enable real -time tracking, 
transparent allocation, and fair distribution of corneal tissues. The system 
streamlines coordination, reduces delays, minimizes wastage, and ensu res that 
patients across regions receive timely and equitable access to sight -restoring 
corneal transplants.

across India. The program was open to applicants aged 18 –35 who are addressing
