PRESS RELEASE   
 
 
 
SJVN Signs Power Purchase Agreements with GUVNL for Supply of Green Power from 
Hydro Electric Projects in Himachal Pradesh  
 
Sh. Bhupender Gupta, CMD, SJVN, informed that SJVN has signed Power Purchase 
Agreements (PPAs) with Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited (GUVNL) for the supply of power 
from its upcoming hydro electric projects in Himachal Pradesh namely 66 MW Dhaulasidh 
HEP, 210 MW Luhri Stage -I HEP and 382 MW Sunni Dam HEP.  
 
The Power Purchase Agreements were signed today at Vadodara, Gujarat, by Sh. Aman Katoch, 
General Manager (C&SO), SJVN and Sh. Sanjay Mathur, General Manager (Commerce), 
GUVNL.  The signing ceremony was attended by senior officials from both organisations.  
 
These agreements strengthen SJVN's portfolio of long -term power sale arrangements with 
leading power utilities across the country and reinforce its position as a key contributor to 
India's transition towards a cleaner, greener and more resilient energy fut ure.
