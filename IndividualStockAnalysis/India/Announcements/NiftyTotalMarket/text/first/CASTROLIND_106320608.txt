PRESS RELEASE 
 
Castrol India launches upgraded MAGNATEC engine oil  range with 
latest global API SQ specification 
 Proudly pioneering API SQ made in India, for India  
 
Mumbai, 1 August 2025:  Castrol India has pioneered to launch engine oils in India meeting the 
world’s newest API SQ specification, setting a benc hmark in passenger car lubrication. What makes 
this milestone even more special – the upgraded Cas trol MAGNATEC range is being produced right 
here in India, reinforcing the Company’s commitment  to Made in India, for India. 
 
The API SQ standard, introduced globally from April  2025, is the most advanced engine oil 
specification by the American Petroleum Institute. Castrol India has brought it to Indian drivers with in 
months of its global debut, showcasing its ability to move fast and deliver cutting-edge technology 
locally. 
 
“Indian drivers expect more from their cars—better f uel efficiency, lower maintenance, and 
performance they can count on in every condition. W ith the new Castrol MAGNATEC range meeting 
API SQ standards, we are giving them exactly that—a  world-class product, made in India, and made 
for India. From start-up protection to long-term re liability, this is another first from Castrol, brin ging a 
global innovation to Indian roads almost immediatel y after its launch worldwide, ” said Saugata 
Basuray, Wholetime Director, Castrol India . 
 
The upgraded MAGNATEC range offers: 
 Stronger wear protection for modern engines 
 Better fuel efficiency 
 Enhanced defence for catalytic converters 
 Compatibility with India’s E20 fuel environment 
 
It will be available in popular viscosities – 0W-20 , 5W-30, and 5W-40 – meeting API SQ, ACEA C2 
or C3, and future-ready performance needs. 
 
The new MAGNATEC range will be rolled out across re tail outlets and workshops across India in the 
coming weeks.
