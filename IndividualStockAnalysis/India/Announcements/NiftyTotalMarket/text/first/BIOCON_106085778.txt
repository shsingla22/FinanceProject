PRESS NOTE  
Kiran Mazumdar -Shaw Honored by ISQ with ‘Jamsetji Tata Award ’  
for Pioneering Biosciences Movement in India   
 
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India: December 14, 2024 : 
 
Biocon Group Chairperson Kiran Mazumdar -Shaw  has been conferred the prestigious 
Jamsetji Tata Award  by the Indian Society for Quality ( ISQ) for pioneering the 
bioscience s movement in India  and serv ing patients in India and across the world 
through an unwavering focus on quality, while leading Biocon to a preeminent global 
position.  
 
The Jamsetji Tata Award, instituted in 2004 by ISQ, 
is named after Jamsetji Tata, regarded as the ‘Father 
of Modern Indian Industry’ and recognizes business 
leaders who have made significant contributions to 
Indian society and demonstrated focus on quality , 
commitment , and a record of outstanding results.  
 
The Award  was announced at the ISQ Annual 
Conference  2024 , held in Bengaluru. Kiran 
Mazumdar -Shaw  received the award from  Janak 
Kumar Mehta , President of ISQ & Chairman & 
Managing Director , TQM International ; B. 
Muthuraman , former Vice Chairman  of Tata Steel ; 
and Dr K .N. Subramanya , Principal  of R V College of 
Engineering  and President  of ISQ’s Bengaluru Chapter . 
 
While presenting the award to Kiran Janak Kumar Mehta, President of ISQ & 
Chairman & Managing Director of TQM International  said: “This Lifetime 
achievement award is being presented to Kiran Mazumdar -Shaw for being a pioneer 
in the bioscience movement in India since 1978, a first -generation entrepreneur to 
establish Biocon Limited and associated companies to serve customers in India a nd 
across the world. This award is actually  awarded to those who  have done not only 
good for their own company, but for the society on the whole. It is a recognition o f 
your outstanding work and bringing India to the map of the world.”  
 
Biocon Group Chairperson Kiran Mazumdar -Shaw said: “I am deeply honoured and 
humbled to accept the 2024 Jamsetji  Tata Award instituted by ISQ. This award holds a 
very special meaning to me as it is named after one of India’s greatest visionaries —a 
man whose legacy of excellence, innovation, and nation -building continues to inspire 
us all. Jamsetji Tata’s commitment  to quality and his belief in the transformative power

About ISQ  
Indian Society for Quality (ISQ) is a not -for profit society established in 1996 to fill the need 
for a national forum for interaction among quality professionals, leaders, practitioners and 
academicians. ISQ is a nonpartisan, independent body that attracts and invites individual 
members from business  organizations, health care and educational institutions, government 
agencies and NGOs.   Members share their knowledge and learn from each other. ISQ is thus 
engaged in both creating and disseminating knowledge. Its mission is to “Contribute to the 
thriving  of humanity in a healthy planet” ISQ is well linked to similar national quality bodies in 
other count ries. It is a board member from its very inception and has represented India in the 
Asian Network for Quality (ANQ). ISQ is a national partner for Quality Sustainability Award 
promoted by International Academy for Quality. ISQ is the face of India in the w orld of 
quality.   To know more, please visit www.isqnet.org
