Subject: Order levying penalty by Additional Commissioner CGST, Pune 
 
Ref: Disclosure under Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (Listing Regulations) 
 
Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
We wish to inform that, the Company has received an order from Additional Commissioner 
CGST, Pune-II Commissionerate, levying tax demand of Rs. 52,55,51,043 and penalty of Rs. 
52,55,76,043 under Section 74 of Central Goods and Services Tax Act read along with other 
applicable rules thereunder, for financial years 2019-20, 2020-21 and 2021-22, levying output 
GST liability for allegedly treating leave and license agreement as finance lease arrangement 
amounting into transfer of proprietary interest in the leased property.  
 
Further, the Company will be filing an appeal in this regard with appropriate Authority.
