Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in  
Gir, Gujarat  
 
New Delhi, 26th September  2024: Lemon Tree Hotels announced its latest 
signing – Lemon Tree Hotel,  Sasan Gir, Gujarat . The property, which shall be 
managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited , is expected to open in FY 202 6.  
 
Gujarat is home to the renowned Gir National Park, which is best known as the 
last natural habitat of the critically endangered Asiatic lion. The park features a 
divers e ecosystem, encompassing dense forests, grasslands, and water bodies, 
creating an ideal environment for a variety of wildlife. Visitors can also encounter 
leopards, antelopes, deer, and numerous bird species. With its stunning 
landscapes and rich biodiver sity, Gir National Park attracts nature lovers and 
wildlife enthusiasts from around the world.  
 
Lemon Tree Hotel, Sasan Gir, Gujarat, will feature 7 4 well-appointed rooms, a 
restaurant , a meeting room, a banquet hall, a swimming pool, a spa, and a 
fitness center. Keshod Airport is approxi mately 60 kms from the property while 
the Diu Airport is at a distance of 100 kms and Hirasar Rajkot International 
Airport  is 180 kms away. The nearest railway stations , Junagadh and Veraval , 
are around 50 km and 45 km away, respectively. The hotel is also well -
connected by road, making it accessible by both public and private transport.  
 
Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO — Managed & Franchise Business, Lemon Tree Hotels said 
on the occasion , “We are excited to share the expansion of  our portfolio in 
Gujarat , where we currently have seven  existing propert ies. This addit ion will 
complement our ten  upcoming propert ies in the state .”
