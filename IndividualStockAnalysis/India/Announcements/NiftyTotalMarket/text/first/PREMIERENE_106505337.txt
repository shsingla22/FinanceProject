Sub: Commissioning of 400 MW Solar Photovoltaic Cell Mono perc facility at E City 
Maheeshwaram, Telangana. 
 
 
With reference to the captioned subject, we are pleased to inform you that the Company, 
through its wholly owned subsidiary, Premier Energies Photovoltaic Private Limited, has 
successfully commissioned a 400 MW Solar Photovoltaic Cell (Mono PERC) 
manufacturing facility at its E-City plant, Maheshwaram, Telangana. 
 
This commissioning is part of a brownfield expansion of the Company’s existing cell 
manufacturing operations and has been achieved through the installation of additional 
equipment while leveraging the existing infrastructure and utilities at the facility.  
 
Post Commissioning the cell line, the operational capacity for cells stands at 3.6 GW.
