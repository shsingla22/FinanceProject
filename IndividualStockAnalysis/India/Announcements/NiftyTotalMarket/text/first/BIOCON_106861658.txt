PRESS RELEASE   
  
Biocon Gets U.S. FDA Approval for Pebrilzo ™ (pertuzumab -qcay ) 
Injection , a Biosimilar to Perjeta® , Expanding Its Oncology Portfolio  
 
• Biocon’s PEBRILZO  approval represents an important step toward broadening  treatment 
option for patients with HER2 -positive breast cancer  in the United States, subject to the 
anticipated commercial launch timeline  
• PEBRILZO further  strengthens  Biocon’s oncology portfolio , now totaling five biosimilar 
therapies for cancer  in the United States  
 
BENGALURU, India and BRIDGEWATER , N.J., United States: November  16, 202 6 
 
Biocon Limited (BSE: 532523; NSE: BIOCON), an innovation -led global biopharmaceutical company,  
today announced that the U.S. Food and Drug Administration  (FDA) has approved Pebrilzo ™ 
(pertuzumab -qcay ), a biosimilar of the reference product P erjeta ® (pertuzumab). PEBRILZO  is a 
monoclonal antibody  used to treat HER2 -positive breast cancer by blocking HER2 receptors on cancer 
cells . The FDA approval further  strengthens Biocon’s oncology portfolio . 
 
Shreehas Tambe, CEO & Managing Director, Biocon L imited , said , “The U.S. FDA approval of 
PEBRILZO , our biosimilar pertuzumab, marks another significant milestone in Biocon’s journey to 
expand access to high -quality biosimilars. For patients diagnosed with HER2 -positive breast cancer, 
the burden of the disease extends well beyond the physical challenge s, often placing considerable 
financial strain on patients and their families. As our fifth oncology biosimilar to receive U.S. FDA 
approval, PEBRILZO strengthens our anti -cancer portfolio and advances our commitment to expanding 
access to high -quality, affordable biologic medicines.”  
 
In September, the European Medicines Agency ’s Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use 
(CHMP) had given a positive opinion  recommending the granting of a marketing authorization for 
Biocon’s  Pertuzumab biosimilar  as 420 mg /14 mL concentrate solution for infusion, under the brand 
name Pebrilzo®, for the treatment of HER2 -positive breast cancer across multiple disease stages in 
Europe.  
 
Biocon also announced a global settlement and license agreement with Genentech  securing the 
earliest licensed launch date for PEBRILZO  in the United States in Q3 of calendar year 2028.  
 
Biocon’s Oncology Portfolio  and Pipeline :  
Biocon’s oncology strategy combines an established portfolio with continued development across 
additional treatment approaches. PEBRILZO  will join its robust p ortfolio of oncology therapies, which 
includes  Ogivri ® (trastuzumab -dkst), Jobevne ® (bevacizumab -nwgd), Fulphila ® (pegfilgrastim -jmdb), 
and Aukelso ® (denosumab -kyqq).  Building on this foundation, Biocon’s growing oncology pipeline 
includes proposed biosimilars to trastuzumab/hyaluronidase, nivolumab and pembrolizumab, 
spanning HER2 -directed therapy and immuno -oncology.  
 
About HER 2-Positive Breast Cancer : 
HER2 -positive breast cancer is a subtype of breast cancer characterized by HER2 protein 
overexpression and/or HER2 gene amplification , which drives aggressive tumor growth.  
 
 
2 
 
Approximately 320,000 women in the United States are expected to be diagnosed with breast cancer in 
2026.1 Of those diagnoses, about 20 % will be HER2 -positive breast cancer2. On a broader scale, 1 in 8 
women are expected to be diagnosed with  breast  cancer during  her lifetime, highlighting the ongoing 
need for treatment options in this area3. 
 
About  PEBRILZO (pertuzumab -qcay ) 
The approval for PEBRILZO  (pertuzumab -qcay ) was based on a comprehensive package containing 
structural and functional analytical characterization, analytical  similarity , and comparative clinical 
pharmacokinetic data, which confirmed that PEBRILZO is highly similar to the reference biologics, 
Perjeta ® with no clinically meaningful differences in quality, safety, efficacy, and immunogenicity.  
 
WARNING  
LEFT VENTRICULAR DYSFUNCTION and  EMBRYO -FETAL TOXICITY  
• Left Ventricular Dysfunction: PEBRILZO can cause subclinical and clinical cardiac failure 
manifesting as decreased LVEF and CHF. Evaluate cardiac function prior to and during 
treatment. Discontinue PEBRILZO treatment for a confirmed clinically significant decrease 
in left ventricular function .  
• Embryo -fetal Toxicity: Exposure to PEBRILZO can cause embryo -fetal death and birth 
defects. Advise patients of these risks and the need for effective contraception .  
 
INDICATION S 
PEBRILZO  is a HER2/neu receptor antagonist  indicated for : 
• Metastatic Breast Cancer (MBC):  PEBRILZO is indicated for use in combination with  
trastuzumab and docetaxel for the treatment of adults with HER2 -positive metastatic breast 
cancer who have not received prior anti -HER2  therapy or chemotherapy for metastatic disease . 
• Early Breast Cancer (EBC): PEBRILZO  is indicated for use in combination with trastuzumab 
and chemotherapy for : 
o the neoadjuvant treatment of adults with HER2 -positive, locally  advanced, 
inflammatory, or early -stage breast cancer (either  greater than 2 cm in diameter or node 
positive) as part of a  complete treatment regimen for early breast cancer .  
o the adjuvant treatment of adults with HER2 -positive early  breast cancer at high risk of 
recurrence . 
 
CONTRAINDICATIONS  
PEBRILZO  is contraindicated in patients with known  hypersensitivity to pertuzumab or to any of its 
excipients .

trastuzumab.

Embryo -Fetal Toxicity  
• Based on its mechanism of action and findings in animal studies,  pertuzumab can cause fetal 
harm when administered to a  pregnant woman. Pertuzumab is a HER2/neu receptor  antagonist. 
Cases of oligohydramnios and oligohydramnios sequence  manifesting as pulmonary 
hypoplasia, skeletal abnormalities, and  neonatal death have been reported with use of another 
HER2/neu  receptor antagonist (trastuzumab) during pregnancy. In an animal  reproduction 
study, administration of pertuzumab to pregnant  cynomolgus monkeys during the period of 
organogenesis resulted in  oligohydramnios, delayed fetal kidney development, and embryo -

based on Cmax.  
 
Infusion -Related Reactions  
• Pertuzumab can cause serious infusion reactions, including  fatal events . 
• In CLEOPATRA, on the first day, when only pertuzumab was administered, infusion -related 
reactions occurred in 13% of patients and < 1% were Grade 3 or 4. The most common infusion 
reactions (≥ 1%) were pyrexia, chills, fatigue, headache, asthenia, hypersens itivity, and 
vomiting. During the second cycle when all drugs were administered on the same day, the most 
common infusion reactions in the pertuzumab -treated group (≥ 1%) were fatigue, dysgeusia, 
hypersensitivity, myalgia, and vomiting. In APHINITY, when pertuzumab was administered in 
combination with trastuzumab and chemotherapy on the same day, infusion -related reactions 
occurred in 21% of patients with <1% of patients experiencing Grade 3 -4 events.  
• Observe patients closely for 60 minutes after the first infusion and for 30  minutes after 
subsequent infusions of pertuzumab. If a  significant infusion -related reaction occurs, slow or 
interrupt the  infusion, and administer appropriate medical therapies. Monitor patients  carefully 
until complete resolution of signs and symptoms. Consider  permanent discontinuation in 
patients with severe infusion reactions . 
 
Hypersensitivity Reactions/Anaphylaxis  
• Pertuzumab can cause hypersensitivity reactions, including  anaphylaxis.  
• In CLEOPATRA, the overall frequency of hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis  reactions was 11% in 
pertuzumab -treated patients, with Grade  3 – 4 hypersensitivity reactions and anaphylaxis 
occurring in 2% of  patients.  
• In NeoSphere, TRYPHAENA, BERENICE, and APHINITY,  hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis events 
were consistent with those observed  in CLEOPATRA. In APHINITY, the overall frequency of  
hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis was 5% in the pertuzumab treated  group. The incidence was 
highest in the pertuzumab plus TCH  treated group (8%) with 1% Grade 3 – 4 events.  
• Observe patients closely for hypersensitivity reactions. Severe hypersensitivity, including 
anaphylaxis and fatal events, have been observed in patients treated with pertuzuma b. 
Angioedema has been described in postmarketing  reports. Medications to treat such reactions, 
as well as  emergency equipment, should be available for immediate use prior to  administration 
of pertuzumab. PEBRILZO  is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to  
pertuzumab or to any of its excipients .
