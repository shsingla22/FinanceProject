 c. Types of customers
  PTC Industries Limited serves a diversified customer base comprising global aerospace and defence majors, Indian defence 
establishments and PSUs, as well as leading companies in the oil & gas, LNG, power generation, petrochemicals, pulp & paper, 
shipbuilding, marine and other specialised engineering sectors. Its portfolio includes critical and supercritical components 
such as titanium and superalloy castings, precision-machined aerospace parts, and air-melt industrial castings, supplied to 
customers that operate in high-technology and high-reliability environments. Long-standing relationships in industrial sectors,
