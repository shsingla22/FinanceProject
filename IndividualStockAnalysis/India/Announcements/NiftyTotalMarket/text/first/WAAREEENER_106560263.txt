Waaree Energies Powers Up As The Title Sponsor For  
Rajasthan Royals  
National, March 18, 2026:  In a move linking India’s renewable energy surge 
with its premier sporting property, Waaree Energies  Limited  (BSE code: 
544277; NSE:  WAAREEENER) , a leading global energy transition company, 
joins Rajasthan Royals as the Title Sponsor  for the upcoming T20 league . As 
part of the association, Waaree Energies  will be featured  on the front of the 
Rajasthan Royals’ official playing and training kits for the season.   
 
The partnership between Rajasthan Royals and Waaree Energies brings 
together two brands aligned in their commitment to innovation and 
sustainability, with a shared vision to inspire positive change through sport.   
 
Established in 1990, Waaree Energies  has been at the forefront of driving 
India’s transition towards clean and sustainable energy . With a strong 
presence across domestic and over 20 international markets, the company 
continues to enable businesses and communities to adopt renewable 
energy solutions at scale.  
 
Hitesh Doshi, CMD  - Waaree  said “In Ind ia, cricket is not just a sport it is a 
powerful cultural force that connects and influences millions  of people . Our 
partnership with Rajasthan Royals enables us to take the message of clean 
energy from industry corridors to the hearts of people across the country. 
At Waaree, we believe the energy transition must be inclusive, aspirational, 
and visible to every Ind ian. 
 
This association marks a proud milestone for us. As India advances towards 
global leadership in clean energy, it is critical that this movement extends 
beyond businesses and becomes a people’s movement. Rajasthan  Royals, 
with their strong global following and community -driven ethos, offer s an 
ideal platform to amplify this vision. Together, we  are not just supporting a 
team we are championing a larger purpose of accelerating India’s journey 
towards a greener, more sustainable future”  
 
Alok Chitre, COO, Rajasthan Royals  added, “We are delighted to welcome 
Waaree Energies to the Royals family as our Main Principal Sponsor. This 
partnership is a strong reflection of our philosophy of transforming society 
through cricket and cricket through innovation. Waaree’s leadership in 
 
driving the global energy transition aligns seamlessly with our 
commitment to building a forward -looking, purpose -led franchise. 
Together, we aim to set new benchmarks in how sport, innovation, and 
purpose -led partnerships intersect, while driving meaningf ul impact within 
communities. Through this association, we look forward to harnessing the 
Royals’ global platform to drive visibility, engage fans, and build a legacy 
that extends far beyond the field ” 
 
This partnership accelerates Waaree’s efforts to translate its global expertise 
in solar modules and sustainable energy into tangible grassroots impact 
and sits right at the centre  of Rajasthan Royals’ mission to “Transform 
Society through Cricket, and Cricket through Innovation” . 
 
About Waaree Energies:  
 
Founded in 1990, Waaree Energies Limited is India’s leading renewable 
energy company, driving the global transition to clean power. 
Headquartered in Mumbai, Waaree operates advanced manufacturing 
facilities with a total solar PV module capacity of ~22.3 GW  worldwide and a 
solar cell capacity of up to 5.4 GW. With a strong footprint across India and 
over 25 countries, Waaree offers a comprehensive portfolio spanning solar 
panels & inverters manufacturing, EPC services, green hydrogen solutions, 
battery energ y storage systems, green energy infrastructure, and data 
centres. Committed to sustainability and innovation, Waaree delivers 
cutting -edge, cost -effective solutions to power a greener future.
