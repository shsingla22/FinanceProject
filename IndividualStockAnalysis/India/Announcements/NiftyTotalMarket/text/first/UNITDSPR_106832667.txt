Bengaluru , India - October  28, 2026: United Spirits L td., one of the leading beverage alcohol compan ies 
in India , reported its unaudited  standalone  results  for the first half and second quarter ended 30 
September 2026.  
 
Mr. Praveen Someshwar , CEO & Managing Director , commenting on the Q2FY27 performance, said:   
“We delivered a resilient quarter despite a  strong prior -year base and regulatory headwinds , while 
continuing to invest behind our brands  for long -term growth.  
The relaunch of J&B Scotch , enabled by the India -UK FTA, offers  consumers a compelling  proposition 
that  will help recrui t and deepen Scotch penetration. We also continue to  unlock new  avenues  for 
growth, most recently, entering the Ready -to-Drink (RTD)  segment with Smirnoff Ice to give 
consumers a  convenient new  choice . We look ahead to the festive quarter with confidence .”

 Q2FY27 performance highlights:  
• Net sales  value (NSV)  at INR 3,258 Cr., up 2.8% YoY . Within the above 2.8% growth, 
Prestige & Above segment grew 6.3% despite lapping a high growth quarter in the 
prior year . This was  on the back of Smirnoff local flavor  innovation , J&B  scotch 
relaunch and growth in the luxury portfolio. This was partially offset by the 
continuing impact of the adverse policy in Maharashtra .   
• NSV for the Popular segment at INR 181 Cr., down 22.7% on a like -for-like basis.  This 
was driven by MML impact in Maharashtra as well as recent policy changes in 
Karnataka , which ha ve adversely impacted the realisations  and growth  in the 
segment owing to excise slab changes.  
• Gross profit grew 4.7% and reported gross margin was at 4 8.0%, an expansion of 88 
bps over pr ior year . This was on the back of better mix, and productivity flow -through  
partially offset by the continued adverse impact from the  West Asia crisis .   
• A&P re -investment rate was 11.6% (vs. 7.6% LY) of net sales  value , reflecting 
healthy investment s behind  the brands  and new launches  ahead of the festive 
season.   
• EBITDA at INR6 43 Cr., down 4.3% year -on-year, impacted by the conscious pro -
active A&P spending ahead of season . 
• EBITDA margin at 1 9.7%, a contraction of 146bps over prior year same quarter  due 
to higher investments behind the trademarks.  
• Finance  cost stands at INR 24 Cr for the quarte r and is primarily on account of 
customary non -debt related items .  
• Profit after tax at INR46 2 Cr., dow n 2.1%, with a net profit margin at 14.2%.  
 H1FY27 performance highlights:  
• Net sales value (NSV) at INR5, 961 Cr., up 4.2% YoY . Within the above 4.2% growth, Prestige 
& Above segment grew 7.8%. This was driven by the strong performance of Smirnoff local 
flavour  innovation and the luxury portfolio partly offset by the  regulatory headwinds in 
Maharashtra and disruption in the Exports business .  
• NSV for the Popular segment at INR354 Cr., declined 20. 6% on account of policy related 
headwinds both in the state of Maharashtra, owing to MML , and Karnataka, due to excise 
slab changes.  
• Gross profit grew 7.5% and gross margin was at 4 7.1%. This is on the back of sustained 
revenue growth management interventions leading to headline pricing and mix flow -through  
as well  as ongoing productivity initiatives offset by West Asia crisis impact.  
• A&P re -investment rate was 11.6 % (vs. 8.4%)  of net sales value on back of targeted 
investment behind the key trademarks.  
• Reported EBITDA at INR1, 075 Cr., down 1.1%.   
• EBITDA margin at 1 8.0%, a contraction of 97bps over prior year.  
• Finance  cost stands at INR54 Cr is on account of customary non -debt related items . 
• Profit after tax at INR85 3 Cr., grew 16.8% over prior year , with a net profit margin at 14.3%.
