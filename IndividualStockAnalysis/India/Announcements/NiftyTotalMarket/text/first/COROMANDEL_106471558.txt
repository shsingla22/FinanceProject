We wish to inform you that our Company has received today a letter dated December 18, 2025 (“ Request 
Letter”) from, Algavista Greentech Private Limited (“AGPL”), seeking re-classification from the ‘promoter 
and promoter group’ of our Company pursuant to Regulation 31A of the SEBI Listing Regulations. 
 
The above mentioned request is due to change in the shareholding of E.I.D.-Parry (India) Limited (EID) in 
AGPL from 50% to nil holdings, because of stake sale in AGPL by EID to its Joint Venture partner                                      
(M/s. Synthite Industries Private Limited).AGPL does not fall under the definition of Promoter group 
specified in 2(pp) of SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 and has 
requested their name to be removed from the Company’s Promoter group list. 
 
As on the date of the request letter, AGPL does not hold any shares in the Company: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The above request letter will be placed before the Board of Directors as stipulated under Regulation                   
31A(3) of the SEBI Listing Regulations and post receipt of approval of the Board, we would submit an 
application seeking no-objection from National Stock Exchange of India Limited and BSE Limited.  
 
We kindly request you to take the above submission on record.
