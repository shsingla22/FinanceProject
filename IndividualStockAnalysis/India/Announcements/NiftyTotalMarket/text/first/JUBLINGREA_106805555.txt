 This is in continuation of the Company's disclosure dated August 18, 2026, regarding the execution of a Binding Term Sheet with Zettaone Technologies India Private Limited ("Zettaone") for the acquisition of a 40% strategic equity stake in Zettaone.  
  As previously disclosed, the proposed investment is aligned with the Company's Pinnacle Growth Strategy and is expected to provide a strong platform for strengthening its presence in the Electronics Development and Manufacturing Services (EDMS) sector. The  investment is 
intended to enable the Company to offer an integrated value proposition across the electronics and semiconductor ecosystem including our fast -developing semicon/electronics chemicals 
business, create long -term sustainable value, and leverage  its established manufacturing and 
customer -centric capabilities while deepening customer engagement across the electronics and 
semiconductor value chain.   The Company hereby informs that the Board of Directors, at its meeting held today, has approved the incorporation of a wholly owned subsidiary, Jubilant Advanced Electronics Limited 
("WOS"). The Board has further approved that the proposed acquisition of the 40% strategic equity stake in Zettaone shall be undertaken through the WOS instead of directly b y the 
Company.   The proposed investment through the WOS is in furtherance of the Company's Pinnacle Growth Strategy and is aimed at creating a focused vehicle for pursuing opportunities in the EDMS and allied electronics and semiconductor segments such as  high purity semicon/electronics materials. 
The investment structure is expected to provide greater operational flexibility and strategic focus while enabling the Company to strengthen its presence across the electronics and semiconductor value chain and drive long- term value creation.
