TCS BaNCS for Treasury marks its entry into Sri Lanka with Indian 
Bank  
The integrated treasury platform will help Indian Bank’s Colombo branch strengthen real -time risk 
visibility, improve straight -through processing, and build a scalable foundation for future growth  
COLOMBO | MUMBAI, October 19, 2026 :  Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS),  a 
global leader in IT services, consulting, and business solutions, has announced an expansion of its long -
standing partnership with Indian Bank , to deploy TCS BaNCS for Treasury at its Colombo branch  in Sri 
Lanka , making the entry of TCS BaNCS for Treasury in the country .  
Following the successful deployment of TCS BaNCS for Treasury across Indian Bank's operations in India 
and at GIFT City  in Gujarat, the platform is now being extended to the Bank's Colombo branch, 
reinforcing a unified approach to treasury operations across geographies. With this expansion, Indian 
Bank  marks a new milestone  platform -led transformation with TCS.  
As part of the collaboration, TCS will help Indian Bank modernize its overseas treasury operations across 
money markets, investment management, and foreign exchange.  The transformation will enable Indian 
Bank’s Colombo branch to move to a single, integrated treasury platform that supports unified deal 
processing, real -time position visibility, stronger risk and limit controls, and compliance -ready reporting. 
The Bank will be able to reduce manual intervention, improve straight -through processing, and 
strengthen audit and regulatory readiness through a single source of truth.   
S Viswanathan, CEO, Indian Bank, Sri Lanka Operations , said, “As our treasury operations  in Sri Lanka  
evolve , we need a platform that can support growth with greater control, transparency and operational 
agility.  We are happy that we have chosen TCS BaNCS for Treasury which has already proven its 
capability for India n Bank’s India and G IFT City oper ations . The platform provides  us with a modern 
foundation to address evolving banking requirements . We look forward to working with TCS as we 
continue to build a more resilient and scalable treasury operation.”  
Venkateshwaran Srinivasan, Global Head, Financial Solutions, Tata Consultancy Services, said, 
“Treasury transformation today is about more than automation —it is about enabling banks to operate 
with speed, insight, and confidence in dynamic market environments . With TCS BaNCS  for Treasury, we 
are proud to support Indian Bank’s overseas business in Sri Lanka with a solution that brings together 
deep treasury functionality, operational resilience, and regulatory confidence.  This partnership reflects 
TCS’ commitment to helping financial institutions modernize core operations and build future -ready 
platforms for growth.”  
With seamless deployment and continued maintenance, TCS will help the bank accelerate time -to-value 
while ensuring smooth integration with its existing systems and international market infrastructure.  
For the Bank’s Colombo branch, the platform will provide greater control over foreign exchange and 
market risk exposures, faster deal execution and settlement cycles, and improved regulatory reporting. 
It will also support strategic decision -making by givi ng treasury teams a consolidated view of positions, 
limits, liquidity, and exposures across instruments and currencies.
