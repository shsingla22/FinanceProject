Hindustan Zinc , IIT Madras advance zinc-air battery 
technology for Next -Generation electric mobility  
 1 kWh zinc -air battery integrated as a range extender, marking a 
milestone in research towards potential value- added applications of zinc 
in energy storage 
 Electric three- wheeler prototype demonstrates the potential of zinc- based 
battery technologies in diversifying energy storage solutions for electric 
mobility  
 Industry –academia collaboration advances indigenous battery 
technology development, leveraging India’s abundant zinc resources  
 
CHENNAI  / UDAIPUR , 26th November 2026:  Hindustan Zinc Limited , (BSE: 500188 and NSE: 
HINDZINC), a Vedanta Group company, is the world’s largest integrated zinc producer, 
in collaboration with the Indian Institute of Technology Madras  (IIT Madras) , has 
unveiled an electric three -wheeler prototype integrated with an approximately 1 kWh 
zinc-air battery, developed as a range extender for electric mobility applications.  
Unveiled during the recently held  IIT Madras Energy Summit 2026, the development 
marks an important milestone in the ongoing research collaboration between the two institutions and advances the development of indigenous zinc -based energy storage 
solutions for emerging applications.  
The electric three -wheeler prototype integrated with the 1 kWh zinc -air battery was 
unveiled in the presence of the Guest of Honor Shri Praveen Chakravarthy, Hon’ble 
Member of Parliament (Rajya Sabha), Mr. Amarendu Prakash, CEO & Whole -time 
Director, Hindustan Zinc Limited, Prof. Manu Santhanam, Dean (ICSR) Dr. Aravind Kumar 
Chandiran, Department of Chemical Engineering, IIT Madras, who led this project, 
along with other stakeholders.  
With the accelerating global transition towards clean energy and electric mobility, the 
demand for diversified, affordable and sustainable energy storage solutions is growing.  
The abundance, recyclability and potential cost and safety advantages of zinc make it 
a promising metal for next -generation battery technologies . India’s abundant zinc 
resources present a significant opportunity to strengthen indigenous capabilities in 
energy storage and support the country’s journey towards energy self -reliance.  
The electric three -wheeler prototype features a dual -battery configuration, with the 
zinc-air battery contributing nearly 10% of the vehicle’s total energy requirements. While 
the existing lithium -ion battery supports acceleration and peak power requirements, the 
zinc-air battery provides a steady supply of energy during operation.  
                                                                    
 
 
Sensitivity: Public (C4)  The development demonstrates the potential of zinc -based battery technologies to 
complement existing energy storage systems and opens up  new possibilities for their 
application in electric mobility.  
Speaking on the development, Mr. Amarendu Prakash, CEO &  Whole -time Director, 
Hindustan Zinc Limited, said , “At Hindustan Zinc, we are constantly exploring 
opportunities to expand the applications of zinc and unlock greater value from this 
versatile metal. With zinc being abundantly available in India, there is an opportunity to explore indigenous energy storage solutions that can contribute to the vision of Viksit Bharat. Our collaboration with IIT Madras reflects our commitment to translating 
scientific research into practical applications, creating new demand avenues for zinc 
and strengthening its role in the global clean energy transition.”  
Zinc-air batteries generate electricity by utilising zinc and oxygen from the surrounding 
air. With the use of a non- flammable, water -based electrolyte and an abundantly 
available and recyclable metal, the technology offers potential advantages in safety, affordability and environmental sustainability. The collaboration, initiated in 2024, has 
now progressed from laboratory -scale research to vehicle -level demonstration, marking 
an important milestone in the development of indigenous zinc -air battery technol ogy.  
Congratulating the research team, Prof. V. Kamakoti, Director, IIT Madras, said, “Energy 
is the major focus of discussion globally, be it artificial intelligence, agriculture all the 
way to advanced manufacturing. Ensuring generation, storage and distribution of energy from different sources, specifically the renewable ones is the most important 
topic for research, development and commercialization. Our collaboration with 
Hindustan Zinc is yet another important effort towards this goal.”  
For Hindustan Zinc, the collaboration is part of a broader effort to explore emerging 
applications of zinc and expand its presence in value -added product segments.  
From renewable energy storage to electric mobility, Hindustan Zinc is actively exploring new frontiers for zinc -based battery technologies. As part of its broader growth and 
diversification strategy under HZL 2.0, the company continues to drive application -led 
innovation, unlock new value -added opportunities and strengthen the role of zinc as a 
critical enabler of the global energy transition.   
ABOUT HINDUSTAN ZINC  
Hindustan Zinc Limited (BSE: 500188 and NSE: HINDZINC), a Vedanta Group company, is the 
world’s largest integrated zinc producer and is amongst the top 10 silver producers globally. The

 ABOUT IIT MADRAS  
 
Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IITM) was established in 1959 by the Government of India 
as an ‘Institute of National Importance.’ The activities of the Institute in various fields of Science 
and Technology are carried out in 18 academic departments and several advanced 
interdisciplinary research academic centres. The Institute offers undergraduate and 
postgraduate programmes leading to B.Tech., M.Sc., M.B.A., M.Tech ., M.S., and Ph.D., degrees in 
a variety of specialisations. IITM is a residential institute with more than 650 faculty and 10,000 
students. Students from 18 countries are enrolled here. IITM fosters an active entrepreneurial 
culture with strong curricular support and through the  IITM Incubation Cell.  
Recognized as an Institution of Eminence (IoE) in 2019, IITM has been ranked No.1 in the  ‘Overall’ 
Category  for the Seven consecutive year in India Ranking 2025 released by  National Institutional 
Ranking Framework , Ministry of Education, Govt. of India. The Institute has also been ranked No.1 
in the  ‘Engineering Institutions’ category  in the same Rankings for 10 consecutive years – from 
2016 to 2025.  
In 2023, IIT Madras became the first IIT to establish an international Campus in Zanzibar, Tanzania 
called ‘IIT Madras Zanzibar. ’
