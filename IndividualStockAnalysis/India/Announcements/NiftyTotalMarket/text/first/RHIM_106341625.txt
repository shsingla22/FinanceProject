shareholders. Therefore, the Companies paying dividend are required to withhold tax at the time of payment/ credit of dividen d at the

the shareholder and the documents submitted by them and accepted by the Company.

together with the documentation requirements from them to enable the Company to comply with its  TDS  related statutory
