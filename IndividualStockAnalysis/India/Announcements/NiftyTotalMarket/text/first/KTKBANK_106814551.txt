PRESS RELEASE 
Karnataka Bank Launches "Trade Konnect" -A Digital Platform For Import, Export 
& Outward Remittance Transactions. 
Karnataka Bank announced the launch of Trade Konnect, a digital platform that enables 
customers to submit requests for import, export transactions and outward cross border 
remittances online, anytime, anywhere eliminating the need for physical branch visits. 
The platform was formally launched by Shri Raghavendra S Bhat, Managing Director & 
CEO of Karnataka Bank, through a virtual ceremony held at the Bank1s Head Office in 
Mangaluru. 
Speaking on the occasion Shri Raghavendra S Bhat, Managing Director & CEO of the 
Bank said: "Implementing the digital platform 'Trade Konnect' for online submission of 
forex transaction and cross-border outward remittance requests is a significant step 
towards enhancing customer experience and accelerating the Bank1s digital 
transformation. The platform is designed to support the evolving requirements of 
businesses engaged in international trade and individuals undertaking foreign 
remittances by providing a seamless, secure and efficient digital channel. Customer

occasion.
