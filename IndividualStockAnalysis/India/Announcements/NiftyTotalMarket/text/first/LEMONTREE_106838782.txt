LEMON TREE HOTELS ELEVATES ITS BRAND PORTFOLIO IN 
BENGALURU AND HYDERABAD  
 
Three hotels transition to new brand identities, reflecting the company's focus on 
strengthening portfolio positioning and enhancing guest offerings  
 
New Delhi, 1st November , 2026: Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd., one of India’s leading 
hotel chains, today announced the strategic brand elevation and repositioning of three 
key properties across major technology and business hubs  in Bengaluru and 
Hyderabad.  
 
As part of this exercise , following renovation,  - Lemon Tree Hotel, Electronics City, 
Bengaluru  is rebranded as Lemon Tree Premier, Electronics City, Bengaluru . 
Similarly, Lemon Tree Hotel, Gachibowli, Hyderabad, will transition to Lemon Tree 
Premier, Gachibowli, Hyderabad . Red Fox Hotel, Hitec City, Hyderabad is 
rebranded as Lemon Tree Hotel, Hitec  City, Hyderabad . 
 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, 
Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd. , said, " Our portfolio strategy is guided by a clear 
understanding of the markets we operate in and the expectations of our guests. As we 
continue to grow, it is equally important to ensure that our existing hotels are 
positioned under the brands that best reflect their offerings and the experiences they 
deliver. As India's key IT and commercial hubs continue to evolve, so do the 
expectations of business travellers. Positioning our hotels in Electronic City, Bangalore 
and Gachibowli, Hyderabad under our upscale Lemo n Tree Premier brand, while 
bringing Red Fox Hotel, Hitec  City, Hyderabad under the upper -midscale Lemon Tree 
Hotel brand, allows us to better align our offerings with distinct customer segments. 
We have also made substantial investments in renovating these hotels to ensure that 
their offerings, design and overa ll guest experience are aligned with the respective 
brand identities and desired positioning. This realignment strengthens our presence in 
these important markets and unlocks greater potential from our existing portfolio ." 
 
Lemon Tree Premier, Electronics City, Bengaluru  offers 175 rooms and suites  
with three dining options, including Citrus Café, Slounge and Republic of Noodles. The 
hotel also features conference and meeting facilities, a business centre, a spa, a fitness 
centre and a swimming pool. Located in Electronics City Phase I, it offers convenient 
access to the area’s prominent corporate and industrial hubs.  
 
Lemon Tree Premier, Gachibowli, Hyderabad  features  190 rooms and suites, 
along with Citrus Café, Slounge and Republic of Noodles. Its facilities include 
conference and meeting spaces, a business centre, a spa, a fitness centre and a 
swimming pool. Strategically located in Hyderabad’s Financial District, t he hotel caters 
to the city’s corporate and technology -driven demand.  
 
Lemon Tree Hotel, Hitec  City, Hyderabad  offers 121 rooms and suites. The 
property features Citrus Café, a dedicated conference room, a business centre and a 
fitness centre. Its location provides convenient access to prominent business parks 
and IT companies, making it well suited to business tr avellers visiting the city.  
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
The rebranding is part of Lemon Tree Hotels' continued focus on building a clearly 
differentiated brand portfolio, with each property positioned to reflect its product 
offering, service proposition and target guest segment.  
 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED   
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India's leading hospitality companies, catering 
to a wide range of customers – from value -conscious travellers to premium business and 
leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels & Resorts, Lemon T ree Premier, 
Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys Select, and Keys Lite – the group offers 
experiences across upper upscale, upscale, upper midscale, midscale, leisure, wildlife, and 
spiritual segments.  
LTHL operates 130+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing pipeline 
of 140+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and 
Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, and Indore – and with an 
international presence in Bhutan and Nepal – Lemon Tree Hotels delivers exceptional 
comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, refreshing experience.  
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 270+ properties 
(operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for both business and 
leisure travellers.
