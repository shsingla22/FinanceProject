Quarterly Update on Business (Q 2 FY2 7) 
This document provides a preliminary update on the standalone performance, highlighting the key trends and 
operational developments during the quarter that ended on September 30, 202 6. This will be later followed by a 
detailed disclosure of financial results and earnings presentation once the board approves the financial results for the 
quarter.  
During the quarter, AWL delivered 12% YoY volume growth and 24% YoY revenue growth, led 
by strong momentum in the Food & FMCG portfolio and Industry Essentials, while the Edible Oil 
business delivered steady volume growth.  
 
Food & FMCG  
Food & FMCG delivered 33% YoY revenue growth during the quarter, driven by broad -based 
growth across categories. The segment crossed ₹2,000 crore of quarterly revenue for the first 
time, marking its highest -ever quarterly revenue . 
 
Wheat and Rice businesses delivered robust growth  during the quarter . Addit ionally , categories 
such as Besan, Pulses, Soya Nuggets, Sugar, Sattu and Poha, among others, now contribute more 
than 35% to the Food portfolio and delivered around 50% YoY revenue growth during the 
quarter .  
 
Pulses remain an important focus category for AWL, given its large addressable market, high 
frequency of consumption and strong adjacency with the Company's existing Daily Essentials 
portfolio. Fortune Pulses has scaled rapidly, with quarterly revenue crossing ₹100 crore, and the 
Company is accelerating distribution across General Trade and Quick Commerce to build the 
category further.  
 
Edible Oil  
The Edible Oil business recorded 3% YoY volume growth during the quarter , led by healthy 
performance in domestic oils, particularly Mustard Oil and Rice Bran Oil.

Industry Essentials  
Industry Essentials recorded 25% YoY volume growth during the quarter, driven by broad -based 
growth across Oleochemicals, Castor and De -oiled Cakes.  
 
Oleochemicals & Specialty Chemicals continues to remain a key focus area within the Industry 
Essentials portfolio, supported by AWL's integrated manufacturing capabilities and growing 
presence across value -added applications.  
 
Alternate Channels  
Alternate Channels (comprising E -Commerce, Quick Commerce and Modern Trade ) delivered 
double -digit growth during the quarter.  
 
Within Food & FMCG, key categories continued to gain traction across E -Commerce and Quick 
Commerce, collectively delivering 40% YoY growth. AWL has established a meaningful presence 
across these channels, with average market shares of 1 0%+ in Atta, 25% + in Besan, 40% + in Soya 
Nuggets, and around 15 –20% across Sugar, Suji and Poha . 
 
Further, e xecution metrics  such as product fill rate  and customer retention  improved , supported 
by better assortment and availability . 
 
Other Channels  
Branded Exports maintained strong momentum with 59% YoY volume growth, while HoReCa 
continued to scale well with 21% YoY growth during the quarter.

AWL Agri Business Ltd. (formerly Adani Wilmar Limited) is one of India’s largest Food & FMCG 
companies, offering a diverse portfolio of essential kitchen staples, including edible oils, wheat 
flour, rice, pulses, and sugar. AWL through its brands, including flagship brand  - Fortune, 
commands the trust of more than 135 million households, reaching to every 1 in 3 Indian families. 
With 24 manufacturing facilities across 11 states, including India’s largest single -location refinery 
in Mundra (5,000 tons per day capacity), AWL ensures seamless production and distribution  
across the country . Its extensive supply chain, supported by 110+ stock points, over 10,000 
distributors and sub -distributors, along with a retail network of 2. 6 million outlets, guarantees 
widespread accessibility across urban and rural India.  
Beyond serving retail consumers, AWL also caters the HoReCa and institutional consumers , with 
staple food products, specialized bakery and Lauric fats, Castor Oil derivatives, Oleochemicals, 
and value -added Soya products. Its advanced castor oil facilities produce pharmaceutical -grade, 
and low -moisture castor oil, catering to global markets.  Additionally, AWL has expanded into the 
Home & Personal Care (HPC) segment, offering soaps, handwashes, and multipurpose cleaners, 
further strengthening its product offerings.
