KhushTech Korea to Launch India’s First D2M Feature Phone Under FreeStream Brand   
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Seoul, New Delhi and Baltimore, MD – August 7, 2025 - KhushTech Korea Co., Ltd. (“KhushTech”), 
is proud to introduce the world’s first Direct -to-Mobile (D2M) feature phone for Free Stream 
Technologies (“FreeStream”), a D2M technology and platform provider incubated at Indian Institute 
of Technology (IIT), Kan pur.   
The innovative and low -cost FreeStream branded phone is powered by Tejas Networks SL -3000 D2M 
demodulator chip embedded in the device and is intended for use in the 470 -608 MHz broadcast 
bands.   
In a recent high -level meeting with Shri Nara Lokesh, Hon’ble IT and Electronics Minister of Andhra 
Pradesh, KhushTech’s Founder and CEO, Eric Shin, showcased the groundbreaking FreeStream D2M 
feature phone and demonstrated its incredible potential to tran sform educational content delivery 
in rural areas and other challenging environments in a cost -effective and resilient manner by 
leveraging the scalable architecture of the next -generation ATSC 3.0 broadcast standard.   
Direct -to-Mobile is a broadcast technology that uses terrestrial TV broadcast airwaves to deliver IP 
based live TV, Video, Audio, data and text messages (multimedia content) of public and national 
importance (such as entertainment, sports, educational prog ramming and emergency alerts) directly 
to phones without the need for Wi -Fi or internet service. In addition to the SL -3000 chipset, Tejas 
Networks has also developed a suite of infrastructure hardware and software products for D2M 
deployment in collaborat ion with Sinclair Inc, USA.   This technology has been under test in live 
networks by Prasar Bharati in partnership with IIT Kanpur and Tejas Networks over the past couple 
of years.   
Sumeet Nindrajog, Director of Free Stream Technologies said, “We are pleased that KhushTech has 
rapidly developed a low -cost device that will appeal to hundreds of millions of users both in India 
and across the world. FreeStream looks forward to supporting  and participating in D2M service 
deployment for Digital Bharat.”   
Eric Shin, Founder and CEO of KhushTech Korea said, “We are very delighted to have successfully 
developed the world’s first D2M Feature Phone. Our low -cost device will be India’s first BIS Certified 
D2M Feature Phone and is ready for commercialization. We would like to thank our strategic partners 
Tejas Networks, FreeStream, and Sinclair for their full support in development over the past year and 

a half. We are keen to set up a world -class manufacturing facility in Andhra Pradesh to support 
Government of India’s “Make in India” initiative and will continue to invest in the development of 
various D2M embedded products.”    
Parag Naik, Executive Vice President, Tejas Networks, said, “Our team has worked hard with 
KhushTech and made clever optimizations to the demodulator, the operating system and middleware 
to work within the resource constraints of a feature phone environmen t. Our end -to-end 
productization of D2M allows us to rapidly tweak, test and validate performance.”   
Chris Ripley, President and CEO, Sinclair, Inc., said, “We applaud KhushTech’s commitment to 
manufacturing affordable devices that enable large scale adoption of “mobile first” ATSC 3.0.   We are 
leading the work towards the next release of the standard - “Broadcast to Everything” (B2X) which 
will unleash next generation broadcast/multicast apps for numerous verticals of Digital Bharat.”
