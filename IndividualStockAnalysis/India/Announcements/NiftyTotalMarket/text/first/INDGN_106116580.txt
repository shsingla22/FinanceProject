Indegene Wins 2025 BIG Innovation Award for Hyper -Automated AI Solution  
Bengaluru, India, January  17, 2025 : Indegene, a digital -first life sciences commercialization company, 
announced  that it is a winner at the 2025 BIG Innovation Awards . The company’s  ground -breaking 
solution, Hyper -automated AI Ecosystem for Accelerated Insights Generation , was recognized as the 
winner in the ‘ Product - Business Services ’ category.  
 
Hyper -automated AI  is a unified framework combining AI, ML, RPA, and NLQ to streamline data 
extraction, quality management, transformation, and actionable insights  - all on a single platform.  
 
An initiative  by the Business Intelligence Group, the BIG Innovation Awards identify and recognize 
organizations devoted to bringing new ideas to life  - hono uring those dedicated to paving a new way 
for products, services, experiences, and community service.  This recognition  underscores Indegene’s  
commitment to pushing boundaries, solving real- world challenges, and delivering impactful solutions to 
customers and communities.  The win is also a testimonial of Indegene’s continued efforts to help 
healthcare organizations be future -ready.  
 Indegene combine s deep healthcare expertise with a digital- first approach to help pharma, biotech and 
medical device companies commercialize their products. It is one of the very few companies globally 
where a medical doctor and a data scientist work next to each other to solve big, impactful problems  in 
life sciences . And this is by design. More than 20% of Indegene’s  employees come from a medical 
education background and they work very closely with about 600 technologists who work on advanced artificial intelligence ( AI), machine learning ( ML) and other technologies.   
 With 6 operation hubs and 18 offices across North America, Europe, and Asia, Indegene’s client base 
includes the world’s top 20 biopharma companies, many emerging biotech firms, and medical device companies. With over 5,000 employees worldwide, Indegene ’s vast global footprint underscores it s 
ability to scale and meet the evolving needs of the world’s leading pharma organizations  across markets .  
 
About Indegene

diverse range of solutions. These aim to deliver, amongst other outcomes, a personalized,
