Cocoblu Retail Limited Earns 2025 -26 Great Place To Work Certification ™ 
New Delhi , 22nd April 2025 : Cocoblu  Retail , a wholly owned subsidiary of RattanIndia Enterprises , 
is proud to announce that it has been Certified ™ by Great Place To Work®  for the year 2025 -26. 
This prestigious recognition is based entirely on real -time feedback from current employees 
about their workplace experience.  
Great Place To Work®  is the global authority on workplace culture, employee experience, and 
leadership behavio urs that drive market -leading results, high employee retention, and 
innovation.  With its roots in the USA, Great Place To Work® has been working with over 5,500 
organizations representing more than 10 million employees for over 30 years. The Great Place 
To Work Certification ™ is the only recognition based entirely on what employees report about 
their workplace experience —particularly how consistently they experience a high -trust 
environment. Every year, more than 10,000 companies across 60 countries apply for this globally 
benchmarked recognition, making it one of the most definitive "employer -of-choice" awards.  
“We’re absolutely delighted to receive this certification! Thank you for recognizing Cocoblu as a Great 
Place to Work – it’s a proud moment for all of us here. This is a testimony to the solid effort put in building 
a culture of transparency, meritocracy,  and performance.”  — Mrs. Anjali Rattan Nashier, 
Chairperson, RattanIndia Enterprises Limited.  
"Great Place To Work Certification is a highly coveted achievement that requires consistent and intentional 
dedication to the overall employee experience,"  says Sarah Lewis -Kulin, Vice President of Global 
Recognition at Great Place To Work.  “By successfully earning this recognition, it is evident that Cocoblu 
stands out as one of the top companies to work for, providing a great workplace environment for its 
employees. " 
At Cocoblu, our people come first. We actively listen to our teams through Cocopulse , our daily 
one-question survey, and implement actions based on feedback every quarter.  Growth is deeply 
embedded in our culture , with clearly defined career paths, a Talent Harvest Program  promoting 
internal mobility, and a merit -based performance framework that ensures fairness and 
transparency.  
Our Cocoblu Compass Values —14 Leadership Principles —guide everything from hiring to 
development, fostering collaboration and excellence. Impact is celebrated through a dynamic 
Rewards & Recognition  program, which includes real -time appreciations , and quarterly and 
annual awards that highlight outstanding contributions.  
We are equally committed to holistic well -being, offering a comprehensive benefits package that 
supports physical, mental, and family health. This includes training reimbursements, daycare 
support, and flexible allowances.  Our Engagement Committee  plays a key role in building 
workplace connection, curating year -round events and celebrations that bring energy and joy to 
our teams.  
   
According to  Great Place To Work ’s global research , employees at Certified™ workplaces are 4.5 
times more likely to find a great boss , 93% more likely to look forward to coming to work , and 
significantly more likely to be paid fairly and have equal opportunities  for growth and 
advancement.

About RattanIndia Enterprises Limited  
RattanIndia Enterprises Limited (REL) is the flagship company of the RattanIndia Group, focused 
on building and scaling new -age, technology -led businesses in India. A public -listed company
