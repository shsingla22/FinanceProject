Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that the Board of Directors 
of Vodafone Idea Limited (‘the Company’)  has today taken note of / approved  the following : 
 
1. Accepted the request of Mr. Ravinder Takkar  (DIN: 01719511)  to step down as the

Director, as the Non -Executive Chairman of the Board  of Directors of Vodafone Idea Limited  
with effect from  5th May 2026.  
3. Approved the appointment of Mr. Ravinder Takkar (DIN: 01719511) , a Non -Executive 
Director, as the Non-Executive Vice Chairman of the Board of Directors of Vodafone Idea 
Limited  with effect from 5th May 2026.
