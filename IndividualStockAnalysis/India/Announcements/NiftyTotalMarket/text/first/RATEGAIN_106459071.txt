Noida, 2nd December 2025:  RateGain Travel Technologies Limited (RateGain ), a leading global provider of AI -
powered SaaS solutions for travel and hospitality , today announced that it has been recognized by Great Place to 
Work® India among the Top 100 India’s Best Workplaces ™ in IT & IT -BPM 2025. This marks RateGain’s second 
consecutive inclusion in this prestigious list.  
 
RateGain’s recognition in 2025 builds on a six -year legacy of being Great Place to Work® certified, reflecting the 
consistency of its people practices and employee experience over time. In addition to the IT & IT -BPM honor, RateGain 
has also been recognize d twice among India’s Top 100 Great Places to Work® reinforcing its position as an 
organization that prioritizes culture not as an initiative, but as a long -term commitment. The milestone also builds on 
RateGain’s recent Great Place to Work® certifications  in Spain and the USA, underscoring the organization’s 
commitment to creating a consistent, empowering, and inclusive employee experience across its offices worldwide.  
 
At the heart of RateGain’s workplace philosophy are its core values of People First and Innovation, supported by 
Teamwork, Ownership, and Customer Obsession. The company continues to invest in an environment where 
employees feel empowered to experiment, co llaborate, and grow enabling them to bring their best selves to work 
while building solutions that shape the future of travel and hospitality.  
 
“Our people are the foundation of everything we do,” said Bhanu Chopra, Founder and Managing Director of 
RateGain Travel Technologies . “Being recognized again among India’s Best Workplaces ™ in IT & IT -BPM is a strong 
validation of the culture we are intentionally building, one where every individual feels trusted, supported, and 
inspired to push boundaries. AI and I is our way of saying that the future is co -created by technology and people,  
together.”  
 
Sahil Sharma, Global Chief Human Resources Officer at RateGain Travel Technologies, added , “This second 
consecutive recognition in India, along with our recent Great Place to Work® certifications in Spain and the USA, 
reflects the consistency of our people practices across geographies. We are committed to fostering a workplace where 
innovation  thrives because employees feel included, empowered, and connected to a shared purpose.”
