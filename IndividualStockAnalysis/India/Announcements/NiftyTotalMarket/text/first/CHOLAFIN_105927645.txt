Press Release 
 
Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limite d appoints Mr. Ravindra Kumar Kundu as 
the Managing Director: 
The Board of Directors at their meeting held this e vening have appointed Mr. Ravindra Kumar Kundu 
as the Managing Director of the Company.  
The appointment is for a period of five years effec tive 7th  October 2024. 
Mr. Kundu has been the Executive Director of the Co mpany since January 2020. Under his leadership, 
the Assets Under Management grew from Rs.67,000 cro res in FY20 to Rs.1,53,000 crores in FY24 and 
profit before tax grew from Rs.1,500 crores in FY20  to Rs.4,500 crores in FY24. 
Mr. Kundu has been with the Company for 24 years an d has an overall experience of 36 years in 
automobile and financial services industry. Mr. Kun du has held leadership roles heading various 
functions including credit, collections and busines ses.  
Mr. Kundu is a Graduate in Commerce and has complet ed Post Graduate Programme in Management 
for Senior Executives from the Kellogg School of Ma nagement, Indian School of Business and an 
Executive Programme in Global Business Management f rom the Indian Institute of Management 
Calcutta.
