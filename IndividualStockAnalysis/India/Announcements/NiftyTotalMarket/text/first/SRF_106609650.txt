 Dear Sirs,  
Subject: Capital Expenditure proposal approved by the Board  
In continuation of our earlier intimation dated 23 July 2025, wherein the Company had informed about the 
proposal to set up a manufacturing facility for BOPP Film at Indore, Madhya Pradesh, at a projected capital 
outlay of ₹490 crore, we wish to inform you  that the Board of Directors, at its meeting held today, has approved 
an indefinite delay of the said capital expenditure proposal.  
 
The decision has been taken in view of material  evaluation in the operating environment for BOPP films, which 
necessitates a reassessment of the timing of this investment.  
 
This decision reflects a prudent and market -responsive approach to capital allocation, balancing near -term 
demand conditions with the Company’s long -term strategic objectives. The Company continues to remain 
well-positioned for sustainable growth, supporte d by its focus on diversification into new substrates, investments 
in downstream assets, and selective capacity expansion in higher -value segments.  
 
We shall inform the stock exchange on further developments in due course of time.  
 
This is for your information and records.
