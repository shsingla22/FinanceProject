Product PortfolioZydus Wellness combines the best healthcare, nutrition and cosmeceutical 
products to provide quality wellness products. Zydus Wellness helps people pursue 
integrated well-being through its widening range of products. 
The Company is developing, producing, marketing and distributing differentiated 
health and wellness products.
Zydus Wellness has a wide range of health and wellness products. The Company’s product 
portfolio includes popular brands with functional benefits like Glucon-D, Complan, Sugar 
Free, Nycil, Everyuth and Nutralite.
