PRESS RELEASE 
Fiem Industries opened New Office in Gurugram:  
R&D-Electronics, Innovation Centre & Corporate Office  
Fiem Industries Limited has announced the opening of New Office in Gurugram, housing its 
R&D-Electronics, Innovation Centre & Corporate Office at one place.  
New Office Address is:
R&D-Electronics, Innovation Centre & Corporate Office 
2nd Floor, Tower A, Plot No. 243 
SP Infocity, Udyog Vihar Phase-1 
Gurugram, Haryana - 122016, India 
Mr J.K. Jain, Chairman & Managing Director,  shared that this strategic move brings together 
core management functions of the Company under one roof, fostering enhanced 
collaboration and efficiency. Opening of this new office is a major step in our commitment to 
driving agility and innovation across our team and streamline communication, expedite 
decision-making, and cultivate a unified organizational culture.  
He said that by this step, we aim to achieve following Key Benefits: 
Improved Synergy:  Facilitates seamless cross-functional coordination, enabling faster 
and more effective collaboration. 
Enhanced Efficiency:  Accelerates decision-making processes and problem-solving 
capabilities. 
Culture of Unity:  Promotes a shared workspace that reinforces collective success and 
organizational cohesion. 
He further added that this move will strengthen our capabilities to better serve our Original 
Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) in the two-wheeler and four-wheeler segments.
