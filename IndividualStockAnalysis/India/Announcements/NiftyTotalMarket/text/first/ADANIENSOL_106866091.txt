Media Release  
 
Adani Energy Solutions wins Bihar transmission project  
Editor’s Synopsis  
• Project to evacuate 2,400 MW of power from Adani Power’s upcoming Pirpainti Ultra 
Super Critical T hermal Power Plant and strengthen Bihar’s transmission network  
• Project adds 881 ckm of transmission lines and 3,160 MVA of transformation capacity 
to AESL's transmission portfolio  
Ahmedabad,  19 November 2026: Adani Energy Solutions Limited (AESL), India’s leading 
private sector power transmission and distribution company, today said it has won a 
transmission project  for evacuat ion of power from the upcoming 2400 MW p ower plant in 
Pirpainti, Bihar . The project, won through the Tariff Based Competitive Bidding (TBC B) 
mechanism , will provide annual tariff of Rs ~Rs 500 cr once commissioned.  
The project , titled, “ Evacuation of Power from 3X800 MW STPP Pirpainti ”, will be implemented through 
the Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) Vikramshila Power Transmission Ltd. and will be delivered 
by the last quarter of 2028. Besides evacuation of power from the Pirpainti Power Plant , the 
project will strengthen the state’s transmission network and enhance supply reliability.  
“The Pirpainti  transmission scheme will create a robust evacuation system for 2,400 MW of 
generation capacity while strengthening Bihar’s transmission backbone. It will enhance grid 
reliability and resilience and help the state meet its growing power demand. We remain 
committed to building reliable and efficient transmission infrastructure that supports the 
country’s long -term energy security ,” said Mr. Kandarp Patel, CEO,  Adani Energy Solutions .  
Upon completion, the project will add 881 circuit kilometres (ckm ) of transmission lines and 
3,160 MVA of transformation capacity to AESL’s portfolio , taking it to 30,683 ckm  and 146,585 
MVA.  With this project, AESL’s transmission capex now stands at ~Rs 91,500 crore.  
The project comprises construction of four Gas Insulated Substations ( GIS) at Jhanjharpur, 
Bethauli and Begusarai, along with associated transformation capacity. The transmission 
infrastructure will include the 400 kV DCDS Pirpainti -Jhanjharpur, Pirpainti -Begusarai and 
Begusarai -Bakhtiyarpur  lines, as well as the 220 kV DCDS Jhanjharpur -Bethauli and Begusarai -
Begusarai Town lines. It will also involve LILO of existing 220 kV and 132 kV transmission lines 
at Jhanjharpur and construction of 400 kV line bays at Bakhtiyarpur GSS.
