Amsterdam , the Netherlands , 26 October  2026 – With reference to the voluntary totalitarian tender 
offer (the " Offer ") promoted  by TML CV Holdings Pte. Ltd.  ("TML CV HS "), through its wholly -owned  
company TML CV Holdings B.V. (the " Offeror "), pursuant to Articles 102 et seq. of the Italian 
Legislative Decree no. 58 of 24 February 1998, as further amended and supplemented (the " CFA"), 
on all the common  shares (the " Common Shares ") of Iveco Group N.V.  ("IVG" or the "Issuer "), 
whose acceptance period, which commenced on 7 September 2026, closed on the date hereof, the 
Offeror hereby announces the following . 
Capitalised terms used in this press release, unless otherwise defined, shall have the meanings 
ascribed to them in the offer document relating to the Offer, approved by CONSOB by resolution No. 
24119 of 3 September 2026 and published on 4 Sept ember 2026 (the " Offer Document "), as well 
as in the Amendment Notice  (as defined below), both available, inter alia , on the Issuer 's website 
(www.ivecogroup.com ) and on the Offeror's website ( www.tatamotors.com ). 
It is recalled that the Offer was amended, pursuant to Articles 36 and 43 of the Issuers ' Regulation, 
on 9 October 2026 by means of an increase of the Price  through the recognition of an additional 
cash component equal to EUR  0.30 for each Common Share tendered to the Offer, as announced 
to the market by means of a press release published on the same date (the "Amendment Notice "). 
1.Provisional results of the Offer
Based on the provisional results communicated by  BNP Paribas,  Italian Branch,  acting as  
Intermediar y Responsible for  Coordinat ing the Collection of the Acceptances, as of the date hereof , 
No. 238,157,212 Common S hares have been tendered in acceptance to the Offer, representing 
approximately 87.81 % of the Issuer 's common share capital and of the Offer  Shares  (i.e., No. 
271,215,400) , for an aggregate  countervalue (calculated on the basis of the Price, as amended by 
the Amendment Notice ) equal to EUR  3,429,463,852.80. 
In light of the above, should the provisional results of the Offer be confirmed , taking into account  (i) 
the No. 238,157,212  Common  Shares tendered to the Offer during the Acceptance Period (equal to  Annexure 
 
2 approximately  87.81% of the Issuer 's common share capital), (ii) the No. 4,868,597  Treasury Shares  
(equal to approximately 1.80% of the Issuer 's common share capital), and (iii) that in the period 
between the Date of the Offer Document (i.e., 4 September 2026) and the date  hereof,  the Offeror 
has not purchased Common  Shares outside the Offer , the Offeror will hold No. 238,157,212 Common  
Shares, equal to approximately 89.42% of the Issuer 's common share capital , net of Treasury 
Shares .  
The final results of the Offer will be announced in a separate press release pursuant to Article 41, 
paragraph 6, of the Issuers' Regulation, which will be issued by the Offeror by 7:29 a.m. ( CET) on 
the Trading Day prior to the Payment Date ( i.e., by 7:29 a.m. ( CET) on 29 October 2026)  (the "Press 
Release on Final Offer Results ").  
2. Price and Payment Date  
The Price for each Common Share tendered in acceptance of the Offer consists of the initial Price 
indicated in the Offer Document equal to EUR 14.10 ( cum dividend ), increased by the Additional 
Price  of EUR 0.30 ( cum dividend ), and therefore is equal to an aggregate amount of EUR 14.40 
(cum dividend ) per Common Share  (the " Price "). 
Without prejudice to the Conditions to the Offer,  the payment of the Price  with respect to the Common 
Shares tendered to the Offer during the Acceptance Period  – against the simultaneous transfer to 
the Offeror of the ownership of the Common Shares  tendered to the Offer  – will take place on 30 
October  2026. 
3. Threshold Condition  and other Conditions to the Offer   
As indicated in the Offer Document, the effectiveness of the Offer is subject, inter alia , to the condition 
that the acceptances of the Offer – together with (i) any Common Shares directly or indirectly held 
by the Offeror and any of its Affiliates , (ii) any Common Shares committed to the Offeror or any of its 
Affiliates in writing , and (iii) any Common Shares to which the Offeror or any of its Affiliates is entitled 
(i.e., shares sold but not yet transferred) – represent at least an aggregate shareholding of 95% of 
the issued and outstanding share capital  of the Issuer  represented by Common Shares ( geplaatst 
en uitstaand gewoon aandelenkapitaal ) on a fully diluted basis (in any case not taking into account 
the Treasury Shares) . Such percentage has been automatically adjusted to 80% of the Issuer's 
issued and outstanding share capital represented by Common Shares ( geplaatst en uitstaand 
gewoon aandelenkapitaal ) calculated on a fully diluted basis (in any case not taking into account the 
Treasury Shares) since the Back -End Resolution was adopted by the EGM on 16 October 2026 and 
remains in full force and effect (the " Threshold Condition "). 
Based on the provisional  results of the Offer  (if confirmed ), taking into account  the acceptances of 
the Offer recorded as of the date hereof , the Threshold Condition has been satisfied.  
With regard to the remaining Conditions to the Offer, as indicated in the Offer Document, the Offeror 
will announce the fulfilment or non -fulfilment of these Conditions to the Offer and, if they have not 
been fulfilled, any decision to waive them, by 7:29 a.m. ( CET) on the Trading Day prior to the 
Payment Date  (i.e., by 7:29 a.m. ( CET) on 29 October 2026 ), in the Press Release on Final Offer 
Results . 
4. Reopening of the Terms  
Should the provisional results be confirmed, and without prejudice to the remaining Conditions to the 
Offer, pursuant to Article 40 -bis, paragraph 1, letter a) , of the Issuers' Regulation, the Acceptance 
Period will be reopened for 5  Trading Days starting from the Trading Day following the Payment 
Date , and, therefore, for the sessions on 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 November 2026  (included), from 8 :30 a.m. 
(CET) to 5:30 p.m. (CET) (the " Reopening of the Terms ").
