In terms of SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we have 
to inform you that  as part of  RBI Inspection for Supervisory Evaluation (ISE) 2026, RBI 
observed that the Bank had included the Specific Provision attributed to Standard Assets with 
probable stress in particular assets/segments amounting to Rs.3823 Crore as part of General 
Provisions and Loss Reserves (GPLR) and incorrectly reckoned  an amount of Rs.2470 Crore 
as part of Bank’s Tier 2 Capital.  It was f urther  observed that the Bank did not apply Credit 
Conversion Factor (CCF) to undrawn exposures on accounts with Unconditional Cancella bility 
Clause (UCC) resulting in under estimation of Credit RWA by Rs.22,079 Crore, on the premise 
that Unconditional Cancellability Clause  (UCC)  was included in respective sanction letters and 
was duly acknowledged by the respective borrowers.
