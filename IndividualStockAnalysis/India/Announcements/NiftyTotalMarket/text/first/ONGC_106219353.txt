The Management of the Company is responsible to ensure compliance with the 
requirements of the Act and the Rules including MCA Circulars and Listing Regulations 
relating to postal ballot by voting through remote e -voting for the resolutions contained in 
the Notice of the Postal Ballot dated April 2, 2025. My responsibility as a Scrutinizer is to 
ensure that postal ballot by voting through remote e -voting is carried out in a fair and 
transparent manner and to provide scrutinizer's report on the votes cast " IN FAVOUR ", 
"AGAINST ' and INVALID VOTE , if any, on the resolutions contained in the Notice of Postal 
Ballot dated April 2, 2025.

2.Physical copies of the Postal Ballot Notice, postal ballot forms and pre -paid business 
reply envelopes were not sent to the Members for this Postal Ballot due to exemption 
provided by the MCA.

-voting facility to all its members to cast their votes

4.The Company issued Newspaper advertisement son April 3, 2025 in Indian Express 
(English) and Jansat ta(Hindi) , to facilitate those Members who had not yet registered 
their e -mail address so that they could receive the Notice of Postal Ballot and also 
participate in the e -voting.
5.In accordance with the Notice of Postal Ballot sent to the members and the 
Advertisements  published pursuant to Rule 20 of the Companies (Management and 
Administration) Rules, 2014, the remote e -voting period was commenced from 10:00 
am IST on Thursday, the 3rd April, 2025 and ended at 05:00 pm IST on Friday, the 
2nd May, 2025 .
6.Members holding shares as on Friday, the March 28, 2025 , (cut- off date ), were 
entitled to vote on the resolutions as proposed in the Notice of Postal Ballot. The 
detailed manner of voting was provided in the instructions given in the Notice of  Postal 
Ballot.  Please note that one equity share of the Company represent one vote. Voting 
right was in proportion to its shareholding  in paid up share capital of the Company and 
fraction was rounded off on higher side for calculation .
7.After the conclusion of the voting at 5.00 PM ( IST) on Friday, the 2nd May, 2025 , the 
votes on remote e -voting were unblocked on the e -voting site of NSDL, in the presence 
of two witnesses who were not employees of the Company and the e -voting results/list
