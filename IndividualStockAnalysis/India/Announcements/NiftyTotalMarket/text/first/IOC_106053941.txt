Press Statement 
Immediate Release 
11 November 2024: Vadodara: Today, a fire was reported at around 3:30 PM 
in a Benzene Storage Tank (1000 KL capacity) at Gujarat Refinery. The 
refinery’s emergency response team is actively tackling the situation, with 
firefighting operations currently underway. The adjacent water sprinkler 
system has been activated to contain the fire, and dousing efforts are 
ongoing. The cause of the fire is yet to be ascertained. 
Safety of our employees and surrounding communities remains our utmost 
priority. The refinery operations are normal. Further updates will be provided 
as the situation develops.
