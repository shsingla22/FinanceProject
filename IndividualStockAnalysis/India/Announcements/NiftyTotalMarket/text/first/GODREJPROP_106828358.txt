Press Release 
Pune, October 26, 2026 
 
Godrej Properties unveils the Pune edition of The Elevated Living Edit  
Study finds wellness, nature and community now define luxury for Pune’s affluent homebuyers; 
report launched at Shubh Aarambh at Godrej Hill Retreat, with actor Mrunal Thakur 
Key Highlights: 
 74% of Pune’s affluent homebuyers prioritise wellness amenities; 78% seek dedicated 
yoga and meditation spaces 
 76% prefer large balconies; 72% want greater access to green spaces 
 68% value community-centric living; 67% prioritise recreational amenities 
 64% prioritise sustainable features; 60% seek smart-home technology 
 79% would invest in Pune’s residential market again 
Godrej Properties Limited (GPL; BSE scrip ID: GODREJPROP), one of India’s leading real estate 
developers, today unveiled the Pune edition of The Elevated Living Edit , a city-specific 
research study of the aspirations and homebuying priorities of affluent consumers.  
The findings suggest luxury in Pune is defined less by size and address than by how a home lets 
buyers live. Wellness is the strongest theme: dedicated yoga and meditation spaces are the 
most sought-after feature (78%), and 74% of respondents prioritise wellness amenities more 
broadly. Access to nature, through large balconies and green spaces, sits close behind, while the 
demand for community and recreation shows buyers want homes that connect them to their 
neighbours. Sustainability and smart-home technology round out the picture, as buyers look 
for future-ready homes. With 79% saying they would invest in Pune again, confidence in the 
city’s long-term growth is strong. 
The report was launched at Godrej Hill Retreat, Mahalunge, Pune, during Shubh Aarambh , 
GPL’s signature home-handover experience, where homeowners, industry stakeholders and 
guests celebrated a new chapter in community living. The event featured a fireside 
conversation between Mayank Poddar, CEO – West-East Zone, Godrej Properties, and actor 
Mrunal Thakur , moderated by Sujit Patil, Chief Communications Officer, Godrej Industries , on 
how wellness, lifestyle and community are shaping modern homeownership. 
Commenting on the findings, Mayank Poddar, CEO – West-East Zone, Godrej Properties , said, 
“Pune continues to be one of India’s most dynamic residential markets, and homebuyers here 
increasingly want experiences that enhance their quality of life. The Elevated Living Edit reflects a 
significant evolution in what homebuyers value today. In Pune, luxury is now defined by wellness, 
meaningful community experiences, access to nature, sustainability and thoughtful design. These 
insights mirror what we are witnessing on the ground and inform how we create future-ready 
communities.”  
Pune remains one of GPL’s most important markets. As GPL scales up deliveries in the city, it 
continues to focus on design, build quality and customer experience. Mayank Poddar added, 
“We are on track to deliver over 4,000 homes in the city by the end of FY27, reinforcing our 
commitment to both execution excellence and customer satisfaction.”  
 
Actor Mrunal Thakur said , “It was a privilege to be part of Shubh Aarambh at Godrej Hill Retreat 
and celebrate with families as they begin a new chapter. Launching ‘The Elevated Living Edit’ was 
equally meaningful, especially learning that 78% of Pune’s homebuyers value dedicated yoga and 
meditation spaces. It reinforces how wellness is becoming central to modern living. It’s inspiring to 
see Godrej Properties on track to deliver 4,000 homes in Pune by March 2027.”  
In line with the report’s findings, Godrej Properties also unveiled the 1,400 sq m Eco-Resort-
themed clubhouse at Godrej Hill Retreat, Pune’s first resort-style development. The clubhouse 
has four zones (the Kids Carnival Zone, Splash Arena, Resort Roof Arena and Tranquil Zone) 
and seven themed gardens. It also houses  Pune’s first operational bowling alley in a residential 
development, an interactive gym and wellness spaces . Together with the project’s other 
offerings, the development brings more than 25 resort-style amenities designed for active, 
connected and experience-led living.
