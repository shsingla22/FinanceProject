Press Release 
  
RITES wins ‘Gold’ ICAI Award for  
excellence in Financial Reporting 
 
Gurugram, February 03, 2025:  RITES Limited, a Navratna Public Sector Enterprise, has been 
awarded the ‘Gold’ under the ‘Public Sector Entities’ category at t he ICAI Awards for 
Excellence in Financial Reporting for the financial year 2023-24. 
 
            
Hon’ble Union Minster Mr. Arjun Ram Meghwal presents the ‘Gold Award’ to 
 RITES’ Director (Finance) Mr. K.G. Agarwal at a ceremony in New Delhi on Saturday.  
 
The ICAI ‘Gold’ Award stands as a testament to RITES' commitment to financ ial excellence, 
transparency, and regulatory compliance. This recognition underscores the company’s 
adherence to the highest accounting standards, meticulous financial reporting , and 
responsible disclosure practices. By setting a benchmark in financial governance, RITES 
continues to uphold its dedication to integrity and stakeholder trust. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
About RITES Limited: 
RITES Limited, a Navratna Enterprise, is a leading player in the transport  consultancy and engineering sector in 
India, having diversified services and geographical reach. The company h as an experience spanning 50 years 
and undertaken projects in over 55 countries in Asia, Africa, Latin Americ a, South America, and Middle East 
region.
