Key Dev elopments  
 
Merger of GMR Airports Ltd  (GAL)  with GMR Ai rports Infrastructure Ltd  (GIL) 1 
With an objective to enhance shareholder value by simplifying the c orporate structure and bringing public 
shareholders closer to the airport assets, GMR had announced the execution of the Agreement and  
Scheme of Merger of GAL with GIL.  In this context, major  steps have  been achieved  pavin g the way further 
for the  merger completion within current expected timeline of Q1FY25. Joint Motion Application was heard 
by Hon’ble NCLT2 on 10 May’24 and 13 May’24  and the order is reserved.  
 
Regulatory developments  
• Delhi Airport (DIAL) :  
o AERA has allowed DIAL to extend existing tariff for further 6 months or till determination of CP43 
tariff whichever is earlier. DIAL is expected to submit Tariff Proposal for CP4 (1st Apr 2024 to 
31st Mar 2029) in Q1FY25  
o Received favorable award6 for arbitration invoked against Airports Authority of India (AAI) 
seeking certain reliefs on account of the occurrence of Force Majeure event (Covid -19 period). 
Key aspects are:  
▪ DIAL is excused from making payment of Monthly Annual Fee (”MAF”) for the period from 
19 Mar’20 to 28 Feb’22  
▪ There shall be extension of the term of OMDA (i.e Concession Period) for 1 year and 11 
months i.e., the period excused under force majeure  
▪ The award has been challenged by AAI  
 
• Hyderabad  Airport ( GHIAL): TDSAT4 pronounced its order with respect to appeal filed by GHIAL 
where some issues were upheld while some were disallowed. Key among these are:  
o Cargo, Ground Handling and Fuel Farm income to be treated as Non-Aeronautical Revenue  
o Income from Real Estate Development cannot be used for cross -subsidization  
o “Other Income” cannot be used for cross -subsidization  
o AERA’s5 decision not to consider 30% of Non -Aero Revenue for computing aeronautical taxes is 
incorrect, improper and unjustified
