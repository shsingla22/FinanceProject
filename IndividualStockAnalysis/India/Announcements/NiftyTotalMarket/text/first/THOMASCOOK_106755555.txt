Thomas Cook India & SOTC Travel Curate unique experiential Fall & 
Winter journeys across Europ e, North America , Australia & New Zealand  
Northern Lights and Arctic adventures; European Christmas markets and Alpine 
escapes; winter sun across Europe; adventure experiences across Australia and New 
Zealand  
 
Mumbai, August 25, 2026: Fall & Winter are emerging as a strong new travel window for Indian travellers, with 
growing interest in journeys that go beyond the traditional summer holiday period and an increasing shift 
towards a more year -round travel calendar. Following the impact of geopolitical uncertainty on summer travel 
plans  to Europe, renewed interest is now emerging in experiential journeys  to these destinations. Responding 
to this shift, Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel travel services company, and its Group 
Company, SOTC Travel, have curated a portfolio of Fall & Winter holidays across key international destinations, 
bringing together seas onal highlights, distinctive experiences and varied travel styles for the Indian traveller.  
 
The Northern Lights continue to remain one of the season’s most compelling draws, with Finland, Norway, 
Sweden and Iceland offering immersive ways to experience the Arctic. Beyond Aurora viewing, travellers can explore snow -covered landscapes through husky/ reindeer s ledding , snowmobile excursions and snowshoeing. 
Travellers are also choosing unique accommodations such as the famous glass -ceilinged  igloo houses , snow -
hotels, log cabins  and Arctic tree houses . 
 Europe takes on a distinctive character in winter  as its cities and towns transition into the festive season. France, 
Germany, Austria, Switzerland, the UK and the Czech Republic offer Christmas markets, illuminated streets, 
seasonal gastronomy and cultural celebrations. For travellers looking beyond the festiv ities, the Alps bring 
together scenic rail journeys, mountain stays, winter sports and panoramic landscapes, while distinctive alpine 
accommodation and local culinary experiences add another layer to the journey.  Across Scandinavia, winter 
cruises and whale- watching experiences offer another immersive way to experience the season, combining 
dramatic fjords, Arctic landscapes and wildlife encounters.  
 
North America adds another dimension to Fall & Winter travel, combining dramatic landscapes, outdoor 
adventure and vibrant festive experiences. Canada’s Rockies offer snow -covered mountain scenery alongside 
skiing, snowshoeing, ice skating, wildlife encounters, gondola rides and scenic winter trails, while the USA 
brings together New York’s iconic holiday season with experiences ranging from festive light -ups and seasonal 
markets to mountain escapes, winter sports, scenic road trips and nature- led adventures.  
 
For travellers who would rather leave the cold behind, Australia and New Zealand offer a completely different 
seasonal proposition, with spectacular New Year celebrations adding to the appeal. Australia brings together 
vibrant city celebrations, spectacular coastlines, wildlife encounters and outdoor adventures, while New 
Zealand offers mountains, lakes, fjords and nature- led experiences. Together, the destinations provide a 
compelling option for travellers seeking sunshine, adventure, the outdoors and memorable New Year 
experiences during the months September to January.  
 
Across these destinations, Fall & Winter travel is evolving beyond sightseeing towards more immersive, season-
led experiences. From Arctic adventures and festive Europe to North American winter landscapes and sun -
filled celebrations in the Southern Hemisphere, Thomas Cook India and SOTC Travel are bringing together journeys that combine destination, season and experience.  
 Mr. Rajeev Kale, President & Country Head – Holidays, MICE, Visa, Thomas Cook (India) Limited  said, 
“Travel is increasingly being defined by the experience a destination can offer at a particular time of year. Fall and winter open up some of the world’s most compelling travel moments, from the Aurora across Arctic skies and snow -covered Alpine landscapes  to Europe’s festive traditions and the outdoors of the Southern 
Hemisphere. At Thomas Cook India, we have been offering these diverse seasonal experiences to Indian 
travellers and bring deep expertise in curating journeys that go beyond conventional itineraries. We are seeing 
  
 
  
 
a clear shift towards holidays that are richer, more immersive and built around meaningful experiences, with 
travellers willing to explore beyond conventional winter destinations. This is reshaping the opportunity for the 
industry, as seasonal travel evolv es from a destination choice into a more experience- led way of discovering 
the world.”  
 
Mr. S.D. Nandakumar -  President and Country Head -  Holidays and Corporate Tours, SOTC Travel 
Limited said, “The winter holiday is evolving from a standard itinerary into a carefully curated experience. 
Travellers are increasingly combining the core destination with experiences that add depth to the journey — 
whether that is a Northern Lights escape, a scenic ra il journey, a unique stay, local gastronomy, wellness or a 
snow -filled adventure. What is particularly encouraging is the diversity of this demand: couples are seeking 
immersive and romantic escapes, families are combining festive celebrations with adventure, while younger travellers and groups are actively seeking experiences and destinations beyond the conventional winter circuit. 
This gives us an opportunity to create more distinctive, experience- rich journeys that reflect how Indians want 
to travel today.”
