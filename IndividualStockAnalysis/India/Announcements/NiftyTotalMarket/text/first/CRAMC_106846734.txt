Item No. 1: To approve appointment of Mr. Sunil Kumar Chugh (DIN: 08309168) as a Director 
of the Company:  
 
In accordance with the special rights granted to the promoters of the Company by the 
Shareholders through a Special Resolution  passed by way of Postal Ballot on December 7, 2025, 
which entitles them to nominate up to two Non -Independent Directors each on the Board of 
the Company , the Company has received the letter dated October 12, 2026 from Canara Bank 
intimating about nominating Mr. Sunil Kumar Chug h (DIN: 08309168) on the Board of the 
Company.  
 
In terms of Sections 152, 160, 161 of the Companies Act, 2013  (“the Act ”), the Board of 
Directors of the Company, based on the recommendation of the Nomination and 
Remuneration  Committee had reviewed the candidature of Mr. Sunil Kumar Chugh  
(DIN: 08309168)  and appointed him as an Additional Director (Non -Executive                                           
Non -Independent) with effect  from October 27, 2026, liable to retire by rotation.  In the opinion 
of the Board, Mr. Sunil Kumar Chugh (DIN: 08309168) meet the fit and proper criteria and is 
person of integrity, and have the necessary knowledge, experience and expertise for being 
appointed as  a Director  of the Company , liable to retire by rotation.  
 
Mr. Sunil Kumar Chugh  (DIN: 08309168) is a Chartered Accountant and a Certified Associate 
of the Indian Institute of Bankers by qualification, has over 33 years of banking experience. 
Began the career in 1993 and risen through the ranks serving in five public sector banks such 
as Punjab National Bank, Oriental Bank of Commerce, Union Bank of India, Punjab & Sind Bank 
and Dena Bank, gaining deep expertise through diverse functional roles and leadership 
responsibilities. Over the years, led key business and operations verticals, inc luding Corporate 
Credit, Retail, Agri & MSME (RAM) Credit, Financial Inclusion, Stressed Assets Management 
and Credit Monitoring. Headed Large Corporate Branches in Mumbai, Kolkata and 
Ahmedabad. Mr. Chugh’s  role spanned Corporate Office, Administrative Offices and Branches, 
combining field -level exposure with strategic insights. He has served as Director on the Boards 
of PNB Cards & Services Limited and Swift India Domestic Services Private Limited.  Currently, 
holding the position of Executive Director of Canara Bank effective from November 24, 2025.  
 
Mr. Sunil Kumar Chugh (DIN: 08309168) does not hold any shares in the Company.  
 
Mr. Sunil Kumar Chugh (DIN: 08309168) has furnished the necessary consent , declarations , 
and disclosures for his appointment as required under the Act, SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ( “Listing Regulations ”), SEBI (Mutual Funds) 
Regulations, 2026 and other applicable statutory provisions.  
 
In terms of Section 160 of the Act, the Company has received a notice in writing from Canara 
Bank proposing the candidature of Mr. Sunil Kumar Chugh (DIN: 08309168) for the office of 
Director on the Board of the Company.  
 
Mr. Sunil Kumar Chugh (DIN: 08309168) is not related to any other Director and Key 
Managerial Personnel of the Company . However, Mr. Sunil Kumar Chugh (DIN: 08309168) and 
Mr. Santanu Kumar Majumdar (DIN: 08223415), Non -Executive Non -Independent Directors 
are associated with Canara Bank and are connected only by virtue of their affiliation with 
Canara Bank.
