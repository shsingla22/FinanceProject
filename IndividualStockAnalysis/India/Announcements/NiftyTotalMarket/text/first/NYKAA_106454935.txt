circular resolution passed on September 24, 2025, unanimously 
consented to appoint Mr. Dipak Gupta as an Additional Non ‑

October 01, 2025 for a first term of 5 (five) years commencing

enormously inter ‑alia from his vast corporate experience,

and risk management.
