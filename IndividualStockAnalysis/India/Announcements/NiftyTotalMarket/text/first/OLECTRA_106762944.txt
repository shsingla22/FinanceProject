c. A brief on types of customers
  Olectra serves a diverse customer base across its E-Vehicles and Composite Insulator business segments.
  In the E-Vehicles segment, the Company’s customers primarily comprise State and Central Government bodies operating 
across various Indian cities, including Pune, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Silvassa, Nagpur, Surat, Tirupati, Kochi, 
Kullu, Panjim and Dehradun.
  In the Composite Insulator segment, the Company’s customer base includes State and Central power generation and 
distribution utilities, private power generation companies, and multinational infrastructure enterprises. These customers 
are located across India as well as international markets such as the United States, Nepal, Mozambique, Malawi, Niger, 
Tanzania and Tunisia.
