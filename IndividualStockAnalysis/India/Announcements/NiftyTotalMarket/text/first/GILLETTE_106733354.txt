Annual Report 2020-21
1
Kumar Venkatasubramanian
Managing Director The Company delivered a strong year in fiscal 2025-26, with consistent growth across both top-line and bottom-
line. For the year ended March 31, 2026, sales were ₹3,100 crore, up 8% versus the comparable period last year, while 
Profit After Tax increased 23% to ₹654 crore. This performance was driven by strong topline growth, supported by 
disciplined productivity and efficiency improvements across the business. We have done this in a context that is 
ever evolving, now at an unprecedented rate. In fact, the company has delivered consistent, balanced results over 
the last 5 years!
These results reflect the continued execution of our Integrated Growth Strategy — a focused portfolio, superiority 
across product, package, brand communication, retail execution and value, productivity, constructive disruption, 
and an empowered, agile and accountable organization. Together, these choices enable us to create value for 
all stakeholders. It is the combination of these five vectors working together that builds consumer preference, 
strengthens brand choice and drives category growth. 
We have some of the most well-known and iconic brands, which have strong, loyal and large consumer bases. We 
know that to continue winning with these consumers, as well as to attract new consumers to our portfolio, we need 
to deliver Irresistible superiority. We do this by delivering consumer relevance superiority every day, every week, 
every month, putting the consumer first in everything we do.
In Grooming, we introduced Gillette Guard 3in1 which has been designed with unique 3 platinum-coated blades, 
anti-slip rubber grip handle and aquagel lubrastrip, a product designed to deliver the performance of a three-blade 
shave at an accessible price point. This comes alongside an improved packaging designed to provide a premium 
look, stand out on shelves, catch consumer attention and make the purchase decision easier. Through innovations 
such as these, Gillette continues to strengthen its leadership position and deepen consumer trust in the category.
In Oral Care, Oral-B expanded its Sensitive Care portfolio with softer-bristle toothbrushes designed for consumers 
with sensitive gums. We also continued our efforts to promote healthy oral care habits among children through 
enhancements across our Kids portfolio, making brushing more engaging and enjoyable for young consumers.
Our focus on superiority extends beyond products to how consumers experience our brands across every shopping 
touchpoint. As the marketplace and path to purchase continue to evolve rapidly, we are strengthening our 
capabilities across both physical and digital channels to ensure consumers can easily find, choose and access our 
brands wherever they shop. At the same time, productivity remains deeply embedded in our operating model, 
enabling us to invest for growth while delivering balanced financial performance.
Beyond business results, we remain committed to creating a positive impact in the communities where we live 
and operate. This year, P&G Shiksha, our flagship CSR program, achieved the significant milestone of positively 
impacting more than 1 crore children from underserved communities. This milestone represents far more than scale 
— it reflects over 1 crore individual learning journeys supported through the program. Aligned with the National 
Education Policy, the program continues to focus on addressing learning gaps and improving learning outcomes 
through sustained interventions and awareness-building efforts.
Looking ahead, the Company is well positioned to capture the significant growth opportunities in our categories 
and overcome the challenges of the external environment. Consumers seek out our brands and use them daily 
because of their superior benefits — holistically defined — in order to best solve the problems they need to solve, for 
themselves and their families. We remain confident that our Integrated Growth Strategy is the right path forward. 
It has been delivering results, and we are confident that by continuing to execute it with discipline and excellence, 
we will strengthen our brands, grow our categories, create value for society and deliver sustainable, long-term value 
for our shareholders.
I would like to express my sincere gratitude to our consumers, customers, employees, business partners and 
shareholders for your continued trust and support. We continue to focus keenly on creating value for our 
stakeholders. As we enter the new fiscal year, we remain focused on keeping consumers first in everything we do 
and serving them through superior products, meaningful innovation and excellent execution.
Thank you for your continued partnership.Dear Shareholders,

DIRECTORS
Mr. Pramod Agarwal, Non-Executive Director
Mr. Pramod Agarwal is an MBA from Indian Institute of Management, Ahmedabad. After over 28 years 
of experience with P&G, he retired in 2016. He has worked in seven geographies - India, Thailand, Japan, 
Philippines, USA, Singapore and Switzerland. He has led several major changes which have had a lasting 
impact on the business and organization. He is now a management consultant, an angel investor and mentor 
to several start-ups. He is very active in giving back to the society with focus on financial independence of 
women, a major donor and has served as President – Rotary Club of Delhi South. 
Mr. Kumar Venkatasubramanian, Managing Director
Mr. Kumar Venkatasubramanian has completed his Bachelor of Engineering from National Institute of 
Technology, Durgapur and has an MBA from Indian Institute of Management, Calcutta. In his last role, he 
was CEO of P&G Australia and New Zealand where he delivered record business performance through 
strong category growth. In his 25+  career, he has led complex categories across some of the world’s largest 
markets including India, Brazil, and Australia and has led large and diverse teams, with extensive leadership 
experience to complement his strong strategic business acumen.
Mr. Krishnamurthy Iyer, Independent Director
Mr. Krishnamurthy Iyer is a member of Institute of Chartered Accountants of India and the Institute of 
Company Secretaries of India. He is a long term global professional with wide and varied experience in 
functional and leadership roles across diverse industries and geographies. He is currently the Managing 
Partner of Rational Equity Partners LLP. During his career, he has worked in senior positions and as CEO in 
various companies such as Walmart, AS Watsons, ANZ Grindlays Bank and Piramyd Retail. As President & 
CEO of Walmart India, he played a pivotal role in growing the brick & mortar and digital footprint of Walmart 
in India. 
Mr. Ghanashyam Hegde, Non-Executive Director
Mr. Ghanashyam Hegde is a B. Com and LL.B from Bangalore University. He holds a Post Graduate Diploma 
in Intellectual Property Rights from National Law School of India University, Bangalore. He is also a qualified 
Company Secretary. He has over 25 years of experience across pharma, media & entertainment, financial 
services and chemical industries. He is currently designated as Vice President and Associate General 
Counsel - India, Middle-East, Turkey and Africa.
Ms. Anjuly Chib Duggal, Chairperson and Independent Director
Ms. Anjuly Chib Duggal, a 1981 batch IAS Officer (Punjab cadre), retired as Secretary to the Government of 
India in the Department of Financial Services. Prior to this, Ms. Duggal was Secretary, Ministry of Corporate 
Affairs. She has worked in various areas of finance (public expenditure and financial services including 
banking, insurance, pensions and financial inclusion), corporate affairs, industry, personnel management 
and environment, among others. 
Mr. Sanjay Asher, Independent Director
Mr. Sanjay Asher is a Senior Partner with M/s. Crawford Bayley & Co. He is a practicing Advocate and Solicitor. 
He is also a qualified Chartered Accountant. He specialises in the fields of corporate and commercial law, 
M&A, cross border M&A, joint ventures, private equity and capital markets.

PRINCIPLES
The Procter & Gamble Company, US (“P&G”) is a world leading multinational consumer 
goods Company, focused on providing trusted, branded products of superior quality, 
performance and value to improve the lives of its consumers around the world. Today, 
P&G products are sold in about 180 countries and territories throughout the world.
Gillette India Limited (“the Company”) was incorporated in India as Indian Shaving 
Products Limited in 1984. P&G acquired the Company in the year 2005. The Company 
is engaged in manufacturing and selling of branded packaged fast moving consumer 
goods in the grooming and oral care businesses. It is one of the well-known FMCG 
companies that has in its portfolio Gillette and Oral-B, which are world’s leading brands. 
The Company has carved a reputation for delivering superior products to meet the 
needs of consumers. The Company is committed in making every day in the lives of 
Indian consumers better through superior value propositions.
