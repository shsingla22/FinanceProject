Press Release  
Affle Appoints Sameer Sondhi as Chief Strategic Investments Officer 
and CEO, North America  
Industry veteran to bolster Affle’s organic and inorganic growth plans in developed markets  
San Francisco, 02 January  2026: Affle, a global technology company driving AI -powered 
consumer intelligence and mobile advertising, today announced the appointment of Sameer Sondhi 
as Chief Executive Officer, North America, with a dual role as the Chief Strategic Investments 
Officer .  
In this senior leadership role, Sameer will be responsible for driving North America expansion plans 
and new strategic investments aligned with Affle 3i vision of delivering 10x growth in its third 
decade, centered on the core pillars of Innovation, Impact  and Intelligence.  
Sameer brings extensive leadership experience in global adtech and digital media, with a strong 
track record of scaling revenue in developed markets. Most recently,  he held senior roles at Verve, 
including CEO and Chief Revenue Officer, leading global strategy, operations and growth while 
strengthening relationships with advertisers, agencies and publishers worldwide. Previously, he 
held leadership roles at InMobi, wh ere he supported expansion across North America, Europe and 
Asia. He holds a master’s degre e in computer science . 
 
Commenting on this,  Anuj Khanna Sohum , Chairperson, MD & CEO of Affle  
said “Welcoming Sameer as CEO for North America marks a pivotal milestone 
in our journey and will elevate our business to even greater heights across 
developed markets. He brings proven leadership experience, a deep 
understanding of our business and credible lon g-term commitment to our vision 
and culture . With Sameer’s dual role, we are uniquely positioned to reinforce 
our organic growth momentum in North America while executing targeted 
strategic investment plan. ” 
Talking about it,  Sameer Sondhi  said “I am excited to join Affle at a defining 
phase in its growth journey . Affle’s strong foundation in AI -driven consumer 
intelligence and performance -led advertising provides a strong foundation for 
scalable, profitable growth. I look forward to working closely with the 
leadership team, partners and customers to unlock long -term value, as Affle 
enters its next phase of expansion. ”
