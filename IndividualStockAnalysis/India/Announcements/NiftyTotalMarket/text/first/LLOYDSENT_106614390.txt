Result Highlights – FY26
❑FY 2025 –26 represents a year of strong earnings delivery for Lloyds Enterprises Limited. On a consolidated basis, the Compan y registered 
revenue from operations of INR 1,756 Crore an increase of approximately 18% over FY25 driven by sus tained growth across its trading and 
investment businesses. Consolidated net profit for the year surged to INR 417 Crore, a near 340% increase over the prior year, reflecting 
operational leverage, efficient capital deployment, and strong returns from strategic i nvestments. The Company's Basic EPS on a 
consolidated basis improved to INR 3.08 from INR 0.97 in FY25.
❑On a standalone basis, revenue from operations was INR 463 Crore for FY26. Standalone net profit witnessed a dramatic improvement to 
INR 268 Crore  from INR 16 Crore in FY25, reflecting significant gains in Other Income of INR 349  Crore, which includes income from 
strategic investments and treasury activities. The Q4 FY26 standalone revenue from operations stood at INR 224.61 Crore and net profit at 
INR 21.45 Crore.  
❑Company subsidiary Lloyds Engineering Works Ltd continues to perform well . The company had reported its highest-ever 
Revenue and PAT in FY25 . continuing its stellar performance in FY26 as well, the company has an order book of  more than ~₹ 8335  
cr as of FY26 (including its subsidiaries)
❑Jonnagiri Enters Pre-Commercial Phase — India’s First Privately -Owned Gold Mine Nears Full Commissioning : In Q2FY26, the company 
made a strategic investment in Geomysore Services India Pvt. Ltd (GMSI ), acquiring a significant stake in India’s first privately 
operated gold mine since Independence. The project is expected to produce up to 1,000 kilograms of refined gold annually, with peak 
output continuing for the next 15 years , supported by a strong resource base of up to 42.5 tonnes of gold (as per JORC estimates). Gold 
extraction has begun.
