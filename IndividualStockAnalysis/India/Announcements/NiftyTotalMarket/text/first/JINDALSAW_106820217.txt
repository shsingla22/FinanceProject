Order Book Position (Standalone) 
The current order book for Pipes and Pellets is ~ US$ 1,135 Million &  LOIs of  ~USD$ 575 Million 
For Q2 FY27, the Company  reported  order  book  of ~ $ 1,135 million  for Iron & Steel  Pipes  and 
Pellets . Iron & Steel  Pipes  account  for ~ $ 1,122 million  and Pellets  for ~ $ 13 million
❑Highlights  of order  book  for Iron & Steel  Pipes :
➢Order  book  is ~1.8 million  MT.
➢Export  orders  in term  of volumes  is ~0.74 million  MT (~41% of total  quantity  of pipe  order  book) .
➢Export  orders  constitute  ~28% of the total  order  book  (in terms  of value) .
➢Execution  of export  orders  to the Middle  East are on hold  due to Middle  East conflict . 
➢Execution  of balance  order  book  is projected  to span  the next  9–12 months .
➢The above  order  book  does  not include  the  LOIs  of USD  575 Million  (~5 Lacs  MT) including  export   
business  of ~USD  430 Million  from  other  than  MENA  region .
➢Apart  from  the above  order  book,  UAE  Entity  has an order  book  of ~USD  195 million  (~ 1,77,000
