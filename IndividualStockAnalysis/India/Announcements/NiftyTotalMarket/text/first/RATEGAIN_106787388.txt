Sojern's Sylvia Weiler Named a 2026 AdExchanger Top Women in Media and 
Ad Tech Honoree 
Weiler, President and GM of Global Destinations, recognized in the Sell-Side Tech Trailblazers category—a first 
for Sojern 
 
SAN FRANCISCO, 22 September 2026 —  RateGain Travel Technologies Limited (BSE: 543417, NSE: 
RATEGAIN), a global leader in AI-powered SaaS solutions for the travel and hospitality industry, today 
announced that Sylvia Weiler, President and General Manager of Global Destinations at Sojern, a RateGain 
brand, has been named a 2026 honoree in AdExchanger's Top Women in Media and Ad Tech Awards, in the 
Sell-Side Tech Trailblazers category. It is the first time a Sojern executive has been recognized by the 
program. 
 
Weiler has spent more than two decades at the intersection of travel, hospitality, and advertising technology, 
holding leadership roles at Sojern, Tripadvisor, Airbnb, and Travelocity. At Sojern, she built the company's 
destination programmatic advertising business from the ground up and led development of an economic 
impact measurement framework that has since become a benchmark for how destination marketing 
organizations evaluate advertising performance—shifting the conversation from impressions and clicks 
toward measurable visitor spending and economic outcomes. She returned to Sojern earlier this year to lead 
the destinations line of business.  
 
Beyond her commercial track record, Weiler has been a longstanding advocate for women in advertising and 
travel technology. She founded Sojern's first Women's Group and has personally mentored more than 15 
emerging women leaders across the industry. 
 
"We were delighted to welcome Sylvia back to Sojern earlier this year, and it's incredibly rewarding to see 
that impact recognized industry-wide so soon after," said Mark Rabe, CEO of Sojern. "Sylvia has spent her 
career redefining what destination marketing can achieve, and this recognition reflects the impact she's had 
not just at Sojern, but across the industry."  
 
“Having spent most of my career in media and advertising, this recognition feels especially meaningful. It’s 
not just a reflection of the work I’ve had the privilege to be part of, but of the generous mentors, colleagues, 
and partners who have shaped my journey along the way,” said Sylvia Weiler, President and General 
Manager, Global Destinations, Sojern.  "I'm incredibly proud of how far this industry has come, and it's an 
exciting time to be part of it, with technology reshaping what's possible faster than ever. I'm grateful to 
continue contributing to its evolution.” 
 
Honorees will be celebrated at the Top Women in Media and Ad Tech Awards Gala on November 3, 2026, in 
New York City.
