Summary of proceedings of the 15th Annual General Meeting (“AGM”) of the Members of  
Aptus Value Housing Finance India Limited (‘the Company’) held on Wednesday, August 14, 2024.  
The 15th Annual General Meeting (“AGM”) of the Members of the Company was hel d on Wednesday, 
August 14, 2024  through Video Conferencing (“VC”) / Other Audio -Visual Means (“OAVM”) at 11:00 
A.M. (IST).  The AGM was held in compliance with all the applicable circulars issued by the Ministry of 
Corporate Affairs (MCA) and Securities and  Exchange Board of India (SEBI) and as per the applicable 
provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and rules made there under.  
Mr. M Anandan, Executive Chairman chaired the meeting virtually from, Chennai. The requisite quorum 
being present, the Chairman cal led the meeting is in order.

minutes post the AGM or by way of e -voting available on the NSDL platform. The proceedings of the 
meeting were  then handed over to the Chairman.
