Proceedings of the Meeting:

quorum being present, the Meeting was called to order by the Chairman.  Thereafter, on 
request of the Chairman, the directors and Company Secretary introduced themselves. 
The Chairman of the Audit committee, Nomination & Remuneration Committee and 
Stakeholders' Relationship  Committee were present at the Meeting as per the 
requirements of the Act and the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI Listing Regulations"). The participation of the partners and 
authorized representatives of Statutory  Auditor, Cost Auditor, Secretarial  Auditor and 
Scrutinizer for the remote e-voting and e-voting during the AGM were also 
acknow !edged.

independent Director and Ms. Radhika Govind Rajan, Non-executive Independent 
Director who had expressed their inability to attend the meeting due to their pre­
commitments.

shareholders. He shared insight on industry trends and highlighted the Company's 
operational and financial performance amidst geopolitical uncertainties and commodity 
price volatility. He outlined the benefits arising from the 'One Exide' operating model, 
progress in research and development, digital transformation initiatives, and 
advancements in the lithium-ion business. He also presented the Company's key ESG 
initiatives undertaken to strengthen its sustainability and environmental stewardship 
efforts.

also made available on the Company's website, as well as on the websites of the Stock 
Exchanges and NSDL, and were accordingly considered as read.
