SOBHA Limited Records  Highest Ever Sale Value of Rs 5,862 Crore , Up 47.2% YoY  
 
Bengaluru, October 19, 2026 : SOBHA Limited continued its growth momentum in Q2 FY27, recording quarterly sales value 
of Rs 22.06 bn , an increase of 16% YoY. For H1 FY27, the Company achieved its highest -ever sales value of Rs 58.62 bn , 
registering 47.2% growth over the corresponding period last year. The performance was supported by a strong response to 
new project launches and sustained demand across its existing portfolio. H1 FY’27 collections also reached an all -time high 
of Rs 40.09 bn , reflecting continued momentum in customer realisations.  
  
Financial  Performance  
• Collections reached  an all-time high of Rs 40.09 bn in H1 FY27 , registering a 4.8% YoY growth, with Q2 FY27 
collections at Rs. 20.84 bn .  
• Revenue stood at Rs. 10.17  bn in Q2 FY27 , taking H1 FY27 Revenue to Rs 23.47 bn , a marginal decline of 1% YoY . 
• PAT stood at Rs. 0.33 bn  in Q2 FY27 , while  H1 FY27 PAT stood at Rs 0.84 bn, registering 2.6% YoY decline . 
• Net debt stood at Rs. -3.40 bn, with a Net Debt -to-Equity ratio of -0.07. 
Sales  and  Operational  Performance  
 
• H1 FY27 sales reached an all -time high of Rs. 58.62 bn, representing a 47.2% YoY growth.  
• Quarterly real estate sales value stood at Rs. 22.06 bn, marking a 16% YoY increase .  
• Average price realization stood at Rs. 15,52 2 per sq. ft. in Q2 FY27 . 
• During H1 FY27, the Company launched 7.9 2 million sq. ft. of saleable area across five projects in Bangalore, 
Gurgaon and Calicut, with an estimated sale potential of Rs. 112 bn.
