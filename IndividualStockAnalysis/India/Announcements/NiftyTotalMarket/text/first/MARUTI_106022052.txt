variants of the New Age Baleno, including the automatic and CNG variants. The Baleno Regal Edition is tailored to elevate the appeal of the popular premium hatchback with enhanced cabin comfort and stylish 
design elements to lend a personalised touch. Equipped with complimentary accessory kit s
