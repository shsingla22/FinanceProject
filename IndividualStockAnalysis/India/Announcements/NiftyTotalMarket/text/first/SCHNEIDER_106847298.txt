Gurugram , Haryana  – 05th November  2026  – Schneider Electric Infrastructure  Limited  (SEIL/Company ), 
a part of Schneider Electric Group,  a global energy technology leader , announced its financial results for the 
second quarter and half year of 2026 -27, ended 30th September 2026.  
 
SEIL recorded its highest -ever quarterly order intake of INR 1,265 crores in Q2 ’FY27, ahead of the previous 
high of INR 915 crores in Q1’FY27. Demand remained strong across the established segments and the emerging ones, such as data centres and semiconduc tors. For H1 ’FY27, order intake was INR 2,180 crores, 
up 24.7% year -on-year. The order backlog stood at INR 2,701 crores as of 30th September 2026, up 49.6% 
year-on-year, which gives the Company healthy sales  visibility.  
 Sales  for Q2 ’FY27 was INR 779 crores, up 19.8% year -on-year and 19.6% over Q1 ’FY27. The conversion of 
order wins into sales  is progressing well, and project execution has picked up. Sales  for H1 ’FY27 stood at 
INR 1,430 crores, up 12.5% year -on-year.  
 
EBIT for Q2 ’FY27 was INR 56 crores, com pared with INR 72 crores in the corresponding quarter of the 
previous year. EBIT for H1 ’FY27 was INR 64 crores, compared with INR 130 crores in H1’ FY26. Profitability 
remains lower than last year. This is due to commodity price volatility and the lag in passing on higher input costs on certain legacy orders. Margins are improving sequentially as the Company executes orders booked 
at current cost levels.  
 
Deepak Sharma, Zone President - Greater India, Schneider Electric, and Board Director, Schneider 
Electric  Infrastructure Limited, said, “SEIL has delivered another strong order quarter, and the recovery in 
sales  in Q2 shows that our execution is gaining momentum. Demand from data centres, semiconductors 
and grid infrastructure continues to be strong as India advances its electrification and industrial 
modernisation journey. While profitability remains below last year, our priority is to convert the strong order 
book  into sales, and to improve project margins, through disciplined execution, cost pass -through and 
portfolio enrichment .” 
 
Udai Singh , MD & CEO,  Schneider Electric Infrastructure Limited,  said,  “Highest -ever orders in Q2, a 
strong sequential recovery in sales and a record order backlog make this a solid half year for SEIL. 
Our focus now is on timely delivery, operational excellence and improving the margin quality of our

Beyond the numbers, SEIL continued to invest in building capacity for growth while strengthening its

To support growing demand, the Board of Directors, at its meeting on 29 October 2026, approved a further 
capacity expansion at the Company's Kolkata Medium Voltage Components (KMVC) facility. The Company plans to inv est INR 211 crores to increase Vacuum Interrupter capacity from 250,000 to 375,000 units per 
annum and to add Breaker capacity of 18,000 units per annum, by the end of FY 2028- 29. The investment 
will be funded through internal accruals and/or external borrowings. It supports the Company's capacity augmentation requirements, export growth plans and technology upgradation initiatives . 
 
Growth at SEIL goes hand in hand with responsible business practices. Subsequent to the quarter end, the 
Company was recognis ed with the Excellence in Sustainability Award in the Manufacturing Corporate 
category at the 4th Annual Sustainability Symposium & Excellence Awards 2026, organised by the Indian Chamber of Commerce (ICC), and with the Golden Peacock Award 2026 for Excellence in Corporate 
Governance. The recognitions reflect SEIL's progress on decarbonisation, including 100% renewable 
electricity across its manufacturing sites and an approximately 80% reduction in Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions over three years, along with its focus on transparency and accountability.  
 
Together with its capacity investments, these efforts underline SEIL's approach of growing the business 
while creating long- term value for all stakeholders.
