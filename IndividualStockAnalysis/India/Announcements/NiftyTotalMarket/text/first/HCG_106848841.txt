Dr. B. S. Ajaikumar, Founder and Non -Executive Chairman , HealthCare Global Enterprises Ltd., said  
“At HCG, our focus remains firmly on advancing oncology care through clinical excellence, innovation, 
research and a deeply patient -centric approach. We believe the future of cancer care lies in bringing 
together advanced medical technologies, specialised c linical expertise and cutting -edge research to make 
treatment increasingly precise, personalised and effective.  
We are strengthening our research capabilities across emerging areas such as multi -omics, organoid 
technology, cellular therapies including CAR -T, and longevity research, with a strong emphasis on 
translating scientific discoveries into meaningful clinical  applications. Our endeavour is to bridge the gap 
between research and clinical practice, enabling us to better understand the biology of cancer, develop 
innovative treatment approaches and continually improve patient outcomes.  
As we expand our network and enhance access to high -quality oncology services across geographies, our 
ambition is to build an institution that not only delivers excellence in cancer care today but also helps shape 
the future of medicine. We remain committe d to advancing medical science, improving patient outcomes 
and creating sustainable long -term value for all our stakeholders. ”

Commenting on the results, Dr. Manish Mattoo, CEO , HealthCare Global Enterprises Ltd. said,  
“We sustained our growth momentum in Q2 FY27, delivering revenue growth of 16% year -on-year to INR 
7,318 million, driven by a 9% increase in patient volumes and a 7% improvement in average revenue per 
patient (ARPP). Adjusted EBITDA grew 17% year -on-year to INR 1,425 million, with a margin of 19.7%.  
This performance reflects our continued focus on improving revenue mix, increasing the contribution from 
high -complexity oncology treatments and driving operational efficiencies. Our strategic emphasis on 
expanding the non -institutional business continues to enhance the quality of revenue, with its share 
improving by 325 basis points year -on-year to 69%.  
Looking ahead, our priorities remain focused on improving asset utilisation, accelerating the ramp -up of 
newer facilities, enhancing productivity across our existing network and executing strategic capacity 
expansions. We remain committed to delivering sus tainable, profitable growth while broadening access to 
advanced, high -quality cancer care. ” 
 
Consolidated Highlights for the Quarter Ending 30th September , 26: 
• HCG reported revenue of INR 7,31 8 Mn in Q2 FY27, representing 16% YoY growth, supported by 
broad -based growth across clusters  
• Patient volumes increased by 9% YoY , while ARPP grew by 7%, driven by a favourable payor mix and 
higher contribution from advanced oncology treatments, including robotic surgeries (up 37% YoY) 
and other high -end therapies  
• Adjusted EBITDA increased by 1 7% YoY to INR 1, 425 Mn in Q2 FY27, with EBITDA margin improving 
to 19. 7%, compared to 19.2% in Q2 FY26  
• International business revenue grew by approximately 54% YoY to INR 302 Mn in Q2 FY27, driven 
by increased patient volumes in radiation and medical oncology  
Key Business Developments for the Quarter Ending 30th September , 26: 
• Capacity Expansion:  Added 25 operational beds in Q2 FY27, comprising 13 beds in Kenya, 7 in 
Kolkata and 5 in Nagpur, taking total bed additions to 146 in H1 FY27  
• Advanced Technology Deployment:  Operationalised  the state -of-the-art adaptive radiation therapy 
system MR -LINAC at North Bengaluru, a LINAC in Africa and a cardiac robotic surgery system at HMS 
Ahmedabad  
• Key Clinical Program Additions:  
o Advanced Cancer Therapies:  Performed first CAR -T cell therapy at HCC Ahmedabad, 
strengthening advanced oncology treatment capabilities  
o Robotic & Minimally Invasive Surgery:  Introduced robotic breast surgery at KR, robotic spine 
surgery at Rajkot and Transoral Robotic Surgery (TORS) at Baroda  
o Precision Diagnostics:  Launched a 1,212 -gene liquid biopsy panel and introduced two new 
nuclear medicine tracers at KR, enhancing precision cancer diagnostics

About Healthcare Global Enterprises Limited (HCG):  
HealthCare Global Enterprises Ltd. (HCG), headquartered in Bengaluru, is the largest provider of cancer care 
in India. Through its network of 26 hospitals across India and Africa, HCG has brought advanced cancer care 
to the doorstep of millions of people. HCG’s comprehensive cancer centers  provide expertise and advanced 
technologies for the effective diagnosis and treatment of cancer under one roof .
