Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing 
Regulations”), this is to inform you that, the Board of Directors at its meeting held on 05th 
November, 2025, inter -alia, considered and approved the following:   
 
1. Resignati on of Statutory Auditor of the Company  
 
This is to inform you that the Statutory Auditor of the Company, M/s . T R Chadha & Co 
LLP, Chartered Accountants, ICAI Firm Registration No. (006711N/N500028) have 
resigned  from the position of the Statutory Auditor of the Company  with effect from 
5th November, 2025  i.e., post submission of Limited Review Report for the quarter and 
half-year ended 30 September 2025 and after furnishing all requisite certifications for 
the af oresaid audit/limited review perio d. 
 
The reason cited by them for their resignation is that, based on their understanding , as 
a part of Adani  Group, the Management is considering to align its process for 
appointment of statutory auditors wherein audit of major listed companies of the group 
is to be conducted by globally affiliated firms.    
 
The outgoing Auditors have not raised any concerns or issues, and there is no other 
material reasons for their  resignation, other than as mentioned  in their letter , is 
enclosed as Annexure 1. 
 
The Board placed on record its deep appreciation for the services rendered by the 
outgoing   auditors during their tenure  as Auditors of the Company . 
 
 
 
 
          
  
  
 
 
 
  
 
2.    Appointment of Statutory Auditor of the Company  
 
Based on the recommendation the Audit Committee  and subject to the approval of the 
members of the Company, the Board of the Company approved the appointment of  Price 
Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP , (FRN: 012754N/N500016 ) as the Statutory 
Auditors of the Company (PWCA LLP) to fill the casual vacancy caused arising from the 
resignation of M/s T R Chadha & Co LLP , Chartered Accountants , ICAI Firm Registration 
No. (006711N/N500028) . 
PWCA LLP  shall conduct   the audit of the accounts of the Company for the financial year 
2025 –26, including the Limited Review for the third quarter   of the said financial year  
and shall hold office until the conclusion of the ensuing Annual General Meeting  to be 
held in the year 2026 . 
Terms of Appointment : With effect from the date of passing of the Board resolution till 
the conclusion of the 48th Annual General Meeting to be held in the year 2026  and t he 
remuneration  payable to the Auditors shall be  as may be mutually agreed  between the  
Board of Directors  and the  Statutory Auditors , subject to the approval of Members  of the 
Company through general meeting , in accordance with the provisions of Section 139(8)  
and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 . 
Brief details  relating to the appointment of PWCA LLP  as Statutory Auditors are enclosed 
as Annexure 2 . 
The requisite disclosures, pursuant to Part A Para A of Schedule III of the Listing Regulations 
and in terms of SEBI Master Circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 11th November 
2024 are enclosed as per annexure s respectively.
