Sterling Launches Premium Offbeat Getaway at Sterling Vanvasa, near Lansdowne  
8th Resort in Uttarakhand | Expanding India’s Most Curated Holiday Network  
 
Chennai, 24 June 2025 : Sterling Holiday Resorts has unveiled its newest property — Sterling 
Vanvasa, near Lansdowne  — a tranquil hill retreat , spread over 30 acres of wooded forest,  designed 
for premium travellers seeking a getaway to escape from the stress of urban life. This marks Sterling’s 
8th resort in Uttarakhand , reinforcing the brand’s position as a pioneer in identifying India’s most 
offbeat and serene holiday destinations.  
Located beyond Juee village , this hideaway sits at over 3,200 feet above sea level , surrounded by 
lush pine and  sal forests, mountain air, and sweeping views of the Garhwal Himalayas . Far from the 
bustle of Lansdowne town and untouched by tourist crowds, Sterling Vanvasa offers guests the 
chance to truly disconnect and unwind — amidst nature, silence, and stunning vistas.  
The resort features 33 colonial -style cottages and family suites , complete with private sit -outs and 
wood -lined interiors . Each room is positioned to maximise scenic views — whether of the valley 
below or the Ramganga river  glinting in the distance. With no TVs in the rooms, guests are 
encouraged to slow down, breathe deep, and embrace the quiet.  
Aranya , the multicuisine restaurant, also serves Garhwali delicacies  prepared with herbs and 
vegetables from the resort’s own gardens. Additional highlights include a hill -facing swimming pool, 
meditation zones , and a n event venue perfect for intimate  gatherings – for families and  corporate 
incentives . 
“Sterling Vanvasa is another example of how we specialise in crafting offbeat destinations for 
discerning  travellers,” said Vikram Lalvani, MD & CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts . “From Munnar to 
Karwar, Yercaud  to Razole, we’ve consistently created sanctuaries where guests can slow down, 
recharge, and reconnect. Vanvasa is a natural extension of that promise — peaceful, panoramic, and 
profoundly rejuvenating.”  
“Sterling’s experience in operating upscale resorts in lesser -known gems made them the right 
partner for Vanvasa,” added Mr. Satendra Rawat, Proprietor, Vanvasa Hotels & Resorts.  “We’re 
confident this resort will redefine what it means to truly escape — blending refined comfort with 
nature’s raw beauty.”  
Sterling Vanvasa offers experiences designed for rejuvenation  and reconnection: sunrise yoga, 
birdwatching, stargazing, and forest dining . Guests can enjoy long nature walks , get informed with a 
wildlife briefing session , indulge in slow meals , and fall asleep to the sounds of the hills — making it 
a compelling year -round getaway . 
With this launch, Sterling continues  its journey of redefining travel for discerning guests — not with 
crowded attractions, but with curated calm.
