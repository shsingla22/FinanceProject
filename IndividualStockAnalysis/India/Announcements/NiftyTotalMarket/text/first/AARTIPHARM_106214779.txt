Aarti Pharmalabs Earns EcoVadis Gold Rating, Among Top 5% Globally for 
Sustainability  
Mumbai , Maharashtra  – April 29, 2025 : Aarti Pharmalabs Limited is proud to announce a 
significant achievement in its sustainability journey. Following evaluation by EcoVadis, the 
company has been awarded a Gold Badge , placing it among the top 5%  of companies 
worldwide in the EcoVadis Sustaina bility Ratings.  
This revised rating reflects Aarti Pharmalabs' enhanced sustainability performance and 
commitment to environmental, social, and ethical practices. The EcoVadis assessment 
evaluate d us on  several key areas  and scores us across : 
 
Evaluation Areas  Score  
Environment  85 
Labor & Human Rights  75 
Ethics  74 
Sustainable Procurement  70 
 
The overall score stands at 77 with 96 percentile .  
"This achievement is a collective success, and we congratulate everyone involved. We are 
committed to maintaining the highest standards of sustainability and contributing to a 
healthier future." added Mrs. Hetal Gogri Gala, Vice Chairperson and Managing Di rector  at 
Aarti Pharmalabs Limited.  
"We are delighted to receive this recognition from EcoVadis. This Gold Badge is a testament 
to our ongoing efforts to integrate sustainability into every aspect of our operations. It 
validates our dedication to responsi ble business practices and motivates us to further improve 
our performance." said Mr. Narendra J. Salvi, Managing Director  at Aarti Pharmalabs Limited.  
"This recognition strengthens our resolve to pursue ambitious sustainability goals and sets a 
new benchmark for our future endeavors." said Mr. Tanaji S. Shinde, Head of Sustainability  at 
Aarti Pharmalabs Limited.  
Aarti Pharmalabs extends its heartfelt gratitude to all stakeholders whose dedication and 
contributions have made this accomplishment possible. The company also expresses its 
sincere appreciation to its management for their invaluable guidance, unwavering support, 
and the trust they have placed  in this initiative.

About Aarti Pharmalabs Limited : 
Aarti Pharmalabs Limited (NSE: A ARTIPHARM | BSE: 543748) is a prominent Indian 
pharmaceutical and nutraceutical manufacturer with a global presence. Since its demerger in 
2022, Aarti Pharmalabs Limited has accelerated its focus on innovation, sustainability, and 
global leadership in the pharmaceutical and nutraceutical sectors. The company specializes in 
producing generic APIs, intermediates, and xanthine derivatives (including Synthetic Caffeine). 
Additionally, Aarti Pharmalabs Limited offers comprehensive CDMO services for small 
molecul e drug substances, catering to clinical and commercial phase projects for innovator 
pharma companies, with dedicated facilities for HPAPIs, corticosteroids, cytotoxic, and 
oncology products.  
About EcoVadis : 
EcoVadis is a globally recognized sustainability ratings platform, assessing companies' 
environmental, social, and ethical performance. The EcoVadis methodology is based on 
international sustainability standards, including the Global Reporting Initiative, the UN Global 
Compact, and ISO 26000.
