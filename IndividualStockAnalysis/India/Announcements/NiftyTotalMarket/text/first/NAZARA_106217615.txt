Nazara Publishing launches FAU -G: Domination at WAVES 2025  
Developed by Mumbai -based Dot9 Games and published by Nazara Publishing, FAU -G: 
Domination is the latest chapter in the FAU -G series from nCore Games.  
The game is dedicated to Bharat Ke Veer with donations pledged to the Bharat Ke Veer 
Trust.  
Mumbai, May 1, 2025: In a defining moment for Indian game development, the highly 
anticipated FAU-G: Domination  has launched on Android at the World Audio Video 
Entertainment Summit 2025 (WAVES 2025) , in the presence of nCore Group and Dot9 
Games mentor Shri Akshay Kumar , marking a major step forward in India's journey to 
becoming a global powerhouse in interactive entertainment.  
 
Developed by Dot9 Games  and published by Nazara Publishing , the gaming arm of the 
publicly listed Nazara Technologies , FAU -G: Domination represents the new face of self -
reliant Indian innovation. This title is the latest in the FAU -G series from nCore Games , and 
showcases India’s growing leadership in building culturally relevant, globally competitive, and 
emotionally resonant gaming experiences.  
 
Inspired by the valor of our soldiers and powered by the vision of Atmanirbhar Bharat, the 
game is now live and available for download:  
 
Google Play Store  — https://bit.ly/sm_FAU -G 
 
A detailed roadmap outlining future content and updates will be announced soon.  
 
The game is India’s answer to global narratives, created for Indian gamers by Indian 
developers —and it reflects the nation's spirit, courage, and technological ambition. FAU -G: 
Domination goes beyond entertainment, serving as a digital tribute to the guard ians of our 
borders and a resounding statement of India's creative strength on the global stage.  
 
Alongside the launch, a high -impact gameplay trailer has been released, showcasing epic 
battles across diverse Indian terrains, immersive storytelling, and intense tactical action:  
 
Trailer — https://youtu.be/YB -628MrGo4  
 
 
 
 

Bharat Ke Veer Initiative  
 
As part of its mission to fuse purpose with play, FAU -G: Domination is dedicated to Bharat Ke 
Veer, an initiative conceptualized by Shri Akshay Kumar, supporting the families of India’s 
Armed Forces martyrs.  
 
This initiative reinforces the commitment of India’s gaming industry to stand in solidarity with 
our security forces and brings a deep sense of national purpose to digital entertainment.
