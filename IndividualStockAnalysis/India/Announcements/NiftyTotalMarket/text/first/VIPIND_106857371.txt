VIP Industries Delivers 15% Revenue Growth in Q2 : Highest in over 2 Years  
• Revenue growth of 15% y -o-y, accelerating from 3% in Q1; targeting sustained double -digit 
growth  
• New, consumer -led product launches driving growth across VIP, Skybags and Aristocrat . ~50+ 
new ranges contributing to ~45% of Hard luggage and Backpack sales .  
• Sequential GM * improvement  from 39% in Q1’27 to 4 4% in Q2’27  
• EBITDA loss narrows to Rs 27 Cr from Rs 96 Cr in Q2 FY26; sharp focus  on break -even  
The Board of Directors of VIP Industries, at its meeting today, approved the financial results for the second 
quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026. VIP Indus tries’ consolidated income from opera tions 
for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 (Q2FY27) was up 15% to Rs 468 crore, compared with Rs 406 
crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The Company’s EBITDA loss narrowed to Rs 2 7 
crore ( -6%) from Rs 96 crore ( -24%) in Q2FY26.  
Revenue growth was led by the successful launch of new products. These new and innovative products, 
steeped in consumer research, have changed the face of VIP, Skybags and Aristocrat in the market.  
Sequential Gross margin improve ment  despite raw material inflation, supported by a strong focus on 
premiumisation. The Company remains sharply focused towards achieving break -even and a clear path to 
profitability.  
Atul Jain, Managing Director, VIP Industries, “Our mission is to make every journey better for Ind ians, and 
product is at the heart of it. The 15%  revenue  growth this quarter has been led by new and innovative 
products, steeped in consumer research, that are changing the face of VIP, Skybags and Aristocrat in the 
market. We are also moving our portfoli o up the price ladder – with premium launches across our brands . 
This has helped us steadily improve gross margins despite raw material inflation. With growth back on 
track, our focus is firmly on sustaining double -digit growth and charting a clear path to  break -even and 
profitability.”
