Sub:     Outcome of the Board Meeting held on 26th October 2026 – Incorporation of a Wholly Owned 
Subsidiary for setting up a Mineral Exchange  
Ref:       Regulation 30 read with Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board 
of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing 
Regulations”)  
 
The Company proposes setting up the Mineral Exchange through a subsidiary as per applicable laws and 
rules, which will offer delivery-based electronic trading in allowed minerals and other related areas. The 
designated regulatory authority Indian Bureau of Mines (IBM) has invited applications for registration of 
companies to establish and operate a Mineral Exchange in India and the last date for submission is 2nd 
November 2026, however the Company has applied for the extension of time for submission of the 
application. 
 
This is to inform that the Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today i.e. 26th October, 
2026, has, inter alia, considered and approved the incorporation of a wholly owned subsidiary company 
(Special Purpose Vehicle), with PTC India Limited as the promoter, for establishing and operating a Mineral 
Exchange  in India under the Mineral Exchange Rules, 2026, subject to receipt of time extension from IBM. 
The Board also approved equity investment of up to Rs. 65 crores in proposed subsidiary. 
 
The details as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read with the SEBI Master 
Circular are enclosed as Annexure-A . We shall inform further details in this regard from time to time in line 
with SEBI Listing Regulations. 
 
The Board Meeting commenced at 12: 15 p.m. and concluded at 12.58 pm. 
 
The above information will also be hosted on the website of the Company www.ptcindia.com .
