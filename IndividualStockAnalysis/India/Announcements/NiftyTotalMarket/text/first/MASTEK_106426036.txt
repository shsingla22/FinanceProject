Mastek Wins ‘Best Enterprise -Wide AI Transformation (Mid -sized Co.)’ at the 
Inaugural ET Making AI Work Awards 2025  
Mumbai, India, November  4, 2025 : Mastek (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted AI -first digital 
engineering and cloud transformation partner, is proud to announce that it has been recognized with 
the ‘Best Enterprise -Wide AI Transformation (Mid- sized Co.)’  award at the inaugural edition of The 
Economic Times Making AI Work Awards 2025 .  
This recognition reaffirms Mastek’s belief that true AI transformation begins from within. Through Mastek’s  ADOPT AI framework encompassing , AI for Technology, AI for Business and AI for Data, Mastek 
has successfully embedded AI across its core operations, strengthening its internal capabilities and 
setting a strong foundation for enterprise -wide transformation.  
“At Mastek, AI isn’t just a capability  we deliver to clients , it is something we live by,” said Umang 
Nahata, CEO,  Mastek . “We knew we had to be proactive to lead the market, make bold decisions, and 
create innovative solutions. We chose to be among the first to adopt AI internally across everything we do, and we  are proud to demonstrate that success back to our clients . This recognition from The 
Economic Times signifies Mastek’s Lead with AI vision to make AI purposeful, responsible  ethical, and 
outcome driven.”  
Mastek continues to be at the forefront with its “Lead with AI” approach, equipped over 3,600 employees with practical AI skills, fostering a culture of enterprise -wide innovation and digital maturity . 
Over the past year, Mastek has embedded AI across its core operations to drive measurable efficiency, 
scalability, and innovation. With enterprise -wide platforms like AI Amigo and ITSM Agents, Mastek has 
transformed workflows across engineering, HR, marketing, and managed ser vices. AI Amigo  delivers up 
to 30%  efficiency gains in software delivery and 20% faster requirement -to-deployment cycles, while 
Resolution.AI halves incident resolution time from 8 hours to 4 hours and Response.AI ensures 100% SLA 
compliance. Over 60% of internal workflows  including recruitment, sales, and IT operations  are now AI -
enabled, powered by tools such as Sales Copilot, Talent Genius, and Policy Helper. Collectively, these initiatives have delivered significant time savings, cost optimization, and productivity improvements, 
making Mastek truly AI -ready from within before taking its innovations to market.  
Presented by The Economic Times, the Making AI Work Awards spotlight leaders and change enablers 
who are transforming ambitious AI ideas beyond boardroom pilots and proofs of concept into real, 
responsible, and scalable AI solutions.
