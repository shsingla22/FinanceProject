PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Group and Hyatt Announce Plans for Two New Hyatt Regency Hotels  
in Bengaluru  
 
The two hotels, together offering 600 rooms, will be part of Brigade’s large -scale mixed -use developments in 
Bengaluru.  
 
Bengaluru (October 26, 2026)  – Brigade Group today announced the signing of management 
agreements with Hyatt Hotels Corporation for two new hotels in Bengaluru: Hyatt Regency 
Bengaluru at Brigade Global Park and Hyatt Regency Bengaluru at Brigade’s upcoming integrated 
township on Whitef ield-Kadugodi  Road. Together, the two hotels are expected to add 600 rooms 
to Brigade’s hotel portfolio in Bengaluru.  
 
Brigade Estates and Projects Private Limited, an entity of the Brigade Group,  signed a 
management agreement for Hyatt Regency Bengaluru at Brigade Global Park , which will be 
located within Brigade Group's 25 -acre integrated advanced engineering and business ecosystem. 
Planned with approximately 350 guestrooms and suites, the hotel will offer extensive meeting and 
event facilities totaling  approximately 25,000 square feet, alongside multiple dining destinations 
designed to cater to business and leisure travelers alike.  
 
Ananthay Properties Private Limited, an entity of the Brigade Group, signed a management 
agreement for Hyatt Regency Bengaluru on Whitefield -Kadugodi Road , which will be part of a 
premium mixed -use destination. The hotel is planned to feature approximately 250 guestrooms 
and suites, extensive food and beverage offerings, and a comprehensive meetings and events 
program, including a ballroom spanning more tha n 1,000 square meters alongside a large pre -
function area and additional meeting spaces.  
 
Located within two landmark mixed -use developments by Brigade Group, the hotels are expected 
to serve the growing demand from business travelers , meetings and events, social celebrations, 
and leisure travelers in Bengaluru, one of India's most dynamic economic centers.  
 
Both hotels are expected to feature a range of amenities, including an all -day dining restaurant, 
specialty restaurant, grab -and-go offering, rooftop bar, pool bar, fitness facilities and flexible event 
spaces, creating vibrant environments where guests ca n connect, collaborate and celebrate.  
 
“At Brigade, hospitality has always been an integral part of our landmark projects," said Nirupa 
Shankar, Jt. Managing Director, Brigade Enterprises Limited . “The signing of two Hyatt Regency 
hotels at our upcoming mixed -use developments represents another important step in 
strengthening our hospitality portfolio in Bengaluru. Both hotels will be part of larger integrated 
developments that bring together mult iple asset classes, reinstating Brigade’s pioneering approach 
to creating destinations that add long -term value to the city. We are pleased to continue our 
association with Hyatt through its globally recognised Hyatt Regency brand and bring it to these 
two significant Brigade developments.”  
 
"Bengaluru continues to demonstrate strong demand across business travel, meetings and events, 
making it a strategic market for Hyatt's growth in India," said Vikas Chawla, President, India & 
 
 
Southwest Asia, Hyatt. "The signings of Hyatt Regency Bengaluru at Brigade Global Park and 
Hyatt Regency Bengaluru on Whitefield -Kadugodi  Road reflect our continued commitment to 
expanding Hyatt's brand presence in high -growth destinations across the country. We are pleased 
to collaborate with Brigade Group on these developments and look forward to welcoming guests 
with the thoughtful servi ce and experiences that define the Hyatt Regency brand."  
 
These projects further deepen the relationship between Hyatt and Brigade Group, underscoring a 
shared commitment to developing high -quality hospitality experiences in key growth markets 
across India.  
 
The term "Hyatt" is used in this release for convenience to refer to Hyatt Hotels Corporation and/or one or 
more of its affiliates.
