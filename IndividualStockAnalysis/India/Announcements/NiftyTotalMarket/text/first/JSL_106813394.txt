Jindal Stainless' Jajpur Unit Wins CII National Award for Excellence in Water 
Management 2026  
• National recognition celebrates innovative water management, circular resource use, and 
sustainability -driven operations.  
 
 
Jajpur , October 10, 2026 : The Jajpur unit of Jindal Stainless, India’s leading stainless steel  
manufacturer, has been conferred the CII National Award for Excellence in Water Management 2026 
as the Winner in the ‘Within the Fence’ category, recognising its exemplary efforts in water 
conservation, resource efficiency, and sustainable manufacturing p ractices.  
Presented by the CII -Triveni Water Institute, the award honours organisations that demonstrate 
excellence in water stewardship through innovative practices, operational efficiency, and sustainable 
water management. For over a decade, the awards have recogn ised industry leaders contributing to a 
more water secure future.  
The recognition reflects Jindal Stainless’ continued focus on embedding sustainability into its 
operations at the Jajpur  unit, the company’s flagship manufacturing hub and one of the world’s largest 
stainless steel production facilities.  
The Jajpur  facility has adopted a range of initiatives focused on responsible resource management and 
environmental stewardship. A key highlight of its sustainability journey is its 100% internal water 
recycling system, enabling efficient water use while reducing de pendence on freshwater resources. 
Over the years, the plant has also implemented several sustainability focused measures, including 
renewable energy projects such as floating and rooftop solar installations, circular economy initiatives, 
and advanced proce ss optimisation to enhance operational performance while conserving natural 
resources.  
Speaking on the achievement,  Unit Head, Jajpur, Jindal Stainless, Deepak Agrawal, said, “This award 
is a testament to our unwavering commitment to responsible manufacturing and water stewardship. 
At Jindal Stainless, sustainability is deeply integrated into the way we operate. The recognition reflects 
the collective efforts of our teams in dri ving innovation, resource efficiency, and environmental 
excellence."  
The Jajpur  unit plays a critical role in Jindal Stainless' sustainability agenda, with a strong focus on 
water stewardship, energy conservation, waste utilisation, and circular economy practices. Through 
continuous investments in technology, digital transformation, and sustainable operations, the 
company remains committed to creating long  term value while safeguarding natural resources for 
future generations.  
About Jindal Stainless  
India’s leading stainless steel  manufacturer, Jindal Stainless, had an annual turnover of INR 42,955 crore (USD 
4.86 billion) in FY26 and an annual melt capacity of 4.2 million tonnes. It has 16 stainless steel manufacturing 
and processing facilities in India and abroad, including in Sp ain and Indonesia, and a worldwide network in 12 
 
 
 
2 countries, as of March 2026. In India, there are ten sales offices and six service centres, as of March 2026. The 
company’s product range includes stainless steel slabs, blooms, coils, plates, sheets, precision strips, wire rods, 
rebars, blade steel, and c oin blanks.  
 
Jindal Stainless relies on its integrated operations to enhance its cost competitiveness and operational efficiency. 
Founded in 1970, Jindal Stainless continues to be inspired by a vision for innovation and enriching lives and is 
committed to social respon sibility.  
 
Jindal Stainless remains focused on a greener and sustainable future. The company manufactures stainless steel 
using electric arc furnace, a process that significantly reduces greenhouse gas emissions and allows for 
recyclability of scrap without compromis ing on quality.
