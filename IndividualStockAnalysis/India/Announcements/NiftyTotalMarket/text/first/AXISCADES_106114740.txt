AXISCADES ’ Board Approves the Appoint ment of Alfonso Martinez as 
the New Group Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director  
Bangalore, India – January 15, 2025:  AXISCADES Technologies Limited, a leading 
provider of Deep Tech and Engineering Services, is delighted to announce  that the 
Board of Directors of the Company, at their  meeting held on 15th January  2025,  
approved the appointment of Alfonso Martinez as its new ‘Group Chief Executive 
Officer and Managing Director ’, effective  20th January 2025 . 
With over 25 years of global experience in Engineering and Technology services, 
Alfonso Martinez has consistently demonstrated his ability to lead complex, multi -
disciplinary teams and drive transformative growth. He joins AXISCADES at a 
pivotal time as the company aims to expand its global footprint and enhance it s 
position as a Leader in Engineering Services , Defense and Deep Tech . 
Proven Leadership and Track Record 
Prior to joining AXISCADES, Martinez held several prominent  leadership roles a t 
globally renowned organizations:  
•At Quest Global, he served as Global Business Head for Transportation and
as President/Managing Partner, driving strategic initiatives and growth across
verticals.
•During his 20 -year tenure with Altran Group (now Capgemini), he discharged
prominent roles,  including:
oManaging Director of Altran Technologies Spain
oCEO of Altran Middle East
oGroup Senior Vice -President for Aerospace, Defense, Railway, and
Life Sciences
Throughout his career, Martinez has been credited with implementing forward -
looking strategies  and business transformations , fostering innovation, and delivering 
sustainable and profitable growth.  
The Board of Directors , while announcing the appointment,  expressed their 
delight and confidence in the new leadership, stating: "The appointment of 
Alfonso Martinez marks a significant milestone in AXISCADES’ journey. Alfonso is 
not just a seasoned leader; he is a visionary who understands the intricacies of  
Engineering Services and Deep Tech landscape and possesses  proven ability to drive 
innovation and profitable growth. His expertise  and network of relationships built 
over the last two decades , aligns perfectly with our long -term goal to enlarge  our 
global footprint, strengthen our market presence, and set new benchmarks in 
 
innovation and customer satisfaction. As we embark on this new chapter, we are 
confident that his leadership will inspire the organization to reach greater heights."  
 
Mr. Alfonso Martinez, the newly appointed Group CEO and MD, shared his 
thoughts and vision for the company: "I am deeply honoured  and humbled to join 
AXISCADES at such a pivotal moment in its growth journey. The company’s 
commitment to innovation, customer -centric solutions, and operational excellence is 
truly inspiring. My vision is t o build on this strong foundation, leveraging the 
incredible talent within the organization to create transformative solutions that 
address the evolving needs of our clients. Together, we will enhance our technological 
capabilities, expand our global reach , drive transformational growth and value 
creation across all dimensions of our business. This is a unique opportunity, and I 
look forward to collaborating with the team to shape an exciting future for 
AXISCADES."  
 
Commenting on the appointment, Dr. Sampath Ravinarayanan, Director and 
Chief Mentor, remarked: "Alfonso’s appointment comes at a crucial juncture as 
AXISCADES is poised to scale its operations and capabilities to meet the growing 
demands of the industry. Alfonso’s track record of driving impactful results and his 
deep understanding of global markets  and relationships built over decades  make him 
an ideal leader to take the company forward. His ability to marry strategic vision with 
execution excellence will be instrumental in shaping the company’s next phase of 
growth. As a mentor, I see Alfonso’s leadership style as one , that will not only drive 
business success but also inspire innovation and collaboration across the organization. 
We are excited about the possibilities ahead."  
 
About AXISCADES Technologies Ltd.  
AXISCADES is a leading, end -to-end technology and engineering solutions provider 
aiding the creation of innovative, sustainable, and safer products worldwide. 
Headquartered in Bangalore with subsidiaries in the USA, UK, Canada, Germany, 
India, and China, a nd offices in Germany, France, Denmark, USA, and Canada. 
AXISCADES has a diverse team of over 3200 professionals working across 17 
locations across North America, Europe, the UK, and Asia -Pacific, striving to reduce 
the program risk and time to market.  
 
AXISCADES has long term relationships with Defence Forces, MoD, Defence Labs 
and PSUs along with global OEMs. We are Designated Offset partner to global 
OEMs on various Aerospace and Defence programs. AXISCADES possesses deep 
domain experience in Avionics,  Electronic Warfare, C4I2, Radars, Drones, Anti -
Drone Systems, Test Solutions, GSE and GHE. We have executed several innovative 
in-house projects and partnered Solutions for Land, Naval, Aerospace and Homeland 
Security.  
 
 
 
 
The comprehensive portfolio of solutions covers the complete product development 
lifecycle from concept evaluation to manufacturing support and certification for 
Fortune 500 Companies in the Aerospace, Defence, Heavy Engineering, Automotive, 
& Industrial Product industries.
