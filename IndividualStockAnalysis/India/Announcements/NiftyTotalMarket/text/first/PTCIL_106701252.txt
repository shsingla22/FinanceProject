Lucknow, India — 17 July 2026  
PTC Industries Limited, a leading advanced manufacturing company serving critical and super -critical 
aerospace, defence  and strategic applications, today announced that it has received a landmark 
order from BrahMos Aerospace Private Limited  for the development, integration and supply of a 
strategic missile sub -system  for the BrahMos programme.  
This order represents a defin ing milestone in PTC’s journey. It is the Company’s first major order of 
this nature in systems -level integration  and marks an important strategic move further downstream 
in the aerospace and defence value chain.  
With this development, PTC begins a new chapter in its evolution — from being a trusted supplier of 
critical materials, precision castings and engineered components to becoming a partner for high -
value systems and sub -systems  for advanced defence and aeros pace platforms.  
A Mission -Critical Defence Assembly  
The order involves the development and integration of a mission -critical structural assembly for one 
of India’s most important strategic defence platforms. Without disclosing programme -specific 
nomenclatu re or configuration details, the assembly is required to perform under demanding 
structural, thermal and dynamic conditions associated with supersonic applications.  
Its development and integration require a combination of high -precision manufacturing, spec ialised 
joining, hermetic integrity, intricate control assemblies, high -accuracy final integration and rigorous 
inspection discipline to ensure reliability, alignment, balance and structural integrity.  
The award of this order reflects BrahMos Aerospace’s c onfidence in PTC’s engineering depth, 
manufacturing discipline, quality systems and execution capability.  
PTC ONE™ — From Melt to Mission™  
This order strengthens PTC ONE™ — From Melt to Mission™ , the Group’s integrated manufacturing 
doctrine for transformi ng advanced materials into mission -critical components, assemblies and 
systems.  
Until now, PTC ONE™ has been expressed through the Group’s growing capabilities in critical 
materials, Titanium and Superalloy manufacturing, precision investment castings, spe cial processes 
and high -precision machining. This BrahMos order adds a new and important layer to the PTC ONE™ 
journey — systems and sub -systems integration . 
The order demonstrates that PTC’s integrated manufacturing system is not limited to producing 
individual materials and components. It can now move further downstream into complex assemblies 
where multiple materials, components, processes, inspections and functional requirements must 
come together with precision and reliability.  
This represents a signif icant expansion of PTC’s role in the value chain and a natural progression of 
the Company’s long -term strategy: to build deeper, more integrated capability for critical aerospace, 
defence and strategic applications.

Commercial Validation of a Long -Term St rategy  
Over the past several years, PTC has been building an integrated advanced manufacturing capability 
spanning critical materials, precision components, machining, special processes and assembly 
capability.  
These investments have been guided by a clear  strategic objective — to participate in a larger share 
of the aerospace and defence value chain by moving from individual manufacturing processes 
towards integrated, mission -critical solutions.  
The BrahMos order is an important validation of this strategy . It demonstrates that PTC’s capabilities 
are now being recognised not only for material and component manufacturing, but also for complex 
integration work required in strategic defence platforms.  
For shareholders, this order represents a significant devel opment. It opens a new avenue of growth 
for PTC in systems and sub -systems, increases the Company’s relevance to strategic programmes, 
and strengthens its positioning as an integrated advanced manufacturing partner for India’s 
aerospace and defence ecosyst em.

Over the past several years, we have been building PTC ONE™ — our ‘From Melt to 
Mission™’ manufacturing system — with the belief that India must develop deeper, more

It validates our long -term strategy of building an integrated enterprise capable of serving

Strategic Significance  
This order represents another important milestone in PTC’s evolution from a precision components 
manufacturer into an integrated advanced manufacturing company serving aerospace, defence, 
space and strategic sectors.  
As defence and aerospace platforms beco me more complex, customers increasingly require partners 
who can provide not only individual components, but integrated assemblies and sub -systems with 
strong process control, quality assurance and execution discipline.
