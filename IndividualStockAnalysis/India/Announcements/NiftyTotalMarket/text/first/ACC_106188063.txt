Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 and other applicable provisions of SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing 
Regulation”), we wish to inform you that based on the recommendation of 
Nomination & Remuneration Committee, the Board of Directors (“Board”) of ACC 
Limited (the Company) at its meeting held today i.e. 28th  March 2025 has inter-alia   
approved the following :- 
 
1. Cessation of Mr. Ajay Kapur (DIN: 03096416) as Whole Time Director & Chief 
Executive Officer (Key Managerial Personnel) of the Company with effect from 
the close of business hours on 31st March 2025;

Wholetime Director and Chief Executive Officer (Key Managerial Personnel) of 
the Company for a term of three (3) years with effect from 1st April 2025; 
 
3. Appointment of Mr. Rakesh Tiwary as Chief Financial Officer (Key Managerial 
Personnel) of the Company with effect from 1st April 2025; 
 
4. Cessation of Mr. Vinod Bahety as Chief Financial Officer of the Company effective 
from closure of the business hours on 31st March 2025; 
 
5. Changes in the Senior Management Personnel (SMP) of the Company as under: 
 
a. Appointment of Ms. Madhavi Isanaka, Chief Digital Officer as SMP 
effective from 1st  April 2025 in place of Mr. Hemal Shah, who will cease to 
be SMP with effect from the close of business hours on 31st March 2025. 
 
b. Appointment of Mr. Vaibhav Dixit, Head Manufacturing as SMP effective 
from 1st April 2025 in place of Mr. Sukuru Ramarao, who will cease to be 
SMP with effect from the close of business hours on 31st March 2025.
