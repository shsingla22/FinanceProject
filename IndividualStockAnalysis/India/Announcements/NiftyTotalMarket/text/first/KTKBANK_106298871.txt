PRESS RELEASE Date: 14-07-2025 
Karnataka Bank appoints Veteran Banker Mr. Raghavendra S Bhat as its 
Managing Director & CEO 
Leading south Indian private bank -Karnataka Bank, is pleased to announce the 
appointment of Mr. Raghavendra S Bhat as the Managing Director & CEO of the 
Bank. The appointment is effective from 16th July 2025 for a period of three months. 
The appointment is subject to approval by shareholders at the ensuing Annual 
General Meeting or within a period of 3 months as per SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 
2015 from the date of assuming charge, whichever is earlier. Karnataka Bank is 
confident that Mr. Raghavendra S Bhat's expertise and leadership will drive the 
bank towards continued growth and success. 
Mr. Raghavendra S Bhat has previously held key positions including COO at 
Karnataka Bank Ltd for nearly 4 decades. 
Chairman Mr. P Pradeep Kumar Said, "We are happy to welcome back 
Mr. Raghavendra S Bhat to Karnataka Bank. His wealth of experience and expertise 
will be invaluable in driving our continued growth and success. We look forward to 
working with him in delivering value to our customers and shareholders." 
Mr. Raghavendra S Bhat said, "I am proud to join Karnataka Bank as Managing 
Director & CEO. With over a century of trust and goodwill built by the Bank, I look 
forward to working with all the stakeholders to further build and grow on that 
strong foundation."
