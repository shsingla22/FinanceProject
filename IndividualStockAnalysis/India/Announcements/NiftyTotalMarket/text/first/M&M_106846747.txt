Commenting on Q2 F27 performance  
 
Dr. Anish Shah, Group CEO & Managing Director , M&M Ltd. , said, "Our Q2 F27 Consolidated PAT 
growth of 25%  reflects the resilience  of our group  and underlines the disciplined execution across 
businesses. Auto continued to grow on the back of strong consumer  demand, while Farm experienced a 
marginal decline on account of the festive season shift.  Mahindra Finance maintained its growth trajectory , 
TechM displayed acceleration in  its transformation journey , and our Growth Gems continued their 
momentum.  Despite the uneven  macroeconomic environment, the group remains focused on  laying the 
foundation for consistent profitable growth .” 
Mr. Rajesh Jejurikar, Executive Director  & CEO (Auto and Farm Sector) , M&M Ltd . said, “Auto 
Business delivered strong performance while Farm business continues to demonstrate strong resilience in 
Q2F27. SUV volume grew 26% YoY and retained market leadership in revenue market share. EV product 
range continue to show momentum with main taining #1 position in revenue market share for the 1 st half 
of the year. Auto consolidated PBIT margin grew to 8.8%, 110 bps up QoQ and Farm consolidated PBIT 
margin grew 20 bps QoQ despite significant hike in commodity prices.”  
 
Mr. Amar jyoti  Barua , Group Chief Financial Officer, M&M Ltd . said, “Q2 F27 reflects the strength  of 
our diversified portfolio . Our businesses are demonstrating strong operational stewardship and resilience 
despite significant commodity and geopolitical headwinds . The robust balanc e sheet and cash generation 
also provides us the flexibility to invest in future opportunities and deliver sustainable value.”
