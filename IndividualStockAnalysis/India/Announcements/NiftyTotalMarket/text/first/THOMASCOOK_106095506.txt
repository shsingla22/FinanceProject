Sterling Unveils An Enchanting  Oasis Amidst the Wilderness: Sterling Bagh Ranthambore  
December 2 6, 2024 –  Sterling Holiday Resorts proudly announces the opening of Sterling Bagh 
Ranthambore, its 10th wildlife resort and 7th property in Rajasthan. Nestled in the heart of Sawai 
Madhopur, this quaint  retreat redeﬁnes wildlife getaways by blending the raw beauty of the jungle 
with reﬁned comfort,  cultural immersion, and bespoke curated  experiences.  
Located just minutes from Gates 1 to 5 of the renowned Ranthambore National Park, Sterling Bagh 
Ranthambore’s tranquil courtyard, featuring a n inviting pool surrounded by swaying palms, evokes 
the charm of a Middle Eastern oasis. Guests are welcomed into tastefully  designed accommodation s 
that combine nature- inspired elegance with modern conveniences. Suites feature separate living 
spaces & scenic views &  pool -facing rooms bring the outdoors closer for a peaceful, immersive stay.  
“We are thrilled to launch Sterling Bagh Ranthambore, our 7th resort in Rajasthan. This exclusive 
retreat embodies Sterling’s vision of creating unique wildlife experiences, combi ning immersive 
storytelling, cultural richness, and Sterling’s signature hospitality to redeﬁne wildlife getaways.” said 
Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director & CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited . 
Also, speaking on the same were Satinder Singh Chandel and Amit Aggarwal, Owners Maxtra 
Hospitality  Pvt. Ltd “We are delighted to collaborate with Sterling Holiday Resorts and bring out the 
best of this iconic wilderness destination through its design, service style and experiences. We are conﬁdent ab out the association and the expertise of Sterling in the Jungle Resorts category. Coupled 
with their network strength in leisure segment, we look forward to making our Sterling Bagh Ranthambore the sought -after destination for travellers”.  
The resort’s si gnature restaurant, Sawai , treats guests to a culinary journey through Rajasthan’s 
authentic ﬂavors. Local delicacies like Jungli Maas, Ker Sangri, and Panchkutta ki Sabzi complemented by dishes crafted with Sawai Madhopur’s famed guavas. The menu also features popular Indian &  
international options, served in a wildlife -inspired ambi ence that enhances the dining experience.  
Sterling Bagh Ranthambore’s bar combines the timeless charm of a classic British pub with a creative local twist. Alongside its curat ed selection of ﬁne spirits and signature cocktails, the bar oﬀers fusion 
creations inspired by the region’s ﬂavors , like  ‘Toddy Fusion ’, which reimagines the traditional local 
brew with a contemporary ﬂair, blending it with herbs, spices, &  modern mix ology techniques. The 
rich wooden interiors & vintage decor  makes it the perfect spot to unwind after a day of exploration.  
The resort’s Discovery Central  brings the region’s rich history and folklore to life with a journey 
through the fables of Ranthambore. Guests can delve into captivating tales, such as Raja Hammir 
Dev’s legendary defenc e of the Ranthambore Fort and the mystical blessings of the Trinetra  Ganesh 
Temple. Storytelling sessions and curated activities celebrate the vibrant heritage of the area, 
oﬀering an enriching experience for all.  
The resort oﬀers unique experiences and a host of tailored activities for wildlife enthusiasts and cultural explorers alike:  
Guided Safari Rides  to witness Bengal tigers and other wildlife in their natural habitat.  
Bird-Watching Tours and Treks  to explore the diverse ﬂora and fauna of Ranthambore.  
 
Heritage Visits to iconic sites such as the UNESCO World Heritag e-listed Ranthambore Fort, the 
Trinetra Ganesh Temple, and the serene Padam Talao lake.  
Evenings are magical, with bonﬁres under starlit skies, cultural programs, and serene walks along the 
expansive lawns. These spaces also provide an ideal setting for t hematic destination weddings, 
corporate events, and intimate celebrations, blending natural beauty with impeccable hospitality.
