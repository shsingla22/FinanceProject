4. Brief profile  
(in case of Appointment)  Keshav will lead the development and execution of a 
comprehensive procurement strategy that aligns with 
our Business Strategy focusing on cost efficiency, 
quality excellence, and adherence to global standards. 
He will oversee the creation and implementation of 
multi-year category roadmaps, and a strong pipeline of 
sourcing initiatives aimed at driving long-term value. 
Additionally, Keshav will concentrate on establishing 
and maintaining a high-performing supplier base—both 
domestically and internationally—to support our 
expanding business with optimized costs and superior 
products. 
 
Keshav holds a B.Tech. in Chemical Engineering from 
the Institute of Chemical Technology (ICT) and a Ph.D. in 
Chemical Engineering from Eindhoven University of 
Technology (TU/e), The Netherlands. He has served as a 
Research Scholar with the Reaction Engineering Group 
at Washington University in St. Louis, USA. Keshav 
brings with him an impressive two-decade career at 
BASF, where he held several strategic roles across 
different regions. Most recently, he served as Director – 
Strategy & Marketing Petrochemicals, Asia Pacific, 
based in Hong Kong.
