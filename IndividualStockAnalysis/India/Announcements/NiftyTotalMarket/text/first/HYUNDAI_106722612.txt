 c. A brief on types of customers:
   Customers of Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) include individuals and entities who purchase vehicles through 
HMIL’s authorized dealer network across India, as well as those engaging directly with HMIL via its direct-to-customer 
(D2C) platforms. This customer base comprises retail buyers for new & pre-owned cars, fleet operators and institutional 
clients seeking personal, commercial, or organizational mobility solutions. Additionally, it includes those who utilize 
HMIL’s authorized service centers for after-sales services, genuine parts and technical support.
