Sub.: Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Coal India Limited 
(CIL) and Damodar Valley Corporation (DVC) 
 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Reference the captioned subject, kindly note that Coal India Limited (CIL) and 
Damodar Valley Corporation (DVC) have executed a Non-Binding 
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on 21.04.2025 at, Kolkata, with an 
intent of setting up 2×800 MW Ultra Supercritical units as part of Brownfield 
expansion of existing Chandrapura Thermal Power Station (CTPS) in 
Jharkhand. The MoU also includes provisions to explore various 
opportunities for jointly developing Thermal Power Projects and Renewable 
Energy Projects, with or without storage, to meet the growing power demand 
in the DVC valley region. 
 
This is for your information and records please.
