Press Release  
Sterling Launches Sterling Rampath Ayodhya  
– A Gateway to Devotion & Discovery  
• Enters 18th state - Uttar Pradesh with Ayodhya  
• 67th Property  in the  57th Destination  
• Sterling’s 11th Spiritual Location  
Chennai, 29th Sept 2025 : Sterling Holiday Resorts today announced the opening of Sterling 
Rampath Ayodhya , its 67th property and 57th destination, in the sacred city of Lord Ram. 
Strategically located just off the ceremonial Ram Path, the hotel offers seamless access to 
the iconic Ram Mandir, making it an ideal retreat for pilgrims, families, and cultural 
travellers.  
With this milestone launch, Sterling strengthens its presence in India’s fast -growing spiritual 
tourism segment, with Sterling Rampath Ayodhya  becoming the brand’s 11th spiritual  
destination . The property reaffirms Sterling’s vision of creating soulful journeys that 
combine devotion with comfort, designed for the rising number of travellers seeking 
spiritual and cultural immersion across the country.  
The hotel features 35 thoughtfully designed rooms, including Cocoon Doubles, Deluxe, and 
Classic categories, all crafted to provide serenity and comfort after darshan and temple 
visits. Dining is anchored by The Divine , a pure -veg multi -cuisine restaurant offering sattvic -
inspired meals, with Jain options available on request —aligned with Ayodhya’s spiritual 
ethos.  
“Ayodhya is at the heart of India’s spiritual and cultural renaissance. Sterling Rampath 
Ayodhya is more than a hotel — it is a gateway to devotion and discovery , serving guests 
who arrive with faith, families, and a sense of cultural curiosity. This milestone opening also 
marks another significant step in our mission to deliver transformational travel 
experiences  across India ,” said Vikram Lalvani, MD & CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts . 
Adding to this, Rishi Uppal , Director Kal yan KK Hotels India Pvt Ltd. said: “ Ayodhya is 
emerging as one of India’s most important spiritual destinations. Our vision was to create a 
property that not only serves pilgrims with comfort and care but also honours the cultural 
fabric of the city. In Sterling, we found a partner who share s this vision —bringing operational 
depth, brand trust, and guest -centric hospitality that ensures every visitor leaves with a truly 
blessed experience. ” 
With recent openings in Guruvayur, Vellore and Rudraprayag, Sterling continues to expand 
its footprint across sacred geographies, building a transformative travel narrative where 
tradition, devotion, and hospitality meet.
