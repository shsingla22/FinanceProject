‘Bioyug on Wheels’ rolls into Kochi to spotlight PLA Innovation at KPLEX Expo 2025  
 
(BCML’s  nationwide  mobile  campaign  arrives  in Kerala  to showcase  PLA bioplastics  and 
drive awareness on circular economy solutions at KPLEX Expo 2025)  
 
Kochi, September 14, 2025 – Balrampur Chini Mills Ltd (BCML) continues to drive its impactful 
nationwide outreach initiative, ‘Bioyug on Wheels’ , with its latest stop in Kochi, Kerala , during the 
prestigious  KPLEX  Expo  2025 – International  Plastics  Exhibition  & Conference , held  from September 
11th  to 14th . This mobile  awareness  campaign  is a cornerstone  of BCML’s  broader  mission  to promote 
circular economy principles and mainstream the adoption of bio -based innovations such as PLA 
(polylactic acid) bioplast ics across India.  
Since its successful launch in Mumbai in May 2025, Bioyug on Wheels has travelled across key 
regions —including cities in Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, and Gujarat —engaging thousands of 
stakeholders through a series of immersive, knowledge -driven rich experien ces. These include live 
demonstrations, interactive exhibits, expert dialogues, and collaborative sessions with academia, 
industry, and policy influencers.  
 
The Kochi edition marks a key moment in the journey of Bioyug on Wheels, bringing the mobile 
exhibition  into the heart  of Kerala’s  innovative  landscape.  At KPLEX,  a major  platform  for materials 
and technology, Bioyug is sharing new possibilities in climate -friendly materials like PLA 
bioplastics —highlighting their role in shaping a more sustainable future across sectors like 
packaging, textiles, and agriculture.  
 
Key Highlights  of Bioyug  on Wheels’  Kochi  Edition  at KPLEX  Expo:  
 
• Institutional Engagement:  Representatives from the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), as 
well as the Presidents of the Telangana, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh Plastic Associations 
respectively visited the Bioyug on Wheels pavilion, showing strong institutional interest in 
sustainable material alternatives.  
• Industry Participation:  More than 250 industry professionals visited the mobile unit on the 
second day alone, underscoring growing momentum for bio -based solutions in the South 
Indian plastics and manufacturing ecosystem.  
• Interactive Format:  The mobile unit’s engaging format —featuring PLA samples and technical 
showcases attracted manufacturers, entrepreneurs, policy advocates, and academia alike.  
Speaking  about  the Kochi  edition,  Ms. Shweta  Suryawanshi,  Business  Development  Head,  BCML  PLA 
Division , said:  “Kerala  is home  to a fast-evolving  ecosystem  of eco-conscious  consumers  and forward - 
looking enterprises. The KPLEX Expo serves as a strategic platform to engage with key industry 
stakeholders,  explore  the transformative potential  of PLA bioplastics,  and spotlight practical,  scalable 
solutions for a more sustainable future. This momentum is further reinforced by Kerala High Court’s 
mandated ban on select single -use plastic (SUP) items, set to take effect on October 2, 2025. The ban 
includes items like plastic water bottles, so ft drink bottles, plates, cups, straws, and bakery boxes — 
particularly in hilly tourist destinations, official functions, and eateries across the state. As regulatory 
landscapes evolve a nd market demand for eco -friendly alternatives intensifies, Bioyug on Wheels 
serves  as a vital  interface  between  innovation  and implementation.  Our goal  is to foster  industry -wide 
collaboration, accelerate adoption of compostable materials, and support the larger shift toward a 
circular bioeconomy.”  
Following its successful showcase in Kochi, Bioyug on Wheels will continue its journey across Southern 
India, engaging a wider spectrum of communities, institutions, and enterprises committed to building 
a greener, more sustainable future.
