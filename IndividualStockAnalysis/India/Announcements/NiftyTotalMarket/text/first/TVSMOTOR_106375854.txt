TVS MOTOR COMPANY LAUNCHES TVS KING DELUXE 
PLUS CNG 3 -WHEELER IN TANZANIA  
 
Dar Es Salaam , September 1 7th, 2025 : TVS Motor Company  – a global leader in two 
and three- wheeler manufacturing – together with its distributor Car & General  
launch ed the TVS King Deluxe Plus CNG  3 Wheeler  in Tanzania. The launch 
announcement was conducted at Dar Es Salaam  Gymkhana Sports Club in the 
presence of dealers, retail finance partners and fleet owners.  This new CNG 3 Wheeler 
is designed to address the dynamic requirements of urban and semi -urban mobility.  
 
The TVS King Deluxe Plus CNG 3  Wheeler combines robust design, performance, 
and reliability with cutting -edge technology. With this launch, TVS Motor Company 
underscores its commitment to providing Tanzanian customers with affordable, durable, and fuel -efficient mobility solutions. The l egacy of “Mfalme Wao” (“Their 
King”), inspired by the enduring roots and heritage of kinship and leadership, acts as an inspiration for the company. This approach reinforces the product TVS King’s identity as a trusted 3  Wheeler segment leader while ensuring a strong connection 
with customers, rooted deeply in the spirit of leadership and unity.  
 The New TVS King Deluxe Plus CNG offers premium features like King size driver seat, Strong Chassis & B ody with H eavy duty pillars, Tubeless tyres with G old 
coloured rims, 5 KG CNG tank with 8 Litre petrol fuel tank, stronger & longer steering column, large foldable mirrors.  
 Commenting on the launch, Mr. Rahul Nayak, Senior Vice President –  International 
Business, TVS Motor Company , said, “ Tanzania being a priority market for us, and 
the launch of TVS King Deluxe Plus CNG  3 Wheeler  highlights our long- term 
commitment to delivering innovative and durable products . This 3 Wheeler will be 
setting a new benchmark in Tanzania, as there is a growing shift towards affordable 
and sustainable mobility solutions. With rising fuel  prices and an increasing need for 
efficient public transport, this vehicle will support in delivering both economic and environmental advantages to drivers and fleet owners.  As we continue to expand our 
presence here, we find Tanzania as a hub for opportunity and growth. ”      
 TVS King Deluxe Plus CNG 3  Wheeler has an unmatched combination of smart 
features, comfort, ergonomics and safety . The vehicle will empower businesses and 
also improve  everyday life of operators. The product will be available across Dar Es 
Salam, Tanzania with our 33 dealers & sales touch points 50 service centres  to deliver 
the vehicles on time with satisfactory experience.  
TVS Motor is globally recognis ed for its engineering, innovation, and quality, with 
products sold in more than 80 countries across Asia, Africa, Latin America and Europe.
