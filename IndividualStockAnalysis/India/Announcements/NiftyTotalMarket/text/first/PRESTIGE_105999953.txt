Prestige Group Launches a Luxury Development in Whitefield with INR 1100 Crore 
Sales Potential 
Prestige Group, the country’s leading real estate developer with a portfolio of more than 305 
delivered properties across 10 cities, has announced the launch of its exquisite residential 
project in Whitefield - Prestige Pine Forest . This development is designed to offer an 
unparalleled living experience, enhanced by meticulously planned green spaces and 
exceptional amenities. 
 
Prestige Pine Forest , located in prime Whitefield has excellent connectivity & it is a few 
minutes away from the IT Parks. IT parks in Whitefield, ITPL, Outer Ring Road and Sarjapur 
Road are accessible via arterial roads, which makes commuting to work a breeze. It provides 
an ideal location for professionals seeking proximity to their workplace. Moreover, more than 
50 esteemed educational institutions are conveniently located just a short drive away. 
 
Boasting architectural brilliance and meticulous craftsmanship, Prestige Pine Forest is the 
epitome of uber living with 316 units in 4 towers nestled in a rich landscape. The development 
is spread across  9 acres of land. Each tower is furnished with spacious conference rooms and 
well-equipped workstations in the lobbies, providing a convenient and productive environment 
for working from home while also allowing you to prioritize quality time with your family. 
 
Each residence is equipped with four balconies providing picturesque views. The residential 
towers are encompassed by meticulously landscaped grounds and expansive open areas. These 
verdant spaces are adorned with lifestyle amenities such as a Pet Park, a Fitness Trail, and 
more. This architectural approach exemplifies the Prestige Group's steadfast commitment to 
cultivating a healthful and invigorating residential environment. 
 
Prestige Pine Forest  offers diverse residences, ranging from 2442 sqft to expansive 3556 
sqft units . These residences encompass 3- and 4-bedroom apartments, complete with the 
option of a family room and spacious decks and balconies that will provide residents with 
panoramic city views. Notably, each residence is designed without shared walls, ensuring a 
private living environment. 
 
Commenting on the launch, Mr. Praveer Shrivastava, Sr. Executive VP- Residential, 
Prestige Group  said, “We are delighted to unveil Prestige Group’s latest architectural marvel 
that exemplifies our commitment to luxury, comfort, and convenience. This project redefines 
the benchmark for quality and craftsmanship and is a testament to our relentless commitment 
to delivering experiences. Boasting a prime location, cutting-edge design, and an array of top-
notch amenities, Prestige Pine Forest is poised to establish itself as the most coveted residential 
community in Bangalore.”  
 
Prestige Pine Forest’s  richly landscaped foliage ascends to a raised level offering undisrupted 
views of the greenery. It has around 7 Acres of open and green spaces , providing its residents 
a serene refuge away from the hustle and bustle of daily life. Prestige Pine Forest is poised to 
become a landmark development in the city, with its delectable array of luxury leisure amenities
