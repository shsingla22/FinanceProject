 PRESS RELEASE 
 
Cyient Establishes Intelligent Engineering Solutions to 
Accelerate Technology-led Growth; Launches CYiNGINE 
Integrated business unit brings together data, domain, and business context- powered by a 
platform-led AI operating model- to drive outcomes across lifecycle engineering   
Hyderabad, October 6, 2026:  Cyient Limited, a global Lifecycle Engineering Services 
company, announced the formation of a new, integrated business unit designed to 
accelerate its technology-led growth: Intelligent Engineering Solutions (IES). IES strengthens 
the company's ability to translate today's technological capabilities into customer value 
while building the technologies that will shape tomorrow.  
IES combines data, deep domain knowledge, and business context across lifecycle 
engineering through a platform-led AI operating model anchored on CYiNGINE, Cyient's 
lifecycle engineering intelligence platform, to connect technology expertise with clear, 
consistent, and measurable client outcomes worldwide.  
The unit will deliver solutions across the lifecycle, from planning and design to operations, 
including managing customers' AI stacks. Three reusable, AI-enabled playbooks cover the 
Engineering, Service, and Quality and Regulatory lifecycles, supported by data engineering, 
analytics, and AI-enabled software development. CYiNGINE provides a common platform, 
data foundation, and outcome measurement framework.   
Business Outcomes Delivered Through CYiNGINE  
CYiNGINE combines governed industrial data, engineering domain knowledge, and a 
modern AI and LLM stack, embedding AI within engineering workflows and translating the 
three playbooks into measurable outcomes:  
 Engineering Lifecycle — faster design and change cycles, improved first-time-right 
rates, and greater reuse of engineering IP.  
 Service Lifecycle — higher asset availability and fewer unplanned outages through 
AI-assisted diagnostics, predictive maintenance, and automated service knowledge.  
 Quality and Regulatory Lifecycle — faster certification and compliance with 
continuous, audit-ready traceability, reducing rework and regulatory risk.  
Delivered through a common platform and measured against client KPIs, these outcomes 
support long-term, outcome-based engagements.  
IES will progress from Explore > Build > Scale > Deliver Impact , moving technology 
innovation into client outcomes.  
     
“We are not pursuing AI for the sake of AI—we are pursuing tangible business outcomes for 
our customers. That means rethinking how AI comes into the core engineering disciplines 
and how it is adopted in the way our customers design, manufacture, and service their 
products. Intelligent Engineering Solutions is how we bring that ambition together in one 
place, and it reflects our commitment to investing in the R&D, partnerships, and talent that 
will define Cyient's next chapter,” said Sukamal Banerjee, Executive Director & Chief 
Executive Officer, Cyient . 
IES will be led by Harjott Atrii, Chief Business Officer, Cyient, who has helped shape the data, 
AI, and technology capabilities at Cyient over the past few months, which now come 
together under the new unit. 
“Our customers do not buy AI. They buy outcomes. What we bring is hard to replicate: 
decades of engineering domain depth, governed industrial data, and the trust of customers 
who let us into their most critical workflows. CYiNGINE brings that data, domain, and AI 
stack together into a single execution layer, and our three playbooks turn it into results our 
customers can measure — faster engineering cycles, higher asset availability, and faster 
regulatory clearance. That is how Cyient intends to move from projects to long-term, 
outcome-based partnerships in industrial engineering,” said Harjott Atrii, Chief Business 
Officer, Cyient . 
The move reinforces the company's positioning as a lifecycle engineering services partner, 
bringing together its engineering capabilities with data, AI, and technology to support 
customers from engineering and development through to deployment and operations.
