Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR 
Regulations”), we hereby inform you that the Company’s first level step-down 
subsidiary i.e. Varroc Italy S.p.A. (located at Via Dei Prati 20, 10020 Cambiano - 
Torino, Italy) has decided to discontinue its manufacturing and laboratories & testing 
activities in Italy and to reorganise part of the R&D department in Italy, while 
retaining only sales and part of R&D functions effective from January, 2027 and, in 
any event, subject to the completion of the statutory consultation procedure with the 
local trade unions in Italy.  
 
The details as required under SEBI LODR Regulations read with SEBI Master 
Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 
2026, are enclosed herewith as Annexure A . 
 
We request you to take this on record, and to treat the same as compliance with the 
applicable provisions of the Listing Regulations. The aforesaid information is also 
placed on the website of the Company at www.varroc.com.
