CMS Info Systems Limited  awarded at Financial Express CFO Awards  
Mumbai, September  16, 202 4:  CMS Info Systems , India’s leading business services company offering 
banking logistics and technology services,  has been awarded the  most coveted  Financial Express CFO 
Awards in the Medium Enterprises Segment . 
Mr. Pankaj Khandelwal, Chief Financial Officer  accepted the Award at an event held on September 13, 
2024 in Mumbai  as a recognition of CMS Info Systems’  financial strength and governance.  
The Award was presented by Union minister for Electronics and IT, Information & Broadcasting and 
Railways , Ashwini Vaishnaw .  
The FE CFO Awards, now in its seventh year, was presented to 14 institutions in recognition of their 
pioneering work , led by a high -quality -jury.
