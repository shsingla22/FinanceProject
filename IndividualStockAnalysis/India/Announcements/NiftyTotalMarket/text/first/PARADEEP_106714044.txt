Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR”), read with SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD -PoD-
1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 (as amended), we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of Paradeep Phosphates Limited (“the Company”), at its meeting held today, has approved 
setting up an Aluminium Fluoride (AlF ₃) Manufacturing Plant with a  capacity of 15000 MTPA  at 
Paradeep .  
The details required under the SEBI Circular are provided below:  
Particulars Details  
Industry or area to which the 
new line of business belongs  Industrial Chemicals – Fluorine Chemicals, specifically manufacture 
of Aluminium Fluoride (AlF ₃), utilizing Hydrofluorosilicic Acid (HFSA), 
a by-product generated during phosphoric acid production.  
Expected benefits  The proposed project will enable value addition to HFSA by 
converting a low -value by -product into a high- value industrial 
chemical, diversify the Company's revenue streams into industrial 
chemicals, improve profitability and operating margins, strengthen 
fluorine management, support circular economy and sustainability 
initiatives, and create opportunities for future expansion into fluorine-
based specialty chemicals.  
Estimated amount to be 
invested  Approximately ₹250 Crores .
