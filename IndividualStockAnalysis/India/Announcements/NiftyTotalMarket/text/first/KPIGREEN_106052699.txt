For immediate release      
KP Group appoints Dr.  Alok Das, an Alumna  of IIT  Kanpur and RE Industry Veteran as Group 
CEO  
Surat , 11th November 2024  - KP Group, a leading player in the renewable energy sector, is 
pleased to announce the appointment of Dr. Alok Das as the new Group Chief Executive Officer 
(CEO), effective on 11th November 2024 . 
Dr. Alok Das brings over 30 years of extensive experience in the Renewable Energy industry, 
specializing in wind , solar , hybrid energy systems , green hydrogen and ammonia includ ing 
offshore technologies coupled with,  other allied RE services  in the sector,  which will help KP 
Group towards rapid growth  of future.   He has a proven track record of leading various renewable 
energy projects, fostering international business development, and advocating for sustainable 
energy policies across India and other countr ies. An alumna of IIT Kanpur and holding PhD in 
Renewable Energy and hands -on leadership in strategic planning, project management, and 
business growth, Dr. Das is poised to lead KP Group to new heights in renewable energy 
innovation and operational excellence.  
He has also held leadership positions at Suzlon Energy,  Reliance Energy and NEPC  Micon  
Limited, where he managed renewable and conventional power projects, driving growth and 
operational efficiency in the country. His pivotal role in shaping the policy & Regulatory 
environment since 1993 i.e. from inception of Renewable Energy initiatives in India has greatly 
advanced the Industry.  
A recognized thought leader in renewable energy, Dr. Das is actively involved with various 
industry organizations, including the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII), the Gujarat Chamber 
of Commerce and Industry (GCCI), and the Canada India Business Coun cil (C -IBC). He also 
serves as Chair of the Renewable Energy Subcommittee at the Federation of Indian Chambers of 
Commerce and Industry (FICCI) in Gujarat.  
Dr. Das’s leadership will be instrumental in driving KP Group ’s continued advancements in 
renewable energy initiatives.  With his visionary guidance, KP Group is confident in its ongoing 
efforts to expand and diversify the renewable energy sector, reinforcing its commitment to a 
sustainable energy future.  
About KP Group   
Founded in 1994 by Dr. Faruk G. Patel, KP Group has grown to become a highly respected 
conglomerate in Gujarat. Originally established as a logistics service provider, the group has 
expanded over the years into diverse sectors, achieving cumulative commissioning of 1.37+ GW 
of renewable energy projects.  
 
 
With more than 30 years of successful operations, KP Group now comprises multiple 
companies, each playing a vital role in its steady and dynamic growth. In the past decade, KP 
Group has experienced remarkable growth through strategic diversification into f abrication and 
galvanizing, renewable energy (solar and wind), and telecom infrastructure. Key entities within 
the group include KPI Green Energy Limited, KP Energy Limited, KP Green Engineering Limited, 
and KP Human Development Foundation. Through its exp ansion in renewable energy, KP Group 
continues to support India’s industrial progress and economic development, aligning with the 
nation’s push for sustainable growth in solar and wind energy.
