Name of the Authority  Reserve Bank of India

Details of violation(s)/contravention(s)

regarding : 
 (i) risk categorisation of its customers  into low, 
medium and high and periodic review of risk

3.90 Crore as on March 31, 2022 and registration of 
the same with ROC
