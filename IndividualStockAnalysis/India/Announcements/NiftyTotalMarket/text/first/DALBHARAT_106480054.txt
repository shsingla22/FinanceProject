Dear Sir/Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations we wish to inform that Dalmia Cement 
(Bharat) Limited ( “DCBL”), wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, has received an Order in 
Appeal from the Commissioner (Appeals), GST, CX & Customs, Bhubaneshwar Commissionerate, 
Odisha, u/s 73(1) of Finance Act 1994, pursuant to appeal filed by the Asst. Commissioner, GST & 
Central Excise, Rourkela-I Division, Orissa, in the matter of demand of service tax for AY 2016-17 
and 2017-18 on the alleged dispute of levy of tax on grant of license by the State for drawing and 
use of natural river water for industrial use. The Order in Appeal has imposed a demand of Service 
tax and penalty as under: 
 
1) Tax: Rs. 10,59,250/-; and 
2) Penalty: Rs. 10,69,250/-. 
 
DCBL has good grounds to contest and will file appeal against the said Order before the Appellate 
Authority (CESTAT) within prescribed timelines. The Order was received on December 27, 2025 
at 12:30 P.M.
