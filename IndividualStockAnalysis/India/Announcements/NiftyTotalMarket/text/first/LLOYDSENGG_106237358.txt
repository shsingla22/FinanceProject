the prestigious Sharad Pawar Museum at its iconic Mumbai’s Wankhede Stadium. The Mus eum is a 
planned tribute to the city’s rich cricketing legacy as well as MCA’s contribution and Mumbai’s 
contribution to Indian and international cricket. The theme of the Mus eum revolves around celebrating 
not just the game but showcasing players and administrators’ contributions and the milestones to

Mr.  Mukesh Gupta, Chairman of the Company humbly states “this is one of the initiatives that Lloyds

Brief Details about the Order  
Introduction  
The Mumbai Cricket Association (MCA), with its storied legacy and unmatched contributions to Indian 
cricket, is proud to announce a visionary initiative —the Cricket Museum Project. This museum aims to 
celebrate the glorious history of Mumbai cricket, honour  its legends, and inspire future generations.  
Objective  
To create a world -class cricket museum that:  
Chronicles the rich history of Mumbai and Indian cricket  
Showcases memorabilia and artifacts of legendary players  
Offers interactive, educational, and immersive experiences  
Becomes a cultural and tourist landmark in Mumbai  
Location  
The proposed museum will be situated at the iconic Wankhede Stadium, leveraging its symbolic stature 
and central location to attract local, national, and international visitors.  
Key Features  
Heritage Gallery: Rare photographs, trophies, jerseys, and personal belongings of Mumbai cricket 
legends like Sunil Gavaskar, Sachin Tendulkar, Dilip Vengsarkar, and more.  
Hall of Fame: Dedicated space to honour  the contributions of cricketers, coaches, and administrators 
from Mumbai.  
Interactive Zones: Virtual reality batting simulators, holographic replays of historic matches, and 
touchscreen displays for fans to explore data and stories . 
Learning Centre: A research and training wing for students, analysts, and young cricketers.  
 
 
 
 
  
  
 
 
 
 
 
Event Space: A multipurpose auditorium for screenings, lectures, and cricket -themed events.  
Significance  
Cultural Legacy: Celebrates Mumbai’s unparalleled contribution to Indian cricket.  
Tourism Boost: Adds a major attraction to the city’s sports and cultural landscape.  
Education and Inspiration: Serves as a learning hub for young fans and  future  players .
