We wish to inform that the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (“NRC”) and the Board of 
Directors of the Bank (“Board”), at their meeting held today, i.e. July 25, 2025, have conducted 
the annual review of list of the Senior Management Personnel (“SMP”) category. Based on the 
recommendation of the NRC, the Board has approved the categorization of Mr. Dhavan Shah, 
Head of Commercial Banking, Mr. Kapil Gupta,  Head - Treasury & Financial Markets,  Mr. Ankur 
Tripathi, Chief Information Officer, and Mr. Manoj Tibrewal, Head of Human Resources, Admin & 
Infra as SMPs, effective July 26, 2025.

SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024, as amended are given in the 
enclosed Annexure . 
 
The above information is also being made available on the website of the Bank  www.aubank.in .

Mr. Kapil Gupta – Mr. Kapil Gupta has done  PGDBM in Finance  from  Indian

India  for more  than  5 years.  
 
Mr. Ankur Tripathi – Mr. Ankur Tripathi holds a Bachelor of Technology from the

transformation, and international trade.  He is working with the Bank since 
March, 2014.   
 
Mr. Manoj Tibrewal – Mr. Manoj Tibrewal holds a Bachelor of Commerce degree

Secretaries of India. He has over 3 decades of rich and diversified experience in

administrative infrastructure.  He is working with the Bank since February, 2009.
