Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations we wish to inform that Dalmia Cement 
(Bharat) Limited ( “DCBL”), wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, has received the following 
two orders: 
 
1. Order from the Jt. Commissioner, Corporate Circle, Varanasi II, Mirzapur, Uttar Pradesh, 
under Section 74 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and Integrated Goods and 
Services Tax Act, 2017, pursuant to adjudication proceedings of a show cause notice regarding 
disallowance of Input Tax Credit (ITC) for FY 2019-20. The Order has imposed a demand of tax 
along with interest and penalty as under: 
 
i. Tax: Rs. 21,74,976/- along with interest; and 
ii. Penalty: Rs. 21,74,976/-. 
 
2. Order from the Assistant Commissioner of CGST & CX, Division-II Bokaro, Jharkhand, under 
Section 74 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and Jharkhand Goods and Services 
Tax Act, 2017, pursuant to adjudication proceedings of a show cause notice regarding 
disallowance of Input Tax Credit (ITC) for FY 2019-20. The Order has imposed a demand of tax 
along with interest and penalty as under: 
 
i. Tax: Rs. 1,26,94,260/- along with interest; and 
ii. Penalty: Rs. 1,26,94,260/-. 
 
 
DCBL has good grounds to contest and will file appeal against the said Orders before the Appellate 
Authorities within prescribed timelines.
