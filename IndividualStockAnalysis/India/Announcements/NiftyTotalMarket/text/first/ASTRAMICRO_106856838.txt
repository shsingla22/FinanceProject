Investor Release
Commenting  on the performance,  Mr. M V Reddy,  Managing  Director,  Astra  Microwave  
Products  Limited,  said
“H1 FY27 has been a strong first half for Astra. Our EBITDA and PAT for the half year  are the 
highest we have ever reported, both in absolute terms as well as margins. Standalone revenues 
for H1FY27 reported were Rs.437 crores, with EBITDA of Rs.120 crores at a 27.4% margin and PAT 
of Rs. 57 crores at a 13.0% margin. This healthy performance was driven by a favourable  product 
mix, led by execution of domestic defence  production orders, alongside better control of 
operating expenses.
As of September 30, 2026, our standalone order book stood at Rs. 3,937 crores and our 
consolidated order book at Rs. 4,708 crores. The order book now has a larger share of production 
orders for indigenous radar and electronic warfare platforms. These programmes  typically run 
over several years with follow -on requirements, and together with repeat orders in software -
defined radios, they give us strong revenue visibility and a clear runway for growth.
We remain focused on strengthening our position in India’s defence  and aerospace ecosystem 
through indigenous product development, deeper technological capabilities and expansion into 
new opportunities, including exports of radar, electronic warfare and software -defined radio 
systems to friendly nations. We will keep investing in R&D and production capacity to stay ahead 
of this demand, and we remain committed to our long -term aspirations.”
About  Astra  Microwave  Products  Limited  
Astra  Microwave  Products  Limited  (Astra)  was incorporated  in 1991  by a team  of distinguished  
scientists  with experience  in RF/Microwave/Digital  electronics  and management  of projects  with 
high technology  content . The company  has  grown   substantially  since   inception  with continuous   
investments   in  infrastructure,   captive   test  facilities   and  other   resources .  
The company  has various  certificates  such as AS9100 D & BS EN ISO 9001 :2015 , 
ISO27001 :2013 , ISO9001 :2015 , ISO14001 :2015 , ISO45001 :2018 , ISO/IEC 17025 :2017 . The 
company’s  product  portfolio   spans   across   Defence , Aerospace  &  Space   Electronics   systems,  
sub-systems  and components . Astra  has 3 Automatic  assembly  lines  for PCB  Assembly,  7 Class  
10K cleanrooms  & 1 Class  100K clear  room . The RF & Microwave  Test Instruments  & 
infrastructure  extends  from  30MHz  up to 40GHz . In-house  Environment  test facilities  including  
EMI/EMC  facility  and a first for any Indian  Private  Industry -Near  Field  Antenna  test and 
measurement  range . Starting   with  a  diverse   range   of  microwave   products   like  filters,   
transmitters,   receivers,  antennas  etc., the company  has produced,  developed  & supplied  space -
borne  hardware  that has  flown   on  Indian   satellites . In  addition,   Astra  has  moved  up  the value   
chain   and  has supplied  multiple  Systems  to various  end users  in Defence , Aerospace  & 
Metrology  segment .
