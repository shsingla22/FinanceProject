PRESS RELEASE  
  
BuzzWorks by Brigade Group Partners with Frido to Elevate Comfort and Wellness at Work  
 
Managed workspaces from Brigade Group, BuzzWorks, has partnered with Frido, India’s leading D2C 
ergonomic innovation brand, to introduce comfort -led wellness solutions across its centres. As part of the 
collaboration, BuzzWorks will integrate Frido Comfort  Rooms into its upcoming centres in Hyderabad and 
Bengaluru, while rolling out Frido Corners across existing locations. Frido will bring its signature design 
language to BuzzWorks centres, creating Comfort Rooms that allow members to unwind and experience 
ergonomic setups first -hand. These dedicated zones will offer members immersive access to Frido’s ergonomic 
innovations, from the 3D Ergo Chair to standing desks and sofa -cum -beds with wedge integrations that 
seamlessly blend comfort into the workday.   For BuzzWorks users, this means improved posture, reduced 
fatigue, and a more balanced work routine, that enables them to recharge, refocus, and perform at their best 
throughout the day.  
  
“At BuzzWorks, we’ve always believed that workspaces should offer more than just a place to work, they 
should enable a sense of balance, belonging, and well -being. Our partnership with Frido strengthens this 
philosophy by introducing comfort -led design as an integral part of our spaces. As we continue to build 
environments that inspire performance and foster community, this collaboration ensures our members 
experience a more holistic workspace, one that supports both their productivity and their overall wel lness,” 
said Siddharth Verma, Business Head, BuzzWorks by Brigade Group.  
  
“At Frido, we believe comfort isn’t a luxury, it is a necessity. And comfort is especially crucial at workspaces, 
where professionals spend a major chunk of their day under immense stress. Through our partnership with 
BuzzWorks, we want to bring a sense of  ease into their everyday routine, not just in moments of rest but while 
they are creating, collaborating, and pushing through challenges. Our vision is to make well -being feel natural 
and effortless at work, woven into the environment itself, so that peop le experience it as part of how they work 
and live each day, rather than something to think about separately,” said Ganesh Sonawane, Co -founder & 
CEO, Frido.  
  
This strategic partnership marks BuzzWorks’ priority to create workspaces that focus on well -being, ease, and 
performance.  
  
About BuzzWorks:  
Brigade Group  entered the flexible office space segment with the launch of  BuzzWorks  in 2019. The brand 
offers managed, customisable workspaces through two key formats:  Built -to-Suit solutions  for enterprises 
with specific design and operational needs, and  Ready -to-Occupy offices  for businesses seeking high -quality, 
immediately functional spaces. BuzzWorks primarily caters to large enterprises and growth -focused 
companies. Managed offices are increasingly seen as enablers of productivity, culture, and scale  in today’s 
dynamic business landscape. With a sharp focus on experience, flexibility and scalability, the brand goes 
beyond workspaces; curating events, activations, and collaborative touchpoints that foster meaningful 
connections and enable professional and cultural exchange among its members.  
  
About Brigade Group : 
Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers with close to four decades of 
expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and winning their customers’ trust. Brigade 
has developed many landmark bui ldings and transformed skylines across India in the cities of Bengaluru, 
Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, and GIFT City with developments across 
Residential, Office, Retail, Hospitality and Education Sectors .
