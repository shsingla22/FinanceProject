HERO MOTOCORP DELIVERS STRONG  44% DISPATCH GROWTH WITH 
5.58  LAKH UNITS SOLD IN FEBRUARY 2026  
 
Hero MotoCorp , the world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters , reported  its strongest 
year -on-year (Y -o-Y) dispa tch performance for February 2026 . The Company dispatche d 558,216  
units  during the month , registering a robust ~44% growth  compared to 388,068 units , during the 
same period last year . The C ompany also recorded 18% Y -o-Y growth  with 458, 346  VAHAN 
registrations  in February 2026 . 
 
*Data from VAHAN except Telangana, as on March 1, 2026.  
 
Scooters Surge; 100 cc–125cc Motorcycles Anchor Volume Growth  
The strong  performance  in February 2026 was driven by continued momentum in the Scooter 
segment, supported by growing consumer preference for new and refreshed models  like Xoom and 
Destini . The 100cc – 125cc segment recorded  an impressive growth , providing a substantial boost to 
overall volume s.  
 
VIDA Scales Up with 3 X Growth  
VIDA, Hero MotoCorp’s emerging mobility business , continue d its growth trajectory, recording a three -
fold increase  in its market share  over  the same period last year . It also recorded positive performance 
with 12,514  VAHAN registrations  in February 2026, highlighting growing electric mobility 
adoption by consumers across its key markets.  
 
Global Business Grows Double -Digit  
The Company’s Global Business maintained its growth momentum , recording dispatch growth of 
~34% with 41,248  units  in February 2026  compared to 30,772  units  during the same period last 
year . This growth, across all its international markets, was primarily driven by a positive consumer 
response to the company’s premium portfolio ..  
 
Awards & Recognitions  
 BBC TopGear India Awards 2026 : Hero MotoCorp was named Two -Wheeler 
Manufacturer of the Year , with the Glamour X 125  and Xtreme 250R  winning Entry Level 
Bike of the Year  and Entry Level Performance Bike of the Year , respectively.  
 Auto Excellence Awards 2026 by CarBike360 : Hero MotoCorp secured  Two -Wheeler 
Manufacturer of the Year . In addition, Glamour X 125  was crowned Bike of the Year  and 
VIDA  was recognized  as the Two -Wheeler EV Brand of the Year ..
