PRESS RELEASE  
Aditya Birla Capital Inaugurates its 100th Dedicated Gold Loan Branch;      
Marks Entry into Mumbai  
~ On track to build a nationwide gold loan franchise with 1,000+ branches over the next three years~  
Mumbai, Maharashtra, November 12, 2026:  The NBFC business of Aditya Birla Capital Limited 
(‘ABCL’), one of India's leading diversified financial services companies, today announced the 
inauguration of its 100th dedicated Gold Loan branch. The milestone branch is located at 
Santacruz, Mumbai, un derscoring the city and Maharashtra’s importance in the organised gold 
lending market. The company also inaugurated a dedicated Gold Loan branch in Andheri, 
establishing its presence in Mumbai with two conveniently located branches serving customers 
across  the city.  
Maharashtra is an important market for Gold Loan business, supported by a large customer base, 
a vibrant economic ecosystem and growing demand for formal credit. According to TransUnion 
CIBIL's Gold Loan Landscape Report  (April 2026), Maharashtra recorded 40% year -on-year 
growth in gold loan origination volumes in 2025 and accounted for 5.8% of the country's total gold 
loan originations.  
In August 2026, Aditya Birla Capital announced its entry into Gold Loan Business with a phased 
rollout of 200 -300 dedicated Gold Loan branches by March 2027 across high -potential markets 
and over 1,000 branches in the next three years. With the inauguratio n of its 100th branch within 
three months of launch, the company has made significant progress towards building a nationwide 
presence in the organised gold lending market. The Gold Loan business had an AUM of                        
₹200 crore as of September 30, 2026.  
The branches were inaugurated by Ms. Vishakha Mulye, Managing Director & CEO, Aditya Birla 
Capital Limited, and Mr. Rakesh Singh, Executive Director and Chief Executive Officer - NBFC, 
Aditya Birla Capital Limited. Designed around convenience, transparency  and secure service, 
Aditya Birla Capital's Gold Loan proposition combines a dedicated branch -led model with digital 
capabilities to deliver a seamless customer experience.  
Commenting on the milestone, Ms. Vishakha Mulye, Managing Director & CEO, Aditya Birla 
Capital Limited , said, “The opening of our 100th Gold Loan branch is a special milestone for us. 
It reflects the strength of our execution capabilities and the confidence that customers place in 
Aditya Birla Capital. As we continue to scale this business, our ambition is to build a differentiated 
franchi se by combining the trust of the Aditya Birla brand, strong  on-ground presence and 
technology -led convenience. We believe the Gold Loan business will play an important role in 
expanding access to formal credit and helping individuals, families and small businesses take the 
next step towards their financial aspirati ons."  
Speaking on the launch, Mr. Rakesh Singh, Executive Director and Chief Executive Officer -
NBFC, Aditya Birla Capital Limited , said, “Mumbai is an important market for us, given its 
diverse customer base and evolving financial needs. Our Gold Loan business is built around 
delivering a simple, secure and convenient borrowing experience through dedicated branches, 
seamless processes and r obust digital capabilities. As we expand across Mumbai and 
Maharashtra, our focus will remain on providing customers with quick access to credit, transparent 
service, and the trust and assurance of safe handling of their pledged gold.”
