Zydus receives final approval from USFDA for Valbenazine Capsules 
 
Ahmedabad, India, 9 August, 2024  
Zydus Lifesciences Limited (including its subsidiaries/ affiliates, hereafter referred to as 
“Zydus”) has received final approval from the United States Food and Drug Administration 
(USFDA) to market Valbenazine Capsules, 40 mg, 60 mg, and 80 mg (USRLD: Ingrezza® 
Capsules).  
Valbenazine Capsules are indicated for the treatment of adults with tardive dyskinesia 
(uncontrollable movement of the face, tongue, or other body parts). The drug will be 
manufactured at the group’s formulation manufacturing facility in Ahmedabad SEZ - II, India. 
Zydus was one of the first ANDA applicants to submit a substantially complete ANDA with a 
paragraph IV certification for Valbenazine Capsules, 40 mg, and 80 mg, and was the first 
ANDA applicant to submit a substantially complete ANDA with a paragraph IV certification 
for Valbenazine Capsules, 60 mg. With this approval, Zydus is eligible for 180 days of shared 
generic drug exclusivity for Valbenazine Capsules, 40 mg, and 80 mg, and is eligible for 180 
days of sole generic drug exclusivity for Valbenazine Capsules, 60 mg. 
 
Valbenazine Capsules had annual sales of USD 1993.6 mn in the United States (IQVIA MAT 
June 2024).  
 
The group now has 400 approvals and has so far filed over 465* ANDAs since the 
commencement of the filing process in FY 2003-04. 
(*as of 30th June 2024 )
