HERO MOTOCORP DISPATCHES 4.43 LAKH  UNITS OF 
MOTORCYCLES AND SCOOTERS IN JANUARY 2025  
 
ACHIEVES YEAR -ON-YEAR  141% INCREASE IN GLOBAL 
MARKETS & 214% GROWTH WITH VIDA IN EV SEGMENT  
 
Hero MotoCorp , the world ’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters, sold  442,873  
units  in January 2025.  
 
The company dispatched  412,378  units in the domestic market, while its global business 
maintained strong momentum with 30,495  units  —achieving  a robust  141% growth over the 
corresponding  period last year (January 2024) . This growth was driven by strong demand in 
key global  markets, including Bangladesh and Colombia.  
 
Hero MotoCorp ’s electric vehicle (EV) brand  – VIDA, dispatch ed 6,669  units of the newly 
launched VIDA V2 electric scooter, which starts at ₹96,000 /-.  
 
The Union Budget 2025 presented today, augurs rural development and fosters inclusive 
growth across the nation.  
 
During the month of January 2025, Hero MotoCorp  launched  five new products across 
multiple  segments. Strengthening its presence in the premium motorcycle category , the 
company launched – the Xtreme 250R and Xpulse 210. Hero MotoCorp also expanded its 
scooter portfolio with the launch of new Destini 125, Xoom 125  and Xoom 160  - a maxi 
scooter.  
 
Hero MotoSports Team Rally – the motorsports team of Hero MotoCorp - completed the 
Dakar Rally 2025  in an impressive 7th place , solidifying its reputation as a formidable 
competitor  in global rally racing . This was the team's second -best performance in nine years, 
following their remarkable 2nd place finish in 2024.
