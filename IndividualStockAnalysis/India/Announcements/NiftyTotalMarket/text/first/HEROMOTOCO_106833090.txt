Press Release 
New Delhi, October 28, 2026 
HERO MOTOCORP ACHIEVES CONSOLIDATED Q2 FY’27 REVENUE OF Rs. 
14,971 CRORES; POSTS PROFIT AFTER TAX (PAT) OF Rs. 1,861 CRORES 
COMPANY CONTINUES STRONG FINANCIAL MOMENTUM, DELIVERING 
ROBUST CONSOLIDATED REVENUE GROWTH OF 23% FOR THE 
QUARTER AND 28% IN H1 FY’27 
Hero MotoCorp , the world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters , today announced its 
financial results for the second quarter (July–September 2026) and half year (April–September 
2026) of FY’27, delivering strong performance across all its segments including premium, scooter, 
electric mobility, global business and commuter. On standalone basis, the Company witnessed 
volume growth of 10% in Q2 FY’27, and a sequential growth of 11% over Q1 FY’27.  
Driven by these factors, the company delivered robust financial performance, recording a 23% 
growth in consolidated revenue in Q2 FY’27. 
Consolidated Financial Highlights: 
Quarterly Performance:  Revenue grew 23% YoY to Rs. 14,971 Crore from Rs. 12,218 Crore 
in Q2 FY’26. PAT stood at Rs. 1,861 Crore, reflecting a 41% YoY growth.  
H1 FY'27 Performance:  The Company reported Revenue and PAT of Rs. 28,097 Crore and 
Rs. 3,278 Crore, respectively (growth of 28% and 8% over H1 FY'26). 
Results for the quarter and half-year include a Rs. 397 Crore gain from the equity 
consolidation  of an associate company, following its capital raise via a Qualified 
Institutional Placement and Private Placement. 
Standalone Financial Highlights: 
During the quarter, the Company sold 18.68 lakh motorcycles and scooters , registering a 
robust 10% growth - reflecting sustained consumer demand across markets and segments. 
The strong retail momentum led to a consistent improvement in ICE Vahan share in each 
of the three months of the quarter . 
Quarterly Performance:  Revenue from operations for the quarter stood at Rs. 14,687 
Crore, reflecting a strong 21% growth over the corresponding quarter of the previous year. 
Despite commodity headwinds, EBITDA margins held steady at 13.4%, translating to an 
EBITDA of Rs. 1,968 Crore and a growth of 8% over previous year. PAT grew by 5% YoY to 
Rs. 1,456 Crore. 
H1 FY'27 Performance:  Revenue from operations stood at Rs. 27,686 Crore (up 28% YoY). 
EBITDA grew to Rs. 3,694 Crore (up 15% YoY), delivering an EBIDTA margin of 13.3%. PAT 
grew by healthy 16% YoY to Rs. 2,911 Crore.  
The Parts, Accessories, and Merchandising (PAM) business grew by 27% YOY, recording a 
revenue of Rs. 1,941 Crore.

Commenting on the performance, Harshavardhan Chitale, Chief Executive 
Officer, Hero MotoCorp  said, “We delivered a strong performance this quarter, with scooters 
emerging as a key growth contributor, supported by healthy momentum across both, ICE and VIDA 
electric portfolio. This reflects the strength of our product offerings and our ability to respond to 
evolving customer aspirations across segments. Our 100cc motorcycle segment also continued to 
contribute to overall growth, underscoring the resilience of our portfolio. Going forward, our focus 
remains on sustaining this positive momentum, driving profitable growth and building a more 
diversified and future-ready business.”
The Company’s Scooter  portfolio grew by healthy 47% to 2.40 lakh units . ICE scooters 
continued to gain market share led by Destini and Xoom series, which grew by 50% year-on-year 
with dispatches of 1.37 lakh units during the quarter. 
VIDA  continued its strong growth trajectory recording 76% year-on-year retail growth, 
reaching 66,531 units during the quarter.  
The Company’s Global Business  expanded its footprint during Q2 FY’27 with its entry into 
Germany, its 54th global market, and also launched XPulse 200 4V and XPulse 200 4V Pro in 
Bangladesh.  
Sharpening its focus on premiumization, as a core pillar of growth, the Company appointed 
Anuj Dua as Chief Business Officer – Premium Segment . The Harley-Davidson Business
delivered strong results, recording 30% sequential growth and 19% YoY increase in H1. The 
Company also continued to strengthen the Harley-Davidson retail ecosystem, expanding the 
network to offer globally sourced CBU models alongside locally manufactured X440 and X440 T. 
Additionally, in September 2026, the Company achieved a significant milestone, surpassing 
50,000 Harley-Davidson X440 and X440T models riders on Indian roads.

About Hero MotoCorp 
Hero MotoCorp, headquartered in New Delhi, India, is the world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters for 25 
consecutive years with ~$5 billion revenue. The Company’s global footprint spans 53 countries across Asia, Africa, Europe, 
Central and Latin America with a customer base of over 130 million. The Company operates eight manufacturing facilities 
- six in India and one each in Colombia and Bangladesh - along with two Research and Development centres: Hero Centre 
for Innovation and Technology (CIT) in India and Hero Tech Centre Germany (TCG). Hero MotoCorp is driving the 
transition to electric mobility through VIDA. Strategic alliances with Harley-Davidson and Zero Motorcycles support the 
design and development of premium and electric motorcycles, respectively. 
Further demonstrating its commitment to the EV future, the Company has made strategic investments in two- and three-
wheeler EV manufacturers, Ather Energy and Euler Motors. Hero MotoCorp is the only Indian two-wheeler manufacturer 
listed on the prestigious Dow Jones Sustainability Index (DJSI).Hero MotoCorp’s Executive Chairman, Dr. Pawan Munjal, 
was named to the 2025 TIME100 Climate List of the 100 most influential leaders driving business climate action. 
Beyond mobility, Hero MotoCorp is a major global promoter of sports, including golf, football, field hockey, and cricket. 
Hero MotoSports Team Rally is India, a flag bearer in global rally racing.
