Sterling Expands its Wildlife Portfolio with Vanyatara Tadoba  
New wilderness retreat adds Tadoba to SHRL’s growing presence across India’s leading 
wildlife destinations  
Chennai, 1 6 October 2026:  Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited (SHRL) has announced the 
opening of Sterling Vanyatara Tadoba  in Maharashtra’s Chimur region, adding another 
signiﬁcant location to its growing portfolio of jungle resorts across India.  
With a presence across many of India’s leading wildlife destinations, Sterling is building 
wilderness travel as a distinct leisure category. Vanyatara brings Tadoba into this 
strategy while strengthening the company’s presence in Maharashtra.  
The opportunity is larger than the safari itself. India’s forests differ in landscape, 
ecology, regional culture and culinary traditions, giving travellers reasons to return —  
not simply for another tiger sighting, but to discover another forest and a different way of experiencing the wild.  
This also makes wilderness travel a richer family holiday . The anticipation of a safari 
can sit alongside comfortable stays, nature -led activities, regional cuisine and time 
together that engages different generations.  
Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director & CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited (SHRL), said:  “India’s forests offer far more than wildlife sightings. Each has its own landscape, 
ecology, culture and cuisine —and therefore a different reason to travel. At Sterling, we 
see wildlife and nature -led holidays as an increasingly important part of our hospi tality 
portfolio, and we are deliberately building depth in this category.  
As Destination Architects,  our ambition is to make the forest the experience, not just 
the safari. Sterling Vanyatara Tadoba strengthens that proposition while creating 
opportunities to connect destinations such as Pench, Tadoba and Tipeshwar  into richer 
journeys for families and wildlife enthusiasts.  
Our ambition is not simply to take travellers on more safaris. It is to give them more of 
India's wilderness to discover.”  
Located with access to ﬁve safari gates of the Tadoba -Andhari Tiger Reserve , Sterling 
Vanyatara Tadoba has been purposely designed as an intimate 18 -key wilderness 
retreat, allowing the stay to remain personal, quiet and closely connected to its setting. 
Indulge Rooms with Sit -out offer elegant furnishing with seamless connection to the 
outdoors, Forest Villas  opens to private lawns and greater seclusion,  and the Two -
Bedroom Villas  provide generous shared and private spaces  with a private lawn. A 
swimming pool, spa and outdoor areas create easy downtime between excursions.

Vidarbha’s culinary traditions provide another connection to the region. At The Jungle 
Table, the bold, ﬁery ﬂavours of Vidarbha's traditional Saoji cuisine  take centre stage . 
The choices here are I nspired by the popular street food of Nagpur and She dgaon with 
the Zunka Bhakar with Thecha and Pitla  -picked from hamlets of the Gond tribes, 
alongside a rich selection of Indian and international favourites. The dining experience 
extends beyond the restaurant with leisurely post -safari high teas and intimate private 
dinners beneath the stars, bringing a touch of indulgence to the wilderness.  
Access to the Alizanza, Madnapur, Shirkheda, Belara and Shedegaon safari gates , 
subject to permits, provides ﬂexibility in planning excursions into the reserve.  
Mr. Ashok L Bhaiyya and Mr. Gaurav Bhaiyya, Partners, Sterling Vanyatara Tadoba, 
said:  “What attracted us to Tadoba was the power of the wilderness and the opportunity 
to create a retreat where the experience continues after guests return from the forest. 
We wanted space, privacy and a strong sense of place to deﬁne the stay. Sterling’s 
understanding of Indian leisure travel and its ability to build richer journeys especially 
around the wildlife destinations makes it a natural partner for that vision.” 
With Sterling already present in Pench and Tipeshwar, Tadoba can become part of a 
Pench– Tadoba –Tipeshwar forest circuit , allowing travellers to experience three 
distinct wilderness landscapes as part of one larger journey.  
For SHRL, this is where scale begins to compound in value. Each new resort broadens 
traveller choice, enables new circuits and gives families more reasons to keep 
discovering India’s forests with Sterling.
