Dear Sir/Madam, 
Sub: Update on execution of agreements by Exide Energy Solutions Limited (a material wholly 
owned subsidiary company) for the purpose of acquisition of solar power by acquisition of 
equity stake of 26% of "Clean Max Beta Private Limited" 
This is to inform you that "Exide Energy Solutions Limited' ("EESL"), a material wholly owned 
subsidiary of Exide Industries Limited ("Company") has entered into a "Energy Supply 
Agreement" dated 14th October 2024 with "Clean Max Beta Private Limited" ("SPV"), under which 
the SPY company will develop and supply solar power of 17 MWp to EESL in state of Karnataka under 
the Captive user Policy. 
EESL has also entered into a Share Purchase Agreement ("SPA") dated 14th October 2024 with the 
SPY and "Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Private Ltd.", the promoter shareholder of SPY 
("Promoter Shareholder") towards subscription of not less than 26% paid-up equity share capital of 
the SPY with a total investment of Rs. 5,95,00,000 (Rupees Five Crore Ninety-Five Lakhs only). 
Please note that this disclosure is below the threshold limit of the Company for materiality for disclosures 
under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as 
amended from time to time. The above transaction doesn't fall within the purview of related party 
transactions and is a step to increase the renewable power consumption at EESL.
