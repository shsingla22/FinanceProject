About Edelweiss Financial Services  
Edelweiss is a diversified financial services company with seven independent and well -governed businesses. The businesses 
include  Alternative Asset Management, Mutual Fund, Asset Reconstruction, Corporate Lending, Housing Finance, General 
and Life Insurance. The businesses have robust operating platforms, dedicated management teams and strong boards that 
ensure the highest standards of governance. Edelweiss employs more than 6 ,000 people, serves around 82 lakh customers, 
and manages  over INR 220,000 Cr worth of assets.
