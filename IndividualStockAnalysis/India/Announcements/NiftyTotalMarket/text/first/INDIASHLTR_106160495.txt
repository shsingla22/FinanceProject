India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited Achieves Global Recognition at LACP Vision Awards 
FY23 -24 for its Annual Report Excellence   
Gurugram, 25th February 2025:  India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited is proud to announce its 
remarkable achievement in the prestigious Vision Awards, where its Annual Report has been ranked 
among the Top 100 reports globally. This recognition underscores the company’s commitment t o 
transparent, insightful, and impactful communication with its stakeholders.  The report earned an 
exceptional 98 out of 100, securing the Gold Award in its category.  
India Shelter has been honored with the following accolades:  
1. Gold Award for Industry Excellence (Financials)    
Recognized for its outstanding annual report within the financial services sector, reflecting the 
organization’s growth, performance, and vision for the future.  
2. Top 100 Reports Worldwide – Ranked #87    
Securing a position in the global Top 100 is a testament to the company’s dedication to delivering 
high -quality, informative, and engaging reports for its investors and the wider community.  
3. Technical Achievement Award    
Awarded for exceptional proficiency in the art and methodology of annual report communication, 
showcasing the company’s innovative and effective reporting practices.  
Among the Housing Finance participants, India Shelter secured second -highest ranking, reaffirming 
its commitment to transparent, insightful and impactful communication.  
Speaking on this achievement, Mr. Rupinder Singh, Managing Director, and CEO of India Shelter 
Finance Corporation  said: "We are thrilled to receive this recognition on a global platform. Our 
annual report reflects not just our financial performance but also our purpose, values, and the 
impact we create in the communities we serve."  
The LACP Vision Awards, conferred by the League of American Communications Professionals LLC, 
is one of the most popular competing platforms for annual reports. This is the maiden submission 
by India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited in LACP Vision Award s and have received global 
recognition for the same.  
India Shelter remains committed to maintaining high standards in its communication strategies, 
ensuring transparency and fostering trust among its stakeholders.
