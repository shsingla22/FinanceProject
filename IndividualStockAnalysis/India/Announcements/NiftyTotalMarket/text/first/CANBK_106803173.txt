Subject: Notice for Exercise of Call Option of Basel Ill Compliant Additional Tier I bond of Rs. 1500 Crore 
issued on 25th October 2021, Coupon@ 8.40% Per Annum (ISIN: INE476A08126 ) 
It has been our privilege to have you as a valued Investor in Canara Bank ("Bank") Basel Ill Compliant Additional 
Tier I Bond (ISIN INE476A08126) , issued on 25th October 2021. As per terms of Information Memorandum , the 
Bond carry a coupon rate of 8.40% p.a. payable annually and Bank may exercise call option after the instrument 
has run for at least 5 years. This is to bring your kind notice that our Bank has decided to exercise the call 
option on 23rd October, 2026 (25th October, 2026 being a holiday). 
Bank has received necessary approvals from the Reserve Bank of India vide their letter dated 15th September 
2026. 
Accordingly , Notice is hereby given with the approval of appropriate authority , that the Bank has decided to 
exercise Call Option on the above-mentioned Bond and declared 8th October, 2026 as record date for this 
purpose. 
In this regard we wish to inform you the following: 
• The principal amount together with interest accrued for the period from 25/10/2025 to 24/10/2026 will 
be paid on 23.10.2026 (25.10.2026 being a holiday), and the bond will not carry any obligation from the 
said call option date. 
• After verification and completion of necessary formalities, the amount payable on 23/10/2026 will be 
remitted through RTGS/NEFT /Direct Credit in favor of the bond holders in the list of beneficiaries 
appearing in the records of NSDL/CDSL in case of Bonds held in demat form as at the closure of Record 
Date which is 8th October 2026. 
• The Bond shall be taken as discharged on payment of interest cum principal amount by the Bank as 
aforesaid. Such payment will be a legal discharge of the liability of the Bank towards the holders of the 
subject bond. 
• On such payment being made, the Bank will inform NSDL/CDSL and accordingly the Bond in the account 
of the Bondholders with NSDL/ CDSL will be extinguished. 
• Bank's liability to the Bondholders towards all their rights including for the payment or otherwise shall 
cease and stand extinguished from the call option date. 
• Payment will be subject to terms contained in the Placement Memorandu m.
