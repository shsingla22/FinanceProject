      VGL achieve d ‘Great Place to Work®’ certification  
for the 8th consecutive year   
February 2 1, 2025  
Vaibhav Global Limited (VGL) is proud to announce that it has been recognized as ‘Great 
Place to Work®’ organization in India for the 8th consecutive year.  
This certification is based on an independent evaluation of our  workplace culture, 
policies , and anonymous employee feedback . The a ssessment cover ed 5 dimensions - 
credibility, fairness, respect, pride, and camaraderie.  
Mr. Puspendra Singh, Vice President - HR, Vaibhav Global Limited, said,  ‘Being recognized 
as a ‘Great Place to Work®’ for the 8th year in a row is a proud moment for us. At VGL, we 
believe in ‘Delivering Joy’ to our teams by prioritizing their growth and creating a supportive  
and collaborative environment.  This recognition reflects our efforts  to invest in people, 
processes, an inclusive culture, and employee well -being.  We truly appreciate our team for 
their trust and honest feedback .’

About Vaibhav Global Limited  
Vaibhav Global Limited (VGL) is listed on stock exchanges in India (BSE: 532156, NSE: VAIBHAVGBL, ISIN 
- INE884A01027). VGL is an omni -channel E -tailer of fashion jewellery, accessories, and lifestyle products 
in developed markets with direct access to ~1 27 million households (FTE) through its TV home shopping 
networks – Shop LC in US, Shop TJC & Ideal World in UK and Shop LC in Germany. The Company’s 
ecommerce websites www.shoplc.com  in the US, www.tjc.co.uk  & www.idealworld.tv  in the UK and 
www.shoplc.de  in Germany, complement TV coverage and diversify customer engagement. The Company 
is committed to ‘Delivering Joy’ to all the stakeholders. Through its flagship midday meal program, ‘Your 
Purchase Feeds…’  where a meal is provided for every piece sold at the retail channels, the Company has 
provided over 97 million meals in US, UK, Germany, and India at a run rate of 69,000 meals being donated 
every school day since program’s inception.
