Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 – Updates on Acquisition  
 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR Regulations”), we had informed vide our letter dated 21st 
January 2026 that the Board of Directors of the Company had approved an investment in the equity share 
capital of August Jewellery Private Limited (“AJPL”), having  brand name “Melorra”, thereby acquiring 68% 
of the equity share capital of AJPL . 
 
In continuation of the aforesaid intimation and subsequent disclosures made in this regard, we wish to inform 
you that the Company has, today, i.e., 25th October 2026, executed the Share Subscription Agreement 
(“SSA”), Shareholders’ Agreement (“SHA”) and other transaction -related documents with AJPL and its 
existing shareholders for the acquisition of 68% of the equity share capital of AJPL for an aggregate 
consideration of INR 68 crore, to be invested in multiple tranches.  The completion of the aforesaid 
acquisition is subject to the fulfilment of certain conditions precedent as specified in the aforementioned 
transaction documents.  
 
The relevant details as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR Regulations, read with SEBI Master
