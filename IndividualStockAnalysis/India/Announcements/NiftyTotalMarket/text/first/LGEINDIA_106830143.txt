The Giant of Entertainment Comes to Indian Homes  
 
New Delhi, 27 October 2026: LG Electronics India Limited (LGEIL) today introduced its 292 cm (115") QNED evo 
AI Mini LED TV, the largest model in its 2026 QNED evo lineup. Positioned as the giant of entertainment, the TV is 
built for viewers who want a bigger and more immersive way to watch, bring ing the scale of the cinema, the energy of 
the stadium and the intensity of gaming into the living room.  
 
Powered by LG’s Alpha 8 AI Processor Gen3, the 292 cm (115") QNED evo combines Mini LED picture quality with 
AI-based picture and sound technologies, so that colour, contrast and detail hold up across every inch of the ultra -large 
screen.  
 
Brian Jung, Director – Media Entertainment Solution, LG Electronics India, said : “The 292 cm (115")QNED evo Mini LED 
TV reflects our commitment to creating more immersive home entertainment experiences. By combining ultra -large 
screen technology with enhanced picture quality, AI -powered sound and advanced gaming features, we are giving

One Screen, Four Big Experiences  
Bringing the Cinema Home  
On a screen this large, picture quality has to work harder. The 292 cm (115") QNED evo uses Precision Dimming Ultra 
to control thousands of local dimming zones, revealing fine detail in dark scenes while keeping bright highlights crisp. 
The result is deeper blacks, cleaner highlights and stronger contrast, with depth preserved even in shadow -heavy scenes. 
 
Dynamic QNED Color Pro optimises colour mapping, so tones stay rich and accurate even in bright HDR scenes. It is 
certified by Intertek for 100 percent Color Volume1, enabling faithful colour reproduction across the full 292 cm (115") 
screen. 
 
The Alpha 8 AI Processor Gen3, which draws on 13 years of LG OLED leadership to bring LG’s OLED light -control 
capability to QNED, also powers AI Super Upscaling. Using deep learning, it analyses on -screen objects and refines 
texture, edges and fine detail,  so 4K visuals look natural and stay crisp on the big screen. AI Picture Pro enhances faces, 
bodies and other key elements to improve perceived depth, while Dynamic Tone Mapping Pro analyses each frame to 
refine resolution, brightness and clarity.  
 
Recreating the Stadium Atmosphere  
The 292 cm (115") QNED evo is built to make live sport feel bigger. Its wide colour range adds impact to bright sports 
scenes, while AI Sound Pro creates virtual 11.1.2 -channel surround sound through the TV’s built -in speakers, delivering 
clearer spatial separation and a more immersive soundstage. Together with AI Super Upscaling and Dynamic Tone 
Mapping Pro, it brings a stadium -scale experience to sports and big -action content. 
 
With the webOS 26 platform, fans can also keep up with the game without leaving the screen. Sports Portal brings live 
content, scores, match schedules and league standings into one place, and Sports Alert sends notifications for chosen 
teams and leagues. K ey stats such as match progress and game -result predictions appear on an on -screen AI Concierge 
card, helping viewers follow several games at once.  
 
Elevating Gaming Immersion  
For gamers, the 292 cm (115") QNED evo turns its ultra -large screen into a responsive gaming display. It supports 
variable refresh rate (VRR) up to 165Hz, AMD FreeSync ™ Premium and Auto Low Latency Mode (ALLM), keeping 
rapid in-game action sharp, highly responsive and free of lag.  
 
The new Motion Booster raises the refresh rate to 330Hz, significantly reducing motion blur and keeping visuals smooth 
in fast-paced shooters and racing games, where split -second reactions matter. LG Gaming Portal adds an all -in-one 
destination with access  to thousands of cloud gaming services and native web games.  
 
Giant-Screen Entertainment, Made Smarter  
As the largest model in LG’s 2026 QNED evo lineup, the 292 cm (115") TV pairs its scale with AI -based personalisation 
on webOS 26. With Voice ID, the TV recognises each user and automatically loads their personalised My Page home 
screen. AI Concierge gives  context-aware suggestions and quick access to useful functions based on what is being 
watched. Multi-AI support, powered by Google Gemini and Microsoft Copilot, lets users ask questions, check 
information and search for related content while watching.
