Hyderabad, India, November  20th, 202 6: NATCO  Pharma Limited  (BSE:  524816  and NSE: NATCO PHARM) ("NATCO") today 
receives Establish ment Inspection Report (EIR) from US FDA Inspection at our Visakhapatnam , Andhra Pradesh,  FDF 
Unit  
Ref: Our letter dated August 21st 2026  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, this is to inform 
you that:  
The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) had conducted an inspection at the FDF manufacturing plant  located in  
Parawada , Visakhapatnam,  Andhra Pradesh , India, which was conducted from August  17th – August  21st, 202 6. On conclusion 
of the inspection, the Company  had received 4 (Four ) observation s in the Form -483.  We hereby notify that the Company 
has received EIR from the U.S. FDA on the classification of the above referred inspection as Voluntary Action Indicated (VAI).   
Please take the above information on record.
