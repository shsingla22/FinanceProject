USFDA Inspection Update - Contract manu facturing facility at Montreal, Canada 
 
Noida, May 29, 2026:  Jubilant Pharmova Limited (“Company”) today announced that Jubilant HollisterStier 
General Partnership (“JHSGP”) has received a communi cation from the United States Food and Drug 
Administration (USFDA) conveying the completion of it s review of the Establishment Inspection Report for 
the inspection conduc ted at JHSGP’s Kirkland, Canada facility (16751 Trans-Canada Highway) during October 
20 to November 3, 2025. The review has result ed in the issuance of a Warning Letter. 
 
JHSGP has been proactively engaging with the USFDA and has already initiated appropriate corrective and 
preventive actions. The Company is committed to  addressing the observations comprehensively. 
 The Company would like to emphasize current operations at JHSGP are proceeding under enhanced controls 
and oversight while remediation ef forts are actively underway.   
 JHSGP is jointly owned by subsidiaries of Jubilant Pharma Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of the 
Company.
