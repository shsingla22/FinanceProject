PhysicsWallah’s Vishwas Diwas demonstrates deep online penetration in Bharat and strong 
student response to ‘Ask AI’  
Sees record growth in new and underpenetrated pin codes  
 
 
New Delhi, March 23, 2026 : Education company PhysicsWallah Limited celebrated the fifth edition 
of its flagship event, Vishwas Diwas (VD), to kick off the new academic year. The company 
witnessed record viewership with over 34 lakh learners tuning in to watch the stream. Students 
from metros as well as emerging education hubs like Patna, Lucknow, Varanasi, Indore, Jaipur, 
Bhubaneswar, and Nagpur, alongside smaller towns like Begusarai, Bhopal, Ranchi, Darbhanga, 
Bhubaneswar and Deoria engaged with the celebration.  
  
This scale of participation from Tier-2 and Tier-3 regions reflects PhysicsWallah’s continued focus 
on making high-quality online education accessible and affordable to learners across India, 
including those from areas where access to coaching infrastructure has traditionally been limited.  
 
This momentum, based on current trends of this year’s Vishwas Divas collections and 
enrollments have shown meaningful increase with most categories growing 30%+ as compared 
to last year collections, reflecting higher student conversions at the start of the cycle in our online 
business. While PW’s affordable online courses remained the main attraction, value-added services 
gained strong traction. One such unique online batch that offers personalised mentorship, physical 
study materials and more, at an additional fee- saw enrolments increasing threefold year-on-year. 
This particular batch offering is now available in 12 new individual exam categories. Similarly, Power 
Batch (with small class sizes and personalised mentorship) and Curious Jr (K8 offerings) online 
categories also recorded a 4x uptick.  
 
New learning initiatives announced during Vishwas Diwas saw strong traction among students, 
highlighting a shift towards personalised, tech-enabled learning experiences. Most popular 
announcements were AskAI, a voice-first AI model that offers real-time, voice-enabled support in 
both English and Hindi during live online classes. Students were also excited to know about  AI 
Sahayak - a personalised learning companion, trained on the study patterns of high-performing 
students from the past two years. Another big draw was the AI-powered interactive books that 
allow students to highlight content, ask questions, and engage in immersive self-study with built-in 
practice, quick assessments, clear explanations, and instant solutions. 
 
Interestingly, Alakh Sir had a live discussion with AI Guru - PW’s hyper personalised 24*7 AI tutor 
that enables real-time doubt resolution and customised academic support for students. This AI 
tool resolves an average of 4.5 Mn doubts every month, helping students learn at their 
convenience. 
 
Strong adoption in vernacular and regional segments also remained a key driver of growth. The 
company recorded a 100% year-on-year increase in online batches for vernacular enrolments 
during the Vishwas Diwas, with offerings now available in 9 languages, including Bangla, Marathi, 
Kannada, Odia, and others, enabling deeper reach among students from non-English speaking 
backgrounds. 
 
State board offerings also saw a significant surge during the Vishwas Diwas, with 180% year-on-
year growth, led by strong demand in Rajasthan and Jharkhand among the six state board 
categories that PW offers, which include Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, and Jammu & 
Kashmir. This growth is underpinned by a large and underserved base of over 55 million students 
in classes 9–12 across state boards, currently serviced by a fragmented local coaching industry. 
Through its product-led approach, affordability, and tech-enabled academic delivery, PW aims to 
meaningfully expand access to this highly fragmented student segment. 
 
Organised annually, Vishwas Diwas serves as a platform for PhysicsWallah to connect with 
students across the country, share academic plans for the upcoming year, and introduce new 
batches and learning initiatives. Alakh Pandey, Educator and Founder of PhysicsWallah, said , 
“Vishwas Diwas is our way of celebrating the student community and creating more opportunities 
for learning. It is also a day to showcase our innovations in tech and pedagogy to students. Seeing 
so many students from tier 2 and tier 3 cities joining in reaffirms my faith in our aim to ensure 
affordable education is accessible to all.”  
 
During the live session, Alakh Sir interacted with students and teachers, sharing preparation 
strategies for competitive and board exams and responding to questions in real time. The platform 
also used AI-powered comment listening and interpretation to better understand student queries 
and adapt the discussion during the live interaction.

About PhysicsWallah 
 
PhysicsWallah Limited (PW), an education platform, was founded in 2020 by Alakh Pandey and 
Prateek Maheshwari. Headquartered in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, PW aims to facilitate education 
through online, offline and hybrid platforms. Initially launched as a YouTube channel in 2014, PW 
now offers education to students through its native app, tech-enabled offline and hybrid centres, 
and YouTube channels. PW’s offerings span various educational segments, including test 
preparation, a skilling vertical, coaching for higher education, and facilitating education abroad, 
with programmes available in multiple vernacular languages. 
 
PhysicsWallah Limited was listed on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and BSE 
Limited (Bombay Stock Exchange) on November 18, 2025.
