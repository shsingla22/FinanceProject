United Breweries Launches London Pilsner Beer in Odisha  
 
• New standard for strong beer has been introduced in Odisha  
• Crafted using European hops and aged for one week at low temperatures, resulting in a 
smooth and strong taste.  
• UBL launches a strong beer positioned at an accessible price point  
 
India, 09 September 2025 – United Breweries Limited (UBL), part of the HEINEKEN Company, is 
delighted to announce the launch of London Pilsner Strong Beer in Odisha. London Pilsner, crafted 
with European hops and subjected to a week -long ageing process at sub -zero temperatures , 
represents sophistication and uncompromising quality. With this launch, UBL brings its much -loved 
beer to Odisha, making international -quality beer affordable for consumers at just INR 160 per 
650ml bottle.  
 
Speaking on the launch, Vikram Bahl, CMO, United Breweries Limited , said:  “By launching London 
Pilsner in Odisha, we are celebrating the state’s growing love for fine brews and recognizing its 
importance as a key market in India’s beer landscape. With London Pilsner, a premium beer positioned at an accessible price point, we ar e confident that this innovation will help bring back the 
category growth .” 
 
London Pilsner will be available across leading outlets in Odisha, marking it as the fourth market 
after Maharashtra, Karnataka , and Madhya Pradesh . 
 
About United Breweries:  
 Bengaluru -headquartered United Breweries Limited, part of the HEINEKEN Company, is the largest 
beer manufacturer in India. The company produces and markets packaged drinking water and 
soda, internationally recognized beer, and non -alcoholic beverages. Its diverse product portfolio
