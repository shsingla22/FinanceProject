Further to our  intimation dated May 17, 2024, on the inspection conducted by the United States 
Food & Drug Administration (USFDA) at our formulations manufacturing facilities (FTO -7 & 
FTO -9) in Duvvada, Visakhapatnam, we wish to inform you that the Company has received the 
Establishment Inspection Report (EIR). The USFDA has classified the inspection as Voluntary 
Action Indicated (VAI) and concluded that the inspection is "closed" under 21 CFR 20.64(d)(3).
