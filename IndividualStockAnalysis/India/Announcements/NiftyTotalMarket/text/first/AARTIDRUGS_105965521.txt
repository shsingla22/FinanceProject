meeting), on a proportionate basis through tender offer route (“Tender Offer”) as 
prescribed under Buyback Regulations, to all the equity shareholders/ beneficiary 
owners who hold Equity Shares on the Record Date (“Buyback”) . The Buyback 
period shall commence from the date of approval of buyback by the Board until the

by the Company is made (“Buyback Period”) . The Bu yback Size does not include

 The Board t ook note of intention of members of the promoter and promoter group
