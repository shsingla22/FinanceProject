Brief Profile of Secretarial Auditor  
 
Dr. Ajay Jain  is a graduate in Commerce (Hons.) & Law , a Fellow  Member of ICSI and has done Ph.D. in 
Capital Markets . He is having a post qualification experience of more than four decades  in the areas of Corporate 
Laws, Compliance Assurance, Corporate Governance, Corporate Finance, Inv estment Banking and Corporate 
Restructuring. He has held Senior Management positions in industrial houses such as Bharti Airtel, Max India, 
Hi-Tech Gears & the United Group  including as Head of Finance .   
 
Ajay  had propagated & implemented in-house Legal &  Compliance Audit of ten Listed Companies of a reputed 
group, way back in 1986 . He has handled various assignments of Legal Due Diligence relating to Capital Market 
Offerings, Take -over & Divestment transactions and Drafting of various Agreements relating to Joint Ventures 
& Shareholders’ Agreement, Loan Agreements and other ancillary agreements.  
 
He was part of the team implement ing Corporate Governance practices  in Max India, in the year 1999, on the 
lines of CII’s Corporate Governance Code, much before t he release of draft Corporate Governance Code of 
SEBI. As part of Corporate Governance & Risk Management practices, he also developed Compliance 
Assurance System s & Processes across all SBUs & locati ons of Max India, in the year 2000.  
 
Ajay handled the first Public Issue of his career in February 1984 & thereafter, handled several Public  issues of 
Equity & Debt besides Rights & Bonus Issues. He also completed various transactions of Private Placement of 
Debentures, Preference Shares & Equity Shares  with Investment Institutions, Mutual Funds & Housing Finance 
Companies . He also helped his clients raising long term & working capital funds from Banks & FIs including 
External Commercial Borrowings  from Foreign Banks, way back in 1996 .  
 
He has been a faculty member in various workshops  organized by ICSI , Indian Institute of Merchant Banking  
& PHD Chamber and has also contributed various articles & papers of professional interest. He has been a 
Member of the  Core Group and Advisory Group of various  committees o f ICSI  in the past .  
 
Earlier, Ajay was practicing as a Company Secretary under the name & style of Ajay Jain & Co, and presently, 
under the name and style of Jus & Associates. The firm ha s been associated with Large and Midcap listed 
companies and MNCs, b oth listed & unlisted in vari ed sectors  besides NGOs of repute.
