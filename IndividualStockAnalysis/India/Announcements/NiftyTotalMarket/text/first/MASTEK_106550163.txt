Mastek Secures £85m Framework Engineering Contract with UK Home Office's Migration 
& Borders Technology Portfolio  to Scale the ATLAS Platform  
Mumbai , India , March 5, 2026 : Mastek (UK) Limited, a Wholly -owned Subsidiary of Mastek Limited (NSE: MASTEK; 
BSE: 523704) , a trusted AI -first digital engineering and cloud transfor mation partner, today announced that it has 
secured an Engineering contract with the Home Office, with an overall budget of circa £85m/$110m+. Delivering to 
Migration and Borders Policy, the engagement deploys specialist engineering practices and teams to support, 
maintain, and enhance the Home Office's flagship ATLAS platform, the core system enabling Visa and Study routes, 
Asylum Casework, Border Force Operations, and Immigration Enforcement.  
The service aims to scale ATLAS, making its common components scalable, resilient and highly available. A 
combination of high -end engineering techniques and AI -driven engineering accelerators will be applied across human 
and automated casework and the modernisation of the platform.  Mastek expects significant growth in this contract, 
as the department plans to deliver various complex policies through the Atlas sy stem over the coming few years.  
Mr. Abhishek Singh h, President UKI & Europe, Mastek , said,  "This milestone deepens our partnership with the 
Home Office and reflects sustained confidence in Mastek’s advanced and modern engineering capabilities. By 
embedding automation, AI, fraud reduction techniques, and platform modernisation, we continue to deliver and 
operate secure, compliant, and resilient services at the national scale. We thank the Home Office for creating an 
excellent environment for supplier collaboration and execution with our teams who anchor Mastek’s role as a trusted 
partner to the UK public sector. ”
