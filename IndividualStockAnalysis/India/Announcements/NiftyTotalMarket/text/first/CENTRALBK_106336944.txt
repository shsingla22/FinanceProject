Sub:  Approval of  Reserve Bank of India to set up IFSC Banking Unit (IBU) at Gift City, 
Gandhinagar, Gujarat . 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we would like to  inform  that the Bank have received approval of Reserve 
Bank of India vide its letter dated 11th August , 2025 to setup an IFSC Banking Unit (IBU) in 
GIFT City, Gandhinagar, Gujarat.  
 
The opening of IBU in Gift City will be significant milestone in B ank’s growth story. This 
development will benefit us to expand our international banking business and also enable  us to 
provide  specialized  banking  services  to our customers.  
 
The I BU will provide the Bank to access International financial markets and allow delivering  
complete range of products to its corporate clients with foreign currency funding requirements. 
The Bank will offer suite of services including foreign currency loans, trade finance solutions, 
treasury and risk management products and enhanced convenient banking solutions to its 
customers .
