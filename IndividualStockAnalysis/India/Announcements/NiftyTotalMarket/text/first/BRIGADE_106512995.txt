PRESS RELEASE  
Brigade's Q3 FY26 Revenue grows 6% to ₹1,623 Crores as compared to Q3 FY25;  
PAT at ₹206 Crores,   
Quarterly collections at ₹ 1,760 Crores in Q3 FY26  
 
Bengaluru, January 30, 2026 : Brigade Group reported a total revenue of 1,623  Crores and Profit After 
Tax of ₹ 206 Crores in Q3 FY26. Net bookings in the real estate segment for Q3 FY26 stood 
at 1.33 Mn sqft with a sale value of ₹1,750 Crores. Collections for Q3 FY26 stood at ₹1,760 crores.   
   
"Our expansion of our land bank in our key markets of Bengaluru, Chennai, and Hyderabad, focusing 
on land parcels that conform to our specific requirements and quality standards to ensure long-term 
value creation for our customers. By maintaining a disciplined approach to capital allocation and 
focusing on high-quality assets, our commitment remains to prioritize long-term value creation while 
meeting the evolving demands of the residential, commercial, and hospitality sectors." said Pavitra 
Shankar, Managing Director, Brigade Enterprises Ltd.    
 
REAL ESTATE    
During the quarter, real estate revenue stood at ₹1,133 Crores as against ₹1,103 Crores in 
Q3 FY25, registering a growth of 3%. EBITDA for the quarter stood at ₹170 Crores.   In Q3 FY26 pre-
sales volume stood at 1.33 Mn sqft valued at ₹1,750 Crores. Real estate collections stood 
at ₹1,258 Crores.  
 
LEASING    
During the quarter, leasing revenue stood at ₹325 Crores, a growth of 16% compared to Q3 FY25 and 
EBITDA stood at ₹231 Crores.  Portfolio’s occupancy stood at 93% with an overall leasing 
of 8.61 Mn sqft out of 9.29 Mn sqft.  
 
HOSPITAITY    
During the quarter, hospitality revenue stood at ₹165 Crores, a growth of 12% compared to Q3 FY25 
and EBITDA stood at ₹58 Crores.    
    
Q3 FY26 CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS    
Revenue in Q3 FY26 grew by 6% to ₹1,623 Crores as against ₹1,530 Crores in Q3 FY25.  EBITDA stood 
at ₹459 Crores in Q3 FY26.  PAT  stood at ₹206 Crores in Q3 FY26.  
  
9M FY26 CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS    
Revenue in 9M FY26 grew by 16% to ₹4,386 Crores as against ₹3,781 Crores in 9M FY25. EBITDA in 9M 
FY26 grew by 4% to ₹1,209 Crores as against ₹1,166 Crores in 9M FY25.  
 
PAT in 9M FY26 grew by 24% to ₹534 Crores as against ₹431 Crores in 9M FY25.
