TCS to harmonise Metro's IT application landscape and support 
scalable growth  
TCS will help international wholesaler METRO simplify its technology environment, reduce operational 
complexity and transition from country -specific  systems  to a more central ly governed operating model  
FRANKFURT  | MUMBAI,  September  03, 2026:  Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS), a 
leading global IT services, consulting and business solutions company, has been selected as a strategic 
partner by international wholesaler METRO AG , to support the next phase of its international strategy by 
harmoni sing its application landscape, simplifying operations, and enabling scalable growth .  
As part of this partnership, TCS will help METRO transition from a highly locali sed, country -specific IT 
application landscape to a centrali sed technology environment  by leveraging AI -based  solutions . This 
transformation is designed to reduce complexity, streamline operations, and enable greater agility across 
the company’s major markets in Europe and Asia.  
METRO has built a strong international footprint with empowered local operations and is now evolving 
towards a more globally aligned and centrally driven technology landscape to support greater scale, 
consistency, and innovation. Through this engagement, TCS will help METRO in consolidating applications 
and technology operations across key markets , enabling the organi sation to free up resources and focus more 
effectively on customer growth , innovation,  and value creation.  
The partnership goes beyond technology harmonization and is a key component of METRO’s broader 
business evolution. It additionally involves significant change management efforts to support the shift 
toward s a more unified operating approach across the organisation.  
Dr. Khaled Bagban, Chief Information Officer, METRO AG, said,  “To advance our wholesale business, we 
need an IT landscape that combines strong local relevance with clear central steering. For METRO, this means 
building systems that are measurable in their impact, scalable across markets, and capable of turning 
complex ity into competitive strength. This partnership comes with high expectations: we need a partner who 
not only understands technology, but also how to translate it into tangible business value for our customers 
and our operations .”  
Bhuwan Agrawal, Head TCS Central Europe, said , “We are excited to begin this partnership with METRO. 
This collaboration represents a significant opportunity to accelerate METRO’s strategy. By bringing our 
collective experience in managing complex, large -scale transformations and leveraging AI -led capab ilities, we 
look forward to partnering closely with METRO  to support  its future growth ambitions. ” 
Abhijit Niyogi, VP and Business Unit Head, Retail – UK, EMEA and India, TCS, said , “This engagement reflects 
an important shift towards simpler and more effective ways of operating at scale. We are proud to partner 
with METRO on this journey and support its efforts to create long -term value through greater consistency, 
efficiency, and inn ovation."  
TCS possesses extensive experience in delivering complex, large -scale, multi -country transformation 
programs for global enterprises. Through this strategic engagement, TCS reinforces its role as a trusted 
partner for global enterprises undergoing complex business transformations . For METRO  AG, TCS will provide 
support  in building a future -ready, flexible, and efficient IT-operating model , better positioned to support

About METRO  
METRO is a leading international food wholesaler which specialises in serving the needs of hotels, 
restaurants, and caterers (HoReCa ) as well as independent merchants (Traders). Around the world, METRO 
has approx. 15 million customers who benefit from the wholesale company’s unique multichannel mix: 
customers can purchase their goods in one of the large stores in their area as well as by delivery (Food Service 
Distribution, FSD) – all digitally supported and connected. Parallel to this, the subsidiary METRO MARKETS 
operates an international online marketplace tailored to the needs of professional customers, which has 
been growing and ex panding steadily since 2019. Acting sustainably is one of the company principles of 
METRO which has been listed in various sustainability indices and rankings, including MSCI, Sustainalytics and 
CDP. METRO operates in over 30 countries and employs more tha n 84,000 people worldwide. In the 2024/25 
financial year, METRO generated sales of €32.4 billion.  
For more information, please visit  www.metroag.de . 
About Tata Consultancy Services Ltd (TCS)  
Tata Consultancy Services (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS) is the technology partner of choice for industry -leading 
organizations worldwide. Since its inception in 1968, TCS has upheld the highest standards of innovation, 
engineering excellence and customer service . 
It has set an aspiration to become the world's largest AI -led technology services company and is enabling its 
clients to transform themselves across the full AI stack, from infrastructure to intelligence.  
Rooted in the heritage of the Tata Group, TCS is focused on creating long term value for its clients, its 
investors, its employees, and the community at large. With a highly skilled workforce spread across 56 
countries and 194 service delivery centers  across the world, the company has been recognized as a top 
employer in six continents. With the ability to rapidly apply and scale new technologies, the company has 
built long term partnerships with its clients. Many of these relationships have endured in to decades and 
navigated every technology cycle, from mainframes in the 1970s to artificial intelligence today.  
TCS sponsors 14 of the world’s most prestigious marathons and endurance events, including the TCS New 
York City Marathon, TCS London Marathon and TCS Sydney Marathon with a focus on promoting health, 
sustainability, and community empowerment.  
TCS generated consolidated revenues of over US $30 billion in the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026.
