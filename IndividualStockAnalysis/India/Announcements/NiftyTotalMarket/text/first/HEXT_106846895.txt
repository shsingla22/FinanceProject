Hexaware Selected as Strategic Technology Partner by 
the European Bank for Reconstruction and 
Development  
Five -year partnership to support the EBRD’s Technology Blueprint Northstar  through AI and 
continuous improvement  
November  5, 2026 : Hexaware Technologies [NSE: HEXT], a global AI- ﬁrst digital and IT solutions 
company, has been selected as strategic technology partner for the European Bank for 
Reconstruction and Development (EBRD). The ﬁve -year Strategic Partner Framework covers the 
Bank’s Run- Change -Transform technology portfolio.  
The EBRD’s Technology Blueprint focuses on driving efficiency, effectiveness and innovation 
through digitally enabled processes powered by AI, data, and intelligent automation. Hexaware 
will help embed AI into the B ank’s operations and establish a continuous improvement engine to 
identify optimization opportunities, automate processes, and drive increased  efficienc ies in our 
delivery model . The partnership focuses on measurable outcomes alongside ﬁxed capacity and 
resource augmentation delivery.  
Partnership Highlights  
• Technology services: Software engineering and quality assurance; IT operations and 
support; IT security, identity and access management; cloud, data, and analytics; SAP 
application support and modernization; treasury risk operations; and low -code, no -code,  
and AI -powered development  
• As part of the framework, Hexaware plan to deploy their AI frameworks: RapidX® for 
agentic software development lifecycle acceleration; Amaze® for AI -powered data 
engineering and modernization; Tensai® Reasoning Ops for intelligent operations and 
AIOps  
• Delivery model: Hexaware operating as an integrated part of the EBRD’s technology 
organization, supported by onshore and offshore delivery centers, with enterprise -grade 
AI deployment at scale . 
Simon Reeves, Director of Technology, EBRD, said, “The Strategic Parter Framework is about 
creating deeper partnerships that are aligned to outcomes yet constantly drive continuous 
improvement. We want our strategic partners operating as part of one team, no matter where they 
are based geographicall y. As an existing Partner, we’re pleased to strengthen and deepen our 
relationship with Hexaware and look forward to their innovation, capability and fresh thinking while helping us to deliver the objectives of our Tech Blueprint.”  
Subhash Jose, Managing Director for IT and Chief Information Officer, EBRD , said, “Our Tech 
Blueprint is about building a technology organisation that continuously improves, adapts and 
delivers greater value to the Bank. The Strategic Partner Framework plays an important role in that 
journey, helping us to combine strong engineering foundations with AI, automation and modern 
ways of working to improve how we deliver technology services.”  
Parameshwaran Iyer, EVP & Head, Europe, Hexaware , said, “This partnership is a signiﬁcant 
validation of our AI -native solution delivery model and our commitment to global development 
and stability. We intend to take this approach to other International Financial Institutions and 
Multi -lateral Developme nt Banks to help them foster long -term economic growth across 
European nations”  
Parag Pawar, BFS Portfolio Head, Europe, Hexaware , said, “We’re excited to bring these 
capabilities to the EBRD and are committed to delivering sustained, measurable efficiency gains 
through intelligent automation, continuous improvement, and enterprise -grade, governance -
compliant AI deployment at scale.”
