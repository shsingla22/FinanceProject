Summary of Proceedings of the 14 th Annual General Meeting  
 
The 14 th Annual General Meeting (‘AGM’) of the members of Alivus  Life Sciences Limited (formerly Glenmark Life

 Mr. Hiren Patel, Chairman & Non- Executive Director presided over the meeting and welcomed the Members and 
Directors participating through video conference.  
 The Chairpersons of the Audit Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee and Stakeholders Relationship Committee were present at the AGM to address the queries of the Members, if any. The representatives of M/s. Walker Chandiok & Co LLP, Chartered Accountants and Bhadresh Shah & Associates ,
