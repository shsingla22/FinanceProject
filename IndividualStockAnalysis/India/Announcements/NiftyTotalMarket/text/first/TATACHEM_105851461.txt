The Board of Directors  had, at its meeting held on Monday, April 29, 2024,  recommended a dividend of

85th AGM.  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 42 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and

a) all Beneficial Owners in respect of shares held in dematerialized form as per the data as may be

 b) all Members in respect of shares held in physical form after giving effect to valid transmission or  
transposition requests lodged with the Company as of the close of business hours on Wednes day, 
June 12, 2024.
