Brief Profile (in case of appointment)  Mr. Ravindra Dhariwal is the Co -founder and Chairman of 
Sagacito  Technologies, a data analytics company that helps 
enterprises drive growth through advanced insights and 
analytics. A seasoned business leader with over four and a 
half decades of global experience, he has held several 
distinguished leadership roles, incl uding Group CEO of 
Bennett, Coleman & Co. Ltd. (The Times Group), India’s 
largest media conglomerate, and was the Worldwide 
President of the International News Media Association 
from 2011 to 2013. Prior to The Times Group, he spent 12 
years with PepsiCo as  its first employee in India, playing a 
key role in launching and expanding the company’s 
beverage business across India, Africa, and Southeast Asia. 
He began his career with Unilever in 1977, serving in sales 
and marketing leadership positions in India an d Australia. 
Mr. Dhariwal currently serves as a senior advisor, mentor, 
and board member to several leading listed and private 
companies, leveraging his extensive international 
experience to help organizations strengthen brands, build 
customer loyalty, and  achieve sustainable growth.
