Dr. Lalit Kanodia , Chairman Datamatics Group of 
Companies bestowed  with the ‘Transformational 
Impact on Industry 2024’ Award    
Mumbai, 26th February 2025 : Datamatics is proud to announce our Honourable 
Chairman, Dr. Lalit Kanodia , has been recognized with the prestigious 
‘Transformational Impact on Industry 2024'  award at the Market Research Society 
of India (MRSI) Golden Key Awards . 
Dr. Kanodia is one of th e pioneers in the Indian software and offshore services industry. 
He founded Datamatics in 1975, before which he was a founding CEO at Tata 
Consultancy Services in 1967.  Dr. Kanodia's  visionary leadership has been instrumental 
in transforming the Indian IT industry, driving digitization, automation, and AI -led 
innovation.  
Dr. Kanodia is a distinguished Alumnus Awardee of IIT, Bom bay. He earned his MBA 
(1965) and Ph.D. (1967) in Manage ment from MIT, USA, becoming the first Indian to 
receive a Ph.D. in Management from the institution.  He was a Consultant to Ford Motor 
Co., Arthur D. Little in the USA, Consultant to State Bank of India, etc., in India.  
Dr. Kanodia has a plethora of experi ence in various fields. He was the President of 
Management Consultants of India (1979 – 1980); Honorary Consul of Chile in Mumbai 
(2002 – 2014); Executive Board Member of MIT, USA (2009 – 2016); National President 
of the Indo American Chamber of Commerce ( 2015 – 2016); and President of IMC 
Chamber of Commerce and Industry (2017 – 2018). He is currently a Member of the 
Advisory Board of Bombay University.
