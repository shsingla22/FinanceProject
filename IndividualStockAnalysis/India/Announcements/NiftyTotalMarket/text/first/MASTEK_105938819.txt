London Metropolitan University drives new ways of working with 
Oracle Cloud HCM & Payroll in 12 months with Mastek  
Reading, UK, August 05, 2024: (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted digital engineering and cloud 
transformation partner , today  announced  the successful go -live of the first phase of London 
Metropolitan University's ambitious People and Finance D igital Transformation programme . This 
milestone marks a signific ant step forward in London Metropolitan University’s  journey to modernis e its 
back -office operations and enhance its digital capabilities through the implementation of Oracle Cloud 
solutions. 
London Met ropolitan University has demonstrated true flexibility and responsiveness to change by 
successfully delivering the first phase of its major People and Finance  transformation programme in May 
2024.   
London Met ropolitan University is a £150m+ turnover London -based University, currently with 12,000 
students enrolled on campus and 16,000 students off campus. London Met ropolitan University procured 
Oracle Cloud Fusion ERP, HCM and Payroll with Mastek as the delivery partner , with the programme 
launching in May 2023. The implementation plan was broken down into two core phases, starting with 
HCM and Payroll and then followed by ERP.  
HCM , Payroll, Talent Management, Learn, Oracle Recruitment Cloud,  and Oracle Time and Labor  went 
live over an unprecedented 12-month  period in May 2024, which is a first for an Oracle Fusion Cloud 
HCM & Payroll implementation in the sector.  The solution also included user -friendly transformational 
tools such as Oracle  Digital Assistant and Oracle Guided Learning , with tailor -made content delivered for 
the University by Mastek , which enhanced the user experience from day 1 of the launch. Phase two of 
the project, which is tracking very well, is due to go  live with ERP in August 2024, which was the original 
planned go -live date.  
One of the most important components in achieving this success has been  collaboration , and the 
success of this project has been down to the dedication and commitment of the London Met ropolitan 
University, Mastek , and Oracle teams  operating as a cohesive whole .  All the parties worked together as 
one project team, built trust , and worked as a partnership , each  bringing their expertise and knowledge 
to the project.   
This programme has been extremely well managed from day one . Key to this has been the agility of 
decision-making, all of which  have been made in a timely manner, with  risks proactively managed and 
mitigated by the teams.   This is down to the commitment of the entire London Metropolitan 
University  and Mastek team.    
Since going live, t he feedback from London Met ropolitan University  users  has been extremely  positive , 
and the adoption of the solution is increasing day by day.  We are now moving towards whole 
organization  adoption with  the introduction of the ERP  modules  from August.  
"Mastek's adaptability, scalability, and flexibility to support the London Met ropolitan  Team have been 
crucial to our programme's success. The one -team collaborative approach between the London 
 
Metropolitan University  and Mastek has fostered a unified, efficient, and straightforward approach, 
allowing us to address the challenges and risks proactively. Mastek's scalable resourcing model with 
continuous onsite presence from the start of the programme until go live backed up by their Centre of 
Excellence team, has enabled the proactive mitigation of risks and potential issues throughout the 
programme and ensured our ambitious HCM and payroll solution was successfully implemented within 
12 months. Their positive attitude a nd buy -in to the aggressive timelines truly set them apart from 
competitors, driving our collective success."  said Will Hopson -Hill, Director of Digital Transformation at 
London Metropolitan University . 
 “We’re delighted with the successful go -live of Oracle HCM and Payroll at London Metropolitan 
University.  The success of this programme was a result of integrated team effort, driven by trust, value, 
and velocity. Our continuous and close collaboration with various stakeholders at the client side ensured 
that all the timelines and associated risks were effectively managed. We congratulate the London 
Metropolitan University team on this milestone and look forward to supporting them  in their next phase 
of implementing Oracle ERP ," said Abhishek Singh, President of UKI & Europe at Mastek.
