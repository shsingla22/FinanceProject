Sub.: Energization of Further Capacity under the 370 MW AC / 677 MW DC Wind -Solar Hybrid Power 
Project  
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
  
 We wish to inform you that KPI Green Energy Limited ("the Company") has energized further capacity 
under its 370 MW AC / 677 MW DC Wind -Solar Hybrid Power Project (IPP) at Bharuch, Gujarat, 
comprising 130 MW AC /  195 MW DC Solar  capacity.  
 
With the energizing of the aforesaid capacity, the Company has cumulatively energized 269.7 MW AC / 
389.7 MW DC capacity under the said Wind -Solar Hybrid IPP Project. This project is being developed by 
the Company as an Independent Power Producer (IPP)  under a long -term Power Purchase Agreement 
with Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited (GUVNL) . 
 
The Company continues to steadily expand its Independent Power Producer (IPP) portfolio, as it 
simultaneously scales its EPC business. The progressive energisation of capacity under long -term PPAs 
further strengthens the Company's annuity -based, recurring revenue streams, enhancing earnings 
visibility and creating sustained long -term value for its stakeholders.
