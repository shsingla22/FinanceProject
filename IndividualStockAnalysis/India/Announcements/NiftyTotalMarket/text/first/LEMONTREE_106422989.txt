Lemon Tree Hotels signs 11th property in Punjab 
 
New Delhi, 31st October 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest-growing 
mid-scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the signing of its latest 
property, Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels, Kurali, Kharar.  
 
This hotel  will be franchised under Lemon Tree Hotels Limited for distribution and 
marketing purposes and further strengthens the company’s presence in Punjab, 
catering to both business and leisure travellers with a fresh, vibrant, and value-driven 
experience. 
 
Property Highlights  
Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels, Kurali, Kharar,  in the dynamic Mohali district 
of Punjab. Kurali is a rapidly developing hub in the Kharar tehsil that balances urban 
convenience with the buzz of urban growth. Just a short drive from Chandigarh, this 
fast-developing corridor is known for its scenic surroundings, easy accessibility, and 
growing mix of business and leisure hubs. Whether you’re visiting for work, a quick 
getaway, or simply passing through, Kurali offers the perfect balance of convenience 
and charm — where progress meets a slower pace of life. 
 
The key features of the hotel will include: 
 38 well-appointed rooms 
 A restaurant 
 A banquet 
 A conference hall 
 Recreational facility includes a fitness center 
 
The Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport, Chandigarh, is approximately 31 
kms away from the property, while the Chandigarh Railway Station is about 30 kms 
away. The hotel is well connected by roadways for both public and private transport 
from all major cities in India. 
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels, commented, "  With this signing, we are delighted to announce 
the signing of this new property in Punjab, where we already have two operational 
and eight upcoming hotels. This further strengthens our expanding footprint across 
key business and leisure destinations in North India. As the region continues to evolve 
into a vibrant residential and commercial hub, this property will offer travellers our 
signature blend of comfort, warmth, and refreshing hospitality that Lemon Tree 
Hotels is known for."  

 
 
 
 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India’s leading hospitality companies, 
catering to a wide range of customers – from value-conscious travellers to premium 
business and leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels & Resorts, 
Lemon Tree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys Select, and Keys 
Lite – the group offers experiences across upper upscale, upscale, upper midscale, 
midscale, leisure, wildlife, and spiritual segments. 
 
LTHL operates 120+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing 
pipeline of 120+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, 
Bengaluru, and Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, and 
Indore – and with an international presence in Dubai, Bhutan, and Nepal – Lemon 
Tree Hotels delivers exceptional comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, refreshing 
experience. 
 
Since opening its first 49-room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 240+ properties 
(operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for both business 
and leisure travellers.
