Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 ("Listing Regulations"), we wish to inform you that Apollo Defence Industries Private Limited 
("ADIPL"), a subsidiary of Apollo Micro Systems Limited ("AMS" / "the Company"), has today, i.e. 
17 November 2026, entered into a Binding Expression of Interest ("EOI") with A-Thon Allterrain 
Private Limited ("A-Thon") for the proposed acquisition of up to 26% of the fully diluted equity share 
capital of A-Thon (the "Proposed Transaction"). 
Building towards a larger stake :- The initial acquisition of up to 26% is the first step of a phased 
acquisition. Subject to satisfactory due diligence and definitive agreements, ADIPL intends to increase 
its shareholding in A-Thon in one or more further tranches at the same valuation as the initial 
acquisition. The Company will disclose each such step to the Stock Exchanges as and when it is 
finalised. 
Strategic significance :- A-Thon is an indigenous designer and manufacturer of all-terrain and side-by-
side utility vehicles and holds an Industrial Licence. The Proposed Transaction marks the Apollo 
Group's entry into a new Land Systems vertical, alongside its established capabilities in defence 
electronics, avionics, missile systems, underwater weapons and aerial platforms. A-Thon's all-terrain 
platforms are expected to provide last-mile ground connectivity for the Group's aerial platforms and to 
address the logistics requirements of the Armed Forces, including in high-altitude and difficult terrain. 
This positions the Apollo Group to offer integrated air–land solutions under the Government of India's 
Aatmanirbhar Bharat  initiative. 
The EOI contains legally binding provisions relating to exclusivity, standstill, confidentiality, costs and 
governing law. Under the exclusivity provisions, A-Thon and its promoters shall not engage with any 
other investor or acquirer until the completion of ADIPL's due diligence and for a period of 90 days 
thereafter. Under the standstill provisions, A-Thon shall not undertake any major corporate action 
without the prior written consent of ADIPL during this period. 
The Proposed Transaction is subject to satisfactory completion of comprehensive financial, legal, tax, 
technical, commercial and regulatory due diligence, negotiation and execution of definitive agreements, 
requisite corporate approvals of both parties, and all necessary statutory and regulatory approvals, 
including those under applicable defence-related regulations. The consideration and final structure 
(fresh subscription, purchase of existing shares, or a combination) will be determined following due 
diligence.
