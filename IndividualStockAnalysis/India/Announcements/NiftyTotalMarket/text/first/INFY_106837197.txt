PRESS RELEASE 
Infosys Announces Expansion of Pocharam Development Center in Hyderabad  
Infosys strengthens its presence in Telangana with a new 1.1 million sq. ft. facility, enhancing 
capabilities in AI, Cloud, Cybersecurity, and next- generation digital services  
Bengaluru, India –  October 30, 2026: Infosys  (NSE, BSE, NYSE: INFY), a global leader in AI -first business 
consulting and technology services, today announced the expansion of its Pocharam Development Center 
(DC) in Hyderabad, Telangana, with the inauguration of a new Software Development Block. The new 1.1 
million sq. ft. facility further strengthens Infosys' presence in Telangana and its commitment to the region's 
growing technology and talent ecosystem.  
 
The inauguration ceremony was graced by Sri Mallu Bhatti Vikramarka , Hon'ble Deputy Chief Minister, 
and Minister for Finance , Planning,  and  Energy, Government of Telangana ; Sri Duddilla  Sridhar 
Babu, Hon'ble Minister for Information Technology, Electronics & Communications, Industries & 
Commerce, and Legislative Affairs, Government of Telangana,  in the presence of Infosys leadership 
represented by Mr. Jayesh Sanghrajka, Chief Financial Officer, Infosys, along with Mr. Sunil Kumar 
Dhareshwar, Executive Vice President, Infosys; Mr. Niladri Prasad Mishra, Senior Vice President, 
Infosys,  and other senior officials from the Government of Telangana and Infosys.  
 Spread across 450 acres, the campus currently houses seven software development blocks with 
approximately 4.58 million sq. ft. of built -up area. The new block will add 10,000 seats, enabling Infosys to 
aim to support an additional workforce of over 13,000 employees under a hybrid working model.  
 
The Pocharam  Development Center supports global clients across Banking, Financial Services and 
Insurance (BFSI), Retail, Life Sciences, and Manufacturing. The expansion will further strengthen Infosys' 
capabilities in AI, cloud, cybersecurity, engineering and next -generation digital services. Aligned with 
Infosys' sustainability commitments, the new facility incorporates energy -efficient radiant cooling, smart 
lighting, rainwater harvesting, wastewater reuse and natural daylight -filled spaces to improve energy 
efficien cy and occupant comfort and wellbeing. It also features flexible workspaces, collaborative areas and 
employee wellness facilities to support a modern, hybrid work environment.  
 
Sri Mallu Bhatti Vikramarka, Hon'ble Deputy Chief Minister, and Minister for Finance, Planning, and 
Energy, Government of Telangana , said, “Telangana has established itself as a global technology 
destination, supported by world- class infrastructure, skilled talent and a thriving innovation ecosystem. The 
expansion of Infosys' Pocharam campus marks another important milestone, creating opportunities for our 
youth to participate in the next wave of technology -led growth. Investments of this scale reinforce 
 
Hyderabad's position as a preferred destination for global enterprises and advance our vision of a future-
ready, innovation- driven economy.”  
 Mr. Jayesh Sanghrajka, Chief Financial Officer, Infosys, said, “Hyderabad has played a pivotal role in 
our growth journey, and the expansion of our Pocharam  campus reflects our continued commitment to the 
region. This facility enhances our capacity to scale operations, build specialized talent, and deliver complex transformation programs for global clients. As enterprises accelerate their adoption of AI, the campus will 
serve as a strategic hub that brings together world- class talent, advanced infrastructure, and deep 
capabilities to help clients drive innovation, improve competitiveness, and realize sustained business 
value.”
