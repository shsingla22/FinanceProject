Revolt Motors Extends ‘Azadi From Petrol’ Offer Till August 2 3 
Following Overwhelming Response  
New Delhi, August 1 8, 2025:  Revolt Motors, India’s No.1 electric motorcycle brand, has announced 
the extension of its ‘Azadi From Petrol’  offer till August 2 3, 2025 , following an overwhelming customer 
response and unprecedented booking demand. The offer, launched earlier this month to celebrate 
India’s 7 9th Independence Day, has seen exceptional interest from riders looking to switch to electric 
mobility.  
This strong demand  reflects a growing consumer movement  that is not just about choosing electric, 
but about choosing independence from rising fuel costs, routine maintenance, and outdated ride 
experiences.  
 
With benefits worth up to ₹20,000 , the offer includes a Zero Insurance Fee  and additional savings on 
Revolt’s entire range of AI -enabled electric motorcycles. Customers now have an extended window to 
book their ride and unlock a smarter, cleaner, and more economical way to commute.  
"This Independence Day, we asked India to choose Azadi  not just in spirit, but on the roads ," said Mrs. 
Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises Limited , "We are not just selling motorcycles; 
we are enabling a shift in mindset. The response to the ‘Azadi From Petrol’ offer has been a powerful 
reminder that Indians are ready to ride into a fuel -free future. With this extension, we are giving more 
people the c hance to break free  once and for all. " 

   
The offer is valid across all authorized Revolt Hubs and on www.revoltmotors.com  till August 2 3, with 
no further extensions planned. This is the final window for customers to avail benefits under the 
campaign.  
Revolt’s product range includes the RV400 , RV400 BRZ , RV1, RV1+ , and RV BlazeX . All are engineered 
for Indian riders with connected tech, smart riding modes, and low running costs. The brand’s growing 
presence spans across 200+ Indian cities reflecting its ambition to lead the EV two -wheeler revolution 
nationwide.  
This Independence Day, Revolt is not just giving a discount . It is giving riders the power to choose 
freedom. From petrol. From compromise. From the past.  
For bookings and more details , visit www.revoltmotors.com  or your nearest Revolt Hub .
