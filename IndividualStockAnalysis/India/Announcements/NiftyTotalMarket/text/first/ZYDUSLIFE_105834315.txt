Dear Sir / Madam,  
 
Sentynl Therapeutics Inc., USA, a wholly owned subsidiary (“ Sentynl ”) of Zydus Lifesciences 
Limited (“the Company ”) has acquired worldwide proprietary rights to Zokinvy®  (lonafarnib)  
from Eiger Biopharmaceuticals Inc., USA (“ Seller ”), pursuant to an asset purchase agreement 
(“APA ”), adding to portfolio of biopharmaceuticals for rare and ultra -rare diseases.  
 
Zokinvy® is approved in the U.S. (2020), European Union and Great Britain (2022), and Japan 
(2024) for the treatment of Proger ia, a collection of ultra -rare, fatal, genetic premature aging 
diseases that accelerate mortality in young patients .
