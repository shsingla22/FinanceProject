PRESS RELEASE  
 
FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER & YEAR ENDED 31st MARCH 2026  
 
As per the Finance Act 2026 , Bank has taken a onetime impact of ₹ 632 crore due to recognition 
of Deferred Tax Asset @ 25.168% as against 34.944%. Major ratio  impacted due to the above 
cited action are –  
 Net Profit impacted.  
 ROA down to 0.56% from 0.91% for quarter -4 of Previous FY .  
 ROE down to 8.43% from 13.40% for q uarter -4 of Previous FY .  
 EPS down to ₹ 0.80 from ₹ 1.19 for quarter -4 of Previous FY .  
 
RESULTS AT A GLANCE  
 
Year 31st March 2026 vis a vis Year 31st March 2025  
 
 Total Business grew by 15.60% to ₹ 812439 crore  (Q4FY -25 - ₹ 702798 crore).  
 Total Deposits increased by 13.38% to ₹ 467923 crore  (Q4FY -25 - ₹ 412697 crore).  
 CASA Deposits stand at  47.30% of total deposits.  
 Gross Advance increased by 18.76% to ₹ 344516 crore  (Q4FY -25 - ₹ 290101 crore).  
 Credit – Deposit ratio stood at 73.80.  
 Gross NPA stood at 2.6 7%, registering an improvement of 51 bps.  
 Net NPA stood 0.49%, registering an improvement of 6 bps.  
 Provision Coverage Ratio stood at  95.97%.  
 Operating Profit for the FY has increased by 4.37% to ₹ 8479 crores from ₹ 8124 crores.  
 Net Profit for the FY has increased by 15.43% to ₹ 4369 crores from ₹ 3785 crores.  
 Net Interest Margin (NIM) stood at 3.07%, with a marginal decline, post absorption of 
the Regulatory rate cuts.  
 ROA ( Return on Asset) improved to 0.89% from 0.86%.  
 ROE ( Return on  Equity) improved to 13.00% from 12.48%.  
 Cost to Income ratio stood at 58.61%.  
 Slippage Ratio stood at 1.16%, registering an improvement of 29 bps.  
 CRAR improved to 17.91%, out of which Tier I is 15.61%.
