SUBJECT: Delisting of 41,17,40,908 Equity Shares of M/s BLS International Services Limited (“the 
Company”) from Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (“MSEI”) 
 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
In continuation of our intimation dated February 04, 2025 and February 11, 2025, this is to inform you that 
MSEI vide Circular No: MSE/LIST/16909/2025 dated March 24, 2025, bearing letter no. MSE/LIST/2025/298 
has granted, Delisting Approval for delisting of 41,17,40,908 Equity Shares of the Company bearing Face 
value of Rs. 1/- each, from the Capital Market segment of MSEI.  
 
The Equity Shares of the Company shall be suspended from trading w.e.f. Monday, March 31, 2025, and 
further will be delisted from the Capital Market Segment of the MSEI w.e.f. Monday, April 07, 2025. 
 
The Equity Shares of the Company will continue to remain listed and traded on National Stock Exchange of 
India Limited (“NSE”) and BSE Limited. 
 
You are requested to please take note of the same.
