of Rainbow Children's Medicare Limited (the "Company") for the quarter ended March 31, 2024 and 
for the year ended March 31, 2024 ("Statement"), attached herewith, being submitted by the Company 
pursuant to the requirement of Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended (the "Listing Regulations").

Statement:

Basis for Opinion 
We conducted our audit in accordance with the Standards on Auditing (SAs) specified under

Standards are further described in the "Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Standalone
