Shop LC  (US)  Conferred With the  ‘Entrepreneurial Vision Award ’ 
at Greater Austin Business Awards 2025  
Austin , November 27, 2025:  
Shop LC (US), a subsidiary  of the VGL Group, has been awarded the ‘Entrepreneurial Vision Award ’ 
at the ‘Greater Austin Business Awards 2025 ’ ceremony , recognizing our purpose -driven business 
model and commitment to delivering positive social & economic impact . 
The award acknowledges Shop LC’s innovative omnichannel retail approach, strong customer 
engagement initiatives, and its long -standing mission of ‘Delivering Joy ’. The Greater Austin 
Business Awards are organized annually to celebrate companies demonstrating leadership, 
innovation, and community contribution . 
Spokesperson from Shop LC (US) , said , “This award reflects strength of our mission -driven culture. 
We would like  to thank the entire Shop LC team for their commitment to delivering joy to our 
customers and our communities . One of our proudest achievements, ‘your purchase feeds…’  
program, has now delivered more than 57 million meals to children in US, demonstrating the impact 
that responsible business es like us  can create . We are further deepening our presence  through 
investments in jewellery casting  process in US , and ongoing local hiring .”

About Vaibhav Global  Limited  
Vaibhav Global Limited (VGL) is listed on stock exchanges in India (BSE: 532156, NSE: VAIBHAVGBL, ISIN - 
INE884A01027 ). VGL is an omni -channel E -tailer of fashion jewellery, accessories, and lifestyle products in 
developed markets with direct access to ~ 127 million households (FTE) through its TV home shopping 
networks – Shop LC in US , Shop TJC & Ideal World in UK  and Shop LC in Germany. The Company’s ecommerce 
websites www.shoplc.com  & https://mindfulsouls.com/  in the US, www.tjc.co.uk , www.idealworld.tv/  and 
www.rachelgalley.com  in the UK and www.shoplc.de  in Germany , complement TV coverage and diversify 
customer engagement. The Company is committed to ‘Delivering Joy’ to all the stakeholders. Through its 
flagship midday meal  program , ‘Your Purchase Feeds… ’ where a meal is provided for every piece sold at the 
retail channels, the Company has provided over 106 million meals in US, UK, Germany , and India at a run rate 
of ~55,000 meals being donated every school day  since program ’s inception .
