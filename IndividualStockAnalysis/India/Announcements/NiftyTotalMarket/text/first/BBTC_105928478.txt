We are pleased to inform you th at the Board of Directors of The Bombay Burmah Trading Corporation, 
Limited  (‘Corporation ’), at its Meeting held on May 13, 2024, has recommended a dividend of Rs. 1.20 
(60%)  per Equity share of face value of Rs. 2 each for the Financial Year ended 31st March, 2024.  
 
The Dividend will be paid after  on or after 22nd August , 2024  to those shareholders holding shares as 
on Friday, August 09, 2024, (cut -off date), subject to th e approval of Members at the 159th Annual 
General Meeting ("A GM") scheduled to be held on Friday, August 16 , 2024.  
 
As you are aware that pursuant to the Income Tax Act, 1961 ("the Act"), as amended by the Finance 
Act, 2020, divi dends paid or distributed by a C ompany on or after 1st April,  2020 shall be taxable in the 
hands of the shareholders.  
 
The Corporation  shall, therefore, be required to deduct tax at source ("TDS") at the time of making 
the payment of the dividend at the rates applicable on the amount of dividend distributed to the 
shareholders, if approved at the ensuing  AGM.
