Milestone with Enterprise -Wide Top Employer Certification  
The newly introduced Enterprise Certification  by the Top Employers Institute,  is awarded exclusively to a 
distinguished group of select companies for excellence in  employee experience  across regions , setting TCS 
apart for its  people practices  globally  
TCS’ recognition as a Global Top Employer for  2025  mark s the 10th straight  year  of this distinction, a 
testament to its pioneering employee engagement and talent development initiatives  
MUMBAI | February 18, 2025:  Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS) , a global leader in IT 
services, consulting, and business solutions, has achieved a significant milestone  by earning its first-ever  
Enterprise -Wide Top Employer Certification  from the Top Employers Institute, a global authority honouring 
excellence in people practices. This new  recognition is awarded exclusively to  a select group of  organizations 
that showcase consistent excellence in workplace practices across all key geographies  where they operate , a 
testament to TCS’ people -first practices.  This milestone builds on  TCS’ achievement as a Global Top Employer  
for 2025,  marking a decade  of receiving this distinction.  
The global certification follows  a series of regional  certifications with TCS being named a Top Employer in 30 
countries and regions, including Europe, the U nited Kingdom , the Middle East, North America, Latin America, 
and APAC . The achievement  reflects its industry -leading people practices and commitment to fostering a 
workplace that prioritizes employee well -being, continuous learning, diversity, and career growth across all 
its global locations.   
David Plink, Chief Executive Officer, Top Employers Institute , said, “We are in a time of rapid change, where 
technological, economic, and social factors continually reshape our world. These extraordinary times bring 
out the best in people and organizations. Being certified as a Global Top Employer for the 10th consecutive 
year demonstrates that TCS consistently prioritizes the growth and well -being of its staff, positioning the 
organization to thrive amid these rapid societal changes as a people -first business.  This year also marks 
another important milestone, as TCS has been recognized as an Enterprise Certified Top Employer. The Top 
Employers Enterprise Certification is a prestigious achievement that recognizes an entire organization as a 
Top Employer. This cer tification demonstrates TCS’s commitment to providing a consistent and exceptional 
employee experience across all regions and subsidiaries. With this recognition, TCS reinforces its status as an 
employer of choice, ensuring recognition for HR excellence at  every level of the organization. ” 
The certification involved a rigorous assessment of HR practices across six key areas and 20 topics, including 
People Strategy, Work Environment, Talent Acquisition, Learning, Well -being, Diversity & Inclusion, and 
Sustainability. This dual achievement underscores TCS’ unwavering commitment to people -centric growth, 
reinforcing its position as a leader in global talent development and workplace excellence.  TCS remains 
dedicated to providing a consistent, enriching experience, with extensive development opportunities and 
well-being initiatives, for its 607,000+ employees  across 55 countries , representing 152 nationalities with 
women comprising 35.3% of its workforce.  
Milind Lakkad, Chief Human Resources Officer, TCS , said, “Reaching this significant milestone of being 
named a Global Top Employer for 10 years in a row is a proud moment for TCS. It reflects our unwavering 
commitment to creating an environment where employees feel valued, empowered, and inspired to grow. 
With our people at the core of everything we do, we  continue to invest in world -class people practices that
