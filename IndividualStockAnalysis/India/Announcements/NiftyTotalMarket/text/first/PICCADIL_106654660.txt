PRESS RELEASE  
 
THE CROWN JEWEL OF INDIAN SPIRITS: INDRI REVEALS “ILIKA” – TRAVEL RETAIL EXCLUSIVE  
 
4th June ’26, New Delhi  - Indri Single Malt Indian Whisky, India's most awarded and  fastest -
growing single malt whisky, today announced the launch of Indri Ilika , an exclusive travel retail 
expression that celebrates the art of subtle peat, layered complexity and exceptional cask 
craftsmanship.  
Crafted at the Indri Distillery near the foothills of the Himalayas, Indri Ilika  is matured in a rare 
combination of Ex-Spanish Sherry  and Ex-American Bourbon oak casks , resulting in a whisky 
that is elegant, evocative and deeply rewarding. Designed exclusively for discerning global 
travellers, Indri Ilika embodies a refined balance of tropical fruit richness, gentle smokiness and 
remarkable depth.  
Derived from the Sanskrit word Ilika, symbolising  the earth and its hidden treasures, the 
expression reflects a whisky shaped by the timeless interplay of wood, fire and time. Unlike 
heavily peated styles, Indri Ilika offers a softer, more nuanced interpretation of smoke, allowing 
layers of ripe fruits, dried apricots, chocolate, vanilla, rich toffee and delicate spice to unfold 
gracefully before culminating in a long, smooth and gently smoky finish.  
The launch further strengthens Indri's reputation for pushing the boundaries of Indian whisky -
making through innovative and distinctive expressions. Over the past few years, Indri has 
consistently captivated whisky enthusiasts across the world with release s such as the acclaimed 
Diwali Collector's Editions, award -winning single casks, House of the Dragon collaborations and 
limited -edition travel retail exclusives.

Speaking on the launch, Madhu Kanna, Head of International Business, Piccadily Distilleries , 
said, "Indri Ilika represents our continued pursuit of innovation and our commitment to creating 
exceptional whiskies for evolving global consumers. Travel retail remains an important platform 
for discovery, and Indri Ilika has been crafted to offer travellers a  unique expression that 
showcases both the elegance of peat and the distinctive character of Indian single malt whisky."  
Since its launch, Indri has emerged as one of the most remarkable success stories in the global 
whisky industry. Recognized as the world's fastest -growing single malt, the brand has surpassed 
the landmark 100,000 -case sales milestone for two consecutive ye ars, driven by exceptional 
consumer demand across India and international markets. Its rapid ascent has redefined the 
global perception of Indian single malts, firmly establishing Indri among the most sought -after 
whisky brands worldwide.  
Its meteoric rise has been supported by an extraordinary run of international accolades. Indri's 
expressions have earned top honours at prestigious competitions across the world, including 
recognition at the World Whiskies Awards , Whiskies of the World Awards , International 
Whisky Competition, Tokyo Whisky & Spirits Competition , and numerous other global 
platforms. These achievements have not only elevated the brand but have also helped position 
India as one of the most exciting whisky -producing nations in the world.  
With the launch of Ilika, Indri once again reinforces its position at the forefront of India's 
premium whisky movement, creating world -class expressions that combine innovation, 
authenticity and craftsmanship while continuing to win the hearts of consumers  across 
continents.  
Available exclusively through select global travel retail channels, Indri Ilika will retail at 
approximately USD 80 , offering discerning whisky enthusiasts a distinctive and limited 
opportunity to experience this travel -retail -exclusive expression.  
Tasting Notes  
Nose:  Delicate hints of chocolate intertwined with subtle vanilla sweetness, dried apple, apricot, 
fruit jam and gentle peat dust with earthy undertones.  
Palate:  Tropical fruits, ripe banana, mango, rich toffee, light spice and soft peat smoke, balanced 
by refreshing melon and lingering fruity sweetness.  
Finish:  Sweet and salty nuances merge seamlessly into a smooth, elegant smoky finish with 
lasting fruit sweetness and gentle spice.

About Piccadily Agro Industries Limited (PAIL)  
 
Piccadily Agro Industries Limited (PAIL) is a publicly listed company with state -of-the-art 
manufacturing facilities in Indri, Haryana and Chhattisgarh, equipped with advanced technology 
for the production of Malt Spirit, Extra Neutral Alcohol (ENA)  and Ethanol.  
 
The company has emerged as a prominent player in the premium spirits segment, with award -
winning brands such as Indri Single Malt, Camikara, Cashmir, and Whistler gaining strong 
recognition across domestic and international markets.  
 
PAIL has further strengthened its leadership position in the Indian single malt category with 
Indri Single Malt becoming the fastest -growing single malt whisky brands globally. Driven by a 
strong focus on innovation, premiumization and strategic capacity e xpansion, the company 
continues to build a future -ready business while creating long -term value for its stakeholders.
