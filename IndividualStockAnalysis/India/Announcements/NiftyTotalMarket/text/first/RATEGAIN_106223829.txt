Noida, 08 May  2025: RateGain Travel Technologies Limited (RateGain),  a global provider 
of AI -powered SaaS solutions for the travel and hospitality industry , today announced that 
Cyprus Airways, the flagship  carrier of the Republic of Cyprus, has selected AirGain, 
RateGain’s advanced pricing intelligence platform  for airlines , to gain real -time competitive 
insights. This strategic collaboration underscores Cyprus Airways' commitment to 
delivering affordable and seamless travel experiences across its expanding network in 
Europe and the Middle East.  
Known for its customer -centric approach, the airline has earned a strong reputation among 
travelers connecting Cyprus to key destinations in Europe and the Middle East. By 
addressing challenges such as rising competition, fluctuating demand, and shifting 
customer preferences, Cyprus Airways continues to strengthen its presence in the region  
with focus on data -driven decision -making, smarter pricing, and enhanced travel 
experiences.  
With AirGain, Cyprus Airways will access real -time competitive pricing data from over 300 
airlines and 50+ OTAs. This enables the airline to refine its pricing strategies, optimize 
promotions, and maintain leadership in the aviation market. By leveraging d ynamic pricing 
and market trends, Cyprus Airways aims to deliver exceptional value to its customers while 
ensuring sustainable growth.  
Alessandro Schuler,  Head of Network and Revenue Management  at Cyprus Airways, 
said,  “In today's fast-paced aviation industry, staying competitive requires innovative 
solutions and actionable insights. With AirGain’s competitive pricing solution, Cyprus 
Airways can optimize our pricing and promotions to better serve our passengers while 
achieving sust ainable growth. This collaboration marks a significant step forward in our 
mission to  strengthen our market share and optimize pricing strategies  
Vinay Varma, Senior Vice President and General Manager at AirGain , commented, “ Our 
partnership  with Cyprus Airways, a prominent name in European aviation, signals an 
important move toward smarter, more agile travel. With AirGain’s AI -powered pricing 
intelligence, Cyprus Airways will navigate market complexities with greater precision, 
sharpen their  competitive edge, and deliver even more value to travelers. We’re proud to 
stand alongside them as they shape the future of air travel in the region.” 
Following the launch of AI -Digest, AirGain introduced the industry’s first daily route 
performance digest for its airline partners, delivering real -time insights into fare trends, 
market dynamics, and pricing anomalies. This AI -powered tool enables revenue  managers 
to make faster, more informed decisions, adapt to changing market conditions, and refine 
pricing strategies with greater confidence. As AirGain continues to drive innovation, this 
solution will be instrumental in helping airlines stay ahead in an  ever -evolving aviation 
landscape.
