Noida , November 11, 2026: Triveni Turbine  Limited (TTL),  a leading global steam turbine OEM with 
end-to-end capabilities for steam turbines and turbomachineries  up to 100 MW and manufacturing, 
refurbishment, retrofit, and lifecycle solutions for steam turbines up to 1,000 MW across leading global 
OEM  platforms,  today announced its performance for the second  quarter and half year ended 
September  30, 202 6. 
 
Commenting on the performance of the Company, Mr. Dhruv M. Sawhney , Chairman & 
Managing Director, Triveni Turbine Limited said , 
“During the first half of FY27, we delivered revenue growth of 6.4% Y -o-Y, with Revenue from 
Operations at ₹ 9.34 billion. Revenue for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹ 4.91 billion, 3.0% lower Y -o-Y, as strong 
domestic growth of 51.8% was offset by lower export revenue d uring the quarter.  As expected, the 
margins for the quarter came under pressure, as current deliveries largely reflect orders secured 
around a year ago, which carried a less favourable  product mix, along with the execution of a strategic 
order and higher input and logistics cost.  Going forward , we believe  these pressures will ease and 
should not weigh on profitability in the coming quarters .  
 
That said, our business fundamentals remain strong, as reflected in a healthy order book and record 
order booking , led by Europe, Turkey and the USA. Looking ahead, rising  global electricity demand 
and the transition to cleaner, more energy -efficient systems continue to support a favorable long -term 
environment. Emerging opportunities in the USA across carbon capture, long -duration energy storage 
and new power generation capacity , rising demand in Europe for clean energy solutions such as 
waste heat recovery and biomass, and sustained investment in domestic and international markets  
are expected to support our long -term growth . 
 
Order booking for the quarter was a record , growing 17.2% Y -o-Y to ₹ 7.64 billion, driven by our 
expanding global footprint and led by exports and the product business, with export order booking up 
49.8% Y -o-Y. For H1 FY27, we delivered record order booking of ₹ 13.33 billion, up  12.2% Y -o-Y, 
with strong international performance across North America, Europe, Southeast Asia and Turkey with 
exports and aftermarket contributing 56% and 29% respectively, strengthening our business mix. 
During the quarter, we made significant inroads into the combined cycle power plant segment globally . 
We secured boiler feedwater (BFW) pump package s with drive turbines for ultra -supercritical project , 
in India . Our closing order book stood at ₹ 24.53 billion, up 10.5% Y -o-Y, with the aftermarket order 
book more than doubling to ₹ 6.39 billion  on the back of contract wins in the rmal renewables (waste 
heat recovery  and biomass), combined cycle and utility segments and continued traction in 
refurbishment and performance optimization solutions.  Alongside, we have undertaken several 
 
 measures to strengthen our supply chain and operational excellence, including implementation of AI -
driven predictive maintenance and localized services globally. We have also augmented our 
production capabilities through the adoption of digitalized hydro t esting for turbine casings and 
advanced laser hardening processes.  
 
Our enquiry pipeline remains diversified. Internationally, it spans biomass, waste -to-energy, utility, 
geothermal and process co -generation applications, while domestic enquiries span  steel, cement, 
sugar and distillery, a long with API -compliant turbines for the utility segment. Together, these provide  
visibility for the coming periods. Backed by our presence across geographies and segments, our order 
book and our execution capabilities, we are well positioned  for an improved performance in H2 FY27 ."

Revenue from Operations for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹ 4.91 billion as compared to ₹ 5.06 billion in Q2 
FY26, lower by 3.0% Y -o-Y, with domestic sales up 51.8% Y -o-Y at ₹ 3.40 billion and export sales 
down 46.6% Y -o-Y at ₹ 1.51 billion. Exports constituted 30.7% o f revenue as compared to 55.7% in 
Q2 FY26, reflecting a higher contribution from domestic sales during the quarter. Product revenue 
grew 4.4% Y -o-Y to ₹ 3.43 billion, while aftermarket revenue declined 16.7% Y -o-Y to ₹ 1.48 billion, 
contributing 30 .1% of revenue. For H1 FY27, Revenue from Operations grew 6.4% Y -o-Y to ₹ 9.34 
billion, with domestic sales up 40.7% Y -o-Y at ₹ 5.80 billion and export sales down 24.0% Y -o-Y at ₹ 
3.54 billion. EBITDA for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹ 85 8 million, with EBITDA margin of 17.5% as compared 
to 26.3% in Q2 FY26. Profit Before Tax stood at ₹ 75 7 million, down 39.2% Y -o-Y, with margin at 
15.4% as compared to 24.6% in Q2 FY26. Profit After Tax stood at ₹ 53 3 million, down 41. 7% Y-o-Y, 
with margin at 10.9%.  
 
Order booking for the quarter stood at ₹ 7.64 billion as compared to ₹ 6.52 billion in Q2 FY26, a growth 
of 17.2% Y -o-Y, led by exports and the product business. Export order booking grew 49.8% Y -o-Y to 
₹ 3.68 billion from ₹ 2.46 billion in Q2 FY26 and acc ounted for 48 .1% of total order booking as against 
37.6% in Q2 FY26. Product order booking grew 32.5% Y -o-Y to ₹ 6.01 billion, while aftermarket order 
booking was lower by 17.8% Y -o-Y at ₹ 1.64 billion, contributing 21 .4% of total order booking 
compared with 30 .5% in Q2 FY26. Domestic order booking was marginally lower by 2.5% Y -o-Y at ₹ 
 
 3.97 billion. For H1 FY27, order booking stood at ₹ 13.33 billion as compared to ₹ 11.88 billion in H1 
FY26, a growth of 12.2% Y -o-Y. Export order booking grew 51.6% Y -o-Y to ₹ 7.52 billion and 
accounted for 56 .4% of total order booking as against 41.8% in H1 FY26, while aftermarket order 
booking grew 12.3% Y -o-Y to ₹ 3.87 billion, maintaining its 29% share. With exports and aftermarket  
making up a larger portion of incoming orders, our business mix continues to improve, adding to the 
quality and diversity of the order book . 
 
The closing order book stood at ₹ 24.53 billion as of September 30, 2026, as compared to ₹ 22.20 
billion a year ago, reflecting a growth of 10.5% Y -o-Y. Export orders grew 29.3% Y -o-Y to ₹ 14.52 
billion and accounted for 59 .2% of the closing order book, compared with 50.6% a year ago. The 
Aftermarket business continued to gain strong momentum, with closing orders increasing 105.4% Y -
o-Y to ₹ 6.39 billion and contributing 26 .1% to the closing order book, compared with 14% a year ago.  
The robust order backlog provides strong revenue  and margin  visibility for the coming quarter s.  
 
OUTLOOK  
 
We remain  well positioned to sustain growth momentum, supported by a healthy enquiry pipeline and 
a robust order backlog across both the Product and Aftermarket businesses. International traction 
continues to strengthen, with exports now accounting for 59% of  the closing order book , and our 
expanding global footprint, diversified portfolio and growing presence in new -technology applications 
position us well for the quarters ahead.  
 
The revenue and profit outlook for H2 FY27 remains positive, underpinned by our order book. 
Structural tailwinds, including increasing power requirements worldwide and a sustained shift toward 
efficient, low -carbon energy systems, continue to expand our addressable market. We also see 
promising prospects in the USA in carbon capture, long -duratio n energy storage and new generation 
capacity, alongside steady industrial investment across domestic and international markets.  
 
We expect performance to strengthen in H2 FY27 , as orders scheduled for execution carry a 
favorable mix, supporting both revenue and margins .
