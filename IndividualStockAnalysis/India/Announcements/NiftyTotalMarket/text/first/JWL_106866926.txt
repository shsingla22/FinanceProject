Press Release 
 
Jupiter Wagons secures repeat order worth over INR 128 crore 
from JSW (South) Rail Logistics  
 
National, November 20th 2026: Jupiter Wagons Limited (JWL), one of India’s leading rolling stock and mobility 
solutions companies, has received a repeat order worth over INR 128 crore from JSW (South) Rail Logistics Private 
Limited, deepening its partnership with the JSW Group. 
 
The order has been received in the form of a Letter of Intent from JSW (South) Rail Logistics Private Limited, valued 
at INR 128.61 crore (incl. GST), for the manufacture and supply of 5 BFNSM1 rakes comprising 302 BFNSM1 
wagons. 
 
This is Jupiter Wagons’ second BFNSM1 order from JSW (South) Rail Logistics, following an INR 122.88 crore order 
from the same customer in June 2026. With the latest win, the Company’s cumulative order intake from the JSW 
Group in 2026 reaches approximately INR 399 crore. 
 
The latest order sustains Jupiter Wagons’ strong run with private sector customers, which runs in parallel with a robust 
Indian Railways business that includes its recent INR 1,140 crore order from the Ministry of Railways for 2,583 
wagons. This balance across private and government demand gives the Company a diversified and resilient order book. 
 
Commenting on the development, Mr Vivek Lohia, Managing Director, Jupiter Wagons Limited, said: 
“A repeat order from the same customer is among the strongest endorsements a manufacturer can earn. JSW’s 
continued patronage underlines Jupiter Wagons’ position as a manufacturing partner of choice for private freight 
operators. As they invest more heavily in their own rolling stock, we are winning a growing share of that demand, 
even as our order flow from Indian Railways remains robust. This mix keeps our order book healthy and well spread, 
and we intend to keep earning our customers’ trust through the reliability of what we build.”
