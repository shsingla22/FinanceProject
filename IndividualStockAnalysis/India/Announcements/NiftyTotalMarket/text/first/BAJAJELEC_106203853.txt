Bajaj Lighting Announces Strategic Tie-Up with SEAK Energetics to 
Revolutionise Tunnel Lighting Technology in India  
 
 
Mumbai, India, 1 6th April 2025:  Bajaj Electricals – India’s trusted Consumer Appliances and Lighting Solutions 
company - is proud to announce a tie-up with SEAK s.r.o., a Slovakia- based pioneer in smart lighting and electric 
vehicle (EV) charging control systems. Bajaj Lighting is a leading force in India's Consumer Lighting and P rofessional 
Lighting industry . It is known for its expertise in indoor, sports, architectural and outdoor l ighting. As a frontrunner in 
tunnel lighting solutions, they sought a partner to deliver best- in-class tunnel l ighting control solutions. This  
collaboration focuses on advanced lighting control equipment specifically for tunnel lighting applications. SEAK 
Energetics, with its patented powerline communication control technology, is the ideal choice for this strateg ic 
alliance. This association marks a significant leap in tunnel lighting , paving the way for smarter, more efficient, and 
future -ready infrastructure across India.  
SEAK, established in 1988, has a distinguished history of developing  powerline communication technologies tailored for diverse lighting  solutions . Their expertise encompasses smart control systems for street and large -scale indoor 
lighting .. 
The Indian government's ambitious infrastructure development plans have placed a significant emphasis on tunnel construction to enhance connectivity and bolster economic growth. The government’s continued focus on enhancing connectivity through multiple tunnel projects —  with many more in the pipeline — highlights a growing 
infrastructure push. This momentum underscores the critical need for advanced tunnel lighting solutions that  can 
ensure both safety and operational efficiency.  
"Our collaboration with SEAK marks a pivotal milestone in our mission to transform tunnel lighting in India," said 
Rajesh Naik, Chief Operating Officer – Lighting Solutions, Bajaj Electricals Ltd.  "By combining SEAK's cutting- edge 
technology with our deep industry expertise, we are set to deliver intelligent and energy -efficient solutions that 
address the evolving needs of India's growing infrastructure. It is our constant endeavour to bring the wo rld's top 
technologies to India, creating greater value for our customers."  
Heliodor Macko, Chief Executive Officer of SEAK  s.r.o., commented, " Collaborating  with Bajaj Lighting offers an 
exciting opportunity to bring our innovative smart lighting technologies to the Indian market. Together, we aim to enhance the safety and sustainability of tunnel infrastructures, contributing to India's vision of world -class  
connectivity."  
Bajaj Lighting has already demonstrated its expertise in this domain by illuminating the prestigious Mumbai Coastal Road’s Tunnel, showcasing the company's capability to handle complex tunnel lighting projects effectively. This 

collaboration with SEAK is set to further solidify Bajaj Lighting's leadership in the tunnel lighting sector, enabling the 
delivery of state- of-the-art solutions that cater to the intricate requirements of modern tunnel environments.
