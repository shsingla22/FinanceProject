Non -Institutional Business  
IIFL Capital is a key player of the capital market with over 2,600+ partners  and 90+  branches across 
India to service clients.  
For the quarter, the average daily market turnover (including F&O) for the broking business was 
₹3,05,073 Crores (BSE + NSE), down  3% Q -o-Q and up 1 6% Y -o-Y.  
Financial Product Distribution AUM stood at ₹ 60,772  crores up 6% Q -o-Q.  
Investment Banking  
Investment banking division completed 28 deals across capital markets and private 
placement/advisory  in Q 2FY2 7. Select transactions completed include s IPO  for National Stock 
Exchange of India, Horizon Industrial Parks, Leap India, MilkyMist Dairy, Rento Mojo, Money View, 
Kanohar Electric als, Molbio Diagnostics, Manipal Payments, Laser Power & Infra , QIP for, Adani 
Enterprises Limited , Adani Energy Solutions , Aditya Infotech , Netweb Technologies , OFS for Life 
Insurance Corporation of India, Knowledge Realty trust  and other advisory transactions . The deal 
pipeline remains robust and there are multiple transactions which are at various stages of execution.  
Update on Fairfax Investment  
The Company has received all requisite regulatory and shareholder approvals for the proposed 
change in control pursuant to FIH Mauritius Investments Ltd  and its affiliate (“Fairfax”)  acquiring up to 
51% stake in the Company through a preferential allotment, open offer and arrangements with the 
existing promoters. The preferential allotment and open offer are under implementation and will be 
completed within the prescribed statutory ti melines. The promoter arrangements will be subject to 
the outcome of shares tendered in the open offer.  
Upon completion of the transaction, Fairfax  will join the Promoter Group alongside the existing 
promoters. Fairfax  will also have the right to nominate up to two directors to the Board, for which 
shareholder approval has been received.  
Proposed Incorporation of a Wholly Owned NBFC Subsidiary  
The Company proposes to incorporate a wholly owned subsidiary that will apply for an NBFC license . 
The initiative aligns  with the Company’s strategy to expand its financial services offerings and develop 
dedicated  lending and credit capabilities, with a focus on high -net-worth ( HNI ) and ultra -high -net-
worth ( UHNI ) clients. Subject to the requisite regulatory approvals,  the subsidiary is intended to offer  
structured lending solutions, including financing against financial assets, promoter funding and 
bespoke credit products to meet clients’  investment and liquidity needs . 
 
 
 
                       
 
Proposed Leadership Succession Planning  
As part of its leadership succession, the Board has approved the appointment of Mr. Vasudev 
Jagannath  as Additional Director and Whole -time Director for three years, subject to requisite 
approvals. With nearly two decades of experience with the Company, deep Capital market knowledge 
and broad leadership experience, Mr. Vasudev is well positioned to lead the Company through its next 
phase of growth and will accordingly be re -designated as Managing Director with effect from January 
1, 2027.  Mr. R. Venkataraman will continue to serve on the Board of Directors.
