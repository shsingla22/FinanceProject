Incorporation of a new entity 
*** 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI Listing Regulations, we 
wish to inform you that the Company has subscribed to 3,400 (Three Thousand Four Hundred) 
equity shares of Rs. 10/- (Rupees Ten only) each, aggregating to Rs. 34,000/- (Rupees Thirty-
Four Thousand only), constituting 34% of the paid-up equity share capital of SHAURYASTRA 
DEFENCE SYSTEMS PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated in India on 21st August, 
2026 under the Companies Act, 2013. 
 
Shauryastra Defence Systems Private Limited has been incorporated as a dedicated 
technology and development entity. The entity will pursue unsolved and futuristic operational 
problem statements of the Armed Forces, under the leadership of Dr. V. K. Saraswat, former 
Secretary, Department of Defence R&D and Director General, DRDO, while leveraging the 
Company's established design, development, manufacturing and integration capabilities. This 
move is a natural extension of the Company's four-decade journey in defence electronics 
towards complete weapon system solutions, in furtherance of the Government of India's 
Atmanirbhar Bharat vision, and is expected to strengthen the Company's long-term positioning 
as a total solutions provider in the defence sector.
