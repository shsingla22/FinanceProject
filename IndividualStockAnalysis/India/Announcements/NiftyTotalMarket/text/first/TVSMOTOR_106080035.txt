TVS Motor Company Introduces TVS iQube 2.2 kWh in Sri Lanka, 
Making Premium Electric Mobility Accessible to All  
 
Colombo , December 08 , 2024: TVS Motor Company (TVSM) - a leading global automaker 
in the two and three -wheeler segments - today launched a new 2.2 kWh battery variant of the 
TVS iQube electric scooter in Sri Lanka . TVS iQube now offers three variants in six vibrant 
colours, matching varying customer requirem ents.   
 
As a pioneer in sustainable mobility, TVSM remains steadfast in its dedication to the EV 
segment, delivering innovative, reliable, and exciting products. This commitment is further 
enhanced by a robust after -sales network, developed in partnership with TVS Lanka.  The new 
2.2 kWh battery variant of the TVS iQube  is designed to make premium electric mobility more 
accessible and reduce the total cost of ownership for customers.  
 
TVS iQube is inspired by three fundamental principles: Giving customers th e POWER OF 
CHOICE  for range, connected technology, charging solutions and price points; COMPLETE 
ASSURANCE  through vehicle safety and overall ownership experience and the SIMPLICITY 
OF USAGE  through easy -to-use features that enhance the riding experience o f customers.  
 
Commenting on the launch,  Madhu Prakash Singh, Vice President, EV, International 
Business, TVS Motor Company , said, “We are delighted by the growing adoption of the TVS 
iQube in Sri Lanka within just a few months of its launch. This reinforce s the trust our 
customers place in TVSM’s innovative and reliable EV solutions. Globally, the TVS iQube has 
crossed a milestone of 400,000  delighted customers, underscoring its success as a 
sustainable and future -ready mobility option. The launch of the new 2.2 kWh battery variant in 
Sri Lanka marks another step in our commitment to making premium electric mobility more 
accessible. With the fastest home charging in its segment,  achieving 0 -80% charge in under 
two hours, coupled with sign ificantly lower running costs, this variant is crafted to deliver 
greater convenience, affordability, and an unmatched riding experience.”  
 
Commenting on the launch, Geethal An thony , Chief Executive Officer , TVS Lanka , said 
“At TVS Lanka, we are proud of the enthusiastic response the TVS iQube has garnered in 
such a short span of time. The rising adoption of EVs in Sri Lanka’s two -wheeler segment is 
a promising sign of the country’s shift towards sustainable mobility, and we are dedicated to 
accelerating t his transition.  With the launch of the new 2.2 kWh variant, we are ensuring its 
availability across the island through our extensive sales network, supported by exceptional 
after-sales service, including readily available spare parts and skilled EV technic ians. ” 
 
The all -new TVS iQube 2.2 kWh battery variant is available for booking starting today on the 
TVS iQube Sri Lanka  website  (https://www.tvsmotor.com/en/lk/our -products/tvs -iqube ) and at 
14 TVSM dealerships across island country . The availability will be scaled to 25 more points 
of sale across Sri Lanka by January 2025.
