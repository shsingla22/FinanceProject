Allotment of Shares under ITC Hotels – Special Purpose Employee Stock Option 
Scheme 
 
In terms of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, 
we write to advise that the Company on 17th September, 2025 issued and allotted  
77,670 Equity Shares of ₹ 1/- each, upon exercise of 7,767 Options by Optionees under  
ITC Hotels – Special Purpose Employee Stock Option Scheme. The Meeting of the 
Committee which allotted the Shares ended at 10:45 a.m. 
 
Consequently, with effect from 17th September, 2025, the Issued, Subscribed and  
Paid-up Share Capital of the Company stands increased to ₹ 2,08,27,19,588/- divided into 
2,08,27,19,588 Equity Shares of ₹ 1/- each.
