IIFL Capital Credit Opportunities Fund  
IIFL Capital Credit Opportunities Fund achieves its first close at ₹500 crores driven by demand from 
HNIs and family offices.  The investment team has already commenced evaluation of opportunities, 
with the deployment of ₹75 crores in the first deal & remaining to be deployed shortly. The fund aims 
to deploy capital across a diversified set of credit opportunities, maintaining a disciplined 
investment approach whilst targeting attractive risk -adjusted returns.  
Investment Banking  
Investment banking division completed 9 deals across capital markets and private 
placement/advisory  in Q4FY26 . Select transactions completed include s IPO for Amagi Media Labs, 
Aye Finance, Clean Max Enviro Energy, Powerica Ltd and debt placement for Redbrick Offices & 
National Highway Infra Trust . The deal pipeline remains robust and there are multiple transactions 
which are at various stages of execution.  
Dividend  
The Board of Directors of the Company, in their meeting held on February 10, 2026, declared an 
interim dividend of ₹3 per equity share, each having a face value of ₹2, for the financial year 2025 -
26. The record date was set as February 16, 2026. The interi m dividend has been duly disbursed and 
is considered as final for the financial year 2025 -26.
