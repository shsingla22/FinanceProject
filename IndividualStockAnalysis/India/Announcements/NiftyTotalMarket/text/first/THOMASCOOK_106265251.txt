Sterling Champions Lifesaving Dialysis Access through CSR Initiative  
 
June 9, 2025 | Chennai, India  – Sterling Holiday Resorts, one of India’s leading hospitality brands, has 
deepened its commitment to community health by supporting a transformative dialysis care 
initiative for underserved regions. Through its Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) progr am, Sterling 
has partnered with the Fairfax India Charitable Foundation (FICF)  on The FICF Dialysis Mission:  a 
scalable healthcare model to fund the installation of dialysis machines in rural and semi- urban 
districts with limited access to life -saving treatment.  
In FY 2024 –25, Sterling contributed ₹ 7.5 million  to deploy 16 dialysis machines  across seven 
healthcare centres  in Tamil Nadu, Madhya Pradesh, and Assam. Building on this impact, the 
company has committed an additional ₹15 million  in FY 2025 –26 to install 22 more machines  
strategically located to serve the micro -communities in the vicinity of Sterling resorts. Combined, 
these 38 machines  are projected to deliver over 18,000 free or subsidized dialysis sessions, resulting 
in estimated savings of ₹36.4 million  for patients —many of whom face prohibitive healthcare costs.  
“This initiative reﬂects Sterling’s belief that hospitality brands can be forces for good,” said Vikram 
Lalvani , MD & CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts.  “Through this CSR initiative we are proud to extend our 
purpose beyond hospitality  to touch lives through essential healthcare access.”  
The FICF Dialysis mission is an eﬀort to take dialysis machines beyond the urban areas into 
underserved regions to bring down the barriers in access, aﬀordability and continuity of care. This is 
being done through a PPP (Public -Private -Partnership) model, combini ng government infrastructure , 
private healthcare operators  and CSR funding from corporate donors  like Sterling. The initiative aims 
to install 2,000 machines across 450 centers in 350 districts  to deliver 4.5 million free/subsidized 
sessions  and g enerate ₹920 crore in patient savings  while creating over 4,500 rural jobs . 
Through its support of this initiative, Sterling Holidays is demonstrating how responsible business can 
cataly ze inclusive growth, blending hospitality excellence with community transformation —one life -
saving treatment at a time.
