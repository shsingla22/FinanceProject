Russian Standard Vodka spreads its wings in India 
with ABD Maestro  
The new super -premium and luxury subsidiary of Allied Blenders and Distillers  
start s expanding  its portfolio with leading global  brand  
Mumbai, 17th June  2025:  ABD Maestro Pvt. Ltd.  (ABDM) , the super -premium and luxury 
spirits subsidiary of Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD), has introduced  Russian 
Standard Vodka , Russia’s number one premium vodka in the Indian market.  The three 
variants have Russian Standard Platinum as the luxury vodka with Gold  and  Original as the 
two super -premium avatars.  
This rollout marks the arrival of globally renowned Russian Standard to vodka  lovers  in India 
with its  unique taste , purity  and  smoothness . Crafted using glacial water from Lake Ladoga 
and winter wheat from the Russian Steppes, Russian Standard Vodka is currently available 
in over 85 countries and celebrated for delivering an authentic Russian vodka experience.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The overall vodka category in India was estimated at 1 4 m illion cases in 2024 , with annual 
growth rate in high teens . ABD M’s strategic partnership with t he Roust Corporation , one of 

 
the world’s leading vodka producers , marks the entry of first Vodka into the ABD Maestro  
portfolio of luxury  spirits ; further  strengthen ing its presence in India’s fast -growing premium 
spirits’  segment.  
Bikram Basu, Managing Director, ABD Maestro Pvt. Ltd. , said, “At ABD Maestro, we are 
curating a portfolio that reflects the evolving aspirations of the Indian consumer,  which is 
global, rooted in quality , and high on character. Russian Standard Vodka in Indian markets 
shows our intent to lead in the premium and luxury spirits. We have seen a gap between 
rising demand for authentic, high -quality vodka  and the limited presence of global leaders . 
Russian Standard is a brand with a fabulous story of quality and  precision .” 
Russian Standard Vodka will be initially available across all major markets in India , at 
premium retail outlets , hotels, bars and  lounges . Consumers will be able to choose from 
variants including Russian Standard Original (₹2400 ) for 750ML , Russian Standard Gold 
(₹2650 ), and Russian Standard Platinum (₹5300 ), available in 700 ML (prices quoted are for 
Maharashtra ). The brand will be supported by ABD’s robust distribution infrastructure  
configured for both retail and specialist on-trade channels . 
About ABD Maestro Private Limited  
ABD Maestro Private Limited is a new super -premium and luxury spirits brand company and a 
subsidiary of ABD. The brands which ABD Maestro is marketing currently  include Arthaus Blended 
Malt Scotch Whisky, Zoya Special Batch Gin and its flavours, Woodburns Contemporary Indian Malt 
Whisky, Pumori Small Batch Gin and Pink Gin, Segredo Aldeia Café and White Rums. Through its 
partnership with Roust Corporation, the company has introduced  Russian Standard Vodka to India 
in three distinct segments: Original, Gold, and Platinum . 
About Roust Corporation  
Roust is one of the largest vodka producers in the world. Roust owns and operates production and 
distribution facilities across Russia, Italy and Hungary. Its extensive portfolio includes Russian 
Standard, the world’s #1 premium Russian vodka and other fla gship brands such as Green Mark, 
Parliament and Talka, exported to over 85 countries. The portfolio also includes Gancia, the first 
Italian sparkling wine, as well as a number of worlds  known agency brands. Roust Group's Founder 
and Chairman of the Board of Directors is Roustam Tariko .
