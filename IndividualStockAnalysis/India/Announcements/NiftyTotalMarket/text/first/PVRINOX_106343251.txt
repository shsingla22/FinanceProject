PVR INOX OPENS BENGALURU ’S NEWEST CINEMA LANDMARK — AN 8 -SCREEN, 1222 -SEAT NATURE -
INSPIRED MULTIPLEX AT MAHINDRA MILLENNIUM MALL  
 
~ Nature -inspired design, bold interiors, AI -enabled occupancy tracking, India’s first dine -in auditorium 
restaurant, Kiddles  2.0, VR/AR gaming, Club Sapphire, and gourmet live kitchens — built with PVR  
INOX ’s own Capex ~  
National, 15 August, 2025: PVR INOX , India’s largest and most premium film exhibition 
company , proudly announces the launch of its 8-screen multiplex  at the newly opened 
Mahindra Millennium Mall  in Bengaluru . Designed under the company’s Capex by PVR  INOX 
model, this wholly owned and operated property  reflects the brand’s deep commitment to 
innovation , architectural storytelling , and thoughtful audienc e engagement . With a total 
capacity of 1222 seats , the cinema offers a holistic and immersive destination —where design , 
technology , and service  combine to create a richer movie -going experience.  
At the core of this development lies a guiding design ethos: “Experience Beyond Just Cinema.”  
PVR INOX envisioned this multiplex as more than a venue for films —it is a modern cultural 
space  for Bengaluru ’s evolved and discerning audience . From foyer to auditorium, every spatial 
decision is aimed at making cinema -going more intentional , relaxing , and sensorially rich . 
The concession lobby sets the tone with a warm, café -inspired atmosphere  blending natural 
finishes  and open -plan design . Muted green accents , vertical gardens , and biophilic elements  
connect the indoor environment with Bengaluru ’s love for greenery , while wooden furniture , 
soft upholstery , and illuminated movie displays  create a stylish yet approachable environment. 
Designed t o encourage dwell time , this space is further enhanced by AI-enabled occupancy 
tracking  and alert systems , ensuring smooth guest flow  and operational efficiency . 
Inside, each of the eight auditoriums offers a distinct visual identity  and cutting -edge 
techn ology . Guests can enjoy crystal -clear visuals powered by a combination of 2K Xenon , 
advanced 4K Laser projection , and the yet -to-be-commissioned Samsung ONYX LED cinema 
screen , which will deliver next -level contrast  and brightness  for the dine -in auditorium . 
Premium Large Format (PLF)  enthusiasts can experience Big Pix , featuring the largest 4K laser 
projection system  integrated with RealD 3D  for spectacular scale and depth. Complementing 
the visuals, the cinema is equipped with Dolby 7.1 surround sound , immersive formats like 
Dolby ATMOS and DTS:X , and sculpted sound solutions for LED screens —ensuring a broader 
sweet spot  for heightened audio immersion . 
Standout experiences include India’s first dine -in auditorium restaurant , serving a curated 
gour met menu  prepared in a live kitchen  and delivered directly to guests’ seats; Kiddles 2.0 , an 
upgraded kids’ auditorium with playful interiors  and family -friendly seating ; and Club Sapphire , 
a premium -format auditorium  with plush recliners  and bespoke servi ce. The experience 
extends beyond the screen with VR/AR gaming stations  in the public areas, adding interactive 
 
entertainment  to the visit. From in-seat dining lounge concepts  to sleek acoustic  wall panelling , 
asymmetrical light installations , and plush re cliners , the property reflects a careful balance of 
design innovation  and audience comfort . 
The material palette —ranging from beige and brown tones  to self-patterned textures  and LED-
enhanced details —supports a seamless fusion of aesthetics  and function . Every space is crafted 
to foster an emotional connection  between the viewer and the venue, from the first step in the 
lobby to the last frame on the screen.  
Mr. Ajay Bijli , Managing Director, PVR INOX Limited , said: “Our vision has always been to make 
cinema -going an experience that resonates far beyond the film itself. With Mahindra 
Millennium Mall, we’ve created  a space that reflects Bengaluru ’s spirit while setting new 
benchmarks for design , technology , and hospitality . It’s an embodiment of where we see the 
future of cinemas —immersive , inclusive , and deeply connected to the communities they serve.”  
Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli , Executive Director, PVR INOX Limited , added: “This cinema is a 
culmination of our most distinctive concepts —from India’s first din e-in auditorium restaurant  
and Kiddles 2.0  to Club Sapphire  and immersive gaming zones —brought together in one 
landmark property. It reflects our wider strategy of creating destination cinemas  in high -growth 
urban centres , where design , technology , and differentiated experiences  can redefine how 
audiences connect with the big screen .” 
With this opening, PVR INOX  strengthens its presence in South India  and sets a new standard 
for design -forward multiplexes . Developed under the Capex by PVR  INOX model, the  Mahindra 
Millennium Mall  property underscores the company’s confidence in creating self-owned, high -
performance cinemas  that integrate entertainment , environment , and engagement . The new 
multiplex is not just another movie theatre —it is a place for communities to connect, relax, and 
celebrate the power of storytelling  through shared space  and design .
