Resurgent ESG Services Private Limited Assign s ESG Rating to PNB Housing Finance  
 
● PNB Housing Finance  has been assigned an ESG score  of 82.10  by Resurgent ESG Services, a 
subsidiary  of Resurgent India  Limited .  
● The rating was conducted using Resurgent ESG’s proprietary framework, combining data and 
direct engagement.  
● The rating recognizes  PNB Housing Finance’s  ongoing efforts to enhance its ESG practices . 
 
Delhi/NCR, September 3 , 202 6 - Resurgent ESG Services Private Limited (Resurgent ESG), a 
wholly owned subsidiary of Resurgent India Limited and a SEBI -registered Category -II ESG Rating 
Provider (ERP), has assigned an ESG rating to PNB Housing Finance . The ESG score assigned to the 
PNB Housing Finance  based on data for the FY 202 5-2026 stands at 82.10 , which corresponds to an 
ESG Rating of A+ , placing the company  within the ‘Leader’ category.  
Demonstrating sustained ESG excellence, PNB Housing Finance has maintained an A+ ESG rating for 
the 2nd consecutive year.  
PNB Housing Finance , one of the country’s leading deposit  taking housing finance compan y, has been 
evaluated through Resurgent ESG’s rigorous and comprehensive ESG assessment framework. The 
rating reflects the company’s current ESG profile, capturing the environmental and social impact of 
its housing finance business, as well as the strength of its governance practices in supporting 
responsible, transparent, and inclusive access to housing finance . 
 
Commenting on the importance of ESG ratings, Mr. Sanjeet Kumar, Director, Resurgent ESG Services 
Pvt. Ltd.,  said, “ ESG ratings are becoming increasingly important for businesses as they influence 
investor confidence, strengthen brand reputation, and demonstrate a company’s preparedness to 
meet evolving regulatory expectations, including SEBI’s BRSR framework . For, companies, strong ESG 
performance is not just about meeting compliance requirements. It reflects sound ESG management, 
responsible lending, stronger customer trust, and a long -term commitment towards sustainable and 
inclusive growth. Ultimately, ESG is about building resilient and future -ready institutions that can 
create sustainable value for all stakeholders .” 
 
Resurgent ESG’s rating methodology goes beyond publicly available disclosures, incorporating direct 
engagement with key stakeholders within the rated entity. This approach ensures that the ratings 
consider  both quantitative and qualitative factors, providing investors and market participants with a 
reliable view of the entity’s sustainability profile and future outlook.
