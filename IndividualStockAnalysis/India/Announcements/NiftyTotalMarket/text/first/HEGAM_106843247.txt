Noida, 3 November 2026:  HEG Advanced Materials Limited (NSE: HEGAM | BSE: 509631), part 
of the LNJ Bhilwara Group, today announced its financial results for the quarter and half year 
ended 30 September 2026.  
For H1 FY27, consolidated revenue from continuing operations stood at ₹ 725.80 crore and total 
income at ₹870.7 7 crore, with EBITDA of ₹438.0 0 crore and profit after tax of ₹269.0 7 crore. As 
on 30 September 2026, consolidated cash and bank balances stood at ₹ 1322.84  crore.  
For Q2 FY27, the Company reported consolidated revenue  from continuing operations of ₹429.94 
crore, total income of ₹546. 59 crore, EBITDA of ₹312. 78 crore and profit after tax of ₹208. 89 
crore.

Business highlights  
● Advanced Materials (TAC C): Global lithium -ion battery production is expected to grow from 
about 2.3 TWh in 2025 to about 8 TWh by 2035, with non -China anode demand set to exceed 
1 Mn tons by 2030 as cell makers diversify their supply chains.  
 
To serve this demand, construction of the Phase I 20,000 MT anode materials facility has 
reached 61% overall progress, with the process building at 72.25 COD  planned  for June 2027.  
Phase II, an additional 24,000 MT plant targeting COD in March 2029 on the existing 
infrastructure, power con nection and land at the Dewas site, will take total anode capacity 
to 44,000 MT.  TACC's R&D pipeline includes next -generation silicon -doped and graphene -
based anodes for higher energy density and faster charging . TACC received the CII Award 
for Excellence in R&D 2026 in the Materials, Chemicals & Textiles - Large Enterprise category 
in October 2026.  
Battery Energy Solutions (REPLUS Engitech):  REPLUS has deployed over 1 GWh of energy 
storage capacity across BESS, electric mobility, telecom power solutions an d hybrid  
 
 
 
 
energy applications. Alongside its operational 1 GWh Giga Factory in Pune, a 5 GWh high -
speed, fully automatic prismatic line expansion facility is under construction, which will 
take total manufacturing capacity to 6 GWh on completion.  
 
● Green Power Businesses:  The Green Power Businesses have a combined hydroelectric 
installed capacity of 278 MW in Himachal Pradesh, through the 86 MW Malana Power 
Company Limited project (MPCL) and the 192 MW AD Hydro Power Limited project, along 
with a 14 MW win d power project in Maharashtra (under HEGAM). Both operating hydro 
projects and the wind project are debt -free, and the existing hydro assets generate annually 
stable cash flows of over ₹300 crore. The 200 MWh BESS IPP in Gujarat for Gujarat Urja Vikas 
Nigam Limited, with a total capex of ₹227.8 crore, has reached 64% project progress and 
targets commissioning in Q3 FY27.  
The HEG Composite Scheme became effective on 1 September 2026. Under the Scheme, the 
graphite electrodes business was transferred to HEG Limited (earlier known as HEG Graphite 
Limited), and Bhilwara Energy Limited was amalgamated into the Company. The resu lts 
accordingly present continuing and discontinued operations separately. Current financial figures 
are not comparable to previous financial figures owing to demerger of HEG Limited.  
About HEG Advanced Materials Limited  
HEG Advanced Materials Ltd., part o f the LNJ Bhilwara Group is a new -age platform spanning 
advanced battery materials with a strong manufacturing, R&D, and innovation foundation, and 
also into the business of battery energy solutions & green power. Compounding on HEG’s 50+ 
years of expertis e in graphitization and manufacturing know -how, the platform is developing a 
large-scale anode materials business and future material development, including silicon -based 
anodes. Its capabilities span research to revenue, from advanced research and product  
development to commercialization, serving the evolving EV, BESS, and energy -storage 
ecosystem.
