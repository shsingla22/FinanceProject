Nikhil Kamath’s WTFund and Nazara announce winners of WTF Gaming 
Podcast: Norian Games and Xigma Games                 
 
Hyderabad, India – 15th November 2024  – WTFund by Nikhil Kamath, and Nazara 
Technologies Limited, a prominent global gaming and sports media platform, are excited to 
announce two winners  Norian Games and Xigma Games.  
 
As part of its mission to empower the Indian gaming sector, during the popular WTF podcast 
on gaming, Nikhil Kamath via the WTFund and Nitish Mittersain via Nazara had pledged an 
overall investment of ₹2 crore subject to necessary approvals. As a follow up , the teams have 
now announced the winners to be Norian Games and Xigma Games.    
   
Norian Games, based in Kerala, is developing “Aldian of Ancients”, a dinosaur survival 
simulation game for PC and console, designed to offer players a captivating and immersive 
experience in prehistoric landscapes.  
 
Xigma Games, an indie studio headquartered in Bangalore, has achieved notable success 
with “The Bonfire: Forsaken Lands” and “The Bonfire 2: Uncharted Shores”, which together 
have amassed over 10 million downloads. The studio’s latest project, “Metal Haven ”, is a free -
to-play, sci -fi action -strategy game.  
 
By supporting Norian Games and Xigma Games, the intent is to nurture homegrown talent 
and contribute to India’s standing in the global gaming arena. Through this investment, Norian 
Games and Xigma Games will gain financial support and  access to mentorship and strategic 
guidance, enabling them to navigate the competitive landscape and scale their ventures 
effectively.  
 
Nikhil Kamath , investor and entrepreneur, commented, “Growing up, we were occupied by 
simpler things – a game of cricket or even just being outside. That was a large part of my 
childhood. For the younger generation, gaming has taken that place, creating new avenues 
for creativity and interaction. This fund is a small step in supporting companies that have made 
a mark with their innovative games, and I look forward to seeing them push boundaries even 
further.”  
 
Nitish Mittersain , Founder and CEO of Nazara , added, “At Nazara, We are committed to 
nurturing the next generation of gaming talent in India and it great to join hands with visionary 
tech pioneers such as Nikhil Kamath to act on the vision of our Prime Minister in building 
strong gaming talent in In dia that can build games for the world”
