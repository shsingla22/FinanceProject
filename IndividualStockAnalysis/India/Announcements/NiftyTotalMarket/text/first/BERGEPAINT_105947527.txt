Sub :   Intimation under Regulation 30 of  the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing 
Regulations”) 
 
In continuation to our earlier letter dated 15th May, 2024 and in compliance with Regulation 
30 of the Listing Regulations, we would like to inform you that based on recommendations of 
the Compensation and Nomination and Remuneration Committee of the Company, the Board 
of Directors of the Company vide  its meeting held earlier during the day i.e., 9th August, 2024, 
had considered and approved the following decisions  – 
 
i. The Board accepted the request of Mr Kuldip Singh Dhingra, Chairman and                       
Mr Gurbachan Singh Dhingra, Vice Chairman of the Company to step down from 
their respective post with effect from close of business hours on 12th August, 2024. 
The Board took on record its deep sense of appreciation for the services rendered 
by them during their tenure. 
 
ii. The Board also unanimously decided to appoint Ms Rishma Kaur, Non-Executive, 
Non-Independent Director as Chairperson and Mr. Kanwar Singh Dhingra,           
Non-Executive, Non-Independent Director as Vice-Chairman of the Company 
w.e.f. 13th August, 2024. 
 
iii. However, considering the vast business experience of Mr Kuldip Singh Dhingra 
and Mr Gurbachan Singh Dhingra and in order to guide their successors with their 
innovative ideas, strategic thinking, leadership and to assist the Board with their 
experience, vision, deep knowledge of the business and their expertise, it was 
decided to designate Mr. Kuldip Singh Dhingra as Chairman-Emeritus and                         
Mr Gurbachan Singh Dhingra as Vice-Chairman-Emeritus of the Company w.e.f. 
13th August, 2024.
