Mr. Subramanian Sarma elevated as Whole -time Director & 
President  
Mumbai, 9th May 2024  
Larsen & Toubro is p leased  to announce  that Mr. Subramanian Sarma  who has been 
Wholetime Director & Sr. EVP Energy is elevated  as Whole -time Director & President, 
Energy  with immediate effect. He will continue with his current responsibilities towards  
Hydrocarbon, Power, and Green Mfg. & Development businesses . 
 
A graduate in Chemical Engineering, Mr. Sarma completed his master’s from IIT Mumbai. A 
seasoned professional, he has over  40 years of experience, with 30 years being in the Middle 
East. During his career span, Mr. Sarma has handled the complete Oil & Gas value chain 
across various geographies . Prior to joining L&T, Mr. Sarma served as Managing Director of 
a large EPC company.  
 
Mr. Sarma joined the L&T Board in August 2015 and has been leading Hydrocarbon Business  
since then . Under his leadership, Hydrocarbon  Business  has emerged as one of the leading 
EPC Contractor  globally, achieving record financial results and also being ranked among the 
top 3 EPC contractors in the Oil & Gas  Sector  (Middle East ) for four consecutive year s. 
 
On this occasion Mr. S.N.Subrahmanyan, C hairman & Managing Director  L&T said “Mr. Sarma 
is credited with changing and reconfiguring  L&T’s H ydrocarbon Business  by bringing in global 
best practices in Project Management, G overnance Mechanisms, Environment  Health & 
Safety  (EHS), HR, and Technology  and thereby transforming it into a leading global EPC 
player . 
 
Mr. Sarma said “ I am honored to accept this position. I want to extend my heartfelt gratitude 
to L&T Board and our CMD for entrusting me with this incredible responsibility. I am truly 
humbled by your confidence in my leadership abilities, and I am committed to continue t his 
journey of growth and innovation alongside each  one of you.”
