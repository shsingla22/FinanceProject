Sub: - Allotment of 22,20,000 equity shares to the PEL ESOP Trust (‘ESOP Trust’) pursuant to 
Premier Energies Limited - Employee Stock Option Plan, 2021 (ESOP Scheme). 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
(‘SEBI Listing Regulations’), this is to inform that the members of the Board of Directors today, i.e. 26th July 
2025, has allotted 22,20,000 fully paid-up equity shares of Rs. 1/- each to the ESOP Trust under the ESOP 
Scheme. The equity shares allotted to the ESOP Trust shall be transferred to, or held for the benefit of, eligible 
employees under the ESOP Scheme upon the exercise of Options that were granted to them prior to the 
Initial Public Offering. 
 
Consequent to this allotment, the paid-up share capital of the Company stands increased from 45,07,74,368 
equity shares of Rs.1/- each amounting to Rs 45,07,74,368/- To 45,29,94,368 equity shares of Rs.1/- each 
amounting to Rs 45,29,94,368/- 
 
Requisite disclosure under Regulation 10(c) of the SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) 
Regulations, 2021 is enclosed in Annexure A  and Disclosure pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 as Annexure B . 
 
You are requested to take the above information on record. 
 
Thanking you,
