Lemon Tree Hotels s igns new property in Itanagar, Arunachal Pradesh  
 
 
New Delhi 14th June 2025  - Lemon Tree Hotels announces its latest signing 
Lemon Tree Hotel, Itanagar , Arunachal Pradesh . The property  shall be managed 
by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree 
Hotels Limited .  
 
Lemon Tree Hotel, Itanagar will feature 70 well -appointed rooms, a restaurant,  a 
bar, a banquet, a meeting room, a swimming pool, a gym, a spa and other public 
areas. The Donyi Polo Airport, Itanagar is approx. 25 kms away from the property.  
Naharlagun (Itanagar) Railway Station is approx. 12 kms from the property. The 
hotel is also well -connected by road, offering easy access to both public and 
private transport.  
Itanagar , the capital and largest town of Arunachal Pradesh  derives its name from  
Itafort , ‘the fort of bricks’ situated in the heart of the city. The irregular -shaped 
fort dating back to the 14th -15th century was built by Mayapur of Ramachandra, 
a king of the Jitari Dynasty. It is one of the most important historical sites in the 
state. Other places of interest include Jawaharlal Nehru Museum, Itanagar  which 
showcases the rich tribal culture of the state.  Visitors also frequent the Gekar Sinyi 
(Ganga Lake) , a beautiful natural lake  for picnic s and recreation.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels commented, “ We are excited to expand further in the eastern 
part of the country . This signing marks a significant milestone in our growth 
journey, as it will be our first property in Arunachal Pradesh. It will further 
strengthen our expanding portfolio. ” 
 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of the largest hotel chains in India and 
owns/leases/operates/franchises hotels across the upscale, upper -midscale, 
midscale and economy segments. LTHL delivers exceptional service offerings that 
are both differentiated and superior, underscored by a compelling value 
proposition.  
 
The company's portfolio comprises seven distinct brands, each tailored to cater to 
diverse guest needs across segments. These include Aurika Hotels & Resorts, 
Lemon Tree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox Hotels, Keys Prima, Keys Select 
and Keys Lite.  
 
 

   
 
 
 
 
Since opening its first hotel with 49 rooms in May 2004, Lemon Tree Hotels has 
delivered exponential growth with a portfolio of over 210 hotels today. This 
includes more than 110 operational hotels, with over 100 new properties slated to 
open soon.  
 
Strategically located across key metro regions including Delhi/NCR, Mumbai, 
Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Chennai, Lemon Tree also maintains a strong 
presence across Tier II, III and IV cities in over 70 destinations including Pune, 
Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, Jaipur, Indore, Aurangabad, Udaipur, Visakhapatnam, 
Kochi, Ludhiana, Thiruvananthapuram, Vijayawada and more . The company has 
also expanded internationally to Dubai, Bhutan and Nepal.
