receipt of the communication  UBL was allotted Plot No. A -1 (42 acres) of leasehold land by BIADA 
located at Kopakalan Industrial Area in Patna in 2011 (‘ Land ’) for 
setting up a Brewery, which commenced operations thereafter. 
Following the implementation of Bihar's prohibition policy, the unit 
was forced to cease beer production and subsequently obtained 
approval for a change in product category from Beer to No n-Alcoholic 
Beverages. However, during periodic inspections, BIADA found the unit to be non -operational and, after issuance of notices, cancelled the 
allotment/lease in 2022 on account of the non -operational status of 
the unit and non -utilisation of the al lotted land.  
 UBL challenged the cancellation before the Patna High Court, which 
passed a status quo order in relation to the land, and the matter 
remained pending. Subsequently, BIADA introduced the Amnesty 
Policy, 2025, pursuant to which UBL applied for restoration of  the 
allotment; UBL submitted the requisite documents & compliances and withdrew the writ proceedings.  
 BIADA has now issued an office order restoring the allotment of the 
aforesaid Plot to UBL under the BIADA Amnesty Policy, 2025. The 
allotment of Land has been restored vide the above communication , 
subject to specified conditions.  Key conditions include submission of a compliance/acceptance letter within 15 days, achievement of trial 
and commercial production milestones within 18 and 24 months, 
respectively, obtaining all statutory approvals, submission of project 
financing & implementation details, payment of applicable charges, 
and compliance with BIADA's conditions regarding use of the land . 
 
The communication formally notifies UBL of BIADA's approval for 
restoration of Land under the Amnesty Policy, 2025 and sets out the 
conditions that UBL must comply with for the said restoration.
