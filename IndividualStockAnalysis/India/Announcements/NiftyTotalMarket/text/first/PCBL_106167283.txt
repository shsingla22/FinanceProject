This is in furtherance of our disclosure dated 28 December 2023 with respect to Order -in-Original dated 26th December, 
2023 (“ Impugned Order ”).  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations, this is to inform you that the Company has been issued an 
Order -in-Appeal  (“Appellate Order ”) by the Ld. Joint Commissioner (Appeals), Central Tax and Central Excise, Kochi  in 
pursuance to an Appeal preferred by the Company against the Impugned Order . The Appellate Order has the effect of 
reducing the original tax demand from Rs. 7,46,626/ - to Rs. 1,41,913/ - and penalty from Rs. 74,663/ - to Rs. 20,000/ - for 
the period July 2017 to July 2018.  
The Company shall challenge the Appellate Order to the extent it confirms the reduced demand by way of filing a further 
appeal before the relevant authority, within the prescribed time limit, in view of the merits existing in our favour.
