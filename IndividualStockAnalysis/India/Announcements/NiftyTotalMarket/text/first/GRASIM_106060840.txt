Sub: Resignation as branch auditor of Grasim Industries Limited 
Dear Sir, 
We were appointed as the branch auditor of Grasim Industries Limited ("the Company') at its annual 
general meeting held on August 20, 2024 for a period of one year till the conclusion of 78th annual 
general meeting. 
In light of the recent emerging regulatory developments regarding the responsibilities cast on the 
principal auditors of the Company relating to group audits, we hereby tender our resignation as the 
branch auditor of the Company effective from today's date, with a view to comply with such 
interpretation in spirit. This has also been discussed with the Management of the Company. Our 
resignation is not resulting from any inability to get required information or sufficient audit evidence.
