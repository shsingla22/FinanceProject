Dear Sir/ Madam 
Pursuant to SEBI Circular Ref. No. HO/38/13/11(2)2026 ‑MIRSD‑POD/I/3750/2026 dated January 30, 
2026, we wish to inform you that the entire equity shareholding of Fedbank Financial Services 
Limited (“the Company”) is presently held in dematerialised form , except for one shareholder  who 
had, upon request, opted for rematerialisation of his shareholding from “DEMAT”  mode to physical 
mode. 
The Company had processed the rematerialisation request and the said shareholder is currently 
holding 5 (Five) shares in physical form. 
As per the aforementioned SEBI Circular, the requirement to publish a newspaper advertisement 
regarding the opening of the “special window” for transfer and dematerialisation would arise only 
where physical shareholders exist to whom the dematerialisation/transfer window is applicable.  
The Company was listed on BSE Limited  (BSE) and National Stock Exchange of India Limited  (NSE) on 
November 30, 2023 with 100% DE MAT shareholding. Accordingly, no share transfer requests were 
lodged prior to 2019. In view thereof, the newspaper publication and reporting requirements 
prescribed under SEBI Circular Ref. No. HO/38/13/11(2)2026 ‑MIRSD‑POD/I/3750/2026 dated January 
30, 2026 is not applicable to the Company.
