Subject: Clarification on delay in disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015  
Dear Sir/  Madam,  
 
This is with reference to your query regarding the delay in submission of disclosure under Regulation 30 of the 
SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.  
 
In this regard, we submit that officials from the Bureau of Indian Standards ( “BIS”) conducted a surprise visit 
at Greater Noida facilit y of the Company  on March 24, 2026. Pursuant to the said visit , certain product samples 
were taken  and certain inventory was seized , and the officials indicated that a formal communication/  notice 
would  be issued subsequently.  
 
At that stage, the information available with the Company was preliminary and incomplete, and a formal  notice 
from BIS, outlining the proposed course of action, was awaited.  Considering the nature of the event, the 
Company undertook an internal assessment in consultation with its legal advisors to evaluate the implications, 
including materiality of the event, and awaited formal communication from BIS to ascertain complete and 
accurate facts.  
 
However, in the absence of any further communication from BIS within a reasonable time, and in order to ensure 
transparency and avoid any further delay, the Company, on a suo motu basis, proceeded to make the disclosure 
to the Stock Exchanges.  
 
The Company respectfully submits that the delay was neither intentional nor deliberate, but occurred due to the 
need to verify facts, assess materiality, and await official confirmation from the regulatory authority.  
 
The Company remains committed to timely and transparent disclosures in compliance with applicable regulatory 
requirements.  
 
Kindly take the above on record.
