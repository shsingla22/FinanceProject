ANNEXURE -I 
 
Reference: BHEL intimation to SEBI bearing ref.: CC/MISC/2025 -26/26 dated 03.09.2025  
regarding LOI from MB Power (Madhya Pradesh) Limited  
 
1. Vide above referred intimation, pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Part A (B) of 
Schedule III of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, it was informed that Bharat Heavy Electricals Ltd. ('the Company") had 
accepted a Letter of Intent (LOI) from MB Power (Madhya Pradesh) Limited for 
supply of equipment (Boiler, Turbine, Generator) for 1x800 MW Anuppur Thermal 
Power Project.  
 
2. Pursuant to Regulation 3 0 read with Part A (B) of Schedule III of SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, it is hereby 
informed that the LOI was subject to execution of a formal contract within the 
agreed timeline. However, despite multiple discu ssions and extensions, the 
contract could not be executed, as per the terms of LOI. Accordingly, BHEL’s 
acceptance to LOI dated 03.09.2025 stands withdrawn.  
 
 
3. BHEL has informed the withdrawal of BHEL’s acceptance to LOI to customer at 
1636 hrs on 17.04.2026 .
