Thomas Cook India opens outlet in Chromepet to tap into Chennai’s 
expanding travel market

 Growth drivers include exclusive  Tamil group tours – accompanied by Tamil  speaking, experi enced Tour

Mumbai,  September 19, 2024:  Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel services company, has 
inaugurated its new franchise outlet in C hromepet, Chennai  - to leverage the high- growth market of the city and 
its surrounding catchments. Chromepet is a preferred residential locality in Chennai - with proximity to the airport 
and connectivity to prominent areas, including IT and business parks.  The new Chennai outlet expands Thomas 
Cook India’s network to 9 locations in the city and 1 5 across Tamil Nadu.  
 
In its commitment to enhance access and convenience,  Thomas Cook’s new outlet will address diverse 
customer segments - families, couples, Young India’s , millennials & Gen Z, business professionals  and seniors. 
Thomas Cook’s Chromepet outlet offers end- to-end travel services: International & Domestic Holidays (Group 
Tours, Personalised H olidays, Cruises, etc.), Value Added Services like Travel Insurance, etc . Additionally, 
Thomas Cook’s AI booking platform as sists customers in planning their personalized vacations in real time by 
checking availability, and making reservations in a matter of minutes . 
 
Customers can also benefit from the Thomas Cook Grand Indian Holiday Sale , till September 25th, 2024. On 
offer are a range of b enefits like e xciting discount of upto Rs. 50000, 0-0- 1 EMI Offer ( 0% Down Payment, 0% 
Interest, 1% Processing fees), prizes for customers booking holiday above Rs. 1.5 lacs like American Tourister

Travel Trends from Chennai: 
 
• Increase in multiple trips per year: from 2 to 4/5 annual holidays  
• Longer stays: from 3 days to 5 - 8 days for short trips; from 5 days to 10 - 15 days for longer trips  
• Chennai customers are well educated, discerning and look for authentic local experiences.  
• Growth of experiential travel: e.g. Northern Light tours in Finland, whale watching in Sri Lanka  
• Emergence of  Bizcations/B -Leisure : extending business trips  or getting family to join for a mini- vacation  
• Emergence of fresh new destinations: Driven by easy visa regimes and connectivity, increased demand 
for Azerbaijan, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Georgia and Vietnam
