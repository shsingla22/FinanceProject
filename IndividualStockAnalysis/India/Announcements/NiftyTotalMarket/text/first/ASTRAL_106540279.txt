 Sub.:  Press Release -  Astral Limited Inclusion in S&P Global Sustainability

 Dear Sir/Madam,  
 Astral Limited (“Astral”) is pleased to announce its inclusion as a Member of the S&P 
Global Sustainability Yearbook 2026, a globally recognized distinction that highlights 
companies demonstrating strong performance across environmental, social, and governance (ESG) parameters relative to global industry peers.  
  
The Sustainability Yearbook is based on the S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment (CSA), one of the most comprehensive global ESG benchmarking frameworks. In 2026, more than 9,200 companies were assessed globally, of which only 848 companies achieved membership in the Yearbook. Astral is proud to be among this select group of companies recognized for their sustainability performance.  
 Astral Limited’s S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment (CSA)  score 
improved to 60 from 48  in previous period.  
 Selection reflects Astral’s continued progress in strengthening governance practices, enhancing transparency, and integrating sustainability into its core business strategy and operations.  
  
Astral remains focused on further strengthening its sustainability initiatives by adopting 
global best practices, enhancing disclosures, and driving operational efficiencies. The Company believes that strong ESG performance supports long- term value creation for 
all stakeholders.
