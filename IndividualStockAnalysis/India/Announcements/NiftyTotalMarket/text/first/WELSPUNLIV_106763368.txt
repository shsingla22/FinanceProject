Dear Sirs/ Madam, 
Subject: Update on acquisition of 26% equity stake in Clean Max Dhyuthi Private 
Limited 
In furtherance to our disclosure dated May 15, 2026, wherein it was informed that the 
Board of Directors of Welspun Living Limited (“WLL” or the “Company”), at its 
meeting held on May 15, 2026, had approved the acquisition of 48,599 (Forty-Eight 
Thousand Five Hundred and Ninety-Nine) equity shares of Rs. 10/- each of Clean 
Max Dhyuthi Private Limited (“CleanMax”), representing 26% of the total paid-up 
equity share capital of CleanMax, from Welspun Corp Limited (“WCL”), a promoter 
group company, for a consideration of Rs. 760 lakhs (Rupees Seven Hundred and Sixty 
Lakhs only). The transaction was proposed to be completed by August 31, 2026. 
CleanMax is primarily engaged in the generation and supply of solar and renewable 
energy under a captive model. 
Considering the current demand, supply and availability of power at the relevant 
location, WLL and WCL have mutually agreed not to proceed with the proposed 
acquisition/sale of the aforesaid 26% equity stake in CleanMax. 
Accordingly, the proposed acquisition by WLL will not be executed and the 
transaction stands cancelled. 
Kindly take the above mentioned on record.
