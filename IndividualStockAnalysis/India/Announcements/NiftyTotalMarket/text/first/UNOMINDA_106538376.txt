Dear Sirs, 
Pursuant to the Notice issued by the National Stock Exchange of India ("NSE") dated December 16, 
2025, a fine of Rs. 5,000/-plus applicable GST was levied on Uno Minda Limited ("the Company") for

half year ended September 30, 2025, under Regulation 23(9) of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

the fine amount on or before February 26, 2026. 
The Company would like to inform that the payment of the aforesaid fine was remitted to NSE on 
December 18, 2025.

note of the same, including the waiver application submitted by the Company, and advised the 
Compliance Officer to ensure that all filings are done within the prescribed time lines mentioned under 
Listing Regulations. The Board further directed to strengthen internal systems to prevent recurrence 
of such issues and-advised that, in case of any future difficulties in filing, the coricerned Stock Exchange 
should be contacted promptly for necessary support and advice.
