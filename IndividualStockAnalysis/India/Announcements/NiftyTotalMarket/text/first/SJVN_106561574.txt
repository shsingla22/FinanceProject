PRESS RELEASE   
 
 
SJVN achieves 1 billion  units generation milestone at 1 ,000 MW Bikaner Solar Power 
Project  
 
SJVN Limited has achieved a significant milestone with its 1000 MW Bikaner Solar Power 
Project by successfully generating 1 Billion  Units (BU) of energy on March 20, 2026. This 
achievement marks a major step in the company’s mission to contribute clean and sustainable 
energy to the national grid.  
 
The project is located in Bikaner district of Rajasthan and is being implemented through its 
wholly owned subsidiary, SJVN Green Energy Limited (SGEL). It has been developed under 
the Central Public Sector Undertaking (CPSU) Scheme – Phase II, Tranche III of the Ministry 
of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), Government of India.  
 
The foundation stone of the project was laid by the Hon’ble President of India, Droupadi 
Murmu, on January 3, 2023, at Jaipur.  
 
The project achieved its Commercial Operation Date (COD) on December 24, 2025, 
reinforcing SJVN’s commitment to enhancing the nation’s renewable energy capacity.  
 
The project is expected to generate approximately 2,454.55 million units (MU) of electricity in 
its first year and about 56 billion units cumulatively over 25 years. Over its operational life, it 
is projected to reduce carbon emissions by approximately 2.7 8 million tonnes, contributing 
significantly to environmental sustainability.  
 
The tariff for the project has been competitively fixed at ₹2.57 per unit, ensuring affordable 
green power. The generated electricity will be supplied to the states of Rajasthan, Jammu & 
Kashmir, and Uttarakhand.  
 
Chairman & Managing Director, SJVN Limited,  said: 
 
“The achievement of 1 Billion Units generation from our 1000 MW Bikaner Solar 
Power Project is a testament to SJVN’ s strong commitment towards accelerating India’ s 
clean energy transition. This milestone reflects our technical excellence and 
operational ef ficiency in executing large -scale renewable energy projects.”  
 
He further added:  
 
“SJVN remains dedicated to expanding its renewable portfolio and contributing 
meaningfully to the Government of India’ s vision of achieving 500 GW of non -fossil 
fuel capacity. Projects like Bikaner are pivotal in ensuring energy security while 
promoting su stainability.”  
 
Director (Personnel), SJVN,  said:  
 
“This milestone underscores SGEL’ s capability to deliver large -scale solar projects 
within stipulated timelines and achieve early generation benchmarks. The Bikaner 
project stands as a model for future renewable projects in terms of scale, efficiency, and 
environmental impact.”
