 Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 - Allotment of 30,54,849 Equity 
Shares pursuant to the mandatory conversion of 43,000 Compulsorily Convertible 
Debentures (CCDs) 
 
The Company vide intimation dated 5 October, 2023 had informed stock exchanges regarding the allotment of 2,00,000, 7.5% CCDs of face value Rs. 1,00,000/- each to eligible qualified 
institutional buyers at an Issue Price of Rs. 1,00,000/- per CCD. In terms of the Placement 
Document, each CCD remaining outstanding as on the maturity date – 30 September, 2026 will be automatically and compulsorily converted into equity shares.   
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended from time to time (the “ SEBI 
LODR Regulations ”), we hereby inform you that the QIP Committee of the Board of Directors 
of the Company has approved the conversion of 43,000 (Forty-Three Thousand) Compulsorily 
Convertible Debentures (“ CCDs ”) of face value of Rs.1,00,000/- each into 30,54,849 (Rupees 
Thirty Lakh Fifty-Four Thousand Eight Hundred and Forty-Nine Only) equity shares of face 
value of Rs. 2/- (Rupees Two) each (“ Equity Shares ”) of the Company at a conversion price of 
Rs. 1,407.59/- (Rupees One Thousand Four Hundred and Seven and Paise Fifty-Nine Only) per equity share. The conversion price is computed at 16.50% discount to the seven trading days 
volume weighted average price of equity shares of the Company traded on NSE preceding               
30 September, 2026 as per the terms of the issue of CCDs.  
1. The Equity Shares so allotted rank pari-passu  with the existing equity shares of the 
Company in all respects, including as to dividends and voting rights and will be listed on BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited.
