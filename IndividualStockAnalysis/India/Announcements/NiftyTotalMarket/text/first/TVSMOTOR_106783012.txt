• Festive Cheer Extended Across TVS Apache p ortfolio and TVS Ronin  
 
Bengaluru/ New Delhi , September 17,  2026 : TVS Motor Company (TVSM), a part of 
TVS VENU, today brought t he spirit of performance to the festive season with new 
enhancements across its premium motorcycle portfolio - TVS Apache  and TVS Ronin.  
At the heart of this festive refresh is TVS Apache  portfolio - a performance- first, 
technology -forward and design- led brand born from TVS R acing . For over two decades, 
every TVS Apache has carried the brand’s ‘Track to Road’ philosophy , translating 
racing expertise into exhilarating everyday performance.  
Today, with over 7 million TVS Apache riders across 90 countries, the brand has 
evolved into a global performance motorcycle community. This festive season , TVS 
Apache brings its latest performance, technology and design enhancements to riders, 
strengthening the experience that has defined the brand for more than 20 years.  
The TVS Ronin brings its own distinctive expression with a new variant  and 
enhancements across the Mid and Top variants , combining contemporary design, 
effortless performance and modern technology for riders who choose to stand apart.  TVS 
Ronin’s #Unscripted philosophy continues to resonate with riders seeking individuality and self -expression, with a global community of over 200,000 customers.   
TVS Apache RTR  
With over four decades of racing heritage, TVS Apache continues to bring together Racing DNA, performance and precision, technology, distinctive design and the thrill of 
riding . This spirit is amplified by the Apache Owners Group (AOG), one of India’s most 
passionate riding communities, with over 500,000 registered members brought together 
through rides, track experiences and immersive brand engagements.  
 
Marking the milestone of over 7 million TVS Apache riders worldwide, TVSM has 
enhanced the TVS Apache RTR 160, TVS Apache RTR 160 4V and TVS Apache RTR 200 4V , with each motorcycle building on a distinct aspect of performance,  bringing new 
technology and features across safety, connectivity, convenience, rider control and 
design  to the TVS Apache RTR portfolio.

TVS Apache RTR 160 - Confidence & Control : 
The TVS Apache RTR 160 is strengthened with Traction Control System , Hazard 
lamps & LED TSLs  bringing greater confidence and control to every ride.  
TVS Apache RTR 160 4V - Connected Performance : 
The TVS Apache RTR 160 4V brings technology closer to the rider with a TFT display 
with Google Map Mirroring, Panic Brake Alert  and a Type -C charger – combining 
connected technology with the motorcycle’s performance character.  
TVS Apache RTR 200 4V - Performance & Rider Control : 
The TVS Apache RTR 200 4V builds on its performance credentials with Front 
Adjustable USD suspension , enabling greater rider control, while TFT display  with 
Google Map Mirroring, Panic Brake Alert  and Type -C charg er add to its technology 
proposition.  
With this update, the existing top- spec variants of the TVS Apache RTR 160, TVS Apache 
RTR 160 4V and TVS Apache RTR 200 4V will transition seamlessly into their new 
configurations, further strengthening TVS Apache's premium technology proposition.
