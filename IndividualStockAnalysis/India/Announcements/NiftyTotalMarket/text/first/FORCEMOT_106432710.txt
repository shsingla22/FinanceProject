2. The Ministry of Environme nt, Forest and Climate Change has notified the Environment Protection (End-of-Life Vehicles) Rules. 2025 ("ELV

obligations are to be fulfilled through the purchase of EPR certificates from registered Vehicle Scrapping Facilities via a Centralised Online Portal.

certificates are yet to be developed.

implementatio n framework for determining the reliable estimate is established.
