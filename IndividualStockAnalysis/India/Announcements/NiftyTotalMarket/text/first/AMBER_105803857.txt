Amber Group extends gratitude to Government of India over PLI disbursal 
approval for AC components 
 
New Delhi, 1st April 2024:  Under the Government of India’s transformative Production Linked 
Incentive (PLI) scheme for AC components, major PLI applicants including Amber Group, has been 
approved  for the disbursal of PLI benefits for manufacturing AC components. 
 
Speaking about this milestone and extending his gratitude towards the government, Mr. Jasbir 
Singh, Chairman and CEO, Amber Group  said, "The PLI scheme has been instrumental in propelling 
the Indian consumer durables industry and in realisation of Atmanirbhar Bharat in this segment. Since 
its inception, this scheme has boosted the domestic manufacturing capacity for ACs, jumping from 
25% in 2021 to 45% till date, showcasing the remarkable impact of this initiative. On behalf of Amber 
Group, I extend our sincere gratitude to the government, DPIIT and the Ministry of Commerce." 
 
With this PLI approval, the Company is now well-positioned to take its manufacturing prowess to 
the global with an aim to achieve 75% local value addition in air conditioner and its components 
production within the stipulated time frame of FY28.
