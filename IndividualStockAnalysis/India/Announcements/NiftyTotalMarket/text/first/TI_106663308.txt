NEWS RELEASE 
Tilaknagar Industries’ Flagship Brands Achieve Global Recognition in Drinks 
International 's Millionaires' Club 2026  
Record Breaking May 2026 Sales Reinforce Strength of Tilaknagar Industries' Portfolio  
Mumbai, June 12, 2026 : Leading Indian-Made Foreign Liquor (IMFL) manufacturer, Tilaknagar Industries 
Limited (TI) (BSE: 507205 | NSE: TI) has earned significant global recognition in Drinks International 's 
Millionaires' Club 2026 report, with its flagship brands Mansion House Brandy, Imperial Blue Whisky and 
Courrier Napoleon Brandy, achieving notable rankings among the world's largest and fastest growing spirits 
brands. 
According to the rankings, Mansion House Brandy has emerged as the second-largest brandy brand globally, 
selling 9.7 million nine-litre cases in 2025, recording a robust 24.4 per cent growth over the previous year. 
The brand also became the ninth-largest spirits brand in India and the third-fastest-growing spirits brand 
among the world’s 25 largest spirits brands. TI’s Courrier Napoleon Brandy secured the eighth position 
globally among brandy brands with sales of 1.5 million cases during 2025. 
The report also highlighted the strong standing of Imperial Blue Whisky, which became a part of the TI 
portfolio in December 2025. Imperial Blue is the ninth-highest-selling spirits brand globally, reflecting its 
enduring consumer appeal and market leadership. Imperial Blue also achieved its highest-ever monthly 
sales under TI ownership in May 2026, reflecting the strong momentum generated through the ongoing 
integration process. 
Mr. Amit Dahanukar, Chairman & Managing Director, Tilaknagar Industries Limited  said, "This 
global recognition is a testament to the strength of our brands, the trust of our consumers and the execution 
capabilities of our team. Mansion House Brandy's continued leadership in the global brandy category and 
Imperial Blue's strong standing among the world's largest spirits brands reinforce our ambition to build 
globally recognised Indian spirits brands.”  
In parallel, Tilaknagar Industries achieved a significant operational milestone, recording its highest-ever 
monthly sales in May 2026. This performance highlights the strength of the company’s expanded portfolio, 
execution capabilities and growing presence across key markets. 
“The global rankings, coupled with our highest-ever monthly sales performance in May 2026 strengthens 
the company’s growth trajectory and our ability to create long-term value across categories and 
markets” , Mr. Dahanukar added. 
Drinks International’s  ‘The Millionaires’ Club’ report is an annual listing that features alcoholic beverage 
brands from around the world that register sales volumes in excess of one-million in nine-litre cases. The 
2026 edition is based on calendar year 2025 sales performance. 
The latest rankings underscore Tilaknagar Industries' growing scale and relevance in the global spirits’ 
landscape. With category leading brands across whisky and brandy, which together account for nearly 85 
per cent of the Indian IMFL industry, the company is uniquely positioned to capitalize on India's 
premiumisation opportunity. Backed by a strengthened pan-India footprint, a portfolio spanning both scale 
and premium segments and a clear focus on elevating consumer experiences, TI is well placed to drive the 
next phase of growth while building enduring brand equity and strengthening its presence in premium 
segment. 
 
 
 About Tilaknagar Industries:  
Tilaknagar Industries is one of India’s leading alcoholic beverage companies, with a rich legacy spanning 
over 90 years. Founded in 1933 by Shri Mahadev L. Dahanukar as Maharashtra Sugar Mills, TI has, over 
the years, transformed into a major player in the India Made Foreign Liquor (“IMFL”) industry. Under the 
leadership of Shri Amit Dahanukar, TI has further strengthened its leadership across categories with 
‘Millionaire’ brands like Mansion House Brandy, Imperial Blue Whisky and Courrier Napoleon Brandy. The 
Company also has a strong presence across Rum and Gin through Madiraa Rum & Blue Lagoon Gin. Further, 
with a strong focus on premiumization, ‘House of TI’ – the Company’s luxury vertical – is anchored by 
Monarch Legacy Edition, its first luxury brandy, and Seven Islands Pure Malt Whisky, an Indo-Scottish 
premium whisky. ‘House of TI’ also includes an investment arm that supports India’s emerging craft spirits 
players.
