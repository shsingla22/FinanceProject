Sub: Intimation of Change in Senior Management Personnel (SMP) of the Bank under

This is to inform you that Mr. Jayaram Vishwanath - Head Corporate Banking, Construction Finance & 
SME of the Bank, has tendered his resignation from the services of the Bank vide his letter  dated 
September 0 7, 2026 to explore opportunity outside the Bank . His last working day with the Bank will be 
close of business hours on November 10, 2026 .  Also, Mr. Jayaram Vishwanath shall cease to be a 
Senior Management Personnel (SMP) of the Bank, with effect from close of business hours of his last 
working day with the Bank.  
 
The Bank places on record its appreciation for the commitment and exemplary contribution made by 
Mr. Jayaram Vishwanath during his tenure and wish him very best for all his future endeavours . 
 
Consequent to above, on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the 
Board of Directors of the Bank in its meeting held on October 23, 2026,  has approved the following 
organisation al changes -  
 
a) Mr. Abhijit Bose , currently serving as the Chief Credit Officer  of the Bank shall take charge as the 
Head - Institutional Business  with effect from opening of the business hours on November 02, 2026 . He 
shall cease to the Chief Credit Officer of the Bank with effect from that date.  
 
b) Mr. Manoj Joshi, currently serving as the Chief Compliance Officer of the Bank  whose existing term 
will be complet ing on June 08, 2027 , shall take charge as the Chief Credit Officer of the Bank with effect 
from opening of the business hours on November 02, 2026 . He shall cease to the Chief Compliance 
Officer  of the Bank with effect from that date.  
 
c) Mr. Shankershan Vasisth, currently serving a s the Deputy - Chief Compliance Officer of the Bank, 
shall be elevated to the position of Chief Compliance Officer of the Bank for a period of three (3) years  
with effect from opening of the business hours  on November 02, 2026  to November 01, 2029 .  
 
Mr. Abhijit Bose, Mr. Manoj Joshi and Mr. Shankershan Vasisth shall be considered as the Senior 
Management Personnel of the Bank.  
 
The details as required under Regulation 30 of SEBI Listing Regulations  read with applicable circular 
issued by the SEBI alo ng with the copy of  resignation letter  is enclosed herewith as Annexure A.

Mr. Abhijit Bose  
 
Currently serving as the "Chief Credit Officer" shall be redesignated as "Head - Institutional 
Business" .  
Mr. Bose has over 32 years of experience in Banking & Financial Services sector. He has managed 
Sales and Distribution, Products, Credit Risk Management and Audit in markets such as India, Asia 
Pacific, Middle East, and Africa. Previously, he w as with Standard Chartered Bank and the last 
position held was as Risk Head - Consumer Banking for Southern African markets. Prior to which 
he has worked in Citibank NA & GIC Housing Finance Ltd. He joined DCB Bank in June'08. Since 
then, he was responsibl e for Retail Assets lending, Secured Credit Cards, Payment Products, 
Securitisation desk and Collections. Additionally, he was also responsible for forging Strategic 
Alliances with Business partners and Fintech start -ups cutting across all verticals and pr oducts of 
the Bank. From April  2017, he has been appointed as Chief Credit Officer and is responsible to 
spearhead the credit risk management framework. His expertise in credit life cycle management 
helps the Bank to drive efficient credit processes and building healthy Advances portfolio 
complementing good growth agenda of the Bank.  
 
Mr. Manoj Joshi  
 
Currently serving as the "Chief Compliance Officer" shall be redesignated as "Chief Credit Officer".  
Mr. Manoj Joshi in a career span of two decades at DCB Bank, has contributed to the growth and 
progress of the organization in various roles. His acumen for banking and finance was honed with 
responsibilities in Corporate Banking, SME and as Business Head for Trade Finance. He was also 
instrumental in setting up the SME vertical in the Bank and launch DCB Remit, a digital platform to 
send money abroad. Prior to DCB Bank, he has worked with ICICI Bank in Jamshedpur, Pune and 
Chennai. The initial years of his career were spent with manufacturing companies namely Uniworth 
Group and thereafter Epcos Ferrites Limited. In these, he was responsible for Finance and Banking. 
Manoj has completed his B. Com, Post Graduate Diploma in Business Management (Finance) and 
CAIIB. He also completed a certificate course conducted by IIBF for SME Banking, Trade Financ e 
& Know Your Customer & Anti Money Laundering.
