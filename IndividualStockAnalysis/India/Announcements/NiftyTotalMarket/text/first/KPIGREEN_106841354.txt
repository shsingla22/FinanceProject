 Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para B of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we are pleased to 
inform you that KPI Green Energy Limited (“Company”) has su ccessfully executed a Power 
Purchase Agreement (PPA) with SJVN Limited for a 300 MW Inter -State Transmission System 
(ISTS) -connected Wind Power Project .  
 
The project, secured through a competitive bidding process, will operate under a long -term 
PPA at a fixed tariff of ₹3.64 per unit for 25 years , with power supply to commence within 24 
months from the effective date of the PPA.  
 
This PPA marks a significant milestone in the Company’s wind energy journey. The long -term 
fixed tariff provides predictable and stable cash flows , while the project meaningfully 
strengthens the Company’s utility -scale wind portfolio and adds strong momentum to the 
Group’s KP 3.0 roadmap, under which the Company is set to build 10 GW of owned IPP capacity 
by FY32 .  
 
The details as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master 
Circular dated January 30, 2026, are enclosed herewith as Annexure -A.
