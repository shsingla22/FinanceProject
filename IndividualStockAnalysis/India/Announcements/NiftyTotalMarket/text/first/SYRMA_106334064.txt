Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, 
we regret to inform the sad demise of Mr. Satyendra Johari, Chairman and MD of Syrma Johari Medtech 
Limited, (“SJML”), (Formerly known as Johari Digital healthcare limited), a subsidiary of Syrma SGS 
Technology Limited (“the Company”) on August 8, 2025. 
 
Mr. Johari was 77 years and was the founder of SJML. We extend our deepest condolences to his family and 
loved ones. The Board of SJML will take necessary steps to ensure continuity of operations.
