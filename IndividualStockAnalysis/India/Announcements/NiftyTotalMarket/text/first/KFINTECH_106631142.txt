KFin Technologies Launches AEGIX, India’s First AI -native Integrated Investor 
Relations Intelligence Platform  
Helping listed companies improve visibility into shareholder ownership, track market expectations, 
and manage investor engagement more effectively  
Mumbai, 20 May 2026: KFin Technologies Limited (“KFintech”), a leading technology driven financial 
services platform providing comprehensive solutions to the capital markets ecosystem, has launched 
AEGIX (Advanced Equity Governance Intelligence  eXchange) , an integrated investor relations 
intelligence platform designed for listed companies in India  and globally . 
As capital markets become increasingly data driven and institutional investor engagement expectations continue to evolve, investor relations teams require unified intelligence platforms that provide deeper ownership visibility, market insights, and structured stakeholder engagement 
capabilities. AEGIX addresses this requirement through a purpose -built  AI native platform that 
combines ownership intelligence, AI powered workflows, market intelligence tools  and integrated 
investor relations capabilities to help teams generate deeper insights and make faster decisions . 
Commenting on the launch, Sreekanth Nadella, MD and CEO, KFin Technolog ies Limited , said, “ Indian 
capital markets are entering a phase where the quality of a company's investor dialogue will 
increasingly shape its cost of capital and its strategic optionality. IR teams need to move from reactive reporting to continuous, data- led engagement, and the tools available to them have not kept pace 
with that shift. AEGIX is our response. We have taken what only a registrar of KFintech's scale can see, combined it with workflows built specifically for IR teams, and added an AI co -pilot  trained for this 
work. The intent is simple: to put Indian listed companies on a stronger footing in every conversation they have with the market ”. 
Currently available for companies listed on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited and BSE  
Limited, as well as companies preparing to go public, AEGIX is designed to support investor relations teams across large cap, mid cap, and small cap companies in India and globally. The platform is available to listed companies irrespective of whether they currently use KFintech as their registrar and transfer agent.  
What AEGIX delivers for listed companies  
AEGIX is organised around four outcomes that matter to IR heads, CFOs, and company boards, each 
supported by capabilities purpose built for evolving in vestor relations requirements.  
1. Gain deeper visibility into ownership and investor movements  
Ownership Analysis capabilities help IR teams track evolving shareholder structures and investor 
composition trends, providing a more contextual understanding of  ownership patterns and market 
participation.  
2. Read market expectations and benchmark performance in one view  
Market Intelligence and Analyst Coverage capabilities bring together stock performance, peer 
benchmarking, analyst estimates, ratings, and marke t signals into a single view to help teams better 
understand market sentiment.  
3. Build more structured and targeted investor engagement  
Investor Targeting, Investor Relations Management, communication workflows, and engagement 
tools help teams identify op portunities, maintain continuity across interactions, and streamline 
stakeholder outreach.  The platform can also support merchant bankers in investor targeting and 
optimising non deal roadshows to enhance engagement effectiveness.  
4. Strengthen governance oversight and disclosure readiness 
Dedicated dashboards for company boards, particularly the Stakeholders Relationship Committee (SRC), provide real time visibility into investor complaints, tracking volumes received, resolution 
status, and pending cases t o support stronger governance oversight.  
5. Run the IR function with an AI co -pilot built for the role  
Ask AiRa helps teams analyse developments, anticipate discussion themes, prepare communications, 
summarise disclosures, and generate faster actionable in sights, reducing manual effort and enabling 
more informed decision making.  
As investor relations continues to evolve into a more data and AI driven function, AEGIX is expected 
to help companies strengthen ownership visibility, institutional engagement, and  stakeholder 
communication.
