Axiro Semiconductor Invests in ThinSiC to Strengthen Position in Next -
Generation Silicon Carbide Power Semiconductors  
BENGALURU, India (October 29, 2026) — Axiro Semiconductor Pvt.  Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of 
CG Power and Industrial Solutions Limited, today announced a strategic investment in ThinSiC Inc., a 
Silicon Valley -based semiconductor company developing next -generation low -cost silicon carbide (SiC) 
power devices. Axi ro joins ThinSiC as Lead Investor in its  latest financing round  and enters into a long -
term commercial partnership to support the development and commercialisation of ThinSiC's silicon 
carbide power device technology.  
The investment advances Axiro's strategy of building a broad, high -performance semiconductor 
portfolio — anchored in its core RF, mmWave, and advanced connectivity capabilities and now 
extending into power electronics, where silicon carbide is emerging as a viable alternative to silicon 
for applications in rail traction, industrial drives, and grid infrastructure. Through CG Power's deep 
presence across India's power transmission and industrial infrastructure, Axiro is uniquely positioned 
to serve as a stra tegic commercial channel for ThinSiC's technology in India and beyond.  
"We are pleased to support ThinSiC as investor and strategic partner. This investment reflects our 
conviction that silicon carbide will be central to the next generation of power electronics — and that 
cost has been the single biggest barrier to its broade r adoption. At Axiro, we are building deep 
capabilities in RF, mmWave, and advanced connectivity, while extending into power semiconductors, 
where we see significant opportunity through the CG Power group's presence across India's power 
transmission and in dustrial infrastructure. ThinSiC's technology addresses exactly the cost and 
scalability challenges that have held high -voltage SiC back. We look forward to working closely with 
the ThinSiC team to translate their innovation into commercial impact."  
— Dr. Naveen Yanduru, Chief Executive Officer, Axiro Semiconductor  
"We are excited to have Axiro as an investor and partner. Their semiconductor expertise, their 
strategic vision for power, and their access to one of India's leading industrial groups would help us 
accelerate the commercialization our advanced SiC devices”  
— T.S. Ravi, Founder and Chief Executive Officer, ThinSiC Inc.
