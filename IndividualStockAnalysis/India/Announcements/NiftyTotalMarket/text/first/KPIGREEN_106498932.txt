Sub.: Receipt of orders for Supply of Goods and Onsite Services for Balance of System (BoS) 
package for 534 MW Solar Power Projects at Khavda, Gujarat  
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Apropos the captioned subject and pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we are pleased to inform you that KPI Green Energy 
Limited (“the Company”) has received orders for the Supply of Go ods from M/s. Adani Green Energy 
Limited  and Onsite Services for M/s. Adani Green Energy Six Limited  for Balance of System (BoS) 
package for an aggregate  534 MW Solar Power Projects  at Khavda, Kutch, Gujarat .  
 
The scope of work of supply of Goods in the Balance of System (BoS) package  includes 
manufacturing, procurement, packing, transportation, factory acceptance tests, and supply of 
commissioning spares, mandatory spares, consumables and associated materials. The scope of 
work of onsite services in the Balance of System (BoS) package  includes receipt, unloading, 
storage, installation, testing, commissioning, civil, mechanical and electrical works, handing over 
and performance guarantee testing for the said Solar Power Projects.  
 
These orders further strengthen the Company’s position as a trusted execution partner for large -
scale utility solar projects  and are aligned with its strategy to scale up its BoS and EPC capabilities 
across marquee renewable energy developments. The projects are scheduled to be executed in a 
phased manner, in accordance with the timelines stipulated under the respective contrac ts. 
 
The details as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR Regulations read with SEBI Master 
Circular dated November 11, 2024, are enclosed herewith as Annexure I.
