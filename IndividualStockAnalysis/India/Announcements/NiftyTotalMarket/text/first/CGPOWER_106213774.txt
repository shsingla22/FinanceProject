PRESS RELEASE  
                           
CG Pumps Launches SmartSENSE – India’s First Cordless Water 
Pump Controller Technology  
Revolutionizing Water Management with Next -Gen Intelligence and Sustainability  
 
 
Mumbai,  28th April 2025 - In a landmark breakthrough for India’s domestic water 
management sector, CG Pumps has launched SmartSENSE  – India’s first cordless, 
float-free automatic water pump controller. This cutting -edge system eliminates the 
need for manual  wiring and float switches, offering a truly smart and intelligent water 
control solution  for Indian households .  
The revolutionary pump system  uses advanced sensing technology to independently 
monitor water levels at both the source (inlet) and the overhead ta nk – without the 
need for any wired connections or float -based sensors. The system automatically 
operates  the pump  to start and shut it off once the tank is full , helping users avoid 
overflows and ensuring efficient water use at all times.  
A First -of-its-Kind Innovation for Indian Homes  
 
Tailored to the needs of modern Indian households , the 1 HP SmartSENSE pump  
supports a dynamic head range of 6 to 50 meters  and a discharge capacity ranging 
from 3300 LPH to 600 LPH . Built with a 100% copper -wound motor and rust -free cast 
iron components, the system ensures long -lasting durability, energy efficiency, and 
high-performance operation in varied conditions.  
 
Engineered for next -generation homes, the advance water pump controller  technology  
uses inte lligent automation to conserve energy and eliminate water waste —
seamlessly and efficiently . 
 
SmartSENSE: Core Features  
 
 100% Cordless Operation  – No wiring from tank to controller  
 Float -Free Detection  – No float switch required in the tank  
 Automatic Start/Stop  – Based on intelligent water level sensing  
 Dry Run Protection  – Prevents pump burnout during low water supply  
 Multiple Tank Connectivity  – Supports parallel overhead tanks  
 Eco-Efficient  – Minimizes power use and prevents water wastage  
 Heavy -Duty  Design  – Copper -wound motor and rust -resistant build for 
longevity  
 
 
 
 
Speaking on the launch, MD Amar Kaul, CG Power and Industrial Solutions  said, 
“This launch is a step forward in our larger mission to build sustainable, resource -
efficient communities. SmartSENSE, offers an  upgrade to smarter living  — a solution 
that’s scalable a nd ready to benefit every home across the country.”  
 
Sriram  Rangarajan, Executive Vice President , Consumer Electricals , added, 
“Water pump automation in India has always meant me ssy wiring or makeshift float 
switches. With SmartSENSE, we’ve removed that complexity. It’s a plug -and-play 
solution built for Indian homes —simple to install, smart in performance, and ready for 
today’s evolving lifestyles ”. 
 
Now available across India, SmartSENSE  strengthens CG Pumps’ legacy of 
innovation by bringing intelligent, cordless automation to everyday water systems — 
setting a new benchmark in India’s pump industry for homeowners, plumbers, and 
trade professionals alike .
