Summary of Profile  
 Ms. Amrita Gangotra  
 
Age: 59 years  
Location: New Delhi, India  
Ms. Amrita Gangotra holds a Bachelor of Science , Mathematics  (Honours) , from University 
of Delhi and is a Gold Medalist in Master of Science, Operational Research, University of 
Delhi.  
Ms. Gangotra's career spans over 30 years, during which she has played key roles in 
numerous business -impacting transformation initiatives. These include a revenue -share IT 
& Digital outsourcing deal with IBM, launching new m -commerce products, creating IT  & 
Digital platforms for global enterprise business at Vodafone, supporting Vodafone's Digital 
Telco strategy, integrating CWW M&A, rolling out 4G networks, and preparing for 5G 
introduction.  
She is a commercially focused, award -winning technology leader with experience in India, 
the UK, and Europe, driving business performance and delivering value in 
Telecommunications, IT services, and FMCG sectors. Ms. Gangotra has extensive 
knowledge of IT & Digital transformation in Telecom, FMCG, and ITES sectors, having held 
group CIO and opco CTIO positions in Telcos. She has been part of the executive 
management teams at Bharti Airtel and Vodafone Hungary and continues to provide 
advisory and consulting  services in Digital and Technology domains across industries.  
In 2019, Ms. Gangotra founded ITyukt Digital Solutions, offering consultancy and advisory 
services in 5G, IoT, and AI/ML -based digital transformation for enterprises. She has served 
as Head of Enterprise Technology at Vodafone UK and Director Technology at  Vodafone 
Hungary, and held senior roles at Airtel, HCL Comnet, and Nestle. She started her career as 
Sr. System Manager at HCL Ltd. Ms. Gangotra also has experience of mentoring start -ups, 
innovation funds and serving as a member of several customer advis ory boards . She is an 
independent board member of multiple listed and unlisted companies, including United 
Spirits Limited, Max Healthcare Institute Limited, Triveni Turbine Limited, India1 Payments 
Limited, Sterlite Technologies Limited  and Pine Labs Pvt Ltd .
