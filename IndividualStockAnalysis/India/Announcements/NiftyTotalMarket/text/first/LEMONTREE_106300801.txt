15th July 2025:  Lemon Tree Hotels Limited announces the opening of Lemon Tree 
Hotel,  Erode . This managed hotel is the group's sixth property in Tamil Nadu.  
 
The fresh and vibrant hotel features 64 well-appointed rooms, Citrus Café – a 
multi-cuisine restaurant,  and a conferenc e facilit y to meet the requirements of 
discerning  guests.  
 
Erode, located in the western part of Tamil Nadu, is a dynamic city celebrated for 
its rich cultural heritage, flourishing textile industry, and agricultural prominence. 
Often referred to as the “Loom City of India” and the “Turmeric Capital,” Erode 
has ea rned its reputation as a key centre for textile manufacturing, handlooms, 
and the turmeric trade.  
 
Set along the banks of the River Cauvery, the city strikes a perfect balance 
between tradition and modernity, making it an appealing destination for travellers, 
entrepreneurs, and investors alike. With seamless connectivity to major cities such 
as Coimbato re, Salem, and Bangalore, Erode has become a vital commercial 
gateway in Tamil Nadu. Its spiritual charm, scenic spots like Kodiveri Dam, and 
tranquil lifestyle further enhance its appeal for families and professionals.
