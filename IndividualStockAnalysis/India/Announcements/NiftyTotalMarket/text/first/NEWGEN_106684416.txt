Dear Sir/ Ma'am,  
 
   Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
(“Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform that the Board of Directors of Newgen Software Technologies 
Limited at its meeting held today i.e., Thurs day, 02nd July 2026, which commenced at 1:30 P.M.  and 
concluded at 1:48 P.M., has inter  alia considered and approved  the following : 
 
1. Appointment of Mr. Tarun Nandwani as Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and Key Managerial 
Personnel (KMP):  
 
We refer to our earlier communication dated  9th June 2026, wherein  the Company had intimated  about  
the resignation of Mr. Virender Jeet  from the position of Chief Executive Officer (Key Managerial 
Personnel) of the Company, effective from the close of business hours on 31st August 2026 , which 
was noted by the Board at its meeting held on 12th June 2026.   
 
In view of the above and b ased on the recommendation of the Nomination & Remuneration 
Committee, the Board has approved the appointment of Mr. Tarun Nandwani  (currently designated 
as the  Chief Operating Officer of the Company ) as the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and Key 
Managerial Personnel (KMP) of the Company for a period of 18 (eighteen) months, with effect from 
01st August 2026 to 31st January 2028.   
 
2. Appointment of Mr. Pramod Kumar as Chief Growth Officer (CGO) and Key Managerial 
Personnel (KMP):  
Based on the recommendation of the Nomination & Remuneration Committee, the Board has 
approved the appointment of Mr. Pramod Kumar  (currently designated as Vice President – Sales with 
the Company's Singapore subsidiary ) as the Chief Growth Officer (CGO) and Key Managerial 
Personnel (KMP) of the Company, with effect from 01st August 2026.   
 
3. Designation of Mr. T. S. Varadarajan as Vice Chairman:  
The Board has approved the designation of Mr. T. S. Varadarajan (DIN: 00263115), Whole -time 
Director of the Company, as Vice Chairman of the Company with immediate effect . Mr. T. S. 
Varadarajan shall continue to serve as the Whole -time Director of the Company, and the designation 
of Vice Chairman shall be in addition to his existing role. He shall discharge such duties, 
responsibilities and exercise such powers as may be entrusted to him by the Board from time to time.
