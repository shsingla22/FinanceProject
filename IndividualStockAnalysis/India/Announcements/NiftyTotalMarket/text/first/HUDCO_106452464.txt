WITNESSETH, That: 
A. WHEREAS, HUDCO a techno-financing agency focusing on promoting and financing 
housing and urban development projects in India, also provides research, training 
and capacity building support to all urban stakeholders in the habitat sector, through 
its research and training institute, Human Settlement Management Institute (HSMI). 
HUDCO supports both public and private sector projects, enhancing accessibility and 
quality of urban environments. Its range of initiatives include slum redevelopment, 
affordable housing, urban infrastructure provision and improvements, township 
development, heritage conservation and recently launched Urban Invest 
Window(UiWIN). 
B. WHEREAS, the IDFC Foundation was set up in 2011 with the primary objective to 
promote inclusive growth, focusing on infrastructure development, livelihood 
enhancement, and climate resilience. Apart from engaging with grass-root 
agencies/entities/foundations for high-impact social sector/infrastructure 
investments and livelihood missions, IDFC Foundation undertakes policy analysis, 
research, knowledge sharing, capacity building activities in the infrastructure space. 
€ WHEREAS, the Parties recognize the growing complexity of urban challenges and the 
pivotal role of cities in driving innovation, climate action, and inclusive service 
delivery. Considering the same, the Parties intend to collaborate on a capacity 
development initiative for the urban sector in India and thereby aims to enhance 
professional capabilities, .institutional resilience, and cross-sectoral learning in the 
urban development sector.

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereto 
agree to collaborate as follows: 
1. OBJECTIVES OF COLLABORATION 
a) The collaboration shall pursue the following objectives: 
i. Strengthen technical, financial, and leadership capacities across urban 
institutions. 
ii. Provide training and technical support for the implementation of national 
urban programmes/ schemes and HUDCO'’s Urban Invest Window(UiWIN). 
iii. Facilitate the integration of climate adaptation, digital governance, and 
data-informed planning in urban management. 
iv. Foster applied research, policy advisory, and development of actionable 
knowledge products. 
v. Promote continuous learning through mentorship, peer exchange, and 
professional networks. 
b) The activities/programmes implemented by the Parties under this MoU shall 
engage a wide range of stakeholders, including: 
i. Government and Implementation Agencies: ULBs, parastatals, Smart 
City SPVs, PMUs, TSUs, and PIUs. 
ii. Academic and Training Institutions: State training institutes, and 
faculty from urban planning, engineering, architecture, and public policy 
domains. 
ii. Private Sector and Civil Society: Urban practitioners, consultants, 
NGOs, and community-based organisations. 
2. IMPLEMENTATION FRAMEWORK 
a) The Parties shall designate nodal officers to jointly oversee strategy, design, and 
implementation of programmes. 
b) The programmes and activities undertaken by the Parties shall be co-developed 
based on stakeholder needs and emerging urban sector priorities. 
c) The Parties shall ensure adaptive management through regular reviews, 
feedback loops, and learning sessions. 
d) The Parties shall systematically develop and disseminate knowledge products 
and documentation. 
3. AUTHORISED REPRESENTATIVES

be taken, and any document required or permitted to be executed, under this MoU 
may be taken or executed by

GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION 
All and any dispute arising out of or in connection with this contract, including any 
question regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be construed in 
accordance with Laws of India. The Parties submit to the jurisdiction of the courts of 
New Delhi only. 
MODIFICATIONS 
This MoU may only be amended by mutual agreement of the parties hereto. All such 
changes shall be in writing, and signed by the duly authorized representative of the 
Parties. 
TERM AND TERMINATION OF MoU 
This MoU expresses the intentions of the Parties herein regarding the mutual 
collaboration in the areas of research, education, training, capacity building and 
handholding for relevant stakeholders and does not constitute a binding relationship 
between the parties herein or their assigns. Neither Party is liable to the other in 
respect of any matter or representation made in the MoU. 
Nothing herein is intended nor shall be construed as creating any exclusive 
arrangement. This MoU shall not restrict either Parties from entering into similar 
arrangement with other entities depending on their business needs. 
Nothing in this MoU will be construed as creating rights and obligations of 
partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship between the Parties under law. 
The Parties understand and agree that this MoU will be valid initially for a period of 3 
(three) years from the date written hereinabove. The Parties may thereafter extend 
this MoU on mutually agreed terms and conditions, in writing. It is agreed that 
irrespective of a decision to terminate this MoU, the Parties are obliged to complete 
all the Activities/Project/Programmes already initiated under this MoU.
