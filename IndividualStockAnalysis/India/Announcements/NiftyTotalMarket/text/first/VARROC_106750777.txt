Press Release     
 
VIT Pune Team Wins Varroc Engineering’s Eureka Challenge 3.0 
with AI -Powered CAD Innovation  
AI-driven design intelligence solution recognised for its potential to 
improve engineering efficiency and accelerate automotive product 
development  
 
Pune, India, 20 August  2026 : Varroc Engineering Ltd. (Varroc), a leading global tier -1 
automotive supplier , today announced the winner of Eureka Challenge 3.0 - Ignite 
Innovation, its flagship engineering innovation competition aimed at nurturing next -
generation engineering talent and encouraging students to develop solutions for real -world 
mobility challenges . 
 
The winning team from Vishwakarma Institute of Technology (VIT), Pune secured the top 
position for its innovative solution, “AI -Driven Design Intelligence for Early -Stage CAD 
Validation.” The solution uses artificial intelligence to identify design issues and 
optimisation opportunities at the early stages of product development, with the potential 
to help engineers reduce development cycles, improve design quality and accelerate 
innovation.  
 
Commenting on the achievement, Arjun Jain, Director & CEO – Business I, Varroc , 
said: “Eureka once again demonstrates the potential of India’s young engineers to solve 
for real -world challenges facing mobility. The breadth of applications and quality of 
research of this year’s teams reflect the scope of innovation needed to shape th e future of 
mobility. What is particularly encouraging is the increased awareness of real -world 
engineering processes and standards. At Varroc, we are committed to creating platforms 
that connect young talent with real industry challenges, enabling the nex t generation of 
engineers to think boldly and innovate purposefully to create safe, smart and sustainable 
mobility.”  
 
Now in its third edition, Eureka Challenge has emerged as an industry -academia platform 
connecting engineering students with real -world challenges faced by the mobility sector.  
 
The 2026 edition received an overwhelming response from engineering students across 
India. The challenge generated more than 25,000 registrations from undergraduate and 
postgraduate students across disciplines including Computer Science, Electronics, 
Electrical, Electronics & Telecommunication, Information Technology, Mechanical, 
Automobile Engineering and allied streams.  
 
More than 1,800 ideas were submitted and evaluated through a rigorous multi -stage 
screening process. Following multiple rounds of assessment, 27 teams were shortlisted for 
the online presentation stage, with the top four teams advancing to the grand finale  held 
at Varroc.  In addition to rewards and industry recognition, the winning team received a 
Pre-Placement Offer (PPO) opportunity to join Varroc in 2027, creating a direct pathway 
from innovation to an industry career.  
 
As a global mobility technology partner with more than 800 R&D engineers and seven R&D 
centres worldwide, Varroc continues to invest in future -ready talent and foster a culture 
of innovation. Through initiatives such as Eureka Challenge, the company aims t o 
strengthen industry -academia collaboration while empowering young Indian engineers to 
develop solutions that contribute to the evolution of safe, smart and sustainable mobility.

About Varroc Engineering Ltd.  
Varroc Engineering Ltd is a global tier -1 automotive component group. It was incorporated 
in 1988. The group manufactures and supplies E -mobility solutions, Body systems 
solutions, Lighting solutions, HMI solutions, ICE powertrain and Advanced electronics to 
leading OEMs with end -to-end capabilities across design, development and manufacturing 
for two -wheeler, three -wheeler, passenger vehicles, commercial vehicle, and off -highway 
vehicle worldwide. The group income was ₹ 89,080 million from continued operat ions in 
FY26. The group employs more than 5400 employees (750 + R&D Engineers), has 37 
global operating manufacturing facilities supported by 7 R&D Centres, and has filed more 
than 130 patents.  
Varroc Engineering Limited’s shares are listed on the National Stock Exchange (VARROC) 
and the Bombay Stock Exchange (541578) in India.
