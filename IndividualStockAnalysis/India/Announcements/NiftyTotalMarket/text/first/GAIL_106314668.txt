Press Release  
GAIL  clocks Revenue of ₹ 34,792  crore , PAT of ₹ 1,886 crore  in Q1 FY -26  
New Delhi, 28th July, 202 5: GAIL (India) Ltd reported Revenue from Operations of ₹34,792  
crore in Q1 FY -26 as against ₹  33,692  crore in Q1 FY -25. Profit before Tax (PBT) in Q1 FY -26 
stood at ₹  2,533  crore as against ₹ 3,642  crore in Q1 FY -25. Profit after Tax (PAT) in Q1 FY-26 
stood at ₹1,886  crore as against ₹  2,724  crore in Q1 FY -25.  
 
Quarter on Quarter basis, Revenue from Operations stood  at ₹34,792  crore in Q1 FY -26 as against 
₹ 35,707  crore in Q4 FY -25. PBT stood at  ₹2,533  crore in Q1 FY -26 as against ₹ 2,701 crore 
during Q4 FY -25. PAT stood at  ₹1,886  crore in Q1 FY -26 as against ₹  2,049 crore in Q4 FY -25. 
 
On Consolidated basis, Revenue from Operations stood at ₹  35,429  crore in Q1 FY-26 as against 
₹ 36,551  crore during Q4 FY-25. PBT in Q1 FY-26 stood at ₹  3,029  crore as against ₹ 3,240  crore 
in Q4 FY-25. PAT (excluding Non -controlling interest) was ₹ 2,369  crore in Q1 FY-26 as against 
₹ 2,492  crore in Q4 FY-25.  
 
Shri Sandeep Kumar Gupta, Chairman & Managing Director, GAIL said that, the Company has 
incurred a Capex of ~ ₹ 3,176  crore during the current quarter , mainly on Pipelines, Petrochemicals, 
Equity  contribution  to JVs, etc.  
 
Shri Gupta also informed that GAIL has got PNGRB’s authorization for capacity expansion of 
Jamnagar -Loni LPG pipeline from 3.25 MMTPA to 6.5 MMTPA  involving an estimated capex of 
~₹5,000 Crores & is expected to be completed within 3 Years . This project wil l cut down on CO₂ 
emissions significantly & reduce road mishaps and leaks.  
 
GAIL (India) Limited has also been included in the prestigious FTSE4Good Index series for the 
7th consecutive year, underlining GAIL’s Sustainability efforts and strong Environment al, Social 
and Governance (ESG) performance.
