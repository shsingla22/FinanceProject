Sub: Update on the matter related to Workers' Union 
 
In continuation of our letter dated September 1, 2025 informing about the temporary injunction 
granted order by the Honorable Additional Civil Judge & JMFC at Nagamangala Court, restraining 
the Workers Union during a strike, from obstructing the Company, its directors, executives, staffs, 
workmen, vehicles, vehicles carrying with finished goods and raw materials etc., from entering 
into and leaving from the factory gates of the Company and are permitting to agitate beyond 25 
meters from the factory gates.  
 
We further inform that, the said temporary injunction has been extended by the aforesaid 
Honorable Court till disposal of the suit, by its order dated November 18, 2025, received by the 
Company on November 20, 2025. 
 
This injunction continues to provide the Company with relief from disruptions by the union and 
ensures the uninterrupted operation of the Company's business during this period.
