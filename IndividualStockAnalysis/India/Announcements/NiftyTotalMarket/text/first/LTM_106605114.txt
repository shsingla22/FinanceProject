LTM Recognized as a Market Leader in HFS Next-gen IT Infrastructure Services, 2026 Report 
 
Mumbai, India – April 30, 2026:  LTM, the Business Creativity partner to the world’s largest enterprises, has been recognized 
as a Market Leader in the HFS Horizons: Next-gen IT Infrastructure Services, 2026  report by HFS Research, a leading global 
research and advisory firm. 
 
The report evaluates service providers across the infrastructure value chain, assessing their strategies, execution capabilities, 
go-to-market approaches, and market impact across areas such as AI readiness, hybrid and distributed architectures, 
regulatory requirements, platform-enabled operations, and autonomous infrastructure models. It provides detailed insights 
into market dynamics, the evaluation framework, and service provider profiles shaping the future of infrastructure services. 
Providers positioned in the Market Leaders Horizon are recognized for moving beyond foundational modernization to 
enable intelligent, policy-aware infrastructure designed for future enterprise needs. 
 
The report places LTM among a select group of service providers identified by HFS for demonstrating advanced capabilities 
in delivering next-generation, AI-native, and ecosystem-led IT infrastructure services at enterprise scale. It recognizes LTM 
for its AI-native infrastructure approach,  agent-led operational models, and strong engineering depth. Additionally, the 
report also highlights LTM’s ability to execute infrastructure modernization across hybrid, cloud, and edge environments, 
backed by live deployments, strategic ecosystem partnerships, and expertise in complex, compliance-driven environments, 
aligned with the industry’s platform-led and Services-as-Software operating models. 
 
“This recognition by HFS Research reflects our continued focus on reimagining IT infrastructure as an intelligent, AI-native 
foundation for enterprise transformation. As infrastructure becomes central to enabling AI, resilience, and trust at scale, 
we remain committed to helping clients modernize efficiently and with confidence,” said Krishnan Iyer, Chief Growth Officer, 
LTM. 
 
“LTM is positioning itself as a front-runner in AI-native infrastructure, with a strong emphasis on agentic operations and 
platform-led execution. Its investments in BlueVerse and the knowledge fabric reflect a shift from traditional automation 
to system-level intelligence embedded into operations. As enterprises look to operationalize AI at scale, LTM’s ability to 
demonstrate real-world deployments, combined with its engineering depth across core, cloud, and edge, gives it growing 
credibility in delivering resilient, self-optimizing infrastructure environment,” said Ashwin Venkatesan, Executive Research 
Leader, HFS Research . 
 
The Market Leaders Horizon recognition underscores LTM’s focus on sustained, long-term infrastructure transformation. 
As enterprises navigate rising AI workloads, regulatory complexity, sustainability expectations, and cost pressures, LTM 
continues to invest in platforms, partnerships, and operating models designed to support resilience, adaptability, and 
enduring business outcomes.
