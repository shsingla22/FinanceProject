MAXIMUM PRICE FOR BUYBACK OF THE EQUITY SHARES AND BASIS FOR 
ARRIVING AT THE MAXIMUM BUYBACK PRICE 
The Equity Shares of the Company are proposed to be bought back at a price not 
exceeding INR 1,530/- (Indian Rupees One Thousand Five Hundred and Thirty only) 
per Equity Share i.e., the Maximum Buyback Price. 
The Maximum Buyback Price has been arrived at after considering various factors 
including, but not limited to, the trends in the volume weighted average market prices 
of the Stock Exchanges where the Equity Shares are listed, possible impact of the 
Buyback on the price earnings ratio, impact on net worth, earings and book value 
per Equity Share and other financial parameters. 
The Maximum Buyback Price per Equity Share represents: 
a premium of 9.81% and 10.17% over the volume weighted average market price of 
the Equity Shares on BSE and NSE, respectively, during one month preceding the 
date of intimation, i.e., August 24, 2026 (“intimation Date”) to the Stock Exchanges 
of the Board Meeting to consider the proposal of the Buyback. 
a premium of 17.38% and 17.42% over the volume weighted average market price 
of the Equity Shares on BSE and NSE, respectively, during the two weeks preceding 
the Intimation Date. 
a premium of 15.12% and 14.78% over the closing price of the Equity Shares on 
BSE and NSE, respectively, as on the Intimation Date. 
Shareholders are advised that the Buyback of the Equity Shares will be carried 
out through the Stock Exchanges by the Company, in its sole discretion, based on, 
amongst other things, the prevalling market prices of the Equity Shares, which may 
be below the Maximum Buyback Price of INR 1,530/- (Indian Rupees One Thousand 
Five Hundred and Thirty only} per Equity Share.
