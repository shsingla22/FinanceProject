IHCL  ANNOUNCES  FINANCIAL  RESULTS  FOR Q2 FY202 7 
18TH CONSECUTIVE RECORD QUARTER: BEST EVER Q2 
UPGRADES  FY2027 REVENUE GUIDANCE  BY 300 BASIS POINTS  
 
REVENUE  UP BY 21% AT INR 2,566 CRORES ; HOTEL SEGMENT UP BY 24%  
EBITDA UP BY 21% AT INR 78 8 CRORES ; PAT UP BY 23% AT  INR 349 CRORES  
DIVERSIFIED BRANDSCAPE POWERS PORTFOLIO TO 670 HOTELS  
MUMBAI,  OCTOBER 21 , 2026:  The Indian  Hotels  Company  Limited  (IHCL ), India’s  largest  hospitality  company, 
reported  its consolidated  financials  for second  quarter  and half year ending  September 30, 2026.  
 
 
Mr. Puneet Chhatwal, Managing Director & CEO, IHCL, said, “Q2 FY2027  marks the eighteenth consecutive 
record quarter with a Consolidated Revenue of INR 2, 566 crores , up 21% over the previous year . For the quarter 
EBITDA stood at  INR 788 crores with EBITDA margin at 30.7% and PAT of INR 3 49 crores , up 23%. This consistent 
performance is a testament to IHCL’s scalable and diversified operating model, which enables robust growth 
while preserving margin strength.  The operating revenue and operating EBITDA grew by 22% and 24%  
respectively with a margin expansion of 50 basis points.   The revenue  drivers were  rise of 25% in RevPAR  from 
domestic same store  hotels, 25% increase  in management fee income led by new openings momentum , 25% 
revenue growth in Select Service Business  and the contribution from our  recent acquisitions  of Brij and 
Atmantan .  
 
He added , “With  47 signings in the first half a cross the brandscape , IHCL today is a portfolio of 670 hotels and is 
set to achieve its guidance under Accelerate 2030 of a 700-hotel  portfolio  well ahead of time.  Building on its 
first-half performance, IHCL is upgrading its Consolidated Revenue guidance from 12-14% to 15-17% growth for 
FY20 27 led by 17-19% hotel segment revenue  with  seasonally  strong quarters ahead , high  demand from social 
and MICE events and  our strong non-like-for-like growth .”   

 
 
 
Mr. Ankur Dalwani, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, IHCL said, “In Q2 FY2027 , IHCL 
Standalone  reported an operating  Revenue  of INR 1,336 crores, 26% over the previous year,  operating EBITDA 
of INR 520 crores, up 40% , driving a strong operating  EBITDA margin of 38.9%,  an expansion of 400 basis points  
and a PAT of INR 393 crores, an increase of  36% . The performance in the quarter in the standalone entity was 
bolstered by RevPAR growth of 28%  in full-service same store hotels led by marquee events in the capital and 
contribution from renovated assets in key markets of Goa, Delhi, Bengaluru and Udaipur.  Maintaining a strong 
balance sheet, IHCL Consolidated reported a gross cash of INR 4,369 crores  as on September 30th, 2026 .”  
 
He added , “In the q uarte r, we also announced merger of OHL  with IHCL, which will simplify group holding 
structure,  expand asset management opportunities and accelerate long term revenue growth. The Scheme has 
been filed with the Stock Exchanges  with an Appointed Date of April 1 , 2027 .”

About The Indian Hotels Company Limited  
 
The Indian Hotels Company Limited (IHCL) and its subsidiaries bring together a group of brands and businesses that 
offer a fusion of warm Indian hospitality and world -class service. These include  Taj – the iconic brand for the most 
discerning travellers and ranked as  World’s Strongest Luxury Hotel Brand 2026   and  India’s Strongest Brand 2026   as 
per Brand Finance; Claridges Collection , a curated set of boutique luxury hotels merging elegance with historical 
charm;  Brij, an experiential leisure offering;  Atmantan , one of India’s leading integrated wellness 
destinations;  SeleQtions , a named collection of hotels;  Clarks,  retreats set amid storied locales; Gateway , full -service 
hotels designed to be your gateway to exceptional destinations; Vivanta, sophisticated upscale hotels;  Tree of Life , 
private escapes in tranquil settings and  Ginger , which is revolutionising the mid -scale segment.  
 
Incorporated by the founder of the Tata Group, Jamsetji Tata , the Company opened its first hotel – The Taj Mahal 
Palace , in Bombay in 1903 . IHCL has a portfolio of 670 hotels including 280 in the pipeline globally across 4 continents, 
15 countries and over 300 locations. The Indian Hotels Company Limited (IHCL) is India’s largest hospitality company by 
market capitalization.
