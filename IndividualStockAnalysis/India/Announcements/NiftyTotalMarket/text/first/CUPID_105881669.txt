Cupid Limited Secures Initial Order from Cipla Health for Male Condoms  
 
June 13, 2024: Cupid Limited is pleased to announce that it has received an initial order from 
Cipla Health to manufacture a range of male condoms under the Cipla brand. 
 
Following this initial rollout, Cipla Health has committed to placing regular, sizeable orders for 
male condoms with Cupid Limited in the coming quarters. 
 
Swapnil Dhage, Marketing Manager at Cupid Limited , commented, "We have had productive 
discussions with the Cipla Health team and are excited about this long-term and mutually 
beneficial partnership. Securing this initial order is a significant milestone for us, and we look 
forward to growing our business with Cipla Health."  
 
He added, "Cupid is dedicated to manufacturing top-quality products, whether under our 
brand or for established players and new market entrants. We are enthusiastic about taking 
on all opportunities to deliver excellence."
