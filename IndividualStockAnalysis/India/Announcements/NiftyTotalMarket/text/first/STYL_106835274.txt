Page 1 of 3 Seshaasai Technologies Reports Strong Q2FY27 with 15.0% YoY Revenue Growth  
and PAT at ₹697.3 million up by 21.0% YoY 
 
Mumbai, India – 29th October 2026:  Seshaasai Technologies Limited (BSE: 544533) (NSE: STYL), a 
technology- driven solutions company empowering Payments, Communication, and IoT, 
announced today its financial results for the Second Quarter and Half Year of FY27, ended on 30th 
September 2026. 
KEY HIGHLIGHTS – Q2FY27 (CONSOLIDATED) 
• Revenue from operation was at ₹4,053.1 million, a growth of 7.7% on a QoQ basis and 15.0% on 
a YoY basis.  
• Continued to maintain momentum in its focused verticals:  
o Payment Solutions contributed 54% of the revenues.  
o Communication & Fulfilment Solutions contributed 32% of the revenues.  
o IoT Solutions contributed 14% of the revenues.  
• EBITDA stood at ₹1,065.6 million, a growth of 12.0% on a YoY basis with an EBITDA margin of 
26.3%. The EBITDA margin improved by 121 bps on a QoQ basis, supported by sequentially 
better product mix in sales, material -cost absorption and operating leverage.  
• Profit After Tax (PAT) stood at ₹697.3 million, a growth of 21.0% on a YoY basis with a PAT 
margin of 17.2%.  
• Top 10 customers contributed 57.0% of the revenues.  
KEY HIGHLIGHTS – H1FY27 (CONSOLIDATED) 
• Revenue from Operations stood at ₹7,817.8 million for the half -year ended 30th September 
2026, a growth of 17.9% on a YoY basis.  
• EBITDA was at ₹2,009.7 million, a growth of 19.0% on a YoY basis with an EBITDA margin of 
25.7%.  
• Profit After Tax (PAT) stood at ₹1,300.7 million, a growth of 37.5% on a YoY basis with a PAT 
margin of 16.6%.  Seshaasai Y 
 
 
 
 
Page 2 of 3 COMMENTING ON THE RESULTS  
Pragnyat Lalwani , Managing Director, Seshaasai Technologies, said : 
“We are pleased to report another quarter of healthy growth, reflecting disciplined execution and 
sustained momentum across our key business verticals. Revenue from operations increased 
15.0% YoY and 7.7% sequentially to ₹4,053.1 million. Revenue growth was broad -based but led by 
Payment Solutions, supported by higher volumes  of payment cards across banks, metal  cards 
growth,  mass -transit program s, and payment card exports which drove both sequential and 
year -on-year momentum. 
Despite cost pressures from elevated USD/INR levels and West Asia -related supply- chain 
disruptions, sequential revenue growth outpaced the increase in material costs, driving an 89 bps improvement in gross margin to 42.5%.  
We continue to see compelling opportunities across premium and mass- transit cards, 
integrated communication and fulfilment, RFID, SIM and eSIM -led applications . We will continue 
to deepen customer relationships and invest in these opportunities with discipline , while 
maintaining our focus on long -term value creation. ” 
Pavan Kumar, Chief Financial Officer , Seshaasai Technologies , said:
  
“Q2FY27 delivered healthy growth and improved profitability. Revenue from operations increased 
15.0% YoY to ₹4,053.1 million. EBITDA grew  12.0% to ₹1,065.6 million, with an EBITDA margin of 26.3%, 
while Profit After Tax grew 21.0% to ₹697.3 million with a PAT margin of 17.2%.  
Sequentially, EBITDA increased 12.9% and EBITDA margin improved by 121 bps. Lower finance costs 
following debt repayment continued to support profitability. For H1FY27, cash flow from operations 
was ₹971 .0 million, while capital expenditure was ₹1,497.1 million.  
As of 30th September 2026, cash and liquid investments stood at ₹3,144 million, borrowings were 
₹875.0 million and net cash was ₹2,269. 1 million. We  continue to remain focused on maintain ing 
financial discipline and efficient capital allocat ion.” Seshaasai Y 
 
 
 
 
Page 3 of 3 ABOUT SESHAASAI TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED  
Seshaasai Technologies Limited (BSE: 544533) (NSE: STYL),  is a technology -led, multi- location solutions 
provider with a strong focus on the banking, financial services, and insurance (BFSI) sector. The Company 
specializes in secure payment solutions, omnichannel communication, and fulfilment services, offering 
innovative, scalable technologies tailored to the evolving needs of its clients. With proprietary platforms 
at the core of its offerings, Seshaasai  delivers recurring, enterprise -grade solutions that power critical 
operations across the BFSI ecosystem in India. In addition to its BFSI -centric services, the Company also 
provides IoT -enabled solutions for a diverse clientele spanning industries such as retail, renewables, 
logistics, and manufacturing. The Company’s facilities are accredited by global and national standards, 
including NPCI, PCI, Global Payment schemes and IBA, for the secure manufacturing of payment cards, 
cheques, and data handling — ensuring full compliance with international IT, cyber and physical security 
protocols.
