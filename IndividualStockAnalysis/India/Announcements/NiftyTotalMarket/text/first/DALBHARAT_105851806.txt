Subject: Commencement of Commercial production at Ariyalur , Tamil Nadu  
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligation and Disclosure Requirement) Regulation, 2015 (“Listing Regulations ”), we are pleased to inform you that  today, i.e. May 17, 2024,  Dalmia Cement 
(Bharat) Limited , wholly owned subsidiary of the Company , has commenced commercial 
production at its new cement plant of 1.0 MTPA at Ariyalur, Tamil Nadu .  
 With the commencement of commercial production of this 1.0 MTPA Cement unit at Ariyalur, Tamil 
Nadu , the total cement manufacturing capacity of the Company stands  increased to 4 5.6 MTPA.
