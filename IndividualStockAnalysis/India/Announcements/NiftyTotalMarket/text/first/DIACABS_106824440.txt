Press release  
 
Diamond Power to add another CCV line for cables up to 132 kV  
 
Vadodara, 2 2 October 2026 : Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (DICABS) is pleased 
to announce that it will add another Catenary Continuous Vulcanizations  (CCV) line at its 
Vadodara plant. The line can manufacture XLPE -insulated power cables up to 132 kV.  
 
The machinery is scheduled for delivery by August 2027, and the line is targeted for 
commissioning by 31 December 2027.  
 
With this addition, DICABS will operate Six CCV lines LO & Five Silane Lines at its 130 -acre 
single -location plant. The expansion will add meaningful high -voltage and extra -high -
voltage capacity. It comes as demand grows for underground cabling from state utilities, 
transmission developers, renewable energy parks and data centers . 
 
The line will be supported by DICABS's existing in -house facilities:  
• Its own aluminium rod mills  
• In-house compounding  
• NABL -accredited high -voltage test laboratories rated up to 765 kV  
Commenting on the development, Mr Kavish Shah, Vice President, Diamond Power 
Infrastructure Limited , said: "Our EHV order book is growing steadily, and customers are 
asking for larger volumes on shorter timelines. This new CCV line strengthens our position 
as one of India's leading MV and EHV cable manufacturers. It will help us serve that 
demand from a single,  integrated location."  
 
About DICABS:  Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited, under the DICABS brand, has 55 
years of experience in the cable industry. It manufactures power cables from 1.1 kV to 
400 kV, along with HTLS, AL -59, AAAC and other high -performance conductors, at its 
integrated plant  in Vadodara, Gujarat.
