2.This Statement, which is the responsibility of the Company’s management and approved by the Board of 
Directors has been prepared in ac cordance with the recognition and measurement principles laid down in

principles generally accepted in India. Our responsibility is to express a conclusion on the Statement based 
on our review.

Institute of Chartered Accountants of India. This standard requires that we plan and perform the review to

interim financial information consists of making inq uiries, preliminary of the Company’s personnel
