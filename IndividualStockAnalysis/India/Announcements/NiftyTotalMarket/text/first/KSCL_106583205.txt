Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure

against the Income Tax demand for the Assessment Year 2022-23 has been decided
in favour of the Company, vide order dated 07ft April, 2026.

primarily on account of disallowance of exemption claimed on Agricultural Income.
The Hon'ble Appellate Authority has now allowed the Company's claim, treating
the said income as agricultural income, thereby setting aside the aforesaid demand.
This is in continuation of our earlier disclosure dated 01't April, 2024, wherein the
Company had informed about receipt of the demand notice and its intention to
pursue appropriate legal remedies.
