Press Release 
For Immediate Release 
 
Prestige Group achieves Record-breaking Sales Milestone of ₹21,040 Cr for FY24, marking 
its highest sales ever. 
 
Bengaluru, April 08, 2024:  Prestige Group, India’s leading real estate developer, has 
announced its Operational Results for the fiscal year and fourth quarter ending on March 31, 
2024. 
 
Operational Results for FY 24 & Q4 FY24  
 Achieved the Highest Ever Annual Sales  – Prestige Group achieved a record-
breaking Sales milestone of ₹21,040 Cr for FY24, marking a growth of 63% YOY (FY 23 
Sales: ₹12,931 Cr); Quarterly Sales touched ₹4,707 Cr, up by 21% YOY.  
 
 Attained the Highest Ever Annual Collections  - of ₹11,954 Cr for FY24, a growth of 
22% YOY (FY 23 Collections: ₹9,805 Cr); Quarterly Collections amounted to ₹3,474 Cr, 
up by 26% YOY. 
 
 Achieved the Highest Ever Area Sold  in the year, totalling 20.25 mn sft, up by 34% 
YOY; Quarterly Area Sold reached 4.11 mn sft, up by 3% YOY.   
 
 Achieved the Highest Ever Number of Units Sold , with 10,068 units sold in FY2024; 
Quarterly Units Sold stood at 1,666 in Q4FY24. 
 
 Achieved the Highest Average Realization  at ₹10,410 psf in FY24. 
 
 Achieved Highest Ever Annual Launches  in a year, with total project launches 
totalling Total Development Area (TDA) aggregating to 40.19 mn sft, in FY 24, up by 
52% YOY. 
 
 Completed delivery of 300 projects  encompassing 190 Mn sft area overall to date. 
 Delivered 19 projects spanning 25.55 mn sft in FY24. 
 Delivered 9 projects spanning 13.33 mn sft in Q4 FY24.

About Prestige Group: - 
Prestige Group, one of the leading real estate developers in the country, has a legacy of over three 
decades in real estate development. It has diversified business model across various segments, viz 
Residential, Office, Retail, Hospitality, Property Management and Warehouses with operations in 
more than 12 major locations in India. The Group has completed 300 projects spanning a developable 
area of ~ 190 mn sft. The company has been graded CRISIL DA1+ by CRISIL and enjoys a credit 
rating of ICRA A+.
