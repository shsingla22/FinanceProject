PRESS RELEASE  
Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited  
NSE Code: STARHEALTH       BSE Code: 543412  
 
Star Health reports Normalised PAT of ₹390 Crores in Q2 FY27, up 70% YoY,  
fifth successive quarter of underwriting profit improvement  
 
Chennai, October 28, 2026:  Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited (“Star Health”), India’s largest 
standalone health insurer, today announced its financial results for the quarter and half-year ended September 30, 
2026. The company’s Normalised Profit After Tax  increased 70% YoY and stands at ₹390 Crores in Q2 FY27. 
Underwriting Profit swung positively from a loss of ₹40 Crores in Q2 FY26 to a profit of ₹99 Crores in Q2 FY27.  
The Company’s Underwriting Profit for H1 FY27 swung positively from a loss of ₹24 Crores in H1 FY26 to a profit 
of ₹210 Crores in H1 FY27. Underwriting Profit for H1 FY27 exceeded FY26 full year underwriting profit of ₹198 
Crores.  
 
Performance highlights for Q2 FY27  
Business Performance  
 GWP (Reported 1/N basis) increased 19% YoY to ₹5,275 Crores  
 Retail Health fresh GWP  (Reported 1/N basis) increased 35% YoY  
Financial Performance  
 Underwriting Profit: ₹99 Crores compared to a loss of ₹40 Crores in Q2 FY26  
 Normalised Profit After Tax: ₹390 Crores (70% YoY Growth | Q2 FY26: ₹229 Crores)  
 Profit After Tax (PAT): ₹265 Crores (233% YoY growth | Q2 FY26: ₹80 Crores)  
 Combined Insurance Service Ratio (CISR): 98.0% (improvement of 2.9% over 100.9% in  
Q2 FY26)  
Performance highlights for H1 FY27  
Business Performance  
 GWP (Reported 1/N basis) increased 19% YoY to ₹9,562 Crores  
 Retail Health fresh GWP  (Reported 1/N basis) increased 36% YoY  
Financial Performance  
 Underwriting Profit: ₹210 Crores compared to a loss of ₹24 Crores in H1 FY26  
 Normalised Profit After Tax: ₹775 Crores (56% YoY Growth H1 FY26: ₹496 Crores)  
 Profit After Tax (PAT): ₹815 Crores (57% YoY growth | H1 FY26: ₹518 Crores)  
 Combined Insurance Service Ratio (CISR): 97.9% (improvement of 2.4% over 100.3% in  
H1 FY26)  
The Company tracks profitability through its ‘Normalised PAT’ framework, which is pegged to a normalised annual 
investment yield of 8% to provide a view of underlying profitability insulated from short-term mark-to-market 
volatility. Normalised PAT for Q2 FY27 increased 70% YoY to ₹390 Crores and increased 56% YoY to ₹775 Crores 
for H1 FY27.  
Claims Excellence & Customer Trust  
 Star Health settled 22.5 lakh claims during H1 FY27  
 Retail Claims Settlement Ratio improved by 1.4% to 92.4% for H1 FY27  
 Persistency improved by 4% YoY to 102%  
 Company Net Promoter Score (NPS) increased by 4 points to 65  
 
 
 
The Company continued to strengthen its claims ecosystem through AI/ML-enabled analytics, network management, 
wellness interventions, teleconsultation and technology-led claims processing, with the objective of improving 
precision, efficiency and customer experience.  
 
Digital First approach  
Digital remained a key lever for both growth and efficiency during the quarter. The Company’s proprietary Digital 
D2C channel was the fastest-growing channel with 137% YoY growth in H1 FY27, led by younger customers, higher 
sum-insured levels and increased participation beyond Metro cities.  
97% of fresh policies were sourced digitally, reflecting the depth of digital adoption across the Company’s retail 
distribution franchise.  
During the quarter, the Company upgraded its customer relationship management platform to Salesforce, sharpening 
lead management and conversion in D2C, and scaled up AI-powered bots across customer acquisition and service. 
More than half of active agents now use ATOM PRO, the Company’s AI-enabled distribution platform. The Star 
Health Customer App exceeded 17 million downloads, with 1.5 million monthly active users. In claims, 96% of claims 
are now processed on the Company’s new AI-enabled platform, with strengthened detection of fraud, waste and abuse.  
 
Distribution Strength  
Star Health’s proprietary distribution continued to drive growth, with Agency and Digital D2C together contributing 
over 90% of GWP in H1 FY27. The Company added 39,000 agents during the half year, taking its agency force to 8.7 
lakh, the largest among standalone health insurers, while agent productivity rose 19% YoY. 93% of fresh business 
came from customers buying health insurance for the first time, and non-metro geographies contributed over 65% of 
fresh business.  
 
CEO Commentary   
Commenting on the performance of the Company, Mr. Anand Roy, CEO & Managing Director, Star Health and Allied 
Insurance Company Limited, said:  
“Our customers are at the heart of how we measure progress. This progress is reflected not only in our financial 
growth, but also in the confidence customers place in us and their experience when they need healthcare support. 
Persistency improved by 4% YoY to 102%, while our Company Net Promoter Score increased by 4 points to 65. These 
measures reinforce our focus on earning customer trust through simpler service, responsive support and a smoother 
claims experience. We are strengthening provider relationships and expanding wellness initiatives to support 
customers beyond claims, while investing in AI and digital capabilities to make access and service more seamless. Q2 
FY27 marked our fifth successive quarter of improvement in underwriting results, and our first-half underwriting 
profit exceeded that of the entire previous year. This progress supports our commitment to building a resilient business 
that delivers lasting value for customers and shareholders.”  
 
About Star Health and Allied Insurance:  Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited (NSE: STARHEALTH; BSE: 
543412), one of India’s leading standalone health insurance companies, commenced operations in 2006 as India’s first standalone 
health insurance company. Star Health provides Health, Personal Accident and Travel Insurance to its customers. The Company 
has grown to emerge as one of the preferred health insurance companies in India with several pioneering products and services to 
its credit. With customer-centricity at its core, the Company has developed superior and innovative product offerings, 
service capabilities  and a seamless claims management process. Star Health offers tailor-made products to cater to needs such as 
cancer, diabetes and cardiac illnesses, as well as specific segments such as senior citizens,  women and children. Star Health is 
India’s first health insurance company to settle over 1 crore claims.  
Star Health and Allied Insurance Co. Ltd.  has a strong multi-channel distribution network of 900+ offices, 8.7 lakh agents and 
robust bancassurance and financial institution partners. The Company has 17,000+ network hospitals and 18,500+ employees.
