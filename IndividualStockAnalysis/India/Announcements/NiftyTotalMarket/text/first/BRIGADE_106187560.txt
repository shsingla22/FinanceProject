PRESS RELEASE 
 
Brigade Group signs JDA for Prime Land Parcel for Luxury Residential Project 
 with Senior Living in Mysuru 
 
Project with Gross Development Value (GDV) of ₹300 crores  to include  25% senior living spaces 
 and 75% luxury apartments   
 
 
Bengaluru, March 28, 2025: Brigade Group has signed a Joint Development Agreement (JDA) for a new 
luxury residential project on Bogadi Road, near the proposed Outer Ring Road, Mysuru, on a land parcel, 
measuring 5 acres and 12 guntas.  The proposed project will feature a meticulously planned residential 
development of approximately 0.45 million square feet, with a Gross Development Value (GDV) of ₹300 
crores. The project will include 25% senior living spaces and 75% luxury apartments comprising 2 and 3 
BHK units. These residences will be complemented by state-of-the-art amenities and a thoughtfully 
curated clubhouse to ensure a vibrant and inclusive community experience. The project is located on 
Bogadi Road, just 4.5 kilometers from Brigade’s landmark Brigade Palmgrove Villa Project. 
 
Speaking about the development, Pavitra Shankar, Managing Director, Brigade Enterprises Limited, said, 
“This land deal is a significant milestone in our efforts to expand our footprint in Mysuru. As part of 
Brigade Group's long-term strategy, we aim to bring thoughtfully designed residential spaces to key 
markets like Mysuru, and nurture vibrant communities that symbolise quality, innovation, and holistic 
living. This project is in line with our vision of creating inclusive communities and caters to diverse lifestyle 
needs, while maintaining the highest standards of quality and innovation.” 
 
With its strategic location, exceptional design, and premium offerings, this new venture reflects Brigade 
Group’s commitment to redefining urban living while meeting the growing demand for luxury housing with 
senior living options.  
 
About Brigade Group: Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers 
with close to four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and 
winning their customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark buildings and transformed skylines 
across South India in the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Trivandrum and Gift 
City, Gujarat with developments across Residential, Office, Retail and Hotels.
