In pursuance of Regulation 30, 33, 43 and 52 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing 
Regulations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended, (‘SEBI LODR’), this is to 
inform that the Board of Directors of POWERGRID (‘the Company’), in its meeting held today i.e.

i. Un-audited Financial Results of the Company for the 3rd Quarter and Nine months ended                         
31st December, 2024 along with the Limited Review Report of the Auditors (Annexure-A) . 
 
ii. Payment of 2nd (second) Interim Dividend of Rs. 3.25/- per equity share of Rs. 10/- each (@32.50%

earlier, the Record Date for the purpose of payment of dividend shall be Friday, 07th February, 2025.

D/C Line at Hindalco Switchyard” at an estimated cost of Rs. 370.02 crore scheduled to be 
commissioned within 30 months from effective date i.e. by December, 2026.
