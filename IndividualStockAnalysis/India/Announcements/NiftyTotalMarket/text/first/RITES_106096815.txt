Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD -
PoD-1/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023, and pursuant to requisite approval of the Board of 
Directors and Shareholders of MMG - Metro Management Group Limited  (a Joint Venture 
Company of RITES ), DMRC and Israeli firm M/s. Poran Shrem Engineering and Appraisal 
Limited) , this is to inform that  the process of “voluntary dissolution ” of MMG - Metro 
Management G roup Limited has been initiated.  
 
MMG - Metro Management Group Limited  was formed by RITES in  Joint Venture  with  DMRC 
and Israeli firm M/s. Poran Shrem Engineering and Appraisal Limited) and an intimation in 
this regard was given to the stock exchange on February 08,202 3.The said company was 
formed for the sole purpose of participating in the tender process for the Management of  the 
Tel Aviv Metropolitan Metro Lines in Israel.  During the process of bid evaluation, the 
consortium could not meet the required eligibility criteria and hence the company is no longer 
required  and shall be wound up . 
 
 
 
  
 
 
 
The share of RITES in  MMG - Metro Management Group Limited is 24.5% and the total equity 
contributed by RITES i s USD 73113.99  (INR 59.65 Lakhs) . The Com pany has made 
provision for dimin ution against the amount invested in the  MMG - Metro Management Group 
Limited.
