Switch Mobility Limited, UK (Switch UK) a stepdown subsidiary of the Company is a 
manufacturer of electric busses having presence across UK & Europe. In the wake of 
continuing general economic uncertainty within the overall bus manufacturing sector in the 
UK and consequent inability to derive the benefits of scale,  the Board of Directors of Switch 
UK today approved commencement of consultation process with the employees which could 
potentially lead to cessation of its manufacturing and assembly activities a t its Sherburn 
facility.  Switch UK has no plans to exit the UK market. It will execute and complete all the 
orders on hand and will continue to provide aftermarket and service support from two other 
facilities in Rotherham and Thurrock for the existing veh icle parc.
