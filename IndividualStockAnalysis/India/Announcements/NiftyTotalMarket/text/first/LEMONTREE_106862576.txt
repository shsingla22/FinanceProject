LEMON TREE HOTELS SIGNS  
 KEYS LITE BY LEMON TREE HOTELS, BANGA  
 
Strengthens the Group’s presence in Punjab, with three operational hotels and 
11 upcoming properties in the state  
 
New Delhi, 16th November , 2026 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, one of India’s 
leading hospitality companies, today announced the signing of Keys Lite by 
Lemon Tree Hotels, Banga  in Punjab.  
The property will be operated under a franchise agreement, further strengthening 
the Group’s presence in the state and expanding its footprint across emerging 
commercial destinations.  
With this signing, Lemon Tree Hotels’ portfolio in Punjab will comprise three 
operational hotels and 11 upcoming properties, reflecting its continued focus on 
building a presence across key business centres and developing markets.  
Located in the Shaheed Bhagat Singh Nagar district, Banga offers connectivity to 
important commercial and industrial centres in Punjab, including Ludhiana, one of 
the state's prominent manufacturing and business hubs. Its location along the 
regional travel  route  between Jalandhar and Ludhiana makes it relevant for 
business travellers, industrial professionals and visitors travelling for commercial 
engagements, while also catering to transit demand.  
The upcoming hotel will feature 42 well -appointed rooms, designed to offer guests 
a comfortable and convenient stay. The property will also include a restaurant, 
banquet and conference hall, a fitness centre and other public areas, catering to 
the needs of  business and leisure travellers as well as social gatherings.  
Commenting on the signing, Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, 
Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd. , said: “Our expansion in Punjab reflects our confidence 
in the state's growing commercial and travel potential which extends well beyond 
its larger cities. Banga is a strategic addition to our network given its connectivity 
to key business centres such as Ludhian a and Jalandhar.  Through Keys Lite by 
Lemon Tree Hotels, we aim to cater to the needs of business travellers and 
industry professionals with comfortable stays and consistent hospitality. With 
three operational hotels and 11 upcoming properties, we continu e to strengthen 
our presence across the state.”  
The hotel will enjoy convenient connectivity to the wider region. Sri Guru Ravidas 
Maharaj Ji Airport, Adampur, is approximately 46 km away, while Banga Railway 
Station is approximately 5 km from the property.
