FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE  
Ellenbarrie posts higher ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA - Q2 FY27 revenue grows 28% to ₹114.3 crore; 
EBITDA rises 21% to ₹40.7 crore  
Kolkata, 4 November 2026: Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases Limited (NSE: ELLEN; BSE: 544421) reported its financial performance for the quarter 
and six months ended 30 September 2026.  
For Q2 FY27, revenue from operations increased 28% year on year to ₹114.3 crore. EBITDA rose 21% to ₹40.7 crore, with an EBITDA margin 
of 35.6%. Profit before tax increased 20% to ₹47.9 crore. Profit after tax (PAT) was ₹35.6 crore, compared with ₹36.7 crore a year earlier, when 
the Company recognised a deferred tax credit of ₹4.5 crore. 
For H1 FY27, revenue from operations was ₹213.0 crore, up 23% year on year. EBITDA grew 21% to ₹79.4 crore and PAT increased 27% to 
₹70.6 crore.

Operating and project highlights  
 The core gases business  reported revenue of ₹112.1 crore in Q2 FY27, up 27% y-y . Gas segment margins improved sequentially to 39%, from 38% in Q1 FY27. 
 The Kurnool and Uluberia II merchant facilities continued to ramp up. A new 320 TPD onsite plant in East India was commissioned during Q2 FY27, with a fuller revenue 
contribution expected from Q3 FY27. 
 The Company received a ₹480.7 crore order to supply and erect a 1,200 TPD cryogenic Air Separation Unit on a turnkey basis for a coal-to-ammonium nitrate project in 
Jharsuguda, Odisha during the quarter. Project is to be executed over FY28 and FY29. 
 As of 30 September 2026, installed bulk and onsite capacities were 915 TPD and 1,018 TPD, respectively. Additional merchant plants are being developed in North and 
West/Central India for FY28. 
Capital expenditure and Balance Sheet  
Planned capital expenditure of ₹250 crore for FY27 and ₹200 crore for FY28 remains on track. This is primarily for augmentation of the 
merchant capacity of the company. Company is well capitalized with cash and equivalents of ₹424 crore as at 30 September 2026.  
Management commentary  
Mr. Padam Kumar Agarwala, Chairman and Managing Director, Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases Limited, said:  
“The quarter saw continued growth in our gases business as recently commissioned capacities increased their contribution. Our new East India 
onsite plant commenced operations during the quarter, and we are focused on improving utilisation across the network. The BHEL turnkey ASU 
order also adds to our project engineering business. We remain attentive to freight and power costs while progressing our planned capacity 
investments.”  
About Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases Limited  
Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases Limited manufactures and supplies industrial, medical and specialty gases, including oxygen, nitrogen, argon and 
hydrogen. Its operations comprise bulk, packaged and onsite gas supply, alongside project engineering services for air separation units and 
related applications.
