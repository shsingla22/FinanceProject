EXPLANATORY STATEMENT FOR BUSINESS ITEM NO. 1 OF THE POSTAL BALLOT NOTICE (PURSUANT TO SECTION 102  OF THE COMPANIES ACT, 2013 ) 
 
The Securities and Exchange Board of India (“SEBI”), through its Consultation Paper dated June 24, 2025, proposed the appoint ment of Executive 
Directors on the Governing Boards of Market Infrastructure Institutions (“MIIs”) with a view to further strengthe ning governance and aligning 
organisational structures with their expanding scale and regulatory significance. Subsequently, SEBI, vide its Notification d ated November 21, 2025, 
amended the Securities Contracts (Regulation) (Stock Exchanges and Clearing Co rporations) Regulations, 2018 (“SECC Regulations”) by inserting 
Regulation 25A which, inter alia, permits recognised stock exchanges to appoint an Executive Director to head Vertical 3 . 
 
In order to further strengthen governance standards and provide dedicated leadership and strategic direction, the Company ini tiated the process for 
identification and selection of a suitable candidate for the position of Executive Director – Business (Vert ical 3) (“ED -3”), in accordance with the 
applicable provisions of the SECC Regulations and the framework prescribed by SEBI . 
 
Pursuant to the SEBI Circular dated December 12, 2025, public advertisements specifying the eligibility criteria and other re quirements were published 
on January 2, 2026, January 5, 2026 and January 6, 2026 in The Economic Times, Mint and Business Standard , respectively . 
 
Further, pursuant to SEBI’s letter dated February 25, 2026, it was clarified that the appointment of an Independent External Professional on the 
Nomination and Remuneration Committee (“NRC”) for the selection of Executive Directors was optional. It was als o clarified that MIIs may devise an 
appropriate framework for appointment of an Executive Director for Vertical 3, subject to approval of appointment  by SEBI and ratification by 
shareholders .  
 
The Company appointed ABC Consultants (“Agency”)  as the executive search agency for shortlisting candidates for the position of ED -3. The Agency 
processed the applications received pursuant to the aforesaid advertisements. The NRC, across its various meetings, evaluated the candidates 
shortlisted by the agency and subsequently interviewed the final shortlisted candidates. Following a comprehensive assessment , the NRC recommended 
two candidates to the Board without any order of preference, along with their proposed terms and conditions of appointment, reporting structure, 
compensation, and appraisal framework.
