SEBI Listing ReguSations read with Section 108 of the Act & Rule 20 of the Companies

proposed in the Notice to be passed at the Meeting by electronic means. The Equity
Shareholders of the Company had the facility and option of voting on the Resolution
proposed in the Notice for casting their votes through: (a) e-voting system at the Meeting; or
(b) by Remote e-voting.
Accordingly, the voting period for Remote e-voting was commenced on Monday, March 24,
2025 at 10:00 A.M. (1ST) and ended on Thursday, March 27,2025 at 05:00 P.M. (1ST) and
e-voting system available at the Meeting (held virtually); and the CDSL e-voting platform was
disabled thereafter.

Meeting were unblocked and calculated after the conclusion of the Meeting.
