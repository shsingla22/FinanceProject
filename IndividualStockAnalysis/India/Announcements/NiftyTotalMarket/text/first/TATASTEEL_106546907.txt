Sub: Sale of Ferro Alloy Plant at Jajpur to Indian Metals & Ferro Alloys Ltd.  
 
This is further to the press release  dated November 4, 2025  in connection with Tata Steel 
Limited (‘Company’) entering into an Asset Transfer Agreement with Indian Metals & Ferro  
Alloys Ltd. for the sale of its Ferro Alloy Plant at Jajpur, Odisha .

at Jajpur, Odisha for a base consideration of ₹610 crore  (net of GST and excluding working 
capital ) to Indian Metals & Ferro Alloys Ltd .
