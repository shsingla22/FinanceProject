Dr. Reddy’s Receives DCGI Approval for Zytorvi® (toripalimab) in 
Advanced Esophageal Squamous Cell Carcinoma , Its Second Approved 
Indication in India  
The approval expands the use of Zytorvi® beyond its initially approved indication in 
nasopharyngeal carcinoma (NPC ) 
Hyderabad, India; October 21, 2026:  Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories Ltd. (BSE: 500124, NSE: DRREDDY, NYSE: 
RDY, NSEIFSC: DRREDDY ; along with its subsidiaries together referred to as “Dr. Reddy’s,” ) today announced 
that the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation  (CDSCO) , under the Drugs Controller General of India  
(DCGI) , has approved Zytorvi® (toripalimab)  in combination with cisplatin and paclitaxel  for the first -line 
treatment of adult patients with unresectable advanced, recurrent or metastatic esophageal squamous cell 
carcinoma (ESCC) .1 
Dr. Reddy’s entered into a licensing and commercialization agreement with Junshi Biosciences in 2023 for 
toripalimab across 21 countries . Globally, Toripalimab is approved by regulatory authorities including the U.S. 
Food and Drug Administration (USFDA), the  European Medicines Agency ( EMA ) and the UK Medicines and 
Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA).2 Notably, the EMA approval is for first -line treatment of ESCC 
irrespective of PD -L1 expression .3 The approved indication in India similarly does not require patient selection 
based on PD -L1 expression , enabling appropriate patients to be considered for treatment regardless of their 
biomarker status .1,3 
In 2024, esophageal cancer was the fourth leading cause of cancer -related death in India , accounting for 
approximately 8.1% of all cancer deaths4. ESCC, the predominant subtype,  accounts for approximately 80% 
of all esophageal cancer cases  in India . Through Zytorvi®, Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories Ltd. aims to expand access 
to first -line immunotherapy for appropriate patients with advanced ESCC in India.  
The approval is supported by the results from the Phase 3 JUPITER -06 trial. In the study,  toripalimab in 
combination with paclitaxel and cisplatin reduced the risk of disease or death by 42% compared with 
chemotherapy alone.7 At the final overall -survival analysis, median overall survival was 17.7 months  with 
toripalimab plus chemotherapy compared with 12.9 months  with chemotherapy alone . Clinical benefit was 
observed across PD -L1-expression subgroups .8 
M.V. Ramana, Chief Executive Officer, Global Generics, Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, said:  
“Following the approval of Zytorvi® in nasopharyngeal carcinoma, this second indication marks an important 
step in extending the reach of immunotherapy to patients with advanced esophageal squamous cell carcinoma. 
It gives physicians an additional first -line treatment option for appropriate patients. As we continue to build our 
oncology portfolio, our focus remains on bringing meaningful advances and expanding access to innovative 
treatment options .” 
About esophageal squamous cell carcinoma  
Esophageal cancer begins in the tissues of the esophagus, the muscular tube that carries food and liquids from 
the throat to the stomach. ESCC develops from the squamous cells lining the esophagus.   
Risk factors associated with ESCC include smoked and smokeless tobacco, alcohol consumption, areca -nut 
use, consumption of very hot beverages, poor diet and certain environmental and socioeconomic factors. Risk

Progressive difficulty in swallowing, pain or discomfort when swallowing, unexplained weight loss, persistent 
chest discomfort and regurgitation may be associated with esophageal cancer. These symptoms may also be 
caused by other conditions and require eva luation by a qualified healthcare professional.5,6 
About Zytorvi® (toripalimab)  
Zytorvi® contains toripalimab, a next -generation humanised IgG4 monoclonal antibody9 directed against 
programmed death receptor -1, or PD -1. By binding to PD -1, toripalimab blocks its interaction with PD -L1 and 
PD-L2, supporting T -cell activity against cancer cells.3 
In 2023, Dr. Reddy’s entered into a licence and commercialisation agreement with Shanghai Junshi Biosciences 
Co., Ltd. for toripalimab. Under this agreement, Dr. Reddy’s holds exclusive rights to develop and commercialise 
toripalimab in 21 countries, inclu ding India, South Africa, Brazil and several countries in Latin America, Australia, 
New Zealand and nine other countries.2 Dr. Reddy’s launched Zytorvi® in India in November 2024 for recurrent 
or metastatic nasopharyngeal carcinoma, making India the third country in the world, after China and the United 
States, to receive access to the medicine.2 
Toripalimab is approved by regulatory authorities including the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (USFDA), 
the EMA and the UK Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA).2 The European 
Commission authorised toripalimab for first -line ESCC in September 2024.3
