With reference to our earlier communication  vide letter NAVA/SECTL/514/2025 -26 dated March 
30, 2026 , regarding the temporary shutdown of the two Furnaces of the Ferro Alloys Plant (FAP) at 
the Company's Odisha  Works  for scheduled proactive  maintenance, we wish to inform you that the

furnace outage.  
 
The Company will resume  operations of both Furnaces with effect from August 1, 2026  with 
captive power .
