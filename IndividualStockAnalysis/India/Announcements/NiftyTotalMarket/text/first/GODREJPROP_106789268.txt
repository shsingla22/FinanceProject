PRESS RELEASE  
Noida, September 23, 2026  
 
Godrej Properties Marks 100th Home Handover at Godrej Nest with 'Shubh 
Aarambh'; Actor Sanya Malhotra Surprises the Family  
Godrej Properties Limited (GPL) (BSE scrip ID: GODREJPROP), one of India's leading real estate 
developers, celebrated its 100th home handover at Godrej Nest, Sector 150, Noida, through 
'Shubh Aarambh', its signature home-handover initiative.  
Godrej Properties has delivered over 3,000 homes in Delhi-NCR in the last 12 months, 
reinforcing the focus on execution and commitment to customers. Over the last few weeks, 120 
families have received the keys to their new homes at Godrej Nest, a community built around 
convenience and well-being. In one of the most memorable moments of this Shubh Aarambh 
season, actor Sanya Malhotra surprised the 100th family as they entered their home at Godrej 
Nest. 
Speaking on the occasion, actor Sanya Malhotra said,  "There is something incredibly special 
about witnessing a family's ﬁrst moments in a new home. It's a milestone ﬁlled with excitement, 
hope and the promise of new memories. What I particularly love about Godrej Nest is the idea that 
life doesn't have to follow a ﬁxed schedule. Movement and wellness have always been central to my 
life, which is why the philosophy of 'Life, On Your Time' resonates so strongly with me. It was 
wonderful to surprise the family today and be part of their Shubh Aarambh as they begin this 
exciting new chapter."  
For many families, homeownership represents years of aspiration, planning and dreams. 
Through Shubh Aarambh, Godrej Properties seeks to honour this milestone by transforming a 
routine possession process into a meaningful celebration that recognises the emotional 
signiﬁcance of receiving the keys to one's home.  
Today's lifestyles are increasingly dynamic. As work, wellness, leisure and family time extend 
beyond conventional schedules, the idea of home is evolving too. 'Life, On Your Time' is rooted 
in the belief that a home should adapt to the rhythm of its residents, rather than requiring 
residents to adapt to it. Godrej Nest has been envisioned around this shi ŉ, oňering a curated 
mix of lifestyle and wellness amenities including a 24×7 gym, swimming pool, tennis and 
basketball courts, cricket facilities, indoor games and yoga and meditation spaces, alongside 
conveniences such as a 24×7 party lounge, e-library and medical support services. Together 
with Godrej Nest's ﬁve-tier security framework, this ecosystem is designed to give residents not 
only access around the clock, but the conﬁdence and freedom to use their community on their 
own terms.  
Commenting on the occasion, Geetika Trehan, CEO – North Zone, Godrej Properties, said,  
"Homebuyers today are seeking more than a well-designed residence. They want communities that 
adapt to their changing routines and support di ňerent dimensions of their lives. Through the 
philosophy of 'Life, On Your Time', Godrej Nest brings together thoughtful planning, ﬂexible access 
to lifestyle amenities, security and well-being, giving residents the freedom to truly live life on their 
own terms."  
 
She further added, “Every handover represents a promise fulﬁlled and, more importantly, a 
signiﬁcant milestone in our customers' lives. We remain ﬁrmly commi Ŋed to delivering with 
consistency, quality and care, and to creating lasting joy for the families who place their trust in us."  
Godrej Nest is located in Sector 150, within one of Noida's premium residential micro-markets 
along the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway. The micro-market's low-density planning, green 
environment and connectivity have contributed to its growing residential appeal. The 
handovers at Godrej Nest mark another step in Godrej Properties' focus on creating 
communities that respond to changing lifestyles and evolving expectations from urban 
homeownership.
