Dear Sir  / Madam,  
 Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, 
we hereby inform that the BSE Limited (“BSE”) and National Stock Exchange of India Limited (“NSE”) 
have issued a warning letter on April 1, 2026 for inti mation submitted by the Company on May 16, 2025 
on cessation of employment of Major Sukriti Shukla Retd. with citing the reason as “ Major Sukriti Shukla 
Retd. is no longer serving as Chief Human Resource Officer and Senior Management Personnel of the 
Compa ny” instead of citing  the reason as “Termination/ Removal”.  
 
Further , we wish to clarify that the Company was mindful of avoiding any public statement that would 
have cast aspersions on the standing of Major Shukla by explicitly stating the reason “Termination/ 
Removal” as it would have negatively impacted her professional reputation in public , resulting into an 
inability to secure any potential employment  and which would have placed an untenable stigma on her 
reputation depriving her of her livelihood.  
 
The warning letters received from BSE and NSE are enclosed herewith.  
 Further, the Company reiterates that it shall ensure that the exact reason is stated in such disclosures 
henceforth in compliance with the applicable provisions of the SEBI regulations.

Sub: Warning Letter  
 
Dear  Sir/Madam,  
 
This is in reference to the disclosure submitted by Transrail Lighting Limited (‘the Company’) on May 16 , 
2025, stating that Major Sukriti Shukla Retd is no longer serving as a Chief Human  Resource Officer and 
Senior Management Personnel of the Company.  
 
On analysis and upon clarification  sought , it was observed that aforesaid cessation was pursuant to 
termination, however, reason for change mentioned in the disclosure was Major Sukriti Shukla is no longer 
serving as Chief Human Resource Officer and Senior Management Personnel (SMP) of the Company . 
 
Accordingly, the Company seem s to be violating below stated provision of SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (‘SEBI LODR’):  
 
1. Regulation 30 read with Sub-para 7 of Para A of Part A of Schedule III of SEBI LODR, 2015 read 
with SEBI Circular dated July 13, 2023 - for inaccurate  reason for change.  
 
2. Regulation 4. (1) The listed entity which has listed securities shall make disclosures and abide by its 
obligations under these regulations, in accordance with the following principles:  
c. The listed entity shall refrain from misrepresentation and ensure that the information provided to 
recognised stock exchange(s) and investors is not misleading.  
d. The listed entity shall provide adequate and timely information to recognised stock exchange(s) and 
investors.  
e. The listed entity shall ensure that disseminations made under provisions of these regulations and 
circulars made thereunder, are adequate, accurate, explicit, timely and presented in a simple language.  
 
The aforesaid non -compliance on your part is viewed seriously. You are hereby warned and advised to be 
careful in future, exercise due diligence and initiate corrective steps to avoid recurrence of such lapses so as 
to ensure due compliance with the applic able provisions of SEBI LODR and related SEBI circulars. Any 
aberration in future will be viewed seriously, and appropriate action would be initiated.  
 
The Company is required to disseminate a copy of this Warning Letter on the Stock Exchanges where it is 
listed.
