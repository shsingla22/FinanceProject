Press Release:  Dated:  25.09.2024  
SML Isuzu Limited appointed  Mr. C S Verma as Chairman of the Company  
SML Isuzu Limited has appointed Mr. C . S. Verma as the new Chairman of the Company 
effective from 22nd September 2024 in place of Mr. S.K. Tuteja  whose term ended on 21st 
September, 2024. Mr. S.K. Tuteja, a retired IAS officer, was associated with the Company 
for the last 26 years.  
Mr. C.S. Verma is a distinguished leader who has held significant posts in prestigious 
organisations of the country a cross various industries. He has a vast experience of more 
than four decades, mainly in the industrial organisations. He has been the Chairman and 
Managing Director (CMD) of Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL) from June, 2010 to June, 
2015 and also hel d additional charge of CMD, National Mineral Development Corporation 
(NMDC) for nearly two years. Besides, he has been the Chairman of International Coal 
Ventures Ltd. (ICVL), a Joint Venture of five leading Central Public Sector Enterprises 
(CPSE). He has  also worked in BHEL for five years as the Director (Finance) of the 
Company.  
His professional recognitions include being the Chairman of SCOPE, the apex body of 
CPSEs; the first president of the Indian Steel Association, the apex body of the Indian Steel 
Industry; and, Chairman of the ‘National Committee on Steel’ of Confederation of Indian 
Industries (CII). Besides, he has been the Member of the Governing Council of All India 
Management Association (AIMA); Member on the Board of Governors of Indian Instit ute of 
Management (IIM), Raipur and the Council Member of Indian Institute of Metals.  
Mr. Verma has made significant contributions to the industry and has been conferred with 
several prestigious awards, including the ‘CEO of the Year’ Platts Global Metals Award, the 
IIM - JRD Tata Award for Excellence in Corporate leadership in Metallurgical Industry and 
SCOPE Award for “Excellence & Outstanding Contribution to the Public Sector Management 
– Individual Category.  
The Company is thrilled and hopes that Mr Ver ma, with his experience and vision, will be 
able to provide a dynamic leadership to the Company to reach even greater heights to 
realise its full potential.  
About SML Isuzu Limited:  
SML ISUZU LIMITED (formerly known as Swaraj Mazda Limited) was incorporate d in July 
1983 to manufacture Light Commercial Vehicles (LCV) in the state of Punjab. It started  its 
commercial operations in 1986.  
Presently, Sumitomo Corporation, Japan (the promoter of the Company) holds a 44% stake 
in the Company and Isuzu Motors, Japa n (the technical partner) holds a 15% stake in the 
Company. The Company manufactures a vast range of Commercial Vehicles – Buses, 
Trucks  and Special Application Vehicles  such as Ambulances, Tipper, Police Van, Water 
Tanker, etc.
