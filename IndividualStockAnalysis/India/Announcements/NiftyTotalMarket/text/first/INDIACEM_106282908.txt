Responsibility of the Management 
The Management of the Company is responsible for ensuring compliances with the

Responsibility as a Scrutinizer 
My responsibility as a scrutinizer for e-voting process is restricted to making a 
Scrutinizer's Report on the votes cast "in favour" or "against" by the members in respect 
of the resolution contained in the Postal Ballot Notice. My report is based on verification 
of data and reports generated from the voting system provided by National Securities 
Depository Limited (NSDL), the authorised agency engaged by the Company to provide 
voting through electronic means i.e. e-voting. 
I submit my report as under: 
As per the MCA Circulars, the Postal Ballot Notice was sent only by e-mail to those 
Members who have registered their e-mail address with the Company / Registrar and 
Share Transfer Agent or with their DP and made available to the Company by the 
Depositories, on the cut-off date i.e., 16 th  May 2025. 
The Notice of the Postal Ballot was also available on the Company's website 
www.indiacements.co.in , websites of the Stock Exchanges i.e. BSE Limited and 
National Stock Exchange of India Limited at www.bseindia.com  and 
www.nseindia.com  respectively and on the website of NSDL i.e. 
www.evoting.nsdl.com . Physical copies of the Notice along with Postal Ballot Form 
and pre-paid business reply envelope were not sent to members for this Postal Ballot. 
The Company has availed the e-voting facility offered by NSDL for providing to its 
members the facility to cast their vote through electronic means. 
The remote e-voting period commenced on 28t h  May, 2025 at 9:00 A.M. (1ST) and 
ended on 26t h  June, 2025 at 5:00 P.M. (1ST). The remote e-voting module had been 
disabled by NSDL for voting thereafter. 
An advertisement regarding notice of Postal Ballot/E-Voting was published in 
Business Line and Business Standard, English News Papers and Dinamani, Tamil 
News Paper on 27th May, 2025. 
The shareholders have cast their votes through e-voting provided by NSDL. 
All the votes cast up to 5:00 PM (1ST) on 26 th  June, 2025, being the last date and time 
fixed by the Company for remote e-voting, were considered for my scrutiny. 
The votes were unblocked by me after the conclusion of e-voting period on 26t h  June, 
2025, in the presence of two witnesses, Ms.M.K.Sangeetha and Ms.V.Revathy, who 
are not in the employment of the Company. 
Thereafter, the details containing the list of Members who voted "in favour" or "against" 
on the resolution were generated from the e-voting website of NSDL. Based on the 
report generated and relied upon by me, data on the e-voting was scrutinized.
