 Sub.: Revision in Vesting Period – Axis Bank Employees Stock Option Scheme –  2000 -01  
 
Ref.: Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
(“SEBI Listing Regulations”).  
 
In furtherance to our letter no. AXIS/CO/CS/ 30/2025- 26 dated April 25, 2025 on the captioned 
subject, we wish to inform you that, based on the directions received from the Reserve Bank of India and on recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board of Directors 
of Axis Bank Limited has today i.e. July 24 , 2025, revised  the vesting of 1,85,537 options stock granted 
under Axis Bank Employees Stock Option Scheme – 2000- 01 equally over 4 (four) years @ 25% every 
year (commencing on expiry of one year from the date of grant) as compared to vesting of 30%, 
30% an d 40% options at the end of one year, two year and third year, respectively. All other terms 
and conditions of the above grants, including exercise price, remains unchanged.   
Please note that the  above matter was passed through circulation at 4.26 p.m.
