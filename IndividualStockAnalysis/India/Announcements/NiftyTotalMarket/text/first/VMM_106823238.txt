Commenting on the result s, Mr. Gunender Kapur, Founder, Chief Executive 
Officer and Managing Director said:   
 
“We delivered another strong quarter, supported by the strength of our own -brand offerings, 
our customer -first approach and disciplined execution. Demand conditions remained steady 
despite renewed inflationary pressures across key raw material categories an d an uneven 
monsoon. Our differentiated merchandise, compelling price positioning and deep 
understanding of customer needs continued to resonate with shoppers, reinforcing the 
resilience of our business.  
 
For Q2 FY27, revenue from operations grew by 14.0% to Rs. 3,398.5 crores. SSSG stood at 
5.4%. The festivals this year have shifted from Q2 to Q3. This has an impact of 3 -4% on SSSG. 
Adjusting for that, SSSG will be about 9%.  
For H1FY27 , revenue from operations grew by 16.4% to Rs. 7,125.5 crores . SSSG stood at 
7.7%. The festivals this year have shifted from H1 to H2. This has an impact of 1. 5-2% on 
SSSG. Adjusting for that, SSSG will be about 9.5% . 
 
PAT for Q2FY27 grew by 3.7% to Rs. 157.9 crores and for H1FY27, grew by 16.3% to Rs. 
416.6 crores . PAT growth was achieved despite lower fixed -cost absorption due to the shift in 
festive buying to Q3 and inflationary  pressures during the quarter .  
 
We continued to advance our store expansion strategy, adding 26 gross new stores during the 
quarter and 53 during the half year. In Q2, we launched the first pilot store of ‘Belong & Co.’ , 
our new apparel format catering to young, affluent urban consumers, with a distinct brand 
proposition offering effortless fashion.  
 
We had a s trong start to the festivals and this has reinforced our confidence in the demand 
momentum as we approach Diwali and the wedding season. Looking ahead, we remain focused 
on capturing growth opportunities while staying agile  in an evolving geopolitical and 
inflationary environment. By strengthening our merchandise offering, enhancing supply chain 
efficiency and maintaining disciplined cost management, we aim to continue delivering 
compelling customer value and sustainable, pr ofitable growth.”
