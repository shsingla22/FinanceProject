Hon’ble High Court, Bombay for recovery of the 
claim. The case was duly represented and 
contested by the Company by taking available 
defenses. The Bank subsequently decided not to 
pursue with the suit against the Company and 
submitted an application of withdrawal of the suit.    
 
Update:  
 
The Hon’ble High Court, Bombay allowed the 
application of the Bank and disposed of the Suit 
as withdrawn and disposed of all the pending 
applications related to the suit.
