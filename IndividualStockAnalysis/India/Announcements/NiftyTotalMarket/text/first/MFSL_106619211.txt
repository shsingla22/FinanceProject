Brief Profile of Mr. V. Krishnan  
 
Mr. V. Krishnan has been associated with the Max Group since 1992 and, in his over 34 years of

ventures, fund raising, mergers & acquisitions, stakeholder management, business restructuring,

smooth and timely completion of projects.
