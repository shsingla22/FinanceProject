Dear Sir/ Madam, 
Pursuant to the above referred, kindly note that the Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting held 
today i.e., Wednesday, August 13, 2025, based on the recommendation of Nomination and Remuneration 
Committee have inter alia considered and approved the introduction & adoption of Aditya Birla Lifestyle 
Brands Limited Employee Stock Option Scheme 2025 ("ESOP Scheme  2025"). 
The aggregate number of Stock Options proposed to be granted under the ESOP Scheme 2025, shall not be 
exercisable into more than 43,70,000 equity shares corresponding to 0.36 % of the equity share capital of the 
Company. 
The introduction & adoption of ESOP Scheme 2025 shall be subject to the shareholders' approval of the 
Company at the ensuing Annual General Meeting ("AGM"). The details required as per SEBI SBEB SE Regulations 
will form part of the explanatory statement to  the notice of the AGM of  the Company. 
The meeting commenced at 11:30 a.m. and concluded at 1:15 p.m. 
The above is being made available on the Company's website i.e., www.ablbl.in
