Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles to increase prices of its Cars & SUVs from December 2026  
Mumbai, November 24, 2026: Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd. (TMPV), India's leading manufacturer of cars and 
SUVs, today announced a revision in the prices of its entire portfolio, covering both internal combustion engine (ICE) 
and electric vehicles (EVs).  The revision will res ult in an average price increase of 0.5% and will come into effect from 
December 1, 2026.  
This revision is being undertaken to partially offset the impact of rise in input costs which continue to remain 
elevated.  While TMPV continues to absorb a significant portion of these increases, a part of the impact is being passed 
on to customers through this increase. The extent of price revision will vary across models and variants,  ensuring 
that every vehicle continues to deliver meaningful value to customers .
