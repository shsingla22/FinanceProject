This is in continuation to our letter dated May 2, 2026 and May 6, 2026 and the approval provided 
by the members  of the Company through postal ballot on June 7, 2026, We would like to inform that 
the Committee of Directors (the “Committee”) at its meeting held today i.e. June 18, 2026 has inter -
alia considered and approved the allotment of 8,15,40,595 equity shares at Rs.10/ - each as fully paid 
up bonus equity shares, in the ratio of 1:3 i.e. 1 (one)  bonus  equity share for every 3 (three) equity 
shares held, to eligible members whose names appear in the Register of Members / List of Beneficial 
Owners as on June 17, 2026 being the Record Date fixed for this purpose.  
 
The paid -up equity share capital of the Company has increased from Rs. 244,62,17,850/ - to                                  
Rs. 326,16,23,800/ - due to the aforesaid allotment.  
 
The aforesaid bonus equity shares allotted shall rank pari -passu in all respects with the existing fully 
paid -up equity shares of the Company.  
 
51620  bonus equity shares forming part of the aforesaid allotment representing fractional 
entitlement(s) of eligible members has been consolidated and allotted in the name of an Independent 
Professional Company by the Committee of Directors. These shares shall be sold by that Company as 
soon as possible and the net sale proceeds shall be distributed after defraying relevant expenses of the 
sale, applicable taxes, if any, to the eligible members of the Company in proportion to their fractional 
entitlement.
