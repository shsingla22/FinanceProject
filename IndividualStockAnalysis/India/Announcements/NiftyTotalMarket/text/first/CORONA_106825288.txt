PRESS RELEASE  
CORONA Remedies receives ANVISA GMP  (Brazil) Certification  for Bhayla Manufacturing 
Facility, Reinforcing Its Commitment to “One World, One Quality ” 
 
Ahmedabad | October  22, 2026   
CORONA Remedies Limited , an India -focused pharmaceutical company with a strong presence across 
Women ’s Healthcare, Cardio -Diabetes, Pain Management, and Urology, has received Good 
Manufacturing Practice (GMP) certification from Brazil ’s National Health Surveillance Agency 
(ANVIS A) for its manufacturing facility at Bhayla, Ahmedabad, covering both Oral Solid Dosage (OSD) 
and Women ’s Hormone production lines.  This milestone further strengthens CORONA ’s 
manufacturing credentials and reaffirms its commitment to maintaining high stand ards of quality, 
compliance, and operational excellence.  
Reaffirming the Philosophy of “One World, One Quality ” 
While  India will remain the company ’s primary market, contributing over 96% of its revenue ; the 
ANVISA GMP certification  further strengthe ns its manufacturing credentials . This marks CORONA ’s 
third major GMP accreditation, building on its EU -GMP approvals for the Women ’s Hormone and OSD 
facilities and its EAEU -GMP certification for the OSD facility .  
Brazil represents one of the largest pharma ceutical markets in Latin America, with an estimated market 
size of USD  44 billion1, this market present s opportunities for pharmaceutical manufacturers with 
strong quality systems and regulatory capabilities.  
Together, these recognitions reflect the compa ny’s continued focus on strengthening quality systems, 
maintaining regulatory compliance, and fostering a culture of continuous improvement across its 
manufacturing operations.  
 
Commenting on th is achievement , Mr. Viral Sitwala , Executive Director , CORONA Remedies Limited, 
said:   
“Quality has always been central to the way we work at CORONA Remedies. Our philosophy of ‘One 
World, One Quality ’ reflects our belief that the standards we follow must remain consistent and 
uncompromised.  
The ANVISA GMP certificati on for our Bhayla manufacturing facility is an important recognition of the 
systems, discipline, and collective efforts that go into maintaining these standards. While India remains 
our primary market and the focus of our business, such accreditations fur ther strengthen our 
manufacturing credentials and reaffirm our commitment to quality and compliance. We will continue 
to strengthen our capabilities and uphold the quality standards that guide everything we do. ”
