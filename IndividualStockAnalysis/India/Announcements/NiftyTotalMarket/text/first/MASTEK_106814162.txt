Mastek and CORT Win ISG Paragon Partnership of the Year 2026 for North 
America, Recognizing a Decade of Digital Transformation  
From digital commerce modernization to an AI -powered future, the award celebrates ten years 
of continuous innovation and measurable business outcomes.  
 
Dallas , Texas , October  12, 2026:  Mastek, a trusted AI -first digital engineering and cloud 
transformation partner and CORT Business Services, a Berkshire Hathaway company, have won 
the 2026 ISG Paragon Partnership of the Year award for North America, recognizing their 
decade -long partnersh ip and continued evolution in digital and business transformation.  
 
For more than 10 years, Mastek has partnered with CORT to evolve CORT’s digital and 
technology landscape across B2C, B2B and international markets. What began as application 
modernization and digital commerce transformation has progressively evolved into a  strategic 
partnership spanning digital engineering, platform modernization, AI -led transformation and 
consolidated managed services.  
 
Together, Mastek and CORT have modernized digital commerce, strengthened customer 
experience  and enabled new business capabilities across B2C, B2B and international markets. 
Mastek supports CORT’s digital ecosystem through application modernization, digital 
engineering, quality engineering, cloud and managed services, helping create a foundation for 
continuous innovation and business growth.  
 
More recently, Mastek designed, built and launched CORT Furniture Outlet, a business -to-
consumer eCommerce platform that enables customers to discover and purchase high -quality 
used furniture online or in -store at discounted prices. Within two weeks of lau nch, the platform 
delivered a 37% increase in online conversion rates, a 69% increase in overall online purchase 
volume and a 30% increase in organic traffic. Mastek’s managed services team has continued to 
support and enhance the platform following its la unch.  
 
Building on this foundation, the partnership is now advancing toward an AI -led business 
transformation roadmap, focused on embedding AI across customer journeys, digital commerce, 
engineering and operations to accelerate decision -making, automate processes  and create new 
sources of business value. The roadmap also explores the evolution of traditional managed 
services toward a “Services as Software” model, combining AI, automation, reusable digital 
capabilities and outcome -based services to deliver technolo gy and business capabilities with 
greater speed, scale and intelligence.  
 
This next phase reflects a broader shift from technology modernization to continuous, AI -
powered business transformation —where digital platforms, data, AI and services work together 
to help businesses respond faster to changing customer expectations and co ntinuously create 
measurable value.  
 
The ISG Paragon Awards recognize innovative technology and sourcing partnerships that drive 
business and IT transformation. Mastek and CORT accepted the award at the ISG Paragon 
Awards North America 2026 ceremony on September 24, 2026.  
 
“Great partnerships are built on trust, strong leadership and a shared commitment to business 
outcomes. Our decade -long relationship with CORT reflects the strength of that collaboration 
and what we can achieve together. As we look ahead, we are excited to  build on this foundation 
through AI -led transformation, intelligent automation and new ways of delivering technology

“For us, the true measure of digital transformation is the business outcome it creates. Our
