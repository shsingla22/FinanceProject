DIGITAL WATER HEATERWIRE & CABLES
The Company is also introducing Cubis, its first digital water 
heater. It is equipped with a smart LED display, precise temperature 
control, and a polymer-coated tank for enhanced durability. Cubis 
incorporates multiple safety features including thermal cutout, 
safety valve, and shock-proof plastic body. The product carries a 
5-star energy rating, an 8-bar pressure rating suitable for high-rise 
buildings, and is backed by a 7-year warranty.
WATER TANK
The Company has also introduced its latest innovation in water 
storage — the Prakash Surya Water Storage Tank, built tough and 
designed to last longer. Crafted from 100% food-grade 
polyethylene, this water tank ensures safe, hygienic, and long-lasting 
water storage for household, commercial, and industrial needs. It 
offers 100% UV protection, weather resistance, heavy-duty 
construction, dust and rust-free performance, and multi-layer 
protection.
Featuring a leak-proof design and odor-free water storage, the tank 
is designed to withstand extreme weather and prevent algae 
growth, with a UV-stabilized outer layer for sun protection. 
BPA-free and hygienic, it promises safe drinking water. Backed by a 
10-year warranty, Prakash Surya Water Tanks combine advanced 
technology with premium-grade materials for superior 
performance and reliability.
