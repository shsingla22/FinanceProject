Chennai, October 31 , 202 5: The Board of Directors of Equitas Small Finance Bank Limited at

Gross Ad vances grew by 9 % YoY and 4% QoQ  
Secured Small Business loan  (SBL) portfolio  crossed Rs. 17,000 C rs, registered a 
growth of 17 % YoY  
Deposits grew by 11 % YoY, Growth in Retail TD of 11 % YoY  
Disbursements grew by 11% YoY & 53% QoQ  
 
1. Key Highlights for Q2FY26: 
 
 Gross Adv ances grew by 9 % YoY and 4% QoQ.  Non Microfinance book grew 17% 
YoY, led by 17% growth in SBL  over previous year.  
 Overall deposits registered a growth of  11% YoY

 Small Business loan portfolio has crossed Rs. 17,0 00 Crs during the quarter  
 Used Car Advances crossed Rs. 210 0 Crs, registered  a growth of 43 % YoY  
 Used CV Advances  crossed Rs. 5000 Crs, registered a growth of 25 % YoY  
 The B ank has sold its NPA assets (Secured Portfolio) amounting to ~Rs.216 Crs to 
an Asset Reconstruction Company  
 CASA ratio stable at 31 % 
 Retail Term Deposits posted a growth of  11% YoY to Rs. 19,272  Crs  
   Key Ratios:
