Dear Sir / Madam,  
 
Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and 
Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we wish to inform that the Company has received an order dated 04.02.2025 from 
the Office of The CGST and Central Excise , Rourkela Commissionerate  (GST Department) 
demand ing GST of Rs. 20,30,67,136 along with applicable interest and Penalty of Rs. 
20,30,67,136 levied under section 74 read with 122(2)(b) of C entral Goods and Services 
Tax Act, 2017.  
 
The aforesaid matter pertains to period from July-2017 to December -2020, wherein the 
Company has paid  GST under the Reverse Charge Mechanism on N et Present Value(NPV)  
charges paid to the Government of India.   
 
The Company maintains that aforesaid demand in respect to the  NPV charges paid is not 
subject to GST  in terms of GST law provisions . However , the Company paid the GST along 
with the applicable interest before the issuance of the Show Cause Notice, while reserving 
its right to contest the issue further. Based on judicial precedents and legal advice, the 
Company believes the levy of Penalty by GST Department is also not sustainable , as the 
matter is revenue neutral  and subject matter of interpretation . Accordingly, the aforesaid 
demand of penalty is proposed to be challenged by the Company in appeal.  
 
We do not anticipate  any impact on the financials, operations or other activities of the 
Company.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
We request you to take this disclosure on record and treat the same as compliance under 
the applicable Regulations of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.  
 
We wish to inform you that the delay in disclosure is on account of delayed communication to the  
Compliance Team.
