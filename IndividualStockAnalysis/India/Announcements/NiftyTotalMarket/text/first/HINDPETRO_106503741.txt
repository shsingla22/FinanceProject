Stellar financial performance with 206% increase in 9M PAT  
Commissions RUF at Visakh Refinery – a major leap in advanced refining  
  
Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) today announced its financial results for the 
quarter and nine months  ending December  31, 2025 . The period  witnessed a  stellar financial 
performance driven by excellent refining and marketing performance . 
 
• 206% increase in 9M Standalone Profit After Tax ( ₹ 12,274  crore)  
• 261% increase in 9M Consolidated Profit After Tax ( ₹ 11,982  crore)  
• Resilient Refinery performance with 5.8% increase in Throughput  in 9M (19.61  MMT ) 
• Steady growth in Marketing with 3.6% increase in Sales volume in 9M (38.45  MMT ) 
• Continuous  progress on reduction of Debt Equity Ratio (Standalone  D/E at 0.89 as compared 
to 1.07 as on 3 0th September  2025 ) 
• Robust  Gross Refining Margin (GRM)  at US$ 8.85 per barrel  in Quarter 3 
• Commissioned Residue Upgradation Facility (RUF) at Visakh Refinery – Bharat’s first 
hydrogen -based residue hydrocracking unit, achieving ~93% conversion of bottom oils into 
high-value products

Strategic Investments and Infrastructure Development  
• Capex in Q3 FY26: ₹ 4,976  crore ; cumulative for 9M FY26:  ₹ 11,094  crore  – focused on 
strengthening refining and marketing infrastructure, including investments in subsidiaries and 
joint venture  companies  to build additional capacities, new business lines and improving 
operating efficien cies 
• Commissioned Residue Upgradation Facility (RUF) at Visakh Refinery, featuring the 
world’s first and largest LC -Max unit  
o With a capacity of 3.55 MMTPA, this LC -Max–based RUF is among the world’s 
most advanced facilities, enabling ~93% conversion of bottom -of-the-barrel residues 
into high -value distillates  
o The unit enhances the refinery’s ability to process heavier and oppor tunity crudes 
while improving the Nelson Complexity Index of the refinery to 11.6  
o This state -of-the-art facility maximises recovery of high -value products from crude 
oil with minimal residue generation  
• HPCL Rajasthan Refinery Limited  (HRRL)
