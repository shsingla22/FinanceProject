Mumbai | August 15th, 2025  
 
We wish to inform you that Mr. Venkat Ramaswamy, will step off from his executive responsibilities at 
EAAA India Alternatives Limited (EAAA) with effect from September 3 0th, 2025 , due to personal reasons.  
Venkat will continue to serve on the Board of Edelweiss Financial Services Limited (EFSL) and maintain 
his close association with EFSL as a promoter, supporting the next phase of Edelweiss’s journey.  
To ensure continuity, further strengthen governance and institutionalisation, Mr. Rashesh Shah, current 
Chairman of EAAA board, will assume the role of Executive Chair providing strategic leadership to the 
business as it prepares to list around April 2026.  
As shared earlier, over the last two months, Mr. Amit Agarwal and Mr. Subahoo Chordia, — both with 
nearly two decades of experience in the firm have taken over as Chief Executive Officers of EAAA, 
ensuring continuity in operations and a smooth leadership t ransition.  
EAAA  is a leading alternatives investment platform in India, managing INR 62, 970 crore in AUM as of 
quarter ended June 2025.  Offering innovative strategies across private credit, real assets, and other 
non-traditional asset classes, EAAA combines a strong track record, disciplined processes, and a 
commitment to responsible investing to build long -term value for investors and stakeholders  
 
Rashesh Shah, Chairman and Managing Director, Edelweiss Financial Services, shared:  "Venkat has 
been a key part of the Edelweis s story  since its inception and has played a defining role in our journey 
— from shaping the Group’s strategy to building the asset management businesses, particularly the 
Alternatives platform, into one of India’s leading franchises. Over nearly three decades, h is market 
insights and partnership have been invaluable. While he steps back from his executive responsibilities, 
I am sure he will continue to add value through his role on the EFSL Board.  
With Amit and Subahoo as CEOs of EAAA, I am confident the business will continue its strong 
momentum as we prepare for the IPO in April 2026."  
 
Venkat Ramaswamy, CEO & MD EAAA also shared:  "Edelweiss has been my professional home for 
nearly three decades, and it has been an incredible journey building this institution alongside Rashesh 
and so many talented colleagues. Together, we have grown the asset management businesses — 
especially the Alternatives platform — into a respected and trusted franchise. As I move on from my 
executive role, I do so with pride in what we have achieved and great confidence in the leadership now 
in place. I look forward to continuing my association with Edelweiss on the EFSL Board and to supporting 
its next phase of growth."  
 
EFSL remains committed to EAAA’s scale -up journey — providing strategic guidance and institutional 
strength — as the platform advances towards its planned IPO in April 2026.

About Edelweiss Financial Services  
Edelweiss is a diversified financial services company with seven independent and well -governed businesses. The businesses 
include  Alternative Asset Management , Mutual Fund, Asset Reconstruction, NBFC , Housing  Finance, General Insurance and 
Life Insurance. The businesses have robust operating platforms, dedicated management teams and strong boards that 
ensure the highest standards of governance. Edelweiss employs nearly  6,000 people, serves around 1.1 Cr customers, and 
manages  around  INR 2,30,000 Cr worth of assets.
