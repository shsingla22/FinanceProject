Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“Listing Regulations ”), we wish to inform you that Dalmia Cement (Bharat) Limited (‘ DCBL’), 
a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, has entered into Share Subscription and Shareholders’ 
Agreement (‘ SSSHA’) and a Power Purchase Agreement ( ‘PPA’) on March 20, 2026 (at around 02:25 
pm), to acquire 26% stake in Ventora Energy Private Limited, an SPV of Ultra Mega Power Private 
Limited, in one or more tranches, at an aggregate consideration of Rs. 4.42 Cr (approx), to source 
wind power as a captive consumer for a capacity up to 8.1 MW located in the State of Tamil Nadu. 
 
Above acquisition is subject to customary conditions precedent and is expected to be completed 
within three months.
