Press Release 
Signature Global Q2FY27 Revenue Rises 148% to INR 8.2 Billion; 
Reports Net Profit of INR 0.7 Billion 
 
 Pre-sales stood at INR 18.3 billion and collections at INR 10.1 billion in Q2FY27. 
 Average sales realization jumped to INR 17,122 per sq. ft. in Q2FY27, compared with INR 
15,250 per sq. ft. in FY26.  
 Added 194.22 acres of land in Farrukhnagar, Gurugram, through acquisitions and 
collaborations, with development potential of approximately 6.77 million sq. ft. 
New Delhi, November 05, 2026:  Signature Global (BSE: 543990 | NSE: SIGNATURE), one of India’s 
leading real estate developers, today announced a 148% year-on-year (YoY) jump in revenue to INR 
8.2 billion  in Q2FY27, from INR 3.3 billion in the same quarter last fiscal year. The Company reported 
a profit after tax (PAT) of INR 0.7 billion in Q2FY27, a significant turnaround from a loss after tax of 
INR 0.5 billion in Q2FY26. 
Pre-sales stood at INR 18.3 billion  in Q2FY27, while collections grew 9% YoY to INR 10.1 billion from 
INR 9.3 billion in Q2FY26. The performance reflects steady demand and strong execution. The 
Company remains focused on sustaining momentum through strategic launches, disciplined delivery, 
and a diversified portfolio. 
Company’s average sales realization rose 12.3%  to INR 17,122 per sq. ft.  in Q2FY27, compared with 
INR 15,250 per sq. ft. in FY26, driven by the successful launch of the premium Tonino Lamborghini 
Residences on SPR, Sector 71, Gurugram. 
The Company’s adjusted gross profit margin expanded to 36% in Q2FY27 from 35% in Q2FY26, 
reflecting continued strength in project execution. 
Operating profitability improved sharply, with adjusted EBITDA margin rising to 18% in Q2FY27 from 
negative 7% in Q2FY26, driven by higher revenue recognition, margin expansion, and operating 
efficiencies. 
At the end of H1FY27, the Company reported net debt of INR 4.5 bn , against INR 2.0 bn at the end of 
FY26, while continuing to maintain a strong liquidity position with cash and bank balances (including 
fixed deposits) of INR 24.2 bn at the end of  H1FY27. 
During H1FY27, the Company added 6.77 million sq ft  of development potential in Farrukhnagar, 
Gurugram, Haryana, marking its entry into a new micro-market. It also acquired 0.38 million sq ft  in 
Sector 37D,  Dwarka Expressway (previously under a collaboration arrangement), 0.10 million sq ft  in 
Sector 71  on the SPR corridor, and 2.70 million sq ft  in Sohna. These additions further strengthen the 
project pipeline and support long-term growth.

Commenting on the company's performance, Mr. Pradeep Kumar Aggarwal, Chairman and Whole-
Time Director,  said, “Our performance during the first half of the current fiscal reflects the strength of 
our business model and the trust homebuyers continue to place in the Signature Global brand. Demand 
for quality housing in Gurugram remains healthy, and our well-planned launches, including the 
premium Tonino Lamborghini Residences, have been received very positively by the market. This has 
helped us improve our realizations and strengthen our brand positioning in the premium segment. We 
have also continued to build our land bank in Farrukhnagar, which gives us a strong pipeline for future 
launches. Our balance sheet remains robust, with a comfortable liquidity position that allows us to 
pursue growth opportunities with confidence. Looking ahead, we are optimistic about the road ahead 
and remain committed to delivering quality homes on time while creating long-term value for all our 
stakeholders.” 
About Signature Global (India) Ltd:   
Signature Global, India’s leading real estate development company, is reshaping the housing market in northern 
India. While it initially made its mark in the affordable housing segment, the company now focuses on the mid 
and premium housing categories. This strategic evolution is driven by a strong emphasis on quality execution, 
value creation, reliability, and adherence to global standards. Backed by marquee institutional investors, 
Signature Global upholds high standards of corporate governance. The company follows a disciplined and 
efficient land acquisition strategy, typically launching projects within 18 months of acquisition—enabling faster 
go-to-market timelines and enhanced project viability. Signature Global holds a market share of 13% in the 
National Capital Region (NCR) and 20% share in Gurugram within the price range of INR 20 million to INR 50 
million. This reflects the company’s dominant position in its target micro markets.  
As of Q2FY27, the company has successfully delivered 19.9 million sq. ft. of real estate. Its project pipeline 
remains robust, comprising 23.2 million sq. ft. of recently launched projects, 27.3 million sq. ft. of forthcoming 
developments, and 9.2 million sq. ft. of ongoing construction (5.1 million sq. ft. under construction and 4.1 
million sq. ft. with OC received), all slated for execution over the next 2-3 years.
