Press Release  
 
Route Mobile Hosts the Belgian Economic Mission led by HRH Princess Astrid, Strengthening 
Indo -Belgium Business Ties Through Strategic Collaboration  
 
Belgian Economic Mission led by HRH Princess Astrid visited the Route Mobile Headquarters to announce 
the launch of the Belgian Economic Forum and improve business ties between the two countries.  
 
 
05 March  2025, Mumbai, India  – Route Mobile Limited (“Route Mobile”), a leading cloud 
communication platform service provider to enterprises, over -the-top (“OTT”) players, and mobile 
network operators, had the honour of hosting the Belgian Economic Mission —led by Her Royal 
Highness Princess Astrid at its Mumbai headquarters.  
 
The event was also graced by HE Maxime Prevot, Federal Government; HE Matthias Diependaele, 
Government of Flanders; Frank Geerkens, Consul General of Belgium in India; David Jordens, Spokesperson, Belgian Ministry of Foreign Affairs; Stefaan De Clerck, Chairman, Proximus Board of 
Directors; Mr Ben A ppel , Chief Corporate Affairs Officer, Proximus and Gautam Badalia, CEO, Route 
Mobile. The visit was also participated by 50+ business dignitaries from Belgium.  
 
This visit offered them a comprehensive view of Route Mobile’s innovative Communications Platform 
as a Service (CPaaS) solutions, showcasing its robust capabilities and advanced approach to enhancing 
communication solutions. Following the tour, attendees engaged in a series of presentations from 
various high -tech startups, stimulating discussions on strategies to strengthen bilateral trade ties 
between India and Belgium.  
 
The successful visit reinforced the company’s commitment to sustained collaboration and innovation in 
digital communications, CPaaS, and DI ecosystem, aiming to enhance growth in cross -border business 
relations.  
Speaking on the occasion, Gautam Badalia, CEO, Route Mobile  said, “We are extremely honoured to host 
the Belgian Economic Mission and play a part in India’s goal to build stronger bilateral ties with the 
country. Our vision is to build global CPaaS and digital identity products and I am confident that our 
synergies with Proximus Group will unlock greater value for enterprises worldwide.”  
 Indo -Belgian Innovation  
Five innovative s tart- ups from India and Belgium united to showcase their ground -breaking 
technologies to Her Royal Highness, Princess Astrid of Belgium, along with the delegation. Their 
presentations spanned various sectors, featuring advancements in spatial computing, ar tificial 
intelligence for e -commerce, and multilingual content generation. Among the highlights were drone 
delivery systems, space technology for defence applications, and decentralized digital twin tools for 
retail, all exemplifying the forefront of innov ation.  
 The economic mission offers unparalleled access to India’s booming infrastructure and smart 
technology sectors.  India being one of the fastest -growing markets in the world, is a land of 
opportunities for companies looking to form new partnerships and expand their international footprint. 
As part of Proximus Group, Route Mobile represents the strengthening of economic partnerships 
between India and Belgium, unlocking value for enhanced digital communication, technology 
innovation and business collaboration.

About Route Mobile Limited ( www.routemobile.com ) BSE: 543228; NSE: ROUTE) 
Established in 2004, Route Mobile Limited (“RML”) is a cloud communications platform service provider 
catering to enterprises, over -the-top (OTT) players, and mobile network operators (MNO). RML’s 
portfolio comprises solutions in messaging, voice, email, SMS filtering, analytics, and monetization. RML has a diverse enterprise client base across various industries, inc luding social media companies, banks 
and financial institutions, e -commerce entities, and travel aggregators. RML is headquartered in 
Mumbai, India, with a global presence in Asia Pacific, the Middle East, Africa, Europe, and the Americas. 
Route Mobile is now a part of Proximus Group, a provider of digital services and communication 
solutions operating in Belgium and international markets.
