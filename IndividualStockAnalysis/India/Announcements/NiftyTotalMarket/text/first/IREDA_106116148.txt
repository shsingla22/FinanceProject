This is in reference to our earlier letter dated 16.07.2024 , wherein the Company has informed that

Urban Infra Limited , GMR Lion Energy Limited  (Mauritius) , SJVN Limited , Nepal Electricity 
Authority , an Undertaking of Govt of Nepal  and GMR Upper Karnali Hydropower Limited , Nepal . 
 
It may be further noted that  DIPAM  vide OM dated 16.01.2025 has accorded approval, on the 
recommendation of Administrative Ministry i.e., Ministry of New and Renewable Energy 
(“MNRE”) dated 15.01.2025 for 5% equity investment by IREDA each in M/s. GMR Upper Karnali 
Hydro Power Limited, Nepal, and M/s. Karnali Transmission Company Pvt. Ltd., Nepal.
