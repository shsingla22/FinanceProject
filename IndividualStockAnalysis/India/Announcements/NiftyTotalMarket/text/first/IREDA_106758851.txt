Dear Sir/Madam,  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), this is to inform that the Company  has received notices dated August 
25, 2026 from National Stock Exchange of India Limited (“NSE”) and BSE Limited (“BSE”) regarding non-
compliance with certain provisions of the Listing Regulations pertaining to the composition of Board of Directors 
and Committees thereof and have imposed a fine of ₹14 ,19,540/- (inclusive of GST) each by NSE & BSE for such 
non-compliance.  
 
In this regard, it is submitted that IREDA, being a Government Company and as per its Articles of Association, the power to appoint Directors on the Board of the Company is vested with the President of India acting through its administrative Ministry, i.e. , Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (“MNRE”), Government of India, and 
the Company has no role in the appointment of Directors.  
 
The matter of imposition of fines by the Stock Exchanges and non- compliance with the Listing Regulations is 
being placed before the Board of Directors . Further, the Company has been consistently pursuing the matter with 
MNRE for appointment of the requisite number of Independent Directors on the Board of the Company to ensure compliance with the above -said Regulations.  
 
 
Thanking You,
