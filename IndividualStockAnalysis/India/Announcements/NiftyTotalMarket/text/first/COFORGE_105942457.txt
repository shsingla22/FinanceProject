stage.

intends to refuse the Client’s demands regarding the arbitration claim resolutions on the basis that, in the Company’s 
view, such amounts ar e not reasonably subject to indemnification.

and there is no tribunal, court, or agency where any litigation or arbitration proceeding has been filed against the 
Company.
