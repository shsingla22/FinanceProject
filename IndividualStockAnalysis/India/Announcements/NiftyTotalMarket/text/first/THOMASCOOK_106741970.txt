Sterling Deepens Wildlife  Leadership with the Launch of      
Sterling Quinta Jim Corbett  
New resort strengthens Sterling’s destination -cluster strategy in Corbett while 
expanding its curated wildlife portfolio to 13 leading jungle destinations  
13 Aug 2026, Chennai:  Sterling Holiday Resorts has strengthened its leadership in India's 
fast-growing wildlife hospitality segment with the launch of Sterling Quinta Jim Corbett, its 
second resort in the Corbett landscape and 11th property in Uttarakhand. The addition 
expands Sterling's curated wildlife portfolio to 13 resorts with over 480 rooms and suites, 
reinforcing the company's long -term strategy of building destination clusters across India's 
most sought -after nature and wild life regions.  
 
As travellers increasingly seek immersive, experience -led holidays over conventional 
sightseeing, wildlife destinations are evolving into year -round leisure markets. Sterling's 
growing presence across India's iconic forests reflects this shift, offering gu ests deeper 
engagement with nature through thoughtfully curated stays and destination experiences.  
 
Located in the tranquil Bail Parao –Kaladhungi belt of Ramnagar, Sterling Quinta Jim Corbett 
offers guests convenient access to the wider Corbett landscape while providing a quieter 
and more spacious alternative to the busier tourism zones. The resort compl ements 
Sterling's existing presence in Corbett, allowing travellers greater flexibility to explore 
different parts of the destination while enjoying the brand's signature hospitality.  
 
With this launch, Sterling now operates one of India's most diversified wildlife resort 
portfolios spanning tiger reserves, lion habitats, rainforest ecosystems and Himalayan 
forests across destinations including Corbett, Gir, Kanha, Pench, Ranthambore, Sa riska, 
Thekkady, Wayanad, Anaikatti and Tipe shwar.  
 
Designed for families, couples, celebration -led travel and corporate retreats, Sterling Quinta 
Jim Corbett features 46 spacious rooms across five categories, including rooms with private 
sit-outs, balconies and exclusive plunge pools. Guests can also enjoy  a swimming pool, spa, 
Nectar —the resort's all -day dining restaurant —and a range of curated outdoor experiences.  
 
The resort also strengthens Sterling's growing leisure -plus -events proposition. A 2,800 sq. 
ft. banquet hall complemented by two landscaped lawns makes it an attractive venue for 
destination weddings, social celebrations, corporate offsites and incentive t ravel.   
Beyond the resort, guests can explore the wider Corbett region through safaris across 
multiple forest zones including Phato and Sitabani, birdwatching trails, plantation walks  
 
 
and local cultural attractions such as Chhoi Village, Shri Hanuman Dham, the Jim Corbett 
Museum and Corbett Falls.  
According to Mr. Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director & CEO , Sterling Holiday Resorts 
Ltd, “Wildlife tourism in India is undergoing a significant transformation. Travellers today 
are looking for experiences that extend well beyond the safari —they want longer stays, 
meaningful engagement with nature and authentic connections with local landscapes  and 
communities. Our strategy has been to build destination clusters across India’s most iconic 
wildlife regions, enabling guests to experience these destinations in richer and more diverse 
ways while enjoying the consistency of Sterling’s hospitality. St erling Quinta Jim Corbett is 
another important milestone in strengthening that vision.”  
Commenting on the association, Mr. Shailendra Goswami and Mr. Amit Aggarwal 
partners at Shreena Hospitalities Pvt Limited , shared that  “Sterling Quinta Jim Corbett 
was envisioned as a peaceful, nature -connected retreat that complements the wider Corbett 
experience. Partnering with Sterling enables us to bring together the resort’s distinctive 
location and design with the scale, trust and hospitality expertise of the Sterling brand. With 
the blessings of Banke Bihari ji, we look forward to a lasting association and to establishing 
the resort as a preferred destination for families, wildlife travellers, celebrations and 
corporate retreats.”  
 
With the addition of Sterling Quinta Jim Corbett, Sterling further strengthens its presence 
across Uttarakhand while reinforcing its long -term strategy of building leadership across 
India's high -potential experiential travel segments including wildlife, mo untains, wellness, 
pilgrimage and heritage destinations.
