Press Release 
SML Mahindra to raise upto Rs. 1450 crore through Rights Issue 
Mumbai, November 16, 2026: The Board of Directors of SML Mahindra Limited (SML Mahindra) on 
November 6, 2026, has approved raising of funds through the issuance of fully paid-up equity shares of 
the Company of the face value of Rs. 10/- each by way of a rights issue ("Rights Issue") for an amount 
not exceeding Rs. 1,450 Crore (Rupees One Thousand Four Hundred and Fifty Crore) to the eligible 
equity shareholders of the Company, subject to receipt of regulatory and statutory approvals. 
Further, pursuant to the in-principle approval received from BSE Limited (“BSE”) and National Stock 
Exchange of India Limited (“NSE”), the Board of Directors has today fixed a rights issue price of Rs. 
3,506 (Rupees Three Thousand Five Hundred and Six Only) per fully paid-up Rights Equity Share, 
leading to an entitlement of 2 (Two) fully paid-up Rights Equity Shares for every 7 (Seven)  fully paid-up 
Equity Shares held by the eligible Equity Shareholders of the Company as on the Record Date i.e. 
Friday, 20th November, 2026 .   
The proceeds of the Rights Issue will primarily be utilised towards financing the acquisition of Mahindra 
Truck and Bus Division (MTBD) and supporting strategic growth initiatives aimed at building an 
integrated and future-ready commercial vehicle platform. 
The acquisition of MTBD, announced in July 2026, is a key step in creating a scaled, unified and focused 
commercial vehicle platform. The integration of SML Mahindra and MTBD is expected to enhance 
product portfolio breadth, manufacturing capabilities, market reach, operating efficiencies and long-term 
competitiveness. 
In addition, the Company will invest in capacity enhancement, manufacturing modernization, research 
and development on new product & aggregates, enhancement of existing products, product innovation 
and technology advancement, alternative fuel mobility solutions, connected vehicle technologies and 
engineering capability enhancement to accelerate the development of next-generation commercial 
vehicles and meet evolving customer expectations as well as applicable regulatory requirements. 
Mahindra & Mahindra Limited, the promoter of the Company, has also confirmed to participate in the 
Rights Issue in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, demonstrating its continued 
commitment to SML Mahindra's growth strategy. 
Mr. Vinod Sahay, Executive Chairman of SML Mahindra , said, "The proposed Rights Issue reflects 
our commitment to pursuing strategic growth opportunities while maintaining a prudent capital structure. 
The funds raised will strengthen our manufacturing capabilities, accelerate innovation, and provide 
additional financial flexibility to execute our long-term growth strategy. We remain focused on creating 
sustainable value for all our stakeholders.”
