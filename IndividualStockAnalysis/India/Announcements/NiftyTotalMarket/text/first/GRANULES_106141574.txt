Sub: Update on CDP  Rating for Granules India Limited  
 
Dear Sir,  
 
We are pleased to inform you that Granules India Limited has received its official 2024 CDP (Carbon 
Disclosure Project) Climate Rating.  
 
Granules India has achieved a “B” rating,  reflecting a two -level improvement from the “C” rating received in 
2023. This underscores the company’s continued commitment to Sustainability and Climate responsibility.  
 
CDP’s rating scale includes four categories: Disclosure (D -/D), indicating the initial stage of environmental 
engagement; Awareness (C -/C), reflecting a level of environmental engagement; Management (B -/B), 
signifying effective management of environmental impacts; and Leadership (A -/A), representing industry 
leadership in environmental sustainability.  
 
About CDP:  
The Carbon Disclosure Project (CDP)  is a global non -profit that promotes transparency in environmental 
reporting, helping companies and organizations disclose their impacts on climate change, water security, and 
deforestation. By providing valuable insights through CDP ratings, it enables investors, companies, and 
stakeholders to make informed decisions and take action towards a more sustainable future.  
 
This is for your information and dissemination to the members of the exchange.
