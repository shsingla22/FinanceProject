Revolt Motors Hono ured as 'Franchisor of the Year – Electric 
Vehicle ’ at 21st National Franchise Excellence Awards  
Recognized for pioneering EV retail excellence and rapid dealership expansion across India  
New Delhi, 19 May 2025 : Revolt Motors, India’s No.1 electric motorcycle  brand, has been 
awarded the prestigious 'Franchisor of the Year – Electric Vehicle' at the 21st National 
Franchise Excellence Awards , held at Yashobhoomi (IICC), Delhi. This accolade celebrates 
Revolt's visionary approach to EV retail and its innovative franchise model that has driven 
rapid, sustainable expansion across the country.  
Organized by Franchise India, the National Franchise Excellence Awards recognize companies 
that exemplify excellence in scalability, profitability, and franchise partner success. Revolt 
Motors stood out in the electric vehicle category for building a high -growth, future -ready 
dealership ecosystem while accelerating India’s transition toward clean mobility.  
Over the past year, Revolt Motors has grown its footprint to over 200 dealership locations 
across 23 states and Union Territories. The company now aims to double its network to 400 
dealerships by FY26, making electric mobility more accessible than ever for  Indian riders.  
“This award is a testament to our relentless efforts in transforming India’s mobility landscape,” 
said Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises . “Our dealer partners have been 
the backbone of this revolution, and we remain committed to enabling their success as we 
scale up further —both nationally and internationally.”  
Revolt’s franchise model provides robust partner support through training, marketing, 
logistics, and after -sales services. This collaborative framework ensures a seamless customer 
experience and has become a benchmark in the evolving EV dealership ecosyste m. 
As the electric mobility sector experiences unprecedented growth, Revolt Motors is actively 
exploring international market expansion, starting with neighbo uring countries. This award 
further positions the company as a frontrunner in shaping the global EV retail model.
