 Sub:  Intimation of execution of lease agreement with APCRDA for development of a 
children’s hospital at Amravati, Andhra Pradesh. 
 Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 We would like to inform you that Rainbow Children’s Medicare Limited (“the Company ”) has 
entered into a  lease agreement with the Andhra Pradesh Capital Region Development Authority 
(APCRDA ), Government of Andhra Pradesh, for approximately 2 acres of land in  Amaravati, the 
capital city of Andhra Pradesh, for a tenure  of 60 years . 
 The leased land is proposed to be utilized for the development of a 250 -bed super speciality 
children’s hospital and perinatal care facility, which is intended to be developed in a phased manner.  
 The Company will keep the Stock Exchanges informed about the progress of the project.  
 The above information will also be available on the website of the Company at 
https://www.rainbowhospitals.in/investors -relations/stock -exchange- disclosures .
