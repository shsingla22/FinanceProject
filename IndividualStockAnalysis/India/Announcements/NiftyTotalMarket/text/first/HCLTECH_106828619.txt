HCLTech scales Climate Action Grant to 24 countries, strengthening global action 
against climate change  
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, Oct. 26, 2026  – HCL Tech, a leading global technology company, 
today announced the expansion of its flagship Climate Action Grant program to 24 countries 
worldwide to support a wider pool of non -profit organizations (NPOs) combating climate 
change and restoring ecosyste ms and biodiversity.  
In 2023, the company launched the HCLTech Grant  for Climate Action  in the Americas to 
support locally relevant solutions that address pressing climate and environmental issues. 
HCLTech initially committed $5 million over five years to eligible NPOs in 10 countries in the 
Americas and has disbursed $3 million over the past three editions.  
Encouraged by the success of the program in the Americas, HCL Tech is expanding the 
program to 24 countries globally. These countries include Australia, Brazil, Bulgaria, Canada, 
France, Germany, Guatemala, Italy, Japan, Malaysia, Mexico, Morocco, the Nethe rlands, the 
Philippines, Poland, Romania, Singapore, South Africa, Spain, Sri Lanka, Sweden, the United 
Kingdom, the United States and Vietnam.  
The winners will be announced on June 5, 2027, following a rigorous screening process. One 
winner will receive $500,000, while three runners -up will each receive $250,000.  
“Great ideas can come from any community, and solutions developed in one place can create 
possibilities in another. The HCLTech Global Grant for Climate Action has evolved through our 
experience in India and the Americas, and taking the Grant global is the next step in that 
journey. By creating a broader platform for climate innovation, we want to support locally 
rooted ideas and help solutions with potential for wider impact reach further,” said Dr. Nidhi 
Pundhir, Global Head of CSR at HCLTech.  
HCLTech has a sharp focus on sustainability and is committed to achieving net -zero by 2040. 
The company has been named to TIME World's Most Sustainable Companies list for the 
second consecutive year. It also achieved EcoVadis Gold rating and MSCI AA (Leade r) ESG 
rating for the second consecutive year in FY26.  
HCLTech’s corporate social responsibility initiatives, delivered through HCLFoundation in India, have impacted over 8. 8 million lives, 54% of whom are women. It has planted over 3. 6 
million saplings, greened 74,000 acres of land and harvested more than 155 billion liters of 
water to date.  
To apply for the HCLTech Global Grant for Climate Action, visit globalgrant.hcltech.com/
