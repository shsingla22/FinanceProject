Shyam Metalics onboards Mr. Chandra Shekhar Verma as Independent Director 
Kolkata, 04th July 2024: Shyam Metalics, a rapidly expanding leader in integrated metal 
production, is pleased to announce the appointment of Mr. Chandra Shekhar Verma as an 
Independent Director of Shyam Metalics and Energy Limited w.e.f. 4‘ July, 2024. He is a 
reputed professional and has profound knowledge and experience of more than four 
decades in the Public Sector undertakings, particularly in the steel sector. 
His professional journey certifies him as a true techno-commercial professional and a 
distinguished leader who has held significant roles in numerous prestigious organizations of 
the country. 
Mr. Chandra Shekhar Verma will join the strategic advisory board and participate in all key 
decision-making processes related to the company's growth and development. 
Commenting on his appointment, Mr. Chandra Shekhar Verma, Independent Director of 
Shyam Metalics and Energy Limited stated, "| am honored to join the esteemed board of 
Shyam Metalics. With their impressive track record as a market leader, | am eager to 
contribute to the critical decision-making processes that drive the company's business 
development. | look forward to playing a role in the company's continued growth." 
On this recent development of the leadership team, Mr. B. Bhushan, Vice-Chairman & 
Managing Director, Shyam Metalics and Energy Limited comments “We are proud to 
welcome Mr. Chandra Shekhar Verma to our team. At Shyam Metalics, we consistently 
strive for excellence, making strong leadership essential to guiding our growth journey. lam 
confident that Mr. Verma’s experience, leadership, and guidance will contribute 
significantly to the company's continued success.” 
Prior to joining Shyam Metalics, Mr. Verma served as the Chairman & Managing Director of 
Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL) from June 2010 to June 2015. Under his leadership, 
SAIL achieved significant milestones including substantial capacity expansion, strategic 
partnerships, and international recognition. He also held additional charges such as CMD 
of National Mineral Development Corporation (NMDC) and Chairman of International Coal 
Ventures Limited (ICVL). 
Throughout his career, Mr. Verma has been recognized for his exceptional contributions to 
the industry, receiving prestigious awards such as the 'CEO of the Year' Platts Global Metals 
Awards and the IIM-JRD Tata Award for Excellence in Corporate Leadership in Metallurgical 
Industries.

Mr. Verma was the First President of the Indian Steel Association, the apex body of the Indian 
Steel Industry. He was the Chairman of the ‘National Committee of Steel’ of Confederation 
of Indian Industries (Cll). He was member of the Council of Scientific and Industrial 
Research Society (CSIR) headed by the Hon’ble Prime Minister of India. He was also Board 
Member, World steel Association and Council Member of Indian Institute of Metals. He was 
the Member of the Board of Governors and Society of Indian Institute of Management (IIM), 
Raipur. 
Mr. Verma holds a Master of Commerce (M. Com), Master of Business Administration (MBA), 
Fellow member of the Institute of Cost Accountants of India (FICMA), Fellow member of the 
Institute of Company Secretaries of India (FCS), and Bachelor of Law & Legislatures (LLB). 
Mr. Verma's appointment is anticipated to bolster Shyam Metalics' market position and 
propel the company's strategic development into its next phase of growth. 
About Shyam Metalics Limited 
Shyam Metalics is a leading and fastest-growing integrated metal-producing company 
based in India primarily in the steel Industry in West Bengal and Odisha with a focus on Long 
Steel Products and Ferro Alloys. The company got listed itself on the exchanges in 2021 and 
as on date of this press release possesses a market capitalization of more than Rs. 20000 
Cr. Spearheaded by Mr. B. Bhushan, Vice Chairman & Managing Director, the company 
strives to deliver unparalleled quality through their customized value-added solutions to 
meet business requirements. Headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal, the company is 
amongst the largest producers of ferro alloys in terms of installed capacity in India (Source: 
CRISIL Report). The company has the ability to sell intermediate and final products across 
the steel value chain. Shyam Metalics is one of the leading players in terms of pellet capacity 
and the largest coal fired player in the sponge iron industry in terms of sponge iron capacity 
in India.
