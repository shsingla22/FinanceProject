14th November, 2026 Vadodara, India 
 
Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited receives USFDA Final Approval for Binimetinib 
Tablets, 15 mg and 45 mg. 
 
Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited (Alembic) today announced that it has received final 
approval from the US Food & Drug Administration (USFDA) for its Abbreviated New Drug 
Application (ANDA) for Binimetinib Tablets, 15 mg and 45 mg. The approved ANDA is 
therapeutically equivalent to the reference listed drug product (RLD), Mektovi Tablets, 15 mg, 
of Array BioPharma Inc. (Array). Binimetinib is a kinase inhibitor indicated, in combination 
with encorafenib, for the treatment of patients with unresectable or metastatic melanoma with 
a BRAF V600E or V600K mutation. It is also indicated, in combination with encorafenib, for 
the treatment of adult patients with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) with a 
BRAF V600E mutation. Refer label for a detailed indication. 
  
Alembic was one of the first ANDA applicants for 15 mg and sole first applicant for 45 mg to 
submit substantially complete ANDA with a paragraph IV certification for Binimetinib Tablets. 
Therefore, with this approval, Alembic is eligible for 180 days of generic drug exclusivity for 
Binimetinib Tablets, 15 mg and 45 mg. 
  
Binimetinib tablets have an estimated market size of US$ 282 million for twelve months 
ending September 2026 according to IQVIA. 
  
Alembic has a cumulative total of 248 ANDA approvals (230 final approvals and 18 tentative 
approvals) from the USFDA.
