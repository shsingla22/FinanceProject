Granules India’s Gagillapur facility completes USFDA inspection with eleven observations  
Hyderabad, India, October 30, 2026  – Granules India Limited today announced that the United States Food 
and Drug Administration (USFDA) has concluded a current Good Manufacturing Practice inspection of its 
finished dosage facility at Gagillapur, Hyderabad. The inspection was conducted from  October 19 to October 
30, 2026, and concluded with the issuance of a Form FDA 483 containing eleven observations.  
This was the first USFDA inspection of the facility since the Warning Letter of February 26, 2025, which 
followed an inspection in August - September 2024.  
None of the observations were related  to data integrity or cross -contamination.  The observations relate to 
enhancements in investigations, change control, process and equipment qualification, visual -inspection 
controls and documentation practices. There is no impact on manufacturing or distribution of products from 
the site . Granules will submit a comprehensive response to the USFDA within the stipulated 15 business days.  
Commenting on the inspection, Dr. Krishna Prasad Chigurupati, Chairman and Managing Director, said: “The 
inspection progressed constructively, and the observations are of a nature that we believe can be fully 
addressed through our responses and corrective and preventive actions. Quality is the foundation of 
everything we do at Granules, and we will continue to engage transparently with the agency.”
