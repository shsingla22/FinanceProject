Sir/Madam,  
 
This is to inform that GAIL Board in its meeting held today i.e. 30.10.2026 accorded                    
in-principle approval for Listing of shares of GAIL Gas Limited, (a wholly owned subsidiary 
of GAIL) (GGL) on the Indian stock exchanges through Initial Public Offering (IPO) route by 
diluting GAIL’s equity in GGL upto 25%.  
 
After GGL submits the final proposal based on BRLM recommendations on valuation, price 
band, modality and other terms & conditions, approval of GAIL Board for the final extent of 
dilution, modality & other terms & condition of IPO will be obtained. The same will be 
informed to Stock Exchanges accordingly.  
 
It was approved by the Board of Directors of the Company in its meeting held today i.e. 
30.10.2026 which commenced at 11.00 a.m. and concluded at 02:25 p.m.   
 
This disclosure is in compliance of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)
