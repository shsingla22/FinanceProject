Press  Release  
Roadshow  
NALCO  showcases  investment  Opportunities  at Angul  
Aluminium  Park  at Investor  Roadshow  in Kolkata  
 
Aluminium  remains  pivotal  to our journey  towards  Atmanirbhar  Bharat;  enhancing  
production,  driving  value  addition,  and strengthening  the downstream  ecosystem  are key 
priorities:  Shri G. Kishan  Reddy,  Hon’ble  Union  Minister  of Coal  & Mines  
 
   
  
 
Kolkata /Bhubaneswar -28/09/2026:  National  Aluminium  Company  Limited  (NALCO),  the 
Navratna  CPSE  under  the Ministry  of Mines,  Government  of India,  in association  with Odisha  
Industrial  Infrastructure  Development  Corporation  (IDCO),  organised  a Roadshow  on Angul  
Aluminium  Park in Kolkata,  today  to showcase  investment  opportunities  in downstream  and value -
added  aluminium  manufacturing.  

 
 
 
 
Shri G. Kishan  Reddy,  Hon’ble  Union  Minister  of Coal  & Mines,  Government  of India  graced  the 
inaugural  session  as the chief  guest.   Shri Keshav  Chandra,  IAS, Secretary,  Ministry  of Mines,  
Govt  of India,  Shri Brijendra  Pratap  Singh,  CMD,  NALCO,  Shri D. Prasanth  Kumar  Reddy,  IAS, 
Managing  Director,  IDCO  and Shri Sanjib  Kumar  Patwari,  Co Chairman  ASSOCHAM  were  
notably  present  on the occasion  as the distinguished  guests.  At the outse t Shri Jagdish  Arora,  
Director  (P&T),  NALCO  delivered  the welcome  address  and called  upon  the investor s to take 
advantage  of the investor  friendly  eco system  which  not only aims  as an investment  destination  
but also having  immense  potential  for employment  generation.  
 
Addressing  the gathering  Shri G. Kishan  Reddy,  Hon’ble  Union  Minister  of Coal  & Mines,  
Government  of India  said that in line with the hon’ble  Prime  Minister’s  vision  of strengthening  
domestic  manufacturing  and building  a self‑reliant  India,  aluminium  - the Green  Metal  and Metal  
of Tomorrow  - is central  to the journey  towards  Atmanirbhar  Bharat.  “India  currently  imports  
nearly  USD  4 billion  worth  of high‑finish  aluminium  products,  even  with substantial  domestic  
capacity.  Bridging  this gap throug h enhanced  production,  greater  value  addition,  and a strong  
downstream  ecosystem  is a key priority ” he said. This proposed  aluminium  industrial  initiative  
will be a pivotal  platform  for investors  and industries  to establish  and expand  operations,  foster  
innovation,  generate  employment,  and strengthen  India’s  aluminium  value  chain.  Such  
collaborative  efforts  will contribute  meaningfully  to the larger  national  vision  of building  a Viksit  
and Atmanirbhar  Bharat , said the hon’ble  union  Mines  Minister  Shri G. Kishan Reddy . 
 
Speaking  on the occasion,  Shri Keshav  Chandra,  IAS, Secretary,  Ministry  of Mines,  Government  
of India,  emphasised  the need  for the aluminium  sector  to move  beyond  primary  production  
towards  greater  value  addition  and high‑value  finished  produc ts. He also stressed  for greater  
infusion  of technology,  innovation,  and indigenous  R&D  capabilities  to deliver  high‑quality  
products  at competitive  costs.  
 
Shri Brijendra  Pratap  Singh,  CMD,  NALCO,  in his address  highlighted  that the Angul  Aluminium  
Park is envisaged  as a key platform  to translate  opportunities  into meaningful  industrial  
partnerships  and to build  a strong,  integrated  downstream  aluminium  ecosystem  around  NALCO’s  
operations  at Angola. He acknowledged  the continued  support  of the Minis try of Mines,  
Government  of India,  and the Government  of Odisha  in creating  an enabling  ecosystem  that has 
allowed  NALCO  to play a meaningful  role in developing  the aluminium  value  chain  in the region  
 
Shri D. Prasanth  Kumar  Reddy,  IAS, MD IDCO,  said that over the last two years,  Odisha  has 
emerged  as a preferred  investment  destination,  driven  by its proactive  approach  and investor -
 
 
 
friendly  policies.  Strategically  located,  the Angul  Aluminium  Park offers  a unique  advantage  with 
its molten  metal  corridor  and ready  availability  of land,  water  and energy.  He further  said that the 
Park is poised  for significant  expansion  and will provide  an enabling  platform  for downstream  
industries,  value  addition  and MSME  growth.  
 
Organised  by NALCO  with the Associated  Chambe rs of Commerce  and Industry  of India  
(ASSOCHAM)  as the event  partner,  the roadshow  brought  together  policymakers,  industry  leaders  
and prospective  investors  to explore  opportunities  for establishing  and expanding  aluminium -
based  manufacturing  operations.  The Roadshow  was attended  by over 250 representatives  from  
the investor  community,  industry  associations,  and other  stakeholders.  The roadshow  placed  
spotlight  on Angul  Aluminium  Park Private  Limited  (AAPPL),  a joint initiative  of NALCO  and 
IDCO  established  at Anugola  in Odisha  to create  a world -class  destination  for downstream  
aluminium  industries.  AAPPL  provides  access  to key industrial  infrastructure,  including  industrial  
land,  reliable  power  and water  supply,  road connectivity,  and R&D  and testing  facili ties, enabling  
investors  to develop  manufacturing  operations  within  a supporting  industrial  ecosystem.  A key 
proposition  for prospective  investors  is the availability  of NALCO’s  hot metal  as a critical  raw 
material  for downstream  manufacturing.  NALCO  will provide  50,000  tonnes  of hot metal  annually  
at a discounted  rate, offering  investors  an opportunity  to secure  a reliable  source  of raw material  
and support  the economics  of establishing  downstream  aluminium  manufacturing  facilities.

Press  Release  
NALCO  Pays  ₹1,083.06  Crore  Dividend  to 
Government  of India  for FY 2025  – 26 
 
 
Bhubaneswar /Kolkata - 28.09.2026: Bhubaneswar,  28.09.2026:  National  Aluminium  
Company  Limited  (NALCO),  the Navratna  CPSE  under  the Ministry  of Mines,  Government  of 
India,  has paid a dividend  of ₹2,112.12  crore  (230%  on Equity  share  of Rs. 5/- each)  for FY 
2025  - 26, including  the Government  of India’s  share  of ₹1,083.06  crore  - the highest‑ever 
dividend  paid by the Company.  The dividend  cheque  of ₹1,083.06  crore  was handed  over to 
Shri G. Kishan  Reddy,  Hon’ble  Union  Minister  of Coal & Mines,  Government  of India,  in the 
presence  of Shri Keshav  Chandra,  IAS, Secretary,  Ministry  of Mines,  Government  of India,  
by Shri Brijendra  Pratap  Singh,  CMD,  NALCO,  during  the Investor  Roadshow  on Angul  
Aluminium  Park held in Kolkata  today.  Shri Jagdish  Arora,  Director  (P&T),  Shri Abhay  Kumar  
Behuria,  Director  (Finance),  and other  senior  officials  from NALCO  and the Ministry  of Mines  
were  notably  present  on the occasion.  
 
It may be mentioned  that NALCO  has reporte d a robust  performance  in FY 2025 –26 with a 
Profit  After  Tax of ₹5,815.76  crore.  The Company  declared  and paid a total dividend  of 
₹2,112.12  crore  during  the year,  including  three  interim  dividends  and a final dividend,  out of 
which  ₹1,083.06  crore  was paid to the Government  of India.
