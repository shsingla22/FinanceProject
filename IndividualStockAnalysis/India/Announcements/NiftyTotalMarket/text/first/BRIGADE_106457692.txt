PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Group Signs Joint Development Agreement for Premium Residential Project in  
Begumpet, Hyderabad  
 
Strategic Expansion with Development Potential of ~0.5 million sq.ft. and ₹ 800+ Crores Revenue  
 
Hyderabad , September 2 9, 2025 : Brigade Group has signed a Joint Development Agreement (JDA)  with a land 
owner  to develop a premium residential project in Begumpet, one of Hyderabad’s most well -connected and 
established urban neighbourhoods. This aligns with Brigade’s continued focus on the Telangana market.  
 
Spread across ~3 acres of prime land, the project will offer a development potential of approximately 0.5 million 
sq. ft., with an estimated revenue potential exceeding ₹800 Crores. Planned as a high -quality contemporary 
residential community, the development will feature thoughtfully designed homes to meet the lifestyle needs 
of modern urban families. Offering seamless access to major business hubs, social infrastructure,  and transport 
networks, the development highlights Brigade’s commitment to thoughtful ly located urban living spaces.  
 
“Hyderabad continues to be a core growth market for Brigade, and we are strengthening our presence with a 
premium residential development in Begumpet. Our focus is to create communities that deliver long -term value 
through exceptional design, sustainabilit y, and a refined living experience. We remain committed to exploring 
strategic opportunities that align with our vision of scalable and responsible expansion in the region.” – Amar 
Mysore, Executive Director, Brigade Enterprises Limited.  
 
With this addition, Brigade continues to deepen its presence in Hyderabad’s residential sector, contributing to 
the evolving skyline of the city.  
 
About Brigade Group:  Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers with 
close to four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and winning their 
customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark bu ildings and transformed skylines across India in 
the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, and GIFT City with 
developments across Residential, Office, Retail, Hospitality and Education Sectors.
