             Raymond Limited Accelerates Andhra Pradesh Expansion to 
Support Growth Momentum  
Mumbai, October 12, 2026 : Raymond Limited through its subsidiary companies is 
accelerating its capacity expansion in Andhra Pradesh to support strong business 
momentum and growing customer requirements. As part of this strategy, the Company’s 
subsidiary is setting up an additional brownfield facility in proximity to its existing Mega 
manufacturing facility in Andhra Pradesh. The facility is being developed as an interim 
capacity augmentation initiative and is expected to support incremental customer 
requirements and volumes during the current financial year. 
The decision to accelerate brownfield capacity addition reflects the Company’s focus on 
responding swiftly to customer demand while maintaining capital and execution discipline. 
In parallel, Raymond’s planned greenfield project in Andhra Pradesh is progressing as 
scheduled. The greenfield facility is envisaged as a larger, long-term manufacturing 
platform that will significantly enhance the Company’s infrastructure and capacity to 
support its medium- to long-term growth plans. 
Sanjeev Sharma , Joint Managing Director & CEO, JKMPTL- Raymond Group said 
“The accelerated expansion in Andhra Pradesh reflects the strong momentum we are 
seeing in the business. While our greenfield project remains the foundation for our longer-
term growth, the additional brownfield capacity will allow us to respond faster to current 
customer requirements. This approach enables us to capture near-term opportunities 
while continuing to build a scalable platform for sustained growth.” 
The combination of near-term brownfield expansion and the planned greenfield facility 
provides Raymond with a two-stage capacity expansion strategy: 
a. Near-term capacity augmentation to address current customer requirements and 
incremental volumes; and 
b. Long-term scalable infrastructure to support future growth and expanding customer 
opportunities. 
This expansion reinforces Raymond Limited’s strategy of building a scalable, capital-
efficient manufacturing platform, while progressively strengthening its capabilities to 
address opportunities across domestic and global markets.

Press Release                                                                                                             
 
With the inception in 1925, Raymond Group has been a pioneer and leader in fabric manufacturing and then forayed 
in other sectors such as engineering and Real Estate. After demerging its Lifestyle Business and Real Estate verticals 
into independent entities, Raymond Limited now has two core businesses within the Engineering vertical - Tools &  
Auto Components and Aerospace and Defence. Raymond’s engineering business commands a leadership position in 
manufacturing files and hand tools and has a significant presence in national and international markets. With the 
acquisition of Maini Precision Products Limited (MPPL) Raymond’s engineering business has forayed into the sunrise 
sectors of Aerospace and Defence. EV components and caters to international as well as domestic markets. 
About Raymond Group: 
Raymond Group is a diversified Indian conglomerate with a presence across Engineering, Aerospace & Defence, 
Automotive, Real Estate and Lifestyle, through its three listed entities — Raymond Limited, Raymond Lifestyle Limited 
and Raymond Realty Limited. The Group is transforming into a new-age enterprise, building businesses with strong 
technology, manufacturing capabilities, execution excellence and long-term growth potential. Raymond Limited is 
focused on Engineering, Aerospace Defence and Automotive businesses, with capabilities spanning precision 
engineering, aerospace components, automotive components and defence applications, serving leading global and 
Indian customers. Raymond Realty Limited is a focused real estate developer with a strong presence across the 
Mumbai Metropolitan Region, developing large-scale residential and commercial projects with an estimated gross 
development value of approximately ₹52,000 crores. Raymond Lifestyle Limited is one of India’s leading integrated 
players in branded apparel, premium fabrics and garmenting, with a retail presence across 600+ cities and 1,600+ 
stores. Built on a legacy of Trust, Quality and Excellence, Raymond is leveraging its strong heritage, manufacturing 
capabilities and consumer franchise to create a diversified group positioned for the next phase of growth across 
Engineering, Aerospace & Defence, Automotive, Real Estate and Lifestyle.
