Sub: Comments of the Board of Directors of the Company w.r.t the fine imposed by 
the National Stock Exchange of India Ltd . for non-compliance of Regulation 
17(1) of SEBI LODR Regulations 
Ref: Your Letter no NSE/LIST-SOP/COMB/FINES/0611 dated 27th May, 2026  
 
With reference to the above letter and pursuant to the SEBI Master Circular No. 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30th January, 2026, we wish to inform 
you that non-compliance which has been identified by the Exchange and subsequent action 
taken by the Exchange was placed before the Board of Directors of FACT at its meeting 
held on 14th August, 2026. 
The Board took note of the matter and acknowledged that the power to appoint Directors on 
the Board of the Company vests with the Government of India. The Board advised the 
Company to continue following up the matter with the Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers for 
appointment of the requisite number of Independent Directors and to request for waiver of 
the fines imposed by the Stock Exchange. 
Based on the notification of the Government of India two Independent Directors have been 
appointed to the Board of FACT on 14.08.2026.  The Company is pursuing with the 
Government of India for appointment of more Independent Directors to fulfill the 
requirement as per the SEBI(LODR) Regulations, 2015.
