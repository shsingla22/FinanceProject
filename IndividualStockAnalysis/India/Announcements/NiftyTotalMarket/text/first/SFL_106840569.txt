Operational & Financial Highlights: H1 FY27  
• Mattress segment grew by 11% and foam segment by 26%  
• Revenue grew by 22% YoY to ₹ 2,077 cr from ₹ 1,696 cr on account of higher growth in both mattress 
and foam segments.  
• Operating EBITDA grew by 23% to ₹ 200 cr from ₹ 162 cr  
• PAT of ₹ 109 cr, up from ₹ 17 cr in H1 FY26, with cash EPS of ₹ 18.7  
 
Operational & Financial Highlights: Q2 FY27  
• Revenue grew by 19% YoY to ₹ 1,045 cr from ₹ 875 cr, led by growth in both mattress and foam 
segments and strong growth in Spain and Australia  
• Mattress value grew by 8%, volume declined by 3% due to shift of festive season  
• Foam business grew by 26%,  volume taper by 5%  as higher raw material costs were passed through 
in pricing  
• Despite sharp increases in polyol and TDI prices and higher brand investment for the Great Sleepwell 
Festival, Operating EBITDA stood at ₹ 8 7 cr against ₹ 87 cr in Q2 FY26  
• PAT of ₹ 46 cr, up from ₹ 10 cr YoY, aided by lower finance costs & depreciation ; Cash EPS at ₹ 8.3 
 
Mr. Rahul Gautam (Chairman & Managing Director) commenting on the results  said that since the West 
Asia crisis began in Feb’26, the environment has been highly disruptive, with raw material prices rising 
sharply, putting pressure on demand and the festive season shifting into Q3. We responded with balanced 
price increases, higher advertisi ng and consumer offers. We crossed ₹1,000 crore of revenue, up 19% YoY , 
and growing H1 Operating EBITDA by 23%, and also  protecting our profitability in absolute terms. This reflects 
the strength of our brands, our supplier relationships and the discipline of our teams.  
With Furlenco becoming our subsidiary from 1st October, Sheela Foam is now a full B2C portfolio company, 
with leadership in mattresses, foam and now furniture rentals. We have also embarked in branded furniture 
space through our flagship brands. Sheela Foa m is now poised as a larger, far more diversified group, with a 
presence across categories, channels and geographies.  
About Sheela Foam Limited  
Leading the science of comfort in three continents, Sheela Foam is an Indian multinational company. Among 
the most R&D -focused foam manufacturers globally, Sheela Foam is a leading producer of polyurethane (PU) 
foam operating across Asia, Australia, and Eu rope. Founded in 1971, the company has always focused on 
delivering comfort to its consumers through its products. In India, its flagship brands - Sleepwell and Kurlon 
serve the comfort needs of over a billion people, reaching all states through a network of 11,000+ retail 
touchpoints. A strong practitioner of giving back to society, Sheela Foam has taken diverse initiatives for 
emotional wellness and multi -dimensional skill development under the Sleepwell Foundation.
