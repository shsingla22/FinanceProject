For Immediate Release  
Equitas SFB celebrates 9 years of Banking with a revamped 
Mobile Banking App  
 
Celebrating 9 years of trust, Equitas offers a smart way of banking with Equitas 2.O Mobile Banking 
App 
India, September 05, 2025:  Equitas Small Finance Bank, one of the largest SFBs in India, is all set to 
celebrate its 9th Anniversary on 5th September, marking nearly a decade of its journey in advancing 
financial inclusion and transf orming lives through responsible banking.  
Celebrating its 9th year, Equitas SFB has announced the launch of its new and improved Mobile 
Banking App ( Equitas 2.0 ), showcasing its growing emphasis on digital banking and customer 
convenience.  
The enhanced Mobile Banking App, Equitas 2.0, delivers a faster, improved and more secure digital 
banking experience to customers. It features key functionalities such as opening accounts online for 
new customers, booking online F ixed Deposits, managing FD and RD, access to IPOs and Mutual Funds, 
card management, utility and bill payments, goal -based deposits, redemption of rewards, service 
request tracking & management and more.  
Commenting on the celebrations, Mr. Vasudevan P N, M D & CEO of Equitas Small Finance Bank  said, 
“We at Equitas, are thrilled to announce the launch of our new Mobile Banking App, which strengthens 
our presence in the digital space. The launch of this app demonstrates the dedication of our 
technology team an d further consolidates our vision of providing banking with convenience.”  

 
 Further elaborating on the same, Mr. Balaji Nuthalapadi, Executive Director - Technology & 
Operations mentioned, “With the launch of our new Mobile Banking App, we are taking a signi ficant 
step towards building a true super app for our customers. Over a period of time this platform will 
seamlessly integrate across the customer lifecycle – from e -commerce to ticketing & entertainment – 
empowering customers with a single destination for  their evolving needs.  
About Equitas Small Finance Bank Limited [ESFB]  
Equitas Small Finance Bank is one of the largest Small Finance Banks in India. As a new -age bank, we 
offer a bouquet of products and services tailored to meet the needs of our customers  – individuals 
with limited access to formal financing channels, as well as affluent and mass -affluent, Small & 
Medium Enterprises (SMEs) and corporates. Our firmly -entrenched strategy focuses on providing 
credit to the unbanked and underbanked micro and s mall entrepreneurs, developing products to 
address the growing aspirations at the ‘bottom of the pyramid’, fuelled by granular deposits and ‘value 
for money’.
