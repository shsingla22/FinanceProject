RELIANCE POWER SUBSIDIARY, RELIANCE NU ENERGIES RECEIVES LETTER OF 
AWARD (LOA), FOR THE LARGEST ALLOCATION IN 1500 MW / 6000 MWh FDRE ISTS 
TENDER ISSUED BY SJVN LIMITED (SJVN), A LEADING NAVRATNA ENTERPRISE  
 
RELIANCE NU ENERGIES SECURED 750 MW / 3000 MWH, REPRESENTING 50% OF 
TOTAL TENDER ALLOCATION IN COMPETITIVE BID ONLINE AUCTION PROCESS  
 
RELIANCE GROUP EMERGES AS INDIA’S LARGEST PLAYER IN SOLAR + BATTERY 
ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM (BESS) WITH 4 GWp SOLAR + 6.5 GWh BESS CAPACITY 
ACROSS 4 TENDERS ALL WITH NAVRATNA ENTERPRISES IN LESS THAN 1 YEAR TIME, 
CEMENTING ITS LEADERSHIP IN NEW ENERGY LANDSC APE 
 
THE PROJECT WILL FEATURE AN INSTALLED CAPACITY OF 900 MWp OF SOLAR 
POWER INTEGRATED WITH OVER 3,000 MWh OF BESS 
 
RELIANCE NU ENERGIES HAS SUCCESSFULLY SECURED A TARIFF OF RS 6.74 PER 
KWH THROUGH A COMPETITIVE BIDDING ONLINE AUCTION PROCESS  
 
Mumbai, November 11, 2025 – Reliance Power Limited’s wholly -owned subsidiary, Reliance NU 
Energies Private Limited, received the Letter of Award (LOA) on November 10, 2025 and has 
emerged as the largest winner in the first -of-its-kind 1500 MW / 6000 MWh Firm and Dispatchabl e 
Renewable Energy (FDRE) ISTS tender issued by SJVN Limited, a leading Navratna enterprise.   
 
Reliance NU Energies secured a capacity of 750 MW / 3,000 MWh, representing the largest single 
share of allocation under the tender. 
 
The landmark FDRE tender ma rks a major step towards enabling round- the-clock renewable 
power supply through hybrid and storage- based energy solutions. The online auction witnessed 
strong industry participation, being oversubscribed 3.3 times, with 14 bidders qualifying for the 
rever se online auction process.  
 With this milestone, the Reliance Group has consolidated its position as India’s largest 
player in the Solar + BESS segment, with a cumulative portfolio exceeding 4 GWp of solar 
and 6.5 GWh of BESS across 4 tenders, under development and implementation. This 
achievement, accomplished in just 10 months, highlights the Group’s rapid execution 
capabilities and its commitment to India’s energy transition and decarbonization goals.  
 The project will supply dispatchable renewable peaking power to DISCOMs from a hybrid 
configuration comprising nearly 900 MWp of solar generation coupled with over 3,000 MWh of 
 
  
 
BESS capacity. Reliance NU Energies secured this capacity at a competitive tariff of ₹6.74 per 
kWh, setting a new benchmark in India’s evolving renewable energy market. 
 
The SJVN FDRE tender represents a pioneering step in India’s energy transition, being the first 
structured initiative to supply renewable peaking power to DISCOMs across the country.  
 The project will serve as a key enabler in achieving India’s ambitious renewable energy and 
decarbonization targets.  
 The project reinforces Reliance Power’s strategic commitment to renewable energy leadership, 
while ensuring sustainable value creation across stakeholders. It marks a significant milestone in 
the company’s long- term vision to transition toward cleaner energy sources and play a pivotal role 
in shaping India’s sustainable energy future.
