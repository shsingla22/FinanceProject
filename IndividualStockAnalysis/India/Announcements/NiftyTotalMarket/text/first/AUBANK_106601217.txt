AU Small Finance Bank Announces Q4’FY26/FY26 Financial Results  
 
Strong all ‑round performance with P AT up 65% YoY to ₹ 832 cr  
 RoA at 1.8%, RoE at 17%; Deposits up 23% YoY and Loans up 21% YoY  
FY26 profit grows 25% to ₹ 2,641 cr

Key financial highlights for Q4’FY26  
o Net interest Margin (NIM) expanded by 24bps to 5.96 % in Q4’FY26 from 5.7% in Q3’FY26  
o Cost of Funds (CoF) further declined by 12 bps QoQ in Q4’FY26 to 6.49%  
o Net Interest Income (NII) grew by 23% YoY and 10% QoQ to ₹2,582cr  
o Core other income grew by 14% YoY and 7% QoQ to ₹748 cr  
o Provisions for Q4’FY26 were down 58% YoY and 19% QoQ to ₹269 cr  
o PAT for Q4’FY26 was up by 65% YoY and 25% QoQ to ₹832 cr; PAT for FY26 up 25% to ₹2,641 cr  
o RoA for Q4’FY26/FY26 was 1.8%/1.6% and RoE at 17.0%/14.2% respectively  
o Asset quality ratios improve sequentially; GNPA ratio at 2.03% at Mar’26 from 2.30% at Dec’25; 
NNPA ratio was 0.74% at Mar’26 compared to 0.88% at Dec’25  
o Cost/Assets ratio declined to 4.1% for FY26 from 4.3% in FY25 (excl CGFMU premium)  
o Total deposits grew by 23% YoY and 10% QoQ to ₹1,52,661 cr  
o CASA deposit grows by 20% YoY and ~9% QoQ    
o Gross loan portfolio grew by 21% YoY and 8% QoQ to ₹1,40,327 cr  
o Secured businesses  (Retail + Commercial)  grew by 23% YoY and 7% QoQ  
o Unsecured businesses (MFI, credit card, PL) saw a turnaround  this quarter and grew by 7% 
QoQ led by MFI and PL; de -grew by 1% YoY  
o Credit cost for FY26 improved to 0.96% of average assets vs 1.3% for FY25; Credit cost at 1% incl.

deposit branches; total touchpoints reach 2,790  
 
Mumbai | April 27, 2026:  The Board of Directors of AU Small Finance Bank Limited at its meeting held 
today, approved the financial results for the quarter and financial year ended 31st March  2026.  
Executive Summary  
Indian macroeconomic environment was relatively on better footing for most part of Q4 but it did see 
some volatility emanating from the ongoing West Asia crisis towards the later half of March. Amidst 
this environment, AU delivered a strong all round quarterly performance to finish the year on a high 
note. With execution firmly on track and fundamentals strengthening, we step into the next phase of 
growth with con viction.
