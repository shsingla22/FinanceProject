With reference to the captioned subject and in compliance with Regulation 30 read with 
Schedule III of the Listing Regulations, we wish to inform you that , the Board of Directors of  
Next Wave Multimedia Private Limited  (“Nextwave”) , a subsidiary of the Company , at their

software and associated trademarks  (“UTP - IP Assets”) by way of Assignment from U Games 
Private Limited (“U Games / Assignor”), the subsidiary of Play Games24x7 Private Limited 
(“Play Games / Confirming Party”) , for an aggregate consideration of INR 10,00,00,000 (Indian 
Rupees Ten Crores Only)  inclusive of applicable taxes , if any, to be paid in cash, in one  or 
more tranches  (“Assignment Consideration”)  on such terms and conditions, as mutually 
agreed in the IP Assignment Agreement proposed to be entered between Nextwave, U Games 
and Play Games .  
 
In furtherance of the same, Nextwave has entered into an “IP Assignment Agreement” with U 
Games and Play Games on April 04, 2024, recording  the terms and conditions of the 
acquisition of UTP – IP Assets of U Games.
