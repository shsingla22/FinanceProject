In terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, this is to inform that Power Grid Corporation of India Limited, pursuant to 
its selection as the successful bidder under Tariff based competitive bidding, has on 
30.05.2024 acquired Khavda IV-E2 Power Transmission Limited, the Project SPV to establish 
“Transmission System for Evacuation of Power from potential renewable energy zone in 
Khavda area of Gujarat under Phase-IV (7GW) Part E2”, on build, own, operate and transfer 
(BOOT) basis from the Bid Process Coordinator – REC Power Development and Consultancy 
Limited. 
 
The project comprises of augmentation works at existing substation in the state of 
Gujarat.
