HCLTech named a Workday Sales Partner  
 
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, July 08, 2025 —HCLTech,  a global technology company , 
announced it has been named a Workday partner. This partnership will help drive broader 
adoption of AI -led human capital management (HCM) solutions and workforce transformation 
across enterprises.  
 
As a Workday partner, HCLTech's deep alignment with Workday's ecosystem will help drive 
Workday adoption among large enterprises via tailored advisory and pre -sales expertise.  
 
HCLTech uses its GenAI -led service transformation platform AI Force  to provide customers 
with AI insights, automation and predictive analytics. When used in combination with  Workday 
Human Capital Management (HCM), Workday Financial Management, Workday Payroll and 
Workday Adaptive Planning solutions, customers will have the tools needed to become more 
agile, scalable and resilient.  
 
"We are excited to embark on this journey wi th Workday. This partnership is pivotal in our 
mission to leverage AI, technology and innovation to redefine the future of human capital 
management. It allows us to deliver exceptional value to our clients and drive future success," said Sadagopan S, Executive Vice President and Global Head of SaaS and Commercial 
Applications  at HCLTech.
