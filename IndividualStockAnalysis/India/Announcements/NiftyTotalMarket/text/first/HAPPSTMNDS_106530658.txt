Dear Stakeholder, 
We are pleased to share that FY26 has continued on a steady and resilient note for Happiest Minds . For the 
nine months ended December 31, 2025 , your Company delivered revenues of ₹1,711 crore , up 12.8% year-
on-year, with constant-currency revenue growth of 10.2% . EBITDA stood at ₹367 crore , with a margin of 
20.6%, comfortably within our guided range. Adjusted PAT for the period was ₹208 crore , compared to ₹195 
crore in the previous year, reflecting disciplined execution and prudent financial management. 
In Q3, total income stood at ₹603.7 crore , up 8.9% year-on-year , while revenues were ₹587.6 crore , growing 
10.7% year-on-year . EBITDA for the quarter was ₹123 crore , translating to a margin of 20.4%, and 
operating margin improved to 17.4% , up 40 basis points sequentially , driven by improved utilization and 
better profitability in the GBS segment, despite fewer working days and forex losses during the quarter. 
Adjusted PAT stood at ₹69.9 crore , which is PAT adjusted for the non-cash acquisition costs and exceptional 
item which in the quarter was the ‘charge for the new wage code’ was at 11.6% compared to the 11% in the 
previous quarter.  
Operationally, utilization increased to 82.0%, the highest in recent times, reflecting better deployment and 
execution discipline. Attrition remained stable at 17.4% , while DSO increased to 92 days , with continued focus 
on collections and billing discipline. 
As we progress through FY26, we remain focused on sustaining double-digit constant-currency growth , 
maintaining EBITDA margins in the 20–22% range , and executing our AI First strategy to drive long-term value 
creation for all our stakeholders.
