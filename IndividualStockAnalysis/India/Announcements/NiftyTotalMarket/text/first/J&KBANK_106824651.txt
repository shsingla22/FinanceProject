PRESS RELEASE  
J&K Bank’s Q2 net profit rises 28.8% QoQ and 10.6% YoY to Rs 546 Cr  
Business grows 19.4% to Rs 3.10 lakh Cr, with decadal best asset quality metrics    
Srinagar, Oct 22:  Supported by growth in earnings and further improvement in asset 
quality, J&K Bank today reported 28.8%  rise quarter -on-quarter and 10.6% rise year-
on-year in its net profit to Rs 546.36  Cr for the quarter (Q2) ended September 30, 
2026, when compared with Rs 424.18 Cr  and Rs 494.11 Cr  recorded during  Q1 FY 
2026-27 and Q2 FY 2025 -26 respectively .  
The Bank announced the financial results after its Board of Directors reviewed and 
approved the quarterly and half -yearly figures at a meeting held at the Bank’s 
corporate headquarters  on 22nd October 2026.  
Operating Performance  
The Bank’s Net Interest Income (NII) rose 5% YoY during the quarter to  Rs 1502 Cr. 
During the quarter, other income doubled to Rs 314.90 Cr  YoY from Rs 153.76 Cr  
recorded in the corresponding quarter last year  while also growing 47% sequentially.  
With controlled operating expenditure , witnessing just 1.5% YoY growth for the half -
year, the o perating Profit for the  half-year has also grown by over 15% YoY, being 
recorded at Rs.1,492 crores. The Cost -to-Income Ratio for the quarter has 
consequently witnessed substantial improvement , being recorded at 56.57%  
compared  60.79% for the corresponding quarter  last year. The Bank’s Return on  Assets 
during the quarter jumped 20 bps  sequentially and has been recorded at  1.07% . 
Commenting on the financial performance, MD & CEO Amitava Chatterjee said, “The 
growth in net interest income, doubling of other income and over 2 6% YoY increase in 
operating profit for this quarter mark an encouraging strengthening of our earnings 
performance. The recovery in Return on Assets to above 1% further underlines the 
improvement over the June quarter.”  
“In Q1, we had emphasized  building the business base and positioning the Bank for 
stronger earnings over time. This quarter’s operating performance represents progress 
in that direction. Our focus for the second half is on sustaining core income growth, 
strengthening recurring revenue streams and enhancing recoveries , while addressing 
funding costs through deeper deposit relationships. These priorities remain central to 
delivering our annual objectives and translating business expansion into sustainable 
profitability,” he added.  
 
Asset Quality  
With the Gross NPA ratio of the Bank declining  substantially by 109 bps on a YoY basis 
to 2.23% and the  Net NPA ratio also improv ing to 0.51% against  0.76%  a year ago, the 
Bank has recorded decadal best asset quality metrics.  The Bank ’s Provision Coverage 
Ratio (PCR)  has also recorded improvement in sequential terms and stood at 91.76 % 
as on 30th September 2026  against 90.53%  as on 30th June 2026, which is again a 
decadal best.  
On the improvement in asset quality, MD & CEO Amitava Chatterjee commented , “The 
continuing improvement in our GNPA ratio by 14 bps QoQ to 2.23% - the lowest level 
in a decade – along with a n annualized  Gross Slippage Ratio of just 0.4% for the half -
year, reflect the cumulative impact of our continued focus on disciplined underwriting  
and proactive credit monitoring mechanism  even as we continue to expand our loan 
book. ” 
“We remain focused on sustaining this momentum through the second half, 
progressing towards our annual asset -quality objectives and building a resilient 
balance sheet that supports the Bank’s long -term growth aspirations” , he added.  
Business Growth  
Continuing its business growth momentum, the Bank’s total business increased by 
19.38%  year-on-year to Rs 3,10,347  Cr as on September 30, 2026.  
Gross advances grew by 23.72%  to Rs 1,33,551 Cr  from Rs 1,07,943 Cr  recorded a 
year ago, while total deposits increased by 16.30% to Rs 1,76,796 Cr.  Notably , on 
YTD basis, over 60% of the net incremental credit has been contributed by Retail, 
Agriculture, MSME (RAM) segment.  
The Bank’s Current Account and Savings Account (CASA) deposits also rose by  1.73%  
QoQ. And as a result, the Bank has been  able to broadly maintain its CA SA Ratio  at 
around 42%, being recorded at 41.97% as on September 30,2026 . 
Connecting the performance with the Bank’s longer -term direction, MD & CEO Amitava 
Chatterjee said, “With nearly 20% year -on-year growth taking our total business to Rs 
3.10 lakh crore, we continue to build towards our Vision 2030 business aspiration of 
Rs 5 lakh crore.”  
“Strengthening our deposit franchise, deepening relationships in Jammu and Kashmir 
and Ladakh, and selectively expanding across the country will remain central to this 
journey, supported by prudent lending and technology -led yet personalised customer 
service”, he added.  
 
 
Capital Adequacy  
The Bank  remains well -capitalised to pursue quality lending opportunities while 
maintaining a prudent cushion against emerging risks with its Capital Adequacy Ratio 
(CRAR) at 16.57  % as on September 30, 2026, compared with 15.27% a year earlier.  
Summing up  the results , MD & CEO Amitava Chatterjee said, “ "Driven by clear 
priorities and disciplined execution, we enter the second half with confidence that 
we will able to further improve upon this performance .” 
“Our endeavour is to translate this progress into sustained profitability and greater 
value for all stakeholders, while widening opportunities for the people and businesses 
we serve. This will guide us as we pursue our annual commitments and advance 
towards  the larger institutional goals under Vision 2030” , he added.
