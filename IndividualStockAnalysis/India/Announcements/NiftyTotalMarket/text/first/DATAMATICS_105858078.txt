Dun & Bradstreet recognizes Datamatics amongst India’s 
Top 500 Value Creators  
 
23rd May 2024, Mumbai : Datamatics, a leading 
global Digital Technologies, Operations, and 
Experiences Company, today announced that it 
has been recognized as  amongst the  'India's Top 
500 Value Creator s 2023' by Dun & Bradstreet . The 
list of top 500 value creators is curated from the 
companies that are listed on BSE and NSE for the 
last five-year period. These companies were 
measured against more than 25 parameters, that 
assessed both market value and intrinsic value of 
the company. Datamatics is identified amongst 
the top value creators for creating long-term value 
to all their stakeholders beyond financi al growth, 
outperforming on business productivity, business 
resilience, and sustainability aspects.  
 
Rahul Kanodia, Vice Chairman and CEO  Datamatics commented ,” We  are happy to 
be recognized by Dun &  Bradstreet, amongst 'India's Top 500 Value Creator 2023’. It 
is a recognition to the value we have created for all our stakeholders, as well as our 
outstanding corporate performance over the years.” He further added, “We are 
committed to enable  enterprises go deep in digital to create operational efficiency, 
superior customer experience and a sustainable competitive advantage. I take this 
opportunity to thank all our stakeholders, including employees, customers and 
shareholders, for being an int egral part of our journey."  
Vipul Oberoi, Director – Marketing, Learning Solutions & CSR said,  “Dun & 
Bradstreet analysed the financial data of companies in the D&B Data Cloud and has 
identified companies that don’t just pursue profits but create long -term  sustainable 
value for all stakeholders.  The D&B Top 500 Value Creators have outperformed peers 
on all the parameters of value creation  and have demonstrated business success and 
performance .” 
Datamatics was recently recognized among the 'Forbes Asia 200 Best Under a Billion 
Companies 2023' . This places Datamatics in the top 1% of 20,000 listed companies
