Commenting on the same, Mr. Adhish Patil, Managing Director , Aarti Drugs Limited said,  
“Q2 FY27 has been a landmark quarter for Aarti Drugs. We achieved our highest -ever revenue from operations 
of Rs. 826 crore and reported our highest -ever quarterly EBITDA of Rs. 138 crore. EBITDA margin expanded 
370 basis points to 16.6%, the highest level in nearly six years. This performance reflects a combination of 
volume growth, strong realisations across our API, formulations and Specialty Chemicals portfolio, and the 
steady progress we have made in building a more integrated manufacturing platform. Th rough a period of 
considerable global volatility, all our facilities operated without disruption which enabled us to serve our 
customers reliably as their trusted partner.  
Growth during the quarter was broad -based across our businesses. Our Specialty Chemicals business nearly 
doubled as our Sayakha facility scaled up, and our Formulations business grew 58%, led by strong demand in 
export markets. Within a year of commissioni ng, Sayakha is meeting a significant share of our metformin 
intermediate requirements in -house, enhancing both our cost competitiveness and the security of our supply 
chain in the anti -diabetic segment. At the same time, our capacity expansions at Tarapur,  Sarigam and Baddi, 
including new USFDA -compliant capacity, are progressing well and will deepen our presence in regulated 
markets.  
This period also marks an important moment in our leadership journey. Starting October 1st 2026, Mr. Prakash 
M. Patil retired as Chairman, Managing Director and CEO after a lifetime of dedicated service to the Company. 
His vision, integrity and leadership laid the foundations on which Aarti Drugs stands today, and on behalf of 
the Board and the entire Aarti Drugs family, I express our deepest gratitude to him. With Mr. Rashesh Gogri 
taking over as Chairman, and as I take charge as Managing Director, we remain committed to the values and 
long -term thinking that have guided the Company for decades.   
Looking ahead, we are building Aarti Drugs into a more integrated, more diversified and more globally 
relevant pharmaceutical company and aim to remain focused on improving capacity utilisation, expanding 
our regulatory footprint and creating sustainable g rowth for all our stakeholders. ”

About Aarti Drugs Limited  
Aarti Drugs Limited was established in the year 1984 and forms part of $6 Billion Aarti Group of Industries 
with robust R&D Division at Tarapur, Maharashtra Industrial Development Corporation (MIDC) in close vicinity 
to manufacturing locations. The Company  is engaged in the manufacturing of Active Pharmaceutical 
Ingredients (APIs), Pharma Intermediates, Specialty Chemicals and produces Formulations with its wholly -
owned subsidiary -Pinnacle Life Science Private Limited. The Company have total 1 4 manufacturing facilities 
out of which 10 manufacturing facilities are in Maharashtra, 3 Manufacturing facilities in Gujarat and 1 
Manufacturing facility of Pinnacle is located at Himachal Pradesh. Products under APIs include Ciprofloxacin 
Hydrochloride, Metronidazole, Metformin HCL, Ketoconazole, Ofloxacin etc. whereas Specialty Chemicals 
includes Benzene Sulphonyl Chloride, Methyl Nicotinate etc.
