a. Company Performance Factor (CPF); and  
 CPF is the percentage  of Actual Performance vis -à-vis the Target Performance 
on the identified Performance Metric  
b. Individual Rating Multiplier (IRM)  
 IRM is the Performance Factor based on Annual Performance Ratings for an 
individual, based on different levels of Performance
