Sub: Intimation of MoU between NBCC and HUDCO  
 
Sir, 
 
This is to inform that NBCC (India) Limited on April 11, 2026, has entered into two different 
Memorandum of Understanding s (MoUs) with Housing and Urban Development Corporation 
Limited (HUDCO) as follows : 
 
1. MoU for the Redevelopment  of August Kranti Bhawan, Bhikaji Cama Place, 
New Delhi  
 
HUDCO and NBCC agree to collaborate and jointly work towards the redevelopment of 
leasehold plot  admeasuring approx. 18830 sqmt. at Block No. 25, August Kranti 
Bhawan, Bhikaji Cama Place, New Delhi. NBCC shall render Techno -economic feasibility 
study, Construction and Project Management, Monetization of built -up space etc. 
 
2. MoU for providing funds for the ongoing and upcoming projects of NBCC’s self -
sustainable model  
 
Reinforcing cooperation and collaboration to leverage respective strengths  for 
undertaking Asset Monetization activities for NBCC’s self -sustainable model projects. 
HUDCO shall provide funds for ongoing and upcoming projects of NBCC’s self -
sustainable model on mutually agreed terms and conditions. NBCC shall provide Project 
Mana gement and Marketing consultancy services.  
 
The partnership is expected to expedite projects  execution, enhance assets value, and 
contribute to urban infrastructure development.    
 
The said MoUs are in the normal course of business of the Company.  
 
This is for your information and record.
