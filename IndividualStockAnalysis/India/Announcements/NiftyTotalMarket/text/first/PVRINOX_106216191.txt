PVR INOX MAKES GRAND DEBUT IN JABALPUR WITH FOCO -MODEL MULTIPLEX AT KA MALL  
~ Featuring 4 uniquely themed auditoriums, the multiplex promises a top -tier movie -watching 
experience ~  
National, April 30, 2025:  PVR INOX, India’s largest and most premium cinema exhibitor, has officially 
made its grand debut in the vibrant city of Jabalpur with the opening of its first -ever multiplex in the 
region . Located on the 4th floor of the newly rebranded KA Mall , near Naudr a Bridge . 
Bringing the signature PVR INOX experience to Jabalpur for the very first time, the new multiplex is a blend 
of cinematic sophistication and thoughtful design. With a total seating capacity of 611, including 25 
recliner seats , each of the four screens has its own vibrant personality —Red, Gold, Purple, and Teal —
enhancing the visual character of the auditoriums. Guests can enjoy Udine slider seating , fabric -finished 
walls, and ambient lighting shaped by elegant ‘V’ fixtures, creat ing a mood -driven environment tailored 
to every film genre.  
Equipped with cutting -edge S&P technology , the cinema features Christie  RGB Laser projectors , Dolby 
7.1 surround sound , and DTS - X immersive sound system, a next -generation 3D system , offering crystal -
clear visuals and immersive audio to elevate the storytelling experience.  
This landmark property also marks another successful chapter in PVR INOX’s Franchise Owned Company 
Operated (FOCO)  model.  The FOCO model enables the company to scale its prem ium formats in high -
growth Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities through partnerships with visionary asset owners. While the infrastructure 
is owned by franchisees, PVR INOX brings in its industry -leading expertise to manage the design, 
development, and daily operation s. This asset -light strategy ensures consistency, quality, and speed of 
expansion —making it a key lever in the brand’s nationwide growth.  
Mr. Ajay Bijli , Managing Director, PVR INOX Limited,  said, "Opening our doors in Jabalpur is a meaningful 
step in our journey. This city has a deep -rooted cultural identity and a growing appetite for quality 
entertainment. With this launch, we’re not just adding another property —we’re proudly introducing the 
PVR INOX experience to Jabalpur for the very first time . This la unch also marks another success for our 
FOCO (Franchise Owned Company Operated) model, which enables us to expand rapidly into high -
potential markets while maintaining our signature quality. It’s always been our endeavour to match the 
energy of the cities we enter, and Jabalpur is no exception ." 
Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli , Executive Director, PVR INOX Limited, added,  "Expanding into cities like Jabalpur 
is a reflection of our belief in the growing demand for better cinema experiences across the country. This 
property at KA Mall is designed to be much more than just a place to watch films —it’s a complete 
entertainment d estination, with thoughtful attention to both design and amenities. With Raid 2 headlining 
our launch, we look forward to welcoming audiences and are confident they will leave impressed.”  
 
 

From a sleek, modern exterior to a welcoming foyer with premium fi nishes, plush seating, and a centrally 
located concessionaire, every touchpoint at PVR INOX KA Mall reflects the brand ’s commitment to 
delivering an exceptional moviegoing experience. The thoughtfully designed layout ensures ease of 
navigation and comfort from the moment guests walk in. Set to redefine the way Jabalpur watches movies, 
the new multiplex is positioned as a cultural and social hub for film enthusiasts. With Raid 2 opening the 
curtains, PVR INOX KA Mall is poised to become the city ’s most exciti ng new entertainment address.  
With this opening, PVR strengthens its growth momentum in FY 2025 -26 with 1738 Screens across 352 
properties in 111 cities (India and Sri Lanka).
