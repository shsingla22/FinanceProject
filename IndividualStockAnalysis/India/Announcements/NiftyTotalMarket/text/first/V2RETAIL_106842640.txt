Results Release – Q2 & H1 FY27 
 
STRONG GROWTH MOMENTUM DESPITE FESTIVE SHIFT; MARGINS EXPAND  
 
High  Revenue Growth Momentum of 42% in H1FY27   
Improved Gross Margin to 29.8% in H1FY27 compared to 28.7% in H1FY26  
Operating Performance : EBITDA Climbs 50% YoY to ₹259.7  Cr in H1FY27

New Delhi , November  3, 202 6: V2 Retail Limited (V2 Retail) , one of India’s leading and fastest 
growing Value Retail Company, with 427 stores  spread across 26 states  and 1 Union Territory 
announced its Financial Results for the quarter and half year ended September  30, 202 6.  
 
Consolidated Financial Performance at a Glance:  
 
 
 
Performance highlights for Q2 FY27 
• Revenue from operations reached ₹905.6 crore in Q2 FY27 , delivering a robust 28% year -
on-year growth  despite the festive season demand being deferred to Q3 compared with 
the previous year. The Company's strategic focus on reducing off -season discounting and 
driving a healthier sales mix resulted in gross margin expansion of 310 basis points , 
improving to 31.1% from 28.0% in Q2 FY26, underscoring its commitment to sustainable and 
profitable growth  
 
• EBITDA for Q2 FY27 grew 41% year -on-year to ₹120.3 crore , from ₹85.4 crore in Q2 FY26. 
EBITDA margin expanded by 120 basis points  to 13.3% , compared to 12.1%  in the 
corresponding quarter last year, reflecting improved profitability and operating efficiency.  
 
• PAT for Q2 FY27 increased to ₹18.2 crore , compared with ₹17.2 crore  in the corresponding 
quarter of the previous year, registering a 6% year -on-year growth . While operating 
performance remained strong, PAT growth was impacted by elevated depreciation and 
amortisation charges associated with the Company's store expansion undertaken in 
recent years. These investments are expected to support future revenue grow th and 
strengthen market presence .

Performance highlights for H1 FY27 
• Revenue from operations stood at ₹ 1,902.8  crores , registering a growth of  42% on Y -O-Y 
basis. Gross Margin improved to 29.8% for H1 FY27 as compared to 28.7% for H1 FY26. 
 
• EBIDTA for H1 FY27 stood at ₹259.7  crores  as compared to ₹ 172.7  crores in H1 FY26, 
registering a growth of 50% on Y -O-Y basis. EBIDTA margin stood at 13.6% for H1 FY27 as 
compared to 1 2.9% for H1 FY26.  
 
• PAT for the H1 FY27 stood at ₹ 60.9 crores  as compared to ₹ 41.9 crores in H1 FY26, 
registering a growth of 43% on Y -O-Y basis.  
 
Key Updates for Q2 FY27: 
 
• As on September  30, 202 6, the Company operates 427 stores with a total retail area of 
~46.3 lac Sq. Ft.  The Company opened 49 stores  and closed 3 store s in H1 FY27.  
 
• SSSG for Q2 FY27 was  ~(14.9%) , largely reflecting the timing shift of festive demand from 
Q2 to Q3. On a normalized basis, however, the business delivered positive SSSG of 0.5% , 
demonstrating resilience in underlying consumer demand despite the calendar -related 
disruption. The deferred festive demand is expected to support performance in Q3 FY27.  
 
• Q2 FY27 witnessed a strong 31% growth in volumes , notwithstanding the festive season 
timing shift and lower discounting . The Company continued to prioritize value over volume, 
with full-price sales accounting for 91% of total sales , highlighting healthy consumer 
acceptance and supporting continued gross margin expansion.  
 
Key Updates for H1 FY27: 
 
• As on September  30, 202 6, the Company operates 427 stores with a total retail area of 
~46.3 lac Sq. Ft.  The Company opened 106 stores  and closed 4 store s in H1 FY27.  
 
• Same Store Sales Growth (SSSG) for H1 FY27 stood at (3.7%) , mainly impacted by the shift 
of the festive season from Q2 to Q3 compared to the previous year. The impact is timing -
related and expected to normalize in the following quarter  
 
• Robust volume growth of 43% in H1 FY27. The full price sales contributed 91% in H1FY27. 
 
Commenting on the results and performance, Mr. Ram Chandra Agarwal, Chairman & 
Managing Director said:  “The Company delivered a resilient performance during Q2 FY27, 
demonstrating the strength of its business model and its continued focus on profitable growth . 
Despite the timing impact arising from the shift of the festive season from Q2 to Q3, the 
Company achieved robust revenue growth of 28% , EBITDA growth of 4 1%, and expansion in 
both gross margins and EBITDA margins . The 31% volume growth  and 91% contribution from 
full-price sales  underscore the effectiveness of the Company's disciplined merchandising, 
pricing and inventory management strategies.

The operating environment continues to remain favourable, supported by increasing 
consumption across non -metro markets , rising penetration of organised retail , and growing 
consumer preference for quality products at compelling value propositions. The Company 
remains well positioned to capitalize on these structural growth opportunities through its 
differentiated value -fashion offering, strong brand equity, and expanding geographic 
presence.  
During the quarter, the Company continued to strengthen its retail footprint through the 
addition of new stores, guided by a disciplined approach towards location selection, store 
economics, capital allocation and return metrics. Investments in analytics -driven 
merchandising , agile supply chain capabilities , and operational excellence  continue to 
enhance scalability, improve responsiveness to consumer demand and support margin 
expansion.  
While reported Same Store Sales Growth was affected by the festive calendar shift, underlying 
demand trends remained healthy , and the Company expects the timing -related impact to 
normalize in the coming quarter. Looking ahead, management remains confident in the long -
term growth prospects of the business and will continue to focus on strengthening its product 
proposition , maintaining a compelling price -value equation , expanding its retail network 
judiciously , and driving operational efficiencies . 
With a strong foundation , healthy balance between growth and profitability , and a clear

expansion , and long -term value creation for all stakeholders ” 
 
About V2 Retail Limited :  
V2 Retail is among the fastest -growing value fashion retailers in India , with strong brand equity 
and a loyal customer base across diverse demographic segments. The Company offers a wide 
portfolio of apparel and lifestyle products , delivering quality fashion at affordable prices  under 
its core philosophy of “Value and Variety ”. 
 
V2 Retail has established a strong presence across Northern and Eastern India , with a focused 
strategy to serve the neo-middle -class population  in Tier II and Tier III cities . By offering well-
curated, trend -forward assortments  at accessible price points, the Company continues to 
address the evolving needs of India's aspirational consumers.
