Premier Energies Recognised as Grade A Manufacturer in Wood Mackenzie’s Global Solar 
PV Module Manufacturer Ranking 2026  
Hyderabad, June 4, 2026  
 
Premier Energies Limited , one of India’s leading integrated solar manufacturers, has been 
recognised among the  Top 25 Global  and Top 3 Indian Grade A Solar PV Module 
Manufacturers  in Wood Mackenzie’s Global Solar PV Module Manufacturer Ranking 2026 . 
This recognition underscores Premier Energies’ growing global presence, manufacturing  
excellence, technological capabilities, and reliability as a trusted solar PV module supplier.  
Wood Mackenzie assessed 48 module manufacturers across 10 countries, collectively 
accounting for 65% of global production capacity and 83% of global module shipments. 
Manufacturers were evaluated on a comprehensive set of criteria, including capacity 
utilisation, technology maturity, financial strength, supply chain resilience, ESG performance, 
product reliability, and research and development capabilities.  
The Grade A classification serves as an independent benchmark for developers, 
procurement teams, and asset owners seeking dependable solar module suppliers.  
Premier Energies currently operates  11.1 GW of annual solar PV module manufacturing 
capacity  and is on track to expand its  annual solar cell manufacturing capacity to 10.6 
GW later this year. The company is also executing a  ₹12,500 crore capital expenditure 
programme  aimed at strengthening upstream solar  manufacturing capabilities and 
diversifying into adjacent clean energy segments, including inverters, batteries, and 
transformers.  
Commenting on the recognition,  Mr. Chiranjeev Saluja, Managing Director, Premier Energies 
Limited , said:  
“Being recognised as a Grade A solar PV module manufacturer by Wood Mackenzie is a 
significant validation of Premier Energies’ manufacturing excellence, technological 
leadership, and long -term reliability as a solar partner. As global customers increasingl y 
assess suppliers based on quality, scale, financial strength, ESG performance, and supply 
chain resilience, this recognition reinforces our commitment to building a globally 
competitive, fully integrated solar manufacturing platform from India.”  
Premier Energies’ inclusion among the world’s leading Grade A module manufacturers 
further strengthens its position as a globally competitive Indian solar company and reflects 
India’s growing prominence in the global renewable energy manufacturing landscap e.
