Sub:   Memorandum of Understanding  (“MoU”)  
 
The Board of Directors of Nippon Life India Asset Management Limited (“NAM India/Company”) at its 
meeting held today i.e. on 13th November 2025, has authorised the Company to enter into a strategic 
collaboration with DWS Group GmbH & Co. KGaA (“DWS”), a leading European asset manager.  
 
The Parties intend to jointly develop and build a leading AIF franchise in India wherein DWS intends to 
acquire a minority stake of up to 40 percent in Nippon Life India AIF Management Limited  (“NAIF”) , a 
wholly owned subsidiary of  the Company, by subscribing to fresh issuance of equity shares of NAIF . 
 
In addition to the above, as part of a wider collaboration, NAM India and DWS will also work closely in 
other areas including passive investment products and global distribution.  
 
The Parties have accordingly entered into non -binding MoU to record their preliminary understanding 
and for the facilitation of further discussions with respect to the strategic alliance.  
 
This proposed arrangement / MoU  is subject to due diligence  exercise , execution of definitive 
agreements and receipt of various regulatory & other approvals , as may be required in this regard.
