Let me now take you through the key business, operational, and financial highlights for the half 
year ended 30th September 2026. Our market share by first-year premium income for six months 
ending September 30th, 2026, is 58.77% as compared to 59.41% for the similar period ended 
30th September 2025. We continue to maintain our leadership in both individual as well as group 
business within the Indian life insurance industry. 
Now, if you bifurcate this overall market share of 58.77% into segment-wise share of individual 
and group business, we would have a market share of 37.51% in individual business and 70.45% 
in the group business for the six months ending 30th September 2026. On a comparable basis, 
for the last year, first half year, the respective market shares for individual and group business 
were 37.21% and 72.74%, respectively. 
For the six months ended 30th September 2026, we have reported a total premium income of 
274,269 crore rupees as compared to 245,680 crore rupees for the six months ending 30th 
September 2025, registering a growth of 11.64% on a year-on-year basis. The individual new

The total annualized premium equivalent, APE, for six months ended 30th September 2026 is 
30,696 crore rupees, which comprises individual APE of 17,224 crore rupees and group APE of 
13,472 crore. Therefore, on an APE basis, the individual business accounts for 56.11%, while 
the group business accounts for 43.89%. Further, of the individual APE, the PAR business 
accounts for 11,364 crore rupees, and non-PAR amounts to 5,861 crore rupees. 
Our non-PAR share of individual APE is 34.02%, and PAR is 65.98% for the six months ended 
September 30th, 2026. As you may recall, for the six months ended 30th September 2025 last 
year, our non-PAR share of total individual business based on APE stood at 36.31%. 
Our protection business on APE basis has witnessed a growth of 58.59% on a year-on-year basis. 
This reflects our enhanced focus on growing the pure protection business. The profit after tax 
for the six months ended 30th September 2026 was 25,568 crore rupees as compared to 21,040 
crore rupees for the six months ended September 30th, 2025, registering a growth of 21.52% on 
a year-on-year basis. 
The net VNB has registered a growth of 33.95% on a year-on-year basis to 6,846 crore rupees 
for the six months ended 30th September 2026 from 5,111 crore rupees for the same period of 
last financial year. Further, the net VNB margin has improved by 470 basis points on a year-on-
year basis to 22.3% for the period ended 30th September 2026 from 17.6% for the same period 
of the last year ended 30th September 2025. 
The solvency ratio as on 30th September 2026 improved to 2.35 as against 2.13 on September 
30th, 2025. The Indian embedded value as on September 30th, 2026 has been determined as
