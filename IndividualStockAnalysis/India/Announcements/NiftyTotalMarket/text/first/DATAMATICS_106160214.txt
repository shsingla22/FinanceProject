 Datamatics Leadership Recognized at   
The Business Leader of the Year Awards 2025

recognition, with its Vice Chairman & CEO, Rahul Kanodia, and Lumina Datamatics 
Managing Director and CEO , Sameer Kanodia,  receiving the prestigious ‘CEO of 
the Year’ award at The Business Leader of the Year Awards 2025.  The recognition 
underscores their commitment to driving b usiness excellence, fostering innovation, 
and shaping a future -ready enterprise.  
 
For over 22 years, The Business Leader of the Year®  has honoured  industry pioneers 
who lead with vision, create sustainable business impact, and contribute to corporate 
respo nsibility. This recognition further reinforces Datamatics' legacy of leadership, 
growth, and transformation.
