New Delhi, June  1, 2024  
 
 
HERO MOTOCORP REGISTERS 4.98 LAKH UNIT SALES IN MAY  
2024   
 
CONTINUES TO WITNESS OVERWHELMING DEMAND FOR 
XTREME 125R  
 
 
Hero MotoCorp , the world ’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters, sold 498,123  
units of motorcycles and scooters in May  2024. Th e company had sold 519,474  units in May  
2023 .  
 
The company has sold 10,31,708  units in the first two months (Apr -May ’24) of the financial 
year FY’25, registering a growth of 12.6%  over the corresponding months (Apr -May ’23) 
when the company sold 915,581 units.  
 
Hero MotoCorp continues to focus on the premiumization  of its product portfolio, customer 
touch -points and overall brand experience. The company is also ramping  up production of 
the Xtreme 125R  to cater to the continued unprecedented demand for the stylish 
motorcycle , which has capture d the aspirational mobility needs of the gen -next customers.  
Hero MotoCorp registered a healthy growth in its export volumes during  the month and 
continues to see increased demand for the VIDA V1 electric scooter . The company is gearing 
up for an energetic upcoming period with a slew of product launches and business 
expansions.  
 
During the month, Hero MotoCorp launched the latest gene ration of the iconic Splendor – the 
Splendor+ XTEC 2.0  – celebratin g the 30th anniversary of the world ’s highest -selling 
motorcycle.  The premium and tech -laden Splendor keeps  its iconic design while integrating 
modern elements such as LED headlights with  HIPL (High Intensity Position Lamp), a unique 
H-shaped signature tail lamp  and multiple convenience & safety features . Showcasing its all-
around dominance, the motorcycle boasts excellent fuel  efficiency of 73 kmpl.  
 
Hero MotoCorp joined the ONDC Networ k, becoming the country ’s first auto company to 
join the network. Partnering with ONDC has further enhance d the company ’s reach, beyond 
its largest physical distribution and service network . The Open Network will initially offer 
two-wheeler parts, accessori es and merchandise. This has also reinforced the Digital India 
mission, contributed to the nation ’s economic growth, and revolutionized the digital 
automotive marketplace.
