Tata Motors registered 37% growth YoY with total sales of 39,641 commercial vehicle units in July 2026  
Mumbai, 1 August 2026: Tata Motors Limited sales in the domestic & international markets for July 2026 stood at 39,641 
units, compared to 28,956 units during July 2025, up 37% YoY.  
 
Category  July 2026  July 2025  Growth  
(Y-o-Y) 
HCV Trucks  8,973  6,735  33%  
ILMCV Trucks  6,100  5,068  20%  
Passenger Carriers  5,938  4,749  25%  
SCV cargo and pickup  12,865  9,880  30%  
Domestic Sales  33,876  26,432  28%  
International Business  5,765  2,524  128%  
Total  39,641  28,956  37%  
 
Domestic sales of MH&ICV in July 2026, was 15,435 units vs 12,378 units in July 2025 (Up 25% YoY).  
Domestic & International sales for MH&ICV in July 2026, was 16,364 units vs 13,669 units in July 2025 (Up 20% YoY).
