Puravankara Q2 Pre -sales up 62% YoY at ₹2,136 Crore  
Collections rise 24% to ₹1,301 crore; adds ₹14,100 crore GDV pipeline in H1FY27  
Bengaluru:  Puravankara Limited (NSE: PURVA | BSE: 532891), one of India’s most trusted and 
admired real estate developers, reported pre -sales of ₹2,136 crore in Q2FY27, up 62% year -on-year 
(YoY) from ₹1,322 crore in Q2FY26. Customer collections grew 24% YoY to ₹1,30 1 crore, up from 
₹1,047 crore in the same quarter last year.  
Sales area for the quarter stood at 1.83 million square feet (msft), up 22% YoY, while average price 
realisation improved 32% YoY to ₹11,651 per sq. ft. During the quarter, Puravankara handed over 977 
homes totalling 0.97 msft, bringing cumulative handovers for H1FY27 to 1,722 homes spanning 1.92 
msft. 
The company added 6.51 msft of potential developable area , with an estimated Gross Development 
Value (GDV) of ₹8,900 crore, in  Q2FY27. This brings total pipeline additions in H1FY27 to 10.74 msft, 
with an estimated GDV of ₹14,100 crore, across Bengaluru, Mumbai and Greater Noida.  
Commenting on the company’s performance, Ashish Puravankara, Managing Director, 
Puravankara Limited , said: “ Our focus is on scaling the business while maintaining discipline in 
pricing, capital deployment and execution. In Q2FY27, pre -sales grew 62% to ₹2, 136 crore, with 
collections up 24% and average price realisation up 32% . This performance supports our strategy of 
investing in differentiated developments in markets where we see enduring demand.  
In H1FY27 , we added development opportunities with an estimated GDV of ₹1 4,100 crore across 
Bengaluru, Mumbai and Greater Noida. We have been deliberate in selecting opportunities that offer 
both scale and long -term value.  
The priority now is to translate this development potential into launches, sales and cash flows. As we 
expand, we will remain selective about where we deploy capital and focused on execution.”  
Q2 Performance Summary  
● Pre-sales rose 62% year -on-year to ₹2,136 crore, compared to ₹1,322 crore in Q2FY26 and 
₹1,439 crore in Q1FY27  
● Customer collections rose 24% year -on-year to ₹1,301 crore, up from ₹1,047 crore in Q2FY26 
and ₹1,199 crore in Q1FY27  
● Sales area grew 22% year -on-year to 1.83 msft, compared to 1.50 msft in Q2FY26 and 1.36 
msft in Q1FY27  
● Average price realisation improved 32% year -on-year to ₹11,651 per sq. ft, compared to ₹8,814 
per sq. ft in Q2FY26 and ₹10,589 per sq. ft in Q1FY27  
Further to the definitive agreement executed in Q1FY27, the company  completed the sale of its 
commercial property, Purva Zentech, to an entity backed by ICICI Prudential AMC on September 30, 
2026. The transaction values the asset at an enterprise value of ₹625.94 crore and was executed 
through the transfer of the Company’s entire shareholding in P urva Ruby Properties Private Limited .

Business Development in FY27  
Puravankara added projects with a cumulative development potential of 10.74 msft and an estimated 
GDV of ₹14,100 crore in H1FY27. Of this, Q2FY27 additions accounted for 6.51 msft and ₹8,900 crore 
in estimated GDV, following Q1FY27 additions of 4.23 msft a nd ₹5,200 crore.  
Q2FY27 Additions  
• South -East Bengaluru:  Entered into a joint development for a 7.83 -acre land parcel with a 
potential GDV of over ₹1,100 crore and a developable area of 0.89 msft.

and an estimated GDV of ₹2,600 crore.

About Puravankara Limited  
The Puravankara Group is one of India's most trusted realty majors, headquartered in Bengaluru with a pan -India 
presence. Over the past five decades, the company has catered to the full spectrum of housing and plotted 
development needs. The group has also forayed into developing Grade A commercial real estate, with a presence 
of approximately 3 msft, and is rapidly expanding its footprint. Additionally, its interior design arm, Purva Streaks, 
serves customers seeking an integrated interior design solution.  
As of June 30, 2026, Puravankara has completed 97 projects totalling approximately 59 msft across nine cities: 
Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Coimbatore, Mangaluru, Kochi, Mumbai, Pune, and Goa. The company's total land 
bank is approximately 40 msft, and o ngoing projects total 35.14 msft.
