Firstsource named Horizon 3 Market Leader in HFS Horizons: 
Customer Experience Services, 2026  
 
Mumbai, India | Octo ber 20, 2026:  Firstsource Solutions Limited  (NSE: FSL, BSE: 532809), a global intelligence 
partner and an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company, has  been recognized as a Horizon 3 Market Leader in HFS 
Research's HFS Horizons: Customer Experience Services, 2026 report . The assessment evaluated the  capabilities of 
33 service providers, including CX specialists, global system integrators, and consultancies across the HFS customer 
experience services value chain.  
 
Horizon 3 – Ecosystem Synergy and New Value Creation  recognizes providers 
that move beyond functional and end -to-end experience transformation to create new 
sources of value through ecosystem collaboration, Services -as-Software models and 
capabilities spanning talent, domain, technology, data and change.  
 
HFS describes Firstsource as "AI -native intelligence rooted in 25 years of CX 
operations." Its assessment highlights Firstsource's Kairos operating system, Agentic 
AI Studio and Intelligent Context Framework as key elements of its approach to AI -
native CX transformation.  
 
Ashish Chawla, Head – CX and Consulting, Firstsource,  said, “We built Kairos on 
a simple premise: AI creates enterprise value only when intelligence is connected to 
the work itself. But ultimately, the value of that intelligence is experienced by the customer, in how quickly they get an 
answer, how easily they resolve an issue, and how relevant and seamless the experience feels. By bringing together 
our deep domain and operations expertise with AI, data and technology, we’ve built an operating system that 
continuously learns from every interaction to improve both customer experience and business outcomes.  HFS’ 
recognition of Firstsource in Horizon 3 is important independent validation of that approach.”  
 
Melissa Fersht, Executive Research Leader, HFS Research,  said, “Firstsource is pairing AI -native intelligence with 
25 years of CX operations expertise, delivered through its Kairos operating system that aims to make one partner 
accountable for transformation, implementation, and operations. That construct, backed by owned IP from Agentic AI 
Studio to the Intelligent Context Framework, earned Firstsource its Horizon 3 (Market Leader) placement in the HFS 
Horizons: Customer Experience Services, 2026 report, positioning the firm to orchestrate customer experience across 
the enterprise, not just manage a slice of it."  
 
Turning intelligence into better customer experiences  
 
For Firstsource, the value of AI -native CX is ultimately measured by what the customer experiences: less waiting, faster 
resolution, easier digital journeys, fewer handoffs and more relevant interactions.  
 
HFS’ 2026 research points to a shift in enterprise CX buying. As GenAI and agentic AI become increasingly embedded 
in customer operations, enterprises are looking beyond technology capability toward partners that can take 
accountability for business outcom es. HFS identifies proprietary data, owned IP, domain expertise, operating models 
and measurable value delivery as increasingly important differentiators.  
 
The report also highlights the gap between buyer appetite for outcome -based models and current adoption: 76% of 
enterprise CX buyers are open to outcome -based or gainshare pricing, while only 5% of contracts use those 
models today.  
 
This is the operating challenge Kairos, Firstsource’s operating system for Intelligence That Operates , was built to 
address.  It brings together domain knowledge, operational data, technology and AI engineering across  transformation, 
implementation and live operations . By learning from customer interactions and the decisions made around them, it is 
designed to make each subsequent interaction smarter and the overall customer experience more consistent over time.
