COMPANY STATEMENT  
 
U.S. FDA Completes Pre-License Inspection at Biocon’s Biosimilars 
Site at Biocon Park, Bengaluru, India  
 
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, April 30, 2026  
 
The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has completed a Pre-License Inspection 
(PLI) at Biocon’s biosimilars site at Biocon Park in Bengaluru, India. The inspection was 
conducted between April 20, 2026, and April 29, 2026, and covered 3 Biologics  
Manufacturing units, 5 Quality testing laboratories and 2 Warehouses.  
 
At the conclusion of the inspection, the U.S. FDA issued a Form 483 with five 
observations . These observations are procedural in nature and do not pertain to data 
integrity or quality oversight. There were no repeat observations noted . The Company  
will submit a comprehensive Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) plan within the 
stipulated timeline and is confident all observations will be addressed fully and 
expeditiously.  
 
Biocon remains committed to upholding the highest standards of Quality and 
Compliance and working collaboratively with global regulatory agencies to ensure the 
safety, efficacy, and reliability of its products.  
 
 
– Company Spokesperson
