We refer to the above-mentioned disclosures made by the Company in connection with the 
Preferential Issue of 33,28,686 convertible warrants on a Private Placement basis. 
 
We wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Company, via a resolution passed through 
circulation on November 19, 2026, has approved the allotment of 33,28,686 (Thirty-Three Lakh 
Twenty-Eight Thousand Six Hundred and Eighty-Six) share warrants (“Warrants”) to Minerva 
Ventures Fund on Preferential basis through Private Placement. 
 
Key details of the allotment are as follows: 
 Each Warrant is convertible into, or exchangeable for, 1 (one) fully paid-up equity share of the 
Company of face value of ₹10/- each at a price of ₹645/- each (including a premium of ₹635/- 
per warrant). 
 The allotment has been made upon receipt of the upfront subscription amount of ₹161.25 per 
Warrant, equivalent to 25% of the total issue price. 
 The Warrants are convertible into an equal number of equity shares upon receipt of the balance 
75% consideration within the stipulated timeframe.
