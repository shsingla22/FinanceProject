Noida, 10th September 2024 – RateGain Travel Technologies Limited, a global leader in AI -
powered SaaS solutions for the hospitality and travel industry, announced today that its Founder 
and Managing Director, Bhanu Chopra, has been elected as Co -Chair of ASSOCHAM’s National 
Council  on Travel & Tourism. With his extensive expertise, Bhanu is deeply committed to 
integrating AI into travel, aligning his efforts with government initiatives that position India as a 
leader in AI -driven solutions. Bhanu’s passion for combining technology w ith tourism will help 
redefine visitor experiences, drive innovation, and support the nation's goal of making travel a 
key economic pillar.  
 
ASSOCHAM’s National Council on Travel & Tourism, under the leadership of its current Chairman, 
Sunjae Sharma, MD -  India & Southwest Asia, Hyatt India Consultancy Pvt. Ltd., collaborates 
closely with the Central and State Tourism Ministries. This council addresses pressing concerns 
and promotes sustainable development within the travel, tourism, and hospitality sector  
The travel and tourism industry, a vital contributor to India’s economy, is undergoing rapid 
transformation. This sector contributed 7.7% to India's GDP in 2023 and has the potential to 
become a $3 trillion economy by 2047, capable of generating 200 millio n jobs. Bhanu’s election 
as Co -Chair comes at a time when India is capitalizing on its tech talent and tourism potential, 
positioning him to lead initiatives that attract more tourists to India.  
 
“I am honored to take on the role of Co -Chair for ASSOCHAM’ s National Council on Travel & 
Tourism,” said Bhanu Chopra, Founder and Chairman of RateGain Travel Technologies , on 
his appointment. “Tourism has a huge multiplier effect on a nation’s economy, as one direct job 
in tourism can create multiple indirect job s. Government of India envisions harnessing tourism 
and artificial intelligence (AI) as dual engines to drive India's economic growth, positioning the 
country as a global leader in both sectors. With this ethos, we can harness this potential to drive 
susta inable growth, create new opportunities, and contribute significantly to the development of 
India’s travel and tourism sector.”  
 
As Co -Chair, Bhanu Chopra will leverage his extensive experience to lead initiatives that address 
the evolving needs of the tra vel industry, ensuring it remains resilient and competitive on the 
global stage.
