PRESS RELEASE 
 
NTPC GREEN ENERGY LIMITED (NGEL) inks MoU with BIHAR STATE POWER 
GENERATION COMPANY LIMITED (BSPGCL) 
 
NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL), a subsidiary of NTPC Limited, signed a Memorandum of 
Understanding (MoU) with Bihar State Power Generation Company Limited (BSPGCL) for 
development of Battery Energy Storage Projects and Renewable Energy Project for 
decarbonization and energy transition in the state of Bihar.  
The MoU was signed on 26th July’2025  between Sh. Sarit Maheswari, CEO (NGEL & NREL)  
and Sh. Abdesh Kumar Singh, Director Technical (BSPGCL), shared in the presence of the 
Hon’ble Minister of Energy, Planning & Development of Govt. of Bihar Sh. Bijendra Prasad 
Yadav, Hon’ble Minister of Industries of Govt. of Bihar Sh. Nitish Mishra , ACS, Department 
of Industries,  Secretary Department of Energy and other senior officials from Government of 
Bihar.
