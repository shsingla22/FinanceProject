PRESS R ELEASE  
 
Brigade Group Signs Agreement to Develop a Third World Trade Center (WTC) Tower at 
Infopark Kochi  
In the Presence of CM Shri Pinarayi Vijayan  
 
Bengaluru, June 19, 2024: Leading real estate developer, Brigade Group, announced it would develop a 
third tower of the World Trade Center (WTC) at Infopark Kochi. The company signed a land lease 
agreement today at a ceremony in the presence of Shri Pinara yi Vijayan, the Hon’ble Chief Minister of 
Kerala; Shri P. Rajeeve, Minister for Law, Industries and Coir; Dr Rathan U. Kelkar, State IT Secretary; 
Susanth Kurunthil, CEO -Kochi Infopark; M.R. Jaishankar, Executive Chairman -Brigade Enterprises 
Limited ; Nirupa Shankar -Joint Managing Director, Brigade Enterprises Limited and other Infopark 
officials.  
 
The Rs 150 -crore project will be developed by Brigade Group. The new tower will be 16 storeys high with 
a car -park of six decks; it will come up on the non -SEZ plot with a built -up space of 2.6 lakh square feet. 
This new tower of WTC will generate around 2 700 direct jobs and the project is expected to complete 
in three years.  
 
Chief Minister Shri Pinarayi Vijayan said 583 new companies have set up offices in Infopark since 2016, 
generating 70,000 jobs. "The coming up of the third tower of WTC will augment the development of not 
just Infopark, but the entire IT sector in the stat e," he noted.  
 
Mr M R Jaishankar, Executive Chairman -Brigade Enterpr ises Limited , said, “We are happy to increase 
our footprint in Kerala with a new tower of WTC for the growing IT and IT -enabled service companies 
in the State. We already have two WTC towers completed with a combined built -up area of 7.7 lakh 
sq.ft, and the office spa ces are completely occupied. The A and B blocks of WTC, with Grade A 
specification and LEED Gold certification, house 37 companies and 8,000 employees in total. Brigade is 
keen to expand in Kerala and we will continue to look for viable land parcels.”  
 
Shri Kurunthil, while speaking of an increasing demand for non -SEZ buildings, said the new building can 
meet the requirements of prospective entrepreneurs. “The workspace will be a major attraction for big 
companies,” he added.  
 
Brigade has also developed the luxury business hotel Four Points by Sheraton Kochi Infopark, which is 
located in Infopark Phase 1. Furthermore, the company is developing Brigade Square in Trivandrum, a 
Grade A office building located in Technopark Phase 1.  Brigade Group is the license owner of six WTCs 
in South India - Bengaluru, Chennai, Kochi, Hyderabad, Trivandrum and Devanahalli. Over the last 38 
years, the Brigade Group has completed around 300 buildings and 90 mn sqft across its diverse portfolio.  
 
About Brigade Group:   Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers 
with over three decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and 
winning their customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark buil dings and transformed skylines 
across cities – Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Gift City -Gujarat, Thiruvananthapuram,
