Press Release 
NALCO Signs MoU with RUSAL for Collaboration in 
Scandium Recovery from Red Mud 
Bengaluru/Bhubaneswar, 18.11.2026:  National Aluminium Company Limited (NALCO), the 
Navratna CPSE under the Ministry of Mines, Government of India, has signed a Memorandum 
of Understanding (MoU) with the RUSAL for collaboration in the recovery of the critical mineral 
Scandium from Red Mud. The MoU was signed on the sidelines of the 44th International 
Conference and Exhibition ICSOBA 2026 being held in Bengaluru on 17th November 2026. The 
signing took place in the presence of Shri Brijendra Pratap Singh, CMD, NALCO, Shri Jagdish 
Arora, Director (Projects & Technical), NALCO, Mr. Viktor Mann, Technical Director, RUSAL, 
and other senior officials of both organisations.  
As India intensifies efforts towards securing strategic mineral resources for emerging 
technologies and clean energy applications, the partnership is expected to facilitate knowledge 
exchange and evaluation of viable pathways for scandium recovery from Red Mud. Worth 
mentioning that RUSAL is among the leading company in the global aluminium industry and the 
world’s largest producer of low-carbon aluminium, has developed technology for the extraction 
of scandium from red mud, a by-product generated during alumina production, thereby 
presenting valuable opportunities for technology sharing and cooperation in the critical minerals 
sector.
