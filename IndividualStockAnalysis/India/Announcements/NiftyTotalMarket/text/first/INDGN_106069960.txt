Information  Note    
 
Indegene Awarded  Silver Medal , Scores 72/100 in ESG Assessment by Eco Vadis 
 
Bengaluru, India, November  26, 2024: Indegene, a digital -first life sciences commercialization 
company, announced  that it has achieved a score of 72/100 on Eco Vadis, the global authority on 
Environmental, Social, and Governance ( ESG) ratings. Further , Indegene has been awarded  the 
Silver Medal in recognition of the company’s sustainability achievement.  
 
Indegene is now in the top 15% (93rd percentile) of all companies rated by Eco Vadis, an 
organization that has rated over 130,000 companies across 220+ industries in over 180 countries. 
This achievement strengthens Indegene’s resolve to further improve its ESG policies and practices, 
while aligning with the sustainability aspirations of all stakeholders.  
 
Further, Indegene is committed to ensuring its ESG goals are closely aligned with those of its client 
organizations, to be a true partner  on their sustainability journey . 
 
With 6 operation hubs and 18 offices across North America, Europe, and Asia, Indegene’s client 
base includes the world’s top 20 biopharma companies, many emerging biotech firms, and medical 
device companies. This vast global footprint underscores Indegene’s ability to scale and meet the 
evolving needs of the world’s leading pharma organizations  across markets .
