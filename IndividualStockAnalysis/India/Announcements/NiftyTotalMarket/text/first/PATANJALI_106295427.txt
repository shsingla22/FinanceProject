Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), and with reference to 
intimation filed by the Company  on March 13, 2025,  informing  about a Prohibition order of sale of 
Patanjali Organic Wild Honey of a Batch , passed by Office of  Administration of Union Territory of 
Ladakh office of the Assistant Commissioner / Designated Officer , Food Safety,  Drugs & Food 
Control Organization , Kargil, UT Ladakh.  The said prohibition order was subject to review in case 
the opinion of the appellate laboratory (Referral Laboratory) differs from the opinion of the Food 
analyst of the Primary lab.  
 
In continuation to the above, we may inform you that  the Office of  Administration of Union 
Territory of Ladakh , Office of the Assistant Commissioner /Designated Officer , Food Safety,  Drugs 
& Food Control Organization , Kargil, UT Ladakh  vide its order dated July 09, 2025 , revoked  with 
immediate effect, the order of Prohibition of the sale of Patanjali Organic Wild Honey  batch upon 
receipt of Referral Laboratory  test report confirming that the Patanjali Organic Wild Honey  batch 
complies with Food Safety and Standards (Contaminants, Toxins and Residues) Regulations, 
2011 and is safe for consumption .
