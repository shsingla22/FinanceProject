“NTPC Renewable Energy Limited Wins 1000 MW in UPPCL’s Solar PV Power Project Auction”  
4th  January 2025  
NTPC Renewable Energy Limited (NTPC REL), a wholly owned subsidiary of NTPC Green Energy Limited 
(NGEL), has emerged as a successful bidder in the e-reverse auction conducted by Uttar Pradesh 
Power Corporation Limited (UPPCL). The tender, aimed at the "Selection of Solar Power Developers 
for Setting up 2000 MW ISTS-connected Solar PV Power Projects in India under Tariff-Based 
Competitive Bidding," conducted on 3rd  January 2025. 
NTPC REL secured a capacity of 1000 MW at an impressive tariff of Rs. 2.56/kWh. The Letter of Award 
(LOA) from UPPCL is awaited.
