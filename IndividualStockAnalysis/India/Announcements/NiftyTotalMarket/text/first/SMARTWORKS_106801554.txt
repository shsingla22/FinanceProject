Smartworks Announces Managed Office Deals Worth ~INR 305 Crore from New Clients  
• The new sign -ups are in addition to the  recently announced ~INR 235 crore contracted rental 
revenue from existing client expansions  
• The incremental contracted rental revenue is in addition  to the Company’s ~INR 5,400 crore 
contracted rental revenue as of June 30, 2026  
 
Gurugram, India, 30 September 2026: Smartworks Coworking Spaces Limited (“Smartworks” or “the 
Company”), India’s largest managed office platform by overall footprint, announced the signing of new 
leasing engagements with large corporate clients  across multiple cities, adding ~INR 305 crore in 
incremental contracted rental revenue . The value represents leasing rental revenue from engagements of 
up to 60 months, and builds on the Company's ~INR 5,400 crore contracted rental revenue base as of June 
30, 2026.  
This follows Smartworks' recent announcement of ~INR 235 crore in incremental contracted rental revenue 
from existing client expansions. The new clients comprise a diverse mix of large Indian and global 
corporates across sectors, including a technology solutions company backed by a Fortune 500 and Forbes 
Global 2000 parent, a research and consulting major, a healthcare company, a sportswear  and lifestyle 
brand, and a Global CXM services provider, among others.  
Together, the two announcements represent ~INR 540 crore in incremental contracted rental revenue, 
reflecting both the addition of new clients and the deepening of existing relationships, and further 
strengthening the sustained momentum in demand for the Company's managed campuses.  
Commenting on the development, Neetish Sarda, Founder and Managing Director, Smartworks, said: 
“India is witnessing a fundamental shift in how businesses think about their workplaces. As companies enter 
new markets and scale across regions, the office can no longer be a constraint on growth; it has to be an 
enabler of it. What large corporates want today is speed, flexibility and the freedom to focus their capital 
and attention on their  core business rather than on running real estate. This is why managed workspaces 
have moved decisively from being an alternative to becoming the default choice. We see this shift 
accelerating, and we are building Smartworks to lead it.”  
Smartworks serves over 760 clients, with ~92% of revenue coming from large corporates, reflecting its 
position as their preferred workspace partner across geographies. The Company has also secured its 
expansion pipeline in prime locations for FY27 and FY28 , and partially for FY29, with capacity locked well 
ahead of demand.  
The Company strategically secures long -term leases for large, full campuses in prime business locations 
across high -demand cities. Beyond office space, its campuses offer cafeterias, convenience stores, gyms, 
recreation zones, crèches and round -the-clock s upport, so companies and their employees have everything 
they need in one place.  
 
About Smartworks Coworking Spaces Limited   
Smartworks is India’s largest managed office platform by overall footprint, with ~16.9 million square feet 
across 70 centres in 15 cities across India and Singapore, as of June 30, 2026. The Company partners with 
 
   
 
real estate developers to transform large, bare -shell assets into fully managed, enterprise -grade campuses. 
Smartworks primarily serves mid -to-large enterprises and counts 760+ clients, including Fortune 500 and 
Forbes 2000 companies, MNCs, leading Indian conglomerates and unicorns. The Company also offers 
SmartVantage, a GCC -focused solution combining scalable campuses with a curated ecosystem of 
partners across legal, compliance, talent, and operational support, enabling rapid and compliant expansion 
in India. Smartworks listed on NSE and BSE on 17 July 2025.
