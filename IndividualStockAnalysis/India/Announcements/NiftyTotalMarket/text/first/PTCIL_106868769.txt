PRESS RELEASE
PTC Industries Secures Major Indian Defence
Order for 400 Tonnes of Titanium Products,
Strengthening India's Defence Self-Reliance
Lucknow, India – 23 November 2026 – PTC Industries Limited, a leading Indian manufacturer of advanced materials and
high-performance engineering components, has secured a contract from the Indian Defence Sector for the supply of
approximately 400 tonnes of Titanium ingots, bars and rods.
The contract represents a significant addition to the Company’s defence order book and reinforces its position as a
domestic supplier of strategically important Titanium products for critical defence applications. The materials supplied
under the contract are intended to support defence systems requiring high strength, corrosion resistance, dimensional
stability and long-term operational reliability under demanding service conditions.
Titanium is recognised as a strategic engineering material across the defence, aerospace, marine and nuclear sectors
owing to its exceptional mechanical properties, low density and superior resistance to corrosion. Establishing indigenous
manufacturing capabilities for such critical materials is fundamental to strengthening India’s strategic supply-chain
resilience and reducing dependence on imports for advanced defence applications.
The order is aligned with the Government of India’s ongoing efforts to promote indigenous manufacturing under the
Atmanirbhar Bharat initiative and supports the broader objective of increasing domestic value addition in defence
production. By expanding the indigenous availability of high-performance Titanium products, the contract contributes to
the development of a robust domestic ecosystem for strategic materials and advanced manufacturing technologies.
PTC Industries, through its wholly owned subsidiary Aerolloy Technologies Limited, has made significant investments in
developing advanced Titanium and Superalloy manufacturing capabilities in India. These investments encompass
specialised alloy melting and ingot production, forging, the development of mill products, precision casting, inspection
and testing, supported by advanced metallurgical expertise and quality assurance systems. By progressively integrating
critical stages of the manufacturing value chain, the Group is building greater control over material quality, traceability,
manufacturing processes and supply reliability. These capabilities are aimed at meeting the stringent requirements of
defence and aerospace applications and strengthening India’s indigenous advanced materials manufacturing base.
Beyond its commercial significance, the contract is expected to contribute to India’s advanced manufacturing ecosystem
by supporting technology development, expanding specialised industrial capabilities, creating opportunities for skilled
employment and strengthening domestic supply chains for critical materials. Such initiatives are integral to achieving
greater self-reliance in strategic manufacturing while enhancing the global competitiveness of India’s defence industry.
The contract also reflects the increasing participation of Indian industry in developing an indigenous defence
manufacturing base and underscores the importance of sustained collaboration between the armed forces and
domestic manufacturers in advancing national capabilities across strategically important sectors.

ABOUT PTC INDUSTRIES LIMITED
PTC Industries Limited is an Indian advanced manufacturing company specialising in critical materials, high-integrity
precision castings and complex engineering components for aerospace, defence, space, energy and other technology-
intensive industries.
Through its wholly owned subsidiary, Aerolloy Technologies Limited, the Group is developing an integrated manufacturing
platform for Titanium and Superalloy materials, including advanced alloy melting, ingot production, forging, rolling and mill
products, alongside precision investment castings, special processes and high-precision machining capabilities.
Guided by its PTC ONE™ — From Melt to Mission™ strategy, the Group is focused on bringing together advanced materials,
metallurgy and manufacturing technologies within an integrated industrial system. Its long-term objective is to establish
globally competitive capabilities in critical materials and mission-critical components, supporting India’s strategic self-reliance
while serving the requirements of international aerospace, defence, space and energy industries.
