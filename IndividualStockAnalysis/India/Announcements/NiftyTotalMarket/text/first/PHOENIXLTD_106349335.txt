Intimation on Tax Deduction at Source (TDS) / withholding tax on  Dividend  for Financial Year 2024 -25 
 
August 22, 2025  
  
Dear Member,  
 
We are pleased to inform you that the Board of Directors at its Meeting held on April 30, 2025, have 
recommended a Final Dividend of Rs. 2.50 per equity share (125%) of face value of Rs.2/ - each for the 
financial year ended March 31, 2025. The  dividend, as  recommended by the Board, if declared at the 
ensuing 120th Annual General Meeting (“AGM”), will be paid to eligible shareholders within 30 days of the 
date of its declaration.  
 
As you may be aware, in terms of the provisions of the Income Tax Act, 1961, ("IT Act") as amended by the 
Finance Act, 2020, dividend paid or distributed by a Company on or after April 1, 2020 is taxable in the 
hands of the members. The Company shall there fore be required to deduct tax at source at the time of 
payment of dividend to shareholders at the applicable rates.
