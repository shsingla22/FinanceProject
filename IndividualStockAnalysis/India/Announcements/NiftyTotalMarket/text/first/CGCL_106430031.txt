 
 
November 6, 2025  
 
Subject: Intimation of allotment of Equity shares against exercise of vested Employee Stock Options 
under ESOP Scheme of the Company.  
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 10(c) of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Share Based Employee Benefits 
and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021, (“SEBI ESOP Regulations”), we hereby inform you that that the 
Management  Committee on Thursday , November 06 , 2025 had allotted 5,18,840 Equity shares of Rs. 1/- 
each pursuant to exercise of Employee Stock Options under the ESOP Schemes of the Company.  
 
The paid -up share capital of the Company has accordingly increased from Rs. 96,16,35,122  consisting of 
96,16,35,122  equity shares having a face value of Rs. 1/- each to Rs. 96,21,53,962  consisting of 
96,21,53,962  equity shares having a face value of Rs. 1/- each  
 
Further, we would like to confirm that the above allotted shares shall rank pari -passu with the existing 
equity shares of the Company and the Company is in the process of completing the other formalities with 
respect to listing of the said allotted shares.  
 
The details as required as disclosure under Part E, Regulation 10(c) of SEBI ESOP Regulations are enclosed 
as “Annexure -A” 
 
The aforementioned information is also available on the Company’s website at www.capriloans.in   
 
You are requested to take the same on record.   
 
Thanking you,
