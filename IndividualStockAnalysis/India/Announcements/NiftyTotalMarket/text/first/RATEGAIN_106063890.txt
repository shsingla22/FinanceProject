Noida, 19th November 2024 : RateGain Travel Technologies Limited (RateGain), a global 
provider of AI -powered SaaS solutions for the travel and hospitality industry, announces the 
appointment of Mukesh Kumar as its new General Counsel and Company Secretary, joining as 
Senior Vice President - Legal & Compliance. In this role, Mukesh will oversee and strengthen 
RateGain’s legal and compliance framework, guiding the organization throug h evolving 
regulatory landscapes and supporting its ambitious global growth.  
 
With over two decades of experience, Mukesh brings valuable expertise from prominent 
companies such as PVR INOX Limited, NIIT, and Hero Motors Limited, among others, where he 
has led legal and compliance teams through complex regulatory and corporate governance 
landscapes. He is well -versed in areas including public issues, private equity investments, joint 
venture transactions, and managing complex mergers. In his new role, Mukesh  will support 
RateGain by providing multi -disciplinary guidance to the Board of Directors and management on 
all secretarial, legal, and compliance matters across India and globally.  
 
“We are delighted to have Mukesh as General Counsel and Company Secretary ,” said Bhanu 
Chopra, Founder and CEO,  of RateGain . “His appointment reinforces RateGain’s commitment to 
building a robust legal and compliance structure that will support our continued global expansion 
and help us navigate the complexities of internationa l markets.”  
 
“I’m excited to join RateGain at such a dynamic stage in the company’s journey,”  said Mukesh 
Kumar, Senior Vice President - Legal & Compliance, RateGain. “I look forward to working 
closely with the team to strengthen RateGain’s legal foundation, navigate the evolving global 
landscape, and contribute to the company’s next phase of growth and success.”  
 
Mukesh has received several industry recognitions, including Indian Achiever’s Award in 2022 
and Golden Pinnacle Award in 2023.  He is als o an Associate Member of the Institute of Company 
Secretaries of India. An ardent movie enthusiast, Mukesh enjoys traveling to new destinations to 
immerse himself in diverse cultures and engage with interesting people.
