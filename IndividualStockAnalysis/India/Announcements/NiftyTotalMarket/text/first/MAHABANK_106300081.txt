FINANCIAL RESUL TS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED 30th  JUNE , 2025 
 
Results at a Glance  
Quarter ended 30th June, 2025 vis-a-vis Quarter ended 30th June, 2024 
 
➢ Total Business grew by 14.63 % to ₹ 546,143 crore.  
➢ Total Deposits up by 14.07 % to ₹ 305,046 crore.  
➢ CASA improved to  50.07 % of Total Deposit.  
➢ Gross Advances increased by 15.34 % to ₹   241,097  crore.
