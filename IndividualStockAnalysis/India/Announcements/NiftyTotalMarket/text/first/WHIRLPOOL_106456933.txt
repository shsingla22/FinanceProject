Item 8.01. Other Events
On November 27, 2025, Whirlpool Corporation’s (the “Company”) wholly-owned subsidiary, Whirlpool Mauritius Limited (“Seller”), executed the sale
of 14,255,000 equity shares of the Company’s publicly listed Whirlpool of India Limited subsidiary (“Whirlpool India”) via an o n-market trade. The
transaction reduces Seller’s ownership in Whirlpool India from 51% to approximately 40%, and generated gross sales proceeds of approximately
$166 million on settlement, which occurred on November 28, 2025. The Company continues to evaluate all transaction options to f urther reduce its
equity stake in Whirlpool India in line with its prior-stated equity reduction goal by the end of the first half of 2026. The C ompany expects to use
transaction proceeds to reduce debt.
