Sun Pharma Establishes Endowed Professorship at Mount 
Sinai to Advance  Dermatologic Care  
• Endowment will fund academic leadership in dermatology in perpetuity, supporting 
research, education and mentorship  
• Mark G. Lebwohl, MD, installed as inaugural professor in recognition of his leadership 
and contributions to dermat ologic care  
MUMBAI, India and PRINCETON, N.J., October 6, 2026 –  Sun Pharmaceutical Industries 
Limited (Reuters: SUN.BO, Bloomberg: SUNP IN, NSE: SUNPHARMA, BSE: 524715 (together 
with its subsidiaries and/or affiliated companies, “Sun Pharma”) ) today announced the 
establishment  of the Sun Professorship in Dermatology at the Icahn School of Medicine at 
Mount Sinai. Mark G. Lebwohl, M .D., will be  installed as the inaugural professor at a ceremony 
held today at Hatch Auditorium at The Mount Sinai Hospital in New York.  
Supported by a $2.5  million endowment from Sun Pharma, the Sun Professorship will fund 
academic leadership in dermatology in perpetuity, beginning with Dr. Lebwohl as its inaugural 
incumbent . Based within the Kimberly and Eric J. Waldman Department of Dermatology at the 
Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, the professorship will advance research and training, 
helping translate emerging knowledge into better care and prepare future leaders in the field.  As 
the second -largest pharmaceutical derm atology company in the U.S. by prescription  volume,  
Sun Pharma is committed  to advancing patient  care through scientific innovation, education and 
partnerships .  
“At Sun Pharma, we believe m eaningful progress in dermatology  care requires supporting the 
people and institutions advancing the field today and in the future ,” said Ahmad Naim, M.D., 
Senior V ice President and Chief Medical Officer , North America. “The establishment  of this 
professorship reflects our long -term commitment  to fostering  the academic leadership needed to 
pursue new ideas and strengthen care for generations to come.”  
Dr. Lebwohl serves as Dean for Clinical Therapeutics at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount 
Sinai and Professor and Chairman Emeritus of the Kimberly and Eric J. Waldman Department 
of Dermatology. Internationally recognized for his contributions to psoriasis research and 
treatment, Dr. Lebwohl has helped advance understanding of inflammatory skin diseases  and 
played a key role in shaping standards of care that have improved patient out comes worldwide. 
His work as a clinician, investigator and mentor has influenced the future generation of 
dermatologists  and helped accelerate innovation across the field. Dr. Lebwohl is Chairman 
Emeritus of the Medical Board of the National Psoriasis Foundation and served as President of 
the American Academy of Dermatology from 2015 to 2016.  

"Mount Sinai and Sun Pharma share a longs tanding commitment to advancing care for patients 
with serious skin diseases. This professorship reflects a shared belief that investing in scientific 
excellence and academic leadership ultimately leads to better outcomes for patients around the 
world,” said Emma Guttman, M .D., Ph.D., Waldman Professor and System Chair of the 
Kimberly and Eric J. Waldman Department of Dermatology at the Icahn School of Medicine at 
Mount Sinai. "Dermatology has undergone a remarkable transformation over the past several 
decades, and continued progress depends on supporting the next generation of clinicians, 
investigators, and innovators. Sun Pharma's generosity will help sustain that momentum and 
strengthen the future of our specialty."  
 “Dr. Lebwohl’s influence on dermatology can be seen not only in his contributions to research 
and clinical care, but also in the many physicians and investigators he has guided throughout 
his career,” said Eric J. Nestler, M.D., Ph.D., Dean of the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount 
Sinai and Chief Scientific Officer of the Mount Sinai Health System. “The Sun Professorship is a 
fitting recognition of his impact on the field, and we are grateful to Sun Pharma for making this enduring academic honor possible.”  
 
“I am deeply honored to be named the inaugural holder of the Sun Professorship in 
Dermatology,” said Dr. Lebwohl. “The greatest value of this professorship is the opportunity it 
creates to pursue important questions, support promising investigators and help turn scientific 
advances into better care for patients.”
