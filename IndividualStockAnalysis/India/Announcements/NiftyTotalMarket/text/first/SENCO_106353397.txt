B. Brief Proceedings of the AGM  
 
Mrs. Ranjana Sen, Whole -time Director & Chairperson  chaired the meeting and  welcomed all  the members at the 
31st AGM  of the Company . She further welcomed the Board of Directors, KMP , Representative of Statutory 
Auditors  and the Secretarial Auditor  present at the AGM.  
 
She informed that: 
• the Statutory Registers and the necessary documents as mentioned in the Notice  of the 31st AGM , were 
kept open for inspection during the meeting . 
 
• 3 valid proxies representing 13 Equity Shares of the Company  were  received . Further,  441 no. of 
shareholders  (including promoter and promoter group)  holding  105449051  equity shares of the Company 
were present in person.  
 
The requisite quorum being  present  the Chairperson  called the meeting to order. She further requested                          
Mr. Suvankar Sen,  Managing Director & CEO,  to start the proceedings of the meeting  and to conduct the same 
on her behalf .  
 
Mr. Suvankar Sen  welcomed all the members.  Thereafter , Mr. Suvankar Sen and Mrs. Joita Sen delivered their 
formal address . Mr. Suvankar Sen briefed the members and gave an overview and highlights o n the performance 
of the Company during the FY  2024-25. 
 
With the consent  of the members the notice dated 25th July 2025  was taken as read.  The members were informed 
that there were  no qualifications  in the statutory audit and secretarial audit reports of the company. Therefore, 
 
 
pursuant to Section 145 of the Companies Act, 2013, it was not required to be read.  
 
He further inform ed that pursuant to the provisions of Companies Act, 2013 and SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirement) Regulations, 2015 the Company had provided the facility of remote e -voting on all the 
Resolutions, as set out in notice calling the 31st Annual General Meeting. The remote e -voting commenced at 9.00 
A. M on Sunda y, 24th August  2025 and ended at 5.00 P. M on Tues day, 26th August  2025.  
 
The Member s were  also informed that the Company has provided facility to those members , who were u nable to 
vote during the remote e -voting period , to cast their vote  through ballot during the continuance of the meeting.  
 
Mr. A tul Kumar  Labh, Proprietor of M/s A. K. LABH & C o, was appointed as the Scrutinizer to scrutinize the
