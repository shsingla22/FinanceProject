PRESS RELEASE  
 
The Anup Engineering’s  consolidated total  revenue stood at Rs 339.1 C rores  for the Half year 
ended September 30, 202 4, higher  by 27.9 % compared to Half year ended  September  30, 202 3.  
EBIDTA  for the H1 FY25 is Rs. 76.3  Crores  higher  by 28.5 % compared to H 1 FY2 4, whereas PAT  for 
the H1 FY25  is Rs. 56.6  Crores higher  by 40.4 % compared to H1 FY24 
 
   
Ahmedabad, October  26, 2024: The Anup Engineering Limited (ANUP), announced its unaudited 
financial results for the  quarter and Half year ended on September  30, 202 4.  
  
• Revenue from operation of Q 2 FY25 at Rs 193.1 Cr with EBIDTA of Rs 43.3  Cr (22.4%)  and 
PAT of Rs  32.5 Cr (16.8%) 
• Revenue from operation of H1 FY25 at Rs 339.1 Cr with EBIDTA of Rs 76.3 Cr (22.5%) and 
PAT of Rs 56.6 Cr (16.7 %) 
• Order book stood at Rs 876 Crores as on  30th September  2024 and INR 932 Crore as on 
26th October 2024.
