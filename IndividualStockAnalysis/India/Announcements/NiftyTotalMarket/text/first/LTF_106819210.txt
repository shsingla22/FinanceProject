The Board of LTF, one of the leading Upper -Layer Non -Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) in India, 
today announced the financial results for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026. As a part of its next 
5-year strategic plan Lakshya 2031 is steadily w orking towards transitioning to an AI -native Retail financial 
services institution through cutting -edge in -house AI & Deep Tech stack. The Company offers Farmer 
Finance, Rural Business Finance, Two -wheeler Finance, Personal Loan, Home Loan and Loan Against  
Property (LAP), SME Finance, and Gold Finance. With a robust business model and super -fast solutions 
development cycle, LTF is dedicated towards building a high -quality risk calibrated retail lending franchise 
with equal dominance in urban and rural segments.  
 
Commenting on the financial results , Mr. Sudipta Roy, Managing Director & CEO, LTF , said, " Q2FY27 
has been yet another strong quarter for L&T Finance with strong delivery across all business parameters, 
laying a solid foundation for our Lakshya 2031 journey with a sustained business momentum.  These 
outcomes reflect the strength of our diversified retail franchise, the discipline of our execution and our 
continued focus on delivering sustainable, risk -calibrated growth. Despite uncertainties in the 
macroeconomic environment , inflation pressures, and seasonal headwinds, our resilient business model 
enabled us to navigate these macro dynamics effectively while expanding key focus verticals like Gold 
Finance, Micro LAP, Personal Loans, and Two -Wheeler Finance.  Our approach will continue to be guided 
by disciplined underwriting, strengthened risk guardrails, granular portfolio monitoring and a strong 
collections framework. We believe that responsible growth and superior portfolio quality must go hand in 
hand.  
 
As we progress on our Lakshya 2031 journey, we remain focused on strengthening our granular distribution 
channels, expanding our branch footprint and enhancing our digital acquisition capabilities. Our 
investments in artificial intelligence, particularly t hrough our AI -powered credit administration  and portfolio 
management frameworks are expected to further enhance credit decision -making, identify early warning 
signals and drive greater operational efficiency. These capabilities will help us build a more agile, scalable 
and resilient organisation.  
 
Our priority remains to sustain profitable growth, improve returns and strengthen the quality of our 
franchise. We are confident that our investments in technology, distribution and risk management will help 
us navigate evolving market conditions while pro gressing towards our long -term strategic ambitions.”  
 
Developing  digital  finance  delivery  as a customer  value  proposition  
 
In Q2FY27, the Company’s flagship PLANET app reinforced its status as a core digital engine for the 
Company - surpassing 2.7 crore downloads . The application now services 94% of the total service requests 
generated by its customers  and has driven collections of Rs. 2,168 Crore during the quarter, coupled with 
a business generating of Rs. 5,934 Crore in business.  
 
Further strengthening its rural reach, PLANET app introduced a dedicated rural interface with a clear, 
intuitive and clutter -free UI, offering multilingual capabilities, including Hindi. This enables rural customers 
  
 
PUBLIC  to navigate the app more easily, access services in a familiar language and complete transactions with

Partner PLANET  
 
The Company’s Partner PLANET app has emerged as a  dedicated digital platform designed to strengthen 
engagement with its dealer network by bringing key business information, retail performance insights and 
operational services onto a single interface. The platform enables dealers to access business metric s, 
monitor retail performance, manage Trade Advance requirements and track operational requests, 
enhancing transparency, convenience and efficiency across the dealer ecosystem.  
 
Outcome of AI Implementations  
L&T Finance continues to advance its Lakshya 2031 ambition of becoming a risk -first, technology -first and 
AI-native retail financial services institution.  The Company is embedding intelligence across customer 
acquisition, underwriting, portfolio management, servicing and cross -selling.  
• Project Hercules – Advancing agentic AI -led cross -selling:  The Company has launched Phase 
1 of Project Hercules, its agentic AI -based cross -sell platform, in beta during Q2FY27. Powered by 
a new Lead Management System (LdMS ), the platform is live for upsell and cross -sell opportunities 
across urban products, with rural products targeted for introduction by mid -November 2026.  
 
• AI Citizen – Embedding an AI -first culture across the organisation:  Extending AI adoption 
beyond technology teams, the Company has launched its AI Citizen initiative to enable employees 
to build and deploy customised  AI agents for their functional requirements. The initiative has already 
engaged over 1,200 employees, generated more than 21,000 chat sessions and enabled the creation 
of over 5,000 agents. With leadership training and an expanding network of AI Champions , the 
programme is designed to deepen organisation -wide AI adoption, strengthen employees’ technology 
capabilities and drive productivity through function -specific applications.  
 
• In-house developed AI solutions and Co -pilots driving operational efficiencies: Co-pilots at 
LTF provide speed and deepen human decisions across underwriting, fraud detection, servicing and 
collections.  
o Project Helios , the underwriting co -pilot for SME, Home Loan and Personal Loan 
businesses, has processed approximately 80,000 files , while reducing underwriting 
turnaround time from 21 to 16 hours for Self -Employed Professionals (SEP) and from 37 to 
22 hours for Self -Employed Non -Professionals (SENP).  
o Project Orion , the Nostradamus portfolio -management co -pilot, has facilitated 
approximately 5,000 conversational self -service business queries, enabling real -time 
portfolio insights and report generation.  
o Project ShighraM , the mortgage legal process automation co -pilot, has processed over 
9,500 files across 11 vernacular languages. The Mortgage Policy and Pricing Assistant 
(MOPPA) handles over 240 queries per day across 15 vernacular languages . 
o Project Argus , the SME fraud -screening co-pilot, has processed approximately 9,700 files.  
Collectively, these solutions are helping improve decision consistency, accelerate processes and 
make AI -enabled capabilities accessible across the organisation.

About L&T Finance Ltd. (LTF):  
 
L&T Finance Ltd. (LTF) (  www.LTFINANCE.com  ), is a leading Non -Banking Financial Company (NBFC), offering a 
range of financial products and services. Headquartered in Mumbai, the Company has been rated ‘AAA’ — the highest 
credit rating — by four leading domestic rating agencies. LTF is also rated by International Rating Agencies. S&P 
Global Ratings has affirmed LTF with  long-term Issuer Credit Rating at “BBB/Stable” and short -term issuer credit rating 
at “A-2”. Fitch Ratings has affirmed LTF Long -Term Foreign and Local -Currency Issuer Default Ratings of “BBB -” with 
a Stable outlook.  It has also received leadership scores and ratings by global and national Environmental, Social, and 
Governance (ESG) rating  providers for its sustainability performance. The Company has been certified as a Great 
Place To  Work® and has also won many prestigious awards for its flagship CSR project – “Digital Sakhi” - which focuses 
on women's empowerment and digital and financial inclusion. Under Right to Win, being in the ‘right businesses’ has 
helped the Company become one of the leading financiers in key Retail products. The Company is focused on 
transforming into a Risk -first, Technology -first, AI -native Retail financial services institution as part of the Lakshya 2031 
plan. The Company has over 3 Crore customer database, which is being leveraged to cross -sell, up -sell, and identify 
new customers.
