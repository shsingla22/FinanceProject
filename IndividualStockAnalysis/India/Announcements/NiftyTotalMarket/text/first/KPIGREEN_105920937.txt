Sub.: Intimation of receipt of Letter of Award (‘LOA’) for 100MW AC Solar Power Project from 
Maharashtra State Power Generation Co. Ltd. (MAHAGENCO)  
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
In continuation of our earlier intimation bearing Ref.: No. KPI/MAT/MAR/2024/484 dated March 
16, 2024, regarding our successful bid in the MAHAGENCO tender, we are pleased to inform you 
that KPI Green Energy Limited has received a Letter of Award (LOA) fro m MAHAGENCO. This 
award is for the development of a 100MW AC Solar Power Project, which includes an EPC package 
with land and three years of Operation and Maintenance (O&M). The project is part of the tender 
for the development of a cumulative capacity of 600 MW AC Solar-PV Power Projects at various 
locations in Maharashtra under the RE Bundling Scheme, as per RFX No. 3000042146 issued by 
MAHAGENCO.  
 
This significant achievement underscores our commitment to advancing renewable energy 
initiatives and supporting the state's energy needs through sustainable and clean energy 
solutions. Moreover, this project represents our strategic expansion beyond Gujar at into 
Maharashtra, allowing us to leverage new opportunities and bring our solar power expertise to a 
broader geographical area, thereby reinforcing our position as a leading player in the renewable 
energy sector.
