Sub:  Intimation regarding re -pricing of stock options under RattanIndia Enterprises Limited Employees 
Stock Option Plan – 2022  
Dear Sir/Madam,  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
and the applicable provisions of the SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 
2021, we wish to inform you that the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of the Board of Directors 
of the Company , at its meeting held on 10th March, 2026 , has approved the re-pricing of 54,00,000 existing 
granted unexercised stock options  under the RattanIndia Enterprises Limited Employees Stock Option 
Plan – 2022 (“REL ESOP 2022”) . 
The stock options were originally granted on December 18, 2025  at an exercise price of Rs. 40.18 per 
equity share . Considering the ongoing market price of the equity shares  of the Company , the Committee 
has approved the revision of the exercise price of such unexercised options to Rs. 24.82 per equity share , 
which is the closing market price of the Company’s equity shares on the National Stock Exchange of India 
Limited as on March 9, 2026 , being the previous trading day’s closing price at the stock exchange  having 
the highest trading volume.  
The vesting schedule and other terms and conditions of the stock options shall remain unchanged  and 
shall continue to be governed by the provisions of REL ESOP 2022.  
The aforesaid re -pricing has been approved in accordance with the provisions of REL ESOP 2022  and the 
applicable regulatory framework.  
The meeting of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee commenced at 03:00 P.M. and concluded 
at 03:10 P.M.  
You are requested to kindly take the above information on record.
