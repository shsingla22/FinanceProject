Hon. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh Visits AXISCADES’ Stall at Aero India 2025  
Bengaluru, India, February 1 1, 2025 – AXISCADES Technologies Limited  a leading Aerospace and 
Defence technology solutions provider , had the  pleasure of  hosting Honourable  Defence Minister Rajnath 
Singh ji at its stall  on the inaugural day of  Aero India 2025 .  
The Defence Minister saw the company's indigenously developed defence products and solutions at display, 
such as Anti-drone Systems, Heavy Payload and Weaponized Drones , Unmanned Ground Vehicles , Direct 
RF, A dvanced Radar, Sonar and Electronic Warfare Systems , various LRUs for Tejas, and Test Benches 
which are currently deployed across the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force.  Noticed  
Dr. S ampath  Ravi narayanan , Chairman of AXISCADES Technologies Limited, expressed his gratitude for 
the Defence Minister's visit and recognition.  He stated , "We are honoured  by the Defence Minister's visit . It 
further motivates us  to develop solutions  that meet the evolving needs of our defen ce forces  and fulfil 
Honourable Defence Minister’s vision  for Atmanirbharta, modernization  and intelligent warfare in defen ce 
technolog ies.”
