workforce solutions , announced its  Q2 FY27 financial results today.

➢ Revenue at  ₹4,396  crore, up by 15% YoY

➢ Board Announces Interim Dividend of ₹ 5 per Share

Commenting on the performance , ED & Group CEO , Mr. Lohit Bhatia said, “We are pleased to report a 
good result for Q2 , with revenue growing by 15% YoY to ₹4, 396 crore and adj. EBITDA rising by 20% YoY 
to ₹92 crore . Our PAT and EPS grew 44%  YoY, reaching ₹75 crore and ₹5  respectively , while delivering  a 
ROE of 25% . 
The Board has approved an interim dividend of ₹ 5 per share, reaffirming our co mmitment to creating 
sustainable value  for our  shareholder s. 
This quarter’s performance was marked by a significant milestone as our headcount crossed 500 k, driven 
largely by headcount  additions  of over 20k  in our General Staffing business. The segment delivered robust 
performance, adding 116 new client logos and achieving 15% YoY revenue growth and 23% YoY EBITDA 
growth, positioning us strongly for the quarters ahead.

 Professional Staffing recorded 13% YoY revenue growth, supported by sustained hiring demand from 
Global Capability Centres (GCCs).   
Despite geopolitical challenges in the Middle East, our Overseas Business maintained momentum, 
delivering 10% YoY revenue growth and 23% YoY EBITDA growth.  This performance was driven by higher 
margins in the Middle East, while Malaysia, Singapore , and Philippines contributed to revenue growth.  
On the people front, Quess was recognised among the Best Workplaces in the Middle East 2026, ranking 
40th overall and 32nd among the Best Workplaces for Women in the Middle East, reinforcing our 
commitment to building an inclusive and empowering workplace. ” 
 
Social Impact and CSR : As part of its commitment to creating a lasting societal impact through healthcare, 
Quess Corp, through its foundation and alongside Fairfax Financial Holdings, supported the establishment 
of Christian Medical College (CMC) Vellore’s new 346 -bed Paediatric  Specialty Hospital at its Ranipet 
Campus.

About Quess Corp  
Established in Bengaluru in 2007, Quess Corp Limited (BSE: 539978, NSE: QUESS) is India’s largest and a 
global leader in staffing and workforce solutions. Quess leverages deep domain expertise and AI -driven 
digital platforms to help businesses enhance prod uctivity and build a future -ready workforce.  Its 
comprehensive suite of technology -enabled staffing and managed outsourcing services spans key sectors 
including BFSI, Retail, Telecom, Manufacturing, IT, and GCCs. Today, Quess has a workforce of 
approximat ely 5,04,000 employees across 8 countries and serving over 2, 300 clients.    
 
Ranked #19 among India's Best Workplaces in 2025, Quess Corp was certified a Great Place to Work for 
the seventh consecutive year in India in 2026, with Quess Singapore earning the recognition for the third 
consecutive time and Quess UAE receiving it for t he first time. Staffing Industry Analysts (SIA) has recognised 
Quess Corp as India’s No.1 staffing company in 2025 & 37th globally by revenue in 2024. Additionally, it 
became the first Indian firm to join the World Employment Confederation in 2025. In just 1 9 years, Quess has 
grown from a start -up to a trusted global workforce leader.   
 
Quess Corp holds a "Strong" ESG rating of 66/100 from CRISIL and high credit quality with an [ICRA]AA 
(Stable) / A1+ status, reflecting robust financial health and enhanced sustainability commitments.
