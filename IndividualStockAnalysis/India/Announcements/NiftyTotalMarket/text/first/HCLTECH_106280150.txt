HCLTech expands partnership with Salesforce to accelerate enterprise adoption of 
Agentic AI with new services  
 
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, June 26, 2025 —HCLTech , a leading global technology company, 
announced the launch of its orchestration consultation and implementation services designed to accelerate enterprises’ adoption of Salesforce Agentforce across various industries,  
including financial services, healthcare, retail and manufacturing, helping them transform into 
AI-augmented enterprises.  
 With a consulting -led approach, HCLTech supports rapid adoption of enterprise -grade 
agentic workflows that unify task orchestration,  reasoning and action execution across 
systems, streamlining marketing, sales, service and operations in highly regulated environments.  
 
HCLTech leverages protocols , including the Agent -to-Agent (A2A) protocol and the Model 
Context Protocol (MCP), to help  clients streamline coordination, task tracking and visibility and  
drive faster and more reliable outcomes.  
 “Salesforce Agentforce represents a transformational shift in how enterprise value is created. 
At HCLTech, we are not just enabling agent deploymen t, we are scaling business outcomes 
through intelligent, governed and connected agent ecosystems,” said  Sadagopan Singam, 
Executive Vice President, Global Head of SaaS and Commercial Application, Digital Business 
Services, HCLTech.  
 
“Organizations need a n ew labor model that unlocks the full potential of humans with AI at 
work. HCLTech is a critical partner for identifying and developing specific use cases with our joint customers across industries,” said Phil Samenuk, SVP of Global Alliances & Channels and  
Outsourcing Service Providers, Salesforce.  
 
These innovations support HCLTech’s enterprise AI approach and its FY26 strategy for 
delivering Total Experience , reinforcing its leadership in multi -agent AI innovation.
