 ITC HOTELS SIGNS DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT FOR  STRATEGIC ACQUISITION OF A 130 KEY OPERATING 
HOTEL IN AHMEDABAD’S VIBRANT COMMERCIAL HUB  
 
ITC HOTELS LIMITED (Company) is pleased to announce the signing of a definitive agreement for  strategic acquisition of 
a hotel  situated within Ahmedabad's vibrant commercial hub. ITC Hotels entered the agreement for acqui ring a 100% 
stake in GHK Hospitality and Infrastructures Ltd  (GHK) that owns Welcomhotel Ahmedabad ('Hotel'). The Hotel is 
currently operated by the Company under an Operating Services Agreement. The transaction will be completed at an 
Enterprise Value of Rs. 155 crores, on a debt -free and cash -free basis, subject to customary adjustments under the 
definitive agreement. The acquisition is expected to be consummated within the current quarter.  
 
The 130 -room Upper Upscale business Hotel has spacious rooms, 8,500 sq.ft of banqueting spaces, a restaurant serving 
the finest Indian and global cuisines and a rooftop swimming pool. The addition of this Hotel would further strengthen  
the Company’s owned asset portfolio in Ahmedabad by straddling across all the branded hospitality market segments 
and price points. This value -accretive acquisition  would reduce time -to-market and optimiz e capital efficiency vis -à-vis 
a greenfield  inve stment and would provide  an opportunity to the Company to capitalize on the diversified year -round 
demand emerging from the city's expanding corporate footprint, industrial corridors, MICE and Sports events.  The 
transaction  would  be EPS  accretive  for the Company in the first full  year of the Hotel ’s operation s post  the acquisition .  
 
Speaking on the development, Anil Chadha, Managing Director, ITC Hotels Limited said, " The acquisition of this Hotel  
will mark  another milestone in pursuit of the Company’s growth aspiration by adding another owned property in one of 
the fastest growing urban centres  of India. Transitioning this established 130- room property from a managed contract 
to full ownership allows us to unlock greater value from the asset. Ahmedabad continues to be one of India’s most 
dynamic commercial and industrial centres , supported by significant infrastructure investments, strong long term 
demand fundamentals, and a diversified demand base. The hotel's prime location, established brand positioning and 
consistent occupancy levels provides an attractive opportunity to enhance yields and cash flow generation over the long 
term.”    
 
The Hotel is  strategically located along Ahmedabad’s prominent Ashram Road, overlooking the scenic Sabarmati 
Riverfront and is in proximity to Ahmedabad International Airport as well as the Narendra Modi Stadium.  Ahmedabad is 
an emerging metropolitan hub and amongst India’s top eight urban economies by GDP supported by prominent 
corporates, industrial corridors like Dholera, GIFT City and Sanand.  
 
ITC Hotels Limited recently acquired The Zuri Kumarakom, Kerala Resort & Spa , a luxury resort in a high growth leisure 
destination.  
 
Both these moves underscore ITC Hotels commitment to strengthening its presence in key commercial and leisure 
destinations nationwide, reinforcing its position as a leading force in the industry's dynamic growth trajectory.
