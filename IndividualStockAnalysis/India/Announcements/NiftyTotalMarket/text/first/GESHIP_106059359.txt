PRESS RELEASE  
G E Shipping delivers its 2005 built Suezmax Crude Tanker  
“Jag Lalit” 
 
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (G E Shipping) delivered its 2005 built Suez max Crude Tanker  
“Jag Lalit” to the buyers . The vessel was contracted for sale in September 2024.  
Excluding Jag Lalit, company’s  current fleet stands at 40 vessels, comprising 27 tankers (5 Crude carriers, 
18 Product carriers, 4 LPG carriers) and 13 dry bulk carriers (2 Capesize, 8 Kamsarmax, 3 Supramax) 
aggregating 3.15 Mn dwt.  
Additionally, the company  has contracted to sell one MR product tanker in Oct 2024 , which is  due for 
deliver y by H2 FY25.
