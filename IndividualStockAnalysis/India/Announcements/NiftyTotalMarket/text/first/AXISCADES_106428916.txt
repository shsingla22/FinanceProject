AXISCADES and Electronic Bird Control Sign  
MoU to Deploy Falcon -Inspired Drones in India  
 
Bengaluru , India / Nimes , France  – 6th November , 2025:   AXISCADES Aersopace & 
Technologies Limited, a leading Indian Defense  Technology solutions provider  & wholly owned 
subsidiary of AXISCADES Technologies Limited (BSE: 532395 | NSE: AXISCADES) , and Electronic 
Bird Control  an innovative  French d rone  company, have formalized a strategic Memorandum 
of Understanding (MoU) to bring the E -Raptor , a state -of-the-art drone, ingeniously crafted to 
resemble a falcon , to the Indian market for both military and civilian applications.  
The E- Raptor whi ch is one of t he ﬁrst of its kind in the world  merges cutting -edge biomimetic 
engineering with advanced UAV technology, oﬀering exceptional stealth, agility, and 
performance in surveillance and bird control operations. Its realistic falcon -inspired design 
ensures operational eﬀectiveness across diver se terrains, supporting missions ranging from 
defense  reconnaissance to essential civilian initiatives like airport safety and wildlife 
management.  
Ravikumar Joghee, Chief Strategy and Marketing Oﬃ cer of AXISCADES, commented, “We are 
delighted to join hands with Electronic Bird Control to introduce the E -Raptor in India. This 
partnership reﬂects our dedication to innovation and our drive to provide solutions that meet the complex demands of both defense  and civilian sectors. We are committed  to localise e -
raptor drone production in India as part of the Make in India initiative. ” 
Adrien  Laﬀon,  CEO, Electronic Bird Control, added, “Our alliance with AXISCADES marks a 
pivotal advancement in drone technology and international collaboration. The E -Raptor’s 
lifelike design and advanced features will raise the bar for surveillance and safety standards 
across India.”  
By formalising this strategic alliance, both AXISCADES Technologies Limited and Electronic Bird 
Control are demonstrating a uniﬁed commitment to providing state -of-the-art defense  
solutions. The partnership not only highlights their mutual vision for excellence but also 
supports the ongoing modernisation of India’s defense  forces, ensuring that the country 
remains at the forefront of global security standards.

About Electronic Bird Control (EBC):  
Founded in 2019, Electronic Bird Control specializes in the prototyping and development of 
drones to combat bird nuisance. The company is specialized in the design of bio -mimetic 
vectors. Leveraging its expertise,  EBC develops and operates drones that resemble birds of 
prey through their E -Raptor range. Thanks to onboard technology and the expertise of the 
management team, the E -Raptor retains the natural eﬃciency , capable of reproducing all the 
movements of a falcon in hunting action  without the constrai nts associated with its use.
