MUMBAI, November  06, 202 6 
Hindalco Industries Limited, the Aditya Birla Group metals flagship, surpassed its previous  
performance benchmarks, delivering  record Revenue and Net Profit  driven by a strong  
performance across its business segments.  
Consolidated EBITDA for the second quarter stood at ₹13,75 7 crore, registering a growth of  
42% year-on-year. Quarterly Net Profit was ₹ 7,533 crore  compared to ₹4,741  crore in the same 
quarte r last year .  
This record performance was underpinned by the strength of Hindalco’s integrated business 
model  led by  a solid primary upstream business complemented by strong  contribution s from 
its downstream business. The Copper business reported its highest -ever quarterly EBITDA, 
while Novelis sustained improved performance building on  Oswego ’s normalisation . Together,  
the non-LME-linked  businesses  contributed almost 50% to the company’s  earnings reflecting 
sustain able growth despite LME volatility and global macroeconomic headwinds.

Commenting on the results, Mr. Satish Pai, Managing Director, Hindalco Industries, 
said,   
“Hindalco delivered its strongest -ever quarterly performance, with Revenue and PAT 
surpassing their previous highs. Our long -term strategy of building an integrated business 
model across upstream metals and downstream value -added products is translating int o a 
stronger and more resilient earnings profile.  
While the Aluminium Upstream business registered an EBITDA growth of 55%, our non -LME-
linked businesses also recorded EBITDA growth of nearly 50%, supported by stable 
operations at Novelis and a record quarter in the Copper Downstream, and Aluminium 
Downstream businesses.  
Looking ahead, the commissioning and ramp up of Bay Minette, copper recycling, alumin ium 
battery foil, Aditya FRP  and Inner Grooved Tubes  will further accelerate the share of our non -
LME-linked businesses. On the upstream front, our aluminium refinery and smelters, copper 
smelters and coal mine projects remain on track. Together, these investments reinforce 
Hindalco’s position as an integrat ed global metals company, combining upstream strength 
with downstream growth to deliver sustainable, long -term value in all market cycles.”

About Hindalco Industries Limited  
Hindalco Industries Limited is the metals flagship company of the Aditya Birla Group.                  
A $31 billion metals  powerhouse , Hindalco is the world’s largest aluminium company by 
revenues, and the world’s second largest Copper rods manufacturer (outside China).  
Hindalco operates across the value chain, from bauxite mining, alumina refining, coal mining, 
captive power plants and aluminium smelting to downstream rolling, extrusions, and foils. 
Along with its subsidiary Novelis, Hindalco is the global leader in flat  rolled products and the 
world’s largest recycler of aluminium.  
Hindalco is India’s largest copper producer, serving more than half the country’s copper 
requirements. Its copper facility in Gujarat, India, comprises a world -class copper smelter and 
refinery complex, downstream facilities, and a captive jetty.  
Hindalco’s global footprint spans 48 manufacturing units across 10 countries. Hindalco has 
been ranked the world’s most sustainable aluminium company in the Dow Jones 
Sustainability Indices (DJSI) for six consecutive years – 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 an d 
2025.
