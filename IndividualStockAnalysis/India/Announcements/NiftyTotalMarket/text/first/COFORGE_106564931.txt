Coforge modernizes its global employee experience by adopting 
ServiceNow’s AI -powered HR Service Delivery p latform  
Greater Noida, India, March 24, 2026 – ServiceNow  (NYSE: NOW), the AI platform for business 
transformation,  and Coforge Limited  (NSE: COFORGE), a global digital services and solutions 
provider, today announced the successful global rollout of the  ServiceNow AI -powered HR 
Service Delivery (HRSD) platform  at Coforge .  
By adopting ServiceNow internally as a customer, Coforge has modernized and enhanced 
the employee experience for its 35,000+ global workforce, marking a significant milestone in the company’s enterprise- wide AI and digital transformation journey.  
The GenAI -enabled HRSD platform redefines how HR services are delivered, accessed, and 
scaled across Coforge. Leveraging intelligent automation, intuitive self -service, and AI -driven 
insights, the platform accelerates service delivery, improves employee sa tisfaction, and 
enables a more consistent and connected experience across geographies.  
As part of the implementation, Coforge has unified critical HR processes including onboarding, background verification, learning, and performance management through 
standardized workflows and deep integrations with learning platforms, verification systems, and core HR tools. The company has further extended the platform by building AI agents that enable predictive onboarding, personalized learning journeys, automated goal- setting 
workflows, and real -time performance analytics —helping deliver faster turnarou nd times, 
stronger compliance, and improved operational efficiency.  
This internal transformation directly supports Coforge’s high- growth trajectory and 
execution- driven culture. As the organization continues to expand across industries and 
regions, the ServiceNow -powered HRSD platform provides a scalable, AI -first foundati on that 
allows HR teams to focus on high- value initiatives while maintaining execution rigor and 
operational excellence.  
The unified HRSD portal empowers employees with seamless, self -service access to HR 
support, while equipping HR leaders with enhanced visibility into service performance, request volumes, and employee journeys. With data -led insights and proactive 
decision- making capabilities, Coforge is driving a modern, employee- centric HR experience 
aligned with its global workforce needs.  
 Ashish Kumar, SVP- Cloud Business, Coforge, said “At Coforge, ServiceNow is not just a 
platform we implement for customers,  but it is also a platform we use internally to run and 
scale our own enterprise. Our adoption of ServiceNow AI -powered HR Service Delivery 
 
(HRSD) demonstrates how global organizations can modernize employee experience while 
maintaining execution discipline at scale.  
HRSD has transformed HR delivery through GenAI -enabled workflows, intelligent self -service, 
and AI agents  by simplifying complex HR operations while keeping employees at the center. 
The same architecture and operating model used at Coforge can be seamlessly extended to global  enterprises, providing a scalable foundation for organizations navigating rapid 
growth and geographic complexity. ” 
Adrian Johnston, President APAC Sales, ServiceNow, said “Coforge is setting a new 
benchmark for how fast -growing enterprises can use AI to deliver a modern, connected 
employee experience. By adopting the latest ServiceNow AI capabilities, Coforge has created an HR ecosystem that is proactive, personalized, and built for global scale. This implementation shows how AI -powered workflows can transform business operations, 
accelerate decision- making, and deliver measurable business impact for organizations that 
value speed, precision, and real -world  business  impact. ” 
The new platform enhances global consistency, reduces dependency on manual processes, and accelerates service timelines across HR, IT, Workplace Services, and Legal. As a result, employees can receive timely, unified support across all functions they interact with.  
ServiceNow, a leader in AI -led business transformation with people at the center, continues 
to shape how modern enterprises evolve. As an Elite ServiceNow Partner, Coforge saw the 
ServiceNow HR Service Delivery (HRSD) platform as the clear and strategic choice for building its new g lobal HR system.  This rollout reinforces Coforge’s position as an AI -first 
enterprise and establishes a scalable, high- performance HR operating model designed to 
support the company’s continued growth and long -term execution strategy.
