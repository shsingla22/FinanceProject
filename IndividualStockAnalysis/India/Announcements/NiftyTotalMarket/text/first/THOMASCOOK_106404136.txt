Thomas Cook India appointed e xclusive Transport  & Accommodation 
partner for 11th edition of Asian Aquatic Championships  
 
Mumbai,  October 10, 2025 : Thomas Cook (India) Limited -  India’s leading omnichannel travel services 
company has signed an agreement  as the exclusive partner  (Accommo dation & Transport)  for the Asian 
Aquatic Championships 2025.  This Olympic -style, multi -sport  extravaganza has been organized by the 
Swimming Federation of India (SFI)  and supported by the Sport Authority of Gujarat  (SAG ), mar king it 
India’s first -ever hosting of the Asian Aquatics Championships — a significant milestone for Indian sports.  
 
Thomas Cook India’s MICE team will  deliver end- to-end management across key portfolios of 
accommodation, transport , logistics, airport arrivals and F&B coordination for a  strong 1100+  contingent, 
including over  650+  athletes, 60+ support staff  and dignitaries from 29 Asian nationalities, competing 
acros s Swimming, artistic swimming, diving and water polo at the Veer Savarkar Sports Complex, 
Ahmedabad.  
 
To ensure a smooth and world -class experience, the Thomas Cook India MICE team  has set up a 
dedicated 40+ memb er team  to manage operations across 6 hotel locations  over a duration of 15 days.  
 
• Airport Arrival & Departure Services  
Thomas Cook’s dedicated airport operations team will oversee the entire arrival and dep arture 
experience, providing 24/ 7 on- ground assistance at both international and domestic terminals in 
Ahmedabad. Exclusive parking bays for cars and coaches are arranged for immed iate transfers, 
while SFI, S AG and Thomas Cook  teams will be stationed at  terminals to ensure smooth arrivals,  
efficient customs facilitation  and seamless transport connectivity.  
 
• Logistics Management  
To ensure s eamless mobility and on- time delivery , Thomas Cook India has deployed premium 
coaches and cars, and carefully pre- planned shuttle services.  A 24/7 logistics control centre 
ensures smooth coordination and on- ground efficiency throughout the event.  
 
• Accommodation & F&B Management  
Thomas Cook India, in collaboration with leading hotel partners, has curated exceptional Indian and 
international  culinary experiences for guests . The menu includes diverse v egetarian, vegan, halal, 
kosher  and gluten- free options to cater to specific dietary preferences, complemented by a selection 
of Asian, Middle Eastern and continental  cuisines designed to offers a variety for participants and 
delegates.  
 
Mr. Rajeev Kale, President & Country Head –  Holidays, MICE, Visa, Thomas Cook (India) Limited  
said, “The Asian Aquatics Championships celebrates Asia’s  top athletes on a global stage. I t is truly an 
honour for us at Thomas Cook Indi a to be selected  as the exclusive MICE partner across key elements 
of accommodation and transport, especially as India hosts  the Ch ampionships for the first time.  
This collaboration reiterates Thomas Cook India’s leadership in the MICE sector and highlights our 
expertise in managing significant size- scale  for government -led sporting event s like the National Games, 
Khelo India and Khelo Para Games  with an unwavering commitment to excellence, service and 
operational efficiencies . 
Our 40+ strong Thomas Cook MICE t eam is fully committed to provide round- the-clock support to wards  
ensuring that every aspect of the event  runs smoothly. My Team and I look forward to providing support 
in making the Asian Aquatics Championships 2025 a resounding success.”
