GAIL (India) Limited enters prestigious FTSE4Good Index Series  
  
New Delhi, 30 July 2026:  GAIL (India) Limited, India’s integrated energy major, has 
entered into the globally recognised FTSE4Good Index Series. The company has been 
awarded the FTSE4Good Index Certificate of Membership, following the June 2026 
review by FTSE Russell.  
  
The recognition underscores GAIL's unwavering commitment to maintaining high 
standards of Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) performance. It reflects the 
company's continued focus on environmental stewardship, social responsibility and 
robust corporate governance, while aligning its sustainability initiatives with globally 
accepted ESG benchmarks.  
  
The FTSE4Good Index Series is a leading global sustainable investment index that 
identifies companies demonstrating strong ESG practices based on internationally 
recognised standards.  
  
FTSE Russell is a trading name of FTSE International Limited, Frank Russell Company, 
Refinitiv Benchmark Services (UK) Limited and their respective group undertakings, 
all of which are members of the London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG).
