Key Highlights of the Results -Q2 FY-2026-27 
 
 Business Growth - Total Business of the Bank stood at Rs.6,37,280 Crore as on 3 0.09.2026 
registering a growth of 18.81 % on Y -o-Y basis, wherein Gross Advances grew by 24.70 % 
on Y -o-Y basis to Rs. 2,87,690  Crore & Total Deposits grew by 14.36 % on Y -o-Y basis to 
Rs.3,49,590  Crore.  
 CASA Growth – Total CASA stood at Rs. 1, 22,259  Crore as on 3 0.09.2026 registering a 
growth of 10.59 % on Y -o-Y basis, Savings Deposit stood at Rs. 1,0 3,736  Crore registering 
a growt h of 9.10%  on Y -o-Y basis and Current Deposit stood at Rs. 18,523  Crore registering 
a growth of 19.70 % on Y -o-Y basis.  CASA Ratio stood at  36.77%. 
 
 Advances in Retail, Agriculture & MSME (RAM) Sectors - RAM segment of the Bank 
increased by 26.92 % to Rs. 1,68,736  Crore Y -o-Y backed by 30.23 % Y-o-Y growth in Retail 
advances , 29.59% Y -o-Y growth in Agriculture advances and 20.32  % Y -o-Y growth in 
MSME ad vances.  
 
 Profitability – Operating Profit for the quarter ended 3 0.09.2026 has shown an impressive 
growth of 16.04% on Y -o-Y basis and stood at Rs. 1,872  Crore . Net profit for the quarter 
ending 3 0.09.2026 stood at Rs. 786 Crore  as against Rs .620 Crore as on 3 0.09.2025 
registering a growth of 26.77 % on Y -o-Y basis.  Net Interest Margin (NIM) - Global stood 
at 3.00% and Domestic NIM at 3. 19% for the quarter ended 3 0.09.2026. 
 
 Reduction in NPA - Gross NPA reduced by 60 bps Y -o-Y to 1.96% as on 3 0.09.2026 
whereas Net NPA reduced by 25 bps Y -o-Y to 0. 18% as on 3 0.09.2026. 
 Capital Adequacy Ratio - Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) stood at 18.85 % as on 
30.09.2026 with Tier I Capital Ratio of 17.27%. 
 Credit to Deposit Ratio - Stood at 82.29 % as on 3 0.09.2026 as against 7 5.47% as on 
30.09.2025. 
 Provision Coverage Ratio  stood at 98.36 % as on 30.09.2026
