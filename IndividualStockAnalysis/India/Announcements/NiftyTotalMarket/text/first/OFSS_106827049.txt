Oracle Financial Services Software Reports Q 2 Fiscal Year 202 7 Results   
Revenue for the Quarter at Rs. 1, 907 Crore, up 7% YoY  
Net Income  of Rs. 702 Crore, up 29% YoY   
 
Mumbai, India —October 23, 202 6: Oracle Financial Services Software Limited (Reuters: 
ORCL.BO and ORCL.NS), a majority -owned subsidiary of Oracle, today announced results fo r 
the quarter  and half year ended September  30, 202 6. 
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, on a consolidated basis as compared to the same 
period in Fiscal Year 2026:  
• Revenue was Rs. 1, 907 Crore, up 7%  
• Operating income was  Rs. 911 Crore, up 25%  
• Net income was Rs. 702 Crore, up 29%  
• Products business posted revenue of Rs. 1, 716 Crore, up 6% 
• Services business posted revenue of Rs. 191 Crore, up 15%  
• Remaining Performance obligation was Rs. 7, 211 Crore, up 1 4%  
For the  half year ended September 30, 2026, on a consolidated basis as compared to the 
same period in Fiscal Year 2026:  
• Revenue was Rs. 5,032 Crore, up 38%  
• Operating income was  Rs. 2,772 Crore, up 77%  
• Net income was Rs. 2,118 Crore, up 78%  
• Products business posted revenue of Rs. 4,652  Crore, up 41%  
• Services business posted revenue of Rs. 380 Crore, up 11% 
“We have delivered another strong quarter, reinforcing the growth momentum witnessed at 
the beginning of the financial year,” said Avadhut Ketkar, managing director and chief 
executive officer, Oracle Financial Services Software. “The year -on-year growth o f 7% in 
revenues, 25% in operating income, and  29% in net income demonstrates the strength of our 
business and our ability to deliver sustained financial performance. Our deep domain 
expertise, comprehensive portfolio of solutions, and  advancements in AI -powered 
capabilities continue to position us strongly to address the evolving business needs of 
financial institutions worldwide. This performance provides a solid foundation as we enter 
the second half of the financial year.”  
“Our operating and net margins remained healthy during the quarter, reflecting the quality 
of our financial performance and  commitment to operational efficiency,” said  Manish 
Bhandari, chief financial officer, Oracle Financial Services Software. “Our strong execution
