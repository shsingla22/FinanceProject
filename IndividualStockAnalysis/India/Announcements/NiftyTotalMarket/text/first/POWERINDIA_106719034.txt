	 c.	 A	brief	on	types	of	customers
   The Company serves a diverse customer base across utilities, industrial sectors, transportation, data centers, 
and infrastructure. Its customers include Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) contractors, 
distributors, Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), and channel partners operating within these 
segments. Through its five business units, Transformers, High Voltage, Grid Integration, Grid Automation, 
and Service, the Company provides integrated and digital solutions, including software, protection and 
control systems, substation integration (220 kV to 765 kV), power and traction transformers, capacitors 
and filters, as well as advanced technologies such as SF₆-free GIS under the EconiQ portfolio.
