2. Rationale and Shareholding Impact 
The rationale aims to establish a dedicated platform for the UAV vertical, facilitate focused management and 
commercialisation, enable investor participation, incentivising and retention of key talent alongside providing 
capital for business expansion. Following the proposed preferential issue, the Company's shareholding in 
Dynauton is expected to reduce from 100% to approximately 83.33%, with Dynauton continuing as a 
subsidiary and the Company retaining control. 
Dr. Udayant Maihoutra, the Founder, conceived the UAV Venture and has devoted substantial personal time 
over several years to its development, from concept to the current R&D stage and ongoing trials of key 
projects. His continued commitment is considered vital to the venture's success and instrumental in building 
confidence among employees and prospective investors. 
Demonstrating his commitment to Dynauton, the Founder has chosen to subscribe for shares in cash at the 
same independently determined price and on the same terms as the Company, foregoing compensation, 
sweat equity or a founder allocation for past contributions. His proposed interest in Dynauton is expected to 
be approximately 5.56%. 
Dynauton ESOP Trust (to be formed) proposed interest in Dynauton is expected to be approximately 
11.11%, subject to final allotment with the objective of incentivising and retaining key talent

3. Transfer of UAV Vertical 
The Company proposes to transfer its UAV Vertical on a slump sale basis as a going concern, 
encompassing the research and development ("R&D") of autonomous technology systems, hardware and 
software, related work- in-progress ('WIP"), inventories, intellectual property, contracts, employees, liabilities, 
and all associated rights and obligations, pursuant to a Business Transfer Agreement ("BTA"), without 
assigning individual values to the assets and liabilities, as contemplated under the Income-tax Act, 2025. 
The required information, in compliance with the Para A of Part A of Schedule Ill of the SEBI LODR 
Regulations, is enclosed herewith as Annexure - II

requisite corporate and shareholder approvals, wherever applicable, and other statutory and regulatory 
compliances. The financial and accounting impact will be recognised in accordance with applicable
