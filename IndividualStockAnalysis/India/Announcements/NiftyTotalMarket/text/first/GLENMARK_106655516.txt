Elmwood Park , New Jersey, June 05 , 202 6 – Glenmark Pharmaceuticals  Inc., USA  (Glenmark) 
is pleased to announce the launch1 of Lacosamide Injection USP, 200 mg/20 mL (10 mg/mL), 
Single -Dose Vials . Glenmark’s Lacosamide Injection USP is bioequivalent and therapeutically 
equivalent to the reference listed drug, Vimpat ®2 Injection, 200 mg/20 mL  (10 mg/mL) , of UCB, 
Inc. [NDA 022254 ].  
According to IQVIA® sales  data for the 12 -month period ending April 2026 , the Vimpat ® Injection

a broader, differentiated product portfolio in the United States. By expanding our injectable product 
portfolio, we are enhancing our ability to meet the evo lving needs of healthcare providers while 
advancing our commitment to improving patient access to quality, affordable treatment options.”
