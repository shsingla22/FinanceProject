Karur Vysya Bank Expands Network to 929 Branches with Eight New Openings  
Bank strengthens presence across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Telangana and Uttar Pradesh  
 
India, October 30, 2026 : Karur Vysya Bank (KVB), one of India's leading private sector 
banks, has expanded its nationwide branch network to 929 branches , with the opening of 
eight new branches across four states. The expansion reinforces the Bank's focus on 
strengthening its prese nce in key markets and improving access to banking services across 
metropolitan, semi -urban and rural locations.  
The Bank’s eight new branches comprise three in Tamil Nadu, two each in Karnataka and 
Telangana, and one in Uttar Pradesh. The Tamil Nadu branc hes are located at Chennai –
Choolai, Thirukazhukundram and Trichy –Vaiyampatti; the Karnataka branches at 
Nagarabhavi and Hulimavu in Bengaluru; the Telangana branches at Bachupally and Tandur; 
and the Uttar Pradesh branch at Agra.  
The expansion includes fou r branches in metropolitan markets, three in semi -urban 
locations and one in a rural area, reflecting KVB's focus on serving customers across diverse 
geographies.  
Shri Ramesh Babu, Managing Director & CEO, Karur Vysya Bank,  inaugurated the 
new Ag ra branch in Uttar Pradesh , marking the Bank’s latest phase of branch network 
expansion. Speaking on the occasion, he said, “ The addition of eight new branches is another 
step towards strengthening our reach and making banking services more accessible to customers 
across key markets. We see significant opportunities across metropolitan, semi -urban and rural 
locations, particularly in retail, MSME and agricultural banking. Our continued investment in physical 
branches, complemented by digital banking capabilities, wil l help us serve customers more 
effectively, deepen relationships and support sustainable business growth .” 
The expansion comes on the back of continued growth in the Bank’s business. As of 
September 30, 2026, KVB’s total business stood at ₹2,39,712 crore, comprising deposits of 
₹1,28,123 crore and advances of ₹1,11,589 crore. The Bank reported a quarterly net profit 
of ₹788 crore in Q2 FY27, registering a year -on-year growth of 37.28%.  
 
Through the expansion of its branch network and continue d investment in digital banking, 
Karur Vysya Bank remains focused on enhancing customer convenience, deepening its 
regional presence and meeting the evolving banking needs of individuals and businesses 
across the country.
