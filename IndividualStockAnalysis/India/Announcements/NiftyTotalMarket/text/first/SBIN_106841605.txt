Press Release  
Q2FY27 RESULTS  
 
Highlights  
Business  
• Business levels at ~₹115 trillion . 
• Deposits and Advances at ₹63 trillion and ₹52 trillion , respectively.  
Profitability  
• Net Profit  for Q2FY27 stands at ₹23,038 crores  witnessing a growth of 14.28 % 
YoY.  
• Operating Profit  for Q2FY27 grew by 7.28% YoY to ₹ 34,226 crores . 
• Bank’s  ROA and ROE for the half year  stand at 1.1 4% and 18.68%, respectively . 
• Net Interest Income  (NII) for Q2FY27 increased by 14.69% YoY.   
• Whole Bank and Domestic NIM for the H1FY27 stand at 2.9 0% and 3.0 6%, 
respectively.  
• Whole Bank NIM and Domestic NIM stood at 2. 94% and 3. 11%, respectively, in Q2FY27, 
improving by 8 bps and 11 bps, respectively, over Q1FY27.  
Balance Sheet  
• Whole Bank Advances  growth  at 17.84 % YoY  with Domestic Advances growth 
at 16.94 % YoY. 
• Foreign Offices’ Advances  grew by  22.86 % YoY  in Rupee terms and 13.84 % in 
Dollar terms .  
• RAM  Advances grew by 17.34 % YoY, with double digit growth in all segments. 
Agri Advances  grew by 22.64 % YoY followed by SME  Advances  growth of 
21.97 % YoY  and Retail Personal Advances  growth of 14.54 %. 
• Corporate Advances registered YoY growth of 16.12 %. 
• Whole Bank Deposits grew by 12.27 % YoY . CASA Deposit s grew by 8.13% 
YoY. CASA Ratio stands at 38.32 % as on 30th September  26. Retail Term 
Deposits  registered  YoY growth of 16.74 %. 
Asset Quality  
• Gross NPA Ratio at 1.39% improved  by 34 bps YoY.  
• Net NPA Ratio at 0.36% improved  by 6 bps YoY.  
• Provision Coverage Ratio  (PCR) stands at 74.43% while PCR ( incl. AUCA)  
stands at 91.83%.  
• Slippage Ratio  for Q2FY27 improved  by 4 bps YoY and stands at 0. 41%. 
• Credit Cost  for Q2FY27 stands at 0. 22%, a reduction of 17 bps YoY .  
Capital Adequacy  
• Capital to Risk Weighted Assets  Ratio  (CRAR) as at the end of Q2FY27 stands 
at 16.09%.
