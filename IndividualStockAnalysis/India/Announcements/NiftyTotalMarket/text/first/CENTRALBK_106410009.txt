Sub:  Intimation under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
– Co-Lending Partnership for Housing Loans  with M/s. Godrej Housing Finance Ltd.  
This is to inform that Central Bank of India has announced a strategic partnership with M/s. Godrej 
Housing Finance Ltd.  to offer Housing  Loans at competitive rates, subject to compliance with the 
applicable law(s) including the applicable guidelines issued by Reserve Bank of India (RBI). The 
participation by both the entities in this Co -Lending arrangement will result in greater expansion of 
portfolio by Central Bank of India and M/s. Godr ej Housing Finance Ltd.  
 
M/s. Godrej Housing Finance Ltd.  is a Housing Finance Company, registered with National Housing 
Bank (NHB).  Godrej Housing Finance which is part of Godrej Industries Group is a leading Housing 
Finance Company having operations across multiple states. It has presence in prime, mass -market and 
affordable housing segment of housing finance.  
 
Under the arrangement, M/s. Godrej Housing Finance Ltd. will originate and process Housing Loan 
proposals as per jointly formulated credit parameters and eligibility criteria and Central Bank of India will 
take into its books  upto 80% of the Home Loans under mutually agreed terms. M/s. Godrej Housing 
Finance Ltd . will service the loan account s throughout the life cycle of the loan s. The Co -Lending 
arrangement is expected to help both the entities to offer convenient experience and customer delight to 
their customers as well as help expand their reach across India .
