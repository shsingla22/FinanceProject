PNG launches the flagship store of YOOU, a brand 
around evolving identities of modern women  
The lightweight diamond jewellery brand brings contemporary jewellery, personalised 
shopping and everyday luxury to Pune 
Pune, August 8, 2026: PNG Jewellers has launched the first flagship store of its newly rebranded 
lightweight diamond jewellery brand, YOOU, at Amanora Mall, Hadapsar, Pune. Spanning 1,445 sq. 
ft., the new store offers customers a fresh retail experience centred around individuality, self-
expression and contemporary design. 
Designed for today’s confident and expressive consumer, YOOU is a contemporary diamond jewellery 
brand offering lightweight designs in 14, 18, 22 and 9 karat gold. The brand brings together modern 
aesthetics and PNG Jewellers’ legacy of trust and craftsmanship, creating versatile jewellery that 
seamlessly complements everyday lifestyles rather than being reserved solely for special occasions. 
The Amanora flagship has been thoughtfully designed with elegant interiors, open layouts and 
carefully curated displays to create an immersive shopping experience. Customers can explore an 
extensive range of rings, earrings, hoops, chains, pendants, bracelets, bangles, mangalsutras and 
men's jewellery. Every piece reflects YOOU's philosophy of Made for YOOU lightweight, versatility and 
effortless elegance, enabling customers to express their individuality every day. 
Speaking on the occasion, Dr. Saurabh Gadgil, Chairman and Managing Director, PNG Jewellers, said, 
"The launch of our first YOOU flagship store represents a new chapter in PNG Jewellers' journey. 
Today's consumers seek jewellery that aligns with their everyday lives, personal style and individuality. 
This is specially made for YOOU to meet these aspirations while carrying forward the trust and 
craftsmanship that PNG Jewellers has built over nearly two centuries. Amanora is the perfect 
destination to introduce this refreshed brand, and we look forward to expanding YOOU across India in 
the coming years."  
The Amanora launch marks the first step in YOOU's ambitious expansion strategy, with stores across 
the country gradually transitioning into the new identity. Backed by PNG Jewellers' heritage and an 
omnichannel approach, YOOU aims to redefine India's lightweight diamond jewellery segment 
through contemporary design, accessible luxury and a customer-first experience. 
  
 
 
About YOOU 
YOOU by PNG Jewellers is a lightweight diamond jewellery brand, created for young jewellery lovers 
who celebrate themselves every day. Offering thoughtfully crafted diamond jewellery in 9K, 14K, 18K 
and 22K gold. YOOU blends effortless design, everyday wearability and timeless craftsmanship. 
Designed for modern lifestyles, the brand champions individuality, self-expression and confidence, 
believing that jewellery should not wait for an occasion, because the wearer is the occasion. 
 
About PNG Jewellers  
PNG Jewellers is one of the most reputed jewellers in the country, with over 194 years of excellence 
in creativity and trusted service for customers all over the world. Under the leadership of Dr. Saurabh 
Gadgil, a visionary entrepreneur, PNG Jewellers continues to evolve as a globally relevant brand while 
staying rooted in its heritage of purity, trust, and craftsmanship.
