Sub:  Classification of US FDA Inspection at the facility of Caplin Steriles Limited (Company's 
Subsidiary) as OAI – Reg 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, 
this is to inform you that further to the United States Food and Drug Administration (US FDA) 
unannounced inspection of Caplin Steriles' injectable and  ophthalmic manufacturing facility located at 
Gummidipoondi between August 13, 2026, and August 21, 2026 which concluded with 10 observations 
that were procedural in nature, the US FDA has now determined that the inspection classification of the 
Caplin Steriles Limited facility is "Official Action Indicated" (OAI).  
 
The Company does not expect any material impact on its financials or operations on account of the above 
as on the date of this intimation. The facility continues to manufacture and supply products and the 
Company remains committed to maintaining the highest standards of quality, current Good 
Manufacturing Practices (cGMP) and regulatory compliance. The company is committed to working 
closely with the FDA to address the observations and demonstrate the effectiveness and sustainability of 
corrective and preve ntive actions.   
 
We will keep the stock exchanges informed if there is any further information relating to the above in the 
future.  
 
The details required to be disclosed under Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI LODR Regulations, to the 
extent applicable, are set out in the Annexure to this intimation.  
 
Kindly take the same on your records.
