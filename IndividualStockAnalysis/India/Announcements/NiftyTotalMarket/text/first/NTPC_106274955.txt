Sub: Declaration of COD of second & last part capacity of 52 MW out of 245 MW at Plot-3 of NTPC’s Nokh 
Solar PV Project (3x245 MW) under CPSU Scheme Phase-II, Tranche –III 
 
In terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we

out of 245 MW capacity at Plot-3 of NTPC's Nokh Solar PV Project (3x245MW), Rajasthan, is declared on
