Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
In continuation of our earlier disclosure dated April 6, 2024 and August 22, 2024, made pursuant 
to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, regarding acquisition of 25.44% stake in O2 Renewable 
Energy V Private Limited (“OREV”) by Dalmia Cement (Bharat) Limited (‘ DCBL’), a wholly owned 
subsidiary of the Company, to source wind power as a captive consumer for a capacity up to 11 
MW located in the State of Karnataka, we wish to inform that DCBL has informed the Company 
that it has disposed of the entire 25.44% equity stake in OREV, and accordingly it ceased to be an 
associate of DCBL. 
 
The details as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with SEBI circular no. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13,2023 are provided in the Annexure 
hereto. The disposal was completed at around 1:15 pm on January 16, 2026. 
 
This is for your information and records. 
 
Thanking you,
