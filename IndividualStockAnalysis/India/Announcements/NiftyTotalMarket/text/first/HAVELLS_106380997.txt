Lloyd Kolors Refrigerator Goes Live on Flipkart, Offering Smart, Stylish 
Cooling to More Indian Homes   
 
23rd September 2025  Lloyd, a leading consumer durables brand from Havells India, today 
announced that the Lloyd Kolors Refrigerator is now available for purchase on Flipkart, India’s 
homegrown e-commerce marketplace and other channels of distribution. Through this collaboration, 
Lloyd is expanding its digital reach and making it more convenient for customers across India to 
purchase a refrigerator that offers   the best of both worlds : smart features and standout design .  
 
With this move, people across tiers will be able to easily browse, compare, and order the Lloyd Kolors 
Refrigerator The refrigerator will be available on Flipkart and other channels of distribution at price 
starting INR 18990.    
 
Speaking on the partnership, Rajesh Rathi, Executive Vice President, Havells India  said, “We are 
seeing a clear evolution in what drives consumer consideration, especially in home appliances. It is no 
longer just about functionality; people want products that reflect their personal style and complement 
modern living. The Lloyd Kolors Refrigerator perfectly embodies this shift. It is packed with practical 
features that simplify daily life, while its vibrant colors bring a fresh, contemporary look to any kitchen. 
By launching it on Flipkart and other channels of distribution, we are making this stylish, high-
performance refrigerator easily accessible to more families, and tapping into the growing base of online 
shoppers looking for the latest, most aesthetically appealing options. Ultimately, it is about offering 
consumers the perfect blend of style, reliability, and convenience.”   
 
Rakesh Krishnan, Vice President – Large Appliances, Flipkart  said, “We are delighted to bring the 
Lloyd Kolors Refrigerator to millions of Flipkart customers across the country. As Indian shoppers 
increasingly look for appliances that combine advanced functionality with modern design, this launch 
aligns perfectly with our commitment to offering high-quality, innovative products that elevate everyday 
living. Through partnerships with brands like Lloyd delivering trusted performance and vibrant design 
philosophy, along with Flipkart’s seamless delivery and customer service network, we’re enabling more 
families across the country to upgrade their homes with smart, stylish cooling solutions.   
 
The Lloyd Kolors Refrigerator is engineered to meet the needs of modern households, offering a host 
of innovations for convenience, freshness, and safety. Its Auto Defrost function  uses advanced 
sensors—light, motion, and temperature—to automatically determine when defrosting is needed, 
eliminating manual upkeep. The Inverter Compressor ensures stable temperatures for longer-lasting 
freshness and reduced energy use. The Rapid Freeze Technology , powered by a hybrid evaporator, 
robust compressor, and extra-thick insulation, delivers superfast cooling and can make ice in just 29 
minutes. Additionally, the Home Inverter Connect  automatically switches to the home inverter during 
power outages, so all food stays cool and safe without interruption.  
 
For added health and flexibility, the Lloyd Kolors Refrigerator features Bactshield technology  to 
prevent up to 99% of bacterial growth, keeping food fresher for longer. The Viruzsafe-coated handle 
kills 100+ microbes and 99% of viruses, making it much safer than regular handles. The Honeycomb 
Crisper locks in moisture to keep fruits and vegetables fresh and crisp, while the Fleximax Design offers 
adjustable glass shelves for efficient storage of items of all shapes and sizes. 
 
The refrigerator is available in stylish pastel shades, Sunkissed Yellow and Minty Green , and sports 
designer handles which makes it a real conversation piece in any kitchen. Available in 195L, 216L, and 
240L capacities, there is a size to fit every home.  
 
With the Lloyd Kolors Refrigerator now on Flipkart and other channels of distribution, Havells is making 
it easier than ever for Indian families to upgrade to a refrigerator that’s as practical as it is stylish.
