Sub: - Clarification on News Reports regarding Burglary Incident at Franchisee Showroom at 
Bettiah , Bihar  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we hereby inform  that a burglary incident occurred at our franchisee showroom located at Bettiah  
in the West Champaran district  of Bihar on 18th November , 2026.  
The concerned franchisee has lodged an FIR with the local police authorities and the matter is currently 
under investigation.  It is pertinent to note that there was no loss of life or major harm to the employees 
during the incident.  
 
Further , we would like to clarify that the said showroom is being operated under the Franchisee Owned 
and Company  Operated (FO CO) model. The inventory at the  aforesaid  showroom is owned and 
recorded in the books of the franchisee and accordingly, there is no loss of inventory or financial impact 
on the Company  on account of the said incident.  However, the Company remains fully committed to 
providing comprehensive support to the franchisee during this time.  
 
 
This is for your information and records.
