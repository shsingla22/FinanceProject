4. With regard to the Shipbuilding contract for construction of 2 ships with a Government 
Customer, the contractual delivery dates (as extended) for both the vessels has already expired. 
At the request of the Customer for reallocation of the vessel for other prospective buyers, the 
delivery of ship has been abated with minor progress. The company has provided for LO for the 
delay upto 29 Apr 2023 and 30 Oct 2023 in respect for the two ships. Since the Company has a 
valid contract with the Customer, the Company has not recognized further liquidated damages in 
the financials beyond the dates mentioned above. 
5. The Company has capitalised major Projects "International Ship Repair Facility " and "New 
Dry Dock" subsequent to Q1 FY 2024-25 at an amount of Rs 83409.01 lakhs (Rs 79344.26 
lakhs as on 31.03.2025) and Rs 131938.92 lakhs ( Rs 131938.92 lakhs as on 31.03.2025) 
respectively.
