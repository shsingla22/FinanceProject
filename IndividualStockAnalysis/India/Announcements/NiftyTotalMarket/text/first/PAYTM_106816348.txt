Sub.: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 - Proposed GST Demand on First Games Reduced; Assessing Solutions Under 
Relevant Provisions; No Incremental Impact on the Company   
 
Dear Sir / Ma’am, 
 
This is in furtherance to our earlier disclosure dated May 27, 2026 and pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), in relation 
to the notices and the decision of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India upholding 28% GST on the online gaming 
companies. The said disclosure was made in reference to First Games Technology Private Limited (“FGTPL”), 
a subsidiary  of One 97 Communications Limited (the “Company”). 
As previously disclosed, FGTPL had received a Show Cause Notice from the GST department on April 28, 2025 
(“SCN”) with proposed GST liability of INR 5,712 crores with applicable interest and penalties payable 
separately, for the period from April 2018 to March 2023. FGTPL informed the Company on October 12, 2026 
at 11:21pm (IST) that it has received a demand order against the said SCN whereby the Additional 
Commissioner of CGST Noida, Uttar Pradesh (‘the Authority’) has reduced the GST liability from INR 5,712 
crore proposed in the SCN to INR 703.66 crore (Indian Rupees Seven Hundred Three Crore and Sixty Six Lakh) 
towards tax for the period from April 2018 to March 2023, with applicable penalty equivalent to the tax 
liability along with applicable interest. 
FGTPL is evaluating the appropriate course forward in accordance with relevant statutory provisions. 
As detailed below, there is no incremental financial impact on the Company arising from this order. 
 
For group consolidation purposes under applicable accounting standards, FGTPL is considered a joint venture. 
Accordingly, its revenues are not consolidated in the Company’s financial statements, and the Company’s 
share of profit or loss under the equity method is nil in its consolidated profit and loss account for the financial 
year ended March 31, 2026. FGTPL discontinued its real-money gaming operations pursuant to the Promotion 
and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025, and its financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2026 
were already prepared on liquidation basis. 
 
The carrying value of investment in FGTPL in the consolidated financial statements of Company as on March 
31, 2026 is already nil.  
 
The details as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular No. 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 are given in Form - A.
