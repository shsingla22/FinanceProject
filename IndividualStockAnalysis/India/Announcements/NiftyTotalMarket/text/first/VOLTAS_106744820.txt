Voltas and Atomberg Join Hands to Build Room AC 
Compressors in India  
 
Partnership to strengthen technology capability, localization and long- term compressor supply chain 
resilience  
 
Mumbai, 14 August 2026: Voltas Limited, a leading air conditioning and engineering solutions company 
from the Tata Group, has signed a Binding Term Sheet with Atomberg Innovation Private Limited 
(“Atomberg Innovation ”), a material subsidiary of Atomberg Technologies Limited which engages in 
the design, manufacture and supply of motors, electronic assemblies, controller systems and related 
components, to establish a Joint Venture for the manufacture of high efficiency Room Air Conditioner 
compressors and par ts related to such compressors in India.  
The proposed Joint Venture is intended to undertake the development, manufacturing, and supply of 
Air Conditioner Compressors, with a strong emphasis on high energy efficiency, operational reliability, 
cost competitiveness, and progressive localization. It aims to bring together Voltas’ deep understanding 
of the Indian Room Air Conditioner market, product and application expertise, manufacturing 
ecosystem, supply chain capabilities and scale with Atomberg Innovation’s capabilities in proprietary 
compressor technology, first -principles engineering, product design capabilities and track record of 
innovation in energy efficient motors and control drives.  
We are aligning this development with the evolving regulatory framework, applicable industry standards, energy efficiency requirements, and the broader objective of strengthening domestic manufacturing and 
building a progressively localized and competitive supply ecosystem.  
Voltas will be an anchor customer of the Joint Venture, providing a strong and stable demand base, 
while Atomberg Innovations will be the technology partner, bringing its technology and innovation 
capabilities to the venture. The Joint Venture will also have the opportunity and flexibility to supply Air 
Conditioner Compressors to other customers, enabling broader market participation, scale, and long-
term competitiveness . 
 
Mr. Mukundan Menon, Managing Director, Voltas Limited, said: “Compressor is the heart of an  
Air- conditioner and is central to its performance, energy efficiency and reliability. This partnership is an 
important step towards building indigenous technology capability and strengthening our long- term 
supply chain resilience. By combining Atomberg’s technology and engineering strengths with Voltas’ 
market knowledge, manufacturing ecosystem and scale, we intend to build compressors designed  
for India.”  
 
Mr. Manoj Meena, Founder, Atomberg, said: “Atomberg has developed deep- tech capabilities in 
efficient motors, control drives and advance engineering. This partnership gives us the opportunity to 
combine our technology strengths with Voltas’ extensive air conditioning expertise, manufacturing capab ility and market scale. Together, we aim to create an efficient compressor technology platform 
developed and manufactured in India.”  
The proposed transaction is subject to satisfactory completion of due diligence, execution of Definitive 
Agreements and receipt of applicable approvals.  
  
 
About Voltas Limited 
 Voltas  Limited,  founded  in India  in 1954 and part of the Tata  Group,  is a premier  air conditioning and 
engineering solutions provider, projects specialist, and one of the leading players in the consumer durables category. Alongside its undisputed market leadership in Room Air Conditioners  
with  a footprint  of over  30,000 touch  points —Voltas  has expanded  into the Home  Appliances  
Segment through a wide range of innovative products under Voltbek Home Appliance s Pvt Ltd. The 
Company’s portfolio also includes Commercial Refrigeration, Home Comfort Products and Commercial 
Air Conditioning Products, offering comprehensive cooling and comfort solutions.  
 
About Atomberg Innovation Private Limited  Atomberg Innovation Private Limited (AIPL) is a material subsidiary of Atomberg Technologies Limited, 
incorporated in 2023.  AIPL is the company’s B2B arm specializing in the design, engineering, and 
manufacturing of motors, controllers, and compressor technology. Its motor portfolio caters to 
sectors spanning consumer durables, supplying critical proprietary components to OEM c lients across 
appliances and adjacent industries.
