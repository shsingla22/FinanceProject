Press Release 
Varroc appoints Mr. Akshaykumar Chudasama as 
an Independent Director   
Pune, May 29, 2025:  Varroc, a leading global Tier-1 automotive supplier, announced the 
appointment of Mr. Akshaykumar Chudasama  as an Independent Director  on its 
Board of Directors, effective May 29, 2025. This strategic appointment reflects Varroc’s 
continued commitment to strengthening governance, enhancing cross-border business 
expertise, and building stakeholder confidence in line with its long-term growth objectives. 
Commenting on the appointment, Mr. Tarang Jain, Chairman & Managing Director, 
Varroc,  said, “We are delighted to welcome Mr. AkshayKumar Chudasama to our Board 
of Directors. His strategic perspective, legal acumen, and governance experience will be 
invaluable to Varroc’s strategic growth.” 
On his appointment, Mr. Akshaykumar Chudasama  said, “I am honoured and excited 
to join the Board of Varroc. I look forward to contributing to its journey of innovation, 
growth, and global expansion.” 
Mr. Chudasama is a seasoned legal expert with over three decades of experience in law. 
He holds a Bachelor of Arts (BA) from St. Xavier’s College, University of Bombay, and an 
LLB from the London School of Economics, University of London, UK. He is enrolled as an 
Advocate with the Bar Council of Maharashtra and Goa, and as a Solicitor with the Law 
Society of England and Wales. 
Currently, Mr. Chudasama is serving as Managing Partner at Shardul Amarchand 
Mangaldas & Co., where he leads the firm’s Mumbai Region and is widely recognized for 
his expertise in cross-border mergers & acquisitions and private equity transactions.

About Varroc Engineering Ltd. 
Varroc Engineering Ltd is a global tier-1 automotive component group. It was incorporated 
in 1988. The group manufactures and supplies E-mobility solutions, Body systems 
solutions, Lighting solutions, HMI solutions, ICE powertrain and Advanced electronics to 
leading OEMs with end-to-end capabilities across design, development and manufacturing 
for two-wheeler, three-wheeler, passenger vehicles, commercial vehicle, and off-highway 
vehicle worldwide. The group income was ₹ 81,718 million from continued operations in 
FY25. The group employs more than 6,100 employees (750 + R&D Engineers), has 37 
global operating manufacturing facilities supported by 7 R&D Centres, and has filed more 
than 120 patents. 
Varroc Engineering Limited’s shares are listed on the National Stock Exchange (VARROC) 
and the Bombay Stock Exchange (541578).
