SUMMARY OF PROCEEDINGS OF THE MEETING OF EQUITY SHAREHOLDERS 
OF NHPC LIMITED HELD ON MONDAY, 29rH SEPTEMBER, 2025 AT 12:30 P.M. 
(IST) THROUGH VIDEO CONFERENCE (VC)/ OTHER AUDIO-VISUAL MEANS 
(OAVM) PURSUANT TO ORDER OF MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS (MCA) 
A meeting of equity shareholders of the Company was held on Monday, 29th 
September , 2025 at 12:30 P.M. (IST) through VC/ OAVM pursuant to MCA order dated 
22nd May, 2025 for the purpose of considering the scheme of amalgamation of 
Jalpower Corporation Limited (a wholly owned subsidiary of NHPC Limited) 
(Transferor Company) and NHPC Limited (Transferee Company) ("Scheme") . The 
meeting commenced at 12:30 P.M. (IST) and concluded at 12:48 P.M. (IST). 87 
shareholders attended and participated in the Meeting. 
1. At the outset, Company Secretary welcomed the members to the meeting 
and informed as under: 
a. The meeting was held through VC/OAVM , in compliance with 
relevant circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, 
Government of India (MCA), Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(SEBI) and as per directions of MCA order. 
b. The Company had provided the facility to its members to cast votes 
electronically on the business mentioned in the notice of meeting, 
using remote e-voting system of M/s KFin Technologies Limited 
(KFin), as well as e-voting during the meeting. 
c. The members who had not cast their votes earlier through remote e­
voting could avail the facility to cast vote during the meeting through 
electronic voting system. 
11. Shri Mohammad Afzal, Joint Secretary (Hydro), Ministry of Power and Govt. 
Nominee Director, who was the Chairperson of the meeting as per order of 
MCA, chaired the meeting and welcomed everyone present in the meeting. 
The Directors present included the Chairperson of Audit Committee , 
Nomination & Remuneration Committee , Stakeholders' Relationship 
Committee and Committee of Directors on Corporate Social Responsibility 
(CSR) & Sustainability . The Statutory Auditors were also present through 
VC/ OAVM. The requisite quorum being present, the Chairman called the 
meeting in order. 
111. Chairman apprised that the Company had taken all feasible steps to ensure 
that maximum number of shareholders participate in the meeting through 
video conferencing and vote thereat. He also informed that Shri Amit 
Kaushal of M/s A. Kaushal & Associates, Company Secretaries has been 
appointed as Scrutinizer to scrutinize e-voting process in a fair and 
transparent manner. 
IV. The Notice, scheme of amalgamat ion and other necessary information as 
mentioned in the explanatory statement appended to the notice of the 
meeting as already circulated to the members of the company was taken as 
read. The question submitted to the meeting was whether the equity 
shareholders agree to the scheme of amalgamation between Jalpower 
Corporation Limited and NHPC Limited. Thereafter, the proposed 
resolutions for approval of the Scheme was placed for approval of 
shareholders . 
v. On invitation of the Chairman , members who had registered themselves as 
speaker shareholder provided their views during the meeting. It was also 
informed that answer to the relevant query shall be provided to concerned 
shareholders through email. 
VI. Chairman informed that the result of electronic voting (remote e-voting and 
e-voting during the meeting) would be intimated to stock exchanges and 
also hosted on website of the Company as well as on the website of e-voting 
service provider i.e. KFin within the prescribed timelines. 
v11. The Chairman thanked all members for their participation and informed that 
the voting would continue till 15 minutes after the conclusion of the meeting. 
He thereafter, declared the closure of the meeting. 
Date: September 29, 2025 
Place: Faridabad For NHPC Limited
