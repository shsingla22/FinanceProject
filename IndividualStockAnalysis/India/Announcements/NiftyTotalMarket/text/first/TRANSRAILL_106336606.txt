Transrail secures fresh orders worth ₹ 701 crore  
Strong Momentum Continues: Transrail Bags new orders , taking total o rder  intake for the current year beyond 
₹2,300 Crore  
 
Mumbai, August 12 , 2025:  Transrail Lighting Limited (BSE: 544317, NSE: TRANSRAILL), a turnkey EPC company with 
prominence in Power Transmission & Distribution (T&D) with a  diversification in Civil, Railways, and Poles & Lighting  
businesses  has bagged new orders totalling ₹ 701 crore from both Indian and overseas clients , pushing its cumulative 
FY26 order inflow beyond ₹2, 300 crore.  
Mr. Randeep Narang, MD & CEO said:  
 “The new orders we booked across International and India markets  emphasises our competitive edge and have taken 
the order intake for the year t o date  to INR 2,300 crore . These orders include T&D orders in international market as 
well as domestic orders in Civil and Pole & Lighting  verticals . Our execution excellence  & customer -centric approach, 
and strong financials are driving traction across our businesses and w e remain committed to delivering high -quality  
projects in a timely manner .” 
About Transrail Lighting Limited:  
Transrail is one of the leading engineering, procurement and construction companies with primary focus on power 
transmission and distribution business with 4 decades of experience. With its headquarters in India, it is a global 
enterprise with a footprint in 59 countries across 5 continents. It provides turnkey solutions from Design, Engineering, 
Supply, Manufacture, Construction, Testing services across all its business verticals which includes Power T&D, Civil 
Construction, Railways, Pole  & lighting and S olar EPC. It has more than 2,200 employees. As part of Power T&D 
business, Transrail has large -scale manufacturing facilities in India for Galvanized Lattice Towers, Overhead Conductors 
and Galvanized Monopoles in addition to a well -accredited Tower testing facility.
