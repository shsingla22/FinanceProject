PRESS RELEASE 
 
Brigade Group Records ₹1,600 Crores in Sales during Launch Weekend at Brigade Autograph, a Premium 
Mixed-use Lifestyle Township in Bengaluru. 
 
Bengaluru, November 24, 2026:  Brigade Group has received an outstanding response at the launch of the first 
phase of Brigade Autograph, a 40-acre mixed-use premium lifestyle township on Whitefield–Kadugodi Main 
Road in East Bengaluru. The township combines premium residences, World Trade Center Bengaluru-Whitefield, 
Hyatt Regency, Orion Mall and a proposed Hospital. The project is being developed as a Joint Venture between 
Brigade Group and Krishna Priya Estates.  
 
Brigade Autograph has a GDV of about ₹12,000 crores, comprising of about 8 million sq. ft. of commercial and 
residential saleable area, which will be launched in multiple phases over the next few quarters. The first 
residential phase, Floris at Brigade Autograph, comprises premium residences across 5 towers of 49 floors each.  
Floris launch received tremendous response from customers with Brigade recording ₹1,600 crores in sales in the 
launch weekend itself. This is the 14th integrated township developed by Brigade across Bengaluru, Chennai and 
Hyderabad, and the success of the launch reflects the Group’s expertise in designing and executing these 
townships to global standards. Based on this success and continuing strong demand, Brigade will advance the 
launch of future phases within the township.  
 
Located in a fast-growing micro-market close to IT hubs, industrial clusters and major infrastructure, Brigade 
Autograph will strengthen the Group’s mixed-use township footprint and help establish the region as a leading 
residential, business, hospitality and employment destination. 
 
World Trade Center Bengaluru-Whitefield will offer global corporations Grade-A commercial space. Hyatt 
Regency will feature about 250 guestrooms and suites. Orion Mall will deliver best-in-class shopping and dining, 
and the proposed Hospital will serve the healthcare needs of East Bengaluru. 
 
“Brigade Autograph will transform eastern Bengaluru, much as Brigade Gateway transformed the Yeshwantpur–
Rajajinagar area in the west. At Brigade, we strive to give our customers and stakeholders the very best, creating 
integrated, vibrant destinations that improve quality of life, help businesses and communities thrive, and leave a 
lasting positive impact on the micro-market,” said Pavitra Shankar, Managing Director, Brigade Enterprises 
Limited. 
 
About Brigade Group: 
 
Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers with four decades of expertise 
in building positive experiences for all its stakeholders and winning the trust of its customers. Brigade has 
developed many landmark buildings and transformed skylines across India in Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, 
Mysuru, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram and GIFT City Ahmedabad, with developments across Residential, Office, 
Retail, Hospitality and Education sectors.
