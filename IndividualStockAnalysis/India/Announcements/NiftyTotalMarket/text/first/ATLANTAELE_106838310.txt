Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), read with the applicable provisions of the SEBI Master Circulars 
issued in this regard, we wish to inform you that AE Components Private Limited, a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Atlanta Electricals Limited , has approved a capital expenditure (CAPEX) of 
approximately ₹193.58 Crore  for setting up a new manufacturing facility for the production of 
transformer tanks, frames and radiators. 
 
The proposed project forms part of the Company's backward integration initiative in respect of 
transformer tanks and radiators, which is envisaged to strengthen supply chain reliability, enhance 
operational efficiencies, and support the Company's long-term manufacturing requirements. 
 
The proposed capital expenditure is expected to be funded through a combination of internal 
accruals, term loans from banks/financial institutions and financial assistance from the holding 
company, as may be required. 
 
The implementation of the project is proposed to be undertaken in a phased manner, with 
completion targeted within a period of one year , subject to receipt of requisite statutory and 
regulatory approvals and completion of other necessary formalities.
