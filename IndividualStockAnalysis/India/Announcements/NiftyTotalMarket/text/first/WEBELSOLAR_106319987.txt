Annexure-B 
 
Current Status of Expansion of Phase II ( Additional 600 MW Cell Line) at Falta 
Location. 
 
 
In continuance to our update regarding Capacity Addition of 600 MW Cell Line at Falta Location, 
West Bengal on 23rd December, 2024, we wish to further inform you that installation and 
commissioning of the new machinery is nearing completion now. 
 
Trial runs of machinery are set to commence in September, 2025. Subject to successful completion 
of the trial phase, the additional 600 MW Cell line is expected to begin commercial production in 
October, 2025. 
 
The commissioning of this new machinery is expected to enhance the overall solar cell production 
capacity to 1200MW from the current 600 MW. Consequently, the Company anticipates a marked 
increase in turnover starting from the third quarter of the Financial Year 2025–26.   
 
The above new solar cell line has been set up from the internal accruals of the company.
