For Immediate Release  
 
SWREL’s H1 FY27 Order Book Grows  34% YoY , Reflecting  Strong Business Momentum  
 
Key Highlights  
~ Records revenue of INR 1,527 crore in Q2 FY27  
~ Reports PAT of INR 70 crore in H1 FY27  
~ The company recently secured prestigious domestic and international orders totaling over INR 985 crore  
~ The unexecuted o rder book stands at INR 12,480 crore,  giving a strong visibility of future revenue  
~ Robust growth in O&M portfolio from 13.5  GW in March 2026 to 18.9  GW as of Sep 2026   
 
Mumbai; October 15, 202 6: Sterling and Wilson Renewable Energy Limited (SWREL) (BSE Scrip Code: 542760; 
NSE Symbol: SWSOLAR), a leading home -grown renewable EPC , today announced its financial results for the 
second quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026, reporting a significant turnaround in profitability and 
growth in its order book.  
The company reported a profit after tax (PAT) of INR 70 crore for H1 FY27, marking a significant turnaround 
from the loss of INR 439 crore recorded in H1 FY26. The company reported a profit of INR 17 crore and revenue 
at INR 1,527 crore  in Q2 FY27 . The company also continued to strengthen its Operations and Maintenance 
(O&M) business, with H1 FY27 O&M revenue increasing 48% year -on-year to INR 183 crore from INR 124 crore 
in the corresponding period last year.  
SWREL’s strong order inflows across domestic and international markets have supported business growth and 
strengthened its project pipeline. Recent wins, including a 534.3 MWp solar project in Rajasthan, two BESS 
projects in South Africa with a combined ca pacity of 616 MWh, and the West Minya Solar Power Project in Egypt 
through its joint venture with Hassan Allam Construction, underscore the company’s growing capabilities across 
solar EPC and energy storage  in domestic as well as international market s. These projects further reinforce 
SWREL’s presence in renewable sector and provide a strong foundation for sustained growth.  
Speaking on the results announcement , Mr. Chandra Kishore Thakur , Global CEO, Sterling and Wilson 
Renewable Energy , said,  “Our performance in the first half of FY27 reflects the progress we have made in 
strengthening our business and enhancing operational efficiency . The steady growth in our O&M business, 
coupled with strong order inflows across domestic and international markets, reinforces our capabilities across 
renewable energy EPC  and energy storage. As we continue to build on this momentum, our focus remains on 
disciplined execution, operational efficiency and converting our growing project pipeline into sustainable, 
profitable growth.”

About Sterling and Wilson Renewable Energy Limited  
Sterling and Wilson Renewable Energy Limited (SWREL) is a global pure -play, end -to-end renewable 
engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) solutions provider. The company provides EPC services for 
utility -scale solar, floating solar , hybrid & energy storage  and wind solutions and has a total portfolio of over 
29.8 GWp (including projects commissioned and under various stages of construction). SWREL also manages an 
operation and maintenance (O&M) portfolio of 18.9  GWp solar  power projects, including for projects 
constructed by third parties. Present in 28 countries today, Sterling  and Wilson Renewable Energy Limited has 
operations in India, South -east Asia, Middle East, Africa, Europe, Australia, and the Americas.
