Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015-Board Approval to enter into Joint Venture 
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations 2015 and SEBI Master Circular dated November 11, 2024, 
we would like to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Company, at their Meeting held on 
June 25, 2025 (which commenced at 04:45 P.M. and concluded at 05:10 P.M.), granted                                  
approval for entering into a joint venture with T.D. Holding GMBH (‘TDH’) for manufacturing, 
marketing and selling sunroof control cables and/or helix cables for passenger vehicles through 
a Joint Venture Company (“JV Co.”), which would be incorporated after execution of the Joint 
Venture Agreement (“JVA”)  
 
The Company shall own 49% of the shareholding in the JV Co. and shall be infusing funds up to 
an amount of Rs. 2,45,00,000/- (Two Crores and Forty-Five Lakhs Only), in one or more tranche 
in the JV Co. out of which an amount of Rs. 49,00,000/- (Forty-Nine Lakhs Only) shall be infused 
at the time of incorporation of the JV Co. Further, the remaining 51% of the shareholding shall be 
owned by TDH. 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 read along with SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 
dated November 11, 2024, the Company shall submit the detailed disclosure with the stock 
exchanges upon signing of JVA. 
 
You are requested to take the same on your record.
