Sub:  Intimation of sale and transfer of project specific SPV/Subsidiary. 
 
महोदय / महोदया 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, it is informed that after receipt of necessary consideration by REC 
Power Development and Consultancy Limited (“RECPDCL” -a wholly owned subsidiary 
of REC Limited), the entire shareholding of Vikramshila Power Transmission Limited, 
comprising of 50,000 equity shares, held by RECPDCL, has been transferred along with 
all its assets and liabilities on November 21, 2026 to the successful bidder selected through 
tariff based competitive bidding process. 
 
Hence, with effect from the aforesaid date, the above project specific SPV/subsidiary is 
not the subsidiary of RECPDCL & REC Limited. The other requisite details are attached 
herewith as Annexure-1.
