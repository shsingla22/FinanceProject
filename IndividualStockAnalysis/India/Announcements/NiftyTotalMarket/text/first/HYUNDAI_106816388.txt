Hyundai Motor Group to Accelerate India Growth with USD 15.5 
Billion Automotive Ecosystem Investment  
 
▪ Hyundai Motor Group to invest USD 15.5 billion from 2026 through 2032, positioning 
India as a core strategic market driving future growth and expansion across 
emerging markets  
▪ Hyundai Motor Group Executive Chair Euisun Chung and Indian Prime Minister 
Hon’ble Shri Narendra Modi Ji discuss India’s automotive industry future and long -
term growth strategy    
▪ Hyundai Motor Group ’s investment spans manufacturing expansion, R&D 
infrastructure development, local supplier cultivation and future technology 
innovation to advance India’s automotive industry ecosystem  
▪ Hyundai Motor Group to establish a dedicated investment entity in India for seamless 
execution and efficient capital management  
▪ Investment to strengthen India's automotive sector, enhance global technology 
competitiveness and bolster the nation as a global export hub  
 
NEW DELHI, October 13, 2026  – Hyundai Motor Group (the Group) today announced a 
comprehensive investment strategy for India, committing USD 15.5 billion from 2026 through 2032 . 
The initiative positions India as a strategic core market for the Group's future growth, emerging 
market expansion, and next -generation automotive innovation.  
  
The initiative was discussed during a meeting between Indian Prime Minister Hon’ble Shri 
Narendra Modi Ji  and Hyundai Motor Group Executive Chair Mr. Euisun Chung  at the Prime 
Minister’s Office in New Delhi on October 13, 2026, reflecting the Group's commitment to 
strengthening its presence in India and contributing to the nation's automotive sector growth.

The seven -year investment spans manufacturing expansion, research and development (R&D) 
infrastructure development, local supplier cultivation for parts localization and future technology 
innovation - fostering a comprehensive automotive industry ecosystem  in India.  
  
To effectively execute this large -scale investment initiative, the Group will establish a dedicated 
investment entity  in India. The entity will systematically coordinate and execute investments across 
R&D infrastructure, future technologies and the broader automotive industry ecosystem. Additionally, 
it will serve as a platform to identify outstanding local partners, sup port strategic partnerships and 
foster collaboration with excellent local suppliers.  
   
“India remains one of Hyundai Motor Group’s most important and strategic markets globally. The 
past 30 years have been defined by our commitment to India. This investment reaffirms our 
commitment to India for the next 30 years and beyond. Through this inve stment, we will create more 
employment opportunities and work together with our partners and stakeholders to support India’s 
continued industrial development.” – Euisun Chung, Executive Chair, Hyundai Motor Group.  
  
How Will Hyundai Motor Group Strengthen India's Automotive Ecosystem?  
 
 The investment extends far beyond new facility development. The Group will build a comprehensive 
automotive ecosystem in the following key areas:  
  
▪ Manufacturing Expansion: The Group plans to expand manufacturing capacity across Hyundai 
Motor India Limited and Kia India Pvt.  Limited to support significant volume growth while ensuring 
localization to meet India’s market demands. This expansion will position India as one of the 
leading global export platforms for the Group.  
 
▪ R&D Infrastructure and Technology: The Group plans to establish a complete R&D system in 
India through infrastructure expansion and local talent cultivation . A key focus is strengthening 
technological competitiveness through the Group's Indian R&D center . With approximately USD 
700 million investment planned in the R&D center, including its vehicle testing track, the Group 
will accelerate next -generation automotive technology development and establish India as a core 
innovation hub.

What Economic Impact Will This Investment Generate?  
  
Hyundai Motor Group employs more than 24,000  people across India. This comprehensive 
investment is expected to create high -quality employment opportunities across manufacturing, R&D, 
engineering, and supply chain sectors. Beyond direct job creation at Hyundai Motor Group facilities, 
the investment w ill stimulate employment across the broader automotive ecosystem through supplier 
partnerships, technology development and emerging mobility ventures.  
  
The initiative is expected to contribute significantly to India's economic development while building 
a robust local talent pool in advanced automotive technologies.  
 
Why is India Important to Hyundai Motor Group's Future Growth?  
  
India represents one of the world's fastest -growing automotive markets and a critical platform for 
The Group's mid -to-long-term strategy.  
  
Hyundai Motor Group has established comprehensive production capacity of more than 1.4 million  
vehicles annually across India, encompassing HMIL's facilities in Chennai (824,000 units capacity), 
HMIL's latest manufacturing facility in Pune (170,000 units capacity), and Kia India's Anantapur plant 
(431,000 units capacity). As Pune's capacity is sche duled to be scaled up in phases, the Group's 
India manufacturing footprint will continue to expand.
