Press Release  
Netweb's H1 FY27 profits  exceeds full -year FY26; quarterly revenue and PAT up over 4  times  
  
Delhi - NCR, 2 4th October  2026: Netweb Technologies India Limited (Netweb), India’s leading Indian origin, owned and 
controlled OEM in the space of High -end Computing Solutions (HCS), announced its Q 2 FY27 results today. The Board 
of Directors of Netweb at its meeting held on 2 4th October  2026, took on record the unaudited Financial Results for 
the second  quarter of Financial Year 2026 -27. Netweb offers a full stack of products and solutions to various end user 
industries such as IT, ITES, BFSI, National Data Centres and Govt. entities such as Defence, Academia and R&D 
Institutions.

Commenting on the  results, Mr. Sanjay Lodha , Chairman and Managing Director, Netweb Technologies said: 
 
“We are pleased to report that Q2 FY27 has been another record quarter for Netweb Technologies, with our highest -ever 
quarterly Revenue from Operations and Profit After Tax. H1 FY27 PAT reached ₹2,220 Mn, surpassing our full -year FY26 PAT 
of ₹2,058 Mn in ju st six months, underscoring the scale of our business and strong growth momentum.  
 
In Q2 FY27, Revenue from Operations grew 335.7% YoY to ₹13,233 Mn. Operating EBITDA increased 334.0% YoY to ₹1,973 
Mn, with a margin of 14.9%, while PAT rose 334.8% YoY to ₹1,367 Mn, translating into a margin of 10.2%. Our ability to sustai n 
margins while scaling , reflects strong execution and operational discipline amid rising demand for high -performance computing 
and AI infrastructure.  
 
AI Systems remained the primary growth driver, contributing ₹7,731 Mn (58.4% of Revenue from Operations) and growing 
1,068.1% YoY. HPC and Private Cloud also delivered robust performance, contributing ₹2,482 Mn and ₹2,448 Mn, respectively, 
highlighting the  strength of our diversified growth portfolio. As of 30 September 2026, our order book stood at ₹26,290 Mn, with 
an additional L1 position of ₹6,525 Mn, providing strong revenue visibility for the coming quarters.  
 
The quarter also marked a significant milestone in our growth journey, as we successfully raised ₹12,000 Mn through our first  
capital raise since listing in July 2023. We are grateful to our investors for their overwhelming response and continued 
confidenc e in our business, growth strategy, and long -term potential.  
 
The world is witnessing an unprecedented surge in demand for AI compute, with India emerging as a significant growth market. 
Government bodies, public institutions and large enterprises increasingly view compute as strategic infrastructure, prioritis ing 
secure, high -performance systems that enable critical data and workloads to remain within the country while optimising AI token 
economics. Achieving cost -efficient token generation requires not just access to advanced compute, but also strong engineering, 
system design and solutioning capabilities. Netweb has invested in building these capabilities over the years, positioning us t o 
address the evolving requirements of this market.  
 
Simultaneously, global cloud service providers (CSPs), data centre operators, enterprises, and Neocloud providers are expandi ng 
GPU -intensive infrastructure to support the growing computational demands of AI training, inference and other AI -driven 
applicat ions, with India emerging as an important destination for their expansion plans.  
 
These developments, coupled with the growing need for indigenous computing capabilities and end -to-end technology stacks, are 
reinforcing the importance of the Sovereign infrastructure. With our integrated design and engineering capabilities, in -house 
manu facturing and end -to-end solution deployment expertise, Netweb is well positioned to meet these requirements.  
 
Our strategies for supercomputing & private cloud have also delivered excellent results. Furthermore,  as the National 
Supercomputing Mission (NSM) 2.0 places greater emphasis on designing and building in India, and HPC adoption expands 
across enterprises, indigenous design & manufacturing and  bespoke solutioning are becoming increasingly important 
differentiators in the high -end computing landscape.  
 
We believe these structural drivers will support a sustained growth cycle in high -end computing space. Our active pipeline of 
₹1,11,518 Mn provides significant visibility into potential business opportunities and reinforces our confidence in the growt h 
outlook.  
 
As a leading Indian -origin OEM in High -end Computing Solutions, we continue to advance our technological capabilities 
through sustained investment in innovation and a chip -agnostic design philosophy. Combined with our alignment with the Make 
in India visio n, a strengthened balance sheet following the recent capital raise and a robust order book, these strengths position 
us to capitalise on the expanding demand for high -end computing, deliver sustainable long -term growth and create enduring 
value for our sta keholders. ” 
 
About Netweb Technologies   
Netweb Technologies is India's leading Indian -origin, owned and controlled High -end Computing Solutions (HCS) provider, with fully integrated design, 
manufacturing and deployment capabilities . Netweb’s HCS offering comprises HPC, Private cloud and HCI, AI systems, High performance  storage (HPS) and Data 
Centre Servers. Founded in 1999, and headed by Mr. Sanjay Lodha, Chairman and Managing Director, Netweb has helped many compa nies globally by following a 
customer centric approach. Netweb has manufacturing facility in Delhi - NCR an d 22 offices across India. Netweb got listed at NSE and BSE in July 2023 .
