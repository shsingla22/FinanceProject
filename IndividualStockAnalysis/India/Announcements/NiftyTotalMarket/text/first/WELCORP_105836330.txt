Subject: Disclosure of information pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Appointment of Mr. Nitin Agarwal as CEO- 
Steel Business. 
 
We are pleased to announce the appointment of Mr. Nitin Agarwal as CEO - Steel Business, with 
effect from May 06, 2024. 
 
Mr. Nitin Agarwal is associated with Welspun Group for over 19 years with a proven expertise in 
Global Supply Chain Management, Business Development, Sales & Marketing.  As a leader, he 
has garnered a distinguished track record in Logistics and Shipping, Procurement and techno 
commercial business acumenship in the field of Steel pipes for O&G and Water sector. After a 
successful decade-long stint in the USA, as the supply chain and commercial function head for US 
operations, Mr. Agarwal is currently President - Global Supply Chain & Marketing at Welspun 
Corp. He was instrumental in commissioning our Little Rock facility from Greenfield to fully 
operational Spiral & HFIW plants, with a capacity of over 500,000 MTPA. 
 
He holds Master's degree in Business Economics & a Bachelor's degree in Commerce, along with 
various other leadership programs from reputed institutes like IIM-B & ISB Hyd. 
 
The above is in compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and the Company’s Code of Practices and Procedure for Fair 
Disclosure of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information.
