Ola Electric to Unveil Next Chapter of Ola Energy on Independence Day  
 
Bengaluru, August 11, 2026:  Ola Electric today announced that it will unveil the next 
chapter of Ola Energy  on August 15, 2026, across its official channels.  
 
As India works towards greater energy independence, building indigenous energy 
technologies and capabilities has become increasingly important. Ola Electric remains 
focused on developing technologies that strengthen India’s energy ecosystem and 
contribute to the country’s energy  independence.  
 
This year, the Company will not hold an on -ground Sankalp event . Instead, the 
announcements will be unveiled directly across Ola Electric’s official channels on 
Independence Day.  
 
Further details will be shared on August 15, 2026.  
 
About O la Electric Mobility Limited  
Ola Electric Mobility Limited is India's leading electric vehicle (EV) manufacturer. It specialises in the 
vertical integration of technology and manufacturing for EVs and their components, including battery cells. 
The Ola Futu re factory in Tamil Nadu, where EVs and critical components are produced, is developing 
India's most significant EV hub. It is supported by Ola's Bengaluru -based Battery Innovation Centre (BIC), 
dedicated to advancing cell and battery technology. Ola's R&D  efforts span India, the UK, and the US, 
focusing on innovative EV products and core components. Ola maintains a direct -to-customer distribution 
network of thousands of stores across India and a robust online presence, making Ola Electric the largest 
compa ny-owned network of automotive experience centres in the country.
