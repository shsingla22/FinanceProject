In terms of  Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015,

issuance of 1,100,000 (One Million One Hundred Thousand only) Equity Shares of INR 5 (Five) each to the 
PSPL ESOP Management Trust (the ‘ESOP Trust’) of the Company .  
 
The Members at the 31st, 33rd  and 34th Annual General Meeting of Persistent  Systems Limited (‘the Company’)  
held in July 2021, 2023 and 2024, respectively, had approved the resolutions enabling the Company to issue 
fresh Equity Shares to the ESOP Trust,  which administers the ESOP Schemes of the Company.  
 
In accordance therewith, the Board of  Directors  approved the issuance of 1, 100,000 (One Million One Hundred  
only) Equity Shares of INR 5 (Five) each to the ESOP Trust.  
 
Further, the Board has authorized the Stakeholders Relationship and ESG Committee to allot the said Equity 
Shares to the ESOP Trust in single and/or multiple tranches at the respective price of the underlying options 
and also delegated the other procedural authorities in this regard.
