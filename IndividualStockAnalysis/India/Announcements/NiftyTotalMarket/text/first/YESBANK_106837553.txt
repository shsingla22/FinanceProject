Ref: Regulation 30 , Regulation 51  and other applicable provisions of the Securities and 
Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“Listing Regulations”) . 
We refer to the prior disclosures made by YES Bank Limited ( the “Bank ”) including on 
October  15, 2026, October  16, 2026, October 18, 2026 and  October 24, 2026 regarding the 
judgment delivered by the Hon’ble Supreme Court regarding the Tier 1 (AT -1) Bonds issued 
by the Bank (the “SC Judgement ”). As previously disclosed, the Bank is examining the 
judgment in detail, including evaluating all legal options and suitable actions  / steps going 
forward.  
 
We wish to disclose that the Bank has also communicated with Axis Trustee Services Limited 
(in its capacity as the trustee for the AT -1 Bonds issued by the Bank) (the “Trustee ”) as regards 
the SC Judgement on October 18, 2026 and October 24, 2026. The Trustee has subsequently on 
October 28, 2026 issued a letter to the Bank (the “Letter ”), in terms of which, the Trustee has 
called upon the Bank to re -instate the AT -1 Bonds and take all necessary  corporate actions 
within 3 (three) days. The Trustee has also stated in its Letter that unless such steps are taken 
within the timelines specified in the Letter, the Trustee will be constrained to initiate contempt 
proceedings before the Hon’ble Supreme C ourt of India against the Bank and its directors and 
officers without further notice on the grounds of wilful  disobedience by the Bank of the SC 
Judgement.  
  
The Bank has responded to the Letter on October 30, 2026 ( “Reply ”) informing the Trustee 
that the Bank acknowledges the SC Judgment and reiterated that the Bank is fully committed 
to act in accordance with the SC Judgment and applicable law. The Bank has informed the 
Trustee that the Bank is cognizant of the matters and  is examining the SC Judgment and its 
implications in detail. The Bank will take all such steps and measures as are necessary and 
permissible under applicable law.  
 
A copy of the Trustee’s Letter and the Bank’s Reply are enclosed herewith and the Bank will 
continue making the requisite disclosures as may be required under the Listing Regulations.
