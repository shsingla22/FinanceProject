c.	 A	brief	on	types	of	customers
 GDS Customers – The GDS business serves a diverse range of customers spanning the globe, including large 
multinational generics companies, generic divisions of major pharmaceutical firms, regional and national-level players, 
R&D companies specializing in dossier development with niche focuses, and distributors.
 CMS Customers – The CMS business primarily caters to innovators seeking an API partner for API development and 
manufacturing throughout the clinical lifecycle, regulatory filing, and commercial supply stages. These customers 
encompass small biotech firms, medium-sized innovators, as well as major pharmaceutical companies.
