LEMON TREE HOTELS ANNOUNCES SIGNING OF MOU WITH OMAXE  
FOR OPERATING ~ 4,000 KEYS  
 
Company’s largest single operating arrangement increas ing its development 
pipeline to nearly 16,000  keys in total  
 
New Delhi, 16th October , 2026 :  Carnation Hotels Private Limited , a wholly 
owned subsidiary  of Lemon Tree Hotels L imited , one of India’s largest hotel chains, 
has entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (“MOU”) with Omaxe L imited,  
a prominent real estate developer , to operate ~ 4,000 keys in multiple key 
destinations in India .  
The proposed hospitality sites shall be located in transit hubs, pilgrimage 
destinations and commercial districts spanning across Lucknow, Vrindavan, 
Amausi, Amritsar, Ghaziabad, Greater Noida , Kaushambi and Faridabad . 
Commenting on this signing, Mr. Neelendra Singh, Managing Director, Lemon 
Tree Hotels L imited, said, “Tier-2 and Tier -3 cities have long been central to our 
India growth strategy, and this operating arrangement lets us scale it faster, from 
transit hubs in Lucknow to temple towns like Vrindavan. Omaxe brings nearly four 
decades of real estate development experience across these markets , while Lemon 
Tree brings over two decades of hospitality expertise and guest trust. This alliance 
combines developer scale with operating strength, and two trusted Indian brands 
with a proven record of execution, to build the next phase of growth.  This will 
accelerate  Lemon Tree Hotels’ asset -light growth strategy, enabling the Company 
to expand its managed portfolio across diverse markets and asset formats. ”  
 
Mr. Mohit Goel, Managing Director, Omaxe Ltd ., said, “India’s hospitality 
opportunity is expanding beyond established metropolitan markets, with Tier 2 
and Tier 3 cities emerging as important destinations for business, leisure and 
pilgrimage travel. Temple towns, growing commercial centres and trans it hubs,  
each have distinct accommodation needs, creating an opportunity to develop 
hospitality assets that are aligned with the demand drivers of their respective 
markets. Our arrangement with Lemon Tree Hotels brings established operating 
expertise to this strat egy, while our sale -led development model enables us to 
pursue this expansion with a disciplined approach to capital deployment.”  
 
 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India's leading hospitality companies, 
catering to a wide range of customers – from value -conscious travellers to 
premium business and leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels 
& Resorts, Lemon T ree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys 
Select, and Keys Lite – the group offers experiences across upper upscale, 
upscale, upper midscale, midscale, leisure, wildlife, and spiritual segments.  
LTHL operates 130+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing 
pipeline of 140+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, 
Bengaluru, and Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, 
and Indore – and with an international presence in Bhutan and Nepal – Lemon 
Tree Hotels delivers exceptional comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, 
refreshing experience.  
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 270+ 
properties (operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for 
both business and leisure travellers.
