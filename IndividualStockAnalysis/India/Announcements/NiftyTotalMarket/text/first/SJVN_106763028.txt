PRESS RELEASE   
 
Successful Completion of 72 -Hour Trial Run of 660 MW Unit -2 of 1320 MW Buxar 
Thermal Power Project in Bihar  
 
Sh. Bhupender Gupta, Chairman & Managing Director, SJVN congratulated the employees 
and all stakeholders on the successful completion 72 -hour trial run operation of 660 MW Unit2 
of 1320 MW Buxar Thermal Power Project in Bihar on 31st August, 2026.  
 
Sh. Bhupender Gupta further highlighted that with successful completion of the trial run, the 
company is now all set for Commercial Operation of Unit -2.  
 
Sh. Ajay Kumar Sharma, Director (Personnel), SJVN, emphasized that the successful 72 -hour 
trial run of Unit -2 marks another significant milestone in SJVN’s journey. He congratulated the 
entire team for their relentless hard work, professionalism and unwavering commitment, 
despite various challenges.  
 
Sh. Parthajit De, Director (Finance), SJVN, also extended his congratulations to all and 
highlighted that the successful trial run of Unit -2 further strengthens SJVN’s generation 
portfolio and reinforces its position as a multidimensional energy company co ntributing to 
India’s sustainable energy growth.  
 
Sh. Rajesh Kumar Chandel, Director (Projects), SJVN, congratulated the employees and 
appreciated the concerted efforts made towards completion of the unit and stated that it is a 
significant achievement in SJVN’s thermal power journey.  
 
The 1320 MW Buxar Thermal Power Project, being implemented by SJVN Thermal Private 
Limited (STPL), a wholly owned subsidiary of SJVN, is based on state -of-the-art supercritical 
technology, ensuring higher efficiency and reduced environmental impact. The project 
comprises two units of 660 MW each and is equipped with advanced emission control 
technology to minimize its environmental impact. Once fully commissioned, the project wil l 
generate approximately 9,828.72 million units of electricity annually, contributing significantly 
towards meeting the nation’s growing power demand.
