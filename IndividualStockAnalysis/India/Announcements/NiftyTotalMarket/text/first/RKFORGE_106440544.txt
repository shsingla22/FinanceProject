Sub:  Announcement under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Outcome of Board Meeting  
 
Further to our intimation dated 11 November, 2025 and pursuant to the Regulation 31A(8) of the 
SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ( SEBI LODR 
Regulations ) the Board of Directors of the Company at their meeting held today i.e., Wednesday, 
12 November, 2025, considered and approved the request received from Ramkrishna Rail 
Infrastructure Private Limited  (RRIPL) , a member of the “Promoter Group ”, holding 65,00,000 
Equity Shares  of the Company  (3.59%), seeking reclassification from “Promoter Group ” category  
to “Public’ Category ” shareholder, subject to the approval of the Stock Exchanges , shareholders  
and such other approvals/confirmations/consents as may be necessary in terms of the SEBI 
LODR  Regulations.  
 
The rationale submitted by RRIPL  inter -alia states  that:  
 
i. RRIPL  holds 65,00,000 Equity Shares of face value of Rs. 2 each, constituting 3.59% of the total 
Equity Share Capital of the Company.  
 
ii. They are not directly or indirectly associated with the business of the Company and do not 
have any control over the management of the Company in respect of the policy decisions in any 
manner or are engaged in the management or operations of the Company in  any manner.   
 
iii. None  of the member of the Promoter & Promoter Group of the Company controls more than 
one-half of the total voting power of RRIPL or controls the composition of the Board of RRIPL.  
 
The Board further took note of the confirmation received from RRIPL that they meet all criteria 
specified under Regulation 31A of the SEBI LODR Regulations for reclassification. Accordingly, 
the Board approved the request for reclassification  from ‘Promoters Group’ Category to ‘Public’ 
Category, subject to the receipt of no -objection of the Stock Exchanges  and the approval of the 
shareholders of the Company.
