4. With regard to the Shipbuilding contract for construction of 2 ships with a Government Customer, the contractual 
delivery dates (as extended) for both the vessels has already expired. At the request of the Customer for reallocation 
of the vessel for other prospective buyers, the delivery of ship has been abated with minor progress. The company 
has provided for LD for the delay upto 29 Apr 2023 and 30 Oct 2023 in respect for the two ships. Since the Company 
has a valid contract with the Customer, the Company has not recognized further liquidated damages in the financials 
beyond the dates mentioned above. 
5. During the nine months ended 31st December 2024, the Company has provisionally capitalized ISRF project cost 
from Capital Work in Progress (CWIP) for an amount of Rs 76731.99 lakhs due to the commercialisation of the 
project for limited review purposes. The final capitalisation adjustments may change based on the completion of 
remaining formalities and further assessments by management.
