Boost to Atmanirbharta  in Naval/Commercial Shipbuilding for 
Marine Engineering, as KSSL and GRSE Sign MoU   
Kolkata (March, 05, 2026) - To strengthen India’s Shipbuilding capabilities in Marine Engineering including 
Propulsion Systems, Steering Gea r and Integrated Platform Managemen t Systems , through collaborative 
participation , Kalyani Strategic Systems Limited (KSSL) and Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers Ltd. (GRSE) 
signed a MoU on Thursday , 05 Mar 2026.  
The partnership envisages collaboration between the sides based on mutual strengths to provide Indigenous 
solution in Ship Propulsion fo r Naval and Commercial ships . This is expected to contribute enhancement in India’s 
maritime technological strengths as well as self-reliance in move segmen t for long term operational capabilities.

About GRSE 
Garden Rea ch Shipbuilders & Engineers Ltd. (GRSE) is a leading Indian shipyard under the administrative control 
of the Ministry of Defence (India), specializing in the design and construction of advanced warships for the Indian 
Navy and Indian Coast Guard. Headquartered in Kolkata, GRSE has built a strong reputation for delivering a wide 
range of naval platforms including frigates, corvettes, and patrol vessels. With decades of experience in indigenous 
shipbuilding, the company plays a key role in strengthening India’s  maritime defence capabilities and supporting 
the country’s vision of self -reliance in defence manufacturing under initiatives like Make in India.  
About Kaly ani Strateg ic Systems Limited 
Kalyani  Strategic  Systems Limited  (KSSL) is a  wholly  owned  subsidiary of  Bharat Forge  Limited,  established  to 
drive  defence  and  aerospace  initiatives  in India.  Leveraging  more  than  five decades  of expertise  in design, 
engineering,  and  manufacturing,  KSSL  develops  advanced  defence  technologies  across  platforms  including 
artillery,  protected  vehicles,  ammunition,  air defence,  marine  systems,  and defence  electronics.  KSSL  supports 
India’s goal of self -reliance in defence under the Atma Nirbhar Bharat initiative and collaborates with leading Indian 
and global partners to deliver indigenous, benchmarked defence solutions.
