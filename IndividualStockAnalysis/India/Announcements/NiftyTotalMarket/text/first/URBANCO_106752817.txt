A. The Settlor had established the Urban Company ESOP Trust (the "Trust") pursuant to a Trust Deed 
dated 9 October 2023, as amended and restated by the Amended and Restated Trust Deed dated 9 
March 2026 (the "Trust Deed"), for the administration and implementation of the Employee Stock 
Option Scheme 2015 of the Company. A copy of the Trust Deed was filed with BSE Limited and the 
National Stock Exchange of India Limited (collectively, the "Stock Exchanges") pursuant to 
Regulation 3(3) of the SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021 
(the "SBEB Regulations"). 
B. Under the Trust Deed, Ms. Neha Mathur is Trustee I and the Managing Trustee and Mr. Rakshit 
Kapoor is Trustee 2 of the Trust (hereinafter collectively referred to as the "Trustees"). 
August 2026. In tenns of Clause 7.4.2 of the Trust Deed, she shall accordingly cease to hold office as 
Trustee and Managing Trustee of the Trust. 
D. In tenns of Clauses 7.4.2 and 7:7 of the Trust Deed and the resolutions passed in writing dated 13 
August 2026, the Trustees are desirous of appointing the Incoming Trustee as a Trustee of the Trust in

minimum two (2) Trustees under Clause 7.1 of the Trust Deed, by way of this Deed of Variation.

requirements of the Trust Deed and the SEBI SBEB Regulations. 
NOW THEREFORE, THIS DEED OF VARIATION WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS: 
I. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION 
All capitalised terms used but not defined in this Deed of Variation shall have the meaning ascribed to 
them in the Trust Deed, unless specified otherwise. This Deed of Variation is supplemental to, and 
shall be read and construed as fonning an integral part of, the Trust Deed. 
2. CESSATION OF TRUSTEE 1 
With effect from the close of business hours on 31 August 2026 ("Effective Date"), Ms. Neha Mathur 
shall cease to hold office as Trustee I and as Managing Trustee of the Trust and shall stand discharged 
from her obligations under the Trust Deed, save that the indemnity available to her under Clause 4.2 
of the Trust Deed shall survive in respect of the period during which she held office as a Trustee. 
3. APPOINTMENT OF THE INCOMING TRUSTEE 
Mr. Utkarsh Bindal is appointed as Trustee in her place and hereby accepts office, with all the powers 
and duties of a Trustee under the Trust deed, and all references in the Trust Deed to "Trustee l" shall 
be read as references to Mr. Utkarsh Bindal.

Clause 7.5.5 of the Trust Deed shall be deleted 111 its entirety and shall be substituted with the
