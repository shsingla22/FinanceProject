Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (SEBI Listing Regulations), we wish to inform you

infused an amount of USD 10,00,000 in Cayman Integrated Healthcare Limited (CIHL), a wholly-owned 
overseas subsidiary of HCCI and a step-down subsidiary of the Company by way of subscription to its 
equity shares equivalent to USD 1 Million, i.e., 1,00,000 ordinary shares in the capital of the Company, 
each as fully paid, with the value of each ordinary shares being set at USD 10.00, which is comprised 
US$ 0.01 of par value and US$ 9.99 of share premium. The above-mentioned shares have been allotted 
by CIHL to HCCI on June 10, 2025 (Tuesday). With this investment, the total investment of HCCI in 
CIHL stands at USD 8,050,000.
