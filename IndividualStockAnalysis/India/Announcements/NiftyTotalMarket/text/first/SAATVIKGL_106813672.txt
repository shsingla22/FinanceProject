Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations, we wish to inform you 
that Saatvik Green Energy Limited (“ the Company ”) has received an order from Maharashtra State 
Electricity Distribution Co. Ltd. (MSEDCL)  for Design, Manufacture, Supply, Transport, 
Installation, Testing and Commissioning of Off-Grid DC Solar Photovoltaic Water Pumping Systems 
(SPWPS) of 3 HP, 5 HP and 7.5 HP capacity at identified farmer’s site in district of Maharashtra,  
including complete system warranty and its repair & maintenance and remote monitoring system for 5 
Years under Magel Tyala Saur Krushi Pump Yojana/ PM KUSUM B  scheme. The total order value 
of 5,000 Solar Photovoltaic Water Pumps is for an approximate amount of Rs. 100 Crores (exclusive of 
GST).
