TVS MOTOR COMPANY LAUNCHES ‘TVS INDUS ’ DESIGN 
HONOURS TO CHAMPION ‘DESIGN IN INDIA’  
 
• TVS Indus is a  national platform to unlock next -generation mobility ideas rooted 
in Indian context and creativity . 
• Invites young creators to shape solutions that are future- forward, culturally 
relevant, and globally resonant.  
 
Bangalore, August  4, 2025: TVS Motor Company (TVSM) — a global leader in the two and 
three -wheeler segment, has announced the launch of ‘TVS Indus’ Design Honours — a new 
annual platform to nurture visionary design talent and redefine the future of mobility. Anchored 
in design- led innovation,  the initiative seeks to amplify the ‘Make in India’ movement while 
championing the idea of ‘Design in India’. By focusing on solutions that are meaningful, inclusive, and deeply rooted in India’s diverse landscapes, lifestyles, and people, TVS Indus Desig n Honours aims to spotlight India as a global design powerhouse.  
 The term “Indus” draws from the Indus Valley Civilization, one of the world’s earliest and most 
advanced societies. It symbolizes TVS Motor’s aspiration to blend India’s rich design heritage 
with a progressive, forward- looking mindset. The initiative aims to revive the spirit of Indus — 
advanced, purposeful, and human -centered, in the context of modern- day mobility.  
 
“Design is the bridge between imagination and impact. With TVS Indus Design Honours, we 
are creating a platform where the next generation of designer s, thinkers and creators can 
reimagine Indian mobility as an original, culturally rooted expression of who we are and where 
we’re headed. This initiative reflects  TVS Motor’s commitment to design- led innovation and 
sustainable mobility . This platform intends to promote Design among the Indian youth and 
nurture talent from both design and non- design backgrounds  to help shape not just how we 
move but  ignite the global conversation on the future of mobility,” says Amit Rajwade, Vice 
President – Design, TVS Motor Company.  
 
Open to Indian residents including students, young professionals, and design enthusiasts, the 
2025 edition of the challenge is theme -based, drawing inspiration from four distinctive regions 
— Thar, Sahyadri, Kutch, and Mumbai. Participants are encouraged to submit individual 
entries that propose mobility concepts spanning monowheel, two- wheeler, and three- wheeler 
formats, while incorporating environmental, social, and cultural relevance. The challenge 
officially opens for registrations today, with the deadl ine for submissions set for Oct 06, 2025. 
 
Submissions will be evaluated by an expert jury comprising leading names from the world of design and mobility. Judging criteria include originality, contextual relevance, user -centricity, 
and feasibility. Top three winners will receive cash prizes up to ₹5 lakh, with additional 
recognition through a Viewers’ Choice award and opportunities for internship consideration 
with the TVS M Design Team.
