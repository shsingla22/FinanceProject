4. the Warrants shall be convertible into Equity Shares, in one or more tranches, within a period of 
18 months from the date of allotment.

shareholding norms prescribed for the Company under the SEBI LODR Regulations and the

Company shall ensure that the allotment of Equity Shares pursuant to the exercise of the
