Transrail to enter renewable power generation through proposed 
49% investment in 990 MW solar portfolio in Maharashtra  
 
Strategic investment to leverage Transrail’s project execution capabilities and create 
opportunities for long- term participation in renewable power generation  
Mumbai, October 19 , 2026  – Transrail Lighting Limited (BSE: 544317, NSE: TRANSRAILL), a leading Engineering, 
Procurement and Construction (EPC) company in the Power Transmission and Distribution sector, today announced 
that its Board of Directors has approved the proposed acquisitio n of a 49% equity stake in NOPL Pace Green Energy 
Private Limited (“NOPL”), at a cash consideration of Rs. 20 cr, subject to the requisite approvals and completion of 
agreed conditions.  
NOPL is developing an aggregate solar power portfolio of approximately 990 MW (AC) in Maharashtra under 
Component C of the Government of India’s PM -KUSUM scheme. The project is intended to support the solarisation of 
agricultural feeders and improve the av ailability of daytime electricity for irrigation.  
The portfolio is backed by a 25 -year Power Purchase Agreement with Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution 
Company Limited (MSEDCL) at a fixed tariff of ₹3.10 per unit. The indicative total project cost is approximately ₹3,900 
crore, and debt financing has been sanctioned by reputed financial institution, subject to applicable financing and 
disbursement conditions.  
Although Transrail will initially acquire a 49% equity stake, the investment is structured to provide the Company with 
financial and project management control under the agreed arrangements with Onix Renewable Limited. The 
agreement also provides flexibility for Transrail to increase its shareholding, potentially up to 100%, subject to mutually agreed terms and applicable approvals.  
The investment is expected to provide several strategic benefits:  
• Entry into renewable power generation: Participation in a large solar portfolio with a long -term contracted 
power offtake arrangement.  
• Leveraging existing expertise:  Application of Transrail’s capabilities in engineering, procurement, project 
management and execution of large power infrastructure projects.  
• Participation across the value chain: Opportunities in project implementation as well as long -term returns 
from ownership of a renewable power generation asset.  
• Diversification : Expansion beyond the EPC business into renewable power generation, with the potential for 
recurring earnings after commissioning.  
 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Randeep Narang, Managing Director & CEO, Transrail said: “This is an 
important step in our  journey from an EPC company to an integrated energy solutions provider. Our entry into solar 
power generation lets us participate across the full energy value chain from generation to transmission & distribution 
apart from our strong manufacturing base for Transmission products. We are proud to foray in India’s rapid growth renewable energy market. O n completion of this project, it will contribute towards supply of reliable & green power to 
farmers  easing irrigation and contributing to nations development . We as Transrail will bring speed to the project with 
our vast experience in managing large infra projects. Such projects will bring steady long- term revenues and margins 
which will further strengthen our overall growth.”
