Dear Sir,  
 
This is with reference to your letter dated June 24, 2026 on the captioned subject, regarding recent 
news item which appeared in the https://www.moneycontrol.com  dated June 24, 2026  captioned 
‘Oberoi Realty shares rise 3% as firm receives RERA approval for Gurugram real estate project ’, 
requiring us to provide our clarification/confirmation in respect of the said news item . 
 
In this connection we wish to humbly submit that the website of the Haryana RERA shows that the said 
project has been registered with Haryana RERA, however the project registration number is yet to be 
issued, and registration certificate generation is in-process and is yet to be received by us.  The 
screenshot from Haryana RERA website is attached as Annexure ‘A’ . 
 
Also, we wish to state that the registration of a typical real estate project with the concerned RERA 
authority is a customary requirement of the provisions of The Real Estate (Regulation and 
Development) Act, 2016 , and is not an unusual, peculiar or a case specific approval. RERA registration 
is one amongst the several approvals that are required in the regular course of sale and development 
of real estate projects . Such approvals are procedural milestones in the development lifecycle of real 
estate project s. 
 
Also, we wish to state that the 14.8 acre Gurugram land under discussion was acquired by the Company 
in November 2023 and the intimation to this effect was made to the stock exchanges vide our letter 
dated November 17, 2023. Vide the said intimation, we had also disclosed that the transaction marks 
the Company’s foray into the NCR region, and that the Company intends to primarily develop a luxury 
residential group housing project on the said land. A copy of the said intimation is attached as Annexure 
‘B’.  
 
Also, the said project has been routinely discussed at the quarterly post earnings conference call on 
results and business update , and the audio recording as well as the transcript thereof has been 
intimated to the stock exchanges  from time to time .
