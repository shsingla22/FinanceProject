PRESS RELEASE  
 
Choice Consultancy Secures Project Mandates worth Total Revenue of ₹140 Crore s across 
Housing, Agriculture, MSME & Urban Development Sectors  
New assignments strengthen CCSPL’s role in shaping India’s inclusi ve and sustainable growth story  
 
August 2 2, 2025 , Mumbai - Choice Consultancy Services Private Limited  (CCSPL), a leading consulting 
firm and a subsidiary of Choice International Ltd., has won prestigious project mandates worth 
approximately ₹1 40 crores (inclusive of GST). Spanning housin g, agriculture, MSME development,  
water resource management  and urban planning, these assignments reinforce CCSPL’s role as a 
trusted partner for high -impact devel opment initiatives across India . Details of key projects secured is 
given below:  
 
1. Slum Rehabilitation (Maharashtra | 72 Months):  CCSPL , in joint venture with its partners,  will 
provide architectural planning, project management, construction supervision, and quality 
control under MHADA’s SRA framework for redevelopment  of Rajiv Gandhi N agar, Malwani, 
Malad West, Mumbai . 
2. PACS Computerization (Odisha | 20 Months):  As System Integrator, CCSPL will digitize 1,625 
Primary Agricultural Credit Societies to boost efficiency, transparency, and farmer -centric service 
delivery.  
3. MSME strengthening under RAMP  (Himachal Pradesh , Rajasthan  | 24 Months):  CCSPL has been 
appointed by a government company to closely  work with MSME, SHG and Artisans of Himachal 
Pradesh and Rajasthan for bringing awareness about government scheme benefits, assisting 
them for Marketing and branding and ensuring quality pr oducts while on the other side will assist 
state government for better MSME  policy based on study and ground feedback . 
4. New Chandrapur City Development (Maharashtra | 84 Months):  CCSPL, in joint venture with its 
partners, will deliver urban strategy, feasibility studies, bid advisory, and quality control to 
support Chandrapur’s planned growth and infrastructure development.  
5. Yamuna Water Transfer DPR (Rajasthan  | 6 months ): CCSPL has been appointed by a 
government company for preparation of a detailed project report for transferring Yamuna water 
to Rajasthan’s arid districts to strengthen long -term water security and agricultural sustainability.  
6. Indira Gandhi Nahar Project (Rajasthan  | 18 months ): CCSPL has been appointed by a 
government company as Engineering Management Consultant under the  Rajasthan Water Sector 
Restructuring Project (RWSRPD), supported by the New Development Bank. The engagement 
covers rehabilitation and modernization of IGNP Stage -I to restore canal capacity, reclaim 
waterlogged land, improve irrigation efficiency, and pr omote climate -resilient agriculture and 
rural development.  
7. Water Resource Projects (Rajasthan | 6 –12 months):  CCSPL has been appointed by a 
government company to tak e Initiatives including  rejuvenation of the Sabi/Sahibi River, 
ecological restoration of Kanota –Chandlai –Nevta dams, and storm water management in 
 
Bharatpur and Deeg. These projects focus on enhancing water security, restoring ecosystems, 
and promoting sustainable resource use.  
“These project mandates represent a significant step in expanding our presence across diverse 
sectors —housing, urban planning,  MSME development, and digital transformation. Each engagement 
underscores our ability to deliver integrated, high -impact solutions that address complex 
developmental priorities. Our focus remains on creating sustainable, scalable outcomes that 
contribute to India’s long -term economic and social objectives,” said Mr. Arun Poddar, CEO of Choice 
International Ltd.  
 
With these assignments, CCSPL continues to reinforce its leadership in consulting and development 
advisory, supporting India’s mission of sustaina ble, inclusive, and technology -driven growth.
