As part of its ongoing international expansion, Elan Incorporated FZE, a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Time Technoplast Ltd., is pleased to announce the incorporation of Elan Steel 
Containers (FZC) in the Sharjah Airport Free Zone (SAIF Zone), UAE. With this development, 
Elan Steel Containers (FZC) has become a step-down subsidiary of Time Technoplast Ltd. 
This strategic addition reinforces our presence in the Middle East and marks our entry 
into steel drum manufacturing in the region. It aligns with our vision of offering complete 
industrial packaging solutions, complementing our existing range of polymer-based products 
including PE drums, jerry cans, Conipack pails, and IBCs. 
Globally, Time Technoplast is a trusted packaging partner to leading multinational 
corporations, operating in 11 countries with a leadership position in 8 markets. In India, our 
product mix already includes both polymer and steel drums, in line with global packaging 
practices that emphasize a comprehensive and complementary portfolio. 
The Middle East continues to be one of the Group’s fastest-growing regions, with operations 
in Sharjah, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, and Egypt. Forecasts indicate volume growth of over 15% 
p.a. for the next 3 years, driven by increasing demand from the chemical, petroleum, solvent, 
and construction sectors. 
The new Sharjah facility is built to international standards, featuring advanced automation 
and stringent quality control systems. Elan Steel Containers (FZC) will produce durable, high- 
quality steel drums compliant with global safety and regulatory norms, catering to the 
growing needs of our multinational customers across the region.
