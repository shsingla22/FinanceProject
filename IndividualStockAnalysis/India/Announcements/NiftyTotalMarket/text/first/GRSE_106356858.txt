Dear Sir / Madam,  
 
1. In terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015,  as amended (the “SEBI Listing Regulations”) , we 
hereby inform you that the Company  has received notices dated 2 9 Aug 2025 from 
National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) and BSE Ltd.  (BSE) regarding non-
compliance with the provisions of Reg 17, 17  (2A), 18 (1) , 19(1)/19(2) , 20(2)/(2A) and 
21(2)  of the SEBI Listing Regulations . The said non- compliances are pertaining to non-
composition of Board of Directors with optimum combination of Non -Executive 
Directors, Independent Directors including Women Independent Director  and also 
non-adherence to requirements pertaining to quorum of Board Meetings. Furt her, due 
to this, the company was unable to reconstitute Audit Committee, Nomination and 
Remuneration Committee, Stakeholder Relationship Committee and Risk 
Management Committee during the quarter ended 30 Jun, 2025.  In view of the said 
non-compliances, a fine of ₹12,54,340 /- has been imposed by each of the Stock 
Exchanges i.e. NSE & BSE.  
 2. In response to the aforesaid notices, the Company has submitted  a letter No. 
GRSE/BD -69/CG/Q1 /2025-26 dated 30 Aug 2025,  that Garden Reach Shipbuilders & 
Engineers Limited is a Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSE) under the 
administrative control of Ministry of Defence (MoD), Government of India. The 
President of India acting through MoD is the Promoter of the Company. The Directors 
on to the Board (including Independent Directors & Independent Woman Director) of 
the Company are appointed by the Government of India through Presidential Order s. 
Further, non- compliance has arisen due to non- appointment of Independent Direc tors 
including Woman Independent Director by the Government of India. The action for 
appointment of Independent Directors including Woman Independent Director on to 
the Board of CPSEs is not in the hands of the Company and also beyond the control 
 
 
of the Company. Therefore, GRSE has requested that it should not be held liable for 
the fines imposed and that the fines may be waived- off.  
 
3. The Company continues to actively pursue the matter with the Government of 
India  through Ministry of Defence  (Promoter of t he Company) , for the timely 
appointment of the requisite number of Independent Directors to ensure compliance 
with Corporate Governance norms enunciated under the SEBI LODR, 2015.  
 
4. This is for your kind information and record.
