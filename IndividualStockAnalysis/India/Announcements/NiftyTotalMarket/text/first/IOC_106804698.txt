This has reference to your letter/e -mail Ref. No. NSE/CM/Surveillance/17637 dated 30 September 2026 
seeking clarification on the news item appearing in The Hindu titled “Indian Oil plans to launch packaged 
drinking water under own brand across 43,000 fuel outlets.”  
 
In this regard, we would like to submit that the initiative is a routine commercial proposal to augment the 
Non -Fuel Revenue (NFR) of the Company. IndianOil continuously explores various business opportunities and 
customer -centric initiatives in the ordinary course of business to earn NFR by leveraging its extensive Retail 
Outlet (RO) network . 
 
The Company has only floated an Expression of Interest (EoI) to seek interest of the aggregators for the 
business proposal. The initiative, if implemented across the RO’s in phases, would not have a material financial 
impact on the Company when viewed in the context of Company’s scale of Operations, Revenue (consolidated 
revenue of Rs. 9,01,452 crore during 2025 -26), Profitability (consolidated profit Rs. 43,667 crore for 2025 -26), 
Net Worth (Rs. 2,25,547 on 31.03.26) and overall business activities .
