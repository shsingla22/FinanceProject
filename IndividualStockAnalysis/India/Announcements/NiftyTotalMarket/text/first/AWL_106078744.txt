Adani Wilmar names Shrikant Kanhere as the  
Deputy Chief Executive Officer  
 
~ This appointment is in addition to his current profile as the Chief Financial Officer~  
 
Ahmedabad, December 5, 2024:  Adani Wilmar Limited (AWL), one of India’s largest food & 
FMCG companies, today announces the appointment of Mr. Shrikant Kanhere as the Deputy 
Chief Executive Officer ( Dy. CEO) with immediate effect . Based in Ahmedabad, Mr. Kanhere will 
assume this role in addition to his current profile as the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) of the 
company.  
 
Mr. Kanhere, who joined AWL in 2013 as the CFO, brings over 25 years of rich experience 
spanning across consulting, manufacturing, services and the FMCG sector. Before his tenure at 
AWL, he was associated with  Reliance Industries Limited and Vodafone Digil ink Limited, further 
enriching his diverse professional background.  
 
Following this appointment, Mr. Kanhere and Mr. Saumin Sheth, COO will work closely with MD 
& CEO Mr. Angshu Mallick to manage the overall operations of the company.  Mr. Kanhere will 
be mainly responsible for business development, investor relations and financial management 
while Mr. Saumin Sheth will continue to lead sourcing of raw materials , trading  and 
manufacturing operations.  
 
The Board of Directors believe that this new arrangement will enable AWL to strengthen its 
management for the next phase of growth.
