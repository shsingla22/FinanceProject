For decades, the Indian ceiling fan market has largely been dominated by induction fans, valued for their high speed and air 
delivery despite higher energy consumption. While BLDC fans have gained traction for their superior energy e Ưiciency and sma rt 
features, consumer perception has often associated them with lower speed and reduced air delivery. Addressing this long-

Leading the portfolio is India's First Super Hi-Speed BLDC+ Fan , engineered to deliver performance that goes beyond

at 400 RPM, the fan eliminates the traditional trade- oƯ between the high -speed performance of induction fans and the energy 
eƯiciency of BLDC technology. Equipped with a powerful silent motor, the fan also features an innovative AC Mode that 
intelligently circulates cooled air across the room for enhanced comfort, lower noise, and optimized energy consumption. 
Additional features include up to 65% electricity savings2, reverse rotation, sleep mode, RF remote control, timer settings of 
up to eight hours, and a 5-star energy rating. 
Expanding its innovation portfolio further, Havells also introduced India’s ﬁrst Silent Air Max BLDC+ ceiling fan range with dual 
control, designed for consumers seeking a quieter home environment without compromising on cooling performance. 
Operating at a whisper-quiet noise level of just 50 dB1, the fan combines silent operation with powerful air delivery and elegant

intelligent AC Mode for uniform cooling. The fan also o Ưers reverse rotation, sleep mode, RF remote operation, up to 65%
