on May 09, 2025, has recommended a final dividend of ₹ 7/ - per equity share of

approval of the Members of the Company at the forthcoming 27th Annual General 
Meeting.  
 
The Final Dividend will be paid to Shareholders holding equity shares of the 
Company, either in electronic or  in physical form, after determining eligibility of 
Shareholders entitled for payment of dividend, which will be based on the record 
date i.e. July 04, 2025. The payment will be made on or after August 08, 2025.
