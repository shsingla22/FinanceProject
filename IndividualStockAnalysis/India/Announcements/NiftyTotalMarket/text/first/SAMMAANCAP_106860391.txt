Sammaan Capital Reports Strong Q2 FY27 Performance; 
Disbursements Rise  62%  QoQ and PAT Grows 28%  
AUM reaches ₹59,188 crore; asset quality strengthens with GNPA at 0.15% and NNPA at 0.11% 
Mumbai, November 14, 2026:  Sammaan Capital Limited (“Sammaan Capital” or “the 
Company”) today announced its financial and operational performance for the second 
quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026, reporting strong sequential growth in disbursements and profitability, alongside an improvement in asset quality.  
The Company’s Assets Under Management (AUM) stood at ₹ 59,188 crore  as of September 
30, 2026, compared with ₹56,239 crore at the end of Q1FY27. Disbursements during the quarter increased to ₹ 6,278 crore , registering a growth of 62% over the preceding quarter. 
The Company disbursed approximately 30,700 loans during Q2FY27,  taking total 
disbursements for H1FY27 to ₹10,153  crore across approximately 42,600 loans.  
Profit before tax (PBT) for Q2FY27 stood at ₹425 crore , up 30% sequentially from ₹326 crore 
in Q1FY27. Profit after tax (PAT) increased 28% quarter -on-quarter to ₹312 crore , compared 
with ₹243 crore in the previous quarter. PAT for H1FY27 stood at ₹556 crore.  
Asset quality continued to strengthen during the quarter. Gross Non- Performing Assets 
(GNPA) improved to 0.15% , compared with 0.21% in Q1FY27, while Net Non -Performing 
Assets (NNPA) declined to 0.11%  from 0.15%. The Company reported a blended cumulative 
collection efficiency of 99.6%. Its total provisions represented 10.2% of the loan book, reflecting a conservative provisioning approach.  
The Company’s net worth s tood at ₹19,445 crore  as of September 30, 2026, excluding the 
proceeds of ₹906 crore received from the first tranche of warrant conversion completed on October 30, 2026. Capital adequacy remained strong at 20.0%.  
Strengthening capital and strategic partner ship 
During the quarter, Sammaan Capital continued to strengthen its institutional and governance framework. H er Excellency  Dalia Khorshid joined the Board as an IHC nominee 
director, while  BDO was appointed as the Company’s internal audit co -sourcing part ner. The 
Company also strategically realigned its control functions of risk, compliance, internal audit 
and cybersecurity to work under the supervision of the Board Sub -Committee and domain 
experts at IHC Centre of Excellence.  
Subsequent to the quarter end, Avenir (IHC) converted the first tranche of warrants into 8.69 
crore equity shares at ₹139 per share, resulting in an infusion of ₹906 crore . Following this 
conversion, the promoter shareholding increased from 28.34% to 33.32%. The remaining 21.98 crore warrants are proposed to be converted by September 2027, subject to applicable terms and conditions, for an additional investment of ₹2,291 crore. Upon full conversion, the promoter shareholding is expected to increase to 43.28%.

2 Expanding distribution and diversifying the product portfolio  
Sammaan Capital continued to expand its distribution footprint, increasing its branch network 
from 217 to 245 branches across 20 states . The Company is deepening its presence in Tier 2 
and Tier 3 markets, with a focus o n reaching underserved customer segments and expanding 
its MSME franchise.  
In personal loans, the Company is progressing from portfolio purchases towards building its 
own origination capabilities through its existing branch network. Its Business Instalment Loan 
(BIL) offering is being prepared for launch. In gold loans, product policies have been approved, with a soft launch planned for Q3FY27 and a commercial launch planned for Q4FY27, subject to market conditions.  
Sammaan Capital also continues to leverage its established mortgage franchise through an 
originate -and-assign model. The Company has cumulatively sold down mortgage pools 
aggregating more than  ₹1 lakh crore across 24 bank and financial institution partners,  with 
less than 0.50% 90- plus days -past -due on sold- down pools.  
Investing in people, technology and governance  
The Company continued to build organisational capacity to support its next phase of growth. It onboarded 1,123 employees during H1FY27, with 95% of hires entering retail functions and 
87% deployed at branches. The Company also strengthened its senior leadership bench with 
20 senior hires during the half year.  
Technology investments remain a key enabler of the Company’s operating transformation. 
Salesforce has been rolled out to pilot branches, with a wider rollout targeted during Q3FY27. The Company has also implemented five IT projects during Q2FY27, including an API gateway and multi -factor authentication on its core system. It is progressing with  its broader 
technology roadmap, including upgrades to its loan management systems and customer -
facing digital platforms.  
FY27 outlook  
Sammaan Capital remains focused on executing its FY27 growth roadmap, with full- year 
targets that include AUM of ₹70,700 crore , a loan book of ₹ 53,000 crore , disbursements of 
₹30,100 crore  and PAT of ₹1,400 crore . The Company is also targeting an expansion of its 
branch network to 270 branches by the end of FY27.  
The Company’s longer -term ambition is to progress from its cur rent AA+ credit rating towards 
an AAA rating, supported by strong capitalisation, sustained asset quality, strategic support, funding and liquidity, and enhanced management and governance standards. Its roadmap also includes achieving a sustainable Return on Managed Assets (ROMA) of above 2%.
