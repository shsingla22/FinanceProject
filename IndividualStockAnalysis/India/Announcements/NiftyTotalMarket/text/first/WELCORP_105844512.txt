Subject: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015. 
 
We would like to inform about resignation of Mr. Priyaranjan Kumar from the position of the Chief 
Human Resource Officer of the Company due to personal reasons, with effect from the close of business 
hours on 30th July 2024. Other disclosures as required in terms of the SEBI Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD- 1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 are not applicable. 
 
Copy of signed resignation letter given by Mr. Priyaranjan Kumar is attached in terms of sub-clause 
7(C) of Para A of PART A of Schedule III under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. 
 
Consequently, we are pleased to announce the appointment of Ms. Bindu Nambiar as Head HR, with 
effect from May 13, 2024. 
 
Ms. Bindu Nambiar has an illustrious career spanning over three decades, marked by her expertise 
across diverse industry segments including Pharma, Retail, and HR Consulting. Joining the Welspun 
Group in 2014, she has navigated through various pivotal roles such as Head of Global Talent 
Acquisition and Head HR for Group Functions & Central Services. 
 
She holds B.Pharm, Dip. in Export Management, Advanced program in HRM. Ms. Bindu Nambiar is 
not related to any Director or KMP of the Company. 
 
The above is in compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and the Company’s Code of Practices and Procedure for Fair 
Disclosure of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information.
