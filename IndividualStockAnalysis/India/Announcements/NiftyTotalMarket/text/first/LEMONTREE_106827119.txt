LEMON TREE HOTELS SIGNS KEYS LITE BY LEMON TREE HOTELS, 
BHILWARA 
 
With this addition, the Group will have 14 operational and 10 upcoming hotels in 
Rajasthan.  
New Delhi, 23rd October, 2026: Lemon Tree Hotels Limited has announced the 
signing of Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels, Bhilwara , further strengthening 
its presence in Rajasthan. The property will be managed by Carnation Hotels 
Private Limited, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited. 
An important commercial centre in Rajasthan, Bhilwara is known for its textile 
industry and growing business activity. The city also provides access to the 
region’s cultural and heritage attractions, catering to both business and leisure 
travellers. 
The hotel will feature 63 well-appointed rooms, along with a restaurant, banquet 
facilities, meeting/conference room, a fitness centre and other public areas. The 
property will cater to the needs of business and leisure travellers, bringing the 
brand’s value-driven hospitality offering to the city. 
Commenting on the signing, Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, 
Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd.,  said, "Rajasthan continues to be an important growth 
market for us, defined by a strong mix of commercial dynamism and rich tourism. 
Bhilwara, a key textile and industrial centre, presents a natural opportunity for 
Keys Lite to cater to the rising demand for reliable, value-driven hospitality. This 
signing scales our footprint to 24 hotels in the state and underscores our focused 
approach to expanding across India’s key industrial hubs and emerging secondary 
markets." 
The hotel will be conveniently connected to key transport hubs. Bhilwara Railway 
Station is approximately 1.5 km from the property, while Maharana Pratap Airport, 
Udaipur, is approximately 148 km away . 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED 
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India's leading hospitality companies, 
catering to a wide range of customers – from value-conscious travellers to 
premium business and leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels 
& Resorts, Lemon Tree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys 
Select, and Keys Lite – the group offers experiences across upper upscale, 
upscale, upper midscale, midscale, leisure, wildlife, and spiritual segments. 
LTHL operates 130+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing 
pipeline of 140+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, 
Bengaluru, and Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, 
and Indore – and with an international presence in Bhutan and Nepal – Lemon 
Tree Hotels delivers exceptional comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, 
refreshing experience. 
Since opening its first 49-room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 270+ 
properties (operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for 
both business and leisure travellers.
