Digit Insurance  Settles Over 6 Lakh Claims in H1 FY27 ; 
Health Cashless Approvals Average 25 Minutes  
• Over 2,000 Gujarat floods motor claims worth ₹28 .5 crore settled at the end of H1FY27 . 
• Average health reimbursement claims settled in 2.4 days ; fastest in 5.4 hours . 
• Customer grievances closed in under 2 days on average; 95% resolved in first interaction.  
Bengaluru, 16 November 2026:  Go Digit  General  Insurance  Limited  (“Digit”) , one of India’s 
leading new -age insurance companies, today released its 15th Transparency Report , offering a 
detailed account of its claims, customer service and financial performance for H1 FY27. During the 
period, the company settled over 6.2 lakh claims , up 32% year -on-year, while cashless health pre -
authorisation requests were approved in an average of 25.1 minutes.  
The report further revealed that Digit settled 2,109 motor claims following the Gujarat floods, 
paying ₹28 .5 crore by the end of H1FY27 . During the same period, a verage health reimbursement 
claims were settled in 2.4 days, while customer grievances were closed in under two days on 
average, with 95% resolved during the first interaction.  
On its busiest day in H1 FY27, Digit settled 11,750 claims  in a single day . Since inception, the 
company has served over 9.4 crore customers and settled more than 48 lakh claims, with a cashless 
network spanning over 23,500 garages and 10,500 hospitals across India.  
Here is a snapshot of key insights from the 15th Transparency Report : 
• Health Claims:  Cashless facility approval requests were approved in an average of 25.1 
minutes , compared with 26.9 minutes in H1 FY26. Reimbursement claims were settled in 
an average of 2.4 days .  
• Motor Claims:  The largest third -party motor claim paid during the period stood at ₹1.9 
crore  for a private car fatal accident  and ₹1.4 crore  for two -wheeler .  
• Customer Grievances:  Grievances were closed in an average of 1 day and 23 hours , while 
95% of customers  received a resolution during their first interaction with the call centre.  
• Policy Processing:  Retail motor insurance proposals were processed in under 30 minutes 
on average , while retail health proposals were processed on average in 5 hours 2 minutes .  
• Peak -Day Capacity:  Digit issued up to 3.5 lakh policies  and settled as many as 11,750 
claims  in a single day, demonstrating the scale supported by its operating systems.  
The company also transparently highlighted service areas where turnaround times increased during 
the period. Average vehicle repair approval time rose from 14 hours 44 minutes to 17 hours 39 
minutes , while cashless health discharge approval time rose from 52.5 minutes to 63.6 minutes . 
Digit also recorded longer processing times for certain motor policy servicing requests, including 
registration and nomination changes, identifying these as areas requiring further improvement.  
Digit launched its Transparency Report in 201 8 to give customers and other stakeholders a clear 
and open view of how the company performs across claims, service and operational parameters.
