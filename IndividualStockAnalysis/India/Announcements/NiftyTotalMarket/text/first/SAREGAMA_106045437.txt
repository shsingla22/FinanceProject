Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Saregama India Limited (‘the Company’)  received a Notice from your good office on 13th September, 
2024 (Reference No. NSE/LIST-SOP/COMB/FINES/1080), imposing a penalty of Rs. 10,000 (plus GST) 
for failing to file the XBRL utility of Voting Results as required under Regulation 44 (3) of the SEBI 
Listing Regulations for the AGM held on 27th August, 2024. 
 
In accordance with NSE circular Ref. No. NSE/CML/2023/74 dated 17th October 2024, SEBI Master 
Circular vide SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/2023/120 dated 11th July, 2023 and Regulation 17(3) of SEBI 
Listing Regulations, the said non-compliance which has been identified by the Stock Exchange and 
subsequent action taken by the Stock Exchange in this regard, shall be placed before the Board in the 
next Board Meeting and the comments made by the Board shall be duly informed to the Exchange. 
 
We wish to inform your good office that the Board in its Meeting held on 5th November, 2024, 
reviewed and noted the non-compliance and provided the following comments: 
 
‘The Board acknowledges that, in response to the penalty, the Company promptly paid Rs. 10,000 plus 
18% GST via NEFT, under UTR No. CMS/001530255894, on 16th September, 2024, and informed NSE of 
the payment. Additionally, the Company submitted a waiver application on 19th September, 2024, 
accompanied by a waiver fee of Rs. 10,000 plus 18% GST (UTR No. CMS/001531373362). In the waiver 
application, the Company clarified that the non-compliance was an isolated technical error and an 
inadvertent oversight, with no adverse impact on stakeholders.  
 
The Managing Director has recommended establishing an internal control system to integrate event-
based, quarterly, and financial compliance, into regular monitoring activities.

Subject: Notice for non-compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”) 
Your attention is drawn towards SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/2023/120 dated 
July 11, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as “Master Circular”), specifying Standard Operating 
Procedure for imposing fines and suspension of trading in case of non-compliance with the Listing 
Regulations. On verification of the Exchange records, it has been observed that your Company has 
not complied/delayed complied with certain Listing Regulation(s). The details of non-
compliance(s)/delayed compliance(s), total fine payable by your Company and the particulars 
about manner in which fine should be remitted to the Exchange is enclosed as Annexure.
You are requested to inform the Promoters about identified non-compliance/delayed compliance 
and to ensure compliance with respective regulation(s) and/or make the payment of fines within 
15 days from the date of this notice, failing which the Exchange may initiate following actions as 
per Master Circular:
1. Initiate freezing of entire shareholding of the Promoters in the Company as well as in other 
securities held in the Demat account of the Promoters.
2. Trading in securities of your Company shall take place on a ‘Trade for Trade' basis, in case 
of consecutive default with Regulations 33 of the Listing Regulations i.e., Shifting of 
trading in securities to Z Category as per Master Circular.
You may also file request for waiver of fines with detailed submission indicating reasons for 
waiver, considering the extant Policy for Exemption of Fines, and shall indicate whether it intends 
to seek personal hearing before the concerned Committee. The Company may file request for 
waiver of fines levied. 
However, before filing an application for waiver of fines, you are requested to refer to the below 
policies available on the Exchange’s website. For ready reference you may refer below links:
i. Policy on exemption of fine:
