Committees.

not due to any negligence / default by the Company. Accordingly, Indian Oil should not be held liable to pay 
the fines and the same should be waived-off. IndianOil regularly  takes up with MoP&NG for appointment of 
requisite number of Independent Directors (including Woman Independe nt Director) to ensure compliance 
with Corporate Governance norms enunciated under SEBI (LODR) as well as the Companies Act.   
 
We would also like to inform that the Company had received similar notices from the BSE & NSE in the past 
imposing fines and waiver request from the Company was considered favorab ly by the Exchanges.
