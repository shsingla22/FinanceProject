PRESS RELEASE  
FOR IMMEDIATE DISSEMINATION  
Medanta Super Speciality Hospital Ushers in  a New Era of Quality 
Healthcare in the Northeast with Bhoomi Poojan of its Upcoming 
Hospital in Guwahati  
-- Hon. Assam Chief Minister Dr Himanta Biswa Sarma the guest of honour for ceremony  
 
Guwahati, October 31 , 2025:  Global Health Limited “Medanta” , one of the largest private                        
multi -specialty tertiary care providers operating in the North and East regions of India, took an 
important step towards delivering world -class healthcare  in the Northeast states with a Bhoomi 
Poojan ceremony in Guwahati to mark the beginning of construction for its upcoming 400+ bed                       
super -specialty facility  in the city . 
The event was graced by the Honourable Chief Minister of Assam Dr . Himanta Biswa Sarma  as the 
guest of honour of the event, and Dr. Naresh Trehan, Chairman & Managing Director, Medanta . 
Other dignitaries present at the occasion included Mr. Pankaj Sahni, Group CEO, Medanta , and senior 
Assam leaders  and members of the lo cal community.  
The upcoming state -of-the art 400+ bed facility  is set to come up at Industrial Estate, Sarusajai, 
Guwahati  along National Highway 27  at an approximate cost of Rs 500 crores.  It will deliver world -
class tertiary and quaternary care, encompassing a comprehensive range of super -specialties including 
a robust Transplant program (including that of the Kidney, Liver, Lung, Heart, Bone Marrow and a 
Robotic Transplant Program); Cardiolog y and Cardiac Surgery; Thoracic Surgery; Peripheral Vascular 
Surgery; Paediatric Cardiology and Paediatric Cardiac Surgery; Neurology, Neurosurgery, and 
Intervention Neurology; Orthopedics, including joint replacements; Medical, Surgical and Radiation 
Onco logy; Gastroenterology and GI Surgery; Nephrology and Urology; Respiratory Medicine and 
Intervention Pulmonology; ENT and Head & Neck Cancer; Plastic, Aesthetic, and Reconstructive 
Surgery; Endocrinology; Critical Care Medicine and Trauma Emergency; Ophtha lmology; and Dentistry 
and Maxillofacial Surgery , among other specialties, as well as all Diagnostic Services, including Labs, 
Radiology, and Nuclear Medicine  as well as Preventive Health Check -ups. 
Speaking about the upcoming super -specialty hospital, Dr. Naresh Trehan, Chairman and Managi ng 
Director of Medanta , shared, “North East India is a region that has long been underserved with respect 
to heath care services. At Medanta, we believe that quality healthcare should be a right, not a privilege, 
and this  new facility is being developed to meet the rising demand for advanced, accessible care, thus 
reaffirming our mission to make cutting -edge healthcare accessible to all, no matter where they live. 
With today’s Bhoomi Poojan ceremony, we take a significant step towards establishing a world -class 
hospital in Guwahati , one that will not only serve the people of the city but also benefit the entire North 
East region .” 
Medanta’s foray into the region will be transformative, setting new benchmarks in quality heal thcare 
delivery, and ensuring access to cutting -edge medical services such as advanced robotic procedures 
not only for Guwahati but also for the entire Northeastern region. Additionally, it is also expected to 
generate significant employment opportunities,  creating 5,000 -7,000 direct and indirect jobs across 
                                                                                                                               
various sectors, including healthcare, administration, and support services, contributing to the 
region’s overall socio -economic development.  
Stating that Guwahati is the gateway for the entire Northeas tern region of India, Mr. Pankaj Sahni, 
Group CEO, Medanta , said,  “There is an urgent need for quality healthcare in India due to rising 
lifestyle -related diseases and an ageing population. Medanta’s patient -centric approach makes us 
uniquely positioned to  address this need and deliver high -quality, accessible, and reliable healthcare 
for all. Guwahati  is often regarded as the gateway to the North East, and offers strong connectivity 
across the entire region. Our upcoming 400+ bed super -specialty hospital in Guwahati  will allow us to 
bridge the healthcare gap to underserved areas in the Northeast and bring world -class, high -end 
medical care within easy r each of over 50 million people. ” 
 
About Global Health Limited (Medanta):  
Founded by Dr. Naresh Trehan, a world -renowned cardiovascular and cardiothoracic surgeon who has 
been awarded the prestigious Padma Bhushan and the Padma Shri, the third - and fourth -highest 
civilian awards in India, and the Dr. B.C. Roy Award in recognition of his distinguished contribut ion to 
medicine.  
Global Health Limited (the “Company”) is one of the largest private multi -specialty tertiary care 
providers operating in the North and East regions of India, with key specialties cardiac science, 
neurosciences, oncology, digestive and hep atobiliary sciences, orthopedics, liver transplant, and 
kidney and urology, according to the report titled “An assessment of the healthcare delivery market 
in India, September 2022” by CRISIL Limited.  
Under the “Medanta” brand, the Company has a network o f six hospitals currently in operation 
(Gurgaon, Indore, Ranchi, Lucknow, Patna and Noida). Spanning an area of 5.5 million sq. ft., its 
operational hospitals have 3,362 installed beds as on September 01, 2025.  
Additionally, four hospitals with a planned bed capacity of approximately 2,000 are in various stages 
of planning or construction in Mumbai, New Delhi (Pitampura), South Delhi (GK), and Guwahati.                   
The Company provides healthcare services in over 30 medical specialties and engages over 2,000 + 
doctors  led by highly experienced department heads.
