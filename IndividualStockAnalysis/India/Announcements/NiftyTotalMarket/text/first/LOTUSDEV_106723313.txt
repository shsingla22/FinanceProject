Press Release 
Mumbai,  4th August  2026
Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Limited,  a leading  developer  of luxury  and ultra -luxury  
residential  and commercial  properties  in Mumbai,  with  a focus  on redevelopment  and 
joint  development  projects,  is pleased  to announce  its appointment  (through  its 
subsidiary)  as the Developer  for a landmark  commercial -cum -retail  redevelopment  
project  in Juhu,  Mumbai .
The project  involves  the redevelopment  of one of Juhu’s  prominent  and well-
established  commercial  shopping  complexes  and is strategically  located  in a prime  
area  of Juhu,  Mumbai .
The proposed  redevelopment  will comprise  a premium  commercial  and retail  
development,  with  an estimated  total  carpet  area  of approximately  3.46 lakh  sq. ft. 
and an estimated  Gross  Development  Value  (GDV)  of ₹1,600 crore . The project  is 
expected  to commence  in Q3 FY28 and is targeted  for completion  within  approximately  
3–4 years  from  commencement .
This appointment  further  strengthens  the Company’s  redevelopment  portfolio  and 
reinforces  its presence  in Mumbai’s  premium  commercial  and retail  real estate  
segment .
Mr. Anand  K Pandit,  Chairman  & Managing  Director,  Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  
Limited,  said,  "This  appointment  reinforces  our growing  presence  in Mumbai's  
commercial  redevelopment  landscape,  an segment  that  is gaining  meaningful  
momentum . Commercial  establishments  across  the city's  prime  micro -markets  
are increasingly  opting  for redevelopment  as a route  to unlock  value,  upgrade  
infrastructure,  and create  modern,  compliant  spaces  that  meet  today's  
occupier  standards . Juhu,  given  its strategic  location  and limited  fresh  supply,  
is well positioned  to benefit  from  this shift . We see continued  demand  from  both  
institutional  occupiers  for well-executed  commercial  redevelopment,  and we 
remain  committed  to delivering  projects  that  combine  design  quality  with  long -
term  value  creation  for all stakeholders . 
This project  adds  to our expanding  portfolio  of redevelopment  and is consistent  
with  our strategy  of deepening  our footprint  in high -value,  supply -constrained  
pockets  for commercial  redevelopment  in Mumbai ."Sri Lotus Developers Appointed as Developer for  a Larg

2About  Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Limited
Incorporated  in 2015  (formerly  AKP Holdings  Limited),  Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Ltd. is 
a Mumbai -based  real estate  developer  specializing  in luxury  and ultra -luxury  residential  
and premium  commercial  projects,  with  a strong  focus  on redevelopment  in the western  
suburbs  of Mumbai . The company  has completed  4 projects  aggregating  over  4.2 lakh  
sq. ft. of carpet  area,  with  8 ongoing  and 14 upcoming  developments  covering  ~44.4 lakh  
sq. ft. of carpet  area  and ~35.4 lakh  sq. ft. of saleable  area .
With  a strong  presence  in Juhu  and Andheri  West . the company  has also  expanded  into 
other  micro  markets  of Mumbai  like South  and Central  Mumbai,  Bandra,  and Versova . 
Lotus  Developers  manages  end-to-end execution —from  design  and construction  to 
sales  and customer  engagement —ensuring  consistent  quality,  superior  materials,  and 
customer  satisfaction . Guided  by its philosophy  of creating  inclusive  and high -quality  
living  spaces,  the company  is shaping  some  of the most  coveted  addresses  in Mumbai’s  
real estate  landscape .
