Datamatics ® Recognized as a Major Contender for the 6th 
Consecutive Year in Everest Group’s Finance and Accounting 
Outsourcing (FAO) PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2025  
 
Mumbai , 23rd September 2025 :  Datamatics, a global Digital Operations Technology, 
and experience  company, has announced that it has been recognized as a Major 
Contender  by Everest Group  for the  6th consecutive year  in the Finance and Accounting 
Outsourcing (FAO) PEAK Matrix® Assessmen t 2025 . 
Datamatics has improved its scores on both Market Impact (Market Adoption) and 
Vision & Capabilities, reaffirming its position as a trusted partner for global enterprises. 
Everest Group has further recognized Datamatics as a Tier 1 player under the  IT+ BPS 
category, underscoring its expanding Finance & Accounting process coverage through 
FINATO ®.  
FINATO ® is a comprehensive digital suite that delivers AI -powered finance 
transformation for enterprises, bringing together artificial intelligence, machine 
learning, automation, analytics, and mobility. It unifies core functions across Procure -
to-Pay (P2P), Order -to-Cash (O2C), Record -to-Report (R2R), Travel & Expense (T&E), 
Contract Management, and Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A). By integrating  these 
modules into a single intelligent platform, FINATO enables enterprises to streamline 
operations, ensure compliance, and empower CFOs with real -time insights for faster, 
data-driven decision -making.  
Key strengths of Datamatics FINATO as mentioned in the Everest Group FAO 
PEAK Matrix ® 2025 :  
 FINATO ’s AI-driven enhancements across P2P and O2C modules, including AI -
enabled collections, predictive insights, and low -code/no -code UI with a mobile 
version for anytime access.  
 Datamatics strengthened FINATO by  embedding advanced AI/ML 
functionalities such as AI -enabled vendor evaluation and shortlisting, and AI -
powered auto extraction of invoice data in P2P module  
 AI capabilities in O2C module include AI -enabled collections and dunning 
strategies, and agentic w orkflows in its integrated receivables module  
 Strong partnership with Microsoft to build AI -powered solutions such as 
Copilots for Accounts Payable, Credit Risk Management, Collections, and Cash 
Applications  collaboration with Google , enhancing advanced analytics 
capabilities for clients.  
 Datamatics has notable experience in serving SMB and mid -market clients, with 
a strong focus on the Manufacturing industry
