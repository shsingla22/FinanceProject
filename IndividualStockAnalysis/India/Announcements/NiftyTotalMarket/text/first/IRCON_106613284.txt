  Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure

A. Board Comments on fine levied by the Exchanges for the quarter ended 31st December 2025: 
 
 
This is with reference to the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (“NSE”) letter and BSE Limited 
(“BSE”) email dated 27th February, 2026, regarding imposing fine on the Company for non-
compliance with Regulation 17(1), 18(1) and 19(1)/19(2) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 i.e. non-
compliance for the composition of the Board and its committees i.e. Audit Committee and Nomination 
& Remuneration Committee for the quarter ended 31st December, 2025, to which the Company has 
sent clarification letter dated 28th February, 2026 to NSE and BSE. The same was also intimated to 
NSE and BSE vide our letter No. IRCON/SECY/STEX/124 dated 28th February, 2026. 
 
As advised by the Exchanges, the aforesaid matter of non-compliance was placed before the Board 
in its meeting held on Thursday, 7th May, 2026, wherein the Board deliberated and noted the position 
of non-compliances with respect to the composition of Board of Directors and its committees i.e., 
Audit committee and Nomination & Remuneration committee and the fine imposed by the 
Exchanges. In this regard, the Board noted that “Ircon International Limited (IRCON) is a 
Government Company as per provisions of Section 2(45) of the Companies Act, 2013. As per 
provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and Articles of Association of the Company, the President of 
India vests with the power to appoint Directors (including Independent Directors/Woman Director) on 
the Board of IRCON. All Directors in IRCON are appointed by the Government of India through its 
Administrative Ministry, Ministry of Railways (MoR) and IRCON has no role to play in the appointment 
of any Director including Independent Directors/Woman Director unless nominated by the 
Government of India. IRCON has been continuously requesting MoR for the appointment of requisite 
number of Independent Directors (including Woman Independent Director) on the Board of IRCON 
to ensure compliances pertaining to composition of the Board and its committee and accordingly levy 
of fine is unreasonable and not applicable to IRCON.” BSE Limited
