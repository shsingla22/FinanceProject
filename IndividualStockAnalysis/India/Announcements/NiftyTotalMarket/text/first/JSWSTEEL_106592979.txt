JSW Steel Named 2026 'Sustainability Champion' by the World Steel Association  
Mumbai, India –  April  17, 202 6 – JSW Steel Ltd. ("JSW Steel" or the "Company") is pleased to announce 
that it has been recognised as a Sustainability Champion by the World Steel Association (worldsteel) for 
the eighth consecutive year.  
worldsteel's Sustainability Champions Programme  honou rs member organisations that demonstrate a 
robust and consistent commitment to sustainable development. To qualify, companies must meet three 
core criteria. First, they must provide verifiable evidence of meeting 20 criteria spanning environmental, 
social,  governance, and economic (ESGE) dimensions, as defined by worldsteel's Sustainability Charter—
a globally recognised framework outlining the principles and practices for responsible and sustainable steel production. Second, they must submit Life Cycle Inve ntory (LCI) data covering over 60% of their 
crude steel production to worldsteel. This data tracks and quantifies environmental inputs (e.g., raw materials and energy) and outputs (e.g., emissions and waste) across the cradle -to-gate life cycle of s teel 
products. Third, they must have received formal industry recognition through a nomination for a 
worldsteel Steelie Award  or acknowledgement under the association’s Safety and Health Recognition 
Programme . 
Commenting on the recognition, Jayant Acharya, Joint Managing Director and CEO -  JSW Steel , said: 
"Being named a worldsteel Sustainability Champion for the eighth consecutive year reflects the 
seriousness with which sustainability is embedded in JSW Steel’ s business strategy. We view sustainability 
not as an obligation, but as a key source of long -term competitiveness —shaping how we invest, innovate, 
and operate at scale. This recognition validates our belief that disciplined execution and measurable 
progre ss are essential as the steel industry transitions toward a low -carbon, resilient future.”  
As the global steel industry accelerates its transition toward low -carbon production, sustained recognition 
of this nature underlines that JSW Steel's sustainability  agenda is not only clearly defined, but also 
measurable, credible, and consistently advancing. JSW Steel extends its sincere appreciation to all internal 
stakeholders whose dedication to sustainable operations and value creation made this achievement 
poss ible. This recognition strengthens the Company's resolve to continue delivering sustainable value that 
endures for its stakeholders, its communities, and the planet, in a manner aligned with its  ethos of being 
#BetterEveryday .
