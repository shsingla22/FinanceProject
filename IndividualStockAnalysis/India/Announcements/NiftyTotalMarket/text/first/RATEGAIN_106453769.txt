RateGain and Arpón  Enterprise Forge Strategic Partnership to Redeﬁne Hotel Technology with Integrated 
PMS and Channel Manager Solution  
 
Noida, 25 November 2025: RateGain Travel Technologies Limited (RateGain ), a leading provider of AI -
powered SaaS solutions for the travel and hospitality industry , today announced a strategic partnership with 
Arpón Enterprise, a leading provider of hotel management solutions. This collaboration unites RateGain’s UNO 
Channel Manager with ArpónWin Surﬁng® PMS, creating a powerful, all -in-one platform designed to maxi mize 
revenue and streamline operations for hotels in a competitive market.  
 
The integrated solution brings together operational and commercial functions, bridging the gap between property 
management, distribution, and pricing, to help hotels automate decisions, ensure price parity, and drive higher 
profitability in real time.  
 
The partnership is already demonstrating signiﬁcant success, with the Hotel Luure CDMX serving as a ﬂagship case 
study. The hotel was seeking to optimize its pricing and distribution strategy to increase revenue, requiring a solution 
that offered seamless multi -channel automation, advanced analytics, and scalability.  
 
"By integrating UNO Channel Manager by RateGain with our ArpónWin Surfing® PMS, we are providing hoteliers with 
an unparalleled competitive edge,"  said Brisceida Beltrán, Sales Manager at Arpón Enterprise.  "This partnership 
is about more than just technological integration; it's about creating a cohesive ecosystem where data -driven decisions 
lead to tangible revenue growth and operational excellence."  
 
Key beneﬁts of the integrated Arpón -RateGain solution include:  
 
● Maximized Direct Bookings: A potential to increase proﬁtable direct reservations by up to 20% through a 
more eﬃcient and integrated booking engine.  
● Advanced Rate Intelligence: Leverages real -time market data for strategic pricing decisions, ensuring hotels 
remain competitive.  
● Seamless Multi -Channel Automation: Synchronizes availability and rates instantly across all Online Travel 
Agencies (OTAs) and the hotel's direct website, preventing overbookings and rate parity issues.  
● Centralized & Scalable Control: An intuitive and fully auditable platform that provides a single source of truth 
for hotel operations, ideal for both individual properties and multi -property groups.  
 
"At RateGain, our mission is to help every hotel unlock revenue potential through smarter, connected technology”, 
added Ashish Sikka, SVP and Business Head -UNO, RateGain . "Our partnership with Arpón Enterprise allows us to 
extend the reach of our distribution leadership, combining UNO, the only channel manager recognized by the four 
largest OTAs globally, with a robust and agile PMS. This directly translates to increased revenue and simpliﬁed 
operations for our mutual clients."  
 
Hoteliers interested in learning more about the integrated solution are invited to contact the partners for a 
personalized consultation.
