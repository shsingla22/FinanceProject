HCLTech recognized as a Leader in the IDC MarketScape for Worldwide Experience 
Build Services 2025  
 
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, Dec 15, 2025 —HCLTech , a leading global technology company, 
has been recognized as a Leader in the IDC MarketScape for Worldwide Experience Build 
Services 2025  Vendor Assessment (doc #US52973125, October 2025) .  
 
IDC defines Experience Build Services as offerings that help enterprises design, develop and 
implement customer -facing products that deliver superior experiences. These services 
integrate Customer Experience (CX) strategy, design thinking, personalization,  analytics and 
advanced technologies like AI and automation to create seamless, engaging experiences 
across digital and physical touchpoints.  
According to the report, HCLTech was recognized for the following strengths: “HCLTech 
combines capabilities in experience cloud suite implementation with a more recent focus on 
digital marketing services. HCLTech has strong personalization and data and ana lytics 
capabilities. It offers leading -edge implementation services and has strong organizational 
change capabilities supported by a strong set of supporting assets and tools. In 
conversations with HCLTech reference clients for experience build services, t hree highly 
commended areas were its client understanding and insight, the quality of its professionals, 
and value for money.”  
 
“At HCLTech, we are committed to building impactful total experiences that connect every 
part of our customers' ecosystem,” said Pawan Vadapalli, Corporate Vice President and Global 
Head, Digital Business Services, HCLTech. “As AI -driven personalization, r eal-time 
engagement and omnichannel consistency become the new standard,  HCLTech AI Foundry 
helps in deliver ing adaptive, human -centric experiences that evolve in real time, 
strengthening brand presence, fostering deeper connections and driving measurable 
business value.”  
 
“HCLTech  has established itself as a trusted partner in Experience Build Services , combining 
strong capabilities in experience cloud suite implementation and digital marketing services 
with an agile delivery model and strong client -centric approach. Organizations seeking a 
strategic partner capable of delivering large -scale, complex transformations that bring 
together IT services with experience platform implementation and AI -driven personalization 
can benefit from  HCLTech ’s mature practice and experience b uild capabilities,” said Douglas 
Hayward, Senior Research Director, Customer Experience Services and Strategies, IDC.

About IDC MarketScape:  
IDC MarketScape vendor assessment model is designed to provide an overview of the 
competitive fitness of technology and service suppliers in a given market. The research 
utilizes a rigorous scoring methodology based on both qualitative and quantitative cri teria 
that results in a single graphical illustration of each supplier’s position within a given market. 
IDC MarketScape provides a clear framework in which the product and service offerings, 
capabilities and strategies, and current and future market succe ss factors of technology 
suppliers can be meaningfully compared. The framework also provides technology buyers 
with a 360 -degree assessment of the strengths and weaknesses of current and prospective 
suppliers.
