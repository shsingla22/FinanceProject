Brief Profile of Mrs. Prerana Puthran  
 
Mrs. Prerana Puthran joined GIC Re in the year 1992 and have been associated across various  profiles 
during her tenure within GIC Re. She is a commerce graduate and a Fellow of  Insurance Institute of 
India (FIII), has handled various departments such as Underwriting,  Enterprise Risk Management, 
Claims Vertical, Corporate Communications, Investor  Relations and Credit Rating during her tenure 
in the Corporation. Presently she is holding the  post of Deputy General Manager and heading the 
Enterprise Risk Management Department,  CSR and Investment Mid Back and has acquired relevant 
experience in  the risk management framework of the Corporation. She has been actively involved  in 
various risk governance initiatives, risk assessment exercises, monitoring mechanisms and  reporting 
requirements of the Corporation.
