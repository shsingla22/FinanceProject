continue as a going concern, disclosing, as applicable, matters related to going concern, and using the

6. The Board of Directors is also responsible for overseeing the Company's financial reporting process. 
Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Statement

in accordance with Standards on Auditing, specified under section 143(10) of the Act, will always detect 
a material misstatement when it exists. Misstatements can arise from fraud or error and are considered 
material if, individually or in the aggregate, they could reasonably be expected to influence the economic 
decisions of users taken on the basis of this Statement.
