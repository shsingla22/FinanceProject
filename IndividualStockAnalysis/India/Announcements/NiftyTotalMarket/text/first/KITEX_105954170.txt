KITEX GARMENTS REPORTS 32% GROWTH IN REVENUE QoQ AND 280% 
GROWTH IN PROFIT BEFORE TAX.  
KITEX GARMENTS LTD ., world’s second largest manufacturer of infant  garments , declared its 
financial results for the first quarter ended June’2024 .  
 
Total Revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 20 24 stood at Rs.19 5.02 crore, as compared to Rs. 147.84  
crore in the same period last year, reflecting a growth of 32% primarily driven by strong demand from 
existing customers.   
With our continued focus on efficiency, profit before tax increased by 2 80% from Rs. 10.42 crore to 
Rs. 39. 57 crore.  The company’s EBITDA margin is at 23.77 % in Q 1FY24-25 as compared to 11.19% 
in Q1 of previous year . The Net Profit stood at Rs. 29.95  crore  for the current quarter, as compared to 
Rs. 7.99 crore for the same period last year  and PAT margin is at 15.36  % in Q 1FY24-25 as compared 
to 5.41 % for Q1 of previous year .
