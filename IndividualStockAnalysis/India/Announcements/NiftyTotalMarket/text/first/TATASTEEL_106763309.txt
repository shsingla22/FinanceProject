Sub:  Divestment of entire stake held in  Jamshedpur Football and Sporting Private 
Limited, wholly owned subsidiary   
 
This is further to our disclosure dated August 14 , 202 6, on the abovementioned subject 
wherein Tata Steel Limited (‘ Company ’) had announced the execution of Share Purchase 
Agreement (‘SPA’) for sale of 100% shareholding in Jamshedpur Football and Sporting  Private 
Limited (‘ JFSPL’ ) to Churchill Brothers Sports Club Private Limited (‘ Churchill’ ), subject to 
satisfaction of certain conditions precedent, including receipt of necessary approvals from the 
All-India Football Federation .  
 
This is to inform you that, the Company  has today i.e. on August 31, 2026 completed the 
above transaction  in accordance with  the terms and conditions  of the SPA. Accordingly, JFSPL 
has ceased to be a subsidiary of the Company.  
 
At the same time, Tata Steel  remains firmly committed to its long legacy of developing 
grassroot sports, expanding access  to sports and strengthening India’s sporting ecosystem. It 
will invest further in enha ncing our sporting infrastructure  at the Tata  Football Academy , 150 
of whose cadets have represented the national team over the years.  The Company will also 
further modernis e and build capability of our youth system, including  Tata Steel Foundation’s 
football training centres which have produced the majority of the players in Jharkhand’s U -14 
and U -16 boys’ teams. Tata Steel ’s legacy in sports goes well beyond football and is  spread 
across 19 disciplines . The Company runs  4 additional world -class sporting academies focused 
on archery, hockey, sport climbing, and rowing. The Tata Archery Academy , India’s premier 
archery institution  counts  8 Olympians among its alumni. The Naval Tata Hockey Academy 
provides elite coaching and high -performance training, nutrition and mental conditioning 
across 3 regional development and 32 grassroots centres.  Tata Steel Chess India, a rapid and  
blitz tournament held in Kolkata, is the only such tournament in Asia, and is part of the Grand 
Chess Tour, attracting World Champions.
