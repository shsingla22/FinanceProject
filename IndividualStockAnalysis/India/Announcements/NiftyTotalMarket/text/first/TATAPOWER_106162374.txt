Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we wish to inform you that TP Solar Limited (TP Solar), a wholly owned subsidiary of Tata Power 
Renewable Energy Limited (TPREL) and a subsidiary of The Tata Power Company Limited (Tata 
Power), has secured a contract worth ₹632 crore from the Solar Energy Corporation of India Limited 
(SECI) for the supply of 292.5 MWp DCR (Domestic Content Requirement) Solar Modules in Ramagiri, 
Andhra Pradesh. 
 
TP Solar, which operates India’s largest single-location solar manufacturing plant, continues to contribute to the country's clean energy transition and facilitate large-scale solar installations. The project is scheduled for delivery over four months, from October 2025 to January 2026.
