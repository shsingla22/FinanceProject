Limited dated 21st November, 2024 alleging non-compliance with Regulations 17(1), 18(1), 19, 20,
and 21(2) of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, and imposed a fine of Rs. 76,700/- (inclusive of
18% IGST) which was duly placed before the Board for its consideration and comments.
The Board after discussion opined that the non-compliances arise out of non-appointment of 
three Directors in the place of three Directors whose terms of office ended on 25th September, 
2024. 
The Company is governed by the Regulations of the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting 
(MIB) and Ministry of Civil Aviation, (MoCA) Government of India and hence, the prior approval 
for the appointment of any new Director have to be obtained from both MIB and MoCA. The 
Board of Directors considered the appointment of three new Independent Directors namely, Mr. 
Mandalapu Harinarayanan Harshavardhan, Mr. Ravivenkatesh Pragadish Karthik and Mrs. 
Jagadeesan Gayathri and also applied for the prior permission to appoint the aforesaid Directors 
sufficiently in advance on 5th April, 2024 to MIB and on 16th April, 2024 to MoCA. The Company 
received the approvals from MIB & MoCA on 5th September, 2024 and 3rd October, 2024 
respectively. On receipt of the approvals, the board appointed the three new Independent 
Directors with effect from 07th October 2024.
