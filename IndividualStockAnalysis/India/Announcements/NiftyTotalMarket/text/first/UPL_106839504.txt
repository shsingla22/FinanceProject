Management Remarks on Q 2 Performance   
Jai Shroff,  Chairman & Group CEO, UPL Limited said, “Our results this quarter reflect the strength of the diversified model we h ave 
built. Despite a volatile global backdrop, our businesses continued to execute with discipline; Advanta and SUPERFORM deliver ed 
strong growth, while our crop protection businesses navigated phased demand and challenging weather .  
With rising commodity prices potentially setting the stage for better farm economics, we remain focused on building a stronge r and 
future -ready UPL, and on creating long -term value for all our stakeholders. ” 
 
Bikash Prasad , Group CFO, UPL Limited, added , ”Our first half performance underscores the resilience of UPL's earnings in a 
challenging environment. Eight consecutive quarters of margins and EBITDA growth reflect our continued focus on quality of 
earnings, sharper portfolio decisions and firm cost and cap ital discipline. These choices have allowed us to protect margins despite 
continued global headwinds.  
Our capital priorities are unchanged: converting earnings into cash, deleveraging and improving returns. Proactive refinancin g and 
improved outlooks from S&P and CRISIL reflect a balance sheet that is getting stronger .”
