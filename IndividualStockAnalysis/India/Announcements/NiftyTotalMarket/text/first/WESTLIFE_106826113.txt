Westlife Foodworld delivers strongest growth momentum with 
double -digit same -store sales growth in Q2 FY27  
 
Q2 FY27 Highlights  
• Revenue : ₹7.47 billion, up 16% YoY  
• Same Store Sales Growth ( SSSG ): at 10.1% , driven by double -digit guest count growth  
• Operating EBITDA grew 29% YoY to ₹9 81 million ; margin at 13.1% 
• 11 restaurants  added  during the quarter, currently 492 restaurants across 80 cities  
 
Mumbai, October 23, 2026:  Westlife Foodworld Limited  (BSE: 505533), owner and operator of 
McDonald's restaurants across West and South India  through its wholly owned subsidiary Hardcastle 
Restaurants Pvt. Ltd., today announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.  
 
The Company delivered one of its strongest quarterly performances in recent years, with Revenue from 
Operations increasing 16%  year-on-year to ₹7.47 billion  and Same Store Sales Growth (SSSG) 
accelerating to 10.1%, driven by double -digit guest count  growth.  
 
Growth was broad -based across channels and regions, supported by the continued strength of the 
Company's value offerings, menu innovation and omnichannel convenience strategy. On -premise sales 
remained robust, contributing 59% of system sales, while McDeli very continued to witness healthy 
momentum, reflecting consumers' growing preference for convenience and seamless digital 
experiences.   
Building on its brand platform, “Let's Family at McD ,” the Company continued to strengthen 
emotional connections with consumers through culturally relevant campaigns, family -centric 
experiences and menu innovations designed for today's evolving customer needs.  
 
Strong operating leverage and disciplined cost management supported continued profitability growth 
during the quarter. Operating EBITDA increased 2 9% year -on-year to ₹9 81 million, with EBITDA margin 
expanding to 13. 1%. Cash PAT stood at ₹52 6 million, translating into a healthy margin of 7.0%.  
 
Westlife Foodworld continued to expand its presence during the quarter , adding 11 new restaurants 
to reach a network of 492 restaurants across 80 cities.  The Company remains focused on enhancing 
accessibility and convenience while expanding its footprint across existing and emerging markets.  
 
Amit Jatia, Chairperson, Westlife Foodworld Limited, said,  “The results of the quarter  reflect the 
strength of our strategy, the discipline of our execution and the enduring relevance of the McDonald's 
brand. Strong guest growth, improving profitability and continued market expansion position us well 
to capture the significant opportunity ahe ad, create long -term value  and further strengthen our 
leadership in the industry .” 
 
Digital continued to be a key growth driver, contributing more than 75% of sales  during the quarter. 
The McDonald's App ecosystem crossed 58 million  cumulative downloads , while monthly active 
users increased to approximately 4.2 million , reflecting the growing strength of the Company's digital 
ecosystem and customer engagement initiatives.  
 
The Company celebrates 30 years of McDonald's in West and South India , having grown alongside 
India's evolving QSR industry and becoming part of the lives of millions of customers across 
generations. As it nears the milestone of 500 restaurants , the Company remains focused on driving 
the next phase of growth by expanding accessibility, enhancing customer experiences and 
strengthening its presence across the region.  
 
 
 
 
About Westlife:  
Westlife Foodworld Limited (NSE: WESTLIFE, BSE: 505533), focuses on setting up and operating Quick Service Restaurants 
(QSR) in India through its subsidiary Hardcastle Restaurants Pvt.  Ltd. (HRPL). The Company operates a chain of McDonald’s 
restaurants in West and South India having a master franchisee relationship with McDonald’s Corporation USA, through the 
latter’s subsidiary.  
 
About Hardcastle Restaurants Pvt. Ltd.:  
HRPL is a McDonald’s franchisee with rights to own and operate McDonald’s restaurants in India’s West and South markets. 
HRPL has been a franchisee in the region since its inception in 1996. HRPL serves over 200 million customers annually at its 
492 restaurants across 80 cities in the states of Telangana, Gujarat, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Chhattisgarh, 
Andhra Pradesh, Goa along with parts of Madhya Pradesh and Union Territory of Puducherry and provides direct employment 
to over 10,000 employees. McDonal d’s operates through various formats and brand extensions including standalone 
restaurants, drive -thrus , McCafé, 24x7, McDelivery, McBreakfast and dessert kiosks. The menu features Burgers, Wraps, Hot 
and Cold Beverages besides a wide range of desserts. Majority of the McDonald’s restaurants feature an in -house McCafé. 
The pillars of the McDonald's system – Quality, Service, Cleanliness and Value – are evident at each of the restaurants that 
HRPL operates.
