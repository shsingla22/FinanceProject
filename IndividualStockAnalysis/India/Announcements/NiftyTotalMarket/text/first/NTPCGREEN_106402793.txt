PRESS  RELEASE  
 
 
 
NTPC Renewable Energy Limited (NTPC REL) , a wholly owned subsidiary of NTPC Green 
Energy Limited (NGEL) signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Govt of Gujarat

Projects of 5 GW in the state.  
 
 
 
 
The MoU was exchanged on 09th Oct’2025 during the Vibrant Gujarat Regional Conference 
held at Mehsana , Gujarat.
