Media Release  
 
Ambuja  Cement s Firms Up Its Next Growth Phase 
with ~30 MTPA Expansion  
EDITOR'S SYNOPSIS  
• With a total growth capex program of around Rs. 14,500 Cr, new expansion programme to add 
~30 MTPA of cement capacity across key markets . 
• Planned capacity additions strengthen Ambuja's manufacturing network across the high growth 
markets in East, West  and North.  
• Expansion includes new capacities across Assam, Bihar, Jharkhand, Gujarat, Maharashtra and 
Uttar Pradesh.  
• Current cement capacity at 113.5 MTPA across a pan -India footprint of 24 integrated plants and 
24 grinding units.  Proposed expansion will take capacity to 155 MTPA by FY
 ’30. 
• 4.6 MTPA commissioned in H1  FY
’27. Another 5.6 MTPA expected to commission / ramp up 
during H2 FY'27  taking total capacity to 119 MTPA by March  2027. 
Ahmedabad , 2nd Novem ber 2026: Ambuja Cements Limited, part of the Adani Portfolio 
and one of India's leading building materials solutions providers, has firmed up its next 
phase of expansion, with new greenfield and brownfield projects planned to add ~30 MTPA 
of cement capacity across key markets. The programme will expand its manufacturing 
network across the East, West and North, building on its current 113.5 MTPA pan -India 
cement capacity across 24 integrated plants and 24 grinding units.  
The expansion includes new grinding capacities of ~15.5 MTPA in West Cluster, 11.6 MTPA 
in East Cluster and 2.9 MTPA in North Cluster. We are adding capacities in East Cluster at 
Rangia (2.9 MTPA), Hojai (2.9 MTPA)  in Assam; Godda (2.9 MTPA)  in Jharkhand; 
Muzaffarpur (2.9 MTPA) in Bihar; West Cluster at Mundra (2.9 MTPA) and Hazira (2.9 
MTPA) in Gujarat; Raigad (2.9 MTPA), Kalyan (2.9 MTPA), Dahanu ( 0.7 MTPA) and 
Amravati (2.9 MTPA) in Maharashtra; and North cluster at Dadri Line 2 (2.9 MTPA) in Uttar 
Pradesh. The programme also includes new clinker capacity in East cluster at Umrangso 
(4 MTPA)  in Assam , West cluster at Maratha (4 MTPA)  and Mundra (2. 3 MTPA) , 
strengthening the upstream network required to support the expanded grinding footprint.  
Mr. Vinod Bahety, CEO 
 – Cement Business, Adani Group
 , said, “ Our next phase of expansion 
is focused on quality of growth and creating a more balanced manufacturing network. By 
strengthening the link between clinker and grinding capacity and expanding closer to  the 
high growth potential key markets, we are building greater flexibility into the network. As 
we scale, our focus is on combining market reach with logistics and cost efficiencies so 
that every capacity addition strengthens the competitiveness of the overall business.  We 
have a vibrant supply chain ecosystem with stronger Brand Equity and share of Premium 
Cement in these markets. The growth footprint will compliment well with our value based 
strategic growth.  
This phase of growth and additional debottlenecking initiatives will ramp up total capacity 
to 155 MTPA by FY
 ’30. The total growth capex program is around Rs. 14,500 Cr. Apart from 
 
 
increasing market share, these capacities will also help to reduce manufacturing cost by 
bringing higher efficiencies. Being closer to the market, it will also help in reduc ing lead 
distance and thereby logistics cost. In all, it will pave the journey to achieve  a cost of Rs. 
4,000 PMT by FY
 ’28
”. 
The new programme builds on capacity expansion already under execution. During H1 
FY27, the Company  added 4.6 MTPA  through the 1.2 MTPA brownfield expansion at Dahej 
in Gujarat, the new 2.4 MTPA Salai Banwa Grinding Unit in Uttar Pradesh and a 1.0 MTPA 
brownfield expansion at Kalamboli in Maharashtra, taking overall cement capacity to 113.5 
MTPA.  
The near -term pipeline  will further strengthen this base, with another 3.2 MTPA  across  
Bathinda  GU (1.2 MTPA)  in Punjab  (North cluster) , Jodhpur Penna  IU (2 MTPA)  in 
Rajasthan , stabilised and expected to ramp up 
 in H2 FY’27.  In addition, the  Warisaliganj  GU 
(2.4 MTPA)  in Bihar  (East Cluster)  will be commissioned in H2 
FY’27 . 
The Company’s t otal 
capacity  is expected to reach 119 MTPA by end of March 2027.  
Since the acquisition, Ambuja has completed 11 capacity projects , adding 16.3 MTPA of 
cement capacity and 9.0 MTPA of clinker capacity  up to 30 September 2026. The new 
30 MTPA expansion programme represents the next leg of this journey, further 
strengthening the scale, geographic reach and efficiency of its pan -India manufacturing 
network  of 25 Integrated Units (IU), 37 Grinding Units (GU) and 3 Clinker  Units (CU) .
