The Company reported revenues of INR 661 crore in Q2, up 20% year -on-year (YoY) and EBITDA 
increased to INR 39 crore for the quarte r. The same store sales (SSSG) grew by 1 5% YoY and t he

A pioneer in organized value fashion, and founded in 2002, V -Mart is an omni retail store chain for the 
complete family, oﬀering fashion apparel, footwear, home furnishings, general merchandise and 
kirana. Primarily focusing on Tier II and Ill citi es, V -Mart is present pan -India with an average store size 
of 8,000 sq. ft. It follows the concept of ‘ value retailing ’ by fulﬁlling the growing aspiration of the rapidly
