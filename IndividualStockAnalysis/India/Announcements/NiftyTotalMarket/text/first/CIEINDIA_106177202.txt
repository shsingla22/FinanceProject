Reference: our letter dated 2nd September, 2024 
 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
This is further to our letter dated 2nd September 2024 whereby the Company had informed about the Share 
Subscription and Shareholders Agreement (SSSHA) entered into by the Company with Ijya Renewables to 
subscribe upto 27% of the equity, on fully diluted basis, of Ijya Renewables and to invest an amount upto Rs. 
25.54 million towards subscription of its equity shares to entitle Company to source green energy from captive 
solar power generating plant to be set up by Ijya Renewables. A copy of the said announcement is enclosed as 
Annexure A  for ease of reference.  
 
It is hereby informed that due to difficulties in completing the development of the Solar Energy Facility, the 
Company and Ijya Renewables have mutually agreed to discontinue the Project and entered into Termination 
Agreement today i.e. 20th March, 2025. In terms of the Termination Agreement the Share Subscription and 
Shareholders Agreement shall stand terminated.  
 
Accordingly, the proposed acquisition of the shares of Ijya Renewables stands cancelled. This cancellation does 
not entail any liability on the Company.  
 
Kindly acknowledge receipt of the same.
