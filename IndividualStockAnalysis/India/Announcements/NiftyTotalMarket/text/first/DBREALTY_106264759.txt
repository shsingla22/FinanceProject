Subject:  Update on Slum Rehabilitation Project at Worli, Mumbai - Disclosure under Regulation 30 of 
the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  
 
We wish to inform you that Valor Estate Limited (the “Company”), through its wholly owned subsidiary 
and in partnership with Prestige Estates Projects Limited, is undertaking a large -scale slum 
redevelopment project via Worli Urban Development Project LLP on land admeasuring approximately  
70,000 sq. meters (C.T.S. No. 17/47 (Part)), located at Dr. E. Moses Road, Lower Parel Division, Worli,  
Mumbai – 400 018, within G/South Ward. This project, popularly known as the Jijamata Nagar 
Redevelopment Project, is being executed on land owned by the Municipal Corporation of Greater 
Mumbai (MCGM). The Company has 50% economic interest in the project.  
 
The project encompasses the rehabilitation of approximately 4,029 residential /commercial structures 
and its occupants. Of these, 3,661 families have already vacated the premises and have been provided 
with alternate transit accommodation or transit rent.  
 
Part of the project land is reserved for the implementation of Stage -II of the Mumbai Sewerage 
Disposal Project at the Love Grove Sewage Pumping Works — a critical infrastructure project of high 
vital public importance.  
 
In furtherance of the development, appropriate statutory authorities have issued demolition orders 
for the balance remaining structures. Subsequent thereto,  consent terms have been filed and 
accepted in ongoing proceedings before the Hon’ble Bombay High Court, whereby the remaining 
occupants are required to vacate their premises within 21 days from June 4, 2025. Accordingly, all 
remaining structures will be vac ated on or before June 25, 2025.  
 
Upon completion of the eviction process  as contemplated above , the site will be ready for construction 
of  “The Prestige Place”, a marquee  one of its kind , world -class mixed -use development with a planned 
sale FSI in excess of 4 million sq. ft.  The Project is being designed by Skidmore, Owings & Merrill LLP 
(SOM), a world leader having designed marquee projects  such as Burj Khalifa, Dubai amongs t others. 
This will be amongst the largest true mixed use project  in South Mumbai and  also one of the largest 
urba n renewal projects in South Mumbai. This Project  will include in -situ rehabilitation of over 4,000 
families. The rehabilitation component is being designed as per global best practices in urban planning 
and sustainability.
