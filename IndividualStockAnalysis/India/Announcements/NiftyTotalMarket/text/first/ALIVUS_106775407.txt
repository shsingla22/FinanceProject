Summary of Proceedings of the 15 th Annual General Meeting  
 
The 15 th Annual General Meeting (‘AGM’) of the members of Alivus Life Sciences Limited (formerly Glenmark Life

commenced at 3.00 p.m. and concluded a t 4.4 0 p.m. 
 
Mr. Hiren Patel, Chairman & Non -Executive Director presided over the meeting and welcomed the Members and 
Directors particip ating through video conference. He  introduced the Directors, the Chief Financial Officer and the 
Company Secretary & Compliance Officer.  All the Directors of the Company attended the AGM.  
 
The Chairpersons of the Audit Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee and Stakeholders ’
