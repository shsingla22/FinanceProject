Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Sub:- Outcome of the Board Meeting of Waaree Energies Limited (“the Company”) 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“the Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform you that the 
Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting held today i.e. Friday, March 20, 2026 has, inter alia, 
considered the following: 
 
a) That the Company and Mr. Amit Ashok Paithankar has mutually agreed that he shall be relieved from 
the position of Whole – Time Director and Chief Executive Officer of the Company with effect from 
today i.e. Friday, March 20, 2026, being an earlier date than May 15, 2026, as mentioned in our earlier 
intimation dated December 29, 2025.  
 
b) Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved the 
appointment of Mr. Jignesh Devchandbhai Rathod (presently CEO – Designate) as Whole -Time 
Director and Chief Executive Officer of the Company w.e.f.  Saturday, March 21, 2026, subject to the 
approval of the shareholders. 
 
c) Took note of the cessation of Ms. Sonal Shrivastava from the position of the Chief Financial Officer 
of the Company from the closure of business hours on Friday, March 20, 2026 upon completion of 
the requisite notice period. The resignation letter is enclosed herewith. 
 
d) Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and approval of 
Audit Committee, approved the appointment of  Mr. Abhishek Pareek (presently Group Head 
Finance) as Chief Financial Officer and Key Managerial Personnel of the Company, to hold office 
w.e.f. Saturday, March 21, 2026. 
 
e) Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved the 
appointment of the following individuals as Senior Managerial Personnels (“SMP”) of the Company: 
 
i. Mr. Munna Singh as Deputy Chief Financial Officer (“Deputy CFO”); 
ii. Mr. Varun Goenka as President – Growth & Strategy.
