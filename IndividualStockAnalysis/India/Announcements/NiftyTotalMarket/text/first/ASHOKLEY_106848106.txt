Annexure-B – Cessation of Subsidiary due to Scheme of Arrangement 
 
S. No. Particulars  Details 
1.  Details and reasons for restructuring The Scheme provides for the merger of HLFL into 
NDL with the objective of creating a stronger listed 
financial services platform, achieving operational 
synergies and efficiencies and enhancing 
stakeholder value. 
2.  Quantitative and/ or qualitative effect of 
restructuring Upon the Scheme becoming effective, HLFL shall 
stand dissolved without winding up and shall cease 
to be a material subsidiary of the Company. 
Consequent thereto, NDL shall become the material 
listed subsidiary of the Company. As a result of the 
merger and the issuance of shares by NDL pursuant 
to the approved share exchange ratio, the 
Company's shareholding shall stand diluted from 
61.12% in HLFL to 59.64% in NDL. 
3.  Details of benefit, if any, to the promoter 
/promoter group/group companies from 
such proposed restructuring No specific benefit is proposed to accrue to the 
promoter, promoter group or group companies. 
4.  Brief details of change in shareholding 
pattern (if any) of all entities Pursuant to the Scheme, equity shares of NDL shall 
be issued to the shareholders of HLFL in accordance 
with the approved share exchange ratio of 25 equity 
shares of NDL for every 10 equity shares held in 
HLFL. Upon the Scheme becoming effective, HLFL 
shall stand dissolved without winding up. 
Consequently, Ashok Leyland's shareholding shall 
stand diluted from 61.12% in HLFL to 59.64% in NDL 
and NDL shall become the material listed subsidiary 
of the Company.
