At Relaxo, we have established ourselves as a beacon in the 
footwear industry, fuelled by an unwavering commitment to 
quality, innovation and customer satisfaction. Our journey from 
humble beginnings to becoming one of India’s largest and most 
trusted footwear manufacturer showcases resilience, evolution, 
and the relentless pursuit of excellence.
In a world of constant change, we forge ahead, adapting swiftly 
to emerging trends, introducing modern designs, and embracing 
the future. Our diverse portfolio, crafted with superior crafts-
manship, state-of-the-art technology leading to maximum 
comfort, connects with consumers across generations, especially 
today’s young and style-conscious individuals. As we look 
forward, our focus is clear: to create best-in-class footwear 
experiences that harmonize style with substance, tradition with 
innovation, and everyday wear with a profound impact.CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER
