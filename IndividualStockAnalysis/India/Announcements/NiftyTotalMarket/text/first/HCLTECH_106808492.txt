Hon’ble CM Yogi Adityanath  graces celebration to mark 10 years of HCL IT City in 
Lucknow   
HCLTech announces AI upskilling initiative for 1,000 students in the state  
HCL Group announces Rs 500 crores investment for expansion of HCL IT City  
 
LUCKNOW and NOIDA, October 06, 2026: HCLTech, a leading global technology company, 
today marked the tenth anniversary of its operations in  Lucknow in  the presence of Hon’ble 
Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh, Yogi Adi tyanath and Roshni Nadar Malhotra, Chairperson, 
HCLTech . 
As part of its continued investment in future -ready talent, HCLTech announced that 1,000 
students across Uttar Pradesh will be trained in AI and allied technologies through its LITMUS 
program during FY27. HCLTech’s TechBee program has already trained and employ ed more 
than 2,200 candidates from the state.  
As a special celebration of the milestone , HCL Group announced the expansion  of HCL IT City  
with a planned outlay of ₹500 crores. Th e proposed  expansion will create fresh capacity  of up to 
9.5 lakh sqft that will be able to house 4,500 technology professionals  and further boost 
Lucknow’s status as a leading technology and GCC hub s of India.  
HCL Group also launched  the HCL -GUVI Emerging Technology Centre  at HCL IT City  to equip 
students and professionals  in the region  with skills in AI, data analytics and emerging 
technologies.  
Yogi Adityanath, Hon'ble Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh said, "Uttar Pradesh is on its way to 
becoming a trillion -dollar economy with representation from diverse sectors that ensure Sabka 
Saath, Sabka Vikaas. With Lucknow's AI City at the heart of that am bition, HCL  IT City  has, over 
the past decade, been the anchor of this story, contributing immensely to the region's 
technology and talent ecosystem. We thank HCLTech for their continued commitment to Uttar 
Pradesh through business investments as well as innovative community  initiatives to contribute 
to inclusive growth.”  
Roshni Nadar Malhotra, Chairperson, HCLTech said, "Uttar Pradesh is our janmabhoomi  and 
karmabhoomi . It is where HCL Group began its journey 50 years ago, laying the foundations of 
India's technology industry. As we celebrate the HCL Group's 50th anniversary this year, this 
expansion reflects our enduring trust in the talent, ambition and potential of U ttar Pradesh, and 
our confidence in the state's continued emergence as a leading destination for technology -led 
growth under the leadership of Hon'ble Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath ji. HCLTech remains 
deeply committed to contributing to the state's digita l and innovation journey."  
Since starting operations in 2016, HCL IT City has become one of HCLTech's key global delivery 
centers, employing more than 12,500 people and serving over 150 global enterprises . A 
significant driver of this growth has been HCLTech's focus on nurturing local talent. Thousands 
of professionals from Uttar Pradesh  have built successful careers at the  Lucknow  campus. 
HCLTech also maintains partnerships with over 40 engineering colleges across the region to 
strengthen industry -academia collaboration and create career pathways for students.
