Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in  Uttarakhand  
 
New Delhi , 28th August 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels announces its latest signing – 
Lemon Tree Hotel , Mohkampur, Dehradun . This propert y shall be managed by 
Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels 
Limited.  
 
Lemon Tree Hotel, Mohkampur, Dehradun  will feature 98 well-appointed rooms , 
a restaurant , a lounge, a conference room , a swimming pool , a fitness center and 
other recreational facilities . The Jolly Grant Airport is approximately 18 kms away 
from the property, while the Dehradun Railway Station is about 9 kms away. The 
hotel is well connected by roadways for both public and private transport.  The 
upcoming hotel enjoys a unique location within the Mall of Dehradun, the city’s 
premier lifestyle and retail dest ination. Offering guests direct access to a vibrant mix 
of shopping, dining, and entertainment options, it stands apart as a hub for both 
business and leisure travellers.  
 
Located in the  Doon Valley  on the foothills of the  Himalayas , Dehradun is nestled 
between  Song River, a tributary of  the Ganges  on the east and the Asan River, a 
tributary of  the Yamuna  on the west. Noted for its picturesque landscape , pleasant 
climate, educational institutions, and proximity to hill stations like Mussoorie, it  
beautifully blends nature, culture, and modernity.   It is the capital of Uttarakhand 
and has several well -known boarding schools like the Doon School,  Welham Boys' 
School  and Welham Girls' School . It is also home to the  Indian Military 
Academy,  Forest Research Institute,  Indira Gandhi National Forest 
Academy,  Rashtriya Indian Military College,  Uttarakhand Ayurveda University,  Wadia 
Institute of Himalayan Geology  and the  Indian Institute of Remote Sensing. It is also 
the headquarters of the  Surveyor -General of India.  Dehradun is known for 
its Basmati  rice and bakery products.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels , commented , "We are delighted to further enhance our footprint 
in Uttarakhand where we currently operate eight hotels and have eight more 
upcoming ones ." 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India’s leading hospitality companies, 
catering to a wide range of customers  – from value -conscious travellers to premium 
business and leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels & Resorts, 
Lemon Tree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys Select, and Keys 
Lite – the group offers experiences across u pper upscale, upscale, upper midscale, 
midscale, leisure, wildlife, and spiritual segments.  
 
Serving over 1.5 million guests annually, LTHL operates 110+ hotels across 7 5+ cities 
in India and abroad, with a growing pipeline of 110+ upcoming properties. From 
metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad to tier II & III cities 
such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, and Indore – and with an international presence in 
Dubai, Bhutan, and Nepal – Lemon Tree Hotels delivers exceptional comfort, 
consistent quality, and a warm, refreshing experience.  
 
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 230+ properties 
(operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for both business 
and leisure travellers.
