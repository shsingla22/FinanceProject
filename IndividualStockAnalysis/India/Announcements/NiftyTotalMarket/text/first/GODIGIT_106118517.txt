Digit Insurance Recognized  as ‘Top Employer in India’ for 2024 
and 2025 for Fostering Culture of Growth and Innovation  
 
Bengaluru, 20th January 2025 : Go Digit General Insurance  Limited  (Digit Insurance) , one of 
India’s leading new -age insurance companies , announced it has been recognized as a “Top 
Employer in India”  for the year 2024 and 2025 by the Top Employers Institute.  Digit 
Insurance is the sole Indian general insurance company certified as a Top Employer  in 2024 
and 2025 , highlighting its commitment to fostering a world -class work environment and  a 
culture of continuous learning and development . 
Headquartered in Netherlands, Top Employers Institute is the top global HR authority on 
certifying excellence in HR strategies and practices.  The Top Employers Institute 
certification is awarded to organizations that demonstrate excellence in employee practices 
across six key domains: Work Environment, Talent Acquisition, Learning & Development, 
Performance Management, Career Development & Succ ession Planning, and HR Strategy.  
Commenting on the recognition, Amrit Jaidka Arora, CHRO, Go Digit General Insurance , 
said, “Being named a Top Employer in India is a tremendous honour  for us and we are 
ecstatic about this prestigious win . This is a true testament to the dedication of all our 
employees.  At Digit, we have cultivated a culture that promotes growth not only through 
effective recognition models but also by fostering an environment of trust , transparency  and 
collaboration. This not only aids in their professional development but also helps build a 

 
cohesive and innovative team and organically creates a setting where employees feel valued , 
and inspired to always challenge the status quo .”  
Digit Insurance's commitment to employee development is evident in its multifaceted 
approach to people practices.  The company fosters a culture of continuous improvement 
through robust feedback mechanism, encompassing both structured and unstructured 
feedback  that  allows for ongoing policy refinement based on employee input. Furthermore, 
the company embraces a culture of innovation  and constant learning and empowers its 
workforce through various learning and development program mes.  
David Plink, CEO, Top Employers Institute , said,  “This year, the Top Employers 
Certification Programme highlights the dedication of our Top Employers as they continue to 
set the standard, consistently delivering world -class HR strategies and practices.  These Top 
Employers strive to foster growth and wellbeing, all while enriching the world of work. We are 
proud to celebrate these people -first leaders and teams as the Top Employers for 2025. ”  
Digit Insurance has previously won various HR -related accolades. It was ranked 5th at 
LinkedIn 2024 Top Companies to Work for and is also a certified Great Place to Work for five 
years in a row. The company also won Business  World People Excellence Award 2024 (Best 
Place of Work)  and Ambition Box Employee Choice Awards 2024 in the tech start -up 
category. As of 30 September 2024, the company had 4,405 on -roll and off -roll employees 
and had 85 branches all across India.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
About Digit General Insurance  
Founded by Kamesh Goyal in 2016, Go Digit General Insurance Limited is a publicly listed general 
insurance company and is one of the leading new -age insurance companies in India. It leverages its 
technology to power what it believes to be an innovative app roach to product design, distribution 
and customer experience for non -life and health insurance products. Some of the promoters of Go 
Digit General Insurance Ltd are Go Digit Infoworks Services Pvt. Ltd and FAL Corporation. With its 
registered office in Pu ne and corporate office in Bengaluru, Digit is one of the first non -life insurers 
in India to be fully operating on cloud.  
Digit won the General Insurance Company of the Year Award 2024 at the prestigious Asia Insurance 
Industry Awards 2024, Singapore. The Company is also part of the Fortune India 500 List  2024  
(Ranked 3 12) and Business Today BT500 List 2024 (ranked 229 th out of 500 most valuable 
companies). It was ranked 5th at LinkedIn 2024 Top Companies to Work for and is also a certified 
Great Place to Work for five years in a row.  
The Company offers motor insurance, health insurance, travel insurance, property insurance, 
marine insurance, liability insurance and other insurance products, which the customer can 
customize to meet his or her needs. Digit, through its tech -enabled proce ss, focuses on product 
innovations to help satisfy real unmet insurance needs.
