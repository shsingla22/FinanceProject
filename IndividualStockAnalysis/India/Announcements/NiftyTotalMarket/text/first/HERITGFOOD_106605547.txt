Dear Sir/Madam,  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we wish to inform you that Heritage Foods Limited has been awarded the ISO 27001:2022 
Certification  for its Information Security Management System (ISMS)  by ISOQAR Limited . 
The certification reflects the Company’s commitment to implementing internationally recognized standards 
for information security management and to safeguarding the confidentiality, integrity  and availability of 
critical information assets across its business functions.  
The Company has adopted and implemented the ISO  27001:2022 framework to systematically identify, 
assess, and manage information security risks in a structured and effective manner. The certified ISMS 
includes established policies, procedures, and control mechanisms designed to strengthen the Company’s 
overall information security posture and ensure secure handling of sensitive organizational data.  
This achievement demonstrates the Company’s proactive approach towards strengthening cybersecurity 
practices, mitigating information security risks, and ensuring operational resilience. It further reinforces 
the trust and confidence of customers, employees, business partners  and other stakeholders who rely on 
the Company’s secure and efficient management systems.  
This certification is a significant milestone in the Company’s ongoing efforts to align its internal processes 
with global best practices in information security and data governance.
