PRESS RELEASE 
 
Brigade Group Signs Agreement for a New Office Space  
on Airport Road, Bangalore 
 
Bengaluru, August 5, 2024:  Brigade Group, through its wholly owned subsidiary, Brigade Tetrarch 
Private Limited, has entered into a Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) agreement to develop a 1.4 million 
square feet leasable office space. The proposed commercial development is expected to generate an 
annual lease income close to Rs. 100 Crores per year, with an investment of close to Rs. 750 Crores.    
 
“This project reflects our commitment to develop world-class, sustainable commercial spaces, with 
amenities that will cater to the new age workforce as well as the dynamic needs of businesses. We are 
confident that this development will set new benchmarks in the office segment and contribute 
significantly to the economic growth of North Bangalore,” said Nirupa Shankar, Joint Managing Director, 
Brigade Enterprises Limited. 
 
The project is strategically located on, Airport Road, NH 44, a prime commercial hub in Bengaluru, with 
excellent connectivity and accessibility. 
 
About Brigade Group:   The Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers with close to 
four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all stakeholders. Instituted in 1986, the 
company has developed many landmark buildings and transformed the skyline of cities across South 
India, namely – Bengaluru, Mysuru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kochi with developments across the 
Residential, Commercial, Retail, Hospitality and Education sectors. Since its inception, Brigade has 
completed 280+ buildings amounting to over 90 million sq. ft of developed space across a diverse real 
estate portfolio.
