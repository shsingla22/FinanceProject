Thomas Cook India expands footprint in Ahmedabad with new South Bopal outlet to 
cater to Gujarat’s high growth travel market  
 
 Year-round travel enthusiasm peaks during summer and Diwali for Gujarati customers  
 The new Sou th Bopal outlet is positioned to cat er to Ahmedabad's growing demand for convenient, personalized 
travel services  
 Growth drivers include Gujarati group tours with Gujarati -speaking tour managers and customized vegetarian cuisine 
options  
 
Mumbai,  October  4, 2024:  Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel services company, has inaugurated its 
new franchise outlet in South Bopal, Ahmedabad , to leverage the high -growth market of the city and its s urrounding areas.  
With the ongoing f estive season , the new South Bopal outlet aims to serve the significant demand for travel bookings 
during Gujarat's key holiday seasons —summer and Diwali.  
South Bopal  is a preferred  residential locality in  the western part of Ahmedabad  with proximity to the airport, railways, 
metros and connectivity to p rominent areas, including  IT hubs, and business parks.  The new South Bopal  outlet expands 
Thomas Cook India’s network to 4 locations in the city and 10 across Gujarat . 
 
In its commitment to enhance access and convenience,  Thomas Cook’s new outlet will address diverse customer segments 
- families, couples, Young India’s,  millennials & Gen Z, business professionals,  and senio rs. Thomas Cook’s South Bopal  
outlet offers end -to-end travel services: International & Domestic Holidays (Group Tours, Personalised hol idays , Cruises, 
etc.), Value Added Services like Travel Insurance, etc . Additionally, Thomas Cook’s AI booking platform assists customers in 
planning their personalized vacations in real- time  by checking availability and making reservations in a matter of minutes.  
 
Travel Trends from Ahmedabad: 
• Gujarati’s are avid travellers and in addition to the peak vacation periods of summer, festive season and y ear-end, 
customers from the State are maximising on public/holidays and weekends for multiple mini-cations 
• Gujarati- Specific Group Tours and Regional Departures Driving Demand: Gujarati customers have a strong 
preference for group travel. Tours featuring Gujarati- speaking managers and pure vegetarian cuisine remain 
popular, catering to the group-sensitive market.  Regional group departures, such as Caravan tours and Pure Veg 
tours, con tinue to be highly sought after  
• Longer stays: Short vacations now extend to 5 -8 days, while longer trips span 10- 15 days, reflecting a shift tow ards 
more immersive experiences  
• Growth of experiential travel: Unique experiences such as Northern Lights tours in Finland, Cherry Blossom viewing 
in Japan, and whale watching in Sri Lanka are gaining popularity  
• Emergence of fresh new destinations: Driven by easy visa regimes and connectivity, increased demand for CIS 
countries/destinations such as Azerbaijan, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, and Georgia  
• Growing Demand for Luxury  Travel &  Cruise Vacations: Luxury cruise holidays are becoming increasingly popular 
among Gujarati travel lers, offering a perfect blend of opulence and adventure. Top destinations include 
Lakshadweep, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Singapore, and Egypt
