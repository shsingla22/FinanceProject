2. EFFECTIVENESS 
This Supplemental Deed is being executed pursuant to Clause 18.4 of the Trust Deed and shall 
come into effect from the Amendment Date. Consequently, any reference to the term 'Trust Deed' 
in the Trust Deed shall be construed to mean the Trust Deed as amended by this Supplemental 
Deed. 
3. CHANGE IN TRUSTEESHIP 
The Parties agree that on and from the Amendment Date, the Trust Deed shall stand amended in 
the manner set out herein, to reflect the revised understanding between the Parties: 
3.1 With effect from the Amendment Date, Incoming Trustee is hereby appointed as a Trustee of the 
Trust in substitution of Mr. Sunny Kumar, who had resigned as a trustee of the Trust on March 17, 
2026. 
3.2 The Incoming Trustee shall be deemed to be a "Trustee" for all purposes under the Trust Deed and 
shall have the same rights, powers, duties and obligations as the other Trustees, as if the Incoming 
Trustee had been an original party to the Trust Deed. Accordingly, all references to "Trustee" in 
the Trust Deed shall be deemed to include the Incoming Trustee.

Deed and those of the Trust Deed, in respect of the matters specifically set out in this Supplemental 
Deed, the terms and conditions of this Supplemental Deed shall prevail. 
4.2 Except as specifically amended herein, all other provisions of the Trust Deed shall remain 
unchanged and shall continue in full force and effect.
