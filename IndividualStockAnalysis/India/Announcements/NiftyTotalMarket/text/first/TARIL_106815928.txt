About Transformers and Rectifiers (India) Limited  
TARIL  is one of India’s leading transformer manufacturers with over four decades of operating legacy. Headquartered 
in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, the Company manufactures a comprehensive range of transformers including power, 
distribution, furnace, specialty , rectifier transformers and shunt reactors , solar application transformers, earthing 
transformers . TARIL’s product capabilities span 5 kV to 1,200 kV and 0.5 MVA to 500 MVA, serving utilities, EPC 
players, industrial customers and renewable energy sectors. The Company has an installed capacity of 75,000 MVA 
across three plants, supported by backward integrated manufacturing facilities, and has an int ernational presence 
across 40+ countries with 18,000+ transformer installations worldwide.
