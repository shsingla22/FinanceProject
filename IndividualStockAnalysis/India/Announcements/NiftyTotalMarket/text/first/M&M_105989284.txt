Pune , September  16, 2024 : Mahindra  & Mahindra  Ltd., India's  leading  manufacturer  of utility  
vehicles  and leaders  in the LCV <3.5  t segment,  today  announced  the launch  of the Mahindra  
Veero,  starting  at ₹ 7.99  Lakh.  Designed  to redefine  the LCV <3.5  t segment,  the Veero  offers  
unparalleled  savings  with  best-in-class  mileage,  exceptional  performance  powered  by robust  
multiple  engine  options,  industry -leading  safety  features,  enhanced  occupant  protection,  and 
a premium  cabin  experience.  
Mahindra’s  innovative  Urban  Prosper  Platform  (UPP)  is India’s  first ground -up multi -energy  
modular  CV platform.  Designed  to offer  best-in-class  total  cost of ownership  and segment -first 
safety  beyond  regulations,  the platform  sets a new benchmark  in the industry.  It is engineered  
to support  payloads  from  1 t to 2 t+ in multiple  deck  lengths  and accommodates  multiple  
powertrain  options,  including  diesel,  CNG,  and electric.

About  Mahindra  Veero : 
Unmatched  Savings:  
Mahindra  Veero  sets a new benchmark  in savings  with  its best-in-class  mileage,  ensuring  
maximum  profitability  for businesses.  The diesel  variant  delivers  an impressive  18.4  km/l*,  
while  the CNG  variant  achieves  19.2  km/kg*  with  a certified  range  of over  500 km. Mahindra  
Veero  minimizes  downtime  and maximizes  productivity  with  its best-in-class  20,000  km regular  
service  interval.  An advanced  engine  Stop -Start  system,  driver  fuel coaching,  and Eco mode  
help save  fuel and maximise  profitability.  Additionally,  Mahindra  Veero  comes  with  a standard  
3-year/1  Lakh  km warranty,  providing  peace  of mind  and further  contributing  to significant  
cost savings  over  time.  
Additionally,  the Mahindra  Veero  is equipped  with  iMAXX  connected  solutions,  with  over  50 
features  that allow  customers  and fleet  owners  to conveniently  monitor  their  vehicles  using  
the iMAXX  application  on smartphones.  The application  is available  in six languages  – English,  
Hindi,  Tamil,  Kannada,  Telugu,  and Malayalam  – making  it easier  for users  across  India  to 
operate.  
Exceptional  Performance  & Capability : 
Performance  and capability  are at the core  of Mahindra  Veero,  which  is engineered  to 
outperform  competitors  in every  aspect.  Whether  powered  by the robust  1.5-litre mDI diesel  
engine  that delivers  59.7  kW and 210 Nm of torque,  or the Turbo  mCNG  engine  with  its best-
in-class  power  of 67.2  kW and 210 Nm of torque,  Mahindra  Veero  is built  to handle  the 
toughest  jobs with  ease.  The vehicle’s  best-in-class  payload  of 1,600  kg for diesel  and an 
impressive  1,500  kg for CNG  allows  businesses  to transport  more  goods  per trip, leading  to

an ideal  choice  for a wide  range  of business  needs.  
Segment -First  Safety : 
Mahindra  Veero  is equipped  with  a range  of segment -first safety  features  that ensure  the 
highest  levels  of protection.  The inclusion  of a driver -side airbag  provides  extra  safety  in case 
of collisions,  whether  on city roads  or highways.  Mahindra  Veero  adheres  to the more  stringent  
AIS096  compliance  crash  safety  standards,  offering  maximum  protection  for occupants.  
Additionally,  the highest  usage  of High -Strength  Steel  (HSS)  in the cabin,  chassis,  and cargo  
body,  combined  with  superior  engineering,  enhances  the vehicle’s  toughness,  delivering  
superior  safety,  ride comfort,  handling  confidence,  and vehicle  durability.  The vehicle  also 
features  a reverse  parking  camera,  enhancing  safety  and convenience  in tight  spaces.  Other  
advanced  safety  features  include  a False  Start  Avoidance  System,  an immobilizer  to prevent  
unauthoris ed starts,  and best-in-class  driver  visibility  for better  control  and awareness.   
Premium  Cabin  Experience : 
Mahindra  Veero  is equipped  with  first-in-class  features  that elevate  comfort  and convenience  
for drivers  spending  long hours  on the road.  These  include  a 26.03  cm touchscreen  
infotainment  system,  steering -mounted  controls,  and power  windows,  all designed  to enhance  
the driving  experience.  The vehicle  also offers  a certified  D+2 seating  arrangement,  ensuring  
compliance  with  legal  requirements  while  providing  a spaciou s cabin  that maximizes  comfort.  
To further  enhance  comfort,  Mahindra  Veero  comes  equipped  with  both  air conditioning  with 
heater  and demister,  ensuring  a comfortable  cabin  environment  in all weather  conditions.  The 
reclining  driver’s  seat and provision  for sleeping  make  long journeys  and breaks  more  
manageable,  while  ample  storage  space  ensures  that drivers  have  room  for their  essentials.   
Standout  Design:  
Mahindra  Veero  boasts  an unmissable  presence  with  its bold stance  and modern  styling,  
making  it stand  out in the crowded  SCV segment.  The striking  front  end features  a distinctive  
grille  and vertical  headlamps,  giving  Mahindra  Veero  a commanding  road  presence.  The design  
is further  elevated  by premium  and comfortable  seats,  ensuring  a superior  driving  experience.  
Inside,  Mahindra  Veero  offers  car-like fully trimmed  interiors  that provide  a touch  of luxury  in 
a utilitarian  vehicle.  The vehicle  is also equipped  with  a first-in-segment  full TFT cluster,  
bringing  essential  information  directly  to the driver’s  view.
