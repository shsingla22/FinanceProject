Dear Madam / Sir,  
 
We refer to our earlier disclosure dated 30th September 2023. Our subsidiaries namely JSW Vayu 
(Tungabhadra) Private Limited, JSW Vayu (Indravati) Private Limited, JSW Wind Power (Pennar) 
Private Limited and JSW Wind Power Limited had filed appeal before the Hon’ble Appellate 
Tribunal for Electricity (“APTEL”) against the Andhra Pradesh Electricity Regulatory Commission 
(“APERC”) order dated 18th October 2023 which directed AP Discom to pay Rs. 0.50 per unit for 
energy supplied beyond the prescribed Capacity Utilization Factor (“CUF”).  
 
We wish to inform you that APTEL, vide its order dated 17th November 2026, has allowed the 
appeal and set aside the APERC order.  APTEL has directed the AP Discoms to pay for energy 
generated by the wind power generators at the applicable generic tariff, irrespective of whether 
the actual generation exceeds the norm ative CUF, together with applicable interest in accordance 
with the respective Power Purchase Agreements .
