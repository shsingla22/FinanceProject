SKF India Reports 20.1% YoY Revenue Growth in Q2  
 
Pune, 3rd November 2026:  SKF India Limited (NSE: SKFINDIA | BSE: 500472), a leading technology and engineering 
bearing solutions provider for the automotive industry, today announced its financial results for the second quarter 
ended September 30, 2026 (Q2 FY 2026 -27). The announcement aligns with the launch of a new brand identity, SKF 
Vertevo, marking an important milestone in its evolution as a focused, stand -alone automotive business.  
 
For the quarter, the Company reported Revenue from Operations of INR 5958 Million , EBITDA  of INR 865 Million , and 
Profit Before Tax (PBT)  of INR 698 Million.  Growth was driven by broad -based demand across key automotive 
segments, including two and three -wheelers, passenger vehicles and exports.  The performance was further aided by 
sustained momentum in the Indian automotive sector, where customers are seeking higher efficiency, reliability and 
performance.  
 
Financial Highlights (Q2 FY 2026 -27), Standalone  
 
 
The quarterly performance comes at a significant point, as on 3rd November 2026 the SKF Vertevo brand is officially 
launched across the organization and to our customers and stakeholders worldwide . The SKF Vertevo brand is inspired 
by the world of racing, where the company has contributed to automotive innovation for decades. The identity is 
designed to keep a connection to the long SKF heritage and expertise, while signalling its ambition to help ac celerate 
the future of mobility.  
 
Speaking on the Company’s performance and the brand launch, Shailesh Kumar Sharma, Managing Director of SKF 
India Limited , said, “ We delivered strong revenue growth of 20.1% year -on-year in Q2, reflecting healthy demand 
across key automotive segments and the strength of our customer -focused strategy. The launch of SKF Vertevo marks 
an important new chapter for our business, building on our strong engineering heritage while sharpening our focus on 
the future of mobility. As a focused automotive business, we will continue to strengthen customer partnerships, 
accelerate innovation and drive sustainable growth .” 
 
Mayank Holani, Chief Financial Officer of SKF India Limited , said, “We continue to maintain a disciplined approach to 
financial performance. Our priorities remain clear: improving operational efficiency, managing costs effectively and 
maintaining a strong financial foundation to support profitable growth. ”       Figures in Million INR

About SKF  
Since 1907, SKF has been making some of the world’s most innovative bearings, seals, lubrication systems, condition 
monitoring solutions, and services to reduce friction. Less friction means more energy saved and by reducing it, we 
make industry smarter, m ore competitive, and more energy efficient, building a more sustainable future where we can 
all do more with less. SKF is represented in approximately 130 countries and has around 17,000 distributor locations
