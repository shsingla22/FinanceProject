Info Edge (India) Ltd. Press Release dated June 8, 2026  
Info Edge appoints Ms. Radha Rajappa and Mr. Rajesh Magow as Independent Directors  
New Delhi, June 8 , 2026: Info Edge (India) Limited (“Info Edge”)  (NSE: Naukri and BSE: 532777) , India’s 
leading consumer internet company, today announced the appointment of Ms. Radha Rajappa and Mr. 
Rajesh Magow as Independent Directors on the Board of the Company, effective June 9 , 2026, subject to 
approval of shareh olders.  
Ms. Radha Rajappa  is an accomplished entrepreneurial business leader with over three decades of 
experience in executive leadership roles in the technology industry. She has successfully incubated, led and hyper -scaled several digital and technology  businesses during her long tenure at Mindtree. Over the 
course of her career, she has also worked with global technology companies including IBM and Microsoft, 
helping enterprises navigate evolving technology landscapes during their respective transformat ive 
phases.  
Currently, Ms. Rajappa is actively engaged with early -stage technology startups and serves as Executive 
Chairperson of Flutura, an Industrial AI company that was acquired by Accenture. She is also on the board 
of Vunet Systems, a deep -tech star tup focused on Business Journey Observability. In addition, she serves 
as an Independent Director on the boards of Zensar Technologies Limited, Bata India Limited and KFin 
Technologies Limited.  
Ms. Rajappa is also an accomplised  executive coach and mentor,  helping professionals unlock leadership 
potential and navigate change effectively. She holds a degree in Electronics and Communications 
Engineering and a management degree from the Indian Institute of Management, Bangalore.  
Mr. Rajesh Magow  is the Co -Foun der and Group Chief Executive Officer of MakeMyTrip, India’s leading 
online travel company. He brings over three decades of professional experience spanning finance, 
operations, strategy and business leadership.  
Mr. Magow has been associated with MakeMyTrip since its formative years and has played a pivotal role 
in the company’s growth journey, including its NASDAQ listing in 2010, merger with Ibibo Group, 
expansion of brands such as Goibibo and redBus, and the company’s post -pandemic digital transformation  
initiatives. Prior to MakeMyTrip, he worked with Ebookers as CFO & Head of Financial Services and earlier with Aptech Limited in regional leadership roles.  
Mr. Magow also serves in industry leadership capacities, including as Chairman of the FICCI Tourism  
Committee. He is associated with Ashoka University as a Founder and Donor and serves as Founder and 
Trustee of Plaksha University. He has also served on the board of Flipkart.  
He has been the recipient of multiple industry recognitions, including the Erns t & Young Entrepreneur of 
the Year – Services (2024), Times Now India’s Impactful CEO (2024), The Economic Times Human Capital 
Awards as the  People -Focused CEO of the Year (2023), and ET Retail eDNA E -commerce Leader of the Year 
(2023). Mr. Magow is a Chartered Accountant from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.  
 
 
 
 
Commenting on the appointments, Mr. Kapil Kapoor, Chairman, said: “We are delighted to welcome Radha and Rajesh to the Board of Info Edge. Their rich experience across 
technology, en trepreneurship, digital transformation and consumer internet businesses will add immense 
value to the Company and further strengthen the Board’s strategic  capabilities .”
