PRESS RELEASE  
Gravita to set up Copper Recycling plant at Mundra  
Jaipur, August 19, 2026:  Gravita India Limited (“Gravita” or the “Company”), a leading global 
recycling company, is pleased to announce its plans to add 59,200 Metric Tonnes Per Annum 
(MTPA) of copper recycling capacity at its Mundra, Gujarat facility.  
Key Highlights  
• Planned Capacity Addition:  59,200 MTPA of copper recycling capacity  in phased 
manner  
• Location:  Mundra, Gujarat  
• Capital Expenditure:  Approximately ₹  64.00  crore s 
• Strategic Focus:  Expansion of non -ferrous metal recycling capabilities  
• Funding:  To be funded through internal accruals  
The proposed copper recycling capacity will be primarily involved in the processing of copper -
bearing recyclable materials, enabling the Company to recover copper and other valuable materials 
through its recycling capabilities. The planned 59,200 MTPA capa city addition will also serve as 
a backward integration initiative for the proposed Mandvi plant in Gujarat, strengthening the 
Company’s raw material sourcing and processing capabilities and creating greater operational 
integration across its copper recycl ing value chain.  The proposed capacity addition is expected to 
be commissioned in phases by March 31, 2029 . 
The planned capacity addition marks another significant step in Gravita’s strategy to strengthen its 
presence in the non -ferrous metal recycling segment and capitalize on the growing demand for 
recycled copper across domestic and international markets.  
This incremental copper recycling capacity will further strengthen the Company’s capabilities at 
its existing Mundra facility. The facility’s strategic location in close proximity to one of India’s 
major ports provides logistical advantages for sourcing raw materials and serving both domestic 
and international markets.  
Strategic Significance  
The planned addition of copper recycling capacity is aligned with Gravita’s long -term growth 
strategy of:  
• Expanding its presence in the copper recycling segment;  
• Increasing its overall non -ferrous metal recycling capacity;  
• Catering to the growing demand for recycled copper; and  
• Strengthening its position in the global circular economy.
