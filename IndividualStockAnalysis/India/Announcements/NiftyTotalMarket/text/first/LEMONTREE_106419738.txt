Lemon Tree Hotels launches its 11th property in  Gujarat  with  
Lemon Tree Hotel , Morbi  
 
The hotel features 69 well -appointed rooms and suites, a multi -cuisine 
restaurant, banqueting facilities, and recreational amenities  
 
New Delhi, 29th October 2025:  Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -
growing mid -scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the launch of 
its latest managed property  – Lemon Tree Hotel, Morbi . This new launch 
further strengthens the group’s footprint in Gujarat, one of the country’s key 
industrial and business hubs.  
 
Strategically located in the ceramic capital of India, the hotel caters to both 
business and leisure travellers. Its convenient location offers seamless 
connectivity to nearby industrial and commercial centres such as Rajkot and 
Jamnagar, making it an idea l choice for corporate guests and traders visiting 
the region. In addition to its proximity to key transportation hubs and Morbi’s 
renowned ceramic companies, the hotel also provides easy access to popular 
local attractions , including Mani Mandir, Green To wer, Wellingdon Secretariat, 
and Nehru Gate.  
 
This property features 69 well-appointed rooms and suites  that are 
thoughtfully designed for both comfort and functionality. The hotel houses 
Citrus Café, a vibrant multi -cuisine restau rant offering Indian, European, and 
Pan-Asian delights; and extensive banqueting facilities, including Tangerine 
Grand, and Tangerine Lawn, ideal for hosting business meetings, conferences, 
and social gatherings for up to 400 guests. Guests can also rejuvenate at the 
spa, fitness center  and swimming pool, ensuring a refreshing and relaxing 
stay. 
 
Commenting on the opening , Mr.  Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, 
Lemon Tree Hotels , said, “ Morbi is one of Gujarat’s fastest -developing cities, 
known globally for its ceramic industry and entrepreneurial spirit. With the 
launch of Lemon Tree Hotel, Morbi, we are delighted to announce the 10th 
operational property in Gujarat to bring our signature comfort, warmth, and 
value to both business and leisure travellers visiting this vibrant city. ”
