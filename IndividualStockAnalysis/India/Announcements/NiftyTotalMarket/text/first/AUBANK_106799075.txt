We wish to inform that based on the recommendations of the Audit Committee and Nomination and

Audit (‘HIA’) for a further term of one year with effect from October 7, 2026 .  
 
Mr. Vinay Vaish is a Chartered Accountant and holds a Bachelor’s degree in  Law. With over 30 years 
of professional experience, he has extensive experience across Risk Management, Credit, 
Collections, Governance and Internal Audit functions, having held several leadership roles in these 
areas.  
 
He has been associated with the Bank since May 2015 and has been heading the Internal Audit 
function since October 2021, after having served as Chief of Credit & Collections (Wholesale Banking) 
and Chief of Credit Risk.
