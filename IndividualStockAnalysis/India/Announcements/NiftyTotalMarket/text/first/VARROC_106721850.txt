Varroc Appoints Eric Hamon as Chief  Technical Officer   
Strategic leadership appointment reinforces Varroc's innovation agenda in 
electrification, connectivity and safe, smart and sustainable mobility  
 
Pune, India, 3 August  2026 : Varroc, a leading global automotive technology company, 
today announced the appointment of Eric Hamon as Chief Technical Officer (CTO), effective 
3rd August 2026 . 
 
Eric joins Varroc with more than 25 years of global automotive technology and engineering 
leadership experience. A seasoned executive with deep expertise in electrification, 
electrical -electronics, software -defined vehicles (SDVs), connected systems, embed ded 
software, functional safety and cybersecurity, he has successfully led large -scale global 
R&D organizations, developed next -generation mobility technologies and partnered with 
leading automotive OEMs to deliver innovative, safety -critical solutions.  
 
In his new role, Eric will report to Arjun Jain, Whole Time Director & CEO - Business I , 
Varroc, and will lead the company's technology and engineering organization. He will be 
responsible for defining Varroc's technology strategy and innovation roadmap, accelerating 
product portfolio optimization across electrification, electrical -electroni cs and connected 
systems, advancing engineering excellence through process optimization and 
industrialization, and strengthening technical engagement with customers and strategic 
technology partners. He will also drive the development of future -ready techn ologies that 
reinforce Varroc's commitment to delivering safe, smart and sustainable mobility solutions.  
 
Commenting on the appointment, Arjun Jain, Whole Time Director & CEO - Business 
I, Varroc,  said: "We are delighted to welcome Eric Hamon to Varroc's leadership team. 
Eric brings a rare combination of deep technical expertise, global engineering leadership 
and a proven ability to translate technology into vehicle requirements. As our industry 
accelerate s towards electrification, connectivity and sustainability, his leadership will play 
a pivotal role our accelerating growth ." 
 
Speaking on his appointment, Eric Hamon, Chief Techn ical Officer, Varroc , said:  "I 
am excited to join Varroc at such a transformative time for the global automotive industry. 
The pace of innovation in electrification, software and connected mobility presents 
tremendous opportunities to redefine the future of mobility.  Varroc has built a strong 
foundation of engineering excellence, customer trust and global manufacturing capability, 
and I look forward to working with talented teams across the world to accelerate 
innovation, deepen customer collaboration and develop tech nologies that enable safe, 
smart and sustainable mobility."  
 
Prior to joining Varroc, Eric served as Director – E Axle & System Platforms at Valeo  E, 
where he led the global product strategy for eAxle platforms.  Over the course of his 
career, Eric has built a strong reputation for combining strategic technology leadership 
with execution excellence. His experience spans technology road-mapping , system 
engineering, embedded hardware and software development, electrified powertrain 
systems, connectivity, digital transformation and global engineering operations. He has 
successfully led multicultural teams, driven innovation across multiple regions  and secured 
strategic customer programs through deep technical collaboration with global OEMs.  
 
Eric's appointment underscores Varroc's continued investment in strengthening its global 
engineering and innovation capabilities as the company expands its technology portfolio 
to address the rapidly evolving needs of mobility customers worldwide.

About Varroc Engineering Ltd.  
Varroc Engineering Ltd is a global tier -1 automotive component group. It was incorporated 
in 1988. The group manufactures and supplies E -mobility solutions, Body systems 
solutions, Lighting solutions, HMI solutions, ICE powertrain and Advanced electronics to 
leading OEMs with end -to-end capabilities across design, development and manufacturing 
for two -wheeler, three -wheeler, passenger vehicles, commercial vehicle, and off -highway 
vehicle worldwide. The group income was ₹ 89,080 million from continued operat ions in 
FY26. The group employs more than 5400 employees (750 + R&D Engineers), has 37 
global operating manufacturing facilities supported by 7 R&D Centres, and has filed more 
than 130 patents.  
Varroc Engineering Limited’s shares are listed on the National Stock Exchange (VARROC) 
and the Bombay Stock Exchange (541578) in India.
