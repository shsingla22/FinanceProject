Noida , Oct 1, 2024 : APL Apollo Tubes Limited (APL Apollo), the World's largest branded structural 
steel tube company, today announced its sales volume performance for Q 2FY25. The Company 
registered the highest  ever sales volume of 7,58,267 Ton in Q 2FY25 compared to 7,21,064  Ton in 
Q1FY25 and 6,74,761  Ton in Q 2FY24.  For H1FY2 5, the company reported sales volume of 
14,79,331  Ton, an increase of 11% YoY.

About APL Apollo Tubes Limited  
APL Apollo Tubes Limited (APL Apollo) BSE: 533758, NSE: APLAPOLLO is India’s leading structural steel tube manufacturer. 
Headquartered at De lhi NCR, the Company operates 1 1 manufacturing facilities with a total capacity of 4.3 Mn Ton. It has a pan -
India presence with units strategically located in Sikandarabad (UP), Hyderabad, Bangalore, Hosur (Tamil Nadu), Raipur 
(Chhattisgarh), Malur (Karnataka), Murbad (Maharashrtra) and Umm AI Quwain (UAE) . APL Apollo’s multi -product offerings include 
over 2,500 varieties for multiple building material structural steel applications. With state -of-the-art-manufacturing facilities, APL 
Apollo serves as a ‘one -stop shop’ for a wide spectrum of products, catering to an array of industry applicat ions such as urban 
infrastructure and real estate, rural housing, commercial construction, greenhouse structures and engineering applications. T he 
Company’s vast 3 -tier distribution network of over 800 Distributors is spread across India, with presence in over 300 towns and cities.
