Swan Realty Launches ‘Bodhi’ – an exquisite, low -density residential project 
in Jayanagar  
• Spread over 30,000 sq. ft, the project offers 24 four -bedroom residences, with only two 
apartment per floor.  
• Designed by Kembhavi Architecture Foundation, the residences are inspired by ‘Biophilic 
Amorphous’ design.  
Bengaluru, October 14, 2026 : Swan Realty, the real estate arm of the century -old diversified 
industrial conglomerate Swan Corp Limited, has announced the launch of luxury project 
‘Bodhi’ in Jayanagar, Bengaluru, offering a rare combination of privacy, nature, and refined 
living. The  project marks Swan Realty’s second development in the city, following the 
successful delivery of Cardinal One in Yeshwanthpur.  
Designed to bring together the experience of an independent home with the convenience of 
apartment living, Bodhi comprises 24 four -bedroom residences, each spanning over 4,000 sq. 
ft., with only two apartments per floor. Located in Jayanagar, one of South Bengaluru’s most 
established neighbourhoods, the low -density development is designed to offer residents 
greater privacy, space and a close connection with nature.  
Designed by Kembhavi Architecture Foundation (KAF), Bodhi follows a ‘ Biophilic Amorphous’ 
approach that mimics nature through fluid forms and a design language that creates a 
seamless connection between the built environment and its natural surroundings. Expansive 
residences, large landscaped private decks, abundant natural light and panoramic views 
extend this philosophy into the homes, while elements such as the Sky Garden, Sundowner 
Court, a Koi pond and a stargazing deck further integrate nature into th e residential 
experience.  
The development also offers a curated range of amenities including a temperature -controlled 
swimming pool, wellness and fitness centre, a celebration lounge and recreational room.  
Jay Shah, Head of Business, Swan Realty,  said, “The successful delivery of Cardinal One in 
Bengaluru validated our conviction that home buyers increasingly value space, privacy and 
liveability alongside location. Jayanagar is one of Bengaluru's most established residential 
neighbourhoods, with l imited opportunities for new development. Bodhi addresses this gap 
through a low -density residential format that combines the privacy of an independent home 
with the convenience of apartment living. At Swan Realty, w e believe successful real estate 
development goes beyond creating buildings. It is about shaping environments that remain 
relevant over time through thoughtful design, quality execution and a clear understanding of 
how people aspire to live.”  
The project is RERA -registered (PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/PR/170126/008409) and is 
scheduled for completion by March 2028.
