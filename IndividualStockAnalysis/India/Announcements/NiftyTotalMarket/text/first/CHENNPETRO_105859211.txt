Pursuant to Reg. 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 (SEBI LODR), we would like to inform 
that the Company has received notices from BSE Ltd. (BSE) and National Stock Exchange of India 
Limited (NSE) dt.22.05.2024 regarding non-compliance with the provision of Regulation 17(1) of the 
SEBI LODR for the composition on of the Board including failure to appoint One Woman Independent 
Director on the Board of the Company & Regulation 19(1)/ 19(2) of the SEBI (LODR) for Non- 
compliance with the constitution of Nomination and Remuneration Committee during the quarter ended 
31st March 2024 and imposition of fine of Rs. 7,51,660/- each by BSE & NSE for such non- 
compliance. 
In response to the notices, Chennai Petroleum Corporation Itd. vide letter dt. 23.05.2024 has represented 
to the BSE & NSE that being a Government Company, the power to appoint Directors (including 
Independent Directors) vests with the MoP&NG, Govt. of India and hence the non-appointment of 
Independent Director including women independent Director on the Board during the quarter ended 
31st March 2024 was not due to any negligence / fault by the Company. Accordingly, Chennai 
Petroleum Corporation Itd. should not be held liable to pay the fines and the same should be waived- 
off. Chennai Petroleum Corporation ltd regularly takes up with MoP&NG for appointment of requisite 
number of Independent Directors (including One Woman Independent Director) and Composition of 
Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC) to ensure compliance with Corporate Governance 
norms enunciated under SEBI (LODR) as well as the Companies Act. 
‘We would also like to inform that the Company had received similar notices from the BSE & NSE in 
the past imposing fines and application for waiver request from the Company was filed with the 
Exchanges and BSE/NSE also favourably considered our Waiver request for certain period.
