Heritage Foods Rings in Festive Season on a Cheery Note  
GST-led dairy price cuts set to boost volumes, reinforce  market leadership, and delight consumers  
Hyderabad , September 18, 2025 - Heritage Foods, one of India’s most respected dairy brands,  today 
announced substantial price reductions across its product portfolio, passing on the entire benefits of the 
Government of India's recent GST rationalization to consumers ahead of the festive season.  
Following the GST Council’s landmark decision, effective September 22, 2025, Heritage Foods  is passing 
on the entire benefit of the GST rate rationalisation on to the consumers and has reduced prices across 
multiple dairy categories. This consumer -first move ensures families across India can continue to enjoy 
their festive celebrations with high -quality dairy products at more affordable prices, stimulating 
significant incremental demand during the festive season . Delivering these savings directly to consumers 
by lowering prices across multiple dairy categories, this approach ensures families nationwide, including 
those in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities , can enjoy premium dairy products, making their celebrations even more 
joyous and accessible.   
“The GST rate rationalisation  for India’s dairy industry is both timely and a welcome  move,” said Brahmani 
Nara, Executive Director,  Heritage Foods.  “By easing the tax burden on staples like paneer, ghee, butter, 
and cheese, the Government has made it possible for us to pass on the entire benefit of GST rate 
rationalisation to our consumers. For us at Heritage, this isn’t just about lower prices , it’s about ensuring 
that the purity our products are known for becomes even more accessible to families across India. Dairy 
is central to every celebration, from festive sweets to daily meals, and with these reductions, the 
authentic taste and purity of dairy will bring added cheer to homes this season.”  
Under the revised pricing:  
• Long shelf -life UHT milk : ₹3 per litre reduction ( No price change for fresh milk as the 
category w as exempted from GST since beginning ) 
• Ghee: ₹50 per litre reduction  
• Butter & Cheese : ₹50 per kg reduction  
• Paneer: ₹25 per kg reduction  
• Ice cream : ₹35 off the 950 ml pack and ₹20 off the 700 ml pack  
 
By extending these benefits, Heritage Foods reaffirms its commitment to providing value to consumers 
while strengthening the role of dairy in festive traditions. With products like ice cream, paneer, and ghee 
playing a central role in traditional recipes across regions, these reductions make festive celebrations 
more enjoyable and accessible. Heritage Foods also continues to ensure that its products are available 
through both retail and online channels, reaching consumers quickly. By combining accessibility, 
affordability, and quality, this move promises to bring added joy, warmth, and inclusivity to festive 
celebrations across the country.

About Heritage Foods  
Heritage Foods is one of India’s leading dairy companies, founded in 1992 with a vision to provide high -
quality, nutritious milk and dairy products to families across the nation. Over the years, Heritage has 
grown into a trusted brand, known for its commit ment to purity, freshness, and sustainability. With 
operations spanning 1 7 states, Heritage Foods serves millions of consumers, offering a wide range of 
milk, curd, butter, paneer, and other value - added dairy products.  
 
Heritage Foods stands out for its farm -to-home approach, ensuring every product is produced with the 
highest standards of quality and care. The company works closely with its network of dairy farmers, 
empowering them with modern farming practices, training , and a fair partnership model, vetted by their 
500+ Quality Experts who perform more than 25 stringent quality tests daily which in turn guarantees a 
consistent supply of fresh, high -quality milk.  
 
Driven by a passion for excellence and a deep understanding of consumer needs, Heritage Foods 
remains at the forefront of India’s dairy industry, constantly striving to provide products that nourish, 
comfort, and support families across the country.
