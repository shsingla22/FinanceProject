 Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 read with Schedule I II to the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we refer  to our earlier letter dated May 28, 
2024, w here we had intimated that Jubilant Biosys Limited  (“JBL”) , a wholly -owned subsidiary 
of Jubilant Pharmova Limited (the ‘Company’), had approved th e proposal for investment 
including  entering into a Power Supply and Offtake Agreement, and  Share  Subscription and 
Shareholders Agreement  on May 28 ,  2024 with Isharays Energy Two Private Limited   (“Isharays 
Energy”) , a wholly -owned subsidiary of SunSource Energy Private Limited, for acquisition of 
upto 14.75% stake of Isharays Energy for purchase of renewable energy (electricity) generated 
from the Captive Generating Plant (CGP)  for facilities located at Greater Noida and Noida . 
 We wish to update you that the RE power available from Isharay Energy is only sufficient to meet the power requirements of Greater Noida facility, the primary user of which facility is the Company. Therefore, the Company is the eligible captive  user for this RE power project. Thus 
instead of JBL , the Company will be  invest ing as captive user  into the equity of Isharays Energy. 
This includes signing of Power Supply and Offtake Agreement to buy electricity from their power 
plant  and supply to Greater Noida facility of the Company, and for which the Company will enter 
into a Share Subscription and Shareholders Agreement  to acquire up to 14.53% of the Isharays 
Energy ’s shares. 
 The Company has embarked on a renewable energy implementation journey across its facilities 
in India to enable reduction in both cost and carbon footprint.  This investment shall enable the 
Company to access renewable energy through captive power arrangement for its facilit y 
located and Greater Noida.
