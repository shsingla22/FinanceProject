PRESS RELEASE  
 
Board level changes in Blue Star  
 
Sam Bals ara to retire after completion of two consecutive 
terms as an Independe nt Director  
 
 
Sam Balsara  (Sam) , who attained the age of 75 years , will retire on 
January 31, 2026,  after completion of two consecutive terms as an 
Independent Director.  
Sam, Chairman of Madison World, one of India’s largest media and 
communication agencies, joined  the Blue Star Board in June 2017  and 
was reappointed for  the second term in June 2022. As a prominent 
figure in the field of marketing and advertising  with over five decades 
of experience , he has guided the Company on various fronts including 
brand building strategies, consumer insights, and media trends.  As the 
Chairman of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, h is 

contribution to the Leadership Development and Succession Planning 
programmes of the Company was invaluable.  
 
M S Unnikrishnan appointed as an Independent Director  
 
 
 
M S Unnikrishnan (Unnikrishnan) has been appointed as  an 
Independent Director with effect from January 29, 2026, for a perio d 
of 5 years.  
Unnikrishnan has over four decades of experience  and is currently the 
Head & CEO of the IITB -Monash Research Academy, a joint venture 
set up between the Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay,  and 
Monash University , Australia . Prior to that he served as the Managing 
Director of Thermax group , an Indian engineering major focused on 
energy and environment solutions, with a manufacturing footprint 
spread across multiple locations in India  and globe.  
Unnikrishnan serves on the boards of  KEC International Limited , 
Kirloskar Brothers Limited , Greaves  Cotton Limited,  and Livguard  

Energy Technologies Pvt. Limited . He also serves as a trustee of 
Akshayapatra  and Jehangir Hospital , Pune.  
Unnikrishnan is a graduate in Mechanical Engineering from 
Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur  and he has also 
completed his Advanced Management Program from Harvard Business 
School.  
 
B Thiagarajan reappointed as  Managing Director for a further 
term from April 1, 2026,  up to  May 24, 2027  
 
 
B Thiagarajan (Thiagarajan) , on completion of his current term on 
March 31, 2026,  as Managing Director , has been reappointed for a 
further term from April 1, 2026,  until May 24, 2027, a day prior to 
attaining the age of 70 years.  
 
B Thiagarajan holds a bachelor’s degree in Electrical  and Electronics 
Engineering from Madurai University. He has also completed the 
Senior Executive Programme from London Business School. 

Thiagarajan has over four decades of professional experience in 
handling B2B and B2C businesses , and h e has been with Blue Star  
since 1998. He was elevated to the Board in 2013 and was appointed 
as Joint Managing Director in 2016 before taking charge as Managing 
Director with effect from April 2019.  
Thiagarajan plays an active role in var ious industry forums and is 
currently a member of the CII National Council. He is also the National 
Chairman of the Indian Green Building Council and CII Green Cooling 
Council. In the past, he has been the President of Refrigeration and 
Air Conditioning Ma nufacturers Association (RAMA), and the Chairman 
of the CII Western Region . 
 
Mohit Sud elevated as Executive Director with effect from 
April 1, 20 26 
 
 
Mohit Sud (Mohit)  has been appointed as Executive Director , Unitary 
Cooling Products for a p eriod of five years with effect from April 1, 2026.  

Mohit joined Blue Star in March 2025  as Group President, Unitary 
Cooling Products , which includes the Room Air Conditioner and 
Commercial Refrigeration businesses. He has been holding end-to-end 
responsibility including sales, marketing, service, R&D, manufacturing, 
and supply chain.  
Mohit , a Mechanical Engineer with an MBA from XLRI, Jamshedpur , has 
rich experience in sales , marketing, distribution,  and business strategy. 
Prior to joining Blue Star, in a career spanning  over two decades with 
Hindustan Unilever , he has successfully led sales & marketing of several 
product categories including Home Care and Beauty & Well Being in 
many geographies across India. In his last assignment as Vice 
President, he was responsible for driving the Premium Retail 
distribution for the Beauty & Well Being business.  
 
Vir S Advani, Chairman & Managing Director  adds, “Blue Star is 
an 82-year-old brand, Sam has played a significant role in our strategic 
efforts to make it stronger, youthful,  and relevant to the new 
generation consumers as well as Tier 3 -4-5 markets. His marketing 
insights helped  the Company to gain market share year after year. On 
behalf of the Board of Directors, I wish to place on record our  deep 
appreciation for his outstanding contribution and exemplary service 
during his tenure.   
Unnikrishnan , with his exemplary track recor d in leadership roles in  
running engineering products and projects businesses  and exposure to 
international markets , is a welcome  addition to the Blue Star Board .
