DHANTERAS BUSINESS UPDATE - October 1, 2026 to November 11, 2026  (41 days)  
Senco Gold Limited  ("Senco" or "the Company") has delivered a record performance during the 41-
day festive period from October 1, 2026  to November 11, 2026 , achieving its highest -ever Retail Net 
Sales Value (NSV) of over ₹2,426 crore during the Dhanteras and Diwali festive season. Despite 
significant volatility and elevated gold prices, the Company registered an impressive 26% growth over 
the corresponding period last year . 
The strong performance was driven by higher customer footfalls, increased transaction volumes, robust 
new customer acquisition and healthy growth across both Company -owned and franchise -operated 
showrooms.  
The Company also achieved its highest -ever topline of over ₹5,000 crore in H1 FY27 and crossed 
the significant milestone of ₹10,000 crore in sales  on a trailing twelve -month (TTM) basis, reflecting 
its sustained growth momentum and increasing scale of operations.

2. Business Performance and Management Commentary  
Commenting on the Company's marketing initiatives and customer engagement during the 41 -day 
Dhanteras festive period , Mr. Suvankar Sen, (Managing Director & CEO, Senco Gold Limited ), 
said:  “The Company adopted a focused and comprehensive marketing strategy during the 41 -day 
festive period, aimed at attracting new customers, deepening existing customer relationships and driving 
higher store footfalls and conversions. The Dhanteras Shagun camp aign, supported by the launch of 
several new jewellery collections, was designed to capture the festive buying sentiment, with a 
particular emphasis on affordable yet aspirational jewellery in the ₹50,000  – ₹2,00,000  price range, 
catering to the diverse preferences of festive shoppers.  
Our marketing initiatives were strategically aligned with two key customer segments:  
• Wedding -led customers:  Strengthening our positioning as a preferred jewellery destination 
for wedding shoppers ahead of the upcoming wedding season through attractive collections, 
contemporary designs and a diverse range of offerings.  
• Young and aspirational customers:  Expanding our reach among younger consumers through 
a wider assortment of lightweight and everyday -wear jewellery across gold, diamond and 
platinum categories, in line with their evolving tastes and preferences.  
 
These targeted initiatives, combined with our differentiated product offerings and customer -centric 
approach, translated into encouraging results, with the Company registering substantial double -digit 
growth in new customer acquisitions compared with the c orresponding Dhanteras period last year. This 
reflects the continued strength of our customer engagement initiatives and our ability to connect with 
diverse consumer segments while creating sustained momentum for the business.”  
The Everlite  collection , promoted by brand ambassador Wamiqa Gabbi, was launched during the 
festive season. The Durga Puja and Diwali campaigns also witnessed encouraging responses, helping 
attract repeat customers and strengthen relationships with existing and loyal customers.  
Under its Aham  collection , the Company also launched the Ti22 Collection , marking a pioneering 
initiative in titanium jewellery crafted with gold and diamonds by an Indian pan -India jewellery retailer.  
Collectively, these initiatives strengthened brand engagement, expanded the customer base and enabled 
the Company to effectively convert latent demand, demonstrating the relative resilience of jewellery 
demand despite elevated gold prices.  
Network Expansion and New Showroom Launches  
The Company continued to strengthen its pan -India retail presence by opening 10 additional showrooms 
during the 41 -day festive period, comprising of 3 COCO, 3 FOCO and 4 FOFO showrooms. In addition 
to these, the Company expects to open another 3 –4 showrooms, taking the total number of new 
showroom additions during the year to approximately 27 –28. 
Diamond Jewellery Performance and Customer Acquisition  
Diamond jewellery continued to be an important growth driver, with sales registering 26% growth 
during the Dhanteras festive period. Its contribution to the overall sales mix increased to 9.6%, 
compared with 9.4% in the corresponding period last year.  
New customers accounted for 37.3% of total customers during the festive period, compared with 35% 
in the previous year, reflecting the success of the Company's focused customer acquisition initiatives 
and growing brand appeal.  
Coin and bullion sales continued to remain in single digits and below 7% of overall sales, reflecting the 
sustained strength of the Company's jewellery -led business.  
The performance reflects strong consumer demand, successful customer acquisition initiatives, healthy 
diamond growth  and increasing contribution from the franchise network.
