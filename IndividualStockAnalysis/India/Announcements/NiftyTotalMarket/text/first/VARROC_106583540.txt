Varroc Appoints Avijit Roy as Group  Chief Human Resources 
Officer  
 
Pune, India, 8 April  2026 : Varroc Engineering Limited, a leading global tier -1 
automotive supplier, today announced the appointment of Avijit Roy as Group Chief 
Human Resources Officer (CHRO)  and reporting to Arjun Jain, Whole Time Director & CEO 
– Business I, effective 10th April 2026. 
 
In this role, Avijit will spearhead Varroc’s g roup human resources strategy, driving a 
people -first, future -ready agenda aligned with the company’s growth ambitions across 
India and key international markets. He will focus on strengthening organizational 
capability, enabling a high -performance culture, an d accelerating talent transformation in 
an increasingly digital, technology -led mobility ecosystem.  
 
Avijit succeeds Kavita Kulkarni, who has served as G roup Chief Human Resources Officer 
for the past seven years. During her tenure, she played a pivotal role in shaping Varroc’s 
people strategy, leading key initiatives across talent development, leadership building, and 
future -ready workforce planning. Her cont ributions have been instrumental in 
strengthening Varroc’s organizational resilience and building a robust global talent 
pipeline. The company extends its sincere appreciation for her leadership and l asting 
impact on Varroc’s transformation journey.  
 
Commenting on the appointment,  Arjun Jain, CEO  – Whole Time Director & CEO -
Business I , Varroc, said , “At Varroc, our people drive our ambition to deliver products 
that enable a world class safer, smarter, and more sustainable mobility ecosystem. Avijit 
comes onboard with demonstrated ability to build agile, future -ready organizations and 
align talent strat egy with business priorities in varied environments. As we drive the next 
phase of growth, his leadership will ensure our growth is sustainable from a people 
perspective. We also extend our sincere appreciation to Kavita for her invaluable 
contribution to helping us evolve into an agile, people first organization. ” 
 
Speaking on his appointment, Avijit Roy  said, “I am excited to join Varroc at a defining 
moment in its transformation led growth journey. The company has a strong legacy and a 
clear vision for the future. The opportunity to build a future -ready organization , where 
human insight and evolving technologies work in synergy , is both inspiring and 
meaningful. I look forward to nurturing a culture that empowers people, drives 
performance, and creates sustainable long -term value.”   
 
Avijit brings over 27 years of rich experience across the automotive and manufacturing 
ecosystem, having held senior HR leadership roles with leading global organizations. Most 
recently, at the Aditya Birla Group, he served as Senior Vice President – Global Human 
Resources, where he led the  large-scale people strategy and transformation for a multi -
billion -dollar business spanning 14 countries and 1 9 location s bringing a distinctive blend 
of global perspective and strategic depth to Varroc.  
 
Prior to this, he held key leadership roles across Asia and India, including Regional HR 
Head- Asia, where he drove transformative people initiatives across diverse markets, 
supported business expansion including China market entry, and led large -scale workforce 
and industrial relations strategies in complex manufacturing environments.

About Varroc Engineering Ltd.  
Varroc Engineering Ltd is a global tier -1 automotive component group. It was incorporated 
in 1988. The group manufactures and supplies E -mobility solutions, Body systems 
solutions, Lighting solutions, HMI solutions, ICE powertrain and Advanced electronics to 
leading OEMs with end -to-end capabilities across design, development and manufacturing 
for two -wheeler, three -wheeler, passenger vehicles, commercial vehicle, and off -highway 
vehicle worldwide. The group income was ₹ 81,718 million from continued operat ions in 
FY25. The group employs more than 6,100 employees (750 + R&D Engineers), has 37 
global operating manufacturing facilities supported by 7 R&D Centres, and has filed more 
than 120 patents.  
Varroc Engineering Limited’s shares are listed on the National Stock Exchange (VARROC) 
and the Bombay Stock Exchange (541578)  in India .
