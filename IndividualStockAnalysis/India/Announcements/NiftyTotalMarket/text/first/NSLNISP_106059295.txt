This is to inform that Ministry of Steel (MoS), Government of India vide Order No. 14013/1/2023-
BLA dated 13.11.2024 has communicated the appointment of Shri Joydeep Dasgupta (DIN: 
10837095), Executive Director, Steel Authority of India Limited as Director (Production) on the 
Board of NMDC Limited from the date of his assumption of the charge of the post, till the date of 
his superannuation i.e. 30.04.2028, or until further orders, whichever is earlier, subject to the 
final outcome of W.P. No. 14454/2024 in the High Court of Karnataka (Bengaluru Bench).

Directors and Government Directors of NMDC Limited will also be the CMD, Functional Directors 
and Government Directors of NMDC Steel Limited respectively on co-terminus basis, till further 
communication in this regard. 
 
Accordingly, Shri Joydeep Dasgupta (DIN: 10837095) will also be Director (Production) on the 
Board of NMDC Steel Limited, subject to assumption of the charge of the post in terms of Order 
dated 13.11.2024 as referred above.
