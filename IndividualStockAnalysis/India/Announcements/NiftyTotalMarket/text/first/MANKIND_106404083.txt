Pursuant to Regulation 30, 51 and other applicable provisions, if any, of the Listing Regulations and in 
continuation to our earlier intimation dated July 31, 2025, we wish to inform you that the Mankind 
Pharma Limited (“Company”)  has executed a Business Transfer Agreement (“BTA”) with Bharat 
Serums and Vaccines Limited (“BSV”), a Wholly Owned Subsidiary Company, on October 10, 2025 
for acquisition of its Branded Generic Business relating to Women Health Rx Portfolio on  going 
concern basis by way of slump sale for a sale consideration of ₹797 Crores, subject to closing 
adjustments, payable in the following manner: 
 
1. 50% of the sale consideration shall be paid on the Closing Date as defined in the BTA; and  
2. Balance sale consideration shall be payable within 150 days from the Closing Date. 
 
The completion of above acquisition is subject to pre-conditions, closing actions and other terms & 
conditions as specified in the BTA.

Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 were already 
provided in our earlier intimation dated July 31, 2025.
