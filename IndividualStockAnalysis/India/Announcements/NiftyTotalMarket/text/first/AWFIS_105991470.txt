Awfis unveils 30,221  Sq. Ft. built -up area , Grade -A Flexible Workplace in  
GIFT City  - Gandhinagar, Ahmedabad  
 
National, September 18th , 2024:  Awfis, India’s largest network of flexible workspaces 
announced the launch of its new centre in Gujarat International Finance Tec -City (GIFT 
City), Gandhinagar. Awfis is the only flex ible space provider in a  Grade -A development  
offering a gamut of services under one brand. This new centre offers  30,221  sq. ft. built -up 
area in one of India's most prominent financial and technology hubs.  With this addition, the 
company is  now offering a total of approx. 83,500 sq. ft. built -up area  in Ahmedabad.   
This premium facility , Awfis  Gold centre  is located on the 26th floor in Gift One Tower -GIFT 
City, Gandhinagar  designed to cater to the evolving needs of modern businesses, offering 
state -of-the-art infrastructure and flexible workspace  solutions .  
Gift City is a thriving financial and technological ecosystem offer ing attractive opportunit ies 
to start -ups, entrepreneurs and large corporations . The project  is well-connected  and 
provides modern social infrastructure , premium residential  and commercial buildings.  This 
centre’s strategic location in GIFT City places Awfis as an ideal destination  to cater  to 
growing commercial flex-space demand.  
Key features of the centre : 
● Awfis Gold Centre : This centre is a premium workspace solution featuring aesthetic 
designs, luxurious finishes, and personalized services to enhance the work 
experience  
● Connectivity:  The centre is strategically located providing connectivity to the GIFT 
City, Metro, Airport, and Ahmedabad City, making it an ideal choice for businesses 
seeking a prime location  
● Mother's Room:   A dedicated space for working mothers providing  a convenient 
workspace fostering work -life balance  
● Amenities:  The  centre  offers parking facility, collaboration areas, and meeting 
rooms  along with flex spaces  (co-working & managed)  
Commenting on the launch , Mr. Amit Ramani, Chairman and Managing Director, Awfis 
Space Solutions Limited , said, "Flex space is undeniably the future of the commercial real 
estate sector, offering flexibility and scalability that modern businesses need. Our 
expansion into GIFT City significantly enhances Awfis’ value proposition by strengthening 
our presence in Ahmeda bad and across the country, positioning us and our clients at the 
heart of India’s emerging financial and technology hub. Gujarat is known for its 
entrepreneurial spirit and business acumen. The new Awfis centre located in a Grade - A 
 
development of Gift City is well -positioned to cater to the needs of the customers in this 
dynamic market, providing comprehensive workspace solutions that support their growth 
and success . Awfis ’ focus on innovation and customer -centric solutions positions it well to 
thrive in the evolving commercial real estate landscape. " 
Awfis, a leader in the coworking industry  and the only publicly listed company  in the sector  
has established the largest flex space network in the country  and showcased the immense 
potential of flex space s in the industry . Awfis plans to expand its presence in India by adding 
40,000 new seats in FY25, bringing its total to 135,000 seats by the end of the fiscal year. This 
growth aligns with the increasing demand for flexible workspaces and positions Awfis as a 
lead ing brand in the  commercial real estate industry .
