Key Highlights  
 
Performance Highlights : 
• Revenue  for Q2  FY27 up 15% QoQ and up 18% YoY  
• EBITDA for Q2  FY27 up 18% QoQ  and down 9% YoY  
• PAT for Q2  FY27 up 59% QoQ and down 7% YoY  
• Revenue for H1 FY27 up 17% YoY & EBITDA down 8% YoY  
• PAT for H1  FY27 up 20% YoY  
• Order Intake of ₹ 1,198 Crores up 19% QoQ and up 36% YoY ; India Order Intake at ₹483 Cr  
• Backlog of ₹ 2,446 Crores up 7% QoQ and up 14% YoY  
Corporate  Highlights:  
• Repayment of approx. INR 116 crores of Net Debt in H1 FY27  
• Maintained CRISIL Ratings at AA- with Stable Outlook  
• CRISIL assigned a rating of CRISIL ESG 61 (‘Strong’ Category) to the Company for FY26, as against 
CRISIL ESG 50 (‘Below Average’ Category) assigned for FY25  
• Group -level leadership structure realigned  with appointment of Mr. Gregory Gelhaus as Group CEO  
and Mr. Ankit Nayyar as Group CFO  providing direction and oversight to the four business divisions  
 
Management Comment s  
 
Commenting on the Company’s Q2 & H1  FY27 results, Mr. Tarak Patel, Managing Director,  said; 
"We are pleased to report our highest -ever quarterly order intake and a record order backlog, which gives us 
good revenue visibility for the coming quarters. Our diversified portfolio continues to drive growth, and our 
focus remains on improving margins and the quality of our earnings .” 
 
Commenting on the Company’s Q 2 & H1  FY27 results, Mr. Gregory Gelhaus, Group CEO , said; 
“Our global transformation program is driving cost efficiencies and m argin improvement s by aligning  common 
priorities and leveraging our global scale across every division . With a record order backlog and healthy 
demand across our markets, we are well positioned for the coming quarters ”

About GMM Pfaudler  Limited 
 
GMM Pfaudler is a diversified global engineering company with a heritage of more than 140 years. The 
Company brings together strong engineering capabilities, a cost -efficient global manufacturing footprint, and 
deep process know -how to help customers impro ve performance, reliability, and efficiency in process 
industries worldwide.  
With 20 manufacturing facilities across four continents and a strong local presence, the Company operates 
through four distinct global division s: Corrosion -Resistant Technologies , Process Performance 
Technologies , Heavy Engineering Technologies , and Process System Technologies , to serve 
customers worldwide.
