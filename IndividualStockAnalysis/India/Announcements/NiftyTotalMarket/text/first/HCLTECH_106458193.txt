HCLTech joins AI Verify Foundation to advance Responsible AI  
 
SINGAPORE and NOIDA, India, Dec 01 , 2025 — HCLTech , a leading global technology 
company, has joined the AI Verify Foundation, a non- profit open source software 
foundation that focuse s on promoting  trustworthy AI, reinforcing the company’s 
commitment to Responsible AI  practices.   
 The AI Verify Foundation brings together AI developers  and deployers , testing and 
assurance service providers, technology solution providers, researchers, users and 
policymakers to build  a global community to advance trustworthy AI. As a member, 
HCLTech will have the opportunity to support  and contribute to the Foundation’s  mission .  
 HCLTech’s Responsible AI tenets —including accountability, fairness, security, privacy, 
transparency, ethics and collaboration— are aligned with the AI Verify Testing Framework, 
which comprises 11 internationally recognized AI governance principles. This ref lects 
HCLTech’s commitment to developing and deploying AI solutions that are ethical, trustworthy, and beneficial to society.  
 
“HCLTech’s membership in the AI Verify Foundation underscores our commitment to 
helping enterprises navigate the opportunities and challenges of AI adoption  responsibly  
as we shape together a future based on transparency, collaboration and trust ,” said 
Heather Domin, Vice President and Head of Office of Responsible AI and Governance , 
HCLTech.   
 
HCLTech  is a member of the Responsible AI  Institute, has signed the EU AI Pact and has 
been named a Responsible AI Partner by Microsoft. The company has received the first 
AI Governance Verification Badge from the Responsible AI Institute and has been named 
one of the world’s most ethical companies for two consecutive years .  
 
For more information about HCLTech’s Responsible AI initiatives and its commitment to ethical and trustworthy AI , visit https://www.hcltech.com/responsible- ai.
