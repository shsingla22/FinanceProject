HCL Tech celebrates 50th anniversary of HCL Group by planting 50,000 saplings  
NOIDA, August  11, 2026 —HCLFoundation, which drives the corporate social responsibility agenda 
of HCLTech  in India , today celebrated the 50th anniversary of HCL Group by carrying out plantation 
of 50,000 native saplings and launching clean -up drives at 50 beaches across the country.   
The mega  environmental drives , organized across 10 states , brought together HCLTech employees, 
their families, nonprofit partners, women’s collective s, youth and other community members . The 
initiative s form part of HCLFoundation's broader sustainability efforts that seek to strengthen 
ecosystems, conserve natural resources and inspire community participation in environmental 
stewardship.  
HCLFoundation has greened 74,000 acres of land  to date , bringing them under community 
management . More than 155 billion liters of water have  been harvested to date, creating a habitat for 
biodiversity and strengthening water security for some of India’s most stressed communities. As part of its coastal conservation efforts, more than 181,000 saplings of terrestrial,  mangrove and shelter  belt 
species have been planted  and over 120,800  kg of ghost nets and  marine debris have been collected  to 
date. 
“As we celebrate 50 remarkable years of HCL Group, we are reminded every  day that growth and 
progress are not separable from a deep commitment to a sustainable planet and resilient communities,”  
said Dr. Nidhi Pundhir, Director, HCLFoundation. “ We believe that meaningful change is achieved 
when communities come together with a shared purpose and w e are grateful to our employees, 
families, partners and volunteers for joining us in contributing to a greener, cleaner and more 
sustainable future. "  
About HCLFoundation  
HCLFoundation delivers the corporate social responsibility agenda of HCLTech in India. The 
Foundation contributes to national and international development goals by investing in long- term 
sustainable programs and special initiatives with thematic focus on education, nurturing grassroots 
sports, health and sanitation, skill development and livelihood, environment and disaster risk reduction 
and response management. To ensure equitable development, child protective strategies, inclusion and 
gender transformat ive approaches are at the core of all the programs of the Foundation. To date, the 
HCLFoundation has positively impacted over 8.5 million lives. To learn more, visit 
www.hclfoundation.org
