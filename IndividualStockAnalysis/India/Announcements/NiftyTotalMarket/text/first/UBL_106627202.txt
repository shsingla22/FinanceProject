Sub:  Closure of  Operations of Ludhiana Brewery/ Unit  
 
We wish to inform that the Company  has decided to close its brewery unit situated at C/60, Focal Point, 
Ludhiana – 141010 , Punjab (‘Ludhiana Brewery’/‘Unit’)  effective June 30, 2026.   
 
We have entered into a long -term capacity lease agreement with a Contract Brewing Unit , thereby  
secured the supplies of the Company’s beer in the State of Punjab and the neighbouring States,  
including Delhi. This long -term lease will play a pivotal role in meeting the future demand  efficiently 
and sustainably through enhanced operations within the State of Punjab. The closure of the Ludhiana 
Brewery will not impact our business performance in the State.  
 We reaffirm our deep and enduring commitment to the State, its communities, and our consumers.                     
We are also committed to taking all necessary and responsible steps to support our employees and workmen through this transition .  
 
The said closure is pursuant to intimation (s) made to the Excise department of Punjab dated  
May  18, 202 6, and also being made to the Stock Exchanges in the interest of transparency.
