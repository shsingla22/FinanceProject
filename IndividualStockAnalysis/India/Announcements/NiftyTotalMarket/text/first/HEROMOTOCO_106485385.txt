HERO M OTOCORP REPORTS  ROBUST 40% GROWTH  IN 
DECEMBER DISPATCHES AT 4.56 LAKH UNITS  
 
 DOUBLE  - DIGIT  GROWTH LED BY  STRONG MOMENTUM  IN 
SCOOTER S AND GLOBAL BUSINESS  
 
 
Hero MotoCorp,  the world ’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters , recorded 
strong dispatch growth of 40% in December 2025 with  456,479 units , compared to 
324,906 units  during the same period last year and recorded 291,732  VAHAN  
registrations  this month. This reflects  robust demand  driven by continued festive 
momentum, new launches, and strong performance in both ICE and EV scooter  portfolio . 
This month also saw strong continued traction in Global business, contributing to overall 
growth across key markets.  
*Data from VAHAN except Telangan a, as on January 1, 2026.  
 
Strong Q 3 2026 P erformance  
Building on its highest -ever festive season, Hero MotoCorp reported  robust performance for 
Q3FY ’26 (October –December  2025) , recording year -on-year growth in both motorcycles 
and scooters . The strong performance during the quarter was led by new model launches 
across portfolios . The ICE scooter segment witnessed a notable growth of 45% during the 
quarter, led by new models including Destini 110, Destini 125, Xoom 125 and Xoom 160.   
 
Global B usiness Success – A Landmark Year  
Hero MotoCorp ’s Global Business continued its upward growth trajectory  with dispatch 
growth of 2 1% in December 2025 with 37,236  units , compared to 30,754 units  during the 
same period last year. During Q3FY ’26, the Company sustained positive traction across its 
key markets  with 4 1% growth in exports , supported by new market entries and portfolio 
expansion.  
 
VIDA Accelerates Its Growth Trajectory, Gains Market Share  
VIDA,  Hero MotoCorp ’s Emerging M obility business , reported robust performance with 
10,701 VAHAN registrations  during December 2025 . VIDA continued sustained growth 
through the year with market share at 11%. The strong performance was driven by the 
success of VX2 EVOOTER, sustained festive momentum and po sitive consumer sentiment 
across markets.
