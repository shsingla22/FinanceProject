The Company had, as per the applicable provisions of the Companies Act and the SEBI LODR Regulations, provided the facility of remote e -Voting and e -Voting at the AGM  for casting

AGM ), in terms of the applicable provisions of the Companies Act and the SEBI LODR 
Regulations (“Scrutinizer’s Report”).

the AGM Notice have been passed with requisi te majority and we are enclosing herewith:  
- the Voting Results of the remote e -Voting and e -Voting during the AGM, as Annexure A ;
