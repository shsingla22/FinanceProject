The Members are informed that, Mrs. Deepti Sehgal (DIN: 09772630) was appointed as Non-Executive Independent Director of the

previously associated with Infogain Corporation and Infogain India Private Limited as a Vice President, Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu India

April, 2026 to 31st March, 2029.

Director for a second consecutive term of 3 (three) years from 1st April, 2026 to 31st March, 2029, not liable to retire by rotation, pursuant
