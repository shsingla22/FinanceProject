electronic means.

The shareholders of the Company holding shares as on the cut-off date of October 23, 
2024, were entitled to vote on the resolution as contained in the postal ballot notice.

i. In accordance with the Postal Ballot Notice and the 'Advertisement' published 
pursuant to Rule 22(3) of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 
2014, the Postal ballot through e-voting commenced at 9.00 a.m. 1ST, Monday, 
October 28, 2024, and closed at 5.00 p.m. 1ST, Tuesday, November 26, 2024, and
