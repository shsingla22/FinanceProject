PRESS RELEASE  
 
KIRLOSKAR PNEUMATIC COMPANY  LTD. AND KOBE STEEL LTD SIGN ED 
MoU TO DEVELOP BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES IN THE OIL & GAS SECTOR  
 
Strategic collaboration to leverage complementary strengths in the non -general 
purpose compression technologies, engineering capabilities and market reach . 
Pune, India – 27th October 2026 : Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Limited (KPCL)  and Kobe Steel 
Ltd. (KOBELCO), Japan have entered  into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to explore 
and develop business opportunities in the Oil & Gas sector.  
The MoU marks an important step towards building a strategic relationship between the two 
companies and leveraging their complementary strengths in engineering, compression 
technologies, manufacturing capabilities and market presence.  
Under the proposed collaboration, Kirloskar Pneumatic and Kobe Steel will jointly explore 
opportunities in the Oil & Gas sector  in India and selective export projects  including potential 
projects and solutions across upstream, midstream and downstream applications. The 
collaboration will focus on identifying customer requirements, developing suitable business 
opportunities and leveraging the technical and commercial st rengths of both organisations.  
Mr. Aman Kirloskar, Managing Director, Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Limited, said : “We are 
delighted to enter  into this MoU with Kobe Steel, a globally respected engineering and technology 
company. This collaboration brings together complementary strengths in technology, engineering 
and market capabilities. We see significant potential to jointly address the evolving requir ements 
of the Oil & Gas sector and create differentiated solutions for customers in India and international 
markets. This partnership is aligned with our strategy of expanding our technology portfolio, 
strengthening our presence in high -value applications and creating sustainable long -term growth.”  
The collaboration reinforces the commitment of both companies towards technology -led growth, 
engineering excellence and delivering reliable and energy -efficient solutions for the evolving 
needs of the global Oil & Gas industry.  By doing so, the companies aim to strengthen their business 
platform and competitiveness in India’s growing market, expand business primarily in the oil and 
gas sectors, and create new business opportunities in carbon -neutrality -related fields.

About Kobe Steel, Ltd. – KOBELCO  
Kobe Steel, Ltd. (KOBELCO)  is one of Japan’s leading diversified industrial groups, with a history 
spanning more than 120 years. Founded in 1905  and headquartered in Kobe, Japan, the Company 
has evolved from a steel manufacturer into a global enterprise with businesses spanning steel and 
aluminum , advanced materials, machinery, engineering, and electric power . 
KOBELCO combines deep manufacturing expertise, advanced technology and strong research 
capabilities to provide high -value products and solutions to customers across diverse industries. 
Its machinery and engineering businesses serve sectors including indust rial manufacturing, 
energy, construction and infrastructure, while its materials businesses support demanding 
applications across automotive, transportation, electronics and other industries.  
With 38,614 employees, 232 group companies and operations across 24 countries  as of March 
31, 2026, KOBELCO has established a significant global presence.

About Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Ltd.  
Established in 1958, Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Ltd. (KPCL) is a diversified company offering a 
comprehensive range of products, including Air, Refrigeration, and Gas compressors and systems, 
vapour absorption chillers, and industrial gearboxes. Serving v arious industries such as steel, 
cement, cold chains, food and beverages, pharmaceuticals, railways, defense and marine, KPCL 
holds a significant presence in the Oil & Gas sector. Notably, the Company is a key player in CNG 
business in India  
 
Leveraging extensive industry experience, KPCL has forged technology partnerships with leading 
global companies and research institutes, ensuring cutting -edge solutions and innovations. The 
company has been recognized with numerous awards for excellence in  quality, innovation, merit, 
training programs, HR initiatives, and in -house communication, establishing itself as a leader in 
the industry.
