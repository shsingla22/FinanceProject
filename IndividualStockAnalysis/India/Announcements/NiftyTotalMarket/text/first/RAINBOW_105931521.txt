following points about e-voting: 
 
 The Board of Directors engaged the services of National Securities of Depository Limited (“ NSDL ”) to 
provide e-voting facility.  
 
 The Company had provided the remote e-voting facility to all the Members to cast their vote 
electronically on all the resolutions set forth in the notice of 26th AGM. The remote e-voting period 
commenced on Friday, July 26, 2024 at 9:00 A.M and ended on Monday, July 29, 2024 at 5:00 P.M.  
 
 The Members who had not cast their votes through remote e-voting can cast their votes during the 
meeting through the e-voting system provided by NSDL.  
 
 The Board of Directors have appointed Mr. K.V.S Subramanyam, Designated Partner of              
M/s. BS & Co LLP, Company Secretaries as Scrutinizer to scrutinize the remote e-voting and e-voting 
process during the AGM in a fair and transparent manner.

Kancharla, Chairman & Managing Director and then he t hanked the Members for attending the meeting.  
 Thereafter, Mr. Ashish Kapil, Company Secretary & Compliance Officer informed the Members that the
