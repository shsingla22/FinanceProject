Dear Madam / Sir  
This is in continuation of our disclosure dated 22nd February 2024 and 2nd April 2025 ,  and 
pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended from time to time (“Listing Regulations ”), the 
Company is  in receipt of the order of the Hon ’ble High Court of Him achal Pradesh (“Hon’ble 
High Court ”) dated 2nd June 2025 (“Order ”) in respect of OMP 94/2024 in Execution Petition 
No. 19/2023 (“Applications ”) which had been filed by the State of Himachal Pradesh seeking 
directions from the High Court for handover of possession, allocation of MRL ’s assets and 
revenues in the reconciliation process, and access to various documents and information in 
connection with the Wildflower Hall property at Shimla, Himachal Pradesh  under the Arbitral 
Award dated 23rd July 2005  (“Arbitral Award ”). 
The Order  adjudicates the financial claims and obligations under the Arbitral Award and inter 
alia provides as follows:  
1. The Hon ’ble High Court has held that the Company is liable to pay a sum of Rs.18.94 
crore to the State of Himachal Pradesh (“State ”) towards penalty and user fee, in 
accordance with the Arbitral Award . This amount had already been deposited in the 
Registry of the Hon ’ble High Court and is directed to be released to the State.  
2. A further prayer of the State seeking additional compensation of Rs.77.39 crore as “user 
fee” was withdrawn and accordingly , not adjudicated upon.  
3. The Hon ’ble High Court has permitted the State to deposit Rs.13.00 cr ore towards 
consideration for acquisitio n of 2 .60 crore equity shares held by the Company and its 
affiliates in MRL and has directed that such shares be transferred to the State, pursuant 
to the Arbitral Award .
