Thomas Cook India & SOTC Travel join hands with Disney Cruise Line  
for their First Consumer Activation in India:  
Gateway to Adventure at Mumbai’s Jio World Drive -  till July 6, 2025 
 
• Thomas Cook India and SOTC's cruise experts will be present throughout the 3 -week activation to inspire 
and guide customers with their cruise vacation bookings  
• Special Offer* from Thomas Cook & SOTC Travel! Complimentary 1- night hotel stay with breakfast in 
Singapore with on- ground transfers on every Disney Adventure cruise booking  
Mumbai, June 19, 2025:  Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel travel services company, 
and its Group Company SOTC Travel, team up with Disney Cruise Line for its first -ever consumer activation 
in India, offering Indian consumers a preview of a Disney cruise vacation and opportunity to avail of a special 
offer on their Disney Adventure cruise bookings. Disney Adventure - the first Dis ney ship in Asia will homeport 
in Singapore from December 2025.  
 
The activation, “Gateway to Adventure”, is open to the public at Mumbai’s Jio World Drive till July 6, 2025. 
Through the mall’s ground and first floors, customers get a unique opportunity to explore and take photos at 
photogenic spaces featuring stories of  Disney, Pixar and Marvel characters inspired by the seven themed 
areas on-board the Disney Adventure cruise.  
India’s consumers are displaying a strong and growing appetite for cruising, and the Thomas Cook and SOTC 
India Holiday Report 2025 reveals that  45% of respondents are keen on cruise holidays. Cruise experts from 
Thomas Cook and SOTC will be present at Jio World Drive’s TGCO Monsoon Hangar until July 6, 2025 to 
guide and assist customers in booking a Disney cruise experience.  
Mr. Rajeev Kale - President & Country Head, Holidays, MICE, Visa - Thomas Cook (India) Limited  said,  
"Cruising is witnessing strong momentum, with a clear shift towards premium, experience- led sailings, rich in 
storytelling. Disney Adventure delivers this and more – a world -class cruise experience designed to captivate 
every age group. We are delighted to be the on- ground travel distributors for the unique “Gateway to 
Adventure” event and to inspire bookings via our special complimentary offer. ” 
Mr. S.D. Nandakumar -  President & Country Head -  Holidays and Corporate Tours, SOTC Travel  Limited  
said, " Disney Cruise Line’s entry into Asia is a great opportunity for Indian travellers to experience the magic 
of Disney, closer to home. What’s encouraging is the strong demand that we are witnessing not only from India’s metros but al so from emerging Tier 2 markets – highlighting a growing appetite for premium, global 
experiences. Indian travellers - across families, honeymooners and groups of friends - are now embracing 
cruising as a preferred holiday format. " 
Guests of the Disney Adventure will enjoy a Disney cruise vacation on a grand scale, including the hallmarks of every Disney cruise – fun and relaxation for everyone, world- class dining, incredible entertainment, and 
exceptional guest service.
