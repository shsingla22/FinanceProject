Discovery of Natural Gas in the Shallow Offshore Block KG -DSF/  
GSKW/2018 of Krishna Godavari Basin by Oil India Limited  
        Oil India Limited (OIL), a National Oil Company & Maharatna CPSE with presence across the entire 
Oil & Gas Value Chain has made a significant stride in its quest for hydrocarbons with the discovery of 
Natural Gas in the otherwise unapprised “Matsyapuri” formation in the Krishna -Godavari Basin.  
        The Well Godavari – 1 has been drilled as an Appraisal Well in the Discovered Small Field Block KG -
DSF/GSKW/2018 in the shallow offshore water of KG Basin, awarded to the Company as a Sole Operator.  
       Out of the selected pay zones, presence of hydrocarbon was reported in the depth range of 2024 
to 2027 meters.  
        Production testing of the zone started from 16th October 2026 and resulted in flow of Natural Gas 
to the surface at a flow rate of about 1 Lakh SCUMD with maximum flow rate of 2 Lakh SCUMD achieved 
on continuous testing with composition of condensate at th e rate of about 170 bopd (API @ 60 °F is 
55.8) and water with Well Head Pressure of 1815 psi. Presently preparations are on for testing the next 
higher up zo ne.
