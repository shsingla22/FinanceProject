Dear Members, 
This has reference to the Notice of 105th Annual General Meeting of the Bank dated July 22, 2026 
(“AGM Notice ”) to transact the following Special Business among others:   
 
Item no. 7: Approval of CSB Bank Employee Stock Option Scheme 2026 (“ ESOS 2026 ” or 
“Scheme ”) as a special resolution.  
 
The Bank is issuing this Corrigendum in respect of the following matters forming part of 
Explanatory Statement annexed to the AGM Notice: 
 
It may be noted that in last couple of years, the Bank has granted employee  stock options 
(“Options ”) at prevailing market price as on date of grant i.e. no discount has  been offered under 
its existing ESOS Scheme. This has been the practice in spite of havi ng flexibility in determining 
the exercise price or discount. In view of this practice, the Bank considers that the pricing flexibility 
in the proposed ESOS 2026 in the present form should be changed to give more clarity and 
predictability to the stakeholders.  
 
Accordingly, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of the Board (“ NRC ”) and the Board 
of Directors of the Bank (“ Board ”) have respectively considered and approved a corrigendum as 
regards the revision of the exercise price clause of ESOS 2026 and point (f)  of the Explanatory 
Statement to the special resolution placed at item no. 7  of the AGM Notice as under: 
 
CORRIGENDUM 
Present clause:  The Exercise price per Option shall be determined by the Committee which shall 
not be less than the face value of Share as on date of grant. 
 
Proposed clause as per corrigendum:  The Exercise price per Option shall be equal to the market 
price as on date of grant.  
 
The Members are requested to read the AGM Notice in conjunction with this Corrigendum . All 
other contents of the AGM Notice, save and except as modified by this Corrig endum, shall remain 
unchanged.
