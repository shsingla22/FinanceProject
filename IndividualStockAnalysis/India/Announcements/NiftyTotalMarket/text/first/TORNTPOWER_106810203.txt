Ref:   Disclosure pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and

With reference to the captioned subject and our intimation dated July 14, 2022, we would like 
to inform you that Torrent Saurya Urja 2 Private Limited, Wholly owned Subsidiary of Torrent 
Green Energy Private Limited which is also Wholly owned Subsidiary of  the Company had 
entered into long term Power Purchase Agreement (“PPA”) of 300 MW wind power Project 
with Solar Energy Corporation of India Limited (“SECI”) on May 04, 2023 after receipt of Letter 
of Award from SECI on July 14, 2022.   
 
The Project was materially impacted by Force Majeure events and delays in the readiness of 
external infrastructure, including transmission connectivity and operationalisation of GNA. 
SECI took note of the circumstances and had, accordingly, granted extensi ons to the 
Scheduled Commercial Operation Date (SCOD).  
 
However, the period of extension had progressively extended beyond a reasonable 
timeframe for implementation of the project under the framework of the PPA, particularly in 
view of the prevailing circumstances and changed project environment. Accordingly, 
considering the continued uncertainty regarding the implementation (SCOD) of the project, 
the 300 MW PPA was terminated.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Pursuant to the applicable contractual provisions, liquidated damages of ₹39.78 crore are 
payable in connection with such termination of the PPA.    
 
You are requested to take the same on records.
