2.  Whether the 
transaction would fall 
within related party transactions? If yes, 
whether the same is done at ‘arm’s length’  Yes, the Company and the Transferor Company are related parties.  
However, in terms of General Circular No. 30/2014 dated July 17, 2014, 
issued by Ministry of  Corporate Affairs (“ MCA Circular ”), the 
transactions arising out of compromises, arrangements and amalgamations under the Companies Act, 2013, will not attract the 
requirements of Section 188 of the Companies Act, 2013. 
Further, the consideration to be  discharged for the scheme of 
amalgamation is determined by independent registered valuers on which a fairness opinion has been is sued by an independent category 1 
merchant banker, the transaction therefore is at arm’s length.

PSL is engaged in the business of buying, selling and otherwise trading

4.  Rationale for 
amalgamation/ merger  The Scheme is in the best interests of the Parties and fair and reasonable 
for the shareholders, employees, cred itors and other stakeholders of 
each of the Parties. and would result in the following advantages as set 
out below: 
 
(a) The merger ensures better alignm ent of the Transferee Company’s 
long-term strategic interests by enabling it to directly hold the 
investment in Bhushan Power and Steel Limited (BPSL). This 
consolidation provides a platform to facilitate any transactions and 
investments related to BPSL’s business, thereby enhancing 
strategic focus without minority shareholders involvement; 
(b) Pursuant to the proposed amalgamation, the Company will have 
 
 
 
 
 
 
additional cash to pursue its growth aspirations in a financially 
prudent manner  
(c) reduction in multiple entities and regulatory compliances will 
further reduce the overall compli ance and overhead costs of the 
Company; 
(d) Reducing time and efforts for consol idation of financials at Company 
level; and 
(e) The scheme will lead to consolidation of administrative and 
managerial functions, eliminating duplicative record-keeping and 
associated expenses. This will result in a reduction of overhead costs and optimal utilization of resources within the Steel group.
