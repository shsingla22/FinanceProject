Highest Ever Second Quarter & Half Year  
Revenue , EBITDA & Profitability  
 
Results for the second  quarter ended September  30, 202 6 
 
▪ H1 FY27 Revenue at ₹ 1,81,086 Mn; up 46% YoY  
▪ H1 FY27 EBITDA at ₹ 25,587 Mn; up 36% YoY  
▪ H1 FY27 PAT at ₹ 17,867 Mn; up 38% YoY  
 
▪ Q2 FY27 Revenue at ₹ 98,989 Mn; up 53% YoY  
▪ Q2 FY27 EBITDA at ₹ 14,225 Mn; up 39% YoY  
▪ Q2 FY27 PAT at ₹ 9,900 Mn; up 43% YoY  
 
 
 
Mumbai , October 15 , 202 6:  Polycab  India Limited (BSE: 542652, NSE: POLYCAB) today announced its results for the 
quarter ended September  30, 202 6. 
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Inder T. Jaisinghani, Chairman and Managing Director, Polycab India 
Limited, said:  “Q2 FY27 marked  another quarter of strong execution and meaningful progress for Polycab. We 
delivered our highest -ever second quarter and half -yearly revenue and profitability, reflecting the strength of our 
market position, the robust performance of our Wires & Cables b usiness, and the continued strengthening of our 
FMEG business . The domestic demand environment continues to be encouraging, supported by healthy 
infrastructure spending, increasing electrification opportunities, and improving private sector investment activity. Our 
international business maintained its growth moment um, despite an uncertain geopolitical environment . As we 
advance our Project Spring initiatives, we remain committed to accelerating growth, driving premiumization, 
deepening market penetration, and consistently delivering superior value to customers, partners, and shareholders. ” 
 
Key Highlights ( Q2 FY2 7) 
 
• The Company’s consolidated revenues delivered a strong 53% YoY growth to stand at ₹ 98,989  Mn. The strong 
growth was driven by robust execution across both Wires & Cable s (W&C)  business  and Fast -Moving Electrical 
Goods ( FMEG ) business .

▪ The Wires & Cables business delivered a strong performance during the quarter, registering 47% year -
on-year growth, driven by a robust 50% increase in domestic revenues. Growth was supported by 
healthy demand across key end -user segments, aided by sustained infrastructure activity and strong 
execution on the ground under Project Spring initiatives. Within the W&C business, the wires segment 
continued to outperform the cables segment, while channel sales grew faster than institutional sales. 
The international business recorded a healthy 17% YoY growth despite a challenging global 
macroeconomic environment. Our diver sified geographic presence, expanding customer base, and 
strong  order book position the business well for continued growth. EBIT margins improved 
sequentially, supported by operational efficiencies and a favourable  business mix .  
 
▪ The FMEG segment delivered another breakthrough quarter, with revenues increasing more than 
doubling year -on-year  (+124%) on the back of strong demand across all key product categories. Solar 
continued to lead the growth story, reporting multifold expansion and retaining its position as the 
largest contributor to segment revenues. The quarter also marked a defining moment in  the 
business's profitability journey, as EBIT margins surpassed the double digit  threshold for the first time 
ever, reaching an all -time high of 10.1%. This  achievement underscores the effectiveness of our scale -
driven growth strategy, enhanced portfolio mix, and sustained focus on operational excellence, 
positioning the segment for continued value creation.  
 
▪ The EPC business demonstrate d strong momentum, delivering 53% year -on-year revenue growth 
during the quarter. Segment profitability remained healthy, with EBIT margins of 7.2%. Backed by a 
healthy  order book, the business is well -positioned to sustain growth.  
 
• EBITDA grew by a strong 39% YoY and 25% QoQ, driven by sequential margin expansion across segments. 
Consolidated EBITDA margins improved by ~53 bps sequentially to 14.4%.  
 
• PAT registered a strong growth of 43% YoY, with PAT margins standing at 10.0%

About Polycab  
 
Polycab  India Limited (PIL) is India’s largest manufacturer of Wires and Cables and one of the fastest growing FMEG 
companies with a consolidated turnover of ₹ 285+ Bn in FY2 6. PIL is at the forefront of providing innovative, safe and 
energy efficient products to a diverse set of customers via a strong distribution network of 3,900+ authorized dealers 
and 190,000+ retail outlets. PIL’s business operations span across India through 2 6 manufacturing facilities, 1 5 offices 
and 35 warehouses. PIL has also served customers in 94 countries globally. PIL’s 4 ,500+ employees are dedicated to 
upholding robust governance practices, preserving a customer centric culture, having a purpose to serve the 
communities, and imbibing a genuine sense of ecological consciousness. For further information, please visit 
www.polycab.com .
