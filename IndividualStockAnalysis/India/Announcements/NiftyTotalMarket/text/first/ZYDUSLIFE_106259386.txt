Zydus receives tentative approval from USFDA for  
Rifaximin Tablets, 550 mg  
 
Ahmedabad, India, June 2 , 2025  
Zydus Lifesciences Limited (including its subsidiaries/ affiliates, hereafter referred to as 
“Zydus”) has received tentative approval from the United States Food and Drug Administration 
(USFDA) for Rifaximin Tablets, 550 mg (USRLD:  Xifaxan® Tablets, 550 mg).  
 
Rifaximin tablets are indicated for the treatment of irritable bowel syndrome with diarrhoea 
(IBS -D) in adults. Rifaximin tablets will be produced at the Group’s manufacturing site at SEZ 
II, Ahmedabad.  
 
Rifaximin tablets had annual sales of USD $2672.9 mn in the United States (IQVIA MAT March 
2025).  
  
The group now has 427 approvals and has so far filed 492* ANDAs since the commencement of  
the filing process in FY 2003 -04.  
 
(*As on 31st March, 2025)
