SAREGAMA ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP SUCCESSION AT POCKET ACES 
  
  
MUMBAI, INDIA –  Aug 12 2026 – Saregama India Limited, an RP -Sanjiv Goenka (RPSG) Group 
company, announced a structured leadership succession for its subsidiary, Pocket Aces. 
  
Effective October 1, 2026, Aditi Shrivastava, co-Founder, MD & CEO of Pocket Aces, will 
transition from her executive leadership role to a strategic position on the Company’s Board of 
Directors. Concurrently, Vinay Pillai, currently serving as Chief Business Officer, will be elevated 
to Chief Executive Officer (CEO) effective the same date. 
  
As CEO since 2021, Aditi built Pocket Aces into one of India's leading digital entertainment companies boasting culture creation brands such as FilterCopy , Dice Media and Clout, leading 
up to its landmark acquisition by Saregama in 2023.   
The elevation of Vinay Pillai to CEO reflects Saregama’s  commitment to nurturing elite internal 
talent. Vinay’s 6-year track record growing the creator management business Clout and then as 
the CBO of Pocket Aces makes him the natural leader to drive the company into its next chapter 
of scale. Vinay is a graduate of Columbia University, brings consulting experience from Booz Allen 
Hamilton, and an entrepreneurial mindset from founding his streaming startup, Dekkho.  
  
Pocket Aces has had a significant role in shaping the short form content, web series, and creator management landscape in India. The company will continue to create content for young Indians 
via its direct-to-consumer and creator channels, and work with the biggest advertisers and streaming platforms in the country. It will also remain to play a critical role as a unique marketing 
arm for Saregama’s new and catalogue music. 
  
About Saregama India Limited:  
Saregama India Ltd., part of the RPSG Group, is India’s leading Entertainment IP company with 
a legacy dating back to 1902. Its diverse portfolio includes film and non-film music, digital series, 
television content, film production, short-format content, artiste and influencer management, live 
events, and retail products like Carvaan.  
 
About Pocket Aces:  
Pocket Aces is India's leading digital media entertainment company, acquired by Saregama India 
Limited in 2023. The company boasts a massive ecosystem of highly popular digital brands 
including FilterCopy and Dice Media, and talent management vertical Clout, commanding a 
massive, highly engaged millennial and Gen-Z audience footprint.
