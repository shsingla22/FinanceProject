31 March 2014. The Company had filed a writ petition on 11 October 2013 before the Hon'ble High Court of Delhi 
against the demand raised by DDA as the revised license fees had been increased manifold and made applicable 
retrospectively from financial year 2007-08. Further, DDA vide communication dated 29 August 2016 had revised the 
total demand (excluding interest) to Z330.73 crores for the period upto 31 March 2016. The same was also reported in 
the previous periods as a contingent liability. 
The matter is pending in the Hon'ble High Court of Delhi and the Company is of the view that such demand is not 
tenable and accordingly, no provision has been made for this aforementioned demand raised by DDA in the books of 
accounts.
