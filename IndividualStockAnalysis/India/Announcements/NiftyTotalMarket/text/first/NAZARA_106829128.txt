Nazara reports  record Q2FY27: revenue up 53% to 
INR 803 crore, EBITDA up 93% to INR 12 0 crore  
 
• First quarter with Bluetile and BestPlay; gaming now 82% of revenue at a 2 1.5% EBITDA margin  
• H1FY27 revenue of INR 1,232 crore (up 20%) and EBITDA of INR 1 66 crore (up 5 2%) at a 
13.5% margin  
• Organic growth of 11% in revenue and 17% in EBITDA in the gaming segment in Q2FY27  
 
Mumbai, 26th October 2026: Nazara Technologies Limited (“Nazara” or “the Company”) today 
announced its financial results for the quarter and half year ended 30 September 2026. The quarter 
marks a defining step in Nazara’s evolution into a global gaming platform , one that  combines a portfolio 
of owned game IPs across mobile, PC & console and offline, shared group capabilities in marketing, 
data, product and technology, and disciplined capital allocation. Q2FY27 is the first quarter to include 
Bluetile and BestPlay, consoli dated from 1st August 2026.  
 
Record quarter  
 
Nazara reported its highest -ever quarterly revenue of INR 803 crore, up 53% YoY. EBITDA grew 9 3% 
to INR 12 0 crore, and EBITDA margin expanded 3 11 bps to 1 4.9%. Profit after tax from continuing 
operations was INR 35 crore, compared with a loss of INR 34 crore in Q2FY26. For H1FY27, revenue 
grew 20% to INR 1,232 crore and EBITDA grew 5 2% to INR 1 66 crore.  
 
A focused, profitable gaming company  
 
Gaming revenue grew 122% YoY to INR 656 crore at a 2 1.5% EBITDA margin. Gaming contributed 
82% of consolidated revenue and effectively all EBITDA before corporate costs. Mobile grew revenue 
167% to INR 550 crore. PC & Console grew EBITDA 6 1%, with Curve’s margin expanding to 38.9%. 
Offline grew EBITDA 29% on flat revenue, with margin rising to 26. 2%. 
 
Bluetile and BestPlay: Scale and Capability  
 
In their first two months as part of Nazara, Bluetile and BestPlay generated revenue of INR 329 crore, 
up 49% over the same period last year. EBITDA grew 6 4% to INR 6 4 crore, and margin expanded to 
19.4% from 17.5%. Growth came from rapid product iteration, expansion into new markets, AI -
automated user acquisition and cross -promotion across the portfolio. Bluetile’s leadership team holds 
5.8% of Nazara, aligning  its in terest s with those of all shareholders.  
 
The existing portfolio keeps compounding  
 
Growth  in existing gaming businesses  was underpinned by strong content engines across the studios . 
WildWorks grew revenue 41% and EBITDA 39% on new Animal Jam content, including a Monster 
High -themed season. Kiddopia grew revenue 21% on a 5% rise in subscribers and an 8% rise in average 
revenue per user , and is reinvesting in user acquisition to accelerate further gains . Fusebox launched 

 
 Traitors and a new Big Brother season. Love Island: The Game ranked #1 among free role-playing 
games on the US App Store in July . Curve grew revenue 27% and EBITDA 61%, driven by the launch 
of Sovereign Tower, rated “Overwhelmingly Positive” on Steam, the mobile debut of Wobbly Life at 
#3 in its genre on the App Store and Google Play, and continued momentum at Human Fall Flat, which 
has passed 60 million players in its 10 th anniversary year . 
 
One platform, every studio  
 
Bluetile’s core capabilities now operate as group Centres of Excellence (CoEs) across Marketing, Data, 
Product and Technology, each led by a dedicated head. AI is embedded in  all four. In a single month, 
the Marketing CoE produced more than 290 ad creatives for three studios . A single AI -ready data stack 
powers analytics  across the group, and all  studios share a common prioritisation and greenlight process. 
Every new game and acquisition now plug straight into proven systems, compounding the advantage 
with each addition . 
 
Raymond A. Stauffer, CEO, Nazara Technologies, said: “This quarter demonstrates the company we 
are building: a portfolio of high -quality games on a single, shared platform. Bluetile and BestPlay grew 
EBITDA 64% in their first two months as part of Nazara, while sustained investment in content 
delivered  organic growth of 11% in revenue and 17% in EBITDA across our existing gaming studios. 
Of greater long -term significance is the platform we have built. Every studio now draws on group 
Centres of Excellence in marketing, data, product and technology, each underpinned by AI. Every game 
we launch and every studio we acquire benefits from proven capabilities from the outset, enabling us 
to scale faster and more profitably.”  
 
Leadership strengthened  
 
Nazara significantly strengthen ed its leadership t eam, adding global gaming expertise at  the top and 
strengthening  India team . Raymond A. Stauffer, Founder  of Bluetile and BestPlay, took charge as CEO  
on 16 September 2026. Mithun Sacheti, Founder of CaratLane, joined as Executive Director on 12 
October 2026, subject to shareholder approval. Arun Agarwal, former Global CFO of Ness Digital 
Engineering, joined as Chief Financial Officer on 16 October 2026 . Together, the team brings the global 
gaming, operating and financial depth to scale Nazara’s platform.  
 
About Nazara Technologies  
 
Nazara Technologies is India’s only publicly listed gaming company. Its key businesses include 
Blue tile, Curve Games, Kiddopia, Animal Jam, Fusebox Games (Love Island, Big Brother, Bigg Boss , 
Traitors ), C.A.T.S., World Cricket Championship  and King of Thieves , along with offline gaming 
businesses such as Funky Monkeys and Smaaash Entertainment. Nazara also operates Datawrkz, a n 
adtech  business  and Sportskeeda, a sports media platform . With presence in India, North America, 
Europe, and other global markets, Nazara is building a global gaming platform with strong IP, 
publishing, and operating capabilities.
