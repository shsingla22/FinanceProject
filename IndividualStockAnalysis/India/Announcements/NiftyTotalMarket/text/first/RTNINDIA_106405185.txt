Revolt Motors lights up the season with Diwali Double Dhamaka 
with  benefits worth up to ₹1 lakh * 
New Delhi, 13th October 2025:  This Diwali, Revolt Motors, India’s No.1 electric motorcycle brand, is 
turning up the celebrations with its biggest -ever festive bonanza , the Diwali Double Dhamaka Offer . 
Customers can now bring home the Revolt electric motorcycle s with exciting cash benefits  and 
assured gifts worth up to ₹1 lakh.  
The offer brings up to ₹13,000 * off along with free insurance worth up to ₹7,000 *, giving customers 
more reasons than ever to make the electric switch this festive season.  
And to double the excitement, Revolt is adding a sparkle of surprise to every purchase with assured 
festive gifts for all customers. From premium TVs, laptops, smartphones, and microwaves to silver 
coins, smartwatches, TWS earbuds, and exclusive Revolt merchandise, there’s something fo r everyone 
to unwrap this Diwali. To make it even more special, one lucky customer will win a gold voucher worth 
up to ₹1 lakh , making this a festive celebration to remember.  
 
 
Ms. Anjali Rattan , Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises Limited , said , “We wanted to make this 
Diwali truly special for our customers. The Diwali Double Dhamaka is our way of celebrating their trust 

   
in Revolt. It’s not just about great offers , it’s about adding joy, excitement, and rewards to every Revolt 
experience. ” 
The offer is valid only till October 21, 2025 , giving customers a limited window to make the most of 
these festive benefits before the lights go out on this exclusive deal.  
At Revolt Motors, the celebrations go beyond electric , they’re about empowering every ride with 
innovation, performance, and the thrill of clean mobility.  
This Diwali, make the switch to electric and double your celebrations with Revolt.  
Customers can book their ride online at www.revoltmotors.com  or visit the nearest Revolt Hub to avail 
the offer.
