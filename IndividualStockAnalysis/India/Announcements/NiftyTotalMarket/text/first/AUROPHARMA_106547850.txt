Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015  and  with reference to your  email  communication mentioned in the reference 
above, seeking verification / clarifications  on news item appearing in media,  we submit that : 
 
on 27.02.2026, the Court of Sh. Jitendra Singh, Special Judge (PC Act) (CBI) -23, Rouse 
Avenue Court Complex, New Delhi in CBI Case No. 56/2022 titled as “CBI Vs. Kuldeep Singh 
& Ors.”, discharged Mr. P. Sarath Chandra Reddy, Non -Executive Director of Aurobindo 
Pharma Ltd., along  with 22 other accused in the alleged Delhi Liquor Exercise Policy case 
(‘Delhi Liquor case’), after two months of day to day arguments and extensive examination of 
evidence and material produced by CBI before the Hon’ble Court.  
 
The case originated from FIR No. RC0032022A0053 dated 17.08.2022, lodged by CBI/ACB, 
under Sections 120 -B, 420 and 201 of IPC and the provisions of Section 7, 7A, 8 and 12 of the 
Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988. Mr. P. Sarath Chndra Reddy was added as Accused No. 
23 vide 4th supplementary chargesheet dated 29.07.2024.  
 
After thorough analysis of allegations of CBI and material on record, the Court discharged Mr. 
P. Sarath Chndra Reddy inter alia holding that there was no material showing that Mr. P. 
Sarath Chndra Reddy had participated in any conspiracy, influenced any public servant, paid 
or received any illegal gratification, or obtained any unlawful benefit in relation to Delhi Liquor 
Policy. The Court further noted that it has failed to comprehend the basis on which Mr. P. 
Sarath Chndra Reddy has been arrayed as an accused in the case.
