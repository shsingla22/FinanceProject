CreditAccess Grameen Appoints Ms. Devika Praveen 
As The Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)  
 
Bengaluru, 11th June  2026:  CreditAccess Grameen Limited  (NSE: CREDITACC, BSE: 541770, ‘CA Grameen’), 
India’s leading rural -focused inclusive financ ing platform,  today announced the appointment of Mrs. Devika 
Praveen  as its Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)  with effect from 11th June 2026.  
Mrs. Devika brings nearly three decades of rich experience in banking and financial services, with deep expertise 
in regulatory compliance, governance, anti -money laundering (AML), regulatory supervision, policy 
management, compliance testing, and Board- level oversigh t. Prior to joining CA Grameen, she served as 
Deputy Chief Compliance Officer at Slice Small Finance Bank.  
 
Over the course of her distinguished career, she has held several key leadership positions across leading 
financial institutions, including Chief Compliance Officer at Fincare Small Finance Bank and senior compliance roles at AU Small Finance Bank, Kotak M ahindra Bank, and ING Vysya Bank. She has extensive experience in 
managing regulatory relationships, overseeing supervisory inspections, strengthening enterprise -wide 
compliance frameworks, driving AML and regulatory reporting initiatives, and ensuring rob ust governance 
practices across organizations.  Mrs. Devika holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from the 
University of Mysore and is a CAIIB -qualified banking professional.  
 Speaking on her appointment,  Mrs. Devika Praveen  said, “I am delighted to join CreditAccess Grameen, an 
institution that has consistently demonstrated its commitment to responsible finance and financial inclusion.                
I look forward to working closely with the leadership team to further strengthen the Company’s compliance and 
governance framework, foster a culture of regulatory excellence, and support its sustainable growth journey.”  
 
Mr. Ganesh Narayanan, Managing Director & CEO, CreditAccess Grameen Limited , remarked, “We are 
pleased to welcome Devika to CreditAccess Grameen. Her extensive experience in regulatory compliance, and 
governance, coupled with her deep understanding of the evolving regulatory landscape, will be invaluable as we 
continue to strengthen our compliance framework and uphold the highest standards of corporate governance. We look forward to her contributions in  supporting the Company’s long -term growth and reinforcing stakeholder 
trust.”  
 
About CreditAccess Grameen Limited  
CreditAccess Grameen Limited is India’s leading rural -focused inclusive financing platform headquartered in 
Bengaluru. The Company provides a curated lifecycle credit suite to low -middle income households spanning 
group loans, individual business loans, secured business loans, affordable housing loans, and two -wheeler 
financing. The Company operates across 451 districts in 16 states (Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Gujarat, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasth an, Tamil Nadu, 
Telangana, Uttar Pradesh & West Bengal) and one union territory (Puducherry) through 2,236 branches. The Company’s Promoter is CreditAccess India B.V., a multinational company focused on inclusive financing, 
supported by leading global inst itutional investors.
