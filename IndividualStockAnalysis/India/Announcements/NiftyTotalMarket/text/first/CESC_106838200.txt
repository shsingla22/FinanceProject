We write to inform you that Purvah Green Power Private Limited (“ PGPPL” or “Company ”), a subsidiary of CESC Limited, has

Companies ”) from ReNew Solar Power Private Limited(“ Seller”).
Post the completion of acquisition of shares, the Target Companies will become step-down subsidiaries of CESC Limited.
