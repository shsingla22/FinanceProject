At the International Spirits Challenge (ISC) 2024 , secured 3 coveted Gold Medals further

1. Gold | World Whisky: ICONiQ White Whisky 
2. Gold | World Whisky: Srishti Whisky 
3. Gold | World Whisky: X&O Barrel Whisky 
 
In addition to its success at ISC, ABDL triumphed at the International Whisky Competition 
(IWC) 2024 , one of the world’s most inﬂuential whisky competitions. The company 
dominated the Best Blended Indian Whisky  category, sweeping the top honour in a ﬁeld of 
intense global competition with Gold for X&O Barrel Whisky. 
 
The company again toasted and cheered at the prestigious India Wine & Spirits Awards 
(IWSA)—India’s largest and most credible blind-tasting competition for both domestic and 
international wines and spirits.  
 ZOYA Gin  was awarded the Best in Show  trophy for Best Indian Contemporary Gin.  
 ICONiQ White Whisky  won Gold for Best Indian Blended Whisky  
Consolidating its reputation, ABDL received the Gold Quality Award  for ICONiQ White 
Whisky at the Monde Selection World Quality Awards 2024 , a distinguished honour for 
quality and world-class production standards.
