either  8% Non-Cumulative Optionally Convertible Redeemable Preference Shares  (NCOCRPS)  or Optionally 
Convertible Debentures  (OCD) in one or more tranches.  
 
2. Borrowing by the wholly owned subsidiary. Borrowing of up to ₹2,400 crore by  WHSL from Banks / F inancial 
Institutions, to fund the capital expenditure described above.
