NGEL Signs MoU with PTC India Limited for Collaboration in Sale 
of Renewable Energy Power 
 
 
 
NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL), a subsidiary of NTPC Limited, signed a Memorandum of 
Understanding (MoU) with PTC India Limited on 31st March 2026. 
 
The MoU has been signed for Exploring the possibilities of power sale of Renewable Energy 
(RE) to PTC India Limited through bilateral arrangements and other market mechanisms. 
The MoU was signed by Sh. Sarit Maheshwari, CEO (NGEL) and Dr. Manoj Kumar Jhawar, CMD 
(PTC) India Limited in the presence of senior officials of NGEL and PTC India Limited.
