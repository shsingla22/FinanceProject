Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
This is in continuation to our earlier intimations dated June 24, 2025, July 1, 2025 and 
September 20, 2025, wherein we had informed that (i) the Board of Directors (“the Board”) 
of Zydus Wellness Limited (“the Company ”) and (ii) the Board and members of Naturell (India) 
Private Limited (“ NIPL”), an erstwhile wholly owned subsidiary, have accorded their approval 
for voluntary liquidation of NIPL. Further, the Liquidator of NIPL, had distributed the entire 
business undertaking of NIPL on a going concern basis to the Company on and with effect 
from September 20, 2025.  
 
In this respect, we would like to inform that the Liquidator had filed the petition for 
dissolution of NIPL on March 25, 2026, in accordance with Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 
2016 read with the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Voluntary Liquidation Process) 
Regulations, 2017, with the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Ahmedabad Bench 
(“NCLT”). The NCLT vide its order dated November 18, 2026 disposed of the petition requiring 
 
 
 
further clarification and submission of supporting documentary evidence on certain stated 
facts in the application.  
 
The NCLT has further clarified that the disposal of the petition is without prejudice to the NIPL 
/ Liquidator’s right to file a fresh application. Hence, the Liquidator will file a fresh application 
for dissolution along with submission of necessary clarification and supporting documentary 
evidence. 
 
The Order of NCLT was received on November 19, 2026 at 10:02 a.m. A copy of the said Order 
is available on the website of NCLT at https://nclt.gov.in/ . 
 
We request you to kindly take this on record.
