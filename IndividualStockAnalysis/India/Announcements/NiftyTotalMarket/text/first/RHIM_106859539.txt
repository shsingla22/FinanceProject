RHIM India’s Q2 FY27 PAT Surges 62% YoY, Reflecting  Strong 
Profitability Momentum  
 
Gurugram, Haryana, November 13, 2026:  RHI Magnesita India Limited, the leading 
manufacturer and supplier of high-grade refractory products, systems and solutions, has 
reported unaudited consolidated financial results f or the second quarter and half year ended 
September 30, 2026 (Q2 & H1 FY27). 
 
Commenting on the results, Pankaj Malhan – Managing  Director & Chief Executive Officer 
said, 
"Our strong business model with customer-centric ap proach & operation excellence with 
disciplined execution have enabled us to deliver pr ofitability despite rising costs. Inflationary 
environment, driven by external factors, have reinf orced the importance of delivering measurable 
value to our customers.  
  Looking ahead, the challenges remain due to volatil ity – but we are committed to build on this 
momentum and strategy which is yielding results, ad ding value for our shareholders and 
customers to drive sustainable growth.” 
Key Financial Highlights for Q2 FY27:  
 Revenue from operations at ₹ 1,030 crore (-0.5% YoY) 
 
 EBITDA at ₹144 crore (+30% YoY) 
 
 EBITDA % at 14% (+332 bps)

 Net Cash/EBITDA ratio at 0.25x 
 
 Shipment Volumes at 118 KT
