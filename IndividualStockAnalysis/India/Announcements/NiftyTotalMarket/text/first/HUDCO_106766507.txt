MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING 
This Memorandum of Understanding is entered on 2nd September, 2026 at Patna, Bihar 
Between 
The Government of Bihar 
And 
[Housing and Urban Development Corporation Limited] 
(hereinafter referred to as the "HUDCO Ltd.") 
1. The Government of Bihar is undertaking development of industrial parks projects across 
different district of Bihar for which it requires timely availability of land. 
2. Infrastructure Development Authority, Bihar (IDA) has been entrusted by the Government 
of Bihar with facilitating and/or implementing identified infrastructure development 
initiatives including development of lndustrial Park (hereinafter referred to as the Project) 
in the State and to fulfil responsibilities it may be required to undertake ącquisition, 
purchase, consolidation or development of land and development of enabling 
infrastructure for Industrial Park projects. 
3. This MoU is made to facilitate Housing and Urban Development Corporation Limited for 
planning, development and financing of the proposed Industrial Parks in Bihar ina time 
bound manner as per the rules and regulations of the State Government. 
4. Housing and Urban Development Corporation Limited shall provide term loans to the 
extent of Rs 25,000 (Twenty Five Thousand) Crore, over a period of S years, for 
development of industrial infrastructure including acquisition of land for the project. 
5. The loan shall be availed in tranches by the statutory authority specifically designated by 
the State Government. 
6. Housing and Urban Development Corporation Limited shall provide funds on flexible 
terms and conditions, including a moratorium period with a flexible repayment schedule 
up to 25 years, with the option of prepayment. 
7. The repayment of the loan shall be made from escrowing the revenue generated from the 
project or by ringfencing of other sources of revenues coming from identified receivables/ 
state budget so as to ensure timely payments to the Housing and Urban Development 
Corporation Limited. 
8. Separate operational agreements with specified scope of work and terms and conditions, 
shall be executed based on mutually agreed terms between the Parties, keeping in view 
of the requirements of the projects. 
9. This MoU is executed to put on record the intent of Parties to undertake the activities as 
stated. This MoU shall remain valid for 3 years from the date of execution subject to annual 
reviewN.
