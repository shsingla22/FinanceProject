TVS MOTOR COMPANY UNLEASHES RONIN MONOTONE AND 
AGONDA VARIANTS IN NEPAL —INTRODUCING A BOLD NEW 
EXPRESSION OF STYLE, INDIVIDUALITY, AND MODERN CRUISING  
Introduces TVS Ronin Monotone and TVS Ronin Agonda at NPR 4,29,900
and NPR 4 ,39,900 (ex -showroom, Nepal) respectively
Expands premium motorcycle lineup for young urban riders in Nepal
Kathmandu, Nepal, May 30, 2026 – TVS Motor Company, a leading global manufacturer of 
two and three -wheelers, today introduced new  TVS Ronin Monotone and TVS Ronin 
Agonda, further strengthening its premium motorcycle portfolio in Nepal. Unveiled at a 
musical event at Nova,  Kathmandu, the new variants  enhance the TVS Ronin lineup with bold 
styling, a distinctive modern- retro character and  strong appeal in Nepal’s  fast  growing 
premium motorcycle segment.  
The TVS Ronin Monotone will be available in Lightning Black and Magma Red and is priced 
at NPR 4,29,900 (ex-showroom, Nepal), while the TVS Ronin Agonda will be offered in Pearl 
White  at a price of NPR 4,39, 900 (ex-showroom, Nepal).   
Agonda Inspired by the sun, sands, and soul of Goa's iconic Agonda Beach , India. It's the 
first of many custom -inspired TVS Ronin motorcycles, showcasing just how versatile the 
modern -retro platform can be. A bright white base represents the sands of Agonda Beach, 
extending across the fuel tank and headlight cover to create a strong, clean road presence . 
The Agonda Edition stands out with an all -white headlamp cowl wrapped around the iconic 
all-LED T -face headlamp. The fuel tank features blue and red retro pinstripes, completing a 
thoughtfully crafted triple- tone combination topped with a custom black TVS stallion emblem. 
Sharp enough to turn heads in the city .  
Complementing the modern- retro styling  with advanced engineering, the TVS Ronin is 
powered by a refined 225.9cc engine  that deliver s 20.4 PS at 7,750 RPM and 19.93  Nm of 
torque at 3,750 RPM. Built  for versatile riding experiences , the motorcycle features Glide 
Through Technology (GTT) for smooth low-speed riding, an assist and slipper clutch for 
effortless gearshifts, and 41mm  upside- down  front forks for superior handling and riding 
comfort . TVS Ronin r epresent s the spirit of #Unscripted motorcycling, offering a distinctive 
blend of style, individuality & modern cruising. 
TVS Motor Company continues to strengthen its footprint in Nepal with a growing portfolio 
across  premium motorcycles,  scooters  and commuter motorcycles. Backed by a strong global 
presence, customer -focused innovation and a rapidly expanding premium product lineup, TVS 
Motor  Company  remains committed to delivering world- class mobility solutions and enhancing 
rider experiences across markets.
