Press Release  
Sterling Holiday Resorts expands presence with  3rd resort in Udaipur  
• 6th Resort in Rajasthan, 3rd resort in Udaipur  
• Ideally suited for Exclusive and Grand Destination Weddings  
• 40,000 sq.ft manicured lawns, multiple banquet spaces, swimming pool  
• 67-Keys including privilege  suites, 4-Bedroom haveli 
 
15th July 2024:  Sterling Holiday Resorts, India’s leading leisure hospitality brand announces the proud 
opening of Sterling Aravalli Udaipur, in Rajasthan. This marks the 3rd resort in the royal city of Udaipur 
and the 6th in the state of Rajasthan.  
The resort, set amidst 2.5 acres of landscaped outdoors with fantastic views of the Aravalli Hills has 
been purpose -built as an Iconic  Destination Wedding venue, in the renowned Destination Wedding 
capital – Udaipur . With 40,000 sq.ft of manicured lush -green lawns  and multiple banquet venues  that 
can accommodate up to 5000 plus guests  for multiple pre /post ceremonies , the resort is ideal for a 
lavish  Destination Wedding.  
The resort can also be booked in its entirety for an Intimate and Exclusive wedding, providing complete 
privacy and total flexibility to the wedding party.  
With live streaming, engaging fun -games and activities for the guests, curated theme decors , a choice 
of grand menus  and d edicated wedding managers to take care of all the details from planning to 
execution of the wedding, the guests can relax and enjoy the momentous occasion.  
Sterling Aravalli Udaipur, is inspired by t he unique haveli architecture that is synonym ous with the 
palatial Rajasthani design , with Jharokas  adorn ing the façade  and inner walls.  The 67 well -appointed 
large rooms and suites include Classic Rooms, Haveli Rooms, Premier Rooms with pool views, spacious Privilege Suites overlooking the Aravalli ranges, and a Four Be droom Haveli Suite that pay homage to 
the region's vintage architectural style.  
A fine dining vegetarian restaurant , ‘Airavat’ is the trendsetter in signature Rajasthani  cuisine along 
with  popular global favourites . The swimming pool in the centre courtya rd, and the s oon to be 
unveiled Signature Spa and Lounge Bar offer additional opportunities for relaxation and entertainment , further enhancing the experience for the guests.  
“We are delighted to  launch  Sterling Aravalli Udaipur. Udaipur is a pivotal leisure destination in the 
country . With 3 resorts in Udaipur –  we now have offerings for multiple segments including Leisure, 
Conferences and Destination Weddings.  We are glad to have strengthened our footprint , in the crown 
jewel of Indian tourism , with o ur 6
th resort in the state of Rajasthan .” said Mr. Vikram Lalvani, 
Managing Director & CEO , Sterling Holiday Resorts  Ltd.  
“It is a great moment for us to have Sterling Aravalli Udaipur join the bouquet of Sterling  resorts . Our 
resort is a landmark destination when it comes to its grandeur of design and its facilities. We are 
thrilled to be partnering with Sterling and look forward to developing the destination together. ” Said 
Mr. Raj Kumar Bapna, Owner, Sterling Aravalli Udaipur.  
Sterling's existing properties in Rajasthan also includes Sterling Jaisinghgarh Udaipur, Sterling Balicha Udaipur, Sterling Mount Abu, Sterling Rewild Sariska, and Sterling Pushkar.
