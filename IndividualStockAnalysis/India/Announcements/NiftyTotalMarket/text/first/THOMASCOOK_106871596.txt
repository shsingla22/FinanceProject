Mumbai, November 26, 2026: Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India's leading omnichannel travel services company, 
has unveiled its Europe Summer  2027 Holidays portfolio, introducing its exciting ‘Holiday Pe Holiday Free’  
proposition for the first 1000 customers. The limited -period launch offers give travellers an opportunity to get more 
out of their European holiday, with complimentary extensions of up to four additional nights across select European 
destinations, complimentary 3 or 4- night  cruises on select tours, or early -bird discounts of up to ₹35,000 per person. 
Designed to encourage early holiday planning and timely visa processing , the offers add significant value to the 
company's extensive range of European holidays for Summer 2027.  
 
With Europe continuing to be a preferred destination for Indian travellers during the summer holiday season, Thomas Cook draws on its decades of destination expertise to help travellers experience the best of Europe.  The 
portfolio features over 60 unique group escorted tours with guaranteed regional departures, alongside customised holidays co -curated with customers to suit their individual preferences.  From first -time travellers  looking to explore 
iconic landmarks and popular European circuits to repeat travellers seeking new destinations and more immersive 
experiences, there is something for every kind of traveller. With premium, value and pocket -friendly options catering 
to diverse traveller segments and budgets, the range combines choice, flexibility and distinctive experiences.  
 Complementing its destination expertise and extensive holiday portfolio is Thomas Cook's TC C.A.R.E. proposition, 
which brings together six key elements designed to enhance the overall holiday experience:  
 
• (T) TravelSure:  Free rescheduling and cancellation in emergencies, along with visa rejection cover, 
offering travellers added flexibility and travel assurance.  
• (C) Carefully curated itineraries:  Itineraries thoughtfully designed, tested and refined through decades 
of experience operating in Europe.  
• (C) Care of the most experienced Tour Managers:  The guidance and support of experienced Tour 
Managers accompanying travellers throughout their holiday.  
• (A) Authentic cuisine:  A selection of local and Indian meals, offering travellers a taste of the 
destination alongside familiar food options.  
• (R) Rated highly by over 50,000 customers:  Reflecting the trust and confidence Thomas Cook has 
built through its customer experiences.  
• (E) Every hotel pre- checked and certified:  Accommodation evaluated for quality and comfort.  
 
The portfolio is strategically organi zed into five distinctive European regions, encouraging travellers to discover 
different facets of the continent through its culture, cuisine, history and landscapes:  
• Northern Europe (Nordic & Baltic): Norway, Sweden, Denmar k, Finland, Iceland, Estonia  
• Western Europe:  UK, Ireland, France, Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg, Germany, Austria, Italy and 
Switzerland 
• Central Europe:  Germany, Poland, Czech Republic, Slovakia, Austria, Hungary and Slovenia  
• Southern Europe (Mediterranean):  Spain, Portugal, Greece  
• Southeastern Europe (The  Balkans):  Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, Serbia, and Romania
