Quarterly Update: Q2 FY27  
This update provides an overall summary of the operating performance for the quarter ended 
September 30, 2026 (Q2 FY27). A detailed Information Update will follow upon Board approval of the 
Q2 FY27 financial results.  
Q2 FY27 is expected to be another robust quarter for Honasa Consumer Limited  (along with its 
subsidiaries), building on the momentum established in Q1 FY27. We expect NSV1 YoY growth2 in 
early -thirties  during the quarter, underpinned by broad -based traction in our focus categories, across 
brands.  
Our largest brand, Mamaearth , has sustained its growth momentum over the last few quarters and is 
expected to deliver high -teens YoY growth2 (NSV1) during the quarter, supported by rising brand 
affinity and a widening offline footprint. Our younger brands3 continue to scale on strong traction and 
are expected to accelerate their YoY growth2 (NSV1) to around mid-forties . 
Offline  continues to lead our growth, with General Trade and Modern Trade  both expected to post 
strong growth , backed by deeper direct distribution in General Trade and sharper execution at the 
point of sale. Online is expected to see continued growth momentum during the quarter.  
On profitability , we continue to deliver on our goal of EBITDA margin improvement , and we expect 
the business to deliver early double -digit operating margin profile  in Q2 FY27  as wel l with strong YoY 
gains  in the quarter . 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1. “NSV ” refers to net sales value after returns, taxes, discounts & cancellation . 
2.Growth is presented on a like -for-like basis, as base period revenue (Q2 FY26) is also adjusted for the change in settlement 
by the Flipkart group, which impacts revenue recognition for marketplace sellers like Honasa. Reported and like -for-like 
growth are  therefore aligned . 
3.Younger brands include The Derma Co., Aqualogica, BBlunt, Dr. Sheth’s, Staze, Lumineve and Reginald Men .
