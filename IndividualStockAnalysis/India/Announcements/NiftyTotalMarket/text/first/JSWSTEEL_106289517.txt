4. Review BRR disclosures on pre-decided frequency.
5. Review progress of Business Responsibility initiatives.
6.  Review annual Business Responsibility Report and present it to the Board for Approval. Composition of this committee consists of both Non-Executive Independent Directors and Executive Directors and chaired by an Independent Director.
