Hyderabad,  February  12, 2026 : Mahindra  Last Mile  Mobility  Limited  (MLMML),  India’s 
No.1*  electric  commercial  vehicle  manufacturer,  defined  a new era of last mile mobility  today,  
unveiling  its most  advanced  electric  three -wheeler  yet, the Mahindra  UDO . The Mahindra  UDO 
draws  inspiration  from  an airplane  and envisages  the auto as a “autoplane” . It is a bold step 
forward  in design,  performance  and comfort  for last-mile mobility.  The word  ‘UDO’ in Hindi  
means  ‘to fly’. The Mahindra  UDO  will give wings  to customer’s  aspirations  and help them  soar 
to greater  heights  in life. It is priced  attractively  at ₹ 3 84 299.00, ex-showroom,  but for a limited  
period,  the UDO  will be offered  for ₹ 3 58 999.00 . 
Redefining  comfort  and design  
The traditional  auto design,  unchanged  globally  since  1948,  has long neglected  driver  and 
passenger  comfort.  India  is the largest  3W EV market  in the world,  and it is natural  that the 
country’s  e-auto pioneer  steps  in and lead the change.  The revolutionary  design  of our new 
autorickshaw  is poised  to elevate  the dignity  of auto drivers,  transforming  their professional  
image  and ownership  experience.  Mahindra’s  Chief  Design  and Creative  Officer  - Auto  and 
Farm  Sector s, M&M  Ltd.,  Pratap  Bose , and his team  at Mahindra  India  Design  Studio , have 
reimagined  the auto. The sleek  design  with bold headlamps,  stylish  mirrors,  and a large  
windshield  of the UDO  creates  a superior  road presence.

Inside,  the “autoplane” features  ergonomically  designed  seats  with driver  and passenger  comfort  
in mind . This includes  a pilot seat for the driver  which  is nearly  20% thicker  than other  vehicles  
in the category  and first-class  seating  for passengers . There  is generous  headroom  & legroom  
fora lounge -like comfort  even  on longer  journeys.  An independent  rear suspension  and dual fork 
front  suspension  further  enhances  stability  as well as ride comfort  on rough  roads.   
With  a first-in-segment  full monocoque  construction,  Mahindra  UDO  boasts  higher  structural  
strength  and best-in-class  ride quality.   
Powerful  performance  
Complementing  the futuristic  design  and unparalleled  comfort  is the blazing  performance.  The 
Mahindra  UDO  delivers  a best-in-class  real-world  range  of 200 km (265 km ARAI -certified  
range).  The UDO  is the most  energy -efficient  vehicle  in its segment.  This is achieved  with an 
IP67 -rated,  11.7 kWh  laser -welded  Li-ion battery  pack . The PMS  motor  helps  deliver  10 kW 
of peak  power  and 52 Nm of torque.  Mahindra  UDO  also has regenerative  braking  which  
combined  with a first-in-segment  reverse  throttle  offers  seamless  speed  control  without  
touching  the brakes . The reverse  throttle  not only gives  Mahindra  UDO  an extended  range  but 
also a smoother  ride. Hill Hold  Assist  and Creep  functions  in the Mahindra  UDO  ensure  
stability  on slopes  and ease of driving  in bumper -to-bumper  traffic.   
The tri-mode  driving  system  of Range,  Ride,  and Race  gives  drivers  the flexibility  to switch  
between  efficiency  and performance  depending  on traffic  conditions.  The top speed  of 55 km/h 
(in Race  mode)  will help drivers  complete  their trips faster  and generate  more  income.   
Exemplary  safety  
The Mahindra  UDO  is backed  by a confidence -inspiring  warranty  of 1.5 Lakh km or 6 year,  
whichever  is earlier.  An IP67 -rated  battery,  drivetrain,  charger  ensures  that the UDO  owner  
can continu e his journey  even  in extreme  weather  conditions .   
 The vehicle  has powerful  drum  brakes  enabling  it the least^  braking  distance  in the segment.   
The UDO  also comes  with 30.48  cm tubeless  tyres  that enhance  the safety .  
Mahindra ’s confidence  in the UDO  reflects  with its industry -first free services  until  1 Lakh 
km. When  the customer  purchases  UDO,  they are enrolled  into Mahindra’s  UDAY  NXT  scheme  
that provides  ₹ 20 Lakh accidental  insurance  to the vehicle  owner,  financial  counselling  and 
more.   
With  its head -turning  design,  segment -first features  and Mahindra’s  reliability,  the UDO  is all set 
to expand  electrification  in the L5M  auto category.  Mahindra  UDO  is built in Mahindra’s  state-
of-the-art manufacturing  plant  in Zaheerabad.  Mahindra  has significantly  inves ted in this plant
