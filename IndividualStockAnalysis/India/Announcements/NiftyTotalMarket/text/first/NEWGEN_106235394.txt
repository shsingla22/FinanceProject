PRESS RELEASE  
Newgen Granted Patent in India for System and Method for  
Data Compression  
Filed 55 patents, out of which 25 have been granted in India and the US . The new  patent will boost 
Newgen’s intellectual property portfolio . 
May  16, 2025: Newgen Software, a leading provider of an AI -first unified digital transformation 
platform , has announced that the Indian patent office has granted a patent ( No. 565385 ) for inventi ng 
the ‘System and Method for Data Compression .' This patent has been filed for a term of 20 years 
commencing from April  26, 2017, in accordance with the provisions of the Patents Act, 1970.  
The invention addresses efficient data compression for large volumes of data files where the majority 
of content is similar, with a smaller amount of varying data, especially in scenarios where traditional 
compression techniques may not be optimal. Since the invention is about a compression technique , it 
aims to  reduce storage space  and i mprove speed and efficiency of data handling , however,  in a much 
better way than conventional compression techniques.  
This is especially relevant in industries handling high volumes of structured documents,  forms, or 
system -generated reports , enabling them to save storage costs  and have faster data handling.  
Newgen's patented system  intelligently identifies homogeneous data files  with similar structures and 
further segments them into static and dynamic regions. The system treats these files as sequential 
frames by leveraging advanced video compression techniques  for highly efficient compression. This 
novel approach results in a single compressed video file, accompanied by metadata for precise storage 
and retrieval.  
The patents acquired by Newgen further strengthen its leadership in building cutting -edge, industry -
specific applications, which are instrumental in driving digital transformation across organizations 
worldwide.
