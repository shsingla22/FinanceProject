RELIANCE INDUSTRIES LIMITED SIGNS A JOINT OPERATING AGREEMENT 
WITH OIL AND NATURAL GAS CORPORATION LIMITED AND  
BP EXPLORATION (ALPHA) LIMITED FOR BLOCK GS -OSH P-2022/2  
 
 
Mumbai, 29th July 2025:  Reliance Industries Limited (“RIL”) has entered into a Joint 
Operating Agreement with Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (“ONGC”) and BP 
Exploration (Alpha) Limited (“BP”) for the exploration Block GS -OSHP -2022/2 (“Block”). 
This Block in located off t he western coast in Saurashtra basin and was awarded to RIL, 
ONGC and BP as part of Hydrocarbon Exploration and Licensing Policy. Parties will pursue 
exploration operation in the Block pursuant to the terms of award of the Block.
