CORRIGENDUM TO THE NOTICE OF THE 31ST ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING  
JSW STEEL LIMITED  
This corrigendum is issued  to amend the Notice of the 31st Annual General Meeting 
(“AGM”) (“AGM Notice ”) of JSW Steel Limited (“ Company ”) circulated to the 
shareholders on July  3, 2025, the AGM being scheduled to be held on Friday, 25 July 2025 
at 11:00 a.m. IST through video conferenc ing / other audio -visual means . 
In relation to Agenda Item No. 15 of the AGM Notice, which pertains to approval for 
undertaking material related party transaction(s) with Bhushan Power & Steel Limited  
(“BPSL ”), certain figures require correction and an additional line item is to be added 
within the overall amount mentioned in the resolution which was inadvertently missed , for 
completeness  in the resolution.   
1. Updation of the resolution (Page  Nos 695 to 696 of the Integrated Report ): 
In the resolution set out under Agenda Item No. 15, as contained in the AGM Notice dated 
July 3, 2025, after the reference to “(c) sale of coal and coke products,” the following 
additional line is hereby inserted:  
“(d) Purchase of coal and coke products.”  
2. Clarification in the explanatory statement (Page  Nos 736 to  740 of the 
Integrated Report ): 
Paragraph  8 in the Explanatory Statement  in relation to Agenda Item No. 15 of the AGM 
Notice  is to be replaced with the below :  
“While t he Company’s Raigarh unit does not have a coke oven plant , it requires coke for 
its steel making operations which is supplied by BPSL on a conversion (job work for 
conversion of coking coal into coke) basis. BPSL imports coal to meet the coke conversion 
requirement s of the Raigarh unit and sells this coal to the Company’s Raigarh unit . The 
Raigarh unit  in turn  provides the same coal to BPSL for conversion into coke. This 
arrangement optimises logistics costs for both BPSL and the Company’s Raigarh unit.  The 
conversion arrangement enables optimum utilisation of coke oven plants and reduce s the 
cost of coke production. Since the Raigarh unit is located close to  BPSL, which has its 
plant in the neighbouring state of Odisha, logistics costs are comparatively lower than 
those of  other suppliers . Accordingly,  both BPSL and the Company benefit from  logistics 
cost advantages. ”
