Thomas  Cook India  expands its global footprint  
Enters th e European Union with its office in Cyprus  
Strategic presence to leverage on the strong growth potential of the Business Travel Segment  
Mumbai,  May 16, 2025 : India’s corporates and multi -national companies are expanding rapidly, and Europe has 
been emerging strongly on their radar. To capitalize on the large EU market potential, Thomas Cook (India) 
Limited, India’s leading omnichannel travel serv ices company has established its presence in the European 
Union (EU) by setting up its first office in Cyprus (Nicosia ). The Company -  Travel Circle International 
(Cyprus) Limited (TCI Euro ) is a subsidiary of Thomas Cook (India) Limited  through its wholly owned 
subsidiary - Travel Circle International (Mauritius)  Ltd. The company  will focus on the business travel needs  
of both its multinational  client s and India -based clients  with a presence in Europe.  
 
TCI Euro,  has been strategically setup to benefit business travellers based in Europe, by offering special local 
pricing  and inventory  for air and non- air services  - that are not available in India.  Setting up its first EU office 
offers  multi- pronged,  long- term opportunity to Thomas Cook India, opening up the Europe market for a diversity 
of its travel segments  – starting with  business travel lers. 
 
The Cyprus office was inaugurated by Indiver Rastogi, President & Group Head, Global Business Travel, 
Thomas Cook (India) Ltd  and Sanjay Tugnait, President & CEO -  Fairfax Digital Services (A Fairfax 
Company) in the presence of esteemed government  officials : Kostas Koumis, Deputy Minister of Tourism of 
Republic of Cyprus , Manish Manish, High Commissioner of India to Cyprus  and business partners  
Karavias Fokion, Group CEO of Eurobank Greece  & Srini Rao, Executive VP & Chief Business Officer - 
LTIMindtree Ltd  
 Thomas Cook India’s EU foray  – Business Rationale  
• EU Marke t Access: inroads into the EU’s extensive travel  opportunity , facilitating seamless operations 
across member states  
• Pricing  Advantage: Offers access to competitive EU local pricing and inventory  
• Improved  Customer Experience: Local presence in Europe to enhance  overall customer service  
• Expansion of segments: Commencing with business travel, opportunity to leverage multiple travel 
sectors  
 
Mahesh Iyer – Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer, Thomas Cook (India) Ltd  said, “The 
inauguration of our office in Cyprus heralds our foray into the high- potential European market. Our  intent is to 
target the rapidly expanding business travel opportunity , across our business travel clients operating with a 
presence in Europe.  The advantages to our business travellers include special local EU-pricing and inventory, 
and a seamless customer experience. ”
