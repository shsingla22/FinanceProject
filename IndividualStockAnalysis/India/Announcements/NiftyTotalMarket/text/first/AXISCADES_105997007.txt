AXISCADES Honoured with Bombardier Diamond Supplier Award  
for Fourth Consecutive Year  
 
Bengaluru, India, September 24, 2024: AXISCADES Technologies Limited,  a leading end to end 
engineering and technology solutions provider, proudly announce s that it has once again been 
honored with Bombardier ’s prestigious Diamond Supplier  recognition  for the fourth consecutive 
year . This accolade underscores  AXISCADES' unwavering commitment as a key partner in 
delivering world -class engineering solutions that align with  Bombardier’s strategic objectives  
and high standards of quality. The award was presented at a ceremony held on  September  16, 
2024  in Montreal, Canada.  
 
The Bombardier Diamond Supplier Program recognizes suppliers who consistently demonstrate  
excellence in operational performance and a commitment to continuous improvement. This 
repeated recognition highlights AXISCADES' enduring partnership with Bombardier and its ability 
to meet the industry's rigorous standards, even amidst global challenges.  
 
Commenting on this development, Mr. Arun Krishnamurthi,  Chief Executive Officer  and 
Managing Director of AXISCADES Technologies Limited, stated : “Receiving the Bombardier 
Diamond Supplier Award for the fourth consecutive year is a tremendous honor. It is an ode to 
the relentless efforts and passion of our teams who constantly strive for excellence and exceed 
Bombardier’s expectations. Our collaboration  with Bombardier is built on a shared commitment 
to quality and innovation , and we take great pride in contributing to the success of their industry -
leading business jets. This award reinforces our resolve to maintain the highest standards of 
operational performance while adapting to the evolving demands of the aerospace sector. We 
are proud to play a part in shaping the future of aerospace together alongside  Bombardier.”  
 
AXISCADES’ collaboration  with Bombardier spans over 12 years covering niche  areas of 
aerospace engineering. The continued recognition  from  Bombardier reaffirms AXISCADES' 
position as a trusted partner  in the aerospace sector , committed to continuous enhancement of 
its processes and solutions  for delivering critical engineering services across the entire product 
lifecycle.   
 
  
 
About AXISCADES Technologies Ltd.  
 
AXISCADES is a leading, end -to-end technology and engineering solutions provider aiding the 
creation of innovative, sustainable, and safer products worldwide. The company is headquartered in 
Bengaluru with subsidiaries in the USA, UK, Canada, Germany, Indi a, and China; and offices in 
Germany, France, Denmark, USA, and Canada. AXISCADES has a diverse team of over 3,200 
professionals working across 17 locations across North America, Europe, the UK, and Asia -Pacific, 
striving to reduce the program risk and tim e to market. The company offers Product Engineering 
Solutions across Embedded Software and Hardware, Digitisation and Automation, Mechanical 
Engineering, System Integration  & Installation, Test Solutions, Manufacturing Engineering, Technical 
Publications, and Aftermarket Solutions. The solutions comprehensive portfolio covers the complete 
product development lifecycle from concept evaluation to manufacturing support and certification 
for For tune 500 Companies in the Aerospace, Defense, Heavy Engineering, Automotive, Energy & 
Industrial Product industries.
