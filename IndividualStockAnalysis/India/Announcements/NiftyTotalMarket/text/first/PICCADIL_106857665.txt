PRESS RELEASE  
 
PICCADILY AGRO DISTILLERY REVENUE GROWS 30.5% YOY TO INR 271.3 CR IN 
Q2 FY27 AS PREMIUM SPIRITS SUSTAIN GROWTH MOMENTUM  
 
12th November 2026, New Delhi - Piccadily Agro Industries Limited (PAIL), one of India’s leading 
producers of premium alcoholic beverages, today announced its financial results for the quarter 
ended September 30, 2026. 
 
The Company reported Revenue from Operations of INR 277.9 crore in Q2 FY27, up 19.4% YoY  
from INR 232.7 crore in the corresponding quarter last year, driven by continued momentum 
in its Distillery business. 
 
Distillery revenue increased 30.5% YoY to INR 271.3 crore  and accounted for 97.6% of the 
Company’s quarterly revenue, compared with 89.3% in Q2 FY26, further strengthening its 
position as the principal growth engine of the Company. 
 
The Company continues to advance its strategic focus on strengthening its premium, super-
premium and luxury spirits portfolio, expanding market presence and distribution, enhancing 
brand equity and leveraging its expanded manufacturing capabilities to drive sustainable long-
term growth.

Commenting on the results, Mr. Natwar Agarwal, Chief Financial Officer, Piccadily Agro 
Industries Limited , said, “Q2 FY27 saw continued momentum in our core Distillery business, 
which grew over 30% YoY, while overall Company revenue increased by 19.4%. Our H1 FY27 
PAT is higher than the corresponding period last year, reflecting the underlying strength of our 
business and continued progress towards our financial objectives. The Distillery business 
remains our principal growth engine, and its increasing contribution reflects the progress we 
are making in strengthening Piccadily’s spirits-led business. 
 
Our Branded Alcobev portfolio continues to expand, supported by our focus on the premium, 
super-premium and luxury segments. The scaling up of the Chhattisgarh Distillery, together with 
our enhanced manufacturing capabilities, positions us well to support our long-term growth 
ambitions. 
 
The Company remains firmly on track to achieve its business objectives, with a continued focus 
on building differentiated brands, expanding its distribution footprint, strengthening 
manufacturing capabilities and deepening its presence across domestic and international 
markets.”

Premium Spirits Sustain Growth Momentum 
 
Piccadily continues to strengthen its presence across the premium alcoholic beverages segment 
through its portfolio of Indri Single Malt, Camikara Aged Rum, Cashmir Vodka and Whistler 
Whisky. 
 
The Company is gearing up for multiple new product launches, further strengthening its 
portfolio, accelerating premiumization and unlocking new growth opportunities across 
markets. 
 
The Company remains focused on strengthening its existing brands, launching new offerings to 
expand its portfolio across attractive spirits categories and widening its presence across 
domestic and international markets. 
 
Piccadily’s enhanced distillation capabilities at the Indri Distillery and the Chhattisgarh Distillery 
will provide additional capacity to support the growth of its branded spirits portfolio. 
 
The Company will continue to leverage its integrated manufacturing capabilities and growing 
business portfolio to pursue long-term value creation for its stakeholders. The Company firmly 
believes that the quality and maturation of brown spirits should be achieved naturally, without 
the addition of colour or flavours. In line with this approach, the Company has revised its spirit 
maturation target to over 1,00,000 barrels by the end of the current fiscal year.

About Piccadily Agro Industries Limited (PAIL)

segments — Distillery and Sugar. The company’s manufacturing facility at Indri, Haryana and 
Chhattisgarh is equipped with advanced technology for producing Malt Spirit, ENA, Ethanol and 
White Crystal Sugar.
