Claro CENAM selects Proximus Global to 
support A2P across LATAM 
The telco giants are partnering to deliver more secure and profitable 
business messaging services —  driving growth while protecting end-users 
from fraud. 
Brussels – 9 September 2025  – Claro CENAM, one of the largest telecommunications brands operating 
across Latin America and a subsidiary of América Móvil, has signed a major deal with Proximus Global, a  
leading digital communications company,  to support the monetization and security of its application-to-
person (A2P) messaging services across the region. T he agreement will use Prox imus Global’s 365squared 
technology. 
The A2P messaging market in Latin America is expected to reach  a projected revenue of 5 billion USD by 
2030. This rapid growth also makes it an attractive target for bad actors attempting to exploit its scale and 
legitimacy. Fraudsters attempt to mimic A2P traffic to  deliver spam, launch smishing campaigns, or generate 
scams and trigger Artificially Inflated  Traffic (AIT). That’s why mobile network operators must stay vigilant 
and secure their networks to protect the trust and effectiveness of A2P as a reliable messaging channel.   
 
By partnering with Proximus Global – as the gateway for these messages, combining the global scale and breadth of services of BICS, Telesign, and Route Mobile – Claro CENAM is building a safer and more equitable A2P ecosystem. This approach protects oper ators, brands and end users, while ensuring that 
businesses using legitimate SMS routes are no l onger disadvantaged by unauthorized practices. This 
partnership safeguards customer experience, pres erves network value, and ensures messages are 
delivered reliably, securely, and with the quality customers expect. 
 
Ben Vandermeulen, Chief Revenue Officer, Proximus Global adds: "We are thrilled to support Claro 
CENAM, not only as a leading force in LATAM, but as a champion of safeguarding customers and the 
communications ecosystem as a whole. Claro CENAM se ts the standard by prioritizing the protection of end 
users and the network—making it a core mission, not  an afterthought. This aligns perfectly with what 
Proximus Global brings to the table: a powerful combi nation of global scale, competitive services, and built-
in value-adds like world-class security and revenue protection." 
Tonio Ellul, CEO, 365squared added: "Protecting operator revenue is at the core of what we do. With Claro 
CENAM, we're proud to bring our advanced A2P firewa ll and analytics technology to the table – ensuring 
that every message is legitimate, every route secure, and every opportunity to monetize legitimate traffic is 
taken.”  prox1mus
