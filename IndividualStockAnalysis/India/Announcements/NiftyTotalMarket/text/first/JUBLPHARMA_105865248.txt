Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 read with Schedule II I to the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to intimate you that Jubilant Biosys 
Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of Jubilant Pharmova Limited (the ‘Company’), has 
approved th e proposal for investment including  entering into a Power Supply a nd Offtake 
Agreement, and  Share  Subscription and Shareholders Agreement  on May  28, 2024 with 
Isharays Energy Two Private Limited   (“Isharays Energy”) , a wholly -owned subsidiary of 
SunSource Energy Private Limited, for acquisition of upto 14.75 % stake of Isharays Energy for 
purchase of renewable energy (electricity) generated from the Captive Generating Plant (CGP).  
 
The company  has embarked on a renewable e nergy implementation journey across its fa cilities 
in India to enable reduction in both, cost and  carbon footprint . This investment shall enable the 
company to access renewable energy through captive power arrangement for its facilities 
located in Noida and Greater Noida.  
 
In January this year, the company had approved a similar investment to access renewable 
power through captive arrangement for its facilities located in Karnataka, India.
