About HDBFS:  
 
HDB Fi nancial Services Limited (HDBFS ) is a non -deposit taking non -banking finance company (‘NBFC’) 
offering wide range of loan products to individuals, emerging businesses and micro enterprises . 
Established in 2007, as a subsidiary of HDFC Bank Limited, HDBFS is categorized as an upper  layer NBFC 
by the RBI. HDBFS offers a large portfolio of lending products that cater to a growing and diverse 
customer base through a wide omni -channel distribution network. Its lending products are offered 
through the three business verticals: Enterprise  Lending, Asset Finance and Consumer Finance. As of 
December 31 , 2025, the Company’s distribution network spans 1,744 branches acro ss 1,165 cities/ 
towns.
