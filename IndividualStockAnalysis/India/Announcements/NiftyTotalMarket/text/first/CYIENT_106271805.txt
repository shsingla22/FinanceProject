Cyient Foundation and ICT Academy Launch 
Entrepreneurship Developm ent Program for Students 
in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh 
Hyderabad, 18 June 2025: Cyient Foundation, the CSR arm of Cyient Ltd., has partnered 
with ICT Academy to launch a comprehensiv e entrepreneurship development program aimed 
at empowering 2,000 students ac ross engineering, arts, and science institutions in Telangana 
and Andhra Pradesh. The initiative is designed to equip young minds from tier-2 and tier-3 
cities with entrepreneurial skills and indust ry exposure through a structured three-stage 
program spanning awareness, intensive training, and mentorship. 
  
Over the course of 12 months, the program ai ms to build entrepreneurial capacity through 
awareness sessions for 2,000 students, followed by hands-on entrepreneurship training for 
1,000 selected participants. The top 100 students will then receive two months of 
personalized mentoring and business facilitation, including guidance on fundraising, 
compliance, leadership, and go-to-market strategies.   
Commenting on the initiative,  Krishna Deevi, Head of Corporate Social Responsibility, 
Cyient, said, “At Cyient Foundation, we belie ve entrepreneurship is the cornerstone of a 
resilient and self-reliant India. Through this pa rtnership with ICT Academy, we aim to nurture 
a generation of confident, capa ble, and socially conscious entr epreneurs from the grassroots. 
This initiative reflects our commitment to inclusive growth by bringing industry-aligned 
opportunities to young people  in underserved regions.” 
  
Speaking on the partnership, Suresh Babu L, Associate Vice President at ICT Academy , 
said, “This collaboration with Cyient Foundation is aligned with our vision of bridging the skill divide and fostering innovati on at the grassroots level. We are proud to deliver a holistic 
entrepreneurial journey—from inspiration to incu bation—for students who will go on to shape 
the future of India’s economy.” 
  
The program will also see active engagement from  Cyient associates, who will contribute as 
facilitators and mentors. The entrepreneurshi p development program aims to support the 
launch of at least 100 student-led enterprises, thereby contributing to job creation and long-
term economic growth in the region.  About Cyient
