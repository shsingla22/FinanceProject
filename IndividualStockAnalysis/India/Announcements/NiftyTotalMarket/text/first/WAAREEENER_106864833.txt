Waaree Energies Named Kiwa PVEL Top Performer in All Eight 
Test Categories of 2026 Scorecard Update  
One of seven manufacturers globally to earn Top Performer status in every category; 
recognition marks Waaree's fifth consecutive year on the Scorecard  
Mumbai, November 18, 2026:  Waaree Energies Limited, India's largest integrated clean 
energy transition company, has been named a Top Performer in all eight test categories of 
Kiwa PVEL's PV Module Reliability Scorecard. The listing is part of the November 2026 
update, the Scorecard 's second edition this year after its May 2026 release. Waaree is one 
of the seven manufacturers worldwide with at least one model type listed as a Top Performer 
in every category tested.  
This is the first year Kiwa PVEL, the independent lab for solar module reliability and 
performance testing, has released its Scorecard twice. The November update names 48 
manufacturers and 311 model types as Top Performers. Waaree's result comes in a year of 
record test failures: 87% of manufacturers and 61% of bills of materials (BOMs) failed at 
least one test.  
Waaree's listing covers 18 module series across its BiN and WMN product families. Kiwa 
PVEL's Product Qualification Program (PQP) subjects  factory -witnessed module samples to 
accelerate  stress tests that replicate decades of field exposure: Thermal Cycling, Damp 
Heat, Mechanical Stress Sequence, Hail Stress Sequence, Potential Induced Degradation, 
Ultraviolet Induced Degradation, Light and Elevated Temperature Induced Degradation, and 
PAN Performance.  
The November result builds on the May 2026 edition, in which Waaree's M10R and G12R 
glass -to-glass N -type TOPCon  modules were named Top Performers, marking the 
company's fifth consecutive year of recognition.  
Speaking on the recognition, Jignesh Rathod , Whole -Time Director & CEO , Waaree 
Energies Limited , said , “Achieving Top Performer status across all eight categories of Kiwa 
PVEL’s testing programme is a significant milestone, particularly in one of the most 
demanding testing years the industry has witnessed. This achievement reflects the 
engineering rigour , quality and reliability embedded in our modules. As solar assets are 
designed to deliver reliable performance over a 30 -year lifecycle, independent validation of 
this nature reinforces our customers’ confidence in our technology and our commitment to 
long-term performance.”  
Waaree supplies modules to customers across India and more than 25 countries, backed 
by manufacturing in India and the United States.
