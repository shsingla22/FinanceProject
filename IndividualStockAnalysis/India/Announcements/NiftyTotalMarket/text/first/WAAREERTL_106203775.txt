ANNEXURE B 
 
Brief Profile of Mr. Sudhir Arya 
 
Sudhir Arya is a fellow member of The Institute of Cost Accountants 
of India, Institute of Chartered Financial Analysts of India and has a 
management degree with major in Finance from Mangement 
Development Institute, Gurugram.  
He worked with NTPC Ltd, India’s premier power utility from 1981 to 
2019.  He was working as the Chief Financial Officer of the Company when he left it to 
join as Director (Finance) of Uttar Pradesh Power Corporation Ltd. He brings with him a 
rich and diverse experience of more than 40 years in various facets of Finance, dealing 
with domestic and overseas investors and lenders, project finance, contracting for 
projects, fuel, power sales & purchase, financial reporting and audit etc.   
He has in the past served as a director on the Boards of many joint ventures and 
subsidiaries of NTPC Ltd and various subsidiaries and associate companies of UPPCL. 
Presently he is an independent director on the Board of IIFL Asset Management Company 
Ltd and Ajay Poly Ltd.
