About Piramal Pharma  Limited : 
Piramal Pharma Limited (PPL, NSE: PPLPHARMA I BSE: 543635), offers a portfolio of differentiated products and services throug h 
its 17* global development and manufacturing facilities and a global distribution network in over 100 countries. PPL includes 
Piramal Pharma Solutions (PPS), an integrated contract development and manufacturing organization; Piramal Critical Care (PCC),
