 Capillary Technologies Secures Five -Year, Strategic 
Deal with One of the Largest Retailers in the United 
States and a Fortune 50 Company to implement 
Loyalty+ and aiRA  
 
Bengaluru, Karnataka - Mar 4, 2026  
Capillary Technologies, a global leader in AI -powere d loyalty and customer engagement solutions, today 
announced that one of the largest retailers in the United States and a Fortune 50 company has selected 
Capillary as its strategic partner to modernize its loyalty and customer engagement ecosystem under a 
five-year agreement with a total contract value of more than $20mn.  
As part of this engagement, Capillary will support the retailer in advancing its customer engagement and 
loyalty capabilities through intelligent, AI -driven decisioning. The partnership wi ll focus on:  
● Enhanced Personalization & Engagement  – Delivering relevant and contextual customer 
experiences  
 
● AI-Driven Offer Optimization (aiRA)  – Improving promotional effectiveness and efficiency 
through intelligent decisioning and targeting  
 
● Real -Time Customer Intelligence  – Enabling dynamic and behavior -driven interactions across 
channels  
 
Through Capillary’s AI -powered platform, the retailer will be able to:  
● Deliver personalized and relevant customer experiences  
 
● Enhance member engagement and lifecycl e value  
 
● Improve promotional effectiveness and efficiency using aiRA’s AI -driven decisioning  
 
● Enable real -time, data -driven marketing and loyalty strategies
