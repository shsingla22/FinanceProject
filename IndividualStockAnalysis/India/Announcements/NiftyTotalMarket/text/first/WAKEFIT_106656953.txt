In furtherance to our letter dated May 7, 2026 and pursuant to Regulation 44 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we are pleased to inform you that the Members of the Company 
through Postal Ballot (remote e-voting only) have passed the “Approval for alteration of Memorandum of 
Association (MOA) of the Company by amendment to existing object clauses and by inclusion of new object clauses” by special resolution. The approval is deemed to have been received on the last date of remote e-voting 
i.e., June 6, 2026. 
 Pursuant to the provisions of Section 110 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with the rules made thereunder, the 
Company conducted the postal ballot by remote e-voting process as set out in the Notice. Mr. Pramod S.M. and 
in his absence Mr. Biswajit Ghosh, Partners of M/s. BMP & Co. LLP, Practicing Company Secretaries were appointed as the Scrutinizer for this purpose. The remote e-voting period commenced at 9:00 a.m. (IST) on 
Friday, May 8, 2026, and concluded at 5:00 p.m. (IST) on Saturday, June 6, 2026 .  
 In accordance with Regulation 44(3) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“ SEBI Listing Regulations ”), please find enclosed the details of voting results and the report 
of the Scrutinizer thereon.  The voting results and the Scrutinizer’s report will also be made available on the website of the Company at https://www.wakefit.co/investor-relations under the Announcements tab and of National Securities Depository
