Celebrating Growth and Tradition this Navratri, PNG Jewellers 
Opens New Stores in Moshi and Wakad  
Pune, 1 October 2025 –  PNG Jewellers, one of India’s most trusted family jewellery brands, 
recently announced the launch of two new stores in Moshi and Wakad during Navratri 2025. 
Marking another milestone in its expansion journey, both stores bring PNG’s signature blend 
of heritage, purity, and contemporary designs to vibrant new customer bases. 
Both located at Pimpri-Chinchwad, the stores will showcase PNG’s complete range of gold, 
diamond, silver, and platinum jewellery, including bridal collections such as Pratha, Golden 
Katha, Eiina, and Saptam across traditional and lightweight modern designs. To celebrate the 
launch, PNG Jewellers is offering up to 50% off on gold jewellery making charges, up to 100% 
off on diamond jewellery making charges, and 0%* deduction on old gold exchange.  
Speaking at the launch in Moshi, Bollywood diva Chitrangada Singh said, “PNG Jewellers has 
always been a brand that connects with families and traditions. Their commitment to serving 
customers with sincerity and artistry makes every new store a true celebration.”  
Dr. Saurabh Gadgil, Chairman and Managing Director of PNG Jewellers, added, “With Moshi 
and Wakad, we strengthen our presence in Pune’s growing hubs and reaffirm our promise to 
remain a jeweller for every generation. The launch during Navratri makes this moment even 
more special, as the festival embodies new beginnings, prosperity, and togetherness, values 
that perfectly resonate with PNG Jewellers’ legacy and vision.” 
With these additions in retail stores, PNG Jewellers further strengthens their network of 
influence, trust, and brilliance across Maharashtra’s evolving jewellery consumer base. 
About PNG Jewellers  
PNG Jewellers is one of the most reputed jewellers in the country, with over 193 years of 
excellence in creativity and trusted service for customers across India and the USA. Under the 
able leadership of Dr. Saurabh Gadgil, a visionary entrepreneur, P N Gadgil Jewellers Limited 
is creating a brand that caters to customers globally.
