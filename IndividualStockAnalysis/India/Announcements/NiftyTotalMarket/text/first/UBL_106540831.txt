Soufflet Malt builds a new malthouse in India in partnership with United Breweries 
Limited to strengthen the brewing ecosystem  
 
Paris, France & Bengaluru, India,  February 20, 2026 –  Soufflet Malt, the world’s leading 
maltster, announces a new project to build a state- of-the-art malthouse in South Rajasthan, India, 
in partnership with United Breweries Limited  (UBL) , part of the HEINEKEN Company and one of 
India’s largest beer manufacturers. The new facility will provide high- quality malt for brewing of 
iconic beers such as Kingfisher and Heineken ®, reflecting both companies’ commitment to 
delivering exceptional beer to consumers in India while supporting sustainable agriculture, job 
creation and regional economic growth.   
 
Malt capacity of 1 10,000 tonnes per year  
The first phase of the facility is expected to be commissioned in the beginning of  2028.  The new 
facility will kick off with a malt production capacity of 1 10,000 tonnes  per year, with plans to double 
capacity during the second phase of the project.  
 
“We are excited  to build our second malthouse in India as part of our MALTiply 2030 strategy of 
leveraging our deep global  capabilities locally ,” confirms CEO Jorge Solis . “We are thrilled 
to partner with United Breweries Limited and are proud to supply high quality malt for their  trusted 
brands.  The company’s  strong reputation for excellence, quality and sustainability  provides a solid 
foundation for long- term value creation . For us, today is much  more than  business growth; it's 
about two industry leaders coming together to deliver the best and  benefit our ecosystem: the  
agricultural community, our partners, suppliers and consumers.”  
 
Brewing local prosperity 
The new malthouse will generate significant employment opportunities, creating 400 direct and 
indirect jobs, along with 700 positions across the supply chain.  Moreover, the  investment will 
empower and train 50,000+  Indian farmers with a  plan to source up to 250,000 tonnes of barley 
annually . This offers  stable income and market access to growers while championing the adoption 
of sustainable farming practices. By building a robust ecosystem involving agronomists and 
logistics providers, Soufflet Malt and United Breweries  Limited  will help ensure success  for 
industries and entrepreneurs throughout Rajasthan.  
 
Sustainability  
The new malt facility is designed with sustainability at its core.  With zero liquid discharge systems 
and advanced water management initiatives, the company is clearly committed to decarbonization. 
Digital grain handling systems will also ensure operations are integrating the latest digital 
technology and automation.  
 
Vivek Gupta, MD & CEO, United Breweries Limited says, “As a category maker, we are 
committed to the long- term growth of beer in India. Our partnership with Soufflet Malt is strategic 
and future focused , strengthening  our backward integration, securing a consistent supply of high-
quality  malt and prepar ing us for the next phase of category growth.  
 This state -of-the-art malthouse reinforces our sustainable sourcing ambitions and further positions 
Rajasthan as an important brewing hub, supporting farmers, local agriculture, economic development and employment opportunities. This partnership brings together two industry leaders 
to strengthen innovation, ensure supply security, and drive long term value.”

Long- Term Commitment  
Soufflet Malt already has roots in India with a malt house in Alwar, Rajasthan, boasting an 18,000-
ton capacity. Since 2014, the company is  working hand- in-hand with local growers to cultivate top-
notch barley that's grown, malted, and sold locally adding value every step of the way.  
 
About Soufflet Malt  
 
Soufflet Malt is the world’s leading maltster, with 39 malting plants across 20 countries in Europe, Asia,  
Africa, Australia and America. Employing more than 2,300 employees, Soufflet Malt has a production  
capacity of 3.7 million tonnes of malt annually to meet the demands of its customers –  large, global  
breweries and artisan craft brewers, distillers and other industrial players –  around the world. Building  
on its local farming roots and global reach, including distribution through Country Malt Group in North  
America and Cryer Malt in Asia, Soufflet Malt acts as a trusted supplier of quality malt, serving  
customers through brands such as Canada Malting Co. Ltd, Great Western Malting, Durst Mal z, Bairds  
Malt, Castle Malting, Tchecomalt, Barrett Burston. With a commitment to pioneer sustainable malt  
solutions, Soufflet Malt remains steadily focused on innovation across its entire value chain.
