Sub: Outcome of Board Meeting held on August 13, 2025 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of Time Technoplast 
Limited at its meeting held on August 13, 2025, inter-alia, considered and approved the 
following businesses: 
1. Appointment of M/s. Raman S. Shah & Co., Chartered Accountants (FRN: 111919W), as 
Joint Statutory Auditors and Noting of Resignation of M/s. Khandelwal Jain & Co., Joint 
Statutory Auditors of the Company 
The Members of the Company at the 34 Annual General Meeting held on September 27, 
2024, appointed M/s. K P M R & Co. and M/s. Khandelwal Jain & Co. as Joint Statutory 
Auditors of the Company for a period of five (5) years to hold office from the conclusion 
of 34th Annual General Meeting till the conclusion of 39tt Annual General Meeting. As per 
the agreed allocation of work between the Joint Auditors i.e. M/s. Khandelwal Jain & Co., 
one of the Auditor have been assigned responsibility of auditing and verifying more than 
23 factories on a quarterly and annual basis, which includes visits to multiple locations 
across India. 
M/s. Khandelwal Jain & Co., Chartered Accountants (FRN: 105049W), one of the Joint 
Statutory Auditors of the Company, have submitted their resignation, citing their 
inability to continue as Joint Statutory Auditors. The reasons for the resignation are 
mentioned below: 
“As per the work allocation among the two Joint Statutory Auditors (viz KPMR & Co. and 
Khandelwal Jain & Co.), we are required to cover/visit 23 to 24 plants during the year within a 
short period of timeframe, this strains our resources heavily. In view of our other client 
commitments, we are unable to determine the impact on our ability to plan, design, perform and 
complete the audit procedures in the timeframe available.”

The resignation of M/s. Khandelwal Jain & Co., Joint Statutory Auditors, does not 
constitute a casual vacancy, as their appointment as Joint Auditor was not mandated 
under the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013. Nonetheless, in view of the Company’s 
wide-ranging business operations across India, the engagement of Joint Statutory 
Auditors was recommended to facilitate smooth operations and ensure an effective 
distribution of audit responsibilities. 
The Audit Committee, at its meeting held today, i.e., August 13, 2025, has taken note of 
the resignation and confirmed that no concerns or issues were raised by the statutory 
auditors, and there are no other reasons for their resignation apart from those stated in 
their letter. The Board of Directors, at its meeting also held today, August 13, 2025, has 
taken note of and accepted the resignation and has similarly confirmed that no additional 
reasons have been cited beyond the contents of the letter of the resignation. 
The Audit Committee and the Board of Directors have placed on record their sincere 
appreciation for the valuable services rendered by M/s. Khandelwal Jain & Co. during 
their tenure as Joint Statutory Auditors of the Company. 
Based on the recommendation of the Audit Committee, the Board of Directors, at its 
meeting held on August 13, 2025, approved the appointment of M/s. Raman S. Shah & 
Co., Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration Number: 111919W) as Joint Statutory 
Auditors of the Company. The Board has recommended their appointment for the 
approval of shareholders at the ensuing 35t Annual General Meeting. 
The proposed appointment is in compliance with the relevant provisions of the 
Companies Act, 2013 and the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015. M/s. Raman S. Shah & Co., Chartered Accountants, have provided 
their consent via a letter dated August 13, 2025, confirming their eligibility for the said 
appointment. 
Upon approval, M/s. Raman S. Shah & Co. will serve as Joint Statutory Auditors for a 
term of five (5) consecutive years, commencing from the conclusion of the ensuing 35t 
Annual General Meeting until the conclusion of the 40 Annual General Meeting of the 
Company.

Sub: Resignation as Joint Statutory Auditors of Time Technoplast Limited (‘the 
company’) from the Second Quarter of Financial Year 2025-2026. 
We Khandelwal Jain & Co (ICAI Firm Registration No. 105049W) were appointed 
as Joint Statutory Auditors of the company to perform the audit of Financial 
Statements of the Company for the Financial Year 2024 — 2025 to Financial Year 2028 
— 2029, at its Annual General Meeting held on September 27, 2024. 
As per the Work allocation amongst the two Joint Statutory Auditors (viz KPMR & 
Co. & Khandelwal Jain & Co.), we are required to cover / visit 23 to 24 plants during

of our other client commitments, we are unable to determine the impact on our ability 
to plan, design, perform and complete the audit procedures in the timeframe available. 
In view of the above, we are tendering our resignation as Joint Statutory Auditors of 
the Company with immediate effect. 
In accordance with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) Code of 
Ethics, please note that we will be able to issue our No Objection Certificate to the 
incoming auditor only after all our dues are settled.
