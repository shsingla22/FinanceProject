Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and 
in continuation of our earl ier intimation dated May 15, 2025 , we wish to inform you that the Company has executed 
a Share Purchase Agreement  with Global Green International N.V.  to acquire its entire shareholding in the following
