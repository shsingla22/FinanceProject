PRESS RELEASE  
 
Fitch Ratings Upgrades Biocon Biologics Outlook from ‘Stable’ to ‘Positive’   
 
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India: February 7 , 202 6:  
 
Biocon Biologics Limited , a fully integrated, global biosimilars company and subsidiary of Biocon Ltd. 
(BSE: 532523, NSE: BIOCON), today announced that Fitch Ratings has revised the outlook on the 
Company’s Long -Term Foreign -Currency Issuer Default Rating (IDR) from ‘Stable’ to ‘Positive’, and has 
affirmed the IDR at ‘BB -‘. Fitch has also affirmed the ‘BB’ rating on the Company’s USD 800 million 
secured notes issued by its subsidiary, Biocon Biologics Global Plc.  
 Fitch said the ‘Positive’ outlook of the C ompany’s credit rating reflects its expectation of a sustained 
reduction in Biocon Limited's financial leverage after it reduced liabilities with proceeds from a recent 
equity issuance.
