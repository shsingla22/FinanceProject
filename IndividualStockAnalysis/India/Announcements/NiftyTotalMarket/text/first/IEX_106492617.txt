Subject: Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Regulations and Disclosure
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 Update to earlier disclosure on CERC’s Suo-Moto Order
Dated July 23, 2025 on Market Coupling
Dear Sir/Madam,
With reference to our earlier intimation dated July 24, 2025, titled “Intimation under
Regulation 30 regarding Suo-Moto Order dated July 23, 2025, in Petition No. 8/SM/2025, on
implementation of market coupling in a phased manner, we wish to inform that the CERC
has issued a corrigendum dated January 8, 2026.
The corrigendum clarifies that the expression “Order” used in the heading and footer of the
publication dated July 23, 2025, in Petition No. 8/SM/2025, shall be read as “Directions” .
Further, any reference to the said publication or to Petition No. 8/SM/2025 dated July 23,
2025, as “this order” or“order” within the Petition No. 8/SM/2025 or in any subsequent
publication, should also be read as “directions” .
A copy of the corrigendum dated January 8, 2026, is enclosed herewith for reference.
The above information will also be made available on the website of the Company:
www.iexindia.com
You are requested to take the above information on record.
