Mumbai, October 10, 2026: Avenue Supermarts Ltd. (ASL), one of the largest food & grocery retailers in India, 
today declared its standalone and consolidated financia l results for the quarter and half year ended September 
30, 2026. 
Standalone results  Total Revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 stood at Rs.19,206 cr ore, as compared to Rs.16,219 
crore in the same period last year. Earnings before In terest, Tax, Depreciation and Amortization (EBITDA) in 
Q2FY27 stood at Rs.1,403 crore, as compared to Rs.1 ,230 crore in the correspondi ng quarter of last year. 
EBITDA margin stood at 7.3% in Q2FY27  as compared to 7.6% in Q2FY26.  
Net Profit stood at Rs.804 crore for Q2FY27, as compared  to Rs.747 crore in the corresponding quarter of last 
year. PAT margin stood at 4.2% in Q2 FY27 as compared to 4.6% in Q2FY26. 
Basic Earnings per share (EPS) for Q2FY27 stood at  Rs.12.32, as compared to Rs.11.47 for Q2FY26. 
 

 
Total Revenue for H1FY27 stood at Rs.37,550 crore, as co mpared to Rs.32,151 crore in the same period last 
year. Earnings before Interest, Tax, Depreciation and Am ortization (EBITDA) in H1FY27 stood at Rs.2,930 crore, 
as compared to Rs.2,543 crore during H1FY26. EBITDA ma rgin stood at 7.8% in H1FY27 as compared to 7.9% 
in H1FY26.  
Net Profit stood at Rs.1,739 crore for H1FY27, as comp ared to Rs.1,576 crore in H1FY26. PAT margin stood at 
4.6% in H1FY27 as compared to 4.9% in H1FY26.   
Basic Earnings per share (EPS) for H1FY27 stood at  Rs.26.67, as compared to Rs.24.22 for H1FY26. 
 
Consolidated results  
Total Revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 stood at Rs.19,644 cr ore, as compared to Rs.16,676 
crore in the same period last year. Earnings before In terest, Tax, Depreciation and Amortization (EBITDA) in 
Q2FY27 stood at Rs.1,393 crore, as compared to Rs. 1,214 crore in the corresponding quarter of last year. 
EBITDA margin stood at 7.1% in Q2FY27  as compared to 7.3% in Q2FY26.  
Net Profit stood at Rs. 743 crore for Q2FY27, as compared to Rs. 685 crore in the corresponding quarter of last

About Avenue Supermarts Limited : (www.dmartindia.com; BSE: 540376; NSE: DMART)  
 Avenue Supermarts Limited is a Mumbai-based company,  which owns and operates D-Mart stores. D-Mart is 
a national supermarket chain that offers customers a range of home and personal products under one roof. 
The Company offers a wide range of products with  a focus on Foods, Non-Foods (FMCG) and General 
Merchandise & Apparel product categories. The Company offers its products under various categories, such 
as grocery and staples, dairy and frozen, fruits an d vegetables, home and personal care, bed and bath, 
crockery, footwear, toys and games, kids’ apparel,  apparel for men & women and daily essentials. The 
Company opened its first store in Mumbai, Maharashtr a in 2002. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had 
518 stores (including one store at  Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra currently closed for customers due to 
reconstruction) with Retail Business Area of 21.4 million sq. ft. across Maharashtra, Gujarat, Telangana, 
Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Madhya Prades h, Rajasthan, Punjab, NC R, Chhattisgarh, Uttar 
Pradesh, Daman, Goa, Odish a, Uttarakhand and Haryana.
