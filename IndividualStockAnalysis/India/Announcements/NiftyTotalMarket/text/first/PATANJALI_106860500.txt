Dear Sirs/Madam,  
 
We would like to inform you that M/s. Patanjali Ayurved Limited (“PAL”) , a promoter group 
company of Patanjali Foods Limited (“the Company”) , has entered into an agreement with the 
Company, for transferring the leasehold rights and interest in respect of the parcels of land 
situated at Sector – 3, Industrial Growth Centre, Pithampur, District Dhar, Madhya Pradesh.  
 
It is hereby clarified that the agreement presently constitutes an “agreement to transfer”  and 
does not itself effect the transfer or assignment of the leasehold rights in respect of the subject 
property, rather the same shall be effected upon the execution and registration of a revised Lease 
Deed with Madhya Pradesh Audyogik Kendra Vikas Nigam  (Indore) Limited.  
 
Further details in respect of the aforesaid are set out in Annexure  - I. 
  
The above information will be made available on the Company’s website at 
https://www.patanjalifoods.com/  
 
This is for your information and records please.
