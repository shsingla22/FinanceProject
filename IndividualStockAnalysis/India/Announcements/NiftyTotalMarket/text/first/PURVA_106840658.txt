Press Release  
November 2, 2026  
 
Puravankara Q2FY27 revenue surges 59% to ₹1,051 crore ; PAT at ₹ 82 crore  
Sales up 109% at ₹2,762 crore ; ₹8,900 crore of GDV added across Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi -NCR 
Bengaluru:  Puravankara Limited (NSE: PURVA | BSE: 532891), one of India’s most trusted and 
admired real estate developers, reported strong growth in Q2FY27, supported by higher handovers, 
improving realisations and sustained sales across its residential portfolio.   
Puravankara reported a Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₹ 82 crore in Q2FY27, compared to a loss of ₹4 2 crore 
in Q2FY26. Total revenue for the quarter stood at ₹ 1,051 crore, up 59% year -on-year from ₹663 crore.  
The company recorded quarterly pre -sales of ₹2, 762 crore (including the divestment value of ~₹626 
crore from Purva Zentech) , up 109% year -on-year, on  sales volume of 2.38 million sq. ft. Average 
realisation increased 32% to ₹11,6 07 per sq. ft., while customer collections grew 24% year -on-year to 
₹1,301 crore.  
For H1FY27, total revenue increased 60% year -on-year to ₹ 1,928 crore, while PAT stood at ₹ 107 crore, 
compared to a loss of ₹11 1 crore in H1FY26. During this period , residential and commercial sales stood 
at ₹3,575 crore. Additionally, the company completed the divestment of approximately 5.46 lakh sq. ft. 
of commercial office space at Purva Zentech for an asset value of ₹625.94 crore. Including this 
commercial asset monetisation, the overall achievement stands at ₹4,200.94 crore, representing 
37.51% of Puravankara’s annual sales guidance of ₹11,200 crore.  
Commenting  on the company’s performance, Ashish Puravankara, Managing Director, 
Puravankara Limited , said, “ Our Q2FY27 performance reflects the progress we are making in 
converting execution into revenue and earnings. Higher handovers, sustained sales and stronger 
realisations supported the quarter’s growth, while net debt reduced by ₹470 crore . With 3,644 
completed units pending revenue recognition, we remain focused  on progressing handovers and 
strengthening cash generation.  
We are also building the foundations for our next phase of growth. During the first half, we added 
₹14,100 crore of potential GDV across Bengaluru, Mumbai and Greater Noida, deepening our presence 
in established markets and entering Delhi -NCR. Our planned launch portfolio of approximately ₹22,600 
crore of GDV provides a substantial growth opportunity. We will pursue this with disciplined capital 
allocation and a clear focus on execution and customer delivery.”

About Puravankara Limited   
The Puravankara Group is one of India's most trusted realty majors, headquartered in Bengaluru with 
a pan -India presence. Over the past five decades, the company has catered to the full spectrum of 
housing and plotted development needs. The group has also forayed into developing Grade A 
commercial real estate  and is rapidly expanding its footprint. Additionally, its interior design arm, Purva 
Streaks, serves customers seeking an integrated interior design solution.  
As of September 30, 2026, Puravankara has completed 9 8 projects totalling approximately 60 msft 
across nine cities: Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Coimbatore, Mangaluru, Kochi, Mumbai, Pune, and 
Goa. The company's total land bank is approximately 4 6 msft, and ongoing projects total 3 3.61 msft.
