TVS Motor Company launches the all-new TVS iQube 2.2 kWh 
variant in Nepal  
 
• Nepal’s favourite EV, the TVS iQube, is now available in three variants,  
• TVS iQube has completed over 1 5 lakh  kilomet er on  the roads of Nepal  in just 
over 12 months . 
 
Kathmandu, November 2 1, 202 4: TVS Motor Company  - a leading global automaker in the 
two and three -wheeler segments  - today launched a new 2.2 kWh  battery  variant of the TVS 
iQube in Nepal. TVS iQube now offers three variants in six vibrant colours , matching varying 
customer requirements . 
 
As a pioneer in sustainable mobility, TVS Motor Company (TVSM) remains steadfast in its 
dedication to the EV segment, delivering innovative, reliable, and enjoyable products. Th e 
engineering and product quality is matched to a robust aftersales network in partnership with 
Jagdamba Motors Pvt. Ltd. The new 2.2 kWh  battery  variant of the TVS iQube, is designed to 
make elect ric vehicles  more accessible and reduce the  total cost of ownership for customers.  
 
TVS iQube is inspired by three fundamental principles: Giving customers the POWER OF 
CHOICE  for range, connected technology, charging solutions and price points; COMPLETE 
ASSURANCE  through vehicle safety and overall ownership experience and the SIMPLICITY 
OF USAGE  through easy -to-use features that enhance the riding experience of customers.  
 
Commenting on the launch,  Madhu Prakash Singh , Vice President, EV , International 
Business, TVS Motor Company , said, “The journey of TVS iQube in Nepal has been deeply 
rewarding, with our customers collectively covering over 15 la kh kilometers in just the past 
year. Globally, over 400,000 customers have chosen the TVS iQube brand —testam ent to our 
commitment to a superior, future -ready EV experience. With our new 2.2 kWh  battery  variant, 
featuring the fastest home charging in its segment, we are bringing electric mobility within the 
reach for even more riders, empowering them to embrace a  cleaner, smarter way of moving.”  
 
Commenting on the launch, Shahil Agrawal, Managing Director, Shanker Group , said “At 
Jagdamba Motors, we have had a  very successful journey with the TVS iQube in the past one 
year.  With the introduction of TVS iQub e 2.2kWh battery variant, we are confident this will 
further boost the  evolution  in the Nepalese two -wheeler EV segment and showcase our shared 
vision to provide top -tier mobility solutions to our customers. We are committed to ensure 
availability of th is new variant across Nepal with our wide sales network  coupled with an 
exceptional after sales support of spare parts  & skilled EV technicians . TVS iQube  2.2kWh 
battery variant  will expand the  already  proven TVS iQube range and make EV 2 wheeler more 
acce ssible to the Nepalese people . 
 
The all -new TVS iQube 2.2 kWh battery  variant is available for booking starting today on the 
TVS Nepal website ( www.tvsnepal.com/iqube ) and at 40 TVSM dealerships across 
Kathmandu.
