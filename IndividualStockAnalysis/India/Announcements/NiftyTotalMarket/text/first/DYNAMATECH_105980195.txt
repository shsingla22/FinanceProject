Mr. Pierre de Bausset chaired the Meeting. The requisite quorum being present, the Chairman called the 
meeting to order. Mr. Pierre de Bausset, Prof Pradyumna Vyas, Ms. Gaitri Issar Kumar, Dr. Ajay Kumar, 
Mr. P S Ramesh and Mr. Udayant Malhoutra directors attended in person and Mr. James Tucker, 
participated the meeting via video conferencing. 
 
The Chairman thereafter addressed the members with his speech. 
 
Mr. Pierre then requested Mr. Udayant Malhoutra, CEO & Managing Director to make a presentation on 
the Company’s performance.” 
 
Mr. Udayant Malhoutra then presented the business highlights and performance of the Company for the 
Financial Year 2023-24 
 
Mr. Pierre then handed over the proceedings to Mr. Shivaram V, Company Secretary and compliance 
officer to take over the compliance matters and resolutions. 
 
Thereafter, Mr. Shivaram informed the members that the remote e-voting commenced on Monday, 
September 02, 2024 (9.00 a.m. IST) and ended on Wednesday, September 04, 2024 (5.00 p.m. IST).  
 
Mr. Shivaram also informed that the members who have not cast their vote(s) through remote e-voting can 
cast their votes at the Meeting using the electronic voting system provided by KFin Technologies Limited.
