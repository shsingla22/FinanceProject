Pursuant to Regulation 30(1) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that, with 
an objective of driving performance of the Company as well as motivating and re taining key 
talent, the Board of Directors (Board) of the Company  has, based on the recommendation of 
the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC), approved a Long -Term Incentive 
Scheme 2024 (Scheme) commencing from financial year 2024 -25 and ending in financial year 
2026-27, unless the same is renewed / extended by the NRC.  
 
Under th e Scheme, the eligible employees  of the Company  shall , upon achievement of the 
performance criteria as specified in the Scheme and subject to continuing undisputed 
employment with the Company (except in case of retiring employees, completion of contract, 
and death of employee), be entitled to the Long -Term Incentive (LTI) which amount shall be 
paid out in cash.  The payout would get determined, amongst other factors, by the Company’s 
share price performance over the Scheme period.  The final incentive valu e and the date of 
payment of LTI to the eligible employees shall be as decided by NRC / Board.  The Scheme 
may be amended by the NRC /  Board from time to time.
