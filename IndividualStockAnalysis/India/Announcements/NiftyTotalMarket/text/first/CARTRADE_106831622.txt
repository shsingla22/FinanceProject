CarTrade Tech Reports Highest -Ever Income of ₹236 crores and EBITDA jumps by 20% to ₹76 crores ;  
OLX India Revenues grow by 26% and EBITDA  zoom s by 36% 
 
Mumbai, 28th October 2026: CarTrade Tech Limited (NSE: CARTRADE, BSE: 543333), India's leading digital 
platform for vehicles and used goods, today announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 
30, 2026 ("Q2 FY27"). The Company reported its highest -ever quarterly Revenue  of ₹2 13 crore s; EBITDA jumps 
by 20% to ₹76 crores  and achieved its highest ever Profit Before Tax of ₹85 crores . OLX India maintained strong 
momentum with a 26% YoY growth in revenue and a 36% YoY increase in EBITDA.  
 
Key Highlights – Q2 FY27 (Consolidated):  
● Highest -ever quarterly Revenue  of ₹2 13 crore s; resulting in YoY growth of 10%. 
● EBITDA of ₹76 crores in Q2FY27, resulting in YoY growth of 20%. 
● Highest -ever quarterly Profit Before Tax of ₹85 crore s, resulting in YoY growth of 7%.  
● Profit after tax for the quarter is at ₹66 crores, resulting in YoY growth of 3%.  
Key Highlights – H1 FY27 (Consolidated):  
● Revenue  of ₹414 crores in H1FY27, resulting in YoY growth of 1 3%. 
● EBITDA of ₹139 crores in H1FY27, resulting in YoY growth of 30% . 
● Profit before tax for the H1FY27 is at ₹160 crores, resulting in YoY growth of 17%.  
● Profit after tax for the H1FY27 is at ₹123 crores, resulting in YoY growth of 10%.

About CarTrade Tech Limited:  (www.cartradetech.com ; NSE: CARTRADE|BSE:543333)  
 
CarTrade Tech Limited is one of the world’s largest digital automotive ecosystems and India’s leading online 
marketplace company. Through platforms including CarWale, BikeWale, Shriram Automall and OLX India, the 
Company serves ~76 million monthly unique users, with 96% of its traffic generated organically. Its consumer 
marketplaces host more than 63% of India’s online used car listings, while its remarketing platforms handle 
~1.6 million auction listings annually. With a strong nationwide network spanning over 500 physical locations 
and a technology -led, asset -light platform model, CarTrade Tech enables millions of consumers, dealers, OEMs 
and enterprises to buy and sell vehicl es and other used products with ease, trust and efficiency.
