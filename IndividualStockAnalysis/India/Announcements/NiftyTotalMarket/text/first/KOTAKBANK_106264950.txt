With reference to the captioned subject, we wish to inform you that  Mr. Paul Parambi would cease to the 
Group Chief Risk Officer (“GCRO”) of the Bank at the end of the day on June 11, 2025 , in view of his 
upcoming retirement . Mr. Parambi would continue as Group President – Risk  and would cease to be a 
Senior Management Per sonnel (“SMP”) of the Bank  upon his retirement on June 30, 2025.

a SMP of the Bank, for a period of five years, with effect from June 12 , 2025 . 
 
Ms. Srishti Sethi brings over 30 years of experience across enterprise and credit risk, banking and wealth 
management, corporate debt, collections, and operational excellence. She has held leadership roles at Hero 
Fincorp, IDFC First Bank, and GE Capital i n the past, and is known for her strategic perspective, deep 
domain expertise and the ability to lead transformation with precision and purpose. A graduate in 
Mathematical Statistics from Lady Shri Ram College and a PGDBM from IMS Ghaziabad, Ms. Sethi is a lso 
a Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) and holds a CRISC certification.
