RattanIndia Enterprises Launches Kaari – A New Line of 
Ethnic Wear Celebrating India’s Rich Embroidery Heritage  
New Delhi, 3rd October 2024 : RattanIndia Enterprises Ltd. is pleased to announce the launch of its 
newest brand, Kaari , through its 100% wholly owned subsidiary Neobrands Limited. Kaari introduces 
a range of Indian ethnic wear for women, celebrating India’s rich heritage of embroidery. Kaari blends 
traditional craftsmanship with timeless elegance, offering an intricately designed collection tailored 
for Indian women.  
                      
The collection features a variety of embroidery artforms, from stunning  Chikankari  to Handcrafted 
Dabka  work, each carefully woven with beads, showcasing the true artistry of Indian craftsmanship. 
Whether for special occasions or daily wear, Kaari’s versatile designs and inclusive silhouettes ensure 
a perfect fit for every woman. With intricate details and a palette of rich festive hues as well as chic 
soft pastels, this collection is ideal for festivals, celebrations, and everyday wear.  
                             
At Kaari , every piece tells a story, and our stunning dupattas are no exception. From the opulent 
textures of Banarasi  brocades to the delicate elegance of organza  adorned with lace and sequin work, 

                                                                                             
our dupattas weave narratives of craftsmanship and tradition that are as captivating as our 
embroidered outfits. Whether you’re looking to make a bold statement for upcoming festivities or 
seeking comfort in everyday styles, Kaari  has something for every Indian woman.  
We have ensured that our entire range of approximately 40 styles is thoughtfully curated, with sizes 
ranging from S to XXL, ensuring every woman can find her perfect fit.  
 
“The Indian consumer has a deep appreciation for the art of traditional textiles, and Kaari is dedicated 
to bringing this cultural richness to the forefront of contemporary fashion ,” said Ms. Anjali Rattan, 
Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises Ltd.  “Through Kaari, we are not only offering elegant ethnic 
wear but also reviving and celebrating the intricacies of India’s embroideries in a way that makes them 
accessible to every woman. We are excited to introduce Kaari as part of our commitment to preserv ing 
Indian craftsmanship. ” 
Kaari  will be exclusively available on the leading e -commerce platform, amazon.in , with prices ranging 
from INR 999 to INR 1799. This strategic partnership allows customers across India to conveniently 
browse and purchase their favourite pieces at www.amazon.in/kaari  and have  them delivered to their 
doorstep.
