 Saatvik Green Energy Reports 77% Sequential Revenue Growth in Q2 
FY27; Commences Commercial Production at Odisha Facility  
New Delhi, November 5th, 2026 : Saatvik Green Energy Limited (“the Company”) (NSE: SAATVIKGL, BSE: 
544526), one of India's leading solar PV module manufacturers, today announced its unaudited financial 
results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026. During the quarter, the Company reported 
revenue of ₹ 9,026 Mn and PAT of ₹ 202 Mn. It s confirmed order book stood at 6.68 GW as of September 
2026. The Company continued to advance its integrated clean energy manufacturing strategy, including the commencement of commercial production at its Odisha facility, and secured a ₹1,041.63 crore order from Solar Energy Corporation of India Limited (SECI) for 600 MWp of solar modules manufactured using domestic solar cells.

 Business & Strategic Updates: 
 
• Odisha manufacturing facility: At the Gopalpur facility, major construction and infrastructure works 
for Phase I, comprising 4 GW of module and 2.4 GW of cell manufacturing capacity, have been 
completed. The dedicated 220 kV substation is ready for charging, while equipment installatio n, testing 
and validation are underway. On November 2, 2026, Saatvik Solar Industries Private Limited, a wholly owned material subsidiary, commenced commercial production of solar PV modules at the facility, with approximately 1.3 GW of Phase I module capacity operational and the full 4 GW expected by December 2026. Trial runs on the 2.4 GW cell line have commenced, with production ramp -up targeted for 
December 2026.  
• Cell manufacturing and backward integration: Saatvik Solar Industries Private Limited, a material subsidiary, applied for ALMM List -II enlistment of its 2.4 GW Odisha cell facility in September 2026. The 
ALMM List -II inspection has been called for and is expected in November 2026, and enlistment re mains 
subject to MNRE approval. The Company has also signed an MoU with IPICOL, Government of Odisha, for a further 3.6 GW solar cell facility at Gopalpur, on a 27 -acre parcel adjacent to Phase I, targeted for 
completion by the start of FY28, with groundwo rk having commenced.  
• Product and manufacturing expansion: At REI Expo 2026, the Company launched its Quad- Cut TOPCon 
3.0 Dual Glass Bifacial module, offering output of up to 670 Wp and module efficiency of up to 24.8%, 
with bifacial output of up to 745 W under specified bifacial rating conditions. Encapsulant 
manufacturing capacity is being scaled up from 2 GW to 5 GW by FY28, while the long -term roadmap 
includes ingot and wafer manufacturing capacity of 6 GW by FY29.  
• Expansion into complementary businesses : The Company has entered the hybrid inverter segment, 
expanded into lithium battery solutions (51.2V, 100Ah, 5.12 kWh, 4,000+ life cycles), commenced e -
commerce sales on Amazon.in, and developed an on- grid Solar Kit for residential and small commercial 
customers. The MSEDCL solar water- pumping order also complements the Company's module business 
and its inverter, transformer and EPC capabilities.  
 
Industry Context  
Cells are the bottleneck. India has about 36.6 GW of cell capacity against 261.7 GW of module capacity (June 2026), and module factories are running at an estimated 35 –40% utilisation. Since ALMM List -II took effect 
on 1st June 2026, modules made with dome stic cells sell at more than a 75% premium to non -DCR modules 
(about ₹24.6 –26.5/Wp against ₹14.2/Wp). Standalone module makers are the most exposed, with little 
access to cells, while integrated players capture the premium. This is the case for Saatvik’s O disha cell- to-
module programme: 2.4 GW of cells, an ALMM List -II application filed in September 2026, and 3.6 GW more 
planned.  
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Prashant Mathur, Chief Executive Officer, Saatvik Green Energy Limited, said: “H1 FY27 is best read as a period of investment ahead of the curve. Our reported numbers 
reflect the transition as we build our next phase, and we chose to prioritise long- term positioning over short -
term optics.  What gives us confidence is the quality of demand. Customers across government, utility and C&I 
segments continue to place repeat and large -scale orders with us, and our order book gives us clear visibility 
well beyond this financial year.  
The industry is shifting from a module -led to a cell -led market, where access to domestic cells will decide who 
can compete for the most attractive projects. That is why Odisha matters so much to us. As our cell and module capacities come online together, Saatvik moves from assembling modules to controlling a critical part of its own supply chain, with better cost, quality and delivery certainty.  
Our focus for the rest of the year is simple: deliver on our commitments, ramp up Odisha without 
compromising quality, and protect margins and working capital. We believe this integration will make Saatvik 
a stronger , more resilient and more profitable business over the coming years.”

About  Saatvik Green Energy  Limited: 
Saatvik Green Energy Limited is one of India's leading solar photovoltaic module manufacturers, with a strong 
presence across utility -scale, commercial & industrial, EPC, and distributed solar segments. The Company 
currently operates a 4.8 GW module manufacturing facility in Ambala, Haryana, and has commenced commercial production at its integrated manufacturing facility in Odisha, which comprises 4 GW of module capacity (approximately 1.3 GW operational) and 2.4 GW of solar cell capacity (Phase I), with a further 3.6 GW of cell capacity planned (Phase II), as part of a broader roadmap to build a fully integrated, cell -to-module 
clean energy platform. With a focus on technology, quality, and execution, Saatvik aims to play a meaningful role in India's renewable energy transition.
