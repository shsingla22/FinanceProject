Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations ”), it is hereby informed that the Board of Directors of Lenskart 
Solutions Pte. Ltd., Singapore (“ Lenskart Singapore ”), a wholly owned subsidiary of Lenskart 
Solutions Limited (“ the Company ”), at its meeting held on December 24, 2025, approved an investment 
of KRW 3,000,000,000 (Korean Won Three Billion) (approximately INR 186 million) for the acquisition 
of 29.24% of the share capital of iiNeer Corp., Ltd. (“ iiNeer”), a company incorporated under the laws 
of the Republic of Korea.  
Lenskart Singapore will be investing in iiNeer, a Korea-based startup, which is focused on developing 
technology-enabled eye-testing and lens-cutting equipment. This investment supports the Company’s 
efforts to build proprietary capabilities in core equipment and is expected to improve operating 
efficiencies and reduce equipment capital expenditure over time. 
The details required to be disclosed pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para A of Part A of Schedule
