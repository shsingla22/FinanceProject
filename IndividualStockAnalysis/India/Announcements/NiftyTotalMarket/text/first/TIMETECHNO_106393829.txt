Further to our earlier intimation dated March 03, 2025, regarding the planned introduction of 
Polyethylene (PE) Pipes for the Gas Distribution sector, we wish to inform you that the Company has 
successfully obtained the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) License under IS 14885:2022 for the 
manufacturing and sale of PE Pipes for Gas Distribution networks. 
This development has been achieved within the committed timeline of Q2 FY26 and marks the 
fulfilment of a key milestone as outlined in our earlier communication. 
PE Pipes offer significant advantages including lightweight design, high durability, and extended 
service life—factors that will strengthen Time Technoplast’s market presence and contribute to 
sustainable infrastructure growth.

e Strong Industry Presence: Supplies PE Pipes across sectors including water supply, irrigation,

e Expanding Customer Base: Targeting 20+ public and private Gas Distribution companies 
alongside existing EPC clients. 
e Growth Outlook: Enhanced capacity utilization with minimal investment, supporting the 
Company’s forecasted 30% growth in the PE pipe segment. 
e Nation-Building Contribution: Aligned with national initiatives such as Jal Jeevan Mission, PM 
Ujjwala Yojana, Smart Cities, and road infrastructure projects through metal-to-PE pipe 
replacement.
