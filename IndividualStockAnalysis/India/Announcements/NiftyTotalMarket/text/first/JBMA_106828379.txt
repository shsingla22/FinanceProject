Gurugram, October 26, 2026: JBM Auto Limited, India’s leading automotive and electric vehicle company 
(BSE: 532605 & NSE Code: JBMA), today announced its consolidated results for the quarter ended and half 
year ended 30™ September 2026 and has registered a 12.46% increase in net profit to Rs. 58.76 crore in 
Q2FY27 as compared to Rs. 52.25 crore in Q2FY26. 
Revenues including other operating income stood at Rs. 1,656.37 crore compared to Rs. 1,368.24 crore in 
Q2FY26, an increase of 21.06%. EBIDTA increased by 3.40% at Rs. 201.35 crore compared to Rs. 194.72 
crore in Q2FY26. Earnings per share (EPS) for Q2FY27 stood at Rs. 2.49, compared to Rs. 2.23 in Q2FY26.
