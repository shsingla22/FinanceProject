reported in the corresponding q uarter of the previous year.

year growth, while Profit After Tax (PAT) was Rs. 4,376 Crore, up 17% over  the previous 
year. 
 
Consolidated Revenue for the quarter stood at Rs. 9,970 Crore r eflecting a growth of 4% over 
previous year, Profit After Tax (PAT) grew to Rs. 1,169 Crore a  growth of 24% over the 
previous year.
