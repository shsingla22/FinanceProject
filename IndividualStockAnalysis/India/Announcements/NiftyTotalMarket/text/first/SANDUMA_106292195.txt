Sub: Receipt of Seven Star Rating of Mines Award for the performance year 2023 -24  
 
Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, this is to inform Stock Exchanges 
that the Company’s  Kammatharu Iron Ore Mine, Mining Lease No .2678 was felicitated as Seven Star Rated 
Mine by the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines for the performance year 2023 -24 on 7  July 2025 at 
Rajasthan International Centre, Jaipur at the award function  to felicitate  Five Star & Seven Star Rat ed Mines 
of India . The Seven Star award was presented by  Shri Bhajanlal Sharma, Hon’ble Chief Minister of 
Rajasthan  in presence of  Shri G. Kishan Reddy, Hon’ble Union Minister of Coal and Mines  to Bahirji A jai 
Ghorpade , Managing Director of  the Company  and attended by Krishna Reddy M, Vice President - Mines  
and Deepak Cukkae  Anilkumar , Deputy General Manager  - Geology & Environment.  
 
Recognising the outstanding efforts of best practices in Green Mining , the Company  was honoured with the 
prestigious Seven Star Rating award , the only mines from South India and first among the three selected 
Seven Star Rated Mines of the Country , and  also being rated as a Five Star Rated Mine  being recognised for 
commitment  and outstanding achievement of mines  in Sustainable Development for the performance year 
2023 -24 for its Kammatharu Iron Ore Mine in Sandur Taluk, Ballari District over an extent of 1860.10 
Hectares.  It is to be noted that the Company’s  Kammatharu Iron Ore Mine  has been awarded pres tigious 
Five Star Rating award for the 10th consecutive time since inception of Star Rating award by Ministry of 
Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines.
