c. A brief on types of customers
 Petronet LNG Limited (PLL) specializes in the import, storage, and regasification of Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG), playing a 
vital role in India’s energy landscape. PLL delivers both LNG and regasified LNG (R-LNG) to a broad spectrum of customers 
nationwide, utilizing multiple delivery channels. PLL’s diverse clients include public sector undertakings, private entities 
and key infrastructure entities, collectively contributing to the reliable and sustainable growth of India’s energy supply.
• R-LNG Supply (via Pipeline Network): 
 R-LNG is transported through cross-country pipelines, owned by its major customers including GAIL, IOCL, BPCL, ONGC, 
GSPL, and Torrent. The R-LNG is primarily utilized in sectors such as fertilizers, city gas distribution (CGD), refineries, 
power generation, petrochemicals, and other industrial entities.
146• LNG Supply: 
 LNG is supplied through road tankers to key customers such as GAIL, IOCL, AG&P , GSPC, IMC etc. Additionally, LNG is 
also supplied to ONGC for extraction of C2, C3 components for their downstream petrochemical entity, namely ONGC 
Petro Additions Limited (OPAL).
