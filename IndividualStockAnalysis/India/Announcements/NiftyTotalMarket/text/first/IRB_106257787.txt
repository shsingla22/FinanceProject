(Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014, as amended ( “SEBI InvIT Regulations” ), in

Tollway Limited ( “KTL” ) and Kishangarh Gulabpura  Tollway Limited ( “KGTL” ) (collectively referred 
as “Project SPVs” ), as the Project SPVs of the  IRB InvIT  Fund , for approximately Rs. 4,466 crore s on 
fixed price basis (inclusive of applicable taxes)  over the balance concession period with weighted average 
life of 20 years , on the key commercial terms tabled before the Board and on such other terms and conditions 
as may be agreed among the parties . Such appointment is proposed to become effective  upon completion 
of the transfer of 100% of the equity share c apital of each of the Project SPVs from the IRB Infrastructure 
Trust to the IRB InvIT Fund .
