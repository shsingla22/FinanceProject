Dear Sir/Madam, 
  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III, Part A Para A of the Securities and Exchange Board 
of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you 
that the Company has, today i.e. March 19, 2026, increased the cement grinding capacity at its JK 
Cement Works, Muddapur, Karnataka by 1.00 MnTPA, from 3.50 MnTPA to 4.50 MnTPA (Clinker 
Capacity: 2.64 MnTPA). 
  
The said expansion has been achieved through debottlenecking and optimisation of the existing 
production system, without setting up any new facility. 
  
Consequent to the above, the  Company’s overall grey cement production capacity stands at 32.26 
MnTPA (including the capacity of its subsidiary companies).
