Press Release     
Varroc and Forsee Power to Form Joint Venture to Scale 
EV Battery Solutions in India  
 
Varroc to Exercise Management and Operational Control, Strengthening 
Its Position in India’s Evolving Electric Mobility Ecosystem  
 
Pune, India | 16  October 2026: As electric mobility gathers momentum across 
the world, the automotive industry is entering a new phase of transformation-  one 
that calls for technology solutions that can evolve with changing vehicle 
architectures, customer requirements and the growing scale of electrification. In India, this transition presents a significant opportunity to build scalable, cost-effective and application- specific solutions that support OEMs across an expanding 
range of vehicle segments.  
Recognising this evolving landscape, Varroc Engineering Limited  (VARROC| 
541578 | INE665L01035 ), a global automotive technology company, and Forsee 
Power  SA, France (FR0014005SB3 – ALFOR) , a specialist in smart battery 
systems, to enter into a joint venture to expand their electric mobility portfolio 
and combine their complementary capabilities to develop  advanced battery -pack 
solutions.   
As part of the transaction, Varroc will acquire a 45% equity stake in Forsee Power India, Forsee Power’s Indian subsidiary focused on battery -pack systems  and 
exercise  management and operational control  on the entity . The joint venture is 
proposed to operate in India subject to applicable regulatory and corporate 
approvals. Varroc will also s ubscribe  to Optionally Convertible Redeemable 
Preference Shares  (OCPRS ) to support the joint venture . 
The joint venture will initially focus on battery packs for three -wheelers, with a 
roadmap to address two -wheelers, light commercial vehicles, buses, trucks, off -
highway and industrial vehicles.  
Commenting on the partnership, Arjun Jain,  Whole Time  Director & CEO 
– Business I, Varroc, said:  “Electric mobility is both evolving and growing 
rapidly, thus creating new opportunities and new requirements for the automotive 
industry. Through this evolution we believe  OEMs will need partners who can 
anticipate changes and translate them into reliable, cost- effective solutions at 
scale. Our partnership with Forsee Power brings together complementary strengths in battery -system technology, automotive engineering, manufac turing 
and customer relationships. It represents an important step in expanding Varroc’s  
portfolio to address  the evolving needs of our market and the future of mobility.  
This will further enhance addressable content per electric vehicle in all the 
segment”  
 
 
Press Release     
“With the rapid growth of electric mobility adoption in India, the opportunity is 
clear and we are ready to scale. Joining forces with Varroc combines our battery 
technology with local industrial strength and customer access. Three -wheelers are 
our starting point, with a clear ambition to expand across two -wheeler, 
commercial and industrial vehicles” highlighted Christophe Gurtner, 
Chairman and CEO of Forsee Power.   
Forsee Power India brings established expertise in battery -pack technology and 
systems, while Varroc contributes automotive engineering capabilities, established 
OEM relationships and cost- effective, world -class manufacturing expertise.  
Together, the companies aim to combine these strengths to develop battery -pack 
solutions adaptable to different vehicle architectures and operating conditions, 
while building the capabilities required to support the continued evolution and 
scale of electri c mobility.  
The joint venture reinforces Varroc’s commitment to anticipating the future of 
mobility, expanding its technology portfolio and supporting OEMs with solutions 
that enable safe, smart and sustainable mobility.

About Varroc Engineering Ltd.  
Varroc Engineering Ltd is a global tier -1 automotive component group. It was incorporated 
in 1988. The group manufactures and supplies E -mobility solutions, Body systems 
solutions, Lighting solutions, HMI solutions, ICE powertrain and Advanced electronics to 
leading OEMs with end -to-end capabilities across design, development and manufacturing 
for two -wheeler, three -wheeler, passenger vehicles, commercial vehicle, and off -highway 
vehicle worldwide. The group income was ₹ 89,080 million from continued operat ions in 
FY26. The group employs more than 5400 employees (750 + R&D Engineers), has 37 
global operating manufacturing facilities supported by 7 R&D Centres, and has filed more 
than 130 patents.  
Varroc Engineering Limited’s shares are listed on the National Stock Exchange (VARROC) 
and the Bombay Stock Exchange (541578) in India.  
For further information on Varroc Engineering Limited please visit  www.varroc.com
