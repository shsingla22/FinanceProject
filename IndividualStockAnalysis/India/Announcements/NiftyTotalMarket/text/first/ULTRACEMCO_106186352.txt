Sub: Company Update: Commissioning of cement capacity  
 
Dear Sirs,  
 
We are pleased to announce:   
 
1. Commissioning a brownfield clinker capacity of  3.35 mtpa along with one out of the two 
cement mills with a capacity of 2.7 mtpa at the Company’s Unit in Maihar, Madhya Pradesh.  
The second grinding mill is expected to be commissioned in Q1FY26.  
 
2. Commissioning of brownfield expansion of Grinding unit at Dhule, Maharashtra with a capacity 
of 1.2 mtpa.  
 
3. Completion and commissioning of brownfield expansion of Grinding unit of 0.60 mtpa at 
Durgapur , West Bengal.  
 
4. A new bulk terminal in Lucknow  with a capacity of handl ing 1.8 mtpa of cement.  This is the 
Company’s first bulk terminal in the state of Uttar Pradesh.   
 
Consequent to the above, the Company’s total domestic grey cement manufacturing capacity 
stands at 183 .36 mtpa. Along with its overseas capacity of 5.4 mtpa, the Company’s global 
capacity stands at 188 .76 mtpa.  
 
The above is for your information and record.
