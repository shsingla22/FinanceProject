2. Board Comments on fine levied by the Exchanges for the quarter ended 30th June 2025:  
 
This is with reference to the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (“NSE”) letter and BSE 
Limited (“BSE”) email dated 29th August, 2025, regarding non-compliance with Regulation 
17(1)(2A), 18(1), 19, 20, 21(2) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 i.e. non-compliance for the 
composition of the Board and its committees for the quarter ended 30th June, 2025, and imposing 
fine on the Company for the same. The same was intimated to NSE and BSE vide our letter No. 
IRCON/SECY/STEX/124 dated 29th August, 2025.  
 
As advised by the Exchanges, the aforesaid matter of non-compliance was placed before the Board 
in its meeting held on 10th September, 2025, wherein the Board deliberated and noted the position
