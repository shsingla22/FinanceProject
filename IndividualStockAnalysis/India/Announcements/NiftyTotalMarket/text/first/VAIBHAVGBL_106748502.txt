      VGL Honored with India Risk Management Award   
  
 
Jaipur , August 18, 2026:  
Vaibhav Global Limited (VGL), a vertically integrated global retailer of fashion jewellery and 
lifestyle products, has been honored with the India Risk Management Award for Best -in-Class 
Risk Management under the Mid Cap Category . 
 
The recognition underscores VGL’s commitment to building a robust and resilient organisation 
through a comprehensive approach to enterprise risk management, governance and business 
continuity. The award acknowledges the Company’s efforts in proactively ide ntifying, assessing 
and mitigating risks while embedding risk awareness across its operations .  
 
Our CFO, Mr. Nitin Panwad  said, “We are honoured to receive the India Risk Management Award 
for Best -in-Class Risk Management in the Mid Cap category. This recognition reflects our 
continued focus on strengthening resilience, governance and preparedness across the 
organisation. We rema in committed to embedding a proactive risk culture that enables us to 
navigate uncertainty effectively while creating sustainable, long -term value for all our 
stakeholders.”

About Vaibhav Global Limited  
Vaibhav Global Limited (VGL) is listed on stock exchanges in India (BSE: 532156, NSE: VAIBHAVGBL, ISIN 
- INE884A01027). VGL is an omni -channel E -tailer of fashion jewellery, accessories, and lifestyle products 
in developed markets with direct access to ~14 1 million households (FTE) through its TV home shopping 
networks – Shop LC in US, Shop TJC in UK and Shop LC in Germany. The Company’s ecommerce websites 
www.shoplc.com  in the US, www.tjc.co.uk  in the UK and www.shoplc.de  in Germany, complement TV 
coverage and diversify customer engagement. The Company is committed to ‘Delivering Joy’ to all the 
stakeholders. Through its flagship midday meal program, ‘Your Purchase Feeds…’  where a meal is provided 
for every piece sold at the retail channels, the Company has provided over 75 million meals in US, UK, 
Germany, and India at a run rate of 50,000 meals being donated every school day since program’s inception.
