Thomas Cook India wins MICE Outbound award at  the Economic Times 
MICE & Wedding Tourism Awards 2024  
 
Mumbai, July 4, 2024 : Thomas Cook (India) Limited - India’s leading omnichannel travel services company was 
honoured with the MICE Travel Agency –  Outbound award at the E conomic Times  MICE & Wedding Tourism 
Awards 2024.  
 
The platform  celebrates the exceptional contributions  by MICE and wedding tour ism companies and professionals. 
This year, the awards highlighted outstanding delivery in innovation, customer service and meaningful 
contributions to their businesses -  setting a benchmark of excellence.  The selection criteria covered a range of 
parameters, with the nominations judged by a grand jury  of industry experts.  
 
Thomas Cook  India’s  MICE team was selected basis  its innovative product development and  deep  understanding 
of its consumer preferences and trends, highli ghting the Company’s effective strategies, and commitment to 
scalability and sustainability.  
 
Mr. Rajeev Kale -  President & Country Head, Holida ys, MICE, Visa -  Thomas Cook (India) Limited  said, “At 
Thomas Cook  India , we believe that MICE is an art ! Innovation is deeply embedded in our DNA and we take 
great pride in delivering  exceptional experiences for our customers - across destinations. It is truly an honour to 
accept the Economic Times MICE Outbound award on behalf of my exceptional team. This prestigious accolade  
underscores our leadership in the MICE sector, and our unerring focus on product development and commitment 
to delivering unparalle led MICE experie nces .”
