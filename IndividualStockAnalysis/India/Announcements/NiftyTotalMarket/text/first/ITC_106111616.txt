Allotment of equity shares by ITC Hotels Limited  
 
Further to our letter dated 1 8th December, 2024 , we write to advise that ITC Hotels Limited 
(ITCHL)  has informed  us the following : 
 
(i) the Board of Directors of ITCHL at the meeting held today i.e.,  11th January, 2025, has 
allotted  125,11,71,040 equity shares of ` 1/- each to the  shareholders of the Company as 
on the Record Date  i.e., 6th January, 2025, pursuant to the  Scheme of Arrangement 
amongst ITC Limited and  ITCHL  and their respective shareholders and creditors under                     
Sections 230 to 232 read with the  other applicable provisions  of the  Companies Act, 2013 .  
 
Consequent to the above, ITCHL has ceased to be a subsidiary of the Company                          
effective  11th January, 2025.  
 
(ii) Necessary application(s) will be made by ITCHL for the purpose of listing of its                         
equity shares ; such shares will remain frozen till the time  listing / trading permission is 
granted by the Stock Exchanges .
