Rainbow Children’s Medicare Limited reports robust Q2 FY27 
performance, with 2 3% revenue growth and continued progress across 
its network  
• Revenue grows 2 3% YoY, with EBITDA and PAT recording double -digit growth  
• Nellore and Guntur hospitals commence operations, strengthening Rainbow’s Andhra  Pradesh  
network  
• Growth initiatives gain traction across pediatric, fertility, and maternity services  
 
Hyderabad , October  31, 2026: Rainbow Children’s Medicare Limited (RCML), India’s leading pediatric and 
perinatal healthcare network, today announced its results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, 
reporting 2 3% year -on-year revenue growth, supported by healthy patient volume, improving performance 
across hospitals and continued progress in its operational and digital initiatives.  
 
Revenue grew by 2 3% year -on-year during the quarter, while EBITDA and Profit After Tax (PAT) recorded 
growth of 14% and 11%, respectively. Inpatient and outpatient volumes remained healthy despite  seasonal  
softness due to  significant monsoon shortfall.  
 
Rainbow’s growth  initiatives through digital and community connect  are beginning to translate into stronger 
patient engagement and business ramp -up, translating into higher OPD footfalls and inpatient admissions.  
 
Rainbow has further strengthened its presence in Coastal Andhra with the commencement of operations 
at its newly acquired 70-bed hospital in Nellore and 50 -bed brand new hospital in Guntur. These additions 
extend Rainbow’s network from Visakhapatnam to Chennai. The company plans to further expand its 
presence in the new  capital city of Amaravati, in And hra Pradesh to establish  specialty  pediatric  hospital in 
a phased ma nner with  an initial  bed capacity of 150 beds , following the allotment of two acres of land by 
the Government of Andhra Pradesh.  
 
With the appointment of a new Group CFO,  Mr. Sudarshan Patodia, Rainbow has  further strengthe ned  its 
leadership team and organizational  capabilities in the  growth phase.

About Rainbow Children’s Medicare Limited  
Rainbow Children’s Medicare Limited operates a network of 26 hospitals, 6 clinics and 2 dedicated fertility clinics across 
11 cities, with a total bed capacity of 2,6851 beds. Its paediatric services, operating under the brand "Rainbow Children’s 
Hospital", include newborn and paediatric intensive care, paediatric multi -specialty services and paediatric quaternary 
care, including organ transplantation. Its women’s healthc are services, under "BirthRight  by Rainbow", encompass 
normal and complex obstetric care, multidisciplinary fetal care, perinatal genetic and fertility care, and gynaecology 
services . 
 
Rainbow Children’s Hospital built on strong fundamentals of a multidisciplinary approach with a full -time consultant led 
clinical service along with 24/7 commitment in a child centric environment. The company follows a hub -and -spoke 
operating model where t he hub hospital provides comprehensive outpatient, inpatient care, with a focus on tertiary and 
quaternary services, while the spokes provide 24/7 emergency care, large outpatient services and comprehensive 
obstetrics, pediatric inpatient and level 3 NICU services. This model is successfully operational at Hyderabad and 
Bengaluru. The endeavor is to replicate this approach in Chennai and across the National Capital Region. Subsequently 
Rainbow intends to expand into tier -2 cities of Southern India.  
 
Rainbow Children’s Hospital embraces a unique doctor engagement model, where doctors work exclusively on a full -time, 
retainer basis. The doctors work in teams and have 24/7 commitment, which is particularly important for children’s 
emergency, neonatal, pe diatric intensive care services and to support pediatric retrieval services. The Company also 
operates the country’s largest pediatric DNB training programme in private healthcare, offering post graduate residential 
DNB and fellowship programme.
