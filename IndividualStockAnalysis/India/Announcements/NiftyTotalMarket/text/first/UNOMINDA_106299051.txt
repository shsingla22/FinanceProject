Sub: -Clarification on the news published in Media/Publications 
Dear Sir/Ma'am, 
In reference to the news published in Business Standard and other news publications titled "Mahindra, 
Uno Minda eye making rare earth magnets in India as China curbs bite" dated July 10, 2025, the 
Company on its own submits its clarifications as under for reference of all stakeholders. 
"Uno Minda, as a leading automotive components manufacturer, continues to actively engage with
