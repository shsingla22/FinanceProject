Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange 
Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we wish to inform you that the Company has been allocated quantities 
through tender(s) participation for supply of 18.06 crore liters of Ethanol with an

Petrol Programme (“EBPP”) during Ethanol Supply Year from 1st November, 2024 
till 31st October, 2025.
