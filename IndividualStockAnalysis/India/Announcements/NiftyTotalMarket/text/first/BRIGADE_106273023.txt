PRESS RELEASE  
 
PAUL to Bring French Culinary Excellence to Bengaluru in August with First Outlet at Orion 
Gateway Mall  
Bengaluru,  19th June, 2025:  Globally renowned French bakery and café PAUL, is set to make its much -
anticipated Bengaluru debut at Brigade Group’s flagship Mall, Orion Gateway Mall, Rajajinagar in August 
this year. With a rich legacy spanning over 135 years, PAUL, set in a stunning alfresco lakeside setting 
spread across 3,850 square feet brings authentic French gastronomy to the city’s discerning food 
lovers offering a meticulously curated menu featuring timeless French classics, including quiche 
lorraine, croque monsieur, savoury crêpes, rich French onion soup, and caramelized canelés. Other 
delicacies will include handcrafted sourdough Napoletana pizzas, crisp tartines, artisanal pasta, 
wholesome rice bowls, vibrant salads, comforting soups, gourmet sandwi ches, creamy risottos, and an 
array of refreshing beverages, all crafted with the finest locally and internationally sourced 
ingredients. The Parisian styled ambience at PAUL at Orion Gateway is such that it makes for a leisurely 
breakfast, a cozy coffee d ate, weekend brunch, client meetings, or even solo indulgences.   
Commenting on the launch, Sunil Munshi, Senior Vice President – Retail, WTC Trades & Projects Pvt 
Ltd, part of Brigade Group, said, "The upcoming launch of PAUL at Orion Gateway Mall, furthers our 
commitment to delivering globally inspired experiences crafted with innovation and care. At Brigade, 
we continually strive to curate a retail landscape that blend s international excellence with local appeal, 
ensuring our patrons experience the thrill of shopping, dining, and engagement with some of the most 
prestigious global names. With PAUL, we are bringing an iconic French culinary tradition to Bengaluru, 
offeri ng not just gourmet delicacies but an immersive, high -quality experience that reflects our 
commitment to variety, innovation, and global exposure. We are confident that PAUL will quickly become 
a favorite among Bengaluru’s discerning diners, adding to the vibrant mix of brands that make Orion 
Gateway a premier lifestyle destination.”  
Vaibhav Kaushish, Chief Operating Officer, PAUL India, said, “Opening our first outlet in Bengaluru at 
Orion Mall is a significant milestone in PAUL’s India journey. We remain committed to using only the 
finest ingredients, free from palm oil, artificial c olors, and additives. Guided by our philosophy of ‘Eat 
Well, Live Well,’ we champion wellness, sustainability, and mindful indulgence. Bringing this ethos to life 
are our core offerings and cherished French imports that define the brand’s authentic appeal,  such as 
hot chocolate, pain au chocolat, viennoiseries, and croissants. We look forward to sharing the joy of 
genuine French culinary artistry with Bengaluru —one croissant at a time.”  
With its legacy of authenticity, tradition, and craftsmanship, PAUL promises to be a must -visit 
destination for Bengaluru’s food connoisseurs, offering an exquisite taste of France right in the heart 
of the city of Bengaluru.
