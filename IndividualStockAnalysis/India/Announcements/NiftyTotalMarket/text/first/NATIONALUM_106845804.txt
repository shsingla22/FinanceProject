Press Release 
 
NALCO’s proposed Smelter Expansion at Anugola gains 
momentum with key approvals and partnerships in place 
 
Anugola/Bhubaneswar, 04.11.2026:  National Aluminium Company Limited (NALCO), a 
Navratna CPSE under the Ministry of Mines, Government of India, is steadily progressing with 
its proposed brownfield Smelter Expansion Project at Anugola, Odisha, to augment its smelting 
capacity by 0.5 million tonnes per annum (MTPA), nearly doubling the existing capacity from 
0.46 MTPA to approximately 1.0 MTPA. The project entails an approved investment of 
approximately ₹30,000 crore, with key statutory approvals, strategic partnerships and enabling 
initiatives in place to facilitate its implementation. 
Recently the meeting of the Rehabilitation and Peripheral Development Advisory Committee 
(RPDAC) was also held to facilitate the acquisition of requisite land for the proposed Smelter 
Expansion Project at Anugola and to expedite land acquisition, compensation and rehabilitation 
issues. Further, NALCO has entered into an agreement with Emirates Global Aluminium (EGA) 
for licensing its proprietary smelting technology for the Anugola Smelter plant, ensuring 
adoption of globally proven and energy-efficient aluminium production processes. The proposed 
expansion will require an additional 1100 MW of power. To meet this requirement, NALCO has 
partnered with NLC India Limited to establish a 50:50 Joint Venture Company (JVC) for setting 
up a 4 × 270 MW (1,080 MW) captive thermal power plant. In keeping with its commitment to 
sustainability and cleaner energy, NALCO is also envisaging the integration of 200–250 MW of 
renewable energy into the project ecosystem. 
 
The 0.5 MTPA Aluminium Smelter Expansion Project at Anugola is targeted for commissioning 
during 2030-31, significantly enhancing NALCO’s metal production capacity. NALCO’s 
expansion initiatives, including the 5th Stream Alumina Refinery Project at Damanjodi and the 
Smelter Expansion Project at Anugola, are being pursued with the support of the Government of 
Odisha and the Ministry of Mines, Government of India. These projects are expected to enhance 
production capacity, generate employment and promote regional economic growth. NALCO 
remains committed to Odisha’s industrial development through sustained investment, skill 
development and promotion of downstream and ancillary industries. 
 
Notably, the commissioning of the ongoing 5th Stream Refinery is expected to generate surplus 
alumina even after meeting the expanded capacity requirements of the proposed Anugola 
Smelter. While the surplus alumina will initially be directed towards export markets, NALCO is 
proactively exploring suitable locations across India for setting up Greenfield aluminium projects 
as part of its long-term growth strategy and strengthening self-reliance in the sector. In this 
regard, NALCO is also further evaluating the establishment of a new 0.5 MTPA Greenfield 
aluminium smelter in the country, aimed at adding domestic value by converting surplus alumina 
into finished aluminium. This strategic initiative aligns with the Government of India’s vision of
