PVR INOX OPENS A 6 -SCREEN MULTIPLEX IN DEHRADUN AT MALL OF 
DEHRADUN  
~PVR INOX expands presence in  Uttarakhand with 3 cinema s in the state, enhancing the 
cinematic experience in Dehradun~  
 
National, December 13 2024 : PVR INOX Limited, India ’s largest and most premium cinema exhibitor, 
announced the opening of a new 6-screen cinema  at Mall of Dehradun. The new 6-screen multiplex marks 
the brand ’s continued expansion and commitment to providing unparalleled entertainment  experiences 
across India . This marks PVR INOX ’s 3rd  Cinema  in Dehradun and the 3rd cinema in Uttarakhand  expanding 
the screen count in the state to 16 screens . 
With this cinema opening, the company continues to reaffirm its position in North India , offer ing world -
class cinematic experiences to audiences in the region.  
Located 30 minutes from Jolly Grant Airport in Dehradun, the new cinema offers seamless accessibility for 
movie enthusiasts.  The property features six state -of-the-art auditoriums with a total seating capacity of 
937, designed to elevate the viewing experience with premium amenities , leatherette recliners, USB 
chargers, swivel tables, and reclining motors for enhanced comfort.  The cinema is equipped with cutting -
edge cinematic technologies  including 4K Barco projectors, Next gen 3D screens for crystal  clear, razor 
sharp, ultra -bright pictures and advanced Dolby 7.1 sound for high -definition audio .  Additionally, the 
property is designed to be inclusive and accessible, offering specially -abled-friendly seating options and 
dedicated pathways to ensure a comfortable and enjoyable experience for all patrons.   
Commenting on the launch, Mr. Ajay Bijli, Managing Director, PVR INOX Limited , said, “With Dehradun 
emerging as a smart city, the demand for modern, high -quality entertainment is increasing.  We are excited 
to bring our premium cinema experience to this vibrant city. The new PVR I NOX  multiplex will provide 
moviegoers with an immersive environment that perfectly complements the city's growing  infrastructure 
and progressive outlook  and only provides a top -tier movie experience but also mirrors the city ’s forward -
thinking vision by offering the latest in cinematic technology, comfort, and design .” 
The cinema foyer features a modern design with s ophisticated finishes like Symphony grey, Black 
Marquina, and Gold metal, creating an opulent atmosphere. The space seamlessly blends modern 
aesthetics with functionality, offering an inviting and opulent environment for guests.  The property 
features exten sive food and beverage facilities, including well -designed concession counters and kiosks.  
These outlets provide a diverse selection of delectable food and beverages, catering to a wide range of 
preferences and further enhancing the overall movie -going ex perience.  
Expressing his views on the launch announcement, Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli, Executive Director, PVR INOX 
Limited said , “We’re happy  to bring our immersive cinema experience to the city  at the Mall of Dehradun, 
the perfect hub for movie -goers, offering an exquisite combination of  luxury retail, high -end fashion, dining,  
 
 
 
entertainment, and wellness. This prime location enhances the cinema experience, making it the ultimate 
destination f or leisure and relaxation. With this opening, we ’re further strengthening our foothold in North 
India, bringing world -class cinematic experiences to a region eager for premium entertainment options . 
We are certain that the cinema would be a great destinati on for movie goers ”.   
With this opening, PVR INOX has strengthened its growth momentum and has opened 256 screens across 
43 properties in 28 cities since the merger.  
 
About PVR INOX  
 
PVR INOX Limited is the largest film exhibition company in India with 1,749 screens across 355 properties 
in 111 cities (India and Sri Lanka).  
Since their inception, both PVR and INOX have created iconic cinema brands with storied histories of 
achieving significant milestones and setting benchmarks in the film exhibition industry. With a collective 
heritage of transforming out -of-home entertainment in the country, the merged company offers a wide 
range of cinema services such as child -friendly a udis, the latest screening technology, superior sound 
systems, a wide range of F&B offerings, film and non -film content , and array of formats in the premium 
screen category.
