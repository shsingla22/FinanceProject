Press Release  
Karur Vysya Bank Inaugurates Its 900th Branch in Tondiarpet, 
Chennai  
February 6th 2026 
Chennai, Tamil Nadu:  
 
Karur Vysya Bank (KVB), one of India’s oldest and most trusted private sector banks, today 
inaugurated its 900th branch  at Tondiarpet, Chennai . The branch was inaugurated by     
Mr. Ramesh Babu,  Managing Director & CEO of Karur Vysya Bank, marking a significant 
milestone in the Bank’s journey of growth and customer -centric banking.  
Dr. S. Uma , IAS,  Additional Secretary to the Government, Health and Family Welfare 
Department, graced the occasion, along with senior officials of the Bank, customers, and 
well-wishers.  
The launch of the 900th branch underscores Karur Vysya Bank’s continued commitment to 
expanding its physical footprint while remaining deeply rooted in the communities it serves. 
Tondiarpet, a vital and evolving part of North Chennai, is known for its stro ng residential 
base and vibrant commercial and industrial activity, including manufacturing, trade, logistics, 
and fishing communities. Its excellent connectivity through rail, metro, and proximity to 
major ports makes it an important economic hub in the r egion.  
The new branch will offer a comprehensive range of banking services, including savings and 
current accounts, deposits, and loans. Customers will have access to tailored financial 
solutions across retail, MSME, and institutional banking, enabling seamless a nd efficient 
banking under one roof.  
Speaking at the inauguration, Mr. Ramesh Babu , Managing Director & CEO , said that the 
milestone reflects Karur Vysya Bank’s philosophy of balancing strong values and traditions 
with progress. “For over a century, Karur Vysya Bank has grown alongside generations of 
customers by building relationships founded on trust. Even as w e embrace technology and 
modern banking solutions, our focus remains on being accessible, inclusive, and personally 
connected to the communities we serve,” he said.  
He added that the last five years have marked a phase of steady and purposeful growth for the 
Bank, supported by a stronger financial foundation, an expanded branch and digital footprint, 
and a continued emphasis on service excellence. “Our progress has be en driven by the trust of 
our customers, which remains our greatest strength,” he noted.  
The new KVB Tondiarpet branch is designed to combine the convenience of digital banking 
with personalised, face -to-face service. It aims to support local businesses, entrepreneurs, and 
families by being a trusted financial partner in their everyday lives.  The branch is located at 
Karur Vysya Bank, Ground Floor, No 729/2  Tiruvottiyur High Road, New Washermanpet, 
Chennai, 600081.  
 
 
 
With this milestone, Karur Vysya Bank reaffirmed its long -term commitment to Chennai and 
Tamil Nadu, and its belief that meaningful growth comes from staying close to customers 
while evolving with changing times.  
About Karur Vysya Bank  
Founded in 1916, Karur Vysya Bank is a leading private sector bank in India. The Bank has a 
network of 900 branches and over 2,226 ATMs and cash recyclers. As on 31.12.2025, the 
Bank’s total business stood at Rs. 2,11,647 crores, comprising deposits of Rs. 1,14,595 crores 
and advances of Rs. 97,052 crores. The Bank reported a net prof it of Rs. 690 crores for Q3 
FY26, with a Net NPA of 0.19%.
