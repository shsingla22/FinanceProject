Press Release
Navi Mumbai, February 11th, 2025: Privi Speciality Chemicals Limited is India's leading 
manufacturer, supplier and exporter of aroma chemicals. Privi is a globally trusted partner and a 
preferred supplier of speciality aroma chemicals to all the Fragrance and Flavors houses and 
FMCG’s. 
For achieving the highest export performance, the company has been awarded the prestigious 
"Trishul Award" by honourable Union Minister of State for Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers & 
Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India Ms. Anupriya Patel and CHEMEXCIL. 
This award recognizes the industry's top exporter in large sector category, signifying exceptional 
export excellence and representing the highest accolade conferred by CHEMEXCIL.
Commenting on the same, Mr. Mahesh Babani, Chairman & Managing Director, said , 
“We are deeply honoured to receive the prestigious Trishul Award from CHEMEXCIL, which 
recognizes our excellence in exports. This award is a testament to our commitment to quality, 
innovation, and a strong association with global clients. 
In recent years, our robust export performance has showcased our capabilities and the trust our 
global customers place in us. This milestone not only reinforces our dedication to excellence but 
also inspires us to expand our global presence, diversify our product portfolio, and drive 
sustainable growth reinforcing the guidance of our honourable Minister. By continuously adapting 
to evolving market demands and upholding the highest standards, we strengthen our position as 
a trusted, long-term partner in the ﬂavours & fragrance industry. 
Privi touches your life everyday — creating memories, evoking nostalgia, and enriching 
experiences with fragrances. We continue to move forward with the same passion, staying true to 
our values, and delivering excellence to our partners and customers. We extend our heartfelt 
gratitude to CHEMEXCIL, our employees, partners, and all stakeholders for their continuous 
support and trust. This achievement reinforces our vision of establishing Privi as a global leader 
in the aroma chemicals industry and fulﬁl our commitment to make in India policy of the 
honourable Prime Minister of India. ”
