PRESS RELEASE  
  
G E Shipping contracts to sell its 2005 built Medium Range Product Tanker 
“Jag Pranav”  
  
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (G E Shipping) has contracted to sell its 2005 built Medium 
Range Product Tanker, Jag Pranav of about 51,383 dwt on July 23, 2024 to an unaffiliated third party. The 
vessel will be delivered to the new buyer by Q2/Q3 FY25. 
   
Including Jag Pranav, Company’s current fleet stands at 43 vessels, comprising 29 tankers (6 crude carriers, 
19 product tankers, 4 LPG carrier) and 14 dry bulk carriers (2 Capesize, 8 Kamsarmax, 4 Supramax) 
aggregating 3.41 Mn dwt. 
  
Additionally, we had contracted to sell one MR product tanker in May 2024 and buy one MR product 
tanker in June 2024, which are due for deliveries in Q2FY25.
