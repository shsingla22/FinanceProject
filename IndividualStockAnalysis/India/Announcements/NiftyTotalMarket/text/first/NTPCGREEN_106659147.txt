Press Release  
  
NTPC Renewable Energy Limited to develop Solar Power Project on Defence Land at 
Sitapur, Uttar Pradesh  
  
   
NTPC Renewable Energy Limited (NTPC REL), a wholly owned subsidiary of NTPC Green 
Energy Limited, shall be developing a 250 MW Solar Power Project integrated with Battery 
Energy Storage System (BESS) on vacant defence land at Sitapur, Uttar Pradesh.  
The project has been approved by Hon’ble Raksha Mantri Shri Rajnath Singh and is the first 
of its kind large-scale solar power project with BESS to strengthen energy security of defence 
establishments.  
The power generated from the project shall be utilised by various defence establishments across 
Uttar Pradesh.
