Annexure B  
 
Profile of Mr. C Srikanth  
 
Mr. C Srikanth aged 58 years holds a Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical engineering and a 
master’s degree in business administration in marketing. He has more than three decades  
of experience in sales and marketing in precision abrasives business. He was earlier 
associated with the Company heading the Sales and Marketing function of the Abrasives 
business before moving to Wendt (India) Limited. He is currently on the Boards of We ndt 
(India) Limited, CUMI Abrasives and Ceramics Company Limited and Wendt Grinding 
Technologies Limited.  
 
Profile of Mr. Shyam C Raman  
 
Mr. Shyam C Raman, aged 61 years holds a Bachelors degree  in Mechanical Engineering and 
a PGDM from IIM Calcutta. Mr. S hyam has served in various capacities for over twenty -five 
years in Murugappa Group companies since July 1998. After stints as Chief Marketing Officer 
of Cholamandalam Cazenove AMC and as Business Head of Cholamandalam Securities , he 
took over as HR Head o f Cholamandalam Financial Services Group in 2002. In 2007, he was 
transferred to Group Corporate as Head of Group HR, a role that he has held since then.  He 
has been anchoring the careers and development of leadership talent in the Group. He has 
also been  the driving force behind several pan -Group initiatives across the spectrum of HR - 
including performance management, employee engagement, compensation and benefits, 
etc., Since 2020, he has also led the Group Corporate Communications function.
