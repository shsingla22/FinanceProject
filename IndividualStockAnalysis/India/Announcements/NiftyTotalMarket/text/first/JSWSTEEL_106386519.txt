Dear Sirs/Ma’ams, 
 
We write with reference to our ea rlier disclosure dated 31.07.2025, wherein we had informed about the 
order of the Hon'ble Supreme Court dated 31.07.2025 ( Review Order ). The Hon’ble Supreme Court 
has today delivered its judgement ( SC Judgement ) in the appeals filed by the erstwhile promoters and 
certain operational creditors of Bhushan Power and Steel Ltd. ( BPSL ).  
 
The Hon’ble Supreme Court has dismissed the appeal s filed by the erstwhile promoters and certain 
operational creditors and upheld the judgement of the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal dated 
17.02.2020.  
 
The Hon’ble Supreme Court has delivered a landmark judgement concerning one of the larges t corporate 
resolutions in the history of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 ( IBC Code ) and has preserved 
the integrity and sanctity of the IBC Code by upholding the finality of implemented resolution plans by 
successful resolution applicants. 
 
The Hon’ble Supreme Court also noted the substantia l efforts of the Company in resolving and turning 
around BPSL as a profit-making company. 
 
A copy of the SC Judgement is available on  the website of the Ho n’ble Supreme Court.
