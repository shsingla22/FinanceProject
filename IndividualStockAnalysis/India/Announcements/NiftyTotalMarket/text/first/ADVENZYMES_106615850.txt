CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL REVIEW (Y -o-Y basis): 
 
Revenue from Operations:  
Revenue from operations on consolidated basis 
increased to ₹ 2,034 million in Q4 FY26 from  
₹ 1,672 million in Q4 FY25, a growth of 22%. 
 
Profit: 
Consolidated EBITDA during Q4 FY26 stands at  
₹ 632 million as compared to ₹ 456 million during 
Q4 FY25, increase of 39%.

FY26 as against ₹ 435 million in Q4 FY25, an 
increase of 38%.

million during Q4 FY26 as compared to ₹ 267 
million during Q4 FY25. 
 Margin:

at 29% and PAT margin stands at 22% during the 
quarter.

as against ₹ 8 million in Q4 FY25.

Depreciation and Amortization charge for Q4 
FY26 stood at ₹ 102 million as compared to ₹ 101 
million in the same quarter previous year.

₹ 3.84 as compared to ₹ 2.37 in Q4 FY25.

OPERATIONAL PERFORMANCE REVIEW:  
 
Segmental Revenue:  
 
 
The human nutrition segment  grew by 33% to  
₹ 1,281 million in Q4 FY26 as compared to ₹ 960 
million in Q3 FY26. The animal nutrition business recorded growth of 
2%, reaching ₹ 250 million in Q4 FY26, as 
compared to ₹ 244 million in Q3 FY26. 
 
The Bio-Processing segment recorded an 10% 
sequential de-growth, generating ₹  324 million in 
Q4 FY26 compared to ₹ 361 million in Q3 FY26. 
Within this segment, the Food business declined 
by 11% to ₹ 276 million and the Non-Food 
business by 7% to ₹ 47 million, compared to the 
previous quarter. 
 
The Specialized Manufactu ring segment delivered 
a growth of 16%, reaching ₹ 180 million in Q4 
FY26, up from ₹ 155 million in Q3 FY26.

FY26. 
 
International Sales grew by 6% to ₹ 973 million in 
Q4 FY26, up from ₹919 million in Q3 FY26. The revenue grew by 14% in Americas, 12% in Asia (ex-
India), while the Europe and rest of the world 
recorded a de-growth of 2% and 43% respectively . 
International sales represent ed 48% of total 
revenue from operations, down from 53% in Q3 
FY26.
