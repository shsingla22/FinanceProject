KEYS SELECT BY LEMON TREE HOTELS, AMRITSAR  IS NOW OPEN  
 
Strengthens North India footprint  with brand’s third hotel in Punjab  
 
 
New Delhi, 8th May 2026: Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, one of India’s leading hospitality 
companies, announces the launch of its latest property, Keys Select by Lemon Tree 
Hotels, Amritsar . 
 
This opening marks a significant step in the Company’s continued expansion into high -
potential cultural and spiritual destinations across India, further strengthening its footprint 
in North India.  
 
The hotel is located in the heart of the Golden City , offering  convenient  access to Amritsar’s 
most iconic sites, including the Golden Temple and Jallianwala Bagh, both within walking 
distance . Positioned  to cater to the city’s strong pilgrimage and leisure demand, the 
property also offers seamless connectivity, situated approximately 2.5 km from Amritsar 
Railway Station and 13 km from Sri Guru Ram Dass Jee International Airport.  
 
The hotel features 50 well-appointed rooms, designed to offer a comfortable and 
contemporary stay. Guests can enjoy a range of facilities, including Keys Café , an all -day 
dining restaurant serving Indian and international cuisine, along with in -room dining 
services. The property also offers a well -equipped boardroom for meetings and a fitness 
cente r for recreation . 
 
“The launch of Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels, Amritsar, reflects our continued focus 
on bringing branded, reliable hospitality to India’s spiritual and leisure destinations ,” said 
Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd.  “Located in close 
proximity to the Golden Temple and Jallianwala Bagh, and offering contemporary offerings, 
the hotel is well positioned to cater to the city’s growing tourism and pilgrimage demand, 
while further strengthening our presence in North India.”  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited operates a diverse portfolio of hotels across multiple segments. 
Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels is designed to offer a seamless blend of comfort, 
convenience, and contemporary design, catering to the evolving needs of today’s tr avellers 
with warm service and a reliable stay experience.
