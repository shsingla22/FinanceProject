Dear Sir / Ma’am, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI (LODR) Regulations”), we wish to inform the exchanges that Ascent 
Circuits Private Limited  (“Ascent ”), a wholly owned subsidiary of IL JIN Electronics (India) Limited, a Material 
Subsidiary of Amber Enterprises India Limited (“ Amber”/ “Company”)  and a step-down subsidiary of the 
Company, has entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (“MoU”) with Guidance, Government of 
Tamil Nadu , for undertaking an expansion investment in the State of Tamil Nadu. 
 
Under the MoU, Ascent proposes to make an expansion investment of approximately Rs. 500 Crore  over a 
period of five years commencing from the year 2027. The proposed investment is intended to further 
strengthen and enhance Ascent’s Bare Printed Circuit Board (“PCB”) manufacturing capabilities , 
supporting its future growth plans and expanding its manufacturing footprint. 
 
This intimation is also available on the website of the Company at www.ambergroupindia.com .
