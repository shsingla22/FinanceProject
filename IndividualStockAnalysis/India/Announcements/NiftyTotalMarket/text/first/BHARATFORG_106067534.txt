Ref :  Disclosure under Regulation 30 of Listing Regulations  
  
With reference to the captioned subject, we would like to inform you that the Company has 
received e-mail dated November 21, 2024 from National Stock Exchange of India Limited 
(“NSE”) and BSE Limited (“BSE”) with respect to imposition of fine of Rs. 1,59,300 (incl. GST) 
each,  for non-compliance of the Regulation 17(1)  of the Listing Regulations  i.e. half of the 
Board was not independent, for the quarter ended September 30, 2024.  
 
In this regard, i t is hereby clarified to Stock Exchanges  that: 
 
a. The Company is in possession of arms licenses to undertake manufacturing of various 
firearms stated therein. The said licenses are  issued by the Ministry of Home  Affairs (MHA) 
and the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) (collectively 
referred as “Licensing Authorities”) and the Company is required to comply with the 
provisions of Arms Rules, 2016  
 
b. As per rule 55(8) of the Arms Rules, 2016, prior approval of the Licensing Authorities 
is required to be taken for any change in directorship  or Chief Executive Officer of the 
Company.  
 
c. The Company had  accordingly applied to the Licensing Authorities seeking their prior 
approval for inducting Non- Executive Independent Director (s) on the Board of the 
Company . The Company  at present is in possession of approval of MHA on the said 
application and awaits prior approval / no- objection of DPIIT as is required under the extant 
provisions of Arms Rules, 2016.  
 
d. The aforesaid licenses are key for Company’s operations and it is im perative for the 
Company to comply with the provisions of Arms Rules, 2016 and conditions prescribed 
under the licenses, failing which these arms licenses are liable to be cancelled .
