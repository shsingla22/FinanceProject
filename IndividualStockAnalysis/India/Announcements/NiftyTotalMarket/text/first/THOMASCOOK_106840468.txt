TCIL Q2 FY27 profits remain resilient, with PBT * at Rs. 848 Mn despite 
global headwinds . 
 
Mumbai, 2nd November 2026  
 
• Financial Services segment delivered a strong growth of 21% y -o-y for Q2 FY27 and 
14% y -o-y for H1 FY27 in Revenue from Operations , with Education and Corporate 
segments contributing well.  
• India Travel Businesses staged a sharp recovery despite the slow start in July , ending 
the quarter just 6% lower than  last year & EBIT margins at a healthy 3. 8%. Domestic 
and Short Haul segments le d the growth . 
• Overseas D estination Management Services  (DMS) business revenue recovered during 
the quarter despite the continued impact of the West Asia conflict. The effect of the 
conflict impacted operations in  specific geographies , resulting in a drop in Revenue 
from Operations and EBIT margin for the segment.  
• Healthy forward bookings  of 30%  for the current quarter  (Q3 FY27)  for the India 
leisure travel business, signal positive future demand trends, despite elevated airfares 
and other input costs.   
 
Consolidated Financial Performance  
• PBT* for Q2 FY27 held firm at Rs. 848  Mn. Vs Rs. 845  Mn in Q2 FY26    
• Total Income for Q2 FY27 stood at Rs. 20,030  Mn, (5) % y-o-y; H1 FY27 stood at Rs. 41,560  Mn, (9)%  y-o-y 
• The Group continues to maintain a strong financial position, with Cash & short -term investments at Rs. 28, 
430 Mn as of September 30, 2026 vs Rs. 26,488 Mn as of June 30, 2026  
• CRISIL has reaffirmed the rating of ‘CRISIL AA/Stable’ on the long -term bank facilities of TCIL and ‘CRISIL 
A1+’ rating on the short -term bank facilities and short - term debt of the company  
Financial Services  
• Revenue from Operations increased by 21% y -o-y for Q2 FY27 and 14% y -o-y for H1 FY27  
• EBIT grew by 10% for Q2 FY27  and 9% for H1 FY27  
• Overall retail sales grew by a healthy 1 2%, driven by a strong 33% growth in Education, despite a decline in 
the segment nationally.  
Travel Services  
• India Travel Businesses staged a sharp recovery despite the slow start in July and ended the quarter with a 
reduced gap of 6% Vs last year & EBIT margins at a healthy 3.8%. Domestic and Short -Haul segments led  
the growth.  
• The India DMS business grew 16% in a seasonally softer quarter, supported by steady inflows from key 
source markets like UK, Germany, France, Portugal & Japan.  
* PBT excludes exceptional items  
 
• International DMS businesses in specific geographies continue to be impacted due to geopolitical factors 
USA (ATP) and GCC region (Desert Adventures).  
• Healthy forward bookings of 30% for the current quarter (Q3 FY27) for the India leisure travel business, 
signal positive future demand trends, despite elevated airfares and other input costs.  
Leisure Hospitality (Sterling Holidays & Nature Trails)  
• 83 Resorts as of September 2026, up by 20% y -o-y  
• 7 Resorts opened in Q2 FY27 (Highest for a quarter) –  Jagdalpur, Sa ttal, Agra, Varanasi, Corbett, Tadoba and 
Jawai  
• Occupancy & ARR also improved by 52%  and 9% y -o-y, respectively  
• Nature Trails saw a doubling of resorts from 3 to 6  with a 10% increase in revenue, 79% increase in room 
inventory and 19% increase in guest ratio  on a y -o-y basis. Agreements for 3 more resorts have been signed 
to go on stream in the current quarter.  
 
Digital Imaging Solutions (DEI)  
• While DEI continued to be impacted by geopolitical headwinds, cost containment measures initiated in Q1 FY27 helped narrow its losses significantly, from Rs 208 M n to Rs 76 M n in Q2 FY27  
 
Commenting on the results, Mahesh Iyer,  Managing Director & CEO, Thomas Cook (India) 
Limited, said, “Q2 FY27 again demonstrated the resilience of the TCIL Group with PBT * holding 
firm at Rs. 848 Mn despite the continued impact of the West Asia conflict. Disciplined execution 
with a clear focus on margins, helped us offset some shortfall in the travel business. Looking 
ahead, we are optimistic of continued momentum in India leisure travel, with forward bookings 
trending at a healthy 30% growth over last year -  even in the face of elevated airfares and other 
input costs. This is a positive signal for the current quarter and beyond”

Corporate Travel  
• Secured new corporate travel accounts across the Pharmaceuticals & Healthcare, Auto Components, 
FinTech & Digital Payments and Information Technology (IT) sectors in Q2 FY27  
• Total Air and Non -Air volumes grew by 18.15% y -o-y in Q 2 FY27, reflecting sustained momentum across 
corporate travel segments  
• Air volumes recorded growth of 18.2% y -o-y during Q 2 FY27  
• Domestic Air volumes increased by 22.7% y -o-y, driven by continued growth in business travel demand 
across India.  
• International Air volumes grew by 15.26% y -o-y, underlining strong demand for cross -border corporate 
travel.  
• Hotel volumes increased by 81.83% y -o-y, making accommodation one of the fastest -growing segments 
within the Company's corporate travel portfolio.  
• Hotel transactions grew by 36.5% y -o-y, reflecting increasing adoption of managed accommodation 
programs by corporate clients  
• Non -Air volumes and transactions recorded q- o-q growth of 19.2% and 19.9%, respectively, demonstrating 
continued expansion of the Company's diversified travel services portfolio.
