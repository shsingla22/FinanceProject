Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR 
 
The Company vide its  letter and a press release dated 29 March 2025 has disclosed 
to the exchanges the receipt of Intimation of Disapproval (‘IOD’) from the 
Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (‘BMC’) for development of proposed Tower 2 in 
IT Park at Nesco Center, Goregaon (East), Mumbai, detailing information on   the said 
development.   
  
IOD is a crucial preliminary approval from the local authorities for development 
/redevelopment of a property.  
  
Following the receipt of the said IOD and considering the terms and conditions 
mentioned in the said approval, the Board at its meeting held today i.e. 30 June 
2025 has reviewed and approved a capex of upto Rs. 3,500 crs for development of 
Tower 2 in IT Park to be utilised over a period of six years subject to receipt of timely 
statutory approvals.  The funding for this project shall primarily be done from internal 
accruals.  
  
The meeting started at 3.50 pm and ended at 9:30 pm.
