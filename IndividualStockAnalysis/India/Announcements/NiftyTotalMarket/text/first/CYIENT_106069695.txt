PRESS RELEASE 
 
 
Cyient’s Founder Chairman Receives Lifetime Achievement 
Award at London Event of Institute of Directors   
 
Hyderabad, India, November 26, 2024:  Cyient, a global Intelligent Engineering services 
company, is proud to announce that its Founder Chairman, Dr. B.V.R. Mohan Reddy, has 
been honored with the prestigious Golden Peacock Award for Lifetime Achievement 
and Leadership in Technological Innovations by the Institute of Directors (IOD). This 
esteemed recognition was presented to him during IOD’s Annual Global Convention on 
Corporate Governance and Sustainability 2024, held in London recently. 
 
The award recognized Dr. Reddy’s visionary contributions to technology and his lasting 
impact on the engineering and innovation landscape.  
 
Dr. B.V.R. Mohan Reddy, a Padma Shri awardee, is widely recognized for establishing 
Cyient as a global leader in the ER&D industry. Dr. Reddy has worked to elevate Cyient 
from its pioneering origins in the geospatial and engineering services sectors to its 
current stature as a global frontrunner as an Intelligent Engineering services company 
across multiple industries. 
 
He is dedicated to fostering innovation and entrepreneurship and has held key industry 
roles, including former NASSCOM Chairman and CII Southern Region Chairman. He is 
also the Founding Director of T-Hub, the largest incubation centre in India for start-ups 
by the Government of Telangana.  
 
Dr.Reddy also serves as Chairman of the Board of Governors of IIT Hyderabad and IIT 
Roorkee. He is also instrumental in establishing the BVR Mohan Reddy School of 
Innovation and Entrepreneurship (BVR SCIENT) at IIT Hyderabad, which aims to nurture 
and develop world-class innovation and entrepreneurial talent from India in the light of 
the country's ambition to become Atmanirbhar Bharat . 
 
Dr.Reddy has also significantly contributed to nation-building through his commitment 
to social responsibility. He established a directive to donate significantly to charitable 
causes through the Cyient Foundation well before India mandated CSR activities. 
Dr.Reddy’s philanthropic efforts through the Cyient Foundation have yielded notable 
improvements in primary education, livelihood skills, digital literacy, innovation and 
entrepreneurial capacity building, community development, and preventive healthcare. 
Cyient Foundation actively supports 33 government schools across India, including 22 
in Telangana, offering quality education to over 21,000 children annually across India. 
The Foundation’s initiatives empower unemployed rural and urban women through the 
 
Cyient Skill Centers, which have trained nearly 10,000 women in sustainable livelihoods, 
with over 2,000 women and girls gaining skills and employment each year. 
Commenting on the Award, Krishna Bodanapu, Executive Vice Chairman & Managing 
Director, said, “Our Founder Chairman’s unwavering vision, innovative spirit, and 
commitment to excellence have not only shaped Cyient into a global leader but also left 
an indelible mark on the technology and innovation landscape. The Golden Peacock 
Award for Lifetime Achievement and Leadership in Technological Innovations is a well-
deserved recognition of his extraordinary contributions, and we are immensely proud 
to celebrate this milestone with him.” 
 
The Golden Peacock Awards, instituted by the Institute of Directors (IOD), India, in 
1991, are globally recognized as a benchmark for corporate excellence.
