Vikram Solar Readies 9 GW TOPCon Cell Plant at Gangaikondan: First 
Made -in-India Cells in December 2026  
One of India's largest single -site solar cell facilities, co -located with Vikram Solar's module plant at 
Gangaikondan, takes the company from module maker to integrated manufacturer, with wafers next  
Kolkata, 23rd October , 2026: Vikram Solar Limited, one of India's leading solar PV manufacturers, today 
announced that its 9-GW N-type TOPCon  solar cell manufacturing facility at SIPCOT Industrial Park, 
Gangaikondan, Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu, is on schedule to produce its first cells in December 2026. 
The full 9 GW will be commissioned in phases thereafter.  
India has built world -class module capacity in record time, yet the cell inside most Indian modules is still 
imported. Cells are the largest component of a module's bill of materials and the stage where efficiency, 
reliability and cost are decided. Gangaik ondan addresses that dependence directly: at full capacity, it will 
supply high -efficiency TOPCon cells for the majority of Vikram Solar's module production . It will enable  
modules made in India from cell to frame for the country's utility -scale, rooftop, PM Surya Ghar and PM -
KUSUM program s where domestic content requirements apply, as well as  for global customers diversifying 
their supply chains. The company intends to seek ALMM List -II enlistment for cells produced at 
Gangaikondan in due course.  
The cell plant is the second of three phases in Vikram Solar's integration roadmap at Gangaikondan. The 
first, the 6 GW module plant, was inaugurated by the Hon'ble Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu in August 2026; 
and the third  being  a 9 GW ingot and wafer facility, that is board -approved and targeted for FY29. Within 
the cell phase, capacity is planned to expand to 12 GW.  
On completion, Gangaikondan will be one of the few sites in India where ingots, wafers, cells and modules 
are made together. The campus will provide more than 2000 direct & indirect employment . The facility is 
being developed through the company's wholly owned subsidiary, VSL Green Power Private Limited, on its 
nearly 200 -acre integrated campus, where co -location shortens supply cycles, tightens quality control and 
lowers logistics cost.  
Gyanesh Chaudhary, Chairman & Managing Director, Vikram Solar, said: “Indian module makers, 
including us, have built their businesses around imported cell s. Gangaikondan ends that for Vikram Solar. 
Cells are where the value, the technology and the risk in a module sit, and in December we start making 
them here. It is the most important step in our integration journey, and not the last: our roadmap runs from 
wafer to module, on Indian soil.”  
Gangaikondan at a glance  
● Location: SIPCOT Industrial Park, Gangaikondan, Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu; nearly 200 -acre 
integrated campus  
● Cell capacity: 9 GW N -type TOPCon; first cells December 2026; full 9 GW commissioned in phases 
thereafter; planned expansion to 12 GW  
● Module plant on site: 6 GW, inaugurated August 2026  
● Next phase: 9 GW ingot and wafer facility, board -approved, targeted FY29  
● Employment: 2000 direct and indirect employment on campus  
● Developing entity: VSL Green Power Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of Vikram Solar 
Limited
