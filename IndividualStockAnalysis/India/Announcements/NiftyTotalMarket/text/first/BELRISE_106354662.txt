Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
We refer to our earlier intimations dated June 30, 2025, under Regulation 37(6) and Regulation 30 of the 
Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, 
regarding the approval by the Board of Directors for the Scheme of Amalgamation between Belrise Industries 
Limited (“ Transferee Company ”) and its wholly-owned subsidiary, H-One India Private Limited 
(“Transferor Company ”), and their respective shareholders and creditors, pursuant to Sections 230-232 and 
other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 (“ Scheme”). 
 
Following the filing of the Scheme with the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) Benches at Allahabad 
and Mumbai by the Transferor Company and Transferee Company, respectively, the Regional Director, 
Northern Region, issued an order dated August 11, 2025, confirming the change in the registered office of the 
Transferor Company from the State of Uttar Pradesh to the State of Maharashtra. Due to jurisdictional 
challenges and the professional and strategic realignment of the Transferor and Transferee Companies, the 
Board of Directors has decided to withdraw the Scheme as filed with the NCLT Benches and to file a fresh 
joint company application before the NCLT Mumbai Bench. Accordingly, the Transferee Company filed a 
withdrawal memo in this regard with the NCLT Mumbai Bench on August 28, 2025. 
 
This submission is also available on the Company’s website under the tab “ Investor Relations ” at 
https://belriseindustries.com/.
