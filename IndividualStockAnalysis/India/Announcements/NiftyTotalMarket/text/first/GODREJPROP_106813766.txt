Mumbai, October 11, 2026  
Adv. Ashish Shelar Inaugurates First Milestone of Bandra Bay Transformation with Godrej 
Properties and RC Group 
New pedestrian corridor on LK Mehta Marg marks the start of the BMC-led plan for a green, 
walkable waterfront 
Adv. Ashish Shelar, Hon’ble Minister for Electronics, Information Technology, Artificial 
Intelligence, and Cultural Affairs, Government of Maharashtra, and MLA, Bandra West, today 
inaugurated the newly developed pedestrian corridor along LK Mehta Marg. Developed by 
Godrej Properties Limited (GPL) (BSE scrip id: GODREJPROP) and RC Group in partnership 
with the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC), it is the first completed public 
infrastructure project under the Bandra Bay Beautification Initiative. 
Designed to enhance pedestrian safety, ease movement and make Bandra Bay a more 
welcoming, walkable and vibrant address for Mumbai, the corridor is the first step in a larger 
plan for the bay's edge. This plan will add a green promenade, open lawns, a pet park, a quiet 
reading zone and an amphitheatre. A mini golf course and a sports and activity zone are planned 
for the next phase. 
Adv. Ashish Shelar, MLA, said, “Bandra Bay holds a special place at the very heart of Mumbai. 
Inaugurating this new pedestrian corridor - adorned with beautiful trees and lush greenery along 
LK Mehta Marg - is a meaningful step toward making our iconic waterfront accessible to all citizens. 
“This is just Phase 1 of our vision. In Phase 2, we will connect LK Mehta Marg directly to the 
waterfront, bringing a host of thoughtful public amenities along the way. A public space truly 
succeeds when the community embraces it, and I invite everyone to come, walk and experience it. 
“My sincere gratitude to the BMC, Godrej Properties, RC Group, MSRDC and the Mumbai Police for 
their hard work and collaboration in bringing this vision to life safely and seamlessly. Bandra has 
always been one of our city’s most vibrant neighbourhoods, and this milestone sets the foundation 
for a lasting legacy at Bandra Bay.” 
To celebrate the inauguration, the corridor came alive with a morning of community wellness 
by the sea, with families and children joining in. Visitors pedalled stationary cycles that lit up 
with every turn, took on a walking challenge along the waterfront, and joined an open-format 
Zumba session and a gentle yoga class that ended on a calm note. They also tried face painting, 
pottery and diya painting, enjoyed healthy bowls and seasonal fruit juices, and recorded their 
mood on an emotion tracker at the start and end of their visit. For those hours, the street 
became an extension of the bay, a place to move, play and pause with the sea as the backdrop. 
Amitesh Shah, CEO, Mumbai MMR Region, Godrej Properties, said, “For us, creating great 
neighbourhoods goes beyond the homes we build; it extends to the streets and public spaces people 
share. Bandra Bay is one of Mumbai's most loved stretches of waterfront, and we are proud to 
partner with BMC and RC Group to make it more accessible for the community. Today's 
inauguration is a first step, and we are committed to seeing the larger plan through.” 
Anup Singh, Head  - Design & Regulatory, RC Group, said, “This stepping stone reflects the 
power of public-private and community collaboration in creating infrastructure that benefits the 
wider community. The inauguration of this corridor marks the beginning of a larger journey for 
 
Bandra Bay. Our shared vision has been to create a waterfront destination that balances 
accessibility and community engagement, and this brings it one step closer to reality.”
