PRESS RELEASE 
 
Cyient Receives Premier Award for Excellence 
from RTX for the 3rd Consecutive Year 
 
 
Hyderabad, India, July 9, 2024:  Cyient, a global Intelligent Engineering company 
delivering solutions for a digital, autonomous and sustainable future, announced that it 
has been honored with a Premier Award from its customer RTX, for the third 
consecutive year.  
 
RTX has recognized Cyient, Ltd. with the Premier Award for performance in 2023 and 
overall excellence in Cost Competitiveness and Collaboration & Customer Service. The 
Premier Award is an annual recognition platform under the RTX Performance+ Program 
to recognize suppliers with superior performance and that have provided exceptional 
value to RTX in one of the four key categories: Cost Competitiveness, Technology & 
Innovation, Business Management, Customer Service, and Collaboration. 
 
Karthikeyan Natarajan , Executive Director & CEO, Cyient, on receiving the award, said, 
"We are grateful for the trust and collaboration from RTX over the last 23+ years, which 
have been fundamental to our success. This prestigious award  is a testimony to our 
customer centricity, commitment to excellence and solving problems that matter to 
our customers.” 
 
“ With such long standing collaboration, we continue to deliver Intelligent Engineering in 
Aerospace & Defence business for a digital, autonomous and sustainable future. " he 
added. 
 
The award highlights Cyient's commitment to cost efficiency, technological 
advancements, and the highest standards of business management. It acknowledges 
Cyient's ability to collaborate effectively and provide exceptional customer service, 
further solidifying its position as a trusted and reliable partner. Over the course of 23+ 
years of engagement, Cyient offered 32+ million hours of engineering support across the 
product life cycle like design, manufacturing, aftermarket and digital.
