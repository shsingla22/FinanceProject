Pursuant to the amendments introduced in the Income -tax Act, 1961 ('the Act') vide Finance Act, 2020, 
w.e.f. 1st April, 2020, the Company will be required to withhold taxes at the prescribed rates on the 
dividend paid to its shareholders. However, no tax will be deducted on payment of dividend to the resident 
individual shareholder if the total dividend, paid during  the FY 2025 -26, does not exceed ₹ 10,000/ - The 
withholding tax rate would vary depending on the residential status, category of the shareholder and is 
subject to provision of requisite declarations/documents to the Company.
