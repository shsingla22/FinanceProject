Singapore/ Kathmandu, September 26, 202 6: TVS Motor Company (TVSM), part of 
TVS VENU, and a reputed global manufacturer of two and three -wheelers , today 
announced the launch of the TVS NTORQ 150 , Nepal’s  1st Hyper Sport Scooter.  
Powered by a 149.7cc race-tuned engine and inspired by stealth aircraft design , the 
TVS scooter  brings together high performance, sporty aesthetics and cutting- edge 
technology to cater to a new generation of riders. The launch event, held in Naxal, 
Kathmandu, brought together the Jagdamba Motors Team, TVS Motor Team, leading 
automotive media, influencers, dealers, and key stakeholders, offering them an 
opportunity to experience the Hyper  performance, advanced technology, and features 
firsthand . 
 Building on the legacy of the TVS NTORQ brand and its 
reputation for evoking awe, the new TVS NTORQ 150 is 
designed to be an icon of tomorrow . Its MULTIPOINT® 
projector headlamps, aerodynamic winglets, coloured 
alloy wheels and signature muffler note highlight its 
racing DNA. With 50+ smart features, the scooter brings an advanced connected experience to riders in Nepal.  
  
Speaking abou t the launch, Mr. Peyman Kargar , 
President  – International Business , TVS Motor 
Company , said, “At TVS Motor Company, we remain 
committed to bringing innovative, customer -focused 
products to markets around the world. Nepal continues to be a strategically important market for TVSM, driven by a young, vibrant and growing community of riders. 
The TVS NTORQ 150 brings together race- tuned performance, futuristic design, 
advanced connectivity and first -in-segment safety and control features, reflecting our 
commitment to delivering world- class mobility solutions to our customers across the 
world.”

 PERFORMANCE  
The TVS NTORQ 150 is powered by a 149.7cc, air -cooled, O3CTech engine  that 
delivers 13.2 PS at 7 ,000 rpm  and 14.2 Nm torque at 5 ,500 rpm . Accelerating from 
0–60 km/h in just 6.3 seconds and reaching a top speed of 104 km/h , it stands as 
the quickest  scooter in its class.  
 SPORTY & FUTURISTIC DESIGN  
Inspired by stealth aircraft , the TVS NTORQ 150 features MULTIPOINT®  projector 
headlamps, sporty tail lamps, aerodynamic winglets, a stubby muffler with 
signature sound, naked handlebar, and coloured alloy wheels.  
• Forward- biased  stance with aggressive , predatory launch- ready poise.   
• Arrowhead front form  sculpted for aerodynamic efficiency and visual speed.   
• Naked motorcycle- style handlebar , enhancing rider control and deliver s a raw, 
connected feel.   
• Jet-inspired vents  and integrated winglets , reinforcing its race- bred identity.   
• MULTIPOINT®  projector headlamps  offering superior illumination .  
• Signature ‘ T’ tail lamp , distinct  and designed for enhanced visibility.   
• Sport -tuned suspension, lightweight alloys, and a performance exhaust that 
visually and functionally anchor its sporty character.  
 TECH- PACKED FOR THE NEW -AGE RIDER 
Equipped with TVS SmartXonnect ™, the TVS N TORQ 150 offers 5 0+ connected 
features including turn- by-turn navigation, last parked location, call/message and ride 
modes , they bring an advanced connected interface designed around the needs of 
today's riders.  
 SAFETY & COMFORT  
The scooter ensures rider confidence with Single Chanel  ABS and traction control  
(first-in-segment), hazard lamps and follow- me headlamps . Comfort is enhanced 
through telescopic suspension, adjustable brake levers, a patented E -Z center stand, 
and 22L under -seat storage.  
 The TVS NTORQ 150 is now available across Jagdamba Motors dealerships in Nepal in three colour options -  Stealth Silver, Racing Red, and Turbo Blue , at an 
introductory price of Rupees 3,69,900 .
