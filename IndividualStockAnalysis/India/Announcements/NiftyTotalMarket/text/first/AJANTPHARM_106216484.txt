Press Release
PAT up by 11%
(4thQuarter FY 2025 Consolidated Results)
Mumbai, 30thApril, 2025: Ajanta Pharma Ltd., a specialty pharmaceutical formulation company 
today reported its excellent performance for 4thquarter and year ended 31stMarch 2025.
Q4 FY 2025 performance highlights (compared to Q4 FY 2024):
xRevenue from operations at Rs. 1,170 cr. against Rs. 1,054 cr.; up 11%. 
xEBITDA at Rs. 297 cr. against Rs. 278 cr.; up 7%; EBITDA at 25%.
xProfit after tax at Rs. 225 cr. against Rs. 203 cr.; up 11%; PAT at 19%.
FY 2025 performance highligh ts (compared to FY 2024):
xRevenue from operations at Rs. 4,648 cr. against Rs. 4,209 cr.; up 10%. 
xEBITDA at Rs. 1,260 cr. against Rs. 1,172 cr.; up 7%; EBITDA at 27%.
xProfit after tax at Rs. 920 cr. against Rs. 816 cr.; up 13%; PAT at 20%.
xCashflow from operations (C FO) was Rs. 1,157 cr., EBITDA to CFO conversion of 92%.
xFree cashflow (FCF) was Rs. 694 cr., FCF to PAT conversion of 75%.
xROCE stood at a healthy level of 32% and RONW at 25%.
Shareholders pay-out:
During the fiscal year, the company distributed a to tal of Rs. 700 cr. (Rs. 349 cr. as dividend and 
Rs. 351 cr. in the form of buyback). This translates to 76% payout of the total PAT of the year.

About Ajanta Pharma Limited
Ajanta Pharma is a speciality pharmaceutic al formulation company having major focus on
branded generic business across India, Asia & Africa. Comp any has ground presence in each
of these 30+ countries. Many of company’s products are 1st to market and are leading in their
sub-therapeutic segments. Company also has presence in USA in generic business and
institution business in Africa.
Company’s state of the art R&D centre is in Mumbai. Company has 7 world class manufacturing 
facilities located in India.
