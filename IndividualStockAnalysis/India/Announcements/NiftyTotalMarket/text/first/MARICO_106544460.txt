PRESS NOTE  
 
Nihar Shanti Pathshala Funwala  strengthens early 
childhood care educ ation  across 100 Anganwadi centres  
in aspirational district Bahraich  
 
National/ Lucknow , February  25, 202 6: Nihar Shanti Pathshala Funwala (NSPF), Marico ’s flagship 
CSR programme  for education, in partnership with Sesame Workshop India, has strengthened 
foundational early childhood education for over 1,700 + children , across 100 Anganwadi centres in 
Bahraich, Uttar Pradesh - a NITI Aayog aspirational district with comparatively low early childhood 
education levels.  
 
Focuse d on the Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (FLN) , the programme adopts a holistic 
approach  by supporting Anganwadi workers, engaging parents, and working closely with government 
systems  to consistently improve the levels of learning for children . In partnership with Sesame 
Workshop India, this initiative trains Anganwadi workers and parents/caregivers, to deploy play -
based materials, low -connectivity digital tools, and mobilises communities to advance NEP -aligned 
early learning across children aged  3-6 years.  100 Anganwadi workers,  100 helpers and 1500 
parents/caregivers have been enrolled in thi s programme so far.  
 
Commenting on reaching 100 Anganwadi centres,  Amit Bhasin, Chief Legal Officer & Group 
Counsel and Secretary, CSR Committee, Marico Limited , said, “At Marico, we strongly believe 
that strong foundations in the early years are critical to shaping lifelong learning outcomes. Through 
Nihar Shanti Pathshala Funwala, our focus has been on building an ecosystem that empowers the 
people and spaces that are c entral to a thriving childhood.  By aligning our efforts with government 
systems and supporting play -based learning in the early years, we aim to create lasting impact for 
children in underserved communities.”  
 
This programme aims to build school -ready FLN skills , through play -based pedagogy aligned with 
the National Education Policy  2020 and N ational Curriculum Framework  2022.  Key interventions 
include structured training for Anganwadi workers  and helpers , deployment of innovative teaching -
learning materials  through  Chalo!  Sesame Street App , and the use of  Pico projectors deployed 
across clusters to enable  engaging video -based learning in low -connectivity environments . The 
programme also emphasises on community participation through  positive parenting sessions and 
Early Childhood Care and Education ( ECCE ) melas.  
 
As part of the initiative, Marico and Sesame Workshop India also hosted Early Foundations, Bright 
Futures , a multi -stakeholder convening that brought together senior officials from ICDS and SCERT 
to encourage open dialogue, cross -sectoral learning, and shared ownership of ECCE priorities.  
 
This multi -layered , community -centric  approach is designed to be scalable beyond Bahraich and 
aims to support  inclusive teaching, deepen community engagement, and promote classroom 
equity, thereby primin g Anganwadis as vibrant hubs for holistic growth  for children .  
 
Driven by a deep sense of purpose, Nihar Shanti Pathshala Funwala (NSPF) , Marico's long -standing 
education  initiative , works to create meaningful societal impact by improving access, quality, and
