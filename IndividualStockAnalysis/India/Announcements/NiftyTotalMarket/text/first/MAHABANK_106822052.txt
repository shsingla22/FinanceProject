About Our Methodology  
Our ESG assessment framework is built on a structured database comprising 30 key factors, over 130 
KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) and 350+ data points, covering both industry -agnostic and industry -
specific aspects, to enable a granular evaluation of ESG  performance of a company. Ratings are 
assigned on a scale of 0 to 100, where higher scores indicate stronger ESG performance. Our 
methodology relies primarily on publicly available disclosures and sustainability reports made by 
companies, with key data inputs sourced from the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report 
(BRSR), as well as globally recognized reporting framew orks including the Global Reporting Initiative 
(GRI), Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB) and the Carbon Disclosure Project (CDP), 
wherever such reports are available. Pillar scores are calculated using a weighted average of key factor 
scores,  with weights derived from a materiality matrix developed through in -depth research.  
The ESG performance of Bank of Maharashtra relative to industry average  practices , along with the 
rationale for the selection of peers, is presented separately in the Annexure to this report.
