Brief Profile Mrs. Dipali Sheth is currently and Independent Director at Advent 
International's DFM Foods Ltd., Latent View Analytics Ltd., Adani Wilmar 
limited and Adani Airport Holdings Limited, Welsupn Corp Limited, 
Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited, UTI Pension Fund Limited, Llyods 
Offshore Global Service Private Limited. She is a strategic Advisor to  
start-ups. 
 
She has previously served on the Board of UTI Asset Management Company 
Limited, Welspun Specialty Solutions Limited and Bharat FIH Limited. 
 
She is a seasoned HR, Marketing and Sales professional with previous 
experience in Standard Chartered Bank for 12 years, where she joined in 
Learning and Development, moved onto Head Resourcing and Talent, Head 
HR Wholesale Bank and included experience in the Standard Chartered 
acquisition of Grindlays. She then left after twelve years of rich and diverse 
roles as Head HR South Asia to join ABN Amro/RBS  
Prior to working in Standard Chartered Bank, she has worked in Procter 
and Gamble India Ltd, where she was the first woman leader to be hired in 
sales in Procter & Gamble India. 
  
Mrs. Dipali is committed to "giving back" and contributed from 2018 to 
2020 to Ashoka University's Strategic vision and was an invitee on their 
Board. She keenly supports several charitable organizations. 
 
She is a B.A. (Hons.), Economics, from University of Delhi.  
 
Mrs. Dipali Sheth brings credibility, integrity, stature and expertise within 
the HR domain and will add tremendous value to the Board.
