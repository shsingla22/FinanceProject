standalone level and other information in the public domain.  
The ESG pillars for the Company reflect continued progress across key performance areas. The 
Environmental score is supported by year on year reductions in greenhouse gas emissions  and energy 
intensity , reduced carbon emissions and higher w aste recycling & recovery . The Social score is driven 
by strong workplace safety performance, zero employee fatalities, comprehensive health insurance 
coverage for permanent employees, improved gender diversity , and CSR spending that exceeds 
regulatory requirements. The Governance score is supported  by a well -structured Board and 
Committee s framework with strong Independent Director representation, compliance with Board
