proposed resolutions are restricted only to remote e -voting (“e -voting”) i.e. by casting votes electronically 
instead of submitting postal ballot forms. The instructions for e -voting are appended to this Postal Ballot 
Notice under the head ‘Instructions for e -voting’ . 
 
Members holding equity shares of the Company on the Cut -off Date mentioned in this Postal Ballot Notice 
are requested to carefully read the instructions mentioned under the head ‘Instructions for e -voting’ in this 
Postal Ballot Notice and record their assent (“FOR”) or dissent (“AGAINST”) on the proposed resolution
