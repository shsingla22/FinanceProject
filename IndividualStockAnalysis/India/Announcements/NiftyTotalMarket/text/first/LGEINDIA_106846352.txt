term growth prospects for the Company.

Further, the brief details of alteration in MOA as required under Regulation 30 read with Part A of the 
Schedule III of the SEBI Listing Regulations  are as follows: 
 
Insertion of new Sub Clause 7 after Sub Clause 6 under the Main Object of Clause III (A), as set

“7. To carry on the business of manufacturing, assembling, importing, exporting, distributing, 
trading, selling, supplying, installing, integrating, servicing, maintaining and providing solutions 
relating to automotive and mobility technologies, products, components, systems and software, 
including in-vehicle infotainment systems, head units, cockpit electronics, automotive displays, head -
up display (HUD), digital cockpits, cent ral information displays (CIDs), audio video navigation 
(AV/AVN), cluster, telematics, telematics control units (TCUs), communication and networking 
systems, advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS), automotive vision and sensing systems, 
wireless/5G connectivity, antennas, driver and occupant monitoring systems, adaptive cruise control, 
emergency braking and steering systems, traffic sign recognition systems, adaptive lighting and 
lighting assistance systems, in -cabin solutions, vehicle computing and electronic control systems, 
High-performance computing (HPC), Electronic control units (ECUs), central control units (CCUs), 
body control units (BCUs), domain control units (DCUs), cockpit domain controllers (CDCs), vehicle 
electrification and power electronics components, human -machine interface (HMI) solutions, 
automotive software, including vehicle operating systems, middleware, applications, software 
platforms, automotive contents platform, cloud -based solutions, software -defined vehicle (SDV) 
solutions, cybersecurity solutions and other electronic, electrical, digital and intelligent solutions for 
vehicles and mobility applications and to undertake related testing, technical support and other allied 
activities.”
