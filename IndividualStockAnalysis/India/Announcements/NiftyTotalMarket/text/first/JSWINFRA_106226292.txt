Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
In connection with the aforesaid subject and in acco rdance with the paragraph 3(7)(iii) of the SEBI 
Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/P/CIR/2023/18 dated February 3, 2023 read with master circular for 
compliance with the provisions of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 by listed entities bearing reference no. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 (“Circular"), Sajjan Jindal Family Trust 
(through its trustees Mr. Sajjan Jindal and Mrs. Sangita Jindal  India), as a Promoter, proposes to sell 
certain Equity Shares of the Company in the open market  for complying with a portion of the minimum 
public shareholding by the Company  ("Proposed Sale "). 
 
Accordingly, we intend to sell up to a maximum of 2% of the total paid up equity share capital of the 
Company, aggregating to 4,20,00,000 Equity Shares, in a single or multiple tranches, over a period 
beginning from May 13, 2025 or onwards till March 31, 2026, subject to any other permissible methods 
of meeting minimum public shareholding in terms of the applicable law. 
 
In this regard, we, on behalf of the Promoters a nd Promoter Group, undertake that we shall not buy any 
Equity Shares of the Company in the open market on the dates on which the Equity Shares of the Company 
are proposed to be sold by us, in accordance with the Circular for the purpose of achieving minimum 
shareholding requirements in accordance with Rule 19(2)(b) and 19(A) of Securities Contracts 
(Regulation) Rules, 1957 and Regulation 38 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 . 
 This undertaking  is issued to enable you to submit to the Stock Exchanges along with the intimation of 
Proposed Sale.
