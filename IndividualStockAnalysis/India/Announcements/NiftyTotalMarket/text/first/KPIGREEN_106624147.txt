Annexure A  
Brief Profile of Mr. Rajesh Shrivastava : 
 
Mr. Rajesh Shrivastava joins as a seasoned business leader with nearly 37 years of distinguished 
experience across the power, infrastructure, and renewable energy sectors. Over the course of his 
career, he has led some of India’s largest and most complex e nergy transition, EPC, and 
infrastructure development initiatives, with deep expertise spanning renewable energy, thermal 
power, strategic planning, project execution, technology integration, organizational building, and P&L 
leadership.  
Rajesh has been associated with more than 30 GW of power projects across fossil fuel and renewable 
energy domains, including over 10 GW of renewable energy projects under development and 
execution. He is widely recognized for his ability to build large -sca le businesses with a strong focus 
on scale, speed, sustainability, and execution excellence.  
Prior to this role, Rajesh served as President & Business Head – Renewable Energy Development at 
Reliance New Energy, where he played a pivotal role in planning one of the world’s most ambitious 
renewable energy ecosystems in Kutch, involving 150 GWp of so lar capacity and large -scale BESS 
infrastructure. He has been instrumental in establishing the organization, driving technology strategy, 
and overseeing massive land and infrastructure development programs spread across nearly 60,000 
acres.  
Earlier, as CEO of Aditya Birla Renewables, he led the rapid scale -up of the renewable business 
portfolio from 600+ MW to over 3.0 GW within two and half years, while shaping the Group’s long -term 
decarbonization and green energy roadmap.  
During his tenure as COO of Adani Green Energy, Rajesh successfully led execution of three of India’s 
largest hybrid renewable projects comprising approximately solar and wind capacity at a single 
location during the challenging Covid period. He also contr ibuted significantly to the strategic vision 
of scaling the business toward 25 GW capacity and introducing enterprise -wide digital project 
management systems.  
His earlier leadership roles include senior positions with Toshiba JSW Power Systems, Toshiba India, 
LANCO Solar, TPSCI, and NTPC, where he managed large EPC projects in India and abroad, global 
engineering operations, manufacturing initiatives, and multid isciplinary teams across India and 
international markets.  
Rajesh is known for combining strong engineering fundamentals with strategic business leadership 
and innovation. He has led multiple first -of-their -kind initiatives involving renewable hybrid systems, 
large -scale land infrastructure, digitization platforms , technology innovation, and next -generation 
energy solutions including Hybrid with PSP and battery storage.  
A Gold Medalist in Civil Engineering and an M.Tech in Structures from IIT Mumbai, Rajesh has also 
completed executive management studies at INSEAD Singapore. He was recognized among the Top 
15 Global Leaders by Toshiba Corporation, Japan, for his outstandi ng leadership contributions.  
Rajesh brings with him a collaborative leadership philosophy centered on empowering teams, 
nurturing future leaders, and building high -performance organizations capable of delivering 
transformational growth.

Annexure B  
Brief Profile of Mrs. Venu Birappa:  
 
Mrs. Venu Birappa brings nearly four decades of experience in the Power Sector to the Board. She 
holds a Bachelor's Degree in Electrical Engineering from Maharaja Sayajirao University Baroda, a 
Diploma in Management from Indira Gandhi National Open University, and a Bachelor of Laws 
(General) from Saurashtra University. She has also comple ted the Quality Management Systems       
Auditor /Lead  Auditor Training based on ISO 9001:2005.  
 
Mrs. Venu Birappa has expertise in regulatory, legal, financial, commercial, and technical 
aspects of transmission, system operation, and distribution. She began her career in 1984 with 
the erstwhile Gujarat Electricity Board (GEB) and later served as Exec utive Engineer (Regulatory 
& Commerce)at  Gujarat Energy Transmission Corporation Limited (GETCO) until July 2021.  
 
Mrs. Venu Birappa has played a key role in the formulation of regulations under the Electricity 
Act, 2003, and has actively handled matters related to transmission agreements, capital cost 
approvals, tariff regulations, open access, intra -state ABT, and re newable energy. She has 
represented GETCO in regulatory and judicial proceedings before the Gujarat Electricity 
Regulatory Commission (GERC), the Appellate Tribunal for Electricity (APTEL), the High Court, 
and the Supreme Court of India.  
 
Her vast knowledge and strategic insights into the power sector make her a valuable addition to 
the Board.
