CarTrade Tech and Spinny  Partner to Transform Used  Car Buying and Selling   
 
Mumbai, July 29, 2026 : CarTrade  Tech Limited, one of India’s leading digital marketplace companies, today 
announced a strategic partnership with Spinny, a leading full -stack used -car platform, aimed at transforming 
the way consumers purchase and sell used cars in India.  
 
Consumers selling their cars on CarWale and OLX India, will get a differentiated selling experience with this 
partnership  of CarTrade and Spinny.  Spinny will sell  quality -assured used -car on CarWale and OLX India. 
The partnership brings together OLX India and Car Wale’s automotive buyers and sellers, technology and data 
with Spinny’s operational capabilities.  
 
Using CarTrade’s proprietary VAYA AI, CarWale and OLX India will connect users instantly with relevant 
Spinny offerings. More than 23 million consumers come to OLX India and CarWale to buy or sell used cars 
annually.  By integrating Spinny’s proposition the companies aim to enhance the customer journey and make 
the entire buying and selling experience simpler for users on Car Wale and OLX platforms.  
 
Commenting on the partnership, Vinay Sanghi, Chairman and Managing Director, CarTrade Tech , said:  
“Our ambition is to fundamentally improve the way used  cars are bought and sold  in India. CarWale  and OLX 
India bring together a large base of consumers actively looking to buy and sell used cars. With this partnership, 
we can make the transaction more seamless and trusted. The partnership is aligned with CarTrade Tech’s 
broader strategy of building a n integrated automotive ecosystem that supports consumers throughout the 
vehicle ownership journey of buying, selling and ownership.”  
 
Niraj Singh, Founder and CEO, Spinny , said:  
“Buying a car is an important and deeply personal decision. At Spinny, our focus has always been on making 
the experience simple, transparent and dependable. Through this partnership with CarTrade Tech, we will be 
able to engage with more customers through  CarWale and OLX India and offer them a trusted way to complete 
their used -car purchase. Together, we have an opportunity to set new standards for used -car marke t in India.”
