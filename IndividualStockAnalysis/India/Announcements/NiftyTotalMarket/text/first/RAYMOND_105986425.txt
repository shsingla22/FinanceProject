Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, this is to inform that the Board of Directors of Raymond Limited  
(‘the Company’) at their meeting held today i.e., September 12, 2024, have approved to invest an 
amount up to Rs. 100 Crore in the form of Redeemable Preference Shares in one or more tranches in 
Ten X Realty West Limited (“TXRWL”), a step down wholly owned subsidiary of the Company. 
 
The terms and conditions of Redeemable Preference Shares would be mutually decided and agreed 
at the time of issue. The amount proposed for investment would be utilized for the purpose of 
execution of redevelopment project by TXRWL. 
 
Details as required under Regulation 30 of Listing Regulations read with Circular no.
