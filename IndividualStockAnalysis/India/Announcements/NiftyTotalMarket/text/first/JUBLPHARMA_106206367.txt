Re: Intimation under Regulation 30(11) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“the Listing Regulations”) by Jubilant Pharmova Limited 
(“the Company”)  
 
 - Update on material development about the disclosure made on February  25, 2025 
  
Dear Sirs,  
 
With respect to the disclosure made by the Company on February 25, 2025 about certain media reports 
on the registration of a FIR by the Police regarding certain allegations made against the Chairman,     
Mr. Shyam S. Bhartia, we would like to update you of material developments in the matter.  
 
The Police officials have filed a Closure Report as the allegations against him have been found to be 
baseless and false. The Hon’ble Court after examining the Closure Report and the statements given 
by the Complainant has passed an Order accepting the said Closure Report. Therefore, there is no 
case against Mr. Shyam S. Bhartia.  
 
Mr. Shya m S. Bhartia, in his personal capacity, in an earlier statement to the Company had denied all 
the allegations being baseless, false and disparaging.  
 
The Company had also reported that this case did not have any material impact on the Company or its 
operations.   
 Date and time of occurrence of event -  April 19, 2025 at 04.00 p.m. IST.  The above- mentioned 
intimation will be simultaneously posted on the Company's website at www.jubilantpharmova.com .  
 
We request you to take the same on record.
