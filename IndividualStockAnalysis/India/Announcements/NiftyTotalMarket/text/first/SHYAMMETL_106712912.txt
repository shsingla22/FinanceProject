Shyam Sel and Power Limited Commissions 8.90 MWp Captive Solar Power Project at 
Jamuria 
 Expands renewable energy portfolio with a combination of 4.60 MWp CAPEX and 
4.30 MWp OPEX models.  
 Reinforces commitment to sustainability, energy cost optimization, and carbon 
footprint reduction.  
Kolkata, India — July 28, 2026  — Shyam Sel and Power Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary 
of Shyam Metalics and Energy Limited, today announced the successful commissioning of an 
8.90 MWp Solar Power Project  at its manufacturing plant located in Jamuria, West Bengal . 
This milestone represents a significant step forward in the group's ongoing initiatives to 
transition toward greener energy sources and reduce its environmental footprint across 
operational facilities.  
Key Highlights of the Project:  
 Total Installed Capacity:  8.90 MWp  
 Execution Breakdown:  
o CAPEX Model:  4.60 MWp  
o OPEX Model:  4.30 MWp  
 Location:  Jamuria, West Bengal  
 Primary Objective:  Captive consumption to optimize operational power costs and 
support green energy integration.  
Mr. Brij Bhushan Agarwal, Chairman & Managing Director, Shyam Metalics and Energy 
Limited, said: "The commissioning of the 8.90 MWp solar power capacity at Jamuria 
underscores our steadfast commitment to sustainable manufacturing and clean energy 
transition. By adopting a blended strategy of both CAPEX and OPEX models, we are effectively 
balancing capital efficiency with long-term cost optimization while reducing our carbon 
emissions."
