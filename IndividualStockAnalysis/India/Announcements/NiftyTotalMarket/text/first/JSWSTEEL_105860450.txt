Sub: Climate Action Report  
 
JSW Steel Limited is pleased  to announce the release of its first "Climate Action 
Report"  marking a significant progress towards bringing transparency in its climate action 
strategy. Through this report the Company  aims to provide an honest and comprehensive 
overview of its progress in reducing its CO2 emissions and  the Company’s  role in 
contributing to global climate change mitigation efforts.  
 
Key highlights of the report include:  
1. The Company’s Commitment to reduce CO2 emissions and achieve net neutral in 
carbon emissions for all op erations under our direct control by 2050.  
2. Detailed insights into the Company’s  transition pathways towards achieving its 
commitment, highlighting technologies and solutions that are being worked upon.  
3. Description of  climate related risks and opportunities including the scenario analysis 
aligned with the recommendations of the Task Force on Climate -related Financial 
Disclosures (TCFD).
