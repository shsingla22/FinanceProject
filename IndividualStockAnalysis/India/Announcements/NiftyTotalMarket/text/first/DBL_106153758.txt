We would like to inform that Dilip Buildcon Limited (the “Company” or “DBL”) worked as EPC Contractor 
for DBL Bangalore Nidagatta Highways Private Limited (DBNHPL) wherein DBL had certain claims with 
DBNHPL under the EPC Contract. DBNHPL referred the claims to National Highways Author ity of India 
(NHAI) for conciliation and amicable settlement for resolution, seeking certain reliefs to which it was 
entitled due to various factors encountered during the execution of the Six laning of Bangalore -Nidagatta 
Section, Km.  18+000 to Km.  74+2 00 of NH -275 in the State of Karnataka to be executed on Hybrid Annuity 
Mode. The Project was awarded under the Hybrid Annuity Mode through the Concession Agreement dated 
April 20, 2018.  The matter was sent to the Conciliation Committee of Independent Exp erts (CCIE) for 
resolution.  
 
During the conciliation proceedings, the Conciliation Committee of Independent Experts (CCIE) decided on 
the Disputes and the Parties reached to an amicable settlement on the following disputes, formalized 
through a Settlement Agreeme nt: 
 
1. Damages arising from delays in the provision of the Right of Way (ROW).  
2. Compensation for additional costs incurred due to the extended stay during construction and after 
the issuance of the Provisional Completion Certificate (PCC).  
3. Compensation for Change -In-Law.  
4. Release of withheld Goods and Services Tax (GST).  
5. Restoration of annuity and interest due to the consideration of only the PCC length.  
6. Compensation for price escalation for work executed from the PCC stage to final completion.  
7. Payment of additional Interest During Construction.  
 
DBNHPL and NHAI signed a settlement agreement for an amount Rs. 98 .30 Crore s wherein Rs. 91 .25 
Crore s is relating to the claims of DBL to DBNHPL.  
 
In accordance with the Settlement Agreement the NHAI is required to disburse the agreed amount to 
DBNHPL within 30 days as a full and final settlement of the claims.
