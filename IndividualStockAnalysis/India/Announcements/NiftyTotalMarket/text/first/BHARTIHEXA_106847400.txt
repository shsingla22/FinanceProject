Gurugram , India, November  05, 2026: Bharti Hexacom  Limited (“Bharti Hexacom ” or “the Company”) today announced its 
audited results for the  quarter ended Sep 30, 2026  
 
Q2’27  Performance:  
 
Bharti Hexacom delivered a nother quarter of  strong performance, with revenues increasing  to Rs 2,582 crore , up 11.4%  YoY and 
2.9%  QoQ . 
 
Mobile revenues increased 9.9%  YoY, driven by our  focus  on winning with quality  customers  and improving portfolio mix . ARPU 
improved to Rs 264 in Q2’27  as compared to  Rs 251 in Q2’26 .   
 
Our smartphone  data customers base  expanded  steadily , with 1.2 million  new customers  added over the past year , representing a  
YoY increase of 5.2% . Mobile data traffic for the quarter surged to  2,526 PBs representing a YoY increase of 30.6% . 
 
During the quarter, we enhanced our  Postpaid plans by adding  Apple’s newly expanded iCloud+ , which now also includes  Apple TV 
and Apple Arcade, offering greater value and strengthening our differentiated postpaid proposition . 
 
We deployed  411 towers  over the last one year to strengthen coverage and enhance customer experience .

About Bharti  Hexacom  
 
Bharti Hexacom is a communications solutions provider offering mobile, fixed -line telephone , Wi-Fi and IPTV services to customers in the Rajasthan 
and the North -East telecommunication circles in India, which comprises the states of Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagala nd 
and Tripura.  The company  offers  its services under the brand ‘Airtel’.  Airtel is a global communications solutions provider with over 650 million 
customers in 15 countries  across India and Africa. Airtel also has its presence in Bangladesh and Sri Lanka through its associate entities. For more 
details visit www.bhartihexacom.in
