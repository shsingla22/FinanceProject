Dear Sir/ Ma’am, 
 
This is in furtherance to our letter dated January 20, 2025, wherein we had informed that our wholly-owned 
subsidiary, Paytm Cloud Technologies Limited, (“PCTL”) will incorporate its wholly owned subsidiary in 
Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA).  
 
We have now been informed by PCTL on July 17, 2025 at 2.11 p.m. (IST) that it has incorporated a wholly 
owned subsidiary named “Paytm Company” in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia on July 17, 2025.  
 
Further, the Board of Directors of PCTL, in its meeting held today, has approved a subscription amount of 
85,00,000 Saudi Riyal towards the acquisition of 8,500 equity shares of 1000 Saudi Riyal each at par in Paytm 
Company.
