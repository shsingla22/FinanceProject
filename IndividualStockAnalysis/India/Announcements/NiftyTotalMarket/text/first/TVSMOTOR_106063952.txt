TVS MOTOR COMPANY LAUNCHES TVS APACHE RTR 160 4V 
WITH NEW FEATURES AND TECHNOLOGY  
 TVS Apache, one of the fastest growing sports motorcycle brand reinforces its leadership by 
introducing an upgrade to its TVS Apache RTR 160 4V to further strengthen the brand’s 
offering packed with innovation 
 The motorcycle comes equipped with a robust 160cc engine, 37mm USD suspension, and 
advanced features like segment-first ride modes, delivering exceptional control and stability 
 A leader in the 160cc segment, TVS Apache 160 4V has been consistently delivering 
exceptional performance, making it the most loved motorcycle in its segment 
 The motorcycle will be available at an attractive price of INR 1,39,990 (ex-showroom New 
Delhi) for all colours 
Bengaluru, November 19, 2024 : Known for delivering on its commitment to 
innovation and technology, TVS Motor Company, a global leader in two- and three-
wheeler vehicles introduces a refreshed TVS Apache RTR 160 4V with new features 
and technology. Equipped with features tailored to meet the evolving needs of 
today’s riders, TVS Motor reaffirms its commitment to bringing high-performance 
motorcycles with cutting-edge technology, refined aesthetics, and enhanced 
capabilities to the market. 
This latest edition of the TVS Apache RTR 160 4V offers a powerful blend of cutting-
edge tech integration, improved performance, and unmatched aesthetic appeal for 
customers, and is designed to cater to a new generation of riders who seek style, 
agility, and precision in every ride. 
Engineered for Agility 
Equipped with a 159.7cc, oil-cooled, fuel-injected, 4-valve engine that generates 
17.55 PS at 9,250 rpm and 14.73 Nm of torque at 7,500 rpm, the TVS Apache RTR 
160 4V offers an impressive power and smooth handling. Its segment-first 37mm 
Upside Down (USD) suspension and three distinct ride modes—Sport, Urban, and 
Rain—enhances control, stability, and adaptability across diverse riding conditions. 
This advanced performance setup enables riders to manoeuvre confidently, whether 
on open highways or navigating city streets. 
Effortless Commuting, Elevated by Innovation 
The latest edition of TVS Apache RTR 160 4V is packed with innovative rider-friendly 
features. TVS SmartXonnect  TM technology enables Bluetooth connectivity, providing 
turn-by-turn navigation, call and SMS alerts, and voice assist. Glide Through 
Technology (GTT) supports a seamless and smooth ride in dense city traffic, while 
adjustable brake and clutch levers enhance comfort, catering to varied rider 
preferences. 
A Palette to Stand Out
