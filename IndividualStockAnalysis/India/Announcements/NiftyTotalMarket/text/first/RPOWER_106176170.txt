Subject: Rejection of application for Reclassification of Promoter/Promoter Group to public 
category under Regulation 31A(9) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
We refer to application no. 206316, submitted by Reliance Power Limited('Company ') seeking 
Reclassification of one of the Promoter/Promoter Group to public category namely Reliance Capital 
Limited under the provisions of Regulation 31 A(9) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. 
It is observed that the NCLT Order submitted by the Company does not specifically mentions about 
the reclassification of Reliance Capital Limited from the company i.e. Reliance Power Limited. 
On seeking clarification on the above by the Exchange, Company informed that there is no such 
order ofNCLT on reclassification of Reliance Capital Ltd as promoter from the Company. 
On the basis of the above facts the Company's application for Reclassification of Promoters/Promoter 
Group to public category filed does not meet the condition as stated under Regulation 31 A(9) of SEBI 
(LODR) 2015 hence the same is rejected. 
In addition to the above, the Company is advised to disseminate this letter immediately to the Stock 
Exchange(s).
