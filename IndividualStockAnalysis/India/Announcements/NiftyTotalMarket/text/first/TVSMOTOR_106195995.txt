and has been shaping India’s motorsport landscape by discovering and training emerging 
talent . The championship provides expert mentorship and national -level exposure, helping 
racers refine their skills and advance Indian motorsports.

TVS One Make Championship –  Category Overview  
 
• TVS Rookie  OMC  (Launched in 2022):  Designed as an entry point for young and aspiring 
racers, the Rookie Category allows riders to gain hands -on experience on race- spec TVS 
Apache RTR 200 motorcycles. Since its launch, over 100 riders have benefited from this 
initiative.  
• TVS RR310 OMC (Launched in 2018): Designed for experienced racers, this category 
features the high- performance TVS Apache RR 310 race bike. Over 300 racers have 
participated in this program, with many progressing to national and international championships.  
• TVS Young Media Racer Program (YMRP):  Launched in 2017, YMRP offers young 
automotive and motorsports journalists, along with influencers, a firsthand racing 
experience. Participants undergo rigorous training, covering both theory and practice, 
before competing on race- spec TVS Apache RTR 200 4V motorcycles. This initiative 
sharpens their riding skills, deepens their understanding of motorsports, and reinforces 
TVS Racing’s commitment to nurturing talent within the media community.  
• TVS Women’s OMC (Launched in 2016):  TVS Racing became the first Indian 
manufacturer to introduce an exclusive racing platform for women, promoting gender 
inclusivity in motorsports. With over 500 trained riders, the initiative provides expert 
coaching and race- prepped TVS Apache RTR 200 mot orcycles.  
 
For over 43 years, TVS Racing has been at the forefront of Indian motorsports, continuously 
pushing the boundaries of racing excellence. This commitment to innovation and performance 
extends beyond the racetrack into TVS Motor Company’s flagship motorcycle brand—TVS 
Apache, which celebrates 20 years of engineering excellence this year . The Apache series 
embodies TVS Racing’s ‘Track to Road’ philosophy, integrating race- derived technology into 
high- performance motorcycles. Spanning 160cc, 180cc, 200cc, and 310cc models, the lineup 
features segment -first innovations, from ride- by-wire te chnology to race -tuned fuel injection.  
 
With over 400,000 riders in the Apache Owners Group (AOG), TVS Apache has built a 
passionate global community, bringing the thrill of racing to streets and track enthusiasts alike. 
As it enters its next chapter, TVS Apache remains dedicated to offering cut ting-edge 
performance, redefining what it means to ride with power and precision.  
 
About TVS Racing  
 
TVS Racing, a pioneer in building Motorsports in India since 1982, has been integral in 
growing the racing performance culture and has been pivotal in engineering the TVS Apache 
series through the company’s “Track to Road” strategy. The brand has redefined the sports 
segment by focusing on race performance, making it a highly desirable product for sports 
enthusiasts. This has resulted in TVS Apache and TVS NTORQ 125 becoming the fastest 
growing brands in their respective segments. TVS Racing is also the pioneer of the One Make 
Championship in the country, the first Indian manufacturer to introduce the series in India in 
1994.
