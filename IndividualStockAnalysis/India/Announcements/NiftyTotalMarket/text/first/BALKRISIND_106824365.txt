Sub: 	Intimation under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations

Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("Listing Regulations"), we wish to inform you that 
Balkrishna Industries Limited ("Company") has today, i.e. 22 October 2026, entered into the following 
agreements with Pirelli Tyre S.p.A. ("Pirelli"): 
a Framework Agreement setting out the structure, timelines and key terms of the overall transaction 
between the Company and Pirelli, including inter alia in relation to a joint venture agreement to be entered 
into between them in future for establishment of a joint venture company in India, to manufacture 
passenger car radial tyres, in which the Company and Pirelli will each hold 50% of the share capital 
("Framework Agreement"); 
an Offtake Agreement for the manufacture by the Company, at its PCR Plant in Bhuj, Gujarat, of Pirelli-
branded passenger car radial tyres as per Pirelli's specifications and their sale to Pirelli and/or its affiliates 
("Offtake Agreement"); and 
a Service Agreement under which Pirelli will provide the Company with certain technical support services 
relating to BKT branded tyre testing, using inter alia Pirelli's proving grounds, simulation systems and 
equipment ("Service Agreement"). 
The details required under the Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/P0D2/CIR/P/202 3/120 dated July 11, 2023 (as amended) are enclosed as Annexure A and 
Annexure B.
