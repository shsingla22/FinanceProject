Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
This is pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations 2015 and further to our intimations dated April 24, 2024 regarding 
Board approval for entering into a joint venture and Signing of joint venture agreement, with Aisin Asia 
(Thailand) Company Limited, Thailand (“AA-T”) and AISIN Automotive Haryana Private Limited, India, 
(“AHL”) (AA-T and AHL collectively referred to as  ‘AISIN Group Companies ’), for marketing and selling 
of the Independent After Market (“IAM”) parts for passenger cars, through a joint venture company (“ JV 
Co.”), which will be incorporated after execution of Joint Venture Agreement (“ JVA”). 
 
We wish to inform you that the JV Co. was incorporated under the name of AISIN ASK India Private 
Limited and accordingly the Company infused Rs, 51,00,000/- (Rupees Fifty One Lakhs) as the 
subscription money for 5,10,000 (Five Lakhs and Ten Thousand) equity shares of Rs. 10/- (Rupees Ten) 
each. 
 
Additionally, we would like to inform you that ASK Automotive Limited has acquired an additional 
1,02,00,000 (One Crore and Two Lakhs) Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- (Rupees Ten) each for an amount 
aggregating to Rs. 10,20,00,000/- (Rupees Ten Crores and Twenty Lakhs) in AISIN ASK India Private 
Limited, a subsidiary company, on January 24, 2025. 
 
In view of the above and pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read along with SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 
November 11, 2024, the detailed disclosure has been enclosed in Annexure-1 for your information and 
record. 
 
Thanking you.
