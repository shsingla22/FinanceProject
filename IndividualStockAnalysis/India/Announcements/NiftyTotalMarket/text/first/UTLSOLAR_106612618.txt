Requirements) Regulations, 2015  (“Listing Regulations”) - Intimation regarding a fire incident  
Dear Madam/ Sir,  
Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of Listing Regulations, as amended from time to time, this is to inform  
that a significant fire accident took place at a Company’s manufacturing facility situated at Bawal Industrial Area, 
Rewari, Haryana, in the late hours of May 06, 2026.  At present, the fire has been largely brought under control, 
and the Company’s team, along with the local fire department, is actively engaged in fully extinguishing the same 
while ensuring the safety of all personnel and mitigating any further damage . 
 
We are relieved to inform that there have been no reports of any loss of human life or serious injury to employees/  
workers due to the incident. All personnel present at the facility were evacuated safely.  
 
The exact cause of the fire is presently under investigation and the Company is extending full cooperation to the 
concerned authorities. The Company is in the process of assessing the extent of damage/  loss caused by the 
incident and evaluating its impact on operations.  
 
Further, the affected assets are adequately insured and the Company has already intimated the insurance  
authorities in this regard. Necessary steps are being taken to minimize business disruption at the earliest.  
 
The Company shall provide further updates, as and when any material development occurs in relation to the 
aforesaid incident.  The details as required under the SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/ HO/49/14/14(7)2025 -CFD -
