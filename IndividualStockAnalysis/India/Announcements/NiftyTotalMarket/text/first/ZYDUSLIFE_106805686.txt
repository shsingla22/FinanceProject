Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
In our earlier intimation dated June 26, 2026, Zydus Lifesciences Limited (“the Company ”) 
intimated about execution of Share Subscription and Shareholders’ Agreement (“ SSSHA”) 
with Sunshine Healthcare Lanka Limited (“ Sunshine ”) and Zydus Sunshine Lifesciences 
(Private) Limited (“ Zydus Sunshine ”) to subscribe 50% of the total paid-up equity share 
capital of Zydus Sunshine (“ Subscription Shares ”). It was also informed that post 
subscription of the Subscription Shares, Zydus Sunshine shall become a 50:50 joint 
venture of Zydus and Sunshine. 
 
This is to inform that Zydus and Sunshine are still in the process of completing condition 
precedents of the SSSHA. In view of the same, and as per the terms and conditions of the 
SSSHA, the Company and Sunshine have mutually decided to extend the date of closing. 
 
The Company and Sunshine have today, i.e. October 1, 2026, executed a letter to extend 
the date of completion as above. In view of the same, the transaction is expected to be 
completed by December 2026.
