Dear Madam / Sir,  
 
JSW Hydro Energy Limited and JSW Energy (Kutehr) Limited, wholly owned step-down 
subsidiaries of the Company, have filed writ petitions before the Hon'ble High Court of Himachal 
Pradesh challenging the constitutional validity and legal sustainability of the  Himachal Pradesh 
Land Revenue (Amendment) Act, 2025, the  Himachal Pradesh Land Revenue (Special 
Assessment) Amendment Rules, 2025, and the demand notices issued pursuant thereto by the 
State of Himachal Pradesh.  
 
The impugned legislation seeks to levy land revenue assessed as a percentage of the "Average 
Market Value" of the hydroelectric projects. Similar writ petitions have also been filed by other 
companies having hydro power plants in State of Himachal Pradesh. The Company has been 
advised that the levy is a charge on electricity generation infrastructure rather than on land, and 
is constitutionally infirm on multiple independen t grounds.
