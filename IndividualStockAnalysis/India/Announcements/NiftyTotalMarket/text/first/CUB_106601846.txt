Financial Performance Q4 FY 26 / FY 25: 
 
1. Net Interest Income 
 
The Bank earned Net Interest Income of Rs.786 Cr for Q4 FY 26 registering 31% 
growth compared to Rs.600 Cr in Q4 FY 25. For year ended FY 26, the NII had grown 
by 22 % and stood at Rs.2,830 Cr as against Rs.2,316 Cr in the last year.

 2. Non Interest Income 
 
Non Interest Income of the Bank for Q4 FY 26 was at Rs.290 Cr as against Rs. 251 Cr 
for Q4 FY 25 registering 16% growth. For FY 26, it stood at Rs. 1,039 Cr as against Rs. 
898 Cr for FY 25 registering 16% growth. Loan processing charges and insurance 
commission are the major contributors towards the growth in other income this year. 
 
3. Operating Expenses 
 
The operating expense is Rs.497 Cr for Q4 FY 26 and Rs.1,854 Cr for FY 26 as 
compared to Rs. 411 Cr in Q4 FY 25 and Rs.1,535 Cr for FY 25.  
 
4. Operating Profit 
 
The Gross Profit for Q4 FY 26 had grown by 31% and improved to Rs.580 Cr compared 
to Rs. 441 Cr in Q4 FY 25. For FY 26 the operating profit was at Rs.2,014 Cr as against 
Rs. 1, 679 Cr in the last year with a growth of 20%. Operating profit growth is in tune 
with the business growth. 
 
5. Provisions 
 
The Bank made a provision of Rs.688 Cr as of 31.03.2026 as against Rs.555 cr in the 
corresponding period last year.  
 
6. Net Profit 
 
The PAT had increased by 25% and increased to Rs.360 Cr in Q4 FY 26 from Rs.288 Cr 
in the corresponding quarter last year. This is the highest PAT we had achieved in a 
single quarter. The PAT for FY 26 is at Rs.1,326 Cr compared to Rs.1,124 Cr in FY 25 
showing 18% growth.  
 
7. Deposits 
 
Total Deposits of the Bank had increased by 23% and increased to Rs.78,308 Cr in     
FY 26 from Rs. 63,526 cr in corresponding period last year. CASA portion stood at 28% 
to total deposits. Cost of Deposits decreased to 5.70% in FY 26 from 5.85 % in FY 25.

8. Advances 
 
Total Advances increased by 26% for FY 26 to Rs.66,699 Cr from Rs. 53,066 Cr in FY 
25. Average Credit Deposit ratio stood at 85%. The yield on Advances for Q4 FY 26 
marginally improved to 9.80% compared to 9.73% in Q3 FY 26.    
 
 
9. Asset Quality 
 
The Gross NPA for FY 26 was reduced to 1.91% from 3.09% in last financial year. Both 
GNPA and NNPA had shown sequential decrease for the past 8 quarters. Net NPA had 
decreased to 0.68% in the current quarter from 1.25 % in FY 25. The provision 
coverage ratio (PCR) as of 31.03.2026 is at 84% (including T/W) and 65% (excluding 
T/W).  
 
10. Net Interest Margin 
 
Net Interest Margin is at 3.74% for FY 26 which is broadly in line with our guidance 
levels given earlier. 
 
11. Return on Assets 
 
Return on assets for FY 26 is 1.56% which is in line with our long-term average levels. 
 
12. Return on Equity 
 
Return on Equity has improved to 13.35% in FY 26 compared to 12.63% in FY 25.  
 
13. Cost to Income Ratio (CIR) 
 
Our cost to income ratio for FY 26 had stood at 47.93% as compared to 47.77% last 
year.   
 
14. Capital Adequacy 
 
The Bank’s capital adequacy as on 31.03.2026 as per Reserve Bank of India (RBI) 
guidelines on Basel III norms is 21.92% and Tier-1 capital adequacy was 20.82%, well 
above regulatory requirements.

About the Bank: 
 
City Union Bank is a scheduled commercial old generation private sector bank regulated 
by the Reserve Bank of India.  It has today on April 27, 2026 has inaugurated 1000th 
branch at Ammachathiram Kumbakonam spreading its geographical presence across 18 
States and 4 Union Territories. It is professionally managed and governed. City Union 
Bank has contemporary technology and infrastructure including ATMs, Net Banking, 
Mobile Banking, E-Wallet, Social Media Banking for personal as well as business banking 
customers. The Bank positioned itself as a banker to SMEs, Agri and Traders apart from 
all other banking activities.
