Sub: Board Comments on fine levied by the Stock Exchanges - Intimation. 
**** 
As advised by the Stock Exchange vide email dated 27.05.2026, the subject matter of the 
Non-compliance of Corporate Governance requirements under Regulation 17(1), 18(1), 
19(1)/19(2), 20(2)/(2A) and 21(2) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for the Quarter ended 
31st March ,2026 and the penalty as levied by the exchange was placed before the Board of 
Directors at the meeting held on 29th May, 2026. 
 
The Board noted the action taken by the Stock Exchanges, involving levy of fines, for  
non-compliance of Corporate Governance requirements prescribed under the  
SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, pertaining to Composition of the Board of Directors 
including failure to appoint Independent director and Women director and non-compliance 
with the constitution of Audit committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee, 
Stakeholder Relationship Committee and Risk Management Committee during the  
Quarter ended 31st March, 2026.  
 
The Board while noting the above, advised to send a communication to the Ministry of Coal, 
being the Administrative Ministry, to appoint requisite number of Independent Directors 
(including Independent Women Director) on the Board of the Company, in compliance to 
SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 and other applicable provisions in order to avoid imposition 
of penalty by stock exchanges in future.
