Dear Sir / Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we 
wish to inform that ATLANTA Electricals Limited (“the Company”) has received a Debarment Letter from one 
of its ex-customers, South Bihar Power Distribution Company Limited (“SBPDCL”), in relation to a purchase 
order executed during FY2023-24, debarring the Company for a period of two years. 
The said communication was received by the Company on 13.05.2026 and pertains to the supply of 4 Nos. 10 
MVA, 33/11 kV ONAN Power Transformers against a purchase order value of less than X3 Crores. 
The company would like to clarify that this matter does not relate to any on-site failure or noncompliance. It 
relates to post-delivery short-circuit testing conducted at CPRI, Bhopal, on one randomly selected transformer 
from the supplied lot in May’24. During the testing process, the transformer successfully withstood all short- 
circuit shots and subsequently met all major electrical, dielectric, thermal, and post-test performance 
parameters. However, during internal inspection after completion of testing, certain observations relating to 
the displacement of insulating components were recorded by the testing agency. 
Based on the said observations of short circuit report, SBPDCL issued a Show Cause Notice dated 22.08.2024, 
to which the Company submitted detailed technical replies and representations on multiple occasions, 
including on 27.08.2024, 30.10.2024, and 08.01.2025. Since then, the Company has continued technical 
discussions and representations with the concerned officials, explaining its position that: 
o The transformer had successfully cleared all critical test parameters. 
« No operational abnormality or unsafe condition had been observed. 
o The matter did not involve any established design failure or systemic quality issue; and 
o Transformers of the same design supplied earlier are operating satisfactorily in the field. 
After the submission of the letter on 08.01.25, the Company had not received any further communication in 
the matter for a considerable period thereafter and was in discussions with the customer for technical 
resolution and reconsideration. Subsequently, the Company has now received the aforesaid Debarment Order 
dated 24.04.2026, which was received by the Company on 13.05.2026.
