Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para- A of Part -A of Schedule- III of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, 
it is to inform that National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and BSE Limited (BSE) vide their letters /email  dated 21
st August , 2024  have levied fine of Rs.  5,36,900 /- each (incl. GST) on the Company for 
the non- compliance with Regulation- 17(1) i.e., half of the Board was not independent  including women 
Independent Director  for the quarter ended 30th June , 2024. 
 The Company, vide its letter dated 22
nd August , 2024 , has clarified to NSE and BSE that Rail Vikas Nigam 
Limited  (RVNL)  is a G overnment company as per provisions of the Section 2(45) of the Companies Act, 
2013. As per provisions of the Companies Act, 201 3 and Articles of Association of the Company, the 
President of India vests with the power to appoint Directors (including Independent Director) on the Board of RVNL . All Directors in RVNL  are appointed by the Government of India through its Administrative Ministry, 
Ministry of Railways (MoR) and RVNL  has no role to play in the appointment of any Director .  
 The fine levied by NSE and BSE has no impact on the financial, operation or other activities of the Company .
