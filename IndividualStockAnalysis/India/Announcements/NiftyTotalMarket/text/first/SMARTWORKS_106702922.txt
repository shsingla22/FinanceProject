Smartworks Expands L&T Technology Services Engagement with 1,100+ Seats in Pune  
Multi -city client portfolio with Smartworks expands to over 2,750 seats; combined expected rental revenue 
~INR 115 Cr.  
Gurugram, 20 July 2026:  Smartworks Coworking Spaces Limited ("Smartworks " or "the Company"), 
India's largest managed office platform by total area under management, has expanded its engagement 
with L&T Technology Services (LTTS), a global leader in AI, Digital & ER&D Consulting Services, through 
the addition of over 1,100 seat s in Pune.  
With this expansion, LTTS's managed office portfolio with Smartworks has grown to over 2,750 seats across 
cities, reflecting the continued growth of a long -standing, multi -city enterprise engagement.  
The combined expected rental revenue from these locations is estimated at ~INR 115 Cr., which includes 
the Pune expansion of ~INR 55 Cr. for a tenure of 60 months.  
Commenting on the development, Neetish Sarda, Founder and Managing Director, Smartworks,  said:  
"Enterprise workplace decisions are increasingly being driven by flexibility, speed and the confidence to 
scale. Our managed campuses deliver exactly that. This expansion reflects the trust our clients place in our 
ability to deliver customised  workplaces with operational excellence and the agility to support their evolving 
business requirements."  
This expansion is consistent with Smartworks' strong positioning in the large -format enterprise segment. 
Notably, clients with more than 1,000 seats contribute approximately 37% of Smartworks' total rental 
revenue.  
This engagement further reinforces Smartworks ' leadership in this segment, where enterprise clients 
contribute ~90% of total revenue, reflecting the Company's position as the platform of choice for large 
organisations seeking managed, scalable workspace solutions.  
 
About Smartworks Coworking Spaces Limited  
 
Smartworks is India's largest managed office platform by total area under management, with a footprint of 
~16.1 million square feet across 66 centres  in 15 cities across India and Singapore, as on March 31, 2026. 
The Company partners with developers to transform large, bare -shell assets into fully managed, enterprise -
grade campuses. Smartworks primarily serves mid -to-large enterprises and counts 770+ c lients, including 
Fortune 500s, Forbes 2000 names, GCCs, MNCs, and high -growth Indian businesses. The Company also 
offers SmartVantage , a GCC -focused solution combining scalable campuses with a curated ecosystem of 
partners across legal, compliance, talent, and operational support - enabling rapid and compliant expansion 
in India. Smartworks listed on NSE and BSE on 17 July 2025.
