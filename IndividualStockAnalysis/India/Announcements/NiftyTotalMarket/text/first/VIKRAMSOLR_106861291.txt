 Vikram Solar Reports Q2 & H1 FY27 results;  
EBITDA per watt improves by 37% QoQ;  
Strengthens Business Mix and Order Inflow;  
9 GW Cell Facility on Track to Produce First Cell in December 2026  
 
Kolkata, India, 14 November 2026: Vikram Solar Limited, one of India's leading solar photovoltaic module manufacturers, today 
announced its financial results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026 . While volumes saw a transitory dip,  
Vikram  Solar made measurable progress during Q2 FY27 in improving the quality of its business mix, strengthening order inflow 
and advancing backward integration. DCR contribution nearly tripled sequentially, distribution and retail became a larger par t 
of volume s, and absolute EBITDA per watt improved by approximately 37% over Q1 FY27. The Company also added 
approximately 1.7 GW of new orders during the quarter.  
Operating Highlights  
• Revenue from Operations increased 17.7% from H1FY26 to H1FY27.  
• Absolute EBITDA per watt improved by 37% sequentially despite lower quarterly volumes.  
• The 9 -GW N -type TOPCon cell facility at Gangaikondan is on schedule to produce its first cells in December 2026; full 
capacity will be commissioned in phases thereafter.  
• 1.7 GW of new orders added in Q2 FY27, compared to 0.8 GW in Q1 FY 2027.  
• DCR share increased from 7.5% in Q1 FY27 to 22% in Q2 FY27.  
• Distribution and retail volumes increased from 14% to 44%, supporting better realization and shorter cash cycles.  
• Net debt  / equity stood at 0.17x as of September 30, 2026.  
 
A Better, More Diversified Mix  
The quality of volumes improved during Q2 FY27. DCR's share rose from 7.5% in Q1 FY27 to 22%, while distribution and retail 
increased from 14% to 44%. At the same time, our dependence on IPP and government volumes have reduced. The change in 
mix supported better realization, quicker cash cycles and 37% sequential increase in EBITDA per watt.  
 
Order Book Focused On Convertibility  
The Company added ~1.7 GW of new orders in Q2 FY27 across EPC, government, IPP, C&I and mid -market customers, compared 
to just 0.8  GW in Q1 FY27, building order visibility to support higher utilization of its expanded capacities in the coming quarters .  
 
9 GW Cell Facility: First Cells Expected in December 2026  
The Company’s 9 GW N -type TOPCon solar cell manufacturing facility at Gangaikondan, Tamil Nadu, is on schedule to produce 
its first cell in December 2026. The full 9 GW capacity will be commissioned in phases thereafter. The facility is expected t o 
strengthen supply -chain control and traceability, support future DCR volumes, and reduce dependence on imported cells. The 
cell facility is a key part of the Company's backward -integration roadmap.  
 
9 GW Ingot and Wafer Facility: Completing the Integration Chain by FY29  
Building on the cell line , the company plans to set up 9 GW of backward -integrated ingot and wafer capacity at the same 
Gangaikondan site by FY29, extending its integration from module to cell to wafer and transforming Vikram Solar from a 
component provider into a fully integrated solar manufacturer. Th e facility decisively addresses India’s national imperative for 
supply -chain indigenization and reduced import reliance.  
 
  
VSL PowerHive: 7.5 GWh BESS Plant at Oragadam  
The Company’s wholly owned subsidiary, VSL PowerHive, is establishing a 7.5 GWh per annu m Battery Energy Storage System 
(BESS) assembly plant at Oragadam, near Chennai, Tamil Nadu, scalable to 15 GWh in later phases. Spread across approximately 
1.65 lakh sq. ft., the facility is slated for inauguration in January 2027, with commercial operati ons expected from April 2027. 
Building on this, PowerHive will  also enter cell manufacturing with 7.5 GWh of capacity from 2028, taking it a step closer to a 
fully integrated cell -to-system battery ecosystem in India.  
 
Mr. Gyanesh Chaudhary, Chairman and Managing Director, said:  “Q2 FY27 was a period in which we strengthened the 
foundations of the business. During the quarter, we were able to significantly improve our revenue mix, which is reflected in  the 
improvement in our EBITDA per watt.  Our DCR share nearly tripled and we added close to 1.7 GW of new orders.   
A major near -term milestone is our 9 -GW N -type TOPCon cell facility at Gangaikondan, which is scheduled to produce its first 
cell in December 2026. This investment will strengthen o ur domestic supply chain, support higher DCR participation and give us 
greater control over technology, cost and product availability, and it comes at precisely the moment our reach in the market is 
expanding fastest.  
Our focus for H2 is clear: convert ex ecutable orders, improve asset utilization, deliver increased DCR volumes and commission 
the cell project in a disciplined manner. We remain positive about the long -term opportunity, but we will measure progress 
through execution and cash generation.”
