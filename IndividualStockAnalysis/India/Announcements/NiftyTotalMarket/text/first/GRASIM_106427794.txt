inter-alia , has approved the acquisition of 26% of equity stake in each of the following Special 
Purpose Vehicles (“SPVs”), formed/ to be formed as a Captive User for renewable energy 
projects: 
 
a. For Vilayat Plant, Gujarat: SPV formed with Prozeal Green Energy Limited (“Prozeal”), 
namely Pro-Zeal Green Power Eighteen Private Limited.  
 
b. For Balabhadrapuram Plant, Andhra Pradesh:  SPV to be formed with Prozeal Green 
Energy Limited.  
 
c. For Ganjam Plant, Odisha: SPV to be formed with GMR Energy Limited (GMR).  
 
The details required as per SEBI Master Circular are enclosed in Annexure A to C respectively.  
 
The meeting commenced at 12:00 noon (IST) and concluded at 01:45 p.m. (IST).
