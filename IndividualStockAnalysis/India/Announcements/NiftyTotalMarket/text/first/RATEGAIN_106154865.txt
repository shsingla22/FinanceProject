Noida, February 18, 2025: RateGain Travel Technologies Limited (RateGain), a global 
provider of AI-powered SaaS solutions for the travel and hospitality industry , announced 
that it has been recognized as an Expedia Group Elite Connectivity Partner for 2025. This marks 
the third consecutive year that RateGain has achieved this distinction, reinforcing its commitment 
to providing seamless, high-quality connections that drive revenue and operational efficiency for 
hoteliers worldwide. 
 
Expedia Group recognizes and rewards a select few connectivity partners based on technical 
excellence, high-performance connections, and the ability to help lodging partners grow their 
business on the Expedia marketplace. In 2025, only the top 2% of Expedia Group’s global 
connectivity partners earned Elite status.  
 
Expedia Group’s extensive network spans 200+ websites, native apps, and 90,000 travel agencies, 
giving RateGain-connected hotels unparalleled access to a diverse and high-value traveller base. 
 
Bhanu Chopra, Founder and Managing Director at RateGain, commented on this 
achievement , “Being recognized as an Expedia Group Elite Connectivity Partner for the third 
year in a row is a testament to RateGain’s focus on innovation and customer success. As the 
industry evolves, connectivity and smarter distribution are becoming essential for hoteliers. We 
remain committed to delivering solutions that help our partners maximize bookings, revenue, 
and guest experiences.” 
 
This recognition further strengthens RateGain’s position as a leader in hotel distribution 
technology, connecting 191,000+ properties to 400+ demand partners and processing 200 
billion+ ARI updates annually. 
 
RateGain’s industry-leading solutions continue to receive global recognition, reinforcing its 
commitment to innovation and excellence in hospitality technology. The company was 
recognized at the 2025 Hotel Tech Awards as one of the world’s top technology providers for 
hoteliers, further cementing its leadership in space.
