We are pleased to announce the receipt of a order from EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and 
Construction) Contractor for approximately 3190 Cr for the supply of HDPE Pipe Products in Power 
Duct application projects carried in: 1. Amaravathi Development Corporation Limited, and 2. Andhra 
Pradesh Capital Region Development Authority. 
Considering this order, the Company’s cumulative order book for HDPE Pipe segment stands at 
around %280 Cr. The company’s installed manufacturing capacity enables it to produce HDPE Pipe 
Products of 2450 Cr (approximately) annually. HDPE Pipe products are voluminous in nature; 
therefore the company has four (4) manufacturing facilities across India: {one (1) in the Western 
Region (Silvassa, U.T.), two (2) in the Southern Region [Gummidipoondi (near Chennai), Tamil Nadu 
and Medchal (near Hyderabad City), Telangana], and one (1) in the Eastern Region [Amta (near 
Kolkata), West Bengal]}. 
In FY 2024-25, company’s revenue from the HDPE Pipe Products segment stood at around %283 Cr, 
reflecting robust demand and execution capabilities. With substantial investments undertaken by 
the Government authorities in India’s infrastructure and smart city development projects, the 
Company anticipates the Indian pipe market to grow at a Compounded Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) 
exceeding 20%, offering major opportunities for sustained growth and market expansion. 
You are requested to take note of the same.
