We would like to inform that the Company – Brigade Enterprises Limited (‘ BEL’) has entered into an 
Investment Framework Agreement with International Finance Corporation (‘ IFC’), pursuant to which the 
Company and IFC have agreed to invest in eligible middle income housing projects. Pursuant to the 
Investment Framework Agreement and in accordance with related transaction documents (including upon 
fulfilment of agreed conditions precedents), Venusta Ventures Private Limited (a wholly owned subsidiary 
of BEL) proposes to issue unsecured, rated, redeemable, rupee-denominated non-convertible debentures, in 
one or more tranches, to IFC that are proposed to be listed on the wholesale debt market segment of BSE.
