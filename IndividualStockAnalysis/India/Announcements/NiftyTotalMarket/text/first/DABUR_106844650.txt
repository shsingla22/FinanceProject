Subject: Resignation as Whole-time Director and Global Chief Executive Officer 
Dear Mohit, 
Further to our recent conversations, please accept this letter as my formal resignation as Whole-time 
Director on the Board of Dabur India Limited and as Global Chief Executive Officer. 
I have accepted a leadership role overseas, effective 16 November 2026. I would be grateful if the 
Board could relieve me before that date, and I will ensure that the handover is complete and seamless 
before I leave. 
Since I joined Dabur as a Management Trainee more than three decades ago, I have watched it grow 
from a '1;250-crore company into a transnational enterprise with a footprint in over 100 countries and 
revenues of more than '1;13,000 crore. Along this journey, from Management Trainee to Global CEO, I 
have had the privilege of building brands and businesses from scratch into multi-crore profit centres. 
I have also had the honour of leading talented, multicultural teams across the Americas, Europe, 
Africa, the Middle East, Asia and India. It has been immensely satisfying to see these businesses 
flourish, and to see our brands become ones that consumers around the world trust and love. 
I am deeply grateful to the Burman family, the Board and my colleagues for their unwavering 
confidence in me, and for the ever-larger responsibilities placed in my hands over the years. Together, 
we have made Dabur an iconic brand and the world's largest Ayurvedic company. 
I believe this is the right moment in my career to take on a new challenge. I leave with pride and 
gratitude, knowing that the strong legacy, clear strategy and highly capable leadership team in place 
will continue to steer Dabur to new heights. 
Thank you for an extraordinary journey. I will always remain a proud ambassador for Dabur.
