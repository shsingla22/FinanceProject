Press Release 
MRPL Flags Off Its First Toluene Parcel 
Mangalore, 16 January 2025: The first truck parcel of Toluene from Mangalore 
Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL) was flagged off by Shri Mundkur 
Shyamprasad Kamath, Managing Director of MRPL, at the Aromatic Complex within 
the Mangalore Special Economic Zone Limited (MSEZL). 
The event was graced by the presence of Nandakumar Velayudhan Pillai Director 
(Refinery), BHV Prasad, Executive Director (Projects), B. S udarshan Executive Director 
( Refinery) Yogish Nayak S, CFO and other senior Officers of MRPL, marking a 
significant milestone in the company's journey towards achieving self-reliance in 
chemical production. 
The launch of MRPL's Toluene product was recently officiated by the Hon' ble Minister 
of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Shri Hardeep Singh Puri, during his visit to the refinery. 
MRPL's entry into Toluene production is a pivotal move toward reducing India's 
dependency on imports and strengthening the nation's industrial and economic 
development. This aligns with the Government of India's vision of Atmanirbhar 
Bharat (Self-Reliant India).
