PRICE SENSITIVE INFORMATION 
 
I. INTRODUCTION  
 
This Code of practices and procedures of fair disclosure of unpublished price sensitive 
information (“ Code”) has been adopted by Hero MotoCorp Limited (hereinafter 
referred to as “the Company ”) pursuant to Regulation 8(1) of SEBI (Prohibition of 
Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 (“ PIT Regulations ”), as amended from time to 
time.  
 
II. OBJECTIVE 
 
This Code has been framed with an aim to: 
 
i) ensure fair, uniform and transparent disclosure of unpublished price sensitive 
information (“ UPSI”). 
ii) preserve the confidentiality of UPSI and prevent its misuse. 
iii) create information symmetry amongst all stakeholders. 
 
III. APPLICABILITY 
 
This Code shall be applicable to all the Insiders (defined hereinafter) who have access 
to UPSI relating to the Company. 
 
IV. EFFECTIVE DATE 
 
This Policy is effective from October 28, 2026. 
 
V. OVERSEEING AND CO-ORDINATING DISCLOSURE  
 
The Board of Directors of the Company may from time to time at its discretion 
designate a senior officer as the Chief Investor Relations Officer (CIRO) who shall be 
responsible for compliance of this Code. In the absence of the CIRO, the Chief Financial 
Officer shall be deemed to be the CIRO of the Company. The CIRO may be contacted 
via email at: investor.relations@heromotocorp.com  
 
If USPI is accidentally disclosed without informing the CIRO, the person responsible 
may immediately inform the CIRO. In such event of inadvertent, selective disclosure 
of UPSI the CIRO shall take prompt action to ensure such information is generally 
available. 
 
The Board has nominated the Company Secretary and Compliance Officer to deal with 
dissemination of information and disclosure of UPSI to the stock exchanges, subject 
to prior consultation with the Chief Executive Officer or Chief Financial Officer or the 
CIRO.

VI. CODE  
 
Code of practices and procedures for fair disclosure of UPSI is set out below:  
 
a. Prompt public disclosure of UPSI that would impact price discovery no sooner 
than credible and concrete information comes into being in order to make 
such information generally available. 
 
The Company shall promptly disclose all UPSI to the stock exchanges where 
its shares are listed. Concurrently, this information shall be published on the 
Company’s official website for access by investors, members, and the public. 
Disclosure of material, non-public information through social media 
platforms/messaging applications is not permissible. 
 
b. Uniform and universal dissemination of UPSI to avoid selective disclosure. 
 
Selective disclosure of UPSI is strictly prohibited. All such information must be 
disseminated uniformly and universally to avoid selective disclosure by 
communicating the same to the stock exchange(s) and disclosing it on the 
Company’s official website. 
 
The Company will use its best efforts to prevent selective disclosure. If price-
sensitive information is disclosed selectively—whether inadvertently or 
otherwise—the CIRO and the Company Secretary & Compliance Officer must 
be notified immediately. The Company will act promptly to make the 
information publicly available, ensuring full disclosure within 24 hours of 
discovering the selective disclosure. 
 
c. Authority for Dissemination of information and disclosure of UPSI. 
 
Employees are prohibited from communicating internal information 
regarding the Company’s prospects, performance, and policies or disclosing 
any unpublished information (whether price-sensitive or not) unless they 
have the appropriate authority and a legitimate business purpose. When in 
doubt, employees must assume the information is material and non-public, 
and refrain from sharing or discussing it.   
 
The persons authorized as spokespersons to communicate with media, 
investors, analysts and others are the Executive Chairman, Whole-time 
Director, Chief Executive Officer, Chief Financial Officer, CIRO, Chief Business 
Officers and the Head of Corporate Communication. However, such authorised

d. Prompt dissemination of UPSI that gets disclosed selectively, inadvertently or

e. If the information is accidently disclosed without prior approval of the 
authorized spokesperson, the person responsible may inform the Compliance 
Officer immediately, even if the information is not considered price sensitive. 
If it is determined that the information disclosed is material and non-public, 
then such information shall be made generally available promptly and not 
later than 24 hours after discovering the unintentional disclosure. 
f. Appropriate and fair response to queries on news reports and requests for 
verification of market rumours by regulatory authorities;  
 
The Company Secretary & Compliance Officer shall promptly respond to any 
queries or requests for verification of market rumors by the stock exchanges 
after consultation with the Chief Executive Officer or the Chief Financial Officer 
or the CIRO. 
 
He/she shall also provide appropriate assistance and fair response to the 
regulatory authorities including the stock exchange for verification of news 
reports and market rumours. 
 
The Company shall not comment on every market rumour unless it is required 
to do so in terms of the SEBI regulations. 
 
g. Ensure that information shared with any analysts or research personnel is not 
UPSI; 
(i) Only Public information to be provided. The Company shall provide 
only publically available information to the analysts, research 
personnel, institutional investors, and other market participants. UPSI 
shall not be disclosed during interaction with such persons, except in 
accordance with applicable law. 
(ii) Handling of unanticipated questions. The Company should be careful 
while making corporate disclosures or dealing with analysts' questions 
that raise issues outside the intended scope of discussions. 
Unanticipated questions may be taken on notice and responded to at a 
later stage after due consideration. Where responding to such 
questions would require the disclosure of UPSI, the Company shall first 
disclose such information to the stock exchange(s) and make it 
generally available to the public before responding.

(iii) Presentations: The authorized spokesperson shall approve the content 
of any presentations prior to disclosure to analysts/research 
personnel/institutions, etc. The presentations prepared for analysts or 
institutional investor meet, post earnings or quarterly calls shall be 
disclosed to stock exchanges prior to beginning of such events. 
(iv) Plant visits: The Company may organize visits to its plants or other 
facilities for analysts, investors or other market participant. The 
Company shall ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to 
prevent access to UPSI or other material non-public information during 
such visits. A representative authorized by the authorized 
spokesperson shall be present during all visits.  
(v) Earnings call: The Company and its employees cannot give revenue / 
earnings guidance in any form (including “soft” or indirect guidance) in 
non-public settings. The Company will use the quarterly earnings call 
to provide general guidance on the financials for the future periods. 
Any statements regarding earnings expectations will be limited to 
press releases, publicly available earnings or conference calls. Only 
authorized spokespersons will be permitted to comment on or provide 
guidance regarding earnings and projections during these calls. 
 
h. Develop best practices to make transcripts or records of proceedings of 
meetings with analysts and other investor relations conferences on the official 
website to ensure official confirmation and documentation of disclosures 
made;  
 
In cases of post-earnings quarterly meeting with analysts, brokers or 
institutional investors, the transcripts or records of proceedings of meetings 
shall be posted on the official website of the Company, to ensure official 
confirmation and documentation of disclosures made. 
 
i. Handle all UPSI on a need-to-know basis  
 
UPSI shall be shared strictly on a “need to know” basis. UPSI may be disclosed 
only to persons who require such information for the furtherance of a 
legitimate purpose, performance of their duties, or discharge of legal 
obligations in relation to the Company. Persons who are privy to UPSI shall 
ensure that such information is disclosed only to those persons who need to 
know the same in the course of the performance of their duties or discharge of 
their legal obligations, and only where such disclosure is in furtherance of a 
legitimate purpose. 
 
j. An insider may communicate, provide or allow access to any UPSI, in 
furtherance of Legitimate Purposes as defined and prescribed in the Policy, in 
compliance with Institutional Mechanism and Company’s Code of conduct for 
Regulating, Monitoring and Reporting of Trading by Designated Persons.
