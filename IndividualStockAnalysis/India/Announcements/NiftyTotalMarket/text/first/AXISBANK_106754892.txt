This Notice  serves  as notice  that:  
 
1 Pursuant to Condition 6.3 of the Notes, the Issuer has elected to redeem 100% of the 
aggregate outstanding principal amount of the Notes (the “ Redemption ”). 
2 The Redemption date will be on or around 8 September 2026 (the “ Redemption Date ”). 
The record date is the close of business on the Business Day prior to the Redemption Date.  
 
 
3 The redemption price shall be 100.000% of the aggregate principal amount of the Notes 
plus accrued and unpaid interest, if any, to, but excluding, the Redemption Date (the 
“Redemption Price ”).  
4 Interest on the Notes called for Redemption shall cease to accrue on and after the 
Redemption Date.  
5 Pursuant to Condition 6.12 of the Notes, we confirm that: (a) the Issuer has obtained the 
prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India (Department of Banking Regulation) on 31 July 
2026; and (b) the Issuer has demonstrated to the satisfaction of the RBI tha t its capital 
position would, following such redemption, be well above its minimum capital requirements 
after the call option is exercised.
