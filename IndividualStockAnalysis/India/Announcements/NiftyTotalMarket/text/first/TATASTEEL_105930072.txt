Sub:  Acquisition of equity stake in T Steel Holdings Pte. Ltd  
 
This is further  to our disclosure dated May 29, 2024,  informing that the Board of Directors  of 
Tata Steel Limited (‘Company’) , have approved infus ion of  funds by way of subscription to 
equity shares of T Steel Holdings Pte. Ltd ( ‘TSHP ’), a wholly owned foreign subsidiary of the 
Company, in one or more tranches, during FY2024 -25. 
 
In this connection, we wish to inform you that, the Company  has today i.e. July 29, 2024 , 
acquired  557,32,48,408 equity shares of face value USD 0.157  each  aggregating to  
USD 875 million  (₹7,324.41  crore *) in TSHP . Post this acquisition, TSHP will continue to 
be a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company.  
 
This disclosure is being made in compliance with Regulation 30 read with Para A of Part A of 
Schedule III , Regulation 51 and other applicable provisions  of the Securities and Exchange 
Board of India  (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as 
amended .
