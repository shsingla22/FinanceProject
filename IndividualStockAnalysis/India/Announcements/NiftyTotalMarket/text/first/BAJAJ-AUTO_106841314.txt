Bajaj Mobility AG Quarterly Report Q3/26:  
Group revenue exceeds EUR 1 billion after three quarters;  
EBIT up EUR 10 million to EUR 11.3 million in Q3/26 
 
 Group revenue reaches EUR 351.1 million (+57.0% vs. Q3/25)  
 Motorcycle revenue1 increases to EUR 288.3 million (+84.0% vs. Q3/25)  
 Motorcycle sales1 rise to 47,390 units (+51.1% vs. Q3/25)  
 EBITDA: EUR 40.7 million (EUR -38 million2 in Q3/25) 
 EBITDA margin improves to 11.6% (-17.2%2 in Q3/25) 
 EBIT: EUR 11.3 million (EUR -71 million2 in Q3/25) 
 EBIT margin increases to 3.2% (-31.7%2 in Q3/25) 
 
Mattighofen, November 3, 2026 
Bajaj Mobility AG continued its positive operating performance in the third quarter of 2026. Group 
revenue rose by 57.0% year over year to EUR 351.1 million (compared with EUR 223.6 million in 
Q3/25). In the first nine months of 2026, group revenue amounted to EUR 1,052.5 million 
(compared with EUR 648.8 million in Q3 YTD 2025). 
Motorcycle sales¹ increased by 51.1% in the third quarter of 2026 to 47,390 units (compared with 
31,362 units in Q3/25). Motorcycle revenue of the KTM, Husqvarna Motorcycles and GASGAS 
brands increased by an even stronger 84.0% to EUR 288.3 million (compared with EUR 156.6 
million in Q3/25).  
EBITDA amounted to EUR 40.7 million in the third quarter of 2026 (compared with EUR -38 million2 
in Q3/25); the EBITDA margin improved to 11.6%. EBIT rose by EUR 10 million, from EUR 1.3 
million in the second quarter of 2026 to EUR 11.3 million in the third quarter of 2026 (compared 
with EUR -71 million2 in Q3/25). The EBIT margin reached 3.2% (-31.7%2 in Q3/25). Bajaj Mobility 
AG thus continued its positive earnings trend after returning to positive EBIT for the first time in the 
second quarter of 2026. 
In the first nine months of 2026, a total of 136,363 motorcycles¹ were sold, representing an increase 
of 75.4% compared with the prior-year period (77,748 units). Motorcycle revenue¹ reached EUR 
867.9 million during the same period (EUR 434.4 million in Q3 YTD 2025). In addition, Bajaj sold 
91,704 motorcycles in India (55,427 units in Q3 YTD 2025). This brought worldwide motorcycle

About Bajaj Mobility AG 
Bajaj Mobility AG is a global leader in premium motorcycles and the listed subsidiary of Bajaj Auto Limited, the world’s most 
valuable two ‑ and three ‑wheeler company. As the holding company of KTM AG, Bajaj Mobility AG brings together some of 
the most iconic and performance ‑driven brands in the global motorcycle industry – KTM, Husqvarna Motorcycles and 
GASGAS. Headquartered in Austria, the company represents a pinnacle of European engineering excellence across 
high‑performance racing motorcycles, street and offroad bikes, and travel ‑oriented models, complemented by premium WP 
components. With an established presence across these segments and a trusted rider base in more than 70 countries, Bajaj 
Mobility AG is recognized for its innovative engineering and strong product portfolio. Through its parent Bajaj Auto Limited, 
the group benefits from extensive technological expertise and a worldwide distribution network, enabling decisive execution 
and long ‑term value creation. Together, the group continues to set benchmarks in performance, quality, and global reach 
across the international motorcycle industry.
