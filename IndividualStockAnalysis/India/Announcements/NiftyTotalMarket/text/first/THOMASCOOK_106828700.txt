Sterling Opens Bandhavgarh Resort , Bringing Together Tiger 
Country, Heritage and Culture  
New addition strengthens Sterling's Central India wilderness portfolio and creates 
opportunities for connected journeys across Bandhavgarh and Kanha.  
Chennai, 2 6 October 2026: Sterling  Holiday Resorts Limited (SHRL) has announced the 
opening of Sterling Bandhavgarh in Madhya Pradesh, strengthening its presence across 
Central India's leading wildlife destinations and adding another distinctive forest 
experience to its growing wilderness portfolio.  
With resorts across 15 celebrated wildlife landscapes  of India,  Sterling is deliberately 
building wilderness travel as a distinct leisure category. Bandhavgarh strengthens this strategy by bringing one of India's most recognised tiger destinations into a portfolio 
designed to offer travellers different forests, differ ent experiences and more reasons to 
explore.  
The opportunity goes beyond the excitement of a tiger sighting. Bandhavgarh combines dense sal forests, open meadows and rich biodiversity with an extraordinary historical landscape. Ancient sculptures, caves, the heritage of Bandhavgarh Fort  and the 
reclining Vishnu at Shesh Shaiya  reveal a destination where wilderness and centuries of 
human history coexist.  
For families, this creates a more complete holiday proposition. The anticipation of a 
safari can be combined with birdwatching, village walks, nature trails, regional cuisine 
and relaxed time together, allowing travellers of different generations to discov er 
Bandhavgarh in their own way.  
Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director & CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited (SHRL ), 
said: "  “India's wilderness destinations are far more diverse than the wildlife they are 
famous for. Every forest has its own landscape, history, culture and cuisine —and 
therefore a different story to discover.  
Bandhavgarh is particularly compelling because it brings together one of India's celebrated tiger habitats with centuries of heritage and the distinctive culture of Baghelkhand. As Destination Architects, our ambition is to bring these dimensions 
together so that a safari becomes part of a richer, more memorable holiday for families.  
With Sterling's growing presence across wildlife and nature destinations, we also have 
the opportunity to connect individual forests into compelling journeys, such as 
Bandhavgarh and Kanha.  
  
 
 We are not simply adding destinations to our wilderness portfolio. We are giving 
travellers more reasons to discover India's forests —and more reasons to return.  
With Bandhavgarh joining our growing presence across Central India, we also see the 
opportunity to develop richer journeys that connect different wilderness landscapes.  
Our ambition is to make wildlife holidays about discovering the forest and everything around it, not just the sighting. "  
Located in Tala village, approximately 2.5 km from Tala Gate of Bandhavgarh Tiger Reserve, Sterling Bandhavgarh offers an intimate retreat with 26 rooms across four 
distinctive categories  - its Machan Suites feature private machans and outdoor 
showers, while Courtyard Rooms with Balconies  offer garden -facing outdoor spaces. 
Safari Tents with Private Sit -outs  bring a sense of adventure to the stay, 
complemented by comfortable Forest Rooms . A swimming pool, gardens and outdoor 
relaxation spaces create easy downtime between excursions.  
Speaking on the launch Mr. Pranav  Sharma, owner of Mahalaxmi Commercial Service 
Pvt Ltd , said :  “Our association with Sterling at Kanha has given us the conﬁdence to 
extend this partnership to Bandhavgarh. Sterling brings strong operating expertise, 
brand reach and an understanding of how to create distinctive destination experiences. 
We are delighted to build on this successful relationship and bring another compelling 
wilderness retreat to travellers ” 
The resort's location provides convenient access to Tala Gate, with opportunities to explore other safari zones such as Magadhi and Khitauli, subject to Forest Department 
permits and regulations. Birdwatching, village walks, nature trails and cycling offer  
additional ways to experience the surrounding landscape, while the region's historical and cultural attractions add depth to longer stays.  
Baghelkhand's culinary heritage provides another connection to the destination.  
At The Sal,  regional preparations such as Birra ki Roti , Saal Patta Murgh , Baans Mein 
Pakaya Chawal  and Kumhde ki Kheer  draw on traditional cooking methods, local grains 
and forest -inspired ingredients. Alongside Indian and international favourites, the 
resort's culinary proposition explores experiences such as the Jungle Thali, showcasing 
the ﬂavours of Baghelkhand, and Gaon ki Rasoi, inspired by the cooking traditions of 
local communities. Outdoor dining and bonﬁre evenings add to the experience of being close to nature.  
 
 
  
 
 Bandhavgarh also opens up opportunities for travellers to explore more of Madhya 
Pradesh's wilderness. With Sterling's presence in Kanha, the two destinations offer the 
potential for a Bandhavgarh–Kanha forest journey, bringing together distinct habitats, landscapes and wildlife experiences within a single holiday.  
With Bandhavgarh joining its growing wilderness network, Sterling is moving beyond 
individual wildlife stays towards a more connected way of discovering India's forests. 
From the tiger landscapes of Central India (Madhya Pradesh) to the distinctive 
wildern ess of Rajasthan  , Maharashtra, Uttarakhand  each destination adds a different 
experience to the portfolio —giving travellers more choice, families more reasons to 
explore together, and Sterling greater opportunity to build journeys that extend beyond a single safari or destination.
