LT Foods Delivers Broad -Based Growth across India and Global Markets 
in H1 FY ’27; Revenue up by 2 2% YoY to ₹6,414 Cr and EBITDA up by 17% 
to ₹721 Cr 
 
New Delhi , India, 27 October 2026: LT Foods, a leading global FMCG company in the consumer 
food space, today announced its unaudited consolidated financial results for the quarter and half year 
ended September 30, 2026.  
 
Mr. Ashwani Arora, Managing Director & CEO, LT Foods, said,  “We delivered a strong 
performance in the first half of FY ’27, reflecting the strength of our brands, the resilience of our 
diversified global business and disciplined execution across markets. For H1 FY ’27, our consolidated 
revenue grew 22% year -on-year to ₹6,414 crores  (Normalized Growth 18% including Golden Star®), 
EBITDA increased 17% to ₹721 crores, and PAT grew 9% to ₹364 crores. This performance reflects 
our continued focus on building strong consumer -focussed brands, driving premiumisation and 
innovation, an d strengthening operational efficiencies across the business.  
 
The S tandalone Revenue  of LT Foods  stood at ₹2,417 crore, growing by 14.1% YOY for H1 FY ’27. 
EBITDA stood at ₹223 crore , up by 33.5% and PAT is ₹134 crore , up by 23.9% . 
 
Our Basmati & Other Specialty Rice  continues to be at the heart of our growth story. For H1 FY ’27, 
it reported a growth of 2 7% year -on-year (Normalized Growth 2 2% including Golden Star®). The 
Organic Food & Ingredients , which is currently undergoing a strategic remodelling, reported a 
revenue in tune ₹520 crores. The Ready -to-Eat (RTE), Ready -to-Cook (RTC) and Ready -to-Heat 
(RTH)  segment delivered 8% growth with revenue at ₹102 crores.  
 
In Q2 FY ’27, consolidated revenue stood at ₹3,253 crores, growing 17% year -on-year and 3% 
sequentially. EBITDA stood at ₹358 crores, up 13% year -on-year, while PAT i ncreased by 10% year -
on-year to ₹180 crores.  
 
India continues to be our strong est growth engine,  which  grew by 24% YOY for H1 FY ’27. W e are 
executing a multi -pronged strategy focused on premiumisation, portfolio expansion, distribution 
expansion and capability building.

About L T Foods Limited  
LT Foods Ltd. [NSE: LTFOODS, BSE: 532783] is a leading global FMCG company in the consumer food space. 
It is a leading player globally in the specialty rice and rice -based foods business for more than last 7 5 years. 
The company delivers the finest quality and taste experiences in more than 8 5 countries across India, the U.S., 
U.K., Europe, the Middle East, the Far E ast and the Rest of the World. The company ’s flagship brands include 
, one of India ’s mos t loved and consumed Basmati brands, Royal®, which is North America ’s most 
loved brand and many more. The company is proudly expanding into the future food preferences of millennials 
by offering organic food in global markets and supplying organic food -ingredients to leading businesses. The 
company is committed to nurturing the goodness of food for people, the community and the planet.  
 
With a consolidated revenue of around Rs. 11,023  crore as of FY ’26, LT Foods has an integrated “Farm to Fork ” 
approach wit h a well -entrenched Distribution Network with Global Supply Chain Hubs backed by automated 
state -of-the-art and strategically located Processing Units in India, the U.S. and Europe, and a robust distribution 
network with 1400+ distributors across the globe .
