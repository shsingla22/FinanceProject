Dear Sir/Madam,  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, this is to intimate that we have been informed by the third – party 
warehouse personnel , which also houses certain inventory belonging to the Company , about 
a fire incident that occurred during the night of 6th August, 2025, at their warehouse situated 
at Annaram , Telangana . 
We have been informed by the third – party warehouse personnel that the fire is largely under 
control  and emergency response actions were promptly undertaken . The  cause of the fire is 
not yet known and is under investigation . 
The value of inventory affected by the incident is currently estimated at Rs. 45 crores. This 
inventory is adequately covered by insurance and the process for claim has commenced.  
We are taking all necessary steps to address the situation and are in the process of working 
with the suppliers for restoration of inventory and with customers to plan for resumption of 
supplies .
