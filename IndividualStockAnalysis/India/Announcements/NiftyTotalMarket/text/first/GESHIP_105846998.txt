PRESS RELEASE
G E Shipping contracts to sell its 2004 built Medium Range Product Tanker 
“Jag Pranam”  
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (G E Shipping) has contracted to sell its 2004 built 
Medium Range Product Tanker, Jag Pranam of about 48,700 dwt on May 15, 2024 to an 
unaffiliated third party. The vessel will be delivered to the new buyer by H1 FY25. 
Including Jag Pranam, company’s current fleet stands at 44 vessels, comprising 30 tankers (6 crude 
carriers, 20 product tankers, 4 LPG carrier) and 14 dry bulk carriers (2 Capesize, 8 Kamsarmax, 4 
Supramax) aggregating 3.46 Mn dwt. 
Additionally, we had contracted to sell one MR product tanker on April 01, 2024, which is due for 
delivery in Q1FY25.
