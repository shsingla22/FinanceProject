MEDIA RELEASE  
 
Religare Enterprises Board approves Capital fundraise  of Rs. 1500  crores through 
preferential allotment  of warrants  
 
- Board approves fundraising  of Rs. 1500 Crores to help grow  the company’s core business 
and explore new opportunities . 
- Promoters add stimulus with contribution of 50% of the capital raise, demonstrating 
strong commitment  to the company  
 
New Delhi, July 11, 2025  — Religare Enterprises Limited  (REL ) - (BSE: [532915], NSE: 
[RELIGARE]), a diversified financial services group  today announced that its Board has approved a 
capital raise of Rs. 1,500 crores to support the Company’s next phase of growth. The fundraise 
totalling Rs. 1500 Crores is intended to fuel business expansion and drive new strategic initiatives.  
  
As part of the approved plan, the capital raise will be executed through:   
 
1. Issuance of 6,38,29,782  (Six crore thirty -eight lakh twenty -nine thousand seven hundred and 
eighty -two) warrants, each  convertible into 1 (one) fully paid up equity share of the company 
of face value of Rs. 10/ - (Rs. ten only) , each to the proposed allottee on a preferential basis . 
2. Contribution of Rs. 750 crores by the Promoter Group, representing 50% of the total capital  
raise . 
 
The Promoters  have  reaffirmed their  long -term  commitment  in the Company’s growth trajectory  
by contributing Rs. 750 crores; thereby providing support for business growth across verticals  and 
driving strategic initiatives . 
 
The recently appointed  Promoter nominee Directors bring deep expertise across insurance, 
strategy, and financial services, further fortifying the Company’s governance and execution 
capabilities . With this capital infusion and new leadership at the helm, R EL is well -positioned to  
sharpen its strategic focus, enhance governance standards, scale its core businesses, and pursue 
new growth opportunities.   
 
Axis Capital  Limited acted as the financial advisor to Religare Enterprises for this fund raising 
initiative.

About Religare:  
 
Religare Enterprises Limited (REL) is a diversified financial services group present across three 
verticals. REL offers an integrated suite of fin ancial services through its underlying subsidiaries 
and operating entities, including loans to SMEs, Affordable Housing Finance, Health Insurance and 
Retail Broking. REL is listed on the BSE (formerly Bombay Stock Exchange) and National Stock 
Exchange (NSE ) in India.  
 
As a group, Religare caters to diverse segments of the market from mass retail to affluent, SMEs to 
mid -size corporates. Subsidiaries service over 1.1 mn. clients from over 1,275 locations having 
presence in more than 400 cities.
