Lemon Tree Hotels s igns a new property in Madhya Pradesh  
 
 
New Delhi,  11th June  2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels announces its latest signing — 
Lemon Tree Premier , Mhow , Madhya Pradesh . The property shall be managed by 
Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels 
Limited . 
 
Lemon Tree Premier , Mhow, Madhya Pradesh  will feature 72 well-appoi nted 
rooms, a restaurant, a meeting room, a banquet, a swimming pool , a spa and 
other public areas. The Indore Airport , the neare st airport, is approximately 30  
kms from the property. Indore R ailway Station is about 29  kms away from the 
property. The resort is also well -connected by road, offering easy access to both 
public and private transport.  
 
Mhow, officially named Dr. Ambedkar Nagar, is a picturesque cantonment town 
that blends military history, spiritual significance, and natural beauty. It is 
renowned as the birthplace of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar and this is commemorated by 
the impressive Bhim Janmabhoomi memorial, attracting both pilgrims and 
tourists, particularly on Ambedkar Jayanti. The town features stunning natural 
attractions like the magnificent 91m Patalpani Falls, that becomes particularly 
enchanting post -monsoon, and peaks such as Janapav Kuti, known for its 
legendary importance and stunning panoramic vistas.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels commented, “We are thrilled to expand our presence in Madhya 
Pradesh, complementing our existing portfolio of four operational hotels with six 
exciting new properties in the pipeline. This strategic investment aligns with the 
state’s emerging role as a hub for wellnes s, spiritual tourism, and heritage 
hospitality. ” 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) stands as one of India's largest and most 
diversified hotel chains, boasting an expansive portfolio that spans the upscale, 
upper -midscale, midscale, and economy segments. Through a strategic blend of 
ownership, leasing, op eration, and franchising, LTHL delivers exceptional service 
offerings that are both differentiated and superior, underscored by a compelling 
value proposition.  
 
The company's impressive brand portfolio comprises seven distinct entities, each 
tailored to cater to diverse guest needs across various segments. These include 
Aurika Hotels & Resorts, Lemon Tree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox Hotels, 
Keys Prima, Key s Select, and Keys Lite.  
 
 
 
 

   
 
 
 
 
 
 
Since opening its first hotel with 49 rooms in May 2004, Lemon Tree Hotels has 
experienced remarkable growth, now boasting a portfolio of over 210 hotels. This 
includes more than 110 operational hotels, with over 100 additional properties 
slated to open.  
 
Strategically located across key metro regions such as Delhi NCR, Mumbai, 
Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Chennai, Lemon Tree Hotels also maintains 
a strong presence across Tier II, III and IV cities in 73 destinations. Furthermore, 
the company has succ essfully expanded its footprint internationally, with hotels in 
Dubai, Bhutan, and Nepal.
