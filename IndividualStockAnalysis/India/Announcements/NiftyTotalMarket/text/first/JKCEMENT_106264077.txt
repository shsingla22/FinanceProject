Company.

from its shareholders and promoters and also fresh issue by Saifco. Upon this acquisition, Saifco has 
become a subsidiary of the Company with immediate effect and the Board of the Saifco shall be re- 
constituted and three persons nominated by the Company will be appointed as Additional Directors 
on the board of Saifco and two Directors would continue to represent existing promoter and promoter 
group of Saifco.
