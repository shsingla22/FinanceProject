Mastek Reimagines Enterprise Software with AI -Native Services as Software (SaS)  
Mastek Introduces Services as Software with AI -native solution s that deliver better business outcomes while 
significantly reducing software subscription costs  
Mumbai, India – Oct 05, 2026:  Mastek (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted AI -first digital engineering and cloud 
transformation partner, today announced a major milestone in its Services as Software (SaS)  strategy, redefining how 
enterprises achieve intelligent transformation . By combining deep domain expertise, AI, data, and engineering, Mastek is 
enabling organizations to move beyond traditional enterprise software toward AI -native services that deliver measurable 
business outcomes, simplify technology landscapes, and signifi cantly reduce software subscription costs. The company is 
applying this approach across its focused industry verticals, helping clients accelerate business transformation with AI at 
the core.   
Mastek's Service as Software vision is built  on three  core principles : 
1. Deliver  Tangible Business Results : Technology investments should deliver measurable business outcomes, not 
just usage metrics or deployment milestones.  
2. Transform  System of Record to System of Intelligence : Traditional enterprise platforms capture, organize, and 
report historical information . Systems of intelligence go further by connecting enterprise knowledge and 
delivering the right insight to the right person at the right time.   
3. Sell Outcomes and Reduce the Cost of Software : Industry studies estimate that enterprises actively use only 
around one -third of the SaaS applications they license, leaving significant investments underused while adding 
complexity to daily operations . Mastek addresses this by assessing actual software usage, retaining only the 
workflows that create business value, and replacing unnecessary complexity with AI -native experiences.  
As the first proof point of this vision, Mastek developed a CRM  solution , an AI -native platform that has replaced its legacy 
CRM  environment. Designed and built at the Mastek AI Center of Excellence, the solution was deployed, stress -tested, 
and refined using the company's Customer Zero  approach, supporting all global sales and marketing teams in live 
production. This internal deployment validated the platform's scalability, usability, and business impact before making it 
available to customers . Mastek aim s to deliver a 10% improvement in its win probability using contextual data to 
transform the CRM from a system of records to a system of intelligence and execution.  
Speaking on the subject Umang Nahata , CEO of Mastek  said “Our learning is that applying AI to just speed up old ways 
of working will only give a good use case for AI to deliver a tangible business benefit . Organizations must  reimagine their 
process es and reskill their talent. We had to also ensure that frontier models are combined  with contextual data of 
Mastek to generate Mastek -specific intelligence. Finally,  adoption  of the solution needs working with sales teams and 
leaders on the ground to understand the grassroot details. Unless the solutions are made f or the specific nua nces of an 
orga nization , it’s hard to get tang ible benefits.”  
“We reimagined our data and buil t Mastek Hub, the company’s centralized knowledge platform . MastekCRM combines 
customer case studies, project experience, proposal assets, and business intelligence to enable AI -driven decision -making. 
The platform helps sales teams prepare for client meetings, improve forecasting, automatically capture institutional 
knowledge, and surface rel evant recommendations in real time .” said Ritwik Batabyal, CTIO at Mastek . 
 
 
Building on this foundation, Mastek is extending the same AI -native SaS approach  across additional enterprise functions. 
Mastek ’s has developed an  AI native Intelligent Document Processing  solution which is being used to replace its legacy 
SAP Procurement, Accounts Payable & Expense Management applications. These new solutions are focused on delivering 
the business goal of faster and simpler process es and tangible cost optimization.  
Together, these solutions demonstrate how enterprises can progressively replace conventional enterprise software with 
intelligent, AI -powered business capabilities . 
Umang also  highlighted , “Mastek's strategy is not about becoming a software product company. Instead, we are 
representing a  new services -led model where deep domain expertise, AI, data, and engineering converge to deliver 
continuously evolving business capabilities. ”
