About Transrail Lighting Limited:  
Transrail is one of the leading engineering, procurement and construction companies with primary focus on power 
transmission and distribution business with more than 4 decades of experience. With its headquarters in India, it is a 
global enterprise with a footprint in 59 countries across 5 continents. It provides turnkey solutions from Design, 
Engineering, Supply, Manufacture, Construction, Testing services across all its business verticals which includes Power 
T&D, Civil  Construction, Railways, Pole  & ligh ting and Solar EPC. It has more than 2,100 employees. As part of Power 
T&D business, Transrail has large -scale manufacturing facilities in India for Galvanized Lattice Towers, Overhead 
Conductors and Galvanized Monopoles in addition to a well -accredited To wer testing  facility.
