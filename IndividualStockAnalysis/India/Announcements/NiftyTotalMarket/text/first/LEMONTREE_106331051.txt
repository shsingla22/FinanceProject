Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
In continuation to our earlier announcement dated January 25, 2024 , we would like to 
inform that the Franchise Agreement dated January 25, 2024  executed for Lemon Tree 
Hotel, Motihari, Bihar, have been terminated today by the parties with mutual consent.   
 
Simultaneously with the execution of the aforesaid Termination Agreement, the parties 
have entered into a Hotel Operating Agreement & License Agreement  for Lemon Tree 
Hotel, Motihari, Bihar. The hotel shall be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, 
a wholly owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
 
This hotel  will feature 50 well -appointed rooms, a restaurant, a fitness center, a 
lounge, a banquet hall, a meeting room, a swimming pool, a bar, a spa and other 
public areas .
