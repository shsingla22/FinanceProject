Sub: Update on Marico’s business operations in Bangladesh  
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
We are writing to share an update on the status of Marico’s business operations in Bangladesh in 
the wake of recent developments in the country.  
 
We would like to inform you that operating conditions in the market are gradually improving, 
however we remain watchful of the evolving situation. 
 
We continue to prioritize the safety of our employees, factory workers, distributors and other 
stakeholders of our business.  
 
After a brief interruption, a large majority of our retail sales force and distributors have now 
resumed operations. We expect our manufacturing operations to resume soon, while we continue 
to maintain an adequate assurance of supply of our products to meet market demand in the 
interim.  
 
We firmly believe that the medium-term prospects of Marico’s business in Bangladesh remain 
intact. 
 
We request you to take the aforesaid update on record.
