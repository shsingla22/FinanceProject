Firstsource Launches FY2025-26 ESG Report  
 
Report details FY2025 -26 ESG performance, strengthened governance, and progress in 
integrating ESG considerations into strategy, operations and risk management  
 
Mumbai, India | October  22, 2026:  Firstsource Solutions Limited  (NSE: FSL, BSE: 532809), a global intelligence 
partner and an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company, release d its ESG Report  for fiscal  year 2025 -26, detailing 
performance against its ESG commitments and reporting parameters, and the continued strengthening of its 
governance.  
 
During the year , Firstsource completed a  Double Materiality Assessment (DMA)  and Climate  Risk Assessment 
(CRA) , strengthened its alignment with global ESG frameworks, and advance d the integration of ESG considerations 
into strategy, operations, risk management and decision -making.  
 
Insights from  these assessments are being integrated into the company’s Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)  
framework, strengthening its understanding of the impacts, risks  and opportunities most relevant to the business and its 
stakeholders  and supporting more informed decision -making and greater resilience.  
 
From ESG commitments to business decisions  
 
Firstsource’s disclosures  are aligned with the GRI Standards 2021, TCFD, IFRS S2, and TNFD, alongside participation 
in global platforms including CDP, the S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment, the UN Global Compact and 
BRSR. The company’s Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 gre enhouse gas inventory and BRSR Core KPIs underwent 
independent assurance, strengthening the rigor and credibility of its ESG disclosures.  
 
Firstsource’s  near-term and Net Zero targets were also validated by the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) in July 
2026, supporting its commitment to achieve Net Zero emissions by 2050.  
 
Dr. Sanjiv Goenka, Chairman of RPSG Group and Firstsource , said, “We don’t believe innovation should be limited 
to technology alone. It must be include creating value in a way that balances the interests of our business, our people 
and the planet. That is the philosophy behind FirstConscious, our global ESG framework, which brings these 
considerations into how we govern the business, manage risk, make decisions, innovate and create long -term value. 
Over the past year, we have strengthened this foundation through greater rigor in our governance and reporting, while 
continuing to invest in employee learning, inclusion and well -being and to expand our impact in the communities where 
we operate. As the nature of work and technology continues to change, our responsibility is to help our people and 
communities adapt  and create greater opportunity. Our focus is to keep building a business that grows responsibly, 
earns trust and remains resilient for the long term. ” 
 
FirstConscious , Firstsource’s integrated ESG framework and operating philosophy, connects these priorities across 
the business. It is designed to embed ESG into governance, strategic planning, operational execution, performance 
management and enterprise -wide decision -making, translating commitments into structured action, measurable 
outcomes and accountability.  
 
Measurable progress across the business  
 
These efforts are translating into measurable outcomes.  The FY2025 –26 ESG Report highlights progress across key 
ESG priorities , including:  
• 30.55% reduction  in market -based Scope 2 emissions  
• 21.76%  of total energy sourced from renewable energy  
• 48.54% women  across the workforce  
• More than 3.7 million learning hours  delivered during the year  
• 79,005 lives impacted  through community initiatives

Responsible technology as a business priority  
 
As technology and AI become increasingly central to how businesses operate, Firstsource is also strengthening the 
governance frameworks around responsible technology.  
 
During FY2025 –26, Firstsource recorded zero cybersecurity and data privacy incidents , while continuing to 
strengthen its data governance, privacy, cybersecurity, ethics and responsible AI practices .  
 
External recognition and validation  
 
Firstsource’s  integrated approach to sustainability and responsible growth continued to receive external recognition 
during the year.   
 
The company achieved an S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment score of 87, ranking first globally  among 
companies  in the Professional Services sector  assessed by S&P Global  and placing Firstsource  in the top 1% globally.  
In the 2026 assessment, S&P Global evaluated more than 9,200 companies worldwide, with 848 companies earning 
Sustainability Yearbook membership.   
 
Firstsource was also included in the S&P Global Sustainability Yearbook for the third consecutive year.  Other 
recognitions during the year included the Golden Peacock Award for ESG 2025, the 3rd Prithvi Awards ESG recognition, 
and an EcoVadis Bronze Medal, among others.
