This Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) is entered on the 10" of July, 2026 at Bhubaneswar, Odisha 
Between 
THE GOVERNMENT OF ODISHA 
And 
HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION LIMITED 
(HEREINAFTER REFERRED TO AS “HUDCO”) 
This MoU is made to facilitate HUDCO for planning, development and financing of the various 
Housing and Infrastruture projects under Urban Challenge Fund (UCF), Bhubaneswar—Cuttack—Puri— 
Paradeep Economic Region (BCPPER) and Mukhyamantri Sahari Vikas Yojana (MSVY) in Odisha 
in a time bound manner as per the rules and regulations of the State Government. 
HUDCO shall provide term loans to the extent of Rupees One Lakh Crore, over a period of 5 years, 
for various Urban Infrastructure Projects, including land acquisition. 
HUDCO, through its UiWIN initiative, shall also provide Project Preparation and Capacity Building 
support, as required by the State Government/Agencies, including technical structuring, DPR and pre- 
feasibility preparation, asset register creation, financial modelling, VGF support for bankable urban 
infrastructure projects, and outcome-based training, consultancy, and knowledge-sharing support for 
capacity enhancement of ULBs. 
The loan shall be availed in tranches on flexible terms and conditions, including moratorium period 
with a flexible repayment schedule up to 25 years, with the option of prepayment by the any statutory 
authority specifically designated by the State Government of Odisha. 
The repayment of the loan shall be made from escrowing the revenue generated from the project or by 
ringfencing other sources of revenues coming from identified receivables/state budget so as to ensure 
timely payments to HUDCO. 
Separate operational agreements with specified scope of work and terms and conditions, shall be 
executed based on mutually agreed terms between both the parties, keeping in view of the requirements 
of the projects. 
This MoU is executed to put on record the intent of both the parties to undertake the activities as stated 
and shall remain valid for 3 years from the date of execution, subject to annual review.
