About Ola Electric Mobility Limited  
Ola Electric Mobility Limited is one of India's leading electric vehicle (EV) manufacturers. It specialises in the vertical i ntegration of 
technology and manufacturing for EVs and their components, including battery cells. The Ola Futurefactory in Tamil Na du, where EVs 
and critical components are produced, is playing a significant role in establishing a robust EV hub in India. It is supported  by Ola's 
Bengaluru -based Battery Innovation Centre (BIC), dedicated to advancing cell and battery technology. Ola's R&D efforts span India, 
the UK, and the US, focusing on innovative EV products and core components. Ola maintains a direct -to-customer distribution network 
of thousands of stores across India and a robust online presence, making Ola Electric one of the lar gest company -owned networks of 
automotive experience centres in the country.
