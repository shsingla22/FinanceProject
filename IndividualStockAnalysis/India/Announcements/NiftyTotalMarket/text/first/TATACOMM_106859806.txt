Tata Communications Receives Frost & Sullivan’s 2026 Indian Communications 
Services Provider for Large Enterprises Company of the Year Recognition  
 
The company  is recognized for its digital infrastructure strategy, enterprise reliability, operational 
visibility, and measurable business outcomes. 
 
SAN ANTONIO, TX. – November 1 3, 2026  – Frost & Sullivan  is pleased to announce that  Indian 
telecommunications company  Tata Communications  has been given the 2026 Indian Company of the Year 
Recognition in the c ommunications services provider for large enterprises industry for its outstanding 
achievements in strategic execution, platform innovation, and customer impact  for large enterprises . This 
recognition emphasizes  Tata Communications’ evolution beyond traditional connectivity into a strategic 
digital infrastructure partner, helping large enterprises reduce complexity, strengthen resilience, and 
accelerate digital transformation.  
 
Guided by a long- term growth strategy , Tata Communications continues to address the growing complexity 
of enterprise technology environments. By integrating global connectivity, cloud, security, edge, customer 
experience, automation, and end- to-end orchestration, the company enables enterprises  to modernize their 
digital infrastructure through a unified operating model. Its software -defined platforms and proprietary 
technologies help organizations improve visibility, centralize management, and establish the secure, 
resilient foundations required to scale AI and digital workloads.  
 
Tata Communications  portfolio  brings together infrastructure, platforms, and managed services to address 
enterprise requirements through connected capabilities rather than stand -alone solutions. AI -enabled life  
cycle processes support service feasibility, provisioning, delivery, assurance, and account management, 
while proactive monitoring and unified control planes improve operational visibility and service reliability. By 
advancing predictive and increasingly a utonomous operations, Tata Communications enables enterprises 
to identify potential service risks earlier, streamline workflows, and strengthen end- to-end service 
assurance.  
 
“The company’s differentiation is not that it offers more individual services; it is that those services are designed to operate as a connected digital fabric,  giving large enterprises a better roadmap from 
infrastructure investment to business value,” according to Rahul Agarwal, Director & Head, ICT Growth 
Practice at Frost & Sullivan.  
 
By combining global infrastructure, deep domain expertise, mature managed services capabilities, and 
dedicated enterprise support, the company helps customers manage complex technology environments 
with greater flexibility and accountability. Its focus on managed operations, automation, and outcome- based 
engagements supports continuous service improvement, reinforces customer confidence, and helps enterprises translate infrastructure investments into measurable business value.  
 
“As a global company with over 60% of our revenue coming from international markets, it is heartening to 
also be recognised as the market leader here at home in India. Our strength lies in bringing together the 
five operating fabrics we have built, spanning network, cloud, cybersecurity, interaction, and intelligence, 
to build tailored solutions to address our customers’ needs. The resilience of these solutions, backed by strong service assurance and accountability, is what we believe earns us the trust of l arge enterprises and 
makes us their partner of choice. ” said Ganesh Lakshminarayan, MD and CEO, Tata Communications .  
 
 
Frost & Sullivan commends Tata Communications for setting a high standard in strategic execution, 
platform leadership, and market responsiveness. Its digital  portfolio , commitment to large enterprises , and 
continued investment in intelligent operations are shaping the future of India’s communications service 
provider market and enabling large enterprises to pursue innovation with greater efficiency, resilience, and 
control.  
 
Each year, Frost & Sullivan presents the Company of the Year Recognition to a company that demonstrates 
outstanding strategy development and implementation, resulting in measurable improvements in market 
share, customer satisfaction, and competitive positioning. It recognizes forward -thinking organizations that 
are reshaping their industries through innovation and growth excellence.  
 
Frost & Sullivan Best Practices Recognition  
 
Frost & Sullivan’s Best Practices Recognitions honor companies across regional and global markets that 
exhibit exceptional achievement and consistent excellence in areas such as leadership, technological 
innovation, customer experience, and strategic product development. Each recognition is the result of a rigorous analytical process in which Frost & Sullivan industry experts benchmark performance through 
comprehensive interviews, deep- dive analysis, and extensive secondary research. The goal is to identify  
true best -in-class organizations that are driving transformative growth and setting new industry standards.
