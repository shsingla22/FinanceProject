Subject:  Clarification under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 regarding media reports on China's proposed 
restrictions on export of solar cell technology 
Dear Sir/ Madam, 
This is with reference to recent media reports concerning China's proposed restrictions on the export of 
technologies and machinery used in solar cell manufacturing. 
Fujiyama Power Systems Limited (“the Company”) wishes to clarify that the machinery and equipment 
required for its announced 1.2 GW DCR (Domestic Content Requirement) solar cell manufacturing 
facility have already been delivered to its plant at Ratlam, Madhya Pradesh. Accordingly, the Company 
does not anticipate any material impact of the proposed restrictions on the capital expenditure or the 
timeline of this expansion, and the facility remains on track to be commissioned as originally planned, 
ie.in Q1 of FY 2027-28. At this stage, the Company has not announced any plans for additional solar 
cell manufacturing capacity. 
The Company is engaged in the retail-focused rooftop solar business, in which it manufactures and 
supplies Solar Power Generation Systems (SPGS) comprising solar inverters, solar batteries and solar 
panels. Solar panel manufacturing is a backward integration of this business, and solar cell 
manufacturing is a further backward integration for solar panel manufacturing. Accordingly, the 
Company does not foresee any material impact of the proposed restrictions on this expansion and the 
facility is expected to be commissioned, as originally envisaged. The Company’s operations are not 
dependent on any ongoing foreign technology transfer and remain fully unaffected. 
The above information is also being made available on the website of the Company at 
www.utlsolarfujiyama.com.
