Thomas Cook India Group enters into a  Joint Venture to build Generative AI 
solutions for the Travel Domain with Atirath Technologies  Pvt. Ltd.   
 
                     Focus on growth & enhanced customer experience  
 
Mumbai, September 30, 2024: Artificial Int elligence (AI)  is changing the entire Travel ecosystem. Digital First 
customers are driving demand for diverse travel experiences that are customised to their preferences and  
want these seamlessly fulfilled. Travel providers on the other hand,  are challeng ed with personalis ation  to 
scale  and  provid ing relevant recommendations, while delivering a seamless customer experience.  
 
The Thomas Cook India Group i ntends  to better serve  the rapidly growing travel market and evolving travel lers 
to create growth oppor tunities  – via custom built , generative AI powered, voice enabled solutions with l ocal 
language transcribing and  speech capabilities , capable of learning and improving continuously.  
 
To achieve a  robust, scalable  and custom built  Gen AI solution  for the T ravel Domain , the Thomas Cook India 
Group has entered into a Joint Venture with Atirath Technologies Private Limited .  
 Projected benefits to the Thomas Cook India Group:  
• Ability to extend reach and serve more customers  
• Address them better with local langu age capabilities  
• Create a seamless digital journey moving customers directly to online transactions  
• Reduce human dependence to scale up  
• Assist sellers -  improving productivity and customer experience  
• Create a completely new distribution model  
  
Madhavan Me non, Executive Chairman Thomas Cook India  Group , said,  “I am delighted to announce our JV 
with Atirath Technologies Private Limited that is aimed at creating potent  growth and customer experience 
capabilities  for the TCIL Group in the rapidly evolving space of AI solutions.  
This is a business imperative, given the need to scale volumes and productivity without a corresponding linear 
cost structure and is aligned with our Customer & Digital First strategy.  
Today, AI (especially Gen AI) has found its way int o every customer use case and is becoming the new normal in 
terms of meeting customer ex pectations  and improved service delivery. I am sure this partnership with the 
dynamic team at Atirath, will help enhance TCIL’s leadership in the travel industry and gi ve us an early mover 
advantage in the exciting new frontier of Generative AI.”
