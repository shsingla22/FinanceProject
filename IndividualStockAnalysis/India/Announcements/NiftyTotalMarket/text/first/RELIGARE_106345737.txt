Detailed Rationale  
Infomerics ratings has withdrawn its rating assigned to the Proposed Non -Convertible 
Debentures (NCDs) of Religare Housing Development Finance Corporation Limited 
(RHDFCL)  with immediate effect  at the request of the company vide email dated 12th August 
2025. Confirmation  has been received over email  from the statutory auditor of the Company 
that RHDFCL  has not issued any Non -Convertible Debentures (NCDs) on or after 30th 
September 2024 using the rating assigned by Infomerics Valuation and Ratings  Limited . The 
rating is withdrawn in accordan ce with Infomerics  Ratings policy on withdrawal  and at the 
request of the company.
