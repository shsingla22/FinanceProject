IIFL Securities Ltd reports strong revenue and profit growth  for the quarter  & 
half year ended September 30, 202 4 
 
IIFL Securities Ltd has announced its consolidated financial results for the Q2FY25 .  
 
Total income grew 32 % on Y -o-Y to ₹703.7 crores  and the growth was led by all the key business 
segments . 
 
• Total brokerage  revenue  was up by 17% on Y-o-Y to ₹317.6  crores .  
• Investment Banking revenues were up 35 % on Y -o-Y to ₹51.4 crores . Thirteen deals were 
consummated during the quarter ended on September 30, 2024 . 
• Financial products distribution (FPD) revenues went up by 25% on Y-o-Y to ₹106.2  crores . FPD AUM 
stood at ₹31,239 crores .  
 
Profit after tax was up 9 1% on Y -o-Y to ₹205.3 crores  driven by margin expansion across all the 
businesses.  
As at end September 2024, Distribution & custody assets under management stood at ₹2,46,308 crores .

About IIFL Securities  
IIFL Securities Ltd (NSE: IIFLSEC, BSE: 542773) is one of the key capital market players in the Indian 
financial services space. IIFL Securities along with its subsidiaries offers broking services, financial 
products distribution, institutional research and investment banking services.  
IIFL Securities has diverse product offerings and a wide network of branches  across India.  The company 
has also sales representatives in Singapore, London and New York.
