03-11-2025. The corresponding shares on which dividend was unclaimed  / unpaid  for seven

Provisions of the Act and Rules.

In case, the company do not receiv e any claim from the shareholder  within the stipulated time

shares to IEPF Authority, without any furthe r notice and initiate the following actions:

amount or shares, once the same are transferred to IEPF Authority, pursuant to the said  Act & 
Rules.
