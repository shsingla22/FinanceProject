Press Note:  "Kirloskar Oil Engines Limited Announces Strategic Integration to Accelerate B2C 
Growth.  
 
Kirloskar Oil Engines Limited ( “Company” ) has transferred by way of a slump sale as a going 
concern, its Water Management Solutions ( “WMS”) – Domestic & Exports Business (collectively, 
the “B2C Segment ”) to its wholly owned subsidiary, La-Gajjar Machineries Private Limited, with a 
view to internally reorganise the B2C Segment.  This strategic transaction is a crucial step in the 
execution of the Company's long-term vision. This transfer and reorganisation directly continues 
the strategic journey initiated by the Company, 3 (three) years ago to bifurcate its operations into 
distinct business-to-consumer (B2C) and business-to-business (B2B) segment to enhance 
operational efficiency and unlock focused growth potential. By reorganising these assets, the 
Company will significantly sharpen its focus on its B2C Segment operations, a key pillar of its long-
term growth strategy. This move is expected to drive significant operational efficiencies, enhance 
resource allocation towards high-growth areas, and create a more agile and simplified 
organizational structure, thereby strengthening Company's market leadership, accelerating 
product innovation, and delivering enhanced value to its customers and shareholders.
