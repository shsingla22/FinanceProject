Crizac Launches Partner Growth Program to Help 
Counselling Partners Grow 
Partner Growth Program brings marketing support, counsellor certification, and professional branding, 
allin one place, so counselling partners can grow their business while staying focused on their students. 
[City], [Date]: Crizac Limited, an Al-native student mobility platform partnering with international 
universities and education counsellors globally, today announced the Partner Growth Program (PGP), 
an initiative to help counselling partners grow their businesses. 
Built especially for small and medium counselling businesses, PGP equips partners with ready-to-use 
marketing, structured counsellor training and certification, and professional branding under their own 
name, all through their existing Crizac login. The program is open to Crizac's network of 17,400+ 
registered counselling partners across 85+ countries. 
Helping partners build stronger businesses 
Counsellors devote their days to guiding students, managing applications, and handling the steady 
rhythm of follow-ups. As a result, they seldom have the bandwidth to work consistently on marketing 
and branding, which can mean missed opportunities to generate new leads and grow their business. 
At the same time, they must stay abreast of destination policies, admissions processes, and industry 
shifts that rarely pause. Finding the time and resources for regular training and certification becomes 
a challenge of its own. 
Crizac believes a platform is only as strong as the counselling partners it works with, and that its role 
is to contribute to their growth. PGP gives that belief practical form. 
"Students trust their counsellor before they trust any university or any platform. That trust is earned 
one conversation at a time, and it is the most valuable thing in our industry," said Dr Vikash Agarwal, 
Managing Director, Crizac Limited. "Yet the counselling partners that earn it often have the least time 
and support to grow. A platform should grow with the partners that trust it, and PGP is our 
commitment to doing exactly that. When counselling partners grow stronger, students get better 
advice, and the whole system works better."
