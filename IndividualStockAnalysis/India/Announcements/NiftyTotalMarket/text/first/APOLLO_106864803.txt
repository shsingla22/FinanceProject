Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that Apollo Micro Systems Limited (“the Company”) in 
the ordinary course of business has today, i.e. 18 November 2026, entered into a Memorandum of 
Understanding in the nature of a Strategic Collaboration and Framework Agreement with Redon 
Systems Private Limited, Hyderabad, for collaboration in the field of Tethered drone systems and 
multi-barrel loitering munition systems and other Aerial Platforms for the Indian defence forces and 
government requirements. 
This MoU marks a wider scope for Apollo in Aerial Platforms and Loitering Munitions. It brings 
together the explosive and energetics expertise of the Apollo Group companies with the Company’s 
proven experience in fuzes, safety and arming mechanisms and avionics, enabling Apollo to offer 
complete, indigenous loitering munition and aerial platform solutions to the Indian Armed Forces. 
The MoU further opens a wider scope for the Company in Unmanned Aerial Warfare and 
Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance (ISR) applications, in line with the Government of 
India’s Aatmanirbhar Bharat initiative.
