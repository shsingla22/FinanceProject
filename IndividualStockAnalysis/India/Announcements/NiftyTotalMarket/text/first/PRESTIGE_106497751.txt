Prestige Estates Announces Operational results for Q3 and 9M FY 26, Records 
Highest-Ever Sales (₹223,273 million) and Collections (₹132,833 million) in 
9M FY26 
Bengaluru, 14th January, 2026:  Prestige Estates Projects Limited announced its operational 
performance for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2025 (Q3 FY26 and 9M 
FY26), delivering record-breaking sales and collections alongside sustained execution 
momentum.
 
Operational Highlights 
Sales Performance 
 The Company recorded pre-sales of ₹41,836 million in Q3 FY26 , representing a 39% 
year-on-year growth . 
 For the nine months ended FY26, pre-sales surged to ₹223,273 million , up 122% year-
on-year, marking the highest sales ever achieved by the and surpassing Prestige’s 
previous full-year peak sales; all within just nine months . This landmark 
performance rea Ưirms the exceptional demand visibility for the Company’s portfolio 
and the strong resonance of its o Ưerings across key markets.  
 Sales volume for Q3 FY26 stood at 2.99 million sq. ft. , while cumulative sales volume 
for 9M FY26 reached 16.95 million sq. ft.  
 During Q3 FY26, the Company sold 1,811 units , taking total units sold in 9M FY26 to 
8,598 units . 
 The geographical sales mix remained well diversiﬁed, with Mumbai contributing 36% , 
Bengaluru 25% , Hyderabad 16% , NCR 16% , Chennai 5%  and Kochi 2%  of total sales, 
during the quarter.  
 Average realization during the quarter stood at ₹14,459 per sq. ft. , reﬂecting a 6% year-
on-year growth . 
 Plot realizations continued to see strong traction, with average realization at ₹9,165 per 
sq. ft., recording a 31% year-on-year increase . 
 
Collections 
 Collections for Q3 FY26 stood at ₹45,475 million , registering a 40% year-on-year 
growth.
