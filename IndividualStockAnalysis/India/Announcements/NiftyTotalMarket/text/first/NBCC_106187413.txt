Subject ( डवषय) :- Shri K.P. Mahadevaswamy, CMD NBCC  elected  as Chairman of 
“SCOPE”  
 
Sir, 
           This is to inform that Shri K. P. Mahadevaswamy CMD, NBCC has been  elected as Chairman of  
“Standing Conference of Public Enterprises  (SCOPE )” on March 27, 2025 . 
           Standing Conference of Public Enterprises (SCOPE), is an apex body of Public Sector Enterprises 
(PSEs) .  
           Representing PSEs through policy advocacy with Govt. auth orities, statutory & regulatory 
bodies has been one of the key objectives of SCOPE. Nationally, SCOPE is closely associated 
with the Department of Public Enterprises, Ministry of Finance; Ministry of Labour and 
Employment; Ministry of Skill Development & E ntrepreneurship; Ministry of Corporate Affairs 
and interfaces with statutory and regulatory bodies such as CIC, CVC, CCI etc. to highlight 
issues concerning the Public Sector fraternity.  
                  Internationally, SCOPE has been representing employ ers’ organization on international 
forums such as the International Labour Organization (ILO); United Nations (UN); Organization 
for Economic Co -operation and Development (OECD) and International Organization of 
Employers (IOE).
