Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that the Board of Directors of 
Zaggle Prepaid Ocean Services Limited (the Company) at their meeti ng held today, i.e., 
November 6, 2026  has considered, discussed and approved following matters:  
 
(a) Appointment of Mr. Venkatesh Ramachandran  as Group Chief Strategy and 
Transformation Officer and cessation from the office of the Group Chief Financial Officer (Key 
Managerial Personnel and Senior Management):  
 
Mr. Venkatesh Ramachandran has been promoted as the Group Chief Strategy and 
Transformation Officer of the Company effective from November 6, 2026, to strengthen and 
focus on efforts required for shaping, refining and executing Zaggle’s high -priority strategic 
structuring initiatives and driving long term enterprise value.  
 
Accordingly, Venkatesh Ramachandran, vide his letter dated November 5, 2026 has resigned 
from the office of Group Chief Financial Officer (Key Managerial Personnel and Senior 
Management) of the Company with effect from the close of business hours of November 5, 
2026. The copy of said letter is attached as Annexure . 
 
(b) Appointment of Mr. Samar Vijay Pupala  as the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) (Key 
Managerial Personnel and Senior Management):  
 
Based on the recommendation of the Audit Committee and the Nomination and Remuneration 
Committee, the Board of Directors has appointed Mr. Samar Vijay Pupala  as the Chief Financial 
Officer (CFO) (Key Managerial Personnel and Senior Management)  of the Company under the 
provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 , effective from November 6, 2026.
