HCLTech partners with MumbaiHacks to accelerate India’s AI innovation 
and talent ecosystem  
 
  
MUMBAI, India, Nov 25, 2025 — HCLTech , a leading global technology company and one 
of the world’s top employers, is joining forces with MumbaiHacks 2025 to advance India’s AI 
innovation and talent ecosystem. Taking place November 28– 29 at the NESCO Exhibition 
Center, Mumbai, MumbaiHacks brings together 3,000+ top innovators from across India to 
build real -world solutions in HealthTech, FinTech and Misinformation, with a special focus on 
Agentic AI.  
 
As the official presenting partner, HCLTech will showcase its leadership in AI, GenAI and Agentic AI, engage with top AI talent and collaborate with the broader startup and academic 
ecosystem in this space. HCLTech’s involvement includes keynote presentati ons, AI 
innovation sessions, mentorship engagements and hands -on activations at its 'Supercharge 
Studio by HCLTech' booth space, demonstrating its commitment to nurturing the next 
generation of AI leaders in India.  
 Speaking of the partnership, Vijay Guntur, CTO and Head of Ecosystems at HCLTech, said: 
“AI’s future will be built by the next generation of innovators. As one of the world’s top 
employers and a global leader in Advanced AI, HCLTech is committed to nurturi ng the 
brightest talent in this space. By sponsoring MumbaiHacks 2025, we are proud to empower 
young minds across India to shape the AI solutions and technologies of tomorrow.”  
 
 “Mumbai Hacks 2025 is a critical platform for unleashing Agentic AI and Advanced AI 
innovation in India. We are thrilled to be hosting over 3000 bright minds tackling complex challenges under one roof. It is not just a competition; it’s a crucible for the next generation 
of founders who will solidify our city’s position as a global AI hub,” said Vivek Khemani, Co-
founder, Quantiphi, and Governing Council Member, TEAM. “We, at TEAM, are very excited to have HCLTech, a recognized leader in this space, joinin g us as the presenting partner, 
which gives significant momentum to our mission,” added Dhruvil Sanghvi, Founder and 
CEO, LogiNext and founding director, TEAM.  
 
For more information, visit www.mumbaihacks.com .
