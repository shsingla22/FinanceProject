Press Release 
Choice International Announces closing of X900 Crore Strategic 
Investment by NH Investment & Securities in Choice Equity Broking 
October 27,2026, Mumbai - Choice International Limited {“Choice”), a diversified financial services 
group, is pleased to announce the successful closing of the strategic investment by NH Investment & 
Securities Co., Ltd. (“NHIS"), a leading South Korean financial institution and subsidiary of NH Financial 
Group, in Choice Equity Broking Private Limited (“CEBPL”), its broking subsidiary. 
CEBPL has received today the fuil subscription consideration of %8,99,99,95,500 (approximately X900 
crore) and allotted 42,25,350 fully paid-up Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (“CCPS”) to 
NHIS at 2,130 per share, pursuant to the Subscription Agreement dated July 9, 2026. 
The closing marks the culmination of the transaction announced in July 2026 and establishes a 
strategic partnership between the two financial services institutions. The proceeds will be deployed 
towards strengthening CEBPL's growth capabilities, including expansion of its Margin Trading Facility 
(“MTF”) business, technology infrastructure, customer acquisition, talent development and product 
innovation. 
Beyond the capital infusion, the partnership provides an opportunity to combine Choice’s 
understanding of the Indian market and domestic distribution network with NHIS’s international 
institutional expertise. Collaboration across institutional broking, research, capital markets, risk 
management, governance and operational practices is expected to support CEBPL’s long-term 
competitiveness and business development. 
Management Commentary 
Mr. Arun Poddar, CEO & Executive Director, Choice International Limited, said: 
The closing of this investment marks an important milestone in Choice’s journey to build an enduring 
financial services institution. Beyond strengthening our broking business and technology capabilities, 
it supports our larger mission of making financial services more accessible and empowering more 
Indians to participate in the country’s growth. 
Aligned with Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi’s vision of Viksit Bharat 2047, we believe financial 
inclusion and awareness are essential to ensuring that economic progress translates into wider 
prosperity. By combining technology with our on-ground distribution network, we aim to reach 
underserved communities, build financial confidence and enable long-term wealth creation. As we 
expand, we remain focused on building a sustainable, institutionally stronger business that creates 
lasting value for our customers, shareholders and the nation.

About Choice International Limited 
Choice International Limited is a diversified financial services and consulting group headquartered in 
Mumbai and listed on BSE and NSE. Serving over 1.6 million clients through a network of 70,000+ 
business associates, the Group offers technology-driven solutions across broking, wealth 
management, lending, insurance, investment banking, asset management, and government and 
infrastructure consultancy. Recognised as a Great Place to Work, Choice combines its pan-India 
distribution network with digital capabilities to expand access to financial services and support long- 
term wealth creation. The Group remains committed to advancing financial inclusion and creating 
lasting value for its clients, shareholders and communities. 
About Choice Equity Broking Private Limited 
Choice Equity Broking Private Limited (CEBPL), a subsidiary of Choice International Limited, is among 
India’s leading retail brokers, serving over 1.6 million customers through a network of branches and 
authorised persons. The company offers technology-led investment and trading solutions to retail, 
HNI and institutional clients across equity, derivatives, currency and commodity markets, alongside 
depository services. Combining its extensive branch network with digital capabilities, CEBPL is 
committed to expanding access to investment opportunities and enabling greater participation in 
India’s capital markets.
