Kaynes Technology India Limited  |   Press Release  |  2 2 August 2026  KAYNES TECHNOLOGY INDIA LIMITED   |  PRESS RELEASE  
BOSGAME ENTERS INDIA WITH KAYNES TECHNOLOGY; 
COMPANIES SIGN STRATEGIC MoU TO BUILD A STRONG 
COMPUTING PRESENCE IN INDIA  
 
Collaboration brings together BOSGAME’s intelligent computing portfolio with Kaynes  
Technology’s engineering, manufacturing and market execution capabilities  
India | 2 2 August 2026: Kaynes Technology India Limited and BOSGAME today announced the signing of 
a strategic Memorandum of Understanding (MoU), marking an important step towards e stablishing and 
expanding BOSGAME’s presence in the Indian market.  
The collaboration brings together BOSGAME’s product innovation and intelligent computing portfolio 
with Kaynes Technology’s extensive capabilities in electronics design, engineering, integr ated 
manufacturing, testing, supply -chain management and lifecycle support.  
BOSGAME’s product design has earned international recognition, including the American Good Design 
Award, the French Design Award and the MUSE Design Awards, underscoring the brand’ s design -led 
engineering approach as it expands into India.  
 
BOSGAME Design Recognition – American Good Design, French Design Award, MUSE Design Awards  
Through this partnership, the companies intend to create a strong platform for introducing BOSGAME 
prod ucts to the Indian market, developing market channels and building a locally supported customer 
ecosystem.  
The collaboration will also explore opportunities for product localisation, value addition and deeper 
participation in India’s rapidly evolving elect ronics ecosystem, aligned with market requirements and 
long -term growth opportunities.  
The MoU was signed on behalf of Kaynes Technology India Limited by Mr. Jaydeep Jadav, Head – Brand 
Business, with Ms. Lucky Wang, Vice President, Six United, representin g BOSGAME.  

Kaynes Technology India Limited  |   Press Release  |  2 2 August 2026   
MoU Signing Ceremony – Kaynes Technology, BOSGAME and Six United  
Mr. Jaydeep Jadav, Head – Brand Business, Kaynes Technology India Limited, said:  
“India is entering an exciting phase in electronics where global product innovation is increasingly 
being complemented by strong local engineering, manufacturing and customer support capabilities. 
Our collaboration with BOSGAME is aligned with this transition. We look forward to combining 
BOSGAME’s product strengths with Kaynes Technology’s capabilities to build a scalable and 
sustainable presence for the brand in India.”  
Ms. Lucky Wang, Vice President, Six United, BOSGAME, said:  
“India is an important technology market with significant long -term potential. We are pleased to 
collaborate with  Kaynes Technology, whose engineering, manufacturing and execution capabilities 
provide a strong foundation for our India journey. Together, we look forward to bringing innovative 
computing solutions closer to Indian customers and building a meaningful pre sence in the market.”  
The partnership comes at a time when India is strengthening its position across the electronics value 
chain, with increasing emphasis on design -led manufacturing, local value creation, supply -chain 
resilience and technology -driven pr oduct development.  
By combining international product innovation with Indian engineering and manufacturing capabilities, 
Kaynes Technology and BOSGAME aim to create a strong foundation for the next phase of BOSGAME’s 
growth in India.  
About Kaynes Technolog y India Limited  
Kaynes Technology India Limited is an end -to-end integrated electronics manufacturer in India and a 
Production -Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme holder across IT Hardware, IoT and Telecom, with capabilities across 
the Electronics System Design and Manufacturing spectrum. With more than three decades of experience, the 
company provides services spanning conceptual design, process engineering, integrated manufacturing and 
lifecycle support across multiple industry verticals.  
About BOSGAME  
Founded in 2019, BOSGAME is a technology company focused on intelligent computing products, including 
mini PCs and laptop product lines. The company integrates R&D, production and sales, with Shenzhen serving 
as its operational hub, and is focused on delivering in telligent hardware products to customers globally.
