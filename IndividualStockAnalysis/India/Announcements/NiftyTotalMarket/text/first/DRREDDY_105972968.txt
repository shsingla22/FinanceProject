and concluded that the production quality

the NMPA, has decided to cancel Dr. Reddy's 
Laboratories Ltd.'s Atomoxetine Hydrochloride 
Capsules “won” status and list the company on the 
"Violation List", suspending the company's eligibility 
to participate in national centralized drug procurement 
activities from August 30, 2024 to February 28, 2026.
