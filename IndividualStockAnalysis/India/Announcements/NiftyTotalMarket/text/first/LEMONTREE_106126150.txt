Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property  
 in Gujarat  
 
Anand, 27th January 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels announce s its latest signing —Lemon Tree Hotel,  
Anand, Gujarat.  The property, which shall be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a 
wholly -owned subsidiary of  Lemon Tree Hotels Limited , is expected to start its operations in FY 
2029.   
 
Anand, Gujarat, is best known for being the heart of the White Revolution which started in 1970 
and intended to make India a self -dependent nation in milk production. Anand, the headquarters 
of Am ul, the biggest dairy in Asia, is a mix of cultural and natural attractions. One can explore the 
Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel National Memorial for a historical perspective, or take a tour of the Amul 
Dairy Factory to witness the making of fa mous dairy products. Nature enthusiasts can relax at 
Kadiya Muvada Lake or enjoy the serene views along the Narmada Canal. The city also features 
spiritual spots like the Swaminarayan Mandir and other temples. Anand's central location makes 
it easy to visi t nearby attractions such as the Ranchhodraiji Temple and Jambughoda Wildlife 
Sanctuary.  
 
Lemon Tree Hotel, Anand, Gujarat will feature 60 well -appointed rooms, a restaurant, a banquet, 
a meeting room, a swimming pool, a spa and other public areas. The nearest airport, Vadodara 
Airport, is approximately 44 kms away from the property while the Anand Railway Station is about 
5 kms away.  The hotel is also well connected by roadways for both public and private transport.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, Lemon Tree 
Hotels commented, “ Gujarat, an economic powerhouse, is known for its rich cultural heritage, 
diverse landscapes, and vibrant history  has become an important focus in the rapid expansion of 
our portfolio . This opening will be in addition to our seven existing and 17 upcoming hotels in the 
state.”  
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTH) is one of the largest hotel chains in India, and 
owns/leases/operate s/franchises hotels across the upscale, upper -midscale, midscale and 
economy segments. It delivers differentiated yet superior service offerings, with a compelling 
value proposition. The group offers seven brands to meet guests’ needs across all levels, vi z. 
Aurika Hotels & Resorts, Lemon Tree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox Hotels by Lemon Tree 
Hotels, Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels and Keys Lite by 
Lemon Tree Hotels.  
 
LTH opened its first hotel with 49 rooms in May 2 004. Today, the company has a portfolio of 200+ 
hotels, which includes over 110 operational hotels and more than 90 hotels set to open in India 
and internationally. Lemon Tree Hotels are located across India, in key metro regions including 
the NCR, Mumbai,  Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Chennai, as well as numerous tier I, II and 
III cities such as Pune, Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, Jaipur, Indore, Aurangabad, Udaipur, 
Visakhapatnam, Kochi, Ludhiana, Thiruvananthapuram, Vijayawada, etc. The company has also 
expanded internationally to Dubai, Bhutan and Nepal.
