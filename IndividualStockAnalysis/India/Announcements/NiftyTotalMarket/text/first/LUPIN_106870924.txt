Lupin Receives Approval from U.S. FDA for Dalbavancin Injection   
for Single-dose Regimen Use 
 
Mumbai, India, Naples, FL, November 25, 2026:  Global pharma major, Lupin Limited (Lupin)  today 
announced that it has received approval from the United States Food and Drug Administration (U.S. FDA) 
for its New Drug Application for Dalbavancin Injection, 500 mg/  5 mL (100 mg/mL) single-dose vial via 
the 505(b)(2) pathway. The product is approved for single-dose regimen use only. 
 
Lupin's Dalbavancin Injection, 500 mg/5 mL single dose vial is a liquid formulation of dalbavancin, the active 
ingredient in Dalvance® (dalbava ncin) 500 mg base/vial of AbbVi e and provides a ready-to-dilute 
formulation designed to simplify preparation. 
 Lupin's Dalbavancin Injection, 500 mg/5 mL single dose vial is indicated as a single-dose regimen for the 
treatment of acute bacterial skin and skin structure infections  (ABSSSI) in adult and pediatric patients 
caused by designated susceptible strains of Gram-positive micro organisms.
