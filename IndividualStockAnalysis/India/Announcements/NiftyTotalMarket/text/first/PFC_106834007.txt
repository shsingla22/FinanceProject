PFC Consulting Limited (a wholly owned subsidiary of Power Finance Corporation Limited) has 
been nominated as the 'Bid Process Coordinator' (BPC) for selection of developer through tariff
based competitive bidding for the Independent Transmission Projects (JTPs) by Ministry of Power, 
Gol. 
As per the provisions oftariffbased competitive bidding Guidelines issued by the Ministry of Power,

clearance, if required. For these activities, One Special Purpose Vehicle (SPY) is required to be
incorporated which will take up various preparatory activities of the transmission project e.g. survey
and preparation of report, initialization of the process of acquisition of land and also initiate the
process of seeking forest clearance, if required. 
The SPY has to be transferred to the Successful Bidder selected through International Competitive
Bidding Process under Tariff Based Competitive Bidding Guidelines.
