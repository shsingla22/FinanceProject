Media  Release  
CDSL Receives Great Place To Work® Certification  
The recognition reflects CDSL’s commitment to building a workplace culture  centred on trust, 
inclusion, well -being and professional growth  
Mumbai, November  03, 2026:  Central Depository Services (India) Limited (“CDSL”), Asia’s first 
listed depository  servicing 19.6+ crore demat accounts, has received the Great Place To Work® 
Certification for the period  October 2026 to October 2027 . The recognition  is based on an 
independent assessment of workplace experience and culture, reflecting CDSL’s continued efforts 
to foster a collaborative, inclusive , and high trust  work environment.  
The Great Place To Work® Certification is a globally recognized  benchmark of workplace 
excellence, based on employee feedback through its Trust Index Survey. The assessment 
evaluates key dimensions  of workplace experience, including credibility, respect, fairness, pride 
and camaraderie.  
Speaking on the recognition, Shri Nehal Vora, Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer , 
CDSL , said,  “At CDSL, we have always believed that institutions are ultimately built by people. As our 
responsibilit y to India’s securities market ha s grown, so has our responsibility to create an 
environment where our people can learn, contribute and grow together.   
The recognition  is a testament to the belief  that our colleagues have in the leadership , and the pride 
they have in being part of the CDSL  team . This only reinforces our continued focus on building a 
workplace anchored in trust, belonging and shared purpose.  
We receive this recognition with gratitude, but also with the understanding that culture is never a 
finished  project. It is something we must continue to strengthen every day. Our endeavour remains 
to build CDSL as an institution where people feel trusted, valued and connected to the larger purpose  
of empower ing every Indian to become an Atmanirbhar Investor. ” 
CDSL ’s approach to human capital development encompasses continuous learning, leadership 
engagement, and holistic well -being. During FY 2025 -26, the organization  provided an average of 
21 hours  of training per employee.  Platforms such as Townhalls, Guru Samvaad  (a knowledge -
sharing platform that enables employees to learn directly from experienced leaders and subject -
matter experts ), Open House sessions and the Digital Suggestion Box provide avenues for 
dialogue, participation and exchange of ideas across the organization . 
CDSL has also continued  to conduct  program s addressing the  physical, psychological, financial , 
and social well -being  of its employees . Its focus on inclusion  was recognized  earlier this year with 
the ‘Best Institution for Diversity, Equity and Inclusion ’ award  at the Asia Asset Management Best 
of the Best Awards 2026.  
The Great Place To Work® Certification reinforces CDSL ’s focus on investing in its people and 
further strengthening its organizational  culture.  
 
 
 
 
  
About  CDSL:    
Established  in 1999,  Central  Depository  Services  (India)  Limited  (CDSL ) set out with  a clear  
mission:  to offer  convenient,  dependable,  and secure  depository  services.  Over  the past  25 years  
CDSL  has played  a pivotal  role  in strengthening  India’s  securities  market  by enabling  
dematerialisation  and digital  services,  improving  ease  of doing  business,  and fostering  inclusive  
market  participation . 
 
As a Market  Infrastructure  Institution  (MII),  CDSL  enables  the electronic  holding,  settlement,  and 
transfer  of securities,  serving  a wide  range  of market  participants  including  depository  
participants,  issuers,  investors,  RTAs,  clearing  corporations,  and exchanges . 
 
CDSL  is distinguished  globally  as the first  listed  depository  in Asia,  managing  over  19.34  crore  
demat  accounts,  partnering  with  more  than  590 depository  participants.  Driven  by technology -
led innovation,  CDSL  has received  multiple  global  recognitions,  including  "Most  Innovative  
Fintech  Company  in Asia -Pacific"  at Global  Finance  Magazine's  The Innovators  2026:  Asia -Pacific  
awards,  ", “Best  Institution  for Diversity,  Equi ty, and Inclusion  (DEI)  - Asia”  by Asia  Asset  
Management  at the Best  of the Best  Awards  2026,   ‘Market  Infrastructure  of the Year’  at the 
Regulation  Asia  Awards  for Excellence  (2024  & 2025),  ‘CSD  of the Year’  at the Asset  Servicing  
Times  Industry  Excellenc e Awards  (2023  & 2025),  and ‘Innovation  in Market  Infrastructure’  at 
the Leaders  in Custody  Asia  Awards  2025.  
 
Additionally,  CDSL's  subsidiaries  offer  extensive  services  to financial  intermediaries  and markets:  
• CDSL  Ventures  Limited  (CVL),  a wholly  owned  subsidiary,  is India's  first  and largest  KYC  
Registration  Agency  (KRA)  and offers  services  including  Registrar  & Share  Transfer  Agent  
(RTA),  GST  Suvidha  Provider  (GSP),  e-Sign,  and e-KYC.  
• Centrico  Insurance  Repository  Limited  (CIRL)  (formerly  known  as ‘CDSL  Insurance  
Repository  Limited’),  registered  under  the Companies  Act, 1956,  is certified  by the IRDAI  
as an ‘Insurance  Repository’.  
• Countrywide  Commodity  Repository  Limited  (CCRL)  (formerly  known  as ‘CDSL  
Commodity  Repository  Limited’),  provides  electronic  warehouse  receipts  (eNWRs  or 
eNNWRs)  for commodities  stored  in WDRA -registered  warehouses,  supporting  Farmers,  
Farmers  Producer  Organizations  (FPOs),  and Manufacturers.
