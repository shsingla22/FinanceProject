In terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, we would 
like to inform that the Company is in receipt of notices from NSE and BSE for imposing 
the penalty of Rs. 5,36,900/- each for the quarter ended 31st March, 2024, for non­
compliance of Regulation 17(1) in respect of composition of Board of Directors due to 
insufficient number of Independent Directors. 
2. In this regard, it may be noted that Hindustan Aeronautics Limited is a 
Government Company under Ministry of Defence. Since, appointment of Independent 
Directors in the Government Companies are being made by the concerned 
Administrative Department of the Government of India, multiple requests were made 
for filling the vacant posts of Independent Directors in the Company. However, the 
same are yet to be filled. 
3. Since, the matter was beyond the control of the Company, applications for waiver 
of fines under Sl. No. 3(a) of the Policy of exemption of fines levied as per the 
provisions of SEBI SOP Circular, were filed with NSE, Designated Stock Exchange for 
the purpose, representing our case, however it was directed to put up the case only 
after compliance.
