  Press Release – Q2FY27  
 
Mumbai, October 29, 2026: Greaves Cotton Limited (BSE: 501455 NSE: GREAVESCOT) one of  
India's leading diversified engineering companies, announced its unaudited financial results for the  
quarter ended September 30, 2026.  
 
 
Consolidated  
 Revenue for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹1,176 crore , up 44% YoY . H1 FY27 revenue stood at ₹2,150 
crore, up 38% YoY. 
 EBITDA increased to ₹73 crore  in Q2 FY27 up by 43% YoY  from ₹51 crore  in Q2 FY26, while 
H1 FY27 EBITDA stood at ₹130 crore  compared with ₹108 crore  in the corresponding period 
last year.  
 Business PBT for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹44 crore , while H1 FY27 Business PBT stood at ₹71 
crore. 
 Reported exceptional and one-off items  of ₹60 crore in Q2FY27.  
 PBT for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹-16 crore , while H1 FY27 PBT stood at ₹11 crore.  
Standalone  
 Revenue for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹682 crore , up 24% YoY , while H1 FY27 revenue grew 20% 
YoY to ₹1,312 crore . 
 EBITDA stood at ₹69 crore  in Q2 FY27 and ₹140 crore  in H1 FY27.  
 PBT for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹56 crore , while H1 FY27 PBT stood at ₹123 crore.  
Greaves Cotton Advances GREAVES.NEXT Strategy; Delivers Strong Growth Across its Core 
and Investee Businesses in Q2FY27   
 
Reports consolidated revenue of ₹1,176 crore in Q2 FY27 (H1 FY27: ₹2,150 crore)  and 
standalone revenue of ₹682 crore (H1 FY27: ₹1,312 crore) . Consolidated revenue grew 
44% YoY in Q2 FY27 and 38% YoY  in H1 FY27, driven by strong performance across both 
Core and Investee Businesses.  
Correspondingly EBITDA grew 43% YoY  in Q2FY27 and 20% YoY  in H1FY27.  
Greaves Cotton's Core Businesses recorded 24% YoY  growth in Q2 FY27 and 20% growth 
in H1 FY27, reflecting continued momentum across Energy Solutions, Mobility Solutions and 
Industrial Solutions.  
GEML consolidated revenue of ₹410 crore in Q2 FY27  (H1 FY27: ₹680 crore)  registering 
106% YoY growth in Q2 FY27 and  103% YoY growth in H1 FY27 . 
Launches new products and variants across the Electric mobility (Ampere Nexus EX+ and 
Magnus G-Max) and Core Businesses (750 HP firefighting engine, 470 cc diesel engine for 
3W applications).  
Financial Performance  
 
 
Parag Satpute, MD & Group CEO, Greaves Cotton Limited, said:  
“Our Q2 FY27 performance reflects disciplined execution across our diversified portfolio, with strong 
growth across our Core Businesses and continued progress on the priorities under GREAVES.NEXT. 
Our diversified portfolio enabled us to deliver robust revenue growth while advancing key strategic 
initiatives across Energy Solutions, Mobility Solutions and Industrial Solutions. During the quarter, we 
strengthened our product portfolio, expanded customer reach, advanced our international presence and 
continued to invest in technology, engineering capabilities and customer-centric solutions.”  
"We remained focused on driving operational excellence through pricing discipline, value engineering, 
productivity enhancements and cost optimisation initiatives, while continuing to invest in strategic growth 
opportunities across our businesses. As we enter the second half of FY27, our priorities remain clear: 
sustaining growth, strengthening operating and financial performance, expanding our international 
footprint and building higher-value customer solutions. With a strong engineering foundation, a diversified 
business portfolio and disciplined execution, we remain committed to creating sustainable long-term 
value for our shareholders and all stakeholders." 
 
 
Energy Solutions  delivered strong growth during Q2 FY27, with revenue increasing 36% YoY  and 28% 
YoY in H1 FY27 , supported by continued momentum in the domestic diesel genset market. Growth was 
led by the MHP genset portfolio (160-750 kVA), which grew 35% YoY in H1 FY27 , while the 
aftermarket business expanded 15% YoY , reinforcing recurring revenue streams and customer 
engagement. During the quarter, the business further strengthened its technology-enabled service 
offerings and expanded its export footprint through deliveries of enhanced gensets to a key Middle East 
market, supporting its focus on profitable growth, customer-centric innovation and market expansion. 
Mobility Solutions  continued to deliver strong growth during Q2 FY27, registering 23% YoY growth  and 
achieving 22% YoY growth in H1 FY27 . The Automotive Engines business strengthened its market 
position through continued momentum in core segments and the launch of the G470 diesel engine , 
enhancing the Company's powertrain portfolio and supporting future growth opportunities. The 
Engineered Components  business delivered robust 30% YoY growth , supported by increased 
customer engagement across domestic and export markets. During the period, the business secured 
new export orders, including from Tata Motors Indonesia, further expanding its international footprint and 
reinforcing its competitiveness in global markets. The Retail business maintained stable performance, 
supported by its established distribution network and customer reach. 
The continued growth across Mobility Solutions reflects the Company's focus on strengthening its Core 
Businesses, increasing engineering-led content, expanding export opportunities, and enhancing its 
product portfolio, positioning the business to capture growth opportunities across automotive and mobility 
ecosystems. 
International Business  remained a strategic pillar under GREAVES.NEXT, contributing approximately 
13% of Core Business revenue during H1FY27 . The Company's long-term objective is to build stronger 
market presence, customer relationships and partnerships across key international markets. 
 Management Commentary  
Business Highlights  
Greaves Electric Mobility Limited (GEML)  delivered a strong growth performance during H1 FY27, 
driven by sustained momentum across both electric two-wheelers and L5 electric three-wheelers. 
Ampere electric two-wheeler VAHAN registrations grew 114% YoY in Q2 FY27 , while the business 
continued to strengthen its market position, achieving 4.8% market share during H1 FY27 . The L5 
three-wheeler business recorded 74% YoY VAHAN registration  growth, supported by expanding 
customer adoption and deeper market penetration. 
The Company's operating performance reflected the benefits of scale and improved execution. 
Consolidated revenue more than doubled to ₹410 crore in Q2 FY27 , compared to ₹199 crore in Q2 
FY26, representing 106% YoY growth . At the same time, EBITDA losses reduced by half year-on-year, 
reflecting improved operating leverage and margin expansion. 
GEML continued to scale its distribution network, with dealer count up 31% YoY  and dealer throughput 
improving 39%, expanding its reach to 600+ touchpoints , supported by growing fleet and e-commerce 
channels 
The business also advanced its innovation roadmap, including the development of a patented rare-
earth-free motor architecture , reinforcing its commitment to technology-led and sustainable mobility 
solutions. With accelerating revenue growth, improving market share, enhanced operating leverage and 
a scalable distribution network, GEML is well positioned to drive profitable growth and create long-term 
value as EV adoption continues to gain momentum in India. 
Greaves Finance Limited (GFL)  continued to expand its EV financing franchise, with AUM growing 
65% YoY to ₹630 crore . During the quarter launched an E3W L5 leasing product , and raised ₹110 
crore of debt  to support future growth. This reflects GFL's continued focus on enabling EV adoption 
through innovative and accessible financing solutions. 
 
During Q2 FY27, the Company continued to advance its GREAVES.NEXT  strategy by strengthening its 
Core Businesses, expanding customer reach, accelerating product development and enhancing its 
international market presence. The Company remains focused on building higher-value customer 
solutions across Energy Solutions, Mobility Solutions and Industrial Solutions, while continuing to invest 
in technology, exports, engineering capabilities and customer-centric innovation. 
The Company also remains focused on increasing engineering-led differentiation and value addition 
across its portfolio. By enhancing the engineering content of its products and solutions, strengthening 
innovation capabilities and expanding into higher-value applications, Greaves aims to improve 
competitiveness, deepen customer engagement and create sustainable long-term growth opportunities. 
Going forward, the Company will prioritise margin recovery, operational excellence and disciplined capital 
allocation. With a diversified business portfolio, a strengthening international presence and a continued 
focus on innovation and execution, Greaves Cotton remains committed to delivering sustainable long-
term value for all stakeholders. The Company remains focused on translating its growth momentum into 
stronger earnings, improved return ratios and long-term value creation for shareholders. 
 
 Outlook 
 
With a legacy of over 165 years, Greaves Cotton Limited is a diversified, future-ready engineering company 
delivering innovative and sustainable solutions to customers across key sectors. Renowned for its precision 
engineering and technology leadership, the Company’s growth strategy is anchored on three core dimensions: 
accelerating its Core Businesses, building new muscle onto the core, and expanding into new horizons across 
Energy Solutions, Mobility Solutions, and Industrial Solutions.  
As one of India’s largest manufacturers of single-cylinder diesel engines, and a pioneer in introducing them for 
microcar applications in the European market, Greaves continues to reinforce its commitment to accessible, 
efficient, and reliable mobility. Guided by its purpose of ‘Empowering Lives’, Greaves is committed to providing 
reliable products, sustainable technology, and customer-centric solutions. With its strong engineering foundation 
and continuous innovation, the Company aims to enable people, businesses, and communities to progress with 
confidence in a future rooted in engineering excellence and driven by efficient energy.  
The Investee companies of Greaves include Greaves Electric Mobility Limited (GEML) with its diverse portfolio of 
electric 2 & 3 wheelers for passenger and cargo mobility, and Greaves Finance Limited (GFL) with ev.fin, a 100% 
EV focused NBFC that leverages cutting-edge technology to offer customised financing options & seamless buying 
experience. Both these businesses play a significant role in accelerating EV adoption in India.
