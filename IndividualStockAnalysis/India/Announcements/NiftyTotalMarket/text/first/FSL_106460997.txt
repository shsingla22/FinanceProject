Firstsource recognized as a Global Leader in Generative AI Services   
in ISG Provider Lens® 2025

provider of business process management services and an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company , has been recognized 
as a Leader  in Generative AI Services  by ISG Provider Lens® 2025 Generative AI Services Study . Firstsource 
earned Leader positions in both the Strategy & Consulting Services and Development & Deployment Services 
quadrants  for midsize companies , reflecting its ability to deliver scalable, responsible, and production -grade GenAI 
solutions for enterprises worldwide.  
 
ISG’s report highlights the growing demand for secure, repeatable, and outcome -driven GenAI platforms. Firstsource 
stands out for its  integrated end -to-end approach —combining domain maturity, AI innovation, and robust enterprise -
grade orchestration to help enterprises  move from pilots to industrialized adoption . The company’s modular GenAI 
platforms, such as its HealthTech platform and AI Coach, enable clients to validate, co -design, and rapidly scale GenAI 
use cases, particularly in regulated industries. The report also underscores how this end -to-end model sup ports 
organizations in moving from ideas to execution with confidence. By bringing together guidance, techno logy, and domain 
understanding, Firstsource enables  smoother adoption, responsible use, and scalable impact, helping enterprises 
innovate, adapt, and grow in an evolving AI landscape .
