In  continuation  of our  earlier  intimation  dated  06.02.2026  &  19.05.2026,  wherein,  we  had

from  45,00,00,000  Equity  Shares  of Rs  10/-each to  60,00,00,000  Equity  Shares  of Rs  10/-
