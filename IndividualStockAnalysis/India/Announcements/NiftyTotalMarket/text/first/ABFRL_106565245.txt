Dear Sir s, 
 
The Board of Directors (“Board”)  of the Company at its meeting held today i.e. Tuesday, March 24, 2026, have 
inter alia considered and approved the following matters:  
 
1. CEO, Pantaloons - Succession:  
 
Based on the recommendation of Nomination and Remuneration Committee  (“NRC”) , Board has appointed  
Mr. Suraj Bahirwani as CEO (Designate)  - Pantaloons  and Senior Managerial Personnel (“SMP”) of the 
Company with effect from April 1, 2026  and as  CEO - Pantaloons and SMP with effect from October 1, 2026 .   
 
Ms. Sangeeta Tanwani , CEO - Pantaloons and Whole -time Director (“WTD ”) & Key Managerial Personnel 
(“KMP”)  of the Company  will oversee a planned  and phased leadership transition of her role as  
CEO-Pantaloons and will continue to be CEO -Pantaloons till September  30, 2026 .  
 
From October 1, 2026 till her retirement on January 31, 2027 , she will support the Managing Director on 
various strategic matters  as Advisor . She will cease to be the WTD & KMP of the Company with effect from 
closure of business hours of January 31, 202 7. 
 
Brief details of appointment and cessation as required under SEBI Master Circular are  enclosed as  
Annexure A. 
 
2.  CEO , OWND!  - Appointment : 
 
Based on the recommendation of NRC, Board has appointed  Mr. Marco Agnolin as CEO - OWND ! and SMP  
of the Company  with effect from April 6, 2026.    
 
Brief details of appointment as required under SEBI Master Circular are  enclosed as Annexure B. 
 
3. Chief Financial Officer - Succession:  
 
Based on the recommendation of the Audit Committee and NRC , Board has appointed Mr. Nikhil Modha as 
Chief Financial Officer (Designate) & SMP  of the Company with effect from April 1, 2026 and as Chief 
Financial  Officer (“CFO”)  & KMP of the Company  with effect from January 1, 2027 .  
 
Mr. Jagdish Bajaj , CFO & KMP of the Company, will oversee a planned and phased leadership transition  until 
December 31, 2026. He will retire from the services of the Company and will also cease to be CFO & KMP
