Sub: India Gate Basmati Rice Brand  wins Silver  at IAMAI Digital Awards  
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
We are pleased to announce that India Gate Basmati Rice  Brand has won Silver at IAMAI Digital 
Awards for Best Use of Social Media for our Campaign “Ab Khaane Mein No Compromise”.  
 
This award category celebrates brand campaigns that harness the power of social media to build 
engagement, amplify brand presence, and drive results.  
 
Under this Campaign, our brand collaborated with food influencers nationwide and  placed hoardings in 
front of famous food joints communicating the message that ‘India Gate offers unmatched quality of 
basmati rice just like the taste of the famous dishes from their favourite iconic eateries’.  
 This campaign also aimed to celebrate the pride that locals take in their native delicious food, fostering 
a deep connection with their culinary traditions and everyday habits.  
 You are requested to kindly take the same on record.
