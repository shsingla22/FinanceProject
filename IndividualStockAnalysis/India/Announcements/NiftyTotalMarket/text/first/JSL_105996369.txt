Jindal Stainless Chairman, Mr Ratan Jindal, conferred with the Stainless Steel 
Executive of the Year Award  
 
New Delhi, September 23, 2024: Steel and Metals Market Research (SMR) GmbH, an international independent market research organisation, recently conferred the Stainless 
Steel Executive of the Year 2024 Award to Chairman, Jindal Stainless, Mr Ratan Jindal. The 
award was given to him for h is exceptional leadership, strategic vision, and ﬁrm commitment 
to driving Jindal Stainless ’ success in the stainless steel industry  globally. The award ceremony 
took place in Rome, Italy, as part of the three -day International Stainless & Special Steel 
Conference 2024.  
 Chairman, Jindal Stainless, Mr Ratan Jindal , said while receiving the honour, “Standing here 
today, I am overwhelmed with a deep sense of gratitude and humility as I receive the Stainle ss 
Steel Executive of the Year Award. While it is a moment of immense pride for me, it ’s also a 
reﬂection of the incredible journey of Jindal Stainless over the past 54 years.
 I want to express 
my deepest gratitude to my team and colleagues, partners and customers who have  been a 
part of this journey.  This award is as much theirs  as it is mine. It is their  hard work, dedication, 
and belief in our shared vision that has brought us to where we are today. All of them  have 
not only made my journey possible, but they  have also made it worthwhile. ” 
 
SMR presents this award annually to individuals who have made signiﬁcant advancements in 
the industry. The three -day conference also included panel discussions around varied topics 
such as fairness in the supply chain and CBAM, generational diﬀerences, labo ur shortag e, 
remote work , chromium as an enabler of sustainable and strategic applications , etc.  
 
Mr Jindal was honoured for his role in transforming India ’s stainless steel industry. Owing to 
his business acumen, Jindal Stainless  has emerged as a major player in t he global stainless  
steel market, renowned for its high -quality products, technological advancements, and 
sustainable practices.   
 His courage of conviction, strategic thinking, and ingenious ﬁnancial planning turned the 
company proﬁtable.  Some of his key milestones include commercialising  the 200 series of 
stainless steel in India; initiating usage of stainless steel in Indian Railways ’ wagons and 
coaches, replacing the conventionally used carbon steel; introducing blade steel and precision  
strips  and making Jindal Stainless  the top producer of razor blade  steel  in the world today;  
ﬁnancial turnaround of Jindal Stainless to make India’s largest and among the world ’s top 5 
stainless steel producing companies;  championing sustainable practice s, including achieving 
Net Zero carbon emissions by 2050; entering into a strategic agreement to set up a nickel pig

 Integrated operations have given Jindal Stainless the edge in cost competitiveness and
