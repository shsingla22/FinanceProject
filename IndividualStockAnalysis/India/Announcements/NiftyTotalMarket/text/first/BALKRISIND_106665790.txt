Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR Regulations”) and in accordance with SEBI Master 
Circular HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, we hereby inform you that 
the Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held on June 17, 2026, has taken note, considered and 
approved the following respectively: 
 
A. Superannuation of Mr. Madhusudan Bajaj, Chief Financial Officer 
     Mr. Madhusudan Bajaj ceases to be the Chief Financial Officer and Key Managerial Personnel of the 
Company upon attaining the age of superannuation, in line with the Company’s policy, with effect from 
the closing of business hours on June 17, 2026. 
 
The Board of Directors places on record its sincere appreciation for his valuable contribution and 
leadership during his tenure. 
 
Post his superannuation, Mr. Bajaj will continue to assist the Company as Special Advisor to the Chairman 
& Managing Director. This is not a case of resignation, and accordingly, submission of a resignation letter 
is not applicable. 
 
B. Appointment of Mr. Saroj Kumar Khuntia as Chief Financial Officer 
 
Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and Audit Committee, 
the Board of Directors has appointed Mr. Saroj Kumar Khuntia as the Chief Financial Officer and Key 
Managerial Personnel of the Company with effect from June 18, 2026.
