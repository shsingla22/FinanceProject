ITD Cementation India Limited  
- SECURED NEW MARINE CONTRACT WORTH ~RS 1,082 CRORE 
INCLUDING TAXES AND DUTIES  
 
 
June 26, 2024, Mumbai:   
 
ITD Cementation India Limited has secured a marine contract for constructing 
Third Berth (Jetty) and specified additional works at Dahej LNG terminal in 
the state of Gujarat  worth approximately Rs 1,082 crore including taxes and 
duties.
