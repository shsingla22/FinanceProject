Coforge Joins SpaceX Super Intelligence Partner Network to Take Grok into 
Enterprise Production  
 
PRINCETON, N.J. and GREATER NOIDA, India — November 6, 2026 — Coforge Limited (NSE: 
COFORGE), a global AI -native engineering services leader, today announced that it has joined the 
SpaceX Super Intelligence (SpaceXSI) Partner Network as a Transformation Partner. Coforge will help 
enterprises put the Grok portfolio to work in their business workflows, building solutions grounded in 
each client’s operating context and taking them into production with measurable business value.  
 
Coforge will take Grok into production through Momentuum blue, its specialized Forward Deployed 
Engineering (FDE) operating unit. Momentuum blue operates within client environments,  embedding 
pods of senior FDEs, associate FDEs, and AI agents that have full accountab ility for delivering business 
outcomes measured against an agreed baseline.  
 
The partnership is built on Coforge’s strengths in AI -led engineering and modernization services, Coforge 
Nuuron, a composable enterprise AI enablement suite, and Coforge's deep industry expertise. Together, 
these capabilities give organizations a path to integrate industry -specific Grok solutions into the systems 
they run today, and seamlessly connect enterprise knowledge, workflows, decisions, and actions.  
 
“Our opportunity with SpaceXSI is to help clients put the Grok portfolio to work across their businesses,” 
said Anup Nair, Executive Vice President and Chief AI Commercial Officer, Coforge.  “From 
software modernization and customer service to enterprise applications, we bring the industry context, 
engineering expertise and adoption capabilities to turn Grok’s technology into solutions that are 
grounded in how each client actually operates."  
 
The Grok portfolio includes Grok for chat, research, and analysis; Grok Models for reasoning; Grok Build 
for software development; Grok Bot for ongoing work across enterprise applications; Grok Voice for 
conversational experiences; and Grok Imagine for ima ge and video creation. The Grok API provides a 
route to integrate these capabilities into enterprise solutions.  
 
“Coforge pairs deep engineering and industry experience with a delivery model built to get Grok running 
in production, inside the systems clients depend on every day,” said Toni Adams, Head of Global 
Partnerships, SpaceXSI . “We’re glad to have Coforge in the network and look forward to working 
together to help our joint customers get measurable results from Grok.”  
 
The SpaceX Super Intelligence Partner Network is SpaceXSI’s official program for companies building 
products, solutions, and services with Grok for joint prospects and customers. Transformation Partners 
help customers deploy the Grok platform across the en terprise and create tangible business value.
