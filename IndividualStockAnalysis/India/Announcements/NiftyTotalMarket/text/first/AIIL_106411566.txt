Members holding equity shares of the Company on the Cut-off Date mentioned in this Postal Ballot Notice

concerned Member. 
 
Members desiring to exercise their vote through the e-voting process are requested to carefully read the 
instructions indicated in this Notice and record their assent (FOR) or dissent (AGAINST) by following the
