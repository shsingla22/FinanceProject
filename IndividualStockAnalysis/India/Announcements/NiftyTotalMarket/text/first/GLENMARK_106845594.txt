NEW YORK, NY, November 4, 2026: Ichnos Glenmark Innovation, Inc. (IGI), a global clinical -stage 
biotechnology company developing Multispecific ™ therapeutics in oncology, today 
announced that updated clinical data from the Phase 1 study of ISB  2001 (ABBV -2001) in 
relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma ( RRMM) will be presented as an oral presentation at the 
68th American Society of Hematology (ASH) Annual Meeting.  
“With longer follow -up from dose escalation and initial results from dose  expansion, ISB 2001 
(ABBV -2001)  continues to demonstrate robust efficacy and tolerability  in heavily pretreated 
patients with relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma ,” said Lida Pacaud, M.D., President and 
Chief Executive Officer of IGI . “These results represent an important milestone in the 
development of this first and only trispecific T -cell engager targeting CD38  x BCMA  x CD3 . 
Together with  our partner , AbbVie, we remain focused on advancing ISB 2001 (ABBV -2001)  as a 
potential new treatment option for patients with relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma ( RRMM) .”

About ISB  2001  (ABBV -2001 ) 
ISB 2001 (ABBV -2001) is a n investigational  first- and only -in-class  trispecific T -cell engager 
targeting CD38  and BCMA  on myeloma cells and CD3 on T cells that is in development for the 
treatment of relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma ( RRMM). Engineered using IGI`s BEAT® 
Multispecific Platform, ISB 2001 ( ABBV -2001 ) coordinates dual -antigen tumor recognition with 
immune engagement through T cells within a single molecular architecture. IGI and AbbVie 
formed an exclusive global licensing agreement for the trispecific in July 2025. ISB 2001 ( ABBV -
2001 ) is being evaluated in multiple myeloma through the Phase 1 TRIgnite -1 study and a Phase 
1/2 combination study.
