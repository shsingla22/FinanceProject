Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Inc., USA acquires and launches  
Acetylcysteine Injection, 6 gm/30 mL (200 mg/mL) Single-Dose Vials  
Mahwah, New Jersey, March 3, 2025 : Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Inc., USA (Glenmark) announces the 
acquisition and launch1 of Acetylcysteine Injection, 6 gm/30 mL (200 mg/mL) Single-Dose Vials. 
Glenmark’s Acetylcysteine Injection, 6 gm/30 mL (200 mg/mL) Single-Dose Vials can be expected to have 
the same therapeutic effect as the listed drug product upon which the Agency relied as the basis of safety 
and effectiveness, Acetadote®2 Injection, 6 gm/30 mL (200 mg/mL) Single-Dose Vials of Cumberland 
Pharmaceuticals Inc. Glenmark acquired the ANDA for Acetylcysteine Injection, 6 gm/30 mL (200 mg/mL) 
Single-Dose Vials from Aspen Pharma USA Inc. 
According to IQVIATM sales data for the 12-month period ending January 2025, the Acetadote® Injection, 
6 gm/30 mL (200 mg/mL) Single-Dose Vials market2 achieved annual sales of approximately $15.2 million*. 
Commenting on the launch, Marc Kikuchi, President & Business Head, North America said,  “We are 
excited to announce the acquisition and launch of Acetylcysteine Injection, 6 gm/30 mL (200 mg/mL) 
Single-Dose Vials.  This is a great addition to our injectable portfolio in the U.S. market as we continue to 
drive growth for the hospital segment.”
