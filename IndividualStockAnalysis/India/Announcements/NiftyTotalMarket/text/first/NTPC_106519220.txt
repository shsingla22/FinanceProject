Sir/Madam,  /  
 
In terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that based on the certificate received from 
Rajasthan Renewable Energy Corporation Limited (RREC) dated 3rd February, 2026, the 
second part capacity of 125 MW out of 500 MW Bhadla Solar PV Project located in Phalodi, 
Rajasthan of NTPC Renewable Energy Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of NTPC Green 
Energy Limited, is declared on Commercial Operation w.e.f. 00:00 hrs of 31.01.2026. 
 
The current commercial capacity of NTPC Green Energy Limited Group stands at 8688.25 
MW. With the addition of this capacity, the total installed capacity of the NTPC Green Energy 
Limited Group will increase to 8813.25 MW.
