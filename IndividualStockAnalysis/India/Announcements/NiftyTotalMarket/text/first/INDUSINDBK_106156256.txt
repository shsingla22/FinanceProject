IndusInd Bank partners with Professional Golf Tour of India as its Official Banking Partner  
Aimed to provide a premium experience to Bank’s PIONEER customers beyond just banking  
 
Mumbai, February 19, 2025:  IndusInd Bank has today announced its partnership with the 
Professional Golf Tour of India (PGTI) as its official Banking Partner.  This collaboration  
underscores the bank’s commitment to providing a superlative experience to its Ultra High Net 
Worth Individual s (UHNIs)  & High Net Worth Individual s (HNIs) under the umbrella of PIONEER  
banking . This partnership is an extension of various initiatives taken by IndusInd Bank over the 
years  that not only caters to the  diverse ba nking requirements  of UHNIs and HNIs , but also 
provides value -added premium experience aligned to their lifestyle and way of living , especially 
in the field of sports.   
 
As an exclusive Banking Partner, Bank will actively support PGTI events throughout the  
tournament , contributing to spread the reach  of the sport and strengthening India’s 
professional golfing landscape. Under the leadership of Kapil Dev, President - PGTI and 
cricketing legend , Amandeep Johl, CEO – PGTI, the tour features more than 20 tournaments 
across the year.  Under this partnership, IndusInd Bank ’s PIONEER customers stand a chance to 
participate in various engagement activities  across the tournament cities .  
 
IndusInd Bank’s PIONEER banking program offers a  comprehensive range of full -stack banking 
and wealth management solutions to the UHNI and HNI segment of the customers. These 
solutions are further categorized into personal banking, business banking, wealth management 
and NRI banking. Wealth management solutions offer  a wide range of investment products 
ranging from Mutual Funds, Alternate products, Sovereign Gold bonds and NPS. Legacy 
planning is another key offering under wealth management solutions.  Under PIONEER program, 
IndusInd Bank offers credit card and debit card as well. A stand -out feature of the credit card 
offering under PIONEER program is the select Golf lessons offered to PIONEER credit 
cardholders aligned to the needs of the Golf enthusiasts .  
 
Mr. Sumant Kathpalia , Managing Director & CEO,  IndusInd Bank, said,  “IndusInd Bank has 
always believed in keeping customers at the centre of everything we do  and provid e an 
experience to our customers, that goes beyond just banking. We have a firm philosophy of 
integrating  an ‘experience’  at every possible touch -point of customers.  Golf is a lifestyle sport, 
and IndusInd Bank’s association with PGTI is another step in our constant endeavour to offer a 
lifestyle  banking experience . We are glad to associate with Professional Golf Tour of India as its 
Official Banking Partner  to promote p rofessional golf in the country. " 
 
Kapil Dev, President, PGTI , commented, “ My association with  Golf as a sport has evolved over 
the years and I find the spirit of golf and cricket alike. Golf is a lifestyle sport and likewise 
IndusInd Bank is also a lifestyle bank which makes IndusInd Bank an ideal Banking partner for 
PGTI . This association will bring a superlative experience for audience as well as the players, 
which aligns with the philosophy of PGTI.”  
 
   
 
 
Apart from the lifestyle connect for this sport, IndusInd Bank firmly believes in the philosophy 
of driving diversity, differentiation and domination through various initiat ives to support sports 
in India under the umbrella of ‘IndusInd For Sports ’, an initiative of IndusInd Bank launched in 
2016. This directly impacts the three most important pillars  of the bank  i.e. customers, 
employees and the community.  The partnership with PGTI is partly an extension of this 
philosophy, at IndusInd Bank.

About PGTI : 
Formed in 2006, Professional Golf Tour of India (PGTI) is the recognised official body of 
professional golf in India. PGTI's objective is to promote professional golf in the country, as well 
as to give players an opportunity to be involved in the decision -making on all aspects of the 
game. Headed by Mr. Kapil Dev (President), PGTI's governing body comprises leading Indian 
golf professionals. PGTI currently has over 300 members.
