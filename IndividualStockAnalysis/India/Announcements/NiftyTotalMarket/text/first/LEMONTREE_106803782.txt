LEMON TREE HOTELS DEBUT S IN DARJEELING WITH  
KEYS PRIMA BY LEMON TREE HOTELS  
 
The 65 -room hotel is the Group's third operational property in West Bengal, with 
three more in the state pipeline  
New Delhi,  30th September , 2026 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, one of India’s 
leading hospitality companies, has announced the opening of Keys Prima by 
Lemon Tree Hotels, Darjeeling . This marks the Group’s first hotel in Darjeeling , 
further strengthening its presence in West Bengal.  
The hotel is managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
Located on Old Kutchery Road, the hotel offers easy access to Darjeeling’s key 
attractions while placing guests close to the town’s distinctive mountain setting. 
Known for its tea estates, panoramic Himalayan views, pleasant climate and 
colonial -era heritage, Darjeeling remains a sought -after hill destinat ion for leisure 
travellers and those looking for a refreshing mountain escape.  
Spread across 65 well -appointed rooms and suites  including Superior Rooms, 
Deluxe Rooms, Studio Suites and Executive Suites, the hotel offers views 
overlooking the valley, city, forest and lush gardens. The hotel features Keys Café, 
a multi -cuisine all -day dining restaurant; Unlock Bar; in -room dining; and a fitness 
centre. An outdoor lawn with a capacity of up to 100 guests provides a setting for 
intimate social gatherings, private celebrations and outdoor events.  
"Darjeeling remains one of India's most -loved hill destinations, be it a classic 
mountain holiday for families or shorter, experience -led trips for younger 
travellers. The tea estates, the food and the character of the town are as much a 
part of the journey  as the view. This makes Darjeeling a natural fit for Keys Prima 
by Lemon Tree Hotels, which combines contemporary comfort with thoughtful 
service and a strong sense of value. With our debut here, we look forward to 
offering travellers our signature hospit ality as they discover Darjeeling, and to 
marking a new milestone in our growing presence in West Bengal ," said               
Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd.  
Guests can explore several of Darjeeling’s prominent attractions, including Tiger 
Hill, Darjeeling Zoo, Himalayan Mountaineering Institute, Tenzing Rock, Japanese 
Temple and Peace Pagoda, Rock Garden, Tea Gardens, Darjeeling Ropeway and 
Batasia Loop. The hotel is approximately 7 0 km from Bagdogra Airport, 2.5 km 
from Darjeeling Railway Station and 1.7 km  from the local bus station .
