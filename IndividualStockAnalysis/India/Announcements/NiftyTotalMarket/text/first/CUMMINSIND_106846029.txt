Cummins India Limited  - Results for the quarter and period  ended September  30, 2026  
 
Pune, India : Cummins India Limited  (NSE:  CUMMINSIND , BSE: 500480 ) The Board of 
Directors of Cummins India Limited (CIL), at their meeting held today, reviewed and approved 
the financial results for the quarter and period ended September  30, 2026 .  
 
Performance Highlights (based on standalone  unaudited financial results) for the quarter 
ended September  30, 2026 :  
• Total  Sales  for the quarter at ₹3,247  Cr, higher by 4% compared to the same quarter 
last year  and lower by 4% compared to the previous quarter .  
• Domestic sales  at ₹ 2,692 Cr higher by 5% compared to the same quarter last year and 
lower by 6 % over the previous quarter.  
• Export Sales  at ₹ 555 Cr higher by 2% compared to the same quarter last year and  higher  
by 6% compared to the previous quarter.  
• Profit before tax at ₹ 601 Cr, with a margin of 18.5 %, lower  by 28.4% compared to 
same quarter last year and lower by 16.6% compared to previous quarter.  
• Profit after tax at ₹ 467  Cr and net profit margin at 14.4%.   
 
Commenting on results, Adegbile Adewunmi , Managing Director, Cummins India Limited, 
said: “Demand remained healthy across key domestic markets, supported by data centers, 
manufacturing, realty and continued growth in the distribution business. Exports also recorded 
growth over the previous quarter. Commodity and freight inflation, coupled with p roduct mix, 
impacted margins . The benefits of pricing actions are expected to flow through progressively. The 
Company remains focused on disciplined order execution, price realization and cost 
management, while continuing to deliver on its promise of dependability and reliability to 
customers. ” 
 
 
 
Cummins Public  Future Outlook:  
 
Looking ahead, demand across key domestic and export markets is expected to remain steady. 
However, the operating environment continues to be influenced by geopolitical uncertainty, 
inflationary pressures, commodity price volatility and evolving supply -cha in dynamics . The 
Company will remain focused on disciplined execution, price realization, cost management and 
operational efficiency, while continuing to closely monitor the external environment.  
 
With a healthy balance sheet and a stable cash conversion cycle, the company continues to 
execute with discipline, deploy capital prudently and keep costs in check. Its proven 
technologies, experienced people, trusted brand, relevant product portfolio, manufacturing 
depth and wide distribution and service reach underpin its commitment to long -term value for 
customers and shareholders  
 
About Cummins India Limited:  
Established in 1962 and headquartered in Pune, Cummins India Limited ( NSE: CUMMINSIND | 
BSE: 500480 ) is one of the leading power solutions providers in the country. As the oldest 
Cummins entity in India, Cummins India Limited is involved in the power generation, aftermarket, 
and export businesses. The company has a strong manufacturing backbone and has four  state -
of-the-art manufacturing plants, assembly, and a distribution center. With 480 customer 
touchpoints, the company is committed to powering the success of its customers and ensuring 
that its services and solutions are readily accessible. The company has over 3,000  employees 
who contribute their expertise and commitment to ensuring Cummins India delivers on its 
mission of “ making people’s lives better by powering a more prosperous world ”.
