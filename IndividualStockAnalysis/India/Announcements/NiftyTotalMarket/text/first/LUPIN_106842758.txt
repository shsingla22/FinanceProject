Lupin Launch es Raltegravir T ablets , USP , 600 mg  in the United States   
As the First-To-File, Lupin is Eligible for 180 -Day Generic Exclusivity  
 
Mumbai, India, Naples,  Fla, November 3, 2026: Global pharma major Lupin Limited (Lupin) today 
announced the launch of  Raltegravir Tablets  USP, 600 mg in the United  States  following the approval from 
the U.S. FDA for its Abbreviated New Drug Application .  
 
Raltegravir Tablets, 600  mg are bioequivalent to  the reference listed drug  (RLD)  Isentress®  HD Tablets, 600 
mg of Merck  Sharp & Dohme, LLC.  and indicated in combination with other antiretroviral agents for the 
treatment of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV -1) infection in adult patients and in pediatric patients 
weigh ing at least 40 kg .  Lupin is the exclusive first -to-file for this product and is eligible for 180 -day generic 
drug exclusivity.  
 
Raltegravir Tablets USP, 600 mg (RLD Isentress® HD) had an estimated annual sale of USD 26 million in the 
U.S. (IQVIA MAT September 2026).
