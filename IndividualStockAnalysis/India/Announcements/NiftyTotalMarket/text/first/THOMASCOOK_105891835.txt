Sterling Holiday Resorts Announces ELDP Program  in partnership with 
Indian School of Hospitality  
 
26th June 2024, Sterling Holiday Resorts, India's leading  leisure hospitality brand 
announces its Emerging Leaders ’ Development Program , powered by knowledge partners 
Indian School of Hospitality (ISH).  
Sterling Holidays, a pioneer in holiday segment across India,  signed an MOU with the 
Indian School of Hospitality (ISH)  – a premier  institute for hospitality education in India. 
The strategic aim of this partnership is to elevate the skill s and talents of the  professionals  
at Sterling , by offering ISH’s globally recognized curriculums, innovative learning 
methods, and expert mentoring from industry leaders. Overall, t he initiative is to help 
foster  young talent and prepare future leaders to support Sterling’s growth plans in the 
country . 
Sterling’s Emerging Leaders Development Program (ELDP), a 12 -month initiative, will 
now feature a 4 -month campus immersion program powered by ISH. This p rogram will 
focus on key areas such as Customer -Focused Marketing Strategies, Personal and 
Organisational Leadership, Financial Business Acumen, Hotel Revenue Management and 
Distribution Strategy, Hotel Asset Management, Digital Age Marketing Strategies, and Leadership in the World of AI.  
 
''The Emerging Leaders Development Program is a testament to ISH's commitment to 
empower the future leaders of the hospitality industry and drive positive change and 
excellence in the segment. The initiative will deliver comprehensive training and promote 
critical thinking, leadership skills and innovation to ensure that the participants excel in their roles as General Managers,'' said  Mr Dilip Puri, Founder & Executive Chairman, 
Indian School of Hospitality.  
 
“At Sterling we are dedicated  to the culture of nurturing  and growing internal talents. My 
own journey here is a testament to this , starting from Head of Revenue and Sales to 
Business Development and now as a CEO & MD.  With the bene�its of a sought -after 
curriculum offered by the industry stalwarts  at ISH, we aim to reinforce this philosophy 
with an expert touch”  said Mr. Vikram Lalvani, CEO & MD, Sterling Holiday Resorts 
Limited.
