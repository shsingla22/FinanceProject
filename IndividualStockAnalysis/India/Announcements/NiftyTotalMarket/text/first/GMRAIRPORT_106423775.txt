Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
This is with reference to a subsidiary of the Company i.e. GMR Hyderabad 
International Airport Limited (“GHIAL”). It may be noted that Airports Economic 
Regulatory Authority of India (“AERA”) had during August 2021, issued the order 
number 12/2021 -22 dated August 31, 2021 for the 3rd Control Period for 
determination of aeronautical tariffs for Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (RGIA), 
Hyderabad. The order included a determination of tariff for the period FY 2021 -22 to 
FY 2025 -26 (3rd CP). The Landing Charges, Park ing Charges and the UDF for the last 
quarter of the 3rd CP, i.e. period of January 01, 2026 to March 31, 2026 were 
maintained at slightly lower levels as compared to other quarters of FY 2025 -26.  
 
GHIAL had requested AERA to maintain the tariff for the fo urth quarter of FY 2025 -
26 (January – March 2026) at the same levels as applicable till December 2025.  
AERA has now accepted the GHIAL request and issued the Amendment to Order no 
12/2021 -22 on October 31, 2025 allowing GHIAL to collect aeronautical charg es 
(Landing, Parking and UDF) at same rates as applicable from 01.04.2025 to 
31.12.2025, even for the last quarter of FY 2025 -26 i.e. January 01, 2026 to March 
31, 2026.  
 
The said AERA Order of October 31, 2025 is available at 
https://aera.gov.in/uploads/airports/17619812247569.pdf . 
 
Further, the original AERA Order dated August 31, 2021 determining the Tariffs for 
the third Control Period (with relevant pages from page no. 297 to 300) is available 
at aera.gov.in/uploads/a irports/16466584108771.pdf
