As earlier reported on 14th August, 2023, 
Voltas had filed a case against OHLC JV to 
recover the monies due and payable towards (I) 
work done; (2) variations; and (3) delays, 
aggregating QAR 771.632 million (Rs.1754.69 
crores approx). In response thereto, OHLC JV 
made counter claims aggregating Rs. 7384.83 
crores including Rs. 6409 crores approx. 
towards damages. These counter claims were 
contested by Voltas. 
OHLC JV had also sent notices to the bank for 
liquidation/ encashment of 2 bank guarantees 
(BGs) aggregating QAR 166.720 million (Rs. 
373 crores approx), earlier issued by Voltas as 
per the terms of the sub-contract. These BGs 
are reflected in the Notes to the Financial 
Statements under the head 'Contingent 
Liabilities' -Guarantees for terminated 
contract. 
As the Bank did not make the payment, OHLC 
JV filed a case against them in Qatar Court. 
The Qatar Court has held that as OHLC JV 
lacked legal capacity in Qatar to litigate, the 
suit filed by Voltas and OHLC JV, including 
the claims and counter claims have been 
dismissed and an English translation of the 
Order is received by Voltas on 151 October, 
2024. Voltas is evaluating the option of filing 
an appeal against the Order.
