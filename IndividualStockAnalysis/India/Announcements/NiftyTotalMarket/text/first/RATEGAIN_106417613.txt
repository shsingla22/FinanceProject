Experience  
 
Noida, 28th October 2025:  RateGain Travel Technologies Limited (RateGain), a leading 
provider of AI-powered SaaS solutions for the travel and hospitality industry, today 
announced the expansion of its strategic partnership with Royal Orchid Hotels, one of India’s 
fastest-growing hotel chains, to power its expanding portfolio, including its new luxury and 
lifestyle brand, ICONIQA Hotels and Resorts. Together, we will work on a single AI-powered 
platform to improve profitability and deliver a seamless guest experience at scale. 
 
Royal Orchid has long relied on RateGain’s distribution and business intelligence products to 
power its commercial strategy. Building on this trusted foundation, the group is now taking a bold 
step forward by adopting RateGain’s AI-first UNO suite - a unified tech stack designed to maximize 
direct bookings. The suite brings together the Booking Engine, VIVA AI Voice Agent, Smart 
Distribution, and Demand Booster solutions. 
 
This partnership marks one of the largest full-suite integrations for RateGain, reinforcing the 
growing shift in the hospitality industry toward AI-led, unified commercial platforms. With 123 
hotels today and plans to scale beyond 250 across its brand portfolio, Royal Orchid is future 
proofing its commercial operations with intelligent technology designed to support rapid 
expansion and maximize revenue performance for its distinct brands. 
 
Arjun Baljee, Founder of ICONIQA and President of Royal Orchid Hotels Ltd., said,  “As we 
scale our portfolio and launch new brands like ICONIQA, it’s essential to have a partner who can 
power both growth and efficiency across a diverse collection of hotels. RateGain’s platform brings 
together everything we need to strengthen our direct business, control distribution costs, and 
deliver consistent, high-quality guest experience, whether it’s at a Royal Orchid or an ICONIQA 
property. This partnership is a key step in building a future-ready commercial engine for our 
entire group.” 
 
Anurag Jain, Executive Vice President, Revenue (APMEA) at RateGain, said , “We’re excited 
to partner with the entire Royal Orchid and ICONIQA family on this next chapter of their growth. 
Their forward-looking approach to managing a multi-brand portfolio aligns perfectly with our 
mission to help hotels unlock revenue through smarter technology. This deployment is proof of 
how leading hotel brands are embracing AI to simplify operations, lower costs, and scale 
profitably.” 
 
Royal Orchid’s decision underscores industry confidence in RateGain’s ability to modernize hotel 
commerce and help brands like Royal Orchid and ICONIQA scale smarter. For RateGain, this 
partnership reflects the accelerating adoption of its AI-powered platform, which unifies 
fragmented systems to deliver improved margins and friction-free guest experiences.
