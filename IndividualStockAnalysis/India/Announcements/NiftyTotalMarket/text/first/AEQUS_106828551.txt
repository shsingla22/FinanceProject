Key Financial Highlights  
• Strong Revenue Growth:  Consolidated revenue increased 45% YoY to ₹4,091 Mn 
in Q2 and 50% YoY to ₹8,046 Mn in H1, supported by growth across Aerospace and 
Consumer.  
• JV Contribution:  Including proportionate JV contributions, Q2 revenue reached 
₹4,480 Mn, up 44% YoY and 4% QoQ, while EBITDA stood at ₹331 Mn, down 36% 
YoY and up 8% QoQ. H1 revenue reached ₹8,781 Mn, up 48% YoY, while EBITDA 
stood at ₹638 Mn, with a margin of approximate ly 7% . 
• Strong Aerospace Momentum:  Aerospace revenue grew 44% YoY to ₹3,514 Mn in 
Q2 and  42% YoY to ₹6,736 Mn in H1, while EBITDA increased 24% YoY to ₹783 Mn 
and 29% YoY to ₹1,514 Mn, respectively. The business continued to broaden its 
manufacturing scope and deepen customer relationships.  
• Consumer Performance:  Consumer revenue increased 52% YoY to ₹577 Mn in Q2 Note:  Q2 and H1 FY27 figures are not directly comparable with the corresponding periods of FY26, as  consumer electronics costs now being expensed, compared 
with the pre -operative phase in FY26.

Business Highlights :  
 
• Aerospace order book reaches USD 1.1 billion:  Order book grew 9% QoQ. The 
Safran A320 wheels agreement marks Aequs’ first serial -production contract 
exceeding 15 years.  
• Expanding portfolio depth:  Expanded the Aerospace product portfolio to 5,978 
SKUs, adding 238 parts during Q2 and broadening participation across customer 
program s. 
• Strengthening Long -Term Partnership with Safran:  Signed new long -term 
supply agreements, expanding Aequs ' relationships to six Safran entities and 
broadening its manufacturing scope across critical aerospace components, 
leveraging Belagavi and upcoming Hosur manufacturing ecosystems.  
• Funding the Next Phase  of Growth : ₹6,500 Mn promoter -group investment 
through preferential warrants reinforces promoter commitment and supports the 
Company's next phase of capacity expansion, strategic JV investments and working 
capital require ments.  
• Strengthening Manufacturing Scale:  Added nine Aerospace CNC machines during 
the quarter, taking the segment’s total to 229. Annualised machining and molding 
capacity across the Company increased to 4.93 million hours.  
• Consumer update:  Customer programs continued to progress, although Q2 
volume ramp progressed slower than anticipated due to extended qualification 
timelines and supply chain constraints . The focus remains on completing the 
remaining milestones and progressing into sustained production volumes.  
• CFO Appointment:  Reenah Joseph was appointed  Chief Financial Officer, bringing 
nearly two decades of experience across corporate finance, capital markets, M&A 
and investor relations.

  Management Remarks:  
 
Mr. Aravind Melligeri, Executive Chairman and CEO, Aequs Limited, said :  
“The first half of FY27 marked continued progress in expanding our business and  
strengthening our long -term growth opportunities. Revenue grew 50% YoY to ₹8,046 
Mn, driven by 42% growth in Aerospace and more than doubling of Consumer revenue 
on a YoY basis. Our Aerospace order book stood at USD 1,096 Mn, providing long -term 
revenue  visibility. The Safran A320 aircraft wheels agreement marks our first serial -
production  contract with a tenure exceeding 15 years. Together, these milestones give 
substance to  the long -term business we are building.  
 
Aerospace continued to deliver strong growth and profitability, supported by healthy  
demand and program execution. In Consumer, the Q2 volume ramp progressed  slower  
than anticipated due to extended qualification timelines and supply chain  constraints. 
We view this as a timing -related shift rather than a change in the underlying  opportunity, 
with customer programs continuing to progress. Given these factors, we are  recalibrating 
the path to Consumer EBITDA breakeven, with our near -term focus on  improving 
utilization and translating installed capacity into profitable growth.  
 
To support this next phase of growth, we continue to invest in our manufacturing  
capabilities. H1 FY27 capital expenditure stood at ₹1,597 Mn, and the  ₹6,500 Mn 
promoter  warrant is intended to support the next phase of expansion,  including Hosur. 
Following quarter -end, new Safran agreements expanded our  relationships to six entities. 
As these capabilities and partnerships grow, our focus  remains  on disciplined execution 
and stronger returns. ” 
 
About Aequs Limited :  
Aequs  Limited is the only engineering -led, vertically integrated precision manufacturer 
operating within a single SEZ in India, with operations across the aerospace and 
consumer segments. The Company operates a unique ecosystem with co -located 
capabilities span ning forging, precision machining, surface treatment, and assembly, 
enabling end -to-end manufacturing of complex, high -precision components.  
 
In aerospace, Aequs is among India’s largest precision component manufacturers, with 
a portfolio of 5,978 qualified parts across engine systems, structures, actuation 
systems, landing systems, and assemblies. The Company is a Tier -1 supplier to leading 
global aerospace OEMs and system integrators, including Airbus, Boeing, Safran, and 
Collins Aerospace, and ma intains long -standing relationships with global customers.

  Alongside aerospace, Aequs  operates a diversified consumer manufacturing 
ecosystem spanning consumer electronics, plastics, toys, and consumer durables, 
leveraging its advanced engineering, tooling, and large -scale manufacturing 
capabilities to serve leading global brands. It opera tes within integrated manufacturing 
clusters in Belagavi, Hubballi, and Koppal  (Karnataka), supported by international 
operations in the United States and France, providing proximity to key global 
customers. The Company has an annual machining and molding capacity of 4.93 
million hours (Annualized figure based on Q 2 FY27)  and advanced special -process 
certifications, positioning it as a long -term manufacturing partner for global OEMs.
