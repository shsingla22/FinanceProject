Revolt Motors Achieves ISO 9001:2015 Certification, Reinforcing 
Commitment to Quality and Operational Excellence  
Marks a key milestone in building globally benchmarked processes and customer trust  
New Delhi, 13 May 2025 : Revolt Motors, India’s No.1 electric motorcycle brand, has been 
awarded the prestigious ISO 9001:2015 certification for its robust Quality Management 
System (QMS). The certification  issued by one of the world’s leading testing and certification 
bodies, validates Revolt’s commitment to consistent quality, process excellence, and customer 
satisfaction across its operations.  
ISO 9001:2015 is the world’s most recognized standard for quality management, awarded only 
to organizations that meet stringent international benchmarks across product design, 
production, supply chain, and customer service. With this recognition, Revolt jo ins an elite 
group of electric vehicle manufacturers adhering to globally standardized quality practices.  
Speaking on this achievement, Mrs. Anjali Rattan Nashier, Chairperson, RattanIndia 
Enterprises Limited , said,  
“This certification is a proud moment for all of us at Revolt. It is a reflection of our tireless 
pursuit of quality, innovation, and operational excellence. As we expand to new geographies 
and strengthen our position in the EV segment, this global certification reinforces the trust our 
customers and partners place in us .” 
Revolt’s certification underscores the company’s structured approach toward product 
development, manufacturing controls, continuous improvement, and customer -centric 
delivery. It also aligns with the brand’s long -term strategic roadmap, which includes aggr essive 
global expansion and deepening its after -sales and service capabilities.  
This milestone further enhances Revolt’s credibility with business partners, international 
collaborators, and institutional stakeholders. It ensures that every Revolt product —from the 
flagship RV400 to the latest BlazeX —is backed by a quality framework tha t meets international 
expectations.  
As Revolt continues to lead India’s transition to electric mobility, this achievement represents 
a critical foundation for delivering safe, reliable, and high -performance products —built not 
just for India, but for the world.  
For more information on Revolt Motors  certified  product, customers can visit their website 
at www.revoltmotors.com  or visit their nearest  authorized dealership .
