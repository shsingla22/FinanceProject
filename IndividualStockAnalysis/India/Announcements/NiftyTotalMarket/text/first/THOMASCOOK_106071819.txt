Sterling Launches Upscale Boutique Resort in Wayanad –  Sterling Lontano Waterfront  
Sterling Holiday Resorts , India’s leading leisure hospitality brand, proudly announces the launch of 
Sterling Lontano Waterfront Wayanad, its ninth property in Kerala and third in Wayanad. Nestled 
amidst the scenic landscapes of the Western Ghats, the resort is located near the i conic Karapuzha 
Dam, offering spectacular views of the Karapuzha waterfront and Cheengeri Hills.  
Spread across 3 acres of Wayanad’s pristine eco -belt, Sterling Lontano Waterfront boasts luxurious 
rooms and suites with breathtaking water views. Designed for  couples seeking tranqui llity and 
rejuvenation, the resort provides easy access to Wayanad’s key attractions, making it an ideal getaway 
for those looking to unwind amidst nature.  
With the addition of Sterling Lontano Waterfront, Sterling’s presence in Way anad now spans three 
distinct experiences: waterfront, forest, and hills . Travelers can explore Wayanad through separate 
holidays or combine these experiences into one unforgettable trip, making Sterling the region’s most 
comprehensive hospitality provider.  
“Sterling Lontano Waterfront Wayanad marks an exciting milestone in our expansion journey. As our 
third property in Wayanad and ninth in Kerala, Sterling now offers the most diverse range of holiday 
experiences in the state —from hill stations and waterfr onts to jungle retreats and heritage 
destinations. We are committed to providing immersive, fun -filled holidays, secluded getaways, and 
refined luxe experiences, all tailored to our guests' preferences,” said Mr. Vikram Lalvani, Managing 
Director & CEO, St erling Holiday Resorts Limited.  
The resort offers thoughtfully designed accommodations, featuring expansive rooms and suites 
equipped with en -suite bathtubs or jacuzzis, plush bedding, and signature amenities. Each room 
includes a private balcony with pano ramic views of the Karapuzha basin and Cheengeri Hills.  
Guests can enjoy a serene infinity pool, the Subuthi Spa, a meditation center, and a scenic cycling 
track. These facilities, combined with the resort’s lush surroundings, provide a perfect escape for 
relaxation and rejuvenation.  
The Tamarind, the resort’s in -house restaurant, offers a gastronomic journey featuring traditional 
Malabari cuisine alongside international favorites. Guests can savor these delicacies while soaking in 
the stunning views of the infinity pool and waterfront. For special occasions, the resort’s outdoor gazebo creates the perfect setting for celebratory dinners or sparkling brunches.  
The resort boasts  of an indoor banquet hall, Celebrations, complemented by a sprawling 10,000 sq. ft. 
outdoor lawn. These versatile spaces are ideal for hosting intimate weddings, corporate offsites, themed celebrations, or any special event, ensuring unforgettable memories.  
Sterling Lontano Waterfront offers curated experiences like yoga sessions, rain dances, and turf sports  
as well as an outdoor Kids Play Area . It also organizes guided tours to Wayanad’s top attractions, 
including Karapuzha Dam, Nellarachal View Point, Jain Temple, Soochipara Water Falls, Edakkal Caves, 
Pookode Lake and the Muthanga Wildlife Sanctuary. These activities ensure guests can immerse 
themselves in the local culture and natural wonders.  
For more information and bookings, visit www.sterlingholidays.com .
