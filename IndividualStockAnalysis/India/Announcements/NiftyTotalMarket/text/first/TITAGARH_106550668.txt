Sub: Intimation regarding sale/transfer/disposal of a business unit of overseas associate company  
 
Pursuant to the Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations 2015, we wish to intimate that we have been informed  by Titagarh 
Firema SpA, Italy - an associate of the Company (“ Firema ”) that Firema  has completed the transfer of its  
business unit to Fabbrica Italiana Treni S.p.A (owned by government of Italy)  (“Ferroviere Della Stato”- 
State Railways ) for a net consideration of  66,095,000 EUR .  
The consideration received by Firema  has been utilized for payment to secured and unsecured creditors 
of Firema  under the CNC process under the Court of Naples.  The residual assets  of Firema  post the 
above transfer will be dealt with as may be deemed appropriate by its Board . As of date there is no 
change in the shareholding of the residual Firema.  
Firema was acquired by  Titagarh Rail Systems Limited (TRSL) in 2015 and has helped TRSL to establish 
its Passenger Rolling Stock business in India  including setting up of aluminium  car body manufacturing 
facility . However, due to various circumstances, Firema has been incurring substantial losses even after  
cash infusion from TRSL over the last few years and this transfer  will eventually help in stoppage of such 
a cash need from TRSL .  
At present TRSL is executing several prestigious orders for Metro Coache s, Vande Bharat Trains as well 
as propulsion systems and the total order book of TRSL  for this segment  (as per last quarter) was INR  
10,791 Crores .   
As TRSL has already established its facility and competence to execute this business independently , the 
abovementioned  transfer shall not have  any impact whatsoever on the operations of TRSL .  
We will submit updates on further developments  in the matter in due course , as these may take place.   
The said disclosure is also available on the website of the Company at www.titagarh.in .  
This is for your information and record.
