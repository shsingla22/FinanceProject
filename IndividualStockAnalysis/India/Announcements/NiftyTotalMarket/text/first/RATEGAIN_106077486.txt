Noida, 5th December  2024 : RateGain Travel Technologies Limited, a leading global provider of AI -
powered SaaS solutions for travel and hospitality , today announced that it has been recognized by Great 
Place to Work® India as one of India’s Best Workplaces in IT & IT -BPM 2024  – Top 100 .  
 
RateGain thrives on its foundational values of Teamwork, Ownership, People First, Innovation, and 
Customer Obsession . These principles drive their efforts to empower employees and set a new 
benchmark for what it means to foster an exceptional workplace culture.  
 
"This recognition reflects our unwavering focus on cultivating an environment where people feel valued 
and emp owered to excel," said Bhanu Chopra, Founder and Managing Director  of RateGain 
Travel Technologies.  "Our people are at the core of everything we do, and we believe in creating 
opportunities for growth, promoting collaboration, and ensuring flexibility. This milestone drives us to 
continue setting new standards of workplace excellence and innovation."  
 
Sahil Sharma, Global Chief Human Resources Officer at RateGain Travel Technologies , added, 
"We are deeply honored to be recognized among India’s Best Workplaces in IT & IT -BPM. This 
achievement is a testament to the dedication of our employees, the visionary  leadership of our teams, 
and our ongoing efforts to build a workplace where everyone feels empowered to bring their best selves 
to work. We remain committed to driving a culture of innovation, inclusivity, and shared success."  
 
As a global authority on wor kplace culture, Great Place to Work® evaluates organizations on their 
ability to create High -Trust, High -Performance Cultures™. This accolade highlights RateGain’s 
consistent efforts to create a supportive environment where employees thrive and contribute to the 
company’s success.
