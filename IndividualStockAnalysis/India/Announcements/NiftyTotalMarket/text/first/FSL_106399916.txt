Firstsource recognized  at 3rd Prithvi Awards 2025 for ESG Excellence  
 
Mumbai, India – October  7, 2025 : Firstsource Solutions Limited  (NSE:FSL, BSE:532809), a leading global provider of business 
process management services and an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company, has been recognized at the 3rd Prithvi Awards 2025 by 
the ESG Research Foundation.  This recognition adds to a growing list of global ESG benchmarks and ratings where Firstsource has 
demonstrated strong performance, including S&P Global Sustainable1 (DJSI), EcoVadis, CDP and other independent assessments.  
 
The Prithvi Awards celebrate organizations that demonstrate exceptional performance across Environmental, Social, and 
Governance  (ESG) spectrum . Firstsource was assessed alongside peers across industries on a wide range of criteria including  
alignment with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UN SDGs), ESG policies and governance practices, 
environmental targets and compliance, certifications such as ISO standards, training programs, and external recognitions acro ss 
ESG segments.  
 
This recognition underscores Firstsource’s future -forward and disciplined approach to ESG. Anchored in  its FirstConscious  
framework and UnBPO ™ mindset , the company embeds sustainability, inclusion, and governance into its operating model, laying 
the foundation and benchmark for building a strong, sustainable business  and long -term value creation.
