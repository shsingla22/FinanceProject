GAIL conferred PPAC Greenfinch Data Excellence Award 2025  
 New Delhi, April 4, 2025:  GAIL is  conferred the ‘PPAC Greenfinch Data Excellence Award 
2025’. Shri Asim Prasad, Chief General Manager (Corporate Strategy Pla nning Advocacy & 
Corporate Affairs), Shri Amitabh Ranjan, GM (CSPA), Ms. Priyanka Gupta, Chief Manager 
(CSPA), and Koushik Basu, Sr. Manager (CSPA) received the award on behalf of GAIL.  
 GAIL submits about 500 distinct data to PPAC monthly to generate eig ht reports. In 2024 -25, the 
practice of data submission was further automated through the integration of API.  
 The Petroleum Planning and Analysis Cell (PPAC), under the aegis of MoPNG, is responsible 
for collating industry data from various oil and gas c ompanies in India related to the country's 
production, import, consumption, and export of oil and natural gas. The data is used to generate 
periodic reports that is used by the Oil & Gas Industry. The data is collated, processed, and 
disseminated to the pu blic and private sector companies in the oil and gas sector.  
 On their 24th Foundation Day, PPAC rolled out the ‘PPAC Greenfinch Data Excellence 
Award’.   They evaluated both the public and private sector companies based on a holistic 
approach comprising p arameters related to data quality submitted by each company, assurance 
practices, data excellence metrics, data availability, accessibility, security, IT infrastructure, future 
readiness and innovation, etc.  
 GAIL is committed to using technology to enhan ce data processing and report generation for 
various requirements.
