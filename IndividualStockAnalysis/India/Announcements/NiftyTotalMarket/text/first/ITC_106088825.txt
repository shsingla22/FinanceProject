Acquisition  of shares  of EIH Limited  and HLV Limited  
 
Further to our letter dated 24th October, 2024 , we write to  advise  that the Company  today  has 
acquired  2.44% of the  share capital (comprising 1,52,32,129 Equity Shares of ` 2/- each) of                  
EIH Limited (‘EIH’) and  0.53% of the  share capital (comprising 34,60,829 Equity Shares of  
` 2/- each) of HLV Limited (‘HLV’), from Russell Credit Limited, a wholly  owned subsidiary 
of the Company .  
 
Post such acquisition, the Company’s  shareholding in EIH and HLV aggregates  16.13% and 
8.11%, of their  paid-up share capital, respectively.
