Statutory Auditors, Secretarial Auditor and Scrutinizer were also virtually present.  
 
On being informed, the requisite quorum  being present, she called the meeting to order.  
 
It was recorded that all feasible planning and execution was done for enabling electronic 
participation and voting of the members for the AGM.  
 She mentioned that the Statutory Registers as mentioned in the Notice of the 34
th AGM  are

held electronically, the proxy related procedures have been dispensed and hence , the proxy 
register is not available for inspection.

members (which started at 9:00 AM (IST) on Sunday , September 15, 202 4, and concluded at 5:00

the AGM Notice. Members who were participating in the meeting and had not cast their votes 
through remote e -voting, were provided the opportunity to cast their votes through e -voting at 
the meeting.  
 
Thereafter, the Chairman proceeded with his address to the members covering achievements of
