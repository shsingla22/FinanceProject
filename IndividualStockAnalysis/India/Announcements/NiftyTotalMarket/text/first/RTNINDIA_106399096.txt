Revolt Motors Fuels India’s Racing Future with Sponsorship of 
Young Racer Ethan Joy at F4 India Championship  
Supporting the next generation of Indian racers through performance, innovation and passion  
 
New Delhi, 7th October 2025:  Revolt Motors, India’s No.1 electric motorcycle brand, announced its 
sponsorship of rising  motorsport star Ethan Joy at the F4 India Championship held on October 4 and 
5, 2025 at the Kari Motor Speedway in Coimbatore.  
The partnership highlights Revolt’s commitment to empowering India ’s next generation of riders and 
racers  and nurturing the spirit of performance -driven innovation.  
From karting circuits to Formula cars, Ethan’s journey reflects the grit and ambition driving India’s 
emerging motorsport community. Beginning his racing career in 2022, he quickly rose through the 
ranks with multiple national podium finishes and a Vice Champion title in the Meritu s Cup 
Championship. His debut at the FIA -certified F4 India Championship marks a major step forward in his 
racing journey.  
 
Over four closely fought races across the weekend, Ethan delivered a strong and consistent 
performance with finishes of P9 in Race 1, P12 in Race 2, P9 in Race 3 and P6 in Race 4 . His steady 
progress through each race demonstrated his growing confidence, technical skill and ability to adapt 
to high -downforce single -seaters —earning appreciation from race engineers and fellow competitors 
alike.  
Commenting on the association, Ms. Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises Limited  
said, “At Revolt we believe performance has no limits on the road or on the racetrack. Ethan represents 
the courage, skill and determination that define India’s new generation of mobility leaders. Supporting 

   
his journey reflects our belief in nurturing young Indian talent and celebrating the passion that fuels 
racing excellence. ” 
Sharing his excitement, Ethan Joy  said, “Racing in Formula 4 is a dream come true and having Revolt 
Motors’ support makes it even more special. Their vision of empowering youth and driving innovation 
aligns perfectly with my own goals. This experience motivates me to continue working towards 
representing India on global circuits. ” 
Through this partnership, Revolt Motors  strengthens its mission to inspire India’s youth and champion 
a future where performance, precision  and progress  move forward together.
