Datamatics Wins Two Prestigious Awards at MRSI 
Golden Key Awards 2024  
 
17th March 2025,  Mumbai : Datamatics is proud to announce that it has won the Best 
Use of Technology  and The Most Humane Organization  awards at the Market 
Research Society of India (MRSI) Golden Key Awards 2024 . 
The Best Use of Technology award recognizes innovative and impactful use of 
technology in market research. Datamatics' unique solution set s a new benchmark in  
the category, securing this victory. Datamatics developed an AI -driven solution that 
dynamically adapts surveys in real -time. It addresses respondent fatigue, which is a 
key challenge.  The solution predicts fatigue levels by analyzing the respondent's 
cognitive load, emotional state, and survey complexity , improving response accuracy 
and minimizing bias.  
Sandeep Arora, President & Global Head - Digital Experiences  at Datamatics , 
stated: "We are honored  to receive the Best Use of Technology  award for our AI -First 
predictive adaptation of surveys. Datamatics' Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)  predict 
survey fatigue in real-time, optimizing user experience and improving response 
quality. This innovation ensures higher engagement and more reliable o utcomes, 
transforming data -driven decision -making."  
The Most Humane Organization  award recognizes companies that foster an 
inclusive, people -centric culture with clear social impact. Datamatics stood out for its 
Diversity Charter (DDC) , which promotes incl usivity beyond gender and supports 
diverse abilities and underrepresented groups.  
Dr. Rima Ghose Chowdhury, President —CHRO & CSR Leader , said: "This 
recognition of our positive People practices at work and beyond is particularly 
meaningful as we soon celeb rate the fifth anniversary of the Datamatics Diversity 
Charter (DDC) . It reinforces our belief that we are on the right path. Thank you, 
Datamaticians! Your dedication creates an inspiring work environment rooted in 
respect for all."  
Additionally, Datamati cs’ Honorable Chairman, Dr. Lalit Kanodia,  was recognized 
at the MRSI Golden Key Awards for his outstanding contributions to the industry. His 
leadership continues to inspire Datamatics to push the boundaries of innovation and 
excellence in market research  and beyond.  
 
These awards reaffirm Datamatics’ commitment to technological advancement and 
people -first initiatives, strengthening its leadership in the market research industry.
