Dear Madam/Sir, 
 
This is pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosures 
Requirement) Regulation, 2015 (“ SEBI Listing Regulations ”) and in continuation to our earlier 
announcements dated February 14, 2025 and March 21, 2025.  
 
On February 14, 2025, Fortis Hospotel Limited (a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company), signed 
definitive agreements for acquisition of entire business operations of Shrimann Superspecialty Hospital, 
Jalandhar (“ Shrimann Hospital ”) along with the underlying hospital land and adjacent land thereto. 
Subsequently, the Company had received a copy of an ex-parte Injunction order dated March 20, 2025 
(“Ex-parte Injunction Order ”), in connection with a petition filed by Cancer Treatment Services 
Hyderabad Private Limited (“ CTSH”) against Shrimann Hospitals and its partners (“ Respondents ”), 
wherein the Respondents had been restrained from consummating the sale of the Shrimann Hospital.  
 
We hereby wish to inform that yesterday at 11:14 AM, the Company has received a copy of an order 
dated May 13, 2025 issued by the Addl. District and Sessions Judge-IX, Jalandhar (“ Dismissal 
Order”), recording that CTSH and the Respondents have settled their differences, and the petition filed 
by CTSH against the Respondents stands dismissed as withdrawn. Therefore, in view of the terms of 
the Dismissal Order, the Ex-parte Injunction Order stands vacated and the judicial restraint placed on 
the Respondents in respect of consummation of the sale of the Shrimann Hospital has been lifted.  
This is for your information and record.
