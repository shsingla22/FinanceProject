The remote e-voting period commenced on June 25, 2024 (9:00 am 1ST) and ended on June 27, 2024 
(5:00 pm 1ST) via e-voting platform on the designated portal / webpage provided by National Securities 
Depositories Limited (NSDL). The Company had also provided e-voting facility to the Members who 
attended through VC/OAVM during the AGM to enable those Members to cast their votes , who had not 
cast their vote earlier through remote e-voting .
