S81 Card and Eternal Launch Co-Branded 'Eternal S81 Card' 
-Offers 10% instant discount for spends on Zomato, Bistro, District, and eligible spends on Blinkit 
-Cardholders also earn 5% cashback with partners including Uber, ixigo, Urban Company, and 
Apollo 24/7 
New Delhi, 15 October 2026: SBI Card, India's largest pure-play credit card issuer, and Eternal 
Limited (formerly known as Zomato Limited) today announced the launch and 'early access' for the 
Eternal SBI Card, a co-branded credit card designed for customers across Eternal and its affiliates. 
The credit card is among the first in the category to offer instant discounts across multiple brands 
within one ecosystem, bringing greater value to customers' everyday purchases while also 
extending benefits beyond the Eternal group brands. Customers can now request early access 
through the Zomato, Blinkit, and District apps to receive an invitation to apply when the card 
becomes available from 22 October 2026, in a phase-wise manner. 
Cardholders get a 10% instant discount on transactions on Zomato, District, and Blinkit ( only on 
products under the category of electronics and beauty) and a 5% instant discount on purchases 
under all other categories (subject to certain excluded categories mentioned below) on Blinkit. 
These discounts shall be subject to a combined cap of 2000 per month across purchases made 
through all the aforementioned platforms. Additionally, there is a 10% instant discount on Bistro 
and a 1+1 offer on the purchase of movie tickets through District (all discounts on Eternal brands 
are over and above existing platform offers). With the Eternal SBI Card, customers can also earn 
5% cashback, up to 1,000 a month, on spends with Apollo 2417, ixigo, Uber, and Urban Company. 
The Eternal SBI Card comes with the cashback auto-credit facility, which allows automatic credit of 
entitled cashback against eligible transactions directly to the SBI Card account on statement 
generation . 
Salila Pande, Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer, S8I Card, said, "SB/ Card has always 
strived to bring products for its customers that are customised as per their diverse lifestyle and 
preferences, offering maximum value and enhanced experience. Launch of the Eternal SB/ Card in 
strategic alliance with Eternal reflects this commitment. Designed around customer's everyday 
spending needs, the card brings together compelling cashback benefits, convenience, and value 
across a wide range of categories. The launch will bolster our retail co-brand credit card portfolio." 
Akshant Goyal, Chief Financial Officer, Eternal, said, "Get more out of every day' is the core 
belief the Eternal SB/ Card is built on. There's a strong opportunity to create more value around the 
high-frequency transactions that are already a part of our customers' lives. With Zomato, Blinkit 
and District at the centre, the card brings together these every day spends in one ecosystem, while 
extending value beyond it."

Beyond the core value offered, the co-branded credit card offers cashbacks and rewards on certain 
spends outside Eternal's ecosystem . Cardholders get a 6-month complimentary Zomato Gold 
membership as a welcome benefit on payment of the 499 joining fee. Along with this, cardholders 
can also enjoy a spend-based annual fee waiver on achieving annual spends of 2,00,000 in the 
previous year. The credit card also provides a 1 % fuel surcharge waiver of up to 200 in a statement 
cycle. 
Available on both the Mastercard and RuPay networks, customers can apply for the credit card 
through the Zomato, Blinkit, or District mobile apps, through a fully digital onboarding process.

About S81 Card 
SBI Cards and Payment Services Limited ('SBI Card') is a non-banking financial company that offers 
extensive credit card portfolio to individual cardholders and corporate clients which includes 
lifestyle, rewards, travel & fuel, and banking partnership cards along with corporate cards covering 
all major cardholder' segments in terms of income profile and lifestyle. The brand has a wide base 
of over 22.6 MM+ cards-in-force. SBI Card has a diversified customer acquisition network that 
enables the organisation to engage prospective customers across multiple channels. SBI Card is a 
technology driven company. 
The Company is listed on National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. ("NSE") and BSE Ltd.('BSE").

and Payment Services Limited'. The company is trading under the entity name 'SBICARD' on stock
