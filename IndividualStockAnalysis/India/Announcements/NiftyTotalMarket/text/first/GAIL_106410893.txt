Press Release  
 
GAIL signs Concession Agreement for setting up an MSW based Compressed  
Bio-Gas Plant in Bengaluru  
 
Bengaluru, October 17, 2025:  GAIL (India) Limited signed a Tripartite Concession 
Agreement on October 16,2025 with the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) and 
Bengaluru Solid Waste Management Limited (BSWML) for setting up a Compressed Bio -
Gas (CBG) plant at KCDC area, Haralakunte Village, Bengaluru South Taluk, Bengaluru 
in the presence of Hon’ble Deputy Chief Minister of Karnataka, Shri D. K. Shivakumar.  
 
Under the Concession Agreement, GBA will provide 18 acres of land on permissive use 
basis, and BSWML will supply 300 tons per day of segregated municipal solid waste, 
which will be processed to produce 12.6 tons per day of CBG. Additionally, the plant will  
produce around 22 tons of Fermented Organic Manure (FOM) daily, promoting circular 
economy and sustainable agriculture. The Concession Agreement will remain valid for 25 
years from the date of commissioning of the project.  
 
This initiative marks a major step towards sustainable waste management and green energy 
generation in Bengaluru, supporting the goals of the SATAT initiative and Swachh Bharat 
Mission of the Government of India.
