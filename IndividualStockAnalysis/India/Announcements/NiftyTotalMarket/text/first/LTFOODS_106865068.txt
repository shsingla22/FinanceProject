Dear Sir/Madam,  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III, Part A, Paragraph A( 1) and, to the extent applicable, 
Paragraph A(5) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (the 
“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform you that LT Foods Americas Inc. (“LTFA”), a step -down 
material subsidiary o f LT Foods Limited (the “Company”), has executed a Stock Purchase Agreement 
(“SPA”) with MK Foods Holdings, Inc. (“MK” or the “Target”) and the shareholders of MK (the “Sellers”) 
for acquisition of 100% of the equity share capital of MK.  
MK is a United States -based branded packaged foods company. Through its wholly owned subsidiary, 
Maya Kaimal Foods LLC, MK is engaged in the business of selling premium Indian -inspired, shelf -
stable sauces, ready -to-eat curries and rice products under the brand name “Maya Kaimal” , which  
is one of the leading brand with mainstream consumers.  
 
 Upon completion of the acquisition, MK and Maya Kaimal Foods LLC will become step -down 
subsidiaries of the Company.  
The proposed acquisition of Maya Kaimal  is directly aligned with our growth strategy and further 
strengthens our Ready -to-Eat (RTE), Ready -to-Cook (RTC) and Ready -to-Heat (RTC, RTH and RTE) 
growth pillar. It gives us an established US consumer brand with a portfolio across sauces and ready -
to-eat meals, together with product -development and culinary capabilities that complement LT 
Foods’ existing strengths.  
The detailed disclosures required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations, read with the 
applicable SEBI Master Circular dated J anuary 30, 2026, as amended from time to time, are enclosed 
as Annexure A and Annexure B.  
In accordance with Regulation 30(8) of the SEBI Listing Regulations, this disclosure will also be made 
available on the website of the Company at www.ltfoods.com
