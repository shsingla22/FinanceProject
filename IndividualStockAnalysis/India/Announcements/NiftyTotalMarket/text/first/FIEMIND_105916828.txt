Sub:  Business Update – Opening of New R&D Centre cum Liaison Office in Japan 
 
Ref:  Regulation 30 read with Para C, Part-A of Schedule-III of SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (Listing Regulations). 
 
We have pleasure to inform that Company has opened a new R&D Center cum Liaison 
Office in Japan under its wholly-owned subsidiary, Fiem Industries Japan Co. Ltd. The 
R&D Centre is inaugurated in august presence of Senior Officers of Honda Motor Co. 
Ltd., Japan.  
 
This strategic move aims to strengthen Company’s R&D capabilities to cater to all 
Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) for Two-Wheeler and Four-Wheeler 
segments. 
   
R&D Center will enhance Company’s capabilities in Product Innovation, Developing 
New Technologies in Automotive Lighting and help Company to maintain its 
competitive edge in the field of Automotive Lighting. The R&D Centre also aims to 
focus on new technologies for electrical vehicles, autonomous driving and other cutting 
edge automotive technologies. 
 
This is for your information please.
