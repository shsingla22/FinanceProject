TTBS Wins Five Prestigious Honours at ET Telecom Awards 2026  
Reinforces its leadership in  Enterprise Innovation and Customer Excellence 
 
New Delhi, April 02 , 2026 : Tata Tele Business Services(TTBS),  one of India’s leading enablers of digital 
connectivity and cloud solutions for businesses, has been honoured with five prestigious awards at 
the ET Telecom Awards 2026 , reinforcing its leadership in driving enterprise innovation  and delivering 
measurable business outcomes. 
TTBS received recognition in the following categories: 
 Enterprise Service Provider of the Year  
 Enterprise Digital Transformation of the Year  
 Best Customer Service  
 Best Wi-Fi Infrastructure Provider – Jury Recognition  
 Best Brand Awareness  
Commenting on the achievement, Vishal Rally, President & Chief Revenue Officer, Tata Teleservices, 
said, “We are truly honored to receive these recognitions at the ET Telecom Awards 2026. These 
recognitions reflect the trust our customers place in TTBS to power their digital journeys. Today, 
enterprises are increasingly focused on adopting dependable, integrated solutions that deliver real 
business outcomes. At TTBS, our smart solutions are designed to simplify complexity, embed security, 
and enable scalable growth, helping businesses build resilience and compete with confidence in a 
digital-first world.”  
As enterprises increasingly embrace cloud, AI-driven technologies and secure digital ecosystems, TTBS 
continues to differentiate itself through its integrated portfolio spanning connectivity, collaboration, 
cloud, and cybersecurity. Backed by strong customer engagement and strategic partnerships, the 
company is enabling businesses especially SMEs to transition from fragmented technology adoption 
to cohesive, scalable digital ecosystems. 
The ET Telecom Awards celebrate excellence and innovation in the Indian telecommunications and 
enterprise technology ecosystem, recognizing organisations that are driving transformation through 
cutting-edge solutions, customer-centric strategies and impactful digital initiatives.
