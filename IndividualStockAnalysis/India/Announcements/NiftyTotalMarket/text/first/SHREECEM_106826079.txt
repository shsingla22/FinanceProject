 Mr. Neeraj Akhoury, Managing Director, Shree Cement Limited, said "We delivered a strong performance during 
the quarter, with volume growth of 18% year-on-year , supported by deeper market penetration, enhanced

reflected in premium products contributing 29% of trade volumes, underscoring the strength of our brands and 
customer relationships. We remained focused on driving operational excellence through efficiency initiatives, 
increased green power utilisation, and digital-led productivity improvements. Backed by initiatives to gain market share and a disciplined approach to cost and efficiency enhancement, we are well-positioned to deliver sustainable and profitable growth in the quarters ahead."

Capacity Expansion 
 
In September, 2026, Union Cement Comp any, a step-down subsidiary of the Company in United Arab Emirates, 
commissioned a new cement mill with a capacity of 3.0 million tonnes per annum, taking its total cement production capacity to 7. 0 million tonnes per annum. 
 
In India, the Company is working on setting up greenfield integrated plant in Meghalaya, which is expected to be 
commissioned by the quarter ending 31
st March 2028. The Company continues to actively evaluate and pursue 
strategic expansion opportunities across its markets to achieve its growth milestones. 
 Sustainability initiatives 
 
Key highlights of the sustainability perf ormance of the Company are as under: - 
 
 The Company’s share of green electric ity in total electricity consumption stood at 65.2% in Q2’FY27 including 
of its wholly owned subsidiaries in India which is one of the highest in the Indian cement industry.  
 The Company is consistently ramping up its green powe r generation capacity which currently stands at 667 
MW (including of its wholly owned subsidiary in India) . The Company is working on new green energy projects 
to further increase its proportion of green power consumption. 
 Thermal Substation Rate (TSR) improved to 5.2% vis-à-vis 2.3% in Q2’FY25-26 
 All the Company’s manufacturing locations are Zero Liquid Discharge, treating, recycling, and reusing 100% of 
wastewater generated from its operations. These effo rts have enabled the Comp any to improve its water 
positivity index to >8 times in FY2025-26. The Compan y endeavours to maintain and further improve these 
levels in current financial year. 
 Ready-Mix Concrete business 
 
The Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) business continued its strong growth trajectory during Q2’FY27, with the 
commercial network expanding to 28 plants and the overa ll network reaching 35 plants including captive units. 
Driven by strong performance from mature plants and steady ramp-up of newly commissioned facilities, RMC 
volumes increased by 153% YoY to 2.73 lakh  m³, while revenue grew by 182% YoY to ₹129 Crore. The business 
remained operationally EBITDA positive, demonstrating improving scale, operating leverage and the long-term 
potential of the RMC platform.  Cement market outlook  
 
India’s economic outlook remains supportive, driven by  sustained infrastructure spending, urban development 
and stable macroeconomic conditions. While a weak  monsoon could weigh on rural demand, continued 
investments in roads, railways, housing and data centres are expected to support cement demand.
