Mumbai, 04th November  2026:  Saregama, a RPSG Group company, announced its 
Financial Results for the quarter ended 30th September  2026.   
 
Q2 FY27  Financial Highlights:  
Music [ Licensing + Artiste M anagement  + Retail]  
 Music Revenue at INR 2,513 Mn registered 35% YoY growth  
 Music EBITDA at INR 1,540 Mn registered 37% YoY growth  
 Music Net Margin at INR 1,165 Mn registered 46% YoY growth  
  
Live Events [Concert + Devotional  + Stand -Up Comedy + Experiential ] 
 Live Events Revenue at INR 447 Mn registered 101% YoY growth , supported by 
a higher number of events . The Company continued to strengthen its multi -
format live entertainment portfolio, leveraging its artiste relationships and music 
IP to engage audiences across geographies and create opportunities for 
monetisation  
 
Video [Film s + TV +Digital Series + Short format ] 
 Overall Video Revenue at  INR 183 Mn register ed a YoY moderation of 16%. The 
Company continues to pursue a calibrated approach to its video business  
focusing on selective content investments and monetisation opportunit ies as it 
pivots away from own film production to investment in Bhansali Production s. 
 
Key Quarterly Operational  Highlights:  
 The Company released  650+ films & non -films tracks in Q2 FY27 spanning 
across  Hindi, Bhojpuri, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada  
languages, etc.   
 
 Music  releases this quarter includes the super hit release of Telugu  film 
album  “The Paradise ” starring superstar Nani , Tamil  film album  “Meesaya 
Murukku 2 ” starring Hiphop Tamizha , chartbuster songs including songs from 
Indian pop music content “I-Popstar”, and other non -film songs like “Bari” in 
Punjabi, “Aa Fariyale ” in Bhojpuri, etc.  
 
 The Company  added 54 artistes  in Q2 FY27, taking the  total roster to 3 40 
artistes  with an aggregate digital  reach of over 4 50 million . 
 
 The Live Events business scaled up its concert s, experiential, devotional and 
stand -up comedy formats with shows of renowned artistes such as Ilaiyaraaja, 
Backstage Siblings , Manoj Muntashir, Manhar Seth , Carvaan Live ’s Sing with 
Kishore , etc.  
 
 During  the quarter, Saregama collaborated with marquee brands such as  Google , 
Cadbury , Apple , Tanishq , Reliance , etc. for high -impact, integrated campaigns, 
leveraging its IPs to drive strong brand engagement.  The other highlight of the 
quarter was the use of ou r songs in marquee projects including Hindi film Hai 
Jawani Toh Ishq Hona Hai , Tamil Film  DC, Mess  produced by HRX Films , etc.   
 
 Digital footprint across Saregama ’s managed artistes,  owned and controlled 
channels continue to improve beyond 700 Mn+ follower s and subscribers  
across key social media platforms including YouTube, Instagram and Facebook  
 
Avarna Jain , Vice Chairperson , Saregama India, said , “We continued to build on our 
growth momentum during the quarter, supported by the performance of our music 
business and the expansion of our presence across live events and other entertainment 
formats. Our focus remains on maximising the value of our intellectual property through 
multiple monetisation avenues, while maintaining financial discipline and investin g in 
opportunities that support sustainable long -term growth. ” 
 
About Saregama India:   
Saregama India Ltd., part of the RPSG Group, is India ’s leading Entertainment IP 
company with a legacy dating back to 1902. Its diverse portfolio includes film and non -
film music, digital series, television content, film production, short -format content, artiste 
and influencer management, live events, and retail products like Carvaan.   
  
About RPSG Group:  
RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group is one of India ’s fastest growing conglomera tes with a 
significant global presence. The Group ’s businesses include power and energy, carbon 
black manufacturing, retail, IT enables services, FMCG, media and entertainment and 
agriculture.
