Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), we write to inform 
that: 
 
1. JMK Holdings Limited, Signature Infrabuild Limited, Sternal Buildcon Limited and Forever 
Buildtech Limited, wholly owned subsidiaries of the Company, have issued a Corporate 
Guarantee, in favour of Vistra ITCL (India) Limited, acting as trustee on behalf of IndusInd 
Bank Limited to secure the Rupee Term Loan facility of up to Rs. 150 crores availed by the 
Company. 
 
2. JMK Holdings Limited, Signature Infrabuild Limited and Sternal Buildcon Limited, wholly 
owned subsidiaries of the Company, have issued a Corporate Guarantee, in favour of Vistra 
ITCL (India) Limited, acting as trustee on behalf of IndusInd Bank Limited to secure the 
Rupee Term Loan facility of up to Rs. 250 crores availed by Forever Buildtech Limited, 
wholly owned subsidiary of the Company. 
 
3. Forever Buildtech Limited, wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, has issued a 
Corporate Guarantee, in favour of Vistra ITCL (India) Limited, acting as trustee on behalf 
of IndusInd Bank Limited to secure the existing facilities up to Rs. 398.3 Crores availed by 
the Company, Signatureglobal Homes Limited, Signatureglobal Developers Limited and 
Signatureglobal Business Park Limited, wholly owned subsidiaries of the Company. 
 
The details required pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master 
Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30th January 2026 are 
enclosed as Annexure – I, II and III . 
 
Kindly take the above information on your record.
