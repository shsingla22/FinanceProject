Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015: Inauguration of the Bolkunda Silo Unit of IDL Explosives Limited, a group company.  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that IDL Explosives Limited (“IDL”), a group company of Apollo 
Micro Systems Limited, has commissioned the Bolkunda Silo Unit, a new bulk explosives silo plant with a 
capacity of 9,000 MT, spread over 8.4 acres of land near Asansol, West Bengal. 
The facility was inaugurated on 22-10-2026 by Sri Baddam Karunakar Reddy, Founder and Managing Director, 
Apollo Micro Systems Limited, in the presence of senior leadership of IDL, and stands dedicated to operational 
excellence. The inauguration was followed by a tree plantation at the unit by the Managing Director and the 
leadership team, reflecting the Apollo Group's commitment to green and responsible operations. 
Located in the heart of the Eastern Coal Belt and the Central Coal Belt, the Bolkunda Silo Unit will serve the 
mining operations of Eastern Coalfields Limited, Central Coalfields Limited and other customers in the region 
with site-mixed bulk explosives. The unit expands IDL Explosives' national network of bulk explosives plants 
and silos and strengthens its capacity to service long-term bulk explosives orders from Coal India subsidiaries. 
We request you to kindly take the above information on record.
