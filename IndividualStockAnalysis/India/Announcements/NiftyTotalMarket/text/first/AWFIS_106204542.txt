Awfis Partners with ECOS  (India) Mobility & Hospitality Limited 
to Launch Premium Corporate Transportation Services  
 
National, 17th April 2025:  Awfis, India's largest and first publicly listed workspace solutions 
company, has today announced a strategic partnership with ECOS  (India) Mobility & 
Hospitality Limited to offer premium chauffeur -driven and employee transportation services 
to its clients nationwide. This collaboration marks a significant expansion of Awfis' service 
portfolio, addressing the growing need for reliable, secure, and cost -effective corporate 
mobility solutions.  
 This strategic alliance represents yet another step in Awfis' evolution beyond traditional 
workspace solutions, adding transportation to its growing suite of allied services designed for 
Global Capability Centers (GCCs) and enterprise clients. Our research also  highlighted that 
commuting difficulties rank among the top challenges for employees returning to office spaces, with professionals citing transportation concerns as a significant drawback to their 
workplace experience. The partnership directly addres ses this pain point by providing reliable 
and comfortable transportation options.  
 Commenting on the partnership, Mr. Amit Ramani, Chairman & Managing Director, Awfis 
Space Solutions Limited , said, "At Awfis, we have always believed that workspace solutions 
extend beyond physical offices. With this partnership, we are enhancing the overall business 
experience by providing seamless transport solutions that complement our workspace 
offerings. T his partnership is a validation of Awfis' commitment to innovation and client 
satisfaction, and we look forward to working with ECOS  Mobility to tran sform the corporate 
mobility landscape in India."  
 The comprehensive suite of transport services includes:  
• Corporate car rental with flexible options  
• Employee shuttle and carpooling solutions  
• Premium chauffeured services for executives and VIP clients  
• Coordinated transport for conferences and corporate events  
• Hotel travel desk services including airport transfers  
• Self-drive options for increased autonomy  
 
"This partnership represents our commitment to solving real business challenges," said Mr. 
Rajesh Loomba, CMD at ECOS (India) Mobility & Hospitality Limited . "By combining Awfis' 
understanding of business needs with our expertise in transportation logistics, we have 
created a solution that delivers consistency, security, and value across India's major business 
hubs."  
 These services have been specifically designed to meet the needs of both coworking 
professionals and corporate clients, offering customizable solutions that can be scaled 
according to organizational requirements.  The service is immediately available to all Awfis 
clients  through a user -friendly booking system with 24 *7 support. With pan- India coverage, 
businesses can rely on consistent service quality regardless of location.  Transportation is a 
critical aspect of business operations, and Awfis is proud to ad dress this need through its 
collaboration with ECOS  Mobility.
