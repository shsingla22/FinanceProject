KP Group joins Delhi Capitals as Associate Sponsor for Indian Premier League (IPL) Seasons 
2026 -2028  
 
Connecting India’s renewable energy growth with the scale and influence of a leading sporting 
platform,  KP Group, a prominent renewable energy player , has entered into a sponsorship 
arrangement with Delhi Capitals , pursuant to which it is associated as an Associate Sponsor  for 
Indian Premier League (IPL) seasons 2026 to 2028 .  
 
The sponsorship has been undertaken by KP Group entities, including KPI Green Energy Limited, KP 
Green Engineering Limited and K.P. Energy Limited. The association will provide KP Group with a 
comprehensive platform for brand visibility and fan engagement across digital, on -ground, and team -
led initiatives within the Delhi Capitals ecosystem.  
 
Through this partnership, KP Group aims to take its renewable energy & sustainability vision beyond 
the industry corridor and connect with millions of cricket fans across India & beyond. By tapping into 
the IPL's massive audience and its deep -rooted influe nce in Indian culture, the Company looks to 
spotlight clean energy initiatives while establishing a more prominent presence for its brand at the 
national and international level.  
 
Management Commentary  
Dr. Faruk Patel,  Founding Promoter , KP Group, stated:  "At KP Group, we are committed to 
accelerating India’s transition towards clean & sustainable energy and building a future powered by 
responsible infrastructure. In India, cricket is not just a sport but a powerful platform that connects 
with millions acro ss geographies and demographics. Our partnership with Delhi Capitals allows us to 
take the message of renewable energy beyond industry conversations and into the everyday lives of 
people across the country. Delhi Capitals, with its stro ng fan base and growing influence in the IPL 
ecosystem, provides an ideal platform for us to amplify this vision. This association marks an 
important milestone in our journey as we continue to scale our renewable energy footprint across 
globe. We believe t hat such collaborations play a key role in making sustainability more visible, 
relatable, and impactful for a wider audience."  
 
About KP Group:  
Established in 1994, by Dr. Faruk G. Patel, KP Group has grown into a multi -faceted conglomerate 
with core expertise in renewable energy, infrastructure, and innovation. Over the past three decades, 
the group has championed sustainability by spearheading t ransformative projects in wind, solar, 
hybrid energy, Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) and green hydrogen, thereby contributing to 
India’s green energy mission. With a strong portfolio of renewable energy projects, KP Group has 
earned a reputation as a  trusted partner for companies committed to reducing their carbon footprint. 
The group remains steadfast in its mission to contribute meaningfully to India’s energy transition 
while setting new benchmarks in the renewable energy sector .
