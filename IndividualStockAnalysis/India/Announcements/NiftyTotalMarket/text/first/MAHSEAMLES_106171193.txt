Subject: Scrutinizer Report on passing of Resolution through Postal Ballot 
(Remote e-voting) 
 
I Pooja Jain, Practising Company Secretary, pleased to present the report of postal ballot 
conducted by Maharashtra Seamless Limited (CIN- L99999MH1988PLC080545) 
seeking consent of its members for the Resolution as contained in the Notice of Postal 
Ballot dated 27th January, 2025.  
 
1. In terms of provisions of Section 110 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with 
Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014, I was appointed as the 
Scrutinizer by the Company on 27th January, 2025 to conduct the Postal Ballot as 
contained in the Notice dated 27th January, 2025. 
 
2. In terms of Sections 110 and 108 of the Companies Act, 2013, and all other applicable 
provisions, if any, of the Act read with Rules 20 and 22 of the Companies (Management 
and Administration) Rules, 2014, General Circular Nos. 14/2020 dated April 8, 2020, 
17/2020 dated April 13, 2020, 22/2020 dated June 15, 2020, 33/2020 dated September 
28, 2020, 39/2020 dated December 31, 2020, 10/2021 dated June 23, 2021, 20/2021 
dated December 8, 2021, 3/2022 dated May 5, 2022, 11/2022 dated December 28, 
2022, 09/2023 dated 25th September, 2023 and 09/2024 dated 19th September, 2024 
(collectively referred to as "MCA circulars"), in relation to "Clarification on passing of 
resolutions by companies under the Companies Act, 2013 and the rules made 
thereunder" issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India; Members' 
approval was sought for the following: 
 
Item No. 1: To appoint and approve payment of remuneration to Mr. Saket 
Jindal as Managing Director  
                      
3. The Company has informed that, on the basis of the Register of Members and the List 
of Beneficial Owners made available by the Depositories viz., National Securities 
Depository Limited (NSDL) and Central Depository Services (India) Limited (CDSL), as 
on Friday, 31st January, 2025 ("the cut-off date"), the Company completed dispatch of 
the Notice of Postal Ballot by E-Mail on 7th February, 2025, to 1,28,279 Members who 
had registered their email ids with the Company / Depositories. 
 
4. The Company published advertisements regarding service of Postal Ballot Notice 
through e-mail to eligible members in English language newspaper “Business Standard" 
and in Marathi language newspaper “Mumbai Lakshdeep” on 8th February, 2025 and 9th 
February, 2025 respectively.

5. In terms of the aforesaid Notice, Members were required to convey their assent or 
dissent, through remote e-voting system, on e-voting platform provided by National 
Securities Depository Limited (NSDL) from Monday, 10th February, 2025 (9:00 a.m. IST) 
to Tuesday, 11th March, 2025 (5:00 p.m. IST). 
 
6. Total 567 Members had cast their votes on the remote e-voting platform till 05:00 
p.m. (IST) on Tuesday, 11th March, 2025. 
 
7. The votes cast through the e-voting process (remote e-voting) were unblocked on 
Tuesday, 11th March, 2025 after 05:00 p.m. (IST) in the presence of two witnesses viz., 
Ms. Twinkle Gupta and Ms. Jalak Garg who are not in the employment of the Company. 
 
8. Members' demographic details, their voting rights and voting pattern were provided 
by National Securities Depository Limited. 
 
9. After the scrutiny of the remote e-voting, I report that the Ordinary Resolution as 
contained in the Notice of Postal Ballot dated 27th January, 2025 has been passed with 
Requisite Majority on 11th March, 2025 being the last date of remote e-voting for the 
members of the Company.  
 
I have annexed with this report, the details of the Postal Ballot through remote e-voting 
and the analysis of the result of the Resolution as contained in the said Notice as 
Annexure-A .
