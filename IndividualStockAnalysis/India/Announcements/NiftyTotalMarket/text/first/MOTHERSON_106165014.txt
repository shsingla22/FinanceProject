Motherson becomes Tier 1 Supplier to  
Airbus commercial aircraft  
    
04th March  2025, Noida (India) – Motherson, a global manufacturer of aerospace components and assemblies, 
announced that it has been selected as a Tier 1 supplier to Airbus commercial aircraft,  a leading aircraft  
manufacturer . Motherson (via its subsidiary CIM Tools India Pvt. Ltd.)  has signed a multi -year contract to 
manufacture and deliver a range of aerospace components and assemblies directly to Airbus' final assembly 
lines. The supplies will commence from a Motherson’s specialised aerospace facility based in Bengaluru, India.  
Motherson is already  a Tier 1 supplier for Airbus Helicopter and Airbus Defence and Space.  
 
Commenting on this development, Mr. Vivek Chaand Sehgal, Chairman Motherson, said,  
 “We are proud to be recognised as a Tier 1 supplier to Airbus commercial aircraft, and I would like to 
express my heartfelt gratitude to Airbus for their trust in Motherson. This significant achievement highlights 
Motherson's manufacturing capabilities, high -quality standards, and commitment to timely delivery.  Our 
team is committed to delivering the highest quality components and assemblies. We look forward to 
strengthening our partnership.”  
 
 
Mr. Kunal Bajaj, President, of Motherson’s Aerospace Business Division, said,  
This Tier 1 status with Airbus Commercial is expected to drive significant growth in our business with them.  This 
new status  reflects our ongoing investments in advanced manufacturing technologies and quality systems to 
serve our global aerospace customers and their growing production needs. The recent recognition underscores 
our commitment to excellence and strategic role in t he aerospace supply chain.”  
 
 
Motherson Aerospace  
Motherson, through its Aerospace Business Division, is a leading global supplier of aerospace components and assemblies. This division, through its various 
companies, focuses on providing innovative, high -quality solutions for the aerospace industry. With a comprehensive product portfolio that includes critical 
aerospace compo nents, Motherson has become a trusted partner for major aerospace original equipment manufacturers (OEMs). The division opera tes 16 
facilities across four countries, which are equipped with advanced manufacturing technologies that facilitate the production  of high -quality aerospace 
components and assemblies, covering all sections of an aircraft from nose to tail.
