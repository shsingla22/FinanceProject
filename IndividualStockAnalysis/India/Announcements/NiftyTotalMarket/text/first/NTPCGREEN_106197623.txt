Sub.: - Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 - – Incorporation of Joint Venture Company “ NTPC -
MAHAPREIT GREEN ENERGY LIMITED ” 
 
Sir/Madam,   
 
It is hereby informed that a Company - “NTPC -MAHAPREIT GREEN ENERGY LIMITED ”, has  
been incorporated on 08.04.2025  as 74:26  joint venture of NTPC  Green Energy Limited  (NGEL)  
and Mahatma Phule Renewable Energy and Infrastructure Technology Limited  (MAHAPREIT ).  
 
NTPC -MAHAPREIT Green Energy Limited  will be engaged in the business of developing, 
operating and maintaining  Renewable Energy Parks including UMREPP/ RE Projects 
comprising of Solar/Wind/Hybrid with or without Storage up to 10 GW capacities in 
Maharashtra and any other state in India .  
 
Additional information in terms of SEBI  Master  circular is enclosed.  
 
This is for your information and records.
