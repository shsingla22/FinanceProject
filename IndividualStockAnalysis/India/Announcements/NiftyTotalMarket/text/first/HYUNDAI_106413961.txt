Hyundai Motor India Appoints Mr. Sunil Moolchandani  
as Function Head for National Sales  
 
Redefining Strategic Sales Leadership Across Indi a 
 
• HMIL m arks the beginning of a new chapter in its sales leadership as Mr. Sunil 
Moolchandani takes on the role of  Function Head – National Sales  
• He brings over two decades of rich industry experience, consistently driving growth, 
fostering innovation and enhancing operational performance across diverse 
markets  
• Mr. Moolchandani brings deep expertise in strategic sales leadership, customer 
lifecycle management, market development across urban, rural, institutional and 
commercial segments, besides  team transformation across large -scale operations  
 
Gurugram |  October 23, 2025:  Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) today announced the  
appointment of Mr. Sunil Moolchandani as the Function Head – National Sales of the 
company. With over two decades of experience in leading high -performing teams and 
driving market expansion, Mr. Moolchandani brings a strong blend of strategic insight and 
operational depth to the role.  
 
His appointment reflects HMIL’s continued focus on strengthening its national sales 
framework and building deeper connections with customers across key markets. With 
extensive experience in sales leadership, strategic planning and market development, Mr. 
Moolchandani brings valuable perspective to the role. HMIL is confident in his ability to 
guide the sales function toward s consistent growth and long -term success . 
 
Mr. Moolchandani has held leadership positions across prominent  automotive and 
mobility brands, where he successfully spearheaded initiatives in sales transformation, 
dealer network expansion  and customer engagement strategies. His proven track record 
in leveraging data -driven insights, optimizing channel performance and fostering 
innovation , positions him as a key driver of HMIL’s future growth.  
 
Mr. Moolchandani’s leadership is expected to further HMIL’s commitment to excellence, 
innovation and customer satisfaction  - core pillars that continue to define Hyundai’s 
journey in India. His appointment underscores HMIL’s focus on nurturing talent, embracing 
transformation and delivering value to customers and stakeholders alike.
