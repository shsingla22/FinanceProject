Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in Maharashtra  
 
New Delhi , 26th September 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -
growing mid -scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the signing of its latest 
property  Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotels, Saj by the Lake , Malshej Ghat, 
Maharashtra . This is the first of the several properties that the company is expected 
to sign with SAJ Hotels Ltd.  
 
This hotel  will be franchised under Lemon Tree Hotels Limited  for distribution and 
marketing  purposes  and further strengthens the company’s presence in  Maharashtra , 
catering to both business and leisure travellers with a fresh, vibrant, and value -driven 
experience.  
 
Property Highlights   
Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotels, Saj by the Lake , Malshej Ghat , 
Maharashtra  is located in the Western Ghats . It is perfect for nature lovers who 
want to observe a diverse range of stunning birds  and explore hills with calming 
waterfalls. Guests can also visit nearby historic sites like Shivneri Fort , the birthplace 
of Chatrapati Shivaji Maharaj  and Ajoba Hill Fort; or plan a picnic at the picturesque 
Pimpalgaon Joga Dam,  surrounded by the Sahyadri Hills or go on a trek to 
Harishchandra Gad . 
 
The key features of the hotel will include:  
• 55 well -appointed rooms  
• A resto bar 
• A banquet and an open event space  
• Recreational facilities including a swimming pool and a spa  
 
The Pune International Airport  is approximately 110 kms away from the property, 
while the Kalyan Railway Station is about 92 kms away. The hotel is well connected 
by roadways for both public and private transport.   
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels , commented , "We are delighted to welcome SAJ Hotels Ltd  as our 
valued partner. This signing will expan d our leisure portfolio in Maharashtra, a state 
where have 13 operational and 11 upcoming properties in addition to this one . While 
expanding its footprint across the country in cities of all tiers, this signing by Lemon 
Tree Hotels perfectly aligns with the company’s vision of becoming the most preferred 
hospitality chain across different segments."
