Press Release  
Sterling Debuts in Punjab with the Launch of Sterling Borderland Amritsar  
• First Sterling Resort in Punjab  
• Iconic City -Centre Location  
• For Leisure, Business & Celebrations  
 
April 07, 2025  – Sterling Holiday Resorts proudly announces the launch of Sterling Borderland 
Amritsar , marking the brand’s foray into the culturally rich and historically vibrant state of Punjab . 
With this opening, Sterling expands its footprint to 53 destinations across India, spanning hills, 
beaches, jungles, heritage hubs, and spiritual centres.  
Located on Lawrence Road , the primary shopping hub,  the hotel offers a seamless blend of elegant 
design and signature Sterling hospitality. Its central location provides easy access to Amritsar’s revered 
Golden Temple  – just 4 kms away , other  landmarks like Jallianwala Bagh and the Wagah Border , as 
well as key shopping streets and office hubs such as Ranjit  Avenue and Queen’s Road —making it ideal 
for both leisure and business travellers.  
The hotel features thoughtfully designed Classic Rooms, Premier Rooms, and Junior Suites , with plush 
interiors and a choice of king or twin beds, suited for solo guests, families, and business visitors.  
Sterling’s signature restaurant, Curry Cravings , offers an eclectic dining experience with authentic 
Punjabi flavours such as Amritsari Fish, Dahi Kebabs, and Patiala Dohri Seekh Kebab, along with global 
favourites, served in in an ambiance that seamlessly blends contemporary flair with traditional charm . 
With over 10,000 sq. ft. of versatile event space  across Darbar 1 and Darbar 2 , the hotel caters to 
corporate events, weddings, and social celebrations . Elegant interiors, premium  amenities, and a 
dedicated events team ensure every gathering is a memorable one.  
“Sterling Borderland Amritsar is a key milestone in our expansion journey,” said Mr. Vikram Lalvani, 
MD & CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts.  “Amritsar, with its deep -rooted heritage and commercial 
relevance, is an ideal location for our ‘bleisure’ model that caters to both the business & leisure 
segments and blends local experiences with world -class service.”  
“We are delighted to partner with Sterling, known for their warm hospitality and professionalism and 
are confident that with Sterling, our guests will have a truly exceptional experience at the hotel.” said 
Mr. Rajinder Chopra(Kitty) & Mr. Gaurav Shingari Managing Director and Mr. Harshvardhan Chopra, 
Director, Borderland  Hotel.  
 From the serenity of the Golden Temple  to the buzz of Lawrence Road , Sterling Borderland Amritsar 
is the perfect base to explore Punjab, while enjoying the signature warmth and comfort that Sterling 
is known for.  
For bookings and more information, visit www.sterlingholidays.com .
