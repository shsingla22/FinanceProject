Pune  | October  14, 2026: Hyundai Motor India  Limited (HMIL) today announced the formal 
inauguration of its state -of-the-art manufacturing facility based in Talegaon, Pune, in 
Maharashtra, marking a significant milestone in the company's growth story in India.  
The facility was inaugurated by Shri. Devendra Fadnavis,  Hon’ble Chief Minister of 
Maharashtra in the presence of His Excellency, Lee Seong -ho, Ambassador of the Republic 
of Korea to India; Smt. Sunetra Ajit Pawar, Hon’ble Deputy Chief Minister of Maharashtra; Mr. 
Euisun Chung, Executive Chair - Hyundai Motor Group (HMG); Mr. Jaehoon Chang, Vice 
Chair - HMG; Mr. José Muñoz, President & CEO - Hyundai Motor Company (HMC); and Mr. 
Tarun Garg, Managing Director & CEO - HMIL.  
On this special occasion, HMIL also celebrated the landmark achievement of cumulative 
exports of 4 million passenger vehicles from India since 1999 , underscoring the 
company's contribution towards establishing India as a global automotive manufacturing and 
export hub. HMIL is cumulatively the largest exporter of passenger vehicles and the first 
automobile manufacturer to achieve the milestone of 4 million exports from India. The 
company has also sold nearly 10 million passenger vehicles in the domestic market, 
reflectin g its strong and enduring presence among Indian customers.  
Commending HMIL for its valuable contribution to India's manufacturing growth, Hon’ble 
Chief Minister of Maharashtra Shri. Devendra Fadnavis, said, "The inauguration of 
Hyundai Motor India's Pune plant  and the company's achievement of 4 million cumulative 
passenger vehicle exports  reflect India's growing strength as a global automotive 
manufacturing hub . Hyundai's continued investments in advanced manufacturing 
technologies, innovation, localization and skill development  will contribute significantly to 
economic growth, employment generation and development of a robust automotive 
                                                                                                                                 
ecosystem in Maharashtra . We welcome Hyundai's  long -term commitment to the state 
and the nation . The Government of Maharashtra shall extend our full support to Hyundai's 
future growth journey ." 
Developed as one of HMC’s most advanced manufacturing facilities globally, the Pune plant 
combines Industry 4.0 technologies, AI -enabled production systems, advanced quality 
management processes and a fully integrated digital manufacturing ecosystem.  
Adding his perspective on Hyundai Motor Group's long -term vision for India,  Mr. Euisun  
Chung, Executive Chair - HMG, said, “India remains one of Hyundai's most important and 
strategic markets globally. The inauguration of the Pune plant reflects our long -term 
commitment to India's growth and our confidence in its bright future. HMIL’s milestone of 4 
million cumulative vehicle exports demonstrates the global competitiveness of Indian 
manufacturing and highlights India's growing role within Hyundai Motor Group's worldwide 
operations.”  
Beyond manufacturing, HMIL is committed to contributing to India's future through innovation, 
research, electrification and talent development . The company is working with Indian 
institutions and partners through Industry -academia partnerships, battery and 
electrification research initiatives and future mobility programs  to help shape the next 
chapter of sustainable mobility.  
Highlighting the strategic significance of the milestone and HMIL’s commitment to India ,  
Mr. Tarun Garg, Managing Director & CEO - HMIL, said , "The inauguration of our Pune 
plant marks the beginning of HMIL’s next chapter of growth in India . We will invest INR 
11,000 crore  in phases at this facility. Beyond expanding manufacturing capacity, the facility 
will drive industrial development, innovation and employment generation in the region . 
Supporting this vision is a strong regional supplier ecosystem, with 55 supplier partners  
having already invested nearly INR 4,600 crore in the state . Together with tier -II vendors, 
the ecosystem has created more than 18,000 employment opportunities in Maharashtra , 
reinforcing the region’s emergence as a key automotive manufacturing hub. As we celebrate 
30 years in India , we remain committed to creating long -term value for our customers, 
partners and communities. We sincerely thank the Government of Maharashtra, under the 
leadership of Hon’ble Chief Minister Shri. Devendra Fadnavis ji and the Government of 
India , for fostering a progressive ecosystem that continues to strengthen manufacturing, 
investment and economic growth in India."  
The Pune plant also houses a state -of-the-art engine shop with advanced manufacturing 
technologies, producing 150,000 engines annually  to support vehicle production.
