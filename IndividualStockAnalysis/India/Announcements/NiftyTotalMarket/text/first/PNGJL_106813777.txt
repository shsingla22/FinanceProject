 PNG Jewellers Brings Its 194-Year Legacy to Ayodhya 
 
~Member of Parliament and actor Ms Kangana Ranaut inaugurates the brand's 81st store 
and 5th in Uttar Pradesh~  
 
Ayodhya, 11 October 2026: India’s leading national jewellery brand, PNG Jewellers, opened 
its new store in Ayodhya, strengthening its presence in Uttar Pradesh. The store is PNG 
Jewellers’ 81st overall and its fifth in the state, following Lucknow, Kanpur, Gorakhpur and 
Varanasi. The opening follows the encouraging response the brand has received from 
customers across Uttar Pradesh and brings its collections closer to customers in Ayodhya. 
Member of Parliament and Indian film actor Ms Kangana Ranaut inaugurated the store. 
Located next to Rikabganj Women Police Station, the new store gives Ayodhya customers 
access to PNG Jewellers’ products, collections, and quality service closer to home. 
The occasion also marks the debut of Ram Katha, an exclusive collection inspired by the 
Ramayana and the historical, cultural and spiritual heritage of Ayodhya. Launched by Ms 
Kangana Ranaut, the collection is being introduced in its initial phase across PNG stores in 
North India, with availability set to expand nationally to all PNG Jewellers stores soon. The 
store also showcases a range of gold, diamond, temple and bridal jewellery for weddings, 
festive occasions and everyday wear. Customers can explore PNG Jewellers’ collections, 
including Saptam, Katha, Pratha, Eiina and Polmi, with designs crafted for local tastes. 
Ayodhya, revered as the birthplace of Lord Shree Ram, draws people from across the country 
for its temples, festivals and deeply rooted traditions. Jewellery is closely tied to weddings, 
festivals and family celebrations in the city. The new store allows PNG Jewellers to serve 
local customers more conveniently while continuing its expansion in Uttar Pradesh. To 
celebrate the opening, the brand introduced limited-period inaugural offers of up to 50% off 
on gold jewellery making charges and up to 100% off on diamond jewellery making 
charges*, valid till 15th November, 2026.  
Adding to the excitement of the festive season, PNG Jewellers brings together its brand 
ambassadors Madhuri Dixit-Nene and Ranbir Kapoor for the first time in a special festive 
television commercial. The campaign marks a significant moment, bringing together two of 
Bollywood’s most iconic stars in a celebration of tradition, elegance and contemporary 
appeal. This much-awaited collaboration also reflects PNG Jewellers’ vision of connecting its 
nearly two-century-old legacy with a new generation of consumers. 
Dr. Saurabh Gadgil, Chairman and Managing Director of PNG Jewellers, said, “Opening a 
store in Ayodhya is a special moment for us. We have received an encouraging response 
from customers across Uttar Pradesh, and we are pleased to bring PNG Jewellers closer to
