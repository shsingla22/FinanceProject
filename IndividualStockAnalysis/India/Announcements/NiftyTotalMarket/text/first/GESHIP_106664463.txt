PRESS RELEASE  
GE Shipping contracts to buy a secondhand Long Range 2 Tanker  
  
On 16th June 2026, The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (GE Shipping) has contracted 
to buy a secondhand Long Range 2 Tanker of about 110,000 dwt. The 2015 built vessel is expected 
to join the Company’s fleet in Q2 FY27.  
 The proposed vessel will be financed entirely from internal accruals. The purpose of the acquisition 
is expansion of the fleet. 
   
The company’s current owned fleet stands at 39 vessels, comprising 24 Tankers (5 Crude Tankers, 
15 Product Tankers, 4 LPG Carriers) and 15 Dry Bulk Carriers (2 Capesize, 10 Kamsarmax, 1 
Ultramax, 2 Supramax) aggregating 3.19 mn dwt. The Company’s current c apacity utilization is 
close to 100%.  
 
Additionally, the company has contracted to buy one secondhand Medium Range Product Tanker, 
and this purchase transaction is expected to be completed in Q1 FY27.
