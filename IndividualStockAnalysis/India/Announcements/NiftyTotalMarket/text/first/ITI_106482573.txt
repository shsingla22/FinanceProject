PRESS RELEASE 
ITI Limited Wins Ice-Hockey Rink Project in Kaza, Lahaul & Spiti in 
Himachal Pradesh Worth Rs. 72.76 Crores 
Bengaluru: ITI Limited, country’s first PSU post-independence and premier telecom 
manufacturing company has received a work order for Ice-Hockey Rink Project in 
Kaza, Lahaul & Spiti in the state of Himachal Pradesh. The value of the work order is 
worth Rs. 72.76 Crores which has been released by the Office of the Deputy 
Commissioner, Lahaul & Spiti, for construction of an Ice-Hockey Rink facility at Kaza 
Soma, Lahaul & Spiti district, Himachal Pradesh. 
Under the awarded contract, ITI Limited will build a full-fledged Ice-Hockey Rink along 
with a 500-kW solar power backup system, as well as install CCTV cameras, lighting and 
other associated accessories, ensuring a high-quality facility suitable for both players and 
spectators. This project is part of the broader effort to develop world-class sports 
infrastructure in the remote high-altitude region of Spiti Valley which will complement 
ongoing initiatives under the High-Altitude Sports Training Centre scheme. With this 
investment, local administration aims to boost sports opportunities, promote youth 
engagement, and develop sports tourism in Lahaul & Spiti district, which is situated at 
high altitude of approx. 12,000 ft. 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Rajesh Rai, Chairman and Managing Director, ITL 
Limited said — “I am very happy that ITI Limited will contribute towards improving 
ice hockey infrastructure and facilities in Himachal Pradesh. As an enabling company, 
ITI Limited is very excited to be a partner in this inspiring journey in Himachal going 
Sforward. 
Mr. Rai added —Himachal Pradesh is also very close to our heart as ITI Limited has 
been mandated to lay 20115 kms of cable in 81 blocks covering 3615 Gram Panchayaths 
of Bharatnet Phase-III project and has to build the State Network Operation Center 
(SNOC). Till date, ITI Limited has laid around 990 kms of OFC cable across the 
Himachal Pradesh”, he said.
Mr. Rai further said — “With the improved infrastructure, continued efforts of all the 
stakeholders, I am confident that Spiti will soon be recognized not only for Ice Hockey 
but for winter sports as a whole”. 
Ice Hockey began in Spiti in 2019, and though it is still nascent, it has already achieved 
significant milestones, including winning multiple medals in the national games.
