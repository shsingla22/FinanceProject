 Sirs/ महोदय, 
This is in reference to email of BSE Limited, dated 02.07.2025 on the subject cited above. In 
this regard, the following is informed:- 
1. Has NHPC Ltd. officially disclosed the current operational status of Parbati-II and Parbati-
III to the exchange? If there are damages, estimated preliminary damages and how long it 
will take to bring them back in operation? 
Reply: NHPC Limited has not disclosed the current operational status of Parbati-II Project, Himachal 
Pradesh (4X200 MW) and Parbati-III Power Station, Himachal Pradesh (4X130 MW), to the Stock 
Exchanges following the flash floods in Himachal Pradesh on June 25, 2025 as the same was not 
required as per Regulation 30 of SEBI LODR. NHPC will assess and, if required, intimate to Stock 
Exchanges once damage assessments and restoration timelines are finalized, as per relevant 
regulatory requirements. 
Parbati-II Status: 
There were some damages due to the flash flood. The restoration works are in progress and 
generation of one unit is expected to be started by 14.07.2025. Design team is at Project site. 
Damage assessment is under progress. 
Parbati-III Status: 
All units were shut down as a precaution. However, all the generating units of Parbati-III have 
been restored by July 02, 2025.
