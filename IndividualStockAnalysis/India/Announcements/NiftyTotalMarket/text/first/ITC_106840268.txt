1.The unaudited· Standalone Financial Results, Segment Results, Balance Sheet and Statement of Cash Flows were
reviewed by the Audit Committee, and approved by the Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting held on 2nd
November, 2026.
2.The continuing significant brand building costs covering a range of personal care and branded packaged food products
are reflected under 'Other expenses' stated above and in Segment Results under 'FMCG-Others'.
3.10,48,850 Ordinary Shares of t 1/-each were issued and allotted under the Company's Employee Stock Option
Schemes during the quarter ended 3oth September, 2026. Consequently, the issued and paid-up Share Capital of the
Company stands increased to t 1253,05, 17,081 /-as on 30th September, 2026.
4.The Company on 1st August, 2026 completed acquisition of the pulp and paper business of Aditya Birla Real Estate
Limited, operated under the name of 'Century Pulp and Paper', along with the assets, liabilities, contracts, employees
etc., as a going concern on a slump sale basis, in accordance with the Business Transfer Agreement dated 31st March,
2025.
The fair value of assets and liabilities of 'Century Pulp and Paper' have been provisionally determined and recorded in
accordance with Ind AS 103 -'Business Combinations'. Accordingly, the financial results of the Company and
'Paperboards, Paper & Packaging' segment include those of 'Century Pulp and Paper' from 1st August, 2026 and
consequently are not comparable with previous periods.
5.The Company on 28th September, 2026 acquired additional 13,445 Equity Shares of t 10/-each of Sproutlife Foods
Private Limited ('Sproutlife'), consequent to which Sproutlife became a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company with
effect from the said date.
Further, the Board of Directors of the Company at the meeting held today i.e. 2nd November, 2026 has approved,
subject to necessary approvals, the Scheme of Amalgamation of Sproutlife Foods Private Limited, with the Company
under Sections 230 and 232 of the Companies Act, 2013. Pending approval, no effect of the same has been given in
these Results.
6.Exceptional Items for the quarter and six months ended 30th September, 2026 of t 25.66 crores represent expenses
incurred in relation to the acquisition of the pulp and paper business of Aditya Birla Real Estate Limited, operated under
the name of 'Century Pulp and Paper' (Refer Note 4 above).
Exceptional Items for the quarter and six months ended 30th September, 2025 of t 88.08 crores represent final
settlement of the insurance claim towards leaf tobacco stocks, which were destroyed due to fire at a third party owned
warehouse in an earlier year.
7.Consequent to expiry of the GST Compensation Cess, the Government of India increased GST and Central Excise duty
on cigarettes with effect from 1st February, 2026. In accordance with Ind AS 115 -Revenue from Contracts with
Customers and Schedule Ill to the Companies Act, 2013, GST and GST Compensation Cess are excluded from Gross
Revenue from sale of products, whereas Excise duty is not excluded. Consequently, Gross Revenue from sale of
products and services and Excise duty for the quarters ended 30th September, 2026, 30th June, 2026 and six months
ended 30th September, 2026 reflect the impact of sharp increase in Excise duty and are not strictly comparable with
those of the previous periods.
8.This statement is as per Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations,
2015.
