TVS iQube – The choice of 600,000 customers globally  – rides into 
Indonesia  at an introductory price of IDR 29.9 Mn 
 
TVS iQube combines cutting edge technology, robust build quality, and class leading 
comfort of a premium scooter, with zero tail pipe emissions  
 
Jakarta,  June  17, 2025 :  PT TVS Motor  - a subsidiary of  TVS Motor Company , one 
of the world’s leading manufacturer of two and three- wheelers  - today announced th at 
bookings for its flagship all- electric scooter –  TVS iQube , will open soon on June 19 . 
It will be display ed at the new TVS Experience Centre in Rumaradal, Jakarta  and will 
also be seen at the Jakarta Fair , which starts on June 19 .  
 TVS iQube , a choice of more than 6 00,000  customers globally , is built for a premium 
riding experience with best -in-class ride comfort . It offers a real-world  range of 115 
km on a single charge, a choice of ECO and POWER modes and a top speed of 78 kmph. Its quick acceleration delivers 0 –40 kmph in just 4.2 seconds making it ideal for 
urban commuting.  With a rob ust dual pack lithium ion battery  and a best-in-class 
portable 950 Watt charger,  the TVS iQube S can be charged from 0 -80% in just 3 
hours making it convenient and reliabl e. An ample storage space of 32 litres  further 
enhances the commuting experience.  
 TVS iQube also has a host of intelligent  connected features through its SmartXonnect 
technology, including  a 7-inch TFT cluste r, in-built music player control  and call 
acceptance . Additional smart features include  vehicle health, safety  alerts , geofencing  
and Q-Park assist. TVS iQube S will be  assembled at PT TVS  Motor Company  
manufacturing facility at East Karawang and will be available in three colours  – 
Mercury Grey, Mint Blue and Copper Bronze.  
 A very attractive  introductory price of IDR 29.9 Million  has been announced for the 
TVS iQube S for  a limited period .
