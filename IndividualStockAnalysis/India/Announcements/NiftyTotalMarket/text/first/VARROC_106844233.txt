Pune,  Nov  4, 202 6: Varroc Engineering Ltd. (Varroc), a global tier -I auto components 
group, reported strong performance for Q2 FY27, supported by robust automotive 
demand, ramp -up of new programmes and continued growth across its India, overseas 
and electric mobility businesses.  
For Q2 FY27, consolidated revenue increased 33.6% year on year and 11.9% sequentially to 
₹29,479 million . India operations grew 29.5% year on year , while overseas operations also 
delivered strong growth.  
For H1 FY27, consolidated revenue increased 31.8% year on year to ₹55,822 million , 
supported by 29.1% growth in India and 70.7% growth in the overseas business . 
Profitability improves sequentially  
Q2 FY27 EBITDA stood at ₹2,611 million , with an EBITDA margin of 8.9% , improving from 
8.5% in Q1 FY27. PBT before JV profits and exceptional items increased to ₹1,401 million, 
or 4.7% of revenue , compared with 4.3% in Q1 FY27 and 4.1% in Q2 FY26.  
Profitability remained resilient despite renewed commodity inflation arising from geopolitical 
developments. The timing gap between input -cost increases and customer non -recoveries 
impacted Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin by approximately 80 basis points . In addition, more than 
₹1,140 million of agreed customer recoveries remained unrealised in cash at the end of 
H1 FY27 , temporarily impacting cash -flow generation.  
The Company continues to focus on accelerating customer recoveries, value engineering, 
localisation, productivity improvements and disciplined fixed -cost management.  
EV business continues to gain scale  
Varroc's participation in electric mobility continued to strengthen, with revenue from products 
supplied to EV models reaching approximately 19% of consolidated revenue in Q2 FY27 , 
compared with approximately 16% in Q1 FY27.  
EV-related revenue grew approximately 130% year on year , while the E -Mobility Solutions 
business recorded revenue of approximately ₹2,773 million  during the quarter.  
During H1 FY27, Varroc secured net new business wins with annualised peak revenue of 
₹10,127 million , with more than 48% linked to EV programmes , providing further visibility 
for future growth.

Strategic actions to strengthen long -term value creation  
Varroc has initiated two strategic actions aimed at improving profitability and strengthening its 
capabilities in future mobility.  
The Company has decided to restructure its two -wheeler lighting manufacturing 
operations in Italy , while preserving customer programmes and technology capabilities. 
Manufacturing is proposed to be relocated to Romania and Vietnam , which is expected to 
improve capacity utilisation, fixed -cost absorption and cash generation. The restructuring will 
involve a one -time restructuring  cost with an expected payback period of approximately 
three years . 
Varroc is also proceeding with the acquisition of a stake in Forsee Power India , providing 
access to an operating battery -pack platform and know -how. The transaction will 
complement Varroc's existing lighting, electronics and electrical portfolio by adding battery 
packs and further strengthening its position in electric mobility.  
Balance sheet remains comfortable  
Consolidated net debt stood at ₹5,547 million as of September 30, 2026 , with a net debt -
to-equity ratio of 0.29  and net debt to annualised adjusted EBITDA of 0.57x . 
Varroc remains committed to maintaining balance -sheet discipline while supporting organic 
growth and selective strategic investments.  
Management Commentary  
Commenting on the performance, CMD Mr. Tarang Jain said:  
“We delivered another quarter of strong growth, with sequential improvement in 
profitability despite commodity inflation and the timing gap in customer recoveries. 
Our EV business continues to gain scale, with EV -related revenue growing 
approximately 130% year on year and accounting for around 19% of consolidated 
revenue during the quarter.  Our overseas business continue to commercialise new 
business win and grow its Revenue exponentially and reduce the losses.  
We are also taking decisive strategic actions to strengthen Varroc's long -term 
competitiveness. The restructuring of our Italy operations is aimed at creating a more 
efficient manufacturing footprint, while our proposed investment in Forsee Power 
India wil l expand our capabilities in battery packs and BMS.  
Our priorities remain clear: grow profitably, improve margins, generate free cash 
flow, maintain capital discipline and enhance return on capital employed . We 
believe consistent execution against these priorities will enable us to create 
sustainable long -term value for all stakeholders.”

About Varroc Engineering Ltd.  
Varroc Engineering Ltd is a global tier -1 automotive component group. It was incorporated 
in 1988. The group manufactures and supplies E -mobility solutions, Body systems 
solutions, Lighting solutions, HMI solutions, ICE powertrain and Advanced electronics to 
leading OEMs with end -to-end capabilities across design, development and manufacturing 
for two -wheeler, three -wheeler, passenger vehicles, commercial vehicle, and off -highway 
vehicle worldwide. The group income was ₹ 8 9,080 million from continued operations in 
FY26. The group employs more than 6,100 employees (750 + R&D Engineers), has 37 
global operating manufacturing facilities supported by 7 R&D Centres, and has filed more 
than 1 30 patents.  
Varroc Engineering Limited’s shares are listed on the National Stock Exchange (VARROC) 
and the Bombay Stock Exchange (541578)  in India .
