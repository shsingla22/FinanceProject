Burger King India same -store sales gr ow 2.8% in Q2 
FY26, Standalone Revenue up by 1 5.6% 
 
 Gross Margins at 68.3%, increasing  by 80 bps YoY  
 Focus on value offerings, menu innovations and technology investments  
 
For the second  quarter of FY 26 ending on  September  30, 202 5, Restaurant Brand s Asia 
Limited (“RBA”) , earlier known as Burger King India  Limited , reported  Revenue  from 
Operations  (Standalone)  at Rs. 5,687 million , an increase of 15.6% over the same period 
last year . Gross  margin was at 68.3%, rising by 80 bps  over the same quarter in the 
preceding year , and by 60 bps  over Q1 FY26 . Standalone Earnings before interest, tax, 
depreciation and amortization (EBI TDA) for the quarter was at Rs 813 million , growing  by 
16.3% year-on-year. Restaurant Count for Burger King India stood at 533 with a net 
addition of 14  stores during the quarter.   
 
Mr. Rajeev Varman , Whole -time Director and Group Chief Executive Officer of RB A 
commented, " Our s ame-store sales for Burger King in India  was up by 2.8%  this quarter  
along with a healthy increase in gross margins. We witnessed  a positive traffic  growth  in 
both the dine -in and delivery channels. Th is is an outcome of our relentless focus on 
exceeding guest expectations through compelling  value offer ings, menu innovations and 
technology investments.  Gross m argin expansion was on the back of on-going supply 
chain and distribution efficiencies. The GST  reforms introduced by the Government from 
September  22, 2025,  coupled with a benign inflation outlook , augur well for boosting 
consumer sentiment. With positive momentum, we remain confident of our overall growth 
going into the second half  of the year. ”

About Restaurant Brands Asia Limited (Formerly known as Burger King India Limited)  
 
Restaurant Brands Asia Limited ( RBA) is the national master franchisee of the B urger  King® brand in India . 
It has exclusive rights to develop, establish, operate and franchise Burger King ® branded restaurants across  
India. RBA’s  subsidiaries are exclusive national master franchisee s of the bran ds Burger King® and 
Popeyes® in Indonesia.  
 
About B urger King ® 
 
Founded in 1954, the Burger King® brand is a global quick service burger chain known for food quality and 
value and as the only place guests can get the iconic flame -grilled Whopper® burger . The Burger King ® 
system operates more than 19,700 locations in more than 120 markets.
