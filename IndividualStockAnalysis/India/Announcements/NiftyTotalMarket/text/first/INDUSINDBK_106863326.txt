Mastercard Move and IndusInd Bank collaborate to enhance outbound cross -
border payments from India  
 
o Collaboration will leverage Mastercard Move to support outbound remittances  for individuals  
and education payments  for students , with IndusInd Bank plan ning  to expand to SME payments  
 
Mumbai, India: November 17, 2026: Mastercard and IndusInd Bank today announced a collaboration to 
enhance outbound cross -border payments from India through Mastercard Move , Mastercard’s portfolio 
of global money movement capabilities. The collaboration will support  non-trade current account 
remittances , including  person -to-person (P2P)  remittances and education -related payments , enabling  fast, 
reliable and seamless  payments from India  to the U.S., UK, Europe, Canada and Australia , with greater 
transparency and end -to-end visibility.  
 
IndusInd Bank has facilitate d international trade and payment flows, including  remittance capabilities  
supporti ng education, travel, business and other overseas payment requirements. The collaboration with 
IndusInd Bank  marks an important milestone for Mastercard Move in India , with the Bank planning to 
expand its scope to support SME payments.  
 
India sits at the center of significant global money flows. Approximately $12.1 billion in personal 
remittances were sent from India in 2025, according to the World Bank1. At the same time, more than 1.2 
million Indian students were enrolled in higher education abroad in 20252, highlighting  the scale of  
outbound cross -border payments for Indian consumers and families.  
 
Mastercard Move enables banks and fintechs to modernize this experience without having to build 
individual connections into every market. For IndusInd Bank, this will help build a stronger cross -border 
payments proposition, deepen customer relationships and create a more scalable foundation to address 
evolving customer needs and opportunities across the global payments ecosystem.  
 
“India is one of the world’s most dynamic markets for cross -border money movement, with growing 
demand from consumers and businesses for better ways to send money internationally. Together with 
IndusInd Bank, we’re bringing the capabilities of Mastercard M ove to important use cases such as 
remittances and education payments, while laying the groundwork to serve a broader range of payment 
needs over time,” said Pratik Khowala, Global Head of Transfer Solutions, Mastercard.  
 
“Cross -border payments are becoming increasingly important as our customers’ needs become more 
global.  IndusInd Bank has built a strong presence in the remittances market over years, and our 
collaboration with Mastercard reinforces our position in this space.  Through this collaboration, we aim to 
simplify  the international payments experience, while strengthening our capabilities to support 
customers’ evolving needs ,” said Ganesh Sankaran, Executive Director, IndusInd Bank.
