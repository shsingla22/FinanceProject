We wish to inform you that the Company has received an Order dated 07th June, 2024, 
which was received on 14th June, 2024 through post, from the Commissioner of Customs 
imposing a fine of Rs. 4,20,00,000/- and penalty of Rs.12,77,08,366 /- under applicable 
provisions of the Customs Act, 1962. 
 
The Customs department has passed an Order for the levy of fine and penalty under 
Section 125(1), Section 114A and Section 114AA of the Customs Act, 1962 towards 
exemption benefit availed under notification no.57/2017. Against this Order, the Company 
intends to file for legal remedy by way of an appeal.  
 
Based on the Company’s assessment, the Company reasonably expects a favorable 
outcome at the appellate level. There is no material impact on the financials, operations 
or other activities of the Company.  
 
The Company, upon examining the Order, is hereby filing the intimation at the earliest 
opportunity.
