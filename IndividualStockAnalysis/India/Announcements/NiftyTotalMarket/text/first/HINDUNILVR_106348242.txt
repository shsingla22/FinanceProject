This is further to our letter dated 10th July , 2025 , intimating that the Board of Directors of the 
Company had approved appointment of  Ms. Priya Nair (DIN: 07119070) as the MD & CEO of

July, 2030, in succession to Mr. Rohit Jawa.

 The Members, whose names appear in the Register of Members/ List of Beneficial Owners as

forth in the Notice.  
 The Board has appointed Mr. S.N. Ananthasubramanian, Practicing Company Secretary , as 
the Scrutinizer for conducting the said Postal Ballot process.
