The above-mentioned press releases/outcomes have been informed to the exchanges in compliance with LODR 
Regulations, 2015. Moreover, the same capacity related information has been disclosed in our recent press release 
titled “Premier Energies Secures Orders Worth ₹2,307.30 Crores in Q3 FY26”, which was intimated to the Stock 
Exchanges on 31st December 2025, that these orders provide sustained revenue visibility and support the 
Company’s ongoing capacity expansion plans. 
 
The said press release also outlined the Company’s expansion roadmap, which aims to achieve 10.6 GW of solar 
cell capacity and 11.1 GW of solar module capacity by September 2026. 
 
Accordingly, the expansion plans referred to in the NDTV article titled “Premier Energies To Add 7.4 GW Cell, 
6 GW Module Capacity Annually At ₹11,000-Crore Capex” are not new or unpublished information. The above-
mentioned details were already in the public domain pursuant to the Company’s press release dated 31st 
December 2025.  
 
The movement in the share price is market driven and there is no Unpublished Price Sensitive Information which 
is pending to be disclosed to the stock exchanges. Further, there are no regulatory or legal proceedings being 
initiated in relation to the aforementioned announcement. 
 
Since the information is already in public domain, the article will not bear any material impact on the Company.
