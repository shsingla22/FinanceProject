Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 - Approval for disbursement of 3rd tranche of 
incentive under Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme for White Goods 
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, as amended, the Company hereby wishes to inform regarding receipt 
of approval for disbursement of incentive under Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme 
of Government of India for White Goods. 
 
PG Technoplast Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of PG Electroplast Limited 
("Company"), has received sanction letter dated March 27, 2026, from IFCI Limited a 
Government of India undertaking, acceptance of which was given on March 28, 2026. The 
sanction letter pertains to the disbursement of the incentive claim to PG Technoplast 
Private Limited. The claim is for the Determined Sales Value of FY 2024-25 under the 
Production Linked Incentive Scheme for White Goods-Air Conditioner, LED Lights Motors 
and Display Panel (LCD/LED) (“PLI-Scheme”). 
 
The payment of Rs. 37,50,00,000/- (Rupees Thirty-Seven Crore Fifty Lakhs only) has 
been sanctioned towards the incentive for the Determined Sales Value of FY 2024-25, as 
per existing terms and conditions of the PLI-Scheme, as amended from time to time. 
 
This disclosure will also be hosted on the Company's website viz. https:// www.pgel.in /  
 
This is for your information and records.
