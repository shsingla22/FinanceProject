LEMON TREE HOTELS SIGNS KEYS PRIMA BY LEMON TREE HOTELS, 
KARNAL  
Strengthens the brand’s growing presence in Haryana with a strategic expansion in one 
of the State’s  key transit and commercial hubs 
New Delhi, May 18, 2026 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, one of India’s leading hospitality 
companies, today announced the signing of Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotels, Karnal  
in Haryana. The property will be franchised under Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
With this signing, the Company expands its footprint in Haryana to 13 hotels, 
encompassing 11 operational and two upcoming properties, further reinforcing its strategic 
presence across vital markets in North India.  
Karnal is rapidly emerging as a prominent urban centr e in Haryana, backed by robust 
infrastructure development, expanding industrial activity, leading educational institutions, 
and its advantageous position along a major highway corridor. The city’s evolving economic 
landscape and rising transit volume contin ue to drive sustained demand for reliable, 
quality -driven branded hospitality catering to corporate, social, and transit travel lers. 
The upcoming hotel will feature 50 well -appointed rooms , along with a restaurant, 
banquet & conference hall, swimming pool, fitness centre  and other public areas, offering 
guests a comfortable and contemporary hospitality experience.  
Commenting on the signing, Mr. Neelendra Singh , Managing Director , Lemon Tree 
Hotels Ltd. , said:  “Karnal’s strategic positioning along the heavy -traffic Delhi –Chandigarh 
commercial highway makes it an essential addition to our Haryana portfolio, taking our 
footprint in the State to 13 hotels. As a key transit and economic hub, Karnal sees a steady 
influx of business, social, and leisure travellers who actively seek dependable, branded 
accommodation. This signing allows us to tap into the high -volume demand of the GT Road 
corridor while steadily building on our multi -brand presence across Haryana's core  growth 
zones.”  
The hotel offers convenient connectivity and is located approximately 127 km from 
Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport, Chandigarh  and around 4.2 km from Karnal 
Railway Station.  
This signing further aligns with Lemon Tree Hotels’ disciplined strategy of expanding across 
high-growth regional markets and anchoring its portfolio along key economic and transit 
links across India.  
 

 
 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India’s leading hospitality companies, catering 
to a wide range of customers – from value -conscious travellers to premium business and 
leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels & Resorts, Lemon T ree Premier, 
Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys Select, and Keys Lite – the group offers 
experiences across upper upscale, upscale, upper midscale, midscale, leisure, wildlife, and 
spiritual segments.  
LTHL operates 130+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing pipeline 
of 130+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and 
Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, and Indore – and with an 
international presence in Dubai, Bhutan, and Nepal – Lemon Tree Hotels delivers 
exceptional comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, refreshing experience.  
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 250+ properties 
(operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for both business and 
leisure travellers.
