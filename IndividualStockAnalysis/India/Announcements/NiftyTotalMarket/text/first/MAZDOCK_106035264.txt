PRESS RELEASE 
Second Edition Of Mazdock Mumbai 10K Challenge 
Celebrating its 250th anniversary, Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Ltd. (MDL) 
proudly presents the second edition of Mazdock Mumbai 10K Challenge, an event 
set to raise the pulse of the city on Sunday, 15" December 2024. This significant 
milestone not only reflects the rich heritage of Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited 
but also symbolizes its continued commitment to excellence in shipbuilding and 
maritime innovation. This year’s event holds extra significance as MDL celebrates 
250 years of maritime excellence and being the first amongst shipyard for earning 
the NAVRATNA status 
Since 1774, MDL has stood tall as a pillar of national pride, crafting warships, 
submarines, and commercial vessels that defend our shores and fuel our economy. 
Now, as we mark this historic milestone, we invite you to join us in a celebration of 
fitness, camaraderie, and community spirit.

The Mazdock Mumbai 10K Challenge is more than just a race. It's a 
celebration of health, community, and national pride. Join us for an unforgettable 
experience filled with: 
> Energetic music and cheering crowds 
> Post-race refreshments and photo opportunities 
> Medal presentation ceremony 
Mazdock Mumbai 10k Challenge aims to bring together avid runners, fitness 
enthusiasts, and the community at large to celebrate the spirit of health, wellness, 
and maritime history. The event will feature a scenic route that showcases the vibrant 
city of Mumbai, starting from Azad Maidan and covering key landmarks along the 
way, such as Ballard Estate lined with heritage buildings. 
Participants will not only have the opportunity to test their endurance but also 
join Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Ltd. in commemorating its remarkable journey 
over the past 250 years. Mazdock Mumbai 10k Challenge is not just a race; it's an 
opportunity for individuals to be a part of history and contribute to the celebration 
of 250 glorious years of Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Ltd.
