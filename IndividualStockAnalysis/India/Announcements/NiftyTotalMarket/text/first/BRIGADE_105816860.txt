PRESS RELEASE 
 
BRIGADE GROUP REPORTS IMPRESSIVE PERFORMANCE IN FY24  
BENGALURU, April 16, 2024 : Brigade Enterprises Limited reported impressive performance and 
growth across all business segments for the year ended March 31, 2024. The company has achieved 
presales of INR 6,013 Cr in FY24 and INR 2,243 Cr in Q4 FY24 – the highest ever for both in terms of 
a quarter as well as financial year. Additionally, Real Estate sales volumes of 7.55 Mn. Sft. in FY24 and 
2.72 Mn. Sft. in Q4 FY24, along with average realisation for FY24 increasing by 23% over FY23. 
Collections across the Group for FY24 stood at INR 5,915 Cr as against INR 5,424 Cr for FY23.  
 
Under the operational Leasing portfolio, leasing grew by 14 % YoY with 1 Mn. Sft. of additional area 
leased over FY23, achieving 97% occupancy in the overall portfolio. Incremental leasing of 0.20 Mn. Sft. 
in the leasing portfolio in Q4 FY24. 
 
In the Hospitality vertical, average occupancy stood at 72% (grown 3 percentage points) and ARR stood 
at around INR 6,480 which recorded around 8% growth in FY 24.  
 
In FY 24, we have launched 5.26 Mn. Sft. in the Residential vertical and 0.94 Mn. Sft. in the Commercial 
vertical.  
 
Commenting on the company’s performance, Pavitra Shankar, Managing Director, Brigade Enterprises 
Limited said, “The residential business continued to drive sales growth, with all other verticals of the 
company contributing significantly and finishing strong in the financial year. This year, we have been able 
to achieve our best ever operational performance, and we aim to leverage this performance in FY25. Our 
outlook is optimistic, as we believe demand for residential space will continue to be strong. Our leasing 
efforts gained momentum and healthy growth in ARR in our hospitality vertical as well. We are 
aggressively pursuing land acquisition opportunities in our target markets and continue to add high quality 
assets to our land bank.”  
 
Brigade Group has a healthy pipeline of new launches of around 12.61 Mn Sft in Residential, 6.33 Mn Sft 
in Commercial and 1.06 Mn Sft in Hospitality.   
 
About Brigade Group :  Established in 1986, Brigade Enterprises Ltd. is one of India’s leading property 
developers with near to four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their 
stakeholders and winning their customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark buildings and 
transformed skylines across cities – Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Gift City-Gujarat, 
Thiruvananthapuram, Mangaluru and Chikkamagaluru with developments across Residential, Office, 
Retail and Hotels.
