Sub: Hon’ble High Court  of Karnataka  passing an order confirming the validity of Forest Clearances  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 , we write to inform Stock E xchanges that the 
Hon’ble High Court of Karnataka vide order dated 29 April 2025 (certified copy issued on  
6 May 2025) has confirmed that the Forest Clearances of the Company are valid.  
 
To recall, vide our letters No. SMIORE/SEC/1402 01/02 dated 1 February 2014  and No. 
SMIORE/SEC/140221/02 dated 21 February 2014 , we had intimated BSE Limited regarding 
communication dated 29 January 2014 issued by Ministry of Environment & Forest (MoEF) directing us to 
suspend mining operations of both the  mining leases of the Company and the subsequent stay granted by 
the Hon’ble Supreme Court allowing us to resume our mining operations.  
 
After prolonged court proceedings, Hon’ble High Court of Karnataka has passed its final judgement in 
favour of the Company confirming the Forest Clearances obtained by the Company vide approvals dated 
12 January 2007 and 14 January 2007 to be valid and the Company can continue its operations without the 
requirement of obtaining renewal of the said approvals . 
 
Stock Exchanges are requested to kindly take the same on record.
