Re: Pricing of U.S.$ 300 million , 7.375% fixed rate notes  due 2028 (“Notes”) under the U.S.$ 750 
million Euro Medium Term Note Programme (“EMTN Programme”)  
In furtherance of our letter s dated 15th and 19th of April 2024, we wish to inform you that the Financial 
Resources and Management Committee of the Board of Directors of Manappuram Finance Limited at 
its meeting held on 08th May 2024 has approved the pricing, tenor and other terms of the Notes under 
the EMTN Programme.  The proceeds of the Notes will be used by the Company for onward lending, 
refinancing and other activities in line with the Reserve Bank of India’s Master Directions on External 
Commercial Borrowings, Trade Credits and Structured Obligations dated March 26, 2019, and other 
applicable laws (as amended, modified or replaced from time to time).  
The required details in relation to the Notes are set out in Annexure A  hereto.  
Further, the Committee has approved the execution of the pricing supplement and the subscription 
agreement.  
You are requested to take the same on record and treat the same as compliance under Regulation 30 and 
other applicable regulations under the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements), 2015 and acknowledge rece ipt.
