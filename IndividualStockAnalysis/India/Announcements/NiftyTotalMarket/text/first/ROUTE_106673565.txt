Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
(“Listing Regulations”) this is inform you that a cyber security incident has occurred at Masivian S.A.S, a wholly 
owned subsidiary of Route Mobile (UK) Limited and a step-down wholly owned subsidiary of Route Mobile 
Limited (“Company”), which has impacted certain systems within the Masivian environment located in South 
America, primarily in Colombia, and relates only to customers and systems based in Colombia. The affected 
systems have since been isolated and containment measures have been implemented. The incident is confined 
to Masivian S.A.S. and has not impacted the Company or any other group companies. 
 
We would like to assure you that the technical team of Masivian S.A.S along with a specialized team of 
cybersecurity experts and the Masivian S.A.S management initiated necessary precautions and protocols to 
mitigate the impact of this incident. Masivian S.A.S is investigating the matter and appropriate containment, 
and remediation actions are being taken in a controlled manner to address the incident. 
 
The aforesaid disclosure is being made in the interest of good corporate governance and transparency. 
 
You are requested to take the above information on record.
