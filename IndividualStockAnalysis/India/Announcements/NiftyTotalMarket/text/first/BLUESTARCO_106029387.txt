represents one equity share of the face value of Rs 2 each;

equity shares of Rs 2 each;

12 months from the date of Grant. The Stock Options granted to the Eligible 
Employees shall vest within the Vesting Period in the manner as set forth in 
the Grant letter ; 
 
f. Exercise Period  - The vested Stock Options can be exercised any time after 
vesting depending on right to exercise within a period of 7 years from date of 
Grant;  
 
g.  The Grants have been made as per the terms and conditions mentioned in 
the ESO P 2024 , as modified, from time to time.
