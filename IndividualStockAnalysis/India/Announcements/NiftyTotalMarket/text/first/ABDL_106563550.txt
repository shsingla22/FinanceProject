ICONiQ White Whisky Makes History: World’s Fastest  Growing  Millionaire 
Spirits  Brand Crosses 10 Million Cases Milestone  
~ Continued growth strengthens ABD’s momentum in the Prestige & Above spirits 
segment ~  
Mumbai, 23rd March 2026 : Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD), the largest domestic 
spirits company by volume in India and the maker of ICONiQ White International Grain 
Whisky , reports  another  historic milestone  as its flagship brand  surpasse s the 100 Lakhs , or 
10 million  case s in FY26  and still counting . With the financial year  drawing to close , this 
landmark achievement  showcas es one of the fastest growth trajector ies within the relatively 
high -margin Prestige & Above  segment . 
Launched in September 2022 with a digital first debut through ABD’s  MetaBar , in the 
metaverse , ahead of its market rollout, ICONiQ White Whisky began its initial product 
introductions in select markets of East and North India before expanding nationally. The brand 
recorded sales of 0.32 million  cases in FY23 during its early rollout phase.  
Momentum accelerated sharply  in FY24 , with volumes  rising to 2. 27 million  cases, earning 
recognition as the fastest growing millionaire spirits brand in the world for calendar year 
2023 as per Drinks International’s Millionaires’ Club Report 2024.  The growth trajectory  
continued in FY25 , with the brand crossing 5.7 million cases , securing the distinction  for the 
second conse cutive year as the fastest growing millionaire spirits brand in the world  for 
calendar year 2024 as per Drinks International’s Millionaires’ Club Report 202 5.  
Just 43 months old,  ICONiQ White Whisky has now achieved a historic milestone of 10 million 
Cases only in FY26, underscoring one of the fastest scale up journeys seen in the global spirits 
industry.  
 
 
In addition, ICONiQ White Whisky has now  expanded its reach and is available in Canteen 
Stores Department (CSD)  as it continues to grow its international footprint with  presen ce in 
multiple  overseas markets.  Together, these developments and successes reflect  ICONiQ White  
Whisky ’s deepening connect with consumers across regions.  
Speaking on the achievement, Alok Gupta, Managing Director, ABD , said, “The journey from 
zero to 10 million cases in such a short period is a landmark achievement that redefines the 
growth playbook in the spirits industry.  From the very beginning, our goal with ICONiQ White 
was to create a world -class whisky in India, and this milestone validates that vision. ICONiQ 
White has evolved from a disruptive 'new -age' launch into a record breaking  spirits brand , 
proving that our product proposition resonates deeply with today’s consumers. As we scale 
across domestic markets and 9 countries, this milestone reinforces our commitment to leading 
the high -margin Prestige & Above segment and building long -term value through consistent, 
global execution.”  
The brand's success is backed by its commitment to quality, which has been recognized  by its 
multiple international accolades , highlighting its  superior craftsmanship . The whisky 
secured  Gold Medals at the International Spirits Challenge (ISC) 2024, International Whisky 
Competition (IWC) 2024, and India Wine & Spirits Awards (IWSA) 2024 . Additionally, it was 
honoured  with the  Gold Quality Award at Monde Selection 2024  and the  Superior Taste 
Award at the International Taste Institute 2024 in Brussels,  reinforcing its standing as a 
premium whisky of international standards.  
Beyond its resounding success, ICONiQ White  Whisky  has cultivated a distinct identity that 
mirrors the energy of India's contemporary youth culture. This natural alignment with a 
generation that prizes individuality and authentic expression has led the brand to the heart of 
the creative community. This sy nergy was most visible at recently concluded ICONiQ White 
NH7 Weekender 2026 , where the brand c ame to life on one of India’s leading cultural 
platforms.  

 
About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited ( www.abdindia.com ) 
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD) is the largest domestic Spirits company in India, in terms of 
annual sales volumes. ABD has a presence in five main flavors, i.e., whisky, brandy, rum, vodka, and gin, 
with ‘millionaire’ brands like Officer’s Ch oice Whisky, Officer’s Choice Blue Whisky, Sterling Reserve 
Premium Whiskies and ICONiQ White Whisky. Currently, its manufacturing network comprises 38 units, of 
which 9 are owned bottling units, 1 is owned PET bottle manufacturing plant, 2 owned distiller ies, and 26 
non-owned manufacturing units.
