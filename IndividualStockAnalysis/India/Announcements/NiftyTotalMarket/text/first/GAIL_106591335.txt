GAIL to set up 600 MW Solar Power Project in UP  
 
New Delhi, April 15, 2026:  GAIL (India) Limited, India’s integrated energy major, 
today signed contract agreements with M/s TUSCO Limited for setting up of 600 MW 
of solar power project at TUSCO’s Solar Park located at Jhansi in Uttar Pradesh. The 
contract agreements were signed by Shri Parivesh Chugh, Executive Director (SD & 
Renewables), GAIL (India) Limited and Shri Anil Tyagi, CEO, TUSCO Ltd. in the 
presence of Shri R K Singhal, Director (Business Development), GAIL (India) Limited, 
Shri Neeraj Verma, Executive Director, THDC and  other senior officials from both the 
companies.  
 
The solar power project will be set up along with a 550 MWh Battery Energy Storage 
System (BESS). The projects will primarily cater to meet the captive energy 
requirements of GAIL’s Petrochemical Plant at Pata in Auraiya district of Uttar Pradesh, 
besides meeting the additional demand arising from Electrification of Gas -based 
equipment and CBG plants, which are being set -up in Uttar Pradesh.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Shri R K Singhal, Director (Business Development), GAIL 
(India) Limited, said, “This solar project reflects GAIL’s commitment for diversifying 
into low -carbon and sustainable energy solutions.” He further emphasized that the 
proje ct also underscores GAIL’s commitment to expand its business footprint in U.P.
