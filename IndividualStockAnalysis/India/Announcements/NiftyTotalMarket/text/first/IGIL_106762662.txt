Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of 
International Gemological Institute FZCO (“IGI Dubai”), a step-down wholly owned subsidiary of International 
Gemological Institute Limited (“the Company”), at its meeting held on August 31, 2026, has inter-alia approved 
the consolidation of control over International Gemological Institute (Botswana) Proprietary Limited (“IGI 
Botswana”), a diamond grading and certification laboratory in Botswana. 
 
Subject to regulatory approvals and upon completion of the above, IGI Botswana will become a wholly owned 
subsidiary of IGI Dubai and step-down subsidiary of the Company.
