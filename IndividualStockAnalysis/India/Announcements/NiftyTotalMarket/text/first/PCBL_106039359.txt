 
 
PCBL reports Strong  Operational and Financial Performance during Q2FY25  
The Consolidated Revenue from operations was up 45% YoY to Rs. 2,163 Cr. in Q 2FY25 from Rs. 1,487 Cr. 
(Q2FY24) on the back of better realization, higher sales volume  in Carbon Black segment (Rubber & 
Specialty Blacks)  and revenue from recently acquired Aquapharm  Chemicals . 
The Sales volume (Carbon black) increased by  14% YoY to 1, 48,693 MT in Q 2FY25 from 1, 30,111  MT in 
Q2FY24 supported by  increased  specialty volume s and higher utilization in PCBL (TN) plant.  
The Consolidated EBITDA was up 53% YoY to Rs.369 Cr. in Q 2FY25 as against  Rs.2 41 Cr. in Q2FY24.  
Consolidated EBIDTA/MT in carbon black business improved to  Rs. 21,324/ - during the quarter.  
Power Generation increased  by 25%  from 167 MU in Q 2FY24 to 209 MU during the quarter with external 
sales volume of 126 MU as against 103 MU in Q 2 FY24. PCBL’s average realization stood at 3.56 /kwh.  
During H1FY25, Consolidated Revenue from operations increased by 52% YoY to Rs.4,307 Cr. from Rs. 
2,834 Cr. (H1FY24) .  Sales volume increased 19% YoY to 3,02,610 MT in H1FY25.  The Consolidated EBITDA 
for H1FY25 was up 62%  YoY to Rs. 738 Cr. FY25 from Rs.455 Cr . 
PCBL(TN) facility in Chennai has received app roval s from major tyre manufacturers in  India and capacity  
utilization remains above  80% during H1FY25 . The sales volume for H1FY25  stood at 54,210  MT. 
Aquapharm Chemicals reported a steady  performance  during the quarter . Q2FY25 revenue stood at 
Rs.3 62 Cr and EBITDA stood at Rs.5 0 Cr. The quarterly sales volume stood at 24, 510 MT. The Company is 
implementing various  cost optimization and operational efficienc y measures which would result in better  
capacity utilization  leading to  improvement in company`s  performance in the ensuing quarters.  
The Company is amid an aggressive capacity expansion program in Carbon Black  (Rubber & Specialty 
Blacks)  and water treatment, detergents and oil & gas chemicals business under Aquapharm.  
The Carbon Black capacity of the Company stood at 7,70,000 MTPA. PCBL ex pects to commission the 
Specialty project of 20,000 MTPA capacity in Mundra and 30,000 MTPA brownfield expansion of Carbon 
Black facility in PCBL(TN) in Q3FY25. The second phase of PCBL(TN) expansion  of 60,000 MTPA  would take 
the total installed capacity to 8,80,000 MTPA in FY26.  
Aquapharm Chemicals  is implementing  an expansion project of  38,000 MTPA  which is likely to be 
commissioned by March 2025.   
PCBL has e xecuted a JV agreement with Kindia Pty Ltd. to form Nanovace Technologies Ltd. for developing 
nano -silicon products to be used in anodes of Li -Ion batteries. Nanovace is s etting up a pilot plant which 
would be  followed by a full -scale plant.  
 
 
 
The Carbon black business continues to be on a strong growth trajectory and is well poised to capture the 
growth in the domestic and exports markets with new capacity additions, wide product suite and strong 
R&D capabilities. The company continues to invest in growth, innovation and supply chain c apabilities.
