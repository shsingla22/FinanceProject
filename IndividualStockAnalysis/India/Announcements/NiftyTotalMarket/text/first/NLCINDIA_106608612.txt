As advised by the Stock Exchanges vide email dated 27.02.2026, the subject matter of the 
Non-compliance of Corporate Governance re quirements under Regulation 17(1) of  
the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for the quarter ended 31st December, 2025 and the 
penalty as levied by the exchanges was placed before the Board of Directors of the 
Company at the meeting held on 26th March, 2026. 
 
The Board noted the action taken by the Stock Exchanges, involving levy of fines, for  non-compliance of Corporate Governance requirements prescribed under the  
SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, pertaining to Composition of the Board of Directors 
including failure to appoint women director during the quarter ended 31
st December, 2025.  
 
The Board while noting the above, advised to  send a communication to the Ministry of 
Coal, being the Administrative Ministry, to appoint requisite number of Independent Directors (including Independent Women Director) on the Board of the Company, in 
compliance to SEBI (LODR) Regulations and ot her applicable provisions in order to avoid 
imposition of penalty by stock exchanges in future.
