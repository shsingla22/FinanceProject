We hereby inform that the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India has today pronounced its judgment  on an 
appeal filed in 2009 by the Commissioner of Central Excise (CCE) for the period 2005 to 2007, against four 
job workers of Marico Limited ( “Company ”) and the Comp any regarding classification of pure coconut 
oil under the erstwhile Excise regime  for levy of excise duty .  
 
In its order pronounced today, t he Hon’ble Supreme Court of India has dismissed the appeal filed by CCE 
and upheld the order passed by the Tribunal classifying pure coconut oil as edible oil for the purpose of 
levy of duty. The Company is yet to receive a certified copy of the order and will provide further updates 
on th e matter once the order is received as per due process.
