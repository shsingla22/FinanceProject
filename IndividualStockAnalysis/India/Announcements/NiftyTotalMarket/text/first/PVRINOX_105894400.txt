PVR INOX Limited EXPANDS FOOTPRINT IN HYDERABAD WITH ALL 4K LASER 
CINEMA  
~Brings Hi-tech Cinema experience to the Hi -tech City with cutting -edge technology ~ 
National , June  27, 2024: PVR INOX  Limited , the largest and the most premium cinema exhibitor in India 
has announced the launch of its new  4-screen c inema in Hyderabad with all 4K Laser Projection in all 
auditoriums.  Located at Prism  Mall, this state -of-the-art cinema promises an unparalleled movie -going 
experience  with  an extensive array of amenities  for Gachibowli’s  cinephiles.  
 
The new cinema will strengthen PVR INOX Limited ’s foothold in Hyderabad  with 97 screens in 17 
properties and in the state of Telangana  with 110 screens in 20 properties and continues its expansion in 
South  India  with 579 screens in 102 properties.  
Located just 7 Km from HITEC City, Hyderabad ’s affluent IT neighborhood, PVR INOX Prism boasts an easily 
accessible location catering to tech professionals, residents  of nearby high -rises, and students from 
esteemed institutions like the University of Hyderabad.  
With a seating capacity of 950 audiences  inclusive of celebrity recliners , the new cinema is equipped with 
the best -in-class theatrical technology to offer an immersive and enhanced cinematic experience. This 
includes the 4K laser  projection, advanced Dolby ATMOS immersi ve surround sound and Next G en 3D 
system .  
Commenting on the announcement, Mr. Ajay Bijli, Managing Director, PVR INOX  Limited , said,  
“Gachibowl i is a destination for comfort, c onnectivity and convenience  and we are  thrilled to add a new 
entertainment landmark to the city of Charminar.  PVR INOX Prism is  the perfect destination for unwinding 
after work or enjoying a weekend outing with friends and family . With the launch of our Gachibowli 
multiplex, we hope to create a space where movie lovers can come together, share experiences, and forge 
lasting memori es through the magic of the silver screen .” 
The newly launched property has an exciting array of attractions. These include an upcoming shop -in-shop 
for movie merchandising, a hydroponics display at the foyer, and the Friday night special ‘Old is Gold ’ 
Telugu karaoke.  
The aesthetics of the cinema have a stunning Art Deco -inspired lobby, where grandeur meets functionality. 
Bold geometric patterns, sleek finishes, and thoughtfully placed pendant ceiling lights create a space that 
is both elegant and inviting . Multiple food outlets cater to diverse tastes, ensuring a complete 
entertainment experience.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
“Gachibowli is a dynamic hub of innovation and energy, and we're thrilled to bring the unparalleled movie -
going experience of PVR INOX  Limited  to its heart. With our new INOX Prism Mall multiplex, we are  not just 
offering cutting -edge 4K laser projection and plush comfort, but also creating a vibrant space where 
entertainment seamlessly blends with the district's lifestyle. We're confident that this new cinema will 
become a cherished destination for movie lovers and a testament to PVR INOX's commitment to redefining 
cinematic experiences. ” said Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli, Executive Director, PVR INOX Limited.  
 
 
About PVR INOX  
 
PVR INOX Limited is the largest film exhibition company in India with 1757 screens across 362 properties 
in 113 cities (India and Sri Lanka).  
Since their inception, both PVR and INOX have created iconic cinema brands with storied histories of 
achieving significant milestones and setting benchmarks in the film exhibition industry. With a collective 
heritage of transforming out -of-home entertainment in the country, the merged company offers a wide 
range of cinema services such as child -friendly audis, the latest screening technology, superior sound 
systems, a wide range of F&B offerings, film and non -film content and array of formats in the premium 
screen category.
