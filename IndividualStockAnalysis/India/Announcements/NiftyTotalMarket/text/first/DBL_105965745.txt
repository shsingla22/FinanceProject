In accordance with the provisions of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.   
 
We would like to inform that the Company has invoked arbitration against the Public Works Department 
(B&R), Punjab seeking certain relief as eligible to it on account of various delays and breaches committed 
by the Public Works Department (B&R), Punjab dur ing execution of the construction of  Development to 
four lanes with paved side shoulder of Shahkot -Moga section of NH -71 [New NH No. 52] from 
existing km 44.600 to km 77.200 including construction of Dharamkot bypass in the state of Punjab 
under NHDP -IV through Engineering, Procurement & Cons truction (EPC) Contract Basis  awarded 
under EPC agreement dated: 22.01.2016.  
 
The Arbitral Tribunal has pronounced the award  and Key Aspects of the Award are as follows:  
 
1. Damages on account of delay in providing the Right of Way (ROW)  
2. Payment of Bonus for early completion of works  
3. Payment on account of Change of Scope  
4. Payment on account of delay in releasing Bank Guarantees for Retention Money  
5. Arbitral fee paid by the Dilip Buildcon Limited  
 
The financial implication of the award is in tune of  Rs. 16.65 Crores . Pursuant to the Award, NHAI is liable 
to pay the Dilip Buildcon Limited the said amount forthwith. In the event NHAI fails to pay the said amount 
an interest at a rate of 12% per annum shall be levied on NHAI till the date of payment of the principal 
awa rded amount.  
 
The award may be challenged by the Public Works Department (B&R), Punjab in accordance with the 
provisions of Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996, in which case  Dilip Buildcon Limited  will appropriately 
defend the matter.
