Investor Release
Commenting on the performance Raj P Narayanam, Founder and Executive Chairman,
Zaggle PrepaidOcean ServicesLimited said,
“I am pleased to report that our continued efforts towards improving operating cash flows have enabled us
to turn OCF positive during Q2FY27 at both standalone and consolidated levels. We believe this marks an
importantsteptowardsbuildingamoresustainableandscalableoperatingmodel.
On the business front, we witnessed strong traction in our loyalty and rewards segment, supported by
increased festive-season activity. This contributed to revenue growth across both Zaggle and Greenedge.
Additionally, our Dice acquisition began contributing SaaS revenues during the quarter, further
strengtheningourtechnology-ledrevenuestreams.
We havealsomademeaningfulprogress in integratingDice, withapproximately 70%ofcustomercontracts
novated and the balance expected to be completed in Q3. The acquisition has expanded our enterprise
network, bringing marquee relationships such as Hindalco, Trident, Bajel, Aakash Educational Services and
IDFCFirstBankintoourfold.
Building on our expanding network, we continue to focus on driving greater adoption of our co-branded
credit cards and UPI offerings, leveraging our existing salaried user base while maintaining disciplined
customeracquisitioncosts.
On the international front, we have taken an important step towards expanding our presence through the
establishment of Zaggle Holdings Limited in Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM). This provides us with a
platformto expandourpresenceintheregionandcreateopportunities for growthoverthemediumto long
term.
Looking ahead, our focus remains on strengthening the core business, improving cash flow generation,
integrating our acquisitions and scaling AI across our platforms. Our expanding enterprise relationships,
diversified product portfolio and international presence provide a strong foundation for sustainable and
profitablegrowth.”
