Brief profile of Shri Samir Kumar  
 
Shri Samir Kumar , GOI Nominee Director assumed office of Director of the Bank on 
13.05.2026.  
 
Shri Samir Kumar belongs to Indian Economic Service, 1995 batch. He is presently 
working as Economic Adviser in Department of Financial Services, Ministry of 
Finance, handling matters related to Pension Reforms, Debts Recovery Tribunals, 
General Administration & Economic Analysis Cell.  
 
He was earlier posted as Joint Secretary, Department of Drinking Water and 
Sanitation where he was closely involved with implementation of two of the flagship 
programmes of Government of India. i.e Swachh Bharat Mission -Grameen & Jal 
Jeevan Mission.  
 
Starting his career from erstwhile Planning Commission, he has rich experience in 
working in various Government Departments - Department of Commerce, 
Department of Economic Affairs (DEA) & Central Economic Intelligence Bureau 
(CEIB) in Department of Revenue.  
 
Shri Samir Kumar is Government Nominee Director on the Board of Indian Bank & 
CERSAI (Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and Security 
Interest of lndia). CERSAI is responsible for keeping records of all securitisation, 
reconstruction, and security interest transactions & Central KYC (CKYC).  
 
Shri Samir Kumar has done his Graduation in Economics (Hons.) from Calcutta 
University and Post -Graduation from Patna University.
