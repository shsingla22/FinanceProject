PRESS RELEASE 
NTPC Green Energy Limited Inks MoU with Bharat Light and Power  Private Limited  
NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL) and Bharat Light and Power Private Limited  (BLP) 
signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on 19.02.2025, to address the need for pacing 
green energy objectives and the GoI’s efforts towards  carbon neutral economy . 
The MoU was exchanged between Sh. DMR Panda, Chief General Manager and Head of 
Hydrogen, NTPC and Sh. T P Chopra , President &  Chief Executive  Officer (CE O), BLP in the 
presence of other senior officials from NGEL  and BLP on 19th February  2025 at SCOPE 
Complex, New Delhi.  
The M OU is to jointly explore the off -take of green hydrogen and its derivatives from NGEL 
and its affiliates to third parties. Opportunities for selling captured carbon or biogenic carbon 
from NGEL will also be in scope.  NGEL/or through its Affiliates shall set up infrastructure 
required for functioning of the green hydrogen project including RE projects and regular 
operation under B -O-O mode , as a part of it’s  Net Zero commitments.
