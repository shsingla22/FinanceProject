Thomas Cook India & SOTC Travel Curate Holidays Combining Year -End 
Celebrations with Immersive Experiences  
 
• From New Year celebrations in Australia and winter festivities in Europe to premium island escapes  and 
spiritual journeys  in India, the portfolio brings together distinctive ways to welcome the New Year  
• Unique group escorted tours with departures from key metros, alongside customised holidays co- curated 
around individual customer preferences  
 
Mumbai, October  30, 2026:  New Year travel is evolving beyond the traditional midnight countdown, with Indian 
travellers increasingly looking to combine end of year celebrations with distinctive destination experiences. 
From spectacular fireworks and summer adventures in Australia and New Zealand to winter festivities in 
Europe, cultural celebrations across Asia and immersive cruise journeys, travellers are exploring new ways to celebrate the year gone by and welcome the year ahead. Recognizing this growing  trend, Thomas Cook (India) 
Limited, India’s leading omnichannel travel services company, and its Group Company, SOTC Travel, have 
curated a portfolio of  domestic and international holidays spanning Australia and New Zealand, Europe, Asia, 
the Middle East , India and the Arctic, alongside luxury ocean and expedition cruises.  
 
Thomas Cook India and SOTC Travel offer a range of year -end holidays through ready -to-book, air -
inclusive group tours and customised holidays tailored to individual preferences. Spanning domestic and 
international destinations, the portfolio brings together seasonal highlights and distinctive experiences for families, couples, friends and multi -generational groups, with options ranging from New Year celebrations 
and sightseeing to cultural discovery and immersive destination experiences.
 
 
Australia & New Zealand: A summer New Year with immersive experiences  
For travellers seeking a different way to ring in the New Year, Australia and New Zealand offer the appeal of 
summer holidays, scenic landscapes and outdoor adventures. Australia’s iconic cities come alive with spectacular fireworks and waterfront celebrat ions. In Sydney, travellers can enjoy an elevated New Year’s Eve 
experience at Water’s Edge Sydney, combining a spectacular harbour setting with a bespoke New Year’s Eve 
menu, a four -course gourmet dinner, a late- night snack station and a 6.5- hour beverage package featuring 
champagne on arrival, wine, beer, soft drinks and juices. Melbourne offers a lively waterfront celebration in 
South Wharf, with a DJ, a vibrant party atmosphere and views of the midnight fireworks over the Yarra River, 
while the Gold Coast brings the excitement of New Year’s Eve at Surfers Paradise. Combination festive 
departures are also available, offering travellers more ways to explore Australia over the holiday season.  
New Zealand complements the experience with its summer landscapes, outdoor pursuits and scenic escapes, 
making the region an appealing choice for travellers looking to combine year -end celebrations with a longer 
holiday.  
 
Europe: Winter Festivities, Alpine Escapes and the Northern Lights  
Europe offers a distinctive year -end atmosphere, with festive markets, illuminated streets, seasonal gastronomy 
and celebrations across some of its most iconic cities.  Destinations such as  London,  Paris, Vienna, Prague and 
Germany’s historic towns bring together Christmas markets, cultural attractions and winter experiences, while 
Switzerland and Austria offer snow -covered Alpine landscapes, scenic rail journeys, mountain stays and winter 
sports. Greece provides a differ ent perspective, with its historic landmarks, coastal settings and cultural 
experiences adding variety to multi -country European itineraries.  
For travellers seeking a more extraordinary winter escape, the Arctic offers the opportunity to witness the 
Northern Lights across destinations such as Finland, Norway, Sweden and Iceland. Beyond Aurora viewing, experiences include husky and reindeer sledd ing, snowmobile excursions, snowshoeing and stays in distinctive 
accommodation such as glass igloos and Arctic cabins. These journeys combine seasonal landscapes with immersive experiences, making them an appealing option for couples, families and travellers seeking something beyond conventional sightseeing.  
                                                                                                                     
 
Asia and Beyond : Cultural Discoveries and Distinctive Celebrations  
Across Asia, year -end travel brings together cultural traditions, vibrant city experiences, local cuisine and 
opportunities to explore destinations at a more immersive pace. Japan offers a distinctive New Year experience, with traditional temple visits and Hatsumode, the first shrine or temple visit of the year, alongside its 
contemporary city attractions. Across Thailand, Malaysia,  Indonesia,  Singapore and Vietnam, New Year 
celebrations take on a more festive and social character, with gala dinners, entertainment and city celebrations 
forming part of the holiday experience. Multi -country combinations such as Singapore- Malaysia and Vietnam -
Bali further allow travellers to turn the year -end break into a longer holiday.  Dubai and Abu Dhabi offer another 
popular combination of festive celebrations, dining, entertainment and spectacular city experiences, while Azerbaijan and Georgia bring together winter landscapes, heritage, local cuisine and cultural exploration  along 
with New year celebrations.  
 Domestic Holidays: Premium Island Escapes, Scenic Getaways and Spiritual Experiences  
For travellers looking to celebrate the year -end closer to home, India offers a diverse range of holiday 
experiences . Lakshadweep is emerging as an attractive choice for travellers seeking a premium island holiday 
without travelling overseas. To make the destination more accessible, Thomas Cook India and SOTC Travel 
have introduced direct charter flights from Bengaluru to Lakshadweep for the year -end season, offering 
travellers a convenient and seamless way to reach the island destination.  Bhutan is another highlight, with 
special charter departures from Mumbai offering convenient access to the Himalayan kingdom and its 
monasteries, mountain scenery and distinctive cultural experiences. Other popular destinations include  the 
Andamans, Kerala, Rajasthan and Sri Lanka, o ffering a choice of island holidays, backwater experiences, 
heritage trails and scenic escapes.  Spiritual tourism is also gaining momentum, with travellers seeking 
meaningful, faith- led experiences across India to end the year. Pilgrimage circuits and temple towns such as 
Char Dham, Mahakaleshwar Temple in Ujjain, Tirupati and Vaishno Devi continue to attract devotees looking 
to combine travel with prayer, reflection and spiritual discovery.  
 
Celebration at sea  
Cruises offer an alternative way to combine New Year celebrations with destination exploration, bringing 
accommodation, dining, entertainment and multiple destinations together within one journey. Onboard gala 
dinners, live entertainment, festive celebrati ons and ocean views create a memorable setting for welcoming the 
New Year, while port calls offer opportunities to explore destinations along the route.  From Southeast Asian 
and Middle Eastern itineraries to Mediterranean voyages, Australia– New Zealand sailings and premium 
expedition cruises across the Arctic and Antarctica, travellers can choose from a variety of cruise experiences suited to different interests.  
 
Mr. Rajeev Kale, President & Country Head –  Holidays, MICE, Visa, Thomas Cook (India) Limited , said,  
“New Year travel is increasingly about combining the excitement of celebration with the opportunity to discover a destination and its experiences. Indian travellers are looking to make the occasion memorable through 
distinctive experiences, whether it is w atching the New Year fireworks over Sydney Harbour, exploring Europe’s 
festive  markets and Alpine landscapes, experiencing multiple destinations on a cruise or c hoosing a premium 
island escape like Lakshadweep or a spiritual journey in India. This calls for holiday curation that brings together 
the right destinations, experiences and travel styles to suit different preferences. Our year -end portfolio reflects 
this diversity, offering travellers different ways to welcome the New Year, from vibrant celebrations to immersive 
cultural and experiential journeys.”  
 
Mr. S.D. Nandakumar, President & Country Head –  Holidays & Corporate Tours, SOTC Travel , said,  “The 
year-end holiday season offers travellers a chance to celebrate the occasion in ways that matter to them, 
whether it is spending quality time with family and friends or exploring somewhere new. While New Year 
celebrations remain an important draw, travellers are also looking to make the most of their trips through local 
traditions, seasonal attractions, outdoor adventures, unique stays and cruise journeys. Through our group tours 
and customised holidays, we bring together these varied experiences, offering travellers a choice of itineraries to suit their interests, whether they are looking for a festive city break, an experience- led destination getaway 
or a multi -destination journey.”
