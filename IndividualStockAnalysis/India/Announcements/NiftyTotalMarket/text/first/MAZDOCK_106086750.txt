Mazdock 10K Challenge 2024 - Second Edition: #RUNTHELEGACY 
MDL Celebrates 250 Years of Excellence 
Mumbai, December 15, 2024 - In celebration of its 250th anniversary, Mazagon Dock 
Shipbuilders Ltd. (MDL) proudly hosted the second edition of the Mazdock Mumbai 10K 
Challenge today, Sunday, December 15, 2024. The event energized the city and brought 
together participants from all walks of life, further cementing MDL’s decp-rooted legacy in the 
maritime and shipbuilding sectors. This milestone not only highlights MDL’s 250 years of 
excellence in shipbuilding but also showcases the company’s ongoing commitment to 
innovation, quality, and national development. 
The event was flagged off today at Azad Maidan by sports icons Ms. Monalisa Baruah Mehta, 
Ms. Diana Edulji, Ms. Lalita Babar, and Mr. Aman Chaudhary in presence of Mr. Sanjeev 
Singhal (CMD), Mr. Biju George (Director, Shipbuilding), Cdr. Vasudev Puranik (IN Retd.), 
Mr. Hari Narayan Jangid (CVO). 
Since its inception in 1774, Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Ltd. (MDL) has been a proud symbol 
of national strength, constructing warships, submarines, and commercial vessels that safeguard 
our shores and drive our economy. 
On the occasion Mr. Sanjeev Singhal, Chairman and Managing Director, MDL stated that this 
event holds special significance as MDL celebrates its remarkable 250-year journey and its 
distinction as the first shipyard to earn the prestigious Navratna status. CMD further said that 
the purpose of the marathon is to promote the culture of health & fitness in the society and also 
create awareness about Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Ltd and its stellar contributions in Nation 
building. 
The second edition of the Mazdock Marathon saw an impressive turnout, with over 6,000 
participants across two race categories — the 10K Challenge and the 3K Fun Run. To mark this 
exceptional event, Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Ltd. (MDL) offered substantial prize money 
for the top finishers in the 10K category, across six age groups for both male and female 
participants. A total of Rs. 5.5 lakhs was awarded in prize money, highlighting MDL’s 
commitment to promoting fitness and achievement. In addition, a special category was 
introduced to motivate MDL employees, with separate awards for both male and female 
employees, further encouraging participation and team spirit within the organization.
