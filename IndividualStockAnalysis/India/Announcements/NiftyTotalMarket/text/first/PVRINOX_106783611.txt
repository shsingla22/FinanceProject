PVR INOX ELEVATES GURUGRAM ’S ENTERTAINMENT EXPERIENCE 
WITH A NEW CINEMA AT DLF SUMMIT PLAZA  
~ Located on Golf Course Road, the new destination combines premium comfort, 
immersive design and the distinctive Sapphire seating experience. ~  
National, September 17,  2026:  PVR INOX Limited, India ’s largest and most premium multiplex 
chain, today announced the launch of its new three -screen multiplex at DLF Summit Plaza, 
Gurugram. Located on the prestigious Golf Course Road corridor, the new destination marks the 
company ’s latest expansion in one of India ’s most cosmopolitan urban markets.  
Gurugram has evolved into a dynamic city where global workplaces, premium residences, 
contemporary dining, retail and entertainment come together. Within this ecosystem , DLF Summit 
Plaza is a well -connected destination for shopping, dining and leisure, surrounded by premium 
residences, corporate offices and hospitality destinations. With easy access to Golf Course Road, 
the Rapid Metro and key business districts, the new  PVR INOX multiplex further adds to DLF 
Summit Plaza ’s appeal as a lifestyle and entertainment address.  
The multiplex features three auditoriums with 352 premium Sapphire seats, finished in luxurious 
leatherette upholstery. The seating offers 650 mm centre -to-centre spacing and a 1,500 mm back -
to-back seat pitch, providing generous personal space and enhanced comfort.  The three -screen 
cinema is equipped with 4K digital laser projection, Dolby 7.1 surround sound and Next -Gen 3D, 
delivering exceptional visual  clarity, immersive audio and a richly detailed, lifelike cinematic 
experience across all auditoriums.  
The auditoriums feature rich monochromatic palettes, giving each screen a distinct identity while 
maintaining a cohesive design language. Softly curved w all panelling, integrated acoustic 
detailing and vertical illuminated feature panels create depth, rhythm and visual distinction.  
A defining feature is the dramatic double -height foyer, designed as a hospitality -inspired social 
space rather than a conventi onal cinema lobby. Sculptural suspended lighting, café -style seating 
and dedicated social zones create an inviting setting to relax, connect and extend the visit beyond 
the screening.  
Hanging greenery and biophilic elements soften the environment, while wa rm ambient lighting 
adds comfort and intimacy. A striking chrome -plated vintage projector installation anchors the 
arrival experience as a signature visual landmark. Metallic accents, textured finishes and warm 
tones complete the refined material palette, bringing together cinema, hospitality and 
contemporary lifestyle in one cohesive setting.  
Commenting on the launch,  Mr. Ajay Kumar Bijli, Managing Director, PVR INOX Limited,  said, 
“Gurugram is a city that embodies India ’s ambition, energy and cosmopolitan way of life. The 
launch of our new multiplex at DLF Summit Plaza reflects our commitment to creating elevated 
cinema experiences in destinations that are central to how modern audi ences live, work and 
socialise. Located in the heart of DLF5 and Golf Course Road, this property brings together 
 
comfort, design, technology and hospitality to create a distinctive new entertainment destination 
for Gurugram. ” 
Mr. Sanjeev Kumar , Exec utive Director, PVR INOX Limited,  added , As India ’s entertainment 
landscape continues to evolve, audiences are seeking spaces that offer more than a conventional 
visit, they are looking for moments of connection, discovery and enjoyment. Our new multiplex at 
DLF Summit Plaza has been designed as a complete leisure destination, bringing together 
thoughtful architecture, premium comfort and a welcoming social environment. With its distinctive 
foyer, immersive auditoriums and carefully curated details, the pro perty reflects our vision of 
making every PVR INOX experience meaningful, memorable and relevant to the way people live 
today. ” 
The launch further reinforces PVR INOX ’s commitment to expanding access to elevated theatrical 
experiences and creating destinat ion-led cinemas that enrich the evolving leisure and 
entertainment landscape across India.
