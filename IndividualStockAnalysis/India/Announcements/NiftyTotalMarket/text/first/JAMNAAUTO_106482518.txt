Company/Depositories, through electTonic means only and has not dispatched physical
copies of the Notice to any member.

requirements of the relevant provisions of the Act and the Rules made thereunder
including MCA circulars; and the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
Requirements) Regujations, 2015, relating to postal ballot and voting by electronic means.
