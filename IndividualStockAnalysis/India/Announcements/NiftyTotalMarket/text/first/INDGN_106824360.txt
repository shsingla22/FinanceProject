Indegene Receives Frost & Sullivan’s 2026 Global Enabling Technology 
Leadership Recognition for AI -Powered Pharma Commercialization  
 
Indegene is recognized for combining domain -led AI, knowledge orchestration, and integrated 
execution to transform pharmaceutical commercialization workflows.  
 
SAN ANTONIO , TX — 22nd October 2026 — Frost & Sullivan  is pleased to announce that Indegene  has 
received the 2026 Global Enabling Technology Leadership Recognition in pharma AI -powered 
commercialization services for its outstanding achievements in AI -powered commercialization, workflow 
transformation, and customer value creation. Th e recognition highlights Indegene’s leadership in helping 
pharmaceutical organizations modernize complex commercialization processes through domain expertise, 
technology, data, and execution support.  
 
Frost & Sullivan evaluates companies through a rigorous benchmarking process across two core 
dimensions: strategy effectiveness and strategy execution. Indegene excelled in both, demonstrating its 
ability to align its AI strategy with the evolving needs of  the pharmaceutical industry while executing at scale 
across commercial, medical, regulatory, safety, analytics, content, and field engagement workflows.  
 
“Indegene  is redefining AI -powered pharmaceutical commercialization by bringing together model -agnostic 
technology, deep life sciences expertise, knowledge orchestration, and process reengineering. Its approach 
moves beyond isolated automation to create governed, c onnected workflows that can deliver greater speed, 
consistency, and reliability across the commercialization value chain,” said Surbhi Gupta, Industry Principal 
at Frost & Sullivan.  
 
Guided by a long -term growth strategy focused on integrating life sciences expertise, technology, data, 
consulting, and execution, Indegene has demonstrated its ability to adapt to a rapidly evolving AI landscape. 
Its Pharma Operating System (OS) connects business decisions, workflow execution, and intelligence 
across the enterprise, helping pharmaceutical organizations replace fragmented processes with integrated 
and adaptive operating models.  
 
Innovation remains central to Indegene’s  approach. Its pharma AI -powered commercialization services 
combine generative AI, data, in -house technology platforms, workflow integration, consulting, and 
execution support to help clients plan, create, review, deploy, and optimize commercial and medical 
content. Its Cortex life sciences -native reasoning engine enables s ubject matter experts to codify 
specialized knowledge, create validated knowledge graphs, and guide AI -enabled workflows across 
applications including regulatory authoring, medical writing, creative adaptation, and medical -legal -
regulatory review.  
 
“Frost & Sullivan’s recognition reinforces our belief that the next wave of value creation will come from 
connecting intelligence, decisions, and execution across the enterprise. AI's true potential is realized when 
it is embedded into how organizations ope rate, not simply how they analyze information. At Indegene, we 
help life sciences companies turn knowledge into action, enabling smarter decisions, faster execution, and 
measurable business outcomes at  scale. Our goal is to help clients build operating advantages that 
strengthen over time and create lasting differentiation in an AI -driven market .” said Vivek Ghai, EVP, 
Enterprise Commercial Solutions at Indegene.  
 
 
Indegene’s  commitment to customer experience strengthens its position in the market. By connecting 
strategy, data, content, execution, and measurable outcomes, the company helps pharmaceutical 
organizations reduce fragmented handoffs and improve commercialization ef ficiency. Its platform -agnostic 
approach allows customers to work in existing technology environments, preferred models, cloud 
relationships, security requirements, and governance structures, reducing adoption friction while protecting 
existing technology investments.  
 
Its data -driven commercialization capabilities support precision marketing, next -best-action journeys, 
healthcare professional targeting, field  force optimization, channel selection, content frequency planning, 
and marketing spend optimization. By connecting digital engagement, field interaction, customer 
relationship management, claims, affinity, and prescribing signals, Indegene enables more int elligent 
omnichannel execution and more measurable commercial decision -making.  
 
Frost & Sullivan commends Indegene for establishing a standard in competitive strategy, technology -
enabled execution, and market responsiveness. The company’s domain -led approach to AI, knowledge 
orchestration capabilities, and focus on transparent customer value are shap ing the future of 
pharmaceutical commercialization and enabling organizations to scale AI transformation with greater 
confidence and accountability.  
 
Each year, Frost & Sullivan presents the Enabling Technology Leadership Recognition to a company that 
demonstrates outstanding strategy development and implementation, resulting in measurable 
improvements in customer value, competitive positioning, and mar ket impact. The recognition highlights 
forward -thinking organizations that are reshaping their industries through innovation and growth excellence.
