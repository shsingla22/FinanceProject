EXPLANATORY STATEMENT 
(Pursuant to the provisions of Section 102 of the Companies Act, 2013) 
 
The following Statement sets out all material facts relating to the business mentioned under Item No. 1 
mentioned in the accompanying Postal Ballot Notice.  
 
Item No. 1  
 
To approve revision in professional fee payable to Dr. Varun Shetty as a Consultant Surgeon, 
an office or place of profit under the Companies Act, 2013. 
  
The shareholders of the Company at the Annual General Meeting of the Company held on 
August 3, 2018 had approved the proposal to engage Dr. Varun Shetty as Consultant Surgeon being 
an office or place of profit under Section 188 of the Companies Act, 2013, as he is related to Dr. Devi 
Prasad Shetty, Chairman and Whole-time Director, Mr. Viren Prasad Shetty, Executive Vice-Chairman 
and Dr. Anesh Shetty, Non-Executive Director. Presently, he is paid a professional fee of  
Rs. 3,60,00,000/- (Rupees Three Crore and Sixty Lakhs Only) per annum for his services as a 
Consultant Surgeon in the Cardiac specialty. 
 
The Board of Directors at their meeting held on May 22, 2026, on the recommendation of Audit, Risk 
and Compliance Committee and Nomination and Remuneration Committee of the Company, has 
approved revision in the professional fee payable to Dr. Varun Shetty as a Consultant Surgeon with a 
annual Remuneration limit of Rs.4,80,00,000/- (Rupees Four Crores Eighty Lakhs Only) per annum 
including quaternary care program development incentive, which is commensurate with his work 
contributions in the area of Cardiac Surgery, experience and as per prevalent industry standards.   
Dr. Varun Shetty leads very high-end tertiary and quaternary cardiac surgical work including Aortic 
Surgery, Heart and Lung Transplant, Ventricular Assist Device (VAD) & Artificial Hearts, CTEPH 
Surgeries at Narayana Institute of Cardiac Sciences, Bengaluru.  
 
In view of Dr. Varun Shetty's significant contribution to the establishment, expansion, and advancement 
of these specialised clinical programmes, it is proposed to revise the annual remuneration ceiling from 
INR 3.60 crore to INR 4.80 crore. The proposed revision represents only an enhancement of the 
maximum permissible payout and does not constitute an assured annual remuneration. Any payout 
under the incentive framework shall remain contingent upon professional fees for the clinical program 
and achievement of defined programme objectives and performance parameters. 
 
Dr. Varun Shetty being a relative of Dr. Devi Prasad Shetty, Chairman and Whole-time Director,  
Mr. Viren Prasad Shetty, Executive Vice-Chairman and Dr. Anesh Shetty, Non-Executive Director of 
the Company is deemed to be holding an office or place of profit in the Company in terms of Section 
188 (1)(f) of the Companies Act, 2013.  
 
Dr. Varun Shetty graduated with MBBS in 2010 and later finished his DNB in Cardiothoracic Surgery 
from Narayana Institute of Cardiac Sciences in 2016. He later did his FRCS In Cardiothoracic Surgery 
from The Royal College of Surgeons, Edinburgh, UK, in 2018. He is now a Senior Consultant in the 
department of Cardiac surgery at Narayana Institute of Cardiac Sciences, Bengaluru, a unit of the 
Company.
