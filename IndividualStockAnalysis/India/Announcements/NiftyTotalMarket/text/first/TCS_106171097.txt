TCS Ex tends  Partnership with Coop Danmark  to Enhance 
Operations & Customer Shopping Experience  
Tata Consultancy Services  will provide end -to-end support to the retailer’s core system on SAP S/4HANA , 
integrate AI and automation for improved efficiency and enable smooth shopping experiences  
COPENHAGEN | MUMBAI, March  12, 202 5: Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS), a global 
leader in IT services, consulting, and business solutions,  has extended its partnership with Coop Danmark to 
support the core business system of Denmark’s leading consumer goods retailer. By man aging Coop’s 
enterprise resource planning (ERP) platform  on SAP S/4HANA, TCS will enable efficient operations for the 
retailer and consistent shopping experiences for customers across 900 -plus stores.  
TCS has been supporting Coop Danmark’s digital transformation journey and adoption of the SAP S/4HANA 
enterprise resource planning platform for the past five years. Through this extended engagement , TCS will 
continue supporting Coop’s ERP to further stabilize, optimize and enhance its central digital core system. To 
remain competitive amidst macroeconomic volatilities and to attract ever more price -conscious shoppers, 
this collaboration aims to leverage  advanced technology  to simplify operations and enhance customer 
experience . 
Lars Ole  Dybdal , Chief Technology Officer, Coop Danmark , commented: “ We are building the Coop of the 
future and using  the latest technology  to offer a shopping experience  customers will keep coming back for. 
To realize our vision, our digital transformation must continue .  We are delighted to extend our strategic 
partnership with TCS to accelerate our journey, innovate, and create value for our two million member s.” 
One of the largest implementations of its kind in the Nordics , the move to SAP S/4HANA has standardized 
business processes throughout Coop’s retail operations, from in -store operations or supply chain 
management.  Under the extended partnership, the aim now is to tap the power of machine learning, artificial 
intelligence and other technologies while continu ing to scale up automation and innovation initiatives along 
with increased efficiency. This partnership will also enhance day-to-day operations and drive faster time to 
market, while reducing the overall operating costs. TCS and Coop will work together to build new capabilities 
required to simplify business operations to provide a better, and more personalized shopping experience.  
Abhijit Niyogi, Business Head, Retail – UK, EMEA & India, TCS, said, “We are proud to extend our partnership 
with Coop Danmark and support the critical core function of their retail business, leveraging our contextual 
knowledge and deep domain expertise. We look forward to integrating AI and automation in Coop’s core 
operations, supporting its goal to create a simpler, more sustainabl e business that meets the needs of every 
shopper.”    
TCS partners with the top 10 global retailers and many others across Europe and around the world to build 
profitable and sustainable businesses and offer immersive, unified, and hyper -personalized omnichannel 
experiences for their customers. TCS’ deep indu stry expertise, backed by decades of experience and relevant 
partnerships in retail, spans across store operations, merchandising, supply chain, marketing, pricing 
strategies, and business model reinvention. TCS’ extensive portfolio of retail platforms and  offerings includes 
TCS OmniStore ™, an AI -powered, unified composable commerce platform, and TCS Optumera ™, an AI -
powered strategic intelligence platform that enables retailers to make optimized merchandising decisions 
across the value chain.  
Vikram Sharma, Country Head, TCS Denmark, said , "Coop Denmark is creating the next generation retail 
company . Technology is key to unlocking potential and provide consumers with a smoother shopping 
experience. We are pleased that Coop continues to trust us to help them with their digital transformation .”
