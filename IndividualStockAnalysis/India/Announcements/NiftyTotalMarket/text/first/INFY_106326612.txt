Sub: Update on McCamish Cyber Incident 
 
In continuation to our statement  dated March 14, 2025, we would like to update that on August 5, 2025 , Infosys 
McCamish Systems (“ IMS”), a subsidiary of Infosys BPM limited (a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys limited) and 
the State of Oregon (United States of America) Attorney’s  General Office entered into an Assurance of Voluntary 
Compliance (“ AVC ”). 
 The Oregon Attorney’s General Office expressed concerns that IMS may have violated the Oregon Consumer Information Protection Act, and the Oregon Unlawful Trade Practices Act with respect to a 2023 data breach.  
  
IMS has entered into an Assurance of Voluntary Compliance  to resolve this matter without any further action. The 
AVC specifically acknowledges that this is not an admission  or finding that IMS violated the Acts as listed above, 
and that IMS does not admit any of the fa ctual assertions contained in the AVC.
