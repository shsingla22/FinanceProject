2. Update on closure  of a wholly owned subsidiary  
 
Further to our intimation dated 30th March 2026 regarding the decision of the Board of CUMI 
International Limited to initiate closure of CUMI Awuko Abrasives GmbH (CAAG), a wholly 
owned step down subsidiary of the Company,  we wish to update that the closure process is 
progressing in accordance with applicable German laws. As part of the closure process, CAAG 
entered into agreements in October 2026 for the sale of all its property, plant and equipment 
and inventory for an agg regate consideration of Rs. 534 lakhs. CAAG has also entered into a 
reconciliation of interest and social plan with all the employees in accordance with applicable 
German labour law requirements. The financial impact arising from these actions has been 
disclosed in the notes to the financial statements for the period ended 30th September 2026 
(enclosed). With the completion of the above steps, CAAG is now proceeding with the final 
liquidation/de -registration in accordance with the applicable  per German laws .
