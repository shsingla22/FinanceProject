Airtel Becomes Kerala's leading telecom operator with the most network sites in 
the state  
  
· Deploys an additional 2500 sites in record time; densifies its network coverage across all 14 districts  
· Opensignal report confirms Airtel as Kerala’s most awarded telecom service provider  
  
Kochi, March 20, 2025:  Bharti Airtel, one of India’s largest telecom service providers, has made significant 
investments in network expansion in Kerala. The company has deployed close to 2500 sites in the last two 
years, taking the total site count to over 11,000 in the state, the most by any telecom operator. 
  
This targeted densification of network has been strategically conducted to cover both rural and urban 
areas that include places like Malappuram, Palakkad, Kasaragod, Kottayam, Pathanamthitta, Kollam, 
Idukki, Alleppey, Wayanad, Palakkad, Trivandrum, Thrissur, Ernakulam, Kozhikode, Kannur, etc.  
  
Gokul J, COO – Kerala, Bharti Airtel, said, “Kerala remains a critical market for Airtel, and we are 
committed to offering our customers the best network experience. In the last two years, we have made 
significant investments in the state in network densification across all the 14 districts. This has resulted in 
delivering a ubiquitous voice and data experience for our customers, no matter where they are in the state. 
We remain committed to Kerala and will continue to invest in technologies that will help elevate service 
experience for our customers.”  
  
The investment made by Airtel has resulted in a significant augmentation of the network experience for 
customers with improved browsing speeds, enhanced voice quality and seamless coverage throughout 
Kerala including the state highways, airports, ports, railway stations, high footfall areas like beaches, 
backwaters and other tourist destinations. This has been endorsed by Opensignal, which has awarded 
Airtel with most number of awards in its recent report. Amongst operators, Airtel has emerged as the top 
telco across several categories that included: 
  
 Video Experience : Airtel is the number one network in Kerala for seamless 5G video streaming. 
Airtel users enjoy a best-in-class video experience with no interruptions or buffering. 
 Live Video Experience : Airtel’s 5G network in Kerala offers the best live video streaming with 
minimal delays. Whether it is sports or music concerts, users enjoy real-time content without lag, 
making Airtel number one for uninterrupted live experiences. 
 Games Experience : Airtel’s low latency network makes it the go-to option for gaming enthusiasts. 
The network supports uninterrupted multiplayer experiences, offering the best possible gaming 
experience across all genres. 
 Upload Speed : When it comes to uploading content, whether it is videos, documents or live 
streaming, Airtel leads the pack with the fastest upload speeds, enhancing the user experience 
for both creators and consumers. 
  
Airtel was also the first to introduce the accessibility of its ultra-fast 5G service connectivity at Kochi's 
water metro stations. The company has now enhanced its 5G coverage to encompass the dramatic cliffs 
of Varkala, known for their pristine beaches and serene landscapes. The company's robust infrastructure 
also extends to the picturesque hill stations of Munnar, nestled amidst lush tea plantations and rolling 
hills. By strategically deploying its cutting-edge technology, Airtel has ensured that tourists and locals alike 
can stay connected with exceptional reliability and speed.
