Sub: Announcement under Regulation 30 of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations & Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 - 5 Star Rated Mines Award  
 
This is to inform you that the Company has been awarded the 5 Star Rated Mines Award,  by the 
Ministry of Mines, Government of India for the financial year 2022 -23, for its Mining Lease No.2678 
under Sustainable Development Framework.  
 
Assessment of Mining Leases under Sustainable Development Framework (SDF) was introduced by 
the Indian Bureau of Mines (IBM), Ministry of Coal & Mines, Government of India from the 
financial year 2014 -15. SDF evaluates the Mining Leases on the following criteria:  
 
(a) Carrying out Scientific and Systematic Mining  
(b) Progress on Progressive and Final Mine Closure  
(c) Addressing resettlement and rehabilitation, issues, local community engagements and welfare 
programmes  
(d) Implementation of green energy source, cleanliness drive and mining software  
(e) Reporting processes as per international standards . 
 
1,256 Mining Leases across the country were assessed for the financial year 2022 -23 and 68 Mining 
Leases secured 5 Star Rating.  
 
G. Kishan Reddy - Hon’ble Union Minister for Coal & Mines and Satish Chandra Dubey - Hon’ble 
Minister of State for Coal & Mines have presented 5 Star Awards to all the 68 Mining Leases, at a 
Felicitation Programme organised by IBM on 7 August 2024 at Dr. Ambedkar International Centre, 
New Delhi.  Mohammed Abdul Saleem - Non-Executive Director and Manjunath Prabhu - Chief 
Operating Officer of the Company have received the 5 Star Award on behalf of the Company.
