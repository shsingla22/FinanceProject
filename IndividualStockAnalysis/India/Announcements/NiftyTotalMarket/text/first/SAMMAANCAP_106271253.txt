Brief profile of Mr. Naveen Uppal is as under:

industry. He has been associated with the Company for over 18 years and presently designated as Chief Risk Officer.

Company, he was associated with ICICI Bank as its Zonal Operations Head. In his previous assignments at ICICI Bank 
he has designed various systems to achieve optimum efficiency. He has handled various projects like ‘LEAN’ & ‘Project

Accountant from I.C.W.A. and holds Post Graduate Diploma from the UCLA Anderson School of Management, USA.
