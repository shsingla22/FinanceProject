Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Further to our intimation dated September 04, 2026, and as directed by the Hon'ble National Company 
Law Tribunal, Mumbai Bench  ("Hon'ble NCLT ") vide its Order dated August 06, 2026, we inform you 
that separate meetings of the Secured Creditors (including Secured Debenture Holders) and the Equity 
Shareholders of the Company (" Meetings "), to consider and approve the scheme of merger by 
absorption of Roselabs Finance Limited and National  Standard (India) Limited with Lodha Developers 
Limited and their respective shareholders and creditors (" Scheme "), were held on October 09, 2026 
through Video Conferencing / Other Audio -Visual Means. The Meetings commenced at 12:00 noon 
(IST) and 12:45 p.m. (IST) and concluded at 12:25 p.m. (IST) and 1:12 p.m. (IST) respectively.  
 
We enclose the following:  
1. Summary of proceedings of the Meeting of Secured Creditors (including Secured Debenture 
Holders), under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations ( Annexure A ); 
2. Summary of proceedings of the Meeting of Equity Shareholders, under Regulation 30 of the SEBI 
Listing Regulations ( Annexure B ); 
3. Voting results of the Meetings, under Regulation 44(3) of the SEBI Listing Regulations read with 
relevant SEBI Master Circular ( Annexures C1 and C2 ); and  
4. Scrutinizer's Reports dated October 09, 2026 ( Annexures D1 and D2 ). 
 
The resolution set out in the Hon'ble NCLT Convened Meeting Notice has been duly passed, through 
e-voting, with the requisite majority by both the Secured Creditors (including Secured Debenture 
Holders) and the Equity Shareholders  of the Company . 
 
Further, the Scheme has been approved by the Equity Shareholders (including the Public 
Shareholders) of Roselabs Finance Limited and National Standard (India) Limited with the requisite 
majority. Accordingly, all the requisite approvals for the Scheme have  been obtained from the 
shareholders and creditors of the companies concerned. The Scheme is subject to the sanction of the 
Hon’ble NCLT and such other approvals as may be required.   
 
The above information is also available on the Company’s website at www.lodhagroup.com .

 Annexure A 
 
SUMMARY OF THE PROCEEDINGS OF THE MEETING OF THE SECURED CREDITORS 
(INCLUDING SECURED DEBENTURE HOLDERS)  OF LODHA DEVELOPERS LIMITED 
(“TRANSFEREE  COMPANY” OR “THE COMPANY”) HELD AS PER THE DIRECTIONS ISSUED 
BY THE HON’BLE NATIONAL COMPANY LAW TRIBUNAL, MUMBAI BENCH (“HON’BLE NCLT”) 
VIDE ITS ORDER DATED AUGUST 06, 2026 (‘ORDER’)  
 
The meeting of the Secured Creditors (including Secured Debenture Holders)  of the Company 
convened pursuant to the Order of Hon’ble NCLT Mumbai was held on Friday, October 09, 2026 at 
12:00 Noon  (IST) through Video Conferencing (‘VC’) / Other Audio -Visual Means (‘OAVM’).  
 
As set out in the Notice, the Hon’ble NCLT had appointed Mr. Satya Ranjan Prasad, Retired Member 
(Technical), NCLT to be the Chairperson for the Meeting (the “Chairperson”). Accordingly, Mr. Satya 
Ranjan Prasad chaired the Meeting.  
 
The requisite quorum being present, the Chairman called the meeting to order.  
 
Mr. Sushil Kumar Modi, Whole -time Director , Mr. Sanjay Chauhan, Chief Financial Officer and Ms. 
Sanjyot Rangnekar, Company Secretary and Compliance Officer of the Company, were present at the 
meeting.  
 
The representatives of the Statutory Auditors, Secretarial Auditors and Scrutinizer were also present at 
the meeting.  
 
The Chairperson thereafter addressed the Secured Creditors (including Secured Debenture Holders) . 
 
The remote e -voting period, before the date of Meeting, commenced on Tuesday, October 06, 2026 at 
09:00 A.M. (IST) and ended on Thursday, October 08, 2026 at 05:00 P.M. (IST).  
 
In terms of the Notice, the following item of business was transacted at the Meeting:  
 
Sr. 
No. Details of the Agenda  Resolution Type  
1. To approve Scheme of Merger by Absorption of Roselabs Finance 
Limited (First Transferor Company) and National Standard (India) 
Limited (Second Transferor Company) with Lodha Developers 
Limited (Transferee Company) and their respective shareholders and 
creditors.  Special Resolution  
 
The Secured Creditors (including Secured Debenture Holders)  were given an opportunity to speak at 
the Meeting by registering themselves as the speaker as per the procedure detailed in the Notice.  
However, no Secured Creditors (including Secured Debenture Holders)  registered themselves as a 
speaker.  
 
As set out in the Notice, Ms. Dipika Shetty, Practicing Company Secretary (Membership No. A62569 
and CoP No. 24561) had been appointed as the Scrutinizer to scrutinize the voting through electronic 
means (i.e., remote e -voting and voting at the Meeting by using electronic system) (“ e-voting ”). 
 
The Chairperson informed the Secured Creditors (including Secured Debenture Holders)  that the e -
voting results along with the Scrutinizer’s Report would be communicated to the Stock Exchanges 
within two working days from the conclusion of the Meeting and would also be placed on the websites 
of the Company, NSDL, and the stock exchange s i.e., BSE Limited  and National Stock Exchange of 
India Limited . 
 
The Company had provided remote e -voting facility to its Secured Creditors (including Secured 
Debenture Holders)  to cast votes electronically on the business set out in the Notice of the Meeting 
from Tuesday, October 06, 2026 at 09:00 A.M. (IST) to Thursday, October 08, 2026 at 05:00 P.M. 
(IST).  
 
The Secured  Creditors  (including  Secured  Debenture  Holders)  who  did not cast  their  votes  through 
remote e-Voting, were also permitted to cast their votes during the course of Meeting through e-voting 
facility at the Meeting provided by NSDL as detailed in the Notice. 
The Chairman, thereafter, thanked the  Secured Creditors (including  Secured Debenture Holders) for 
attending  and participating  at the  Meeting  and requested  the Secured  Creditors  (including  Secured 
Debenture Holders) who had not voted earlier, to complete e-voting in the next 15 minutes. 
Thereafter, the Meeting concluded at 12:25 P.M. (IST) after being open for 15 minutes for e-voting to 
be completed by the Secured Creditors (including Secured Debenture Holders). 
This is for your information and records.
