Gabriel India  Limited partners with South Korea’s HL Klemove for Autonomous 
Driving  including ADAS and Automotive Electronic s as it  accelerates  its 
transformation into a diversified mobility solution conglomerate.  
Pune , August  26, 2026: Gabriel India  Limited (GIL) , the NSE listed flagship company of the $2.2+ billion 
auto components manufacturer ANAND Group, has entered into a strategic joint venture with South 
Korea -based  HL Klemove , under which Gabriel India will become a strategic shareholder in  HL Klemove 
India Private Limited . The partnership marks a significant  step in Gabriel India's strategy to expand  its 
presence across high -growth automotive segments  and participate in the growing demand for 
Autonomous Driving Solutions including Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS), and Automotive 
Electronic  Products  through this joint venture . 
As India's automotive industry advances toward smarter and safer mobility, Autonomous Driving and 
Automotive Electronics technologies are becoming increasingly important. HL Klemove is among st a 
select group of companies  globally  with established  development and manufacturing capabilities in 
autonomous driving technologies  including ADAS and automotive electronic technologies . With this 
joint venture company, Gabriel India will participate in this high -growth segment,  enabl ing deeper 
engage ment with  automakers and broaden ing its presence in an evolving mobility landscape.  
Commenting on this,  Mr. Jaisal Singh, Vice Chairman of ANAND who spearheads the group’s M&A , 
said, “As a key growth engine of the ANAND Group, Gabriel India is focused on building scale , 
enhancing competitiveness , and broadening its presence across high -growth automotive segments . 
Our latest joint venture with HL Klemove represents a strategic step forward in this endeavou r.” 
Mrs. Anjali Singh, Executive Chairperson of ANAND  Group and Gabriel India Limited , said  “Bolstering  
our position across critical automotive systems while expanding our participation  in future -oriented  
mobility and automotive technology segments , this new JV for Autonomous Driving and Automotive 
Electronics marks an important inflection point, enabling Gabriel India to further diversify its portfolio 
and strengthen its participation in emerging mobility segments .”  
Mr. Mahendra K Goyal, Group Chief Executive Officer of ANAND  and Managing Director of Gabriel 
India Limited , said  “Beyond unlocking new opportunities for growth, this collaboration will foster 
deeper OEM engagement , expand our participation in future mobility solutions  and create enduring 
value for all stakeholders. ” 
To form the  joint venture , Gabriel India  Limited  will acquire a 30 per cent less one share stake in HL 
Klemove India for US$98.44 million  (approximately ₹9 35 crore) . 
Gabriel India  Limited will, through  HL Klemove India , develop | manufacture | and sell | a range of 
advanced mobility solutions, including radar, front cameras, lidar, automated driving and parking 
control units , with  embedded ADAS software, as well as automotive electronics products such as brake 
ECUs, steering ECUs, chassis control units and torque sensors.  
 
 
 
                                                                                                  
 
Building a Comprehensive Mobility Platform  
Over the last few years, Gabriel India  Limited  has been steadily diversifying its portfolio through 
strategic joint ventures in high -growth segments such as sunroofs, lubricants, EV fluids and precision 
fasteners. The partnership with HL Klemove builds on this momentum, broadening Gabriel India's 
parti cipation in the rapidly evolving Autonomous Driving and Automotive Electronics space through 
HL Klemove India, while enhancing its competitive position and expanding its presence in a segment 
central to the future of mobility.

About ANAND Group:  
Founded in 1961, ANAND is a US$2.2 billion + (approximately ₹22,000 crore)  conglomerate, highly 
acclaimed in the manufacture of world -class products and systems for the automotive industry. The 
group comprises 2 5 operating companies, including 1 5 joint venture s and 4 technical collaborations

About Gabriel India:  
Established in 1961, Gabriel India Limited is the flagship company of the ANAND Group and one of 
India’s most trusted names in automotive component manufacturing. With a legacy spanning over six 
decades, Gabriel has evolved as a market leader in ride contr ol products, including shock absorbers, 
struts, and front forks, serving every major automotive segment - two and three -wheelers, passenger 
cars, commercial vehicles, and railways.  
With a footprint across  25 countries , including Belgium, the USA, Australia, the Netherlands, Latin 
America, and Africa, and trusted partnerships with leading OEMs, including those in the growing 
electric vehicle (EV) segment, Gabriel continues to be at the forefront of India’s mobility revolution.  
 
About HL Klemove  HL Klemove is a South Korea -headquartered autonomous driving and automotive 
electronics solutions company with end -to-end capabilities across perception, decision and control. Its 
portfolio spans advanced sensing technologies including radar and cameras, h igh-performance 
computing and control units, ADAS software, as well as automotive electronic control products 
including braking and steering controllers. HL Klemove holds a leading position in Korea’s autonomous 
driving market and has a strong track record  of pioneering ADAS technologies. With more than 2,700 
patents and a global manufacturing and R&D network across Korea, the United States, China, India 
and Mexico, the company provides advanced mobility solutions to leading automotive OEMs globally. 
The company recorded approximately KRW 1.8 trillion in revenue in 2025.
