As set out in the Disclosures, (a) Tega Industries Limited (“ Company ”) and the ‘Apollo Funds’ (as 
defined in the Disclosures) had entered into certain ‘Acquisition Documents’ and ‘Transaction 
Documents’, including the ‘Equity Purchase Agreement’ and the ‘SSA’ (each as defined and disclosed 
in the Disclosures), in connection with the acquisition of the equity interests of the Molycop group 
(i.e. direct and indirect subsidiaries of AIP MC Holdings LLC and their respective investments, including 
joint ventures, each of which is listed in Annexure B  hereto (collectively, “ Molycop” and each a 
“Molycop Company ”) by the Consortium formed by Tega and Apollo Funds (details of which are set 
out in the Disclosures) (such transactions, the “ Acquisition ”) and (b) it was intimated that the closing 
of the transactions contemplated therein will be subject to customary conditions precedent, including 
regulatory and anti-trust approvals.  
The Company wishes to inform you that the closing of the Acquisition and the relevant transactions 
contemplated under the Acquisition Documents and Transaction Documents in relation to the 
Acquisition (the “ Transaction” ), upon satisfaction of the applicable conditions precedent, including 
regulatory and anti-trust approvals, has been completed today i.e. June 01, 2026.   
Accordingly, (a) Tega MC Investment Pte. Ltd. (“ Tega HoldCo ”) (a wholly owned subsidiary of the 
Company) has acquired ~84.2% of the ordinary shares in Tega MC JV Holdings Pte. Ltd. (“ SG 
Company ”) for an aggregate consideration of USD 394,295,423 and AP Jupiter Holdco (SG) Pte. Ltd. 
(“Apollo HoldCo ”) has acquired ~15.8% of the ordinary shares of the SG Company for an aggregate 
consideration of USD 74,107,477, (b) Apollo HoldCo has completed its investment in redeemable 
preference shares of Tega MC SG Investments I Pte. Ltd (a wholly owned subsidiary of SG Company) 
for an aggregate consideration of USD 270,000,000, and (c) the SG Company has (through its wholly 
owned subsidiaries), acquired the equity interests of the Molycop Companies.

Molycop group is a leading global supplier of grinding 
media to the mining industry, with a focus on the 
manufacture and sale of grinding media for use in both 
semi autogenous grinding (SAG) mills and ball mills. 
These products are critical to mineral extraction/ 
 
 
processing of multiple minerals, but most specifically 
Copper and Gold.  
 
Molycop group is a leading company with an 
impressive multi-decade track record in the grinding 
media space. It has established and built upon its 
industry leading business, a premium brand (i.e., high 
net promoter score and brand recognition), long-
standing and robust relationships with leading miners, 
and the most comprehensive offering of products/ 
services among peers. These advantages, a global 
supply network, and local presence that can land 
premium grinding media at competitive cost anywhere 
in the world make Molycop Group well positioned to 
attract more customers. 
 
Molycop group has a global manufacturing and sales 
network with 13 grinding ball manufacturing facilities, 
3 active joint ventures, and 1 potential joint venture 
currently in progress. These facilities are strategically 
located close to customers' operations and have a 
local presence in over 40 countries, including key 
territories such as USA, Canada, Mexico, Chile, Peru, 
Australia, and Indonesia.
