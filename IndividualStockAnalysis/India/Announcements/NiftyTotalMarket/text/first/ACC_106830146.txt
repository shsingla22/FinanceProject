Ahmedabad, 27 October 2026: ACC Limited, part of the diversified Adani Portfolio and 
one of India’s most trusted building materials and solutions companies, announced its 
results for the quarter  ended  30 September 2026 . 
Mr. Vinod Bahety, Whole -time Director and CEO, ACC Limited , said, “We delivered a 
resilient performance in Q2 and H1 FY’27, supported by an improving sales mix and 
higher premium volumes. While the reported numbers are not strictly comparable due 
to the impact of MSA, the underlying business momentum remains encouraging,  
supported by our trade partners.  
Our growth remains trade -led, in line with our strategy, while we continue to strengthen 
the institutional business through a comprehensive solutions appro ach with a focus on 
value and profitability. Industry demand remains supportive and is expected to 
strengthen post -monsoon, aided by housing, infrastructure and capital expenditure, 
while improving pricing momentum should moderate the impact of elevated fu el and 
energy costs.

Industry Outlook:   
• Q2 FY’27 was marked by geopolitical uncertainties , monsoon impact, seasonal 
shutdowns and elevated energy costs amid global uncertainties and constraints 
in domestic coal availability.  However, the medium -term demand outlook 
continues to be supported by infrastructure investment, urbanisation, rural 
housing and industrial activity. Central Government capex increased 18% YoY to 
Rs 5.1 lakh crore during Apr –Aug FY ’27, compared with Rs 4.3 lakh crore in the 
corresponding period last year.  
• ACC continues to focus on operational efficiency, premiumisation, logistics 
optimisation and disciplined capital allocation. Its improving business mix, 
expanding Ready -Mix Concrete network, trusted brand, premium and 
sustainable product portfolio, and str ong balance sheet provide a solid 
foundation for growth. Subject to the remaining approvals, the proposed 
amalgamation with Ambuja Cements is expected to facilitate closer 
coordina tion across the combined manufacturing, logistics and market network.

ACC continued to strengthen  its business with value focus across its product segments .

About ACC Limited  
 
ACC Limited, a subsidiary of Ambuja Cements and part of the diversified Adani Group, is one of India’s most 
trusted building materials and concr ete solutions companies. With a legacy of nearly nine decades, ACC 
operates 22 cement manufacturing sites, 125 ready -mix concrete plants, and a nationwide network of 
channel partners serving customers  across India.  The Company is advancing its sustainability priorities 
across renewable energy, circular material use, water stewardship, sustainable logistics and product 
development. ACC was recognised as the Most Sustainable Company in Cement under the Sectoral 
Excell ence in Manufacturing category at BT India ’s Most Sustainable Companies 2026 by Business Today. 
ACC, alongside Ambuja Cements, is among four large -scale building materials companies globally 
committed to achieving net -zero emissions by 2050, with near -term and net -zero targets validated by the 
Science Based Targets initiative. Its blended cement portfolio is GreenPro -certified and included in the 
GRIHA Product Catalogue. ACC has also been recognised among India’s Most Trusted Cement Brands by 
TRA Research and among the Iconic Brands of India 2025 b y The Economic Times for the third consecutive 
year.
