CreditAccess Grameen Broadens  Its Global Lender Base   
With USD 1 75 Million  Syndicated Social Loan  Facility   
 
Bengaluru, 12th October  2026: CreditAccess Grameen Limited (NSE: CREDITACC, BSE: 541770, ‘CA Grameen’ 
or the ‘Company’), India’s leading rural focused inclusive financing platform  has signed  USD 175 million  
syndicated social loan facility , qualifying as ECB (‘External Commercial Borrowing’) under the automatic route 
of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). Standard Chartered Bank and DBS Bank acted as the Joint Mandated Lead 
Arrangers and Bookrunners .   
 
Despite global macroeconomic uncertainties  and heightened competition in the ECB market from FCNR (B) 
deposits mobilisation, t he syndication transaction received strong response coupled with competitive loan 
pricing and secured participation from ten financial institutions across Asia  and the Middle East  & Africa  
regions. In addition to the anchor contributions from Standard Chartered Bank (Singapore)  and DBS Bank (GIFT 
City), other participants included Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC (Sri Lanka) , The Korea Development Bank 
(South Korea) , Sampath Bank PLC (Sri Lanka) , SBI (Mauritius) Ltd , BCP Bank (Mauritius) Ltd., Al Masraf (UAE), 
The National Bank of Ras Al Khaimah (P.S.C.) (UAE),  and KEXIM Asia Ltd. (Hong Kong) .  
 
The transaction reflect s the continued confidence of international lenders in CA Grameen’s business model and  
corporate  governance standards.  This marks the fifth ECB syndication transaction successfully completed by 
CA Grameen over  the past five years, mobilising USD 640 million  till date . The Company currently holds around 
24% of its liability profile  in foreign borrowings, building a  strong lender base across 15 countries.   
 
Over the past decade, CA Grameen has been continu ously working towards  diversif ication of  its liability 
franchise  beyond traditional bank borrowings, building presence in domestic debt capital market and 
expanding outreach in global loan market  leveraging its strong ESG credentials . During FY27 , the Company 
has also mobilised INR 940 crore through  multiple  private placements of non-convertible debentures (NCDs), 
with pricing broadly in line with its average cost of borrowings.  
 
Commenting on th e transaction , Mr . Nilesh Dalvi , Chief Financial Officer , CreditAccess Grameen, 
remarked , “The successful closure of the USD 175 million facility  at competitive pricing,  amid st global 
macroeconomic uncertainties  and heightened competition from FCNR(B) deposits  mobilisation , demonstrates the 
strength of CA Grameen franchise and the confidence we have earned from international lenders. The CA 
Grameen brand rests on a proven performance track record, strong corporate governance standards, deep 
customer relationships across India’s hinterland and disciplined risk management. As we diversify our sources of 
funding , our focus remains on optimising funding costs, maintaining a robust asset -liability management (ALM) 
profile and strengthening balance sheet resilience to support sustainable growth and our long -term commitment 
to financial inclusion. ”
