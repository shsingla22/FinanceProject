With reference to your email/letter dated March 27, 2026 seeking clarification/confirmation in 
respect of the above -mentioned news item, we wish to inform  that the said news pertains to a 
temporary production pause at the Solihull manufacturing facility of Jaguar Land Rover ( ‘JLR’), 
a wholly owned subsidiary of Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited (formerly Tata Motors 
Limited)  (‘the Company ’), due to a part supply constraint from a supplier. The disruption is  
short -term in nature and limited in scope .  
 
As per the official statement issued by JLR, production has been temporarily paused on certain 
vehicle lines and JLR is working closely with the supplier to resolve the issue as quickly as 
possible and to minimize any impact on c lients or operations.
