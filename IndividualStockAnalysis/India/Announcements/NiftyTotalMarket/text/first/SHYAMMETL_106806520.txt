Shyam Metalics Signs MoU with arashtra Govt. to Set Up 250,000 Crore Integrated Steel Complex in 
Chandrapur INDIA 
The proposed 9 MTPA greenfield facility is expected to create 30,000 direct and indirect jobs while driving industrial 
transformation across Vidarbha. 
MUMBAL, October 5, 2026 — Shyam Metalics & Energy Ltd (SMEL), India’s leading metals and minerals producer, 
signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Government of Maharashtra to establish a 9 million tonnes 
capacity integrated steel complex in Chandrapur. 
The agreement was formally executed in Mumbai by Dr. P. Anbalagan, IAS, Principal Secretary (Industries, Investment 
& Services), representing the Government of Maharashtra, and Mr. Brij Bhushan Agarwal, Chairman & Managing 
Director of Shyam Metalics and Energy Ltd, marking a shared commitment toward expanding the state's industrial 
footprint and creating long-term regional value. 
The project will be set up at an estimated investment of 50,000 crore and will mark one of Vidarbha region’s largest 
single industrial investments. It also aligns with the state's vision to accelerate manufacturing growth and expand India’s 
domestic steel-making capabilities. 
Chandrapur district, the chosen site for the proposed project sits close to iron ore reserves in Gadchiroli, coal deposits 
across the Chandrapur mining belt, and has proximity to essential minerals such as limestone and dolomite, all supported 
by well-connected rail, road, and power infrastructure. 
The Greenfield Integrated Steel Complex will be developed in two phases: 
®  Phase-I (3.75 MTPA Finished Steel Capacity) 
®  Phase-II (5.25 MTPA Additional Finished Steel Capacity) 
As a fully integrated greenfield complex built from the ground up, the facility will manage the entire steel production 
Jjourney under one roof. The plant will undertake onsite raw material conversion through pelletisation, sintering, and 
coke making, followed by ironmaking via blast fumace and direct reduced iron (DRI) processes, steel melting, 
continuous casting, and downstream rolling and finishing. 
This end-to-end operation is designed to manufacture high-quality, value-added steel for key sectors including 
infrastructure, construction, automotive, railways, renewable energy, oil and gas, and heavy engineering. By supplying 
these core industries with domestically manufactured steel, the facility will directly support India’s self-reliance goals 
and reduce reliance on imported steel. 
In near future the complex will become a major driver of regional economic prosperity generating approximately 10,000 
direct jobs and 20,000 indirect employment opportunities. This substantial workforce demand will spur comprehensive 
skill development initiatives across Vidarbha, opening career paths for local youth while catalyzing an extensive 
downstream industrial ecosystem. Micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs), fabrication units, engineering 
workshops, and transport and logistics providers across the region stand to gain substantial business momentum from 
the plant's operational requirements. 
At the same time, the development incorporates advanced environmental safeguards to ensure long-term, energy- 
efficient operations. The plant will deploy clean technologies including waste heat recovery systems to generate power, 
coke dry quenching to conserve water and trap energy, and closed-loop water treatment systems for complete recycling 
and reuse. Coupled with state-of-the-art pollution-control infrastructure and high-efficiency machinery, Shyam Metalics 
aims to set a strong benchmark for sustainable steel manufacturing in Maharashtra.

About Shyam Metalics and Energy Limited 
Shyam Metalics is a leading and fastest-growing integrated metal-producing company based in India primarily in the 
steel industry in West Bengal, Odisha, Jharkhand, and Madhya Pradesh with a focus on Long & Flat Steel Products, 
Ferro Alloys, Aluminium, and Stainless Steel. The company got listed itself on the exchanges in 2021 and possesses a 
‘market capitalization of more than ¥ 29,000 Cr. Headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal, the company is amongst the 
largest producers of ferro alloys in terms of installed capacity in India. The company has an aggregate installed metal 
capacity of 16.93 MTPA across the steel value chain and is supported by a 467 MW aggregated installed capacity of 
captive power plants. For more information, please visit our website at https://www.shyammetalics.com/.
