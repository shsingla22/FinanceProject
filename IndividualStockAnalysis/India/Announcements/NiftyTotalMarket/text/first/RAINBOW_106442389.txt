Rainbow Children’s Medicare Limited posts steady performance and 
continued expansion momentum for Q2 FY26  
• An eventful quarter marked by the addition of 350 beds through strategic acquisitions and greenfield 
expansion, strengthening RCML’s regional leadership.  
• The state -of-the-art Electronic City (~90 beds) and Hennur (~60 beds) hospitals are in the final stages of 
completion , Bengaluru cluster to reach ~600 beds by Dec 2025.  
• Continued focus on quality with NABH re -accreditation for BG Road and first -time accreditation for 
Sholinganallur, underscoring RCML’s unwavering commitment to clinical excellence and patient trust.  
 
Hyderabad , November 13 , 2025: Rainbow Children’s Medicare Limited (RCML), India’s leading pediatric 
multi -specialty and perinatal care hospital chain, announced its financial results for the second quarter and 
first half of the financial year FY2 6 ended September 30, 2025.  
 
During the quarter, the company continued to strengthen its presence across key markets with the acquisition  
of Prashanthi Hospital, Waranga l, Pratiksha  hospital, Guwahati marking RCML’s entry into the Northeast 
region, and the launch of its new unit in Rajahmundry, further deepening its footprint in Andhra Pradesh.  
 
The company is now gearing up for the commencement of two new spoke hospitals in Bengaluru — Electronic 
City (90 beds) and Hennur (60 beds) — further strengthening its footprint in the city. With these additions, RCML 
will conclude a high -bed expansion  phase, adding 780 beds over the past two years through strategic 
expansions and acquisitions.  
 
The quarter saw softer financial performance, driven by a lower incidence of seasonal  illness and an early 
festive season that compressed the typical peak period. Despite this, RCML delivered operational stability, with 
strong patient traction and steady ramp -up across new hospitals. As the present  expansion phase nears 
completion, the company remains focused on strengthening and consolidating operations to build a robust 
platform for the next phase of growth.  
 
The company is pleased to announce the appointment of Mr. Abrarali Dalal as Group Chief Executive Officer, 
effective January 20, 2026. This appointment marks an important milestone as the company strengthens its 
leadership team to drive the next phase of g rowth.

About Rainbow Children’s Medicare Limited  
 
Rainbow Children’s Medicare Limited network comprises of 2 2 hospitals and 5 clinics in 9 cities, with a total bed 
capacity of 2, 285 beds. Our Pediatric services operating under the brand “Rainbow Children’s Hospital” includes 
newborn and pediatric intensive care, pediatric multi -specialty services, pediatric quaternary care (including 
organ transplantation); whereas our women care se rvices under “Birthright by Rainbow” offers perinatal care 
services which includes normal and complex obstetric care, multi -disciplinary fetal care, perinatal genetic and 
fertility care along with gynecology services.  
 
Rainbow Children’s Hospital built on strong fundamentals of a multidisciplinary approach with a full -time 
consultant led clinical service along with 24/7 commitment in a child centric environment. The company follows 
a hub -and -spoke operating model where t he hub hospital provides comprehensive outpatient, inpatient care, with 
a focus on tertiary and quaternary services, while the spokes provide 24/7 emergency care, large outpatient 
services and comprehensive obstetrics, pediatric inpatient and level 3 NICU services. This model is successfully 
operational at Hyderabad and is gaining traction in Bengaluru. The endeavor is to replicate this approach in 
Chennai and across the National Capital Region. Subsequently Rainbow intends to expand into tier -2 cities of 
Southern India.  
 
Rainbow Children’s Hospital embraces a unique doctor engagement model, where doctors work exclusively on a 
full-time, retainer basis. The doctors work in teams and have 24/7 commitment, which is particularly important for 
children’s emergency, neonatal, pe diatric intensive care services and to support pediatric retrieval services. The 
Company also operates the country’s largest pediatric DNB training programme in private healthcare, offering post 
graduate residential DNB and fellowship programme.
