National, November 11, 2026:  Tata Power, one of India's largest integrated power companies, 
delivered its highest-ever quarterly Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₹ 1,652 crore in Q2 FY27, up  33% year -
on-year, demonstrating the strength of its diversified and future-ready energy portfolio. R evenue rose  
26% to ₹ 19,886 crore and EBITDA increased 5% to ₹ 4,245 crore during the quarter.  
For H1 FY27, the Company reported record PAT of ₹ 3,053 crore, up 22% year -on-year, while 
Revenue grew to ₹ 38,784 crore and EBITDA reached ₹ 8,494 crore, refl ecting strong operational 
performance across businesses.  
The quarter's performance was driven by broad-based growth across Generation, Renewables, 
Transmission & Distribution and emerging consumer -focused green businesses. The quality and 
resilience of the earnings continue to strengthen, with regulated and long-term contracted businesses 
accounting for an increasing share of EBITDA, providing greater predictability and visibility of cash 
flows and profitability.  
During H1 FY27, the Company invested ~₹10,000 crore, its highest half -yearly capital deployment, 
reinforcing its commitment to building critical infrastructure to meet India's growing energy needs and support its transition towards a secure, reliable and sustainable energy future. 
India's electrici ty demand continued to witness sustained growth in H1 FY27, driven by industrial and 
economic activity, agricultural consumption, rising household demand, increasing EV adoption and the expanding data centre ecosystem. Extreme heatwaves and a delayed monsoon further 
accelerated cooling-related consumption, reinforcing the need for reliable and uninterrupted supply. 
Tata Power is well -positioned to address this evolving demand through its diversified portfolio of fuel -
secure thermal generation assets, expanding renewable energy capacity, energy storage solutions 
and robust transmission and distribution infrastructure.  
Renewable businesses remained a key driver of earnings growth, underpinned by 1.4 GW of capacity 
additions over the last twelve months, strong manufacturing margins and yields, and continued 
leadership in rooftop EPC with over 5.1 GWp of cumulative installations. Odisha distribution continued 
its strong operational performance, with AT&C losses improving by 5.7% over Q1 FY27. Following 
Cyclone Ar nab, electricity supply was restored within 48-72 hours in affected area, highlighting the 
resilience of the tech powered network and operational capabilities of field teams. AI-led digitalisation 
is being scaled across operations to drive efficiency, pred ictive maintenance and improve customer 
experience.  
The Company’s Conventional  generation assets delivered strong operating and financial 
performance. Mundra plant continues to operate at full capacity and runs currently under Section 11 
till December end. Company is actively advancing discussions with multiple states to secure 
Supplementary Power Purchase Agreements (SPPAs), which are at an advanced stage.  
 
Tata Power is also advancing its pumped storage hydro portfolio, with construction at Bhivpuri PSP 
progressing on schedule and Board approval secured for the capital commitment for the Shirwata project, reinforcing its commitment to building long-duration energy storage capacity to support India’s evolving power requirements.

About Tata Power:  
The Tata Power Company Limited, a vertically integrated power company and part of the Tata Group , India’s largest 
multinational business conglomerate, has built a diversified portfolio across the entire power value chain, with its total 
operational and under -construction capacity surpassing 27 GW. This includes approximately 18.3 GW of clean and green 
energy capacity (including projects under construction) and around 8.8 GW of thermal generation capacity, ensuring a 
balanced and resilient energy mix. The Company also has a strong transmission and distribution footprint, with over 7,800 
circuit kilometres of transmission lines, including pipeline projects, and serves  over 13 million customers across its 
distribution businesses, making it one of India’s largest private power distribution companies.  Further strengthening its 
leadership in future-ready energy solutions, Tata Power has established 4.9 GW of integrated solar cell and module 
manufacturing capacity, signed 2.8 GW of pumped hydro storage projects, and expanded its EV charging network to over 7,000 public charging points acros s 793 cities and towns in India.  As a pioneer in India’s clean energy transition, Tata 
Power remains committed to achieving Net  Zero by 2045 and continues to partner with public and private stakeholders to 
deliver reliable, sustainable, and technology -led energy solutions across the country.
