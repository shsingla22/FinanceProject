NISM and CAMS Collaborate to Advance Technology -Enabled Compliance Learning 
through CAMS Lens  
- AI-powered regulatory intelligence platform to support practical learning and research 
at the NISM Compliance & RegTech Lab  
 
Mumbai, October 14th, 2026 - The National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)  and 
Computer Age Management Services Limited (CAMS)  have entered into a 
collaboration to integrate CAMS Lens into the Regulatory Intelligence module of the NISM 
Compliance & RegTech Lab.  
 
The collaboration brings together NISM’s focus on securities -market education and 
capacity building with CAMS’ technology and regulatory intelligence capabilities. It is 
aimed at providing students, faculty members and market professionals with practical 
exposure to technology -enabled regulatory research and compliance workflows relevant 
to India’s capital markets.  
 
Established as a technology -driven platform for education, training, simulation, research 
and innovation in financial -market compliance, the NISM Compliance & RegTech Lab 
provides a multi -software environment spanning RegTech, risk management, analytics, 
artificial intelligence, machine learning and market -surveillance solutions. Its applied 
learning model enables participants to work through realistic regulatory scenarios using 
synthetic identities, transactions and case files.  
 
As a part of the collaboration, the Regulatory Content and Intelligence module of CAMS 
Lens will be made available across devices in the NISM Compliance & RegTech Lab, with 
additional access for faculty members . Participants will be able to use the platform to 
research and understand regulatory and compliance requirements across the capital -
markets ecosystem, with a primary focus on SEBI -regulated intermediaries.  
 
CAMS Lens is an AI -powered regulatory intelligence and compliance execution platform 
designed for participants across the Indian capital -markets ecosystem. The platform 
brings regulatory information and intelligence together in a structured environment, 
helping organisations strengthen regulatory management, compliance oversight and 
audit readiness.  
 
Its integration into the Lab will support participants as they navigate regulatory 
requirements and develop a more practical understanding of how technology can 
facilitate regulatory interpretation and compliance execution. The deployment will also 
complem ent the Lab’s broader pedagogy, which takes learners through workflows such 
as screening, risk assessment, transaction monitoring, investigation, evidence 
evaluation and reporting.  
 
Commenting on the collaboration, Shri Sashi Krishnan, Director, NISM, said , “The 
growing complexity of financial markets requires compliance professionals to combine 
sound regulatory knowledge with the ability to work effectively with technology. The NISM 
Compliance & RegTech Lab has been created to provide this practical exposure . With four 
 
decades of experience serving the mutual fund ecosystem, CAMS brings a deep 
understanding of the regulatory environment and the complexities involved in navigating 
it. The integration of its in -house platform, CAMS Lens, will strengthen the Regulatory 
Intelligence module of the NISM Compliance & RegTech Lab and enable participants to 
engage with regulatory information in a structured, technology -enabled environment.”  
 
Mr. Anuj Kumar, Managing Director, CAMS, said , “Effective compliance begins with the 
ability to access, understand and apply regulatory information with clarity. Through CAMS 
Lens, we seek to make regulatory intelligence more accessible and actionable for 
participants across the capital -markets ecosys tem. Our collaboration with NISM allows 
us to extend this capability to the learning environment and contribute to the 
development of technology -enabled compliance professionals.”  
 
The collaboration agreement was formally signed in Mumbai  on October 14, 2026, by Shri 
Sashi Krishnan, Director, NISM, and Mr. Anuj Kumar, Managing Director and CEO, CAMS.

About NISM:  
 
The National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM) is a public trust established by the 
Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) in 2006 to strengthen the quality and 
integrity of India's securities markets through education, certification, research,  capacity 
building and financial literacy. As India's premier institution for securities market 
education and investor awareness, NISM works with regulators, market participants, 
academic institutions and investors to enhance professional competence, promo te 
financial literacy and support a fair, transparent and resilient securities market 
ecosystem through its academic programmes, professional certifications, research 
initiatives and nationwide outreach activities.
