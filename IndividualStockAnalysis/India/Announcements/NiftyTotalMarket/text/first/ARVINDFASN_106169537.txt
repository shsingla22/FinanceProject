Lucknow.

concierge, will expand its presence with the addition of t wo new locations this yea r. The stores will be 
located Himayatnagar, Hyderabad and HSR Layout, Bangalore .  Club A customers can now also shop for 
the much -anticipated, refreshingly new Spring Summer -25 Collections from all the in -house brands.
