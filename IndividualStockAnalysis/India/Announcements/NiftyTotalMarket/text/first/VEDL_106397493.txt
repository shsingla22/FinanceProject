Vedanta Group is a global leader in critical minerals, transition metals, energy, and technology, with operations spanning

producer of zin c, the fourth -largest global producer of silver, and one of the top producers of aluminium globally, Vedanta 
plays a pivotal role in the global supply of essential materials for the energy transition. The Company is also India’s only 
private oil and gas pr oducer and one of the largest private power producers. A global ESG champion, Vedanta is committed
