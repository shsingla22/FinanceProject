Lemon Tree Hotels signs two new propert ies in Vrindavan and in 
Navsari  
 
 
New Delhi, 21st March 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels announces its  two latest 
signing — Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels , Vrindavan , Uttar Pradesh  and Keys 
Select by Lemon Tree Hotels, Navsari, Gujarat . While the former is set to open in 
FY26, the la tter is proposed to open by FY28. Both these propert ies shall be 
managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon 
Tree Hotels Limited . 
 
Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels, Vrindavan, Uttar Pradesh will feature 5 4 well-
appointed rooms, a restaurant , a banquet , a meeting room , a swimming pool, a 
Spa, a fitness center and other public areas. The Indira Gandhi International 
Airport , New Delhi is approximately 148 kms and the upcoming Jewar 
International Airport is about 105 kms from the property . The Mathura Railway 
Station is about 16 kms away from the property. The hotel is also well -connected 
by road, offering easy access to both public and private transport.  
 
Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels, Navsari, Gujarat  will feature 5 4 well-appointed 
rooms, a restaurant , a banquet, and other public areas. Surat International Airport 
the nearest airport, is approximately 45 kms from the property. The Navsari 
Railway Station is about 6.4 kms away from the property. The hotel is also well -
connected by road, offering easy access to both public and private transport.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels commented, “ We are thrilled to announce our latest signing s, 
which will further expand our presence in Gujarat and Uttar Pradesh . These 
signing s are a testament to our commitment to providing exceptional hospitality 
and unforgettable experiences in India’s most vibrant destinations.”  
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of the largest hotel chains in India, and 
owns/leases/operates/franchises hotels across the upscale, upper -midscale, 
midscale and economy segments. It delivers differentiated yet superior service 
offerings, with a co mpelling value proposition. The group offers seven brands to 
meet guests’ needs across all levels, viz. Aurika Hotels & Resorts, Lemon Tree 
Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox Hotels by Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Prima 
by Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Select by Lem on Tree Hotels and Keys Lite by Lemon 
Tree Hotels.  
 
 
 
 

   
 
 
 
 
LTH opened its first hotel with 49 rooms in May 2004. Today, the company has a 
portfolio of 210+ hotels, which includes over 110 operational hotels and more than 
100 hotels set to open in India and internationally. Lemon Tree Hotels are located 
across India, in key metro regions including the NCR, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, 
Hyderabad and Chennai, as well as numerous tier I, II and III cities such as Pune, 
Ahmedabad, C handigarh, Jaipur, Indore, Aurangabad, Udaipur, Visakhapatnam, 
Kochi, Ludhiana, Thiruvanan thapuram, Vijayawada, etc. The company has also 
expanded internationally to Dubai, Bhutan and Nepal.
