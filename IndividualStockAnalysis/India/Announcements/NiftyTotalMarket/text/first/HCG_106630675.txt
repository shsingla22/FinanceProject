In furtherance of our intimation dated May 1 9, 2026, we would like to inform you that HealthCare 
Global Enterprises Limited (“the Company”) has entered into a Share Purchase Agreement (“SPA ”) 
dated May 19, 2026, with BACC Health Care Private Limited (“ BACC”) and Inviga Healthcare Fund 
I (“Buyer ”) in relation to the divestment by way of sale of the Company’s entire equity shareholding in 
BACC to the Buyer and its nominee, for a consideration of INR 37,64,44,788 (Indian Rupees Thirty 
Seven Crores Sixty Four Lakhs Forty Four Thousand Seven Hundred and Eighty Eight Only), on the 
terms agreed under the SPA. BACC is not a material subsidiary of the Company and is engaged in the 
business of fertility and reproductive healthcare services, which is not a core business activity of the 
Company. The said transaction has been approved by the Board and Audit Committee pursuant to the 
meetings held on May 19, 2026, as disclosed by our intimation dated May 19, 2026. 
 
Please refer to Annexure - A for more details in relation to the SPA. The additional disclosures as
