This is with reference to the above l etter seeking clarification in relation to the news item 
appearing on CNBC -TV18 on 15 October 2026, captioned “Yes Bank share price sees sharp 
fall of 10% after Supreme Court ruling on ₹8,400 crore AT -1 bond write -off”. 
At the outset, YES BANK Limited (“Ba nk”) submits that the subject matter of the news item 
concerns judicial proceedings relating to the write -down of Additional Tier -1 Bonds (“AT -1 
Bonds”) aggregating ₹8,415 crore undertaken in March 2020.  
The Bank’s response to the specific queries raised b y the Exchange is set out below:  
1. Whether such negotiations/events were taking place and, if so, the sequence of events  
The matter concerns judicial proceedings that have remained in the public domain and in 
respect of which the Bank has made disclosures to the Stock Exchanges at the relevant 
stages.  
The material sequence of events is summarised below:  
a. On 14 March 2020 , the Bank informed the Stock Exchanges, vide letter no. YBL/CS/2019 -
20/186(2) , regarding the write -down of the AT -1 Bonds pu rsuant to the applicable 
contractual and regulatory framework.  
b. Writ petitions were thereafter filed before the Hon’ble Bombay High Court challenging 
the write -down of the AT -1 Bonds.  
c. On 26 May 2020 , the Bank, vide letter no. YBL/CS/2020 -21/009 , respo nded to a news 
clarification sought by the Exchange and stated, inter alia, that Axis Trustee Services 
Limited, on behalf of the AT -1 bondholders, had filed a writ petition before the Hon’ble 
Bombay High Court challenging the write -off and that the matter was sub judice.  
 
 
d. On 20 January 2023 , vide letter no. YBL/CS/2022 -23/135 , the Bank informed the Stock 
Exchanges that the Hon’ble Bombay High Court had set aside the decision to write down 
the AT -1 Bonds; that the operation of the judgment had been stayed for six weeks at the 
Bank’s request; and that, based on legal advice, the Bank was in the process of preferring 
an appeal before the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India.  
e. On 4 March 2023 , vide letter no. YBL/CS/2022 -23/158 , the Bank informed the Stock 
Exchang es that Special Leave Petitions had been filed before the Hon’ble Supreme Court 
and that, by its order dated 3 March 2023, the Hon’ble Supreme Court had extended the 
stay granted by the Hon’ble Bombay High Court, subject to the final orders of the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court.  
Moreover, as regards this matter, the Bank had also made suitable disclosures from time to 
time in its financial statements as part of Notes to Accounts.  
f. On 15 October 2026 , the Hon’ble Supreme Court pronounced its judgment in the appeal s 
arising from the judgment dated 20 January 2023 of the Hon’ble Bombay High Court.  
The Bank is in the process of obtaining and examining the final order copy of the judgment, 
and its implications and will make such further disclosure as may be required.  
2. Whether the Bank is aware of any information that has not been announced to the 
Exchanges which could explain the movement in trading, and reasons for not disclosing 
the same earlier under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015  
The Bank is not aware of any information or event concerning the Bank, other than the 
pronouncement of the judgment referred to above and the information already available in 
the public domain that has not been announced to the  Stock Exchanges and that could 
explain the aforesaid movement in the Bank’s securities.  
The Bank respectfully submits that there has been no failure to disclose a material event or 
information under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Discl osure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015.  
The initiation and material developments in the proceedings were disclosed by the Bank to 
the Stock Exchanges from time to time as mentioned in point 1 above, The outcome before 
the Hon’ble Supreme Court arose only up on pronouncement of the judgment on 15 
October 2026.  
3. The material impact of the article on the Company  
The Bank notes the movement in the market price of its equity shares following publication 
of the news article and pronouncement of the judgment. Insofar as the impact of the 
underlying judgment of the Hon’ble Supreme Court is concerned, the Bank is in the  process 
 
 
of obtaining and examining the final order copy of the judgment and assessing its 
implications and will make such further disclosure as may be required.  
The Bank remains committed to ensuring timely, accurate and adequate disclosure of 
material i nformation to the Stock Exchanges.  
This clarification is being submitted for your information, record and appropriate 
dissemination.
