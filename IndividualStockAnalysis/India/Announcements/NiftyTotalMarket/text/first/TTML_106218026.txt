Press information Note  
 
Tata Tele Business Services honoured with Four Prestigious Wins at ET Telecom 
Awards 2025 
New Delhi, 02 May, 2025 : Tata Tele Business Services(TTBS),  one of India’s leading enablers of digital 
connectivity and cloud solutions for businesses, has won four prestigious awards  at the ET Telecom 
Awards 2025 , reaffirming its leadership and commitment to driving innovation for India’s enterprise 
segment. 
TTBS received accolades in the following categories: 
 Enterprise Digital Transformation of the Year  
 Best Customer Service  
 Best Brand Awareness Campaign  
 Cloud Service Provider & Cloud Technology (Jury Recognition)  
Commenting on the achievement, Mr. Vishal Rally, Sr. VP & Head - Product, Marketing and 
Commercial, Tata Teleservices Ltd, said,  “We are truly honored to receive these prestigious 
recognitions at the ET Telecom Awards 2025. These awards reaffirm our customer-first approach, 
innovation-driven strategy and the profound trust businesses place in us.  They reflect the real impact 
we’re making by democratising technology and helping enterprises scale, thrive and lead in the 
evolving digital-first world.”  
As the telecom and enterprise tech space continues to evolve, TTBS stands out with its innovative 
cloud platforms, unparalleled customer service, and insight-driven marketing that empowers 
organizations to do big in the digital era. 
The ET Telecom Awards celebrate outstanding contributions and innovations in the Indian 
telecommunications industry, recognizing excellence in network technologies, service offerings, 
customer-centric strategies and transformative digital solutions. 
 
About Tata Teleservices   
Tata Teleservices Limited along with its subsidiary Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Limited (NSE: 
TTML, BSE:  532371) (Tata Teleservices) is a growing market leader in the Enterprise space. It offers a 
comprehensive portfolio of Connectivity, Collaboration, Cloud & SaaS, Security, and Marketing 
solutions for businesses in the country under the brand name Tata Tele Business Services (TTBS). Tata 
Teleservices has an extensive, high quality and robust wireline network and offers its products and 
services in more than 60 cities across India. Tata Teleservices has one of the largest enterprise-focused 
teams in the industry with deep customer engagement and technology orientation offering focused 
sales and service experience to customers. Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Limited is listed on BSE 
and NSE in India.
