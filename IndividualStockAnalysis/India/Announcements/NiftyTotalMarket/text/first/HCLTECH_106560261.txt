HCLTech named AWS European Sovereign Cloud launch partner  
 
POTSDAM, Germany  and NOIDA, India,  March 18, 202 6—H CLTec h , a leading global technology 
company,  has been named an official launch partner  for the AWS European Sovereign Cloud  
(ESC) , a new independent cloud for Europe launched  in the State of Brandenburg, Germany.  
 
The AWS European Sovereign Cloud  provides a comprehensive, independently managed 
infrastructure located entirely within the EU. It is built with rigorous technical safeguards and 
legal frameworks specifically designed for European public sector and enterprise needs. By 
operating separate ly from existing AWS Regions, it provides localized control over data 
residency and operational autonomy.  
 
As a launch partner, HCL Tech will enable clients to meet stringent operational autonomy and 
data residency requirements within the European Union (EU)  with security , speed and agility.   
 HCL Tech was selected due to its proven ability to manage complex, highly regulated workloads across Europe, its strength in operating governed cloud environments and its deep technical 
alignment with AWS. As a Premier Tier Services Partner and a frequent p articipant in AWS 
early -access programs, HCLTech brings extensive cloud and security expertise, backed by 
multiple AWS competencies spanning security, networking, cloud resilience, high -performance 
computing, managed security services and GenAI. Recognized  as AWS Partner of the Year in 
Financial Services (APJ) and a Global Finalist in GenAI Consult, HCLTech will work closely with AWS to deliver the operational readiness and governance required for sovereign cloud 
adoption.  
 
“European organizations are seeking cloud innovation that respects sovereignty without 
slowing transformation. Being a launch partner for the AWS European Sovereign Cloud allows 
us to apply our deep regulatory and operational expertise in a way that gives clients confidence, control and continuity as they modernize their most critical systems, " said Prabhakar Appana, 
SVP and Global Head of AWS Business Unit  at HCLTe c h.
