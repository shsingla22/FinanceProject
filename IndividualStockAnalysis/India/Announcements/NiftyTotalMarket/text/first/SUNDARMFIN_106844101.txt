Re:  Acquisition of 100% stake in Sundaram Alternate Assets Limited from Sundaram Asset

We wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of the  Company has, at its meeting held today, 
accorded its approval for the proposed acquisition of 100% stake in Sundaram Alternate Assets 
limited (SAAL), a wholly -owned subsidiary of Sundaram Asset Management Company Limited 
(SAMCL), which in turn is a wholly -owned subsidiary of the Company . The proposed acquisition shall 
be subject to necessary regulatory approvals. Upon completion of the proposed acquisition, SAAL  
shall become a wholly -owned subsidiary of the Company.
