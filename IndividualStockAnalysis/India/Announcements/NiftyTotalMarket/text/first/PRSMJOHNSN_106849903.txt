Performance Review Prism Johnson delivered 8.3% YoY growth in consolidated revenue to ₹1,874 
Cr in Q2 FY27, supported by growth across all three businesses. EBITDA  
increased 4.3% YoY to ₹194.3 Cr, led by improved profitability in H&R 
Johnson (HRJ), partially offset by lower EBITDA in Prism Cement and Prism 
RMC.
In Q2 FY27, PBT (before exceptional items) increased to ₹57.3 Cr from ₹14.8 
Cr in Q2 FY26, further supported by lower finance costs and depreciation (1).
ROCE improved to 15.1% from 9.6%, reflecting stronger operating 
performance and disciplined capital allocation, supported by an asset -light 
operating mix, including outsourced cement grinding, strategic tile sourcing 
and select franchisee -led RMC operations.
The Company made significant progress in strengthening its balance sheet, 
with effective net debt —including financial obligations, LC and CAD bills —
declining to ₹467 Cr as of September 2026 from ₹888 Cr as of March 2026. 
The reduction was supported by proceeds from the divestment of non -core 
assets (2) and cash accruals from operations.“
(1) The decrease in depreciation charge during the current period is primarily attributable to certain classes of assets being 
substantially depreciated in earlier periods under the useful -life based depreciation policy as per Schedule II of the Companies  
Act, 2013 / Ind AS 16, and certain assets having reached their residual value. This, coupled with limited incremental capex 
during the period, has resulted in lower depreciation.
 (2) Divestment of RQBE was completed on 1 July 2026 for a consideration of ₹325.87 Cr. A gain of ₹161.56 Cr was recognised  in 
Q2 FY27 under discontinued operations and was not recognised  in Prism Johnson’s continuing operations P&L.
