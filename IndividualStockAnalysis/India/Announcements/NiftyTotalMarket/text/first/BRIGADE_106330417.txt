PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Group Unveils the 18th Edition of its Flagship Property Expo – Brigade Showcase  
 
Bengaluru, August 7, 2025 – Brigade Group has announced the  18th edition of its flagship property expo, 
Brigade Showcase, which will be held from August 8th – 10th, 2025 at Sheraton Grand Hotel, Brigade Gateway 
Campus, Bengaluru.  
 
This year’s Showcase offers an exclusive opportunity for homebuyers to explore over 15 outstanding Brigade 
projects across Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad, with prices ranging from Rs. 65 Lakhs to Rs. 21 Cr. In a 
special offer for the event, Brigade is extending waivers on other charges including Clubhouse, Maintenance, 
Gas Connection, Property Assessment, Generator, and Infrastructure charges on select projects.  
 
The expo will also spotlight five new project launches across Bengaluru and Chennai, reinforcing Brigade’s 
commitment to bringing innovative and customer -centric developments.  
 
“This year’s event comes at a time of rising demand in the housing sector and showcases a compelling portfolio 
including integrated townships, apartments, plotted developments, ownership offices and coworking spaces. 
We will also have on display our interi or, rental & resale offerings. With a strong focus on customer needs, 
construction quality, and sustainable design, we are confident that this edition will receive an enthusiastic 
response. We look forward to welcoming homebuyers to explore the wide spectr um of our offerings,” said 
Smarajit Mishra, Senior VP – Marketing and CX , Brigade Enterprises Limited . 
 
About Brigade Group  
Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers with close to four decades of 
expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and winning their customers’ trust. Brigade 
has developed many landmark bui ldings and transformed skylines across India in the cities of Bengaluru, 
Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Trivandrum, and GIFT City with developments across Residential, 
Office, Retail, Hospitality and Education Sectors.
