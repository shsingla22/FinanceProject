Revolt Motors Kickstarts  Operations  in Sri Lanka  
New Delhi, 2 8th November 2024:  Revolt Motors, India’s largest  electric motorcycle company , made 
its highly anticipated debut in Sri Lanka with the launch of its flagship models, the RV400 and RV400 
BRZ. Hosted at the prestigious Water’s Edge, the event marked a significant milestone in Sri Lanka’s 
journey toward greener and more innovative transportation solutions.  With the first dealership 
already operational at Maradana Road, Colombo , Revolt Motors plans to establish a robust 
presence across the country by opening additional dealerships in 11 strategic locations, including 
Kurunegala, Madurankuliya, Matara, Embilipitiya, Elpitiya, Batticaloa, Kirindiwela, Horana, Kelaniya, 
and Tangalle.  
            
The launch was graced by the presence of Mrs. Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of RattanIndia  
Enterprises  Pvt Ltd , alongside  Mr. Bob Kundanmal , Chairman  of Evolution Auto , the exclusive 
distributor of Revolt Motors in Sri Lanka . The dignitaries spoke passionately about the future of 
electric mobility and Revolt’s pioneering role in redefining the two -wheeler segment with advanced 
technology and a focus on sustainability.  
            

   
Revolt’s flagship model, the RV400, is a game -changer in the electric motorcycle landscape. 
Designed to meet the needs of Sri Lankan riders, it offers a range of up to 1 60 km on a single charge 
and a top speed of 85 km/h, making it perfect for both urban commuting and longer journeys. With 
features such as customizable riding modes (Eco, City, and Sport), AI -enabled connectivity, and 
mobile app integration, the RV400 combines performance, innovation, and affordability.  
Mrs. Anjali Rattan , Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises Pvt Ltd.  emphasized the significance of 
the launch, stating, “The RV400 is more than just a motorcycle; it’s a revolution in mobility that 
bridges the gap between sustainability and modernity. This is a defining moment not only for Revolt 
Motors but for Sri Lanka’s shift toward a greener future.” She highlighted the brand’s commitment 
to empowering individuals and communities with cleaner, more efficient alternatives to traditional 
vehicles  and the potential of electric vehicles to reduce carbon emissions while providing riders with 
cost-effective, high -performance transportation options . 
Mr. Bob Kundanmal, Chairman of Evolution Auto , expressed his excitement about introducing 
Revolt’s transformative electric motorcycles to Sri Lanka, calling it a pivotal step toward 
revolutionizing the country’s mobility landscape. He emphasized that the partnership with Atman 
Group and Sino Lanka Private Limited is poised to accelerate EV adoption, supported by strong 
infrastructure and dedicated after -sales service.  
The RV400 is equipped with an IP67 -rated battery, ensuring durability in Sri Lanka’s varied weather 
conditions, and features such as dual disc brakes, a combined braking system, and adjustable 
suspension for a smooth and safe ride on diverse terrains. Beyond its practical features, the RV400 
sets itself apart with advanced AI -enabled functions, including real -time alerts, geo -fencing, reverse 
mode, and keyless ignition. Riders can enjoy a seamless integration of technology into their daily 
commutes, with features like navigation assistance, call alerts, and remote start accessible through 
the mobile app.  
The launch event, attended by industry leaders, media, and motorcycle enthusiasts, featured live 
demonstrations that showcased the RV400’s capabilities.  The launch of the RV400 and RV400 BRZ 
symbolizes the dawn of a new era in Sri Lanka’s transportation history. With its perfect combination 
of style, affordability, and cutting -edge technology, Revolt Motors invites Sri Lankans to elevate their 
ride and join the global movement toward sustainable mobility.  
Motorcycle enthusiasts can visit authorized dealerships to explore more about the RV400 and 
RV400 BRZ .
