1. Food Safety Risk Safety issues arising from 
contamination of food in our dine-in 
restaurants or in food delivered for  
at-home consumption can lead to 
severe reputational risk for the 
organization.The Company maintains standard 
operating procedures ('SOP'), which are 
in compliance with all local and applicable 
regulations. The Company continues to 
strengthen its food safety and quality 
assurance framework through enhanced 
testing protocols, regular vendor 
compliance assessments, employee 
training and certification programmes, 
periodic audits, facility inspections, 
and adherence to applicable regulatory 
guidelines. In addition, consumer 
awareness and education initiatives have 
been undertaken across digital and in-
store platforms to reinforce transparency 
and trust. The Company also continues to 
work closely with regulatory authorities 
and supply chain partners to maintain 
high standards of food quality, safety, and 
hygiene across operations.Negative: 
Failure to maintain food 
safety standards may result 
in increased compliance

which could adversely affect 
sales performance and long-
term brand equity.
2. Waste 
ManagementOpportunity Food and packaging wastage pose 
disposal and potentially severe 
pollution problems.
The Company collects plastic waste 
in line with the Extended Producer 
Responsibility (EPR) plan submitted to 
Pollution Control Board. The Company 
has also completed the EPR targets 
with the help of third party recycler for 
the reporting period.
Waste oil generated at the restaurants 
is disposed through designated vendors 
for production of bio-diesel and other 
approved by-products.
E-waste generated from restaurant 
operations, corporate offices and IT 
infrastructure is disposed through 
authorised recyclers and vendors in 
compliance with applicable e-waste 
management regulations. The Company 
follows responsible disposal practices 
to ensure safe handling, recycling and 
environmentally sound disposal of 
electronic waste.
Paper waste generated is disposed

Effective waste management
