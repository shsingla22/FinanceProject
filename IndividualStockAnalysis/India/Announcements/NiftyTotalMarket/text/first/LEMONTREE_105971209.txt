Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in  
Amritsar  
 
New Delhi, August  30th, 202 4: Lemon Tree Hotels announced its latest 
signing  – Lemon Tree Hotel, Amritsar . The property , which shall be managed by 
Carnation Hotels Private Limited , a wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree 
Hotels Limited,  is expected to open in  FY 202 8.  
 
Amritsar, in Punjab, is rich in history, culture, and spirituality. Known primarily 
as the home of the Golden Temple, the holiest shrine in Sikhism, the city 
attracts millions of pilgrims and tourists every year. It is also steeped in 
historical significance, with landmarks like the Jallianwala Bagh memorial 
commemorating the tragic massacre during British rule. Amritsar's vibrant 
markets, delicious cuisine, an d warm hospitality make it a must -visit destination 
for those seeking a deep connection to India's cultural and spiritual heritage.  
 
Lemon Tree Hotel, Amritsar  will feature 68 well-appointed rooms, a  restaurant, a 
rooftop bar, two  banquet  halls, meeting ro oms, a fitness center  and a swimming 
pool. The Sri Guru Ram Das Jee International Airport  is about  19 kms from the 
property while the Amritsar  Railway Station  is 9 kms away. The hotel is also well 
connected by roadways for both public and private transport . 
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels commented, “We are excited to share that we've signed our 
fifth upcoming property in Punjab, further underscoring our commitment to 
delivering exceptional hospitality and memorable experiences for our guests. 
This new venture highlights our dedication to growing our presence in this 
vibrant region, providing trave llers with a seamless blend of comfort and 
authentic local charm.”

midscale and economy segments. LTHL delivers differentiated yet superior 
service offerings, w ith a compelling value proposition. The group offers seven

Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Selec t by Lemon Tree Hotels and Keys

are located across metro regions, including the NCR, Mumbai, Kolkata, 
Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Chennai, as well as numerous tier I, II and III cities 
such as Pune, Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, Jaipur, Indore, Aurangabad, Uda ipur, 
Visakhapatnam, Kochi, Ludhiana, Thiruvananthapuram, Vijayawada etc. The 
company expanded internationally with hotels opening in Dubai in December 
2019, in Bhutan in February 2020 and in Nepal in April 2024.
