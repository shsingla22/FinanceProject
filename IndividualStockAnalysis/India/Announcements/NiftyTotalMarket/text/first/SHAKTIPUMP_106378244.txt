Shakti Pumps Empanelled for 34,720 Off -Grid Solar Pumps by MSEDCL;  
25,578 SPWPS Already Selected by Farmers Across Maharashtra  
Indore , Madhya Pradesh (India) , 19 September 2025 – Shakti Pumps (India) Limited (SPIL) [BSE: 531431, NSE: 
SHAKTIPUMP], a leading manufacturer of energy -efficient pumps and motors, is pleased to announce continued 
progress under its empanelment with the Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Company Limited (MSEDCL).  
 
Following the Letter of Empanelment dated 12th August 2025 for the supply and installation of  34,720 Off -Grid DC 
Solar Photovoltaic Water Pumping Systems (SPWPS)  under the  Magel Tyala Saur Krushi Pump Yojana / PM KUSUM B, 
Shakti Pumps has now successfully completed three tranches of farmer bookings.  
• First Tranche (10,000 SPWPS): Opened on 27th August 2025, fully booked within 1.5 hours.  
Order Value: Approx.  Rs. 268.88 Crores (inclusive of GST).  
• Second Tranche (12,451 SPWPS): Opened subseque ntly, also fully booked.  
Order Value: Approx.  Rs. 347.41 Crores (inclusive of GST).  
• Third Tranche (3, 127 SPWPS): Recently opened and fully booked by farmers.  
Order Value: Approx.  Rs. 86.40  Crores (inclusive of GST).  
 
With  25,578 SPWPS already selected by farmers across three tranches valuing the total order to Rs. 702.69  Crores  
(inclusive of GST), Shakti Pumps is well on track to fulfill its empanelled commitment of 34,720 SPWPS, reinforcing its 
leadership in delivering sustainab le water solutions at scale.  
 
Mr. Dinesh Patidar, Chairman of Shakti Pumps (India) Limited, shared his thoughts on this achievement: “We are very 
happy to see how quickly farmers are choosing Shakti Pumps. It shows they trust our products and services. With our 
strong presence in Maharashtra and experience in delivering large projects, we are confident we will complete this 
order on time and continue supporting farmers with reliable solar water pumping solutions.”  
 
About Shakti Pumps (India) Limited  
SPIL founded in 1982 as a partnership firm and later converted to a public limited company in 1995, manufactures solar pumps, 
energy -efficient stainless -steel submersible pumps, pressure booster pumps, pump -motors, and other products. SPIL is the only 
company that manufactures a wide range of products for solar pump installation in -house, including Variable Frequency Drives, 
Structures, Motors, Invertors, and so on. Pithampur, Madhya Pradesh, is home to two manufacturing facilities with a combined 
capacity of 5 00,000 pumps and motors per year (India). Shakti Pumps is at the forefront of sustainable innovation and reliability 
in solar pumping solutions, while also being environmentally responsible. The company has been at the forefront of transforming the agricul ture sector through solar pump technology. All Shakti submersible pumps are based on Stainless Steel 
(SS), which is a testimony to the latest technology and quality in manufacturing. Notably, Shakti Pumps has the distinction o f 
being India’s first 5 -star r ated pump manufacturer, supplying its products to more than 100 countries across the globe and 
manufacturing its own solar pumps, motors, structures, controllers & VFDs. Shakti Pumps is committed to helping India meet it s 
energy goals.
