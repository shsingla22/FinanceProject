Madam/Sir,  
Sub: Outcome of the Board Meeting held today i.e.,  19th March, 2026 
1. In furtherance to our disclosures dated 2nd March 2023 and 11th April, 2023 intimating you about the  
Manufacturing -cum-Maintenance Agreement executed with the Ministry of Railways, Government of 
India (GOI)  (MCMA) in consortium with Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL)  and in terms of 
Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations 2015, it is hereby informed that the Board of Directors of Titagarh Rail 
Systems Limited (TRSL) at its meeting held today i.e.,  19th March, 2026  has approved formation of a 
Joint Venture Company with BHEL as a special purpose vehicle for Maintenance Obligations of   Vande 
Bharat Trains to be supplied by the (TRSL - BHEL) Consortium in accordance with the MCMA  under 
Make -in-India initiative for Atma Nirbhar Bharat program of GOI,   subject to regulatory approvals 
required in this regard and authorised Shri Umesh Chowdhary, Vice Chairman and Managing Director 
of the Company to finalise the Joint Venture Agreement  (JVA) with such modifications, if any as may 
be necessary  and to nominate TRSL’s representative  for signing the JVA.  
  
The disclosure as required under SEBI Listing Regulations read with Schedule III and SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/  
CFD/CFDPoD -1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023, SEBI Circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 
dated November 11, 2024 and SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD -PoD-2/CIR/P/2024/185 dated 
December 31, 2024  shall be submitted upon execution of the JVA.  
  
2. The Board has also approved furnishing of  a corporate guarantee in favor of Oil and Natural Gas 
Corporation  Ltd. (ONGC) for and on behalf of Titagarh Naval Systems Limited  (Wholly Owned 
Subsidiary)  (TNSL) for bidding by TNSL in the tender floated by ONGC . 
The disclosure as required under SEBI Listing Regulations read with Schedule III and SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/  
CFD/CFDPoD -1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023, SEBI Circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 
dated November 11, 2024 and SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD -PoD-2/CIR/P/2024/185 dated 
December 31, 2024  is given  in Annexure – 1 to this letter.  
The meeting commenced at 02.30 P.M. and concluded at 03.00 P.M.
