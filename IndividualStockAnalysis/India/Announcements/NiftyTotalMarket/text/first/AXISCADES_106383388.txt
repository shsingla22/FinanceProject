MEDIA RELEASE  
AXISCADES Receives Fifth Consecutive Bombardier Diamond Supplier 
Award  
AXISCADES -India’s Pride on the Global Aerospace Stage  
 
Bengaluru, India  / Montreal, Canada,  September 25, 2025 : AXISCADES Technologies 
Ltd.  (BSE:  532395  | NSE:  AXISCADES ), a leading player in Aerospace, Defence, Electronics, 
Semiconductors, AI and an end -to-end engineering and technology solutions provider, is proud  
to announce a signiﬁcant achievement that underscores the success of a strategic  initiative  with 
Bombardier since  2010. AXISCADES has been awarded the prestigious Bombardier Diamond 
Supplier recognition  for the ﬁfth consecutive year  in the  Indirect Goods and Services (IGS)  – 
Engineering Services category . This award is a testament to our consistent operational 
performance .   
The award was presented at a ceremony held on 15th September in Montreal, Canada, and it 
represents the highest level of recognition for operational performance (in the areas of quality 
and delivery) and competitiveness levels needed to qualify for Bombardier Diamond Supplier 
status  among the global suppliers.  
Commenting on this milestone, Mr. KP Mohanakrishnan, Dy . CEO and President of Aerospace at 
AXISCADES, said  “We are honoured to receive the Diamond Supplier Award from Bombardier for the 
ﬁfth consecutive year, highlighting our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction. We take 
immense pride in our team's dedication in India and Canada. This recognition inspires us to continue 
delivering world- class solutions as we strive to become a US $1bn company. We extend our gratitude 
to Bombardier for their continued partnership and l ook forward to deepening our collaboration in the 
years to come. ” 
AXISCADES’ partnership with Bombardier spans more than a decade covering niche areas of 
aerospace engineering and advanced engineering services. The strong growth in the aerospace 
sector presents exciting new avenues for collaboration and engagement . We remain 
committed to investing in innovation, talent, and capabilities that will allow us to further strengthen our partnerships and deliver world -class solutions for the future.
