The impugned judgment / order dated June 27, 
2023 of  the Single Judge was challenged by the 
WBS IDC and the Industries Department by filing 
Writ Appeals before the Division Bench of the 
Calcutta High Court seeking reversal of the 
Single Judge judgment / order including stay 
during the pendency of Writ Appeal s.

3, 2024 and t he Division Bench of Calcutta High 
Court dismissed the Writ Appeal s filed by 
WBS IDC.  The Hon’ble Court has observed that it 
will be well open to the State to seek review of the Single Judge’s order, if so advised.  
 The Company is of the view that limitation for filing the review is over and further review has a 
very limited scope wherein only error apparent 
on the face of record of the court and no new 
grounds can be looked into.
