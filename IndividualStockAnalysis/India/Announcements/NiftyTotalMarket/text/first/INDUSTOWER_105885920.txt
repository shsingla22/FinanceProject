Vodafone sells 18.0% stake in Indus Towers
 
Vodafone Group Plc ("Vodafone") announces that it has sold 484.7 million shares in Indus Towers Limited ("Indus"),
representing 18.0% of Indus' share capital through an accelerated book-build offering ("Placing").
 
The Placing raised INR 153.0 billion (€1.7 billion) in gross proceeds which will be used to substantially repay Vodafone's
existing lenders in relation to the outstanding bank borrowings of €1.8 billion secured against Vodafone's Indian assets.
 
Following the Placing, Vodafone holds 82.5 million shares in Indus, equivalent to a 3.1% shareholding.
