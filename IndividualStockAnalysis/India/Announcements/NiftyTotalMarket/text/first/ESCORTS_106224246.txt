Faridabad, May 08, 2025: Escorts Kubota Limited (EKL) today reported Revenue from continuing 
operations for the year ended March 2025 at z 10,187.0 crore up by 4.7% as against z 9,730.7 crore in the 
previous year. EBIDTA from continuing operations came at z 1,177.8 crore up 4.0% as against z 1,133.0 
crore in the previous year. 
Standalone PBT excluding exceptional items and including discontinued operations came at z 1,555.3 crore 
up by 7.9% as against z 1,440.9 crore in previous year. Standalone Net Profit after tax including 
discontinued operations came at z 1,250.9 crore for the year ended March 31, 2025, up by 15.7% as against 
a profit of z 1,081.6 crore in the previous fiscal year. Standalone Earnings per share (EPS) for the year 
ended March 2025 at _z 113. 77 up by 19.0% as against z 95.59 in the previous fiscal year. 
For the quarter ended March 2025 for Standalone continuing operations, Revenue came at z 2,430.3 crore 
up by 6.1 % as against z 2,289.6 crore in corresponding quarter and as against z 2,935.4 crore in sequential 
quarter. EBIDTA came at z 292. 9 crore as against z 291.0 crore in the corresponding quarter and as against 
z 335.3 crore in sequential quarter. Profit before tax and exceptional items came at z 358.4 crore up by 
9.7% as against z 326.6 crore in corresponding quarter and z 380.2 crore in sequential quarter. During the 
quarter there is an adverse impact of z 27.1 crore on account of impairment of investment in an overseas 
wholly owned subsidiary company and in a domestic joint venture company. Net profit after tax came at z 
250.7 crore as against z 248.5 crore in corresponding quarter and as against z 290.5 crore in Sequential 
quarter. 
Net Profit after tax including discontinued operations came at z 297.5 crore quarter ended March 2025 up 
by 8.2% as against z 275.0 crore in corresponding quarter and as against z 323.2 crore in Sequential 
quarter.

On Consolidated basis, for the year ended March 2025 Revenue from Continuing operations came at z 
10;243.9 crore up by 4.5% as against z 9,803.6 crore in the previous fiscal year. Consolidated Net Profit 
After Tax from continuing operations came at z 1,124.1 crore up by 19.2% as against z 943.0 crore in the 
previous fiscal year. 
Consolidated Net Profit after tax including discontinued operations came at z 1,265.0 crore for the year 
ended March 2025, up by 17.5% as against z 1,076.6 crore in the previous fiscal year. Earnings per share 
(EPS) reported for the year ended March 2025 at z 115.04 up by 17.3% as against z 98.10 in the previous 
fiscal year. The Board has recommended a final dividend of 180% for the financial year '25, equivalent to 
Rs. 18.0/-per share. With the interim dividend already paid, the total payout for FY2025 will amount to 
Rs. 28.0/-per share (face value of z 10 each), representing an increase of 56% compared to the previous 
year.

SEGMENT WISE PERFORMANCE 
Agri Machinery Products 
For the year ended March 2025, Tractors volume came at 1,15,554 units up by 1.0% as against 1,14,396 
units in the previous year. Segment revenue came at z 8,447.2 crore as against z 7,897.4 crore in the 
previous year. The EBIT margin for year ended March 2025 came at 10.7% as against 11.2% in the previous 
year. 
For the quarter ended March 2025, Tractors volumes came at 26,633 units up by 7.6% as against 24,747 
units in corresponding quarter and as against 32,556 units in sequential quarter. Segment revenue came 
at z 1,974.8 crore in quarter ended March 2025 as against z 1,776.7 crore in corresponding quarter and 
as against z 2,416.6 crore in sequential quarter. EBIT margin for the quarter ended March 2025 came at 
11.4% as against 11.5% in corresponding quarter and 10.4% in sequential quarter. 
Construction Equipment 
For the year ended March 2025, construction equipment volumes were at 6,484 units as against 7,141 
units in the previous year. Segment revenue came at z 1,730.1 crore as against z 1,818.2 crore in the 
previous year. EBIT margin for the year ended March 2025 were up at 9.9% as against 9.2% in the previous 
year. 
For the quarter ended March 2025, Construction Equipment's sales volume was at 1,719 units as against 
1,958 units in corresponding quarter and as against 1,989 units.in sequential quarter. Segment revenue 
came at z 453.9 crore in quarter ended March 2025 as against z 505.8 crore in corresponding quarter and 
as against z 515.7 crore in sequential quarter. EBIT margin for the quarter ended March 2025 were at 9.1 % 
as against 11.0% in corresponding quarter and 11.0% in sequential quarter. 
Railway Equipments (Discontinued operations) 
For the year ended March 2025, Revenue from Railway Equipments Segment came at z 912.8 crore as 
against z 950.4 crore in the previous year. PBT for the year ended March 2025 came at z 188. 7 crore up 
by 5.6% as against z 178. 7 crore in the previous year. 
For the quarter ended March 2025, Segment revenue came at z 256.5 crore as against z 213.4 crore in 
corresponding quarter and as against z 200.4 crore in sequential quarter. PBT for the quarter ended March 
2025 came at z 62.7 crore as against z 35.6 crore in corresponding quarter and z 43.8 crore in sequential 
quarter.
