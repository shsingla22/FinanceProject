Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
This is to inform that there was a sudden breakdown in one of the three kilns located at 
Rajgangpur, Odisha on 13th March 2026. The impacted kiln provides a major share of the total 
clinker consumption in our grinding units located in Odisha, West Bengal and Jharkhand. Post 
necessary repairs and maintenance, the kiln was successfully relit, and we started getting clinker 
output from 27th March 2026. Our operations have now stabilized and clinker dispatch to all 
grinding units is returning to normal. 
 
As a result of this breakdown that lasted 14 days, and also due to the fact that we were already 
operating at low inventory levels, cement sales in our key eastern markets have been impacted 
by almost 2.5 ~ 3.0 Lac Tons in March 2026. 
 
We regret the disruption in supplies to our esteemed channel partners during this peak demand 
period due to this unforeseen event. We would like to reassure them that all necessary measures 
are being undertaken to normalize our supply chains at the earliest. 
 
The aforesaid event does not qualify to be a material event in terms of regulation 30 of SEBI 
(LODR) Regulations, 2015 read with Industry Standards Note on Regulation 30, however, this 
intimation is being made voluntarily out of caution for information of all concerned stakeholders. 
 
This is for your information and records.
