Sub: Subsidiary Updates - Sale of 27.89% stake in Ojaha Renewables Private Limited (ORPL) by 
CIE Hosur Limited (a Wholly Owned Subsidiary Company). 
 
Dear Sir /Madam,  
 
This is further to our letter dated 29th January, 2024 and 25th April, 2024 wherein the Company 
had informed that: (i) CIE Hosur Limited (CHL), a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, had 
executed Share Subscription and Shareholders Agreement on 29th January, 2024 (SSSHA), under 
which CHL agreed to subscribed upto 27.89% of the equity share capital of Ojaha Renewables 
Private Limited (ORPL) and invest an amount upto Rs. 16.2 million towards subscription of equity 
share capital for the procurement of green energy from captive solar power generating plants 
to be set up by ORPL and (ii) Accordingly, on 25th April, 2024 ORPL allotted 2,02,500 equity shares 
of Rs. 10/- each to CHL, constitutes 27.89% of the Paid-up Equity Share Capital of ORPL and ORPL 
become an Associate of CHL. 
 
It is hereby informed that in accordance with the terms of SSSHA and the Letter Agreement dated 
25th June 2026 executed between the Parties, CHL has disinvested and sold all the 2,02,500 equity 
shares of ORPL held by CHL, for a consideration of Rs. 1,62,00,000 (Rupees One Crore Sixty-Two 
Lakh Only) to CSE Development (India) Private Limited. Consequently, ORPL shall cease to be an 
Associate of CHL and the Company.
