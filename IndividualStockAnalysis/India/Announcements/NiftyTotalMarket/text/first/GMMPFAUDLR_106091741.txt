Pfaudler GmbH to establish a manufacturing facility in Poland 
 
Mumbai, December 23, 2024: GMM Pfaudler Limited through its subsidiary  Pfaudler GmbH, Germany, has entered 
into an Investment and Shareholders’ Agreement on December 20, 2024, to acquire 51% stake of a limited liability 
company in Poland. 
• This acquisition aims to establish a manufacturing and engineering facility in Poland, enhancing  our 
global manufacturing capabilities and competitiveness. 
• The acquired entity specializes in the manufacturing of high-quality stainless-steel equipment  for the 
pharmaceutical and food industries. 
• Investment of PLN 12.2 million (~ ₹25.3 crore) will be made to establish this facility and the transaction 
will be funded through internal accruals 
 
Management Comment  
Mr. Thomas Kehl, CEO International Business  said:  "This acquisition reflects our continued focus on delivering cost-
effective, high-quality solutions for our customers. Establishing this fac ility in Poland aligns with our vision of 
strengthening our market presence in Europe and beyond."
