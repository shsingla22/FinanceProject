▪ In 2QFY27, Tata Steel India crude steel production was 6.21 million tons. Crude steel p roduction was up 8% QoQ 
and 10% YoY , primarily driven by higher output at Jamshedpur and Kalinganagar facilities. On half year basis, 
production rose 10% YoY to 11.97 million tons.  
▪ Tata Steel India deliveries for the quarter stood at 5.97 million tons. Deliveries were up 1 5% QoQ and 7% YoY, 
supported by enriched product mix and stable demand across market segments despite  the seasonal rains. On 
half year basis, deliveries rose 8% YoY to 11. 14 million tons.  
▪ Key highlights of domestic deliveries  for the quarter : 
o ‘Automotive & Special Products’  vertical  achieved ‘best -ever 2Q’ volumes of ~ 1.1 million tons . Volumes grew 
9% QoQ and 19% YoY, driven by the ramp -up of Continuous Annealing and Galvanising lines  at Kalinganagar 
and Combi mill at  Jamshedpur . 
o ‘Branded Products & Retail’  vertical  achieved ‘best -ever quarterly ’ volumes of ~ 2.2 million tons , supported by 
strong performance of well -established brands  and continued market penetration . Tata Tiscon achieved ‘best -
ever quarterly ’ volumes and grew 7% QoQ  while Tata Kosh , our galvanised  brand, grew 25% QoQ . 
o ‘Construction & Solutions’ vertical achieved volumes of ~0.6 million tons, with ready -to-use solutions registering 
growth of 32% YoY. ‘Engineering and Packaging’ vertical achieved volumes of ~0. 4 million tons , up 12% QoQ  
driven by higher sales to Oil & Gas .  
o Our downstream sales including Cold rolled, Galvanised,  Tubes , Tinplate , Wires and Colors continue d to grow . 
Colors achieved ‘best -ever quarterly’ volumes, up 34% QoQ . 
o Gross Merchandise Value from our e -commerce platforms, Tata Steel Aashiyana  and DigECA , was  Rs 2,860 
crores  for the quarter , up 30% QoQ .  
▪ Tata Steel Netherlands liquid steel production in 2QFY 27 was ~1.52 million tons and deliveries were ~1.30 million 
tons. The Direct Sheet Plant restarted in Aug ’26 and is operating at rated capacity under the agreed framework 
with local regulatory authorities. On half year basis, production was 3.07 mn tons and deliveries were 2.70 mn tons.  
▪ Tata Steel UK is serving its customers via downstream processing of purchased substrate. Deliveries  for the quarter  
were 0.38 million tons , impacted by inventory buildup  in the  UK supply chain ahead of the implementation of new 
safeguard measures on 01st July 2026. Separately, w e have fully ramped up production at Llanwern pickle line.  
▪ Tata Steel Thailand saleable steel production was 0.32 million tons and deliveries stood at  0.31 million tons for the 
quarter . Deliveries were marginally lower QoQ , primarily due to heavy rains in the month of September 2026.

About Tata Steel  
• Tata Steel group is among the top global steel companies with an annual crude steel capacity of 36 million tonnes per 
annum.  
• It is one of the world's most geographically diversified steel producers, with operations and commercial presence across 
the world.  
• The group recorded a consolidated turnover of around US$26 billion in the financial year ending March 31, 2026.  
• Tata Steel Limited, together with its subsidiaries, associates, and joint ventures, is spread across five continents with an 
employee base of over 77,000.
