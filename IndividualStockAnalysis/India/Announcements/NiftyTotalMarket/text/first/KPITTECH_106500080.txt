made by the Company. Further, I am not involved in the day-to-day activities of the
Company.
3.I hereby confirm that I am eligible for such reclassification as public shareholders

any other applicable law.

i am and the persons related to me (as defined by sub-clause (i), and (ii) of sub­
clause (pp) of sub-regulation (1) of Regulation 2 of the SEBI (Issue of Capital and
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018):
i. together, do not hold more than 10 (ten) percent of the total voting rights
of Company;
ii. do not exercise control over the affairs of the Company, whether directly or
indirectly;
iii. do not have any special rights with respect to the Company through formal
or informal arrangements, including through any shareholder agreements;
iv. are not represented in any capacity on the board of directors of the
Company (including through any nominee director);
v. are not acting as key managerial personnel in the Company;
vi. are not 'wilful defaulters' as per the Reserve Bank of India guidelines; and
vii. are not fugitive economic offenders.
4.I also undertake to abide by the conditions listed in Regulation 31A(4) of the Listing
Regulations after reclassification as public shareholders of the Company pursuant
to the approval of such reclassification by the Company and the Stock Exchanges,
failing which, I shall automatically be reclassified as promoters/ persons belonging 
to the promoter group, as applicable. 
5.I am requesting you to consider and approve application for reclassification from
the 'Promoter and Promoter Group' category to the 'Public' category of shareholders
of the Company. Further, I am requesting that the required applications be made to
Stock Exchanges to give effect to the reclassification in accordance with Regulation
31A of the Listing Regulations.
