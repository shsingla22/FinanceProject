Supriya Lifescience Reports 14.8% Revenue Growth in H1 FY27  
 
Mumbai, 6th November 2026: The unaudited financial statements for Q2FY27 have been 
released by Supriya Lifescience Ltd., a cGMP -compliant business with a strong track record in 
API manufacturing and a focus on products from a variety of therapeutic segments, including 
anti -histamine , anti -allergic, vitamin, anaesthetic, and anti -asthmatic. The company has spread 
its business in more than 120 countries across the globe.

Key Highlights for Q1 FY27:  
In Q2 FY27, Supriya Lifescience Ltd. recorded revenue from operations of Rs 206.30 crore, up 
3.2% year -on-year from Rs 199.83 crore in Q2 FY26.  
EBITDA for Q2 FY27 stood at Rs 45.40 crore, with an EBITDA margin of 22.0%, compared to Rs 
72.65 crore and a margin of 36.4% in the corresponding quarter of the previous financial year.  
The reasons for impact in EBITDA are as follows:  
Firstly, the regulatory changes in LATAM market affected demand for some of our higher -margin 
products. This resulted in a shift in our product and geographic mix, with a higher share of sales 
coming from semi -regulated markets during the quarter.  

Secondly, we incurred higher operating expenses at our Ambernath facility, which is currently in 
the ramp -up phase. As commercial production and sales from the facility scale up  in coming 
quarters, we expect to see better absorption of these costs and an improvement in operating 
leverage.  
Thirdly, due to the ongoing war and geopolitical tensions, raw material and solvent prices 
remained high which also impacted our margins during the quarter.  
The Profit After Tax (PAT) for Q2 FY27 stood at Rs 29.25 crore, against Rs 50.43 crore in Q2 FY26. 
The PAT margin was 14.2% during the quarter, compared to 25.2% in the corresponding period 
last year.  
For the first half of FY27, the Company recorded revenue of Rs. 396.04 crore, registering a growth 
of 14.8% over Rs. 344.91 crore in H1 FY26. EBITDA stood at Rs. 92.86 crore, while PAT was Rs. 
53.29 crore during the period.  
The Company's therapeutic portfolio continued to diversify during H1 FY27. Vitamins increased 
their contribution to revenue to 16% from 12% in H1 FY26, while the analgesic segment's 
contribution rose from 2% to 6%. Anaesthetics remained the largest therape utic segment, 
accounting for 48% of revenue, while anti -asthmatics contributed 8%.  
Europe emerged as the Company's largest geographical market in Q2 FY27, contributing 43% of 
revenue compared to 37% in Q2 FY26. Asia accounted for 36% of revenue, followed by LATAM 
at 12%, North America at 5% and other markets at 4%. Exports continued to c ontribute 
approximately 80% of Q2 FY27 revenue, reflecting the Company's diversified international 
presence.  
The Company's backward -integrated business model continued to support its operations, with 
20 products across five therapeutic categories. These products accounted for 74% of H1 FY27 
revenue. The model enables greater control over raw material supplies, re duces exposure to 
market price fluctuations and supports manufacturing efficiency.  
During the quarter, the Company strengthened its product portfolio with the launch of two new 
anaesthetic liquid inhalation products from its Ambernath  facility. Scale -up of these products is 
expected over the coming quarters. The Company is also progressing with its plans for 
approximately two product launches in the ADHD segment during FY27. Its existing ADHD 
product continues to scale up, supported by  demand from LATAM and Europe.  
The Ambernath  facility has commissioned its finished dosage manufacturing capabilities across 
tablets, capsules, liquids, nasal, inhalation and injectable dosage forms. Commercial 
manufacturing has commenced, and the Company has received an exhibit batch order for Rest  of 
the World (RoW) markets. Validation and registration batches are also underway for dossier 
submissions. An EU audit for the facility is scheduled for November 2026.  
Supriya Lifescience operates five manufacturing blocks with a combined reactor capacity of 932 
KLPD across a manufacturing area of approximately 35,000 sq. metres. Capacity utilisation stood 
at 73% during H1 FY27. The Company continues to strengthen its ma nufacturing infrastructure 
through investments in quality control laboratories, warehousing, research facilities and other 
supporting infrastructure.  
The Company continues to strengthen its R&D capabilities through a team of over 68 scientists 
focused on API and formulation process development. Its two R&D centres at Ambernath and 
Lote support new product development, formulation improvements and lifecycle enhancements. 
With four process patents filed in India, the Company continues to target the addition of 3 –4 
products annually.  
Dr. Satish Wagh, Executive Chairman and Whole -Time Director, Supriya Lifescience Ltd., 
commenting on the results, said, “Q2 FY27 reflects continued revenue growth and the progress 
we are making in expanding our product portfolio and manufacturing capabilities. During the 
quarter, we launched two new anaesthetic liquid inhalation products fro m our Ambernath  
facility, marking another step in strengthening our finished dosage capabilities. Our focus 
remains on scaling new products, expanding our presence across therapeutic segments and 
strengthening our R&D and manufacturing infrastructure. With our diversifie d global presence, 
backward -integrated business model and ongoing investments in new capabilities, we remain 
committed to building a stronger and more diversified business for the future.”

About Supriya Lifescience Ltd.:  
Supriya Lifescience Ltd., a world -renowned producer of active pharmaceutical ingredients, was 
founded in 1987. Our state -of-the-art facility has certifications from Health Canada, EUGMP, 
EDQM, USFDA, and NMPA. The company is headquartered in Mumbai, India,  and our facility is 
located in Khed , District Ratnagiri. Our globally compliant facilities USFDA, EDQM, WHO, PMDA, 
KFDA and ANVISA are supported by robust R&D, eight active CEPs, and fourteen active USDMFs. 
EHS requirements and ethical principles guide our core business. The company's prima ry focus 
is to build intermediates and APIs for innovators and generic firms. We also exclusively 
collaborate with partners in the capacity of CMOs.
