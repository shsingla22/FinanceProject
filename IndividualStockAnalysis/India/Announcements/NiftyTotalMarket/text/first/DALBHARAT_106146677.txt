Dear Sir/Madam, 
We wish to inform you that Dalmia Cement (Bharat) Limited (“ DCBL”), a wholly owned subsidiary 
of the Company, has received order on February 10, 2025 under Section 122(1)(vii) of the Central 
Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 read with SGST Act and IGST Act from the Joint Commissioner, 
CGST & CX, Thane, confirming penalty of Rs. 9,20,24,387/- for allegedly availing ineligible ITC for 
the financial years 2017-20.  
DCBL has a good case on merits to defend the matter before the Appellate Authority and will 
appeal against the said orders before the Commissioner (Appeals)/file Writ Petition before High 
Court within prescribed timelines.
