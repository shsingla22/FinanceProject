NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL) wins 200 MW/ 800 MWh BESS capacity in WBSEDCL’s 500 
MW/ 2000 MWh Standalone BESS Tender 
 
New Delhi, 07th August 2026 
 
NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL), a subsidiary of NTPC Limited, has emerged as a successful 
bidder in the e-reverse auction conducted by the West Bengal State Electricity Distribution 
Company Limited (WBSEDCL) for the development of Standalone Battery Energy Storage System 
(BESS) projects in the state of West Bengal. The tender aimed at setting up of 500 MW/ 2000 MWh 
of standalone BESS capacity through a Tari Ư-Based Competitive Bidding (TBCB) process. 
Under the terms of the Request for Selection (RfS), NGEL has secured 200 MW/800 MWh of BESS 
capacity at a discovered tari Ư of ₹4.35 lakh per MW per month in the aforesaid tender.  
The e-reverse auction was concluded on August 7, 2026.
