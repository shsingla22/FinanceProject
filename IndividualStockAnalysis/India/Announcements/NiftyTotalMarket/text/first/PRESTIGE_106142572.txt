Bengaluru, 6th Feb,2025:  Prestige Estates Projects Limited (PEPL), one of India’s leading

The reaƯirmation underscores Prestige’s long-standing execution track record, strong

Crisil has factored in the company’s diverse presence across residential, commercial,

geographies.  
Strong Financial Position & Diversiﬁed Growth Strategy  
Prestige’s ﬁnancial strength was a key factor in the rea Ưirmation, with consolidated net

Prestige continues to focus on strategic expansion into new geographies, including
