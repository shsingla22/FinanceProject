Sub.:  Intimation of Receipt of Notifications of Award (NOAs) from NTPC Renewable Energy Limited 
for 500 MW Solar BOS Project in Rajasthan  
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Apropos the captioned subject and pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that KPI Green Energy Limited  (“the 
Company”) has received Notifications of Award (NOAs)  from NTPC Renewable Energy Limited 
(“NTPC REL”) , a subsidiary of NTPC Green Energy Limited, for the Balance of System (BOS) Package 
pertaining to the development of 500 MW Grid Connected Solar PV Projects at Bikaner, Rajasthan . 
 
The award is divided into two blocks of 300 MW and 200 MW  and includes the scope of supply of plant 
and equipment, inland transportation, insurance, installation, testing and commissioning, civil and 
allied works, along with comprehensive Operation and Maintenance services for 3 years from COD 
plus 10 -year AMC . The aggregate contract value is ₹621 crore, excluding GST , comprising ₹367 crore 
for the 300 MW block  and ₹254 crore for the 200 MW block . The contract agreement will be executed 
in accordance with the terms of the Notifications of Award.  
 
This project marks the Company’s significant  entry into Rajasthan , one of India’s most prominent 
renewable energy hubs. The award expands its geographical footprint  and supports its strategy of 
participating in large utility -scale solar opportunities  across renewable -rich states.  
 
This award further strengthens the Company’s positioning in India’s fast -growing renewable energy 
landscape. It aligns with the Government of India’s initiatives to expand green energy capacity and 
support sustainable infrastructure development. The Company believes this will contribute 
meaningfully to its future growth and stakeholder value creation.  
 
This is for your information and records.  
 
Thanking you,  
 
Yours faithfully,
