Press Release 
Capri Gold Loan launches Navratri Special Offer, announces 50% 
waiver on processing fees 
Four-day Navratri special offer from 12th October to 15th October, 2026, available across all Capri 
Gold Loan branches 
Mumbai, October 13, 2026:  Capri Global Capital Limited, also known by the brand name ‘Capri 
Loans’, one of the leading non-banking financial companies, today announced the launch of its  
‘Navratri Special Offer’ , a limited four-day festive initiative offering customers a 50% waiver on gold 
loan processing fees. 
The offer is available to all customers from October 12 to 15, 2026, seeking a gold loan from Capri 
Loans. The initiative aims to make gold loans more affordable during the festive season, enabling 
customers to utilise the financial value of their gold to meet their business and personal financial 
needs. 
The festive season often brings additional financial requirements for households, traders and small 
businesses. Through its Navratri Special Offer, Capri Loans is helping customers access funds against 
their gold at a lower upfront cost. To avail the offer simply visit the nearest Capri Gold Loans branch. 
Capri Gold Loan offers its Gold Loan services to customers through 1100+ branches across 16 States 
and UTs. It has served over 14.5 Lakh customers till date. 
About Capri Global Capital Limited: 
Capri Global Capital Limited (“Capri Loans”) is a well-diversified retail focused Non-Banking Financial 
Company listed on the BSE and the NSE. Capri Loans has an AUM of over Rs. 40,000 crores and serves 
its customers through 1,400+ branches with an employee base of 13,700+ as of June 30th, 2026 across 
India. Capri Loans offers a wide range of secured and collateralized loans across four primary lending 
segments – Gold Loans, MSME Loans, Construction Finance and Housing Loans offered through its 
100% subsidiary, Capri Global Housing Finance Limited (“CGHFL”). Capri Loans also has fee-based 
businesses such as insurance distribution through corporate agency 
license and distribution of car loans through its subsidiary Capri Loans Car Platform Private Limited 
(“CLCPPL”).
