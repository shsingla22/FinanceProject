PRESS RELEASE  
CONCOR AND TERI sign MOU for  Setting up Centre of Excellence (COE) for Green & Sustainable 
Logistics  
[New Delhi, 7th March 2025] – With an aim to promote Green & Sustainable Logistics, Container Corporation 
of India Ltd.(CONCOR) and The Energy and Resources Institute (TERI) have signed a Memorandum of 
Understanding (MOU) for Setting up Centre of Excellence (COE) for Green & Sustainable Logistics in TERI.  
The signing of the MoU was held on the sidelines of the World Sustainable Development Summit (WSDS) 
2025, TERI’s annual flagship event. The WSDS 2025, scheduled from 5th to 7th March at India Habitat 
Centre, New Delhi, focuses on the theme: ‘Partnerships f or Accelerating Sustainable Development and Climate 
Solutions”.  
The centre will be named as “ CONCOR -TERI Centre of Excellence for Green and Sustainable Logistics ”. 
The MoU signing ceremony took place at India Habitat Centre, Lodhi Road, New Delhi, India in the august 
presence of Shri Sanjay Swarup, CMD, CONCOR, Shri Ajit Kumar Panda, Director (Projects & 
Services),CONCOR and Dr. Vibha Dhawan, DG, TERI . The agreement was formally signed by Shri Ahmed 
Wasi Khan , Group General Manager(P&S) , CONCOR, and Shri  Sanjay  Seth, Sr. Director, 
Sustainable Infrastructure Programme,  TERI. 
           The centre will be an integral part of Transport and Urban Governance Division of TERI. Under this centre, 
TERI will undertake detailed research and policy development work in collaboration with CONCOR. The 
Collaboration seeks to maximize detail ed research and to find innovative solutions towards sustainable 
logistics, particularly relevant to the rail transportation, terminal operations, warehousing & storage 
management, greening of built infrastructure and to comply various legislative framewor ks. 
 
During the signing ceremony, Shri Sanjay Swarup , CMD , CONCOR, highlighted CONCOR’s ESG -focused 
initiatives, including deployment of 130  LNG trailers  for first mile last Mile service , 95% cargo movement 
through rail, Plan to use  electric vehicles  for within terminals , construction of  the 1st  net-zero warehouse of 
130,000 sq. ft. at Sriperumbudur, and utilizing solar panels at CONCOR terminals. These efforts underscore 
CONCOR’s commitment to green, sustainable, and environmentally responsible logistics . 
Shri Sanjay Seth, Sr. Director, Sustainable Infrastructure Programme, TERI, congratulated CONCOR and 
emphasized that it is at the forefront of promoting green and sustainable logistics, which can be both cost -
effective and environmentally friendly. He highlighted that this collaboration will help transform CONCOR’s 
warehouses into 5 -star-rated green infrastructure and contribute to India's National Logistics Policy goal of 
reducing logistics costs from 14% to 8% . He concluded that this collaboration will serve as a platform for 
driving research in sustainable logistics, benefiting stakeholders, industries, and policymakers.  
 
Key Highlights of MOU:  
1. Develop strategies, detailed research and policy development work for green logistics solutions, 
promoting environmentally responsible freight transportation, and exploring innovative technologies 
for reducing carbon footprint in logistics operations.  
2.  Develop tools and rating system to promote green initiatives in the warehousing and storage 
infrastructure, including development of GRIHA Rating for the same.  
3. Capitalize on commercialization opportunities in accordance with this agreement  
4. Necessary support in organising/co -organising Sustainable and Green Logistics related 
Events/Workshops etc.  
CONCOR firmly believes in contributing and adopting best ESG principles in all of its activities. The 
partnership with TERI is further step towards its commitment towards Green & Sustainable Logistics.
