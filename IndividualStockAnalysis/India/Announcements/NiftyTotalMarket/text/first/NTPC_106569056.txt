Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations,

governance, and climate responsibility. It underscores the company’s ongoing transition towards a 
cleaner energy portfolio and stronger ESG framework embedded at the management and Board levels.

prepared the report based on the data of the Company available in public domain.
