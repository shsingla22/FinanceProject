PRESS RELEASE 
NLCIL Signs MoU with IPICOL for Establishment of a 3200 MW (4x800 MW) 
Ultra Super Critical coal-based thermal power plant and Exploration of Coal 
Gasification-Based Fertilizer Production and Beach Sand Mineral Processing 
Facility in Odisha 
NLC India Limited (NLCIL) signed Memorandum of Understandings (MoUs) with the 
Industrial Promotion and Investment Corporation of Odisha Limited (IPICOL) at 
Mumbai on 22.10.2026 for: 
1. Coal Gasification project at Odisha and exploring opportunities for establishing 
linkages between coal gasification and fertilizer production . NLCIL will explore 
the technical feasibility of coal gasification and the production of synthesis gas 
(Syn-Gas) for potential applications in the fertilizer sector. 
2. Establishment of a Beach Sand mineral processing facility with a tentative 
investment of 2000 Cr and expected to generate potential employment of 500 
numbers, contributing to industrial development and socio-economic growth in 
the region. 
3. Development of 3200 MW (4x 800 MW) Ultra super-critical coal-based thermal 
power plant in Odisha. Involving a potential investment of Rs.35,000 crore. The 
proposed project is expected to generate employment opportunities for 
approximately 4,000 persons, contributing to the State's economic growth and 
industrial development. 
The MoUs were signed by Shri I.S. Jasper Rose, Executive Director (Mines & 
Land), NLCIL, and Smt. Aboli Sunil Naravane, IAS, Managing Director, IPICOL, in 
the presence of Shri.Mohan Charan Majhi, Hon'ble Chief Minister of Odisha, Shri. 
Hemant Sharma IAS Additional Chief Secretary , Industries Department , Energy, 
Govt. of Odisha and Dr.Prasanna Kumar Acharya, Chairman-cum-Managing 
Director (CMD), NLCIL. 
Through this initiative, NLCIL and IPICOL reaffirm their commitment to partnering 
in the i!7dustrial and infrastructure development of Odisha, strengthening the 
State's power infrastructure and supporting its long-term development objectives , 
to explore new avenues for integrating the coal and fertilizer sectors, supporting 
the Government of India's vision of Atmanirbhar Bharat while contributing to 
Odisha's industrial development and economic growth and promoting mineral­
based industries , generating employment and supporting the economic growth of 
Odisha.
