With reference to the captioned subject, we wish to inform that Aditya Birla Digital Fashion Ventures 
Limited (“ABDFVL”), a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, has today, i.e., September 17, 2026, 
acquired an additional 15.62% equity stake in its subsidiary, Imperial Online Services Private Limited 
(“Imperial”).  
 
Pursuant to the aforesaid acquisition, Imperial has become a wholly owned subsidiary of ABDFVL and 
consequently, step-down wholly owned subsidiary of the Company. 
 
Brief details in accordance with the SEBI Listing Regulations read with aforesaid SEBI Master Circular 
are annexed herewith as Annexure A .
