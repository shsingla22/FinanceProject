JOINT PRESS RELEASE  
Graphite India Limited and Kivoro Announce Exclusive Partnership to Commercialize 
Graphene -Based Heat Transfer Additives in India  
San Sebasti an, Spain / Bengaluru, India — 09.12.2025  — Graphite India Limited (“GIL”), one 
of India’s oldest and largest graphite materials companies, and Kivoro , a corporate spin -off of 
Graphenea and a leading innovator in graphene -based industrial additives, signed an Exclusive 
Distribution and Commercial  Partnership Agreement  to bring Kivoro’s next -generation 
Graphene -based Heat Transfer Additive (HTA)  technology to the Indian corrugated 
paperboard industry.  
Under the agreement, GIL becomes the exclusive distributor  of Kivoro’s Heat Transfer Additive 
across the Republic of India, focusing on deployment within the corrugated paperboard sector — 
a fast -growing, efficiency -driven industry where thermal performance improvements can 
significantly reduce energy consumption and costs.  
A Strategic Collaboration to  Accelerate Industrial Adoption  
Kivoro’s proprietary HTA is engineered to dramatically enhance heat transfer efficiency in 
corrugator plants, enabling higher throughput, reduced steam consumption, and improved board 
quality. Leveraging GIL’s strong industr y presence  and deep customer relationships  across 
India, the partnership aims to accelerate technology adoption and support the modernization of 
corrugated production lines nationwide.  
“This agreement marks an exciting milestone as we scale our graphene -enabled solutions globally,” 
said Jesus de la Fuente, CEO of Kivoro . “India is one of the world’s most dynamic corrugated 
markets, and GIL’s industrial legacy, customer trust, and technical expertise make them the ideal 
partner to bring our HTA technology to  producers who are pursuing energy efficiency, 
productivity, and sustainability.”  
Speaking on the partnership, Siddhant Bangur, Business Development Executive at Graphite 
India , added: “Graphite India has long been committed to introducing high -impact inno vations 
that deliver strong operational benefits to  the Indian market . Kivoro’s graphene -based Heat 
Transfer Additive is exactly the kind of breakthrough that the Indian industry  is seeking, and we 
look forward to bringing this world -class technology to partners across the corrugated value chain.”  
Advancing Sustainability and Efficiency in the Corrugated Industry  
The corrugated paperboard sector is rapidly modernizing amid rising energy costs and demand for 
higher -quality packaging. By incorporating graph ene-based thermal additives directly into their 
production processes, Indian manufacturers can unlock:  
 Higher machine speeds  
 Lower steam and energy consumption  
 Reduced production costs  
 Improved board quality and consistency  
 Shorter startup times and fewer defects  
The partnership reinforces both companies’ commitment to sustainability, technological 
advancement, and value creation  for industrial customers.  
About Kivoro  
Kivoro, a spin -off of Graphenea, specializes in advanced graphene -based additives engineer ed to 
enhance industrial processes through improved thermal, mechanical, and chemical performance. 
With a strong R&D foundation in nanomaterials and real -world industrial integration, Kivoro 
delivers scalable, plug -and-play solutions for energy -intensive s ectors worldwide.  
About Graphite India Limited (GIL)  
Graphite India Limited is India’s leading provider of graphite -based industrial solutions, with
