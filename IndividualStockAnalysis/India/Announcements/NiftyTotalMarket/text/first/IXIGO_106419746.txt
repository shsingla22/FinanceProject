Headline Results
Gross Transaction Value (GTV) crossed ₹43,474.97 Mn in Q2 FY26, growing by 23% YoY.
Flight GTV grew 29% YoY, Bus GTV rose 51% YoY while Train GTV grew 12% YoY for Q2 FY26 vs Q2 FY25.
Revenue From Operations grew by 37% YoY in Q2 FY26 to ₹2827.41 Mn from ₹2064.70 Mn in Q2 FY25.
Contribution Margin (CM) increased by 20% YoY, reaching ₹1095.84 Mn in Q2 FY26.
Adjusted EBITDA (EBITDA plus ESOP Expenses less Other Income) increased to ₹284.76 Mn for Q2 FY26, 
an increase of 36% from ₹209.96 Mn in Q2 FY25.
