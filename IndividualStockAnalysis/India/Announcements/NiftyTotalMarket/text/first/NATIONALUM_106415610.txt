Sub: Comments of Board on fine levied by the Exchanges. 
 
 
Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
This has reference to your mail dated 29.08.2025 informing imposition of fine of Rs.  
7,51,660/- (including GST @ 18% on basic fine amount of Rs. 6,37,000/-) for non-
compliance with provisions of Regulations 17(1) and 18(1) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 
2015. In this regard, we would like to submit the following: 
 
a) As advised, the matter regarding imposition of fine for non-compliance with provisions 
of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 was informed to the Promoter of the Company i.e. 
Government of India through Ministry of Mines, being the Administrative Ministry on 
08.09.2025 and request was made to expedite for early appointment of requisite 
number of Independent Directors to ensure compliance of all statutory requirements of 
SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. 
 
b) Further, the Company vide letter dated 08.09.2025 clarified to BSE and NSE that, 
NALCO is a CPSE and the authority for appointment of Directors is vested with the 
President of India and hence, the Company does not have any control on the same. 
Therefore, the non-compliances, stated in the e-mails dated 29.08.2025 for the quarter 
ended 30.06.2025 may be condoned and penalties imposed may be waived. 
 
c) In response to NALCO's clarification vide letter dated 08.09.2025, NSE vide its mail 
dated 09.09.2025 informed that the Company is Compliant with the Committee 
composition requirement and non-compliant with Composition of Board. Further NSE 
advised the Company to submit waiver application under the Composition of 
Committee along with waiver fee. Accordingly, waiver application was submitted to BSE 
on 18.09.2025. It is worth mentioning that Company will apply for waiver of penanty 
under Regulation 17, when the Company will be compliant with composition of Board 
as per provisions of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

d) The matter was also placed before the Board of Directors in their 359th meeting held 
on 25.09.2025. The Board took cognizance of the communications of BSE and NSE 
dated 29.08.2025, imposing fine of Rs. 7,51,660/- (including GST @18% on basic fine 
amount of Rs. 6,37,000/-) each for non-compliance with provisions of Regulations 17(1) 
and 18(1) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for the quarter ended 30.06.2025. The 
Board advised the Company to communicate BSE and NSE that, NALCO is a CPSE 
and the authority for appointment of Directors is vested with the President of India and 
hence, the Company does not have any control on the same. Therefore, the non-
compliances, stated in their e-mails dated 29.08.2025 for the quarter ended 30.06.2025 
may be condoned and penalties imposed by them for the quarter may be waived 
(Extract of minutes of 359th Meeting of Board of Directors is enclosed as Annexure ). 
 
e) Further, the Board also advised Chairman-cum-Managing Director to appraise the 
Administrative Ministry about the penalties being imposed by BSE and NSE due to 
absence of adequate number of the Independent Directors on Board and requesting 
for early appointment of the requisite number of the Independent Directors to comply 
the provisions under the Companies Act, 2013 and SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.  
 
f) It may be appreciated that appointment of Independent Directors is beyond the control 
of the Company, since all Directors are appointed by the President of India. The matter 
relating to early appointment of requisite number of Independent Directors is 
continuously being taken up with the Administrative Ministry for compliance with the 
provisions of Companies Act, 2013 and SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. In view of the 
above, it is requested that, waiver application submitted on 18.09.2025 may kindly be 
considered favourably and non-compliance with provisions of Regulations 17(1) under 
the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 by the Company for the quarter ended 30.06.2025 
may kindly be condoned and imposition of fine of Rs. 7,51,660/- (including GST @ 18% 
on basic fine amount of Rs. 6,37,000/-) in this regard may kindly be waived. 
 
Thanking you,
