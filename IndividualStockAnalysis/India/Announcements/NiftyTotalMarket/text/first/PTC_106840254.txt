PRESS RELEASE  
  
Results for Q2- FY2 7 
 
New Delhi, 2nd Nov 202 6: PTC India Limited, the leading provider of power trading solutions in India, 
announced its consolidated & standalone financial results for the second quarter  FY 202 6-27 end ing 
30th Sep 202 6. 
 
Summary of Standal one Financial Performance for Q 2-FY2 7 
• Trading volume grew  by 24% to 32.46 BUs in Q2- FY2 7. 
• The standalone Profit After Tax (P AT) in Q 2-FY27 is INR 1 13.23 Crores .  
• Consulting income for Q2-FY2 7 stood at  Rs 9.86  Crores  
• Earnings Per Share ( EPS) of  Rs 3.83  for Q2 - FY27   
Summary of Consolidated Financial Performance for Q 2-FY2 7 
• Consolidated Profit Before  Tax (P BT) in Q2-FY2 7 is INR 171.10 Crores . 
• Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT) in Q2-FY2 7 is INR 159.10 Crores.  
• Earnings Per Share ( EPS) of  Rs 4.85 for Q2 - FY27  
 
Management commentary:  
Commenting on the results, Dr. Manoj Kumar Jhawar , MD  & CEO , PTC India Ltd., said  
"Trades across different tenure s have  contributed to the growth of 24% in trading volume in Q 2-FY 2 7. The 
short -term  trades have contributed 65% of the volume and balance has been contributed by medium - & 
long -term  contracts.  The value adds in the form of energy market insights are provided to our consulting 
clients .  
The cross -border power from Bhutan has shown a significant dip due to high er domestic requirement in 
Bhutan leading to decrease in trading income to that extent. Power demand is expected to remain robust, 
albeit with seasonal variations, supporting firm short -term power prices.   With the introduction of market -
oriented  initiatives by CERC  and policy initiative s of the Ministry of Power on BESS and suitable fiscal 
incentives , the price is expected to soften as storage capacity gets onstream. With our pan India reach 
and innovative products & service s basket we expect to penetrate deeper into the opportunity space and 
maintain our leadership position. " 
 
 
 
 
 
About PTC India Limited  
PTC India Ltd., a Government of India initiative, is the pioneer in starting a power market in India. The 
Company has maintained its leadership position in power trading since inception. PTC has also been 
mandated by the Government of India to trade electr icity with Bhutan, Nepal and Bangladesh.  
 
The trading activities undertaken by PTC include long -term  trading of power generated from large 
power projects including renewables as well as short -term  trading arising as a result of  supply and 
demand mismatches, which inevitably occur in various regions of the country.  
 
The Company is credited with a number of products in the electricity trading market to meet the 
divergent  needs of the custome rs Driven by the vision of shaping a vibrant power market, PTC has 
been providing value -added services to its clients and built trust as a reliable partner. The electricity 
traded from cross -border  resources is a significant component of the total electricity traded by the 
Company.  
 
PTC is a unique example of a successful public -private partnership with major PSUs of the Ministry 
of Power, Government of India as promoters and a widely held ownership interest. The diverse 
composition of its Board of Directors includes eminent people as  independent directors .
