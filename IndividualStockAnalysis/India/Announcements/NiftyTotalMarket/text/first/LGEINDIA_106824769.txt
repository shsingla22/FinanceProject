LG Electronics India Limited earns Great Place to Work ®  
certification third year -in-a-row  
Recognition reflects LG Electronics India’s continued focus on building a workplace 
culture rooted in employee trust, engagement , and well-being 
New Delhi, October 22, 202 6: LG Electronics India Ltd. (LGE India), one of India’s leading consumer         
durables companies, has earned the Great Place To Work® Certification ™ third year in a row. Great Place 
To Work®, a global authority on workplace culture and employee experience , awarded LGE India this cer-
tification basis employee feedback through the Trust Index ™ survey, alongside an assessment of workplace 
culture. By drawing on employees’ perspectives, the assessment provides insight into how trust, engage-
ment and the everyday workplace experience shape an organisation’s culture.  
First achieving the certification in 2023, LGE India has continued to build on this recognition through its focus 
on employee experience, workplace culture , and an environment that supports professional growth.  This 
recognition will continue to help in securing the best talent in the job market and will also enhance 
shareholder confidence.    
Commenting on the recognition, Mr. Hong Ju Jeon, M anaging Director, LG Electronics India Ltd., 
said, “Earning the Great Place To Work® Certification for the third year  in a row is both, an important mile-
stone and a key validation for LG Electronics India ’s progressive HR practices. Our people are central to 
building a resilient organisation and delivering on our long -term ambitions. We believe that a culture rooted 
in trust, respect and collaboration creates the conditions for our employees to contribute their best and 
realise their potential. This recognition reflects our continued focus on listening to our employees and fos-
tering a workplace where individual growth and organisational progress go hand in hand. ” 
Anurag Gupta, Executive Vice President, Great Place To Work India  said, “Earning the Great Place To 
Work Certification™ for the third year in a row is a powerful testament to LG Electronics India’s steadfast 
commitment to fostering a people -first workplace. Over the years, LGE India’s culture has stood out for 
embracing great talent and, more importantly, nurturing an environment where individuals feel truly valued, 
accepted, and empowered to bring their authentic selves to work every day. Building a workplace where 
cultural harmony and personal authenticity thrive side by side  is a rare achievement, reflecting deep -rooted 
trust and empathetic leadership.  On behalf of Great Place To Work, hearty congratulations  to the leadership 
and entire team on sustaining t his exemplary workplace culture .” 
LGE India’s Great Place To Work® journey began in 2023 under the leadership of Mr. Hong Ju Jeon , MD, 
and Mr. Jwa Nam Kim, Chief Human Resources Officer  (CHO), LGE India. The company continues to focus 
on employee feedback, people -centric initiatives, and family engagement as part of its efforts to strengthen 
workplace culture and foster a sense of belonging. These efforts reflect the organisation’s focus on building 
an environment where employees feel connected to the organisation and supported in the ir professional 
development. 
As LG Electronics India continues to strengthen its presence and pursue its growth ambitions in the country, 
investing in its people and workplace culture remains an important part of building the organisation for the  
long term. Recently, LGE India also won the “National Best Employer Brand ” at World HRD Congress  and 
was also recognized as “Top Organisation with Innovative HR Practices” at Asia Pacific HRM Congress 
Award. Additionally, Mr. Jwa Nam Kim (CHO) was also conferred with the “Most Impactful HR Leader Award”  
 
 
 
 
 
  
   
& “Top HR Iconic Leader” for his leadership in driving HR transformation and positioning people as a 
strategic enabler of business growth.
