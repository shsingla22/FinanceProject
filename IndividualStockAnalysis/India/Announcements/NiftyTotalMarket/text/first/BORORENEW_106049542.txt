Sub: Preliminary findings of Ministry of Commerce and Industry on its investigation 
concerning imports of Textured Tempered Glass / Solar Glass from China and Vietnam   
This is to inform that based on the application of the Company, Ministry of Commerce and 
Industry  had initiated an  anti-dumping investigation into alleged dumping of  solar glass from 
China and Vietnam  into India and to determine the effect of  the said dumping and recommend 
the appropriate duty for safeguarding the interests of domestic solar glass manufacturers . 
The Ministry of Commerce and Industry , in its preliminary findings , has recommended 
imposition of provisional anti -dumping duty, pending completion of the investigation , vide its 
notification dated November 05 , 2024.  
The matter is now with the Ministry of Finance.  
You are requested to take the same on record.
