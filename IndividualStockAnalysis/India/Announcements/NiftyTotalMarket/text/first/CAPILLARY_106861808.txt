Hero Cycles Selects Capillary to Power Loyalty Across Its 3,000+ Dealer 
Network  
 
Subtext: Hero Cycles has already launched the program with 50 dealers, with plans to scale the 
initiative across its nationwide network.  
  
New Delhi, India – Hero Cycles, India’s largest bicycle manufacturer, has selected Capillary 
Technologies to power a new dealer loyalty program across its network of more than 3,000 dealers 
nationwide. The program has already been launched with 50 dealers, marking the first phase of 
a broader rollout across Hero Cycles’ dealer ecosystem. The initiative will help Hero Cycles 
strengthen dealer relationships through a more unified, data -driven a pproach to rewards, 
engagement and performance.  
With one of the largest dealer networks in the Indian bicycle industry, Hero Cycles is looking to 
build on its existing incentive initiatives with deeper channel insights, more personalised rewards 
and sustained engagement mechanisms. The program is designed to strengthen dealer 
preference and participation while supporting long -term business growth across its portfolio.  
Capillary’s platform will be integrated with Hero Cycles’ existing BIZOM app, creating a seamless 
experience for dealers. The program will recognise and reward a range of activities, including sell -

out performance, training participation, store branding and cross -category selling. Through 
personalised rewards, gamified experiences and greater visibility into dealer engagement, Hero 
Cycles aims to build a more structured and continuous relationship with its dealer network.  
Aditya Munjal, CEO at Hero Cycles, shared: “Our dealer network remains at the heart of our 
business strategy. As Hero Cycles continues to grow and evolve, we see an opportunity to further 
strengthen dealer relationships through a more structured and engagi ng loyalty approach. With 
the program now live with our first 50 dealers, we are seeing the foundation of a more rewarding 
experience for our partners, while creating greater opportunities for collaboration, participation 
and business growth across the network.”  
Sumit Kumar, President - India, Middle East & Africa at Capillary Technologies, commented: “Hero 
Cycles is an iconic Indian brand with one of the most extensive distribution networks in the country. 
With our experience helping leading brands build stronger  customer and partner relationships at 
scale, we see this partnership as an opportunity to transform every dealer interaction into a driver 
of preference, performance, and long -term value.”  
For Capillary Technologies, the partnership expands its presence in B2B and channel loyalty, 
applying its experience in large -scale loyalty and engagement programs to India’s manufacturing 
and distribution ecosystem. As businesses increasingly look beyond transactional incentives to 
build deeper relationships with their partners and channels, the initiative reflects a growing shift 
toward more personalised, data -driven  approaches to dealer engagement.
