Pine Labs and Google Cloud Collaborate to Advance Agentic Commerce in India  
 
Bengaluru — September 24, 2026: Pine Labs today announced a strategic collaboration with 
Google Cloud to ensure that every Indian merchant, from established enterprises to the new 
generation of merchants  who are tech -first, fast -growing, and ready to compete on any surface,  
is a full participant in agentic commerce.  
 
Agentic AI to Enable Merchants Across the Value Chain  
 
AI agents are already here. They discover products, compare options, and complete purchases 
on behalf of consumers, without the consumer ever visiting a website or opening an app. The 
merchants who benefit first are the ones whose products are structured a nd readable by AI 
systems, whose inventory is live and accessible, and whose checkout can be completed 
automatically. That often describes a large enterprise. It does not yet describe the new generation 
of Indian SMBs which is tech -first, fast -growing, and  competing on ambition rather than access 
to infrastructure.  
 
“Without the right platform, agentic commerce repeats a pattern India has seen before: new 
technology reaches the top of the market first, and small businesses wait.  Pine Labs is creating 
the next generation of agentic commerce products so that does not happen,” said B. Amrish Rau, 
CEO, Pine Labs.  
 
By putting Gemini -powered AI agents to work, Pine Labs will be creating a unified ecosystem that 
bridges merchant operations, engagement, and payments infrastructure. For India’s SMBs, a 
newly developed AI ads agent democratizes marketing by creating and p lacing high -quality ads 
across surfaces such as Search, YouTube, Pine Labs' POS devices, delivering enterprise -level 
reach to businesses. Simultaneously, Pine Labs Issuing is building ‘Jarvis’, a multi -agent platform 
built on Gemini Enterprise. Jarvis stre amlines operations by assisting merchants with program 
creation, system diagnostics, and customer support across the Woohoo platform. The result is a 
singular, comprehensive Pine Labs experience where AI fuels both day -to-day efficiency and 
scalable top -line growth.  
 
The collaboration between Pine Labs and Google Cloud further addresses three specific barriers 
that SMBs seeking to participate fully in agentic commerce, face:  
 
1. Discoverability and scalability : If an AI agent cannot read a merchant’s catalog, it cannot 
recommend them. But discoverability is only the first step. Pine Labs will offer India’s 
SMBs the full customer acquisition stack that is built on Google Cloud's AI and data 
infrastructure, as we ll as AI -powered campaign management, digital presence tools, and 
customer targeting capabilities that work across the surfaces where their customers 
already spend time.  
 
 
2. Receiving payment:  Pine Labs’ P3P is the payment layer that makes a transaction inside 
an agentic flow real -time, compliant, and settled. Starting on UPI and expanding across 
payment rails, P3P means a merchant does not need to rebuild their payment setup every 
time a new A I surface emerges.  
3. Governance : Agentic commerce at scale requires infrastructure that banks and regulators 
can rely on. Grantex ensures every transaction  that flows through this infrastructure is 
auditable and meets India’s financial regulatory requirements.  
 
To power this next -generation agentic commerce solution, Pine Labs will use Gemini Enterprise 
Agent Platform for catalog enrichment, search, and the rapid build and deployment of custom 
commerce agents. The Pine Labs platform will also offer native integra tion support for the 
Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) and Agent -to-Agent (A2A) frameworks, which will enable 
seamless, cross -platform transactions.  
 
Building an open ecosystem  
 
“The shift to AI -driven buying is already underway. The question is whether India’s 60 million small 
businesses  get to be part of it from the start, or wait for infrastructure that was built for someone 
else first,” Rau added.  “With Google Cloud, we’re building the infrastructure layer that makes this 
real for every merchant in India - from established enterprises to the new generation of merchants 
who are tech -first, fast -growing, and ready to compete on any surface. We’re tal king about the 
same rails, the same AI agents and the same access to credit, one commerce operating layer for 
every merchant.”  
 
Amit Kumar, Managing Director, Digital Natives Business, Google Cloud India, remarked, 
“Addressing the intricate requirements of this market demands a platform capable of deep, 
extensive scale, such as Pine Labs. By grounding Pine Labs’ commerce layers in Google Cloud’s 
secure, real -time AI and analytics infrastructure, we are empo wering merchants throughout India 
with the tools needed to be discoverable, trusted, and fully active participants in agentic 
commerce.”
