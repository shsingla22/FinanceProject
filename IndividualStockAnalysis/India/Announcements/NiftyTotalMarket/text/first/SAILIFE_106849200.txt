SAI LIFE SCIENCES LIMITED  | WWW.SAILIFE.COM   | Q2FY27 EARNINGS RELEASE  | PAGE 1 OF 2 Sai Life Sciences delivers strong growth with healthy operating performance  
Reported Q2 FY27 revenue growth of 23% YoY, sustaining EBITDA margin  
 
Hyderabad,  November  05, 2026:  Sai Life Sciences  Limited  (BSE:  544306 I NSE:  SAILIFE),  one of India’s  fastest  
growing Contract Research, Development and Manufacturing Organizations (CRDMO) announced its financial results 
for the second quarter and half year ended September 202 6. 
Commenting on the performance during the quarter, Mr. Krishna Kanumuri, Managing Director and CEO, Sai Life Sciences Limited, said , “Q2 FY27 was a strong quarter for Sai , with continued business momentum and 
progress across our key strategic priorities . We see growing traction with global pharmaceutical companies, increasing 
adoption of our integrated outsourcing model, and continued progress in building the capacity and capabilities required 
to support customers over the long term. Importantly, we are seeing c ustomer engagements evolve from individual 
projects into larger, more strategic relationships spanning discovery, development and manufacturing. " 
Mr. Siva Chittor, Chief Financial Officer, Sai Life Sciences Limited added , “We are pleased to report a healthy  
performance this quarter , with revenue growing by 23% year -on-year. The performance was broad- based, with both 
CRO and CDMO delivering healthy growth, while EBITDA margins stood at 28%. We continue to expect a stronger 
second half, supported by the progression of our development and commercial pipeline and the ramp- up of new 
capacity.  Our capacity and capability expansion programme remains on track with a total of ₹621 crore invested in H1 
FY27 .”

About Sai Life Sciences Limited (BSE: 544306 I NSE: SAILIFE)  
Sai Life Sciences is one of India’s fastest growing Contract Research, Development, and Manufacturing Organizations 
(CRDMO) that partners with innovator pharmaceutical and biotech companies to accelerate the discovery, development, 
and commercialization of new  medicines. Headquartered in Hyderabad, India, with a strong global presence, the 
company offers integrated solutions spanning discovery, process development, clinical and commercial manufacturing, 
and advanced technology platforms. Sai Life Sciences is committed to delivering high- quality, cost -effective, and 
scalable solutions while upholding the highest standards of safety, compliance, an d integrity. With a focus on innovation 
and operational excellence, the company continues to strengthen its capabilities to support emerging therapeutic 
modalities and meet the evolving needs of the life sciences industry .
