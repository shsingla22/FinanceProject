Vedanta L td Doubles Zinc Production Capacity in South Africa with  
US$ 600 Mn investment  
 
- South African President  inaugurates expanded capacity  
 
- Company  eyes global zinc leadership with  a vision of 3 million  tonnes of annual 
production across India and South Africa  
 
New Delhi, October 1 8, 2026:  Vedanta Group’s f lagship company , Vedanta Limited (NSE: 
VEDL ; BSE: 500295 ) announce s the doubl ing of  its annual zinc production capacity to 
500,000 tonnes1 at its  South Africa zinc operations . This is through an investment of nearly  
US$ 600 Mn at Gamsberg (Phase II ) at Vedanta Zinc International  (VZI) . South African 
President Cyril Ramaphosa and Vedanta Limited Director Priya Agarwal -Hebbar 
inaugurated th is expansion . Gamsberg , in South Africa’s Northern Cape province, has one of 
the world’s most significant  zinc deposits  and VZI’s flagship zinc mine . This expansion  marks one 
of the  largest mining investments in South Africa in th is decade .  
The expansion further advances Vedanta Limited’s position as  the world’s largest2 zinc 
producer , with a vision of achieving 3 million tonnes of annual production  across India and 
South Africa . Of this, 2 million tonnes are targeted from  its subsidiary , Hindustan Zinc 
Limited (NSE: HINDZINC; BSE: 500188), the world’s largest integrated zinc producer,  while 
Vedanta Zinc International business aims to contribute 1 million tonne s annually . 
Zinc consumption is growing rapidly as it is  essential to modern industrial  and infrastructure  
development. Through galvanization it protects steel , the world’s most widely used metal , from 
corrosion, extending its service life and supporting applications across construction, automotive , 
manufacturing and other sectors. Together,  Hindustan Zinc and V edanta Zinc International  
strengthen Vedanta Limited’s position to serve this growing global demand for zinc . 
This expansion forms part of the ZAR 21 Bn (nearly US$ 1.5 Bn) investment  commitment 
Vedanta made at the 2018 South Africa Investment Conference.  At last month’s 18th BRICS 
Summit in New Delhi, President Ramaphosa called for increased Indian investment in key South 
African industries. Vedanta’s continued investment in the Northern Cape demonstrates the 
tangible value of this growing economic partner ship.  
“We have come to celebrate an investment that has moved from commitment to construction, and 
from construction to production . We welcome Vedanta’s ambition to establish zinc smelting 
capacity in the Northern Cape. Government must work with Vedanta and other partners to 
address the ir requirements. ” said , South Africa  President Ramaphosa . 
“For Vedanta, Gamsberg Phase II is more than an expansion of its zinc business. It is a 
commitment to South Africa’s future. My father, Vedanta founder and Chairman Anil Agarwal,

oncology centre , a priority community project,  in Namakwa earlier this year.
