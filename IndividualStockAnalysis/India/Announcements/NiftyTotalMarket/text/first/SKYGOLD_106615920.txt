Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
In compliance with Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing 
Regulations”), We wish to inform that the meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company was 
held today i.e., Saturday, 9th May, 2026, at the Registered Office of the Company, wherein the 
following matters were considered and approved: 
 
i. Considered and approved the appointment of M/s. M S K A & Associates LLP (Formerly 
known as M S K A & Associates), Chartered Accountants as the Statutory Auditors of the 
Company: 
 
In accordance with the provisions of Section 139 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with the 
Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014 and on the recommendation of the Audit 
Committee of the Company, M/s. M S K A & Associates LLP (Formerly known as M S K A & 
Associates), Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No: 105047W/W101187) have been 
appointed as the Statutory Auditors to hold office from the date of appointment until the 
conclusion of the ensuing Annual General Meeting of the Company; subject to approval of the 
members in the ensuing General Meeting of the Company, to fill the casual vacancy arising as 
a result of the stepping down of M/s. V J Shah & Co., the erstwhile / outgoing statutory 
auditors of the Company. 
 
Further, the Board of Directors of M/s Speed Bangle Private Limited (“SBPL”), a wholly owned 
subsidiary of the Company, and the Boards of Directors of the wholly owned material 
subsidiaries of the Company, namely M/s Starmangalsutra Private Limited (“SPL”) and  
M/s Sparkling Chains Private Limited (“SCPL”), have separately approved, the appointment of  
M/s. M S K A & Associates LLP (Formerly known as M S K A & Associates), Chartered 
Accountants (Firm Registration No: 105047W/W101187) as their Statutory Auditors to fill the 
casual vacancy arising as a result of the stepping down of their Statutory Auditor, i.e.,  
M/s. V J Shah & Co.
