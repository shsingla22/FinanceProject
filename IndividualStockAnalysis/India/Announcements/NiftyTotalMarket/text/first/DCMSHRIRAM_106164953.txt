Dear Sir, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (‘SEBI Listing Regulations’), we hereby inform you that the Board of Directors 
of the Company at its meeting held today on March 4, 2025, has, inter alia, considered that the 
Company is operating various businesses viz. Chemicals, Vinyl, Sugar, Agri Inputs (Shriram Farm 
Solutions, Bioseed & Fertilizer), Building Material (Fenesta) etc. as independent business 
verticals with stable financials, having unique risks, cyclicality, rewards and regulatory 
requirements. Accordingly, it was discussed to evaluate options of restructuring/reorganising Agri 
Inputs and Building Material businesses of the Company, which are also consumer facing 
businesses, and any other business and/or Company reorganisation, with the objective of:  
 
(a) enabling strategic growth with optimal capital structure;  
 
(b) leveraging strengths of each businesses in a focused way;  
 
(c) potentially unlocking shareholder value.  
 
For this purpose, the Board has constituted a Committee to evaluate various options of 
restructuring/reorganising and make its recommendations to the Board. Any decision in this 
regard will be after due evaluation, consideration and approval by the Board and shall be subject 
to all necessary consents and requisite approvals.  
 
Necessary announcements and disclosures in accordance with the SEBI Listing Regulations and 
other applicable laws will be made by the Company post consideration and approval of the matter 
by the Board.  
 
The meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company commenced at 11.30 A.M. and concluded 
at 12:15 P.M.
