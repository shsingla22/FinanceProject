Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
In our earlier intimation dated September 17, 2024, Zydus Lifesciences Limited (“the Company ”) 
had informed about the execution of a Business Transfer Agreement (“ BTA”) for the acquisition 
of the API business (“the Target Business ”) of Sterling Biotech Limited (“ SBL”), subject to 
conditions precedent and closing date adjustments as specified in the BTA. It was stated therein 
that the transaction was expected to be completed by December 31, 2024.  
 
Vide letter dated September 30, 2025, the Company informed that the Company and SBL 
mutually agreed to extend the closing date to December 31, 2025.  
 
We hereby inform that SBL is still in the process of completing certain conditions precedents of 
the BTA and hence the Company and SBL have executed a letter to extend the closing date by 
June 30, 2026. In view of the same, the transaction is expected to be completed on or before 
June 30, 2026. 
 
Please take the above on record.
