India  
Partnership to offer affordable and powerful cybersecurity solutions to protect Micro, Small 
and Medium Enterprises  from growing cyber threats and scams  
 
• Cybercrime, particularly financial fraud, is projected to cost India Rs 1.2 lakh cror e by 
2025 * 
• The AI powered  all-in-one solution  combines McAfee’s trusted protection with

and accelerates  digital transformation.  
 
New Delhi -August 12, 2025 – Cybercrime is on the rise, affecting people whether they’re 
working or browsing at home. In India,  MSMEs  are increasingly targeted by scams, phishing 
attacks, and malware —putting both employees and customers at risk. To help MSMEs  stay 
productive and protected ,  Tata Tele  Business Services (TTBS)  has partnered with McAfee to 
deliver simple, effective cybersecurity solutions that safeguard people at work and at home .

securing their operations has become more critical than ever,” said

integrated solutions that combine productivity and cybersecurity, 
allowing MSMEs to operate efficiently while staying protected against 
the evolving threat landscape. This collaboration reflects our continued 
commitment to enabling MSMEs achieve secure, sustainable growth in 
a digital -first economy

Addressing Cybersecurity Challenges for MSMEs    
 
The rapid growth of digital payments, particularly for MSMEs, requires  robust cybersecurity 
measures to protect against evolv ing cyber threats. Many MSME s in India face significant 
cybersecurity challenges , often lacking  access to advanced security tools and in-house 
expertise . This leaves them vulnerable to scams and cyberattacks with potentially  serious 
consequences. A first of its kind  of partnership in India between McAfee  and TTBS, will bridge 
that gap with affordable, easy -to-use protection s olutions  that empower  MSME s to protect 
their operations and customers from rising online threats.  
 
McAfee’s suite of solutions, including AI -powered scam detection, device protection, and data 
security, will be bundled with TTTBS’s offerings  for a comprehensive, one -stop cybersecurity 
solution. This partnership will help MSMEs defend against the most common and damaging 
cyber threats, ensuring their operations remain secure and resilient.  
 
As cyber threats evolve, McAfee and TTBS remain  committed to continuously improving and 
innovating their offerings to meet the needs of MSMEs . The partnership ensures that MSMEs can 
stay ahead of threats, with an easy -to-implement solution that’s designed to simultaneously 
support growth and security.
