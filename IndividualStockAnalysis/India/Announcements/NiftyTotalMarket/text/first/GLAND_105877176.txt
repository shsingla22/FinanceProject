Sub: Outcome of the Board Meeting  
 
In compliance with the provisions of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 , this is to inform that the Board of Directors (the “ Board ”) of Gland Pharma 
Limited (the “ Company ”) at its Meeting held today, i.e., Friday, June 7, 2024 , has inter -alia-  
 
➢ has taken on record  the completion of tenure  (one term of five years)  of Mr. Yiu Kwan Stanley Lau, 
DIN: 08455325 , Chairman and Non -Executive Independent Director of the Company , with effect 
from June 9, 2024 . 
 
Mr. Yiu Kwan Stanley Lau  has been associated with the Company since June 10 , 2019 , and has played a 
key role in many of its strategic decisions during his tenure as Chairman and Independent Director on the 
Company's Board . 
 
The Board of Directors of the Company and the entire Gland Pharma family express their deep appreciation 
for his remarkable leadership and contribution to the Company's significant growth . 
 
Mr. Yiu Kwan Stanley Lau will also cease to be a Director on the Board of Gland Pharma International 
Pte. Ltd, the Company's material subsidiary . 
  
The details , as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015,  are given in Annexure -A, which is  enclosed herewith . 
 
➢ Appointment  of Mr. Srinivas Sadu, DIN: 06900659 , as Executive Chairman & CEO of the Company  
 
Mr. Srinivas Sadu (DIN: 06900659 ), Managing Director & CEO of the Company since April 25, 2019 , has 
been appointed as Executive Chairman and CEO of the Company with effect from June 10, 2024,  on such  
terms and conditions as approved by the shareholders vide postal ballot dated March 10, 2022.  
 
The details , as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
