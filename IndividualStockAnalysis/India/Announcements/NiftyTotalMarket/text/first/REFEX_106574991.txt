Dear Sir(s) / Madam, 
 
We wish to inform you that, in accordance with the terms of Class B Optional Convertible Debentures (“ Class B 
OCDs”) issued by Venwind Refex Power Limited (“ VRPL”), a subsidiary, such Class B OCDs have been converted into 
equity shares. 
 
Pursuant to the aforesaid conversion, Refex Industries Limited has been allotted 24,866 equity shares of face value of 
₹10/- each of VRPL, at fair valuation obtained from a Registered Valuer.  
 
Consequently, the Company’s shareholding in VRPL has increased by 4.11%, i.e., from 73.28% to 77.39%. 
 
Details as required under Regulation 30 read with Schedule III – Para B of Part A of the SEBI Listing Regulations 
are as per Annexure-A. 
 
Thanking you.
