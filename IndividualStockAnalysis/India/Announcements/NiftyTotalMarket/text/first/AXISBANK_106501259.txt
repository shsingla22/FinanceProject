I, CS K.V.S. Subramanyam, Partner of Ahalada Rao. V and Associates, Company Secretaries, 
Hyderabad, Practising Company Secretary holding Membership No. F5400 and Certificate of 
Practice No. 4815, was appointed as the Scrutinizer by the Board of Directors of Axis Bank 
Limited ("the Bank") for the purpose of scrutinizing the postal ballot process conducted 
through remote electronic voting in respect of the resolutions set out in the Notice of Postal 
Ballot dated December 18, 2025, in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 
2013, the rules made thereunder, the circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs 
from time to time, and Regulation 44 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015. 
The management of the Bank is responsible for ensuring compliance with the applicable 
provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the Companies (Management and Administration) 
Rules, 2014, the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, 
the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, the directions and guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank 
of India, and the MCA circulars, with respect to the conduct of the postal ballot through 
electronic mode, maintenance of proper records, provision of remote e-voting facility, and 
disclosure of the voting results in the manner prescribed under law. 
Our responsibility as Scrutinizer was limited to scrutinizing the votes cast through remote e­
voting, verifying their validity in a fair and transparent manner, and submitting this report of 
the results of such voting. We have not verified or expressed any opinion on the merits of the 
resolution~ or on any other matter beyond the scope of my appointment. 
The Bank had fixed Friday, December 12, 2025, as the cut-off date for determining the 
eligibility of members to vote on the resolutions proposed in the postal ballot. Only those 
members whose names appeared in the Register of Members or in the list of beneficial owners 
furnished by the depositories as on the said cut-off date were entitled to cast their votes. 
The Bank provided the facility of remote e-voting through National Securities Depository 
Limited ("NSDL"). The remote e-voting period commenced at 9:00 AM. on Saturday, 
December 20, 2025 and concluded at 5:00 P.M. on Sunday, January 18, 2026. After the 
conclusion of the remote e-voting period, the e-voting facility was disabled by NSDL in 
accordance with the applicable rules. 
At 05.01. P.M., Sunday January 18, 2026, The votes cast through remote e-voting were 
unblocked by me in the presence of two witnesses not in the employment of the Bank, and the 
data relating to votes cast was downloaded from the NSDL e-voting system. I have scrutinised 
the remote e-voting data and verified the same with reference to the records maintained by 
the Bank and the information provided by NSDL, including verification of voting rights as on 
the cut-off date ..
