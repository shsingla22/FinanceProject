future, firmly grounded in our core principles of stability,

Section A: General Disclosures
Equitas embodies fairness and transparency, seamlessly integrating 
business acumen with a profound sense of social responsibility. From 
our humble beginnings in microfinance, we have evolved into a full-
fledged Bank with a mission to create the most valuable bank for all 
stakeholders through happy employees. Our guiding ‘3S’ philosophy—
stability, sustainability, and scalability—has enabled us to nurture a 
diversified portfolio and a robust deposit franchise, while venturing 
into digital innovations. But our impact goes beyond banking. Through 
our CSR initiatives, we are committed to uplifting society by focussing 
on education, healthcare, and empowerment. Equitas’ legacy is one 
of meaningful inclusivity, balancing commercial success with societal 
progress. We are dedicated to ensuring a transformative future for all 
stakeholders, leaving an enduring mark of positive change.
