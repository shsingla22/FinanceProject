Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Sale of 
100% stake in Mileta a.s., a step down wholly owned subsidiary of the Company 
 
This is to inform that Alok Industries International Limited (“AIIL”), a step down wholly owned 
subsidiary of the Company, has informed that it has today, i.e., on December 16, 2025, entered 
into a Share Purchase Agreement (“SPA”) with CRESCON, a.s. for sale of its 100% equity stake 
in Mileta a.s. (“Mileta”) for an aggregate cash consideration of EUR 558,825. 
 
CRESCON, a.s. is a company incorporated and existing under the laws of the Czech Republic 
and develops residential and resort properties. CRESCON, a.s. does not belong to the 
promoter, promoter group and group companies of the Company.  
 
Mileta, a wholly owned subsidiary of AIIL, is in the business of manufacturing of textile fibres, 
fabrics, other textile goods, wholesale trade, yarn dyeing and chemical processing of fabrics. 
Mileta had a turnover of Czech Koruna (CZK) 421,339 ths. during FY 2024-25 contributing 
~4.11% to the consolidated turnover of the Company for financial year 2024-25. Further, Mileta 
has a net worth of CZK 161,553 ths. as on March 31, 2025 (consolidated net worth of the 
Company being negative Rs. 16,755.15 crore).  
 
The above transaction is subject to certain customary closing conditions and is expected to be 
completed by March 2026. Upon completion of the sale, Mileta will cease to be a subsidiary of 
the Company.  
 
The transaction is not a related party transaction.  
 
The Company received an intimation about the execution of the SPA on December 16, 2025 at 
5:48 pm.
