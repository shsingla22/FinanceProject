In the past, Zydus Lifesciences Limited (“ ZLL”) had received multiple show cause notices from 
Central Excise and Service Tax Authorities (“ Tax Authorities ”) pertaining to the alleged 
availment of CENVAT credit on input services for an aggregate amount of ₹ 392.47 million. These 
notices were covering the period from March 2008 to June 2017. Pursuant to detailed oral and 
written submissions made by the Company, the Tax Authorities have dropped the demand of ₹ 
358.97 million, except for the matter pertaining to CENVAT credit on sales commission paid to 
foreign entities and the demand of  ₹ 33.50 million with interest and penalty has been confirmed 
by the Office of the Commissioner, Central Goods and Services Tax and Central Excise vide its 
order dated September 19, 2025 (“the Order”), which was sent to the Company by way of email 
on September 24, 2025.
