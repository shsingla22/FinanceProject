This is to bring to notice that GMR Airports Infrastructure Limited (“GIL” or 
“Company”), received a Letter of Award (LOA) from Delhi International Airport 
Limited (DIAL), intimating that the Company has emerged as the Selected 
Bidder to develop, operate, manage and maintain the Duty-Free Outlets at 
the IGI Airport, New Delhi (Delhi Duty Free Concession). Subsequent to the 
issuance of the LOA, the Company has entered  into a License 
Agreement  today, i.e., August 21, 2024  towards the said Delhi Duty Free 
Concession, to take up the operati ons from July 28, 2025 onwards.  
 
It may be noted that GMR Airports Limited (since merged into the Company) 
had participated in the bidding process and submitted its bid for the RFP 
issued by DIAL for the grant of License for a long -term period to design, 
develop, finance , set up, operate , manage and maintain the Duty Free 
Outlet(s) at IGI Airport, New Delhi in April 2024.
