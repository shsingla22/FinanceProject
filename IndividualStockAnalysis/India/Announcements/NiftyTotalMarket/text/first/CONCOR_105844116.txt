ORDER 
09.05.2024: This application is filed by Respondent No. 1 for early 
hearing of I.A. No. 5518/2023. 
2. In brief, Respondent No. 1 filed an application under Section 9 of the 
IBC, 2016 (In short 'Code') which was admitted on 12.07.2023. The Corporate 
Debtor filed an appeal bearing CA (AT) (Ins) No. 887 of 2023 in which notice 
was issued on 14.07.2023 and stay was granted till the next date of hearing. 
3. This case was being adjourned earlier because of an arbitration award 
passed in favour of the Applicant/Respondent No. 1 on 01.06.2022 which was 
challenged by the present Appellant before the Hon'ble Single Bench of the 
Delhi High Court. The said objection filed under Section 34 of the Arbitration 
and Conciliation Act, 1996 (in short 'Act') was dismissed on 05.04.2024. 
3. Counsel for the Applicant has submitted that this case was last fixed 
for hearing on 03.05.2024 and has been adjourned to 24.05.2024. It is argued 
that since the objection of the Appellant, filed under Section 34 of the Act, has 
already been rejected, therefore, the stay granted by this Court vide order 
dated 14.07.2023 deserves to be vacated. He has thus prayed that the date of 
hearing of the main appeal and the application bearing I.A No. 5518 of 2023 
which is now posted for hearing on 24.05.2024 may be preponed to an early 
date. 
4. Counsel appearing on behalf of the Appellant on advanced notice given 
by the Applicant has submitted that against the order dated 05.04.2024, the 
Appellant had filed an appeal under Section 37 of the Act before the Hon'ble 
Division Bench of the High Court of Delhi in which the arguments were heard 
and order was reserved by the Hon'ble Division Bench on 08.04.2024. It is 
also submitted that on 03.05.2024 this Court had passed the order that 
"pleadings are complete. List for hearing on 24.05.2024". It is further 
submitted that it is only a case of admission of petition filed under Section 9 
of the Code in which there is no such urgency . 
5. We have heard Counsel for the parties and after perusal of the record 
are of the considered opinion that the present application is totally 
misconceived because after the dismissal of the objection filed under Section 
34 of the Act, the Appellant filed appeal under Section 37 of the Act which is 
still pending adjudication before the Hon'ble Division Bench of the Delhi High 
Court in which order is yet to be passed. Moreover , the next date of hearing 
in this case is not very far off as the case is listed on 24.05.2024. 
6. In view of the aforesaid facts and circumstances, the present 
application is hereby dismissed.
