Mastek Named a Supplier  on th e UK Crown Commercial Service’s  Digital 
Capability for Health 2 (DCfH 2) Framework  
Reading, UK , 6th March 2025: Mastek (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted digital engineering and 
cloud transformation partner, announ ced that it has been named a supplier on the UK  Crown 
Commercial Service’s RM6345 Digital Capability for Health 2 (DCfH 2) framework. This is  a single -lot 
agreement supporting digital transformation programmes in the health and social care  sector . 
The DCfH 2  framework  will op erate for the next three years, with the option for a one- year extension. 
This framework will provide Health and Social Care organisations with access to Mastek’s expertise in 
delivering agile development services for new digital solutions, supporting existing p roducts and 
services, and offering data management services –  including health data collectio n, processing, and 
distribution .  
Through DCfH  2, Health and Social Care organisations  can procure Digital, Data, and Technology (DDaT) 
resour ces to support  digital transformation programmes across the UK public health and social care. 
This includes both the development of new digital solutions and the ma intenance of existing services . 
This single -lot agreement  encompasses five key  service provisions.  
1. Development and Operations (DevOps) : Continuous improvement of live services.  
2. Digital Definition Services:  Support for discovery and alpha phase projects.  
3. Build and Transition Services : Service development through beta and live phases.  
4. End- to-End Development Services : Full lifecycle support from discovery to live.  
5. Data Management Services : Data collection, processing, analysis, and governance.  
“Mastek has a long- standing history of delivering digital engineering expertise to transform UK public 
services. We are delighted to be named as a supplier under this framework, reflecting our role  as a 
valued and trusted partner.  We are comm itted to drive  digital advancements that enhance patient care 
and promote social well -being. We look forward to collaborating with both new and existing 
organisations  through this framework to support the UK’s healthcare transformation journey.”  said 
Abhishek Singh , President of UKI & Europe, Mastek.  
The agreement aims to support the ongoing digitisation and modernisation of the NHS digital 
infrastructure, aligning with the UK G overnment’s plans for the NHS. Mastek continues to play  a crucial  
role in supporting the provision of h ealthcare services and enabling  faster, safer, and more convenient 
patient care.
