Press Release  
 
Karur Vysya Bank inaugurated 4 new branches in Tamil Nadu today  
 
27th January 2025 , Karur : Karur Vysya Bank (KVB) inaugurated 4 new branches in Tamil Nadu 
today . By inaugurating these branches,  the total branch network of the Bank increased to 
870. 
 
These branches will offer a  comprehensive range of banking services like accounts, deposits 
and loans including savings and current accounts and locker facilities etc.,  
 
The new branches that were opened  today : 
 
1. 867th Branch : No 340/1B, Indra Nagar, Pollachi Main Road Malumichampatti, 
Coimbatore - 641 050 - inaugurated  by Shri N. Nagaraj M.Com ., DLL, Assistant 
Commissioner Hindu Religious and Charitable Endowments Department Arulmigu 
Eachanari Vinayagar Temple, Eachanari . 
 
2. 868th Branch : MCET College Campus, No 11, Udumalai Road, Makkinampattti Post, 
Pollachi - 642 003 - inaugurated Dr. M. Manickam M.Sc., MBA., Chairman M/s. Sakthi 
Groups and NIA Educational Institution  
 
3. 869th Branch: No12/3/75, Bhruntha Nagar, Palamedu, Madurai -625503 – Inaugurated 
by Shri. G Karthikeyan M.Sc., Dy Commissione r, State Taxes Madurai and SPL PA to 
Minister of Commercial tax and Registration, Tamil Nadu  
 
4. 870th Branch: No. 67, Govinda Chetty Street, Agaram Road, Kaveripattinam, 
Krishnagiri District – 635112 – Inaugurated by Dr. K. Amudhavalli M.B.B.S., M.D., Dean 
– Government Dharmapuri Medical College and Hospital, Dharmapuri  
 
The new branches shall cater to all basic banking transactions and specific needs of 
customers, offering the entire spectrum of banking products and financial services covering 
Retail, Institutional and Consumer lending.  
 
KVB offers Internet Banking and Mobile Banking facilities.  KVB DLite, the Mobile Banking app 
of the Bank,  offers convenience of financial and non -financial services through 150+ features.  
The app has been recently upgraded with several customer friendly features.
