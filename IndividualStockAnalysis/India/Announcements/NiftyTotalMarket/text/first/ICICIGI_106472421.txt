We refer to our intimation dated May 1, 2024 wherein the Company had informed 
the stock exchanges regarding receipt of an Order from the Joint Commissioner,

the period April 2018 to March 2021. Subsequently, the Company filed an appeal 
against the said Order.

₹ 87,607,880/-, Interest of ₹ 1,154,037/- along with applicable interest under 
Section 50(3) of the CGST Act, 2017 read with the corresponding provisions of the 
SGST Act, 2017 & IGST Act, 2017 and Penalty of ₹ 8,777,337/-. The Company 
would pursue an appeal / evaluate any other legal options against the said Order 
including filing of writ petition. The requisite details of the said Order are enclosed 
herewith as an Annexure 1.
