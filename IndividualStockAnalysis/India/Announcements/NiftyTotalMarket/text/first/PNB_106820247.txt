             PRESS RELEASE  
   
Financial Results for the Quarter Ended 30th September  2026 
 
Key Highlights   
 
❖ Net Profit increased Y -o-Y by 12.8% to ₹ 5,532 Crore in Q2 FY’27 as against ₹4,904 
Crore in Q2 FY’26 . 
❖ RoA stood at 1.05% in Q2 FY’27, at the same level as 1.05% in Q2 FY’26.   
❖ Global Net Interest Margin improved to 2.5 8% in Q 2 FY’27 from 2. 50% in Q 1 FY’27. 
❖ CRAR increased to 1 7.95% as on September ’26 from 17. 19% as on September ’25.  
❖ Book value per share (Tangible) improved to ₹111.94  in September ’26 from ₹9 5.92 
in September ’25. 
❖ Global Business grew by 11.9% on Y -o-Y basis to ₹31,18,434 Crore as on 
September’26 from ₹ 27,86,673 Crore as on September’25.  
❖ Global Deposits registered a growth of 9.9% on Y -o-Y basis to ₹ 17,77,240 Crore 
as on September’26 from ₹ 16,17,080 Crore as on September’25.  
❖ Global Advances increased by 14.7% on Y -o-Y basis to ₹ 13,41,194 Crore as on 
September’26 from ₹ 11,69,592 Crore as on September’25.  
❖ CD Ratio stood at 75.5% as on September '26 as against 7 2.3% in September ’25. 
❖ GNPA ratio improved by 90 bps on Y -o-Y basis to 2.55% as on September’26 from 
3.45% as on September’25 . 
❖ NNPA ratio improved by 11 bps on Y -o-Y basis to 0.25% as on September’26 from 
0.36% as on September’25 . 
❖ Provision Coverage Ratio (including TWO) improved by 41 bps on Y -o-Y basis to 
97.32% as on September’26 from 96.91% as on September’25.  
❖ Slippage ratio stands at  0.77% as on Q2 FY'27 as compared to  0.71% as on Q2 
FY’26 . 
 
Business Performance in Key Parameters   
 
Deposit  
❖ Savings Deposits increased to ₹ 5,42,893 Crore registering a Y -o-Y growth of 6.7%.  
❖ Current Deposits increased to ₹ 83,053 Crore registering a Y -o-Y growth of 11.9%.  
❖ CASA Deposits increased to ₹ 6,25,945 Crore recording a Y -o-Y growth of 7.3%.  
❖ CASA Share of the bank stands at 36.6% as on September’26.  
 
Advances  
❖ Retail Advances  excl. IBPC  record ed a Y-o-Y growth of 16.3%.  
❖ Housing Loan grew by 14.3% Y -o-Y to ₹1,41,836 Crore .  
❖ Vehicle Loan posted a growth of 29.6% Y -o-Y to reach ₹ 38,234 Crore .

Profitability  
 
❖ Operating Profit for Q2 FY’27 was at ₹ 7,532 Crore and ₹ 15,051 Crore for HY1 
FY’27 recording growth of 4.2% and 5.2%, respectively on YoY basis.  
❖ Net Interest Income for Q2 FY’27 was at ₹ 11,601 Crore and ₹ 22,400 Crore for 
HY1 FY’27 recording a growth of 10.8% and 6.4%, respectively on YoY basis.  
❖ Total Income for Q2 FY’27 was at ₹ 38,185 Crore and ₹ 75,416 Crore for HY1 FY’27 
recording a growth of 5.4% and 2.7%, respectively on YoY basis.  
❖ Total Interest Income for Q2 FY’27 was at ₹ 34,585 Crore and ₹ 67,482 Crore for 
HY1 FY’27 recording growth of 8.5% and 5.7%, respectively on YoY basis.  
❖ Fee based income of the Bank is at ₹  1,954  Crore for Q 2 FY’27 increased from 
₹1,685 Crore for Q 2 FY’26, recording a growth of 16.0% on Y-o-Y basis.
