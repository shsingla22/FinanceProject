Regulations read with SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD -PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 
(“SEBI Circular ”), this is to inform that in the ongoing l itigation between Honasa Consumer Limited 
(“Company ”) and RSM General Trading LLC (“RSM ”) where RSM had filed a lawsuit in the Dubai Court 
(“Dubai Court ”) for unlawful termination of its distributorship by the Company . In furtherance of the 
same, the Dubai  Court  on 06th June 2024, upon  RSM’s precautionary attachment application, allowed to 
attach the Company’s assets in UAE, however, it categorically rejected to cancel the trading license of 
Honasa Consumer General Trading LLC (“ Honasa Dubai Entity ”) (“Precautionary Attachment  Order ”). 
Both the Company and RSM filed respective grievances against the Precautionary Attachment Order 
which were rejected by the Dubai Court on October 1, 2024 (“ Dubai Court Order ”). The Company and 
RSM further appealed the Duba i Court Order before the Court of Appeals (“Appeal Court ”).    
 
The Company is now in receipt of judgment dated December 10th 2024 (received on December 2 2, 2024 
at 12:16PM IST) passed by the Appeal Court wherein the Appeal Court has rejected the appeals filed by 
both the Parties and has ordered to attach assets of the Company in UAE and refused to cancel the 
trading license of Honasa Dubai Entity (“ Appeal Court Order ”). However,  this Order stands 
unenforceable since the Hon’ble Delhi High Court (“ Delhi High Court ”) had directed RSM to file a request 
before the Appeal Court for suspension of the precautionary attachment proceedings  (“Delhi High Court 
Order ”). Appeal Court, upon RSM’s request, suspended the precautionary att achment proceedings . 
There shall be no material financial impact on the Company, due to (a) the Delhi High Court Order and , 
(b) since the Company (Honasa Consumer Limited) has no assets located in UAE .  
 
We also find it imperative to clarify here that the Delhi High Court had allowed Section 9 petition and 
granted anti -enforcement injunction to the Company against which RSM filed an appeal (“Indian 
Appeal ”). In the Indian Appeal, the Delhi High Court direc ted RSM to make a request before the Appeal 
Court for suspension of the Dubai Court Order  and to not execute the original judgment passed by the 
Dubai Court in UAE or any other jurisdiction until the disposal of Indian Appeal . RSM filed a request 
before Appeal Court for suspending the precautionary attachment proceedings  as directed by the 
Delhi High Court.  The Appeal  Court suspended the proceedings,  and hence,  the Appeal Court Order  
stands unenforceable . This suspension will continue till the Indian Appeal is decided by the Delhi High 
Court.  Further, the Company has filed an appeal before the Cassation Court against the original 
judgment passed by the Dubai Court , and till its pendency, no execution proceedings can be initiated 
by RSM in UAE .
