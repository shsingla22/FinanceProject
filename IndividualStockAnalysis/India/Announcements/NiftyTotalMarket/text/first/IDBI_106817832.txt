Ministry of Finance
Government Approves Strategic Restructuring of IDBI
Bank Ltd.
प्रव  तथ : 14 OCT 2026 4:50PM by PIB Delhi
IDBI Bank Ltd. is a scheduled commercial bank regulated by the Reserve Bank of India
(RBI). Due to its poor financial health, the Bank required repeated infusion of capital,
initially provided by the Government of India and later by the LIC. On 5th May, 2017, the
Reserve Bank of India (RBI) put the IDBI Bank under the Prompt Corrective Action
Framework and imposed several restrictions in view of its non-performing assets and
losses incurred by the Bank. After considering the improvement in the Bank’s financial
health, the RBI later, vide its letter dated 10th March, 2021, lifted the restrictions imposed
on the IDBI Bank subject to certain conditions and continuous monitoring.
It was against this backdrop that the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA)
approved strategic restructuring of the IDBI Bank Ltd., and in compliance thereof, it was
decided that an aggregate 60.72% of the equity stake in the IDBI Bank Ltd be sold to a
Strategic Buyer, of which, the GoI share would be 30.48% and the LIC share would be
30.24%, leaving a residual shareholding of 15% with the GoI and 19% with the LIC.
The decision of the CCEA was implemented through a two-stage, competitive bidding
process supported by a multi-layered, collective decision-making mechanism consisting
of the Inter-Ministerial Group (IMG), the Core Group of Secretaries on Disinvestment
(CGD) and the Alternative Mechanism (AM) consisting of an empowered Group of Cabinet
Ministers.
The Preliminary Information Memorandum (PIM) inviting Expression of Interest (EoI) from
all interested parties was issued on 07.10.2022. Only the parties found ‘Fit and Proper’ by
the RBI and granted security clearance by the MHA could participate in the bidding stage,
for which a Request for Proposal was issued for inviting technical and financial bids from
the Qualified Interested Parties (QIPs). Of the five parties who initially submitted their
expression of interest, four parties qualified as per the eligibility criteria and
disqualification conditions prescribed therein, of which, two finally submitted their
techno-financial bids and became Qualified Bidders (QBs) for this Transaction.
After comprehensive evaluation by the CGD and after considering the recommendations
of the Alternative Mechanism, the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) in its
meeting held on 14/10/2026 approved M/s 10955230 Canada Inc. (parent entity M/s
Fairfax Financial Holdings Ltd.) as the Strategic Buyer for this Transaction for a purchase
consideration of Rs.52,878.0806181 Crores (in words Rupees Fifty-Two Thousand Eight
Hundred Seventy-Eight Crore Eight Lakh Six Thousand and One Hundred Eighty-One only)
for the acquisition of 60.72% stake in the IDBI Bank Ltd, comprising 30.48% equity
shareholding held by the Gol and 30.24% equity shareholding held by the LIC.
In such transactions, there is no a-priori benchmark for bidding. The bidders do their own
valuation and submit their bids in sealed envelopes in a confidential manner.
Independent agencies are hired to do a fair valuation of the enterprise
contemporaneously, which is then used as a comparator to assess the reasonableness of
the bids submitted.
Under this transaction, the initial bids were invited in the financial year 2025-26. For
comparison, a reserve price of Rs.94.42 per share was fixed at that time, which was
directly and indirectly influenced by the impacted market price. The unimpacted counter-
factual was not visible at that time, except that the unimpacted pre-EoI price of the IDBI
stock was Rs.42.40 per share on the last trading day before the issue of the PIM/EoI on
07.10.2022. In contrast, the impacted price was Rs.108.12 per share on the last trading
day before the invitation of initial bids on 08.01.2026. The initial bids fell short of the
reserve price arrived at that time and were not approved. When it became known that the
initial bids have not succeeded, the impacted stock market price itself corrected, and fell
significantly below the discovered bid price of Rs.75 per share.
Subsequently, in exercise of the powers delegated and conferred upon the collective
decision-making mechanism, and to enhance the value of the transaction as far as
possible, sealed bids reflecting their best offers were invited from the qualified bidders in
the financial year 2026-27. The stock price of the IDBI Stock was Rs.70.38 per share on the
trading day last before the invitation of best offer bids on 18.05.2026.
The stock market price of the narrowly traded IDBI stock continued to be volatile and
fluctuated both above and below the bid discovered price with a high-low range of
Rs.118.38 to Rs.61.01 per share over the valuation period. The stock market price was
consequently disregarded, as it tended to misalign valuation with the intrinsic value of
the enterprise. Instead, Relative Valuation by the Market Multiple Method, which
overcomes this problem, was used to determine the fair market value of the enterprise
on the basis of 31 March, 2026 closing accounts of all public sector banks. Additionally,
the Asset Valuation Method, which considers both tangible and intangible assets,
including land and properties along with the liabilities, was used to determine the fair net
asset value of the enterprise as per the 31 March, 2026 audited accounts of the IDBI
Bank Ltd.st

Accordingly, the fair valuation of the enterprise was carried out by two professional
valuation agencies using two independent methodologies as explained above. The
average of these fair valuations was Rs.75.99 per share, which served as the benchmark
price for the evaluation of the qualified bids. Since the winning best offer bid of Rs.81 per
share was above the benchmark price, as well as fair value estimates of both the
independent valuation agencies, it was adjudged to be reasonable and approved.   
The successful bidder will be required to present its credentials to the Reserve Bank of
India in accordance with the Fit and Proper Assessment conducted at the EoI stage and
submit its roadmap for the compliance of regulatory conditions prescribed therein. Once
completed, this will be the largest FDI in the financial sector of our economy so far.
The CCEA has also delegated powers to the Alternative Mechanism to undertake minority
stake sales of the GoI residual shareholding (15% of the total shareholding of the IDBI
Bank Ltd.) through the open market in accordance with the standard operating
procedure. The LIC will be free to manage its residual shareholding in accordance with its
internal procedures while ensuring compliance with the directions of its regulators.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on Strategic
Restructuring of IDBI Bank Ltd.
प्रव  तथ : 14 OCT 2026 4:51PM by PIB Delhi
Q1.  Who is the Strategic Buyer for the IDBI Bank Ltd.?
Answer: M/s 10955230 Canada Inc. (parent entity M/s Fairfax Financial Holdings Ltd.) has
been approved as the Strategic Buyer for the IDBI Bank Ltd.
Q2.  What is the size of this Transaction?
Answer: The purchase consideration for this transaction is Rs.52,878.0806181 Crores (in
words Rupees Fifty-Two Thousand Eight Hundred Seventy-Eight Crore Eight Lakh Six
Thousand and One Hundred Eighty-One only).
Q3.  How will it be split between the Government of India (GoI) and the Life
Insurance corporation of India (LIC)?
Answer: 60.72% stake out of the total shareholding of the IDBI Bank Ltd is being sold
through this transaction, comprising 30.48% shareholding held by the Gol and 30.24%
shareholding held by the LIC. The proceeds will be divided in the same proportion.
Q4.  How many entities were involved in the bidding process?
Answer: Of the five parties who initially submitted their Expression of interest, four
parties qualified as per the eligibility criteria and disqualification conditions prescribed in
the Preliminary Information Memorandum (PIM), of which, two finally submitted their
techno- financial bids and became Qualified Bidders (QBs) for this transaction.
Q5.  What methodol ogies were used for the valuation of IDBI Bank Ltd.?
Answer: The fair value of the enterprise was determined using two alternate
methodologies, namely the Market Multiple Method and the Asset Valuation Method.
Q6.  Who carried out the valuation exercise?
Answer: The valuation of enterprise was carried out by two independent, professional
valuation agencies appointed for the purpose. Their names cannot be disclosed due to
confidential guarantees enshrined in the process.
Q7.  What role did the stock market price play in the valuation of the IDBI Bank?
Answer: The volatile stock market price of the narrowly traded IDBI stock fluctuated both
above and below the bid offer price (low high range of Rs.61.01 to Rs.118.38 over the
valuation period). It was disregarded, as it tended to misalign the valuation with the
intrinsic value of the enterprise, and instead, relative valuation by the market multiple
method, which overcomes this problem, was used to determine the fair market value of
the enterprise based on the 31 March, 2026 closing accounts for all public sector banks.
Q8.  How have the assets, including land and properties, been valued for the IDBI
Bank?
Answers: The Asset Valuation Method, which considered both tangible and intangible
assets, including land and properties, along with the liabilities, was used to determine the
fair net asset value of the enterprise as per the 31st March, 2026 audited accounts of the
IDBI Bank.
Q9.  What was the benchmark price used for this transaction?
Answer: The average of the two independent valuations (Rs.75.99 per share) served as
the benchmark price for the evaluation of the qualified bids. The winning bid was found
to be higher than the benchmark price as well as the fair value estimates of both the
independent valuation agencies.
Q10.  How was the benchmark price determined?
Answer: The benchmark price was the average of the fair market value determined by
the Market Multiple Method and the fair net asset value determined by the Asset
Valuation Method.
Q11.  What is the further course of action?
Answer: The Successful Bidder would now present its credentials to the RBI in
accordance with the ‘Fit & Proper’ assessment conducted at the EoI stage and submit a
roadmap for the compliance of regulatory conditions prescribed therein.
Q12.    What is the residual equity stake left with the GoI and the LIC?
Answer: GoI will retain 15% residual equity stake while the LIC will retain 19% equity
stake. Both of them will withdraw their nominees from the Board and become public
shareholders.

Bank?
