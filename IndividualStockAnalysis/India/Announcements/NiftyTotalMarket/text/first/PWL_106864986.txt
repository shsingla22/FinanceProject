Physicswallah  FY30 Ambition  
Aiming  for about 25%+ revenue CAGR over FY27 –FY30 and a pre -Ind AS EBITDA margin of 
20%+, with online contributing about two -thirds of revenue by FY30.  
Mumbai , November 18 , 2026 – Physicswallah Limited (NSE: PWL | BSE: 544609), one of 
India's largest education companies,  hosting its ﬁrst Investor Day in Mumbai, coinciding with 
the ﬁrst anniversary of its stock market listing, and presenting its medium -term strategic 
vision  through FY30.  
Physicswallah's ambition for FY30 are : 
• Revenue range of ₹9, 000 - ₹10,000 crore by FY30 , up from ₹3,900 crore in FY26  
• Revenue CAGR of about 25% + between FY27 and FY30  
• Pre-Ind AS EBITDA margin of 20% + by FY3 0 
• PW estimates  two -thirds of revenue from the online business by FY30, with the 
rest from offline business and other businesses

About Physicswallah  
Physicswallah (PW), an education platform, was founded in 2020 by Alakh  Pandey and 
Prateek Maheshwari. Headquartered in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, PW aims to democratise education through online, offline and hybrid platforms. Initially launched as a YouTube 
channel in 2016, PW now offers education to students through its native ap p, tech -enabled 
offline and hybrid centres, and YoTube channels. PW's offerings span various educational 
segments, including test preparation, a skilling vertical, higher education and education 
abroad, with programmes  available in multiple vernacular languages. Physicswallah Limited 
was listed on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and BSE Limited on 
November 18, 2025. (Scrip codes: NSE – PWL; BSE – 544609.)
