Airtel to acquire 400 MHz spectrum in 26  GHz band from Adani Data Networks Limited  
 
New Delhi, April  22, 2025:  Bharti Airtel Limited and its Subsidiary, Bharti Hexacom Limited 
(“Airtel” or  “Company”) have ente red into  definitive agreement s with Adani Data Networks 
Limited (ADNL), a subsidiary of Adani Enterprises to acquire rights to use of 400 MHz of 
spectrum in the 26 GHz band in Gujarat (100 MHz), Mumbai (100 MHz), Andhra Pradesh (50 
MHz ), Rajasthan (50 MHz), Karnataka (50 MHz) and Tamil Nadu (50 MHz).  
 
The closing of the transaction is subject to s atisfaction of the standard conditions (including 
conditions stated in the Spectrum Trading Guidelines)  and statutory approval (s).
