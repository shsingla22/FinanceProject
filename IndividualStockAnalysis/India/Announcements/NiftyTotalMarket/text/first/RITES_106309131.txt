Press Release  
 
RITES & CMPDI sign MoU to tap  
into Mining & Renewable potential  
 
Gurugram, July 23, 2025: RITES Ltd., a prime transport infrastructure consultant, today signed an MoU with 
Central Mine Planning & Design Institute Limited (CMPDI), a subsidiary of Coal India Limited (CIL), to 
establish a strategic framework for collaboration. This partnership aims at identifying, exploring, and jointly 
pursuing prospective projects and consultancy assignments—both in India and internationally—particularly 
in the fields of mining, renewable energy and related infrastructure development. 
 
 
RITES and CMPDI officials at the MoU-signing ceremony in Ranchi 
 
Under this MoU, RITES will leverage its international experience, market intelligence, and logistics 
infrastructure expertise for effective evacuation to support CMPDI in securing mining projects, undertaking 
consultancy assignments, and designing capacity-building programmes. The two organizations will jointly 
identify opportunities and conduct feasibility studies and detailed assessments for mining, power, 
renewable energy, and related infrastructure projects in mutually agreed geographies. They will also 
collaborate on consultancy assignments and jointly submit proposals for mining and evacuation projects, 
both in India and abroad. 
 
 
 
About RITES Limited: 
RITES Limited, a Navratna Public Sector Enterprise, is a leading player in the transport consultancy and engineering sector in India, 
having diversified services and geographical reach. The company has an experience spanning 51 years and undertaken projects in 
over 55 countries in Asia, Africa, Latin America, South America, and Middle East region.
