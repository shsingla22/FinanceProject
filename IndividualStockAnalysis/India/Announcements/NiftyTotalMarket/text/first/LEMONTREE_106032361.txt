Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in  
Maharashtra  
 
 
New Delhi, 23rd October  2024: Lemon Tree Hotels announced its latest 
signin g – Lemon Tree Hotel, Adalat Road, Aurangabad,  Maharashtra .  The 
property, which shall be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -
owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, is expected to open in FY 2028.  
 
Aurangabad, a historical city in Maharashtra, India, is famous for its impressive 
ancient monuments. The UNESCO World Heritage Sites of Ajanta and Ellora 
Caves, with their breathtaking rock -cut sculptures, are must -see attractions. The 
Bibi Ka Maqbara, often called the "Mini Taj Mahal," is another architectural gem 
showcasing Mughal elegance. A urangabad is a bustling center for trade and 
tourism, offering a delightful mix of historical sites, vibrant culture, and delicious 
local cuisine.  
 
Lemon Tree Hotel, Adalat Road, Aurangabad , Maharashtra will feature 60  well-
appointed rooms, a restaurant, a banquet hall, a meeting room, a swimming 
pool, a spa and other public areas. Aurangabad  Airport is approximately 8 kms 
while the Aurangabad Railway Station is 2.5  kms from the property. The hotel is 
also well -connected by road, making it accessible by bo th public and private 
transport.  
 
Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO — Managed & Franchise Business, Lemon Tree Hotels said, 
"We are excited  to announce our further expansion in Maharashtra . This new 
addition will complement our twelve  existing and four upcoming propert ies in the 
state, further enhancing our commitment to providing exceptional hospitality 
and experiences in this beautiful region.”
