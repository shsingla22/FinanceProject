Instructions for Remote E-Voting 
 
In terms of the SEBI circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CMD/ CIR/P/2020/242 dated 
9th December 2020 on the e-voting facility provided by listed companies and as 
part of increasing the efficiency of the voting process, e-voting process has been 
enabled for all individual Members holding securities in demat mode to vote 
through their demat account maintained with depositories / websites of 
depositories / depository participants. Members are advised to update their 
mobile number and email ID in their demat accounts in order to access e-voting 
facility. 
In conformity with the regulatory requirements, Members can vote only through 
remote e-voting, for which purpose they are requested to carefully read the 
instructions.
