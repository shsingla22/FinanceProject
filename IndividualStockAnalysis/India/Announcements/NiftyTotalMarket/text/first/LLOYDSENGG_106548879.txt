ORDER 
The applicant Company has filed this application under Section 13(4) of the Companies Act, 2013 (hereinafter referred to as 'the Act') read with Rule 30 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014 [the Rules] vide Service Request Number AB9832348 for confirmation of alteration of the registered office clause of the Memorandum of Association of the company as approved by special resolution passed on 25.10.2025 in accordance with Section 114 of the Act in its General meeting. 
The undersigned is competent to consider the present application and pass order in terms of powers of the Central Government delegated vide Notification Number S.0. 1352(E) dated 21.05.2014. 
Taking into consideration the contents of the application and affidavits filed in support thereof, report of the Registrar of Companies, Ahmedabad dated 22.12.2025 informing that no complaint, 
Inquiry/Inspection/ Investigation/ Prosecution is pending, the same has been also confirmed by 3i cell 
of this directorate (NWR), and further the company has disclosed information regarding no pendency of 3i's and prosecution as well in Point no. 8 of the e-form INC-23 supported with affidavit dated 
02.12.2025, I am of the considered opinion that the application deserves to be allowed as being in 
compliance to Section 13(4) of the Act and Rule 30 of the Rules. 
THE REGIONAL DIRECTOR (NWR) DOTH HEREBY ORDERS 27 FEB 2026 
That the application is allowed. The alteration in Clause II of the Memorandum of Association of the 
Petitioner Company as approved by Special Resolution passed as referred to above is hereby confirmed 
as set forth in the schedule hereto and forming part of this order subject to the condition that interest of 
employee working at the registered office of the company shall not be adversely affected either by way 
of transfer or retrenchment or otherwise and there will be no jurisdictional change in the legal 
proceedings pending against the company on the date of this order. 
2. Master File SCHEDULE 
II The Registered Office of the company shall be situated in the State of Maharashtra.
