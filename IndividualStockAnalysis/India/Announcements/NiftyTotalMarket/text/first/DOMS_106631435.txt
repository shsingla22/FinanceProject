Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with sub-para (20) of para (A) of part (A) of Schedule III of SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosures Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended, (‘ SEBI LODR Regulations ’) we, 
DOMS Industries Limited (‘ the Company ’), wish to inform that the Company has received the Final Order 
dated May 05, 2026, issued by the Office of the Commissioner of Customs (Appeals), Mumbai-II under 
Section 128A of the Customs Act, 1962, in relation to the alleged misclassification of certain goods imported 
by the Company, which resulted in short payment of Goods and Services Tax (‘ GST’).  
 
The Company had earlier received an Order-in-Original dated November 30, 2023, from the Office of the 
Commissioner of Customs, NS-V, under the Customs Act, 1962, and being aggrieved by the same, had 
preferred an appeal. The Final Order now received upholds the said Order-in-Original, with a modification 
limited to the recalculation of the demand. 
 
The Final Order was received by the Company on May 19, 2026. 
 
The relevant details as per the requirement of Regulation 30 of SEBI LODR Regulations, read with SEBI 
Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, is enclosed in 
‘Annexure - A ’. 
 
We request you to please take the same on record.
