Sub.: Intimation of Sad Demise of Mr. Mahendra Doshi, Independent Director 
 
We regret to inform the untimely and unfortunate demise of Mr. Mahendra Doshi (DIN: 00123243), 
Independent Director of the Company, who passed away on August 06, 2025. 
 
We are deeply saddened by the sudden departure of Mr. Doshi, and his contributions will be greatly 
missed. His wisdom, integrity, and unwavering commitment to governance have left an indelible 
mark on our organization. The Chairman and Board members of the Company extend their heartfelt 
condolences and profound sympathy to his family during this difficult time. 
 
Mr. Doshi was associated with the Raymond Group as director for over 10 years. He was Chairman of 
Stakeholders Relationship Committee and member of Corporate Social Responsibility Committee and 
Investment & Finance Committee of the Board. He had significantly contributed to the Company with 
his knowledge of Finance, Capital Markets and Business Administration. He was known for his 
business acumen and simplicity.
