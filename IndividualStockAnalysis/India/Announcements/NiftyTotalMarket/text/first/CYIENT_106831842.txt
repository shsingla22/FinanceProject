 Hyderabad, India / San Jose, CA – October 28, 2026  – Cyient Semiconductors  will present its semiconductor 
portfolio at electronica 2026, taking place from 10 to 13 November in Munich, Germany, at Hall C6, Booth 510. 
The global fabless semiconductor company combines two complementary businesses: application-specific standard products (ASSPs) based on proprietary semiconductor IP, and custom ASIC solutions tailored to individual customer requirements, from ar chitecture through production silicon. 
 Protect and Convert: Power management semiconductor products from Kinetic Technologies, a Cyient Semiconductors company 
With over 3 billion units shipped, mo re than 250 products, and over 100 pa tents, Kinetic Tec hnologies is the 
semiconductor products business of Cyient Semiconductors, offering customers an established portfolio of power management solutions, proprietary semiconductor IP, and high-volume manufacturing and supply-chain capabilities.  Featured solutions at electronica 20 26 include the KTS1642Q automotive load switch, designed to protect 
vehicle electronics against reverse-battery, load-dump, an d other electrical transient conditions, as well as USB-
C protection solutions supporting the transition to 48V Extended Power Range. For power conversion, Kinetic's 
fast-transient buck regulators address the demanding re quirements of edge AI platforms and advanced SoCs, 
responding rapidly to changing loads to support efficient, reliable power delivery. 
 Custom ASICs: from architecture to production silicon Cyient Semiconductors' turnkey custom ASIC solutions br ing together high-precision analog and mixed-signal, 
RF, IoT connectivity, embedded power and digital processing in custom silicon tailored to the requirements of 
each application. These capabilitie s span architecture, de sign, prototyping, qualification, manufacturing 
coordination and continued silicon supply.  Rising power density and efficiency demands are crea ting new challenges at higher voltages. Cyient 
Semiconductors is developing 800V high-voltage power tech nologies for emerging use cases in AI data centers, 
industrial and factory automation, and e-mobility. These technologies remain under development as part of the company's next-generation roadmap. 
 
“We are building a global semiconductor product company by combining differentiated power IP with turnkey custom silicon,” said Suman Narayan, CEO of Cyient Semiconductors . “Kinetic Technologies is already shipping
