Crompton, Alongside CEO Promeet Ghosh, Honoured for Transformative Leadership and Sustainability 
Impact 
 
Recognised for steering strategic growth, innovation, and setting benchmarks in sustainability  
 
September 25, 2025, Mumbai – Promeet Ghosh, Managing Director & CEO at Crompton, has been awarded The Economic Times 
Energy Leadership Award in the Chief Executive category, recognising outstanding leadership and vision in the energy sector. With a 
strong focus on sustainability transition, Mr. Ghosh has been instrumental in driving Crompton’s strategic transformation under the 
Crompton 2.0 vision, ensuring growth that is both profitable and environmentally responsible. 
 
Under Mr. Ghosh’s leadership, Crompton has advanced its sustainability agenda through initiatives such as #TechWithHeart and 
indigenous platforms like NUCLEUS and XTECH. These have enabled the company to develop energy-efficient, high-performance 
products that reduce carbon footprints while promoting responsible resource use. Beyond product innovation, ESG principles are 
embedded across operations, from renewable energy adoption and water conservation through rainwater harvesting to circular 
economy practices, reflecting Mr. Ghosh’s commitment to integrating sustainability into every aspect of the business. 
 
Crompton’s sustainability efforts have been recognised globally. The company received the Energy Transition Award for pioneering 
cleaner, sustainable energy systems and reducing carbon footprints. Its comprehensive ESG initiatives, including product carbon 
footprint assessments and Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) programs, highlights a consistent commitment to responsible 
resource management and environmental stewardship. 
 
Looking ahead, Crompton continues to innovate responsibly, integrating sustainability across product design, manufacturing, and 
operations while enhancing consumer experiences. Through strategic investments in technology, energy-efficient solutions, and local 
manufacturing capabilities, the company remains committed to delivering smarter solutions for modern homes, driving profitable 
growth, and contributing to a sustainable future. 
 
 
About Crompton:  
With a brand legacy of over 85 years, Crompton Greaves Consumer Electricals Ltd. is India’s market leader in the category of fans and 
residential pumps. Over the years, the organization has continuously strived to produce a range of innovative products that cater to 
the modern consumer including superior quality and high-performance fans, pumps, lighting solutions and a range of other categories 
like water heaters; air coolers; small kitchen appliances like mixer grinders, air fryers, OTG, electric kettles etc.; other home appliances 
like irons & built-in kitchen appliances. The company has further invested in brand and innovation to not only better understand and 
meet consumer needs, but to also help drive energy efficiency. The consumer business also has a well-established and organized 
distribution network driven by a strong dealer base across the country offering a wide service network and robust after sales service 
to its customers.  
 
The company's consistent dedication to developing energy-efficient products has led to significant recognition. It has been honored 
with three prestigious National Energy Consumer Awards (NECA) by BEE, Ministry of Power. The recent award was presented by the 
Hon’ble President of India, Smt. Droupadi Murmu for the company's storage water heater in 2023. In 2019, the brand won in two 
categories: Ceiling Fans and LED Bulbs. Additionally, it was recognized as one of India's Best Managed Companies 2022 by Deloitte 
Private and listed among ‘India’s Top 500 companies 2022’ by Dun & Bradstreet India. The company has also been featured in Brand 
Top 75 most valuable Indian brands list for 2020 released by WPP and Kantar. Furthermore, Crompton was also recognized as the 
Brand of the Decade 2021 by Herald Global and BARC Asia in the Consumer Electrical category.
