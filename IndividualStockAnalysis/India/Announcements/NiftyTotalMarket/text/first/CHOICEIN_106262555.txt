PRESS RELEASE  
Choice Consultancy Services Secures Landmark Projects Worth ₹63.47 Crores across  
Maharashtra and Odisha  
Wins prestigious World Bank -backed engagement under MahaSTRIDE and emerges top -ranked bidder in 
Odisha’s Urban Infrastructure Program  
Mumbai, June 5, 2025:  Choice Consultancy Services Pvt Ltd, the public sector advisory arm of Choice 
International Limited, has announced two significant project developments totalling  appro ximately 
₹63.47 crores (inclusive of GST), further strengthening its footprint in public sector transformation and 
infrastructure planning across India.  
The company has been awarded a work order valued at ₹52.80 crores  by the Maharashtra Institution 
for Tr ansformation (MITRA) , Government of Maharashtra. This prestigious assignment is part of the 
World Bank -supported MahaSTRIDE Program  and involves establishing District Strategic Units (DSUs)  
across the Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Division . The five -year engag ement aims to enhance 
institutional capabilities at the district level and drive data -backed, growth -oriented decision -making 
aligned with India’s vision of a $5 trillion economy  and Maharashtra’s goal of achieving $1 trillion 
economy . The project is set t o be executed over a five -year period.  
Additionally, Choice Consultancy Services has emerged as the H1 (highest -ranked) bidder  for the 
project titled “Providing Engineering Consultancy Services in 58 Urban Local Bodies of Odisha State,”  
floated by the State Urban Development Agency (SUDA)  under the Housing and Urban Development 
Department , Government of Odisha. The bid, with a proposed value of ₹10.67 crores , focuses on 
bolstering urban infrastructure planning and technical capacity to manage rapid urbaniza tion across 
the state. This engagement is expected to span a duration of two years, pending final work order 
confirmation.  
Commenting on these developments, Arun Poddar, CEO of Choice International Ltd , said:  
"We are proud to deepen our engagement with public sector partners through these important 
assignments. Our commitment to advancing Institutional capabilities and urban infrastructure  is 
unwavering, and we remain focused on delivering impact -driven, techno logy -enabled solutions that 
empower communities and contribute to India’s long -term growth. These project wins further validate 
our strategic direction and reinforce our positioning as a trusted execution partner in high -value public 
sector initiatives."  
These initiatives align closely with the Government of India's vision to modernize institutions, expand 
digital access, and foster inclusive economic growth. The company's involvement in these programs 
reinforces its position as a trusted partner in executi ng high -impact public development strategies 
across the nation.
