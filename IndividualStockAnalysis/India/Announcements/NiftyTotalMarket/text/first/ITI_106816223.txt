Item No 4: Lt. Gen. Vivek Dogra, SM, Signal Officer-in-chief, Ministry of 
Defence, Government Director (DIN:  11851484)
Lieutenant General Vivek Dogra has taken over the charge as Government 
Director of ITI Limited with effect from 27th July 2026 and is a 1988 
batch officer of the Indian Army. He is an accomplished academician and 
decorated soldier, aptly personifying the role of “Scholar Warrior”. His vast 
experience of serving in combat zones gives him a unique insight into the 
user-end requirements of non-kinetic and kinetic Ops.
He holds an M. Tech degree in Computer Science and is qualified as instructor 
in Technical Staff Course from Institute of Armament Technology, Girinagar 
Pune. He also holds two M. Phil degrees. He has been the Commandant 
& Executive Head of the prestigious Military College of Telecommunication 
Engineering, MCTE, the premier technical training establishment of Indian 
Army, wherein he worked closely with various institutions of repute including 
IITs, IIITs & NITs, pioneering research and academic development of UG, PG 
& research scholars, prior to taking over as Signal Officer-in-Chief on 01 
Jan 2026.
As SO-in-C, he is spearheading the Indian Defence forces transformation 
towards resilient, data centric and state-of-the-art technology infused 
combat force.
Item No 5: Shri Ajai Kumar Srivastava, Director Production (DIN: 11865448)
Shri Ajai Kumar Srivastava has taken over the charge as Director 
(Production) of ITI Limited with effect from 31st July 2026. An Electronics 
& Communication Engineering graduate from J.K. Institute of Technology 
University of Allahabad, Shri Ajai Kumar Srivastava joined ITI Limited in 
1998. Over the past 28 years he has built an illustrious career, holding 
several key positions across Production, Projects and Marketing, and has 
made significant contributions to the Company. He possesses extensive 
experience in telecom manufacturing and has successfully led the production 
of Transmission, Switching, and Access products. He played a pivotal role in 
establishing GPON manufacturing capabilities at ITI Limited, which enabled 
the Company to secure a substantial share of the BharatNet Phase-I project 
and execute it successfully across multiple telecom circles.
As Unit Head of ITI’s manufacturing units at Mankapur and Naini, he led 
several operational and business initiatives that resulted in enhanced 
production efficiency, growth in turnover, and the introduction of new 
products into the Company’s portfolio. During his tenure as Head of the 
Marketing Unit at Lucknow, the orders saw a remarkable jump due to his 
initiatives.
Prior to assuming charge as Director (Production), Shri Ajai Kumar Srivastava 
served as Chief General Manager (Production & Defence Projects) where he 
was responsible for overseeing the operations of all production units of ITI 
Limited and is an ideal candidate to take ITI forward on its path to become 
a leading telecom player.
Item No 6: Shri Sanjiwan Sinha, Government Director (DIN: 03316960)
Shri Sanjiwan Sinha has taken over the charge as Government Director on 
the Board of ITI Limited with effect from 10th August, 2026, a 1994-batch 
IP&TAFS officer, currently holds the position of Director General, National 
Communications Academy – Finance with additional charge of Project Director, Programme Monitoring Unit, Digital Bharat Nidhi in the Department 
of Telecommunication, Government of India. With a diverse career spanning 
various departments and organizations, he has been instrumental in driving 
innovation and leveraging Information and Communication Technology (ICT) 
for transformative solutions. He holds the Degree of MBA(Finance), LLB and 
PhD in Social Science
Throughout his cadre postings in DAP , DOT, MTNL, BSNL, and BBNL, Sinha 
showcased his commitment to utilizing ICT for efficiency. Notably, as Joint 
CCA in Jharkhand, he operationalized the first website of any CCA office in 
2005.
During his deputation in the Government of Bihar, as Managing Director of 
Bihar State Educational Infrastructure Development Corporation in 2012, he 
implemented a unique organizational structure with centralized planning, 
tendering and payment along with decentralized physical supervision, 
facilitated by the App ‘Concurrent Monitoring of Projects’.
His tenure as the Managing Director of South Bihar Power Distribution 
Company saw the conception and implementation of the paperless end-to-
end customer service platform ‘Suvidha App’. This innovative App. With 1 
million+ downloads and a 4-star rating on Google Play Store, transformed 
transparency in the workflow of the power distribution company during the 
Covid-19 period.
He successfully steered the implementation of Smart Prepaid Meters in 
Bihar, fostering a behavioral change in consumer-serving approaches. This 
initiative garnered attention and now Bihar has become the State with the 
highest Smart Prepaid Meter consumers.
Shri Sanjiwan Sinha’s remarkable career journey reflects a consistent 
commitment to leveraging ICT for transformative solutions across various 
sectors, promoting transparency, efficiency and innovation in public service.
