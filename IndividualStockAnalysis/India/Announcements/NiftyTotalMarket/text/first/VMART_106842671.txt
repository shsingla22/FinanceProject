V-Mart Retail reports strong Q2 FY27 performance, with 18% revenue growth and 2 0% growth in

V-Mart Retail Limited , one of India's leading value fashion retailers, today announced its unaudited

Company's Board of Directors at its meeting held on November 3, 2026.

The Company delivered a strong top -line performance during the second quarter, with total revenue 
growing 18% YoY to ₹953 crore, driven by healthy underlying consumer demand.  
During the current year, f estive dates shifted from Q2 to Q3 due to a 19 -day shi ft in the festive 
calendar, with Navratri commencing on 11 October 2026 in current year versus 22 September 2025 
last year. A djusting for festive timing shift, same store sales growth (SSSG) for the quarter stood at 
11%, with V -Mart at 10% and Unlimited at  12% reflecting robust underlying demand momentum 
across the retail footprint . The calendar SSSG  for Q2  stood at 3% for the quarter, with V -Mart at 2% 
and Unlimited at 11% .  
On the operational profitability front, EBITDA increased 20% YoY to ₹86 crore, driven by operating 
leverage gains and cost discipline.  
For H1 FY27, Revenue grew by 21% YoY to ₹2,042 crore, SSSG stood at 6%. EBITDA margins improved 
by 40 bps to 12.1%  and PAT grew by 25% YoY , reflecting continued improvement in profitability.  
The Unlim ited store format in South India delivered another quarter of standout performance, posting 
25% revenue growth in Q2 (29% YTD) and a 38% YoY increase in EBITDA (40% YTD). Increased store 
productivity, sharper merchandise assortments, and operational efficiencies continue to power 
scaling across southern states.  
The Company's continued focus on operational efficiency and inventory management resulted in a 7% improvement in inventory days to 90 days as of September 30, 2026, compared to the corresponding 
period last year.  
The Company maintained its expansion momentum by opening 23 new stores and closing one underperforming location . A significant operational milestone of 600 stores crossed during the 
quarter . As of September 30, 2026, V -Mart operated 613 stores across 29 States and Union Territories, 
with a total retail footprint of approximately 52 lakh sq. ft.  
Founded in 2002, V -Mart is a pioneer in organised value fashion retail in India, oﬀering a diverse range 
of apparel, footwear, accessories and home furnishings for the entire family. With a strong presence 
across Tier II and Tier III cities, the Company operates stores with an average size of approximately 
8,000 sq. ft., catering to the aspirations of India's growing middle class through quality fashi on at 
aﬀordable prices. Through its omni -channel platform, LimeRoad ( www.limeroad.com ), and presence 
across leading online marketplaces, V -Mart continues to strengthen its reach and deliver a seamless 
shopping exper ience across physical and digital channels.
