THE KING OF 3 -WHEELERS: TVS MOTOR COMPANY LAUNCHES TVS 
KING DURAMAX PLUS AND TVS KING DELUXE PLUS,  IN MEXICO  
 
Oaxaca, February 27,  2025:  TVS Motor Company  (TVSM) - a leading global 
automaker  in the two and three- wheeler segment -  today launch ed the TVS King 
Duramax Plus and TVS King Deluxe Plus three- wheelers  in Mexico . Both these  
passenger carriers are designed like a car on three wheel, with several smart features for driver and passenger comfort, with a durable and reliable engine for a low total cost 
of ownership. The engines deliver best -in-class power yet are fuel efficient and low on 
maintenance.  Both variants will be available in Mexico starting March 2025 through 
the retail channels of the Company’s official distributor – Motomex . 
 Launching the products,  Martin Corsunsky , Business Head, Latin America, TVS 
Motor Company, said,  “The three- wheeler segment is a great space for us . We have 
amongst the most advanced engineering and technology and we have years of experience, from all across the globe . The two launches today , further enhance the 
benchmark s in the three wheeler segment. Add to this, the service, spare and after -
sales capability of our partner Motomex , and TVS three wheelers become a complete 
package. We are excited by what drivers and passengers are set to experience in Mexico.”  
 The TVS King Duramax Plus  has a powerful liquid cooled 225 cc engine, best -in-
class power and gradability, all -gear-start technology, new LED headlamps, tubeless 
tyres and durable ribbed drum brakes among other features. There is extra space at the back for passenger comfort. It has a driver foot -rest and twin lockable dashboard 
storage. A new signature front grill considerably enhances the aesthetics.  
 The TVS King Deluxe Plus  comes with a 200 cc duralife engine , feather touch start 
with the i -Touch button, and several other segment -first features . It is built on a ladder 
type chassis with additional cross beam for passenger safety and strong build quality. 
King size driver seat, extra -large utility box , large foldable mirrors  and tubeless tyres 
make it a compelling proposition.  
 With the two new products, TVS M takes a significant step in Latin America, reinforcing 
its commitment to providing efficient and convenient mobility solutions.   TVS Motor  Company is globally recognized for its engineering, innovation,  and quality. 
The company’s products are sold in more than 80 countries across Asia, Africa , 
Europe and Latin America.
