Sub:  Intimation about Launch of LC-3 Cement (Portland Calcined Clay Limestone Cement) 
by the Company 
We are pleased to inform you that JK Cement Ltd. has achieved the milstone and became the 
first company in India and the entire Indian subcontinent to commence commercial 
production and dispatch of Portland Calcined Clay Limestone Cement (LC-3) at JK Cement 
Works, Mangrol, Dist: Chittorgarh, Rajasthan State (a Unit of JK Cement Ltd.) 
LC-3, produced under IS 181 89:2023 and certified by BIS, reduces CO: emissions by up to 
40% versus Ordinary Portland Cement, reinforcing the Company’s commitment to 
sustainability and climate-conscious construction practices. The LC-3 is targeted to serve 
marquee infrastructure projects in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh and other parts of 
India. This launch represents a significant step towards achieving the Company’s 
sustainability strategy built on four pillars: Decarbonization, Circular Economy, Resource 
Efficiency, and Sustainable Innovation. Its material composition makes it unique from other 
existing cement, which having Clinker-50%, Calcined-30% and Limestone-15%. This launch 
is expected to benefit the Company financially in the long run.
