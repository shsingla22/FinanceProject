Subject:   POWERGRID declared as successful bidder under TBCB 
 
Dear Sir,  
 
In terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended, this is to inform that Power Grid Corporation 
of India Limited (“POWERGRID”) has been declared as successful bidder under Tariff Based 
Competitive Bidding (“TBCB”) to establish Intra-State Transmission System for the project 
namely “Establishment of 3X500MVA 400/220kV sub-station at Humnabad in Bidar District 
and associated transmission lines” on Build, Own, Operate and Transfer (BOOT) basis, at 
quoted Annual Transmission Charges of Rs. 243.34 Crore. The Letter of Intent (LoI) dated                   
10th November, 2026 has been received by Power Grid Corporation of India Limited on                  
11th November, 2026. 
 
The project comprises establishment of new 400/220 kV Sub-station at Humnabad in 
Bidar District in the state of Karnataka. Further, the project also involves construction of 400kV 
and 220kV transmission lines traversing through the state of Karnataka along with the 
associated bays. 
 
With this project, POWERGRID has secured eleven (11) transmission system projects 
under TBCB in FY 2026-27 till date, with cumulative Annual Transmission Charges of                             
Rs. 8,106.97 Crore.
