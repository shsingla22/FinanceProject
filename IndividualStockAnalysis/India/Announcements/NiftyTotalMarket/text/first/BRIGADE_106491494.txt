BuzzWorks Partners with Born Good to Integrate High-Performance, Plant-Based Cleaning 
Solutions Across its Managed Workspaces 
 
Bengaluru, January 8, 2026 : BuzzWorks, the managed office space vertical of Brigade Group, has 
announced a strategic partnership with Born Good, a leading high-performance, plant-based 
cleaning solutions brand to enhance its toxin-free and ESG focused facility management. The 
partnership comes at a time when sustainability and employee wellness have become core decision 
drivers for global corporations. 
 
Commenting on the partnership, Siddharth Verma, Business Head, BuzzWorks India, said, “At 
BuzzWorks, sustainability is not a standalone initiative but a core operational principle. Partnering 
with Born Good allows us to integrate safer, high-performance, and more ESG-aligned cleaning 
solutions into our workspaces, reinforcing our commitment to long-term wellbeing for both people 
and the environment.” 
 
Born Good specializes in creating professional-grade cleaning products that prove powerful results 
do not require the use of harsh, toxic chemicals. Unlike conventional industrial cleaners that often 
contain ingredients linked to respiratory issues and skin irritation, Born Good’s formulations are 
USDA Certified BioPreferred and independently lab-tested for biodegradability and safety. 
 
Mohit Belani, Founder & CEO, Born Good said, “Great workspaces deserve great care. We're excited 
to work with BuzzWorks to prove that high-performance cleaning doesn't require harsh chemicals. 
Together, we're setting a new standard for what clean, safe, and comfortable workspaces should feel 
like.” 
 
Through this collaboration, BuzzWorks is reinforcing its position as a forward-thinking managed 
office provider by adopting a comprehensive facility management range that covers surface care, 
specialty maintenance, and wellness solutions. This initiative aligns with global ESG standards and 
highlights the role of everyday operational choices in reducing the overall carbon footprint of a 
corporate campus. 
 
About BuzzWorks:  Brigade Group entered the flexible office space segment with the launch of 
BuzzWorks in 2019. The brand offers managed, customisable workspaces through two key formats: 
Built-to-Suit solutions for enterprises with specific design and operational needs, and Ready-to-
Occupy offices for businesses seeking high-quality, immediately functional spaces. BuzzWorks 
primarily caters to large enterprises and growth-focused companies. Managed offices are 
increasingly seen as enablers of productivity, culture, and scale in today’s dynamic business 
landscape. With a sharp focus on experience, flexibility and scalability, the brand goes beyond 
workspaces; curating events, activations, and collaborative touchpoints that foster meaningful 
connections and enable professional and cultural exchange among its members. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
About Brigade Group: Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property 
developers with close to four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their 
stakeholders and winning their customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark buildings 
and transformed skylines across India in the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, 
Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, and GIFT City with developments across Residential, Office, Retail, 
Hospitality and Education Sectors.
