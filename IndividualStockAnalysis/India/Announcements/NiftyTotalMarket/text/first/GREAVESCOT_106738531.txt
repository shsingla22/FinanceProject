Ampere Magnus G Max Gets Smarter With 30+ Connected Features 
Powered by NxG.io for Everyday Bharat Riders  
• Introduces  IoT connectivity along with Cruise Control, Hill Hold Assist, Turn -by-Turn Navigation 
and a richer connected ecosystem  
• Introduces a new cinnamon copper colour option, alongside premium dual -tone colours, and 
is priced at INR 1,09,999  
 
Bengaluru, 12th August  2026:  Ampere, the  E2W brand from  Greaves Electric  Mobility  Limited , 
today  announce d a major  technology upgrade  for its best -selling – the Magnus G Max.   Powered by 
the new NxG.io software platform , the upgraded Magnus G Max now offers 30+ IoT-enabled features 
designed to make  every day  riding easier, safer and more convenient . The upgrade  introduces Cruise 
Control, Hill Hold, Turn -by-Turn Navigation, Find My Scooter and Ping My Scooter  on the Magnus G 
Max, making it a smarter alternative to traditional petrol scooters.   
 
The new connected features  get an intuitive and  vibrant  looking UI on Ampere Connect App  and have 
been designed with the realities of everyday Indian mobility in mind and address challenges familiar 
to riders in cities and towns.  Hill Hold brings greater confidence in stop -and-go traffic while navigating  
inclines and flyovers, Cruise Control improves comfort on longer stretches and Turn -by-Turn 
Navigation on the new 5 -inch PMVA digital cluster helps riders stay on course without repeatedly 
checking their phones. Find My Scooter and Ping My Scooter further  simplify locating the vehicle in 
crowded parking areas, bringing connected technology into practical everyday use , reflecting 
Ampere ’s approach to delivering technology with real everyday relevance . 
 
The connected ecosystem also includes real -time charging status, live tracking, geo -fencing, theft and 
tow alerts, remote vehicle diagnostics, service booking, charging history and battery health insights. 
Over -the-air update capability ensures the scooter ’s connected experience can continue to evolve 
over time. The upgraded Magnus G Max also introduces a new Cinnamon Copper colour option, 
alongside premium dual -tone colours, and is priced at INR 1,09,999.  
 
Speaking on the launch , Mr. Vikas Singh, Managing Director, Greaves Electric Mobility, said,  “For 
Bharat, smart mobility must also be practical, dependable and easy to use every day. With the 
upgraded Magnus G Max, Ampere is bringing meaningful intelligence to the family scooter segment  – 
features that help riders navigate better, ride more comfortably, locate their scooter easily and own 
an EV with greater confidence. This is technology designed around real customer needs, and it 
strengthens our commitment to making electric mobility the f irst choice for customers transitioning 
from petrol scooters.”  
 
The connected technology upgrade complements the performance and ownership proposition of the 
Magnus G Max. The scooter comes with a 3 kWh LFP battery and a 2.4 kW peak motor that offers an 
IDC-certified range of 1 42 km and a real -world range of over 100 km, with a top speed of 65 kmph. 
With 12 -inch wheels, telescopic front suspension and dual rear shock absorbers, the scooter is 
engineered to take on challenging  road conditions  across different terrains and weather conditions . 
The Magnus G Max continues to be  equipped with next -generation LFP battery technology , designed 
for a long battery life of 2 ,00,000 km . For customers looking to upgrade from petrol scooters, this long -
life battery proposition, combined with lower every day  running costs, delivers  a stronger total cost 
of ownership advantage across the vehicle’s lifecycle. Adding to the long -term ownership confidence, 
the scooter also comes with a 5 -year/75,000 km battery warranty , further strengthening long -term 
ownership confidence . 
 
 
Key highlights of the upgraded  Ampere Magnus G Max include:  
• Cruise Control for a more relaxed riding experience  
• Hill Hold Assist for greater confidence on inclines and stop -and-go traffic  
• Turn -by-Turn Navigation with a new  5-inch PMVA display  
• Smart IoT features including Find My Scooter, Ping My Scooter and OTA updates  
• High -performance motor delivering 130 Nm at the wheel  torque   
• New Cinnamon Copper colour option alongside premium dual -tone colour choices  
 
The upgraded  Ampere Magnus G Max will be available across Ampere dealerships, offering customers 
a smarter, feature -rich electric scooter built around everyday practicality, connected technology and 
long -term value .

About Greaves Cotton Limited:  
With a legacy of over 165 years, Greaves Cotton Limited is a diversified, future -ready engineering company 
delivering innovative and sustainable solutions to customers across key sectors. Renowned for its precision 
engineering and technology leadership, th e Company’s growth strategy is anchored on three core dimensions: 
accelerating its core businesses, building new muscle onto the core, and expanding into new horizons across 
Energy Solutions, Mobility Solutions, and Industrial Solutions.  
 
As one of India’s largest manufacturers of single -cylinder diesel engines, and a pioneer in introducing them for 
microcar applications in the European market, Greaves continues to reinforce its commitment to accessible, 
efficient, and reliable mobility. Th e Company also has a longstanding association with the Indian armed forces, 
with its products supporting a range of defence  and naval applications, embodying its role in industrial 
advancement and nation -building. Guided by its purpose of ‘Empowering Lives’, Greaves is committed to 
providing reliable products, sustainable technology, and customer -centric solutions. With its st rong engineering 
foundation and continuous innovation, the Company aims to enable people, businesses, and communities to 
progress with confidence in a future rooted in engineering excellence and driven by efficient energy.  
 
The investee companies of Greaves include Greaves Electric Mobility Limited (GEML) with its diverse portfolio of

EV focused NBFC that leverages cutting -edge technology to offer customised financing options & seamless buying
