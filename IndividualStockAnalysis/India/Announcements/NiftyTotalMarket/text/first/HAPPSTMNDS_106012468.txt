Happiest Minds Technologies recognized among Top 100 India’s  Best 
Workplaces ™ for Women 2024  
San Jose, London and Bengaluru, October 4, 2024:  Happiest Minds Technologies Limited (NSE: 
HAPPSTMNDS), a ‘Born Digital . Born Agile’, Mindful IT Company, today announced that it has been 
recognized among  the Top 100 India’s  Best Workplaces™  for Women 2024  by Great Place To 
Work® India. This prestigious recognition underscores the company’s unwavering commitment to 
fostering an inclusive, diverse, and supportive work environment for women at all levels.  
Great Place To Work® India’s recognition is based on a rigorous assessment that evaluates women's 
workplace experiences , the company’s policies and practices, and its overall commitment to 
fostering a culture of inclusion. Happiest Minds stood out among numerous organizations for its 
efforts to create a work environment where women feel supported, valued, and empowered to 
excel.  
Joseph Anantharaju, Executive Vice Chairman & CEO of Product and Digital Engineering 
Services  (PDES) , Happiest Minds,  said, "This recognition is a proud moment for all of us at Happiest 
Minds. It reflects our collective effort to create a workplace that values diversity and actively 
champions the growth and success of women across all levels. We are committed to further enhancing 
our policies and initiatives to ensure that everyone feels empowered, valued, and equipped to thrive 
in their careers."  
Sachin Khurana, Chief People Officer,  Happiest Minds,  said, "Our recognition as one of the best 
workplaces for women is a milestone and reflects  our commitment to nurturing a culture that 
prioritizes the well- being, growth, and success of our employees. We believe that an inclusive 
workplace leads to greater innovation, engagement, and overall success, and we will continue to foster 
a culture that  empowers all our employees to reach their full potential."  
Deepti Moolya, Associate Director & Lead, DEIB, Happiest Minds,  added,  "At Happiest Minds, 
inclusivity is at the heart of everything we do. Being recognized among India's Best Workplaces for Women is a testament to our continuous efforts to create an environment where women feel supported and respected and have equal opportun ities to advance and succeed. This achievement 
motivates us to keep pushing the boundaries of being a truly inclusive workplace."  
Happiest Minds has been recognized every  year for its considerable efforts in fostering a  people-
oriented workplace for its people. Some of its recent achievements include 50 Best Firms for Data Scientists to Work for 2024, Top 50 India’s Best Workplaces ™ in IT & IT -BPM 2023, Top 50 India’s 
Best Workplaces ™ for Building a Culture of Innovation 2023 , and Top 50 India’s Best Workplaces 
for Women ™ (2020, 2021, 2022, 2023 ). As part of its ongoing commitment to diversity and inclusion, 
Happiest Minds will continue to focus on initiatives that promote gender equality, provide opportunities for professional development, and foster a culture of respect and collaboration.
