Thomas Cook India & SOTC Travel sign  strategic MOU with 
Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams  Sri Lanka  
Announce partnership to unlock the India market potential  
 
Mumbai, February 5 , 2026: Thomas  Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel travel services company 
and its group company SOTC Travel, have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU ) with the Cinnamon 
Life at City of Dreams Sri Lanka, South Asia’s first and largest integrated resort. This colla boration brings 
together Thomas Cook India and SOTC’s  market  leadership and travel  expertise with Cinnamon Life at City 
of Drea ms’ one of a kind integrated hospitality, lifestyle, dining, retail, entertainment and event ecosystem. 
The partnership aims to accelerate visitation from India to Cinnamon Life by jointly developing enriched travel 
products, enhancing awareness, and creating elevated, seamless experiences for Indian travellers.  The 
Memorandum of Understanding was signed by Mr. Rajeev Kale, President & Country Head, Holidays, 
MICE, Visa -  Thomas Cook (India) Limited, Mr. SD Nandakumar, President & Country Head - Holidays 
& Corporate Tours - SOTC Travel  and Mr. Hishan Singhawansa - CEO, Cinnamon Hotels & Resorts . 
 
This strategic partnership intends to capitalise on Thomas Cook India and SOTC Travel’s powerful  leisure , 
MICE & Business -BLeisure segments in a multi -pronged initiative that focuses on: product development, 
knowledge & training, integrated marcom and consumer activations  – to build top ‑of‑mind visibility and drive 
visitations to C innamon Life at City of Dreams in Sri Lanka.  
 
Rajeev Kale - President & Country Head, Holidays, MICE, Visa – Thomas Cook (India) Limited said, 
“Through our strategic partnership with Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams Sri Lanka, we are excited to co-
create a compelling portfolio around one of South Asia’s most distinctive new hospitality destinations. 
Cinnamon Life brings together contemporary accommodation, a diverse culinary landscape, premium retail, 
entertainment, event and wellness facilities — offering Indian travellers a truly all- in-one experience within an 
integrated setting . Supported by our strategic omnichannel model, goldmine of consumers  and deep market 
expertise, this partnership enables us curate differentiated offerings across our segments. Together, we aim 
to unlock the significant India market potential for Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams and deliver elevated, seamless experiences for our customers .” 
 
SD Nandakumar, President & Country Head - Holidays & Corporate Tours - SOTC Travel said,  “We are 
delighted to partner with Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams Sri Lanka, a landmark property that aligns strongly with the evolving expectations of today’s Indian traveller. With luxury stays, curated dining and nightlife, premium lifestyle zones and st ate-of-the-art venues, Cinnamon Life offers a comprehensive proposition for 
India’s leisure, business and MICE travellers alike. Via our  partnership we aim to co- create innovative and 
differentiated products and go- to-market with a comprehensive marketing strategy - to build awareness and 
drive visitations  for Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams across India’s metros as well as Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets.”  
 
Hishan Singhawansa - CEO, Cinnamon Hotels & Resorts  said,  Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams Sri Lanka 
marks a transformative moment for the country’s tourism landscape, bringing together world- class hospitality, 
lifestyle, entertainment, and MICE within a single integrated destination. India is a key growth market for us, 
and our strategic partnership with Thomas Cook India and SOTC Travel enables us to tap into their strong 
distribution, deep consumer insight, and market leadership. Together, we aim to co- create differentiated, 
experience- led travel offerings that drive sustained visitation and position Cinnamon Life as a compelling 
choice for Indian leisure, business, and MICE travellers.”
