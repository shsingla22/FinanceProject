Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
In accordance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations And Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, as amended and  Clause 4 of the Company’s Policy on determination of materiality of events, we, 
hereby, inform you that “The Oberoi Grand, Kolkata” (“Hotel”), owned and operated by the Company, will 
close rooms, suites and the two restaurants as part of the 1st phase of the hotels renovation from 15th August 
2024.  During this phase of the renovation all back of house areas of the hotel will also be renovated.   
 
The banquet facilities of the Hotel will continue to operate and will close on 15th March, 2025 for 
renovation . 
 
The first phase of renovation will be completed in 18 months and the Hotel will reopen with 50 rooms and 
suites, all the restaurants, banqueting, back of house facilities and the MEP infrastructure fully upgraded.  
 
The second phase of renovation will ta ke an additional 12 months, after which, the hotel will have 200 
completely renovated rooms and suites.  
 
The Company is filing the requisite details of the Order as set out as Annexure A hereto pursuant to 
Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with  SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD -
PoD1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated 13th July 2023.  
 
The above may please be taken on record.
