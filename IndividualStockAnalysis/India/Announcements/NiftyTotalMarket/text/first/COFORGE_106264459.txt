Gurugram , June 7, 2025 : Coforge Limited  (NSE: COFORGE), a global leader in digital services 
and solutions, today inaugurated ‘ The Coforge Public Library ’ in Gurugram – a one-of-its-kind 
public library , near Badshahpur  Chowk , Sohna Road , Sector 48, Gurugram .  
 
The Coforge Public Library is a flagship CSR initiative of Coforge. Last year in Feb 2024, the first 
Coforge Public Library was inaugurated in Noida. This is the second li brary to be inaugurated 
under the same initiative.  
 The Coforge Public L ibrary , Gurugram was inaugurated  by Anuradha Sehgal, Global Head – 
Branding and CSR at Coforge, in the presence of local schools ’ and communit ies’ representative s. 
Speaking at the launch, Anuradha explained the motivation behind the project : “The Coforge 
Public Library is a heartfelt initiative born from Coforge’s commitment towards social 
responsibility . The space has been designed with great love and care, the collection of 16000 
books has been thoughtfully curated. Today, we are delighted to open its doors to the people of 
Gurugram. We hope this beautiful space will  be a sanctuary of knowledge and lifelong learning; 
we hope it will inspir e people  from all walks of life  and across all ages  to delve into the wonderful 
world of books , a time -tested repository of wisdom and knowledge down the ages.”  
 Located on the Ground Floor of Bestech Business Center, Badshahpur, Sohna Road Highway, 
Sector 48, Gurugram, the  library is housed  in a 15,000 -square -foot space  and offers  a seating 
capacity of 160 . The library is well-lit with pleasing interiors, its large windows offering refreshing 
views of the greens outside. The aisles have been kept wide to ensure wheelchair access. iPads 
provide access to thousands digital titles.  There’s also a children’s zone  to encourage early 
learners to explore books in a playful, stimulating environment.   
 The library boasts  an enviable  collection  of 16 ,000+ books  across all popular genres  including 
2000 Hindi books  — from fiction, non -fiction,  children’s literature to self -help, business, 
spirituality, art, technology , photography, poetry and more . The collection includes works by over 
13,000 unique authors , from 350+ leading publishers such as Penguin, HarperCollins, Amar Chitra 
Katha, Oxford University Press,  and Sahitya Akademi  among others.  The library is fully digitized 
for cataloguing and management, ensuring a seamless reader experience.  

 
 
The library will be open from 9 am to 8 pm, seven days a week. The Google  map location of the 
Coforge Public Library can be found here.  
 
Sudhir Singh, CEO & Executive Director,  Coforge,  said, “The Coforge Public Library reflects our 
belief that access to knowledge is a powerful catalyst for long -term social transformation. Its  
vision is to support India’s journey towards comprehensive development by providing individuals 
from all walks of life the opportunity to access knowledge, learn, and grow. Our aim is to build a 
lasting network of free public libraries across India — beacons of hope, inclusivity, and community 
empowerment.”
