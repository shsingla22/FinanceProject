HCLTech  included in S&P Global Sustainability Yearbook for the third 
year in a row  
 
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, May  15, 2025—HCLTech,  a leading global technology company, 
has been named  in the S&P Global Sustainability Yearbook 2025 for the third consecutive year , 
underscoring  its strong  commitment to integrating  sustainable practices into its business 
operations.  
 
"We are honored to be a part of this globally respected platform consistently . It is a validation 
of our sustainability strategy anchored on the unique  Act.  Pact.  Impact framework," said Vipul 
Arora, Global Head of Sustainability at HCLTech.  
 
HCLTech is at the forefront of sustainable  business  practices.  Since FY 2020 , the company  has 
successfully  reduced its  Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emissions by 25%.  This commitment to 
climate action is further reflected in its energy -efficient practices, waste reduction and green 
building initiatives,  with 98% of its owned buildings achieving 'Platinum' certification.  
HCLTech's  efforts to integrate climate risks into its business continuity planning  ensure  
resilience and sustainability across its global operations . 
 
HCLTech has received the World's Most Ethical Companies® recognition for two years from 
Ethisphere, a global leader in defining and advancing ethical business practices standards . 
 
Recently, HCLTech  received an 'A -' rating for Climate and a 'B' rating for Water Security from 
CDP, the world's foremost independent climate disclosure system. These ratings underscore 
HCLTech's proactive approach to reducing carbon emissions, enhancing water security, and 
ensuring transparency in climate and water -related disclosures.  The company also received 
the Gold status in EcoVadis Sustainability Rating 2024 , reinforcing HCLTech’s commitment to 
sustainability and its efforts to reduce waste, energy consumption  and emissions.  
 
Through the HCLFoundation, the company supports community -based water projects, 
including the rejuvenation of water bodies and rainwater harvesting. In FY24, HCLTech 
replenished 32 times the water it consumed across its India operations.  
 
For more information on HCLTech's  sustainability  efforts , visit hcltech.com/sustainability
