Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in  
Vijayawada  
 
New Delhi, 18th September  2024: Lemon Tree Hotels announced its latest 
signing – Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels, Metropolitan, Vijayawada . The 
property, which shall be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -
owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, is expected to open in FY 2026.  
 
Vijayawada, a bustling  city in Andhra Pradesh, India, is celebrated for its rich 
cultural heritage, historic landmarks, and vibrant energy. Nestled along the 
banks of the Krishna River, it serves as a major hub for industry and commerce. 
The city boasts several ancient temples, with the Kanaka Durga Temple being a 
significant Hindu pilgrimage s ite. With its mix of historical charm, cultural 
richness, and modern flair, Vijayawada offers travel lers a distinctive and 
rewarding experience.  
 
Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels,  Metropolitan , Vijayawada  will feature 44  well-
appoin ted rooms, a restaurant, meeting room, and banquet facilities . The 
Vijayawada I nternational Airport is about 15 kms from the property while 
Vijayawada  Railway Station is about 6  kms away. The hotel is also well 
connected by roadways for both public and private transport.  
 
Mr. Vila s Pawar, CEO — Managed & Franchise Business, Lemon Tree Hotels said 
on the occasion , “We are excited to share the expansion of our portfolio in 
Vijayawada  where we have one upcoming and two operational properties. This 
addition will complement our existing four hotels and four  upcoming properties 
in Andhra Pradesh .”
