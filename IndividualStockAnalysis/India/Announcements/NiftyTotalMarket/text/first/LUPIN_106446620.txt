Lupin Announces Closure of Inspection by U.S.  FDA at its Nagpur Unit -1 Facility  
with No Observations  
 
Mumbai,  November 15, 2025:  Global pharma major Lupin  Limited  (Lupin)  today  announced that the 
United States  Food  and Drug  Administration  (U.S. FDA)  has completed  a product specific  Pre-
Approval  Inspection  at its Unit -1 oral solid  dosage  manufacturing  facility  in Nagpur . The inspection  
was carried  out from November  10 to November 14 , 2025, and concluded  with  zero  483 
observations.  
 
Nilesh  Gupta,  Managing Director,  Lupin  said,  “The successful  outcome  of the U.S. FDA inspection  at 
our Nagpur  Unit -1 facility  exemplifies  our commitment to uphold and maintain the highest standards 
of quality , compliance , and safety across our facilities. We remain dedicated to  improving  the lives 
of our patients globally.”
