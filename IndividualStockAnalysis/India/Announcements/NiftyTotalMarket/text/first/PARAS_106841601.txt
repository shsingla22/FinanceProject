CORRIGENDUM TO THE POSTAL BALLOT NOTICE DATED OCTOBER 29, 2026  
 
This corrigendum is being issued in continuation of the Postal Ballot Notice dated October 29, 2026, issued by 
Paras Defence and Space Technologies Limited ("Company") and submitted to the Stock Exchanges  today , i.e. 
November 03, 2026.  
  
The Company wishes to inform its Members that an inadvertent error  has occurred in the wording of the resolution 
forming part of the Postal Ballot Notice pertaining to the approval of Material Related Party Transaction(s) between 
the Company and Paras Anti -Drone Technologies Private Limited ("Subsidiary Company").  
  
In the said resolution, the words:  
 
"from the date of the Annual General Meeting until the date of the next Annual General Meeting"  
 
shall be read and construed as:  
 
"for a period of one year from the date of approval".  
  
It is hereby clarified that the aforesaid correct tenure of one year from the date of approval has already been 
correctly mentioned in the Explanatory Statement accompanying the said resolution.  
  
The Company further clarifies  that, due to an inadvertent administrative error, an incorrect version of the Postal 
Ballot Notice was uploaded only on the websites of the Stock Exchanges and has not been circulated to the 
shareholders. The correct version of the Postal Ballot Notice  will be circulated shortly to the Members of the 
Company.  
  
Accordingly, this corrigendum is being issued to rectify the inadvertent error in the wording of the resolution and to 
ensure consistency between the resolution and the Explanatory Statement. The correct version of the  Postal Ballot 
Notice is enclosed herewith .  
  
You are therefore requested to take note of the a forementioned  correction  and take the enclosed  corrected Postal 
Ballot Notice on record.  
 
We regret the inconvenience caused.  
 
Thanking  you,
