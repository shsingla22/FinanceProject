Pine Labs to Deploy 10 Lakh Soundboxes Across India to Power Digital Commerce  
 
Mumbai , September  24, 202 6 – As digital payments move beyond small ticket transactions, Pine Labs 
today announced a strategic invest ment  to deploy 10 lakh Soundboxes across India. The investment is 
aimed at strengthening digital payments infrastructure in India . 
 
This development reflects a broader evolution in India's digital payments ecosystem as UPI and other 
digital payment modes gain traction beyond everyday low -ticket transactions.  Through the planned 
deployment, Pine Labs also aims to improve UPI penetration  into new markets  and business segments . 
 
“With new monetization levers emerging, we want to walk the talk by investing back into the ecosystem 
and deepen digital payments adoption. Pine Labs ' commitment to deploy 10 lakh soundboxes reflects our 
belief that sustainable economic s and financial inclusion can reinforce each other ,” said B Amrish Rau, 
CEO, Pine Labs .  
 
Built on cloud -first architecture, the soundbox es enable merchants to receive real -time audio 
confirmation of the payment transaction . The platform supports remote device management, over -the-
air software updates, and centralized monitoring, helping merchants scale their payment infrastructure 
efficiently as transaction volumes grow.  
 
The rollout will support businesses as they participate in India's next wave of digital commerce growth.
