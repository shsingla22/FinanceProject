Commenting on the performance, Mr. Amit Dahanukar , Chairman  & Managing Director , said  
“Integration Update : Post Q1, the states which transitioned out of TSMA under TI operations have been stabilized 
- marking another step towards successful integration. The last remaining state will also be transitioned towards end 
of FY27. As a result, exceptional costs relati ng to the transition have significantly reduced during Q2 FY27 and will 
continue at the same levels over the next 2 quarters.  
Volumes & Market Share: While primary volumes in Q2 have remained flattish, our secondary volumes have 
remained robust and shown an impressive 8% growth on QoQ basis with Imperial Blue secondaries growing at 8.5% 
and existing business secondaries growing at ~7%. Imperial Blue has  further strengthened its position with a Pan -
India market share gain of 100bps+ during the quarter. The market share gains were led by strong performance in 
Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Assam and Rajasthan. Existing business volumes have grown in 
Karnataka and Eastern region, with other key states performing consistently well. Our NSR per case has moderated 
slightly during the quarter on account of state mix, however, we are confident of sustained growth in NSR going 
forward. The NSR growth will  be driven by favourable state -mix, price increases and expected new launches (FY28 
onwards).  
Margins and Inflation: Ongoing geopolitical tensions have resulted in continued inflationary pressures across 
packaging inputs, particularly glass, resulting in a meaningful increase in packaging costs during the last 2 quarters. 
While exiting Q2 FY27, ENA prices have also start ed to show an inflationary trend with rising fuel and grain costs. 
Consequently, gross margins were under pressure. However, as committed in Q1, we have been working 
consistently on our cost optimization initiatives which have shown positive results, helpi ng us protect our margins.  
Marketing Initiatives: We have consistently focused on our brand communications and have made impactful strides 
during the current quarter. On the proud occasion of Independence Day, we had two great outcomes. Firstly, Mansion 
House Brandy and Monarch Legacy Edition Brandy were showcased in Times Square, New York City, for 72 -hours 
with 2,500+ plays and 1.2 mn + visitors. Secondly, TI launched its first ATL campaign for Imperial Blue Packaged 
Drinking Water under “Men Will Be Men” which continued with its central theme of humour.  
Policy Update: Policy and regulatory environment continued to see encouraging developments in Q2. Karnataka, 
on the back of the recent excise policy reforms, has witnessed strong growth in the P&A category with significant 
benefits for both Imperial Blue Whisky and Mansi on House Brandy. Further, there are early signals of reform in Tamil 
Nadu, the largest Brandy market in India. Mansion House Reserve Brandy, has seen 25%+ QoQ volume growth with 
50% YoY growth in Sep -26, emerging as the highest volume month ever.  
Going Forward: Our focus continues to deliver profitable growth in Imperial Blue and Mansion House, with a clear 
vision on long -term value creation. We will continue to accelerate our luxury and super -premium portfolio across key 
markets, while leveraging IB’s strong dis tribution network to introduce new products across premium price points. ”

About Tilaknagar Industries - 
Tilaknagar Industries Ltd. (TI) is one of India’s leading IMFL manufacturers, with 90 years of excellence in the 
consumer goods category. Founded in 1933 by Shri Mahadev L. Dahanukar as Maharashtra Sugar Mills, the 
company was originally engaged in the man ufacture of sugar and allied products. The Dahanukar family continues 
to be the promoter of TI, guided by the same vision and values as its founders. Under the leadership of Shri Amit 
Dahanukar, TI has grown to become the largest manufacturer of premium br andy in India. The company leads the 
spirits market with its two ‘Millionaire’ brandy brands, Mansion House and Courrier Napoleon, and has a strong 
presence across whisky, rum and gin through Mansion House Whisky, Madira  Rum and Blue Lagoon Gin. TI has 
further strengthened its whisky portfolio with the acquisition of Imperial Blue, a well -established legacy brand in India. 
Reflecting its focus on premiumisation, TI’s luxury vertical, House of TI, is anchored by Monarch Le gacy Edition  
Brandy , its first luxury brandy, and Seven Islands Pure Malt Whisky, an Indo -Scottish luxury  whisky. Alongside these, 
its portfolio also extends to strategic partnerships and investments in emerging Indian craft spirits companies, 
including Bartisans, Spaceman Spirits Lab and Bodega Suprema No.5.
