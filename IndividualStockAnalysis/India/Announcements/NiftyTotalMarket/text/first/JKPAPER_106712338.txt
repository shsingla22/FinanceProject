Press Release 
 
Unaudited Results for Quarter ended June,2026 
 
Consolidated Quarterly Turnover at Rs.1,999 Cr, up by 13%,  
PAT increases by 63 % 
JK Paper Ltd., (JKPL) one of India’s largest Paper & Packaging Solutions Companies, 
recorded a Consolidated Turnover of Rs. 1,998.67 Cr (up 13 %), EBITDA of Rs.320.90 
Cr (up 18%) and Profit after Tax (PAT) of Rs.136.27 Cr (63% increase), for Quarter-1 
ended June,2026.  
 
Commenting on the results, Shri Harsh Pati Singhania said, “While the Paper and Board 
market remained challenging, higher volume and improved product mix led to better 
performance during the current quarter as compared to the corresponding period. The 
Company’s packaging conversion businesses have also done well both in terms of 
revenue and profit.  
 
He further added, “JK Paper has acquired additional stake of 15.40% in Borkar Packaging 
Private Limited during the quarter in line with the Share Purchase Subscription and 
Shareholders Agreement entered with them.” 
 
The Company has commenced production of Hardwood Bleach Chemical Thermo-
Mechanical Pulp (“BCTMP”) Plant at Unit CPM, Gujarat, from 30th June 2026. 
 
Focus on social farm forestry continued around all plant locations to strengthen availability 
of adequate raw material besides containing cost and improving green cover in the states 
of Odisha, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. During the Quarter, 
2.61 Cr. Saplings have been planted covering 20,177 Acres. 
 
The Company’s CSR activities covered 876 villages across 6 states and 1 Union Territory 
with over 6 Lac direct beneficiaries for FY 26-27 Q1. The key areas of intervention include 
Livelihood Promotion through Farm and Non-Farm Based Income Generating Activities, 
Women Empowerment, Education & Skill Development, Environment & Natural Resource 
Management, Promotion of Art & Livelihood, Sports and Inclusive Community 
Development encompassing Healthcare, supporting the specially abled person & Rural 
Infrastructure.
