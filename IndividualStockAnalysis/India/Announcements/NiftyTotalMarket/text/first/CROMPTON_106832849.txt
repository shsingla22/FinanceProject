needs, Crompton has launched a range of 5.9L Instan t Water Heaters. Designed to bring together faster heating and enhanced 
capacity, the new water heater range is built to ad dress the needs of modern households and kitchens. 
 
For households, getting hot water without long wait s while dealing with varying water conditions and p ressure can be an 
important consideration when choosing an instant wa ter heater. Hard water can lead to mineral deposits  that affect the heating 
process over time, while homes in high-rise buildin gs may also experience higher water pressure. Addre ssing these considerations, 
Crompton’s Range of 5.9L Instant Water Heaters offe rs faster heating and more hot water capacity, whil e being engineered to 
perform in hard water conditions. 
 
Crompton’s Range of 5.9L Instant Waters combines fe atures designed to support reliable and convenient hot water use: 
 
 Anti-Scale Technology Designed for Hard Water : Helps protect the heating element from hard-water  deposits upto 1000 
PPM 
 Powerful 3kW Heating Element: Enables approximately 40% faster heating, reducing the waiting time for hot water.  
 Advanced 4-Level Safety and 6.5 Bar Pressure Rating : Equipped with an auto thermostat, thermal cut-out a nd pressure 
safety valve, fire safety providing four layers of protection during operation. It is also designed to  withstand high water 
pressure, making it suitable for high-rise building s. 
 Food-Grade Stainless-Steel Tank: Built with a food-grade stainless-steel tank for du rability and long-lasting performance. 
 Compact Wall-Mount Design: Designed for wall mounting, the water heater featur es a compact form, pristine-finish 
outer body and LED indicators that complement conte mporary household spaces. 
 Enhanced 5.9L Capacity: Offers approximately 18% more hot water capacity to  cater to everyday household 
requirements. 
 Warranty: Backed by a 5-year warranty on the tank, 2-year war ranty on the heating element and 2-year warranty on  the
