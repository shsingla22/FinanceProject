Subject:  Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Institution of Civil Defamation Suit and its update  
 
Dear Sir / Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”)  in accordance with Securities and Exchange Board of India Circular 
No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD -PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023, we hereby inform you that the 
Company has instituted a civil defamation suit before the Hon’ble High Court of Delhi on February 19, 
2026 against Sakshi Media House (including Sakshi Telugu Newspaper  and news channel Sakshi TV ) 
and other concerned parties, in connection with a series of false, baseless, malicious, and defamatory 
publications and social media allegations targeting the Company.  
 
The aforesaid publications are wholly unfounded and have been made with the apparent intent to 
tarnish the reputation, goodwill, and credibility of the Company. In order to safeguard its interests and 
protect its stakeholders, the Company has initiated appropriate legal proceedings . 
 
The Company has, inter alia, sought the following interim prayers befo re the Hon’ble High Court Delhi.  
 
1. Permanent and Mandatory Injunction  restraining the Defendants from posting, publishing, 
uploading, writing, speaking, distributing, broadcasting, or republishing any false, misleading, and 
defamatory content concerning the Company in any manner whatsoever, including on platforms 
owned, controlled, or maintained by Defendant Nos. 1 to 4, as detailed in Annexure – A. 
 
2. Permanent and Mandatory Injunction  directing the Defendants, their agents, representatives, 
affiliates, or any person acting on their behalf or under their authority, to forthwith remove and 
cease dissemination of all false, misleading, and defamatory content published against the 
Company on all platforms, including those maintained by Defendant Nos. 5 to 8. as detailed in 
Annexure – A. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
3. Damages of Rs.100 Crores (Rupees One Hundred Crores)  against Defendant Nos. 1 to 4 as 
specified in Annexure – A, on account of the grave and irreparable harm caused to the Company’s 
reputation and business by their defamatory acts.  
 
The details as required under SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD -PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated 
July 13, 2023, in respect of the institution of the aforesaid legal proceedings are enclosed as 
Annexure – A. 
 
Further, we wish to inform you that the company has filed a suit vide CS(OS) No.162/2026, before 
the Hon’ble Delhi High Court against the defendants therein inter alia seeking mandatory injunction 
i.e., restraining the Defendants from posting, publishing, uploading, writing, speaking, distributing, 
broadcasting, or republishing any false, misleading, and defamatory content concerning the Company 
in any manner whatsoever, including on platforms owned, controlled, or maintained by Defendant 
Nos. 1 to 4 and to forthwith remove and cease dissemination of all false, misleading, and defamatory 
content published against the Company on all platforms, including those maintained by Defendant 
Nos. 5 to 8 and to award the damages of Rs.100 Crores against Defendant 1to 4. Upon submissions 
made by the counsel of the company,  
 
The Hon’ble Court, vide its Order dated 19.02.2026, has been pleased to grant, inter alia, the 
following directions:  
 
a) The defendant  nos. 1 to 4 and defendant  no. 9 are restrained from posting, publishing, 
uploading, writing, speaking, distributing and/or republishing any false, misleading and 
defamatory content against the plaintiffs, on any web platform or in a physical newspaper, 
including, the platforms maintained by defendant nos. 6 to 8 : 
b) The defendant nos. 1 to 4 are directed to take down the impugned article and impugned reports, 
and any other publication containing identical as well as similar statements, maintained on their 
profiles on the platforms of defendant nos. 6 to 8  
c) The defendant nos. 1 to 3 are directed to take -down within 24 hours the impugned article dated 
14th February, 2026, or any other related articles published by defendant nos. 1 to 3, on their 
platform  www.sakshi.com  
d) Defendant nos. 1 to 3 are directed to take -down, within 24 hours of this order, the impugned 
reports dated 14th February, 2026, or any other defamatory interview/ debate/ report published 
under the name of “Sakshi TV ”, on various online platforms maintained by defendant nos. 6 to 8, 
the links of which, are given in Annexure 1 of this order;  
e) In case, the aforesaid direction is not complied with by defendant nos. 1 to 3 within 24 hours of 
this present order, the defendant nos. 6 to 8 shall take down the impugned content, details of 
which, are given in Annexure 1 of this order;   
and such other directions as mentioned in the order  passed by the Hon’ble High Court of Delhi . 
The details as required under the aforesaid SEBI Circular with respect to the update on the said  
matter are enclosed as Annexure – B. 
A copy of the Order passed by the Hon’ble Court is enclosed herewith . 
The Company remains firmly committed to protecting its reputation, preserving stakeholder 
confidence, and ensuring accurate, fair, and transparent dissemination of information. The Company 
will continue to pursue all lawful remedies available to it in this regard.  
We request you to take this on record, and to treat the same as compliance with the applicable 
provisions of the SEBI Listing Regulations.
