Dear Sirs,  
 
This has reference to Company’s Annual General Meeting  scheduled for 29th August 2025 
wherein one of the agenda items  pertains to  appointment of M/s. Makarand M. Joshi and Co., 
(MMJC) as Secretarial Auditor of the Company . Over the last few days, the Company has 
received a query  from  section of investors in relation to fees paid to the outgoing Secretarial 
Auditor.    
 
To enable Company’s shareholders to better appreciate and understand the proposal, we would 
like to clarify that as mentioned in  the explanatory statement of the AGM Notice , Company 
proposes to pay INR 4,50,000 towards secretarial audit  to M/s. MMJC . Further, in the interest 
of transparency, Company has also disclosed in the explanatory statement of the AGM notice 
that Company proposes to pay INR 2,40,000 towards other permissible services such as Annual 
Secretarial Compliance Report , certification in Form MGT -8 etc. Hence, the total fees proposed 
to be paid to M/s. MMJC is INR 6,90,000. The Company had paid INR 6,85,000 to outgoing 
Secretarial Auditor for these same services translating into an overall increase of 0.73%.  
 
This is for your information and records.
