Dear Sirs,  
Sub: Imposition of anti-dumping duty on imports from China and Vietnam   
 
On February 12, 2025 , we had informed that the DGTR (Ministry of Commerce & Industry) 
had recommended levy of a duty on all the imports of solar glass originating in or exported 
from China and Vietnam, for a period of five years from the date of issue of the preliminary 
duty notification No. 26/2024-Customs (ADD) dated December 04, 2024.  This had been 
forwarded to the Ministry of Finance for confirmation and issue of the necessary 
notification.  
We are pleased to inform that Ministry of Finance has vide its notification dated May 08, 
2025, notified levy of anti -dumping duty for a period of five years from December 04, 2024, 
on import of solar glass from China and Vietnam .    
The Company welcomes this decision of the imposition of anti -dumping duty.  
 
We firmly believe that while restoring fair competition and creating a level playing field for 
domestic manufacturers, this will encourage rapid and significant growth in Domestic 
manufacturing of solar glass, leading to exponential growth in India’s solar glass industry.  
 
The notification issued by the Ministry of Finance is  attached.   
 
You are requested to take the same on record.  
 Your faithfully  
For Borosil Renewables Limited
