Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
(SEBI (LODR ) Regulations , 2015) , this is to inform that the Company has received notices dated 29th May 2025 
from National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and BSE Limited (BSE)  regarding non- compliance with 
Regulations  17(1), 17(2A), 18(1), 19(1), 19 (2), 20(2), 20(2A), and 21(2) of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 
of the SEBI (LODR ) Regulations , 2015 and have impos ed a fine of Rs. 14,16,000/- each for such non- compliance.  
 
In response to the aforesaid notice s, NTPC Limited vide letter dated 30th May 2025 has submitted to NSE & BSE 
that NTPC  Limited  is a Government Company as per Section 2(45) of the Companies Act, 2013 and as per the 
Articles of Association of the Company, the power to appoint or remove the Directors vests with the President of 
India through its Administrative Ministry i.e. Ministry  of Powe (MoP).  MoP vide its orders dated 16th April 2025 & 
15th May 2025, has appointed five Independent Directors on the Board of NTPC Limited. Consequentially, the 
Statutory Committees have been reconstituted by the Board in line with SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, from 
time to time. The Company, currently, is in compliance with Regulation 17(1)(a), 17(2A), 18(1), 19(1), 19(2), 
20(2), 20(2A) and 21(2) of SEBI (LODR) Regulation s, 2015.  
 
Further, we are consistently following up with MoP for appointment of requisite number of independent directors 
on the Board of NTPC Limited so as to comply with Regulation 17(1) (b) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.  
Hence, the aforesaid fine w.r.t. non- compliance of the SEBI (LODR), Regulations, 2015 should not be leviable . 
 
Further, i n continuation of our letter dated 18th March  2025  intimating the fine levied by National Stock Exchange 
of India Limited (NSE) and BSE Limited (BSE) regarding non- compliance with the provisions of SEBI (LODR) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that such non- compliance, imposition of fines by the Stock Exchanges and 
replies thereto by the Company were placed before the Board of Directors at its meeting held on 24th May 2025. 
The Board took note of the same.  
  
This is for your information and dissemination.
