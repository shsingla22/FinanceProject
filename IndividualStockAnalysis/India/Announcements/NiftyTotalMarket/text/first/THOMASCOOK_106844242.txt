STERLING POSTS 27TH CONSECUTIVE PROFITABLE QUARTER  
RECORD EXPANSION TAKES PORTFOLIO PAST 4,000- KEY MILESTONE  
Strong H1 performance, record resort openings, a growing managed-resort business and a debt -
free balance sheet position Sterling for its next phase of growth.  
Key Highlights 
• 27 Consecutive Proﬁtable Quarters:  Sustains proﬁtability even in the seasonally weakest 
quarter, with record Q2 revenue of ₹1,272 million, up 17% YoY .  
• Biggest Quarterly Expansion:  Opens 7 resorts in Q2, taking the portfolio to 83 resorts with 
over 4,000 keys.  
• Strong H1 Performance:  Revenue grows 19% to ₹2,971 million; EBITDA rises 15% to ₹910 
million and PBT increases 22% to ₹623 million.  
• Improving Operating Metrics:  H1 occupancy increases 500 bps to 68%, while ARR rises 11% 
to ₹7,222.  
• Strong Balance Sheet:  Debt -free, with cash reserves of ₹3,805 million, providing signiﬁcant 
headroom to invest and expand.  
Chennai, 4 November 2026:  Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited (SHRL), one of India's leading 
hospitality companies, continued its strong growth trajectory, reporting its 27th consecutive 
proﬁtable quarter  and delivering record Q2 revenue  of ₹1,272 million, up 17% year -on-year, despite 
the quarter traditionally being the weakest in the hospitality calendar. Q2 EBITDA stood at ₹289 million, while occupancy improved to 60%, ARR increased 12% to ₹6,494 &  Total RevPAR grew 12%.  
Record Expansion; Crosses 4,000 Keys  
Sterling recorded its highest -ever quarterly expansion, opening seven resorts in Q2 FY27 and taking 
its portfolio to 83 resorts, crossing the milestone of 4,000 keys. Six additional resort sign -ups during 
the quarter further strengthened its expansion pipeline.  
The managed -resort business is emerging as a signiﬁcant growth driver. Q2 revenue from managed 
resorts grew 67% year -on-year to ₹379 million, contributing 23% of enterprise revenue. With 42 
managed resorts, Sterling is scaling its asset -right model, expan ding its footprint across India while 
building a meaningful and growing contribution to overall turnover. 
Strong Operating Performance in H1  
Sterling delivered a strong H1 FY27 , with Revenue from Operations growing 19% year -on-year to 
₹2,971 million. EBITDA grew 15% to ₹910 million, while Proﬁt Before Tax increased 22% to ₹623 
million, reﬂecting sustained operating momentum and improving business fundamentals. 
  
 
 
 
The company's ﬁrst -half performance was supported by higher room demand and improved pricing. 
Occupancy increased 500 bps to 68%, while ARR rose 11% to ₹7,222. Sterling continued to focus on 
improving revenue productivity and maximising revenue beyond roo ms. 
Strong Balance Sheet to Support Growth  
Sterling ended Q2 FY27 with cash reserves of ₹3,805 million, up 12% sequentially, while continuing 
to maintain a zero -debt balance sheet. Operating free cash ﬂow for H1 grew 16% to ₹593 million. 
This ﬁnancial strength provides the ﬂexibility to invest i n resort upgrades, technology, guest 
experience and expansion opportunities, while maintaining capital discipline.  
Guest Experience and Industry Recognition  
Sterling's focus on guest experience is reﬂected in its improved TripAdvisor rating of 4.63, compared 
with 4.60 a year ago, with 31 resorts ranked No. 1  in their respective areas.  
The company was recognised as the "Fastest Growing Leisure Hotel Chain"  at the Today's Traveller 
Awards 2026 for the third consecutive time. Sterling ONE , its distribution platform, received the 
"Smart Tech Enabled Platform of the Year 2026 –  B2B" award at the Indian Travel & Technology 
Summit & Awards 2026.  
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director & CEO, SHRL,  said:  
“Our 27th consecutive proﬁtable quarter reﬂects the resilience of Sterling's business model and our 
ability to sustain growth through diﬀerent demand cycles. Delivering record Q2 revenue in a 
seasonally weaker leisure quarter is signiﬁcant alongside as  our increasingly diversiﬁed operating 
model gains scale and momentum.  
Our record pace of openings, crossing 4,000 keys, and a strong, debt -free balance sheet give us the 
conﬁdence to accelerate our growth while remaining disciplined about returns. We remain focused on building a larger, sustainably proﬁtable and capital -eﬃcient hospitality enterprise while 
consistently enhancing guest experience and creating long-term stakeholder value.  
With the seasonally stronger second half ahead, we remain optimistic about sustaining our growth momentum, improving revenue productivity across our portfolio and delivering greater value from 
our expanding hospitality network.”  
 
About Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited
