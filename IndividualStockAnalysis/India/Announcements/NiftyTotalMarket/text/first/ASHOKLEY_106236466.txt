Ashok Leyland Declares Second Interim Dividend of ₹4.25 
per Share  
 
Chennai, May 16, 2025  – Ashok Leyland Limited, the Indian flagship of the Hinduja Group and a global 
manufacturer of commercial vehicles, today announced that its Board of Directors, at a meeting held 
earlier in the day, has declared a Second Interim Dividend of ₹4.25 per equi ty share  of face value ₹1/ -
, for the financial year ended March 31, 2025.  
 
In all, the Company has announced so far for FY25 a total interim dividend of 625% ie., Rs. 6.25 per 
share on a face value of Re. 1.  
 
This dividend decision reflects the company’s strong operational performance and robust financial 
position. This also reinforces Ashok Leyland’s ongoing commitment to enhancing shareholder value 
and returns . The second interim dividend will be paid to elig ible shareholders on or before June 14, 
2025.
