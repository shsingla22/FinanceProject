PRESS  RELEASE  
Ravi Dhariwal resigns from the Board of Raymond Lifestyle Limited  
Mumbai, April  11, 202 5, Raymond Lifestyle Limited, has announced the resignation of Ravi 
Dhariwal  as a Director on the Board of the company. The resignation is effective today.  
Commenting on his resignation, Gautam Hari Singhania, Chairman Raymond Lifestyle Limited 
said, “We are thankful for Ravi’s contribution to Raymond Lifestyle Limited during his  tenure. 
He brought in a lot of insights especially from the consumer side during his tenure . We wish 
him the very best for his future assignments.”  
Commenting on his resignation, Ravi Dhariwal , said , “I would like to thank the Executive 
Chairman, other fellow members of the Board and the Executives of the Company I have 
worked with for making my stint meaningful to me. I shall always cherish the time I spent  with 
you , and carry fond memories of Raymond Lifestyle. ”
