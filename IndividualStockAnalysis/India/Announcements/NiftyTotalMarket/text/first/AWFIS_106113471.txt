Awfis Brings Integrated Workspace Solutions to Lucknow’s  Vibrant  
Business Community  
 
Mumbai , 14th January 2025:  Awfis Space Solutions Limited, India’s first and only listed flexible 
workspace solutions  provider , proudly announces the launch of its first centre in Lucknow, spanning 
an impressive 47,694 sq. ft. Strategically located in Gomti Nagar, a vibrant business district and the 
city’s thriving IT corridor, the area has emerged as a hub for technology and com mercial activity. With 
rapid development in IT parks, business complexes, and residential projects, Gomti Nagar stands out 
as a prime destination for real estate investment. As a vital contributor to Uttar Pradesh's ambitious 
$1-trillion economy goal, Luck now accounts for 3.85% of the state's GDP.  
 
 Key Features of the centre: - 
• Design & Build - The centr e is thoughtfully designed to integrate collaborative and private 
spaces, providing  ready to move  environments that meet the modern  needs of clients  from 
diverse sectors  
• Strategic Location : The new centre is in the heart of Lucknow, o ffering excellent connectivity 
to Indira Nagar Metro Station and easy access to key social infrastructure hubs  
• Grade -A Infrastructure : The centre is in a Grade -A, fully compliant development to meet the 
highest standards of quality and functionality  
• State -of-the -Art Amenities : Includes lounge area, Awfis Café, ample parking facilities, and 
cutting -edge IT infrastructure to cater to modern business needs  
• Comprehensive Workspace Solutions : Offers versatile options such as meeting and 
conference rooms, co -working spaces, and tailored enterprise solutions  
 
Mr. Amit Ramani, Chairman & Managing Director of Awfis Space Solutions Limited, s aid : 
“We are thrilled to announce the launch of our first centr e in Lucknow, a city undergoing remarkable 
transformation fueled by robust infrastructure development, progressive  government policies, and 
the rapid growth of IT and ITES industries. With exceptional connectivity and dynamic investment 
potential, Lucknow has emerged as a vibrant hub for commerce and innovation, attracting 
homebuyers, businesses, and global brands.  Our new centr e is meticulously designed to provide a 
productive workspace, enabling startups, entrepreneurs, and businesses to flourish. With con tinuous 
infrastructure advancements, a skilled talent pool, and strong reforms, Lucknow is well -positioned to 
attract large enterprises, create jobs, and drive sustainable economic growth.  
 
With the launch of its new center in Lucknow, Awfis solidifies its position as India’s leading provider of 
flexible workspace solutions. As an industry pioneer, Awfis was the first to enter a Tier -II city in 2018. 
Since then, it has expanded to nine  cities, including Chandigarh, Bhubaneswar, Guwahati, Kochi, 
Jaipur, Nagpur, Pune, Indore,  and Lucknow establishing the most extensive presence in these regions. 
This milestone underscores Awfis’ commitment to addressing the growing demand for high -quality, 
flexible workspaces. By offering cutting -edge, customizable solutions tailored to the evolving needs 
of businesses and entrepreneurs, Awfis empowers organizations to thrive in today’s dynamic and 
competitive landscape.

About Awfis Space Solutions Limited  
Awfis Space Solutions Ltd. (‘Awfis’) is India’s leading and only listed flexible workspace solutions 
provider offering the largest network of agile workspaces. The company enables small and large 
corporates to seamlessly book and utilize workspaces as per their requirement and convenience. The 
comprehensive suite of solutions includes Flex Space Solutions (Coworking and Customized Managed 
Offices), Mobility Solutions, Design & Build, Awfis Café, and TechLabs -advanced tech infrastructure. 
With a strong prese nce in 18 cities and 200+ centres  (LOI & Operational) , Awfis serves ~3000  clients 
across diverse industries, ensuring scalable and adaptable workspace solutions for businesses of all 
sizes. For more information: Please log on to www.awfis.com . In addition, connect with us on Facebook , 
Instagram , and LinkedIn .
