This is in furtherance to our earlier disclosure dated July 16, 2026, submitted under Regulation 30 read with 
Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI Listing Regulations"), regarding the execution  of a Confidential Release and Settlement Agreement 
("Settlement Agreement") between Sansera Engineering Limited ("Company") and Metaldyne Powertrain Components Inc. ("Metaldyne") in relation to the civil suit titled Metaldyne Powertrain Components Inc. 
v. Sansera Engineering Limited, Case No. 2:21- cv-03588- BHH, pending before the United States District 
Court for the District of South Carolina, Charleston Division.  
 
Pursuant to the terms of the Settlement Agreement, the Company has today, i.e. July 28, 2026, paid an 
amount of USD 2,000,000 (Two Million United States Dollars) to Metaldyne towards full and final settlement for the aforesaid matter,  and thereby discharging its obligations under the Settlement Agreement.   
 Consequent to the payment of the settlement amount in accordance with the Settlement Agreement, there 
are no further material financial implications on the Company arising out of the said matter.  
 
The above information will also be made available on the website of the Company at www.sansera.in .
