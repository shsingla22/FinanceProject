similar line of business i.e. related to Automotive Sector, hence, 
after consolidation of both the companies in a single entity,

Company and the Transferee Company as a single entity, which

structure by elimination of multiple entities driven with single 
focused management team.

objectives and business strategies of both the companies thereby 
accelerating growth, e xpansion and development of the respective 
businesses through the Transferee Company. The amalgamation 
will thus enable further expansion of the Transferee Company and 
provide a strong and focused base to undertake the business more 
advantageously. Further , this arrangement would bring
