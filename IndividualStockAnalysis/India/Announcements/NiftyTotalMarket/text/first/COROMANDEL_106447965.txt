Demise of our Chairman Emeritus 
We write to inform that Coromandel family deeply mourns the demise of Shri A Vellayan, Chairman 
Emeritus of the Company and former Chairman of the Murugappa Group, today.  
 
He served the Murugappa group with distinction for several decades, providing strategic leadership 
across its diverse businesses. His vision, discipline and commitment played a key role in strengthening 
and expanding the Group into one of India’s most respected conglomerates.  
 
His vision, leadership and strategic direction will always be remembered. 
 
With profound sorrow
