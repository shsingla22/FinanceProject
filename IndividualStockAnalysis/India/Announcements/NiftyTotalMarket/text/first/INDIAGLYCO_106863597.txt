Press Release 
Post Scheme of Arrangement, India Glycols reports its first financial results for Q2 FY27 
Revenue of Rs. 358.18 Crore and EBITDA of Rs. 39.34 Crore 
Noida, November 17th, 2026: India Glycols Limited (NSE: INDIAGLYCO | BSE: 500201) has 
announced its unaudited standalone financial results for the second quarter (Q2 FY27) 
and half year (H1 FY27) ended September 30, 2026. These results cover only the 
Company’s continuing operations which retains Biobased Specialty Materials; 
Sustainable & Performance Chemicals, Gases. Its Spirits & Biofuel Undertaking and its 
Biopharma Undertaking have been demerged into IGL Spirits Limited and Ennature Bio 
Pharma Limited, and both are now separately listed. India Glycols’ Revenue and EBITDA 
for the period are as follows: 
Results are not comparable in the context of demerger into three separate entities  as 
detailed in the Note below *

IGL Spirits Limited and Ennature Bio Pharma Limited, respectively 
Key Highlights for Q2 FY27: 
On a standalone basis, revenue from operations stood at Rs. 358.18 crore in Q2 FY27, 
compared to Rs. 2,410.71 crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. 
EBITDA for Q2 FY27 stood at Rs. 39.34 crore, compared to Rs. 160.25 crore in Q2 FY26. 
Profit After Tax (PAT) stood at Rs. 11.24 crore, as against Rs. 53.94 crore in the 
corresponding quarter of the previous year. 
The Q1 FY27 figures have been included in the table for reference. The Q2 FY26 
comparative figures are also presented as reported in the previous year and should be 
read in the context of the demerger.

Key Highlights for H1 FY27: 
For H1 FY27, India Glycols Limited reported standalone revenue from operations of Rs. 
712.82 crore, compared to Rs. 4,913.74 crore in H1 FY26. 
EBITDA for H1 FY27 stood at Rs. 79.55 crore, compared to Rs. 309.51 crore in the 
corresponding period of the previous year. 
The Profit After Tax (PAT) for H1 FY27 stood at Rs. 23.59 crore, as against Rs. 106.79 crore in 
H1 FY26. 
Commenting on the results, Mr. Rupark Sarswat, Chief Executive Officer, India Glycols 
Limited, said, “The implementation of the demerger marks an important step in the 
evolution of India Glycols, enabling a sharper focus on our core business of bio-based 
specialities and performance chemicals. We remain focused on strengthening our 
capabilities, enhancing operational efficiencies and building on our expertise in the bio-
based chemicals space. Going forward, our efforts will be directed towards expanding 
our business opportunities and creating sustainable long-term value for our stakeholders.” 
Note*: The Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Allahabad Bench, Prayagraj, vide its order dated July 17, 
2026, approved the Scheme of Arrangement among India Glycols Limited, IGL Spirits Limited and Ennature Bio Pharma 
Limited for the demerger of the Spirits and Biofuel Undertaking into IGL Spirits Limited and the Biopharma Undertaking 
into Ennature Bio Pharma Limited. The Scheme became effective on September 1, 2026, with an appointed date of 
April 1, 2026. Accordingly, the Spirits and Biofuel Undertaking and the Biopharma Undertaking were demerged and 
vested in the respective resulting companies as going concerns with effect from the appointed date. The financial 
results of India Glycols Limited for Q2 FY27 and H1 FY27 do not include the financial performance of these two 
undertakings. The Q1 FY27 figures have been restated to give effect to the Scheme. The comparative figures for Q2 
FY26 and H1 FY26 include the Spirits and Biofuel Undertaking and the Biopharma Undertaking, which have been 
demerged from the Company with effect from April 1, 2026. Accordingly, these comparative figures are not 
comparable with the corresponding current-period figures.
