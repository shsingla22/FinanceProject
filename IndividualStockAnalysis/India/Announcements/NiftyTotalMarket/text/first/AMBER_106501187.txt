Sub:   Allotment of Land by Yamuna Expressway Industrial Development Authority (“YEIDA”) for 
establishment of Two Manufacturing Units  
 
Dear Sir/Ma’am,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30(2) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we hereby inform the Stock Exchanges that Amber Enterprises India Limited (“Company”) has been 
allotted 100 acres of land by the Yamuna Expressway Industrial Development Authority (“YEIDA”) on 18th 
January 2026 for the establishment of a new manufacturing facility at Sector 8, YEIDA, near Jewar Airport.  
 
Further, Ascent-K Circuit Private Limited, a subsidiary of IL JIN Electronics (India) Private Limited, which is a 
Material Subsidiary of the Company, has been allotted 16 acres of land by YEIDA for setting up a separate 
manufacturing unit at Sector 10, YEIDA, near Jewar Airport.  
 
The combined proposed investment for the above-mentioned facilities shall be INR 6,785 Crore, to be 
undertaken over a period of investment scheme, and is expected to generate direct employment for over 
3,000 people in a phased manner.  
 
These projects will signiﬁcantly enhance the Group’s manufacturing capabilities, promote localisation of 
critical components in line with “ Atmanirbhar Bharat” , and strengthen domestic production capacity.  
 
This information will also be made available on the Company’s website: https://www.ambergroupindia.com/  
 
You are requested to kindly take the above on record.
