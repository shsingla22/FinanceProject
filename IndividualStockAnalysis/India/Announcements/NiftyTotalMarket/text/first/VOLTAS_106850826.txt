Voltas Delivers Steady  Q2 FY27 Performance, on the back of market leadership and Execution Excellence  
 
 Mumbai, 6 November 2026:  The Board of Directors of Voltas Limited, India's No. 1 Air Conditioning brand from 
the Tata Group, announced the consolidated financial results (including the consolidated segment report) of the 
Company for the quarter ended 30 September 2026.  
 
Voltas continued to reinforce its leadership in the Indian Room Air Conditioner (RAC) market during Q2 FY27, with 
the RAC business recording  32% volume  growth during the quarter. The Company maintained its No. 1 position in 
the industry, achieving a market share of  17.4% for the year to date ended September 2026, with a  4.2% lead over 
its nearest competitor. The performance reflects the continued strength of the Voltas brand, its product portfolio, extensive distribution network and sustained focus on marke t execution.  
 The Unitary Cooling Products business recorded 29% revenue growth during the quarter, led by RAC, while 
improving profitability despite commodity and currency related cost pressures. Voltbek continued to expand its 
presence in the home appliances market, achieving record Q2 sales and further strengthening its market position 
in Washing Machines and Refrigerators.  
 
The Electro -Mechanical Projects and Services business maintained order momentum, supported by an order pad 
exceeding ₹6,800 crore, providing revenue visibility. The Engineering Products and Services segment registered 
30+% growth , supported by Mining and Construction Equipment and Textile Machinery.  
 Across its businesses, Voltas continued to focus on market competitiveness, product innovation, distribution expansion, cost optimisation and disciplined execution, while strengthening the foundations for sustainable and profitable growth.

Consolidated Segment  Results for the quarter and year  ended 30th September  2026 :  
A. Unitary Cooling Products (UCP)  
The Unitary Cooling Products business recorded 29 % revenue growth in Q2 FY27, driven primarily by the Room 
Air Conditioner business  which  materially outperform ed the broader industry.  
Voltas continued to consolidate its position as India's No. 1 Room Air Conditioning brand, maintaining a market 
share of 17.4% for the year to date ended September 2026. The Company sustained a 4.2 % lead over its 
nearest competitor, reflecting its established market presence, consumer preference and distribution 
strength.  
The performance was supported by the Company's comprehensive product portfolio, continued product 
innovation, effective channel engagement and an expanding distribution footprint. Voltas maintained its focus 
on addressing evolving consumer preferences through relevant product offerings, technology enhancements  
through its new AI range of split ACs  and greater accessibility across markets. 
The Company's leadership in RAC continues to be supported by its extensive distribution and service network, brand recall and sustained investment in product development and consumer engagement. These capabilities 
remain central to its strategy of consolid ating market leadership while expanding its presence across 
geographies and consumer segments.  
Commercial Air Conditioning continued to deliver stable growth, while Home Comfort products registered 
healthy growth during the quarter. Commercial Refrigeration recorded a flat quarter.  
Despite industry wide pressures arising from commodity inflation and adverse currency movements, the 
Unitary Cooling Products business improved profitability through calibrated pricing actions and ongoing cost 
optimisation initiatives. These included localisation, sourcing efficiencies, product redesign and improvements in manufacturing productivity.  
The business remains focused on sustaining its competitive position, strengthening product differentiation, expanding distribution reach and improving operating efficiencies. With a comprehensive product portfolio, 
healthy inventory position and expanding market presence, Voltas continues to pursue opportunities for 
market expansion and profitable growth.  
 
  
 
 
 
Voltas Classification | Public  Voltbek: Continued Expansion in Home Appliances  
 
Voltbek delivered another quarter of growth, achieving  record Q2 sales, supported by an improving product 
mix, market share gains and continued expansion of its distribution network.  
 
The business continued to strengthen its presence in the Indian home appliances market, achieving year to date 
market shares of  8.8% in Washing Machines and 7. 0% in Refrigerators.  
 
Voltbek has continued to expand its scale through a wider product portfolio  including a new line up of Frost 
Free Refrigerators and Fully Automatic Washing Machines , sharper product segmentation, enhanced consumer 
engagement and greater distribution penetration. The business remains focused on building relevance across consumer segments through innovation, competitive product offerings and improved market accessibili ty. 
 
Product development remain s an important priority , supported by sourcing efficiencies and portfolio 
optimisation initiatives aimed at strengthening competitiveness and improving profitability over time.  
With its expanding presence in Washing Machines and Refrigerators, Voltbek continues to contribute to the 
diversification of the Voltas consumer durables portfolio, enhance Voltas’ channel relevance complementing 
the Company's established leadership in cooling products  and making Voltas an all season consumer durable 
brand.
  
 
B. Electro -Mechanical Projects and Services  
 
The Electro -Mechanical Projects and Services business continued to maintain order momentum, supported by 
opportunities across infrastructure, industrial and institutional projects.  
 Domestic Projects recorded order wins across industrial infrastructure, electronics manufacturing, metro and 
tunnel projects, and data centres. The business maintained its focus on selective order booking, execution 
discipline, working capital management and project profitability.  The Company's approach remains centred on 
pursuing opportunities that align with its technical capabilities, execution strengths and risk management 
framework, while maintaining appropriate commercial discipline.  
 
In the International Projects business, the favourable resolution of the Sidra Bank Guarantee matter eliminated 
the related contingent liabilities, strengthening the risk profile of the business. The focus continued to remain 
on disciplined project executi on, prudent risk management and selective bidding.  
 
As of 30 September 2026, the carry forward order book for the segment stood at  
over ₹6,800 crore , providing 
revenue visibility across Domestic and International Projects. The business remains focused on converting its order book through disciplined execution, maintaining working capital efficiency and pursuing opportunities 
that support sustainable p rofitability.  
 
C. Engineering Products and Services  
 
The Engineering Products and Services segment recorded  robust  revenue growth during Q2 FY27, supported by 
performance across Mining and Construction Equipment and Textile Machinery.  
 
The Mining and Construction Equipment business benefited from growth in crushing and screening equipment, 
continued execution of operations and maintenance contracts, stable performance in African operations and contributions from aftermarket and service businesses. The business continued to focus on expanding customer 
relationships, improving service capabilities and strengthening its presence across equipment and aftermarket 
opportunities.
