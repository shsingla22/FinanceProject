Thomas Cook India strengthens Maharashtra footprint with Kolhapur outlet  
Expands consumer access to 29 locations across the State  
 
Mumbai,  March  11, 202 6: With a strategic focus on capitalising on the strong and growing travel demand from 
Maharashtra— one of its key source markets, Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel travel 
services company, has inaugurated its new outlet in Kolhapur. The new outlet expands Thomas Cook India’s 
network to 2 locations in the city and 29 across the State, enhancing access to its comprehensive range of travel 
services.  
 Kolhapur, a key commercial hub in southern Maharashtra, is witnessing rising affluence and a growing appetite for premium travel experiences. The new outlet will cater to travellers from Kolhapur and its neighbouring 
catchments across diverse customer segments —families, couples, Young India’s Millennials and GenZ, 
business travellers and GenS (seniors) . Thomas Cook’s Kolhapur  outlet offers end- to-end travel services: 
International & Domestic Holidays (Group Tours, Personalised H olidays, Cruises, etc.), Value Added Services 
like Travel Insurance, etc . Additionally, Thomas Cook’s AI booking platform assist s customers in planning their 
personalized vacations in real time by checking availability  and making reservations in a matter of minutes .

customers from Kolhapur and its surrounding catchments. Strategically located, the outlet offers customers
