Mastek and Innovaccer Partner to Accelerate AI -Powered Healthcare Transformation  
Mumbai, India, August 3, 2026 : Mastek Inc., a wholly owned step -down subsidiary of Mastek Limited  (NSE: MASTEK; 
BSE: 523704), a trusted AI -first, digital engineering, and cloud transformation partner, has entered into a strategic 
Platinum Partnership with Innovaccer, the autonomous operations platform for healthcare.  
Innovaccer’s Healthcare Intelligence Platform, Gravity, which unifies fragmented clinical, claims and operational data 
into a single autonomous operations platform for healthcare. Mastek brings the engineering, data, and AI expertise with 
healthcare delive ry experience needed to integrate, implement and scale the platform across complex enterprise 
environments. Together, the companies will help healthcare organizations transform siloed data into actionable 
intelligence, improving care outcomes, lowering the  cost of care and unlocking new AI -driven use cases.  
This partnership makes Mastek Innovaccer’s platinum global partner  with the expertise to implement and scale the 
Gravity Platform for healthcare organizations across North America, UK, Europe and Middle East.  
The partnership marks a significant milestone in Mastek's vision of becoming a leading AI transformation partner for 
the healthcare industry . By combining Mastek's expertise in Data, AI, and healthcare transformation with Innovaccer's 
market -leading platform, the two companies will help healthcare organizations build intelligent, connected, and data-
driven healthcare ecosystems.  
For Innovaccer, the partnership expands its global transformation capabilities by leveraging Mastek's extensive 
healthcare delivery expertise and global presence to accelerate adoption of the Gravity Platform across North America 
and other strategic market s. 
Umang Nahata, CEO, Mastek,  said, “Healthcare  organizations need AI that is simple, trusted and built on connected 
data. Our Platinum partnership with Innovaccer strengthens Mastek’s ability to help customers turn healthcare data into 
actionable intelligence and accelerates our journey to become a le ading AI transformation partner for the healthcare 
industry."  
"When leading healthcare -focused global systems integrators independently choose to build their delivery practices 
around Gravity, it tells the market that this platform is trusted to carry the most complex, high- stakes healthcare 
transformation programs i n the world. Mastek joining our Platinum GSI program is a validation of that, and it gives us 
access to four decades of institutional relationships with health systems and governments across three continents. 
Together, we are bringing autonomous healthcare  operations to markets where the ambition has always been there and 
the right platform and delivery partner have not been until now."  said Abhinav Shashank, Co -Founder and CEO of 
Innovaccer.  
The partnership strengthens Mastek's healthcare portfolio while expanding Innovaccer's ecosystem of strategic 
partners, enabling both organizations to deliver faster, scalable, and outcome -driven digital transformation for the 
global healthcare industry.
