Proceedings of the Extraord inary General Meeting (EGM)  of the Bank held on                 
Monday , 16th March, 2026  through Vide o Conference/ Other Audio-Visual Means 
(VC/OAV M) 
 
1. As per the Notice dated 19th February, 2026 , the Extraordinary General Meeting 
(EGM) of the shareholders of the Bank commenced  at 11.00 am on Monday                             
16th March, 2026  through Video Conference/Other Audio -Visual Means (VC/OAVM).  
 
2. The EGM was attended by 23 members including 1 Authorized  representative of 
Government of India (GOI).  
 
3. Shri Vikash Gupta, Company Secretary of the Bank , welcomed the shareholders to 
the EGM  and provided a brief overview of the agenda items.  
 
4. In terms of Regulation 59 (i) of the U CO Bank (Shares and Meetings) Regulations 2003, 
Shri Aravamudan Krishna Kumar , Non-Executive Chairman  of the Bank , took the chair 
of the EGM and welcomed the shareholders participating  in the EGM  through 
VC/OAVM.  
 
5. Upon  confirmation of the requisite quorum by the Company Secretary, the Chairman 
called the meeting to order . 
 
6. The Chairman introduced the Directors  and Authorized  Representative of GOI 
present at the meeting.  
 
7. As the Notice convening the EGM had already been circulated  to the shareholders 
in the prescribed manner , published in newspapers and hosted on the website of the 
Bank, the Chairman , with the consent of the shareholders present, took the Notice as 
read.  
 
8. The Chairman informed the shareholders that agenda relating to the election of a 
Shareholder Director was not required to be  transacted at this EGM as Bank had 
received only one valid nomination i.e. from Mr.  Rajesh Kumar Ailawadi, before the 
last date pre scribed  for submission of nomination s. After  due scrutiny, the nomination 
of Mr. Rajesh Kumar Ailawadi was found  to be  fit and proper by the Nomination and 
Remuneration Committee of the Board at its meeting held on 07th March, 2026 in 
terms of Reserve Bank  of India (Commercial Banks - Governance) Directions, 2025 
dated 28th November,  2025.  Accordingly,  Mr. Rajesh Kumar Ailawadi was deemed to 
have been elected as a shareholder Director on 07th March, 2026 and assumed office 
on 08th March, 2026. His profile  has also been submitted to Stock Exchange s and is 
available on the website of the Bank.

Agenda - Approval for Extension of Tenure of Shri Rajendra Kumar Saboo, Executive

The Chairman informed that Mr. Rajendra Kumar Saboo was initially appointed as

The s hareholders were further informed that as per proviso to Clause 17(1C) of the 
SEBI (LODR)  Regulations, 2015, the appointment /reappointment  of Director has to be 
approved at the meeting of the Shareholders. Accordingly, the a genda for approval 
of the extens ion of tenure of Shri Rajendra Kumar Saboo  as Executive  Director was 
placed before the shareholders for their approval.  
 
10. The Chairman then requested those  shareholders who had not casted their vote s 
through remote e -voting , to cast their vote through e -voting facility available  during 
the meeting .  
 
11. The s hareholders were informed that , based on the scrutiny of the remote                       
e-voting and e -voting at the meeting, the Scrutinizer  appointed  for the EGM would 
submit their report, upon which the voting r esults would be intimated to the stock 
exchanges and hosted on Bank’s Website and e -voting Portal within 48 hours of the 
conclusion of the meeting .   
 
12. The Chairman expressed gratitude to the shareholders for their participation and 
informed that the e -voting facility would remain open for the next 15 minutes. There 
being no other business to transact, the meeting concluded thereafter.
