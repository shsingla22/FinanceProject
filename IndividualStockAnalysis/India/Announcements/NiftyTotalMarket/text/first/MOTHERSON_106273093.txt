The Board of Directors of Samvardhana Motherson International Limited (“ SAMIL”/ “the 
Company”) have approved to execute a Joint Venture Agreement with Egtronics Co. 
Ltd.(“Egtronics”) , a company listed on KOSDAQ South Korea .  
 
The Strategic partnership  will enable SAMIL via its Wiring Harness Division to provide  innovative  
solutions of vehicle power electronics  for clean mobility (i.e EV and Hydrogen)  to customers  in 
the commercial vehicle segment  thereby enhancing the technological capabilities of the division 
with the addition of patented product portfolio . The strategic relationship will have its 
manufacturing operations  established in India with global sales  (excluding South Korea)
