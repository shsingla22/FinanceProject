Biocon Malaysia Receives EMA Approval for New Drug Product 
Fill Finish Unit for Insulins  
 
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, July 1 6, 2026  
 
Biocon Ltd. (BSE code: 532523, NSE: BIOCON) has received approval from the 
European Medicines Agency (EMA) for a new drug product fil l finish line for Semglee® 
(insulin glargine) at its insulin manufacturing facility in Malaysia.  
 
Supplies to Europe from this line are expected to begin from Q2FY27.
