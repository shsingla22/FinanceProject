Re.: Inspection held at the Injectable Manufacturing site situated at Jarod, near Vadodara  
 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
We wish to inform that the USFDA conducted an inspection at the Injectable Manufacturing site 
situated at Jarod, near Vadodara. The inspection was conducted from April 15 to April 23, 2024.

address and respond to the observations in an expeditious manner.

Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
