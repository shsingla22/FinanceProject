PRESS RELEASE  
 
G E Shipping contracts to buy a secondhand Kamsarmax Dry Bulk Carrier  
  
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (GE Shipping) has contracted to buy a secondhand 
Kamsarmax Dry Bulk Carrier of about 81,094 dwt on March 4, 2026. The 2014 Japanese built 
vessel is expected to join the Company’s fleet by Q1 FY27.  
 The proposed vessel will be financed entirely from internal accruals. The purpose of the acquisition 
is expansion of the fleet. 
   
The company’s current owned fleet stands at 41 vessels, comprising 27 Tankers (5 Crude Tankers, 
17 Product Tankers, 5 LPG Carriers) and 14 Dry Bulk Carriers (2 Capesize, 9 Kamsarmax, 1 Ultramax, 2 Supramax) aggregating 3.25 mn dwt. The Company’s current ca pacity utilization is 
close to 100%.  
 
Additionally, the company has contracted to sell one Very Large Gas Carrier “Jag Vishnu”, and 
this sale transaction is expected to be completed in Q4 FY26.
