Waterways (MoPSW) and the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas (MoPNG), the Shipping

Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd. (BPCL), Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Ltd. (HPCL),

The purpose of this collaboration is to build and operate fleet togethe r that will support the

manage vessels. These vessels shall be used for interna tional trade as well as coastal 
transport of petroleum, petroleum products, petrochemicals , and other hydrocarbon cargoes .
