Press Release  
Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd Secures EcoVadis 
Platinum for Second Consecutive Year  
 
Kolkata, India | Jan 15, 2026:  Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd , has once again 
been awarded the EcoVadis  Platinum Medal, marking its second consecutive year 
at the highest level of recognition in the globally respected sustainability 
assessment.  
The Platinum distinction places Himadri among 
the top 1% of more than 15 0,000 + companies 
assessed and rated by Ec oVadis worldwide, 
reaffirming Himadri’s consistent leadership in 
responsible business practices and sustainable 
value creation. The EcoVadis evaluation assesses 
performance across 21 criteria under four core 
pillars —Environment, Labour & Human Rights, 
Ethics, and Sustainable Procurement —and is 
benchmarked against internationally recognised 
frameworks such as the UN Global Compact, ILO 
conventions, GRI and TCFD framework.  
This back -to-back Platinum achievement reflects Himadri’s deeply embedded 
sustainabilit y approach, underpinned by strong governance, climate -conscious 
operations, ethical business conduct and responsible supply chain management.  
Sustainability has been a core pillar of Himadri’s 
growth strategy, reflected in focused investments 
across ener gy efficiency, emission reduction, 
circularity, employee well -being, and transparent 
disclosures aligned with global ESG standards.  
Commenting on the achievement, Mr Anurag 
Choudhary, CMD & CEO, Himadri  Speciality 
Chemical Ltd  said:  
“Earning the EcoVadis P latinum rating for the second 
consecutive year is a significant milestone for Himadri and a strong validation of 
the way sustainability is integrated into our business strategy and decision -
making. This recognition reflects our teams’ collective commitment  to responsible 
manufacturing, ethical practices and continuous improvement. As we continue to 
scale our global presence which currently connects 56 nations across hemispheres, 
sustainability will remain central to how we create long -term value for our global 
stakeholders and contribute meaningfully to a low -carbon, resilient future.”  

EcoVadis ratings are widely relied upon by global customers, investors and 
partners as a credible, evidence -based assessment of ESG performance. For 
Himadri, reaffirming of  this recognition further strengthens its position as a 
trusted partner in global value chains, aligned with evolving regulatory, customer 
and societal expectations.  
Himadri remains committed to advancing its sustainability agenda through 
measurable action , transparency and innovation, as outlined in its publicly 
disclosed Sustainability report  and ESG frameworks.  
 
About Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd  
Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd is a gl obal speciality chemical conglomerate with 
a strong focus on research and development (R&D), innovation and sustainability.  
As pioneer in the production of lithium -ion battery materials in India, it 
continuously develops and innovates raw materials of lith ium-ion battery value 
chain. It’s diverse product portfolio includes speciality carbon black, coal tar pitch, 
refined naphthalene, advance materials, SNF, speciality oils, clean power, etc. 
catering to various industries such as lithium -ion batteries, pain ts, plastics, tyres, 
technical rubber goods, aluminium, graphite electrodes, agrochemicals, defence 
and construction chemicals. Himadri operates in both domestic and international 
markets, exporting to 56 countries across the globe.  
With a strong commitmen t to corporate governance, safety and sustainability, it 
has 8 zero -liquid discharge manufacturing facilities and utilizes in -house clean 
power for 100% of its electrical energy needs. Himadri is a people first organization 
and is making a positive impact on the industry, both in India and globally through 
its focus on innovation fueled by extensive R&D, new business opportunities and 
sustainability.
