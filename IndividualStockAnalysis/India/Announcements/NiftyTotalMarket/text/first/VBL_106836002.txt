Varun Beverages ’ Q3 & 9M CY202 6 Financial Results  
 
   
 
 
 
 
Gurgaon , October  30, 2026: Varun Beverages Limited (BSE: 540180, NSE:  VBL), a key player in the beverage 
industry, announced its financial results for the  third  quarter and nine months  ended September  30, 202 6.  
  
Financial Performance Highlights * 
Performance Review for Q3 2026 vs. Q3 2025 
• Revenue from operations  (net of excise / GST) grew by  33.2% YoY to Rs. 66,075.6  million in Q3 2026 
as compared to Rs. 49,606.9  million in Q3 CY202 5 
o Consolidated sales volume grew by 28.7% to 354.4 million cases in Q3 CY2026 from 275.3 
million cases in Q3 CY2025, driven by robust volume growth of 22.5% in India and 39.9% in 
international territories  
o Consolidated beverage realization per case improved by 2.2% at the consolidated level 
supported by a 0.5% increase in India on account of growth in high value categories and 
further launch of premium categories (Ad Rush, Evervess mixers range), calibrated 
discounting and an efficient price pack arc hitecture.  
o In International markets, realization per case improved on account of improved realization 
in South Africa and foreign exchange impact.  
o International volumes includes 15.3 million cases during the quarter from the acquisition of 
Twizza in South Africa.  
o CSD constituted 73.3%, JBD 4.8% and Packaged Drinking Water 21.9% in Q3 CY2026.  
• Gross margin declined by 334 bps to 53.1% in Q3 CY2026, primarily due to higher packaging 
material costs, including preforms , shrink films, closures, and cans, driven by geopolitical disruptions

etc. in India, which increased by 10 4% during the quarter and has a higher COGS, impacting

Commenting on the performance for Q 3 CY2026, Mr. Varun Jaipuria, Executive Vice Chairman , 
Varun Beverages Limited , said,  
 
“We are pleased to report a strong performance in the third quarter of CY2026. Consolidated sales volumes 
grew by 28.7%, with volume growth of 22.5% in India and 39.9% in international territories. Revenue from 
operations increased by 33.2% YoY to Rs. 66,07 5.6 million, while EBITDA grew by 25.1% to Rs. 14,267.1 million. 
Higher packaging costs weighed on margins during the quarter, although improved realizations and operating 
leverage from strong volume growth helped partially offset these pressures.  
In India, we continued to witness healthy volume growth during the quarter, extending the positive trend seen 
since May. This performance was supported by our extensive distribution network, expanded manufacturing 
capacities, and ongoing investments in chi lling infrastructure. We also saw improved realizations, supported by 
growth in higher -value categories such as dairy beverages and the introduction of premium offerings, including 
‘Ad Rush’ and the ‘Evervess’ mixers range.  
Our international business continued to make strong progress during the quarter, with Africa remaining a key 
continent for expansion. With effect from 01 August 2026, the merger of Twizza with BevCo  has been 
completed, and we expect to unlock significant operational synergies in the coming quarters. During the 
quarter, we have also started beverage operations in Kenya and introduced two new beverage brands ‘BOLT 
UP’ and ‘XTREME FIZZ’, marking another  step towards strengthening our footprint in the fast -growing Africa 
region. We also entered into an exclusive distribution agreement with Mondelez in Zimbabwe, further 
broadening our product offerings in the market.  
We are also taking measured steps towards diversifying our business into adjacent categories. The 
incorporation of KIVA Spirits and Company Limited marks an important step in exploring opportunities in ready -
to-drink beverages, alcoholic beverages, and all ied products. Our approach will remain focused on pursuing 
opportunities that leverage our execution capabilities and offer attractive long -term growth potential.  
In line with our dividend policy, the Board of Directors has approved an interim dividend of 25% of the face 
value, i.e., Rs. 0.50 per share, resulting in a total cash outflow of ~Rs. 1,691 million.  
Looking ahead, our focus will remain on driving growth across our existing markets, supported by the 
investments we have made over recent years. Alongside this, we will selectively pursue opportunities to 
broaden our business, while maintaining a clear emp hasis on profitability and long -term value creation. ”

About Varun Beverages Limited:  
 
Varun Beverages Limited (“VBL” or the “Company”) is a key player in beverage industry and one of the largest 
franchisee  of PepsiCo in the world (outside USA). The Company produces and distributes a wide range of 
carbonated soft drinks (CSDs), as well as a large selection of non -carbonated beverages (NCBs), including 
packaged drinking water sold under trademarks owned by Pe psiCo. PepsiCo CSD brands produced and sold 
by VBL include Pepsi, Pepsi Zero, Mountain Dew, Sting, Adrenaline Rush, Seven -Up, Mirinda, Nimbooz Jeera 
Soda and Evervess . PepsiCo NCB brands produced and sold by the Company include Slice, Tropicana Juices 
(100% and Delight), Seven -Up Nimbooz, Gatorade as well as packaged drinking water under the brand 
Aquafina.  
 
VBL has been associated with PepsiCo since the 1990s and have over three decades consolidated its business 
association with PepsiCo, increasing the number of licensed territories and sub -territories covered by the 
Company, producing and distributing a wide r range of PepsiCo beverages, introducing various SKUs in the 
portfolio, and expanding the distribution network. As on date, VBL has been granted franchises for various 
PepsiCo products across 26 States and 6 Union Territories in India. India is the larges t market and contributed 
~67% of revenues from operations (net) in Fiscal 2025. VBL has also been granted the franchise for various 
PepsiCo products for the territories of Nepal, Sri Lanka, Morocco, Zambia, Zimbabwe, South Africa, Lesotho, 
Eswatini & DRC a nd distribution rights for Namibia, Botswana, Mozambique and Madagascar.
