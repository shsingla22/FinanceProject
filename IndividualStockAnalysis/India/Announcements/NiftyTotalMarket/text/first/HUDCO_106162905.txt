Sub.: Corporate Disclosure under Regulation 30 –  Change in Registrar and Share Transfer

 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ 
 Sir/Madam, 
 In continuation to our earlier intimation dated 30.12.2024 and 07.02.2025 (copy enclosed), 
we wish to inform that Alankit Assignments Limited (Alankit) has provided No Objection 
Certificate (NOC) to shift the database and electronic connectivity to Beetal Financial & 
Computer Services Private Limited (Beetal). However, till such time the database and 
electronic connectivity is shifted to the Beetal, Alankit shall continue to provide its share 
registry services to the Company. 
 Further, upon completion of necessary formalities, effective date for change in RTA shall be 
intimated in due course.

Sub.: Corporate Disclosure under Regulation 30 -Appointment of Registrar and Share 
Transfer Agent (RTA) of the Company 
Sir/Madam,

appointment of Beetal Financial & Computer Services Private Limited as Registrar and

effective date of change of RTA will be intimated in due course.
