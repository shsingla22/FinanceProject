Ampere Accelerates Growth Journey with 50+ New Experience Centers Across 
India  
Doubles Its Sales and Service Footprint in Just Over a Year  
• Pan-India expansion is a critical pillar of Ampere’s aggressive growth strategy, bringing integrated 
product experience, sales and dependable service closer to customers across priority markets.  
 
New Delhi, 0 9 October 2026:  Ampere today announced  the addition of  more than 50 new Ampere 
Experience Centers across 15 states during the Navratri festive period , marking a decisive step in its 
aggressive growth journey. The expansion strengthens  Ampere’s ability to deliver an integrated 
product experience, sales  and service proposition closer to customers. From around 300 Experience 
Centers  over a year  ago to 600+ today, Ampere has doubled its network footprint in just over a year, 
creating a strong retail and service platform for its next phase of growth.  
 
India’s electric two -wheeler market is entering a new phase, with penetration approaching 11% * and 
consumer acceptance continuing to rise. Broader product choice, improving technology maturity and 
stronger value propositions are making electric scooters both aspirational and practical for everyday 
mobility. As adoption accelerates, physical access t o products, high -quality customer experience and 
dependable after -sales support will be decisive. Ampere’s rapid network build -out is designed to 
address this need at scale.  
 
Commenting on the expansion, Mr. Vikas Singh, Managing Director, Greaves Electric Mobility , said, 
“Building a nationwide network of Experience Centers  is a critical  pillar of Ampere’s aggressive growth 
journey. We are moving with speed,  and we have doubled our footprint in just over a year . This reflects 
the scale of our ambition and our commitment to bring product experience, sales and dependable 
service closer to customers in every priority market. Under our ‘Built for Bharat’ approach, we are 
creating not merely a larger distribution network, but a stro ng and trusted ownership ecosystem for 
India’s rapidly expanding electric mobility market.”  
 
The larger network will give more customers direct access to Ampere’s portfolio of award -winning 
electric scooters  across varied  needs and use cases. The portfolio includes the Ampere Nexus EX+, 
powered by a new 5 kW motor; Magnus GMax, combining  everyday practicality with connected 
technology; Magnus Neo, engineered  for comfortable city riding; and the recently launched Ampere 
Reo Vyb  for younger riders. Nexus EX+ and Magnus GMax are also available with “Intellipack ”, offering  
30+ intelligent convenience features. The Nexus platform has demonstrated its real -world capability 
by conquering the 70 hairpin bends of Kolli Hills, reaching Shipki La Pass at 13,200 ft , while the Magnus 
GMax IoT recently completed a nearly 4,000 km east -to-west endurance ride across India.  The portfolio 
is underpinned by next -generation  LFP battery technology , with  up to a 5 -year/75,000 km standard 
battery warranty and battery life of up to 2,00,000 km.  
 
 
 
The 50+ new Experience Centers span key markets across four regions: North  - Delhi, Haryana, Uttar 
Pradesh and Rajasthan; East  - West Bengal, Odisha and Jharkhand; West  - Maharashtra, Madhya 
Pradesh and Gujarat; and South  - Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Kerala.  
 
The expansion brings Ampere’s ‘Built for Bharat’ proposition to life at scale  - combining products 
engineered for India’s diverse roads, terrains and everyday mobility needs with a rapidly growing sales 
and service ecosystem. Through this network, Ampere is laying the retail and service foundation to 
deepen customer trust and accelerate adoption across India.

About Greaves Electric Mobility Limited:  
Greaves Electric Mobility Limited (GEML), the electric mobility business of Greaves Cotton Limited 
(GCL), is one of the first companies in India to focus on the evolving market of electric vehicles (“ EV”) 
and has been among the frontrunners at driving EV adoption in the country. With over 17 years  of 
experience designing and manufacturing electric vehicles, GEML serves both the electric 2 -wheeler 
and 3 -wheeler segments. GEML along with its subsidiaries, operates three manufacturing plants 
located in Hyderabad, Noida, and Ranipet. The company supports customers through 600+ Ampere 
 
 
touchpoints across India, enabling wider access, dependable service and an enhanced ownership 
experience.  GEML holds 8 India Book of Records* and 1 Asia Book of Records* titles, reflecting its 
focus on technology and design led innovative & durable mobility solutions . Subsidiaries MLR Auto 
Limited and Bestway Agencies Private Limited help extend GEML’s market presence. Committed to 
affordability and sustainability, GEML provides clean mobility solutions contributing to India’s shift 
toward electric transportation.
