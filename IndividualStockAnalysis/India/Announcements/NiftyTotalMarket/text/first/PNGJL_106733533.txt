PNG launches YOOU, a lightweight diamond jewellery brand 
reflecting the evolving identities of modern women 
Built on PNG Jewellers' legacy of trust, YOOU redefines lightweight diamond jewellery with a 
renewed focus on individuality, self-expression and everyday luxury.  
Pune, August 8, 2026:  PNG Jewellers has announced the rebranding of its lightweight diamond 
jewellery brand, Litestyle, as YOOU, marking a significant step in the brand's evolution. The 
transformation reflects a renewed vision to create jewellery that celebrates individuality, confidence 
and self-expression. Rooted in PNG Jewellers' legacy of trust and craftsmanship since 1832, YOOU 
personifies women who view jewellery not just as an accessory for occasions, but as an extension of 
their personality and everyday life.  
Launched to bridge the gap between traditional jewellery and contemporary everyday wear, YOOU  
specialises in lightweight diamond jewellery for consumer preferences evolved towards more 
expressive, design-led pieces, thus creating a brand that resonates more deeply with today's women. 
The transition to YOOU reflects this evolution, placing the wearer at the centre of every design 
philosophy while retaining the quality, purity and craftsmanship that customers have come to 
associate with PNG Jewellers. 
As a contemporary diamond jewellery brand, YOOU offers lightweight designs across 14, 18, 22, and 
9 karat gold. Every collection is designed to seamlessly fit into modern lifestyles, offering versatile 
rings, earrings, chains, pendants, bracelets, bangles, mangalsutras and men's jewellery that can be 
worn from the workplace to social occasions. The brand has 13 exclusive brand outlets across 
Maharashtra and Goa, along with a shop-in-shop presence across five states, including Maharashtra 
and Goa. , YOOU aims to become India's preferred destination for lightweight diamond jewellery and 
targets to open 100 new stores (in SIS and EBO format) by 2030. 
Sara Tendulkar, Brand Ambassador, YOOU by PNG Jewellers , said, "Jewellery today is about 
expressing who you are rather than waiting for a special occasion. What I love about YOOU is that it 
encourages women to celebrate themselves every single day through designs that are contemporary, 
effortless and deeply personal. It is a brand that truly reflects the confidence and individuality of 
today's generation. You don't need an occasion to shine. You are the occasion! Welcome to YOOU!" 
Dr. Saurabh Gadgil, Chairman and Managing Director, PNG Jewellers , said, "With YOOU, we are 
taking that journey forward by creating a brand that speaks directly to today's woman and her 
aspirations. Every design has been created to complement her everyday life while carrying forward 
PNG Jewellers' enduring promise of trust, purity and craftsmanship. Customers can expect more 
innovation, more contemporary collections and a jewellery experience that is deeply personal."  
 
 
About YOOU 
YOOU by PNG Jewellers is a lightweight diamond jewellery brand, created for young jewellery lovers 
who celebrate themselves every day. Offering thoughtfully crafted diamond jewellery in 9K, 14K, 18K 
and 22K gold. YOOU blends effortless design, everyday wearability and timeless craftsmanship. 
Designed for modern lifestyles, the brand champions individuality, self-expression and confidence, 
believing that jewellery should not wait for an occasion, because the wearer is the occasion. 
 
About PNG Jewellers  
PNG Jewellers is one of the most reputed jewellers in the country, with over 194 years of excellence 
in creativity and trusted service for customers all over the world. Under the leadership of Dr. Saurabh 
Gadgil, a visionary entrepreneur, PNG Jewellers continues to evolve as a globally relevant brand while 
staying rooted in its heritage of purity, trust, and craftsmanship.
