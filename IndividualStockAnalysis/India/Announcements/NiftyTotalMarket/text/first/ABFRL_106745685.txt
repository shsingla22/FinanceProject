With reference to the captioned subject, we wish to inform that Aditya Birla Digital Fashion Ventures 
Limited (“ ABDFVL”), a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company has today i.e., August 14, 2026, 
purchased an additional 6.21% equity shares of its subsidiary Pratyaya E-Commerce Private Limited  
(“Pratyaya”) .  
 
The transaction is in accordance with the terms of the Share Subscription and Shareholders’ 
Agreement dated June 23, 2022 with pre-defined milestones for increase in stake of ABDFVL in 
Pratyaya through stake purchase/secondary transaction. Consequent to the above, holding of ABDFVL 
in Pratyaya has increased from 87.57% to 93.79%. 
 
Brief details in accordance with the SEBI Listing Regulations read with aforesaid SEBI Master Circular 
are annexed herewith as Annexure A . 
 
The above is being made available on the website of the Company i.e., www.abfrl.com .
