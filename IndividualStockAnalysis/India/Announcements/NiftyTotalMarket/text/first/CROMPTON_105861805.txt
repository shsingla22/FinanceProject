of such unpaid/  unclaimed dividends  are regularly updated on the website of IEPF Authority  at 
www.iepf.gov.in  as well as on the Company's website  at https://www.crompton.co.in/pages/investors -
rela tions#UnclaimedDividend .

respect of which dividend has  remained unpaid or unclaimed for seven consecutive years or more shall be

of Court or Tribunal or Statutory Authority restraining any transfer of such shares  and payment of dividend 
or where such shares are pledged , the company shall not transfer such shares to  IEPF.
