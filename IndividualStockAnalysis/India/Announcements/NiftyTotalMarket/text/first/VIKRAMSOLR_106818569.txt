Vikram Solar Secures  a 187 MW Module Supply Order for Solar Projects in 
Karnataka  and Tamil Nadu  
Kolkata, India, 15 October 2026:  Vikram Solar, one of India’s leading solar PV module manufacturers, 
has secured a 187 MW module supply order from a leading renewable energy developer for solar 
projects at three  locations across  Karnataka  and Tamil Nadu . 
Under the agreement, Vikram Solar  will supply its high -efficiency N -Type TOPCon G12R modules, 
featuring glass -to-glass construction and bifacial technology, with power ratings ranging from 615 –
625 Wp.  
The engagement builds on Vikram Solar’s existing association with the developer, reflec ting continued 
confidence in the company’s technology portfolio and execution capabilities. The order adds to Vikram 
Solar’s growing pipeline as an integrated domestic manufacturer at a time when India is scaling up 
toward its target of 500 GW of non -fossi l capacity. With ongoing expansion in high -efficiency cell 
manufacturing and sustained demand from leading developers, the company continues to strengthen 
its order book and its contribution to the country’s clean energy transition.  
Mr. Sameer Nagpal, Chie f Executive Officer, Vikram Solar,  said, “As India’s solar sector continues to 
scale, the focus is increasingly on technologies that can deliver performance, reliability and long -term 
value across utility -scale projects. At Vikram Solar, we remain committe d to advancing our technology 
capabilities and maintaining the manufacturing and execution standards that our customers expect, 
delivering dependable, high -efficiency solar solutions that support the evolving needs of India’s 
renewable energy sector.”  
Abou t Vikram Solar Limited:  
Vikram Solar Limited is one of the leading Indian solar module manufacturers, specializing in efficient 
photovoltaic (PV) module manufacturing, with an international presence across 39 countries. Headquartered in 
Kolkata, West Bengal, it is one of the largest PV module manufacturers in India. Vikram Solar is a 9th time ‘Top 
Performer’ in PVEL’s PV Module Reliability scorecard and has been included in the Tier 1 solar PV modules 
manufacturer list of Bloomberg NEF for 9 consecutiv e quarters. Vikram Solar Limited has established a pan -India 
presence through an extensive distributor network of 119 authorized distributors and more than 750+ dealers.
