Mumbai, March 25, 2025:  With a strategic intent to capitalise on the strong and growing demand from 
Pune, Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel forex services company, has 
inaugurated a new owned-store in the high potential Balewadi region. The new store expands Thomas 
Cook India’s Foreign exchange network to 4 locations in Pune. This sustains aggressive expansion 
plans with 5 new branches/outlets opened pan India in the last six months.  Balewadi – a rapidly developing hub in Pune’s northern periphery was carefully chosen as a location for the Foreign exchange store as it offers the Company a range of valuable consumer segments including students, business and leisure travellers. Its  close proximity to Hinjewadi – Pune’s largest 
corporate hub ensures demand from working profe ssionals with global travel needs. With seamless 
connectivity and a growing number of commercial and residential establishments, Balewadi presents a strong opportunity for Thomas Cook to serve a dive rse and high-value customer base. The new store 
will offer travellers end-to-end Foreign Exchange products and services.
