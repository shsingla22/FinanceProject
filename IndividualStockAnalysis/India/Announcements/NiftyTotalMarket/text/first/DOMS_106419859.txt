Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
This is with reference to our letter no. DOMS/SE/25-26/60 and DOMS/SE/25-26/61 dated October 24, 
2025 and October 28, 2025, respectively, wherein it was, inter alia, informed that DOMS Industries 
Limited (‘the Company’ ) had received a Show Cause Notice ( ‘the said notice’ ) on October 22, 2025,

impugned order under protest and has received the release order from Office of the State Taxes 
Officer, Kashmir. The Release Order was received by the Company on October 29, 2025. 
 
The relevant details as per the requirement of Regulation 30 of SEBI LODR Regulations, read with SEBI
