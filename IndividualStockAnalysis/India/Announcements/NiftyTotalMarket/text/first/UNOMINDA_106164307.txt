Uno Minda Announces Leadership Transition and expansion of R&D 
capabilities into Czech Republic 
Gurugram -March 03, 2025: Uno Minda Limited ("Uno Minda"), a global Tier-1 supplier of 
proprietary automotive systems and solutions to original equipment manufacturers ("OEMs"), 
announced the superannuation of Mr. Rajeev Gandotra, CEO of the Lighting and Acoustics 
Domain (LAS)-2 and elevation of Mr Vivek Jindal as the CEO for the consolidated LAS Domain. 
Leadership Transition: 
Mr. Rajeev Gandotra, who has held various leadership roles in last 31 years with Uno Minda 
Group, has retired after a distinguished career. Uno Minda extends its sincere gratitude to 
Mr. Gandotra for his invaluable contributions and wishes him a fulfilling retirement. 
Mr. Vivek Jindal, Whole time Director and also formerly CEO of LAS-1, has been appointed as 
the CEO of the consolidated Lighting and Alternate Fuel Systems (LAS) Domain. Mr. Jindal 
brings extensive experience and a proven track record of success to this expanded role. Uno 
Minda has combined and strategically restructured its LAS-1 and LAS-2 domains to form the 
Lighting and Alternate Fuel Systems (LAS) Domain. This reorganization will enhance 
operational efficiency, streamline decision-making, increased focus on sustainable mobility 
and foster greater synergy across the lighting and alternate fuel systems businesses. 
Expansion of R&D Capabilities: 
In a move to further strengthen its technological leadership in advanced lighting solutions, 
Uno Minda has established a new R&D and engineering center in the Czech Republic. This 
state-of-the-art facility will focus on developing cutting-edge lighting technologies to meet 
the evolving needs of the global automotive industry.

Uno Minda Limited incepted in 1958, is a leading global Tier-1 manufacturer and supplier of 
innovative automotive solutions and systems to OEMs. They design and manufacture over 
25 categories of components and systems for vehicles across all segments (passenger cars, 
commercial vehicles, two-and three-wheelers) catering to both internal combustion engines 
(ICE) and electric/hybrid vehicles. 
It is one of the leading manufacturers of automotive switching systems, automotive lighting 
systems, automotive acoustics systems, automotive seating systems and alloy wheels. It has 
a leadership position in India in almost all the products it manufactures. Technology and 
innovation are the two strong pillars of the organisation, on the basis of which it has 
continued to lead the emerging trends in the automotive sector, over the past six decades. 
The Group is a global player in the automotive sector with 74 manufacturing facilities in India, 
Indonesia, Vietnam, Germany, Spain, and Mexico, as well as 37 R&D and Engineering Centres 
in India, Germany, Japan, Taiwan, Korea & Spain. It has 19 JVs/Technical Agreements with 
world renowned manufacturers from Japan, Germany, Korea, and China.
