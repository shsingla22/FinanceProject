5 November 2026 , Ahmedabad:  Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Limited (A PSEZ ), India’s 
largest and one of the world’s fastest growing Integrated Transport Operator , agrees to  acquire 
100% stake  in Netherlands -headquartered Evos B.V. (Evos) from Igneo Infrastructure  Partners . The 
proposed transaction is an all -cash deal at an enterprise value of  €2.56 bn. The transaction values 
Evos at an equity value of € 1.18 bn, implying an acquisition multiple of 11 .7x CY25 EV/EBITDA. For 
CY25, Evos delivered  revenue and EBITDA at €392 mn & €219  mn respectively (5 6% EBITDA margin). 
Pro forma net debt -to-EBITDA for the combined entity stands at 2. 76x (based on APSEZ FY26 and 
Evos CY25 financials ) and is expected to be around 2.5x by FY27. The transaction will be funded via 
debt  and is subject to regulatory and other approvals  and works council consultation .  
“Evos marks an important milestone in APSEZ’s trajectory towards handling 1 billion tons of cargo 
and building a truly global, integrated transport platform. We are delighted to welcome Evos into 
our portfolio, giving us significant presence across key global  liquid storage and energy hubs. We 
look forward to partnering with Europe, supporting its sustainable energy transition and creating 
enduring value while maintaining the financial prudence that underpins our growth ,” said Mr. Karan  
Adani, Managing Director, APSEZ . 
Evos is Europe’s second -largest independent liquid storage operator  with 6.3 m n cubic meters of 
storage capacity  at eight strategically located port -based terminals across the Netherlands, 
Germany, Spain, Belgium and Malta. Evos’ terminals are positioned in Europe’s most important 
energy and industrial hubs, including the Amsterdam -Rotterdam -Antwerp (ARA) region, Hambur g 
and the Mediterranean. The platform comprises  452 tanks , 63 vessels  and barge berths , and has 
several integrated pipeline connectivities  (including Schiphol airport and Central Europe Pipeline 
System) . Evos handled 37 MMT of throughput in CY25 . Evos operates a diversified liquids platform 
spanning both conventional and transition fuels. Its product mix includes gasoline, gasoil, marine 
fuels and jet fuel, alongside transition fuels such as sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), biofuels, 
methanol and b ioethanol, positioning the business to participate in Europe’s evolving energy mix.  
Evos is an investment -grade rated issuer.
