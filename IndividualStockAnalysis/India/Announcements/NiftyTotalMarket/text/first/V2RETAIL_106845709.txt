V2 Retail Limited Enters into Strategic Retail Partnership 
with Cupid Limited Across 425+ Stores  
 
Dedicated Retail Space of 400 Sq. Ft. Across Existing And Future Stores To Strengthen 
Personal Care, Wellness And Consumer Product Offerings . 
 
New Delhi , November  4, 202 6: V2 Retail Limited (V2 Retail) , one of India’s leading and fastest 
growing Value Retail Company, with 427 stores  spread across 26 states  and 1 Union Territory 
has entered into a long -term strategic retail arrangement with Cupid Limited, a growing player 
in the personal care, wellness and consumer healthcare segment.  
Under the arrangement, Cupid Limited will receive 400 square feet of dedicated retail space 
across V2 Retail's existing network of over 425 stores, as well as all future stores opened by the 
Company. The initiative is aimed at strengthening V2 Retail's pro duct assortment by 
introducing a wider range of personal care, hygiene, wellness and consumer products across 
its retail network.  
Supply operations are expected to commence shortly, with the products targeted to be 
available across V2 Retail's entire store network by mid -December 2026. The partnership is 
expected to enhance product accessibility, provide customers with greater choice  and 
further strengthen V2 Retail's in -store shopping experience.  
Commenting on the development results and performance, Mr. Ram Chandra Agarwal, 
Chairman & Managing Director said:   
"We are delighted to enter into this long -term strategic arrangement with Cupid Limited, a 
company that is rapidly establishing itself as a strong and trusted Indian brand in the personal 
care and wellness segment. At V2 Retail, our focus has always been o n delivering exceptional 
value to our customers through a combination of quality, affordability and choice.  
Cupid's growing portfolio of high -quality personal care and wellness products will significantly 
strengthen our in -store offerings and allow us to provide our customers with superior -quality 
products at highly compelling price points. We see significant po tential in this partnership as 
personal care, hygiene and wellness become increasingly important categories for Indian 
consumers.  
By providing Cupid with dedicated retail space across our entire store network, we intend to 
build a strong, differentiated offering that enhances the shopping experience, drives 
incremental customer engagement and creates meaningful value for both organis ations.  
As V2 Retail continues expanding its footprint across India, we look forward to growing this 
partnership and bringing Cupid's products to an increasingly large customer base."
