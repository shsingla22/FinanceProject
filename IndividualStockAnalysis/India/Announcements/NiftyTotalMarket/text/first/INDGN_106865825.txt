 PRESS RELEASE   
 
C-CAMP and Imperial College London Announce Indegene as First Industry Partner  
of Indo -UK Life Science Corridor  
 
Bengaluru, November 19, 2026: The Centre for Cellular and Molecular Platforms (C -CAMP) , in 
partnership with Imperial College London , welcomed Indegene  as the first industry partner of the 
Indo -UK Life Science Corridor . 
Supported through the Imperial Global India initiative , the Corridor connects world -class research in 
India and the UK with entrepreneurship, industry expertise, and pathways to translation. It is designed 
to enable formal, two -way sharing of knowledge and evidence on priority challenges, innovation 
readiness , and ecosystem needs. Indegene, C -CAMP, and Imperial College London will co -develop 
entrepreneurship programs and support the exchange of knowledge and talent between the two 
countries.  
Indegene is the AI -native operating partner for modern life sciences, bringing deep industry context to 
the Corridor , and a track record of working with the world’s top 20 biopharma companies.  
As part of the collaboration, Indegene  will help define industry challenges and support startup 
exchanges and ecosystem immersion programs. Initial focus areas include antimicrobial resistance, 
diagnostics, digital health, agritech, brain science, and mental health.  
The three organizations made the announcement at the C -CAMP –Imperial joint Roundtable on 
International Partnerships for AI Discovery and Innovation, convened under the Government of 
Karnataka’s Global Innovation Alliance  initiative as part of the Bengaluru Tech Summit 2026 . 
“Global health challenges cannot be solved in isolation ”, said Dr. Taslimarif Saiyed, CEO, C -CAMP. 
“This understanding was central to our vision for the Indo -UK Life Science Corridor, which connects 
the complementary innovation ecosystems of Imperial College London and C -CAMP to enable cross -
border problem -solving . Exposure to both ecosystems will produce smarter, more scalable, and more 
accessible innovations relevant to both populations.”  
Amanda Wolthuizen, V ice-President  (Strategy ), Imperial College London,  said: "Welcoming Indegene  
as the first industry partner of the Indo -UK Life Sciences Innovation Corridor is an important moment 
for this partnership. When we launched the initiative with C -CAMP earlier this year, we wanted to 
create a platform where research, talent and industry c ould come together to accelerate new 
technologies and solve shared global challenges. Indegene's expertise in life sciences make them an 
ideal first partner as we strengthen India -UK links and grow our network of industry collaborators.”  
Dr. Saurabh Jain, V ice President  - Global Delivery Centers, CSR, and Advocacy, Indegene, said, “The 
next decade of life sciences innovation will be defined by how well we connect science, talent, and 
industry across borders. This Corridor brings together the strengths of India and the UK, giving 
promising ideas a clearer path from discovery to real -world impact.  The partnership reflects our 
continued commitment to support deep science, strengthen translation pathways, and help promising 
innovation reach scale. ” 
  
About C -CAMP  
For over a decade, C -CAMP has been a cornerstone of innovation, funding, incubating, and helping advance 
more than 700 startups. These startups have raised more than $720 million and have a cumulative valuation of 
approximately $2.7 billion.  
C-CAMP has enabled the completion of more than 6,000 research projects and trained over 2,200 scientific 
personnel. More than 420 life sciences products are in the market, 1,000 patents have been filed by C -CAMP 
startups, and over 300 publications have bee n supported. These technologies have touched more than 70 
million lives.  
About Imperial Global India  
Imperial College London launched Imperial Global India in 2025 to drive high -impact science and technology 
programs with academic, industrial, and innovation partners, nurture a two -way flow of STEMB talent between 
the UK and India, and support Imperial’s growing Indian alumni network.
