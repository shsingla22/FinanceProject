Brief Profile of Shri Jayakumar S. Pillai
Mr. Jayakumar S. Pillai (DIN: 10041362), DMD of IDBI Bank has over 33 years
of experience in commercial banking, having last served as Chief General Manager of
Canara Bank before joining IDBI Bank in June 2023. He has led branches across diverse
regions, worked twice as Circle Head, and headed Canara Bank’s UK operations, gaining
significant international exposure. Presently, Shri Jayakumar leads the Bank’s corporate
business vertical along with several other critical functions such as Legal Department;
Human Resource (HRD); Information Technology & Digital Banking Department;
Finance & Accounts Department; Corporate Strategy & Planning Department (CSPD);
Administration & FIMD. He also has administrative oversight on Compliance and Risk
Department. He is also the Chairman of the Board of IDBI Trusteeship Services Ltd.
