Sub: Declaration of COD of the third & last part capacity of 100 MW out of 320 MW Bhainsara Solar PV 
Project of NTPC Renewable Energy Limited  
 
In terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we 
wish to inform you that based on certificate received on 25.03.2025 from Rajasthan Renewable Energy 
Corporation Limited, the third & last part capacity of 100 MW out of 320 MW Bhainsara Solar PV Project in 
Jaisalmer, Rajasthan of NTPC Renewable Energy Limited, a stepdown subsidiary of NTPC Limited through its 
subsidiary NTPC Green Energy Limited, is declared on Commercial Operation w.e.f. 20.03.2025. 
 
The first part capacity of 160 MW and second part capacity of 60 MW has already been declared on commercial 
operation w.e.f. 28.08.2024 and 07.01.2025 respectively. 
 
With this, the total installed and commercial capacity of NTPC group has now become 77,561.50 MW.
