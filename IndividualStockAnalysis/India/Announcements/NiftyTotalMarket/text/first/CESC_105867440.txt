Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, 2015, it is hereby informed that on 
Wednesday, May 29, 2024 at around 12.30  p.m. IST, a fire inciden ce occurred at Unit -1 Generator Transformer 
in the plant located at Baneswar Chak near Jhikurkhali village, Haldia, West Bengal  owned by Haldia Energy 
Limited (‘HEL’) , a wholly owned subsidiary of CESC Limited [‘the Company’]  causing partial disruption in plant 
operation.   
 The fire has subsided and HEL  is currently assessing the cause of the fire and the extent of damage. HEL has 
insurance coverage on the plant and machinery and the insurers have been informed about the incident.   
 
HEL  and the Company are taking all necessary steps to address the situatio n and aim to resume operations  at the 
plant as soon as possible .
