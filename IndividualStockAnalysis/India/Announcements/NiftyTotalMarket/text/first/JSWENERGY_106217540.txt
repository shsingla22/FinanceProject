JSW Energy Signs PHSPPA  for 1,500  MW / 12,000  MWh  PSP with UPPCL  
 
Mumbai, India  — May 1 , 202 5 — JSW Neo Energy Limited, a wholly owned  subsidiary 
of JSW Energy Limited (“The Company”) has signed Pumped Hydro Storage Power 
Procurement Agreement  (“PHSPPA ”) with Uttar Pradesh Power Corporation Ltd. 
(“UPPCL”)  for procurement of 1,500 MW / 12,000 MWh of pumped hydro energy storage.  
The PHSPPA  is for supply of energy storage capacity for a period of 40 years where the 
company will be entitled to receive a fixed capacity charge of ₹ 77.2 lakhs per MW per 
annum. The aforesaid  project , located at Sonbhadra district , Uttar Pradesh will be capable 
of a scheduled discharge of 8 hours  (with maximum continuous 6 hours) per day. The 
project is expected to be commissioned in next 6 years . 
Subsequent to this, the Company's total locked -in energy storage capacity now stands at 
28.3 GWh, which includes 26.4  GWh of PSP. The Company is well -positioned to achieve 
its target of 40 GWh of energy storage by 2030 .
