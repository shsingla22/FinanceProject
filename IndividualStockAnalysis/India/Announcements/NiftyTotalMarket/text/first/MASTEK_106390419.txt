Mastek Transforms Harmony Australasia’s HR with AI -Powered Oracle HCM Cloud and 
Redwood UX/UI  
 
Mumbai , India  / Australia , September 29 , 2025 : Mastek (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted AI -first 
digital engineering and cloud transformation partner, today announced  the successful implementation  
of Oracle HCM Cloud Applications at Harmony Australasia, a global mining leader in Australasia. 
Completed in just 56 weeks, the program has delivered a unified HR platform covering five legal entities 
and two business units, setting a new benchmark for HR trans formation in the mining sector.  
 Mining is a demanding sector, operationally challenging , labor- intensive, and governed by stringent legal 
and safety compliance formalities. Harmony Australasia, with operations in Australia and Papua New 
Guinea, embarked on a digital revamp to create a smart and streamlined human resource management 
function that caters to the highest safety standards and enhances employee experience for its diverse 
workforce. Entrusting Mastek as a technology partner, the mining leader aimed at effective workforce 
plannin g through real -time wo rkforce visibility to make informed decisions. Automation has significantly 
reduced manual intervention  and paperwork, while compliance management, and reporting have 
achieved high standardization, ensuring faster and more accurate responses.  
 
The transformation journey began with an advisory phase, where Harmony gained a clear understanding 
of Oracle’s modules and industry best practices. This was followed by the deployment of Oracle 
Recruiting Cloud (ORC), Core HR, and Learning. The final phas e brought Talent, Compensation, and HR 
Helpdesk onto the platform. All modules were rolled out in a single go -live event, creating a seamless 
end-to-end HR experience.  
 
Transformational  business and functional outcomes: 
• Unified HR Data: A single, accurate source of truth across Australia and PNG supports reliable 
analytics and compliance reporting.  
• Greater Efficiency:  Automation of manual processes has lowered costs and freed up HR teams 
to fo cus on higher -value activities.  
• Faster Decisions:  Dashboards and real -time insights now help leaders make more informed calls 
without delay.  
• Workforce Mobility : Standardized processes simplify employee lifecycle events . 
• Employee Engagement: User -friendly mobile tools have improved adoption, making HR services 
more accessible to employees.  
• Change Adoption: Careful planning, phased milestones, and open communication built 
confidence across the workforce.  
• Redwood UX/UI:  Leveraging both AI and Redwood’s capabilities, Harmony Australasia improved 
its user experience to foster stronger workforce engagement.  
 
  
  
“The successful implementation of Oracle Fusion HCM Cloud is a proud milestone in Harmony 
Australasia’s digital journey toward a smarter, more connected HR function. By unifying workforce 
processes on a single cloud platform, it simplifies operations acros s geographies and creates a scalable 
foundation to support their evolving workforce needs in long- term ” says  Sandra Almeida, Group HR 
Manager , Harmony Australasia . 
 ”A year ago, we set the ambitious goal of going live in August. Achieving this on schedule with zero 
escalations reflects not just the strength of our collaboration but also the dedication and discipline 
throughout the project execution journey. Harmony Australasia has been an exceptional client 
throughout, and we congratulate them on this successful transformation that sets a new benchmark for 
HR excellence.”  said Surya Nunna, Executive Vice President, AMEA, Mastek.
