Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
In compliance with the provision of Regulation 30 and 51(2) read with Schedule III of SEBI (LODR) 
Regulation 2015, as amended, this is to inform that the Board of Directors of Indian Renewable Energy 
Development Agency Limited (IREDA) in its meeting held t oday i.e., Monday, March 17, 2025, inter -

Commercial Borrowings (ECB) /Short term loans & WCDL from Bank .
