The 45th Annual General Meeting (AGM)  of the members of Deepak Fertilisers And

Mr. Sailesh C. Mehta, Chairman and Managing Director of the Company chaired the

compliance with the circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Govern ment of

(KMP) since the last Annual General Meeting : 
 
1. Mr. Subhash Anand, President – Strategy has been appointed as the President and

resignation of Mr. Deepak Rastogi.
