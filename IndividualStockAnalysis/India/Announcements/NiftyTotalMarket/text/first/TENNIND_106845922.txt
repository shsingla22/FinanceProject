PRESS RELEASE Q 2 and H1 FY’27 
 
Business  Highlights:  
Our strong momentum continued through Q2 and H1 FY ’27, driven by overall market growth, market share 
gains1 and disciplined execution  across the business. Value -Added Revenue grew 32. 2% in Q2 and 25.3% in H1, 
Volumes grew 25.4% in Q2 and 20.7% in H1 in line with growth in our served markets and reinforcing our 
leadership positions across Clean Air Commercial Trucks and Passenger Vehicle Suspension Systems.  
EBITDA grew 20.7% year -on-year in Q2 to INR 2,616 million at a margin of 17.2% on VAR, supported by 
productivity gains under the Company’s P3 (People, Performance, Pride) operating system and commercial 
discipline, partially absorbed elevated commodity costs and the incremental cost of operating as a listed entity. 
PAT was INR 1,734 million  with a PAT margin of 11.4%.  
Overall b ooked business now covers more than 100% of the Company’s FY ’30 revenue plan . The total order 
book stands at approximately ~INR 165,000 million and is split roughly 35% of CA / PT and 6 5% for ART.  Overall 
booked business includes  ~INR 8,750 million from export programs, strengthening multi -year revenue visibility.  
To keep capacity ahead of these commitments, the company has announced a new Clean Air manufacturing 
plant  in West India , with capital expenditure of ~INR 450 million. This is in addition to the North Indi a plant 
announced in FY ’26.

About Tenneco Clean Air India Limited:  
Tenneco Clean Air India Limited is part of the Tenneco Group, a US -headquartered global Tier -1 automotive component supplier. The 
Company manufactures and supplies critical, highly engineered and technology -intensive clean air, powertrain and suspension so lutions 
tailored to the needs of Indian OEMs and the export market. Its customers span passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles (inclu ding 
commercial trucks and off -highway vehicles) and industrial applications.  
The Company’s portfolio includes catalytic converters, diesel particulate filters (DPFs), selective catalytic reduction (SCR)  systems, mufflers, 
exhaust pipes, after -treatment systems, bearings, sealings, spark plugs, strut assemblies and shock absorbers t hat help OEMs meet stringent 
emission norms while improving vehicle performance, fuel efficiency and comfort. Its Champion®* spark plugs and Monroe®* susp ension 
brands are widely used in the Indian automotive market.  
Tenneco Clean Air India  and its subsidiaries  operate  12 manufacturing facilities and two R&D centres of global standard, located in key 
automotive OEM hubs in India.
