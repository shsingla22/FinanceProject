Delhivery Elevates Six Leaders to C -Suite, Strengthening 
Executive Leadership for the Next Phase of Growth  
Gurugram, India – May 16, 2026 – Delhivery (NSE: DELHIVERY, BSE: 543529), India’s largest 
logistics services provider, today announced the elevation of six senior leaders to Executive 
leadership positions. The appointments underscore the company’s continued growth trajectory, 
organisation al maturity, and its commitment to building a deep and capable leadership bench 
from within.  
The following appointments are effective immediately for Delhivery:  
Arun Bagavathi  has been appointed Chief Operating Officer - Network Operations.  Arun has 
led Delhivery’s network operations, fleet management, and control tower functions. In his elevated 
role, he will hold enterprise -wide accountability for the end -to-end performance and efficiency of 
Delhivery's nationwide logistics network.  
Prashant Gazipur  has been appointed Chief Operating Officer - In-city Operations.  Prashant 
has driven last -mile operations across Delhivery’s urban delivery network and has been 
instrumental in scaling the company’s last mile reach and capacity. He will now lead all city -level 
operations strategy including building out last -mile franch ise pickup and drop operations.  
Nikhil Ummat  has been appointed Chief Operating Officer - Engineering and Automation.  
Nikhil has led Delhivery’s automation initiatives and fulfillment centre operations, and has been at 
the forefront of the company’s proprietary drone and robotics programme under Delhivery 
Robotics. As Chief of Engineering & Automation, he will drive the company’s engineering and 
automation infrastructure strategy.  
Sunny Raja  has been appointed Chief Procurement Officer.  Sunny has led Delhivery’s 
commercials and procurement function, building scalable and cost -efficient supply -side 
capabilities. As Chief Procurement Officer, he will take on broader accountability for effective 
capex strategy, partner management, and comme rcial optimisation across the company.  
Varun Bakshi  has been appointed Chief Sales Officer.  Varun has been instrumental in scaling 
Delhivery’s Part Truckload (PTL) business and building long -term enterprise relationships with 
leading brands across categories. As Chief Sales Officer, he will lead the company’s overall direct 
and cross -selling str ategy across all business lines and customer segments.  
Vikas Kapoor  has been appointed Chief Strategy Officer.  Vikas has led Delhivery’s Full 
Truckload (FTL) business and brings a strong commercial and market understanding. In his new 

role, he will be responsible for shaping the company’s long -term strategic priorities, identifying 
new growth opportunities, and driving cross -functional alignment.  
  
“These elevations recognise the exceptional depth and quality of leadership we have built 
within Delhivery over many years. Arun, Prashant, Nikhil, Sunny, Varun, and Vikas have each 
demonstrated outstanding operational capability and business acumen in the ir respective 
domains. As we scale the organisation to serve a larger customer base, manage greater 
complexity, and pursue bold new opportunities in technology and commerce, it is both natural 
and important that our most seasoned professionals take on ente rprise -wide mandates. I am 
proud of this team and look forward to what we will accomplish together in the years ahead.”  
— Sahil Barua, Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer, Delhivery
