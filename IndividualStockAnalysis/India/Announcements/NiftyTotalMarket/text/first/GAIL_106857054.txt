Signing of MoU between GAIL (India) Limited & Krishak Bharati Cooperative 
Limited for exploring the opportunity to setup a gas -based fertilizer project in the 
State of Andhra Pradesh  
 
Visakhapatnam, 12th November 2026:  GAIL (India) Limited, India’s flagship natural gas 
company, today signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with  Krishak Bharati 
Cooperative Limited (KRIBHCO) to explore the opportunity of setting up a gas -based fertilizer 
project in the State of Andhra Pradesh.  
 
The MoU was formally signed by Shri Sanjay Agarwal, ED (BD, Start -up & R&D), GAIL and 
Shri C.S.Azad, Executive Director (Technical), KRIBHCO, in the presence of Shri V Sudhakar 
Chowdary, Chairman, KRIBHCO,  Shri S.  S. Yadav, Managing Director, KRIBHCO and               
Shri R.  K. Singhal, Director (Business Development), GAIL.  
 
The urea production facility is envisaged to have a capacity of 1.27 Million Metric Tons Per 
Annum (MMTPA) in Mulapeta, Srikakulam district of Andhra Pradesh along GAIL’s 
Srikakulam Angul Natural Gas Pipeline (SAPL). GAIL and KRIBHCO have agreed to join 
hands for implementation of the project through Joint Venture mode.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Shri V Sudhakar Chowdary, Chairman, KRIBHCO, said:  
 
“The signing of this MoU marks the beginning of a strategic partnership between GAIL and 
KRIBHCO. I am confident that KRIBHCO’s experience combined with GAIL’s leadership 
position in natural gas sector would bring value to the proposed project for successful 
implementation ." 
 
Shri R.  K. Singhal, Director (BD), GAIL, stated that, “It represents a strategic integration of 
India's gas and fertiliser value chains and demonstrates GAIL’s commitment to supporting 
sustainable industrial and agricultural growth.”
