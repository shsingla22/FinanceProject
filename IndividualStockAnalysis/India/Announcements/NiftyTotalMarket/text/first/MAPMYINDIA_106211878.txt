Press Release  
Rakesh Verma, Co -Founder & CMD, MapmyIndia, Honoured 
with Lifetime Achievement Award at the 16th Geospatial 
World Forum in Madrid, Spain  
 
 
Photo caption: Mr. Rakesh Verma, Co -Founder & CMD, MapmyIndia, being honoured with the Geospatial World Lifetime 
Achievement Award by His Excellency Mr. Dinesh K. Patnaik, Indian Ambassador to the Kingdom of Spain, at the Geospatial World  
Forum in Madrid, Spain.  
 
New Delhi, 25 April, 2025: In recognition of his pioneering contributions to India’s 
geospatial sector and his leadership in founding MapmyIndia — the country’s 
leading location and deep -tech company — Mr. Rakesh Verma , Co-Founder and 
Chairman & Managing Director, MapmyIndia, has be en conferred the prestigious 
Lifetime Achievement Award  at the 16th Geospatial World Forum , held in 
Madrid, Spain . The award was presented by His Excellency Dinesh K. Patnaik , 
Indian Ambassador to Kingdom of Spain.  
 
Organized by the Geospatial World Forum  (GWF)  — the premier global platform 
for geospatial thought leadership and innovation — the award celebrates Mr. 
Verma’s instrumental role in establishing indigenous digital mapping capabilities 
in India and taking Geo -tech to the global stage.  
 
Award Citation 
“Rakesh Verma, Co-Founder and C MD of MapmyIndia, is a visionary whose 
pioneering efforts laid the foundation of India's digital mapping industry. At a time 
when geospatial innovation was nascent, he dared to dream of a sef -reliant India 
in mapping. His leadership and advocacy have driven critical policy reforms and 
technological innovation, shaping MapmyIndia into a national geospatial 
powerhouse. Beyond business, his commitment to education and social good is 
commendable. We are honored to present h im with the Geospatial Leadership - 
Lifetime Achievement Award. ” 
Responding to the honor , Mr. Rakesh Verma  said “ I accept this recognition with 
immense pride and deep gratitude. But this achievement is not mine alone. I 
share it with Rashmi Verma, Co -Founde r & CTO of MapmyIndia, without whom 
this journey would never have begun. I also dedicate this award to Rohan Verma, 

 
Director at MapmyIndia, whose vision and leadership have been instrumental in 
scaling the company and driving technological advancements in our solutions.  
I extend this recognition to everyone who has contributed to building 
MapmyIndia, and most importantly, to our country — India.  
My sincere thanks to the Geospatial World Forum and the esteemed jury for this 
incredible honour. This award is a  tribute to the enduring spirit of Indian enterprise 
and innovation. I hope the MapmyIndia journey, and our success in building 
world -class geospatial technology, inspires the next generation of entrepreneurs 
and innovators to dream boldly and build indige nously.” 
Established in 2007, the Geospatial World Forum Awards  recognize and celebrate 
outstanding contributions by professionals, institutions, government bodies, and 
businesses in the global geospatial ecosystem. Winners are selected through a 
rigorous and credible evaluation process led by an eminent international jury. This 
year’s jury was chaired by Dr. Greg Scott (Australia) , Director, SDG Data Alliance, 
PVBLIC Foundation, and included Dr. Nadine Alameh (USA) , Director, Taylor 
Geospatial Institute; Willy Govender (South Africa) , CEO, Terra Group; Sallie 
Payne (Belgium) , Secretary General, EuroGeographics; and Raj Alla (India) , CEO, 
IIC Technologies.
