Zensar registered 4.1% sequential constant currency revenue growth for Q2FY27 
 
Pune, India, October 28, 2026 : Zensar Technologies , a leading Experience, Engineering and 
Engagement solutions company, announced its consolidated financial results for its second quarter, 
ending September 30, 2026, of the fiscal year 2026-2027. 
 
Financial Highlights: 
 
 In Q2FY27, the company posted revenue of $166.2M, sequential quarter constant currency 
growth of 4.1%. In INR terms, this performance reflects a quarterly QoQ growth of 5.1% and 
YoY growth of 11.5%. 
 In Q2FY27, the company reported EBITDA at 15.8% of revenue, a QoQ increase of 120 bps. 
 US region registered a QoQ revenue growth of 4.5% and quarterly YoY growth of 2.5% in 
constant currency. 
 Europe region registered a QoQ revenue growth of 1.3% and quarterly YoY growth of 0.3% in 
constant currency. 
 Africa region registered a QoQ revenue growth of 6.7% and quarterly YoY growth of 2.2% in 
constant currency. 
 Banking and Financial Services registered a QoQ revenue growth of 7.1% and quarterly YoY 
growth of 17.1% in constant currency. 
 Healthcare and Life Sciences registered a QoQ revenue growth of 2.4% and quarterly YoY 
decline of 9.9% in constant currency. 
 Manufacturing and Consumer Services registered a QoQ revenue growth of 2.9% and 
quarterly YoY decline of 0.1% in constant currency. 
 Telecommunication, Media and Technology registered a QoQ revenue decline of 2.2% and 
quarterly YoY decline of 21.6% in constant currency. 
 
Manish Tandon, CEO and Managing Director, Zensar, said, “Our focused investments in strategic 
priorities are delivering tangible results, reflected in strong, broad-based constant currency growth of 
4.1% in Q2FY27, supported by healthy volume momentum across all our focus geographies, while the 
improvement in EBITDA margin underscores the strength of our execution and operating discipline. 
 
Building on the momentum of our ZenseAI suite, we are progressing from proof of value to production-
scale adoption across several clients. AI-influenced deals now represent 36% of our order book for 
Q2FY27, demonstrating growing client confidence in our AI-led capabilities. We remain committed to 
delivering sustainable, profitable growth and creating long-term value for our clients and shareholders, 
while continuing to invest in the capabilities that will shape our future.” 
 
Pulkit Bhandari, CFO, Zensar, commenting on the Q2FY27 performance, said , “Our revenue grew 
across all geographies during the quarter. EBITDA margin stood at 15.8%, a sequential increase of 120 
bps. We continue to maintain a robust balance sheet, enabling us to pursue strategic investments and 
capitalize on emerging technology trends. 
 
The demand environment today is largely supported by continued investments in AI & digital 
modernization. This has led to evolving workforce needs, and an increasingly complex cybersecurity 
landscape. We believe the next phase of growth will favour organizations that can pivot seamlessly 
into newer trends, combine AI capabilities, domain expertise, and secure execution at scale.” 
 
 
Significant Wins in Q2FY27: 
 
 AI-native product modernization of a unified cloud-native customer portal, consolidating 
legacy systems for a leading enterprise software provider.  
 Zensar enabled inventory optimization through AI-driven predictive recommendations that 
reduce waste and improve purchasing decisions for a leading book distributor.  
 AI-led procurement and supply chain transformation, deploying intelligent automation to 
improve efficiency and decision-making for a leading global mining company.  
 Agentic AI workflows and operational intelligence for Change and Release Management 
transformation to accelerate decision-making for a leading global wealth management firm.  
 Deployment of proprietary ZenseAI.Guidewire to automate release impact analysis and 
CI/CD workflows, improving delivery efficiency for a leading insurance provider.  
 AI-powered itinerary planning platform development through a dedicated AI POD, 
accelerating travel experience innovation for a leading travel technology provider.
