2. Purpose of this Code 
 
2.1 Suzlon Energy Limited (“SEL or the “Company”) has adopted this Code in terms of 
Regulation 8(1) of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Proh ibition of Insider 
Trading Regulations), 2015.  
 
2.2 The purpose of this Code is to lay down practices and procedure s for fair disclosure of 
unpublished price se nsitive information. 
 
3. Applicability of this Code 
 
3.1 This Code applies to the Company.

6. Principles of Fair Disclosure for purposes of this Code 
 
The Company shall ensure:  
  
a. Prompt public disclosure of Unpu blished Price Sensitive Informa tion that would impact 
price discovery no sooner than credible and concrete informatio n comes into being in 
order to make such information generally available. 
 b. Uniform and universal dissemina tion of Unpublished Price Sensit ive Information to avoid 
selective disclosure.
