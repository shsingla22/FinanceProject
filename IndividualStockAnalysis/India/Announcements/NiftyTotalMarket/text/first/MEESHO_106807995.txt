BENGALURU,  Meesho, India’s largest e -commerce platform by Annual Transacting 
Users and Placed Orders, today announced that Net Merchandise Value (NMV) through its Content 
Commerce ecosystem grew 152% year -on-year supported by over 1.6 lakh active creators  over the 
last 12 months across India.  
The growth reflects a broader shift in how consumers across Bharat are shopping online. Shopping 
journeys do not always begin with a pre -existing intent to search for a specific product.  Consumers 
also browse for inspiration, explore recommendations and discover products along the way, with short -form 
video and creator content adding greater context to that journey.  
Video brings products to life by allowing consumers to see how something looks in a real -world setting, 
understand its size, fit or features and see how someone else uses or styles it before making a purchase 
decision. By combining discovery with demonstra tion, Content Commerce can help consumers move more 
intuitively from discovering a product to evaluating it and deciding whether to buy it.  
Bharat is at the centre of this participation. Around 90% of Meesho’s active Content Commerce 
creators are nano* creators and 81% creators are from non metro India, widening participation beyond 
established influencers and India’s largest cities. Tier 3 and Tier 4 markets such as Varanasi, Agartala, 
Jabalpur, Madurai accounted for 66% of Content Commerce orders , highlighting the role smaller towns 
and cities are playing in creator -led shopping.

and builds trust online. Increasingly, shopping journeys are becoming discovery -led, with consumers

Creating new pathways to digital entrepreneurship  
Content Commerce is widening participation in India’s creator economy. Homemakers account for 40% 
of Meesho’s active Content Commerce creator base, while young graduates account for 30%, or 
around 50,000 creators. Around 78% of active homemaker creators come from non -metro India.  
Meesho’s Content Commerce ecosystem is bringing a new generation of creators into the fold, with 90% 
of active creators being nano creators * and 25% being first -time creators . With a large base of these 
creators coming from smaller towns, the ecosystem is expanding participation in the creator economy 
beyond established influencers and opening up content -led commerce to more Indians.  
Importantly, the ecosystem is built around commerce outcomes rather than content engagement alone, 
with creators earning commissions on orders generated through their content. This gives creators a way to 
connect their creativity and understanding of their  audiences directly with economic outcomes.  
Megha Bhagotra, a creator with around 12,000 followers from Chandigarh , shared her journey with 
Meesho Creator Club, saying, “When I first started creating content, I was simply doing something I loved 
without knowing where it could take me. Over the last year, Meesho Creator Club has given me the 
opportunity to turn that passion into my primary source of income while building a  community that has grown 
significantly. The journey has also opened doors to collaborations with brands I had always dreamt of 
working with. Today, content creation is much more than something I enjoy. It has given me the 
independence to build a career on  my own terms and the confidence to dream bigger as a creator.”  
Making creator -led demand more accessible to sellers  
For sellers, Content Commerce creates an additional demand -generation channel. Building an influencer 
network independently can require marketing expertise, resources and access that many smaller 
businesses may not have. Through Meesho’s Content Commerce e cosystem, sellers can tap into a broad 
network of creators who can showcase their products to relevant audiences.

Today, more than 4 lakh sellers have opted into the Content Commerce ecosystem on Meesho . The 
model is performance -linked, with creators earning commissions when their content translates into orders. 
This gives sellers another way to generate demand through creator content while linking the economics to 
actual commerce outcomes.  
Creators can produce and distribute content both on Meesho and across third -party social platforms, 
allowing product discovery to begin wherever consumers are engaging with content and helping sellers 
reach potential customers beyond traditional search -led journeys.  
Short -form video brings discovery closer to transaction  
As the ecosystem scales, short -form video is becoming an important bridge between discovery, evaluation 
and purchase. A large and growing base of consumers is engaging with Video Finds , Meesho’s short -
form content surface, to discover and evaluate products through video.  
Over the last 12 months, Video Finds recorded nearly 4,800 crore content views and around 9,000 crore 
minutes of watch time , reflecting the scale at which consumers are engaging with short -form shopping 
content. By integrating product discovery more closely with the shopping journey, Video Finds allows 
consumers to move from seeing a product demonstrated to exploring and purch asing it on Meesho.  
While women’s fashion remains the largest category for creator -led shopping, discovery is expanding 
across a much wider range of products. Content can play different roles across categories, from helping 
consumers see how fashion looks or fits, to demonstr ating how an electronic accessory works or giving 
greater context around personal care and home products.  
As content, community and commerce become increasingly interconnected, Meesho’s Content Commerce 
ecosystem is bringing together three sides of digital commerce: helping consumers discover products 
with greater context, enabling creators to build income opportunities, and giving sellers new ways 
to generate demand.  With Bharat driving both creator participation and consumer adoption, creator -led 
discovery is becoming an increasingly important part of how India shops online.
