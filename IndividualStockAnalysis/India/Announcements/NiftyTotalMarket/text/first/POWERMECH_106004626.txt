The Compliance Officer welcomed the S hareholders, Directors, Auditors and other  Stakeholders to the Company’s 25th 
AGM. After ensuring that the requisite quorum was present, he requested Mr. Kishore Babu Sajja , Chairman and Managing 
Director, to commence the proceedings of the meeting.  
 
Mr. Kishore Babu Sajja , Chairman and Managing Director of the Company, C haired the meeting and conducted the 
proceedings of the meeting. Thereafter , both Mr. Kishore Babu Sajja, Chairman and Managing Director, and Mr. Rohit Sajja, 
President of the Company, individually delivered their speeches. Their addresses covered key areas including the Company’s 
performance, its positioning within the  industry, strategic initiatives for the upcoming years, and other broad aspects of the 
Company’s offerings.  
 
Thereafter, the Compliance Officer informed the members that the statutory registers such as register of Directors and Key 
managerial personnel an d their shareholding (as per Section 170 of the Companies Act, 2013 , (the  Act)) and register of 
Contracts (as per Section 189 of the Act) were made available electronically for inspection  during the AGM .

informed the members that the Reports of statutory auditors and Secretarial Auditor did not contain any qualifications.
