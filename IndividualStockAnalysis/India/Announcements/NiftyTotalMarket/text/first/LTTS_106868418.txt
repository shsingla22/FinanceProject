L&T Technology Services Launches SDVault.ai ™ to Bring 
Engineering Intelligence to the Software -Defined Vehicle V -Cycle  
 
 
Highlights:  
• 250+ domain -aware AI agents , paired  with deterministic automation and safety, cybersecurity and 
compliance guardrails  
• 12 interconnected engineering domains across the SDV V -cycle, from requirements to release  
• Early pilots indicate 30 –40% additional engineering capacity  
Bengaluru (India), November  23, 2026 : L&T Technology Services (BSE: 540115, NSE: LTTS) , a global 
leader in Engineering Intelligence Solutions & ER&D Consulting Services, today announced the launch of 
SDVault.ai ™, an AI -powered engineering and assurance toolchain  that helps automotive OEMs and Tier -1 
suppliers accelerate the development, integration and validation of Software -Defined Vehicles (SDVs). 
SDVault.ai ™ is part of LTTS’ EI for Engineering  suite and is built on EIOne , the company’s Engineering 
Intelligence  platform.  
SDVault.ai ™ brings Engineering Intelligence across 12 interconnected engineering domains, spanning 
requirements, system and E/E architecture, functional safety, cybersecurity, process assurance, software and 
code intelligence, verification, embedded debugging, virtua l ECU validation, trace and network analysis, root -
cause intelligence and regression and release assurance.  
 
With more than 250 domain -aware AI agents, SDVault.ai ™ combines AI with deterministic, rule -based 
engineering automation, particularly for safety -critical activities where repeatability, traceability and engineering 
control are essential. Engineers remain firmly in control of engineering decisions, verification and certificat ion. 
SDVault.ai ™ creates a connected engineering thread across the V -cycle, linking requirements and architecture 
with code, tests, failures, corrective actions and release evidence. This enables engineering teams to understand 
not only what changed, but what is impacted and whether the change has been adequately verified.  
Designed as a modular platform, SDVault.ai ™ can be adopted end -to-end or deployed selectively alongside 
existing OEM and Tier -1 engineering toolchains. Sensitive engineering data can remain within customer -
controlled environments, supporting enterprise security and IP -protection requirements.
