2025, were entitled to vote on the proposed resolutions as set out at Item No's. 1 to 6 in the Notice 
of the AGM of Sumitomo Chemical India Limited. 
• The voting period for remote E-voting remained open from Thursday, the 31 July 2025 (09.00 
a.m.) to Sunday, 03 August 2025 (5.00 p.m.) and the MUFG Intime India Private Limited e-
voting platform was blocked thereafter. 
• The Company also provided electronic voting facility to the shareholders attending the AGM 
through VC / OAVM and who had not cast their vote earlier.

employment of the company.
