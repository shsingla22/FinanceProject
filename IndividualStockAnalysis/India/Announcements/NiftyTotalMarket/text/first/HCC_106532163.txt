HCC reports Net Profit of ~85.9 Cr for Q3 FY26 
Construction Business records turnover of ~921.8 Cr in Q3 FY26 
Mumbai: February 12, 2026: HCC E&C reported standalone turnover and net profit of 
~921.8 crore and ~85.9 crore, respective ly, in 03 FY26, as against ~1 ,002.1 crore and 
(~216.4) crore, respective ly, in 03 FY25. The Company's order book stood at ~13,148 crore 
as of December 31, 2025. HCC reported consolida ted revenue of ~925.3 crore in 03 FY 26 
versus ~1 ,006.8 crore in 03 FY 25-the figures are not comparable owing to the 
divestment of Steiner AG in the last fiscal year. The consolidated profit was at ~8.07 crore in 
03 FY26 versus (~38.9) crore in 03 FY25. 
Financial highlights- HCC unaudited standalone results 
-Turnover of ~921 .8 crore in 03 FY 26 versus ~1 ,002.1 crore in 03 FY 25 
Net Profit of ~85.9 crore in 03 FY 26 versus (~216.4) crore in 03 FY 25 
EBITDA margins at 15.2% in 03 FY 26 compared to 14.7% in 03 FY 25 
Demonstrating sustained momentum in order acquisition, the Company secured two orders 
aggregating ~1 ,478 crore in joint venture from the Northern Frontier Railways, in which HCC 
holds 65% stake. The company is also the lowest bidder in projects worth ~2,675 cr (HCC 
share: {1, 517 cr) and the bid pipeline remains robust at approximately ~53,820 crore, 
reflecting strong business momentum and a healthy outlook. 
HCC reduced its corporate guarantee exposure to PRPL by ~3 ,364 crore, with the obligation 
now limited to ~571 crore (20% of the principal amount) following requisite approvals . 
Furthermore, the company has strengthened its balance sheet with a ~1 ,000 crore Rights 
Issue which was subscribed -200%. 
Steady progress was achieved across multiple infrastructure projects in 03 FY26. Hon'ble 
Chief Minister of Bihar, Shri Nitish Kumar, inaugurated the commencemen t of works for the 
Patna Metro Rail Project (Packages 05 & 06). At the Indore Metro Rail Project, piling works 
are underway at all five stations, while station excavation is in progress at the Airport and 
Rajwada stations. 
Among key updates, Vishnugad -Pipalkoti HEP has achieved a significant portion of dam 
concrete works, and 8.5 km of the planned 12.1 km HRT excavated using TBM. Work at the 
Agardanda Creek, Tehri Pumped Storage, and Bhivpuri Pumped Storage projects is 
progressing on schedule.
