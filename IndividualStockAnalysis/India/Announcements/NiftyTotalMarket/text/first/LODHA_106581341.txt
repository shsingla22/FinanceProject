We would like you to take note of our key operational updates for Q4FY26:  
 
a) Pre-sales:  We achieved our best ever quarterly pre -sales of  ₹ 58.9bn in Q4FY26 (+23% YoY). Our pre -
sales for the full year  stood at ₹ 205.3bn in FY26 (+16% YoY growth) – March saw select deferral of 
sales due to the Iran war leading to pre -sales being ₹ 4.7 bn below guidance.  
  
b) Collections:  Collections were ₹ 52.3bn for Q4FY26 (+18% YoY),  significantly picking up from previous 
quarter as construction activity ramped up. FY26 collections were ₹ 151.6bn, (+5% YoY).  
 
c) Business development: We added  one project in MMR with GDV of ₹ 13bn in Q4FY26. During FY26, 
we added twelve projects across MMR, Pune, Bengaluru and NCR with  GDV of ₹ ~600bn, 2.4x our 
annual guidance . As of 1st April 2026, the Company has GDV amounting to ₹ ~2 trillion available for 
sale (excluding land bank in townships which will not be used in next 5 years). Consequently, we 
expect to reduce business development investments over the next 24 months and increase free cash 
flow. 
 
d) Net debt:  Our net debt reduced by ₹ 8.0bn to ₹ 53.7bn  during the quarter on the back of strong 
collections. Despite significant investment in business development in FY26, our Net debt/Equity  
stands at 0.23x , well below our ceiling of 0.5x Net debt/Equity.
