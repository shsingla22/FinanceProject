 KEC International receives a n Income Tax Refund of Rs. 17 7 crores  
 
Mumbai, March 05 , 202 5: KEC International Ltd., a global infrastructure EPC major, an RPG Group Company, 
announced today that it has received a refund of Rs. 17 7 crore s from the Income Tax Department.  
 
Mr. Vimal Kejriwal, MD & CEO, KEC International Ltd. commented , “We are pleased with  this substantial 
income tax refund, which includes both the principal amount and applicable interest  from past tax 
assessments . This refund strengthens our liquidity position and further enhances our financial  leverage . As we 
move forward, we remain committed to operational excellence and prudent capital management to drive 
sustainable growth across our business verticals .” 
 
About KEC International Limited  (www.kecrpg.com  ): 
KEC International is a global infrastructure Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) major. It has a 
presence  in the verticals of Power Transmission & Distribution, Civil, Transportation,  Renewables, Oil & Gas 
Pipelines, and Cables . The Company is currently executing infrastructure projects in 30+ countries and has a 
footprint in 1 10+ countries (includes EPC, Supply of Towers and Cables). It is the flagship Company of the RPG 
Group.  
 
About RPG Enterprises ( www.rpggroup.com  ): 
RPG Enterprises, established in 1979, is one of India’s fastest -growing business groups with a turnover of US$ 
4.8 Billion. The group has diverse business interests in the areas of Infrastructure, Tyres, Pharma, IT and 
Specialty as well as in emerging innovation -led technology businesses .
