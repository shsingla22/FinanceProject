He was also awarded with the prestigious ‘Turnaround 
Management Association (TMA) - 2020 Mega Company 
Turnaround Award’ for the resolution of Essar Steel India Limited 
by TMA (a Chicago based global firm focusing on recognition of 
corporate restructuring and turnarounds) in September 2020. 
He was also associated with TMA India Chapter for promoting 
turnaround management practice and was a member of its 
advisory Board.
