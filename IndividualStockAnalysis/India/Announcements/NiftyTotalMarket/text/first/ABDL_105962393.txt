Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited Adopts Digitisation with Lexplosion's 
SaaS Offering for Enhanced Regulatory Compliance Management 
Mumbai, 22nd August 2024 : Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABDL), the 3rd largest spirits 
company in India, has initiated a strategic alliance to strengthen its legal compliance framework with 
Lexplosion Solutions to deploy Komrisk, a cutting-edge regulatory compliance management software. 
This partnership aims to further enhance ABDL's compliance framework, ensuring seamless and 
robust regulatory adherence across its expansive national footprint. With Komrisk, ABDL is poised to 
elevate its operational excellence, reinforcing its commitment to integrity and industry leadership. 
The implementation of Komrisk is expected to provide ABDL with an edge to ease the operational 
efficiencies across the group. This includes centralized compliance management across all locations 
including own bottling units, ENA distillery and regional offices and regulatory domains, as well as real-
time regulatory updates and alerts. Komrisk will also enable improved risk assessment and mitigation, 
while offering enhanced reporting and analytics capabilities. Additionally, ABDL will further be able to 
streamline workflows for compliance-related tasks, thereby improving efficiency and effectiveness in 
managing statutory regulatory requirements. 
Alok Gupta, Managing Director of Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited, stated, "In today's complex 
regulatory environment, maintaining robust compliance is crucial for any business. With Lexplosion 
coming on board through its compliance management offering in Komrisk, we are certain to augment 
our compliance management capabilities. We expect the new SaaS offering to improve efficiencies 
with respect to time. This move aligns with our commitment to operational excellence and corporate 
governance framework." 
Indranil Choudhury, CEO of Lexplosion Solutions, commented , "We are privileged to partner with 
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited in their journey towards comprehensive compliance 
management. Komrisk's cloud-based platform will provide ABDL with real-time regulatory updates, 
streamlined compliance processes, and enhanced visibility across its operations. This partnership 
underscores the growing importance of technology-driven solutions in managing complex regulatory 
landscapes." 
  

 
 About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited ( www.abdindia.com ) 
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABDL) is the third largest Spirits company in India, in terms of 
annual sales volumes between FY14 and FY24. ABDL has a presence in five main flavors, i.e., whisky, 
brandy, rum, vodka, and gin, with ‘millionaire’ brands like Officer’s Choice Whisky, Officer’s Choice Blue 
Whisky, Sterling Reserve Premium Whiskies and ICONiQ White Whisky. Currently, its manufacturing 
network comprises 33 units, of which 9 are owned bottling units, 1 owned distillery, and 23 non-owned 
manufacturing units.
