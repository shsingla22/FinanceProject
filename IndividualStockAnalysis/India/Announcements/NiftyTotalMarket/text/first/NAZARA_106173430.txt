Hasbro and Kiddopia Partner to Bring PJ Masks to the Kiddopia App 
Platform  
 
India , 17th March, 2025  – Kiddopia, the award -winning  early learning app , has teamed up with 
Hasbro, a leading games, IP and toy company , to bring the beloved PJ Masks characters to its 
immersive digital platform. This collaboration will introduce exciting, educational, and interactive 
experiences featuring Catboy, Owlette, and Gekko, offering preschoolers a whole new way to 
engage with their favorite ni ghttime heroes.  
 
As part of the partnership, Kiddopia will launch a series of exclusive PJ Masks -themed activities 
and games, designed to blend entertainment with early childhood learning. The in -app 
experiences will encourage creativity, problem -solving, and skill -buildin g while keeping children 
engaged in a safe and enriching digital environment.  
 
"We are thrilled to partner with Hasbro to bring PJ Masks to Kiddopia," said Dhaval  Sheth, COO, 
KIddopia . "Our mission is to make learning fun, and by integrating these beloved characters into 
our platform, we are creating an exciting new way for kids to explore, learn, and play."  
 
The new PJ Masks content on Kiddopia will include:  
 
- Interactive mini -games that reinforce early math, literacy, and cognitive skills  
 
- Creative storytelling experiences featuring the PJ Masks heroes  
 
- Engaging problem -solving challenges that promote teamwork and resilience  
 
"PJ Masks has captivated young audiences worldwide, and we look forward  to bring ing these 
heroic adventures to the Kiddopia app," said Nick Mitchell, SVP of Business Affairs at Hasbro . "This 
partnership aligns perfectly with our goal of delivering engaging, high -quality play experiences 
across multiple platforms."  
 
The PJ Masks experience on Kiddopia will be available  later this year , giving young fans an 
interactive way to join their favorite heroes on educational adventures. The update will be 
accessible to all Kiddopia subscribers on iOS and Android.  
 
For more information, visit https://kiddopia.com/  or download Kiddopia from the App Store and 
Google Play.
