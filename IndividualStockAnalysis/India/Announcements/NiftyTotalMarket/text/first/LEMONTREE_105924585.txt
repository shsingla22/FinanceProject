Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in  
Kota, Rajasthan  
 
New Delhi, July 24th, 2024 : Lemon Tree Hotels announced its latest signing – 
Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotel s, Kota. The property, which shall be managed by 
Carnations Hotels Private Limited, a  wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels 
Limited , is expected to open in  FY 2025 .  
 
Kota, situated along the Chambal River in Rajasthan, is a captivating fusion of 
historical allure and contemporary vibrancy. Renowned as the "Coaching Capital of 
India," it draws numerous students annually who seek success in competitive 
exams. However, be yond its academic prominence, Kota boasts a wealth of 
heritage including magnificent forts, tranquil palaces, and bustling markets. This 
city provides a distinctive experience for visitors, blending the excitement of 
academic endeavo urs with the enchantmen t of Rajasthani traditions.  
 
Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotels, Kota will feature 58 well -appointed rooms, a 
restaurant, banquets, meeting rooms, a swimming pool, a fitness center and other 
public areas. The Kota Airport is about 2 kms a way while Jaipur Inte rnational 
Airport is about  246 kms from t he property. The Kota Junction R ailway Station is 
about 10 kms away. The hotel is also well connected by roadways for both public 
and private transport.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Fra nchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels commented, “ We are delighted  to announce the expansion of 
our port folio in Rajasthan . We are expanding our footprint in tier II, III and IV cities  
to make Lemon Tree Hotels the most preferred brand across different segmen ts. 
This opening will be in addition to our nine existing and four  upcoming hotels and 
resorts in the state.”  
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of the largest hotel chains in India, and 
owns/leases/operates/franchises hotels across the upscale, upper -midscale, 
midscale and economy segments. LTHL delivers differentiated yet superior service 
offerings, with a compelling value proposition. The group offers seven brands to 
meet guests’ needs across all levels, viz. Aurika Hotels & Resorts, Lemon Tree 
Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox Hotels by Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Prima by 
Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Select by L emon Tree Hotels and Keys Lite by Lemon Tree 
Hotels.  

 
LTHL opened its first hotel with 49 rooms in May 2004. Today, the company has a 
portfolio of 160+ hotels, which includes over 100 operational hotels and more than 
60 hotels set to open in India and inte rnationally. Lemon Tree Hotels are located 
across metro regions, including the NCR, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad 
and Chennai, as well as numerous tier I, II and III cities such as Pune, Ahmedabad, 
Chandigarh, Jaipur, Indore, Aurangabad, Udaipur, V isakhapatnam, Kochi, Ludhiana, 
Thiruvananthapuram, Vijayawada etc. The company expanded internationally with 
hotels opening in Dubai in December 2019, in Bhutan in February 2020 and in 
Nepal in April 2024.
