Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Apropos the captioned subject and pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that Sun Drops Energia 
Limited (formerly known as Sun Drops Energia Private Limited) , a subsidiary of  KPI Green Energy 
Limited (“the Company”),  has received an international order from FVE Lifecare General 
Trading LLC,  United Arab Emirates  for the supply, installation, and commissioning of a 33 kWp 
Solar Power System integrated with a 573 kWh Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)  to 
power a containerized Data Centre Facility.  
The receipt of this order reflects the Company’s expanding international presence beyond 
Indian territory and demonstrates its growing capability to enter newer growth segments. The 
project highlights the increasing relevance of integrated solar and/or wind & battery energy 
storage solutions  in ensuring uninterrupted and efficient power supply for commercial and 
industrial applications including Data Centres.  
Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) projects are grid -connected or standalone energy 
storage facilities that enable  energy storage during off -peak periods and discharge during 
peak demand —supporting grid stability, peak load management, and renewable energy 
integration. In addition,  BESS plays a critical role in providing reliable backup power for mission -
critical infrastructure such as data centres, ensuring uninterrupted operations during grid 
outages.  
With the rapid growth of data centres  globally, which require highly reliable, customised , 
round -the-clock power solutions , this order underscores the company’s capability to address 
the evolving energy needs  of data centre operators through integrated renewable energy and 
energy storage solutions . 
This milestone reflects another important step in the Company’s growth journey,  as it continues 
to achieve meaningful operational and business progress  while steadily expanding its 
capabilities across emerging clean energy segments.
