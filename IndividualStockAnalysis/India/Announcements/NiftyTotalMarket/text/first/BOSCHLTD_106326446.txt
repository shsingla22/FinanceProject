Mr. Soumitra Bhattacharya chaired the Meeting. The requisite quorum being present, the Chairman 
called the Meeting to order. All Directors  except Ms. Hema Ravichandar, Chairperson Nomination &

 The Chairman thereafter addressed the members with his speech.  
 The Chairman informed the members that the remote e- voting commenced on Friday ,

Services (India) Limited.  
 
The Chairman  further  informed  that Mr. Prasanna Bedi, Practicing Company Secretary  is the 
Scrutinizer  appointed by the Board  to scrutinize the votes  cast through  remote  e-voting and 
electronic  voting  at the AGM .
