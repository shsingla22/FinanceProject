About Edelweiss Financial Services  
Edelweiss is a diversified financial services company with seven independent and well -governed businesses. The businesses 
include  Alternative Asset Management , Mutual Fund, Asset Reconstruction, NBFC , Housing  Finance, General Insurance and 
Life Insurance. The businesses have robust operating platforms, dedicated management teams and strong boards that 
ensure the highest standards of governance. Edelweiss employs over  6,000 people, serves  around 1.4 Cr customers , and 
manages  over INR 2,80,000 Cr worth of assets.
