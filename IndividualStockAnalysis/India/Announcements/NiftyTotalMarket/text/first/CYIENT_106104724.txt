PRESS RELEASE  
 
BVR SCIENT Announces SCIENT SPARK Certificate 
Program for Aspiring Students and Start-Up Enthusiasts 
 
Hyderabad, India, January 4, 2025: Dr. BVR Mohan Reddy School of Innovation and 
Entrepreneurship (BVR SCIENT), in collaboration with the Department of 
Entrepreneurship and Management, IIT Hyderabad, launched SCIENT SPARK, a 
structured and immersive two-month entrepreneurship program designed for 
students and aspiring entrepreneurs.  
SCIENT SPARK offers a holistic and hands-on approach to entrepreneurship, guiding 
participants through key methodologies such as design thinking, opportunity 
recognition, market analysis, and prototyping. The program is structured to 
culminate in creating a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and a compelling pitch deck, 
equipping participants with the tools needed to transform innovative ideas into 
viable ventures. 
Combining faculty-led sessions with insights from start-up founders and industry 
mentors, SCIENT SPARK ensures a perfect balance of academic rigor and real-world 
exposure. Dedicated working sessions led by seasoned entrepreneurs provide 
participants with focused mentorship to refine their ideas into actionable business 
opportunities. The program also offers continuous offline resources and guidance, 
creating an ecosystem that nurtures entrepreneurial potential at every step. 
Dr. Sudhakar Potukuchi, CEO of BVR SCIENT, said, “At BVR SCIENT, we are 
committed to nurturing the next-generation innovators and entrepreneurs who will 
shape the future of our nation and beyond. The launch of SCIENT SPARK reflects our 
vision to provide budding entrepreneurs with a structured platform to transform 
their ideas into impactful ventures. Programs like this are critical for creating a 
strong entrepreneurial ecosystem that bridges academic knowledge with real-world 
application. With full scholarships for promising students, we aim to make this 
opportunity accessible to talented individuals, regardless of their financial 
background.” 
“Through SCIENT SPARK and other similar initiatives, BVR SCIENT is paving the way 
for innovation and entrepreneurial excellence, empowering young minds to turn 
their passion into purpose,” he added.   
"SCIENT SPARK is a testament to our commitment to fostering innovation and 
entrepreneurship. This initiative, in collaboration with BVR SCIENT and the 
Department of Entrepreneurship and Management, IIT Hyderabad, reflects our 
commitment to bridging academic knowledge with practical application. Designed 
to prepare our students for success, this program is not exclusive to IIT students. It 
is open to all aspiring entrepreneurs, providing an inclusive platform for everyone," 
said, Prof. B.S. Murty, Director, IIT Hyderabad.

About BVR SCIENT 
Dr. BVR Mohan Reddy School of Innovation and Entrepreneurship (BVR SCIENT) is 
established in collaboration with IIT-Hyderabad, Cyient Foundation (the CSR wing 
of Cyient Ltd.), and the Shibodhi Foundation (Dr. BVR Mohan Reddy's 
family foundation) with a state-of-the-art building, teaching and learning facilities 
at the IIT Hyderabad Campus. The school aims to nurture and develop world-class 
innovation and entrepreneurial talent through capability building for potential 
entrepreneurs and capacity building of entrepreneurial mindset.
