In compliance with the various circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs and the Securities 
and Exchange Board of India, the 23rd Annual General Meeting ( “AGM ”) of Anupam Rasayan India 
Limited (the “ Company ”) was held on September 30, 202 6, at 09:30 a.m. IST through Video Conferencing 
(“VC”) / Other Audio -Visual Means (“OAVM”) to seek approval of the Members  of the Company on 
resolutions set out in the Notice convening the AGM.  
 
The AGM was scheduled to commence  at 09:30 a.m. IST and commenced at 09:39 a.m. IST.  
 
Dr. Kiran Patel, the Chairperson, extended a warm welcome to all the Members. The Chairperson 
informed that the AGM is being conducted through Video Conferencing mode  (“VC”) /Other Audio -
Visual Means (“OAVM”) in accordance with the circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs 
and the Securities and Exchange Board of India. The Annual Report, along with the Notice of 23rd AGM, 
was sent to all the eligible Members  electronically on September 08, 202 6, in compliance with the issued 
circulars. He also informed that the Members attending the AGM  through VC are counted for the 
purpose of quorum under Section 103 of the Companies Act, 2013.  
 
Mr. Ashish Gupta, Company Secretary & Compliance Officer (“CS”) confirmed that the sufficient 
quorum was present to commence the AGM . He further informed that the Directors and Key Managerial

Thereafter, the Chairperson called the AGM  to order. He requested all the Directors and KMP, who had 
joined through VC from their respective locations as panelists , to introduce themselves. Thereafter, all 
the Directors and KMP introduced themselves and informed their place of attending the AGM . 
 
The Chairperson also introduced CA Sunil Vatvani , Partner of M/s. Natvarlal Vepari & Co., Chartered 
Accountants, Surat , Statutory Auditor and CS Mohan Baid, Partner of M/s . M D Baid & Associates, 
Secretarial Auditor of the Company and Scrutinizer, who were also present in the AGM  through VC from 
their respective locations. Further, the Chairperson invited the CS to provide general instructions to the 
Members regarding participation in th e AGM . 
 
Thereafter, the CS greeted the Members and took them through certain key points regarding 
participation at the AGM . He informed the Members that the remote e -voting facility on the resolutions 
to be passed in the AGM was provided to all the Members from Sunday , September 27, 202 6 (from 9:00 
a.m. IST) and ended  on Tuesday , September 29, 202 6 (at 5:00 p.m. IST) , through N ational Securities 
Depository Limited (“NSDL”) . The e -voting facility was also provided at the AGM, which remained open 
for 15 minutes after conclusion of the AGM. Further, the Members were informed that those who had 
not cast their votes during the remote e -voting may cast their votes after the conclusion of the AGM and 
the Members who had already cast their votes cannot cast the ir vote  again.  
 
The CS also informed that the Register of Directors and Key Managerial Personnel and their 
Shareholding, the Register of Contracts or Arrangements in which the Directors are interested, Register 
of Members, a Certificate from the Secretarial Auditor of the Company under the Securities and Exchange 
Board of India (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021, the ESOP Scheme 
and a copy of the Memorandum and Articles of Association of the Company were available electronically 
for inspection by the Members during the AGM .  
 
Thereafter, the Chairperson addressed the Members and gave a brief speech. He then requested Mr. 
Anand Desai, Managing Director, to address the Members.  
 
Thereafter, Mr. Anand Desai welcomed the Members and gave a brief speech. Mr. Gopal Agrawal, CEO, 
also delivered his speech and apprised the Members about the financial and operational highlights of the 
Company. He also extended his gratitude to all the board members, employees, Members  and customers 
for their continued  support and dedication  towards the Company.  
 
The Chairperson then requested the CS to provide a summary of the auditor’s reports.  
 
The CS stated that M/s. Natvarlal Vepari & Co. , Statutory Auditor  and M /s. M D Baid & Associates,  
Secretarial Auditor,  have expressed an unqualified opinion in their respective audit reports for the 
financial year ended March 31, 202 6. As there were no qualifications, observations or adverse remarks  
on financial statements and matters, which have any material bearing on the functioning of the Company, 
the respective audit reports were taken as read.  
 
The Chairperson then took the notice convening the AGM as read and mentioned about the facility to 
cast the vote electronically on all resolutions set forth in the Notice of AGM. Further, he took up the 
resolutions as set forth in the Notice of AGM and read out the matters for resolutions:

Ordinary Businesses:  
 
Item no. 1 : Ordinary Resolution:  To receive, consider and adopt the Audited Standalone Financial 
Statements for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, the Audited Consolidated Financial Statements 
for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, the Auditors’ Report thereon and the Board of  Directors’ 
Report of the Company for the financial year 2025 -26. 
 
Item no. 2 : Ordinary Resolution:  To declare a final dividend on Equity Shares for the financial year 
ended March 31, 2026 . 
 
Item no. 3 : Ordinary Resolution:   To appoint a Director in place of Mr. Anand Sureshbhai Desai, 
Managing Director (DIN: 00038442), who retires by rotation as Director in terms of Section 152(6) of the 
Companies Act, 2013, and being eligible, offers himself for reappointment as Director . 
 
Special Business : 
 
Item no. 4 : Ordinary Resolution:  To ratify the remuneration payable to the Cost Auditor for cost audit 
for the period from April 01, 2026 to March 31, 2027 . 
 
It was informed that all the resolutions were already put to vote through remote e -voting so there would 
be no proposing and seconding of the resolutions  and that there would be no voting by show of hands.  
 
Thereafter, the Chairperson invited the Member s to ask their questions and requested the moderator to 
facilitate the question -answer (Q&A) session. The moderator commenced the Q&A session after 
instructions to the  Members . Few Members  who had registered themselves as speaker, failed to join the 
Q&A session. The questions of the other Members  were replied by Mr. Gopal Agrawal, CEO . 
 
The Chairperson then thanked all the panelists  and the Members and expressed his gratitude towards 
the Members and the Directors for joining the AGM . He wished good health to all and declared the 
proceedings of the AGM  as concluded.  
 
The AGM  was concluded at 10:14  A.M. IST. 
 
The e -voting platform remained open for 15 minutes after the conclusion of the AGM .
