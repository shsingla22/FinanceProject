      PRESS RELEASE  
 
Himadri’s C MD & CEO  Anurag Choudhary Honoured as BT India’s Best CEO 
2026 in Chemicals Category  
 
 
 
 
 
Kolkata | April 2 , 202 6: Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd (HSCL), a global leader in speciality 
chemicals and advanced materials, would like to announce that our Chairman, Managing Director 
and CEO, Anurag Choudhary, has been honoured as Business Today India’s Best CEO Awards 
2026 (Chemicals) . 
The recognition, presented as part of the BT MindRush 2026 , celebrates India’s top corporate 
leaders who are building globally competitive enterprises and driving economic growth in a 
rapidly evolving global environment.  
Our CMD, Mr. Choudhary has been recognised in the Chemicals category  for his role in steering 
Himadri’s transformation into a diversified speciality chemicals and advanced materials company, 
with a strong focus on innovation, value -added product segments, and global expansion. Under 
his leadership, the Company has strengthened its presence across speciality carbon black, 
advanced materials, and emerging segments such as battery materials, while maintaining a sharp 
focus on operational excellence, sustainabi lity, and long -term value creation.  
In addition, Mr. Choudhary also participated in a leadership panel discussion at the event titled 
Leading Through Disruption , where leading CEOs shared insights on strategy, risk, and navigating 
business transformation i n a rapidly changing environment.  

 
 
This recognition reinforces Himadri’s position as a forward -looking organisation, driven by strong 
leadership and a clear strategic roadmap for sustained growth.  
 
About: Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd  
Himadri Speciality  Chemical Ltd is a global speciality chemical conglomerate with a strong 
focus on research and development (R&D), innovation and sustainability.  
As a pioneer in the production of lithium -ion battery materials in India, it continuously 
develops and innovate s raw materials of the lithium -ion battery value chain. Its diverse 
product portfolio includes speciality carbon black, coal tar pitch, refined naphthalene, 
advanced materials, SNF, speciality oils, clean power, etc., catering to various industries such 
as lithium -ion batteries, paints, plastics, tires, technical rubber goods, aluminium, graphite 
electrodes, agrochemicals, defence and construction chemicals. Himadri operates in both 
domestic and international markets, exporting to 56 countries across the gl obe.  
With a strong commitment to corporate governance, safety and sustainability, it has 8 zero -
liquid discharge manufacturing facilities and utilises in -house clean power for 100% of its 
electrical energy needs. Himadri is a people -first organisation that  is making a positive impact 
on the industry, both in India and globally, through its focus on innovation fuelled by extensive 
R&D, new business opportunities, and sustainability.
