From Rebrand to Reinvention: Coforge Marks Six Years of 
Transformation and Growth  
 
Coforge Day celebrations commence today across 47 global locations recognizing the collective contributions, 
resilience, and relentless drive of over 46,000 employees who power the firm’s success every day  
 
Princeton, NJ and Greater Noida, India, August 24, 2026  – Coforge Limited (NSE: COFORGE), a global 
AI-native engineering services leader, today celebrates six years of Coforge Day, a milestone that unites 
employees across 47 locations globally to commemorate the company’s rebranding and the remarkable growth, 
innovation, and transformation achi eved since embarking on its Coforge journey.  
 
What started as a rebrand six years ago has become a defining transformation story. Coforge has since scaled 
its global operations, deepened its engineering and industry expertise, and forged a distinctive identity built on 
hyper specialization, innovation -led growth, and an unwavering focus on execution intensity.  
 
“Six years ago, Coforge represented an ambition to build a differentiated technology services company. Since 
then, we have delivered sustained growth, evolved continuously, and remained relentlessly focused , with an  
execution intensity that is uniquely our own,” said Sudhir Singh, CEO & Executive Director , Coforge. “As we 
celebrate Coforge Day, we reflect on that journey and look ahead to helping clients move from AI adoption to 
enterprise autonomy. The future lies in embedding intelligence across workflows, decisions, and operations in 
a way that is secur e, scalable, and delivers measurable business outcomes.”  
 
“The strength of Coforge lies in its people,” said Pankaj Khanna, Chief People Officer, Coforge. “Coforge Day 
brings together colleagues across 47 locations in 33 countries to celebrate the shared purpose, innovation, and 
commitment that have powered our s uccess. Recognizing more than 1,200 award winners this year is a 
reflection of the talent, passion, and impact that our teams bring to clients and communities every day.”  
 
As it enters its next phase of growth, Coforge remains focused on helping clients navigate one of the most 
significant technology shifts of the modern era, bringing its AI -native engineering expertise to the challenge of 
operationalizing autonomy across th e enterprise.
