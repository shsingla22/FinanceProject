Thomas Cook India’s  Nature Trails expand s beyond Maharashtra  with 
its 4th resort  
Launches Nature Trails Rock Valley  at Ponda,  Goa  
 
Mumbai, January 8th 2026: As Young India increasingly seeks experience- driven holidays, travel that 
brings together nature, environment and adventure is becoming a marked preference. Aligned with this 
shift, Nature Trails —the experiential hospitality brand by Thomas Cook (India) Limited—has expanded its 
portfolio adding to its existing three resorts at Durshet, Sajan and Kundalika in Maharashtra,  with the launch 
of Nature Trails Rock Valley, marking the brand’s entry into Goa with a distinctive resort  focused on 
immersive outdoor  activities and  experiences.  
 
Located in Ponda and spread across a scenic valley at the foothills of the Western Ghats, the property extends across a sprawling 15 acres, featuring rooms close to nature, each exceeding 250 sq ft and 
complemented by private sit -out areas with views of the surrounding forests, river and open farmland—
offering guests an immersive connection with the outdoors.  Among the key highlights are two infinity pools 
- one overlooking the valley and the other a river, and an extensive integrated adventure zone.  
 
Guests can also engage in exciting adventure experiences such as zip lining, high and low rope courses, 
archery, target shooting, ATV rides, rappelling and kayaking, with safety assured through trained facilitators 
and professionally guided experiences, making the resort well -suited for individual families - especially those 
with children,  family & friends groups and business conferences/ corporate team- building engagements. 
Water -based experiences like kayaking and boating on the calm Khandepar backwaters  allow guests to 
explore the landscape at a slower, more immersive pace. The resort also features an in- house spice farm, 
offering guided walks that introduce guests to Goa’s spice heritage, biodiversity and local ecology.  
 
In addition, Nature Trails Rock Valley Goa offers experiential learning opportunities for school and college 
groups through curated, nature- based programs and activities designed and conducted by Nature Trails —
combining outdoor adventure, environmental awareness and hands -on learning.  
 
Stays at Nature Trails Rock Valley Goa are designed to be completely  immersive, with all -inclusive dining 
offers featuring Goan specialties  alongside other Indian and global cuisines, access to scenic walking trails, 
common recreation areas and dedicated spaces for children, besides being pet -friendly !  
It also features multi ple restaurants  & a conference room with a capacity of 120 guests  making it suitable 
for corporate teambuilding offsites and incentives.  
 Nature Trails Rock Valley is conveniently located for easy access –  just an hour’s drive from Dabolim 
Airport , a 40-minute  drive  from Madgaon Station and 45 minutes to  Colva beach – making it an ideal venue  
to cater to a  diverse range of travel requirements —from family holidays and friends’ getaways to corporate 
off-sites, team- building programs , day picnics and school /college adventure trips . 
 
Mr. Mahesh Iyer – Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, Thomas Cook (India) Limited  said, 
“Nature Trails is Thomas Cook’s  strategic investment  to tap into India’s booming domestic opportunity  by 
offering novel, experience- led travel offerings blending  nature, activity  and adventure rooted in authentic, 
local hospitality and flavor . Our expansion beyond Maharashtra into Goa was logical , given Goa’s strong 
year-round appeal, connectivity, varied landscapes and appeal across multiple traveler  segments . We plan 
to open more Nature Trails resorts at other exciting destinations soon –  so watch this space for more.”

About Nature Trails:  
Nature Trails by Thomas Cook (India) Limited, is an experiential outdoor hospitality brand focused on 
imme rsive, nature- led getaways. Nature Trails is focused on offering quality,  local, authentic activities that 
bring together nature- led experiences in a unique blend of  comfort, fun, adventure, learning and 
sustainability.  
Nature Trails operates destinations across Maharashtra’s Durshet, Kundalika and Sajan and Goa ’s Ponda, 
located amidst large naturally forested landscapes and riversides. These properties are well -suited for 
weekend escapes, educational camps, corporate offsites, group travel and family retreats.  
Nature Trails resorts specialise in offering safe outdoor experiences and a wide range of curated activities 
such as trekking, kayaking, zip- lining, high and low rope courses and guided nature trails  among others . 
Nature Trails also offers experiential learning and development programmes for youth and professionals .
