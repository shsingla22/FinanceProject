Datamatics Wins Prestigious UN Women  India, Women 
Empowerment Principles (WEPs) Award for Transparency & 
Reporting  
 
11th December 2024 , Mumbai:  Datamatics, a global Digital Technologies, Operations, 
and Experiences company , has been recognized as the WINNER  in the 'Transparency 
& Reporting ' category at the UN Women India 2024 Women Empowerment Principles 
(WEPs) Awards India . This r einforc es Datamatics'  commitment to advancing  diversity, 
equity, and inclusion (DEI).  
The UN Women WEPs Awards 2024  recognize Enterprises' commitment  to gender 
equality, transparency, and impactful practices. Evaluated on criteria such as 
transparency in reporting , measurable impact , and alignment with the Women's  
Empowerment Principles , the awards honor businesses, nonprofits, and public sector 
entities that lead in advancing women's  empowerment and fostering diversity and 
inclusion.  
In 2020, Datamatics introduced its Diversity Char ter, taking significant strides to 
strengthen its Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) agenda. The initiative emphasizes 
not just gender diversity but also generation, qualification, culture, experience, ability, 
thoughts, and opinions.  With a four-prong ed approach —Attract & Hire, Engage, 
Encourage, and Retain — Data matics has built a robust framework to create inclusive 
workplaces. Guided by the mantra 'Unify to Amplify, ' the Leadership team continues 
championing  initiatives that challenge biases and driv e meaningful progress toward a 
more equitable and diverse world.  
While accepting this award, Dr. Rima Ghose Chowdhury, President & CHRO; 
Datamatics expressed , "At Datamatics, we firmly believe that when we empower 
women, a whole nation progresses. Winning the UN Women India 2024 Women 
Empowerment Principles (WEPs) Award  for Transparency & Reporting  underscores our 
continued commitment to fostering a diverse, inclusive, and balanced workforce. We 
have made intentional efforts to build a dynamic team and ensure one of the best 
gender  ratios in the industry, and this recognition validates the progress we have 
made. We would like to extend our heartfelt thanks to the UN Women team for this 
prestigious recognition. " 
Rahul Kanodia, Vice Chairman and CEO, D atamatics mentioned,  "At Datamatics, 
we value diversity as a key driver of growth and ingenuity . Our commitment to building 
 
an inclusive and gender -equal workplace has guided our efforts to create a supportive 
environment across our global operations. Receiving the UN Women India 2024 
Wom en Empowerment Principles  Award for Transparency & Reporting underscores 
our dedication to promoting inclusivity and advancing gender equality."  
 
About UN Women 2024 Women Empowerment Principles (WEPs)  
The UN Women 2024  Women Empowerment Principles (WEPs)  is a global framework 
that encourages businesses to align their operations with gender equality, 
empowerment, and diversity. The WEPs recognize Enterprises  for their efforts in 
fostering inclusive workplaces, promoting gender equality, and advancing women's 
rights across all sectors.
