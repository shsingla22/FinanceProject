GAIL reports Revenue from Operations of ₹45,924 crore and  
PAT of ₹4,650 crore for Q2 FY2026 -27 
  
New Delhi, 30th October 2026 : GAIL (India) Limited today announced its financial results for 
the second quarter of financial year 2026 -27. 
 
Financial Performance (Standalone)  
 
GAIL (India) Limited reported Revenue from Operations of ₹ 84,905 crore for the period April 
– September 2026 as compared to ₹69,824 crore in corresponding period of Financial Year 
2025 -26. Profit Before Tax (PBT) for H1 FY27 stood at ₹11,611 crore as comp ared to ₹5,357 
crore for the corresponding period in previous year. Profit After Tax (PAT) stood at ₹8,943 
crore in H1 FY27 as compared to ₹4,104 crore in corresponding period of previous year.  
 
Revenue from Operations was reported at ₹45,924 crore in Q2 FY27 as against ₹ 38,981 crore 
in Q1 FY27. PBT stood at ₹5,838 crore in Q2 FY27 as against ₹5,773 crore during Q1 FY27. 
PAT stood at ₹4,650 crore in Q2 FY27 as against ₹4,292 crore in Q1 FY27 . 
 
The quarter reflected resilience in transmission and Natural Gas marketing performance. 
Despite geopolitical headwinds, the Company saw robust volumes across all major segments.  
 
Financial Performance (Consolidated)  
 
On consolidated basis, for H1 FY27, Revenue from Operations stood at ₹88,651 crore, 
compared to ₹71,086 crore in H1 FY26. PBT stood at ₹12,368 crore, compared to ₹5,594 crore 
in corresponding period of previous year. PAT (excluding minority interest) stood  at ₹9,385 
crore, as against ₹4,342 crore in H1 FY26.  
 
Capital Expenditure  
 
The Company recorded cumulative capex during H1 FY27 at ₹7,600 crore, which includes 
₹1,425 crore incurred in Q2 FY27 against the Annual Planned Capex of approximately ₹12,900 
crore in line with its long -term growth strategy.  
 
Operational Highlights (Q2 FY27 vs Q1 FY27):  
 
• Natural Gas Transmission : 130.10 MMSCMD vs. 122.36 MMSCMD  
• Gas Marketing Volume : 97.23 MMSCMD vs. 93.82 MMSCMD  
• LHC Production : 256 TMT vs. 232 TMT  
• Polymer Production : 204 TMT vs. 51 TMT  
• LPG Transmission : 1,130 TMT vs. 1,077 TMT
