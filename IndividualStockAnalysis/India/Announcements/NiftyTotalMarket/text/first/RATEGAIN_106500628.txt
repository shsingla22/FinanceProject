RateGain  Enhances Global People & Culture Leadership to Enable Next -Phase Growth  
 
India, 19th January 2026: RateGain  Travel Technologies Limited (NSE: RATEGAIN), a global leader of AI -
powered SaaS solutions for the travel and hospitality industry, today announced the appointment of Ashu Sharma 
as Senior Vice President, People & Culture (APMEA), Nathan Sheranian as Vice President, People & Culture (Americas), 
and Bilal Ahmed as Vice President – Talent Acquisition. These strategic additions to the glo bal People & Culture 
leadership team strengthen RateGain’s leadership depth across regions as the company continues to scale  its global 
operations.  
 
Rooted in its core values of People First, Innovation, Teamwork, Ownership Mindset, and Customer Obsession, 
RateGain invests in leaders who enable teams to collaborate, innovate, and do their best work while building solutions 
for the future of travel and hospitality.  
 
The newly appointed leaders, reporting to the Chief Human Resources Officer, include:  
 
• Ashu Sharma , an accomplished HR leader with experience at Adobe and Vodafone, will oversee Corporate 
HR, People Business Partnering across APMEA (including the corporate office), and Workplace Experience, 
bringing greater consistency, governance, and scale to RateGai n’s people practices.  
• Nathan Sheranian , who brings over two decades of global HR leadership experience across organizations 
including GE, Cisco, Freshworks, and Marigold, will lead People & Culture across the Americas, with a focus on 
leadership development, learning initiatives, and advancing  diversity and inclusion.  
• Bilal Ahmed , a seasoned talent acquisition leader with global experience across India, Indonesia, Singapore, 
and Hong Kong, will lead RateGain’s Talent Acquisition function. He brings deep expertise across in -house 
hiring, executive search, and RPO, with prior experi ence at Korn Ferry, GoTo Group (Gojek), Ambition and 
Hudson, supporting hiring at scale across diverse and multicultural environments.  
 
“As we begin this new year, we’re excited to welcome Ashu, Nathan and Bilal to RateGain,” said  Sahil Sharma, Chief 
Human Resources Officer, RateGain . “Their expertise, leadership, and shared philosophy of empathy and integrity 
help us build a workplace where every individual can thrive. Together, we’re strengthening our foundation for the 
future, one built on people, purpose, and progress.”  
 
These appointments underscore RateGain’s continued focus on building a globally connected, inclusive, and purpose -
driven organization powered by its people.
