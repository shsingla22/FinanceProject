Any breach in data security could result in severe consequences, including financial loss, reputational damage, legal implications, and loss of trust from stakeholders.To address the risk of privacy and data security, CDSL can implement several measures: 
Robust Information Security 
Infrastructure: CDSL invests in advanced

Expert Manpower: CDSL employs a team 
of skilled professionals with expertise in information security to continuously monitor and respond to potential threats. 
These experts should be well-versed in the

Training and Awareness: Conducting
