Aurionpro continues expansion in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia with 
another significant win  
Signs deal with a leading bank to deliver its innovative iCashpro + trans action 
banking platform  
Mumbai, India, December 11, 2024  – Aurionpro Solutions Limited (BSE: 532668) (NSE: 
AURIONPRO) is pleased to announce  an order win with one of the largest banks in the 
Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA) to modernize their corporate banking technology using 
Aurionpro’s global transaction banking platform “iCashpro+”. This collaboration aims to 
streamline the bank's operations and elevate their digital offerings, thereby driving 
innovation and strengthening customer relationships.  
Aurionpro's cutting -edge technology continues to set the benchmark for digital 
transformation in global transaction banking, earning the trust of leading banks globally. This 
second major win in the KSA  this year underscores Aurionpro's exceptional expertise and 
commitment to innovation, further strengthening its presence in the region.  
This partnership aligns well with the KSA's Vision 2030, advancing the nation's goals of 
economic diversification and d igital innovation. By leveraging Aurionpro's technology, the 
bank will empower its corporate customers with a seamless and efficient banking experience.  
Aurionpro's iCashpro+ solution offers a comprehensive suite of features for end -to-end 
transaction bank ing, leveraging advanced digital capabilities to enable an enhanced customer 
experience. The platform:  
• Enables speedy launches of new products and services through a flexible architecture  
• Incorporates AI -enabled automation to significantly improve cus tomer service levels.  
• Allows seamless integration with existing systems, while its intuitive user interface  ensures 
a superior customer experience.  
This partnership marks a significant milestone for Aurionpro as it expands its reach into the 
Middle Eas t and North Africa region, demonstrating the Company's commitment to providing 
innovative solutions that empower financial institutions in the digital age.  
Commenting on this latest win, Sandeep Chiber, EVP Sales at Aurionpro, expressed his 
enthusiasm:  "We  are thrilled to partner with this prestigious bank in the KSA, reinforcing our 
intent to become a key player in the region’s financial technology landscape. Our cutting -edge 
transaction banking platform, iCashpro+ delivers the most advanced functionality available 
today, including AI -driven automation, thereby enabling banks to offer a superior experience 
to their customers."  
He added:  “Aurionpro stands at the forefront of innovation, where software, products, 
platforms, and hardware converge to deliver co mprehensive solutions. This distinction sets 
us apart as a global leader in products and platforms, with a singular mission to empower our 
customers with end -to-end excellence.”
