Dear Sir / Madam,  
 
This is in continuation of the Company’s prior disclosure dated February 17 , 2026 under Regulation 
30 of the Listing Regulations , regarding update on the ongoing litigation between Honasa Consumer 
Limited (“ Company ”) and RSM General Trading LLC (“ RSM ”) in the Dubai Court s.  We hereby inform  
that the Cassation Court , Dubai, UAE (“ Cassation Court ”) has passed a judgment on July 29 , 2026  
(“Cassation Court  Judgement ”), translated copy of which  was received by the Company on August 
07, 2026 at 16:35  pm (IST).  
 
Vide  the said Judgment, the C assation Court has dismissed the Appeals filed by both Parties. The 
Cassation Court has  upheld the judgment issued by the Court of Appeal s on February 11, 2026 , vide 
which,  it awarded  an amount of  AED 1,707,407.06 (INR 4,43,12,369/ -) to RSM as compensation for 
material and moral damages. We find it imperative to mention here that RSM in its appeal had 
sought damages to the tune of AED 45,000,000 , which has categorically been rejected by the 
Cassation Court.  
 
We also find it imperative to clarify here that the Arbitral Tribunal as appointed by the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court of India (“Indian Arbitral Tribunal ”), passed  an Arbitral Award dated May 14, 2025 
as amended by Section 33 Order dated June 26, 2026 (“ Arbitral Award ”) in favour of the Company . 
The Arbitral Award declared that (i) RSM is permanently injuncted from initiating/continuing any 
proceedings against the Company before the Dubai Courts , and (i i) RSM shall pay to the Company  
damages aggregating to any amount sought to be recovered from the Company pursuant to the 
decision of any Dubai Court in proceedings related to the termination of the A greement , including 
but not limited to the judgment dated 11.02.2026 in the Dubai proceedings, or any appellate or 
revisional order arising therefrom . In view of the above reliefs granted by the Arbitral Tribunal  
vide  the Arbitral Award , the Company expects no financial impact . 
 
Further,  under the governing provisions of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908 , the Cassation Court 
Judgment may not be conclusive and binding upon the Company as the governing law of the 
Agreement between the Parties is Indian law and the disputes between the Parties are subject to 
arbitration or exclusive jurisdiction of New Delhi courts .
