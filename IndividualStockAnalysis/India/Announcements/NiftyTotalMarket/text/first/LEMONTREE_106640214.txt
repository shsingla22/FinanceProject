LEMON TREE HOTELS SIGNS LEMON TREE HOTEL, KUMBAKONAM  IN 
TAMIL NADU  
Strengthens South India presence with a premium hospitality offering in Tamil Nadu’s 
historic temple town  
New Delhi, May 25, 2026 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, one of India’s leading hospitality 
companies, today announced the signing of Lemon Tree Hotel, Kumbakonam  in Tamil 
Nadu.  The hotel will be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
With this signing, the company further strengthens its presence in Tamil Nadu and expands 
its footprint across key cultural, tourism, and commercial destinations in South India. This 
milestone addition brings the group’s footprint in the state to eight hot els, comprising six 
operational and two upcoming properties, including this hotel.  
Kumbakonam, widely recognized as one of South India’s prominent heritage and spiritual 
destinations, continues to witness strong demand from pilgrims, cultural travellers, and 
business visitors alike. Growing transit movement and increasing regional connec tivity 
have further accelerated the need for quality branded hospitality in the city.  
The hotel will feature 66 well-appointed rooms  designed to deliver a modern, deeply 
comfortable stay. The property will also house a curated range of facilities including a 
multi-cuisine restaurant, versatile banquet spaces, a business meeting room, a refreshing 
swimming pool, a rejuvenating spa, and a modern fitness center.  
Commenting on the signing, Mr. Neelendra Singh , Managing Director , Lemon Tree 
Hotels Ltd. , said:  “Expanding our presence across India's iconic spiritual destinations is 
a strategic focus for us, and Kumbakonam - the Temple Town of Tamil Nadu fits perfectly 
into this. It’s a bustling heritage city with an immense, steady flow of pilgrims and cultural 
travellers, closely linked to key neighbours like Thanjavur and Trichy. Signing Lemon Tree 
Hotel, Kumbakonam presents a significant opportunity to serve this thriving market with 
the trusted hospitality that is synonymous wit h the Lemon Tree brand. ” 
The property is located approximately 101 km from Tiruchirappalli (Trichy) International 
Airport, offering an accessible drive of under two hours. For rail commuters, the hotel is 
situated just 300 meters from the Kumbakonam Railway Station, ensuring a hig hly 
convenient 5 -minute transit for arriving guests.  
This milestone further aligns with Lemon Tree Hotels' roadmap  of expanding across high -
potential Tier II and Tier III markets, anchoring its corporate pipeline along crucial 
economic, spiritual, and transit corridors across India.  
 
 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India’s leading hospitality companies, catering 
to a wide range of customers – from value -conscious travellers to premium business and 
leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels & Resorts, Lemon T ree Premier, 
Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys Select, and Keys Lite – the group offers 
experiences across upper upscale, upscale, upper midscale, midscale, leisure, wildlife, and 
spiritual segments.  
LTHL operates 130+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing pipeline 
of 130+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and 
Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, and Indore – and with an 
international presence in Dubai, Bhutan, and Nepal – Lemon Tree Hotels delivers 
exceptional comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, refreshing experience.  
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 250+ properties 
(operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for both business and 
leisure travellers.
