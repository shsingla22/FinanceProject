PRESS RELEASE  
Blue Star appoints Mohit Sud as  
Group President, Unitary Cooling Products  
 
Blue Star  Limited  has announced the appointment of Mohit Sud (Mohit) 
as Group President, Unitary Cooling Products with effect from March 31, 
2025. Unitary Cooling Products is a fast-growing  segment,  and it 
includes room air conditioners and commercial refrigeration products 
such as deep freezers, water coolers, visi coolers and modular cold 
rooms.  Mohit will hold the end -to-end responsibility including sales, 
marketing, service, R&D, manufacturing,  and supply chain. He will also be  
part of the Group  Management Committee of the Company.   
Mohit  is a Mechanical Engineer with an MBA from XLRI, Jamshedpur. He 
has over two decades of experience in Hindustan Unilever, where he has 
successfully managed  various Sales & Marketing roles for several  product 
categories  including  Home  Care and Beauty & Well Being  in many 
geographies across India. In his last assignment as Vice President, he was 

responsible for driving the Premium Retail distribution for the Beauty & 
Well Being business.   
B Thiagarajan, Managing Director, Blue Star Limited, states, “The  market 
for room air conditioners and commercial refrigeration products is poised 
for exponential growth in the coming years , and we aim to further build 
on our leadership position and grow our market share. Mohit’s rich 
experience in sales, distribution and marketing will be extremely 
valuable.’ ’ 
Vir S Ad vani, Chairman & Managing Director, Blue Star Limited, adds, 
“The Company has been on a growth path, and I am very optimistic about 
our prospects. To be future -ready, we have been making significant 
investments in R&D, manufacturing, digitalisation, sales & marketing . 
Simultaneously, we are strengthening our leadership team with external 
and internal talent. Mohit  is an important addition to our team as we 
scale our consumer -facing businesses. ”
