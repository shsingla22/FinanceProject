Brief Profile of Shri Satya Gopal, IAS (Retd.)  
Shri Satya Gopal, a retired officer of the Indian Administrative Service (IAS) from the 1988 batch 
of the AGMUT cadre, has had a distinguished career spanning over three and a half decades in 
public administration. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Economics  (Hons.) from St. Stephen’s 
College, University of Delhi, a Master’s degree in Economics from the Delhi School of Economics, 
and an MBA in Financial Management from Lalit Narayan Mishra Institute of Economic 
Development and Social Change, Patna.  
Key Leadership Roles and Contributions:  
Chairman, Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA), Punjab (Dec 2022 – Feb 2024)  
 
• Led regulatory oversight for Punjab’s real estate sector, ensuring transparency and 
accountability.  
 
Additional Chief Secretary to Chief Minister, Govt. of NCT of Delhi (Jan 2022 – July 2022)  
 
• Oversees policy coordination across multiple government departments under the Chief 
Minister’s leadership.  
 
• Supports governance reforms, strategic planning, and administrative efficiency.  
 
Additional Chief Secretary (Power) & CMD of Delhi’s Power Entities, Govt. of NCT of Delhi 
(March 2021 – July 2022)

networks.

power utilities.  
 
• Expanded renewable energy adoption, increasing its share in Delhi’s electricity 
consumption from 15% to over 21%.  
 
• Led Delhi’s EV infrastructure expansion, installing 176 EV public charging stations, with

• Introduced energy efficiency measures, saving ₹697 crores annually for consumers.
