Firstsource awarded  Top 1%  of S&P Global  CSA Score for ESG  
Included in S&P Global Sustainability Yearbook for Third Consecutive Year  
 
Mumbai, India | March  05, 2026: Firstsource  Solutions Limited  (NSE:FSL, BSE:532809), a leading global provider of business 
process management services and an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company,  has been recognized as the “Top 1% in the S&P Global 
Corporate Sustainability Assessment (CSA)  Score”  for 2026 . The recognition  also comes with an inclusion in the S&P Global 
Sustainability Yearbook , for the third consecutive year . 
 
The S&P Global CSA Score and Sustainability Yearbook serve as a trusted benchmark for corporate sustainability , assessing 
companies across environmental, social, and governance dimensions.  With over  9,200 companies assessed , only 848 achieved 
Yearbook membership.   
 
Firstsource’s Top 1% placement reflects the company’s sustained progress in purpose -led, sustainable growth and its standing 
among top -performing companies across industries worldwide . Moreover, its inclusion underscores its leadership in the 
'Professional Services' category, with an S&P Global Sustainable1 ESG Score and CSA Score of 87  each , placing it in the top 99th 
percentile.  
 
Dr. Sanjiv Goenka, Chairman of RPSG Group and Firstsource, shared,  “Being considered a  leader in corporate sustainability this 
early in our ESG journey is a testament  to the dedication of our people in building a sustainable business and long -term value 
creation. In an industry where ESG transformation often takes many years to institutionalize, we chose to embed climate 
discipline, governance strength, and social impa ct into the core of our operating model. Being placed among the top 1% globally 
affirms th at this integrated approach is working — and strengthens our resolve to build a business that is resilient, responsible, 
and future -ready .” 
 
Firstsource has accelerated its ESG journey  under FirstConscious, by achieving key milestones, including:  
• Environmental:  Disclosure of Scope 1, 2 , and 3 emissions with external assurance; 26% of operations powered by renewable 
energy sources ; commitment to Science Based Targets initiative (validation underway) and Net Zero by 2050; EV transition 
with a 50% fleet conversion target by 2027.  
• Social & Talent:  46% gender diversity; 11,000+ impact hires through inclusion programs to date; launch of INDEX 2.0 and an 
AI Coach for workforce transformation; sustained 95% client satisfaction.  
• Governance & ESG Intelligence:  Focused engagement to align external stakeholders on climate goals; rollout of Phase 1 of 
ESGFirst, a digital ESG reporting platform; and 85% of supplier spend evaluated against ESG metrics . 
 
For more details on the S&P Global Sustainability Yearbook 2026, visit: The Sustainability Yearbook - 2026 Rankings .
