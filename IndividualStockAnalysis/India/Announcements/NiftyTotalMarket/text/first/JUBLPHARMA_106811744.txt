PRESS RELEASE 
Noida, October 8, 2026 
 
Jubilant’s Radiopharma bu siness announces investment of CAD 94.5 million 
to double production capacity of its flagship product, RUBY-FILL® 
The Government of Canada will provide a financial support of  CAD 23.8 million to this project through the Strategic 
Response Fund’s innovation funding stream . 
 
Jubilant Draximage Inc., a subsidiary of company’s wholly owned subsidiary Jubilant Pharma Limited, today 
announced an investment of CAD 94.5 million to moderniz e and expand its Kirkland, Québec facility and increase 
production capacity across its radiopharmaceutical portfolio pipeline. Specifically, the investment is aimed to double 
the production capacity of its flagship  product RUBY-FILL®. The Government of Canada will provide a financial support 
of CAD 23.8 million to this project. 
The business continues to see very strong demand for the RUBY-FILL®, the customer base has doubled in the last 3 
years. This expansion will allow Jubilant to fulfil the elevated demand.  
 
Speaking on the announcement, Harsher Singh, CEO, Jubilant Radiopharma business said , “This investment 
recognizes Jubilant Radiopharma’s innovation capabilities in Nuclear Medicine and the important role our sector plays in advancing both diagnostic and therapeutic treatments. This  project will build our deep capability here in Quebec, 
create and secure high-value jobs, and ensure a reliable supply of essential radiopharmaceutical products in Canada and around the world. ” 
Jubilant Radiopharma’s  portfolio spans both diagnostic and therapeutic  solutions that support precision medicine 
across cardiology, oncology, and nuclear medicine. Specifically, RUBY-FILL®  enables the on-site production of 
Rubidium-82 for cardiac PET imaging, helping clinicians accurately assess coronary artery disease and improve patient 
care.  The Technetium-99m (99mTc)  diagnostic portfolio supports a broad range of SPECT imaging procedures. These 
products play a critical role in diseas e detection, diagnosis, staging, and treatm ent planning across multiple clinical 
specialties, including oncology, cardiology, and neurology. In the therapeutic space, our Iodine-131 products have 
long supported the treatment of thyroid disease, thyroi d cancer and neuroblastoma through targeted delivery of 
radiation to diseased tissue.  
The Kirkland facility is the only site in Canada producin g these product categories. The investment will support the 
modernization and expansion of the facility, including the installation of state-of-the-art production equipment, 
enabling Jubilant Radiopharma to double its manufacturing capacity to meet growing gl obal demand for RUBY- FILL®.  
 
About Jubilant Radiopharma  
 Jubilant Radiopharma is an industry-leading radiopharmaceutical business that comprises development, 
manufacturing and commercialization of products through its Radiopharmaceuticals business and distribution 
through its national network of Radiopharmacies. The division’s Radiopharmacies business is the second largest 
radiopharmacy network in the US with 45 pharmacies distributing nuclear medicine products to the largest national Group Purchasing Organisations (GPOs), regional health systems, stand-alone imaging centres, cardiologists and 
hospitals. Jubilant Radiopharma is focused on “Improving Lives through Nuclear Medicine” on a global scale.
