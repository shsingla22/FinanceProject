Annexure I 
Brief Profile of Mr. Krishnan Sridhar Seshadri  
 
Mr. Krishnan Sridhar Seshadri  is the Chief Risk Officer of the Bank since May 2017 and has over 40 years of 
banking experience, covering various aspects of branch banking including the role of branch manager involving 
origination and management of Loans and Advances, system administration, accounting and financial control, etc 
amongst other aspects. Prior to joining DCB  Bank as Financial Controller in 2009, he was with ICICI Bank, 
having joined it from almost its inception, and earlier with Syndicate Bank and State Bank of India.  
 
Mr. Seshadri completed the Master of Commerce (M. Com) Degree and is a Certified Associate of the Indian 
Institute of Bankers (CAIIB).
