Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in  
Pune  
 
New Delhi, 11th September  2024: Lemon Tree Hotels announced its latest 
signing – Lemon Tree Hotel, Pune. The property, which shall be managed by 
Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree 
Hotels Limited, is expected to open in FY 2026.  
 
Pune is one of the major cities of Maharashtra and is known for its rich history, 
cultural  heritage, and modern development. It is often called the "Oxford of the 
East" because of its numerous prestigious educational institutions, including 
Savitribai Phule Pune University, Fergusson College,  Symbiosis International 
University  and various engin eering and management colleges . Pune boasts a 
youthful, cosmopolitan atmosphere that blends traditional Maharashtrian and 
modern urban lifestyles. With a combination of history, education, industry, and 
culture, it is one of India’s most dynamic cities.  
 
Lemon Tree H otel, Pune will feature 1 17 well-appointed rooms, a restaurant, a 
bar, a meeting room, banquet facilities, a swimming pool and a spa. The Pune 
International Airport is about 21 kms from the property while Pune Railway 
Station is about 20  kms aw ay. The hotel is also well connected by roadways for 
both public and private transport.  
 
Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO — Managed & Franchise Business, Lemon Tree Hotels said 
on the occasion , “We are excited to share the expansion of our portfolio in Pune 
where we have f our existing properties across upscale to midscale brands. This 
addition will complement our existing twelve hotels and t hree upcoming 
properties in Maharashtra. ”
