Dear Sirs,  
 The Board of Directors of Kansai Nerolac  Paints Limited has, in its meeting held today i.e. on 
Tues day, 6th May, 2025, inter alia  approved the following:  
 
 
1. Annual General Meeting:  The 105th  Annual General Meeting of the Company (“AGM”) 
will be held on Monday , 30th  June, 2025. Notice of the same will be submitted separately, as 
and when the same is dispatched to the Shareholders of the Company.  
 
 
2. Book closure:  The Register of Members and Share Transfer books of the Company will 
remain closed from Tues day, 24th June , 2025 to Monday , 30th June , 2025 (both days 
inclusive), for the purpose of AGM and dividend.  
 
3. Date of p ayment of Dividend:  The Board has recommended dividend of 375% (Rs. 3.75 
per share) which includes special dividend of 125% (Rs. 1.25 per share) for the financial year 
ended 31st March, 2025. This is same as dividend paid for the financial year ended  
31st March, 2024;  Dividend, when declared, will be payable on or after Saturday , 5th July, 2025 to those 
Members whose names are registered in the Register of Members of the Company as on 
Monday , 23rd  June , 202 5 and to the Beneficiary holders as per the beneficiary list as on 
Monday , 23rd  June , 2025 provided by National Securities Depository Limited and Central 
Depository Services (India) Limited.
