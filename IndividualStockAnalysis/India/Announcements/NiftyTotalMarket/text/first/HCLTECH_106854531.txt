HCLTech and  PTC collaborate  to accelerate semiconductor product lifecycle 
management  
  
NOIDA, India, Nov. 10, 2026  – HCLTech , a leading global technology company, today 
launch ed a pre-configured semiconductor Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) solution 
built on the PTC Windchill® PLM platform to help organizations streamline product 
development, strengthen quality and compliance and reduce operational complexity.  
  
The solution incorporates best practices and accelerators across the semiconductor 
ecosystem. It establishes a unified digital thread by connect ing people, processes and data 
across the semiconductor lifecycle , from IP management, new product introduction  and 
supplier collaboration to quality and regulatory compliance. The solution integrates 
seamlessly with existing business, manufacturing and design systems for end -to-end 
visibility and traceability.  
 
“Our collaboration with HCLTech has continued to evolve as customers look for new ways to 
transform increasingly sophisticated product development environments,” said John Haller, 
General Manager of PLM, PTC. “By bringing PTC’s product lifecycle capabiliti es together with 
HCLTech’s deep industry and engineering expertise, we are enabling  customers  with  a 
stronger foundation to modernize how products are developed and managed at scale.”  
 
“As chip innovation accelerates and products become more sophisticated, engineering 
excellence is only part of the equation. The ability to turn innovation into products quickly 
and consistently is becoming a defining competitive advantage,” said Hari Sada rahalli, 
Corporate Vice President and Global Head, Engineering and R&D Services, HCLTech. “Our 
focus is on helping clients build that capability and create the foundation for sustained 
innovation and growth.”
