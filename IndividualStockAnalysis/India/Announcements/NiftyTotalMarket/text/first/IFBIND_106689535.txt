In 1974 he set up IFB's ﬁrst ﬁne blanking plant in Kolkata, bringing to India a 
precision manufacturing technology that was entirely new. It was a small 
beginning. But it was the best technology of its kind.
IFB's Home Appliances Division brought front-load washing machines to 
Indian homes. In a market accustomed to semi-automatic machines, this was another ﬁrst. And again, it was a product built on the best technology.
Over the decades, as IFB grew across appliances and engineering, his words served not as a slogan but as a blueprint. Growth was about delivering the best.
This year, three new things took shape.
Our Founder built this Company on the conviction that only the best was 
worth pursuing. Fifty years later, we are still making that choice, in every 
product, every plant, and every partnership. His vision lives on at IFB - not as a memory alone but as a responsibility carried forward.Chain & Sprockets

This deepens IFB's precision engineering roots, de-risks the existing 
aftermarket operation and opens the door to supplying OEM partners with chain and sprocket kits.Advanced Electronics Division

relationship with a big and important global customer and could lead to big opportunity for which work is ongoing. We are also working on the design of the complex part of the same customer - which if successful will give us good growth. SAAG is providing the necessary support in design of the said complex part.Schmid Automotive and Appliances GmbH (SAAG)
