SKF India (Industrial) Limited announces financial results for Q 2 FY2026 -27 
Pune,  November 04 2026 : SKF India (Industrial) Limited (NSE: SKFINDUS | BSE: 544572) , India’s leading 
technology and solutions provider of bearings and units, condition monitoring, and services,  today 
announced its financial results for the quarter ended September  30, 2026.  
During this quarter , SKF India (Industrial) Limited reported revenue from operations of Rs. 10,428.6  million 
with profit before tax at Rs. 1,343.7 million , demonstrating continued execution strength against a backdrop 
of healthy industrial demand and steadfast  operational efficiency .

MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY  
Sujeeth Pai , MD, SKF India (Industrial) Limited , said,   
“Our Q2 performance reflects strong industrial demand and disciplined execution of our strategy. Revenue 
grew 7.4% sequentially to Rs. 10,428.6 million, while PBT increased 54.6%, strengthening PBT margin to 
12.9%.  
Customer proximity, deeper localisation and application -led innovation remain our priorities for sustaining 
profitable growth. Looking ahead, our ambition is to enable a more reliable, efficient and competitive 
Indian industry , creating lasting value for customers and shareholders .  
 
India’s industrial and infrastructure story is in its early chapters , and SKF India (Industrial) is built to grow 
with it.”  
 
STRATEGIC OUTLOOK  
SKF India (Industrial)’s long -term direction is to align its manufacturing footprint and technology 
capabilities with the evolving needs of India’s industrial and infrastructure sectors. Its planned 
investments, including the ‘Factory of the Future’ in Pun e, anchor this approach, supporting the 
 
 
 company’s growth ambitions beyond the current financial year. As a focused industrial business, SKF aims 
to build a stronger foundation for sustained revenue growth and long -term margin expansion.  
 
About SKF  
Since 1907, SKF has been making some of the world’s most innovative bearings, seals, lubrication systems, condition 
monitoring solutions, and services to reduce friction. Less friction means more energy saved and by reducing it, we 
make industry smarter, m ore competitive, and more energy efficient, building a more sustainable future where we can 
all do more with less. SKF is represented in approximately 130 countries and has around 17,000 distributor locations 
worldwide. Annual sales in 2025 were SEK 91,583  million and the number of employees was 37,271.
