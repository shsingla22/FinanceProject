Media Release  
 
Adani Energy Solutions receives LOA for 2500 MW  
renewable round -the-clock power supply to WBSEDCL  
Ahmedabad,  21st November 2026: Adani Energy Solutions Ltd. (AESL) today said its wholly 
owned subsidiary Powerpulse Trading Solutions Ltd. (PTSL), has received a Letter of Award 
(LoA) from West Bengal State Electricity Distribution Co. Ltd. (WBSEDCL) for supply of 1,750 
MW of Renewable Energy Round -the-Clock (RE -RTC) power for a period of 25 years . The LoA 
provides a 750 MW greenshoe option, which upon exercising, would take the aggregate 
contracted capacity to 2,500 MW.  
The award follows a competitive bidding process in which PTSL emerged as the successful 
bidder after the e -reverse auction. Power Supply is scheduled to commence from 1st July 2028 
for 1750 MW capacity and from 1st Apr 2029 for 750 MW, once gree nshoe option is exercised.  
“Round -the-clock renewable power will be critical to India’s energy transition, combining the 
sustainability of renewables and optimising transmission systems usage. This significant order 
win strengthens our commitment to deliver scalable energy solutions , and allows us to partner 
with WBSEDCL in supporting West Bengal’s long -term sustinable  power needs. PTSL is rapidly 
emerging as a partner of choice for bulk power consumers, with the ability to structure 
bespoke and scalable solutions around their specific energy requirements." said Mr. Kandarp 
Patel, CEO, AESL.  
The order represents a significant addition to PTSL’s expanding portfolio of energy solutions. 
While it has tied -up ~4.6 GW of solar & wind, and ~3.5 GWh of BESS capacity on the sourcing 
side, it is steadily stitching the supply side by augmenting long term supply contracts with 
bulk consumers like industries, data centres, commercial entities, and Discoms.  
About Adani Energy Solutions Ltd (AESL)  
AESL, part of the Adani portfolio, is a multidimensional organization with presence in various facets of 
the energy domain, namely power transmission, distribution, smart metering, and cooling solutions. 
AESL is the country’s largest private transmission c ompany with a cumulative transmission network of 
30,683 ckm and 146,585  MVA transformation capacity. In its retail electricity distribution business, as 
on AESL serves approximately 13 million consumers in metropolitan Mumbai and the industrial hub of 
Mundra SEZ. AESL is ramping up its smart metering business and is on course  to become India’s leading 
smart metering integrator. AESL, with its integrated offering through the expansion of its distribution 
network through parallel licenses and competitive and tailored retail solutions, including a significant 
share of green power , is revolutionizing the way energy is delivered to the end consumer. AESL is a 
catalyst for transforming the energy landscape in the most reliable, affordable, and sustainable way.
