PRESS RELEASE Date: 18-11-2026 
Karnatak.a Bank Appoints Seasoned Banker Mr. Udaya Kumar Hebbar as Managing 
Director & CEO 
Karnataka Bank, a leading private sector bank with a legacy of over a century, is 
pleased to announce the appointment of Mr. Udaya Kumar Hebbar as its new 
Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer (MD & CEO). 
Mr. Hebbar has been appointed as an Additional Director to take up the role of Whole­
Time Director in the category of Managing Director & CEO for a period of three years, 
with effect from the date of assuming charge. The appointment is subject to the 
approval of shareholders, as per the Listing Regulations and the applicable provisions 
of the Companies Act, 2013. 
Karnataka Bank is confident that his extensive experience, strategic vision and proven 
leadership will further accelerate the Bank's growth journey and reinforce its position in 
the Indian banking sector. 
Mr. Udaya Kumar Hebbar brings with him over four decades of experience in Banks 
and NBFCs, with expertise spanning banking operations, commercial banking, rural 
and agricultural finance, micro-banking, corporate governance, risk management. 
Prior to joining Kamataka Bank, he served as the Chief Executive Officer of 
CreditAccess Grameen Limited from September 2010 to June 2015 and subsequently as 
its Managing Director & CEO from June 2015 to June 2025. Following his executive 
tenure, he continued on the Board as a Non-Executive Director. He has also held several 
1 
board-level positions, including at CreditAccess Life Insurance Limited and 
CreditAccess India, BV, Amsterdam. 
Earlier in his career, he held key leadership and operational positions at prominent 
• banking institutions, including Barclays Bank PLC, ICICI Bank Limited and 
Corporation Bank, gaining extensive experience across diverse banking functions. 
Mr. Pradeep Kumar P, Chairman of the Bank said: 
"The appointment of Mr. Udaya Kumar Hebbar marks an important milestone in 
Karnataka Bank's journey towards its next phase of growth and transformation. His rich 
banking experience, strategic understanding and proven leadership will steer the Bank 
towards greater heights. As we build on our century-old legacy of trust and resilience, 
we look forward to strengthening our market position, embracing new opportunities 
and creating sustainable value for all our stakeholders. We are confident that, with his 
leadership and the collective commitment of our team, Karnataka Bank will scale new 
milestones in the years ahead." 
Smt. Biji S S, Managing Director & CEO on interim basis said: 
"We are delighted to welcome Mr. Udaya Kumar Hebbar to Karnataka Bank. His rich 
experience, deep understanding of the banking sector and proven leadership 
capabilities will be invaluable as we continue to strengthen the Bank's foundations and 
pursue our growth aspirations. We look forward to working together as one team to 
create greater value for our customers, stakeholders and shareholders.''
