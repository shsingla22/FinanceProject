WAAREE RENEWABLE TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED 
 
Delivers 36.53% revenue growth YoY; maintains positive EBITDA trajectory  
 
Revenue from operations for Q2 FY27 stood at Rs. 1,057.84 crores a growth of 36.53% YoY 
Q2 FY26 PAT stood at Rs. 81.48 crores  
Strong unexecuted orderbook of Rs. 5,500+ crores sustains revenue visibility 
 
October 19, 2026, Mumbai:  Waaree Renewable Technologies Limited (WRTL), the EPC arm of the 
Waaree Group, stands among the leading players in the EPC and T&D space. Beyond its core renewable 
EPC business, WRTL has been expanding its capabilities across adjacent segments. Further the 
acquisition of Associated Power Structures Pvt. Ltd. (APSPL) strengthens the company's presence in the 
transmission and distribution (T&D) space. Company is also actively pursuing EPC opportunities in 
Battery Energy Storage Systems and Data Centre. These steps reflect WRTL's effort to build a broader 
and more integrated presence across the clean energy value chain. It has announced its unaudited 
financial results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026.

 Commenting on the results Mr. Manmohan Sharma, CFO, Waaree Renewable Technologies 
Limited said:   
 
“We are pleased to report yet another quarter of healthy performance, with revenue from 
operations for Q2 FY27 standing at ₹1,057.84 crores, reflecting a growth of 36.53% year-on-year. 
For H1 FY27, revenue from operations stood at ₹1,982.09 crores, registering a growth of 43.84% 
over the corresponding period last year. The growth during the quarter was driven by steady 
progress across our project portfolio, timely execution, and our expanding capabilities across the 
renewable energy value chain and T&D segment. This performance was strengthened by our 
strategic acquisition of APSPL which consolidated our position across T&D infrastructure value 
chain.” 
 
“While acquisition-related financing costs and the addition of business with a different margin 
profile have influenced in near-term profitability, our focus remains firmly on creating long-term 
value through greater scale, diversified capabilities and operational efficiencies. We believe this 
expansion provides a strong foundation for the next phase of WRTL’s growth journey” 
 
“The government’s policy support is also getting stronger. The recently approved Green Energy 
Corridor Phase-III targets the evacuation of up to 135 gigawatts of renewable energy, along with 50 
gigawatt-hours of battery energy storage, by FY2032-33. Alongside this, the Pradhan Mantri Surya 
Sarovar Yojana plans 5 gigawatt-hours of floating solar. Together, these highlight the government’s 
clear commitment to clean energy. These developments are expanding opportunities across 
renewable generation, storage and T&D infrastructure. These are the areas where we continue to 
strengthen our capabilities and market presence.” 
 
“Backed by a healthy unexecuted order book of Rs. 5,500+ crores, WRTL remains focused on driving 
operational efficiencies strengthening business integration and improving profitability across its 
expanded portfolio.” 
 
 
 
About Waaree Renewable Technologies Limited 
Waaree Renewable Technologies Limited (WRTL) is subsidiary company of Waaree Group and 
spearheading the Solar EPC business. We are also a solar developer that finances, constructs, owns and 
operates solar projects. Headquartered in Mumbai, we operate across geographies, focusing on long-
term investments within the commercial and industrial customer segments. This subsidiary was formed 
to tap into the growing renewable energy market. Waaree group is a leading renewable energy 
company that caters Individual, Industrial and Commercial customers by encouraging them to adapt 
energy solutions that helps in reducing the release of carbon. The company provides clean energy to its 
clients by setting up both on-site solar projects (rooftop and ground-mounted) and off-site solar farms 
(open access solar plants). The company has also taken measured steps to expand into adjacent 
segments of the T&D space. WRTL is additionally pursuing EPC opportunities in Battery Energy Storage 
Systems and data centre infrastructure
