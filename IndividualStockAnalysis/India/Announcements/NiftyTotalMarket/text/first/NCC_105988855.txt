Annexure-A 
 
Profile of Dr A S Durga Prasad (DIN:00911306) 
 
 
Dr A S Durga Prasad is a Fellow Member of the Institute of Cost Accountants of India and holds a PhD. 
He has four decades of expertise in Financial and Cost Management across various sectors, including 
Pharmaceuticals and IT.  
 
Dr. Durga Prasad has served as the President of the Institute of Cost Accountants of India (ICAI), also 
known as the Institute of Cost and Works Accountants of India (ICWAI). He was a Member of 
Company Law Committee, Ministry of Corporate Affairs.  
 
Dr. Durga Prasad was appointed as an Independent Director on the Board of NCC Limited for the 2nd  
w.e.f  23rd May 2021.
