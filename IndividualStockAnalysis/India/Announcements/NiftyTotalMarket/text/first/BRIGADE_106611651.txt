PRESS RELEASE  
Brigade Revenue Increases 11% to ₹ 5,909 Crore in FY26;  
EBITDA at ₹ 1,638 Crore  
Bengaluru, May 06, 2026:  Brigade Enterprises Ltd., reported real estate sales of 1.95  Mn sft with sales 
value of ₹ 2,521 Cr for Q4 FY26. The average realization stood at ₹ 12,915, an increase of 7% over Q4 FY 
2025. Brigade launched ~ 4 Mn sft across seven projects during the last quarter of FY26.  
Performance Highlights FY26  
• The Real Estate  business registered pre -sales of 6.13 Mn sft  translating to a value of  ₹ 7,424 Cr. 
The segment clocked a revenue of ₹ 4,002 Cr, witnessing a growth of 11% over FY25.  
• Leasing  witnessed an increase of 12% in revenue, growing from ₹ 1,165 Cr in FY25 to ₹ 1,303 
Cr in FY26. Brigade’s retail footfalls increased by 7% over the past fiscal year with 18% growth 
in retail sales consumption during FY26 over FY25.  
• The company’s Hospitality  portfolio occupancy stood at 76% for the financial year. ARR stood 
at ₹ 7,453, an increase of 11 % during FY26. Revenue grew to ₹ 604 Cr in FY26 from ₹ 536 Cr in 
FY25, an increase of 13%.  
• Total collections stood at ₹ 7,476 Cr during FY26 & the net cash flow from operations stood at 
₹ 1,411 Cr.  
Dividend  
The Board of Directors has recommended a final dividend of ₹ 2/- per equity share (20%) of  
₹ 10/- each for the Financial Year 2025 -26. 
Bonus Issue  
The Board of Directors has recommended a bonus issue of 1: 3 i.e. 1 (one) bonus equity share of Rs. 10/ - 
each fully paid up for every 3 (three) equity shares of Rs. 10/ - each fully paid up held by the 
shareholders as on record date subject to the approval of the members of the Company.  
Commenting on the company’s performance , Pavitra Shankar, Managing Director, Brigade 
Enterprises  Ltd.  Said “The Q4 of FY26 reflected the sales momentum we had been building towards. 
We recorded our strongest quarter of FY26, with pre -sales of ₹2,521 crore, a 44% sequential increase, 
driven by strong new -launch absorption and disciplined pricing. Average realisations improved 9% 
year -on-year, underscoring market confidence in Brigade’s product quality. Overall, Brigade enters 
FY27 with a strong pipeline, balanced portfolio and clear execution focus. To commemorate the 40th of 
Brigade, the Board has recommended a bonus issue of 1:3.”  
 
Financial Summary (Consolidated)  
Q4 FY 26 Performance compared with Q4 FY 25  
- Revenue Rs 1,523 Cr as compared to Rs 1,532 Cr  
- EBITDA Rs 430 Cr as compared to Rs 488 Cr  
- Reported PAT Rs 190 Cr as compared to Rs 249 Cr  
- EPS (Diluted) Rs. 5.94 as compared to Rs 10.32  
 
 
 
 
 
  
 
FY26 Performance compared with FY 25  
- Revenue Rs 5,909 Cr as compared to Rs 5,314 Cr  
- EBITDA Rs 1,638 Cr as compared to Rs 1,654 Cr  
- Reported PAT Rs 725 Cr as compared to Rs 680 Cr  
- EPS (Diluted) Rs. 26.33 as compared to Rs 28.68
