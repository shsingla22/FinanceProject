Subject: Order of Hon’ble Madras High Court Quashing Income Tax Demand of ₹3,567.22 lakh for AY 2016–17. 
 
We hereby inform you that with reference to the assessment order dated May 31, 2023, issued by the Deputy 
Commissioner of Income Tax - Central Circle 3(4), Chennai for the Assessment Year 2016-17, involving a demand of 
₹3,567.22 lakh.  
 
The Company had filed the writ petition before the Hon’ble Madras High Court challenging the same and on the 
grounds that the said assessment order was passed without providing reasonable opportunity of being heard as 
mandated u/s 144A of the Income Tax Act, 1961 and no appropriate approval obtained for issuing the notice. 
 
The Hon’ble Madras High Court, after considering the submissions, has set aside the aforesaid assessment order 
passed by the Assessing Officer. The order of the Hon’ble Madras High Court has been received by the Company today 
i.e. April 28, 2026. 
 
Consequently, the tax demand of approximately ₹3,567.22 lakh stands nullified.
