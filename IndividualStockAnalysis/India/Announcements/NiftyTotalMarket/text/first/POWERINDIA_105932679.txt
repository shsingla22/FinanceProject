July 2024 and the inward date for the shipment is 27th June

prescribed by DFGT. The delay of 13 days was due to difficulty

migrated to a new ERP system. Due to the said delay, the 
goods imported under said Bill of Entry are liable to 
confiscation under section 111(d) and the Company being the 
importer is liable to pay penalty under section 112(a) of the 
Customs Act, 1962.
