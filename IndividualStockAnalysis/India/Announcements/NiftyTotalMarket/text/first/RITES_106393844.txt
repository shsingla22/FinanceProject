 RITES Limited CIN: L74899DL1974GOI007227                            Press Release   RITES signs MoU with Etihad Rail to  further augment business collaboration   Gurugram, September 30, 2025: RITES Limited, the leading Transport Infrastructure Consultancy and Engineering firm, today signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Etihad Rail for business collaboration with its subsidiary National Infrastructure Construction Company (NICC) LLC, a UAE-based infrastructure construction company, to take forward further business collaboration with Etihad Rail in the mobility sector in the geographies of UAE and beyond.  
 An MoU-ceremony being held during the Global Rail Transport  Infrastructure Exhibition & Conference held in Abu Dhabi.  The MoU was signed by H.E. Shadi Malak, CEO of Etihad Rail, and Mr. Rahul Mithal, Chairman and Managing Director of RITES Ltd., in the presence of His Highness Sheikh Theyab bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Chairman of Etihad Rail, and Hon’ble Ambassador of India to the UAE H.E. Sunjay Sudhir during the Global Rail Transport Infrastructure Exhibition & Conference held in Abu Dhabi.  This partnership aims at combining RITES’ five decades of expertise in consultancy, transport infrastructure, and engineering solutions with the execution capabilities of NICC, thereby creating synergies for shaping infrastructure projects in the region. With this MoU, RITES strengthens its international footprint under its strategic initiative ‘RITES Videsh’ and reaffirms its ethos: ‘We Shape What Shapes Lives’.    About RITES Limited: RITES Limited, a Navratna Public Sector Enterprise, is a leading player in the transport consultancy and engineering sector in India, having diversified services and geographical reach. The company has an experience spanning more than 5 decades and undertaken projects in over 55 countries in Asia, Africa, South America, and Middle East region.  About NICC: Established in 2022 as a subsidiary of Etihad Rail, the National Infrastructure Construction Company (NICC) is an infrastructure construction company based in the UAE. Its expertise extends into the design and development of comprehensive infrastructure projects, including railway infrastructure, terminals, facilities, and depots.
