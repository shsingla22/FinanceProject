Institute of Chartered Accountants of India. The impact of the above change amounting to Rs. 960.70 Lakhs was disclosed as "Exceptional Item" in the Standalone Financial Results for the year ended 
March 31, 2026.

Limited ceases to be subsidiary of the Company.
