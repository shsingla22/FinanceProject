िहोदय  / िहोदया , 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, it is informed that after receipt of necessary consideration by REC Power 
Development and Consultancy Limited (“RECPDCL” - a wholly owned subsidiary of REC  
Limited ), the entire shareholding of WRNES Talegaon Power Transmission Limited, 
comprising of 50,000 equity shares, held by RECPDCL, has been transferred at par value 
along with all its assets and liabilities on May 30, 2025 to  the successful bidder selected 
through tariff based competitive bidding process.  
 
Hence, with effect from the aforesaid date, the project specific SPV/subsidiary is not the 
subsidiary of RECPDCL & REC  Limited . The other requisite details are attached  herewith 
as Annexure -1.
