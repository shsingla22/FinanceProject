Investor Release
Mumbai,  15th October  2026
Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Limited,  a leading  Mumbai -based  developer  of luxury  and ultra  
luxury  residential  and commercial  properties  in Mumbai,  with a strong  focus  in redevelopment  & 
joint  development  projects  announced  its unaudited  financial  results  for the quarter  and half year  
ended  30th September  2026 .
H1 FY27 has been  a defining  period  for Sri Lotus,  as the Company  expands  its footprint  across  
Mumbai’s  most  sought -after  micro -markets  while  maintaining  the financial  discipline  that 
distinguishes  it. Successful  project  launches,  new redevelopment  wins  and steady  demand  for 
premium  homes  have  together  driven  growth  alongside  industry -leading  profitability . With  a net 
debt -free balance  sheet  and a well-defined  launch  calendar  for H2, the Company  enters  the 
second  half with strong  momentum .
KEY  BUSINESS  & FINANCIAL  UPDATES
❖ Pre-sales  momentum : Q2 FY27 pre-sales  rose  83% YoY to INR 470 Crs, reflecting  sustained  
demand  for the Company’s  premium  offerings

Commenting  on the performance,  Mr. Anand  K Pandit,  Chairman  & Managing  Director,  Sri 
Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Limited  said,  
“I am delighted  to report  a strong  second  quarter,  which  completes  an outstanding  first half for 
Sri Lotus . Pre-sales  for Q2 FY27 grew  83% YoY to INR 470 Crs. For H1 FY27, pre-sales  stood  at 
INR 879 Crs, up 176% YoY and on track  to achieve  our full-year  guidance . This is a clear  
endorsement  of our approach  of pairing  premium  products  with Mumbai’s  most  sought -after  
micro -markets  and delivering  them  with discipline .
Our growth  has not come  at the cost  of profitability,  which  remains  a hallmark  of our model . In 
Q2, total  revenue  rose  53% YoY to INR 269 Crs, EBITDA  more  than  doubled  to INR 107 Crs at a 
margin  of 39.9%, and PAT grew  91% to INR 89 Crs.
We also made  significant  progress  on business  development  during  the quarter,  where  
company  was appointed  as preferred  developer  for three  new projects : Lotus  Supremus  (Juhu),  
Lotus  Empressa  (Shivaji  Park,  Dadar  West),  Lotus  Stellar  (Versova)  and additional  area  secured  
for Lotus  Insignia  which  together  adds  INR 2,700-2,800  Crs of GDV . 
GUIDANCE  FOR  FY27
These  additions  deepen  our presence  in some  of Mumbai’s  finest  neighborhoods  and give us 
meaningful  visibility  on growth . Redevelopment  and joint  development  remain  at the core  of our 
strategy,  and we will continue  to add quality  projects  to our pipeline ..
As we enter  the second  half, we are well prepared . Four  launches  are planned,  Lotus  Sky Plaza,  
Lotus  Portofino,  Lotus  Odyssey,  and Lotus  Aurelia  with a combined  estimated  GDV  of ~INR  
3,500 -4,000  Crs. Together  with the momentum  from  our recent  launches  and a growing  
pipeline,  this positions  us well for the months  ahead . We remain  confident  of delivering  our 
stated  guidance  for FY27 of a pre-sales  of INR 1,800 -2,000  Crs, and revenue  and PAT growth  of 
55-60% over  FY26 levels .
I thank  our customers,  joint  development  partners,  and the entire  Sri Lotus  team  for their  trust  
and commitment . We will continue  to build  with the same  focus  on design,  quality  and financial  
prudence,  and to create  lasting  value  for all our stakeholders .”

3About  Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Limited
Incorporated  in 2015  (formerly  AKP Holdings  Limited),  Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Ltd. is a Mumbai -based  real 
estate  developer  specializing  in luxury  and ultra -luxury  residential  and premium  commercial  projects,  with a strong  
focus  on redevelopment  in the western  suburbs  of Mumbai . The company  has completed  4 projects  aggregating  over  
4.2 lakh sq. ft. of carpet  area,  with 8 ongoing  and 16 upcoming  developments  covering  ~46.8 lakh sq. ft. of carpet  area  
and ~36.9 lakh sq. ft. of saleable  area .
With  a strong  presence  in Juhu  and Andheri  West . the company  has also expanded  into other  micro  markets  of 
Mumbai  like South  and Central  Mumbai,  Bandra,  and Versova . Lotus  Developers  manages  end-to-end execution —
from  design  and construction  to sales  and customer  engagement —ensuring  consistent  quality,  superior  materials,  
and customer  satisfaction . Guided  by its philosophy  of creating  inclusive  and high-quality  living  spaces,  the company  
is shaping  some  of the most  coveted  addresses  in Mumbai’s  real estate  landscape .
