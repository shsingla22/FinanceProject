U.S. Polo Assn. Sets Sights on ₹4,000 Crore in Consumer Sales in India in FY27 
The brand records high double digit teens growth,, with retail expansion and category diversification 
contributing to its India business 
Bengaluru, November 19, 2026:  U.S. Polo Assn., the official sports brand of the United States Polo 
Association (USPA), continues to strengthen its position in the Indian apparel market. The brand recorded 
high-teens growth, reflecting its strong consumer connect and established presence in the country’s 
casualwear market. 
India is the brand's third-largest market globally, following the United States and Turkey, and its fastest-
growing market. Through its long-standing partnership with Arvind Fashions Limited, U.S. Polo Assn. has 
built a significant presence in the Indian apparel industry, supported by its strong brand positioning, 
extensive retail network and established consumer base. 
Globally, U.S. Polo Assn. recorded retail sales of US$2.7 billion in 2025, with a presence across 190 
countries, approximately 1,200 branded retail stores and thousands of additional distribution points 
worldwide. India contributes approximately 20% to the brand's global sales, underlining its significance 
within the international business. 
Commenting on the brand's performance and presence in India, J. Michael Prince, President, CEO & 
Board Member, USPA Global , said, "India has established itself as one of the most important markets in 
U.S. Polo Assn.'s global business, driven by the strong connection consumers have developed with the 
brand. Our partnership with Arvind Fashions Limited has played an important role in translating U.S. Polo 
Assn.'s global identity and heritage into a proposition that resonates with Indian consumers. The brand's 
performance in India reflects the strength of this partnership, its relevance across consumer segments 
and the continued demand for quality, versatile apparel." 
In India, U.S. Polo Assn. has established a strong presence in the menswear segment, where it is 
recognised as one of the country's leading sports-casual brands. Its portfolio spans menswear, 
womenswear, kidswear, footwear, accessories, and underwear, catering to a diverse consumer base. The 
brand's product proposition combines comfort, versatility, quality and contemporary styling, while 
remaining rooted in its association with the sport of polo. 
 

The brand's distribution strategy is supported by an integrated retail and digital ecosystem, with a 
presence across exclusive brand stores, multi-brand retail, department stores and online channels. This 
omnichannel approach enables U.S. Polo Assn. to engage with consumers across multiple shopping 
touchpoints. 
The brand's performance also aligns with Arvind Fashions Limited's broader focus on strengthening its 
direct-to-consumer ecosystem. Direct channels currently account for 53% of Arvind Fashions' overall 
sales, with the company targeting an increase in this contribution to 65% over the long term .  
Sharing his perspective on the brand's India journey, Mr. Kulin Lalbhai, Vice Chairman and Non-Executive 
Director, Arvind Fashions Limited , said, "U.S. Polo Assn. has built a strong and enduring connection with 
Indian consumers over the years, supported by its distinctive brand positioning and ability to remain 
relevant across generations. Our partnership has been instrumental in translating the brand's global 
identity into a proposition that resonates with the Indian market. The brand's performance reflects the 
strength of its consumer franchise, distribution network and product portfolio. Its established presence 
across categories and channels has been central to building its scale in India." 
U.S. Polo Assn.'s India business reflects the strength of its global brand heritage, combined with its 
understanding of local consumer preferences. With an established retail footprint, a diversified product 
portfolio and a strong omnichannel presence, the brand continues to be a significant part of India's 
casualwear market. The brand has also been strengthening its marketing and consumer engagement 
efforts through strategic brand associations, high-impact campaigns, collaborations and initiatives 
featuring Global Brand Ambassador HH Maharaja Sawai Padmanabh Singh (Pacho), reinforcing its 
connection with the sport of polo while building relevance among India's evolving consumer base. 
About U.S. Polo Assn. and USPA Global  
U.S. Polo Assn.  is the official sports brand of the United States Polo Association (USPA),  the largest 
association of polo clubs and polo players in the United States, founded in 1890. With a multi-billion-dollar 
global footprint and worldwide distribution through more than 1,200 U.S. Polo Assn. retail stores as well 
as thousands of additional points of distribution, U.S. Polo Assn. offers apparel, accessories, and footwear 
for men, women, and children in more than 190 countries worldwide. U.S. Polo Assn.’s Global Polo Shirt 
Campaign, An Icon Born from the Game, is a powerful tribute to the iconic polo shirt’s authentic sports 
origins and its evolution into one of the world’s most enduring style essentials.  
The global sports brand sponsors major polo events around the world, including the U.S. Open Polo 
Championship®, held annually at the USPA National Polo Center in The Palm Beaches, the premier polo 
tournament in the United States. Historic deals with ESPN in the United States, TNT and Eurosport in 
Europe, Star Sports  in India, and BeIN Sports in the Middle East now broadcast several of the premier polo 
championships in the world, sponsored by U.S. Polo Assn., making the thrilling sport accessible to millions 
of sports fans globally for the very first time. 
U.S. Polo Assn.  has recently been named one of USA Today’s  Most Trusted Brands and in 2026 was ranked 
the top sports licensor in the world, surpassing the NFL, PGA Tour, and Formula 1, according to License 
Global. The sport-inspired brand has been recognized internationally with awards for global growth and 
sports content. Due to its tremendous success as a global brand, U.S. Polo Assn. has been featured 
in Fortune, Forbes, Modern Retail,  WWD, and GQ, as well as on Yahoo Finance  and Bloomberg , among 
many other noteworthy media sources around the world.  
USPA Global  is a subsidiary of the United States Polo Association (USPA) and manages the multi-billion-
dollar sports brand, U.S. Polo Assn. USPA Global also manages its subsidiary, Global Polo, which is the 
worldwide leader in polo sport content. To learn more, visit globalpolo.com  or Global Polo on YouTube.
