Sub:  Intimatiion regarding the expression of interest/intent received from Promoter Group and 
Promoter Family, to donate 1% of the Equity Share Capital for charitable purposes 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III  of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform that the  
promoter family of Waaree, has committed to donate 1% of the equity share capital of the Company 
towards Charitable Purposes .   
 
The announcement  was made by the Chairman of the Company on the occasion of the 35th Annual 
General Meeting of the Company and it reflects the Promoters dedication to make a positive impact on 
society and aligns with the Company’s core values of social responsibility and giving back to the 
community. 
 
Mr. Hitesh Chimanlal Doshi, the Chairman and Managing Director of the Company, while making this 
announcement emphasised that, “We as the promoter family of Waaree, are deeply grateful to society 
and committed to giving back. We as a family have decided to donate our stake in the company to 
support charitable causes, with a special focus on education, healthcare, and community 
development. By investing in these vital areas, we hope to empower individuals and inspire lasting 
positive change. Together, we can build a more equitable and hopeful future.” 
 
Further, we shall inform the Exchange and ensure all the necessary compliances including but not 
limited to Listing Regulations, SEBI (Substantial Acquistion of Shares and Takeover) Regulations, 2011 
and SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 and all other applicable provisions as and 
when applicable.
