3.  Brief profile (in case of

 Mr. Paul Verghese Parambi is a seasoned financial 
services professional with over 36 years of leadership 
experience driving strategy, risk mitigation, and 
growth across banking and comprehensive financial 
services. 
 
Most recently, he served as Group President, Group 
Chief Risk Officer, and member of the Group 
Management Council of the Kotak Mahindra Group. 
In this capacity, Mr. Parambi  oversaw risk 
management for Kotak Mahindra Bank and all its 
group companies. Prior to this, he served as President – Consumer & Commercial Bank (Credit) of the Bank 
and as Head of Group Strategy for Kotak Group, 
playing a pivotal role in charting the strategic 
direction, evaluating key investments, and driving 
organizational development for the Bank and Group.  
Mr. Parambi  was the Head – International Business 
for the Kotak Group for several years and played a 
pivotal role in its expansion from 1995 to 2012. He has 
served on the boards of international subsidiaries of 
the Kotak Group. He joined the Kotak Group in 1991.  
Mr. Parambi began his professional career with 
Hindustan Unilever Limited in 1989. He is a graduate of IIM Ahmedabad and IIT Madras. 
 
Alongside his corporate mandates, Mr. Parambi  is 
passionate about social impact initiatives and serves 
as a Trustee of the Kotak Education Foundation.
