GROWING  STRONGER,  GOING  FURTHER.Commenting  on the Results,  Mr. Ramprakash  Bubna,  Chairman  and  MD,  said , 
“I am pleased  to report  another  quarter  of healthy  growth . In Q2 FY27, revenue  grew  13% year -
on-year  to Rs. 1,052 crores . Gross  margins  expanded  by 140 basis  points  to 35.9%, and EBITDA  
rose  18% to Rs. 164 crores . Profit  after  tax grew  23% to Rs. 92 crores . 
NAFTA  was the standout  this quarter . Revenue  from  the region  grew  about  30% year -on-year,  
with significantly  better  margins . LATAM  also did well, with agrochemical  revenue  up over  80%. 
Together,  these  regions  more  than  made  up for a softer  quarter  in Europe .
Europe,  our largest  market,  saw a slight  moderation  this quarter,  coming  off an exceptionally  
strong  FY26. The weather  challenges  we highlighted  last quarter  continued  through  the 
September  quarter,  with extended  heat  and drought  conditions  in parts  of central  and eastern  
Europe . As a result,  distributors  have  taken  a cautious  approach  to restocking . We see this as a 
temporary,  weather -led impact  rather  than  any change  in underlying  demand . Our registration  
portfolio  and customer  relationships  in Europe  remain  strong,  and as conditions  normalise,  we 
expect  the region  to do well in the second  half, which  is seasonally  our stronger  period .
We continue  to maintain  our FY27 revenue  growth  guidance  of 10–15%. We also expect  EBITDA  
margins  to improve  through  the year  and remain  between  18% and 20% for FY27, supported  by a 
better  product  mix and operating  leverage  in the second  half.
We remain  focused  on strengthening  our long-term  growth  platform . We invested  Rs. 419 crores  
in capex  during  the first half,  in product  registrations . We now hold  3,044 registrations,  with 946 
more  in the pipeline . Our balance  sheet  remains  robust : we are debt -free,  with cash,  bank  
balances  and liquid  investments  of Rs. 846 crores .”Investor Release

GROWING  STRONGER,  GOING  FURTHER.About Us
Sharda  Cropchem  Limited  (SCL)  is a fast-growing  global  agrochemicals  company  with a peer  
position  in the crop  protection  chemicals  industry . Its vast and growing  library  of dossiers  and 
IPRs provides  a solid  foundation  for growth  in the global  marketplace,  especially  in advanced  
markets  such  as Europe,  North  America,  and Latin  America . It enables  to operate  globally  in a wide  
range  of formulations  and active  ingredients . The Company’s  deep  domain  knowledge  and 
extensive  experience  provides  a significant  competitive  edge,  enabling  to expand  businesses,  
both  in existing  markets  and new geographies . This has further  positioned  the Company  as a 
prominent  player  in the field  of crop  protection  chemicals . SCL offers  a diverse  range  of products,  
including  fungicides,  herbicides,  insecticides,  and biocides,  to a global  clientele . Through  its 
expertise  and conviction,  SCL has earned  a reputation  as a trusted  partner  in the global  
agricultural  industry . The Company  continues  to support  farmers  and agricultural  businesses  
across  the globe,  fostering  growth  and sustainability  in the sector .
