Press Release  
Federal Bank Q2 Profit Rises 45.10% to Record ₹1,386 Cr; Gross and Net NPA at Decadal Lows  
Total business crosses ₹6 lakh crore | Gross advances cross ₹3 lakh crore | NII up 26% | CASA up 23% | 
Fee income at all -time high, up 32% | RoA at 1.32%  
Federal Bank today reported net profit of ₹1,386.09 crore  for the quarter ended 30 September 2026 (Q2 
FY27), up 45.10%  over the corresponding quarter of the previous year. This is the highest quarterly profit 
in the Bank’s history. The core franchise drove the earnings growth: net interest income grew 26.38%, fee 
income grew 31.86%, and gross and net NPA both reached deca dal lows.  
Key Highlights – Q2 FY27  
• Profitability:  Net profit was ₹1,386.09 crore, the highest ever, up 45.10% YoY and 17.77% QoQ. 
Earnings per share rose 44.34% to ₹22.26. Operating profit grew 35.35% to ₹2,225.37 crore.  
• Core Earnings Engine:  Net Interest Income grew 26.38% to ₹3,153.40 crore, ahead of advances 
growth of 21.66%.  
• Margin Expansion:  NIM expanded 26 bps YoY to 3.32%. Cost of Funds fell 40 bps YoY . Cost of 
Deposits declined 39 bps YoY to 5.18%.  
• Record Other Income:  Other income reached its highest -ever level of ₹1,490.44 crore, up 37.73% 
YoY . Fee income grew 31.86% to ₹1,167.71 crore. Fee income to average earning assets is at an 
all-time high of 1.11%.  
• Asset Quality at Decadal Best:   
o Gross NPA improved to 1.32% and Net NPA to 0.17%, both decadal lows. These are 
improvements of 51 bps and 31 bps YoY respectively.  
• Fortified Coverage:  The Provision Coverage Ratio (excluding TWO) strengthened to 86.78%, up 
1,333 bps YoY . Coverage including technical write -offs stands at 94.49%. Credit cost declined 19 
bps YoY to 0.31%.  
• Balance Sheet Scale:  Total business franchise now stands at over ₹6.4 lakh crore up 20.12% YoY . 
Total deposits stood at ₹3,42,693.56 crore, up 18.61% YoY . Gross advances crossed ₹3 lakh crore 
to reach ₹3,01,687.80 crore, up 21.66% YoY .  
• CASA Franchise:  CASA balances reached ₹1,09,803.15 crore, up 22.56% YoY and growing faster 
than the overall deposit book. The CASA ratio improved 103 bps YoY to 32.04%. Average CASA 
crossed ₹1 lakh crore.  
• NR Franchise:  NR deposits (NRE and ONR) reached ₹1,19,055.98 crore, up 26.63% YoY . FCNR(B) 
deposits rose 239.14% YoY to ₹23,547.72 crore.  
• Chosen Segments Delivering:  Granular, higher -yielding businesses continued to lead growth.  
o Gold Loans grew 37.13% YoY .  
o LAP grew 32.21% YoY .  
o Credit Cards grew 28.27% YoY .  
o Commercial Banking grew 25.46% YoY .  
o CV/CE grew 21.74% YoY .  
o Corporate & Institutional Banking grew 17.19% YoY to ₹1,04,666.02 crore.  
o Business Banking returned to growth at 12.39% YoY .

MD & CEO’s Comment  
Commenting on the performance, Mr. KVS Manian, Managing Director & CEO , said:  
“This quarter marks several milestones for the Bank: our highest -ever profit, our total business franchise 
now stands at over ₹6.4 lakh crore and advances crossing ₹3 lakh crore. More importantly, the growth is 
coming from the core. Net interest income gre w 26% and fee income 32%, and fee income relative to our 
earning assets is at an all -time high.”  
“Gross and net NPA are at their lowest levels in a decade, slippages are down 27% and provision coverage 
stands at 87%. We are growing faster while keeping the quality of the book intact.”  
“Our chosen segments are delivering as intended, and the CASA and NR franchises continue to deepen. As 
the rate environment turns, we enter the second half of the year with a strong capital position, a granular 
balance sheet and good momentum in our core b usiness.”  
Asset Quality and Resilience  
Asset quality improved further. Gross and net NPA are at decadal lows, and the leading indicators point in 
the same direction. Fresh slippages declined 27.48% YoY , and the slippage ratio improved to 0.59% from 
0.94% a year ago. The restructured book has re duced to ₹1,389.55 crore, or 0.46% of gross advances. The 
Bank’s balanced approach to risk and its continued shift towards secured, granular segments provide a 
meaningful buffer against external uncertainty.
