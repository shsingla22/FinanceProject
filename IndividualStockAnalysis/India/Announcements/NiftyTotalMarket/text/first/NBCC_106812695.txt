ORDER 
(Hearing held on 23 I 09 12026) 
Joint petition dated 16.07.2026 has been filed by the applicant companies 
with this Ministry on 20.07.2026 in respect of the proposed Scheme of 
Amalgamation of Mis HSCC (India) Limited (Transferor Company) with Mis 
NBCC (India) Limited (Transfer ee Company) ul s 230-232 of the Companies Act, 
2013 read with Government of India, Notification NO.GSR.582 (E) dated 
13.06.2017. 
2. The Scheme of Amalgamation was approved by Board of Directors of 
Transferor Company on 14.07.2026 and Transferee Company on 10.04.2026, 
copy of the corresponding Board Resolutions have been duly signed and filed 
along with the present application. 
3. The Applicant Companies have stated that the provisions relating to the 
accounting treatment for the proposed scheme were in conformity with 
applicable provisions of the Compan ies Act, 2013. Certificate from Statutory 
Auditor of the Applicant Companies on the accounting treatment as proposed in 
the Scheme, were annexed to the application and it is clearly stated that the 
accounting treatment, is in conformity with that prescribed under section 133 of 
the Companies Act, 2013. 
4. The application is supported by the Affidavit ofShri Hrishikesh Kumar, 
authorized signatory of the Transferor Company, who is duly authorized to file 
the same on behalf of the Transferor Company , in terms of the Board of Directors 
resolution dated 14.07.2026. 
5. The application is supported by the Affidavit of Ms. Deepti Gambhir, 
authorized signatory of the Transferee Company, who is duly authorized to file 
the same on behalf of the Transferee Company, in terms of the Board of Directors 
resolution dated 10.04.2026. 
6. The Transferor Company and Transferee Company submitted the audited 
finan~al statement for the financial year 2023-24, 2024-25 and 2025-26.

7. The Transferor Company i.e. M/s HSCC (India) Limited and Transferee 
Company i.e. M/ s NBCC (India) Limited have broadly prayed for the following 
relief (s):-
I. Dispense with the requirement of convenmg the meeting of equity 
shareholders and obtaining consent affidavit of the Transferor Company to 
consider the scheme; 
ii. Dispense with the requirement of convenmg the meeting of equity 
shareholders and obtaining consent affidavit of the Transferee Company 
to consider the Scheme; 
111. Dispense with the requirement of convening the meeting of unsecured 
creditors and obtaining consent affidavit of the Transferor Company to 
consider the scheme; 
IV. Dispense with the requirement of convening the meeting of unsecured 
creditors and obtaining consent affidavit of the Transferee Company to 
consider the Scheme; and 
v. Pass such other and further orders as this Hon'ble MCA may deem fit and 
proper in the facts and circumstances of the case. 
8. After considering the prayers and submissions made by both the 
companies, it is directed that: 
1. The meeting of equity shareholders of the Transferor Company is 
dispensed with as the Transferor Company has furnished consents by way 
of affidavit from all the equity shareholders. 
ii. The meeting(s) of the secured creditors of the Transferor and Transferee 
Company are dispensed with since Transferor Company furnished a 
certificate from S.R.Dinodia & Co. LLP, Chartered Accountant dated 
14.07.2026 and the Transferee Company furnished a certificate from D K 
Chhajer & Co., Chartered Accountant dated 15.07.2026 certifying that 
there is nil/no secured creditors as on 31.03.2026. 
iii. It is directed that the meeting of equity shareholders of the Transferee 
Company is dispensed with keeping in view the submission made in this

regard. The legal counsel of the petitioner companies gave reference of the 
judgments namely: Reliance Industries Ltd. V. Registrar of Companies, 
Company Appeal (AT) No.109 of 2023 and Patel Hydro Power Private 
Limited, in Company Appeal (AT) No. 137 of 2021, wherein it is held that 
since the Transferor Company is a wholly owned subsidiary of the 
Transferee Company and that no shares are required to bc issued as 
consideration, therefore there is no reorganization of the share capital of 
the Transferee Company and consequently the rights of the shareholders 
are not being affected. Further, the assets of the Transferor and Transferee 
Company is far exceeds their liabilities. 
IV. It is directed that the meetings of unsecured creditors of the Transferor 
and Transferee Company are dispensed with keeping in view the 
submissions made in this regard. The legal counsel of the petitioner 
companies gave reference of the judgments namely, Reliance Industries 
Ltd. V. Registrar of Companies, Company Appeal (AT) No.1 09 of 2023 and 
Patel Hydro Power Private Limited, in Company Appeal (AT) No. 1370f2021 
and a reference of the order issued by this ministry in the matter of 
Composite Scheme of Amalgamation and Arrangement amongst Gujarat 
State Petroleum Corporation Limited (Transferor Co.l), Gujarat State 
Petro net Limited (Transferor Co.2) and GSPC Energy Limited (Transferor 
Co.3) and Gujarat Gas Limited (Transferee/Demerged Co.) and GSPL 
Transmission Limited (Resulting Company) issued on 10.09.2025. It is 
submitted during the hearing that the scheme does not propose to alter 
any ofthe rights and remedies of the Unsecured Creditors and the scheme 
does not propose to reduce or alter any of the amounts payable to any of 
the Unsecured creditors of any of the companies. However, it is directed 
that Individual Notices along with scheme and other relevant documents 
shall be sent to the Unsecured Creditors above the threshold amount of 
Rs.1,00,OO,OOO/- in each of the applicant companies, as per the list of 
unsecured creditors certified by chartered accountant of each of the 
companies as on 31.03.2026, inviting objections to the Scheme of Merger, 
if any. Any objection received by any of the applicant companies together 
with the response thereto shall be filed with this Ministry along with the 
Confirmation Petition.
