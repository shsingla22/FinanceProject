Shri. Anand Rathi to gift  equity shares of Anand Rathi Wealth Limited to about  5,000 
employees of the group and personal staff  
 
Mumbai, India, 16th November 2026  
In a gesture of appreciation and gratitude, and recognising the contribution of people who 
have helped building the institution, Shri. Anand Rathi, Promoter, Founder and Chairman of 
Anand Rathi Wealth Limited, has decided to gift up to 1, 25,000 equity shares of the 
Company, representing approximately 0.0 8 per cent of its equity share capital and 
amounting to approximately Rs. 30 crore  (at today’s price) , from his personal holding to 
about  5,000 individuals. Each recipient will receive upto  25 equity shares of the Company. 
The recipients include employees of the Company , Group companies  and personal staff .  
 The transfer is made purely as a gesture of appreciation and gratitude. It carries no monetary 
consideration, and no obligation, retention commitment or condition of continued service of any kind attaches to it.  
 Speaking on the initiative, Shri . Anand Rathi said, “ Our employees are the backbone of our 
organisation and an integral part of everything we have achieved. This gesture is a reflection 
of our gratitude for their hard work, commitment and belief in our vision. We firmly believe 
that the people who contribute  to building the Company's future should also have an 
opportunity to participate in the value it creates. Through this initiative, we wish to strengthen 
the spirit of ownership, belongingness and shared success."  
 The Company remains committed to fostering an inclusive and appreciative culture in which contribution at every level is recognised. The initiative underscores Company's people -
centric philosophy and its commitment to building a long -term relationship with its 
employees. It also represents a meaningful step towards recognising employees not merely 
as contributors to the organisation bu t as partners in its growth journey.  
 Katalyst Advisors LLP acted as an advisor on the transaction and for obtaining relevant 
approvals from SEBI.  
 The Company has obtained necessary regulatory approvals from Securities and Exchange 
Board of India  (SEBI) . The Company will make the appropriate disclosures to the stock 
exchanges in compliance with the SEBI approval .
