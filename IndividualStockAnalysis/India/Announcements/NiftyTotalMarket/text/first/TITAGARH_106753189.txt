Titagarh Rail Systems Inaugurates New Design and Operations Centre at Uttarpara, WB, Advancing the 
Vision of Hon ’ble Chief Minister Shri Suvendu Adhikari Ji, in the Presence of Shri Tapas Roy, Hon ’ble 
Minister of Industry & Commerce, and Shri Arjun Singh, Hon ’ble Minister of Labour & Transport, 
Government of West Bengal, Strengthening the Revival of Bengal  
 
Kolkata, 21 August 2026:  Titagarh Rail Systems Limited (TRSL) today inaugurated its new Design & 
Operations Centre  at its Passenger Rail Systems (PRS) facility in Uttarpara, West Bengal. The facility was 
inaugurated in the presence of Shri Tapas Roy, Hon ’ble Minister of Industry & Commerce, Government of 
West Bengal, and Shri Arjun Singh, Hon ’ble Minister of Labour & Transport, Government of West B engal.  
This significant step marks an important step in Titagarh ’s journey towards strengthening its in -house 
design, engineering and operational capabilities. The facility will serve as a hub for advanced engineering, 
design -led innovation and technology development, supporting Titagarh ’s growing portfolio across 
passenger rail systems.  
The inauguration is a direct reflection of the Sonar Bangla  vision championed by Hon ’ble Chief Minister 
Shri Suvendu Adhikari  Ji, under whose leadership the Government of W est Bengal has placed the revival 
of Bengal and its industrialisation at the centre of the State ’s development agenda.  
The inauguration underscores Titagarh ’s continued commitment to building world -class rail manufacturing 
capabilities in India from Bengal  and strengthening West Bengal ’s position in India ’s evolving rail 
manufacturing ecosystem. It also reinforces Titagarh ’s commitment to “Make in India, Make for the 
World. ” Titagarh remains committed to growing alongside the Government of West Bengal ’s renewed 
focus on industrial development  and looks forward to new policies, infrastructure and investment 
opportunities.  
Commenting on the occasion, Shri Tapas Roy, Hon ’ble Minister of Industry & Commerce, Government of 
West Bengal , said, “I am truly amaze d to see metro coaches and trains being manufactured from scratch 
here in Bengal, and Titagarh ’s trains being supplied to cities such as Pune, Surat, Ahmedabad Bengaluru, 
Patna, Mumbai, also next generation modern Vande Bharat Sleeper Trains. In coming day s we are confident 
that we will be able to see Kolkata Metro added to this list, and I hope we hear this news soon. The work 
being undertaken by Titagarh is truly commendable and aligns with the vision of Viksit Bharat and the 
revival of Bengal . A Viksit B engal is essential for a Viksit Bharat . We must work together to realise this 
vision and restore Bengal ’s glory as a leading hub of industry and manufacturing. ” 
Commenting on the occasion, Shri Arjun Singh, Hon ’ble Minister of Labour & Transport, Governmen t of 
West Bengal , said, “The Government of West Bengal will continue to play its role in creating a beneficial 
environment for industrial growth, under the guidance of our Hon ’ble Chief Minister, Shri  Suvendu 
Adhikari ji.  However, the responsibility for dr iving industrial development rests with industry itself. 
Enterprises such as Titagarh have a vital role to play in shaping Bengal ’s industrial future. Together, through 
a strong partnership between the Government and industry, we will work towards strength ening Bengal ’s 
industrial ecosystem and unlocking the State ’s immense potential for sustainable growth. ” 
Commenting on the occasion, Shri Umesh Chowdhary, Vice Chairman & Managing Director, Titagarh Rail 
Systems Limited , said, “We are encouraged by the renewed focus of the Government of West Bengal on 
accelerating Bengal ’s industrial and economic growth. The vision of Hon ’ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra 
Modi Ji for a Viksit Bharat, with its emphasis on Make in India and  Atmanirbhar Bharat, aligns strongly  
 
 
with Titagarh ’s commitment to building advanced manufacturing and engineering capabilities in Bengal 
for India and the world.  The inauguration of our new Design & Operations Centre marks another milestone 
in strengthe ning our design, engineering and innovation capabilities. Titagarh is fully committed to 
contributing to Bengal ’s manufacturing legacy and working closely with the Government towards its 
revival and industrialisation, under the leadership of Hon ’ble Chief Minister Shri Suvendu Adhikari Ji and 
his vision of Sonar Bangla.  We are grateful for the presence and support of the Hon ’ble Ministers, which 
further reinforces Bengal ’s growing role in India ’s manufacturing, engineering and mobility ecosystem. ” 
As Bengal  aspires to emerge as a global manufacturing hub in line with the Hon ’ble Chief Minister ’s 
vision, Titagarh is ready to contribute to this new phase of industrialisation and the revival of Bengal, and 
to support the State ’s growth ambitions.
