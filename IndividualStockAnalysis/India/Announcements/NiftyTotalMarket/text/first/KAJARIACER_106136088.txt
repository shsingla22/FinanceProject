KAJARIA CERAMICS LIMITED                                                    3 of 17                                      INVESTOR UPDATE  |  Q3 FY25Despite  sustained  softness  in domestic  demand  and  
continued  weakness  in tile exports,  our tile volumes  in 
Q3FY25 grew  by 7% YoY  to 28.90 MSM .
Our EBITDA  margins  remained  soft at 12.78% for the quarter  
due to lower  realisations  and loss  in bathware  division  which  
was  largely  attributable  to new  sanitaryware  unit  commenced  
in Morbi  during  the year .
We however  remain  optimistic  on the demand  outlook  for the 
tile industry  in the near  to medium  term .
