Hexaware Positioned as a Visionary in the 2026 Gartner® 
Magic Quadrant ™ for Custom Software Development 
Services  
 
October 7, 2026 : Hexaware Technologies (NSE: HEXT), a global provider of IT solutions and services, 
today announced it has been positioned as a Visionary in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™  for 
Custom Software Development Services.  
 
Hexaware believes this positioning reflects its strategy of delivering custom software development 
through persistent engineering pods, powered by its proprietary AI platform stack and a talent model 
built around senior engineering expertise.  
 
The company's proprietary platforms, including RapidX®, Agentverse ™, and Tensai ®, form the 
foundation of its AI -native delivery approach, enabling clients to accelerate software builds and 
modernization programs through pre -built AI agents and automated development lifecycles operating 
under human- supervised quality gates.  
 
 
 

Gartner, Magic Quadrant for Custom Software Development Services, By Jaideep Thyagarajan, Ryan 
McKinney, Nathan Davie, 07 October 2026  
  "To us, being recognized as a Visionary reflects what our teams have been building toward —  an 
engineering model where AI does the heavy lifting on speed and scale, and our people focus on the 
decisions that actually matter to clients. That combination is what makes the difference in complex 
modernization work."   
Sanjay Salunkhe, President & Global Head –  Digital & Software Services, Hexaware
