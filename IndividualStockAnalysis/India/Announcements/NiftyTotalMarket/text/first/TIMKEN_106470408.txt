SUB: APPEAL AGAINST ORDER  PASSED BY DISTRICT CONSUMER DISPUTES 
REDRESSAL COMMISSION, SHIMLA : UPDATE   
This is in furtherance to our earlier intimation dated 26 September, 2024. The Company had 
filed appeal against order passed by District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, Shimla 
in the matter of Mr. Ravi Shankar Sood v/s Timken India Limited and CB Management Services 
Private Limited. Parties have reached settlement and therefore, appeal has been disposed off 
without any costs, penalty to the Company. Relevant details are given below : 
Name of the Authority passing order State Consumer Disputes Redressal 
Commission, Himachal Pradesh (State 
Commission) 
Details of the violation(s)/contravention(s) 
alleged to be committed 
 Mr. Ravi Shankar Sood had applied for 
transmission of shares in year 2020-21. Shares 
were held in the name of his father Mr. D N 
Sood who passed away in 2013. C B 
Management Services Private Limited, 
Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA) asked 
Mr. Sood to provide relevant documents as 
per SEBI Guidelines. RTA  claimed that it did 
not receive requisite documents hence did not 
give effect to transmission. While Mr. Sood 
claimed that he has submitted all requisite 
documents relating to transmission.  
 
                                                                                                                                                    
 2 Mr. Sood had filed complaint before  District 
Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, 
Shimla (District Commission) stating 
deficiency in service by RTA /Company to 
give effect to transmission.  
District Commission had asked Mr. Sood to 
provide necessary documents to 
RTA/Company and had asked 
RTA/Company to give effect to transmission 
within 60 days of receipt of all documents and 
on completion of all formalities. District 
Commission also asked RTA/Company to 
pay Rs 15,000 as compensation and Rs 10,000 
as litigation costs to Mr. Sood.  
Nature and details of the action(s) taken, 
initiated or order(s) passed  
 Appeal was filed by the Company and RTA 
against District Commission Order before the 
State Commission. However, during pendency 
of appeal before State Commission, Parties 
reached settlement and appeal stands disposed 
with consent of parties without any costs, 
compensation.  
Date of receipt of direction or order  
 E-copy of the order was received on 16 
December, 2025.  
Impact on financial, operation or other 
activities of the Company, quantifiable in 
monetary terms to the extent possible  
 Nil.  
The Company and RTA is not required to pay 
Rs 15,000 as compensation and Rs 10,000 as 
litigation costs to Mr. Sood as awarded by 
District Commission.
