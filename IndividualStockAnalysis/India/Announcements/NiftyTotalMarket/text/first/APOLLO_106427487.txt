Q2 FY26 Highlights 
 
In Q2 FY26, we recorded robust revenue growth of 40% year-on-year , reaching ₹2252.6 million , 
compared to ₹1607.1 million in Q2 FY25. This growth was primarily driven by the seamless 
execution of our strong order book and the transition of several high-value systems into full-scale 
production. 
 
On the profitability front, our EBITDA (excluding other income) increased by 80% to ₹591.9 
million, up from ₹328.9 million in the same quarter last year . Our EBITDA margin improved 
by 630 basis points , reaching a new high of 26.3% as compared to 20% in Q2 FY25  
underscoring the strength of our operating leverage and execution efficiency. 
 
This performance also translated into robust bottom-line growth. Our Profit After Tax (PAT) 
registered a 91% year-on-year increase to ₹300.31 million, compared to ₹157.3 million in Q2 
FY25. Our PAT margin expanded by 350 basis points, improving to 13.3% from 9.8% year-
on-year and quarter-on-quarter. 
This achievement is not only a testament to the strength of our products and capabilities but also highlights 
the trust and confidence that international clients are placing in us. As we continue to execute on our 
strategic vision, this order underscores our potential to unlock substantial opportunities in new markets
