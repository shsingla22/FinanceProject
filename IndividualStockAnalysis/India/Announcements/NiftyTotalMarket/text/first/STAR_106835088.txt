“Badree Komandur  MD and Group CEO
Strides  delivered  a strong  second  quarter,  with  revenue  of ₹14,297 m growing  
~17% YoY ,  driven  by Ex-US Markets . Our  diversification  strategy  continues  to play  
out,  with  Ex-US Markets  growing  31% YoY  to ₹6,963 m and,  for the first  time,  
surpassing  our US business  in quarterly  revenue . Our  US business  delivered  9% 
YoY  growth . 
EBITDA  grew  to ₹2,509 m, crossing  the ₹2,500 m mark  in a quarter  for the first  time . 
This  was  despite  higher  raw  material,  freight  and  operating  costs  arising  from  
geopolitical  disruptions . We remain  focused  on mitigating  these  pressures  
through  continuous  cost  optimization  initiatives  during  the year .
Net  Debt  increased  by ₹2,114 m during  H1 due  to an elongated  cash -to-cash  cycle  
and  higher  inventory  holding  to navigate  current  market  conditions . We expect  the 
Net  Debt  to EBITDA  ratio  to stabilize  by the end  of the year .
We are also  pleased  to welcome  Mr. Biren  Gabhawala  to our Board  as an 
Independent  Director . His deep  expertise  in financial  reporting,  audit  and  
corporate  governance  will further  strengthen  the Board’s  oversight .
While  the external  environment  remains  dynamic,  we are committed  to delivering  
sustainable  and  profitable  growth .
