Q2 & H1 FY27 Business UpdateSurya Roshni Limited
New  Delhi,  October  1, 2026 : Surya  Roshni  Limited,  the largest  exporter  of ERW  
Pipes,  largest  producer  of ERW  GI pipes  and one of the largest  Lighting  Companies  
in India,  announces  its business  update  for the quarter  and half year  ended  
September  30, 2026 .
Key Highlights
➢Steel  Pipes  & Strips  sales  volume  stood  at 2.67 lakh tonnes  in Q2FY27 as against  2.18 lakh 
tonnes  in Q2FY26, a growth  of 23% YoY , marking  the ever -highest  Quarterly  sales  volume  
for the business .
➢For H1FY27, sales  volume  stood  at 4.95 lakh tonnes  as against  4.07 lakh tonnes  in H1FY26, 
a growth  of 22% YoY.
➢The Lighting  & Consumer  Durables  segment  also delivered  its ever -highest  Q2 volume  
performance  in Q2FY27, supported  by festive  season  demand  and broad -based  growth
