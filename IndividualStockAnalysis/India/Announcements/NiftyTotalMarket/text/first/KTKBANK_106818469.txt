Karnataka Bank Unveils 'Nava Dhamaka 2026' -Enhancin g Banking Experience 
Karnataka Bank, in its continued pursuit of digital transformation, customer-centric 
innovation and technology-driven banking, today unveiled a new range of banking 
products and services under the theme "Nava Dhamaka 2026 -Enhancing Banking 
Experience." The launch programme was held at the Bank's Bull Temple Road Office 
(BTRO), Bengaluru. 
The programme was inaugurated by the Bank's Managing Director & CEO Sri 
Raghavendra S Bhat in the presence of distinguished guests, technology partners, 
senior executives of the Bank along with officials from the Bengaluru region. 
Under Nava Dhamaka 2026, the Bank unveiled nine key solutions spanning 
investments, digital banking, payments, customer engagement and lending. These
