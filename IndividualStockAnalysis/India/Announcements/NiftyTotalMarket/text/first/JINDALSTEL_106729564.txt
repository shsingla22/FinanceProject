Tax Transparency Report 2025-26 01At Jindal Steel Limited, we believe that responsible 
business is built on integrity, transparency, and 
accountability. As part of this commitment, we are 
pleased to present our Tax Transparency Report  
for FY 2025–26 .
Our approach to taxation is guided by full compliance 
with applicable laws, sound commercial principles, 
and strong corporate governance. Our tax strategy, 
reviewed regularly by the management team, 
ensures that tax decisions remain aligned with our 
business objectives while upholding the highest 
standards of ethical conduct. We do not engage 
in artificial tax planning or structures that lack 
commercial substance.
Responsible tax practices are an important part of 
our Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) 
framework. Through transparent reporting and 
disciplined governance, we seek to strengthen 
stakeholder confidence while contributing to India's 
economic growth and sustainable development.
FY 2025–26 was a year of resilient performance. The 
Company produced 9.25 million tonnes of crude steel 
and recorded sales of 8.68 million tonnes, delivering 
Gross Revenue of ₹62,412 crore, EBITDA of ₹9,660 crore, 
and Profit After Tax of ₹3,361 crore.
To provide a transparent and consistent view 
of our fiscal contribution, this report presents all 
figures on a cash-payment basis, reflecting actual 
payments made during the financial year rather than 
accounting accruals.Our fiscal contribution is presented under 
two categories:
 »Direct Contributions (Taxes Borne): Corporate 
Income Tax, Customs Duties, Mining Royalties & Bid 
Premiums, other taxes borne such as Electricity 
Duty/Cess, Property Tax and Stamp Duty, and 
Employer Social Security Contributions.
 »Indirect Contributions (Taxes & Revenue 
Collected):  Net GST, Withholding Taxes (TDS/
TCS), and Employee Social Security Contributions 
collected and remitted to the Government 
on behalf of customers, employees, and 
business partners.
During FY 2025–26, Jindal Steel Limited contributed 
₹11,348 crore to the public exchequer, reflecting our 
commitment to responsible corporate citizenship and 
nation building.
This report reflects our commitment to responsible 
tax conduct and reinforces our belief that 
transparency, accountability, and sound governance 
are essential to creating sustainable value for our 
stakeholders and contributing to India’s long-term 
economic development.

Jindal Steel Limited 02Tax Governance and 
Structural Oversight
At Jindal Steel, responsible tax 
management forms an integral 
part of our corporate governance 
framework and supports our 
commitment to sustainable value 
creation. We seek to conduct 
our tax affairs with integrity, 
transparency and in accordance 
with applicable laws and 
regulations in every jurisdiction in 
which we operate.
The Company's tax affairs are 
handled by a dedicated in-house 
tax team comprising experienced 
professionals responsible for tax 
compliance, reporting, transfer 
pricing, litigation management 
and evaluation of significant tax 
positions. The tax team works 
closely with business functions 
to ensure that tax considerations 
are appropriately embedded in 
business decisions and processes.
Our tax governance framework 
establishes clear roles, 
responsibilities and decision-
making processes, supported 
by robust internal controls and 
defined escalation mechanisms. 
This framework enables effective 
oversight of tax matters and 
promotes consistency in the 
implementation of our tax 
principles across the organisation.
Significant tax matters, emerging 
developments and material tax 
positions are periodically reviewed 
by senior management. Where 
appropriate, the Company also 
engages independent external 
advisors to obtain specialised 
advice on complex matters and 
evolving regulatory developments.Tax Strategy
Our tax strategy is aligned with our 
Code of Conduct and broader 
principles of responsible business. 
We seek to comply with both the 
letter and spirit of applicable 
tax laws and ensure that tax 
considerations are appropriately 
reflected in our commercial 
and strategic decision-
making processes.
We adopt a principle-based 
approach to tax and do not 
engage in aggressive tax 
planning or arrangements that 
lack commercial substance. 
Related-party transactions 
are undertaken on an arm's 
length basis in accordance with 
applicable laws and the OECD 
Transfer Pricing Guidelines, and 
our business structures are driven 
by genuine commercial and 
operational considerations.
Where tax incentives, exemptions 
or reliefs are available under 
applicable legislation, we seek to 
avail them in a transparent and 
responsible manner, consistent 
with the intent of the law and our 
long-term business objectives.

We are committed to maintaining

compliance framework, supported

ensure compliance with applicable 
tax laws and accurate tax reporting

Timely compliance and

commitment to responsible 
corporate citizenship and 
maintaining the trust of our 
stakeholders. We periodically 
review our compliance processes 
to strengthen efficiency and 
ensure readiness for evolving 
regulatory requirements.
We continuously invest in

initiatives and engagement with

Tax Disputes & Risk 
Management
We maintain a proactive 
framework for identifying, 
assessing and managing tax 
risks arising from changes in 
legislation, judicial developments 
and business operations. Tax 
positions are supported by sound 
technical analysis and appropriate 
documentation, and complex 
matters are evaluated with the 
support of professional advice 
wherever necessary.
Where differences in interpretation 
arise, we endeavour to resolve 
matters through factual and 
legal discussions with the 
relevant authorities in a fair 
and professional manner. Early 
engagement and constructive 
dialogue help us minimise 
uncertainty and achieve greater 
clarity on tax matters.
If a matter remains unresolved, 
the decision to pursue litigation 
is taken after considering the 
technical merits of the case, 
judicial precedents, financial implications and broader business 
considerations. Material tax 
risks and significant disputes 
are subject to appropriate 
management oversight.
