The Restated SHA inter alia records the revised terms and conditions governing the 
management and governance of the Subsidiary, and the  inter se rights and obligations 
between the Company and AOPL in respect of the Subsidiary. The Restated SHA supersedes 
the shareholders’ agreement dated March 28, 2012, and the l etter dated April 17, 2015 , 
entered into by and between the Company, AOPL and the Subsidiary (“ Original SHA ”). 
 
(c) shareholding,  if any, in the entity  with whom  the agreement  is executed;  
 
Prior to the execution of the Restated SHA, the Company held 74% of the share capital of the 
Subsidiary and AOPL held the balance 26% of the share capital of the Subsidiary. The 
aforesaid shareholding pattern continues to remain unchanged after execution of the Restated 
SHA.  
 
(d) significant terms of the agreement (in brief) special rights like right to appoint directors, first 
right to share subscription in case of issuance of shares, right to restrict any change in  
capital structure etc.;  
 
The key amendments that have been made pursuant to the Restated SHA, are described 
below:  
 
i. Termination on account of change in control : The right of AOPL and HCG (each an 
“SHA Party ”) to terminate the shareholders’ agreement in case of change in ‘control’ 
of the other SHA Party as set out in the Original SHA, has been deleted;   
 
ii. Exit Rights:  The Original SHA inter -alia envisaged that upon completion of 5 years 
from the date of the Original SHA (i.e., by March 28, 2017), the Subsidiary is required 
to provide AOPL an exit on (i) the expiry of five years form the date of the Original 
SHA or (ii) on a material breach of the Original SHA. The aforesaid clauses have been 
deleted in the Restated SHA since AOPL has agreed to continue its partnership with the 
Company with a revised exit horizon as explained in paragraph (iii) below.
