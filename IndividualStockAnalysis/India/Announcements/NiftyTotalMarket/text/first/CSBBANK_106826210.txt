PRESS RELEASE 
 
CSB Bank- Financial Results  
 
The Board of Directors of CSB Bank took on record the financial results for the quarter (Q2  FY 2027) and half year 
ended 30.09.2026 (H1 FY 2027 ) which were subject to limited review by the Statutory Auditors in their meeting  held 
on 23. 10.2026 
 
Highlights 
 
a) Total Deposits grew by 18% YoY to ₹ 46,889 crore as on 30.09.2026 from ₹ 39,651 crore as on 30 .09.2025 
The CASA ratio stood at 19% as on 30 .09.2026. 
 
b) Advance (Net) grew by 22% YoY to ₹ 41,852 crore as on 30.09.2026 from ₹ 34,262 crore as on 30 .09.2025 
supported by a robust growth of 44% in Wholesale and 36% in Gold loan portfol ios on a YoY basis. 
 
c) Net Interest Income (NII) grew by 20 % YoY to ₹ 510 crore for Q2 FY27 from ₹ 424 crore for Q2 FY26 and up 
7% QoQ from ₹ 479 crore for Q1 FY27.  
 
d) Non-Interest Income  grew by 20% QoQ to ₹ 274 Crore for Q2 FY27 from ₹ 229 crore for Q1 FY2 7.  
e) Cost Income Ratio (CIR)  stood at 64.80% for Q2 FY27 compared to 63.86% for Q2 FY26 an d 64.55% for Q1 
FY27. 
     
f) Cost to Assets stood at 3.59% in Q2 FY27 compared to 4.11% in Q2 FY26 and 3.31% in Q1 FY2 7. 
 
 
g) Operating Profit grew by 10% QoQ to ₹ 276 Crore for Q2 FY2 7 from ₹ 251 Crore for Q1 FY27.  
 
h) Profit after Tax (PAT)  up by 22% QoQ to ₹ 183 crore for Q2 FY2 7 from ₹ 150 crore for Q1 FY27. We continue 
to maintain the accelerated provisioning policy during this quarter as well . Return on Assets and NIM were 
at 1.29% and 3. 81% respectively during Q2 FY27. 
 
i) Robust Capital Structure - Capital Adequacy Ratio stood at 19.55 % as on 30.09.2026 as against 20.99% as on 
30.09.2025, which is well above the regulatory requirement.  
 
j) Asset Quality & Provisioning – Gross non-performing assets were at 1.58% as on 30 .09.2026 as against 1. 75% 
as on 30.06.2026 and 1.81% as on 30 .09.2025. 
Net non-performing assets were at 0.33% as on 30 .09.2026 as against 0. 39% as on 30.06.2026 and 0. 52% as 
on 30 .09.2025. 
Provision coverage ratio (including technical write off) was  at 87.65% as on 30.09.2026 as against 86.83% as 
on 30.06.2026 and 84. 14% as on 30.09.2025

MD & CEO Speak: 
The solid foundation established through the Sustain and Build phases of our SBS 2030 journey is now translating 
into meaningful business outcomes. During the quarter, we delivered strong toplin e performance, with deposits and 
advances growing by 18% and 22% year- on-year, respectively. Our bottom-line performance was equally 
encouraging, with net profit increasing by 14% year- on-year and 22% sequentially. Our liquidity position remains 
comfortable, and all key financial and regulatory ratios continue to be stable an d comfortably within the guided 
range. The quarter gone by was exceptional as far as asset quality is concerned with GNPA and  NNPA level being the 
lowest since last few quarters. While NNPA levels stood at the lowest over the past 11 quarters; GNPA ratio of 1.58% 
is the lowest since Q1 FY 26. Looking ahead, we remain focused on sustaining this momentum. In the se cond half of 
FY27, we plan to accelerate growth across our key business segments, enabling us to mo ve steadily towards our SBS 
2030 aspiration of becoming a mid-sized bank. 
 
 
 
 
About CSB Bank Limited:  
 
We are one of the oldest private sector banks in India with an existence of over  100 years. While our Bank has a long 
operating history as a traditional bank, we are currently focusing on imple menting strategic changes in business 
model to function efficiently like a full service new age private sector bank. We have a strong base in Kerala along 
with significant presence in Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh. We off er a wide range of 
products and services to our customers, across SME, Retail, and Corporate segments. We d eliver our products and 
services through multiple channels, including 8 72 branches and 8 44 ATMs/CRMs spread across the country and 
various alternate channels such as debit cards, internet banking, mobile banking, point of sale se rvices and UPI.
