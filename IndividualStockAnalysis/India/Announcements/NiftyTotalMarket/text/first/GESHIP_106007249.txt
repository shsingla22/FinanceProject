PRESS RELEASE
G E Shipping delivers its 2005 built Medium Range Product Tanker 
“Jag Pranav” 
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (G E Shipping) delivered its 2005 built Medium 
Range Product Tanker “Jag Pranav” to the buyers. The vessel was contracted for sale in July 2024. 
Excluding Jag Pranav, Company’s current fleet stands at 42 vessels, comprising 28 tankers (6 
Crude carriers, 18 Product carriers, 4 LPG carriers) and 14 dry bulk carriers (2 Capesize, 8 
Kamsarmax, 4 Supramax) aggregating 3.36 Mn dwt. 
Additionally, we had contracted to sell one Supramax drybulk carrier in Aug 2024 and one 
Suezmax crude tanker in Sep 2024, which are due for deliveries by Q3 FY25.
