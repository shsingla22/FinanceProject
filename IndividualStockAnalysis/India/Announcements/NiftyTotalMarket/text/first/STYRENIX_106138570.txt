Dear Sir, 
 
In terms of the provisions of Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), we write to inform that the Company 
has entered into definitive agreements to acquire 36.13% equity shares of Clean Max Jasper 
Pvt. Ltd., a company formed by Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (“Promoter 
Shareholder”) and engaged in generation and transmission of renewable energy. The 
acquisition is for complying with regulatory requirements related to captive power consumption 
under electricity laws and for the purposes of meeting the Company’s green energy needs, 
optimising energy cost.
