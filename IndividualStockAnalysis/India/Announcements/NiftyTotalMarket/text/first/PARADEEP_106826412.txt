PRESS RELEASE  
 
Paradeep Phosphates Signs MoU intending  to Invest ₹4,750 Crore in New Chemical and Fertilizer  
Facilities in Odisha  
• MoU signed at an In vestors meet  by Odisha Gove rnment  on the sideline of India Chem 2026 
in Mumbai in the presence of Shri Mohan Chara n Majhi , Chie f Minister, Odisha  
• Investment to support industrial growth and employment generation in the State  
Mumbai, October 22, 2026: In a major boost to Odisha’s industrial growth, Paradeep Phosphates 
Limited (PPL), one of India’s leading fertilizer  manufacturers and a key contributor to Odisha’s 
industrial landscape, has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Government of 
Odisha, intendi ng to investment  ₹4,750 crore towards the development of new chemical and 
fertilizer  facilities i n the state  in coming years . 
The MoU was signed during India Chem 2026 in Mumbai in the presence of Hon’ble Chief Minister of 
Odisha, Shri Mohan Charan Majhi. The proposed inv estment will support the establishment of a new 
chemical plant and a bio -fertilizers  manufacturin g facility, alongside initiatives aimed at enhancing 
farm productivity.  
The MoU was signed by Mr Hemant Kumar Sharma,  Addl . Chief Secretary , Industries , on beh alf of 
the Government of Odisha, and Shri N. Suresh Krishnan, Managing Director and CEO of Paradee p 
Phosphates Limited, on behalf of the company .  
The signing ceremony took place in the presence of Shri Mohan Charan Majhi, Chief Minister, 
Odisha , along wit h senior go vernment officials  of Odisha , Mr Rajeev Nambiar, J oint Managing 
Director,  Mr Bijoy Kuma r Biswal,  CFO and Mr  Bibhu datta Panigrahi, Head - Corporate Affairs.  
The investment marks a significant step towards strengthening Odisha’s position as a major  
destination for investments in the chemicals and fertilizers  sector. It is also expected to suppo rt the 
expansion of the state’s industrial ecosystem and generate  direct and indirect employment across  
the value chain . 
The investment reinforces Paradeep Ph osphates’ long -standing association with Odisha and its role 
in supporting the India’s fertilizer  requirements. With its established industrial presence in the state, 
the company continues to contribute to the integration of manufacturing, agriculture and regional 
economic development.  
The MoU further reflects the Government of Odisha’s commitment to strengthening manufacturing 
capabilities and creating an enabling environment for sustainable economic growth. Through 
initiatives to promote industrial development and value addition, Odish a is positioning itself to 
emerge as a competitive hub for chemicals, fertilizers  and allied industries.
