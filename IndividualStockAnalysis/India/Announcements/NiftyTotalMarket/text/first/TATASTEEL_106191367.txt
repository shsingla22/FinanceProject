Sub:  Acquisition of equity stake in Indian Foundation for Quality Management  
 
This is further to our disclosure dated  June 25, 2024  on the abovementioned subject.   
 
This is to inform you that, Tata Steel Limited (‘ Company ’) has today i.e., on April 1 , 2025, 
acquired 1,24,90,000 equity shares of face value ₹10/- each aggregating to ₹12.49 crore  
in Indian Foundation for  Quality Management (‘ IFQM ’). Post the acquisition, the Company’s 
aggregate shareholding in IFQM has increased from 9.09% to 16.66%.
