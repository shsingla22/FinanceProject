OIL Signs MoU with MCD to Establish Compressed Bio-Gas Plants Based on Municipal 
Solid Waste in Delhi 
Oil India Limited (OIL), a Maharatna CPSE under the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas, 
Government of India, and a leading integrated energy company, has signed a Memorandum of 
Understanding (MoU) with the Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD) for the establishment of 
Compressed Bio-Gas (CBG) plant(s) based on Segregated Organic Municipal Solid Waste in 
Delhi. 
The MoU was signed on 30th July 2026 in the august presence of Smt. Rekha Gupta, Hon’ble 
Chief Minister of Delhi, and Sh. Pravesh Wahi, Hon’ble Mayor, Delhi along with senior officials 
from the Government of Delhi, MCD, and Oil India Limited.  
The occasion marks a significant milestone in fostering collaboration between OIL and MCD 
towards sustainable municipal solid waste management and the production of clean, renewable 
energy. 
The Memorandum of Understanding establishes a framework for cooperation between OIL and 
MCD to facilitate the development of CBG plants. Initially to take this forward two CBG plants of 
the capacity 500 TPD and 300 TPD respectively utilizing segregated organic municipal solid 
waste generated within Delhi are bring planned. These plants will produce 30 to 32 TPD of 
Compressed Biogas. 
The initiative aims to convert biodegradable waste into renewable green fuel, thereby reducing 
landfill dependency, improving urban sanitation, lowering greenhouse gas emissions, and 
supporting the Government of India’s energy transition and circular economy objectives. 
The partnership reinforces Oil India Limited’s commitment to expanding its waste to energy 
portfolio and contributing to the national objectives of Swachh Bharat Mission, SATAT, and Net 
Zero through the deployment of sustainable and environmentally responsible energy 
infrastructure.
