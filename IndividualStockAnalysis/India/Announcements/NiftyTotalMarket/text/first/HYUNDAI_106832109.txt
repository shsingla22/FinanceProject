Revenue surpassed /uni20B920,000 crore milestone, EBITDA m argin at 9.5% 
  
Gurugram | October 28, 2026:  The Board of Directors of Hyundai Motor India 
Limited (HMIL) today approved the unaudited financi al results (Standalone 
and Consolidated) for the second quarter and half y ear of FY 2026-27. 
 
Key Highlights (Q2 FY27): 
• Recorded highest-ever quarterly total sales of 219,072 units, supported by 
healthy growth of 15% YoY  and  23% QoQ. 
• Strong momentum in domestic, volumes up 19% (YoY and QoQ), 
complemented by all-time high rural penetration of 26.6% and a record 
CNG contribution of 19.1% in any quarter. 
• Export witnessed growth of 4% YoY and a  strong sequential growth of 
38% , despite geo-political challenges. 
• BAYON marks start of HMIL’s golden product cycle,  garners encouraging 
response. 
• Commodity headwinds weighed on profitability , however operating 
leverage, healthy mix and cost reduction initiative s helped improve EBITDA 
margin sequentially. 
Note : Export volumes include CBU+CKD
