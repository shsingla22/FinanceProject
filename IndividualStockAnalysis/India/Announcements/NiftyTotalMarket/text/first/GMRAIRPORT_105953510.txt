and GMR Infra Developers Limited (“GIDL”)  with the Company,

of the Company.  
 
Further, to re-align the MOA of the Company pursuant to the Table A 
of Schedule I of the Companies Act, 2013, it is proposed to adopt the 
new format of the MOA.   
 
The above alterations are subject to the approval of the shareholders 
of the Company in the ensuing general meeting.
