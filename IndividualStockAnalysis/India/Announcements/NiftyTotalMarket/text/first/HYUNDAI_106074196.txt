Hyundai Motor India Limited Registers Total Sales of 61,2 52 units  in 
November 2024  
 
▪ HMIL registers total monthly sales of 61,2 52 units in November 2024  
▪ Domestic sales of 48,2 46 units and export sales of 13,00 6 units in November  2024  
▪ Overall SUV contribution to domestic sales continues to stay strong at 68.8 % in November  
2024  
▪ Achieved highest ever monthly rural contribution of 22.1%  in November 2024 , augmenting 
HMIL’s reach in the hinterland of India  
▪ CNG contribution of 14.4 % in November  2024  backed by innovative Hy -CNG Duo technology  
 
Gurugram  | December  01, 202 4: Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) registered total sales 
of 61,2 52 units (Domestic: 48,2 46 units and Export: 13,00 6 units) in November 2024. HMIL 
achieved total (Domestic + Export) YTD sales  (Jan-Nov 2024) of 7,0 9,041  units.  
 
Commenting on HMIL sales , Mr. Tarun Garg, Whole -time Director and  Chief Operating Officer , 
HMIL  said, “HMIL ’s push towards fortif ying S UV supremacy continued in November with SUVs 
contributi ng 68.8 % of our total Domestic sales . We also bolstered HMIL’s  presence in the 
hinterland of India, by achieving highest ever monthly rural contribution of 22.1%  in November . 
Our innovative Hy -CNG technology continues to strengthen  HMIL ’s CNG sales,  with a 14.4 % 
contribution in November 2024 .”
