Tata Power Renewables Marks a Major Milestone with Commissioning of 
190.5 MW FDRE Project in Rajasthan  
 
 
 
 
 
 
  
 
 
 
Photo Caption: Tata Power Renewables  advancing India's energy transition through its 190.5 MW FDRE 
project at Kalasar, Bikaner, Rajasthan.  
• The 190.5 MW project has been developed under SJVN Firm and Dispatchable 
Renewable Energy ( SJVN FDRE ) Tranche- 1 and is a part of the bigger 460 MW FDRE 
project  
• Project to strengthen grid reliability through 115 MWHr advanced Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)   
 
National August  24, 2026:  Tata Power Renewable Energy Limited (TPREL), one of India's 
leading renewable energy company and a subsidiary of The Tata Power Company Limited 
(Tata Power), has commissioned its 190.5 MW  Solar  FDRE project at Kalasar, Bikaner, 
Rajasthan which is under t he contractual capacity of 460 MW Firm and Dispatchable 
Renewable Energy (FDRE) Project.  
The 190.5 MW project has been developed under SJVN FDRE Tranche- 1. The project will 
supply energy to Haryana Power Purchase Centre ( HPPC ), Maharashtra State Electricit y 
Distribution Company Limited ( MSEDCL ) and Noida Power Company Limited ( NPCL ) 
discoms.  
The project marks another significant milestone in India's clean energy journey by integrating large- scale solar generation with advanced energy storage and grid suppor t technologies to 
deliver reliable, dispatchable renewable power.  
The project demonstrates TPREL's engineering excellence and execution capabilities, having overcome several complex challenges including compressed project timelines, global supply chain dis ruptions impacting the availability of transmission line materials, module 
mounting structures and other critical equipment. It has successfully adopted several new 
technologies such as BESS, Harmonic Filter Bank and Static Var Generator systems.  

 
Among the project's notable execution milestones was the completion of the switchyard within 
just three months and the Harmonic Filter Bank within one month, reflecting TPREL's focus 
on speed, quality and execution excellence.  
With this project, TPREL's total renew able utility capacity has reached 12.4 GW. Of this, 
around 6.9 GW is operational, including 5.6 GW of solar and 1.3 GW of wind capacity, while close to 5.5 GW is under various stages of implementation, are expected to be commissioned in phases over the nex t 24 months.   
The company remains committed to developing innovative clean energy solutions that contribute to India's target of achieving 500 GW of non- fossil fuel capacity by 2030 and Tata 
Power's vision of a sustainable energy future.  
 
About Tata Power:  
The Tata Power Company Limited, a vertically integrated power company and part of the Tata Group, India’s largest 
multinational business conglomerate, has built a diversified portfolio across the entire power value chain, with its total 
operational and under -construction  capacity surpassing 26 GW. This includes approximately 17. 7 GW of clean and green 
energy capacity (including projects under construction) and around 8.8 GW of thermal generation capacity, ensuring a 
balanced and resilient energy mix. The Company also has a strong t ransmission and distribution footprint, with over 7,800  
circuit kilometres of transmission lines, including pipeline projects, and serves  over 13 million customers across its 
distribution businesses, making it one of India’s largest private power distribut ion companies.  Further strengthening its 
leadership in future-ready energy solutions, Tata Power has established 4.9 GW of integrated solar cell and module 
manufacturing capacity, signed 2.8 GW of pumped hydro storage projects, and expanded its EV charging network to over 
7,000 public charging points across 7 17 cities and towns in India.  As a pioneer in India’s clean energy transition, Tata 
Power remains committed to achieving Net Zero by 2045 and continues to partner with public and private stakeholders to 
deliver reliable, sustainable, and technology -led energy solutions across the country.
