Shyam Metalics’s Ramsarup Industries Limited to Commission Modern Blast 
Furnace and Linear Sinter Plant to Boost Steel Production Capacity 
National, 29th October 2025: Ramsarup Industries Limited (RIL), part of Shyam Metalics, one of India’s leading 
integrated metal manufacturing companies, is set to commission a state-of-the-art Modern Blast Furnace with Stove 
Heating started today and after the completion of all the defined process and preliminary activities this ultra-modern 
facility is scheduled to commence commercial production by end of November 2025. Simultaneously, the company’s 
Linear Sinter Plant will also be commissioned, with commercial production starting in the third week of November 
2025, as part of a Rs 369 crore investment. 
 
This strategic expansion is aimed at enhancing the company’s production capabilities to meet the growing demand 
for high-quality Alloy, Carbon, and Special Steel products through EAF, LRF, and VD processes across multiple 
industries. 
 
The newly commissioned Blast Furnace has a capacity of 0.45 million tonnes per annum (MTPA), significantly 
increasing the plant’s production output. With a volume of 350 cubic meters, the Blast Furnace is equipped with 
three top-fired stoves, modern coal injection technology, and a dry gas cleaning plant, ensuring an optimized and 
environmentally responsible steel-making process. The facility incorporates advanced energy recovery systems, 
including a 2.5 MW Top Gas Pressure Recovery Turbine (TRT) and 8 MW recovery through the BFG Boiler, 
contributing to an additional 10% energy recovery. A specialized evaporative cooling system further reduces water 
and power consumption by approximately 20% compared to conventional systems.  The plant's zero process water 
discharge design promotes sustainability and increases process efficiency. 
 
In addition to the Blast Furnace with Pulverized Coal Injection (PCI) and Oxygen Plant (O₂), the Linear Sinter Plant 
will process iron ore fines into feedstock suitable for the Blast Furnace. This addition is expected to enhance 
operating efficiency and reduce production costs, aligning with Ramsarup Industries Limited’s vision of operational 
excellence. 
 
Mr. Brij Bhushan Agarwal, Chairman & Managing Director, Shyam Metalics & Energy Limited,  said, 
"The commissioning of our Modern Blast Furnace and Linear Sinter Plant is a proud milestone for Ramsarup 
Industries. This advanced facility not only increases our production capacity but also brings in energy-efficient and 
environmentally responsible technology, underlining our commitment to excellence and sustainable growth. With 
these upgrades, we are well-prepared to meet the growing demand for high-quality Alloy, Carbon, and Special Steel 
products across industries. This expansion highlights our vision of building a stronger, more competitive steel business 
while contributing to India’s industrial progress." 
 
This brownfield expansion highlights Ramsarup Industries Limited’s focus on increasing production efficiency, 
leveraging high top pressure (1.3 bar) and high-temperature hot blast (1250°C) technology for greater coal injection 
and energy savings. The initiative reinforces the company’s strategic vision of delivering superior quality steel while 
adopting environmentally responsible practices. 
 
About Shyam Metalics  
 
Shyam Metalics is a leading and fastest-growing integrated metal-producing company based in India primarily in the 
steel Industry in West Bengal, Odisha, Jharkhand and Madhya Pradesh with a focus on Long Steel Products, Ferro 
Alloys, Aluminium and Stainless Steel. The company got listed itself on the exchanges in 2021 and as on date of this 
press release possesses a market capitalization of ₹ 25,554 Cr. Spearheaded by Mr. B. Bhushan Agarwal, Chairman 
and Managing Director, the company strives to deliver unparalleled quality through their customised value-added 
solutions to meet business requirements. Headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal, the company is amongst the 
largest producers of ferro alloys in terms of installed capacity in India (Source: CRISIL Report).
