Press Release                                            October  15, 202 6 
 
South Indian Bank Records Highest Ever Quarterly Net Profit of Rs. 426.13 Crore in 
Q2 FY 2026 -27 
 
The South Indian Bank Ltd. declared Net Profit of Rs . 426.13 Cr . for Q2 FY 2026 -27 registering a 
growth of 21.28 % compared to Rs. 351.37 Cr . in Q2 FY 2025 -26.  
 
Key Highlights of Q2 FY 202 6-27 
 
➢ Highest ever Net Interest Income of Rs . 1,116 Cr . with a growth of 37.95% on Y -o-Y basis  
➢ Operating Profit increased to Rs . 732 Cr . with a growth of 37% on Y -o-Y basis  
➢ Net Interest Margin improved from 2.80% to 3.33% on Y -o-Y basis  
➢ GNPA came down by 169 bps from 2.93% to 1.24% on Y -o-Y basis  
➢ NNPA dropped by 47 bps from 0.56 % to 0.09 % on Y -o-Y basis  
➢ PCR excl. write off increased by 1,113 bps from 81.29 % to 92.42 % Y -o-Y basis  
➢ PCR including write off increased by 760 bps from 90.25 % to 97.84 % Y -o-Y basis  
 
o Deposits  
 
➢ Retail Deposit grew by Rs . 16,728 Cr . from Rs . 1,12,625 Cr . to Rs . 1,29,353 Cr ., showing an 
increase of 14.85 % on Y -o-Y basis  
➢ NRI Deposit grew by Rs . 5,008 Cr . from Rs . 33,195 Cr . to Rs . 38,203 Cr ., showing an increase 
of 15.09 % on Y -o-Y basis  
➢ CASA grew by 17.56 % on Y -o-Y basis with growth in Savings Bank by 14.34 % and Current 
Account by 31.19 % respectively  
 
o Advances  
 
➢ Gross advances grew by Rs. 17,231 Cr . from Rs . 92,286 to Rs . 109,517 Cr ., showing an 
increase of 18.67 % on Y -o-Y basis  
➢ Corporate Segment grew by Rs . 6,156 Cr . from Rs . 37,008 Cr . to Rs. 43,164 Cr . showing an 
increase of 16.63% on Y -o-Y basis  
➢ 99.10% of Large Corporate Segments are rated A & Above

About South Indian Bank  
South Indian Bank is a leading Kerala -based Private Sector Bank with a nationwide presence. The Bank’s 
shares are listed on The Stock Exchange Mumbai (BSE) and The National Stock Exchange of India Ltd., 
Mumbai (NSE). South Indian Bank has 959 branches, 127 7 ATMs/CRMs across India, and a 
Representative Office in Dubai, UAE. South Indian Bank is a pioneer in technology -based banking, 
offering an array of digital products and services. Over 98% of all transactions are done through digital 
channels. It has one o f the youngest workforces in the banking sector in the country.
