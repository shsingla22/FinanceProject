TVS MOTOR COMPANY ROLLS OUT TVS KING 
KARGO HD CNG  
◼ Rollout begins in key markets in phase wise manner with an 
introductory price of  Rs. 2,70,648/- (Ex-showroom , Delhi )* 
◼ Designed for efficient last -mile cargo operations with higher  load 
carrying capacity , comfort and cost of ownership.  
Bengaluru, February  5, 2026: TVS Motor Company, a global leader in two and three -wheeler 
manufacturing , today announced the availability of the TVS King Kargo HD CNG . The rollout will 
commence in Delhi NCR, Haryana, Rajasthan , Gujarat , Maharashtra, Karnataka  and Uttar 
Pradesh  in a phase wise manner.  The TVS King Kargo HD CNG is available at an introductory  
price of Rs. 2,70,648 /- (Ex-showroom , Delhi )*.  
Powertrain  & Key Features  
Powered by a 301.1 cc liquid -cooled engine , the TVS King Kargo HD CNG delivers 9.1 kW of 
power  and 22.4 Nm of torque , ensuring consistent and reliable performance even under heavy 
load, making it ideal for demanding last -mile cargo operations across urban and semi -urban 
markets . 
Key Features:  
• Best -in-segment 6.6 ft load deck  for higher load carrying capacity  
• Industry -first fully rolling windows  for improved in -cabin comfort  
• Power gear  enhancing gradability, mileage and ease of driving under load  
• Engineered for efficient last -mile cargo operations  in congested city conditions  
Durability & Comfort  
Built for durability and ease of operation, the vehicle comes with a robust leaf spring suspension . 
It offers  an upscale and ergonomic cabin, with a top speed of 62 km/h, ground clearance of 235 
mm and a turning radius of 3,420 mm. The TVS King Kargo HD CNG is backed by a 3 -year / 1.5 
lakh km warranty, reinforcing TVS Motor Company’s commitment to quality and lon g-term 
customer assurance.

Service touchpoints  
The vehicle is backed by a robust service ecosystem comprising over 470 service touchpoints 
across authorized main dealers, branches, dealerships, and service centers , along with 3 years 
of roadside assistance for added peace of mind . This is further strengthened by a well -established 
secondary support network for private garage mechanics, ensuring wide accessibility, quicker 
turnaround, and dependable after -sales support across markets.  
 
Commenting on the launch,  Rajat Gupta, Business Head - Commercial Mobility, TVS Motor 
Company,  said,  “The TVS King Kargo HD CNG has been developed to address the real -world 
needs of last -mile cargo operators. With segment -first features, higher load capacity and focus 
on driver comfort, it enables customers to improve operational efficiency while reducing  fatigue 
and overall cost of ownership.”  
With this launch, TVS Motor Company continues to deliver reliable, efficient and future -ready 
mobility solutions that support customer livelihoods, in line with its brand promise of ‘ Thakaan 
Kam. Kamaai Zyada’  (Less tired. More income).
