Information Note   
 
Indegene Recognized Among  Top 50 Companies with Great Managers  
in the Great Manager Awards 2024  by People Business  
 
Bengaluru, India, December 17, 2024: Indegene, a digital -first life sciences commercialization company, announced that 
it has been recognized a mong the Top 50 Companies with Great Managers in the Great Manager Awards 2024  by People 
Business. This is Indegene ’s second consecutive year of winning this recognition.  
 
This prestigious hono ur is a testament to the exceptional leadership, unwavering dedication, and positive impact 
Indegene has  created within the organization and beyond.  Since 2020, Indegene has consistently ranked among the Top 
100 Companies with Great Managers and Top 100 Great Managers showcasing a dedication to leadership excellence. This 
is an annual study undertaken by People Business.   
 
Indegene emerged as a standout organization, with two winners - Radha S. Nair and Kanchan Kumari  - among the Top 100 
Great Managers for the second consecutive year . This recognition further highlights Indegene’s  dedication to fostering 
an inclusive environment where talent can thrive. This achievement solidifies Indegene’s reputation as a leader in building 
managerial excellence and empowering individuals to achieve their full potential.  
 
With 6 operation hubs and 18 offices across North America, Europe, and Asia, Indegene’s client base includes the world’s 
top 20 biopharma companies, many emerging biotech firms, and medical device companies. With over 5,000 employees 
worldwide, Indegene’s  vast global footprint underscores its ability to scale and meet the evolving needs of the world’s 
leading pharma organizations across markets.
