Aerolloy Technologies Signs Landmark Agreement with Airbus for Titanium Castings 
for A320neo, A330neo and A350 Programmes  
 
Fully Machined, Ready -to-Fit Titanium Castings to be Produced through Aerolloy’s 
Integrated Material -to-Component Capability  
 
Key Highlights  
 
● Landmark agreement with Airbus.  Aerolloy Technologies Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary 
of PTC Industries Limited, has signed an agreement with Airbus for the development, 
production and supply of Titanium castings for Airbus aircraft programmes.  
● Covers major Airbus aircraft families.  The agreement relates to Titanium casting 
requirements for the  A320neo, A330neo and A350 programmes , representing some of the 
world’s most important commercial aircraft platforms.  
● Fully machined, ready -to-fit supply.  Aerolloy will supply Titanium castings in a  fully 
machined, ready -to-fit condition , manufactured through its integrated route from Titanium 
material to finished aerospace components.  
● Pathway to serial production.  The agreement establishes the development, qualification and 
industrialisation pat hway for Aerolloy to progress into production supply, subject to 
successful completion of Airbus’ qualification and programme requirements.  
● Strengthening PTC ONE™.  The agreement is a strong validation of  PTC ONE™ — From Melt 
to Mission™ , demonstrating Aero lloy’s ability to combine Titanium material capability, 
precision casting, machining, inspection and aerospace execution within one integrated 
system.  
● Make in India, Make for the World.  The agreement supports the creation of advanced 
Titanium aerospace com ponents in India for global commercial aircraft programmes.  
 
Why This Matters  
 
This is a landmark development because Aerolloy is moving beyond standalone casting supply into a

For PTC Industries, it validates the long -term investments made in Titanium materials, precision

Lucknow, India — August 06  2026  
 
Aerolloy Technologies Limite d, a wholly owned subsidiary of PTC Industries Limited, today 
announced that it has signed a landmark agreement with  Airbus  for the development, production 
and supply of  Titanium castings  for the  A320neo, A330neo and A350 aircraft programmes .

The agreement follows Airbus’ evaluation process, under which Aerolloy has been selected to 
undertake development and industrialisation activities for Titanium casting requirements across 
these programmes. The scope includes development, qualification and production activities, with 
qualification forming an important milestone towards serial supply.  
 
Under this agreement, Aerolloy will manufacture Titanium castings through its integrated route — 
beginning with Titanium material produced by Aerolloy and conti nuing through precision casting, 
machining, inspection and delivery in a  fully machined, ready -to-fit condition . 
 
This marks an important step in Aerolloy’s evolution as an integrated aerospace manufacturing 
partner and reinforces PTC Industries’ strategy o f participating in a larger and more valuable part of 
the global aerospace value chain.  
 
A Landmark Material -to-Component Agreement  
 
The significance of this agreement lies in the completeness of the manufacturing responsibility.  
 
Aerolloy will manufacture Titanium material, convert that material into complex Titanium castings, 
machine the castings and deliver them in ready -to-fit condition for Airbus aircraft programmes. This 
positions Aerolloy beyond conventional casting supply and into a more integrated r ole in commercial 
aerospace manufacturing.  
 
In aerospace supply chains, critical stages such as material production, casting, machining and 
inspection are often spread across multiple suppliers and geographies. Aerolloy’s integrated model 
brings these stages together within one manufacturing environ ment, supporting stronger 
traceability, process control, quality consistency and supply -chain resilience.  
 
This is strategically important for Aerolloy: Aerolloy gains a deeper role in global aerospace 
manufacturing .

PTC ONE™ — From Melt to Mission™  
 
This agreement is a direct expression of  PTC ONE™ — From Melt to Mission™ . 
For this programme, the integrated route is:  
 
Titanium material manufactured by Aerolloy  
→ Titanium Alloy production  
→ Precision Titanium castings  
→ Machining and inspection  
→ Fully machined, ready -to-fit aerospace components  
 
PTC ONE™ was created to bring material capability, manufacturing depth, quality systems, machining 
and execution discipline into one connected system. The agreement with Airbus demonstrates that 
this integr ated model is being recognised by one of the world’s leading aircraft manufacturers for 
major commercial aircraft programmes.  
 
Mr. Sachin Agarwal , Chairman & Managing Director, PTC Industries Limited, said:  
 
“This agreement with Airbus is a landmark moment  for Aerolloy Technologies and PTC Industries. 
Airbus represents the highest standards of global commercial aerospace, and being selected for 
Titanium castings across the A320neo, A330neo and A350 programmes is a strong validation of the 
capability we have  built.  
 
What makes this agreement especially significant is the integrated nature of the responsibility. 
Aerolloy will manufacture these components from its own Titanium material, convert that material 
into precision castings, machine them, inspect them an d deliver them in a fully machined, ready -to-
fit condition.  
 
This is exactly the purpose of  PTC ONE™ — From Melt to Mission™ : to build capabilities that are not 
isolated, but connected — material, metallurgy, manufacturing, quality, machining and execution 
working as one system.  
 
This agreement takes Aerolloy deeper into the global commercial aerospace value chain and is a clear 
example of  Make in India, Make for the World . We are proud to support Airbus programmes and 
remain committed to building an advanced  manufacturing institution from India for the world.”  
 
Strategic Significance  
 
This agreement marks another important step in PTC Industries’ transformation into an integrated 
advanced manufacturing company serving global aerospace, defence, space and ener gy markets.  
 
The aerospace industry increasingly requires suppliers who can offer greater integration, traceability, 
reliability and supply -chain resilience. Aerolloy’s Titanium route directly addresses this requirement 
by combining material production, pre cision casting and machining within one manufacturing 
environment.
