Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
With reference to the captioned subject, we wish to inform that the Board of Directors of the 
Company, at its meeting held today, i.e., August 8, 2026, have considered the proposal to corporatize 
Sabyasachi Calcutta LLP into a newly incorporated company viz. Sabyasachi India Limited and 
approved its capitalization through infusion of ~ Rs. 5.10 crore for ~51% of the equity share capital of
