Bengaluru, August 25, 2026:  Building on the milestone of one million  customers for TVS 
iQube,  TVS Motor Company, part of TVS VENU and a globally reputed manufacturer of two 
and three- wheelers, today introduced  the TVS iQube MillionR special edition. Inspired by 
the trust and confidence of over one million customers who have embraced electric mobility 
with TVS iQube, the new MillionR edition marks this achievement while reinforcing TVS 
iQube’s position as India’s favourite family EV .  
As the flagship electric scooter in TVS Motor's EV portfolio, TVS iQube has been instrumental 
in strengthening the company's leadership in the electric two- wheeler category, contributing 
to TVS Motor emerging as the No. 1 player by market share this year.  
Based on the popular TVS iQube 3.5 kWh 
variant, the MillionR Special Edition offers 
an IDC range of 145 km . It boasts of  an 
exclusive Aurora Green Dual Tone colour inspired by the Northern Lights, along with 
signature design elements including a premium  seat upholstery and exclusive 
MillionR badging. The special edition 
further elevates everyday convenience 
with a new front utility storage 
compartment, taking the total storage 
capacity from 32 litres to 35.5 litres, 
complemented by an integrated Type C 
charging port and over 118 connected 
technology features.  
From TVS Jupiter , TVS Motor’s  long-standing flagship scooter brand, to  TVS iQube , India’s 
favourite family EV , the company has continued to translate these insights into mobility 
solutions across powertrains.  
Commenting on the launch, Aniruddha Haldar, Senior Vice President – Head Commuter 
& EV Business and Head Corporate Brand & Media, TVS Motor Company said, “TVS 
Motor has played a significant  role in the scooterisation of India. Over the years, we have 
remained focused on creating mobility solutions that move Indian families forward, irrespective 
of the drivetrain, with a commitment to relevance, reliability and best -in-class quality. The 9 
million achievement of TVS Jupiter and the one million milestone of TVS iQube mark 
significant chapters in this journey, reinforcing our focus on evolving with the needs of Indian families. In 2016, TVS Jupiter celebrated reaching one million customers with the MillionR 
Edition. Today, TVS iQube joins this legacy with its own MillionR Edition as its community 
reaches one million. I thank every customer who has placed their trust in TVS iQube. Their TVS iQube MillionR Special Edition

About TVS Motor Company 
 
TVS Motor Company (BSE:532343 and NSE: TVSMOTOR),  part of TVS VENU, is a reputed 
two and three -wheeler manufacturer globally, championing progress through sustainable 
mobility with four state -of-the-art manufacturing facilities located in India and Indones ia.

making internationally accepted products of the highest quality through innovative and 
sustainable processes. TVS Motor is the only two- wheeler company to have won the

IQS and APEAL surveys. We have been ranked No. 1 Company in the J.D. Power Customer

across 90 countries in which we operate.
