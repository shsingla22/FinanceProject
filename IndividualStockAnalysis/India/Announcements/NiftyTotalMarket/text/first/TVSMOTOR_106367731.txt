TVS MOTOR COMPANY TO PASS ON THE FULL BENEFIT OF GST 
RATE REDUCTION TO CUSTOMERS . 
 
Bengaluru,  September 08, 2025:  TVS Motor Company  (TVSM) will pass on the full 
benefit of GST rate reduction to customers across its ICE portfolio, following the recent 
reforms introduced by the GST Council. With the Council reducing GST rates on ICE 
vehicles from 28% to 18%, there will be significant  savings on our products.   
 
This landmark decision by the govt, appreciably  enhances the accessibility of our ICE 
products. Electric vehic les remain unaffected and continue to enjoy the concessional rate 
of 5% GST.  The benefits will be available to customers starting September 22, 2025.  
 Commenting on the benefits,  KN Radhakrishnan, Director & CEO, TVS Motor 
Company,  said: “The GST rate rationalisation is a bold and transformative move that will 
accelerate consumption across society.  We sincerely thank the Government of India for 
these progressive reforms. T he Government’s consistent efforts to boost growth and 
strengthen the middle class’s spending power are vital steps towards realising the Hon’ble Prime Minister’s vision of Viksit Bharat 2047. We will pass on the the full benefit of GST 
rate reduction to customers across our  ICE portfolio . TVS Motor  remain s committed to 
providing mobility solutions that are trusted, reliable and provide unparalleled user 
experience.”  
 The Company will be undertaking extensive communication measures to apprise customers of the benefits accruing from GST rate reductions.  
 TVSM reaffirms its commitment to making a wide range of mobility solutions available to 
customer and contribut e to the mission of Viks it Bharat 2047.
