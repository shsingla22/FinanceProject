Crizac Strengthens Its Position in Mexico and Expands into the 
Netherlands with Acquisition of Inova Education
Founder Eric Wijmenga joins Crizac as Regional Director, UK and Europe, to lead the company's 
expansion into the Netherlands and wider Europe. 
Kolkata, 28th July: Crizac Limited, one of India's leading technology-driven international student recruitment 
platforms, today announced that it has entered into an agreement via its wholly owned Subsidiary Crizac Ltd. to 
acquire 100% of the shareholding of Inova Consultancy Limited (“Inova Education”), a move that expands its 
presence in Mexico. The acquisition strengthens Crizac's university partnerships across the UK and Europe and 
marks the company's entry into the Netherlands as a new European destination market, reinforcing its ambition 
to build one of the world's leading international education platforms. 
As part of the acquisition, Crizac will acquire the business of Inova Consultancy Limited, including its 
recruitment platform, university partnership portfolio, commercial contracts, operational processes, business 
infrastructure and associated goodwill. Inova will continue to operate independently under Crizac's ownership. 
Eric Wijmenga, Founder of Inova Education, will join Crizac as Regional Director, UK and Europe, where he 
will lead Crizac's UK and Europe recruitment strategies, strengthen university partnerships, support business 
development, and drive regional growth initiatives.  
Inova Education has a long-standing presence in student recruitment, university partnerships, and international 
education marketing in Mexico. Over the years, the company has built strong relationships with universities 
across the UK and the Netherlands, with established operations across Mexico and Europe. Its leadership brings 
more than 25 years of combined experience in international education and university partnerships, positioning 
the combined business to accelerate student recruitment from Mexico while building new pathways into the 
Netherlands and across Europe. 
The acquisition is closely aligned with Crizac's long-term strategy of expanding its footprint in high-growth 
international education markets. Combined with Inova's established presence in Mexico, the transaction 
positions Crizac to grow student mobility into the Netherlands and across Europe, and between Europe and its 
other international markets, as the company works toward its ambition of becoming a leading platform 
connecting Mexico with the UK, the Netherlands and wider European higher education.  
By combining Inova's regional market knowledge and university relationships with Crizac's technology-enabled 
platform and international network, the transaction is expected to accelerate student recruitment, deepen 
institutional partnerships and foster greater cross-border collaboration across the education ecosystem. 
Commenting on the acquisition, the leadership teams of both organisations highlighted the strategic and cultural 
alignment between Crizac and Inova, as well as the opportunities the combination creates for universities, 
recruitment partners and students. 
“This acquisition reflects our continued commitment to investing in quality partnerships and 
sustainable international growth. Inova's relationships with universities and its deep operational 
experience across Mexico and the Netherlands complement Crizac's global scale, digital capabilities 
and international reach, and together we will create greater opportunities for universities, 
recruitment partners and students. 
Mexico and the Netherlands represent increasingly important markets within global education, and 
this acquisition strengthens our ability to connect institutions with high-quality students across these 
corridors. This is another important step towards our vision of becoming one of the world's leading 
international education platforms.” 
— Dr Vikash Agarwal, Chairman & Managing Director, Crizac Limited 
“I've spent more than 25 years working in higher education, including building relationships with 
universities across Europe. Joining Crizac gives that work a global platform, technology and reach 
to support many more students and institutions, in Mexico and as we expand further into Europe. 
We share a common commitment to innovation, quality partnerships and student success, and I'm 
excited about the opportunities this partnership will create for universities, recruitment partners and 
students.” — Eric Wijmenga, Founder, Inova Education; Regional Director, UK and Europe, Crizac Ltd
