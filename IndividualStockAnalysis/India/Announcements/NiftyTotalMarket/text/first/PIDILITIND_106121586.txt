03PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW
➢Current  quarter’s  revenue  growth  of 9.3% was aided  by UVG of 9.7% across  categories  and geographies .
➢Consumer  and Bazaar  (‘C&B’)  UVG was 7.3%, while  Business  to Business  (‘B2B’) maintained  growth  
momentum  with UVG of 21.7%.
➢Gross  Margins  improved  by 100 bps year on year, primarily  due to benign  input  prices .
➢With  stepped  up A&SP  spends,  EBITDA  Margins  were  24.3% as compared  to 25.1% in Q3 FY24.
➢For nine months  of the current  financial  year, UVG was 9.2% with C&B UVG of 7.0% and B2B UVG of 20.2%. 
➢Gross  Margins  improved  by 284 bps over previous  year, on account  of lower  input  costs . EBITDA  Margins  
were  24.5%, compared  to 23.7% in previous  year.
