Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
In terms of the above-referred provisions of SEBI LODR read with the Master Circular no. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 (“SEBI Circular”) and our letters 
dated October 18, 2023, January 30, 2024 and January 10, 2025 pertaining to Jaipur Stamp Duty 
matter, we wish to inform that the Company has filed a Writ Petition against the State of Rajasthan 
before the Hon’ble Rajasthan High Court, challenging the calculation/levy of stamp duty on its 
property in Rajasthan, which is likely to be listed for hearing on Tuesday, April 08, 2025. 
 
Furthermore, while the Company was in the process of filing the Writ Petition, it also received a 
Demand Notice from the DIG Stamps, Rajasthan dated March 5, 2025 to which the Company 
submitted its response on March 26, 2025 informing the DIG Stamps that the Company had 
assailed the Order dated March 6, 2023 and requested that no further steps or actions be taken in 
pursuance of the said Demand Notice.  
 
We are enclosing herewith the brief details of the aforesaid changes as prescribed under SEBI 
LODR read with SEBI circular is enclosed herewith as Annexure – 1 .
