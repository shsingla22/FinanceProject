Affairs (MCA) and SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/DDHS/P/CIR/2023/0164 dated 6th October 2023 and SEBI/HO/CFD/

Circulars”), companies are allowed to hold AGM through VC/Other Audio-Visual Means (VC/OAVM), without the 
physical presence of Members at a common venue. Hence, in compliance with the Circulars, the 28th Annual 
General Meeting (AGM) of the Company is being held through VC.

her behalf and the proxy need not be a Member of the Company. However, since the AGM is being held in 
accordance with the Circulars through VC, the facility for the appointment of proxies by the Members will not 
be available and hence, the Proxy Form and Attendance Slip are not annexed to this Notice.
