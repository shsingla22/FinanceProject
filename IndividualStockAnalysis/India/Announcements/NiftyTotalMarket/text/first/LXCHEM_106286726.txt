We wish to inform you about the following changes in the Senior Leadership Team in compliance with the 
provisions of Regulation 30(4) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015: 
 
a. Retirement of Mr. Prashant Patil  
 
Mr. Prashant Patil will be retiring from his position as President – Manufacturing effective close of 
business on July 31, 2025, in line with the Company’s age-based retirement policy. During his tenure, 
Mr. Patil has played a pivotal role in driving operational excellence and contributing significantly to the 
growth and success of our organisation. We extend our heartfelt gratitude for his invaluable 
contributions and dedicated service, and we wish him the very best in his future endeavours. 
 
b. Appointment of Mr. Rajesh Naik  
 
Mr. Rajesh Naik has been appointed as President - Manufacturing, in the category of Senior 
Management Personnel of the Company w.e.f. August 1, 2025. He will succeed Mr. Prashant Patil and 
lead the Operations function. 
 
The details as required to be disclosed under the SEBI Listing Regulations, read with SEBI Master Circular 
dated November 11, 2024, have been provided in Annexure A  to this letter. Kindly take the above 
information on record. 
 
This is for your information and records. We kindly request you to take the above on record.
