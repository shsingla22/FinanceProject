Karur Vysya Bank Strengthens Presence in Maharashtra with New Branch in 
Kalyan  
Mumbai , Novem ber 20, 2026: Karur Vysya Bank (KVB), one of India's leading private 
sector banks, today inaugurated its new branch at Kalyan, Maharashtra. With this addition, 
the Bank's branch network has increased to 930 branches across the country, further 
strengthening its presence in key markets . 
The new branch was inaugurated by Shri. Dhananjay Thorat, Asst. Commissioner, 
Kalyan Dombivli Municipal Corporation, 2 B Ward Office, Kalyan, in the presence 
of Dr. Smita Patil Zope, MD, Pediatric Neurologist, Jupiter Hospital, Dombivli, 
who lit the traditional Gayathri lamp.  
The branch is located at  Balaji Aqua,  Shop No.6, Ground Floor, Khadakpada Circle, Kalyan 
(W), Mumbai – 421301, Maharashtra.  
Shri Ramesh Babu, Managing Director & CEO, Karur Vysya Bank,  “The opening of 
our new branch in Kalyan marks another important milestone in our efforts to strengthen 
our presence across Maharashtra. The region offers significant opportunities for growth, 
supported by its vibrant business ecosystem and diverse customer base. We remain 
committed to expanding our reach through strategically located branches and robust digital  
banking services, ensuring greater accessibility, personalised banking experiences and 
comprehensive financial solutions for individuals, entrepreneurs and businesses.”  
The expansion comes on the back of continued growth in the Bank’s business. As of 
September 30, 2026, KVB’s total business stood at ₹2,39,712 crore, comprising deposits of 
₹1,28,123 crore and advances of ₹1,11,589 crore. The Bank reported a quarterly net profit 
of ₹788 crore in Q2 FY27, registering a year -on-year growth of 37.28%.  
Through t he expansion of its branch network and continued investment in digital banking, 
Karur Vysya Bank remains focused on enhancing customer convenience, deepening its 
regional presence and meeting the evolving banking needs of individuals and businesses 
across the country.

crore and advances of ₹1,11,589 crore. The Bank reported its highest -ever net profit of
