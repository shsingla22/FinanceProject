Hyundai Motor India Limited Q2 FY26 Financial Resul ts 
 
Q2 PAT stood at INR 15,723 Mn. up 14.3% yoy,  EBITDA at 13.9%  
 
Gurugram | October 30, 2025:  The Board of Directors of Hyundai Motor India 
Limited (HMIL) today approved the unaudited financi al results (Standalone and 
Consolidated) for the second quarter and half year of FY 2025-26. 
 
Q2 Highlights: 
 GST 2.0 reforms & vibrant festive boost, domestic  volume up 5.5% on qoq 
basis. 
 Highest-ever Domestic SUV Contribution at 71.1%.  
 Riding rural thrust with highest-ever contribution at 23.6%.  
 Robust Export momentum, volumes up 21.5% on y-o-y basis, 27% export 
contribution in overall sales volumes. 
 Revenue at INR 174,608 Mn.  (up 1.2% yoy) & EBITDA at INR 24,289 Mn. (up 
10.1% yoy).  
 EBITDA margin at 13.9% (up 113 bps yoy) , driven by favourable product & export 
mix and cost optimization efforts. 
 Profit after tax stood at INR 15,723 Mn., up 14.3% on yoy basis.
