About GLP-1 Drug Category 
GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1) receptor agonists are well-established prescription medications for the 
management of type 2 diabetes and obesity . They work by enhancing insulin secretion, reducing glucagon 
levels and help regulate appetite. They have been shown to be effective at decreasing haemoglobin A1c 
(HbA1c), promoting weight loss and reducing the risk  of cardiovascular events by mimicking the hormone 
GLP-1.
