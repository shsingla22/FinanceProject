Press Release  
 
Gokaldas Exports'  consolidated second -quarter FY27 Total Income stood at ₹1,135 crores, 
up by 13% YoY; EBITDA stood at ₹127 crores, up by 52% YoY ; EBITDA  margins  improved  to 
11.2% during the quarter . 
 
Gokaldas Exports registered a total income of ₹1,135 crores in 2QFY27, with a 13% YoY growth in a seasonally 
weak quarter. Total income from the India business grew by 10% YoY on a strong order book and superior 
execution. Africa business delivered robust growth of 32% YoY , driven by higher volumes and better 
realisations.  EBITDA during the quarter stood at ₹127 crores, up 52% YoY . While an improved product mix and 
productivity gains supported this growth, it was also aided by a lower base in the corresponding quarter last 
year, which was impacted by US tariff.  An increase in wages, coupled with higher factor costs driven by rising 
oil prices, capped the gains.  
 
In 1HFY27, total income stood at ₹2,315 crores with a 17% YoY growth. Total income from the India business 
grew by 1 3% YoY , benefiting from a higher order book and improved execution . Africa business registered a 
growth of 39% YoY during the period , on the back of higher volume and realisations . EBITDA during the period 
grew by 32% YoY , driven by better product mix, higher productivity gains , and aided by a lower base in the 
corresponding quarter last year, which was impacted by US  tariff .  
 
On the balance sheet front, net debt declined to ₹429 crore as of September 2026, compared to ₹553 crore 
as of March 2026, reflecting an improvement in the company's net debt position during H1 FY27.

About Gokaldas Exports : 
Established in 1979, Gokaldas Exports has evolved into a one -stop solution for some of the world’s most 
recognized apparel brands. Gokaldas Exports is one of India’s largest manufacturers and exporters of apparel, 
exporting to more than 50 countries , and  currently has over 30+ production units that can produce about 92 
million garments annually. 5 5,000+ employees work together to deliver  the Company’s vision of being a 
globally reputed apparel manufacturer and exporter, with an emphasis on innovation and sustainability.
