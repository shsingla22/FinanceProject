Media Release  
Adani Airports raises ~USD 300 million  primary equity from 
360 ONE Asset  
Fresh equity takes Adani Airport Holdings Limited’s total raise to ~USD 1.35 billion  
 
Editor’s Synopsis : 
• Adani Airport Holdings Limited’s (AAHL) binding agreement with 360 ONE Asset to 
raise ₹2,790 crores ( ~USD 300 million ). 
• AAHL had the first round of binding agreement of ~USD 1 billion of primary equity capital from a consortium of Alpha Wave Global, Premji Invest, Temasek and BlackRock -managed funds  (Initial Investors), at a pre- money equity valuation of 
~USD 18 billion . 
• Total AAHL equity raise now stands at ~USD 1.35 billion.  
• Upon completion, the investors will , in aggregate,  hold approximately 7.01%  in AAHL.  
 
Ahmedabad, 23 November 2026 : Adani Airport Holdings Limited (AAHL), a subsidiary of Adani 
Enterprises Limited (AEL)  and one of India's largest private airport operators , has entered into a 
binding agreement with 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited , acting through its funds 
and affiliates ( 360 ONE Asset) , to raise ₹2,790 crores (~ USD 300 million), taking its total equity 
raise to ~USD 1.3 5 billion  so far . 
The transaction builds on an earlier round of ₹9,825 crores (~USD 1 billion) of primary equity 
capital from a consortium of leading domestic and global investors including Alpha Wave Global, 
Premji Invest, Temasek and BlackRock managed fund s. This represent ed one of the largest 
primary equity investments from financial institutions in India’s airport infrastructure sector.  
AAHL and 360 ONE Asset have signed a Share Subscription Agreement for 360 ONE Asset to 
subscribe to new equity shares in AAHL on the same terms and valuation as the Initial Investors, 
implying a pre -money equity valuation of ~ USD 18 billion. 360 ONE Asset has also acceded to the 
Shareholders’ Agreement entered with the Initial Investors. Following completion of both 
transactions, the investors will collectively own approximately 7.01% of AAHL. The transaction is subject to customary conditions precedent, including receipt of applicable approval s. 
“This partnership  is yet another milestone in building out the Adani Airports platform. Having 
such marquee, long -term investors on board is a testament to their confidence in India’s aviation 
growth story,” said Mr. Jeet Adani, Non -Executive Director, Adani Airport Holdings Limited. “We 
are committed to developing a complete ecosystem around our airports by transforming them from simple transit hubs into vibrant airport cities that merge aviation, commerce, leisure, and regional logistics to drive sustaine d national growth,” he added.  
The fresh round, which brings 360 ONE Asset alongside Alpha Wave Global, Premji Invest, 
Temasek and BlackRock managed funds, reflects the institutional endorsement of AAHL’s scale, 
operating capabilities and long -term growth potential.  
 
 
These multiple rounds of capital injection are  dedicated to three major strategic priorities that 
will shape the future of the company’s aviation and infrastructure portfolio  as it aspires to handle 
double its historical passenger handling capacity of ~96 million, scaling to 200 million annually .  
AAHL has initiated a massive modernisation and upgrade program across its entire airport 
network. This extensive campaign will be driven by the development of Adani Airport Cities, 
bringing world -class infrastructure to each location, in addition to rampin g up its passenger -
facing services and other vital non -aeronautical businesses to deepen commercial monetisation 
and elevate the overall passenger journey. By seamlessly blending infrastructure with retail and 
hospitality, the initiative will ultimately st rengthen and secure the group's integrated airport 
ecosystem.  
About Adani Airport Holdings Limited  
Adani Airport Holdings Limited (AAHL), a subsidiary of Adani Enterprises Limited, is one of India's largest 
private airport operators, managing eight airports across India and serving over 23% of India’s total 
passenger traffic.  AAHL manages and develops an integrated airports platform spanning aeronautical 
operations, passenger -facing non -aeronautical offerings and city -side development . AAHL’s pioneering 
journey blends advanced technology, memorable consumer experiences and sustainable practices in airport development.
 
About Adani Enterprises Limited  
Adani Enterprises Limited (AEL) is the flagship company of Adani Group, one of India’s largest business organisations. AEL has a long -standing track record of incubating, building and scaling infrastructure and 
utility businesses of strategic importance to  India.  
Over the years, AEL has developed businesses including Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone, Adani Energy Solutions, Adani Power, Adani Green Energy and Adani Total Gas into sizeable platforms. AEL’s current portfolio of emerging businesses spans airports , roads, data centres, the green hydrogen 
ecosystem, copper and other strategic industrial sectors, each aligned with India’s long -term infrastructure 
and economic development priorities.
