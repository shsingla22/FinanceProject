PRESS RELEASE  
 
Newgen witnesses healthy revenue growth  at 5% YoY ; Revenues reached  ₹ 422 
crore s in Q 2 FY'27  
Margins maintained in business with operational efficiencies and cost controls . PAT Margin at 
20.6% in Q2 FY ’27 
 
New Delhi, India, Oct 23 , 2026:  Newgen Software Technologies Ltd. today announced its financial 
results for the second  quarter ended September 30 , 2026, reinforcing  its commitment to helping 
organizations orchestrate intelligent enterprises . 
Key Highlights - Q2 FY’27 v/s Q2 FY’26 
 
 
 
 
Commenting on the Company's performance, Mr. T.S. Varadarajan,  Co-Founder and 
Executive Vice Chairman , Newgen Software Technologies Ltd., said: “Q2 FY’27 was a 
quarter of healthy  revenue growth, resilient margins and a stronger recurring -revenue 
mix. Revenues witnessed a growth of 5% to reach  Rs 422 crores  and Profit after Tax  grew 
by 6% YoY to reach Rs 87 crores. We continue to focus on enhancing our operational 
efficiencies and cost controls.  
With 34 years of experience in serving regulated industries, Newgen is well positioned to 
address evolving customer requirements through its governed AI platform, industry -
specific AI solutions , and focus on delivering measurable business value to customers . For 
the first half of the year, we had 10 AI -based deal bookings across Banking and Financial 
Services and Government . We remain committed  to investing  in NewgenONE and 
advancing its AI -led orchestration capabilities , enabling  enterprises to transition  from 
fragmented automation to governed , intelligent execution. ”
