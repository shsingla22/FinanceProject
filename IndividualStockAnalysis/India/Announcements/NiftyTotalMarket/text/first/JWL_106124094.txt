Jupiter Electric Mobility and Porter Unite to Sign a MoU to Empower Drivers 
Through JEM Udaan Program  
Revolutionizing Last -Mile Logistics with Sustainable Mobility and Entrepreneurial Opportunities  
National: January 24, 2025 : Jupiter Electric Mobility (JEM) , a subsidiary of Jupiter Wagons Limited, 
announces a strategic partnership with Porter a leading Goods Transport Agency offering a wide range of 
intra- city and inter- city logistics solutions This collaboration focuses on fostering entrepreneurial 
opportunities and promoting sustainable electric mobility through JEM's innovative JEM Udaan program . 
Through this partnership, Porter will provide guidance to Drivers and Small Business Owners  who select 
JEM Tez as their platform to switch to electric commercial mobility , JEM’s flagship electric light 
commercial vehicle. Porter will support in onboarding of JEM Drivers and help them unlock business 
growth opportunities through various initiatives which will help maximise revenue earnings for driver 
entrepreneurs. These too ls are designed to support Drivers in scaling their operations, boosting earnings, 
and contribute to a greener, more sustainable logistics ecosystem while uplifting communities.  
Reflecting on the motivation behind this partnership Mr. Vivek Lohia, Managing Director, Jupiter Group  
said, " At Jupiter Electric Mobility, we are driven by a vision to redefine the possibilities of sustainable 
business models in the logistics industry. Our partnership with Porter spotlights this commitment, combining cutting- edge technology with meaningful initiatives to uplift individuals and communities. The 
JEM Udaan program represents another pivotal step in this journey —empowering drivers to become 
entrepreneurs while contributing to a greener future. Together, we aim to create an ecosystem where 
sustainability fuels growth and prosperity, delivering long- term value to all our stakeholders ." 
On this occasion,  Tanuj Khandelwal, VP, Porter, says, " We are excited to be a part of the JEM Udaan 
Programme. This will be a meaningful step towards fostering a green logistics ecosystem. Through this initiative, we aim to empower individuals with entrepreneurial skills and enhance their livelihoods. Our 
collaboration with Jupiter Electric Mobility reflects a mutual commitment to sustainability and 
empowering individuals in logistics."  
As part of this commitment, JEM introduced the JEM Udaan program , a transformative initiative designed 
to empower local small entrepreneurs and drivers to establish and grow their businesses in the electric 
logistics segment. The program provides comprehensive business support, professional training and 
access to essen tial EV resources, guiding entrepreneurs through its dealership network. By facilitating 
connections between drivers, small businesses and companies seeking sustainable logistics solutions, JE M 
promotes electric mobility while fostering societal growth through support for local operators.  
Porter has a large customer base and a network of over 7.5 lakh drivers across 22 cities and offers a 
dependable and scalable logistics network, ensuring seamless order fulfilment as demand grows. While 
optimizing delivery speed and reliability, Porter shares JEM’s commitment to enhancing driver livelihoods. 
This partnership also advances Porter's efforts to support drivers by offering a comprehensive pathway 
to entrepreneurship as well as being sustainable and green. As the demand for electric mobility and  
sustainable delivery solutions grows, this collaboration marks a significant step toward building a more 
efficient, sustainable, and driver- centric model in the logistics industry. Together, JEM and Porter are 
committed to driving positive change for driv ers, businesses, and the environment.

About Porter  
Founded in 2014, Porter is a leading Goods Transport Agency offering a wide range of intra- city and inter -
city logistics solutions. Working towards the company’s inspirational dream of ‘moving a billion dreams, 
one delivery at a time’, Porter has serviced over 1.5 crore customers across 22 cities in India, with a 
workforce of 2,600 employees in the last 10 years. The company strives to improve the lives of over 7.5 lakh owner -drivers by providing them with financial access, affordability and financial indep endence. 
Porter started operations in India as Smartshift Logistics Solutions Private Limited, founded by Pranav 
Goel, Uttam Digga, and Vikas Choudhary. Porter has disrupted various domains of logistics by launching 
an on -demand marketplace for trucks, bik es, Packers & Movers and intercity courier services. For more 
information, please visit https://porter.in/
