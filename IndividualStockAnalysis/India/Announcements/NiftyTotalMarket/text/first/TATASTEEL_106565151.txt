Dear Sir,  
Sub: News Clarification  
 
This is with reference to the e-mail communication dated March 23, 202 6 received from the 
National Stock Exchange of India Limited  (‘NSE’) , seeking clarification from Tata Steel Limited 
(‘Company’) relating to the news item  titled “ Hindustan Zinc partners with Tata Steel to 
integrate low carbon zinc into sustainable steel manufacturing ” dated March 23, 202 6, 
published online at www.business -standard.com   
 
In this connection, we wish to inform you that the Company has entered in a partnership with 
Hindustan Zinc Limited to integrate ‘EcoZen’, a low -carbon zinc solution, into its galvanised 
steel manufacturing. This strategic partnership is entered into in the normal course of business 
and is aligned with the Company’s sustainabili ty goals and low -carbon steel -making ambitions. 
This arrangement does not qualify for disclosure under Regulation 30 read with Schedule III 
of the SEBI (Listing Obligation and Disclo sure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (‘SEBI Listing 
Regulations’).  
We further wish to inform you that t he Company, in the normal course of business, enters  
various strategic, commercial, and operational partnership arrangements / collaborations  from 
time to time  for furtherance of its business objectives . The Company has robust processes to 
comply with its disclosure  obligations and is committed to making accurate and adequate 
disclosures in a timely manner as required by Regulation 30  and 51  of the SEBI Listing  
Regulations read with the Company’s Policy on Determination of Materiality for Disclosure.  
 
This clarification is being provided in terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations . 
 
This is for your information and records.
