Brief profile of Ms. Manasa Gangotri Kata  
 
Ms. Manasa Gangotri Kata , GOI Nominee Director assumed office of Director of the 
Bank on 23.10.2026 . 
 
Ms. Manasa Gangotri Kata is an Indian Revenue Service (Customs and Indirect 
Taxes) officer of the 2007 batch.  
 
She holds a B.Tech. degree in Electrical Engineering from the Indian Institute of 
Technology (IIT), Bombay, and a Master’s degree in Business Law from the 
National Law School of India University (NLSIU), Bengaluru.  
 
During her career, she has served in various capacities in Customs, GST and the 
Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI), including postings in Chennai Customs. 
She was appointed Director in the Department of Financial Services, Ministry of 
Finance, under the Central Staffing Scheme in December 2024.
