Dear Sir, 
 
With reference to our earlier intimation bearing Ref. No. OIL/CO/SE/2021-22/09 dated June 17, 2021, whereby 
the Company had enclosed a press release regarding the appointment of Mr. A. Gururaj as Managing Director 
of Optiemus Electronics Limited (“OEL”), and pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, this is to inform you that the Board of 
Directors of OEL, a Wholly Owned Subsidiary of the Company, at its meeting held today, i.e. on July 1, 2026, 
has taken note of the resignation tendered by Mr. Gururaj Ayekawadi (DIN: 00606019) from the office of 
Managing Director and Key Managerial Personnel of OEL with effect from closure of business hours on June 
30, 2026,  due to some health issues. 
 
To ensure seamless leadership transition and continuity in the operations and strategic direction of OEL, the 
Board of Directors of OEL has,  subject to the approval of the shareholders, approved the appointment of Mr. 
Neetesh Gupta, an existing Non-Executive Director of OEL, as its Managing Director with effect from July 1, 
2026. 
 
Mr. Neetesh Gupta, belongs to the Promoter Group of Optiemus Infracom Limited and holds a Master's Degree 
in Business Management from Nottingham University, England. He possesses over 11 years of experience in 
the telecommunications, electronics’ manufacturing and trading sectors, with expertise in product development, 
sourcing, procurement and operational management across mobile phones, mobile accessories and IoT 
products. Having played a significant role in the growth of the Optiemus Group, he is well positioned to lead 
OEL in its next phase of expansion and further strengthen its manufacturing capabilities in line with the Group's 
long-term vision.
