PFC Consulting Limited (a wholly owned subsidiary of Power Finance Corporation Limited) has been 
nominated as the 'Bid Process Coordinator' (BPC) for selection of developer through tariff based 
competitive bidding for the Independent Transmission Projects (ITPs) by Ministry of Power, Go!. 
As per the provisions of tariff based competitive bidding Guidelines issued by the Ministry of Power, 
the BPC has to prepare the project profile and initiate the process of land acquisition and forest 
clearance, if required. For these activities, a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPY) is required to be 
incorporated which will take up various preparatory activities of the transmission projects e.g. survey 
and preparation ofreport, initialization of the process of acquisition of land and also initiate the process 
of seeking forest clearance, if required. 
The SPY has to be transferred to the Successful Bidder selected through International Competitive 
Bidding Process under Tariff Based Competitive Bidding Guidelines for Transmission Service issued 
by Ministry of Power. 
The Government of Maharashtra vide its Government Resolution No. Sankirna O 125/P. No. 32/Urja-4 
dated 27.02.2026 has notified and intimated that Empowered Committee (EC) in its meeting held on 
12.01.2026 has appointed PFC Consulting Limited (PFCCL) as Bid Process Coordinator (BPC) for 
selection of bidder as Transmission Service Provider (TSP) to establish Intra-State transmission system 
for "Establishment of 765 kV GIS Pune West (District -Pune)" through Tariff Based Competitive �
