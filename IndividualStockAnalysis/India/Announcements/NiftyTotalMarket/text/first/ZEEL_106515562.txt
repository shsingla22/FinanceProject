For Immediate Release  
 
‘Z’ strengthens  its core leadership team ; on-boards Sandeep Mehrotra as 
Chief Operating Officer – Advertisement Revenue  
• Company continues to enhance its Human Capital in line with its ambitions for the future  
• Sandeep to leverage his rich experience with a sharp focus not only on enhancing  the existing 
revenue streams, but also developing new forms of growth  across businesses  
 
 
Mumbai, 2nd February  2026 : Leading Content and Technology powerhouse, Zee 
Entertainment Enterprises Ltd. ( ‘Z’), has further fortified its leadership team with the 
appointment of Sandeep Mehrotra  as Chief Operating Officer – Advertisement Revenue . In 
this role, Sandeep will be responsible for maximizing the advertis ement  revenue generation 
capabilities of the Company by leveraging the convergence between the linear and digital 
business. Sandeep will be based at the Company’s headquarters in Mumbai and  will report 
into the Chief Executive Officer, Punit Goenka.   
Laxmi Shetty will continue to lead the advertisement revenue vertical as Head - 
Advertisement Revenue, Broadcast &  Digital , with a firm focus on further boosting 
advertisement revenue under Sandeep’s  guidance.  
In line with the Company’s ambitions to  build a robust growth trajectory for the future, it is 
implementing the required steps to strengthen its monetization potential  across all aspects. 
As the entertainment landscape evolves and the lines between consumption mediums 
continue to blur, it is pertinent for the Company to enhance the synergies between the linear 
and digital business segments to drive higher revenue generation.  Leveraging his rich 
experience of over 31 years, Sandeep  will be responsible of further enhancing advertisement 
revenue  and building scale with a sharp focus on delivering meaningful value to the 
 
advertisers . He will play a crucial role in  strategizing, innovating, planning and driving revenue 
across the linear and digital business .  
Speaking on this development , Mr. Punit Goenka, CEO, Zee Entertainment  Enterprises Ltd.  
said, "The evolving entertainment landscape demands sharper monetisation strategies that 
are outcome -driven and platform agnostic. As we sharpen our revenue generation capabilities 
to build sustained growth for the future, we are also taking strategic steps to enhance  our 
bench strength with experienced leaders. I am glad to welcome Sandeep, whose deep 
understanding of the advertiser ecosystem and expertise to build integrated monetisation 
frameworks  across linear and digital segments, will play a pivotal role in driving sustainable 
and diversified growth for the Company. I look forward to working with Sandeep and team 
closely, to build a stronger and converged revenue generation model in line with ou r long -term 
ambitions.”  
Speaking about his appointment , Mr. Sandeep Mehrotra, Chief Operating Officer – 
Advertisement Revenue, Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd. said, “I am pleased to join ‘Z’ at 
this pertinent juncture, as the Company builds a robust monetization engine for the future. 
The long -term aspirations of the Company present a unique opportunity to drive higher 
engagement with advertisers and deliver meaningfu l value. ‘Z’ has a robust presence across 
the linear and digital segments, and I look forward to leveraging these strengths to shape a 
furthermore integrated and scalable advertisement revenue ecosystem for the future.”  
Sandeep comes with vast experience in driving large -scale P&L, multi -portfolio monetization 
and enterprise growth across complex, consumer -facing businesses. During his career, he has 
transformed traditional sales models into client -centric, outcome -driven  commercial engines, 
delivering exceptional  revenue performance, market -leading efficiency and sustained 
profitability. He is recognized as a proven builder of high -performance teams and scalable 
 
revenue systems, with deep expertise in sales transformation, integrated go -to-market 
strategy, digital enablement and long -term partnership creation. Sandeep was previously 
associated with Culver Max Entertainment Pvt. Ltd. (formerly known as Sony Pictures 
Networks India ) as the Head of Advertisement Sales - Network Channels.  
With this step , the Company is further enhancing  its Human Capital to build capabilities that 
align with its aspirations for the future. Sandeep’s appointment as the Chief Operating Officer 
– Advertisement Revenue, is with effect from 3rd February 2026.
