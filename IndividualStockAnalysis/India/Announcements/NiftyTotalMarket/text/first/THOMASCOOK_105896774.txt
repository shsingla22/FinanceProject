Thomas Cook India expands retail  distribution 
Opens new outlet in Wanowrie -Pune ; increasing consumer access to 6  
locations across Pune City & 26 in Maharashtra  
 
Mumbai,  June 28, 2024:  Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel services compan y, has 
inaugurated a new franchise outlet in Pune - to leverage the high -growth  market of Pune and the surrounding 
catchments of Wanow rie, National Institute of Bank Management , Magarpatta,  Hadapsar and Kondhwa. This 
outlet expands Thomas Cook India’s netw ork to 6 locations in the city and 26 in Maharashtra State.  
 
In its strategy to increase access and convenience, Thomas Cook’s new Wanowrie outlet wil l benefit a range of 
customers, from business professionals, families, couples, and Young India’s millenni als-GenZ; also GenS 
(seniors).  
 
Thomas Cook’s new outlet offers end- to-end travel services: International & Domestic Holidays (Group Tours, 
Personalised holidays, Cruises, etc.), Value Added Services like Travel Insurance, etc.
