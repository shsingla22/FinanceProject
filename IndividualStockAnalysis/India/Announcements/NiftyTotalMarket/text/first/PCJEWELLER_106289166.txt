The Company is pleased to submit its business update for the quarter ended June 30, 2025. 
 
The recently concluded quarter has been very promising and fulfilling one for the Company. The Company 
closed this quarter on a very strong note clocking robust performance riding on its customer’s trust and

further reduced its outstanding debts towards its bankers by another approximately 7.50% during the quarter.
