 Dear Sirs,  
 
In reference to our letter dated 1 9
th August 2023, intimating the pendency of 
litigation in accordance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with Para B of Part A of Schedule III of SEBI Listing Regulations, we would like to inform that the Company has received an order from the Hon'ble Appellate Tribunal for 
Electricity ( “APTEL ”) vide its Judgment dated August 29, 2025 .  
 APTEL  has allowed the Appeal filed by Mahan Energen Ltd. ( “MEL”)  (erstwhile 
Essar Power M.P. Limited), a subsidiary of the Company, against the claim of relinquishment charge of Rs. 26 32.54 c rores imposed by Central Transmission 
Utility of India Limited (“CTUIL” ) in terms of the order from C entral Electricity 
Regulatory Commission ( “CERC”) dt. October 7, 2019  and declared the claim of 
CTUIL as having been rendered infructuous, and  these relinquishment 
compensation against the Appellant stands extinguished in law.  
 
The detailed disclosure with respect to the above as required under the SEBI 
Master Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 11th November 2024, as amended by SEBI Circular dated 31st December 2024, is enclosed herewith as ‘Annexure – A’. 
 
We request you to take the above on record.
