Sub: Intimation of Compounding Order from the Reserve Bank of India 
 
 This is to inform you that Reserve Bank of India vide its Order dated November 16, 2026, has imposed a 
monetary penalty of Rs. 11,01,752/- (Rupees Eleven Lakhs One Thousand Seven Hundred and Fifty Two 
only) with reference compounding application filed by the Company for contravention of the provisions 
of the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (FEMA) and the regulations issued thereunder. The 
aforesaid contraventions pertain to the period from 2004 to 2016 and primarily relate to the allotment of 
shares to employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOPs), 
as well as delays in filing the Foreign Liabilities and Assets (FLA) Returns for certain financial years.
