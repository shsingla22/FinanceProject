Brief Profile of Shri Satyakam Mishra  
Shri Satyakam Mishra (b -1963), IRS, serving nearly 37 years in various positions in the Income Tax 
Department retired recently.  He possesses profound expertise in the areas of Accountancy, 
Administration, Corporate Finance, Management, Policy Formulation, Regulatory Framework and 
Taxation.  
Before his retirement from IRS, he served as Principal Chief Commissioner of Income Tax 
(International Taxation), Delhi over about 3 years.  
 
During his role as Principal Chief Commissioner of Income Tax (Income Tax), he was overseeing the tax 
framework for Non -Residents and Foreign Companies at all India jurisdiction.  
 
He has expertise in understanding the intricacies of Financial Discrepancies, Corporate Cross Border 
Transactions and the matters of Corporate Governance.  
 
He has excellent academic track record to his credit.  He obtained his M.A. (Philosophy) in first class 
and D Phil (in Advaita Vedant) in Philosophy from University of Allahabad.  He also obtained his 
degrees in Law from University of Mumbai and master’s in law (LLM) from Savtribai Phule University, 
Pune.
