Regulations, 2011 (“SEBI (SAST) Regulations”) , is less than 54.00% (fifty four percent) of 
the diluted voting share capital of the Company, then the Purchasers will acquire additional 
number of Sale Shares (i.e. Second Tranche Shares), in accordance with the SPA, such that the 
aggregate shareholding of the Purchasers, reaches 54.00% (fifty four percent) of the diluted 
voting share capital of the Company.  Accordingly, the execution of the SPA has triggered an 
obligation of the Purchaser 1 to make an open offer in accordance with Regulation 3(1) and 
Regulation 4 of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations.
