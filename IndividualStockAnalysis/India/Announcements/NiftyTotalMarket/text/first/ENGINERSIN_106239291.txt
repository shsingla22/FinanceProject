Sub: Board Comments on fine  levied by the Exchange  
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
This is with reference to NSE Letter and BSE email dated 17.03.2025, regarding imposition of fine for non 
compliance of several provisions of SEBI Listing Regulation for the quarter 31.12.2024  and our letter dated 
20.03.2025, seeking a waiver of the imposed fines .  
 
In this regard, we wish to inform that as advised by the Exchanges, the matter regarding imposition of fine 
by Stock Exchanges for non -compliance was placed before EIL’s Board in its 381st Meeting held on 16.04.2025 .  
The Board took of the following:  
 
Quote  
The Company Secretary informed the Board that National Stock Exchange of India Limited through a  
letter March dated 17 , 2025 and BSE Limited vide email dated March 17,2025 have issued notices 
levying fine s amounting to Rs.8,28,360/ - (inclusive of 18% GST) each for failing to comply with the 
provisions as mentioned in the Regulations 17(1), 18(1), 19(1)/(2), 20(2)/(2A) & 21(2) of SEBI 
(LODR) Regulation, 2015, during  the period from November 08, 2024 to December 31, 2024. He 
explained that these non -compliances are  due to non-appointment of independent directors including 
woman director on the Board of the Company and non -compliance with constitution of statutory 
committees.  
The Board noted that EIL being a Public Sector Undertaking, only the Administrative Ministry i.e. , 
MoPNG has the power to appoint  or change the Directors on the Board of the Company. The Board 
was informed that the Company vide its letter dated November 18, 2024 has requested the Ministry 
to appoint requisite number of independent directors and woman director on the Board of the 
Company.  In this regard, two Non -Official Independent Directors were re -appointed by the Ministry 
on March 28, 2025. Further , the Company has submitted a request letter on March 20, 2025, to the 
respective Stock Exchanges seeking a waiver of the imposed fines. However, the NSE responded that 
the waiver could only be considered after the necessary compliance measures were achieved.   
unquote  
 
Considering above, the Stock Exchanges are requested to waive of fines for the quarter 31.12.2024, since 
the non-compliance is beyond the control of the Company.  
 
This is for your kind consideration.  
 
Thanking you,  
 
For Engineers India Limited
