Press Release  
 
Performance for the Financial Year 2024  
The company for the Financial Year ended 31st March 2024, reported a consolidated total revenue of ₹ 32,124 mn, up by 
53.5% YoY, as compared to ₹ 20,921 mn for the corresponding period ended 31st March 2023, primarily driven by 
Healthcare, Automotive and Consumer segments.  
The Consolidated EBITDA was  up by 11.1% YoY to ₹ 2,571 mn as against ₹ 2,315 mn for the corresponding period of the 
previous year. Profit after Tax was ₹ 1,243 mn, up by 1.1% YoY as compared to ₹ 1,231 mn last year.
