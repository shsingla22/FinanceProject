PRESS RELEASE  
 
Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi Dedicated Second Unit of SJVN's 1320 MW Buxar 
Thermal Power Project in Bihar to the Nation  
 
In a significant milestone towards strengthening India's power infrastructure , Hon'ble Prime 
Minister Shri Narendra Modi virtually dedicated the  Second Unit (660 MW) of SJVN's 1320 
MW Buxar Thermal Power Project to the Nation from Vaishali, Bihar today. The Second Unit, 
involving an investment of ₹7,221.09 crore, is part of  strategically important thermal power 
project developed by SJVN ’s wholly -owned subsidiary, SJVN  Thermal Private Limited 
(STPL) . 
 
The historic occasion was graced by Hon'ble Governor of Bihar, Lt Gen Syed Ata Hasnain 
(Retd.), Hon'ble Chief Minister of Bihar, Shri Samrat Choudhar y, Hon'ble Union Minister of 
Housing and Urban Affairs and Power, Shri Manohar Lal, along with Union Ministers, Deputy 
Chief Ministers of Bihar, State Ministers, senior MoP officials and other distinguished 
dignitaries . 
 
On this occasion, an event was organised at Buxar Thermal Power Project, attended by project 
employees and local community . SJVN Management and employees of SJVN and its 
subsidiaries across India and Nepal witnessed the occasion virtually through live streaming.  
 
Located at Chausa in Buxar district of Bihar, the 1320 MW (2×660 MW) Buxar Thermal Power 
Project is a major investment in  nation’s  power generation infrastructure.  Hon’ble Prime 
Minister Shri Narendra Modi laid foundation stone of Buxar Thermal Power Project on 9 
March 2019 and inaugurated its First Unit (660 MW) on 22 August 2025. With both units now 
commercially operational, the Project has achieved its full capacity of 1320 MW. Total 
approved revised cost of the Project is Rs. 14,269.40 crores.  
 
The Project is designed to generate approximately 9,828.72 million units of energy  annually at 
an 85% Plant Load Factor, with an estimated cumulative generation of about 2,45,718 million 
units  over 25 years. Significantly, 85% of power generated from the Project has been allocated 
to Bihar under a long -term Power Purchase Agreement . 
 
Buxar Thermal Power Project is based on  Ultra  Super Critical Technology and modern 
Emission -control Systems, designed to achieve higher operational efficiency and reduce 
environmental impact of coal -based e nergy  generation.  For long term linkage of domestic coal,  
a Fuel Supply Agreement has been executed with Central Coalfields Limited (CCL), a 
subsidiary of Coal India Limited.  
 
The energy generated from the Project  will contribute to meeting Bihar's growing electricity 
requirements, particularly during periods of peak demand, while strengthening reliability and 
stability of Eastern Regional Power Grid. This will  support industrial activities, agricultural 
operations, commercial establishments and household consumers, thereby contributing to 
overall socio -economic development of Bihar and neighbouring regions.

The project aligns with Government of India’s vision of Atmanirbhar Bharat and Viksit Bharat 
through extensive use of domestically sourced materials, including approximately 2,54,932 
MT of steel and 2,84,291 MT of cement in its construction.  
 
During various stages of construction, the project engaged approximately 65,000 workers, 
generating significant direct and indirect employment opportunities and supporting local 
livelihoods. In its operational phase, the project will continue to create emp loyment and 
economic activities in the region.  
 
Under its Corporate Social Responsibility & Sustainability initiatives, SJVN has undertaken 
various community development programs in and around Project Affected Areas, ensuring that 
benefits of the Project extend beyond power generation to socio -economic development of 
local communities.  
 
SJVN Limited, a Navratna  CPSE under Ministry of Power was incorporated on 24 May 1988 
as a Joint Venture of Government of India and Government of Himachal Pradesh. With an 
installed capacity of 4,931.50 MW and 3,860.50 MW under -construction, SJVN continues to 
expand its presence across India and Nepal, contributing to national energy security and 
sustainable development.
