 PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Group Launches Brigade Misty Greens,  
a Premium Residential Development in Mysuru with a Revenue Potential of over Rs. 300 Crores  
 
Bengaluru, July 21, 2026:  Brigade Group has announced the launch of Brigade Misty Greens, a premium 
residential development in Mysuru through a Joint Development Agreement. With a projected revenue 
potential of over Rs. 300 crore s, the project spans 4.5 acres. The launch further consolidates Brigade’s 
longstanding presence in Mysuru, where the company has developed several landmark residential 
communities and continues to expand its footprint in one of South India’s most promising real estate markets.  
 
Brigade Misty Greens features three and four bedroom spacious and thoughtfully planned apartment units and 
14 exclusive Duplex units, designed to appeal to discerning homebuyers and long -term investors seeking 
flexibility, connectivity, and strong value ap preciation. Located near the Jayachamaraja  Wadiyar Golf Club and 
the iconic Chamundi Hills, the project enjoys a truly distinctive setting with uninterrupted green and hill views, 
an offering rarely found in the Mysuru residential market. Its proximity to key commercial hubs, educational 
instituti ons, healthcare facilities, malls, hotels, and resorts ensures unparalleled convenience for residents.  
  
“Mysuru has been an important market for Brigade. We are the only listed player to have a strong presence in 
the city with over 26 projects across residential, commercial and hospitality. With Brigade Misty Greens, we are 
furthering our vision of developin g communities in locations that offer both enduring value and an elevated 
lifestyle. This project represents another significant milestone in our growth journey and reflects our continued 
commitment to delivering thoughtfully planned, high -quality communit ies.” - Pavitra Shankar, Managing 
Director, Brigade Enterprises Limited .   
 
About Brigade Group:  Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India ’s leading property developers with 
close to four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and winning their 
customers ’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark buildings and transformed skylines across India in 
the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Trivandrum, and GIFT City with developments 
across Residential, Office, Retail, Hospitality and Educa tion Sectors.
