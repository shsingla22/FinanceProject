Reference:  
 
1. U.S. Executive Order titled ‘Restriction on Entry of Certain Non -Immigrant Workers’ 
dated September 19, 2025 ( “U.S. Executive Order ”) 
2. Memorandum dated September 20, 2025, issued by the U.S. Citizenship and 
Immigration Services  (“USICS”) (“Memorandum ”) 
 
Based on our preliminary assessment  and analysis  of the  U.S. Executive Order related  to fee 
increase  for the  H-1B visa applications and subsequent Memorandum issued by the USICS , 
we wish to inform that we do not expect any significant impact on our operations or financials.  
 
Further , we would like to highlight , during  the Financial Year 26 (till date) , the Company had 
filed 135 new H -1B visa petitions , of which  32 were approved by USCIS.  
We are proactively evaluating opportunities to reduce our dependence on the H -1B visa 
program through a combination of in -market lateral and campus hiring, nearshoring and 
driving more offshoring.  
 
We will continue to monitor the developments closely and will provide updates, as  
appropriate.
