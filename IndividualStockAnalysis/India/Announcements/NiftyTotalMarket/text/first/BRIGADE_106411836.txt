PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Foundation Launches a Landmark Tree Planting Drive of Over 100,000 Trees in 
Bengaluru’s KIADB Area  
 
Bengaluru, October 17, 2025 : In a conscientious step toward ecological enhancement and community well -
being, Brigade Foundation has launched a landmark tree planting drive. Titled “From Saplings to a Sanctuary: 
Planting 100,000 Trees for a Greener Tomorrow”, this initiative, which takes root in Brigade Group’s Urban Forest 
philosophy, was symbolically inaugurated by Shri. M B Patil, Hon. Minister for Large and Medium Industries 
& Infrastructure Development, Government of Karnataka in the presence of Shri M R Jaishankar, Lifetime 
Trustee, Brigade Foundation and Executive Chairman Brigade Enterprises Limited . These saplings will be 
planted in and around Brigade Group’s project in Bengaluru’s KIADB area, with the aim to reinforce these 
landscapes as thriving ecological reserves. Brigade Foundation has partnered with Bengaluru based Vanantara, 
an ecologically f ocused firm, to nurture the saplings to  mature until they grow into resilient trees.  
 
After planting a tree alongside other dignitaries, Shri. M B Patil, Hon. Minister for Large and Medium 
Industries & Infrastructure Development, Government of Karnataka , said, “Our Bengaluru has always been 
known as the Garden City. However, due to rapid urbanisation and industrialisation, that distinction has 
somewhat faded. Yet, through this initiative, Brigade Foundation has shown how to balance development with 
envir onmental responsibility. It has made a meaningful contribution to the very idea of the Ga rden City. 
Similarly, in Vijayapura, we have planted around 15million saplings since 2016. Everyone should undertake 
such efforts in their own regions. Usually, people expect someone else to come forward and do something good 
for the environment. But Briga de Group has demonstrated that protecting the environment is our own 
responsibility, something we must take up ourselves. My congratulations to the entire team.”  
 
Speaking at the event Shri. K.H. Muniyappa, Hon. Minister for Food and Civil Supplies, Govt. of Karnataka  
said, "The Brigade Group has undertaken a significant and commendable initiative. It is very important to take 
care of the earth in a balanced manner. In this regard, it is essential for private companies to join hands with 
the government. The fact that t he Brigade Group has carried out such a meaningful effort is truly 
commendable."  
 
Speaking at the inauguration, Shri M R Jaishankar, Lifetime Trustee of Brigade Foundation, and Executive 
Chairman  of Brigade Enterprises Limited  said “Brigade Foundations focus has been on education, health and 
community development. But we also believe deeply that progress must go hand in hand with responsibility. 
This tree plantation drive is a natural extension of that belief. We believe that ev ery sapling planted is an act of 
faith for the future and hence we have already planted one lakh trees across some of our properties in Bengaluru 
and Mysuru. We are proud to contribute to a greener, more resilient Bengaluru.  We also congratulate the 
Government of Karnataka's initiative to plant around 15million trees for Vijayapura District and this encourages 
us at Brigade to increase our contribution beyond the 1lakh saplings we plant here today."  
 
According to Varun Ravindra is the Founder and Chief Conservator, Vanantara , “Enhancing ecosystems at 
scale requires vision and patience, and this initiative is a testament to what can be done when urban 
development embraces ecological responsibility. These saplings will be nurtured into resilient trees that 
promote biodiversity,  improve air and water quality, and reconnect communities with nature. Today marks the 
beginning of what is certain to be a lush forest in the years to come.”
