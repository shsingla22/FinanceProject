Mastek Selected by Staffordshire County Council to D rive AI Enabled  - 
Oracle Cloud T ransformation  
Mastek using its proprietary ADOPT- AI platform will help the council modernise finance, 
procurement, HR and payroll services, supporting future -ready public service delivery and 
local government reorganisation readiness  
 
London, October  08, 2026:  Mastek  (UK) Limited, a wholly -owned  Subsidiary of Mastek 
Limited (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted AI -first digital engineering and cloud 
transformation partner, today announced that it has been selected by Staffordshire County 
Council to deliver a significant Oracle Cloud transformation programme spanning Finance, 
Procurement, Human Resources and Payroll.  
Under the engagement, Mastek will use its ADOPT AI platform  to deliver  Oracle Cloud  as the 
Council’s single, integrated platform for Finance, Procurement, HR and Payroll.  The new 
environment will enable the Council to consolidate legacy systems, strengthen data governance, enhance employee experience and establish a modern digital foundation to 
support ongoing transformation.  
As one of England's largest county councils, Staffordshire County Council serves more than 
875,000 residents and delivers a broad range of essential public services across education, 
social care, highways and community services. Supporting these services requires efficient, 
resilient and future -ready corporate operations capable of adapting to evolving 
organisational and service delivery requirements.  
The programme forms a key part of Staffordshire County Council's wider ambition to 
modernise  its corporate services and establish a more agile, digitally enabled operating 
model. The c ouncil is focused on simplifying and standardising back -office processes, 
improving operational visibility and governance, reducing manual effort, and creating a 
scalable foundation capable of supporting future organisational change.  
Abhishek Singh, President UKI & Europe, Mastek , said,  "We are delighted to partner with 
Staffordshire County Council on this important transformation programme . Local authorities 
are increasingly focused on modernising operations while preparing for future structural and service delivery changes. By combining Oracle Cloud technology with Mastek's extensive 
public sector transformation expertise  and AI native approach, we look forward to helping 
the council build a more efficient, resilient and future -ready operating model that delivers 
long- term value for employees, stakeholders and residents."  
 
Pete Shakespear, Director of Finance & Resourc es, Staffordshire County Council,  said,  "As 
we continue to evolve our operating model and prepare for future organisational change, it 
is important that our corporate systems are modern, efficient and capable of supporting 
long- term transformation. Mastek demonstrated a strong understanding of our objectives, 
 
and we look forward to partnering with them to deliver a platform that will help us build a 
more agile and future -ready organisation."  
The programme  marks another important milestone in Mastek's continued growth within 
UK Local Government and reinforces its position as a trusted transformation partner for 
large -scale public sector modernisation initiatives.  
Following a competitive procurement process, Mastek was selected based on its Oracle 
Cloud expertise, extensive UK local government experience, collaborative delivery approach 
and strong understanding of Staffordshire County Council's transformation object ives.  
The engagement further strengthens Mastek's position as a trusted transformation partner 
within UK Local Government and expands its Oracle Cloud footprint across f inance, 
procurement and h uman capital management. The programme is particularly significant as 
councils across England prepare for l ocal government reorganisation and seek modern, 
scalable technology platforms capable of supporting organisational change while improving 
service resilience. The win also enhances Mastek's growing portfolio of Oracle Cloud 
transformation programmes and provides a strong reference point for other local authorities 
pursuing enterprise -wide modernisation initiatives.
