 QUARTER UPDATES  
• Big Billion Days shifts from September to October , moving associated sales from Q2 into Q3  
and impacting reported revenue for the quarter  
• Online mix rebounds to ~31% from ~22% in Q1 FY27 , returning broadly to normalized levels  
• RedTape.com sales grow 712% YoY , demonstrating strong traction of the Company's own 
website  
• 49 EBOs and 5 shop -in-shops added  during the quarter, continuing the expansion of RedTape's 
physical retail footprint  
 
Mr. Arvind Verma, Whole Time Director, RedTape Limited said, “Q2 is seasonally a softer quarter for our 
business, and this year the performance was also influenced by the shift in the festive and promotional 
calendar, which moved a part of the sales opportunity into Q3. Despite the softer revenue base, we maintained  
healthy margins and delivered strong profitability, reflecting the operating leverage in the business and 
continued discipline across costs and channels.  
  
As we enter the second half, we move into a seasonally stronger period for the business, supported by festive 
and winter demand. At the same time, we continue to strengthen our omnichannel presence, with our digital 
business gaining momentum and our physic al retail network continuing to expand. Our focus remains on 
strengthening the RedTape brand and driving sustainable, profitable growth across channels.”  
 
OUTLOOK  
Looking ahead, RedTape remains focused on disciplined growth, sharper  execution across retail and digital 
channels, continued operating efficiency and protection of its consumer value proposition. Supported by 
improving demand trends, a healthy retail network, stronger product and category mix, the Company 
believes it is well positioned to pursue profitable growth while continuing to strengthen its brand, channel 
mix and operating model through FY27 .

 About REDTAPE Limited  
REDTAPE Limited (BSE: 543957 | NSE: REDTAPE) is one of India’s  leading lifestyle brands, offering a comprehensive 
range of footwear, apparel, and accessories for men, women, and children. With a legacy of quality, style, and innovation, 
RedTape has established itself as a trusted name in the Indian fashion and lifest yle industry. The Company operates 
through a robust multi -brand portfolio , including RedTape, Mode, Bond Street by RedTape, OZARK, catering to diverse 
consumer preferences across casual, formal, sports, and outdoor segments.  
 
Headquartered in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, RedTape Limited leverages an asset -light business model with strategic vendor 
partnerships to ensure rapid product turnaround, efficient supply chain management, and cost optimisation. With 756 
stores across 3 69 cities in 20 states and 4 union territories, the Company continues to expand its retail footprint and 
strengthen its market presence across metro and emerging markets. For details visit www.redtape.com
