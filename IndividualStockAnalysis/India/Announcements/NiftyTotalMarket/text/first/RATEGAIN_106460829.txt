RateGain Launches Rev -AI Clarity, a Revenue Assistant for Car Rentals  
Rev-AI Clarity – complex data, simple answers, smarter decisions.  
 
India, 4th December 2025 – New Delhi:  RateGain Travel Technologies Limited (RateGain), a leading provider of AI -
powered SaaS solutions for travel and hospitality, today announced the launch of Rev -AI Clarity , an intelligent 
revenue assistant built specifically for car rental operators as part of the Rev -AI product suite.   
 
Designed for revenue and commercial teams , Rev-AI Clarity cuts through fragmented dashboards by turning complex 
demand, pricing, and performance data into clear, conversational insight. Revenue leaders can ask direct questions 
about recommended price, tomorrow’s demand, pacing by city, or month -to-month  performance , and receive concise  
narratives that highlight key drivers, risks, and opportunities within  seconds.  
 
Built on the Rev -AI engine, Rev -AI Clarity  synthesizes   historical bookings, forecast models, and live market signals  
into  natural language responses, that present context -rich insights and recommended next  steps replacing manual 
number  analysis  with instant, decision -ready i ntelligence . 
 
Key capabilities include:  
• Always -on revenue assistant delivering  instant answers on demand, pricing, fleet, and channel  performance  
• Context -aware, explainable answers that  combin e bookings, forecasts, and market signals.  
• Native integration with  Rev-AI,  connecting pricing, pace, and demand in a single conversational view.  
 
“Rev-AI Clarity is an industry -leading  step forward  to help mobility providers grow  their business” said Sanchit Garg, 
EVP & GM, Rev -AI at RateGain . “By putting an intelligent revenue assistant on top of our Rev -AI data and forecasting 
engine, we turn complex signals into instant, decision -ready answers  on demand, so teams can price, plan fleet, and 
manage demand with far greater speed and confidence.”  
 
Rev-AI Clarity is available as part of the Rev -AI suite for car rentals and integrates seamlessly with existing RateGain 
deployments, giving customers precise, transparent insight into every price recommendation.  
For more information on Rev -AI Clarity , please visit rev-ai.io  or contact your RateGain representative.
