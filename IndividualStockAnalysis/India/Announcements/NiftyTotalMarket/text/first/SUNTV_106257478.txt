With reference to the above, we further hereby submit/ inform that: 
The Board of Directors at their meeting held on 30th May 2025, (commenced at 2.30 p.m. and concluded
at 4.30 p.m.) have also considered the following: 
The Company received a notice from the National Stock Exchange of India Limited and BSE Limited 
dated 17th March, 2025 alleging non-compliance with Regulations 17(1), 18(1), 19, 20, and 21(2) of the 
SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, and imposed a fine of Rs. 92,040/- (inclusive of 18% IGST) each for the 
period 1st October 2024-6U1 October 2024, which was duly placed before the Board for its consideration
and comments. 
The Board after discussion opined that the non-compliances arise out of non-appointment of three 
Directors in the place of three Directors whose terms of office ended on 25th September, 2024. 
The Company is governed by the Regulations of the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting (MIB) 
and Ministry of Civil Aviation, (MoCA) Government of India and hence, the prior approval for the 
appointment of any new Director have to be obtained from both MIB and MoCA. The Board of 
Directors considered the appointment of three new Independent Directors namely, Mr. Mandalapu 
Harinarayanan Harshavardhan, Mr. Ravivenkatesh Pragadish Karthik and Mrs. Jagadeesan Gayathri 
and also applied for the prior permission to appoint the aforesaid Directors sufficiently in advance on 
5th April, 2024 to MIB and on 16th April, 2024 to MoCA. The Company received the approvals from 
MIB & MoCA on 5th September, 2024 and 3rd October, 2024 respectively. On receipt of the approvals, 
the board appointed the three new Independent Directors with effect from 07th October 2024 
Further, the Company submitted a reply along with a waiver application dated 28th March, 2025 to the 
stock exchanges citing "impossibility of compliance" under Clause 3(a) of Policy for Exemption of Fines­
Levied as Per the Provisions of SEBI SOP Circular on account of "Appointment to the Board of Directors 
/ KMPs due to pending approvals from the Government (Ministry)/ Regulator/ Any Statutory 
Authority." 
The Company is expecting a favorable response on its merits from the stock exchanges for the waiver 
application filed
