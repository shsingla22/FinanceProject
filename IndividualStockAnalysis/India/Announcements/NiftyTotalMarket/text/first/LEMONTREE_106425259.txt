Lemon Tree Hotels launches its 9th property in  Uttarakhand  with  
Lemon Tree Hotel , Mall of Dehradun, Dehradun  
 
 
The hotel features 98 well-appointed rooms and suites,  a restaurant  and 
banqueting spaces  
 
New Delhi, 3rd November 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest  
growing mid -scale and premium hotel chain, launched its latest managed  
property , Lemon Tree Hotel,  Mall of Dehradun, Dehradun . 
 
This launch marks another milestone in the brand’s expansion across northern  
India and further reinforces its presence in Uttarakhand , where it has eight 
operational and nine upcoming propert ies. 
 
Located in the  Doon Valley  on the foothills of the  Himalayas, the city is nestled 
between  Song River, a tributary of  the Ganges  on the east and the Asan River, a 
tributary of  the Yamuna  on the west. Noted for its picturesque landscape, pleasant 
climate, educational institutions, and proximity to hill stations like Mussoorie, the 
city beautifully blends nature, culture and modernity.  
 
The property offers contemporary interiors, seamless service and a whiff of the 
signature lemon fragrance. It has 98 vibrant rooms & suites, Citrus Café – a multi -
cuisine restaurant and banquet spaces including  Tangerine  1, Tangerine 2. The 
hotel shares the premises with Mall of Dehradun, the biggest mall in Uttarakhand, 
which has high -end national and international brands.  
 
Commenting on the opening , Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, Lemon 
Tree Hotels , said, “ With the opening of Lemon Tree Hotel, M all of Dehradun , 
Dehradun, we are pleased to extend our signature comfort and warmth to 
travellers visiting Dehradun where we already have two hotels. Its prime location 
will give the guests multiple recreation and shopping options .”
