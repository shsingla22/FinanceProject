 PRESS RELEASE  
Happiest Minds Announces Board Approval of FY27 Plan; Announces 
its Flagship  Strategic AI Initiative: The Enterprise AI Platform  
 
Bengaluru, Seattle and London, May 25th, 2026:  Happiest Minds Technologies Limited (NSE: 
HAPPSTMNDS), an AI First, customer -centric digital engineering and Mindful IT company, today announced 
that its Board of Directors has approved the Company’s FY27 Plan, reaffirming its commitment to delivering 
sustainable and profitable growth.  
 
Happiest Minds h as now reconfirmed its FY27 growth guidance of 12.5%, while also reiterating that 
its aspirational goal of achieving 15% growth remains intact. The Company Board noted strong 
alignment between the Company’s strategic priorities, demand trends across digital transformation, 
and opportunities that continue to emerge from accelerat ed AI adoption.  
 
Happiest Minds also announced its flagship strategic AI initiative  - creation of Enterprise AI Platform . This 
is the 12th strategic AI initiative of the company and builds on its expanding suite of AI offerings and realigns 
its services from enabling our clients to enhancing operational efficiencies . The platform will serve as a 
modular, scalable, and secure foundation for enabling enterprise -grade AI adoption, covering the full 
spectrum from GenAI to agentic automation. It will bring together reusable comp onents, accelerators, 
industry solutions, guardrails, and governance frameworks to help customers operationalize AI with speed, 
safety, and accelerate  growth and business performance .  
 
Ashok Soota, Chairman & Chief Mentor, Happiest Minds  said “Our confidence in our growth outlook 
remains unchanged. AI is not a headwind but a tailwind, opening up newer opportunities for enterprises to re -
imagine, re -engineer, and re -accelerate their digital journeys. Happiest Minds  with its Enterprise  AI platform  is 
well-positioned to partner with our customers in this AI shift and adoption.”  
 
Joseph Anantharaju, Co -Chairman & CEO,  Happiest Minds  said, “The launch of our Enterprise AI Platform 
marks a major milestone in our journey as an AI -first company  and our investments in AI innovation, 
engineering, and platforms continue to strengthen our long -term competitiveness. ” 
 
Sridhar Mantha, CEO, Gen AI Business Services, Happiest Minds said, “Our Enterprise AI Platform delivers 
a reusable foundation of Agentic AI capabilities, enabling rapid development of domain -specific solutions. 
Backed by a strong verticalization roadmap, it unifies our engineering excellence, AI expertise, industry contex t, 
and responsible AI frameworks to accelerate scalable, industry -focused innovation.”  
 
The Company’s FY27 plan is designed to build on momentum across creating AI solutions, AI across all our 
services like SDLC, AI -led modernization, Cloud Ops, Cybersecurity etc., with a continued focus on:  
• Deepening customer engagements  in our Fortune 500 accounts  
• Expanding strategic tech partnerships  
• Investing in talent for AI and next-generation technologies  
• Scaling new AI -powered solutions and platforms  
 
Happiest Minds will release its financial results for the quarter and fiscal year ended March 31, 2026, on May 
28, 2026.
