Sub: Company Update: Commissioning of cement capacity  
 
Dear Sirs,  
 
This is with reference to our communication earlier in the day.  
 
We are pleased to inform the commissioning of 8.7 mtpa of cement capacity as detailed 
hereunder : 
 
(i) 2.7 mtpa cement grinding unit at Shahjahanpur , Uttar Pradesh  
(ii) 3.0 mtpa cement griding unit at Vi sakha patnam , Andra Pradesh  
(iii) 3.0 mtpa cement griding unit at Patratu , Jharkhand  
 
Consequent to the above, the Company’s total domestic grey cement manufacturing capacity 
now stands augmented to 200.1  mtpa. Alongwith its overseas capacity of 5.4 mtpa, the 
Company’s global capacity stands at  205.5  mtpa.  
 
The Company ranks as the world’s largest cement company by sales volume and is also the 
largest single -country cement manufacturer globally (excluding China ).
