Key Developments – Q2 FY26
Consolidated Revenue  in Q2FY26 increased by 12.2% to Rs 5,351 Cr., 
while  Consolidated PBT increased to Rs. 565 Cr. as against  Rs. 462 Cr. in 
Q2FY25.

fuel and power  procurement .
The company  maintained continuous  focus  on reduction in T&D loss
