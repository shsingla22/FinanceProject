Jubilant Therapeutics Announces Oral Presenta tion of Preliminary Phase 1 Clinical 
Data for JBI-802, a First-in-Class Oral Du al LSD1/HDAC6 Inhibi tor, in Essential 
Thrombocythemia and Related Myeloprolifer ative Neoplasms at the 68th ASH Annual 
Meeting  
– Abstract data show rapid platelet reductions and durable hematologic control  
– Company to host an event on December 14, 2026  
YARDLEY, PA., November 10, 2026  — Jubilant Therapeutics, Inc., a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical 
company, advancing precision therapeutics for myel oproliferative neoplasms (MPNs), today announced that 
clinical results from its Phase 1/2 study of JBI-802, a fi rst-in-class, orally administered dual inhibitor of LSD1 
and HDAC6 (CoREST), have been selected for an oral  presentation at the 68th American Society of 
Hematology (ASH) Annual Meeting and Exposition, ta king place December 12–15, 2026, in New Orleans and 
online. 
“We are pleased to have the preliminary clinical data fo r JBI-802 selected for oral presentation at ASH,” said 
Daniel O’Connor, President and Chief Executive Officer of  Jubilant Therapeutics. “T he abstract data from this 
ongoing Phase 1/2 study show encouraging early evidence of rapid platelet reductions and hematologic control 
in patients with ET and related MPNs. We look forward to presenting additional efficacy, safety and molecular 
data at ASH as we continue to ev aluate JBI-802 in these patients.” 
The oral presentation will incl ude results from the Phase 1 portion of the study, an open-label, multicenter trial 
designed to evaluate the safety, tolerability and prelimin ary efficacy of JBI-802. The study has enrolled patients 
with essential thrombocythemia (ET), polycythemia vera (PV) and myelodyspla stic/myeloproliferative 
neoplasms (MDS/MPN) with thrombocytosis who were rela psed, refractory or intolerant to standard of care 
(JBI-802-02; ACTRN12624000478516). 
Key findings from the abstract (data as of June 30, 2026) include: 
• All 8 patients who initiated treatment at the 15 mg achieved a >50% platelet reduction or platelet 
normalization (<400 ×10 ⁹/L) by Week 3, with a median time to response of 15 days. 
• Platelet normalization was maintained through 33, 44 and 46 weeks in patients enrolled at 15 mg with 
>12 weeks of follow-up. 
• Seven of 11 patients with leukocytosis at 15 mg achieved a complete hematologic response. 
• No dose-limiting toxicities were observed at 5, 7 or 15 mg. 
Additional efficacy, safety and molecular data will be pr esented during the oral session. The abstract is 
available on the ASH Annual Meeting website.

About JBI-802  
JBI-802 is an orally administered dual inhibitor of lysine-specific demethylase 1 (LSD1) and histone 
deacetylase 6 (HDAC6) that targets the CoREST complex.  It is designed to act on multiple disease-relevant 
processes, including abnormal megakaryocyte differentiati on, platelet production, l eukocyte proliferation and 
inflammatory signaling. JBI-802 is being evaluated in an ongoing Phase 1/2 study in patients with ET and other 
thrombocytosis-predominant myel oid malignancies (JBI-802-02; ACTRN12624000478516 ; NCT07612280 ).
