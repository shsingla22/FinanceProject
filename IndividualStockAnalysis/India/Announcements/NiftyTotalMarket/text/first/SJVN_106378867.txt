PRESS RELEASE  
 
Hon’ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi laid foundation stone  of SJVN’s 
200 MW Solar Power Project (GUVNL Phase – XVII, Khavda Solar Park)  
SHIMLA: 20.09.2025  
In a proud moment for SJVN, Hon’ble Prime Minister Shri. Narendra Modi virtually laid the 
foundation stone of SJVN’s 200 MW Solar Power Project (GUVNL Phase – XVII, Khavda Solar 
Park) at Khavda, Gujarat, today.  
This historic occasion was graced by, Chief Minister of Gujarat Shri Bhupendrabhai Patel, 
Union Minister of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, Shri Sarbananda Sonowal, Union Minister 
of Labour and Employment; and Youth Affairs and Sports, Dr. Mansukh Mandaviya , Union 
Minister of Jal Shakti, Shri Chandrakant Raghunath Patil, Union MoS Ports, Shipping and 
Waterways, Shri Shantanu Thakur, Union MoS Consumer Affairs, Food and Public 
Distribution, Smt. Nimuben Jayantibhai Bambhaniya and other dignitaries.  
The event was virtually joined by CMD SJVN Shri. Bhupender Gupta, Director (Personnel) 
Shri. Ajay Kumar Sharma, Director Finance (Addl. Charge) Shri. Sipan Kumar Garg , CEO, 
SGEL, Sh Ajay Singh and senior officials of SJVN and SGEL . 
200 MW Khavda  Solar Power Project is situated in Khavda Solar Park being developed by 
Gujarat State Electricity Corporation Limited (GSECL) in Kachchh district of Gujarat. The 
Power generated from the project will be supplied to Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited 
(GUVNL)  for 25 Years at a tariff of Rs. 2.88/ Unit.  
The project will be built at an estimated cost of about ₹866.8 crore. It is expected to generate 
504.92 million units of electricity in the first year and a cumulative 11,620 million units over a 
period of 25 years. Additionally, the project is anticipated  to reduce carbon emissions by about 
5,69,250 tonnes over 25 years. The project is scheduled to be commissioned by 31st 
December 2026.  
The installation of this project will contribute to India’s commitment towards achieving 500 GW 
of renewable energy capacity by 2030 and Net Zero emissions by 2070. It will also create 
direct and indirect employment opportunities in the region. Moreover, t he project will utilize the 
vast arid land of  Kachchh  for clean power generation and help meet the renewable energy 
requirements of Gujarat.  
 SJVN, a Navratna CPSE under Ministry of Power, Government of India has commissioned 
fourteen projects with a total installed capacity of 2968 MW and 123 km of transmission lines. 
The company is currently operating and implementing power projects across In dia and in the 
neighboring country of Nepal.
