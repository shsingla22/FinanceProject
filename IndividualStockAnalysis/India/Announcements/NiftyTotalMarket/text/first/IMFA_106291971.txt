4. Water 
ManagementRisk Release of wastewater may 
lead to statutory violations 
and may also have adverse 
impacts on the local 
community.All our manufacturing plants are Zero 
liquid Discharge plants. Wastewater 
from our operations is reused entirely, 
for cooling various equipment, 
suppressing dust, or for ash cooling. 
Domestic wastewater is treated through 
Sewage Treatment Plants (STP) and 
used for gardening. There is no discharge 
of wastewater from our Plants.
Therubali plant is ‘Water positive’ which 
recharges more water to ground than 
quantity extracted.
In Mines, only seepage water are 
discharged to nearby surface water 
outside lease only after treatment.Negative
