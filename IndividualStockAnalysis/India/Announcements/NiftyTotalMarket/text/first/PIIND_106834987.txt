Press Release :  
PI Industries unveils Pioxaniliprole, India's first indigenously discovered 
insecticide  molecule  
Decade -long research breakthrough marks a defining milestone in India's  ability to originate 
world -class agricultural science and proprietary innovation.  
 
New Delhi, October 29, 2026: In a historic moment for Indian agricultural science, PI Industries today 
unveiled  Pioxaniliprole, India's first indigenously discovered insecticide molecule, developed through 
more than a decade of original research, sustained investment and scientific innovation.  
The achievement marks a defining milestone for PI and India's crop- protection industry. Pioxaniliprole 
demonstrates the country ’s growing capability to originate novel scientific discoveries and create 
proprietary intellectual property.  
India is home to one of the world's largest and most diverse agricultural ecosystems, spanning a wide 
range of crops, agro -climatic conditions and  evolving pest pressures. For over eight decades, PI has 
worked alongside Indian agricultur al universi ties and scientists, building a deep understanding of these 
complexities through generations of engagement with farmers.  
Pioxaniliprole brings together this understanding of Indian farming realities with PI's advanced scientific discovery capabilities.  Developed to global scientific standards, the molecule has undergone extensive 
evaluation under Indian agricultural conditions, including multilocation fie ld trials, scientific assessments 
and regulatory studies, with participation from India's agricultural research ecosystem, including State Agricultural Universities.  
Pioxaniliprole has received registration from the Central Insecticides Board and Registrat ion Committee 
(CIB&RC), under the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India. The Committee formally recognised it as the first insecticide active ingredient discovered in India.  
Mayank Singhal, Vice Chairperson & Managing Director, PI Industries Ltd, said "More than a 
decade ago, we set out to build the capability to discover original crop -protection  in India. It required the 
courage to challenge conventional wisdom, substantial long- term investment and the conviction to pursue 
what had  never been achieved before.Today, Pioxaniliprole demonstrates what is possible when our deep 
understanding of Indian agriculture meets world -class science. It is a proud moment for PI and for Indian 
agricultural innovation.  This achievement strengthens our ambition to build a global, innovation -led Life 
Sciences company, creating original science and intellectual property from India for the world .” 
The discovery reflects PI's long -term investment in an integrated research and development ecosystem 
spanni ng molecular discovery, chemistry, biology, formulation science, toxicology, agronomy, process 
development and manufacturing.  
                                                                                                                                                                              
With over 700 scientists, more than 200 PhDs and a portfolio of over 260 patents, PI has built the 
scientific depth and infrastruc ture to advance original discoveries through development and 
commercialisation.  
The significance of Pioxaniliprole extends beyond a single molecule. It is the first outcome of a broader innovation pipeline at PI, spanning chemistry, biology and biochemical  solutions. Together, these mark 
an important step in India’ s journey towards greater scientific self -reliance, strengthening indigenous 
research capabilities and creating opportunities for Indian -origin agricultural innovation to address 
challenges of glo bal relevance . 
Narayan K. Seshadri, Chairperson , PI Industries Ltd, said " Pioxaniliprole represents an important 
milestone in the evolution of Indian agricultural science. Breakthrough innovation requires the ability to 
think beyond established boundaries,  backed by scientific capabilities, patient investment and long -term 
commitment. For PI Industries, Pioxaniliprole represents an important foundation for the next generation 
of discoveries. It reinforces the company's commitment to building a sustained inn ovation pipeline, 
advancing indigenous research capabilities and developing differentiated technologies for global 
agriculture. ” 
From India's first indigenous insecticide discovery to the discoveries of tomorrow, Pioxaniliprole opens a 
new chapter for PI a nd Indian agricultural innovation.
