Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
 
This is to inform you that as per  the Group Captive Scheme of the Electricity Rules, 2005 , 
the electricity consumers of the captive power generating Companies are required to 
hold equity shares in such power generating  companies in direct proportion to the 
quantum of power being consumed  by such electricity consumers . 
 In accordance with the above -mentioned provisions, DLF Info City Chennai Limited  
(‘DICCL’)  [‘electricity consumer’], a wholly -owned subsidiary of DLF Cyber City 
Developers Limited in which DLF Limited holds ~66.67% of its total paid -up share capital, 
has acquired  additional  ~4.95% of total paid -up share capital of  Tulip Renewable 
Powertech Private Limited ( ‘Tulip ’), being power generating  compan y. 
 
Consequent to the above  and in compliance with the provisions of the Electricity Rules, 
2005, DICCL holds ~15.42% of the paid -up share capital in Tulip.
