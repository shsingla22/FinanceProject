Dear Sir / Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 and other applicable provisions of the LODR Regulations, this is to inform 
that Crisil ESG Ratings has assigned the ESG Rating of ‘Crisil ESG 63’  to KFin Technologies Limited 
(“Company ”). This rating is based on the Company’s public disclosure on the ESG parameters at the 
standalone level and other information in the public domain.  
The strong rating is attributable to the rise in environmental (E) and social (S) scores. This is largely 
owing to the better performance on key E and S parameters such as energy consumption, Scope 1 and 
2 emissions, gender diversity, employee turnover, amongst others. Further, the ESG rating is also 
supported by a healthy Governance (G) score. The G score was supported by the adequate board 
structure (10 members) commensurate with the size and complexity of the business, majority 
independent audit, nomination and remuneration, and risk management, committees, and split in the 
managing director and chairperson positions.
