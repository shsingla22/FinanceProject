 Punjab Government Notifies Physicswallah Digital University in Patiala, Marking PW's 
Entry into Higher Education  
 
The move completes PW’s education ecosystem with degree-led learning, leveraging its existing digital 
infrastructure and academic capabilities. 
Noida | November 23, 2026:  Physicswallah Limited (“PW”) is expanding into formal higher education with 
the establishment of ‘Physicswallah Digital University’ in Patiala, Punjab. The initiative marks a new phase 
in the company’s journey, extending its offerings beyond K-12 and competitive exam preparation to 
undergraduate, postgraduate and career-oriented programmes through an online model.  
The Punjab Government notified the Physicswallah Digital University, Patiala Act, 2026 (“Act”), on October 
29, 2026, following the Governor’s assent on October 23, 2026. Nextseed Foundation,  a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Physicswallah Limited, is the sponsoring body of the university and is headquartered at 
Patiala, Punjab and will operate on a self-financed model, in accordance with the provisions of the Act.  
The University will adopt an AI-enabled approach to online and distance learning, integrating technology 
across teaching, assessments, student engagement and academic support. This will be complemented by 
live sessions, faculty mentoring and a global faculty connect, bringing diverse academic perspectives and 
international expertise to the students. Together, these initiatives aim to equip students with the 
knowledge, practical skills and global workforce capabilities needed to navigate an evolving professional 
landscape. The University’s digital infrastructure will further enable mobile-first access and low-
bandwidth connectivity, making higher education more accessible to learners in smaller towns and 
remote locations. 
With the first session targeted to commence in July 2027, Physicswallah Digital University plans to offer 
programmes across five academic schools: Business and Management; Computer and Information 
Technology; Humanities & Social Sciences; Journalism & Media Communication, and Skills & Vocational 
Studies. Its proposed portfolio will include undergraduate and postgraduate degrees, along with 
certifications in areas such as Banking and Financial Services, and Digital Marketing with AI and Social 
Media Marketing, subject to UGC recognition and other applicable regulatory approvals.  
Speaking on the development, Alakh Pandey, Founder and Educator, Physicswallah, said, “Since its 
inception, PW has been focused on providing students across the country with accessible quality learning 
at affordable costs and equal opportunity for all students irrespective of geography or economic status. 
The Digital University will help us take this vision into formal higher education. We want students to access 
good teachers, the right guidance, and learning opportunities that prepare them for robust careers. We 
are grateful to the Punjab Government for its support and hope to make a meaningful difference in the 
lives of students.” 
The University has been established under the Act, on the basis of the Punjab Private Digital Open 
Universities Policy, 2026. Its framework provides for credit mobility through distance and online courses 
 and micro-credentials, subject to applicable regulations, and an independent ombudsman to address 
student grievances. The University will also be required to comply with applicable University Grants 
Commission (UGC) regulations, including those governing online and open and distance learning 
programmes. 
Through this initiative, PW aims to extend its role in education beyond its K-12 offerings and competitive 
test preparation, creating opportunities for learners to pursue higher education and develop skills 
relevant to their professional aspirations. 
The initiative comes at a time when the importance of aligning higher education with evolving industry 
needs and career aspirations is gaining greater attention. NITI Aayog’s Reimagining Skilling for Viksit 
Bharat@2047 report, citing the Economic Survey 2024–25, notes that 8.25% of graduates are employed 
in roles aligned with their qualifications. This highlights the growing relevance of education models that 
integrate academic learning with practical skills and industry-oriented knowledge to support learners in 
preparing for the evolving world of work. 
 
About Physicswallah (PW) 
 
Physicswallah Limited (PW), an education platform, was founded in 2020 by Alakh Pandey and Prateek 
Maheshwari. Headquartered in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, PW aims to facilitate education through online, 
offline, and hybrid platforms. Initially launched as a YouTube channel in 2014, PW now offers education 
to students through its native app, tech enabled offline and hybrid centres, and YouTube channels. PW’s 
offerings span various educational segments, including test preparation, a skilling vertical (PW Skills and 
Earners), coaching for higher education, and facilitating education abroad, with programmes available in 
multiple vernacular languages. Physicswallah Limited was listed on the National Stock Exchange of India 
Limited (NSE) and BSE Limited (Bombay Stock Exchange) on November 18, 2025.
