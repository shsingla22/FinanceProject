About Edelweiss Financial Services  
Edelweiss is a diversified financial services company with seven independent and well -governed businesses. The businesses 
include  EAAA , Mutual Fund, Asset Reconstruction, Corporate L ending, Housing Finance, General  and Life  Insurance. The 
businesses have robust operating platforms, dedicated management teams and strong boards that ensure the highest 
standards of governance. Edelweiss employs nearly  6,000 people, serves over  1 crore  customers, and manages  nearly  INR 
220,000 Cr worth of assets.
