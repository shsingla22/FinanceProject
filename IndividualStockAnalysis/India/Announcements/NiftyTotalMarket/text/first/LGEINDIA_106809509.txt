LG Electronics India Announces "Celebration of Trust" Diwali Offers on 
Home Appliances and Consumer Electronics  
Cashback of up to 26%, EMIs from ₹888  onwards, zero down payment and 
extended warranties across select models  
 
New Delhi, 7th October 2026: LG Electronics India today announced its Diwali 
campaign, "Celebration of Trust,"  to celebrate a brighter Diwali with exclusive offers 
and the trust of LG. The festive offers span select ed home appliances and consumer 
electronics, with benefits designed to make upgrading the home easier and more 
rewarding.  
 
Under Diwali offers customers can enjoy:  
• Up to 26% cashback on select debit and credit cards, up to ₹30,000, on select 
models 
• Fixed EMI from ₹888 onwards, on select models  
• Zero down payment, with the rest payable in EMIs, on select models  
• 3-year warranty on select LG TVs  
• Free mini refrigerator worth up to ₹14,190 on select side -by-side refrigerators  
• Free maintenance worth ₹4,200, as part of the 1st-year package, on select 
water purifier models  
• 10-year warranty on the compressor  
• LG BestCare AMC with up to 30% discount  
 
Speaking on the campaign, Sanjay Chitkara Co -Chief Sales & Marketing Officer 
(CSMO) said: " We are humbled by the trust Indian households have placed in us 
for years. With our Celebration of Trust campaign, we aim to make this festive 
season even more rewarding by offering attractive cashback, easy EMI options, zero 
down payment, and extended warranties, helping more families bring home the latest 
upgrades with ease.  We thank our customers for their continued faith in us and wish 
everyone a happy and prosperous Diwali ".
