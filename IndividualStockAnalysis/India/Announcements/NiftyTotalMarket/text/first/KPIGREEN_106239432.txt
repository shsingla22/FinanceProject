Sub.: Grant of In -Principle ISTS Connectivity for 642.6 MW Wind Power Project in Gujarat  
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we wish to inform you that  Miyani Power Infra LLP, wholly  owned LLP of KPI Green Energy Limited 
has received an In-Principle Grant of Connectivity  from the Central Transmission Utility of India Ltd. 
(CTUIL)  for its 642.6 MW Wind Power Project located at Gujarat.  
The salient details of the grant are as under:  
• Name of Entity Granted Connectivity:  Miyani Power Infra LLP , wholly  owned LLP of KPI Green 
Energy Limited   
• Project Capacity:  642.6 MW  
• Connectivity Point:  Jam Khambhaliya, Gujarat 400 kV  
• Dedicated Transmission Line:  Sharing of KPEL -Jam Khambhaliya PS 400kV S/C line  
• Communication & Technical Standards:  As per applicable CERC and CEA Regulations  
• Purpose:  For facilitating connectivity to the Inter -State Transmission System (ISTS) under the GNA 
Regulations, 2022  
 
Miyani Power Infra LLP , wholly  owned LLP of KPI Green Energy Limited , is proud to develop  projects that 
are directly connected to the Inter -State Transmission System. The implementation of dedicated 400kV 
transmission line from the generation site to Jam Khambhaliya pooling station under ISTS marks a major 
step forward —not only in expanding ou r renewable generation portfolio, but also in contributing to India's 
national grid infrastructure.  
 
The grant of this connectivity marks a significant milestone in the implementation of our project and aligns 
with our long -term vision to contribute meaningfully to India’s renewable energy targets.  This is strategic 
backbone  that unlocks potential across not just Gujarat but India’s renewable corridor, accelerates 
market access through open access & power -exchange routes, and positions as a national -scale green -
energy enabler.
