Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the  Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  (the “SEBI Listing Regulations ”), read with the 
applicable provisions of Part A of Schedule III thereto and the SEBI Master Circular  No. 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025 -CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026  dated January 30, 2026  (“the SEBI Master 
Circular”) , we hereby inform you that Nahar Retail Trading Services Limited (“Nahar”) , a wholly 
owned subsidiary of the Company, has entered into a binding term sheet dated 22nd October 2026 
with TreBits Investment Trust , a Small and Medium Real Estate Investment Trust registered with 
the Securities and Exchange Board of India  (the “Trust”) , in relation to the proposed divestment 
of its entire shareholding (100%) in its wholly owned subsidiary, Netria Property Holdings Limited.   
 
The said acquisition of Netria by the Trust  is subject to the execution of  the definitive transaction 
documents and  completion of the initial public offer of the proposed first scheme of the Trust.  
 
Upon completion of the proposed transfer, Netria shall cease to be a subsidiary of Nahar and, 
consequently, of the Company. The Company proposes to continue to occupy the premises 
owned by Netria pursuant to appropriate lease arrangements with the Trust ac ting on behalf of 
the proposed first scheme.  
  
The disclosure in relation to the aforesaid divestment, as required under Regulation 30 read with 
Schedule III of the SEBI Listing Regulations and the SEBI Master Circular , is enclosed herewith 
as Annexure A.  
 
The above information will also be available at the Company’s website  at www.trentlimited.com.
