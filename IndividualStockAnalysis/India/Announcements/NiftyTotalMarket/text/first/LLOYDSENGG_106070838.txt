Mumbai, November 27, 2024:  
 
Overview of the Collaboration  
Lloyds Engineering Works Ltd (Lloyds) has entered a strategic partnership with Fincantieri S.p.A., a 
global leader in shipbuilding. This collaboration aims to jointly manufacture high -quality products for the 
Indian Navy and Coast Guard, enhancing India’s defence manufacturing capabilities and supporting the 
country’s self -reliance in this critical sector.  
 
Lloyds Engineering Works Ltd: Over three decades of expertise in defence manufacturing, supplying 
critical systems to the Indian Navy and Coast Guard.  
 
• Defence sector currently accounts for 6% of the company’s order book.  
 
• In April 2024, the company received orders worth ₹81 crore for the Navy. We are supplying only 
two naval components in the said  Navy Ships: Ship Steering Gear Systems and Ship Stabiliser 
Systems for 11 different Naval Ships each.   
 
• Strategic focus areas include:  
o Product diversification in defence and aerospace manufacturing.  
 
o Investments in advanced technology for warship self -defence systems.  
 
o Development of systems integrating telecommunication, signal processing, and aerospace 
engineering.

providing company in India. It provides a complete engineering and infrastructure solutions package by

Plants, Nuclear Plant Boilers and Turnkey Projects. Various authorities like the Industrial Boiler 
Regulato ry Authority, SGS UK, Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation, etc. have approved its 
facilities. The company's state -of-the-art manufacturing facilities are located in Murbad, Thane and
