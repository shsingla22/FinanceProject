Sub:  General Update - Surrender of online payment aggregator authorisation and withdrawal of 
application for authorisation to operate as issuer of prepaid payment instruments by Zomato Payments 
Private Limited (“ZPPL”)  
 
Dear Sir/Ma'am,  
 
Further to our intimation(s) dated August 4, 2021 and January 25, 2024 , we would like to inform the exchange 
that the board of directors of ZPPL, a wholly owned subsidiary of Zomato Limited (“ the Company ”) at its 
meeting held today i.e. May 13, 2024 has decided the following:  
 
- to voluntarily s urrender the certificate of authorization issued by RBI  to operate as an online payment 
aggregator; and  
- to withdraw the application dated November 11, 2021 submitted with the RBI to operate as the issuer of 
pre-paid payment instruments.  
 
Thanks to RBI’s efforts, the payments landscape in India has evolved meaningfully over the past couple of years 
since the time we applied for these licenses. This has over time, therefore, resulted in a seamless payment 
experience for customers. At Zomato,  we do not see ourselves having a significant competitive advantage against 
the incumbents in the payments space and hence we don’t foresee a business in payments space as commercially 
viable for us, at this stage. While we were conscious of these developm ents as they unfolded, the real impact 
was more apparent as we got closer to putting in place the structure to commence the operations.  
 
The Company believes that there is no material impact on the revenue/ operations of the Company on account 
of such voluntary surrender and withdrawal, hence this disclosure is being made voluntarily.  
 
The above information will also be available on the website of the Company  i.e. www.zomato.com .
