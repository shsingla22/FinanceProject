Media Release  
 
The Anup Engineering Limited and Graham join forces to expand manufacturing and 
supply capabilities  
 
GUJARAT, INDIA, 30 August, 2024  — The Anup Engineering Limited, a leading provider of 
process equipment for various industries, and Graham Corporation (USA), a global business specializing in critical equipment, have announced a strategic partnership through a Manufacturing and Supply Agreement, for India and International Business. This agreement will enable Anup Engineering to manufacture and supply a wide range of critical products for and on behalf of Graham Corporation for their global project solutions.  
 Mr. Reginaldo D'Souza, MD & CEO of The Anup Engineering Limited, expre ssed his enthusiasm 
for the partnership, stating, "This agreement marks a significant milestone for both Anup 
Engineering and Graham Corporation. We are excited to combine our expertise and resources 
to deliver exceptional products and services to our customers. Graham’s Engineering 
solutions coupled with our strong capabilities of Design & Manufacturing of process equipment, will provide significant business opportunities to both our companies. Our competitivene ss will surely help gain more market share globally for these critical products. 
Our long -term vision is to be the preferred choice for process equipment solutions globally."  
 
About The Anup Engineering Limited:  
The Anup Engineering Limited is a leading provider of process equipment for industries such 
as Oil & Gas, Petrochemicals, LNG, Hydrogen, Fertilizers, Chemicals, Power, Water, Paper & Pulp, and Aerospace. With a diverse product range and a strong focus on quality, Anup Engineering has established itself as a trusted partner for process industries worldwide.  
 About Graham Corporation:  
Graham Corporation is a global business specializing in critical equipment for the defence/space, energy, advanced energy, and chemical/petrochemical industries. With a  
reputation for excellence and innovation, Graham Corporation is committed to delivering reliable solutions that meet the unique needs of its customers. They have presence in India through their subsidiary company.
