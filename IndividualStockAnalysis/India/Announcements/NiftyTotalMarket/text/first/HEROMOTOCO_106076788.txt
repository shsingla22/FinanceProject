Further strengthening its commitment to make VIDA  aspirational, accessible , and affordable, 
Hero MotoCorp , the world's largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters, has 
launched the all -new power -packed VIDA V2 range of electric scooters.  
 
Combining practicality , convenience and comfort with  advanced technology, the VIDA V2 
seamlessly integrates innovative features with Hero’s trusted legacy of reliability, offering 
an exciting new choice for customers seeking eco -friendly mobility solutions.  
 
Featuring a bold design, the V2 is tailored for c ustomers seeking a stylish and efficient 
mobility solution. Additionally, a host of advanced features like keyless entry, cruise control, 
re-gen braking, and custom riding modes make every ride smarter, smoother, and more 
enjoyable.   
 
Powered by VIDA’s adv anced removable battery technology, the VIDA V2 ensures easy 
charging and an impressive IDC range of up to 165 km per charge. Additionally, the 
swingarm -mounted permanent magnet synchronous motor with 6kW of peak power and 
25Nm torque makes every journey e ngaging.   
 
The VIDA V2 is available in three variants – 
 V2 Lite at Rs 96,000   
 V2 Plus at Rs 115,000   
 V2 Pro at Rs 135,000
