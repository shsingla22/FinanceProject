MMRCL partners with MapmyIndia Mappls  
The Mappls App to enable access to  accurate & real-time metro data  
 
• Over 45 Million Mappls App users to benefit with  real-time Mumbai Metro information like routes, 
fares and train frequencies at a glance  
 
• The integration of Mumbai Metro Line 3 (Aqua Line) into the Mappls App will enable seamless end -
to-end journey planning across the metro network, supporting a more co nnected travel experience 
in Mumbai.  
 
Delhi , October 14, 2026:  Mumbai Metro Rail Corporation Limited (MMRCL) today signed a Memorandum 
of Understanding (MoU) with MapmyIndia Mappls , India’s leading digital mapping and deep -tech 
company, to integrate Mumbai Metro data into the Mappls App. The integration will enable commuters 
to discover Mumbai Metro Line 3 (Aqua Line) stations, explore routes, access information on fares and 
train f requencies, and plan seamless end -to-end journeys from origin to destination through the Mappls 
App. By making official metro information readily available on a single platform, the collaboration will 
benefit daily commuters, residents, visitors and touris ts, helping them make informed travel choices and 
navigate the Aqua Line network with greater ease and convenience.  
 
Commenting on the partnership, Shri R. Ramana , Director (Planning & Real Estate/NFBR), MMRCL, said, 
“Mumbai Metro Rail Corporation is committed to making metro travel more accessible, convenient and 
technology -enabled for commuters. The integration of Mumbai Metro Line 3 (Aqua Line) information 
into the Mappls App will help commuters plan their journeys more effectively, navigate the network with 
ease and access relevant travel information at one place. Our partnership with MapmyIndia marks 
another step towards delivering a smarter and more seamless m etro experience across Mumbai.”  
 
Mr. Rohan Verma , Joint Managing Director, MapmyIndia  Mappls , said, “We are happy  to partner with 
Mumbai Metro Rail Corporation to make official information easily accessible to M umbaikars  and those 
traveling to the city . Starting with Mumbai Metro Line 3 (Aqua Line), t he Mappls app will now be their 
Metro Guide and offer accurate/real -time  enabling  commuters to efficiently plan their journeys and 
access  the Mumbai Metro  network with greater ease. This collaboration demonstra tes the role of 
Swadeshi technology i s facilitating  smarter urban mobility and reinforces MapmyIndia Mappls  
commitment to wards  an Aatmanirbhar and digitally connected India.”

maps and location technology. Once integrated, Mumbai Metro Line 3 (Aqua Line) information will be 
accessible to over 45 million Mappls App users  in real -time , making it easier for commuters to access 
official information and plan t heir metro journeys with confidence :
