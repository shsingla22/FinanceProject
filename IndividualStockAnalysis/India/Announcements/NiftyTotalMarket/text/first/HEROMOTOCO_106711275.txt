VIDA Marks its Global Debut  with Entry into Nepal  
VIDA, powered by Hero, today announced its global debut into Nepal , marking  the brand's first 
international market entry and the start of its global operations through a distributor partnership 
with CG Motors (Chaudhary Group)  -  a prominent leader in Nepal’s business sector, Chaudhary 
Group  currently operates a vast network of more than 160 companies  and 123 brands worldwide.   
The VIDA VX2 EVOOTER  range and the VIDA  DIRT.E K3  youth off -roader will now be available 
to customers in Nepal to match the diverse lifestyle requirements of the Nepalese market. For 
everyday  commuters, the technologically advanced VIDA VX2 EVOOTER  portfolio  delivers an 
optimal range, robust performance, and has been ranked highest in Two -Wheeler Initial Quality 
Study  by JD Power , a global leader in intelligence and insights for the automotive ecosystem . 
Mean while  the VIDA  DIRT.E K3  offers an exciting, all -electric off -road experience tailored 
specifically for younger riders.   
Commenting on the milestone, Kausalya Nandakumar, Chief Business Officer, Emerging 
Mobility Business Unit, Hero MotoCorp , said, “This launch marks a proud and defining  moment 
as VIDA goes global. We are thrilled to take the very best of our engineering and design capabilities 
beyond India, starting with Nepal, a strategically crucial market where riders are quickly adopting 
smart electric mobility. The VIDA VX2  EVOOTER range and the VIDA DIRT.E K3 are built to redefine 
the future of mobility, and through our collaboration with CG Motors, we are ensuring  that the  
Nepalese customers have access to premium, reliable, and technologically advanced riding 
experience. ” 
Speaking on the occasion, Nirvana Chaudhary, Managing Director, Chaudhary Group , Nepal , 
added, “The entry of VIDA perfectly aligns with Nepal's shift toward s sustainable mobility. By 
offering advanced electric  products &  solutions tailored for both , everyday commuters and the next 
generation of riders, this product launch redefines our two -wheeler landscape. CG Motors is proud 
to strengthen its partnership with Hero MotoCorp and partner  with VIDA to drive our nation's green 
transition forward ." 
The V IDA VX2 EVOOTER  line-up features two distinct variants tailored to different riding needs  
of Nepalese customers : the VX2 Plus and the  VX2 Go . Designed to conquer Nepal’s challenging 
terrain, the VX2 Plus combines an 18 -degree gradeability  with a high -performance 4.4 kWh setup 
(featuring dual 2.2 kWh removable battery units). It offers a 140 km * real -world range, a top 
speed of 90 km/h, and brisk 0 –40 km/h acceleration in 3.1 seconds *. Additionally, DC fast 
charging enables a 0–80% charge i n just 65 minutes *.  
 
The VX2 Go comes equipped with a 2.2 kWh removable battery,  offering a 75 km * real world  
range  and a top speed of 70 km/h. It also delivers 0 –40 km/h  acceleration in 4. 2 seconds *, and 
supports DC fast charging (0 –80%) in 65-minutes *.  
 
Both variants prioritize user convenience with removable batteries for easy home charging via  a 
standard 5A socket, alongside a suite of smart connected  features including the My VIDA app,  
navigation, geo -fencing, call/SMS alerts, and OTA updates.  
 
Complementing the line -up, the VIDA DIRT.E K3 is an electric off -roader engineered to grow 
alongside young riders. It features a three -size adaptive system that effortlessly transforms 
across small, medium, and large configurations, ensuring continuous erg onomic support and 
postural comfort. It has a robust app -based parental controls  that  allow s adults to manage

About Hero MotoCorp  
Hero MotoCorp, headquartered in New Delhi, India, is the world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and 
scooters for 25 consecutive years with ~$5 billion revenue. The Company’s global footprint spans 5 3 
countries across Asia, Africa, Europe, Central and Latin America with a customer base of over 1 30 million. 
The Company operates eight manufa cturing facilities - six in India and one each in Colombia and 
Bangladesh - along with two Research and Development centers: Centre for Innovation and Technology 
(CIT) in India and Tech Centre Germany (TCG).  
Hero MotoCorp is driving the transition to elect ric mobility through VIDA. Strategic alliances with Harley -
Davidson and Zero Motorcycles support the design and development of premium and electric motorcycles, 
respectively. Further demonstrating its commitment to the EV future, the Company has made strat egic 
investments in two - and three -wheeler EV manufacturers, Ather Energy and Euler Motors. Hero MotoCorp 
is the only Indian two -wheeler manufacturer listed on the prestigious Dow Jones Sustainability Index 
(DJSI).  
Hero MotoCorp’s Executive Chairman, Dr. P awan Munjal, was named to the 2025 TIME100 Climate List of 
the 100 most influential leaders driving business climate action.  
Beyond mobility, Hero MotoCorp is a major global promoter of sports, including golf, football, field hockey, 
and cricket. Hero Moto Sports Team Rally is India's flag bearer in global rally racing.
