Sub.: Update on Intimation filed under Regulation 30 of the Securities and 
Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ('Listing Regulations')  
  
We refer to our letter dated August 14, 2023 (‘letter’) wherein the Company had, in 
accordance with Regulation 30 read with Para B of Part A of Schedule III of the Listing 
Regulations and SEBI circular dated July 13, 2023, informed the Stock Exchanges about 
the pending litigations/disputes (Material Disclosure).  
 
The Annexure G to the Material Disclosure included information in relation to the pending 
litigation of dispute with Service Tax Authority, Andhra Pradesh, for an aggregate amount 
of Rs 75.05 crores.  
 
In this regard, we would like to inform you that, the Company has received an order from 
Custom, Excise and Service Tax Appellate Tribunal, Hyderabad (CESTAT) dismissing 
the revenue appeal filed in this matter by Service Tax Authority, Andhra Pradesh. 
 
The copy of the said order was uploaded on website of CESTAT on April 16, 2024 which 
was downloaded by the Company on April 16, 2024 at 3:06 pm. 
 
The detailed disclosure with respect to the above as required under the SEBI Circular 
SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 is enclosed herewith as 
‘Annexure – I’. 
 
The above information is also being placed on the website of the Company at 
www.bluestarindia.com  
 
Kindly take the same on record.
