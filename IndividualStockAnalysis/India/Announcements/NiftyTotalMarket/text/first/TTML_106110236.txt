TTBS Celebrates IT Professionals Day, Calling on Businesses to Recognize Their Tech Heroes 
Leads the Charge with #DontForgetToThankIT Campaign  
 
New Delhi, January 10, 2025: , Tata Tele Business Services (TTBS) , India’s leading enabler of 
connectivity and communication solutions, celebrates second edition of the ‘IT Professionals 
Day’ today on January 10, reinforcing its commitment to honoring IT professionals who are the 
backbone of the digital age. With the #DontForgetToThankIT  campaign, TTBS continues its 
tradition of recognizing the important role played by IT professionals in ensuring seamless 
operations and driving digital transformation across industries.   
Introduced in 2024, IT Professionals Day was conceptualized by 
TTBS to provide recognition for IT teams and individuals. The 
date, January 10, symbolically reflects the binary code 10.01, 
which forms the foundation of the digital ecosystem. Since its 
launch, this annual celebration has grown into a key initiative 
for TTBS, serving as a platform for organizations and individuals 
to honor and appreciate the often-overlooked heroes of the 
digital realm. 
 
This year’s campaign celebrates the resilience, creativity, and innovation of IT professionals while also 
highlighting key technology trends shaping the industry. Cloud adoption continues to gain momentum as 
businesses seek scalable and secure solutions, with IT professionals leading these complex transitions. 
Cybersecurity remains a top priority as digital threats become more sophisticated, requiring IT teams to 
work smart to safeguard critical organizational assets. Additionally, artificial intelligence and machine 
learning are revolutionizing workflows, and IT professionals are at the forefront of integrating these 
technologies into business processes. 
  
Mr. Vishal Rally, Sr. Vice President – Product and Marketing, Tata Teleservices , shared his thoughts on 
the occasion: “IT Professionals Day has evolved beyond a celebration—it is a heartfelt tribute to the 
indispensable contributions of IT professionals who shape the digital landscape. TTBS is proud to empower 
these professionals with cutting-edge solutions that enable them to drive innovation and deliver 
measurable business success.” 
 
As part of the campaign, TTBS launched digital content celebrating the achievements of IT teams and 
encouraging businesses to join the movement by recognizing their own IT professionals. Employees, 
partners, and industry stakeholders actively engaged, amplifying the message of gratitude and 
appreciation.

TTBS’s #DontForgetToThankIT initiative is a testament to the brand’s commitment in empowering the IT 
community with solutions that address the challenges of a rapidly evolving technological landscape. By 
celebrating IT Professionals Day, TTBS aims to build a culture of acknowledgment and respect for those 
who ensure the continuity and advancement of digital infrastructure. As the campaign gains momentum, 
TTBS invites other brands and stakeholders to come together and honor the invaluable contributions of 
IT professionals, the architects of our connected world.
