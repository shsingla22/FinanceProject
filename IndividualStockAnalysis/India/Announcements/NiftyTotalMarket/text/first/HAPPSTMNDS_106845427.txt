Dear Stakeholder,  
 
We are pleased to share that your Company, Happiest Minds, continued its growth momentum in Q2 FY27, 
delivering healthy revenue growth and improved profitability, supported by disciplined execution and strong 
customer relationships.  
 
During Q2 FY27, your Company reported operating revenue of ₹645 crore , growing 12.5% year -on-year 
and 2.7% sequentially  in rupee terms. In constant currency, revenue grew 6.8% year -on-year and 2.4% 
sequentially. H1 FY27 revenue stood at ₹1,274 crore , reflecting 13.4% year -on-year growth.  
 
Operating profit grew 22.4% year -on-year to ₹119.1 crore , with operating margin improving by 100 basis 
points sequentially to 18.5% . Adjusted PAT stood at ₹79.1 crore, with Adjusted EPS of ₹5.25. For H1 FY27, 
operating profit stood at ₹228 crore and Adjusted PAT at ₹159 crore. Your Company maintained a strong balance 
sheet, with annualized ROCE of 24.0% and ROE of 14.7%.  
 
Operationally, your Company served 311 active customers , including 95 billion -dollar corporations contributing 
approximately 60% of revenue. Repeat business stood at 95.1%, IP -led revenue at 10.5%, and utilization improved 
to 81.8%. Our workforce stood at 6,466 Happiest Minds.  
 
In line with our progressive dividend policy and strong performance , the Board has declared an interim 
dividend of ₹3.05 per equity share of face value ₹2 , with November 6, 2026, as the record date . 
 
ITC Infotech also delivered a strong Q2 FY27 performance , with revenue growing 18.5% year -on-year and 
an adjusted EBITDA margin of 18.4% . Further details on its performance are available on its website.  
 
The proposed combination with ITC Infotech  continues to progress within the indicated timelines, subject to 
requisite approvals. Upon completion, the combined enterprise is expected to bring together over 19,000 
professionals , with an aspiration to achieve US$1 billion in annual revenue by FY28 . 
 
Looking ahead, we reiterate our FY27 constant currency revenue growth guidance of 12.5%  and remain 
focused on disciplined, profitable growth and creating sustainable long -term value for our shareholders.  
We thank you for your continued trust and support.
