Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Mahindra University (“MU”) and Mahindra Educational Institutions (“MEI”) wish to inform 
Mahindra & Mahindra Limited (“M&M”) that they have today, 12 August 2026, received an 
endowment contribution of M&M equity shares from Mrs. Anuradha Mahindra and Mr. 
Anand Mahindra, in fulfilment of the personal pledge of INR 500 Crore (Indian Rupees Five 
Hundred Crores) announced on 27 March 2024. 
 
MU has received 6,50,270 equity shares of M&M, representing 0.05% of M&M’s total voting 
share capital, and MEI has received 2,16,757 equity shares of M&M, representing 0.02% of 
M&M’s total voting share capital. In aggregate, the contribution comprises 8,67,027 equity 
shares, representing 0.07% of M&M’s total voting share capital. 
 
The contribution is an endowment contribution made without consideration and no proceeds 
will accrue to Mrs Anuradha Mahindra and Mr Anand Mahindra in connection with it.
