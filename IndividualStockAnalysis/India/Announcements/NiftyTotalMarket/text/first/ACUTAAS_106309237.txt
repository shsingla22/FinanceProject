With reference to the captioned subject and pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule Il of the 
Listing Regulations, we wish to inform you that Acutaas Chemicals Limited (“the Company”) has on

exceeding Rs. 150 crores by way of loan or equity subscription or mix of both, in one or more tranches 
in AAML, wholly owned subsidiary of the Company.
