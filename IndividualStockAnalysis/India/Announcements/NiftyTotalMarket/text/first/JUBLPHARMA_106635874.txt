Brief Profile of Mr. Priyavrat Bhartia  
  
 
 
 
Mr. Priyavrat Bhartia, 49 years, has around 29 years of industry experience. He holds a 
Bachelors’ Degree in Economics from Dart mouth College, USA and Masters in Business 
Administration from Stanford University, USA. He  has served as a Non-Executive Director on 
the Board of Jubilant Pharmova Limited since May, 2017 and was re-designated as a Managing 
Director of the Company since June, 2023. He has demonstrated strong leadership 
capabilities, strategic acumen, deep business and industry understanding and provide 
strategic commitment to the Company’s long term vision.  
  
He will continue to be entrusted with the overall management and administration of the 
Company’s affairs, subject to the superintendence,  control and direction of the Board. In view 
of his qualifications, professional experience and his extensive involvement in the operations 
of the Company, the Board is of the opinion that the Company will continue to benefit 
significantly from his leadership, strategic insights and guidance.
