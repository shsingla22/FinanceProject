Sub:  Reduction of Share Capital of Konkan LNG Limited pursuant to implementation of 
Hon'ble NCLT Order dated 03.06.2026  
Sir/Madam,  
This is in continuation of our letter of even no. dated 10.06.2026.   
 
It is informed that pursuant to the implementation of the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal 
(NCLT) Order dated 03.06.2026 approving the Scheme for Reduction of Equity Share Capital of 
Konkan LNG Limited (KLL), the consideration was remitted to GAIL (India) L imited (GAIL) on 
06.07.2026. KLL informed that the consideration was also remitted to MSEB Holding Company 
Limited (MSEB) on same date i.e. on 06.07.2026.  
 
Accordingly, as informed by KLL,  upon payment of the consideration to the respective shareholders  
by KLL , an aggregate of 14,81,10,440 equity shares of ₹10 each, including 7,40,55,220 equity 
shares held by GAIL and 7,40,55,220 equity shares held by MSEB in KLL stand cancelled  and 
extinguished  i.e. MSEB holding in KLL reduced to Nil  and GAIL shareholding increased to  100% 
of the equity share capital of KLL.  
 
This disclosure  is in compliance of Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
