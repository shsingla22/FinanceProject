PRESS RELEASE  
 
LEELA PALACES, HOTELS & RESORTS DELIVERS CONTINUED OUTPERFORMANCE IN  
Q2 FY27 WITH 30% REVPAR GROWTH AND 36% OPERATING EBITDA GROWTH  
 
 Q2FY27 Operating Revenue grows 33% to ₹4, 119 million and Operating EBITDA rises 36% to ₹1, 877 
million; PAT up 27% to ₹ 946 million  
 
Mumbai, 14 October 2026:  Leela Palaces Hotels & Resorts Limited (NSE: THELEELA), India's only 
listed pure -play luxury hospitality company, today announced another quarter of industry -leading 
growth, reaffirming the strength of its premium positioning, pricing power and disciplined  expansion 
strategy. Backed by resilient domestic and reviving international luxury demand, and one of the 
industry's strongest luxury development pipelines, the Company continues to strengthen its position as 
India's premier luxury hospitality platform  and ranks #2 among the World’s Best Hotel Brands . 
Commenting on the results, Mr. Anuraag Bhatnagar, Whole -time Director and Chief Executive 
Officer , said, “Our Q2 FY27 performance is a testament to the enduring strength of The Leela brand 
and the structural tailwinds in India’s luxury hospitality market. Our o perating revenue grew 33% driving 
a 36% rise in operating EBITDA , reflecting our robust business fundamentals and operational 
excellence across verticals. Our 30% RevPAR growth in Q2 was led by a record 30% increase in ADR, 
demonstrating our sustained pricing power and the strength of demand for differentiated luxury 
experiences. Importantly, the demand  was broad -based across retail, corporate and leisure segments, 
supported by strong demand for our suites and our ability to command a premium during marquee 
events.”

Q2 FY27 FINANCIAL AND OPERATIONAL PERFORMANCE  
The Company delivered another quarter of industry -leading financial performance, translating premium 
brand strength into superior revenue growth, margin expansion and profitability . 

 
 
 
2 Page  
 • Operating revenue grew 33% YoY to ₹4,119 million, while operating EBITDA increased 36% to 
₹1,877 million and profit for the period rose 27% to ₹ 946 million. Operating EBITDA margin 
stood at 46% in Q2 FY27, up 118 bps YoY.  
• RevPAR increased 30% YoY to ₹17,225, led by a 30% rise in ADR to ₹ 25,046, on an expanded 
portfolio including Coorg.  
• Record ADR  growth was  supported by robust demand across key customer segments and 
sustained traction in the luxury hospitality market. Domestic segment witnessed a 30%+ 
revenue growth , and continued premiumization was  reflected in a more than 700 basis points 
improvement in suite occupancy. Demand was further bolstered by marquee events such as 
the BRICS Summit  in Delhi . This reiterates  The Leela's track record of capturing high -value 
event demand, from the G20 in FY24 through India Energy Week, the AI Impact Summit, and 
upcoming events such as Aero India scheduled for Q4 FY27.  
• Performance remained broad -based  across business segments : Room Revenue grew 3 9%, 
F&B Revenue grew 2 1%, and HMA Fees were  up 76% reflecting strong ramp -up of managed 
properties and a growing brand footprint.  Non-room revenue exceeded 50% of total revenue in 
H1 FY27, reflecting continued diversification beyond rooms.   
• Brand website contribution to room revenue reached 15.1% in H1 FY27, a 2.8x increase since 
H1 FY25, improving distribution economics and reducing reliance on third -party channels.  
GLOBAL BRAND RECOGNITION AND LEADERSHIP  IN GUEST ADVOCACY  
The Leela’s operating performance is underpinned by strong guest advocacy and growing global 
recognition.  
• The Leela continued  its Net Promoter Score (NPS) leadership with a score of 87 in H1FY27, 
significantly ahead of the luxury segment benchmark of 74 in the APAC region, reaffirming its 
focus on service excellence and guest -centricity.  
• Three Palaces Awarded Michelin Keys for Second  Consecutive Years . The Leela Palace New 
Delhi  and The Leela Palace Jaipur  were awarded two Michelin keys and The Leela Palace 
Chennai  was awarded one Michelin key . 
• ZLB23 was recognized among the 30 Best Bars in India.  
DISCIPLINED CAPITAL - EFFICIENT EXPANSION  
The Company continues to execute its capital -efficient growth strategy by expanding into India's highest -
value luxury destinations while maintaining a balanced portfolio of owned and managed assets.  
• The Company's pipeline of 1,038 keys across 10 properties is progressing on schedule, 
spanning into Wildlife, Heritage, Hill Station, Desert  and Spiritual.   
• The Leela Jaisalmer and The Leela Luxury Residences, Mumbai which marks  the brand’s foray  
into a new business vertical are both in active pre -opening stage  with plans to commence 
operations  in FY27 . 
BALANCE SHEET BUILT TO SUPPORT GROWTH  
• Net debt stood at ₹ 12,150 million as of September 30, 2026, with Net debt to LTM EBITDA 
improving to  1.4x, compared to 1.6x in March 2026 and 3.7x in March 2025, providing 
substantial headroom to fund future growth.

About The Leela Palaces, Hotels and Resorts  
The Leela Palaces, Hotels and Resorts is India’s largest institutionally owned and managed pure -play 
luxury hospitality brand. Backed by Brookfield, The Leela’s portfolio comprises 15 operating  award -
winning properties  spanning owned, operated, and managed hotels and another 10 in the pipeline 
across leading  leisure and business destinations. Recognized globally for architectural excellence and 
bespoke services, The Leela was ranked #2 globally among the best Hotel Brand in the World by the 
Travel + Leisure World's Best Awards 2026 and  has been previously ranked as the World’s Best Hotel 
Brand by Travel + Leisure World’s Best Awards  in 2020 and 2021, and among the global top three in 
2023, 2024 and 2026. The brand remains committed to delivering timeless Indian hospitality through 
world -class luxury experiences.
