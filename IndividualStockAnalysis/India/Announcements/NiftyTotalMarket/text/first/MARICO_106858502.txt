PRESS NOTE  
 
Marico unveils Nihar Shanti Mazbooti Ki Paathshala , a renewed identity for its 
flagship  CSR education initiative  
 
The renewed identity brings the programme’s educational efforts under the unifying belief, “Mazboot 
Faislon Se Banta Hai Mazboot Bhavishya” , reaffirming commitment to developing stronger futures 
through education  
 
National, November 13 , 2026 : Marking the occasion of the upcoming Children's Day, Marico Limited, one 
of India’s leading FMCG companies, announced a renewed identity for its flagship education -focused CSR 
initiative, with Nihar Shanti Pa athshala Funwala evolving into Nihar Shanti Mazbooti Ki Paathshala . This 
transition aligns the initiative with the evolving purpose of Nihar Shanti celebrating and empowering 
women to take strong decisions that help their families and build a better future.  
 
Over the years, the programme has focused on improving foundational learning and educational 
outcomes among children from underserved communities with a focus on having meaningful impact in 
society through education. Through its various interventions, the programme has trained more than 1.26 
lakh teachers and benefitted approximately 12.69 lakh students through various interventions. In FY2025 -
26 alone, the initiative has benefited 3,500+ children (3 -6 years of age), 260 Anganwadi Workers and 
Helpers, and 1 ,500+ parents.  
 
Commenting on the transition, Amit Bhasin, Chief Legal Officer & Group Counsel and Secretary, CSR 
Committee, Marico Limited  said, “ Education has the power to strengthen not only a child’s future but also 
uplift families and communities. Over the years, we have worked to improve learning outcomes and create 
greater possibilities for children through accessible, high -quality education. Through our focus on 
Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (FLN), aligned with the vision of the National Education Policy (NEP), 
we are committed to helping children develop the essential skills, confidence and learning foundation 
needed to thrive in an evol ving world. With the renewed identity, Nihar Shanti Mazbooti Ki Paathshala 
moves beyond creating moments of fun and engagement to creating meaningful, long -term impact. This 
move reaffirms our belief that strong educational foundations can help build stron ger futures for children, 
and our societies at large.”  
 
The evolution reflects the belief that  good quality  education is one of the strongest decisions an individual, 
family or society can make. By supporting children’s learning and development, Mazbooti Ki Paathshala 
aims to help build the confidence, capabilities and opportunities required to create a stronge r future  for 
India . The transition brings the brand and its CSR platform together under one unifying thought – 
‘Mazboot Faislon Se Banta Hai Mazboot Bhavishya’. While the brand celebrates women who make strong 
decisions, the CSR initiative supports education that empowers c hildren and strengthens their prospects 
for a stronger future.
