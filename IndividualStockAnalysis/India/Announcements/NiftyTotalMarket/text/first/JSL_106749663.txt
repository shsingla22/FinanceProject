Jindal Stainless wins multiple honours at People Matters Infini -T Awards 2026  
 
• Recognised for driving digital transformation across HR operations and enhancing employee 
experience  
 
New Delhi , August 1 9, 2026 : Jindal Stainless, India's leading stainless steel manufacturer, has been 
recognised with two honours at the People Matters Infini -T Awards 2026, winning the Gold Award in the 
HR Operations category and the Silver Award in Digital Adoption & Change Leadership. The awards 
recognise the Company's efforts to transform its HR function into a digitally enabled, scalable and 
employee -centr ic ecosystem.  
 
The recognition reflects Jindal Stainless' organisation -wide transformation of HR operations, implemented 
across 17 entities, covering more than 12,700 employees  (direct as well as indirect)  across corporate 
offices and manufacturing facilities. The Company complemented its technology rollout with a structured 
change management programme involving employee co -created workflows, certified change champions, 
pilot deployments and extensive engag ement initiatives to drive organisation -wide adoption.

Speaking on the recognition, Chief Human Resources Officer, Jindal Stainless, Mr Sushil Baveja, said , 
“Digital transformation creates lasting value only when it improves everyday experiences for employees. 
Our focus has been on building HR systems that are simple, accessible and scalable while ensuring that 
people remain at the centre of every change. By c ombining technology, AI -enabled automation and strong 
change management, we have enhanced operational efficiency while delivering a more seamless 
employee experience. These recognitions reflect the collective efforts of our teams in building a future -
ready  workplace that supports the Company's long -term growth.”  
 
The transformation has delivered measurable outcomes across talent acquisition, employee experience 
and HR operations. Time -to-hire has significantly reduced, while the digital recruitment process has 
enabled the Company to process more than 11,000 candidates and optimise recruitment costs, resulting 
in cost savings in external consultant spend. Employee satisfaction  from HR process and tools  increased 
from 45% to 87%, while employee Net Promoter Score (eNPS) rose to 64.  
 
The Company has also enhanced HR service delivery through AI -powered automation and end -to-end 
digitisation of employee processes. A 24x7 AI -enabled HR assistant now resolves more than 95% of routine 
employee queries, while self -service workflows have enab led 100% employee -led leave and attendance 
management without manual HR intervention. Integrated digital systems across HRMS, payroll, SAP, 
banking and enterprise platforms have streamlined employee lifecycle processes, reducing monthly HR 
helpdesk tickets  by 91%. This has enabled the HR function to support the Company's growing workforce 
without a corresponding increase in administrative headcount . 
 
 
 
About Jindal Stainless  
India’s leading stainless steel manufacturer, Jindal Stainless, had an annual turnover of INR 42,95 5 crore (USD 4. 86 
billion) in FY2 6 and an annual melt capacity of 4.2 million tonnes . It has 16 stainless steel manufacturing and 
processing facilities in India and abroad, including in Spain and Indonesia, and a worldwide network in 12 countries, 
as of March 202 6. In India, there are ten sales offices and six service centres, as of March 202 6. The company’s 
product range includes stainless steel slabs, blooms, coils, plates, sheets, precision strips, wire rods, rebars, blade 
steel, and coin blanks.  
 
Jindal Stainless relies on its integrated operations to enhance its cost competitiveness and operational efficiency. 
Founded in 1970, Jindal Stainless continues to be inspired by a vision for innovation and enriching lives and is 
committed to social respon sibility.  
 
Jindal Stainless remains focused on a greener and sustainable future. The company manufactures stainless steel 
using electric arc furnace, a process that significantly reduces greenhouse gas emissions and allows for recyclability 
of scrap without compromis ing on quality.
