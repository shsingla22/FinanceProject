Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Regulations) 2015, we hereby inform that, in the Board 
Meeting held today (17.05.2024 ),  the Board noted the following:  
 
Maamba Collieries Limited (MCL), the Company’s Zambian step down subsidiary has a subsisting 
Small Scale Mining License for Magnetite ore in the Central Province of Zambia.  
 
This License was originally issued in 1997 for an extent of 92.4 Ha, for Magnetite ore which was 
intended by MCL (before privatization) for use in its erstwhile coal processing plant  without 
progressing further . This License was being renewed from time to time and is currently valid up to 
6th May , 2027.  
 
 A recent Magnetic Survey of the license d area has revealed  the prevalence of high -quality 
Magnetite ore, amenable for wide usage. The survey has also indicated continuity of ore beyond 
the license d area, suggestive  of large resource . 
 
In this context, MCL  has sought and obtained approval of the Government for additional mining 
rights taking the total licensed area to about 323 Ha.  
 
MCL proposes to carry out detailed exploration of the license d area over the next one year to 
ascertain  the mineable resources and extractable reserves for commercial exploitation  following 
which a business plan will be drawn up.
