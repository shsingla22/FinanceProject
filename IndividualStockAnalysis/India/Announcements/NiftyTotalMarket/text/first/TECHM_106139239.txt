Tech Mahindra Named Global Partner of the Year by Temenos  
Pune  – February 6th, 2025:  Tech Mahindra  (NSE: TECHM), a leading global provider of 
technology consulting and digital solutions to enterprises across industries, announced it has been 
named the Global Partner of the Year by Temenos at its annual sales and partner kick -off event , 
TKO 2025, held in Dubai. Temenos is a global leader in banking software , with comprehensive  
best-of-suite core banking and best-of-breed  solutions for financial functions such as deposits, 
payments, lending, and digital banking.  
This prestigious recognition highlights Tech Mahindra’s excellence in delivery, sales, and 
innovation across the globe, showcasing its expertise as a certified Migration, Upgrade, 
Development, and Maintenance partner of Temenos . With over two decades of partnership, Tech 
Mahindra has consistently driven successful system integration and transformation projects, 
including eight go-lives in 2024, spanning across the Americas, APAC, and the Middle East and 
Africa.  
Atul Soneja, Chief Operating Officer, Tech Mahindra, said,  “Technology is transforming 
financial services, driven by SaaS- based solutions. Tech Mahindra is focused on transforming 
legacy core systems with modular capabilities, and being recognized as Temenos’ Global Partner 
of the Year reaffirms our position as a trusted partner in driving digital transformation. This 
accolade reflects the collaborative efforts of our teams worldwide and commitment to delivering 
innovation and excellence in the banking industry.”  
Over the years, Tech Mahindra has collaborated with Temenos to deliver solutions for leading 
banks , most recently with  CEC Bank in Romania . Together, in 2024, Tech Mahindra and Temenos 
also develo ped a tailored core banking offering based on Temenos SaaS , specifically for Electronic 
Money Institutions (EMIs) in the UK and Europe. These achievements underscore Tech 
Mahindra’s deep alignment with Temenos’ vision and its ability to deliver impactful, scalable 
solutions.  
Monty Bhatia, EVP Global Alliances and Partner Ecosystem , Temenos , said, 
“Congratulations to Tech Mahindra on this well -deserved award , which reflects the strength of 
 
 
our collaboration over many year s. We’re pleased to recognize the important role Tech Mahindra 
plays in delivering enhanced value and faster innovation to Temenos customers around the world.”  
Competing against leading global system integrators, this recognition underscores the trust and 
respect Tech Mahindra has earned within the Temenos ecosystem. It reflects the organization’s 
relentless focus on innovation, customer -centricity, and delivering measurable outcomes.
