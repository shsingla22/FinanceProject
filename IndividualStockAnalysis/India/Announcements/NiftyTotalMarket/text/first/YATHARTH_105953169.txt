1. Considered and approved Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services

1.1 The Option Pool of the Scheme shall be 2,50,000 (Two Lakh Fifty 
Thousand) Employee Stock Options (“Options”)  (or such other 
adjusted figure for any bonus, stock splits or consolidations or other 
reorganization of the capital structure of the Company as may be 
applicable from time to time and as defined in the scheme) exercisable 
into be 2,50,000 (Two Lakh Fifty Thousand) Equity Shares, having face 
value of Rs. 10/- each, on such price as determined by the Nomination 
and Remuneration Committee, after providing suitable discount . 
 
1.2 Each Option is convertible into 1 (One) Equity Share of the Company. 
 
1.3 The Scheme shall be administered by the Nomination and 
Remuneration Committee of the Company and shall be implemented 
through direct route, for extending the benefits to the eligible 
Employees by way of fresh allotment from the Company. 
 
2. Considered and approved of the grant of Options to Employees of the 
Company including Subsidiary, in India under Yatharth Hospital & Trauma 
Care Services Employee Stock Option Scheme – 2024. 
  
 
 
 
3. Appointed M/s Corporate Professionals Capital Private Limited, a category I
