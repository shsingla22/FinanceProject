Sub: Intimation  under  Regulation  30 of SEBI (Listing  Obligations  and Disclosure  
Requirements) Regulations, 2015  for  Update on Notice issued to Tata Chemicals Magadi Limited  
 
In continuation to our disclosure dated July 29, 2026 , under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 , on the Notice from the Ministry of Mining, Blue 
Economy and Maritime Affairs, Kenya (the Ministry) dated July 28, 2026 to Tata Chemicals Magadi 
Limited (TCML)  the Kenyan subsidiary of Tata Chemicals Limited  (the Company) , and the status update 
provided on September 9, 2026 thereof , the Company is providing  a further  update on the said matter 
as mentioned hereunder.  
 
The High Court of Kenya has passed an order dated October 23, 2026, that the Hon’ble Court has no 
jurisdiction on the dispute and cannot take any further steps. TCML, is examining the order and will take 
legal and all other necessary steps for way forward in this matter.  
 
It may further be noted that the Committee led by the  Principal Secretary for Mining  on behalf of the 
Ministry and the  Chief Executive Officer of TCML  is in the process of undertaking a detailed technical 
review of all matters and will submit a report to the office of the Cabinet Secretary for consideration and 
further direction to establish a sustainable framework that promotes responsible mining, value addition, 
community development and mutually beneficial partnerships.  
 
We request  you to kindly  take the above  information  on record.
