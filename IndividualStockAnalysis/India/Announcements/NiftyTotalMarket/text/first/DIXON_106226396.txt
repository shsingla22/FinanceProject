Dixon’s Wholly Owned Subsidiary Padget Electronics partners with NxtCell India to lead Alcatel’s 
‘Make in India’ Manufacturing Ecosystem  
 
 Dixon  Technologies (India) Limited’s  Wholly Owned Subsidiary , Padget Electronics Private Limited (“Padget  
Electronics ”) has entered into a Contract Manufacturing Agreement with NxtCell India  Private Limited  
(“NxtCell India”)  to manufacture  smartphones  for the iconic French tech brand Alcatel in the Indian market.  
 
Noida, 9th May, 2025  
 
Dixon’s wholly owned subsidiary - Padget Electronics has entered into a Contract Manufacturing Agreement with 
NxtCell India to lead Alcatel’s ‘Make in India’ Manufacturing Ecosystem.  
 
Commenting on this association, Mr. Atul B. Lall, Vice Chairman & Managing Director , Dixon 
Technologies (India) L imited  said “We are delighted to announce that Padget Electronics has entered into a 
Contract Manufacturing  Agreement with NxtCell India  to manufacture smartphones for  the iconic brand 
Alcatel . This strategic association with NxtCell India will further strengthen our manufacturing excellence and 
execution capabilities. Together, we shall aim to bring premium, next -generation products  with innovative 
technology to the Indian market . This partnership is engineered to deliver high -performance devices, fortify 
local supply chains, catalyse employment, and stimulate ancillary industries within India’s burgeoning 
electronics ecosystem.”  
 
About Dixon Technologies (India) Limited   
 
Dixon Technologies (India) Limited is the largest* home grown design -focused and solutions company engaged 
in manufacturing products in the consumer durables, lighting and mobile phones markets in India. Their 
diversified product portfolio includes (i) Consumer electronics like LED TVs; (ii) Home appliances like washing 
machines; (iii) Lighting products like LED bulbs and tubelights, downlighters; (iv) Mobile phones; and (v) 
Wearables and Hearables (vi) Refrigerat ors and (vii) Telecom and IT hardware products. Dixon also provides 
solutions in reverse logistics i.e. repair and refurbishment services of LED TV panels.    
 
*Source: Project Rise: Indian Consumer Electronics & Appliances Market Study, issued by Frost & Sullivan 
India Private Limited  
 
About NxtCell India  
 
NxtCell India Private Limited is a 100% Indian -domicile company, wholly owned by Indian shareholders. 
Headquartered in Gurgaon, NxtCell holds exclusive brand authorization from TCL to represent and opera te the 
Alcatel brand in India and select international markets. The company is led by a dynamic leadership team 
comprising Ansh  Rathi, Chief Operating Officer  and Atul Vivek, Chief Business Officer bringing together 
strategic vision and operational excelle nce. With a strong focus on innovation, accessibility, and customer -
centricity, NxtCell is committed to redefining the consumer technology landscape in India. NxtCell India plans to 
start smartphone manufacturing within the next 12 to 18 months.
