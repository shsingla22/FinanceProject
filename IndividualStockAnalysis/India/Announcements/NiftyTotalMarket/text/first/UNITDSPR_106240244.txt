Bengaluru , India - May  20, 2025: United Spirits L td., one of the leading beverage alcohol compan ies in 
India , reported its audited  consolidated & standalone  results  for the fourth quarter and financial  year  
ended 31 March  2025. 
 
Mr. Praveen Someshwar , CEO & Managing Director , commenting on the FY25 performance, said:   
 
 “The challenging demand environment notwithstanding, we have delivered 13.2% NSV growth for 
P&A in Q4FY25 and 9.9% P&A growth for FY25, and a leveraged EBITDA growth  that takes us to our 
medium -term guidance .   
The Board of Directors have recommended a final dividend of INR8.0 per share for the fiscal year  
2024 -25, subject to Shareholder’s approval.  
Looking ahead, we remain focused on delivering sustained growth while creating long -term value
