TARIL Announces Rs. 1,250 Crore Investment in Three Greenfield 
Manufacturing Plants to Strengthen India's Power Infrastructure Ecosystem and 
Create 1,000+ Direct Jobs  
 
New manufacturing facilities at Chiyada , Gujarat, to localise critical transformer 
components and support India's journey towards Viksit Bharat 2047  
 
Ahmedabad, October 2026 : As India undertakes one of the world's largest power 
infrastructure expansions to meet rising electricity demand, renewab le energy 
integration and transmission network modernisation, Transformers and Rectifiers 
(India) Limited (TARIL) today announced an investment of Rs. 1,250 crore  to establish 
three greenfield manufacturing plants  at its Chiyada manufacturing complex in 
Gujarat. The new facilities will manufacture Bushings (OIP, RIP & RIF up to 800 kV), 
Pressboard & Insulation Materials and Continuously Transposed Conductors 
(CTC) , critical components that form the backbone of high -voltage transformer 
manufacturing and are e ssential for strengthening India's power transmission 
infrastructure.  
 
As India expands its transmission network to integrate renewable energy, strengthen 
interstate power connectivity and meet growing electricity demand, the availability of 
critical transf ormer components has become increasingly important for ensuring 
reliable and timely project execution.  
 
The investment represents a significant step towards building a more resilient and self -
reliant domestic power equipment ecosystem. By localising the man ufacturing of these 
critical components, TARIL aims to reduce import dependence, strengthen supply 
chain resilience and improve the availability of critical equipment required for India's 
rapidly expanding transmission and distribution network.  
 
The three p lants will be housed under a wholly owned consolidated ancillary subsidiary  
of TARIL, providing complete control over manufacturing quality, technology, 
procurement and long -term strategic direction. Construction of these facilities 
commenced in December 2 025, with commercial operations expected to begin in Q1 
FY2027 -28. Once fully operational, the facilities are expected to generate an annual 
revenue potential of approximately Rs. 2,000 crore  while creating more than 1,000 
direct employment opportunities  across engineering, manufacturing, operations, 
quality assurance and research & development.  
 
The Bushing manufacturing facility will produce Oil Impregnated Paper (OIP), Resin 
Impregnated Paper (RIP) and Resin Impregnated Fibre (RIF) bushings up to 800 
kV, among the most technologically advanced components used in extra -high-voltage 
transformers. Together with the Pressboard & Insulation and CTC facilities, the plants 
will significantly strengthen TARIL's vertically integrated manufacturing ecosystem and 
reinforce India's capability to manufacture critical power equipment components 
domestically.  
 
 
 
 
Speaking on the announcement, Mr. Jitendra Mamtora, Chairman, Transformers 
and Rectifiers (India) Limited (TARIL),  said:  "India's aspiration to become a 
developed nation by 2047 will require one of the world's most robust and future -ready 
power infrastructure ecosystems. Every new transmission corridor, renewable energy 
project and industrial corridor depends on reliable pow er equipment. Our investment 
is aligned with this national vision by strengthening domestic manufacturing 
capabilities for critical transformer components, reducing import dependence and 
contributing to a resilient power infrastructure ecosystem built in I ndia, for India and 
the world."  
 
Commenting on the strategic investment, Mr. Satyen Mamtora, Managing Director 
& CEO, Transformers and Rectifiers (India) Limited (TARIL),  said:  "This 
investment represents far  more than a capacity expansion, it is a strategi c move 
towards deep vertical integration. Manufacturing Bushings, Pressboard & Insulation 
materials and CTC in -house will provide greater control over quality, lead times and 
operational efficiency while improving supply chain resilience. As India's demand  for 
transformers continues to grow, we are building an integrated manufacturing platform 
capable of supporting the country's evolving power infrastructure requirements while 
creating sustainable long -term value for customers, shareholders and the nation."  
 
With this investment, TARIL continues to strengthen its position as one of India's 
leading transformer manufacturers while contributing to the creation of a resilient, self -
reliant and globally competitive power infrastructure ecosystem that will support India's 
support India's expanding transmission network, renewable energy ambitions and 
long-term energy security . The project also reinforces the Government of India's Make 
in India  and Atmanirbhar Bharat  initiatives by strengthening indigenous 
manufacturi ng capabilities in the power equipment sector.
