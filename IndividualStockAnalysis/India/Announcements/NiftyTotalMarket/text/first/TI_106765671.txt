Tilaknagar Industries enters the Tequila market through a ₹22 Crore Strategic 
Investment in Black Tiger Distilleries, Maker of Bodega Suprema No. 5   
 
The investment marks the company’s third strategic investment in an emerging craft beverage company and its 
first foray into the agave category, specifically tequila, supporting Black Tiger Distilleries’ ambition to make 
authentic 100% Blue Weber Agave tequila more accessible in India  
Recently launched in India, Bodega Suprema No. 5 is a range of 100% Blue Weber Agave tequilas, crafted and 
bottled at origin in Jalisco, Mexico  
 
Mumbai, September 02, 2026: Tilaknagar Industries Ltd. (TI), one of India’s leading alcoholic beverage 
companies, has entered India’s fast-growing tequila category through a strategic investment in Black 
Tiger Distilleries (BTD), the Indian spirits company behind Bodega Suprema No. 5. As part of the 
partnership, TI will invest a total of ₹22 crore (₹6 crore in the first tranche, followed by a second 
investment of ₹16 crore on or before 12 months from first tranche) for a 30% shareholding in the 
company on a fully diluted basis. 
Tequila today is one of the world's fastest-growing premium spirits, and India plays a meaningful part 
in that story. According to IWSR, India is the fastest-growing major tequila market globally; volumes 
grew at approximately 60% CAGR between 2020 and 2025, up approximately 34% in the last year 
alone, with a 13% CAGR growth forecast through 2030. 
Tequila is gaining ground in India, driven by growing interest in premium spirits, cocktail culture and 
new drinking occasions. This is creating space for a wider range of authentic tequilas, across price 
points and styles, to find a place in the market. So far, consumers have effectively had two options: 
inexpensive mixto tequila, or genuine 100% Blue Weber Agave tequila priced firmly in the luxury 
segment. A genuine 100% Blue Weber Agave tequila at an accessible and attractive price point has 
been elusive, till now.  
Black Tiger Distilleries was founded by Mohnish Mehta to bridge that gap, supported by Productos 
Finos de Agave (PFA) – a tequila manufacturing company in Mexico and Mansha Group of Companies 
– one of India’s leading alcohol beverage distribution companies for craft and premium products. Its 
flagship Bodega Suprema No. 5 is a 100% agave tequila, positioned at ₹4,200–₹5,500 per bottle , 
offering consumers an authentic tequila expression at an accessible premium price point. Tilaknagar 

Industries has entered a strategic partnership by investing in this venture, supporting its plans to build 
and grow the tequila category in India. 
Sanaya Dahanukar, General Manager – Growth & Innovation, Tilaknagar Industries Ltd., said , “What 
excites us about Bodega Suprema No. 5 is the opportunity to enter a category we see as an emerging 
trend with significant long-term potential. As we build House of TI into a more comprehensive luxury 
portfolio, having a presence in tequila is an important part of that evolution, alongside categories such 
as whisky, brandy, gin, rum and vodka . It allows us to bring our experience in building premium spirits 
into a category with strong consumer interest, while creating a more diverse portfolio that reflects how 
drinking preferences in India are evolving.”  
Ameya Deshpande, Chief Strategy Officer, Tilaknagar Industries Ltd., adds , “Tequila represents an 
exciting opportunity for us to participate in a category that is still at an early stage of development in 
India, but has strong potential to grow. With Bodega Suprema No. 5 positioned at ₹4,200–₹5,500, we 
see an opportunity to bring authentic 100% agave tequila to a wider premium consumer base while 
building the category with the right focus on provenance and quality. Our role is to bring our experience 
in building beverage brands and our understanding of the Indian market, while enabling Black Tiger 
Distilleries to build a distinctive tequila proposition for India.”  
For Tilaknagar Industries, the investment marks the company’s third strategic investment in an 
emerging beverage business and its first foray into the tequila category. Beyond capital, TI will provide 
strategic support across distribution, sales, marketing, supply chain, finance and regulatory functions, 
leveraging its pan-India footprint and experience in building and scaling premium spirits brands. 
Mohnish Mehta, Founder, Black Tiger Distilleries, said , “As we built Black Tiger Distilleries, finding the 
right partners with deep category expertise and a strong understanding of the Indian spirits markets 
was key. With Tilaknagar Industries’ support, we see an opportunity to make authentic 100% Blue 
Weber Agave tequila more accessible to Indian consumers at a compelling premium price point. 
Bodega Suprema No. 5 is the first step in building that proposition and introducing it to consumers 
across India.”  
Introducing Bodega Suprema No. 5  
Bodega Suprema No. 5 has three expressions made exclusively for the Indian market that took three 
years to develop. Every bottle of Bodega is made from 100% Blue Weber Agave, sourced from the 
Highlands of Jalisco, where the agave matures at 7,152 feet above sea level. The region's higher 
elevation and red, mineral-rich volcanic soil help develop the naturally sweeter, fruit-forward 
character that Highland tequila is known for.  
The name Bodega reflects the role the brand hopes to play in people's lives. In New York City, a bodega 
is the neighbourhood corner store: an everyday institution where people step in not just to shop, but 
to chat, discover local products and feel a part of the community. Just as a neighbourhood bodega 
becomes part of everyday routines, the brand believes good tequila shouldn't be reserved for 
milestone celebrations or special occasions; it should have a place at the dinner table, behind the bar, 
and among friends. 
The bottle carries the same philosophy as the liquid inside. Instead of relying on familiar imagery from 
Mexico such as sombreros, skulls or Gold Rush-era graphics, the label design draws on the idea of 
cultural exchange between Mexico and India. One of its key references is the visual language of mid-
century diplomatic postage stamps, created to commemorate friendships and trade between nations.  
 
The Range  
Bodega Blanco  
Blanco is where the Bodega range starts, and the 
expression made with the least intervention. It never 
touches wood, so there's nothing standing between 
the drinker and the agave itself, and that's why the 
production process is built the way it is. The agave is 
cooked slowly in brick ovens rather than industrial 
autoclaves, fermented in open stainless-steel tanks, 
and distilled through column stills: a process aimed at 
drawing out the plant's own character. What is 
bottled is fresh, bright, and agave-forward, easy to 
drink neat or on the rocks, and versatile enough to 
shake into a classic margarita. 
 
 Tasting Notes:  Fresh Agave · Mint · Caramel 
· Roasted Pumpkin · Cashew 
 Appearance:  Crystal clear with delicate 
silver hues. 
 Best enjoyed:  Neat or on the rocks, or 
shaken into a classic margarita; alongside 
fresh ceviche, citrus dishes, or a wedge of 
lime 
 ABV: 38% | Sizes: 750ml | 50ml 
 Price (in Maharashtra):  
 750ml – ₹4,200 
 50ml – ₹400  
 Bodega Reposado  
Reposado takes the 100% Blue Weber agave spirit 
along the same route, with an additional destination: 
an ex-Tennessee whiskey American oak barrel. Once 
the liquid comes off the still, it moves into these 
barrels for a minimum of two months: just long 
enough to pick up warmth and roundness without 
burying the natural agave flavour.  
 
 Tasting Notes:  Vanilla · Caramel · Peach · 
Candied Orange · Oak Spice 
 Appearance:  Bright gold with amber hues 
 Best enjoyed:  Neat, on the rocks, or as the 
base of a tequila Old Fashioned; with grilled 
meats, roasted vegetables, or dark 
chocolate 
 ABV: 38% | Sizes: 750ml | 50ml 
 Price (in Maharashtra):  
 750ml – ₹4,500 
 50ml – ₹450 

Bodega Añejo  
Añejo is the most mature expression in the Bodega 
range, where time in barrel becomes the defining 
part of the production process. Built on the same 
100% Blue Weber Agave spirit as the Blanco and 
Reposado, it is matured for a minimum of twelve 
months in ex-Tennessee whiskey American oak 
barrels. Bodega deliberately ages its Añejo at the 
category minimum rather than pushing for longer 
maturation, ensuring the oak complements rather 
than overwhelms the tequila's natural character. The 
result is a richer, fuller-bodied expression, best 
enjoyed neat or over a single large ice cube rather 
than as the base of a cocktail. 
 
 Tasting Notes:  Vanilla · Golden Apple · 
Apricot · Citrus · Oak 
 Appearance:  Bright with golden, silver, and 
amber hues 
 Best enjoyed:  Neat, or with a single large ice 
cube, savoured slowly; after dinner with 
dried fruit, dark chocolate, or a good cigar 
 ABV: 38% | Sizes: 750ml | 50ml 
 Price (in Maharashtra)::

About Tilaknagar Industries Ltd.: Tilaknagar Industries Ltd. (TI) is one of India’s leading IMFL manufacturers, 
with 90 years of excellence in the consumer goods category. Founded in 1933 by Shri Mahadev L. Dahanukar as 
Maharashtra Sugar Mills, the company was originally engaged in the manufacture of sugar and allied products. 
The Dahanukar family continues to be the promoter of TI, guided by the same vision and values as its founders. 
Under the leadership of Shri Amit Dahanukar, TI has grown to become the largest manufacturer of premium 
brandy in India. The company leads the spirits market with its two ‘Millionaire’ brandy brands, Mansion House 
and Courrier Napoleon, and has a strong presence across whisky, rum and gin through Mansion House Whisky, 
Madiraa Rum and Blue Lagoon Gin. TI has further strengthened its whisky portfolio with the acquisition of 
Imperial Blue, a well-established legacy brand in India. Reflecting its focus on premiumisation, TI’s luxury vertical, 
House of TI, is anchored by Monarch Legacy Edition, its first luxury brandy, and Seven Islands Pure Malt Whisky, 
an Indo-Scottish premium whisky. Alongside these, its portfolio also extends to strategic partnerships and 
investments in emerging Indian craft spirits companies, including Bartisans, Spaceman Spirits Lab and Bodega 
Suprema No.5. For more information about Tilaknagar Industries, our people, our brands, and our performance, 
visit us at: https://www.tilind.com/  
About Black Tiger Distilleries: B lack Tiger Distilleries (BTD) is an Indian spirits company focused on building 
authentic agave brands for the country's rapidly evolving premium spirits market. Founded by Mohnish Mehta 
in partnership with Mansha Group of Companies and Productos Finos de Agave (PFA), one of Mexico's most 
respected independent, family-run tequila producers, the company develops spirits that honour Mexico's 
tequila-making heritage while creating products designed specifically for Indian consumers. Its first brand, 
Bodega Suprema No. 5, is crafted in Jesús María, Jalisco, using 100% Blue Weber Agave and bottled at origin in 
Mexico. For more information, please visit: www.bodegasuprema.com  | @drink.bodega
