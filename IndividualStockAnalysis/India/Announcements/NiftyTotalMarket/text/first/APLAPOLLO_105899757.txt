Noida , July 1, 2024 : APL Apollo Tubes Limited (APL Apollo), the World's largest branded structural 
steel tube company, today announced its sales volume performance for Q 1FY25. The Company 
registered the highest  ever sales volume of 7,21,064  Ton in Q 1FY25 compared to 6,61,501  Ton in 
Q1FY24 and 6,78,556  Ton in Q 4FY24.  Volume was strong despite the quarter being  impacted 
due to the general elections in the country.  
A few highlights for Q 1FY25: 
1) The contribution from the value -added segment increased to 60% in Q1FY25, up from 57% in 
Q1FY24 .  APL Apollo’s strategy of de -commoditization is intact . 
2) The ramp -up of the new Raipur plant is on expected  lines, with capacity utilization levels 
reaching 63% in Q1FY25.  
3) The Dubai plant began  partial  production in December 2023 with 2 operational mills which  
achieved capacity utilization of 95% in Q1FY25 . Two new mills will be operational in Q2FY25 
which will further boost sales volume .

About APL Apollo Tubes Limited  
APL Apollo Tubes Limited (APL Apollo) BSE: 533758, NSE: APLAPOLLO is India’s leading structural steel tube manufacturer. 
Headquartered at De lhi NCR, the Company operates 1 1 manufacturing facilities with a total capacity of 3.8 Mn Ton. It has a pan -
India presence with units strategically located in Sikandarabad (UP), Hyderabad, Bangalore, Hosur (Tamil Nadu), Raipur 
(Chhattisgarh), Malur (Karnataka), Murbad (Maharashrtra) and Umm AI Quwain (UAE) . APL Apollo’s multi -product offerings include 
over 2,500 varieties for multiple building material structural steel applications. With state -of-the-art-manufacturing facilities, APL 
Apollo serves as a ‘one -stop shop’ for a wide spectrum of products, catering to an array of industry applications such as urban 
infrastructure and real estate, rural housing, commercial construction, greenhouse structures and engineering applications. Th e 
Company’s vast 3 -tier distribution network of over 800 Distributors is sprea d across India, with presence in over 300 towns and cities.
