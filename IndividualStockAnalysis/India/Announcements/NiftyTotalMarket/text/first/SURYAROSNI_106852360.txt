Surya Roshni Limited announces Q2 & H1 FY27 Results

producer  of ERW  GI pipes  and one of the largest  Lighting  Companies  in India,  has declared  its 
unaudited  financial  results  for the quarter  and half-year  ended  September  30, 2026 .

The Lighting  & Consumer  Durables  segment  delivered  its highest -ever  Q2 revenue  of ₹507 crore,  up

With  a combined  order  book  of over  ₹1,400 crore  across  both  businesses  and the second  half 
traditionally  being  our strongest  period,  we enter  H2FY27 with  strong  visibility  and remain  on track  to 
deliver  our FY27 targets .”Commenting  on the results,  Company’s  Managing  Director,  Mr. Raju  Bista,  said  
3
“In Lighting  and Consumer  Durables,  Q2FY27 was our best -ever  second  quarter . Revenue  grew  17% 
YoY to ₹507 crore  and 11% QoQ,  even  though  Diwali  falls in Q3 this year . H1FY27 revenue  grew  16% 
YoY to ₹964 crore .
Input  costs  rose  across  copper,  aluminium , steel  and petroleum -based  materials,  and staggered  price  
increases  across  the industry  made  it harder  to bring  distributors  and retailers  on board  and raised  our 
working  capital  needs . End-consumer  demand,  however,  has not slowed ; the challenge  has been  
material  availability  and cost.
LED bulb  volumes  grew  11% YoY in Q2 and 25% in H1. Our focus  on higher -wattage  bulbs,  which  grew  
24% and carry  better  margins  for us and our distributors,  also lifted  lower -wattage  sales,  so category  
profitability  grew  faster  than  volumes . Battens  volume  held  steady  in Q2 with  11% growth  in H1, while  
premium  downlighters,  which  add to both  value  and brand  image,  grew  4% in Q2 and 9% in H1. In 
Professional  Lighting,  our credentials  have  strengthened  with  several  prestigious  projects,  and an order  
book  of over  ₹200 crore,  our strongest  in many  quarters,  gives  clear  visibility  for H2.
In consumer  durables,  our wider  focus  on products  that sell year -round  is paying  off. In volume  terms,  
ceiling  fans  and water  heaters  grew  26% each,  mixer  grinders  20% and table,  pedestal  and wall fans  
18%. Fans  also gained  from  new  counters  added  through  our wires  business,  and BLDC  fans  grew  
strongly  from  a small  base . Induction  and infrared  cooktops  grew  at a double -digit  rate despite  Q1's 
LPG-driven  surge,  and we see the shift  to electric  cooking  as structural  as electricity  availability  and 
solar  adoption  expand . PVC pipes  delivered  high  double -digit  growth .
EBITDA  was ₹40 crore  at a 7.8% margin,  in line with  Q1FY27. Margins  reflected  higher  input  costs  and 
working  capital,  EPR payments  made  in H1 and a planned  step -up in brand  investment . In-house  
manufacturing  absorbed  much  of the cost increase,  we are passing  on price  increases  quickly,  and 
higher  H2 volumes  will spread  these  costs  over  a larger  base .
Q3 and Q4 are traditionally  the industry's  strongest  quarters . With  Diwali  in Q3, factories  running  at full 
capacity,  encouraging  pre-festive  demand,  a strong  order  book  and expanding  distribution,  we expect  a 
better  second  half.”
“In the Steel  Pipes  and Strips , Q2FY27 was a defining  quarter . Volumes  reached  a record  2.67 lakh  
tonnes , up 23% YoY and 17% QoQ  and ahead  of our 2.60 lakh  tonne  guidance,  making  a seasonally  
weak  quarter  our strongest  ever  and reflecting  the structural  strength  we have  built . Revenue  grew  
38% YoY to ₹1,941 crore  and EBITDA  was ₹103 crore . EBITDA  per tonne  improved  to ₹4,105 from  
₹4,006 in Q1; the YoY comparison  is against  an exceptional  ₹5,013 in Q2FY26, which  was lifted  by one-
time  European  and Canadian  pre-buying  ahead  of CBAM  and quotas . In H1FY27, volumes  grew  22% to 
4.96 lakh  tonnes  and revenue  35% to ₹3,531 crore,  with  EBITDA  per tonne  of ₹4,059. Capacity  
utilisation  rose  to about  84% from  82% in Q1.
Value -added  products  were  about  48% of volumes,  against  about  47% in Q1, as domestic  API and 
spiral  volumes  were  subdued ; we expect  the share  to rise in H2 as API orders  are to be executed . 
Hollow  sections  grew  35% YoY as rising  steel  prices  from  August  led the trade  to restock . API export  
volumes  to the US more  than  doubled  QoQ,  at above -average  EBITDA  per tonne , while  domestic  API 
remained  modest . GI pipes  picked  up from  September,  led by our premium  2-inch range  in southern  
markets  such  as Bengaluru,  unusually  for the monsoon . Spiral  volumes  were  26,004 tonnes  as we 
prioritised  scarce  HR coil for higher -margin  products  over  fixed -price  spiral  orders,  and cold rolled  pipes  
were  constrained  by a CR coil shortage,  which  we are now  addressing  by converting  HR coil at our new  
Bahadurgarh  cold rolling  mill.
Exports  rose  about  52% QoQ  and 64% YoY to about  64,408 tonnes , or about  24% of volumes  against  
about  18% in Q1. This included  about  17,000 tonnes  deferred  from  Q1 owing  to vessel  availability  and 
the Middle  East  situation,  and a weaker  rupee  aided  realisations . Domestic  volumes,  about  76% of the 
total,  gathered  pace  from  mid-August  as rising  prices  encouraged  channel  restocking .

We enter  H2 with  our strongest  visibility  in years . The order  book  is about  ₹1,200 crore,  including  about  
1 lakh  tonnes  of export  orders  worth  about  ₹850 crore,  of which  about  80,000 tonnes  (about  ₹700 
crore)  are API pipes,  all for execution  in Q3 and Q4. Oil & gas tendering  has revived  decisively  after  a 
prolonged  lull. Our domestic  API order  book,  built  on city gas distribution  and oil company  tenders,  has 
grown  to about  20,000 tonnes ; we expect  about  15,000 tonnes  including  the ONGC  pipes  over  the next  
three  months . Supplying  over  1 lakh tonnes  of API pipes  in H2 will lift utilisation  of our ERW  API, spiral  
and coating  capacities,  which  were  underutilised  in H1, adding  operating  leverage .
Margins  should  also improve . All legacy  export  orders  are executed  and every  order  in hand  reflects  
current  freight  rates,  so H2 export  profitability  will be better  than  in Q2, while  about  70,000 tonnes  of 
imported  raw material,  100% hedged,  will benefit  Q3 and Q4. Inventory  impact  was broadly  neutral,  
with  August  losses  offset  by September  gains . With  about  half of our business  on fixed -rate contracts  
and HR coil expected  at ₹60,000–65,000 per tonne  through  March,  inventory  risk is limited . Having  
delivered  about  5 lakh  tonnes  in H1, we are confident  of about  6 lakh tonnes  in H2 and our FY27 target  
of 11 lakh  tonnes .”
“In Lighting  and Consumer  Durables,  manufacturing  depth  and backward  integration,  including  in-
house  PCBs  and diffusers,  give us control  over  cost,  quality  and speed  to market . Our LED batten  range  
is India's  widest : around  30 SKUs  from  10W to 60W in 1-ft to 4-ft lengths,  plus festive  colour  variants .

machinery  due within  six weeks ; a decision  on another  20,000 sq m is due by December  2026 , on the 
path  to 1 lakh sq m, and we are evaluating  backward  integration  into PCB laminates . We aim to make  
80% of appliances  in-house  by FY30 (about  10% today),  starting  with  about  20% in fans . At Kashipur,  
we now  make  ventilation  fans  and induction  and infrared  cooktops,  our air-delivery  testing  lab is being  
NABL -accredited,  and new  appliances  will launch  by January  2027 .

in Q1. Our electrician  loyalty  programme  has about  40,000 members  (36,000 in August),  exclusive  wire  
distributors  are joining  us, new  SKUs  have  been  launched  across  thicknesses,  and our Malanpur  plant  is 
certified  for green  production,  a credential  few in the industry  hold . We are confident  of ₹250 crore  in

Exports,  at 3–4% of segment  revenue  in Q2, will rise in H2 with  export  and contract -manufacturing

sourcing  and India's  freight  advantage  are bringing  enquiries  for fans  and mixer  grinders,  and our 
ability  to ship lighting,  appliances,  wires  and steel  pipes  in one container  makes  Surya  a natural  
partner .
Advertising  and publicity  spend  rose  to about  ₹10 crore  in H1FY27 from  ₹6.5 crore,  behind  wires,  
gifting  and appliances . Our ‘INDIA  BOLE  SURYA  KO YA’ campaign  has lifted  our share  of voice,  and we 
showcased  our full range  at the Gift Expo  in Bengaluru  and Delhi  and the Fan Expo . Our 3 MW  of

more  is due by December  2026 .
We are on track  for our FY27 guidance  of 22–23% value  and 25% volume  growth,  or about  ₹2,200

CAGR  through  FY30, with  EBITDA  margins  of about  9%. With  our broad  portfolio,  manufacturing  depth  
and reach,  we are confident  of consistent,  profitable  growth .”Adding  further,  Mr. Vinay  Surya  – Managing  Director  said,

“In the Steel  Pipes  and Strips , our record  Q2 shows  that the investments  of the last two years  are 
delivering,  and the bigger  step -up lies ahead . Of our three  new  DFT mills,  Anjar  (Gujarat)  was 
commissioned  in October  2026  and has already  shipped  about  1,500 tonnes  to export  customers ; 
Malanpur  (Gwalior)  follows  in December  2026  and Bahadurgarh  in January  2027 , adding  volumes  from  
Q4. Our DFT range  now  spans  80x80 mm (from  150x150 mm earlier)  to 200x200 mm,  opening  up 
much  more  of the hollow  section  market,  which  we see as the fastest -growing  pipe  category  over  the 
next  two to three  years  on infrastructure  and construction  demand .
At Hindupur,  the first 60,000 tonnes -per-annum  mill will be operational  by March  2027  and two more  
during  FY28, adding  about  1.2 lakh  tonnes  and strengthening  our presence  in the south . Installed  
capacity  will reach  about  16.5 lakh  tonnes  by March  2027 . Capex  of about  ₹150 crore  in FY27 and 
₹150 crore  in FY28 will be funded  through  internal  accruals . We target  about  14 lakh  tonnes  in FY28 at 
EBITDA  per tonne  of about  ₹4,500–4,600, and about  20 lakh  tonnes  by FY30 on capacity  of about  22 
lakh  tonnes .
We aim to take  value -added  products  above  50% of volumes  within  two years . This quarter  we began  
producing  13⅜-inch API casing  pipes  and launched  0.6 mm and 0.8 mm CRFH  pipes  from  our new  
Bahadurgarh  cold rolling  mill. We have  also secured  raw material  supply  by adding  new  HR coil 
suppliers  and raising  volumes  with  existing  ones .
Our manpower,  fuel and energy  initiatives  have  saved  about  ₹500 per tonne  in absolute  terms ; with  
operating  leverage,  we are on track  for our targeted  ₹1,100 per tonne  reduction  on a full-year  basis . 
Another  1 MW  of rooftop  solar  at Bahadurgarh  by mid-January  2027  will take  steel  solar  capacity  to 
about  4 MW,  saving  about  ₹2.5 crore  a year,  with  1 MW  more  planned  at Hindupur .
Exports  remain  a structural  growth  lever . We target  22–25% of volumes,  or about  2.5 lakh  tonnes  in 
FY27, with  the US alone  at about  1.2 lakh  tonnes  and Australia  improving . Quotas  in Europe  and 
Canada  will persist,  but CBAM  applies  equally  to all exporters,  and our wide  product  range  lets us 
direct  volumes  where  we are most  competitive . With  new  capacity,  a richer  mix,  a leaner  cost base,  a 
stronger  brand  and a wider  export  footprint,  Steel  Pipes  & Strips  is entering  its strongest  phase  of 
profitable  growth .”
