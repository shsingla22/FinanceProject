Jamnagar , 21st October  2026  
 
 
MEDIA STATEMENT  
 
 
Reliance Industries Limited Chairman and Managing Director Shri Mukesh Ambani and 
Executive Director Mr Anant Ambani welcomed US Secretary of State Mr Marco Rubio and US 
Ambassador to India Mr Sergio Gor to Jamnagar, home to the world’s largest refinery. The 
Honorable visitors were given a tour of the world -class refinery complex, offering a first -hand 
view of its scale, advanced technology and integrated operations.   
 
Secretary Rubio and Ambassador Gor were also explained Reliance’s New Energy giga -
factories, which are currently being commissioned in phases, and the data centres under 
construction. The tour provided an overview of the company’s investments across energy and 
digital infrastructure, bringing together its well -established advanced manufacturing and deep -
tech capabilities. Shri Mukesh Ambani and Shri Anant Ambani outlined Reliance’s long -term 
vision for the Jamnagar complex and its development as a centre for deep -tech, innovation and 
advanced manufacturing.  
 
The visit included wide -ranging discussions on the global energy landscape and opportunities 
for commercial partnerships across conventional and new energy. The exchange covered 
reliable and affordable energy supplies, technological advances, resilient supply chains and the 
investment required to meet growing demand.   
 
Discussions also explored the increasing intersection of energy and digital infrastructure, 
including the role of dependable power in supporting data centres and the expansion of the 
digital economy. Shri Mukesh Ambani and Shri Anant Ambani shared their perspectives on 
opportunities for collaboration with US companies, drawing on complementary strengths in 
technology, engineering and advanced manufacturing. The interaction provided a constructive 
opportunity to exchange industry perspectives and explore areas of mutual commercial interest.  
 
We are extremely thankful to Secretary  of State and the Ambassador for their esteemed visit .
