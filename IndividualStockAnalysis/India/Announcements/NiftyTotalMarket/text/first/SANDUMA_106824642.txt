PRESS &  MEDIA R EL EASE
Bengaluru, 22 October 2026
Royal  Sandur  forays  into Medical  Devices  and Consumables ; Royal  Sandur  
MedTech  leases  land at Andhra  Pradesh  MedTech  Zone,  Visakhapatnam
The Sandur Manganese & Iron Ores Limited (‘SMIORE’ or ‘the Company’), the flagship company of 
the Royal Sandur Group, has taken a decisive step towards its foray into the Medical Devices and 
Consumables business. Royal Sandur MedTech Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of the 
Company, has taken land on a long -term lease at the Andhra Pradesh MedTech Zone (‘AMTZ’), 
Visakhapatnam, India’s premier medical technology park with common manufacturing facilities and 
scientific facilities.
The lease deed was executed on 19 October 2026 between Royal Sandur MedTech Private Limited 
and Andhra Pradesh MedTech Zone Limited, the Government of Andhra Pradesh undertaking that 
owns and operates AMTZ, for setting up a manufacturing unit at AMTZ. Royal Sandur MedTech 
Private Limited has also taken office space at World Trade Center AMTZ to support project 
development. Key terms of the land lease are as follows:
•Developed land measuring 6.2 acres (about 25,090 square metres ), with road, power, water and 
effluent infrastructure provided by AMTZ
•Lease tenure of 33 years from the date of execution, with an exclusive right to convert the 
leasehold rights into freehold ownership after 10 years from the date of commissioning of 
business
This marks a concrete move by SMIORE towards new frontiers, as part of its comprehensive 
diversification strategy. Further details on the capital expenditure plans and project timelines will be 
announced in due course.
Commenting  on the development,  Bahirji  A. Ghorpade,  Managing  Director,  SMIORE,  said:
“Our diversification  strategy  is about  building  new engines  of growth  alongside  our core mining  and 
metals  businesses . Medical  Devices  and Consumables  sector  offers  considerable  opportunities  for 
domestic  manufacturing  to grow,  and the Andhra  Pradesh  MedTech  Zone,  with its common  
manufacturing  and scientific  facilities,  gives  a new entrant  the right platform  to build  capabilities  in a 
focused  and capital -efficient  manner . Securing  this land is the first concrete  step in this journey,  and 
we intend  to pursue  it with the same  discipline  that has defined  our enterprise  over seven  decades .”
Welcoming  Royal  Sandur  MedTech  to the ecosystem,  Dr. Jitendra  Sharma,  Managing  Director  
& Founder  CEO,  Andhra  Pradesh  MedTech  Zone  (AMTZ),  said:
“We were pleased  to welcome  Royal  Sandur  MedTech  to the AMTZ  ecosystem . Their  investment  
reflected  the growing  confidence  of Indian  industry  in the nation’s  medical  technology  manufacturing  
capabilities  and reinforced  AMTZ’s  role as a catalyst  for innovation,  indigenous  manufacturing,  and 
global  competitiveness . Such collaborations  continue  to accelerate  India’s  journey  towards  becoming  a 
global  hub for medical  technology  manufacturing  and healthcare  innovation .”

About  The Sandur  Manganese  & Iron Ores  Limited
The Sandur  Manganese  & Iron Ores Limited  (“SMIORE”)  is one of India’s  most  respected  private  sector  merchant  miners  and commodity  producers  with 
an operational  track  record  spanning  over seven  decades . At present,  the Company’s  operations  span  over three  business  segments  i.e., Mining  
(Manganese  & Iron Ores),  Ferroalloys,  and Coke  and Energy . Further,  the Company  has presence  in specialty  steel  sector  through  its material  subsidiary  
Royal  Sandur  Metals  Private  Limited,  which  is the leading  independent  SBQ players  in the country . Together,  these  assets  work  in conjunction  with each 
other  to leverage  the benefits  of being  an integrated  Company . 
About  Royal  Sandur  Group
As SMIORE’s  operations  have  grown  across  mining,  ferroalloys,  coke  and energy,  and steel,  the Company  has adopted  a unified  Group  brand  name,  ‘Royal  
Sandur  Group’,  and its logo,  to support  its long-term  strategic  vision  and enable  it to explore  diverse  business  opportunities . The name  carries  forward  the 
Company’s  royal  heritage  and its enduring  bond  with Sandur,  built over seven  decades  of enterprise . Under  this unified  banner,  each business  entity  
retains  its own focus  while  contributing  to one corporate  identity,  presenting  the organisation  to customers,  investors  and communities  as a single,  
integrated  group .
