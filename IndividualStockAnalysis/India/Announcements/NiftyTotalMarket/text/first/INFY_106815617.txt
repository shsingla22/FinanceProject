      PRESS RELEASE 
Infosys Foundation Enables Over 2.5 Lakh Job Offers Across India Through 
Infosys Springboard Livelihood Program  
Milestone marks the halfway point towards Infosys Foundation's aspiration of creating half a 
million job opportunities in India by 2030. 
Bengaluru, India –  October  13, 2026:  Infosys Foundation, the philanthropic and CSR arm of Infosys , 
today announced that the Infosys Springboard Livelihood Program has enabled more than 2.5 lakh job 
offers across India, marking the halfway milestone towards its aspiration of creating half a million job 
opportunities in India by 2030. This reflects the Foundation's commitment to creating sustainable livelihoods 
through industry -relevant skilling, employment pathways, and strong ecosystem partnerships.  
  
Since its launch last year, the Infosys Springboard Livelihood Program has enabled employment opportunities for youngsters through a network of 20 implementation partners, including Nirmaan 
Organization, ICT Academy, Magic Bus, Centum Foundation and Aga Khan Rural Support Programme  
(India).  The program has enabled more than 1.17 lakh job offers for graduates and over 96,000 job offers 
for undergraduates,  helping bridge the gap between education and employment. The program has also 
enabled more than 1.25 lakh job offers for women and for over 1000 Persons with Disabilities (PwD), 
reinforcing Infosys Foundation's commitment to creating inclusive and equitable livelihood opportunities. 
Infosys Foundation collaborated with  AssisTech  Foundation (ATF)  for the employment platform and will 
work with  The Association of People with Disabilit y (APD)  to expand access to career opportunities for 
people with disabilities.  
  
Sumit Virmani, Trustee, Infosys Foundation, said, “At Infosys Foundation, we believe the true success 
of education is not the number of people skilled, but the number of lives meaningfully transformed through 
employment. Reaching the halfway milestone of our 2030 goal with 2.5 lakh job offers demo nstrates what 
is possible when learning is closely aligned to industry demand. Beyond livelihoods, this milestone 
represents greater economic independence, social mobility, and opportunities for individuals and families 
to build a more secure future. We remain committed to enabling access to future- ready skills, inclusive 
employment opportunities, and pathways for more young people to participate meaningfully in India's 
growth story.”  
  
V. Srikanth, CEO, ICT Academy , said, "The Infosys Springboard Livelihood Program demonstrates how 
strategic partnerships can create meaningful and lasting impact for India's youth. At ICT Academy, we 
believe that skills become truly transformative when they lead to sustainable livelih oods and economic 
empowerment. Through this initiative, we are seeing young people, many from underserved and first -
generation learner backgrounds, gain industry -relevant skills, digital competencies, and the confidence to 
  
pursue meaningful careers. What makes this program particularly impactful is its focus on connecting 
learning with real -world employment opportunities, helping bridge the gap between potential and 
opportunity. Beyond enhancing employability, it is enabling youth to become financially independent, 
support their families, and contribute to their communities. We appreciate Infosys' commitment to building 
an inclusive, future -ready workforce and are proud to partner in empowering young people to realize their 
aspirations and drive India's growth story."  
  
Mayur Patnala, Founder, Chairperson & Global CEO, Nirmaan Organization,  said, "India's 
demographic dividend can only be realized when every young person has access to the skills, opportunities, 
and support needed to succeed in the workforce. The Infosys Springboard Livelihood Program is helping 
bridge that gap by focusing not  just on employability, but on sustainable livelihoods and long -term career 
growth. We are proud to partner with Infosys Foundation in empowering youth from underserved communities with industry -relevant skills, workplace readiness, and the confidence to pursue their 
aspirations. Together, we are creating opportunities that can transform lives, strengthen families, and build a more inclusive and skilled nation.”  
 
About Infosys Foundation 
Established in 1996, Infosys Foundation supports programs in the areas of education, healthcare, women 
empowerment, and environmental sustainability, amongst others. Its mission is to work with the 
underprivileged across the country and strive towards a more equitable society. Infosys Foundation takes 
pride in working with all sections of society, selecting projects with infinite care, and working in areas that are traditionally overlooked by society at large.
