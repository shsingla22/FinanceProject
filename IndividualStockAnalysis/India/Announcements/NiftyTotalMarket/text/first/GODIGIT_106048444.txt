Digit Insurance wins big, bags General Insurance Company 
of the Year Award at Asia Insurance Industry Awards 2024  
• Digit adjudged Asia’s best General Insurance Company of the Year for third time in 6 years .  
• Digit Insurance the only Indian insurance company to bag an award this year . 
• Winners picked from a nomination of 200 entries in 17 categories from insurers in Asia.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
7 November 202 4, Bengaluru:  Go Digit 
General Insurance Limited  (“Digit 
Insurance”) , one of India’s leading new -age 
insurance company , announced it has won 
the “General Insurance Company of the 
Year”  Award at the prestigious 28th Asia 
Insurance Industry Awards (AIIA)  2024  held in 
Singapore  on 4 November. It is also the sole 
Indian company to win  the prestigious award 
this year.   
This is Digit  Insurance ’s fifth AIIA award  in 
last six years. The award was received by 
Jasleen Kohli (MD & CEO) and Amrit Arora 
(CHRO) at the ceremony held at Marina Bay 
Sands Expo & Convention Centre in 
Singapore . The company last won the AIIA 
General Insurance Company of the Year 
Award in 2019 and 2020. It bagged the Digital 
Insurer of the Year Award in 2021 and 2023. It 
is also the only Asian insurance company  to 
have bagged the General Insurance Company 
of the Year award thrice in the last six years .

Commenting on the win, Jasleen Kohli, MD & CEO, Go Digit General Insurance , said, “We are 
thrilled and honoured to win the prestigious General Insurance Company of the Year Award at 28th 
Asia Insurance Industry Award s. Being obsessed with the idea of simplifying insurance and using 
technology to bring in transparency and trust is what has helped us create a niche for ourselves and 
reach 60 million customers in a short span of 7 years. The journey ahead is a long one fo r us as we 
continue to chase our mission of making simplified insurance solutions accessible to every Indian.”  
The Asia Insurance Industry Awards, 2024 stated  that "Since its inception in 2017, Digit General 
Insurance has seen growth that has impressed many. A core business idea of bringing simplified 
insurance solutions to the tier -2 and tier -3 cities and villages of India has allowed it to become one 
of the fastest  growing general insurers in the nation."   
Digit Insurance was also among the top three finalists in three other categories  namely “Health 
Insurance Company of the Year”, “AI Initiative of the Year” and “Women Leader of the Year (Jasleen 
Kohli)”. It was also among the only two companies nominated in as many as four categories  this 
year.  
The winners of AIIA go through very tough competition  each year from top insurance companies in 
Asia. The judging process has a clearly defined criteria, transparent process, and is independen tly 
scrutinized by KPMG . The finalists this time were selected from over 200 nominations  received 
across 17 categories. The process is rigorous with two rounds of judging done by 20 prominent 
judges  chosen from the Asian insurance industry. This is followed by interviewing the shortlisted 
candidates . 
 
 
About Digit Insurance   
Founded by Kamesh Goyal in 2016, Go Digit General Insurance Limited is a publicly listed general 
insurance company and is one of the leading new -age insurance companies in India. It leverages its 
technology to power what it believes to be an innovative approach to product design, distribution 
and customer experience for no n-life insurance products. Some of the promoters of Go Digit General 
Insurance Ltd are Go Digit Infoworks Services Pvt. Ltd and FAL Corporation. With its registered office 
in Pune and corp orate office in Bengaluru, Digit is one of the first non -life insurers in India to be fully 
operating on cloud . 
Digit won the Digital Insurer of the Year 2023 Award at the prestigious Asia Insurance Industry Awards 
2023 , Singapore . The Company is also part of the Fortune India 500 List (Ranked 349) and Hurun 
India 500 List 2023 (ranked 146th out of 500 most valuable companies). It was ranked 5th at LinkedIn 
2024 Top Companies to Work for and is also a certified Great Place to Work for four year s in a row.  
The Company offers motor insurance, health insurance, travel insurance, property insurance, 
marine insurance, liability insurance and other insurance products, which the customer can 
customize to meet his or her needs. Digit, through its tech -enabled proce ss, focuses on product 
innovations to help satisfy real unmet insurance needs.
