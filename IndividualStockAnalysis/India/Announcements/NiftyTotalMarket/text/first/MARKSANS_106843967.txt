Q2 & H1 FY27  Financial Results  
Marksans Pharma crosses Rs. 900 crore quarterly revenue for the first time; Q2FY27 
EBITDA up 48% to a record Rs. 214 crore, H1FY27 PAT nearly doubles to Rs. 303 crore  
 
Mumbai , November 4th , 2026 – Marksans Pharma  Ltd. (NSE:  MARKSANS ; BSE Code:  524404 ) reported the financial 
results for the quarter and half year ended on September 30, 2026.  
Mark Saldanha, Founder, Chairman & Managing Director , said, “Q2FY27 marks a landmark quarter for Marksans 
Pharma, with the Company delivering its strongest -ever performance. Revenue reached ₹940 crore, up 30.5% YoY. 
Adjusted for acquisition (QliniQ) impact, revenue growth remained healthy at 25%.  
EBITDA grew 48.3% to a record ₹214 crore, with margins expanding 274 bps to 22.8%. For H1FY27, revenue grew 32.9% 
to ₹1,781 crore, EBITDA rose 74.7% with margins up 574 bps to 24.0%, and PAT nearly doubled to ₹303 crore, already 
over 70% of our full -year F Y26 profit.  
Growth was broad -based. UK & Europe grew 64.9% to a record ₹405 crore in Q2, with the base business growing 48.6% 
organically and QliniQ, our new European front -end, contributing around ₹8 4 crore in H1. Australia & New Zealand 
grew 64.0%, and US & North America delivered its highest -ever quarter at ₹421 crore. Equally encouraging was the 
strength of cash generation, with H1 operating cash flow reaching ₹322 crore, 4.3 times the previous year,  and cash 
balances closing at ₹1,124 crore. Our trailing twelve -month ROCE also improved to 24.7%.  
We enter the next phase of our growth journey from a position of considerable strength, supported by an expanding 
global footprint, a robust product pipeline and a strong net -cash position. Our focus remains on sustaining profitable 
organic growth while se lectively pursuing strategic acquisitions that strengthen our capabilities and market presence. 
We remain confident in our growth trajectory and committed to creating sustainable long -term shareholder value.  
Q2FY27  Financial Highlights  
▪ Operating Revenue  stood at Rs. 940 cr, up by 30.5% YoY  
▪ Gross profit came in at Rs. 550 cr, up by 33.6% YoY, with a gross margin of 58.5%  
▪ EBITDA stood at Rs. 214 cr, up by 48.3% YoY, with an EBITDA margin of 22.8%  
▪ PAT stood at Rs. 144 cr, up by 44.8% YoY, with a net margin of 15.0%  
▪ Diluted EPS was at Rs. 3.1 1 
H1FY27  Financial Highlights  
▪ Operating Revenue  stood at Rs. 1,781 cr, up by 32.9% YoY  
▪ Gross profit came in at Rs. 1,047 cr, up by 36.0% YoY, with a gross margin of 58.8%  
▪ EBITDA stood at Rs. 427 cr, up by 74.7% YoY, with an EBITDA margin of 24.0%  
▪ PAT stood at Rs. 303 cr, up by 92.5% YoY, with a net margin of 16.6%  
▪ Diluted EPS was at Rs. 6.58  
Business Highlights  
US Market  
▪ US & North America Formulation business reported revenues of Rs. 421 cr in Q2FY27, up 8.7% YoY and 11.6% QoQ 
— the highest -ever quarterly revenue for the region  

 
 
 
 ▪ Largest market, contributing ~45% of consolidated revenue. H1FY27 revenue of Rs. 798 cr, up 11.7% YoY  
▪ Order book remains  strong despite a softer US consumer environment, supporting the outlook for H2FY27; price 
erosion in the Rx portfolio remained in single digits  
UK and Europe Market  
▪ Revenues from the UK & Europe Formulation business stood at Rs. 405 cr in Q2FY27, up 64.9% YoY and 13.7% 
QoQ — the highest -ever quarterly revenue for the region  
▪ H1FY27 revenue of Rs. 761 cr, up 69.4% YoY  
▪ Excluding QliniQ, UK & Europe revenue grew 48.6% YoY to Rs. 365 cr in Q2FY27; QliniQ B.V. (Netherlands) 
contributed ~Rs. 40 cr in the quarter  
▪ After a flat FY26, the UK market has stabilised; new launches are driving both topline growth and margin 
improvement  
Australia and New Zealand  Market  
▪ Australia and New Zealand businesses reported revenues of Rs. 101 cr in Q2FY27, up 64.0% YoY and 14.8% QoQ, 
driven by strong traction in the branded prescription (Rx) portfolio under Nova Pharmaceuticals  
▪ H1FY27 revenue of Rs. 188 cr, up 59.0% YoY; ANZ now contributes 10.7% of consolidated revenue  
RoW Market  
▪ RoW business reported revenues of Rs. 14 cr in Q2FY27, down 47.0% YoY and 29.6% QoQ, amidst the West Asia 
crisis  
▪ Middle East shipments impacted; stabilization in geopolitical conditions could support recovery in the quarters to 
come.  
Other Highlights  
▪ Net cash from operating activities of Rs. 322 cr in H1FY27, 4.3x H1FY26; EBITDA -to-cash conversion of 75% 
(H1FY26: 31%)  
▪ Capex of Rs. 46 cr incurred during H1FY27; free cash flow of Rs. 275 cr  
▪ Working capital cycle of ~122 days in Q2FY27, improved from ~132 days in Q1FY27 (H1FY27: ~128 days vs ~138 
days in FY26)  
▪ Cash balance of Rs. 1,124 cr as on 30th September 2026; net cash of Rs. 1,095 cr, up Rs. 135 cr in H1FY27 after Rs. 
95 cr of acquisitions and Rs. 41 cr of dividend  
▪ Trailing twelve -month ROCE of 24.7% (Sep -25: 18.7%) and ROE of 18.6% (Sep -25: 13.6%)  
▪ R&D spends at Rs. 23.9 cr in Q2FY27, 2.5% of consolidated revenue

 About Marksans Pharma  Ltd 
Marksans Pharma Limited (www.marksanspharma.com) headquartered at Mumbai, India is engaged in Research, 
Manufacturing & Marketing of generic pharmaceutical formulation in the global markets. The company's 
manufacturing facilities are approved by several le ading regulatory agencies including USFDA, UKMHRA and Australian 
TGA. The company's robust product portfolio spreads over major therapeutic segments of CVS, CNS, Antidiabetic, Pain 
Management, Gastroenterological  and Anti -allergies. The company is marketing these products globally.
