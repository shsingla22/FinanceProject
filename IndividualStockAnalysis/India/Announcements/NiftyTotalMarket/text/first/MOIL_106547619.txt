Sub: - Information relating to fixation of price of different grades of 
Manganese Ore for the month of February 2026 in Jan -Mar’2026 quarter  
w.e.f. 01.02.2026.  
 
Stock Code: NSE – MOIL

This is to inform you that the details of revision in prices of different grades of 
Manganese Ore for the month of February 2026 for the Quarter Jan -Mar’2026 
effective from 01.02.2026 are as under:  
 
1. The prices of all Ferro grades of manganese ore with Manganese content 
of Mn - 44% and above have been increased by 5% for the month of 
February 2026 on the prices prevailing since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight 
of 31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  
 
2. The prices of all other Ferro grades of manganese ore with Manganese 
content below Mn -44% have been increased by 5% for the month of 
February, 2026 on the prices prevailing since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight 
of 31.01.2026/01.02.2026 except BG4584. In case of  BG4584, continued 
the January 2026 price for month of February, 2026 w.e.f. midnight of 
31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  
 
3. The prices of SMGR (Mn -30%) and Fines grades have been increased by 
5% for the month of February, 2026 on the prices prevailing since 
01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  
 
4. The prices of SMGR (Mn 25%) grades have been continued for the month 
of February 2026 prevailing since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 
31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  
 
5. The prices of all Chemical grades have been increased by 5% for the month 
of February 2026 on the prices prevailing since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight 
of 31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  

 
 
 
 
 
6. The prices of SMGR (Mn 20%) grades have been continued for the month 
of February 2026 prevailing since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 
31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  
 
7. The prices of Metal Mandi Fines namely (DBF575 and MSF592 except 
UKF532) have been continued for the month of February 2026 prevailing 
since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 31.01.2026/01.02.2026. In case of 
UKF532, price have been increased by 10% for the mo nth of February 
2026 on the prices prevailing since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 
31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  
 
8. The basic price Rs.1,90,000/ - PMT of EMD have been continued for the 
month of February 2026 prevailing since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 
31.01.2026/01.02.2026.
