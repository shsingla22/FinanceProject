PRESS RELEASE  
Solar Industries Reports Strong Broad-Based 
Performance in Q2 and H1 FY2026-27, 
Confident of Surpassing Full-Year Guidance  
 
Solar Industries India Limited (SIIL) today announced its unaudited financial results 
for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026. 
 
Key Highlights 
 Consolidated revenue of ₹3519 crore in Q2 FY27, representing growth of 69% year-on-
year, and ₹7187 crore in H1 FY27, representing growth of 70% year-on-year. 
 EBITDA of ₹1021 crore in Q2 FY27, with an EBITDA margin of 29.02%, and ₹2045 crore 
in H1 FY27, with a margin of 28.45%. 
 Profit after tax of ₹650 crore in Q2 FY27 and ₹1316 crore in H1 FY27. 
 Order book of ₹20178 crore, providing visibility for future execution.

Management Commentary 
 
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Manish Nuwal, Managing Director and CEO, said : 
 
“We are pleased to report another quarter of strong and broad-based performance. 
Consolidated revenue was ₹3519 crore for the quarter and ₹7187crore for the first half, 
representing year-on-year growth of 69% and 70%, respectively. Quarterly EBITDA 
was ₹1021crore, with a margin of 29.02%, and profit after tax was ₹650 crore. For the 
first half, EBITDA and profit after tax were ₹2045 crore and ₹1316 crore,respectively.  
 
Performance was supported by contributions from Domestic Explosives, International 
Explosives and Defence. The results reflect the resilience of our business model, 
disciplined execution and the benefits of our strategic investments.” 
 
 
 
                         
 Domestic Explosives 
 
Commenting on the Domestic Explosives business, Mr. Nuwal said: 
 
“Our Domestic Explosives business delivered a healthy performance during the 
quarter, supported by demand from the mining and infrastructure sectors, stronger 
customer engagement ,an expanded manufacturing footprint and technical-service 
capabilities enabling us to serve customers efficiently and participate in India’s long-
term mining and infrastructure growth .” 
International Explosives 
 
Commenting on the International Explosives business, he added: 
 
“Our International Explosives business maintained its momentum, supported by 
deeper customer relationships, improved market penetration and sustained demand 
across key operating geographies. Our integrated portfolio of explosives, initiating 
systems and technical mining solutions supports our position as a long-term partner to 
global mining customers. We remain focused on scaling our international operations 
by entering into growing markets through acquisitions and greenfield expansions.” 
Defence 
 
Highlighting the performance of the Defence business, Mr. Nuwal said:  
 
“The Defence business continued to grow, supported by execution of the existing order 
book, an expanding portfolio of indigenously developed products and opportunities in 
domestic and international markets. Our capabilities across the ammunition value 
chain, the order pipeline, execution of key programmes and ongoing customer 
engagement support our confidence in our long-term prospects. We are likely to get 
large orders from international and Indian defence sector, which include guided pinaka 
rockets as well in near future. 
 
Outlook 
 
Concluding the performance update, Mr. Nuwal said: 
 
“The Company’s first-half performance, together with momentum across Domestic 
Explosives, International Explosives and Defence, has reinforced our confidence in 
its growth trajectory. Based on the results achieved, the strength of the order 
book and the opportunities across our businesses, we expect to surpass our 
stated annual guidance for FY2026-27.  
 
Our expanding manufacturing footprint, disciplined capital allocation, technology-led 
product development and broader presence in domestic and international markets 
provide a foundation for sustainable growth. We will remain focused on operational 
excellence, timely execution, prudent growth and the creation of long-term 
stakeholder value.”  
 
 
                         
  
About Solar Industries India Ltd  
 One of the world’s leading manufacturers of explosives and initiating systems. 
 One of the most valued explosives companies in the world. 
 Worldwide presence with 40 manufacturing facilities. 
 Global footprint in 90+ countries with manufacturing facilities in 11 countries. 
 First private sector company in India to set up an integrated facility for defence 
products such as high-energy materials, rocket propellants, warheads and rockets. 
 First private sector company to receive ready-to-use ammunition orders from the

 First private sector company to receive defence export orders for ready-to-use 
ammunition. 
 First private sector company to indigenously develop, receive and supply orders for 
drone-based loitering munitions.

SITBEX-1 and SIMEX-4.
