TVS MOTOR COMPANY ACCELERATES EV 
TRANSITION IN SRI LANKA WITH THE LAUNCH OF 
TVS KING EV MAX 
• Offers 179 kms range, fast charging, battery capacity 9.2 kWh  
• Combines long range, smart connected features and superior 
comfort for modern EV urban transport  
• 6-year warranty to give complete peace of mind  
 
Colombo , August 13, 2026: TVS Motor Company  (TVS M), part of TVS VENU, and a reputed global 
manufacturer of two and three- wheelers,  today  launched its connected passenger electric three-
wheeler, TVS King  EV MAX  in Sri Lanka.  The TVS King EV MAX is a powerful blend of technology, 
sustainability, and performance, offering a smart and future- ready solution for urban commuting. 
Designed to dominate the roads with its robust build, superior durability, and unmatched performance, 
it reinforces TVS Motor Company's commitment to innovation and sustainability.   
 
With an impressive range of 179 km on a single charge, the TVS King EV MAX is designed to maximize 
productivity while minimizing running costs. Its fast -charging capability enables the battery to charge 
from 0 to 80% in just 2 hours and 30 minutes, reducing downtime and keeping users on the move. 
Delivering best -in-class acceleration of 0 to 30 km/h in just 3.7 seconds, the TVS King EV MAX offers 
responsive performance ideal for city operations. Equipped with TVS SmartXonnect ™, it provides 
advanced connected features such as real -time navigation, vehicle alerts, and diagnostics directly 
through a smartphone, ensuring greater convenience and operational efficiency.  
 
The TVS King EV MAX is powered by 
a high- performance 11 kW motor and 
a reliable 51.2V lithium -ion LFP 
battery, making it ideal for urban 
mobility. Offering a top speed of 60 
km/h across ECO (40 km/h), City (50 
km/h), and Power (60 km/h) modes, it 
delivers efficient and dependable 
performance for daily operations. Its 
spacious cabin and ergonomic 
seating ensure enhanced passenger 
comfort, making every ride smooth and enjoyable.  
 Three wheelers play a vital role for Sri Lanka’s  urban mobility, livelihoods, tourism, and last -mile 
connectivity. As the country advances its transition towards sustainabl e mobility , electric three -wheelers 
will see rapid a cceptance  due to their lower operating and maintenance costs, improved economics, 
and suitability for shared and intermediate public transport.
