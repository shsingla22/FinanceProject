its meeting held today, i.e., Saturday, October 17, 2026, has, inter alia, approved the acquisition by the 
Company of a 51.98% stake, on a fully diluted basis, in Cogkni t Semantics Private Limited (“ CSPL ” 
or the “ Target Company ”), for an aggregate cash consideration not exceeding Rs. 135.1 5 crore 
(Rupees One Hundred Thirty -Five Crore and Fifteen Lakh only), by way of:  
(i) purchase of equity shares of the Target Company from an existing shareholder; and
(ii) subscription to Series A Compulsorily Convertible Cumulative Preference Shares of the
Target Company (“Series A CCCPS ”)
(together, the “ Proposed Transaction ”). 
Pursuant to the above approval, the Company has, on October 17, 2026, executed a share purchase 
agreement, a share subscription agreement and a shareholders ’ agreement in relation to the Proposed 
Transaction (together, the “Transaction Documents ”) 
Complet ion of the Proposed Transaction is subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions 
precedent set out in the Transaction Documents.  Upon completion of the Proposed Transaction, the 
Target Company will become a subsidiary of the Company.
