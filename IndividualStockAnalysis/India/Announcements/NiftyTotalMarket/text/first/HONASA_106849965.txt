HONASA CONSUMER CLOCKS ~36% REVENUE GROWTH IN Q2FY27, ITS HIGHEST SINCE LISTING; 
EBITDA NEARLY DOUBLES TO INR 93 CRORE  
 
Gurugram, November 06, 2026 : Honasa Consumer Ltd., India’s leading beauty and personal care House 
of Brands, announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.  
“We have always believed that the opportunity for Honasa in India’s beauty and personal care market is 
much larger than what we have tapped into so far. There is still a lot we want to build, and Q2FY27 is a 
strong reflection of this ambition. We delivered  our highest growth since listing, with revenue growing 
~36% YoY and EBITDA nearly doubling to INR 93 Cr. What makes this growth particularly encouraging is 
the strength of the business behind it. Our Focus Categories grew over 40% and now contribute more than 
80% of our business, with strong momentum across General Trade, Modern Trade and eCommerce. This 
reflects the strength of our category playbooks and our ability to reach consumers across an increasingly 
diverse retail landscape.  
At the brand level, each business is writing its own growth story. Mamaearth has sustained its high -teens 
growth, The Derma Co. continues to grow profitably. Our Younger Brands grew over 45% YoY, reflecting 
highlighting the potential of our emerging portfo lio. This is what makes our House of Brands approach 
exciting. Each brand has a distinct consumer, proposition and opportunity, and we want to give each the 
focus, capabilities and independence to become a meaningful player in its category. There is still so much 
potential ahead, and we are excited to build on this momentum by creating a future -ready House of Brands 
that can shape the next phase of India's beauty and personal care industry.”, said Varun Alagh, Chairman 
and CEO & Co -founder, Honasa Consumer Limited  
Commenting on the same,  Ghazal Alagh, CIO & Co -founder, Honasa Consumer Limited, added, 
“Innovation remains central to this ambition. While we continue to strengthen the core of our flagship 
brands, we are also creating room for our younger brands to experiment with new formats and consumer 
propositions. From Mamaearth's Kulhad Chai Tinted Lip  Balm, in collaboration with Chaayos, to Dr. Sheth’s 
Collagen & Vitamin C Overnight Moisture Wrapping Mask, our innovations reflect our effort to stay 
culturally relevant and anticipate evolving consumer preferences, without compromising on product 
superio rity. As we enter the next phase of growth, we will continue to push the boundaries of innovation, 
deepen our presence in core categories and make our products accessible to more consumers across 
channels.”  
Key Highlights:  
 
● Q2FY27 recorded the highest growth since listing,  with revenue growing ~36% YoY  to INR 731 
Cr; EBITDA nearly  doubled to INR 93 Cr , indicating improving profitability  
● Focus Categories grew 40%+ , driven by strength across key channels  
● Mamaearth continued on its  high -teens growth trajectory with mid-teens EBITDA,  reinforcing 
the strength of our flagship brand in driving profitable growth  
 
● The Derma Co.  continued its strong profitable growth trajectory, driven by consumer love with 
over 65,000 outlet reach.  
● Younger Brands grew 45%+, with each brand building on its own growth drivers and finding new 
opportunities within its category  
● Offline momentum continued,  with sharper execution in General Trade expanding reach to  ~3.2 
lakh outlets , while Modern Trade recorded  40%+ YoY growth in offtake  
● Staying ahead of the curve through innovation , with new products and formats being introduced 
across the portfolio  
● Focus on developing superior products through effective formulations, with  Mamaearth, The 
Derma Co. and Aqualogica  demonstrating strong product performance in independent blind 
tests and clinical studies.
