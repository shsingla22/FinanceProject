The Member Technical, T ANSHA . 
 
B) The Hon’ble High Court of Madras heard the case  on dated 16 th February 202 6 and uploaded  
the judgement  on 18th  February 2026 , by stating that even though the learned counsel for the 
Appellant would strenuously urge this Writ Appellate Court to examine the correctness and 
validity of the reasons for rejection, the Hon’ble High Court of Madras is of the view that this 
aspect needs to be examined first by the Appellate Authority under Section 11 of the Tamil 
Nadu Transparency in Tenders Act, 1998. before it  is brought to the Writ Court .  
 
C) Therefore, under that circumstances, the appeal was disposed by the Hon’ble High Court of 
Madras, giving the Appellant liberty to file appeal before the competent statutory authority 
within five days from 16th February 2026 and as statutorily mandated the Appellate Authority 
shall take a decision one way or the other within a period of 15 days from the date of submission 
of the appeal.  
 
D) Consequently, the interim  application stands closed.
