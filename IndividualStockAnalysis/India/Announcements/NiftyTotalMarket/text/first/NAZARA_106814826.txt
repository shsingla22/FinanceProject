4. Investment in Nazara Technologies UK Limited  
 
The Board has a pproved the infusion of funds aggregating up to USD 77 .00 Million  (~₹ 742.72 
Crores ) into Nazara Technologies UK Limited (“Nazara UK”) , a wholly owned subsidiary of the 
Company,  by way of subscription to its equity shares, in one or more tranches, subject to compliance 
with applicable laws and regulatory requirements . 
 
The proposed investment in Nazara UK is intended to facilitate funding of the second tranche of the 
purchase consideration payable to the sellers of Bluetile Games, S.L. ("Bluetile") and Bestplay 
Systems, S.L. ("Bestplay "), pursuant to the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement, the amendments 
thereto and other transaction documents executed in connection with the acquisition.  
 
The aforesaid investment is expected to support the completion of the acquisition transaction and 
strengthen the Company's international gaming portfolio through its wholly owned subsidiary.  
 
The details for infusion of funds , as required under the Listing Regulations read with the SEBI Master 
Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025 -CFD -POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, is enclosed 
herewith as Annexure  – E. 
 
5. Conversion of outstanding inter -corporate loan into equity share capital  
 
➢ The Board has approved the conversion of the outstanding inter -corporate loan  aggregating upto 
~₹129.35 Crores  into equity share capital of Smaaash Entertainment Private Limited ( “Smaaash ”), 
a wholly -owned subsidiary of the Company.  
 
 
 
The Company had extended financial assistance to Smaaash pursuant to the resolution plan 
approved under the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process of Smaaash and in terms of the Inter -
Corporate Loan Agreement dated June 6, 2025. The said agreement provide s the Company with a 
right to convert the whole or any part of the outstanding loan into equity shares of Smaaash.  
 
The proposed conversion is being undertaken with the objective of strengthening the capital base 
and net worth of Smaaash , improving its debt -equity profile, supporting its future growth plans 
and aligning its capital structure with its long -term business requirements.  
 
➢ The Board has approved the conversion of the outstanding loan aggregating  up to GBP 23 .00 
Million  (i.e. ~₹ 294.40 Crores ) advanced by the Company to Nazara Technologies UK Limited  
("Nazara UK"), a wholly -owned subsidiary of the Company, into equity share capital of Nazara UK.  
 
The Company has, from time to time, provided financial assistance to Nazara UK in compliance 
with the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 
1999 and applicable overseas investment regulations.  
 
The proposed conversion is intended to strengthen the capital base and net worth position of Nazara 
UK, improve its financial position and support its long -term growth and business requirements.  
 
Pursuant to the approval, Nazara UK and Smaaash shall issue and allot such number of equity shares to the 
Company as may be determined based on the valuation report obtained from an independent valuer and in 
accordance with the applicable provisions of the Law. 
 
Post conversion, Smaaash and Nazara UK shall continue to remain wholly -owned subsidiaries of the 
Company and there shall be no change in the ownership or control of these subsidiaries.  
 
The details for conversion of loan into equity share capital, as required under the Listing Regulations read 
with the SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025 -CFD -POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, 
is enclosed herewith as Annexure – F.
