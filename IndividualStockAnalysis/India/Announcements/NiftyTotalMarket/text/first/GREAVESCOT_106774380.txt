Ampere Magnus GMax  Begins the Ultimate Endurance Ride -  
Sets Out on a ~4,000 km ride from East to West of India  
The Ultimate Endurance Ride is a Sunrise to Sunset journey from India's easternmost point in Dong, 
Arunachal Pradesh, to westernmost point in Guhar Moti, Gujarat, testing the scooter across different 
terrain and weather conditions India has  
 
Bengaluru/New Delhi, 08 September 2026: On this  World EV Day, Ampere , the electric two -wheeler 
brand of Greaves Electric Mobility, has embarked on the Ultimate Endurance Ride - a ~4,000 km test of 
the Ampere Magnus GMax  from India's easternmost sunrise point in Dong, Arunachal Pradesh, to 
westernmost sunset point in Guhar Moti, Gujarat . Ridden by Girish Seth of Weekend on Wheels, the 15 -
day journey will traverse 8 states, taking the scooter across high -altitude mountain roads, highways, urban 
stretches, heat, humidity and challenging terrains.  Over these 15 consecutive days the Ampere Magnus 
GMax  will face a temperature swing of roughly 20° from around 15°C in the humid, monsoon -hit 
conditions of Dong to over 35°C in the Gujarat heat  - descent of over 1,200 meters from Dong to sea level, 
and five distinct terrain types  - rocky mountain passes, forested hill roads, smooth national highways, 
dense urban city stretches, and the coastal/salt -flat terrain near Guhar Moti . Every kilometer, every 
charge and every reading will be recorded.  
 
The expedition brings Ampere’s “Built for Bharat” philosophy to life . India is not one kind of road, one 
kind of rider or one kind of riding condition - and scooter s built for that reality must  prove it in the 
conditions themselves.  The Ultimate Endurance Ride  takes this philosophy to the road, putting the 
Ampere Magnus GMax  to the test across ~4,000  km across  8 states, from the cold, steep terrain of 
Arunachal Pradesh to the hot and long stretches of Gujarat. Along the way, the scooter will showcase how 
its Next Gen LFP battery  which offers 2,00,000 km battery life , dual frame chassis  and NxG.io smart 
technology  come together to deliver durability, reliability, endurance and connected intelligence.  
 
Speaking about the expedition, Mr. Vikas Singh, Managing Director, Greaves Electric Mobility Limited , 
said, “At Ampere, ‘Built for Bharat’ is rooted in a simple belief – India is not one kind of road, one kind of 
rider or one kind of riding condition. It is a country of immense diversity, and our products need to be built 
with that reality at their core. The Ultimate Endurance Ride takes that belief and tests it. Over ~4,000 km 
from Dong to Guhar Moti, the Magnus GMax  will be put to test the durability, reliability and intelligence 
capabilities across  challenging  weather and terrain conditions our customer s will ever use . This journey is 
about demonstrating that when technology, reliability and performance are built around Bharat, electric 
mobility can become a truly smart ride for India .” 
 
The expedition will also make key stops in Patna, Varanasi and Jaipur, creating opportunities for 
engagement with Ampere’s dealer network, riders and local stakeholders, while taking the Built for Bharat 
story into different parts of the country.  The completion of the expedition will mark another milestone in 
Ampere’s effort to demonstrate, why the Ampere Electric Scooters are  Bharat Ki Smart Ride.

About Greaves Electric Mobility Limited:  
Greaves Electric Mobility Limited (GEML), the electric mobility business of Greaves Cotton Limited (GCL), 
is one of the first companies in India to focus on the evolving market of electric vehicles (“ EV”) and has 
been among the frontrunners at driving EV adoption in the country. With over 17 years  of experience 
designing and manufacturing electric vehicles, GEML serves both the electric 2 -wheeler and 3 -wheeler 
segments. GEML along with its subsidiaries, operates three manufacturing plants located in Hyderabad, 
Noida, and Ranipet. The company suppor ts its network through 400 dealer touchpoints across India, 
thereby aiming  minimal downtime and enhanced customer experience . GEML holds 8 India Book of 
Records* and 1 Asia Book of Records* titles, reflecting its focus on technology and design led innovative 
& durable mobility solutions . Subsidiaries MLR Auto Limited and Bestway Agencies Private Limited help 
extend GEML’s market presence. Committed to affordability and sustainability, GEML provides clean 
mobility solutions contributing to India’s shift toward electric transportation.
