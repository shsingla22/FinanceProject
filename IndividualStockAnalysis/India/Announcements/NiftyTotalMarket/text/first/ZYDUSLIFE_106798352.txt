USFDA inspection concludes at Zydus' manufacturing plant at SEZ II, Ahmedabad 
 
Ahmedabad, India, 28 September, 2026  
  
We wish to inform that the USFDA conducted cGMP inspection for the company’s manufacturing plant 
at SEZ II, Ahmedabad. The inspection was conducted from 21st to 28th September 2026. 
  
The inspection closed with one observation. There were no data integrity related observations. The 
Company will closely work with the USFDA to address the observation expeditiously. 
 
This may kindly be considered as a disclosure pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
