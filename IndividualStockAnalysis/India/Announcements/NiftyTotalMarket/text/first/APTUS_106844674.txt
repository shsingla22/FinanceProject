Commenting on the results, Mr. P. Balaji, Managing Director, said,  “Q2 FY27 witnessed 
continued improvement in business momentum, supported by steady execution of our strategic 
growth initiatives, alongside ongoing technology enhancements and process improvements. Our AUM grew 20 %  Y o Y  t o  ₹ 14,177  Cr, supported by strong disbursements of ₹ 1,153  Cr, 
reflecting healthy business growth.  
 
The strategic initiatives undertaken at the start of the financial year are progressing steadily and contributing to our growth momentum.  
 
• We continu e to expand our presence in Maharashtra and Odisha, where we are witnessing 
encouraging business traction, while also deepening our penetration in existing markets.  
• The branches opened over the last few quarters are increasingly contributing to our business growth. Our total branch network now stands at 397, following the addition of 58 branches 
during H1 FY27 (including 25 branches in Q2). The branches opened during H1 are expected to contribute to business growth from Q 3 onwards . 

• Our efforts to broaden and diversify our sourcing channels are gaining traction, supported 
by the expansion of our connector network and greater emphasis on digital marketing initiatives. The connector channel is steadily strengthening its contribution to overall disbursements, providing an additional avenue to support our growth ambitions.  
• Increasing our Average Ticket Size (ATS) remains an important strategic priority, with a focus 
on strengthening portfolio quality, improving customer economics and supporting 
sustainable growth.  
• We continue to explore opportunities to diversify our lending portfolio beyond Home Loans and SME Loans, while carefully evaluating potential products that align with our long -term 
growth strategy.  
Our continued focus on strengthening customer selection and maintaining prudent underwriting standards is yielding encouraging results, with a noticeable improvement in the quality of customers.  
Our continued focus on technology and data analytics is helping streamline operations and strengthen credit assessment. Digital adoption remains high, with over 92% of agreements executed digitally and 94% of collections processed through digital channels. Increased usage of Account Aggregators, CKYC and Credit Bureau integrations, along with ongoing process improvements, is expected to further streamline operations, enhance underwriting efficiency and improve the overall customer experience.  
On the asset quality front, collections were impacted by the  performance in the NBFC business 
leading to an increase in delinquencies. However, the underlying portfolio quality remains 
resilient, and we have undertaken initiatives to strengthen our collection framework, particularly in the NBFC business. The GNPA and Net NPA stood at 1 .68% and 1 .27%, 
respectively. We remain confident of improved collections and a gradual normalisation of asset quality metrics to earlier levels by the end of FY27.  
 
On the profitability front, our spreads stood at 8.9%. The Opex ratio remained at 2 .8%, while 
credit cost stood at 0 .7%. Net profit for the quarter stood at ₹ 267 Cr, translating into an RoA of 
7.7% and an RoE of 20. 1%, among the best in the industry.  
 Given the progress on our growth initiatives and factoring in the current operating environment, 
we remain confident of achieving AUM growth of our earlier guidance of around 22% for FY27, 
while maintaining our outlook on spreads and operating costs.
