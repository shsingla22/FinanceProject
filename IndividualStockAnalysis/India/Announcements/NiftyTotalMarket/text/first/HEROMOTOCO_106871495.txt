Harley -Davidson Marks 17 Years in India, Commits to Expanding brand’s  
presence and accelerating growth  in Partnership with Hero MotoCorp  
New Delhi, November 26,  2026:  Harley -Davidson President & CEO Artie Starrs, together 
with Hero MotoCorp’s Executive Chairman Dr. Pawan Munjal commit to further 
strengthening Harley -Davidson brand’s presence in one of the world’s largest two -wheeler 
markets through a strategic initiativ e - Mission: The Big RIDE . Marking 17 years since entering 
the Indian market, this initiative in partnership with Hero MotoCorp, will focus on an enhanced 
customer experience, wider dealer footprint, expanded product portfolio and deeper engagement 
with th e country’s growing premium motorcycle and rider community.  
Dr. Pawan Munjal, Executive Chairman, Hero MotoCorp , said, " Our partnership with Harley -
Davidson brings together two strong, valued organisations with complementary strengths – Harley -
Davidson's i conic brand and product legacy, and Hero MotoCorp's deep understanding of the Indian 
consumer, manufacturing excellence, engineering capabilities and extensive market reach. 
Together, we have enabled the Harley -Davidson brand experience accessibility to a wider audience 
while creating new opportunities in India's growing premium motorcycle segment. As we celebrate 
17 years of Harley -Davidson in India, we remain committed to building on this strong foundation 
and taking the partnership to new frontiers."  
Artie Starrs, Global CEO, Harley -Davidson , said, "Our partnership with Hero MotoCorp gives us a 
strong foundation to continue growing in India, combining Harley -Davidson’ global brand      
recognition and product expertise with Hero MotoCorp's expansive retai l network. We’ve seen 
market share gains this year across all of our segments, reflecting improving demand and sustained 
retail momentum. Going forward, we have an opportunity to strengthen our relationship with riders 
and dealers as we expand the Harley -Davidson community.  
Strengthening the product proposition  
As part of its India strategy, Harley -Davidson offers its global motorcycle portfolio across Custom 
Vehicle Operations, Touring, Cruisers (Softail), Adventure Touring and Sport categories. The 
brand will also continue to introduce new models under the Harley -Davidson X range.  
Building a stronger riding community  
At the heart of Harley -Davidson identity  is the passionate riding community, with an exciting 
calendar of experiences through March 2027. Thi s features the Northern and Eastern H.O.G. 
Rides, participation at India Bike Week featuring Flat Track Racing, Harley Rock Riders and 
Custom Build -Off, the India H.O.G. Rally and Destination X.  
With over 3,000 members, India’s Harley Owners Group (H.O.G.)  is the largest H.O.G. network 
across Asia’s emerging markets. Further reinforcing this strong rider fraternity, more than 100 
Indian Harley -Davidson riders will participate in Asia Harley Days in Thailand on November 27 –
28, 2026, making India one of the m ost strongly represented markets at the regional event.  
Strong business momentum and expanding retail footprint  
Harley -Davidson has crossed a significant milestone in India, with its cumulative customer base      
exceed ing 80,000 motorcycles since commenci ng its dealer operations in 2010. The Harley -
Davidson X range, co -developed with Hero MotoCorp, has emerged as a significant growth driver,

About Hero MotoCorp  
Hero MotoCorp, headquartered in New Delhi, India, is the world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and 
scooters for 25  consecutive years with ~$5 billion revenue. The Company’s global footprint spans 54 
countries across Asia, Africa, Europe, Central and Latin America with a customer base of over 135 million.  
The Company operates eight manufacturing facilities - six in Ind ia and one each in Colombia and 
Bangladesh - along with two Research and Development centres: Hero Centre for Innovation and 
Technology (CIT) in India and Hero Tech Centre Germany (TCG). Hero MotoCorp is driving the transition 
to electric mobility through VIDA. Strategic alliances with Harley -Davidson and Zero Motorcycles support 
the design and development of premium and electric motorcycles, respectively.  
Further demonstrating its commitment to the EV future, the Company has made strategic investments in 
two- and three -wheeler EV manufacturers, Ather Energy and Euler Motors.  
Hero MotoCorp is the only Indian two -wheeler manufacturer listed on the prestigious Dow Jones 
Sustainability Index (DJSI).Hero MotoCorp’s Executive Chairman, Dr. Pawan Munjal, was named to the 2025 
TIME100 Climate List of the 100 most influential leaders driving business climate action.  
Beyond mobility, Hero MotoCorp is a major global promoter of sports, including golf, football, field hockey, 
and cricket. Hero MotoSports Team Rally  is India, a flag bearer in global rally racing.
