Press Release for Immediate Circulation   
 
 
    DCB Bank announces  Second  Quarter 202 7 Results  
 
October  23, 2026 , Mumbai : The Board of Directors  of DCB Bank  Ltd. (BSE: 532772; NSE: 
DCB) at its meeting in Mumbai on October 23, 2026, approved the unaudited financial results 
for the quarter and half -year ended September  30, 2026  (Q2 FY 202 7) along with  limited 
review report by statutory auditors ‘ Varma & Varma , Chartered Accountants’ and ‘ Deloitte 
Haskins & Sells , Chartered Accountants’.  
 
 
Highlights:  
  
 
1) The Bank’s Profit After Tax (PAT)  for Q 2 FY 2027  was at INR 236 Cr. In comparison 
Profit After Tax for Q 2 FY 2026  was at INR 184 Cr., growth of 29%.   
 
2) Advances  growth year -on-year was at 21% and Deposits  growth year -on-year was at 
19%.  
 
3) The Gross NPA  as on September  30, 2026 , was at 2.29%. Net NPA was at 0.73% as 
on September 30, 2026 .  
 
4) The Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR)  as on September  30, 2026  was at 81.38% and 
PCR without considering Gold Loans NPAs was at 8 2.01%.  
 
5) Capital Adequacy continues to be stron g and a s on September  30, 2026 , the Capital 
Adequacy Ratio  was at 16.63% (with Tier I at 14.57% and Tier II at 2.06% as per Basel  
III norms).  
 
Speaking on the Q2 2027 results Mr. Praveen  Kutty , Managing Director & CEO said,  
  
“Robust growth in both deposits and advances, steadily increasing operating profit, Gross NPA 
& Net NPA at multi year lows and PCR at multi year high, culminating in the highest ever 
quarterly PAT for the fifth consecutive quarter, ROA in excess of 1% and R OE in excess of 
14.5% ”.

About DCB Bank  
 
DCB Bank Limited is a new generation private sector bank with 48 3 branches (as on September  
30, 2026) across 20 states and 2 union territories. It is a scheduled commercial bank regulated by 
the Reserve Bank of India. It is professionally managed and governed. DCB Bank has contemporary technology and infrastructure, including state- of-the-art inter net banking and mobile 
banking app for personal as well as business banking customers.  
 
The Bank’s business segments are Retail, micro -SMEs, SMEs, mid -Corporate, Microfinance 
Institutions (MFI), Agriculture, Government, Public Sector, Indian Banks, Co- operative Banks and 
Non Banking Finance Companies (NBFC).
