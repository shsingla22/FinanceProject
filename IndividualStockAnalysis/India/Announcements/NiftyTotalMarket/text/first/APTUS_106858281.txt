As you may be aware, in terms of the applicable provisions of the Income -tax Act, 2025  (“the Act”) , 
dividend paid or distributed by a company is taxable in the hands of the shareholders. Accordingly, the 
Company is required to deduct tax at source at the time of credit of dividend to the account of the 
shareholder or at the time of payment, whichever is earlier. The applicable tax deduction / withholding 
rate may vary depending on the residential status and category of the shareholder. Accordingly, 
dividend will be paid after deduction of tax at source / withholding tax, including surcharge and cess , 
wherever applicable.

Limited1.

Tax shall be deducted at source in accordance with Section 393(2), Table Sl. No. 17(h), of the Income -tax 
Act, 2025, at the rate of 20% on dividend income payable to non -resident shareholders, other than the 
specified dividend income from units in an International Financial Services Centre (IFSC).  
 
The applicable tax rate on dividend income is prescribed under Section 207(1), Table Sl. No. 1, of the 
Income -tax Act, 2025.The above rates shall be increased by applicable surcharge and Health and 
Education Cess , wherever applicable.  
 
However, in accordance with Section 159 of the Income -tax Act, 2025, a non -resident shareholder may 
opt to be governed by the provisions of the applicable Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA) 
between India and the country of tax residence, where the provisions of the DTAA are more beneficial. 
The benefit of the lower treaty rate shall be considered subject to submission of the requisite 
documents and fulfilment of the applicable conditions.  
 
For this purpose, the non -resident shareholder seeking to avail the benefit of the applicable DTAA shall 
furnish the following documents:  
 
• In case  of FPI / FII, copy  of SEBI  registration  certificate;  
• Self-attested  true  copy  of Tax Residency  Certificate  (“TRC”)  obtained  from  the tax authorities 
of the country of which the shareholder is resident for the Financial Year 2026-27; 
• Self declaration  in Form  41 Click Here  to download  Form  41) 
• Self-attested  true  copy  of the PAN  Card  if allotted  by the Indian  Income  Tax authorities;  
• Self-declaration  in the format  given  below,  certifying  the following  points:
