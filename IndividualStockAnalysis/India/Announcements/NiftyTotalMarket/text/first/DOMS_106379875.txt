Dear Shareholders, Good A fternoon!  
 
Happy Navratri!  
 
It’s my privilege to address all of you today at the 19th Annual General Mee ting of our Company - 
DOMS Industries Limited.  
 
As we begin, we take a moment to honour and pay tribute to our esteemed founders – my father Late 
Shri Rasiklal Raveshia and my uncle Late Shri Mansukhlal Rajani – whose pioneering spirit, foresight, 
dedication and entrepreneurial acumen have been the corn erstone of our Company’s success. 
Additionally, let us also take a moment to cherish the memory of Late Shri Amarjeet Sharma, a 
remarkable leader who played an instrumental role in shaping the DOMS success story.  
 
I am pleased to inform you all that Financial Year 2025 has demonstrated our organization's resilience 
and adaptability in the face of challenging market conditions. Driven by steady sales growth 
momentum, we have not only navigated challenges but have eme rged stronger on the back of 
effective execution, strategic expansion and future ready investments.  
 
Our true mantra to success has always been our strong emphasis on product innovation, design 
excellence and product engineering, all cantered around our consumers' needs. Our products in the 
stationery and art materials space today cater not only to studen ts, young adults and professionals, 
but also to hobbyists and creative communities. Last year, we further diversified through strategic 
initiatives into new categories such as baby hygiene with Uniclan and back -to-school products with 
SKIDO Industries broa dening our market presence. These initiatives align with our long -term vision of 
becoming a multi -product child -centric organization .  
 
We continued to strengthen our presence in the stationery and art material segment, with office 
supplies and paper stationery categories emerging as key growth drivers for Financial Year 2025. 
Further, our portfolio was enhanced with launch of highlighters  with differentiated pack aging, 
scholastic adhesives in ergonomic bottles, fine art range with a greater variety of tools and attractively 
curated kits and combo packs, all of which were met with positive market reception. These strategic 
initiatives in product expansion have reinfo rced our brand’s appeal and are fuelling our continued 
growth trajectory.  
 
Our agility in responding to market needs is rooted in a seamlessly integrated ecosystem that 
combines in -house product design and engineering, and backward -integrated driven  production 
infrastructure. W e continued to expand our manufacturing infrastructure by an additional 0.5 million 
square feet in the previous financial year and are  set to further increase the capacity in Financial Year 
2026. A major step towards this expansion is the development of our 44 -acre site located in 
Umbergaon, Gujarat where construction is progressing positively with the possession  of the first 
building expected by beginning of Q4 in the current financial year for installation of plant and 
machinery. Once fully operational, this facility will significantly strengthen our production capabilities 
and help us capture greater market share.  
 
Our strength lies not just in what we produce but how we reach our consumers. Our extensive multi -
channel distribution network has been key to our growth. Present across India and exporting to over 
55 countries in 6 continents, we have created a discipline d distribution network ensuring effective 
market penetration, thus further facilitating our growth strategy.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Over the past year, we have continued to strengthen our consumer engagement through a strategic 
blend of digital outreach and on -ground initiatives. Our growing presence on platforms like YouTube 
with over 3 million subscribers, and more than 100,000 Insta gram followers has positioned us as one 
of the most followed brands in the stationery and art material segment.  To further strengthen our 
connect with consumers, we launched DOMS Art League online and opened DOMS Painting Studio 
across KidZania in Mumbai, NCR region and recently in Dubai, encouraging children to express 
themselves.  
 
Our Corporate Social Responsibility initiatives are designed to support the well -being of the society. 
We have made significant contribu tions towards the construction of a state -of-the-art hospital in 
Umbergaon and addi tionally provide educa tional scholarships for economically disadvantaged 
students, enabling them to pursue their academic goals and achieve self -sufficiency. Further, with our 
commitment towards sustainable growth, our company has taken initiatives like use of solar energy, 
third  party ambien t air quality assessments, noise monitoring, waste -water analyses and installation 
of Sewage Treatment Plants to treat domestic waste water and promote sustainable water usage.  
 
Our people /employees are critical in strengthening our competitive position. Their dedication and 
contribution are what truly drive our progress. I take this opportunity to recognize the enthusiasm 
and dedica tion of our team whose con tinued support has ensured sustainable growth. As a step 
towards recognizing  their contribution, we have granted ESOPs to over 925  people /employees across 
functions and will continue to do so in the future, making them part of this success lead journey.  
 
Coming to our consolidated financial performance during Financial Year 2025, we are happy to share 
that our revenue from operations grew by 24.43% and stood at ₹ 1,912 crores as compared to ₹ 1,537 
crores in Financial year 2024. This growth was driven by c onsistent performance across our core 
categories, successful new product launches and the integration of Uniclan, which collectively 
contributed to this performance. The sales growth was backed by growth in EBITDA which grew by 
27.8% to ₹ 348 crores in Fin ancial Year 2025 as compared to ₹ 273 crores in Financial Year 2024, 
coupled with expansion of EBITDA margin from 17.7% to 18.2% in Financial Year 2025. PAT for 
Financial Year 2025 grew by 33.7% to ₹ 214 crores as compared to ₹ 160 crores in Financial Year  2024 
and PAT margin for Financial Year 2025 rose to 11.2% as compared to 10.4% in Financial Year 2024.  
 
Speaking about the Way Forward. . .  
 
We at DOMS have always aimed to set the Company on the course of a sustained high growth 
trajectory. On the back of this, we continue to focus on expanding our manufacturing infrastructure. 
In addition to the greenfield 44+ acre project, we are also comple menting this by execution / expansion 
initiatives within the adjoining areas, as well as on the new land and building purchase made during 
the last few quarters, positioning us strongly to capitalize on the latent demand of our products. 
Additionally, to improve regional diversification and enhance capabilities in Eastern India, we acquired 
a 51% stake in Super Treads Private Limited , a company engaged in paper stationery business.  
 
India, with its demographic advantage and on the back of proac tive decision making, has emerged as 
the most a ttractive consump tion destination, providing a huge growth opportunity to be addressed. 
Notwithstanding macro headwinds, we continue to prioritize our growth in the Indian markets with 
focused distribution network expansion. On exports front too, in addition to positive resu lts from 
exports of our own brand of products within our existing served export markets, we are also very  
 
 
 
 
 
 
excited about our distribution agreement with FILA which will enable us to leverage their group 
network  and infrastructure, partially offsetting the current dip in demand from US due to tariff 
measures and  pressures.  
 
In conclusion, I want to reiterate our long -term commitment to you, our valued shareholders that 
DOMS will continue to be driven by innovation, backed by execution and fuelled by purpose. We are 
building DOMS not just for today, but for the future, a futur e where we empower every learner, 
creator and dreamer with the tools they need to prepare for tomorrow.  
 
As we say, "Every Ambition Needs Preparation" and at DOMS, we are prepared to dream bigger, aim
