Mumbai, August 1, 202 5: Tata Power,  India's largest vertically integrated power compan y, 
today reported Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₹ 1,262  crore  (up 6 % YoY)  in the first quarter ended 
June 30, 202 5. The Company’s revenue rose to ₹ 17,464 crore  (up 4 % YoY) ; while EBITDA 
grew to ₹ 3,930 crore  (up 17 % YoY)  in Q1 FY2 6.

About Tata Power:
