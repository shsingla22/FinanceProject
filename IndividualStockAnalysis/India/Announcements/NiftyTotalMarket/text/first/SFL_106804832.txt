Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR”), we wish to inform you that Sheela Foam Limited (“Company” 
or “SFL”) entered into an amendment agreement on 1st October 2026 with House of Kieraya 
Limited and its significant shareholders to change the Affirmative Vote Matters in the 
Shareholders’ Agreement executed on 17th July 2023. Consequently, the status of House of 
Kieraya Limited has changed from a jointly controlled entity to a subsidiary of the Company, 
effective from the amendment date, i.e., 1st October, 2026.

Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR and the SEBI Master Circular dated 30th January 2026.
