Brief Facts:  
 
The Company had initiated arbitration proceedings against Airport 
Authority of India, Goa Airport (Goa) (“AAI”) in relation to the 
F&B Concession at Goa Airport, seeking rebate in concession fee 
due to non-provision of electricity by AAI, along with release of 
security deposit, bank guarantee and other cash deposits with AAI. 
AAI, in turn, had raised its counterclaims pertaining to alleged non-
payment of concession fee.  
An arbitral award dated September 26, 2022 was passed wherein 
certain amounts were awarded in favour of the Company and 
AAI’s counter claims were rejected (“Arbitral Award”).  
 
Aggrieved by the same, AAI filed the application under section 34, 
Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, challenging the Arbitral 
Award wherein the present Order has been passed, thereby 
allowing the AAI’s petition. Our Company also had filed an 
application for the execution in April 2024 (“Execution 
Application”) of the Arbitral Award.  
 
In terms of the said Order, without getting into the merits of the 
award and  based on a recent judgement of Hon’ble Supreme Court, 
Hon’ble High Court held that the unilateral appointment of 
Arbitrator by AAI in the matter  to be ‘ void ab initio’, thereby 
rendering the arbitral award a ‘nullity’ in the eyes of law . 
Consequently, the Execution Application filed by the Company in 
relation to the said Arbitral Award has also been disposed as 
infructuous.
