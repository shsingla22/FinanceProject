Chairman’s Message
“During  the quarter,  Graphite  India  delivered  Net Sales  of Rs. 826 Cr, up by 13.3% y-o-y, driven  by higher  volumes  and 
realizations . The Company  recorded  EBITDA  of Rs. 189 Cr and Net Profit  of Rs. 94 Cr, after  an exceptional  item  of Rs. 22 Cr 
arising  from  the closure  of German  operations . Capacity  utilization  was 96%, compared  to 99% in Q2 FY2026 . The Company  
maintained  a strong  balance  sheet,  with  Net Cash  of Rs. 3,827 Cr at the end of September  2026 . 
Global  crude  steel  production  remained  subdued  during  the nine  months  ended  September  2026 , declining  by 0.5% y-o-y to 
1,367 million  MT, with  performance  varying  across  major  regions . Chinese  crude  steel  production  decreased  by 2.9% y-o-y to 
725 million  MT, while  production  in the European  Union  remained  broadly  stable . North  America  recorded  growth  of 5.2%, 
while  the production  in the Middle  East as expected  declined  by 9.3%. In sharp  contrast,  India’s  crude  steel  production  
increased  by 6.2% y-o-y to 130 million  MT, significantly  ahead  of global  production  trends .
India’s  steel  demand  outlook  remains  encouraging,  supported  by infrastructure  development,  real estate  activity  and 
manufacturing  expansion  plans . Renewable  energy  investments,  growth  in automotive  production,  export  opportunities  and 
supportive  policy  measures,  continue  to drive  steel  consumption .
Graphite  electrode  prices  have  shown  some  improvement  during  the quarter,  supported  by a continued  recovery  in demand . 
However,  higher  input  costs  have  limited  the extent  of margin  expansion . The USA recently  imposed  preliminary  tariffs  on 
graphite  electrodes,  but given  the Company’s  geographically  diversified  export  revenue  base,  this will not have  any material  
impact .
The steel  industry’s  focus  on decarbonisation  including  the adoption  of low carbon  technologies  is expected  to shape  its long  
term  development . Against  this backdrop  and ongoing  industry  consolidation,  Graphite  India  remains  optimistic  about  the 
medium  term  outlook  for graphite  electrodes . The Company  as always  remains  focused  on operational  efficiency,  
sustainability,  cash  flow  generation  and long  term  value  creation  for all its stakeholders .”
K K Bangur 
Chairman
