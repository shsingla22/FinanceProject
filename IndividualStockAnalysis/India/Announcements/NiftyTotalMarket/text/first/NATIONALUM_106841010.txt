Press Release 
 
NALCO Scripts History with Best-Ever Half Yearly 
Financial Results Since Inception  
Achieves record all-time high Revenue, EBITDA, PBT, and PAT 
for the first half of FY 26-27 
 
 H1 Net Profit up 54% YoY to Rs. 3837 crore 
 Q2 Net Profit up 28% YoY to Rs. 1835 crore 
 
 
 
Key Highlights of H1 FY 2026 – 27 
 
 All time High Total income: Rs.11,143 crore 
 All time High EBIDTA: Rs.5,539 crore 
 Highest-ever PBT: Rs.5,160 crore  
 Highest-ever PAT: 3,837 crore 
 Production:  Highest-ever H1 Aluminium Cast Metal Production 
 Sales: Highest-ever H1 Domestic Alumina & Domestic Aluminium Sale 
Bhubaneswar, 02.11.2026:  National Aluminium Company Limited (NALCO), the Navratna CPSE 
under the Ministry of Mines, Government of India, has delivered its best-ever financial performance 
for the first half of FY 2026–27 since its inception, achieving record levels across all key financial 
parameters, including Total Income, EBITDA, Profit Before Tax (PBT) and Profit After Tax (PAT). 
The Company's Total Income for H1 FY 2026–27 stood at Rs.11,143 crore, as against Rs.8,374 crore 
in the corresponding period of the previous year, registering a robust growth of 33%. Demonstrating 
strong operational and financial performance, EBITDA increased by 50% to Rs.5,539 crore, while 
PBT and PAT recorded impressive growth of 55% and 54%, reaching Rs.5,160 crore and Rs.3,837 
crore, respectively compared to corresponding period of FY 2025–26. 
 
======================================================= 
 
Similarly, in the second quarter of FY 2026–27, the company has registered an impressive 28 % 
growth in net profit at Rs.1835 crore for the quarter ended September 2026, compared to Rs.1,433 
crore reported during the corresponding period of the previous financial year.  
According to the unaudited financial results approved by the Board of Directors at its meeting held 
in New Delhi on November 02, the company's total income from operations stood at Rs.5419 crore, 
up by 26% as against Rs.4,292 crore recorded in the second quarter of FY 2025–26, reflecting 
NALCO's continued operational excellence and robust business performance. 
It may be mentioned that on the production front, NALCO has achieved Highest-ever Aluminium 
Cast Metal Production of 2.35 lakh tonnes for the first half of FY 2026–27.  Similarly, on the sales 
front, NALCO achieved its highest-ever cumulative domestic sale of Alumina and metal sales in H1 
FY 2026–27. 
The company has announced an interim dividend of Rs.4.50 per equity share (90% on the face value 
of Rs.5 each), amounting to Rs.826.48 crore for FY 2026–27. 
Speaking on the results, Shri Brijendra Pratap Singh, CMD, NALCO, said that the Company’s strong 
performance during Q2 and H1 of FY 2026-27 was driven by robust operational efficiency, strong 
physical performance, and favourable market conditions supported by prudent marketing strategies. 
Highlighting the Company's growth roadmap, Shri Singh said that NALCO remains committed to 
its expansion initiatives, with the 5th Stream Expansion of the Alumina Refinery at the advanced 
stage of commissioning and groundwork for the proposed 0.5 MTPA brownfield Smelter Expansion 
Project at Anugola, Odisha, progressing steadily. As we continue to pursue key expansion projects 
and capacity augmentation plans, we remain confident of creating long-term value for all 
stakeholders while contributing to the nation’s industrial growth,” Shri Singh added. 
Shri Singh attributed the Company’s sustained growth and strong financial performance to the 
dedication and commitment of its employees, continuous improvements in operational excellence, 
and the unwavering confidence of its stakeholders. He also expressed his gratitude to the Ministry 
of Mines, Government of India, and the Government of Odisha for their continued guidance and 
support in advancing NALCO’s strategic objectives and growth initiatives.
