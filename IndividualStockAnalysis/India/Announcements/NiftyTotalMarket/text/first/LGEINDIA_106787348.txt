LG India Introduces New Range of Semi -Automatic Washing Machines, Combining 
Powerful Performance, Smart Convenience and Bold Design  
• New lineup features premium aesthetics and advanced washing technologies,  
• Features include – Toughened Glass Lid, Dual Spin Shower, Roller Jet Pulsator+, Ultra 
Scrub Wall and Wind Jet  Dry+ across 15 models from 8 kg to 12 kg capacities.  
• Exports scheduled to begin in 2027 
 
New Delhi, 22nd September 2026: LG Electronics India Limited today announced the launch of 
its new range of Semi -Automatic Washing Machines, designed to deliver powerful washing 
performance, smarter convenience, durable construction and bold premium design. Available 
across 15 models in 8 kg to 12 kg capacities, the new lineup offers Indian consumers greater choice 
across family sizes, laundry needs and design preferences.  Exports of AX Semi -Automatic 
Washing Machines are scheduled to begin in 2027, targeting over 10 countries globally . 
 
With this launch, LG is redefining expectations from the semi -automatic washing machine 
category by addressing everyday laundry challenges such as detergent residue, lint, stubborn 
stains, slow drying and fragile lids. The range brings together practical i nnovation and stylish 
design to make laundry simpler, faster and more effective.  
 
“The new Semi-Automatic Washing Machine range is designed for consumers who expect more 
from everyday laundry. It combines LG’ s trusted technology, durable engineering, premium design 
and smart features that solve real household challenges,”  said Young Min Hwang - Home 
Appliances Solution Sales Division Leader , LG Electronics India Limited . 
 
At the heart of the new lineup is Dual Spin Shower, that uses two powerful water inlets to rinse 
away residual detergent more effectively, leaving clothes cleaner and fresher. The Roller Jet 
Pulsator+, inspired by a whale -tail design, creates dynamic water  movement to improve cloth 
circulation and help remove stubborn dirt while caring for fabrics.  
 
It also features Ultra Scrub Wall, designed with a special scrub pattern to tackle tough dirt on cuffs, 
collars and heavily soiled areas. The New Smart Filter efficiently captures lint during the wash 
cycle and is easy to remove and clean, helping maintain better wash qua lity with less effort.  
 
The range also introduces Wind Jet  Dry+, that uses powerful airflow during spinning to remove 
excess moisture and help clothes dry faster. It also offers 3+1 wash programs for greater laundry 
flexibility and Rat Away Technology for added protection and durability.  
 
Designed to stand out, the new range features a strong box -type body, bold Pattern,  durable knobs 
and premium colour options including Onyx Black, Middle Black and Burgundy . Select models
