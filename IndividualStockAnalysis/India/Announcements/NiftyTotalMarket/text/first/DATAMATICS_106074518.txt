Datamatics Achieves UK Government -Backed Cyber 
Essentials Certifications, Enhancing Cybersecurity 
and Providing Greater Protection for Customers  
 
2nd December 2024, Mumbai : Datamatics, a global Digital Technologies, Operations, 
and Experiences Company, announced today  that it has acquired Cyber Essentials and 
Cyber Essentials Plus certifications. These industry -leading certifications, backed by the 
UK Government's  National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC), validate Datamatics'  
commitment to cybersecurity and further enhance the company's  ability to deliver 
secure, reliable services to its customers globally.  
The Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus certification schemes are UK 
Government -backed programs designed to help Enterprises  safeguard against  the 
most common cyber threats. Techforce Cyber, an independent third -party auditor, 
conducted the audit, which involved a detailed technical assessment to ensure the 
company’s defences  are robust and effective against a range o f cyber -attack scenarios.  
Key Highlights of the Certification:  
 Comprehensive Security Assessment:  The certification process involved over 
100 questions covering five key control areas: Firewalls, Secure Configuration, 
Security Update Management, User Acces s Control, and Malware Protection.  
 Global Coverage:  The certification scope includes Datamatics' network 
operations in the United Kingdom and India, ensuring cybersecurity practices 
are consistently applied across regions.  
 Increased Client Confidence:  By achieving both Cyber Essentials and Cyber 
Essentials Plus, Datamatics strengthens its position as a trusted partner 
committed to safeguarding clients’ sensitive information and maintaining 
business continuity.  
On this occasion, Rahul Kanodia, Vice Chairman and CE O of Datamatics, 
expressed,  “Achieving the Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus certifications is a 
significant milestone in our journey to uphold the highest standards of cybersecurity. 
It highlights our commitment to safeguard  customer data w hile maintaining the 
highest cybersecurity standards across our global operations. ” 
Gopal Ranjan, Global Head Quality and DPO  of Datamatics expressed,    “I am 
pleased that Datamatics has achieved the Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus  
certifications. This recognition follows a rigorous assessment by Techforce Cyber, 
evaluating more than 100 technical controls to ensure we meet stringent cybersecurity 
 
standards. It underscores our dedication to protecting client data and reinforces the 
trust of our customers to deliver secure, dependable solutions.”  
Datamatics also became an early global recipient of the ISO 42001:2023 certification, 
recognizing our commitment to implementing the Artificial Intelligence Management 
System (AIMS) framework. This  demonstrates our ongoing dedication to maintaining 
the highest quality and security standards for our customers.
