I. FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS  
In Q2FY27, the extended summer season resulted in robust demand for Room ACs and it helped the 
company to post a strong revenue growth. However, margins were under pressure across the 
businesses due to continued escalation in material cost and our inability to fully pass on the same. In 
the meanwhile, various cost optimization initiatives are beginning to show results.  
Financial highlights for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, on a consolidated basis, are 
summarized as follows:  
 Revenue from operations for Q2FY27 grew 21.0% to ₹2,930.95 cr as compared to ₹2,422.37 
cr in Q2FY26.  
 EBIDTA (excluding other income) for Q2FY27 improved to ₹202.58 cr (EBITDA margin 6.9% 
of revenue) as compared to ₹183.41 cr (EBITDA margin 7.6% of revenue) in Q2FY26.  
 PBT (before share of Profit/(Loss) of JV and exceptional items) grew marginally to ₹133.61 cr 
in Q2FY27 as compared to ₹133.16 cr in Q2FY26.  
 Tax expense for Q2FY27 was same at ₹33.39 cr as compared to Q2FY26.  
 Net profit grew 25.4% to ₹123.91 cr in Q2FY27 as compared to ₹98.78 cr in Q2FY26.  
 Carried-forward order book as of September 30, 2026, marginally grew to ₹7,229.22 cr, as 
compared to ₹7,120.44 cr as of September 30, 2025. Carried-forward order book as of March 
31, 2026, stood at ₹6,923.00 cr.  
 The capital employed as of September 30, 2026, decreased to ₹3200.36 cr as compared to 
₹3530.88 cr as of September 30, 2025.  
 Net Cash Position of ₹289.43 cr as on September 30, 2026 as compared to Net Borrowings of 
₹417.06 cr as of September 30, 2025.  
 
II. BUSINESS HIGHLIGHTS FOR Q2FY27  
Segment I: Electro-Mechanical Projects & Commercial Air Conditioning Systems  
Segment I revenue grew 16.3% to ₹1,935.99 cr in Q2FY27, as compared to ₹1664.21 cr in Q2FY26. 
Segment result was ₹133.15 cr (6.9% of revenue) in Q2FY27 as compared to ₹147.14 cr (8.8% of 
revenue) in Q2FY26.  
1. Electro-Mechanical Projects business  
During the quarter, the order inflows are mainly from Data Centers, while the enquiry inflows 
are good from Factories as well. We expect significant growth in order inflows in the coming 
months. 
The growth of billing during this quarter was sluggish as execution of a few projects 
experienced delays.

Carried-forward order book of the Electro-Mechanical Projects business was at ₹5141.78 cr as 
on September 30, 2026, as compared to ₹4,840.46 cr as on September 30, 2025, a growth of 
6.2%. 
2. Commercial Air Conditioning Systems 
The Commercial Air Conditioning business recorded strong revenue growth this quarter, driven 
by robust demand across the healthcare, education, and industrial segments.  
During the quarter, the company launched range of new generation VRFs and Ducted Systems 
and continues to maintain its market leadership in Commercial Air Conditioning Systems.  
International Business  
With growing acceptance of our heat pump solutions in the US market, the supplies continued 
despite challenging supply chain and logistics environment. 
We are pursuing development and testing of products and solutions for a few more OEMs in 
the US and Europe. We expect this business to accelerate once the tariff related uncertainties 
are resolved. 
Segment 1’s margins at 6.9% of revenue in Q2FY27 compared to 8.8% of revenue in Q2FY26, 
primarily due to material cost escalations. 
Segment II: Unitary Products  
Segment II revenue grew by 29.4% to 897.66 cr in Q2FY27 as compared to ₹693.81 cr in Q2FY26. 
Segment result was ₹20.74 cr (2.3% of revenue) in Q2FY27 as compared to ₹42.72 cr (6.2% of 
revenue) in Q2FY26.  
1. Cooling and Purification Products business  
Delayed monsoon and extended summer supported the Room AC business with strong demand, 
and we have maintained our market share. While we have been increasing the prices 
periodically, only a portion of material cost escalations could be passed on, in the process the 
margins continued to be under pressure. The company has embarked on a program to rejig the 
product portfolio with differentiated and cost optimized SKUs in order to improve the margins. 
  
2. Commercial Refrigeration business  
We are beginning to see revival in demand for the Commercial Refrigeration products. Here 
again, the inability to pass on the entire material cost escalation had kept the margins under 
pressure.

Thus, the segment margins were lower compared to previous corresponding quarter (2.3% in Q2FY27 
as compared to 6.2% in Q2FY26).  
Segment III: Professional Electronics and Industrial Systems  
Segment III revenue grew by 51.2% to ₹97.30 cr in Q2FY27 as compared to ₹64.35 cr in Q2FY26. 
Segment result was ₹23.10 cr (23.7% of revenue) in Q2FY27 as compared to ₹6.17 cr (9.6% of 
revenue) in Q2FY26.  
During this quarter, the Company sold its MedTech business for total consideration of  ₹35.65 cr. The 
transaction was completed on August 31, 2026.  
Since the Company is no longer required to render warranty obligations for MedTech business, the 
provision pertaining to the same has been written back, which resulted in improved profitability. 
Industrial solutions continued its strong growth momentum on the back of manufacturing and testing 
demand. Data Security Solutions continued with steady performance driven by robust demand from 
BFSI and large enterprises.  
 
III. BUSINESS OUTLOOK  
We continue to witness robust demand for Room ACs during the festival season and many more 
orders for Data Center MEP projects are expected to be finalised in the coming months. There is 
revival of demand for Commercial Air Conditioning and Refrigeration products. The monsoon shortfall, 
consequent drop in farm income and interest rate hike can have potential impact on demand. On the 
other hand, the conclusion of the US trade deal and possible resolution of West Asia crisis can be 
tailwinds.  
While we remain optimistic about the revenue growth momentum in H2FY27, our thrust will be on 
improving the margins through price revisions and product cost optimization.
