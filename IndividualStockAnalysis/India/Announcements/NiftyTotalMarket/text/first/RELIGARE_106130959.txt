I.A. 2371/2025 (for exemption)
1. Plaintiff shall file legible and clearer copies of exempted documents, 

compliant with practice rules, before the next date of hearing. 
2. The application for exemption is allowed, subject to the plaintiff 
granting inspection of the documents filed, as and when required to do so, or 
filing the original documents at the stage of admission/denial.  
3. The application stands disposed of.
I.A. 2373/2025 (enlargement of time for Court fee)
1. Let the requisite Court fees be paid within two working days. 
2. Application stands disposed of. 
CS(OS) 61/2025 and I.A. 2370/2025
1. Heard learned senior counsel appearing on behalf of the plaintiff. 
2. The plaint be registered as a suit. Summons of the suit and notice of 
I.A. 2370/2025 be also issued to the defendant by all permissible modes on 
filing of process fee. Dasti notice of I.A. 2370/2025 is also permitted. Let 
the requisites be filed during the course of the day. Let the affidavit of 
service be filed thereafter. 
3. The summons shall indicate that the written statements must be filed 
within thirty days from the date of receipt of the summons. The defendant 
shall also file affidavits of admission/denial of the documents filed by the 
plaintiff, failing which the written statements shall not be taken on record. 
4. The plaintiff is at liberty to file replications thereto within thirty days 
after filing of the written statements.  The replications shall be accompanied 
by affidavits of admission/denial in respect of the documents filed by the 
defendant, failing which the replications shall not be taken on record. 
5. It is made clear that any unjustified denial of documents may lead to 
an order of costs against the concerned party. 

6. Any party seeking inspection of documents may do so in accordance 
with the Delhi High Court (Original Side) Rules, 2018. 
7. At this stage, Mr. Nalin Kohli, Dr. Abhishek Manu Singhvi, Mr. 
Mahesh Jethmalani, Mr. Dayan Krishnan, Mr. Abhimanyu Bhandari, learned 
senior counsel, who appear on behalf of the Directors and Shareholders of 
defendant-company, contend that the instant civil suit is not maintainable for 
various grounds. They further contend that various proceedings were earlier 
taken up, which need to be brought to the attention of the Court. All those 
contentions are vehemently opposed by Mr. Mukul Rohatgi, learned senior 
counsel appearing on behalf of the plaintiff, who contends that they do not 
have any right to oppose the instant civil suit. 
8. All these aspects shall be considered on the next date of the hearing. 
9. In the meantime, the parties those who are represented by learned 
senior counsel or otherwise, shall be at liberty to file the appropriate 
application(s). The same shall be filed within two working days from today. 
10. Reply/Rejoinder thereto, if any, shall be placed on the digital record 
before 03.02.2025. 
11. It is made clear that the pleadings, whatsoever, required to be made by 
the parties, shall be completed in all respects by 03.02.2025 for the purposes 
of considering the question of interim relief.  
12.  The parties are further directed to keep ready their short notes ready 
along with relevant judgments [with an index of the relevant paragraph(s) of 
the decisions and the corresponding proposition of law] sought to be relied 
upon in a hard copy to be presented at the time of hearing of I.A. 2370/2025.  
13. List I.A. 2370/2025 for consideration on 04.02.2025. 
14. List CS(OS) 61/2025 before the concerned Joint Registrar for taking 

up necessary steps and completion of pleadings in accordance with extant 
rules on 17.03.2025.
