Dear Sir,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 and other applicable provisions of the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR Regulations”), 
we wish to inform you that the Company has, executed a Sh are Purchase Agreement dated October 
28, 2026 (“SPA”) with CP Senior Housing (India) LLC, USA (“CP Senior”), for the acquisition of 1,12,44,599 
(One Crore Twelve Lakh Forty -Four Thousand Five Hundred and Ninety -Nine) equity shares of face 
value INR 10/ - (Indian Rupees Ten only) each, representing the entire 50% (fifty per cent) equity stake 
held by CP Senior in Embassy Columbia Pacific ASL Private Limited (“Embassy Columbia”), a joint 
venture of the Company in which the Company presently holds the remaining 50% (fifty per cent) 
equity stake , at an agreed consideration of INR 32,65,27,241/ - (Indian Rupees Thirty -Two Crore Sixty -
Five Lakh, Twenty -Seven Thousand, Two Hundred and Forty One only), arrived at on the basis of the 
enterprise value of Embassy Columbia, to be settled in cash . 
 
Upon consummation of the proposed acquisition, which remains subject to the satisfaction of 
customary closing conditions and the occurrence of closing in terms of the SPA, Embassy Columbia 
will become a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company  and the existing joint venture arrangement 
in relation to Embassy Columbia stand terminated . The acquisition is intended to consolidate the 
Company’s interest in Embassy Columbia and strengthen its control over the entity and operational 
integration.
