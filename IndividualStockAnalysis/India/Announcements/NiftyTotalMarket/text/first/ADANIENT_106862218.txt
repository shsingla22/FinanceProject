Adani Wind Begins Production of India's Longest Wind Turbine Blade; 
5 MW Platform Certified for Serial Production 
Editor Synopsis  
 Adani New Industries Limited (ANIL) commence s production of India's longest 91.2-metre 
onshore wind turbine blades at its integrated manufacturing facility in Mundra, Gujarat. 
 The made-in-India blades are designed for Adan i Wind’s MWL185-5.0 MW turbine, with a 185-
metre rotor diameter and 140-metre hub height, enabling more consiste nt generation across 
varying wind speeds and supporting an improved capacity utilization. 
 WindGuard Certification GmbH, an  independent German wind turb ine testing and certification 
body, certifies Adani Wind's 5 MW plat form, enabling serial production.  
 The twin milestones strengthen India's domestic wind manufacturing capabilities and advance 
the development of next-generat ion wind energy technology. 
 
Ahmedabad, November 16, 2026:  Adani New Industries Limited (A NIL), a wholly owned subsidiary 
of Adani Enterprises Limited (AEL ) has commenced production of 91 .2-metre onshore wind turbine 
blades at its integrated manufacturing facility in Mundra, Gujarat. The company has also secured 
certification for its MWL185-5.0 MW  wind turbine platform, clearing  the way for serial production. 
The 91.2-metre blades are the long est wind turbine blades  manufactured in India. Developed for the 
MWL 185-5.0 MW platform, they will be used on a turbine featuring a 185-metre rotor diameter, the 
largest in India, and a hub height of 140 metres . The rotor sweeps nearly 26,600 square metres, 
equivalent to more than three and a half standard football pitches. The MWL185-5.0 MW platform harnesses varying wind speeds, th us delivering a more consistent generation profile, better 
utilization of available wind resources, and an improved overall capacity utilization factor (CUF). 
The new blade extends the range of products manufa ctured at Mundra, where the facility currently 
produces blades measuring 78.6 metres and 80.5  metres. Scaling to 91.2 metres represents a 
substantial progress in blade desi gn, materials engineering, manufa cturing precision and handling 
capability, strengthening India’s ca pacity to manufacture larger wi nd turbine components for next-
generation wind turbines. 
Mr Milind Kulkarni, Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Adani Wind,  said: 
“Producing India's longest 
wind turbine blade and securing certification for our 5 MW platform mark an important milestone 
for Adani Wind and the country’s wind manufacturing sector. Larger blades and higher-capacity 
turbines enable each machine to capture more energy, including at sites with lower wind speeds. 
This helps in broadening the geography availa ble for wind generation. We are committed to 
developing and manufacturing advanced wind technology in India to support the country's 
growing clean energy needs.”  
 
Expanding India's wind energy potential  
A turbine's energy output depends on its rotor sw ept area, rated capacity, hub height and the wind 
resource at the site. The MWL185-5.0 MW platform, with rotor diameter of approximately 185 
metres and a swept area of nearly 26,600 square metres  is designed to improve energy capture at 
low- and moderate-wind locations, potentially making a wider range of sites technically and commercially viable for wind power projects.  
   
WindGuard Certification GmbH, an independent German wind turbine testing and certification body, 
has certified Adani Wind's MWL185-5.0 MW platform  following prototype test ing at its installation 
site in Mundra, Gujarat. The MWL185-5.0 is buil t in partnership with W2E (Wind to Energy), our 
German Technology partn er. The certification confirms co nformity with the internationally 
recognised IEC 61400 series of standards for wind turbine design, te sting and manufacturing.  
Adani Wind has three turbine plat forms enlisted under the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy's 
(MNRE) Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM-Wind). It is also the only Indian wind 
turbine manufacturer featured in BloombergNEF's Global Top 15 turbine manufacturers ranking for 
2025. 
Its MWL 160-5.2 MW platform, one of India's highest-capacity onshore wind turbines listed under 
ALMM-Wind, has crossed 2 GW of installations at Khav da, Gujarat, home to the world's largest 
renewable energy plant.  
 
With recent orders, Adani Wind's cumulative orders from independent power producers (IPPs) 
outside the Adani Group now exceed 1 GW.
