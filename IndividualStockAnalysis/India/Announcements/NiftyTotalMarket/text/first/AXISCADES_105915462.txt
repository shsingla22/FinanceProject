AXISCADE S WELCOMES INDUSTRY VETERAN TANMOY 
CHAKRABARTY TO THE BOARD AS NON -EXECUTIVE 
INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR  
 
Bengaluru , 16th July 2024:  AXISCADES Technologies Ltd ., a leading technology and 
engineering solutions company, has announced the appointment of Mr. Tanmoy Chakrabarty 
as an Additional Director in the category of Non -Executive Independent Director o n the Board 
of the Company  with effect from 13th July 2024 . With an illustrious career spanning over four 
decades , Mr. Chakr abarty brings to the table a wealth of experience in corporate strategy, 
government affairs, and digital transformation .  
Mr. Chakrabarty has previously played an instrumental role in several high-profile projects for 
the Government of India , including implementation of  MCA 21, and the Passport Seva 
Program s. His influence also extends internationally, having led digital tax initiatives across 
East Africa and significantly contributing to eGo vernance programs in the UK and the US.  He 
also r epresents several disruptive &  innovative technology solutions in the automotive sector .  
Mr. Chakrabarty commenced his career in 1981 in Kolkata, with notable roles at esteemed 
organizations such as Eurek a Forbes Ltd., Bradma of India Ltd., Siemens Nixdorf, Electronic 
Data Systems, and Tata Consultancy Services (TCS). He served as the Group Government 
Affairs Officer at Tata Sons until October 2022, where he was overseeing strategic 
government interactions  for the Tata Group. He founded Chakrabarty Consulting Services, 
which focuses on collaborating with small and medium enterprises and international 
companies to enhance government interactions and promote ease of doing business in India. 
Currently, he is i nvolved in several advisory roles, including partnerships with organizations 
like Byteedge, eTrans Solutions , Precision Biometrics and Aabo Private Limited . 
Commenting on his appointment at AXISCADES, Mr. Chakrabarty said  “I look forward to 
contributing to  the long -term mission and advancement of AXISCADES Technologies with my 
insights on the challenges and opportunities faced by modern organizations in the public and 
private sectors. The Company’s commitment to excellence  and domains of business align wel l 
with my professional  skills and  values, and I am eager to collaborate with the talented team 
here. I believe there are significant opportunities for our mutual growth and development, 
especially in enhancing government and industry partnerships. Together , we can drive 
impactful initiatives that benefit our clients and contribute to the broader goals of modernizing 
India’s technological landscape.”  
Mr. Arun Krishnamurthi, the CEO and MD of AXISCADES, added “Mr. Chakrabarty’s 
extensive background in government relations and his proven track record in driving 
transformative projects will significantly enhance our strategic direction. His leadership will be 
instrumental as we continue to innovate and expand our  offerings and operations. His deep 
understanding of complex systems and his ability to navigate multifaceted regulatory 
environments will be invaluable as we pursue our goals in diversified verticals such as 
aerospace, defense, and heavy engineering. His insights will guide our efforts to build 
collaboration with government entities and ensure that our innovations align with 
requirements .”
