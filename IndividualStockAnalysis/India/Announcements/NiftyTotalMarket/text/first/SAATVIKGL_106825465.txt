Saatvik Green Energy Strengthens Product Portfolio by launching High- Eﬃciency Quad -Cut 
TOPCon 3.0 Dual Glass Bifacial Module at REI Expo 2026  
• New module built on advanced N -type TOPCon 3.0 cell technology with bifacial double -glass 
construction; output reaches up to 745 W under bifacial rating conditions  
• Industry -leading 30 -year linear power warranty with only 0.35% annual degradation, backed 
by Munich RE insurance support  
• Launch marks Saatvik’s decisive move into next -generation, high- wattage solar modules 
designed for India’s utility -scale and commercial solar market  
New Delhi, 22 October 2026: Strengthening its focus on next -generation solar technology, Saatvik 
Green Energy Limited, one of India’s leading integrated solar manufacturers, today unveiled its Quad-
Cut TOPCon 3.0 Dual Glass Bifacial Solar PV Module  at the Renewable Energy India (REI) Expo 2026 
in Greater Noida. The module was inaugurated by Mr Mohammad Rihan, Director General, National 
Institute of Solar Energy (NISE) , in the presence of Mr Neelesh Garg, Chairman and Managing 
Director; Mr Manik Garg, Managing Director; Mr Prashant Mathur, Chief Executive Oﬃcer; and Mr 
Pushpendra Samadhiya, Chief Revenue Oﬃcer , of Saatvik Green Energy Limited.  
The new module delivers a power output of up to 670 Wp and a maximum module eﬃciency of 24.8%, built on N -type TOPCon 3.0 cell architecture in a bifacial double- glass construction. With bifacial gain, 
output can reach up to 745 W under standard bifacial rating conditions (BNPI). The product launch 
represents Saatvik’s most signiﬁcant technology statement to date, a clear commitment to next -
generation solar manufacturing at a time when India’s market is rapidly shifting toward higher -wattage, 
higher -eﬃc iency modules.  
Commenting on the launch,  Mr. Prashant Mathur, Chief Executive Oﬃcer, Saatvik Green Energy 
Limited, said: “As India’s solar sector scales up, the focus is shifting towards higher -eﬃciency 
modules that can improve project economics while delivering reliable, long -term performance. Our 
Quad -Cut TOPCon 3.0 Dual Glass Bifacial Module reﬂects Saatvik’s commitmen t to advancing solar 
technology through products that combine higher power output, optimised system costs and long -
term performance assurance. Backed by advanced N -type TOPCon 3.0 technology and a robust 
warranty structure, the module is designed to addres s the evolving needs of India’s utility -scale and 
commercial solar markets. This launch marks another step in strengthening our technology 
capabilities and contributing to the next phase of India’s solar manufacturing journey.”  
The module features a quad -cut (quarter -cut) cell design comprising 264 cells in a 6 × 44 conﬁguration. 
This design reduces current loss per cell, helping lower resistive losses, minimise hot -spot risk and 
extend module life. With an output of up to 670 W p, the module enables developers to deploy more 
capacity with fewer modules, helping reduce balance -of-system costs associated with mounting 
structures, cabling, land use and installation eﬀort per megawatt.  

A temperature coeﬃcient of Pmax of - 0.26%/°C enables the module to retain more of its rated output 
at higher temperatures, oﬀering a material advantage under Indian ﬁeld conditions.  
The module is backed by a 12 -year product warranty and a 30 -year linear power warranty, with ﬁrst -
year degradation of only 1% and annual degradation of 0.35% thereafter. At the end of 30 years, the 
module is warranted to deliver at least 88.85% of its rat ed power. The warranty is supported by Munich 
Re insurance. The module is manufactured under ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001 and SA 8000 management systems.  
About Saatvik Green Energy Limited  
Saatvik Green Energy Limited is one of India’s leading solar photovoltaic module manufacturers, with 
a strong presence across utility -scale, commercial & industrial, EPC, and distributed solar segments. 
The Company currently operates a 4.86 GW module manuf acturing facility in Ambala, Haryana, and is 
developing a Greenﬁeld integrated manufacturing facility in Odisha with 4 GW of module capacity and

play a  meaningful role in India’s renewable energy transition.
