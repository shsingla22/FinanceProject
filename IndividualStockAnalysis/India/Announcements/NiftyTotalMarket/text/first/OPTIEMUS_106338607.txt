Press Release  
 
Optiemus launches Rhino Tech – Made -in-India Tempered Glass Screen 
Protectors with Glass Engineered by Corning ™  
 
Optiemus eyes revenue of INR 1800 -2000 Cr. by end of FY27 (March 2027)  
 
New Delhi, August 13th, 2025: In a pathbreaking move to redefine mobile tempered glass screen 
protector  experience  for Indian customers, Optiemus Infracom Limited , a leader in India’s 
electronics manufacturing sector, today launched India’s first premium tempered glass screen 
protectors’ brand ‘RhinoTech ’ – a Made -in-India  product  which uses glass  Engineered by 
Corning ™. With the launch  of RhinoTech  products, Optiemus is looking a t a revenue of INR 1800 -
2000 crore  by end of  FY27 (March 2027).  
 
Rhino Tech brings global technology and innovation to Indian consumers, delivering world -class 
durability, unmatched clarity and enhanced hygienic benefits with anti -microbial glass. It is the 
first time in India that such advanced screen protection, developed with glass  from Corning 
Incorporated, will be produced locally and made available for both domestic and inter national 
markets.   
 
Reinforcing the spirit of ‘Make in India’ for world -class mobile accessories, RhinoTech  screen 
protectors will be manufactured at Optiemus ’ facility  in Noida, Uttar Pradesh. Our state -of-the-
art manufacturing plant enables RhinoTech  to reliably meet the growing demand of Indian 
consumers while maintaining consistent quality and widespread availability across the nation.  
RhinoTech ’s screen protectors are designed for the unique needs of Indian consumers, combining 
technology created in Corning’s labs with Optiemus’ trusted manufacturing expertise.  
 
Key Features  – First Time in India:   
• ‘Engineered by Corning ™’: A specialized line of screen protectors utilizing technology created 
in Corning’s labs and that is designed for the ultimate screen protector.    
• Anti -microbial  Glass:  For safer, more hygienic device use.  
 
Key Features – First Time in the World:  
• Unlimited replacement up to 1 year:  A first for consumers, ensuring worry -free device 
protection.  
• Fog marked for authenticity  of product

Addressing an Unorganized Market  
The Indian domestic market for tempered glass screen protectors is estimated  to be  400 million  
pieces with a retail value of INR 20,000 crore in 2025, highlighting its vast p otential. Currently, 
over 90%  demand is met through low -cost imports, leading to inconsistent quality and an 
unorganized market. To address this, the government has introduced the world’s first Indian 
standard for “Chemically Tempered Glass Screen Protectors” via the Bureau of Indian Standards 
(BIS)  on 28 July 2025 . This milestone will  ensure high -quality, standardized products, boosting 
consumer confidence in mobile screen protection.
