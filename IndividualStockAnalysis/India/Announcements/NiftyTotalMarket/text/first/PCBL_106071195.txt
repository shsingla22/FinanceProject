1GOVERNMENT OF ANDHRA PRADESH
ABSTRACT
Industries & Commerce Department – Proposal of M/s. Philips Carbon Black 
Limited for establishing rubber black and value-added chemicals 
manufacturing unit - Consideration of the request for allotment of 116.62 
acres of lands – Orders - Issued.

INDUSTRIES & COMMERCE (P&I) DEPARTMENT
G.O.MS.No. 78 Dated: 26-11-2024
                                                                            Read the following:
1. M/s.Philips Carbon Black Limited submitted their proposal to 
VC&MD, APIIC, Dt:20.09.24.
2. Minutes of the State Investment Promotion Committee meeting 
held on 16.11.2024.
3. Minutes of the State Investment Promotion Board meeting held on 
19.11.2024.
***
ORDER:
In the 1st reference read above, M/s.Philips Carbon Black Limited 
(PCBL) have proposed to set up plant for manufacture of New Rubber Black 
& Value-added chemicals at MPSEZ, Naidupeta with an investment of 
Rs.3,718 Crores and generate direct employment to 200 people and 
requested to allotment of 116.62 acres at a cost of Rs.1,948/- per sqm.
2. Accordingly, the proposal was placed before the State Investment 
Promotion Board (SIPB) meeting held on 19.11.2024 with recommendations 
of State Investment Promotion Committee (SIPC) in its meeting held on 
16.11.2024. The SIPB after detailed discussion, has recommended to place 
their recommendations for allotment of 116.62 Ac of lands at a cost of INR 
1,948 per Sqm subject to de-notification of the Naidupeta SEZ.
3. Government, after careful consideration of the decision of the State 
Investment Promotion Board, hereby accord approval for allotment of 
116.62 Ac at cost of INR 1,948 per Sqm subject to de-notification of the 
Naidupeta SEZ in favor of M/s.Philips Carbon Black Limited (PCBL) for 
setting up plant for manufacture of New Rubber Black & Value-added 
chemicals at MPSEZ, Naidupeta. The allocation of land shall be made 
applicable subject to company adhering to the definite time lines for 
implementation of the project and investment and employment commitment 
from the company as per the proposal submitted and subject to other 
conditions, as applicable, as per the allotment regulations of APIIC.
24. The Special Chief Secretary/Principal Secretary/Secretary of the 
concerned Departments such as Power, Water Resources Department, 
Industry and Commerce (CIP), Skill Development Department, Revenue 
(Land, Registration and Stamps), Finance Department and other 
departments concerned are requested to instruct their HODs and PSUs under 
them for expeditious grounding and implementation of this large project.
5. The Vice Chairman & Managing Director, APIIC Ltd., Mangalagiri and 
the Director of Industries, Andhra Pradesh shall take further necessary 
action accordingly in coordination with the line departments.
6. As envisaged under the Andhra Pradesh Industrial Development Policy 
(4.0) 2024-29, the Director of Industries shall assign the handholding for 
facilitations of all the inputs as required for expeditious grounding and 
implementation of this large project to a senior level officer and should try to 
ensure that the project is implemented as per the expected timelines, duly 
facilitating the required approvals and clearances under the Single Desk 
Portal 2.0 and duly implementing the project as per the intended spirit of the 
Speed of Doing Business.
7. The Director of Industries should ensure that the PMU with the 
Department monitors the progress of the mega project, duly bringing the 
issues that are to be resolved, to the respective line departments, from time 
to time and resolve the issues and intimate the status of the progress of the 
project to the Government from time to time and bring the issues for 
resolution at the government end, if any, for smooth execution of the 
project.
 (BY ORDER AND IN THE NAME OF THE GOVERNOR OF ANDHRA PRADESH)
