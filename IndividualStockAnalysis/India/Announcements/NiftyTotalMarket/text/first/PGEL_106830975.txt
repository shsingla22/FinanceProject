Advancing with Discipline and Confidence  
New Delhi, India – October  27, 202 6: PG Electroplast Ltd. (PGEL), one of India’s pioneers and leading 
players in Electronic Manufacturing Services (EMS) and Plastic Molding, announced its unaudited financial 
results for the quarter ended Sept.  30, 202 6, as approved by its Board of Directors.  
 
 
Quarter ended Sep 30th, 2026 
• Revenues  stood at INR 933.88 crores , growing 4 2.5% YoY  
• EBITDA  stood at INR 69.14 crores  vs. INR 44.68 crores in 2QFY2 6 – up 54 .8% YoY 
• Net Income  for the quarter stood at INR 13.67 crores , vs. INR 2.76 crores in 2QFY2 6 
Half-year ended Sep. 30, 202 6 
• Net Sales for the period were INR 2967.83 crores – growth of 37.4% YoY.  
• EBITDA stood at INR 255.3 7 crores versus INR 184.10 crores in 1HFY202 6 .  
• Net Income stood at INR 89.88 crores versus INR 69.74 crores in 1HFY202 6. 
Other Highlights  
• 1HFY27 has been a strong growth period as consolidated Half-yearly revenues crossed INR 2, 950 
crores for the first time  in the company's history.  
• Our 100% subsidiary, PG Technoplast, reported INR 2,130.5 crores  in revenue. The order book 
remains healthy across all product categories.  “During the quarter, we continued to make progress on our capex plans for new manufacturing 
facilities and backward integration. These initiatives form an important part of our long -term 
strategy to expand capacity, strengthen operational capabilities and b uild greater control over 
our supply chain. Our focus remains on disciplined execution, prudent capital allocation and 
aligning investments with long -term business priorities.  
The industry witnessed steady growth across product lines, providing a supportive backdrop 
for our expansion plans. While we remain mindful of evolving market conditions, this broad -
based progress reinforces our measured confidence in the opportunities ahe ad. 
Our priority is clear: translate strategic investments into stronger capabilities, greater 
competitiveness and sustainable growth. With discipline guiding our execution and confidence 
shaping our outlook, we are building not just for the coming quarters, b ut for enduring value 
creation .” 
— Vishal  Gupta, Managing Director – Finance  
  
• Product business contributed 70.3% of total revenues in 1 HFY27, growing 41.1% YoY , with the AC 
business growing 3 6.2% YoY to INR 1, 561.1 crores , the Washing Machines business growing 
60.7% to INR 504.8 crores , and Coolers growing 4.8% YoY to INR 1 9.2 crores . 
• The Electronics business grew 90.3% YoY  and contributed 12.1% of total revenues  in 1HFY27 . 
Plastic Moulding and components contributed INR 521.6 crores  and grew 7.5% YoY  in 
1HFY27 .  
• Our 50:50 JV, Goodworth Electronics , posted revenues of INR 623.8 crores  in 1HFY27 vs. INR 
483.4  crores  in 1HFY26, with EBITDA of INR 22.7 crores  vs. INR 10.3 crores YoY.  
• All our Expansion project are tracking their respective completion timelines and we hope to start 
commercial production from two new facilities in 4QFY27. PGEL’s flagship Washing Machine 
manufacturing facility  has come online in a new campus in DMIC, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh . 
A state -of-the-art plant, it will have the capacity to manufacture 1.8mn washing machines per 
annum . Also a new plastic component & Coolers facility at Salarpur , Bhiwadi is operational and 
will start contributing from 3QFY2027.  
• Gross contribution as a percentage softened YoY  due to elevated commodity prices . Since 
product pricing in the industry is typically structured on a per -unit rupee margin basis , rising 
input costs mechanically lower the margin percentage  even as per-unit economics stay 
stable . Raw material cost increases have been fully passed through to customers . 
• Our focus on controlling expenses  in FY27 is already bearing fruit , and we remain committed 
to building long -term resilience  and enhancing capital efficiency . 
• Balance sheet remains healthy and liquid and company has over 422 crores of cash and cash 
equivalents at end of 1HFY27. Advances for all the capex is completed and most of the working 
capital requirements are also tied up for 2HFY2027.  
• Strategic priorities include R&D, new product development, backward integration, and 
capability enhancement .  
The company plans to continue investing in developing capabilities and capacities in existing 
and adjacent product segments to support future growth.  
Future Outlook  
The management sees increased opportunities from both existing and new clients. With enhanced 
capacities and technological capabilities, PGEL is well -positioned in India’s consumer durables and 
plastics ecosystem.  
In the coming years, the company aims to:  
• Achieve industry -leading revenue growth  
• Drive gradual  margin expansion  through operational efficiencies  and operational leverage  
• Maintain best-in-class capital efficiency  through improved cash flows  and balance sheet 
optimization  
Several capacity projects initiated over the past year are progressing toward commercialization and are 
expected to come online through FY27, including:  
  
• The refrigerator campus  in Sri City, Andhra Pradesh  
• The state of the art  room air conditioner compressor manufacturing plant  in Supa , Maharashtra  
• The facility for plastic components, tooling, and coolers  in Salarpur, Rajasthan

About PG Electroplast Limited (PGEL) (BSE: 533581; NSE: PGEL)  
 
PG Electroplast is a trusted one -stop solution provider for Electronic Manufacturing  
Services  (EMS)  and contract  manufacturing  to most  leading  consumer durable and 
electronics brands in India. The company has one of the biggest capacities  in Plastic  
Injection moulding  and has capabilities across  the value chain in Original Equipment 
Manufacturing (OEM) and Original Design Manufacturing (ODM) products like Washing 
Machines, Room ACs, Air -Coolers and LED TVs.
