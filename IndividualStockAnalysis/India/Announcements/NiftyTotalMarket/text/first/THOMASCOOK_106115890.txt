Thomas Cook India taps into Kolkata's high-potential market  
Opens outlet in Mangoe Lane – Dalhousie  
Increases consumer access to 9  locations in the city  
 
 Experiential travel is seeing strong demand from Kolkata  and the state of West Bengal  
 Growth drivers include exclusive  Bengali group tours – accompanied by Bengali  speaking, experienced 
Tour Manager ; local cuisine on tour  
 
Mumbai,  January 16, 2025:  With a strategic intent to capitalise on the strong and growing demand from 
Kolkata, Thomas Cook (India) Lim ited, India’s leading omnichannel services company, has 
inaugurated a new outlet  in the high potential market of Mangoe Lane- Dalhousie Square and its 
surrounding catchments. In addition to the customers from the city of Kolkata, the new branch will also 
service customers from the surrounding catchments of  Central Kolkata. The new outlet expands 
Thomas Cook India’s network to 9  locations in the city and state.  
 
In its commitment to enhance access and convenience,  Thomas Cook’s new outlet will address 
diverse customer segments - families, couples, Young India’s , millennials & Gen Z, business 
professionals  and seniors. Thomas Cook’s Dalhousie outlet offers end- to-end travel services: 
International & Domestic Holidays (Group Tours, Personalised Holidays, Cruises , etc.), Value Added 
Services like Travel Insurance, etc . Additionally, Thomas Cook’s AI booking platform assist s 
customers in planning their personalized vacations in real time by checking availability, and making 
reservations in a matter of minutes . 
 
Dalhousie’s position as an important market offers a range of valuable consumer segments, including business professionals, government employees, families, couples, Young India’s millennials and Gen 
Z, and seniors alike. Thomas Cook’s new outlet offers end -to-end travel services: International and 
domestic Holidays (Group Tours, Personalised holidays, Cruises, etc.), and Value- Added Services like 
Travel Insurance .
