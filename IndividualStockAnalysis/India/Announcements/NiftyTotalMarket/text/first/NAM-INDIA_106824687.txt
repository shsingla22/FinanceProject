(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.The Board of Directors at its meeting held on October 22, 2026 have approved an interim dividend of ₹ 9.50/- per equity share.
During the financial year 2024-25, the Company received a show cause notice dated August 30, 2024 from the Securities and Exchange Board of
India (SEBI) alleging non-compliance with certain provisions of securiti es laws in relation to investments made by certain schemes of Nippon India
Mutual Fund in AT-1 bonds issued by Yes Bank Limited. The Company and certain of its officials named in the show cause notice filed settlement
applications under the SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2 018, without admitting or denying  the findings of fact and c onclusions of law.
During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, SEBI communicated its in-principle acceptance of the settlement terms. Pursuant thereto, the
Company remitted ₹7.68 Crore towards settlement amounts in respect of the Company and the said officials and ₹89.08 Crore towards
management fees earned on the said investments, together with applic able interest thereon. The aggregate amount has been recognised as an
exceptional item in the standalone financial results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026. Further communication from SEBI is
awaited and based on the current assessment, no further provision is considered necessary.
The Hon’ble Supreme Court on October 15, 2026 dismissed the appeals filed against the judgment of the Hon’ble Bombay High Court dated January
20, 2023, which had set aside the write-off of the Additional Tier-1 bonds of Yes Bank Limited under taken in March 2020. In th ese proceedings, the
interests of the bondholders, including the relevant schemes of Nippon India M utual Fund, were represented by Axis Trusteeship Services Limited,
the debenture trustee for the said bonds. Any recovery pursuant to the judgme nt will accrue to the unitholders of the relevant schemes and will not
have any impact on the financial results of the Company.
The Company is in the business of providing asset management services to the schemes of Nippon India Mutual Fund, funds launched in GIFT city,
portfolio management service and advisory service to the clients / scheme s. The primary segment is identified as asset management services. As
such, the Company's financial results are largely reflective of the asset management business and accordingly there are no separate reportable
segments as per Ind AS 108 Operating Segment.
