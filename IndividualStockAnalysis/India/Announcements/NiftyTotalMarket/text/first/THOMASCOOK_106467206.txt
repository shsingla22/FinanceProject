Thomas Cook India strengthens footprint in Madhya Pradesh  
Opens third outlet in Indore ’s to high -potential market  
Increases consumer access to 5  locations in the State  
 
Mumbai,  December 12, 2025 : With a strategic intent to capitalise on the strong and growing demand from Madhya 
Pradesh , Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel services company, has inaugurated a new 
outlet  in the high potential market of Indore. The new outlet expands Thomas Cook India’s network t o 3 locations 
in the  city and 5 locations in the State . The outlet was inaugurated by Dr. Upinder Dhar, Former Vice Chancellor, 
Shri Vaishnav Vidyapeeth University,  Mr. Pawan Bhalika, Vice Pres ident, Maheshwari Samaj Indore and Mr. 
Romil Pant, Executive Vice President & Head –  Holidays, Thomas Cook India Limited.  
 
The new Thomas Cook outlet will  cater to travellers from Indore and its neighbouring regions  across diverse 
customer segments - families, couples, Young India’s mille nnials & GenZ, business travellers  and GenS 
(seniors ). Thomas Cook’s Indore outlet offers end- to-end travel services: International & Domestic Holidays 
(Group Tours, Personalised H olidays, Cruises, etc.), Value Added Services like Travel Insurance, etc . 
Additionally, Thomas Cook’s AI booking platform assist s customers in planning their personalized vacations in 
real time  by checking availability  and making reservations in a matter of minutes .
