Firstsource earns ‘A’ in CDP Supplier Engagement Assessment ;  
Secures ‘B’ rating in CDP Climate and Water Security Disclosures for FY25  
 
Mumbai, India | April  09, 2026: Firstsource Solutions Limited  (NSE:FSL, BSE:532809),  an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company,  has 
earned an ‘A’ rating  in CDP’s Supplier Engagement Assessment (SEA),  and ‘B’ rating in CDP Climate  Disclosure  and Water Security  
Disclosure for FY25 , marking further and notable  progress in its environmental performance and supplier engagement .  
 
The ‘A’ rating in the Supplier Engagement Assessment places Firstsource among leading organizations globally that are activel y 
engaging suppliers on climate -related priorities, including governance, emissions targets, Scope 3 disclosures, and value chain 
collaboration.   
 
Dr. Sanjiv Goenka, Chairman of RPSG Group and Firstsource , shared,  “Sustainability, at its core, is a question of institutional 
character. The recognition we have received from CDP — across supplier engagement, climate, and water security — tells me that 
Firstsource is building the right kind of company: one where accounta bility runs through the business, not around it.  What I find 
particularly meaningful is that these ratings assess not just what we do, but how we govern, how we engage our supply chain, and 
how we steward the natural resources our business depends on. That is the full picture of responsible enterprise. O ur commitment 
to Net Zero 2050 is not a target we are managing towards; it reflects  the values we are choosing to operate by, today . And it is 
that choice — made consistently, across every level of the organization — that I believe define s Firstsource's st anding as a long -
term, responsible business."  
 
The company’s FY25 results build on its FY24 performance, when it received a ‘B’ rating in CDP Climate Disclosure and an ‘A’ rating 
in Supplier Engagement Assessment. In FY25, Firstsource maintained these ratings, while adding Water Security to its CDP 
disclosure for the first time, with a ‘B’ rating.  
 
Firstsource has committed to achieving Net Zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2050, with targets aligned to the Science Based 
Targets initiative (SBTi) and currently undergoing validation. Over 26% of its total power consumption now comes from renewable 
sources, reflecting increased adoption of clean energy.  
 
Firstsource provides full disclosure of its Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 emissions, with greenhouse gas data externally assu red. 
Across its value chain, around 85% of supplier spend is assessed on ESG criteria, and the company is targeting 50% conversion  of 
its fleet to electric vehicles by 2027.  
 
Together, these results signal a maturing sustainability approach, with increasing emphasis on execution, transparency, and v alue 
chain impact as Firstsource advances towards its Net Zero 2050 commitment.
