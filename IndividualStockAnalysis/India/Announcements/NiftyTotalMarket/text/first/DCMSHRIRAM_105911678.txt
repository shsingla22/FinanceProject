 Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
This is in furtherance to our earlier intimation dated March 05, 2024 under Regulation 30 of SEBI 
LODR Regulations, regarding three electricity duty orders, inter -alia communicating penalty 
levied by Joint Commissioner, State Tax, Commercial Taxes Department  on the Company 
amounting to Rs.0.03 lakhs, besides electricity duty &  interest  aggregating to Rs.1,356.38 lakhs. 
(copy encl osed). The said authority ha d also  stated that electricity duty is leviable on all 
consumption of electricity by the captive power plants, even if it is auxiliary energy consumption.  
Accordingly, t he Company had filed an appeal / stay applicable against th ese demands / Duty 
Orders.  
  
Now , the Government o f Rajasthan , vide its Notification  dated July 10,  2024,  has allowed rebate of 
electricity duty and waived interest and penalty, payable on auxiliary energy consumption of self -
generated energy used exclusively for generation of energy in captive power plants , provided that 
the party  deposit s 10 % of past demand  of the Principal amount outstanding since 2014- 15 to 
2024- 25 (upto the date of notification).
