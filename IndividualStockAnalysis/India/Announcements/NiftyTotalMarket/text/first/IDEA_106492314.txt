Subject: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Update on AGR matter  
 
Ref: Vodafone Idea Limited (Symbol –  IDEA, Scrip code -  532822)  
 
This is further to our communication dated 31 December 2025 in relation to the AGR matter.  
 
We wish to inform that we have received a communication from Department of Telecommunication, 
which provides the following relief to the Company in the AGR matter:  
 
1. The AGR  dues (Principal + Interest + Penalty and Interest on Penalty) of the Company (for the 
period from FY 2006- 07 to FY 2018- 19) as of 31.12.2025 shall be frozen and shall be payable as 
follows:   
 
(a) Maximum Rs. 124 crore to be paid annually over next 6 years i.e. March’ 2026 to March’ 2031;  
(b) Rs. 100 crore to be paid annually over 4 years i.e. March’ 2032 to March’ 2035;  
(c) The remaining AGR dues, has to be paid in equal installments annually over 6 years, i.e. 
March’ 2036 to March’ 2041.  
 
2. A committee is to be cons tituted by DoT to reassess the AGR dues, whose decision shall be final. 
Thereafter, the reassessed amount is to be repaid between March’ 2036 to March’ 2041 in equal 
annual installments.  
 
The above is for your information and dissemination.
