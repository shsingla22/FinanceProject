Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we are pleased to inform you that DBL-

City Centre and Cyber City (26.65 Km) and Spur from Basai Village to Dwarka Expressway (1.85 Km) with total 27 stations. ”,
