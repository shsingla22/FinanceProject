HF Deluxe – Flex Fuel Motorcycle  
Hero MotoCorp has showcased  its first flex -fuel motorcycle, the ethanol -based Hero HF 
Deluxe, in alignment with the Government of Ind ia’s vision to promote green and renewable 
fuel technologies. This flex -fuel prototype has been developed at the company’s state -of-the-
art R&D hub, the Centre for Innovation and Technology (CIT) in Jaipur. Powered by a 100cc 
BS6 engine, the vehicle is designed to operate on ethanol -blended gasoline with ethanol 
content ranging from 20% (E20) to 85% (E85).  
 
VIDA V2  
Further strengthening its commitment to make VIDA a spirational, accessible, and affordable, 
the company showcased the VIDA V2 e -scooter at the pavilion. Launched in December 2024, 
VIDA V2 is available at a starting price of Rs. 96,000 /-. Powered by VIDA’s advanced 
removable battery technology, the VIDA V2 ensures easy charging and an impressive IDC 
range of up to 165 km per charge. Additionally, the swingarm -mounted permanent magnet 
synchronous motor with 6kW of peak power and 25Nm torque makes every journey 
engaging.   
   
SURGE S32 – CHAMPIONING INNOVATION  
Moving ahead with the evolving technological landscape and changing customer behaviors, 
Surge, the in -house startup of Hero MotoCorp, has showcased  the Surge S32, a first -of-its-
kind class -convertible vehicle . Earning various accolades across the globe, th e Surge S32 
acclaimed  as one of TIME Magazine's  Best Innovations of 2024. It was also  honored with the 
prestigious Red Dot: Best of the Best Award and secured Platinum at the A' Design Awards, 
reinforcing its place as a trailblazer in design and innovation.

Xtreme 250R  
Hero MotoCorp made a powerful debut in the 250cc segment with the launch of the Xtreme 
250R. The Xtreme 250R is inspired from the Xtunt 2.5R concept and elevates it with a focus 
on performance and handling. Powered by a 250cc liquid -cooled, 4 -valve DOHC engine tuned 
for all out performance, producing 30 PS @ 9,250 rpm  of peak power and 25 Nm @ 7250 
rpm  of peak torque, making it the fastest 250cc motorcycle in its cla ss. The motorcycle's 
rigid trellis frame and 43mm USD dual cartridge  front forks ensure precise and agile 
handling.  
 
Rider safety is paramount with features like dual -channel ABS, rear -wheel lift -off 
protection , and Emergency Brake Alert . The Xtreme 250R also boasts first -in-segment 
features such as Intelligent Automatic Illumination and Hero Connect 2.0 . With over 35 
connectivity features , including turn -by-turn navigation and music control, riders stay 
connected and informed on every jo urney.  
  
Xpulse 210  
Crafted & Tested by Hero Motosports riders, the Xpulse 210 is designed to empower the 
spirit of adventure. The powerful 210cc DOHC liquid -cooled engine generates 24.6 P S @ 
9250  rpm and 20.7 Nm @  7250  rpm of torque. The class -leading sus pension setup , with 
210mm front and 205mm rear travel and a generous 220mm ground clearance , ensures 
superior performance across varied terrains. The motorcycle ’s versatile ergonomics, 
comfortable seating with a wide and flat profile enhances the comfort d uring daily commutes 
as well as off -road adventures. The 6-speed gearbox with a slipper and assist clutch  
ensures higher cruising speeds and smooth gear shifts.  
  
The Xpulse 210 embraces minimalistic yet purposeful design elements such as front and rear 
shrouds for enhanced rider support, while distinctive features like the rugged front grab bar 
and upswept exhaust exude adventure readiness. Adding to its visual appeal, the Xpulse 210 
features a Class -D LED projector headlamp  and signature H -shaped DRLs for  superior 
visibility. The compact H -shaped LED tail lamp further enhances its distinctive look. The 
Xpulse 210 is equipped with a sophisticated 4.2" TFT display  in the top variant. The Dual -
channel switchable ABS  offers Road, Off -Road, and Rally modes for varied riding 
conditions.
