PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2026. This positioning unde rscores Coforge's sustained momentum 
in the Duck Creek services market, as insurers accelerate cloud -native modernization and 
prioritize faster time- to-value from core platforms. It also reflects Coforge's decades of deep 
industry expertise and specialized delivery capabilities for Property and Casualty (P&C) 
insurance carriers and its ongoing investments in proprietary accelerators and Agentic AI –led 
solutions across Duck Creek delivery and key workflows.  
 The contenders of the Everest Group Duck Creek Services were evaluated across two key

Star Performer reflects Coforge's strong performance across both dimensions, w ith the Star

year improvement across multiple parameters on the assessment.
