Step into the castle! The Traitors: Interactive Game is out now on iOS 
and Android  
 
Complete Missions, forge alliances and root out the Traitors in this choice -driven narrative 
experience where NO ONE can be trusted..!  
 
London, October 5, 2026 - Fusebox Games ( Big Brother: The Game, Love Island: The Game ), UK -
based subsidiary of Nazara Technologies, today revealed that The Traitors: Interactive Game  is 
now available to download on iOS and Android platforms. Inviting you into the halls of the 
infamous Traitors Castle to compete alongside 22 other players, The Traitors: Interactive Game 
is designed to be a player -driven experience packed with impactful decisions at each and every 
turn.  
 
Guiding you through all of this is the formidable host Vivienne, who will also lead you through 
each day's mission.  This is your game, though, and you’ll start it by choosing a defining trait: 
the outspoken Leader, the adaptable Chameleon or the chaotic W ildcard, which will form the 
foundation of your character. Just be mindful that acting in line with your chosen trait at the 
right time can sway opinion and even turn players in your favour.  
 
It’s not just what you say, either; curating your daily wardrobe is another tactic you can use to 
forge a path to win the gold! But while each Mission and Round Table calls for a new look, 
ultimately, it’ll be down to your ability to build the pot on missi ons that’ll earn favour with your 
fellow players. The missions won’t just offer Gold but also immunity -providing shields that will 
protect anyone who grabs one from that night’s murder!  
 
Set across 12 days, your time at the castle is all about building trust with other players as well 
as building believable theories, especially when it comes down to The Round Table! Whether 
you’re working alongside fellow faithfuls or quietly operating fro m the shadows as a Traitor, 
your ability to build alliances during the day and vote tactically at The Round Table will 
determine whether you avoid being banished.  
 
"When it comes to adapting iconic entertainment IP, Fusebox Games is as faithful a partner as they 
come. Our history is built on story -based, choice -driven games that put players in control, making 
the deceit and strategy of The Traitors a natural fit,”  said Fusebox Games CEO Terry Lee.  He 

added: “We’re giving fans the chance to step out of the audience and take a seat at the table and 
tell their own story at the castle."  
 
VP Licensing (Sales) Jason Easy at All3Media International, now to be part of Banijay Rights, 
commented, “The Traitors is a global phenomenon, available in more than 40 territories, so we’re 
thrilled that fans around the world will now have the chance to discover how they would fare as they 
experience the tensions, twists, deceptions and betrayals of the form at through Fusebox’s new 
mobile game.”  
 
The Traitors: Interactive Game  is now available on iOS and Android. Stay up to date with news 
and updates on the game by joining the official Fusebox Games Discord  and following the 
official The Traitors: Interactive Game Instagram .
