N Abdul Hameed (A-55), S/o. Mr. Nagoor Kani, Proprietor,  
M/s I K Enterprises, Residing at (I) 34 LGN Road, Border Thottiah, 
Mount Road, Chennai-600002, (ii) 16-2 Jani Basha Street,

Hong Kong, Singapore, UAE and China, punishable under section 
120-B, r/w 417, 467, 471, IPC and Sec. 13(2) r/w 13(1)(d) of

the said N Abdul Hameed (A-55) has absconded.

84 of BNSS, 2023, that the said N Abdul Hameed (A-55) is 
required to appear in person before the XI Additional City Civil & 
Sessions Court for CBI Cases, Singaravelar Maaligai, Chennai-1 to 
answer the said complaint on the 27th day of March, 2025 
without fail.
