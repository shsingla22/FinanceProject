Sub:   Intimation under Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  (‘SEBI Listing Regulations’)  
With reference to the captioned subject, this is to inform you that the Company has received on  August 6, 
2024, an Order dated  July 22, 2024, from the  Additional  Commissioner of Customs , Nhava Sheva, against 
Company’s written submission vide letter dated July 13, 2023 . The said order has been issued denying the 
Merchandise  Exports from India Scheme  (MEIS ) refund claim of Rs. 29,21,540/ - due to classification of 
goods  and imposing penalties aggregating to Rs. 15,00,000/ - and a redemption fine of Rs. 25 ,00,000/ -. 
 
Based on the merits of the matter, prevailing law and the advice of the counsel, the Company is planning 
to appeal against this order before the Commissioner of Customs (Appeals) and reasonably expects a 
favorable  order from the appellant authority.  The Company does not foresee any material impact on its 
financial, or operational or other activities.
