October	 15,	 2026:	 Heritage	 Foods	 Limited	 (BSE:	 519552;	 NSE:	 HERITGFOOD),	 a	 leading	 dairy	
company	offering	milk	and	value -added	dairy	products,	announced	its	results	for	the	quarter	and	half	
year	ended	September	30,	2026.	
Q2	&	H1	FY27	Results	Key	Highlights
Volume	Growth	Amid	Supply	Constraints	and	Elevated	Milk	Procurement	Costs 	
•Milk	 Procurement	Network	Reinforcement: 	Milk	procurement	volumes	grew	7%	YoY	in	Q2
FY27	and	4%	in	H1	FY27	despite	monsoon -relat ed	supply	constraints.	Village -level	outreach	is
being	intensified	to	strengthen	milk	availability	and	farmer	engagement.
•Persistent	 Milk	 Inflation :	Average	 landed	 milk	 procurement	 prices	 rose	 11%	 YoY	 to
INR47.73/L	 in	 Q2	 FY27,	 up	 Rs.4.74/Lt	 compared	 to	 Q2	 FY26,	 driven	 by 	sustained	 demand,
limited	improvement	in	milk	arrivals	and	higher	feed	and	fodder	costs.
Strong	Consumer	Franchise	Driving	both	Volume	and	Value	growth	in	equal	measure 	
•Milk	 Revenue	 Growth	 Driven	 by	Volumes	 and	 Calibrated	 Pricing: Milk	 revenue	 grew  
8.5% YoY	 in	Q2	FY27,	 supported	 by	2.5%	 volume	 growth,	 led	by	accelerating	 growth	 in	core  
markets,	 and	calibrated	 consumer	 price	 increases.	 Average	 milk	 realisation	 improved	 6%	YoY  
to	INR	60.34	 per	litre	 during	 the	quarter.
•Value-Adde d	Products	 Delivered	 Strong,	 Broad-Base d	Growth: HFL	 recorded	 robust  
revenue	 growth	 across	 key	 VAP	 categories,	 led	by	Lassi	 (+91%),	 Ice	Cream	 (+81%),  
Buttermilk	 (+66%),	 Paneer	 (+38%)	 and	 Curd	 (+26%).	 Importantly,	 this	 growth	 was  
underpinned	 by	continued	 strong	 volume	 momentum	 across	 categories,	 with	 Lassi	 growing  
89%,	 Buttermilk	 56%,	 Ice	Cream	 44%,	 Paneer	 21%	 and	Curd	 15%	 in	volume.
•Strong	 Category	Milestones: Daily	 curd	 sales	 surpassed	 500	tons	 per	day	duri ng	the	quarter,  
while	 yogurt	 volumes	 crossed	 100	 tons	 for	the	quarter.	 Nourish+	 High	 Protein	 Paneer	 now  
accounts	 for	over	 15%	 of	the	Paneer	 portfolio.

Business	Highlights
•Robust	 Revenue	 Growth:	 Consolidated	 revenue	 grew	 19%	 YoY	 to	INR13,196	 million	 in	Q2 
FY27	 and	 18%	 YoY	 to	INR26,577	 million	 in	H1	FY27,	 driven	 by	strong	 VAP	 growth,	 higher 
product	r ealisations	and	 continued	 traction	across	 the	consumer	 portfolio.
•Milk	 Procurement:	 Milk	 procurement	 volumes	 were	 17.28	LLPD	 in	Q2	FY27	 (+7%	 YoY)	 and

•Value-A dded	 Products	 Gaining	 Share:	 VAP	 revenue	 (excluding	 Ghee/Butter)	 grew	 32%	 YoY

H1	(vs	33.7%	 in	H1	FY26),	 supported	by	 strong	 volume	 growth	 and	improved	realisations.

and	 Butter,	 supported	 by	wider	 distribution,	 disciplined	 execution,	 growing	 consumer

Management	Commentary
Commenting	on	the	Results,	Mrs.	Brahmani	Nara	 -	Executive	Director,	said:	“ We	are	pleased	to	report	
another	quarter	of	strong	execution,	with	Heritage	Foods	delivering	19%	YoY	revenue	growth	and	a	
98bps	sequential	recovery	in	EBITDA	margin	to	5.6%,	despite	elevated	milk	procurement	costs.	This	performance	reflects	the	resilience	 of	our	integrated	dairy	model,	strong	consumer	franchise	and	
continued	traction	across	our	 VAP	portfolio.	
	Our	 focus	 remains	 on	 strengthening	 the	 procurement	 ecosystem,	 expanding	 VAP	 capacity	 and	
accelerating	innovation	across	premium	and	nutrition -led	categories.	Broad -based	volume	growth,	
calibrated	pricing	and	an	increasing	contribution	from	higher -value	pr oducts	are	strengthening	our	
business	mix	and	positioning	us	for	sustained	profitability	improvement. 	
While	near- term	cost	pressures	persist,	we	remain	confident	in	our	ability	to	drive	sustainable	margin	
improvement	through	continued	premiumisation,	 tight	cost	control	measures,	improved	 operating	
leverage	and	disciplined	execution.	We	 remain	firmly	focused	on	our	long- term	vision	of	profitable	
growth,	 supported	by	strategic	investments,	an	increasingly	differentiated	product	portfolio	and	the	
enduring	trust	of	our	consumers	and	stakeholders. ”

About	Heritage	Foods	Limited
Media	Release 	
Heritage	Foods	Limited	(NSE:	HERITGFOOD;	BSE:	519552)	is	one	of	India’s	leading	dairy	companies,	
founded	in	1992	with	a	vision	to	provide	high	quality,	nutritious	milk	and	dairy	products	to	families	
across	the	nation	and	to	empower	farmers.	Over	the	years, 	Heritage	has	grown	into	a	trusted	brand,	
known	 for	its	commitment 	to	purity, 	freshness,	 and	sustainability. 	With 	operations 	spanning 	13	
states, 	Heritage	 Foods	 serves	 millions	 of	consumers,	 offering	 a	wide	 range	 of	milk, 	curd, 	butter,	
paneer,	and	other	value- added	dairy	products.	
Heritage	Foods	stands	out	for	its	farm -to-home	approach,	ensuring	every	product	is	produced	with	
the	 highest	 standards	 of	quality	 and	 care.	 The	 company	 works	 closely	 with	 its	network	 of	
dairy	 farmers,	 empowering	 them	 with	 modern	 farming	 practices,	 training,	 and	 vetted	by	their	500
+ Quality	Experts	who	perform	more	than	25	stringent	quality	tests	daily	which	in	 turn	guarantees	a 
consistent	supply	of	fresh,	high-q uality	milk.
Driven 	by	a	passion 	for	excellence 	and	 a	deep 	understanding 	of	consumer	 needs, 	Heritage	 Foods	
remains	 at	the	forefront	 of	India’s	 dairy	 industry,	 constantly	 striving	 to	provide	 products	 that	
nourish	and	support	families	across	the	country.
