Official Statement dated 19th September, 2024 
 
VIP Industries Limited has recently noticed a false and malicious video circulating on WhatsApp, 
purporting to be an advertisement from our company. We categorically deny any association 
with this video, which promotes harmful and divisive content related to "Love Jihad." 
 
This is not the first time our brand has been targeted with such misinformation. A similar incident 
occurred on social media platforms last year and we wish to emphasize that VIP Industries does 
not create, endorse, or distribute any content that promotes social discord or religious 
intolerance. 
We have taken immediate and requisite action by filing fresh Police Complaint in Mumbai. We 
have also escalated the matter to Cyber cell, Mumbai and email correspondence with grievance 
officer of the whatsApp Inc. to take cognizance of the fake video and block the circulation of the 
same. 
 
Our legal team is actively pursuing this matter to identify and take appropriate action against 
those responsible for creating and spreading this fake advertisement. 
 
We urge the public to be vigilant and not to share or forward this false content. If you encounter 
this video, please report it to the relevant authorities and inform us through our official channels. 
 
We appreciate the continued support of our customers and stakeholders and remain dedicated 
to providing quality products and fostering positive values in our community. 
 
 
Thank you for your trust and support. 
  
VIP Industries Team
