The transfer of the Company's entire shareholding comprising 100 shares of Common Stock, representing 
100% of the issued share capital of Kaleyra, Inc., to T ata Communications ( Netherlands) B.V. (‘TCNL’)  has

October 31, 2026.

the ownership structure of the Tata Communications ’ Group  and does not result in any change in the ultimate 
beneficial ownership  and beneficial economic interest of the Company in Kaleyra  Group entities .
