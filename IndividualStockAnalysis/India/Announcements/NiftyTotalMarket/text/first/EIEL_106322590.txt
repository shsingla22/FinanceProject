Sub: Update on Cyber Fraud Incident 
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”) and in reference to our earlier intimation 
dated 02.07.2025 regarding the cyber fraud incident in the Company to the tune of ₹ 11.15 Crore, we 
wish to provide the update on the incident as per SEBI master Circular dated 11.11.2024, as follows: 
 
a) Actual amount involved in the fraud/default (if any):  An amount of ₹ 11.15 Crores was 
fraudulently transferred from the Company's bank account to certain unidentified accounts through 
impersonation of a Company’s Director. 
 
b) Actual impact of such fraud/default on the Company and its financials:  The Company has till 
date recovered ₹ 2,50,22,719 and recovery efforts are ongoing. Accordingly, the net financial impact 
on the Company currently stands at ₹ 8,64,77,281. The matter is under active investigation by the 
Cyber Crime authorities. 
 
c) Corrective measures taken by the Company on account of such fraud/default:  The Company 
has taken immediate corrective measures, including: 
 
 Strengthening of internal controls and authorization protocols related to banking transactions. 
 
 Awareness on cyber fraud risks and preventive measures among employees.  
 
d) Mitigation measures taken by the Company on account of such fraud/default: As informed 
earlier, the Executive Directors of the Company continue to voluntarily forego their salaries until the 
entire loss amount is fully compensated, as a measure to mitigate the financial impact of the cyber 
fraud incident.  
  
We continue to extend full cooperation to the investigating authorities, and appropriate actions will be 
taken based on the outcome of the investigation. 
 
Kindly take the above information on record.
