Press Release  
Mankind Pharma to Acquire Majority Stake in Armed Cell Therapeutics, Marking 
Strategic Entry into Next-Generation CAR-T Therapies 
Armed Cell Therapeutics is a Belgium-based biotechnology company developing next-generation 
armed CAR-T therapies for cancer. 
New Delhi, India, 15th October, 2026:  As a part of its long term strategy to build a global innovation-
led pharmaceutical business, Mankind Pharma (BSE: 543904 | NSE: MANKIND) through its subsidiary 
Mankind Pharma (Netherlands) B.V., has entered into a definitive agreement under which Mankind 
Pharma will acquire a majority stake in Armed Cell Therapeutics B.V., a Belgium-incorporated 
biotechnology company developing next-generation CAR-T (chimeric antigen receptor T-cell) therapies 
for cancer. 
Transaction Summary: 
Mankind to invest EUR 48 million for 70% stake in Armed Cell Therapeutics
Armed Cell Therapeutics (ACTx) has a pipeline of next-generation CAR-T assets, with lead
programs in non-Hodgkin lymphoma and small cell lung cancer
ACTx will pursue innovative means of CAR- T cell therapy including in vivo CAR-T cell generation
ACTx’s R&D activities are led by highly experienced teams located in US and EU
Armed Cell Therapeutics was founded by a team with experience in drug development, cell therapy 
and building companies. Its founders, include Paul Stoffels, MD, Chair of the Board, former Chief 
Executive Officer of Galapagos and former Chief Scientific Officer of Johnson & Johnson; John Mellors, 
MD, Chief Scientific Officer, Distinguished Professor of Medicine at the University of Pittsburgh and 
former SVP Discovery and Early Development at Galapagos; and Peter Verhaeghe, former Chair of 
argenx. 
CAR-T therapies have transformed the treatment of some blood cancers. Yet many patients relapse 
from lack of CAR-T cell persistence, CAR-T exhaustion or emergence of resistant cancer cells. Solid 
cancers are especially hard for CAR-T cells to penetrate and function in hostile tumor 
microenvironments. Armed CAR-T cells are engineered with additional payloads to strengthen and 
sustain their anti-tumor activity, with the goal of deeper and more durable responses. Targeting more 
than one cancer antigen is designed to make it harder for cancer cells to escape. Research and 
development activities by highly-experienced teams are located in the US and Europe. 
This partnership propels Mankind’s vision of being an innovative, research-focused healthcare 
company and strengthens its ability to deliver advanced, innovation-led therapies globally, while 
remaining committed to quality, affordability and accessibility. 
Mr. Arjun Juneja, Chief Operating Officer, Mankind Pharma Ltd., shared,  “Innovation will be one of 
the defining growth engines for Mankind over the coming decades. This investment is the first step in 
building a global pipeline of breakthrough therapies. As the company scales its presence across complex 
and chronic therapy areas, Mankind, with this venture, is getting access to cutting-edge & 
differentiated scientific platforms, experienced leadership team and potential to generate multiple 
next-generation CAR-T candidates. Our long-term ambition is not only to participate in scientific

About Mankind Pharma 
Mankind Pharma (BSE: 543904 | NSE: MANKIND)  is one of the largest pharmaceutical companies in 
India, which focuses on the domestic market with its pan-India presence. Mankind operates at the 
intersection of the Indian pharmaceutical formulations and consumer healthcare sectors with the aim 
of providing quality products at affordable prices. The company is a leading player in the domestic 
pharmaceuticals business present across acute and chronic therapeutic areas including anti-infectives, 
cardiovascular, gastrointestinal, antidiabetic, neuro/CNS, gynaecology, VMN and respiratory, among 
others with a strategy to increase chronic presence going ahead. In the consumer healthcare business, 
the company operates in the condoms, pregnancy detection, emergency contraceptives, antacid 
powders, vitamin and mineral supplements and anti-acne preparations categories, among others, with 
several category-leading brands. Following the acquisition of BSV, Mankind Pharma has further 
strengthened its leadership in the domestic women’s health segment. Mankind's distribution network 
includes a robust field force of 18,500+ professionals and a reach extending to over five lakh doctors 
across urban and rural markets. The company has 32 manufacturing facilities in India manufacturing a 
wide range of dosage forms, including tablets, capsules, syrups, vials, ampoules, blow fill seal, soft and 
hard gels, eye drops, creams, contraceptives and other over-the-counter products. Mankind has a 
consistent track record of product innovation through 7 dedicated R&D facilities backed by more than 
740 scientists. For more information, visit www.mankindpharma.com  
 
About Armed Cell Therapeutics 
Armed Cell Therapeutics is a biotechnology company developing next-generation armed CAR-T 
therapies for cancer. Its lead programs focus on non-Hodgkin lymphoma, where relapse after current 
CAR-T therapy remains common, and small cell lung cancer, an aggressive tumor with few effective 
treatment options. The company is headquartered in Belgium, with operations in the Netherlands, 
research and pre-clinical operations in Pittsburgh, USA, and early development CAR-T cell therapy 
activities in Europe, Asia, and the United States. For more information, visit www.armedcelltx.com.  
 
About Dr. Paul Stoffels 
Paul Stoffels, MD, is Chair of the Board of Armed Cell Therapeutics. He is the former Chief Executive 
Officer of Galapagos and former Vice Chairman and Chief Scientific Officer of Johnson & Johnson. 
 
About Dr. John Mellors 
John W. Mellors, MD, is Chief Scientific Officer of Armed Cell Therapeutics and Distinguished Professor 
of Medicine at the University of Pittsburgh. He co-founded AboundBio and is the former SVP Discovery 
and Early Development at Galapagos.
