MEDIA RELEASE: IEX POWER MARKET UPDATE, H1FY’27 Q2FY’27 AND SEP'26
H1FY’27
● IEX ACHIEVES 77.2 BU ELECTRICITY TRADED VOLUME WITH 14.2% YOY GROWTH
● ACHIEVES VOLUME OF 32.5 BU IN RTM, INCREASE OF 16.5% YOY
Q2FY’27
● IEX ACHIEVES 39.7 BU ELECTRICITY TRADED VOLUME WITH 12.7% YOY GROWTH
● ACHIEVES VOLUME OF 16.5 BU IN RTM, INCREASE OF 10.5 % YOY
SEPTEMBER 2026
● IEX ACHIEVES MONTHLY ELECTRICITY TRADED VOLUME OF 12.2 BU IN
SEPTEMBER’26, AN INCREASE OF 10.4% YOY
● ACHIEVES VOLUME OF 5.3 BU IN RTM, INCREASE OF 10.6% YOY
● HIGHEST EVER SINGLE DAY VOLUME OF 315 MU IN RTM, ON SEP 26,2026
New Delhi, 06 October 2026: Indian Energy Exchange, India’s premier electricity exchange,
for the second quarter of the fiscal year 2027, achieved electricity traded volume of 39,685
MU, marking a 12.7% year on year increase. The average market clearing price in the Day-
Ahead Market at Rs 5.7/unit during Q2 FY’27 increased 46% compared to Q2 FY’26. Similarly,
the average market clearing price in the Real-Time Market at Rs 5.2/unit during Q2 FY’27
increased 49% compared to Q2FY’26. During the same quarter, country’s energy consumption
touched 502 BUs, registering a year-on-year growth of 11.5%.
In September’26, IEX achieved a monthly electricity traded volume of 12,220 MU, a 10.4 %
increase year-on-year. India’s power demand remained strong in the same month, with peak
demand touching 269 GW on September 10, 2026. According to government data published
in September 2026, the country’s energy consumption reached 162 BUs, reflecting an 11%
year-on-year growth. Strong power demand led to a 281% year-on-year increase in buy bids
in the Day-Ahead Market, resulting in higher market prices. The average market clearing price
in the Day Ahead Market at Rs 7.3/unit during September 2026, increased 105% year-on-year.
Similarly, the average market clearing price in the Real Time Market at Rs 6.9/unit during
September 2026, increased 108% year-on-year.
ELECTRICITY MARKET: DAY- AHEAD, TERM- AHEAD & REAL-TIME MARKET
The Day-Ahead Market (DAM) including HPDAM, registered total volumes of 14,855 MU
during Q2 FY '27, as compared to 14,534 MU volume in Q2 FY '26, increase of 2.2% YoY. The
DAM segment accounted for 37.4% of the total electricity traded volumes in Q2FY’26. The
DAM segment including HPDAM achieved 4,251 MU volume in September’26 as compared to
4,228 MU volume in September’25, a marginal increase of 0.5% YoY.

The Real-Time Electricity Market (RTM) registered total volumes of 16,490 MU during
Q2FY'26, as compared to 14,925 MU volume in Q2 FY '26 an increase of 10.5 % YoY. The RTM
segment accounted for 41.6% of the total electricity traded volumes in Q2FY’26. RTM volume
increased to 5,294 MU in September ‘26, from 4,786 MU in September'25, registering an
increase of 10.6% YoY.
Day Ahead Contingency and Term-Ahead Market (TAM) ,comprising of HPTAM, intraday,
contingency, daily & weekly and monthly contracts up to 3 months, traded 5,477 MU in
Q2FY’27, as compared to 2,719 MU volume in Q2 FY '26 an increase of 101.5% YoY. The total
volume on the segment during September’26 was 1,939 MU as compared to 967 MU volume
in September 25, an increase of 100.6% YoY.
GREEN MARKET: GREEN DAY-AHEAD & GREEN TERM-AHEAD MARKET
IEX Green Market, comprising the Green Day-Ahead and Green Term-Ahead Market
segments, during Q2FY’27, achieved volume of 2,863 MU, as compared to 3,040 MU volume
in Q2 FY '26, decline of 5.8% YoY. The segment achieved 737 MU volume during September’26
as compared to 1,084 MU in September’25, decline of 32% YoY. The weighted average price
in Green Day-Ahead Market (G-DAM) for September’26 at Rs 4.9/unit, increased 56.3% YoY.
RENEWABLE ENERGY CERTIFICATE MARKET (REC MARKET)
A total of 15.27 lakh RECs were traded during Q2 FY’27, decline of 65.5% YoY.
A total of 5.25 lakh RECs were traded in September ’26, down 16.2 % YoY due to lower
participation. Trading sessions were held on 09thSeptember’26 and 30thSeptember’26, at a
clearing price of Rs.345/REC and Rs. 343/REC respectively.
The next REC trading sessions at the Exchange are scheduled on 14thOctober’26 and 28th
October’26.
INDIAN ENERGY EXCHANGE LIMITED
IEX is India’s premier energy exchange providing a nationwide, automated trading platform for physical delivery of
electricity, renewable energy and certificates including renewable energy certificates as well as the energy saving
certificates. The exchange platform enables efficient price discovery and increases the accessibility and transparency of
the energy market in India while also enhancing the speed and efficiency of trade execution. The Exchange is ISO Certified
for quality management, Information security management and environment management since August 2016. The
Exchange is a publicly listed company with NSE and BSE since October 2017 and is approved and regulated by Central
Electricity Regulatory Commission since 27 June 2008. For further details, log on to: www.iexindia.com
