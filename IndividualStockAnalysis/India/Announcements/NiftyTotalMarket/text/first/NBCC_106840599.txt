Sub: General Update: NBCC (India) Limited has signed Memorandum of 
Understanding with various departments of the Government of Bihar to 
undertake four redevelopment projects in Patna under Phase -I  
 
Sir, 
 
This is to inform that, in the presence of the Hon'ble Chief Minister of Bihar, Shri 
Samrat Choudhary, Shri K. P. Mahadevaswamy (CMD -NBCC), and other 
dignitaries of the State Government, and officials of NBCC, Memorandum of 
Understanding has been signed with various departments of the Government of 
Bihar to undertake four redevelopment projects in Patna under Phase -I on a 
self-financing basis without budgetary assistance from the State Government.  
 
The proposed  projects are as under : 
 
I. Residential redevelopment of BSHB colony at Sector 5 (TV Tower), Patna  
 
II. Residential redevelopment of BSHB colony at Sector 6 (Bahadurpur), Patna.  
 
(Items (i) and (ii) together provide 3,331 residential flats for EWS, LIG, MIG 
and HIG categories, with shopping complexes, clubhouses, gyms, community 
facilities, office spaces and other supporting facilities)  
 
III. Modern IT Tech Park for DIT Bihar at Gardanibagh, Patna (the first Tech 
Park of the State)  
 
IV. Hotels / commercial spaces at Water Resources Department site, Bailey 
Road, Patna  
 
 
 
 
 
Pursuant to the cabinet note of the Urban Development and Housing 
Department , Govt. of Bihar, and MOUs, the  expected sale/lease proceeds would 
be Rs. 5,152 crore (approx.).  
 
Further, the  tentative project cost would be  Rs. 2,000 crore (approx.) exclusive 
of GST.  
 
The PMC charges are 6% and the marketing fee is 1.25%.  
 
Project -wise details would be provided in due course.  
 
 The same is available on the website of the company: 
https://www.nbccindia.in/webEnglish/announcementNotices
