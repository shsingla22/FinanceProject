Delhivery Accelerates Last -Mile Electrification with 
Strategic EV Deployment Across India  
 
Guwahati, India – February 16, 2026  – As part of its efforts to accelerate the electrification of its 
last-mile fleet, Delhivery Limited (NSE: DELHIVERY, BSE: 543529) today announced a strategic 
partnership with electric mobility startup RIDEV (ANV Web Ventures Pvt Ltd) to deploy 150 high -
performance electric vehicles (EVs). This deployment, scheduled over the next three months across 
North East India, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad, marks a significant step in the company’s roadmap to 
scale green logistics.  
 
The collaboration introduces a specialized "EV -as-a-Service" leasing model designed to accelerate 
the transition for Delhivery’s delivery partners. By eliminating high upfront costs and technical 
barriers, this model ensures that the shift to an electrifie d last -mile ecosystem is both operationally 
seamless and financially lucrative for the gig workforce.  
 
Delhivery already operates a substantial EV fleet, having integrated close to 1,000 electric two, three 
and four -wheelers in recent years. This partnership signals a move toward a nationwide, systematic 
replacement of traditional internal combustion engine  (ICE) vehicles. The initiative follows a 
successful pilot in Delhi -NCR and Bengaluru, where the transition to RIDEV’s electric two -wheelers 
resulted in the reduction of 4,260 Kg of CO2 and lowering daily operational costs for riders by more 
than 50%.  
 
This initiative is a critical component of Delhivery’s strategy to systematically decarbonize its urban 
delivery network. Moving beyond isolated pilots, the company is now focusing on the rapid 
replacement of fossil -fuel-dependent vehicles. Following the c urrent rollout, a phased expansion is 
planned for major hubs, including Mumbai, Pune, Chennai, and Goa, aiming to eliminate tailpipe 
emissions and align with India’s PM E -DRIVE objectives for a cleaner transportation future.  
 
“Electrification is a fundamental shift in our approach to last -mile logistics,”  said Prashant Gazipur, 
Senior Vice President of Last Mile Operations at Delhivery. "Our partnership with RIDEV is a 
transformative step in our journey toward sustainable last -mile operations. By providing our delivery 
partners with a low -friction entry into electric mobility, we are not only advancing our carbon 
neutrality goals but also  significantly enhancing the economic well -being of our riders through 
reduced operational o verheads,"  added Prashant.  
 
“Integrating our tech -backed fleet into Delhivery’s network accelerates the transition to green 
logistics while simplifying daily work for the gig economy,”  said Manish Kumar Jain, Founder, 
RIDEV . “Our platform allows riders to focus on performance while we manage the complexities of 
energy and maintenance.”
