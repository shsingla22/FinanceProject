5 Specific commitments, goals and targets set by the entity with defined timelines, if any.NLCIL has set an ambitious growth objective to become a significant player in the energy sector, with a goal to boost its power generation capabilities to 20,130 MW by the year 2030. To achieve self-reliance in fuel supply, the company intends to increase its lignite extraction capabilities from the present 30.10 million tonnes per annum (MTPA) to an impressive 44.85 MTPA.
