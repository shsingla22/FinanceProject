PRESS RELEASE  
India/ Kolkata, November 1 4, 2026 : Senco Gold Limited , one of India's leading jewellery retailers, 
announced a strong business performance for the second quarter and half year ended September 30, 
2026 , demonstrating sustained consumer demand, robust same -store growth and continued expansion 
of its retail footprint across India.  
Consolidated Financial Snapshot:  
(Rs in Cr)  
  
Strong Revenue Growth Driven by Healthy Consumer Demand  
• Senco Gold Limited adds net 14 New Showrooms in H1, Takes Retail Network to 215 
Showrooms ; 
• Senco Gold Limited delivers Q2 growth of 34%, H1 growth of 52% & Consolidated 
Revenue Growth in Q2 FY27;  
• Senco Gold Limited achieves H1 FY27 Sales of Over ₹ 5114.8 Crore; TTM Sales Cross 
₹10,000 Crore  
• PAT growth of 78% YoY in Q2, & 22% in H1  
The Company recorded 40% year -on-year growth  in retail revenue during H1 FY27, supported by 
healthy consumer demand and robust same -store sales growth (SSSG) of 19% in Q2 and 30% in H1. 
The performance underscores the resilience of jewellery consumption in India, driven by festive and 
wedding -related purchases, evolving consumer preferences and increasing demand for lightweight 
jewellery.  
The Company achieved its highest -ever topline of over ₹ 5,114.8 crore  during H1 FY27, while its 
trailing twelve -month (TTM) sales crossed the significant milestone of ₹ 10,000 crore , reflecting the 
strength of its brand franchise and growing consumer preference for organised jewellery retail.  
Accelerated Retail Expansion – 14 New Showrooms Added in H1 FY27   
Senco Gold continued to strengthen its pan -India retail presence, opening 6 new showrooms during Q2 
FY27 and taking the total net showroom additions during H1 FY27 to 14  (7 Own S howrooms  and 
7 Franchise S howrooms ). With these additions, the Company's retail network expanded to 215 
showrooms across the country, comprising both franchisee -operated  92 showrooms  and Company -
owned  123 showrooms . 
The Company continues to pursue a balanced retail expansion strategy through both Company -Owned 
Company -Operated (COCO) and franchisee -led showrooms. This approach enables it to deepen market 
penetration, strengthen its presence across existing markets and  tap into new customer segments.  
With a healthy pipeline of expansion opportunities, Senco Gold plans to launch another 6-7 
showrooms during H2 FY27 , further strengthening its presence across key  markets , core & future .

Strong Consumer Demand Amid Elevated Gold Prices : 
Consumer demand remained buoyant during the quarter despite elevated and volatile gold prices, which 
ranged between approximately ₹1,40,000 and ₹1,50,000 per 10 grams. The sustained demand reflects 
the resilience of jewellery consumption, supported by stro ng consumer sentiment and the continued 
importance of jewellery purchases during festive and wedding occasions.  Old gold Exchange s rose to 
51% in H1Fy27.  
The quarter benefited from a favourable festive calendar, including Ratha Yatra, Raksha Bandhan, 
Janmashtami, Onam, Ganesh Chaturthi and Independence Day. Additionally, the shift in the timing of 
Pitru Paksha, with only four days falling in Q2 FY27 compare d with 15 days in the corresponding 
quarter last year, also contributed to improved sales momentum.  
The Company's continued focus on contemporary designs, product innovation, lightweight jewellery, 
customer -centric offerings and a diversified product portfolio continued to drive customer engagement 
and support purchasing momentum. New collections featuri ng lower -karatage and lightweight 
jewellery, developed to align with evolving consumer preferences and budget considerations, further 
strengthened the Company's product proposition.  
As part of its innovation -led approach, Senco Gold & Diamonds introduced Ti22 by Aham, an 
innovative titanium jewellery collection under its men's jewellery brand Aham. The collection brings 
together titanium, gold and natural diamonds to create a differen tiated proposition in contemporary 
men's fine jewellery, combining strength, durability and lightweight properties with the elegance of 
precious materials. This launch reinforces the Company's commitment to pioneering product innovation 
and expanding its p resence in the evolving men's jewellery segment.   
The Company also strengthened its offerings across everyday wear and bridal jewellery, catering to a 
wider spectrum of customer preferences, lifestyle requirements and jewellery -buying occasions.  
Management Commentary : 
Commenting on the performance , Mr. Suvankar Sen, (Managing Director & CEO, Senco Gold 
Limited ), said:  "We are pleased to report a strong performance in Q2 FY27, with 52% growth at the 
ground level, 29% growth in retail revenue and 19% same -store sales growth (SSSG).  Our H1 
performance, with sales exceeding ₹5,000 crore and trailing twelve -month (TTM) sales surpassing 
₹10,000 crore, reflects the continued strength of our brand, the trust reposed in us by our customers, the 
resilience of India's jewellery consumption ma rket and the growing contribution of diamond jewellery.  
Retail expansion continues to be a key pillar of our growth strategy. With 14 new showrooms added 
during the first half of the financial year, taking our network to 215 showrooms, we have further 
strengthened our presence across existing and emerging marke ts. We are targeting the launch of another 
10–12 showrooms in the second half of the financial year.  
As we enter the festive and wedding season, we remain optimistic about the demand environment. Our 
focus will continue to be on offering differentiated designs, strengthening customer relationships, 
expanding our retail footprint and delivering sustainable  and profitable growth.  
The performance of our subsidiaries has also remained satisfactory. At the standalone level, EBITDA 
margins in Q2 remained muted, primarily due to gold price volatility, elevated old gold exchange 
volumes, intense market competition and the prevailing hedg ing ratio. At the consolidated level, EBIT 
registered a robust growth of 81% year -on-year to ₹190 crore  though H1  FY 27  EBITDA stood at 7. 6%. 
Consolidated PAT for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹86.7 crore, translating into a margin of 4.2%, while H1 FY27 
PAT stood at ₹187.8 crore, with a margin of 3.7%.  
 
Commenting on the same, Mr. Sanjay Banka (CFO, Senco Gold Limited)  said, “ To support our 
future growth plans and diversify our funding sources, we have been among the early players in the 
jewellery industry to successfully raise short -term funds through the Commercial Paper route, reflecting 
the confidence and trust placed in the C ompany. Our strategy remains focused on deepening our 
presence in existing markets, acquiring new customers and enhancing customer convenience through a 
wider variety of designs, particularly in the current environment of significant gold price volatility.  
On the balance sheet front, inventory increased to ₹5,487.8 crore, primarily driven by the addition of 
new showrooms and strategic stocking in preparation for the Dhanteras and Diwali festive season. 
Borrowings increased from ₹2,346.0 crore to ₹2,612.7 cro re to support higher inventory levels and 
working capital requirements ahead of the peak jewellery buying season.  
Looking ahead, we remain committed to achieving value growth of over 2 5% in H2 FY27 and an 
EBITDA margin of 7.5%, while working towards a sustainable PAT margin in the range of 4.0% –4.5%, 
with a continued focus on improving Return on Capital Employed (RoCE). Our key priorities will 
remain margin stabilisation, improving inven tory days, optimising finance costs through an appropriate 
mix of Gold Metal Loans (GML) and strengthening the performance of our subsidiaries. These 
initiatives will support our endeavour to deliver sustainable, profitable growth while maintaining a 
disci plined approach to capital allocation.  Senco Gold Limited have been nominated as one of the top 
trusted jewellery brands by TRA and 2nd most desired brand by TRA (2026) due to constant effort in 
building trust & prominent brand ”. 
Outlook :  
Looking ahead, Senco Gold expects demand momentum to remain encouraging in H2 FY27, supported 
by the upcoming festive and wedding seasons, improving rural sentiment and the underlying affinity 
for gold in India.  The Dhanteras festive season had a sale of Rs  1800cr and growth of above 30 %.  
The Company expects the festive occasions of Dhanteras, Diwali, Durga Puja, Karwa Chauth, Chhath 
Puja and Christmas, along with the upcoming wedding calendar, to support jewellery consumption.  We 
expected minimum min 25% growth for the whole year based on the  existing trend of sales.  
While elevated gold prices continue to present affordability and working -capital challenges, the 
Company believes that its established brand presence, sourcing capabilities, customer exchange 
programmes, product innovation and disciplined inventory managem ent position it well to capitalise on 
the long -term opportunity in India's organised jewellery market.  
Senco Gold remains committed to expanding its national footprint, enhancing customer experience and 
creating sustainable long -term value for all stakeholders.  
About Senco Gold Limited : 
Senco Gold & Diamonds  (Senco) is a leading pan -India jewellery retailer with a rich legacy of over 
87+ years, and the largest in Eastern India by number of showrooms. Senco has been featured in the 
Global Top 100 Luxury Brands list by Deloitte  in its February 2024 report. Senco is also certified as a 
Great Place to Work . Senco Gold Limited has been nominated as 3rd most trusted brand and 2nd most 
desired brand by TRA in 2026.   
Senco Gold & Diamonds launched ‘The  Shape  of You’ , a pioneering, technology -led jewellery styling 
initiative that uses AI -powered face -shape analysis to help customers discover jewellery designs that 
complement their individual facial features and contours.  
 
The initiative combines design expertise with technology to offer a personalised jewellery discovery 
experience. Available through select Senco showrooms and the Senco mobile application, the feature 
analysis’s  facial structure and recommends suitable jewellery styles, particularly earrings and 
necklaces, based on individual face shapes.  
Senco offers an extensive range of jewellery, including gold, diamond, silver, and platinum, along with 
precious and semi -precious stones. Its vast catalogue features over 156,000 designs in gold jewellery 
and more than 86,000 designs in diamond jewellery,  showcasing a diverse portfolio of handcrafted 
pieces. Most designs are developed in -house by Senco’s designers, in close collaboration with skilled 
local karigars in Kolkata and across India.  
The company’s brand portfolio includes Everlite , focused on lightweight jewellery; Gossip , its silver 
and costume jewellery line; and Aham , catering to men. It also offers premium wedding collections such 
as Vivaha  and Rajwada , designed to celebrate timeless elegance and grandeur.
