Sub: Company statement on the U.S. Permanent Labor Certification Program  
 The recent announcement by the U.S. Department of Labor regarding the suspension of new 
PERM applications for certain companies, relates to a specific permanent residency pathway and 
is distinct from broader skilled talent mobility programmes such as the H 1B Visa. We don’t 
anticipate any material business impact from this announcement.   
  
Over the years, Wipro has pursued a deliberate localisation strategy in the U.S., with more than 
80% of our workforce in the country locally hired. We remain committed to investing in local talent, 
complying with all applicable U.S. laws and regulations, and ensuring business continuity for our 
clients.    
 This is for your information and records.  
 For additional information, visit us at www.wipro.com .
