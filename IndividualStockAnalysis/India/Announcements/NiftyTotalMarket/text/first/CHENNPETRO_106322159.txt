1.  Pursuant to General Circular No. 14/2020 dated 8th April

AGM through VC/OAVM, without the physical presence

with the Circulars, the AGM of the Company is being held 
through VC/OAVM.

entitled to appoint a proxy to attend and vote on his / 
her behalf and the proxy need not be a member of the

exemption from the requirement of appointing proxies. 
Hence for this AGM the facility for appointment of proxy 
by the members is not being provided. Accordingly, the 
proxy form, attendance slip, and the route map of the 
venue have also not been provided along with the notice. 
The members are requested to participate in the AGM in 
person through VC /OAVM from their respective location.
