Mumbai, India | October 15 , 2026  
Strong Q2FY27  Performance: PAT at ₹ 805 Cr, up 13% QoQ; AUM crosses ₹1.24 lakh Cr . 
IIFL Finance Limited (“IIFL Finance”), a leading retail -focused NBFC, today announced its financial results for the 
quarter ended September 30 , 2026, delivering strong growth, an improve ment in overall GNPA , and robust 
profitability.  
Performance Overview  
•IIFL Finance delivered a robust quarter, with consistent growth across its core businesses, supported
by its secured -lending focus, disciplined portfolio rebalancing and improving operating efficiency.
•Total income rose 23% YoY to ₹2,339.6 crore, while pre -provision operating profit increased 31%
YoY to ₹1,355.1 crore. Profit before tax rose 93% YoY to ₹1,073.7 crore, supported by operating
leverage and lower loan losses and provisions.
•For Q2FY27 , PAT (pre -NCI) stood at ₹ 805.0  Cr, marking a 93% YoY growth, highlighting the continued
strength  of the company’s secured lending strategy
Key Highlights  
➢Consolidated AUM grew to ₹1,24,268 Cr, up 8% QoQ and 38% YoY
➢Profit After Tax (pre -NCI) stood at ₹ 805.0  Cr, up 13% QoQ and 93% YoY
➢Gross NPA at 1.5% (down 7 bps QoQ)
➢Net NPA at 0.9% (up 5 bps QoQ)
➢Provision Coverage Ratio at 91%
➢Book value at ₹351.5
➢ROA at 3.1% and ROE at 20.1% (annualized for H1FY27).
➢Strong liquidity position of ₹ 13,805 Cr
➢Computed consolidated CRAR at 23.5%
Mr. Nirmal Jain, Founder & Managing Director, IIFL Finance said:  
“Q2 FY27 delivered robust growth, with AUM crossing ₹1.24 lakh crore, up 38% year -on-year, a 90% secured 
AUM  and GNPA of 1.5%. ROE above 20% reflects strong gold and mortgage franchises, lower credit costs 
and improved operating efficiency.  
As gold loan growth moderates, we expect home loans and secured MSME lending to gain momentum. We 
are strengthening risk, audit, compliance and customer service through AI, while deepening bank 
partnerships. The Fitch upgrade underscores growing confidence  in our credit profile. We expect to sustain 
resilient, capital -efficient growth through FY27 while serving underserved India .” 
Business Segment Performance  
•Gold Loans: Gold loan AUM surged to ₹ 65,729  Cr, up 90% YoY and 13% QoQ, emerging as the primary
growth driver. Yields improved marginally by 15bps QoQ. Asset quality remained healthy  with GNPA at
0.90%
•Home Finance: Total AUM stood at ₹ 43,070  Cr, up 4% QoQ.  Asset quality improved  with GNPA declining
to 1.38%, reflecting continued focus on prudent underwriting
•MSME Loans: Continued shift  towards secured MSME lending , with MSME AUM growing 11% QoQ to
₹11,948 Cr and  disciplined reduction in unsecured exposure, leading to improved risk profile
•Microfinance: Consolidated microfinance AUM stood at ₹9,489  broadly  flat QoQ  with improving asset

Key Updates  
• IIFL Finance successfully completed a ₹25 crore tokenised bond transaction, becoming the first Non -PSU 
Non -Banking Financial Company (NBFC) in India to undertake a transaction of this nature  
• Fitch Ratings upgraded IIFL Finance (IIFL) Long -Term Issuer Default Rating (IDR), senior secured debt and 
global medium -note programme (GMTN) ratings to 'BB -' from 'B+';  outlook stable  
• CRISIL  ESG Ratings has assigned an ESG rating of ‘ CRISIL  ESG 66’ and Core ESG rating of ‘ CRISIL  Core ESG 
70’ to the Company  
 
Strategic Focus  
The company continues to execute on its strategy of “Resilient, Capital -Efficient Growth” anchored on:  
• Secured lending focus (Gold + Mortgages)  
• Expansion in underserved MSME and Bharat segments  
• AI-led operating model driving productivity and risk management  
• Branch -led distribution with hyperlocal sourcing  
• Co-lending partnerships enabling capital -light growth  
• Strong capital efficiency through DA/PTC and co -lending  
 Off-book AUM continues to scale meaningfully with  cumulative DA + co -lending originations of ₹1.7 lakh Cr since 
FY14 (through Q2FY27) . 
AI Transformation   
IIFL Finance is accelerating its AI -led transformation across underwriting, collections, customer engagement, and 
cross -sell: 
• Voice AI is live in gold -loan sales, with acquisition cost below 1% of loan value in the pilot and a target of 
below 0.3% at scale.  
• AI credit assessment is now used for 85%+ of SME LAP applications to generate credit assessment 
memoranda.  
• AI video -based sales training has engaged 12,000+ sales employees through interactive, customised 
content in their own language.  
• AI-powered fraud detection has analysed over 11 lakh gold jewellery images and is implemented across 
all gold -loan branches.  
Outlook  
IIFL Finance enters the rest of FY27 with strong momentum, a robust balance sheet, and a clear strategic roadmap 
focused on:  
• Scaling secured lending franchises  
• Expanding co -lending partnerships with banks  
• Progressing the proposed equity raise, pursuant to the enabling shareholder approval received in 
July 2026  
• Maintaining superior asset quality and capital discipline

About IIFL Finance  
IIFL Finance Limited, along with its subsidiaries IIFL Home Finance and IIFL Samasta Finance, is a leading retail -
focused NBFC offering diversified loan products, including home, gold, MSME, microfinance, and capital market 
finance. With a network of 5, 091 branches, IIFL leverages a robust phygital model to serve underserved segments 
across India.
