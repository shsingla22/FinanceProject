  NSE Scrip Code: ELGIEQUIP / BSE Scrip Code: 522074 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform you that M/s. Elgi Equipments Limited, Coimbatore, 
received a notice dated March 7, 2025, from the Uttarakhand SGST Department`s mobile 
team Officer for payment of Rs.5,68,800 under penalty head for mismatch in delivery address 
between tax invoice and lorry receipt (LR) for an ex-works consignment. The Company 
immediately filed a reply on March 10, 2025, stating that the delivery address mentioned in 
invoice as well as LR are both registered premises of the Customer under GST Act, this is an 
inadvertent error and there is no intention to evade tax by the Company. But the GST Officer 
confirmed the penalty demand on March 15, 2025, and hence the intimation is being made 
today. 
 
We are enclosing herewith the details of the above as prescribed under SEBI Listing 
Regulations read with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155  dated November 11, 
2024, as Annexure A.
