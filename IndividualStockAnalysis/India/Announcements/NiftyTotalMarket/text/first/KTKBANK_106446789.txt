PRESS RELEASE 
Karnataka Bank appoints Veteran Banker Mr Raghavendra S Bhat as its New 
Managing Director & CEO 
Leading south Indian private bank - Karnataka Bank, is pleased to announce the 
appointment of Mr. Raghavendra S Bhat as the new Managing Director & CEO of the 
Bank. The appointment is effective from 16.11.2025 for a period of one year. 
With extensive expertise across various domains, Mr. Raghavendra S Bhat's 
professional journey encompasses Banking, Finance and also agriculture as his roots are 
deeply tied to an agriculturist family background. During his tenure in the Bank his 
diverse experience spans and covers all key areas of Banking and Finance, including 
HR, IT and Digital Banking, Treasury and Forex Operations, Insurances (Third Party 
products) and Rural Economy.  Having held top management positions for more than a 
decade, his leadership and vision have been pivotal in shaping strategic decisions at the 
highest levels. Karnataka Bank is confident that his leadership will drive the bank 
towards continued growth and s uccess. 
Mr. Raghavendra S Bhat while expressing happiness, assured to meet the 
expectations  of all stake holders.
