19 . CUT-OFF DATE
The cut-off for the purpose of ascertaining shareholders entitled for remote e-voting and voting at the Meeting 
is July 16, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the “Cut-off Date”). A person, whose name is recorded in the Register of 
Members or in the Register of Beneficial Owners maintained by the Depositories as on the Cut-off Date only shall 
be entitled to avail the facility of remote e-voting as well as voting at the Meeting. The voting rights of members 
shall be in proportion to their shares of the paid-up equity share capital of the Company as on the Cut-off Date. A 
person who is not a shareholder as on the cut-off date should treat this Notice for information purpose only.

not be allowed to change it subsequently. Members, who cast their vote by remote e-voting, may attend the 
Meeting through VC / OAVM, but will not be entitled to cast their vote once again on the resolutions.
