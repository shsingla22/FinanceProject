 Dear Shareholders,  
Q2FY27 marked further progress across our businesses, with improving operating momentum and tangible 
outcomes from our strategic priorities. Key highlights included sequential and year -on-year expansion in 
standalone EBITDA margins, a sustained recovery in  Domino’s India LFL growth, over 50% LFL growth at 
Popeyes, and stronger  than expected cash generation from operations in Turkey. Together, these achievements 
reflect the resilience of our businesses and the growing traction of our strategic initiatives.  
 
Consolidated revenue from operations grew 11.8% YoY to ₹2,608 crore, while EBITDA increased 7. 3% to 
₹513 crore. Standalone revenue from operations grew 11.5% YoY to ₹1,885 crore, with EBITDA rising 12.4% 
to ₹372 crore. Domino’s India recorded LFL growth of 4.1%, up from 2.5% in Q1FY27 and 0.2% in Q4FY26.  
Popeyes  delivered its fourth consecutive quarter of 40 –50% LFL growth. Across the group, JFL added 108 
stores.  
 
At Domino’s India, growth was supported by sustained momentum in Delivery and early signs of recovery in 
Dine -In and Takeaway (DITA). Product innovation across the Maxxx range, Double Cheese Burst, Cold 
Coffee and Mousse has broadened our proposition and improved  ticket -size. We remain focused on 
introducing products and formats that expand the occasions in which customers engage with the brand . 
 
Programme to turnaround the performance in DITA is gaining traction. Infrastructure upgrades have been 
completed in approximately 20% of identified DITA -heavy stores, while My Meal @ ₹119 has been rolled 
out nationally. These initiatives are laying the foundation for a more sustainable recovery in the chan nel. 
 
Popeyes is emerging as a meaningful second growth engine for JFL. With a presence across 25 cities and 
system Average Daily Sales (ADS) broadly comparable with established players in most markets, the brand 
is gaining scale and customer love . Gross margin expanded by 150 bps YoY. Our priority is to translate this 
momentum into a profitable national network, with expansion guided by store-level returns.  
 
DP Eurasia delivered healthy growth across revenue, operating profitability and PAT, with reported EBITDA 
margins above 20% and PAT margins consistently in the 8 –10% range. The business remains accretive to 
JFL’s consolidated financials.  
 
We have navigated elevated input costs through procurement efficiencies, calibrated pricing, menu -mix 
optimisation and tighter cost controls, supporting YoY gross margin expansion. Investments in supply -chain 
capacity and technology are also improving prod uctivity across stores, delivery and inventory management, 
creating the foundation for greater operating leverage . 
 
Our priorities remain clear: sustain disciplined execution in Domino’s India, scale Popeyes while improving 
unit economics, develop our international businesses and embed technology across operations. We will 
continue to allocate capital towards growth opp ortunities and revenue -generating assets, while maintaining a 
sharp focus on returns and long -term value creation.  
 
I wish all of you a very happy Diwali . 
 
Sameer Khetarpal  
CEO  & MD
