Subject: BHEL enters into Strategic Partnership Agreement with HIMA for Railway 
Signalling Business  
 
BHEL has entered into a Strategic Partnership Agreement for the Railway Signalling Business with M/s HIMA Middle East FZE, Dubai (A wholly owned subsidiary of HIMA Paul Hildebrandt GmbH, Germany).   HIMA is world’s leading solution provider for safety -related automation in the railways and 
process industries.  
 BHEL has been supplying Locomotives, Electrics for EMU/MEMU, Propulsion Systems, Traction Motors, Traction Alternators, Traction Transformers, etc. to Indian Railways. The partnership with HIMA will further enhance BHEL’s offerings to Indian Railways.
