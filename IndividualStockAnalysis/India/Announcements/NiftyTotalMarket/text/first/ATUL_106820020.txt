Clarification on treated effluent discharge from Atul site 
 
Atul, October 16, 2026 : It has come to the notice of  the Company that certain incorrect 
information and videos  concerning the discharge of effluent  from Atul site in to the Par river 
are being circulated.  
 
The Company uses a n approximately  4-km long pipeline,  laid entirely within the estuary , to 
discharge treated effluent  into the estuarine zone.  Considering the age of the existing pipeline 
and the need to mitigate potential environmental risks, the Company ha d proactively initiated 
the installation  of a new pipeline. The necessary approvals have been obtained from the 
relevant authorities  and the work is at an advanced stage.  
 
During the p eriod , accidental damage was identified in the existing pipeline at a location 
approximately 1 km from the effluent treatment plant of the Company , within the estuary. This 
resulted in the inadvertent release of treated effluent into the estuarine zone for a short period, until the damage was detected. The treated effluent released during this period co mplied with  
the prescribed standards of the Gujarat Pollution Control Board .  
 Immediately  upon detecting the damage, the Company isolated the affected section of the 
pipeline and voluntarily suspended effluent discharge through it as a precautionary measure.  
The necessary repair work has been initiated,  and a competent agency has been engaged to 
undertake the required repairs at the earliest . Discharge through the affected pipeline will 
remain suspended until the repairs are completed and the pipeline is inspected and confirmed fit for safe operation.  
 
The Company does not expect any material impact due this incident.  
 
The Company  reaffirm s its commitment to environmental protection and to complying with all 
applicable environmental laws, rules  and regulatory requirements.  
 
About the Company  
Atul Ltd  was founded on September 5, 1947, with the vision of creating wealth in rural India, 
generating large-scale employment and making India self -sufficient in selected chemicals.  
The Company  is a leading integrated chemical company in India, with annual revenue of 
approximately ₹6,500 crore. It manufactures around 900 products and 400 formulations. Its 
production facilities, along with those of its associate, joint venture and subsidiary entities, are located at Ambernath, Ankleshwar, Jodhpur, Panoli, Tarapur and Valsad in India, and Somerset in  the UK.  To serve its customers better and expand the breadth and depth of its 
business,  it has established subsidiary companies in Brazil, China, Ireland, the UAE, the UK 

and the USA.  The first site of the Company at Atul, Vals ad, Gujarat  is one of the greenest 
chemical complexes in the world.
