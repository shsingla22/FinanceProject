Air India Elevates International Travel Experience with 
Tata Communications -Powered EasyRoam Travel SIM  
 
Easy and affordable international roaming access in 30 countries  
 
 
Gurugram/ Mumbai, India , October  8, 2026  — Air India , India’s leading global airline and Tata 
Communications , a leading global communications technology player , today announce d the launch 
of EasyRoam  – an Air India -branded travel SIM, offering international roaming  service , developed in 
partnership  with Tata Communications.  
 
EasyRoam is an ancillary product available for purchase as a physical SIM as well as eSIM on a 
dedicated Air India -branded webpage. It allows Air India guests access to voice and data services on 
roaming while travelling internationally .  
 
Inaugural offer and geographical coverage  
 
In its inaugural phase of the rollout, the EasyRoam SIM is available free of cost, but roaming pack 
charges will apply. The roaming packs – available for 30 countries across North America, Europe 
(including the UK), Australia, Asia Pacific and the Middle East - start from INR 241  (excluding taxes) , 
with pric es varying by destination and plan selected.  
 
Customers purchasing a data plan can also avail up to 1GB of additional complimentary data, 
applicable in all countries except Maldives and Mauritius.  
 
The EasyRoam SIM’s geographical coverage will progressively expand to up to 190 countries  in total . 
Air India guests can choose from destination -specific or worldwide  travel plans, with options for 
data-only or voice + data connectivity, based on their travel needs.  
 
“Today ’s travellers expect travel experience  to be seamless, instant and secure ,” said Vivek 
Manglik, Executive Vice President – Interaction Fabric , Tata Communications . “Through our 
collaboration with Air India , we’re bringing  travel  connectivity into the booking journey itself, 
enabling customers to choose and activate their mobile plan before departure and go online the 
moment they arrive. Together, we’re making international connectivity simpler, more secure and 
more transparent, giving travellers greater control, visibility and peace of mind wherever they 
travel. ” 
 
Nipun Aggarwal, Chief Commercial Officer, Air India, said: “The way people travel has evolved 
over the years. Today’s travellers increasingly live, work and socialise across borders, and they 
expect uninterrupted access to the digital services that power their daily lives. EasyRoam  addresses 
this need by enabling our customers to stay connected effortlessly while travelling internationally, 
without the hassle of arranging for connectivity after arrival. It is another step in our effort to 
create a more seamless, digitally enabled tr avel experience in partnership with Tata 
Communications.”  
 
 
 
 
   
 
Simple purchase and activation process  
 
Designed as a digital -first travel connectivity service, EasyRoam  offers customers a simple activation 
process, along with greater transparency and control over their mobile usage. Travellers will receive 
timely notifications and alerts to help them monitor consumption and manage costs while travelling 
abroad. Air India customers opting for the SIM can retain their number for up to one year at a 
nominal cost.  
 
Tata Communications’ extensive global footprint and partnership with 600+ mobile network 
operators enables  travellers to access high -quality connectivity and choose mobile plans best suited 
to their destination and travel needs.  
 
Easy Roam is currently available for purchase through the Air India website and will subsequently 
be available through the Air India mobile app, offering customers a convenient way to stay 
connected while travelling internationally.  
 
 
About Air India Group  
The Air India Group – comprising full -service global airline, Air India, and value carrier, Air India Express – is 
spearheading a new era of Indian aviation. The Air India story began in 1932 when JRD Tata piloted the airline’s 
inaugural flight and opened the skies for aviation in India. Today, Air India Group employs more than 30,000 people, 
operates over 300 aircraft and carries travellers to 60 domestic and 51 international destinations across five 
continents.  
Returning to Tata Sons in 2022 following 70 years under Government ownership, Air India Group is in the midst of a 
five-year transformation programme, Vihaan.AI. As part of the transformation, Air India has placed orders for 600 
new aircraft. In addition t o taking new aircraft deliveries, Air India is progressively retrofitting all its legacy aircraft.  
The Air India Group operates South Asia’s largest aviation training academy in Gurugram, India. The construction of 
a new flying school and a greenfield maintenance base is in progress.  
With transformation underway across all facets of the business and India’s rich legacy of hospitality, Air India is 
committed to being a world class global airline with an Indian heart.
