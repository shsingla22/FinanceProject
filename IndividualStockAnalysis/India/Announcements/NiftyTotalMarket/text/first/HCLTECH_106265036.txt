HCLTech launches its second delivery center in Kerala, India  
THIRUVANANTHAPURAM, India, June 9, 2025 —HCLTech , a leading global technology 
company, today inaugurated its delivery center in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala. Located at the 
Technopark, the center will deliver projects across AI, GenAI, Cloud and emerging 
technologies to HCLTech’s clients across industry verticals.  
Sri Seeram Sambasiva Rao, IAS, Special Secretary, Electronics & IT (E&IT ), Government  of 
Kerala , Fabrizio Salvatore, Head of Infrastructure Services and Information Security, EMT 
Member, Allianz Technology, Jayant Tulsiani, Branch Head, Allianz Technology India and Sudip Lahiri, Executive Vice President and Head o f Financial Services —Europe and UKI, HCLTech,  
were present on the occasion.  
This is HCLTech’s second delivery center in Kerala, following the inauguration of the first center in Kochi last October. The company aims to cultivate innovation -driven learning a nd 
offer career opportunities for aspiring IT professionals in the region.
