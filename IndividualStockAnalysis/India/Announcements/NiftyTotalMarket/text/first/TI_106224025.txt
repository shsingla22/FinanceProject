Monarch Legacy Edition Becomes the Only  Indian Brandy to Win at the 
prestigious London Spirits Competition  2025  
The first luxury brandy from India’s largest brandy maker, Tilaknagar  Industries Ltd., earns a global win 
in its debut year  
Mumbai, May 08, 2025 – Monarch Legacy Edition by Tilaknagar Industries Ltd. (TI) has made history by 
becoming the only  Indian brandy to win at the prestigious London Spirits Competition 2025. In its debut 
year, Monarch earned a Bronze medal in the brandy category — a breakthrough moment for Indian 
brandy on the global stage and the first accolade for the country’s only 10 0% pure grape luxury brandy 
that is crafted with a blend of French and Indian grape spirits.  
Held annually in the UK, the London Spirits Competition is one of the most respected events in the global 
spirits industry. Entries are judged not just on taste, but also on packaging and value by an influential jury 
of bartenders, retail buyers, and exper ts who help shape the world’s top bars and shelves. To ensure that 
the judging process is fair and transparent, the taste and value scores are assigned through blind tasting. 
The judges only view the product packaging after all quality and value scores hav e been entered. This 
year’s edition of the competition had over 500 entries from more than 30 countries.  
Brandy is India’s second -largest category within the IMFL (Indian Made Foreign Liquor) segment, yet it has 
seen relatively little disruption or innovation over the years. That narrative is changing  with Monarch 
Legacy Edition, which has now made history as the only  Indian brandy to win at the prestigious London 
Spirits Competition 2025.  
“This is a huge win — not just for Monarch, but for Indian brandy ,” said Amit Dahanukar, Chairman & 
Managing Director, Tilaknagar Industries Ltd.  “We launched Monarch to shift the conversation around 
Indian brandy. This complex spirit has so much potential when it’s made with care — but it often does not 
get the recognition it deserves. With over forty years of expertise in brandy -making, we felt a r esponsibility 
to change that. For Monarch’s very first international showing to earn a win like this — it’s a  strong 
validation of everything we set out to do. ” 
“In a contest dominated by celebrated brandy -producing nations such as France, Spain, and Australia, 
Monarch’s recognition marks a significant milestone —not just for the brand, but for India’s evolving spirits 
landscape, particularly the brandy market. And  we’re excited to lead this shift,”  said Sanaya Dahanukar, 
Marketing Manager, Tilaknagar Industries Ltd.  
What Sets Monarch Apart  
Unlike anything else on the Indian market, Monarch is a distinctive blend of fine French grape spirits aged 
up to 8 years and carefully selected Indian grape spirits from Maharashtra’s Sahyadri region — a nod to 
local terroir. Distilled in coal -fired coppe r pot stills using traditional Cognac -style methods and matured in 
French oak ex -wine casks, it’s a spirit with depth, nuance, and serious character. Its flavour brings together 
notes of dried fruit, velvety oak, and warm spice.  
  
From the visuals to the final sip, Monarch signals luxury in every aspect. The symmetrical ridges of the 
bottle catch and refract the light, enhancing the deep amber tones of the liquid within. The golden metallic 
label, shaped like a shield, is among the few in India’s spirits industry to be crafted from metal. The regal 
bottle, inspired by royal chalices, has a metal -plated shield label and a monocarton featuring miniature 
style paintings of kings — creating a standout bottle unlike anything else in the I ndian brandy space.  
  
A New Era for Tilaknagar Industries Ltd.  
Launched in November 2024, Monarch Legacy Edition marks TI’s move into the super -luxury space, 
expanding a portfolio that already includes global success stories like Mansion House Brandy and Courrier 
Napoleon. TI is the world’s second -largest brandy maker  — and with Monarch, it is now leading the charge 
in premiumisation within the category.  
  
Tasting Notes   
• Aroma:  Rich grape and fruit notes with subtle hints of apple, nutmeg, and tobacco.  
• Palate:  Velvety sweetness balanced by creamy oak and indulgent dried fruits.  
• Finish:  Long and warm with cinnamon, peach, and clove nuances.
