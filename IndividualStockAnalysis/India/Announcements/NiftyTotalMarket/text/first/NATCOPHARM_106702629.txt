Hyderabad, India, July 18th, 2026: NATCO  Pharma Limited  (BSE:  524816  and NSE: NATCO PHARM) ("NATCO") today received 
tentative approval from  the U.S. FDA for Olaparib , 100 mg, and 150 mg, as bioequivalent to reference listed drug (RLD)  
Lynparza ® tablets  of AstraZeneca Pharmaceuticals  for the indication in the approved labelling . NATCO  will be 
manufacturing Olaparib tablets and our partner  Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited (Alembic), would be distributing the 
product in the U.S.  
The Para IV litigation is on -going. Olaparib  tablets had estimated sales of  approximately  USD 1.4 billion in the U .S. for 12 
months ending Mar ’26 as per industry sales data.
