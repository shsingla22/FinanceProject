Hindustan Zinc to be World’s Largest Zinc & Silver Producer with  Rs. 
80,000 crore expansion  
• The 3 to 5-year expansion will increase refined metal capacity to 2 MTPA and silver refinery 
capacity to 1,500 TPA, with matching mines and mills expansion  
Udaipur, November 17,  2026:  Hindustan Zinc Limited  (BSE: 500188 and NSE: HINDZINC) , a Vedanta 
Group company and the world’s largest integrated zinc producer , announced that its Board has 
approved the investment of Rs. 80,000 crore * to double metal production. This fresh investment will be 
made over the  next three to five years  and will see the Company’s zinc production capacity  double to  2 
million tonnes per annum (MTPA) , capable of independently catering to 15% of global zinc demand. The 
company will  also double silver production  capacity to 1,500 tonnes per annum (TPA).   
This investment will deepen H industan Zinc’s  contribution to Rajasthan’s mining and metals ecosystem.  
As the largest private investor in the state,  Vedanta Group has invested Rs.1.5 lakh crore in Rajasthan 
to date,  and this new investment is part of Vedanta’s additional Rs. 1 lakh crore  investment  in the state.  
This will catalyze the creation of over 2 lakh jobs and over 500 MSMEs.  
Hindustan Zinc is India’s largest and only producer of zinc, lead and silver. This expansion will make 
Hindustan Zinc the single largest zinc and silver producer in the world. This development is significant 
as it comes at the time of continued global deficit in the zinc and silver market s. Since the government 
disinvested its stake in 2002 and subsequent acquisition by Vedanta Limited, the company’s zinc 
production has grown 4 times while silver production has grown more than 20 times. The company is 
amongst the lowest cost zinc producers globally and holds the 2nd highest zinc reserves & resources 
globally with over 25 years of mine life.  
 Zinc consumption is growing rapidly as it is  essential to modern industrial  and infrastructure  
development. Through galvanization it protects steel , the world’s most widely used metal , from 
corrosion, extending its service life and supporting applications across construction, automotive, manufacturing and other sectors.  Silver is a precious metal that finds critical applications in electronics, 
semiconductors,  nanotechnology, photovoltaic cells for solar power, electric vehicles , medical 
equipment etc.  The expansion is intended to strengthen the domestic availability of zinc and silver for 
India’s rapid economic and industrial growth.  Lead is vital for energy storage systems & batteries, 
radiation shielding and defence industries.  
In addition, Hindustan Zinc’s  proposed fertiliser manufacturing capacity will also contribute to India’s 
food and fertilizer security.  Together, these plans support India’s efforts to build resilient supply chains 
and expand its manufacturing base.  
Commenting on the development, Mr. Amarendu Prakash, Chief Executive Officer, Hindustan 
Zinc Limited,  said: “HZL 2.0 is a significant next step in the Company’s growth journey and in our 
contribution to India’s industrial development. By investing in Rajasthan and expanding our integrated 
capabilities across mines, mills and smelters, we aim to strengthen domestic supply of metals that 
support infrastructure, manufacturing and the energy transition. As the plan progresses, it will also create 
opportunities for employment, local businesses and the wider industrial ecosystem.”

About Hindustan Zinc  
Hindustan Zinc Limited (BSE: 500188 and NSE: HINDZINC), a Vedanta Group company, is the world’s largest integrated zinc 
producer and is amongst the top 10 silver producers globally. The company supplies to more than 30 countries and holds a market share of  about 74 % of the primary zinc market in India. Hindustan Zinc has been recognized as the world’s most 
sustainable company in the metals and mining category for the third consecutive year by the S&P Global Corporate 
Sustainability Assessment 2025, reflecting its operational excellence, innovation, and leading ESG practices. The company 
also became the first Indian company to join the prestigious International Council on Mining & Metals (ICMM) in 2025. The company also launched EcoZen, Asia’s first low carbon ‘green’  zinc brand, produced using renewable energy. Hindustan Zinc 
is also a certified 3.32 times Water -Positive company and is committed to achieving Net Zero emissions by 2050 or sooner. 
The company is also transforming the lives of nearly 30 lakh people through its focused social welfare initiatives. As an energy 
transition metals company, Hindustan Zinc is pivotal in the journey of sourcing critical metals essential for a sustainable f uture.
