Dear Sir/Madam, 
This is in reference to the application submitted by Computer Age Management Services Limited 
(“Company”) for seeking Reclassification of Promoter Shareholders under the provisions of 
Regulation 31A of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, 
(LODR) wherein Regulation 31A(2) states: 
Regulation 31A(2) 
Re-classification of the status of any person as a promoter or public shall be permitted by the stock 
exchanges only upon receipt of an application from the listed entity along with all relevant 
documents subject to compliance with conditions specified in these regulations; 
Provided that in case of entities listed on more than one stock exchange, the concerned stock 
exchanges shall jointly decide on the application 
It has been observed that the Company made the corporate announcement on May 28, 2024 
mentioning that Company made the application to NSE on May 28, 2024. However, the company 
has submitted the application through NSE’s NEAPS portal on July 29, 2024. 
The aforesaid non-compliance on your part is viewed seriously. You are hereby advised to be 
careful in future, exercise due caution and initiate corrective steps to avoid recurrence of such 
lapses so as to ensure compliance with the Exchange circulars/guidance/communications. 
Yours faithfully, 
For National Stock Exchange of India Ltd.
