This is with reference to NSE Letter and BSE Email dated May 27, 2026, regarding 
non-compliance by REC Limited ("REC" / "the Company") with the requirements 
pertaining to the composition of Board of Directors during the quarter ended March 31, 
2026, as stipulated in the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosur~ Reguirements) 
Regulations, 2015 ("Listing Regulations") . 
As advised by the Stock Exchanges, the aforesaid matter of non-compliance was placed 
before the Board of Directors of the Company in their meeting held on June 28, 2026, 
wherein the Board noted the position of non-compliance with respect to the composition of 
the Board of Directors and the fines imposed by the Stock Exchanges related therewith. 
In this regard, the Board desired that regUlar follow-up be made with the appointing 
authority i.e. Ministry of Power, Government of India, to expedite the process of 
appointment of requisite number of Independent Directors on the Board of REC. 
Further, we would like to submit that, being a Government Company and as per Articles of 
Association of the Company , inter-alia the power to appoint Independent Directors on the 
Board of the Company is vested with President of India acting through administrative 
Ministry i.e. Ministry of Power, Government of India and the Company has no -role in the 
appointment of Directors. Accordingly, the fines so imposed on the Company by the stock 
exchanges may be waived off.
