SUBJECT: UPDATE ON ACQUI SITION OF  CHEMMAN LABS PRIVATE  LIMITED  
 
REF.: INTIMATION  DATED 02 AUGUST 2024  
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
We refer to our earlier intimation dated August 02 , 2024 , for acquisition of 100% stake in Chemman Labs Private 
Limited . 
 
It had been  previously communicated that the transaction was expected to achieve closure by September 30, 
2024 .  
 
While the ownership of Chemman by way of transfer of shares and payment of consideration against it has been 
completed , the closure still awaits the receipt of some documents, agreed for the effective closure of the 
transaction .  
 
The remaining documents are purely incidental to the transaction and merely await their release from applicable 
Government departments. Their absence does not in any manner impede the use of the manufacturing facility 
acquired in terms of the transaction . The signing parties have  therefore  mutually agreed to extend the closure 
timeline to October 31, 2024 .  
 
Thanking You .
