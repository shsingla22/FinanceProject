TVS SCS doubles PBT to ₹50.6  Cr in Q2 FY27 as revenue grows 3 8% 
~ EBITDA up 50% to ₹268 .1 Cr; PBT rises  108% and PAT increases  124% to ₹3 6.5 Cr. 
~  EBITDA  margin expand ed by 60 bps  to 7. 3% from  6.7%  
 
Chennai, 13th November , 202 6: TVS Supply Chain Solutions Limited (NSE: TVSSCS, B SE: 
543965), a global supply chain solutions provider and one of the largest and fastest growing 
integrated supply chain solutions providers in India, today announced its consolidated 
unaudited financial results for the  quarter  ended 30th September , 2026  and  the first half of 
FY2027 .  
 
The company delivered a strong performance  across all geographies in Q2 FY27, with 
consolidated revenue from operations growing 38% year -on-year to ₹3,687.2 crore  compared to 
₹2,662.6 crore in Q2 FY26. The company also recorded strong sustainable improvement in 
operating profitability, with EBITDA increasing 50 .2% year -on-year to ₹268.1 crore. EBITDA 
margin improved to 7. 3%, compared to 6.7% in Q2 FY26, reflecting the improved operating 
performance.   
 
Profit before tax (PBT) increased 1 08% to ₹50.6 crore, compared to ₹2 4.4 crore in Q2 FY26.  Profit 
after tax (PAT) more than doubled to ₹3 6.5 crore, over ₹16.3 crore in the corresponding quarter 
last year.  
 
For the first half of FY27, consolidated revenue grew 33.6% to ₹7,022.4 crore, compared to 
₹5,254.9 crore in H1 FY26. EBITDA increased 42.2% to ₹500.3 crore, with margins improving to 
7.1% from 6.7% in the corresponding period last year. PBT for H1 FY27 st ood at ₹82.7 crore, 
compared to ₹ 43.2  crore in H1 FY26 , a growth of 91.4%.  
 
Key highlights – Q2 FY27 : 
• ISCS revenue  grew 24.8% to ₹248 7.9 Cr while GFS revenue  increased 79.1% to ₹1199 .3 
Cr  
• Margin improvement:  ISCS EBITDA margin expanded 50 bps to 9.2% from 8.7%, while 
GFS EBITDA margin improved 1 70 bps to 3.9% from 2. 2% 
• India  Geography  business:  Revenue grew by 64.1% to ₹1,177.4 Cr  vs ₹  717.4 Cr   
• Rest of the World revenue  grew 29% YoY  
• Visibility:  Order Pipeline for the quarter  continued to grow  to reach ₹ 8,200 Cr  
• Business momentum:  Revenue from new business at ₹ 523  Cr 
Commenting on the Q 2 FY2 7 results, Mr. Vikas Chadha, Managing Director, TVS Supply Chain 
Solutions  said, “Our stellar Q2 performance  is the direct result of the growth and optimi sation 
initiatives that we have championed over the past several quarters. Our revenue growth has been 
broad -based across all regions and segments, driven by strong momentum in both new and 
existing accounts. Notably, we achieved a hat -trick  of surpassing ₹500 Cr in quarterly new 
business revenue, alongside substantial expansion within our existing  client base .” 
 
 
He further added,  “Central to this success is our reimagined and institutionalised ‘TVS SCS 
Framework ’, which aligns our teams around four core pillars: business development, 
operational rigo ur, technology innovation and customer satisfaction. Backed by continuously 
growing business development pipeline of ₹8,200 Cr and a steady conversion rate, we are 
targeting over 20% revenue growth across both segments. Strong growth  in ISCS, structural 
enhancements driving GFS profitability and our ability to deliver tech -led solutions put us firmly 
on track to double our profits in FY27. ” 
 Mr. R. Vaidhyanathan, Global Chief Financial Officer, TVS Supply Chain Solutions , said, " Our 
Q2 FY27 result s reinforce the improvement in the quality of our earnings, with consolidated 
EBITDA increasing 50% Y -o-Y to ₹268 crore and EBITDA margin expanding by 60 bps to 7.3%  
driven by consistent execution and tighter cost control across regions . This improvement reflects 
consistent execution , procurement efficiency  and tighter cost control across regions. With 34% 
growth in topline and margin expansion across both segments in H1 FY27, we remain confident 
of accelerat ing profitable growth and  strengthening the long -term fundamentals of the business. ”

About TVS Supply Chain Solutions  
Promoted by the erstwhile TVS Group and now part of TVS Mobility Group, TVS Supply Chain 
Solutions  Limited  (“TVS SCS” or “Company”) is an India -based multinational company that 
pioneered the development of the supply chain solutions marke t in  India.  For the last two 
decades, TVS SCS has managed large and complex supply chains across multiple industries in 
India and in key global markets through customised tech -enabled solutions. The Company’s 
customers span across numerous industries such as automotiv e, industrial, consumer, tech 
and tech infra, rail and utilities, and healthcare.  
 
The company operates in 26 countries with over 16,500 employees and works with 100 Fortune 
Global 500 customers.
