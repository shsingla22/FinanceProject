BLS International Reports  Strong Q2 & H1 FY27  Performance ;  
Q2FY27 Revenue Grows to Rs. 97 3 Crores , the Highest Ever  
 
H1FY27 Revenue up by 28. 7% at Rs. 1,86 3 Crores  
EBITDA grew  by 20.0% YoY to Rs. 500 Crores  in H1FY27  
 
New Delhi,  03rd November  2026:  BLS International Services Limited , a leading global AI and 
tech -enabled services partner for governments and citizens,  announced its unaudited  
consolidated financial results for the quarter  and half year ended 3 0th  September  2026. 
BLS International continued its strong growth trajectory in Q2 and H1 FY27, delivering its 
highest -ever quarterly revenue of ₹973 crore in Q2 FY27. The company recorded a 28.7% year -
on-year increase in revenue to ₹1,863 crore during H1 FY27, while EBITDA grew by 20% to ₹500 
crore, reflecting sustained business momentum and strong operational performance.  
During the quarter, the company announced the strengthening of its AI -led technology 
ecosystem through investmen ts in automation, secure infrastructure and strategic 
partnerships. These initiatives are driving operational efficiencies, reinforcing data security and 
enhancing the applicant experience.  
BLS International achieved a significant milestone by surpassing 5 00 million applications 
processed across its Visa, Consular and Citizen Services businesses worldwide. During the 
quarter, the company was also appraised at CMMI Level 5, supported by internationally 
recognised ISO certifications and robust governance fram eworks.  
Speaking on continuing growth momentum , Mr. Shikhar Aggarwal, Joint Managing Director, BLS 
International Services Ltd. said:  
“We are pleased to report another quarter of strong growth across both our business verticals. 
In Q2 FY27, Revenue from Operations grew 3 2.0% YoY to Rs. 97 3 Crores, EBITDA increased 
16.6% to Rs. 24 8 Crores, and PAT grew 8.6% to Rs. 20 2 Crores. For H1 FY27, Revenue grew 28. 7% 
YoY to Rs. 1,86 3 Crores, EBITDA increased 20.0% to Rs. 500 Crores, and PAT grew 1 0.0% to Rs. 
403 Crores. While EBITDA margins expanded across both business segments, a shift in the 
segment mix resulted in a slight moderation in the consolidated EBITDA margin.  
Our Visa & Consular Services business maintained steady momentum, with Q2 revenue growing 
11.2% YoY to Rs. 5 10 Crores and EBITDA also increasing 11.2 % to Rs. 2 14 Crores, with a healthy 
EBITDA margin of 4 1.9%. Despite geopolitical and travel -related disruptions in certain markets, 
our diversified global presence and operating capabilities supported th e performance.  
During the period, we continued to strengthen our global platform. BLS now operates across 
100+ countries and achieved the milestone of processing 500 million applications globally 
across Visa, Consular and Citizen Services.  BLS

About BLS International Services Limited:  
BLS International Services Ltd. is a trusted global tech -enabled services partner for governments and 
citizens, having an impeccable reputation for setting benchmarks in the domain of visa, passports, 
consular, citizen, e -governance, attestation, biometric, e -visa, and retail services since 2005.  
The company is recognized as “India ’s Most Valuable Companies ” by Business Today Magazine, “Best 
under a Billion ’ Company ” by Forbes Asia and  ranked among “Fortune India ’s Next 500 companies ”. The 
company works with over 46 client governments, including Diplomatic Missions, Embassies, and 
consulates, and leverages technology and processes that ensure data security. The Company now has 
an extensive network of more than 50,000+ centers globally, with a robust  strength of over 60,000+ 
employees and associates that provide consular, biometrics, and citizen services.  
BLS International is certified with CMMI DEV ML5 V2.0 & SVC ML5 V2.0, ISO 9001:2015 for Quality 
Management Systems, ISO 27001:2013 for Information Security Management Systems, ISO 14001:2015 
for Environmental Management Systems, and more.  
BLS International is the only listed company in this domain with operations in over 70 countries.  BLS
