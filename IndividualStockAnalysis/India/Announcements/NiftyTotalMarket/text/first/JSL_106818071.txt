Jindal Stainless deploys electric trucks for the first time, strengthens green 
mobility initiatives  
 
• Company’s phased green mobility programme spans electric, LNG and CNG vehicles, with expected 
CO₂ savings of 71 tonnes per month  against diesel -based transportation . 
 
National, October 1 5, 2026 : Jindal Stainless, India’s leading stainless steel manufacturer, has deployed 10 
dedicated electric trucks for finished goods transportation for the first time, marking a significant step in 
its green mobility initiatives and broader decarbonisation efforts . The electric trucks will operate on routes 
connecting the company’s Jajpur plant with Kharagpur and Kolkata, as part of its phased transition 
towards electric and alternative -fuel mobility solutions across logistics operations.  
 
The deployment builds on the company’s ongoing adoption of alternative -fuel vehicles across key 
transportation corridors. Jindal Stainless currently operates 80 LNG trucks for finished goods 
transportation from its Jajpur and Hisar facilities to western In dia, alongside beginning of deployment of 
CNG trucks to transport import cargo from Dhamra to Jajpur. The company has also deployed 60 CNG 
buses for employee transportation at its Hisar and Jajpur facilities.

Commenting on the initiative, Managing Director, Jindal Stainless, Mr Abhyuday Jindal, said, “We 
believe that responsible manufacturing must extend beyond our plants to the entire value chain, including 
the way we transport our products. The deployment of electric trucks marks an important milestone in our 
efforts to decarbonise  logistics and integrate lower -emission transportation solutions into our operations. 
Our approach is focused on adopting practical, scalable solutions that reduce our environmental footprint 
while maintaining operational efficiency. We will continue to ad vance such initiatives as part of our 
commitment to  ESG goals. ” 
 
Together, these green mobility initiatives are expected to facilitate the movement of approximately 8,000 -
10,000 tonnes of cargo per month using electric and alternative  fuel vehicles. The company plans to 
progressively expand the deployment of these solutions across suitable logistics routes, taking into 
account the operational requirements of different transportation corridors.  
 
Based on a modelled monthly green dispatch volume of 8,000 -9,000  tonnes  and an equivalent truck travel 
distance of approximately 240,000 kms , the combined transportation mix of electric, CNG and LNG 
vehicles is expected to reduce emissions by approximately 71 tonnes CO₂ per month . This represent s a 
reduction of around 2 6% compared with diesel -based transportation under regular operating conditions.  
The green mobility programme complements Jindal Stainless’ broader environmental initiatives, which 
include enhancing energy efficiency, increasing the use of renewable energy and strengthening resource 
circularity across its operations.  
 
About Jindal Stainless  
India’s leading stainless steel manufacturer, Jindal Stainless, had an annual turnover of INR 42,95 5 crore 
(USD 4. 86 billion) in FY2 6 and an annual melt capacity of 4.2 million tonnes . It has 16 stainless steel 
manufacturing and processing facilities in India and abroad, including in Spain and Indonesia, and a 
worldwide network in 12 countries, as of March 202 6. In India, there are ten sales offices and six service 
centres, as of March 202 6. The company’s product range includes stainless steel slabs, blooms, coils, 
plates, sheets, precision strips, wire rods, rebars, blade steel, and coin blanks.  
 
Jindal Stainless relies on its integrated operations to enhance its cost competitiveness and operational 
efficiency. Founded in 1970, Jindal Stainless continues to be inspired by a vision for innovation and 
enriching lives and is committed to social respon sibility.  
 
Jindal Stainless remains focused on a greener and sustainable future. The company manufactures stainless 
steel using electric arc furnace, a process that significantly reduces greenhouse gas emissions and allows 
for recyclability of scrap without compromis ing on quality.
