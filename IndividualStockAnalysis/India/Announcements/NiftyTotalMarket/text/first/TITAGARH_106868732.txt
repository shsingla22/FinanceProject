Titagarh Rail Systems  bags new order from JSW for 181 BFNSM1 wagons  and 3 BVCM wagons . 
Gets approval to manufacture auto carrier wagons expanding its product offering  
 
Kolkata, 23 November, 2026:  Titagarh Rail Systems Limited has further strengthened its position in the freight 
wagon industry by bagging an order from JSW(South) Rail Logistics Pvt Ltd.  for INR. 77.09 crs.   
In addition , it received route clearance from Indian railways to manufacture and despatch specialised auto carrier 
wagons which enable Titagarh to become one of the few wagon manufacturers to have the capability of 
producing all kind of Fr eight wagons from pressurised tank wagons to carry gas and petroleum products to 
carrying all kind of automobiles.  
The approval from the  railway authorities for the operation and movement of auto carrier  wagons connects  its 
Bharatpur Manufacturing Unit to the Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Limited (DFCCIL) network .  
Automobile movement on rail has caught momentum recently and the market of auto carriers has been very 
buoyant over the last few months. In absence of this approval, Titagarh was unable to address this growing 
market seg ment in absence of this approval which Titagarh plans to get very actively engaged in doing based on 
the approval. Titagarh has also started taking steps to manufacture a prototype wagon for these auto carriers in 
order to offer shorter delivery timelines to the customers . 
Commenting on the development, Shri Umesh Chowdhary, Vice Chairman & Managing Director, Titagarh Rail 
Systems Limited, said:  
“The introduction of ACT wagons into our freight rolling stock portfolio marks another significant step towards 
broadening our product offerings and strengthening our capabilities in specialised freight solutions.  With the 
approval for movement from our Bharatpur Manufacturing Unit to the DFCCIL network now in place, we are 
better positioned to enhance our manufacturing and delivery capabilities and contribute more meaningfully to 
the DFC network. We believe this wi ll further strengthen the freight rail ecosystem, support greater efficiency  in 
freight movement and contribute  towards the national target of achieving 3,000 MT freight loading by 2027.  
This development also reinforces our commitment to the vision of Make in India and Aatmanirbhar Bharat. We 
remain committed to supporting the Government’s vision through the development and delivery of innovative, 
reliable and indigenously manufactured rol ling stock solutions that contribute to the continued growth and 
modernisation of India’s railway and freight infrastructure.”
