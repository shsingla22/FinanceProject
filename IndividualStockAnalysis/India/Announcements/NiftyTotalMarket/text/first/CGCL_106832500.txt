Business and Earnings Performance  
 
Strong quarterly momentum continues; Consolidated A UM rises to Rs 43,878 crores  
 CGCL sustained its strong growth momentum in 2QFY27, with consolidated A UM rising 6 2% YoY and 
9% QoQ to Rs 43,878 crores. Growth remained broad- based across its secured lending businesses, 
led by Gold Loans at Rs 21,004 crores, up 102% YoY, Construction Finance at Rs 7,5 34 crores, up 49% 
YoY, Affordable Housing at Rs 8,325 crores, up 39% YoY, and MSME Loans at Rs 7,016 crores, up 25% YoY. The Gold Loan portfolio represented approximately 48% of consolidated A UM at quarter -end.  
  Profitability scales to a new high; quarterly PAT increases 77% YoY  
 CGCL reported its highest -ever quarterly PAT of Rs 4 18 crores in 2QFY27, an increase of 77% YoY and 
18% QoQ, supported by strong portfolio growth, healthy lending spreads and operating leverage. Net Interest Income rose 69% YoY to Rs 812 crores, while pre -provision operating profit increased 
84% YoY to Rs 636 crores. Yield on net advances remained at 17. 5% and cost of borrowings stood at 
9.2%, resulting in a spread of 8 .3%, compared with 6.9% a year earlier. The improvement in profitability 
lifted RoAE to 2 1.6% from 14.4% in 2QFY26  hence improvement of 7 16 bps and RoAA to 4.4% from 
4.0% registering growth of 4 1 bps. 
 
1HFY27 performance demonstrates sustained earnings momentum  
For 1 HFY27, CGCL reported Net Interest Income of Rs 1,549 crores, up 74% YoY, and non -interest 
income of Rs 4 87 crores, up 3 1% YoY. Operating profit grew 78% YoY to Rs 1, 168 crores, while PAT 
increased 88% YoY to Rs 7 72 crores. The cost -to-income ratio improved to 42.6 % from 48.1% in 
1HFY26, reflecting stronger operating leverage. 1H FY27 RoAE stood at 20. 4%, reinforcing the 
Company’s progress on sustainable profitability.  
 
Non -interest income strengthens earnings diversification  
 
Non -interest income grew 34% YoY and 2 8% QoQ to Rs 273 crores in 2QFY27, contributing 
approximately 25% of net total income. The growth reflects increasing contributions from the Company’s asset -light distribution businesses and other fee -based activities, complementing its core 
lending operations and support ing a diversified earnings profile.  Insurance distribution income 
increased 82% YoY to Rs 51 crores, supported by cross- selling opportunities across the growing 
customer base and partnerships with 23 insur ance companies . Net car loan distribution income grew 
35% YoY to Rs 34 crores, while loan originations through the car loan distribution platform rose 27% YoY to approximately Rs 3,601 crores during the  quarter. Co -lending/direct assignment income stood 
at Rs 72 crores, with co -lending/direct assignment A UM representing 20.7% of consolidated A UM. 
 Operating leverage improves as distribution network expands  
The Company expanded its branch network to 1,609 locations in  2QFY27, up from 1,433 in the 
preceding quarter . The branch network now comprises of  1,154 Gold Loan branches and 455 Non-
Gold branches. As the network scales, productivity gains and targeted technology integration continue to support operating efficiency. The cost -to-income ratio improved sharply to 41.4% in 
2QFY27 from 49.4% in 2QFY26 and 43.9% in 1QFY27, while operating profit grew 84% YoY to Rs 63 6 
crores.   
Asset quality remains manageable with disciplined risk oversight  
 Gross Stage 3 assets stood at 1.2%  of the loan book at the end of 2QFY27, compared with 1.3% a year 
earlier and 1.1% in the preceding quarter. Net Stage 3 assets stood at 0. 8%, versus 0.7% in 2QFY26 
and 0.6% in 1QFY27. Credit costs, including write -offs and ECL provisions, were Rs 79 crores for the 
quarter, equivalent to approximately 0. 8% of average total assets on an annualised basis. Stage 3 
provision coverage stood at 3 3.3%. The Company remains focused on prudent underwriting, 
monitoring and collections as the portfolio expands.  
 
Robust Capital Position and Liquidity  
 
CGCL maintained a strong capital position with a standalone capital adequacy ratio of 2 2.7% as at the 
end of  2QFY27. Consolidated total equity increased 19% YoY to Rs 7,9 58 crores, while consolidated 
borrowings stood at Rs 30,489 crores. Debt -to-equity remained comfortable at 3.8x. Cash and bank 
balances of Rs 2,835 crores, alongside diversified funding sources, provide liquidity to support the Company’s continued growth.  
 Managing Director Mr. Rajesh Sharma Commented:

for our stakeholders.”  
 
 
About Capri Global Capital Ltd:  
 Capri Global Capital Limited (“Capri Loans”) is a well -diversified retail -focused Non -Banking Financial 
Company listed on the BSE and NSE. As of September 30, 2026, Capri Loans had consolidated A UM 
of approximately Rs 43,878 crores and operated through a network of 1,609 branches with over 
15,400 employees across India. The Company offers secured and collateralised loans across four principal lending segments – Gold Loans, MSME Loans, Construction Finance and Affordable Housing 
Loan s, the latter through its wholly owned subsidiary, Capri Global Housing Finance Limited 
(“CGHFL”). Its fee -based businesses include insurance distribution through a corporate agency licence 
and car loan distribution through Capri Loans Car Platform Private Limited (“CLCPPL”).
