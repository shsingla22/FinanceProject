Commenting on the Business Outlook, Ms. Suprita Reddy, MD & CEO said   
“I am pleased to share that we have sustained our strong growth momentum during Q2 FY27, delivering 26.7% year-
on-year revenue growth, supported by 16.5% volume growth. Our existing centres grew by approximately 20%, while 
newer centres continued to ramp up well. Our core markets maintained strong momentum, while Pune has been 
witnessing a meaningful turnaround with consistent double-digit growth since few quarters.  
We continue to see encouraging momentum in West Bengal, as our brand awareness strengthens. We are 
particularly pleased with the response in Tier-II markets such as Krishnanagar and Diamond Harbour. 
On the expansion front, we have commissioned 2 hubs and 7 spokes so far during FY27. Looking ahead, we expect to 
commission another 3 hubs & 7 spokes in H2 FY27, followed by 3 additional hubs in Q1 FY28, further strengthening 
our network and expanding our presence across key markets.

During the quarter, we entered into Business Transfer Agreement to acquire Arya Wellness Centre in Guwahati, 
Assam. This marks our entry into the North-East region and is an important step towards expanding our geographical 
presence beyond our existing markets. We believe this acquisition provides us with a strong platform to establish our 
presence in Guwahati and gradually explore further growth opportunities across the North-East, leveraging our 
expertise in integrated diagnostics and operational excellence. 
We remain committed to strengthening our presence across key markets, investing in advanced technology and 
delivering high-quality diagnostic services to support sustainable long-term growth.” 
 
About Us: 
Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Limited (Vijaya), is one of India’s largest integrated diagnostic chains, providing one-stop 
solution for pathology and radiology investigations through an extensive operational network spanning 165+ 
diagnostic centres and 29 reference laboratories in 27 cities and towns spread across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, 
Maharashtra, Karnataka, NCR and West Bengal. The company offers comprehensive diagnostic services ranging from 
basic pathology to high-end radiology.
