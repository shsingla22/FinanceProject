The impugned Order pertained to matters relating to industry wide issues of: - 
 
1. non-payment of GST on the Co -insurance premium accepted as follower in case of 
coinsurance transactions wherein 100% GST was paid by leader on behalf of follower 
during the period July 2017 to March 2022, and  
2. non-payment of GST on re -insurance commission accepted on the reinsurance premium 
cede d to various reinsurance companies during the period July 2017 to March 2022  
 
The Hon’ble Court directed the adjudicating authority to reassess its order dated December 31, 
2023.

Adjudicating Authority, CGST Commissionerate, Faridabad  (Adjudicating Authority) on September 
30, 2025 confirming GST demand of ₹ 35,92,08,706/ -, a penalty of ₹ 3,59,20,871/ - and applicable 
interest for the period from July 2017 to March 2022  raised in impugned order . The Company
