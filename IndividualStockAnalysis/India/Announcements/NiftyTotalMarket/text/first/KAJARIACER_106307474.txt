KAJARIA CERAMICS LIMITED                                                    3 of 17                                      INVESTOR UPDATE  |  Q1 FY26In Q1 FY26, overall market demand continued to remain soft.
 
Our consolidated revenue for the quarter stood at ₹ 1104 
crores, indicating a 1% yoy decline compared to the 
corresponding period last year mainly due to very low growth 
in tiles volume and decline in ply sales due to closer of this 
division.
However, the margins have improved significantly in Q1 FY26 
to 16.72% as compared to 15% in Q1 FY25.
This margin improvement was driven by our focus on cost 
reduction; optimization of human resources and other 
corrective measures being undertaken by the company. We 
are confident that our efforts will yield more efficiency as we 
move forward.
