The single bench of Hon’ble High Court of Delhi has allowed Petition under Section 34 of the 
Arbitration and Conciliation Act , 1996 filed by NTPC Limited (NTPC) against the Arbitration Award 
won by Jindal ITF Ltd. (JITF) . JITF is a subsidiary of Jindal SAW Ltd.  
 
An Arbitral Tribunal comprising of three members, out of which two were retired Supreme Court Judges 
and one  was retired High Court Judge , had passed a unanimous Arbitration Award in favour of JITF.   
 
The Hon’ble High Court  of Delhi , however, has protected JITF from any adverse action by NTPC till 
an appeal is filed  by JITF against this order .  Therefore, this order has no adverse financial impact on 
JITF.   
 
JITF,  based on expert legal advise , will appeal this order before the Division  Bench of High Court of 
Delhi.
