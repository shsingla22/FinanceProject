1. Mr. Vaibhav Agwan  will cease to be Execution Unit Head (Competence Hub )

Mr. Naha is a seasoned business and digital transformation leader with over  25 years of experience  
in Digital Products & Solutions, Engineering, Project Management, Manufacturing Operations,  
Supply Chain and Services across the energy sector.  Mr. Naha has been associated with the

functions  in India and abroad . As Business Segment Head  - Digital and Innovation, h e currently leads 
digital teams and drives the Digital Community for Execution Units in the India region.  Mr. Naha holds 
a B.E. (Hons)  in Mechanical Engineering  from Birla Institute of Technology .

Siemens group . 
 
4. Mr. Ganesh Nadgouda , who is currently  General Manager  - Plant Operations  (Grid Technologies - 
Transformer)  of the  Company , will additionally act as the interim Execution Unit Head  
(Grid Technologies - Transformer ) with effect from April  1, 2026, till further announcement.   
Brief profile  of Mr. Nadgouda is as under:  
 
Mr. Nadgouda  is a seasoned Manufacturing and Operations leader with over 30 years of experience 
across Plant Operations, Supply Chain and Business Unit Management in the power and energy 
sector.  Mr. Nadgouda  has been associated with the Company  / Siemens group since  2018 . 
Mr. Nadgouda  holds a Diploma in Management Studies from the University of Mumbai and a  
B.E. in Production Engineering  from VJTI, Mumbai .
