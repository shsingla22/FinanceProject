Press Release 
 
NTPC Green Energy Limited inks MoU with Nxtra Data Limited for development of RE 
Projects 
 
NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL), a subsidiary of NTPC Limited, signed a Memorandum 
of Understanding (MoU) with Nxtra Data Limited (Nxtra) on 24th March’2026 at NTPC 
Bhawan, New Delhi. The MoU was signed by Sh. Sarit Maheshwari, CEO (NGEL & NTPC 
REL) and Sh. Ashish Arora, CEO (Nxtra).  
 
 
 
The MoU aims to explore business opportunities of mutual interest towards development of 
Renewable Energy Projects for the supply of RE RTC power to Nxtra Datacenters on PAN 
India basis for their captive consumption.
