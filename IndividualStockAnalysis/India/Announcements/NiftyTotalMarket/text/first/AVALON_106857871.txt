ANNEXURE  
 
Re-classification request received from  Mr. Bhaskar Srinivasan, Promoter and the Promoter Group Trusts  
 
Further to our intimation dated November 03, 2026, the Board at its meeting held  today, i.e.  on November 12, 
2026, considered the request letter dated November 02, 2026, received from Mr. Bhaskar Srinivasan, Promoter of 
the Company, seeking reclassification of himself and the promoter group (collectively, the “Applicants”) from 
the “Promoter/Prom oter Group” category to the “Public” category under Regulation 31A of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (the  “SEBI Listing Regulations ”). 
 
The shareholding of Mr. Bhaskar Srinivasan and the Trusts forming part of his Promoter Group, as on the date of 
the Board Meeting, is set out below:  
 
Shareholder  No. of Shares  % 
Bhaskar Srinivasan  49,34,120  7.38%  
BBS Family Trust  6,84,475  1.02%  
KBS Family Trust  6,84,475  1.02%  
Dolphin Family Trust  1,000  0.00%  
TOTAL  63,04,070  9.42%  
 
The Board considered the contents of the request letter and the rationale set out therein and  took note of the 
confirmation provided by the Applicants that BBS Family Trust, KBS Family Trust, Dolphin Family Trust and 
Mr. Bhaskar Srinivasan, individually and collectively, hold less than 10% (ten per cent) of the voting rights in the 
Company. The Boa rd further noted that neither the said Trusts nor Mr. Bhaskar Srinivasan are involved in the 
management or day -to-day affairs of the Company or exercise control, directly or indirectly, over the affairs of 
the Company.  
 
The Board also noted the undertakings furnished by the Applicants confirming  that they, along with the persons 
related to them (as defined under Regulation 31A(1)(b) of the SEBI Listing Regulations ): 
 
(a) together, do not hold more than 10% (ten percent) of the total voting rights in the Company;  
(b) do not exercise control over the affairs of the Company, directly or indirectly;  
(c) do not have any special rights with respect to the Company through formal or informal arrangements, including 
through any shareholder agreements;  
(d) are not represented on the board of directors (including by way of any nominee director) of the Company;  
(e) do not act as a key managerial person in the Company;  
(f) are not a “wilful defaulter” as per the Reserve Bank of India Guidelines; and  
(g) are not a fugitive economic offender.  
 
Taking into consideration the above and the undertakings furnished by the Applicants, the Board approved the 
reclassification request, subject to compliance with the applicable requirements of Regulation 31A of the SEBI 
Listing Regulations and receipt of t he requisite approvals.  
 
The Board further noted the Applicants’ undertaking to continue to comply with the applicable conditions under 
Regulation 31A  of the SEBI Listing Regulations following reclassification.  
 
Accordingly, the Board approved the  request  of Mr. Bhaskar Srinivasan, and the promoter group trusts  for 
reclassification, subject to compliance with the requirements under Regulation 31A of the SEBI Listing 
Regulations . The above matters, including the deliberations of the Board and the approvals granted in this regard, 
shall also be appropriately recorded in the minutes of the meeting in accordance with the provisions of the 
Companies Act, 2013, the rules made thereunder  and the applicable provisions of the SEBI Listing Regulations.
