Press Release  
 
RITES & SAIL sign MoU for loco  
leasing & maintenance services  
 
Gurugram, February 02, 2026:  RITES Limited, the leading Transport Infrastructure Consultancy and 
Engineering firm, today signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Steel Authority of India 
Limited (SAIL), a CPSE under the Ministry of Steel, Government of India. The MoU provides a 
framework for collaboration to support SAIL’s plants and mines through specialised railway services, 
including wet leasing of locomotives and higher schedule maintenance of SAIL-owned diesel-electric 
locomotives.  
 
 
Officials of RITES and SAIL at the MoU-signing ceremony  
Under the MoU, RITES will leverage its expertise to provide locomotive leasing with operation and 
maintenance (O&M) support, along with higher schedule maintenance of diesel-electric locomotives. 
The collaboration aims at enhancing efficiency, reliability, and safety of SAIL’s in-house railway 
systems, while enabling SAIL to focus on its core steel production operations.  
With extensive experience in railway operations, maintenance, and locomotive leasing, RITES has 
been supporting SAIL plants for over two decades. The MoU formalises a long-term engagement to 
strengthen rail-based logistics critical to SAIL’s expanding steel production capacity.
