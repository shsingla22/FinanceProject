Star Health Insurance Organises “Har Ghar Star” Bike Rally in Ahilyanagar on Independence Day  
Ahilyanagar, August 18 , 2025: Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited (Star Health Insurance), India’s 
largest retail health insurance provider, successfully hosted the “Har Ghar Star” Bike Rally in Ahilyanagar on 15th 
August 2025. The rally began from the Star Health Insuranc e Ahilyanagar branch at 7:30 a.m. and covered key routes 
across the city. More than 250 riders, including employees, agents, and local residents, took part, creating a striking 
visual presence that drew enthusiastic responses from people along the way. The  event was flagged off by Prof. (CA) 
Dnyanesh Kulkarni, Editor at Daily Samachar, Professor at I.M.S.D.R, and Former Chairman of Ahmednagar Branch 
of ICAI, in the presence of Dr. Ravindra Somani, MD (Med), Saideep Hospital, and Mr. Vitthal Landage, Chief E ditor 
at Daily Lok Awaj. 
The rally celebrated the spirit of Independence Day while promoting health awareness in the community. Riders from 
different parts of the city, each wearing the Star Health colours and carrying the national tricolour, rode with the  
message of good health and the spirit of freedom. Volunteers distributed brochures and flyers with information on 
health insurance plans, benefits, and claim processes, helping raise awareness about the importance of financial 
protection for medical needs . 
Himanshu Walia, CMO, Star Health and Allied Insurance , said, “The rally was a celebration of our Independence 
Day and a reminder that good health is the f oundation of true freedom. It was heartening to see such strong 
participation from the community and  the enthusiasm to learn more about health protection. Health insurance plays a 
vital role in ensuring that families are financially prepared for medical needs, and spreading that awareness is 
essential.” 
The rally drew an active response from the communit y, with residents lining the streets to watch and interact with the 
riders. Many accepted brochures and engaged in discussions on the need for health insurance, while some shared 
personal experiences about managing medical expenses. These interactions help ed make the conversation around 
health protection more relatable and encouraged greater awareness.  
The “Har Ghar Star” Bike Rally added a sense of celebration to Independence Day in Ahilyanagar while delivering 
an important message on health protection. Th e strong turnout, enthusiastic participation, and warm community 
response made the event a memorable step in Star Health Insurance’s ongoing efforts to promote awareness and access 
to quality healthcare.
