Mr. Surinder Kumar Negi Appointed as Chief Operating Officer of KPI Green Energy 
Limited  
Surat, March 11, 2025 :  KPI Green Energy Ltd., a leading player in India's renewable energy 
sector, is pleased to announce the appointment of Mr. Surinder Kumar Negi  as its Chief 
Operating Officer (COO) . Mr. Negi brings over four decades of extensive experience in the power 
transmission, renewable energy, and infrastructure development sectors , having held 
leadership roles at NTPC, POWERGRID, ALSTOM, Reliance Energy, Gujarat Energy 
Transmission Corporation (GETCO), and Skipperseil Ltd.  
With a proven track record in power transmission, renewable integration, and project 
execution , Mr. Negi has been instrumental in shaping India's energy infrastructure. As Managing 
Director of Gujarat Energy Transmission Corporation (GETCO), he added 830 new substations 
and 21,300 km of transmission lines, expanding Gujarat’s power grid to support over 23,000 MW 
of generation capacity. Under his leadership, GETCO invested over ₹12,000 crore in 
infrastructure development, achieving industry -leading transmiss ion losses below 4% and 
improving system availability to 99.6%. His initiatives played a critical role in ensuring 24x7 
uninterrupted power supply across Gujarat.  
Mr. Negi’s leadership has been recognized at the national and global levels:  
• Best Engineer Award (2014 -15) by CBIP  for his contributions to India’s power 
transmission sector.  
• Distinguished Fellow Member Certificate by CIGRE (2016)  for excellence in grid 
modernization and high -voltage transmission solutions.  
• Spearheaded India’s first large -scale Smart Grid Technology integration , deploying 
Optical CT, Digital Substations, PMUs, and AI -driven grid analytics . 
• Led GETCO in implementing one of India's most advanced Load Despatch Centres , 
ensuring real -time RE forecasting, grid balancing, and smart transmission control.  
At KPI Green Energy, Mr. Negi will focus on scaling renewable energy projects, strengthening the 
company’s transmission infrastructure, and expanding its domestic and international footprint. 
His expertise in large -scale project execution, grid integration, and operati onal excellence will be 
instrumental in KPI Green Energy’s next growth phase.

Dr. Faruk Patel, Chairman and Managing Director of KPI Green Energy , stated:
