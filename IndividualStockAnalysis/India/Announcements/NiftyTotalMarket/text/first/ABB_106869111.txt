ABB to acquire Ziehl -Abegg , 
expanding offering  for HVAC 
and industrial cooling 
applications  
• Acquisition adds electronically commutated (EC) fans, a mission -
critical component of cooling and ventilation systems, to ABB's 
Motion portfolio of motors and drives  
• Broadens ABB’s offering for heating, ventilation and air 
conditioning (HVAC), industrial cooling and thermal management 
applications  
• Helps  equipment manufacturers improve system performance 
and energy efficiency while reducing integration complexity, 
development costs and time -to-market  
 
ABB has signed an agreement to acquire Ziehl -Abegg , a leading manufacturer of high -efficiency 
electronically commutated (EC) fans, integrated electric motors, ventilation and control technology, for 
an enterprise value of $2.65 billion  and an equity value of $ 2.22 billion . The acquisition complements 
ABB’s existing motors and drives portfolio with fan s and integrated motors , broadening the company’s 
offering for customers ’ mission -critical cooling systems across the growing heating, ventilation and air 
conditioning ( HVAC ), industrial cooling and thermal management markets.  
 
Ziehl -Abegg  is expected to generate  approximately  $1.4 billion  of revenue s in 2026, with an Operational 
EBITA margin in the  mid -single digit  range . ABB expects Ziehl -Abegg  to achieve an Operational EBITA 
margin broadly in line with its Motion business area average  within three  years  after closing of the 
transaction.  The transaction implies an EV/ Sales multiple of 1.9x and EV/EBITDA multiple of 18.6x based  
on the year  ending  2026 , with the la tter expected to  materially  decrease towards low double -digits 
including estimated strong mid -term synergies  supported by improving operational performance as we 
combine ABB’s and Ziehl -Abegg ’s complementary strengths . ABB expects the transaction to generate a 
return on invested capital well above its weighted average cost of capital . 
Closing of the transaction is expected in the second half of  2027, subject to regulatory approvals and 
other customary closing conditions .

Expanding capabilities in energy -efficient air movement  
Headquartered in Künzelsau, Germany , Ziehl -Abegg  has established a premier brand and a strong track 
record of innovation. Its comprehensive portfolio includes axial, centrifugal and process fans, internal 
and external rotor motors, ventilation systems and integrated control systems. Its products are used in  
HVAC, data centers, clean rooms, medical technology, industrial processes and agriculture, as well as in 
elevator drives.  
The company employs around 6,300 people * across Europe , the Americas , and Asia . Ziehl -Abegg  
expanded its US presence by opening a €100 million manufacturing, logistics, and R&D hub in 2024. In 
2026 , the company launched a separate €90 million global investment program to scale capacity across 
Europe, North America, and Asia, positioning it to capture ongoing growth in its key geographical 
markets .  
Ziehl -Abegg  brings more than 100 years of engineering experience in external rotor motors, strong 
research and development capabilities, and proprietary ferrite -magnet technology that reduces 
dependence on rare earth materials.  In turn, Ziehl -Abegg  will benefit from ABB's global scale, 
distribution reach, service presence and customer relationships, supporting the expansion of its 
offering in Europe, the Americas, Asia and other growth markets . 
Demand for HVAC and industrial cooling solutions is supported by several long -term growth drivers, 
including increasing cooling requirements in manufacturing and process industries, the expansion of 
commercial HVAC applications, growing investment in data centers, and rising demand for energy 
efficiency.  
A fan typically represents around 5 percent of the capital cost of a cooling system but accounts for 
around 80 percent of its lifetime energy consumption. Fan efficiency is therefore a key lever for 
customers addressing rising electricity demand, and EC te chnology is central to delivering these 
efficiency improvements.  
Commitment to presence in Germany  
Germany is ABB’s third -largest market by revenue, with around 8, 300 employees across 35 sites. ABB 
recognizes Ziehl -Abegg ’s strong heritage in Germany, including its local manufacturing and R&D 
presence and intends to support leadership continuity working closely with Ziehl -Abegg ’s management 
team following completion. ABB  intends to retain Ziehl -Abegg 's headquarters  in Künzelsau, and 
Germany will remain an important location for the business.

Ziehl -Abegg  (Künzelsau , Germany) is one of the leading global companies in the field of ventilation, 
control and drive technology. In the 1950s, Ziehl -Abegg established the basis for modern fan drives: 
external rotor motors which even today are still seen as state -of-the-art wo rldwide. Another area of 
business is electric motors which provide the power, for example, for elevators or medical applications 
(computer tomography equipment).  
The high -tech company has an impressive innovative capability (all figures and statements refer to the 
year 2025). Ziehl -Abegg employs 3,000 personnel in its production plants in southern Germany. The 
company has a global workforce of 6,300  spread between 18 production plants, 30 companies  and 114 
sales locations. The products, approx. 30,000 in all, are sold in more than 100 countries. Turnover totals 
more than one billion euros.  
Emil Ziehl founded the company in Berlin in 1910 as a manufacturer of electric motors. After World War II 
the company’s headquarters were relocated to southern Germany. Ziehl -Abegg SE is not a listed 
company but instead is family -owned.
