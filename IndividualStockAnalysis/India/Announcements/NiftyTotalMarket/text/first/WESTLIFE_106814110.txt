McDonald's India (W&S) brings festive families together with the new 
Spicy Patakha Range  
Celebrating festive togetherness with bold, makhani -inspired flavours made for India's 
family moments  
 
Mumbai, 12 October 2026:  As families come together to celebrate the festive season, McDonald's 
India (West & South) is bringing a bold new flavour experience to the table with the launch of its 
Spicy Patakha Range.  Introduced by Westlife Foodworld Ltd.,  which owns and operates 
McDonald's restaurants across West and South India, the range features the Spicy Patakha Paneer 
Burger and Spicy Patakha Chicken Burger.  
 
Building on the brand's "Let's Family at McD" platform , which celebrates the role McDonald's plays 
in bringing families together through everyday moments and special occasions, the launch embraces 
the spirit of festive togetherness. From family outings and shopping breaks to festive catch -ups  with 
loved ones, McDonald's continues to be a place where people come together to create meaningful 
memories.  
 
Inspired by India's love for festive indulgence, both burgers combine smoky, spicy flavours with 
rich makhani -inspired notes to create a distinctly Indian taste experience.  The Spicy Patakha 
Paneer Burger features a spiced paneer patty, while the Spicy Patakha Chicken Burger comes with a 
spicy chicken patty, both layered with a flavour -packed smoky sauce and served in soft, toasted buns.  
 
Akshay Jatia, CEO, Westlife Foodworld Ltd., said, “The festive season is one of the most meaningful 
times for families to come together and celebrate. It is also a time when consumers look for flavours 
that feel familiar, indulgent and festive. The Spicy Patakha Range is inspired by these insights and 
reflects our continued commitment to creating menu innovations that resonate with Indian tastes 
while giving families another reason to come together and enjoy special moments.”  
 
To bring this festive proposition to life, McDonald's India (West & South) has launched an integrated 
campaign conceptualised by McCann India, part of Omnicom Advertising India.  Built on the 
insight that the joy of festivals often begins long before the celebrations themselves, the campaign 
shines a spotlight on the moments leading up to the big day, from decorating homes to festive 
shopping and everything in between.  
Through the bold, festive flavours of the Spicy Patakha  Range, the films transform these everyday 
preparations into occasions worth celebrating. Whether it's cousins pausing festive decorating 
duties for a burger break or a husband and wife finding joy amidst a hectic shopping trip, the 
campaign captures the s pirit of 'Tyohaar ki Taiyaari Mein Thodi Mazedaari'  and reinforces the belief 
that every family moment can be a reason to celebrate at McDonald's.  
 
The launch of the Spicy Patakha  Range reflects the long -standing commitment by McDonald's India 
(West & South) to understand evolving consumer preferences and creating menu innovations rooted 
in local tastes. For nearly three decades, the brand has continuously listened to its customers , 
translating consumer insights, flavour trends and changing dining occasions into products that 
resonate with Indian tastes.  
 
From pioneering India -specific menu adaptations in its early years to introducing iconic favourites 
such as the McAloo  Tikki, Maharaja Mac  Veg & Chicken,  and the McSpicy range , McDonald's has 
consistently evolved its menu to reflect what Indian consumers want. The Spicy Patakha Range 
continues this journey of innovation, bringing together the growing demand for bold, indulgent 
flavours with the familiar appeal of makhani, one o f India's most loved taste profiles.  
 
 
The Spicy Patakha  Range will be available across McDonald's restaurants in West and South India 
from 11 October 2026 for dine -in, takeaway and delivery, while stocks last. Prices start at ₹199 
onwards, exclusive of taxes.

About Westlife Foodworld:  
Westlife Foodworld Limited (NSE: WESTLIFE, BSE: 505533), formerly known as Westlife Development Ltd (WDL), 
focuses on setting up and operating Quick Service Restaurants (QSR) in India through its subsidiary Hardcastle 
Restaurants Pvt.  Ltd. (HRPL). The Company operates a chain of McDonald’s restaurants in West and South India 
having a master franchisee relationship with McDonald’s Corporation USA, through the latter’s subsidiary.  
 
About Hardcastle Restaurants Pvt. Ltd.:  
HRPL is a McDonald’s franchisee with rights to own and operate McDonald’s restaurants in India’s West and 
South markets. HRPL has been a franchisee in the region since its inception in 1996. HRPL serves over 200 million 
customers, annually, at over 478 McD onald’s restaurants across 78 cities in the states of Telangana, Gujarat, 
Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Chhattisgarh, Andhra Pradesh, Goa along with parts of Madhya 
Pradesh and Union Territory of Puducherry and provides direct employment to o ver 12,000 employees.

thrus, McCafé, 24x7, McDelivery, McBreakfast and dessert kiosks. The menu features Burgers, Wraps, Hot and 
Cold Beverages besides a wide range of desserts. Majority of the McDonald’s restaurants feature an in -house 
McCafé. The pillars of the McDonald's sys tem – Quality, Service, Cleanliness and Value – are evident at each of 
the restaurants that HRPL operates
