Information  Note    
 
Indegene Ranked Among Top 100 Companies for Women to Work in India by AVTAR   
 
Bengaluru, India, December  5, 2024: Indegene, a digital -first life sciences commercialization 
company, announced  that it has been ranked  among  the Top 100 Companies for Women to Work 
in India by AVTAR , a leading diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI) consultancy. This is Indegene’s 
eighth consecutive year of being recognized among the Best Companies for Women to Work in 
India.  
 
Each year, A VTAR  collaborates with over 300 companies to thoroughly analyze their policies, 
practices, and programs aimed at promoting gender inclusion in the workplace.  This year, 
approximately 361 companies participated in the assessment . 
 
Indegene is committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive workplace where every individual's 
unique background and perspective is valued and celebrated. The company  recognize s that its 
strength lies in the rich tapestry of experiences, cultures, and ideas that its employees bring to the 
table.  Women form nearly 45% of Indegene’s overall employee base, which is one of the highest 
in the industry, demonstrating the company’s commitment to furthering gender diversity.  
 
With 6 operation hubs and 18 offices across North America, Europe, and Asia, Indegene’s client 
base includes the world’s top 20 biopharma companies, many emerging biotech firms, and medical 
device companies. With over 5,000 employees worldwide, t his vast global footprint underscores 
Indegene’s ability to scale and meet the evolving needs of the world’s leading pharma 
organizations  across markets .
