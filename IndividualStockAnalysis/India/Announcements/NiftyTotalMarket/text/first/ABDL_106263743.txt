‘ICONiQ White’ Retains Crown of World’s Fastest Gro wing Millionaire Spirits Brand   
 ~  Creates history by clinching the title for two c onsecutive years~ 
Mumbai, 6th June 2025  - Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD), the  largest domestic spirits 
company by volume in India, is thrilled to announce a historic triumph. ICONiQ White Whisky has 
once again been crowned the World’s Fastest-Growing Millionaire Spirits Brand by  Drinks 
International  in its highly acclaimed Millionaires’ Club 2025  report. The brand this year has 
officially been ranked amongst the top 20 whiskies in the world , an extraordinary milestone 
achieved within just three years of launch.  
In addition to ICONiQ White’s stellar performance, ABD ’s flagship legacy brand Officer’s Choice 
continues to hold strong, retaining its position amo ngst the Top 10 spirits brands globally  and 
ranking 5th amongst all whiskies worldwide . Meanwhile, Sterling Reserve has also retained its 
position amongst the Top 20 whiskies in the world , reinforcing ABD’s strength across multiple 
segments of the spirits category and highlighting th e depth of its award-winning portfolio. 
Published annually, The Millionaires’ Club is the def initive global report that ranks spirits brands 
selling over one million liters cases per year, spot lighting the highest-performing and most 
dynamic names in the international liquor industry. T he above rankings are for the calendar year 
2024. 
Speaking about this milestone achievement, Alok Gup ta, Managing Director, ABD, "This is 
indeed a proud moment to be globally acknowledged by the Millionaires’ Club.  ICONiQ  White’s 
achievement  as the world’s fastest-growing millionair e spirits brand consecutively for the second 
year is heartwarming, especially as we see it emerging as  an internationally loved brand. The 
strong global position retained by our legacy brands  like Officer’s Choice and Sterling Reserve is 
a moment of collective pride for ABD. This demonstra tes the power of our multi-brand, multi-
market strategy coupled with the continuous efforts of our team, positioning us well for the 
future." 

This recognition underscores ABD’s continued commit ment to crafting innovative and high-
quality spirits that resonate with evolving consumer preferences both in India and across global 
markets. 
About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited ( www.abdindia.com )  
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD) is the largest domestic Spirits company in India, in 
terms of annual sales volumes. ABD has a presence in five main flavors, i.e., whisky, brandy, rum, 
vodka, and gin, with ‘millionaire’ brands like Office r’s Choice Whisky, Officer’s Choice Blue Whisky, 
Sterling Reserve Premium Whiskies and ICONiQ White Wh isky. Currently, its manufacturing 
network comprises 36 units, of which 9 are owned bottl ing units, 2 owned distilleries, and 25 non-
owned manufacturing units.
