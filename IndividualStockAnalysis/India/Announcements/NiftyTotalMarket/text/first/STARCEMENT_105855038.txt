Sub: Intimation of Partial disposal of shares held in Star Cement (I) Limited under 
Regulation 30 of read with Schedule III of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2 015 (“the Listing Regulations”) as amended,  we 
would like to inform you that Star Cement Limited  has partially disposed of its shareholding 
in Star Cement (I) Limited . 
Star Cement Limited has disposed of 4,500 equity shares  of Rs.  10/- each  (i.e. 9% of total 
shareholding) and consequently M/s.  Star Cement (I) ceased to be the wholly -owned 
subsidiary of Star Cement Limited .
