consecutive years or more, shall be transferred by the company to IEPF Authority, within 30 days of the due date. Accordingly, any shares in respect of which 
the dividend remains unclaimed or unpaid for seven consecutive years as on the due date of August 21, 2026, will be transferred to IEPF Authority within 30
