Sub :  Disclosure of information under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations 2015 - 
Investment approval for Yarn  Project  at Bhadrak (Odisha ) 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of SEBI (LODR) Regulations 2015, it is 
hereby informed  that the Board at its meeting held  today i.e. on 20th December 2024 has accorded  
Investment approval  for setting up of  a Yarn Project consisting of  900 TPD Continuous  
Polymerization (CP) unit with downstream units of  Draw Textured Yarn ( DTY), Fully Drawn Yarn 
(FDY) , Polyester chips  & associated facilities at Bhadrak (Odisha)  at an estimated cost of Rs. 
4,382.21 crore  through  (50:50) Joint Venture with MCPI Pvt. Ltd . with IndianOil’s equity 
contribution of Rs. 657.33 crore . 
 
The meeting of the Board of Directors commenced at 11:30 AM  and concluded at 3:30 PM.
