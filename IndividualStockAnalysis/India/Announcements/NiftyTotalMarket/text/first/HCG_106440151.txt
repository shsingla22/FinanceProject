ANNEXURE – B  
 
Detailed information in accordance with SEBI circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD -PoD-
1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023, and SEBI Master Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024, as amended from time to time, with 
respect to issuance of guarantee for Cancer Care Kenya Limited (“CCK”) by HealthCare Global 
Enterprises Limited (the Company”).  
 
(a) N ame of party for which such guarantees or indemnity or surety was given;  
 
The Company proposes  to provide guarantee in the form of a Standby Letter of Credit (SBLC) to secure 
credit facilities to be availed by Cancer Care Kenya Limited (CCK), Nairobi, Kenya, a step -down 
subsidiary of the Company, for its business expansion.  
 
(b) Whether the promoter/promoter group/promoter group companies have any interest in the 
transaction? If yes, nature of interest and details thereof and whether the same is done at “arm’s 
length”;  
 
CCK is a step -down subsidiary of the Company and is a related party and the transaction is on an arm’s 
length basis . None of the promoter/ promoter group/ promoter group companies have any interest in this 
acquisition.  
 
(c) Brief details of such guarantee or indemnity or becoming a surety viz. brief details of agreement 
entered (if any) including significant terms and conditions, including amount of guarantee;  
 
CCK proposes to raise about USD 1.8 million (approximately INR 16 C rore) as a term loan, backed by a 
Standby Letter of Credit (SBLC) to be issued out of the existing credit facilities of the Company. The 
Company’s potential liability under the guarantee could be up to 105% of the outstanding loan of CCK 
under this arrang ement. The guarantee shall be released upon repayment in full  of the amounts due under 
the arrangement by CCK.  
 (d) Impact of such guarantees or indemnity or surety on listed entity;  
 The guarantee will be treated as a contingent liability for the Company.
