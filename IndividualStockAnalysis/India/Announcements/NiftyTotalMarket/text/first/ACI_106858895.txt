Subject: Signing of MOU with Andhra Pradesh Economic Development Board   
Reference: Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  
 
We wish to inform you that Archean Chemical Industries Limited (“Company”) has entered into 
a non-binding Memorandum of Understanding (“MoU”) with the Andhra Pradesh Economic 
Development Board, Government of Andhra Pradesh , to explore the establishment of a 
Battery Energy Storage Giga Factory at Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh.  
The Company, leveraging its established presence in specialty chemicals, had made a strategic 
investment in Offgrid Energy Labs , which is developing advanced chemistry-based battery 
energy-storage systems, particularly for stationary and long-duration energy-storage 
applications. Offgrid Energy commenced operations of its pilot plant in July 2026 and is now 
further evaluating initiatives towards technical and commercial scale up.   
Considering the evolving energy-storage landscape and the increasing focus on domestic 
battery manufacturing, the Company has entered into the aforesaid MoU. The proposed project 
envisages a manufacturing capacity of up to 5 GWh per annum , with an indicative investment 
of approximately Rs.2,000 crore, through a proposed Special Purpose Vehicle, potentially in 
collaboration with technology and/or ﬁnancial partners.  
The proposed investment is subject to feasibility assessment, technology validation, ﬁnancing 
arrangements, requisite Board and regulatory approvals, mutually acceptable government 
incentives, applicable land allotment/bidding processes and execution of deﬁnitive agreements.  
The MoU is non-binding and does not constitute a ﬁrm investment commitment by the 
Company at this stage. The Company will make further disclosures as and when material 
developments occur.  
The above information shall be made available on the website of the Company at 
www.archeanchemicals.com
