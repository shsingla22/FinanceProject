PRESS RELEASE  
Infosys Becomes the World’s First Organization to Achieve CMMI AI Maturity 
(AIM) Level 5  
Recognition underscores Infosys’ mature approach to scaling responsible AI across 
software engineering and service delivery  
Bengaluru, India –  November 25, 2026: Infosys  (NSE, BSE, NYSE: INFY), a global leader in AI -first 
business consulting and technology services, today announced that it has become the world’s first 
organization to be assessed at Maturity Level 5 of the CMMI AI Maturity (AIM) Model. The assessment was 
conducted by Benchmarked P ty Ltd, a Licensed Partner of the CMMI® Institute , an ISACA enterprise and 
global leader in helping organizations reduce risk, boost performance and build capability. The milestone demonstrates Infosys’ structured approach to scaling AI across the software engineering lifecycle, with a focus on responsible governance, continuous improvement, and predictable, quality -driven outcomes . 
The recognition marks a significant progression in Infosys’ AI maturity journey, following its participation in 
the CMMI AI Maturity (AIM) pilot assessment. The CMMI AI Maturity (AIM) Level 5 assessment covered a 
broad array of projects across service lines, geographies, and independent functions globally, sampling out 
of more than 1,000 projects. It evaluated how AI is governed, deployed, and measured across Infosys’ 
global delivery environment, and its impact on overall performance and client outcomes .  
Infosys has delivered consistent and predictable outcomes within time and budget, reflected in accelerated 
productivity improvement, faster release cycles and time- to-market, improved business performance, 
reduced total cost of ownership, and a sustained f ocus on innovation and continuous improvement . 
The assessment recognized Infosys’ focus on adopting mature AI practices and integrating responsible AI 
governance and risk management into the software engineering lifecycle through use of AI tools and 
platforms. It also confirmed that Infosys has achieve d statistically validated, business performance gains 
from CMMI AIM guided processes incorporating AI solutions and technologies .   
Infosys is leveraging its AI -first capabilities in Infosys Topaz  to operationalize AI at scale across service 
delivery and internal business processes. Together with its Responsible AI Office , AI centers of excellence, 
and governance mechanisms, these capabilities help embed secure and ethical practices across the AI 
lifecycle. This enables Infosys to draw on its own enterprise -scale experience to help clients unlock AI value 
at scale.  
 
 
Dinesh Rao, Executive Vice President and Chief Delivery Officer, Infosys, said, “ We are immensely 
proud to be the world’s first organization to achieve the highest level of CMMI AI Maturity (AIM). This 
recognition is a testament to the rigor, innovation, and collective commitment with which we have embedded 
AI across Infosys, combining strong engineering discipline with responsible governance and continuous 
improvement. Powered by Infosys Topaz, this deep enterprise experience positions us strongly to help our 
clients unlock AI value at scale and translate its transformative potential i nto measurable business 
outcomes .” 
Chris Dimitriadis, Chief Global Strategy Officer, ISACA , said,  “We are grateful for and congratulate the 
Infosys team on becoming the first organization to achieve CMMI AI Maturity (AIM) Level 5, demonstrating 
how responsible governance, disciplined innovation, and continuous improvement can translate AI ambition 
into measurable business value. Infosys's incredible results are proof that CMMI AIM not only helps address 
the AI challenges enterprises are facing, but  adds significant competitive advantage as well .”
