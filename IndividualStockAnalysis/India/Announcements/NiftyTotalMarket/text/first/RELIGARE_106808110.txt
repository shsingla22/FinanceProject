3 Brief Profile (in case of 
appointment)  Gaurav Sharma brings close to 30 years of experience at the 
intersection of financial services and technology, making him one of 
the strongest CTO candidates for a modern NBFC. Over the course of 
his career, he has le d large -scale digital transformation programs 
across lending, insurance, capital markets and banking, with deep 
expertise in enterprise technology modernization, analytics, AI -led 
transformation and scalable operating models.  
 
Before joining RFL, he was serving as Chief Technology Officer at IIFL 
Finance Group since July 2022, Gaurav leads the group -wide mandate 
across Technology, Digital and Analytics for IIFL Finance, IIFL 
Securities and 5paisa. During his tenure, he has been instrumental in 
re-platforming the lending stack, modernising infrastructure 
architecture and building an Analytics & AI Centre of Excellence 
anchored on unified data and GenAI capabilities. Under his leadership, 
the organisation has transitioned toward Ku bernetes -led 
infrastructure, DevOps -driven operations and open -source 
ecosystems, while simultaneously reshaping technology talent 
capability and innovation culture through a dedicated Bangalore 
innovation centre.  
 
Prior to IIFL, Gaurav served as Group CTO  at Poonawalla Fincorp 
(September 2021 - February 2022), where he led technology, digital 
and analytics functions across NBFC, HFC and insurance businesses. 
One of his key contributions was institutionalising fintech and co -
lending partnerships, including with YUBI, helping accelerate sourcing 
and balance sheet velocity for the organisation.  
 
At L&T Financial Services (June 2018 – August 2021), Gaurav drove a 
multi -year enterprise transformation agenda across lending and 
corporate technology platforms. His  work included LMS consolidation 
across multiple businesses, Salesforce -led loan origination 
transformation, enterprise data warehousing on GCP, complete data 
centre migration to cloud infrastructure and implementation of SAP 
HANA -based finance systems. He  also led large -scale operational 
transformation initiatives including HRMS modernisation and SD -
WAN -enabled branch connectivity .
