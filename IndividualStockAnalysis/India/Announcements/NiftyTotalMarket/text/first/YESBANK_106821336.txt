 We refer to our intimation dated September 16, 2026, bearing the Ref No. YBL/CS/2026 -27/94 , 
wherein YES Bank Limited (“ the Bank ”) had intimated that pursuant to the cessation of the 
office of the then Chief Vigilance Officer (CVO) pursuant to the resignation tendered by Mr. 
Binu Soman, the Bank had, in the interim, designated Mr. Harish Sehgal, (Head – AML & 
Principal Officer of the Bank) as the interim CVO of the Bank,  effective September 16, 2026,  until 
the appointment of a new CVO.  The Bank would like to  inform that basis the recommendation of the Nomination and 
Remuneration Committee of the Bank, the Board of Directors of the Bank at its meeting held today i.e. on October 17, 2026, has, inter -alia approved the appointment / designated Mr. Sai 
Venkataramana Kosuri - Head Legal Retail and Business Banking of the Bank as the  new  Chief 
Vigilance Officer  (CVO)  and SMP of the Bank  with effect from November 25, 2026, and  he shall 
discharge responsibilities exclusively in the capacity of Chief Vigilance Officer (CVO) from the said effective date.  
   
Consequent to the above, Mr. Harish Sehgal (Head – AML & Principal Officer  ), who has been 
Interim Chief Vigilance Officer  and SMP , with effect from September 16, 2026 ,  shall  demit the 
office of Interim Chief Vigilance Officer  with effect from November 24, 2026 end of business hours 
and shall  continue / re-designated as Head – AML & Principal Officer of the Bank  and cease to 
be classified as an SMP  of the Bank.
