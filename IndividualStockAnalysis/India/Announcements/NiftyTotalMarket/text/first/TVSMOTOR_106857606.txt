TVS MOTOR COMPANY STRENGTHENS ITS PRESENCE IN 
MAURITIUS LAUNCH OF TVS RONIN AND TVS NTORQ XP  
• TVSM also has  a new distribution partnership with Le Warehouse  
 
Singapore/ Mauritius,  November  12, 2026: TVS Motor Company (TVSM), part of TVS VENU, and a 
reputed global manufacturer of two and three- wheelers, today announced a new distribution partnership 
with Le Warehouse in Maurit ius and 361 as its reseller. This announcement also marked the 
introduction of two new models –  TVS Ronin and TVS NTORQ  XP. The event was attended by Osman 
Mahomed, Minister of Land Transport, Mauritius ; Pradeep Chaurasiya, Head of Africa Business, TVS 
Motor Company; and senior representatives from Le Warehouse and 361. 
For more than 10  years, TVS M has established a strong presence in the Mauritian market by 
contributing to the mobility landscape and earning the confidence of local customers.  This new 
partnership builds on that foundation while bringing renewed ambition to the brand's development on 
the island. By combining TVS Motor's manufacturing expertise with Le Warehouse ’s local market 
knowledge and 361’s retail presence, the partnership aims to strengthen the brand's connection with 
Mauritian customers.  This will further support the brand’s market growth as customers' mobility needs 
continue to evolve.  
Announcing this new partnership and the launch, Peyman Kargar, President – International 
Business, TVS Motor Company  said, “The launch highlights the importance of the African market in 
our international business ambition and development as well as TVS Motor’s commitment to strengthen its presence in Mauritius. With the introduction of the TVS Ronin and TVS NTORQ XP, we are expanding our portfolio to offer Mauritian customers greater choice across distinct riding segments.”  
Speaking at the launch, Pradeep Chaurasiya, Head of Africa Business, TVS Motor Company, said, “Mauritius has been an important market for TVS Motor  Company  for over a decade, and we are 
pleased to build on this longstanding association with a renewed focus on strengthening our presence 
in the country. Our partnership with Le Warehouse and 361 brings together TVS Motor's global manufacturing expertise and a s trong local understanding of customer needs. We look forward to 
working closely with our partners to deepen our engagement with customers and contribute to the 
evolving mobility landscape in Mauritius.”  
As the world's third- largest two- wheeler manufacturer, we bring extensive international experience, 
proven manufacturing capabilities and deep industry expertise to the Mauritian market. Its established 
presence across markets with diverse mobility needs provides a strong foundation for strengthening its footprint in the region.  
Amit Kantilal Shah , CEO , Le Warehouse, said , "TVS Motor Company brings together the strength of 
an established international manufacturer and a strong focus on innovation, performance and value, which aligns well with our own philosophy. Through 361, we have built strong relationships with Mauritian households by making recognised international brands accessible and supporting customers throughout their ownership journey. Extending this experience into mobility marks an important strategic diversification for Le Warehouse and an opportunity to play a greater role in the  everyday lives of our 
customers . With TVSM already having an established presence and an existing community of owners 
in Mauritius, our focus is to build on this legacy. We will leverage 361's retail network, digital channels and customer relationships to expand the brand's reach, improve product accessibility and offer 
customers a wider choice of mobility solutions that  meet their everyday needs.”
