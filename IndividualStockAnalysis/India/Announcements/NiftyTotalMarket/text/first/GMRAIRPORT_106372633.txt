Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
This is further to our earlier intimation dated December 08, 2023, wherein it was 
informed that The Airports Economic Regulatory Authority (AERA) has issued Tariff 
order bearing no. 27/ 2023 -24 dated 07.12.2023 in favour GMR Goa International 
Airport Limit ed (GGIAL), a subsidiary of the Company, in the matter of determination 
of aeronautical charges / Tariff for Manohar International Airport, MOPA, Goa (GOX) for 
the first control period from 1st April 2023 to 31st March 2028.  
 
GGIAL had filed an appeal before the Telecom Disputes Settlement & Appellate Tribunal 
(TDSAT) regarding various matters including on the matter related to Reduction of 
Project Management Consultancy (“PMC”) expenses, determination of Aeronautical and 
Non-aeronautical revenue and taxes thereof, finalisation of tariff rate card, treatment 
of license fees etc.  
 
TDSAT has now passed the Judgement upholding the contentions of GG IAL by  quashing 
and setting aside various appealed matters from the aforesaid Tariff Order No. 27/2023 -
24 dated 07.12.2023 and requisite directions have been given to AERA for different 
issues as stated in the judgement.  
 
The detailed order can be accessed at https://tdsat.gov.in/Delhi/services/judgment.php .
