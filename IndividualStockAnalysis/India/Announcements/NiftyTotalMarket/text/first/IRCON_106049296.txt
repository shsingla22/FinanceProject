Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 [“SEBI (LODR) Regulations”] and in continuation of our letter even no. dated 
6th November, 2024, it is hereby informed that the Board of Directors of the Company at their 
meeting held on 7th November, 2024 has approved the appointed of Shri Anupum Singh, 
[DIN:10637375] ED CE (B&S)-II, Railway Board as Government Nominee (Part-Time Official) 
Director on the Board of IRCON with effect from 6th November, 2024, till he holds the post of ED 
CE (B&S)-II, Railway Board or further orders, whichever is earlier. 
 
In compliance with SEBI Circular No. LIS/COMP/14/2018-19 dated 20th June, 2018, and as per 
the confirmation received from Shri Anupum Singh, this is to confirm that he has not been 
debarred from holding the office of director by virtue of any order of SEBI or any other such 
authority.  
               
The requisite details pertaining to the appointment of Shri Anupum Singh, pursuant to Regulation 
30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations read with SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-
