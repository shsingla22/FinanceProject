Lemon Tree Hotels launches its second property in Nepal with 
Tigerland Safari — A Lemon Tree Resort, Chitwan, Nepal 
The property features 35 well-appointed cottages, banquet areas, a 
restaurant, and exclusive local experiences 
12th April 2024: Lemon Tree Hotels Limited announces the opening of 
Tigerland Safari — A Lemon Tree Resort, Chitwan, Nepal. This is the second 
property of the company in Nepal, which announced the launch of a Lemon 
Tree Premier in Kathmandu earlier this month. 
This wildlife resort, with aesthetically designed interiors that bring the 
outdoors in, is located in the undisturbed vestiges of the Terai region of 
Nepal. The resort offers 35 well-appointed cottages with a view of lush 
green Chitwan National Park, along with Citrus Café — a multi-cuisine coffee 
shop and an expansive banquet space to meet all discerning guests’ event 
and conference needs. The vibrant ambiance, paired with specially curated 
experiences, makes it a perfect option for guests. 
Chitwan, located in the southern part of Nepal, is a region renowned for its rich 
biodiversity and stunning natural landscapes. Home to the Chitwan National 
Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, this area is a haven for wildlife enthusiasts 
and nature lovers alike. The region also offers visitors the opportunity to 
immerse themselves in the vibrant culture and traditions of the Tharu people, 
who have inhabited the region for centuries. 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of the largest hotel chains in India, and 
owns/leases/operates/franchises hotels across the upscale, upper-midscale, midscale and 
economy segments. LTHL delivers differentiated yet superior service offerings, with a 
compelling value proposition. The group offers seven brands to meet guests’ needs across 
all levels, viz. Aurika Hotels & Resorts, Lemon Tree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox 
Hotels by Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Select by Lemon 
Tree Hotels and Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels. 
LTHL opened its first hotel with 49 rooms in May 2004. Today, the company has a portfolio 
of 160+ hotels, which includes over 100 operational hotels and more than 60 hotels set 
to open in India and internationally. Lemon Tree Hotels are located across metro regions, 
including the NCR, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Chennai, as well as 
numerous tier I, IT and III cities such as Pune, Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, Jaipur, Indore, 
Aurangabad, Udaipur, Visakhapatnam, Kochi, Ludhiana, Thiruvananthapuram, Vijayawada 
etc. The company expanded internationally with hotels opening in Dubai in December 
2019, in Bhutan in February 2020 and in Nepal in April 2024.
