Dear Sir/Ma’am,  
 
Pursuant  to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements), Regulations 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”) read with Para B of Part A of Schedule III 
of SEBI Listing Regulations, we hereby inform that  IAC International Automotive India Private Limited 
(formerly known as Lumax Integrated Ventures Private Limited)  (hereinafter referred to as “IAC India”), a 
material Unlisted Wholly -owned Subsidiary of the Company has today, i.e., May 28, 2025 inaugurated i ts 
two (2) new manufacturing plant s at Chakan (Pune), Maharashtra.  
IAC India is a well -established Tier -1 plastic interior systems and components supplier to key 
automotive OEMs in India including Mahindra, Maruti Suzuki, Volkswagen and Volvo Eicher 
Commercial Vehicles among others. IAC India is a leader in the vehicle in terior space and the sole 
supplier of integrated cockpits and door panels for the recently launched BEV model from Mahindra – 
BE6 & XEV 9e. It also has a strong and well -established engineering centre providing advanced design 
and engineering services to cu stomers in India and overseas which provides it a competitive advantage.  
This strategic expansion marks a significant milestone in IAC’s continued growth journey and reaffirms its 
commitment to delivering high -quality interior systems to leading automotive OEMs across India.  With this 
expansion, having an approx. investment of Rs. 640 million, IAC India is poised to increase its production 
capacity by approximately 50%, further strengthening its ability to meet rising customer demand with agility 
and precision.  
 
The first facility, an extension of IAC’s existing site within the Mahindra Supplier Park, spans 94,000 sq.ft. 
will primarily support Mahindra Vehicle Manufacturers Ltd. (MVML). The second facility , adjacent to the 
Park, covering 70,000 sq.  ft., has been developed to cater MVML and other major four -wheeler OEMs.  Both 
sites are equipped with next -generation technologies including laser scoring, robotic ultrasonic welding, 
rotary conveyor systems, advanced cockpit production lines for Just -In-Sequence (JIS) d elivery, Cut & Sew 
operations, and pressure lamination units. Designed with a sustainability -first approach, the facilities feature 
a roof -mounted solar power installation with a capacity of 750 KVA, ensuring up to 30% of their energy 
needs are met through  renewable sources, as well as water harvesting and recycling systems.  
 
This latest expansion signals the company’s readiness to align with the evolving needs of the automotive 
ecosystem while reinforcing the Lumax Group’s broader vision of driving growth through technology, 
quality, and sustainable practices.
