MOIL Achieves Highest -Ever July Production i n FY’2 6 
 
 
MOIL  achieved  manganese ore production of 1.45 lakh tonnes in July 2025, marking an 
impressive growth of 1 1.4% over the corresponding period last year (CPLY) , despite adverse  
weather conditions . 
 
Despite heavy rainfall, MOIL demonstrated strong operational momentum during April –
July 2025, with production at 6.47 lakh tonnes (7.8% growth y -o-y), sales of 5.01 lakh tonnes 
(10.7% higher than CPLY), and exploratory drilling of 43,215 meters (up 11.4% over CPLY).  
 
Shri Ajit Kumar Saxena, CMD, congratulated the MOIL team for this remarkable 
performance and appreciated the continued efforts to enhance production & sales even 
under challenging weather  conditions.
