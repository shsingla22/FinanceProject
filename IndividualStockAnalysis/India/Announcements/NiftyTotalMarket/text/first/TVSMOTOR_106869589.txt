TVS Jupiter : The Scooter of India,  Trusted By 
One Crore Indians  
 
• Becomes youngest  automotive brand to reach one crore milestone  
• India’s most awarded scooter is also the preferred mobility choice of new India  
Bengaluru, November 24 , 2026:  TVS Motor Company, part of TVS VENU and a global leader 
in two and three-wheeler manufacturing, today announced the rollout of TVS Jupiter, marking 
the one crore cumulative production milestone from its manufacturing facility in Hosur, Tamil 
Nadu. With this milestone, TVS Jupiter becomes India's fastest and youngest scooter brand 
to reach one crore cumulative production units, reinforcing its position as one of the country's 
most iconic mobility brands.  
 
Over 13 years, TVS Jupiter has evolved alongside India, 
becoming an integral part of 
everyday mobility for 
households across the 
country. Introduced in 2013 
with the philosophy of Zyada 
Ka Fayda, the brand set out 
to redefine everyday 
mobility by offering M ore.    
 Today, TVS Jupiter 
continues to deliver more  performance, more mileage, 
more comfort and more 
convenience, evolving its promise of Zyada with 
India's growing confidence, 
aspirations and changing 
mobility needs . 
 
 
Speaking on the milestone, Aniruddha Haldar, Senior Vice President, People Mobility, 
India 2W Business, TVS Motor Company  said, “Over the last decade, the collective 
consciousness of India has changed. We have moved from  a mindset of scarcity to one of 
confidence, growth and abundance, with  customers  increasingly aspiring for more. TVS 
Jupiter, with its philosophy of Zyada, has been the embodiment of this changing India and its growing aspirations. For a crore Indians, TVS Jupiter has been part of everyday journeys, 
bringing happiness to their lives. This achievement reflects the trust TVS Jupiter has built and 
the faith reposed in us by our customers . It also reflects  the hard work of our employees, 
dealer partners and suppliers, and the strength of our engineering, manufacturing and 
distribution 
capabilities . I am confident that many more milestones await us as India continues 
to progress and reach new heights.”
