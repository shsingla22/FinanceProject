Company are responsible for maintenance of adequate accounting records in accordance with the
provisions of the Act for safeguarding of the assets of the Company and for preventing and detecting
frauds and other irregularities; selection and application of appropriate accounting policies; making

of the Company.
Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Standalone Annual Financial Results

results as a whole are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to

detect a material misstatement when it exists. Misstatements can arise from fraud or error and
are considered material if, individually or in the aggregate, they could reasonably be expected
to influence the economic decisions of users taken on the basis of the standalone annual financial
results.

override of internal control.
