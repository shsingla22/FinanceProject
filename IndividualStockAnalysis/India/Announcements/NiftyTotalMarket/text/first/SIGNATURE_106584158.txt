b) Sales realization: Average Sales realization stood at INR 15,250 per sq. ft . in FY26 versus INR 12,457 per sq. ft. in FY25. Higher 
realization were driven by increased sales in premium markets and sales price increase across key regions.  
    
c)      Collections: Collections were INR 40.0 bn in FY26 and INR 9.1 bn for Q4FY26 in comparison to INR 43.8 bn in FY25 and INR 11.7 bn for 
Q4FY25. In addition to the above collections, the company also received Rs. 12.93 bn from Millennia Realtors Private Limited (a group 
company of RMZ Group) as consideration for the joint venture in one of the subsidiary companies. This transaction also marks entry of the 
company in large scale commercial development in the NCR region.  
 
d)    Net Debt:  Our net debt stood at a historic low of INR 2.0 bn at the end of FY26 in comparison to INR 8.8 bn at the end of FY25. The 
company has INR 27.70 bn of cash and cash equivalents as of 31st Mar 2026, which enables a very strong balance sheet position to 
strategize our foreseeable future.
