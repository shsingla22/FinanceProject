Revolt Motors launches RVX, its most powerful electric bike 
yet, at an introductory price of ₹1.24 lakh  
Featuring 5.3 kW peak power, 160 km IDC range and an all -new Boost Mode, the RVX launches 
alongside 'India Karega Revolt', a new campaign featuring Hardik Pandya and celebrating a 
generation that moves differently.  
New Delhi, 02nd July 2026:  Revolt Motors, India's No.1 electric bike and a wholly owned subsidiary of 
RattanIndia Enterprises Limited, today launched the all -new RVX, its most powerful and 
technologically advanced electric bike  yet. Designed for a generation that seeks performance, 
intelligence and individuality in equal measure, the RVX marks a significant new chapter for Revolt and 
India's rapidly evolving electric mobility landscape.  
Priced at an introductory ex -showroom price of ₹1.24 lakh  (inclusive of applicable PM E -DRIVE 
incentives) for a limited perio d. The bike  will be available across India through Revolt's expansive 
network of over 200 dealerships.  
Following the implementation of the new Delhi EV Policy and applicable incentives, customers in Delhi 
will be able to own the RVX at an effective ex -showroom price of ₹94,990 , making it one of the most 
accessible performance -oriented electric bike s in the country.  
As India's electric mobility journey enters its next phase, riders are increasingly seeking products that 
deliver excitement, technology and self -expression. The RVX has been developed precisely for this 
new generation of riders, bringing together exhilara ting performance, advanced connectivity and 
everyday practicality in one compelling package.  
Commenting on the launch, Mrs. Anjali Rattan, Chairperson, RattanIndia Enterprises Limited , said, 
"India's new generation is ambitious, expressive and deeply optimistic about the future. They expect 
technology to do more than solve problems, they want it to excite, inspire and reflect who they are. 
The RVX embodies this spirit. It represents the coming  together of Indian engineering, purposeful 
innovation and our belief that the future of mobility can be both sustainable and exhilarating."  
Engineered as a performance -oriented electric bike , the RVX is powered by a new 4 kW Mid -Drive 
PMSM motor that delivers a peak output of 5.3 kW and up to 230 Nm of torque at the wheel, making 
it the most powerful bike  ever produced by Revolt Motors. The bike  accelerates from 0 to 40 kmph in 
just 3.9 seconds and reaches the speed of 90 kmph in the all -new Boost Mode.  
The RVX features a 3.24 kWh removable NMC battery that delivers an IDC range of 160 kilometres on 
a single charge. It can be charged from 0 to 80 per cent in just 80 minutes using the fast charger.  
Built to inspire confidence across riding conditions, the bike  features upside -down front forks, a rear 
mono -shocker and four riding modes —Boost, Sports, City and Eco. It also comes equipped with a new 
Digi-dash with 3.5 -inch IP67 -rated PMVA display, Bluetooth connectivity, telematics, geo -fencing, OTA 
updates, call and message notifications, hill hold assist, reverse mode, walk assist, immobiliser and 
vehicle locator. Collectively, these features make the RVX equally suited for daily commutes, spirited 
weekend rides and everything in between.  
Available in Pearl  Black, Electric Blue, and Eclipse Red, the RVX has been designed for riders who see 
mobility as an extension of their ambitions and individuality.  
                                                                                                                     
 
Coinciding with the launch, Revolt Motors also unveiled its new brand campaign, 'India Karega Revolt', 
featuring brand ambassador and cricket icon Hardik Pandya. The campaign celebrates a generation 
that is self -assured, ambitious and unafraid to make its own moves.  
For more information on the all -new RVX and to explore the world of Revolt electric bike s, customers 
can visit the official website at www.revoltmotors.com  or experience the RVX first -hand at their 
nearest authorised Revolt dealership across India.
