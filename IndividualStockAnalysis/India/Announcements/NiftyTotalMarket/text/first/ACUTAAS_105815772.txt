AMI Organics Limited Accredited Gold Medal by EcoVadis  
Surat, April 15, 2024: Ami Organics Limited  (AMI) (BSE: 543349, NSE: AMIORG), a leading 
global manufacturer of advance pharmaceutical intermediates and speciality chemicals, 
proudly announces its attainment of the prestigious Gold Rating accreditation by  EcoVadis, 
recognizing the company's outstanding commitment to sustainability.  
This significant recognition underscores Ami Organics' unwavering dedication to the 
fundamental principles of sustainability. The Gold Medal distinction places the company in the 
top 5% of all organizations assessed within EcoVadis's rigorous framework globally, showcasing 
Ami Organics' exceptional performance in environmental, social and governance stewardship 
through its policies, actions and results. 
Mr. Naresh Patel, Executive Chairman & Managing Director of Ami Organics Limited, 
expressed his elation regarding this achievement, stating , "I am delighted to announce that 
we have achieved the 'Gold Medal' accreditation  by EcoVadis. I extend my sincere thanks and 
congratulations to the entire Ami Organics Team  for their remarkable feat in obtaining this 
certification within just three years of  initial audit by EcoVadis." 
He further emphasized the company's commitment to driving tangible change, remarking , 
"We firmly believe that decisive actions catalyze real change, and thus, we have continuously 
strived to improve ourselves, adhering to our vision of becoming a beacon of innovation and 
sustainability in the pharmaceutical and specialty chemicals industry. Despite being in the  
chemicals manufacturing industry, we remain steadfast on our ESG goals propelled by an 
intensified focus on green chemistry and green initiatives. This commitment underscores our 
proactive approach to environmental responsibility and sustainability  by setting new standards 
of excellence in environmental sustainability within the global industry landscape.“ 
ABOUT AMI ORGANICS LIMITED: 
Ami Organics, headquartered at Surat, is a R&D driven manufacturer of specialty chemicals with 
varied end usage, focussed on the development and manufacturing of advanced 
pharmaceutical intermediates (“Pharma Intermediates”) for regulated markets and generic 
active pharmaceutical ingredients (“APIs”) and New Chemical Entities (“NCE”) and key starting 
material for agrochemical and fine chemicals. AMI has developed and commercialised over 520 
Pharma Intermediates for APIs across 17 key therapeutic areas since inception and NCE, with a 
strong focus on R&D across select high-growth high margin chronic therapeutic areas.
