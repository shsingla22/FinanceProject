PRESS RELEASE  
 
G E Shipping contracts to buy a Suezmax Crude Carrier  
 
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (G E Shipping) has contracted  to buy a Suezmax 
Crude Carrier of about 164,715 dwt on August  25, 2025. The 2010 built vessel is expected to join 
the C ompany’s fleet by Q3 FY2 6.  
 The Company’s current owned fleet stands at 38  vessels, comprising 26  tankers ( 5 crude carriers, 
17 product tankers, 4 LPG carrier s) and  12 dry bulk carriers aggregating 3.04 mn dwt . The 
Company’s current capacity utilization is close to 100%. Additionally, the company has 1 
Secondhand Kamsarmax Dry Bulk Carrier on order , which was contracted to buy in Jul’25 and 
expected to join the fleet by Q3 FY26.  
 The proposed ship will be financed entirely from  internal accruals. The purpose of the acquisition 
is expansion of the fleet.  
 
Post delivery of the se 2 vessel s, the Company will have 40 vessels aggregating 3.29 mn dwt.
