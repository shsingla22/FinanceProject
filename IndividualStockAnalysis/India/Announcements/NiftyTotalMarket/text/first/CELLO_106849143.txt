Investor Release
Commenting  on the Result,  Mr. Pradeep  Rathod,  Chairman  & Managing  Director,  Cello  World  Limited  said :
“In Q2 FY27, revenue  stood  at ₹633 crore,  registering  a growth  of 7.8% YoY and 20.2% QoQ . EBITDA  and PAT 
margins  stood  at 21.3% and 12.9%, respectively . For H1 FY27, revenue  amounted  to ₹1,160 crore,  with  
EBITDA  and PAT margins  of 21.7% and 13.4%, respectively . As the festive  season  falls later  this year,  a portion  
of the seasonal  and institutional  gifting  demand  has shifted  from  Q2 to Q3 FY27.
Input  costs  remained  elevated,  with  the West  Asia conflict  driving  up prices  of polymers,  fuel,  gas and 
packaging  materials . Despite  these  cost pressures,  gross  margin  improved  by approximately  70 bps YoY to 
50.2%, primarily  driven  by a favourable  product  mix. The operating  environment  remained  competitive,  with  
low-priced  imports  continuing  to exert  pressure  across  certain  product  categories .
Writing  Instruments  delivered  strong  growth,  led by the Cello  brand . In Consumerware , our in-house  steel  
bottle  manufacturing  facility  in Rajasthan  is progressing  towards  the ramp -up phase . We expect  production  to 
scale  up steadily  through  H2 FY27, supported  by a gradual  expansion  of the SKU portfolio .
Looking  ahead,  our key priorities  remain  focused  on enhancing  operational  efficiencies,  rationalising  the 
product  portfolio,  improving  capacity  utilisation , deepening  market  penetration  and maintaining  disciplined  
working  capital  management . H2 is seasonally  our stronger  half,  and with  a portion  of festive  demand  shifting  
into Q3, we expect  business  performance  to improve  over  the coming  quarters .”
About  Cello  World  Limited
Cello  World  Limited  is a prominent  player  in the consumer  ware  market  in India  with  presence  in the 
consumer  houseware,  writing  instruments  and stationery,  and moulded  furniture  and allied  products  and 
consumer  glassware  categories . 
The Company  operates  14 manufacturing  facilities  across  six locations  in India . The manufacturing  capabilities  
allow  them  to manufacture  a diverse  range  of products  in-house . 
The Company  has a strong  PAN  India  distribution  network  with  5,000+ distributors  and 1,80,000+ retailers  
across  India . The Company’s  strengths  include  a Well -established  brand  name  and strong  market  positions  
with  a track  record  of scaling  up new  businesses  and product  categories . 
The Company  is looking  at continued  innovation  to grow  wallet  share  and expand  consumer  base,  expand  
distribution  network,  scale  up branding,  promotional  and digital  activities  and grow  manufacturing  
capabilities  and expand  production  capacities .
