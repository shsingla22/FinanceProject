Tendering Equity 
Shares in the 
Buyback   • Eligible Shareholder s can only participate in the Buyback by placing a bid 
through a Stock Broker registered either with the BSE or the NSE . 
• Eligible Shareholder s are required to instruct their respective Stock Broker of the 
details of the Equity Shares intended to be tender ed under the Buyback.

placing  a bid.

(as defined below) .
