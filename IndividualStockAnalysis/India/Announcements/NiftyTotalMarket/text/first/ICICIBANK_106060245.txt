In terms of Regulation 30, 33, 52(4) and other applicable provisions of the SEBI (Listing

consolidated) for the quarter and six months ended September 30, 2024. We enclose

a. Unaudited financial results (standalone and consolidated) for the quarter and six

corresponding period; and 
c. News Release on unaudited financial results for the quarter and six months ended 
September 30, 2024.

read with para A of part A of Schedule Ill of the SEBI Listing Regulations, as amended, read
