Clarification on Media Reports regarding Proposed Stake Sale  
by Mr. Bhaskar Srinivasan’s Promoter Group  
 
Chennai, September 30, 2026 : Avalon Technologies Limited (“Avalon” or the “Company”) has 
noted certain media reports regarding a proposed stake sale in the Company through block 
deal  mechanism  by Mr. Bhaskar Srinivasan and his promoter group, comprising BBS Family 
Trust and KBS Family Trust.  
 
The Company wishes to clarify that the reference to  “KBS Family Trust” should not be 
construed as a reference to Mr. Kunhamed Bicha or his promoter group.  Mr. Kunhamed Bicha 
and his promoter group are not selling any shares in connection with this proposed stake sale . 
 
For clarity, KBS Family Trust  (Kavya B haskar Srinivasan Family Trust)  forms part of Mr. 
Bhaskar Srinivasan ’s promoter group . As of September 29, 2026  (yesterday) , the 
shareholding of Mr. Bhaskar Srinivasan’s promoter group in Avalon is as follows : 
 
Shareholder  No. of Shares  % 
Bhaskar Srinivasan  62,70,355  9.38%  
BBS Family Trust  20,20,710  3.02%  
KBS Family Trust  20,20,710  3.02%  
Dolphin Family Trust  1,000  0.00%  
TOTAL 1,03,12,775  15.42%  
 
The Company remains committed to maintaining transparency and providing accurate 
information to its shareholders and stakeholders. Investors and other stakeholders are 
advised to refer to the Company’s official regulatory disclosures for information relati ng to 
the shareholding of its promoters and promoter group.
