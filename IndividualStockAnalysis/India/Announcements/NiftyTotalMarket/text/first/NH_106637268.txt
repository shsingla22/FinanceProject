Dear Sir/ Madam,  
In terms of the above referred NSE notice (enclosed herewith as Annexure A ) and SEBI Master circular 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, we wish to inform you that the Board 
of Directors of the Company (“Board”), at its meeting held on May 22, 2026, considered and took note that:  
1. SE imposed a fine of Rs. 4,000/- (excluding GST) on the Company for delay in submission of the 
Shareholding Pattern for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, under Regulation 31 of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.  
2. The Company had filed the said disclosure with BSE Limited on April 20, 2026, within the prescribed 
timeline.  
3. The delay occurred due to a technical/system-related issue, as the filing status appeared to have 
been successfully submitted on April 20, 2026, and no error message or failure notification was 
displayed on the NSE portal.  
4. Upon receipt of communication from NSE regarding non-submission, the Company immediately re-
submitted the Shareholding Pattern on April 23, 2026 and also submitted necessary clarification to 
NSE. 
5. The Company has submitted a request letter to NSE seeking waiver of the fine imposed, which is 
currently under consideration.  
The Board noted that the delay was inadvertent and unintentional and there was no mala fide intention or 
deliberate non-compliance. The Board advised the management to ensure compliance with applicable 
regulatory requirements well within time going forward.  
Kindly take the above on your record.
