Authority. With respect to dividend declared by the Company

31, 2017, the un-claimed dividend is due for transfer to IEPF

all shares in respect of which dividend has not been paid

by the Company to the designated Demat Account of the 
IEPF Authority (“IEPF Account”) within a period of thirty days

timeline. The Members, whose unclaimed dividend/shares 
have been transferred to IEPF, may claim the same by making 
an application to the IEPF Authority, in Form No. IEPF-5 
available on www.iepf.gov.in. The Members/Claimants can file 
only one consolidated claim in a financial year as per the IEPF 
Rules.
