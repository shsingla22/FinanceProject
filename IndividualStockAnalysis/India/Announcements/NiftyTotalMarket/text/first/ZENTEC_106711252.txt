On Kargil Vijay Diwas Anniversary | Zen Technologies Unveils 
India's First High -Altitude Man -Portable Anti -Drone System 
for Modern Mountain WarfarePR ES S  & MED IA R ELEAS E 1/2Hyderabad,  26 July 2026  | On the occasion  of Kargil  Vijay  Diwas,  Zen Technologies  Limited,  India's  leading  defence  
technology  company,  today  unveiled  its High -Altitude  Man -Portable  Anti -Drone  System  (MPADS) , an indigenous,  
lightweight  counter -UAS solution  engineered  to protect  troops  operating  in mountainous  and remote  terrain .
The launch  commemorates  the courage  and sacrifice  of the Indian  Armed  Forces  during  the Kargil  conflict  while  
addressing  one of the most  pressing  challenges  in modern  warfare : the growing  use of drones  across  high -altitude  
battlefields .
Designed  for rapid  deployment  in difficult  terrain  where  conventional  vehicle -mounted  systems  are impractical,  the 
Man -Portable  Anti-Drone  System  combines  advanced  RF detection,  intelligent  data  fusion,  and omni -directional  
jamming  into a compact,  mission -ready  solution . The system  can be carried  by soldiers,  pack  animals,  all-terrain  
vehicles  (ATVs),  or unmanned  ground  vehicles  (UGVs),  ensuring  mobility  across  diverse  operational  environments .
Zen Technologies  High -Altitude  Man -Portable  Anti -Drone  System,  Features :
•Drone  detection  ranges  of up to 5 km through  continuous  wideband  RF scanning  across  the 400 MHz  to 6 GHz 
spectrum .
•Omni -directional  jamming  up to 3 km, capable  of disrupting  drone  command  links  and GNSS  signals  
simultaneously .
•Multi -target  and swarm  detection  capabilities,  enabling  forces  to counter  coordinated  drone  attacks . 
•24x7, all-weather  operation  with  indigenous,  state -of-the-art architecture . 
At the heart  of the solution  is Zen's  RF-Based  Drone  Detector  (RFDD),  which  employs  advanced  Correlative  
Interferometry  and Digital  Beamforming  techniques  to accurately  determine  the bearing  of hostile  drones . This is 
integrated  with  a Data  Fusion  & Command  Centre  (DFCC)  that consolidates  sensor  inputs  into a single  tactical  
picture,  allowing  operators  to monitor  threat  zones,  classify  aerial  threats,  analyse  spectrum  activity,  and make  
rapid  engagement  decisions .
With  increasing  drone  activity  in high -altitude  regions  and contested  border  areas,  portable  counter -drone  
capabilities  are becoming  an essential  component  of frontline  force  protection . Zen Technologies'  latest  solution  
strengthens  India's  indigenous  defence  ecosystem  by delivering  a scalable,  rapidly  deployable  capability  that enables  
troops  to detect,  track  and neutralize  aerial  threats  wherever  the mission  demands .
