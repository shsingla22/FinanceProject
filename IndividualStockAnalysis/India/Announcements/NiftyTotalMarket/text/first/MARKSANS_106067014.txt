Marksans Pharma Limited  receives USFDA  approval for Loratadine Tablets USP 10 
mg . 
 
Marksans Pharma Limited (Reuters: MARK.BO; Bloomberg: MRKS IN; NSE: 
MARKSANS; BSE: 524404) hereby announces the final approval of the Company’s 
Abbreviated New Drug  Application (ANDA)  for Loratadine Tablets USP 10 mg  from 
USFDA.  
 
They are indicated for the treatment of allergic rhinitis caused by pollen and upper respiratory 
tract allergy for over -the-counter (OTC) use.  
 
This product is generic version of Clartin tablet in the same strength of Bayer Healthcare.  
 
Loratadine is an antihistamine that treats symptoms such as itching, runny nose, watery eyes, 
and sneezing from "hay fever" and other allergies.
