Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
We wish to inform that as required by the NSE, the subject matter of non – compliance with 
various Regulations of SEBI Listing Regulations pertaining to pertaining to Composition of 
Board, statutory committees, requirement of quorum and non-compliance with requirements 
pertaining to quarterly filing of shareholding pattern under Regulation 31(1) of the SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for the quarter ended 
December 31, 2024, and subsequent actions taken by BSE and NSE was placed before 
Board of Directors of the Company at the subsequent meeting held on 16.05.2025. 
 
The Board of Directors had discussed and taken note of the matter of Non-compliance.  
 
A) The Comments made by the Board of Directors with respect to fine levied as per Notice 
ref no. NSE/LIST-SOP/COMB/FINES/0307 vide email dated 17.03.2025 pertaining to 
Composition of Board, statutory committees and requirement of quorum are reproduced 
below: 
 
Quote 
The Board noted that SCI being a Public Sector Undertaking, only the Administrative 
Ministry i.e. MoPSW has the power to appoint/ remove/ change the Directors on the Board 
of the Company. The Board further noted that the Company has requested the MoPSW to 
appoint requisite number of Independent directors and woman Independent Director on the 
Board of the Company. The Board also noted that the appointment of requisite number of 
Independent Directors including woman Independent Director is under active consideration 
of the Ministry. 
Unquote 
 
 
 
The Board also noted that the administrative Ministry i.e. MoPSW has 
appointed/reappointed three Non-official Independent Directors w.e.f. 11.04.2025 and 
consequently, the statutory committees were reconstituted with effect from 11.04.2025. 
 
Please note that the Company has also sent a request letter dated 19.03.2024 to both the 
Stock Exchanges to waive-off the fines levied till date in view of the fact that pursuant to 
appointment/reappointment of Indepedent Directors, the Company is duly compliant with 
quorum requirement as well as of composition of Statutory Committees.  
 
B) The Comments made by the Board of Directors with respect to fine levied as per Notice 
ref no. NSE/LIST-SOP/Reg.31/FINES/0195 vide email dated 14.02.2025 for non-
compliance with Regulation 31 of SEBI Listing Regulations are reproduced below: 
 
Quote 
With respect to the fine levied as mentioned in Annexure 3 of the Agenda, Board after taking 
on record NSE letter dated 14.02.2025, noted that following a logical representation 
submitted by the Company, the fine so imposed was promptly waived by NSE. 
Unquote
