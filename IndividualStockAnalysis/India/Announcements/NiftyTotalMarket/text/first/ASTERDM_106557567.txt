Sub: Intimation of Disruption in Operations and Nurse Strike at Hospitals in Kerala   
Ref: Disclosure  under Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the  SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended (“LODR Regulations”)  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the LODR Regulations , read with SEBI  Master Circular No. 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025- CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, we wish to inform you that certain 
nursing staﬀ across hospitals in Kerala have been participating in a statewide strike from 9 March 2026, 
led by the United Nurses Association, raising demands relating to revision in wage structures.  Given that 
the Company operates seven hospitals in Kerala, the strike  has resulted in temporarily disruption of 
operations, including reduced occupancy levels and skeletal nursing staﬃng i n most of our units . 
The Company has activated internal contingency measures to ensure continuity of critical services and 
patient care across its facilities.  It has also mobilized nurses from its other units across the neighboring 
states like Karnataka to ensure continuity of critical care.   
We further inform that the Kerala Private Hospital Association approached the Hon ’ble High Court of 
Kerala at Ernakulam with a writ petition on this matter. By its order dated March 13, 2026, the Hon’ble 
High Court directed the Nurses Association to defer the strike and ensure that its members do not abstain from work or engage in any form of strike or obstruction until March 19, 2026.  The Hon’ble High Court has 
also referred the hospitals and the nurses to the mediation centre for facilitating and negotiati ng a 
settlement. This order has provide d relief to the Company, enabling uninterrupted operation s during this 
period, as  certain nurses have rejoined and resumed duties .  
Given that the Company, along with industry bodies and other hospital associations in the State, were 
actively engaging with relevant stakeholders to facilitate an early resolution of the situation and given that 
a petition was also instituted in the Hon’b le High Court of Kerala at Ernakulam, the Company is only now 
making this disclosure at this stage after evaluating and assessing the impact of the nurse strike on its 
operations and the materiality of the business.  
  
 
The Company will continue to monitor developments closely and will keep the stock exchanges informed 
of any material updates.
