Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
This is with reference to the news item published/ broadcasted in mainstream media on May 23, 
2025, titled “ Dalmia Bharat under I-T lens post HC nod on reopening of assessment ”, and the material 
movement in the share price of the Company on May 26, 2025 on stock exchanges. 
 
We wish to clarify that the issue pertains to initiation of income tax reassessment proceedings for 
the Assessment Year 2011-12 of the Company and its two subsidiaries, namely Dalmia Cement 
(Bharat) Limited and Dalmia Power Limited.  
 
The said reassessment proceedings were challenged before the Hon’ble Madras High Court, Madurai 
Bench (“High Court”) by way of Writ Petitions by the Company and its subsidiaries. The above 
reassessment proceedings were quashed by the Single Bench of the Hon’ble Madras High Court vide 
a reasoned judgment dated October 30, 2019 since the basis of invoking the extended period for the 
reassessment proceedings were not made out.  
 
The Income Tax Department (“Revenue”) preferred writ appeals before the Division Bench of the 
High Court. The Division Bench, vide its judgment dated April 23, 2025 (“impugned judgment”), has 
allowed the appeal of the Revenue. We are of the view that the findings of the Hon’ble Division 
Bench of the High Court are unsustainable. 
 
The Company and its subsidiaries, being aggrieved by the impugned judgment, have preferred 
Special Leave Petitions before the Hon’ble Supreme Court. The Company is of the view that it has a 
good case on merits. As on date there is no financial impact on the Company/ any of its subsidiaries. 
 
This is for your information and records.
