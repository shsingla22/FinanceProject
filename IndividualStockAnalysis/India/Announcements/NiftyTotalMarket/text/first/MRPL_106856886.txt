PM to Dedicate  MRPL’s  Rs. 330-Crore  Marketing  Terminal  to the
Nation
Bengaluru  , November  12, 2026: Hon’ble Prime Minister  Shri Narendra  Modi is scheduled  to
dedicate  to the nation Mangalore  Refinery  and Petrochemicals  Limited’s  (MRPL)  state-of-the-art
Devangonthi  Marketing  Terminal  in Bengaluru  on November  13, 2026. Developed  at an investment
of Rs. 330 crore, the terminal  marks  a significant  milestone  in strengthening  Karnataka’s  energy
infrastructure,  enhancing  fuel supply reliability  and supporting  the region’s  growing  economic  and
industrial  needs.
Spread  across 20 acres, the terminal  is connected  to MRPL’s  Mangaluru  refinery  through  the 363-km
Petronet  Mangalore—Hassan—Bangalore  Pipeline.  With a storage capacity  of 77,000 kilolitres,  the
facility  handles  petrol,  diesel and Aviation  Turbine  Fuel (ATF),  catering  to Bengaluru  airport,  MRPL’s
HiQ retail outlets,  Oil Marketing  Companies  and industrial  consumers.  Equipped  with 10 automated
truck-loading  bays, advanced  digital control systems  and dedicated  biofuel blending  facilities,  the
terminal  combines  operational  efficiency  with modern  safety and environmental  standards.
A major environmental  benefit  of the project  is the shift from long-distance  road transportation  to
pipeline-based  fuel movement,  particularly  through  the ecologically  sensitive  Western  Ghats. This is
estimated  to avoid approximately  40,000 tonnes  of CO: emissions  annually,  while improving  road
safety . The terminal  also supports  India's clean energy transition  through dedicated  ethanol and
biodiesel  storage  and blending  infrastructure.
Beyond  strengthening  regional  energy  security,  the project  is expected  to generate  over 280 direct  and
indirect employment,  while facilitating  the expansion  of fuel distribution  networks  and industrial
activity  in Karnataka  and neighbouring  markets.
The dedication  of the Devangonthi  Marketing  Terminal  to the nation  will mark another  important  step
in MRPL’s contribution  to India's energy security, sustainable  infrastructure  development  and
economic  growth,  reinforcing  its commitment  to delivering  reliable,  high-quality  energy  to the nation.
