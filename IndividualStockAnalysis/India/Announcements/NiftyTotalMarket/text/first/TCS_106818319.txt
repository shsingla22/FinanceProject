TCS launches Industrial Intelligence Foundry to accelerate AI -led 
autonomy and engineering  
The domain -specific offering  comprises a repository of configurable AI agents, workflows, and 
governance capabilities to help industrial enterprises scale AI applications across their value chains  
 
The Foundry  can help enterprises reduce new product introduction timelines by 25% and improve 
manufacturing productivity by 20 -25%.  
 
MUMBAI , October  15, 2026:  Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS), a global leader in IT 
services, consulting, and business solutions , has launched the  TCS Industrial Intelligence Foundry, an AI 
offering that enables industrial enterprises to move from AI experimentation to scaled deployment across 
engineering and manufacturing. It offers a repository of configurable AI agents, workflows, and governance 
capabilities designed to accelerate enterprise AI adoption.  
 
The Foundry will help enterprises  embed intelligence into core engineering and manufacturing workflows for 
aerospace, mobility, semiconductors, medical devices, industrial electronics, utilities and energy. Unlike 
horizontal copilots or general -purpose generative AI platforms that are not industry specific and require 
enterprises to build AI agents from scratch, the Industrial Intelligence Foundry  offers domain -engineered 
vertical AI applications that are industry specific and integrate with existing workflows.  
 
The offering  can help enterprises reduce new product introduction timelines by 25% and improve 
manufacturing productivity by 20 -25%. As data foundations and operating ecosystems mature, productivity 
gains could exceed 40%, while maintaining quality, governance, and op erational performance.  
 
The TCS Industrial Intelligence Foundry supports industrial AI use cases across the product development and 
manufacturing lifecycle, including:  
• Engineering intelligence and product development acceleration  to streamline design, validation, 
documentation, and new product introduction workflows.  
• Manufacturing operations optimization  through AI -driven insights, workflow orchestration, and 
production performance.  
• Asset performance and operations intelligence  to improve reliability, utilization, and operational 
efficiency.  
• Quality and compliance management  with contextual recommendations, continuous monitoring, 
and governance frameworks.  
• Service lifecycle and workforce enablement  through the Human+AI Service Autonomy Model that 
augments decision -making across engineering, manufacturing, and field operations.  
 
Ramachandran S, Practice Leader, Engineering and Manufacturing, HFS Research, said,  “Most enterprise AI 
still lives on desktops, while there is untapped value on shop floors in industrial and physical AI. According to 
our research, industrial organizations plan to double their physical AI spending over the next two years. That 
is an oppor tunity to unlock latent value across engineering and manufacturing workflows, especially in critical 
areas where prevailing technologies fall short due to cost or functionality. Industrial Intelligence Foundry is 
TCS’ bet to fill this gap with domain -speci fic AI, with the potential for significant business impact, scaling AI 
Implementations and not just another agentic AI project.”

Sreenivasa Chakravarti, Global Head & Vice President of Industrial Autonomy and Engineering, TCS,  said,  
“The next wave of industrial AI will be shaped by how effectively AI agents are  orchestrated to transform 
engineering, plant systems, and asset -centric operations. While enterprises are scaling their use of AI agents, 
our firm belief is that the AI era will  be won by context -aware agentic deployment across enterprises. With 
the Industrial Intelligence Foundry, TCS is helping enterprises to deploy domain -specific, AI applications at 
scale that can accelerate autonomy, and translate industrial knowledge into s calable business outcomes. ” 
 
The TCS Industrial Intelligence Foundry addresses  growing demand for context -aware industrial AI and 
provides a foundation for physical AI -powered o perations.  Its context -aware AI combines domain knowledge, 
engineering data, industry standards, operational constraints, and lifecycle context to deliver relevant and 
actionable outcomes. A model -agnostic architecture provides flexibility across AI ecosystems, while  
applications continuously learn to generate compounding returns. The solution  combines domain -specific 
vertical AI applications with agentic AI and multi -agent workflow orchestration. It uses the Human+AI Service 
Autonomy Model to accelerate adoption while maintaining oversight, trust, and business alignment.
