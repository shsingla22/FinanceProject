PVR INOX GIVES KOCHI A NEW DESTINATION FOR CINEMA, 
CULTURE AND CONTEMPORARY DESIGN WITH MYMOON  
~ Five state -of-the-art screens | 814 premium seats | A Kerala -inspired experience shaped 
by art, design and technology  ~ 
National, September 25,  2026 : PVR INOX, India ’s largest and most premium multiplex chain, has 
unveiled its latest cinema destination at MyMoon, Kochi, bringing together the magic of cinema and the 
spirit of the city. Inspired by Kerala ’s artistic heritage and designed for contemporary audiences, the new 
property combines architecture, technology, dining and community in a distinctive entertainment 
experience.  
PVR INOX has consistently created cinemas that reflect the character of their locations, drawing inspiration 
from local art, culture and design. At MyMoon, this approach comes to life through handcrafted details, 
vintage -inspired elements and a contemporar y interpretation of Kerala ’s visual heritage. Located on the 
prominent Chittoor Road, the property further strengthens PVR INOX ’s presence in South India.  
Antique cinematographic equipment and musical instruments across the public areas pay tribute to the 
evolution of cinema and music. Reclaimed wood, live -edge tabletops and sustainable materials add warmth, 
texture and a refined sense of place to the interiors.  Nature is central to the concept of a “multiplex in a 
garden ”, with plants integrated throughout  the property to create a softer, more open environment.  
The triple -height foyer features comfortable seating, a coffee kiosk, framed photographs and vintage 
musical instruments, creating an inviting arrival experience with the charm of a bygone era. A de dicated 
food street, accessible from both ends, offers covered seating and convenient ordering through piano 
windows.  
With value, choice and transparency at the heart of the offering, MyMoon brings a wider range of  food and 
beverages  options across price points, alongside exceptional, personalised service. The menu includes 
entry -level offerings, MRP food and beverage options, weekday specials, clear vegetarian and non -
vegetarian segregation, and dedicated health and wellness and kids ’ menus. Audiences can  also avail of 
unlimited free refills on the purchase of Large Popcorn and Large Cola, adding greater value to the overall 
cinema experience.  Self-ordering food kiosks have been introduced to help guests skip queues and enjoy a 
faster, more seamless orderi ng experience.  
Additional seating, retail and social spaces encourage audiences to arrive early, stay longer and enjoy the 
destination beyond their film.  Self-ticketing kiosks, digital tabletop menus and app -based ordering make 
the customer journey simple and seamless. The cinema comprises five state -of-the-art screens with a total 
seating capacity of 814.  
Each auditorium has its own visual identity, with distinct themes, colours and design treatments creating a 
varied yet cohesive experience. Premium recl iner seating is available in the last row across four 
auditoriums, while one auditorium features loungers. Advanced projection and sound technologies, 
including PXL, 4K laser projection, Dolby Atmos immersive sound and RealD 3D, bring greater clarity, 
depth and impact to the cinematic experience.  
 
 
 
Mr. Ajay Bijli, Managing Director, PVR INOX Limited, said, “Kerala holds a distinctive place in India ’s 
cinema landscape, with audiences who see films as an expression of culture, identity and community. As 
one of the state ’s leading commercial and cultural centres, Kochi is central to our vision for the future of 
cinema in Kerala. With our new property at MyMoon, we have created more than a cinema , we have 
envisioned a cultural destination rooted in its surroundings and designed for the evolving expectations of 
today ’s audiences. Inspired by Kerala ’s artistic herit age and brought to life through contemporary 
architecture and advanced technology, MyMoon marks a meaningful milestone in our journey and 
strengthens PVR INOX ’s presence in the state. ” 
Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli, Executive Director, PVR INOX Limited, added, “Cinema today is about the 
complete experience, encompassing both the film and the moments around it. At our new cinema at 
MyMoon, we have thoughtfully integrated design, architecture, food and technology to create an inviting 
space where audiences can rela x, connect and spend time together. Drawing on Kerala ’s design heritage 
while responding to contemporary lifestyles, the property reflects our commitment to creating distinctive 
entertainment destinations that are relevant to each market and the audiences they serve. ” 
At MyMoon, PVR INOX brings together the art of the city, the energy of cinema and the ease of 
contemporary entertainment , creating a destination that feels distinctly Kochi, yet globally styled.
