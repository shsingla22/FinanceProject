365squared launches AI-powered voice 
fraud protection solution, 365callguard  
 
New solution helps operator s protect subscribers from 
increasingly sophisticated voice scams and fraud. 
 
MALTA, 3rd November 2026  – 365squared , a Route Mobile company within Proximus Global, has 
launched 365callguard, a new AI-powered voice fraud protection solution designed to help mobile and 
fixed-line operators and voice carriers detect and sto p fraudulent calls before they reach subscribers. 
 
Voice fraud is a fast-growing threat facing operators and carriers. As fraudsters increasingly leverage 
AI and social engineering techniqu es, operators face growing financia l losses, regulatory scrutiny and 
damage to subscriber trust. 
 
365callguard helps operators detect and mitigate seven major types of fraud through a single 
platform: vishing, CLI spoofing, Wangiri, IRSF, SIM box fraud, flash calls and robocalls. The solution combines dedicated AI models with rule-based de tection to provide real-time analysis and clear 
visibility into fraud impact and revenue protection.  
Tonio Ellul, Chief Executive Officer at 365squared , said: “Voice fraud is evolving rapidly and 
becoming more difficult to detect using traditiona l methods. Operators need solutions that not only 
identify suspicious activity but also stop fraud before it affects customers. 365callguard brings 
advanced AI-driven protection across multiple frau d vectors in a single pl atform, helping operators 
protect both revenues an d subscriber trust.”  
 365callguard integrates with existing operator infrastructure and can automatically trigger actions 
such as call blocking, caller ID modification or su bscriber alerts, depending on operator policies and 
network capabilities. 
 Tushar Agnihotri, CEO, Route Mobile , said: “As fraudsters become more sophisticated, security has 
become a strategic priority for operators worl dwide. With 365callguard, 365squared is further 
strengthening Proximus Global’s portfolio of trusted communications solutions, helping our 
customers protect revenues, safegu ard subscribers, and deliver more  secure digital experiences.”  
 365callguard complements 365smsguard, 365squared’ s AI-powered messaging protection platform, 
extending the company’s fraud prevention capabili ties across both voice and messaging channels. 
 
365squared’s solutions also contribute to Proximus  Global’s wider focus on communications security 
across BICS, Route Mobile, Telesign, and their subsidiaries.
