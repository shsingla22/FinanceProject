Bengaluru, 31st July 2025:  Ather Energy Limited, India's leading electric two -wheeler manufacturer , 
has introduced the 450S with a longer IDC range of 161 kms strengthening its performance -focused 
450 Series portf olio. The newly introduced 450S variant is now available with a 3.7kWh battery pack 
catering to riders who demand both thrilling performance and extended range capability. The new 
variant addresses a key market gap by offering a higher range  at a more accessible price point, 
expanding choices for enthusiasts who appreciate the 45 0 series’  sporty character and advanced 
technology.  
Ravneet Phokela, Chief Business Officer, Ather Energy Limited, said, "The 450 series has always 
set the bar for performance, technology, and reliability in the Indian electric scooter market. With the 
launch of the 450S featuring a 3.7 kWh battery pack and an IDC range of 161 kms, we're bringing the 
extended range previousl y exclusive to the 450X. The new 450S variant is specifically designed for 
riders who prioritize the sporty appeal and long -range capability of the 450 series over the advanced 
software features of the 450X. With an IDC range of 161 kms offered by the new 450S, riders can now 
confidently plan longer journeys and still experience the thrilling performance the 450 series is 
renowned for, all at a more accessible price."  
The 450 line has been appreciated for its performance, technology, and reliability since its inception. 
The top -tier 450X 3.7 kWh stands out for its impressive 161 kms IDC range and its premium 
positioning , The 450S 2.9 kWh provided a reasonable entry point into the 450 family  but the new 3.7 
kWh variant bridges this gap by offering extended range at a more attainable price point.  
Sporty Design and Premium Build Quality  
The new 3.7 kWh variant retains all the sporty design elements that define the 450 experience. The 
sharp and angular stance with its distinctive LED headlamp unit maintains the premium appeal that 
performance enthusiasts love. Despite the larger battery pa ck, the new variant maintains Ather's 
renowned engineering standards with stringent testing and attention to detail that performance riders 
expect.  
Extended Range Without Compromise  
In a segment where riders often choose between performance and practical range, the 450S with 3.7 
kWh battery pack offers the perfect balance. The 3.7 kWh variant delivers all the performance 
characteristics that make the 450 series exciting - the same 5.4 kW motor producing 22 Nm of torque, 
0-40 km/h acceleration in 3.9 seconds, and a top speed of 90 km/h. The enhance d battery capacity 
extends the IDC range to 161 km while maintaining the zippy performance and agile handling that 
defines the 450 DNA. The scooter comes with four ride modes - Smart Eco, Eco, Ride, and Sport - 
allowing riders to choose between maximum ran ge and thrilling performance based on their mood and 
requirements.  
Advanced Features and Connectivity  
The 450S 3.7 kWh comes loaded with best -in-class technology and safety features. The scooter 
features a 7 -inch DeepView ™ Display with Bluetooth connectivity , turn -by-turn navigation, and 
automatic brightness adjustment. For safety and convenience, it includes AutoHold ™, Fall Safe, 
Emergency Stop Signal, auto indicator cut -off, Tow & Theft Alerts, Find my scooter, and Amazon Alexa 
Skills integration (all available with the Ather StackTM Pro). The 3.7 kWh variant receives over -the-air 
(OTA) updates, ensuring the scooter stays current with the latest software enhancements. The scooter 
provides seamless access to Ather's comprehensive fast -charging network, the Ather Grid, which 
spans over 3300 + charging points across the country. For home charging, the variant includes Ather's 
efficient charging solution with 0 -80% charging in 4 hours 30 minutes.  
 
Comprehensive Ownership Support  
Like all Ather scooters, the 450S with 3.7 kWh battery pack comes with the option of Ather's 
comprehensive warranty programme, 'Ather Eight70', that provides a comprehensive 8 -year or 80,000 
km (whichever comes first) warranty that guarantees a minimum 70%  battery health over the 
ownership period. The scooter is supported by Ather's expanding network of experience centres 
across the country, where customers can test ride, purchase, and receive after -sales service.  
Availability and Booking  
Bookings for the 450S with 3.7 kWh battery pack are now open across India through Ather's retail 
network and online platforms. Deliveries are expected to commence from August 2025.  
The 450S with 3.7 kWh battery pack will be available at INR 148,047 (ex -showroom) in Delhi, INR 
148,258 (ex -showroom) in Mumbai, INR 145,999 (ex -showroom) in Bengaluru and INR 147,312 (ex -
showroom) in Chennai, positioning it competitively in the extended -range performance electric scooter 
segment while maintaining Ather's value proposition for performance enthusiasts.  
 
About Ather Energy Limited  
Ather Energy Limited (ATHERENERG | 544397 | INE0LEZ01016), a pioneer in the Indian electric two -wheeler 
("E2W") market, (CRISIL Report) was founded by Tarun Mehta and Swapnil Jain in 2013. The company's current 
E2W portfolio comprises two product lines -- the Ather 450 line, which caters to customers seeking performance 
scooters, and the Ather Rizta line, which is targeted at customers seeking convenience scooters for their families. 
The company was the first two -wheeler ("2W") OEM to establish a 2W fast ch arging network, the Ather Grid, in 
India (CRISIL Report). Ather has installed 3,611 Fast chargers and Neighbourhood chargers globally, which 
includes 3,578 Fast chargers and Neighbourhood chargers across India and a total of 33 Fast chargers across 
Nepal a nd Sri Lanka as of March 31, 2025. As at March 31, 2025, globally, Ather Energy had 309 registered 
trademarks, 203 registered designs, and 47 registered patents, in addition to pending applications for 97 
trademarks,10 designs, and 313 patents.
