PRESS RELEASE  
 
G E Shipping contracts to buy a Kamsarmax Dry Bulk Carrier  
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (G E Shipping) has contracted  to buy a Kamsar max 
dry bulk c arrier of about 81,922 dwt on September  2, 2025. The 2016 built vessel is expected to 
join the C ompany’s fleet by Q 3 FY2 6.  
 
The Company’s current owned fleet stands at 38  vessels, comprising 26  tankers ( 5 crude carriers, 
17 product tankers, 4 LPG carrier s) and  12 dry bulk carriers aggregating 3.04 mn dwt . The 
Company’s current capacity utilization is close to 100%. In addition to this contract , the company 
has 1 s econdhand Kamsarmax dry bulk carrier  contracted to buy in July’ 25 and 1 Suezmax  crude 
carrier , which was contracted to buy in Aug ’25. Both these ships are  also expected to join the fleet 
by Q3 FY26.  
 
The proposed ship will be financed entirely from  internal accruals. The purpose of the acquisition 
is expansion of the fleet.  
 
Post delivery of the se 3 vessel s, the Company will have 41 vessels aggregating 3.37 mn dwt.
