V-Mart Honoured with Silver Shield at ICAI Awards for Excellence in Financial 
Reporting 2024–25  
V-Mart Retail Limited, one of India’s leading value fashion retailers, is proud to 
announce that it has been conferred the Silver Shield in the Services Category at the 
prestigious ICAI Awards for Excellence in Financial Reporting 2024 –25, in January 
2026.  
The award has been instituted by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) 
— the apex statutory body regulating the accounting profession in India. The ICAI 
Awards are widely regarded as among the most credible and distinguished recognitions in the field of financial reporting, celebrating excellence in transparency, disclosure 
standards, statutory compliance, and the overall quality of financial statements across 
corporate India.  
This recognition marks a significant milestone in V -Mart’s journey of building robust 
governance frameworks and maintaining best -in-class financial reporting practices. It 
reflects the Company’s unwavering commitment to accountability, integrity, and 
stakeholder trust.  

With this honour, V -Mart joins  a distinguished league of organisations that have 
received all three premier accolades in governance and reporting excellence:  
● Golden Peacock Award for Corporate Governance (2022) –  awarded excellence 
in corporate governance presented by the Institute of Directors.  
 
● ICSI Award for Excellence in Corporate Governance (2024) –  awarded by the 
Institute of Company Secretaries of India, acknowledging exemplary governance 
frameworks and processes.  
 
● ICAI Award for Excellence in Financial Reporting (2025) –  recogni sing 
outstanding financial transparency and reporting standards.  
 
As V -Mart celebrates this proud moment, the Company reaffirms its commitment to 
further elevating its financial reporting and governance benchmarks while continuing to 
drive sustainable grow th.
