VIDA – powered by Hero , today marked a major milestone in its global journey with the start of its 
European operations with the commencement of custom er deliveries across the European cities .  
Supported by its strategic partner Elektrorider , VIDA's Direct -to-Consumer (D2C) sales model  will 
activate across 10 European countries: Italy, Spain, Germany, France, Slovenia, Slovakia, 
Romania, Hungary, the Czech Republic and Croatia , for the VIDA DIRT. E K3.   Both the VIDA VX2 
and VIDA DIRT.E K3 will re tail at stores across six markets: Romania, Hungary, the Czech 
Republic, Slovenia, Slovakia and Croatia.  
 
   
Backed by Hero MotoCorp's global scale and proven manufacturing credibility, VIDA brings a family -

convenient home charging, and a first electric ride for young  riders.

the convenience of a traditional scooter with electric mobility.   
 
 Performance : 140 km WMTC range (Eco Mode) and a 90 km/h top speed.  
 Charging:  Twin r emovable IP67 -rated batteries for convenient at -home charging.  
 Availability:  Test rides and retail availability begin in December 2026 across Romania,

Novus Labs: Hero MotoCorp Introduces it s Future -Mobility innovation   Hub  
Hero MotoCorp introduces Novus Labs, its Chairman -led, dedicated future -mobility innovation hub 
at EICMA 2026. Novus Labs starts from people rather than products. It explores unmet mobility 
opportunities, experiments with solutions and executes them as working prototypes. The results 
graduate into Hero MotoCorp, VIDA or new ventures when ready.  
 
First showcased as Novus Series at EICMA 2025, its NEX range won the Red Dot Design Concept 
Award 2026 . At EICMA 2026, Novus Labs presented NEX 1 and NEX 2 as working prototypes , 
demonstrating its ambition to transform emerging ideas into future -ready mobility solutions. 
Explore, Experiment, Execute is a continuing cycle, not a one -time showcase. Novus Lab s will keep 
exploring new mobility opportunities, with further concepts following from its idea bank over time.  
 
 
 
   
NEX 1: A personal, portable and compact   electric vehicle - Built for the fragmented, in -between 
stretches of a journey, NEX 1 folds from a ca rried object into a rideable one. Showcased last year at 
EICMA 2025 as a concept, it returns as a new, improved version. Product highlights: a top speed of 
15 km/h and a range of 20 kms.  
 
NEX 2: Agile, Stable and Multi -Use Trike: Pairing two -wheeler agilit y with greater safety, stability 
and utility of  three wheels , NEX 2 is moving into its next stage of development. Product highlights: 
a targeted range of over 150 km (WMTC), a top speed above 100 km/h and generous storage space.  
 
Novus Labs is open by des ign. It invites collaborators, innovators, technologists and institutions who 
are passionate about shaping the mobility opportunities of the future to build next with it.  
 
Shaping the Next Generation of Mobility at a special Technology Showcase   
At a dedic ated Technology Hub, Hero MotoCorp and VIDA presented their long -term research 
direction for two -wheeled mobility. Rather than focusing solely on immediate product launches, the 
showcase explored emerging technologies and their potential applications acros s four areas.  
 Rider -Centric, Intuitive Experiences : AI-powered interfaces simplify vehicle data through 
features such as facial recognition for keyless ignition and an AI voice assistant integrated 
into a 7 -inch TFT Android Auto display.  
 Expanding the EV Ecosystem : VIDA’s evolving ecosystem pushes electric mobility beyond 
the ride. The newly unveiled Energy Dock transforms the battery into a versatile power hub, 
introduced alongside a seamless wireless charger for the VIDA VX2 platform.   
 Reimagining Powert rains : A multi -pathway approach advances both ICE and electric 
technologies, focusing on multi -fuel capability, smart controls and advanced thermal 
management to enhance performance and ownership value.  
 Intelligent Safety : Guided by Safety as the New Perfo rmance, innovations focus on crash 
prevention through radar - and sensor -based rider assistance, AI -enabled monitoring, 
adaptive lighting, and VIDA’s Smart Helmet with on -vehicle AI -powered chin -strap detection 
that operates without cloud dependency.  
 
Together, these initiatives reflect Hero MotoCorp’s commitment to developing mobility solutions that 
combine engineering excellence, rider -focused innovation and a global perspective.  
 
About Hero MotoCorp  
Hero MotoCorp, headquartered in New Delhi, India, i s the world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and 
scooters for 25 consecutive years with ~$5 billion revenue. The Company’s global footprint spans 54 countries 
across Asia, Africa, Europe, Central and Latin America with a customer base of over 130 mill ion. The Company 
operates eight manufacturing facilities - six in India and one each in Colombia and Bangladesh - along with two 
Research and Development centres: Hero Centre for Innovation and Technology (CIT) in India and Hero Tech 
Centre Germany (TCG). Hero MotoCorp is driving the transition to electric mobility through VIDA. Strategic 
alliances with Harley -Davidson and Zero Motorcycles support the design and development of premium and 
electric motorcycles, respectively.  
 
Further demonstrating its commit ment to the EV future, the Company has made strategic investments in two - 
and three -wheeler EV manufacturers, Ather Energy and Euler Motors. Hero MotoCorp is the only Indian two -
wheeler manufacturer listed on the prestigious Dow Jones Sustainability Index (DJSI).
