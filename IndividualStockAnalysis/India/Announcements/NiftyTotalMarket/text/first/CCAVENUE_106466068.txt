edge technologies such as artificial intelligence, ma chine learning, data analytics, and automation to 
strengthen and scale its digital payments  and service offerings.  The proposed object clause also 
supports the Company’s vision to create a unified brand architecture for its upcoming AI -powered 
offerings, i ncluding predictive payments, automated customer engagement, low -code/no -code 
application frameworks, intelligent business solutions, and other advanced digital innovations.
