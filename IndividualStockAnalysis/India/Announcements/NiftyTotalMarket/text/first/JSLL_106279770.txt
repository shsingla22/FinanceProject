Dear Sir/Madam,  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we 
wish to inform you that Jeena Sikho Lifecare Limited has successfully received approval on clinical trial of 
Diabetes Tablets named as “ Shuddhi Dr. Madhumeh” for persons suffering from diabetes.  
The trial has been prospectively registered with the Clinical Trials Registry – India (CTRI) under registration 
number CTRI/2025/02/ 080640 dated February 17, 2025 . 
Key highlights of the study are as follows:  
- Trial Design: Open -label, single -arm, post -marketing surveillance study . 
- Sample Size: 40 participants  
- Study Duration: 3 months  
- Trial Site: GS Ayurveda Medical College & Hos pital, Pilkhuwa, Ghaziabad (U.P.)  
- Principal Investigator: Dr. Rakhi Mehra  
- Primary Sponsor: Jeena Sikho Lifecare Limited  
This development is in line with our commitment to ensuring product safety and efficacy through structured 
scientific evaluation  and is expected to support future product validations and market positioning.  
The copy of Clinical Trial Report is also being made available on the Company’s website www.jeenasikho.com . 
 
The above is for your information and record.
