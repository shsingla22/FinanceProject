Pursuant to the approval of the Board of Directors and in continuation to our letters dated March 27, 
2025, April 29, 2025 and July 31, 2025, regarding the acquisition of additional 13.0% stake in Pioneer 
Stationery Private Limited (‘ Pioneer’), a subsidiary of DOMS Industries Limited (‘ the Company ’), we 
are pleased to inform that, the Company has completed the partial acquisition of additional shares in 
Pioneer. The Company has purchased 3,900 Equity Shares from certain existing shareholders of 
Pioneer, for a total consideration of ₹ 5,53,80,000 (Rupees Five Crores Fifty Three Lakhs Eighty 
Thousand Only) thereby increasing the Company’s shareholding in Pioneer to 57.5% from 51.0% of its 
total paid-up equity share capital. This transaction was completed on August 30, 2025.

December 31, 2025, as communicated earlier vide our letter dated July 31, 2025.
