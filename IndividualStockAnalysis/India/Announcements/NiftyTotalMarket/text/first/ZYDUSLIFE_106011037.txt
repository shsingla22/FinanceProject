Zydus receives tentative  approval from USFDA for 
Enzalutamide Tablets, 40 mg and 80 mg  
 
Ahmedabad, India, 02 October , 2024  
Zydus Lifesciences Limited  (including its subsidiaries/ affiliates , hereafter referred to as 
“Zydus”) has received tentative approval from the United States Food and Drug Administration 
(USFDA) to manufacture  Enzalutamide Tablets , 40 mg  and 80 mg  (USRLD: Xtandi® Tablets , 
40 mg , 80 mg ).  
Enzalutamide tablets  are androgen receptor inhibitors indicated for the treatment of patients 
with castration -resistant prostate cancer and metastatic castration -sensitive prostate cancer.  
Enzalutamide Tablets  will be produced  at the Group’s manufacturing site at  SEZ, Ahmedabad.   
Enzalutamide tablets , 40 mg  and 80 mg  had annual sales o f USD 1417.2 mn in the United 
States (IQVIA MAT  July 2024).  
The group now has 400 approvals and has so far filed over 465* ANDAs since the 
commencement of the filing process in FY 2003 -04.  
 
(*as of 30th June 2024)
