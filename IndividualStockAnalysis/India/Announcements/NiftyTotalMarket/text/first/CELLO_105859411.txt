CommentingontheResult,Mr.PradeepRathod,Chairman&ManagingDirector,CelloWorldLimitedsaid
“Cello World has shown growth in both revenue and profitability compared to Q4 FY23. In the quarter gone
by, revenues increased by 5%, while EBITDA rose by 20%. We experienced good growth in our writing
instrumentsandfurniturebusinessesduringthisperiod.
For the full year, our Revenues have grown by 11% with profitability growing even faster at 24% despite the
well-recognized sluggish demand within the industry throughout the year. This is a testament to our
widespreadmarketpresence, well-establishedbrand,scale ofoperations, andPANIndiadistributionnetwork,
which help us identify the changing needs and preferences of our customers and, hence, innovate our
products accordingly to cater to those requirements and stay relevant in the market, as well as having an
edgeovercompetitors.
Looking forward to FY25, we anticipate a growth rate of 15%-17%, with demand expected to rise in the
second half ofthe year.Our new glassware facility inRajasthanwill commenceoperations inQ2 FY25, witha
ramp-up by year end. This will substantially enhance our glassware business, driving rapid growth in this
vertical over the next two years. Additionally, on the e-commerce front, new avenues have emerged,
presentingexcellentopportunitiesforfuturegrowth.
At Cello, we always focus on outperforming industry growth and continuously improving our position in the
consumermarket.Weremainconfidentinourbusiness'sgrowthpotentialanditsdiverseportfolio,whichalso
benefitsfromthetrendofpremiumization.”
