 c.  A brief on types of customers
   The Company is primarily engaged in the wholesale trade and contract manufacturing of maternity, baby, and kids’

education, training, and allied areas.

customer base.

  •  Retailers engaged in the sale, promotion, and distribution of maternity, baby, and kids’ products,
