Subject - Update on N Dream AG (“N-Dream”) Subsidiary of the Company   
 
Ref -    Disclosure of events & information pursuant to Regulation 30 SEBI (Listing  
    Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, (“Listing Regulations”) 
 
This is in reference to the outcome of Board Meeting held on January 29, 2026, we wish to inform 
you that the Company has completed the transfer of its 26% stake in N-Dream to KPIT 
Technologies (UK) Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary. Following this transaction, KPIT UK now 
holds a 90% stake in N Dream, resulting in N Dream becoming a step-down subsidiary of KPIT 
India. 
  
The purpose of this transfer is to achieve parity and restructure within the KPIT group’s 
organizational structure. KPIT group’s overall holding in N-Dream remains unchanged at 90%. 
 
N-Dream is a Cloud based Game Aggregation Platform company, based in Switzerland. This 
strategic investment in N-Dream is part of KPIT’s roadmap to enable Automotive OEMs enhance 
the driver & passenger experience in the Cockpit of the Future. KPIT will offer complementary 
software integration & validation services to N-Dream’s Automotive clients. Both parties will 
collaborate towards offering value-added data products for Automotive OEMs, thereby enabling 
them to create additional monetizable experiences & feature
