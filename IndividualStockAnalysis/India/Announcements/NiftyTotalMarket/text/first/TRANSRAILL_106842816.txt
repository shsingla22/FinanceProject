Earnings  Press  Release   
Transrail delivers 32% Y -o-Y Revenue Growth in Q2 FY27  
while Sustaining Strong EBITDA Margins ; 
Achieves Highest ever quarterly Revenue of Rs. 2,056 Crore  
 
Cash flow from operations increased to Rs.  435 Crore  for H1 FY 27  
 
Mumbai | Tuesday, 3rd November 2026 - Transrail Lighting Limited  (Transrail) , a leading Indian 
engineering, procurement and construction company with a primary focus on Power T&D and integrated 
manufacturing facilities for lattice structures, conductors and monopoles, today announced its financial 
results for Q2 & H1 FY27.

Operational Highlights : 
• Delivered highest quarterly performance  with revenue from operation of Rs. 2,056 crore  and EBIDTA 
of Rs. 238 crore . 
• Successfully completed a major river crossing transmission project in Bangladesh, demonstrating 
strong execution capabilities and further strengthening Transrail’s track record in international 
markets.  
• Healthy unexecuted order book of Rs. 14,218 crore  as of 30th September 2026. Additionally,  
received new order s worth  Rs. 1,747 crore  post quarter end.  
• Significantly expanded manufacturing capacity, with tower capacity increasing to  184,400 MTPA 
and conductor capacity to  40,800 KMPA, strengthening Transrail’s ability to support execution as 
well as growth in engineered product sales.  The remaining planned capex is on schedule.  
• Continued focus on s trengthen ing the balance sheet, Cashflow from ope rations  for H1 FY27 have 
increased to Rs. 435 crore  and the NWC days stood at 82 days . 
 
Commenting on the results, Mr. Randeep Narang, MD & CEO said:  
“We delivered a remarkable performance during the quarter, with growth in both revenue and profitability  
Y-O-Y and superior cashflow management, supported by strong execution across our businesses. This robust 
performance is achieved despite the continued impact on supply chain and logistics , due to the West Asia 
conflict.  
The demand environment for Power T&D remains strong, driven by investments in transmission infrastructure 
and renewable energy integration. Our enhanced capacities and capabilities position us to capitalise on these 
opportunities, while selectively pursuin g adjacent businesses that leverage our engineering and execution 
strengths.

About  Transrail  Lighting  Limited:  
Transrail  is a leading  turnkey  engineering,  procurement  and construction  (EPC)  company with  primary  focus  on 
power  transmission  and distribution  business  with  4 decades  of experience.  With its headquarters  in India,  it is a 
global  enterprise  with  a footprint  in 64 countries  across  6 continents.  It provides turnkey  solutions  from  Designing,  
Engineering,  Supply,  Manufacture,  Construction,  Testing  services  across  all its business verticals which include  
Transmission Lines, Substations, Civil Construction, Railways, Solar and Pole & Lighting.  It has more  than  2,900 
employees  on its roll.  As part  of Power  T&D  business, Transrail  has large -scale manufacturing  facilities  in India  for 
Galvanized  Lattice  Towers,  Overhead  Conductors  and Galvanized  Monopoles  in addition to a well -accredited 
Tower testing facility.
