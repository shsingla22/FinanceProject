Clinical Achievements: - 
KIMS Kurnool  
·        The doctor at KIMS Hospitals, Kurnool have successfully removed stones from the 
Kidney of a six month old baby. This is the first reported case of such a condition in the state, 
which occurs in 10 out of every one lakh children.  
·        A rare and extraor dinary case of Kidney disease has emerged in Kurnool, where a 4 -
year-old boy was diagnosed with Lipoprotine Glomerulopathy (LPG), one of the rarest kidney 
disorders worldwide. The medical team at KIMS Hospital, Kurnool, successfully treated the 
boy, making  this the first reported case in south India and the youngest patient globally to be 
diagnosed with LPG.  
Lipoprotine Glomerulopathy has only 200 documented cases worldwide, primarily in China 
and Japan, and is usually diagnosed in adults. Identifying it in  a 4 year old is unprecedented.  
Lipoprotine Glomerulopathy is an exceptionally rare disorder, where Cholesterol and 
triglycerides accumulated in the kidneys, causing protein leakage and potential kidney 
failure. This discovery of this disease in a child hi ghlights the importance of early diagnosis 
and intervention to prevent severe complications.  
  
·        In a remarkable medical feat, doctors at KIMS Hospital, Kurnool, performed three 
complex surgeries simultaneously to save a critically ill patient, who se condition had been 
deemed inoperable even by major hospitals in Hyderabad. The doctors removed gallbladder, 
repositioning the exterior section of intestine and excising the damaged portion, followed by 
reattachment of the upper and lower intestinal sect ions. As a result, his condition is fully 
stabilised and he can now eat normally.  
KIMS Sunshine  
·        A 30 year old Baharaini male patient successfully underwent a complex Navigation 
Guided Awake Craniotomy surgery at KIMS -sunshine Hospitals, Begumpet,  to treat a brain 
cavernoma. The patient has suffered recurrent seizures for three months. During surgery, 
the patient remained conscious, interacting with the team and responding to stimuli on a 
monitor screen. The patient was discharged on the second pos toperative day in stable 
condition, walking independently without new neurological deficits.  
  
·        In a significant advancement for orthopaedic care, the doctors at KIMS Sunshine 
Hospital have successfully performed the city’s first arthroscopic cart ilage repair surgery. The 
pioneering procedure was conducted on a 25 year old male athlete who suffered a 
debilitating shoulder injury during a basketball game. The Procedure not only marks a 
milestone for the hospital but also stands as a testament to the  importance of timely medical 
intervention for shoulder injuries.  
KIMS Saveera  
·        A rare and life -threatening condition involving stones in the liver was successfully 
treated at KIMS Saveera Hospitals, Anantapur. A 50 year old farmer from Tadipatri 
underwent a complex surgery at the hospital successfully. Investigation revealed multiple 
stones in the liver, an extremely rare occurrence. Stones are typically found in the kidney or 
gall bladder, but in this case, the stones had migrated from the bile d uct to the liver. 
Additionally, the small intestine had become significantly narrowed, compounding the 
patient’s issues.  
  
·        Doctors at KIMS Saveera Hospitals successfully treated a 62 year old farmer from 
Dharmavaram who had been experiencing sever e shortness of breath for the past three 
months, along with coughing up blood. The timely intervention, involving a bronchial artery 
embolization procedure, saved his life. If this treatment was delayed, the patient’s condition 
could have worsened, even be coming life threatening.   
KIMS ICON – Vizag  
·        In a strange incident, a 52 year old man unknowingly swallowed his set of implanted 
teeth in his sleep. The six -tooth set reached his right lung and got lodged in it. However, the 
set was successfully re moved by doctors without causing any damage to his lungs.    
  
·        A 46 year old  patient, was presented with a massive staghorn stone measuring 5x5 cm 
in his left kidney, was successfully treated at KIMS ICON Hospitals. The patient was passing 
blood in his urine for three months and had consulted multiple hospitals, where his conditio n 
could not be addressed. It was discovered that the staghorn stone had significantly impacted 
his kidneys, reducing it to only 30% functionality. The doctors opted for a minimally invasive 
laparoscopic approach to remove the stone.  
Staghorn kidney stones are massive stones that occupy a significant portion of the kidney, 
often impairing its functionality and causing severe complications such as recurrent infections 
and blood in the urine.  
  
·        A 64 year old individual diagnosed with a brain aneurysm received treatment through 
the Trenza embolization system at KIMS_ICON Hospital, marking the first such procedure in 
the Telugu states AP and Telangana. The Interventional radiology team utilized the Ternza 
device, employing the intra, employing the ‘intra sascular flow diversion’ technique, which 
represents a significant advancement in the treatment of brain aneurysms.  
Brain Aneurysms are characterized by bulges in the blood vessels of the brain, which pose 
a risk of rupture and subsequent haemorrhage. The  ‘intrasaccular flow diversion’ method, 
facilitated by the Trenza device, effectively addresses these conditions.

About KIMS Hospitals:  
KIMS Hospitals is one of the largest corporate healthcare groups in India with hospitals in Telangana, 
Andhra Pradesh, and Maharashtra, providing multi -disciplinary integrated healthcare services, with 
a focus on tertiary and quaternary healthcare at an affordable cost. Our network of 16 hospitals and 
over 5000 beds is spread across Telangana (Secunderabad, Kondapur, Gachibowli, and 
Begump et), Andhra Pradesh (Nellore, Rajahmundry, Srikakulam, Ongole, Vizag - 2 units, 
Anantapur, Guntur and Kurnool), Maharashtra (Nagpur and Nashik) and Kerala (Kannur). Our new 
units, under construction, include 2 hospitals in Bangalore and one in Mumbai.  We offer a bouquet

neurosciences, gastric sciences, orthopedics , organ transplantation, renal sciences, and mother &
