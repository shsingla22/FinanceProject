HCLTech wins 2025 ServiceNow Emerging Industry Partner of the Year award  
 
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, May 6, 2025 —HCLTech,  a global technology leader, has won  the 
2025 ServiceNow Emerging Industry Partner of the Year. This award recognizes  HCLTech's 
capabilities in  delivering transformative digital solutions and redefining total experience 
through AI, automation and ServiceNow -powered innovations  that improve customer 
experiences and drive measurable outcomes.  
 
HCLTech's  ServiceNow offerings, enhanced with GenAI and Agentic AI capabilities, transform 
enterprise service management. By integrating cognitive intelligence and adaptive 
automation, these solutions em power enterprises in BFSI, Manufacturing, IT and 
Telecommunications to modernize their IT and operations efficiently, make insightful 
decisions and achieve greater agility and innovation.  
 
HCLTech's deep understanding of emerging industries such as Manufac turing,  Financial 
Services , Life Sciences and Healthcare (LSH), Telecommunications, and more has enabled 
them to develop and deliver truly impactful solutions that address the unique challenges and 
opportunities within these sectors. Their expertise and co mmitment to customer success have 
earned them the Industry Partner of the Year award, showcasing the transformative power of the Now Platform across diverse industries," said Holly Briedis, Senior Vice President, Global 
Industry Go To Market, ServiceNow.  
 
"We are honored to be named the 2025 ServiceNow Emerging Industry Partner of the Year.  
With the growing adoption of  GenAI, our partnership with ServiceNow enables enterprises to 
seamlessly integrate diverse systems, adopt new -age operating models, modernize  
operations and deliver AI -driven Total Experience (TX) solutions ," said Ravi Yeddanapudi, 
Executive Vice President of Digital Foundation  Services  at HCLTech.  
 
The 2025 Partner Awards recognize the invaluable contributions of ServiceNow partners i n 
driving innovation and customer success . These awards celebrate excellence across all go -to-
market motions —honoring those who lead implementations, manage services, expand market 
reach and build cutting -edge solutions. By acknowledging global impact and regional 
expertise, the awards reinforce the power of collaboration in delivering exceptional value to 
businesses worldwide.
