Sub: Consideration of Soltrix Energy Solution Private Limited as Subsidiary

Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
In our earlier intimation dated 23.06.2025, we had informed about the acquisition of 49% 
equity stake in Soltrix Energy Solution Private Limited (“Soltrix”)  by EIE Renewables 
Private Limited (“EIE Renewables”),  a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company and 
subsequent execution of Share Purchase and Options Agreement for acquiring the 
remaining 51% equity stake in Soltrix after the expiry of one year from the completion of the 
project awarded to Soltrix. It was also stated that, upon such acquisition, Soltrix would be 
classified as an Associate Company of the Company. 
 
However, based on consultations with our legal advisors and after reviewing the clauses of 
the Share Purchase and Options Agreement—particularly those granting EIE Renewables 
effective control over the management and operations of Soltrix—it has been concluded that 
Soltrix qualifies as a subsidiary  of EIE Renewables and, consequently, a Step-Down 
Subsidiary of the Company w.e.f. 23.06.2025, instead of being classified as an Associate 
Company as previously communicated. 
 
Kindly take the above information on record.
