Sterling Relaunches Reimagined Sterling Corbett with Unforgettable Experiences  
 
12th Dec 2024 Sterling Holiday Resorts, India's leading hospitality brand, proudly unveils the revitalized 
Sterling Corbett, a serene retreat nestled amidst the pristine beauty of Uttarakhand. This relaunch 
adds to Sterling’s growing portfolio of wildlife and getaway de stinations, including Kanha, Pench, Gir, 
Sariska, Wayanad, Thekkady, Anaikatti and upcoming resorts in Ranthambore, Jawai , with more to 
come.  
Located away from the hustle and bustle of Ramnagar , Sterling Corbett is a  3-acre haven offer ing 
sweeping views of  the majestic Shivalik hills and the tranquil Ramganga River, blending nature's 
serenity with contemporary comforts. Perfectly positioned for wildlife enthusiasts and seekers of 
peaceful escapes alike, Sterling Corbett promises a harmonious balance of adve nture and relaxation.  
“Sterling Corbett is uniquely positioned to offer guests the dual charm of Corbett’s natural splendour 
and refined hospitality. After an exhilarating safari in the national park, our guests can unwind in a 
tranquil environment with up graded facilities that elevate their stay. At Sterling Holiday Resorts, we 
are dedicated to curating exceptional and immersive experiences, ensuring every visit leaves lasting 
memories,” shared Mr. Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director & CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited . 
The resort has been reimagined with newly refurbished accommodations and enhanced spaces, 
including an upgraded restaurant, spa, swimming pool, banquet hall &  lush lawns. Each room, whether 
a Mountain View Privilege Suite, Premier Room, or  Classic Room, is thoughtfully designed to immerse 
guests in the surrounding natural beauty, with several offering private balconies with breathtaking views of the surrounding hills and river.  
The culinary journey at Sterling Corbett is equally enchanting. The in-house restaurant, aptly named 
Tree House , serves a delectable array of regional Kumaoni delicacies such as Bhat Ki Chudkani  
alongside global cuisines, ensuring a feast for the senses.  
After thrilling morning and evening safaris, guests can relax with a refreshing swim in the pool overlooking verdant landscapes, indulge in holistic treatments at the spa, which combines Ayurvedic 
traditions with modern wellness techniques, enjoy a cozy bonfire under the stars, try stargazing for a 
magical cele stial experience, or simply savor hi-tea while taking in the spectacular views of the pristine 
hills.  
The resort’s upgraded banquet facilities  with a  270
0 spectacular view , make it an inspiring choice for 
corporate events  and social celebration s. With its banquet and multiple lawns , the resort is the ideal 
choice for dreamy & intimate destination weddings. The expansive lawns and the option to book the 
entire resort for an exclusive wedding further enhance its appeal.  
Sterling Corbett is one of 18-Sterling resorts that warmly welcome furry friends, offering designated 
pet-friendly areas and a specially curated menu for pets, ensuring an inclusive and delightful 
experience for every family member.  
Conveniently located near Pantnagar Airport and Ramnagar Railw ay Station, Sterling Corbett serves 
as an excellent gateway to explore Uttarakhand. Its proximity to other Sterling properties in Haridwar, 
Rishikesh, Dehradun, Mussoorie, and Nainital opens the door to a rich tapestry of experiences across 
the state.  
 
Discover the magic of the wild and the serenity of the hills at the all -new Sterling Corbett, where every 
stay is designed to delight and inspire.
