 Coforge Launches BridgeCX Real -Time Personalization Solution Built on Pega Customer 
Decision Hub and Mastercard Dynamic Yield  
 
New solution  transforms fragmented personalization into intelligent 1:1 customer engagement  
 
Princeton, NJ, USA | Greater Noida, India – November 3, 2026 - Coforge Limited  (NSE: 
COFORGE),  a global AI-native  engineering  services  leader,  today  announced  the launch  of BridgeCX , 
a next -generation customer engagement solution built on Pega Customer Decision HubTM and 
integrated  with Mastercard  Dynamic  Yield.  
 
Coforge’s  BridgeCX  connects  enterprise -grade  decisioning  with real-time digital  execution  to help 
organizations deliver truly intelligent, hyper -personalized customer experiences at scale. Developed  by 
Coforge’s  digital experience  experts,  the solution  bridges  the critical  gap between customer insight and 
customer action by seamlessly integrating strategic decisioning with digital personalization.  
 
In today’s  digital  economy,  many  enterprises  capture  massive  volumes  of customer  signals  across  
channels  but struggle  to translate  those  signals  into immediate,  meaningful  engagement. BridgeCX 
addresses this challenge by creating a continuous intelligence loop that unifies data, decisioning, and 
experience delivery.  
 
BridgeCX is built on Pega Customer Decision Hub, the AI -powered decisioning platform for 
personalized experiences at scale. It unifies data, analytics, and channels to determine the next best 
action for every customer interaction, helping marketers simplif y engagement, move faster, and 
increase customer lifetime value.  
 
By combining  the power  of Pega’s  AI-driven  decisioning  with Mastercard’s  Dynamic Yield digital  
personalization engine, BridgeCX enables enterprises to analyze customer intent, determine the next -
best action, and instantly activate personalized experiences across digital touchpoints in real time.  
The result  is a unified  engagement  framework that moves enterprises beyond  fragmented 
personalization efforts toward decision -led, context -aware customer journeys.  
 
BridgeCX  enables  organizations  to: 
• Turn omnichannel  customer  signals  into real-time digital  actions  
• Deliver  hyper -personalized  experiences  across  web, mobile,  and digital  channels  
• Enable  closed -loop adaptive  learning  that continuously  optimizes  engagement  outcomes
