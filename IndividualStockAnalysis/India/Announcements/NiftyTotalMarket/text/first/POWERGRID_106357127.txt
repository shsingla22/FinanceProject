POWERGRID Board comprised of five Executive (functional) directors including the

Independent Directors were appointed on 16th April, 2025 and one Independent Woman

the Board Meeting scheduled on 01st May, 2025. No meeting of these Statutory

compliant with the requirement of having Woman director/ Independent Woman Director

under Regulation 18(1), 19(1)/ 19(2), 20(2)/ (2A) and 21(2) of the SEBI LODR for major 
part of the quarter ended 30th June, 2025.

Woman Director).
