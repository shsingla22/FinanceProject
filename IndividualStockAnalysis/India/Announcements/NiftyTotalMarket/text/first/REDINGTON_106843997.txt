Information Classification: RL \Public  Redington Q2 FY27 revenue rises 48% to ₹43,002 crore  
● PAT excluding exceptional items grows 5 0% to ₹ 581 crore  
● India revenue increases 67%; Middle East and Africa revenue rises 41%  
● Technology Solutions Group revenue grows 91%  
 
Chennai, India, 4th November 2026: Redington Limited (NSE: REDINGTON; BSE: 532805), a leading 
technology solutions provider , today announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 
2026.  
Redington reported consolidated revenue of ₹ 43,002 crore in Q2 FY27, representing year-on -year growth 
of 48%, while profit after tax (PAT), excluding exceptional items, increased 50% to ₹ 581 crore. PAT margin 
for the quarter stood at 1. 35%.  PAT excluding Arena was ₹657 crore, up by 55% YoY; PAT margin @1.57%.  
The quarter’s performance was supported by growth across major business groups and key markets. India 
revenue grew 67% year-on -year, driven by enterprise technology and data centre investments, demand 
for PCs and mobility devices, and continued adoption of cloud and cybersecurity solutions.  
Revenue across the Middle East and Africa increased 41% year -on-year, supported by demand for 
enterprise infrastructure, cloud and cybersecurity solutions.  
 
Commenting on the results, V. S. Hariharan, Managing Director & Group CEO, Redington Limited, said:  
“Our Q2 performance reflects strong demand across key markets and the disciplined execution of our 
teams and partners. As customers invest in enterprise infrastructure, cloud, cybersecurity and AI, they need the right technologies and capabilities to work together. Our role as a technology ecosystem 
orchestrator is to connect these capabilities with our partner network, helping customers adopt, secure and manage their technology. We will continue to expand our market reach, deepen partner engagement and inv est in services, digital routes to market and AI capabilities to support long -term growth.”  
 Business performance  
Technology Solutions Group (TSG). Revenue grew 91% year -on-year, driven by enterprise and data centre 
investments and demand for infrastructure modernisation. Mobility Solutions Group (MSG). Revenue increased 40% year -on-year, supported by demand for 
premium smartphones, new product introductions and the expansion of retail -led distribution models.  
Software Solutions Group (SSG). Revenue rose 39% year -on-year, reflecting demand for cybersecurity, 
cloud and software solutions, alongside the adoption of AI-enabled offerings and subscription -based 
models.  
Endpoint Solutions Group (ESG). Revenue grew 38% year-on -year, supported by commercial PC demand 
and higher device prices.  
Redington continues to monitor global geopolitical developments, with a focus on operational resilience and business continuity across its markets.
