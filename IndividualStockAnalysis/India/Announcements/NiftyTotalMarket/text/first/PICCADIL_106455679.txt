PRESS RELEASE  
 
CAMIKARA WINS GLOBAL “RUM BRAND OF THE YEAR” AT THE 
SPIRITS BUSINESS AWARDS 2025 
Indian Rum Makes History with Biggest Global Award, Beating World's Leading Rum Brands 
27th November 2025, New Delhi — Camikara, India’s first pure cane-juice aged rum, has set a new global 
benchmark by becoming the first-ever Indian rum to win the prestigious ‘Rum Brand of the Year’  at The 
Spirits Business Awards 2025  in London. By claiming this top global honour, Camikara has surpassed 
iconic and long-established rum brands from traditional powerhouses such as the Caribbean, Latin 
America and other legendary rum-producing regions. This landmark victory marks a seismic shift in the 
world’s perception of Indian rum, firmly positioning India as a rising, formidable force in the premium 
aged spirits category. 
 
India has always had a natural affinity for rum, long before whisky became the dominant spirit of choice 
after colonization. With sugarcane growing abundantly from Kashmir to Kanyakumari, the country has 
historically been a rum-drinking nation, deeply connected to cane-based beverages for thousands of 
years. Ancient references to sidhu in the Rigveda further underscore India’s early mastery of fermenting 
sugarcane. Though colonial influence later shifted consumer preferences toward whisky, India’s roots in 
cane spirits never disappeared—they simply waited for revival.  
 
In recent years, India’s rum landscape has undergone a dramatic evolution. A category once viewed 
through a mass-market lens is now stepping confidently into the global spotlight, fuelled by distillers 
who are embracing the country’s rich sugarcane heritage and diverse climates to craft refined, world-

ABOUT CAMIKARA 
 
Camikara is India’s first pure cane-juice aged rum, crafted by Piccadily Distilleries. Derived from the Sanskrit word 
for “liquid gold,” Camikara showcases India’s rich sugarcane heritage through a natural, unadulterated rum-making 
process. Distilled from fresh sugarcane juice and aged in American oak barrels under the intense North Indian 
climate, Camikara delivers remarkable depth, complexity and character without any added colour, flavour or sugar. 
 
Available in 3-year, 8-year, and 12-year expressions, Camikara has earned global acclaim, including major 
international awards, and is redefining India’s position in the premium rum category. As a pioneering, homegrown 
sipping rum, Camikara represents a new chapter for Indian spirits on the world stage.
