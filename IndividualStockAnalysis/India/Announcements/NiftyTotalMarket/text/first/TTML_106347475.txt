Tata Tele Business Services Unveils ‘Mira’  
       A warm, trusted, and tech-savvy guide to make digital transformation simpler and more human  
 
New Delhi - August 21, 2025: Tata Tele Business Services (TTBS) , one of India’s leading B2B digital and 
cloud solutions providers, has announced the launch of its brand mascot ‘Mira’, a smart, approachable 
digital advisor created to help India’s Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) navigate their digital journey 
with ease. 
As India’s SME sector accelerates its digital adoption, many businesses face challenges in understanding 
and implementing the right technological tools. Mira, with her human-like qualities and engaging 
personality, will act as a trusted advisor, making technology more accessible while fostering confidence 
among business owners in adopting smart solutions.   
 “With Mira, we’re redefining how businesses engage with digital solutions 
through simplicity, warmth, and trust” said Vishal Rally, President & Chief 
Revenue Officer, Tata Teleservices . “Mira reflects our values of being 
approachable and expert-led ensuring SMEs feel supported at every step of their 
growth. Mira will bridge the gap between complex tech-speak and real-world 
business needs, making digital transformation an inclusive and seamless 
experience.” 
 
Why Mira Matters for MSMEs:   
 Makes Tech Simple:  Presents technical 
information in an easy-to-understand manner.   
 Guiding Businesses: Adds a friendly touch to 
TTBS experience, helping businesses navigate 
and adopt the right solutions smoothly.   
 Trusted Companion:  Reinforces TTBS’s 
promise of being a dependable partner in 
progress. 
Mira, as the female persona, brings relatability to TTBS’s brand identity, while naturally aligning with the 
growing role of women as key drivers in business and entrepreneurship.  
Mira will play a central role in TTBS’s customer engagement, marketing communication, digital content, 
and support journeys personifying the brand promise of “Progress, the Smarter Way.” From guiding SMEs 
on the right technology adoption to narrating their success stories, Mira will enhance the relevance and 
effectiveness of TTBS’s offerings.
