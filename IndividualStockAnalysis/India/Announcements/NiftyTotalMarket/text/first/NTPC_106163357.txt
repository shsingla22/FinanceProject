Sub: Declaration of COD of fourth and last part capacity of 18.32 MW out of 300 MW Shambu ki Burj -2 Solar 
PV Project of NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL) 
 
In line with disclosure requirements under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations 2015, we wish to inform that, Consequent upon successful commissioning and due 
approvals, fourth & last part capacity of 18.32 MW out of 300 MW Shambu ki Burj-2 (Kolayat) Solar PV Project 
at Bikaner, Rajasthan of NTPC Green Energy Limited, is declared on Commercial Operation w.e.f. 00:00 Hrs. of 
01.03.2025.  
 
The first part capacity of 150 MW, second part capacity of 98.78 MW and third part capacity of 32.90 MW has 
already been declared on commercial operation w.e.f. 00:00 Hrs. of 29.09.2022, 23:00 Hrs. of 30.09.2024 and 
00:00 Hrs of 31.10.2024 respectively. With this, the total installed and commercial capacity of NTPC group has 
now become 77,411.50 MW.
