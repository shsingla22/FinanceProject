Ranveer Singh Joins CP PLUS as Brand Ambassador, India’s Leading Brand in Smart 
Security Revolution  
 
National, October 27, 2026 : As India rapidly embraces smarter, connected lifestyles, the need 
for intelligent security has never been more important. Reinforcing its commitment to making 
advanced security accessible, reliable, and proudly Made -in-Bharat, CP PLUS, India's leading 
security and surveillance brand, has announced Ranveer Singh as its Brand Ambassador.  
More than a brand association, the partnership represents a shared vision of confidence, 
innovation, and trust: values that continue to define CP PLUS as it empowers millions of homes, 
businesses, institutions, and public spaces with next -generation securi ty technologies.  

With one of India's largest integrated manufacturing facilit ies for video security products in 
Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh, the country's largest portfolio of STQC -certified solutions, and a 
steadfast commitment to the Government of India's Make in India and Atmanirbhar Bharat 
initiatives, CP PLUS has emerged as one of Ind ia's most trusted technology brands.  
Known as the ultimate showman and a generational performer, Ranveer Singh is uniquely 
positioned to amplify the company's vision of making intelligent security solutions more 
accessible, relevant and widely adopted across the country.   
Talking about the association, Ranveer Singh said:  “It’s exciting to partner with CP PLUS, a 
brand that has consistently set new benchmarks in India's security industry. As technology 
becomes increasingly integral to creating safer homes, workplaces, and communities, CP PLUS 
is leading the way with innova tions proudly built for India. I'm happy to be a part of a brand that 
is transforming the way India thinks about security.”  
Commenting on the partnership, Aditya Khemka, Managing Director, CP PLUS, said , “At 
CP PLUS, our journey has always been driven by the purpose to build a safer, smarter, and 
more secure India through innovation that people can trust. Today, technology is an essential 
part of everyday life and it is equally important to create greate r awareness about the role 
intelligent security plays in protecting what matters most.  
We are thrilled to welcome Ranveer Singh to the CP PLUS family. His authenticity, confidence, 
and ability to inspire millions resonate strongly with our brand philosophy. Together, we look 
forward to taking the message of smart, Made -in-Bharat security sol utions to every corner of the 
country while continuing to strengthen the trust that customers have placed in CP PLUS over 
the years.”  
As CP PLUS continues to lead India's security transformation, this association marks another 
significant milestone in the brand's journey of combining world -class innovation with indigenous 
excellence. Through this association, CP PLUS and Ranveer Singh ai m to inspire greater 
confidence in smart security innovations while encouraging individuals, businesses, and 
institutions alike to embrace technologies that create safer environments for everyone.  
Today, CP PLUS safeguards millions of locations across the country, from homes and retail 
establishments to educational institutions, healthcare facilities, transportation networks, 
government infrastructure, and critical installations. Backed by cutting -edge innovation, 
indigenous manufacturing, and one of the industry's strongest nationwide distribution networks, 
CP PLUS continues to redefine what it means to secure India's future.
