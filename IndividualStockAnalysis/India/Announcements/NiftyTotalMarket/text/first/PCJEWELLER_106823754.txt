Highlights 
PC Jeweller Q2 FY27 Net Operating PAT Rises 107%, Revenue 
Up 28% 
Turns Debt-Free; Commencement of Realisation of Outstanding Export Receivables; 
Retail Expansion Plans Under Way; New Brand for GEN-Z customers; Vertical 
Integration through Mining activities 
 
PC Jeweller Limited, one of India’s prominent jewellery retailers and manufacturers of gold 
and diamond jewellery, has announced its unaudited financial results for the quarter and half 
year ended September 30, 2026. Backed with healthy consumer demand, the Company’s 
strong performance continues in Q2FY27 as well reporting Consolidated revenues of Rs 
1,054 crores, a year-on-year growth of 28%.  
 
A key milestone during the quarter was the Company achieving debt-free status after 
repaying its outstanding dues to all 14 consortium banks ahead of schedule. With interest 
costs expected to reduce significantly from the second half of FY27, the Company expects 
its net profit to have a positive impact of the same.

Other Key Highlights: 
 
Highlight  Details 
Debt-free status Discharged the entire outstanding debt of its 14 consortium 
banks, ahead of the scheduled due dates under the settlement 
agreement dated September 30, 2024. The company now 
carries zero bank borrowings. Further, all the charges, 
securities, guarantees (personal as well as corporate) and 
undertakings provided by the Company and the personal and 
corporate guarantors also stand fully released. 
 
Export receivables Received remittances of about ₹142 Cr from export debtors 
during the quarter following negotiations and resolving of 
issues between the company and the export debtors, marking 
the start of realisation of long-pending receivables. 
 
Retail expansion Plan to rollout up to 100 large-format showrooms across Tier 
I, Tier II and Tier III cities over the next 12–18 months, as a 
mix of own as well as asset-light franchise model. The 
company is confident that it will start establishing new 
showrooms in this financial year itself. 
 
Micro-franchise network 
through strategic 
partnership with 
Government under CM 
YUVA and NSDC Already executed MoU’s with NSDC under the Government of 
India and with the Government of Uttar Pradesh under the CM 
YUVA scheme for onboarding of upto 2,00,000 and 1,000 
franchisee partners respectively under the PC Jeweller brand 
which will help to expand in untapped rural and semi-urban 
markets located in Tier 4 and below cities where no other 
established brand has its presence yet, thereby giving first-
mover advantage to the company. 
 
New brand for GEN-Z 
customers The company is working on and planning to launch a new 
jewellery brand which will mainly focus on demi-fine / 
lightweight jewellery and lab-grown-diamond fine jewellery for 
younger and self-purchasing buyers. The lightweight jewellery 
and lab-grown diamond jewellery segment in India is expected 
to be a large opportunity. The segment typically carry 
materially higher gross margins ratio than traditional jewellery 
and it is capital efficient thereby improving inventory turns and 
the return on capital as the business scales. 
 
Gold mining licence, 
Chad PCJ Mining SARL, a step-down subsidiary of the Company, 
which has been granted license for semi-mechanized 
artisanal mining of gold in the Republic of Chad, has 
commenced on-site development work and related activities 
during the quarter ended September 2026, a step towards 
backward integration into gold sourcing. 
 
Festive readiness Showrooms are positioned to meet demand through the 
festive and wedding season, which falls largely in the second 
half of the year. 
 
 
The Company remains committed to building a stronger, more resilient and growth-oriented 
organisation. With rich experience from the past, going forward, the Company is committed 
to expansion of its retail footprint. Initiatives like MoU’s with the Government and focus on 
the franchise model will help the demographic expansion. The company already caters to 
different market segments through its jewellery design. With launch of a new brand for 
youngsters, the company will further expand segment wise. This will help the company to 
reach a broader customer base and enhance its market presence and is aligned with the 
Company’s future roadmap for growth and creating value for its stakeholders.
