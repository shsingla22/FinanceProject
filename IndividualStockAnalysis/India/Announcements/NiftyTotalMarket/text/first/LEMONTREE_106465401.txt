Lemon Tree Hotels signs two new propert ies in Andhra Pradesh and 
Chhattisgarh  
 
Vijayawada/ New Delhi , 10th December 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, 
India’s fastest -growing mid -scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the 
signing of its two latest propert ies – Lemon Tree Resort, Bapatla, Andhra Pradesh  
and Lemon Tree Hotel, Bhilai, Chhattisgarh . 
 
Both the  properties  shall be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly  
owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited . These signings will further 
strengthen  the company’s presence in Andhra Pradesh  and Chhattisgarh , while 
catering to both  business  and leisure travellers for a fresh, vibrant, and value -driven 
experience.  
 
Property Highlights  
Lemon Tree Resort, Bapatla, Andhra Pradesh  is located at a serene coastal town 
in Andhra Pradesh, known for its peaceful Suryalanka Beach . With its old -world charm 
and calm pace, the city offers a refreshing escape by the sea . 
 
The key features of the hotel will include:  
 
• 90 well-appointed rooms  
• A restaurant  and a bar   
• A banquet and a meeting room   
• Recreational facility includes a swimming pool , spa and a fitness center  
 
The Vijayawada International  Airport , is approximately 100 kms aw ay from the 
property, while the Vijayawada  Junction  is about 85 kms away. The hotel is well 
connected by roadways for both public and private transport.  
 
Property Highlights  
Lemon Tree Hotel, Bhilai, Chhattisgarh is located in  the Steel City of India, that 
blends industry with calm, green neighbourhoods.  Known for the iconic Bhilai Steel 
Plant, it stands as a symbol of progress and urban simplicity.  
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
The key features of the hotel will include:  
 
• 72 well-appointed rooms  
• A restaurant   
• A banquet and a meeting room   
• Recreational facility includes a swimming pool and a spa  
 
The Swami Vivekananda Airport , Raipur  is approximately 58 kms aw ay from the 
property, while the Raipur Railway Station is about 41 kms away. The hotel is well 
connected by roadways for both public and private transport.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels, commented, " With these signings, we are pleased to extend our 
footprint to two unique locations . From thriving commercial hubs to serene spiritual 
and nature -led destinations, both states offer immense potential for quality 
hospitality. Andhra Pradesh  has six operational and three upcoming hotels, while  it 
will be our first property in Chhattisgarh .”
