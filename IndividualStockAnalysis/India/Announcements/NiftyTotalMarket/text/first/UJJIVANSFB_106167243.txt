1. Pursuant to the latest applicable circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs and Securities and 
Exchange Board of India (hereinafter collectively referred to as “the Circulars”), companies are allowed to hold

the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and rules made thereunder read with the Circulars, the

Office of the Bank.
