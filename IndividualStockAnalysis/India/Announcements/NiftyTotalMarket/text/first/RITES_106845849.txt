RITES Limited CIN: L74899DL1974GOI007227            Press Release  RITES Q2FY27 Revenue ₹583 cr; PAT ₹97 cr Declares 2nd Interim Dividend of ₹1.9/share   Gurugram, November 4, 2026: RITES Ltd. (NSE: RITES, BSE: 541556), the leading Transport Infrastructure Consultancy and Engineering firm, today announced its standalone and consolidated financial results for the Quarter ended on September 30th, 2026.   The Takeaways  § Sequential growth of 7.6% in Revenue (standalone) & 31.4% in PAT (standalone)  § Order book of ₹9388 crore, with 130+ orders worth ₹446+ crore secured in Q2 § 2nd Interim Dividend of ₹1.90 per share, with Payout Ratio of 97%  Q2FY27 Financials (Consolidated)  § Operating Revenue at ₹557 crore § EBITDA at ₹123 crore with Margins of 22.1%  § PAT at ₹97 crore with Margins of 16.7%   H1FY27 Financials (Consolidated)                                    § Total Revenue stands at ₹1144 crore against ₹1091 crore in H1FY26 § EBITDA at ₹243 crore with Margins at 22.3% § PAT at ₹195 crore with Margins at 17.0%  Commenting on the results, Mr. Rahul Mithal, Chairman and Managing Director, RITES Limited, said, “The performance in this quarter has been muted, with certain planned revenue milestones sliding marginally into Q3. We remain focused on making up for this in the remaining quarters and achieving the targets set for the year.”  Financial Performance in Q2FY27 RITES operating revenue (consolidated), excluding other income, stands at ₹557 crore in Q2FY27 as against ₹549 crore in Q2FY26. Total revenue is ₹583 crore as against ₹579 crore in Q2FY26. EBITDA and PAT stand at ₹123 crore and ₹97 crore with margins of 22.1% and 16.7%, respectively. Year-on-year, revenue registered a slight increase, driven by growth across all segments except exports.  Standalone Operating revenue, excluding other income, stands at ₹522 crore in Q2FY27 against ₹514 crore in Q2FY26. Total standalone revenue is ₹565 crore against ₹561 crore in Q2FY26. EBITDA and PAT, with respective margins of 17.9% and 16.7%, stand at ₹93 crore and ₹94 crore against ₹100 crore and ₹102 crore, respectively, in Q2FY26.  Financial Performance in H1FY27 RITES operating revenue (consolidated), excluding other income, stands at ₹1089 crore in H1FY27 
 
as against ₹1038 crore in H1FY26. Total revenue stands at ₹1144 crore as against ₹1091 crore in H1FY26. EBITDA and PAT stand at ₹243 crore and ₹195 crore against ₹250 crore and ₹200 crore, respectively, in H1FY26. EBITDA and PAT margins at 22.3% and 17.0%, respectively.   Segmental Performance  The Consultancy business continues to provide the highest revenue to the company and achieved the revenue of ₹285 crore with margins at 33.0%. Leasing revenue stands at ₹49 crore, maintaining the margins of 39.5%. Turnkey revenue stands at ₹187 crore. The pickup in export revenue commenced in Q3 with the supply of the first rake comprising 18 coaches to Bangladesh.  Dividend The Board of Directors has declared the second interim dividend of ₹1.90 per share amounting  ₹91.31 crore. The record date for the purpose of payment of dividend is November 11, 2026.  Order Book The company has secured more than 130 orders (including extension of works) worth more than ₹446 crore in Q2FY27, thereby maintaining an order book of ₹9388 crore as on September 30th, 2026.  Outlook  On the growth prospects, Mr. Mithal said, “The visibility of fresh orders as well as the planned execution of the current order book makes us confident that substantial growth, as envisaged, is on track.”
