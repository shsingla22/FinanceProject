demonstrating operational excellence and turnaround capabilities of the  Group

About Yathart h Hospital  & Trauma Care Services Ltd :  
Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Limited (Yatharth Hospitals) is one of North India’s leading healthcare 
providers, known for its commitment to delivering high quality medical care through a network of state -of-the-art 
facilities. With seven  super special ity hospitals located in North India, i.e., at Noida, Greater Noida, Noida Extension , 
Greater Faridabad , newly operationalized Model Town facility , and yet to be operationalized Faridabad facility  in 
Delhi NCR, and Jhansi -Orchha in Madhya Pradesh , it has a total capacity of 2,300+ beds . Its Noida Extension Hospital 
and Greater Noida are the 8th and 10th largest private hospital s in Delhi NCR respectively, in terms of number of 
beds.
