Chief of Defence  Staff Unveils Zen Technologies’ AI -Powered Defence  Systems to 
Enhance Surveillance, Firepower & National Security 
Zen Technologies Limited Showcases Cutting -Edge Anti -Drone and Combat Solutions at Aero India 2025
Advanced Defence  Innovations Strengthen India’ s Military Readiness and Technological SuperiorityPR ES S  & MED IA R ELEAS E
Zen Technologies  Limited,  India’s  leading  Anti-Drone  technology  and Defence  training  solutions  
provider  today  announced  the launch  of three  advanced  defence  systems  at Aero  India  2025  at 
Yelahanka,  Bengaluru . In a major  step toward  modernizing  the defence  system,  Chief  of Defence  
Staff,  General  Anil Chauhan  highlighted  the growing  importance  of indigenous  innovation  in 
strengthening  India’s  national  security .
Aero  India  serves  as a prominent  global  platform  for showcasing  state-of-the-art defence  
capabilities . Zen's  latest  product  offerings,  designed  to enhance  combat  precision,  aerial  firepower,  
and anti-drone  defence  drew  significant  attention  from  defence  officials,  military  leaders,  and global  
industry  representatives .
Game -Changing  Innovations  for Modern  Warfare
Zen Technologies  introduced  three  cutting -edge  defence  systems  that address  emerging  security  
threats  with unparalleled  efficiency :
• Zen Vyomkavach  – Advanced  Anti -Drone  System  – In an era where  aerial  threats  are 
rapidly  evolving,  Zen Vyomkavach  stood  as a major  highlight  of the event . Vyomkavach  is an 
advanced  anti-drone  innovation  that provides  comprehensive  real-time drone  detection  and 
neutralization  against  all types  of drone  threats  including  the likes  of Byraktar  TB2 and 
Swarms  for safeguarding  military  installations  and critical  infrastructure .
• Four  Barrel  Rotary  Machine  Gun  (12.7 x 99 mm)  – A state-of-the-art weapon  system  
engineered  for anti-drone  and high-intensity  combat  applications . Designed  to deliver  
unmatched  firepower,  precision,  and adaptability,  this cutting -edge  firearm  is the ultimate  
choice  for countering  aerial  threats  with maximum  efficiency .
• Twin  Barrel  Autocannon  (Turreted  20 mm)– This advanced  twin-barrel  system  provides  
enhanced  strike  capability  for combat  helicopters . With  superior  stabilization  and high 
accuracy,  it ensures  pilots  can engage  targets  effectively  while  operating  in fast-moving  battle  
scenarios .
Stressing  on the need  to move  forward  from  imitative  to innovative  and distinctive  technologies,  Mr. 
Ashok  Atluri,  Chairman  and Managing  Director  of Zen Technologies , said, "It is an honour  for 
Zen Technologies  to have our products  unveiled  by Honourable  Chief  of Defence  Staff. This 
recognition  reinforces  our commitment  to delivering  world -class  defence  solutions  that strengthen  
India’ s  security  and global  defence  presence . Our cutting -edge  systems  are designed  to counter  
evolving  threats  with precision  and agility ."
PRESS & MEDIA RELEASEPR ES S  & MED IA R ELEAS EAbout Zen Technologies Limited
Zen Technologies  Limited  is a pioneer  and leader  in providing  world  class  state-of-the art Defense  
Training  and Anti-Drone  solutions  and has a proven  track  record  in building  training  systems  for 
imparting  defense  training  and measuring  combat  readiness  of security  forces . With  a dedicated  
R&D  (recognized  by the Ministry  of Science  and Technology,  Government  of India)  and 
production  facility  in Hyderabad,  the Company  has applied  for over 155 patents  and shipped  more  
than 1,000 training  systems  around  the world . With  over 30 years’  experience  fielding  realistic  
combat  training  solutions  to armed  forces  worldwide,  Zen constantly  updates  its portfolio  to 
include  new solutions  for emergent  threats . Leveraging  their world -class,  in-house  production  
facilities,  Zen has developed  strategic  solutions  tailored  to the specific  needs  of End Users  Ministry  
of Interiors  and Ministry  of Defence  including  customized  training  programs  aligned  with the 
requirements  of the U.S. Army  and U.S. Department  of Defense .
