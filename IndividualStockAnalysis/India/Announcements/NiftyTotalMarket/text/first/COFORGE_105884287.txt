Coforge recognized with Intelligent Automation a ward  from  
Pega  at the Partner Awards  at PegaWorld iNspire  
 
Greater Noida, India, June 1 8, 2024: Coforge Limited  (NSE: COFORGE), a global digital 
services and solutions provider, announced today that it has been honored with  the Intelligent 
Automation award  from Pegasystems , the leading enterprise AI decisioning and workflow 
automation platform provider , for its work in demonstrating exceptional capabilities in delivering 
end-to-end automation solutions for a leading American insurance company. Coforge’s 
automation solutions , based on Pega , helped the firm to optimize business processes, enhance 
customer experiences, and drive operational efficiency to support their clients in achieving 
significant business outcomes.  The Coforge leadership team received the trophy at the Partner 
Excellence Awards during PegaWorld iNspire in Las Vegas. The Pega Partner Awards  celebrate 
organizations that empower Pega customers to thrive by offering innovative solutions that 
enhance customer agility and drive competitive advantage.   
 
Judy Buchholz, senior vice president of global partner ecosystems  at Pega, 
congratulated Coforge on their achievement: “Coforge’s deep understanding of Pega’s intelligent 
automation capabilities unlocks game -changing business value for our clients. Their ability to 
implement Pega solutions at scale drives significant improvements across entire enterprises.  With 
a significant pool of Pega certified consultants globally, Coforge brings extensive domain expertise 
into every implementation and deployment. We are jointly investing in sales, marketing, and 
solution development to help solve complex business challenges for clients across industries 
including insurance, travel, manufacturing, and public sector segments .” 
 
As a global Elite partner of Pega for over a decade, Coforge has helped  clients across various 
industries in delivering robust intelligent automation and digital transformation solutions. 
Coforge’s expertise lies in providing advanced, end -to-end solutions that bridge the gap between 
customer engagement, intelligent automation, customer decisioning, and exceptional service. By 
combining personalized user experiences across digital channels with intel ligent bots and AI-
powered decisioning models, Coforge helps clients streamline business processes, robotics, and 
case management, paving the way for a smarter future.  
 
“We are honored to receive this prestigious  Intelligent Automation award at the  Pega Partner 
Award from Pegasystems,” said John Speight, Chief Customer Success Officer , Coforge. He 
added, “ By leveraging Pega’s industry -leading platform and frameworks alongside Coforge’s deep 
domain expertise and proven transformation capabilities, we empower our clients to achieve 
significant improvements in IT and business agility. Our partnership with Pega exemplifies a 
commitment to innovation, responsible AI, and ethical practices, ensuring our advancements 
benefit the societies we serve.”  
  
 
 
 
As a proud Pega Global Elite Partner, Coforge brings deep industry expertise and a track record
