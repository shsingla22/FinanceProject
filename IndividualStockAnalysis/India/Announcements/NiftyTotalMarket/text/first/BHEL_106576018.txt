To  
1. National Stock Exchange of India Ltd., Mumbai  
 
Subject: Clarification /Confirmation on news item appearing in “Media/Publication”  
Sir/Madam,  
This has reference to letter no. NSE/CM/Surveillance/16633 dated 30 -03-2026 seeking 
confirmation on recent news item  which appeared in the “Website -www.economictimes.com” 
dated March 30, 2026 captioned “Centre relaxes Land Border norms for BHEL tenders.”.  
 
In this regard, it is clarified that BHEL is in receipt of OM No F.7/10/2021 -PPD(II)  dated 
27.03.2026 of Departmen t of Expenditure (DoE), Ministry of Finance, wherein relaxation has 
been accorded to BHEL under Rule 144(xi) of GFR , for a period of five years, in terms of 
procurement of 21 items, from entities representing countries sharing land borders with India.  
 
Further clarifications on the queries are as under:  
 
a) Whether such negotiations/events were taking place? If so, you are advised to provide the 
said information along with the sequence of events in chronological order from the start of 
negotiations/events till date.  
 
Reply:   While there were no negotiations,  a communication dated 31.12.2025 from DoE was 
forwarded by our Administrative Ministry seeking details of the equipment/items wherein, 
procurement is being done from other than Land border sharing  countries due to the GFR rule 
144(XI). The inputs  were provided by BHEL  to our administrative ministry on 06.01.2026.  
 
b) Whether you/company are aware of any information that has not been announced to the 
Exchanges which could explain the movement in t he trading, if any? Further, you are advised to 
provide the said information and the reasons for not disclosing the same to the Exchange earlier 
as required under regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.  
 
Reply: We are not aware of any such  information. These are communications issued by Govt. 
which are not in our control, and cannot be predicted in advance.  
 
c) The material impact of this article on the Company.  
 
Reply:  The impact on the competitive intensity of procurement for certain  products in future  
is being examined.  
 
For Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited
