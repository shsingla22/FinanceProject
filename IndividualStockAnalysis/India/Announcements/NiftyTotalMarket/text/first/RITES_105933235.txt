Dear Sir / Madam , 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)  
Regulations, 2015, this is to inform that Board of Directors of the Company  at its 
meeting held on July 31st, 2024,  has approved the proposal for change in Registrar 
and Share Transfer Agent (RTA) of the Company  from M/s Link Intime India Private 
Limited  (Link Intime ) to M/s Beetal Financial & Computer Services Private Limited 
(Beetal).  
 
Link Intime will continue to render RTA services to the Company and its shareholders 
till such time  the electronic connectivity is shifted to Beetal  and the confirmations to 
this effect are received from both the  Depositories  i.e. National Se curities Depository  
Limited and Central Depository Services (India) Limited. The requisite tripartite 
agreement will be entered into between the Company, Link  Intime and  Beetal . The 
effective date of change of RTA will be intimated to the Stock Exchanges in due 
course of  time.  
 
The details as required under Regulation 30 and Schedule III of SEBI (LODR)
