Press Release 
HCC reports Net Profit of ~37 Cr for Q2 FY27 
Construction business records turnover of ~896 crore in 02 FY27 
Mumbai: November 5, 2026: HCC's standalone E&C business recorded a turnover of 
~895 .8 crore and a net profit of ~36.8 crore in 02 FY27, compared with ~957.8 crore and 
~36.7 crore, respectively , in the corresponding quarter last year. On a consolidated basis, 
revenue stood at ~896 .8 crore, compared with ~960.7 crore in 02 FY26, while consolidated 
profit was ~22.6 crore against ~47.8 crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. 
Financial highlights- HCC unaudited standalone results 
-Turnover of ~895 .8 crore in 02 FY27 versus ~957.8 crore in 02 FY26 
Net Profit of ~36.8 crore in 02 FY27 versus ~36. 7 crore in 02 FY26 
EBITDA margins at 12.2% in 02 FY27 compared to 16.1% in 02 FY26 
During the quarter, HCC secured a new order worth ~524 crore and emerged as the lowest 
bidder for projects valued at ~2.01 0 crore (HCC share: ~1 ,557 crore). In addition, bids 
submitted aggregating approximately ~34,805 crore remain under evaluation. 
Execution across HCC's project portfolio progressed well during the quarter. In the 
transportation sector, Factory Acceptance Tests for the first Tunnel Boring Machine (TBM) 
were completed for both Indore and Patna Metro projects, with two TBMs successfully 
lowered at Indore. At the Agardanda Creek Bridge, work progressed on the foundations and 
construction of bridge segments. 
In the power segment, excavation and steel liner fabrication progressed at the Bhivpuri 
Pumped Storage Project. Furthermore, dam and intake concreting at the Vishnugad 
Pipalkoti Hydroelectri c Project reached 79% completion. Excavation also progressed at 
Tapovan Vishnugad, while mobilisation began for the Wangchhu Hydroelectric Project in 
Bhutan. 
Water infrastructure projects achieved key milestones, with the Parwan Dam reservoir 
undergoing initial filling and three dredgers commissioned at Salal Dam following 
mobilisation and testing. 
As part of its deleveraging strategy, HCC prepaid ~1 00 crore of debt in 02 FY27 and has 
begun executing a further pre-payment of ~1 08 crore by December 2026. 
About HCC: 
HCC is a business group developing and building responsible infrastructure through next practices. With an 
engineering heritage of 100 years, HCC has executed a majority of India's landmark infrastructure projects, 
having constructed 26% of India's Hydro Power generation and 60% of India's Nuclear Power generation 
capacities, over 4,036 lane km of Expressways and Highways, more than 402 km of complex Tunnelling and 403 
Bridges. Today, HCC serves the infrastructure sectors of Transportation , Power and Water.
