JYOTI CNC AUTOMATION LIMITED  
Jyoti CNC Automation expands presence in France with commencement 
of operations at the Huron facility, doubling overall capacity  
Rajkot, Gujarat: 2 1st November 2025 - Jyoti CNC Automation Ltd., one of India’s 
leading CNC machine manufacturers with a global footprint, announced the 
commencement of operations at its subsidiary, Huron Graffenstaden SAS, following 
the completion of the facility’s expansion in Strasbourg, Fr ance, on 19th November 
2025.  
As part of this expansion, the company has doubled its capacity and  scale -up is 
aligned with Jyoti CNC’s strategic growth plans and strengthens its manufacturing 
capabilities to meet rising global demand.  
The event was graced by the presence of Mr. Parakramsinh Jadeja,  Chairman & 
Managing Director, and Mr. Mark Troia , CEO of Huron Graffenstaden SAS. It was also 
attended by esteemed guests including Mr. Philipps Thibault , City Mayor of Illkirch 
Graffenstaden; Mr. Thierry Bouchad , Vice President of EVOLIS and Administrator at 
FIMS; and Mr. Avinash Rao , Second Secretary at the Indian Embassy.

Commenting on the expansion, Mr. Parakramsinh Jadeja, Chairman & 
Managing Director said,  
“We are delighted to announce commencement of additional capacity at Huron. 
Huron has been a hub for the company with technological advantage with high 
end machines made for global customers. With the doubling of our capacity we 
are now better equipped to meet the rising demand from aerospace sector and 
serve global customers.  
Huron has played a pivotal role in shaping Jyoti into what it is today, and this 
expansion marks an important milestone in our growth journey. This enhanced 
capability not only strengthens our global manufacturing presence but also 
reinforces our long -term  commitment to technological excellence.  
We are immensely proud of the dedicated team at Huron  & Jyoti , whose relentless 
efforts have made this achievement possible.”  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
About Jyoti CNC Automation Ltd.  
(www.jyoti.co.in , BSE: 544081 ; NSE: JYOTICNC)  
Jyoti CNC Automation Limited was incorporated in 1989 and is one of the largest 
CNC machine manufacturers of the Nation. Company has a very widespread 
product basket consisting of series of CNC Turning Centers, CNC Turn Mill Centers, 
CNC Vertical Machining  Centers, CNC Horizontal Machining Centers, CNC 5 Axis 
Machining Centers and CNC Multi -tasking Machining Centers along with Industry 
4.0 and Artificial Intelligence (AI) Solutions.  
Company has a wholly owned subsidiary Huron Graffenstaden SAS located at 
Strasbourg, France. The company is considered to be the pioneer of the 5 Axis 
technology and has very large and diversified customer base of more than  
1,35,000 + machines installations across the globe .
