Dear Shareholders,  
 
We are pleased to inform that the Board of Directors of the Company at its Meeting held on May 7, 
2024,recommended payment of dividend of ₹ 0.30/ - per Equity Share of ₹ 10/ - each for the year ended 
March 31, 2024. The dividend will be paid to the members of the Company after approval at the 44th 
Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the Company.  
 
Payment of dividend, if approved at the AGM, will be made to those members whose names will be on 
the Company's Register of Members and to those whose names will appear as Beneficial Owners as per 
the details to be furnished by the Depositories, viz. Natio nal Securities Depository Limited (NSDL) and 
Central Depository Services (India) Limited (CDSL) for this purpose. The details with respect to Dividend 
will be provided in the Notice of the 44th AGM.  
 
In accordance with the provisions of the Income Tax Act, 1961 read with the provisions of the Finance 
Act, 2020, with effect from 1st April, 2020, the Company would be required to apply withholding 
tax/deduct taxes at source (TDS) at the prescribed rates o n the dividend paid to its shareholders. The 
withholding tax rate would vary depending on the residential status of the shareholder and the 
documents submitted by them and as acceptable to the Company.  
 
This communication summarizes the applicable TDS provisions in accordance with the provisions of the 
Income Tax Act, 1961, for various categories, including Resident or Non -Resident members.
