Datamatics Appoints Dinesh Kumar VK as  
Executive Vice President and Global Sales Head  
 
12th April 2024, Mumbai : Datamatics, a leading global 
Digital Technologies, Operations, and Experiences 
Company, today announced that Dinesh Kumar VK has 
been appointed as Executive Vice President and Global 
Sales  Head . He will be reporting to Rahul Kanodia - Vice 
Chairman & CEO  of Datamatics.  
 
Dinesh will lead the Datamatics Sales function globally, 
which has a presence across 4 continents and has over 300 
global enterprise customers across Banking, Financial Services, Insurance, Healthcare, 
Manufacturing, International Organizations, and Media & Publishing . 
A seasoned global sales leader w ith over two decades  of experience in the technology  
industry, Dinesh  has been instrumental in revitalizing  key accounts  and driving revenue 
growth and cost efficiencies through delivery -focused strategies . Dinesh has held 
prominent leadership positions within renowned organizations  such as Mphasis, 
Amdocs , and Hewlett Pack ard. With a proven track recor d in identifying  customers' 
pain points, Dinesh is adept at fostering and expanding relationships while securing 
high-value deals. His expertise extends to full P& L oversight and global sales 
management spanning North America, EMEA, and ANZ regions.  
Dinesh joins Datamatics from Mphasis, where he held the position of Senior Vice 
President of Sales. During his 13 year stint at Mphasis, Dinesh held responsibility  for 
full sales cycle from pitch to delivery in North America, the United Kingdom, Germany, 
Australia and New Zealand with full P&L oversight.  Recently, Dinesh was responsible 
for AI driven solution selling.  
Rahul Kanodia, Vice Chairman and CEO of Datamatics  commented, "We are very 
pleased to welcome Dinesh Kumar to the Datamatics family. Dinesh brings with him a 
wealth of experience and a proven track record in driving sales excellence within the 
technology industry. As Datamatics continues to expand its globa l footprint and help 
customers go Deep in Digital , Dinesh  will play a pivotal role in  further strengthening 
our position as a leader in Digital Solutions ."  
Dinesh Kumar, EVP & Global Sales Head, Datamatics  said, "I am honored  and 
excited to join Datamatics as the Executive Vice President and Global Sales Head . In 

 
this era of rapid  AI led  digital transformation, my vision is to lead our global sales 
function with agility, innovation, and a customer -centric approach."   
Dinesh holds a degree in Applied Sciences and Computer Technology from PSG 
College of Technology, Coimbatore, India . Dinesh is currently based in Dallas , USA.
