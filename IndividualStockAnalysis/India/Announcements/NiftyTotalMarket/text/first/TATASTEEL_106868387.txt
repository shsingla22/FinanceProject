Sub: Outcome of Board Meeting  of Tata Steel Limited  
 
The Board of Directors (‘ Board ’) of Tata Steel Limited (‘ Company ’) at its meeting held today, 
i.e. November 22, 2026, inter alia, transacted the following business:  
 
1. Transfer of Tubes Business of the Company and entering into Business Transfer 
Agreement between the Company and APL Apollo Tubes Limited  
 
Considered and approved the transfer  of the business of manufacturing, processing, 
marketing, sale and distribution of steel tubes and pipes at and from the steel tubes 
manufacturing facilities of the Company  at Jamshedpur, Hosur, Khopoli and Sahibabad, in 
each case, including products manufactured directly by the Company  or through contract 
manufacturing arrangements, and certain cold rolling and surface coated steel product 
facilities  at Khopoli, together with all related plants, facilities, operations an d activities ancillary 
or incidental thereto  (‘Tubes Business ’) of the Company to APL Apollo Tubes Limited  
(‘APL Apollo ’), as a going concern on a slump sale basis for a lump sum consideration  of up 
to ₹11,700  crore . Towards this, the Board also considered and approved the Business Transfer 
Agreement  (‘BTA’) to be entered into  between the Company and APL Apollo.  
 
The transaction is subject to customary conditions precedent , including approval from 
Regulatory Authorities  as may be necessary.  
 
For further details refer to Annexure 1 .  
 
2. Divestment of entire  equity stake in Tata Steel Colors Private Limited  
 
The Company  directly holds  43,30,00,000 equity shares representing 50% equity stake in its 
wholly owned subsidiary Tata Steel Colors Private Limited (formerly known as Tata BlueScope 
Steel Private Limited)  (‘TSCL ’). The Company , indirectly through  its another wholly owned 
subsidiary,  Tata Steel Downstream Products Limited  (‘TSDPL ’), holds the balance  
43,30,00,000 equity shares representing the remaining 50% equity stake in TSCL . 
 
 
 
 The Board of the Company  at its meeting held today, c onsidered and approved the sale and 
transfer of its entire  equity stake  of 100%  (held directly or indirectly through one of its wholly 
owned subsidiaries, TSDPL ), in its wholly owned subsidiary, TSCL , to APL Apollo , for a 
consideration of ₹16,300  crore .  
 
The Board also considered and approved the Share Purchase  Agreement to be entered into 
between the Company, TSCL , TSDPL  and APL Apollo , pursuant to which each of the 
Company and T SDPL will sell their entire shareholding in TSCL to APL Apollo for an aggregate 
consideration of ₹16,300 crore . Upon consummation of the transactions contemplated under 
the Share Purchase Agreement  (‘SPA’), 100% of the total paid up equity share capital of TSCL 
will be owned by APL Apollo.  
 
The transaction is subject to customary conditions precedent , including approval from 
Regulatory Authorities  as may be necessary.  
 
Post this transaction,  TSCL  will cease to be a subsidiary of the Company .  
 
For further details on this transaction, please refer to Annexure 2 . 
 
The Board meeting commenced at 1 p.m. (IST) and  concluded at 5 p.m. (IST).
