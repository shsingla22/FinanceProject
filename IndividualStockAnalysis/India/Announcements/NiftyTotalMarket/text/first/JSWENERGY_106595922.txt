Dear Madam / Sir, 
 
JSW Mahanadi Power Company Limited (“JMPCL”), a subsidiary of JSW Energy Limited, has 
received a demand from the Water Resources Division, Janjgir, Chhattisgarh, seeking ₹1,447.34 
crores towards water charges, majorly pertaining to the period prior to approval of the Company’s 
resolution plan for JMPCL under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (“IBC”).  
 
The National Company Law Tribunal, Hyderabad, vide its order dated 13th February, 2025                  
(“Plan Approval Order”) while approving the resolution plan, has held that all crystallised liabilities 
and unclaimed liabilities of JMCPL as on the date of the order shall stand extinguished and 
henceforth no creditors of JMPCL can claim anything other than the liabilities referred to in the 
resolution plan. Post implementation of the resolution plan, JMPCL is regularly paying water 
charges.  
 
The Company believes, based on judicial precedents under the IBC and the Plan Approval Order, 
that the aforesaid claim stands extinguished and is not payable. Accordingly, JMPCL has 
challenged the said demand before the Hon’ble High Court of Chhattisgarh at Bilaspur.
