Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Sub.:  Disclosure under the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (‘SEBI LODR’) – Sale of identified NPA Portfolio to ARC  
 
This is in continuation to the letter bearing Ref. No.: BBL/SEC/ 061/2026 -27 dated June 15, 2026,  
informing about the approval of the Board of Directors of Bandhan Bank Limited (the ‘ Bank ’) for 
sale of identified Non -Performing Assets (' NPA ‘) with more than 180 Days Past Due (‘ DPD ’), 
pertaining to the Housing Finance Portfolio of the Bank, through bidding process with Asset 
Reconstruction Companies (‘ ARC’). Pursuant to the applicable provisions of the SEBI LODR, this 
is to inform that upon conclusion of Swiss Challenge Method , the Bank has concluded the sale 
of the afore said NPA portfolio , having principal outstanding amounting to Rs. 291.44  Crore  (as 
on May 31 , 2026 ) to Assets Care and Reconstruction Enterprise Limited  (‘ACRE ’), for 
consideration amounting to Rs. 119.49  Crore  (41% of aforesaid portfolio) , on outright cash  basis . 
 
You are requested to take note of the above.  
 
This disclosure is being simultaneously uploaded at the Bank’s website, www.bandhan.bank.in .
