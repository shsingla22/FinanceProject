Revolt Motors Launches India’s First Electric Motorcycle  in the 
Commuter Segment  
New Delhi , September 17, 2024 : Revolt Motors, India’s No.1 electric motorcycle company , is 
poised to transform the motorcycle market with the launch of its groundbreaking electric 
motorcycle  in the commuter segment , the Revolt RV1 . India is primarily a motorcycling 
country with motorcycles comprising 70% of all two -wheelers sold in India. Within the 
motorcycles market of about 1.25 crores motorcycles sold annually in India, the commuter 
segment comprises the largest share with more than 80 lac motorcycles sold in the commuter 
segment.  
                       
The RV1 and its premium variant, RV1 +, are offered in four striking colo urs, priced at an 
introductory ₹ 84,990  for the RV1 and ₹ 99,990  for the RV1 +. These models bring electric 
motorcycling to a wider audience with their affordability and offer up to three times lower 
ownership costs compared to petrol motorcycles  in the same category. This launch further 
strengthens Revolt Motors premium electric lineup, which includes popular models like the 
RV400 and RV400 BRZ . 
Setting a new benchmark in the electric motorcycle industry, the RV1 boasts a robust 250 kg 
payload capacity —significantly higher than the 150 kg average seen in other commuter 
motorcycles. It features broader t yres for improved grip and stability, making it perfect for 
both city streets and longer journeys. Powered by a mid-motor  and a premium chain drive 
system , the RV1 ensures seamless acceleration and smooth riding performance.  
Dual disc brakes , a feature not commonly found in other commuter motorcycles, enhance 
safety by providing superior stopping power. The bike also offers multiple speed modes  for 

  
versatile riding and introduces a reverse mode —a first in its category —ensuring ease in tight 
parking spaces.  
A sleek 6-inch digital LCD display  provides real -time ride data and error codes, ensuring a 
connected and informed riding experience. Stylish LED headlights  and taillights enhance both 
the aesthetics and visibility of the RV1, making it a standout on the road.  Riders can enjoy the 
added benefit of built -in charger  storage  in both the variants.  With the fast-charging 
technology, the RV1 + can get fully charged in just 1.5 hours . 
In terms of range, the RV1 comes with two battery options: a 2.2 kWh  battery offering up to 
100 km  range, and a 3.24 kWh  battery extending the range to 160 km —perfect for longer 
rides. Both battery options are IP67 -rated  for water resistance, ensuring reliable performance 
even in adverse weather conditions.  
Comfort remains a key highlight of the RV1. With the longest seat  in its category and a frame 
designed for multiple passengers, it is ideal for families and daily commuters alike. The 
advanced suspension  system ensures a smooth and comfortable ride, even on rough terrains, 
making the RV1 the perfect blend of practicality and performance for modern riders.  
Furthermore, Revolt Motors is excited to announce the upgrade of its flagship model, the 
RV400 , which now comes with enhanced features to further improve performance, 
convenience, and safety. The new fast charger  fully charges the bike in just 90 minutes , while 
the addition of reverse mode  simplifies parking in tight spaces. The enhanced digital display , 
comfortable seat height , and Bluetooth connectivity  for music make the riding experience 
even more enjoyable. Safety has also been improved with a built -in leg guard  and centre  
stand . 
The RV400 now boasts an extended range of 160 km  and is available in a new colo ur, Lunar 
Green . With new CAN based communication system to make it more  intelligent , this upgrade 
solidifies the RV400’s position as a market leader, providing a smarter, more efficient, and 
stylish electric motorcycle option for riders.  
Revolt Motors latest launch reflects its unwavering commitment to innovation  and 
sustainability , offering Indian commuters an affordable, high -performance electric vehicle 
that’s kinder to the planet. Speaking at the launch of the Revolt RV1, Mrs. Anjali Rattan, 
Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises Limited , shared, “ Our commitment to sustainability 
and our quest to engineer a motorcycle that is budget -friendly yet does not compromise on 
quality, features, or safety have come together in the form of the RV 1.  With modern and 
updated looks and features that are best in the segment, RV1 brings a new level of style and 
practicality to the electric motorcycle segment. A perfect blend of cutting -edge technology and 
thoughtful design, RV1 has been made to provide  an exhilarating ride experience for the 
discerning Indian rider .” 
Motorcycle enthusiasts can now secure their RV1 by visiting Revolt Motors official website at 
http://www.revoltmotors.com  or by visiting an authorized dealership near them. Be part of 
the revolution in commuting and experience the future of electric motorcycling with the 
  
Revolt RV1, a machine that combines cutting -edge technology, affordability, and 
environmental consciousness.
