Sub:  Acquisition of equity stake in Tata Steel Colors Private Limited (Formerly Tata 
BlueScope Steel Private Limited ) 
 
This is further to our disclosure s dated December 31 , 2025 and November 12, 2025 on the 
abovementioned subject.  
 
This is to inform you that, Tata Steel Limited (‘Company’) has today i.e. on April 9, 202 6, 
completed the acquisition of remaining 10,000  equity shares (0.01%  shareholding ) of face 
value ₹10/- each  for a consideration of ₹0.03 crore in Tata Steel Colors Private Limited 
(Formerly Tata BlueScope Steel Private Limited)  (‘TSCPL’) , subsidiary company,  from 
BlueScope Steel Asia Holdings Pty Ltd as per the terms and conditions of the Share Purchase 
Agreement executed on November 12,  2025 . Post this transaction, the Company ’s 
share holding in TSCPL has increased from 99.99 % to 100% and accordingly , TSCPL has 
become an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of the Company.  
 
This disclosure is being made in compliance with Regulations 30 and 51 of the Securities and 
Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, as amended.
