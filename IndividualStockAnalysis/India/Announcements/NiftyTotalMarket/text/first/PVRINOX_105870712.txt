PVR INOX LIMITED REOPENS RENOVATED MUMBAI’S HERITAGE LIDO CINEMA 
AFTER THREE DECADES  
 
~ Transforms the Heritage Single Screen Cinema into a 3 -screen modern multiplex ~ 
 
National, May 31  2024:  PVR INOX Limited, the largest and the most premium cinema exhibitor in India has 
transformed the historic Lido Cinema at Juhu Tara Road in Santacruz, Mumbai into a versatile and vibrant 
space while retaining its heritage charm. With this new restoration p roject, PVR INOX Limited has set a 
precedent for its commitment to preserve and restore iconic cinemas of the country.  
 
Originally a single -screen cinema, LIDO has been designed into a stunning building with a seamless blend of 
modern features and the unm istakable beauty of its original Art Deco Retro charm.  
 
The new cinema will augment PVR INOX’s foothold in Mumbai to a total of 25 cinemas across 125 screens 
and 56 cinemas across 280 screens in Maharashtra.  With this opening, PVR INOX consolidates its presence 
in Western India with a total of 376 screens in 81 properties.  
Situated close to Mumbai’s Chhatrapati Shivaji International Airport, the cinema has undergone a 
remarkable makeov er, now converted into a three -screen multiplex that has a total of 326 seats. To offer 
its patrons an unparalleled movie -watching experience, it features state -of-the-art projection and sound 
technologies including  2K laser projectors, Next Gen 3D and Dol by 7.1 sound.  
Commenting on the opening, Mr. Ajay Bijli, Managing Director, PVR INOX Limited , said, “The reopening 
of LIDO Cinema represents a significant moment for us at PVR INOX Limited. Such iconic cinemas represent 
the distinctive architectural legac y and fabric of the city that they belong to and we are happy that in 
reviving this historic cinema, we could preserve a slice of Mumbai’s heritage and cultural identity. The 
unique appeal of the new cinema lies in its ultra -modern viewing experience that will be enjoyed by 
generations to come”.  
The interior of the cinema is done in an intriguing contrast of black, white, and grey stones with hints of 
blue. These stunning contrasts give the cinema an exclusive, grand, and sophisticated aura, matching the 
taste of modern cinemagoers .  
The exterior of the cinema is as striking as its beautiful interiors.  Its larger -than -life mural covering half the 
building with a massive PVR INOX Limited signage serves as a homage to the rich legacy of Bollywood film 
heritage  and the cinemas of yesteryears and makes the cinema building unmistakably visible from a 
distance.    
This transition of Lido Cinema, which first opened its doors in 1948 and was the oldest cinema in Juhu with 
wooden seats to its more recent renovation in 1979, and now to this modern and artistic avatar is 
synonymous with the changing preferences of movie watchers.  
 
 
 
 
 
“LIDO Cinema has been a part of Mumbai’s landscape for almost eight decades. We are incredibly happy 
that our efforts to restore this cinema have helped it transition to an avant -garde experiential space. We 
have prioritized cinema aesthetics, technology, and experience of the moviegoers and hope for it to become 
the preferred movie -watching destination for people from all walks of life.” said Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli, 
Executive Director, PVR INOX Limited.
