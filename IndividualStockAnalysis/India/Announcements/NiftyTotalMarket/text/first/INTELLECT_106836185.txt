 I. Advancing AI -First Enterprise Transformation with MSOCK  
 
 
Q2 FY27 marked a significant milestone in Intellect’s AI -first journey with the launch of MSOCK 
(Multidimensional Multilayer System of Connected Knowledge), an AI -native engineering system 
designed to help enterprises understand, modernise and transform co mplex, mission -critical systems 
with greater speed, precision and intelligence.  
 
MSOCK introduces a new enterprise knowledge layer between AI 
and applications, making enterprise knowledge connected, 
computable and visual. By connecting knowledge across business, 
operations, risk and technology, it enables AI to understand 
relationships, dependencies and the potential impact of change before acting, moving beyond code 
generation to enterprise -context -aware engineering.  
 
MSOCK has accelerated Intellect deliveries for 250 clients : MSOCK enables Intellect project managers to 
deliver projects many weeks ahead of planned schedules. MSOCK has 39 patents pending. Built around a 
21-dimensional Enterprise Spatial Graph, it provides the enterprise context and architectural ground truth 
required for AI -native engineering across BUILD, RUN, CHANGE and MODERNISE.  
 
Designed to complement foundation models, copilots, AI agents and AI -DLC (AI -driven Development 
Lifecycle) pipelines, MSOCK provides the enterprise knowledge infrastructure for AI to operate more 
precisely on mission -critical systems.

IV. Board Announcement  
 
 
Appointment of Mr. Suresh Eshwara Prabhala as Additional Director (Independent Director)  
 
The Board of Directors has approved the appointment of Mr. Suresh Eshwara 
Prabhala as an Additional Director, designated as an Independent Director, on the 
recommendation of the Nomination, Remuneration and Compensation 
Committee (NRCC). His appointment is  for a term of five years, subject to the 
approval of the shareholders through a postal ballot by way of a Special 
Resolution.  Suresh Prabhala  is an experienced financial services professional with 
a total of 28 years of experience in the Indian financial services industry, and the 
last 22 years focused on leading and managing private equity investments in India.  
 
His investment focus is on privately held, mid -market companies that are family -owned and run, with core 
sectors of focus being manufacturing, healthcare and business services. Currently, Suresh Prabhala  is the 
CIO at 2380 Capital – a newly established Alternative Investment Fund. Previously, he was General Partner 
and Co -Founder of ADV Partners Capital Management (ADV), an Asia -focused private equity firm with 
total assets under management of US$ 1.25 bi llion.  
 
Prior to co -founding ADV, in 2009, Suresh set up the India business as Managing Director and Head of 
India for Mount Kellett Capital Management, a global private equity fund with US$ 8 billion under 
management, where he was also a member of the Global Investment Committee. Immediately prior to 
that, Suresh was Executive Director and India head for Principal Investments at JPMorgan. He was part of 
the group’s Asia Management Committee and also on the Management Committee of JPMorgan India.
