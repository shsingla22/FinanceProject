2. Performance Commentary:  
 
• Gold Price:  Gold prices remained elevated and volatile during Q2 FY27 and  ~28% higher YoY  while on QoQ 
basis their was insignif icant change . Domestic 24K gold prices rose ~ 1.4% from ₹1.44 lakh/10 gm at the 
beginning of Q2 to ₹1. 46 lakh/10 gm at quarter -end, while international gold prices increased ~ 3% from  
$4031/Oz to ~$4,153/ Oz.  
• Marketing Campaigns:  Marketing remained sharply focused on driving footfall and demand , leveraging a 
strong mix of festive, cultural and occasion -led campaigns , Monsoon Campaign highlighting diamond jewellery ; 
and Azadi Utsav, showcasing chains for Independence Day . Under Aham  collection , we launched the  “Ti22 
Collection ”, marking the first -ever launch of titanium jewellery  crafted with gold and diamonds  by a pan -India  
jeweller . These initiatives strengthened customer engagement  and enabled Senco to convert latent demand  and 
demonstrating price inelasticity  during the festive period . 
• New Showrooms Opened:  We launched 6 more showrooms in Q2 (3 COCO and 3 Franchisee), taking our H1 
additions to 14 and our total network to 215 showrooms. With a strong pipeline of both COCO and Franchisee 
stores, we expect to launch another 1 0–12 showrooms in H2, further strengthening our  pan-India  network.  
• Revenue & Same Store Sales Growth (SSSG): Q2 FY27 delivered robust revenue and SSSG growth, supported 
by a favourable festive calendar spanning Ratha Yatra, Rakhi, Janmashtami , Onam, Ganesh Chaturthi  and 
Independence Day , as well as  mixed monsoon . Moreover,  shifting the impact of Pitri Paksha  to Q3(only  4days in 
Q2 as against 15days last year ) also led to improvement in Q2 sales . E-commerce and digital channel sales grew 
an impressive 53% YoY, highlighting the channel’s strong potential and the continued improvement in digitally 
influenced sales.  Coin and bullion sales remained in si ngle digit and below 7% mark.  TTM sales reached ~Rs 
10,000  Cr+ mark .  
• Diamond Jewellery  (Stud  Ratio) : Stud Ratio improved significantly from 11% in Q1 to 12.2% in Q2 as  diamond  
jewellery revenue grew  by 31% YoY  along with 7% volume growth, reflecting  upscaling in the ticket mix.   
• Old Gold Exchange : Based on the appeal of the Honourable  Prime Minister , we continued  special campaigns  
and Schemes to promote old gold exchange  to preserve our  precious foreign exchange as well as  to ensure sales 

momentum.  This led to  strong acceleration in Old Gold Exchange, rising  to 50%+level in Q2 and  significantly  
enhancing affordability  and demand conversion . However, higher OG exchange may put slight pressure on 
margins as stated earlier.  
 
The business is therefore increasingly managing growth through a combination of value, design and customer 
affordability rather than relying only on gold -price -led revenue growth.  
 
3. Q3 & H2 Outlook : 
Q3 FY27 is expected to witness strong seasonal demand, supported by underlying affinity for gold fuelled by wedding 
demand  (~1.35  Cr weddings in CY 26) , which is mostly price inelastic  and provid ing a structural  demand backdrop from 
wedding -led jewellery consumption ; and a favourable festive calendar, including Dhanteras, Diwali, Durga Puja, Karwa 
Chauth, Chhath Puja and Christmas.  With the monsoon season now behind us, improving rural sentiment, the festive 
season and the upcoming wedding calendar are expected to provide a more supportive demand environment in H2.   The 
underlying, with wedding and festive demand providing resilience.  
The underlying jewellery consumption opportunity remains strong. While elevated gold prices create affordability 
and working -capital challenges, they also provide an opportunity for organised players with strong brands, sourcing 
capabilities, exchange prog rammes and disciplined inventory management to gain market share.  
 
The focus remains on driving lightweight and 9K collections, improving inventory turns and reducing inventory days, 
replenishment discipline and faster conversion of non -moving inventory.
