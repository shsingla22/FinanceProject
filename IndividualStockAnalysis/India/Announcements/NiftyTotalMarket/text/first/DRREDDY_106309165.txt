4 Business purchase agreement with Haleon:

property, employees, manufacturing contracts, and product licenses, The marketing authorisations will transition gradually into the Company in a

markets, facilitating successful integration of the business across various geographies into the Company . 
During the three months ended 30 June 2025, the transfer of local marketing authorizations were completed for the United Kingdom and Nordic 
countries on the respective cut over dates.
