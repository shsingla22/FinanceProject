About  The Sandur  Manganese  & Iron Ores  Limited
The Sandur  Manganese  & Iron Ores Limited  (“SMIORE”)  is one of India’s  most  respected  private  sector  merchant  miners  and commodity  producers  with an operational  
track  record  spanning  seven  decades . At present,  the Company’s  operations  span  over three  business  segments  i.e., Mining  (Manganese  & Iron Ores),  Ferroalloys,  and 
Coke  and Energy . Further,  the Company  has presence  in specialty  steel  sector  through  its material  subsidiary  Arjas  Steel  Private  Limited,  which  is the leading  
independent  SBQ players  in the country . Together,  these  assets  work  in conjunction  with each other  to leverage  the benefits  of being  an integrated  Company  in the 
metals  and mining  industry .

The Sandur  Manganese  & Iron Ores  Limited  (‘SMIORE’  or ‘the Company’),  one of the leading  private  sector  metals  & 
mining  players,  has been  honoured  with  the distinguished  Seven  Star Rated  Mine  award  in recognition  of our 
outstanding  efforts  and best  practices  in green  mining  by the Ministry  of Mines,  Indian  Bureau  of Mines,  for its 
exemplary  performance  during  the year  2023 -24. The award  ceremony  took  place  at the Rajasthan  International  
Centre,  Jaipur,  on 7 July 2025  as part  of the national  event  to felicitate  Five Star and Seven  Star Rated  Mines  of India .
The award  has been  instituted  by the Indian  Bureau  of Mines  (IBM) . The Seven  Star Rating  was presented  by Shri 
Bhajanlal  Sharma,  Hon’ble  Chief  Minister  of Rajasthan,  in the esteemed  presence  of Shri G. Kishan  Reddy,  Hon’ble  
Union  Minister  of Coal  and Mines .
SMIORE  is the only  mine  from  South  India  and the first among  the three  selected  Seven  Star Rated  Mines  in the 
country . Additionally,  acknowledging  its commitment  to sustainable  development  at its Kammathuru  Iron Ore Mine  
in Sandur  Taluk,  Ballari  District  the company  has been  awarded  the Five Star Rating  for the year  2023–24. The mine  
spans  an area  of 1,860.10 hectares .
These  awards,  instituted  by the Indian  Bureau  of Mines  (IBM),  aim to promote  sustainable  mining  and recognize  
exemplary  practices  that set new  benchmarks  while  meeting  key performance  parameters .

generations . I am immensely  proud  of our team,  who  have  consistently  delivered  excellence  in this critical  facet

Rating  each  year  since  its inception  — a distinction  that has now  culminated  in the highest  honour  of the Seven  
Star Rating . I congratulate  our team  for leading  the new  way of mining  and setting  new  benchmarks .”
