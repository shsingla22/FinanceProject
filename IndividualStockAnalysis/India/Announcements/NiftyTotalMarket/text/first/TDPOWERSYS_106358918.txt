Sub: Update on  the strike announced by  the Workers'  Union  
 
In continuation of our letter dated August 22 , 202 5 informing about the receipt of a strike notice  
from the Workers Union, we further inform that, the Company  approached the Nelamangala Court 
with an application to obtain an injunction to restrain  the Workers’ U nion from obstructing 
operations  & ensuing smooth passage of  employees and vehicles carrying goods from entering 
and leaving the factory premises during a strike or agitation.   
 
The Honorable Additional Civil Judge & JMFC at Nelamangala Court  by its order dated August  
28, 202 5 received by the Company on September  1, 202 5, has granted a temporary injunction  
order restraining the Workers Union, during a strike,  from obstructing the Company , its directors, 
executives, staffs, workmen, vehicles, vehicles carrying with finished goods and raw materials 
etc., from entering into and leaving  from the factory gates  of the Company and are permitted to 
agitate within  beyond 25 meters from the factory gates  until the date of next hearing i.e. September 
24, 2025.  
 
This injunction provides the Company with relief from disruptions by the union and ensures  the 
uninterrupted operation of the Company's business  during this period.  
  
Kindly take the above on record.
