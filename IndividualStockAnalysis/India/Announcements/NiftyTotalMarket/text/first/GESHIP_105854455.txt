PRESS RELEASE 
G E Shipping delivers its 2004 built Medium Range Product Tanker 
“Jag Pahel” 
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (G E Shipping) delivered its 2004 built Medium 
Range Product Tanker “Jag Pahel” to the buyers. The vessel was contracted for sale in April 2024. 
Excluding Jag Pahel, company’s current fleet stands at 43 vessels, comprising 29 tankers (6 Crude 
carriers, 19 Product carriers, 4 LPG carriers) and 14 dry bulk carriers (2 Capesize, 8 Kamsarmax, 
4 Supramax) aggregating 3.41 Mn dwt. 
Additionally, we had contracted to sell one MR product tanker in May 2024, which is due for 
delivery in H1FY25.
