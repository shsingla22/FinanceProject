We wish to inform that the Company has received communication from Honda Motorcycle & 
Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. ("HMSI" or "OEM") informing about the global recall of their bikes on 
account of leakage of oil observed from the engine caused due to dislodgement of sealing 
plug cone out of the Engine Cover ("recalled part") which was supplied by the Company's 
plant situated at Bawal, Haryana. 
Although, the value of the recalled part supplied by the Company is approximately Rs 3.2 
crores but the likely amount intimated by the OEM on account of this recall is estimated to 
be Rs. 76 crores, on account of various costs incurred by the OEM for the replacement of the 
recalled part in various countries across the globe. The Company has an adequate product 
recall insurance policy and accordingly, the Company has intimated the aforesaid matter to 
the insurance company. 
The Company is hopeful of settlement of this amount by the insurance company and does not 
foresee any material impact on financial or operational or other activities of the Company.
