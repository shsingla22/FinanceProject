In furtherance to our earlier intimations dated 24.03.2025 & 2 7.03.2025 with respect to the Issue which

Board  at its meeting held today, i.e. 2 8.03.2025, approved the issue and allotment of 37,04,61,842  Equity 
Shares, to eligible QIBs at the issue price of ₹ 40. 49 per Equity Share which includes a discount of ₹ 2.13              
i.e., 4.99% of the floor price, as determined in terms of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue of

Thousand F ive Hundred Crores Only), pursuant to the QIP.

increased  from ₹ 86,80,93,94,320  consisting of 868,09,39,432 Equity Shares to ₹  90,51,40,12,740  consisting 
of 905,14,01,274  Equity Shares of ₹ 10 each. The shareholding pattern of the Bank before and after the Issue, 
will be submitted along with listing application  in the format specified in Regulation 31 of the Securities and 
Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended 
(“SEBI Listing Regulations”).
