November 26, 202 6 considered and approved the allotment of 76,50,000  equity shares of the

pursuant to the exercise and conversion of 76,50,000  convertible warrants against receipt of the 
balance subscription amount of Rs. 315,56,25,000 (Three Hundred and Fifteen crores Fifty Six lakhs 
Twenty Five Thousand only) , being 75% of the issue price from MCPL .
