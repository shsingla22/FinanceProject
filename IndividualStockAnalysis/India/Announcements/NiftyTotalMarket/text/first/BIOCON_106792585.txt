Biocon Malaysia Receives MHRA Approval for New Drug 
Product Fill Finish Unit for Insulins  
 
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, September 25 , 2026  
 
Biocon L imited (BSE code: 532523, NSE: BIOCON) has received approval from the 
Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) , UK  for its new drug 
product fil l finish unit for Semglee® (insulin glargine) at its insulin manufacturing facility 
in Malaysia.  
 
In July, Biocon had secured regulatory approval from the European Medicines Agency 
(EMA)  for this new drug product fill -finish line .
