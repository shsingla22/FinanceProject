 Sir/ महोदय, 
In continuation to our ealier letter dated 06.06.2023 and in compliance to Regulation 30 of SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, it is to inform that NHPC Limited had 
earlier entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Department of Energy, Govt. of Maharashtra 
for establishment of Energy Storage Systems (Pumped Storage Systems) alongwith Other Renewable Energy 
Source (like Solar/  Wind/ Hybrid etc.) with a total capacity of 7,350 MW.  
 
Now, pursuant to issuance of corrigendum dated 31.07.2024 with respect to Policy for development of 
Pumped Storage Projects (PSPs), PSPs cum LIS and co-located PSP-Solar/  Other Renewable Energy Hybrid 
Projects through Public Private Partnership by Department of Water Resources (WRD), Govt. of Maharashtra, 
the earlier MoU has been signed once again with WRD, Govt. of Maharashtra  on 03.09.2024 to establish 
Pumped Storage Project(s) viz. Kengadi (1,550 MW), Savitri (2,250 MW), Kalu (1,150 MW), Jalond (2,400 
MW) in Maharashtra with total capacity of 7,350 MW. 
 
This is for your information and records.
