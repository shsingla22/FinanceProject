Allied Blenders and Distillers Wins Gold at Global LACP Vision Awards Annual 
Report Competition 
~ Recognized among the Top 5 Indian Reports and Awarded for Technical Excellence~ 
Mumbai, 8th October 2025  – Allied Blenders and Distillers Ltd. (ABD), India ’s largest domestic 
spirits company by volume has received global recognition at the  2024 Vision Awards Annual 
Report Competition (Summer 2025 ), hosted by the League of American Communications 
Professionals (LACP).  ABD won the Gold Award in the Food & Beverage category (up to $1 
billion in revenues) , placing its annual report among the best in corporate communicati ons 
worldwide. 
Taking a step further, ABD ’s annual report earned a spot among the Top 5 Indian Reports, a proud 
milestone in a global competition that witnessed nearly 1,000 entri es. Additionally, it received 
the Technical Achievement Award , highlighting overall excellence in the art and method of 
annual report communications.  
ABD ’s annual report excelled across every judging category, showcasing strengths in narrati ve 
and creativity. The company received near-perfect scores in Message Clarity and Information 
Accessibility, and full marks in First Impression, Cover, Narrative, Financials, C reativity, and 
Shareholder Letter, demonstrating the report ’s quality in both content and presentation. The 
Annual Report showcases the company ’s growth, key milestones, and strategic direction, while 
highlighting its diverse brands and focus on sustainability. 
Speaking about the achievement, Alok Gupta, Managing Director of ABD , said, "We are honored 
to receive the Gold Award and to be recognized among the Top 5 Indian Reports by the LACP. This 
recognition underscores our commitment to delivering meaningful insigh ts on business 
performance and upholding the high standards of disclosure. This acknowledgment serves as a 
milestone in our pursuit to solidify our position as a trusted leader i n the Indian spirits industry 
and to build a distinct and resonant Indian spirits voice o n the world stage. ” 

ABD ’s continued recognition at the LACP Vision Awards reflects its strong track record in 
corporate communication and its standing among global peers. These h onors reinforce ABD ’s 
commitment to maintaining world-class standards and it remains dedicate d to strengthening the 
Indian spirits industry ’s voice on the world stage. 
About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited ( www.abdindia.com ) 
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD) is the largest domestic Spirits company in India, in terms of annual sales 
volumes. ABD has a presence in five main flavors, i.e., whisky, bra ndy, rum, vodka, and gin, with ‘millionaire’ brands 
like Officer’s Choice Whisky, Officer’s Choice Blue Whisky, St erling Reserve Premium Whiskies and ICONiQ White 
Whisky. Currently, its manufacturing network comprises 37 units, o f which 9 are owned bottling units, 2 owned 
distilleries, and 26 non-owned manufacturing units.
