PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Group Unveils the 17th Edition of its Flagship Property Expo, Brigade Showcase  
 
Bengaluru, August 8, 2024 –  Brigade Group is holding the 17th edition of its much -awaited property 
exhibition, Brigade Showcase. The event will take place from August 9th to 11th, 2024, from 10 AM to 
8 PM at the Sheraton Grand Hotel – Brigade Gateway, Rajajinagar.  This year’s Showca se is a unique 
opportunity for homebuyers to explore over 20 exceptional Brigade projects, ranging from Rs. 49 lakhs 
to Rs. 21 crores in the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad.   Brigade is offering a  100% waiver 
on maintenance and clubhouse charges  for all bookings made during the event.   Brigade Showcase 2024 
will witness insightful panel discussions with key stakeholders of Brigade and industry experts on August 
10th. Topics will include evolving community experiences, interpreting net zero for residential and 
market watch.  
 
“We are excited to present the 17th edition of Brigade Showcase, our premier annual property expo.  
Brigade has consistently delivered exceptional experiences. This year’s showcase reflects the dynamic 
demand in the housing market, featuring an impressive array of options, including integrated townships, 
apartments, villas, plots, and ownership offices.  With a strong focus on customer preferences, quality, 
and sustainability, we are confident that this year’s event will attract a remarkable turnout. We look 
forward to welcoming all prospective homebuyers to explore the diverse range of Brigade properties .” 
said Smarajit Mishra, VP Strategy and Marketing -Residential, Brigade Group.  
 
Customers will also have a chance to win exciting prizes, including iPads, through a lucky draw. The Group 
has partnered once again with popular comedian Danish Sait for their digital marketing campaign, adding 
a touch of humor and entertainment.  
 
About Brigade Group:  Brigade Group is in property development  business  for  close to four decades 
with expertise in building positive experiences for all stakeholders. Instituted in 1986, the company has 
developed many landmark buildings and transformed the skyline of cities across South India, namely – 
Bengaluru, Chennai Hyderabad, Mysuru and Kochi with d evelopments across the Residential, Commercial, 
Retail, Hospitality and Education sectors. Since its inception, Brigade has completed 290+ buildings 
amounting to over 87 million sq. ft of developed space across a diverse real estate portfolio.
