About CMS Info Systems Limited (CMSINFO):  
CMS Info Systems Limited (BSE: CMSINFO | 543441, NSE: CMSINFO) is India’s leading business services company providing 
logistics and technology solutions to banks, financial institutions, organized retail, and e -commerce companies with a presence
