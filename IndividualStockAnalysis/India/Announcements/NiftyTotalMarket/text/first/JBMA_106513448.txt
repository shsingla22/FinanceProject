Gurugram, January 30, 2026: JBM Auto Limited, India’s leading automotive and electric vehicle company 
(BSE: 532605 & NSE Code: JBMA), today announced its consolidated results for the quarter ended 315 Dec 
2025 and has shown 4.29% increase in net profit to Rs. 54.68 crore in Q3FY26 as compared to Rs. 52.42 
crore in Q3FY25. 
Sales including other operating income stood at Rs. 1,613.98 crore compared to Rs. 1,396.15 crore in 
Q3FY25, an increase of 15.60%. The company recorded a profit before tax of Rs. 77.45 crore in Q3FY26 as 
compared to Rs. 72.75 crore during the third quarter in the previous year, an increase of 6.46%. 
EBIDTA increased by 5.24% at Rs. 202.93 crore compared to Rs. 192.83 crore in Q3FY25. Earnings per 
share (EPS) for Q3FY26 stood at Rs. 2.33, compared to Rs. 2.23 in Q3FY25.
