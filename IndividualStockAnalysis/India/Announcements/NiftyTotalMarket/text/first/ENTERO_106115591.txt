Annexure A  
 
Brief Profile of Ms. Sanu Kapoor 
 
Ms. Sanu Kapoor is an associate member of the Institute of Company Secretaries of 
India (ACS) and the Institute of Cost and Management Accountants of India (ACMA). 
She is a graduate in Law and Commerce from Mumbai University and also holds an 
EMBA degree from NMIMS, Mumbai. 
 
She is a seasoned Governance and Legal Professional with over two decades of 
experience spanning diverse sectors, including retail, civil aviation, heavy engineering 
(elevators), advertising and media, pharmaceuticals and construction. Before joining 
her current company, she held key roles with leading Indian conglomerates and 
multinational corporations, such as Tata Motors' associate, Automobile Corporation 
of Goa Ltd, Praxis Home Retail Ltd, Otis Elevator (I) Ltd, Publicis Groupe, Go Airlines, 
Unichem Laboratories, and Pepe Jeans London. 
 
With a proven track record in managing complex projects, including mergers and 
acquisitions, she has been instrumental in driving strategic legal initiatives, ensuring 
regulatory compliance, and strengthening governance frameworks. As a key member 
of the Leadership Team, she has worked in close collaboration with the Board of 
Directors. 
 
Her expertise spans corporate governance, the development and implementation of 
the best-corporate policies and codes of conduct, and leadership in key areas such as 
contract management, litigation oversight, investigations, and intellectual property 
matters.
