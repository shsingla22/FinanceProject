Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
V2 Retail delivered a strong second quarter with revenue rising 28.4% year -on-year, supported by our

Q2 FY27 Highlights – Expansion Ahead of the Festive Season  
 
1. Revenue Growth  
 
• Standalone re venue stood at ₹ 905 Cr  in Q2 FY27, vs. ₹ 705 Cr in Q2 FY26  
• Delivered a 28.4%  year -on-year revenue growth in Q2, even though the festive season shifted 
from this quarter last year into the next quarter this year.  
 
2. Same Store Sales Growth (SSSG)  
 
On a festiva l-normalised basis, which compares each day of the quarter with the corresponding

falls in October 2026 (Q3 FY27). This timing shift underscores the importance of looking through 
seasonal distortions to see the underlying stability of our performance.

new stores and the timing of the festive season, while underscoring the scalability of our 
expanding footprint.
