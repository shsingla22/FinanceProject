Quarterly Update on Business (Q 1 FY2 7) 
This document provides a preliminary update on the standalone performance, highlighting the key trends and 
operational developments during the quarter that ended on June  30, 202 6. This will be later followed by a detailed 
disclosure of financial results and earnings presentation once the board approves the financial results for the quarter.  
During the quarter , the Company delivered a mid-single digit volume growth. The quarter was 
supported by momentum in the Food & FMCG portfolio, sustained outperformance in Alternate 
Channels  and healthy growth in Industry Essentials . The Company continues to execute its long -
term strategy to build India's largest and trusted Food Platform, leveraging its pan -India 
distribution, strong consumer brands , integrated manufacturing network and multi -category 
product portfolio.  
 
Food & FMCG  
Food & FMCG deliver ed 20%+ YoY revenue growth during the quarter , with underlying volume 
growth of 17%+ . 
 
Rice continued its strong growth trajectory, recording revenue growth of 40% + YoY , while Wheat 
Flour continued to recover . The Company's Food portfolio excluding Wheat and Rice  business, 
which includ es categories such as  pulses, besan  (chickpea flour) , soya nuggets, sugar, poha and 
personal care products , continued to scale well, delivering a revenue growth of 25% YoY.  
 
The Company continues to strengthen its multi -category Food portfolio through focused 
investments in brand building, distribution expansion, premiumi zation and product innovation.  
 
During the quarter, the Company entered  a brand licensing arrangement for the Madhur brand, 
further strengthening its Food portfolio. The addition of Madhur complements the Company's 
multi -brand  and multi -product  strategy.   AVVL

Edible Oil business recorded 13% YoY revenue growth, while volumes remained broadly flat 
during the quarter . Due to the ongoing geopolitical  events  impacting commodity prices, trade 
remained cautious in building inventories, which affected  primary sales during the latter part of 
the quarter , particularly in general trade . However, within the Alternate Channel , the segment 
grew in low teens  (volume) . 
 
Industry Essentials  
Industry Essentials recorded 14% YoY volume growth and 30% YoY revenue growth during the 
quarter. The performance was supported by robust demand across the Oleochemicals & Specialty 
Chemicals portfolio, which continued to deliver high -teen volume growth, along with healthy 
performance across castor and de -oiled cakes . 
 
NPD & Health & Convenience Portfolio  
The Company continues to strengthen its premium and value -added portfolio through focused 
investments in category expansion. The product s under premium category  such as Fortune 
Sharbati Atta, Fortune Multi Grain Atta, Fortune Premio Olive Oils and Fortune Premio Cold 
Pressed Oils, continue to witness encouraging consumer response across markets.  
 
The Company is also expanding its Health & Convenience portfolio, comprising products such as 
soya Nuggets, brown rice, biryani kit (RTC), rice bran oil, cold pressed mustard oil and olive oil. 
This portfolio remains an important pillar of the Company's long -term growth strategy as 
consumers increasingly shift towards healthier and convenience -led food choices.  
 
Alternate Channels  
Alternate Channels  (E-Commerce, Quick Commerce & Modern Trade ) continued to significantly 
outpace the broader industry, delivering 27% YoY growth during the quarter.  
 
Quick Commerce remained one of the fastest -growing channels for the Company, delivering 
significantly high double -digit growth, supported by the Company's multi -category portfolio, 
digital -first capabilities and strong execution across platform partners.

distribution efficiency and market execution.

The Company's subsidiaries continued to deliver healthy growth during the quarter. GD Foods,

growth of 19%. Omkar Chemicals also recorded 18% YoY revenue growth .

AWL Agri Business Ltd. (formerly Adani Wilmar Limited) is one of India’s largest Food & FMCG 
companies, offering a diverse portfolio of essential kitchen staples, including edible oils, wheat 
flour, rice, pulses, and sugar. AWL through its brands, including flagship brand  - Fortune, 
commands the trust of more than 135 million households, reaching to every 1 in 3 Indian families. 
With 24 manufacturing facilities across 11 states, including India’s largest single -location refinery 
in Mundra (5,000 tons per day capacity), AWL ensures seamless production and distribution  
across the country . Its extensive supply chain, supported by 110+ stock points, over 10,000 
distributors and sub -distributors, along with a retail network of 2. 6 million outlets, guarantees 
widespread accessibility across urban and rural India.  
Beyond serving retail consumers, AWL also caters the HoReCa and institutional consumers , with 
staple food products, specialized bakery and Lauric fats, Castor Oil derivatives, Oleochemicals, 
and value -added Soya products. Its advanced castor oil facilities produce pharmaceutical -grade, 
and low -moisture castor oil, catering to global markets.  Additionally, AWL has expanded into the 
Home & Personal Care (HPC) segment, offering soaps, handwashes, and multipurpose cleaners, 
further strengthening its product offerings.
