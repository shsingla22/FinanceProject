Annexure I 
 
Brief profile of Shri Gunjan Dinesh Kumar Shah 
 
 
 
Shri Gunjan Shah, 51 years of age, holds a Bachelor of Technology (Computers) from VJTI, 
Mumbai and a Post Graduate Diploma in Management from Indian Institute of Management, 
Calcutta.  Shri Gunjan Shah is an accomplished leader with nearly three decades of 
experience in Consumer durables, Retail, FMCG and Telecom sectors.  
 
Prior to this he served as the Managing Director and CEO of Bata India Limited. During his 
five-year tenure, he led the company’s growth and transformation agenda, with a focus on 
franchise-led expansion, brand premiumization and casualization, supply-chain efficiencies, 
technology transformation and strengthening Bata’s market presence. 
 
Earlier, Shri Shah spent nearly 14 years with Britannia Industries Limited, progressing through 
several leadership positions, including Regional Sales, Head of Enterprise-wide 
Transformation (Britannia NEXT), General Manager – Replenishment and Vice President – 
Sales, before becoming the Chief Commercial Officer. He played an important role in 
advancing Britannia’s growth, strengthening its go-to-market capabilities and driving its 
transformation agenda. He also served as Head of Britannia’s International Business, with 
end-to-end responsibility for its P&L and growth strategy. 
 
Shri Shah began his professional career with Asian Paints and subsequently worked with 
Motorola, gaining extensive experience in sales, marketing and market development. 
 
The Committee may note that Shri Gunjan Shah brings with him strategic, consumer-focused 
and execution-driven leadership with proven experience in business transformation and 
growth. It is felt that his appointment will significantly strengthen the Company’s operations 
and accelerate its journey towards continuing to be trusted industry leader.
