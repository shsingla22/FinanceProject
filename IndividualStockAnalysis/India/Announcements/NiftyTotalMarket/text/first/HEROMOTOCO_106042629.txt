Press Release  
 
New Delhi, November 1, 2024  
 
 
HERO MOTOCORP SELLS 6.8 LAKH MOTORCYCLES & SCOOTERS 
IN OCTOBER 2024; REGISTERS 18% GROWTH  
 
 
Hero MotoCorp , the world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters, 
sold  679,091 units of motorcycles and scooters in October 2024. 
 
The Company registered a healthy double-digit growth of 18% over the corresponding 
month of the previous fiscal when it sold 574,930 units in October 2023. This significant 
growth was primarily driven by robust demand from key urban and rural areas, particularly 
in the 100cc and 125cc segment during the festive period.  
 
Hero MotoCorp’s electric vehicle brand, VIDA, continued to have a strong growth trajectory 
this year and achieved its highest-ever monthly dispatches, reaching 8,750 units in October 
2024.  
 
With the commencement of Hero MotoCorp’s Grand Indian Festival of Trust (GIFT), ‘Shubh 
Muhurat Aaya,  Hero Saath Laya, the company launched attractive offers in October, providing  
customers an opportunity to bring home their favourite Hero products. The festive retail has 
been doing very well, with Hero MotoCorp recording its highest-ever Dhanteras day, 
registering a double-digit growth over last year.  
 
Hero MotoCorp has swiftly expanded its premium dealership network to over 50 “Premia” 
outlets in as many cities, aiming to reach 100 such outlets by March . During the month, the 
company inaugurated nine Premia outlets in various cities across the country, making its 
premium portfolio more accessible to customers. These Premia dealerships feature 
dedicated sections for Hero MotoCorp, VIDA, and Harley-Davidson products under one roof. 
 
Hero MotoCorp achieved significant recognition by winning the prestigious Sword of Honour 
from the British Safety Council. The company was among 70 organizations globally to be 
honored with this award for demonstrating excellence in health, safety, and well- being at 
work. The honor was bestowed upon Hero MotoCorp’s Gurugram manufacturing facility, 
acknowledging its commitment, dedication, and professionalism. 
 
Hero MotoCorp launched a new campaign for its Hero Xtreme Power Brand featuring 
international cricket icon, Virat Kohli. The campaign, directed by renowned German 
 ilmmaker Oliver, is centered around the brand's motto, "Challenge the Extreme," and 
showcases the power, precision, and performance of the Xtreme 125R and Xtreme 160R 4V 
motorcycles in the backdrop of London.
