Manappuram Finance Q2FY27 Consolidated Net Profit Rises 222.90% YoY 
to ₹701 crore; Consolidated AUM at ₹73,303 crore, a growth of 60.1 % year 
on year 
Valapad, Keralam; November 12, 2026:  Manappuram Finance Ltd, a leading non-
banking financial company (NBFC), today announced its unaudited financial results 
for the quarter and half-year ended September 30, 2026 (Q2FY27 and H1FY27). 
The gold loan NBFC reported a 222.90% year-on-year (YoY) growth in 
consolidated net profit to ₹701 crore. In the same quarter of the previous fiscal year 
(Q2FY26), consolidated net profit stood at ₹217 crore.  
For the quarter under consideration, the consolidated revenue from operations grew 
46.2% YoY to ₹3,356 crore, compared with ₹2,296 crore in Q2FY26. Consolidated 
assets under management (AUM) as on September 30, 2026 grew 60.1% YoY to 
₹73,303 crore. Gold loan AUM stood at ₹60,640 crore, a growth of 92.5% YoY. 
Non-gold loan businesses accounted for 17.27% of consolidated AUM.  
The gold tonnage for Q2 was 70 tonnes and it grew year on year to 22 % and 3.5 % 
sequentially. For the quarter under consideration, the customer base grew 
sequentially 2.3 % to 46.9 lakh customers.    
Asirvad Micro Finance Ltd, the company’s microfinance subsidiary, reported a 
profit of ₹32 crore for the quarter, with AUM of ₹7,919 crore as on September 30, 
2026.  
For the half-year ended September 30, 2026 (H1FY27), consolidated net profit stood 
at ₹1,286 crore, compared with ₹350 crore in H1FY26, while consolidated revenue 
from operation stood at ₹6,403 crore, against ₹4,546 crore in H1FY26. 
On a consolidated basis, the company’s branch network stood at 5,375 as on 
September 30, 2026. 
The Board of Directors has declared an interim dividend of ₹1 per equity share of 
face value ₹2 each for FY27. 
Mr V.P. Nandakumar, Chairman and Managing Director, Manappuram 
Finance Ltd, said: “In Q2, we have delivered strong growth in profitability and 
AUM. The year-on-year growth in consolidated net profit sets a strong tone for 
the coming quarters. Our subsidiaries have also shown improved performance 
on a year-on-year basis. Looking ahead, the outlook for the gold loan business 
remains positive as we continue to strengthen the business to navigate future 
challenges. We are confident of achieving our targets for this fiscal year.” 
 
About Manappuram Finance Ltd  
Manappuram Finance Ltd is one of India’s leading gold loan NBFCs, engaged in 
providing finance against gold jewellery. Incorporated in 1992, the company was 
promoted by Mr V.P. Nandakumar, whose family has been involved in the gold 
loan business since 1949. It is headquartered in Valapad, in the Thrissur district of 
Kerala. The company went public in August 1995, and its shares are listed on the 
BSE and NSE.
