HCLTech recognized as a Leader in Everest Group ’s Custom Application 
Development  Services PEAK Matrix® Assessment 202 5 
 
NEW YORK  and NOIDA, India, May 28, 2025 —HCLTech , a leading global technology company,  
has been named a Leader in Everest Group ’s Custom Application Development  Services PEAK 
Matrix® Assessment 202 5. The recognition acknowledges HCLTech's capabilities in modern 
application development, cloud -native develop ment and API/microservices strategies . 
 
The report highlights HCLTech ’s investments in expanding its custom application development 
(CAD)  capabilities  through IP development  and co-innovation  with clients . Strategic 
acquisitions  such as ASAP Group, Quest Informatics and Confinale , and partnership extensions 
with partners like HPE and Temenos have strengthened HCLTech ’s delivery of industry -specific 
solutions at scale.   
 
"HCLTech has solidified its position  as a Leader in custom application development by 
leveraging strategic acquisitions and delivering domain -specific solutions at scale," says Ankit 
Gupta, Vice President at Everest Group. “ HCLTech ’s investments across diverse industries—
from BFSI to telecom —have expanded its capabilities and enhanced its ability to serve clients 
with tailored, industry- aligned solutions. Clients also value HCLTech ’s flexible commercial 
models and strong technical and domain expertise, which drive successful outcomes in 
compl ex engagements. These attributes have contributed to HCLTech ’s recognition as a 
Leader in Everest Group ’s Custom Application Development Services PEAK Matrix® 
Assessment 2025.”  
 
"We believe this recognition highlights our commitment to providing scalable, secure and 
flexible custom application development  services that align perfectly with our clients' business 
needs. By leveraging AI Force , we drive service transformation across the software engine ering

innovation to our clients, helping them navigate the complexities of digital transformation and

Global Head  of Digital Business Services at HCLTech.
