From the CMD’s Desk  
Commenting on the company’s performance, Mr. Srinivasagopalan Rangarajan , Chairman & 
Managing Director, Data Patterns (India)  Limited  said, "The business momentum during H1FY27 
has been encouraging, particularly on the order inflow front. We received fresh orders worth Rs. 
929 crore s in Q2FY27, taking H1FY27 inflows to Rs. 1,046 crore, nearly three times the level 
recorded in H1FY26. Our curren t order book, including orders received and negotiated, stands at 
approximately Rs. 2,698  crore s, providing healthy revenue visibility. We are also seeing 
encouraging opportunities for larger, complete -system contracts across radar, electronic warfare 
and communication systems, supported by our investments in technology and manufacturing 
capabilities.  
 
Q2FY27 was a more muted quarter from a revenue perspective, following the exceptionally strong 
execution in Q2FY26. With several program s milestones progress ing and deliveries expected to 
pick up, we expect H2FY27 to be stronger than H1FY27, supported by our healthy order  book  and 
execution momentum.

Looking ahead, we remain confident in our business outlook. Strong order inflows, a healthy 
pipeline and continued progress in execution position us well for the remainder of the year. We 
remain on track to achieve our full -year targets, and our FY27 guidance remains unchanged " 
 
About Data Patterns   
Data Patterns’ core competencies include design and developm ent across electronic hardware, 
software, firmware, mechanical, product prototype besides its testing, validation and 
verification. Its involvement has been across Radars, Electronic Warfare Suites, Communication s, 
Avionics, Small Satellites, Automated Tes t Equipment, COTS and programmes catering to Tejas 
Light Combat Aircraft, Light Utility Helicopter, BrahMos and other Communication & Electronic 
Intelligence Systems.  
The company works closely with the defence PSUs such as Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd and Bha rat 
Electronics Ltd as well as government organisations involved in defence and space research like
