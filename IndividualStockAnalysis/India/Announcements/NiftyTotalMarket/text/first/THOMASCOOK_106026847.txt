Sterling  strengthens its Wildlife presence with  2nd resort at Pench  
• Located in Maharashtra, c lose to the Khawasa and Khursapar Gates . 
• 4 acres of secluded wilderness setting . 
• Spacious rooms and tents with sit -outs . 
• Alfresco restaurant and treetop machan for private dining experience.  
 
Sterling Holiday Resorts  – India’s leading leisure hospitality brand  has announced its second resort in 
Pench , on the Maharashtra side of the Pench wildlife sanctuary . The resort is con veniently located just off 
NH44 and easily accessible from Nagpur.  
The 4 -acre property is embellished by manicured lawns and flagstone paths and is a green haven, making 
it an ideal getaway from Nagpur. Located close to and midway between the Khawasa and Khursapar Gate, 
it can be an ideal base to explore the Pench wildlife sanctuary.  
The resort has a choice of villa- styled rooms  and large Luxury Tents, all equipped with a sit -out for guests 
to relax and enjoy the serene surroundings especially in the evenings. The rooms give a rustic look from 
outside, blending well with the surroundings, while they are equipped with all the modern amenities for 
a comfortable stay. Special features include open -to-sky showers, bathtubs with a separate shower are a 
and premier rooms have a dedicated powder room within and a private lawn space outside.  
“Sigri” is an  open -format restaurant th at provides a unique dining experience with beautiful view s of the 
serene surroundings. One can enjoy popular favorites and del icious local dishes from Maharashtra and 
Madhya Pradesh. The “Machan” provides a tree -top dining experience ideal to enjoy a private celebration 
dinner, or a special gathering while enjoying the views of the surroundings.  
The resort also boasts of a 4,500 sq.ft. banqueting space, and a 20,000 sq.ft lawn, ideal for small 
conferences, intimate gatherings and celebrating social events  and weddings . Guests can also unwind in  
the swimming pool  or at The Spa  after enjoying a morning safari.  
"Sterling Orient Woods, is our second resort at Pench . The rustic setting  and the greenery make it ideal 
not only as a getaway for people from Nagpur, but also to explore the Pench wildlife sanctuary while 
enjoying all creature comforts. " said Mr. Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director  & CEO , Sterling Holiday 
Resorts.  
“We are extremely pleased to collaborate with Sterling Holiday Resorts. Sterling has a strong presence in 
wildlife resorts and we are sure they will bring their expertise in hospitality in general, and wildlife resorts 
in particular to make Sterling Orient Woods Pench, a grand success ,” said Mr. Afzal Mitha, MD Orient 
Group of Hotels & Resorts.   
The resort als o has a holiday activity center that provides options of indoor and outdoor games to bond 
and spend quality time with friends and family.  Besides safaris, t he resort also provides unique 
experiences like boating on the Khokha river, Nature walks, visit to pottery village where guests can 
indulge in pottery -making.
