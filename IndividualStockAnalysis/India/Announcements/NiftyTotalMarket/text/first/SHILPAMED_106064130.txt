Shilpa Pharma Lifesciences Limited, Received CEP from EDQM for API, Nifedipine 
 
 
This is to inform you that the Shilpa Medicare Limited’s 100% subsidiary, Shilpa Pharma Lifesciences Limited 
received certificate of suitability (CEP) from EDQM (European Directorate for the Quality of Medicines & 
Healthcare) for API, Nifedipine. 
 
Nifedipine is in a class of medications called calcium-channel blockers in the dihydropyridine subclass. 
Nifedipine is used as an antihypertensive and as an anti-anginal medication. It lowers blood pressure by 
relaxing the blood vessels so the heart does not have to pump as hard. It controls chest pain by increasing 
the supply of blood and oxygen to the heart 
 
The CEP showcases our expertise and commitment to quality-oriented development and commercial 
production, which meets the expectations of global quality standards.
