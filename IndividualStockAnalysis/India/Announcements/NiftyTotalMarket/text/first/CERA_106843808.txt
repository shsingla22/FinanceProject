CMD’s Message  
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Vikram  Somany, Chairman & Managing Director , said,   
“We are pleased to report a healthy performance in Q2 FY27, with revenues growing by 27.4% year -on-
year, supported by broad based volume -led growth across our key business segments. The 
sanitaryware and faucetware segments registered growth of 24% and 38%, respectively. Retail demand 
continued to recover and was complemented by a steady performance in the projects business. 
Business momentum was sustained throughout the quarter, reflecting improving underlying demand 
conditions and consistent execution.  
The industry continues to navigate a challenging cost environment, particularly with elevated brass and 
gas prices. We have undertaken calibrated price revisions, including a price revision in faucetware in 
September, alongside measures to strengthen prici ng discipline and protect profitability in the projects 
business. While the benefits of these initiatives will materialise progressively, we have already
