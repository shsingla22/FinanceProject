opportunities . Deeply  passionate  about  Bio-economy  and Environment,  Dr. Chaudhari  is committed  to develop  clean  and green  technologies . Dr. Chaudhari  is a ‘Distinguished  Alumnus  of 
IIT Bombay  (1971 )’ and an alumnus  of Harvard  Business  School  (AMP  1995 ). He is the first Indian  to receive  the global  honour  of the prestigious  ‘George  Washington  Carver  Award  2020 ’ 
by BIO-Impact,  Washington  DC, USA and the first Asian  recipient  of the prestigious  ‘William  C. Holmberg  Award  2022 ’ for Lifetime  Achievement  in Bioeconomy
