Revolt  Motors  secures  order  for 1,100 electric  motorcycles 
from Bihar Police  
Major  institutional  order  puts  electric  motorcycles  in focus  for police  mobility  across  India  
New  Delhi,  4th November  2026:  Revolt  Motors,  India's  No.1  electric  bike  brand and a wholly  owned  
subsidiary of RattanIndia Enterprises Limited, has secured an order to supply 1,100 BlazeX electric 
bikes from Bihar Police for patrolling operations. The order marks an important step in the company's 
institutional  business,  extending  the application  of its elsectric  bikes  beyond  individual  riders  to public -
sector mobility and law enforcement requirements.  
A fleet of 1,100 electric motorcycles entering service in a single order takes electric motorcycles well 
beyond  trial use in law enforcemen t. This order  will also work  as a catalyst  for other  state  police  forces, 
defense  organizations, government departments, public -sector undertakings and other institutions 
looking to electrify their two -wheeler fleets.  
For patrolling teams, the usefulness of a bike extends beyond its on -road performance. Charging 
flexibility, the ability to carry essential equipment and the distance covered between charges can all 
influence  day-to-day operations.  The Revolt  BlazeX  offers  a range  of up to 150 km and can be charged 
using  a standard  10A power  socket,  giving  operators  greater  flexibility  in identifying  suitable  charging 
points without necessarily depending on dedicated charging infrastructure. Its removable battery adds 
another charging option, while under -seat storage allows the charger to be carried on the bike and 
kept accessible  when required. It also  provides significant savings for  organizations since electric bikes  
have  significantly  less running  and maintenance  costs  than  a petrol  equivalent,  savings  that add up 
quickly across a fleet of this size.  
The BlazeX also comes with inbuilt GPS, offering location -related functionality that supports vehicle 
tracking. Together, these features make the BlazeX a practical electric mobility option for 
organizations  evaluating  the use of electric  bikes  in regular  field  operations.  The order  also represents 
an opportunity for Revolt to serve a use case where vehicle availability, charging arrangements and 
day-to-day usability matter alongside performance.  
Patrolling requi res dependable mobility and practical arrangements for keeping vehicles ready for 
daily  duty.  Factors  such  as charging  access,  range  and the ability  to manage  vehicles  across  operational 
areas make Revolt electric bikes an ideal option for electric fleet.  
Commenting on the order, Mrs. Anjali Rattan, Chairperson, RattanIndia Enterprises Limited , said, 
“Securing  an order  for 1,100  electric  bikes  from  Bihar  Police  is a significant  step  for Revolt.  Institutional 
mobility presents a different set of  requirements  from individual ownership, and engagements of this 
scale give us an opportunity to take our products into real -world operating environments. We see 
institutional adoption as an important avenue for expanding the role of electric bikes in India. ” 
The bikes  are scheduled  to be delivered  in Patna.  The order  carries  a contractual  completion  timeline 
of January 2027, while Revolt Motors is working towards completing deliveries by November 2026 
signifying its strong execution capabilities.  
The order  builds  on Revolt  Motors'  presence  in India's  electric  bike  market,  supported  by a community 
of more  than  60,000 riders  and a network  of over 182+ dealerships across  the country. The  company 
continues to expand its reach through products designed for everyday mobilit y and opportunities to 
serve a broader range of institutional requirements.
