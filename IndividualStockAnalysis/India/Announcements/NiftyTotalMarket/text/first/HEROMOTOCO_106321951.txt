HERO MOTOCORP DISPATCHES 4.5 LAKH UNITS OF 
MOTORCYCLES & SCOOTERS IN JULY 2025, ACHIEVES  21% 
YEAR -ON-YEAR  GROWTH  
 
VIDA SURGES AHEAD WITH  RECORD BREAKING SALES  – SURPASSES 11,200 UNITS IN 
JUL Y 2025  
 
GLOBAL BUSINESS ACCELERATES AHEAD WITH 37,3 58 UNITS  SOLD , OUTPAC ING  
INDUSTRY GROWTH  
 
Hero MotoCorp, the world ’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters, dispatched  
449,755  units in July 2025, registering a 21% increase over 370,274  units recorded in July 
2024.  
 
Hero MotoCorp registered over 339,827  VAHAN * retail registrations in July 2025, in line with 
market trends across both urban and rural regions. Retail activity remained stable , and with 
the upcoming  festive season,  volumes are projected to rise  in the comi ng months.  
*Data from VAHAN, excluding Telangana, as on August 1, 2025.  
During the month, the company strengthened its presence across key segments. In scooters, 
market share gains were led by the strong performance of the Destini 125  and Xoom 125.  
In the motorcycle category, Hero MotoCorp  expanded the HF Deluxe Portfolio with the 
launch of HF Deluxe Pro . Featuring refreshed design, segment -leading features, and superior 
fuel efficiency, the model enhances the appeal of the entry -level motorcycle segment fo r a 
broad spectrum of customers.   
VIDA, powered by Hero MotoCorp, achieved a major  landmark  in July 2025, delivering its 
highest -ever monthly performance with 11,22 6 units dispatched and 10,489 VAHAN 
registrations . The company also double d its EV VAHAN market share year -on-year (YoY) to 
10.2%, underscoring  the rapidly accelerating consumer adoption of Hero MotoCorp’s 
electric mobility portfolio.  
The recently launched VIDA Evooter VX2 – “Badalte India ka Scooter ” has received an 
exceptional market respons e, reinforcing its position in the fast -growing electric two -
wheeler segment. The VIDA Evooter offers  a new riding experience by combining electric 
innovation with proven  scooter reliability,  with its game -changing Battery -as-a-Service 
(BaaS) model, making  EV adoption simpler  for customers.  
 
Building on its strong growth trajectory  and staying ahead of industry trends , Hero 
MotoCorp ’s global business maintained strong  momentum in July 2025, with sales exceeding 
37,300 units. The company ’s expanding global footprint highlight s its commitment to
