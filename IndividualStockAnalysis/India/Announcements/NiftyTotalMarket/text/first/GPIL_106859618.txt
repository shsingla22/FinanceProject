Sub: Outcome of Board Meeting and submission of Financial Results for the Quarter and 
Half Year ended 30th September, 2026.  
 
The Board of Directors of Godawari Power and Ispat Limited (“the Company”) at its Meeting held 
today i.e. on 13th November, 2026, has considered and;      
  
1. Approved the Un- Audited Financial Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results for the 
Quarter and Half Year ended 30th September, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 33 of SEBI (Listing 
Obligation and Disclosure Requirements) Regulation 2015. 
 
We are enclosing herewith the Un-Audited Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results of 
the Company duly approved by the Board along with the Limited Review Report issued by the 
Statutory Auditors on the Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results of the Company for 
the Quarter and Half Year ended on 30th September, 2026 collectively as Annexure-A . 
 
2. Approved the Capex of Rs.135 Crores for setting up a 3 GWh BESS project for C&I Application 
in the Godawari New Energy Pvt Ltd (GNEPL) at Auric Industrial Area, Sambhajinagar, 
Maharashra alongside the exiting BESS project under implementation. The Project 
implementation shall commence shortly and expected to be commissioned by March 2028. The 
project will be funded through additional investment of Rs.135 Crores from GPIL in GNEPL.  
 
The additional information to be given pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of SEBI 
(LODR) Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular are given in Annexure B.  
 
3. Approved the proposal for making additional investment of Rs.110 Crores by way of 
equity/quasi equity/loan or any other permissible mode in Godawari New Energy Private 
Limited (GNEPL), a wholly owned subsidiary company, from time to time in one or more 
tranches to undertake a new line of business of Commercial & Industrial (C&I) Energy Storage. 
 
The proposed investment is in addition to the existing investments approval of Rs.700 Crores 
for the purpose capex and working capital requirements. Thus, the total investment of the 
Company in GNEPL shall be Rs.810 Crores.

9. Approved the further investment of Rs.20 Crores including initial investment of Rs 1.00 Lakh

Limited (GESPL), a wholly owned subsidiary company, from time to time in one or more 
tranches from the surplus/internal accruals of the Company. The above-said investment is not

GPIL.

equipment and materials, construction, erection, installation, testing, commissioning, project 
management, operation and maintenance and other incidental and ancillary activities. 
 
10. Approved the strategic investment of Rs.26.00 Crores for obtaining 26% stake in Solarcraft 
Power India 32 Private Limited (Solarcraft), by way of subscription to equity and other 
securities, as may be mutually decided, by the Company and Solarcraft, to enable the Company 
to procure long term renewable power for its manufacturing operations and support its objectives 
of optimising power costs and increasing the share of renewable energy in its energy mix.

Reason or rational for Change of Logo:  
 
The Company has, over the years, established its presence in the steel and power sectors under 
the existing logo “HIRA” and the tagline “Growing Stronger Together”.  
 
Considering the Company's evolving business profile, expansion plans, increasing focus on 
new energy initiatives and long-term growth strategy, the management considers it appropriate 
to adopt a brand identity that more closely reflects the Company's name, business philosophy, 
future aspirations and growth trajectory.  
 
The change relates only to the Company’s brand identity and logo. There is no change in the 
legal name of the Company, which continues to remain Godawari Power and Ispat Limited, and 
there is no impact on the Company’s shareholding pattern, capital structure, business 
operations, or statutory obligations. All statutory, regulatory, and contractual documentation
