MOIL achieves remarkable growth in H1 FY ‘27: Net Profit Surges 22.5%  
 
 
Delhi, November 2nd, 2026: The Board of Directors of MOIL Limited approved the financial 
and operational results for the second quarter and Half year of FY 27. The company recorded a growth across production, sales and profitability in the first half year of the current financial year. The Board also declared first interim divided @ Rs. 2.20 per equity share of Rs. 10 each for 
FY 27.

Financial Highlights (H1 FY 2026 -27 vs H1 FY 2025- 26)

● Total Revenue:  Increased by 5.81%   
● Profit Before Tax (PBT):  Registered a 22.9% growth

● Manganese Ore Sales: Increase of 9.67%.  
● Manganese Ore Production:  Increase of 1.76%  
 
Appreciating the efforts of the entire MOIL team, Shri V Suresh, Chairman and Managing 
Director, MOIL said:  
 
“Our strong H1 performance underlines the potential of our human resource, whose hard work remains 
the bedrock of our success. Through incisive strategic planning and optimized mining practices, we have 
successfully unlocked new efficiencies. We are heavily prioritizing our 'Waste to Wealth' interventions 
to turn low grade mining products into high -value assets, ensuring sustainable growth while firmly 
maintaining our market price leadership to support the domestic steel ecosy stem.”  
Further, he assured all stakeholders that “MOIL Limited is focussed on exploring new growth 
opportunities, both in domestic and international, across mining and downstream value addition”  
 
About MOIL Limited:  
 
MOIL Limited is a Schedule ‘A’ Miniratna Category -I Central Public Sector Enterprise under 
the Ministry of Steel, Government of India. It is the largest producer of manganese ore in the 
country, operating underground and open -cast mines across Maharashtra and Madhya 
Pradesh.
