Press Release  
Greaves Finance appoints Mukul Kanchan as Executive Director & CEO to scale 
up EV financing  
Bengaluru, 13 October  2026:  Greaves Finance Limited (GFL), a Non -Banking Financial Company and 
wholly owned subsidiary of Greaves Cotton Limited, appointed Mukul Kanchan as its Executive Director 
& Chief Executive Officer, effective October 1, 2026, subject to necessary approvals.  
Mukul brings nearly two decades of experience across lending, corporate finance, private equity and 
M&A. He joins from Bengaluru -based employee benefits platform Plum, where he served as CFO, leading 
finance, legal, compliance, fundraising, investor relati ons and strategic transactions. Earlier, he spent six 
years at Clix  Capital, playing a key role in its 2016 acquisition of GE Capital’s India lending businesses. He 
began his career with The D. E. Shaw Group and EY in private equity, investment banking and advisory 
roles.  Mukul holds an MBA in Finance from ICFAI Business  School (IBS), Hyderabad.  
Mukul will lead GFL’s next phase of growth, scaling tailored financing for electric two -wheeler buyers 
through ev.fin, its 100% EV -focused lending platform. Launched in 2023, ev.fin  is GFL’s consumer finance 
technology platform for electric mobility. It works with EV manufacturers and their dealer networks to 
offer financing at the point of sale, serving customers across 86 cities.  
Commenting on his appointment, Mukul Kanchan, Executive Director & CEO, Greaves Finance Limited , 
said, “Electric two -wheelers are fast becoming the everyday choice for India’s commuters, gig workers and 
small businesses, and access to simple, fair credit is often what turns that intent into ownership. ev.fin has 
built strong foundations with leading EV OEM’s across the country. I am delighted to join Greaves Finance, 
and I look forward to scaling the business responsibly, with sharp credit discipline, a diversified funding 
base and partnerships that mak e EV ownership easier for more Indians.”  
At GFL, Mukul will focus on deepening ev.fin’s  partnerships with EV OEM’s, including brands like Ampere, 
broadening the company’s funding base, and building a scalable, well -governed lending franchise with 
strong credit discipline.

About Greaves Finance Limited:  
Greaves Finance Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of Greaves Cotton Ltd , through its 100% EV-focused  
lending platform evfin, is India's only EV-focused non -banking financial company (NBFC).  With a mission 
to democratise the EV experience, Greaves Finance Limited, under its platform evfin , provides innovative 
financing solutions exclusively tailored to electric vehicle ownership, supporting the growth of sustainable 
mobility in India.  
For more information, visit  www.evfin.co  
 
 
About Greaves Cotton Limited:  
With a legacy of over 165 years, Greaves Cotton Limited is a diversified, future -ready engineering company 
delivering innovative and sustainable solutions to customers across key sectors. Renowned for its 
precision engineering and technology leadership, th e Company’s growth strategy is anchored on three 
core dimensions: accelerating its core businesses, building new muscle onto the core, and expanding into 
new horizons across Energy Solutions, Mobility Solutions, and Industrial Solutions.  
  
As one of India’s largest manufacturers of single -cylinder diesel engines, and a pioneer in introducing them 
for microcar applications in the European market, Greaves continues to reinforce its commitment to 
accessible, efficient, and reliable mobility. Gu ided by its purpose of ‘Empowering Lives’, Greaves is 
committed to providing reliable products, sustainable technology, and customer -centric solutions. With 
its strong engineering foundation and continuous innovation, the Company aims to enable people, 
businesses, and communities to progress with confidence in a future rooted in engineering excellence and 
driven by efficient energy.  
  
The investee companies of Greaves include Greaves Electric Mobility Limited (GEML) with its diverse 
portfolio of electric 2 & 3 wheelers for passenger and cargo mobility, and Greaves Finance Limited (GFL) 
with ev.fin, a 100%  EV-focused NBFC that leverages cutting -edge technology to offer customised financing 
options & seamless buying experience. Both these businesses play a significant role in accelerating EV 
adoption in India.
