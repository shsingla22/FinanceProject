Sirs, Ma'am, 
After close to 3 years of wonderful & fulfilling career with the company, I wish to pursue 
opportunities outside. The Company has gone through transformational journey and 
today it is future ready and well positioned to lead & stay as a significant player in the 
industry. 
I wish to resign from the position of MD & CEO of PNB Housing Finance Limited. I take 
this opportunity to thank each member of the board, all stakeholders, Management and 
colleagues for their trust & support. It has been an exciting and rewarding journey 
professionally and otherwise. 
I also wish to thank the board of Directors who offered me the opportunity to serve on the 
boards of the Company's subsidiaries PHFL Home Loans & Services and PEHEL 
Foundation. Working with each of these organizations has widened my perspectives and 
I will forever cherish my time with PNB Housing Group. 
While the management team is very capable in taking over the mantle with professional 
ease, I am committed to a smooth transition and will always make myself available to 
assist in anyway possible during the period. 
Wishing the company continued success in the future. 
I request the board to relieve me within 90 days. 
Sincerely, 
Girish Kousgi
