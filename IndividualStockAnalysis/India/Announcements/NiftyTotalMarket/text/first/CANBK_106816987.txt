FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER AND PERIOD ENDED 30th SEPTEMBER 2026

                               Key Highlights  
                         (September  2025 V/s September  2026) 
 
 Global Business stood at ₹ 30,72,365 Cr grew by 15.88%

 RAM Credit grew by 21.03%

 Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR) at 95.34% improved by 175 bps

Key Summary of Business Performance (as on 30.09.2026)  
         Business 
 
 Global Business increased by 15.88% (y.o.y) to ₹ 30,72,365 Cr as at September 2026, Global Deposits 
increased by 13.13% (y.o.y) to ₹16,97,305 Cr and Global Advance (gross) increased by 19.46% (y.o.y) 
to ₹13,75,060 Cr. 
 Domestic Deposit of the Bank stood at ₹15,47,979 Cr as at September 2026 with growth of 10.97% 
(y.o.y). 
 Domestic Advances (gross) of the Bank stood at ₹12,64,753 Cr as at September 2026 grew by 16.95% 
(y.o.y). 
 RAM credit increased by 21.03% (y.o.y) to ₹ 8,12,310 Cr. 
 Retail lending Portfolio increased by 31.61% (y.o.y) to ₹3,30,587 Cr.  
 Housing Loan Portfolio increased by 17.35% (y.o.y) to ₹1,34,501 Cr. 
 
Asset Quality 
 
 Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) ratio improved to 1.33% as at September 2026 reduced from 1.57% 
as at June 2026, 2.35% as at September 2025. 
 Net Non-Performing Assets (NNPA) ratio improved to 0.30% as at September 2026 reduced from 0.36% 
as at June 2026, 0.54 % as at September 2025. 
 Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR) stood to 95.34% as at September 2026 against 94.76% as at June 2026, 
93.59% as at September 2025. 
Capital Adequacy  
 
 
 CRAR stood at 17.19% as at September 2026. Out of which CET1 is 12.80%, Tier-I is 2.25% and Tier-II 
is 2.14%. 
 
 
 
Priority Sector & Financial Inclusion 
 
 
 The Bank has achieved Targets in Priority Sector at 44.99% and Agricultural Credit at 20.61% of ANBC 
as at September 2026, as against the norm of 40% and 18% respectively. 
 Credit to Small and Marginal Farmers stood at 12.74% of ANBC, against the norm of 10.00%. 
 Credit to Weaker Sections stood at 18.73% of ANBC, against the norm of 12.00%. 
 Credit to Micro Enterprises stood at 9.22% of ANBC, against the norm of 7.50%. 
 Credit to Non-Corporate Farmers stood at 15.55% of ANBC, against the norm of 14.00%.  
Network 
 
 
 As on 30.09.2026, the Bank has 10146 Number of Branches, out of which 3215 are Rural,3040 Semi 
Urban,1994 Urban & 1897 Metro along with 10764 ATM & Recycler. Bank is also having 4 Overseas 
Branches in London, New York, Dubai & IBU Gift City Branch.
