from the Reserve Bank of India  (“RBI”)  or any other regulatory/statutory authority .

Dear Members of the Board, 
Subject: My Retirement from the Board 
After deep reflection and with a heart full of gratitude, I wish to formally inform you of my decision to 
retire from my role as a Director and the Deputy Managing Director on the Board of Kotak Mahindra 
Bank Limited, effective upon the completion of my current term on October 31, 2025. 
It has been an extraordinary journey, one that has spanned over three decades, during which I have 
had the privilege of serving this institution in various diverse capacities. I have genuinely looked forward 
to every single day at the Bank. To have played a part in shaping an organization that now holds a 
distinct and respected position in the industry is a source of immense pride and fulfilment for me. 
I am sincerely grateful for the opportunity to have worked alongside visionary colleagues, outstanding 
leaders and a Board that consistently placed integrity, innovation and long-term value at the cente� of 
its deliberations. The learnings, relationships and memories I carry from this journey are irreplaceable 
and enduring. 
As I approach the close of this chapter, I remain committed to supporting a seamless transition and to 
extending my full co-operation to the Board and the leadership team during this period. 
Thank you for the trust reposed in me and for allowing me to contribute to the Bank's remarkable story. 
I shall always emain one of its most ardent well-wishers .
