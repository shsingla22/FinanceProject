This has reference to our letter dated October 17, 2024 regarding reduction in domestic gas 
allocation to the Company effective from October 16, 2024.

agency for domestic gas allocation), this is to inform that there has been further reduction in domestic gas allocation to the Company effective from November 16, 2024. The revised
