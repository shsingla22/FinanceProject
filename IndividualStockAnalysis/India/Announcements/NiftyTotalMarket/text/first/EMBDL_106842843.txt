Embassy Developments Posts Highest -Ever Quarterly Pre -Sales of ₹3,225 
Crore  
• Q2 FY27 pre -sales reached ₹3,225 crore, up 272% QoQ; collections rose 10% QoQ to ₹546 crore  
• H1 FY27 pre -sales stood at ₹4,093 crore, up 574% YoY and reaching 51% of FY27 pre -sales 
guidance  
• Three project launches with a combined GDV of ₹8,397 crore during the quarter – an increase  
from the company’s pre -launch estimate of ₹7,500 crore  
• Embassy Riverine and Embassy South Reserve recorded combined sales of ₹1,813 crore within 
two weeks of launch; Embassy Terazza recorded ₹758 crore in launch sales, including a ₹711 crore 
single -home transaction , the largest single residential transaction in India to date  
 
Mumbai/Bengaluru – November 3, 2026:  
  
Embassy Developments Limited (NSE: EMBDL / BSE: 532832) ("EDL" or "the Company")  today 
announced its results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, recording  its highest -ever 
quarterly pre -sales of ~₹3,225 crore in Q2 FY27, up ~272% quarter -on-quarter and ~689% year -on-
year. H1 FY27 pre -sales reached ~₹4,093 crore, up ~574% year -on-year and equivalent to ~51% of 
the Company's ₹8,000 crore FY27 pre -sales guidan ce. 
Collections rose 10% quarter -on-quarter to ~₹546 crore, up ~52% from ₹359 crore in the 
corresponding period last year. H1 collections grew ~53% year -on-year to ~₹1,041 crore, 
representing ~35% of the ₹3,000 crore FY27 guidance.  
During the quarter, EDL launched three projects with a combined Gross Development Value (GDV) 
of ₹ 8,397 crore during the quarter – an increase from the company’s pre -launch estimate of 
₹7,500 crore, which reflects the premium pricing the Embassy brand commands . In North 
Bengaluru, Embassy Origins, comprising Embassy Riverine and Embassy South Reserve, recorded 
combined sales of ~₹1,813 crore within two weeks of its launch. Embassy Riverine, with a GDV of ₹ 
3,155  crore, recorded sales of ~₹933 crore, while Embassy South Reserve, with a GDV of ₹ 1,957 
crore, recorded ~₹880 crore.  
In Mumbai, Embassy Terazza, Juhu, recorded ~₹758 crore in sales on launch against an estimated 
GDV of ₹3,285 crore. This included a ~₹711 crore sale for a single residence comprising an entire 
residential tower, which is India's largest single -home residential  transaction.

About Embassy Developments Limited   
Embassy Developments Limited (formerly known as Equinox India Developments Limited) (EDL) is one of 
India’s largest listed real estate developers, specialising in the development of residential and commercial 
projects across key urban markets. With a strat egic focus on Bengaluru, the Mumbai Metropolitan Region 
(MMR), and the National Capital Region (NCR), the Company also has a presence in Chennai and Indore. 
EDL has a diversified residential portfolio with a well -balanced mix of developments across mid -income, 
premium, and luxury segments. Its portfolio of ready, ongoing, and future residential projects includes 
branded residences, uber -luxury apartments and villas, exclusive town homes, condominiums, integrated 
townships, senior living communities, and con temporary homes. The Company is listed on BSE Limited (BSE) 
and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and holds a long -term debt rating of IVR A (Stable). 
EDL benefits from the strong institutional foundation of its promotor, the Embassy Group, which brings over 
three decades of experience and a proven track record of delivering and managing more than 100 million 
sq. ft. across commercial, residential, hospi tality, services, and education sectors in India. Further information 
is available at embassyindia.com
