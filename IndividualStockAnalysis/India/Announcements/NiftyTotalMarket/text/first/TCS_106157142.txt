AMSTERDAM | MUMBAI, February 20, 2025:  Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS), a 
global leader in IT services, consulting, and business solutions, has been ranked number one  for Customer 
Satisfaction  in Europe , according to an  independent  survey by Whitelane Research .  
 
The Whitelane Research 2024/2025 IT Sourcing Study  gathered insights from nearly 2,300 participants from 
Europe’s leading  organizations. The study evaluated over 6,000 unique IT sourcing relationships and more 
than 6,500 cloud platform sourcing relationships , reinforcing TCS’ unique  position  in the industry . This marks 
the 12th straight  year that TCS has held the highest ranking in this survey.  Additionally, the organization also 
secured  the #1 spot for general satisfaction in Financial Services  (84% vs industry average of 77%) and 
Transport & Logistics (84% vs industry average of 73%) , further strengthening its position as a trusted IT 
partner.  
 
Jef Loos, Head Sourcing Europe at Whitelane Research,  said, “Congratulations to TCS for 12 years in first 
place. It is no easy feat to achieve this ranking . This level of consistency over the years speaks of the quality 
of TCS’ IT services and customer focus. Technology  needs can change dramatically year -on-year, just as we 
have seen with the increase in investment in AI and machine learning solutions. An IT service provider that 
can adapt to and anticipate changing needs is therefore essential for customers, and TCS’ ra nking 
demonstrates that they have this ability.”  
 
This recognition underscores TCS’  commitment to serving as a trusted IT partner for European enterprises , 
with a presence in the region  for over 45 years. Throughout this journey, TCS has driven  digital transformation 
for some of Europe’s  leading multinationals across industries such as banking and financial services, 
manufacturing, telecom, retail, travel, logistics, and more. With a diverse and agile workforce operating from 
62 offices across Europe, TCS continues to be a strategic trans formation partner, empowering businesses to 
navigate  change and achieve sustained success.  
 
TCS demonstrated outstanding performance across six critical IT domains, including Digital Transformation 
(79% satisfaction vs. industry average of 75%), Cloud & Infrastructure Services (80% vs. 74%), Workplace 
Services (80% vs. 74%), Security Services (81 % vs. 74%), Network & Connectivity Services (81% vs. 72%), and 
Application Services (82% vs. 76%).  
 
Sapthagiri Chapalapalli, Head of Europe at TCS , said,  “We believe in excellence in our IT services, and we are 
delighted to see this consistently reflected in our European customer satisfaction ranking as we celebrate our 
twelfth year at the top. We are especially proud to have ranked as an ‘excellent performer ’ across every 
service pillar assessed. TCS values the trust placed in us by our customers, and our TCS teams prioritize always 
delivering exceptional customer service.”
