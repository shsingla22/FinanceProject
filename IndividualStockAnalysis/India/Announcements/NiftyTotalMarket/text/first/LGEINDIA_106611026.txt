PRESS RELEASE  
 
LG Electronics India Expands Home Appliances Portfolio: Introduces Premium Range of 
French Door Refrigerators, AI  DD Washing Machines, and the All -New ‘Essential Series’  
• Expands affordable -premium Essential Series range and introduces new premium range 
including French Door Refrigerators, AI DD Washing Machines, Convertible 
Oven and True Steam Dishwashers  
• New launches reinforce Make -in-India to drive both, Make -for-India and Make-India-
Global 
• 2026 export target for Essential Series: to be exported to 22 countries across Asia, 
Middle East and Africa  
Noida, 6th May 2026: LG Electronics India Ltd. (LGE India) today launched its 2026 Home 
Appliances Range. The new range , launched across both, affordable -premium and premium 
segments reinforces the commitment to making trusted LG ’s technology accessible to a wider 
range of Indian households. The new range is meticulously  engineered to address the full spectrum 
of Indian consumer needs — bridging the gap between premium, design -led experiences and 
reliable, accessible technology for Indian households.  
As part of its 2026 range expansion, LGE India has expanded its affordable -premium Essential 
Series, launched in Oct 2025. The range expansion introduces more designs, capacities and price 
points across home appliances, catering to the needs of a larger co nsumer segment, aimed at 
fulfilling LG India’s mission of “Har Ghar Appliances, Har Ghar Happiness,”  
Simultaneously, LGE India also introduced new products in the Premium category , to address 
evolving lifestyle needs across higher price segments. Complementing the Essential Series launch, 
the expanded premium portfolio also introduces new, advanced, design -led innovations, ensuring 
that Indian consumers can access reliable, meaningful t echnology tailored for everyday use across 
multiple price points.  
Highlighting its growing focus on ‘Make -India-Global’, LGE India has also begun exports of 
Essential Series from Q1 -2026. Exports of the Essent ial Series are expected to touch 22 countries 
across Asia, the Middle East and Africa  in 2026. The products being exported include 
Refrigerators, Washing machines and Residential air conditioners. 
 
Commenting on the launch,  Mr. Hong Ju Jeon, Managing Director, LG Electronics India 
Limited, said, "India is a strategic growth market for LG Electronics, and our commitment goes 
beyond scale — it is about delivering technology and features that are meaningful to Indian 
lifestyles. This portfolio expansion reflects that philosophy across every category. With the  
Expansion of both, our recently launched Essential Series and premium appliances range, we are 
raising the bar across multiple price points and aspiration segments to cater to the needs of every 
Indian household. In addition to enhancing our commitment to Make-in-India and Make-for-
India, we have also set an ambitious target of exporting the Essential Series to 22 countries across 

  
Asia, Middle East and Africa, thereby leveraging LG Electronics India’s manufacturing strength 
and enhancing our Make -India-Global vision.” 
 
 
Designed for India: Expanding Essential Series Portfolio to drive household penetration  
  
Refrigerators  
Building on the success of the affordable -premium LG Essential Series range, LGE India is  
now expanding this affordable -premium range by adding new capacities and designs , 
alongside widening its distribution footprint  to reach more Indian households across multiple 
price segments. In refrigeration, the  sub-300L portfolio has been expanded  to include 225L, 
251L and 276L capacities, addressing a wider range of household needs. Powered by  Smart 
Inverter Compressor Technology , the new range delivers energy -efficient and quieter operation, 
while Smart Mode adapts cooling based on usage patterns. Features such as  Convertible with 
Dairy Mode converts freezer section to fridge with dedicated Dairy Mode, Turbo Fresh 
Zone support longer freshness retention, and  Auto Smart Connect  ensures seamless operation on 
home inverters during power interruptions.  
 
The Refrigerators are offered in contemporary finishes including  Shiny Steel, Onyx Black, 
Radiant Inox, Radiant Onyx , Wine Glory, Royal Glory, Scarlet Lotus and Emerald Lotus  
blending modern aesthetics with everyday functionality.  
 
Washing Machines  
The Essential Series washing machine range has also been strengthened with new  top-load 
washing machines in 8kg ~10kg capacities, designed to perform reliably under challenging 
operating conditions.  Low Pressure Fill enables effective washing even with limited water supply 
while Intelligent Auto Restart  ensures uninterrupted operation during power fluctuations. While 
the ProShield motorTM offers added protection against dust and moisture,  Eco Wash and Saree 
Course programmes  cater to daily and traditional laundry needs and  Turbo Spin helps reduce 
drying time. 
LGE India has also strengthened its presence in the  entry segment through the new AX 
series semi-automatic washing machines, designed for performance -driven households seeking 
robust washing solutions. Available in capacities ranging from 8kg to 12kg, the series  
Features Dual Spin Shower  and Roller Jet Pulsator  technologies to deliver powerful washing 
action with efficient water usage. The  Ultra Scrub Wall enhances stain removal through increased 
fabric friction during washing. Built for durability, the machines include  toughened glass lids with 
soft-close functionality, improving safety and ease of use. The AX semi -automatic range includes 
15 SKUs. 
 
 
 
 
  
Cooking Appliances  
In the cooking appliances segment, the Essential Series range has been extended with new  28-litre 
Convertible Convection Microwave Ovens , combining microwave, convection and air -fry 
functions in a single appliance. The  Air Fry mode supports low-oil cooking, while  Charcoal 
Lighting Heater™ technology enhances taste and texture closer to traditional cooking methods. 
The ovens feature over 330 pre-set auto Cook menus , including 28 Indian bread menu , aimed 
at simplifying routine home cooking and everyday meal preparation.

The 2026 Premium Segment Range  
Refrigerators: French Door and Side -by-Side Door Expansion  
LG Electronics India has reinforced its premium refrigerators portfolio with introduction of its 
new French Door Refrigerator range , led by India’s 1st French Door Refrigerator with Auto 
Ice Maker in the 33 -inch (83.82Cms) width segment, offering on-demand ice without requiring 
a plumbing connection. Available in capacities ranging from 574L to 610L, the range features 
InstaView Door-in-Door™, Fresh Converter+, Door Cooling+ ™ and Hygiene Fresh+ ™ to 
support freshness management and food hygiene. Smart Learner ™ enables adaptive cooling based 
on usage patterns, Express Freeze supports rapid cooling, while AI ThinQ ™ Wi-Fi connectivity 
allows smart monitoring and control. The French Door range prices start at ₹1.18 lakh.  
Complementing this, LG E India has also expanded its  Make-in-India Side-by-Side (SBS)  
Refrigerator portfolio , extending capacities up to 790L and is available in finishes such as Black 
Mirror Glass, Shiny Steel and Natural Beige.  
LGE India has also launched its biggest Double Door Refrigerator with 674L capacity offering 
separate compartments for storing fruits and vegetables.  
Premium and Mid -Segment Laundry: Strengthening Fabric Care Portfolio  
In the washing machine segment, LGE India has strengthened its washing machine portfolio 
across premium, mid and entry segments , addressing diverse household needs and usage 
conditions. The company continues to build on its leadership in advanced fabric care while 
expanding options tailored for Indian operating environments such as hard water, humidity and 
high-frequency usage. 
At the premium end,  LG’s NT, VX and GVX  Front-load Washing Machine series , with 
capacities starting at 12kg, are powered by  AI DD™ 2.0 technology, to detect fabric type, load 
weight and soil levels and automatically optimize wash motions. This helps improve cleaning 
performance while reducing fabric wear, water usage and energy consumption. Additional features 
include EZ Dispense for precise deterg ent dosing, Steam+ ™ for wrinkle and hygiene care, and 
specialized Pet Care and Microplastic Care cycles, catering to consumers with advanced hygiene 
and sustainability requirements.  
In the mid segment , LGE India has expanded its portfolio with the  TX series top load washing 
machine, available in 10kg and 11kg capacities, with a 9kg variant to be introduced soon.  
  
Designed for Indian conditions, the machines feature an  IPX4-rated rear panel to protect against 
splashes and humidity. Key features include  Hard Water Wash  for mineral build up in 
water, Stain Clean for tackling tough everyday stains, and  Turbo Spin to reduce drying time. The 
range also incorporates  eZ Mix technology  for uniform detergent distribution and 
a corrosion-resistant stainless-steel pulsator, ensuring durability and consistent performance.  
 
Dishwashers: LG True Steam Dishwasher for Indian Kitchens  
LGE India has also strengthened its 2026 dishwashing category with the  15 Place Setting LG 
True Dishwasher, designed specifically for Indian cooking habits and utensil types. Capable of 
accommodating up to 92* Indian utensils, including large  kadhais and cookware, the dishwasher 
features TrueSteam™ technology for hygienic cleaning and effective grease removal. 
QuadWash™ technology, with four spray arms, ensures powerful, all -angle wash coverage. 
Designed to handle heavy oil and masala residues while using water more efficiently than manual 
washing, the dishwasher is positioned as a convenience -driven solution for urban h ouseholds

Water Purifiers: Hygiene -Focused Solutions  
LGE India has also strengthened its portfolio in the health and hygiene segment with an expanded 
range of water purifiers designed for Indian water conditions. The range features air -tight 
stainless-steel tanks, UV sterilization during storage, and Digital Sterilizing Care for contactless 
maintenance. Additional features include alkaline balanced drinking water with 7+ pH and  High 
Recovery Membrane technology delivering up to 60 per cent water recovery, addressing growing 
consumer focus on safe and hygienic drinking water.

About LG Electronics India Ltd                
LG Electronics India Limited (LGEIL) was established in January 1997 in India. It is focused on various consumer electronics and 
B2B businesses, from home appliances and media entertainment to HVAC and commercial displays. LGEIL's manufacturing units 
at Greater Noida and Ranjangaon, Pune has the capacity to manufacture LED TVs, air conditioners, commercial air conditioning 
systems, washing machines, refrigerators, and monitors.
