Sustained Revenue Growth with Strong Earnings Momentum
▪Consolidated  revenue  grew  by 18% YoY in Q2FY26, led by robust  performance  in the W&C  segment  and  healthy  growth  in 
the FMEG  business,  partially  offset  by a decline  in the EPC business
▪EBITDA  increased  by 62% YoY and  19% QoQ,  supported  by stronger  margins  in the W&C  business  and  a one-off gain  in 
the EPC segment,  resulting  in a sequential  margin  improvement  of ~130 bps to 15.8%.
▪PAT registered  a strong  growth  of 56% YoY,  with  PAT margins  improving  ~260 bps YoY and  ~50 bps QoQ  to 10.7%
