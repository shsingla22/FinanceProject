Notes: 
1. The group has defined its businesses in two verticals (segments) namely: 
a) Plumbing (Pipes, fittings, water tanks, bathware). 
b) Paints and Adhesives (Erstwhile Resinova + Seal It + Astral Coatings Private Limited 
(Gem)). 
 
2. During the quarter, PVC/CPVC Raw Material prices were highly volatile in spite of that 
Company was able to maintain its guidance of 15% plus volume growth. 
 
3. During the quarter ended June 30, 2024 Bathware registered sale of Rs. 262 Million and 
growth of 90% on Y-O-Y basis.   
 
4. Company’s Hyderabad plant has already started trial production and going to start 
commercial production by end of August 2024. 
 
5. As mentioned in our earlier communication Company is planning to enter in new product 
i.e., O-PVC and we are happy to inform that we have placed all the orders of machineries 
and we are expecting our First Machine in October 2024.  
 
6. Company has launched “ASTRAL PAINTS”  in Gujarat & Karnataka in the last week of June 
2024. We are going to open in more states in phased manner. 
 
7. Company has registered 40.55% highest GP % (on consolidated basis) in the decade but 
due to additional recruitment of Manpower for our new plants & new Verticals and opening 
of new geographies, Employee cost has gone up sizably which will help us in coming 
quarters once the volume will pickup in existing and new verticals. Also during the quarter 
Company has spent additional Rs. 20 Cr compare to last year on brand building and 
promotional expenses such as IPL, Exhibitions and trade promotions for dealers, Astral 
Paints Launch, plumbers activities etc. Due to this our EBDITA margin has not gone up in 
the same proportion. 
 
8. Consolidated cash (including cash equivalents) and bank balances as at June 30, 2024 is            
Rs. 5,528 million.  
  
9. The figures for the previous periods have been regrouped / reclassified wherever necessary 
to confirm with the current period’s classification.
