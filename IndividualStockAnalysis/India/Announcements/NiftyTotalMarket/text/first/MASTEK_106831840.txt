Mastek Expands Trichy Centre to Create Future -Ready Technology and Talent Hub  
New center to accommodate 150 –200 professionals, strengthen capabilities across Salesforce, Oracle, Data & AI, and 
build deeper partnerships with local colleges and communities . 
MUMBAI, India, Oct 28, 2026 : Mastek (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted AI -first digital 
engineering and cloud transformation partner, is expanding its presence in Trichy with a future -ready 
office designed to support the company’s growing technology teams while creating a stronger talent 
and innovation ecosystem in the region.  
The expanded Trichy center will have the capacity to accommodate approximately 150 –200 
professionals and bring together teams across key service lines, including Salesforce, Oracle, Data & 
AI, among other digital capabilities. The center  is being envisioned not only as a delivery hub but also 
as a platform for collaboration, learning, innovation and employee engagement.  
Commenting on the expansion, Umang Nahata, CEO of Mastek , said, “Mastek’s Trichy expansion is an 
important step in our strategy to build technology capabilities closer to emerging talent ecosystems. We see 
tremendous potential in the region, particularly as enterprises accelerate their adoption of AI, data and 
cloud -led transformation. The new center will bring together our teams across Salesforce, Oracle and Data & 
AI while creating meaningful opportunities for students and young professionals to engage with real -world 
technology. Through our partnerships with local col leges, industry sessions and hands -on projects, we want 
to contribute to building a future -ready talent pool in Trichy while making the center a space for innovation, 
learning and collaboration. ”  
A key focus of Mastek’s expansion will be to tap into the strong talent pool emerging from colleges 
and educational institutions. The company plans to build deeper relationships with colleges in and 
around Trichy through technology -focused engagement progr ams, including Deep Blue Sessions  for 
final -year students and dedicated AI -specific sessions. These initiatives will provide students with 
exposure to emerging technologies, industry perspectives and real -world problem -solving.  
The expansion will also strengthen Mastek’s commitment to the wider Trichy community. Mastek 
Foundation will coordinate with local NGOs to identify opportunities for community engagement and 
social impact, enabling employees to contribute their skills and time towards initiatives that address 
local needs.  
 
It reflects Mastek’s broader approach of building technology capabilities alongside talent and 
community ecosystems. By combining delivery capabilities in areas such as Salesforce, Oracle, Data & 
AI with structured engagement with educational institutions, e mployee development and community 
initiatives, the Trichy center  is expected to play a growing role in Mastek’s technology and talent 
strategy.
