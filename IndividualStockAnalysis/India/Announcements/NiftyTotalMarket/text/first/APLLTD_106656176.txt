For dematerialisation of shares, you may approach any SEBI registered depository participant (DP) and follow the process given below: 
 Open a demat account (This step is not applicable if you already have a demat account) 
 Once the demat account is opened or if you already have the demat account, provide the following documents to your DP: 
 Share Certificates 
 Demat Request Form 
 Copy of PAN 
 Address proof 
 Cancelled cheque 
 Other KYC documents if not updated. 
 Other documents as may be required by the DP
