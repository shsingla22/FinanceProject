Datamatics Recognised as a Major Contender in Everest 
Group’s Finance and Accounting Services PEAK Matrix® 
Assessment for the Seventh Consecutive Year 
 
16th November 2026, Mumbai, India:  Datamatics, a global Digital Operations, 
Technology, and Experiences company, has been recognised as a Major Contender in 
Everest Group’s Finance and Accounting Services (FAS) PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2026 
for the seventh consecutive year. 
Datamatics combines finance expertise, AI-powered automation, and proprietary 
finance transformation platform - FINATO, to help enterprises transform finance 
operations, improve efficiency, optimise costs, and strengthen financial control. Its 
integrated approach brings together technology and finance professionals to deliver 
measurable business benefits across the finance value chain. 
Key strengths of Datamatics highlighted in the Everest Group FAS PEAK Matrix® 
Assessment 2026 include:  
 Technology-Led Finance Transformation:  End-to-end F&A services across 
accounts payable (AP), accounts receivable (AR), general accounting, taxation, 
and financial planning and analysis (FP&A), supported by FINATO, its finance 
orchestration platform. 
 AI-Powered Finance Operations:  Advancing FINATO’s capabilities towards 
agentic AI orchestration across targeted procure-to-pay (P2P) and order-to-
cash (O2C) use cases, including invoice processing, general ledger coding, 
collections, and cash application. 
 Integrated Technology Ecosystem:  Combining FINATO with proprietary 
solutions such as TruBot, TruBI, TruAI, and TruCap+ to enable intelligent 
automation, data-driven decision-making, and greater process efficiency. 
 Industry-Specific Solutions:  Delivering tailored F&A solutions for industries 
including manufacturing, logistics, energy, utilities, hi-tech, telecom, and 
business services, aligned with sector-specific processes and requirements. 
 Flexible, Value-Driven Engagements:  Offering technology-led transformation 
supported by flexible commercial models, including gainsharing arrangements, 
to align service delivery with business value. 
 
Vignesh Kannan, Vice President, Everest Group, said, “Datamatics has differentiated 
its F&A proposition as a technology-led modular offering, combining managed services 
with productized automation, industry-contextualized solutioning, and flexible 
commercial constructs. FINATO is evolving in a modular agentic orchestration across 
targeted P2P and O2C use cases, enabling buyers to adopt automation and 
transformation capabilities incrementally. Its preconfigured integrations and rapid 
transition methodologies - strengthens its relevance for buyers seeking accessible, value-
oriented finance transformation. This reinforces its position as a Major Contender in 
Everest Group’s 2026 Integrated Finance and Accounting Services (FAS) PEAK Matrix® 
Assessment.” 
Rahul Kanodia, Vice Chairman & CEO, Datamatics, said,  “Our recognition by Everest 
Group for the seventh consecutive year reflects our deep F&A expertise and AI-
powered capabilities. By bringing AI, automation, and finance professionals together, 
we help enterprises enhance efficiency, optimise costs, and strengthen financial control 
while delivering measurable business outcomes.”
