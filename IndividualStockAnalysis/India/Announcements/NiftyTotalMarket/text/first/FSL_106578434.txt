Firstsource Named  a Leader by Everest Group and NelsonHall in 2026 Healthcare Paye r 
Assessments  
Dual recognition highlights platform -led, AI -powered approach to transforming healthcare payer 
operations  
 
Mumbai, India | April  02, 2026: Firstsource Solutions Limited  (NSE:FSL, BSE:532809),  an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company,  has 
been named  a Leader in Everest Group Healthcare Payer Intelligent Operations PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2026  and a Leader in 
the Overall Market Segment of NelsonHall ’s Healthcare Payer Agility & Innovation NEAT Evaluation 2026 .  
 
In the Everest Group Healthcare Payer Intelligent Operations PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2026, Firstsource is one of nine Leaders  
among 33 providers evaluated globally, reflecting market impact, vision, and delivery capability across payer back -office intellig ent 
operations. Key Everest callouts include double -digit revenue growth driven by BPaaS and intelligent operations, along with 
packaged LoBs (Medicare in a Box, Medicaid in a Box), the UnBPO ™ approach, relAI ™ platform capabilities , and a payer -specific 
claims control tower.  
 
NelsonHall’s NEAT Evaluation 2026 assessed 11 Healthcare Payer BPS vendors , recognizing those as Leaders that  deliver 
immediate benefit while meeting future client requirements. NelsonHall highlighted Firstsource’s production -ready AI workflows, 
the relAI ™ platform as the anchor of its digital delivery model, the UnBPO ™ approach, and R&D investments reflected in the 2025 –
26 roadmap. NelsonHall also cites a ~70% reduction in manual effort through AI -generated feedback loops.  
 
Matthew Barlow, Head – Health Plan and Healthcare Services Business, Firstsource  said, "We've moved from traditional 
outsourcing to a platform -led model where AI is embedded into production, not sitting in pilots. Through our UnBPO ™ approach 
and relAI ™ platform, clients are seeing real operational impact — including up to 70% reduction in manual effort through AI -
generated feedback loops. That is what Intelligence  That Operates  means in practice. Being recognized  as a Leader by both Everest 
Group and NelsonHall reflects that progress — toward outcome -led, AI -first operations."   
 
Payers continue to face cost pressure, constrained premium growth, and legacy core fragmentation, while regulatory and qualit y 
standards (CMS interoperability, real -time prior auth, NCQA provider data requirements) increase operational complexity. These 
pressures, combined with persistent staffing constraints, are accelerating the move from labor -heavy BPO to platform -led, 
production AI and BPaaS models.  
 
“Health plans are increasingly demanding transformation that shows up in the metrics – lower leakage, faster cycle times, and 
stronger engagement outcomes  – without adding further operational complexity. This is driving a market shift toward platform -
led, orchestrated models that move generative and agentic AI from pilot initiatives into scaled production . Firstsource is leaning 
into this shift with its UnBPO ™ approach, capitalizing on BPaaS -led growth and packaged LoB offerings, such as Medicare in a Box 
and Medicaid in a Box. Further, its relAI ™ platform – embedding agentic AI for automation, knowledge surfacing, and workflow 
orchestration – has strengthened its AI capabilities, earning it a Leader position in Everest Group’s Healthcare Payer Intelligent 
Operations PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2026 ,” said Vivek Kumar, Practice Director, Everest Group .  
 
“Firstsource was identified as a Leader in the Healthcare Payer Agility & Innovation NEAT for its transition towards an AI -first 
framework and its continued investments in scalable, production -ready automation. This includes embedded AI -powered co -
pilots, a gentic AI workflows, and process mining capabilities, all designed to deliver measurable operational impact ,” said Bilal 
Chaudhry, Principal Analyst, Healthcare & Insurance, NelsonHall.
