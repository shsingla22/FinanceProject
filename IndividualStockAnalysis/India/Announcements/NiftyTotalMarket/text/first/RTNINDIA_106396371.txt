Revolt Motors Strengthens Leadership Team with Appointment of 
Raghava Rao as Chief Business Officer  
 
New Delhi, 3rd October 2025:  Revolt Motors, India’s No.1 electric motorcycle brand  and a wholly 
owned subsidiary of RattanIndia Enterprises Limited , is pleased to announce the appointment of Mr. 
Raghava Rao as Chief Business Officer (CBO) . This appointment marks a significant step in 
strengthening Revolt’s leadership team as the company advances its ambition of becoming the No.1 
electric vehicle company in India.  
Mr. Rao is a seasoned industry leader with over 25 years of diverse experience across the automotive 
sector, spanning two -wheelers, tractors, three -wheelers, and commercial vehicles. His expertise lies in 
channel sales, customer support, customer experienc e, and market insights, with a proven track record 
of driving business growth and customer success.  
Before joining Revolt Motors, Mr. Rao held senior leadership positions at Kinetic Engineering Ltd, Bajaj 
Auto, Tata Motors, Piaggio Vehicles, and TI Clean Mobility (Montra Electric). His rich experience in 
business development, network expansion, and vendo r ecosystem management will be instrumental 
in bringing industry -leading practices to Revolt as the company enters a high -growth phase.  
Commenting on the appointment,  Ms. Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises Limited , 
said, “We are delighted to welcome Raghava Rao to the Revolt family. His deep expertise in the 
automotive industry and proven leadership across multiple business functions will play a crucial role in 
accelerating our growth journey and further strengthening Revo lt’s position as India’s No.1 electric 
motorcycle brand.”  
Mr. Raghava Rao is an alumnus of the Indian School of Business (General Management Program), holds 
a B.Tech degree from BITS Pilani, and a PGCSM from XLRI Jamshedpur.  
With this strategic appointment, Revolt Motors continues its commitment to building a strong 
leadership foundation to drive innovation, scale, and adoption of sustainable mobility solutions across 
India.
