16 December 2025 
 
Mr. B Sairam new Coal India Chief 
 
Mr. B Sairam took over as Chairman-cum-Managing Director of Coal India Limited 
(CIL) effective 15 December 2025. He succeeds Mr. Sanoj Kumar Jha, Additional 
Secretary, Ministry of Coal, who held the interim charge as Chairman, from 1 
November 2025, after the superannuation of Mr. P M Prasad on 31 October. 
 
Mr. Sairam headed Northern Coalfields Limited (NCL) as its CMD, a coal producing 
subsidiary of CIL, prior to assuming the top chair of the public sector Maharatna  coal 
mining behemoth.  
Mr. Sairam said, “Increased coal production and improved quality coal supplies 
remains our core functional area in meeting the energy demand of the country. But 
aligning with the changing business scenario and energy sector dynamics we are also 
actively foraying into solar power, critical mineral acquisitions and coal gasification. 
The company is also committed to sustainable mining practices”.  
A coal mining veteran of three and half decades, Mr. Sairam graduated in mining 
engineering from National Institute of Technology, Raipur. He also holds MBA degree 
in Energy Management from NTPC School of Business.  
His experience spans across coal mine operations, planning, logistics, and regulatory 
affairs. Prior to taking over as CMD, NCL he was Director (Technical) in Central 
Coalfields Limited another coal producing arm of CIL.
