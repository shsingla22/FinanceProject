Ref:     Our letter dated 18th February 2026. 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30(7) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we hereby provide an update that, in respect of the debarment order issued by 
National Highways Authority of India ( NHAI), the Company had filed a writ petition before the 
Hon’ble Delhi High Court challenging the said order. The Hon’ble Court, vide its order dated 20th 
February 2026 has granted interim protection by directing that the debarment order shall remain in 
abeyance till 10th March 2026. 
 
The Company is taking necessary steps to approach the appropriate forum for further relief in the 
matter as per the order of the Hon’ble Court.
