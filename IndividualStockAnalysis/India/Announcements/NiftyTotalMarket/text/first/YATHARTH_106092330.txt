Yatharth Hospital Becomes Uttar Pradesh’s First JCI-Accredited Hospital 
Yatharth Hospital: Uttar Pradesh's First JCI-Accredited Healthcare Provider 
 
Noida, 23rd December 2024 - Yatharth Hospital, Noida Extension, has achieved a historic 
milestone by becoming the first hospital in Uttar Pradesh to earn the prestigious Joint 
Commission International (JCI) accreditation. This landmark achievement underscores Yatharth 
Hospital’s unwavering dedication to delivering world-class, patient-centered, 360-degree 
healthcare in the region. 
 
JCI accreditation is recognized globally as a hallmark of quality and a commitment to patient and 
employee safety, signifying that a healthcare institution adheres to rigorous international 
standards. At Yatharth Hospital, a team of four international surveyors conducted an intensive 
five-day audit, evaluating 282 standards and 1,192 measurable elements across all departments 
— from in-patient departments, including critical areas such as operation theatres, cath lab, 
endoscopy, bronchoscopy, dialysis, and diagnostic units like laboratory, radiology, nuclear 
medicine, and radiation oncology, to all out-patient departments, including the child 
development centre. 
 
"Achieving JCI accreditation is a proud milestone for Yatharth Hospital and a testament to our 
unwavering commitment to delivering world-class healthcare in Uttar Pradesh," said Mr. 
Yatharth Tyagi, Director, Yatharth Group of Hospitals . "This prestigious recognition is not only a 
reflection of the hard work and dedication of our entire team but also an assurance to our patients 
that they are receiving care that meets the highest international standards. We are committed to 
continuously raising the bar in healthcare quality, safety, and patient-centered care, and this 
accreditation strengthens our resolve to set new benchmarks for medical excellence in the 
region." 
 
The JCI accreditation represents the hard work of a year-long process of preparation and 
continuous improvement at Yatharth Hospital. This achievement has significantly enhanced the 
hospital’s protocols, safety measures, and patient care processes, reinforcing its commitment to 
healthcare excellence. As Yatharth Hospital celebrates this accomplishment, it reaffirms its vision 
to not only meet but exceed patient expectations by staying at the forefront of healthcare 
innovation, quality, and patient safety in India. 
 
 
 
 
 About Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Ltd 
Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Limited (Yatharth Hospitals) is one of North India’s 
leading healthcare providers, known for its commitment to delivering high quality medical care 
through a network of state-of-the-art facilities. It operates five super specialty hospitals located 
in North India, i.e., at Noida, Greater Noida, Noida Extension and Faridabad in Delhi NCR, and 
Jhansi-Orchha in Madhya Pradesh. It’s Noida Extension Hospital and Greater Noida is the 8th and 
10th largest private hospital in the Delhi NCR, respectively, in terms of number of beds in Fiscal 
2023. Its total bed capacity stands at 1,605 beds as of June ’24. Yatharth Hospitals has recently 
announced addition of two new hospitals, in Delhi and Faridabad, through strategic acquisitions.
