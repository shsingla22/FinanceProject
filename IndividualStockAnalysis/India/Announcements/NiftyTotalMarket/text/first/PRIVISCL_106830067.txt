Dear Sir/ Madam,
Pursuant to the Orders dated September 7, 2026 passed by the Hon'ble National Company Law
Tribunal, Mumbai Bench ("NCLT"), a meeting of the Equity Shareholders of the Company was held on
Tuesday, October 27, 2026 at 12:30 p.m. (1ST) ("Meeting") at deemed venue for the purpose of
considering, and if thought fit, approving the proposed Scheme of Amalgamation of Privi Fine Sciences
Private Limited, the Transferor Company 1 and Privi Biotechnologies Private Limited, the Transferor
Company 2 with Privi Speciality Chemicals Limited, the Transferee Company ("Scheme") under
sections 230 to 232 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 ("Act") along with the
Companies (Compromises, Arrangements and Amalgamations) Rules, 2016 ("Merger Rules") and
other applicable rules under the Act.
We would like to inform that the business contained in the Notice was transacted and passed by the
Equity Shareholders with requisite majority.
As required under the Para A of Part A to Schedule III of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulation, 2015, we submit herewith proceedings of the Meeting of
the Members of the Company held on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, at 12:30 p.m. through Video
Conference (VC) / Other Audio Visual Means (OAVM) and concluded at 01:13 p.m.

The Chairman welcomed all the Members and informed them that the meeting was being conducted

Board of India (SEBI), and in compliance with the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013,

As the meeting was held through VC/OAVM and there was no physical attendance of Members, the
Members were informed that the requirement of appointing proxies was not applicable.
The Chairman informed that pursuant to the Orders dated September 7, 2026 passed by the Hon'ble
National Company Law Tribunal, Mumbai Bench, the Meeting of the Equity Shareholders of the

2 with Privi Speciality Chemicals Limited, the Transferee Company.
Total 57 members were present for this Meeting. The requisite quorum of members being present, the
meeting was called to order.

F8603 and COP No. 9917) has been appointed as the Scrutinizer for the Meeting to scrutinize the e-
voting during the meeting and the remote e-voting process and to ensure that meeting is conducted
in a fair and transparent manner.

its rationale. With the consent of the Members, the Notice of the Meeting was taken as read.
