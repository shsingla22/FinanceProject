Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing and Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations ”), we would like to inform that the Board of Directors of 
the Company at its meeting held today, i.e., on Oc tober 29, 2026, inter alia,  has approved for acquisition 
of 60% share capital of Galenvs Sciences Inc. (“Galenvs ”), a Canada based company, at a pre -money 
valuation of USD 3.50 million. The Company proposes to invest a total of USD 3.75 million, which 
includes primary infusion of USD 2.75 million and a secondary acquisition of USD 1.00 million (the 
“Proposed Transaction ”).  
 
Galenvs  is a contract research and development organization (CRO/ CDMO) specializing in custom 
oligonucleotide synthesis and functional magnetic -nanoparticle (MNP) technologies for the diagnostics 
and therapeutics markets. It operates a vertically integrated plat form spanning discovery, optimization, 
synthesis, purification, validation and pilot -scale production.  
 
The Proposed Transaction is contemplated by way of:  
  
a. primary subscription to 8,092,857 common shares of the Galenvs at an aggregate consideration of 
USD 2.75 million, equivalent to 44% of the post -issue paid -up equity capital of Galenvs on diluted 
basis; and  
 
b. secondary acquisition of 2,942,857 common shares of Galenvs at an aggregate consideration of USD 
1.00 million from the Founder shareholder, equival ent to 16% of the post -issue paid -up equity capital 
of Galenvs on diluted basis.  
 
Following such acquisition pursuant to (a) and (b) above, the Company shall hold 60% of the common 
share capital of the Galenvs on diluted basis and Galenvs will be a subsid iary company of the Company. 
Further, the Galenvs has one wholly owned subsidiary, namely, Galenvs Solutions Inc, and consequently, 
post-acquisition it will become step -down wholly owned subsidiary of the Company.
