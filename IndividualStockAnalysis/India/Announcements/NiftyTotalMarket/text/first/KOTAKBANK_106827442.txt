Kotak Mahindra Bank Announces Results  
Kotak Mahindra Bank Standalone PAT for Q2FY27 at ₹ 4,212 crore , 
 up 29% YoY and 2 % QoQ  
Consolidated PAT for Q2 FY27 at ₹ 5, 645 crore,  
up 26% YoY and 3 % QoQ  
 
 
Mumbai , 24th October , 2026: The Board of Directors of Kotak Mahindra Bank (“the Bank”) approved the 
unaudited standalone and consolidated results for the quarter  and half -year ended September 30, 202 6, at the 
Board meeting held in Mumbai , today.  
 
Kotak Mahindra Bank standalone results  
 During the quarter, t he Bank mobilised  FCNR(B) deposits aggregating to ~₹ 55,344 crore and provided  loans 
aggregating to  ~₹ 16,11 0 crore through  it’s international branches .  
 
The Bank’s PAT for Q2FY27 increased to ₹ 4,212 crore, up 29% YoY from ₹ 3,253 crore in Q2FY26 (up 2% QoQ 
from ₹ 4,123 crore in Q1FY27).  
 Net Interest Income (NII) for Q2FY27 increased to ₹ 8,459 crore, up 16% YoY from ₹ 7,311 crore in Q2FY26 (up 7% QoQ from ₹ 7,928 crore in Q1FY27).  
 
Net Interest Margin (NIM) for Q2FY27 stood at 4.31% versus 4.54% in Q2FY26 and 4.53% in Q1FY27. The 
moderation in NIM was largely on account of FCNR(B) mobilisation during the quarter.  
 Cost of funds was 4.52% for Q2FY27 (4.70% for Q2FY26 and 4.46% for Q1FY27).   
 Fees and services for Q2FY27 increased to ₹ 2,926 crore, up 21% YoY from ₹ 2,415 crore in Q2FY26 (up 17% 
QoQ from ₹ 2,500 crore in Q1FY27).  
 
Operating expenses for Q2FY27 increased to ₹ 5,27 1 crore, up 14% YoY from ₹ 4,632 crore in Q2FY26 ( up 3% 
QoQ from ₹ 5,135 crore in Q1FY27). Cost to assets improved to 2.40% for Q2FY27 (2.67% for Q2FY26 and 
2.66% for Q1FY27).  
 Operating profit for Q2FY27 increased to ₹ 6,382 crore, up 21% YoY from ₹ 5,268 crore in Q2FY26 (up 4% QoQ from ₹ 6,131 crore in Q1FY27).  
 Provisions for Q2FY27 stood at  ₹ 80 3 crore, down 15% YoY from ₹ 947 crore in Q2FY26 (from ₹ 668 crore in 
Q1FY27). Credit cost (annualised) for Q2FY27 stood at 0.35% (0.79% for Q2FY26 and 0.46% for Q1FY27).  
 
Net Advances increased 25% YoY to ₹ 577,295 crore as at September 30, 2026 from ₹ 462,688 crore as at September 30, 2025. Excluding loans provided against FCNR (B) deposits an d loan portfolio acquired from 
Kotak mahindra Investments Ltd (KMIL) , net advances grew by 19% YoY.   
 
Customer Assets which comprise Advances (incl. IBPC & BRDS) and Credit Substitutes grew to ₹ 626,801 crore 
as at September 30, 2026, up 23% YoY from ₹ 509,399 crore as at September 30, 2025.

Total period -end Deposits grew to ₹ 651,491 crore for Q2FY27, up 23% YoY from ₹ 528,776 crore for Q2FY26.  
Excluding FCNR (B) deposits, total deposits grew by 13% YoY.   
 
Average Total Deposits grew to ₹ 606,234 crore for Q2FY27, up 19% YoY from ₹ 510, 138 crore for Q2FY26.  
Average Current Deposits grew to ₹ 82,026 crore for Q2FY27, up 17% YoY from ₹ 70,220 crore for Q2FY26.  
Average Fixed -rate Savings Deposits grew to ₹ 129,731 crore for Q2FY27, up 14% YoY from ₹ 113,894 crore for 
Q2FY26. Average Term Deposits grew to ₹ 379,765 crore for Q2FY27, up 22% YoY from ₹ 311,889 crore for Q2FY26.  
 
CASA ratio as at September 30, 2026 stood at 38.2% (42.3% as at September 30, 2025), reflecting the increase 
in the term deposit base due to FCNR (B) deposit inflows. Excluding FCNR (B) deposits, CASA ratio as at 
September 30, 2026 stood at 41.8%.  
 
Credit to Deposit ratio as at September 30, 2026 stood at 88.6% (87.5% as at September 30, 2025).  
 
Slippages for Q2FY27 decreased to ₹ 1,230 crore, down 24% YoY from ₹ 1,629 crore in Q2FY26. As at 
September 30, 2026, GNPA was 1.04% and NNPA was 0.24% (GNPA was 1.39% and NNPA was 0.32% as at 
September 30, 2025). As at September 30, 2026, Provision Covera ge Ratio stood at 78% (77% as at September 
30, 2025).  
 
Standalone Return on Assets (ROA) for Q2FY27 (annualised) was 1.92%. Return on Equity (ROE) for Q2FY27 
(annualised) was 11.65%.  
 
Capital Adequacy Ratio of the Bank, as per Basel III, as at September 30, 2026 was 21.9% and CET1 ratio was 
21.4 %. 
 Customers as on September 30, 2026 were 5.1 crore.

About Kotak Mahindra Group  
Established in 1985, Kotak Mahindra Group is one of India's leading financial services conglomerates. In 
February 2003, Kotak Mahindra Finance Ltd. (KMFL), the Group's flagship company, received banking license 
from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), becomin g the first non -banking finance company in India to convert into 
a bank -  Kotak Mahindra Bank Ltd (KMBL).  
 Kotak Mahindra Group (Group) offers a wide range of financial services that encompass every sphere of life. From commercial banking, to stock broking, mutual funds, life and general insurance and investment banking, the Group caters to the diverse financial needs of individuals and the corporate sector. The premise of Kotak 
Mahindra Group’s business model is concentrated India, diversified financial services . The bold vision that 
underscores the Group’s growth is an inclusive one, with a host of products and services designed to address 
the needs of the unbanked and insufficiently banked.  
 Kotak Mahindra Group has a global presence through its subsidiaries in UK, USA, Gulf Region, Singapore and 
Mauritius with offices in London, New York, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Singapore and Mauritius respectively. As on 
30
th Sep,  2026 , Kotak Mahindra Bank Ltd has a national footprint of 2,327 branches and 2, 673 ATMs  (incl. cash 
recyclers) , and branches in GIFT City and DIFC (Dubai).
