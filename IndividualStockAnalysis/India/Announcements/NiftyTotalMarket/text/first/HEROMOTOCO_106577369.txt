HERO MOTOCORP RECORDS 24% DISPATCH GROWTH IN Q4 2026  
 
CLOSE S FY 2026 WITH  10% VOLUME GROWTH AND  6.5 MN DISPAT CHES  
 
Hero MotoCorp , the world ’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters,  reported a strong 
performance in March 2026 with dispatches of 598,198 units  as compared to 549,604 units  during 
the same period last year . Retail performance also remained robust during the month with 542 ,436 * 
VAHAN registrations  indicating  growth  of ~24%.  
*Data from VAHAN except Telangana, as on April 1, 2026.  
 
Demand led by 100 -125cc segment; Double -digit growth in scooter segment  
Strong performance continued  in March the ICE scooter segment  with robust double -digit growth  
of 24%, supported  by a strengthened product portfolio and scale -up of the Glamour and Xtreme 
range  of Motorcycles . Additionally, the d emand senti ment improved in the rural markets aided by 
early festive tailwinds . 
 
VIDA Scales New Heights with highest monthly VAHAN registrations  
VIDA , Hero  MotoCorp’s Emerging Mobility business , continued its upward growth  
trajectory with highe st monthly VAHAN registrations of 21,434 , record ing a ~70%  month -on-
month  growth in sales volume as compared to the previous month .  Vida dispatches recorded full 
year growth of 154% in FY26 . As part of  its partnership with Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR),  VIDA 
unveiled the VX2 Plus - KKR Limited Edition , featuring  a custom helmet in a knight -inspired purple 
and gold theme.  Additionally, the Vida V2  & Vida VX2 reinforced  its leadership in the EV scooter 
segment, emerg ing as the highest -ranked models in the 2026 India Two -Wheeler Initial Quality Study 
by J.D. Power.  
 
Global Business: Close d FY 2026 on a strong note with highest ever dispatches  
The C ompany’s Global Business  recorded highest ever  dispatches with  growth of ~ 16%  in March 
2026 with  45,693 units, compared to  39,518 units  during the same period last year .  The segment 
also recorded an all-time  high  of ~40% dispatch  growth in FY26 . Strong demand for premium 
portfolio  products  across motorcycles and scooters drove grow th in key international markets.  
 
Hero MotoCorp continued to strengthen its brand presence through marquee events and notable 
recognitions. The Company, in partnership with the DP World Tour and the Indian Golf Union, 
successfully hosted the Hero India Open from March 26 –29, 2026 culminati ng in  Alex Fitzpatrick 
clinc hing  his maiden DP World Tour title. Additionally, the Hero MotoSports Team Rally delivered a 
commendable performance at Rally Raid Portugal 2026, securing a strong top -10 finish.
