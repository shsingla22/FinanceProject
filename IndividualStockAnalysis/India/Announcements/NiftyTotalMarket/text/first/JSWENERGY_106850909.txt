JSW Energy’s Installed Capacity Scales to 15.2 GW; ~1.8 GW Added since April 2026  
 
Mumbai, India — November 6, 2026  – JSW Energy Limited ("the Company") has added 1,784 
MW of generation capacity since April 2026, including 1,484 MW of renewable capacity. With 
these additions, the Company’s total installed capacity stands at 15,237 MW.  
The renewable capacity added in FY27 to date comprises 624 MW of solar, 108 MW of wind, 601 
MW of hybrid and 150 MW of hydro projects, along with 300 MW of inorganic thermal capacity 
of Maruti Clean Coal & Power Ltd. 
Renewable energy constitutes ~61% (9,279 MW) of the Company's operational portfolio, 
comprising 3,125 MW of wind, 2,457 MW of solar, 1,916 MW of hybrid, and 1,781 MW of hydro 
capacity. The thermal portfolio stands at 5,958 MW.  
JSW Energy has 15.2 GW of installed generation capacity, 15.3 GW under construction, 2.0 GW 
of group captive opportunities under advanced discussions (out of 4.5 GW of signed MoUs), and 
a development pipeline of 2.4 GW. 
The Company is targeting 30 GW of generation capacity and 40 GWh of energy storage by 2030, 
and carbon neutrality by 2050.

ABOUT JSW ENERGY:  JSW Energy Ltd is one of the leading Private sector power producers in 
India and part of the USD 25 billion JSW Group which has significant presence in sectors such as 
steel, energy, infrastructure, cement, sports among others. JSW Energy Ltd has established its 
presence across the value chains of power sector with diversified assets in power generation, 
and transmission. With strong operations, robust corporate governance and prudent capital 
allocation strategies, JSW Energy continues to deliver sustainable growth, and create value for 
all stakeholders. JSW Energy began commercial operations in 2000, with the commissioning of 
its first 2x130 MW thermal power plants at Vijayanagar, Karnataka. Since then, the company has 
steadily enhanced its power generation capacity from 260 MW to 15.2 GW, ensuring diversity in 
geographic presence, fuel sources and power off-take arrangements. The Company is presently 
constructing various power projects to the tune of 15.3 GW, with a vision to achieve a total power 
generation capacity of 30 GW by 2030. Additionally, the Company is also targeting 40 GWh of 
energy storage by 2030.
