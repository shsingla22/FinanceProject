NLC INDIA ENTERS INTO EXECUTION OF COAL MINE DEVELOPMENT 
AND PRODUCTION AGREEMENT (CMDP A) OF MACHHAKAT A (REVISED) 
COAL MINE IN ODISHA: 
NLC India Limited has emerged as the successful bidder for the Machhakata 
(Revised) Coal Mine in Angul District of Odisha in the 8th round of commercial coal block 
e-auction held recently by the Ministry of Coal, Gol. The Coal Mine Block has a total coal 
reserve of 1.38 Billion Tonnes (approximately) with an opera table peak-rated capacity of 30 
Million Tonnes Per Annum. 
This is the second commercial coal mine block won by NLC India after North 
Dhadu Coal Block (Western Part) and the Company is committed to its capacity addition 
in line with its vision of achieving more than 100 MTP A by 2030 from the present 50 MTP A. 
Coal produced from this coal block will help in addressing the energy security of the 
country indigenously. It also paves way for contemplating alternative means of energy 
production from coal like gasification thus meeting sustainability requirements. 
Nominated Authority, Ministry of Coal and NLC India Limited executed the 
Coal Mine Development and Production Agreement (CMDP A) of Machhakata 
(Revised) Coal Mine (30 MTP A) on 15.07.2024 during the agreement ceremony held at 
Shastri Bhavan, Ministry of Coal, New Delhi. Shri. M. Nagaraju, Addl. Secretary, & 
Nominated Authority, Ministry of Coal, Shri. Prasanna Kumar Motupalli, Chairman & 
Managing Director, NLCIL,Dr. Suresh Chandra Suman, Director (Mines & P&P (AC)), 
NLCIL, Shri. Cheruku Dayanand, CGM, NLCIL and Officials from NLCIL and MoC 
attended the ceremony.
