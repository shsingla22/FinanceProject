TBO Tek Ltd posts a strong  Q2 FY27 performance with continued  
operating leverage driving  organic  margin expansion  - Revenue up 
53% YoY  and Adj. EBITDA at ₹152 Crores, up 47% YoY  
 
Press Release   
 
• The quarter was characterized by robust performance despite continued macro headwinds . 
GTV for the quarter stood at ₹11, 695 Cr, up 3 1% YoY.  
• GTV for the Hotels + Ancillary business reached ₹7,889 Cr, up 39% YoY and GTV for Airlines 
business reached ₹3,807 Cr, up 1 8% YoY  
• Monthly Transacting Buyers (MTBs) for the quarter reached 3 4,529, up 1 3% YoY.  
• The quarter saw a broad -based  growth across India, APAC, Europe,  and North America  with a 
strong recovery in the Middle east business   
• Cash and Cash Equivalents (including bank balances, bank deposits and liquid investments) 
were ₹2,060  Cr as on 30th September 2026 , after a $10 Million prepayment of term debt.  
New Delhi , November  2nd, 202 6: TBO Tek Limited (BSE: 544174 ) (NSE: TBOTEK ), one of the largest  
global travel distribution platform s, announced its unaudited financial results for Q 2’FY27 today.  
Consolidated Financial Performance for Q2 FY2027 [YoY Growth]  
• GTV of ₹11,695 Cr v/s ₹8,901 Cr [+ 31% YoY]  
• Revenue from operations of ₹866 Cr v/s. ₹568 Cr [+ 53% YoY]  
• Gross Profit of ₹561 Cr v/s. ₹363 Cr [+ 54% YoY]  
• Adjusted EBITDA  of ₹152 Cr v/s. ₹104 Cr [+ 47% YoY].  
• PAT of ₹76 Cr v/s. ₹68 Cr [+ 13% YoY].  
 
*All numbers have been rounded off  
 
Key Business Updates  
The Quarter continued to face macro headwinds including geopolitical disruptions in the middle east 
and higher fuel prices, the business continued to deliver on fundamentals with broad based growth, 
expanding agent activity on the platform and continued de livery of operating leverage. The robust 
recovery in our Middle East business , while the underlying market remains impacted likely indicates a 
market share gain for TBO  driven by service differentiation and reliability.  
• For the Hotels + Ancillary Segment, GTV for the Europe  and APAC  markets grew at 36% YoY, 
and 33% YoY, respectively . 
• The India business had another strong quarter with sustained  growth trajectory , with the 
Airline segment  and Hotels + Ancillary segment  reporting  14.5% YoY  growth , and 12.2% YoY 
growth  for the quarter , respectively . 
• Monthly transacting buyers in the organic international business grew 17.4% YoY to a record 
14,449 during the quarter. We view this growth as a leading indicator of the continued 
benefits of last year’s investment in KAM hiring .
