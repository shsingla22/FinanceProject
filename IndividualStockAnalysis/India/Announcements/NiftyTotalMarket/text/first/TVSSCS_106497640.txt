January 14, 2026  
TVS Supply Chain Solutions a ppoints Vikas Chadha as  the Global CEO  
TVS Supply Chain Solutions Limited today announced a structured leadership transition plan 
following the decision of Mr. Ravi Viswanathan, Managing Director, to retire during the course of 
the Financial Year 2026 –27.  
As part of this transition plan, the Board  of Directors has appointed Mr. Vikas Chadha as Global 
Chief Executive Officer, effective from 22nd January 2026. This appointment follows a 
comprehensive succession planning process led by the Board of Directors and its Nomination 
and Remuneration Committee.  
During the transition period, Mr. Ravi Viswanathan will continue to serve as Managing Director 
and will work closely with  Mr. Vikas Chadha to ensure leadership continuity and execution of the 
Company’s strategy.  
The Board  of Directors  intend to complete the leadership transition during the Financial Year 
2026 –27.  
Commenting on the transition, R. Dinesh, Chairman , said , “As the Company continues its strong  
focus on long -term value creation for all stakeholders, succession at the top leadership level and 
the planned transition reﬂect our commitment to continuity . Vikas brings extensive international 
experience, and I look forward to working closely with him. ” 
Commenting on the appointment, Ravi Viswanathan , Managing Director , said,  “I remain fully 
committed to ensuring a smooth and well -coordinated transition and to supporting Vikas as he 
is inducted and prepares for a seamless leadership transition. ” 
On his appointment,  Vikas Chadha , said, “ I am honoured to join TVS Supply Chain Solutions at 
an exciting phase of its journey. TVS SCS has built a strong global platform with deep customer 
partnerships and a talented team. I look forward to working closely with Ravi and the leadership 
team  to drive the next phase of growth. ”  
Mr. Chadha brings over three decades of leadership experience across multinational 
organisations, with a strong track record in strategy execution and managing large, geographically 
diverse operations. He  is an Electronics and Communications Engineer from Delhi College of 
Engineering  and holds an MBA from the Indian School of Business, Hyderabad.
