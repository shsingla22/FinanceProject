EXPANDING DEMAND ACROSS SEGMENTS; SOLID EXECUTION  2/7 Country Managing Director, Sanjeev Sharma’s commentary  on Q3 CY2026  
“We delivered a strong third quarter, with record Q3 orders, revenue growth and a healthy backlog 
reflecting continued customer confidence in ABB’s technology and solutions. Demand remained broad-
based across industries, supported by investments in productivity, energy efficiency and sustainable 
growth.  
 
Despite a dynamic operating environment, we maintained our focus on disciplined execution and 
operational performance. Looking ahead, India’s structural growth drivers in industrialization, 
electrification, digitalization and the energy transition continue to support attractive growth opportunities, positioning us well for the quarters ahead.  I thank our employees for their continued 
commitment and customer focus. ” 
 Outlook  
ABB India retains  strong backlog, which is providing visibility into the coming quarters. Demand trends 
remain supported by investments in data centers, grid modernization, renewable energy integration and 
energy -transition projects, alongside ongoing capex plans across metals, cement, rail , buildings . 
 The Company remains focused on successful backlog execution, implementing continuous efficiency 
measures and disciplined capacity expansions to support a profitable growth momentum . ABB India's 
local manufacturing base, technology leadership and broad market exposure position it to capture 
opportunities linked to electrification, automation and industrial digitalization amid a dynamic demand 
environment.  
 
EXPANDING DEMAND ACROSS SEGMENTS; SOLID EXECUTION  3/7 Orders   
Total orders for the third  quarter CY2 026 were at INR 4 ,733  crore, grew 56% YoY and reached a quarterly 
record level . Electrification led the order growth journey with growth across all segments, Motion 
witnessed growth across select segments especially railways  and service and Automation , while muted, 
clocked an increase in export orders. Key growth segments include data centers , utilities , metals and 
mining, food and beverage, cement and buildings and infrastructure.  
 
Key orders for the quarter included:  
Electrification:  Modular low voltage switchgear panels for hyperscale data center majors, compact 
secondary substations, e- houses for IT services and software major, Air circuit breakers (ACBs), 
moulded- case circuit breakers (MCCBs) for national major in electrical products distribution. ACBs and 
MCCBs for renewable energy projects, indoor breakers and relays for power equipment major  
Motion:  Propulsion equipment  for railways ; high- performance motors  & system drives for metals and 
mining  equipment customer, wind turbine converters  for energy company, high voltage synchronous 
motors for an engines and generation solutions company  
Automation:  SCADA solutions for oil & gas  company ; measurement and analytics for mining; control and 
instrumentation solutions for cement  major
