1. The standalone unaudited statement of financial results for the quarter and half yearended September
30, 2026, have been reviewed by Audit Committee and approved by Board of Directors at its meeting

on the above results in terms of Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligation and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ('the Regulation'), as amended and expressed an unmodified 
conclusion.
2. The standalone unaudited statement of financial results of the Company have been prepared in 
accordance with the recognition and measurement principles laid down in the Indian Accounting 
Standard 34 'Interim Financial Reporting', prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013 
read with relevant rules issued thereunder and other recognised accounting principles generally 
accepted in India and in terms of the Regulation.
3. During the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026, the Company has allotted 22,83,333 
and 27,83,333 fully paid-
a securities premium of Rs. 51 per Equity Share, pursuant to the exercise of stock options by 
employees in accordance with the Company's stock option scheme.
4. On April 7, 2015, the Company acquired the Film and Media Services business from Reliance 
MediaWorks Limited (RMW) under a BTA dated November 19, 2014, for equity consideration of Rs. 
35,000 lakhs, with a conditional obligation to assume debt facilities of Rs. 20,000 lakhs. Subsequently, 
the Company entered into a Loan Agreement dated February 25, 2019, with Raspalfa Services Private 
Limited (RASPL), for Rs. 20,000 lakhs.
Conclusion of IBC Proceedings:
On August 29, 2023, RASPL filed a Section 7 petition under the IBC before the NCLT for Rs. 
35,379.75 lakhs alleging default under the Loan Agreement.
The NCLT admitted the petition and initiated CIRP on May 6, 2026. On May 12, 2026, the NCLAT 
stayed the order and directed a deposit of Rs. 35,379.75 lakhs, placed in a fixed deposit with the 
Registrar, NCLAT.
On July 10, 2026, the NCLAT set aside the NCLT order, approved the Discharge Agreement dated 
July 1, 2026, for Rs. 40,800 lakhs towards full and final settlement, closed the CIRP and released the 
fixed deposit.
The fixed deposit of Rs. 35,379.75 lakhs was released and the balance Rs. 5,420.25 lakhs was paid 
during the quarter ended September 30, 2026, thereby completing the settlement of Rs. 40,800 lakhs.
As at March 31, 2026, the Company had accrued Rs. 35,379.75 lakhs. The additional impact of Rs. 
7,335.63 lakhs (including legal and professional fees and tax-related expenses) was recognised as 
an exceptional item during the half year ended September 30, 2026, comprising Rs. 7,144.05 lakhs 
in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and Rs. 191.58 lakhs in the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
