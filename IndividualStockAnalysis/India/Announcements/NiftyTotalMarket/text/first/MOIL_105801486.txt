In terms of Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and

midnight of 31.03.2024/ 01.0 4.2024  for the month of April 2024.  
 
3. The prices of all  SMGR (Mn -30% & Mn -25%) , Fines  and Chemical  grades have been 
increased by 3% on the prevailing prices since 01.0 3.2024 w.e.f. midnight of 
31.03.2024/ 01.0 4.2024  for the month of April 2024 .
