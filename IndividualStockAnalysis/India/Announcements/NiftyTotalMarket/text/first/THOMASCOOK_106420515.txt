Thomas Cook India and SOTC Travel launch private Van Tours  across 
multiple destinations  
Combines the convenience and affordability of a group tour with the flexibility & comfort of a 
personalized holiday  
 
• The c onvenience and value -pricing of a group tour with flexibility of personalization  
• Leisurely exploration of destinations –  at the customer’s pace 
• Premium private vehicles  
• Expertise of a local, English speaking driver -guide  
• Quality 4-star hotels within the city  limits , with breakfast  
 
Mumbai, October 30,  2025: Data from Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel travel  
services company, and its group company SOTC Travel, highlights that with increasing travel intent, 
and frictionless access via visa free/easy  visa regimes, Indian consumers are actively considering 
holidays – as a year- round opportunity. To maximize  on this potential, the Companies have introduced 
Van Tours  — a convenient  and affordable solution with in- built flexibility, ideal for India’s new age 
travellers . 
 
Thomas Cook India and SOTC Travel’s Van Tours offer an ideal bl end of structure and spontaneity 
— allowing customers  to explore a destination at their own pace with the expertise and support  of an 
English- speaking local driver- guide, ensuring a seamless and enriching experience.   
 
Designed for small intima te groups of 4 to 12 travellers, Thomas Cook & SOTC Travel’s Van Tours 
are ideal for famil y/multi- generational family  units , adhoc groups of friends, women travellers, mini-
reunions, or celebratory getaways such as birthdays, anniversaries  and bachelor/bachelorette trips.  
 
Each itinerary spans an average of 7 d ays, offering an exciting combination of discovery and relaxation 
across a range of  short -haul destinations , including Vietnam,  Japan, South Korea, Oman, Philippines,  
China,  Dubai, Abu Dhabi,  Georgia, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Cambodia and Laos. The Van Tours 
include must -see attractions , along with the comfort of premium private v ehicles and four-star hotel 
stays with breakfast.  
 
Thomas Cook and SOTC Travel’s Van T ours, starting at  just INR 38,000 per person for 7 nights to 
Bali to INR 3 lakhs to Japan (exclusive of airfare and meals) make outbound travel both comfortable 
and value- driven. Additional benefits include unlimited bottled water and soft drinks.  
 
Mr. Rajeev Kale, President & Country Head – Holidays, MICE, Visa, Thomas Cook (India) 
Limited , said , “With Indians embracing frequent and year- round travel, our Van Tours offer a perfect  
solution to the growing demand for convenience, affordability and flexibility . This unique concept of 
premium, chau ffeur- driven vans is  thoughtfully designed for smaller groups of  families/ extended 
families and friends.  In addition to the set itinerary, customers can also personalize their j ourney s: 
start their day a little later, extend time at preferred attractions , add-on local hidden gems and increase 
the length of their trip.  The accompanying chauffeur- guide is a valuable inclusion and our careful 
selection of local  English- speaking  driver- guides,  adds to the reassurance and comfort  of our 
customers .” 
 
                                                                             
Mr. S D Nandakumar, President & Country Head - Holidays & Corporate Tours - SOTC Travel,  
said,  “We are witnessing growing travel intent not only from metros, but also from regional India’s tier 
2 and 3 markets. India’s f amilies, groups  of friends / frolleagues are value seekers  – they want enriching 
experiences at attractive pricing, without compromising on comfort.  To capitalize on this evolving 
consumer trend, our newly launched Van Tours  are perfectly suited for intimate groups of 6 to 12 
travellers – offering comfort and attractive pricing and the handholding of any accompanying driver-
cum-guide. We have carefully selected visa-free/easy visa  short -haul destinations for our Van Tours 
to enhance access and appeal for our customers .”
