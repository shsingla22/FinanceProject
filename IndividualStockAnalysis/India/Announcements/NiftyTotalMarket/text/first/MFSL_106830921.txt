Axis Max Life’s profitability measure VNB increases 21% YoY; Individual Adjusted 
First Year Premium grew 12% YoY in H1 FY’27  
 
First Half Financial Year 2027 (“H1 FY’27”) Highlights:  
 
● Max Financial Services Limited reports 16% growth in consolidated revenue excluding investment income  
● Axis Max Life Insurance Limited’s private market share at 10.1% in H1 FY’27  
● Individual Adjusted First Year Premium at ₹4,368 crores, grew by 12%  
● VNB (measure of profitability) at ₹1,180 crores with a YoY growth of 21.1%  
● New business margin improved by 210 bps to 25.4% in H1 FY’27 and by 150 bps to 27.0% in Q2 FY’27  
● Embedded Value at ₹31,437 crores, grows by 17% with an Operating RoEV of 17.3%  
● Gross Written Premium: ₹ 18,066 crores, up 17% Year -on-Year (YoY)  
● Individual New Business Sum Assured grew by 18%  
● Assets Under Management (AUM) at ₹2.06 lakh crores, as of September 30, 2026; up by 11%  
 
Noida, October 27, 2026: Max Financial Services Limited  has recorded consolidated revenue excluding 
investment income at ₹17,484 crores that grew by 16% YoY  in H1 FY’27. The consolidated revenue including 
investment income stood at ₹26,582 crores up by 17%, while consolidated PAT stood at ₹219 crores in H1 FY’27 
up by 137%.  
 
Axis Max Life Insurance Limited , formerly known as Max Life Insurance Company Limited ( “Axis Max Life” / 
“Company” ), has announced new business growth (Individual Adjusted First Year Premium) of 12% to ₹4,368 
crores with a private market share at 10.1%.  The Value of New Business (VNB) for H1 FY’27  increased by 21% YoY  
to ₹1,180 crores, while the VNB margin expanded by 210 basis points to 25.4%, supported by continued 
momentum in the protection and retirement segments.  
 
The Company strengthened its leadership position in protection, with the pure retail protection business growing 
37% YoY , driven by sustained customer demand and disciplined execution. Retirement continues to represent a 
significant long -term growth opportunity for Axis Max Life. During Q2 FY’27, the Company launched Smart 
Retirement Income with Sustained Earnings Plan (SMA RT RISE) (UIN: 104N160V01 ), an innovative solution 
combining flexible lifelong income with a market linked variable annuity option. Supp orted by strong customer 
acceptance, the Company’s annuity business grew 130% YoY  in Q2 FY’27.  
 
Axis Max Life also delivered robust renewal performance, with renewal premium growing 18% YoY  to ₹11,247 
crore s in H1 FY’27. Consequently, Gross Written Premium increased 17% to ₹18,066 crore s, reflecting the 
strength of the Company’s franchise and customer engagement.  
 
Sumit Madan, Managing Director  and CEO , Axis Max Life , said, “Our H1 FY’27 performance reflects the strength 
of our distribution franchise, disciplined execution, and continued focus on delivering sustainable, customer -
centric growth. We welcome IRDAI’s efforts to evolve the distribution framework in a manner that b alances 
customer interests with industry growth objectives. The proposed emphasis on simpler distribution economics, 
stronger persistency outcomes, better alignment of commissions and expenses, a nd enhanced policyholder 
protection is a step in the right direction. We believe a balanced and consultative approach will further strengthen 
trust in the insurance ecosystem, support innovation and accessibility, and accelerate the sector’s long -term 
growth.”  
 
Amrit Singh, Chief Financial Officer, Axis Max Life, said, “We delivered a strong performance in the first half of 
the fiscal, with Individual Adjusted First Year Premium growing by 12%. Our renewal premium rose 18%, driving 
17% growth in Gross Written Premium. An improved product mix lifted the VNB Margin to 25.4 % and supported 
21% growth in Value of New Business.  Our Embedded Value as of Sep 30, 2026 stood at ₹31,437 crore s, growth of 
17% YoY. Annualized operating RoEV improved by 100 bps to 17.3% in H1 F Y27, reflecting our continued focus on 
delivering profitable and sustainable growth.”

About Max Financial Services Limited  
 
Max Financial Services Limited (MFSL) is part of India’s leading business conglomerate - the Max Group. Focused

About Axis Max Life Insurance Limited (https://www.axismaxlife.com ) 
 
Axis Max Life Insurance Limited (the “Company”), formerly known as Max Life Insurance Company Ltd., is a 
subsidiary of Max Financial Services Limited (“MFSL”) with Axis Bank Limited and its affiliates also being 
shareholders of the Company. Axis Max Life o ffers comprehensive protection and long -term savings life insurance 
solutions through its multi -channel distribution, including agency and third -party partners. The Company has built
