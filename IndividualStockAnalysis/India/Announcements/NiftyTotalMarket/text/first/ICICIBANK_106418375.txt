Subject : Change in domain name of ICICI Bank Limited from ’.com’ to ‘ .bank.in’ 
 
ICICI Bank Limited (the Bank) has changed its website domain from www.icicibank.com  
to www.icici.bank.in  in line with RBI guidelines.  
 
The Bank has taken all the necessary measures to ensure smooth transition of change in 
Bank’s website from old domain to new domain. As part of this transition, all the 
stakeholders visiting www.icicibank.com  will be automatically redirected to 
www.icici.bank.in . 
 
The stakeholders can access the content in compliance of Regulation 46 and other 
applicable provisions of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 and services previously available on the old domain on the new 
domain. 
 
Please take the above information on record.
