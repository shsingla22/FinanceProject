Executive Summary  
Q2’FY27 unfolded against a dynamic geopolitical and macroeconomic backdrop, supported by resilient 
domestic demand, improving consumption and sustained economic activity. The operating environment 
continued to evolve, shaped by changing monetary and liquidity conditions, even as competitive intensity 
across  lending and deposits remained elevated.  
Consumer sentiment remained constructive, supported by  onset  of festive season , while business activity 
stayed healthy across key markets. Regulatory focus remained centred on governance, customer protection, 
responsible lending and operational resilience. Meanwhile, accelerating digital adoption and advancements 
in technology and A I continued to reshape customer engagement and operating model, opening new avenues 
for efficiency, innovation and sustainable growth.  
In this environment, AU Small Finance Bank delivered a strong performance while remain ing focused on 
deepening customer relationships, strengthening its deposit and liability franchise, building technology -led 
capabilities and leveraging AI . The Bank maintained a disciplined approach to growth, profitability and asset 
quality  while  deliveri ng consistent performance across key business parameters, as highlighted below:
