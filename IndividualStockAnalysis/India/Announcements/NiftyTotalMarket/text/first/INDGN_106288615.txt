FAST India  Collaborates with  Indegene to Make Indian  Biotech  Ecosystem  Future -Ready   
 
New Delhi , July 3, 2025 : The Foundation for Advancing Science and Technology (FAST ) India  today 
announced a strategic , long -term  partnership  with Indegene to scale up  India’s biotech  ecosystem . The 
partnership will focus on expanding research and collaboration between academia and the industry, helping  
accelerate  innovation from the ground up . 
 
FAST India, a non -profit organization  (NPO), is committed to positioning India among the world’s top three 
science and technology nations within the next decade.  The NPO ’s key initiatives include the flagship India 
Science Festival, national science competitions, platforms to showcase R&D from India and abroad, and 
research that promotes translational science and effective industry -academia collaboration .  
 
FAST India undertakes nationwide efforts to gauge public interest in science and technology, with a focus 
on students, young professionals, and the broader public. These studies also explore perceptions of 
translational research  and successful industry -academia collaborations. Insights are compiled into research 
reports and case studies that help strengthen India’s science and technology  ecosystem.  
 
“A vibrant science ecosystem is crucial to India’s economic growth and human development ”, said Sheel 
Kapoor, CEO , FAST India . “We are thrilled to join hands with  Indegene on this journey . Their commitment 
to science and healthcare innovation aligns with our vision of enabling science -led development in the 
region . We look forward to working together closely to scale our programs and deepen our impact.”  
 
“We are committed to accelerating biotech  innovation in India , and enabl ing policy modernization,” said 
Manish Gupta, CEO, Indegene . “Our collaboration with FAST India is another meaningful step in this 
journey. Together, we look forward to advancing India’s biotech prowess  and helping build a science -first, 
future -ready innovation ecosystem.”  
 
About FAST India  
 
The Foundation for Advancing Science and Technology (FAST India) , registered as the Center for 
Development and Promotion of Science , is a non -profit institution dedicated to building capacity and 
advancing policy solutions that foster scientific enquiry and research, and facilitate the creation, 
dissemination, and translation of new scientific knowledge. The foundation will work with a variety of 
stakeholders to develop and strengthen the science ecosystem in India to advance scientific research and 
its tra nslation into economic value and social good.

Indegene  Limited (BSE: 544172, NSE: INDGN) is a digital -first, life sciences commercialization
