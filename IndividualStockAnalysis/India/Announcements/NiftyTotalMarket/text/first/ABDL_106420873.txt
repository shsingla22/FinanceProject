ABD named  ‘Company of the Year ’ at Allied Spiritz Conclave & Achievers’ 
Awards 2025 , showcasing Premium Innovation  through ABD Maestro portfolio.  
 
ABD sets the Gold Standard in India’s premium spirits space  by winning ‘Company of the Year’ and Grand 
Gold for  ABD Maestro portfolio’s ARTHAUS , WOODBURNS, PUMORI, and Gold for RUSSIAN STANDARD . 
 
Mumbai , 30th October  2025 : Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD), India’s  third  largest 
spirits compan y, has been named ‘Company of the Year – Domestic’ at the Spiritz Conclave & 
Achievers’ Awards 2025 ; recognizing  its leadership in driving innovation, excellence, and 
premiumisation across the Indian alcohol beverage  landscape.  The recognition underscores 
ABD’s remarkable journey — from building iconic Indian brands to setting new benchmarks in 
the super -premium and luxury spirits segment through its new subsidiary , ABD Maestro Pvt. Ltd  
(ABD Maestro) .  
 
Reflecting India’s growing confidence and craftsmanship in the luxury spirits segment, ABD 
Maestro secured multiple accolades across whisky, gin, vodka, and rum categories. Leading the 
wins were ARTHAUS Blended Malt Scotch Whisky , WOODBURNS Contemporary Indian Malt 
Whisky, PUMORI Small Batch Gin, each winning the competition’s most coveted ‘Grand Gold ’ 
Medal for their exceptional quality . ABD Maestro  also received Gold Medals for SEGREDO ALDEIA 
Café Rum,  RUSSIAN STANDARD V odka  (Original and Platinum) , and Z OYA Special batch Gin. 
 
ABD’s breakout flagship brand , ICONiQ WHITE Whisky, which has been setting records since 
launch,  won in two major categories of the ‘ Fastest Growing Brand of the Year ’ and ‘Best 
Promotion & Activation ’– reflecting ABD’s prowess in building  brands that are contemporary and 
relevant.  
 
o ARTHAUS Blended Malt Scotch Whisky secured a ‘GRAND GOLD’, affirming ABD Maestro’s 
growing stature in the super -premium Scotch segment with expressions that deliver depth, 
complexity, and distinction.  
o WOODBURNS Contemporary Indian Malt Whisky achieved a ‘GRAND GOLD ’ for its bold 
reinterpretation of all-Indian  ingredients .  
o PUMORI Small Batch Gin, crafted in limited batches with rare botanicals from the Himalayas, 
was awarded ‘GRAND GOLD’  in recognition of its quality and being at the forefront of India’s 
flourishing gin renaissance . 
 
 
 
RUSSIAN STANDARD Platinum and Original were  recognized for its unparalleled  smoothness and 
purity.  
 
Speaking on the achievement , Alok Gupta, M anaging Director , ABD , said: “We have been 
keeping a close finger on the pulse of what drives Indian consumers when it comes to spirits, and 
immersive experiences. Being recogni zed as ‘Company of the Year’ is a proud moment that 
reflects our unwavering focus on innovation and our deep understanding of the Indian consumer’s 
evolving tastes. ” 
 
Commenting on its wins in the super -premium and luxury segment s, Bikram Basu, Managing 
Director, ABD Maestro,  said, “ABD Maestro  is curating a portfolio that  is rooted in quality , 
authenticity,  and innovation.  Multiple Grand Gold ’s hono urs across categories are a testament to 
the strength of our expressions  as we build our brands with discerning consumers. ” 
 
The Spiritz Conclave & Achievers’ Awards, now in its 12th  edition , is among India’s most credible 
and transparent industry platforms – celebrating innovation, quality, and leadership across the 
industry . ABD’s wins this year mark a milestone moment — reinforcing its leadership in shaping 
the next chapter of India’s fast evolving spirits’  story.  
 
About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited  
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD) is the largest domestic Spirits company in India, in terms of annual 
sales volumes. ABD has a presence in five main flavors, i.e., whisky, brandy, rum, vodka, and gin, with 
‘millionaire’ brands like Officer’s Ch oice Whisky, Officer’s Choice Blue Whisky, Sterling Reserve Premium 
Whiskies and ICONiQ White Whisky. Currently, its manufacturing network comprises 37 units, of which 9 are 
owned bottling units, 2 owned distilleries, and 26 non -owned manufacturing units.  
 
About ABD Maestro Private Limited  
 
ABD Maestro Private Limited is a new super -premium and luxury spirits brand company and a subsidiary of 
ABD. The ABD Maestro portfolio include Arthaus Blended Malt Scotch Whisky, Zoya Special Batch Gin and its 
flavours, Woodburns Contemporary Indian Malt Whisky, Pumori Small Batch Gin and Pink Gin, Segredo Aldeia 
Café and White Rums. Through its partnership with Roust Corporation, the com pany has introduced Russian 
Standard Vodka to India in three distinct segments: Original, Gold, and Platinum.
