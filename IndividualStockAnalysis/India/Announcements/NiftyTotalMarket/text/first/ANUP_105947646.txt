PRESS RELEASE  
 
The Anup Engineering  Limited bags order valued over  INR 100 Crores.  
 
   
Ahmedabad, August 9 , 2024: The Anup Engineering Limited (ANUP) has bagged two critical orders 
this week valued over INR 100  Crore. About 90% of this is from one of renowned EPC contractor in 
Europe for a critical project in UAE. The scope consists of manufacturing and supplying pressure 
vessels of complex metallurgies  
 
Commenting on occasion, Mr Reginaldo D’souza, Managing Director and CEO of company  said, "We 
are pleased to announce very encouraging new order intake this week in line with our growth plans. 
Winning repeat orders from renowned EPC companies, for such critical projects, for such complex 
equipment’s, and on aggressive timelines, signifies our c apability to serve the most complex 
requirements of our customers. The substantial exports order also emphasises that we are truly a 
global player. I believe this, along with our existing order book and strong order pipeline, provides a 
good visibility and  reinforces our commitment towards achieving substantial growth in FY25 and 
beyond. We are grateful to our esteemed Customers for their trust and considering us as their reliable 
partners for delivering such important projects towards fulfilling the daily  energy  needs”
