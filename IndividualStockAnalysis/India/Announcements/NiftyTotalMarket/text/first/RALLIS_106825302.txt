Rallis India delivers resilient kharif season performance  in Q2 FY27 amidst challenging 
operating environment;  H1 FY27 Revenue  & PAT grew 3% and 6% respectively.  
Mumbai, October 22, 2026:  Rallis India Limited (A TATA Enterprise), a leading player in the Indian agri -
inputs industry, announced its financial results  for the second quarter and half year ended September 30, 
2026.  
Key Highlights: Q2 & H1 FY27  
Announcing the results, Dr. Gyanendra Shukla, Managing Director & CEO, Rallis India Limited, said:  
Rallis navigated a disrupt ive business environment during Q2 FY27  by focussing on portfolio  and 
operational efficiencies .  
During the quarter, the Company recorded revenue of ₹ 858  crore , compared to ₹861 crore in Q2 FY26. 
PAT stood at ₹ 85 crore, compared to ₹102 crore in the corresponding quarter last year, while EBITDA 
stood at ₹ 126  crore, against ₹154 crore in Q2 FY26. The Company’s EBITDA margin stood at 14.7 %, while 
PAT margin was 9.9%. 
For the half year ended September 2026 , Rallis reported revenue of ₹ 1,880  crore, compared to ₹1,818 
crore in H1 FY26. EBITDA stood at ₹ 311 crore, against ₹304 crore in the previous year, while PAT stood at 
₹210  crore, compared to ₹197 crore in H1 FY26. The Company’s EBITDA margin was 16.5 %, while PAT 
margin stood at 11.2 %. 
Business Performance: Q2 FY2 7 
The Crop Care business  recorded topline growth of 4%, primarily driven by 17% growth in the B2B 
segment  with a strong recovery in CSM  portfolio . Domestic Formulations (B2C) remained flat year -on-
year, supported by 16% growth in herbicides , while insecticides registered flat growth. The Soil & Plant 
Health (SPH) business recorded 22% growth , primarily driven by price growth . 
During the quarter, in the Crop C are category, Rallis launched four new products —Silentra  (Insecticide) , 
Marcellus  (Fungicide) , Blitox Super (Fungicide) and Trynage  (Herbicide) . The new products cater to critical 
crop -pest segments like Rice BPH and sheath blight segments, sugarcane herbicide and disease  control 
in key horticulture crop .   
The Seeds business  recorded a decline, primarily due to higher returns from the Kharif season.  
 
Management Comment  
 
Weather -related disruptions and delayed sowing continued to impact market demand during the 
quarter /season . We remain focused on improving market execution, driving cost efficiencies and 
strengthening customer engagement to navigate these challenges and enhance business performance.

About Rallis India:  
 
Rallis India Limited is a subsidiary of Tata Chemicals Limited and a part of over US$ 1 80 billion Tata 
Group. Rallis is one of India’s leading agri science companies, with more than 77 years of experience 
in serving rural markets with  a comprehensive portfolio of products/solutions for Indian farmers. 
Rallis is known for its deep understanding of Indian agriculture, sustained con nect  with farmers, 
quality agrochemicals, branding and marketing expertise along with its strong product portfolio in 
seeds and crop care which is available through a vast distribution network of 7, 200 dealers and over 
95,000 retailers across India. Rallis has marketing alliances with several multinational agrochemical 
companies. Rallis is also known for its manufacturing capabilities and ability to develop new 
processes and formulations ; hence it is considered a preferred partner for contract manufacturing by 
leading global corporations.
