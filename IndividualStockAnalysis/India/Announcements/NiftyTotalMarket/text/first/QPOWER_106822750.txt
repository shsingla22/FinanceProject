Subject: Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 – Commencement of 
Manufacturing Operations at the New Global Coil Factory, Sangli, Maharashtra  
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
We are pleased to inform you that Quality Power Electrical Equipments Limited has commenced 
manufacturing operations at its new Global Coil Factory at Kupwad MIDC, Sangli, Maharashtra, 
with effect from 20 October 2026, coinciding with the auspicious occasion of Vijayadashami 
(Dussehra). 
 
The greenfield facility has been developed as a major manufacturing centre for high-voltage air-
core dry-type reactors and specialised coil products, catering to HVAC, HVDC, FACTS, renewable 
energy integration and other critical power transmission applications. 
 
The facility incorporates advanced coil winding infrastructure, dedicated high-voltage testing 
capabilities and integrated conductor processing facilities, including manufacturing capabilities for 
specialised HVDC winding conductors. The investment is intended to strengthen manufacturing 
integration, improve production efficiency and enhance the Group's ability to execute large and 
technically complex international projects. 
 
The commencement of operations marks a significant milestone in the Company's capacity 
expansion programme and establishes the foundation for its next phase of growth in the global 
high-voltage equipment market. 
 
The facility will progressively ramp up production as additional manufacturing lines and associated 
infrastructure are commissioned, in line with the Company's planned expansion programme. 
 
This development is in continuation of the Company's previously communicated capital 
expenditure and manufacturing capacity expansion initiatives. 
 
The above information is being submitted for your records pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
