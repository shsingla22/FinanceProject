LEMON TREE HOTELS DEBUT S IN AJMER WITH THE OPENING OF  
KEYS LITE BY LEMON TREE HOTELS, AJMER  
 
The opening marks the group’s 14th operational hotel in Rajasthan and strengthens its 
presence in one of India’s leading spiritual and heritage destinations  
 
 
New Delhi, 30th June , 2026:  Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s largest and fastest -
growing mid -scale and premium hotel chain , announce d the opening of Keys Lite by 
Lemon Tree Hotels, Ajmer . This marks the brand's debut in the historic city and takes 
the group's operational portfolio in Rajasthan to 14 hotels, with nine more properties in 
the pipeline across the state.  
 
The hotel is managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
 
Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels, Ajmer will feature a total of 31 well -appointed rooms 
across three categories —Deluxe Room, Executive Room, and Executive Suite —designed 
to deliver a comfortable and value -driven stay experience. The hotel will commence 
operations with 24 rooms, with the remaining seven rooms scheduled to open in the 
second phase.  Dining options include Keys Café, a multi -cuisine all -day dining restaurant 
with seating for 35 guests on the fifth floor, and Terrace Café, a distinctive rooftop di ning 
venue featuring a striking skylight and accommodating up to 45 guests. Unlock Bar 
provides a relaxed setting for evening refreshments and beverages. The property also 
offers a versatile conference room  that can be configured for corporate meetings, 
conferences, and social gatherings . 
 
Commenting on the new opening,  Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, Lemon 
Tree Hotels Ltd ., said, “Destinations like Ajmer are defined by purpose -driven travel, 
where guests value a comfortable and dependable base that supports the purpose of their 
visit. Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels, Ajmer is designed to address this need by offering 
an efficient st ay experience at an accessible price point. This opening strengthens our 
presence in Rajasthan while reinforcing our commitment to serving markets with strong 
and sustained travel deman d with hospitality solutions tailored to the evolving needs of 
today’s travellers.”  
 
Conveniently located opposite the city's main bus terminal, the hotel provides easy access 
to Ajmer's key spiritual and cultural attractions, including the revered Dargah Ajmer Sharif 
(2.5 km), Ana Sagar Lake (1.5 km), and Ajmer Museum (2.3 km). It also serves as an 
ideal base for travellers visiting the Brahma Temple in nearby Pushkar, located just 15.5 
km away. The hotel is approximately 2  km from Ajmer Railway Station and around 24 km 
from Kishangarh Airport.
