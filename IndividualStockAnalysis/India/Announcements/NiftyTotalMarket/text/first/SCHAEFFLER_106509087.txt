Schaeffler India showcases Next – Generation  Technologies  at 
SIAT 2026   
 
 
Pune  | January  28 2026 | Schaeffler  India Limited , a leading motion technology 
company, announces its participation at SIAT 2026, showcasing a portfolio of 
next -generation technologies designed to support India’s rapidly evolving 
automotive landscape at Hall: Zone D, Booths: 341 & 348 . 
Visitors can experience Schaeffler’s comprehensive range of Electrification 
Solutions - Drives, Motors & Modules, Efficient Powertrain solutions  for optimized 
ICE and hybrid technologies,  Innovative Chassis components , Smart Sensors & 
Fuel Delivery modules, Friction reducing b earing  solutions  for Next Generation 
Vehicles .  
Speaking on Schaeffler’s participation and display of advanced solutions, Mr. 
Viswanathan Sambasivam, Division Head - Powertrain & Chassis India , said, 
“SIAT provides an ideal platform to demonstrate how we are shaping mobility for 
India. Our focus is on co -engineering with OEMs, accelerating technology transfer, 
and strengthening local value creation. The solutions we are bringing this year 
represent the next step in advancing clean, safe, and smarter mobility .” 
Mr. Omkar Kulkarni, Division Head - E-Mobility India , added, “ India is one of our 
most important engineering hubs. Our teams here are working closely with 
customers to design for India, build in India, and innovate for India . The 
technologies displayed at SIAT underline our ability to combine global expertise 
with local development capabilities .” 
Schaeffler leadership will also be present at SIAT 2026 . Ms. Madhurisha 
Vippatoori, Chief Technical Officer, Schaeffler India, will serve as Session Chair for 
the Testing & Evaluation track on Day 1 (28 January). Mr. Deepak Kasturi, Head of 
Innovation India – System Engineering & Advanced Development, will del iver a 
keynote address on “Euro 7 Insights: Shaping the Future of Real -Time Emissions 
Monitoring” during the Emission Measurement & Control Technology session on 
Day 3 (30 January).  
Mr. Harsha Kadam, CEO & MD, Schaeffler India  Limited , said, “ As a leading 
mobility solutions provider in India, we at Schaeffler are proud to be associated 
with all the leading automotive customers in shaping the future of mobility. We 
are equally committed to supporting our customers across various types of
