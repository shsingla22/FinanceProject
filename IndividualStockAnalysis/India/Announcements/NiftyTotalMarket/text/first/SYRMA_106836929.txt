PRESS RELEASE 
 
SYRMA SGS TECHNOLOGY LIMITED  
 
Syrma SGS Delivers Highest Ever Quarterly Performance; Revenue at ₹19,995 Mn, EBITDA at ₹2,031 
Mn and PAT at ₹1,242 Mn  
 
Chennai, India – 30th October 2026: Syrma SGS Technology (BSE: SYRMA; 543573 | NSE: INE0DYJ01015), a 
leading player in India’s electronics manufacturing and design ecosystem, announced its financial results for 
the quarter ended on September 30th, 2026. 
 
CONSOLIDATED Q2 FY27 FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS  
 
 
CONSOLIDATED H1 FY27 FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS  
 
 
Commenting on the result and performance, Mr. Jasbir Singh Gujral, Managing Director of Syrma SGS 
Technology Ltd said: “The first half of FY27 reflects the continued strength of our execution, with Total Revenue 
up 71% and PAT nearly doubling. Further, the quarter just gone by marking a new milestone, where the Total 
Revenue reached ₹2,000 Cr, up 73% year-on-year, driven by Automotive, Industrials and strong 80% rise in 
exports. Through industry-wide component constraints, we met our customer commitments while bringing 
working capital back to 63 days and turning operating cash flow strongly positive for half year. 
 
We continue to build on this momentum, backed by a strengthening customer pipeline: expanding our global 
footprint, scaling our niche, technology-intensive segments, and advancing our PCB project as the next pillar of 
growth for Syrma SGS.”

Key Strategic & Corporate update: 
 Inauguration of the new Bengaluru facility of Syrma SGS Elemaster Private Limited, the Company’s joint 
venture with Elemaster S.P.A. Tecnologie Elettroniche, marking an important milestone in expanding its 
manufacturing capabilities and strengthening its presence in high-growth sectors.  
 Syrma SGS Technology’s subsidiary, Syrma Johari MedTech, inaugurated a new medical plastics and 
precision molding facility in Jodhpur to strengthen its MedTech manufacturing capabilities and support 
global medical-device programmes. 
 
About Syrma SGS Technology Limited: 
Syrma SGS Technology (BSE: SYRMA; 543573 | NSE: INE0DYJ01015) is a leading Indian Electronic Systems 
Design and Manufacturing company. With over 45 years of expertise, Syrma SGS has been the preferred 
partner for customers seeking innovative and efficient solutions. The Company serves more than 270 
customers across 20+ countries. It has a pan-India manufacturing footprint with facilities in Chennai, 
Bangalore, Manesar, Gurgaon, Pune, and Baddi, along with R&D centres in Chennai, Pune, Gurgaon, and 
Stuttgart, Germany. 
The Company offers a comprehensive range of services, including Product Design, Assembly (PCBA & Box 
Build), Quick Prototyping, and Tester Development Services, positioning us as a catalyst for growth in the 
industry. 
In addition to EMS, Syrma SGS also provides OEM solutions for RFID tags & inlays, high-frequency magnetic 
components, and electro-mechanicals. The Company’s commitment to excellence is evident in the hundreds 
of millions of units delivered globally in the past decade, and the multi-decadal relationships with several global 
customers.
