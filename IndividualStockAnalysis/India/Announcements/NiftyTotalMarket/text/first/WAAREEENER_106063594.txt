About Waaree Energies  Limited  
Established in 1990, Waaree Energies Limited  (WAAREE ) is India's leading renewable energy company , accelerating the global 
energy transition . Headquartered in Mumbai, we operate state -of-the-art manufacturing facilities  with an installed capacity of 
~13.3GW for solar PV modules  (including 1.3GW of Indosolar) . With presence across  India and 2 5+ countries worldwide, we 
offer innovative solar solutions, including panel manufacturing, EPC services, project development, and rooftop systems. 
Committed to sustainability, Waaree empowers a greener future by delivering cutting -edge, cost -effective energy solutions.
