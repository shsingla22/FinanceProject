About ACME Solar Holdings Limited  
ACME Solar Holdings Limited  is a pure play fully integrated R enewable Energy Company in India 
with a diversified portfolio across solar, wind, hybrid and Firm and Dispatchable Renewable 
Energy (“FDRE”) projects. The company is one of the top 10 renewable energy Independent Power 
Producer in India with an operational capacity of 2,540 MW and under construction capacity of 
4,430 MW. 
The Company generates long term stable cashflows through the sale of electricity to various off -
takers including central and state government -backed entities via long term contracted PPA 
typically for fixed tariff rates for 25 years. The company has in -house EPC and O&M capabilities 
allowing it to control processes, costs and timelines and giving flexibility in the choice of 
technology and suppliers.
