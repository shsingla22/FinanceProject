PRESS RELEASE  
For Immediate Release  
 
JM Financial reported its highest ever quarterly operating PAT. Consolidated  PAT 
of Rs. 454 crore , YoY increase of 166% for Q1FY26 . Net worth has crossed 
Rs.10,000 crore .  
 
Mumbai, August  11, 2025: JM Financial Limited, announced its unaudited 
financial results for the first quarter ended June  30, 202 5.  
 
Key Highlights  
 
➢ Highest ever operating PAT at Rs. 454 crore ( YoY increase of 166%)  
➢ Net worth has crossed Rs.10,000 crore. Book value per share at ~Rs. 106.4  
➢ Net revenue increase of 22% YoY to Rs. 779 crore  
➢ Pre-Provisioning operating profit increase of 22% YoY to Rs. 38 9 crore  
➢ Real estate loan book declined 56% YoY to Rs. 2,063 crore  
➢ Over last 12 months JM Financial Asset Reconstruction Company Limited ’s 
share of recoveries stood at Rs.1,368 crore   
➢ Net reversal of impairment on financial instruments of Rs. 204 crore  
➢ Borrowing has reduced by ~Rs. 4,300 crore in the last one year  
➢ Increased shareholding in JM Financial Credit Solutions (JMFCSL) to 100%  
➢ Bajaj Allianz Life Insurance Company Limited to acquire 2.1% stake in JM 
Financial Home Loans for Rs.  65.5 crore , valuing the business at ~Rs. 3,120 crore  
➢ Strong execution in wealth management expansion  – YoY addition of 13 
branches , sales and wealth RMs headcount has increased by 45% YoY to 947, 
recurring AUM up 37% YoY  to Rs.  31,180 crore  
➢ Mutual Fund AUM increase of 49% YoY  to Rs .13,901 crore , SIP flow of ~Rs. 120 
crore per month  
➢ Affordable H ome Loans AUM increase of 29% YoY to Rs.2,911 crore  
➢ #1 in Equity Capital Markets in Q1FY26 with 10 marquee transactions. Pipeline 
of transactions is very strong , 45 filed IPO transactions aggregating to ~Rs. 
100,000 crore ( further IPOs to be filed would be additional )
