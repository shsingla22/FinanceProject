CONSOLIDATED RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED 30th SEPTEMBER 2026  
 
 
 
Mumbai, 28th October 2026:  Hindustan Unilever Limited announced its results for the quarter ended 30th September 2026.  
Key highlights:  
• Delivered Turnover of ₹ 18,07 1 crores with USG1 of 15%.  Underlying Volume Growth2 was strong at 7%.  
• This marks our highest Underlying Sales G rowth in the last 14 quarters . 
• EBITDA grew 8%  year -on-year  to ₹ 4,089  crores. EBITDA Margin  at 22.6%  was within the guided range.  
• Profit After Tax before exceptional items grew 9%  year -on-year  reflecting our focus on competitive,  volume -led profit 
growth.  Reported Profit A fter Tax at ₹ 2,79 6 crores was  flat due to one -off tax related gains of ₹ 273 crores in exceptional 
items in base period . 
• First half  recorded Turnover of ₹ 35,25 5 crores with  USG of 12% and UVG of 6%.  EBITDA grew by 8% while PAT (bei)3 grew 
by 9%  year -on-year.  
• The Board of Directors declared an interim dividend of ₹ 19 per share  for financial year ending 31st March 2027.
