Sub:  Acquisition of equity stake in TM International Logistics Limited  
 
This is further to our disclosure dated May 15 , 202 6, on the abovementioned subject. The Board 
of Directors of Tata Steel Limited (‘Company’) had approved the acquisition of 23% equity stake in 
TM International Logistics Limited (‘TMILL’) subject to necessary approvals.  
 
This is to inform you that  the Company  on receipt of approval from the Competition Commission 
of India on August 18, 2026 , has today i.e. on August 20, 2026, completed the acquisition of 
41,40,000 equity shares of face value ₹10/ - each  (23% equity stake)  for an aggregate 
consideration of ₹335 crore in TMILL  [a 51:26:23 joint venture company between the Company, 
NYK Holding Europe B.V (‘NYK’) and IQ Martrade Holding Und Management GmbH (‘IQ’) ] from 
IQ as per the terms and conditions of the Share Purchase Agreement executed on May 15, 2026 . 
Post this transaction the Company holds 74% equity stake and NYK holds 26% equity shares in 
TMILL . TMILL has become a subsidiary of the Company . 
 
Consequent to the aforesaid  transaction, the Joint Venture Agreement dated July 26, 2001, 
between the Company and IQ, and the Deed of Adherence dated November 26, 2009,  amongst 
the Company, TMILL, NYK, and IQ stand s terminated  with effect from August 20 , 2026 . 
 
This disclosure is being made by the Company in compliance with Regulations 30 and 51  of the 
Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, as amended.
