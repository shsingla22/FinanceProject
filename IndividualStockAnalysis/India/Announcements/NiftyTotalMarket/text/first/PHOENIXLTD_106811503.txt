Sub: - Company Update  – Reporting of Quarterly Operational Business Update  
 
The Phoenix Mills Limited (“Company”) generally issues a  quarterly operational business update 
shortly after the end of each quarter, providing stakeholders with an early view of key operating 
metrics across its retail, office, hospitality and residential portfolio.  The Company follows this up 
with quar terly financial results within a month of the end of each quarter.  
   
Given the short  interval between the disclosure  of the  operational update and the quarterly results,  
the Company has decided to consolidate the reporting of key operating metrics along with its 
quarterly financial results  itself . This change is intended solely to streamline the reporting and 
provide stakeholders with a consolidated view of the operating and financial performance.  A 
separate quarterly operational business update will therefore not be issued  from Q2 and H1 FY27 .
