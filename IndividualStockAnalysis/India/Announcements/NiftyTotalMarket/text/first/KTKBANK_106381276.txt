Brief  Profile  of M/s. SVJS & Associates , Company Secretaries  
 
M/s. SVJS & Associates , established  in the year 2008 , is a firm  of Practicing 
Company Secretaries committed to excellence in compliance and governance 
practices. Headquartered in Kochi with offices in Bengaluru , Chennai  and 
Mumbai,  the firm  operates with a team of over 50 professionals and is led by five 
experienced partners.  
The firm is ISO 9001: 2015 certified and continuously evolves its service offerings 
to meet the dynamic needs of the business environment. The firm  was hono ured 
with the National Award for Best CS Firm by the Institute of Company 
Secretaries of India (ICSI) in year 2021, under the jury chaired by Hon’ble Mr. 
Justice P. Sathasivam, Former Chief Justice of India. SVJS holds a unique position 
in Kerala’s start -up ecosystem, being the only CS firm empaneled  with Kerala 
Start -up Mission (KSUM) and IIMK LIVE, supporting entrepreneurs through 
entity registration, legal compliance, and investment due diligence. Guided by a 
philosophy of Positive Reciprocity , SVJS blends traditional values with modern 
governance to redefine compliance practices in India.
