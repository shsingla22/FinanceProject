MACRO  ECONOMIC  & OPERATING  CONTEXT  
India remains the fastest -growing major economy. As per IMF’s recent World Economic Outlook, India’s 
growth outlook over the medium term is well supported by resilient domestic demand, favourable 
demographics and sustained investment activity. The RBI has projected FY27 real GDP growth at 7.1%, 
underpinned by healthy consumption and continued momentum in economic activity. Inflationary 
pressures, geopolitical tensions and disruptive climate events, however, remain the key monitorables 
particularly relevant for the hospitality sector.  
Global travel demand witnessed moderation during the period, with growth in international tourist 
arrivals slowing amid geopolitical uncertainties and a softer global economic environment.  Domestic air 
passengers traffic declined by 6% in Aug -Sept’26 due to rising fuel costs and reduction in capacity  by 
Airlines .  
Against this backdrop, India's hospitality industry continued to demonstrate resilience, supported by 
strong domestic demand, favourable supply -demand dynamics and improving connectivity across the

PERFORMANCE HIGHLIGHTS - CONSOLIDATED  
The Company registered a milestone second quarter, with consolidated income crossing ₹ 1,100 cr, 
underpinned  by robust growth across Rooms, Food & Beverage and Management Fees.  
➢ Consolidated Revenue from Operations (ex -Branded Residences) grew 17% YoY to ₹ 983 cr.  
• Room Revenue grew 22% YoY , driven by growth in both rates and volumes. Performance was 
supported by healthy transient demand across key markets and buoyancy in MICE (Meetings, 
Incentives, Conferences and Exhibitions) business, aided by the BRICS Summit in New Delhi.  
▪ Occupancy2 rose 481 bps to 76% and ADRs grew 14%,  resulting in overall RevPAR growth of 
21% YoY.  
▪ The Company maintained a RevPAR premium of 4 4% over the industry3, reflecting the 
strength of its brands and sustained guest preference.  
• Food & Beverage (F&B) revenue grew 12% YoY,  led by Speciality  restaurants, lounges & bars and 
banqueting. As the exclusive food & beverage service provider, the Company served  Heads of 
State, Ministers and global delegates  during the prestigious BRICS India 2026 Summit in New 
Delhi , reinforcing its leadership in marquee global  events.  The F&B portfolio continued to earn 
recognition for culinary excellence, winning multiple honors  at the Travel + Leisure Delicious 
Dining Awards 2026, including Best Family Restaurant (Bukhara, ITC Maurya), Best South Indian 
Restaurant (Avartana, ITC Grand Chola) and Best Sustainable Kitchen (Royal Vega, ITC Royal 
Bengal). Further reinforcing its lea dership in India's fine -dining landscape, Avartana, ITC Grand 
Chola, was recognised among Tatler's Best 20 Restaurants in India 2026.  
• Management and Operating Fees grew 25% YoY , reflecting the continued scale -up and 
stabilisation of the managed portfolio.  
➢ EBITDA margin (ex -Branded Residences) expanded by 263 bps to 32% , with EBITDA growing by 28% 
to ₹ 314 cr. Margin expansion reflects strong operating leverage and sustained cost efficiencies.  
➢ ITC Ratnadipa4 continued to register strong revenue growth, with Q2 RevPAR at 1.8x over FY25  levels 
while sustaining its leadership position in RevPAR. The hotel delivered positive EBITDA during the 
quarter, and for the half year. Progressive handover of Sapphire Residences also continued during 
the quarter.
