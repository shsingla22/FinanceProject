Item No:1
Appointment of Dr. R Raghuttama Rao (DIN:00146230), as a Non-Executive Independent Director of the Company.
The Board of Directors of the Company at their meeting held on 11th November 2026, based on the recommendations 
of the Nomination & Remuneration Committee, have co-opted Dr R Raghuttama Rao (DIN:00146230) as an Additional 
Director to function as a Non-Executive Independent Director in terms of Sections 149, 150, 152, 161 and other 
applicable provisions of the Companies Act,2013, the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules, 
2014 read with Schedule IV of the Companies Act, 2013, and Reg. 16 (1) (b) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, to hold office for a term of 5 (five) consecutive years with effect from 
11th November 2026.
Notice has been received from a member of the Company under section 160 of the Companies Act 2013, proposing 
the appointment of Dr R Raghuttama Rao as a Non-Executive Independent Director of the Company . 
In the opinion of the Board, Dr. R Raghuttama Rao fulfils the conditions prescribed in the Companies Act 2013 
and rules made there under and applicable provisions of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations 2015 for his appointment as a Non-Executive Independent Director of the Company and is Independent 
of the Management.
The Board of Directors considers that Dr. R Raghuttama Rao’s knowledge, experience and expertise would be of 
immense benefit and he fulfills the skill and capabilities required for the role of an Independent Director and accordingly, 
the Board recommends the special resolution relating to the appointment Dr. R Raghuttama Rao, (DIN:00146230) 
as a Non-Executive Independent Director for approval by the shareholders of the Company. The approval of the 
shareholders is obtained in terms of Regulation 17(1C) read with Reg. 25(2A) of the LODR Regulations of the SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations 2015. The details of his profile are furnished under 
Particulars of Directors annexed to the Notice. 
INTEREST OF DIRECTORS AND KEY MANAGERIAL PERSONNEL 
Except, Dr. R Raghuttama Rao, Director and his relatives, none of the Directors and Key Managerial Personnel of 
the Company and their relatives is concerned or interested in the resolution.
