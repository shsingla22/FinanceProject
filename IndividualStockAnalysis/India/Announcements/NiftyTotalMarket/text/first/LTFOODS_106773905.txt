 c.  Types of customers
  LT Foods is a global consumer food company specialising in Basmati and other specialty rice, along with 
a range of convenience and health-focused products. The Company’s operations are organised into 
three key segments:
 1.  Consumer (B2C): Markets flagship Basmati and other specialty rice brands such as DAAWAT® and Heritage for 
retail consumers.
 2.  Institutional (B2B): Serves the HORECA (Hotel, Restaurant, and Catering) segment through its 
brand Chef’s Secret.
 3.  Convenience: Offers health and convenience-focused products, including DAAWAT® Cuppa Rice and DAAWAT® 
Biryani Kit, catering to evolving consumer lifestyles.
