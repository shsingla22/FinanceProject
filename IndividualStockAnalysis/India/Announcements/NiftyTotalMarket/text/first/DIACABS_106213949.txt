ABOUT DIAMOND POWER INFRASTRUCTURE LIMITED: 
About Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (DPIL) is India’s One of the Largest single location Cables and 
Conductors Manufacturers of India with a consolidated turnover of INR 343 cr.(FY 2023-24) DPIL is India’s 
Single Largest Single Location Power Cables and Conductors manufacturer having 5 CCV lines and an Installed 
capacity of 2,50,000 MT p.a. of Conductors, the company is at the forefront of providing Robust, innovative, 
energy efficient products to a diverse set of customers. 
DPIL was acquired through the NCLT process by GSEC-Monarch Group, DPIL’s business operations span across 
India with a large Manufacturing plant at Vadodara admeasuring 110 acres, the company is in phases re- 
commissioning its production facility and expected to complete the process by September, 2025. DPIL has 
also served customers in 12+ countries globally. DPIL’s 1000+ employees including workers are dedicated to 
upholding robust governance practices, preserving a customer centric culture, having a purpose to serve the 
communities, and imbibing a genuine sense of ecological consciousness, company’s products are certified 
for EPD and Product Lifecycle.
