Commenting on the Business Outlook, Ms. Suprita Reddy, MD & CEO said   
“I’m pleased to start on a positive note, as we delivered a strong year-on-year revenue growth of approximately 
22.8%, supported by volume growth of nearly 16.5% in Q1 FY 27. Revenues also surpassed Q4 FY26 in absolute terms, 
which is notable given that Q1 is typically moderate season for the diagnostic Industry. 
Coming to PH, we delivered year-on-year growth of approximately 18%, primarily driven by network expansion.  
In April, we commissioned a hub centre in Gachibowli, Hyderabad with a 160-slice cardiac CT. 
In July, we commissioned our flagship hub centre in Bengaluru at JP Nagar, with advanced imaging technologies, 
including a digital PET-CT with an in-built cardiac CT and a wide bore of 75cm 3T Omega MRI.

This wide Bore 3T Omega MRI is first of its kind in Karnataka, offering significantly faster scan times while delivering 
enhanced patient comfort and a superior imaging experience. 
During Q1 FY27, we further expanded our network with the successful commissioning of 4 spokes across Hyderabad, 
AP and Pune. 
As we expand our footprint across our Core Geographies, West Bengal, Karnataka and Pune, we remain focused on 
building a stronger technical and mid-to-senior leadership team to enable disciplined execution and sustainable 
scaling of the business. 
 
About Us: 
Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Limited (Vijaya), is one of India’s largest integrated diagnostic chains, providing one-stop 
solution for pathology and radiology investigations through an extensive operational network spanning 160+ 
diagnostic centres and 27 reference laboratories in 27 cities and towns spread across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, 
Maharashtra, Karnataka, NCR and West Bengal. The company offers comprehensive diagnostic services ranging from 
basic pathology to high-end radiology.
