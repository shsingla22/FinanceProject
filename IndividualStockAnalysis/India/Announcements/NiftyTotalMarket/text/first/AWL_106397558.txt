Quarterly Update on Business (Q2 FY’26) 
This document provides a preliminary upda te on the standalone performance, high lighting the key trends and operational 
developments during the quarter that ended on September 30, 2025. This will be later followed by a detailed disclosure 
of financial results and earnings presentation once the Board approves the financial results for the quarter. 
Consumer demand was softer than anticipated over  the fiscal year; however, we saw sequential 
gains during the quarter and remain optimistic about continued momentum moving forward. 
In Q2, the Company recorded 5% YoY volume growth and 24% YoY revenue increase, driven 
mainly by edible oils and the Industry Esse ntial segment. Most Food & FMCG products 
demonstrated solid volume gains; however, the segment’s overall growth was held back by a sharp 
decline in non-branded Rice exports, one-off G2G Rice sales in the prior year and weak consumer 
demand for wheat flour.  
Basmati Rice, Pulses & Besan, Sugar and Poha experienced strong double-digit volume growth. 
Quick commerce sales maintained strong moment um, delivering 86% YoY volume growth in Q2 
and overall revenue from alternate channels (MT + Ecom) surpassed INR 4,400 crores over the last 
twelve months. According to internal estimates, ou r market share in e-commerce (including quick 
commerce) stands at ~50% in Soya oil, ~40% in Mu stard oil, ~30% in besan (gram flour), ~25% in 
Sunflower oil and in the low teens for wheat flour - reflecting strong consumer preference for the 
‘Fortune’ brand. 
In the General Trade channel, the Company re mained focused on enhancing throughput by 
boosting salesman efficiency and integrating techn ology. Aiming to enhance rural distribution, we 
are rolling out micro fulfillment centers that enable direct deliveries to small distributors within 4 to 
5 hours, reducing the need for sub-distributors. 
Branded edible oils & foods exports continued to grow well and registered volume & value growth 
of around 40% YoY on LTM basis. 
Sustainability 
To mark National Nutrition Month, Fortune launched ‘Har Ghar SuPoshan’, a campaign leveraging 
influencers to raise awareness about malnutrition by sharing healthy recipes and promoting simple, 
nutritious meals in Indian households.

The Company has partnered with the Solvent Extrac tors' Association of India (SEA) and Solidaridad 
Regional Expertise Centre (SREC) to advance the Regenerative Mustard Mission – Sustainable 
Mustard Farm Project. This project aims to es tablish 3,000 mustard model farms in 9 districts 
across the Country to promote regenerative agriculture practices among 20,000 farmers, 
ultimately increasing productivity and income and to some extent reducing India's reliance on 
imported edible oils. 
Edible Oil 
Edible oils saw a volume increase of nearly 4% YoY. Branded products volume also achieved low 
single-digit growth, affected by low-single digit decline in Palm oil and Mustard oil sales.  
In Q2, imports from certain SAARC countries under the free trade agreement declined following 
customs duty reductions, supporting sales growth. Palm oil sales improved after two weak 
quarters, as a result of spread with Soya oil moving towards reasonable levels, though not yet fully 
normalized. However, in the Mustard category, despite our market leadership and strong growth 
momentum, we faced challenges as its price surged sharply relative to Soya oil, prompting 
consumers to shift their preference toward Soya oil. 
In response to the grammage war in Soya oil, we  introduced a 750ml pack size late in the quarter 
and have seen encouraging sales traction.  
The Company strengthened its presence in under-indexed markets with targeted, insight-driven 
campaigns to boost consumer awareness. In South India, we launched new messaging highlighting 
‘17% less oil absorption’ for our Sunflower oil. On World Heart Day, a new TVC for Fortune Rice 
Bran Oil showcased Oryzanol’s cholesterol-lowering benefits, positioning it as the heart-healthy 
choice. 
Food & FMCG 
The segment recorded a strong 20% QoQ volume growth (Q2 vs Q1). Excluding G2G business, it 
posted its highest-ever quarterly revenue and best-ever monthly performance in September. 
On a YoY basis, Q2 Food & FMCG volumes (excluding G2G) rose 1%, driven by double-digit gains 
in Basmati Rice, Pulses & Besan, Sugar, and Poha. However, declines in non-branded Rice exports, 
branded regional rice consolidation and weak wheat flour demand weighed on volume growth. 
Revenue was also affected by softening prices in Pulses & Besan and Soya nuggets. 
Wheat flour revenue grew mid-single digits, with market share gains over the past 12 months 
(MAT). Our priorities remain expanding distri bution and growing our value-added portfolio. 
Moreover, Fortune Atta bundling initiatives led to 3 million+ trials during the quarter across key 
staples and condiments categories.

About AWL Agri Business Ltd. (formerly known as Adani Wilmar Ltd.) 
AWL Agri Business Ltd. (formerly Adani Wilmar Limited) is one of India’s largest Food & FMCG 
companies, offering a diverse portfolio of essential kitchen staples, including edible oils, wheat flour, 
rice, pulses, and sugar. Its flagship brand, Fort une, commands trust of more than 123 million 
households, reaching to every 1 in 3 Indian families . With 24 manufacturing facilities across 11 states, 
including India’s largest single-location refinery  in Mundra (5,000 tons per day capacity), AWL 
ensures seamless production and distribution. Its extensive supply chain, supported by 98 stock 
points, over 10,000 distributors and sub-distribu tors, along with a retail network of 2.1 million 
outlets, guarantees widespread accessibility across urban and rural India. 
Beyond serving retail consumers, AWL also caters th e HoReCa and institutional sectors, with staple 
food products, specialized bakery and Lauric fats, Castor Oil derivatives, Oleochemicals, and value-
added Soya products. Its advanced castor oil facilities produce pharmaceutical-grade, and low-
moisture castor oil, catering to global markets. Additionally, AWL has expanded into the Home & 
Personal Care (HPC) segment, offering soaps, handwashes, and multipurpose cleaners, further 
strengthening its product offerings.  For more information, please visit Website - www.awl.in
