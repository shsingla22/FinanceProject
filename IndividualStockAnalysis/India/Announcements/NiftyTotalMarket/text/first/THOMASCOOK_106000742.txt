September 26, 2024: Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India's leading omnichannel travel services company, and 
its Group Company, SOTC Travel have partnered with Cricket Australia for the highly anticipated 
Border -Gavaskar Trophy 2024 / 25. On the back of the collaboration, the Companies’ have curated 
exceptional products offering cricket aficionados an unforgettable travel experience centered around the 5 
thrilling Test matches from November 22, 2024 -  January 7, 2025. The matches include the covet ed Boxing 
Day fixture at the hallowed MCG (Melbourne Cricket Ground), the iconic Gabba at Brisbane and Sydney 
Cricket Ground.  
Thomas Cook India and SOTC Travel have been granted the right to operate as Licensed Travel Operators 
for the 2024 -25 Internationa l Summer of Cricket fixture which includes the Border -Gavaskar Trophy 
2024- 25. The agreement covers product development, marketing and promotions, booking services for 
travel programs that incorporate match tickets and ancillary services. The Companies’ in tend to target a 
wide range of segments including Leisure, B -Leisure and MICE.  
For Indians, Cricket is akin to a religion. Hence the upcoming Border -Gavaskar series offers a unique 
opportunity, not just to cricket fans, but also groups of friends, multi -generational families, couples, as well 
as India Inc.’s powerful MICE segment. The fierce rivalry between the two great cricketing nations has 
already seen a 7x surge in interest for the quarter compared to the previous with respect to online 
searches for th e Border Gavaskar Trophy.  
To maximize on the partnership, Thomas Cook and SOTC Travel have curated a range of attractive group 
and customized tours around the 5 Border -Gavaskar Test matches including top destinations such as 
Sydney, Cairns, Gold Coast, Per th, Melbourne and Adelaide. The Thomas Cook and SOTC ready -to-book 
holidays range from 4 -10 nights with the option of flexible add- ons. The portfolio enables customers to 
immerse themselves in both thrilling cricketing action and experience Australia's stu nning landscapes, 
vineyard tours and gastronomy, private yachting, 360 degree tours by helicopter, scenic self -drive tours 
and vibrant cosmopolitan culture and nightlife.  
In addition to the special Border -Gavaskar products, customers who have booked Thomas  Cook and SOTC 
tours to Australia can also include tickets to the 5 Test matches.  
Rajeev Kale - President & Country Head, Holidays, MICE, Visa - Thomas Cook (India) Limited  said, “We 
are delighted to announce our partnership with Cricket Australia for the exciting Border -Gavaskar Trophy 
2024 / 25. Building on our successful track record of operating group tours and customized holidays for 
major global cricketing events and the rising interest in sports tourism over the last few years, we’ve 
crafted an excepti onal Border -Gavaskar portfolio to leverage this demand. This includes not only the 5 Test 
matches but also unique Australian experiences like sand boarding, sunset kayaking under the Sydney 
Harbor Bridge, snorkeling in the Great Barrier Reef, vineyard tour s, dolphin watching on the Moonshadow 
Cruise, stargazing, Michelin -star dining in Melbourne, and scenic drives along the Great Ocean Road and 
South West Edge.”  
 
S.D. Nandakumar, President and Country Head - Holidays and Corporate Tours, SOTC Travel Limited,  said, 
"We are honoured to have Cricket Australia select SOTC Travel as a Licensed Travel Op erator for the 
Border -Gavaskar T est series. As we celebrate 75 years of delivering exceptional travel services, this
