     MOIL Achieves Best -Ever October Production Since Inception  
 
 
MOIL has recorded production of 1.60 lakh tonnes of Manganese ore in October, 2025 , 
which is its best ever October month production  since inception, higher by 9 .1% in 
comparison to corresponding period last year (CPLY). During first seven months of 
FY’26, the company has recorded production of 11.04 lakh tonnes, which is higher by 
8.5% over CPLY.  
   
Contin uing its strong focus on exploration, MOIL has achieved its highest -ever 
exploratory core drilling of 57,275 meters  during the period April–October 2025 . 
Commenting on the performance, Shri Ajit Kumar Saxena, CMD, MOIL , stated,  
“It is encouraging that the upward trend in production during the first seven months 
has been maintained. The MOIL team is fully geared up to register yet another 
impressive performance in the current year.”
