Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that KEI Industries Limited has been awarded the 
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification for its Information Security Management System (ISMS) by

certified ISMS comprises the management, operation and protection of information assets, IT 
infrastructure, enterprise applications and supporting business processes, ensuring

activities.
