Proximus Global achieves ‘Visionary’ status in Gartner Magic Quadrant for 
CPaaS    
M
arking its debut as a newly formed  entity, Proximus Global enters the Magic 
Quadrant as a Visionary,  reflecting its global scale and complete strategic vision 
across CPaaS.  
B
russels – 25 July  2025 – Proximus Global, the leading digital communications company formed by the 
combined strengths of BICS, Telesign, and Route Mobile, has proudly achieved ‘Visionary’ status in Gartner ’s 
Magic Quadrant in the Communications Platform as a Service (CPaaS) category.  
Es
tablished on January 1, 2025 , this marks the first tim e Proximus Global  has featured in the Magic Quadrant. 
Achieving Visionary status right fr om the start underscores the strategic value of uniting BICS,  Telesign, and  
Route Mobile. Gartner defines ‘Visionaries’ as businesses that not only understand the direct ion of the market 
but also actively shape its future . This recognition highlights Proximus Global’s effort s to future -proof its CPaaS 
offering  through a unified go -to-market strategy, a steadfast focus on security  and fraud prevention, and  the 
unmatched  global scale of its network backbone. Staying ahead of market demands, Proximus Global is 
expanding its global RCS coverage to cater to the  growing demand for rich, app -like, interactive  messaging . 
Furthermore , the company is enhancing its CPa aS stack with Network APIs, enabling developers to access telco -
grade capabilities such as location services, Quality of Service (QoS), and device information.

P
roximus Global’s CPaaS offering brings together the strengths of Route Mobile, Telesign, and BICS. Route 
Mobile has extensive reach across emerging markets in APAC and EMEA, along with conversational AI tools for 
customer engagement. Telesign contributes network APIs that support phone number identification, 
transactional risk scoring, and omnichannel verification. Finally, BICS brings travel eSIM capabilities and extensive global telecom infrastructure, including a large SS7 network that supports reliable and scalable CPaaS 
delivery.
