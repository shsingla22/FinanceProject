Star Health Insurance Launches Doc Kendra in Tiruppur , Tamil Nadu  to Bring Healthcare Closer to 
Communities  
 Doc Kendra offers primary health care services in Star Health branches acting as a gateway to 
advanced care  
 Managed by doctor and paramedic  with access to a general physician and specialists via 
telemedicine , the clinics ensure accurate advice and treatment for patients  
 Open to the local community from Monday to Saturday, providing essential healthcare services 
free of charg e 
Tirup pur, Tamil Nadu, 16 April 2025 : Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited (Star Health 
Insurance), India's largest retail health insurance company, is p roud to announce the launch of Doc 
Kendra, an  initiative aimed at transforming healthcare access in Tiruppur , Tamil Nadu . The launch will be 
graced by the presence of the Chief Guest , Thiru. T Christuraj, I.A.S, Distric t Collector, Tiruppur District 
and Thiru.  MKM.R.  Balasubramaniam – Deputy Mayor, Tiruppur City Municipal Corporation .  
Balaji Babu, Executive Presid ent, Star Health Insurance , said, “Our Doc Kendra initiative is a step towards 
making healthcare more accessible and inclusive for everyone. By utilizing our branch network, we aim to 
complement the existing healthcare facilities and provide an additional layer of support to communities. 
Our initiative aims to offer people a convenient and dependable first point of contact for their health 
need s while ensuring they are guided toward the right care when needed. We believe that being part of 
a community means  actively contributing to its well -being and through this effort, we strive to make a 
meaningful difference in the lives of those we serve. ” 
Speaking  on the inauguration , the Chief Guest, Thiru. T Christuraj, I.A.S, District Collector, Tiruppur 
District an d Thiru. MKM.R. Balasubramaniam – Deputy Mayor, Tiruppur City Municipal Corporation , 
said, “ The Doc Kendra initiative by Star Health Insurance represents an innovative approach to addressing 
healthcare accessibility challenges in our community. This program  will help bridge critical gaps in our 
healthcare infrastructure and provide our citizens with convenient access to basic medical services. I 
believe such initiatives are essential for strengthening our healthcare ecosystem and ensuring that quality 
health care reaches all segments of our society. The Tiruppur City Corporation , Tamil Nadu  welcomes this 
initiative and looks forward to its positive impact on the health and well -being of our residents."  
As part of the Doc Kendra initiative, Star Health branches in Tiruppur , Tamil Nadu  will feature a dedicated 
clinic zone with a consultation area, managed by a doctor and a trained paramedic during branch working 
hours from Monday to Saturday. The clinic wi ll offer primary care s ervices and provide access to a  general 
physician for consultation and diagnosis. The paramedic will check basic health indicators and supporting 
the doctor in providing accurate consultation and care. Specialist doctors will also be  available through 
telemedicine for advanced care when needed. This facility will be available to the local community, with 
the option of being offered free of cost or at a nominal fee.  
The Doc Kendra initiative marks an important step in Star Health’s jou rney to support healthier 
communities. By bringing basic healthcare services closer to people, it offers them a trusted space to 
address their health concerns and find guidance for further care when needed. This initiative serves as a 
bridge, connecting pe ople to the care they need and guiding them through their health journeys with 
 
support and care. More than just an initiative, Doc Kendra reflects Star Health’s understanding of the 
importance of being present, involved, and supportive in the lives of the people it serves.
