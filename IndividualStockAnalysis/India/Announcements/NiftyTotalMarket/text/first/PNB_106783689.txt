Brief Profile  
 
Smt. Vandana Bhagavatula is a Fellow Chartered Accountant with 26 years of experience 
in Accountancy, Banking, Finance, Audit & Risk. She also holds a Diploma in Information 
Systems Audit.  
 
She was an independent woman director in Shakti Pumps (India) Limited . She also 
worked in H DFC Bank Ltd. as Vice -President  and as a Director in Aneja Associates , a 
Chartered Accountant (CA) Firm . Earlier in her career, she was the proprietress of M/s 
VB & Associates, Chartered Accountants, Mumbai.  
 
She presently holds the position of Shareholder Director  on the Board of Punjab National 
Bank and is an Honorary advisor for Kamala Sundaram Foundation for their Blind Free 
India Project.
