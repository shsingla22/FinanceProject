Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
In continuation of our letter dated 18th July 2025 and 23rd July 2025 and pursuant to 
Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform that the Board of Directors of the Company 
in its meeting held today i.e. 29th July 2025, has, inter-alia, considered and approved the 
following: 
 
1. Re-appointment of Shri Gurdeep Singh (DIN: 00307037) as Chairman & Managing 
Director of NTPC Limited  
 
In terms of Order No. 8/1/2024-Th.I(271803) dated 18th July 2025 issued by Ministry of 
Power on behalf of the President of India, Shri Gurdeep Singh (DIN:00307037) has been 
appointed as Additional Director and designated as Chairman and Managing Director 
(Key Managerial Personnel) of the Company w.e.f. 1st August 2025 till 31st July 2026 or 
till assumption of charge of the post by the regular incumbent or until further orders, 
whichever is earliest, on the terms and conditions to be decided by the Government of 
India, subject to approval of shareholders of the Company at the ensuing Annual 
General Meeting. 
 
Further, in compliance with Circular No. LIST/COMP/14/2018-19 and 
NSE/CML/2018/02 dated 20th June 2018, issued by the BSE and NSE, respectively and 
as per confirmation received from Shri Gurdeep Singh, we hereby confirm that he is not 
debarred from holding the office of Director by virtue of any Order passed by the 
Securities and Exchange Board of India or any other such authority. He is holding 5282 
Equity Shares of NTPC.
