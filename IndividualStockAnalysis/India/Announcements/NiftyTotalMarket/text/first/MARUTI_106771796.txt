Bengaluru , 05 September , 2026 : Maruti Suzuki India Limited (Maruti Suzuki) introduces the Stunning New 
Baleno , designed to captivate young, ambitious urban achievers  who lead their lives with sophistication  
and elegance.  Developed with the philosophy of ‘Dare to go Glam’, the Stunning New Baleno introduces a 
premium and progressive design language which elevates the striking appeal of the premium hatchback. 
Introducing class -leading features that redefine the premium hatchback segment,  the Stunning New 
Baleno offers a new level of safety and convenience with Level 2 ADAS featuring radar and camera  
combination  and Ventilated Front Seats  with plush leatherette upholstery . Bookings for the Stunning New 
Baleno  are now open at INR 11 000, with  prices  starti ng at INR 6 09 900 (ex-showroom~).

New Advanced Z12E engine  – Powering the Stunning New Baleno is the  Advanced Z12E powertrain with 
a maximum power output of 61 kW @ 5700 rpm and 112.4 Nm of peak torque @ 4500 rpm.  Equipped 
with Idle Start -Stop technology  and Dual VVT , the powertrain is engineered to optimise driving pleasure 
with low CO 2 emissions . Available with 5 -speed manual  (MT)  and Auto Gear Shift (AGS) automatic 
transmission options, the Stunning New Baleno offers  fuel-efficiency of 23.80 km/l^ for the MT model  and

predecessor . The Baleno S -CNG  is available with the  Z12E engine  and 5 -speed manual transmission 
delivers a  fuel efficiency of 33.61 km/kg^ in S-CNG (MT) , an improvement of 9.8%  over its predecessor.
