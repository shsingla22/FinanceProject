Firstsource tops Professional Services sector in S&P Global Sustainable1 
FY25  
 ESG and CSA score of 87  
 
Mumbai, India | January  22, 2026: Firstsource Solutions Limited  (NSE:FSL, BSE:532809), a leading global provider 
of business process management services and an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company , has achieved an ESG and 
Corporate Sustainability Assessment (CSA) score of 87 each  in the S&P Global Sustainable1 assessment for 
FY25.   
 
This marks the third consecutive year of strong upward momentum : FY23 : 62 → FY24 : 81 → FY25 : 87. This 
progression position s Firstsource as the #1 ranked firm in the Professional Services sector, globall y, enabled by its 
global sustainability framework FirstConscious, and its UnBPOTM approach.  
 
Dr. Sanjiv Goenka, Chairman of RPSG Group and Firstsource, shared, “Amid growing client emphasis on 
sustainable operations and responsible sourcing, Firstsource has emerged as one of the fastest -rising ESG performers 
in the global professional services sector.  Our rapid climb in S&P Global’s CSA assessments reflect  how deeply 
sustainability now informs our growth strategy. ESG isn’t peripheral , it’s our blueprint for resilience, relevance, and 
responsible transformation. This recognition is a milestone, but our focus remains forward – deepening climate action, 
strengthening  our partner ecosystem, and creating lasting value for all stakeholders .” 
 
Key FY25 achievements include:  
Environmental Progress  
• Disclosure of Scope 1, 2 & 3 emissions along with external assurance.  
• 26% of operations powered by renewable  energy.  
• Commitment to Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi  – Validation underway ) and Net Zero by 2050.  
• EV transition underway with a 50% fleet conversion target by 2027.  
Social & Talent Impact  
• 46% gender diversity achieved; 11,000+ impact hires  through  inclusion programs  to date . 
• Launch of INDEX 2.0 and AI Coach for workforce transformation.  
• 95% client satisfaction maintained.  
Governance & ESG Intelligence  
• Focused drive to encourage and align external stakeholders  towards climate goals.  
• Phase 1 of ESGFirst, a real -time digital ESG monitoring platform . 
• 85% of supplier spend evaluated on ESG metrics.  
 
S&P Global CSA  evaluat es more than 12,000  companies  annually  on their management of financially material ESG 
risks and opportunities. These independent, industry -specific benchmarks are vital tools for investors and clients to 
assess  resilience and long -term value.  
 
This recognition builds  on recent  ESG  accolades, including the Golden Peacock Award for ESG Performance, Prithvi 
ESG Awards, and consistent CDP scores , reinforcing  Firstsource’s  leadership in transparency, governance, and 
sustainability outcomes.
