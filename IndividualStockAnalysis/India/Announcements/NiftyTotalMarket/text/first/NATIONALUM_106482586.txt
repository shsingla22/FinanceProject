Sub: Comments of Board on fine levied by the Exchanges. 
Madam/ Sir, 
This has reference to your mail dated 28.11.2025 informing imposition of fine of 
Rs.5,42,800/- (including GST @18% on basic fine amount of Rs.4,60,000/-) for non­
compliance with provisions of Regulation 17(1) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. In this 
regard, we would like to submit the following: 
a)As advised, the matter regarding imposition of fine for non-compliance with provisions
of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 was informed to the Promoter of the Company i.e.
Government of India through Ministry of Mines, being the Administrative Ministry on
05.12.2025 and request was made to expedite for early appointment of requisite
number of Independent Directors to ensure compliance of all statutory requirements of
SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
b)Further, the Company vide letter dated 05.12.2025 clarified to BSE and NSE that,
NALCO is a CPSE and the authority for appointment of Directors is vested with the
President of India. The Company does not have any control on the same. Therefore,
the non-compliances, stated in the e-mail dated 28.11.2025 for the quarter ended
30.09.2025 may be condoned and penalties imposed may be waived.
c)The matter was also placed before the Board of Directors in their 362nd meeting held
on 09.12.2025. The Board took cognizance of the communications of BSE and NSE
dated 28.11.2025, imposing fine of Rs.5,42,800/- (inc.luding GST @18% on basic fine
amount of Rs. 4,60,000/-) each for non-compliance with provisions of Regulation 17(1)
of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for the quarter ended 30.09.2025.
d)The Board advised the Company to communicate BSE and NSE that, NALCO is a
CPSE and the authority for appointment of Directors is vested with the President of
India and the Company does not have any control on the same. Therefore, the non­
compliances, stated in their e-mails dated 28.11.2025 for the quarter ended 30.09.2025
may be condoned and penalties imposed by them for the quarter may be waived
(Extract of minutes of 362nd Meeting of Board of Directors is enclosed as Annexure).
