Particulars  Details of RSUs granted  
 
Brief details of RSUs granted   
26,50,735  RSUs have  been granted .  
Each RSU shall entitle the RSU holder one fully paid -up equity share 
of ₹ 2/ - each of the Company against each RSU vested and 
exercised  and accordingly, up to  26,50,735  equity shares of ₹ 2/ - 
each  (representing  0.098% of the paid -up equity share capital of the 
Company  as on date ) shall be transferred as per the terms of the  
aforesaid RSU Plan to the eligible employee .  
 
The aforesaid RSU Plan  has been  implemented through a trust 
mechanism wherein the trust acquires the shares from the 
secondary market and transfers the same to the eligible employee 
on exercise. No fresh shares shall be issued by the Company either 
to the Trust or to the employee  in terms of the RSU Plan.
