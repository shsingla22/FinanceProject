LEMON TREE HOTELS EXPANDS PRESENCE IN PUNJAB WITH THE 
OPENING OF KEYS LITE BY LEMON TREE HOTELS, MOGA  
 
The Group’s fourth  operational hotel in the State  marks its entry into Moga, with 
nine more properties in the pipeline  
 
New Delhi,  15th September , 2026 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, one of India’s 
leading hospitality companies, has announced the opening of Keys Lite by 
Lemon Tree Hotels, Moga , marking the Group’s entry into Moga and 
strengthening its presence across Punjab.  With this opening, Lemon Tree Hotels 
now has four operational hotels in Punjab, with nine more properties in the pipeline 
across the state.  
The property is operated under a franchise agreement, further expanding the 
group’s robust pipeline across the region.  
Located on GT Road, Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels, Moga offers easy access to 
the city’s commercial areas, key transit points and local attractions, making it a 
convenient choice for both business and leisure travellers.  Moga’s strong agro -
industrial ecosystem , coupled with a steady influx of corporate travel lers, regional 
transit guests, and visiting diaspor a, makes it an ideal market for a structured, 
midscale hospitality brand.  
Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels, Moga offers 33 well -appointed rooms and suites, 
complemented by dining and recreation facilities. The hotel features Keys Café, a 
multi-cuisine restaurant, along with 24 -hour In -Room Dining and a well -equipped 
fitness centre . The property offers a choice of Superior Double Rooms, Deluxe 
Double Rooms, Deluxe Twin Rooms and Studio Suites, designed to provide comfort 
and convenience for a seamless stay.  
Commenting on this new hotel launch, Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, 
President, Lemon Tree Hotels  Ltd. said, “ The opening of Keys Lite by Lemon 
Tree Hotels , Moga reflects our focus on tapping the potential of India’s fast -
growing Tier -3 markets. As emerging commercial centres like Moga evolve , we 
are seeing a clear shift towards trusted, organised hospitality that can serve the 
needs of local business travel, highway transit and the visiting diaspora. Located 
on GT Road, this hotel is well placed to meet that demand with the consistency 
and co mfort that travellers increasingly expect from a trusted hospitality brand.  
With this opening, we now have four operational properties  in Punjab, with nine 
more in the pipeline, further strengthening our presence in  the state .” 
The hotel offers a convenient base to explore Moga’s religious and cultural 
landmarks, including Geeta Bhawan Temple, Dhudike Village, the birthplace of 
Lala Lajpat Rai, and Gurudwara Nanaksar Sahib and Gurudwara Takhtupura Sahib. 
It is approximately 3.5 k m from Moga Railway Station and 4 km from the Bus 
Station, while Ludhiana Airport is around 75 km away.  
 

 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India's leading hospitality companies, 
catering to a wide range of customers – from value -conscious travellers to 
premium business and leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels 
& Resorts, Lemon T ree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys 
Select, and Keys Lite – the group offers experiences across upper upscale, 
upscale, upper midscale, midscale, leisure, wildlife, and spiritual segments.  
LTHL operates 130+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing 
pipeline of 140+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, 
Bengaluru, and Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, 
and Indore – and with an international presence in Bhutan  and Nepal – Lemon 
Tree Hotels delivers exceptional comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, 
refreshing experience.  
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 270+ 
properties (operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for 
both business and leisure travellers.
