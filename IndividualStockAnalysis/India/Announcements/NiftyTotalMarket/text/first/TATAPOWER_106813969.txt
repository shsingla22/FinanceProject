Tata Power Renewables Records over 130% YoY Growth in Utility -Scale 
Execution in Q2 FY27, delivering 419 MW Capacity along with 131.2 
MWhr of Battery Storage Projects 
 
 
TPREL adds 419 MW of utility -scale capacity and 131.2 MWhr of storage in Q2 FY27  
 
• H1 FY27 execution reaches 645 MW of utility -scale projects along with 161.2 MWhr of Battery Storage Projects  
• Utility -scale operational capacity reaches 7.2 GW along with 281 MWhr of Battery Storage Projects reinforcing 
TPREL’s commit ment to advancing India’s clean energy goals  
 
Mumbai, October 12, 2026 : Tata Power Renewable Energy Limited (TPREL), one of India’s largest renewable 
energy companies and a subsidiary of The Tata Power Company Limited (Tata Power), delivered a strong 
execution performance in Q2 FY27, commissioning 419 MW of utility -scale projects representing over 130% 
year-on-year growth compared to the 182 MW of utility -scale capacity executed during the corresponding 
quarter of the previous fiscal year.  It has also executed 131.2 MWhr of storage projects (101.2 MWhr of storage 
capacity for in- house TPREL, while the remaining 30 MWhr of storage capacity comprised third- party projects) 
during Q2FY27.  
  
Building on this strong Q2FY27 performance, TPREL managed to execut e a total of 645 MW of utility -scale 
projects and 161.2 MWhr of storage projects during H1FY27 (of this, 101.2 MWhr of storage projects are for in-
house TPREL, while the remaining 60 MWhr of storage projects have been delivered for third- party customers)  
  
During H1 FY27, the company successfully commissioned several key projects, including the 100.8 MW Jewali 
Wind Project, the Kalasar  FDRE Project comprising 227.5 MW of solar capacity and a 101.2 MWhr Battery 
Energy Storage System (BESS), the 100 MW Kayathar Group Captive Project, and the 72.5 MW Tata Steel 
Captive Project.  
  
The company's strong execution momentum reflects its focus on quality, safety, and operational excellence. 
Backed by a resilient supply chain, experienced partners, and robust project management capabilities, TPREL 
continues to deliver renewable energy and storage projects efficiently across diverse geographies.  
  
With these additions, the company’s utility -scale operational portfolio has grown to 7.2 GW, comprising 5.8 GW 
of solar, 1.3 GW of wind, and 281.2 MWhr   of Battery Storage capacity. TPREL's expanding renewable energy 
footprint continues to support India's  clean energy transition while contributing to national energy security 
and   sustainability.  
  

 
More than 2 GW  of projects are expected to be executed in H2 FY27, comprising 1075 MW of solar,  969 MW 
of wind, and 274 MWhr  of BESS.  Apart from the mentioned i nhouse projects there will be 300 MW of TPREL –  
3rd party  projects are also to be executed in H2 FY27. Wi  
  
By the end of the year, close to 9.3 GW  of projects are expected to be executed, which include 6899 MW of 
solar, 2298 MW of wind, and 555.2 MWhr  of Battery Storage.  
  
In FY 28, the balance projects of ~2.6 GW consisting of 570 MW Solar, 350 MWhr of Battery Storage and 1,700 
MW Wind will be completed and will take the overall operational capacity of TPREL to ~12 GW.  
 
About Tata Power:  
The Tata Power Company Limited, a vertically integrated power company and part of the Tata Group, India’s largest 
multinational business conglomerate, has built a diversified portfolio across the entire power value chain, with its total 
operational and under -construction  capacity surpassing 26 GW. Th is includes approximately 17. 7 GW of clean and green 
energy capacity (including projects under construction) and around 8.8 GW of thermal generation capacity, ensuring a 
balanced and resilient energy mix. The Company also has a strong transmission and dist ribution footprint, with over 7,800  
circuit kilometres of transmission lines, including pipeline projects, and serves  over 13 million customers across its 
distribution businesses, making it one of India’s largest private power distribution companies.  Furth er strengthening its 
leadership in future-ready energy solutions, Tata Power has established 4.9 GW of integrated solar cell and module 
manufacturing capacity, signed 2.8 GW of pumped hydro storage projects, and expanded its EV charging network to over 7,000 public charging points across 7 17 cities and towns in India.  As a pioneer in India’s clean energy transition, Tata 
Power remains committed to achieving Net Zero by 2045 and continues to partner with public and private stakeholders to 
deliver reliable, sustainable, and technology -led energy solutions across the country.
