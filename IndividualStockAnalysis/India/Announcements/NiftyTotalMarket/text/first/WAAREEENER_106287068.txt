Mumbai   Dear Sir,  I have completed more than 4 (four) years as an Independent Director on the Board of the Company and have had the most satisfying experience during this period. I am glad that I was a part of the Board during a period when the company was laying its foundation for its listing. The commitment and hard work of the management with the guidance of the Board saw the Company’s listing being a huge success. More importantly, the Company is on a solid growth path and playing a major role in India’s economic growth by helping it  achieve its ambitious renewable energy goals.    On the personal front, I am at a stage where I feel that there is not much I can contribute to the Board. Further, I am also looking at realigning my existing board roles to be able to create adequate time and to accept some other board engagements.  I would therefore wish to submit my resignation from the Board of Waaree Energies Ltd.  I request the Board to accept the same with effect from the close of business on July 1, 2025.   I wish to reiterate that there is no other material reason for my resignation other than what I have stated above.   I wish the Company and its management continued success in the future.
