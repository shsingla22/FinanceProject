 The Mixed -Matrix Membrane Diaphragm 
Technology developed by BARC is an effective replacement of asbestos diaphragm material 
used in Electrolyser systems. Further, this 
diaphragm will be a low -cost import 
substitute for Zirfon which is used in water 
electrolysers.  
 
The Mixed- Matrix Membrane Diaphragm 
Technology acquired from BARC shall help 
BHEL to achieve complete indigenous 
development of alkaline electrolyser  
systems. This will be a step further in BHEL’s 
contribution to the ‘National Green Hydrogen 
Mission’ and contribute to the Government’s ‘Make in India’ initiative.
