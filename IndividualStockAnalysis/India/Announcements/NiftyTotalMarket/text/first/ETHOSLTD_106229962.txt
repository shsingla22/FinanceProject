Subject:  Launch of 'City of Time , Gurugram ' Comprising 5 Exclusive Brand Boutiques  and 2 Multi -
brand Boutiques  
 
Dear  Sir/Madam,  
 
We are pleased to inform you that Ethos Limited (“the Company”) has formally launched its most 
ambitious luxury retail initiative —City of Time , Gurugram , India’s largest horological destination.  
 
Located at Emaar Capital Tower 2, MG Road, Sector 26, Gurugram – 122002 , City of Time  spans over 
22,000 square feet and features  5 Exclusive Brand Boutiques  of globally iconic watch brand  and 2 
Multi -Brand Boutiques representing over 50 leading independent luxury brands.  
 
In addition to retail, City of Time  is designed as an experiential destination housing watchmaking zone 
and curated lifestyle spaces —offering an unparalleled celebration of time, craftsmanship, and luxury in 
the country . 
 
This flagship project marks a major step in the Company’s long -term strategy to redefine the luxury retail 
experience in India and further consolidate its market leadership in the premium watch segment.
