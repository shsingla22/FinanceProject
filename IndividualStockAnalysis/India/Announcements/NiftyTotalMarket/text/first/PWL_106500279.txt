Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
This is in continuation of our earlier intimation dated December 08, 2025 , wherein the Company had informed the 
Stock Exchanges about the fund infusion of approximately INR 400 crore  in Penpencil Edu Services Private Limited  
(“Penpencil”), a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, to support its future expansion plans and strategic 
investments aimed at driving growth . 
 
Further, vide our intimation dated December 23, 2025 , the Company had disclosed that, as part of the aforesaid 
expansion plan, Penpencil had acquired land  to support its future business expansion. In continuation thereof, 
and Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI Listing Regulations, we would like to inform you 
that Penpencil, has executed a Sale Deed dated January 17, 2026 for the acquisition of land admeasuring area 1.16 
Acres , situated at Village - Tupudana, District Ranchi, in furtherance of its approved expansion strategy.  
 
Further, in continuation of and in furtherance of the aforesaid fund infusion and expansion plans, Penpencil may, 
from time to time, acquire additional parcels of land from the funds infused by the Company, as part of its strategic 
investments.
