Capri Loans  Among Top Global Performers in S&P ESG Assessment  
Mumbai, 13th December  2024 : Capri Global Capital Ltd ., a leading non -banking financial company  with the 
brand name Capri Loans , has achieved a significant milestone in its sustainability journey . The company 
earned an impressive score of 48 in the S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment  (CSA) for FY 2023 -
24, marking its debut filing.  This performance significantly surpasses the industry average score of 30 and 
positions Capri Loans  among the top 20% of respondents globally within its sector.  
 
The S&P Global CSA, recognized worldwide for evaluating Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) 
practices, highlights companies that demonstrate excellence in sustainable and responsible business 
operations.  This achievement  underscores Capri Loans ’ dedication to responsible business practices . Over 
the past year, the company has made significant strides in formalizing its ESG framework, ensuring 
sustainability is integrated into decision -making processes at every level.   
 
Speaking on the achievement, Jinisha Sharma – Principal ESG, Capri Loans , stated, "  Capri Loans’ 
outstanding ESG performance demonstrates that sustainability and business success go hand in hand. This 
recognition fuels our mission to lead by example and innovate in ways that benefit both society and the 
environment ." 
 
Capri Loans ’ comm itment to sustainability is evident in its impressive achievements across key ESG 
dimensions. The company boasts a data availability rate of 79%  placing it in the top category , highlighting its 
dedication to robust disclosures and transparency. It has established itself as a sector leader   with a high 
percentile i n critical areas such as Financial Inclusion  (99th Percentile) and Business Ethics  (93rd percentile) 
with other parameters like Policy Influence , Tax Strategy , Human Rights , Occupa tional health & safety  
ranging in 90+ percentile , showcasing impact on fostering responsible business practices. Additionally, Capri 
Loans  continues to set new standards in ESG excellence, consistently outperforming industry peers and 
cementing its position as a global benchmark in sustainability.  
 
The achievement resonates with Capri Loans ’ broader mission to balance profitability with purpose, 
reinforcing its position as a responsible industry frontrunner.
