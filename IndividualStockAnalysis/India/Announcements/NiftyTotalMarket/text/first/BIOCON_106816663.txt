NOTIFICATION TO STOCK EXCHANGE  
 
COMPANY STATEMENT  
 
Biocon Secures Earliest  Licensed Launch  Dates for Pebrilzo® 
(bPertuzumab)  Following Global Patent Settlement  
with Genentech   
 
 
BENGALURU, India: Octo ber 14, 202 6 
 
Biocon Limited  (BSE: 532523, NSE: BIOCON) has entered into a global settlement and license 
agreement with Genentech, securing earliest licensed launch dates  for Pebrilzo®, its biosimilar 
to the reference product Perjeta® ( Pertuzumab).   
  
Biocon and Genentech executed the patent settlement agreement to resolve all pending 
disputes between the companies , including in the U.S., Canada, and other jurisdictions.  
 
This global patent settlement  and license  agreement enables Biocon to launch Pebrilzo® in Q1 
of calendar year ( CY) 2027 in the European Union and UK, Q3 of CY 2028 in the U .S., and in CY 
2026 -2028 in other Rest of the World (RoW) countries or earlier in certain circumstances.  
 
The other terms of the agreement are confidential.  
  
Pebrilzo® is a biosimilar medicinal product  and the active substance is Pertuzumab, 
a monoclonal antibody that binds with high affinity and specificity to the human epidermal 
growth factor receptor 2 (HER2), inhibiting the proliferation of tumour cells that overexpress 
HER2.  Pebrilzo®  is used for the treatment of HER2 -positive breast cancer across multiple 
disease stages .
