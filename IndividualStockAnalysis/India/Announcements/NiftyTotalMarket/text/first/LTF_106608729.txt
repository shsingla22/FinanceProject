c. A brief on types of customers
 LTF serves the lending needs of both the urban and rural sectors and has a customer base of 2.8 Cr+
 Rural Finance:
   Rural Business Finance serves rural individuals and enterprises including Rural Group Loans, Micro Finance (JLG) and 
Micro LAP , Farmer Finance caters to farm-equipment and agri-allied needs
   Rural customers largely consist of women entrepreneurs and farmers with a customer base of 1.8 Cr+
 Urban Finance:
   Urban Finance serves the needs of individuals and business owners for products including Home Loans, Loan 
Against Property (LAP), Personal Loans and Two-Wheeler Finance
   Urban customers consist of salaried, self-employed, business persons and professionals with a customer base of 1 Cr+
   SME Finance serves professionals, traders and other self-employed segments
  Gold Finance caters to the financing needs of individuals through gold loans
