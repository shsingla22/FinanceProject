Brief Profile of Mr. Sanjay Vij  
 
Mr. Sanjay Vij is a veteran of the Life Insurance Industry, having worked with companies like Digit life as Dy. CEO, 
Exide Life as Executive Director and Principal Officer and HDFC Life from 2001 till 2021 in various capacities with the 
last being Group Head -Bancassurance and Chief Values Officer.  
Prior to joining HDFC Life, he was associated with HCL Limited, Blue Star, Core Parenterals Ltd., Span Medicals 
Limited and Birla AT&T Communications Limited.
