Sub: Company Update: Commissioning of cement  capacity  
 
Dear Sirs,  
 
We are pleased to announce the commissioning of the second cement grinding mill with a 
capacity of 1.8 mtpa  at the Company’s Unit located in Maihar, Madhya Pradesh , as part of the 
Company’s ongoing expansion plan. The first mill was commissioned in March 2025.  
 
The Company continues to enhance its production capabilities to meet the growing demand 
for cement.  
 
Consequent to the above, the Company’s consolidated domestic grey cement capacity stands 
at 18 6.86 mtpa. Along with its overseas capacity of 5.4 mtpa, the Company’s global cement 
capacity stands at 1 92.26 mtpa.  
 
The above is for your information and record .
