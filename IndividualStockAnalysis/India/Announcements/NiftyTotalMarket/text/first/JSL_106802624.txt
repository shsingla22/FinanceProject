Jindal Stainless supplies stainless steel to reinforce the six -decade -old Sadhana 
Bhawan at Shri Kripalu Dham  
 
• Structural reinforcement strengthens the 1965 meditation hall while preserving its original 
architectural character  
 
National, September 30, 2026:  Jindal Stainless, India’s leading stainless steel manufacturer, has 
contributed to the structural reinforcement of Sadhana Bhawan at Shri Kripalu Dham in Mangarh, Uttar 
Pradesh. The new stainless steel  structural system is designed to work alongside the original structure 
without altering its architectural character or the experience of devotees.  
 
Founded in 1965 by Jagadguru Shri Kripalu Ji Maharaj, Shri Kripalu Dham has evolved into a spiritual centre  
that draws devotees from across India and the world. The campus is anchored by the marble Bhakti 
Mandir and a charitable hospital that treats hundreds of patients a day, without any cost. Sadhana 
Bhawan, the campus' meditation and assembly hall, has been an integral part of this journey, hosting large 
congregations and spiritual gatherings over the years.  
 
The reinforcement approach focused on strengthening the structure from within rather than rebuilding 
it. The new system is designed to share the load with the original structure, providing additional strength.  
Jindal Stainless facilitated the supply over 160 tonnes of especially -fabricated building components like 
load -bearing columns, beams, and cross girders through its subsidiary company, Jindal Stainless Steelway 
Limited (JSSL). Hot Rolled Annealed and Pickled (HRAP) plates of Stainless Steel  grade 304L, manufactured 
at Jindal Stainless plants, was used for this project. This end -to-end supply ecosystem has been one of the 
key strengths of Jindal Stainless in bringing such projects to life.  
 
Commenting on the initiative, Managing Director, Jindal Stainless, Mr Abhyuday Jindal said, “The 
reinforcement of Sadhana Bhawan demonstrates how advanced stainless steel  solutions can strengthen 
ageing structures while preserving their original character and architectural heritage. Our contribution to 
this project reflects Jindal Stainless’ commitment to enabling durable, long -term , ready -to-use solutions 
through stainless steel. We are honoured to have contributed towards a project of such significance.”  
 
Grade 304L was selected for its suitability for the indoor, non -marine and non -industrial environment, 
offering a combination of strength, corrosion resistance and durability. Its high strength -to-weight 
characteristics enabled the structural reinforcement  to provide the required support without adding 
unnecessary bulk. The material’s fabrication flexibility also facilitated its integration with the existing 
structure while meeting the project’s preservation requirements.  The project was undertaken in 
collaboration with the Jagat Guru Kripalu Parishad team, which served as the design consultant, and Vats 
Group, which was responsible for fabrication and installation. Jindal Stainless served as the material and 
technical partner  for the project.
