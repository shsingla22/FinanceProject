Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, we wish to inform that due to the ongoing

constant engagement with oil marketing companies (OMCs) to remain apprised of the latest  
developments and plan operational responses accordingly, given the rapidly evolving nature 
of the situation.  
 
The Company has resilient systems and processes in place to navigate short ‑term operational 
challenges and will continue to closely monitor the situation.
