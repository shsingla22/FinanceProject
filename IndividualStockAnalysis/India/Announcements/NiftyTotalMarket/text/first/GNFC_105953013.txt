About GNFC:  
GNFC is a joint sector enterprise promoted by the Government of Gujarat and the Gujarat State 
Fertilizers & Chemicals Ltd.(GSFC).  It was set up at Bharuch, Gujarat in  1976.  
GNFC started its manufacturing and marketing operations up in 1982 with one of the  world’s 
largest single -stream ammonia -urea fertilizer complex  and gradually expanded in chemicals.  
Today, chemicals form significant segment of revenue and key  contributor of segment results .
