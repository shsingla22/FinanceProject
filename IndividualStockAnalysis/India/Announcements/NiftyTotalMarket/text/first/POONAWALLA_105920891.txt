Ref.: (i) Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”); and (ii) SEBI 
Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD -PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 (“SEBI 
Circular dated July 13, 2023”)  
 
In terms of the provisions of Regulation 30 read with Point 7 of Paragraph A of Part A of Schedule III 
of Listing Regulations, we wish to inform to you the change in senior management personnel of the 
Poonawalla Fincorp Limited (“ Company ”). 
 
The Board of Directors of the Company (“ Board ”), at its meeting held today i.e. , July 20, 2024 has 
considered and approved the following items:  
 
I. Approved the early retirement of Mr. Manoj Gujaran, existing Chief Compliance Officer 
(“CCO ”) of the Company from the employment of the Company, which shall be effective from 
July 20, 2024 based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee 
(“NRC ”) and Audit Committee of the Board (“ ACB ”). 
 
           Further, the Board, has at its meeting held today i.e. , July 20, 2024, basis the recommendations 
of the NRC and ACB, approved the proposed appointment of Mr. Bholananda Behera as the new 
CCO of the Company, for a tenure of 5 years from the date of his joining. He is expected to join 
the Company by  October 29, 2024   
 
          Further, as approved and recommended by the NRC and the ACB, the Board has approved that 
in the interim i.e., till the date of joining of the new CCO, Mr. Su mit Talreja, Deputy Chief 
Compliance Officer of the Company, will officiate the functions of the CCO of the Company.  
 
II. Approved the early retirement of Mr. Anup Agarwal , existing Chief Internal Audit or (“CIA”) of 
the Company from the employment of the Company, which shall be effective from  July 20, 2024 , 
based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (“ NRC ”) and 
Audit Committee of the Board (“ ACB ”). 
 
Further, the Board, has at its meeting held today i.e. , July 20, 2024 , basis the recommendations 
of the NRC and ACB, approved the proposed appointment of Mr. Nitin Sane  as the new CIA of 
 
 
the Company, for a tenure of 5 years from the date of his joining. He is expected to join the 
Company by  October 03, 2024 .  
 
Further, as approved and recommended by the NRC and the ACB, the Board has approved that 
in the interim i.e., till the date of joining of the new CIA, Mr. Vishal Pandey , Deputy Vice 
President Internal Audit of the Company, will officiate the functions of the CIA of the Company.  
 
III. Approved the early retirement of Mr. Rajendra Tathare, existing Chief Risk Officer (“ CRO ”) of 
the Company from the employment of the Company, which shall be effective from July 20,2024 
based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (“ NRC ”) and 
Risk Management Committee of the Board (“ RMC ”). 
 
Further, the Board, has at its meeting held today i.e. , July 20, 2024, basis the recommendations 
of the NRC and RMC, approved the proposed appointment of Mr. Bhaskar Pande  as the new 
CRO of the Company, for a tenure of 5 years from the date of his joining. He is expected to join 
the Company by August 30, 2024 .  
 
Further, as approved and recommended by the NRC and the RMC, the Board has approved that 
in the interim i.e., till the date of joining of the new CRO, Mr. Mitul Kirtikumar Budhbhatti , Head 
- Credit Monitoring and Risk  of the Company, will officiate the functions of the CRO of the 
Company.  
 
IV. Further, the Company has approved the early retirement of Mr. Manish Chaudhari, existing Head 
of Retail Assets (“ Head Retail Assets ”) of the Company , from the employment of the Company, 
which shall be effective from July 20, 2024 .  
 
V. Further, the Company has approved the early retirement of Ms. Smita Mitra, existing Head 
Human Resource (“ Head HR ”) of the Company, from the employment of the Company, which 
shall be effective from July 20, 2024 . 
 
We are enclosing herewith the brief details of the aforesaid change as prescribed under the Listing 
Regulations read with the SEBI Circular dated July 13, 2023, as Annexure A , Annexure B, Annexure 
C, Annexure D , and Annexure E  respectively . 
 
Kindly take the above intimation on record.
