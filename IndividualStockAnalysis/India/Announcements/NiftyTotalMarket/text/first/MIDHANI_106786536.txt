ADDENDUM TO THE NOTICE CONVENING THE 52ND ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING
This Addendum is being issued in continuation of and 
shall be read together with the Notice dated August 28, 
2026 convening the Fifty-Second (52nd) Annual General 
Meeting (“AGM”) of the Members of Mishra Dhatu Nigam 
Limited (“MIDHANI”/“Company”), scheduled to be held 
on Wednesday, September 30, 2026 at 11:00 A.M. (IST) 
through Video Conferencing (“VC”)/Other Audio-Visual 
Means (“OAVM”).
Subsequent to issuance and dispatch of the 
aforesaid Notice, Ministry of Defence, Department of 
Defence Production, vide Office Memorandum No. 
8(29)/2026-D(Coord/DDP) dated September 17, 2026, 
has conveyed nomination of Ms. Meera Mohanty, JS (P&C) 
(DIN: 03379561) as Government Nominee Director on 
the Board of Mishra Dhatu Nigam Limited, in place Shri 
Prakash Rajpurohit, JS (DIP) (DIN: 06895578), with effect 
from September 17, 2026.
This Addendum shall be deemed to be an integral part of 
the original Notice dated August 28, 2026 together with 
notes.
A. WITHDRAWAL OF ITEM NO. 5 UNDER SPECIAL 
BUSINESS:
Subsequent to dispatch of the Notice of the 52nd AGM, 
Ministry of Defence, Department of Defence Production, 
vide Office Memorandum No. 8(29)/2026-D(Coord/DDP) 
dated September 17, 2026, has conveyed nomination 
of Ms. Meera Mohanty, JS (P&C) (DIN:03379561), as 
Government Nominee Director on the Board of MIDHANI, 
in place of Shri Prakash Rajpurohit, JS (DIP) (DIN: 06895578), 
with effect from September 17, 2026.
Consequent thereto, Shri Prakash Rajpurohit  
(DIN: 06895578) ceased to hold office as Government Nominee Director on the Board of MIDHANI with effect 
from September 17, 2026.
Accordingly, the special business set out at Item No. 5 
of the Notice relating to “Appointment of Shri Prakash 
Rajpurohit (DIN: 06895578) as Government Nominee 
Director of Company” stands withdrawn and shall not be 
placed before the Members for consideration or remote-
e-voting/voting at the 52nd AGM.
All references to Item No. 5, including the Explanatory 
Statement pursuant to Section 102 of the Companies Act, 
2013 and the particulars/profile of Shri Prakash Rajpurohit 
forming part of the Notice of 52nd AGM, shall accordingly 
stand withdrawn.

Pursuant to Article 67 of Articles of Association (AoA) of

Ministry of Defence, Department of Defence Production 
vide 8(29)/2026-D (Coord/DDP) dated September 17, 
2026 conveyed the appointment of Ms. Meera Mohanty  
(DIN: 03379561), Joint Secretary (Personnel & 
Coordination), MoD, as Government Nominee Director on 
the Board of MIDHANI w.e.f. September 17, 2026.
Ms. Meera Mohanty (DIN: 03379561), will continue to hold 
office until further orders, conveyed by Administrative 
Ministry in this regard. She shall not be liable to retire by 
rotation pursuant to Section 152 of the Companies Act, 
2013. The Government Nominee Director on the Board of 
Company does not draw any sitting fees, commission or 
remuneration from the Company.
Ms. Meera Mohanty is an Indian Administrative Service 
(IAS) officer of the Himachal Pradesh cadre, belonging to 
the 2005 batch. At present, she is Joint Secretary (P&C), 
Ministry of Defence, Department of Defence Production. 
Prior to her present assignment, she was Joint Secretary 
in the Prime Minister’s Office (PMO). Ms. Mohanty is also 
a Government Nominee Director on the Board of Bharat 
Electronics Limited (BEL).

and Disclosure Requirements) Regulation, 2015, the appointment of Director is subject to the approval of 
shareholders.

conveyed by Ministry of Defence.
