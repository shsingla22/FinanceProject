Part A – Sun Pharma  Employee s Stock Option Plan (ESOP 2026)  and Connected Matters  
 
Part A (Item No. 1 to 3) relate to the proposed Employee Stock Option Plan 2026 ( “ESOP 2026” ), which is intended to strengthen the Company ’s long- term 
incentive framework by providing eligible employees with performance -linked, share -based rewards. The proposals cover adoption of ESOP 2026, acquisition of 
shares through a Tr ust for implementation of the Plan and extension of the Plan to eligible employees of subsidiary company(ies). The Board beli eves that these 
proposals will help attract, motivate and retain talent while aligning employee interests with long -term shareholde r value creation. The Plan will be implemented 
using existing shares acquired from the secondary market and will not involve a fresh issue of shares by the Company thereby not involving any dilution . 
 
Part B - Funding and Acquisition  and Connected Matters  
 
Part B  (Item No. 4 to 7)  relate to certain enabling approvals required in connection with the proposed acquisition of Organon & Co. and the associated financing 
arrangements. These proposals include enhancement of limits for investments, loans, guarantees, securities and borrowing s, creation of security in favour of lenders 
and approval under Regulation 24(5) of the SEBI Listing Regulations. These approvals are intended to facilitate implementation framework necessary to support 
the proposed acquisition and related funding requirem ents.
