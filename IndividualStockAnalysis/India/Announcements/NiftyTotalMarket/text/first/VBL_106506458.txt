Sub: Administrative Warning - Violation of provisions of SEBI (Listing
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, (“LODR
Regulations”) in the matter of Varun Beverages Limited
1. This has reference to the various complaints received by SEBI against Varun 
Beverages Limited for failure to comply with disclosure obligations under SEBI 
(LODR) Regulations, 2015.
2. In this regard, it is observed that the disclosure relating to material events Le 
termination of Share Purchase Agreement with SBC Beverages Tanzania Limited, 
Tanzania (SBCT) and SBC Beverages Ghana Limited, Ghana (SBCG) has not been 
made adequately and timely. Consequently, Regulation 4(1),(d),(e) & (h) and 
Regulation 30(6) and (7) of the LODR Regulations, 2015 has been violated.
3. The above non-compliance has been viewed seriously. Accordingly, the Company 
is hereby warned and advised to be careful in future to avoid recurrences of such 
lapses. Any repetition of the such violation in future will be viewed seriously and 
appropriate enforcement action would be initiated in accordance with the provisions 
of the SEBI Act, 1992, the Rules, the Regulations or Circulars issued thereunder.
