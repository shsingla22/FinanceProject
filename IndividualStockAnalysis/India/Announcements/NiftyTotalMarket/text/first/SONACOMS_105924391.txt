Sona BLW Precision Forging s Ltd. announces Q 1 FY25 Results  
Highest-ever Revenue, EBITDA, BEV Revenue and BEV revenue share in a quarter 
Revenue grows 22% YoY, EBITDA grows 23%, and PAT grows 27% YoY in Q1 FY25 
BEV revenue grew 53%, and BEV revenue share increased to the highest-ever 33% 
 
Gurugram, India, July 24th, 2024 : Sona BLW Precision Forgings Ltd.  (Sona Comstar ), a leading automotive 
technology company providing mission -critical systems and components for electrified and non -electrified 
powertrain segments, today announced its financial results for the quarter  ended June 30th, 2024.  
Key Financial Highlights – Q1 FY25  
• Revenue of Rs. 893 crore s with 22% YoY growth  
• Battery Electric Vehicles (BEV) revenue growth of 53% YoY , BEV Revenue share at 33%  
• EBITDA of Rs. 251 crore s with a m argin of 2 8.1% and 23% YoY  growth  
• PAT of Rs. 142 crore s with a net profit margin of 15. 9% and 27%  YoY  growth  
• The net order book* increased to Rs. 2 3,300 crores from Rs. 22,600 crores as of March 31st, 2024

About Sona BLW Precision Forgings Limited  
Sona BLW Precision Forgings Limited ( Sona Comstar ) is one of the world’s leading automotive technology 
companies . Sona Comstar was founded in 1995 and is headquartered in Gurugram, India. It is a global 
supplier with manufacturing and assembly facilities, R&D centres and engineering capability centres across 
India, USA, Serbia, Mexico, and China.  
Sona Comstar primarily designs, manufactures, and supplies highly engineered, mission -critical automotive 
systems and components to automotive OEMs. Sona Comstar is a leading supplier of driveline and traction 
motor solutions to the fast -growing global electric vehicle  (EV) market. The company has strong R&D, 
engineering and technological capabilities in precision forging, mechanical and electrical systems, mmWave radar sensors, and base and application software development. It is diversified across 
geograp hies, products, vehicle segments and customers.
