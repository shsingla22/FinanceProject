LEMON TREE HOTELS ANNOUNCES THE SIGNING OF A NEW PROPERTY  
ALONG THE INDORE -UJJAIN HIGHWAY WITH  
KEYS SELECT BY LEMON TREE HOTELS, PANTHPIPLAI  
 
Strengthens Lemon Tree Hotels' presence in Madhya Pradesh with its 15th 
property  
 
New Delhi, 2 8th July, 2026:  Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, one of India's leading 
hospitality companies, today announced the signing of Keys Select by Lemon Tree 
Hotels, Panthpiplai,  Ujjain  in Madhya Pradesh. The property will be managed by 
Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels 
Limited.  
This signing takes the group's portfolio in Madhya Pradesh to 1 5 properties, 
comprising three  operational hotels and 12 upcoming properties , as it continues 
to strengthen its presence across the state's key commercial and pilgrimage 
markets.  
Situated directly on the Indore –Ujjain Road, the property is strategically 
positioned to benefit from the economic strength of Indore, the state's commercial 
capital, alongside the strong appeal of Ujjain, one of India's most prominent 
spiritual destinatio ns. The location offers the potential to serve a diverse mix of 
demand generated by the two cities.  
The upcoming hotel will feature 51 well -appointed rooms, a restaurant, a banquet  
hall, meeting/ conference hall, a swimming pool and fitness centre. This full-service 
midscale hotel will be well suited to the needs of travellers seeking convenient 
access between Indore and Ujjain.  
Commenting on the signing, Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, 
Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd. , said, “Panthpiplai  presents a compelling opportunity, 
with Ujjain seeing significant investment in infrastructure and tourism, building on 
its long -standing importance as one of India's foremost spiritual destinations. Its 
proximity to Indore, a major commercial and industr ial centre, further strengthens 
the location's potential. As a trusted midscale brand, Keys Select by Lemon Tree 
Hotels is well suited to this market, offering guests a dependable, comfortable and 
contemporary stay at the right value. With Simhastha  2028 further elevating 
Ujjain's profile and continued infrastructure development strengthening 
connectivity, we see strong potential for the hotel and the market in the years 
ahead.”  
The hotel  location  offers excellent road connectivity, linking efficiently to major 
regional transport hubs. Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport (Indore) is approximately 
41 km from the property, while the Ujjain Junction Railway Station is located 18.5 
km away.  
 
 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India's leading hospitality companies, 
catering to a wide range of customers – from value -conscious travellers to 
premium business and leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels 
& Resorts, Lemon T ree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys 
Select, and Keys Lite – the group offers experiences across upper upscale, 
upscale, upper midscale, midscale, leisure, wildlife, and spiritual segments.  
LTHL operates 130+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing 
pipeline of 140+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, 
Bengaluru, and Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, 
and Indore – and with an international presence in Bhutan and Nepal – Lemon 
Tree Hotels delivers exceptional comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, 
refreshing experience.  
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 270+ 
properties (operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for 
both business and leisure travellers.
