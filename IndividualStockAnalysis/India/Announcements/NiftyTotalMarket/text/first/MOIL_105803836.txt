MOIL scripts history  in FY’24  
 
 FY production surges  by 35%,  
Sales rac es ahead by 30 % 
 
MOIL has recorded best ever production of any financial year since inception at 17.56 lakh 
tonnes, registering a significant growth of 35%  y-o-y. This is 29% higher than the previous 
best ever production of 13.6 4 lakh tonnes in FY 2007 -08. 
 
On the sales front, the company has achieved its best ever sales of 15.36  lakh tonnes in 
FY’24, registering a remarkable growth of 30% y-o-y. This is 10% higher than the previous 
best ever sales of 13.92  lakh tonnes in FY 2007 -08. 
 
The Company has also achieved best ever production of Electrolytic Manganese Dioxide 
(EMD) at  1413 tonnes , higher by 28% y -o-y.  EMD is a 100% import substitution product, 
used mostly for pharmaceuticals and manufacturing of batteries.  
 
Giving utmost thrust to exploration , MOIL has carried out  its best  ever exploratory  core 
drilling of  87,661 meters which is more than double than the previous year.   The same will 
not only form the basis of enhanced production from its existing mines but will also be the 
foundation of opening new  manganese mines in the country.  
 
Shri Ajit Kumar Saxena, CMD, MOIL along with all F unctional Directors & other senior 
officials personally visited all the mines on 1st April, 2024 and congratulated large cross 
section of employees at each mine for this historical performance.   The enthusiasm in the 
organisation was palpable along with the quest to achieve bigger milestones in the coming 
year.
