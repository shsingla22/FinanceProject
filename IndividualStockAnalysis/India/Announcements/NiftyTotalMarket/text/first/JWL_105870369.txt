Jupiter Electric Mobility Pvt. Ltd.  (a subsidiary of Jupiter Wagons Ltd.) 
Receives ARAI Approval for the JEM TEZ Production  - its battery operated 
Light Commercial Vehicles  
JEM TEZ: The First 1 -Ton Commercial EV set for production – to produce 1000  vehicles 
in 2024 -25 
 
Highlights:  
• ARAI approval paves the way for JEM TEZ production  
• JEM TEZ: India's first 1 -ton commercial EV launched  with  rapid  charging: 0 -100% in 20 
minutes  
• Certified 12 7 km range for efficient commercial use  
 
Kolkata, India – May  31st, 2024  – Jupiter Electric Mobility Private Limited ( “JEM” ), a subsidiary of Jupiter 
Wagons Limited,  India’s leading wagon manufacturing company, receives the approval from The 
Automotive Research Association of India ( ARAI ) vide Certiﬁcate No. AATB 0182 dated May 29 th, 2024. 
This certiﬁcation ensures compliance with the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, marking a signiﬁcant 
milestone for JEM , as it prepares to commence the commercial production of its innovative Battery 
Operated Light Commercial Vehicles under the brand name JEM TEZ . 
J E M  i s  s e t  t o  r e v o l u ti o n i z e  t h e  m a r k e t  b y  b e c o m i n g  t h e  ﬁ r s t , to launch a 1 -ton commercial electric 
vehicle (EV). The JEM TEZ is designed for optimal performance and eﬃciency, featuring cutting -edge 
fast charging technology that can charge the vehicle from 0% to 100% in approximately 20 minutes at combined chargin g stations, which is the fastest charging time in the industry. Additionally, the onboard 
charger allows the vehicle to be fully charged in less than 2 hours. The JEM TEZ boasts a certiﬁed range  
of 12 7 kilometers, making it an ideal solution for various commercial applications.  
In addition to the vehicle’s impressive technical speciﬁcations, JEM TEZ comes equipped with Telematics  
to monitor the health and performance of all its EVs. This will enable real -time diagnosis, maintenance 
and support, ensuring that any issues can be swiftly addressed, thereby enhancing the reliability and 
eﬃciency of the JEM TEZ ﬂeet.  
Mr. Vivek Lohia, Managing Director of Jupiter Wagons Limited , expressed his enthusiasm: “We are 
thrilled to receive ARAI approval, which validates our commitment to innovation and excellence in the electric mobility sector. The JEM TEZ represents a signiﬁcant step forward in our eﬀorts to contribute to 
a greener and more sustainable future for India.  We see a huge potential for the EV business  in India, 
considering the rapid growth we all are witnessing across sectors.  Looking at the cost beneﬁts of EVs 
and sustainable approach, EVs will eventually take over the ICE market. The government’s vision of 
creating a green future and favourable policy support will only boost this segment further. ” 

Mr. Gaurav Jalota, Chief Executive Oﬃcer of Jupiter Electric Mobility Pvt. Ltd ., added: “With the JEM 
TEZ, we are setting new benchmarks in the commercial EV segment. Our focus has been on delivering 
a product that not only meets the highest standards of quality and performance but also supports the 
nation’s goals of reducing pollution and emissions. We are excited to lead the charge in transforming India’s transportation landscape  and aim to lead the way for a cleaner  and greener mobility solutions. ” 
The company with its manufacturing facilities in Jabalpur, Indore, and Kolkata has over 200 acres on campus and as expertise in mass -scale manufacturing and auto component engineering. With the tech-
enabled processes and highly qualiﬁed and skilled workfo r c e ,  J E M  i s  i n  a  p o s i ti o n  t o  c r e a t e  a n  
advantageous spot for its well- researched and strongly engineered CEV products in the market. The 
company plans to have a complete bouquet of electric vehicles from MCV, HCV, and Bus in the future.  
The production of JEM TEZ will commence shortly, positioning Jupiter Electric Mobility Pvt. Ltd. as pioneers in driving positive environmental change and advancing the adoption of electric vehicles in India.
