BSE’s BANKEX  becomes First Indian Sectoral Index Futures Contract 
Approved by US CFTC for offer and sale in the US  
 
Mumbai , November 16, 2026:  BSE Ltd. (“BSE”), Asia’s first stock exchange, today announced 
that the United States Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) has approved the offer 
and sale of BSE BANKEX Index Futures (“BANKEX Futures”) in the United States, subject 
to applicable pro visions of the US Commodity Exchange Act and CFTC regulations.  
The approval follows the successful completion of the CFTC's  45-day expedited review 
process, during which the BANKEX Contract was determined to meet the applicable 
requirements under the US Commodity Exchange Act (CEA).  
On this occasion, Mr. Sundararaman Ramamurthy, MD & CEO of BSE  said “We are 
please d to receive the confirmation of the CFTC for offering BSE BANKEX Index Futures 
contract in the US . With this confirmation, US investors will be able to access the Indian 
banking sector through a well -regulated and transparent venue. We will continue our efforts to 
widen the range of BSE products available to global investors.”  
The BSE BANKEX Index tracks the performance of leading banking sector stocks listed on 
BSE, and the BANKEX contract, based on the index, offers an efficient means of taking or 
hedging that exposure through a single instrument. The CFTC’s confirmation permits the 
BANKEX contract to be offered and sold to investors located in the US, making it simpler and 
more convenient for them to deal in Indian banking sector index derivatives on BSE while 
remaining fully subject to Indian laws and regulations governing the securities market.  
The approval is expected to provide US institutional investors, including asset managers, hedge 
funds and proprietary trading firms, with a regulated avenue to gain exposure to India's banking 
sector or hedge their positions in Indian banking stocks. Incre ased participation from US 
investors is also expected to enhance the depth and liquidity of the BANKEX contract, further 
strengthening BSE's derivatives market.
