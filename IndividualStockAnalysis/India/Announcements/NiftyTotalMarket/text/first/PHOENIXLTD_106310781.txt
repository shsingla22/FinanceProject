The Phoenix Mills Limited to facilitate exit of CPP Investments  
from Island Star Mall Developers Private Limited 
 
Mumbai, India ( July 24, 202 5): The Phoenix Mills Limited (BSE: 503100 | NSE: PHOENIXLTD | “PML” 
or “the Company”) announced today that its Board of Directors has approved  the proposed 
transaction for the buy -out of the 49% shareholding of Canada Pension Plan Investment Board  (“CPP 
Investments”)  in Island Star Mall Developers Private Limited ( “ISMDPL ”). The transaction is subject to 
approval of the Company’s shareholders and obtaining CCI and other requisite regulatory approvals. Post completion, PML’s ownership in ISMDPL will increase from 51% to 100%.  
 The aggregate consideration payable to CPP Investment s for the proposed transaction is agreed at 
approximately Rs. 5,449 crores , to be paid over 36 months in four tranches  in compliance with 
applicable laws  and regulations , with suitable adjustments , including as maybe necessary in case of 
prepayment of a relevant tranche . The proposed transaction will be implemented through a mix of 
buyback, capital reduction and dividend payout by ISMDPL and/or secondary purchase by PML  and / 
or its affiliates . 
 Commenting on the development,  Mr. Atul Ruia, Chairman at The Phoenix Mills Limited, said: 
“ISMDPL marked the beginning of our long- standing partnership with CPP Investments. What started 
with a single asset has grown into a portfolio of premium, retail -led mixed -use developments across 
key urban centres. As we consolidate our stake in the JV, we  remain focused on building on this strong 
foundation and creating some of the most vibrant and high- performing mixed- use destinations in the 
country.  We are deeply appreciative of CPP Investments’ role in shaping this journey and look forward 
to continuing our valued partnership through our other joint ventures  which continue as planned. 
These comprise of Mindstone Mall Developers Private Limited – housing the under -development 
Phoenix Grand Victoria in Kolkata, and Plutocrat Commercial Real Estate Private Limited –  which 
includes Project Rise, a large -scale office -led development in Lower  Parel, Mumbai. We truly value like -
minded partners who share our long- term vision, and we look forward to exploring new opportunities 
together. ”  
 Commenting on the development, Mr. Shishir Shrivastava, Managing Director at The Phoenix Mills Limited, said: “This transaction allows us to consolidate full ownership of a portfolio of high- quality, 
retail -led mixed -use assets across key Indian cities. ISMDPL has grown into a strong platform with 
scale, stability, and clear visibility for future growth. Today, 4.4  million sq. ft of retail space is already 
operational, delivering over Rs. 600 crores  of EBITDA in FY25 alone. Over the next few years, this 
platform will include over 5.2 million sq.  ft of retail, around 4 million sq.  ft of office space, and 2 to 3 
hotels totalling  approximately 1,000 keys. The platform already owns the land and development 
potential in place to deliver this vision . The completed office portfolio of 2.2 million sq.  ft will start 
contributing from FY26, while the upcoming phases — including Phase 2 retail and offices, and the 
Grand Hyatt hotel in Bengaluru — are expected to become operational between 2026 and 2027. We 
believe this will drive sustained EBITDA growth and strong cash flows, while maintaining a healthy leverage profile.”  
 
Bansi S. Mehta  Valuers LLP  acted as the independent valuer and determined the fair value of ISMDPL 
for the proposed transaction.  
 
Morgan Stanley India Company Pvt. Ltd. acted as the financial advisor and provided fairness opinion 
for the proposed transaction to PML.

 About Island Star Mall Developers Private Limited (ISM DPL):  
ISMDPL, a subsidiary of PML and operator of premium retail and retail- led mixed -use destinations in 
India.  ISMDPL along with its subsidiaries (“ISMDPL Platform”) owns and manages four marquee retail destinations with a combined Gross Leasable Area (GLA) o f ~4.4 msft  comprising Phoenix MarketCity 
Bangalore, Phoenix Citadel in Indore, Phoenix Mall of the Millennium in Pune and Phoenix Mall of Asia in Bengaluru.  
 In the commercial offices space, ISMDPL Platform has recently completed two Grade A office developments totalling around 2.2 msft  comprising Phoenix Asia Towers in Bengaluru ( completed and 
OC received) and Millennium Towers in Pune ( all three towers are now completed , OC received for 
one tower  and OC for the remaining two towers expected in 2025 ; the asset is currently  under leasing) . 
These world -class office assets are expected to start contributing to EBITDA in the near -term as the 
occupancy ramp s up at t hese recently completed developments .  
 ISMDPL’s flagship Phoenix MarketCity Bangalore complex, in Whitefield, has completed,  under -
construction and under -planning developments, including:   
• a planned retail expansion of ~ 0.8 msft  in two phases of which the first phase is expected to 
be operational in 202 6,  
• Offices of 1.8  msft of which first phase is expected to be completed in 2027,  and 
• Two hotels including one of which is the 400 -key Grand Hyatt hotel, which is under 
construction and likely to be completed by 2027.  
 Additionally, ISMDPL Platform holds balance  FSI potential across its existing hubs in Bengaluru, Pune, 
and Indore, which can be developed in phase s and also present the opportunity to monetize through 
future partnerships . 
 Transaction Rationale and Growth Drivers  
The transaction is expected to be earnings -accretive and capital- efficient from first year itself. It  is 
expected to be largely funded through internal accruals and incremental leverage at ISMDPL, with payouts envisaged over four tranches across 36 months in accordance with applicable law. This enables PML to consolidate the cash flows, preserve liquidity at the Group level and continue 
executing on it’s broader stated growth plans without any material impact on it’s balance sheet 
strength.  It also allows  for efficient cash utilisation and upstreaming  to PML . Further, it also p rovides 
optionality to PML to explore f uture platform monetisation at underlying asset SPVs , at its discretion . 
 
EBITDA growth at ISMDPL  
ISMDPL reported EBITDA of Rs. 617 crores  in FY25, primarily from the four operational malls. This is 
expected to grow meaningfully over the next 4 –5 years, driven by: (i) leasing of completed offices  
expected to kick -in 2026, (ii) ramp up in retail occupancy and rental increase, (iii) Re -leasing  at 
substantially higher rents by churning and premiumisation  of Phoenix MarketCity Bangalore, (iv) 
activation of under -construction and planned assets, and (v) phased development of balance FSI. 
These levers are exp ected to drive sustained value creation for PML shareholders.
