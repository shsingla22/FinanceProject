Summary of proceedings: 
• The EOGM of the Members of INOX India Limited (the "Company") was held on

EOGM were also present during the Meeting. 
• The Company Secretary informed that the Company had provided remote e-voting facility 
to the members on resolutions proposed to be considered at the EOGM from Monday, 
March 23, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. to Wednesday, March 25, 2026 at 5:00 p.m .. Further, the 
facility for e-voting during the EOGM was also provided to the Members of the Company
