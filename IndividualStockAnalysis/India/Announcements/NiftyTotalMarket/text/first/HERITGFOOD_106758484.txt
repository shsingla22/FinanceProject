Press  Release  
  
Heritage Foods Executive Director Ms. Brahmani Nara Recognised in 
Fortune India’s 40 Under 40, 2026  
 
Hyderabad, A ugust 27 , 2026:  Heritage Foods Limited is pleased to announce that Ms. Brahmani 
Nara, Executive Director , Heritage Foods Limited, has been recognised among Fortune India’s 40 
Under 40, 2026 . The recognition was presented at an award ceremony held in Mumbai  on August  26, 
2026, acknowledging her contribution to the Company’s growth and evolution in India’s dairy sector.  
  
Commenting on the recognition, Ms. Brahmani Nara, Executive Director, Heritage Foods Limited , 
said, “I am deeply honoured by this recognition. More than a personal milestone, I see it as a reflection 
of the people at Heritage Foods who make the work happen every day. The work I am most proud of 
happens far from headquarters in the fields with our farmers , in our plants and in the everyday effort 
to bridge the demand -supply gap in India’s dairy sector.  This recognition reflects that collective effort 
and the dedication of everyone who contributes to Heritage Foods’ journey.”  
 
Over the past year, Heritage Foods has strengthened its focus on value -added dairy products, 
including ghee, paneer, curd, butter and flavoured milk. The Company’s  Heritage Innovation 
framework  supports the identification, development and scaling of new ideas, enabling the Company 
to respond to evolving consumer preferences and build a stronger portfolio across the dairy value 
chain.  
 
The recognition comes as Heritage Foods continues to focus on portfolio expansion, innovation and 
execution , while deepening engagement with its farmer and consumer ecosystems. The Company 
remains committed to building differentiated products and strengthening its brands to create 
sustainable, long -term value for all stakeholders.  
 
About Heritage Foods Limited:  
Heritage Foods Limited (NSE: HERITGFOOD; BSE: 519552) is one of India’s leading dairy companies, 
founded in 1992 with a vision to provide high quality, nutritious milk and dairy products to families 
across the nation and to empower farmers. Over the years,  Heritage has grown into a trusted  brand,  
known  for its commitment  to purity,  freshness,  and  sustainability. With operations spanning 13 
states, Heritage Foods serves millions of consumers, offering  a wide range of milk, curd, butter, 
paneer, and other value -added dairy products.
