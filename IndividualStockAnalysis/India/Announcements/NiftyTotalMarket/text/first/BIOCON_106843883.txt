BRIEF PROFILE - MUKESH RATHI 
 
Mukesh Rathi brings over 27 years of leadership experience across digital 
transformation, AI-led innovation, process excellence, and technology 
management. 
 
Prior to joining us, he served as Group Chief Digital Officer at Hinduja Group, 
where he spearheaded the Group’s enterprise-wide digital and AI strategy. Before 
that, he spent over a decade at Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories in senior leadership roles, 
including Global Chief Digital and Information Officer, driving enterprise-wide 
transformation, strengthening process excellence, and leveraging technology to 
accelerate business performance and innovation. 
 
Earlier in his career, Mukesh held leadership and consulting roles at Hewlett Packard, SAP, and Unilever, 
building a strong foundation in technology, business transformation, and operational excellence.
