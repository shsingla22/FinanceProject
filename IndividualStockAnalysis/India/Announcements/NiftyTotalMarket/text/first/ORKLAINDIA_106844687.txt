Orkla India delivers broad -based Q2 growth; revenue rises 19.4% and PAT 
grows 19.2%  
 
Bengaluru,  4th November 2026:  Orkla India Limited, a portfolio company of Orkla ASA , today 
announced its unaudited consolidated financial results for the quarter and half year ended 30 
September 2026. Revenue from operations for Q2 ’FY27 grew 19.4% year on year to ₹776 crore, while 
revenue from sale  of products increased 18.4%, with volume growth of 2.9%. This builds on the 
growth momentum established in the first quarter, when revenue from operations grew 10.4%.  
 
Growth was broad -based across categories , geographies , and channel . Spices revenue increased 
25.1% to ₹498 crore, while Convenience Foods grew 7.7% to ₹271 crore, despite the shift in the 
festive calendar to Q3. Domestic revenue rose 20.0% to ₹623 crore, while international revenue grew 
11.7% to ₹145 crore.  
 
EBITDA for the quarter was ₹124 crore, up 12.6% year on year, with a margin of 15.9%. Profit after 
tax (PAT) increased 19.2% to ₹91 crore. For H1 FY27, revenue from operations grew 15.1% to ₹1,435 
crore, EBITDA increased 7.8% to ₹239 crore and PAT was ₹179  crore  before exceptional items (net of 
tax) was 178 crores , up 14.4% .

Key Business Highlights for Q 2’FY27  
• Revenue from operations grew 19.4% to ₹776 crore; product sales increased 18.4%, with volume 
growth of 2.9%.  
• EBITDA rose 12.6% to ₹124 crore with resilient EBITDA margins at 15.9% despite elevated input 
costs  and PAT grew 19.2% to ₹91 crore . 
• Spices revenue increased 25.1% to ₹498 crore. Convenience Foods grew 7.7% to ₹271 crore, 
despite the shift in the festive calendar.  
• Domestic revenue rose 20.0% to ₹623 crore, while International revenue grew 11.7% to ₹145 
crore  despite volatile geopolitical environment . 
• Orkla India introduced 15 innovations. Digital commerce grew 23.0% year on year, contributing 
10.0% of domestic revenue; the channel grew 33.6% outside South India .

 Strategic Initiatives  
 
• Building a pan -India growth platform  largely through digital commerce : Orkla India is combining 
portfolio innovation with digital commerce to extend its reach beyond South India. Digital 
commerce grew 23% year on year in Q2 and contributed 10% of domestic revenue, with the 
channel growing 33.6% outside South India. Project Bo lt has helped build the capabilities 
supporting this expansion.  
• Strengthening portfolio relevance: The company introduced 15 innovations during the quarter, 
broadening consumer choices and buying occasions. Its innovation agenda is focused on portfolio 
diversification and staying relevant to evolving consumers, including Gen Z.  
• Kerala distribution restructu ring: The project continues to progress as planned with the 
implemented initiatives already showing encouraging results. As highlighted earlier, this is a 
multi -quarter transformation, and we expect the benefits to build progressively  
 
 
Commenting on the performance, Sanjay Sharma, Managing Director & CEO, Orkla India , said,  “Q2 
marks another step in building a stronger, more relevant food business with reach across India. Broad 
based growth reflects the strength of our brands and the discipline of our execution. We see headroom 
both in our core market s and in new markets unlocked by Digital commerce expanding our reach to 
Gen Z consumers . 
With festive activity extending into Q3, we expect the coming quarter to capture a significant share of 
seasonal demand. Our focus remains on sustaining this momentum and creating long -term value 
through disciplined execution.”  
About  Orkla  India  
Orkla India Limited is a leading multi -category food company. It is a collection of heritage brands 
offering a diverse range of products under the well -loved MTR, Eastern and Rasoi Magic brands. Its 
portfolio  spans  multiple  categories,  including blended  and pure  spices  and convenience foods  such  
as ready -to-cook (RTC), ready -to-eat (RTE) meals, vermicelli, and more - delivering quality and 
authenticity to consumers across meal occasions.
