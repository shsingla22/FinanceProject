Sub.: Addendum to the Notice of 38th Annual General Meeting of Bajaj Finance Limited  
 
Bajaj Finance Limited (the “ Company ”) had issued a Notice dated 29 April 2025  (“AGM 
Notice ”) for convening the 38th Annual General Meeting (“ AGM ”) of the Shareholders of the 
Company scheduled to be held on Thursday, the 24th day of July, 2025 at 3:30 P.M. (IST) at 
Hotel Hyatt Regency, Viman Nagar, Opposite Weikfield IT Citi Info Park, Nagar Road, Pune – 
411014. The AGM Notice has already been disp atched to all the Shareholders of the Company 
in compliance with applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.  
 
As stated in the AGM Notice, the Company has provided its Shareholders  the facility to cast 
their votes electronically through remote e -voting, which commenced on Sunday, 20 July 
2025 at 9:00 a.m. (IST) and will continue until 5:00 p.m. (IST) on Wednesday, 23 July 2025. 
The Company has also enabled voting at the AGM venue.  
 
However, to ensure that the Shareholders  are apprised of the latest material developments 
affecting the business to be transacted at the AGM, the Company considers it necessary to 
issue this notice (“ Addendum ”) to inform the Shareholders  of a development which has 
occurred with respect to Agenda Item No. 3 of the AGM Notice. Accordingly, this Addendum 
is being issued to inform the Shareholders  the following update:  
 
Subsequent to the dispatch of the AGM Notice, Mr. Anup Kumar Saha, has tendered his 
resignation for personal reasons as the Managing Director and Director of the Company 
which has been duly noted and accepted by the Board of Directors at its meeting held o n                 
21 July 2025. The resignation is effective from the close of business hours on 21 July 2025, 
and appropriate disclosure in this regard has been made by the Company to the stock 
exchanges on 21 July 2025, in accordance with the SEBI (Listi ng Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
