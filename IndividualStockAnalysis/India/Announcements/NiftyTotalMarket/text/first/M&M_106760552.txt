Press Note  
 
Mahindra expands Battery -as-a-Service across its entire  
Electric Origin SUV portfolio  
Mumbai, August 28, 2026:  Mahindra today announced the expansion of its Battery -as-a-Service 
(BaaS)  program me across its entire Electric Origin SUV portfolio, making it available on the BE 6 
SPORTEQ, XEV 9S and XEV 9e . 
Introduced with the launch of the BE 6 SPORTEQ , BaaS makes EV ownership more accessible by 
enabling customers to finance the vehicle and battery separately through a unique dual -financing 
structure.  
Under BaaS, customers can pay ₹11.45 Lakh# for the BE 6 SPORTEQ  and finance the battery at an 
effective usage cost of ₹3.75/km#. This significantly lowers the upfront purchase cost while 
providing customers greater flexibility in managing their monthly outflow , offering a smarter way 
to own an EV.  
The program me has received an overwhelming response from customers across the country, 
validating the demand for ownership solutions that lower the barriers to EV adoption. Building on 
this feedback, Mahindra is now extending BaaS across all variants of the BE 6 SPORTEQ  and 
introducing it to its flagship Electric Origin SUVs, the XEV 9S  and the XEV 9e . 
As part of this BaaS programme,  
• XEV 9S  now starts at ₹12.65 Lakh# with battery financing  at an effective usage cost of  
₹3.75/km# 
• XEV 9e  now starts at ₹13.90 Lakh# with battery financing  at an effective usage cost of 
₹3.75/km# 
• BaaS is now available across all variants of the BE 6 SPORTEQ, XEV 9S and XEV 9e  
This expansion marks another important step in Mahindra's mission to simplify EV adoption by 
lowering entry barriers and putting customer choice at the centre of the ownership experience , 
through smarter and flexible ownership options.   
#BaaS (Battery -as-a-Service) / Dual Loan is a finance product offered by the respective financier and is not a pay -per-use 
program. Finance approval is solely at the discretion of the finance partner and subject to applicable terms and conditions. BaaS 
prices mentioned are based on a Battery EMI  starting at  ₹6 975 at an effective usage cost of  ₹3.75/km, considering 60 km/day 
usage and a specific down payment, and exclude charging, maintenance, and repair costs. Battery EMI will vary as per battery 
size, downpayment and tenure. BaaS prices are exclusive of charger cost, road tax, insurance, TCS,  and other applicable statutory 
levies. BaaS is for personal use only.
