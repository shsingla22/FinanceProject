Thomas Cook India strengthens footprint in Gujarat with new outlet in 
Ahmedabad 
Expands consumer access to 12 locations across the State  
 
Mumbai,  August 10, 202 6: With a strategic focus on Gujarat’s strong and growing travel market, Thomas Cook 
(India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel travel services company, has inaugurated its new outlet in 
Ahmedabad’s Nikol area. The outlet expands Thomas Cook India’s network to 5  locations in the city and 12  
across Gujarat, strengthening access to its comprehensive portfolio of travel and holiday solutions.  
 
Ahmedabad is a key source market for Gujarat and the wider West India region, with travellers increasingly 
exploring new destinations and experiences through multiple short breaks, weekend getaways and public -holiday 
travel. The market also has a strong preference for group travel, regional departures and curated experiences 
tailored to local cultural and dietary preferences.  
 The new outlet will cater to families, couples, millennials  & GenZ, business travellers and GenS ( seniors ), 
offering end- to-end solutions including domestic and international holidays, group tours, personalised holidays, 
cruises and travel insurance. Thomas Cook India’s AI -powered booking platform further enables customers to 
plan personalised vacations, check availability and make reservations seamlessly.  
 
Ahmedabad -Gujarat Travel Trends : 
• Year -round travel: Gujarati travellers are increasingly using public holidays and weekends for multiple 
short breaks and mini -cations beyond the traditional  holiday season.  
• Strong preference for group travel: Gujarati -specific group tours remain popular, with travellers 
valuing Gujarati -speaking Tour Managers and vegetarian/Jain food preferences.  
• Gujarati -specific regional and festive tours: Departures such as Albelu Europe, Aneru Europe and 
Adhbhut Asia are gaining popularity, offering curated experiences with Gujarati language and cuisine 
preferences.  
• Growing spiritual tourism: Pilgrimage and spiritual travel continue to gain momentum, with Thomas 
Cook India  strengthening its Spiritual Tourism portfolio to cater to this segment.  
• Unique experiences and new destinations: Travellers are increasingly seeking differentiated 
experiences and exploring emerging destinations beyond conventional itineraries.  
• New connectivity- led opportunities : Improved air connectivity is opening up new outbound 
opportunities, with Sri Lanka  emerging as an attractive option. Thomas Cook India has introduced 
special packages with direct flights to Sri Lanka ex-Ahmedabad, offering greater  convenience to 
travellers.
