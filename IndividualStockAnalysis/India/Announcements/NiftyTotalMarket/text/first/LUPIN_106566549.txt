Lupin  Receives  Tentative Approval  from  U.S. FDA  for Pitolisant Tablets  
 
Mumbai,  India,  Naples,  Fl, March 25, 202 6: Global pharma major Lupin Limited (Lupin) today 
announced that it has received tentative approval from the United States Food and Drug Administration 
(U.S. FDA)  for its Abbreviated New Drug Application for Pitolisant Tablets , 4.45mg and 17.8 mg. This 
product would be manufactured at Lupin’s Nagpur facility  in India.  
 
The U.S. FDA has tentatively  approved Lupin’s Pitolisant Tablets 4.45mg and 17.8 mg as bioequivalent to 
Wakix®  for the indication in the appro ved labeling .
