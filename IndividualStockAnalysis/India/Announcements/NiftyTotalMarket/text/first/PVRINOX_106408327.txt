 PVR INOX FOCO EXPANDS TO SILIGURI – ‘THE GATEWAY TO THE NORTHEAST’ WITH A STATE -
OF-THE-ART ALL -LASER 4 -SCREEN MULTIPLEX  
~ PVR INOX Elevates Siliguri ’s Entertainment Landscape with an All -Laser 4 -Screen Premium FOCO 
Multiplex at ML Acropolis Mall, opening  on October 15, 2025, Featuring 4K Laser Projection, Dolby 7.1 
Sound, and Plush Recliners ~  
National, 15 October  2025 : PVR INOX, India’s largest and most premium multiplex chain, today 
announced the launch of its new all-laser  cinema in Siliguri at ML Acropolis Mall, developed under the 
company’s successful FOCO (Franchise Owned, Company Operated)  model. This marks PVR INO X’s third 
cinema in Siliguri, expanding its presence in the city to 12 screens  and further cementing its leadership in 
the East India market.  
Strategically located on Sevoke Road , a key arterial route connecting Siliguri to the upper Himalayas 
including Darjeeling, Kurseong, and Kalimpong , ML Acropolis Mall stands at the heart of Siliguri’s 
commercial and residential hub. Surrounded by popular brands like Shoppers Stop, Kama Ayurveda, 
Nykaa, KFC, and Wow! Momo , the mall attracts steady footfalls from nearby  residential areas within a 
1.5 km radius . With Siliguri Airport just 17 km away and Anandaloke Hospital located opposite, it serves 
as a convenient and vibrant entertainment destination for the city.  
Spread across 25,780 sq. ft. , the new property features 4 mainstream auditoriums  with a total seating 
capacity of 931 seats , including 824 plush sliders  and 107 luxury recliners . Each auditorium has been 
uniquely designed with a distinct color palette —Blue, Olive, Mocha,  and Pink —to enhance the overall 
visual aesthetics and ambiance.  
The new cinema is powered by cutting -edge technology, featuring 4K Laser Projection in one auditorium  
and 2K Laser Projection in three others , making it a true all-laser multiplex . Two auditoriums are 
equipped with Dolby Atmos , delivering immersive, multidimensional sound, while the remaining two 
feature Dolby 7.1 Surround Sound  for crystal -clear audio precision. Next -Gen 3D technology  brings 
unparalleled depth and realism to every frame, enhancing the ove rall cinematic experience. For an added 
touch of indulgence, the Celebrity Recliners are designed with premium leatherette upholstery, USB 
charging ports, swivel tables for dine -in convenience, and motorized reclining mechanisms —ensuring 
comfort that compl ements the on -screen spectacle.  
Mr. Ajay Bijli , Managing Director, PVR INOX Limited  “We are delighted to bring the PVR INOX experience 
to ML Acropolis Mall, marking our third cinema in Siliguri. The city has always shown an appetite for high -
quality cinema  experiences, and this new property  developed under our FOCO model  reflects our 
commitment to expanding our footprint while ensuring every guest enjoys comfort, technology, and design 
excellence. As we continue to grow in East India, our focus remains on d elivering world -class entertainment 
experiences that redefine the moviegoing culture.”  
Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli, Executive  Director, PVR INOX Limited , added, “Siliguri is a cornerstone of PVR 
INOX ’s East India growth strategy, and with our third property in  the city, we are further strengthening our 
FOCO presence in this high -potential market. This expansion allows us to bring premium cinematic 
experiences to more audiences while consolidating our leadership in the region. ” 
 
The foyer  of PVR INOX Acropolis Mall is designed to be welcoming, vibrant, and functional  balancing 
premium aesthetics with an intuitive layout. Smooth circulation between ticketing, concessions, and 
waiting areas enhances convenience, while layered lighting, refin ed finishes, and symmetrical design 
elements create a premium and cohesive brand experience.  
The concession area features an extensive food menu , while the washrooms  are finished with premium 
flooring and contrasting wall textures , aligning with the overal l upscale environment.  
The launch of PVR INOX Acropolis Mall, Siliguri, follows the company’s recent FOCO property openings in 
Raipur and Jabalpur , further underscoring its strategic focus on expanding through the FOCO model 
across emerging Tier -II and Tie r-III markets.

About  PVR INOX  Limited  
PVR INOX  Limited  is the largest  film exhibition  company  in India  with  1,761  Screens  across

and setting benchmarks in the film exhibition  industry.  With  a collective  heritage  of
