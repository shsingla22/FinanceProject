Tejas  Networks  partners  with  MahaIT  to nurture  telecom engineering  talent in 
Maharashtra 
Bengaluru , May 21, 2026: Tejas  Networks  [BSE:  540595,  NSE:  TEJASNET]  today  announced  
that it has signed  a Memorandum  of Understanding  (MoU) with  the Maharashtra  Information  
Technology  Corporation  Limited  (MahaIT)  to jointly  impart  world -class  training  and 
certification  in emerging  telecom  technologies  to engineering  students  across  Maharashtra,  
especially  in the underserved  regions  of the state . The MoU  was signed  in the presence  of 
Shri Devendra  Fadnavis , Hon’ble  Chief  Minister  of Maharashtra,  and Adv.  Ashish  Shelar,  
Hon'ble  Minister  of IT, Government  of Maharashtra alongside  other  esteemed  dignitaries  at 
the ‘TechMahaImpacT  2026’ , Maharashtra's  premier CSR & Technology  Conclave , held  in 
Mumbai  yesterday . 
 MahaIT  serves  as the nodal  agency  of the Government  of Maharashtra  for the efficient  
implementation  of its Information  and Communications  Technology  (ICT) initiatives  including  
prestigious  projects  such  as Aaple  Sarkar,  Direct  Benefits  Transfer  (DBT),  Mahanet  
(Bharatnet),  Smart  City among  others.  The purpose  of the MoU  is to jointly  enable  skilling  and 
certification  of engineering  students,  especially  from  Tier-2, Tier-3, and underserved  regions  
of Maharashtra,  in emerging  telecom  and digital  technologies  through  online  and instructor -
led training  programs.  The MoU  also aims  to explore  joint  areas  of collaboration  in skilling , 
industry -academia engagement,  and technology -led capacity building  in association  with  
Maha  IT. 
Sanjay  Katkar, IAS, Managing  Director of MahaIT  said, “We are delighted  to partner with  Tejas 
Networks  to accelerate  the training  and capacity -building  of telecommunication  engineers  in 
Maharashtra.  This collaboration  marks  a significant  step  towards  realizing  the vision  of a 
modern  and inclusive  “Digital  Maharashtra”.  As a key technology  supplier  for several  pan-
India  public  networks  of national importance  such  as BSNL  4G/5G,  BharatNet,  National  
Knowledge  Network,  utility  and defence  networks , Tejas  is the ideal  partner  for this important  
initiative .” 
Arnob  Roy,  Managing  Director  and Chief  Executive  Officer  of Tejas  Networks  said,  “The 
availability  of world -class  engineering  talent  skilled  in emerging  telecom  technologies  is 
critical to realizing  Government  of Maharashtra’s  vision  to transform  the state  to a trillion -
dollar economy  in the coming  years . We are committed  to advance  the state’s  efforts  in this 
area through our high -quality  skilling  and certification  programs  that are designed  to enhance  
the employability  and industry  readiness  of young  engineers  both in urban and rural  areas .”   
Sunil Handoo , Vice  President  (Sales)  of Tejas  Networks  said,  “Maha IT’s ICT initiatives  have  
been  powering  Maharashtra’s  emergence  as a leader  state  in digital governance  by leverag ing 
advanced  technolog ies to improve  the quality  of public  services . We are proud to collaborate  
with  MahaIT  and contribute  our technology  expertise  to further  strengthen these  initiatives  
and help  develop  a future -ready  engineering  workforce  in the state .”
