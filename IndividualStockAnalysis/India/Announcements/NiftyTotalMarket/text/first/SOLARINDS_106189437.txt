Sub: Visit of Hon’ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi ji to Solar Defence and Aerospace Limited 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
We are pleased to inform you that, the Hon’ble Prime Minister of India  Shri Narendra Modi ji, visited the 
manufacturing and testing facilities of our wholly owned subsidiary, Solar Defence and Aerospace Limited, on 
March 30, 2025.  
 
During the visit, the Hon’ble Prime Minister inaugurated the Loitering Munition Testing Range and a 
specialized runway facility for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs),  marking a significant milestone in India’s 
defence and aerospace capabilities. This initiative aligns with the Government of India’s focus on ‘Atmanirbhar 
Bharat’ and indigenous defence production.  
 
We remain committed to supporting the nation’s vision for self -reliance in defence manufacturing and 
technological advancements. 
 
Thanking You,
