A Collector ’s Basmati : LT Foods i ntroduces DAAWAT® Saffron Basmati  
for connoisseurs  
 
New Delhi (India), 17th March  2026 : LT Foods Ltd, a leading 
billion -dollar global FMCG company in the consumer food space, 
has launched DAAWAT® Saffron Basmati Rice, a limited ‑edition 
basmati offering  for connoisseurs  who value the finest culinary 
experience. Infused with the finest saffron , sourced exclusively 
from Jammu  & Kashmir (J&K) , this is a first -of-its-kind innovation 
in the basmati category, reinforcing LT Foods ’ continued 
commitment to delivering an exceptional food experience.  
 
The rice is cultivated on farms fed by t he Chenab Ri ver , where 
farmers nurture each grain using time ‑honoured, pesticide ‑free 
methods passed down through generations. Every grain is hand -
selected with meticulous care, preserving the natural aroma, 
purity, and authenticity of traditional basmati cultivation.   
 
DAAWAT® Saffron Basmati Rice is a rare and  collectable culinary 
offering. Each limited -edition pack is designed as a luxury experience, containing vacuum -sealed saffron -
infused basmati rice , a handcrafted jute storage bag, a personalised note by the farmer with a certified 
pack number , and curated cooking instructions —transforming the product into a keepsake as much as a 
culinary indulgence.  
 
This rare edition is reserved exclusively for true connoisseurs  and is only available through an expression 
of interest on our website at: https://www.daawat.com/saffron -rice/   
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr . Ritesh Arora, CEO – India Business & Far East, LT Foods Ltd., said, “The 
introduction of DAAWAT® Saffron Basmati Rice is a strategic step in strengthening our premium portfolio 
and enhancing value creation across the premium basmati ecosystem . As custodians of a legacy built 
over seven decades, we continue to invest in innovations that strengthen the  premium end of the 
portfolio while deepening the value we create for consumers and stakeholders. This limited ‑edition 
offering is an embodiment of our long -term vision —where authenticity, p roduct excellence and 
exclusivity c ome together to reinforce DAAWAT®’s leadership and set new category benchmarks. With 
this launch, we are not only celebrating heritage but also shaping the future trajector y of premium 
basmati worldwide. ”
