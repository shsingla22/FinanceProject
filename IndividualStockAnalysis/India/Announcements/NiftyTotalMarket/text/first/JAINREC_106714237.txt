Subject:   Intimation of Permission to Resume Manufacturing Operations at Manufacturing Facility  
 
This is in continuation to our letter dated July 15, 2026, wherein the Company had informed the Stock Exchanges regarding the  
Prohibition Order issued under Section 40(2) of the Factories Act, 1948 by the Joint Director -I, Industrial Safety and Health, Che nnai, 
Tamil Nadu, in connection with the fire incident that occurred on July 14, 2026 at the Company's Unit -II manufacturing facility 
situated at Plot Nos. R1, R2 and R3, SIPCOT Industrial Complex, Pappankuppam Village, Gummidipoondi  Taluk, Tiruvallur District 
– 601 201, Tamil Nadu.  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you 
that the Director of Industrial Safety and Health, Chennai, acting as the Appellate Authority under Section 107 of the Factor ies Act, 
1948, vide Order No . C3/18838/2026 dated July 27, 2026, has permitted the Company to recommence manufacturing operations 
at its Unit -II manufacturing facility, except for the shed affected by the fire incident on July 14, 2026 . 
 
The said order has been passed pursuant to a review petition filed by the Occupier of the Company and upon consideration of the 
inspection report submitted by the Additional Director, Industrial Safety and Health, Chennai, following a re -inspection conducted 
on July 25, 2026. The inspection report confirmed that the deficiencies identified in the Prohibition Order in respect of the 
manufacturing areas of Unit -II for which resumption of operations has been permitted under the said order have been satisfactorily 
rectified and the  requisite safety measures have been implemented.  
 
Renovation and restoration activities in respect of the manufacturing shed affected by the fire incident are in full swing, a nd the 
Company is taking all necessary steps to complete the requisite statutory and safety -related compliances at the earliest. Th e 
Company will periodically update the progress of the renovation works and the regulatory approvals required for recommencemen t 
of manufacturing operations in the said shed.

letter.
