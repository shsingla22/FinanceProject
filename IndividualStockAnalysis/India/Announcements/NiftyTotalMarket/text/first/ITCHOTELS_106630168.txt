Dear Sirs,  
Acquisition  of shares  - Zuri Hotels and Resorts Private Limited  
Further to our letter dated 15th May, 2026, we write to advise that the Company has  
today i.e., 19th May, 2026 acquired 100% of the share capital of Zuri Hotels and Resorts Private  
Limited (‘ZHRPL’).  Confirmation to this effect has been received by the Company  
today at 2:07 p.m. 
 
Consequently, ZHRPL has become a wholly owned subsidiary of ITC Hotels Limited with effect 
from 19th May, 2026.  
 
The details, as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with the SEBI  Master Circular dated
