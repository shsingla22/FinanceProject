Press release  
 
Pharma leaders drive conversations on GenAI, technology 
transformation and commercialization strategy at Indegene 
Digital Summit | October 17 -18, 2024 | Virtual -only 
 
 
Bengaluru, India / Princeton, United States, October 10, 2024: Indegene, a digital -first, life sciences 
commercialization company, announced the sixth edition of Indegene Digital Summit, one of the most 
anticipated events for pharma leaders across the world, on October 17 and 18, 2024.  
 
The virtual summit brings together senior pharma executives and tech innovators to discuss strategies and best practices to make the pharma industry future- ready. This year’s edition offers practitioners' 
perspectives on key topics such as Generative AI, A dvanced Analytics, Technology Transformation and 
Operations Transformation.  
 
With no vendors or booths, the summit continues to facilitate an open exchange of ideas, foster collaboration, and promote thought leadership.  
 Check out the detailed agenda and speaker  info at:  https://digitalsummit.indegene.com/  
 
Keynote speakers include renowned pharma CXOs:  
 
• Olivier Charmeil, EVP, General Medicines, Sanofi  
• Emmanuel Caeymaex, Chief Commercial Officer, UCB 
• Adele M. Gulfo, CEO, Biopharma Commercial Unit, Sumitomo Pharma America, Inc  
• Suneet  Varma, Global Oncology & US President, Pfizer  
• Alok Sonig, EVP and Group President, Pharmaceuticals, Baxter  
• Dave Lennon, President and CEO, Aadi Bioscience  
 “In a GenAI world, pharma commercialization leaders are at crossroads, navigating important decisions 
in processes and technology to build resilient, future -ready organizations,” said Gaurav Kapoor, 
Executive Vice President of Indegene. “This year, Indegen e Digital Summit brings together pharma and 
digital experts to enable the industry to cut through the noise and chart a transformative path with a multi- decadal perspective.”  
 Bookmark these sessions:  
 
• Unlocking Gen AI's potential in life sciences beyond productivity and efficiency  
• Marketing Ops 2.0 – Driving commercial excellence through advanced content and campaign 
capabilities  
• Scaling up commercial and medical services in GCCs with technology  
• Leveraging AI readiness activities to drive better omnichannel effectiveness and efficiency  
• State of adoption of Generative AI in PV: The emerging picture
