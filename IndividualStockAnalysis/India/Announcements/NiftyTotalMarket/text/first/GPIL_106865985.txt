We are pleased to inform you that Godawari Power & Ispat Limited (the “Company”) has 
received regulatory permission from the Office of the Divisional Forest Officer, East 
Bhanupatrapur Forest Division, Bhanupatrapur, Chhattisgarh, vide Letter No. 8677 dated 18.11. 
2026 on 19.11.2026 (i.e. Today), allowing tree felling over an aggregate area of 60.84 hectares of 
land allotted to the Company for dumping of overburden/ mines refuse at its Ari Dongri Iron Ore 
Mines, situated at Village Parrekodo, Tehsil Durgkondal, District: Kanker, Chhattisgarh. 
 
The Company will undertake the necessary ground clearance activities, strictly in accordance with 
the conditions prescribed by the Forest Department and other applicable statutory requirements, 
which are expected to be completed over the next 3-4 weeks, following which, the Company 
expects to gradually increase its iron ore mining operations at full capacity of 6 million tons per 
annum. 
 
This development is expected to facilitate the restoration of normal mining operations and ensure 
sustained availability of iron ore for the Company's captive requirements.
