Jupiter Wagons Bags Order Worth Over Rs. 97.66 Crore from GATX 
India Private Limited  
National: September 8, 2026: Jupiter Wagons Limited, a provider of comprehensive mobility 
solutions encompassing rail, road and marine transportation, has secured a significant order from 
GATX India Private Limited , a subsidiary of GATX Corporation, USA, further strengthening Jupiter 
Wagons' presence in India's private wagon leasing ecosystem.  
Jupiter Wagons has signed a Rs. 97.66 crore (incl. applicable tax ) Wagon Purchase Agreement with 
GATX India Private Limited  for the manufacture and supply of BFNS22.9  rakes  
The order adds to Jupiter Wagons' growing footprint with global wagon leasing players and reflects the Company's continued alignment with the Indian Railways' Wagon Leasing Scheme, which enables private players such as GATX to lease and operate rolling sto ck on the Indian Railways network.  
Commenting on the development, Mr. Vivek Lohia, Managing Director, Jupiter Wagons Limited,  
said:  
“This agreement with GATX India further strengthens our position in India's private wagon leasing 
landscape and reflects the confidence global players continue to place in Jupiter Wagons' engineering 
and delivery capabilities. As leasing companies expand t heir presence on the Indian Railways network, 
the demand for specialise d wagons such as the BFNS22.9 continues to grow. We remain committed to 
supporting this evolving ecosystem through consistent execution, robust quality systems, and reliable 
long- term partnerships.”  
This order reinforces Jupiter Wagons' role as a preferred manufacturing partner for global wagon leasing 
companies operating in India and reflects the Company's ability to deliver specialised, high- payload 
rolling stock at scale. Backed by its integrated m anufacturing footprint and consistent execution track 
record, Jupiter Wagons remains focused on deepening its presence across India's evolving private 
wagon ownership and leasing landscape.
