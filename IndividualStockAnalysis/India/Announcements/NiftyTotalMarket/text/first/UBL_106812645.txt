Heineken® Silver Launches in Assam  
 
India, October 9, 2026 – United Breweries Limited (UBL), part of the HEINEKEN Company, announced 
the launch of Heineken® Silver in Assam. The brand is now available in 11 markets, reflecting the 
growing demand for premium and international beer experiences among Indian consumer s. 
 
Consumers are increasingly seeking beers that deliver quality, refreshment, and a smooth drinking 
experience. The strong response to Heineken® Silver across existing markets has reinforced its appeal among consumers looking for a contemporary premium lager.  
 
Heineken® Silver is brewed using only three natural ingredients, barley, hops and water, alongside 
Heineken®’s signature A- Yeast and a distinctive horizontal brewing process. This unique brewing 
method contributes to its smooth, crisp and subtle taste prof ile, making it a refreshing and easy -to-drink 
lager.  
 
Vikram Bahl, Chief Marketing Officer, United Breweries Ltd , said, “The continued expansion of 
Heineken® Silver reflects the growing consumer preference for premium beer offerings across India. 
Assam is an important market, with consumers increasingly exploring premium beer choices. With our 
entry into the state, we are making Heineken®  Silver available to more consumers, bringing its smooth 
and refreshing taste experience to the market.”  
 
The company continues to drive premiumization through its extensive pan -India manufacturing 
network, strong portfolio of brands, and innovation -led growth strategy. Building on the launch of 
Heineken® Silver in Kerala, Odisha and Madhya Pradesh, UBL is accelerating the brand’s availability 
across key markets in India.  
 
Heineken Silver is available in Assam, priced at INR 120 for a 330 ml pint, INR 160 for a 500ml can and 
INR 200 for a 650ml bottle across retail outlets.
