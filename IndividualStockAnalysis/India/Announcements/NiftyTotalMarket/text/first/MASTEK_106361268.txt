Mastek Ap points Sourabh Mukherjee as President of Americas  
Mumbai , India,  3 September  2025 : Mastek Inc, a step -down subs idiary of Mastek Limited (NSE: 
MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted AI -first digital engineering and cloud transformation partner,  today 
announce d the appointment of Mr. Sourabh Mukherjee as President, Americas , effective immediately. 
Sourabh will directly report to CEO Umang Nahata.  
Sourabh brings over two decades of global IT services experience across Healthcare & Life Sciences, 
Manufacturing, Supply Chain, Retail, and Consumer Goods, with a career spanning North America, the 
UK, Europe, APAC, and  South America. His deep expertise in digital product design for biopharma, cloud 
transformation, data and analytics, and cell/gene therapy has yielded impactful outcomes. He has 
successfully led hyper -growth initiatives, large -scale digital transformations, P&L management, strategic 
partner ships, and strengthened C -suite engagement while enhancing customer experience.  
Umang Nahata, CEO, Mastek , said , “Sourabh’s brings rich history of leadership and diverse domain 
expertise, particul arly in Healthcare &  Life Sciences . His proven ability to buil d client -centric businesses 
positions  him well to lead the next phase of growth in the Americas. We are pleased to welcome him on 
board and look forward to his fresh  perspectives in further shaping Mastek’s growth strategy in the 
region.”  
Prior to joining Mastek, Sourabh spent a decade at Cognizant , where he built the Health Sciences 
Strategic Business Unit on the US West Coast . Earlier, he held leadership roles at Wipro  across delivery, 
pre-sales, and sales leadership in multiple continents. He has earne d distinguished  accolades, including 
the Americas President Circle Award (2021), Mission Impossible Award (2021), Life Sciences BU Leader Awards (2019, 2020), and the Hyper Growth Account  & Client Partner Award (2018).   
 “Mastek, being a trusted digital and cloud transformation partner, presents immense opportunities in 
the Americas. As a newly joined Mastekeer, I am excited to be part of a culture where our people and customers are of utmost value . I look forward to strengthening our presence in the Americas by 
delivering meaningful outcomes to our clients and unlocking new growth opportunities,”  said Sourabh 
Mukherjee, President, Americas, Mastek.  
Sourabh holds a BE in Computer Science, an MBA in Marketing & Information Sys tems, and has 
completed program s in Genetics & Genomics at Stanford University and Manufacturing Process 
Excellence at MIT.
