PRESS RELEASE  
Paradeep Phosphates Limited Expands its Crop- Speciﬁc Nutrition Portfolio with the Launch of JAI 
KISAAN NAVRATNA CROPFIT for Sugarcane Cultivation in Karnataka  – New  customized fertilizer 
designed to deliver balanced major, secondary and micronutrient nutrition for sugarcane  
Karnataka | 20 August 2026: Paradeep Phosphates Limited (PPL), one of India’s leading integrated agri-
nutrient companies, today announced the launch of JAI KISAAN NAVRATNA CROPFIT , a scientifically  
formulated customized fertilizer developed specifically for sugarcane cultivation in Karnataka .  
The launch reinforces PPL’s commitment to advancing crop -specific and balanced plant nutrition by 
offering farmers solutions that address the actual nutritional requirements of individual crops, while 
improving nutrient use efficiency, crop productivity and farm economics.  
Addressing the Nutritional Needs of Sugarcane  
Sugarcane is a long- duration crop with high and sustained nutrient requirements. Imbalanced fertilizer 
practices, including excessive application of nitrogen and inadequate supplementation of secondary and micronutrients, can adversely affect nutrient use efficiency, crop performance and soil health.  
JAI KISAAN NAVRATNA CROPFIT  has been developed to address these challenges through a balanced 
combination of primary, secondary and micronutrients tailored to the nutritional requirements of sugarcane during its critical early growth stages . 
The product contains:  
• NPK:  14:14:13 
• Sulphur (S): 4% 
• Zinc (Zn):  1% 
• Boron (B): 0.1%  
• Iron (Fe):  1% 
• Application: Basal  
The precision -blended formulation is designed to provide a more comprehensive nutrient package 
through a single application, supporting uniform crop establishment and efficient nutrient availability.  
Designed for Better Nutrient Eﬃciency and Farm Productivity  
Field evaluations undertaken for the product indicate the potential of balanced crop nutrition to improve 
crop vigour, nutrient use efficiency and overall crop performance. In suitable agronomic conditions, 
adoption of balanced nutrition practices can contribute to higher cane yields, improved sugar recovery and better economic returns . 
JAI KISAAN NAVRATNA CROPFIT is designed to help farmers:  
• Improve nutrient use eﬃciency (NUE)  
• Promote healthier and more vigorous crop growth  
• Address deﬁciencies of key secondary and micronutrients 
 
• Support improved cane development and productivity  
• Potentially enhance sugar recovery  
• Optimize fertilizer expenditure through balanced nutrient application  
• Reduce nutrient losses associated with imbalanced fertilization practices  
With its uniform granule size and precision -blended formulation, the product is also designed to facilitate 
even  field distribution and efficient nutrient uptake . 
Scientiﬁcally Developed for Karnataka’s Sugarcane -Growing Regions  
JAI KISAAN NAVRATNA CROPFIT has been developed specifically for the nutritional requirements of 
sugarcane grown under the agro -climatic co nditions of Karnataka. The FCO-approved grade is 
recommended for sugarcane cultivation across key sugarcane -growing districts of the state, including 
Bagalkot, Belagavi, Ballari, Bidar, Chamarajanagara, Gadag, Kalaburagi, Koppal, Mandya, Mysuru, 
Vijayapura, Yadgir and Vijayanagara .
