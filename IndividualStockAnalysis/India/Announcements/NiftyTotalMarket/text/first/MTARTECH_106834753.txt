MTAR TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED 
 
MTAR reports another strong quarter, achieving its highest-ever 
quarterly revenue in Q2 FY27, with 200% YoY growth in 
revenue and 483.3% YoY growth in EBITDA  
Hyderabad, Oct 29, 2026. MTAR Technologies Ltd ( “MTAR ”), a leading manufacturer 
engaged in manufacturing and development of mission critical precision engineered systems 
catering to Clean Energy – Civil Nuclear Power, Fuel Cells, Hydro Power & Others, 
Aerospace and Defence sectors has announced its unaudited financial results for the second 
quarter of FY 27. 
 
 
YoY Q2 FY27 vs Q2 FY 26  
 
• Revenue from Operations stood at Rs. 406.8 Cr. in Q2 FY 27 as against Rs. 135.6 Cr. in  
Q2 FY 26, 200.0 % increase YoY 
• EBITDA reported at Rs. 99.2 Cr. in Q2 FY 27 as compared to Rs. 17.0 Cr. in Q2 FY 26 , 
483.3% increase YoY 
• Profit Before Tax stands at Rs. 80.3 Cr. in Q2 FY 27 as against Rs. 5.7 Cr. in Q2 FY 26, 
1314.6% increase YoY 
• Profit After Tax was at Rs. 59.8 Cr in Q2 FY 27 as against Rs. 4.2 Cr. in Q2 FY 26, 1309.5% 
increase YoY 
 
Q0Q Q2 FY 27 vs Q1 FY 27  
 
• Revenue from Operations stood at Rs. 406.8 Cr. in Q2 FY 27 as against Rs. 360.7 Cr. in 
Q1 FY 27, 12.8 % increase QoQ 
• EBITDA reported at Rs. 99.2 Cr. in Q2 FY 27 as compared to Rs. 85.1 Cr. in Q1 FY 27 , 
16.6% increase QoQ 
• Profit Before Tax stands at Rs. 80.3 Cr. in Q2 FY 27 as against Rs. 67.4 Cr. in Q1 FY 27, 
19.1% increase QoQ 
• Profit After Tax was at Rs. 59.8 Cr in Q2 FY 27 as against Rs. 50.2 Cr. in Q1 FY 27 , 19.1% 
increase QoQ 
 
 
  
 
 
Commenting on the results, Mr. Parvat Srinivas Reddy, Managing Director, MTAR 
Technologies,  said, “The Company continues to deliver strong quarterly performance, 
consistently surpassing the achievements of preceding quarters. We expect H2 FY27 to be 
stronger than H1, supported by a robust order book and sustained execution momentum, in line 
with our annual guidance. While we remain focused on strengthening our presence across key 
business segments, execution continues to be our key priority. Looking ahead, we remain 
confident of sustaining strong growth momentum over the coming years, supported by strong 
industry tailwinds across the sectors in which we operate.”  
 
 
About MTAR Technologies Ltd (www.mtar.in) BSE: 543270; NSE: MTARTECH 
MTAR has sixteen strategically based manufacturing units including an export-oriented unit
