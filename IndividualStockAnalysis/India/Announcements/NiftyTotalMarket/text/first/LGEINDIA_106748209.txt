LG Electronics wins big at DIGIT India's Top Appliance Awards 2026  
  10  Products recognized for excellence in Innovation Across TV, Refrigerator, Residential Air Conditioner, 
Washing Machine, Microwaves, Dishwasher  and Air Purifier Categories 
 
New Delhi, 18th August 2026: LG Electronics India Limited  (LGEIL) has been honoured with multiple 
product (10) wins at DIGIT India's Top Appliance Awards 2026, held at a gala ceremony in New Delhi. 
An initiative by Times Network and presented by digit.in India's oldest technology media brand, with over 
25 years of authority in independent product testing the awards  recognise excellence in home appliance 
innovation through India's first strictly data -driven evaluation mechanism for the category. The ceremony 
was graced by Dr. Jitendra Singh, Union Minister of State for Science & Technology, Earth Sciences, 
Prime Minister's Office (PMO), Personnel, Public Grievances & Pensions (PP/DoPT), and Atomic 
Energy & Space, as Chief Guest, alongside Brajesh Pathak, Deputy Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh, as 
the second Chief Guest of the evening.  
 
Unlike consumer-voted popularity contests, DIGIT India's Top Appliance Awards is built on a rigorous, 
lab-first evaluation process. Every nominated product is tested inside DIGIT's state -of-the-art laboratories 
in Mumbai and Noida, benchmarked against a ma trix of over 150 technical parameters ranging from 
thermal imaging diagnostics to the entire customer support cycle. This technical scoring is then reviewed 
by an independent expert jury panel, whose oversight forms the final layer of evaluation before win ners are 
selected. The awards span 36+ appliance categories and are amplified across DIGIT's editorial ecosystem, 
which reaches over 40 million consumers monthly across digital, video, print and social platforms.  
 
The jury comprised Tarun Pathak,  Research Director, Counterpoint  Research, Navkendar Singh, AVP, IDC 
and Manas Tiwari, Technology Editor, Times Network along with senior members of DIGIT's editorial  
team, who assessed nominated products on the basis of  laboratory testing data, real -world usability, and 
innovation relevance to Indian consumers.

Commenting on the recognition , Mr. Hong Ju Jeon, Managing Director, LG Electronics India  Limited 
said, "This recognition from DIGIT India's Top Appliance Awards reaffirms our commitment to bringing 
globally advanced, India -first innovation to every Indian household. Being evaluated through such a 
rigorous, lab-driven process — and validated by an independen t jury — is a strong endorsement of the 
reliability and performance our consumers have come to expect from LG  Electronics. We are grateful to 
DIGIT for introducing this much -needed benchmark for the appliance industry, and we remain committed 
to deepening our investment in technology and consumer insight tailored to India."   
 
The ceremony was attended by LG Electronics India Limited  senior leadership including Sanjay Chitkara 
Co-Chief Sales & Marketing Officer (CSMO ), alongside DIGIT's editorial and jury panels, over 100 tech 
opinion leaders, micro -influencers from the Digit Squad community, and Country Heads and CEOs from 
other winning appliance brands.  
 
The winning line-up reflects LG Electronics India Limited continued focus on combining global technology 
leadership with product design tailored to Indian household needs from convertible cooling and inverter -
compatible refrigeration to wash cycles built for local water conditions.  
 
 
 
About LG Electronics India Ltd                
LG Electronics India Limited (LGEIL) was established in January 1997 in India. It is focused on various 
consumer electronics and B2B businesses, from home appliances and media entertainment to HVAC and 
commercial displays. LGEIL's manufacturing units at Gr eater Noida and Ranjangaon, Pune has the capacity 
to manufacture LED TVs, air conditioners, commercial air conditioning systems, washing machines, 
refrigerators, and monitors.
