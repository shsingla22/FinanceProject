LEMON TREE HOTELS SIGNS KEYS LITE BY LEMON TREE HOTELS, 
MANALI  
 
Strengthens the group's presence across Himachal Pradesh  with a portfolio of 11 
hotels  
 
 
New Delhi, 5th August , 2026 :  Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, one of India's leading 
hospitality companies, today announced the signing of - Keys Lite by Lemon 
Tree Hotels, Manali . This addition marks the Keys Lite brand's entry into Manali, 
one of North India's most popular leisure destinations, further strengthening the 
group's presence in Himachal Pradesh.  
The property will be operated under a franchise agreement, further expanding the 
group's presence across the region. With this signing, the group's portfolio in 
Himachal Pradesh will comprise 11 hotels, including four operational and seven 
upcoming propert ies. 
Set amidst the Himalayan landscape, the upcoming hotel will feature 24 well -
appointed rooms, a restaurant, lounge, fitness centre and thoughtfully designed 
public spaces. The property will cater to leisure travellers, families and small 
groups, offering a contemporary and comfortable stay experience in a destination 
known for its natural beauty, adventure offerings and strong leisure appeal.  
Commenting on the signing, Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, 
Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd.,  said: “Manali is among India's most sought -after 
holiday  destinations, drawing travellers across seasons , particularly families, 
couples and road -trip enthusiasts. With its comfortable, contemporary and value -
led proposition, Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels , Manali  is well suited to the diverse 
needs of travellers visiting the destination. This signing further strengthens our 
presence across Himachal Pradesh and reflects our focus on expanding our 
portfolio in destinations with strong leisure demand. ” 
The hotel will offer convenient connectivity to key transportation hubs. Bhuntar 
Airport, Kullu -Manali, is approximately 56 km from the property, while Kiratpur 
Railway Station is approximately 200 km away, providing connectivity for 
travellers arriving by  air and rail.  
 
 
 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India's leading hospitality companies, 
catering to a wide range of customers – from value -conscious travellers to 
premium business and leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels 
& Resorts, Lemon T ree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys 
Select, and Keys Lite – the group offers experiences across upper upscale, 
upscale, upper midscale, midscale, leisure, wildlife, and spiritual segments.  
LTHL operates 130+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing 
pipeline of 140+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, 
Bengaluru, and Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, 
and Indore – and with an international presence in Bhutan and Nepal – Lemon 
Tree Hotels delivers exceptional comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, 
refreshing experience.  
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 270+ 
properties (operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for 
both business and leisure travellers.
