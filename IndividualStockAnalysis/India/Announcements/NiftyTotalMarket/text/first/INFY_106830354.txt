PRESS RELEASE 
Infosys Successfully Delivers Syngenta’s Global SAP S/4HANA Transformation for 
Crop Protection Business 
New digital core supports more than 10,000 employees across 80 countries, simplifying operations 
and creating a scalable foundation for future innovation  
Bengaluru, India - October 27, 2026:  Infosys  (NSE, BSE, NYSE: INFY), a global leader in AI -first business 
consulting and technology services, today announced the successful implementation and go- live of Crop 
Protection Core (CP Core), Syngenta’s  global SAP S/4HANA transformation for its Crop Protection business. 
Delivered jointly by Infosys and Syngenta, the program establishes a unified digital core supporting more than 
10,000 employees across 80 countries, helping simplify business operations, strengthen governance, and 
create a scalable foundation for future innovation that enables Syngenta to better serve farmers worldwide.  
 
Built on SAP S/4HANA, CP Core brought together multiple ERP environments into a single global platform, 
simplifying Syngenta’s technology landscape and reducing core customizations in line with SAP best practices. 
The platform provides a unified digital co re across research, manufacturing, supply chain, regulatory, finance, 
and commercial functions, improving collaboration, strengthening governance, and providing greater visibility across Syngenta’s global Crop Protection business. The simplified technology  landscape creates a stable 
foundation for future innovations while improving operational efficiency at scale.  
 
Powered by Infosys Topaz , an AI -first set of services, solutions and platforms using generative and agentic AI 
technologies, and Infosys Cobalt  cloud offerings, the transformation helped modernize business processes, 
improve data quality, and established a trusted enterprise data foundation. AI capabilities were introduced to 
automate routine activities and enable greater standardization while kee ping people at the center of critical 
business decisions.  
 
Feroz Sheikh, Chief Information and Digital officer, Syngenta, said, “CP Core gives our people a simpler, 
more reliable way to work, helping us better serve farmers across all our markets. By bringing our operations onto one modern digital foundation, we are strengthening collaboration, improving agility, and enabling  our 
teams to focus on advancing innovation and delivering the science and solutions that growers rely on. This transformation has simplified our technology landscape and established a strong platform for future innovation. 
Working with Infosys has been in strumental in making this transformation possible and positioning our global 
business for continued growth.”  
 
Jasmeet Singh, EVP and Global Head of Manufacturing, Infosys , said, “Large- scale ERP transformations 
require precision, deep industry expertise, and flawless execution and the successful implementation of CP 
Core reflects all three. Together with Syngenta, we have simplified the enterprise landscape, established a 
modern digital core, and created a scalable platform for continuous innovation. This milestone builds on 
Infosys' long -standing collaboration with Syngenta, combining our deep understanding of their business with 
capabilities of Infosys Cobalt, Infosys Topaz to deliver a resilient foundation that will support Syngenta’s business transformation for years to come.”  
 
 
 
About Syngenta  
Syngenta is a leading science- based agricultural technology (AgTech) company, creating Breakthroughs for 
farmers, in every field™ to help meet the demands of modern agriculture.  Using cutting- edge innovation, and 
guided by its Sustainability Goal  of higher yields with lower impact, Syngenta helps farmers to grow resilient, 
healthy crops that can feed a growing global population, while protecting and enhancing the planet. Syngenta employs more than 30,000 employees in over 90 countries and is part of Syngenta Group. It consists of two 
business units: Syngenta Crop Protection, headquartered in Switzerland, and Syngenta Seeds , headquartered 
in the United States.
