Karur Vysya Bank Expands Presence with New Branch in 
Ahmedabad  
Ahmedabad , Novem ber 23, 2026: Karur Vysya Bank (KVB), one of India's leading private 
sector banks, today inaugurated its new branch in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. With this addition, 
the Bank's branch network has increased to 931 branches across the country, further 
strengthening its presence in key markets.  
The new branch at Ahmedabad , was inaugurated by Shri. R. P . Patel , Patron a nd 
President , Vishv Umiya Foundation , Ahmedabad . The branch is located at Shop No 8, 
Atlantis One, Ground Floor , Near Sagar Sangeet Cross Road , Kargil Petrol Pump Road, 
Science City, Sola , Ahmedabad - 380 060 , Gujarat  
Shri Ramesh Babu, Managing Director & CEO, Karur Vysya Bank,  “The 
inauguration of our new branch in Ahmedabad reflects our continued commitment to 
expanding our presence in strategically important markets and bringing banking services 
closer to customers. Gujarat is an important market with co nsiderable potential across 
retail, MSME and business banking. Through our expanding branch network and strong 
digital banking ecosystem, we aim to provide convenient, accessible and customer -centric 
banking solutions while strengthening our relationships with individuals and businesses in the 
region.”  
The expansion comes on the back of continued growth in the Bank’s business. As of 
September 30, 2026, KVB’s total business stood at ₹2,39,712 crore, comprising deposits of 
₹1,28,123 crore and advances of ₹1,11,589 crore. The Bank reported a quarterly net profit 
of ₹788 crore in Q2 FY27, registering a year -on-year growth of 37.28%.  
Through the expansion of its branch network and continued investment in digital banking, 
Karur Vysya Bank remains focused on enhanc ing customer convenience, deepening its 
regional presence and meeting the evolving banking needs of individuals and businesses 
across the country.
