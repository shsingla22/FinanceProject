PRESTIGE GROUP MAKES GRAND ENTRY INTO NCR RESIDENTIAL MARKET  
Launches ‘The Prestige City – Indirapuram’ with ₹9,000 Crore GDV  
Bengaluru, April 29, 2025 : Prestige Group proudly announces its official entry into the 
National Capital Region (NCR) residential market with the launch of the first phase of ‘The 
Prestige City – Indirapuram’: Phases Oakwood and Mulberry.  
With all approvals secured, Prestige Group has begun marketing the first phase - Oakwood 
and Mulberry - which together represent a Gross Development Value (GDV) of over ₹9,000 
crore — making it one of the most significant real estate debuts in NCR ’s history. When the 
second phase “Mayflower” is launched, it will take the total GDV of the entire residential 
development to a staggering ₹12,000 crores.  
Oakwood and Mulberry feature a combined 3,421 premium homes across 19 towers , offering 
a sophisticated mix of 2 BHK, 3 BHK, 3 BHK + Home Office, and 4 BHK + Home Office 
residences, with unit sizes ranging from 1,681 sq. ft. to 6,026 sq. ft.  
Set across a sprawling 62.5 acres  in Indirapuram Extension on National Highway 24 , the 
township is designed to deliver an unparalleled lifestyle, with world -class amenities, two 
grand clubhouses spread over 100,000 sq. ft., and an integrated Forum Mall covering 1.18 
million sq. ft.  of retail and entertainment experiences.  
Strategically located, The Prestige City – Indirapuram is poised to become NCR’s next iconic 
address, benefiting from excellent connectivity, robust social infrastructure, and the dynamic 
growth of the Indirapuram Extension corridor.  
Mr. Irfan Razack, Chairman and Managing Director, Prestige Group,  said:  
“Today marks a monumental chapter for Prestige Group. We are thrilled to make our debut 
in the vibrant NCR residential market with The Prestige City – Indirapuram. This project 
embodies the scale, ambition, and integrated lifestyle that Prestige stands for . 
It is only the beginning — we are deeply committed to expanding our presence in NCR, and 
The Prestige City – Indirapuram is the first of many more landmark developments we have 
planned for this dynamic market.  
We are confident it will set a new benchmark for the region and redefine what modern living 
means in NCR.”
