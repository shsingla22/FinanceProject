Dear Sir/Madam, 
Sub: - ICSI Awards 
This is to inform that the Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI) has awarded ICSI Life 
Time Achievement Award for Translating Excellence in Corporate Governance into Reality  to 
Mr. B.V.R. Mohan Reddy, Founder Chairman of Cyient Limited (the Company).  The award is in 
recognition of his lifetime commitment and lasting contribution to the advancement of 
Corporate Governance. 
The Company was also awarded  “CSR Excellence Award - Best Corporate under Small & Emerging 
Category” . 
A Jury headed by Hon’ble Mr. Justice P. Sathasivam, Former Chief Justice of India  decided to bestow 
the above awards.  
The awards were presented at a ceremony in Bangalore on 20 December 2024.
