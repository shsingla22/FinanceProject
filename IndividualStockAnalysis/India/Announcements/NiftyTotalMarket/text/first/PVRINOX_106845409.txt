PVR INOX BRINGS RAJASTHAN ’S FIRST IMAX® TO JAIPUR  THIS FESTIVE SEASON , 
OPENING WITH THE WIDELY -ANTICIPATED RAMAYANA 
~ The world’ s most immersive big -screen experience arrives at Pacific Mall, Mansarovar, with 4K 
laser projection, Dolby Surround Sound, and next -gen 3D technology ~ 
National, November 4th, 2026: PVR INOX Limited, India ’s largest and most premium film exhibition 
company, has announced the opening of its eighth cinema in Jaipur at Pacific Mall, VT Road, Mansarovar. 
The five- screen property features one IMAX® auditorium and four premium mainstream auditoriums. With 
this addition, PVR INOX further expands its IMAX® footprint in the c ountry, taking its network to 27 IMAX® 
screens, while its total screen count in Jaipur increases to 78.  
Arrivin g during the festive season, the new cinema, with a seating capacity of 949 seats,  adds to Jaipur's 
Diwali celebrations, offering audiences an opportunity to come together and experience the magic of cinema on the big screen. With the much- awaited Ramayana leading its opening line- up, the launch brings 
an added sense of occasion to the festive season.  
Designed to place audiences at the centre of the cinematic experience, IMAX® combines a giant, custom -
designed screen, precision- aligned sound, proprietary image remastering and theatre geometry to deliver 
exceptional scale, clarity, contrast and realism.  
Located at the heart of Mansarovar , one of Asia’ s largest townships , Pacific Mall is positioned as North 
India’s largest mall and Rajasthan’s most premium lux ury retail and entertainment destination. Featuring a 
curated mix of international fashion, lifestyle and luxury brands, the mall provides a fitting home for PVR 
INOX’s newest entertainment destination.  
Across its five auditoriums, the new cinema features 4K la ser projection, Dolby Surround S ound and next -
generation 3D technology. The experience is further elevated by Celebrity Recliners, a Cinema Food Court 
concept and intuitive Self -Ordering Food Kiosks  across the lobby, guests can conveniently browse, 
customize, and order their favourite food and beverages with ease. This seamless blend of technology and 
hospitality elevates convenience and creates a modern, premium moviegoing experience.  
Mr. Ajay Kumar  Bijli, Managing Director, PVR INOX Limited, said , “Jaipur has always had a deep 
connection with storytelling, culture and shared experiences, and cinema remains one of the most powerful 
ways to bring people together. The introduction of IMAX® in Rajasthan reflects our vision to make world -
class entertainment more accessible across India. IMAX® is not simply a larger screen, it is a complete 
reimagining of how audiences experience a film, with extraordinary scale, precision sound and exceptional 
image quality. As audiences continue to seek richer and more differentiated experiences, we remain 
committed to bringing globally recognised formats to India’ s most dynamic cities. ” 
Mr. Sanjeev Kumar, Executive Director, PVR INOX Limited, added:  “At PVR INOX, we  believe a great 
cinema is defined by the experience that surrounds the film. At Pacific Mall, Mansarovar, we have brought 
together advanced audiovisual technology, Celebrity Recliners and an integrated Cinema Food Court to create a destination that feels premium from the moment guests arrive. We look forward to welcoming 
audiences in Jaipur to experience films in exceptional comfort, on a truly grand scale, and in an environment built for shared memories. ” 
The launch reinforces PVR INOX ’s continued focus on expanding in key urban markets and introducing 
differentiated, future- ready formats in response to evolving audience preferences. By combining IMAX® ’s 
globally recognised cinematic experience with premium design and hospitality, PVR INOX continues to 
strengthen cinemas as destinations for entertainment, culture and collective storytelling

About PVR INOX Limited  
 
PVR INOX Limited is India ’s largest film ex hibition company, operating 1,802 screens across 35 9 properties 
in 114 cities in India and Sri Lanka. Formed through the merger, in 2023, of two of the country ’s most 
established and widely recognized cinema brands, PVR Limited and INOX Leisure Limited, the company 
represents the convergence of scale, operational excellence, and long- term creative vision. Operating as a 
unified platform, PVR INOX brings together deep industry expertise and a strong consumer -centric 
approach to deliver world -class cinema experiences.  
PVR INOX continues to invest in continuous innovation, next -generation formats, and immersive 
experiences, enabling the seamless introduction of global content and storytelling to audiences across the region. Guided by international benchmarks and evolving audience preferences, the company remain s 
committed to shaping the future of out -of-home entertainment in South Asia. Its portfolio spans thoughtfully 
designed child- friendly auditoriums, state- of-the-art projection and immersive sound technologies, a diverse
