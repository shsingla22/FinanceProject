Awfis Launches ~ 1.45 Lakh Sq. Ft. of Premium Workspaces 
Across Key Metro Cities  
 
National, October  28th, 2026 : Awfis Space Solutions Ltd., India’s largest flexible workspace 
solutions platform, today announced the launch of its new centres in  Pune, Bengaluru, and 
Mumbai . The company has added ~1,45,000  sq. ft.  of chargeable area with the latest design 
versions to cater to modern enterprises, GCCs , and large corporates.  All the new centres are 
built on Awfis’ latest design version and add to its growing portfolio of premium, Grade A+ 
inventory. These additions are over and above the ~ 1,00,000 sq. ft. of premium workspace of 
the recently launched Elite Centre in Aerocity, New Delhi, taking the cumulative chargeable area 
across these launches to ~ 2,45,000 sq. ft.  
 
A key part of this strategy is Awfis 6.0, which represents the evolution of the company’s design 
philosophy. Reflected in its Mumbai and Pune centres, the format combines contemporary 
aesthetics, intelligent space planning,  and operational efficiency to create future -ready work 
environments. Its design approach prioritises spatial optimisation, refined material palettes, 
enhanced lighting and acoustics, and thoughtfully planned layouts that elevate the overall 
workplace expe rience. Each element balances visual appeal, user comfort, durability,  and 
commercial viability.  Underpinned by a standardised  yet adaptable design language, Awfis 6.0 
enables consistent quality and efficient execution across centres while allowing flexibility to 
respond to different workplace requirements. More than a design upgrade, it translates design 
excellence into smarter,  efficient and experience -led workspaces, aligned with Awfis’ vision for 
the future of work.  
 
Commenting on the launch, Amit Ramani, Chairman & Managing Director, Awfis Space 
Solutions Ltd.,  said, "India’s commercial real estate landscape is undergoing a structural shift, 
with enterprises and GCCs increasingly prioritizing agility, high -quality infrastructure, and 
seamless mobility. Expanding our presence in prime micro -markets like Pune, Delhi -NCR, 
Bengaluru, and Mumbai allows us to stay ahead of this demand curve. These new additions 
reflect our core focus on deepening our footprint in Grade -A assets, driving high operational 
efficiency, and empowering businesses of all sizes with global -standa rd workspaces."  
 
The launch supports Awfis’ strategy of premiumisation and demand -led expansion. The 
company is deliberately shifting its portfolio mix towards high -end assets, such as its Awfis 6.0, 
Gold and Elite centres, and aims to raise the share of premium and Grade A+ centres significantly  
by the end of FY27.   Located at Punyaee Business Square (PBS)  in Pune, Prestige Tech Forest in 
Bengaluru, and Lodha i-Thin k in Mumbai, the new centres address strong demand for premium, 
thoughtfully designed workspaces. They provide  private offices, coworking desks, 
meeting /podcast  rooms, and event spaces, supported by high -speed connectivity, 24/7 access, 
and hospitality -led services. The latest design incorporates refined aesthetics, enhanced 
acoustics and lighting, wellness -focused layouts, upgraded collaboration zones, and techn ology -
enabled experiences.  
 
  
 
 
About Awfis Space Solutions Limited  
Awfis Space Solutions Ltd. (‘Awfis’) is India’s largest and first listed flexible workspace solutions provider 
of modern workspaces. The company enables small and large corporates to seamlessly book and utilise 
workspaces as per their requirement and conve nience. The comprehensive suite of solutions includes 
Flex Space Solutions (Coworking and Enterprise Solutions), Transform (Design & Build), and Allied 
Services, enhancing customer service. With a strong presence in 18 cities and 250+ centres, Awfis serves  
3600+ clients across diverse industries, ensuring scalable and adaptable workspace solutions for 
businesses of all sizes. The figures include operational and fit -out centres and seats. For more 
information, please log on to www.awfis.com . In addition, connect with us on Facebook, Instagram,
