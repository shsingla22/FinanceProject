Crompton Simplifies Household Water Management with  INTELLISENSE SmartPro  
The wireless water level controller integrates cont rol panel functionality, automates pump operation a nd enables remote monitoring 
through the Crompton Senze App  
 
Mumbai, 09 th  October 2026: From monitoring overhead tank levels to  ensuring the pump operates safely, conventional sy stems can 
require extensive wiring, multiple devices and regu lar intervention. Simplifying this process, Crompto n Greaves Consumer Electricals 
Limited has introduced Crompton INTELLISENSE SmartP ro, a wireless water level controller with integrat ed control panel 
functionality, designed to bring greater convenienc e, control and protection to everyday household wat er management. 
Traditional water level controllers used with subme rsible and openwell pumps typically require wiring between the overhead tank 
and the pump installed at ground level. This can in volve additional installation work, civil modificat ions and associated costs, 
particularly in multi-storey buildings. While conve ntional pump panels offer essential motor protectio n, water level management 
often requires a separate controller, resulting in two independent systems and additional wiring. For consumers, a single integrated 
solution can simplify installation, reduce costs an d enable hassle-free everyday water management.  
Key Features of the Crompton INTELLISENSE SmartPro  
 Wireless Communication using LoRa Technology: Enables wireless communication between the Main Wat er Level Controller 
(MWC) and Overhead Tank Controller (OHT), supportin g connectivity from the ground floor up to the seve nth floor without 
requiring inter-floor wiring, helping reduce instal lation costs and hassle. 
 Automatic Water Level Controller: Automatically starts the pump when the tank is empt y and switches off once the tank is full, 
helping prevent overflow. A four-level LED tank sta tus indicator on the main controller provides visib ility into water levels. 
 App-Based Control: Compatible with the Crompton Senze App, available o n iOS and Android, allowing consumers to remotely 
monitor water levels, control pump operation, set u p to 10 schedules and receive fault alerts on their s martphones. 
 Protection Features:  Helps protect the connected pump against voltage f luctuations, overload conditions and dry-run 
conditions 
 Weatherproof Design: Comes with an IP65 weatherproof rating, making it sui table for installations exposed to varying weather 
conditions. 
Designed for single-phase submersible pumps up to 2 HP, INTELLISENSE SmartPro is suitable for use with Oil-filled submersible 
pumps, Water-filled submersible pumps and openwell pumps.  
Commenting on the product, Anand Kumar N, Business Head – Pumps & Kitchen Appl iances, Crompton Greaves Consumer 
Electricals Ltd , said, “ At Crompton, our focus has always been on developin g solutions that make everyday household responsibi lities 
simpler and more convenient. Water management is an  essential part of daily life, yet conventional sys tems can involve complex 
installations and multiple devices to manage water levels and protect the pump. With INTELLISENSE Smar tPro, we have brought 
these functionalities together in a single integrat ed solution, giving consumers greater control over their water management needs 
while reducing installation-related complexities. T his reflects our continued commitment to developing  smart, connected solutions 
that combine comfort and convenience, offering cons umers greater peace of mind, while delivering relia bility and practical 
innovation .” 
With INTELLISENSE SmartPro, Crompton continues to e xpand its portfolio of smart water management solut ions, offering consumers
