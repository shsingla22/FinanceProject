Sub: - Clarification on News Reports regarding Burglary Incident at Franchisee Showroom at 
Dhuliyan, West Bengal  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we hereby inform  that a burglary incident occurred at our franchisee showroom located at 
Dhuliyan, Murshidabad, West Bengal on 14th May, 2026.  
The concerned franchisee has lodged the necessary FIR with the local police authorities and timely 
intimation has also been sent to  the Insurance Company for taking necessary actions . Most importantly,  
all employees at the store are safe and unharmed.  
In this regard, we would like to clarify that the said showroom is being operated under the Franchisee 
Owned and Franchisee Operated (FOFO) model. The inventory at the showroom is owned and recorded 
in the books of the franchisee and, accordingly, there is  no loss of inventory or financial impact on the 
Company  on account of the said incident.  However, the Company remains fully committed to providing 
comprehensive support to the franchisee during this time.  
 
 
This is for your information and records.
