Sub: Voluntary Intimation –  Fire Incident at Panihati Distribution Sub- station  
 The Company would like to inform that a fire incident occurred at its Panihati Distribution Sub -station in the 
afternoon of September 13, 2026.  
 
The incident was promptly attended to by the Company’s emergency response and operations teams. There were 
no casualties or injuries. Demonstrating the Company’s strong commitment to continuity and quality of power 
supply, approximately 80% of the affected  supply was restored within two hours and 100% within approximately 
three and a half hours.  The Company has insurance coverage on the plant and machinery and the insurers have 
been informed about the incident.   
 
The Company continues to accord the highest priority to safety, reliability and quality of service and has taken 
necessary measures to ascertain the cause of the incident and strengthen preventive measures, as appropriate.  
 
This intimation is being made voluntarily in the interest of transparency.
