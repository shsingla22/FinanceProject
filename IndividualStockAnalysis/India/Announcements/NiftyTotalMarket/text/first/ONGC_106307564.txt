Madam/ Sir, 
 
The Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting held today i.e. 22.07.2025, has inter-alia considered and 
approved the following proposals: 
 
1. Appointment of Shri Om Prakash Sinha as Key Managerial Personnel 
 
The Board of Directors has approved the appointment of Shri Om Prakash Sinha (DIN: 09696074) , Director 
(Exploration) as a Key Managerial Personnel of the Company with immediate effect. 
 
2. Appointment of Secretarial Auditors for the period FY’26 to FY’30 
 
The Board of Directors has approved the appointment of M/s Agarwal S. & Associates, Practicing Company 
Secretaries, as the Secretarial Auditor including issuance of Annual Secretarial Compliance Report for the five 
consecutive financial years from FY’26 to FY’30, subject to the approval of shareholders in the ensuing Annual 
General Meeting. 
 
The Brief Profile of M/s Agarwal S. & Associates, Practicing Company Secretaries is enclosed at “Annexure-A”

A. Business Items relating to South Sudan Project: 
 
Background: 
  
OVL has an overseas subsidiary in the name of ONGC Nile Ganga BV (ONGBV), which holds 25% PI in 
Blocks 1, 2 and 4 of South Sudan. The Project manages its operating activies through a Joint Operating 
Company called Greater Pioneer Operating Company Ltd. ( GPOC), ONGBV holds shares equivalent to its PI 
holding. 
 
Proposed Material RPT:  
 
Cash Call payment for operating Block 1, 2 and 4 of South Sudan by ONGBV to GPOC of amount(s) upto 
USD 130 million ( ₹1,170 crore) and USD 140 million ( ₹1,260 crore) [at an Exchange Rate of ₹90/USD] for 
FY’26 and FY’27 respectively.
