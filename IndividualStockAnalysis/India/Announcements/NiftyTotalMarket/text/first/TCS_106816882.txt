Confidential  MyState Limited  selects TCS BaNCSTM to transform core banking 
operations  
 
TCS will deploy its TCS BaNCS ™ Global Banking Platform to support the consolidated brand  following the 
merger of MyState Bank with Auswide Bank  
The implementation will help streamline operations , support further acquisitions , and enhance customer 
experience  
 
HOBART , AUS TRALIA  | MUMBAI , IND IA, October 14,  2026: Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, 
NSE: TCS), a global leader in IT services, consulting, and business solutions, has been selected by MyState 
Limited, one of Australia’s leading financial institutions, to deploy the TCS BaNCS ™ Global Banking Platform . 
The platform will support MyState Bank’s  core banking transformation and consolidation  of its operations  
following its merger with Auswide Bank.  
The partnership  will help  MyState Limited  consolidate multiple banking systems, business processes, data 
repositories, and customer servicing capabilities  into a single operating model . The implementation of  TCS 
BaNCS Global Banking Platform  will support post -merger integration and deliver seamless  digital banking 
customer experience.  
 
Brett Morgan, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director, MyState Limited , said, “The decision to 
implement a modern, AI -enabled core banking platform is an important step in building a simpler, stronger 
and more scalable bank for our customers and our people. This platform will help us consolidate systems, 
reduce complexity,  and create the foundation for faster innovation, better digital experiences,  and continued 
growth. It also supports our ambition to use technology and data more effectively so we can deliver more 
personalized , responsive and reliable banking services for customers across Australia. ” 
 
Venkateshwaran Srinivasan, Global Head , Financial Solutions , TCS, said, “We are pleased  to partner with 
MyState Limited  on this transformative journey  to build  a unified,  future -ready  banking platform. TCS BaNCS  
brings together  capabilities across  core banking, payments, customer engagement, relationship 
management, and digital operations . This modernisation  will help MyState Bank improve  customer 
experience , strengthen  operational efficiency, realize merger synergies , and accelerate growth . The 
transformation will help build  agility  and scalability  needed  to support AI -powered operations and meet the 
evolving needs of  its customers .” 
 
The transformation will leverage TCS BaNCS  Global Banking Platform’s  model -bank architecture, 
configurable product framework, workflow automation capabilities, API -led integration approach, and AI -
based transformation tools . These capabilities  will help  simplify the bank’s technology landscape, improve 
operational efficiency, and accelerate product and service innovation . The scalable platform  will also 
support  the bank’s  future growth and potential acquisitions.
