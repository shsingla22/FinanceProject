Lemon Tree Hotels signs two new propert ies, one each in Uttar Pradesh 
and Madhya Pradesh  
 
New Delhi , 23rd September  2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels announces the signing of 
two new properties – Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotels, Varanasi  and Keys 
Select by Lemon Tree Hotels, Rewa .  
 
Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotels, Varanasi  will be franchised under Lemon Tree 
Hotels Limited  and will feature 153 well-appointed rooms . Additionally , it will feature  
a restaurant , banquets, a conference room , a swimming pool , a fitness center and a 
spa along with other public areas . The Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport , 
Varanasi  is approximately 31 kms away from the property, while the Varanasi  Railway 
Junction  is about 12 kms away. The hotel is well connected by roadways for both 
public and private transport.   
 
Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels, Rewa  will be managed by Carnation Hotels 
Private Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  It will 
feature 50 well-appointed rooms , two restaurant s, a banquet hall , a swimming pool, 
along with other public areas . The Rewa Airport  is approximately 19 kms away from 
the property, while the Rewa  Railway Station is about 13 kms away. The hotel is well 
connected by roadways for both public and private transport.   
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels , commented , "We are delighted to announce the expansion of our 
portfolio in the Hindi heartland of Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh . While 
expanding its footprint across the country in cities of all tiers, this signing by Lemon 
Tree Hotels perfectly aligns with the company’s vision of becoming the most preferred 
hospitality chain across different segments."
