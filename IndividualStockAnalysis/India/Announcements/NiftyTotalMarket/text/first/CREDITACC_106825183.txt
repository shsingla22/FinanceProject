CreditAccess Grameen Limited  – Second Quarter  FY26-27 Results  
 
Highest Quarterly PAT Of INR 502 Cr, Growing Over 5x In Past 5 Years  
PAT up 298.7% YoY, AUM up 19.5% YoY , ROA of 5.8% , ROE of 23.2%  
Raised FY27 Guidance  To ROA: 5.2-5.8%  and ROE: 21-24% 
 
Bengaluru, 22nd October 2026: CreditAccess Grameen Limited  (NSE: CREDITACC,  BSE: 541770 ,  
‘CA Grameen ’), India’s leading rural-focused inclusive financ ing platform , today announced its unaudited and 
limited reviewed financial performance for the second quarter and first half of the financial year 2026 -27. 
 
Business Highlights : Q2 FY27 
• AUM grew  19.5% YoY from 25,903 crore to  INR 30,946 crore  
• Disbursements increased by 19.4% YoY from INR 5,322 crore to INR 6,355 crore  
• Retail Finance portfolio share at 23.3% in Sep-26 Vs 20.6% in Jun-26   
• Healthy new borrower addition of 2.3 lakh with 35% being New -to-Credit (NTC)  & 4.9 lakh in H1 FY27  
• PAR 0+ stood at  2.1% in Q2 FY27 with PAR 90+ at 1.1%   
• X-Bucket Collection Efficiency stood at  99.71% in Sep-26 
• Branch network grew by 5.3% YoY from 2,209 in Sep-25 to 2,326 branches  in Sep-26 
• Employee base grew by 4.5% YoY from 21,701  to 22,677   
 
Financial Highlights: Q 2 FY27 
• Total income increased by 26.2% YoY  to INR 1,904.8 crore   
• Pre-provision operating profit (PPOP)  increased 39.6 % YoY  to INR 970.2 crore   
• Credit cost  declined by 43.2% YoY to INR 299 crore   
• Profit Before Tax (PBT) grew 296.6% YoY  from INR 169.2  crore to INR 670.9 crore  
• Profit After Tax (PAT) grew 298.7 % YoY from INR 125.8  crore to INR 501.6  crore  
• GNPA / NNPA measured predominantly at 60+ dpd  stood  1.79% / 0.59%, with PAR 90+ of 1.11% 
• Robust l iquidity  of INR 3,501.9  crore  of cash, cash equivalents , and investments , 10.2% of the total assets  
• Robust  capital position with a CRAR of 26.9%  
• Credit Rating : AA-/Stable by CRISI L, ICRA  & India Rating  
 
 
Commenting on the business performance, Mr. Ganesh Narayanan, Managing Director and Chief 
Executive Officer of CreditAccess  Grameen  Ltd, said, “We reported our highest quarterly PAT of INR 502 
crore in Q2 FY27, having grown f ive-fold over the past five years . FY27 marks a clear turnaround in profitability, 
recording INR 995 crore PAT in H1 FY27  as against INR 778 crore PAT in FY26. Our H1 FY27 ROA stood at 5.9% 
and ROE at 23.8%, reflecting strong business performance. Our ROA guidance has been revised upward from 4.0 –
4.8%  (earlier)  to 5.2 –5.8%  (revised)  and ROE guidance from 16 .0–20.0% (earlier) to 21 .0–24.0% (revised)  while 
retain ing our AUM growth at 20 .0–25.0% and credit cost at 3 .0–4.0%. We have reduced our  loan pricing by 50 
bps applicable from October month  in line with our board approved  pricing  policy .

About CreditAccess Grameen Limited  
CreditAccess Grameen Limited is India’s  leading rural-focused inclusive financing platform  headquartered in 
Bengaluru . The Company provides a curated lifecycle credit suite to low -middle income households spanning 
group loans, individual business loans, secured business loans, afford able housing loans , and two -wheeler 
financing. The Company  operat es across  460 districts in 16 states (Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Goa, 
Gujarat, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, 
Telangana, Uttar Pradesh & West Bengal) and one union territory (Puducherry) throug h 2,326 branches. The 
Company’s Promoter is CreditAccess India  B.V., a multinational company focused on inclusive financing, 
supported by leading global institutional investors .
