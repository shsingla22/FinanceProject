NALCO Q1 FY27 results:  
Net Profit soars 88% to Rs.2002.38 crore 
 
 Sets New Q1 Records in Bauxite Excavation, Calcined 
Alumina Production and Alumina/Hydrate Sales 
 
Bhubaneswar, 31.07.2026:  National Aluminium Company Limited (NALCO), the 
Navratna CPSE under Ministry of Mines, Govt. of India and country’s leading producer 
and exporter of alumina and aluminium has made a strong beginning to the financial year 
2026–27, delivering robust operational and financial performance in the first quarter. 
Backed by operational excellence and prudent financial management, the Company's 
performance reflects its continued focus on efficiency, value creation, and sustainable 
growth.  
According to the financial results approved by the Board of Directors at its meeting 
held in New Delhi today, NALCO has reported an outstanding performance for the first 
quarter ended June 30, 2026. The Company registered 88% increase in net profit, which 
rose to Rs.2002.38 crore from Rs.1063.86 crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous 
financial year. Revenue from operations also recorded a robust growth of 39% over 
corresponding period last year, increasing to Rs.5302.38 crore during the quarter. 
 
The Board has also recommended final dividend of  Re.1/- per equity share (20% on 
face value of Rs.5/- each) amounting to Rs.183.66 crores for the FY 2025-26, for approval 
of shareholders in the ensuing 45th Annual General Meeting.  
 
Highlighting on the Company's strong first-quarter performance, Shri Brijendra 
Pratap Singh, Chairman-cum-Managing Director, NALCO, attributed the impressive 
results to the strategic guidance of the Board of Directors and the unwavering commitment 
of the Company's employees, business associates and stakeholders. "NALCO has 
commenced FY 2026–27 on a strong note, reflecting the Company's operational resilience 
and prudent business strategy. The robust performance during the first quarter was 
supported by a favourable global aluminium price, higher production and sales volumes 
particularly increased domestic alumina sales, and a conducive domestic business climate”, 
he said.

Mines, Government of India, and the continued support of the Government of Odisha, in
