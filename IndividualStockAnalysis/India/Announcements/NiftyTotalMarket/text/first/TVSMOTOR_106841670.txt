TVS MOTOR COMPANY STRENGTHENS ITS 
EUROPEAN PRESENCE WITH FOUR PRODUCTION-
READY MODELS AT EICMA 2026  
 
• TVS Motor expands its European presence through dedicated engineering facilities , 
a strengthened retail network and products developed for local customer needs  
 
• Four production-ready models broaden the company's offering across electric 
mobility, premium urban transportation and lightweight sports motorcycling 
 
• Global manufacturing scale, technology expertise and international engineering 
capabilities provide the foundation for TVS Motor's long-term success in Europe 
 
Singapore/Milan, November 03, 2026:  TVS Motor Company, the world's third-largest two-
wheeler manufacturer, today unveiled four production-ready models at EICMA 2026 in Milan, 
reinforcing its commitment to Europe as a strategic market. The showcase highlights products 
developed for  European customers, supported by dedicated engineering capabilities, an 
expanding retail network , and the strengths of TVS Motor's global manufacturing, technology 
and product development ecosystem.  
The four models, the TVS Orbiter, TVS M1-S, TVS X and TVS RTR 125, reflect TVS Motor's 
approach to serving Europe's evolving mobility needs across electric commuting, premium 
urban transportation and lightweight sports  motorcycling. Together, they demonstrate the 
company's ability to combine market-specific product development with global engineering 
expertise and manufacturing excellence.  
Europe plays an increasingly important role in TVS Motor's international business as the 
company continues to strengthen its presence in developed markets. Building on decades of 
manufacturing expertise, advanced technology capabilities and a growing inter national 
engineering footprint, TVS Motor is creating the foundations for sustainable long-term success 
in the region. This strategy also reflects the company's objective to increase the contribution 
of international business to its overall revenue, with the potential to reach 30% or more by 
2030.  
Peyman Kargar, President, International Business, TVS Motor Company, said: “Europe 
is an important market for TVS Motor and a key part of our international growth plans. We see 
significant opportunities across electric mobility, performance motorcycles and premium urban 
transportation. Success in these markets requires more than a competitive product. It requires 
a deep understanding of customer needs, strong local execution and a robust distribution and service network. Over the past year, we have focused on strengthening our distribution footprint, developing our dealer and servi ce capabilities and preparing a portfolio suited to 
European riding needs.

Europe: A strategic market for TVS Motor  
  
TVS Motor's approach to Europe is centred on three priorities: developing products aligned to 
local customer needs  and matched to European riding conditions , establishing strong 
distribution and aftersales capabilities, and leveraging its international engineering and design 
ecosystem to compete in technologically advanced markets.  
 The company has steadily expanded its retail presence, beginning with Italy and extending 
into Spain and Portugal, with further development planned in France and Germany. Its Italian 
network now comprises more than 100 retailers, supported by encouraging s ales momentum. 
Sales in Italy have increased by 110 per cent over the past year, while an additional 22 retailers 
are operational across Spain and Portugal.  
 
This expansion reflects TVS Motor's focus on building a sustainable business beyond initial 
product launches . The company continues to invest in market development, dealer capabilitie s 
and aftersales support, to ensure strong customer relationships and to establish trust and long-
term brand consideration across European markets.  
   
A market specific portfolio for Europe's evolving mobility needs 
  
The four models presented at EICMA 2026 are designed to address distinct customer 
segments. The electric range spans everyday city commuting, more versatile urban 
transportation and premium connected mobility, while the TVS RTR 125 extends the 
company's sports  motorcycle offering to younger and first-time riders.  
  
TVS Motor's electric mobility experience provides the foundation for this expansion. With more 
than one million electric scooters sold globally and investments exceeding $1 billion in electric 
vehicle and battery ecosystems, the company is applying its experience in EV engineering, 
software integration and manufacturing to products developed for international markets. 
  
TVS Orbiter: Making every day electric mobility more accessible 
  
TVS Orbiter is positioned as a practical electric mobility solution for European urban 
commuters seeking convenience, manageable running costs and the flexibility to meet every 
day travel requirements. 
  
Designed for city environments, TVS Orbiter combines compact urban mobility with functional 
features suited to regular commuting, short journeys and daily errands. Its upright riding 
position and 34-litre underseat storage support everyday usability, while the removable battery

The European-specification model offers two battery options – 1.8 kWh and 3.1 kWh –  with 
expected WMTC ranges of 70 km and 105 km, respectively. Riders can charge the battery on 
the scooter or remove it for charging using a household power socket and dedic ated dock.  
  
With a top speed of 45 km/h, TVS Orbiter is positioned in Europe's L1e category, targeting 
riders who prioritise accessible, straightforward and efficient urban transportation.  
  
TVS M1 -S: Bringing electric mobility to the maxi-scooter segment  
  
TVS M1 -S addresses a different customer requirement: riders seeking the comfort, practicality 
and road presence of a maxi -scooter, combined with the benefits of an electric powertrain. 
  
Positioned for commuters travelling across urban and suburban environments, the M1- S offers 
a more substantial riding experience than a conventional compact city scooter. Its design and engineering focus on comfort, performance and everyday versatility, making it relevant to 
customers considering an electric alternative to traditional mid-sized scooters.  
  
The M1-S is powered by a 4.2 kWh battery and delivers 12.5 kW of peak power, enabling a top speed of 105 km/h. The battery is positioned low in the floorboard to optimise the centre 
of gravity and support stable, confident handling.   
A 10 –80% charge takes 75 minutes, while a 26-litre storage compartment accommodates a 
full-face helmet. Integrated LTE connectivity provides connected functionality without relying 
on a paired smartphone. 
  
With this combination of practicality, performance and connectivity, the M1-S broadens TVS 
Motor's electric portfolio beyond short-distance commuting into a more versatile urban mobility 
category.  
  
TVS X: Premium electric performance and connected technology 
  
TVS X represents the premium, technology -led end of the company's European electric 
scooter portfolio. Designed for riders who value performance, advanced connectivity and 
distinctive engineering, the TVS X brings together electric powertrain technology, dynamic 
handling and a digitally integrated riding experience. 
  
Positioned within the European A1 category, the TVS X is aimed at customers seeking an 
electric alternative to premium urban motorcycles and scooters without compromising on 
performance or technology. 
  
Its European specification features 14-inch wheels at the front and rear, an aluminium frame 
and dual -channel ABS. The electric motor delivers 11.7 kW of peak power, enabling 
acceleration from 0– 40 km/h in 2.5 seconds and a top speed of 110 km/h.

TVS RTR 125: Introducing a new generation to sports motorcycling 
  
Complementing the three electric scooters, the RTR 125 strengthens TVS Motor's offering in 
Europe's lightweight performance motorcycle segment. 
  
Developed for A1 licence holders, the RTR 125 targets younger riders and customers entering the performance motorcycle category who seek an engaging riding experience alongside the 
practicality required for everyday use. 
  
The model draws on TVS Motor's performance engineering experience and the company's racing heritage, which dates back to 1982. It brings the principles of responsive performance, 
agility and rider control into an accessible 125 cc category.  
  
TVS RTR 125 also serves as an entry point to TVS Motor's wider  motorcycle portfolio in 
Europe, which includes the RR 310, RTR 310, Ronin and RTX 300. This range enables the 
company to address different stages of motorcycle ownership, from first-time performance riders to customers seeking more sophisticated sport, roadster and adventure motorcycles.  
  
The RTX 300, introduced at EICMA 2025, is also scheduled to reach European dealerships, 
further expanding TVS Motor's presence in the adventure touring segment. 
  
Global engineering capabilities supporting international commitment  
  
TVS Motor's presence in Europe is supported by a global engineering and manufacturing 
ecosystem, with India playing a central role in product development, advanced engineering, 
manufacturing and quality. 
  
The company's engineering ecosystem brings together capabilities across India, Singapore, the United Kingdom and Bologna, Italy. The establishment of its Global Centre of Excellence 
for Design and Engineering in Bologna, following the acquisition of Engines Engineering 
S.p.A., has further strengthened its ability to develop products for premium and technologically 
advanced markets.  
  
This international engineering network allows TVS Motor to combine product development at 
scale with specialised expertise in design, electric powertrains, connected technologies and 
performance engineering. It also enables the company to incorporate customer and market 
requirements earlier in the product development process.
