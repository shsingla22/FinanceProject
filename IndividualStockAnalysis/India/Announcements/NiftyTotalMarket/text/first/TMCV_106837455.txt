Mumbai, India/Turin, Italy/Amsterdam, the Netherlands – 30 October 2026. With reference to the offer document 
published on 4 September 2026 (the “ Offer Document ”), the amendment notice published on 9 October 2026 
and the notice published on 27 October 2026 (the “ Notice on Final Results ”) on the final results of the voluntary 
tender offer (the “ Offer”) promoted by TML CV Holdings Pte. Ltd., through its wholly-owned company TML CV 
Holdings B.V. (the “ Offeror”) for all issued common shares of Iveco Group (“ Common Shares ”) at a price of 
EUR 14.4 (cum dividend) per Common Share in cash, Tata Motors Limited (“ Tata Motors ”) (NSE: TMCV), a 
global automotive leader, and Iveco Group N.V. (“ Iveco Group ” or “Iveco”) (EXM: IVG), an international leader 
in commercial vehicles and mobility, jointly announce completion of settlement of the Common Shares tendered 
to the Offer during the Acceptance Period as per the Notice on Final Results. Terms not defined in this press 
release will have the meaning as set forth in the Offer Document.  
Following completion on the date hereof of settlement following the Acceptance Period, the Offeror holds No. 
238,157,212 Common Shares, equal to approximately 89.42% of Iveco Group's common share capital, net of 
Treasury Shares. As a result, Iveco Group has become part of Tata Motors.  
As announced in the Notice on Final Results, the Acceptance Period of the Offer will be reopened for five Trading 
Days starting from the Trading Day following the Payment Date, and, therefore, for the sessions on 2, 3, 4, 5 
and 6 November 2026 (included), from 8:30 a.m. (CET) to 5:30 p.m. (CET) (the " Reopening of the Terms "). 
Settlement of the Common Shares tendered to the Offer during the Reopening of the Terms will take place on 
13 November 2026. Further information regarding the post-offer steps to be taken in accordance with the Offer 
Document, including, as applicable, the Voluntary Purchase Procedure, the Dutch Legal Squeeze-Out or the 
Post-Offer Demerger and Liquidation, will be communicated by the Offeror in due course.  Annexure B

Iveco Group changes resulting from settlement of the Tender Offer following the 
Acceptance Period  
 
- New Board composition 
- New Board Committees compositions 
- Amended corporate governance documents in place 
- All Special Voting Shares repurchased 
- Q3 financials disclosure brought forward to 3 November 2026 
 
As of today, Iveco Group’s Board is composed of the following members:  
- N. Chandrasekaran, Non-Executive Director, with the titles “Chairperson” and “Senior Non-Executive 
Director”; 
- Olof Persson, Executive Director, with the title “Chief Executive Officer”; 
- P. B. Balaji, Non-Executive Director, with the title “Non-Independent Director A”; 
- Girish Wagh, Non-Executive Director, with the title “Non-Independent Director A”; 
- Guenter Butschek, Non-Executive Director, with the title “Independent Director B”; and 
- Charles Nichols, Non-Executive Director, with the title “Independent Director B”. 
 
In accordance with the amended corporate governance documents of Iveco Group, including Committee 
charters, available here (Regulations of the Board of Directors) and here (charters of the Board Committees), 
as of today the Board Committees are composed as follows:  
- Audit Committee: Mr. Nichols (acting as the Committee’s chair), Mr. Balaji and Mr. Butschek; 
- Compensation Committee: Mr. Butschek (acting as the Committee’s chair), Mr. Balaji and Mr. Nichols; 
- ESG Committee: Mr. Wagh (acting as the Committee’s chair) and Mr. Butschek. 
 
Pursuant to the amended terms and conditions of Iveco Group's Special Voting Shares available here and the 
authorisation granted by the Extraordinary General Meeting on 16 October 2026, as of today all Special Voting 
Shares outstanding immediately prior to settlement of the Offer have been repurchased for no consideration ( om 
niet), resulting in no Special Voting Shares remaining outstanding.  
 
Finally, the financial results of Iveco Group for the 3rd Quarter 2026 will be released on 3rd November 2026, 
instead of 5th November 2026, as previously communicated.
