Remuneration Committee (“NRC”) and given the knowledge, background and experience of Mr. Umang Nahata, approved 
the appointment of Mr. Nahata as a Whole-Time Director and as Chief Executive Officer of the Company with managerial 
remuneration, for a fresh tenure of 3 (three) years, commencing from January 16, 2025.

Mr. Umang Nahata was the founder and CEO of Evosys Group which under his leadership had grown to become one of 
the top Oracle Cloud partners globally. He was leading Mastek’s Oracle Business and also held the position of President of Mastek North America, APAC, and ME regions. He is a Chartered Accountant by qualification. Mr. Nahata has also worked for other well-known IT Services companies in the past.

with a declaration to the effect that he is not disqualified from being appointed as a Director in terms of Section 164(2) of the 
Companies Act, 2013 and has not been debarred or disqualified from being appointed or continuing as Director of a Company by 
the Securities and Exchange Board of India, Ministry of Corporate Affairs or any such other Statutory Authority.
Mr. Nahata has offered to undertake the role which was accepted by the Company’s NRC / Board.
