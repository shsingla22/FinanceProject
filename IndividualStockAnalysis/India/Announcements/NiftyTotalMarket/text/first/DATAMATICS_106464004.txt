Rahul Kanodia, Vice Chairman &  CEO, Datamatics, 
Conferred Honorary Doctorate by Amity University , Kolkata  
 
9th December 2025, Mumbai : Datamatics is proud to announce that Rahul Kanodia, 
Vice Chairman & CEO of Datamatics, has been conferred an Honorary Doctorate by 
Amity University,  Kolkata,  in recognition of his outstanding contributions to the global 
technology landscape, his visionar y leadership, and his role in advancing digital 
transformation on the world stage.  
Speaking on the occasion, Atul Chauhan , Chancellor, Amity University , Kolkata, 
said, “We are delighted to confer the Honorary Doctorate upon Mr. Rahul Kanodia, 
whose visionary leadership and relentless pursuit of innovation have made a significant  
impact  on the global digital and AI ecosystem. His contributions have not only shaped 
the tra jectory of Datamatics but have also had a deep and lasting impact on global 
technology enablement. He represents the excellence, integrity, and for ward -thinking 
spirit that Amity  University celebrates. ” 
 
While acknowledging the recognition, Rahul Kanodia, Vice Chairman & CEO, 
Datamatics , said, "My sincere appreciation to Amity University, Kolkata,  for 
conferring this honorary doctorate. Amity's  commitment to strong values and shaping 
future professionals i s truly inspiring. " He added,  "This recognition reflects our 
ongoing pursuit of excellence in technology and AI -driven innovation, and the 
dedicated efforts of the Datamatics team.  It further strengthens our resolve to deliver 
meaningful impact and help Enterprises  navigate their digital journeys with confidence 
and purpose. ” 
Rahul ’s leadership has been consistently acknowledged across the industry, including 
being awarded ‘CEO of the Year ’ at the Asian Leadership Summit & Awards in 2014 
and 2019, and most recently being recognized as ‘CEO of the Year ’ at The Business 
Leader of the Year Awards 2025.  Under his leadership, Datamatics has also earned 
several notable global recognitions, including being featured in Forbes Asia ’s 200 Best 
Under A Billion, being acknowledged by Dun & Bradstreet as a Top 500 Value Creator, 
and becoming one of the first digital technology companies worldwide to receive the 
ISO 42001:2023 Certification for AI Management Systems.  
The convocation ceremony, which celebrated nearly 2,000  graduating students, was 
also attended by Vice -Chancellor Dr. Ashok K. Srivastava, and Senior Vice -President Mr. 
 
U. Ramachandran and Mr. Abhay Chauhan, Sr Vice President and Trustee . It highlighted 
academic excellence, leadership, and the values shaping t he next generation of 
professionals in a rapidly evolving world.
