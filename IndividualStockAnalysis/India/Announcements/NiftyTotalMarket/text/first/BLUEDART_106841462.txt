PRESS NOTE  
 
 
Blue Dart Delivers More Smiles for Less this Diwali with Special Domestic and 
International Shipping Offers  
 
 
Mumbai, 3rd November  2026:  Blue Dart Express Limited, South Asia’s premier express 
air & integrated transportation and distribution company, has launched Diwali Express 
2026, a festive shipping initiative designed to help customers deliver more smiles for 
less this festive season.  
Running from 8th October to 14th November 2026, the campaign offers special savings 
on domestic and international shipments, making it easier, more affordable and 
convenient to share Diwali joy with family, friends and business associates across India 
and around the world.  
Festive gifts within India can be shipped using the  special discounted shipping rates of 
up to 50% on eligible domestic consignments weighing between 2 kg and 10 kg. To 
make the experience even more convenient, customers can also avail of free doorstep 
pickup and home booking services, subject to service availability.  
Extending the festive spirit beyond borders, customers can also enjoy significant savings 
of up to 60% on eligible international gift consignments. The offer covers a range of 
shipment weights and destinations, making it easier to send festive gifts, sweets and 
hampers across the globe through Blue Dart's reliable international express network. 
Terms and conditions apply.  
Available for a limited period, the Diwali Express 2026 offer can be availed  by the 
customers  through Blue Dart -DHL ’s trusted  network by visiting their nearest retail 
stores  and Franchisee Collection Centres . Customers can also book shipments through 
Blue Dart's website or Customer Care.  
For assistance, customers may contact Blue Dart Customer Care at 022 4061 1234 , or 
email customerservice@bluedart.com .  
For more information or to book a shipment, visit www.bluedart.com .
