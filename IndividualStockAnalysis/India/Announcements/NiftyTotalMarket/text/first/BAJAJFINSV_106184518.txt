Sub.:  Intimation  under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations , 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”) - Conversion of 
Warrants issued by Bajaj Finance Limited into Equity Shares  
 
We wish to inform you that  pursuant to the preferential issue , in terms of SEBI (Issue of Capital 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, by Bajaj Finance Limited in the FY2024, the 
Company was allotted 15,50,000 warrants, convertible into equivalent number of shares on                  
2 November 2023. The Company has paid 25% of the amount committed i.e., Rs. 297.21 crore 
on subscription to warrants i.e., on 2 November 2023.  
 
Now we wish to inform that as per the terms and condition s of the issue, the Company has 
opted for conversion of the said warrants by remitting the remaining 75% of the price 
consideration i.e., Rs. 891.64 crore.  
 
Accordingly, Bajaj Finance Limited has allotted 15,50,000 equity shares  of face value of Rs. 2/ - 
today i.e., 26 March 2025.
