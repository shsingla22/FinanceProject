Press Release  
 
NTPC Green Energy Limited inks MoU with Government of Uttar Pradesh  for 
development of RE  and Green Hydrogen  Projects  in Uttar Pradesh  
 
 
NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL), a subsidiary of NTPC Limited, signed a Memorandum 
of Understanding (MoU) with  Government of Uttar Pradesh on 22nd Jan’2026 during World 
Economic Forum Annual Meeting in Davos for development of Renewable Energy  and Green 
Hydrogen Projects in Uttar Pradesh . 
 
 
 
The MoU was signed between Sh. Vijay Kiran Anand , CEO, Invest UP & UPSIDA and Sh . 
DMR Panda, Executive Director  in presence Sh.  Suresh Kumar Khanna , Hon’ble  Minister  of 
Finance  and Parliamentary Affairs , Govt of Uttar Pradesh and  other  senior officials .
