Prestige Group Signs Definitive Agreement with BMRCL to Adopt 
Bellandur Metro Station 
Bengaluru | 21 February 2026 
 
Prestige Group today announced the signing of a landmark Definitive Agreement with 
Bangalore Metro Rail Corporation Limited (BMRCL) to adopt and co-brand Bellandur Metro 
Station as “Prestige Bellandur Metro Station.” 
 
Under the agreement, Prestige Beta Projects Private Limited will invest Rs. 115 Cr (excluding 
GST) towards station upgradation and enhancement, infrastructure support, and long-term 
partnership rights over a 30-year concession period. 
 
The partnership grants Prestige: 
 ∙ Exclusive station naming rights for 30 years 
 ∙ 3,000 sq. ft. of commercial space within the station 
 ∙ 1,000 sq. ft. of advertising entitlement 
 ∙ Provision for a future elevated connectivity bridge to Prestige Lakeshore Drive 
 
Prestige, on its part, will strive to make this the best metro station in the city and will undertake 
interior upgrades, finishing works, and station enhancements in alignment with global metro 
standards and IGBC green building benchmarks, reinforcing Bengaluru’s push toward 
integrated, sustainable urban infrastructure. 
 
Strategically located on the 17 km ORR Metro Corridor from Silk Board to KR Puram, 
Bellandur Station sits at the heart of India’s largest office micro-market, serving thousands of 
professionals daily. 
 
This partnership represents a progressive Public–Private Partnership (PPP) model, combining 
infrastructure development with private sector participation to deliver seamless multi-modal 
connectivity for Bengaluru’s citizens and the ORR workforce. 
 
“This is our way of giving back to the city — by strengthening mobility where India works and 
helping our future occupiers of Prestige Lakeshore Drive experience convenience, comfort, 
and seamless connectivity to one of the largest office parks coming up on the ORR.” — Juggy 
Marwaha, CEO & Executive Director, Prestige Group – Office  
 
With this collaboration, Prestige reinforces its commitment to building not just office 
campuses, but integrated urban ecosystems where work, transit, and community converge.
