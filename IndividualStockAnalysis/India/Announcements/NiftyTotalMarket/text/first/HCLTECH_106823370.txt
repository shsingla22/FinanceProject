HCLTech launches NVIDIA- powered HCLTech Verification and Validation for Auto to 
transform software -defined vehicle validation  
NOIDA, India, Oct. 21, 2026 —  HCLTech , a leading global technology company, today 
announced the launch of its AI -powered Verification and Validation (V&V) offering, powered 
by NVIDIA’s simulation stack, agentic capabilities and open model strategy. Designed for 
automotive vehicle manufacturer s and Tier 1 suppliers, the offering combines agentic AI, 
physical AI -powered simulation and intelligent orchestration to help automate elements of 
vehicle software validation workflows and accelerate releases.   
As modern vehicles become increasingly software -defined, technological complexity, over -
the-air updates and evolving regulatory frameworks are placing greater demands on the 
verification and validation processes. Automotive organizations face challenges of  
fragmented tool chains, manual workflows and labor -intensive compliance processes, 
creating a bottleneck between software development and release.  
As part of HCLTech's Industry AI solutions  portfolio, this V&V offering for Auto leverages a 
simulation -first operating model that connects requirements, test creation, execution, 
analysis and compliance evidence across the validation lifecycle. The solution enables 
customers to automate validation workflows, expand scenario coverage, identify and 
investigate test failures faster, and maintain a continuous digital record linking requirements, 
test results and compliance reporting.  
HCLTech’s V&V for Auto combines HCLTech’s automotive engineering expertise, agentic AI capabilities and Validation Factory model with the NVIDIA stack, including NVIDIA Omniverse , 
NVIDIA Cosmos , NVIDIA Alpamayo , NVIDIA Omniverse NuRec  and NVIDIA Isaac Sim , to 
generate and validate a wide range of scenarios in virtual environments, including rare and 
safety -critical conditions. This reduces reliance on physical testing, expands test coverage 
and improves visibility into release readiness. Leveraging the NVIDIA Agent Toolkit , this 
solution orchestrates multiple AI agents with reasoning powered by NVIDIA Nemotron  open 
models across the V&V lifecycle.   
“Automotive software is evolving faster than traditional validation processes were designed 
to handle. Frequent software updates, increasingly complex vehicle systems and the need to 
demonstrate compliance across changing configurations can make validation  slow, 
fragmented and difficult to scale. Powered by NVIDIA, HCLTech’s V&V for Auto is designed to 
help organizations shift from periodic, labor -intensive testing to continuous, intelligent
