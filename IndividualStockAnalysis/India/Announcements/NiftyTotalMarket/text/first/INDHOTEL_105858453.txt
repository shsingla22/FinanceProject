Climate 
StrategyRisk IHCL acknowledges that climate change presents both 
risks and opportunities that are essential to address 
for the sustainability of its operations. The recognition 
of climate-related risks—including regulatory shifts, 
resource scarcity, and physical impact to assets—
compels IHCL to integrate a robust Climate Strategy 
into its business model. This focus on climate 
resilience ensures compliance, operational continuity, 
and asset protection.IHCL’s climate strategy is underscored by the 
integration of renewable energy (RE), energy 
efficiency interventions and other resource 
conservation actions. The company is shifting 
towards renewable sources like solar and wind 
energy to power its operations, reducing reliance on 
fossil fuels and curbing emissions. Alongside, IHCL 
is upgrading its properties with energy-efficient 
technologies, encompassing state-of-the-art HVAC 
systems, lighting, and smart building solutions to 
minimise energy consumption. The IHCL Business 
Continuity and Disaster Response Plan has been 
developed to safeguard the smooth operation of 
critical business functions and ensure the well-being 
of guests and employees during disruptive incidents 
or disasters. These actions not only mitigate climate-
related risks by enhancing operational resilience 
and resource optimisation but also position IHCL 
as a leader in sustainable hospitality, committed to 
reducing its environmental footprint and catering 
to the sustainability expectations of guests and
