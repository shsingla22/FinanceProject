Sub: Intimation  of the date of the 26th Annual General Meeting of Central Depository Services

Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
A. Twenty -Sixth (26th) Annual General Meeting of CDSL:  
 
This is  to inform you that the Twenty -Sixth (26th) Annual General Meeting (“AGM”)  of the 
Shareholders  of the Company  will be held on Saturday , August 17, 2024 at 11:00 A.M. (IST)  
through Video Conferencing (“VC”)/Other Audio Visual Means (“OAVM”) in accordance with 
the applicable circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs and the Securities and 
Exchange Board of India from time to time.  
 
The Notice of AGM and Annual Report of the Company for the financial year 2023 -24 will be 
sent separately to the Stock Exchange and the Shareholders  of the Company in due course.  
 
B. Record  Date  for Dividend : 
 
The Board of Directors of the Company at its Meeting held on May 04, 2024, had recommended 
a final dividend for the financial year 2023 -24 of ₹ 19/- and a special dividend of ₹ 3/- per 
equity share  of the face value of ₹ 10/- bringing the total dividend payout to ₹ 22 /- per equity 
share, for approval by the Shareholders at the AGM.  
 
In terms of  Regulation 42 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations,  2015,  the Company has fixed Tuesday , July 16, 2024 , as the “Record Date ” for 
the purpose of determining the Shareholders  eligible to receive dividend for the financial year 
2023 -24. The Dividend, if approved at the AGM, will be paid within a period of 30 days from 
the conclusion of the AGM.  
 
The above  information will be made available on the Company’s website www.cdslindia.com
