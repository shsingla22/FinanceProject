VE Commercial Vehicles  announces exten sion of  full GST reduction benefit 
to customers  
 Addresses needs of Eicher truck and bus customers as we jointly drive efficiency in logistics 
as envisaged in the PM Gati Shakti program  
 GSO on Diesel, CNG and LNG range reduced from 28% to 18%    
 Electric range continues to enjoy beneficial GST rate of 5%  
New Delhi, September  10th, 2025 : VE Commercial Vehicles (VECV)  today announced that it will pass 
on the complete benefit of the revised GST rates to customers. Coinciding with the auspicious start  
of Navratri , Eicher customers purchasing new vehicles from September 22, 2025, can enjoy lower 
acquisition costs on Eiche r’s range of modern and fuel-efficient  trucks and buses . 
Mr. Vinod Aggarwal, MD & CEO, VECV,  said, “We sincerely thank the Hon’ble Prime Minister, 
Hon’ble Finance Minister and the GST Council for this landmark decision to reduce GST on trucks and 
buses fro m 28% to 18%. This reform  in line with the vision of Hon’ble Prime Minister  provides timely 
relief after a period of slowing sales and rising input costs in the CV sector. Beyond immediate 
customer benefits, the macro -economic impact is equally significant —lower logistics costs in line 
with the PM Gati Shakti program,  stronger consumer sentiment, and a predicted boost to GDP from 
heightened activity in logistics and allied industries. ” 
For truck operators, the GST  cut will help ease cost pressures and stimu late demand in freight and 
logistics, while for buses it will encourage fleet modernisation by state transport corporations and 
private operators, improving public mobility. This festive gift from the government will enable faster 
adoption of safer, fuel -efficient, and modern Eicher vehicles —strengthening India’s automobile 
industry  and supporting the India’s  vision  in its Amrit Kaal . 
VECV customers can now enjoy benefits up to Rs. 6 lakhs  (as per applicable GST rate reduction 0 
indicated below.  
Product Range  Price Reduction (Rs.)  
LMD Trucks  From 1.0 to 2.0 lakhs  
HD Trucks  From 1.5 to 6.0 lakhs  
Buses  From 1.1 to 3.4 lakhs  
Electric trucks and buses continue to enjoy the beneficial rate of 5% GST.  
By extending the full benefit of the GST reduction to customers, VECV reinforces its commitment to 
enabling greater efficiencies across the transport ecosystem. This initiative will help fleet operators, 
small businesses, and owner -drivers alike reduce their Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), improve 
profitabilit y, and invest in vehicles that are  future  ready . 
About VE Commercial Vehicles Limited (VECV):  
VE Commercial Vehicles Limited (VECV) is a joint venture between the Volvo Group and Eicher 
Motors Limited, offering a comprehensive range of Eicher -branded truck s and buses, and exclusive  
distribution of Volvo Trucks in India. Recognized for modernizing commercial transportation in India, 
VECV is at the forefront of providing innovative and sustainable transportation solutions.
