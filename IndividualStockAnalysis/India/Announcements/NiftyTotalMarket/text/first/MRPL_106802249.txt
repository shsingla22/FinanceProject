MRPL  Press Release : MRPL  CHT Unit Fire Incident
Mangaluru,  30" September  2026
An incident  involving  rupture  of the Cold Separator  operating  at high pressure  in the Coker
Hydrotreater  Unit occurred  at MRPL,  resulting  in a fire around  noon today.
The battery  limits of the affected  unit have been isolated,  and MRPL’s  emergency  response  and
firefighting  teams are actively  engaged  in controlling  and dousing  the fire. Fire has been brought
under control.
The firefighting  operation  may continue  for some time as part of the safe handling  of the incident.
No major injuries  have been reported.  One minor injury has been reported  and shifted  to hospital  and
is out of danger and necessary  medical  attention  is being provided.
The situation  is being closely  monitored,  with safety of personnel  and the facility  being accorded  the
highest  priority.
Further  verified  information  will be shared  as soon as it becomes  available.
There  is absolutely  no reason  to panic for individuals  within  and outside  MRPL  premises.
