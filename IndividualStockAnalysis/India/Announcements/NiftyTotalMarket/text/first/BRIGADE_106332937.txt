PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Group Launches Brigade Cherry Blossom, a Premium Plotted Development in Malur                      
(East Bengaluru) with a revenue potential of over ₹225 crores  
 
Bengaluru, August 8, 2025:  Brigade Group has announced the launch of  Brigade Cherry Blossom, a strategic 
joint venture plotted development in the fast -evolving micro -market of Malur (East Bengaluru). With a 
projected revenue potential exceeding ₹225 crores, the project spans 20 acres and 11 guntas, offering a total 
development area of 4.51 lakh sqft. This marks a significant addition to Brigade’s expanding footprint in East 
Bengaluru.  
 
Brigade Cherry Blossom features 338 thoughtfully planned plots, available in two versatile typologies – 30 feet 
x 40 feet and 30 feet x 50 feet – designed to appeal to discerning homebuyers and long -term investors seeking 
flexibility, connectivity, and str ong value appreciation.  
 
“With Brigade Cherry Blossom, we are strengthening our vision of shaping well -integrated neighbourhoods in 
emerging growth corridors. Malur is an evolving micro -market and upcoming residential hub, and this 
development reflects our commitment to delivering  high -quality and sustainable projects to our customers. 
Located just 1.5 km from the Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR) and the Bengaluru –Chennai Expressway (NE -
7), Malur offers excellent connectivity to Whitefield, Sarjapur, and Hoskote. Its inclusion in th e Chennai –
Bengaluru Industrial Corridor and proximity to key infrastructure upgrades make it a high -potential residential 
and investment destination. Brigade Cherry Blossom marks an important step in our strategic growth roadmap 
for Bengaluru and seamlessl y aligns with our broader strategy to build future -ready communities rooted in 
access, sustainability, and thoughtful planning.” – Pavitra Shankar, Managing Director, Brigade Enterprises 
Limited . 
 
Located close to upcoming infrastructure developments, Brigade Cherry Blossom offers excellent connectivity 
while providing residents a serene setting amidst nature, striking a perfect balance between accessibility and 
tranquillity.  
 
About Brigade Group:  Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers with 
close to four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and winning their 
customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark bu ildings and transformed skylines across India in 
the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Trivandrum, and GIFT City with developments 
across Residential, Office, Retail, Hospitality and Education Sectors.
