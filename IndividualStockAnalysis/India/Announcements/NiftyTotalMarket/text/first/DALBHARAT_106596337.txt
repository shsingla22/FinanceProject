Dear Sir/Madam, 
This is in continuation of our earlier disclosures mentioned above, regarding Provisional Attachment Order 
(“PAO”) dt. March 31, 2025 against Dalmia Cement (Bharat) Limited (“ DCBL”), a material wholly owned 
subsidiary of the Company, by the Enforcement Directorate (“ ED”), Hyderabad, provisionally attaching land 
parcels of DCBL. The PAO was confirmed by the Adjudicating Authority, PMLA on September 22, 2025.  
Further on appeal, the PMLA  Tribunal vide its final order dated March 9, 2026 had partially allowed the appeal 
reducing the Proceeds of Crime (“PoC”) to Rs.92.52 crs and also ruled that as far as application of the Company 
for substitution is concerned, the ED may decide the same. 
Without prejudice to its right and contention, DCBL made an application to ED along with a bank guarantee of 
Rs.92.52 crs. The ED vide its order dated April 21, 2026 has ordered release of entire attached land parcels.  
Accordingly, as on date the alleged POC has been reduced to from Rs.793.34 cr to Rs.92.52 crs. DCBL is in the 
process of filing an appeal against the impugned order w.r.t. the alleged POC of Rs. 92.52 Cr.  
The details as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with SEBI circular No.
