Puravankara
Expands

Portfolio

Bengaluru,
09

2025:
Puravankara
Limited
(NSE:
PURVA
|

532891),
one

a
3.63-acre

in

This

with
the
company’s
strategic
vision
to

its

in
key
Bengaluru
micro-markets
with
robust
infrastructure,

and
residential
demand.
The

acquired
land
parcel
in
Vajrahalli,
Kanakapura
Road,
spans
an
area
of
3.63
acres,
with
a
saleable
area
of
5.42
lakh
sq
ft
and
a
total
estimated
Gross
Development
Value
of
Rs
700+
crore.
Commenting
on
the
acquisition,
Ashish
Puravankara,
Managing
Director,
Puravankara
Limited
,
said:
"
We
are
excited
to

the
acquisition
of
this
land
parcel
at
a
prime
location
on
Kanakapura
Road,
Bengaluru.
This
strategically
located
land
parcel
is
in
a
micro-market
with
buoyant
demand
and
low
availability
of
land
for

Given

excellent

and
well-established
infrastructure,

residential
development.
Our
focus
will

to
be
on
business
development
to
replenish
our
land
bank
in
such
strategic
locations
with
favourable
demand-supply
conditions.
”
The

location
