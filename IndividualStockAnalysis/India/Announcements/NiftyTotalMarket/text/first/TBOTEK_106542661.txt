Ms. Aditi Madhok-Naarden joins TBO as Global Chief Human 
Resources Officer  
Seasoned global HR executive with over two decades of international experience joins TBO to lead its people 
strategy as the company accelerates its global expansion  
Gurgaon, India, February 23, 2026  — TBO Tek Limited (NSE: TBOTEK, BSE: 544174), one of the world's 
leading B2B travel technology platforms, informed that Tek Travels DMCC, UAE, a Material Subsidiary of the 
Company has appointed Ms. Aditi Madhok-Naarden as Global Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO), effective 
February 23, 2026. In this role, Ms. Aditi will lead TBO's global people strategy. 
 
Ms. Aditi joins TBO with over two decades of international HR leadership experience across Technology, 
Financial Services, Food & Beverages and FMCG sectors. She has held senior people leadership roles at globally 
recognised organisations including Lloyds Banking Group, Tate & Lyle, The Body Shop International, 
MasterCard, Deutsche Bank, Prudential, IBM and Infosys — leading large-scale transformation programmes, 
driving equity, diversity and inclusion agendas, and building world-class people organisations across Asia 
Pacific, Europe, the Middle East & Africa and the Americas. 
 
A trained coach & mentor and a British Chevening Scholar, Ms. Aditi holds an MSc in Human Resource 
Management from the London School of Economics and Political Science. She has been awarded for Change 
Management by ‘Human Resources’ magazine and also recognised as a finalist for 'Best Workplace Culture & 
Engagement’ at the HRM Awards. Ms. Aditi is a frequent speaker at major international HR forums. 
 
Commenting on the appointment, Mr. Ankush Nijhawan and Mr. Gaurav Bhatnagar, Co-Founders and 
Joint Managing Directors of TBO, said:  
“We are thrilled to welcome Aditi to TBO at such an exciting time in our journey. As we continue 
to scale globally, our people are our greatest asset. Aditi's breadth of international experience, 
her passion for building inclusive, high-performing cultures and her deep expertise in and 
commitment to talent development make her exactly the right leader for this role. We look 
forward to working with her to build an organisation that truly reflects our ambitions.”  
 
Ms. Aditi Madhok-Naarden commented:  
“I am truly excited to be joining TBO at a pivotal moment in its global journey. TBO's technology-
first approach, its remarkable reach across 100+ countries and its commitment to its people 
create an extraordinary platform for impact. I look forward to partnering with the leadership 
team to co-create a people organisation that is not only future-ready, but one where every 
individual feels seen, heard, valued and empowered to do their best work. When people thrive, 
organisations thrive — and that is the culture I am committed to building here.”  
 
About TBO Tek Limited  
TBO Tek Limited (NSE: TBOTEK, BSE: 544174) is the world's leading B2B travel technology platform, 
connecting over 159,000 travel buyers with millions of travel suppliers across more than 100 countries. 
Founded in 2006 and headquartered in Gurgaon, India, TBO offers a comprehensive suite of travel products — 
including air, hotels, rail, holiday packages, car rentals, transfers, sightseeing, cruise and cargo — powered by 
proprietary AI/ML technology. TBO employs approximately 2,700 people across six continents.
