NEW YORK, NY, November 17 , 2026:  Ichnos Glenmark Innovation, Inc. (IGI),  a global clinical -
stage biotechnology company focused on developing Multispecific™ therapeutics  in oncology, 
today announced the submi ssion  of an Investigational New Drug (IND) application to the U .S. 
Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for ISB 2301 . 
ISB 2301  is a first -in-class, investigational multispecific immune cell activator  designed to target 
three tumor -associated antigens and engage both T cells and NK cells . This pentaspecific  
molecule combines five binding specificities: three tumor -associated antigens, plus T -cell and 
NK-cell activation . By combining tumor -cell recognition with complementary immune activity , 
ISB 2301 is being developed to address biological heterogeneity and immune -suppres sive 
features of solid tumors . 
"Submission of the IND for ISB 2301 marks an important  milestone in advancing IGI’s multispecific 
oncology pipeline into clinical development ,” said Lida Pacaud, M.D., President and Chief 
Executive Officer , IGI. “This pentaspecific candidate  is designed to integrate multiple tumor -
targeting and immune -engaging functions within a single molecule.  Our preclinical results hav e 
demonstrated  a compelling anti -cancer profile for ISB 2301, a candidate we believe to be  highly 
differentiated from current approaches.  We plan to initiate a Phase 1 clinical trial of ISB 2301 in 
patients with solid tumors in 2027.”  
ISB 2301 was engineered using IGI’s proprietary BEAT® Multispecific Platform and is designed to 
recognize three tumor -associated antigens while engaging both T cells and NK cells through 
complementary mechanisms, including antibody -dependent cellular cytotoxicity ( ADCC ), 
checkpoint inhibition, and the induct ion of a sustained type 1 immune response.  
In preclinical studies, ISB 2301 demonstrated favorable  pharmacokinetic and tolerability profiles 
in non -human primates, supporting the planned evaluation of its safety, tolerability, 
pharmacokinetics, pharmacodyn amics, and anti -tumor activity in patients with advanced solid 
tumors.
