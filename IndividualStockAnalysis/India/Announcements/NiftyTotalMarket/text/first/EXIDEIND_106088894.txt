I, S. M. Gupta, Proprietor of M/s. S. M. Gupta & Co. , Practicing Company Secretaries having its 
office at P-15, Bentinck Street, Kolkata - 700 001 have been appointed as Scrutinizer by EXIDE 
INDUSTRIES LIMITED (“the Company”) for the purpose of scrutinizing the Postal Ballot by remote e-
voting process in a fair and transparent manner and ascertaining the requisite majority of voting 
carried out as per the provisions of Section 108 and 110 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with Rule 
20 and Rule 22 of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014 as amended on the 
resolution mentioned in Postal Ballot Notice dated 4th November 2024. The Company had provided 
to the Members facility of Postal Ballot by remote e-voting.  
We submit our report as under: 
1. The postal ballot by remote e-voting period started on Tuesday, 19th November 2024 at 9.00
a.m. and ended on Wednesday, 18th December 2024 at 5.00 p.m. and the Members were
required to cast their votes electronically conveying their assent/dissent in respect of the
Resolution on e-voting platform provided by NSDL. The remote e-voting module was disabled by
NSDL for voting thereafter.
2. The shareholders holding shares as on the “cut off” date i.e. 5th November 2024 were entitled to
vote on the proposed Resolution as set out in the notice of Postal Ballot of the Company.
3. The Company had completed the dispatch of Notice of Postal Ballot dated 4th November 2024
electronically on 18th November 2024 to all the members whose names appeared in the Register
of Members/Beneficial owners as on Tuesday, 5th November 2024, being the cut-off date.
4. The Company had published notice in the newspapers on 19th November 2024 in Business
Standard (English) and Aajkal (Bengali) for dispatch of Postal Ballot Notice electronically to
members.
5. The votes cast by remote e-voting on the NSDL Platform were finally unblocked on 18th
December  2024 at around 5.15 p.m. in the presence of two witnesses, Mrs. Enakshi
Bhattacharya and Miss. Komal Pugalia who are not in the employment of the Company. They
have signed below in confirmation of the votes being unblocked in their presence.
