Mastek named a ‘Leader’ in the Everest Group’s Digital Transformation Services 
for Mid -market Enterprises PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2024  
Mumbai, India, 02nd December , 2024 : Mastek (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted digital engineering 
and cloud transformation partner, today announced that it has been named a ‘Leader’ by the global 
analyst firm Everest Group in its Digital Transformation Services for Mid -market Enterprises PEAK 
Matrix® Assessment 2024. The report analyzed  the 21 leading  digital transformation service providers, 
assessing their market impact, vision, and capabilities.  
The PEAK Matrix® framework assessed Mastek’s ability to address the dynamic needs of mid -market 
enterprises in their digital transformation journeys . The report highlights  Mastek’s experti se in next- gen 
technology areas , including cloud modernization , data, analytics, automation, and AI . 
Mastek combines tailored client assessment s with  integrated service offerings to drive operational 
agility, reduce time -to-market, and ensure platform stability and quality. Its proprietary solutions and 
strategic partnerships  with technology leaders  such as Oracle, Salesforce, Microsoft, Snowflake, AWS, 
and ServiceNow further enhance its ability to deliver comprehensive front, mid, and back -office 
transformation services.  
Key Assessment of Mastek's Strengths:  
• Industry Expertise : Deep domain expertise in healthcare, retail, and financial services, serving 
clients across North America, Europe, and the APAC region.  
• Technology Leadership: Expertise in digital commerce, cloud transformation, and application 
development.  
• Oracle Capability : A highly skilled talent pool for platforms like Oracle , making it a trusted 
partner for Oracle -based transformations.  
• Client -Centric Approach: Proactive client management and easy access to leadership teams are 
valued by clients.  
• Comprehensive Services: Comprehensive digital transformation service offerings, focusing on 
operational agility and client experience.  
"The mid -market segment presents significant opportunities, with enterprises seeking trusted partners to 
drive agility, efficiency, and cost optimization in their digital transformation journeys. We're delighted that this leading position reaffirms Mastek’ s dedication to delivering innovative, scalable solutions that 
empower enterprises to achieve business excellence." said Umang Nahata , Global CEO at Mastek Group.  
“Mid -market enterprises are increasingly embracing digital transformation to achieve operational 
resilience and enhance customer experiences."  says Parul Trivedi, Practice Director at Everest Group.  
"Mastek emerges as a strong player in this space, by delivering measurable outcomes, such as significant cost reductions and improved system reliability, through its expertise in transforming legacy systems and leveraging advanced technologies like cloud- native architectures and AI -driven automation. Its ability  to 
offer tailored, domain -specific solutions, supported by a well -integrated partner ecosystem and a strong 
focus on innovation, enables mid- market enterprises to address complex challenges while maintaining 
 
agility. These qualities underscore Mastek's recognition as a Leader in the Everest Group Digital 
Transformation Services for Mid- Market Enterprises PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2024."
