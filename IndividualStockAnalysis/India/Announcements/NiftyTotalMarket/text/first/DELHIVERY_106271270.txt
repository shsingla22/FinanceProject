Ref.: Delhivery to acquire Ecom Express Limited 
 
 
Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
This is further to our communication dated April 5, 2025 informing about the approval of the board 
of directors of Delhivery Limited (the “ Company ”) on acquisition of shares equivalent to at least 
99.4% of the issued and paid up share capital, on a fully diluted basis, of Ecom Express Limited, for a 
purchase consideration not exceeding INR 1,407 Crore, subject to approval of the Competition 
Commission of India (“ CCI”).  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30(7) of the SEBI Listing Regulations, we would like to inform you that the CCI 
has vide its letter dated June 17, 2025 has accorded its approval to the said proposed acquisition. 
Copy of the letter is attached. The detailed order of the CCI is awaited. 
 
 
Request you to kindly take the same on record.
