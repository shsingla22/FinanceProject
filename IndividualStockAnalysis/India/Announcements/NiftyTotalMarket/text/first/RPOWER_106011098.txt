Thimphu, Bhutan (October 2, 2024)  —Reliance Group today entered into a strategic 
partnership agreement with Druk Holding and Investments Ltd.  (DHI) , the commercial and 
investment arm of the Royal Government of Bhutan, to undertake various initiatives aimed at 
bolstering the investment in the renewable and green energy landscape of Bhutan.  
 
The partnership between Reliance Group and Druk Holding will focus on green energy 
generation, specifically solar and hydropower initiatives, while also exploring innovative green  
technologies. Reliance Group will collaborate with government -owned companies in Bhutan to 
implement infrastructure projects that support Bhutan's net -zero goals . 
 
The partnership agreement was signed today by Mr. Harmanjit Singh Nagi, President 
(Corporate  Development), Reliance Power Ltd. and Mr. Ujjwal Deep Dahal, CEO, Druk 
Holding and Investments, in the presence of Mr. Anil Ambani .
