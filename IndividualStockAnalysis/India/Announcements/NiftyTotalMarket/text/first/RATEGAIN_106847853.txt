 Q2 FY27 Revenue at INR 745 Cr, up 153% YoY; Adjusted EBITDA Up 215% with  
Margins at 22.7% 
 
India, 5 November 2026 : RateGain Travel Technologies Limited (NSE: RATEGAIN, BSE: 
543417), a global leader in AI-powered SaaS solutions for the travel and hospitality industry, 
today announced its financial results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026, 
reporting steady performance with sustained revenue growth, healthy margins and strong free 
cash flow generation, despite an evolving macroeconomic and geopolitical backdrop.   
 
The company reported operating revenue of INR 745.4 Cr, up 152.6% year-on-year (YoY), with 
Adjusted EBITDA growing ahead of revenue at 215.4%, on the back of healthy progress on 
integration of Sojern and operating leverage kicking in. Adjusted EBITDA margin stood at 22.7% 
in Q2 FY27 compared to 18.2% in same quarter last year. The Adjusted PAT stood at INR 90.8 Cr, 
up 78% YoY. The YoY growth reflected the scale of the combined RateGain and Sojern platform, 
led by continued growth in MarTech and DaaS verticals. Margins remained healthy, reflecting 
operating discipline across the business. 
 
RateGain’s travel intent data and AI-led delivery set it apart within MarTech. Hotels are placing 
greater focus on growing their share of direct bookings, and as one of the few scaled AI-led 
platforms in this space, RateGain reaches travellers while they are planning a trip and turns that 
intent into bookings on the hotel’s own channels. This helped its Properties business, which 
accounts for 44.7% of MarTech revenue, grow more than 20% YoY in the first half. Destination 
Marketing Organizations (DMOs) rely on the same intent data to reach prospective visitors, and 
on RateGain’s measurement tools to see the return on their marketing. That has made RateGain 
a preferred provider for several DMO customers. Its Destinations business, which accounts for 
39.7% of MarTech revenue, also grew more than 20% YoY in the first half.  
 
In APMEA, Q2 new sales were more than three times the year-earlier level as RateGain continues 
to strengthen its presence in the region. For the second half of FY27, RateGain is focused on 
strengthening Sojern’s product, go-to-market and AI capabilities and on cross-sell across its base 
of more than 14,000 customers.

Bhanu Chopra, Founder & Managing Director, RateGain Travel Technologies Limited, said:  
“Our customers are operating in a market where demand keeps shifting, and they are relying on our 
data and AI to stay ahead of it. We remain positive about the future and focused on long-term 
growth. That means adding customers, deepening how much of our platform each of them uses, and 
extending our AI into more of their everyday work. In the second half, our priority is to bring the full 
RateGain and Sojern platform to more of these customers and to execute well”. 
 
“Our Q2 performance reflects the progress we are making in building a stronger, more profitable 
business. Adjusted EBITDA grew ahead of revenue, margins remained healthy, and our earnings 
continued to translate into strong cash flow. This performance gives us confidence to accelerate 
investments in product innovation, AI and market expansion, while maintaining a disciplined 
approach to capital allocation. We remain focused on translating the scale of our platform into 
sustainable, profitable growth and long-term value creation”, said Chetan Garg, Chief Financial 
Officer, RateGain Travel Technologies Limited. 
 
About RateGain Travel Technologies Limited 
RateGain Travel Technologies Limited is a global provider of AI-powered SaaS solutions for travel and 
hospitality that works with 14,000+ customers and 700+ partners in 160+ countries, helping them 
accelerate revenue generation through acquisition, retention, and wallet share expansion. 
RateGain today is one of the world's largest processors of electronic transactions, price points, and travel 
intent data, helping revenue management, distribution and marketing teams across hotels, airlines, meta-
search companies, package providers, car rentals, travel management companies, cruises and ferries drive 
better outcomes for their business. 
 
Founded in 2004 and headquartered in India, today RateGain works with 33 of the Top 40 Hotel Chains, 4 
of the Top 5 Airlines, 7 of the Top 10 Car Rentals, and all leading OTAs and metasearch websites, including 
25 Global Fortune 500 companies, in unlocking new revenue every day.
