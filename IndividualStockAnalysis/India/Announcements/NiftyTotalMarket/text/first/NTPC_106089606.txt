Sub: Declaration of COD of second part capacity of 30 MW out of 150 MW Gujarat Solar PV Project of NTPC 
Renewable Energy Limited (NTPC REL)  
 
In terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we 
wish to inform you that on the basis of certificate dated 18.12.2024 issued by Gujarat Energy Development 
Agency, second part capacity of 30 MW out of 150 MW Gujarat Solar PV Project in Mesanka, Gujarat of NTPC

Green Energy Limited, is declared on Commercial Operation w.e.f. 00:00 Hrs of 11.12.2024.
