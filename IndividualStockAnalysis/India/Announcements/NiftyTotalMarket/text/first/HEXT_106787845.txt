Hexaware Receives  Five 2026 Brandon Hall 
Group ™ HCM Excellence Awards®  
The ﬁve wins span certiﬁcation, learning strategy, blended learning, talent development, and 
AI in recruitment  
September 22, 2026 : Hexaware Technologies [NSE: HEXT], an AI -ﬁrst digital and IT services 
company, has received ﬁve honors at the 2026 Brandon Hall Group ™ HCM Excellence Awards®, 
with four gold  awards and one bronze.  
The HCM Excellence Awards®  recognize HR and learning programs for their design, innovation, 
adoption, and business results. Hexaware was recognized across ﬁve categories:  
• Best Certiﬁcation Program  (Gold) : SONIC, Hexaware’s enterprise certiﬁcation program, 
helps employees build skills aligned to their roles. It maps 1,000+ certiﬁcations to 800 
roles; employees completed 44,742 in 2025, including 9,393 advanced AI certiﬁcations.  
• Best Unique or Innovative Learning and Development Program  (Gold) : SONIC also gives 
employees diﬀerent ways to develop beyond certiﬁcation, through AI -guided learning, 
instructor -led programs, hackathons, and peer learning. In 2025, 81% of Hexaware’s 
technology workforce completed digital training.  
• Best Learning Strategy  (Gold) : Mavericks, Hexaware’s campus talent development 
strategy, helps graduates move from college to project work through structured and 
hands -on learning. It cut time -to-deploy 30%, certiﬁed 432 engineers and reached 
10,000+ students in over 30 colleges.  
• Best Use of Blended Learning  (Gold) : Segue, Hexaware’s blended learning program for 
campus hires, combines self -paced and instructor -led learning with projects, peer 
learning, gamiﬁcation, and AI personalization. It grew from 200 to 3,200+ learners, 
raised oﬀer acceptance 30%, cut time -to-productivity 25%, and improved retention 
15%.  
• Best Use of AI for Talent Acquisition  (Bronze) : Hexaware uses AI across sourcing, 
screening, and interview coordination, with human oversight and bias controls built in. 
The approach cut time -to-hire 25 –40%, recruitment costs up to 35%, external portal 
reliance 30 –50% and delivered 2 –3x ﬁrst -year ROI.  
Satyendu Mohanty, E VP & Global Head – Talent Management, Hexaware , said, “The advantage 
comes from seeing talent development as a continuum, from how people enter the 
organization to how they build depth, move across roles, and stay relevant as the work 
changes.” 
 
 
Vinod Chandran, Chief Operating Oﬃcer, Hexaware,  added, “Workforce readiness has to move 
at the pace of technology and client demand. These programs give us a more systematic way 
to build capability ahead of need and deploy it faster.”  
“Every year, the HCM Excellence Awards®  remind us that innovation is ultimately about impact. 
The organizations recognized this year have demonstrated that great HR and learning strategies don't just improve programs, they strengthen organizations, empower people , and deliver 
measurable business results. We are honored to celebrate their achievements,” said 
Rachel 
Cooke, COO of Brandon Hall Group ™ and HCM Excellence Awards® Program Leader.
