Mastek Launches ADOPT.AI to Elevate Gen  AI Innovation and Scale 
Enterprise AI Adoption  
Mumbai, India, 19  June  2025  – Mastek (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted digital engineering and 
cloud transformation partner, announced the launch of  ADOPT.AI , a compr ehensive suite of AI solutions 
and services aimed at accelerating AI innovation and driving enterprise -wide AI adoption.  
ADOPT.AI services are designed to deliver faster, smarter, and more reliable  outcomes across three core  
dimensions: ADOPT. AI for Technology, ADOPT. AI for Business and ADOPT. AI for Data . 
With  80+ AI Agent assets and 100+ use cases tailored to boost productivity across  business applications , 
ADOPT.AI empowers Mastek’s clients to seamlessly navigate the full  AI adoption lifecycle from  incubation 
to enterprise- scale maturity.  
ADOPT.AI strengthens Mastek ’s AI capabilities  across key dimensions:  
• ADOPT.AI for Technology – Driving Efficiency Gains across Key V erticals: Mastek is advancing 
Software Delivery Excellence through a best- of-breed strategy that combines propr ietary tools 
with leading industry  solutions. By leveraging in -house innovations like AI Amigo, AI Enabler, and 
Mastek ITSM Agent , alongside industry tools such as GitHub Copilot, Mastek has achieved up to 
40% efficiency gains  - enhancing productivity, streamlining delivery, and im proving application 
quality.  
• ADOPT.AI for Business Application Transformation: Mastek has developed a holistic framework 
to embed AI across the enterprise application landscape, structured around three strategic pillars: 
(a) a platform -led approach leveraging core business applications, (b) agentic process automation 
to streamline workflows, and (c) tailored agentic AI solutions for domain -specific needs. With a 
growing library of solution blueprints and assets spanning legal, marketing,  customer service, HR , 
ERP, finance, and more, Mastek is simplifying and accelerating enterprise AI adoption.  
• ADOPT.AI Academy –  Building an AI -Ready Workforce : In 2024, the Mastek AI Academy achieved 
a key milestone by certifying the majority of its workforce, reinforcing the company’s  AI-led 
transformation journey . Over 3600 people  in our workforce are AI certified , covering a range of 
competencies  from foundational AI and AI -infused SDLC practices to advanced specializations in 
platforms such as Oracle, Salesforce, AWS, and Microsoft . 
• AI Engineering Excellence:  Mastek recently  launch ed its AI Engineering Center of Excellence (CoE ), 
a dedicated hub for AI consulting, integration services, and the development of  AI Agents , AI-
enabled applications  and industry -specific use cases . This  initiative is bolstered by strategic  
collaborations with platform leaders such as Microsoft, Oracle, and Salesforce, enabling seamless 
AI integration into core business applications.  
• Strategic Partnerships: To further scale  AI innovation, Mastek has forged strategic partnerships 
with NVIDIA and Open Ana,  leveraging their advanced AI capabilities. Backed by a strong talent 
pool across these ecosystems, Mastek is uniquely positioned to deliver scalable, secure, and high -
impact AI solutions that transform how enterprises operate and compete.  
 
 
Umang Nahata, CEO, Mastek commented,  “As AI becomes a defining force in business transformation, 
we’re proud to introduce ADOPT.AI, a significant step forward in making enterprise AI adoption 
purposeful and scalable. We understand the real challenges organizations face, from fragmented adopti on and lack of contextualization to workforce readiness. ADOPT.AI  is our response to these needs, 
embedding AI across business processes and enabling real -world impact, delivered ethically, responsibly, 
and at scale.”
