  Embassy Developments Unveils Embassy ONE Residences – an Exclusive 
Collection of 59 Ultra -Luxury Managed Residences   
• Receives Occupancy Certificate (OC) and all requisite approvals  
• With a GDV of ~₹1,400 crore, the launch takes the company’s cumulative GDV launched in 
FY27 to around ₹9,000 crore.  
• Introduces Fifth Key, Embassy’s signature luxury hospitality brand for select luxury 
residences  
Bengaluru – November  17, 2026:  
Embassy Developments Limited (NSE: EMBDL/BSE: 532832)  today announced the launch of 
Embassy ONE Residences , a collection of 59 ultra -luxury homes that marks a further evolution 
of the company’s residential offering, integrating luxury hospitality into the homeownership 
experience through dedicated services tailored to residents’ lifestyles.  
With a Gross Development Value (GDV) of ~₹1,400 crore , the development is  located at Mekhri 
Circle , at the intersection of Sadashivanagar, Jayamahal and Dollars Colony , among 
Bengaluru’s most established and prestigious residential neighbourhoods . 
Embassy ONE Residences is designed as an intimate enclave, with just two residences per 
floor and private lift lobbies for every home. Spanning approximately 4,500 sq. ft. to 15,000 sq. 
ft., the residences include three -, four - and five -bedroom apartments,  duplexes and 
penthouses . The project offers bare -shell residences, giving homeowners the flexibility to 
customise their layouts and interiors to suit their individual preferences.  Ceiling heights of 10 
to 14 feet enhance the sense of scale, while landscaped spaces draw from Bengaluru’s Garden 
City heritage.  
Embassy ONE Residences marks the introduction of Fifth Key , Embassy’s signature luxury 
hospitality offering developed specifically for its luxury residential portfolio. Building on 
Embassy’s experience in residential community management, Fifth Key adds a personalised 
hospitality layer to established property man agement and maintenance services, with the 
scope of services tailored to each development. Residents have access to a dedicated 
Residence Manager as a single point of contact, supported by a concierge team. Services 
span  arrival coordination, visitor management and housekeeping, as well as private chefs, 
wellness services, in -residence spa and salon treatments, personal training, gifting and other 
bespoke experiences.

 Commenting on the launch , Aditya Virwani, Managing Director, Embassy Developments 
Ltd.,  said, “Embassy ONE  Residences  brings together a convergence of luxuries that rarely 
coexist: a commanding, storied address, grand private residences and views designed to 
endure – Palace Grounds on one side, protected defence greens on the other. However, the 
meaning of luxury is evolving too . Through Fifth Key, hospitality becomes increasingly intuitive 
and personal, shaped by how  each household  lives. We are building for India’s new urban 
elite – people who have outgrown compromise and are looking for a lifestyle that has finally 
caught up with them. For them, luxury is turnkey, considered and complete. With the project 
having received OC and all requisite approvals, Embassy ONE  Residences  is the first 
expression of that ambition .” 
Saarang Ganapathi, CEO, Embassy Services,  added, “With greater exposure to global 
hospitality, Indian consumers are seeking a more personalised experience at home. Fifth Key 
bridges that gap through a curated luxury hospitality offering that serves not just needs, but 
tastes – creating a life without friction. It will be available  exclusively across  Embassy’s  select 
luxury  residential developments .” 
The 30 -storey Embassy ONE Residences forms part of Embassy ONE, Bengaluru’s integrated 
luxury destination bringing together premium residences, Grade -A office spaces and a 5 -star 
hotel within a single address.

About Embassy Developments Limited   
Embassy Developments Limited (formerly known as Equinox India Developments Limited) (EDL) is one of 
India’s largest listed real estate developers, specialising in the development of residential and 
commercial projects across key urban markets.  With a strategic focus on Bengaluru, the Mumbai 
Metropolitan Region (MMR), and the National Capital Region (NCR), and presence in Chennai and Indore.  
EDL has a diversified residential portfolio spanning mid -income to ultra -luxury segments, encompassing 
apartments, villas, branded residences, townships, town  homes and senior living communities across 
ready, ongoing and future developments.  EDL is listed on BSE Limited (BSE) and the National Stock 
Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and holds a long -term debt rating of IVR A (Stable) from Infomerics. EDL
