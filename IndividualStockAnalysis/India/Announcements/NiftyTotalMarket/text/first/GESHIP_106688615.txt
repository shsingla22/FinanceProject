PRESS RELEASE  
GE Shipping contracts to sell its 2009 built Long Range 2 Tanker  
“Jag Lokesh”  
  
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (GE Shipping) has contracted to sell its 2009 built 
Long Range 2 Tanker “Jag Lokesh” of about 105,900 dwt, to an unaffiliated third party. The vessel 
will be delivered to the new buyer in Q2 FY27.  
 Including Jag Lokesh, the company’s current owned fleet stands at 40 vessels, comprising 25 
Tankers (5 Crude Tankers, 16 Product Tankers, 4 LPG Carriers) and 15 Dry Bulk Carriers (2 
Capesize, 10 Kamsarmax, 1 Ultramax, 2 Supramax) aggregating 3.24 mn dwt. T he Company’s 
current capacity utilization is close to 100%.  
Additionally, the company has contracted to buy one secondhand Long Range 2 Product Tanker 
(2015 built) and this purchase transaction is expected to be completed in Q2 FY27.
