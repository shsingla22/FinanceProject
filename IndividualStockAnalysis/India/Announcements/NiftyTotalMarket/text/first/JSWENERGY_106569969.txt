Dear Madam / Sir, 
We refer to our earlier disclosure dated September 30, 2023 regarding writ petition filed by the 
Indian Wind Power Association, of which JSW Neo Energy Limited (subsidiary of the Company) 
is a member, challenging the order dated July 28, 2018 of the Andhra Pradesh Electricity 
Regulatory Commission (“ APERC”) permitting Andhra Pradesh DISCOMs to deduct Generation 
Based Incentive (“ GBI”) from energy payments, impacting JSW Neo Energy Limited’s 
subsidiaries.  
We wish to inform that in the concurrent proceedings, the Hon’ble Supreme Court, vide judgment 
dated March 25, 2026, has dismissed the appeal filed by the Andhra Pradesh DISCOMs against 
the order of the Appellate Tribunal for Electricity, which had set aside the aforesaid APERC order, 
and held that GBI is intended to be disbursed to the generating companies over and above the 
tariff.
