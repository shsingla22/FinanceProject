Noida, 5th September 2024: RateGain Travel Technologies Limited (RateGain), a global provider 
of SaaS solutions for travel and hospitality, announced today that TAAG Angola Airlines E.P., 
Africa’s premier airline with 70 years of operation, has chosen AirGain, an airline revenue 
management product by RateGain, to gain real- time competitive insights. This partnership 
enables TAAG Airlines to make informed pricing decisions for both domestic and international markets.  
 In a world where the commercial aviation indust ry is constantly evolving, TAAG Angola Airlines 
E.P. is committed to not only being a part of this evolution but leading it in the regions it operates. 
The airline, which has been a symbol of Angolan pride and a connector of dreams across 
continents, opera tes domestic services within Angola, medium- haul services across Africa, and 
long- haul services to countries like Brazil, Cuba, and Portugal.  
  
The partnership with RateGain is a strategic leap for TAAG Angola Airlines E.P., underlining its 
dedication to leveraging cutting -edge technology to enhance its market responsiveness. The 
decision to adopt AirGain was influenced by the product's superior r eal-time analytics and 
competitive intelligence capabilities. AirGain's advanced analytics provide an unparalleled depth 
of insight, allowing the airline to foresee market trends and adjust pricing strategies proactively. 
This partnership is particularly crucial as it equips TAAG with the agility to respond to the global 
aviation market's dynamic nature, especially post -pandemic.   
  Nowel Ngala, CCO of TAAG Angola Airlines E.P.,  expressed his enthusiasm about the 
partnership, stating, "Aligning with AirGain  is a strategic move towards fortifying our position in 
the global market. The comprehensive insights provided by AirGain will enable us to optimize our pricing strategy, ensuring that we continue to offer unparalleled value to our customers while 
maintain ing profitable operations. This partnership is a testament to our commitment to 
embracing modernity and continuously striving for excellence, in alignment with our core values."   
 AirGain’s data will facilitate TAAG’s revenue team in monitoring and responding to market 
changes by tracking dynamic price fluctuations. The platform’s capability to configure preset 
alerts based on relevant parameters ensures that TAAG will be among the first to be aware of 
significant market shifts, thereby maintaining a compet itive edge.  
 Vinay Varma, Senior Vice President and General Manager, AirGain , commented, "We are 
delighted to partner with TAAG Angola Airlines E.P. and increase our presence in the African region. With this collaboration, we aim to support TAAG in navigating through the complex 
pricing landscapes of the commercial aviation industry, ensuring they remain competitive and continue to make dreams take off for their diverse customer base."   
TAAG Angola Airlines E.P., revered for its rich history and commitment  to uniting horizons, 
selected AirGain for its intuitive interface and real -time price intelligence, which empowers 
airline revenue and pricing managers to make prompt and informed pricing decisions. Particularly enticing for TAAG was the innovative Market  Manager feature, which streamlines the 
process of market data tracking, enabling the efficient analysis of multiple Origin & Destination (O&D) pairs. For a deeper dive into AirGain's capabilities, visit w ww.airgain.ai
