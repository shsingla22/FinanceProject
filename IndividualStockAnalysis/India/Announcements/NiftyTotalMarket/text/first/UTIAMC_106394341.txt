Information Classification: UTI AMC - Internal  Proceedings of 8th Extra -Ordinary  General Meeting  
The 8th Extra -Ordinary  General Meeting ( 8th EGM) of UTI Asset Management Company 
Limited (the Company) was held on Tues day, the 30th September , 202 5 at 1400 hrs IST through 
Video Conferencing / Other Audio Visual Means (VC / OAVM) in accordance with the circulars 
issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (SEBI) read with the provisions of the Companies Ac t, 2013 and the rules made thereunder 
and the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015.  
Mr. Dinesh Kumar Mehrotra, Non –Executive Chairman  and Independent Director presided over 
the 8th EGM of the Company.  
The Company Secretary & Compliance Officer informed the members that the Notice of the 8th 
EGM was circulated to the members through permitted mode and  with the permission of 
Chairperson  and members, the same was taken as read.  
The relevant statutory records were made available electronically for inspection during the 8th 
EGM. As the 8th EGM was held through VC / OAVM, the option for appointment of proxies by 
the members was not applicable and hence, the proxy register was not made available for 
inspection.   
66 members participated in the 8th EGM. 
The Company Secretary and Compliance Officer  also informed that the Company had made 
adequate arrangements to enable members to participate and vote on the items proposed in the 
8th EGM. 
Members participated through video conferencing was counted for the quorum as per Section 
103 of the Companies Act, 2013 and the circular issued by MCA and SEBI. The Company had 
necessary quorum present for the meeting.
