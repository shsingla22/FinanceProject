    Revolt Motors Appoints Hardik Pandya As Brand Ambassador  
New Delhi, 5th Feb 2026 : Revolt Motors, India’s No. 1 electric motorcycle brand, today 
announced the appointment  of Indian cricketer Hardik Pandya as its brand ambassador. This 
collaboration comes at a pivotal moment as Revolt Motors has built a strong foundation for 
scalable growth —through a diverse portfolio of multiple models, nationwide  footprint, robust 
service infrastructure, and cutting -edge technology.  
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
Hardik Pandya as brand ambassador will amplify brand visibility, connect deeply with India's 
youth and performance -oriented audience, and accelerate the transition of electric motorcycles 
as a mass -market mobility product. This partnership will strengthen Revolt Motors connection 
with India’s young, progressive and performance -driven audience.  
 
Revolt Motors has been at the forefront of redefining electric mobility in India by combining 
smart technology, sustainable engineering and bold design. With this association, the brand 
welcomes Hardik Pandya —one of India’s most dynamic and influential spo rting icons —as the 
face of its vision for the future of mobility.  
Known for his fearless approach, strong individuality and ability to redefine the game on his own 
terms, Hardik Pandya mirrors Revolt Motors’ philosophy of breaking the status quo. Both the 
brand and the cricketer represent a new generation that believes i n pushing boundaries, 
embracing innovation and leading with purpose, making the partnership a natural fit.  
Commenting on the association, Ms. Anjali Rattan , Chairperson , RattanIndia Enterprises Private 
Ltd., said, “Hardik Pandya represents the spirit of modern India —bold, fearless and forward - 
looking. At Revolt Motors, we are building more than electric motorcycles; we are building a 
movement towards smarter and more responsible mobility. Hardik’s personality, jour ney and 
influence resonate deeply with our brand values, and we are excited to have him on board as we 
accelerate our growth and expand our presence across the country”  
Sharing his thoughts on the partnership, Hardik Pandya, said, “I’ve always believed in supporting 
ideas that challenge the norm. Revolt Motors is transforming how we look at mobility by making 
electric riding exciting, performance -led and relevant. Electric is the future, and Revolt is building 
it with confidence. I’ m excited to be part of this journey.”  
As part of the association, Hardik Pandya will feature in Revolt Motors’ upcoming integrated 
marketing campaigns across digital, social and on -ground platforms. He will also be associated 
with key brand initiatives aimed at strengthening awareness and acce lerating the adoption of 
electric motorcycles in India.
