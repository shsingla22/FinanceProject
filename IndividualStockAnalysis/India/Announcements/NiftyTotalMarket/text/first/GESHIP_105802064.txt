PRESS RELEASE  
G E Shipping contracts to sell its 2004 built Medium Range Product Tanker 
“Jag Pahel”  
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (G E Shipping) has contracted to sell its 2004 
Medium Range Product Tanker, Jag Pahel of about 46,319 dwt on April 01, 2024 to an unaffiliated 
third party. The vessel will be delivered to the new buyer by Q1 FY25. 
Including Jag Pahel, company’s current fleet stands at 42 vessels, comprising 28 tankers (6 crude 
carriers, 18 product tankers, 4 LPG carrier) and 14 dry bulk carriers (2 Capesize, 8 Kamsarmax, 4 
Supramax) aggregating 3.36 Mn dwt. 
Additionally, we had contracted to buy two MR product tankers in February 2024 and March 2024, 
which are due for delivery in Q1 FY25.
