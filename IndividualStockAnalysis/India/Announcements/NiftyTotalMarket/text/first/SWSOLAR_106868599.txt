Mumbai; November 23, 2026:  Sterling and Wilson Renewable Energy Limited (SWREL) (BSE Scrip Code:  
542760; NSE Symbol: SWSOLAR), a leading renewable EPC has announce d three marquee domestic order s 
– a 600 MWh BESS turnkey project in Gujarat  awarded by a leading PSU  and a solar project  from a Private 
IPP in Rajasthan. The company has also recently emerged L1 for a prestigious solar turnkey project in 
Madhya Pradesh . The total value of all the projects  is more than INR 2050 crore.  
 
Speaking on the new developments , Mr. Chandra Kishore Thakur, Global CEO, Sterling and Wilson 
Renewable Energy said, “We are honored and privileged to have received two significant orders and for 
being  selected as an L1 bidder for a prestigious project in India . These orders mark another noteworthy 
milestone in SWREL’s growing renewable project portfolio and reinforces our position as a competitive, 
trusted reliable EPC player in India’s rapidly expanding renewable sector. We look forward to supporting our 
customer’s long -term growth, exceeding their expectations, and delivering meaningful impact as we build 
on this momentum."  
 
About Sterling and Wilson Renewable Energy Limited  
Sterling and Wilson Renewable Energy Limited (SWREL) is a global pure -play, end -to-end renewable 
engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) solutions provider. The company provides EPC services 
for utility -scale solar, floating solar, hybrid & energy  storage and wind solutions and has a total portfolio of 
over 29.8 GWp (including projects commissioned and under various stages of construction). SWREL also 
manages an operation and maintenance (O&M) portfolio of 18.9 GWp  solar power projects, including for 
projects constructed by third parties. Present in 28 countries today, Sterling and Wilson Renewable Energy 
Limited has operations in India, South -east Asia, Middle East, Africa, Europe, Australia, and the Americas.
