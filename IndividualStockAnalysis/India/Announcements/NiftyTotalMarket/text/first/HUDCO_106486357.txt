Sub.:  Intimation of achievement of ‘Excellent’ rating in MoU Performance for the

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- --- 
 
Sir/Madam  
 
In line with the corporate disclosure requirement under Regulation 30 of the Securities 
and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations,  2015, this is to inform that Housing and Urban Development Corporation 
Limited has  achieved ‘Excellent’  rating by securing 97.90 marks out of 100  for the 
Memorandum of  Understanding (MoU) signed with the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs 
(MoHUA),  Government of India for the Financial Year 202 4-25. The rating is awarded by the 
Department  of Public Enterprises (DPE), Ministry of Finance for operational and financial 
performance  of the Company as per prescribed guidelines in this regard.  
 
This is for your kind information.
