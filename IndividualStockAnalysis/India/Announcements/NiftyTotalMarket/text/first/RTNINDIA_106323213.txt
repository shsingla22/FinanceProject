RattanIndia Enterprises Drone Company Neosky  Partners with the 
Karnataka Government to Deploy  Drone s for Enhanced  Security  
 
New Delhi, August  4, 202 5: RattanIndia Enterprises Ltd (REL)’s 100% subsidiary, NeoSky India 
Limited, has strengthened its presence in the security domain with significant drone 
deployments in Karnataka. NeoSky recently supplied drones to the Kalaburagi district 
administration . NeoSky will soon supply an additional 60 Tavas drones to the Karnataka 
Police. Tavas is a foldable drone featuring swappable payload and six -sided obstacle 
avoidance using LiDAR technology.  
NeoSky has been collaborating with the Indian armed forces, paramilitary forces, state police, 
and other agencies to support the nation’s evolving security needs. Recently, a range of 
surveillance drones was deployed to the Karnataka Police. This includes , Nimble -I which is  
equipped with day & night surveillance, swappable payloads, and AI -enabled capabilities  and 
NS01 , a micro drone weighing approximately 550 grams, equipped with a day camera.  
At an event held on July 24, 2025, at the Vidhana Soudha and presided over by the 
Honourable Home Minister of Karnataka, Shri Gangadharaiah Parameshwara, NeoSky 
officially handed over NS01 and Nimble -i drones to the Kalaburagi administration.   
Shri Gangadharaiah  Parameshwara , Honourable Home Minister of Karnataka , said, “Our 
vision is to equip every police station in the State with drones. These drones will enhance the 
agility and responsiveness of our forces. Their indigenous design reflects how local innovation 
can serve public interest ” 
NeoSky is a full -stack drone company with expertise in Done Training, Research & 
Development, Manufacturing and Services. NeoSky & its subsidiary , TAS, hold a DIPP license 
to manufacture drones for defence, are the first in India to receive DGCA approval, and among 
the pioneers to successfully conduct BVLOS (Beyond Visual Line of Sight) trials.  The company 
is also ISO -certified.  
NeoSky is also scaling up its drone skilling initiatives across multiple sectors. The company 
trains students from armed forces, paramilitary units, rural communities, agriculture 
universities, schools, colleges, and even international learners and content  creators.  
NeoSky holds a 10 -year DGCA license to train and certify drone pilots in India. Currently, it is 
training 500 graduates and postgraduates from agricultural universities under this 
programme.  
At the same event , Shri Priyank Kharge, Honourable  Minster for IT, BT, Rural development 
& Panchayat Raj , announced a major skilling initiative for the region. Under this, NeoSky will 
train 500 youth from Kalaburagi district, equipping them with DGCA -approved drone pilot 
licenses.  Commenting on the initiative, Shri Karge said,  “Our technological strength should 
become a catalyst for rural transformation and throw open opportunities. Drones are among 
the fastest -growing sectors globally. By training our youth in cutting -edge drone technology, 
we are preparing them for future jobs while fostering local innovation and tech -driven 
ecosystems .” 
Reflecting on this progress , Mrs. Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises 
limited , commented , “NeoSky’s partnership with the Government of Karnataka reflects our 
deep commitment to building a safer and more self -reliant India. Supplying 60 Tavas drones 
is a step forward in that mission. But what excites me even more is welcoming 500 youth from 
    
                                                                         
North Karnataka into our drone skilling programme —because real impact happens when 
technology uplifts lives. ”
