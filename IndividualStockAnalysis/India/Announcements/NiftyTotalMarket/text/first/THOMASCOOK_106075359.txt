Thomas Cook India Group wins top honours at the ET Travel & Tourism 
Annual Awards 2024 across categories  
Best Outbound Travel Operator  (Thomas Cook)  
Best MICE Travel Operator (Thomas Cook)  
Best Domestic Tour Operator  (SOTC) 
Best Inbound Tour Operator  – Jury recognition  (TCI - Sita)  
 
Mumbai, December 03, 2024 : Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India’s leading omnichannel travel services company, 
along with its Group C ompanies, SOTC Travel and Sita, celebrated a stellar achievement at t he ET Travel & 
Tourism Annual Awards 2024. The Group emerged as winners across multiple prestigious categories:  
• Thomas Cook  (India) Limited : Outbound Travel Operator of the Year and MICE Travel Operator of the Year  
• SOTC Travel: Domestic Tour Operator of the Year  
• Sita: Inbound Tour Operator of the Year  (Jury Recognition)  
The ET Travel & Tourism Awards recognize exc ellence in the travel industry, celebrating companies for their 
innovation and exceptional service. Winners are selected through a rigorous, multi -tiered jury process monitored by 
a credible external audit agency, ensuring transparency and credibility.  
By winn ing accolades for its outbound,  inbound , domestic  travel operations and MICE, the Group has demonstrated 
its unmatched ability to cater the evolving needs of traveller s in India and around the world. These awards celebrate 
the Group's excellence and highlight its achievements over the past year, driven by a strong omnichannel ‘phygital’ 
strategy that integrates digital and physical experiences, digital acceleration with innovations like the Thomas Cook 
Holidays App and AI -powered platform,  the expansion of its physical footprint a cross India to reach customers’ –
right at their doorstep, and a customer -centric approach focused on delivering bespoke travel solutions and 
seamless end- to-end experiences.  The Group’s rapid recovery and accelerated growth further underscore its 
leadership in the travel industry.  
Mr. Madhavan Menon, Executive Chairman, Thomas Cook (India) Limited said, "Winning across categories at 
the ET Travel & Tourism Annual Awards 2024 is a proud moment for the Thomas C ook India Group. These 
recognitions across our businesses reflect our continued leadership and commitment to excellence in meeting the 
diverse needs of our customers . This recognition is a shared celebration with our team and loyal customers who 
inspire us to excel every day . As we celebrate these achievements, we remain dedicated to pushing boundaries 
and setting new benchmarks in t he travel and tourism industry. ”
