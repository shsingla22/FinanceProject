Coforge Expands AgenticOps Capabilities to Power Enterprise 
Autonomy at Scale  
 
Princeton, NJ, USA | Greater Noida, India | September 22, 2026  – Coforge Limited 
(NSE: COFORGE), a global AI -native engineering services leader, today announced continued 
momentum for its AgenticOps capabilities, helping enterprises build the operational 
foundation required to scale AI -driven autonomy securely, resilie ntly, and cost -effectively. 
The announcement reflects growing client demand for AI infrastructure and operations 
models designed specifically for AI agents and autonomous systems.  
 
As organizations move from AI pilots to production deployments, agent fleet s expose 
resiliency, security and control gaps that conventional operating models were never designed 
to handle . They include  insufficient governance and controls, agent drift, a widening set of 
attack vectors, unreliable tool integration and orchestration, data security and sovereignty 
exposure, limited agent accuracy and observability, and escalating token consumption costs.  
 
AgenticOps within the Coforge Nuuron enablement suite  
 
Coforge's Age nticOps approach is purpose -built to close these gaps, giving enterprises the 
governance, security, and trusted AI Infrastructure  needed to run agentic AI safely at scale. 
It provides centralized controls and continuous monitoring to detect and correct agent drift, 
embeds zero -trust security to guard against emerging attack vectors, and flexibility to 
integrate with existing tools for reliable, scalable orchestration.  
 
With built -in data security and sovereignty controls, real -time observability, and intelligent 
token optimization, Agent icOps gives enterprises the confidence and control to move agentic 
AI from pilot to production.  Coforge is working with more than 160 clients to enable their 
AgenticOps journey. 37 clients are already experiencing 98.2% accuracy with AgenticOps 
across 392 secure environments spanning  AI workloads, cloud, and AI infrastructure.  
 
At the center of the offering is EvolveOps.AI, the AgenticOps layer within the Coforge Nuuron  
AI autonomy enablement suite. EvolveOps.AI brings governance , control , and a single 
enterprise -grade harness for operationalizing agents , including  continuous evaluations and 
automated drift detection, token consumption monitoring, and a governed registry of 
approved tools and model control protocols ( MCPs ). One harness replaces the fragmented 
tooling that leaves most agent programs stranded in pilot.   
 
"The intelligence layer and the data capabilities beneath it are now well understood. What 
decides how far an enterprise can actually take  autonomy is the strength of its AgenticOps 
foundation," said Ashish Kumar, Global Head, Cloud & AI Infrastructure, Coforge .
