Dear Sir/Madam,     
In terms of Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, it is to inform that in line with the business practice of 
fixing/revising prices of Manganese Ore and other produc ts, the Company has fixed/revised prices of 
different grades of Manganese Ore and other products, effective from 01. 04.2026 as under.    
1. The prices of all Ferro grades of manganese ore with Manganese content of Mn - 44% and above 
have been increased by 15% on the prices prevailing since 01.03.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 
31.03.2026/01.04.2026 for the month of April, 2026.  
 
2. The prices of all other Ferro grades of manganese ore with Manganese content below Mn -44% 
have been increased by 17.5% on the prices prevailing since 01.03.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 
31.03.2026/01.04.2026 for the month of April, 2026.  
 
3. The prices of SMGR (Mn -30%), SMGR (Mn 25%) and Fines grades have been increased by 
17.5% on the prices prevailing since 01.03.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 31.03.2026/01.04.2026 for 
the month of April, 2026.  
 
4. The prices of Chemical grades have been increased by 17.5% on the prices prevailing since 
01.03.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 31.03.2026/01.04.2026 for the month of April, 2026.  
 
5. The basic price Rs.1,80,000/ - PMT of EMD have been continued for the month of April,2026 
prevailing since 01.03.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 31.03.2026/01.04.2026 for the month of April, 
2026.
