Operational & Consolidated Financial Performance  
 
Q2 FY202 7 
 
During the quarter, the C ompany ’s India cargo volumes grew by 8% YoY while the total cargo 
volumes stood at  29.4  million tonnes . 
 
The increase in volume was driven by improved capacity utilization at Paradip Coal, Ennore Bulk 
and Mangalore Coal terminals and  was further contributed by the interim operations at the 
Tuticorin Bulk Terminal and Kolkata Container Terminal. The overall gr owth was partially offset 
by a challenging operating environment at Fujairah Liquid Terminal along with lower volumes 
at Jaigarh Port and Paradip Iron Ore Terminal.  
 
Operational revenue for the ports segment increased by 9% during the quarter to ₹1, 202 crore, 
compare d with ₹1,103  crore in Q2  FY2026 . The revenue increase  was driven by volume growth, 
increase in the ancillary services and rupee depreciation.  Operating EBITDA for the Port 
segment stood at ₹594 Crore an increase of 2% primarily driven by th e volume growth.  
 
Logistics business generated revenue from operations of ₹279 crore  compared to ₹163 crore in 
the same quarter previous year.  Operational EBITDA for the logistics  segment stood at 81 Crore 
up from 25 crore , an increase of 3.3x. EBITDA margin rose to 2 8.9% from 15 .2% earlier , driven 
by the growing contribution from rail logistics business.  The rail rake fleet increased from 42 
rakes at the beginning of the quarter to 78 rakes by quarter -end.  Further , 32 rakes are expected 
to be de livered over H2 which would take the total fleet to 110 by March 2027 . 
 
Increase in  port volumes and growth momentum  in the logistics segment  resulted in  
consolidated operating revenue increase  of 17% to ₹1,4 81 crore from ₹1,2 66 crore in Q 2 
FY2026. Operational EBITDA grew by 11% to ₹674 crore  compared to ₹ 610 crore in the 
corresponding quarter last year.  
 
Profit Before Tax (PBT) stood at ₹4 31 crore compared with ₹4 63 crore in Q 2 FY2026, the decline 
was primarily driven by unrealized foreign exchange losses and higher depreciation charges, 
reflecting the commissioning of growth projects in the ports business and the continued scale -
up of the logistics segment. The impact on PBT was partially offset by hig her other income 
during the period.  Consequently,  Profit After Tax (PAT) stood at ₹32 8 crore, compared with 
₹369 crore in the corresponding period last year.

About JSW Infrastructure Limited:   
JSW Infrastructure Limited, a key entity of the JSW Group, is India’s second -largest private 
commercial port operator  and fast -growing logistics platform , renowned for its 
environmentally sustainable seaports and terminals.  The Company  operates  thirteen  
strategically  located  port  concessions  along  India’s west  and east  coasts,  complemented  by an 
international  presence  with  a 465,000  cubic  meter liquid  tank  storage  terminal  and two O&M  
contracts  for port  terminals  in UAE.  The Company’s ports  and terminals  are equipped  to handle  
a diverse  range  of cargo  and accommodate  vessels up  to Cape  size,  with  highly  mechanized  
systems  ensuring  swift  turnaround  times  and optimal resource utilization. The strategic 
positioning of these facilities has made the Company a preferred choice for its growing 
customer base. Leveraging locational advantages and efficient asset utilization, the Company 
has significantly diversified i ts cargo mix. Looking ahead, JSW Infrastructure is on track to 
expand its total cargo -handling capacity from the current 18 6 Million Tonnes Per Annum 
(MTPA) to 400 MTPA by 2030. Further, the Company is rapidly scaling its logistics business 
following the a cquisition of Navkar Corporation in October 2024 and has now established a 
pan-India logistics network comprising 8 strategically located logistics assets and a rail fleet of 
78 rakes, providing integrated multimodal solutions to customers across key consu mption and 
industrial corridors.  Aligned with international standards, the Company is dedicated to 
enhancing its ESG performance across its operational ecosystem, reinforcing its commitment 
to sustainability.
