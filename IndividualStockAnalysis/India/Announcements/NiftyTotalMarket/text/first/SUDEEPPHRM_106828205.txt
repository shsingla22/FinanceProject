FDA Approval Enables Commencement of Operations at New Pharma Greenfield Facility  
at 179 Nandesari, Gujarat 
 
 Received FDA approval for the new pharma greenfield manufacturing facility at 179 Nandesari, Gujarat, 
authorising the commencement of manufacturing operations at the site. 
 The facility has an annual installed capacity of 51,200 MT, strengthening manufacturing capabilities 
across Pharma, Food & Nutrition and Specialty Ingredients. 
 Approval allows the Company to bring the new capacity on stream and serve growing demand from 
pharmaceutical, food and nutrition customers in domestic and international markets. 
 
Vadodara, October 26th, 2026: Sudeep Pharma Limited (NSE: SUDEEPPHRM, BSE: 544619), a leading 
manufacturer of pharmaceutical excipients, food and nutrition ingredients and specialty mineral-based 
solutions, today announced that its new pharma greenfield manufacturing facility at 179 Nandesari, Gujarat, has 
received approval from the Gujarat Food and Drug Control Administration (FDA), authorising the Company to 
commence manufacturing operations at the site. 
 
The approval marks a significant milestone in the Company's growth journey and confirms that the facility's 
manufacturing systems, quality controls and compliance framework meet the requirements for commencing 
manufacturing operations. It reinforces the Company's commitment to strengthening manufacturing 
capabilities, quality standards and regulatory compliance. With an installed annual capacity of 51,200 MT, the 
facility represents an important addition to the Company's manufacturing infrastructure and will support its 
growing business requirements across domestic and international markets. Commercial supplies from this 
facility are expected to commence in H2 FY27, following the completion of customer qualification processes. 
 
Approximately 60% of the facility's capacity is allocated to the Company's existing Pharma, Food & Nutrition 
portfolio, while the remaining capacity will cater to newer product categories, including phosphates, glycinates, 
gluconates and citrates.  
 
The facility will also provide dedicated capacity for higher-value ingredients, reinforcing the Company's focus 
on portfolio diversification and strengthening its presence in regulated markets. Importantly, the additional 
capacity will enable the Company to manufacture new-age, high-value molecules such as bisglycinates, for 
which capacity at the existing mother plant was constrained. This will allow the Company to scale up production, 
cater to growing customer requirements and capitalise on emerging opportunities in specialised ingredients. 
 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Sujit Bhayani, Managing Director, Sudeep Pharma Limited, said:  
"The FDA approval for our 179 Nandesari greenfield facility marks an important milestone in our growth journey, 
enabling us to commence manufacturing operations and unlock significant additional capacity. It strengthens 
our ability to serve a wider customer base across pharmaceutical, food and nutrition segments in India and 
international markets. With the approval in place, we can now accelerate customer qualification processes and 
audits at the facility, facilitating the onboarding of both new and existing customers. 
 

 
 
 The facility also provides us with the flexibility to expand our presence across existing and newer product 
categories, including high-value, specialised ingredients such as bisglycinates. This will enable us to address 
evolving customer requirements, scale up our specialised ingredients portfolio and pursue larger opportunities 
in international markets. We believe this investment will serve as an important growth driver as we progress into 
the next phase of our journey." 
 
About Sudeep Pharma Limited  
 
Sudeep Pharma Limited is a leading manufacturer of pharmaceutical excipients, food and nutrition ingredients 
and specialty mineral-based solutions, serving customers across India and international markets. With a strong 
foundation in mineral chemistry, manufacturing expertise and quality control, the Company offers a diversified 
portfolio of ingredients catering to pharmaceutical, food, nutrition and other specialised applications. The 
Company continues to expand its manufacturing capabilities, product portfolio and global presence, with a 
focus on innovation, value-added products and emerging growth opportunities, including battery materials 
through its subsidiary, Sudeep Advanced Materials.
