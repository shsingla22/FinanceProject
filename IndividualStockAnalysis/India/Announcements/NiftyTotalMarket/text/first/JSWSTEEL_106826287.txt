1) 5 MTPA Expansion and upgradation of existing plant facilities at Sambalpur by 
JSW JFE Steel Limited (“JJSL”), a 50:50 joint venture of the Company and JFE 
Steel Corporation, Japan (“JFE”) 
 
JSW JFE Steel Limited (“JJSL”), a 50:50 joint venture of the Company and JFE, has a                 
4.5 MTPA integrated steel plant at Sambalpur,  Odisha and two downstream facilities at 
Chandigarh & Kolkata. The integrated steel pl ant at Sambalpur is operating at near full 
capacity. 
 
Considering the increasing domestic steel de mand and JJSL’s long-term growth strategy, 
the Board of Directors of the Company at it s meeting held today, have approved a 5 
MTPA expansion project and upgradation of ex isting plant facilities at Sambalpur by 
JJSL. The equity requirement for the project is estimated at Rs. 7,500 crore, to be 
contributed equally by the Company and JFE, i.e. Rs. 3,750 crore each. 
 
The project implementation is expected to commence from April 2027, subject to receipt 
of requisite statutory and environmental approv als, and is targeted to be completed over 
approximately 3.5 years. 
 The project will strengthen JJSL's market po sition, improve economie s of scale, expand 
value-added product offerings, an d enhance export opportunities.
