Shilpa Pharma Lifesciences Limited, Received CEP from EDQM for API, Ursodeoxycholic Acid. 
 
This is to inform you that the Shilpa Medicare Limited’s 100% subsidiary, Shilpa Pharma Lifesciences Limited 
received certificate of suitability (CEP) from EDQM (European Directorate for the Quality of Medicines & 
Healthcare) for API, Ursodeoxycholic Acid. 
 
Ursodeoxycholic acid or ursodiol is a naturally occurring bile acid that is used dissolve cholesterol gall stones 
and to treat cholestatic forms of liver diseases including primary biliary cirrhosis. Ursodiol has been linked to 
rare instances of transient and mild serum aminotransferase elevations during therapy and to rare instances 
of jaundice and worsening of liver disease in patients with preexisting cirrhosis.
