This is in reference to the above -mentioned subject.  
 
On November 7, 2026,  Tata Steel Limited (‘Company’) completed the acquisition of 38,222  
equity shares of face value ₹10/- each in Creative Port Development Private Limited (‘CPDPL’), 
a subsidiary of the Company, due to conversion of 25,10,830 Optionally Convertible 
Redeemable Preference Shares (‘OCRPS’) held by the Company in CPDPL in the ratio of 1 
equity share of ₹10/- each for every 65.69 number of OCRPS of ₹100/- each , as per the terms 
of the issue.  
 
Post this conversion , the Company’s equity stake in CPDPL ha s increased by 6.5% from 51% 
to 57.5% and CPDPL continues to remain a subsidiary of the Company.
