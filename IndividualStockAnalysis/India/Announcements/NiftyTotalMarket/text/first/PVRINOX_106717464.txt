PVR INOX STRENGTHENS ITS PRESENCE IN INDIA'S GROWTH MARKETS WITH 
A NEW CINEMA DESTINATION IN JABALPUR; SPIDER -MAN: BRAND NEW DAY 
MARKS THE GRAND OPENING  
~ Premium 3 -screen multiplex blends timeless design, luxury seating and cutting -
edge cinema technology  ~ 
National, July 30, 2026 : Reinforcing its strategic focus on India's fast -growing Tier II markets, 
PVR INOX Limited, India's largest and most premium cinema exhibitor, today announced the 
launch of its new 3 -screen multiplex in Jabalpur. The opening mark s another milestone in the 
company's expansion across emerging cities, bringing a world -class cinema experience to one of 
Madhya Pradesh's fastest -growing urban centres. The cinema opens with Marvel Studios' Spider -
Man: Brand New Day , one of the year's big gest global cinematic events, giving audiences the 
opportunity to experience the film's scale, action and spectacle in a destination designed for 
premium moviegoing.  
With this launch, PVR INOX Limited now operates  10 cinemas with  55 screens across Madhya 
Pradesh and 42 cinemas with 204 screens across Central India, further strengthening its footprint 
across India's growth markets. Developed in partnership with Satya Prakash Group, the multiplex 
marks the company's second cinema in  Jabalpur and spans approximately 19,430 sq. ft., 
reflecting PVR INOX's continued investment in bringing premium entertainment infrastructure to 
high-potential emerging cities.  
Strategically located in the Madan Mahal area, one of Jabalpur's prominent comm ercial hubs, the 
multiplex forms part of Mall 11, a vibrant mixed -use destination featuring leading retail, dining and 
lifestyle brands. Surrounded by established residential neighbourhoods, it is set to become a 
preferred destination for entertainment and  shared experiences in the city.  
Inspired by Art Deco, the cinema combines classic elegance with contemporary design, featuring 
a sculptural ceiling, ambient lighting and refined finishes for a sophisticated guest experience.  
Featuring three premium audito riums with 659 luxury recliner seats, the cinema offers 2K 
projection, Dolby 7.1 surround sound and Next Generation 3D for a superior cinematic 
experience.  Guests can also enjoy an extensive food and beverage menu featuring freshly 
prepared gourmet snacks and signature cinema favourites, thoughtfully curated to complement 
every movie outing.  
Speaking on the launch , Mr. Ajay Bijli, Managing Director, PVR INOX Limited , said , "India's 
next phase of cinema growth will increasingly be driven by emerging cities, where rising 
aspirations, improving infrastructure and growing consumer demand are reshaping the 
entertainment landscape. Jabalpur exemplifies this opportunity. As one of Central India's fastest -
growing urban centres, it is ready for a world -class cinema d estination, and we are delighted to 
bring the PVR INOX experience to the city. Opening with Spider -Man: Brand New Day , one of the 
year's biggest global releases, makes this an especially exciting milestone. This launch reflects 
our long -term commitment to expanding across India's growth markets and bringing the finest 
Indian and international cinema closer to audiences, supported by cutting -edge technology, 
exceptional comfort and warm hospitality."  
 
 
Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli, Executive Director, PVR INOX Lim ited, added , "As audiences 
increasingly seek meaningful experiences outside the home, cinemas continue to play a unique 
role as places where people come together to connect, celebrate and create lasting memories. 
At PVR INOX, every new cinema is designed t o reflect the character of the city it serves while 
offering something truly distinctive. With its elegant Art Deco -inspired design, luxury recliner 
seating and thoughtfully curated hospitality, our Jabalpur cinema has been created as a 
destination that au diences will enjoy returning to time and again. We are delighted to welcome 
Jabalpur to the PVR INOX family and look forward to bringing communities together through the 
magic of cinema for generations to come."  
With this launch, PVR INOX continues to acce lerate its expansion across India's growth markets, 
strengthening its presence in Tier II cities and bringing premium cinema experiences to the 
country's next generation of moviegoers.
