New Delhi, October 1, 2026  
 
 
HERO MOTOCORP RECORDS 12% YoY GROWTH IN SEPTEMBER DISPATCHES, 
BUILDS POSITIVE MOMENTUM AHEAD OF THE FESTIVE SEASON  
 
DISPATCHES REACH  7.66 LAKH IN SEPTEMBER 2026  
 
Hero MotoCorp , the world's largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters , reported total 
dispatches of 7,66,348  units in September 2026 , compared to 6,87,220  units  during the same 
period last year. This represents a strong 12% YOY growth , driven by high consumer dem and and 
strong momentum as the C ompany prepares for the upcoming festive season. Domestic r etail growth 
momentum was  strong with VAHAN growth of 31 % over previous year, reflecting robust  demand 
conditions ahead of the peak festive period.  
*Data from VAHAN except Telangana, as on October 1, 2026.  
 
DOMESTIC ICE BUSINE SS DELI VERS STRONG GROWTH  
The Com pany’s Domestic ICE business delivered  strong dispatches of 7,10,436  units  in September 
2026 as compared to 6,32,253  units in the same period last year. This growth was led by the ICE 
scooter s - recording a 60% YoY dispatch growth . The Company also witnessed  strong performance 
in retail sales  with domestic ICE VAHAN growth of 29%, reflecting continued momentum from 
healthy consumer demand and strengthening market traction ahead of the festive seas on. 
To mark the on set of the festive season, the C ompany launched a vibrant festive campaign that brings 
together five distinctive Indian folk art forms. The campaign combines traditional art forms with a 
unique soundscape created using the Hero Destini, transforming the scooter’s sounds into a 
contemporary expression of India’s diverse cultural and festive traditions. The initiative highlights 
the brand’s approach of celebrating India’s rich cultural heritage through engaging and innovative 
storytelling.  
As a responsible corporate citizen, the Company announced a strategic partnership with Swiggy to 
empower delivery partners across India. As part of its CSR initiative, Ride Safe India, this partnership 
will deliver comprehensive road safety training to Swi ggy’s delivery partners, combining digital 
learning modules with instructor -led sessions. The program aims to promote preventive safety 
practices, encoura ge responsible riding behaviour  and foster a culture of road safety among delivery 
partners.  
VIDA SUST AINS STRONG RETAIL MOMENTUM  
VIDA , Hero MotoCorp's Emerging Mobility business unit , sustained its strong growth momentum in 
September 2026 with dispatch es of 28,798  units and a healthy  VAHAN growth of  83%.   
 
During the month, VIDA VX2 powered its way into the Asia Book of Records, becoming the first 
electric scooter to tow a passenger aircraft over a record distance . This extraordinary feat put the 
spotlight on the VX2’s formidable torque, exceptional durability and engineering  prowess of the VIDA 
platform.  The brand also strengthened its commitment to nurturing the spirit of sport and inspiring 
excellence by serving as the Title Sponsor of India’s Cricket Tour of New Zealand.
