New Delhi, August 1, 2026  
 
HERO MOTOCORP DELIVERS STRONG DOUBLE -DIGIT GROWTH WITH 5.33 LAKH 
DISPATCHES IN JULY 2026  
 
Hero MotoCorp , the world's largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters , maintained its upward 
growth trajectory in July 2026, reporting total dispatches of 533,416 units . This compares to 
449,755  units  during the same period last year, reflecting  a robust 19% year -on-year growth .  
 
VIDA SUSTAINS STRONG RETAIL MOMENTUM; EXPANDS GLOBAL FOOTPRINT  
VIDA , Hero MotoCorp's Emerging Mobility business unit , sustained its strong performance in July 2026 
with  dispatch of  22,714  units. The business expanded its international footprint with its first global 
debut into Nepal, marking a significant milestone in Hero MotoCorp's electric mobility journey. VIDA 
also unveiled its refreshed brand identity, VOOM, and introduced a new high -capacity 4.4 kWh 
variant, offering enhanced range and performance.  
 
DOMESTIC ICE BUS INESS MAINTAINS ROBUST RETAIL MOMENTUM  
The Company's domestic ICE business  recorded dispatches 478,689 units  in July 2026, compared to 
401,171  units  in the same period last year, reflecting approximately 19% year -on-year growth .  
 
This sustained momentum was driven by healthy retail performance across key segments. Both 
Splendor and new Passion maintained strong retail traction, complemented by robust performance 
from the Glamour X and upgraded Super Splendor XTEC 2.0. The scooter p ortfolio also delivered 
significant growth, with the Destini and Xoom series leading the category. Complementing its 
operational milestones, Hero MotoCorp launched a new marketing initiative for the Hero Destini, 
titled "Hero Ka Scooter, Scooter Ka Hero." Centered on imaginative storytelling, the campaign 
highlights the heartwarming bond between a father and daughter, positioning the Destini as a trusted 
family partner.  
 
GLOBAL BUSINESS EXPANDS ITS MARKET PRESENCE  
The Company’s Global Business unit recorded dispatches of 32,013  units in July 2026 , driven by 
aggressive  international expansion . Strengthening  its global presence , the company officially entered 
Ecuador and Germany . The entry into Germany marks a major milestone as Hero MotoCorp’s 53rd 
global market, reinforcing its strategy to deliver sustainable, premium mobility solutions to 
international markets.  
 
COMMITMENT TO “MAKE IN INDIA  FOR INDIA AND THE WORLD ” CONTINUES  
In a significant move to strengthen its operational f oundation  and supply chain network , Hero  
MotoCorp  laid the foundation stone for its second state -of-the-art Global Parts Center (GPC) in 
Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh. This new facility will serve as a strategic hub for domestic and export spare 
parts logistics. Furthermore, the expansion aims to scale up annual production capacity at the 
Tirupati plant —where 100% of Hero MotoCorp’s EV portfolio is engineered and built. Coupled with 
ongoing investments in Ather Energy and EV ecosystems, this move unders cores the company's 
commitment to sustainable mobility and long -term vision.
