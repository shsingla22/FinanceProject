PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Group Debuts Flagship ‘Brigade Showcase’ in Hyderabad on February 21 and 22  
 
The flagship property exhibition will showcase residential projects in Hyderabad, alongside upcoming launches and 
exclusive event -only offers.  
 
Hyderabad, February 20, 2026:  Brigade Group’s growth strategy focuses on three key South Indian markets 
– Bengaluru, Chennai , and Hyderabad. With Hyderabad closely aligned to Bengaluru in terms of 
demographics and economic growth, the Group is debuting its flagship property exhibition, Brigade 
Showcase , in the city. This follows the successful debut of the Showcase in Chennai in 2025, and builds on the 
success of 18 earlier editions held in Bengaluru. The event will take place on February 21 and 22 at Park 
Hyatt Hyderabad, from 10 AM to 8 PM. The exhibition will feature over 12 exclusive projects and four new 
launches across key markets in Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, and Mysuru. Brigade Showcase will offer 
attendees direct access to Brigade Group’s latest  architectural landmarks and completed projects, making it 
an ideal opportunity for homebuyers. Our focus is on the three big South Indian markets of Bengaluru, 
Chennai and Hyderabad,  
 
“As we look to expand operations in Hyderabad after the successful launch of our third project in the city, 
introducing the Brigade Showcase underlines our commitment to providing the city’s potential homebuyers 
with premium living spaces and high -value in vestment opportunities. Hyderabad remains a key market in 
our growth strategy, supported by its rapid urban development and investor -friendly ecosystem, which 
position it among India’s most promising real estate destinations. Through this platform, we will  showcase 
thoughtfully designed, high -quality developments that reflect the city’s evolving lifestyle aspirations, while 
engaging directly with the local community through exclusive offerings available during this inaugural 
showcase.” said Amar Mysore, Exe cutive Director, Brigade Enterprises Ltd.  
 
Brigade Showcase will also feature limited time offers, including gold worth up to INR 5 Lakh for every 
booking made during the event.  
 
About Brigade Group : Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers 
with close to four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and winning 
their customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark b uildings and transformed skylines across 
India in the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, and GIFT City 
with developments across Residential, Office, Retail, Hospitality and Education Sectors.
