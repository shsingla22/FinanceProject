Dear Sir / Madam,  
In continuation of intimation  letter dated October 21, 2023, and in accordance with Regulation 
30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the 
Company had earlier intimated to the Stock Exchanges its plan to enhance the annual 
manufacturing capacity  of ABS and Polystyrene in a phased manner, as part of its growth and 
expansion strategy.  
The Company wishes to update that the proposed capacity enhancement of ABS is going on 
and with respect to the Polystyrene , the Company has successfully enhanced its annual 
manufacturing capacity from 66 KT to 100 KT through debottlenecking . Considering the 
prevailing market conditions and demand outlook, the Company believes that the enhanced 
capacity of 100 KT is sufficient to meet the current and foreseeable market requirements. 
Accordingly, the Company has decided to delay further capaci ty expansion of Polystyrene 
looking to market conditions in future.
