OneSource Receives USFDA EIR with VAI Classification, 
Marking Completion of Inspection Process at its Sterile 
Product Division  (SPD)  Facility in Bengaluru  
 
Bengaluru, India, October 30, 2026 – OneSource Specialty Pharma Limited (BSE: 544292, NSE: 
ONESOURCE) today announced that it has received the Establishment Inspection Report (EIR) from 
the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (USFDA) for its Sterile Product Division (SPD) facility in 
Bengaluru , with a 'Voluntary Action Indicated' (VAI)  classification. The report follows an inspection 
of the facility conducted from June 22 to June 30, 2026.  
 
Commenting on the development, Neeraj Sharma, Managing Director & CEO, OneSource 
Specialty Pharma, said,  “We are pleased to receive the EIR with VAI classification from the USFDA 
for our SPD facility. This inspection outcome reflects our continued commitment to maintaining high 
standards of quality, regulatory compliance and operational excellence across our operations. Our 
SPD facility plays an integral role in our sterile manufacturing capabilities, enabling us to support the 
evolving needs of our global partners.”  
 
The development adds to OneSource’s regulatory track record as it continues to build a differentiated, 
globally competitive CDMO platform spanning four key modalities — drug -device combination, 
biologics, sterile injectables and soft gelatin capsules.  
 
About OneSource Specialty Pharma  Limited  
OneSource Specialty Pharma Limited (BSE: 544292, NSE: ONESOURCE) is a pure -play specialty 
pharmaceutical CDMO. The company focuses on the development and manufacturing of complex 
pharmaceutical products including biologics, drug -device combinations, steril e injectables, and oral 
technologies (soft gelatine capsules). It has five state -of-the-art manufacturing facilities approved by 
global regulatory authorities and a dedicated team of over 1, 600 professionals. OneSource with its 
development capabilities, industry leading manufacturing capacities, and strong compliance track 
record, has won trust of global pharmaceutical companies seeking efficient, end -to-end solutions.
