BLS International Categorically Rejects Allegations of Involvement in Visa 
Irregularities 
BLS International categorically rejects any involvement in the alleged irregularities 
referred to in media reports. There is no evidence establishing any wrongdoing by BLS 
International or its employees in relation to the issuance of visas. 
BLS International’s role is strictly administrative and is carried out in accordance with 
the requirements prescribed by the competent authorities. All decisions relating to the 
processing and issuance or refusal of visas rest solely with the relevant diplomatic and 
consular authorities. BLS International remains committed to the highest standards of 
integrity, transparency, compliance and service delivery, and to its role as a trusted 
service partner to governments and citizens across the world.
