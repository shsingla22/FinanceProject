Subject - Update on Strategic investment in N Dream AG (“N-Dream”).   
 
Ref -    Disclosure of events & information pursuant to Regulation 30 SEBI (Listing  
    Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, (“Listing Regulations”) 
 
This is with reference to our earlier intimation dated November 9, 2023, regarding approval of 
the Board of Directors of the Company for the strategic investment of 13% in N-Dream. Further 
on July 24, 2024, we had informed about additional acquisition of 13% shareholding in N-Dream 
to take the total shareholding of KPIT to 26%. Further on August 8, 2025, we had informed about 
increasing stake in N-Dream to 51% at a consideration of Euro 6.5 million. Consequently N-Dream 
will become a subsidiary of the Company. Going forward the Company shall acquire a stake up 
to 90% in two or more tranches during the current financial year.  
  
Further on October 7, 2025, we had informed about acquisition of further stake of 62.9% by the 
Company, through its wholly owned subsidiary, KPIT Technologies (UK) Limited taking the total 
of KPIT group shareholdings to 88.9% in N-Dream at a consideration of EURO 16.35 million. As a 
result, N-Dream has become a step-down subsidiary of the Company. 
  
Now we wish to inform you that the Company, through its wholly owned subsidiary, KPIT 
Technologies (UK) Limited, has acquired further stake of 1.1 % at a consideration of EURO 2.82 
million, taking the total of KPIT group shareholdings to 90% in N-Dream. 
  
N-Dream AG is a Cloud based Game Aggregation Platform company, based in Switzerland. This 
strategic investment in N-Dream is part of KPIT’s roadmap to enable Automotive OEMs enhance 
the driver & passenger experience in the Cockpit of the Future. KPIT will offer complementary 
software integration & validation services to N-Dream’s Automotive clients. Both parties will 
collaborate towards offering value-added data products for Automotive OEMs, thereby enabling 
them to create additional monetizable experiences & features.
