The 2 4th Annual General Meeting (“AGM”) of the Company was duly convened on Monday , 
August 2 6, 202 4 at 05:00 P.M. through Video Conference (VC) or Other Audio -Visual Means 
(OAVM) facility. The Meeting was conducted in compliance with the relevant provisions of the 
Companies Act, 2013,  Secretarial Standards,  SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure
