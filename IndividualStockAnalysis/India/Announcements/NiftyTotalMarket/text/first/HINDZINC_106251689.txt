Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations,

been declared as 'Preferred Bidder' for the Jhandawali – Satipura Amalgamated Potash  and Halite 
Block of Rajasthan (on highest Final Price Offer of 03.05%) and Nawatola -Laband REE  Block  of Uttar 
Pradesh (on highest Final Price Offer of 3.55%). 
 
As per the tender document, the Jhandawali – Satipura Amalgamated Potash and Halite Block is at 
G3 level of exploration with total area of 1841.22  hectares for the block, and Nawatola -Laband REE 
Block  is at G 3 level of exploration with total area of 201. 01 hectares for the block.

subject to making of necessary payment of Performance Bank Guarantee, completion of other terms 
and conditions of the Tender Document, obtaining of necessary approvals/ permissions/ clearances 
from various Government Departments / Agencies and execution of necessary agreements/deeds in 
the matter.
