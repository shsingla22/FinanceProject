FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE  
 
Emmvee H1 revenue up 45% to ₹3,131.9 crore; PAT up 79% to ₹762.3 crore  
 
Q2 FY27 revenue grows 39% year on year to ₹1,576.4 crore  
 
Bengaluru, 14 October 2026:  Emmvee Photovoltaic Power Limited (NSE: EMMVEE, BSE: 
544608) today announced its consolidated unaudited financial results for the second quarter 
and six months ended 30 September 2026.  
 
For H1 FY27, revenue from operations increased 45.1% to ₹3,131.9 crore, EBITDA grew 
47.4% to ₹1,105.0 crore, and profit after tax (PAT) rose 79.1% to ₹762.3 crore.  
 
For Q2 FY27, revenue from operations grew 39.4% year on year to ₹1,576.4 crore. With a PAT 
increase of 60.6% to ₹382.0 crore.  
Operating and order book highlights  
 
• Solar PV module production in Q2 FY27 was 952 MW, compared with 682 MW in Q2 
FY26.  
• Solar cell production increased to 513 MW from 319 MW in the corresponding quarter 
last year.  
• Effective capacity utilisation in Q2 FY27 stood at 89% for solar cells and 44% for 
modules.  
• The order book stood at 10.4 GW at the end of Q2 FY27, supported by new order 
inflows of approximately 1.5 GW during the quarter.  
As at 30 September 2026, the Company had installed annual capacity of approximately 10.3 
GW in modules and 2.94 GW in TOPCon solar cells. Emmvee is a leading integrated module 
and cell manufacturer.  
 
Capacity expansion and integration  
Emmvee is progressing with its 6 GW integrated TOPCon  module and cell manufacturing 
expansion. Commercial sales and utilisation are expected to ramp up progressively following 
commissioning.  
 
The Company also plans phased backward integration into 9 GW of ingot and wafer 
manufacturing, comprising 5 GW in FY29 and a further 4 GW in FY30. This remains subject 
to regulatory clarity on ALMM III implementation timelines and market conditions. The 
estimated capital expenditure for the proposed ingot and wafer project is ₹ 5,000 -5,500 crore, 
expected to be funded largely through internal accruals.  
 
Balance sheet and cash flow  
 
Net debt to equity was 0.12x as at 30 September 2026. Net cash generated from operating 
activities during H1 FY27 was ₹409.9 crore, compared with ₹120.7 crore in H1 FY26. The 
reduction in finance costs also supported earnings growth during the period.  
 
 
Commenting on the performance, Mr. DV Manjunatha, Chairman & Managing Director, 
Emmvee Photovoltaic Power Ltd., said:  
 
"Our performance during the first half of FY27 reflects continued growth in the business, 
supported by sustained demand, operating efficiencies and the benefits of integrated 
manufacturing. Our 6 GW integrated TOPCon solar cell and module manufacturing fac ility 
expansion project is progressing in line with our schedule, with most orders for hard costs 
already placed. We expect to commission the 6 GW module line by December 2026, followed 
by the 6 GW cell line by March 2027. Commercial sales will be aligned with commissioni ng, 
with utilisation increasing progressively thereafter. Once fully commissioned, the expansion 
will increase our annual manufacturing capacity to approximately 16.3 GW of modules and 
8.9 GW of cells. This represents an important step in deepening backwar d integration, 
strengthening domestic sourcing and supply -chain resilience, and building a more competitive 
business for the long term."  
 
About Emmvee Photovoltaic Power Limited  
 
Established in 2007, Emmvee Photovoltaic Power Limited is an Indian manufacturer of solar 
photovoltaic modules and TOPCon solar cells. The Company has a manufacturing footprint 
concentrated in Karnataka and serves domestic solar markets while expanding its  integrated 
manufacturing capabilities.
