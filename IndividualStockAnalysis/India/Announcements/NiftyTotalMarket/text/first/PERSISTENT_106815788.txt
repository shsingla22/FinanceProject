Persistent Opens Third Hyderabad Facility to 
Strengthen AI and Modernization Delivery   
New facility provides secure, dedicated environments for B anking, 
Financial Services and Insurance  clients   
Octo ber 13, 2026  
Hyderabad  and Pune, India  
News Summary  
Persistent Systems  (BSE: 533179 and NSE: PERSISTENT), a global Digital Engineering and 
Enterprise Modernization leader , has inaugurated its third facility in Hyderabad at Phoenix 
Equinox in Gachibowli. The expansion strengthens Persistent’s ability to deliver programs in AI 
deployment, payments modernization, cloud platforms and operational resilience for banking, 
financial  services and insurance clients.  
 
The facility provides secure, dedicated workspaces for program delivery and areas for 
Persistent and client teams to collaborate on AI deployment and systems modernization. It 
builds on the Company’s Financial Services  expertise and digital engineering capabilities to 
support clients working on critical technology programs within regulated environments.  
 
Persistent has more than 3,000 employees in Hyderabad, with existing offices at WaveRock 
and Sattva Knowledge City. The new facility extends this established presence and supports 
access to engineering and financial services talent in the city’s ecosystem of financial 
institutions, global capability centers and fintech companies . 
Barath Narayanan, Global BFSI and Europe Geo Head, Persistent  
“Financial institutions are putting AI into production while modernizing the systems that underpin 
their businesses. Executing these programs requires domain understanding and engineering 
expertise, with security and operational continuity central to the wo rk. Our new Hyderabad 
facility provides dedicated environments for delivery and closer collaboration with client teams. 
This investment also reflects our long -term commitment to developing financial services 
expertise and building careers in the city.”
