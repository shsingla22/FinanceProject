Zydus Sunshine Lifesciences signs agreement with Board of Investment, Sri Lanka to 
establish a pharmaceutical manufacturing facility in Sri Lanka 
 
Ahmedabad, India, 15 October, 2026  
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
We wish to inform you that Zydus Sunshine Lifesciences (Pvt) Ltd, the proposed joint venture between 
Zydus Lifesciences Limited (Zydus) and Sunshine Healthcare, part of Sunshine Holdings PLC, signed an 
agreement with the Board of Investment of Sri Lanka on 13 October 2026 to establish a pharmaceutical 
manufacturing facility in the BOI zone in Horana, Sri Lanka. Both companies had announced the 
formation of a Joint Venture on 26 June 2026.  
 
The project envisages a phased investment of over USD 20 million. The facility will be developed on a 
site of nearly four acres, with commercial operations expected to commence in 2028, subject to project 
timelines and relevant regulatory approvals. 
 
In its first phase, the facility will manufacture oral solid dosage forms for the Sri Lankan market. The 
investment supports the Zydus’ international presence and commitment to expanding access to quality 
medicines. Local manufacturing is expected to strengthen medicine supply chain resilience and support 
the availability of essential medicines in Sri Lanka. 
 
Kindly take the above information on record.
