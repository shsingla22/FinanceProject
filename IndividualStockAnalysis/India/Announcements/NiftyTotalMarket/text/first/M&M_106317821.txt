In Memoriam  
 
Mahindra Group Mourns the Passing of Mr. T.N. Manoharan, Esteemed 
Board Member and a Champion of Governance and Ethics  
  
Mumbai, July  30, 2025:  With deep sorrow, the Mahindra Group mourns the passing of 
Mr. T.N. Manoharan , our esteemed Independent Director at Mahindra & Mahindra.  A 
Padma Shri awardee , Mr. Manoharan brought exceptional wisdom and stature to our 
Board.  
 
“We are deeply saddened by the tragic and sudden passing of Mr. T.N. Manoharan, our 
Lead Independent Director. His wisdom, integrity and unwavering commitment to good 
governance left an indelible mark on the Mahindra Group ," said Mr. Anand Mahindra  
Chairman, Mahindra Group.  
 
Mr. Manoharan, a distinguished Chartered Accountant, has held pivotal roles in 
prominent banks, government institutions, financial regulatory bodies and acclaimed 
professional organizations. As an Independent Director, Mr Manoharan was a powerful 
voice for upholding the highest standards of corporate governance, ethics and 
transparency. His strategic foresight, business acumen and integrity shaped Mahindra 
Group's long -term vision and strengthened its institutional values.  
 
For us at Mahindra, Mr Manoharan was a guide, mentor and a leader who led with 
example, conviction and compassion. We will sorely miss his care, nurturing and steady 
hand.  
 
Our heartfelt condolences to Mr. Manoharan's family and loved ones.
