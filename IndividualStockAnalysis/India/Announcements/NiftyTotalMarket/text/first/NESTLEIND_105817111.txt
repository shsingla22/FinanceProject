How  to cast your  vote electronically  on NSDL  e-Voting  system?  
1. After  successful  login  at Step 1, you will  be able to see all the companies  “EVEN” in  which  you are holding  shares  and whose  voting  cycle  are active.  
2. Select  “EVEN”  of Nestlé  India  Limited.  
3. Now you are ready  for e-Voting  as the voting  page  opens.  
4. Cast your vote by selecting  appropriate  options  i.e., assent  or dissent,  verify/modify  the number  of shares  for which  you wish to cast your vote and click on “Submit”

7. Once you confirm your vote on the resolution, you will not be allowed to modify your vote.
