Paradeep Phosphates Ltd Posts Q2 FY 2 7 Results :  
02nd November,  2026, Bengaluru:  Paradeep Phosphates Ltd (BSE: 543530, NSE:  
PARADEEP) , one of India ’s leading fertilizer companies announced  its financial 
results for the quarter ended 30th September  2026.  
In Q2 FY27, Revenue  from operations  increased  by 7% YoY to ₹ 7,364 crore , 
EBITDA  reduced by 38% YoY to ₹ 431 crore,  PBT reduced  65% YoY to ₹ 164 crore  
and PAT stood at ₹ 122 crore , down  64% YoY.   
In H1 27, the Company has achieved a Turnover of Rs 13,488 Cr higher by 19%, 
EBIDTA of Rs 1173 Cr . Despite th e global uncertainty,  particularly sulphur pricing / 
availability and impact of the  Rabi notification  is yet to be factored  in, the company 
delivered the EBITDA/ton for H1 of INR 5 866 vis a vis INR 5719 of H1 FY 26 . We 
have been able to achieve sales volume of 1 Mn in Q2 FY 27  and 2 Mn tons in H1 FY 
27 despite subdued monsoon in the country . 
We continue to invest in expansion projects and o ur key expansion project of Phos 
Acid expansion (Phase 1) from 500,000  MTPA to  700,00 0 MTPA at Paradeep is  on 
track . The public hearing for the environmental approval for the capex program  at 
Paradeep has been completed and we are expecting to receive environmental 
approvals  shortly  
The energy efficiency improvement project at Goa has been completed and the energy 
consumption has been optimized by 0.3 GCcal/MT of Urea.  
Looking ahead, we believe  the glob al uncertainty will prevail and we will remain 
committed to deliver consistent performance  through integrated supply chain and 
product mix optimization.  
About Paradeep Phosphates Limited  
Paradeep Phosphates Ltd (PPL) is one of India’s largest phosphatic fertilizer companies, with 
an annual capacity of 3.7 million MT across Paradeep (Odisha), Goa, and Mangalore. The 
company produces DAP, NPK grades (N -10, N -12, N -14, N -19, N -20, N -28), and Urea, and 
markets them under trusted brands Jai Kisaan Navratna  and Jai Kisaan Mangala .  
PPL serves over 1 2 million farmers through 100,000+ retailers across 18 states in India, 
supported by strong backward integration, a robust logistics network, and ESG -driven growth .
