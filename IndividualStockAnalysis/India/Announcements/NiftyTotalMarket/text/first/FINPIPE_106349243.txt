The Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (‘IEPFA’) vide  its circular dated July 16, 
2025, launched  a 100 -day Campaign titled “Saksham Niveshak”  from July 28, 2025 to November

unpaid or unclaimed, and to encourage them to update their details and claim their entitlements 
before the amounts are transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF) .

the “Saksham Niveshak” campaign.
