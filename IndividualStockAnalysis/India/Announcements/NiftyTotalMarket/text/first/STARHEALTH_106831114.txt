RECLASSIFICATION OF STHITAPRAJNA ADVISORS LLP FROM PROMOTER GROUP 
CATEGORY TO PUBLIC SHAREHOLDER CATEGORY  
 
The Company Secretary informed the Board that, consequent to the completion of the transmission of 
shares held by late Mr. Rakesh Jhunjhunwala in accordance with the Probate and the directions of IRDAI, 
Mr. Utpal Hemendra Sheth was classified as one of the  Promoters of the Company pursuant to Regulation 
31A of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.  
She informed that Mr. Utpal Hemendra Sheth , being the Designated Partner and Partner of Sthitaprajna 
Advisors LLP, the sai d LLP was classified as a part of the Promoter Group of Mr. Utpal Hemendra Sheth  
in terms of Regulation 2(1)(pp ) of the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure R equirements) Regulations, 
2018. 
She further apprised the Board that  Mr. Utpal Hemendra Sheth  ceased to be the Designated Partner and 
Partner of Sthitaprajna Advisors LLP with effect from August 13, 2026. Pursuant thereto, the Company 
received a request letter dated September 30, 2026 from Sthitaprajna Advisors LLP, seeking its 
reclassification from th e Promoter Group category to the Public Shareholder category and the Company 
has intimated the same to the Stock Exchanges within the prescribed timeline of 24 hours.  
A copy of the disclosure submitted to the Stock Exchanges along with the request letter received from 
Sthitaprajna Advisors LLP was placed before the Board as Annexure A for perusal.  
The Board noted that the conditions for reclassification as prescribed under Regulation 31A of SEBI 
Listing Regulations shall be duly satisfied and the said reclassification will be subject to obtai ning requisite 
approvals. 
Based on the consent of the Stakeholders Relationship Committee, the Board deliberated and passed the 
following resolution:  
 
“RESOLVED THAT pursuant to Regulation 31A of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended, and other applicable 
provisions, if any, and subject to the receipt of requisite approvals as may be required, the cons ent of the 
Board of Directors be and is hereby accorded for the reclassification of Sthitaprajna Advisors LLP, from 
Promoter Group Category to Public Shareholder Category.  
 
“RESOLVED FURTHER THAT any one Director or Company Secretary be and are hereby severally 
authorised to make necessary applications, filings, disclosure and take all such steps as may be necessary 
to give effect to this resolution including filing the application with the Stock E xchange(s) and any other 
regulatory authority(ies) as may be required.”
