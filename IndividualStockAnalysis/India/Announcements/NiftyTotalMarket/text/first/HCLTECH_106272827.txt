HCLTech named a Leader in Everest Group's Intelligent Document Processing 
Products PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2025  
 
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, June 19, 2025—HCLTech,  a leading global technology 
company, has been recognized as a Leader in Everest Group's Intelligent Document Processing 
(IDP) Products PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2025.  
 
The Everest Group report highlights HCLTech's GenAI -driven approach to IDP, particularly 
through its flagship product EXACTO ™, a domain- centric machine learning product based on 
next-generation autonomous intelligence that automatically extracts information from a variety of 
sources . HCLTech's  advanced IDP capabilities, AI -driven tools and process expertise help 
enterprises in banking, manufacturing, insurance, healthcare, hi -tech, telecom, media and 
entertainment boost productivity and enhance customer experience with higher precision and 
faster decisions.  
 
"HCLTech has strengthened its position to emerge as a Leader in the Intelligent Document 
Processing (IDP) Products PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2025 ," says Vaibhav Bansal, Vice 
President, Everest Group. Key enhancements include a virtual agent that converts voice to text 
and processes information using GenAI and IDP , as well as deeper integration of HCLTech 
EXACTO™ with its digitalCOLLEAGUE , Operations Command Center  and Toscana platforms to 
enable intelligent workflow automation and insight generation. HCLTech is also investing in 
Agentic AI , exploring partnerships and developing use case- specific agentic solutions. Clients 
appreciate HCLTech's strong collaboration, consistency of capture and impact on cost savings 
and stakeholder experience."  
 "At HCLTech, we lead with innovation, modernizing  workflows through adaptive learning and 
customizable solutions. Our AI Force and GenAI capabilities enhance performance, while our 
focus on robust security ensures trust  and resiliency . Investing in cutting- edge technologies and 
strategic partnerships, we continue to enhance our IDP capabilities and deliver transformative 
and impactful solutions across industries," said Raghu Kidambi, Corporate Vice President and 
Global Head, Digital Pr ocess Operations, HCLTech.
