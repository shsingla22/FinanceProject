registering a growth of 4.92%.

registering a growth of 14.55%. 
• Achieved NLCIL (Thermal) Power Gross Generation of 8,306.66 MU as against 
7,922.6 MU, registering a growth of 4.85%. 
• Total Revenue from operations for the Half Year ended 30th Sep 2026 is Rs. 
5,562.61 Cr against Rs. 5,060.51 Cr in the corresponding period of previous year, 
registering a growth of 9.92%. 
• Total Comprehensive Income (Comprising Profit or (Loss) and Other 
Comprehensive Income) for the period ended 30th Sep 2026 is Rs.1,623.41 Cr 
against Rs.842.25 Cr in the corresponding period of previous year, registering a 
growth of 92.75%.

in the previous year, registering a growth of 15.25%. 
• The Collection efficiency of Power debtors till 30.09.2026 stood at 104.58 % 
reflecting robust recovery measures and sustained improvement in receivables 
management.
