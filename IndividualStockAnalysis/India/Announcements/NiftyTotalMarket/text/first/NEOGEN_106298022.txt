With reference to the captioned subject and pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the 
Listing Regulations and our intimation of Board meeting dated July 9, 2025, we wish to inform you

inform you that the Company had received an email dated June 12, 2025 at 13:27 hours with subject 
line 542665-Fines as per SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November

attain an age of 75 years. 
 
The said Notice received from BSE levied a fine on the Company and the payment of which was made 
within 15 days from the date of the Notice.
