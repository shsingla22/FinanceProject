including  seeking the approval of its shareholders, ascertaining availability of name from the Registrar of 
Companies  (“ROC” ), applying and obtaining approval for change in name from ROC, applying for 
registration of trademark before the Registrar of Trademarks and carrying out extensive awareness campaign informing the public at large about the change in name of the Company. The Company is
