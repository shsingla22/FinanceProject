LEMON TREE HOTELS ANNOUNCES SIGNING OF LEMON 
TREE HOTEL, RAJENDRA NAGAR , HYDERABAD  
 
Strengthens the company’s footprint in Telangana with a strategic addition in 
Hyderabad  
New Delhi , 3rd March 2026 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -growing 
midscale and premium hotel chain, today announced the signing of Lemon Tree 
Hotel, Rajendra nagar , Hyderabad.  The hotel will be managed by Carnation Hotels 
Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
Hyderabad, the capital of Telangana, is one of India’s leading technology, 
pharmaceutical and infrastructure hubs. Known for its dynamic business 
ecosystem and rich cultural heritage, the city continues to attract corporate, MICE 
and leisure travellers ali ke. Rajendra nagar’s  evolving commercial landscape and 
proximity to key transit points make it a compelling location for branded hospitality 
development.  
The property will feature 63 well -appointed rooms, a restaurant, banquet hall, 
meeting room , a spa, fitness centre and swimming pool . The hotel is conveniently 
located approximately 22 km s from Rajiv Gandhi International Airport, Hyderabad 
(around 32 minutes by road) and about 16 km s from Shamshabad Railway Station 
(approximately 30 minutes), ensuring seamless connectivity for domestic and 
interstate travellers.  
Commenting on the signing, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO – Managed & Franchise 
Business, Lemon Tree Hotels , said,  “Hyderabad continues to be a high -growth 
market for us, driven by its strong corporate base and sustained infrastructure 
development. The signing of Lemon Tree Hotel, Rajendra nagar, Hyderabad  reflects 
our strategy of strengthening presence in key urban centres while tapping into 
emerging micro -markets within metro cities. We remain committed to delivering 
reliable comfort and quality hospitality experiences across Telangana.  
This addition further reinforces Lemon Tree Hotels’ continued focus on expanding 
its managed portfolio in high -potential markets, aligned with evolving travel 
demand across business and leisure segments.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
About Lemon Tree Hotels Limited  
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India’s leading hospitality companies,

premium business and leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels 
& Resorts, Lemon T ree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys 
Select, and Keys Lite – the group offers experiences across upper upscale, upscale, 
upper midscale, midscale, leisure, wildlife, and spiritual segments.  
LTHL operates 120+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing 
pipeline of 130+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, 
Bengaluru, and Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, and 
Indore – and with an international presence in Dubai, Bhutan, and Nepal – Lemon 
Tree Hotels delivers exceptional comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, 
refreshing experience.  
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 260+ 
properties (operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for 
both business and leisure travellers.
