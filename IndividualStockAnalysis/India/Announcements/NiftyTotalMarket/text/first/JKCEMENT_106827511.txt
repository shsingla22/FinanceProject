4) Capacity expansion by Wholly Owned Subsidiary:  The Board took note and approved 
the decision of the Board of JK Maxx Paints Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of the 
Company, to set up a new water-based paint manufacturing plant with a capacity of 75,000 
KLPA at Raghunadhapalem, Suryapet District, Telangana. JK Maxx currently has a 
capacity of 73,000 KLPA (Utilisation rate- 60%) at Bhiwadi, Rajasthan. The new plant will 
involve an estimated capital cost of INR 130 Crore, to be funded within the investment of 
INR 600 Crore in the paints business already approved by the Board and is expected to 
be commissioned in about 12 months from the st art of construction. The rationale for the 
above is that the existing Bhiwadi facility is projected to reach full utilisation by FY 2027-
28.
