Prestige Group and Autodesk Announce Enterprise Partnership to Accelerate Digital 
Transformation Across Real Estate Development  
 
Enabling  enterprise -wide digital  execution, integrated  project delivery, and sustainable 
development across Prestige Group’s portfolio  
 
14 April 2026  – Bengaluru, India  – Prestige Group, one of India’s leading real estate 
developers, today announced a three -year strategic partnership with Autodesk, the global 
Design & Make platform company, to deploy Autodesk Forma, formerly known as Autodesk 
Construction Cloud (ACC) and the Autodesk AEC Collection across its enterprise. This 
landmark engagement marks one of the largest new enterprise collaborations  and 
underscores Prestige Group’s commitment to digitally transforming its end -to-end project 
lifecycl e.  
 
Through this landmark partnership, Prestige Group will standardize on Autodesk’s complete 
Design and Make platform —spanning industry -leading design authoring tools and Autodesk 
Construction Cloud —to create a unified, connected ecosystem across its portfoli o of 
residential, commercial, retail, hospitality, and mixed -use developments. The transformation 
will also introduce process standardization, ecosystem -wide digital alignment, and 
capability -building initiatives for partners —redefining how projects are de signed, managed, 
and delivered across the organization.  Beyond technology deployment, it signals a structural 
shift in operating models —bringing consultants, contractors, and stakeholders into a digitally 
integrated framework designed to support scale, transparency, and long -term resilience.  
 
As part of its broader digital transformation initiative, Prestige Group will additionally 
implement advanced 4D and 5D capabilities at scale —integrating design, scheduling, cost, 
and execution workflows within a unified digital environment. This enterprise -wide 
approach positions Prestige among the first real estate developers globally to pursu e 4D/5D 
implementation at this scale using Autodesk technologies.  
 
“Prestige Group has always believed in setting new benchmarks in quality and execution. As 
we scale our portfolio and vision, digital transformation becomes central to how we operate,” 
said Mr. Vishesh Ka ul, Chief Information & Digital Officer, Prestige Group . “By partnering 
with Autodesk, we are building a connected digital backbone that unifies design and 
execution across our enterprise. This move enables greater collaboration, improved efficiency, 
and stronger control across our projects while positionin g us for a more sustainable and 
future -ready growth journey.”
