meeting held today i.e., April 1 4, 2025 , has allotted 35,61,20,505 equity shares having face value

i.e. April 7, 2025, pursuant to the Scheme .

having face value of INR 2  (Rupees Two)  each ] stands cancelled and reduced, without any 
consideration as an integral part of the Scheme pursuant to Sections 230 to 232 of the Companies 
Act, 2013.

effect from April 1 4, 2025 .
