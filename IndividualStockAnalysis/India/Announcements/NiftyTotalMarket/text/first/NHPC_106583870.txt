Sirs/महोदय, 
 
In compliance to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015,  it is to inform that the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) has 
approved investment of ₹26,069.50 Crore for construction of 1720 MW Kamala Hydro Electric 
Project (HEP) in Kamle, Kra Daadi & Kurung Kumey Districts of Arunachal Pradesh. The estimated 
completion period for the project is 96 months.  The installed capacity of Kamala HEP is 1720 MW (8 
x 210 MW & 1 x 40 MW), which is expected to generate 6870 MUs of energy annually. 
 
The Project will be implemented through a Joint Venture Company between NHPC Limited 
and the Govt. of Arunachal Pradesh. Govt. of India shall extend ₹4,743.98 crore as budgetary 
support towards flood moderation component and ₹1,340 crore as budgetary support for enabling 
infrastructure viz. construction of roads, bridges and associated transmission system besides 
Central Financial Assistance of ₹750 crore towards the equity share of the State. 
 
This is for your information  and record.
