This is to inform you that: 
 
a) Pursuant to the MCA Circulars and SEBI Circulars issued from time to time, the 53rd Annual General Meeting (AGM) of

for casting vote during the AGM.

entitlement of the members of the Company to receive Dividend is Wednesday, 13th August, 2025.

will remain closed from Thursday, 14st August, 2025 to Wednesday, 20th August, 2025 (both days inclusive)  for the 
purpose of Annual General Meeting.
