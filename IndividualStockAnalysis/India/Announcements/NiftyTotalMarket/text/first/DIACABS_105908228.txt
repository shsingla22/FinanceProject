ABOUT DIAMOND POWER INFRASTRUCTURE LIMITED: 
About Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (DPIL) is India’s One of the Oldest Cables and Conductors 
Manufacturers of India and one of the fastest growing FMEG companies with a consolidated turnover of INR 
343 cr. DPIL is India’s Single Largest Single Location Power Cables and Conductors manufacturer having 5 CCV 
lines and an Installed capacity of more then 2,50,000 Mtpa of Conductors ,the company is at the forefront of 
providing Robust ,innovative, energy efficient products to a diverse set of customers . 
DPIL was acquired through the NCLT process by GSEC-Monarch Group, DPIL’s business operations span across 
India with a large Manufacturing plant at Vadodara admeasuring 110 acres, the company is in phases re- 
commissioning its production facility and expected to complete the process by June 2025. DPIL has also served 
customers in 12+ countries globally. DPIL's 800+ employees are dedicated to upholding robust governance 
practices, preserving a customer centric culture, having a purpose to serve the communities, and imbibing a 
genuine sense of ecological consciousness, companies are products are certified for EPD and Product Lifecycle.
