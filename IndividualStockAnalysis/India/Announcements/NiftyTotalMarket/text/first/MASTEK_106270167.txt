Mastek Awarded NHS England Contract for Board and SIRO 
Cybersecurity Training  
Mumbai, India, 16th June  2025 : Mastek (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted digital engineering and 
cloud transformation partner, is excited to announce that it has been awarded a contract by NHS 
England to provide NHS board and Senior Information Risk Owner (SIRO) cybersecurity training in 
partnership with Templar Executives . This training is aimed at NHS B oard s and executives to help 
enhance their understanding of the cyber landscape and build resilience across the healthcare system.  
The one- year contract, with the option to extend for an additional year, will see Mastek and its partner 
Templar Executives, deliver tailored cybersecurity training specifically designed for NHS executive 
Boards and SIROs. The training will focus on equipping these critical leaders with the knowledge and 
skills needed to effectively manage cyber risks, ensure compliance with legislation, and foster a culture 
of cyber resilience within their organisations.  
As part of the contract, Mastek and Templar Executives will provide two key training services:  
1. SIRO Training : This program me is aimed at the crucial roles of SIRO and Deputy SIRO within NHS 
organisations. It will help these leaders  and their teams  gain essential knowledge to improve 
cyber resilience and support positive clinical, financial, and patient -centred outcomes.  
2. Board Training : Targeted at NHS executive B oards, this training will ensure Board members fully 
understand their responsibilities in governance, leadership, and compliance with relevant cybersecurity legislation.  
Abhishek Singh, President of UKI & Europe at Mastek , commented, “We are happy to expand our 
support into NHS England in building stronger cybersecurity leadership across the NHS. As healthcare 
systems continue to become more digital, effective cyber governance at the B oard level is essential to 
safeguarding patient data and ensuring the resilience of critical services.”  
Rekha Babber, MD Cyber Academy, Templar Executives , commented, “ We are delighted to be 
partnering with Mastek to deliver  training assured by the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) . With 
the growing integration of healthcare services, o ur tailored approach and experience  encourages Boards 
and executives
 to take accountability for  cyber and information risk, alongside clinical and financial risk , 
to deliver  quality patient care .”  
The partnership with NHS England reflects Mastek’s continued commitment to supporting the public 
sector in building cyber resilience, ensuring that essential services remain secure in an increasingly 
digital world.  
For more information about Mastek and its cybersecurity services, please visit www.mastek.com .
