Lemon Tree Hotels signs three new propert ies in Rajasthan   
 
New Delhi , 2nd September  2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels announces the signing  of 
three new properties  – Lemon Tree Premier, Pushkar, Lemon Tree Premier, 
Ajmer  and Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels, Ajmer . These propert ies shall be 
managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon 
Tree Hotels Limited.  
 
Lemon Tree  Premier, Pushkar  will feature 96 well-appointed rooms , a restaurant , 
a conference room , a swimming pool , a fitness center and a spa along with other 
public areas . The Kishangarh Airport is approximately 39 kms away from the 
property, while the Ajmer  Railway Station is about 14 kms away. The hotel is well 
connected by roadways for both public and private transpo rt.  
 
Lemon Tree Premier, Ajmer  will feature 78 well-appointed rooms , a restaurant , a 
lounge, a banquet hall, a conference room, a swimming pool, a fitness center and a 
spa along with other public areas . The Kishangarh Airport Rajasthan is approximately 
30 kms away from the property, while the Ajmer Railway Station is about 6 kms 
away. The hotel is well connected by roadways for both public and private transport.   
 
Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels , Ajmer  will feature 31 well-appointed rooms , a 
restaurant , a conference room, a fitness center , along with other public areas . The 
Kishangarh Airport Rajasthan is approximately 24 kms away from the property, while 
the Ajmer Railway Station is about 2 kms away. The hotel is well connected by 
roadways for both publi c and private transport.   
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels , commented , "We are delighted to further enhance  and 
strengthen our footprint in Rajasthan , where we currently operate 11 hotels and have 
seven  more upcoming ones . These signings will align with our strategy of expanding 
across spiritual locations , as this is a rapidly expanding sector and we look to delight 
our guests with these comfortable options. "
