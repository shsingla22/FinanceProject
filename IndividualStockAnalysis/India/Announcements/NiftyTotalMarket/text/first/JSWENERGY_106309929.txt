JSW Energy Signs PPA with BESCOM for Solar  Project  with Battery Storage  
 
Mumbai, Ind ia — July 24, 2025  – JSW Renew Energy Forty  Five Limited , a step -down  subsidiary 
of JSW Energy Limited (“ the Company”), has signed Power Purchase Agreement with Bang alore 
Electricity Supply Company (BESCOM) for a grid connected 100MW Solar Project with 100MWh 
Battery Energy Storage System  (BESS) . 
The aforesaid project  will be developed on a designated site and connected to the 220/400 kV 
KPTCL Firozabad substation in Kalaburagi district. The Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) has been 
signed for a tenure of 25 years at a tariff of ₹4.31 per kWh.  
Subsequent to this t he Comp any's total locked -in energy storage capacity stands at 29. 4 GWh, 
including 3.0 GWh of BESS and 26.4 GWh of Pumped Hydro Storage. The Company is well -
positioned to achieve its target of 40 GWh of energy storage by 2030.
