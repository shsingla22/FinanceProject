COMPANY STATEMENT  
 
Biocon Announces U.S. FDA Approval for Yesintek ® 
(ustekinumab -kfce ) Single -dose Prefilled Auto injector  
 
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, August 18 , 2026 
  
Biocon Limited’s subsidiary in the United States has received supplemental U.S. Food and 
Drug Administration (FDA) approval for Yesintek® 45 mg/0.5  ml single -dose prefilled 
autoinjector and Yesintek® 90 mg/ml single -dose prefilled autoinjector . Yesintek® was 
originally approved by the U.S. FDA on November 29, 2024 . 
 
The Yesintek® single -dose prefilled autoinjector offers patients  with another important 
treatment option. This new delivery format supports more tailored treatment approaches 
across different care settings and patient needs.  
 
Yesintek® is indicated for the treatment of moderate to severe plaque psoriasis and active 
psoriatic arthritis in adult and pediatric patients 6  years of age  and older , and moderate to 
severely active Crohn’s disease and ulcerative colitis in adults —a range of debilitating 
autoimmune conditions that affect s tens of thousands of Americans . 
 
This supplemental approval enhances Biocon’s comprehensive portfolio of immunology 
products in the United States and r eaffirms the company’s commitment to i mproving access 
to affordable medicines for patients around the world.
