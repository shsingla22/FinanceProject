Acquisition of  100% equity stake of  Kandla G HA Transmission Limited  
 
The Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today, have approved 
acquisition of 100% equity stake of Kandla GHA Transmission Limited  (“KGTL ”), from PFC 
Consulting Limited ( “PFCCL ”), for an aggregate consideration not exceeding  
Rs. 20 crore . This is i n accordance  with the terms of the tender awarded to the Company , 
for establishment of Turnkey Construction of 765/400kV GIS Substation at Kandla 
including Transformers  and Reactors, 765kV Transmission lines between Halvad and 
Kandla, 765kV Bay extension at Halvad and STATCOM . Post acquisition, KGTL  will 
become a wholly owned subsidiary of the  Company . 
 
KGTL  was incorporated in India on November  27, 2024  and is yet to commence 
commercial operations . 
 
The said investment will enable the Company to execute the Independent Transmission 
Project (ITP) “ Transmission System for Supply of Power to Green Hydrogen / Ammonia 
manufacturing potential in Kandla area of Gujarat (Phase -I: 3 GW) ”. 
 
The acquisition is not a related party transaction and none of the Company’s promoter  / 
promoter group / group companies have any interest in the above transaction.  
 
The Company does not require any governmental or regulatory approval for the said 
acquisition.
