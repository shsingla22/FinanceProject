Members Present:  
 The meeting was attended by 105 Members . 
 Proceedings: 
 
The Company Secretary & Compliance Officer, Ms. Trushali Shah , extended a warm and hearty 
welcome to everyone present at the Meeting.  She then introduced the Board members and other 
officials of the Company.  
 Mr. Mukesh Gupta, Chairman of the Board of Directors took the Chair and presided over the Meeting.  
The Chairman announced that the requisite quorum was present and the M eeting was called to order.  
He further mentioned that since the A GM was being held through VC, appointment of proxies by 
Members was not allowed.  
 
The Chairman apprised the Members about the performance of the Company and its future prospects 
and answered all queries raised by the Shareholders.  
 
The Company Secretary then explained to the Members the process of casting votes during the A GM. 
Subsequently, the Company Secretary requested shareholders attending the Meeting, who had not 
already voted via remote e -voting, to cast their votes using the e- voting facility provided by NSDL for 
the items of business outlined in the Notice of the 47
th Annual General Meeting
