Pursuant to  Regulation  30  SEBI  (Listing  Obligations  and  Disclosure  Requirements)  Regulation
2015,  it is  hereby  inform  that a  incident  occurred  at  Godawari  Power  and  lspat  limited  (GPIL)
due  to  collapse  of refractory  of side  wall  of travel  grate  of the  iron  ore  pellet  plant  situated  at
Siltara Industrial Area,  Siltara,  Raipur Chhattisgarh on  Friday on  26th  September,  2025,  evening
around  5.00  PM  during  the  routine  plant  inspection  activity  causing  death  of 6  employees  and
injury  to  6  other  employees  working  in  the  vicinity.  The  injured  employees  were  immediately
rushed  to  the  hospital  for  medical  treatment.  Out  of 6  injured,   I   employee  has  already  been
discharged and the condition of other 5  employees  is also stable and  hopefully all  of them  will  be
discharged duly cured within a week's time.
The management of GPIL deeply mourns the unfortunate death of 6 employees in the incidence.
An  internal  enquiry  is  being  conducted  for  a thorough  investigation  and  GPIL  is  committed  to
taking   all   necessary   measures   to   prevent   reoccurrence   of  such   incident   in   future.   Till   the
completion of internal enquiry and due diligence to determine the route cause of the  incident and
till the resetting of the refractory lining in the travel  grate of the pellet plant, the operations of the
iron ore pellet plant will be closed.
