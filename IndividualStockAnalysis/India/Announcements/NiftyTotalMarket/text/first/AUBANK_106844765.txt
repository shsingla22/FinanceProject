Sub: Reply to clarificatio n sought by Stock Exchanges  
 
This is with reference to the clarification sought by the Stock Exchanges dated November 02, 
2026 in relation to the media report titled “AU Bank probes potential data leak”.  
  
In this regard, we wish to clarify that the Bank became aware (on October 29, 2026) about a 
potential data leakage incident involving a third -party Software -as-a-Service (“SAAS”) platform. 
This platform, which is hosted and managed by a reputed global third -party vendor , is used for 
onboarding Bank’s vehicle loan customers and contains customers’ information obtained at the 
time of loan onboarding.  
  
Based on the preliminary findings so far, the incident may have resulted in data leakage of some 
of the Bank’s vehicle loan customers. The Bank is undertaking a detailed internal and external 
forensic investigation, with the help of leading cyber security firms, to ascertain the nature and 
extent of potential data leakage (there are ~28.5 lacs vehicle loan customers onboarded through 
this platform).  
  
The Bank’s other systems remain unaffected, and all services are operating normally. The 
incident does not have any impact on the Bank’s business operations, financial performance or 
business continuity . We hereby reaffirm to all stakeholders that customer protection continues 
to be our highest priority . 
  
The matter has been reported to regulatory authorities including the Reserve Bank of India and 
CERT -In in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.  
  
The Bank continues to closely monitor the situation and will make further disclosures, if required, 
in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.  
  
This is for your information and records.
