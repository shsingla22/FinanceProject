Indo -American Chamber of Commerce Felicitates Dr. Lalit 
Kanodia for His Contribution to US -India Business Relations  
 
Mumbai, 27th August , 2026 : Datamatics, a global Digital Technologies, Operations, 
and Experiences company, today announced that Dr. Lalit S. Kanodia, Founder and 
Chairman of the Datamatics Group of Companies, was felicitated by the Indo -American 
Chamber of Commerce (IACC) at its Nat ional Convention held in Mumbai on August 
18, 2026.  
 
The honour recognizes Dr. Kanodia ’s exemplary leadership, distinguished service, and 
enduring contribution to IACC and to strengthening business and economic relations 
between the United States and India  during his tenure as President of the Chamber.  
Dr. Kanodia was felicitated in the presence of Mr. Devendra Fadnavis, Hon ’ble Chief 
Minister of Maharashtra, and H.E. Mr. Sergio Gor, US Ambassador to India.  
IACC is committed to promoting bilateral trad e, commerce, and investment between 
the United States and India . Dr. Kanodia served as National President of the Indo -
American Chamber of Commerce during 2015 –2016. During his tenure, he 
championed stronger economic engagement between the two countries, wi th a focus 
on expanding bilateral trade and creating greater opportunities for businesses in both 
markets.  
 
 

 
Commenting on the recognition, Dr. Lalit S. Kanodia, Founder and Chairman, 
Datamatics Group of Companies, said , “It is an honour to be recognized b y the Indo -
American Chamber of Commerce. I have always believed in the immense potential of 
India and the United States  working more closely together. Technology, innovation and 
entrepreneurship continue to create new opportunities for businesses in both c ountries, 
and I am proud to have contributed to strengthening this important relationship. ” 
A pioneer of India ’s technology and offshore services industry, Dr. Kanodia was 
instrumental in setting up Tata Consultancy Services in 1967 and founded Datamatics 
in 1975. His vision of building globally competitive technology capabilities from India 
has shaped Datamatics ’ journey for over five decades.  
The recognition of Dr. Kanodia celebrates his enduring contribution to industry, 
entrepreneurship, and US-India  business collaboration, and his continuing legacy in 
shaping Datamatics ’ global journey.
