Sub:  Clarification under Regulation 30 (11) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and    
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  (“Listing Regulations”)  
  
The Company has come across an Article titled “M&M may drive its key units down 
separate paths” dated 9th October  2025 published in Economic Times newspaper and 
similar article has been published online which references an ongoing review of hiving off 
its various divisions.  
  
Mahindra Group weighs major restructuring; may spin off tractors, PVs & trucks into 
separate entities - The Economic Times  
  
Similar articles have also been published in other mainstream media.  
  
In view of the above speculation, the Company on its own considers it necessary to clarify 
to the Stock Exchanges that there is no plan for a  demerger  of the Auto and Tractor 
businesses. The Company has clarified this in the past and maintains that it sees much 
greater value from synergies by keeping these businesses within the M&M entity.  
 
The Company has complied with and continues to comply with the relevant disclosure 
norms under the Listing Regulations.
