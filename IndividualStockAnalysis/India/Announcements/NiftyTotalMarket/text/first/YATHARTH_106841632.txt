Yatharth Hospitals Delivers Another Quarter of Strong  Performance  
Laying a Solid Foundation for the Next Phase  of Growth, with Advent Partnership
 
Noida, November 3, 2026: Yatharth Hospital and Trauma Care Services Ltd (“the Company”) (NSE: YATHARTH, 
BSE: 543950), a leading private super speciality hospital chain in North India, today announced its financial 
results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026.

Key Highlights  for the Quarter  
 
• Revenue reaches a record Rs 4,314 mn, up 5 2% YoY and 10% QoQ  
✓ New hospitals contribute  Rs 1,205 mn, contributing 28% to Group’s revenue  (vs 11% in Q2 
FY26), led by ramp -up across Greater Faridabad, Model Town, Faridabad Sector -20 and Agra  
✓ Mature hospitals sustained strong momentum,  growing 24% YoY   
✓ Total Volumes  grew  50%  YoY; Inpatient Volumes  were up by 67% YoY  
 
• Group ARPOB reaches Rs 35,948, up 1 1% YoY  
✓ Premium NCR hospitals  of Noida Extension and New Delhi approached ~Rs 50k ARPOB -mark  
 
• EBITDA reaches record Rs 1,020 mn, up 5 0% YoY and 11% QoQ  
✓ EBITDA margin s improve d sequentially by 30 bps , as newer hospitals  turned profitable  
✓ Acquisition playbook delivering early success , as Faridabad Sector -20 and Agra  stood EBITDA 
positive in H1; New Delhi expected to breakeven in H2  
✓ Adjusted EBITDA (excluding ramp -up impact of newer hospitals ) stood at 28%  
 
• PAT at Rs 485 mn, up 17% YoY , despite higher depreciation from capacity additions  
✓ Cash Profits  (PAT plus depreciation) at  Rs 816 mn, up 36% YoY , reflecting the underlying 
earnings strength of the expanded network  
✓ Operating cash flow (pre -tax) of Rs 1,113 mn, up 49% YoY  in H1  
✓ Total cash  and bank balances  stood at  Rs 4,289 mn 
 
 
 
Strategic Partnership with Advent - Building the platform for the next phase  
 
• Definitive agreement with Advent International  for a  primary capital investment of Rs 3,150 crore  
through issuance  of equity shares and warrants , awaiting regulatory approvals  
✓ Advent to hold ~24.9% on full conversion ; the Promoter  family remains the largest shareholder  
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Yatharth Tyagi, Whole Time Director, Yatharth Hospitals said:  
“Q2 FY2027 marks a defining milestone in Yatharth Hospitals’ growth journey. Our performance continues to 
reinforce the strength of our underlying business model and our ability to execute consistently.  Our quarterly 
revenue and profits have nearly tripled since our listing  in 2023 , reflecting our consistent record growth quarter -
over -quarter . Our acquisitions since listing have also progressed strongly, now contributing 28% of Group 
revenue and achieving EBITDA breakeven  within a short period, underscoring our ability to successfully 
integrate and scale acquired assets.  Our strategic partnership with Advent International represents a strong 
endorsement of the platform we have built, our execution capabilities and the significant growth opportunity 
ahead. The partnership brings together growth capital to support the acceleration of our expansion plans to 
~7,000 -8,000 beds over the next few years. Beyond capital, Advent brings global healthcare experience and 
strategic perspective that will be valuable as we scale our platform.  As we embark on this next phase, we remain 
focused on building a larger, more integrated healthcare network while consistently delivering superior clinical 
outcomes, enhancing patient experience and creating sustainable long -term value for all our stakeholders.”

About Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Ltd:  
Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Limited (Yatharth Hospitals) is one of North India’s leading healthcare 
providers, known for delivering high -quality medical care through a network of state -of-the-art super -speciality 
hospitals. The Group operates a robust network across key regions including Noida, Greater Noida, Noida Extension, 
Greater Faridabad, Faridabad Sector -20, New Delhi and Jhansi -Orchha in Madhya Pradesh, with a growing presence 
in Agra and an upcoming hospital in Gurugram. With over 2,80 0 beds across 9 hospitals, including the upcoming 
Gurugram hospital, and an overall announced capacity of ~3,255 beds, Yatharth Hospitals has consistently 
strengthened its footprint through a combination of organic growth and strategic acquisitions. Yatharth Hospitals’ 
has recently announced the strategic partnership with Advent International , with a Rs 3,150 crore investments, 
marking one of the largest primary fund infusion by a private equity in India’s hospital sector.
