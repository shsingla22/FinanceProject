Sub:  Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Update on the Proposed Joint Venture with 
Atomberg Innovation Private Limited  
 
This is in furtherance to our disclosure dated 14th August,  2026, wherein we had informed that 
the Board of Directors of the Voltas Limited (‘Company’ / ‘Voltas’) had approved entering 
into a Binding Term Sheet with Atomberg Innovation Private Limited (‘AIPL’), a subsidiary 
of Atomberg Technologies Limited, for setting up a Joint Venture in India for the development, 
manufacture and commercialization of room air -conditioner compressors.  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (‘SEBI Listing Regulations’), we hereby inform that Voltas has today, i.e. 
19th November, 2026, executed a Joint Venture Agreement (‘JVA’) with AIPL for setting up 
the aforesaid Joint Venture.  
 
In terms of the JVA, Atomberg  Airtech Private Limited ("AAPL"), an existing wholly owned 
subsidiary of AIPL, shall be the joint venture entity. Voltas shall acquire 50% of the equity 
share capital of AAPL from AIPL upon Closing, as contemplated under the JVA, resulting in 
both parties  holding equal shareholding in the Joint Venture. Voltas shall have the right to 
control the composition of the Board of Directors of AAPL and, accordingly, AAPL shall 
become a subsidiary of Voltas upon completion of the transaction. The name of AAPL is 
proposed to be changed following the Closing of the transaction. The Closing is subject to the 
satisfactory fulfilment of the conditions precedent set out in the JVA.  
 
The information as required under Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI Listing 
Regulations read with relevant SEBI Circulars is enclosed in the Annexure.
