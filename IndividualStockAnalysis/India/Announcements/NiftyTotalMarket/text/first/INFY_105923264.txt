Brief background  Unternehmertum  Venture Capital (UVC) Partners is a leading 
Germany based early -stage venture capital firm that invests in 
European B2B tech startups. UVC invests in startups striving to solve global challenges through technology and innovation.  
 The focus areas include AI, Software, Deep Tech, Space Tech, Semiconductors, Quantum, Robotics, Decarbonization, Nuclear Fusion, Hydrogen and Mobility.  
As an exclusive partner of UnternehmerTUM, one of the most 
extensive innovation and startup center in Europe, UVC has access to 
an ecosystem of industrial network and talent from TUM, a leading European technical university.  
 
UVC brings an extensive investment and exit experience, ability to build sustainable category leaders with a competitive advantage, and 
passion for growing the gamechangers of tomorrow.
