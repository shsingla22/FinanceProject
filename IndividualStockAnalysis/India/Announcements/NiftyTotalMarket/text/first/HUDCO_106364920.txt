Sub: Change of Record date

Sir/Madam, 
 
Kindly refer to our letter dated 21st August, 2025 intimating the Calendar of Events of 55th 
Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the Company, inter-alia, intimating Monday, September 8, 
2025 as the Record Date for determining the eligibility of members for payment of final dividend 
for the financial year 2024-25 and subsequent letter dated 23rd August, 2025 regarding Notice 
of 55th AGM scheduled to be held on 15th September, 2025 and Annual Report for Financial 
Year 2024-25. 
 
In continuation of the above, this is to inform that on receipt of requests from the Stock 
Exchanges, due to declaration of clearing/settlement holiday on Monday, 8th September, 2025 
by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), the record date for final dividend is changed from 
Monday, 8th September, 2025 to Tuesday, 9th September, 2025 . Accordingly, the Record 
date referred in the AGM Notice dated 22nd August, 2025, which forms part of the Annual 
Report for Financial Year 2024-25, shall be read as Tuesday, 9th September, 2025.  
 
The final dividend on equity shares, as recommended by the Board of Directors, if declared at 
the AGM, will be paid to the members within 30 days of declaration.
