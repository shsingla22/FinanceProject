In pursuance to Regulation 30 and Schedule III of Securities and Exchange Board 
of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements), Regulations, 2015  
(“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of 
the Company in its meeting held today i.e. 28th May, 2024 has, inter-alia, 
considered and approved the followings: -   
 
1. Re-appointment of M/s. S.S. Kothari Mehta & Co. LLP (formerly as M/s. S.S. 
Kothari Mehta & Co.), Chartered Accountants, on the recommendation of the 
Audit Committee, as Internal Auditors of the Company for the financial year 
2024-25. 
 
2. Re-appointment of M/s. R.J. Goel & Co., Cost Accountants, on the 
recommendation of the Audit Committee, as Cost Auditors of the Company 
for the financial year 2024-25. 
 
3. Re-appointment of M/s. K.N. Gutgutia & Co., Chartered Accountants, on the 
recommendation of the Audit Committee, as Tax Auditors of the Company for 
the financial year 2024-25. 
 
4. Re-appointment of M/s. Mukesh Agarwal & Co., Company Secretaries, on the 
recommendation of the Audit Committee, as Secretarial Auditor of the 
Company for the financial year 2024-25. 
 
5. Appointment of Shri Uttaran Das  as Plant Head - Dehradun  in the category of 
Senior Management Personnel, on recommendation of the Nomination & 
Remuneration Committee, of the Company w.e.f. 28th May, 2024.
