  c. A brief on types of customers
    Gabriel India has strategically expanded its production capacity and diversified its product range through 
dedicated Strategic Business Units (SBUs), enhancing its responsiveness to customer demands. With a 
customer-centric approach, the Company operates four SBUs: Two- and Three-wheelers, Passenger Cars, 
Commercial Vehicles and Railways, and Aftermarket. Each SBU focuses on bespoke product development to 
meet specific customer needs.
    Under the leadership of a committed Chief Operating Officer (COO), Gabriel India promotes responsible 
manufacturing, collaborative product development, and innovative solutions.
    The Company has forged partnerships with numerous Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) across 
segments such as Two- and Three-wheelers, Passenger Cars, Commercial Vehicles, and Railways, mitigating
