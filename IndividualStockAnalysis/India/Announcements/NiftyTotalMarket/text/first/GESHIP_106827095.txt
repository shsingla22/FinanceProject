PRESS RELEASE   
GE Shipping contracts to buy a secondhand Very Large Gas Carrier  
  
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (GE Shipping) has contracted to buy a secondhand 
Very Large Gas Carrier of about 82,000 cbm  on October 23, 2026. The 2015 built vessel is 
expected to join the Company’s fleet in Q3 FY27.  
 The proposed vessel will be financed entirely from internal accruals. The purpose of the acquisition 
is expansion of the fleet. 
   
The company’s current owned fleet stands at 40 vessels, comprising 25  Tankers (5 Crude Tankers, 
16 Product Tankers, 4 LPG Carriers) and 1 5 Dry Bulk Carriers (2 Capesize, 10 Kamsarmax, 1 
Ultramax, 2 Supramax) aggregating 3.24 mn dwt. The Company’s current capacity utilization is 
close to 100%.  
 
In addition, the Company has contracted to purchase two secondhand Kamsarmax dry bulk carriers. These transactions are expected to be completed in Q3 FY27. Furthermore, the Company 
has entered into a contract for the acquisition of a new -building Suezmax t anker, which is 
scheduled for delivery in the second half of FY 2028- 29.
