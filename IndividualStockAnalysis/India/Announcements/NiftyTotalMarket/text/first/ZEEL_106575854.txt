Dear PG, 
 
After careful consideration, I am writing to tender my resignation from the position of Head – Strategy, 
M&A, and Investor Relations at Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd. after an enriching journey. 
 
Over this period, I have had the privilege of growing alongside the Company witnessing and contributing to 
its evolution through both its highs and its challenges. This journey has been deeply fulfilling, offering me 
invaluable learning, diverse experiences, and opportunities to work on assignments that were both engaging 
and demanding. 
 
I am sincerely grateful for your trust, guidance, and support extended to me throughout my tenure. 
 
I have decided to pursue new opportunities outside the organisation. While this is not an easy decision, I do 
so with a sense of pride, having worked with a company which has been a pioneer with many industry-firsts 
to its credit. 
Thank you once again for your leadership and for making this journey so meaningful. 
Wishing you and the Company all the success in the years to come! 
 
 
Warm regards, 
Vikas Somani
