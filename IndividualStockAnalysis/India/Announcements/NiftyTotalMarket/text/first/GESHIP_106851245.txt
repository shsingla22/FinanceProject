PRESS RELEASE  
GE Shipping takes delivery of 2015 built secondhand Very Large Gas Carrier  
“Jag Vikrant”  
 
On 6th November, The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (GE Shipping) took delivery of 
2015 built secondhand Very Large Gas Carrier “Jag Vikrant” of about 82,000 cbm . The company 
had contracted to buy the vessel in Q3 FY27. This vessel is financed entirely from internal accruals.  
 
After this transaction, the company’s current owned fleet stands at 42 vessels, comprising 26 
Tankers (5 Crude Tankers, 16 Product Tankers, 5 LPG Carriers) and 16 Dry Bulk Carriers (2 
Capesize, 11 Kamsarmax, 1 Ultramax, 2 Supramax) aggregating 3.38 mn dwt. The Company’s 
current capacity utilization is close to 100%.  
Additionally, the Company has contracted to buy one secondhand Kamsarmax dry bulk carrier, 
with the transaction expected to be completed in Q3 FY27. Furthermore, the Company has entered 
into a contract for the acquisition of a newbuilding Suezmax tanker, which is scheduled for 
delivery in the second half of FY 2028–29.
