directed Director General (“DG”) to re -investigate healthcare 
industry  w.r.t.  the pricing of all medicines, packages and 
consumables provided by Respondent  Hospitals in Delhi  
(includ ing 6 Max Network Hospitals) . DG submitted its report 
which was challenged by Respondent Hospitals.  
 
CCI after considering the supplementary investigation report  
filed by DG , the Hospital's objections, oral arguments, and 
written submissions, rejected the DG's findings  in relation to 
abuse of dominant position .  
 
CCI has passed separate orders  dated  May 21, 2026  in favour of 
various Max Network hospitals , holding that no case of abuse of 
dominant position under Section 4 of the Competition Act, 2002 
is made out. Accordingly, the matter is closed  with no adverse 
order . 
 
The orders were received by the Company on May 21, 2026 at 
9.47 pm (IST). The said order s are also available on the website
