to be constituted under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, shall be reconstituted after appointment of 
Independent Directors.  Hence, the aforesaid fine w.r.t. non -compliance of the SEBI (LODR), 
Regulations, 2015 should not be leviable .

wish to inform that such non -complia nce, imposition of fines by the Stock Exchanges and replies
