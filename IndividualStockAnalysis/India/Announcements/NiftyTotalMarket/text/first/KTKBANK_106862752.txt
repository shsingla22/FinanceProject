PRESS RELEASE Date: 16-11-2026 
Karnataka Bank appoints Veteran Banker Mrs. Biji Sreekrishnavilas 
Sankaranarayanan as its New Managing Director & CEO on interim basis. 
Leading south Indian private bank -Karnataka Bank, is pleased to announce the 
appointment of Mrs. Biji Sreekrishnavilas Sankaranarayanan as the new Managing 
Director & CEO of the Bank on interim basis. The appointment is effective from 16th 
November 2026 for a period of two months or up to the date on which the 
fulltime/regular MD&CEO assumes charge, whichever is earlier. Karnataka Bank is 
confident that Mrs. Biji S S's expertise and leadership will drive the bank towards 
continued growth and success. 
Mrs Biji S S who is presently working as Executive Director of Karnataka Bank is a 
Strategic Banking Leader with 31 years of end-to-end experience in South Indian bank 
across corporate banking, retail & branch banking, liabilities, secured assets, 
transformation and digital modernization. 
1 
Mrs. Biji S S has a proven track-record in steering nationwide banking networks as 
independent profit centres, scaling corporate banking verticals, strengthening risk 
frameworks, improving asset quality, enhancing customer acquisition models and 
architecting digital / operational turnaround journeys. 
Mrs. Biji S S said, "I am honoured to take on the responsibility of leading Karnataka 
Bank as its Managing Director & CEO on interim basis. Having been associated with the 
Bank as Executive Director, I have had the opportunity to understand its rich legacy, 
strengths and the immense potential it holds. 
I strongly believe that the success of any institution is built on teamwork, shared vision 
and collective effort. With the continued support and wholehearted involvement of 
every member of the Karnataka Bank family, I am confident that we can build on our 
strengths, embrace emerging opportunities and take the Bank to newer heights of 
growth and excellence. Further, I look forward to working closely with all the 
stakeholders in carrying forward the legacy of over a century of trust and goodwill of 
the Bank, strengthen our business, accelerate digital transformation and enhancing 
customer experience"
