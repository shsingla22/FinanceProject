Vedanta Divests 1.6% Stake in Hindustan Zinc, Strengthens 
Balance Sheet  
 
Mumbai, June 18, 2025 : Vedanta Limited ( "Vedanta" ) announces that it has sold 66.7 million shares in 
Hindustan Zinc Limited, a subsidiary of the Company , to institutional investors by way of an accelerated 
bookbuild process, representing c. 1.6 per cent of the issued ordinary share capital. The gross proceeds from 
the sale of shares amount to c. INR 3,028 Cr.  
 
As we actively progress towards the demerger of Vedanta into sector focused entities across diverse 
verticals, this capital raise will help de -leverage the balance sheet and enhance financial flexibility, enabling 
each of the demerged entities to pursue th eir independent growth plans.  
 
This transaction reflects continued investor confidence in Vedanta’s strategic direction, particularly the 
progress made over recent quarters in delivering record production, driving cost efficiencies, and execution 
of its deleveraging and demerger initiatives aimed at long term value creation for all stakeholders.
