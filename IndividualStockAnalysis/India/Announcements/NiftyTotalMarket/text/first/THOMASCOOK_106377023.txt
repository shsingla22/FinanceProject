Sterling Darshan Guruvayur  Unveiled – Devotion meets Destination Weddings  
• Expands its existing foray with a 2nd Property in Guruvayur   
• Largest Key Count by a National Brand in the Pilgrimage Town   
• Kerala footprint grows stronger with 11th resort in the State.  
• Sterling’s 10th resort in Pilgrimage destination s in line with increasing trend of spiritual travel  
• Premier Venue for Weddings  
Chennai, 1 8th Sep 2025:  Sterling Holiday Resorts today announced the opening of Sterling Darshan 
Guruvayur , its second property in the sacred temple town of Guruvayur. With the addition of this 
new resort, Sterling becomes the national brand with the largest number of keys in Guruvayur , 
offering devotees and travellers a wider choice of stays while retaining Sterling’s signature warmth 
and hospitality.  
Strategically located just steps away from the iconic 5,000 -year -old Sri Krishna Temple, Sterling 
Darshan Guruvayur is designed as a serene retreat for pilgrims, families, and cultural explorers. The 
resort blends devotion with comfort through its 30 thoug htfully designed rooms, authentic Kerala 
dining at Annapoorani Restaurant, and a range of spiritual and cultural experiences curated by the in -
house travel desk.  
Sterling Darshan Guruvayur complements the existing Sterling Guruvayur, giving travellers two 
distinct yet spiritually aligned Sterling experiences in the same destination. Guests can also extend 
their holiday circuits with nearby Sterling resorts at Athir appilly, Munnar, or Alleppey, combining 
spiritual, cultural, and leisure experiences in Kerala.  
Beyond its proximity to the temple, Sterling Darshan Guruvayur also emerges as the largest 
destination in Guruvayur for weddings and social gatherings . Its spacious Kalyan Mandapam, with 
a capacity of 300 guests and a dedicated dining hall , offers an unparalleled venue for traditional 
ceremonies and large -scale events —making it the perfect choice for those planning weddings in the 
temple town.  
“With Sterling Darshan Guruvayur, we are deepening our footprint in one of India’s most revered 
pilgrimage destinations. Together with our first property, Sterling Guruvayur, we are proud to offer 
travellers more choice than ever before. Keeping in line wi th the increasing trend for spiritual 
tourism, we will continue to increase our presence in several heritage destinations across the 
country,” said Vikram Lalvani, MD & CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts . 
“We are delighted to partner with Sterling to launch this resort. Guruvayur has always been a hub for 
spiritual tourism, and with Sterling Darshan, we are bringing a new dimension —one that celebrates 
not just pilgrimage but also weddings and cultural gathe rings. The large Kalyan Mandapam, coupled 
with Sterling’s trusted hospitality, makes this a landmark property in Guruvayur,” said  Mr. Jose 
Matt hew , Owner & Managing Director , EVM Motels Pvt. Ltd.
