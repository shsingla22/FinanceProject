Dear Ma’am / Sir, 
 
Please refer to our intimation dated 30th September , 2023 , on captioned subject, inter -alia, 
informing that JSW  Hydro Energy Limited  (JSWHEL) , a material step -down wholly owned 
subsidiary  has filed a writ petition before the Hon’ble High Court of Himachal Pradesh, challenging 
the notices of the Government of Himachal Pradesh demanding supply of approximately 18% free  
power  with effect from 1 3th September, 2023 in accordance with the Implementation Agreement 
dated 18th November, 1999 and the subsequent Supplementary Implementation Agreements 
entered into with the Government of Himachal Pradesh  (“Implementation Agreements ”) instead 
of the 13%  free power capped as per the CERC Tariff Regulations, 2019 (" CERC Regulations "). 
 
In this regard, this is to inform you that the Hon’ble High Court of Himachal Pradesh vide Judgment 
dated 28th May, 2024 , has allowed the above w rit petition and directed the Government of 
Himachal Pradesh and its instrumentalities to align the provisions of the Implementation 
Agreements  with the provision of the CERC Regulations in respect of quantum of free power to 
be supplied to the State of Himacha l Pradesh  and has further directed that the power, if any, 
supplied to the Home State by JSWHEL over and above the maximum ceiling limit prescribed by 
the CERC Regulations from 13th September, 2023 shall be adjusted accordingly.
