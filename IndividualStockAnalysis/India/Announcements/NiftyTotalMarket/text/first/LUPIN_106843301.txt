U.S. 
U.S. sales for Q 2 FY202 7 were INR 30,202 Mn up 9.3% compared to INR 27,624  Mn in Q2 FY202 6; (USD 317 Mn  
in Q2 FY202 7 compared with USD 314 Mn in Q 2 FY2026); accounting for 39% of Lupin’s global sales.  
  
The Company received 4 ANDA and 1 NDA 505(b)(2) approvals from the U.S. FDA, and launched 1 product in the 
quarter in the U.S. The Company now has 149 generic products in the U.S . Market.  
 
Lupin continues to be the 3rd largest pharmaceutical player in both U.S. generic market and U.S. total market by 
prescriptions  (IQVIA  Quarter September 2026). Lupin is the leader in 61 of its marketed generics in the U.S. and 
amongst the Top 3 in 1 13 of its marketed products (IQVIA Quarter  September  2026 by extended units ). 
 
India  
India sales for Q 2 FY202 7 were INR 23,836 Mn, up 14.7% compared to INR 20,777 Mn in Q 2 FY202 6; accounting 
for 31% of Lupin’s global sales.  India Region Formulation sales up by 13.8% in the quarter as compared to Q 2 
FY202 6.  
 
The Company launched 5 brand s across therapies during the quarter.  Lupin is the 8th largest company in the Indian 
Pharmaceutical Market  (IQVIA MAT September  2026). 
 
Other Developed Market s 
Other Developed market s sales for Q2 FY202 7 were INR 11,823 Mn, up 45.7% compared to INR 8,117  Mn in Q 2 
FY202 6; accounting for 1 5% of Lupin’s global sales.  
 
Emerging  Markets   
Emerging  Markets sales for Q2 FY202 7 were INR 8,908  Mn, down  3.5% compared to INR 9,228  Mn in Q 2 FY202 6; 
accounting for 11% of Lupin’s global sales.  
 
Global API  
Global API sales  for Q2 FY202 7 were INR 3,086  Mn, up 20.2 % compared to INR 2,568 Mn in  Q2 FY202 6; accounting 
for 4% of Lupin’s global sales . 
 
Research and Development  
Investment in R&D was INR 6,181 Mn (7.9% of sales) for the quarter as compared to INR 5,222  Mn (7.6% of sales) 
for Q 2 FY202 6. 
 
Lupin received approval for 4 ANDA and 1 NDA 505(b)(2) from the U.S. FDA in the quarter. Cumulative ANDA filings 
with the U.S. FDA stand at 429 as of September  30, 202 6, with the company having received 353 approvals to date.  
 
The Company now has 48 First -to-File (FTF) filings including 21 exclusive FTF opportunities . Cumulative U.S. DMF 
filings stand at 94 as of September  30, 202 6.

About Lupin  
 
Lupin Limited is a global pharmaceutical leader headquartered in Mumbai, India, with products distributed in over 
100 markets. Lupin specializes in pharmaceutical products, including branded and generic formulations, complex 
generics, biotechnology product s, and active pharmaceutical ingredients. Trusted by healthcare professionals and 
consumers globally, the company enjoys a strong position in India and the U.S. across multiple therapy areas, 
including respiratory, cardiovascular, anti -diabetic, anti -infec tive, gastrointestinal, central nervous system, and 
women’s health. Lupin has 15 state -of-the-art manufacturing sites and 6 research centers globally, along with a 
dedicated workforce of over 26,000 professionals. Lupin is committed to improving patient health outcomes 
through its subsidiaries – Lupin Diagnostics, Lupin Digital Health, and Lupin Manufacturing Solutions.
