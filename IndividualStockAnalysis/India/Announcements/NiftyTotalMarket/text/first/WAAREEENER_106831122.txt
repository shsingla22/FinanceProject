Commenting on the results Mr. Jignesh Rathod, Whole Time Director & CEO, Waaree Energies Ltd said:  
Our H1’ FY27 performance reflects the strength of our business fundamentals and consistent execution of our growth 
strategy. For H1’FY27 a 47% increase in revenue s, coupled with 15% EBITDA growth and  significant improvement in 
Operating Cashflows by almost 5x , demonstrates the resilience of our execution . 
We are particularly encouraged by the strong momentum in our retail business and domestic US manufacturing , which 
recorded revenue growth of 191% and 113%, respectively  in H1’FY27 . These businesses are becoming important 
contributors to our growth trajectory.  As we continue to expand our capabilities and strengthen our presence across the 
clean energy value chain, we expect our Battery Energy Storage System (BESS), Transformers, Inverters, EPC T&D  and 
Green Hydrogen businesses to contribute to future growth.  
As we will enter FY28 , Waaree will be full ready to execute on  capacities namely ~ 29GW of Solar PV, 15.4GW of Solar Cell,  
~5 GWh of BESS  Pack & Container , ~3.5  GWh Battery Cell manufacturing, 4GW of Inverter, 20,000  MVA of Transformers 
and a larger integrated EPC entity (Projects + T&D).  
Waaree sits at the inflexion of becoming vertically and horizontally integrated with high operating leverage  potential 
ahead , driven by increased utilization.  With a clear strategic roadmap, expanding capabilities and a strong focus on 
execution, we are confident in our ability to deliver sustainable, long -term growth.
