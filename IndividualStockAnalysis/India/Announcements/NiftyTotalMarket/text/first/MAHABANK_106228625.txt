Subject: Election of One Shareholder Director  and fixation of Specified date  
 
Bank  of Maharashtra has  raised Rs. 3500 crore equity share capital through QIP on 5th 
October, 2024 resulting in increase in public shareholding. As the Public Shareholding of the 
Bank is 20.40% i.e., more than 16%, the Bank is entitled to have  two Director s from amongst 
Shareholders other than the Central Government.  The Shareholders of th e Bank have 
already elected Shri Praveen Kumar  as a Shareholder Director who assumed office on 
30.06.2024  and Bank is now having vacancy of  one more Shareholder Director.  
 
Earlier, Board of the Bank v ide meeting dated 16.12.2024 had  approved to conduct election 
of Shareholder  Directo r through EGM scheduled on 24.01.2025. However, Bank had not 
received any valid nominations and thus the EGM scheduled  solely for this pur pose was 
cancelled which  was informed to you vide our letter dated 10.01.2025.  
 
With a view to fill in the said vacancy , at the Board Meeting held today i.e., on 12th May, 2025  
the Board approved a proposal to conduct Election of one Shareholder Director  amongst 
shareholders other than the Central Government, pursuant to the Banking Companies 
(Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970 , read with The Banking  Regulation Act 
1949, The Nationalised Banks (Management and Miscellaneous Provisions) Scheme, 1970, 
The Bank of Maharashtra (Shares & Meetings) Regulations, 2004 as amended from time to 
time, Notifications of Reserve Bank of India and Government of India and SEBI (LODR) 
Regulations, 2015 at ensuing Annual General Meeting.  
 
In view of the above, the Bank has fixed Friday, 23rd May, 2025  as the Specified Date for 
the purpose of ascertaining the Shareholders of Bank eligible to participate i.e. to Nominate, 
Contest and Vote in the said E lection and the l ast date for submission of nomination forms 
for contesting Election of Shareholder Director  is 10th June, 2025 .  
 
The date and notice of AGM  will be shared in due course.  
 
This is for dissemination under Regulation 30 of the  SEBI (LODR) Regulations
