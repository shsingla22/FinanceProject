OPERATIONAL  PERFORMANCE  HIGHLIGHTS  OF 2025-26 (PROVISIONAL)
> Refineries  achieved highest-ever  crude throughput  of 75.4 MMT, with operational
reliability  of 99.5%.
> Pipeline  throughput  reached  a record 105.3 MMT.
> Achieved  highest-ever  consolidated  sales volume  of 104.4 MMT of Petroleum  Products,
surpassing  the previous  year’s 100.3 MMT (~4% growth).
> Commissioned  a record 909 retail outlets,  leading  to positive  market  share across  9 of the
top 10 National  Highways.
> Lubes business  achieved  record sales of 855 TMT, reflecting  ~15% growth against
industry  growth of ~4%.
> Petrochemicals  reported  highest-ever  sales of 3.22 MMT and production  of 3.4 MMT.
> Gas business  recorded  RLNG sales of approximately  5.60 MMT (excluding  internal
consumption).
