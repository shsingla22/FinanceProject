Sub: Update on the Joint Venture between Intellect Design Arena Limited UK and

Ref: Intimation vide letter bearing ref . IDAL/2025 -26/SE/11 dated August 21, 2025  
 
This is in furtherance to our earlier communication dated August 21, 2025, regarding the 
Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) signed between our wholly -owned subsidiary, 
Intellect Design Arena Limited, UK , and ITIXA Limited  (a Group Company of System 
Holdings Limited) for the establishment of a Joint Venture entity  in GIFT City, Gujarat.  
 
We wish to inform you that both parties have mutually agreed not to proceed with the 
proposed Joint Venture and to allow the MOU to expire without renewal. The existing 
business relationship between System Holdings Limited (parent company of ITIXA 
Limited)  will continue as is without any material impact whatsoever.   
 
Strategic Rationale  The primary objective of the proposed Joint Venture was to provide 
AI-enabled bookkeeping, payroll, and business support services specifically targeting the 
Small and Medium Enterprise (SME) sector in the United Kingdom.  
 
Since the signing of the MOU, the Company has conducted a rigorous assessment of the 
evolving market dynamics in the UK. Following the UK Government’s Autumn Budget in 
October 2024, the operating environment for UK SMEs has shifted significantly. Key 
regul atory changes —specifically the increase in Employer National Insurance 
contributions to 15% and the reduction of the secondary threshold to £5,000 —have 
introduced new financial pressures on the target customer base.  
 
In light of these developments, it has been determined that the economic climate for UK 
SMEs has become unfavourable  for the immediate launch of the specific services 
envisaged under this JV. As a result, the commercial assumptions underpinning the 
original business case no longer meet the Company’s stringent thresholds for capital 
allocation and risk -adjusted returns.  
 
Impact on the Company  We wish to clarify that this decision is a result of prudent 
strategic reassessment and does not reflect any inability to execute on the part of Intellect 
Design Arena Limited. The cancellation of this specific MOU will have no impact on the 
Company’s exi sting operations, financial standing, or its broader growth strategy in the UK 
and European markets.   
 
 
 Intellect Design remains committed to its core strategy of delivering mission -critical 
technology to the global financial sector and will continue to deploy capital into high -growth 
opportunities that offer superior long -term value for our shareholders.
