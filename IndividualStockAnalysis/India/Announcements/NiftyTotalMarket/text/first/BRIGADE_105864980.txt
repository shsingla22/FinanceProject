PRESS RELEASE  
BRIGADE GROUP RECORDS HIGHEST EVER SALES OF ₹6,013 CRORES & TOTAL REVENUE OF ₹5,064 
CRORES in FY24; PROFIT BEFORE TAX OF ₹569 CRORES  
Bengaluru, May 28, 2024:  Brigade Group reported its highest ever real estate sales of 7.55 Mn sft for 
the financial year ended March 2024. Total revenue stood at ₹5,064 Cr which grew by 42% over FY23. 
EBITDA stood at ₹1,362 Cr which grew by 39% over FY23. Total collections stood at ₹5,915 Cr which 
grew by 9% over FY23. Net cash flow from operations stood at ₹1,575 Cr.  
In FY24, the Real Estate business registered revenues of ₹3,662 Cr which is an increase of 51% over 
FY23. The leasing vertical grew by 25% over FY23 with revenues of ₹938 Cr with overall occupancy 
standing at 97%. Growing by 18% over FY23, the hospitality portfolio clocked revenues of ₹464 Cr, 
with occupancy at 72% and ARR of ₹6,483 during FY24.  
Commenting on the Group’s performance, Pavitra Shankar, Managing Director, Brigade Group, said, 
“All verticals of the company contributed significantly to our growth in FY24 on the back of robust 
demand. With a strong pipeline of 22 Mn sft of ongoing projects and around 16 Mn sft of upcoming 
projects, we are confident of sustained performance in the coming quarters as well. We continue to 
focus on Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad for suitable land parcels that are at par with our quality 
standards and our consumer-first focus.” 
FY24 HIGHLIGHTS (Consolidated)  
Revenue for FY 24 stood at ₹5,064 Cr; an increase of 42% over FY23. 
EBITDA for FY 24 stood at ₹1,362 Cr; an increase of 39% over FY23. 
PBT for FY 24 stood at ₹569 Cr; an increase of 105% over FY23. 
Q4 FY24 HIGHLIGHTS (Consolidated)  
Revenue for Q4 FY24 stood at ₹1,763 Cr; an increase of 102% over Q4 FY23. 
Real estate revenue grew to ₹1,390 Cr in Q4 FY24; an increase of 143% over Q4 FY23. 
Leasing revenue grew to ₹247 Cr in Q4 FY24; an increase of 32% over Q4 FY23. 
Hospitality witnessed a growth of 13% from ₹112 Cr in Q4 FY23 to ₹126 Cr in Q4 FY24. 
EBITDA stood at ₹493 Cr in Q4 FY24 as against ₹231 Cr in Q4 FY23, an increase of 113%. PAT during 
Q4 FY24 stood at ₹211 Cr as compared to ₹63 Cr in Q4 FY23, an increase of 234%. 
Collections for Q4 FY24 stood at ₹1,838 Cr as compared to ₹1,463 Cr in Q4 FY23. 
 
 
 
 
  
 
 
Final Dividend  
The Board of Directors has recommended a final dividend of ₹2/- per equity share (20%) of ₹10/- each 
for the Financial Year 2023-24. 
Induction of Independent Director 
Mr. Abraham Stephanos has been inducted as an Independent Director for a period of five years 
with effect from May 28, 2024, s ubject to shareholders approval. 
Mr. Abraham Stephanos holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Mechanical Engineering with a Post 
Graduate Diploma in Management from IIM, Calcutta (specialisation in Marketing and Strategic 
Management), and has around 4 decades of rich and versatile experience including sales and 
marketing. He was associated with the Tata Group for over 25 years in various capacities. He was 
Managing Director of Tata Steel Downstream Products Ltd for around 10 years. 
About Brigade Group: Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers 
with over three decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and 
winning their customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark buildings and transformed 
skylines across the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, and 
Gift City, Gujarat with developments across Residential, Office, Retail, Hospitality and Education 
Sectors.
