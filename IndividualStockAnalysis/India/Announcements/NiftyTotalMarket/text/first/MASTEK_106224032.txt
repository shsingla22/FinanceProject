Mastek Partners with Zulekha Healthcare Group  to Drive Digital Transformation 
with Oracle Fusion Cloud  
Mumbai, India, 08th May  2025 : Mastek (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted digital engineering and 
cloud transformation partner, today announced its strategic collaboration with Zulekha Healthcare Group. 
Mastek will deploy an extensive Oracle Fusion Cloud suite which closely integrates with Oracle Health 
(Cerner)  system . This transformation aligns with Zulekha’s vision of delivering “Patient First” healthcare 
while enhancing clinical workflows, operational efficiency, and fiscal agility.  
Zulekha Healthcare Group, a UAE -based leading healthcare provider, aims to enhance financial oversight, 
streamline inventory and procurement processes, reduce manual interventions, strengthen compliance, 
and enable data -driven decision -making. It is now on e of the largest private healthcare networks in the 
UAE, employing more than 2,500 personnel, including over 300 qualified doctors, 600+ staff nurses, and paramedical staff. The Group chose Mastek for its global reputation as a leading Oracle -recommended 
partner, backed by deep expertise and trusted capabilities in the healthcare sector. By leveraging AI, cloud 
technology, and interoperability frameworks, Mastek drives healthcare innovation for its clients.
 
As a leading Oracle Cloud partner with 1,500+ implementations across 40+ countries, 100+ industry -
specific solutions, and a talent pool of 2,000+ skilled consultants, Mastek will implement a unified Oracle Fusion Cloud solution fully integrated with Zulekh a Healthcare Group’s Hospital Information System (HIS) 
and Revenue Cycle Management (RCM). Zulekha  has 285 beds across its two hospitals, offering inpatient 
and outpatient care that spans a wide range of medical and surgical specialties. It also operates a  smart 
medical fitness center offering services related to visa requirements, including medical assessments, 
vaccinations, occupational health cards, and embassy fitness certification issuances.  
Managing Director at Zulekha Healthcare Group, Taher Shams, shares,  “Our collaboration with Mastek 
marks a crucial juncture in our digital journey. By leveraging Oracle Fusion Cloud solutions and Mastek’s 
healthcare digital transformation expertise, we aim to drive greater agility, strengthen decision- making, 
and enhance data security to deliver continuous excellence in our patient -first healthcare services.”  
“Leveraging Mastek’s close working relationship with Oracle  and deep healthcare domain expertise,  we 
empower  organizations like Zulekha Healthcare Group to achieve smarter, faster, and more patient -centric 
operations . We are committed to supporting their transformation journey and are excited to drive 
innovation and deliver measurable outcomes together .” said Surya Nunna, Executive Vice President, 
AMEA, Mastek .
