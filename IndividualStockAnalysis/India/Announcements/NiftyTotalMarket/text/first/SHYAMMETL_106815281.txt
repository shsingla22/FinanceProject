Shyam Metalics Group Secures Strategic Mining Rights for Phutamura 
Coal Block to Strengthen Backward Integration  
Kolkata, India, October 13, 2026 - Shyam Metalics Group has announced a major strategic 
advancement in its resource security and operational footprint through an investment in Alom 
Solar Private Limited, who has been declared as the successful bidder for the Phutamura Coal 
Block mining rights.   
Located in the Raigarh District of Chhattisgarh within the prolific Mand Raigarh coalfield, the 
Phutamura Coal Block spans an area of approximately 7.324 sq. km. and holds estimated coal 
reserves of approximately 170 million tonnes. It is expected that mining Operations and Coal 
Production shall commence from July 2027.  
This strategic development serves as a crucial backward integration initiative for Shyam 
Metalics Group, designed to secure a stable, long-term, and cost-effective coal supply to 
support its captive power generation and core steel manufacturing processes. By securing vital 
raw material reserves, the Group reinforces its commitment to operational self-sufficiency, 
sustainable growth, and long-term value creation for its stakeholders.  
Commenting on this significant development, Mr. Brij Bhushan Agarwal - Chairman & 
Managing Director of Shyam Metalics and Energy Limited said:  
"Our Groups investment in Alom Solar Private Limited and the securing of the Phutamura Coal 
Block marks a pivotal milestone in our strategic journey toward absolute self-reliance. As we 
continue to scale our manufacturing capabilities, securing high-quality, long-term raw material 
linkages is paramount. With an estimated 170 million tonnes of coal reserves in the Mand–
Raigarh coalfield, this development significantly strengthens our backward integration 
framework, ensuring uninterrupted fuel security for our captive power plants and core steel 
operations. It underscores our unwavering commitment to sustainable growth, operational 
resilience, and long-term value creation for all our stakeholders."  
About Shyam Metalics and Energy Limited  
Shyam Metalics is a leading and fastest-growing integrated metal-producing company based 
in India primarily in the steel industry in West Bengal, Odisha, Jharkhand, and Madhya Pradesh 
with a focus on Long & Flat Steel Products, Ferro Alloys, Aluminium, and Stainless Steel. The 
company got listed itself on the exchanges in 2021 and possesses a market capitalization of 
more than ₹ 30,000 Cr. Headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal, the company is amongst the 
largest producers of ferro alloys in terms of installed capacity in India.  
The company has an aggregate installed metal capacity of 16.93 MTPA across the steel value 
chain and is supported by a 467 MW aggregated installed capacity of captive power plants. For 
more information, please visit our website at https://www.shyammetalics.com/ .
