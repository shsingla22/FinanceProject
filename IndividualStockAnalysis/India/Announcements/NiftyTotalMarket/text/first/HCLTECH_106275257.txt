HCLTech recognized as the 2025 Global Alliances AI Partner of the Year by Dell 
Technologies 
 
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, June 23, 2025—HCLTech,  a leading global technology 
company, has been recognized as the 2025 Global Alliances AI Partner of the Year at Dell 
Technologies World. This acknowledgement underscores HCLTech’s deep expertise in AI , its full-
stack innovation capabilities and the continued success of its strategic collaboration with Dell .  
 
HCLTech also received the 2025 Global Alliances EMEA Acquisition Partner of the Year award, 
further affirming the company’s expanding impact across markets and sectors.   
 
“The recognitions from Dell Technologies  speak to the power of  partnership in driving AI -led 
transformation ,” said Anand Swamy, EVP, Head of Tech and ISV Ecosystems , HCLTech. 
“Together, we are not only building  scalable GenAI solutions ; we are accelerating  business 
outcomes and reimagining what’s possible for our clients in the AI -first world. ” 
 
HCLTech, in collaboration with Dell and other partners, has launched a suite of AI -driven 
solutions , including Hybrid Cloud -as-a-Service , private GenAI deployments , SAP Transformation , 
Experience- as-a-Service  and Intelligent Operations at the Edge.  
 
Rece ntly, the collaboration enabled a comprehensive AI -led transformation of port operations for 
one of the world’s largest marine terminal operators , enabling improved efficiency, safety and 
decision- making at scale.  
 
To learn more about the HCLTech and Dell  Technologies partnership, visit: 
https://www.hcltech.com/cloud/delltech.
