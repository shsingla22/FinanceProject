DETAILS OF THE JOINT VENTURE PARTNERS 
 
Action Construction Equipment Limited (ACE) was established in 1995  and is 
headquartered in Palwal, Haryana. ACE is World’s largest Pick & Carry cranes 
manufacturer and leading Construction Equipment manufacturer in India. In addition to 
Mobile Cranes, ACE also offers Mobile/Fixed Tower Cranes, Electric Crane, Aerial Work 
Platforms, Crawler Cranes, T ruck Cranes, Lo rry Loader Cranes, Backhoe Loaders, 
Loaders, Motor Graders, Vibratory Rollers, Concrete Placing Boom, Forklifts, Warehousing 
Equipment, Tractors, Combine Harvesters and other Agri Machinery. ACE has also 
introduced Tele-handlers, Aerial Work Platforms – Self Propelled & Truck Mounted & 
Scissor Lifts making it a one stop solution for all Material Handling and Construction 
Equipment requirement, the company has one of the widest Sales and Service network, 
with over 125+  locations supported by 13  regional offices in India and also exports to 
over 37 countries across Middle East, Africa, Asia and Latin America. 
 
The proposed Joint Venture partner is KATO WORKS CO., LTD., Japan, a g lobally 
recognised construction equipment manufacturer with decades of experience in the 
design, development and manufacture of mobile cranes, e xcavators and related 
construction machinery. KATO has an established international footprint and is known 
for its engineering  capabilities , product  reliability  and technological  expertise.

SCOPE AND BUSINESS OF THE JOINT VENTURE 
 
The proposed Joint Venture will bring together the global technology, design, and 
engineering expertise of KATO Works Co. Ltd.   w ith ACE’s strong manufacturing base, 
extensive market reach, and operational capabilities in India. This collaboration is 
expected to enhance product competitiveness by improving performance and cost 
efficiency, while also enabling the partners to address new growth opportunities across 
domestic and international markets. 
 
The Proposed Joint Venture will engage in the manufacturing of Heavy Cranes namely 
Truck Cranes, C rawler Cranes and Rough Terrain Cranes.
