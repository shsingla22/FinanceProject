This is to inform you that, consequent to the merger of Prudent Broking Services Private Limited 
("Transferor Company") with Prudent Corporate Advisory Services Limited ("Transferee 
Company" or "PCASL"), effective from August 30, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), the Transferor 
Company, which was previously engaged in the commodity business but has remained non-
operational for a significant period without generating revenue from such activities, has now 
ceased its operations in this domain. 
 
In the best interest of the Transferee Company, the Board of Directors of PCASL has resolved 
today via circular resolution to apply for the surrender of the memberships held by the 
Transferor Company in various exchanges, including Multi Commodity Exchange of India 
Limited (MCX), Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL), National 
Commodity & Derivatives Exchange Limited (NCDEX), and Metropolitan Stock Exchange of 
India Limited (MSEI). The surrender of these memberships will not have any material impact on 
the financial, operational, or other activities of the company. 
 
This disclosure is being made in compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
