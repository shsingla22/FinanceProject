Press Release  
 
Nuvoco  Vistas  announces  its financial  results  for Q2 FY27 
▪ Achieved  a consolidated  volume  of 4.7 MMT , registering  a revenue  growth  of 13.5%  YoY   
▪ EBITDA  increased  by 11% YoY  to reach  Rs. 411  Cr. 
▪ Profit  After  Tax  increased  by 78% YoY  to Rs. 65 Cr. 
▪ Commissioned  2 MMTPA  of grinding  capacity  at Limla  Cement  Plant,  Surat;  strengthening  its 
footprint  in Western  India  
 
Mumbai,  October  22, 202 6: Nuvoco  Vistas  Corp.  Ltd.,  a leading  building  materials  Company  in India,  announced  
its financial  results  for the year  ended  September  30, 2026.   
 
Nuvoco  complete d ten years  in the cement  business  this  year.  What  began  in 2014  with  a single  manufacturing  
operation  at Nimbol  with  2.3 MMTPA  cement  capacity  has  grown  into  a diversified  building -materials  company  
spanning  Cement,  Ready -Mix Concrete,  and  Modern  Building  Materials.  We have  sustained  our growth  journey  
across  market  cycles  and  are now  well  positioned  to reach  35 MMTPA  of cement  capacity  by FY28 , nearly  15x  
growth  over  the past  decade.  
 
The  Company  achieved  cement  sales  volume  of 4.7 MMT  in Q2 FY2 7, registering  a 9% YoY  growth.  Consolidated  
Revenue  grew  13.5%  YoY  to Rs. 2790  Cr. in Q2 FY27 . The  Company  also  reported  a growth  of 11% YoY  in EBITDA  
to Rs 411  Cr. in Q2 FY2 7.  
 
On the sustainability  and  cost -efficiency  front,  the Company  partnered  with  CleanMax  for a 46.4  MW  wind–solar  
hybrid  renewable  energy  project  in Rajasthan.  Once  operational,  the project  is expected  to generate  approximately  
100  million  units  of renewable  electricity  annually  and  deliver  long -term  cost  certainty  by reducing  dependence  on 
conventional  power  sources , thereby  strengthening  the Company’s  energy  mix and  improving  long -term  cost  
efficiency  across  Rajasthan  operations.  
 
Commenting  on the performance  of the Company,  Mr. Jayakumar  Krishnaswamy,  Managing  Director,  
Nuvoco  Vistas  Corp.  Ltd.,  stated,  “Q2 FY2 7 was  a testament  to resilient  execution  in a challenging  operating  
environment.  Despite  intense  and  erratic  rainfall  in our key markets , global  and  regional  logistics  & supply  chain  
disruptions,  and  elevated  input  costs  stemming  from  ongoing  geopolitical  uncertainty,  the Company  delivered  
strong  YoY  growth  in Revenue,  EBITDA,  and  PAT.  Our  performance  reflects  the strength  of our strategic  levers  of 
premiumisation  and  geographical  mix optimisation , which  helped  realisations  amid  muted  cement  prices .”  
 
Furthermore , he added , “During  the quarter,  the Company  achieved  a significant  growth  milestone  with  the 
successful  commissioning  of the 2 MMTPA  Grinding  Unit  at Limla,  Surat,  underscoring  our strong  project  execution  
capabilities.  This  achievement  reinforces  our confidence  in delivering  the remaining  modules  of the Vadraj  Cement  
Project  at Kutch  and  Sachana  within  the planned  timelines.  Looking  ahead,  while  we remain  mindful  of evolving  
macroeconomic  and  geopolitical  dynamics,  we remain  focused  on operational  excellence , accelerating  
premiumisation,  optimising  supply  chain  efficiencies,  and  executing  our growth  projects  as planned .” 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
About  Nuvoco  Vistas   
 
Nuvoco  Vistas  Corporation  Limited  (“Nuvoco”)  is a building  materials  company  with  a vision  to build  a safer,  smarter,  
and  sustainable  world.  It is among  the leading  players  in East  India,  with  a strong  presence  in North  and  West  India.  
Nuvoco  started  opera tions  in 2014  with  a greenfield  cement  plant  in Nimbol,  Rajasthan,  and  subsequently  acquired  
Lafarge  India  Limited,  which  entered  India  in 1999,  Emami  Cement  Ltd.  in 2020,  and  Vadraj  Cement  Limited  in April  
2025.  Alongside  these  initiatives,  the Company's  capacity  expansion  programme  is progressing  as planned  and  will 
increase  Nuvoco's  total  cement  capacity  to 35 MMTPA  by FY2028.  The  Company  reported  total  income  of ₹ 11,362  
crore  in FY 2025 -26, underscoring  its strong  and  sustainable  growth  trajectory.  
 
Nuvoco  offers  a diversified  business  portfolio  across  three  segments:  Cement,  Ready -Mix Concrete  (RMX),  and  Modern  
Building  Materials  (MBM).  Nuvoco’s  Cement  portfolio  includes  Concreto,  Duraguard,  Double  Bull,  PSC,  Nirmax  and  
Infracem,  offering  a complete  spectrum  of Ordinary  Portland  Cement  (OPC),  Portland  Slag  Cement  (PSC),  Portland  
Pozzolana  Cement  (PPC)  and  Portland  Composite  Cement  (PCC).  Nuvoco’s  RMX  business  has  a pan -India  presence  
and  offers  value -added  products  under  the Concreto  (Performance  conc rete),  Artiste  (Decorative  concrete),  InstaMix  
(ready -to-use  bagged  concrete  - the first -of-its-kind  in the industry),  X-Con  (M20  to M60)  and  Ecodure  (Special  green  
concrete)  brands.  It is also  a proud  contributor  to landmark  projects  such  as the Mumbai -Ahmedabad  Bullet  Train;  Birsa  
Munda  Hockey  Stadium  (Rourkela);  Aquatic  Gallery  Science  City  (Ahmedabad);  and  Metro  Railway  (Delhi,  Jaipur,  Noida  
and  Mumbai),  among  many  others.  Nuvoco’s  MBM  portfolio,  under  the ‘Zero  M’ brands,  comprises  construction  
chemica ls, tile adhesives,  wall  putty  and  cover  blocks.  Through  the NABL -accredited  Construction  Development  and  
Innovation  Centre  (CDIC)  based  in Mumbai,  Nuvoco  identifies  market  gaps  and  offers  innovative  products  to meet  
customer  requirements.
