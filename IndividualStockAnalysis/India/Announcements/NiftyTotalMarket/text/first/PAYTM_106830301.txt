ONE 97 COMMUNICATIONS LIMITED 
CODE OF PRACTICES AND PROCEDURES FOR FAIR DISCLOSURE OF UNPUBLISHED 
PRICE SENSITIVE INFORMATION  
 
Purpose of this Code  
 
Regulation 8(1) of the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 ("PIT Regulations") 
requires every listed company to formulate and publish, on its official website, a Code of Practices and Procedures for Fair Disclosure of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information ("Code"), adhering to 
the principles set out in Schedule A to the PIT Regulations.

I. Objective  
 
This Code sets out the practices and procedures of One 97 Communications Limited (the 
"Company") for fair disclosure of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information ("UPSI").  
 
II. Definitions  
 
"Insider" shall have the meaning assigned to it under the PIT Regulations.  
 "UPSI" shall have the meaning assigned to it under the PIT Regulations.  
 
Any other term not defined in this Code shall have the meaning assigned to it under the PIT 
Regulations, Companies Act, 2013, the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, applicable accounting standards, or other applicable law.  
 
III. Principles of Fair Disclosure 
 
In accordance with Regulation 8(1) read with Schedule A to the PIT Regulations, the Company 
shall:  
 
1. Cause prompt public disclosure of UPSI that would impact price discovery no sooner than 
credible and concrete information comes into being in order to make such information generally 
available.  
2. Uniformly and universally disseminate UPSI to avoid selective disclosure.  
3. Promptly disseminate UPSI that gets disclosed selectively, inadvertently or otherwise to make 
such information generally available.  
4. Appropriately and fairly respond to queries on news reports and requests for verification of 
market rumours by regulatory authorities.  
5. Ensure that information shared with analysts and research personnel is not UPSI.  
6. Develop best practices to make transcripts or records of proceedings of meetings with analysts and other investor relations conferences on the official website to ensure official confirmation and 
documentation of disclosures made.  
7. Handle all UPSI on a need- to-know basis.  
 
IV. Chief Investor Relations Officer  
 
The Chief Financial Officer is designated as the Chief Investor Relations Officer ("CIRO"), 
responsible for overseeing the dissemination of information and disclosure of UPSI. The CIRO 
may consult relevant officials within the Company to verify the correct ness and credibility of UPSI 
proposed to be disclosed.  
 
V. Legitimate Purpose  
 
An Insider may share UPSI for a legitimate purpose, in accordance with the Company's Policy for 
Determination of Legitimate Purposes as formulated under Regulation 3(2A) of the PIT 
Regulations and set out in Annexure A to this Code.  
 
VI. Leak or Suspected Leak of UPSI  
 
On becoming aware of any leak or suspected leak of UPSI, the Company shall follow the inquiry 
policy formulated under Regulation 9A(5) of the PIT Regulations, and the CIRO shall ensure 
compliance with such policy.  
 
VII. Disclosure of this Code

In accordance with the PIT Regulations, this Code will be hosted on the Company's website and

VIII. Conflict  
 
In the event of any conflict between this Code and applicable law, the applicable law in force from 
time to time shall prevail.

Annexure A  
 
Policy for Determination of Legitimate Purposes 
 
I. Introduction  
 
This Policy for Determination of Legitimate Purposes ("Policy") is formulated under Regulation 
3(2A) of the PIT Regulations to identify ‘legitimate purposes’ for which UPSI relating to the 
Company may be shared by an Insider, as an exception to the general  prohibition on 
communication of UPSI under the PIT Regulations, provided such sharing is not carried out 
to evade or circumvent the PIT Regulations.  
 
II. Legitimate Purpose –  Explanation  
 
(a) Whether a particular instance of sharing constitutes a ‘legitimate purpose’ depends on the 
specific facts and circumstances of that instance. “Legitimate Purposes” means sharing of 
UPSI in the ordinary course of business by an Insider with the following, provided that such 
sharing has not been carried out to evade or circumvent the prohibitions of PIT 
Regulations: (i) auditors, accountancy firms, legal advisors, merchant bankers, 
consultants; (ii) Company’s contractual counterparties and business / commerci al 
partners; (iii) lenders (if any); (iv) customers and suppliers; and (v) any other advisors/consultants/partners. Any such person with whom UPSI is shared pursuant to a 
“legitimate purpose” shall be considered as an “insider” for purposes of PIT Regulati ons 
and shall be duly notified to maintain confidentiality of such UPSI in compliance with PIT 
Regulations.  
 
(b) Any person with whom UPSI is shared for a legitimate purpose shall be considered an ‘Insider’ for the purposes of the PIT Regulations, and due notice shall be given to such 
person of the obligation to maintain confidentiality of the UPSI, use the UPSI solely for the 
purpose for which it was disclosed and not trade in the securities of the Company while in 
possession of the UPSI.  
 
(c) Where UPSI is shared for a transaction of the nature described in Regulation 3(3) of the PIT Regulations, the Compliance Officer shall ensure the execution of a confidentiality 
agreement with the recipient, as required under Regulation 3(4) of the PIT Regulations.  
 
III. Structured Digital Database 
 
(a) In accordance with Regulations 3(5) and 3(6) of the PIT Regulations, the Company shall 
maintain and not outsource, a structured digital database recording such details and for 
such specified time period as set out therein.  
 
(b) Information relating to UPSI not originating internally shall be entered into the database 
within 2 (two) calendar days of its receipt.
