time reaching  Rs. 225 crore in Q4 FY24

ABOUT AMI ORGANICS LIMITED:  
Ami Organics, headquartered  at Surat, is a  R&D driven manufacturer of specialty chemicals 
with varied end usage,  focussed on the development and manufacturing of advanced 
pharmaceutical intermediates (“Pharma  Intermediates”) for regulated  markets and generic 
active pharmaceutical ingredients (“API s”) and New Chemical Entities  (“NCE”) and key 
starting material for agrochemical and fine chemicals . AMI has developed and 
commercialised over 520 Pharma Intermediates for APIs across 17 key therapeutic areas  since 
inception and NCE, with a strong focus on  R&D across select high -growth high margin chronic 
therapeutic areas.
