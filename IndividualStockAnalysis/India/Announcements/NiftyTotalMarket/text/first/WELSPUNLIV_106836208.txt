Welspun Living achieves net -cash position, delivers double -digit growth and 
further expands EBITDA margins  
 
Mumbai,  October  30, 2026: Welspun Living Ltd. (WL L), a global leader in Home Textiles and part of the 
US$ 9 billion Welspun Group, announced Q 2FY27 results today.   
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Key Highlights  
 
Q2FY27 Financial Highlights:  
▪ Revenue s grew 16.9% year -on-year and 1 .5% sequentially  to ₹2,8 70 crore  
▪ EBITDA  stood  at ₹370 crore with  EBITDA M argin  improv ing to 12. 9%, up 600 basis points 
year-on-year and 40 basis points sequentially  
▪ PAT grew 1 3x YoY to ₹1 69 crore  
▪ Balance sheet continued to strengthen  
▪ Achieved a net -cash position , compared with net debt of ₹775 crore in March 2026.  
▪ Cash conversion cycle  improved significantly to 4 4 days, from 82 days in March 2026.  
▪ Capital Efficiency: ROCE improved to 12 .7%, reflecting continued progress in capital 
utilization  and returns.  
 
Q2FY27 Business Highlights:  
▪ Home textile exports  grew 18.3% YoY, supported by improving demand  
▪ US pillow  business grew 2. 4x YoY in Q 2 with both manufacturing facilities  operational and 
remains on track to double revenues in FY27  
▪ Domestic business delivered strong growth 28.2% year -on-year,  with our brand “ Welspun ” 
growing 37%, reflecting sustained consumer traction and continued momentum in India  
▪ Flooring revenue grew 19.3% year -on-year, while EBITDA margin improved to 1 0.8%, 
▪ Operational Restoration: Restoration of the Vapi operations is progressing, with full 
restoration expected during the course of the year  
▪ Green power transition:  Anjar facility now operates on 100% green power  marking a 
significant step forward in our sustainability journey .

Management  Comment ary:   
Speaking about the performance, M s. Dipali Goenka, Managing Director & CEO,  Welspun Group  said: 
“Q2 FY27 reflects our continued progress in driving profitable growth and strengthening our financial 
position. We delivered 16. 9% revenue growth, expanded EBITDA margins to 12.9% and achieved a net -cash 
position, supported by a significant improvement in cash conversion. These milestones reflect our focus on 
operating discipline, capital efficiency and the quality of our growth.  
The global home textiles market continues to evolve, with increasing emphasis on sourcing diversification, 
supply -chain resilience, product innovation and speed to market. India’s growing competitiveness in global 
sourcing and expanding trade access presen t meaningful opportunities. Our manufacturing scale, customer 
partnerships and product capabilities position us well to capitalise on these shifts. Growth across Home 
Textiles exports, our US onshore pillow business and the domestic market, alongside impro ving profitability 
in Flooring, demonstrates the breadth of our business.  
Looking ahead, we remain focused on sustaining double -digit growth, maintaining margins in the low teens, 
improving returns on capital and generating strong cash flows. While the external environment remains 
dynamic and restoration of our Vapi operations r emains a near -term priority, we will continue to invest 
selectively in capabilities, innovation, brands and sustainability to strengthen our competitive position and 
create long -term value for our stakeholders.”

About Welspun Living Ltd: 
 
Welsp un Living Ltd (WLL), part of $ 5 Bn Welspun Group, is a global leader in Home textiles. With a distribution network in more than 60 countries 
and world class manufacturing facilities in India, Welspun is strategic partne rs with top global retailers. WL L is driven by its differentiation strategy 
based on Branding, Innovation and Sustainability.  
 
About Welspun Group:  
A US$ 9 billion enterprise, Welspun Group is one of India’s fastest growing conglomerates with businesses in Line Pipes, Home Textile s, 
Infrastructure, Warehousing, Oil & Gas, Advanced Textiles and Floorings. The Group has a strong footh old in over 60 countries with 40,000 
employees and over 100,000 shareholders. Headquartered in Mumbai, Welspun Group’s manufacturing facilities are strategically located in India,  
USA and Saudi Arabia. Known for technological and operational excellence, the Group has established a leadership position in the Line Pipe & Home 
Textiles sectors globally. Its clients include most of the Fortune 100 companies.
