VIDA - Powered by Hero MotoCorp, Unveils a New Era of Human-Centric, Intelligent, and 
Sustainable Mobility

lineage —three bold expressions of the future of mobility. The VIDA Novus portfolio marks a 
transformative leap in redefining sustainable, intelligent, and connected mobility, driven by the 
brand’s philosophy— Life is Movement . 
 NEX 1 : A new mobility archetype—portable, personal, and designed to move with you, not 
merely carry you. 
 NEX 2:  A compact, self-balancing electric trike that combines the thrill of a motorcycle with 
three-wheel stability for urban practicality. 
 NEX 3:  A sleek, all-weather personal EV that offers the safety and comfort of four wheels in 
an elegant and versatile form, for both urban and rural journeys.

VIDA Accelerates Global Expansion and Unveils Next-Generation E-Scooters and E-
Motorcycles at EICMA 2025 
Building on its India success, VIDA today announced the European launch of the VIDA VX2  urban 
scooter at EICMA 2025. Designed to blend the practicality of traditional scooter with cutting-edge EV 
technology, the VIDA VX2 offers an impressive 125 km WMTC range, a top speed of 90 km/h, twin 
removable IP67-rated batteries, and an IP68-rated PMSM drivetrain. VX2 is a testament to VIDA’s 
commitment to reliable, globally accessible electric mobility solutions. 
In addition, VIDA makes a historic stride in its global electrification journey with the unveiling of two 
groundbreaking electric motorcycle concepts: 
 VIDA Concept Ubex: VIDA’s first global electric motorcycle, the Ubex, is a Neo-Naked Urban 
Explorer combining futuristic design with multi-terrain capability. Built on a modular 
platform with advanced smart connectivity, the Ubex offers class-leading ride comfort and 
versatility, setting a new standard for electric motorcycles.  
 VIDA Project VxZ : Developed in collaboration with Zero Motorcycles USA, Project VxZ  
marks a major milestone in global electrification. This cutting-edge e-motorcycle platform 
strengthens the partnership between Hero MotoCorp, the world’s largest manufacturer of 
motorcycles and scooters, and Zero Motorcycles, a leader in performance EV motorcycles. 
Together, this alliance accelerates the transformation of the global two-wheeler landscape.  
VIDA DIRT.E Series: Hero MotoCorp Unveils the Future of Electric Off-Roading— “Ride Beyond 
the Wild” 
Hero MotoCorp, through its electric brand VIDA, today unveiled the VIDA DIRT.E Series , a brand-
new line of off-road electric motorcycles designed for those who “Ride Beyond the Wild.”  With the 
launch of these innovative models, VIDA continues to push the boundaries of electric mobility, 
blending sustainability, performance, and thrill  for riders of all ages. 
 DIRT.E K3: The first-of-its-kind electric motorcycle for children which features three adjustable 
sizes, designed to grow with riders from ages 4 to 10 years. Born from dust, grit, and glory, the 
DIRT.E K3 redefines how young riders learn to ride— safely, skillfully, and sustainably.  
Honored with the prestigious Red Dot Design Concept Award 2025 , the DIRT.E K3 will make 
its debut in Europe and India, with official pre-interest registrations now open. 
 DIRT.E MX7 Racing Concept: The DIRT.E MX7 Racing Concept is a high-performance, agile 
electric off-road motorcycle built for riders who refuse to settle for the ordinary. Designed with 
a fierce off-road attitude , the MX7 combines cutting-edge electric technology with Hero’s 
legendary Dakar Rally engineering expertise. Lighter, nimbler, and built for endurance , the 
DIRT.E MX7 offers an unparalleled riding experience for off-road enthusiasts.

Hero MotoCorp Unveils Performance Machines: Hunk 440SX & Rally-Ready Xpulse 210 
DAKAR EDITION  
Hero MotoCorp unveiled two internal combustion engine (ICE) models—the Hunk 440 SX and the 
Xpulse 210 Dakar Edition —engineered with direct insights from the demanding challenges faced 
by the Hero MotoSports Team Rally. These consumer centric  machines reflect Hero’s commitment 
to performance, precision, and global expansion,  offering a seamless blend of cutting-edge 
technology, capability, and reliability. 
Both models are designed for global markets and fully compliant with Euro5+ emission standards, 
reinforcing Hero MotoCorp’s dedication to delivering sustainable mobility solutions across both 
electric and ICE segments. 
 Xpulse 210 DAKAR EDITION: Rally Champion DNA :  In celebration of its 2024 FIM World Title 
and recent Dakar Podium finish, Hero MotoCorp unveiled the Xpulse 210 Dakar Edition—a true 
rally-ready motorcycle designed for the ultimate adventure enthusiast. 
o Unmatched Performance:  Powered by a liquid-cooled 210cc DOHC engine, the Xpulse 
210 Dakar Edition delivers exhilarating performance and precision control, ensuring 
riders experience every adventure to its fullest. 
o Game-Changing Suspension:  Equipped with fully adjustable, rally-grade suspensions, 
the Xpulse 210 Dakar Edition offers an impressive 280mm of travel and a towering 
270mm ground clearance, making it built for extreme off-road challenges. 
o Market Compliance:  The Xpulse 210 Dakar Edition is also Euro5+ compliant, meeting 
stringent European emissions standards while delivering top-tier performance for the 
most demanding off-road enthusiasts. 
 
 Hunk 440 SX: The Modern Scrambler: The Hunk 440 SX is built for riders who demand more 
from their motorcycles. As a modern scrambler, this rugged yet refined model is built for those 
who live to explore both city streets and untamed trails. 
o Engine & Technology:  Powered by a 440cc oil-cooled, long-stroke engine, the Hunk 440 
SX is equipped with Ride-by-Wire technology, offering multiple ride modes and 
switchable ABS with traction control for enhanced safety and control in all conditions. 
o Chassis & Capability:  Its rugged design is supported by a high-strength perimeter frame, 
tall 18-inch front wheel, long-travel suspension, and high ground clearance, ensuring it 
can tackle both urban streets and untamed trails with confidence. 
o Global Readiness:  Fully Euro5+ compliant, the Hunk 440 SX is prepared for evolving 
European emission standards, affirming Hero’s ongoing commitment to cleaner, more 
sustainable ICE technology.

About Hero MotoCorp 
Hero MotoCorp, headquartered in New Delhi, India, is the world’s largest manufacturer of 
motorcycles and scooters for 24 consecutive years with ~$5 billion revenue. The Company’s global 
footprint spans 52 countries across Asia, Africa, Europe, Central and Latin America with a customer 
base of over 125 million. The Company operates eight manufacturing facilities - six in India and one 
each in Colombia and Bangladesh - along with two Research and Development centres: Centre for 
Innovation and Technology (CIT) in India and Tech Centre Germany (TCG). 
Hero MotoCorp is driving the transition to electric mobility through VIDA, powered by Hero. Strategic 
alliances with Harley-Davidson and Zero Motorcycles support the design and development of 
premium and electric motorcycles, respectively. Further demonstrating its commitment to the EV 
future, the Company has made strategic investments in two- and three-wheeler EV manufacturers, 
Ather Energy and Euler Motors. Hero MotoCorp is the only Indian two-wheeler manufacturer listed 
on the prestigious Dow Jones Sustainability Index (DJSI). 
Beyond mobility, Hero MotoCorp is a major global promoter of sports, including golf, football, field 
hockey, and cricket. Hero MotoSports Team Rally is India's flag bearer in global rally racing.
