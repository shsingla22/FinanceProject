Tanla Announces Q2 FY27 Results  
 
HYDERABAD, India - October  27, 202 6 - Tanla Platforms Limited, an AI -native platform company, today announced its financial 
results for  Q2 FY27.  
 
Key Metrics: Second  Quarter ( July – September 2026) 
• Revenue was at ₹1,352 Cr; grew by  10.2% QoQ & 25.3% YoY   
• Gross profit was at ₹353 Cr; grew by  8.2% QoQ & 23.1% YoY  
• EBITDA was at ₹214 Cr; grew by  6.6% QoQ & 20.8% YoY   
• Profit after tax was ₹146 Cr, grew by  3.0% QoQ & 17.1% YoY    
• Earnings per share at ₹11.09 
• Free cash flow of ₹232 Cr, 158% of PAT  
Uday Reddy, Founder Chairman & CEO, said, "We delivered another strong quarter with double -digit growth across all key 
financial metrics. Our Enterprise Business grew 27.2% YoY, reflecting the strength of our customer relationships and competit ive 
position. With over ₹1,400 Cr  in cash and a debt -free balance sheet, we are well positioned to pursue our growth ambitions while 
maintaining financial discipline ." 
Significant events during the quarter:  
• ValueFirst Overseas Acquisition:  Tanla completed the acquisition of ValueFirst's overseas business, strengthening its 
international presence.  
• Panel Discussion at London Business School:  Tanla and London Business School hosted a panel discussion on Trust in an 
AI-driven Digital Economy , bringing together leaders from industry, regulation and academia.  
• Fast Company:  Tanla ranked #8 on Fast Company's Best Workplaces for Innovators 2026 list, recognizing  its culture of 
innovation.  
• BIG Sustainability Awards 2026:  Tanla received the Sustainability Leadership Award, reflecting  its commitment to 
responsible and sustainable business practices.  
• Global Fintech Fest 2026:  Karix showcased its AI -powered customer engagement solutions, highlighting the role of AI and 
trust in transforming enterprise communications.

About Tanla  
Founded in 1999, Tanla Platforms Limited is an AI -native platform company transforming digital interactions by empowering users 
and enabling enterprises through its innovative -led SaaS solutions. With a unique enterprise  and user -centric approach, Tanla drives 
advancements in data security, privacy, and protection against spam and scams. Tanla has deep partnerships with leading telco s 
to build trusted and scalable communication ecosystems. Headquartered in Hyderabad, India , Tanla is the preferred partner for 
over 3,200 enterprises across industries, including global tech leaders like Google, Meta, and Truecaller. Recognized as a 'Visionary ' 
in the 202 6 Gartner® Magic Quadrant ™ and ranked among the “1000 High -Growth Companies in Asia Pacific” by the Financial Times, 
Tanla is publicly traded on the NSE and BSE (NSE: TANLA; BSE: 532790).
