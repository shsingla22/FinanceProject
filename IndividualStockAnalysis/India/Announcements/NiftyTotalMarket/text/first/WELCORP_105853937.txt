Subject:- Appointment of Mr. G R Arun Kumar as Group CFO, Welspun World. 
 
We are pleased to announce the appointment of Mr. G R Arun Kumar as Group CFO, Welspun 
World with effect from May 20, 2024, reporting to the Group Managing Director, Mr. Rajesh 
Mandawewala. 
 
Mr G R Arun Kumar previously served as the Group President & Chief Financial Officer, Ola 
group for three years. Under his strategic leadership, he played a crucial role in establishing Ola 
Electric as India’s leading EV company within 30 months including operating roles, raising critical 
project debt & closing equity funding rounds. Prior to that he served at the metal & mining and 
oil & gas conglomerate of Vedanta for eight years as Group CFO where he managed effectively 
the balance sheet including capital market activity, investor relations, strategic tax admist 
commodity cycles. Pre Vedanta he worked in the global MNCs of Hindustan Unilever & General 
Electric (GE) in various finance & non finance roles contributing across the value chain of 
businesses including business turnaround. At GE he also held international roles leading global 
teams.  Across companies he has driven excellence in the finance function around controls, 
automation, process excellence, cost reduction, working capital & compliance. He also served on 
the board of Vedanta Limited alongside industry stalwarts. He brings around 30 years of broad 
corporate experience. 
 
He holds Commerce Graduate from Loyola College and an FCA from the Institute of Chartered 
Accountants of India, ranking 17th nationally.
