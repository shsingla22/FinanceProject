As is our tradition, we begin by emphasizing our safety performance, an 
essential pillar of our operational excellence and corporate culture.  
By the end of nine-months period September 2025, RAIN achieved a 
Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR) of 0.09, marking a notable improvement over the same period last year. This achievement reflects 
the deep -rooted commitment of our workforce to maintaining a safe and 
secure working environment across all our operations. It is a testament to 
the vigilance, discipline, and care demonstrated daily by our teams, and it 
continues to set benchmarks within our industry.  
 
We operate in highly complex industrial environments, where the 
potential for safety incidents  is a constant concern. That is why safety is 
not just a priority;  it is a core value embedded into every aspect of our 
operations. From leadership to frontline teams, we reinforce safety 
protocols consistently and make it an integral part of every employee’s 
routine.  
Page 5 of 29  
  
During the third quarter, we recorded two incidents  at our European 
facilities. While any incident is one too many, we approach each with transparency and a commitment to learning and improvement. 
Importantly, our overall safety performance has shown steady progress 
over the past several years.  
This positive trajectory is the result of our proactive safety culture  which 
includes  a drive for employees to report any unsafe acts or conditions 
before they can cause an incident, rigorous training programs, and the 
collective vigilance of our global teams. We are proud of the strides we have made and remain fully committed to raising the bar even further, ensuring that safety continues to be a defining strength of RAIN.
