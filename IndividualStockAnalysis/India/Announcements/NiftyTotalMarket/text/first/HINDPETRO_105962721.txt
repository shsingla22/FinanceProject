We write to inform that the Company has received Notices from BSE Limited (BSE) and National 
Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) for non-compliance of provisions of Regulation 17 (1) of

having required number of Independent Directors on the Board for the quarter ended June, 2024 
and consequent levy of fine as per the details indicated below: 
Period of Non-compliance 01-04-2024 to 30-06-2024 
Amount of Fine (including GST @ 18%) Rs. 5,36,9001- each from BSE & NSE

the Company being a Government Company , the power to appoint Directors including Independent 
Directors on its Board vests with Government of India. The Company is following up with the 
Government from time to time for appointing required number of Directors on its Board and GOI is 
seized of the matter.

considered favourably by the Exchanges .
