PRESS RELEASE 
 
 
Brigade Group Acquires 2.25-Acre Land Parcel in Secunderabad for Approx. ₹1000 Crore Premium 
Residential Development 
 
The outright acquisition further strengthens Brigade’s residential footprint  
in Hyderabad. 
 
Bengaluru, November 23, 2026:  Brigade Group has announced the acquisition of a prime 2.25-acre land parcel 
in Secunderabad through an outright purchase at 180 Cr, with the sale deed registration now completed. The 
site is proposed to be developed into a premium residential project with an estimated revenue potential of 
approximately ₹1000 crores.  
 
The acquisition marks another step in Brigade Group’s continued expansion in Hyderabad and strengthens its 
residential portfolio in the city. Located between MG Road and Necklace Road, Secunderabad, the proposed 
development will benefit from the area’s central location, established social and commercial infrastructure, and 
strong connectivity to key parts of Hyderabad. The development will cater to the growing demand for high-
quality, thoughtfully designed homes, combining contemporary architecture, modern amenities and Brigade’s 
established development standards. 
 
Commenting on the acquisition, Amar Mysore, Executive Director, Brigade Enterprises Limited, said, 
“Hyderabad continues to be a key growth market for Brigade, and this acquisition further strengthens our 
presence in the city. Secunderabad is an established location with strong connectivity and access to social and 
commercial infrastructure, making it well suited for a premium residential development. As we scale our 
presence in Hyderabad, we remain focused on acquiring strategically located land parcels that offer strong 
development potential and align with Brigade’s principles of quality, governance and long-term value 
creation.”   
 
Brigade Group marked its debut in Hyderabad with Brigade at No.7 in Banjara Hills and subsequently expanded 
its presence with Brigade Citadel in Moti Nagar. The Group continues to strengthen its footprint through Brigade 
Gateway at Neopolis, a 4.5 Mn sq. ft. premium mixed-use development, Brigade Enclave and Brigade Manor 
in Moti Nagar, and more recently Brigade Barcelona at Neopolis. The acquisition in Secunderabad further builds 
on this growing portfolio and reinforces Hyderabad as a key market in Brigade’s expansion strategy.  
 
About Brigade Enterprises Limited: Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property 
developers with four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and winning 
their customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark buildings and transformed skylines across India 
in the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, and GIFT City with 
developments across Residential, Office, Retail, Hospitality and Education Sectors.
