KPI Green Energy Limited Successfully Energises 965 MWp Capacity out of its 2.17 
GWp Independent Power Producer (IPP) Portfolio   
 
KPI Green Energy Limited (“KPI” or “the Company”) has recently energised an additional 376 
MWp capacity  (comprising ~182 MWp solar capacity in GUVNL 250/350 MWp project and ~164 
MWp Solar capacity and ~30 MWp Wind capacity in GUVNL 370 MW/677 MWp project). With this, 
the Company’s total energised capacity under its Independent Power Producer ( “IPP” ) 
portfolio  has reached 965 MWp,  marking a significant milestone in the phased development of 
its utility -scale renewable energy assets.  
 
With a total IPP portfolio of 2.17 GWp, the balance capacity is under execution and is expected 
to be commissioned in a phased manner, in line with scheduled timelines. This continued 
execution across KPI Green’s IPP portfolio strengthens its position as a  leading Independent 
Power Producer (IPP), while reflecting its strong project development and delivery capabilities 
and enhancing long -term revenue visibility through a growing base of contracted renewable 
energy assets under long -term Power Purchase Agre ements (PPAs).  
 
Key IPP Project Developments:  
 
200 MW/240 MWp Khavda Solar IPP Project  
 
KPI successfully commissioned and started injecting power from its 200 MW (240 MWp) solar IPP 
project at Khavda Solar Park (South Block) in January 2026, making it among the earliest projects 
operational within the 1.8 GW solar park developed by GSECL. The  project is connected under a 
long -term Power Purchase Agreement with Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited (GUVNL)  
 
50 MW/92 MWp Hybrid Renewable Energy IPP  
 
KPI Green achieved commissioning and power injection ahead of the scheduled July 2026 
commercial operation date for its 92.15 MWp hybrid project, comprising 16.95 MW wind capacity 
and 75.2 MWp solar capacity. The early synchronization demonstrates the Comp any’s strong 
execution capability in complex hybrid renewable projects.  
 
620 MW/102 7 MWp Solar and Hybrid Renewable Energy IPP  
 
In October 2024, KPI executed two long -term PPAs with GUVNL of renewable capacity 
comprising:  
 
• 250 MW/350 MWp grid -connected solar IPP project  
 
KPI has energised 181MW / 251 MWp capacity on a phased basis under its 250 MW/350 MWp 
solar IPP project. The remaining capacity is currently under active execution with full completion 
targeted within the October 2026 timeline.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
• 370 MW/67 7 MWp grid -connected hybrid IPP project  
 
KPI has successfully energised 110  MW AC / 164 MWp solar capacity and 29.70 MW wind 
capacity , aggregating to a total energised capacity of 140 MW AC / 194 MWp  under the hybrid 
project. The balance capacity is currently under active execution with full completion targeted 
by October 2026.  
 
150 MW Wind IPP Project  
 
KPI has signed PPA for 150 MW with GUVNL in November, 2025. The project has successfully 
achieved financial closure marking a significant milestone in its development. Execution 
activities are now underway and progressing as per schedule, with commissionin g expected by 
October 2027, and efforts focused on achieving completion ahead of schedule.  
 
445 MW / 890 MWh Standalone Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)  
Sun Drops Energia Limited (Formerly Known as Sun Drops Energia Private Limited), a KPI Green 
subsidiary focused on energy storage solutions, executed a Battery Energy Storage Purchase 
Agreement (BESPA) with GUVNL for 445 MW / 890 MWh of standalo  ne battery energy storage 
systems.  
 
This marks KPI Green’s entry into utility -scale energy storage, strengthening the Company’s 
capability to integrate renewable generation with advanced grid -stability and energy 
management solutions.
