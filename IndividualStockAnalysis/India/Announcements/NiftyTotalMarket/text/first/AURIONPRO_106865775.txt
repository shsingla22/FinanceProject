Aurionpro secures additional order of nearly ₹ 100 crore from global hyperscaler, 
expands AI -ready data centre engagement in Southern India  
Expanded engagement takes total value with the customer past ₹400 crore, 
reflecting confidence in Aurionpro’s data centre capabilities and bolstering its 
order book.  
Mumbai, November 1 9, 2026  – Aurionpro, a leading end -to-end data centre 
solutions  and enterprise technology  provider, is pleased to announce an additional 
order worth close to ₹100 crore from a global hy perscaler for the development of 
an AI -ready data centre in Southern India. This order represents an expansion of 
the existing engagem ent with the hyperscaler, announced earlier this year, further 
strengthening the Company’s relationship with the customer .  
With this latest order, the aggregate value of engagements with the hyperscaler 
has exceeded ₹400 crore, reflecting the growing scale of the relationship. The 
project is scheduled to be executed over a period of nine months.  
Bhaskar Bhattacharya, EVP & He ad – Enterprise Business, Aurionpro,  said:  
“We thank our customer for their continued trust and confidence in 
Aurionpro. As demand for AI -ready infrastructure accelerates, we are 
expanding our presence across the data centre value chain.  
Alongside our delivery teams, we are investing in advanced 
manufacturing as part of a backward integration strategy . This will 
enable us to gain greater control over the supply chain, improve 
operational efficiency, and scale faster to meet the 
requiremen ts of hyperscale clients. With growing traction 
and recent order wins, we are well positioned to further 
strengthen our presence in this market and capitalise on 
the strong demand. ”
