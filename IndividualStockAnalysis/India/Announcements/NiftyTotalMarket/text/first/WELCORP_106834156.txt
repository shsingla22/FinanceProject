3.Setting up of Additional Spiral Facility in USA
On the back of a robust order book, strong demand and long-term visibility in the USA for
Oil & Gas pipeline projects, we are pleased to inform that the Board has approved a capex
of USD115 million towards debottlenecking and capacity augmentation and accordingly
have authorised for setting up of an additional Spiral (HSAW) Pipe facility at our existing
location at Little Rock, Arkansas, USA.
Existing HSAW Pipes capacity in US: 350 KMTPA
Proposed HSAW Pipes capacity augmentation: 225 KMTPA
The proposed facility is expected to be operational in March, 2028
Investment required: upto USD 115 million
Mode of Financing: Through internal accruals
4.Perma-Pipe and Welspun Corp Announce Partnership in Jordan to Establish Pipe
Manufacturing and Coating Facilities
Further to our previous disclosure dated 1 September, 2026 whereby we had informed,
that the Company together with Perma- Pipe International Holdings, Inc. (“Perma -Pipe”),
had signed, a Memorandum of Understanding (“MOU”) with the developers of Jordan’s
National Water Carrier Project and the Government of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan
to advance the development of pipe manufacturing and advanced coating facilities in
Jordan.
We are now pleased to inform you that the Board has approved total project investment of
upto ~ USD 125 million in Jordan, to be funded in ~ 50:50 proportion by each of the two
partners, towards establishing spiral pipe manufacturing and coating facilities in Jordan.
The proposed facilities will manufacture and coat pipes in range of 24” to 120”. The
detailed terms of the partnership, including the governance and operational arrangements,
will be set out in the definitive agreements, which we will be executed in due course.
The proposed facilities will be capable of serving major water, oil and gas, energy, and
infrastructure projects in the Kingdom and across surrounding regional markets.
The partnership is expected to bring together Welspun’s large-diameter steel pipe
manufacturing capabilities and Perma- Pipe’s expertise in anti -corrosion coating systems
designed to protect pipelines from corrosion and extend service life, engineered piping
solutions and project execution expertise.
The partnership is intended to create a strategically located regional manufacturing hub
that can support large-scale infrastructure programs, strengthen regional supply chains,
increase local value creation and develop skilled technical capabilities in Jordan. Over
time, it could also support infrastructure development and reconstruction requirements in
Syria, Lebanon, Iraq and Gaza, as well as potential future opportunities associated with
the development and rerouting of regional oil and gas infrastructure toward the Port of
Aqaba.
 
 
 
5.  Setting-up IFBE Plant in India:- Acquisition of 49% direct stake in Welspun Special 
Coating Private Limited  (“WSCPL”) , a step down wholly owned subsidiary  of the 
Company  
 
As previously informed, WSCPL was recently incorporated with an initial paid-up share capital 
of Rs. 1,00,000/- and proposes to enter in the business of undertaking, executing and providing 
IFBE coating works and related activities in India. 
 
The acquisition of 49% equity stake in WSCPL from Welspun DI Pipes Limited (“WDI”), a 
wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company, for an aggregate consideration of Rs. 49,000/-. 
 
Simultaneously, WDI will enter into a Share Purchase Agreement with Perma-Pipe Qatar, 
pursuant to which Perma-Pipe Qatar will acquire the remaining 51% equity stake in WSCPL 
from WDI for an aggregate consideration of Rs. 51,000/-. The Company will also enter into 
Shareholders’ Agreement (JV Agreement) with Perma -Pipe Qatar.  
 
The Board of Directors of WDI has also approved the sale and transfer of its entire equity 
shareholding in WSCPL to the Company and Perma-Pipe Qatar in the aforesaid proportion and 
has approved the execution of the aforesaid Share Purchase Agreement. 
 
Internal FBE (Fusion Bonded Epoxy) coating is an epoxy-based powder applied electrostatically 
to the interior of steel pipes to protect against corrosion and chemical damage. The major 
applications are in Onshore and offshore Oil & Gas Pipelines; Slurry Pipeline; Chemical 
processing plants ; Food processing application s; Underground or underwater water pipelines.  
 
This JV’s capability to do IFBE coating will position the Company favorably to win both export 
and domestic Orders for IFBE Coated Pipes. 
 
Upon completion of the aforesaid transactions, WSCPL will cease to be a wholly-owned 
subsidiary of WDI and will become a direct associate company of WCL, with the Company 
holding 49% of its equity share capital and Perma-Pipe Qatar holding the remaining 51%. 
 
The total investment in WSCPL is estimated at upto ~ Rs. 1 10 Crores which will be shared with 
JV Partner in the proportion of the above shareholding.  
 
About Perma-Pipe International Holdings, Inc. 
  
Perma-Pipe International Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: PPIH) is a global leader in engineered piping

detection systems, anti-corrosion coatings and related engineered products and services to 
customers across the energy, district energy, infrastructure, industrial, Oil & Gas, water 
transmission, and other critical infrastructure markets. 
  
Perma-Pipe operates manufacturing and service facilities across North America, Middle East, 
North Africa, India and other strategic markets, enabling the Company to serve customers
