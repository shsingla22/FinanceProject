Coforge announces appointment of Manish Hemrajani as Head 
of Investor Relations  
 
Greater Noida /Princeton , July 28, 2025  – Coforge Limited  (NSE: COFORGE) , a global 
digital services and solutions provider, today announced the appointment of Manish Hemrajani  
as Head of Investor Relations.  Manish , a seasoned capital markets professional will lead 
Coforge’s investor relations strategy and deepen engagement with global institutional 
investors, analysts, and other key stakeholders.  Manish will work out of New York and 
Princeton offices of Coforge.  
 
Being a capital markets professional with over two decades of experience, Manish brings a 
strong track record across investor relations, equity research, and financial communications. 
Over the course of his career, he has worked with globally recognized br ands across diverse 
sectors including travel, technology, and financial services such as Yatra Online, Inc., 
Oppenheimer & Co., Deloitte Consulting, Fujitsu Consulting, and CIBC World Markets. This 
cross-sector experience equips him with a nuanced understa nding of investor expectations 
and capital market dynamics.  
 
In his new role, Manish will lead Coforge’s global investor relations strategy, driving clear and 
consistent communication with shareholders and analysts. He will oversee earnings 
disclosures, investor presentations, and regulatory compliance to strengthen  shareholder 
engagement, strategic planning, and reinforcing governance standards to build long -term 
investor trust.  
 
On Manish’s appointment, Sudhir Singh, CEO of Coforge , commented, “ We are pleased to 
welcome Manish Hemrajani to Coforge. Our company has been delivering consistent, broad -
based growth over the past eight years. As we continue to build on our success, strengthening 
our engagement with the investor community is a strategic  priority. Manish’s deep expertise 
in capital markets and investor communications will be instrumental in enhancing 
transparency, shaping market perception, and reinforcing our long -term va lue creation 
narrative.”  
 
Manish holds an MBA in Finance from NYU Stern School of Business . He also holds the Investor 
Relations Charter (IRC) certification.
