 Rozana champions everyday meal consistency in new 
campaign with Celebrity Chef Ranveer Brar  
 
The campaign brings alive the promise of consistent, perfectly cooked Basmati rice 
every day with ‘Har Din Baney Perfect ’ 
 
Delhi (India), 26th October  2026 : LT Foods Ltd., a global FMCG company in the consumer food 
space, today launched a new campaign for 
 Rozana, spotlighting what matters most in 
everyday cooking , i.e., consistency. Featuring celebrated chef, author and television personality, Chef 
Ranveer Brar , the film taps into a universal truth of Indian households: cooking rice perfectly every 
single day is easier said than done . 
 
Built around the thought  “Har Din Ba ney Perfect ” and highlights 
 Rozana ’s proposition of 
delivering consistent basmati rice for everyday meals.   
 
Set at a familiar family dining table, the film , developed by BBDO India , opens with a mother 
questioning her son Rohan about his inconsistency in his examination  scores. Quick -witted as ever, 
Rohan points out that the rice at home is no different, fluffy one day and sticky the next  day. This sets 
up the film ’s central insight before Ranveer Brar introduces 
  Rozana as an upgra de from 
loose basmati rice.  Ranveer Brar brings the proposition alive by highlighting that 
  Rozana 
is carefully selected and delivers consistent results from Monday to Sunday. The film culminates with 
the message,  “
  Rozana. Har Din Baney Perfect! ” before returning to the family, where 
the cooked rice earns “full marks ” from the child.
