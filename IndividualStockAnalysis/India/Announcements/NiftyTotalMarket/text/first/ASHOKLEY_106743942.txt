Sub:  Intimation under Regulation 30 SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 – Change in Auditors  
 
We wish to inform you that Price Waterhouse & Co. Chartered Accountants LLP, the existing statutory 
auditors of the Company, will complete their tenure of 10 years as Statutory Auditors of the Company 
at the conclusion of the 78th Annual General Meeting to be held in the Year 2027. 
 
In this regard, based on the recommendation of the Audit Committee, the Board at its meeting held 
today, has approved the appointment of M/s Deloitte Haskins & Sells Chartered Accountant LLP, 
Chartered Accountants, (Firm Registration No. 117364W/W100739) as Statutory Auditors of the 
Company for a period of 5 (five) consequent years from the conclusion of 78th Annual General Meeting 
to be held in the Year 2027 till the conclusion of 83rd Annual General Meeting to be held in the Year 
2032, subject to the approval of the shareholders of the Company to be sought during the 78th AGM 
to be held in the Year 2027.
