Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015-Imposition of fine by BSE and NSE for the quarter 
ended 30th June, 2026 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para-A of Part-A of Schedule-Ill of the SEBI (LODR) 
Regulations, 2015, it is to inform that National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and 
BSE Limited (BSE) vide their e-mail dated 25th August, 2026 has levied fine of Rs. 5,36,900 
(incl. GST) each for quarter ended 30th June, 2026_ on the Company for non-compliance 
pertaining to the composition of the Board, under Regulation 17(1) of SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. 
The Company has clarified to NSE & BSE that MSTC Limited is a Mini Ratna Category-I PSU 
under the administrative control of the Ministry of Steel, Government of India. The President 
of India vests with the power to appoint Directors (including Independent Directors) on the 
Board of MSTC Limited. All Directors in the company are appointed by the Government of 
India (GOI) through its Administrative Ministry i.e. Ministry of Steel (MOS) and MSTC Limited 
has no role to play in the appointment of any Director unless nominated by the Government 
of India. 
Further, it is also clarified that the aforesaid non-compliances were neither due to any 
negligence/default by the Company nor within the control of MSTC's management and 
continuous efforts were made to meet the compliance requirements. We have continuously 
followed up with the Ministry of Steel for appointment of independent directors. 
We would like to inform you that the BLA cell of administrative ministry vide their orders 
bearing no. 1 /1 /2026-BLA dated 14th July, 2026 has nominated Shri Siraiong Singpho 
(DIN: 11835817) and Shri Niwas Manda I (DIN: 11837616) as Non-Official Independent 
Directors on the Board of MSTC Limited for a period of three years. The Board of 
Directors, thereafter appointed Shri Siraiong Singpho and Shri Niwas Manda! as 
Independent Directors on the Board of the Company w.e.f 18th July, 2026. 
After the appointment of aforesaid two Independent Directors, the company w.e.f 18th 
July, 2026 is fully complied with the provisions of Regulation 17 of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 pertaining to Board 
Composition. The Stock Exchanges are requested to waive-off the above mentioned fine 
levied/applicable on the Company till date.
