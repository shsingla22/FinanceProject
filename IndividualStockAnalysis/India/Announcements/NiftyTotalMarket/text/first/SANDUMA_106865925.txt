1 Sandur plans ₹280 crore beneficiation capex, targets 
up to 1.7 MTPA of upgraded ore output
First 3.5 MTPA unit at Deogiri is targeted for commissioning in August 2027; beneficiation process is 
expected to improve Fe (iron) content from about 50% to over 60%.  
Bengaluru, 19th November:  The Sandur Manganese & Iron Ores Limited  (SMIORE/the Company)  
(NSE: SANDUMA; BSE: 504918) plans to invest approximately ₹280 crore in its first in -mine iron ore 
beneficiation plant at Deogiri Village in Sandur Taluk, Ballari District, Karnataka. The first 3.5 MTPA 
unit is expected to produce 1.0 -1.2 MTPA of upgraded iron ore initially, with potential output of up 
to 1.7 MTPA.  
The plant will process ore from SMIORE’s KTIO mines and some ore from its Deogiri mines. It is 
designed to upgrade lower -grade iron ore, with iron content of about 50% Fe, into concentrate with 
more than 60% Fe, suitable for pellet manufacturing.  
The project is being implemented in two phases, with a long -term plan for two 3.5 MTPA units. The 
first unit is currently about 25% complete, with civil works progressing, structural fabrication under 
way and orders for long -lead items placed. Commissioning is targeted for August 2027.  
The project is expected to be funded through a combination of debt and internal accruals.  
Bahirji A. Ghorpade, Managing Director, said “This will improve the quality and marketability of 
lower -grade ore, reduce waste and support better revenue realisation. The pellet plants in and 
around Sandur have an aggregate capacity of more than 24 MTPA. The beneficiated material could 
also support iron -making within the Group’s integrated steel value chain.”  
The mine -head location is expected to reduce the transportation of waste -bearing ore and support 
tailings management. The project is expected to generate 150 –200 direct and indirect jobs.  
SMIORE reported consolidated total income of ₹1,519 crore for Q2 FY27, up 22% year on year. 
EBITDA increased 37% to ₹391 crore, while profit after tax rose 88% to ₹261 crore.  
For H1 FY27, consolidated total income increased 21% year on year to ₹2,909 crore. EBITDA grew 
25% to ₹749 crore and profit after tax increased 60% to ₹489 crore.
