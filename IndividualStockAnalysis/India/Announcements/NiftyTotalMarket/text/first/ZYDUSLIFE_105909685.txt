Zydus receives tentative  approval from USFDA  for Diroximel 
Fumarate Delayed -Release Capsules, 231 mg  
Ahmedabad, India, 10 July, 2024  
Zydus Lifesciences Limited  (including its subsidiaries/ affiliates , hereafter referred to as 
“Zydus”) has received tentative  approval from the United States Food and Drug Administration 
(USFDA) to market Diroximel Fumarate Delayed -Release Capsules, 231 mg (USRLD: 
Vumerity® Delayed -Release Capsules tablets ).  
Diroximel Fum arate Delayed -Release Capsules is indicated  for the treatment of relapsing 
forms of multiple sclerosis (MS) in adults . The drug will be manufactured at the group’s 
formulation manufacturing facility in Ahmedabad SEZ , India.  
Diroximel Fumarate Delayed -Release Capsules  had annual sales of USD  847.4 mn in the 
United States (IQVIA MAT  May 2024).  
 
The group now has 39 8 approvals and has so far filed over 4 60* ANDAs since the 
commencement of the filing process in FY 2003 -04. 
(*as of 31st March 2024)
