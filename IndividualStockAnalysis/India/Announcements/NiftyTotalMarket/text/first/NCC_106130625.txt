Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
Sub:   Outcome of the National Company Law Tribunal convened Meeting of the Secured 
Creditors of NCC Limited (the Company) held on 30th January 2025  
  
Ref:   Our letter dated 28th December 2024 – Notice of the Meeting of the Secured Creditors.  
 
This is in continuation to our letter cited above on the subject, we would like to inform that as per 
the directions of the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Hyderabad Bench (NCLT) the 
meeting of the Secured Creditors of the Company, was held today (Thursday, 30th January 2025) 
at 12 Noon, at the Registered Office of the Company for considering and approving the proposed 
Scheme of Arrangement between NCC Infrastructure Holdings Limited (Transferor Company) 
and NCC Limited (Transferee Company), and their respective Shareholders . 
 
Mr. Md. Hidayathullah Shareef, who was appointed as the Chairman of the meeting of the secured 
creditors by the Hon'ble NCLT, occupied the Chair and conducted the proceedings of the Meeting. 
Fifteen (15) Secured creditors were present at the meeting and requested the Chairman to 
adjourn the Meeting on the reasoning that they need more time to evaluate the proposed scheme 
and get the approval from their appropriate authority. In view of the requests received, and 
voice votes taken at the meeting, from the majority of the Secured Creditors in favour of 
adjournment of the Meeting, the Chairman adjourned the aforesaid Meeting to Wednesday, 
16th April 2025 at 11:30 a.m . 
 
We request you to kindly take the aforesaid intimation on record.
