PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Foundation and BCIC inaugurate ‘Brigade - BCIC Skill Development Academy’  
 
Bengaluru, August 7, 2024 : Brigade Foundation , the philanthropic arm of  Brigade Group,  today 
announced the inauguration of the first phase of  ‘Brigade -BCIC Skill Development Academy (BBSDA) -
a Centre of Excellence’,  with state -of-the-art infrastructure on a 5 acres land parcel in 
Aerospace  Park,  Bangalore North. Launched in association with  Bangalore Chamber of Industry and 
Commerce (BCIC)  as knowledge partners, this academy is in line with Brigade Foundation’s founding 
principles. This ‘Not -for-Profit’ initiative aims to make a positive contribution to the lives of unemployed 
and underemploy ed people, apart from meeting the needs of the industry. BBSDA will focus on the 
construction, hospitality & tourism, manufacturing, and retail sectors and skill potential candidates to 
make them employable to their full potential.  BBSDA is affiliated to t he Construction Skills Development 
Council of India and Tourism and Hospitality Sector Council of India.   
 
Speaking at the Inauguration,  M. R. Jaishankar, Executive Chairman , Brigade Enterprises Limited  said, 
“Brigade Foundation has a strong focus on education, health and community development which aligns 
seamlessly with Brigade Group’s commitment to skill development and job creation. By setting up BBSDA, 
Brigade Foundation is playing a pivotal role in providing the necessary support services to boos t skill 
development in the state. The scope for skill development is tremendous. Today there is a shortage of 
over 11 lakh skilled employees in the fields of construction, hospitality & tourism, manufacturing, and 
retail industry sectors.  The academy will work closely with BCIC to provide curriculum and identify 
candidates who can be skilled for employability. Also, many of those who are employed are generally 
underemployed due to lack of skill sets to progress in their careers. Through this initiative, we will 
actively promote the Government of India’s skill India program, significantly bridge the existing skilling 
gap in the state and enhance employability for a large number of people.”  
 
Vineet Verma, President, BCIC  said, "BCIC will be the knowledge partner for BBSDA and we are 
confident that with our 880 plus member companies, we would be able to make healthy contributions to 
the growth of industries in Karnataka. BBSDA will be a skilling platform for both freshers and working 
professionals. The idea is to create a centre of excellence where training imparted translates to better 
skills and productivity for the industry. This is a unique opportunity for Industry and academia to work 
toget her to create a more skilled workforce and create better employment opportunities.”  
 
About Brigade Foundation:  Brigade Foundation established in 2003, a not -for-profit trust of Brigade 
Group, with the objective to support  Community Development, Education & Healthcare. Brigade 
Foundation has successfully established 3 well known CBSE / ICSE schools in Bengaluru under the name 
'The Brigade School', at JP Nagar, Malleswaram and Whitefield. Brigade Foundation in association wit h 
the Bangalore Chamber of Industry & Commerce (BCIC) is setting up the Brigade -BCIC Skill Development 
Academy – a Centre of Excellence at Aerospace Park, Bangalore North. Brigade Foundation has also set 
up The Indian Music Experience Museum (IME), India’s  first interactive music museum and is in the 
process of setting up Set Up 2 Not -for-Profit Hospitals of 100 beds each at Brigade Meadows, 
Kanakapura Road and Brigade EL -Dorado, AeroPark in Collaboration with St John’s Medical College 
Hospital.
