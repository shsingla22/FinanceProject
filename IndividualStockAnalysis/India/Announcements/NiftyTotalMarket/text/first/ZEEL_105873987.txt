Dear PG, 
  
It is with a mixture of deep gratitude and profound emotion that I tender my resignation as 
President HR & Transformation. I have been contemplating this and as we wind down the year end, I 
think this is the right time for me to move on.  
  
Reflecting on the past six transformative years, I am deeply moved by the journey we have shared. 
Leading the internal transformation of ZEE has been one of the more rewarding and challenging 
chapters of my career. The opportunity to reimagine and drive our vision forward through the ZEE 
4.0 transformation plan has been both an honour and a privilege.  
  
I joined ZEE to help realize the vision that you had for the Company – of building a truly global M&E 
player based in India and I saw the immense potential and commitment within our teams. However, 
it was through our collective efforts, the alignment of our shared goals, and an unwavering 
dedication to innovation that we were able to achieve many of our milestones. The work we've done 
transcends professional achievements; it's also been a deeply personal journey of growth and 
learning.  
  
The camaraderie and collaboration I've experienced within this organization's executive team have 
been instrumental not only in our professional victories but also in my personal development. 
Together, we navigated the complexities of transforming ZEE, celebrated our successes, and turned 
challenges into opportunities for growth.  
  
The past several years have been a testament to our collective resilience and your visionary 
approach. As I now prepare to embark on a different professional path, the lessons learned and 
experiences garnered here will remain invaluable to me. I carry forward the memories of our 
achievements and the profound impact this role has had on my life, both professional and personal.  
  
Parting ways with ZEE is a decision filled with bittersweet emotions. While I look forward to new 
opportunities, leaving an organization that has shaped so much of my professional identity is 
challenging. I am dedicated to ensuring a smooth transition and will do everything in my power to 
support and guide my successor. 
  
I want to express my heartfelt thanks for the unwavering support, guidance, and the incredible 
opportunities you have provided me. I will always carry your warmth and your genuine concern for 
my wellbeing and that for the extended family of ZEE, wherever I go. Leading the transformation of 
ZEE alongside such a passionate team has been an experience that will always be a significant part of 
my career and one that I will always look back on with happy memories.  
  
Thank you once again, and I look forward to watching the continued success and growth of ZEE. 
  
Yours sincerely, 
Animesh
