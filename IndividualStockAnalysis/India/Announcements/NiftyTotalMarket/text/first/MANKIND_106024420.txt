Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
Subject: Allotment of Non-Convertible Debentures  
 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 and in continuation to our letter dated September 30, 2024, the Fund Raising Committee of 
the Directors of the Company vide circular resolution passed today i.e. October 16, 2024, has 
allotted the following Non-Convertible Debentures; 
 
1. 125,000 INR denominated, listed, rated, secured, redeemable, transferable non-convertible 
debentures having a face value of INR 100,000 each, aggregating up to INR 12,500,000,000 
(NCD Series 1); 
 
2. 125,000 INR denominated, listed, rated, secured, redeemable, transferable non-convertible 
debentures having a face value of INR 100,000 each, aggregating up to INR 12,500,000,000 
(NCD Series 2); and 
 
3. 250,000 INR denominated, listed, rated, secured, redeemable, transferable non-convertible 
debentures having a face value of INR 100,000 each, aggregating up to INR 25,000,000,000 
(NCD Series 3). 
 
The detailed disclosures in respect of NCDs is enclosed as Annexure I. 
 
You are requested to kindly take the above information on your records.
