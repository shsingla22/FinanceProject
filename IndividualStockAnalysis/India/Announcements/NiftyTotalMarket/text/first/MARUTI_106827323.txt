Maruti Suzuki VICTORIS  AURUM  Edition Launched to Celebrate 1 Year of Record Sales Success  
•VICTORIS has won the hearts of customers in its first year by setting the record for the highest cars sold
during the 1st Year for a new model launch#1.
•As a tech -loaded SUV developed for digital natives, the VICTORIS has become the fastest -selling
passenger vehicle  in India  to surpass  both 1.5 lakh#2 and 1 lakh sales milestones#3 since launch.
•To celebrate the milestones , Maruti Suzuki launches the VICTORIS AURUM Edition  Strong Hybrid
featur ing unique styling elements that set it apart from the crowd this festive season.
•The new VICTORIS AURUM Edition is styled with  copper coloured  inserts for the front, side, rear skid
plate designs  and wheel arches. A striking new range of chrome accessories garnish the  front grille,
fenders, bumpers, body side mouldings  and door visors, along with new  all-weather 3D cabin mats, a
3D boot mat  and a rear trunk sill guard .
•For the launch, Maruti Suzuki has  teamed up with Bollywood icon Shahid Kapoor as the face of the
VICTORIS AURUM Edition .
New Delhi, October 24, 2026 : Maruti  Suzuki India Limited (Maruti Suzuki) celebrates the first anniversary 
of the VICTORIS  ‘Got it All’ SUV with the launch of the new AURUM  Edition. Designed and developed for 
young, ambitious , tech -savvy customers, the VICTORIS  has won the hearts of customers in its first year  by 
setting a record for the highest cars sold during the 1st Year for a new model launch in the Passenger 
Vehicle  (PV) category#1. As a tech -loaded SUV developed for digital natives, the new celebratory VICTORIS  
AURUM  Edition with Strong Hybrid technology  and EV mode  has been launched with a kit price of 
INR 22 990.  
Commenting on the launch of the Maruti Suzuki VICTORIS  AURUM  Edition,  Mr. Partho Banerjee, Senior 
Executive Officer, Marketing and Sales, Maruti Suzuki India Limited , said, “Conceptualised as a ‘Got it All’ 
SUV for today’s dynamic young go -getters  who ‘want it all ’, the VICTORIS  has truly won the hearts of 
buyers. As we celebrate its first anniversary, it’s worth noting that the VICTORIS  has become the fastest -
selling PV to  clock  both the 1.5 Lakh and 1 Lakh sales milestones, going on to achieve the highest sales for 
any car in India during its 1st year of launch.  Recognising these  extraordinary achievements, we are 
introducing the VICTORIS  AURUM  Edition  Strong Hybrid , with tasteful enhancements that are sure to grab 
eyeballs  and make this festive season feel all the more special for our valuable customers.  Our association 
with Shahid Kapoor , for the new AURUM Edition is  also sure to  complement the VICTORIS’ contemporary 
appeal. ”
