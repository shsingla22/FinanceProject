PRESS RELEASE  
Brigade Group Reports Revenue Growth of 26%, PAT Growth of 48% in Q2 FY26  
over Q2 FY25   
Achieved pre -sales of ₹2,034 Crores in Q2 FY26  
Bengaluru, 29th October 2025:  Brigade Group reported a total revenue of ₹1,430 Crores in Q2 FY26, a 
growth of 26% over Q2 FY25. The company reported a PAT of ₹170 Crores in Q2 FY26, registering a 
growth of 48% over Q2 FY25. Net bookings in the real estate segment for Q2 FY26 stood at 1.90 Mn Sqft 
with sales value of ₹2,034 Crores.  Collections for Q2 FY26 stood at ₹2,003 Crores.   
 
“We are entering the second half of the fiscal year with strong tailwinds. We have a robust business 
development and launch pipeline across our key markets, as well as healthy leasing activity and growth 
in the hospitality business. Operational performance  has also grown substantially since the prior year, 
and business sentiment remains positive.” said Pavitra Shankar, Managing Director, Brigade 
Enterprises Ltd.   
 
REAL ESTATE  
  
Real estate revenue stood at ₹951 Crores, growing 31% from ₹727 Crores in Q2 FY25. Real estate 
collections stood at ₹1,528 Crores, as against ₹1,470 Crores in Q2 FY25. EBITDA stood at ₹110 Crores.  In 
Q2 FY26 pre-sales volume stood at 1.90 Mn Sqft valued at ₹2,034 Crores.   
 
LEASING  
  
During the quarter, revenue from leasing stood at ₹341 Crores, growing 17% compared to Q2 FY25. 
EBITDA stood at ₹223 Crores. Portfolio occupancy stood at 92 % with an overall leasing of 8.67 Mn Sqft  
out of 9.38 Mn Sqft.   
 
HOSPITALITY   
 
Hospitality revenue in Q2 FY26 stood at ₹138 Crores, a growth of 16% over Q2 FY25. EBITDA stood at 
₹42 Crores.    
  
Q2 FY26 CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS   
 
Overall revenue in Q2 FY26 stood at ₹1,430 Crores as against ₹1,138 Crores in Q2 FY25, an increase of 
26%. EBITDA stood at ₹375 Crores in Q2 FY26 as against ₹358 Crores in Q2 FY25. PAT stood at ₹170 
Crores as against ₹115 Crores in Q2 FY25, an increase of 48%.
