LG Enters a New Category with Its Convertible Chest Freezer Range   
New Delhi , 17th September  2026:  LG Electronics India Limited (LGEIL)  today announced its entry 
into the chest freezer category in India  with the launch of its Convertible  Chest Freezer range . Marking 
a significant expansion of LG’s refrigeration portfolio, the new range brings LG’s expertise in cooling 
technology to the evolving cold -storage requirements of businesses across retail, HoReCa, dairy, ice cream, 
pharma and food storage  along with home consumption . [Watch the product video ] 
Built on LG's convertible cooling technology, the new range allows users to switch the chest freezer 
between chilling and deep -freezing  modes across  a wide temperature range of −31°C to +10°C, giving 
business owners the flexibility to adapt storage to changing needs without investing in multiple appliances. 
The range spans four gross capacities - 102L, 205L, 315L and 530L  - with select 3 15L and 5 30L models 
certified under the latest BEE 5 -star (2026) rating, to help commercial users lower running costs without 
compromising on performance.

said:  “Commercial refrigeration is an important and evolving opportunity in India, with businesses across

energy efficiency, durability and ease of operation to deliver solutions that are designed around the

One Freezer, Multiple Possibilities  
A key highlight of the new portfolio is its convertible cooling and freezing capability , enabling businesses 
to adapt the appliance according to their storage requirements. Select PRO models offer a wide temperature 
range of -31°C to +10°C , allowing users to switch between cooling and deep -freezing requirements through 
one-touch convertible operation . 
The PRO range also features Ultra Freeze  for rapid cooling when required. Digital temperature control 
and a digital temperature display on select PRO models provide convenient monitoring and greater control 
for different storage needs.  
Designed for Energy -Efficient Operation  
Energy efficiency is a key focus of the new LG Convertible Chest Freezer portfolio. The 315L and 530L 
PRO models carry 5 -star BEE ratings , helping businesses manage energy consumption while 
maintaining reliable cooling performance. The portfolio also includes 4  star and  3-star rated models  
The chest freezers also feature enhanced insulation comprising a 70mm PUF -insulated cabinet, 75mm 
insulated door and 100mm insulated mid -part. This insulation design helps deliver better temperature 
retention for improved cooling efficiency . 
Built for Demanding Commercial Environments  
Designed for sustained commercial use, the LG Convertible Chest Freezer range incorporates features 
focused on durability, accessibility and ease of operation. The UV-protected body  is designed for enhanced 
durability, while flushed locks and pocket handles provide convenient everyday access.  
The chest freezers also include removable gaskets  for easier maintenance and castor wheels with brakes  
to facilitate easy movement and secure positioning. LED lights and premium baskets further support 
visibility, organisation and convenient access to stored products.  
A Portfolio Designed Around Business Needs  
The LG Convertible Chest Freezer portfolio comprises six models across 102L, 205L, 315L and 530L 
Gross capacities , available across PRO and PLUS series variants.  
 
 
The PRO series  offers advanced functionality including one-touch convertible operation, Ultra Freeze, 
digital temperature control, digital temperature display, and LED lights. The PLUS series  provides flexible 
cooling solutions with knob control and a temperature range of -25°C to +5°C . 
Together, the portfolio is designed to address diverse commercial applications, from frozen food and ice 
cream storage to requirements across retail, dairy, HoReCa, pharma and food storage.
