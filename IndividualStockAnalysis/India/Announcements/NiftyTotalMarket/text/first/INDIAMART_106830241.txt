IndiaMART InterMESH Limited  
Second  Quarter Ending  September 30, 202 6 - Results Press Release  
 
Noida , India,  October 27, 2026: IndiaMART  InterMESH Limited (referred to as “ IndiaM ART ” or the 
“Company”), today announced its financial results for the second  quarter ending September  30, 202 6.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Consolidated Financial Highlights (Q 2 FY202 7): 
 
IndiaMART reported consolidated Revenue from Operations of Rs. 429 Crore as compared to Rs.  391 
Crore in the corresponding quarter of last year , representing a growth of 10%. This includes IndiaMART 
Standalone Revenue of Rs . 389 Crore and Busy Infotech Revenue of Rs 37 Crore .  
 
Collections from Customer grew to Rs. 443 Crore for the quarte r, representing YoY growth of 9%, 
primarily comprising  IndiaMART Standalone Collections of Rs. 394 Crore and Busy Infotech 
Collections of Rs 46 Crore.  
 
Deferred Revenue as on September  30, 202 6 increased to Rs. 2,029  Crore representing a YoY growth 
of 16%. This primarily includes IndiaMART Standalone Deferred Revenue of Rs. 1, 863 Crore and 
Busy Infotech Deferred Revenue of Rs. 1 55 Crore.  
 
Net Profit for the quarter was Rs. 91 Crore.  Cash Flow from Operations for the quarter was Rs.  135 
Crore.  Cash and Investments balance stood at Rs. 3,234 Crore as  on September  30, 202 6.

Standalone Financial Highlights (Q 2 FY202 7): 
 
 
Standalone Revenue from Operations increased to Rs. 389 Crore  as compared to Rs. 3 60 Crore last 
year representing a growth of 8%. The growth was primarily driven by improvement in realization 
from paying suppliers . 
 
Collection s from Customer  grew to Rs. 394 Crore for the quarter representing a YoY growth of 8% 
and Deferred Revenue as on  September  30, 2026 increased to Rs. 1,863 Crore  representing a YoY 
growth of 14%.  
 
EBITDA for the quarter was  Rs. 156 Crore representing margin of 40%. Net Profit for the quarter was 
Rs. 106 Crore .  
 
Operational Highlights  (Q2 FY202 7):   
 
IndiaMART registered Unique business enquiries of  27 million in Q 2FY27. Supplier Storefronts grew 
to 8.9 million, an increase of 4% YoY and paying suppliers at the end of the quarter were 217K. 
 
Commenting on the performance, M r. Dinesh Agarwal, Chief Executive Officer , said:  
 
We remain focused on driving sustainable growth by building a trusted and reliable marketplace for 
both buyers and sellers. Our efforts to strengthen platform trustworthiness are fostering greater 
confidence and deeper, more meaningful engagement across th e marketplace. AI remains central to 
enhancing user experience, from discovery and matchmaking to conversational tools, while we also 
explore new monetisation  avenues. With a robust business model and strong cash generation, we remain 
well positioned to create long -term value for all our stakeholders.

About IndiaMART : 
IndiaMART is India's largest online B2B marketplace for business products and services. IndiaMART  
makes it easier to do business by connecting buyers and sellers across product categories and 
geographies in India.  IndiaMART provides ease and convenience to the buyers by offering a wide 
assortment of products and a responsive seller base while offering lead generation, lead management 
and business enablement solutions to its sellers .
