Financial Results for the Quarter and Half Year ended 30th September 2026  
 
AWL Delivers Strong Q2 FY27 Growth; Revenue Grows  26% to INR 22,202 Crore with 
Operating EBITDA up 11% YoY  
Growth led by Food & FMCG, with Revenue crossing INR 2,200 Crore, up 32% YoY  
 
AWL Reports Strong H1 FY27 Performance ; Revenue Rises 22% to INR 42, 250 Crore  
Operating EBITDA grows 25% to INR 1,3 47 Crore in H1 FY27  
 
 
 
Key Highlights – Q2 FY27  
▪ Consolidated revenue grew 26% YoY to INR 22,202 crore, supported by strong underlying 
volume growth of 14% YoY  
▪ Normalized Operating EBITDA grew 1 1% YoY to INR 623 crore  
▪ Food & FMCG revenue grew 32% YoY to INR 2,21 6 crore, with an EBITDA margin of 3. 2%, in 
line with FY27 guidance  
▪ Industry Essentials delivered 2 6% volume growth, supported by a higher contribution from 
Oleochemicals & Specialty Chemicals  
▪ Health & Convenience Portfolio crossed INR 1,400 crore in LTM revenues, reflecting the 
growing scale of AWL's differentiated food and edible oil offerings  
Key Highlights – H1 FY27  
▪ Consolidated revenue grew 22% YoY to INR 42,250 crore, with underlying volume growth of 
11% 
▪ Normalized Operating EBITDA grew 25% YoY to INR 1,3 47 crore  
▪ PBT (including profit  from joint venture s) grew 27% YoY to INR 822 crore, while PAT grew 22% YoY 
to INR 591 crore

www.awl.in   Registered Office:  Fortune House , Nr Navrangpura Railway Crossing, Ahmedabad 380 009, Gujarat, India   AWL Agri Business Limited ( AWL ), one of India's largest Food & FMCG companies, today 
announced its financial results for the quarter and half year ended 30 September 2026.  
 
The Company delivered strong revenue and volume growth in Q2 FY27, led by the continued 
expansion of its Food & FMCG portfolio and healthy performance in Industry Essentials. 
Consolidated Revenue grew 26% YoY to INR 22,202 crore, with underlying volume growth of 
14%. Normalized Operating EBITDA increased 1 1% YoY to INR 6 23 crore, while PAT stood at INR 
239 crore, compared with INR 245 crore in the corresponding quarter last year.  
 
The marginal decline in PAT was primarily attributable to minimum corporate tax computed on 
revenue of Bangladesh subsidiary under local regulations, which impacted consolidated tax 
expenses during the quarter.  
Management Remarks  
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Shrikant Kanhere, MD & CEO, AWL Agri Business 
Limited , said:  
 
"We delivered a strong performance in Q2 FY27, with revenue growing 26% and underlying 
volume growth of 14%, reflecting the strength of our diversified portfolio and consistent execution 
across businesses. Our Food & FMCG business remained a key growth driver, delivering 32% 
revenue growth and crossing INR 2,200 crore in quarterly revenues, while maintaining 
profitability in line with our expectations.  
 
Our strategy remains clear: to build one of India's largest and most trusted Food Platforms, 
anchored in Daily Essentials and Cent re-of-the-Plate categories. The shift towards branded and 
packaged foods  presents a significant growth opportunity, and our strong brands, integrated 
manufacturing capabilities and extensive distribution network position us well to capit alize this 
opportunity.  
 
Our focus remains on accelerating growth in packaged foods, expanding our premium, health and 
convenience portfolio, and improving profitability through scale and operating efficiencies. We 
will continue to strengthen our leadership in Edible Oils and scale  our value -added Oleochemicals 
& Specialty Chemicals portfolio, with a clear focus on delivering sustainable and profitable growth 
across businesses  over the long  term ."

Food & FMCG  
The Food & FMCG segment recorded 32% YoY revenue growth to INR 2,21 6 crore, supported by 
strong performance across its portfolio of packaged food and Daily Essentials. Segment EBITDA 
margin stood at 3. 2%, within the management's FY27 guidance range of 3 –4%. 
 
▪ Rice & Wheat Flour:  Both categories maintained double -digit revenue growth. The Basmati 
Rice business continued to gain market share, while the Kohinoor brand also recorded double -
digit revenue growth during the quarter.  
▪ Emerging Food Categories:  Fortune Pulses grew over 40% YoY, with quarterly revenues 
crossing INR 100 Crore. AWL is expanding its regional SKU portfolio and distribution across 
General Trade and Quick Commerce, targeting the significant opportunity within branded 
products in this largely unorgani zed category. Besan, Soya Nuggets, Sugar and other Daily 
Essentials remain important growth categories within the packaged food portfolio.  
▪ Health & Convenience Portfolio:  LTM revenues crossed INR 1,400 crore, supported by a 
diversified range of products including Soya Nuggets, Poha, Sattu, Brown Rice, Biryani Kits, 
Olive Oil, Cold -Pressed Mustard Oil and the Fortune Xpert range of functional oils. The 
Company continues to expand its premium, health -oriented and convenience -led categories 
through an expanded range of offerings.  
 
Edible Oils  
The Edible Oils segment recorded 5% YoY volume growth, with EBITDA of around INR 4, 100 per 
MT, in line with management's guidance of INR 4,000 –4,500/MT.  
 
The Company maintained its market share despite a challenging operating environment, 
supported by growth in domestic oils, particularly Mustard, Groundnut and Cottonseed Oils. AWL 
continues to focus on strengthening its branded portfolio, premium offerings and market 
execution.  
 
Industry Essentials  
Industry Essentials recorded 2 6% YoY volume growth, with EBITDA of approximately INR 
4,300/MT, ahead of management estimates, supported by an improving product mix.

AWL Agri Business Ltd. (formerly Adani Wilmar Limited) is one of India’s largest Food & FMCG 
companies, offering a diverse portfolio of essential kitchen staples, including edible oils, wheat 
flour, rice, pulses, and sugar. AWL through its brands, including flagship brand  - Fortune, 
commands the trust of more than 135 million households, reaching to every 1 in 3 Indian families. 
With 24 manufacturing facilities across 11 states, including India’s largest single -location refinery 
in Mundra (5,000 tons per day capacity), AWL ensures seamless production and distribution  
across the country . Its extensive supply chain, supported by 110+ stock points, over 10,000 
distributors and sub -distributors, along with a retail network of 2. 6 million outlets, guarantees 
widespread accessibility across urban and rural India.  
Beyond serving retail consumers, AWL also caters the HoReCa and institutional consumers , with 
staple food products, specialized bakery and Lauric fats, Castor Oil derivatives, Oleochemicals, 
and value -added Soya products. Its advanced castor oil facilities produce pharmaceutical -grade, 
and low -moisture castor oil, catering to global markets.  Additionally, AWL has expanded into the 
Home & Personal Care (HPC) segment, offering soaps, handwashes, and multipurpose cleaners, 
further strengthening its product offerings.
