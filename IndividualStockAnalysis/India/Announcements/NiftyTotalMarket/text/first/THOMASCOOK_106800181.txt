Sterling Deepens Spiritual Tourism Strategy with Entry into 
Varanasi  
Sterling Varanasi opens near Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple, strengthening the 
brand’s network across India’s high-intent spiritual destinations . 
Chennai, 29th September 2026:  Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited has announced its 
entry into Varanasi with the opening of Sterling Varanasi, adding one of India’s most 
signiﬁcant spiritual destinations to its growing portfolio.  
Located near the revered Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple, the 33 -room hotel offers 
convenient access to Assi Ghat, Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Banaras Hindu University and the ghats of the Ganges, giving guests a well -connected base from which 
to experience  Kashi.  
The opening strengthens Sterling’s presence across destinations where faith and 
spirituality are powerful reasons to travel. With Ayodhya, Haridwar, Rishikesh, 
Guruvayur, Tiruvannamalai, Pushkar, Madurai and Puri already part of its network, 
Varanasi adds an important destination to the company’s growing spiritual travel 
footprint.  
For Sterling, the opportunity goes beyond pilgrimage. Faith-led journeys are increasingly 
intersecting with family travel, culture, cuisine, heritage and discovery, while travellers seek greater comfort, reliability and quality hospitality.  
Commenting on the opening, Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director & CEO, Sterling 
Holiday Resorts  (SHRL) , said:  
“Our strategy is to understand why India travels and build around those reasons. 
Spiritual travel has always been one of India’s most enduring travel motivations; what is 
changing is the traveller and the experience around the journey.  
In Varanasi, the reason to travel may begin with faith, but the journey can extend into 
family, culture, cuisine, history and discovery. Our role is to make that experience more 
comfortable, reliable and meaningful. Kashi therefore becomes an important addition 
to Sterling’s destination -led growth strategy. ”  
Sterling Varanasi features 33 contemporary rooms, including the unique Premier Rooms with balconies offering views of Kashi.  
  
Its vegetarian restaurant, RASA KASHI, draws inspiration from Varanasi’s food traditions 
while offering a wider menu for contemporary travellers, bringing together two elements 
deeply associated with the city —food and faith.  
On the ﬁfth ﬂoor, KASHI REGALIA,  a partially covered glass -roof venue with 180 -degree 
views across Varanasi, offers a distinctive setting for intimate celebrations, family gatherings and special occasions.  
Guests can build their stay around the rhythms of the destination— from early -morning 
boat journeys on the Ganges and the evening Ganga Aarti to temple visits, Sarnath, Banarasi cuisine, silk and local craft traditions.  
Mr. Arya Vishwajeet Singh, Aditi Group, Owner of Sterling Varanasi,  said:  
“Our vision was to create a contemporary hospitality address that understands why 
people come to Varanasi and makes that journey easier and more comfortable. Sterling 
brings the brand reach, operating expertise and distribution strength to establish the 
property as a trusted hospitality address in the city. ”  
The addition of Varanasi further advances Sterling’s Destination Architect strategy —
building its network around why people travel, rather than conventional destination 
categories alone.  A journey may begin with the hills, wildlife, wellness, culture or 
spirituality.
