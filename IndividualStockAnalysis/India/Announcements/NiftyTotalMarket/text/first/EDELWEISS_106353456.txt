             CLARIFICATION ON RECENT REPORTS  IN THE MEDIA REGARDING EDELWEISS  
             
 
Mumbai, August 27, 2025 – Edelweiss Financial Services Limited (“EFSL”) wishes to set the record 
straight on a misleading article published by NDTV Profit and certain other media platforms on 
August 26, 2025.  
 
The original article incorrectly stated that Mr. Rashesh Shah, Promoter of EFSL, had sold shares in 
the Company. Mr. Shah has not sold any shares.  
 
Correct facts:   
• The block transaction was executed by the Edelweiss Employee Welfare Trust (EWT) in 
compliance with regulations.  
• The shares were acquired by Abakkus Asset Manager Pvt. Ltd., an asset management 
firm.  
 
Notably, the publications in question did not verify facts with Edelweiss before  publishing the 
original  misleading articles .  
 
Link to the revised article subsequently published by the media house:  Link 
  
Unverified reporting of this nature can create investor confusion,  reputational damage and risks 
unwarranted volatility in the Company’s share price.
