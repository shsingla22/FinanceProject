 a. In compliance with the provisions of Section 108

facility to cast their vote electronically, through the

 b.  However, as per the SEBI Master Circular bearing 
reference no. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-

September 21, 2026.

individual shareholders, who acquires shares of the

vote.

under “Login method for remote e-Voting and
