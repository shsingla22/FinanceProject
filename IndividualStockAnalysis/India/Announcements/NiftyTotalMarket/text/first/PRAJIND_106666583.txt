RESULTS OF POSTAL BALLOT THROUGH REMOTE ELECTRONIC VOTING 
CONDUCTED FOR RESOLUION S SET OUT IN THE POSTAL BALLOT NOTICE  
 
The Board of Directors of the Company, at their meeting held on 28th April , 202 6, decided to 
obtain consent of the members through Postal Ballot under Section 110 of the Companies Act, 
2013, read with Rule 20 and 22 of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 
2014, in respect of the proposals for passing Resolution s by way of postal ballot only by voting 
through electronic means.  
 
The Company had made  arrangements for e -voting and availed the services of National 
Securities Depository Ltd . (NSDL) to provide the facility to the Members for e -voting. 
Members whose names appear on the Register of Members/List of Beneficial Owners as on 8th 
May, 2026 i.e. cut -off date, were considered eligible for the purpose of voting. In view of 
prevailing MCA Circulars on postal ballot process, the voting on the Resolution s covered in 
the Notice took place through e -voting only and no physical ballots were collected. The 
Members were requested to cast their votes electronically up to 5 :00 p.m. on Tues day, the 16th 
June, 2026 . 
 
The voting period commenced on Monday , the 18th May, 202 6 at 9:00 a.m. and ended on   
Tues day, the 16th June, 202 6 at 5:00 p.m. (inclusive of both the days). The e -voting module 
was disabled by NSDL at 5 :00 p.m. on Tuesday , the 16th June, 202 6. 
 
Accordingly, the shareholders voted on the following Resolution s, proposed in the Postal Ballot 
Notice dated 28th April , 202 6.
