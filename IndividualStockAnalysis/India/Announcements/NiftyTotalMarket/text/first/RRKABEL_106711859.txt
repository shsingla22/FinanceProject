RRKabel  Limited  is a leading  consumer  electrical  company  in India  and has an operating  history  of over  27 years inthe
country .ItisIndia’s largest exporter ofwires and cables, offering awide range ofelectrical products including wires &
cables, fans, lighting, electrical accessories, and appliances forresidential, commercial, industrial, and infrastructural
