PRESS RELEASE 
 Arvind posts all time high quarterly Revenue and EBITDA supported by strong 
momentum in Advanced Material Business, Headwind in cost persists!  
Highlights for Q2 FY27 (Comparison on a YoY basis): 
 Consolidated Revenue & EBITDA are at ₹2919 & ₹331 Cr, a growth of 23% & 27% respectively 
 AMB India revenue and EBITDA grew 23% and 29% respectively with 14% margin 
 Greenfield garmenting facility at Varanasi goes live, to add ₹250 Cr in revenue at full utilization 
 Arvind’s credit rating upgraded by one notch from “AA-” to “AA with a stable outlook”. 
Ahmedabad, 28th October 2026: Arvind Ltd, India's leading textile, apparel and technical textile company, today 
announced its financial results for second quarter and half year ended 30 September 2026. The results reflect 
continuation of strong demand scenario and resilient performance across key business segments.

The first half was impacted by Middle East-led volatility in energy, freight and input costs, while demand in key 
Western markets remained cautious. As the quarter progressed, India benefited from a more favourable trade 
position and stronger festive order inflows. Backed by our integrated textile value chain and growing Advanced 
Materials business, we delivered healthy growth while maintaining stable margins. 
Business highlights for Q2 FY27 (Comparison on YoY basis) 
The quarter witnessed healthy volume momentum across AMB and some of the textile businesses, supported 
by strong execution, higher vertical integration and portfolio addition. 
 Denim fabric volumes crossed 18 Mn metres, registering growth of 19%, driven by higher demand while 
Woven fabric volumes stood at 34 Mn metres 
 Garmenting volumes registered 11 Mn pieces for the second time, recording 3% growth.  
 In AMB, Protective Gear [HP] segments witnessing robust traction as we continue to invest in capability 
building including new product development for the future 
Financial Highlights for Q2 FY27 (Comparison on YoY basis) 
 Consolidated Revenue and EBITDA for the quarter stood at ₹2,919 Cr and ₹331 Cr, up 23% and 27% 
respectively. While excluding Dalco-GFT, our Revenue and EBITDA stood at ₹2,663 Cr and ₹290 Cr, up 
by 12% and 11% respectively.  
 During the quarter, Textile division reported revenue@ of ₹1,999 Cr, up 11%, with EBITDA of ₹190 Cr. 
However, EBITDA growth was curtailed due to rise in input cost led to net impact of ~75 bps in textile 
margins considering normalized base. 
 Garmenting division reported revenue of ₹577 Cr, supported by 9% volume growth.  
 AMB reported revenue of ₹805 Cr (up 81%) with EBITDA of ₹119 (up 97%) translating to margin of 15%. 
While India business grew by 23% and touched ₹550 Cr of revenue with ₹78 Cr EBITDA. 
 Dalco-GFT contributed revenue of ₹256 Cr and EBITDA of ₹41 Cr during the quarter. EBITDA margin 
stood at 16% 
 Quarterly Profit after tax (PAT) before exceptional item stood at ₹123 Cr, a growth of 16%. 
 During the first half of FY 27, The company has spent about ₹372 Cr in various capex projects. 
@Reported revenue for textile division stood at ₹2146 Cr for Q2 FY27 & ₹4008 Cr for H1 FY27, the difference of ₹147 Cr (₹2146 - ₹1999) & 
₹274 Cr (₹4008 - ₹3734) respectively represents inter segment sales between textile & Advance Materials business which is eliminated to 
present a fair picture 
 
Other Highlights 
 The Company enhanced its global footprint during the quarter through the establishment of a European 
presence with a denim design and marketing hub in Amsterdam, the global centre of denim innovation 
and fashion helps improve network, strengthen customer engagement and supporting future growth 
opportunities in that market. 
 During the quarter, the Company commissioned Phase 1 of its greenfield garment manufacturing 
facility in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. The project is expected to serve the global customer and support 
future growth. 
 Care Rating has upgraded the credit rating of Arvind Ltd to AA with a stable outlook. 
Guidance for Q3 FY27 
 Raw material cost inflation continues to pressure margins, while ongoing global uncertainties and 
cautious consumer demand pose near-term challenges despite healthy demand trends in select end-
use sectors. 
 AMB India business demand is driven by industrial capex, infrastructure, mobility and specialty 
applications and expected to achieve ~18% Revenue and EBITDA growth with a stable margin of 14-
15% 
 Dalco integration is in full steam, capex programs are preponed to foster growth with 15%-16% margin. 
 Textile segment is expected to achieve early double-digit revenue growth, while margin may remain at 
current levels due to elevated cost scenario.  
 All ongoing capex programs are on track. Expected to cross ₹500 Cr in full year FY27. 
About Arvind Ltd:  
Arvind is a textile to retail conglomerate with focus on textiles, apparels, advanced materials, environmental solutions, telecom and Omni-
channel commerce. Arvind Limited is an integrated solutions provider in textiles with strong fiber to fashion capabilities for a global customer 
base. It is also a design powerhouse implementing innovative concepts and generating intellectual property. It ranks amongst the top 
suppliers of fabric worldwide. The company strives every day to create opportunities beyond conventional boundaries and believes that the 
possibilities are endless. For more information, please visit https://www.arvind.com/
