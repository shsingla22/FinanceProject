Happiest Minds Earns Global Top 50 Ranking at LACP 2026; 
Integrated Annual Report and ESG Report Win Gold  
Bengaluru, Kolkata , Seattle, and London, November 23, 2026:  Happiest Minds Technologies Limited 
(“Happiest Minds ”) (NSE: HAPPSTMNDS), an AI First, customer -centric digital engineering 
company,  announced that its Integrated Annual Report (IAR) 2026 has secured the #43 position in the 
global rankings  at the League of American Communications Professionals (LACP) 2026 Spotlight Awards. 
This marks an improvement of 1 3 positions from its #5 6 global ranking in 2025. The Company's ESG 
Report 2026  has also received a Gold award  in the competition.   
The LACP Spotlight Awards recognize excellence in corporate communications, annual reports, and other 
communication materials. The 2026 competition received entries from nearly 20 countries, with fewer than 
one in four submissions receiving award -level rec ognition.  
Venkatraman Narayanan, Managing Director, Happiest Minds Technologies , said, “We are pleased that 
our Integrated Annual Report 2026 has secured the #43 position in the global rankings, moving up from #56 
last year. Winning Gold for both our IAR and ESG Report makes this year's recognition particularly special. 
This is the sixth con secutive year that Happiest Minds has been recognized by LACP, making th is award  even 
more meaningful.”  
 
 

 
 
Happiest Minds has received recognition at the LACP Spotlight Awards for six consecutive years, winning 
Platinum in 2021 and Gold in the Integrated Report category from 2022 to 2026. Its ESG Reports also 
received Gold in 2025 and 2026. The Company has addi tionally won the SAFA Best Presented Annual 
Report Award, ICAI Award for Excellence in Financial Reporting, ICSI National Award for Excellence in 
Corporate Governance, and Golden Peacock Award for Excellence in Corporate Governance.
