The group has defined its businesses in two verticals (segments) namely: 
a) Plumbing (Pipes, fittings, water tanks, bathware).  
b) Paints and Adhesives (Erstwhile Resinova + Seal IT + Astral Chemie Limited + DSS 
(Speciality Chemicals)). 
 
Plumbing Business  
 
 
a) Overall demand scenario in plastic pipe industry was robust in Q2 FY-27, primarily due to weak 
quarter of Q1 FY-27 for Industry and very light inventory in the system. Polymer prices initially 
in the begging of July fall but from mid July onwards started upwards journey which supported 
the industry with good volumes across the board and we at Astral always believe in gaining 
market share continuously which we continued this year also which you can see in our 
numbers that we are the only company in country which has delivered double digit growth in 
H1 FY-27 10% in Plumbing Pipe vertical and we are happy to inform that we are in line with 
what we have given guidance of double digit volume growth for the full year 2026-27. We are 
confident that we will be able to deliver the same in H2 also. Not only that but we have 
achieved the highest EBIDTA Margins in H1 FY-27 of 17.4% among all the industry players and 
within our guided range of 16% to 18% for full year.  
 
b) The realization was slightly better during the quarter due to that the Q2 FY-27 value growth 
was 25.0% against the volume growth of 19.1%. The volatility in polymer prices are still 
continue but will be comparatively low because of implementation of MIP which will protect 
the downside of polymer prices.  
 
c) Polymer prices went up during the Q2 FY-27 but the full pass through happened in Q3 FY-27 
because of that the margins were under pressure. We at Astral have already pass on full price 
rise of Q2 FY-27 for PVC & CPVC in Q3 FY-27 so we are expecting better margins and better 
realizations in Q3 FY-27.  
 
d) During H1 FY-27, the Company has increased its Pipes and Fittings production capacity from 
4,17,645 M.T to 4,25,195 MT.  
 
e) Our new backward integration plant of CPVC Resin (40,000 M.T.) Phase I is progressing as per 
schedule and we are going on as per our planned schedule. At present Machinery installations 
are going on and we are confident that our trials will be completed by Q4 FY-27 and plant will 
be ready for commercial use by Q4 FY-27. We are expecting this plant will help us in gaining 
market share of CPVC Pipe and Fitting and also improve margins . The full benefit will be 
available from FY-28 onwards.  
 
f) Our New Product Pex (Aluminum Pex) is ready for launch, we are planning to launch by Q3 FY-
27. 
 
g) During Q2 FY-27, Bathware business has been grown by 26.8% in Sales.  
 
 
 
Paints and Adhesives Business  
Adhesives – Strong volume-led momentum 
 
a) Strong volume momentum continues in the Maintenance, New Bharat segment in Q2 FY-27 
with new dealers and distribution additions. 
 
b) Joinery continues to show growth quarter on quarter basis through distribution expansion and 
expanding product portfolio. 
 
c) Construction division growth is driven by backward integration and range expansion in Epoxy 
Grouts, Tile adhesives and other advanced chemistries. 
 
d) During Q2 FY-27, Adhesive India Business has delivered a robust growth of 25.3% in Sales with 
EBIDTA margin of 10.9%. We are continuously gaining market share in Adhesive Business in 
India but due to continued higher price of Raw Material on account of geopolitical pressure 
and the price pass through take place with gap. This has resulted a pressure on margin in Q2 
FY-27 which we are confident to overcome in coming quarters. 
International Adhesive Business 
a) During Q2 FY-27, Adhesive Overseas Business has grown by Robust 30.1% in Sales with Positive 
EBIDTA Margin of 6.3%. As against our guidance of 10%+ revenue growth, we have already 
achieved revenue growth of 28.1 % in H1 FY-27. 
Specialty Chemicals – Portfolio Expansion 
a) Astral Chemie Limited (formerly known as Astral Coatings Private Limited), a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Astral Limited has entered in to the definitive agreements dated June 11, 2026, 
to acquire a 60% partnership interest with effect from April 1, 2026 in Differentiated & 
Sustainable Solutions LLP ("DSS").  
With the acquisition of Differentiated & Sustainable Solutions LLP (DSS) in Q1 FY-27, we have 
expanded the portfolio into Specialty Chemicals. In H1 FY-27, it has contributed to Rs. 120.4 
million in Revenue and EBIDTA of Rs. 16.6 million on Standalone basis. Necessary 
infrastructure upgrade and capacity enhancement are currently undertaken which will 
support volume and product portfolio expansion in FY-28. 
Paints – Broad-based acceleration 
a) Paint Business has been grown by robust 39.9% in Sales during Q2 FY-27 and by 44% during 
H1 FY-27.  Due to volatile raw material prices, margins have remained under pressure and lag 
in pass through of the costs. We are expecting improved margins in the coming quarter. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
b) Extura Vivid, the new exterior emulsion, launched in Q1 FY-27, has further strengthened the 
portfolio through Strong dealer, contractor, and consumer initiatives were driven during the 
quarter. The product continues to witness strong acceptance across the network.
