PRESS RELEASE  
ITI Limited Wins 4G Expansion Phase IX Order from BSNL  Worth  
Rs. 856.39  Crores  
 
Bengaluru : ITI Limited, country’s f irst PSU post -independence and premier telecom 
manufacturing company has recently received  work order  for expansion of 4G Mobile 
Network in West India  from Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited (BSNL)  for total value 
of Rs 856.39 Crore . The scope of work includes Planning, Engineering, Supply, 
Installation, Testing and Commissioning of 4G Mobile Network on a turnkey basis for 
7613 sites in the West Zone. As part of India’s Atmanirbhar  telecom initiative, BSNL is 
implementing the prestigio us project of indigenous 4G telecom equipment to achieve self -
reliance in the telecom segment to fulfil domestic market needs.   
 
ITI Limited along with TCS as a consortium partner had successfully executed the turnkey 
project worth Rs  2640 Crore s for provisioning 4G mobile network under Phase IX.2, for 
BSNL West Zone states that include Maharashtra, Goa, Gujarat, Chhattisgarh, Madhya 
Pradesh and Mumbai LSA. The pro ject consisted  of RF planning, engineering, supply, 
installation, testing, commission ing, integration and annual maintenance of the 4G 
mobile network at 23633 sites in the West Zone. On successful completion of this project, 
ITI Limited has now received a work order  recently for expansion of 4G Mobile Network 
in BSNL West Zone including sa turation sites (DBN funded) for supply, installation 
and commissioning of 7613 sites.  The total value of the order amounts to Rs 856.39 
Crore s.  
 
Speaking on the  occasion, Mr. Rajesh Rai, Chairman and Managing Director, ITI 
Limited said – “It is a very proud moment for ITI Limited as our prestigious client 
BSNL continues to repose great trust in ITI Limited’s execution capability by awarding 
work order  to us. ITI Limited has steadfastly been executing and has proved to be an 
able ally in the country’s Atmanirbhar telecom initiatives.”  
 
 

 
 
 
 
ITI Limited is currently executing Bharatnet Phase III projects worth Rs. 7000 Cr  for 
BSNL in  Himachal Pradesh, West Bengal, Andaman & Nicobar, Arunachal Pradesh, 
Nagaland , and Manipur state s.
