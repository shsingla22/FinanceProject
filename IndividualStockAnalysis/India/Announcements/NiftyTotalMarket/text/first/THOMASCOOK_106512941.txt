PRESS RELEASE  
Sterling Announces  Arka Suites Puri, an All- Suite Boutique Addition to Its 
Flagship Puri Resort  
• A Seaside Haven Shaped for Unhurried, Indulgent Stays  
Chennai, 30th 2026:  Sterling Holiday Resorts has announced the launch of Sterling Arka  
Suites , Puri’s first all- suite upscale boutique hotel, marking a refined new chapter in the 
brand’s coastal journey in Odisha. Although, l ocated within the same precinct as the existing 
Sterling Puri resort, this new elevated block  has been conceived as a distinct, boutique 
sanctuary —offering enhanced space, privacy and a more indulgent stay experience, while 
remaining seamlessly connected to the larger destination resort.  
With the arrival of Sterling Arka Suites Puri , Sterling now offers the most expansive canvas of 
stays in the sacred seaside town . Featuring pool facing private balcony in all suites, 
thoughtfully designed and spacious interiors, walls  accented with contemporary 
interpretations of Odisha’s Pattachitra art, and plush settings  in living and bath areas, Arka 
Suites  is crafted for discerning travellers who cherish space, privacy and unhurried luxury —
inviting a more intimate way to unwind in  Puri.  
Commenting on the launch, Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director and CEO of Sterling said,  
“Sterling had an early and committed presence in Puri, and over the years we’ve seen the 
destination evolve into a more layered leisure and cultural hub. Sterling Arka Suites Puri 
marks the next phase of that journey —introducing Puri’s first all- suite, upscale boutique 
experience within a destination we’ve helped shape over time. This all -suite addition allows us 
to offer a more intimate, elevated stay while remaining seamlessly connected to the larger Sterling Puri e cosystem, giving travellers greater choice, space and de pth in how they 
experience the city —whether for leisure, culture or longer, immersive stays.”  
Dining at the property is anchored by Solara, a thoughtfully curated restaurant that pairs 
global flavours with subtle local influences, all delivered with an easy coastal elegance. Guests 
also enjoy access to Amo Odisha , the award -winning Odiya speciality restaurant at the 
adjoining Sterling Puri, deepening the destination’s culinary narrative. Beyond the table, 
curated cultural experiences bring Odisha to life —from Pattachitra art sessions and Odissi 
performances to san d-art workshops, Chappan Bhog culina ry masterclasses, and immersive 
festival showcases including Rath Yatra and the Konark Dance Festival.  
Arka Suites  strengthens Sterling’s ability to host celebrations and gatherings in Puri, with 
versatile  event spaces that can be configured to accommodate more than  700 guests,  while 
remaining caters to cultural- learning corners and activity zones designed to engage younger 
travellers . 
Located at driving distance from the  revered Jagannath Temple and the city’s golden 
coastline, the property invites guests to experience the spiritual and cultural essence of Puri while enjoying spacious suite stays and personalised hospitality.  
 
 
  With Sterling Arka Suites Puri , the brand deepens its commitment to destination- led coastal 
hospitality,  cultural events , offering guests the choice of scale and vibrancy at Sterling Puri 
alongside a more refined, all- suite boutique experience —within a single, integrated 
destination.
