Press Release 
 
GHCL announces Q2/H1 FY27 Results 
PAT grew by 19% in Q2 FY27 YoY (Standalone basis) 
Net revenue grew by 11% to Rs. 821 crores in Q2 FY27 YOY 
 
New Delhi, 31st October 2026:   GHCL Limited, India’s leading Chemical Company, today announced 
its financial results for Q2/H1 FY27. 
 
Commenting on the financial performance, Mr. R S Jalan, Managing Director, GHCL Limited said,  
“During the second quarter of FY27, the global soda ash industry continued to face pricing pressure, 
with realisations remaining soft amid ongoing global oversupply, a trend likely to persist over the next 
few quarters.  
  
While global oversupply and steady import pressure kept near-term realisations soft, our low-cost 
positioning, disciplined operational efficiencies and structural cost management enabled us to remain 
resilient through the quarter.  
  
We continue to strengthen our strategic diversification and backward integration. During the quarter, 
we successfully commenced commercial production of Vacuum Salt, powered by waste heat 
recovered from our soda ash operations. Subsequent to the close of the quarter, we commenced 
commercial production of Bromine, marking yet another milestone in our diversification journey.  
  
Looking ahead, our strategic focus remains firmly on operational excellence, optimizing our newly 
commissioned Vacuum Salt and Bromine capacities, and advancing our long-term greenfield 
expansion plans. Backed by best-in-class operations, resilient domestic demand and a strong balance 
sheet, we remain confident in our ability to navigate near-term cyclicality and deliver sustainable, 
long-term value to all stakeholders.”  
 
Financial Performance (Standalone) 
 
Q2 FY27 VS Q2 FY26 
 
 Net Revenue grew by 11% to Rs. 821 crores as compared to Rs. 739 crores in the 
corresponding quarter of the previous year  
 EBIDTA grew by 16% to Rs. 202 crores as compared to Rs. 175 crores in the Q2 FY26 
 Net Profit grew by 19% to Rs. 127 crores as compared to Rs. 107 crores in the Q2 FY26 
 
H1 FY27 VS H1 FY26 
 
 Net Revenue grew by 4% to Rs. 1,619 crores as compared to Rs. 1562 crores in the H1 FY26 
 
 EBIDTA grew by 9% to Rs. 435 crores as compared to Rs. 399 crores in the H1 FY26 
 Net Profit grew by 26% to Rs. 319 crores as compared to Rs. 252 crores in the H1 FY26 
 
 
 
ABOUT GHCL 
 
GHCL Limited is the largest manufacturer of soda ash at a single location in the country. Soda Ash (Anhydrous Sodium 
Carbonate) is a major raw material for the detergent & glass industries and Sodium Bicarbonate (baking soda). It is an 
important raw material for solar glass and lithium batteries as well. The company has a state-of-the-art manufacturing 
plant of soda ash at Sutrapada, Gujarat with an installed production capacity of 1.2 million tons per annum. Sustainability 
is a core element of business strategy as defined by the ‘GHCL Way’ with its four pillars, i.e. Responsible Stewardship, 
Social Inclusiveness, promoting relationships and adding value. GHCL Limited remains committed to working closely with 
stakeholders for promoting the agenda of sustainability underpinning on the core values of Respect, Trust, Ownership 
and Integrated Teamwork.
