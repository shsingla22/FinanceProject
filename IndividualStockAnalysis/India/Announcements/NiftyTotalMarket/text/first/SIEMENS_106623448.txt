Sub: Change of Financial Year of the Company: Update
Dear Sir / Madam,
This is in continuation of our earlier communication dated 8th August 2025, wherein the
Company had informed about the Board of Directors’ decision to change the financial year of
the Company to a uniform financial year cycle from 1st October-30thSeptember to
1st April-31st March, every year and observing a one-time transitional 18 months financial year,
from 1st October 2024 to 31st March 2026.
This is to update you that the Company has today received an order from the Central
Government through the Regional Director, Western Region Directorate I, Ministry of
Corporate Affairs, Mumbai, vide its order dated 13th May, 2026, approving the following:
i. Adoption of a uniform financial year from 1st April to 31st March starting 1st April
2026 to 31st March, 2027 and onwards; and
ii. Observing 18 months financial year from 1st October 2024 to 31st March 2026 as a
consequence to change in the Company’s financial year cycle.
