In compliance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013  (the “Act”) , SEBI (Listing Obligations

On confirmation of the requisite quorum present in the meeting , Ms. Sweta Poddar, Company

Mr. Akshay Bansari lal Arora, Chairman of the Company chaired the meeting.  
 
The Company Secretary  informed that the mee ting was held through VC/OAVM  facility in
