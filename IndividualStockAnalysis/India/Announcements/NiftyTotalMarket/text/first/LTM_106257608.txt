SUMMARY OF PROCEEDINGS OF THE 29th ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF LTIMINDTREE LIMITED 
 
 
The 29th Annual General Meeting (‘AGM’) of LTIMindtree Limited (‘the Company’), was held on 
Friday, May 30, 2025 at 3:00 P.M. (IST) through Video Conference or Other Audio-Visual Means, in 
accordance with the requirements laid under the Companies Act, 2013 read with circulars issued 
by Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and Securities Exchange Board of India (SEBI). 
 
Mr. S.N. Subrahmanyan, Chairman, chaired the AGM and welcomed the Members. He then 
introduced the Directors, Chief Financial Officer, Company Secretary attending the AGM. He also 
informed the Members that senior officials of the Company, Statutory Auditor and Secretarial 
Auditor were also virtually present.

Members 
 
59 Members attended the AGM. 
 
Requisite quorum being present, Chairman called the AGM to order and mentioned that since the 
29th AGM was being held through VC, appointment of proxies by Members was not allowed.  
 
He informed the Members that electronic copy of the Integrated Annual Report for FY-25 and 
Notice convening the 29th AGM were sent to Members by e-mail on their registered e-mail id.  
 
With due permission of Members, Notice convening the 29th AGM, reports by the Statutory 
Auditor and Secretarial Auditor were taken as read.  
 
Chairman then addressed the Members, highlighting the Company’s business activities during FY-
25. He also informed that Mr. Debashis Chatterjee, Chief Executive Officer & Managing Director 
opted retirement from his office due to personal reasons and today would be his last working day 
in the Company. He whole-heartedly appreciated Mr. Debashis Chatterjee’s leadership and 
immense contributions to the overall growth & success story of LTIMindtree. Further, he 
mentioned that the Board of Directors at their meeting held prior to the commencement of the 
 
 29th AGM upon recommendation of the Nomination & Remuneration Committee had approved 
appointment of Mr. Venugopal Lambu as Chief Executive Officer & Managing Director w.e.f. May 
31, 2025. Mr. S.N. Subrahmanyan wished that Mr. Venugopal Lambu would drive growth & create 
shareholder’s value for LTIM in the years to come.  
 
Thereafter, he requested the Company Secretary to brief Members on the AGM proceedings. 
 
Ms. Angna Arora, Company Secretary then apprised the Members on the guidelines for e-voting at 
the meeting on the resolutions, the start date & time and end date & time for remote e-voting, 
details of the Scrutinizer, submission of voting results and advice to those Members who had 
registered themselves as speaker at the AGM. 
 
Ms. Arora then handed over the proceedings back to the Chairman. 
 
With due permission of the Members, Chairman tabled the agenda items as contained in the 
Notice convening the 29th AGM and mentioned that since all the resolutions were already put to 
vote during the remote e-voting period, no motion would be moved at the meeting for the 
proposed resolutions.

Chairman then opened the floor to the Members, to raise queries/questions. 
Questions/Queries raised by Members were suitably responded by the Chairman and Mr. Venu 
Lambu.  
Chairman then authorised the Company Secretary to receive Scrutinizer’s report and 
subsequently, declare the voting results by informing the Stock Exchanges.  
He informed the Members that the e-voting window was open on the NSDL e-voting platform for 
15 minutes from  the conclusion  of the proceedings of the AGM and requested  Members  to cast 
their votes, in case they had not cast vote during the remote e-voting period.  
Chairman concluded the proceedings of the AGM by thanking all the Members and Directors for 
joining the meeting. 
The AGM concluded at 4: 10 p.m. (IST).
