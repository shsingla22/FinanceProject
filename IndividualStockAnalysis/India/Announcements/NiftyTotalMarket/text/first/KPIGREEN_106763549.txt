Dear Sir/Madam,  
  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we are pleased to inform you that KPI Green Energy Limited ("the Company") has received a 
Letter of Intent (LOI) for development of a Wind -Solar Hybrid Power Project under a Group Captive 
arrangement . 
The project comprises 33.6 MW of Wind capacity and 33.6 MW AC / 43 MW DC of Solar capacity , 
aggregating to 33.6 / 76.6 MW DC capacity , and will be developed for a reputed c ustomer under the 
Group Captive framework.  
The project strengthens the Company’s presence in the C&I and Group Captive renewable energy 
segments, enhances its Wind, Solar and Hybrid capabilities, and adds to its order book.  
It supports the Company’s strategy of expanding through IPP, Group Captive and EPC opportunities 
while delivering long -term stakeholder value.
