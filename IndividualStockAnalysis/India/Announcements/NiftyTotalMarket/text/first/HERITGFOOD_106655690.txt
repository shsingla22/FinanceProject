Heritage Foods Honored with IR Excellence Award in the Food & Beverage 
Category  
Recognition reflects the Company's commitment to high standards of disclosure, sound 
corporate governance and meaningful engagement with the investment community.  
June  5th 202 6: Heritage Foods Limited (BSE: 519552; NSE: HERITGFOOD), a leading Dairy Company 
offering milk and Value -added dairy products  has been honored with the IR Excellence Award in 
the Food & Beverage Category  by the IR (Investor Relations) Society . 
The award was received on behalf of Heritage Foods by Mr. Umakanta Barik, Company Secretary & 
Compliance Officer, at the awards ceremony organized by the IR Society  
The award recognizes Heritage Foods' commitment to high standards of disclosure, sound corporate 
governance and constructive engagement with the invest or community. It reflects the Company's 
continued focus on fostering trust through timely communication, transparency and responsible 
business practices.  
Heritage Foods has consistently strengthened its investor relations framework through regular 
interactions with investors and analysts, comprehensive disclosures  and a disciplined approach to 
communication. These efforts have helped enhance understanding of the Company's business 
performance, strategic priorities and long -term growth opportunities.  
 
Commenting on the recognition, Ms. Brahmani Nara, Executive Director, Heritage Foods Limited , 
said:  “We are honored to receive this recognition from the IR Society. This award reflects our 
commitment to transparency, strong governance, and meaningful engagement with the invest or 
community. We believe trust is built through consistent communication, accountability, and responsible 
business conduct. This achievement reinforces Heritage Foods’ standing as a professionally managed 
organization and is a testament to the collective eff orts of our Board, leadership team, and employees, 
who continue to uphold the highest standards of integrity, accountability, and excellence in corporate 
governance ” 
 
About Heritage Foods Limited:  
Heritage Foods Limited (NSE: HERITGFOOD; BSE: 519552) is one of India’s leading dairy companies, 
founded in 1992 with a vision to provide high quality, nutritious milk and dairy products to families 
across  the nation. Over the years, Heritage has grown into a trusted brand, known for its commitment 
to purity, freshness, and sustainability. With operations spanning 1 3 states, Heritage Foods serves 
millions of consumers, offering a wide range of milk, curd, butter, paneer, and other value -added 
dairy products.
