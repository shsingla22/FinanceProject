Mr. Al-Noor Ramji is a veteran CEO, CIO, CTO, Board Advisor, Investor and Entrepreneur with 
more than 30 years of experience driving digital strategy and transformation for global 
enterprises, advising boards and mentoring and investing in startups. He is curr ently Chairman 
of Digital Growth Advisors Limited. His most recent executive position was as Group Chief 
Digital Officer at Prudential plc. Prior to that, he held CEO and senior technology leadership 
roles at organizations like Misys PLC, Calypso Technolog ies, BT Group, Qwest 
Communications, Dresdner Kleinwort Benson, UBS Group AG and Credit Suisse First Boston. 
He is also the founder or co -founder of successful tech start -ups. He has experience of several 
IPOs and successful proxy battles. Mr. Ramji holds a BSc in Electronics from the University of 
London, is a Chartered Financial Analyst and has been a multi -year recipient of CIO  
awards globally.
