We wish to inform the Stock Exchange that Titagarh Rail Systems Limited has received approval from the 
Ministry of Railways, Government of India, through the Railway Board, for registration as a Wagon Leasing 
Company (WLC) under the Wagon Leasing Scheme (W LS) of Indian Railways.  
 
Pursuant to the said approval, the Company is eligible to own railway wagons and offer such wagons on lease 
for operations on the Indian Railways network, in accordance with the provisions, terms, and conditions 
prescribed under the Wagon Leasing Scheme.  
 
The Wagon Leasing Scheme has been introduced by Indian Railways with the objective of encouraging private 
sector participation in rolling stock ownership, improving availability of wagons, and enhancing efficiency and 
capacity in freight transportation. Th e Company shall comply with all applicable operational, safety, 
maintenance, and regulatory requirements as stipulated by Indian Railways and other competent authorities 
from time to time.  
 
The grant of Wagon Leasing Company registration represents a significant strategic development for the 
Company and marks its entry into the wagon leasing segment. This approval strengthens the Company’s 
integrated presence across wagon manufacturing, asset  ownership, and freight mobility solutions and is 
expected to enhance long -term business visibility and value creation within the rail logistics ecosystem.  
 
The Company remains committed to pursuing opportunities aligned with its long -term growth strategy and 
focused on sustainable growth, operational synergies, and value creation for its stakeholders.  
 
The Company shall keep the Stock Exchange informed of any further material developments in this regard, as 
required under the applicable provisions of the Listing Regulations.
