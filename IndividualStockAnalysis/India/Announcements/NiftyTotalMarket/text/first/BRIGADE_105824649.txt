PRESS RELEASE 
 
IHG Hotels & Resorts and Brigade Group to bring the InterContinental brand to 
Hyderabad  
The latest collaboration between the two brands is in line with IHG’s strategy to expand its luxury and 
lifestyle portfolio in the country and Brigade’s plan to develop its hospitality footprint in Hyderabad.  
Bengaluru, April 25, 2024: IHG Hotels & Resorts, a global hospitability company, and Brigade Group, 
renowned property developer in India have announced the signing of a management agreement to debut 
the InterContinental brand in Telangana − InterContinental Hyderabad Neopolis . Slated to open in 2029, this 
newly constructed hotel is expected to be an integral part of Brigade’s upcoming, mixed-use development.  
The world-renowned brand, InterContinental has over 220 open hotels across more than 60 countries. 
Commemorating its 75th Diamond Anniversary in 2021, InterContinental continues to uphold its esteemed 
status as the world's first and largest luxury hotel brand. In 2023, the brand reached a pipeline of 100 hotels 
and resorts for the first time, representing a substantial 45% growth of its current system size. 
InterContinental Hyderabad Neopolis, proposed to have 300 rooms, once opened, is expected to showcase 
five dining options, including a signature restaurant, two specialty restaurants, and a lobby lounge. The hotel 
is also proposed to offer conferencing facilities, a fitness center, spa, a swimming pool, an outdoor recreation 
area and a retail outlet. To cater to MICE demands and large social gatherings, the hotel is proposed to 
feature eight meeting rooms including a spacious ballroom.  
Commenting on the announcement, Sudeep Jain, Managing Director, South West Asia, IHG Hotels & 
Resorts, said, “We are once again delighted to partner with Brigade Enterprises Ltd to introduce our luxury 
& lifestyle brand, InterContinental to the city of Hyderabad. Hyderabad is gaining prominence as one of the 
best cities in the country with the upcoming IT sector, global retail presence and heavy industries. This can 
present an appealing opportunity to us to deliver unparalleled luxury experiences to a new set of travelers.”  
 
 
 
 
 
 
He further added, “The signing is an opportunity to bring quality luxury accommodation to the city, catering 
to the growing needs of diverse travelers and contributing to the city’s economic development. In line with 
our strategy to expand our luxury brands in the country, we look forward to welcoming guests and providing 
them with luxury amenities that InterContinental is renowned for.” 
Nirupa Shankar, Jt. Managing Director of Brigade  Enterprises Limited  added, “We are happy to once again 
partner with an internationally and locally trusted hospitality brand like IHG, this time to bring 
InterContinental brand to Hyderabad. With a combination of an ideal location and IHG’s scale of global 
systems, technology, and loyalty programme, we hope that InterContinental Hyderabad Neopolis will 
become a preferred choice for business, leisure and spiritual travelers visiting the region. After Holiday Inn 
Chennai OMR IT Expressway, Holiday Inn Bengaluru Racecourse and Holiday Inn Express & Suites Bengaluru 
OMR, this will be our fourth hotel in partnership with IHG” 
IHG® currently has 46 hotels operating across five brands in India, including Six Senses, InterContinental 
Hotels and Resorts®, Crowne Plaza®, Holiday Inn Resort® and Holiday Inn Express®, and a strong pipeline of 
49 hotels due to open in the next 3-5 years.
