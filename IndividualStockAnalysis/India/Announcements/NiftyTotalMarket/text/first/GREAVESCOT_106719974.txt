Re: Proposed initial public offering of equity shares bearing face value of ₹1 each (“Equity Shares”) of 
Greaves Electric Mobility Limited (the “Company” and such offering, the “Offer”)  
This is with respect to  the draft red herring prospectus dated December 23, 2024  (the “ DRHP ”) filed by the 
Company with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (the “ SEBI ”), BSE Limited (“ BSE ”) and the National 
Stock Exchange of India Limited (“ NSE ”, together with BSE, the “ Stock Exchanges ”) in connection with the 
Offer . 
 
In connection with the above, the Company had received in-principle approval for the Offer from (i) the NSE 
pursuant to its letter  bearing reference  no. NSE/LIST/5023; and (ii) the BSE pursuant to its letter  bearing reference  
no. LO\IPO\PJ\IP\386\2024 -25, each dated February 17, 2025 and final observations from the SEBI pursuant to 
its letter  bearing reference  no. SEBI/HO/CFD/RAC -DIL3/P/OW/2025/12588/1 dated May 8, 2025  (“SEBI Final 
Observations ”).  
 
In terms of the SEBI Final Observations, the proposed Offer was required to open for subscription within 12 
months from the date of issuance of SEBI Final Observations, i.e., May 7, 2026.   
 
Further, in order to support the execution of the planned business initiatives, growth investments, expansion 
priorities, and associated operational and capital expenditure requirements, the Company is undertak ing a rights 
issue.  
 
Accordingly, in view of the proposed rights issue and the restrictions under the SEBI ICDR Regulations in relation 
to further issuances, we have been informed that the Company does not propose  to avail the one time relaxation 
granted pursuant to the SEBI circular no. HO/49/11/11(123)2026 -CFD -RAC -DIL2/I/8760/2026 dated April 7, 
2026  which permits extension of  validity of all observation letters issued by the SEBI, and expiring between April 
1, 2026 and September 30, 2026 till September 30, 2026 , and accordin gly, the validity of the SEBI Final 
Observations has lapsed . Consequently, the Company will not be proceeding with the Offer.
