This is in furtherance to our letter dated May 17, 2024 , wherein the Company had intimated that a 
subsidiary of the Company viz. Alliance Spaces Private Limited (“ASPL ”), has received a show cause 
notice from the GST authorities for payment of tax liability along with the applicable interest and 
penalty for the tax period April 2019 to March 2020.  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30(6) read with Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange 
Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, (“Listing 
Regulations”),  we wish to inform you that subsequent to the  reply submitted  by ASPL  to the said 
notice , it has received a demand order under Section 73 of GST Act, from the GST authorities  and the 
authorities have revised the amount of tax, interest and penalty and advised ASPL  to pay the revised 
amount of tax as determined, along with the applicable revised interest and penalty for the tax period 
April 2019  to March 2020 .  
 
ASPL will contest the matter and prefer an appeal against the said demand order at an appropriate 
forum.

(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with SEBI Circular No.
