Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
With reference to the captioned subject and in terms of the provisions of Regulation 30 of 
SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“ SEBI Listing 
Regulations ”), we hereby would like to inform that, the Board of Directors of the NGM at 
their meeting held today i.e., Saturday, October 24, 2026, has inter-alia, noted/considered 
and approved the following: 
 
1. Appointment of Mrs. Ruchika Bansal as Non-Executive Independent Director on the 
Board of Next Generation Manufacturers Private Limited (“ NGM”) a Wholly Owned 
Step-Down Subsidiary of PG Electroplast Limited. 
 
2. Resignation tendered by M/s. M.S. Barmecha & Co., Chartered Accountants (Firm 
Registration No. 101029W) as the existing Statutory Auditors of the Next Generation 
Manufacturers Private Limited (“NGM”) , a Step-down Wholly Owned Subsidiary of     
PG Electroplast Limited.  
 
The Board placed on record its appreciation for the professional services rendered by 
the Statutory Auditors of NGM during their tenure with the Company. 
3. The Board of Directors of the company in order to align and consolidate the statutory 
auditors of the subsidiary companies with the auditors of its parent company took note 
of the appointment of M/s. B S R & Co. LLP, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration 
No: 101248W/W-100022) as the New Statutory Auditors of NGM for a term of five 
years, subject to approval of the Shareholders of the company.
