News Release 
INDIA’S LARGEST-SELLING PREMIUM BRANDY MANSION 
HOUSE HITS 10 MILLION CASES IN SALES 
Tilaknagar Industries to Expand Brand’s Reach Nationally 
 
Mumbai April 01, 2026: Leading Indian-Made Foreign Liquor (IMFL) manufacturer, 
Tilaknagar Industries Limited (TI) (BSE: 507205 | NSE: TI), has achieved a significant 
milestone with its flagship brand, Mansion House Brandy (MHB), surpassing 10 million cases 
in sales in FY26, up from 8.7 million cases in FY25.  
This achievement has been driven by the strong performance of the entire Mansion House 
portfolio, including Mansion House French Brandy, Mansion House Reserve and Mansion House 
“Flandy” (Flavoured Brandy).  
MHB has also earned global recognition in Drinks International’s ‘ The Millionaires’ Club’  reports 
of 2024 and 2025 for two consecutive years, as India’s largest-selling brandy and the world’s 
second highest-selling brandy by volume, reflecting its consistent performance on the global 
stage. 
Mr Ahmed Rahimtoola, Chief Marketing Officer, Tilaknagar Industries  said, “Crossing 
the 10 million case milestone is not just a scale achievement, it reflects the journey of 
transforming Mansion House into a brand that is culturally relevant and consistently engaging 
for today’s consumer. Over the recent years, our focus has been on building a strong category 
narrative around warmth, inclusivity and social connection, while ensuring that the brand 
stays contemporary in how it shows up across markets. This milestone reinforces our belief 
that when storytelling, product quality and distribution come together, brands can truly scale 
with strength.”  
With a strong and diverse portfolio, backed by consumer trust, the company is now poised to 
drive the next phase of growth with renewed focus, strategic vision and determination.  
Mr Nishant Jain, Chief Sales Officer,  Tilaknagar Industries  said, “Brandy consumption 
in India has traditionally been concentrated in the southern markets. As category leaders, we 
see a significant opportunity to expand its footprint across the country. With the enhanced 
distribution strength from our recent acquisition of Imperial Blue, we are well-positioned to 
take our brandy portfolio to a truly pan-India audience .” 
As India’s leading brandy player, TI continues to drive category innovation through a strong 
and evolving portfolio. The company recently launched Monarch Legacy Edition Brandy, the 
only 100 per cent pure grape brandy made from Indian and French grape spirits, as a part of 
the company’s luxury portfolio. TI’s brandy presence is further bolstered by the fast-growing 
Courrier Napoleon Brandy, another millionaire brand.  
  
About Tilaknagar Industries:  
Tilaknagar Industries is one of India’s leading alcoholic beverage companies, with a rich legacy 
spanning over 90 years. Founded in 1933 by Shri Mahadev L. Dahanukar as Maharashtra 
Sugar Mills, TI has, over the years, transformed into a major player in the India Made Foreign 
Liquor (“IMFL”) industry. Under the leadership of Shri Amit Dahanukar, TI has further 
strengthened its leadership across categories with ‘Millionaire’ brands like Mansion House 
Brandy, Imperial Blue Whisky and Courrier Napoleon Brandy. The Company also has a strong 
presence across Rum and Gin through Madiraa Rum & Blue Lagoon Gin. Further, with a strong 
focus on premiumization, ‘House of TI’ – the Company’s luxury vertical – is anchored by 
Monarch Legacy Edition, its first luxury brandy, and Seven Islands Pure Malt Whisky, an Indo-
Scottish premium whisky. ‘House of TI’ also includes an investment arm that supports India’s 
emerging craft spirits players.
