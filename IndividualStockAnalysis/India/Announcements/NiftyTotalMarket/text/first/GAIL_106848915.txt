GMPL, A Wholly Owned Subsidiary of GAIL (India) Limited, Successfully 
Commissions 1.25 million tons per annum PTA Manufacturing Plant at Mangalore  
 
                
Mangalore, November 5, 2026 : GAIL Mangalore Petrochemicals Limited (GMPL), a 
wholly owned subsidiary of GAIL (India) Limited (GAIL) has successfully commissioned 
its 1.25 MTPA Purified Terephthalic Acid (PTA) manufacturing plant located in the 
Mangalore Special Economic Zone (MSEZ), K arnataka. The plant was originally 
established by JBF Petrochemicals Limited (JBPL), which subsequently underwent 
Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP). In the year 2023, GAIL acquired JBPL 
through the CIRP, pursuant to the Resolution Plan approve d by the National Company Law 
Tribunal (NCLT) and subsequently undertook extensive revival and refurbishment of the 
plant. The successful commissioning is the culmination of an extensive revival and 
refurbishment exercise undertaken by GAIL, involving sign ificant engineering, 
technological and operational challenges.  
  
The successful commissioning represents a significant milestone for GAIL in its strategic 
endeavour to strengthen its presence in the downstream petrochemical sector. It is a 
testament to GAIL's commitment, sustained management support and the collective e fforts 
of GMPL -Team, technology partners, consultants, contractors and other stakeholders. This 
achievement marks not merely the revival of an industrial asset, but the realisation of a 
strategic vision and the beginning of a new chapter in GAIL's petroche mical growth 
journey.  
  
PTA is a key raw material used in the manufacture of polyester fibre, yarn, PET resin and 
other polyester -based products, finding extensive applications in the textile, apparel and 
packaging industries. With an installed capacity of 1.25 million tons per a nnum, GMPL's 
facility represents a significant addition to India's domestic PTA manufacturing capacity 
and is expected to strengthen domestic availability of this critical raw material, reduce 
import dependence and support the growth of India's downstream polyester industry. 
Strategically located near New Mangalore Port, the plant is also supported by dedicated 
logistics infrastructure for efficient movement of raw materials and finished products. 
GAIL will market the product under the brand name 'G -Tex', l everaging its established 
marketing network and customer reach.
