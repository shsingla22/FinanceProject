Sub: Intimation of Drop/Closure of GST Proceedings against the Company 
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
This is in reference to our earlier intimation dated 24.06.2025 regarding the Show Cause Notice 
issued to the Company by the GST Department, Gujarat, alleging discrepancies in GST 
amounting to ₹ 5,06,65,760 for the tax period 2018-19 to tax period 2023-24 and demanding 
penalty and interest aggregating to ₹ 3,33,24,242 under Section 74(5) of the CGST/SGST Act. 
 
We wish to inform that the Company had duly submitted its detailed reply, along with requisite 
clarifications and supporting documents, to the Department within the prescribed timeline. 
 
Upon examination of the submissions made by the Company, the GST Department, vide its 
Order Ref No. ZD241225087382F dated 18.12.2025, has dropped the proceedings initiated 
against the Company.  
 
Accordingly, the matter stands fully concluded, and no demand, penalty, or interest is payable 
by the Company in this regard.
