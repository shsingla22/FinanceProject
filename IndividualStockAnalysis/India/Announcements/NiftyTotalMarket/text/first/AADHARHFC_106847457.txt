Commenting on Q 2 & H1  FY27 performance, Mr. Rishi Anand, MD & CEO of Aadhar Housing Finance Ltd said:  
 
"During the first half of FY27, Aadhar Housing Finance continued to build on its growth momentum, with a 
strong focus on disciplined expansion, prudent underwriting and portfolio quality . Disbursement during the 
quarter grew by a strong 22%, Assets Under Management (AUM) stood at Rs 32,643  crore as of September 
30th, 2026, registering a year -on-year growth of 18.5%. Profit after Tax for the quarter  (Adjusted for ESOP - 
Refer Note 2)  increased by  20% YoY  and for H1 FY27  grew by 22 %. 
 
This quarter marked an important milestone , with India R atings  and ICRA  upgrad ing our long -term credit 
ratings to AA+ (Stable) . All three rating agencies now rat e the company at AA+ (Stable)  on standalone basis . 
This reinforces our strong financial profile, capitalisation and enables diversified funding access.  
 
During the quarter, we expanded our footprint to Jammu , an important territory for us by adding one branch 
there, taking our network to 632 branches across 23 states and over 555 districts as of 30th September 2026.  
This helps  strengthening our reach in underserved markets.  
 
While the evolving interest -rate environment and heightened competition warrant continued vigilance, the

   About:  
Aadhar Housing Finance Ltd. (Aadhar), established in 2010, is one of India's leading low -income housing finance 
companies dedicated on providing affordable housing solutions to EWS (Economically Weaker Section) and LIG  
(Low Income Group) particularly in semi -urban and rural areas across India. With an average loan size of Rs 
11.3 lakhs  and AUM of  Rs 32,643 , Aadhar operates with  a network of  632 branches, across 23 states and union 
territories. It offers an array of mortgage -related products, including loans for buying and constructing 
residential properties, home improvement and extension, and micro loan against property. Aadhar leverages 
advanced technology and da ta analytics for efficient underwriting, collections, and asset quality monitoring. 
The company secures financing from diverse sources, including term loans, NHB financing, and NCDs. Aadhar's 
extensive network and focus on EWS/LIG segments makes it a signi ficant player in the low -income housing 
finance sector in India.
