Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, this is to 
inform you that:  
 
The United States Food and Drug Administration (US FDA) inspected Unit -I, a Formulation manufacturing 
facility, of Eugia Pharma Specialities Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, situated at Kolthur Village, 
Shameerpet Mandal, Ranga Reddy, 500078, Telangana , from February  16 to February  27, 202 6.  
 
The  inspection concluded with 4 observations . We will respond to the US FDA within the stipulated timelines.  
 
The Company is committed to maintaining the highest quality manufacturing standards at all of its facilities 
across the globe . We will keep the stock exchanges informed if there is any further information relating to the 
above in the future.
