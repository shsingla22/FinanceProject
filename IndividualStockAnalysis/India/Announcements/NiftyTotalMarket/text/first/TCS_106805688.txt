Best Buy’s Global Capability Center in India to transition to TCS  
The strategic deal  will bring Best Buy™ India capabilities into TCS, creating an AI -centered technology 
and innovation hub grounded in deep retail expertise  
 
MINNEAPOLIS | MUMBAI,  October  1, 2026 : Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS)  and 
Best Buy (NYSE: BBY) today announced an agreement to transition Best Buy’s Global Capability Center 
(GCC) in India to TCS. The agreement will combine the GCC’s deep retail and enterprise knowledge with TCS’s technology, engineering, AI and global delivery capabilities  to accelerate innovation , scale AI -
powered solutions and deliver greater business value . 
 As part of the agreement, the Best Buy ™ India entity will trans ition  to TCS , ensuring business continuity ; 
while embarking on its transformation into an AI -native Capability Center (AICC) under a multi -year 
agreement.  Leveraging TCS’ Global Value & Innovation Centres framework, teams will redesign workflows, 
develop AI -powered experiences and solutions, and scale capabilities across the business , transitioning  
into an AI Capability  Center over time.  
 Krishnan Ramanujam, President, Consumer Business Group, TCS,  said,  “Best Buy has built a strong 
technology organization with deep knowledge of its customers and business. By bringing that talent 
together with TCS’ retail expertise, AI, engineering and global execution capabilities, we will create an AI -
native capability engine that aligns Best Buy’s priorities. This partnership will also create a repeatable value platform that can potentially benefit the broader retail industry. ” 
 Ahrey Smith, Vice President of Technology Strategy and Transformation, Best Buy , said “Our India team 
has played an important role in advancing Best Buy ’s priorities, and we wanted a partner that understood 
both the business context and the value of this team . TCS brings that understanding, along with the 
technology and AI capabilities to build on the team’s strengths and meaningful opportunities for employees to continue learning and growing in their careers.”  
 TCS will welcome Best Buy India employees into its global organization, preserving the deep business and technology knowledge to advance Best Buy priorities while opening access to TCS’ global learning and innovation ecosystem, AI skills and global opportunities. The partnership also demonstrates TCS’ end -to-
end GCC capabilities, from building and scaling capability centers to transforming them into AI -led engines 
of innovation and enterprise value.
