Subject: Update on Acquisition of Strategic Equity Stake in Zettaone Technologies India Private 
Limited  
 
This is in continuation of our letter dated October 1, 2026, wherein we had informed that the acquisition 
of a 40% strategic equity stake in Zettaone Technologies India Private Limited ("Zettaone") would be 
undertaken through our wholly owned subsidiary, J ubilant Advanced Electronics Limited ("JAEL"), 
instead of directly by the Company.  
 The disclosures required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, read with the applicable SEBI Circulars, were duly submitted to the 
Stock Exchanges vide the aforesaid letter.  
 In this regard, we wish to inform you that JAEL has executed the Share  Subscription and Share  Purchase 
Agreement ("S SPA") for the acquisition of the aforesaid strategic equity stake in Zettaone, with an option 
to increase its shareholding to 51% during the next year, subject to the terms and conditions of the SPA.  
 
The key terms and details of the transaction have already been disclosed to the Stock Exchanges through 
our letter dated October 1, 2026.  
 
Through this acquisition, the Company, via JAEL, has entered the rapidly growing Electronics Design 
and Manufacturing Services (EDMS) sector , which will open  a large opportunity for the Company. The 
investment is expected to capitalize on this and create a strong platform to serve customers across 
semiconductors , aerospace & defense, automotive, and other electronics segments . With the option of 
taking  the shareholding to 51% in FY28, this acquisition becomes more critical for the overall growth 
journey.    
 
In continuation to our previous communication, this acquisition also enables the Company to leverage its 
proven manufacturing excellence and customer -centric capabilities in the CDMO space alongside the 
newly acquired EDMS capabilities, and  offer an integrated value proposition to its customers , which will 
accelerate the growth of the Company’s semiconductor and electronics chemicals vertical.
