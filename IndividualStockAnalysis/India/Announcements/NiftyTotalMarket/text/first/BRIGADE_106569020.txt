PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Group Launches Brigade Lumina in Bengaluru with a Revenue Potential  
of over ₹700 Crores  
 
Net-zero  Carbon  development spread across 4.11 acres in the rapidly developing corridor  
of Northwest Bengaluru  
 
Bengaluru, March 2 6, 2026:  Brigade Group has announced the launch of its latest residential offering,  Brigade 
Lumina, a net -zero  carbon  development spread across 4.11 acres in the rapidly developing corridor of 
Northwest Bengaluru on  Tumkur Main Road. The joint development project, which has an estimated revenue 
potential of over ₹700 crores,  offers spacious two and three -bedroom units ranging from around 1100 to                 
1800 square feet. Each home is designed with large decks that offer panoramic views and ample natural light. 
As a net zero carbon  development, Brigade Lumina stands as a testament to Brigade Group's vision of leading 
the industry toward a more sustainable and environmentally conscious future.  
 
Commenting on the launch, Pavitra Shankar, Managing Director, Brigade Enterprises Limited said, 
“Northwest  Bengaluru  is one of the key  focus area s for us, and the launch of Brigade Lumina underscores our 
commitment to delivering world -class construction that responds to the evolving expectations of today’s 
homebuyer while being inherently future -ready.  There i s an undersupply of premium developments in this 
corridor, and the metro connectivity of the project is one of its biggest selling points.  With  the in tegration of 
renewable energy,  careful selection of materials,  advanced water management and zero -waste systems, 
Brigade Lumina is designed  as a Net Zero Carbon project  to remain climate -resilient over the long term.”  
 
Brigade Lumina is strategically located on Tumkur Main Road,  a mere  five sminutes ’ walk  from 
the Manjunathanagara  Metro Station on Namma Metro’s operational Green Line, offering residents 
unparalleled connectivity to major office hubs, reputed schools, and healthcare facilities. The project is located 
in close proximity to popular retail landmarks and major shopping malls, m aking it an ideal choice for families 
seeking a seamless urban lifestyle.   Amenities include a grand 15,000+ sq. ft. clubhouse and a suite of premium 
offerings designed for all age groups. Sports enthusiasts c an take advantage of the futsal court, basketball 
practice court, and pickleball court, while the expansive swimming pool and jacuzzi with island seating offer 
a retreat for relaxation. The community also features dedicated zones for children, including a tot-lot and play 
area, as well as a pet park and outdoor gym.  
 
About Brigade Group:  Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers with 
close to four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and winning their 
customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark bu ildings and transformed skylines across India in 
the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, and GIFT City with 
developments across Residential, Office, Retail, Hospitality and Education Sectors.
