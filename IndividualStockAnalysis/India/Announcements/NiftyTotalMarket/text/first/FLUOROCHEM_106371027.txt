Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
We would like to inform that on 10th September , 2025, an incident occurred at the Company’s Ranjitnagar 
Plant, where a minor rupture at one of the bellows  in the gas pipeline led to a temporary gas leak. The 
situation was instantly brought under control through the plant’s safety mechanisms. Sprinklers  were 
activated, and the DSC control system safely halted plant operations and all personnel were immediately evacuated from the affected  area.  
 Workers with potential gas exposures were administered immediate first -aid at our on -site Occupational 
Health Centre (OHC) and few of them were shifted to nearby hospitals for further treatment. We regret to 
report that one casualty and a few injuries have been recorded. While the post -mortem report is awaited, 
prima facie it appears to be duri ng the rush of evacuation process. We deeply regret this incident and the 
loss of life. Our thoughts and condolences are with the bereaved family, and we are extending our full support during this difficult time.  
 
The Company is conducting a detailed inves tigation and continues to prioritize the highest standards of 
safety across all its operations.
