Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that the Board in its meeting 
held today (i.e. 20th August 2026), has inter -alia, considered and approved the 
internal re -organisation of Departments, by forming a Centralized Credit 
Department, bifurcating Operations Department into Banking Operations and 
Infrastructure Management Group.  Further, approved the following 
appointments of Senior Management Personnel  in tune with the re -organ isation 
of mentioned Departments:  
 
1. Shri V S R A Kumar Ravutu, General Manager has been appointed as                 
Chief Credit Officer  to head the Centralized Credit Department with effect 
from 01.09.2026. Shri V S R A Kumar Ravutu in his previous assignment was 
heading Credit Monitoring and Recovery Department in the Bank . 
 
2. Consequent to bifurcation of Operations Department into Banking 
Operations and Infrastructure Management Group, Shri Vijayakumar P V, 
General Manager  - Head of  Operations Department has been redesignated as  
Head of Infrastructure Management Group with effect from 01.09.2026.
