LEMON TREE HOTELS ENTERS ASSAM WITH THE OPENING OF 
KEYS SELECT BY LEMON TREE HOTELS, CHIRANG  
 
Strengthens Northeast India footprint with first property in the State 
 
 
New Delhi, 15th May 2026: Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, one of India’s leading hospitality 
companies, announced the opening of Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels, Chirang . 
This marks  the group's first hotel in Assam and its latest step in building a sustained 
hospitality presence across Northeast India.   
 
The hotel is managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited . 
 
Situated on NH -31C, the property is strategically positioned along the Chirang –Bongaigaon 
corridor, a region steadily emerging as an important commercial and transit hub. Driven 
by industrial activity, including the Indian Oil Refinery, and its role as a g ateway for cross -
border trade with Bhutan, the corridor reflects the growing economic and infrastructure 
momentum shaping the region.  
 
The property also serves as a convenient base for travellers exploring western Assam’s 
natural landscapes and nearby attractions such as Manas National Park and the Indo -
Bhutan border. The hotel is located approximately 5 km from New Bongaigaon Railway 
Station and around 188 km from Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport.  
 
The hotel features 40 well -appointed rooms and suites, thoughtfully designed to offer a 
comfortable and contemporary stay for business, transit, and leisure  travellers.  Guests 
can enjoy a range of facilities including Keys Café, an all -day dining restaurant, Unlock Bar  
along with in -room dining services. The property also offers banquet and meeting spaces, 
a fitness centre, a spa and modern amenities designed to cater to corporate events, social 
gatherings, and extended stays.  
 
Commenting on the new opening,  Mr. Neelendra Singh , Managing Director , Lemon 
Tree Hotels Ltd ., said,  "We are delighted to make our debut in Assam with Keys Select 
by Lemon Tree Hotels, Chirang. With our KEYS brand, we introduce to the region, a 
hospitality experience that our guests throughout India have come to trust. Across every 
city where Keys Select i s present, we have delivered hospitality that is elegant, quality -
driven, and accessible without compromise. This opening reflects our continued 
commitment to bring the same standard of hospitality to Assam, and we look forward to 
deepening our presence ac ross the region ." 
 
With contemporary accommodation, versatile dining options, and modern event facilities, 
Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels, Chirang is well positioned to cater to the region’s 
growing business and transit -driven travel demand.  
 
 

   
 
 
 
 
 
 
The opening further expands Lemon Tree Hotels’ footprint across Tier -II and emerging 
markets, reinforcing the group’s continued focus on delivering branded hospitality 
experiences across India’s evolving travel landscape.
