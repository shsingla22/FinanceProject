Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited boosts it's leadership with new Chief 
Revenue Officer 
Mumbai, 17th October 2024 :  Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABDL) further augmented 
it's leadership team with the appointment of Mr. Manoj Kumar Rai as the new Chief Revenue 
Officer (CRO). Manoj will be responsible for company's revenue strategies, focusing on 
enhancing existing revenue streams and exploring new business opportunities across ABDL’s 
diverse portfolio.  
With a career spanning over 27 years of experience across diverse industries, including 
engineering, music-entertainment, consumer goods, and alcobev. Prior to joining ABDL, 
Manoj spent nearly 19 years at Pernod Ricard India, where he was instrumental in driving 
revenue growth and profitability, managing multiple business zones across India, as well as 
handling international markets like Nepal and Sri Lanka.  
Manoj holds a Post-Graduation Diploma in Business Management (PGDM) from the Indian 
Institute of Management (IIM), Lucknow, and a Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) from the 
Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi. His extensive background includes key roles at Marico 
Industries Limited, Saregama India Limited, and Sulzer Thermetec – PBG, where he enhanced 
his skills in sales strategy, business development, and team leadership.  
Alok Gupta, Managing Director, Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited,  welcomed Manoj’s 
appointment stating, "We are thrilled to welcome Manoj Kumar Rai to our team. His in-depth 
experience in driving revenue and profitability across competitive markets aligns perfectly 
with our goal of focusing on profitable growth at ABDL. Manoj's strategic vision and leadership 
will be invaluable as we continue to innovate and expand our presence in the industry. We 
look forward to his contributions in providing a further impetus to our business."  
Manoj Kumar Rai, Chief Revenue Officer, Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited  expressed, “I 
am honored to join Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited, a company renowned for its 
commitment to quality and innovation in the alcobev industry. I am eager to collaborate with 
the talented team and explore new avenues for growth and deliver outstanding results. We 
will work towards strengthening our brand and maximizing our impact in the market."  
In his new role, Manoj will be responsible for developing new revenue models and leveraging 
his comprehensive industry knowledge to expand the company’s revenue streams and forge 

strategic alliances. His commitment to building strong partnerships and delivering exceptional 
value to our customers and stakeholders will be a key to success.  
About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited ( www.abdindia.com ) 
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABDL) is the 3rd largest Spirits company in India, in terms of 
annual sales volumes between FY14 and FY24. ABDL has a presence in five main flavours, i.e., whisky, 
brandy, rum, vodka, and gin, with ‘millionaire’ brands like Officer’s Choice Whisky, Officer’s Choice Blue 
Whisky, Sterling Reserve Premium Whiskies and ICONiQ White Whisky. Currently, its manufacturing 
network comprises 33 units, of which 9 are owned bottling units, 1 owned distillery, and 23 non-owned 
manufacturing units.
