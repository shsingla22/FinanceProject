The operating environment continued to be characterised by heightened uncertainty amidst renewed 
geopolitical tensions and trade disruptions. Crude oil and crude -linked input prices remained elevated, while 
supply chain disruptions and higher freight costs  persisted. Global inflationary pressures intensified, driven by 
higher energy and food prices, alongside elevated sovereign bond yields and tighter financial conditions. In India, 
headline inflation moved up, driven by a broad -based increase  across food, fuel and core components.

 3 Despite these pressures, the Indian economy continued to demonstrate strength, underpinned by resilient 
domestic demand across rural and urban markets. However, deficient and uneven monsoons and lower reservoir

Geopolitical developments, climatic conditions, inflationary headwinds and consumer sentiment would remain

The macroeconomic fundamentals of the Indian economy remain resilient, supported by proactive and agile 
policy actions by the Government of India . Sustained public capex, improving trends in private capex, strong 
credit growth, healthy bank and corporate balance sheets, and measures to curb foreign exchange volatility and 
strengthen investment activity augur well for sustained growth of the Indian economy.
