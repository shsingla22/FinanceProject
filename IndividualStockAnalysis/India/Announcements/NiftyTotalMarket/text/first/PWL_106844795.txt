Physics wallah Posts Record Q2; profit doubles to Rs 142 Cr YoY 
Noida | November 4, 2026:  PhysicsWallah Limited, India's leading tech- enabled education company, 
announced its financial results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026, reporting its 
highest -ever quarterly revenue from operations of ₹1,242.26 Cr, up 18.2% year- on-year. EBITDA rose to 
₹348 Cr, a margin of 28.0% against 21.7% a year ago, and Adjusted EBITDA stood at ₹399 Cr. Profit after tax doubled to ₹142 Cr.  
For H1 FY27, revenue from operations grew 20.7% to ₹2,292.60 Cr, and the company reported a profit 
after tax of ₹55 Cr against a loss of ₹57 Cr in H1 FY26.  
Alakh Pandey, Founder and CEO of PhysicsWallah Limited, said, "This quarter shows the strength of our 
model. We delivered record revenue and a sharp improvement in profitability, while our learner base kept 
widening across K -12, vernacular and new categories. Our focus remains on scaling affordable, tech -led 
education with disciplined capital allocation."  
Demand Rebounds as NEET Cycle Normalises  
In Q2, NEET enrolments grew over 30%, as demand delayed by the NEET UG postponement came through. 
Because fees are recognised over the course term, online revenue for H1 grew 29%, ahead of H1 online 
collections growth of 17%. 
Profitability Improves as Demand Returns  
Profitability improved as the recovery worked through the business. In Q2, Pre -IndAS EBITDA rose 45% to 
₹233 Cr, a margin of 18.8% against 15.3% a year ago. For H1, Pre -IndAS EBITDA rose 2.6x to ₹190 Cr, a 
margin of 8.3% against 3.8%, while EBITDA stood at  ₹401 Cr and Adjusted EBITDA at ₹536 Cr. Employee 
costs fell to 42.3% of H1 revenue from 44.6%, and other expenses to 23.4% from 24.6%.  
Profit after tax was ₹142 Cr in Q2, up from ₹70 Cr a year ago, after a ₹20 Cr goodwill impairment and a 
₹10 Cr fair- value gain. This takes H1 profit after tax to ₹55 Cr, against a loss of ₹57 Cr in H1 FY26. Treasury 
stood at ₹5,268 Cr as of September 30, 2 026, including IPO proceeds.

Online Leads Growth; Offline Loss Narrows in H1  
Online revenue grew 26% to ₹694 Cr in Q2, with a Pre -IndAS EBITDA margin of 34.4%. Offline revenue 
grew 9% to ₹541 Cr, and its H1 Pre-IndAS EBITDA loss narrowed to ₹108 Cr from ₹120 Cr. 
 
Learner Engagement Grows as Online K- 12 and Early Learning Scale  
Growth is broadening beyond JEE –NEET. In H1, categories beyond JEE –NEET made up 73.8% of online 
enrolments, up from 64.8%. Boards and CUET enrolments rose 130% to 4.55 lakh, Foundation enrolments 
rose 15% to 5.70 lakh, vernacular enrolments rose 77% to 1.23 lakh across 10 languages, and Skills, 
including Earners, grew 10.3x to 1.69 lakh. H1 online K -12 Revenue YoY growth 75%, with enrollment 11.1 
Lacs up by 47%.  
Total paid users rose 7% to 3.86 Mn, Daily Active Users reached 4.1 Mn+, and Infinity and Infinity Pro 
collections rose 32% in H1.  
South India Emerges as the Next Growth Leg  
South India, with about 15 Mn students in its addressable market across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil 
Nadu, Karnataka and Kerala, contributed 9% of H1 revenue. PW is taking its online -first playbook there, 
with 70+ regional- language faculty, free learning through about 20 lakh YouTube subscribers on 24+ 
channels, and paid regional batches. In H1, learners on the PW app from the region rose 32% to 4.1 lakh and paid learners rose 14% to 1.17 lakh, and PW now has 63 centres across the south. Results are bui lding 
the brand, with 79 KCET ranks under 5,000 against 53 last year.

About PhysicsWallah (PW)  
PhysicsWallah (PW), an education platform, was founded in 2020 by Alakh Pandey and Prateek 
Maheshwari. Headquartered in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, PW aims to democratise education through online, offline and hybrid platforms. Initially launched as a YouTube channel in 2016, PW now offers education to students through its native app, tech -enabled offline and hybrid centres, and YouTube 
channels. PW's offerings span various educational segments, including test preparation, a skilling vertical, higher education and  education abroad, with programmes available in multiple vernacular 
languages. PhysicsWallah Limited was listed on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and BSE Limited on November 18, 2025. (Scrip codes: NSE – PWL; BSE – 544609.)
