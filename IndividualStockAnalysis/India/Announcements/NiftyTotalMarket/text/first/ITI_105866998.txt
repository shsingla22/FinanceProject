Sub: Intimation regarding Award passed in the Arbitration Case No. 56/2017 
Ref: Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 
With reference to the captioned subject, this is to inform that HCL Infosystems Ltd (HCL) 
initiated Arbitration Proceedings against ITI Limited for non-payment of invoices for 
services in GSM project. HCL has invoked the arbitration clause in 2017 and filed its claim 
against ITI of Rs. 103.62 Cr against which ITI has filed its counter claim of Rs. 110.82 Cr for 
breach of contract and loss of short closure of BSNL/MTNL PO. In this regard an award 
was passed in favour of HCL by the Arbitral Tribunal. 
The Arbitral Tribunal accepted some of the claims of HCL and rejected claims of ITI 
Limited and passed an award directing ITI to pay Rs. 73 Cr. with an interest of 11 % per 
annum from the date of cause of action till the date of payment. 
The Company is reviewing the matter on future course of action to protect the interest of 
the Company.
