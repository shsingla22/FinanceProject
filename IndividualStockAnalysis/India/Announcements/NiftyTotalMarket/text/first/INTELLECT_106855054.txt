Step 1: Access to the NSDL e- voting system

shareholders holding securities in demat mode are allowed to vote through their demat account maintained with 
Depositories and Depository Pa rticipants. Shareholders are advised to update their mobile number and email Id in 
their demat accounts in order to access the e -voting facility.
