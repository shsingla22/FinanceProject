Delhivery Wins Golden Peacock Award for 
Excellence in Corporate Governance 2026  
A national recognition of Delhivery’s governance standards  
GURUGRAM, INDIA, October 12, 2026:  Delhivery has been recognized as the winner of the 
Golden Peacock Award for Excellence in Corporate Governance 2026 (National)  in the 
Logistics sector, awarded by the Institute of Directors (IOD), India.  
The Company has successfully met the rigorous standards and evaluation benchmarks of the 
Golden Peacock Excellence Model, reflecting a strong commitment to sustainability -driven 
excellence. The award has been adjudged by a distinguished jury under the Chai rmanship of 
Hon’ble Justice Uday U. Lalit, Former Chief Justice of India.  
Commenting on the recognition, Vivek Pabari, Chief Financial Officer, Delhivery , said, “The 
Golden Peacock Award for Excellence in Corporate Governance validates our emphasis on 

governance and inspires us to keep getting better every day. We are grateful to the esteemed 
jury and the Institute of Directors for this recognition.”  
Madhulika Rawat, Company Secretary and Compliance Officer, Delhivery , said, "Effective 
governance isn’t just a policy; it’s the foundation of every decision we make. Receiving this 
recognition from the Institute of Directors validates our continuous effort to set higher standards 
across our organization."  
The Golden Peacock Awards were instituted by the Institute of Directors in 1991 and recognize 
organisations across a range of corporate excellence categories. The 2026 awards for Corporate 
Governance will be presented during the Annual London Global Convention on Corporate 
Governance & Sustainability  in November 2026.
