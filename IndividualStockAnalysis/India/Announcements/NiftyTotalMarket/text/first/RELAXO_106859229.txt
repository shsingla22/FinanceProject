Classification: Internal2Commenting  on the results  and performance,  Mr. Ramesh  Kumar  Dua,  Chairman  and Managing  
Director  said: 
“We are pleased  to report  a steady  performance  in Q2 and H1 FY27, with growth  in Revenue  
while  broadly  maintaining  EBITDA  and Profit  After Tax levels . This performance  was 
supported  by our diversified  brand  portfolio,  extensive  distribution  network  and disciplined  
execution  amid  continued  inflationary  pressures  and softness  in mass -market  demand .
Maintaining  profitability  was challenging  during  the period,  as raw material  price  volatility  
arising  from the continuing  conflict  in West  Asia,  together  with higher  labour  costs,  exerted  
pressure  on margins . We remained  focused  on cost management  while  making  selective  
investments  in brand  building,  distribution  capabilities  and retail  expansion  to strengthen  the 
business  over the long term.
Expanding  our EBO  store  network  and providing  consumers  with a contemporary  shopping  
environment  remain  key priorities . We added  16 outlets  during  the quarter,  taking  the network  
to 445 stores,  and remain  on track  to reach  500 EBOs  by the end of FY27. We have  also 
refreshed  our EBO  store  format  in line with changing  consumer  expectations . The redesigned  
stores  feature  improved  merchandising,  greater  product  visibility  and dedicated  brand  zones,  
facilitating  easier  navigation  and providing  an enhanced  platform  to showcase  our premium  
offerings .
As we enter  the second  half of FY27, our focus  remains  on growing  revenue,  improving  
product  mix, driving  operating  efficiencies  and expanding  our consumer  reach . Our 
established  brands,  expanding  retail  network  and product  innovation  will support  us as we 
prudently  navigate  the evolving  external  environment  and continue  to strengthen  the business  
for the long term.”
