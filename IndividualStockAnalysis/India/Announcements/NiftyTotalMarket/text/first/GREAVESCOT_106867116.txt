Ampere achieves 6 % E2W market share, consolidating its Top 5 position  
 
• Records 7,956  E2W registrations in November 2026 (1 -18 Nov), securing a 6. 0% market share  
• Volumes up 70% over MTD October, ahead of overall EV two -wheeler market growth of 20% 
• Market share increased by 170 bps MoM, from 4.3% in MTD October to 6.0% in MTD 
November.  
• Present in ~900 RTO markets, a top 3 player in ~300 of them  
 
New Delhi/  Bengaluru, 20 November 2026:  Ampere, the electric two -wheeler brand of Greaves 
Electric Mobility Limited (GEML), has reached 6% market share in India’s electric two -wheeler market, 
building on its entry into the Top 5 earlier this week and a sustained run of volume and share gains.  
 
As per VAHAN data for November 1 –18, 2026, GEML recorded 7,956 E2W registrations, securing a 
6.0% market share and consolidating its No. 5 position. Volumes grew 70% over the same period in 
October, significantly ahead of the overall E2W market growth of 2 0%. This helped Ampere’s market 
share to grow from  4.3% on October 1 –18 to 6.0% on November 1 –18. This builds on the 5.7% share 
recorded till November 15, when Ampere reached the Top 5, reflecting continued growth momentum.  
 
The brand’s growth this financial year has been equally encouraging. Till October, GEML’s VAHAN 
registrations were up 92% year -on-year. With 72,553 E2W registrations in FY27 up to November 18, 
GEML has already well surpassed the 61,842 registrations recorded during the whole of FY26.  
 
Commenting on the milestone, Mr. Vikas Singh, Managing Director, Greaves Electric Mobility , said, 
“India’s electric future will be won by those who deliver real value to everyday consumers. Achieving 
6.0% market share is a powerful endorsement of Ampere’s promise: reliability, affordability and 
technology which matters. With a presence across nearly 900 RTO markets, and a “top three” position 
in around 300 of them, Ampere is no longer a big -city story.  It is winning the trust of Bharat. Through 
‘Bharat Ki Smart Ride’, we will deepen our consumer connect, expand our reach and scale with 
discipline, building an electric mobility business that is sustainable and profitable.”  
 
Ampere’s multiple award -winning EV portfolio, including the Nexus EX+, Magnus GMax, Magnus Neo, 
and Reo Vyb, is designed around the diverse everyday mobility needs of Indian consumers. The 
portfolio reflects the Ampere’s ‘Bharat Ki Smart Ride’ proposition of combining practical innovation, 
reliability and value for India’s evolving mobility needs.  
 
Ampere has also demonstrated product capability in demanding real -world conditions. The Magnus 
GMax with Intellipack  recently completed the Ultimate Endurance Ride, covering over 4,000 km in just 
15 days, from Dong Valley in Arunachal Pradesh to Guhar Moti in Gujarat, traversing mountains, plains 
and desert. The Nexus has previously conquered the 70 -hairpin bends of Kol li Hills and reached Shipki 
La Pass at 13,200 ft, demonstrating performance and capability across varied Indian terrain.  
 
Ampere continues to strengthen its product portfolio, retail reach, financing ecosystem and customer 
experience as it builds towards making electric mobility a more accessible and preferred choice for 
consumers across India.

About Greaves Electric Mobility Limited:  
Greaves Electric Mobility Limited (GEML), the electric mobility business of Greaves Cotton Limited 
(GCL), is one of the first companies in India to focus on the evolving market of electric vehicles (“ EV”) 
and has been among the frontrunners at driving EV adoption in the country. With over 17 years  of 
experience designing and manufacturing electric vehicles, GEML serves both the electric 2 -wheeler 
and 3 -wheeler segments. GEML along with its subsidiaries, operates three manufacturing plants 
located in Hyderabad, Noida, and Ranipet. The company suppor ts customers through 600+ Ampere 
touchpoints across India, enabling wider access, dependable service and an enhanced ownership 
experience. GEML holds 8 India Book of Records* and 1 Asia Book of Records* titles, reflecting its 
focus on technology and design led innovative & durable mobility solutions . Subsidiaries MLR Auto 
Limited and Bestway Agencies Private Limited help extend GEML’s market presence. Committed to 
affordability and sustainability, GEML provides clean mobility solutions contributing to India’s shift 
toward electric transportation.
