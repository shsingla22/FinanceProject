Schaeffler India Honored  with CII National Award  for 
Water Management  
 
  
• Under Excellence in Water Management for its Jal Samruddhi CSR Initiative . 
Pune , India | 4 Dece mber  2025 – Schaeffler India, a leading motion technology company,  
has been recognised at the CII National Award for Excellence in Water Management  
2024 –25 for its flagship CSR initiative, Jal Samruddhi, under the category “Beyond the 
Fence.” The award acknowledges high -impact community -centric efforts that strengthen 
water security, enhance livelihoods, and enable socio -economic development at the 
watershed level.  
Presented annually by the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII), the National Award for 
Excellence in Water Management is one of India’s most respected platforms recognising 
organisations for exemplary work in water stewardship and sustainable community 
development.  
Schaeffler India’s Jal Samruddhi Project, implemented in partnership with the BAIF 
Development Research Foundation , has transformed water -scarce regions of Wakalwadi 
and Gosasi villages in Rajgurunagar block, Pune district. Spanning July 2021 to December 
2023, the project adopted an integrated watershed management approach to address 
acute water scarcity, low agricultu ral productivity, and livelihood instability.  
Key highlights of the initiative include:  
• 143,000 cubic meters  of water conservation potential created through water 
absorption trenches, recharge ponds, gabions, and boulder structures  
• 1,268 acres  of land treated to reduce soil erosion and improve moisture retention  
• 40% improvement  in cropping intensity and significant gains in agricultural yield  
• 84% of households  reporting enhanced water access  
• Household income increase  from ₹60,312 to ₹1,06,938 on average  
• 70% reduction  in tanker -water dependency  
Through soil and water conservation, agricultural development, micro -irrigation support, 
and the promotion of sustainable farming practices, Jal Samruddhi has enabled more than 
400 rural families to transition toward water security, resilience, and improve d quality of 
life. 
Ms. Shibi Mathew, Head of HR and CSR, Schaeffler India , stated , “We are honoured to 
receive the CII National Award for Excellence in Water Management. At Schaeffler India, 
we believe that enabling water security is fundamental to building resilient communities. 
 
2 The Jal Samruddhi initiative showcases our commitment to driving meaningful change 
beyond our operations —through science -led watershed development, community 
participation, and a long -term focus on sustainable livelihoods. This recognition further 
strength ens our resolve to expand such impact -driven initiatives across the country.”   
A Model for Scalable, Sustainable Impact  
The impact of Jal Samruddhi has been validated through an independent assessment by 
Sattva Consulting, reinforcing the initiative’s success in delivering measurable 
improvements in water availability, soil health, agriculture, and household income. The 
project aligns with several UN Sustainable Development Goals while demonstrating a best -
practice model that can be replicated across other water -stressed regions.  
Schaeffler India is already extending this approach to its aspirational CSR geographies in 
Gujarat and Tamil Nadu , strengthening its long -term commitment to community 
development and environmental sustainability.
