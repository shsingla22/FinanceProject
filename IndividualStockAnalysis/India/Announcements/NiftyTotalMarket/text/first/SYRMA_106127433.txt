   Good Results aided by Healthy Margins  
Performance for the Quarter end ed 31 December 2024  
       The company for the Quarter  ended 3 1st December,  2024, reported a consolidated total revenue of ₹ 8,915  mn, up by 
24% YoY, as compared to ₹ 7,188  mn for the corresponding period ended 3 1st December,  2023, primarily driven by  
Auto and Industrials  segments.  
The Consolidated EBITDA was up by 96% YoY to ₹ 1,013 mn as against ₹ 516 mn for the corresponding period of 
the previous year. Profit after Tax was ₹ 530 mn, up by 161% YoY as compared to ₹ 203 mn last year.

Performance for the Nine Months  ended 31 December 2024  
The company for the Nine Months  ended 3 1st December,  2024, reported a consolidated total revenue of ₹ 28,973  
mn, up by 40% YoY, as compared to ₹ 20,631  mn for the corresponding period ended 3 1st December,  2023, primarily 
driven by  Auto and Industrials  segments.  
The Consolidated EBITDA was up by 43% YoY  to ₹ 2,430 mn as against ₹ 1,703 mn for the corresponding period of 
the previous year. Profit after Tax  was ₹ 1,130 mn, up by 43% YoY as compared to ₹ 791 mn last year.
