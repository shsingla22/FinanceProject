About Granules India Ltd. (BSE: 532482, NSE: GRANULES)  
Granules India Limited, incorporated in 1991 is a vertically integrated fast growing Indian 
pharmaceutical company headquartered at Hyderabad with best -in-class facilities and 
commitment to operational excellence, quality, and customer service. We are amon g the few 
pharmaceutical companies in the world to be present in the manufacturing of entire value chain – 
from Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), Pharmaceutical Formulation Intermediates (PFIs) , 
Finished Dosages (FDs)  and Peptide s CDMO . Our product s are being distributed to over 300+ 
customers in regulated and semi -regulated markets with a global presence extending to over 80+ 
countries with offices across India, US , and UK . The Company has 11 manufacturing facilities out 
of which 8 are in India , 2 in the USA and 1 in Switzerland  and has regulatory approvals from US 
FDA, EDQM, EU GMP, COFEPRIS, WHO GMP, TGA, K FDA, DEA, MCC , and HALAL.
