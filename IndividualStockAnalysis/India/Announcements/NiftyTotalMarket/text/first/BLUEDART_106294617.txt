PRESS RELEASE  
 
Blue Dart Launches Rakhi Express Campaign , Offering Up to 50% Off for Festive Shipments  
 
The campaign celebrates the spirit of Raksha Bandhan by offering faster and more affordable shipping 
solutions from 8th July to 9th August 2025  
 
Mumbai, July 9, 2025:  Blue Dart Express Limited, South Asia's premier express air and 
integrated transportation & distribution company, has announced the launch of its ‘ Rakhi 
Express Campaign’ , a specially curated festive initiative aimed at making Raksha Bandhan more 
memorable and accessible for customers across India and worldwide. Running from 8th July to 
9th August 2025, the campaign features exclusive discounts on domestic and internationa l 
shipments, allowing families to easily and affordably send rakhis, gifts, and warm wishes across 
distances.  
As part of this limited -time offer, domestic customers can avail up to 40% off on shipments 
weighing between 0.5 kg and 2.5 kg, making festive gifting more economical. For international 
deliveries, customers can save  up to 50% off on freight charges for packages ranging from 0.5 
kg to 2.5 kg, as well as 5 kg, 10 kg, 15 kg, and 20 kg, applicable across all lanes and zones. The 
offer applies to Domestic Priority (DP) shipments and International Duts product types and is 
valid across all regions and destinatio ns, ensuring seamless and cost -effective deliveries both 
within the country and globally . 
Speaking on the launch, Dipanjan Banerjee, Chief Commercial Officer at Blue Dart , said,  “At 
Blue Dart, we understand the emotional significance of Raksha Bandhan. Through the Rakhi 
Express Campaign , we aim to make celebrations easier by providing an efficient and cost -
effective way to connect loved ones across the country and the world. With these special 
discounts, our customers can ship gifts and rakhis with the same reliability and speed that Blu e 
Dart is known for, while embracing the festive spirit.”  
Through such initiatives and  campaign s, Blue Dart continues to reinforce its commitment to 
connecting loved ones, bridging distances, and honouring enduring bonds with timely, reliable, 
and affordable logistics solutions.  
This limited time offer is available across all Blue Dart DHL stores and Franchisee Collection 
Centres (FCCs).   
For further details on the campaign, customers are encouraged to visit their nearest Blue Dart 
DHL store. Customers can also contact Blue Dart's Customer Care at 1860 233 1234 or email 
enquires at:  customerservice@bluedart.com.  
For more information or to book shipments, visit  www.bluedart.com.
