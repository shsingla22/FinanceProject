Ampere launches new Nexus EX+, strengthening its award -winning 
Nexus EV with IoT -enabled Intellipack  
New Nexus EX+ upgraded with a 5-kW motor and IP65 -rated charger  
• The new Nexus EX+ strengthens Ampere’s award -winning Nexus EV with IoT -enabled 
Intellipack, bringing powerful connected technology and a smarter ownership experience 
through the Ampere Connect App  
• The new 5 kW motor delivers stronger pick -up, 0 -40 kmph acceleration in 4.5 seconds and 
enhanced gradeability for steep climbs, hilly roads, flyovers and load -bearing use cases  
• The Nexus EX+ also gets an IP65 -rated water -resistant charger;  
• Nexus EX+ is priced at INR 1,29,999 and Nexus EX+ with Intellipack at INR 1,35,999  
 
Bengaluru, 05 October  2026 : Ampere, with an international and  six national records  from Greaves 
Electric Mobility, today  launched the new Nexus EX+, strengthening its Nexus EV with IoT -enabled 
Intellipack, a more powerful 5 kW motor and an IP65 -rated water -resistant charger. Designed on the 
Nexus platform’s proven performance and record -setting credentials, the new Nexus EX + brings 
powerful connected technology and a smarter  ownership experience through the Ampere Connect 
App, with 30+ intelligent features. The new Nexus EX+ further enhances the award -winning Nexus EV 
proposition by combining smarter ownership, stronger performance, durability and convenience for 
demanding rea l-world riding conditions.  
Intellipack is Ampere’s IoT -enabled intelligence and connectivity suite, delivered through the Ampere 
Connect App. It brings powerful connected technology to the Nexus EX+, including real -time charging 
status, live tracking, geo -fencing, Find My Scooter, t urn-by-turn navigation, remote vehicle diagnostics, 
trip reports, ride stats, charging history, energy insights, battery health score and service booking . The 
two-variant strategy gives customers a clear choice — the value -focused Nexus EX+, or the Nexus EX+ 
with Intellipack, offering a smarter, IoT -enabled ownership experience.  
The new 5 kW motor produces peak 202 Nm torque at the wheel and enables the Nexus EX+ to 
accelerate from 0 -40 kmph in 4.5 seconds, giving riders stronger pick -up, improved gradeability for 
steep climbs, flyovers and hilly terrain and better overall performance. The scooter also comes with an 
IP65 -rated water -resistant charger, adding durability and convenience across varied weather 
conditions. The new Nexus EV will be available in two variants: Nexus EX+ priced at INR 1,29,999 and 
Nexus EX+ with Intellipack priced at INR 1,35,999 , offered in two premium colours: Carbon Knight and 
Steel Grey.  
Speaking on the launch,  Mr. Vikas Singh, Managing Director, Greaves Electric Mobility , said, “The 
Nexus has consistently demonstrated Ampere’s focus on purposeful innovation, durability and real -
world performance. With the launch of the new Nexus EX+, we are strengthening this award -winning 
EV platform with IoT -enabled Intellipack, stronger perfor mance and greater everyday confidence. 
Intellipack makes ownership smarter and more convenient, while the 5 kW motor enhances pick -up 
and gradeability across flyovers, hilly roads, pillion riding and load -bearing conditions. The IP65 -rated 
charger further adds to the product’s reliability across varied weather conditions. This is another step 
 
in building electric scooters that are practical, resilient and aligned with the evolving needs of Indian 
riders.”  
The new Nexus EX+ comes equipped with next -generation LFP battery technology, designed for a long 
battery life of 2,00,000 km. Combined with lower every day running costs, the long -life battery 
proposition provides a strong total cost of ownership advantag e for customers moving from petrol 
scooters to electric mobility. The new Nexus EX+ also comes with a 5 -year/75,000 km battery warranty. 
The new Nexus EX+ will be available across Ampere dealerships nationwide, marking another step in 
Ampere's commitment to building electric scooters that evolve with the changing needs of Bharat.

About Greaves Electric Mobility Limited:  
Greaves Electric Mobility Limited (GEML), the electric mobility business of Greaves Cotton Limited 
(GCL), is one of the first companies in India to focus on the evolving market of electric vehicles (“ EV”) 
and has been among the frontrunners at driving EV adoption in the country. With over 18 years  of 
experience designing and manufacturing electric vehicles, GEML serves both the electric 2 -wheeler 
and 3 -wheeler segments. GEML along with its subsidiaries, operates three manufacturing plants 
located in Hyderabad, Noida, and Ranipet. The company suppor ts its network through 400 dealer 
touchpoints across India, thereby aiming  minimal downtime and enhanced customer experience . 
GEML holds 8 India Book of Records* and 1 Asia Book of Records* titles, reflecting its focus on 
 
technology and design led innovative & durable mobility solutions . Subsidiaries MLR Auto Limited 
and Bestway Agencies Private Limited help extend GEML’s market presence. Committed to affordability 
and sustainability, GEML provides clean mobility solutions contributing to India’s shift toward electric 
transportation.
