by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). A review of interim financial information

accounting matters and applying analytical and other review procedures. A review is substantially less

143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013 and consequently does not enable us to obtain assurance that we 
would become aware of all significant matters that might be identified in an audit. Accordingly, we do 
not express an audit opinion.
