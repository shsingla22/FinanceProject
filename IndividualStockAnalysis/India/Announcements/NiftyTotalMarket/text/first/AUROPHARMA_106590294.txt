TheraNym Biologics Pvt . Ltd., a subsidiary of Aurobindo Pharma Ltd,  announces the addition to the

large scale mammalian Drug Substance manufacturing facility (“Unit 2”) housing a total of 60 KL 
bioreactor capacities.
