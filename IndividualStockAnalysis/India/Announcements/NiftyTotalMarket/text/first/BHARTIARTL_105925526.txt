services providers, today, announced that it has increased its Wi-Fi service to an additional 4.1 
million new households in Gujarat.  
  
With Airtel Wi-Fi, a customer not only gets high-speed reliable wireless internet service but a 
whole host of other benefits including access to unlimited streaming, 20+ OTT services and over

App or by calling 8130181301.

has now reached every nook and corner of Gujarat. With Airtel Wi-Fi, customers can unlock a 
wide range of entertainment options including access to 20+ OTTs, 350+ television channels and
