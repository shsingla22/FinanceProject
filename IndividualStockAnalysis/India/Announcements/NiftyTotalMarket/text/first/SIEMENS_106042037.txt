Sub: Scheme of Arrangement between Siemens Limited and Siemens Energy India Limited
and their respective shareholders and creditors – Updates
Dear Sir / Madam,
We refer to our letter dated May 14, 2024 informing about the decision of the Board of Directors of the
Company approving the scheme of arrangement between the Company (“Siemens Limited / Company
/ Demerged Company”) and Siemens Energy India Limited (“SEIL / Resulting Company”) (a wholly
owned subsidiary of SL, which was incorporated on February 7, 2024) and their respective
shareholders and creditors (“Scheme”), providing for the demerger of the Company's Energy Business
(as defined in the Scheme) to SEIL in compliance with Sections 230 to 232 and other applicable
provisions of the Companies Act, 2013.
The Company vide letters dated September 18, 2024 and September 19, 2024 has informed about
receipt of observation letter from BSE Limited with ‘no adverse observations’ and observation letter
from National Stock Exchange of India Limited with ‘no objection’, respectively, in relation to the
Scheme.
Further, the meetings of the equity shareholders and unsecured creditors of the Company, as directed
by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Mumbai Bench are scheduled to be held on Monday,
December 2, 2024, which has been informed by the Company vide letters dated October 30, 2024.
The Scheme remains subject to applicable regulatory and other approvals.
We take this opportunity to also update that Mr. Guilherme Vieira De Mendonca and Mr. Harish Shekar
have been identified to be the “Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer” and “Executive Director
and Chief Financial Officer”, respectively, of the Resulting Company. Mr. Vieira De Mendonca and
Mr. Shekar are currently Head of Energy Business and Head Accounting and Controlling, respectively
of the Demerged Company. Mr. Shekar is also currently a Non-executive, Non-Independent Director
of the Resulting Company.
