New Delhi,  October  22, 202 6: The Board of Directors of Jindal Stainless Limited (JSL) today approved the  
financial results for the quarter and half year ended September  30, 202 6. The company reported a resilient 
operating  and financial performance in Q 2FY27. The Q2FY27  net revenue stood at  INR 12,874 crore, 
marking a n 18.2%  year -on-year  (YoY) increase. EBITDA was reported  at INR 1,43 3 crore, while Profit After 
Tax (PAT) rose to INR 898 crore, registering a YoY  growth of 3.2% and 11.1%, respectively.  The 
consolidated net debt was at INR 3,121 crore, while the net debt -to-equity ratio was at 0.15x .  
 
The quarter marked a resilient recovery following the operational disruptions in Q1, which had been 
impacted by geopolitical situation and industrial gas availability constraints. On a QoQ basis, the C ompany 
saw a 14 .4% increase in overall sales volumes and a 14.1% growth in net revenue.  Demand performance 
was underpinned by higher contributions from value -added products, along with momentum in the 
automotive and ornamental pipe & tube segments.  Globally, plates and ferritic grades gained wider 
acceptance, particularly across white goods , lifts and elevators.  
 
The domestic market has been seeing  increased  import pressure from  April 2026 . Imports of stainless 
steel flat products from China in July 2026 were 2.9 times the level of FY26 monthly average, and the first 
four months of FY27 alone account for about 63% of the entire previous year's imports from China. The 
FY26 average was 18,576  MT while in July 2026 alone, the number stood at 53,271 MT.  
 
Export s surged during the quarter, with export growth expanding by 20% QoQ and 36% YoY. The Company 
continued to diversify its global reach with a strategic emphasis on growing geographies such as South 
Korea, Colombia, South Africa, Brazil, and Japan.

The Board approved the amalgamation of  Chromeni Steels Limited, Rabirun Vinimay Private Limited, JSL 
Super Steel Limited and Jindal United Steel Limited into Jindal Stainless Limited with the aim to  simplify 
the Group structure. The consolidation of wholly owned subsidiaries would optimise utilisation of financial 
and other resources, improve operational efficiency, reduce administrative and compliance costs, 
eliminate inter -company transactions and balances, strengthen corporate governance and facilitate 
better management oversight and decision  making, thereby creating long -term value for all stakeholders.

Management Comments:  
 
Commenting on the performance of the company, Managing Director, Jindal Stainless, Mr Abhyuday 
Jindal, said,  "We delivered growth in Q2, building on the recovery from the geopolitical crisis  that impacted 
operations in Q1. Performance was underpinned by resilient domestic demand, supported by robust 
economic growth and improved sensitivity to wards lifecycle costing approach.  
 
Following the suspension of Quality Control Orders (QCOs) and the resultant influx of substandard  stainless 
steel imports into India, particularly from China and of Chinese -origin, we focused on sustaining sales 
volumes by catering to growing domestic markets and pursuing new export markets , wherever feasible.  
Going ahead, we will continue expanding our market outreach initiatives and  focussing on an improved 
product mix to cater to domestic and global markets."  
 
About Jindal Stainless  
India’s leading stainless steel manufacturer, Jindal Stainless, had an annual turnover of INR 42,95 5 crore (USD 4. 86 
billion) in FY2 6 and an annual melt capacity of 4.2 million tonnes . It has 16 stainless steel manufacturing and 
processing facilities in India and abroad, including in Spain and Indonesia, and a worldwide network in 12 countries, 
as of March 202 6. In India, there are ten sales offices and six service centres, as of March 202 6. The company’s 
product range includes stainless steel slabs, blooms, coils, plates, sheets, precision strips, wire rods, rebars, blade 
steel, and coin blanks.  
 
Jindal Stainless relies on its integrated operations to enhance its cost competitiveness and operational efficiency. 
Founded in 1970, Jindal Stainless continues to be inspired by a vision for innovation and enriching lives and is 
committed to social respon sibility.  
 
Jindal Stainless remains focused on a greener and sustainable future. The company manufactures stainless steel 
using electric arc furnace, a process that significantly reduces greenhouse gas emissions and allows for recyclability 
of scrap without compromis ing on quality.
