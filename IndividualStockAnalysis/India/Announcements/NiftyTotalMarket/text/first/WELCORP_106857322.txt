In continuation of our earlier disclosure dated 29 October, 2026 regarding the proposed acquisition of 
49% equity stake in Welspun Special Coating Private Limited (“WSCPL”) and formation of a Joint 
Venture with Perma-Pipe Qatar for setting up an Internal Fusion Bonded Epoxy (IFBE) coating facility 
in India, we wish to inform you that the aforesaid transaction has been completed. 
 
In this regard, the Company has entered into a Share Purchase Agreement with Welspun DI Pipes 
Limited (“WDI”), a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company, Perma-Pipe Qatar and WSCPL, pursuant 
to which: 
 
 Perma-Pipe Qatar  has acquired 51% equity stake in WSCPL from WDI for an aggregate 
consideration of Rs. 51,000/-; and 
 Welspun Corp Limited  has acquired the remaining 49% equity stake in WSCPL from WDI for an 
aggregate consideration of Rs. 49,000/-. 
 
The Company has also executed a Shareholders’ Agreement (Joint Venture Agreement) with Perma-
Pipe Qatar to govern the operations and management of WSCPL as a Joint Venture entity. 
 
Consequently, WSCPL has ceased to be a step-down wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company and 
has become a direct associate company of Welspun Corp Limited, with the Company holding 49% of 
its equity share capital and Perma-Pipe Qatar holding the remaining 51%. 
 
The Joint Venture will undertake Internal FBE (Fusion Bonded Epoxy) coating works and related 
activities in India. IFBE coating is an epoxy-based powder applied electrostatically to the interior of steel 
pipes to protect against corrosion and chemical damage. The major applications are in Onshore and 
offshore Oil & Gas Pipelines; Slurry Pipeline; Chemical processing plants; Food processing 
applications; Underground or underwater water pipelines.
