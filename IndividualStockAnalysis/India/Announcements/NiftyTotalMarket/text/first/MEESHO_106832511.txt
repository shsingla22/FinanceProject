As we flagged last quarter, our Mega Blockbuster Sale (MBS) moved from Q2 last year to Q3 this year, so Q2 will look softer than the underlying trend and Q3 stronger. The two are best read together.  Several measures suggest underlying demand held up: ● Festive-aligned growth: Shopping in India follows the festival calendar, not fiscal quarters. Demand builds through Navratri and peaks around Dussehra and Diwali, and our sale is timed to that cycle. This year, Dussehra fell about 19 days later than last year, so the festive build-up moved partly out of Q2 and into Q3. Comparing the 90 days before Dussehra in each year lines up the same stretch of festive demand, sale included, regardless of where the quarter ends. On that basis, NMV grew 33% YoY. ● Quarter on quarter: NMV grew ~11% QoQ, in a quarter with no sale. 
● Habit: LTM frequency rose 7% YoY to 10.4, even as first-time buyers grew 16% QoQ. New users take time to settle in, so frequency rising alongside them means existing users are shopping with us more often. ● NMV grew faster than orders this quarter mainly because last year was affected by temporary inefficiencies from consolidation in the third-party logistics industry, which did not recur this year. As a result, more of the orders customers placed were delivered. Meesho Mall ● Meesho Mall now hosts ~1,800 brands. Its NMV grew 81% YoY in Q2 FY27, and 89% in Tier 3+ cities, with about half of its customers in the quarter buying on Mall for the first time. Meesho Mall is commission-free, so brands can reach these customers without paying to get there, and shoppers get genuine brands at prices they can afford. ● What people buy on Meesho Mall is changing. When we started, most branded purchases were essentials such as soap, shampoo, detergent and diapers, and those purchases built trust. Today, the same customers are also buying face serums, sunscreens, conditioners and face rollers, and discretionary products make up a growing share of what they buy on Meesho Mall. These are first steps beyond the basics, taken in towns that many brands have not reached directly before. ● Our scale also lets us work closely with brands. We see emerging consumption trends, regional preferences and how shopping habits are changing in smaller towns. We share these insights with brands to help them understand these customers and plan for growth across the country. Content Commerce ● Content Commerce NMV grew 160% YoY in Q2 FY27, and the number of content pieces generating orders grew 144% to 2.2 million. What began as a way for customers to discover products is now how a growing number of them decide what to buy: they watch someone they trust use a product, then buy it. ● It works for everyone involved. Customers find products through creators they relate to, sellers reach demand they could not reach on their own, and creators earn from what they make. Over 4 lakh sellers are now part of the ecosystem. Because creators earn only when their content leads to a purchase, everyone’s incentives point the same way. Contribution Margin – Marketplace 
 

Contribution Margin rose to 6.0% of NMV in Q2 FY27, from 4.6% in Q1. Two things drove it: Revenue growth: Revenue from Operations (Marketplace) was ₹4,119.9 crores, up 34% YoY, as more sellers invested in ads that reliably convert into orders, and better matching of seller demand to user intent made every rupee they spend work harder. Logistics efficiency: greater network density, better network design, routing and partner allocation, and improvements in Geo-India LLM made delivery c.15% faster YoY and lowered cost per shipment as volumes grew, so each additional order now brings overall cost down while improving the experience. TrustMesh, our AI model for preventing abuse at scale, continues to get better at stopping potentially fraudulent orders before they are placed, so fewer shipments end up cancelled or returned to origin We may not hold Contribution Margin at this level in the near term. Our platform has increasing returns to scale, so fulfilling each order gets more efficient as we grow. Denser logistics networks lower the cost of each shipment. Every additional transaction gives our AI models more signal, which improves relevance, visits and conversion, and makes our advertising more valuable to sellers. As the business becomes more efficient, we will reinvest part of the gains: lowering sellers’ cost of doing business, improving the experience for everyone on the platform, and scaling earlier-stage flywheels like Meesho Mall and Content Commerce. We believe a larger, healthier platform running at a reinvested margin will generate more free cash flow over the long term than a smaller one optimised for near-term margin. We are making this trade-off to deliver on our mission of Democratizing Internet Commerce for Everyone faster.
