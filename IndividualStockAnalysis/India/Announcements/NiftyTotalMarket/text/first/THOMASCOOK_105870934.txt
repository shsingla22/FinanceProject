Sterling Holiday Resorts Arrives in Dehradun!  
Sterling Marbella Dehradun - first Spanish-Themed hotel in the heart of Uttarakhand  
 
 
May  31, 2024: Sterling Holiday Resorts announces the launch of Sterling Marbella 
Dehradun, the city’s  first -ever Spanish -themed hotel  in the Himalayan foothills, an idyllic 
haven for year -round adventures.   
 
Dehradun is the gateway to the Garhwal region,  and Sterling Marbella Dehradun is ideally 
situated  on the popular Rajpur road  in the heart of the city . Inspired by the life and culture 
of Spain, this iconic hotel exudes the charm of Spanish and Moorish architecture through 
every nook and corner  – 500 pieces of paintings, photographs and sculptures  adorn the 
walls and ceilings of this traveller’s sanctuary.  
 Sterling Marbella Dehradun offers 37 European- styled rooms and suites, designed to 
provide an authentic Andalusian experience. Each accommodation option features stylish interiors, making it perfect for families, solo travelers, and business visitors.   
 The hotel’s rooftop venue, Dreamers Restro, stands out as a popular destination for both 
private events and lively nightlife. Featuring indoor and outdoor spaces, Dreamers Restro 
offers an eclectic menu that features  Indian favo urites and Spanish specialities . Guests 
can savor delicacies like paella, tapas, tortilla, gazpachos , and sangrias  while enjoying live 
music and the pleasant Dehradun weather.  
 
Sterling Marbella Dehradun also features versatile banquet spaces, including an indoor 
board room accommodating 16 guests and an outdoor rooftop terrace for up to 75 guests, ideal for intimate celebrations and private meetings.  
 Mr. Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director and CEO of Sterling Holiday Resorts, shared, “We are thrilled to introduce Sterling Mar bella Dehradun, a perfect amalgamation of India n 
hospitality in Spanish themed setting. Dehradun for us is a crucial entry point for 
Uttarakhand and is an ideal mix of leisure and business travel converging between 
Haridwar , Mussoorie or Rishikesh .”   
 "We  are delighted to collaborate with Sterling Holiday Resorts through our transaction 
partner, Horizon Consulting. About our hotel, we wanted to bring the essence of Spain 
to our hometown and therefore we travelled to Marbella in the south of Spain, to imbib e 
the architecture and culture of the place. We have incorporated the same style in the 
architecture and iconic designs across the interiors & exteriors of our hotel. Sterling Marbella Dehradun promises to be a landmark destination that offers guests uniqu e 
 
cultural and culinary experiences,” said Mr. Inderjeet Singh and Mr. Komalbir Singh, 
Founding Partners, Marbella Hotel & Resorts, LLP.   
 
Guests at Sterling Marbella Dehradun can explore a myriad of local attractions and 
activities. Discover the Tapkeshwar Mahadev Temple, Robbers Cave, paragliding adventures, the Mindrolling Monastery, and vibrant shopping at Rajpur Road. The hotel’s central location makes it a perfect base for exploring other Sterling resorts in Uttarakhand, including those in Mu ssoorie, Rishikesh, and Haridwar.
