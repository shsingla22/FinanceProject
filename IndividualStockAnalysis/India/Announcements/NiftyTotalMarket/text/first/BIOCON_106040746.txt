NOTIFICATION TO STOCK EXCHANGE  
 
 
COMPANY STATEMENT  
 
Biocon Biologics ’ Drug Substance Facility in Bengaluru Classified as 
Voluntary Action Indicated ( VAI) 
 
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, Oct 30 , 2024  
 
 
“The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)  has classified Biocon Biologics ’ Drug 
Substance Facility at Biocon Campus, (Site 1) located in Bengaluru, Karnataka , as 
Voluntary Action Indicated ( VAI).  
 
This relates to the cGMP inspection conducted by the agency between February 20 -
28, 2024 and pertains to the supply of rh -Insulin (rhI) Drug Substance to the United 
States.  
 
Biocon Biologics remains committed to global standards of Quality and Compliance.”  
 
 
– Company Spokesperson
