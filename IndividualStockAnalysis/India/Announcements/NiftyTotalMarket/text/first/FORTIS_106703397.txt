MEDIA RELEASE 
Agilus Diagnostics Appoints Vijender Singh as Managing Director & 
Chief Executive Officer 
Gurugram, 20 July 2026 : Agilus Diagnostics, one of India’s leading diagnostic service providers, today 
announced the appointment of Mr. Vijender Singh  as its Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer 
(CEO). This follows the resignation of Dr. Anand K , who has decided to pursue opportunities outside the 
organisation after being associated with the company for nearly six years. The Board and management 
thank Dr. Anand K for his contributions to Agilus Diagnostics and wish him success in his future 
endeavours. 
Mr. Vijender Singh brings with him over three decades of leadership experience across diagnostics, 
healthcare, consumer healthcare and FMCG sectors. He has a proven track record of building and scaling 
businesses, driving operational excellence, strengthening governance frameworks and creating high-
performing organisations. 
Prior to joining Agilus Diagnostics, Mr. Singh served as Chairman and Managing Director of Diagnum 
Healthcare, where he focused on strategic growth, governance and business development. He has 
previously held leadership positions as Group CEO of Metropolis Healthcare, CEO of Healthians and spent 
nearly a decade with Dr. Lal PathLabs, where he played a key role in building one of India’s most successful 
diagnostics platforms. He has also worked with Jashvik Capital as an Operating Partner and held leadership 
roles with Ranbaxy Consumer Healthcare, Bausch & Lomb and Blow Plast. 
Commenting on the appointment, Dr. Ashutosh Raghuvanshi, Managing Director & Chief Executive 
Officer, Fortis Healthcare Limited and Chairman, Agilus Diagnostics , said, “We are delighted to welcome 
Vijender Singh as the Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer of Agilus Diagnostics. Vijender brings 
extensive experience in building and scaling diagnostic businesses, driving operational excellence and 
creating sustainable value across healthcare organisations. His deep understanding of the diagnostics 
industry, coupled with a strong track record of business transformation and customer-centric growth, 
makes him exceptionally well positioned to lead Agilus into its next phase of growth. As the diagnostics 
landscape continues to evolve, we believe his leadership will further strengthen our capabilities, accelerate 
innovation, enhance customer experience and reinforce Agilus’ position as one of India’s most trusted 
diagnostic brands.” 
On his appointment, Mr. Vijender Singh, Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer, Agilus Diagnostics , 
said, “I am excited to join Agilus Diagnostics at a time when the diagnostics industry is witnessing 
significant transformation and growth. Agilus has built a strong reputation for quality, scientific excellence 
and customer trust, supported by a vast network and talented team. I look forward to working closely with 
the leadership team and employees to further strengthen the organisation’s market position, expand 
access to high-quality diagnostics, enhance operational excellence and create long-term value for patients, 
customers and all stakeholders.” 
 
 
About Vijender Singh 
Mr. Vijender Singh is a seasoned business leader with over 30 years of experience across diagnostics, 
healthcare, consumer healthcare and FMCG sectors. He has held senior leadership positions at some of 
India’s leading diagnostics organisations, including Dr. Lal PathLabs, Metropolis Healthcare and 
Healthians. 
At Dr. Lal PathLabs, he played a key role in building the company’s retail-focused diagnostics model, 
strengthening operational excellence and contributing to its successful IPO journey. As Group CEO of 
Metropolis Healthcare, he led the integration and transformation of multiple acquired businesses, 
strengthened governance and operational efficiencies, and played a significant role in the company’s 
successful public listing. Most recently, he served as Chairman and Managing Director of Diagnum 
Healthcare, focusing on strategic growth, governance and business development. 
Widely recognised for his expertise in business transformation, operational excellence, customer 
experience and organisational leadership, Mr. Singh has consistently delivered profitable growth and 
scalable business models across the healthcare sector.

About Agilus Diagnostics 
(A subsidiary of Fortis Healthcare Ltd.) 
Instituted in 1995, Agilus Diagnostics Ltd., is one of India’s largest diagnostics service providers in terms of number of laboratories 
in India as of and for the financial year ended March 31, 2023 (Source: CRISIL Report). Agilus Diagnostics has a network of 410+ 
labs, 3700+ customer touch points, and 12000+ direct clients/pick up points spread across 1000+ cities, 25 states and five union 
territories in India as of March 31, 2023. The company has recently undergone a brand transformation journey and has adopted 
a new name Agilus Diagnostics. Agilus is a portmanteau of ‘Agile’ and ‘Us’ and is inspired from the Latin term ‘agilis’ which means 
‘agility’. Agilus offers more than 3,000 tests and test combinations, ranging from routine tests to esoteric and genetic tests. The 
company has the largest number of NABL accredited labs in the country as of March 31, 2023 (Source: CRISIL Report) with 43 
NABL accredited labs, 3 NABL M(EL)T certified labs and 2 CAP accredited labs. Its international network consists of centers across 
the SAARC region, Sub-Saharan Africa, South-East Asia and the Middle East. Agilus is committed to research and development 
and focused on genomics and ‘next-generation diagnostics’ and delivering a customer-centric experience through integrated and 
seamless technology platforms and data-driven actionable insights.
