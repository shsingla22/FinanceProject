New Delhi, February  1, 2026  
 
HERO MOTOCORP MARKS 25 CONSECUTIVE YEARS OF MARKET 
LEADERSHIP  
 
RECORDS 26% DISPATCH GROWTH WITH ~5.58  LAKH UNITS SOLD 
IN JANUARY 2026  
 
Hero MotoCorp  has maintained its position as the world's largest two -wheeler manufacturer 
for 25 consecutive years,  reinforcing  its market leadership through FY 2025 -26.1 The Company 
recorded dispatches of 557,871  units in January 2026, registering a robust ~26% growth 
compared to 442,873  units in January 2025. The Company also recorded 491 ,986 * VAHAN 
registrations  this month , reflecting healthy retail momentum across markets . 
*Data from VAHAN except Telangana, as on February 1, 2026.  
 
Strong Product Portfolio Drives Growth  
The strong growth  in January 2026  was driven by robust  performance in the S cooter segment  
supported by growing consumer demand for new offerings such as Xoom and Destini. The 
motorcycle portfolio also witnessed strong traction , led by  Xtreme and  Glamour  X.  
 
VIDA Builds Momentum in Emerging Mobility Segment  
VIDA, Hero MotoCorp’s Emerging Mobility business , continued  its market share performance with 
13,000 VAHAN registrations in January 2026, thereby posting ~21% month -on-month  
growth in retail  sales volume as compared to  10,701  units in the previous month. The positive 
growth was driven by sustained demand for  VIDA VX2, supported by strong retail footprint and 
a healthy pipeline of bookings across key markets.  
 
Global Business  Maintains Double -Digit Growth  
The company’s  Global Business maintained strong momentum  with dispatch growth of ~24% 
in January 2026 with  37,663  units, compared to  30,495  units during the same period last year. 
Strong consumer  response for the premium portfoli o ensured growth across  international 
markets .  
 
Recognition  
This month, Hero MotoSports Team Rally  concluded the 48th edition of Dakar Rally 2026  
with a double top 10 finish, marking a historic milestone in the global rally -raid competition. 
Additionally, Hero MotoCorp was r ecognized as an Environment,  Social and Governance  (ESG ) 
Leader among automotive companies by NSE Sustainability Ratings & Analytics Limited , a 
SEBI -registered ESG Rating Provider . Further strengthening its innovation credentials , VIDA’s 
DIRT.E K3  was recognized as CES 2026 Innovation Award Honoree  for its innovative size - 
adaptive design.
