Lupin Appoints Brian Savage as President, Chief Legal Officer 
 
Mumbai, October 23, 2026 : Global pharma major Lupin Limited (Lupin) announced the appointment 
of Brian Savage as President, Chief Legal Officer. Brian will lead Lupin’s global Legal, Compliance, and 
Intellectual Property Management Group (IPMG) functions, and will be based in New Jersey. 
 
Brian joins Lupin from Teva Pharmaceuticals, where he held several senior leadership roles since 
2015. Most recently, he served as Teva’s Executive Vice President and Interim Global Chief Legal 
Officer. During his tenure, he also led Teva’s global litigation strategy and served as General Counsel 
for its U.S. Generics business. Prior to joining Teva, Brian practiced law at Dechert LLP.  
 
Brian brings extensive experience navigating the complex legal and regulatory landscape in the global 
pharmaceutical industry.   He holds a Juris Doctor (J.D.) from Yale Law School and a B.A., summa cum 
laude, from the University of Pennsylvania. 
 
Commenting on the appointment, Lupin CEO Vinita Gupta and Managing Director Nilesh Gupta said,  
“We are delighted to welcome Brian to our executive leadership team. His deep understanding of the 
pharmaceutical industry landscape, extensive legal expertise, and proven leadership will be valuable 
as we continue to strengthen our governance framework, navigate an evolving regulatory 
environment,  and advance our long term growth ambitions.” 
 
About Lupin  
Lupin Limited is a global pharmaceutical leader headquartered in Mumbai, India, with products distributed in over 100 
markets. Lupin specializes in pharmaceutical products, including branded and generic formulations, complex generics, 
biotechnology products, and active pharmaceutical ingredients. Trusted by healthcare professionals and consumers 
globally, the company enjoys a strong position in India and the U.S. across multiple therapy areas, including respiratory, 
cardiovascular, anti-diabetic, anti-infective, gastrointestinal, central nervous system, and women's health. Lupin has 15 
state-of-the-art manufacturing sites and 6 research centers globally, along with a dedicated workforce of over 26,000 
professionals. Lupin is committed to improving patient health outcomes through its subsidiaries - Lupin Diagnostics, 
Lupin Digital Health, and Lupin Manufacturing Solutions.
