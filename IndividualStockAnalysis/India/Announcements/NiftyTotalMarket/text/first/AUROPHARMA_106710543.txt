July 24, 202 6 
 
 
 
 
Rio de Janeiro, Brazil — Aurobindo Pharma Limited is pleased to announce that it has entered into  a non-exclusive 
voluntary licensing agreement with MSD  (trade  name of Merck & Co., Inc., Rahway, N.J., USA (NYSE: MRK) . 
Aurobindo Pharma is among a select group of global  generic manufacturers to have signed this agreement. The 
royalty -free agreement will enable supply of generic alimatravir  to cover 129 Low- & Middle -Income Countries  
(LMICs ) which account for the majority  of new HIV diagnoses globally , subject to applicable regulatory approvals and 
requirements.  
 
Executing these agreements before Phase 3 trial enrollment is completed is a first in HIV prevention and is intended 
to enable the right  scale -up where Aurobindo Pharma  is licensed  to manufacture and supply  generic alimatravir , 
which will meet  internationally recognized standards following regulatory approvals.  
 
“At Aurobindo Pharma, expanding access to high -quality, affordable medicines remains central to our purpose. Our 
collaboration with MSD  reflects our continued commitment to partnering with innovators to accelerate equitable 
access to potential new therapies. Through this voluntary licensing agreement, we look forward to making 
alimatravir  available across eligible low - and middle -income countries, subject to successful development and 
regulatory approvals, helping strengthen global efforts towards HIV prevention.”  said Umesh K, Associate President 
& Head – Global Access & Licensing.   
 
“We are pleased to be working with Aurobindo and the other manufacturers involved in these voluntary licensing 
agreements to manufacture generic alimatravir in 129 LMICs,  if approved, ” said Gregg Szabo, senior vice president, 
Global HIV Marketing, MSD.   “We hope this early action will help stakeholders across the HIV response begin planning 
for the scale -up needed to reach people who could benefit from HIV prevention , following the necessary regulatory 
approvals .”
