Brief Profile of Mr. Indra nil Chatterjee 
Mr. Indranil Chatterjee holds a Bachelor's degree in Production Engineering from Jadavpur 
University, Kolkata, and an Advanced Management Diploma from the Wharton School, 
University of Pennsylvania, USA. 
He brings over three decades of deep and hands-on experience m manufacturing excellence, 
operational leadership, project management, and business transformation across sectors such as 
FMCG, industrial ceramics, and automobiles. His core expertise lies in large-scale operations, 
capacity expansion, process optimization, cost leadership, and adoption of global manufacturing 
best practices. 
Mr. Chatterjee joined Exide Industries Limited in 2014 as Head of the Two-Wheeler Battery 
Manufacturing Facility in Haryana and subsequently led the Shyamnagar manufacturing unit in 
West Bengal. He later served as Chief Manufacturing -Industrial, with end-to-end responsibility 
for three major manufacturing locations at Haldia, Shyamnagar, and Hosur (Tamil Nadu). 
Immediately prior to his current role as Executive Vice President & Head -Operations, Mr. 
Chatterjee served as the Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer of Chloride Metals Limited, 
a I 00% subsidiary of Exide Industries Limited. Under his leadership, Chloride Metals Limited 
significantly scaled up its recycling operations and successfully implemented several trend-setting 
initiatives, including Producer Gas systems, Integrated Battery Breaking Units, and Plastic 
Recycling facilities. Operating through three state-of-the-art manufacturing plants located at 
Haldia, Malur, and Supa, the company today records a turnover in excess of t6,000 crore and has 
earned the distinction of being one of the largest smelters in Asia.
