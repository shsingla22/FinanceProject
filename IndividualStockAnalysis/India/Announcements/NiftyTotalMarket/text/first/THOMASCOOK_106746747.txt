 Thomas Cook India & SOTC Travel Strengthen Luxury Cruise Portfolio 
Featured experiences include Queen Mary 2, Mediterranean Cruises across France,  
Italy, Spain and Greece ; Northern Lights Expedition Cruise  (Arctic) and Antarctica  
 
Key Highlights  
• Curated land + cruise holidays spanning Europe, the Mediterranean, Antarctica and the Arctic, 
combining iconic ocean voyages with immersive destination experiences  
• Introduction of luxury Antarctica expedition cruises and Arctic Delights – Northern Lights Special 
featuring a unique Icebreaker Cruise  
• End-to-end holiday packages with flights, hotels, transfers, sightseeing and premium cruise 
experiences through leading global cruise partners  
 
Mumbai, August 17, 2026: Cruise holidays are witnessing growing popularity among Indian travellers, driven 
by a rising preference for immersive, experience- led vacations that combine multiple destinations  and luxury 
hospitalit y. Recognizing this growing trend, Thomas Cook (India) Limited, India's leading omnichannel travel 
services company, and its Group Company, SOTC Travel, have strengthened their luxury cruise holiday portfolio with curated ocean and expedition voyages across Europe, the Mediterranean, Antarctica and the Arctic.  
 The expanded portfolio brings together iconic ocean voyages, premium Mediterranean sailings and expedition -
led experiences, complemented by thoughtfully curated land itineraries. Highlights include journeys aboard the 
legendary Queen Mary 2, luxury Mediter ranean cruises across France, Italy, Spain and Greece, all -inclusive 
Antarctica expedition cruises featuring Zodiac excursions and guided shore landings, and the Arctic Delights –  
Northern Lights Special, combining a unique Icebreaker Cruise with spectacul ar fjords, Lapland adventures and 
opportunities to witness the magical Aurora Borealis. Designed as seamless end- to-end holidays, each itinerary 
includes flights, hotels, transfers, sightseeing and cruise experiences, allowing travellers to discover multiple 
destinations . 
 Backed by partnerships with leading international cruise operators and the Companies' extensive destination 
expertise, Thomas Cook India and SOTC Travel offer professionally curated cruise holidays that combine world -
class cruising with immersive onshore experiences. The portfolio caters to a diverse range of travellers —from 
couples and families to multi -generational groups and affluent explorers seeking distinctive, once- in-a-lifetime 
journeys , delivering the convenience and assurance of a single trusted travel partner from planning through to 
return.  
 Featured Cruise Experiences  
 
Queen Mary 2 –  United Kingdom  
Experience the timeless elegance of Cunard's iconic flagship with signature White Star Service. Cunard Line 
markets itself with high -end elegance;  industry experts and passengers generally view the experience as 
premium and true Royalty, reserving true luxury status for its high- end suite accommodations.  
 
Mediterranean Cruises –  France, Italy, Spain & Greece  
Discover vibrant coastal cities, idyllic islands, celebrated gastronomy, renowned wine regions and rich cultural 
heritage while enjoying premium cruising with Costa and MSC cruise line s across the Mediterranean. Select 
winter departures offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds, making them ideal for leisurely exploration.  
 
Antarctica Expedition Cruises  
Designed for adventure- seeking travellers, these all -inclusive expeditions include a stay in Buenos Aires, flights 
to Ushuaia and luxury voyages aboard purpose- built polar expedition vessels  such as Swan Hellenic and Atlas 
Expedition. Guests enjoy expertly guided Zodiac excursions, shore landings and extraordinary encounters with 
penguins, whales, seals and other Antarctic wildlife.  
  
 
  
 
 
Arctic Delights –  Northern Lights Special with Icebreaker Cruise  
An unforgettable Arctic journey featuring a unique Icebreaker Cruise, spectacular Norwegian fjords, Lapland adventures and opportunities to witness the magical Northern Lights. The itinerary spans Tromsø, Stokmarknes, Narvik, Kiruna, Rovaniemi, Kemi and He lsinki, bringing together breathtaking Arctic landscapes, 
immersive Nordic experiences and one of the world's most extraordinary winter adventures.   
 
Mr. Rajeev Kale, President & Country Head –  Holidays, MICE, Visa, Thomas Cook (India) Limited,  said, 
"Cruise holidays are entering an exciting phase in India as travellers increasingly view the journey itself as an 
integral part of their holiday. Beyond traditional sightseeing, today's travellers are seeking thoughtfully designed itineraries that combine multiple destinations, distinctive onboard experiences and immersive exploration. Our 
strengthened cruise portfolio reflects this evolution, bringing together iconic ocean voyages and extraordinary 
expedition experiences across Europe, the Mediterranean, A ntarctica and the Arctic. Through our partnerships 
with leading global cruise operators and curated land experiences, we are enabling Indian travellers to discover some of the world's most sought -after cruise destinations through one seamless holiday."  
 Mr. SD Nandakumar, President & Country Head –  Holidays & Corporate Tours, SOTC Travel, said,  "Cruise 
holidays offer a compelling travel proposition by bringing together relaxation, exploration, premium hospitality and the convenience of experiencing multiple destinations in a single journey. This makes them increasingly relevant to a wide spectrum of travellers —from couples and families to multi -generational groups and those 
seeking unique, once- in-a-lifetime experiences. Our expanded portfolio offers customers greater choice across 
cruise formats and destinations, while our end- to-end holiday solu tions, backed by thoughtfully curated land 
experiences, ensure every aspect of the journey is seamless, convenient and memorable."
