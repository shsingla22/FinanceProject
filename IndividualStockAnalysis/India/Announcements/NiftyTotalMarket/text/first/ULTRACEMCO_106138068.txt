Sub: Company Update: Commissioning of cement capacity  
 
Dear Sirs,  
 
The Company is pleased to announce the commissioning of an additional 0.6 mtpa grinding 
capacity at its existing unit situated at Sonar Bangla, West Bengal . 
 
This additional capacity will help UltraTech to service composite cement  demand in the East  
region and will also help to increase its blended cement ratio.  
  
Consequent to the above , the Company’s total domestic grey cement manufacturing capacity 
stands at  166.31 mtpa. Along with its overseas capacity of 5.4 mtpa, the Company’s global 
capacity  stands at 171. 71 mtpa.
