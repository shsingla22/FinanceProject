 Prime Focus’ BRAHMA AI To Collaborate With Google Cloud To Scale High- Fidelity, 
Interactive Digital Humans for Global Enterprises 
 
Mumbai - March 11, 2026 
 BRAHMA AI , an enterprise AI content platform backed by Prime Focus Limited, and Google 
Cloud , have announced a multi -year strategic partnership to enable high -fidelity, multilingual, 
interactive communication via digital humans that bridge the gap between organizations and 
their global audiences.  
 
Google Cloud will partner with BRAHMA AI to advance its innovation and product lifecycle journey. The combination of G oogle Cloud’s secure, scalable AI infrastructure and generative AI 
models with BRAHMA AI’s industry -leading enterprise AI content platform can deliver movie-
quality, interactive digital humans, or ATMANS. These are hyper -personalized digital 
likenesses, wi th multilingual audio generated through VAANI  in BRAHMA AI Studio , and 
leveraging BRAHMA AI Cor e, an enterprise -grade data platform, making information more 
accessible and relatable for audiences in any language.  
 
Speaking about the collaboration, BRAHMA AI CEO Prabhu Narasimhan said, “Artificial 
intelligence is fundamentally changing how organisati ons create and deliver content. BRAHMA 
AI was built as an end -to-end enterprise platform to help organisations create, manage and 
scale AI content across languages and global markets. From day one, we designed the platform 
around a strong architectural foundation of ethics, security and digital identity governance so 
enterprises can innovate while protecting identity, rights and intellectual property.  
 
Our collaboration with Google Cloud validates our industry -leading AI tools, allowing us to take 
our high- fidelity, movie -grade digital humans (ATMAN) and make them truly interactive using 
Google Cloud’s generative AI models. It also accelerates enterprise adoption and enables 
organisations across media and entertainment, sports, healthcare, advertising, and global 
consumer brands to deploy secure AI -driven workflows at scale, exponentially expanding 
BRAHMA AI’s total addressable market  which is currently expected to exceed $130 billion by 
2030. Together, we are co- innovating on next -generation AI technologies  and bringing them to 
global enterprises through a joint go -to-market approach that combines BRAHMA AI’s 
Hollywood- grade AI with Google Cloud’s infrastructure and reach .”
