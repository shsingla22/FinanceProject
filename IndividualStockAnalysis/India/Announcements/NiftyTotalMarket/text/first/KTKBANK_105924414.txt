PRESS RELEASE 
KARNATAKA BANK ANNOUNCES RECORD Ql NET PROFIT AT Rs. 400.33 CRORE 
In the Meeting of the Board of Directors held today at Bank's Head Quarters. 
Mangaluru, the Board has approved the financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 
2024. 
Karnataka Bank has posted a record Net Profit of Rs. 400.33 crores for Ql of FY25 as 
against Rs. 370.70 crores during the corresponding Ql of FY 24 with an YoY (Year-on­
Year) growth rate of 7.99%. 
The Bank achieved its highest Business turnover at Rs. 1,75,619 crores for Ql of FY25 
compared to Rs. 1,49,971 crores for Ql of FY 24 registering a YoY growth of 17.10%. The 
Aggregate deposits of the Bank stood at Rs. 1,00,164 crores for Ql of FY 25 as against 
Rs. 86,960 crores for Ql of FY 24 with a YoY growth of 15.18 %. Bank's Gross advances 
stood at Rs. 75,455 crores as against Rs. 63,012 crores as of Ql of FY24 with a YoY 
growth of 19 .75 % . 
The operating profit of the Bank stood at Rs. 558.59 crores and Net Interest Income 
stood at Rs. 903.36 crore as at the quarter ended 30.06.2024. 
The book quality is steadily improving with Gross NP As declined to 3.54% at end of Ql 
of FY25 compared to 3.68% in the corresponding Ql of FY24. Net NPAs [NNPAs] stood 
at 1.66 % in Ql of FY25 from 1.43% in the corresponding Ql of FY24. 
The Capital Adequacy Ratio of the Bank has further improved to 17.64% at end of Ql of 
FY25 as compared to 17.00% at end of Ql of FY24. 
Announcing the results at the Bank's Head Quarters at Mangaluru, Shri Srikrishnan H, 
Managing Director & CEO ·of the Bank said "We are happy to report multiple 
milestones in financial performance crossing INR 1.75 Lakh Crores in Business 
Turnover, reaching INR 1 Lakh crores in Aggregate deposits and INR 75 lakh Crores in 
Gross Advances. This robust performance underscores the positive trends in our

committed to overall health of balance sheet. "
