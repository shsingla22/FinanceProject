Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR Regulations”), and further to our earlier intimation dated 23rd 
September, 2026, we hereby inform you that, the Search and Seizure proceedings initiated by the Maharashtra 
Goods and Services Tax Department (“Department”) on 23rd September, 2026 at the premises of the Company 
and its wholly -owned subsidiary have been concluded on 29th September, 2026.  
 
Further, pursuant to the conclusion of the proceedings, the Department has determined a GST liability of ₹9.9 
crore under the Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM). The Company is eligible to avail the entire amount of such 
GST liability as an Input Tax Credit, subject to the applicable provisions of law. In addition, the Department has 
determined interest and certain non -creditable tax liabilities aggregating to ₹ 2.0 crore. The Company has duly 
considered the matter and has deci ded to discharge and settle the aforesaid outstanding liability. Accordingly, 
the aggregate amount of ₹ 2.0 crore towards interest and non -creditable tax liabilities represents the net 
financial impact/cost to the Company arising from the proceedings.  
 
We w ould also like to reiterate our Stakeholders that, above said proceedings have not caused any disruption 
to the Company’s business operations. The Company’s manufacturing and other operations have continued in 
the normal course, with the financial impact r emaining negligible, as stated above. The Company continues to 
remain steadfast in its commitment to the highest standards of compliance, corporate governance and 
regulatory discipline. The Company has extended, and will continue to extend, its full and pr oactive co -operation 
to the concerned authorities and provide all necessary assistance and information as may be required , from time 
to time.
