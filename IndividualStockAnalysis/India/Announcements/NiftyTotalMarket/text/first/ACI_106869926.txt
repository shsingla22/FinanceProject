Reference: Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR”)  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI LODR, we wish to inform you that Mr. Rajesh Nair 
has been appointed as Chief Executive Ofﬁcer of SiCSem Private Limited (“SiCSem”), a 
step-down subsidiary of the Company, with effect from November 09, 2026.  
SiCSem is setting up a Silicon Carbide power semiconductor fabrication and ATMP 
facility at Info Valley, Khordha, Odisha, under the India Semiconductor Mission. As Chief 
Executive Ofﬁcer, Mr. Nair will lead SiCSem through the construction, commissioning 
and commercial operation of the facility.  
The requisite details, as per Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Listing 
Regulations and SEBI Circular HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 
January 30, 2026, are attached with this intimation as Annexure-I.
