Adani Data Networks to transfer 400 MHz spectrum to Bharti Airtel  
 
 
Ahmedabad, 22 April 2025 : Adani Data Networks Limited (ADNL), a wholly -
owned subsidiary of Adani Enterprises Ltd, has signed definitive agreements 
with Bharti Airtel Ltd and its subsidiary Bharti Hexacom Ltd to transfer the 
rights to use 400 MHz of spectrum in the 26 GHz band.  
 
The spectrum covers six telecom circles — Gujarat (100 MHz), Mumbai (100 
MHz), Andhra Pradesh (50 MHz), Rajasthan (50 MHz), Karnataka (50 MHz) and 
Tamil Nadu (50 MHz).  
 
The closing of the transaction is subject to customary statutory approvals.
