Dear Sir/Madam, 
This is in reference to the Outcome of Board Meeting held on July 29, 2025 for the 
approval of issuance of upto 1,43,80,000 (One Crores Forty-three Lacs Eighty 
Thousand) Equity Shares and upto 4,57,15,000 (Four Crores Fifty-seven Lacs Fifteen 
Thousand) Convertible Warrants on preferential basis and the said Outcome was filed 
with BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited (collectively "Stock 
Exchanges") on July 29, 2025. 
 
This corrigendum is being submitted to the Stock Exchanges regarding the disclosure 
made pursuant to the SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 
dated 11th November, 2024 regarding the pre-preferential shareholding of one of the 
proposed allottee i.e. Bhavini Ajay Shah holding 15,000 equity shares which was 
inadvertently & unintentionally missed out and revised post issue shareholding of 
proposed allottee(s) on fully diluted basis considering the 16,70,599 outstanding ESOPs.  
 
Further, we wish to inform you that the Company has locked-in the entire pre-
preferential shareholding held by Bhavini Ajay Shah in terms of the provisions of SEBI 
(Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018.  
 
Hence, the revised disclosure pursuant to SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with Master Circular No 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 11th November, 2024 is enclosed as 
Annexure-A . Except as detailed in the attached corrigendum, all other terms and 
contents of the outcome of the Board Meeting shall remain unchanged.  
 
Kindly take the same on your record.
