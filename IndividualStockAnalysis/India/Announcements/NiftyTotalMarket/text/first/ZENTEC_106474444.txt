PRESS & MEDIA RELEASE
Zen Technologies Secures Indian Patent for 60 mm Mortar SimulatorPR ES S  & MED IA R ELEAS E 1/2Hyderabad,  December 22, 2025:   Zen Technologies, a pioneer in defence training and 
simulation systems, announces that the Indian Patent Office has granted an Indian 
patent for its “60 m m Mortar Simulator”. 
This grant  marks  a key milestone  in Zen  Technologies’  innovation  journe y, taking  its 
portfolio  to 57 Indian  patents,  85 patents  worldwide,  and  the  8th patent  granted  in 
2025.  The  achievement  reinforces  the company ’s sustained  focus  on indigenous  R&D 
and intellectual property creation in advanced defence training technologies.
The patented  invention  delivers  a comprehensive,  end-to -end  solution  for training  on 
high-angled  firing  weapons,  including  mortar  systems.  It integrates  a realistic  weapon 
architecture  with  advanced  sensor  assemblies,  azimuth  and  elevation  control 
mechanisms,  recoil  simulation,  and  computer-generated  imagery  based  battlefield 
scenarios.  The  system  enables  accurate  replication  of operational  conditions  while 
allowing  safe,  indoo r, and  all-weather  training  without  dependence  on  live 
ammunition or field firing ranges.
The grant  of  this  patent  significantly  enhances  Zen  Technologies’  global 
competitiveness  and  export  potential.  With  defence  forces  worldwide  increasingly 
adopting simulation-based training to improve readiness and reduce risk, the patented 
high-angled  firing  weapon  simulator  is well  positioned  for  international  markets. 
Strong  IP protection  supports  Zen’s participation  in global  tenders,  defence 
collaborations,  offset  programs,  and  long-term  export  engagements.  This  patent 
further  strengthens  Zen  Technologies’  position  as a research-driven  defence 
technology  company  and  underscores  its commitment  to delivering  world-class, 
indigenous  training  solutions  that  meet  evolving  operational  requirements  of armed 
forces in India and abroad.
