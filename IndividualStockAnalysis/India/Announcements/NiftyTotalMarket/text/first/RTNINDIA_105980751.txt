RattanIndia Enterprises ’ Cargo Drone L15 Supplying Essentials to Flood 
Hit Andhra Pradesh  
 
New Delhi, September 6, 2024: The state of Andhra Pradesh is hit by massive floods , caused 
by torrential rains impacting thousands of acres of crops, power lines, damaged railway lines, 
submerged roads. Thousands of families are cut -off not able to receive essentials like milk, 
fresh water, food & other essentials, due to submerged roads.  
 
                                           
 
We are proud to announce that we deployed our L -Series cargo drones in the state of Andhra 
Pradesh to help the local authorities connect the last mile. Our L15 cargo drones did close to 
30 sorties a day, delivering milk packets, water bottles, food, vegetables, groceries and many 
more. Our drones were loaded with items in a basket, took -off from the nearby bridge and 
hovered over the building allowing the residents to collect  these items without stepping out 
of their homes.  
 
L-Series cargo drones can carry up to 20 kg payload, can travel up to 5  kms one way, having 
an endurance of up to 45  mts. L -Series can do operations in an auto -mode flying on a pre -
programmed route with limited pilot supervision. These drones are rugged and can withstand 
wind speeds and operate in tough terrains. L-series drones are the best technological aid 
during any disaster  management.  
 
Speaking on this occasion, Mrs.  Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises 
Limited  commented:  “RattanIndia’s mission is to solve problems, through technology that 
create sustainable value for the society. It pains me to see the suffering of our people during 
the recent floods, especially infants, kids & women as they are cut -off from receiving essentials 

    
                                                                         
due to sub -merged roads. In times of crisis, it is our duty to leverage our drone technology to 
provide swift and effective support during the disaster relief.  
Our drone’s role is the bridge the gap and ensure we connect the last mile, we  are glad that 
our L -Series cargo drones can do our bit in delivering essentials to the one in need. We are 
committed to standing by the communities affected by this disaster and doing everything we 
can to assist ”
