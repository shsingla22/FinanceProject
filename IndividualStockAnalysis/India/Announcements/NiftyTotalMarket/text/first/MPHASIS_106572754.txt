quarter ended December 31, 2025 , the matter was placed before the Board of Directors at its 
meeting held on March 27, 2026.  
 After deliberation, the Board took note of the following : 
 1. The selection /appointment  of Chairperson required careful consideration , given the role 
demands signiﬁcant competence, skill, experience , and seniority.  
 
2. No Board meeting was convened during the period from December 11, 2025, to January 6, 
2026. Accordingly,  there was no  requirement for a Chairperson to preside during this period . 
The ﬁrst board meeting following the appointment of Chairperson on January 7, 2026,  was 
held on January 22, 2026.  The governance operations remained unaﬀected and functioning of 
the Board was not impaired during the period.  
 
3. The Board further noted that the Company always elects a regular Non -Executive Chairperson  
and continuously maintains  compliance with Regulation 17 of SEBI Listing Regulations
