This is to inform you that the Bank has received a communication on May 21, 2024, from 
Reserve Bank of India (RBI) stating that RBI had imposed a monetary penalty of Rs. 5,000/- 
on the Bank on account of deficiencies observed during incognito visit by RBI in one of the 
Bank branches.  
 
This penalty has been imposed in accordance with Master Direction DCM (CC) No.G- 
3/03.44.01/2023-24 dated April 03, 2023 on “Scheme of Penalties for bank branches and 
Currency Chests for deficiency in rendering customer service to the members of public”. 
 
Information as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
