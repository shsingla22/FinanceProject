Dear Madam/Sir,  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations, we wish to inform you that the board of 
directors (“Board”) of Relaxo Footwear Limited (“ Company ”), at its meeting held today  i.e. Friday, 
November 13, 2026, has approved the proposal for setting up a joint venture with the Kenafric 
Group (defined below ) through Relaxo Kenafric Footwears Limited (“RKFL”), a company 
incorporated under the laws of Kenya, for undertaking the business of manufacturing, marketing, 
sale and distribution of footwear products in Kenya and such other agreed territories as 
contemplated under the definitive agreements.  
In this regard, the Board has, inter alia, approved the execution of the following definitive agreements: 
1. Share Subscription and Shareholders Agreement (“SSHA”):  To be entered into amongst 
the Company, Zarrar Holding Limited, Chehar Bharatkumar Shah and Mikul Nilesh Shah 
(collectively referred to as the “Kenafric Group”) and RKFL, setting out the terms of the joint 
venture, including the subscription to equity shares of RKFL, proposed shareholding structure, 
management and governance arrangements and respective rights and obligations of the shareholders. Pursuant thereto, the Company proposes to subscribe to equity s hares of RKFL 
for a cash consideration of up to ₹35.70 Crore (Rupees Thirty -Five Crore and Seventy Lakhs  
Only) , representing 51% of its issued and paid-up share capital on completion, while the Kenafric 
Group will collectively subscribe to equity shares representing the remaining 49% of its share 
capital. Upon completion of the transactions  contemplated under the SSHA , RKFL will become a 
subsidiary of the Company.  
2. Asset Transfer Agreement (“ATA”):  To be entered into amongst the Company, Kenafric 
Manufacturing Limited (“KML”) and RKFL, pursuant to which the Company proposes to transfer 
certain identified movable assets, including plant, machinery, equipment and tools, and assign/novate identified customer and supplier contracts relating to its footwear business to 
RKFL, against cash consideration payable by RKFL to the Company, in accordance with the 
terms of the agreement. 
3. Intellectual Property Licensing Agreement (“IP Licensing Agreement”)  and other 
ancillary agreements: To be entered into amongst the Company and RKFL, pursuant to which, 
the Company proposes to license the use of certain specified trademarks owned by it. in relation 
to the footwear business to RKFL for cash consideration. The Board has also approved the BSE Limited,
