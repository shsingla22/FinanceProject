Happiest Minds recognized among Top 100 India’s Best Workplaces™  for IT & 
IT-BPM 2024  
San Jose, London and Bengaluru, December  04, 2024:  Happiest Minds Technologies Limited 
(NSE: HAPPSTMNDS), a ‘Born Digital . Born Agile’, Mindful IT Company, today announced that it 
has been recognized among  the Top 100  India’s Best Workplaces ™ for IT  & IT-BPM 2024 by 
Great Place To Work® India.  
Great Place To Work® India honors  organizations that exemplify exceptional leadership and 
prioritize employee well-being. The Top 100 list highlights companies that stand out for their effective people practices and their commitment to fostering a culture of trust by acting on employee feedback.  
Joseph Anantharaju, Executive Vice Chairman, Happiest Minds, said, “We are happy to be 
honored among the Top 100 India’s Best Workplaces ™ for IT & IT-BPM 2024 by Great Place to 
Work®. Through our constant efforts to integrate new technologies and empower our people with 
the right skills, w e have been able to foster a workplace where both personal and organizational 
growth go hand in hand. ” 
Sachin Khurana, Chief People Officer, Happiest Minds,  said, “From a people perspective, this 
accolade celebrates our collective efforts to provide a nurturing, growth -oriented environment where 
everyone feels inspired to contribute and innovate. This reco gnition reflects our focus on ensuring 
that leadership is accessible  and supportive . We look forward to making more strides in promoting 
diversity and inclusion, which are key pillars of our organizational culture and success. ” 
With a focus on innovation and progress, Happiest Minds  has earned recognition through several 
prestigious awards, including Top 100 India’s Best Workplaces ™ for Women 2024,   Best Companies 
for Women in India in IT and Exemplar of Inclusion in the Most Inclusive Companies Index  by Avtar 
and Seramount,  WAW – Wellbeing at Workplaces Award 2024 and 50 Best Firms for Data Scientists 
to Work for 2024.
