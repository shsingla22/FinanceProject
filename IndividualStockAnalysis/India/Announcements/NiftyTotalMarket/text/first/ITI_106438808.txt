PRESS RELEASE  
ITI Limited joins Kerala Gov ernment  to launch  Kerala Sava ari 
2.0 
 
Palakkad /Bengaluru : ITI Limited, country’s first PSU post -independence and premier 
telecom manufacturing company has joined hands with the Gov ernment  of Kerala to 
launch the upgraded version of its online auto and cab booking platform, Kerala 
Sava ari 2.0 . The project was launched by the State  Minister for Labour Mr. Sivankyutty 
in Thiruvananthapuram on November 4, 2025 in the presence of Labour Commissioner 
Smt.  Safna Nazarudeen IAS, technical partners for the project, labour union leaders and 
the media representatives.  
 
Kerala Sava ari has already been made fully operational in Thiruvananthapuram and 
Kochi  cities . Kerala Sava ari will become a multi -modal transport system app by 
December 2025. This system, which will integrate metro, water metro, metro feeder 
buses, autos and cabs, will be a model for the whole of India . The scheme will be extended 
to other districts and cities so on after its full -fledged operation in Thiruvananthapuram 
and Kochi  cities . 
 
The Kerala Motor Workers Welfare Fund Board (KMWWFB), under the Labour 
Department, in association with the Government of Kerala, Police  Department , 
Transport, IT, Planning Board a nd other departments is implementing the project. Unlike 
other online platforms, Kerala Sava ari works on a subscription mode . This system of 
fixed subscription rates by the government ensures higher income for the drivers. The 
trial run s were satisfactoril y conducted in Thiruvananthapuram and Kochi cities from 
April 2025.  
 
The project has already onboard ed 23,000 drivers, conduct ed 3,60,000 trips and has 
earn ed Rs. 9.36 Cr ores  revenue by drivers with an average of 1200 daily trips in 
Thiruvananthapuram and  Kochi  cities . 

 
 
 
 
Kerala Sava ari aims to become a multi -mobility app rather than just an auto/taxi app. 
For this, the design has been completed which works in sync with the ticket booking in 
metro, water metro, tourism, temple ticketing, railway and pre -paid auto counter. 
Ambulances and goods vehicles can be integrated with this platform as per the rates fixed 
by the government. Speaking on the occasion Mr. Sivankyutty , Labour Minister , Kerala 
Government  said “ Kerala Sava ari is not just an app, it is a symbol of the Kerala 
government's co mmitment to ensuring worker welfare and safe travel at affordable rates 
for the public”.  
 
Speaking on the  occasion, Mr. Rajesh Rai, Chairman and Managing Director, ITI 
Limited said – “ITI Limited is delighted to partner with Government of Kerala to 
launch this commuter friendly online platform called Kerala Sava ari in Kerala.  Our 
Palakkad Unit has been helping the Kerala Government through various means like 
supplying driving licens e and registration certificates for the Transport Department, 
carrying out digitisation of records  for the Kerala Motor Workers Welfare Fund Board , 
supplying laptops to Kerala schools  and so on . This time I am glad that commuters 
across Kerala will be benefitted with this revamped app.”  
 
Mr. Rai added  –The shortcomings in the pilot project launched in 2022 have been 
addressed and the trial run has been conducted in Thiruvananthapuram and Kochi 
cities . ITI Palakkad Unit will provide continuous technical support to ensure smooth 
functioning of the app”.  
 
Kerala Sav aari is a fully government -owned initiative implemented under the Kerala 
Motor Workers Welfare Board, in collaboration with the Departments of Labour, Police,  
Transport, IT, and the Planning Board .
