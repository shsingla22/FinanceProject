Pune, 1  April  2025 : Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited (Star Health Insurance), India's largest 
retail health insurance company, has strengthened its presence in Mahara shtra with the inauguration of its  new 
Regional Office in Yerawada, Pune.  This expansion is part of Star Health' s ongoing efforts to deepen its presence 
in semi -urban and rural markets in the region , where healthcare financing remains  a critical need for millions of 
families. The company will also focus on enhancing  customer engagement and building  stronger relationships 
in the Pune region.  
Gracing the inauguration with his presenc e, Mr. Anand Roy, MD & CEO, Star Health Insurance , said , “Pune 
has always been a key market for us, and with the opening of our new Regional Office, we are further 
strengthening our commitment to the region. As Maharashtra continues to evolve as a hub for businesses and 
individuals seeking quality healthcare p rotection, our focus is on making health insurance more accessible and 
seamless. We are aligned with IRDAI’s vision of ‘Insurance for All’ and will continue working towards greater 
awareness and deeper penetration, ensuring that individuals and families ac ross Pune and beyond have the 
financial security they need in times of medical emergencies. With a strong local team and a customer -first 
approach, we are excited to be part of this journey of expanding health coverage and building a healthier, more 
secure  future for the region ” 
The newly inaugurated Regional office is  located at 13 to 16, Above KIA Showroom, 1st Floor, Yerawada, Pune 
– 411006 . It houses dedicated teams across key departments including Admin, Claims, Finance & Accounts, 
Hospital Relations, Learning & Development, Legal  and Sales. In Maharashtra  ex Mumbai , the Company has a 
current business portfolio of over Rs. 2,20 0 crore and is projected to grow by  20% in FY26 . The region is 
supported by a robust network of over 1,17 ,000 agents, many specifically trained to address the unique health 
insurance needs of rural communities. Star Heal th Insurance has insured over 32  lakh lives with health insur ance 
in Maharashtra and over 6.8  lakh lives in Pune  in FY25 (Apr -Dec’24) , with ambitious targets to increase rural 
coverage in the coming years.  In Pune, Star Health has a network of close to 47 0 hospitals, 20 branches and 360  
employees.  
Pune region plays a pivotal role in Star Health's rural expansion strategy, serving as a gateway to numerous 
villages and small towns across western Maharashtra.  The new Yerawada office will serve as a strategic hub for 
Star Health's outreach to  many of these under penetrated and underserved markets, including areas like 
 
Hinjewadi, Talegaon, Chakan, and other developing industrial corridors and townships and increase the health 
insurance penetration in smaller villages of Maval, Mulshi, Bhor, Vel he, and Junnar talukas, where health 
insurance penetration has traditionally been lower than in urban centers.  
With the launch of this new Regional Office, Star Health aims to enhance customer service and build closer, 
more personalized relationships with agents and policyholders across Pune, ensuring they fe el heard, valued, 
and cared for .
