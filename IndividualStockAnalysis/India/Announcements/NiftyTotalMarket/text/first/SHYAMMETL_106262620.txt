National, 05" June 2025: Shyam Metalics Foundation, the philanthropy arm of Shyam Metalics and Energy 
Ltd., one of India’s leading names in the steel industry, has been recently honoured with the CSR 
Contribution Award at the prestigious Edelgive-Hurun India Philanthropy Awards 2025 for its impactful 
and sustained efforts in driving community development through high-impact initiatives in health, 
education, livelihood opportunities, social infrastructure, and environmental resilience. The awards, 
presented by Hurun India, a leading global research, luxury publishing, and events group, are known for 
spotlighting India’s entrepreneurial and philanthropic excellence through credible data and insights. 
Tanishi Agarwal, Head- CSR, Branding & Corporate Affairs, representing Shyam Metalics Foundation, 
received the award on behalf of the Foundation during a distinguished ceremony held at the Four Seasons 
Hotel, Worli, Mumbai. 
Driven by a vision of inclusive growth, the foundation has touched over 25,000 lives and impacted 43 
villages across West Bengal and Odisha. With focused initiatives aligned to the UN Sustainable 
Development Goals (SDG) 2030, the Foundation has improved lives through targeted medical camps, 
comprehensive skill development programs for youth and women, and the construction of 32 sanitation 
units in tribal areas. The Foundation maintains an active presence in Jamuria and Mangalpur (West Bengal) 
and Sambalpur (Odisha), furthering community bonds through regular blood donation drives, 
environmental awareness campaigns, and holistic village development projects. 
Expanding on the social responsibility that is its core, Shyam Metalics Foundation makes a difference in 
four areas of focus: livelihood, education, healthcare, and sustainability. Initiatives such as Kalpviksha and 
Uday teach computer literacy, sewing, food cultivation, and jute and mushroom processing, along with 
building Self-Help Groups (SHGs). 
In the area of education, more than 1,600 rural children are impacted through tuition centres, school 
infrastructure development, and scholarships. The Shyam Metalics Football Academy, Eastern India- 
focused, identifies talented youth from economically and disadvantaged communities and develops them 
through professional training, all-round development, and facilitate competitive opportunities. 
Over 18,800 people have been provided with medical care through mobile clinics, homeopathy services, 
vaccination campaigns, and WASH (Water, Sanitation & Hygiene) awareness programs through the SEHAT 
initiative. The environmental efforts of the company involve organic farming, rainwater harvesting,

About Shyam Metalics and Energy Limited 
Shyam Metalics is a leading and fastest-growing integrated metal-producing company based in India 
primarily in the steel Industry in West Bengal, Odisha, Jharkhand and Madhya Pradesh with a focus on 
Long Steel Products, Ferro Alloys, Aluminium and Stainless Steel. The company got listed itself on the 
exchanges in 2021 and as on date of this press release possesses a market capitalization of Rs. 23,840 Cr. 
Spearheaded by Mr. B. Bhushan, Chairman and Managing Director, the company strives to deliver 
unparalleled quality through their customised value-added solutions to meet business requirements. 
Headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal, the company is amongst the largest producers of ferro alloys in 
terms of installed capacity in India (Source: CRISIL Report). 
The company has the ability to sell intermediate and final products across the steel value chain. Shyam 
Metalics is one of the leading players in terms of pellet capacity and one of the largest coals fired player 
in the sponge iron industry in terms of sponge iron capacity in India.
