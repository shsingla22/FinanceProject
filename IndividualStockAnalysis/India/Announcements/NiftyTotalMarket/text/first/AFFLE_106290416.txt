This is to inform that the Company has been granted another patent in India. This grant further builds upon 
our previously granted IPs that collectively strengthen the quality and effectiveness of our conversion -
driven CPCU model, as we further fortify our  AI-powered consumer platform stack.  
 
The patent’s subject area is “Method and system to detect advertisement fraud” and it is aimed at addressing 
the challenge of detecting and filtering out fraudulent activities in digital advertising, with particular focus 
on identifying traffic generated t hrough artificial or incentivized means. It introduces a real -time, machine 
learning -powered monitoring framework that analyzes user engagements in defined time intervals across a 
plurality of connected devices, including smartphones, smart/connected TVs, wearables etc. and across a 
diverse range of advertising formats including video ads.  
 
This machine learning based intelligent system fundamentally evaluates the quality of conversion rates by 
real time classification of events into high -conversion and low -conversion segments. These methods 
distinguish genuine user engagements from low -value  actions or suspicious activity, thereby enhancing 
precision and impact in conversion -driven, outcome -based strategies.  
 
This marks the 14th patent granted to us, as part of our growing IP portfolio comprising 36 patent 
applications filed to date. It reflects our continued focus on developing innovative technologies that deliver 
greater transparency and drive high -impact use r conversions for advertisers globally.
