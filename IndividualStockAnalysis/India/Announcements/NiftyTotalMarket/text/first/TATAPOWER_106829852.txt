Tata Power Signs MoU with BPCL to Develop 110 kV Power 
Infrastructure for its Landmark PRFCCU Project at Mumbai Refinery  
 
 
  
Mumbai October, 27, 2026:  Tata Power, one of India’s largest integrated power companies has 
signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (BPCL) 
for the development of 110 kV power infrastructure to meet the 90 MW power requirement of BPCL’s 
Petro Resid Fluidized Catalytic Cracking Unit (PRFCCU) project at its Mumbai Refinery.  
 
The PRFCCU project is a landmark initiative in India’s refining sector - the first project of its kind being 
executed in India, and a strategic milestone in BPCL’s journey towards higher -value product recovery 
and petrochemical diversification. With a processing capacity of 3 million metric tonnes per annum 
(MMTPA) and a project outlay of approximately ₹14,203 crore, it is BPCL’s second- largest project 
currently under execution and the largest ever undertaken at Mumbai Refinery. By enhancing 
domestic value addition, PRFCCU will contribute to India’s energy security and the national vision of 
Aatmanirbhar Bharat . 
Under the MoU, Tata Power will provide 110 kV connectivity and undertake the design and 
development of the associated power infrastructure required to meet the project’s power requirement. 
The infrastructure development entails a significant investment in strengthening power connectivity 
and transmission infrastruct ure.  This infrastructure development is envisaged to be completed over 
approximately 24 months with a combined estimated outlay of approx. ₹ 250 crore for Tata Power 
and BPCL, subject to applicable approvals and project milestones.  
Beyond meeting BPCL’s im mediate power requirements, the initiative will strengthen Tata Power’s 
transmission network in the Trombay area, supporting anticipated growth in electricity demand from 
large industrial consumers and other concentrated- load developments in this strategic ally important 
industrial zone.  

 
As part of its long -term plan to build a robust, reliable and future- ready power network for Mumbai, 
Tata Power also plans to strengthen its transmission infrastructure at Trombay through the 
development of a 400 kV transmis sion facility over the next five years. This planned augmentation is 
expected to enhance power evacuation capability and support reliable grid availability for Mumbai’s 
industrial and commercial ecosystem.  
Through this strategic collaboration, Tata Power a nd BPCL reaffirm their commitment to strengthening 
Mumbai’s energy infrastructure and enhancing energy security. It also reinforces Tata Power’s 
commitment of investing in transmission & distribution infrastructure to support long term industrial 
growth & energy self -reliance.  
 
About Tata Power:  
The Tata Power Company Limited, a vertically integrated power company and part of the Tata Group, India’s largest 
multinational business conglomerate, has built a diversified portfolio across the entire power value chain, with its total 
operational and under -construction  capacity surpassing 26 GW. This includes approximately 17. 7 GW of clean and green 
energy capacity (including projects under construction) and around 8.8  GW of thermal generation capacity, ensuring a 
balanced and resilient energy mix. The Company also has a strong transmission and distribution footprint, with over 7,800  
circuit kilometres of transmission lines, including pipeline proje cts, and serves  over 13 million customers across its 
distribution businesses, making it one of India’s largest private power distribution companies.  Further strengthening its 
leadership in future-ready energy solutions, Tata Power has established 4.9 GW of  integrated solar cell and module 
manufacturing capacity, signed 2.8 GW of pumped hydro storage projects, and expanded its EV charging network to over 
7,000 public charging points across 7 17 cities and towns in India.  As a pioneer in India’s clean energy transition, Tata 
Power remains committed to achieving Net Zero by 2045 and continues to partner with public and private stakeholders to 
deliver reliable, sustainable, and technology -led energy solutions across the country.
