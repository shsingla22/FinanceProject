Dear Madam / Sir, 
 
We refer to our earlier disclosure dated 10th January 2026, wherein we informed you about the 
appeal filed by Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Company Limited (“MSEDCL”) before 
the Appellate Tribunal for Electricity (“APTEL”) against the order dated 24th December 2025 
passed by the Hon’ble Maharashtra Electricity Regulatory Commission (“MERC”). MERC, by the 
said order, had rejected the contention of MSEDCL on applicability of direction issued by the 
Central Government under Section 11 of Electricity Act, 2003 to the Company’s unit at Ratnagiri, 
and directed MSEDCL to pay tariff as per the provisions of the Power Purchase Agreement dated 
23rd February 2010 with applicable Late Payment Surcharge.  
 
We hereby inform you that MSEDCL had filed a civil appeal before the Hon’ble Supreme Court 
challenging the interim order passed by APTEL rejecting its application to stay the order passed 
by MERC. The Hon’ble Supreme Court by its order dated 30th April 2026 (uploaded on Supreme 
Court website on 5th May 2026) disposed of the civil appeal by (a) directing MSEDCL to make 
interim part payment of Rs 250 crores to the Company; and (b) requesting APTEL to re-hear the 
interim application filed by MSEDCL.
