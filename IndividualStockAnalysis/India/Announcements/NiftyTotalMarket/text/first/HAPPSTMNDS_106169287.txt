Happiest Minds Wins the WOW Workplace Award for IT, ITeS & GCC  
Bengaluru,  San Jose and London, March 10, 2025 : Happiest Minds Technologies Limited  (NSE: 
HAPPSTMNDS), a ‘Born Digital . Born Agile’, Mindful IT Company, today announced that it has been 
honored with the WOW Workplace Award for IT, ITeS & GCC . This recognition affirms the 
company's sustained dedication to fostering an innovative, collaborative, and employee-centric 
workplace environment.    
The WOW Workplace Award is given to organizations that possess a high-performance culture and 
foster a sense of belongingness. This award is a testament to Happiest Minds' strategic focus on 
creating a culture that prioritizes employee well-being, professional development and inclusivity.  
 
Joseph Anantharaju, Executive Vice Chairman , Happiest Minds  said, “From inception, Happiest 
Minds has strived to create a people-centric culture where all Happiest Minds feel valued, supported, 
and inspired to grow. This award from WOW adds to several other recognitions and reinforces the 
company's position as a leader in cultivating a workplace where talent thrives and innovation 
flourishes.”  
Sachin Khurana, Chief People Officer, Happiest Minds said, "For us, being mindful is not just a 
philosophy; it is the very fabric of how we operate. Our foundation is mindful engagement. We believe 
workplaces thrive on gratitude and presence, exemplified by our 'Applaud' program, a daily narrative 
of appreciation for every contribution. Our 'Mindfulness Matters' initiative, fostering present-moment 
awareness, has strengthened team cohesion. This WOW Workplace Award reflects these stories of 

 
connection and mindful presence. It reaffirms our commitment to building a workplace where every 
Mindful Maker is truly seen, not just valued."  
The company has previously been acknowledged for its commitment to diversity, equity, inclusion, 
and belongingness (DEIB), including Top 100 India’s Best Workplaces™ for Women 2024, Top 100 
India’s Best Workplaces™ for IT & IT-BPM 2024, 2024 Avtar & Seramount Best Companies for Women 
in India in IT and Exemplar of Inclusion in the Most Inclusive Companies Index, WAW – Wellbeing at 
Workplaces Award 2024 at the HR Anexi Conclave and AIM 50 Best Firms for Data Scientists to Work 
for 2024.  
Happiest Minds remains dedicated to advancing its workplace culture through ongoing initiatives 
that promote diversity, inclusion, and employee empowerment. These initiatives underline the 
company's commitment to attracting and retaining top talent and driving business success.
