The MCA had notified provisions relating to unpaid / unclaimed dividend under Sections 124 and 125 of the 
Act and Investor Education and Protection Fund (Accounting, Audit, Transfer and Refund) Rules, 2016. As 
per these Rules, dividends which are not encashed / claimed by the shareholder for a period of seven 
consecutive years shall be transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF) Authority. The 
IEPF Rules mandate the companies to transfer the shares of shareholders whose dividends remain unpaid / 
unclaimed for a period of seven consecutive years to the demat account of IEPF Authority. Members, who 
have not yet claimed/ encashed the dividends are requested to encash / claim their respective divi dends.
