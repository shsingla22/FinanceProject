Prestige Group Completes Three Landmark Projects in Mumbai, Delivering Over 800 Units 
Across 2.8 Million Sq. Ft. 
Mumbai, 28th May 2025 – Prestige Group, one of India’s leading real estate developers, 
proudly announces the successful completion and inauguration of its first three landmark 
projects in Mumbai — Siesta at The Prestige City, Mulund, Prestige Jasdan Classic, 
Mahalaxmi, and Prestige Turf Tower, also in Mahalaxmi . These developments collectively 
span 2.8 million square feet and mark the Group’s significant debut delivery in the city since 
its Mumbai launch in 2022. 
The Group will be handing over nearly 700 premium residences and 130 office units in the 
coming months, reinforcing its commitment to timely delivery and exceptional quality. 
Siesta at The Prestige City, Mulund 
Siesta marks the first residential phase of The Prestige City, a large-scale, mixed-use integrated 
township located at Yogi Hills, Mulund. This premium 54-storey tower comprises 462 
exquisitely designed sky-lounge residences. A signature multi-level clubhouse is integrated 
into the development, offering an extensive suite of lifestyle amenities for families. 
Prestige Jasdan Classic, Mahalaxmi 
Situated in one of Mumbai’s most sought-after locations, Prestige Jasdan Classic is a boutique 
luxury residential development spread across 2 acres. The 45-storey tower houses 233 
elegantly crafted residences. Its residents will enjoy a curated lifestyle with facilities that 
include an outdoor swimming pool, children’s play area, banquet hall, state-of-the-art 
gymnasium, badminton and squash courts, and a multipurpose arena. 
Both projects showcase meticulously planned interiors, premium finishes, and offer residents 
expansive homes with panoramic city views — creating refined living experiences in the heart 
of Mumbai. 
Prestige Turf Tower, Mahalaxmi 
In addition to the residential offerings, Prestige Group has also launched Prestige Turf Tower, 
a Grade A commercial development overlooking the Mahalaxmi Race Course. The project 
adds to the growing footprint of Prestige Group in the city’s premium commercial real estate 
segment.
