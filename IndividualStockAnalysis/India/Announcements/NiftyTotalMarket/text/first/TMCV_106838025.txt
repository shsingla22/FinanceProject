Dear Sirs, 
We were appointed as statutory auditors of Tata Motors Limited (‘the Company’) (formerly known 
as TML Commercial Vehicles Limited) in the first AGM held on 26 September 2025 for a period of 5 years until the conclusion of the sixth AGM to be held in 2030.   
We have completed the audit of the interim standalone condensed financial statements and financial 
results and limited review of the interim consolidated condensed financial statements and financial 
results of the Company for the quarter ended 30 September 2026 and issued our reports dated 23 
October 2026. 
We refer to our recent discussions with the management, those charged with governance and the 
subsequent communication dated 30 October 2026  received from the Company in respect of the proposed change of statutory auditors of the Company.  
We understand that the Company’s  acquisition of Iveco Group N.V . (“Iveco”) is getting completed 
today.  We are informed by the Company that post completion of the acquisition, Iveco is expected 
to become a subsidiary of the Company and the financial statements of Iveco will be consolidated 
with the consolidated financial statements of the Company, and for synergies and maintaining consistency in the audit process with the audit firm of Iveco and the statutory auditors of the Company, the Company has expressed its intention to align the auditors within the group post the 
above acquisition. 
Based on the above and as mutually agreed with the Company, we hereby resign as the statutory 
auditors of the Company with immediate effect. 
Please find attached completed Annexure A, with regard to the information to be obtained by the 
Company from the auditors for the resignation as required by Section V-D of SEBI Master circular 
no. SEBI/HO/49/14/14(7) 2025-CFD-PoD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30 January 2026, pertaining to 
‘Resignation of statutory auditors from listed entities and their material subsidiaries'. Annexure B
