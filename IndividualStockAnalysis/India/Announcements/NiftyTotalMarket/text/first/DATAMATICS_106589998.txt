Mumbai Metro Lines 2B & 9 Go Live with Datamatics’ AFC 
and Mobile Ticketing Systems  
15th April 2026, Mumbai:  Datamatics, a global Digital Technologies, Operations, and 
Experiences company, announced that it is privileged to be associated with  Phase 1 of  
Mumbai Metro line 2B and 9 as implementing partner of  Automatic Fare Collection 
(AFC) and mobile ticketing systems . The  AFC system  has provided for faster entry -exit 
and auto fare deduction. The system is suitable for QR coded ticket as well as common 
mobility card. Phase 1 of both these lines was inaugurated on April 8, 2026, by 
Honorable Chief Minister of Maharashtra, Shri. Devendr a Fadnavis , and Hon orable 
Deputy Chief Minister of Maharashtra, Shri. Eknath Shinde . 
Datamatics has also implemented the AFC solution for the Mumbai Metro lines  2A and 
7. 
Commenting on the development, Rahul Kanodia, Vice Chairman and CEO, 
Datamatics, said , "We take immense pride in our continued association with Mumbai 
Metro Lines 2A and 7, and now with Lines 2B and 9. Through our advanced AFC and 
mobile ticketing systems, we are happy to enable faster, smarter, and more seamless  
mobility for the city. Being headquartered in Mumbai makes this association 
particularly special for us as these projects are positively impacting millions of 
Mumbaikars. " 
Datamatics has successfully executed over 30 AFC projects globally, including in high-
traffic cities such as  London, New York, Melbourne, Hong Kong, and  Dubai. Datamatics 
also implemented its AFC solution for the Memphis Area Transit Authority (MATA) in  
Memphis, USA. In India, Datamatics  has successfully executed AFC solution on a 
turnke y basis for the Mumbai Metro, Delhi - Meerut RRTS, Kolkata Metro, Pune Metro, 
Lucknow Metro, and Shri Mata Vaishnav Devi shrine cable car in Jammu.
