CHALET HOTELS LIMITED REPORTS  STRONG  Q2 FY27 RESULTS  
CORE EBITDA GROWS 13% YoY; MARGINS EXPAND 11 0 BPS 
LEISURE PORTFOLIO DELIVERS STRONG GROWTH; SECOND ATHIVA HOTEL OPENS  
Mumbai  | November  04, 202 6: Chalet Hotels Limited  announces results for the second  quarter ending 
September 30, 202 6. 
 
Chalet Hotels Limited reported an 11% year-on-year increase in income from its core hospitality and annuity 
businesses to INR 5,110 million in Q2 FY27. EBITDA from core operations (excluding residential) grew 1 3% to 
INR 2,274 million, with margins expanding by 11 0 basis points to 44.5%.  
 
Key Highlights for Q 2 FY27: 
• Total Income  from core operations  (ex-Resi)  at INR 5,110  Mn, up 11% as compared to Q 2FY26  
• EBITDA  from core operations  (ex-Resi)  at INR 2,274 Mn up 13% as compared to Q 2FY26  
• EBITDA Margin from core operations  (ex-Resi)  at 44.5%, up 110 bps as compared to Q 2FY26  
• Consolidated PAT  at INR 2,163  Mn, up 40% as compared to Q2FY26  
• Hospitality Segment Performance:  
o RevPAR up by 7% YoY to INR 8,702 Mn; leisure portfolio performing exceptionally well  
o Revenue at INR 4,115 Mn, up by 8% from Q 2FY26  
o EBITDA at INR 1,691 Mn, up by 11% from Q 2FY26  
• Commercial Real Estate (Rental/Annuity) Performance:  
o Occupancy at 91%  
o Monthly revenue run -rate moving up to INR 300 Mn in Sep’26 – up from INR 2 90 Mn for Jun’26 
o Revenue at INR 901 Mn, up by 22% from Q 2FY26  
o EBITDA at INR 750 MN, up by 23% from Q 2FY26   
• Rebranded business hotel at Navi Mumbai as ATHIVA® Pulse, Navi Mumbai – Autograph 
Collection  
 
Speaking on the financial results, Shwetank Singh, MD & CEO, Chalet Hotels Limited said,  “Our Q2 
performance reflects the strength of our hospitality portfolio and our continued focus on driving operational 
efficiencies. Despite reduced air connectivity and higher airfares impacting certain business travel markets, we 
delivered an 11.3% YoY gr owth in ADR and a 7.2% increase in RevPAR. Our leisure properties performed 
particularly well, delivering 34% RevPAR growth, supported by improvements in both occupancy and room rates.  
Overall , the business continued to demonstrate healthy growth, contributing to improved operating margins. We 
are also making  encouraging progress across our brand portfolio, with the launch of ATHIVA Pulse in Navi 
Mumbai. The initial response  from the guests  has been positive, reinforcing our confidence in its potential.  
With Taj Delhi International Airport and CIGNUS II, Powai progressing towards completion, and an expanding 
ATHIVA pipeline, we are well -positioned for our next phase of growth. Our strong balance sheet and disciplined 
approach to development give us the co nfidence to capitalise on the long -term opportunities in Indian hospitality.”

About  Chalet Hotels Limited  
 
Chalet Hotels Limited (CHL), part of K Raheja Corp, is an asset -anchored owner -operator and developer 
of high -end hotels and luxury resorts in India. Its portfolio spans 11 operating hotels and resorts (3,389 
keys) across globally recognized hospitality br ands — JW Marriott, The Westin, Marriott , and Novotel — 
with ~ 2,036 rooms under development with brands such as The Ritz-Carlton, Taj, Hyatt Regency and 
more. This is complemented by an expanding commercial real estate platform, growing from 2.4 million to 
3.3 million square feet.  
CHL has also launched Athiva®, its homegrown premium lifestyle hotel brand, embodying assurance -first 
hospitality, wellness by design, and joyfully local experiences . 
CHL is Great Place to Work -Certified ™ seven  years  running , ranking No. 8 among India’s Great Mid -
Size Workplaces . It also ranks No. 2 globally in the S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment 
(CSA) for Hotels, Resorts & Cruise Lines (score 82; 27/02/2026). Additionally, it is the first hospitality 
company worldwide to join RE100, EP100, and EV100, having succes sfully achieved its EV100 
commitment in 2025.
