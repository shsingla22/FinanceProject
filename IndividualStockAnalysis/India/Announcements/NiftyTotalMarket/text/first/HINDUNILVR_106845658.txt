Sub:  Postal Ballot for Revision in remuneration payable to Non -Executive Directors  
 
It is hereby informed that the Board has decided to seek the approval of the Members of the Company, 
through Postal Ballot, for revision of the annual aggregate limit of remuneration payable to the  
Non-Executive Directors, for a period of five years commencing from April 1, 2026 to March 31, 2031, 
to an amount not exceeding ₹15 crore per annum or 1% (one percent) of the Net Profits of the Company 
per annum, or such other percentage as may be specified under the Companies Act, 2013 from time to 
time, computed in the manner prescribed under Section 198 thereof, whichever is lowe r. 
 
The Members, whose names appear in the Register of Members/ List of Beneficial Owners as on Friday, 
6th November 2026  being the cut -off date are entitled to vote on the Resolution set forth in the Notice.  
 
The Board has appointed Mr. S.N. Ananthasubramanian, Practicing Company Secretary, as the 
Scrutinizer for conducting the said Postal Ballot process.  
 
The tentative Calendar of Events for the Postal Ballot process is attached for your reference.  
 
You are requested to take the above information on record.
