PRESS RELEASE
Financial Results for Q2FY27
1
Mumbai,  October  27, 2026 : A meeting  of the Board  of Directors  of Tata Capital  Limited  (TCL)  
was held today  to consider  and approve  the unaudited  consolidated  financial  results  for the 
quarter  and half year ended  September  30, 2026 .
Commenting  on the performance,  Mr. Rajiv  Sabharwal,  Managing  Director  & CEO, Tata 
Capital  said:
“We achieved  a significant  milestone  during  the quarter,  with our loan book  crossing  ₹3 trillion,  
underscoring  the scale,  diversification  and resilience  of our franchise . Our AUM  stood  at ₹3,08,030 
crore,  up 26% year-on-year, while  PAT grew  51% year-on-year to ₹1,660 crore . Excluding  Motor  
Finance,  AUM  grew  32% year-on-year. Asset  quality  remained  stable,  reflecting  our continued  focus  
on portfolio  quality,  prudent  underwriting  and effective  collections .”  
“Artificial  intelligence  remains  a core strategic  priority  for the organization . During  the quarter,  we 
continued  to scale  AI-led capabilities  across  onboarding,  operations,  underwriting,  servicing  and 
collections . More  than 70% of retail  applications  are now processed  through  AI, while  AI-assisted  
underwriting  has been  adopted  across  95%+ of Personal  Loan  and Business  Loan  cases,  improving  
processing  turnaround  time by over 39% and enhancing  underwriting  productivity . Our AI-powered  
operations  platform  has achieved  adoption  across  over 95% of Business  Loan,  Two-Wheeler  and 
Micro  Finance  workflows,  delivering  meaningful  gains  in productivity,  turnaround  time and 
operating  efficiency . In customer  servicing  and collections,  AI agents  now handle  a significant  share  
of customer  interactions  and early -bucket  collections,  enabling  faster  response  times,  wider  
customer  reach  and lower  servicing  cost. These  capabilities  are helping  us enhance  customer  
experience  and build  a more  scalable  growth  platform  for the future .”
On the macroeconomic  environment,  he added,  “While  global  uncertainties  and evolving  market  
conditions  warrant  continued  vigilance,  India’s  strong  economic  fundamentals,  structural  growth  
drivers,  and rising  credit  penetration  continue  to create  significant  long-term opportunities . We 
remain  focused  on pursuing  growth  in a disciplined  manner,  while  maintaining  strong  risk 
management  and balance  sheet  resilience .”Loan Book Crossed ₹3 Trillion Milestone

About  Tata Capital
Tata Capital  Limited  (TCL)  is the flagship  financial  services  company  of the Tata Group  and a subsidiary  of 
Tata Sons  Private  Limited . Tata Capital  is a listed  company  and registered  with the Reserve  Bank  of India  
(RBI) as a Non-Banking  Financial  Company  – Investment  and Credit  Company  (NBFC -ICC) and has been  
categorized  as an Upper  Layer  NBFC  under  the RBI’s  Scale  Based  Regulatory  Framework . TCL conducts  its 
lending  business  together  with its material  subsidiary  Tata Capital  Housing  Finance  Limited  (TCHFL),  
which  is registered  with the National  Housing  Bank  (NHB)  as a Housing  Finance  Company  (HFC)  and is 
classified  as a Middle  Layer  NBFC  under  the RBI’s  Scale  Based  Regulations .
TCL offers  a comprehensive  suite  of over 25 lending  products,  catering  to a diverse  customer  base 
comprising  salaried  and self-employed  individuals,  entrepreneurs,  small  businesses,  small  and medium  
enterprises,  and corporates . In addition  to its lending  offerings,  TCL also distributes  third -party  products  
such as insurance  and credit  cards,  provides  wealth  management  services,  and acts as a sponsor  and 
investment  manager  to private  equity  funds . TCL is rated  “AAA  with stable  outlook”  from  each of CRISIL,  
ICRA  and CARE . It has an international  rating  of BBB (Stable)  by both  S&P Global  Ratings  and Fitch . As of 
September  30, 2026  TCL’s  distribution  network  spans  1,515 branches  across  26 states  and union  
territories . For more  information  visit: https ://www .tatacapital .com/
