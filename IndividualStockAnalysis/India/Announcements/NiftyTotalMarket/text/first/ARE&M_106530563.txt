Sub: Board Comments on fine levied by the Exchange. 
 
The letter received from the National Stock Exchange of India Limited dated December 16, 2025 
pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 
2024, was placed before the Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting held on February 
11, 2026. The Board noted the below: 
 
The Company had filed the Related Party Transactions (RPT) XBRL as part of the Integrated 
Financials XBRL with BSE within the stipulated timeline on November 6, 2025, i.e., on the date of 
the Board Meeting. However, due to an inadvertent oversight, the Company assumed that the 
said filing was API-integrated and that filing with one Stock Exchange would suffice for 
compliance with both Stock Exchanges.  
 
Upon realization, the Company promptly filed the requisite XBRL with NSE on November 7, 2025, 
resulting in a delay of one day. The Company has paid the fine as stipulated by NSE and has 
also submitted a waiver application, explaining the bona fide nature of the delay, with no intent to 
deviate from the applicable regulatory framework. Further, NSE vide an email dated January 2, 
2026 has informed about extension of single filing system for Integrated filing (financials) XBRL 
(i.e henceforth filing on one stock exchange will suffice). 
 
While noting that the delay was unintentional and due to inadvertent oversight, the Board advised 
the strengthening of internal compliance monitoring and review mechanisms to avoid recurrence. 
The Company reaffirms its continued commitment to comply with all applicable laws and 
regulatory requirements in letter and spirit and confirms that necessary measures have been 
undertaken to further strengthen its compliance processes. 
 
This is for your kind information.
