PRESS RELEASE  
 
DIVERSIFIED BRANDSCAPE POWERS IHCL TO A 670- HOTEL PORTFOLIO  
CLOCKS 47 SIGNINGS AND 20 HOTEL OPENINGS IN H1 FY2027  
 
 
MUMBAI, OCTOBER 12, 2026: Indian Hotels Company (IHCL) , India’s largest hospitality company, continues its 
growth momentum with the signing of 47 hotels in the first half of FY2027 reach ing a portfolio  of 670 hotels with 
an industry leading pipeline of  280 hotels. In H1, the company also opened 20 hotels across its brandscape.  
 
Ms. Suma Venkatesh, Executive Vice President -  Real Estate & Development, IHCL  said, “With a strong 
momentum of 47 signings and 20 openings in H1 FY2027 , IHCL is on track to achieve its guidance under 
Accelerate 2030 of  a 700-hotel  portfolio ahead of time. D riven by a diversified strategy across brands, markets 
and contract types , this growth underscor es IHCL’s scalable and resilient hospitality ecosystem.  With a portfolio  
mix of capital- light and capital- heavy assets, IHCL drives sustainable and profitable growth through the operating 
leverage of owned and leased hotels and a growing high -margin fee -based business. ” 
 She added, “With ten hotel  brands , reflective  of India’s heterogeneous landscape, IHCL’s H1 signings are well 
represented across segments from 4 Taj  hotels , a Brij and an Atmantan in luxury to driving scale in  the mid -scale 
with 19 signings in Ginger and Tree of Life. Through our openings we increased our presence in key lodging 
markets of Mumbai, Gurugram, Goa  and Hyderabad and forayed into emerging destinations of Kandla, 
Ayodhya, Tadoba, Kota, Bokaro  and Jorhat  in India . Enhancing our global presence , we extended the Taj  
footprint with the opening of hotels in Frankfurt  and Kruger  in South Africa , taking ou r operating hotels to 390. ”

About The Indian Hotels Company Limited  
The Indian Hotels Company Limited (IHCL) and its subsidiaries bring together a group of brands and 
businesses that offer a fusion of warm Indian hospitality and world -class service. These include Taj – the 
iconic brand for the most discerning travellers and ranked as  World’s Strongest Luxury Hotel Brand 
2026   and India’s Strongest Brand 2026   as per  Brand Finance ; Claridges Collection , a curated set of 
boutique luxury hotels merging elegance with historical charm;  Brij, an experiential leisure 
offering;  Atmantan, one of India’s leading integrated wellness destinations;  SeleQtions , a named collection 
of hotels;  Gateway , full- service hotels designed to be your gateway to exceptional destinations;  Vivanta , 
sophisticated upscale hotels;  Tree of Life, private escapes in tranquil settings and  Ginger, which is 
revolutionising the mid -scale segment.  
 Incorporated by the founder of the Tata Group, Jamsetji Tata , the Company opened its first hotel –  The Taj 
Mahal Palace , in Bombay in 1903. IHCL has a portfolio of 670  hotels inclu ding 280 in the pipeline globally 
across 4 continents, 15 countries and over 300  locations. The Indian Hotels Company Limited (IHCL) is India’s 
largest hospitality company by market capitalization.
