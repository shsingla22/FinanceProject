LEMON  TREE  HOTELS  TO ENTER  THE  HEART  OF NEW  DELHI  WITH  
AURIKA,  NEHRU  PLACE,  NEW  DELHI  
Letter of Award for prime land in Nehru Place, New Delhi  by DDA  
 
New Delhi, August 1 5, 2025  – Lemon Tree Hotels Limited is pleased to 
announce that its material subsidiary Fleur Hotels Limited has been declared 
as the Successful  Bidder vide Letter of Award dated 14.08.2025 in e-auction  
conducted by Delhi Development Authority (“DDA”) in respect of prime land 
parcel admeasuring 2.2 acres in the heart of National Capital  located  at Nehru 
Place for development and operations of a 5 Star Hotel. The said Hotel shall 
be developed as “Aurika, Nehru Place”, which will mark the debut of our upper 
upscale brand in the National Capital  Region.  
 
Aurika, Nehru Place  is set to become one of Delhi’s largest hotels with 500+ 
keys, and will offer elegant rooms and suites, signature dining, expansive 
banqueting, and best-in-class leisure facilities. Designed on the lines of Aurika, 
Mumbai SkyCity , it will blend grandeur with a distinctive Delhi flair, catering 
to both sophisticated business travellers and leisure guests.  
 
This landmark development builds on Lemon Tree’s already robust presence 
across the NCR, further strengthening its position as a key player in one of 
India’s most dynamic hospitality markets.  
 
Highlights:  
• NCR Debut  – Aurika enters Delhi, expanding the upper upscale 
portfolio.  
• Scale & Impact  – 500+ keys, among the city’s largest.  
• Prime Address  – Located in the heart of Nehru Place, with seamless 
citywide connectivity.  
 
Mr. Patanjali Keswani, Chairman & Managing Director, Lemon Tree Hotels, 
said: "Delhi has always been a strategic focus for us. Aurika’s entry 
will deliver a luxury experience that’s about living, not just staying – 
a landmark in style, comfort, and service for the region."  
 
                                                                          
 
 
 
Construction begins shortly, reinforcing Lemon Tree Hotels’ commitment to 
expanding in India’s most vibrant markets with experiences that inspire, 
service that delights, and spaces that remain unforgettable.  
 
About Lemon Tree Hotels Limited:  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India’s leading hospitality companies, 
catering to a wide range of customers  – from value -conscious travellers to premium 
business and leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels & Resorts, 
Lemon Tree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys Select, and Keys 
Lite – the group offers experiences across u pper upscale, upscale, upper midscale, 
midscale, leisure, wildlife, and spiritual segments.  
 
Serving over 1.5 million guests annually, LTHL operates 110+ hotels across 7 5+ cities 
in India and abroad, with a growing pipeline of 120+ upcoming properties. From 
metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad to tier II & III cities 
such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, and Indore – and with an international presence in 
Dubai, Bhutan, and Nepal – Lemon Tree Hotels delivers exceptional comfort, 
consistent quality, and a warm, refreshing experience.  
 
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 230+ properties 
(operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for both business 
and leisure travellers.
