New Delhi, 1st October 2025: Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -
growing mid -scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the opening of its 
latest property,  Lemon Tree Premier, Navi Mumbai . 
 
This managed hotel features 67 well-appointed rooms that delight guests with 
impeccably clean interiors, efficient service  and an affordable value proposition. It 
also has a multi -cuisine coffee shop  – Citrus Café – along with a banquet, a 
meetin g room, a spa, salon, swimming p ool and  a well -equipped fitness center to 
keep you feeling fresh -as-a-lemon.  With this opening, the company also marks 
the milestone of Lemon Tree’s operational inventory reaching 11,000 rooms.

Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India’s leading hospitality companies,
