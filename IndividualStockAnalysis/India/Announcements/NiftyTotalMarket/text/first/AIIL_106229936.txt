the total sale shares (under the Agreement) now represent 31.85% of the voting rights in ISARC (as 
calculated on Expanded Paid Up Share Capital as calculated on a Fully Diluted Basis ), and (b)  the total 
purchase consideration payable under the Agreement has been revised to Rs. 1,19,32,42,500/ - (Indian

Company will be making an investment of Rs. 3,12,50,42,500 /- (Indian Rupees three hundred twelve crore fifty
