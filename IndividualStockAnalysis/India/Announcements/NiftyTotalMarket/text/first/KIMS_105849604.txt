Clinical  achievements  during  the Quarter :  
 
KIMS SECUNDERABAD  
• A Zambian family sought medical help from KIMS for their boy of 14 years battling severe Sickle 
cell disease. His seven year old sister bravely agreed to donate her Bone Marrow to rescue her 
brother. The transplant was successfully done to the delight of the family. The procedure 
becomes not only a medical milestone but also a profound testimony to the power of familial 
bonds and selflessness.  
• Racing against time, Doctors at KIMs Se cunderabad saved a patient who was rushed from Oman 
suffering from a severe abdominal aortic aneurysm. There was no time to be lost. The team of 
doctors developed a unique treatment plan that involves a procedure called aortic aneurysm 
stenting with bilate ral renal artery fenestration stenting. The surgery was a true test of surgical 
skills as every move had to be executed with precision as even a minor mistake could have 
catastrophic consequences. The patient made full recovery following the surgery and le ft for their 
homeland.  
KIMS ICON VIZAG  
• A rare case of Takayasu’s    arteritis also known as pulseless disease was successfully treated at 
KIMS ICON. It typically presents in young females between the ages of 11 and 30, and it is more 
prevalent in certain re gions like India, Japan, Southeast Asia and Mexico. The 30 year old woman 
attended a wedding where she had a brain stroke resulting in impairment of right leg and hand 
and speech also. Proper diagnosis and timely treatment saved that patient.  
• A 34 week Pre term baby girl, weighing only 1.3 Kgs, has undergone a complex open heart 
surgery at KIMS ICON Vizag. The procedure lasted for over eight hours and involved directing 
and rerouting the pulmonary veins, which were draining abnormally to the liver and the le ft side 
of the heart. There is high mortality rate world over in preterm small weight babies who undergo 
complex cardiac surgery. However, this, one of the smallest -weight preterm babies in the Telugu 
states has successfully undergone surgery with positive  outcomes and discharged from hospital.  
KIMS KURNOOL  
• A young boy of four years had an accumulation of nearly half a litter pus around his heart, which 
made it difficult for him to breathe. The child was fully cured and discharged following surgery. It 
was done free of cost under Arogya Sri Scheme.  
• A ten year old boy was treated for dense deposit disease  which is a rare disease that affects 
one child in one lakh population. It affects the Kidney functioning. Only 9 Paediatric patients with 
Dense Deposit Dise ase have been reported from South India in the literature and this is the first 
case in AP. Previously, such cases could be diagnosed only in Metros like Hyderabad and 
Bangalore. Now these facilities are available at KIMS hospital Kurnool, which is a small  town.  
• A 48 year old women was brought to KIMS Kurnool with a large potato size tumor in the left side 
of the brain that is responsible for speech and memory. It was a difficult scenario for the family 
as there were high chances of further worsening of the se functions with a surgery. However the 
hospital has state of the art facilities along with specialist doctors who decided to take up the 
challenge. It was a 14 hour long surgery performed successfully without compromising to her 
abilities. All her sympto ms settled well and she leads a normal life now.    
• Here again at KIMS KURNOOL, a Kidney swap transplant was performed for the first time in that 
region. Swap transplant is exchange of organs between two families, who cannot do within family 
because of bloo d group mismatch. After getting due approvals from government, our doctors 
along with 20 healthcare staff did the surgery lasting 12 hours. Both patients and donors were 
discharged with stable kidney function.  
 
KIMS ANANTHAPUR  
• A rare form of Cancer known a s Transitional Cell Carcinoma of Ureter has incidence of only 2 in 
one lakh population. A septuagenarian of 75 years was diagnosed with this cancer. The doctors 
performed a laparoscopic surgery to remove the tumour along with the right Kidney. This is the 
first instance in the Ananthapur dist. made possible because of latest equipment and technical 
expertise.  
• Calcification that forms in Coronary arteries can be very hard and difficult to remove. In the past, 
doctors used balloon -based techniques to treat th is condition. However, a new cutting -edge 
technology called ROTAPRO ROTABLATOR was recently launched in India in April 2022. 
Doctors at KIMS Ananthapur used this ultramodern technology to treat a 65 year old woman 
successfully.  
• A 5 year boy underwent ballo on angioplasty in KIMS Ananthapur. Coarctation of Aorta, which 
affects only 0.06 to 0, 08% of children was diagnosed with this boy. This rare ailment causing 
decreased blood supply to legs, was swiftly treated through a state -of-the-art balloon angioplasty , 
a first in Ananthapur for a child of his age. The procedure was done free of cost under 
Arogyasree.

About  KIMS  Hospitals:  
 
Founded by Dr. B. Bhaskara Rao and headquartered in Hyderabad, KIMS Hospitals is one of the largest  
corporate healthcare groups in Telangana and AP, providing multi -disciplinary integrated healthcare  
services,  with a focus  on tertiary  and quaternary  healthcare  at affordable  cost.  The Krishna  Institute  of 
Medical Sciences (KIMS) has a network of 12 hospitals and 3,975 beds spread across Telangana  
(Secunderabad,  Kondapur,  Gachibowli  and Paradise  Circle),  Andhra  Pradesh  (Nellore,  Rajahmundry,  
Srikakulam, Ongole, Vizag, Anantapur and Kurnool) and Maharashtra (Nagpur). The Group offers a  
comprehensive  bouquet  of healthcare  services  in specialties  and super  specialties  across  more  than 40 
fields. The Group’s flagship hospital at Secundarabad, is one of the largest hospitals in India with 
capacity of 1,000 beds in a single location.
