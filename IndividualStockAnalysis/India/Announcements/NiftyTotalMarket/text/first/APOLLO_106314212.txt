 Sub: Board Comments on Notice received from National Stock Exchange of India 
Limited dated 27th June, 2025 on non-compliance/delayed Compliance of Regulation 33 
of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015- Reg. 
 
This is to inform you that the notice received from National Stock Exchange of India Ltd on 
non-compliance/delayed Compliance of Regulation 33 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 was placed before the Board at its meeting held 
on 28th July, 2025. (The next Board Meeting held after receipt of Notice from NSE). 
The Company had by oversight submitted the Limited Review Reports instead of Statutory 
Auditor’s Reports for both Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results for the year ended 
March 31, 2025, along with the financial results on 23rd May, 2025.  However, the Company 
after noticing the error in submission of the Financial Results has filed revised Financial Results 
on 31st May, 2025 enclosing the Statutory Auditors’ Report for both Standalone and 
Consolidated Financial Results for the year ended March 31, 2025, with the Stock Exchanges. 
 
Subsequently, the Company has received a Notice from NSE dated 27th June, 2025 stating non-
compliance/delayed compliance and imposed a penalty of Rs. 88,500/- (including GST). The 
Company has paid the said penalty amount on 30th June 2025 to the NSE. 
After due deliberations, the Board took note of the aforesaid notice and the subsequent action 
taken by the Exchange in this regard. The Board instructed the Company Secretary & 
Compliance Officer of the Company to thoroughly review the documents prior to their upload 
and submit them to the Exchanges only after such review. The Board also noted that this 
inadvertent error occurred for the first time; however, it instructed the Company Secretary to 
exercise due care and caution while submitting documents to the Exchanges henceforth.   
We request you to kindly take on record the information and disseminate the same.
