PRESS RELEASE  
 
 
BuzzWorks Expands Hyderabad Footprint  
 
Infor India Private Limited Signs Deal for 24,000 Sft at BuzzWorks, Auro Orbit in HITEC City  
BuzzWorks Targets 1 Million Sq. Ft. Portfolio by FY2026  
 
Hyderabad, 19th June, 2025:  Brigade Group’s premium flexible and managed workspace brand, 
BuzzWorks, continues to strengthen its presence in Hyderabad with Infor India Private Limited sign ed 
up for a 24,000 sq. ft. workspace at Auro Orbit, HITEC City. This adds to BuzzWorks’ growing 
footprint in the city and supports its broader plan to reach 1 million sq. ft. of managed workspace 
across its portfolio by the end of FY2026.  
 
Infor, a global leader in business cloud software for industry -specific markets, has taken up space at 
BuzzWorks Auro Orbit, a centre known for its premium design, strategic location and well -curated 
amenities that support modern workplace needs.  
 
“Available data suggests Hyderabad’s commercial real estate is undergoing a structural shift; 12.5 
million sq. ft. of Grade A space was leased in 2024, a 56% year -on-year growth, led by IT services and 
Global Capability Centers. The fact that flexible work space operators accounted for 14% of this uptake 
and continue to hold a 14% share across India’s top cities signals more than just demand. It points to 
a redefinition of value in office space. At BuzzWorks, we see this inflection point as an opportunity to  
lead the category transition from space providers to experience enablers, and from fixed assets to 
office -as-a-service,” said Siddharth Verma, General Manager, BuzzWorks . 
 
BuzzWorks Auro Orbit is designed around an ‘Urban Forest’ theme that blends technology and biophilia 
to enhance creativity, well -being and sustainability. Located on the 18th floor, the workspace features 
AI-powered smart systems, eco -conscious materials a nd thoughtfully curated amenities, offering 
panoramic city views and a vibrant work environment.  
 
About BuzzWorks:  
 
BuzzWorks was launched by Brigade Group in 2019 to offer customisable, managed and flexible office 
solutions tailored to evolving business needs. With a growing presence across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, 
Chennai, Kochi and Trivandrum, BuzzWorks serves a wide sp ectrum of clients, from startups and 
freelancers to large enterprises. The brand also features BuzzWorks Live, a curated platform for 
events, experiences and networking, exclusively for its workspace community.
