Lupin Launches Prucalopride Tablets in the United States  
 
Mumbai, Naples, June 26, 2025 : Global pharma major Lupin Limited (Lupin) today announced the 
launch of Prucalopride Tablets, 1 mg, and 2 mg, in the United States, following the recent approval of 
its Abbreviated New Drug Application (ANDA) from the U.S. FDA.  
 
Prucalopride Tablets are bioequivalent to Motegrity® Tablets, 1 mg and 2 mg, of Takeda Pharmaceuticals 
U.S.A. Inc., and indicated for the treatment of chronic idiopathic constipation (CIC) in adults. 
 
Prucalopride Tablets, 1 mg, and 2 mg (RLD Motegrity®) had estimated annual sales of USD 184 million 
in the U.S. (IQVIA MAT April 2025).  
 
About Lupin  
Lupin Limited is a global pharmaceutical leader headquartered in Mumbai, India, with products distributed in 
over 100 markets. Lupin specializes in pharmaceutical products, including branded and generic formulations,

globally, along with a dedicated workforce of over 23,000 professionals. Lupin is committed to improving patient
