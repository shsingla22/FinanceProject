In terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“the 
Listing Regulations”) read with Schedule III to the Listing Regulations, we wish to inform you that the  
Nomination and Remuneration Committee through Circular Resolution passed today, i.e., May 29, 2026 have

Employee Stock Option Plan 2021.
