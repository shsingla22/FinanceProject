BRIEF PROFILE OF DR. YOGESH R CHHABRA  
 
 
Dr. Yogesh R Chhabra aged 49 years, has been appointed as Company Secretary, BHEL  
w.e.f. 11th July, 2024. Prior to his appointment as Company Secretary, Dr. Chhabra had 
been holding the position of Addl. General Manager (Finance), BHEL.  
 
Dr. Chhabra is a Commerce graduate from University of Mumbai. He is a Fellow Member 
of the Institute of Company  Secretaries of India and is also an Associate member of the 
Institute of Cost Accountants of India. Dr. Chhabra is also a doctorate (Ph.D.) in 
Commerce. In his about 28 years of professional experience, he has equipped himself with 
various facets in the a rea of Corporate Finance, Governance, Company Law, Listing 
Regulations and other Board related matters in various capacities.
