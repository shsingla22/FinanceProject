Press Release  
 
New Delhi, July 1, 2026  
 
HERO MOTOCORP POSTS STRONG 23% DISPATCH GROWTH IN Q1 FY'27; 
MAINTAINS HEAL THY DEMAND WITH ~5.41 LAKH DISPATCHES IN JUNE 2026  
 
Hero MotoCorp , the world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters,  reported dispatches of 
541,159 units  in June 2026. The Company continued to witness healthy domestic retail momentum 
during the month, with VAHAN registrations of 4.66 lakh units  re lecting a growth of ~18%  over 
the corresponding period last year.   
*Data from VAHAN except Telangana, as on July 1, 2026. 
 
STRONG MOMENTUM IN Q1 FY’27  
 
Hero MotoCorp  carried strong growth momentum through Q1 FY’27 (April – June 2026), 
dispatching 16.8 lakh units  and recording a robust 23% year-on-year growth . This momentum 
was driven by broad-based demand across its core, premium and scooter segment including ICE and 
EVs. Domestic retail demand also remained healthy during the quarter with VAHAN registrations 
reaching 15.3 lakh units  and ~9%  growth over the same period last year.   
 
VIDA SUSTAINS STRONG RETAIL MOMENTUM 
 
VIDA , Hero MotoCorp’s Emerging Mobility business unit , delivered another month of strong retail 
performance in June 2026 with 21,812 VAHAN registrations . This represents a 185%  year-on-year 
growth alongside a steady 8% month-on-month increase. 
 
VIDA was recently recognized as the Global Most Trusted Brand  at the Global Brand & Leadership 
Conclave at the House of Lords, British Parliament, London - reinforcing its position as one of India's 
fastest-growing and most trusted electric mobility brands. 
 
DOMESTIC BUSINESS RECORDS HEALTHY SEGMENT-LEVEL PERFORMANCE 
 
The Company's domestic motorcycle and scooter business sustained a healthy positive momentum 
into June 2026.  The growth was primarily driven by the scooter portfolio, which achieved strong 
double-digit growth, while motorcycle demand remained resilient. Additionally, the recently 
launched Super Splendor XTEC  2.0 continued to garner encouraging customer response.  
 
The Company also launched its first flex-fuel motorcycles in the presence of Hon’ble Union Minister 
for Road Transport and Highways , Shri Nitin Gadkari  and Hon’ble Union Minister for Petroleum and 
Natural Gas, Shri Hardeep Singh Puri , underscoring the government's push for cleaner and 
sustainable mobility solutions. 
 
Adding to the month's achievements, Hero MotoCorp was also named the winner for ‘ Best Use of AI'  
category at the 2026 ACKO TOTM Marketing Awards , validating the Company's commitment to 
leveraging advanced technology-led innovation in its marketing and consumer outreach initiatives. 
 
GLOBAL BUSINESS MAINTAINS ITS UPWARD GROWTH TRAJECTORY 
 
The Company's Global Business maintained its upward growth trajectory in June 2026, dispatching 
38,269 units , to register a robust 33%  year-on-year growth. Momentum across its international
