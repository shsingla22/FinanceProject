Fixation of Record Date — 13th January, 2026 
Pursuant to the requirement of provisions of Regulation 60 of the Securities and 
Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we would like to inform that the Bank has decided to exercise the 
Call Option on Bank of India Additional Tier I Bonds Series VI (ISIN No.
