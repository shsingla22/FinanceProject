Gear Division
Our Gear  division  continued  to deliver  strong  growth  in Q2 FY27, with  revenue  increasing  10.6% Y-o-Y to ₹488 
crores  driven  by disciplined  execution,  reinforcing  the division’s  strong  contribution  to our consolidated  
performance .
EBIT  for the quarter  increased  27.2% Y-o-Y to ₹108 crores , while  the EBIT  margin  expanded  by 290 bps to 22.1%. 
The margin  improvement  was driven  by outpacing  in revenue  growth,  a favourable  revenue  mix,  and supported  by 
operating  leverage .
Order  intake  remained  broadly  stable  at ₹493 crores  during  Q2, while  H1 order  intake  grew  8.8% to ₹1,063 crores . 
Our open  order  book  increased  37.0% Y-o-Y to ₹1,056 crores , providing  healthy  revenue  visibility .
MHE Division
The MHE  division  registered  a revenue  growth  of 10.4% Y-o-Y during  Q2 FY27. While  project  execution  improved  
compared  to Q1, a further  acceleration  in execution  is anticipated  during  the remainder  of H2 FY27.
The moderation  in EBIT  margin  was primarily  attributable  to unfavorable  product  mix,  increased  input  costs,  and 
operating  deleverage  resulting  from  lower  scale  of operations  on account  of delayed  clearances  for the projects .
Order  momentum  remained  strong,  with  intake  growing  32.5% to ₹253 crores  in Q2, supported  by overseas  orders  
of ₹68 crores .

Commenting on the results, Mr. Prayasvin B. Patel, Chairman & Managing Director of 
Elecon Engineering Company Ltd.  said, 
“Q2 FY27 marked  another  quarter  of healthy  performance  for Elecon,  with  Consolidated  Revenue  
growing  10.5% Y-o-Y to ₹639 crores , while  EBITDA  increased  17.7% to ₹148 crores . EBITDA  margin  
expanded  by 140 bps to 23.1%, supported  by a higher  contribution  from  the gear  division,  driven  by 
disciplined  execution  and the ability  to maintain  operations  through  challenges . Profit  After  Tax stood  
at ₹96 crores , with  a PAT margin  of 15.1%. For H1 FY27, revenue  stood  at ₹1,160 crores , representing  
11.1% growth  over  adjusted  H1 FY26, while  adjusted  EBITDA  grew  11.4% to ₹257 crores  with  a margin  
of 22.2%.
Our Gear  Division  continued  to drive  growth,  with  Q2 revenue  increasing  10.6% Y-o-Y to ₹488 crores  
and EBIT  growing  27.2% to ₹108 crores , resulting  in an improved  EBIT  margin  of 22.1%. The division  
also maintained  healthy  momentum  during  H1 FY27, with  revenue  increasing  13.1% and EBIT  growing  
21.8%. The open  order  book  for the division  stood  at ₹1,056 crores , providing  a strong  executable  
backlog . The Material  Handling  Equipment  (MHE)  Division  reported  Q2 revenue  of ₹151 crores , 
registering  growth  of 10.4% Y-o-Y. However,  EBIT  margin  moderated  to 20.2%, due to an unfavorable  
product  mix,  and slower  project  execution . Encouragingly,  MHE  order  intake  grew  32.5% Y-o-Y to ₹253 
crores , including  overseas  orders  of ₹68 crores , while  the open  order  book  increased  27.3% to ₹579 
crores .
Our overseas  business , comprising  exports  and overseas  subsidiaries,  continued  to gain  momentum,  
with  revenue  reaching  ₹159 crores  during  Q2 FY27, and registering  30.6% Y-o-Y growth . While  global  
macroeconomic  and geopolitical  uncertainties  persist,  we remain  focused  on strengthening  our reach  
across  international  markets .
Our consolidated  order  intake  increased  8.4% Y-o-Y to ₹746 crores  during  Q2 FY27 and 15.3% to 
₹1,501 crores  during  H1 FY27. The consolidated  open  order  book  stood  at ₹1,635 crores  as of 30th 
September  2026 , up approximately  33% Y-o-Y, with  healthy  contributions  from  both  divisions . Our 
robust  order  book  not only  provides  strong  revenue  visibility  but also reinforces  our confidence  in 
continuing  to be the preferred  choice  of our customers .
As we mark  75 years  of Elecon , we remain  focused  on strengthening  our leadership  in industrial  gear  
solutions  and material  handling  equipment,  expanding  our international  presence,  and advancing  our 
engineering  and manufacturing  capabilities  to create  sustainable  long -term  value  for all our 
stakeholders .”H1FY27 Highlights

About Elecon Engineering Company Limited:
Elecon  Engineering  Company  Limited  (ELECON)  was  established  in 1951  and  is one  of the 
largest  manufacturers  of Industrial  Gears  and  Material  Handling  Equipment  with  over  
seven  decades  of experience  and  expertise  in Asia . The  company  has been  able  to 
establish  its position  as one  of Asia’s  largest  and  India’s  largest  Industrial  Gear  
Manufacturing  Company,  having  manufacturing  facility  spread  over  3,35,000  Square  
Meter . The  company  has a strong  global  presence  serving  95+ countries  through  a 
network  of distributors,  dealers  and  customer  representatives . The  product  solutions  
include  designing,  manufacturing,  supply,  erection  and  commissioning  of the products  
and  are majorly  used  in Cement,  Sugar,  Defense,  Steel,  Mining  and  Power  sector  along  
with  other  sectors
