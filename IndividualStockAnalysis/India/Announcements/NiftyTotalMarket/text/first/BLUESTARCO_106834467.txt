Blue Star ’s Q2FY27  consolidated  revenue grows 21% to       
Rs 2930.95 crores ; Operating P rofit grows by 10.5% to        
Rs 202.58 crores  
 
In Q2FY27, an extended summer season drove robust demand for Room A Cs, contributing 
to strong revenue growth. However, profitability remained under pressure across businesses 
due to continued escalation in material costs and the limited ability  to fully pass on these 
increases.  
 
Consolidated Financial Performance for Q 2FY27 
 
• The Company’s Revenue from Operations grew by 21.0% to Rs 2930.95  crores for the 
quarter ended September  30, 202 6, as compared to Rs 2422.37  crores during the same 
period in the previous year.  
 
• The Operating  Profit (EBITDA excluding  Other  Income)  for the quarter  increased by 
10.5% to Rs 202.5 8 crores (6.9% of Revenue)  compared to Rs 183.4 1 crores  (7.6% 
of Revenue)  in Q2FY26. 
 
• Other Income, including income from treasury investments, for Q 2FY27 was Rs 15.79  
crores  as compared to Rs 10.01 crores in Q 2FY26, due to higher surplus levels in 
Q2FY27.  
 
• Finance costs for the quarter remained about the same at  Rs 16.85  crores as compared 
to the same period of the previous year, owing to  lower borrowing levels in Q2FY27.  
 
• Tax expense for the quarte r was the same at Rs 33.39 crores as compared to Q 2FY26. 
 
• Profit Before Tax (before exceptional items  and share of profit/loss from JV ) grew 
marginally t o Rs 133.61 crores in Q 2FY27 compared to Rs 133.16 crores in Q 2FY26.  

• Net Profit for Q2FY27  grew by  25.4% to Rs  123.91  crores as compared to Rs 98.78  
crores reported in the same period of the previous year.  During the quarter, a n 
exceptional gain of Rs 23.02 cr ores was recognised on the sale of the MedTech 
business.  
 
• Earnings per share (no t annualised) for Q 2FY27 (Face value of Rs 2.00) was Rs 6.03 
compared to Rs 4.80 in Q2FY26. 
 
• Carried Forward Order Book as on September  30, 202 6, grew marginally  to Rs 7229.22  
crores as compared to Rs 7120.44  crores as on September  30, 202 5. 
 
• As of September 30, 202 6, due to normali sed working capital levels,  the capital 
employed decreased  to Rs 3200.36 crores, as compared to Rs 3530.88  crores on 
September 30, 202 5.  
 
• The quarter ended with a net cash position  of Rs 289.43 crores as on September 30, 
2026, as compared to net borrowings  of Rs 417.06  crores as of September 30, 202 5.  
 
 
Consolidated Segment Performance for Q 2FY27 
 
• Revenue of Segment 1, comprising Electro -Mechanical Projects, Commercial Air 
Conditioning Systems, Service and International  Business , grew by 16.3% to Rs 
1935.99  crores in Q 2FY27, compared to Rs  1664.21  crores in Q 2FY26. The Segment 
result for Q 2FY27 stood at  Rs 133.15  crores ( 6.9% of Revenue)  as compared to Rs 
147.14  crores ( 8.8% of Revenue)  in the same quarter of the previous year . During the 
quarter,  in the Electro -Mechanical Projects business,  order inflows were primarily 
driven by data centres, while enquiries from the factory segment also remained strong . 
However, b illing growth was subdued due to pr oject execution delays . The Company 
expects a significant increase in order inflows in the coming months . The Commercial 
Air Conditioning business recorded strong revenue growth, driven by robust demand 
across healthcare, education and industrial segments.  The Company launched  its next-
generation VRF and ducted systems, further strengthening its portfolio while 
maintaining its market leadership. In the US, heat pump supplies continued despite 
supply chain and logistics challenges, supported by growing marke t acceptance. 
Product development and testing with additional OEMs in the US and Europe are 
underway, with business growth expected to accelerate as tariff -related uncertainties 
ease. 
 
• Unitary Products  Segment , compris ing Room Air Conditioners  and Commercial 
Refrigeration , recorded  a revenue growth  of 29.4% to Rs 897.66  crores in Q 2FY27, 
compared to Rs 693.81  crores in Q 2FY26. Segment result stood at  Rs 20.74  crores 
(2.3% of Revenue)  in Q2FY27 as compared to Rs  42.72  crores ( 6.2% of Revenue)  in 
Q2FY26. A delayed monsoon and extended summer supported strong demand for 
Room ACs . Despite periodic price increases, only part of the material cost escalation 
could be passed on, keeping margins under pressure. The Company is  in the process 
of rationalising its product portfolio through differentiated, cost -optimised models  to 
improve margins. Commercial Refrigeration is also showing signs of demand revival, 
although margins remain under pressure due to limited ability to  pass-through material 
cost increases.  
 
• Revenue of the Professional Electronics and Industrial Systems  Segment , increased  by 
51.2% to Rs  97.30  crores in Q 2FY27, compared to Rs 64.35  crores in Q 2FY26. The 
Segment result stood at  Rs 23.10  crores ( 23.7% of Revenue)  in Q2FY27, compared to 
Rs 6.17 crores ( 9.6% of Revenue)  in Q2FY26. During the quarter, the Company 
completed the sale of its MedTech business for a total consideration of Rs 35.65 crore s 
on August  31, 2026. Following the divestment, the write -back of warranty provis ions, 
owing to the cessation of MedTech product -related obligations, contributed to 
improved profitability. Meanwhile, Industrial Solutions sustained strong growth, driven 
by manufacturing and testing demand, while Data Security Solutions maintained 
steady  performance, supported by robust demand from BFSI and large enterprises.  
 
Consolidated Financial Performance for the half -year ended September 30,  2026 
(H1FY2 7)  
 
• For the half -year ended September 30, 202 6, the Company reported Revenue from 
Operations of Rs 6308.87 crores compared to Rs 5404.62  crores over the same period 
in the previous year,  recording  a growth of 16.7%.  
 
• Operating Profit (EBITDA excluding Other Income) was Rs 377.53 crores (6.0% of 
Revenue) compared to Rs 383.41 crores (7.1% of Revenue) during the same period 
last year.  
• Net Profit for the half -year ended September 30, 2026, was Rs 226.42 crores compared 
to Rs 219.60 crores in H1FY26. During the current period,  an exceptional gain of Rs 
32.19 crores was recognised , which includes  Rs 23.02 crores on the sale of the 
MedTech business  and Rs 9.17 crore s profit on sale of property.  
  
Outlook  
 
Vir S Advani, Chairman & Managing Director, Blue Star Limited , adds, “We continue to see 
strong demand for Room A Cs through the festive season, while a healthy pipeline of Data 
Centre MEP projects  is expected to translate into significant order inflows in the co ming 
months. We are also witnessing a revival in demand across Commercial Air Conditioning and 
Commercial Refrigeration  businesses , providing further impetus to our growth prospects. 
While a deficient monsoon  leading to potential impact on agricultural incomes and higher 
interest rates could impact  near-term demand, the conclusion of the US trade deal and 
resolution of West Asia crisis could provide growth  tailwind s. We remain confident of 
sustaining our revenue growth mo mentum in H2FY27, with a clear focus on strengthening 
profitability. Our priorities will be to drive calibrated price revisions, accelerate product cost 
optimisation and improve operational efficiencies to enhance margins .”
