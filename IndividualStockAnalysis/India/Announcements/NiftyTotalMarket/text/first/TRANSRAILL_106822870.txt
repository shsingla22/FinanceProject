Press Release            
 
Transrail order intake momentum continues,  
secures fresh  orders worth ₹ 927 Crore  
Wins anchored by 765kV & 400kV Domestic T ransmission Line  Projects  
 
Mumbai , October 20, 2026  – Transrail Lighting Limited (BSE: 544317, NSE: TRANSRAILL),  one of the leading 
Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) players in the Transmission & Distribution (T&D) sector, 
today announced that it has bagged new orders worth ₹ 927 crore mainly in its Domestic Power T&D 
business.  Post these fresh orders, the order intake for the year has increased  to ₹3,356 crore.   
The order wins mainly includes  EPC projects in domestic market for 765kV & 400kV transmission line s. It also 
includes conductor supply orders to USA. With these new jobs , the winning streak has continued for Transrail 
with more than ₹2,300  crore  order declared in last 3 weeks .  
Transrail’s  expanded  inhouse manufacturing capabilities  for tower and conductors , paired with robust 
domestic and international T&D demand, position s the Company perfectly to drive sustainable growth.  
Mr. Randeep Narang, MD & CEO, quoted , 
“The strong momentum in our order inflows underscores Transrail’s growing scale and competitiveness in 
the power T&D sector. Our ability to secure complex transmission EPC projects, backed by our in -house tower 
and conductor manufacturing capabilities, rema ins a key differentiator. With a strong foothold in the 
domestic market and an expanding international business, we are focused on building a well -diversified 
business across our products and services .” 
 
 
About Transrail Lighting Limited:  
Transrail is a leading turnkey engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) company with primary focus 
on power transmission and distribution business with 4 decades of experience. With its headquarters in India, 
it is a global enterprise with a footpri nt in more than 60 countries across 6 continents. It provides turnkey 
solutions from Designing, Engineering, Supply, Manufacture, Construction, Testing services across all its 
business verticals which include Transmission Lines, Substations, Civil Construction, Railways, Solar and Pole 
& Ligh ting. As part of Power T&D business, Transrail has largescale manufacturing facilities in India for 
Galvanized Lattice Towers, Overhead Conductors including HTLS and Galvanized Monopoles in addition to a 
well-accredited Tower testing facility.
