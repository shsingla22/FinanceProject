O R D E R
In view of the judgment of this Court in “Disortho S.A.S. v.
Meril Life Sciences Private Limited”1, we are of the opinion that
this Court has jurisdiction to appoint the third Arbitrator/Umpire.
In fact, during the course of hearing today, learned Senior
Advocate appearing for the petitioner filed a copy of the letter
dated  03.04.2025  addressed  by  the  Singapore  International
Arbitration Centre, stating that it does not have jurisdiction to
appoint the third Arbitrator/Umpire.
Having regard to the aforesaid facts, we hereby appoint
Mr. Quentin Loh as the third Arbitrator/Umpire.
Recording the aforesaid, the arbitration petition is disposed
of.
