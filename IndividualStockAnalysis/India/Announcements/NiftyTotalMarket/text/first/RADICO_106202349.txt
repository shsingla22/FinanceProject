Press Release  
Radico Khaitan Celebrates Global Recognition as MD , Mr. Abhishek Khaitan Inducted into 
World Drinks Awards Hall of Fame  
 
New Delhi, April 15, 2025 – Radico Khaitan Ltd., one of India’s leading manufacturers in the Indian 
Made Foreign Liquor (IMFL) industry (referred to as “Radico Khaitan” or the “Company”), is 
pleased to announce a significant achievement. The Company's Managing Director, Mr. Abhishek 
Khaitan, has been inducted into the esteemed World Drinks Awards – Hall of Fame. This honour 
represents not only a personal achievement for Mr. Khaitan but also a global acknowledgment of 
Radico Khaitan’s continued excellence in the spirits industry.  
 
The induction ceremony occurred on April 12, 2025, as part of the renowned Icons of Whisky 
Awards, hosted annually by Whisky Magazine. This prestigious event celebrates top-notch 
personalities  and the innovations within the global spirits industry  – with the Hall of Fame 
recognizing outstanding contributions by individuals to the whisky world.  
 
Mr. Khaitan’s induction as the 103rd member of the Whisky Hall of Fame marks a significant 
moment not only for Radico Khaitan but also for India’s growing presence in the global premium 
spirits’ market. This recognition highlights his leadership, commitment to innovation, and the 
success of Indian brands on the international stage.  
 
Mr. Khaitan joins a n elite group of influential industry leaders in the Hall of Fame, including 
Richard Paterson (Whyte & Mackay), Dr. Bill Lumsden (Glenmorangie), John Teeling (Cooley 
Distillery), and Jim Beveridge OBE (Johnnie Walker). His induction, as the youngest -ever 
honouree , signifies his extraordinary contributions to the global whisky industry and marks a new 
era of leadership that will continue to shape the future of the sector.  
 
This prestigious honour serves as further recognition of his vision in expanding Radico Khaitan’s 
influence across the globe and solidifying India’s role in the world of fine spirits.

Radico Khaitan Limited at a Glance:  
Radico Khaitan Limited ( “Radico Khaitan” or the Company) is among the oldest and one of the 
largest manufacturers of IMFL in India. Earlier known as Rampur Distillery 
Company,  Radico Khaitan commenced its operations in 1943 and over the years emerged as a 
major bulk spirits supplier and bottler to other spirit manufacturers. In 1998 the Company started 
its own brands with the introduction of 8PM Whisky.  Radico Khaitan is one of the few companies 
in India to have developed its entire brand portfolio organically.  
The Company’s brand portfolio includes Rampur Indian Single Malt Whiskies, Sangam World Malt 
Whisky, Spirit of Victory 1999 Pure Malt Whisky, Jaisalmer Indian Craft Gin, Royal Ranthambore 
Heritage Collection Royal Crafted Whisky, Happiness in a Bottle: A H appily Crafted Gin, 
Morpheus and Morpheus Blue Brandy, Magic Moments Vodka, Magic Moments Remix Pink 
Vodka, Magic Moments Dazzle Vodka (Gold & Silver), Magic Moments Verve Vodka, 1965 The 
Spirit of Victory Premium XXX Rum and Lemon Dash Premium Flavored Ru m, After Dark Whisky, 
8PM Premium Black Whisky, 8PM Whisky, Contessa Rum and Old Admiral Brandy.  
Radico Khaitan is also one of the largest providers of branded IMFL to the Canteen Stores 
Department (CSD), which has significant business barriers to entry. The Company has distilleries 
situated in Rampur, Sitapur and Aurangabad, Maharashtra which is a 36% joint  venture. The 
Company has a total owned capacity of 320 million litres and operates 43 bottling units (5 owned, 
29 contract and 9 royalty bottling units). It is also one of the largest exporters of Alcoholic 
beverages from India, with brands availab le in over 102 countries.
