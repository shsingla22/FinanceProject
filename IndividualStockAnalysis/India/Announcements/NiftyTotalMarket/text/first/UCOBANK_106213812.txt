PRESS RELEASE  
 
 
 
 
Key Highlights of the Results -Q4 FY-2024-25 
 
 Business Growth  - Total Business of the Bank stood at Rs. 5,13,527 Crore  as on 
31.03.2025 showing an increase  of 14.12%  Y-o-Y, wherein Gross Advances 
increased  by 17.72%  Y-o-Y to Rs. 2,19,985 Crore   & Total Deposits grown by 
11.56%  Y-o-Y to Rs. 2,93,542 Crore . 
 Profitability & Return – Net profit for the quarter ending 3 1.03.2025 stood at 
Rs.652 Crore  as against Rs. 526 Crore  for the same period of preceding year, 
registering a growth of  23.98% Y-o-Y. Operating Profit for the quarter ended 
31.03.2025 stood at Rs.  1699 Crore  as against Rs. 1273 Crore showing an increase 
of 33.48 %  Y-o-Y basis. 
 
 Advances in Retail, Agriculture & MSME (RAM) Sectors - RAM segment of the 
Bank increased  by 25.74 % to Rs.  1,22,613  Crore  on Y-o-Y backed by 35.09% Y-
o-Y growth in Retail  advances  20.02% Y-o-Y growth  in Agriculture advances  and 
18.55    % Y-o-Y growth in MSME advances.  
 
 Reduction in NPA  - Gross NPA reduced by  77 bps Y-o-Y to 2.69 % as on 
31.03.2025 whereas Net NPA reduced by 39 bps Y-o-Y to 0.50 % as on 3 1.03.2025. 
 
 
 Capital Adequacy Ratio - Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) stood at  18.49%  as on 
31.03.2025  with Tier I Capital Ratio  of 16.37%  
 
 Credit to Deposit Ratio  stood at 74.94% as on 3 1.03.2025.
