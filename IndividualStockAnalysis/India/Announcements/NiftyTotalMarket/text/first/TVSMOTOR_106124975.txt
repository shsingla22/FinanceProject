Ezz LCV celebrates 5 -year milestone with TVSM with a New 
Assembly Line in Giza  
~ Relocates assembly to s trengthen its  the footprint in Egypt  
Giza , January 25 , 2025: Ezz LCV, the authorized distributor of TVS Motor Company  (TVSM)  
in Egypt, today inaugurated a new assembly line in Giza, investing approximately 6.5 million  
U.S. Dollars (360 Million EGP)  to enhance local manufacturing capabilities. The state -of-the-
art facility will have a production capacity of 100,000 units per year, significantly expanding 
Ezz LCV's ability to meet growing market demand for two -wheelers.  
The new assembly line will assemble popular TVSM popular  models, including the TVS 
Apache RTR Series, TVS HLX Series, and TVS XL 100. By localizing production, Ezz LCV 
aims to improve delivery times, support its extensive dealer network, and provide high -quality 
motorcycles tailored to Egyptian customer prefere nces.  
"Over the past five years, our partnership with TVS Motor Company has been instrumental in 
delivering premium, reliable two -wheelers to the Egyptian market ," said the Mr. Amr Ezz El 
Alarab, CEO Ezz LCV . "This new facility represents our commitment to meeting the evolving 
needs of local riders by producing seven flagship models."  
Celebrating this partnership, Mr. Rahul Nayak, Vice President, International Business, 
TVS Motor Company , said, "Congratulations to Ezz LCV  on the inauguration of t his assembly 
line in Giza . It marks an important milestone as the  expansion reflects the potential of  high 
quality, reliable, trustworthy TVSM products in Egypt. This new facility will strengthen our 
position in Egypt and create job opportunities that will maximize the positive economic impact 
on the region.”     
The assembly line leverages TVS Motor Company's globally recognized engineering expertise 
while allowing Ezz LCV to strengthen its market position. The facility is strategically designed 
to serve the growing customer base seeking innovative, reliable, and affordable two -wheelers 
in Egypt.  
The current product lineup in Egypt includes the TVS Apache RTR 200 4V, TVS Apache RTR 
160 4V, TVS Star HLX 150, and TVS HLX 150 F models.
