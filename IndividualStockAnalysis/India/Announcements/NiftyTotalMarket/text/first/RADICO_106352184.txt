Radico Khaitan’s The Spirit of Kashmyr Sets Benchmark; Awarded National 
Recognition in First Month of Launch 
India’s first homegrown luxury vodka bagged the prestigious award presented by  
Mr. Amitabh Kant, Former CEO of NITI Aayog & G20 Sherpa, at the 7th ICONIC Awards 
New Delhi, India, August 26, 2025 – Radico Khaitan’s latest innovation, The Spirit of Kashmyr , India’s 
first homegrown vodka in the luxury segment, has been recognised with its first national award within 
a month of launch. This early recognition highlights the brand’s exceptional craftsmanship, quality, and 
its role in setting new benchmarks in India’s alcobev industry. 
 
The award was presented by Mr. Amitabh Kant, former CEO of NITI Aayog and G20 Sherpa, at the 7th 
Edition of the ICONIC Awards, organised by TV9 Network. The ICONIC Awards are known for celebrating 
pioneers and visionaries who are shaping the future of luxury, travel & tourism, and allied industries by 
embracing innovation, sustainability, and new growth horizons. 
Speaking on the achievement, Mr. Amar Sinha, Chief Operating Officer at Radico Khaitan , said, “We 
are thrilled that The Spirit of Kashmyr has been honoured with its very first award, and that too within weeks 
of its launch. Winning the ‘Best Vodka’ title at the 7th ICONIC Awards is a proud milestone for us and marks a 
significant step in the beverage market. Inspired by the soul of Kashmir and infused with its rarest treasures, 
The Spirit of Kashmyr is more than a spirit, it is an experience drawn from the snow-kissed landscapes of the 
valley, the regal warmth of Pampore saffron, and the poetic stillness of Dal Lake at dawn. This appreciation 
only strengthens our resolution to keep pushing boundaries and bringing world-class offerings from India to 
the world.”  

 
                                            Press Release 
 
 
TV9 Network and Red Hat Communications were the official partners of the ICONIC Awards. Adding to 
this, Mr. Raktim Das, Chief Growth Officer, TV9 Network , remarked, “Radico Khaitan has consistently 
demonstrated leadership and innovation in the alcobev sector, and it is commendable to see The Spirit of 
Kashmyr earning recognition so quickly. Such milestones reaffirm the industry’s potential to make a strong 
mark globally.”  
The awards also featured panel sessions that witnessed the presence of distinguished global leaders 
and dignitaries, including H.E. Nguyen Thanh Hai, Ambassador, Embassy of the Socialist Republic of 
Vietnam; H.E. Stella Nkomo, Ambassador, Embassy of the Republic of Zimbabwe; H.E. Dr. Nikolay 
Yankov, Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary, Embassy of the Republic of Bulgaria; H.E. 
Reuben Gauci, High Commissioner, High Commission of Malta; H.E. Aliki Koutsomitopoulou, 
Ambassador, Embassy of the Hellenic Republic – Greece; H.E. Benedikt Hoskuldsson, Ambassador, 
Embassy of Iceland; H.E. Lalatiana Accouche, High Commissioner, High Commission of the Republic of 
Seychelles; H.E. Amb Peter Munyiri, High Commissioner, High Commission of the Republic of Kenya; 
and H.E. K.L. Ganju, Consul General (Hony.), Embassy of the Republic of Union of Comoro. 
The award ceremony, which followed these insightful sessions, honoured leaders and brands driving 
excellence, vision, and inspiration across sectors, an apt stage for The Spirit of Kashmyr to receive its 
first milestone recognition.
