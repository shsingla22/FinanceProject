Coforge Earns Great Place To Work Certification for Sixth Year in a Row  
 
 
Greater Noida, India | August 26, 2026 : Coforge Limited (NSE: COFORGE), a global AI -native 
engineering services leader,  announced that it has been certified as a Great Place To Work® by Great 
Place To Work India for the sixth consecutive year.  
 
The Great Place To Work Assessment is considered to be  a ‘Gold Standard’ in workplace culture 
assessment. Great Place To Work measured employee perceptions among 5 ,000 Coforge employees 
using its rigorous Trust Index ™ Employee Survey. Coforge continues to uphold the Great Place To 
Work Assessment benchmarks for pride, satisfaction, growth, and well -being.  
 
The achievement reflects Coforge’s belief that great workplaces are built by great people, and its 
commitment to creating an environment where employees feel valued, supported, and motivated.  
 
Pankaj Khanna, Chief People Officer, Coforge , said, “Achieving the Great Place To Work® 
Certification ™ for the sixth consecutive year is a testament to strong employee -centric  culture we 
have built at Coforge . We believe in creating an environment our employees are valued, respected, 
and provided with the tools and encouragement to reach their full potential. We invest in well -being, 
learning, and equal opportunities, which is why people choose to stay and grow  at Coforge . This is 
reflected in our high retention  rates and long-term employee trust and satisfaction. We are committed 
to people e xcellence, ensuring that Coforge remains an employer of choice for top talent across the 
globe."
