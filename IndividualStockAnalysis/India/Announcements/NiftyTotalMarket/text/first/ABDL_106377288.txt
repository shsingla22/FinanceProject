ABD Maestro Brands Debut at Bengaluru Airport Duty -Free  
 
~Nationwide roll out to major airports within a month, paving the way for global travel retail 
expansion ~ 
 
Bengaluru , 16th September 2025 : ABD Maestro Pvt.Ltd., the super -premium and luxury spirits subsidiary 
of Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD), announce d the debut of its portfolio at the Duty -Free  Travel 
Retail at Kempegowda International Airport, Bengaluru . 
 
Bengaluru  as the first,  with plans to follow in Delhi International Airport and Mumbai in quick succession , 
this milestone  marks ABD Maestro’s entry into the country’s Airport Duty Free T ravel Retail business . The 
expansion underscores the company’s confidence and ambition to place its super premium and luxury 
spirits alongside the world’s finest.  
 
ABD  Maestro  brings  an exceptional portfolio of fine spirits designed for discerning travel lers. It includes 
ARTHAUS Blended Malt Scotch Whisky , a curated expression of Speyside and Highland single malts, the 
award -winning WOODBURNS Contemporary Indian Malt Whisky  crafted in Goa for a new generation of 
whisky enthusiasts; PUMORI Small Batch Gin , celebrated for its rich blend of Himalayan Juniper and Indian 
botanicals; and ZOYA Special Batch Gin , for its crisp character and modern mixability. ABD Maestro is also 
the exclusive distribution partner for India Domestic and Travel Retail for the trend setting RUSSIAN 
STANDARD  Vodka .  
 
Commenting on the launch  in Bengaluru with Avolta , which operates across 70 countries globally , Bikram 
Basu, Managing Director, ABD Maestro  said,  “The debut at Bengaluru Duty Free is a defining moment  for 
ABD Maestro . It initiates showcas ing our new and powerful p ortfolio  of high quality and creative products . 
We plan to reach international trave llers in key global markets  at the earliest .” 
 
With millions of international and domestic travellers passing through Indian airports every year, this 
expansion represents a n aspiration to serve the growing demand for authentic, world -class alcohol 
beverages and build a stronger bridge between India and the global community.  
 
About ABD Maestro Private Limited  
ABD Maestro Private Limited is a new super -premium and luxury spirits brand company and a subsidiary 
of ABD. The brands which ABD Maestro is marketing currently include Arthaus Blended Malt Scotch Whisky, 
 
Zoya Special Batch Gin and its flavours, Woodburns Contemporary Indian Malt Whisky, Pumori Small Batch 
Gin and Pink Gin, Segredo Aldeia Café and White Rums. Through its partnership with Roust Corporation, 
the company has introduced Russian Standard Vodka t o India in three distinct segments: Original, Gold, 
and Platinum.
