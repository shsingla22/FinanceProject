Collaboration
Highlights: 
 Collaboration to support Nokia engineering and R&D activities in India
 Work to include product engineering, new product development, system integration and ongoing support 
Bengaluru, October 26, 2026: L&T Technology Services (BSE: 540115, NSE: LTTS), announced 
it has established a five-year contract with Nokia to support engineering and R&D activities in India. 
This collaboration brings together LTTS’ engineering capabilities with Nokia’s telecommunications 
technology work.  
Under the agreement, LTTS will support Nokia R&D programs across areas including product 
engineering, software development, system integration, and product support.
As part of this vision, LTTS will establish a dedicated Offshore Development Center (ODC) in India to 
support Nokia's engineering and R&D initiatives. This aligns with LTTS’ Lakshya 31 strategy and its 
focus on next-generation compute and AI infrastructure. The ODC will operate at this intersection, 
bringing data-center-grade compute and AI-infrastructure engineering to next-generation telecom 
solutions.  
LTTS will also create a dedicated engineering team in India to support Nokia’s Technology Center 
globally, with work spanning areas such as 5G networks, intelligent automation, AI-driven operations 
and AI infrastructure. 
LTTS will work with Nokia’s global R&D teams through an integrated operating model. The 
engagement will cover research and development, product engineering, new product development, 
embedded systems, software development, system integration, and ongoing support. 
Vivek Jaiswal, Senior Vice President of Engineering at Nokia, said, “As a leader in AI 
infrastructure, Nokia’s continued investment in R&D is essential to developing the advanced 
connectivity technologies that will power the AI supercycle. India is an important innovation hub for 
Nokia, and LTTS is a critical partner that will bring engineering expertise and mechanisms that will 
help us expand our R&D footprint and accelerate innovation in this strategically important geography.”
Alind Saxena, Executive Director & President – Strategic Initiatives & Growth Markets at L&T 
Technology Services commented, “ This strategic engagement represents a significant milestone 
for LTTS and our Technology Segment. It reflects the trust that leading technology innovators place 
in our Engineering Intelligence approach and deep-tech engineering expertise. This collaboration also 
aligns strongly with LTTS’ Lakshya 31 Big Bet around Next Gen Compute and AI infrastructure, an 
area that will be fundamental to the evolution of future communication networks and intelligent 
datacenters.”
