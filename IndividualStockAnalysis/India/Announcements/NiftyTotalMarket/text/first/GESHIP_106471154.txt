PRESS RELEASE  
  
G E Shipping contracts to sell its 2011 built Kamsarmax Dry Bulk Carrier  
“Jag Aarati”  
  
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (G E Shipping) has contracted to sell its 2011 built 
Kamsarmax Dry Bulk Carrier , Jag Aarati of about 80,324 dwt on December 18, 2025 to an 
unaffiliated third party. The vessel will be delivered to the new buyer in Q4 FY26.  
   
Including Jag Aarati, the company’s current owned fleet stands at 40 vessels, comprising 26 tankers (6 crude tankers, 16 product tankers, 4 LPG carriers) and 14 dry bulk carriers (2 Capesize, 
10 Kamsarmax, 2 Supramax) aggregating 3.32 mn dwt.  
 
The company has contracted to sell one Suezmax crude tanker and this transaction is expected to 
be completed in Q3 FY26. Additionally, the company has contracted to buy one secondhand Ultramax dry bulk carrier and this transaction is expected to be complet ed in Q4 FY26.
