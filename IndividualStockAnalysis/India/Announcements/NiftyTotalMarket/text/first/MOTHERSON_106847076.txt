    SAMIL Reports Highest Ever Quarterly and Half Yearly Revenues.   Booked Business at all time high of USD 106 Billion.  India (5th November 2026) – Samvardhana Motherson International Limited (SAMIL) today announced its financial results for the second quarter and half year of fiscal 2026-27, which ended on 30th September 2026.  Commenting on the results, Mr. Vivek Chaand Sehgal, Chairman, Motherson, said, “We are pleased to report a resilient performance, which reflects the continued trust of our customers and the efforts of our teams across the world. We are grateful for their support to Motherson. We achieved our highest-ever quarterly revenues, underscoring our strong execution and continued focus on operational excellence. As we continue to integrate our newly acquired businesses, we are actively working on identifying and realising synergies across the Group. These efforts are expected to create new opportunities for growth and further strengthen our capabilities. With a strong booked business of USD 106 Bn and a healthy balance sheet, reflected in a leverage ratio at 0.8x, we remain well positioned to support our customers and pursue sustainable growth opportunities to create long term value for our stakeholders.”  Key Highlights  1) Record quarterly revenue of Rs. 38,280 Cr (+27% YoY); H1FY27 revenue of Rs. 73,524 Cr (+22% YoY). 2) Booked business at an all-time high of USD 106 billion. 3) Capex of Rs. 3,585 Cr in H1FY27 (56% of EBITDA). FY27 capex outlay of Rs. 6,000 Crores +/- 10%. 4) 01 plant operationalised during the quarter; 5 expansions announced. Total 17 facilities at different stages of completion. 5) Leverage ratio at 0.8x, well within the 2.5x financial policy. 6) Acquisition of Rotary Connectors (India) announced during the quarter.           Samvardhana Motherson International Limited (SAMIL)  Samvardhana Motherson International Limited (SAMIL) is a global *Design, Engineering, Manufacturing, Assembly and Logistics (D.E.M.A.L.) specialist. The company was listed on the BSE and NSE in India in 1993. The company is focused, dynamic, and progressive, providing customers with innovative and value-added products, services, and solutions. With a diverse global customer base that includes nearly all leading automobile manufacturers worldwide, the company supports its customers from over 475 facilities across 47 countries on six continents. The company has diversified to support customers in non-automotive businesses, including technology and industrial solutions, health & medical, aerospace, consumer electronics and logistics. SAMIL is currently the largest auto ancillary in India and is ranked among the top 10 automotive suppliers worldwide. The company was selected as one of the Best Companies for 2024 by TIME.
