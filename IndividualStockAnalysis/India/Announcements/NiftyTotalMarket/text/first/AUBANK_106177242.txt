Dear Sir,  
 
We bring to your kind attention the persistent and ongoing rumour -mongering targeting AU Small 
Finance Bank (“ AU SFB”).  
 
Post a recent announcement by a peer Private Sector Bank, we have come across multiple 
unverified, misleading and malicious rumours being circulated in the market by unidentified market 
participants with the intent of causing panic amongst shareholders and  manipulating our stock price 
for speculative gains. The spread of this misinformation has led to sudden and unwarranted 
fluctuations in our share price, which can impact public trust and stability of our Bank and  in the 
Banking industry.  
 
We categorically deny these baseless rumours and wish to clarify that:  
 
• Bank’s Chief Financial Officer  (“CFO”) and Key Managerial Personnel, Mr. Vimal Jain, has been 
with the Bank since January 2010, and continues to be in his CFO position leading our Finance , 
Accounts  and other functions. He has never tendered his resignation as is being speculated in 
the rumour.  
 
• We have applied to the Reserve Bank of India ( “RBI”) for voluntary transition into a Universal 
Bank, as we had announced previously. Our application is under process with RBI, who is 
evaluating it as per its internal standard operating procedures which include inspection. 
Currently, t here is no other special audit being conducted by RBI, as is being alleged in the 
rumour.  
 
It is also important to note that such rumour -mongering about AU SFB is not a new phenomenon. 
Over the past -several years, since AU SFB was included in F&O trading segment in 2021, there have 
 
 
 
 
been multiple instances where unverified information and malicious rumours (about governance 
issues, and RBI investigation) have been deliberately circulated to create panic amongst investors 
and benefit from sudden share price fluctuation at the cost of small shareholders. We have handled 
these situations in the past, and all these rumo urs have ultimately proven to be wrong.  
 
We are a young team which has built the largest Small Finance Bank from ground -up over the last 28 
years since starting as a Direct Selling Agent (DSA), a milestone which few have achieved , and have 
now applied for voluntary transition into a Universal Bank. We are committed to building a strong, 
and stable Bank that can contribute towards building a Viksit Bharat, drive financial inclusion and 
support country’s growth for generations to co me. We have a strong Governance framework in our 
Bank, spearheaded b y an independent Board with 7 out of 10 directors being independent. Our 
franchise remains strong.  
 
We strongly condemn these unidentified individuals who are spreading this misinformation and will 
approach various regulatory and investigative authorities to take immediate action to investigate the 
sources of these rumours and  implement stringent measures against those responsible for 
spreading false narratives with malicious intent.  
 
We also want to clarify that our concern is not about our share price fluctuation, as we know that it 
will ultimately stand on the merits of our performance. Our concern is about the impact on trust that 
public as deposit holders, repose in our Bank and in the Banking industry, and how these malafide 
rumours and misinformation can undermine public’s trust in the Banking industry and harm our 
country’s growth.
