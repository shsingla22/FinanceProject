Dear Sir/Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations we wish to inform that Dalmia Cement 
(Bharat) Limited ( “DCBL”), wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, has received an Order from 
the Superintendent of CGST & CX, Range-II, Div-II of Bokaro, Jharkhand, under Section 74 of the 
Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 read with Jharkhand Goods & Service Tax Act 2017, 
pursuant to adjudication proceedings of a show cause notice regarding disallowance of Input Tax 
Credit (ITC) for FY 2018-19 to 2022-23. The Order has imposed a demand of tax along with 
interest and penalty as under: 
 
1) Tax: Rs. 11,15,812/- along with interest; and 
2) Penalty: Rs. 14,06,739/-. 
 
DCBL has good grounds to contest and will file appeal against the said Order before the Appellate 
Authority within prescribed timelines. The Order was received on December 31, 2025 at 04:50 
p.m.
