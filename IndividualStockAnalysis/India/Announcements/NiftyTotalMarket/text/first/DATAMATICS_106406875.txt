Well Pharmacy, UK’s Largest Independent Pharmacy Chain, 
onboards Datamatics to Automate Medication Record 
Management 
Mumbai, 14th October 2025  Datamatics, a global leader in Digital Technologies, 
Operations, and Experiences, today announced that Well Pharmacy, the UK’s largest 
independent pharmacy chain, has signed a long-term contract with the company to 
modernize medication record-keeping for patients in care homes. The initiative will 
leverage Datamatics’ AI-powered Intelligent Automation platform - TruCap+ for 
Intelligent Document Processing (IDP) and TruBot for Robotic Process Automation (RPA). 
Well Pharmacy operates more than 650 pharmacies across the UK and supports the health 
of local communities through consultations and essential services. In line with its digital 
transformation strategy, Well Pharmacy has selected Datamatics’ Intelligent Automation 
platform to deliver safe, consistent, and efficient medicines administration for its care 
homes. 
Datamatics will automate the Medication Administration Record (MAR) process by 
integrating its intelligent automation products, TruBot and TruCap+. Leveraging AI and 
GenAI capabilities, TruBot will automate the mundane and repetitive tasks and support 
end-to-end automation of MAR process and TruCap+ will play a pivotal role in accurately 
extracting and digitizing key patient dosage information from unstructured MAR 
documents, ensuring speed, precision, and compliance. This automation enables the 
creation of reliable Monitored Dosage System (MDS) records, minimizing manual effort, 
reducing errors, and enhancing patient safety, while empowering healthcare providers 
with real-time, actionable data. 
This solution will ensure greater accuracy in patient records, improve efficiency by 
automating updates, and reduce costs through lower manual effort and less wastage. It 
will also provide scalability to manage varied prescription formats at high volumes while 
boosting staff productivity by allowing pharmacists and care teams to spend more time 
on patient care. 
Andrew Caplan, Chief Retail Officer from Well Pharmacy said , “Accuracy is critical in 
the medical industry, and our long-term agreement with Datamatics provides a flexible, 
easy-to-use solution that ensures precision while simplifying a complex process. By 
digitizing medication records with speed and reliability, we can reduce manual errors, 
enhance patient safety, and give our teams more time to focus on delivering quality care. 
As we plan to scale to support over 70,000 patients in care homes, Datamatics’ 
automation platform will play a vital role in helping us meet growing healthcare needs 
with consistency and confidence.” 
Mitul Mehta, Chief Marketing Officer at Datamatics, said, “We are excited to partner 
with Well Pharmacy in their automation journey. With our experience in the Healthcare 
 
industry, we are confident to deliver the desired benefits of automation including 
accuracy, efficiency and process compliance.” 
Datamatics’ Intelligent Automation platform is empaneled by NHS Shared Business 
Services (SBS) in the UK for its value-driven digital solutions and is also used by several 
healthcare alliances worldwide. The Well Pharmacy contract further reinforces Datamatics’ 
position as a trusted provider of intelligent automation in the global healthcare sector. 
About Well Pharmacy   
Well Pharmacy (part of Bestway Group) is the UK’s largest independent pharmacy with
