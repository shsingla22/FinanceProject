Revolt Motors Appoints Mr. Roy Kurian as President to Accelerate 
Next Phase of Growth  
 
New Delhi, 9th October 2025:  Revolt Motors, India’s No.1 electric motorcycle brand , is proud to 
announce the appointment of Mr. Roy Kurian  as President.  With over three decades of proven 
leadership across automotive and electric mobility sectors, Mr. Kurian brings transformational 
expertise to drive Revolt’s next phase of scale and innovation.  
A seasoned business leader with 31 years of experience , Mr. Kurian has been instrumental in building 
iconic brands, steering corporate transformations, and delivering sustainable growth. He has held 
senior leadership roles at Montra Electric, Greaves Electric Mobility (Ampere), Baxy Mobility, and Tork 
Motors,  contributing significantly to EV product development, network expansion, and brand building.  
A major highlight of his career was his eight -year tenure at Yamaha Motor India  as Senior Vice 
President – Sales & Marketing . During this period, he spearheaded a remarkable turnaround, growing 
sales from 2 lakh units in 2010 to over 1 million units by 2016.  This milestone not only revitalized 
Yamaha’s brand presence but also positioned it as a formidable player in the highly competitive Indian 
motorcycle market.  
Commenting on the appointment, Ms. Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises Limited , 
said, “We are delighted to welcome Roy Kurian as President of Revolt Motors. His outstanding 
leadership track record, deep expertise in both ICE and EV sectors, and ability to drive large -scale 
transformations make him the perfect choice as Revolt steps into its  next growth phase. His 
appointment strengthens our leadership team as we continue to accelerate India’s transition to clean 
mobility.”  
On joining Revolt Motors, Mr. Roy Kurian  said, “I am thrilled to return to Revolt Motors at such an 
exciting juncture. Revolt has already established itself as India’s No.1 electric motorcycle brand, and 
the opportunity to build on this foundation to scale growth, expand reach, and strengthen customer 
connect is truly inspiring. Together with the talented team at Revolt, I look forward to shaping the 
future of sustainable mobility in India.”  
Mr. Kurian holds a BBA from Bhopal School of Social Sciences, a Post -Graduate Diploma in Marketing 
Management from Safia College, Bhopal, and has completed Global Executive Programs in Japan. 
Recognised as one of the Top 50 Brand Leaders in India by the CMO Council , his career reflects 
visionary leadership and measurable impact.  
With this appointment, Revolt Motors reaffirms its commitment to bringing in world -class leadership 
talent to power its ambition of becoming not only India’s No.1 electric motorcycle brand, but also a 
global force in sustainable mobility.
