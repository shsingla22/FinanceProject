significance within the global aerospace industry. The historic visit underscores India's rise as 
a strategic hub for Airbus, reflecting the company’s commitment to integrating India further 
into its global value chain and reinforcing its role as a reliable industrial partner.

Proprietary Information of Dynamatic Technologies About Dynamatic Technologies Limited  
Dynamatic Technologies designs and builds highly engineered products for automotive , aeronautic , 
hydraulic and security applications. With futuristic design, engineering and manufacturing facilities in 
Europe and India, we can meet customers' exacting requirements on six continents.  
Our facilities which are in  India (Bangalore ) the United Kingdom (Swindon, Bristol) and Germany 
(Schwarzenberg), are lean, green and clean, and designed to support neighbouring communities as well 
as the environment.  
Our global presence includes design labs in India and Europe, backed by a team of 90 scientists and 
650 engineers specializing in Mechanical Engineering, CAE, Metallurgy, Fluid Dynamics, and Aerospace 
R&D. We hold numerous patents and are recognized as a leading private R&D organization.  
Customer -centric innovation drives everything we do. Our focus on delivering complex, cost -effective 
solutions has helped us build lasting relationships based on trust, performance, and technological 
excellence.
