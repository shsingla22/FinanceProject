Subject: Commencement of Special Audit as Directed by RBI  
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
In compliance with the requirements under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that the special audit directed by the Reserve 
Bank of India (RBI) has commenced as of today, April 23, 2024 . 
 
This follows the RBI’s embargo dated March 4,  2024  which suspended the disbursement of new gold 
loans by our company until the satisfactory completion of the said audit. This measure was part of RBI’s 
supervisory action over concerns related to certain loan disbursement practices.  
 
We are committed to  extending  full cooperation to the special audit team to ensure a comprehensive and 
thorough audit . 
 
We shall continue to keep the stock exchanges and all relevant stakeholders informed about material 
developments if any related to the progress of the audit an d adhering to regulatory guidelines and 
ensuring complete transparency.  
 
Thank you for your continued cooperation.
