Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
At its meeting held on November 5, 2025, the Board of Directors approved a change in the Company’s 
financial year from ‘January 1 to December 31 of each year’ to ‘April 1 to March 31 of each year’. 
Pursuant to this decision and applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the Company filed 
Form RD-1 with the Regional Director. 
 
The Regional Director – Western Region, Ministry of Corporate Affairs, through Order No. 
RD(WR)/2(41)/CFY/International/AB8993231/2025/6695 dated December 18, 2025, has granted 
approval for the Company’s application to shift its financial year from the calendar year (January–
December) to the uniform financial year (April–March). The order was received by the Company 
today, i.e., December 22, 2025. 
 
Accordingly:  
- The Company’s financial year has been changed from January 1 – December 31 to April 1 –  
March 31. 
- To enable a smooth transition, the current financial year has been extended to a 15 ‑month period, 
commencing on January 1, 2025 and ending on March 31, 2026. 
- Thereafter, each financial year will begin on April 1 and conclude on March 31 of the following 
year. 
 
 
Further, in compliance with Regulation 33(3)(d) of SEBI Listing Regulations, the Company will submit 
its Audited Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results together with the Audit Report for the  
15-month period from January 1, 2025 to March 31, 2026 on or before May 30, 2026. 
 
This is for your information and record.
