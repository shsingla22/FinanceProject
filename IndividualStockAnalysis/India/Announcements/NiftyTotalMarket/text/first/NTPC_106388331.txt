We wish to inform that the Board of Directors of NTPC Limited  (‘the Company’) , in its meeting held 
today, i.e., 2 6th September 2025, has approved  the following:  
 
1. Equity infusion of upto Rs.2444.39 crore in North Eastern Electric Power Corporation L imited , 
Wholly Owned Subsidiary of the Company  for three hydro projects and  one solar project .  
 
2. The proposal of Aravali Power Company Private Limited (APCPL) , Joint Venture of the 
Company  with Haryana Power Generation Corporation Limited ( HPGCL ) and Indraprastha 
Power Generation Co. Limited  (IPGCL ), for setting up 1200 TPD capacity Municipal  Solid Waste 
(MSW) to Torrefied Charcoal Facility at Faridabad, Haryana at an  estimated cost of Rs.365.40 
crore and 1500 TPD capacity MSW to Torrefied Charcoal Facility at Gurugram, Haryana at an 
estimated cost of Rs.414.10 crore.   
 
The Board meeting commenced at 6:30 p.m. and concluded at 8:05 p.m.
