Press Release  
NTPC partner s with Africa50 to jointly develop power  projects 
across Africa  
 
New Delhi, India , 15th October 2026  – NTPC  Limited , India’s largest power utility  and  
Africa50, the specialist investor in African infrastructure, have signed a Cooperation 
Agreement to identify, assess and develop power -generation projects and cross -border 
electricity trading opportunities across Africa.  
The partnership will draw on NTPC’s engineering and operational capabilities and 
Africa50’s project development and ﬁnancing expertise to advance commercially viable 
power projects, attract investment and expand reliable electricity supply for households 
and businesses.   
NTPC will bring its extensive technical, engineering, project development, construction 
management and operations expertise to assess project feasibility, develop viable 
solutions and support the progression of identiﬁed opportunities towards 
implementati on. Africa50 will complement these capabilities by leveraging its 
proprietary continental -scale pipeline and unparalleled access to governments and 
regulators through its 34 African country shareholders to identify priority power projects, 
structure them f or investment and support their progress across the project life cycles . 
Speaking on the occasion, the leadership of both organizations underscored the 
signiﬁcance of the Cooperation Agreement for develop ment of commercially viable 
power projects across Africa.   
Mr. Gurdeep Singh, Chairman and Managing Director of NTPC Limited, said:  “We look 
forward to working with Africa50 to develop commercially viable power projects in 
mutually agreed countries in Africa. Combining NTPC’s technical and operational 
experience with Africa50’s project development capabilities and relationships across the 
continent creates a strong foundation for supporting reliable power supply and regional 
electricity trade. ”  
Mr. Alain Ebobisse, Group Chief Executive Officer  of Africa50, said:  “Reliable  and 
competitively priced power is central to Africa’s industrial and economic transformation. 
This partnership combines Africa50’s ability to develop, de -risk and deliver investable 
projects with NTPC’s proven technical expertise to translate public ambition into private 
investme nt in power generation. Together, we aim to strengthen regional power systems 
that support industrial growth, expand trade and create jobs .”  
The agreement strengthens investment partnerships between India and Africa, 
supporting the development of power infrastructure that drives economic growth across 
the continent.  
About NTPC  
NTPC Limited is a Government of India enterprise and India's largest integrated power 
company. Incorporated in 1975, NTPC has been listed on the stock exchanges since 
2004. The NTPC Group has an installed capacity of over 91.5 GW, spread across a 
diversiﬁed mix of energy sources comprising coal, gas, hydro, solar and wind, and 
contributes around one -fourth of the country's total electricity generation.  
Beyond conventional coal and gas -based generation, the NTPC Group's portfolio 
encompasses hydro, renewable and extends to coal mining, consultancy, power trading 
and power -sector training. The Group is also advancing battery energy storage systems 
(BESS) , pumped storage projects (PSP) and Nuclear Power Generation to support the 
integration of renewable energy into the grid. NTPC has set a target of 149 GW of total 
installed capacity by 2032, including 60 GW of renewable energy, in furtherance of India's 
ene rgy security and its transition to a sustainable energy future. For more information visit 
www. ntpc.co.in.  
About Africa50  
Africa50 is a specialist investor in African infrastructure, developing, de -risking and 
delivering projects to unlock economic growth across the continent. It combines a 
proprietary continental -scale pipeline, deep government relationships and structuring 
solutions to develop investment-ready projects and scalable platforms. With deep 
investment expertise across the energy, transport, and digital sectors, Africa50 mobilizes 
African and international capital to deliver sustainable ﬁnancial returns while cre ating 
economy-scale impact.  
Africa50 currently has 39  shareholders, comprising 34 African countries, the African 
Development Bank, the Central Bank of West African States (BCEAO), Bank Al -Maghrib, 
the Public Investment Corporation (PIC), and African Reinsurance Corporation. For more 
information, visit www.africa50.com .
