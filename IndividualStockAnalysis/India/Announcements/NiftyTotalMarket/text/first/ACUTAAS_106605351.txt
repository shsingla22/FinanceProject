ABOUT ACUTAAS CHEMICALS  LIMITED:  
Acutaas Chemicals Limited is a speciality chemicals manufacturer serving a wide range of 
industries, including pharmaceuticals, semiconductors, battery chemicals , personal care, 
agrochemicals, and fine chemicals. Our core strength lies in the research -driven 
development and manufacturing of advanced pharmaceutical intermediates used in both 
regulated and generic APIs, as well as intermediates for New Chemical Enti ties (NCEs).  
We are also expanding our portfolio to include high -value speciality chemicals for emerging 
technologies such as battery materials, semiconductor, and next -generation electronic 
applications.
