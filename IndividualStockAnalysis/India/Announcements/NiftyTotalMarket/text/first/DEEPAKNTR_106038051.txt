This is with reference to the intimation given by the Company to the Stock Exchanges on 19th December, 
2023 with regard to approval granted by the Board of Directors of Deepak Phenolics Limited (‘DPL’), a 
wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, for execution of a binding Term Sheet between DPL and Petronet

and Hydrogen by PLL to DPL.  
 
In furtherance of the above and pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Clause B -4 of Part A of Schedule III and 
other applicable provisions of the SEBI Listing Regulations, this is to inform you that the Board of Directors 
of DPL, has approved execution of de finitive Agreements between DPL and PLL for supply of 250 KTPA of 
Propylene and 11 KTPA of Hydrogen by PLL to DPL.
