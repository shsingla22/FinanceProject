Sub: Mandatory furnishing of PAN, KYC and Nomination details by holders of physical securities 
This is to bring to your notice that SEBI, vide its circular dated 03.11.2021  (subsequently amended by 
circular dated 10.06.2024 ) mandated that the security holders (holding securities in physical form) are 
required to update the below mentioned details - 
 
a) PAN 
b)Contact Details: Postal Address with PIN and Mobile Number
c)Bank Account Details (Bank and Branch name, bank account n umber, IFS code)
d)Specimen signature
e)Choice of Nomination (either opt -in or opt -out)- optional
The security holder(s), whose folio(s)  do not have  the above details updated  except choice of nomination 
(which is optional) , shall be eligible:  
to lodge grievance or avail any service request from the  RTA ONLY  after furnishing PAN, Contact Details
including Mobile Number, Bank Account Details  and Specimen Signature .
for any pay ment including dividend, interest or redemption in respect of such folios, ONLY  through
electronic mode with effect from 01.04.2024.
Since the aforesaid details with respect to the shares held by you in the Company are not present in the 
database in entirety, we request you to kindly furnish the same to the Registrar and Transfer Agents of the 
Company (M/s MCS Share Transfer Agent Limit ed) through the Form ISR-1, along with the necessary 
attachments / documents as stated in the form itself. While filling up the form, please strike out the portion(s) 
which are not applicable to you. 
Forms to be submitted as per the applicable case- 
S. No. Purpose  Form No.  
   1. Request for registering PAN, KYC details or changes / 
updation thereof  Form ISR – 1* 
   2. Confirmation of signature of shareholder by the Banker 
and bank account details  Form ISR – 2 
   3. Registration of Nomination  Form SH – 13 
   4. Change in existing Nomination  Form SH - 14 
   5. Declaration Form for opting out of Nomination  Form ISR – 3 
   6. Cancellation of existing Nomination  Form SH – 14 and Form ISR -3 
*Please note that the PAN to be furnished by you should be linked with Aadhaar . In case the same is not
so linked, you are requested to do the same immediately . In the event such linkage is not done then your 
PAN will be deemed to be invalid and consequently your folio will be treated in the same manner as 
applicable in case of folios for which no PAN  has been furnished .
