compliance with the provisions to General Circular No. 03/2025 dated September 22, 2025 issued by the 
Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) read with circulars issued earlier on the subject by MCA.
Regulation 36(1)(b) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, requires listed entities to send a letter providing the weblink, including 
the exact path, where complete details of the Annual Report is available, to those Member(s) who have not 
registered their Email Ids with the Company / Depository Participants (DP).
Pursuant to above we would like to inform you that the weblink along with the exact path to access the Notice 
of the 22nd AGM together with the Annual Report for the F.Y . 2025–26 of the Company is available at below:
Weblink:  https://www.coronaremedies.com/regulation-46-annual-reports/
The same can also be accessed at below path on Company’s website:
Path:  Investors> Annual Report> Annual Report 2025-26
This letter is being sent to those member(s) who have not registered their Email Ids either with the Company 
or with DP .

share of ₹ 10/- each for the financial year ended on March 31, 2026, subject to approval by the members at 
the ensuing Annual General Meeting scheduled to be held on Thursday, July 09, 2026.
As per the guidelines issued by the SEBI, shareholders are eligible to receive the dividend only through 
electronic mode. Members are further requested to update KYC details viz., PAN, choice of nomination, Email 
Ids, mobile number, bank account details, specimen signature etc. with their respective DP .
We encourage you to register / update your Email Ids to receive electronic communication and support the 
green initiative.
