From, 
Jay Mistry 
Mumbai 
To 
The Board of Directors

Subject: Resignation from the position of Company Secretary (Key Managerial Personnel) 
& Chief Compliance Officer of the SBFC Finance Limited (“Company”) 
Dear Sirs / Madam, 
I hereby tender my resignation from the position of Company Secretary (Key Managerial 
Personnel), Compliance Officer & Chief Compliance Officer of SBFC Finance Limited 
(“Company”), to pursue other career opportunity outside the Company. 
I request the Board to relieve me of my duties effective closing of business hours on August 07, 
2024. 
I further convey my sincere thanks to the Board of Directors of the Company, MD & CEO, CFO 
and the Senior Management for their support and cooperation extended to me during my tenure 
as Company Secretary, Compliance Officer & Chief Compliance Officer. 
I also tender my resignation from the position of Company Secretary in SBFC Home Finance 
Private Limited, wholly owned subsidiary of Company, effective closing of business hours on 
July 27, 2024. 
Further, I request the Company to file the necessary forms with the Registrar of Companies, 
Ministry of Corporate Affairs, and intimation to the Stock Exchanges and the Reserve Bank of 
India, to give the effect of this resignation. 
Thanking You, 
Yours Sincerely 
Jay Mistry
