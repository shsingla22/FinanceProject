PRESS RELEASE  
G E Shipping takes delivery of 2010 built Suezmax Crude Carrier  
“Jag Laadki ” 
 
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (G E Shipping) took delivery of a 2010 built 
Suezmax Crude Carrier “Jag Laadki” of about 164,716 dwt . The company had contracted to buy 
the vessel in Q2 FY26. This vessel is financed entirely from internal accruals.  
The Company’s current owned fleet stands at 41 vessels, comprising 27 tankers (6 crude carriers, 
17 product tankers, 4 LPG carriers) and 14 dry bulk carriers aggregating 3.37 mn dwt.  
Additionally, the company has contracted to sell one Suezmax crude tanker and one Medium 
Range product tanker. These two sale transactions are expected to happen in Q3 FY26.
