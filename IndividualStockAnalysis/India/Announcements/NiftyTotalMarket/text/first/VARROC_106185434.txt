Varroc appoints Ms. Liselott Kilaas as an Independent 
Director   
 
Pune, 27 March  ‘25: Varroc, a leading global tier -1 automotive supplier, announced the 
appointment of M rs. Liselott Kilaas  as Independent Director  its Board of Directors. This 
strategic appointment reflects Varroc’s commitment to strengthening its leadership with 
global expertise and diverse perspectives.  
Commenting on the appointment,  Mr. Tarang Jain, Chairman & Managing Director, Varroc, 
said, “We are delighted to welcome M rs. Liselott Kilaas to our Board of Directors. Her vast 
experience and strategic insights will be invaluable in driving Varroc’s growth and 
enhancing our global competitiveness.”  
On her appointment, M rs. Liselott K ilaas said, “I am honoured  and excited to join Varroc’s 
Board as an Independent Director. I look forward to contributing my expertise  to support 
the company’s growth and strategic vision. ” 
Mrs. Kilaas holds an MBA from IMD Business School and a master’s degree in mathematics 
and statistics, providing a strong academic foundation that supports her distinguished 
career. Currently, she serves on the boards of several multinational corporations an d has 
a proven track record of steering businesses through dynamic and complex environments. With extensive experience across the healthcare, technology, finance, industrial, and 
sustainability sectors, she has held key leadership positions in globally r enowned 
organizations. She brings a wealth of expertise in strategic leadership, operational 
efficiency, corporate governance, and sustainability.

About Varroc Engineering Ltd.  
Varroc Engineering Ltd is a global tier -1 automotive component group. It was incorporated 
in 1988. The group manufactures and supplies electricals -electronics, polymers, metallics 
and exterior lighting systems to leading OEMs  with end -to-end capabilities across design, 
development and manufacturing for two -wheeler, three -wheeler, passenger vehicles, 
commercial vehicle, and off -highway vehicle worldwide. The group income was ₹ 75,795 
million in FY24. The group employs more than  7,000 employees (750 + R&D Engineers), 
has 36 global operating manufacturing facilities supported by 7 R&D Centres, and has 
more than 100 patents.  
Varroc Engineering Limited’s shares are listed on the National Stock Exchange of India 
Limited & BSE Limited (VARROC) (541578).
