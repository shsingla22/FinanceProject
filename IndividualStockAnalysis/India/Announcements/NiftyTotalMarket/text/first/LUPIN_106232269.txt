Adoption of new set of Articles of Association of the Company p ursuant to the provisions of the 
Companies Act, 2013. 
 
The existing Articles of Association (‘AoA’) of the Company wer e based on the erstwhile provisions of the 
Companies Act, 1956 and several clauses contain references to s pecific sections of the Companies Act, 1956. 
With implementation of the Companies Act, 2013 (‘the Act’) and other statutory revisions, the existing AoA 
requires alteration of several clauses. Since the changes requi red in the existing AoA to align with the 
provisions of the Act, were numerous, the Board considered it e xpedient to adopt the new set of AoA by 
replacing the existing AoA. 
 Accordingly, the Board at its meeting held on May 14, 2025, app roved the adoption of new set of AoA of 
the Company, subject to approval of the Members of the Company by way of Special Resolution.
