Dear Sir,  
 
In continuation to our intimation dated August  07, 202 5 and in terms  of Regulation 30 of the  SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015  [‘Listing Regulations ’], we wish to inform 
you that at the meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company held  today i.e. on August  13, 2025, inter 
alia, the following matters were considered : 
 
1. Approval of  the standalone and consolidated unaudited financial results for the first quarter ended on 
June  30, 202 5 along with a Limited Review Report from the Auditors for the first quarter ended on 
June  30, 202 5, in terms of Regulation 33 of the Listing Regulations.  
 
A copy of the unaudited financial results , along with the limited review report is enclosed .  
 
2. Declaration of Interim  Dividend  for F.Y. 2025-26 of Rs. 31 per equity share of Rs. 10 each.  
 
The record date for the purpose of determining the members eligible to receive the interim  dividend  
2025-26, has been fixed as  August 21, 2025.  
 
The dividend will be paid within prescribed timelines.   
 
The Board meeting commenced at 16.01 hours (IST) and concluded at 16:25  hours  (IST) .
