Maruti Suzuki India Limited Financial Results Q2 and H1 FY  2026-27 
New  Delhi,  October  27, 2026: The Board of Directors of Maruti Suzuki India Limited (MSIL) today approved the 
financial results for the period Q2 (July -September) FY  2026-27. 
Highlights: Q2 (July- September), FY 202 6-27 
The Company achieved its highest -ever quarterly total sales volume of 696,654 units, registering a growth of 
26.5% over the same period previous year. The Company’s growth in the domestic passenger vehicle market was 
broad -based, with small car sales increasing by 35.9% and SUV sales by 68.2% . The Company’s recently introduced 
SUVs  received a  strong market response and contributed to this robust growth.  The Company’s domestic market 
share increased by 2.2 percentage points to 40.8%. Recent capacity additions have enabled the Company to serve  
the strong demand. Even with enhanced production capacity, demand continued to outpace supply resulting in  a 
low network inventory of about  17 days and pending bookings of about  200,000 units at the end of the quarter.  
In export markets, despite the challenging geopolitical situation, the Company could achieve sales of 108,119 
units in Q2 FY 2026 -27, as compared to 110,487 units in Q2 FY25 -26.  
During the quarter, the Company registered its highest -ever N et Sales of INR 506,155  million  against INR 401,358  
million in the same period of the previous year.  
The Operating Profit (EBIT) in the quarter stood at INR 28,037 million as compared to INR 33,820 million in Q2 
FY25 -26 owing to West- Asia conflict linked commodity increases. Sequentially this translated to a growth of 
10.8% from Q1  FY26 -27 to Q2 FY26 -27. 
However, non -operating income declined sharply  in Q2 owing to mark -to-market losses on invested surplus. 
With this, the Net Profit for the quarter stood at  INR 24,149 million compared to  INR 33, 028 million the same 
period previous year.  
Highlights: H1 (April- September), FY 202 6-27 
During this period, the Company achieved its highest -ever half- yearly sales volume of 1,379,378 units, registering 
a growth of 27.9%  year -on-year. This comprised record half- yearly domestic sales of 1,146,523 units and exports 
of 232,855 units.  
The Company posted  its highest -ever half- yearly Net Sales of INR 1,005,746 million  in H1  FY 2026-27 as compared 
to INR 767,564 million in H1  FY 2025-26.   
Net Profit in H1  FY 2026-27 was at INR 57,67 0 million as against INR 70,609 million in H1  FY 2025 -26. 
Osamu Suzuki Centre of Excellence  
Mr. Osamu Suzuki has been a legend who put his trust in India and over more than four decades transformed 
the Indian car industry with his fundamental philosoph y and manufacturing concepts. The Board decided to 
honour and remember his contribution by spreading these widely for the growth and competitiveness of 
manufacturing sector at large in India. Accordingly , the Board approved the incorporation  of a Section 8 company 
for setting up  the Osamu Suzuki Centre of Excellence . The Centre of Excellence will using Japanese manufacturing 
philosoph y, cultur e and teachings of Mr. O samu  Suzuki, promote and enhance vocational  and future ready 
technical skills and concepts for advancing manufacturing excellence across India, as a Corporate Social 
Responsibility activity.
