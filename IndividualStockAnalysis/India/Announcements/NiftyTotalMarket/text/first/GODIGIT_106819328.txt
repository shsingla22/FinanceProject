TORONTO, October 14 , 2026  
 
Fairfax Declared  “Strategic Buyer ” in Auction to Acquire  Interest in IDBI Bank ; Plans to 
Contribute  Digit Insurance  and IIFL Capital  to IDBI Bank  
 
Fairfax Financial Holdings Limited (“ FFHL ”) (TSX: FFH and FFH.U) and Fairfax India Holdings 
Corporation (“ FIH”, and  together with FFHL, collectively, “ Fairfax ”) (TSX: FIH.U) jointly 
announce today  that the Government of India has declared  a wholly -owned subsidiary of FFHL  (the 
“Fairfax Purchaser ”) the “Strategic Buyer ” for a 60.72% equity stake in IDBI Bank Limited 
(“IDBI Bank ”) pursuant to the Government of India’s proposed strategic disinvestment of IDBI 
Bank. Under the terms of its bid, the Fairfax Purchaser  (or an affiliate of it)  would acquire the equity 
stake  in IDBI Bank (the “ Purchase Transaction ”) at a price of INR 81 per share for a total of 
approximately INR 528.78  billion (approximately US $5.6 billion) , representing a premium to the 
book value of IDBI Bank  as of June 30, 2026 , as measured pursuant to Indian Accounting Standards , 
of approximately 23%. 
“We are very excited to be selected by the Government of India to be the purchaser of the 
Government’s interest in IDBI Bank ,” said Prem Watsa, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of 
FFHL. “ We thank Prime Minister Narendra Modi and his team for undertaking a highly transparent 
and competitive process before selecting us as the buyer . We assure the Government of India that 
we are committed to build ing the world’s finest financial institution from India.  This transaction 
represents a once -in-a-lifetime opportunity to purchase a majority interest in a bank in one of the 
world’s fastest growing economies and to combine it with a successful and growing insurance 
company and an established provider of investme nt banking and wealth management services in the 
same jurisdiction. We intend to support India’s journey toward Viksit Bharat and a US$30+ trillion 
economy, and our ambition is to play a critical role in the Prime Minister’s vision of India as a 
developed economy by 2047. We believe a successful IDBI Bank can transform the lives of average 
Indian citizens, and our vision is to help enhance India’s credit -to-GDP ratio. Combining IDBI 
Bank, Digit, and IIFL Capital would create an entity that is well-positioned to participate in the 
continued growth and success of Indian markets, and we expect it will provide meaningful  and 
significant benefits to Indian consumers and businesses, as well as to IDBI Bank, Digit, IIFL Capital 
and to Fairfax for many decades to come. We believe that a  unified financial services company that 
can offer a one -stop solution for the financial needs of Indians will be of significant benefit to India 
and its economy and represents an incredible opportunity for IDBI Bank and Fairfax to benefit from 
the continu ed growth of an emerging market with a growing middle class. We expect bank assets 
and deposits in India to continue to grow, and we feel that a newly -revitalized  IDBI Bank that can 
also offer clients insurance products through Digit and investment banking and wealth management 
  services through IIFL  Capital would  be in a very competitive position to take advantage of this 
growth.”  
Fairfax’s objective in buying t he 60.72% interest in IDBI Bank is to make it, over time, a leading 
global Indian bank headquartered in  India. To assist in achieving this objective, Fairfax proposes to  
contribute all of FFHL’s  interests in Go Digit Life Insurance  Limited  and Go Digit General 
Insurance Limited  (collectively, “Digit ”) and all of FIH’s interest in IIFL Capital Services Limited 
(“IIFL Capital ”) to IDBI Bank . As a leading unified Indian financial services company,  IDBI Bank 
would have the ability to become a  leading provider of not just banking services in India, playing a 
critical role in enhanc ing India’s credit -to-GDP ratio, but also property and casualty insurance  
services , investment banking  and wealth management services , and has the potential to significantly 
transform the lives of average Indian citizens . Among other necessary approvals, t he approval of 
the Reserve Bank of India will be required in connection with the proposed contribution of Digit 
(the “ Digit Contribution ”) and the proposed contribution of IIFL  Capital  (the “ IIFL Capital 
Contribution ”, and together with the Digit Contribution, the “ Contribution Transactions ”) and 
Fairfax’s plan to create a leading Indian global bank.   
“We are very excited about the prospect of combining  Digit with IDBI  Bank  as it w ould significantly 
strengthen Digit’s distribution footprint, allowing us to continue our growth and service offerings 
for the benefit of all Indians ,” said Kamesh Goyal, Founder and Chairman of Digit . 
“This partnership would create  a unique opportunity for IIFL Capital to accelerate growth across 
investment banking, institutional equities, wealth and asset management ,” said Nirmal Jain, Founder 
of IIFL Group . “By combining our capital markets expertise with the scale and customer reach of a 
leading bank, we can bring a broader range of investment and wealth solutions to millions of 
customers. Given the significant under -penetration of wealth and asset management  in India, we 
believe th is combined platform would be  exceptionally well positioned for long -term growth.”  
Fairfax currently expects the purchase price for the Purchase Transaction to be satisfied through a 
combination of cash on hand or otherwise available to Fairfax through existing credit sources and 
potential  asset sales. In connection  with the Contribution Transactions , Digit  and IIFL  Capital  would 
be contributed to IDBI Bank at their respective market prices , all in accordance with all applicable 
law and regulation . In addition to the approval of the Reserve Bank of India  and all other necessary 
legal and regulatory approvals , each of the Contribution Transactions will be subject to the review 
and the approval of the Securities and Exchange Board of India, and the Digit Contribution will be 
subject to the approval of the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority  of India . 
Following the consummation of the proposed  Purchase Transaction and Contribution Transactions , 
Fairfax expects  that the operations of the newly combined company w ould continue to be led by 
Rakesh Sharma, the current Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of IDBI Bank. Fairfax 
also plans to include in the leadership group for IDBI Bank C. VR. Rajendran, the former Chief 
Executive Officer of CSB Bank,  as well as N. S. Kannan, the former Managing Director and Chief 
Executive Officer of ICICI Prudential Life Company and Chief Financial  Officer of ICICI Bank , 
who will act as Chairman , all subject to approval of the Reserve Bank of India . Each of Mr. Sharma, 
Mr. Rajendran and Mr. Kannan are well -known to Fairfax, and have proven to be capable and 
effective executives of Indian financial institutions over decades .  
  Fairfax does not expect there to be any change in the governance, management or operating structure 
of either Digit  or IIFL Capital resulting from the  proposed Contribution  Transactions . The current 
leadership team at Digit , led by Kamesh Goyal, would  remain in place and continue to be responsible 
for Digit’s strategy and execution , and the management team of IIFL Capital , led by R. 
Venkataraman, Vasudev Jagannath  and Nemkumar H , would  continue to execute on its plan to serve 
as a leading platform for capital market services in India, encompassing institutional equities, 
investment banking, wealth and asset management, and mutual fund distribution.

Transactions Conditional  
While the Government of India has declared the  Fairfax Purchaser as the “ Strategic Buyer ” in 
connection with  the Government of India’s proposed strategic disinvestment of IDBI Bank, this 
does not represent  the final stage of the Government’s sale process . While  Fairfax expects to 
continue to work diligently and on an exclusive basis with the Govern ment of India in order to 
satisfy all remaining requirements in connection with the IDBI Bank disinvestment process , no 
binding definitive agreements in connection with the Purchase Transaction or the Contribution  
Transactions  have been entered into by any of Fairfax, IDBI Bank, the Government of India, or any 
other party, and there can be no assurances that any such transactions will be consummated on the 
currently proposed terms, or at all. It is also possible that the Purchas e Transaction could  occur 
without consummation of either or both of the Contribution Transactions . Furthermore, i f definitive 
transaction agreements relating to the proposed Purchase Transaction or the Contribution  
Transactions  are entered into  by the parties , each of the proposed Purchase Transaction and 
Contribution Transactions  would be subject to the satisfaction of numerous governmental, 
regulatory, equityholder, debtholder, and other approvals and consents.   
This news release  does not constitute an offer or a solicitation of any kind, and  no offer will be made
