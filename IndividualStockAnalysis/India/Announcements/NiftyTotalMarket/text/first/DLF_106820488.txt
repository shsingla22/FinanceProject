PRESS RELEASE  
 
DLF announces Financial Results for Q2FY27  
Operating Cash ﬂow of Rs 1,858 crore further reinforces underlying business strength  
Net proﬁt at Rs 1,301 crore, y -o-y growth of 11%  
 
New Delhi, October 16, 2026  
DLF Limited delivered a strong Q2FY27 performance, combining resilient demand, disciplined 
execution and robust cash generation. The results reinforce the strength of DLF’s operating platform, 
capital position and ability to create sustainable long -term va lue. 
Financial Highlights for Q2FY27 –  DLF Limited (Consolidated)  
 Consolidated Revenue stood at Rs 2,298 crore  
 Gross margins at 47%  
 EBITDA stood at Rs 1,060 crore  
 Net Proﬁt Rs 1,301 crore  
 Operating cash ﬂow of Rs 1,858 crore  
Financial Highlights for Q2FY27 –  DLF Cyber City Developers Limited (Consolidated)  
 Consolidated Revenue stood at Rs 2,014 crore  
 EBITDA stood at Rs 1,537 crore, y -o-y growth of 9%  
 Net Proﬁt at Rs 752 crore, y -o-y growth of 17%  
New sales bookings stood at Rs 3,800 crore during the quarter. The Aureva in Gurugram, DLF’s curated luxury retirement housing address, witnessed a complete sell out underscoring strong demand for 
such diﬀerentiated oﬀerings. Our super -luxury oﬀering - The Dahlias, continues to attract strong 
demand from our discerning customers.  
 
DLF’s brand leadership, deep market presence and deﬁned strategy provide strong visibility on its 
stated medium -term growth goals.  
 
Operating cash flow was Rs 1,858 crore, marked yet another quarter of sustained cash generation in 
the business. The net cash position stood at Rs 15,084 crore despite a significant dividend outflow of 
Rs 1,980 crore  
 
Our rental portfolio, spanning approximately 50 msf, continues to demonstrate robust operating 
strength with industry -leading occupancy. A strong operational portfolio, marquee destinations 
currently under development along with an identiﬁed pipeline have  laid down a strong foundation to 
deliver steady and sustainable long -term growth across our rental business.  
With a signiﬁcant land bank, a robust launch pipeline across development and rental businesses, a 
strengthened balance sheet and consistent cash ﬂow generation, DLF is well positioned to capitalize on the structural upcycle in the sector . We remain focus ed on delivering sustained, proﬁtable growth 
and long- term value for all stakeholders.  
 
About DLF  
DLF is India's leading real estate developer and has close to eight decades of track record of sustained 
growth, customer satisfaction, and innovation. DLF has developed more than 185 real estate projects and developed an area more than 352 million square feet (approx.). DLF Group has 275 msf (approx.) 
of development potential across residential and commercial segment including current projects under 
execution in the identiﬁed pipeline. The group has an annuity portfolio of 50 msf (approx ). DLF is 
primarily engaged in the business of development and sale of residential properties (the 
“Development Business”) and the development and leasing of commercial and retail properties (the 
“Annuity Business”).
