Sub: Comments of Board on fine levied by the Exchanges. 
Madam/ Sir, 
This has reference to your mail dated 27.05.2026 informing imposition of fine of 
Rs.5,31,000/- (including GST @18% on basic fine amount of Rs.4,50,000/-) for non-
compliance with provisions of Regulation 17(1) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. In this 
regard, we would like to submit the following: 
a) As advised, the matter regarding imposition of fine for non-compliance with provisions
of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 was informed to the Promoter of the Company i.e.
Government of India through Ministry of Mines, being the Administrative Ministry on
02.06.2026 and request was made to expedite for early appointment of requisite
number of Independent Directors to ensure compliance of all statutory requirements of
SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
b) Further, the Company vide letter dated 02.06.2026 clarified to BSE and NSE that,
NALCO is a CPSE and the authority for appointment of Directors is vested with the
President of India. The Company does not have any control on the same. Therefore,
the non-compliances, stated in the e-mail dated 27.05.2026 for the quarter ended
31.03.2026 may be condoned and penalties imposed may be waived.
c) The matter was also placed before the Board of Directors in their 369th meeting held
on 14.07.2026. The Board took cognizance of the communications of BSE and NSE
dated 27.05.2026, imposing fine of Rs.5,31,000/- (including GST @18% on basic fine
amount of Rs. 4,50,000/-) each for non-compliance with provisions of Regulation 17(1)
of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for the quarter ended 31.03.2026.
d) The Board advised the Company to inform BSE and NSE that, NALCO is a CPSE and
the authority for appointment of Directors is vested with Govt. of India and hence the
Company does not have any control on the same. Therefore, the non-compliances,
