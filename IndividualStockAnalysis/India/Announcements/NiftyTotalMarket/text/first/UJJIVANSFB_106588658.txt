Sub:  Update on our  Application to the Reserve Bank of India for Voluntary Transition from Small Finance

Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure  Requirements) Regulations , 2015 and in 
continuation to our letter no. USFB/CS/SE/2024 -25/116 dated February 04, 2025 , we hereby inform that we are in 
receipt today of RBI letter dated April 13, 2026 .

portfolio. However, they were of the view that there is scope for progress in this area.  Therefore, RBI have  returned 
the above -mentioned  application and advised the Bank to consider applying again after demonstrating a diversified 
loan portfolio.
