Sub: Upgradation of GRSE to 'Schedule A' CPSE by the Government of India 
Dear Sir/ Madam, 
1.Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements),
Regulations 2015, we are pleased to inform that Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers Limited
(GRSE) has been upgraded from 'Schedule S' to 'Schedule A' CPSE by the Government of India. This is
a significant milestone for GRSE, reflecting its robust financial performance, operational efficiency, and
contribution to national security.
2.The Department of Public Enterprises, Ministry of Finance, Government of India, officially
notified this upgradation on its website on 17 Sep 2024.
3.This upgradation of GRSE to 'Schedule A' status will enable GRSE to enhance its senior
management capacity, allowing it to more effectively oversee and manage its business.
