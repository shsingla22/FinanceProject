Mumbai, November  07, 202 6 
Tata Steel reports Consolidated EBITDA of Rs 10,665 crores  for the quarter                               
and Rs 20,035 crores for the half year ended September 30, 2026  
Highlights:  
 
▪ Consolidated Revenues for the Jul – Sep 2026 quarter were Rs 65,436 crores and EBITDA was Rs 10,665 
crores with a margin of around 16%. EBITDA improved by 14% QoQ and 17% YoY.  
o India1 revenues were Rs 4 2,653 crores and EBITDA was Rs 10,634 crores, which translates to a margin 
of 25%.  Crude steel production was up 7% QoQ to 6.18 million tons . Deliveries were up 16% QoQ to 
5.98 million tons . 
o Netherlands revenues were €1, 395 million and EBITDA was € 72 million , aided by the restart of the Direct 
Sheet Plant in early August. Liquid steel production was 1. 52 mn tons and deliveries were 1. 30 mn tons.   
o UK revenues were £ 411 million and EBITDA loss stood at £ 56 million. Deliveries stood at 0. 38 million 
tons in the quarter.  
▪ Consolidated Revenues for the half year were Rs 1,26,231 crores and EBITDA was Rs 20,035 crores with 
a margin of around 16%. EBITDA improved by 21% YoY despite the challenging operating environment.  
o India1 revenues were Rs 79,641 crores and EBITDA was Rs 20,541 crores, which translates to an 
EBITDA margin of 26%. EBITDA improved by 27% YoY.  
o Netherlands revenues were € 2,840 million and EBITDA was €76 million . 
o UK revenues were £ 895 million and EBITDA loss narrowed by 22% YoY to £83 million .  
▪ The company spent around Rs 3,2 80 crores on capital expenditure during the quarter and Rs 6,860 crores 
for the half year .  
o Significant capex projects underway include 4.8 MTPA capacity expansion at Neelachal Ispat Nigam Limited  
and downstream growth projects such as the 0.7 MTPA Hot Rolled Pickling & Galvanising Line and 300 kt of  
the Tinplate expansion . 
▪ Net debt stood at Rs 91,423 crores and Net debt to EBITDA was 2. 4x. Our group liquidity remains strong at                  
Rs 40,076 crores, which includes cash & cash equivalents of ~Rs 10,300 crores .

Management Comments :  
Mr. T V Narendran, C hief Executive Officer & Managing Director:  
“We delivered strong growth in deliveries and earnings this quarter, backed by a resilient supply chain and close 
customer engagement, despite ongoing geoeconomic uncertainty and higher raw material costs . Deliveries grew 9% 
QoQ to ~7.92 million tons and EBITDA was up 14% QoQ to ~Rs 10,665 crores. India continued to be the cornerstone 
of our performance  with volumes growing 16% QoQ despite a seasonally softer quarter , reflecting the strength of 
our market presence and customer reach . Our A utomotive & Special Products vertical achieved ‘best ever’ 2Q 
volumes of ~1.1 million tons, driven by 26% growth in hi -end sales.  Tata Tiscon sales reached ~2.7  million tons  over 
the last twelve months  and generated revenues ~$1.8 billion . Our  e-commerce platforms, Aashiyana and DigECA, 
generated Gross Merchandise Value of around Rs 2, 961 crores, up 50% YoY . Downstream sales maintained their 
growth momentum, along with significant gains in Tata Steel colors and Tinplate . We also expanded presence in 
emerging segments such as shipbuilding, data centers and containers.  We continue to make steady progress on our 
volume and value growth  agenda in India. The 4.8 MTPA expansion at Neelachal Ispat Nigam Limited has entered 
the execution phase. Construction is progressing on the Hot Rolled Pickling & Galvanising Line and the Tinplate 
expansion project.  In Netherlands, the successful restart  of the Direct Sheet Plant and its return to rated capacity 
has supported improvement s in both the product mix and cost profile . In the UK, while the Llanwern pickle line is 
now fully operational , the market conditions  continue to be influenced by elevated imports and inventory build -up 
across parts of the  value chain . We remain engaged with policymakers and regulators to support a more balanced 
and sustainable market environment .” 
Mr. Koushik Chatterjee , Executive Director & Chief Financial Officer : 
“Tata Steel deliver ed resilient  performance in a volatile operating environment. Higher volumes in India and 
operational normalisation in the Netherlands helped offset elevated input costs.  Consolidated EBITDA  for the quarter 
rose 14% QoQ to Rs 10,665 crores  and remained above Rs 13,000 per ton. This was primarily driven by strength of 
the India business, which delivered EBITDA of Rs 17, 790 per ton  and a margin of 25%,  despite a seasonally softer 
quarter . Neelachal Ispat Nigam Limited generated EBITDA of Rs 318 crores , and t he amalgamation of NINL with 
Tata Steel remains on track for completion during the financial year, subject to final NCLT approval . In Netherlands , 
EBITDA improved from €4 million in 1Q  to €7 2 million  in 2Q , aided by favourable pricing and restart of the Direct 
Sheet Plant in early August. We are working closely with the regulators and are focused on maintaining compliance 
and operational stability . In the UK, subdued  demand and elevated inventor ies across the  supply chain weighed on 
volume s and performance . The UK EAF project continues to advance as construction activities ramp up . On half 
year basis, Consolidated EBITDA was  Rs 20,035 crores , up 21% YoY . We delivered savings of around Rs 1, 793 
crores and are on track to achieve Rs 7,140 crores for the full year.  We spent Rs 3,280 crores for the quarter and 
Rs 6,860 crores in the half year . Dividend payout was Rs 4,989 crores.  Net debt st ands  at Rs 91,423 crores , which 
translates to Net debt to EBITDA of 2.4x. Our group liquidity remains strong at Rs 4 0,076 crores, which includes 
cash & cash equivalents of ~Rs 1 0,300  crores . Our focus remains on cost optimisation, operational improvements 
and working capital management to maximise cash flows .”

About Tata Steel  
• Tata Steel group is among the top global steel companies with an annual crude steel capacity of 36 million tonnes 
per annum.  
• It is one of the world's most geographically diversified steel producers, with operations and commercial presence 
across the world.  
• The group recorded a consolidated turnover of around US$26 billion in the financial year ending March 31, 2026.  
• Tata Steel Limited, together with its subsidiaries, associates, and joint ventures, is spread across five continents 
with an employee base of over 77,000.  
• Tata Steel has announced its major sustainability objectives including Net Zero by 2045.  
• The Company has been on a multi -year digital -enabled business transformation journey intending to be the leader 
in ‘Digital Steel making’. The Company has received the World Economic Forum’s Global Lighthouse recognition 
for its Jamshedpur, Kalinganagar, and IJmuiden Plants. 78% of our steel comes from these global lighthouses. 
Tata Steel has been recognised with ‘Digital Enterprise of India - Steel’ Award 2024 by Economic Times CIO.  
• The Company has been recognised with the World Economic Forum’s Global Diversity Equity & Inclusion 
Lighthouse 2023 and is the world’s largest SA8000 Certified organisation in terms of workforce coverage.  
• The Company has been a part of the DJSI Emerging Markets Index since 2012 and has been consistently ranked 
among the top 10 steel companies in the DJSI Corporate Sustainability Assessment since 2016.  
• Tata Steel’s Jamshedpur Plant is India’s first site to receive ResponsibleSteelTM Certification. Subsequently, its 
Kalinganagar and Meramandali plants have also received the certification. In India, Tata Steel now has more than 
90% of its steel production from ResponsibleSteelTM certified sites.
