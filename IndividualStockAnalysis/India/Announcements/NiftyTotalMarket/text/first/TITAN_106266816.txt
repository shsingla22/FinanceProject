  TANISHQ HONOURED WITH DIAMONDS DO GOOD AWARD FOR EXEMPLARY PURPOSE -DRIVEN 
LEADERSHIP 
The prestigious award recognizes  Tanishq & Mia by Tanishq’s leadership in ethical sourcing, community 
upliftment, and sustainability in the natural diamond industry  
June 2025 : Tanishq and Mia by Tanishq, leading jewellery brands from the House of Tata, are proud to be 
honored  with the Diamonds Do Good Purpose -Driven Business Leadership Award  at the prestigious 
Diamonds Do Good Awards 2025, held in Las Ve gas. The award was received by Mr. Ajoy Chawla, CEO, 
Jewellery Division, Titan Company Ltd  on behalf of Tanishq and Mia by Tanishq.  The award acknowledges 
the brands’ exemplary leadership in embedding ethics, sustainability, and community upliftment at the 
core of the natural diamond business.  
Organized by the Diamonds Do Good Foundation, the renowned awards celebrate global leaders driving 
meaningful change across the natural diamond industry —through ethical sourcing, social upliftment, and 
education. This recognition puts the spotlight on Tani shq and Mia not just as jewellery brands, but as 
champions of responsible luxury in India and globally.  
At the core of this recognition is the brands’ implementation of the Titan Supplier Engagement Protocol 
(TSEP), which ensures responsible sourcing through the 4P framework —Place, People, Process, and 
Planet —and validated by third -party audits. The diamonds  are fully traceable and in line with the 
Kimberley Process Certification Scheme (KPCS). Additionally, both brands promote gold exchange as a 
sustainable alternative to further mining. Tanishq and Mia have also established artisan parks, providing 
safe, po llution -free and dignified workspaces to India’s skilled karigars —underscoring their dedication to 
craftsmanship and well -being. These efforts are part of Titan’s broader, ambitious ESG agenda , setting 
new benchmarks for the industry.  
Tanishq and Mia by Tanishq were especially recognized for their enduring commitment to:  
●Ethical sourcing and full traceability of natural diamonds
●Uplifting artisan communities, especially India’s karigars
●Championing sustainability across the value chain
●Redefining responsible luxury in India and globally
In acceptance speech, Mr. Ajoy Chawla, CEO, Jewellery Division, Titan Company Ltd,  said: “In a free 
enterprise, the community is not just another stakeholder in business but is, in fact, the very purpose of its 
existence.” — Shri Jamsetji Tata.  
At the Tata Group, this guiding principle has shaped a legacy — one built on trust, empathy, and inclusive 
progress. Titan was born of that legacy, and Tanishq is its living expression — where every piece we design 
reflects the beliefs of those who wear it  and honors  the hands that shape it. On behalf of everyone at  
Tanishq and Titan Company Limited, I thank you for this incredible recognition. I feel proud to stand amidst 
a global community that believes in celebrating the good that diamonds can do.  
We pledge to play our part — both as a responsible member of this industry and as part of the Tata Group, 
which has always placed purpose and community before profit.  
This honor  is a reminder that there is still much to be done — because being good is a journey, a fulfilling 
one indeed; and because “being good is more important than being great.”
