This is in continuation of our letter s dated October 01, 2018 , March 09, 2019 , March  
20, 2020 , August 20, 2021  and May 10, 202 2 this is to inform you that the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court of India vide its Order dated September  27, 2024, declined to exercise 
jurisdiction to interfere with its judgement dated May 09, 2022. The Supreme Court 
judgement upheld the  judgement of the Hon’ble Nagpur Bench of the Bombay High 
Court quash ing and set ting aside the Letter dated March 19, 2020 issued by MIHAN 
India Limited  annulling the bidding process for the Nagpur Airport , and thus, directed 
MIHAN India Limited to execute the Concession Agreement with G MR Airports Limited 
/ its Special Purpose Vehicle .  
 
The Hon’ble Supreme Court has dis posed of  the Curative Petitions  filed by Union of 
India and Airport Authority of India by recording the submission of the Ld. Solicitor 
General of India , that the said Curative Petitions were not pressed before the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court by the Petitioners  on account of no valid grounds under the curative 
jurisdiction to challenge the earlier ruling of court in its order dated May 09, 2022.  
 
With all the  legal hurdles finally concluded , the Authorities are required to execute 
the Concession Agreement at the earliest for  the Nagpur Airport with GMR Airports 
Limited / its Special Purpose Vehicle .  
 
GMR had emerged as the highest bidder in the tender process conducted by MIHAN 
India Limited  for the Nagpur Airport in 2019. The Letter of Award was issued to GMR 
in March 2019. However,  Government of Maharashtra decided to cancel the tender 
process in March 2020 and MIHAN India  Limited issued a Letter annulling the bid 
process, which was set aside by the Hon’ble Nagpur Bench  of the Bombay High Court 
in August 2021 and the said judgement was upheld by the Hon’ble  Supreme Court 
vide its judgement dated May 09, 2022.  The said Order was challenged by the 
Authorities seeking for a reconsideration  of the judgement through a review petition 
and later curative petition  that was ultimately disposed -off by Hon’ble Supreme Court 
by its order dated September 27, 2024.
