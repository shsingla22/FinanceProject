as Defence Subject Matter Expert Revealed  
 
● Developed by Mumbai -based Dot9 Games and published by Nazara Publishing, FAU -
G: Domination is a brand new game in the FAU -G series from nCore Games

Mumbai, India, September 5, 2024: Nazara Publishing, the publishing arm of publicly listed 
company Nazara Technologies revealed the gameplay trailer for made -in-India mobile shooter 
FAU-G: Domination at the FAU -G: Domination World Premiere Event held in Mumbai. In 
addition to this, pre -registrations for the game are live now on Google Play.  
 
FAU-G: Domination is Nazara and nCore Games’ answer to Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s 
Independence Day call to action  to the nation. He urged India’s games industry to focus on 
designing world -class games. He highlighted the country's talent and potential to become a 
global leader in gaming.  
 
With this in mind, the trailer entices players to win on FAU -G: Domination’s many maps all 
while showcasing a variety of weapons. It also highlights multiple locations and firefights to 
show the scope and size of the experience Dot9 and nCore Games will br ing to gamers in 
India and the world. You can check out the trailer right here:  
 
Under the mentorship of Shri Akshay Kumar, nCore is dedicating the game to Bharat Ke Veer. 
Bharat Ke Veer Trust gives homage and support to India’s Bravehearts. To support the 
families of India’s Armed Forces Martyrs, Nazara will donate Rs. 25,00,000 to Bh arat Ke Veer 
and FAU -G: Domination will also feature specific cosmetic items such as badges, of which 
100 percent of the proceeds will go towards Bharat Ke Veer.  
 
The Bharat Ke Veer Trust is conceptualised by Akshay Kumar and implemented by the 
Ministry of Home Affairs, to support the families of India’s Armed Forces Martyrs.  
 
In order to unlock the potential of made -in-India games such as this, Nazara Publishing has 
undertaken an extensive marketing campaign to raise awareness among Indian gamers to 
pre-register FAU -G: Domination. These include partnering with some of India’s b iggest content 
creators and influencers as well as generous pre -registration bonuses, details of which you 
can find at the end of this press release.  
 
Nitish Mittersain, Founder and CEO of Nazara Technologies, said, “We’re delighted to finally 
showcase FAU -G: Domination and open pre -registrations for the game after a period of public 
testing that verified what we knew — FAU-G: Domination can address the demand of Indian 
gamers for locally relevant content. We can’t wait to share more with you on the road to release 
through our marketing initiatives.”  
 
“It is a matter of great pride to respond to PM Modi’s call and present a game made -in-India 
for India — and the world. FAU -G: Domination is well on the way to launch and our new 
gameplay trailer along with our pre -registration campaign our are first steps  in revealing more 
to players,” said Vishal Gondal, Co -Founder of nCore Games. “India has amazing game 
design and development talent, and this signals India's arrival as a major player in the global 
gaming industry. Along with Nazara's publishing experience we're excited to bring a Made -in-
India game to the world.”  
 
“After several playtests and positive player feedback, we’re confident that FAU -G: Domination 
is in a state that Indian gamers expect,” said Deepak Ail, Co -Founder and CEO of Dot9 
Games, FAU -G: Domination’s developer. “Shooter fans are perhaps FAU -G most d emanding 
audience right now, wanting high performance with high fidelity graphics on their smartphones, 
thankfully our team is up to the task.”  
 
NIBE Limited Is the Defence Subject Matter Expert for FAU -G: Domination  
 
In order to raise awareness of India’s defence and military prowess, NIBE Limited — India’s 
leading defence manufacturer is the Defence Subject Matter Expert for FAU -G: Domination. 
Through NIBE Limited’s participation, players will learn about India’s self -sustained defence 
efforts en route to launch and while playing the game. These initiatives will give them a glimpse 
into India’s growing defence sector.  
 
“We’re proud to contribute to FAU -G: Domination towards bringing a true -to-life representation 
of India’s defence efforts to the widest possible audience,” said Ganesh Nibe, Chairman and 
Managing Director, NIBE Group. “The next -generation of the nation are  digital natives and 
FAU-G: Domination gives us a platform to showcase what keeps India safe and secure.”  
 
NIBE is involved in various segments of defence production; from fabrication of structures and 
sub-assemblies for programmes such as Modular bridge, Rudder blade, Pinaka launcher, and 
MRSAM launcher for the tri -services of Indian Defence, to components of Electronic systems, 
Small Arms (such as Assault Rifles and LMGs), Ammunition (Large and Medium calibres), 
and Space projects, for domestic as well as international applications.  
 
More details on this unique India -first collaboration will be revealed soon.
