Happiest Minds Recognized by Avtar and Seramount  Among 2025 Best Companies 
for Women in IT and as an Exemplar in the Most Inclusive Companies Index  
Bengaluru, Seattle, and London, December  3, 2025 : Happiest Minds Technologies Limited (NSE: 
HAPPSTMNDS), an AI -led, customer -first digital engineering and Mindful IT company, has been 
recognized among  the 2025 Avtar and Seramount Best Companies for Women in India in IT  for the 
third consecutive year. The company has also been named an ‘Exemplar of Inclusion ’ in the 2025 Most 
Inclusive Companies Index, reflecting its sustained commitment to fostering a workplace that values 
diversity, prioritizes belongingne ss, and ensures equitable access to opportunity.  
Avtar and Seramount evaluate organizations that demonstrate sustained impact through inclusive 
practices and measurable progress toward equity. Happiest Minds obtained both distinctions from a 
competitive field across industries, reflecting the organizatio n’s ongoing investment in strengthening 
representation, building inclusive leadership , and championing a culture where every individual’s voice 
carries weight and value.  
 
Joseph Anantharaju, Co-Chairman  & CEO , Happiest Minds  Technologies , said, “Achieving this 
recognition for the third consecutive time validates our long -term commitment to diversity and inclusion. 
Through leadership development initiatives, structured mentorship, the Women in Break program, and 
AURA, we continue to strengthen the ecosystem that enables women to learn, lead, and re -enter the 
workplace with confidence. This milestone encourages us to scale these opportunities and reinforce inclusion 
at every l evel of the organization.”  
Sachin Khurana, Chief People Officer , said, “At Happiest Minds, diversity and inclusion are lived principles 
that shape how we support our teams and design our people practices. These recognitions reflect the 
commitment and care that our teams bring to creating an environment where everyone feels re spected and 
encouraged to contribute their strengths. Our initiatives for women and people with disabilities represent 

 
 
our aspiration to build a workplace that is both progressive and deeply human. We remain focused on 
strengthening this foundation and ensuring that every individual can pursue growth without barriers.”  
Deepti Moolya, Director and Lead for DEIB , said, “This acknowledgment from Avtar and Seramount 
reinforces the importance of intentional and sustained action. Our efforts continue to be guided by the belief 
that inclusion must be experienced consistently across every stage of an individual’s journey with us. Aura, 
our women leadership development initiative, has emerged as a powerful catalyst for advancement and 
confidence -building . Our broader DEIB programs continue to ensure equitable access to support, re sources , 
and opportunity. We remain committed to creating a workplace where every person feels empowered to 
thrive, contribute , and lead.”  
Happiest Minds has been consistently recognized for its people -first culture and dedication to inclusive 
growth. Recent accolades include India’s Best Workplaces for Women Top 100 2024 by Great Place To 
Work, 50 Best Firms for Data Scientists to Work For 2 024 by AIM , and Gold for Talent Acquisition,  along 
with Silver for Future of Work at the Brandon Hall Group HCM Excellence Awards 2024.
