PRESS RELEASE  
 
Tata Communications Drives Pan-India launch of 
BSNL eSIM Service s  
 
Enabling millions of BSNL mobile users across the country to access seamless connectivity 
through nex t generation eSIM technology  
 
 
Mumbai, INDIA, 1st October  2025  — Tata Communications , a leading global communications 
technology player, today announced the successful deployment of its digital infrastructure 
solutions to power Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited’s (BSNL) newly launched eSIM services for 
millions of subscribers across India.  
 
Tata Communications is powering BSNL’s eSIM services through its state -of-the-art, GSMA -
accredited eSIM Subscription Management platform  — Tata Communications MOV E™ — delivered by 
Tata Communications Collaboration Services Private Ltd. (TCCSPL), a wholly owned subsidiary of 
Tata Communications Ltd.  This platform will enable BSNL to facilitate eSIM provisioning for their 
pan-India mobile subscribers.     
 
The launch of  BSNL’s  eSIM services is a big step towards enhancing customer experience and future 
proofing mobile connectivity  in India with increased convenience, flexibility and security in 
cellular services. Powered by Tata Communications MOVE ™, BSNL eSIMs will enable remote 
provisioning of 2G/3G/4G connectivity service through a secure QR code,  eliminating the need for 
a physical SIM . Moreover, users with devices  support ing dual-SIM functionality can use  an eSIM 
alongside a physical  SIM, making it easier to securely connect with a ny local operator while 
travel ling abroad .  
 
A.S. Lakshminarayanan, MD and CEO, Tata Communications , said: “We are hono ured to partner 
with BSNL to bring secure, scalable, and future -ready eSIM services to millions of citizens  and 
enabling India’s shift towards next -generation connectivity . This collaboration reflects Tata 
Communications’  commitment to driving digital innovation and supporting sovereign 
infrastructure. Together, we are laying the foundation for a more connected and resilient India.” 
 
A Robert Ravi, Chairman and Managing Director, BSNL said: “The launch of Pan -India eSIM 
service  represents a strategic advancement in our national telecom capabilities. With Tata 
Communications ’ strong connectivity experience and forward -looking innovation , we are 
enhancing the flexibility, security, and efficiency of mobile services for citizens across India. It 
aligns with our commitment to fostering digital independence  and ensuring robust infrastructure 
for the future. ” 
 
This partnership fosters Tata Communications’ commitment to support the Government’s vision of 
Digital India. While currently the launch is focussed on consumers, the Tata Communications 
MOVE ™ platform is designed to scale for enterprise IoT applications  supporting digital 
transformation of Indian enterprises .
