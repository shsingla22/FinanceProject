PRESS RELEASE  
KPCL announced  the results for Q2  & H1 FY2 7  
H1 FY27 Revenue from operations  at Rs 620 Cr.  
 PBT Rs. 85 Cr.  
 
Pune, India – 27th October 2026: Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Ltd (KPCL) (BSE : 505283, NSE: 
KIRLPNU), a prominent player in Air, Refrigeration & Gas Compression business in India, 
announced to day its un-audited financial results for Q 2 & H1 FY27. 
 
For Q2 FY2 7, revenue from operations reached Rs. 3 20 Cr, and Profit Before Tax (PBT) was Rs. 40 
Cr., Net Profit After Tax for the quarter stood at Rs. 40 Cr. 
Total income for H1 FY27 stood at ₹620 crore, compared with ₹650 crore in H1 FY26. Profit Before 
Tax (PBT) was ₹85 crore, translating into a PBT margin of 13.4%, compared with ₹94 crore and 
14.1%, respectively, in H1 FY26.  
Commenting on the results, Aman Kirloskar, Managing Director, said , “Our performance during 
the first half reflects the immediate headwinds of an increasingly volatile global landscape. 
Geopolitical tensions and shipping disruptions directly impacted on the timing of our export 
clearances and dispatches, leading to temporar y delays in certain consignments and deferred 
order finalizations. While these external uncertainties have moderated our near -term revenue and 
profitability, our core operational foundation remains resilient."  
As of October 1, 202 6, our order book stands at INR 2,040 Cr., an increase by 10% from Rs. 1, 863 
Cr. at the beginning of the year.  
 
Compression business continues to be around 9 4% of the Company’s revenue for H1 FY2 7 and 
remains the only reporting segment.  
 
During the quarter, Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Limited (KPCL) and Kobe Steel Ltd. (KOBELCO), 
Japan have entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to explore and develop business 
opportunities in the Oil & Gas sector.  The collaboration reinforces the commitment of both 
companies towards technology -led growth, engineering excellence and delivering reliable and 
energy -efficient solutions for the evolving needs of the global Oil & Gas industry. By doing so, the 
companies aim to strengthen their business platform and competitiveness in India’ s growing 
market, expand business primarily in the oil and gas sectors, and create new business 
opportunities in carbon -neutrality -related fields

About Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Ltd.  
Established in 1958, Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Ltd. (KPCL) is a diversified company offering a 
comprehensive range of products, including Air, Refrigeration, and Gas compressors and systems, 
vapour absorption chillers, and industrial gearboxes. Serving v arious industries such as steel,

Leveraging extensive industry experience, KPCL has forged technology partnerships with leading
