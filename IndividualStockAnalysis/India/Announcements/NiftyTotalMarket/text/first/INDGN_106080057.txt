Indegene Wins Deloitte Technology Fast 50 India 2024 Award in HealthTech  
Bengaluru, India, December 9, 2024: Indegene, a digital -first life sciences commercialization company, announced that 
it is a winner in HealthTech at the Deloitte Technology Fast 50 India 2024 Award s, for the second consecutive year. Now 
in its 19th edition , the Deloitte Technology Fast 50 India programme celebrates and honours the fastest -growing 
technology -enabled businesses based on their average revenue growth over a three -year period . 
Thriving in a competitive and ever -evolving environment while addressing global uncertainties and disruptions demands 
relentless innovation, adaptability, and an exceptional ability to harness technology effectively.  Deloitte firmly believe s 
that the Top 50 winners of its 2024 list exemplify these qualities , and t he winners’ accomplishments highlight their 
resilience and ingenuity, setting new benchmarks for the industry.  
Indegene combine s deep healthcare expertise with a digital -first approach to help pharma, biotech and medical device 
companies commercialize their products.  It is one of the very few companies globally where a medical doctor and a data 
scientist work next to each other to solve big, impactful problems  in life sciences . And this is by design. More than 20% of 
Indegene’s  employees come from a medical education background and they work very closely with about 600 
technologists who work on advanced artificial intelligence ( AI), machine learning ( ML) and other technologies.   
Take generative AI ( GenAI ) for example . Indegene  remain s focused on helping its clients harness the potential of GenAI 
with targeted solutions to address some of their most pressing operational challenges and make their business future -
ready.  In summary, Indegene’s  investments in real -world data and digital tools continue to help its clients navigate 
changing regulatory environments and improve operational efficiency  around the world . 
With 6 operation hubs and 18 offices across North America, Europe, and Asia, Indegene’s client base includes the world’s 
top 20 biopharma companies, many emerging biotech firms, and medical device companies. With over 5,000 employees 
worldwide, Indegene’s  vast global footprint underscores its ability to scale and meet the evolving needs of the world’s 
leading pharma organizations across markets.

technology  and an  agile operating model to provide a diverse range of solutions. These aim to deliver, amongst other outcomes, a
