This is in continuation of our earlier disclosure dated May 31, 2024, made pursuant to Regulation 30 
of the Listing Regulations read with SEBI Circular dated July 13, 2023, regarding Order received by 
Dalmia Cement (North-East) Limited (“ DCNEL”), subsidiary of the Company, from the Assistant 
Commissioner, Central (Goods and Service Tax) & Central Excise Division, Silchar, Assam, under Section

Commissioner (Appeals), CGST, Central Excise & Customs, Guwahati, Assam, has now passed an Order 
allowing the said appeal and has dropped the entire aforesaid demand of tax, interest and penalty.
