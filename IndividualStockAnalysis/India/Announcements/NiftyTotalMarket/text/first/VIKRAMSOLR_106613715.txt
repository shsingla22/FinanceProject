Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure

Committee, pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 33 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. 
 
We also hereby declare that M/s GARV & Associates, Chartered Accountants, Statutory Auditors of the Company

format along with the Auditors’ Report thereon are enclosed herewith as “ Annexure – A ”.
