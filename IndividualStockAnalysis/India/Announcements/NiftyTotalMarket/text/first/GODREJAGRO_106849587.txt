MANAGING DIRECTOR’S COMMENTS   
Commenting on Q2 performance, Mr. Sunil Kataria , Chief Executive Officer & Managing Director, GAVL , said:  
“Godrej Agrovet delivered a strong  Q2 FY27 despite a challenging macro -operating environment, marked by El Niño -related 
weather disruptions, unprecedented  geopolitical volatility  and inflationary pressures . Consolidated revenue grew 17% and 
PAT rose 22% , even as input cost inflation held EBITDA flat .  
 
2 
 
 
 
Animal Nutrition and Oil Palm led the quarter, growing revenue by 14% and 28% respectively, with Animal Nutrition 
segment profit up 32% on better realizations, product mix and operating efficiencies. Crop Care revenue rose 42% on a low 
base, but El Niño weighed on profitability. Astec revenue was down about 3%, as 15% gr owth in CDMO was offset by weak 
enterprise demand. Dairy grew 6%, led by calibrated pricing and value -added products, and Foods grew 8%, led by B2C, 
while our ACI -GAVL joint venture in Bangladesh grew revenue 32% on 21% higher volumes.  
 
Our strategic transformation is progressing well. In Animal Nutrition, we are premiumiz ing the portfolio and have exited 
shrimp to focus on profitable, scalable segments. In Oil Palm, we are investing  for growth through plantation expansion 
and the commissioning of the Khammam complex, CPKO refinery and biogas plant. In Crop Care, Ashitaka® is scaling fast, 
with H1 sales  six times that of last year. In Foods, B2C salience rose to ~38% from ~27% , with a growth of around 50% . In 
Dairy, value -added products now make up ~49% of the portfolio, up from ~ 44%, and calibrated pricing action together 
with our go -to-market revamp pilot positions us to build further. At Astec, we remain focused on growing CDMO, new 
products and capacity utilization.  
 
Our strong performance in an exceptionally challenging environment demonstrates the inherent strength and resilience of 
our unique agri  and foods platform, giving us the confidence to build on this foundation and take the business to the next 
phase of growth . As we enter the second half of FY27, we remain committed to advancing our strategic transformation  and 
creating sustainable long -term value for all stakeholders.”

About Godrej Agrovet Limited  
At Godrej Agrovet, part of the Godrej Industries Group, we craft abundance through innovation that empowers farmers and 
nourishes our nation. We are one of India’s leading foods  and agri businesses, with  a presence across Animal Nutrition, Oil Palm, 
Crop Care , Dairy, and Food s, serving farmers and partners across the agricultural value chain.  
We are the number one producer of Crude Palm Oil in India and the leading player in Animal Nutrition , contributing to food security 
and farmer livelihoods. Sustainability is at the core of how we operate. We are recognized  as a leader, with a CDP A - leadership 
band rating in Climate and Forests, and as the first agri company in India with an SBTi -approved emission reduction target.
