FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER/HALF-YEAR ENDED 30TH SEPTEMBER 2026 
 
RESULTS AT A GLANCE 
Quarter ended 30th September 2026 vis a vis Quarter ended 30th September 2025 
 
 Total Global Business grew by 25.16% to ₹ 7,05,766 crore 
 Total Global Deposits increased by 21.50% to ₹ 3,76,407 crore 
 Gross Global Advances increased by 29.61% to ₹ 3,29,359  crore 
 Overseas Deposit stood at Rs. 721 crore 
 Domestic Credit - Deposit (CD) Ratio stood at 82.85% 
 Gross NPA declined to 1.25% 
 Net NPA reduced to 0.12% 
 Provision Coverage Ratio improved to 98.53% 
 Operating profit grew by 30.86% to ₹ 3,369 crore 
 Net Profit increased by 41.42% to ₹ 2,310  crore 
 Net Interest Income increased by 21.76% to ₹ 3,954 crore 
 Domestic Net Interest Margin (NIM) improved to 3.97% 
 Global NIM stood at 3.86% 
 Return on Assets (ROA) improved to 2.02% 
 Return on Equity (ROE) improved to 26.98% 
 CRAR improved to at 18.51% of which Tier I is 16.37%

Profitability (Quarter Ended 30th September 2026): 
 Net Profit increased by 41.42% to ₹ 2,310 crore in Q2 FY27 on Y-o-Y basis as against ₹ 1,633 crore 
for Q2 FY26.  The same is increased by 14.33% on a Q-o-Q sequential basis. 
 Operating Profit has shown a growth of 30.86% on Y-o-Y basis to ₹3,369 crore for Q2 FY27 as 
against ₹ 2,574 crore for Q2 FY26.The same has improved by 8.07% on a Q-o-Q basis.  
 Net Interest Income (NII) grew by 21.76% on Y-o-Y basis to ₹3,954  crore in Q2 FY27 as against       
₹ 3,248 crore for Q2 FY26.  The same is increased by 4.89% on a Q-o-Q sequential basis. 
 Cost to Income Ratio improved to 33.73% for Q2 FY 27 as against 37.10% for Q2 FY26. The same 
was 35.04 % for the quarter ended 30.06.2026. 
 Return on Assets (ROA) improved to 2.02 % for Q2 FY27 against 1.82% for Q2 FY26. 
Profitability (Half Year Ended September 30th, 2026) 
 Net Profit has shown growth of 34.22% to ₹ 4,330 crore on Y-o-Y basis for half year ended 30.09.2026 
against ₹ 3,226 crore for the half year ended 30.09.2025.   
 Operating Profit has shown a growth of 26.08% to ₹ 6,486  crore on Y-o-Y basis for half year ended 
30.09.2026 against ₹ 5,144 crore for the half year ended 30.09.2025.  
 Net Interest Income (NII) grew by 18.12% on Y-o-Y basis to ₹ 7,724 crore for the half year ended 
30.09.2026 as against ₹ 6,539 crore for the half year ended 30.09.2025.   
 Fee based income increased by 3.54% on Y-o-Y basis to ₹ 866  crore for the half year ended 
30.09.2026 as against Rs. 836 crore for the half year ended 30.09.2025. 
 Cost to Income Ratio improved to 34.37% for the half year ended 30.09.2026 as against 37.34% for 
the half year ended 30.09.2025. 
 Return on Assets (ROA) improved to 1.96% for the half year ended 30.09.2026 as against 1.81% for 
the half year ended 30.09.2025.  
 Return on Equity (ROE) improved to  25.29% for the half year ended 30.09.2026 against 22.30% for 
the half year ended 30.09.2025.
