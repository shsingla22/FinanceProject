Sub:  Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 – Heritage Foods 
Limited receives “Best Performing Wind Farm Award” for FY 2025 –26 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform 
you that the Company’s Wind Farm, comprising three Wind 
Turbine Generators (WTGs) with an aggregate installed capacity of 
6.3 MW, has been awarded  the “Best Performing Wind Farm” for 
FY 2025 –26 in Zone -III. 
 
The prestigious award was presented by the Chairman of the 
Indian Wind Power Association (IWPA), Shri T. Shivaraman, at the 
IWPA Conference held in Coimbatore on August 22, 2026.  
 
The award was conferred based on the performance of wind farms 
across the four wind energy zones in India. Andhra Pradesh, 
Telangana and Karnataka constitute Zone -III for the purpose of the 
evaluation.   
 
This recognition reflects Heritage Foods Limited’s continued commitment to sustainability and its 
consistent efforts towards promoting renewable energy and contributing to a greener and more 
sustainable future.  
 
Kindly take the above information on record and disseminate the same on the website of the Exchange.
