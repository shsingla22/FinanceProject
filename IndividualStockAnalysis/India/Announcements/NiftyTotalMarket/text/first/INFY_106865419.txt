Infosys will bring its global AI -powered deep engineering 
experiences, Infosys’ Topaz and Metaverse foundry capabilities to 
build the next frontier for immersive experiences, digital twins and 
virtual commerce in Japan, for the world.  
Infosys will participate as a minority investor and is the strategic technology and AI partner for the joint venture. The investment 
aligns with Infosys’ strategy to strengthen presence in Japan and adds to its successful Joint Venture HIPUS.
