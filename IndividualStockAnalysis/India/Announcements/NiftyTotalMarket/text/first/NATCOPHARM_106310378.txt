Ref: Our letter dated June 13th 2025  
Hyderabad, India, July 24rd, 2025: NATCO  Pharma Limited  (BSE:  524816  and NSE: NATCO PHARM) ("NATCO")  we hereby 
inform you that  the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) had conducted an inspection at the Active Pharmaceutical  
Ingredient Division located in Mekaguda, Hyderabad, India, from June 9th – June 13th, 2025 . The Company received 1 
(One) observation in the Form -483 which  was classified as “Voluntary Action Indicated” (VAI) . 
In connection with the above we inform you that the U.S. FDA has now issued EIR for the API facility located in Mekaguda  
on July 24th 2025 .  
Please take the above information on record.
