been treated as invalid.

Act, 2013, rules made there-under and Relevant Circulars relating to electronic voting on the resolutions 
contained in the Notice of the Meeting. 
My responsibility as scrutinizer for electronic voting restricted to making a scrutinizer report of the votes 
cast in favour or against the resolution(s).
