Sub: Update on Disruption in Operations at some of the manufacturing sites at Haridwar  
 
In continuation to the intimation given by the Akums Drugs and Pharmaceuticals Limited (“the Company”) on    
14th May, 2026 and 16th May, 2026, this is to inform that all the manufacturing operations at the affected sites of 
the Company and its Subsidiaries, situated in the state of Uttarakhand, have been successfully resumed at 100% 
operations capacity with effect from 18th May, 2026. The partial disruption in production at Haridwar sites lasted 
for four days which resulted in delayed supply of approx. INR 20 Cr. We are hopeful to make up for the production 
loss in the coming weeks.
