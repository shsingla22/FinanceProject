PRESS RELEASE  
Date: 11.08.2025  
 
Tamilnad  Mercantile Bank Partners with Bajaj Allianz General Insurance to 
Expand Non -Life Insurance Offerings  
 
 
Thoothukudi, India:  Tamilnad  Mercantile Bank Ltd. (TMB), one of India’s most 
trusted private sector banks with over 100 years of service excellence, has announced 
a strategic tie -up with Bajaj Allianz General Insurance Company Ltd. (BAGIC) to offer 
a comprehensive range of non -life insurance products to its customers.  
 
This new arrangement will enable TMB to offer products such as motor insurance, 
health and personal accident insurance, asset protection covers for loan customers, 
and cyber insurance — accessible through the bank’s extensive branch network and 
digital platforms.  
 
As per IRDAI guidelines, TMB, registered as a corporate agent, can partner with up to 
nine companies for marketing non -life insurance products. With existing tie -ups with 
United India Insurance Company Ltd., Cholamandalam MS General Insurance 
Company Ltd.,  and Zurich Kotak General Insurance Company Ltd., the addition of 
BAGIC further strengthens TMB’s  non-life insurance portfolio.  
 
Commenting on the partnership, Mr. Salee S Nair, Managing Director & CEO of 
TMB ,said:  
"At TMB, our focus is to deliver complete financial solutions under one roof. Partnering 
with Bajaj Allianz General Insurance will allow us to offer customers robust, tech -driven 
protection plans while supporting our strategy to grow fee -based income."  
BAGIC will provide TMB with a dedicated service team, free training for staff, IT 
integration, and web portal linkages to ensure a seamless insurance experience for 
both Liability as well as Asset customers.  
 
This collaboration builds on TMB’s successful association with Bajaj Allianz Life 
Insurance Company Ltd. (BALIC) since December 2022 and reaffirms the bank’s 
commitment to combining its century -long legacy with modern financial solutions.  
 
This new tie -up reinforces TMB’s commitment to being a one-stop financial 
solutions provider , combining its century -long trust with modern, technology -driven 
offerings for a diverse customer base.
