Press Release  
 
Alkem launches Renocia  cyclical  therapy , a nutrition al 
supplements kit, to support hair growth  
• It is a scientifically -designed, convenient weekly nutritional regimen with 
separate formulations  for men and women  
• The supplements are made using only vegetarian -sourced ingredients  
Mumbai, January  8, 202 6: Alkem Laboratories Ltd. (BSE: 539523, NSE: ALKEM, 
“Alkem ” and its subsidiaries) today announced the launch of Renocia  cyclic al therapy  kit, a 
scientifically -designe d weekly  nutritional supplementation regimen to support hair growth , 
strengthen hair follicles  and manage hair loss. The supplements in the kit are made using only 
vegetarian -sourced ingredients.  
Renocia  cyclical therapy kit contains a set of nutritional supplements, including vitamins, 
minerals, and amino acids, each to be taken on designated days of the week.  This structured 
weekly protocol  enables  better  nutrient absorption and utilisation.   
Commenting on the launch, Dr. Vikas Gupta, Chief Executive Officer, Alkem, said , 
“Nutritional supplementation plays an important role in hair health but outcomes may vary 
depending on nutrient selection, absorption dynamics, and long -term adherence. Renoci a 
cyclical therapy offers a structured and targeted approach that aligns with the body ’s natural 
nutritional requirements through the week. By using only vegetarian -sourced ingredients, we 
aim to make this product acceptable and accessible to all sections  of the Indian population. ” 
For convenience and better adherence, Renocia cyclical therapy kit is available in a wallet 
pack containing nutritional supplements for four weeks  with a clear description of which  
tablets need to be taken on each day of the week. Renocia cyclical therapy is formulated 
separately for men and women to match their specific nutritional needs  and available in 
separate kits. This is a prescription product.  
Alkem has a growing portfolio of products in the dermatology segment , which includes skin 
and hair care. Under the Renocia brand, the company offers several products for hair care 
such as hair revitalizing shampoo, conditioner and serum, as well as oral supplements.
