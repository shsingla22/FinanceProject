HCLTech recognized as a Leader in Everest Group's Application Management 
Services PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2025  
 
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, May 05, 2025 —HCLTech,  a leading global technology company, 
has been recognized as a Leader in Everest Group's Application Management Services  (AMS)  
PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2025. This highlights HCLTech's  expertise in handling complex 
application environments and ensuring smooth operations.  
 
HCLTech is enhancing its AMS capabilities with its advanced Gen AI-driven service 
transformation platform AI Force , which significantly enhances efficiencies , velocity and 
quality of AMS delivered . 
 Additionally, the company's alliances with solution leaders like ServiceNow and Workato and 
investments in tools and technologies like Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) demonstrate its 
commitment to offering  high -quality app lication management solutions  to its clients .  
 "HCLTech's leadership in this space is strengthened by its robust frameworks , such as ASM 
and FENIX,  and its GenAI -driven service transformation platform, AI Force . Clients trust 
HCLTech for its high- quality resources and flexible project management, making it a reliable 
partner in their AMS journeys," says Ankit Gupta, Practice Director, Everest Group.  
 
"At HCLTech, we adapt to the dynamic technology landscape and leverage GenAI  to meet our 
clients' unique  needs . Our approach encompasses customized operating models, DevSecOps, 
proactive monitoring and predictive analytics, all aimed at driving continuous improvement 
and delivering exceptional value ," said Pawan Vadapal li, Corporate Vice President and Global  
Head of Digital Business Services , HCLTech.
