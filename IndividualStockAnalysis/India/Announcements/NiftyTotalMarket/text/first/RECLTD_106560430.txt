("Listing Regulations").

the Board of Directors of the Company in their meeting held on March 16,2026, wherein the

Directors and the fmes imposed by the Stock Exchanges related therewith . 
In this regard, the Board desired that regular follow·up be made with the appointing authority 
i.e. Ministry of Power, Government of India, to expedite the process of appointment of 
requisite number ofIndependent Directors on the Board ofREC. The Board also desired that 
the stock exchanges be requested to waive the fmes so imposed on the Company and also to 
not impose any further fme/penalty, as the matter of appointment ofIndependent Directors 
is beyond the control of the Company and there is no violation on the part of the Company.

Association of the Company, il7ter-alia the power to appoint Independent Directors on the 
Board of the Company is vested with President of India acting through administrative Ministry 
i.e. Ministry of Power, Government of India and the Company has no role in the appointment 
of Directors. Accordingly , the fmes so imposed on the Company by the stock exchanges m ay 
be waived off.
