Press Release  
IndusInd Bank leverages  J.P. Morgan Payments to enhance outward remittance 
experience by expanding coverage to  123 currencies  
J.P. Morgan  Payment s’ Xpedite solution enables direct settlement in beneficiaries’ local currencies and 
expands currency access for IndusInd Bank ’s customers.  
Mumbai , November  06, 2026 : IndusInd Bank and J.P. Morgan Payments  will enhance the outward 
remittance experience for IndusInd Bank customers  so they can  send payments in 123 currencies. Through 
the integration of J.P. Morgan  Payment s’ Xpedite  solution, IndusInd Bank customers will be able to send 
funds directly in the beneficiary’s local currency, benefiting from best -in-class settlement  capabilities  that 
simplify the remittance journey and reduce  the need for intermediary currency conversions.  
The Xpedite solution uses  the global network of  J.P. Morgan  Payments , provides automate d currency rate 
feed s, and streamline s processing  to make transfers faster and more efficient . 
Currently, customers can access  this service  at IndusInd Bank branches. Soon, t he service will be available 
through online and mobile banking, enabling customers to initiate outward remittances anytime , 24/7. 
Ganesh Sankaran , Executive Director, IndusInd  Bank , said, “Our customers are increasingly engaging with 
global markets, making simpler and more seamless international payments an important priority for us. 
By collaborating with J.P. Morgan Payments, we are strengthening our cross -border payments 
capabilities, expa nding access t o 123 currencies and enabling direct local -currency settlements. This 
enhances the remittance experience for our customers while reinforcing our commitment to building a 
more connected, convenient and future -ready banking experience.”  
Guhaprasath Rajagopa l, Head of India Payments , J.P. Morgan  Payments , said, “Through J.P. Morgan 
Payments ’ Xpedite solution, we will deliver an integrated cross -border payments experience  for IndusInd  
Bank customers . By combining our global platform with local expertise, we serve as a trusted 
correspondent bank  for cross -border solutions , enabling customers to move money faster and more 
safely .”   
The collaboration further strengthens IndusInd  Bank’s ability to serve customers with international 
payment needs, while providing access to a broader range of currencies through a single banking 
relationship.  Click here  for more information . 
J.P. Morgan Payments combines treasury services, trade & working capital, blockchain and tokenization, 
and card and merchant services capabilities to help clients pay customers or employees, in different 
currencies, around the world. It processes nearly  $12 trillion payments daily, operating in over 160 
countries and over 120 currencies.
