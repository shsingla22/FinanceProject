30% July’26, New Delhi : Indri Single Malt continues its remarkable winning streak at 
the world's most prestigious and coveted whisky competitions, adding yet another global 
triumph to its growing legacy. The latest comes at the International Whisky 
Competition (IWC) 2026, widely regarded as one of the world's most respected and 
rigorous whisky competitions, where Indri once again secured the highest honours. 
In a historic achievement, Indri swept the Top Three Positions in both the Best Indian 
Whisky and Best Single Malt Indian Whisky categories, while becoming the only 
Indian whisky to score above 90 points at this year's competition.

This latest recognition adds to Indri's growing legacy of triumphs at the world's most 
prestigious whisky competitions, where it has consistently competed alongside—and 
outperformed—many of the finest names in global whisky. 
For Piccadily, however, these victories represent far more than medals. They reflect a 
long-term commitment to redefining what India stands for in the world of premium spirits. 
For decades, India was rarely associated with luxury, craftsmanship or world-class 
whisky. Piccadily set out to challenge that perception by creating exceptional spirits with 
uncompromising quality at every stage. 
From sourcing the finest ingredients and investing in world-class distillation systems to 
thoughtful cask selection, patient maturation and uncompromising quality standards, 
every decision has been guided by a single philosophy: the liquid comes first. 
At Piccadily, craftsmanship is never compromised, shortcuts are never taken, and 
excellence is never negotiable. Every bottle reflects a relentless pursuit of quality, 
authenticity and transparency. 
The company's vision extends beyond building award-winning brands. It is about 
creating an ecosystem where Indian spirits are recognised globally for luxury, integrity, 
innovation and craftsmanship. 
Commenting on the achievement, Shalini Sharma, Head of Marketing, Piccadily 
Agro Industries Limited, said,"Every global recognition reinforces what we have 
believed from the very beginning—that India has the capability to produce spirits that 
stand alongside the very best in the world. Our ambition has never been merely to win 
awards; it has been to redefine the global perception of Indian spirits. We have invested 
relentlessly in quality, craftsmanship and authenticity because we believe extraordinary 
spirits can only come from uncompromising standards. These awards belong not just to 
Indri, but to every individual who believes India can lead the world’s luxury spirits 
conversation through excellence." 
Indri's consistent performance at the world's most credible and respected competitions 
demonstrates that Indian whisky is no longer an emerging category—it has established 
itself among the finest whiskies in the world.

About Indri Single Malt Whisky: 
Launched in 2022, Indri Single Malt Whisky, crafted by Piccadily, has quickly become the 
fastest-growing single malt in the world, earning over 40 prestigious accolades, including 'Best 
Indian Single Malt' and 'Best Whisky in The World.’ Produced in Indri, a small village in Haryana, 
using premium 6-row Indian barley from Rajasthan, Indri single malt whisky showcases Indian 
excellence and innovation. 
In a market once dominated by imported labels, Indri stands tall as a beacon of Indian 
excellence. It's not just a brand, it has become a symbol of national pride, elevating the status 
of Indian spirits to unparalleled heights. Indri isn't just leading the charge; it's leading a revolution. 
As Indri continues to redefine the standards of Indian single malts, Piccadily remains dedicated 
to pushing boundaries, exploring new horizons, and delivering exceptional experiences to 
whisky enthusiasts worldwide.
