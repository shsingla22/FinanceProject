Revolt Motors Hits Landmark Milestone: 50,000th Electric 
Motorcycle Rolls Out, Powering India’s EV Future  
New Delhi, 20th June  2025 : In a defining moment for India’s electric mobility revolution, Revolt 
Motors, India’s No.1 electric motorcycle brand, proudly celebrates the production of its 
50,000th electric motorcycle from its state -of-the-art manufacturing facility in Manesar, 
Haryana. This milestone is not just a number. It is a symbol of trust, resilience, and a growing 
collective movement toward cleaner, smarter, and sustainable transportation.  
The 50,000th unit, an RV1+ in the signature Titan Red Silver colour , was assembled as part of 
the company’s expanding portfolio of high -performance electric motorcycles. More than just 
a numerical milestone, it is a testament to Revolt’s commitment to building reliable, AI-
enabled , and sustainable mobility solutions — designed for India, built in India.  
Over the past  few years, Revolt Motors has rapidly scaled up its manufacturing and retail 
footprint to meet the surging demand for electric two -wheelers. The Manesar facility, 
powered by a skilled and dedicated workforce, upholds the highest standards of quality, 
preci sion, and performance in every unit produced. With an installed production capacity of 
1.8 lakh units annually, Revolt currently operates over 200 dealerships across India and has 
recently expanded to international markets including Nepal and Sri Lanka — reinforcing its 
position as a homegrown leader in the EV two-wheeler space.  
On the occasion,  Ms. Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises , said:  
"This 50,000th motorcycle carries more than a chassis number – it carries the aspirations of 
lakhs of Indians who believe in a cleaner, more progressive future. It symbolizes every customer 
who made the shift to electric. It reflects every team member who worked tirelessly on the 
assembly line, and every kilometre that challenged convention. At Revolt, we are not just 
manufacturing  bikes; we are building momentum for a generational change. This moment 
reaffirms our belief that an electric tomorrow is not ju st coming — it is already here and being 
led from India."  
Revolt Motors has established itself as the fastest -growing brand in India’s electric motorcycle 
segment. The company's flagship models, including the RV400, RV1+, and RV BlazeX, are 
equipped with advanced lithium -ion batteries, smart riding modes, regener ative braking, 
great payload capacity, and responsive, calibrated suspensions — features designed 
specifically for Indian roads and riders.  
Looking ahead, Revolt Motors aims to double its annual production capacity to over 3 lakh 
units by the end of 2026. The company is also planning to introduce new models , expand its 
dealership network to 400 locations, and further strengthen its presence across South Asia 
and the Middle East. With every step forward, Revolt continues its mission to shape the future 
of mobility through innovation, scale, and rider -first design.
