ensuring greater governance, transparency, and alignment with shareholder interests.

ESOPs would vest. While it may not be feasible to disclose individualized vesting details for each level of 
employee, vesting conditions are structured in a way that aligns employee rewards with business performance.

shares of ACME Renewtech, in compliance with the share lock-in restriction set out in the bidding
