Hon’ble Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath Inaugurates 300 KLPD Grain -Based Distillery 
by Keyaan Distilleries, Designed & Engineered by Praj Industries  
Gorakhpur, 8 April 2025  — In a major step towards strengthening Uttar Pradesh’s 
industrial and agricultural landscape, Keyaan Distilleries Pvt Ltd inaugurated its state -
of-the-art 300 KLPD grain -based distillery, scalable up to 500 KLPD. The facility was 
inaugurated by Hon’ble Chief Minister Shri Yogi Adityanath Ji, reaffirming the state’s 
commitmen t to green growth, rural development, and investment -led transformation.  
Designed, engineered, and commissioned by Praj Industries Ltd, a global leader in 
industrial biotechnology, the facility will produce fuel ethanol and rectified spirit for 
medicinal use using broken rice, maize, and corn as feedstock. The project strengthe ns 
the agro-processing sector and contributes to India’s ethanol blending roadmap.  
Spread across 30 acres, the distillery is expected to create over 1,200 direct and indirect 
jobs, while providing consistent demand for local grains —empowering farmers and 
integrating them into the clean energy value chain.  
During the inauguration, CM Yogi Adityanath praised the project for enhancing energy 
security, promoting foreign exchange savings through reduced crude imports, and 
setting the stage for advanced green fuels like Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF). He 
lauded both Keyaan Distilleries and Praj Industries for building a world -class facility that 
aligns with the vision of Atmanirbhar Bharat.  
As the technology partner, Praj Industries has delivered an integrated, future -ready 
distillery solution, combining high efficiency with environmental sustainability. With 
over 1,000 successful projects globally, Praj continues to drive innovation and 
transformation in the bioeconomy sector.  
The inauguration marks a defining moment in the journey toward a self -reliant, energy -
secure, and agriculturally empowered India.
