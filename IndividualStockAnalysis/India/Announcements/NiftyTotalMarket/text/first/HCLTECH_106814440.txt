HCLTech and Anthropic sign Global Premier Partnership to scale governed Agentic 
AI across global enterprises 
HCLTech to establish a dedicated Anthropic Business Unit and Centre of Excellence (CoE) to 
accelerate deployments of Claude in enterprise environments  
HCLSoftware becomes an Anthropic ISV partner, embedding Claude and Claude Code into 
select enterprise products with frontier safety guardrails  
 
Santa Clara, USA and NOIDA, India, Oct 12, 2026 -  HCLTech , a leading global technology 
company, today announced a Global Premier Partnership with Anthropic, to accelerate 
enterprise adoption of Agentic AI with a focus on safety, cybersecurity and governance. 
HCLTech’s services teams will build industry solutions  and offerings powered by Claude, 
Cowork and Claude Code, while HCLSoftware, as an Anthropic ISV partner, will embed these 
into it select enterprise products.  
HCLTech will establish a dedicated Anthropic Business Unit with a Center of Excellence (CoE ) 
to build Agentic AI solutions and provide secure sandbox environments for enterprise 
deployments of Claude through FDEs. It will also help clients adopt AI- native engineering 
practices, train their workforces and build the organizational capabilities nee ded to operate 
Agentic AI safely at scale.  
As an ISV, HCLSoftware  will embed Claude capabilities and have access to advanced models 
to integrate into select enterprise products across cybersecurity, automation and business 
process application and application development, including HCL BigFix and HCL AppScan, 
while enabling Actian AI analyst.  
Additionally, HCLTech will integrate Claude and Claude Code in its agentic software 
engineering platform AIForce  across IT support, code remediation and digital employee 
services. These workflows are supported by policy guardrails, exception handling and human oversight for high- impact changes.
