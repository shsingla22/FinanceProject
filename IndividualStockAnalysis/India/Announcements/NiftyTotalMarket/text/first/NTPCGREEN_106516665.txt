Press Release  
 
NTPC Green Energy Limited inks  MoU with Assago Industries Private Limited for 
supply of Green Ammonia , CO 2, RE Power and other related utilities for Green Urea 
Plant . 
 
NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL), a subsidiary of NTPC Limited, signed a Memorandum 
of Understanding (MoU) with Assago Industries Private Limited  on 2nd Feb’2026 at NTPC 
Bhawan, New Delhi  for supply of Green Ammonia, Carbon Dioxide, Renewable Energy Power 
and other related utilities for the lat ter for production of 1000 TPD of Green Urea. The Urea 
plant being explored to be setup in the NGEL Green Hydrogen Hub at Pudimadaka (near 
Visakhapatnam), AP . 
 
The MoU was signed in the august presence senior officials  from NGEL, NTPC  and Assago.
