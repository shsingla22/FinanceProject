Airtel expands its network footprint in Sehore District under its rural 
enhancement project 
Densifies its network across 973 villages by deploying additional sites for seamless connectivity 
Sehore, April 04, 2024:  Bharti Airtel, one of India's leading telecommunications service providers, 
today announced that it has deployed additional sites in Sehore district to densify its network.  
Sehore district network enhancement project was undertaken across 973 villages covering 11.35 
Lac rural population. Customers in the tehsils of Ashta, Budni, Sehore, Ichhawar, Nasrullaganj 
under Sehore districts will directly benefit from this network enhancement. The expansion will 
enable seamless access to high-speed connectivity that will ensure reliable mobile network for 
work, study and entertainment in rural Sehore. In this year alone, Airtel has doubled down on its 
commitment to the state and has invested additional capex to enhance its network connectivity 
for seamless experience on 4G, 5G, Broadband and Fiber.  
To expand coverage in rural villages in the state and to bring customers to enjoy the power of 
high-speed connectivity, the rural enhancement project covers across Madhya Pradesh including 
Indore, Dewas, Shajapur, Ujjain, Rajgarh, Hoshangabad, Gwalior, Rewa, Satna and Bhopal.  
Airtel has been running this rural enhancement project nationally and will augment network 
connectivity across 60,000 villages in the country by 2024. Madhya Pradesh has been one of the 
big focus markets for the Company and with this initiative, the Company has already augmented 
its network coverage across 5000 villages covering the entire state.  
With the aim of stepping up network capacity and taking services deeper into rural and 
unconnected areas. Airtel has also enhanced its fiber presence in the state by deploying 
additional capacities.  The addition of fresh fiber capacity will support the growth of demand for 
high-speed data services in this region. Airtel’s network umbrella in the region now covers all key 
urban, semi urban and rural areas including highways, tourist destinations and trade centers.

Airtel expands its network footprint in Thiruvananthapuram District under its 
rural enhancement project 
   Densifies its network across 48 towns and 42 villages in Thiruvananthapuram district  
Thiruvananthapuram, April 04, 2024:  Bharti Airtel, one of India's leading telecommunications 
service providers, today announced that it has deployed additional sites in Thiruvananthapuram 
district to densify its network. The network enhancement project was undertaken across 48 
towns and 42 villages covering 8 lakh population base. The additional sites will further augment 
customer experience in this region on both voice and data connectivity.  
Customers in the tehsils of Chirayinkeezhu, Kattakkada, Nedumangad, Neyyattinkara, 
Thiruvananthapuram, Varkala under Thiruvananthapuram district will directly benefit from this 
network enhancement. This expansion will enable seamless access to high-speed connectivity, a 
massive service for customers in rural & smaller towns in Thiruvananthapuram thereby ensuring 
reliable mobile network for work, study and entertainment. In this year alone, Airtel has doubled 
down on its commitment to the state and has invested additional capex to enhance its network 
connectivity for seamless experience on 4G, 5G, Broadband and Fiber.  
Airtel has been running rural enhancement project nationally and will augment network 
connectivity across 60,000 villages in the country by 2024. Kerala has been one of the big focus 
market for the Company and with this initiative, the Company will be augmenting its network 
coverage across 1600 villages and 355 towns covering the entire state.  
To expand coverage in high potential villages in the state and to bring customers to enjoy the 
power of high-speed connectivity, the rural enhancement project covers all 14 districts of Kerala. 
With the aim of stepping up network capacity and taking services deeper into rural and 
unconnected areas. The addition of fresh fiber capacity will support the growth of demand for 
high-speed data services in this region.   
Airtel’s network umbrella in the region now covers all key urban, semi urban and rural areas 
including highways, tourist destinations and trade centers. With this, all popular tourist 
destinations ranging from hill stations to beaches across the state will experience enhanced 
network footprint. Hill Stations in Wayanad, Idukki and Pathanamthitta are equipped with better 
network, making Airtel available even in remote locations.
