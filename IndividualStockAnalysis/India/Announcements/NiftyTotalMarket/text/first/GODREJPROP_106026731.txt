The National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (NCDRC) by its order dated October 
11, 2024 directed Godrej Projects Development Limited, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Godrej 
Properties Limited, to refund over Rs 4 crore to homebuyers in five separate cases for alleged 
failure to construct 24-metre-wide road, falling outside its project known as  ‘Godrej Summit’ 
in Sector 104 in Gurgaon. The said order was received by the Company on October 15, 2024. 
 
While your email indicates that the article was published on October 17, 2024, it first appeared 
in ET Realty on October 16, 2024 at 8:34 am and can be viewed at the link -  NCDRC orders 
Godrej Projects to refund Rs 4 crore to homebuyers (indiatimes.com)  
  
It may further be noted that this refund has been ordered for non-construction of a 24-metre 
road falling outside the Project, even though NCDRC in a previous order, for the same project, 
observed that the developer is not liable to construct the 24 meter road falling outside the 
project. That matter is sub-judice. We are confident of our merits in the matter and are 
preparing to file appeal against the orders.
