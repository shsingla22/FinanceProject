Firstsource positioned a  ‘Leader’ in Everest Group's Healthcare Payer Business 
Process as a Service Solutions PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2024  
 
Mumbai, India | Louisville, US,  October  17, 2024: Firstsource Solutions Limited  (NSE: FSL, BSE:532809), a leading global 
provider of specialist domain -led Business Process Services (BPS) and an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company, today 
announced  that it has been  positioned as a ‘ Leader ’ in Everest Group ’s inaugural Healthcare Payer Business Process as 
a Service (BPaaS) – Solutions PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2024 . This recognition highlights Firstsource’s commitment to 
delivering tech -enabled solutions that help healthcare payers drive efficiency, reduce costs, and enhance  member 
experiences.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
This report shares insights into key industry shifts driving demand for pay -as-you-go, integrated platform and operations 
commonly known as BPaaS, Everest Group evaluated Payer BPaaS  service providers to measur e their market impact, 
vision, and capabilities. Among  the 14 organizations  assessed , Firstsource was highlighted  as one of only three other 
companies in the top category of ‘Leader .’ Everest  Group  noted  Firstsource ’s robust  partnership ecosystem of 
integrators, analytics, cloud and automation providers , as well as the company’s modular go -to-market strategy that 
offer s both one-off and specific solutions alongside  more holistic support. The company also received recognition for 
its strong client relations and support, and investment in digital tools , accelerators, AI , and process mining solutions  
aimed at  streamlin ing operations and improve decision -making .  
 
Venkatgiri Vandali, President – Healthcare & Lifesciences, Firstsource , shared, “We are honored to be recognized as a 
Leader  in Everest G roup’s Healthca re Payer BPaaS Solutions PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2024. This recognition 
underscores the strategic depth of our BPaaS solutions , including a dynamic partnership ecosystem and the ongoing 
investment & integration of technology innovation required to drive new levels of efficiency and cost savings.   As health 
plans navigate an increasingly complex landscape, BPaaS has become essential for enabling scalability and innovation. 
We are committed to empowerin g payers to not only adapt to change but to lead it, ensuring they stay ahead of industry 
challenges while optimizing member outcomes and care delivery”  
 
Vivek Kumar, Practice Director at Everest Group , shared, “Healthcare payers are increasingly focusing on exceptional 
member experience and outcomes -driven results, fueling the need for customizable platform -based solutions that can 
ensure seamless and transformational operations . Firstsource’s customizable BPaaS solution, which includes both ‘in -
a-box’ offerings and functional BPaaS for claims, supported by a diverse partnership ecosystem, has established the 
company as a transformation -driven leader in the  payer BPaaS market. With a digital -first approach, strengthened by 
investments in AI -powered technology, and a library of accelerators, Firstsource has earned its position as  a Leader in 
Everest Group’s Healthcare Payer BPaaS Solutions PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2024.”
