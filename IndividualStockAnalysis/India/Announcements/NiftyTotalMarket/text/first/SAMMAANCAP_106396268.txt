2. Amendments to the AOA  
 
Subject to the approval of the shareholders of the Company, the Board has approved the 
amendment of the AOA to incorporate certain  rights of Investor as envisaged in the SSA  
with effect from the categorization of the Investor as a ‘ promoter’ of the Company in 
accordance with the terms of the SSA. Briefly, these include nomination rights on Board 
and certain committees of the Company, pre -emption and information rights of the Investor 
till the time the Investor remains a promoter of the Company.  
 3. Convening an extra -ordinary general meeting of the Company and related matters  
 
The Board has approved the convening of an extra -ordinary general meeting of the 
shareholders of the Company (“ EGM ”) on October 29, 2025 through video conferencing/ 
other audio visual means for seeking shareholders’ approval inter alia  for: 
 
(i) Preferential Issue; and  
 
(ii) Amendment of the  AOA to incorporate the relevant rights of Investor as envisaged 
in the SSA, some of which qualify as special rights under Regulation 31B of the 
Listing Regulations , and other related matters.
