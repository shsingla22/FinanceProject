Happiest Minds’ Integrated Annual Report 2024 , ranked #42 globally , wins Gold 
and Silver Awards at the LACP 2024 Spotlight Awards  
Bengaluru,  San Jose and London, November 27, 2024 : Happiest Minds Technologies Limited  
(NSE: HAPPSTMNDS), a ‘Born Digital . Born Agile’, Mindful IT Company, today announced that i ts 
Integrated Annual Report (IAR)  for FY 2023-24 has ranked #42  and is among  the Top 100 
worldwide . The IAR has  won two awards –  Gold for the Integrated Report and Silver for the ESG 
Report. This is the fourth year in a row that Happiest Minds has been awarded for its  Annual Report  
from  the League of American Communication Professionals ( LACP ). 
Happiest Minds collaborated with Wyatt Prisms Communications to design the award-winning 
Integrated Annual Report, themed ‘Exploring New Horizons with Generative AI’. From approximately  
1500 entries , Happiest Minds’ IAR 2024 was particularly noted for its  overall narrati ve and visual 
design , creativity, message clarity and relevance.  
 
Venkatraman Narayanan, MD & CFO , Happiest Minds,  said, “We are happy to have  our Integrated 
Annual Report 2024 achieve an impressive rank of #42, placing us among the Top 100 worldwide . This 
year, we are especially pleased to have won Gold and Silver for our Integrated and ESG Reports. 
Winning this honor from LACP for the fourth consecutive year motivates us to keep pushing for excellence and delivering great results .”  
Previously, Happiest Minds was awarded Platinum in 2021, and Gold in 2022 & 2023 for its Integrated 
Annual Reports by LACP. Some other  awards include  Gold at the SAFA Best Presented Annual Report 
Awards  2022 and ICAI Award for Excellence in Financial Reporting 2021 -22.
