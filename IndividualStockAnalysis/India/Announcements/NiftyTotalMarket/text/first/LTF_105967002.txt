Dear Sir/Ma’am,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 and in accordance with Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the 
Listing Regulations read with circular dated July 13, 2023, and other applicable regulations, if 
any, we hereby inform that the Company has received an order dated August 27, 2024 (“the 
Order”) in the name of L&T Finance Limited (which has been merged with the Company 
effective December 4, 2023) whereby the Deputy Commissioner of State Tax, Special Circle, 
Patna, Bihar has imposed a monetary penalty of Rs. 28,73,927.88 under Section 73 of the 
Central Goods and Service Tax Act, 2017.  
 
The Company is contesting the Order in higher appellate forums and the Company does not 
reasonably expect the Order to have any material financial impact.   
 
A copy of the Order was received by the Company on August 27, 2024 at around 02:16 p.m. 
(IST). The details of the Order are enclosed as Annexure - A.  
 
We request you to take the aforesaid on records.
