Sub.: Clarification under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
It has come to our knowledge that there are certain apprehensions about the shortage of 
commercial LPG in our plants as fuel, which may have impacted our production. In this 
connection, we wish to clarify that we use multiple sources of fuel -- the main source being PNG 
(Piped Natural Gas) along with LPG in a very limited manner in our heat treatment process in 
various plants. There is absolutely no disruption in the availability of either PNG or LPG in any of 
our facilities and all our plants continue to operate normally. Further, we also wish to state that 
we are in the process of modifying our fuel system so as to also enable us to use LDO (Light Diesel 
Oil) also in addition to PNG and LPG. 
This clarification is issued for the general information of all the concerned stakeholders in 
accordance with Regulation 30 and Schedule Ill of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
