Dear Sir/Madam, 
In continuation of our earlier correspondence dated 08" November, 2024, we are pleased to inform 
you that the Hon’ble Regional Director, South-East Region, Hyderabad, Ministry of Corporate Affairs, 
has, vide its confirmation order dated 03 May 2025, approved the Scheme of Amalgamation 
between NED Energy Limited (Transferor) and Power Build Batteries Private Limited (Transferee)— 
along with their respective shareholders and creditors in accordance with Section 233 of the 
Companies Act, 2013 read with rules made thereunder. 
NED Energy Limited (Transferor) is a Subsidiary of Time Technoplast Limited, while Power Build 
Batteries Private Limited (Transferee) is its wholly owned step-down subsidiary. The Appointed Date 
for the approved Scheme of Amalgamation is 1* April 2024. 
This merger is a strategic step in our ongoing efforts to simplify the corporate structure, enhance 
operational and administrative efficiency. By bringing both businesses under a single entity, we aim 
to streamline decision-making, optimize resource utilization, improve financial strength and 
reinforce our presence in the energy solutions sector. It also represents a significant milestone in 
uniting our collective strengths to create a more dynamic, innovative, and growth-focused 
organization. 
The manufacturing operations at all units, service support, existing product portfolio and product 
development functions of NED Energy Limited will be seamlessly continued post-merger.
