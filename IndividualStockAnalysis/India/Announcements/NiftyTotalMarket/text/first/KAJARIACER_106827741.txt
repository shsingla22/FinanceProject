CHAIRMAN’S MESSAGE
In Q2 FY27, we achieved  10% YoY volume  growth,  led by 
bette r demand  and gain in market  share . However,  export  
demand  in H1FY27 remained  subdued,  with elevated  freight  
rates  and limited  container  availability  continuing  to pose  
challenges  for manufacturers  in Morbi . Our consolidated  
revenue  grew  by 24% YoY to INR 1468 crores,  supported  by 
healthy  volumes  and the market’s  positive  response  to price  
hikes . EBITDA  margin  for Q2 FY27 stood  at 19.5%.
Our capacity  expansion  plans  at Srikalahasti  (12.50 MSM,  
increased  from 10 MSM)  and Gailpur  (11 MSM)  remain  on 
track . These  facilities  are expected  to strengthen  our 
manufacturing  capabilities  and enable  us to deliver  high-
quality  products  at competitive  costs .
Looking  ahead,  we remain  confident  in our ability  to sustain  
healthy  growth,  strengthen  our market  position  and create  
long-term value  for all stakeholders .
