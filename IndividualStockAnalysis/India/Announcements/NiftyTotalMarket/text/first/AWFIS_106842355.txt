MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY - AMIT RAMANI (CHAIRMAN & MANAGING DIRECTOR)  
“We are pleased to report another quarter of steady performance, supported by continued demand from large 
enterprises & GCCs, alongside our ongoing focus on premiumisation and operational efficiency. Normalised Revenue 
for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹451 Cr , growing 26% YoY , taking H1 FY27 revenue to ₹888 Cr , up 30% YoY . Cash EBITDA grew 
45% YoY  to ₹49 Cr , with margins at 10.8% , taking H1 FY27 Cash EBITDA to ₹93 Cr , up 40% YoY   with  margins  at 10.5%  
 
Our Reported Revenue stood at ₹439 Cr  in Q2FY27 and ₹864 Cr in H1FY27, growing 20%  and 23%  YoY, respectively, 
while Reported EBITDA grew 31%  and 30%  YoY to ₹174 Cr  and ₹336 Cr , with margins of 39.6%  & 38.9%   respectively.  
 
Our Co-working business  continued to see strong demand from large corporates, MNCs and multi -centre clients, with 
revenue growing 31% YoY  to ₹376 Cr  in Q2 and 34% YoY  to ₹740 Cr  in H1 FY27, while Transform  contributed ₹75 Cr  
and ₹149 Cr , respectively, growing 8% in Q2 and 16%  in H1 supported by a healthy project pipeline and growing cross -
selling opportunities.  
 
During the quarter, we added 6 new centres , taking our total supply to 254 centres  with approximately 174k seats  
across 18 cities , serving a diversified client base of over 3,700+ companies . Overall portfolio occupancy stood at 76.2% , 
while occupancy at centres with more than 12 months  of vintage reached 84.1% . 
 
Our premiumisation strategy  continues to gain momentum, with our Gold and Elite portfolio expanding to 39 
centres . We have strengthened our presence across Pune, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi NCR with new premium 
Grade A+ centres. Our latest Awfis 6.0  design further enhances workspace quality, space utilisation and operational 
efficiency, supporting higher realisations.  
 
Our GCC portfolio  continues to scale meaningfully, we currently serve 105+ unique GCC clients , contributing 
approximately 24%  of rental revenue. Our integrated offerings across co -working, managed offices, design and build, 
and allied workplace services position us as a long -term partner for GCCs, from their initial entry into India to 
expansion across multiple locations.  
 
Importantly, our five growth engines of GCC expansion, premiumisation, multi -format supply, organic growth & 
retention and value beyond Flex continue to strengthen our capabilities and support sustainable growth. Our focus 
on improving occupancy, strengthening client renewals and expanding through capital -efficient supply models is 
expected to drive higher realisations and profitability.  
 
As we move ahead, we are confident that our strategy is delivering the desired results, with Gold and Elite centres 
gaining traction and execution strengthening over the last 4 –5 quarters. FY27 is a year of transformation, with a 
continued focus on  driving  high -quality earnings, improving occupancy across our centres thereby achieving Cash 
EBITDA target of ₹190 –200 Cr   for the full year . ”

ABOUT AWFIS SPACE SOLUTIONS LIMITED  
Awfis Space Solutions Ltd. (‘Awfis’) is India’s leading and listed flexible workspace solutions provider offering the 
largest network of agile workspaces. The company enables small and large corporates to seamlessly book and utilize 
workspaces as per their  requirement and convenience. The comprehensive suite of solutions includes Flex Space 
Solutions (Coworking and Customized Managed Offices), Mobility Solutions, Design & Build, Awfis Café, and Tech 
Labs -advanced tech infrastructure. With a strong presence in 18 cities and 2 50+ centres, Awfis serves over 3, 700 
clients across diverse industries, ensuring scalable and adaptable workspace solutions for businesses of all sizes. For 
more information: Please log on to www.awfis.com . In addition, connect with us on Facebook, Instagram and 
LinkedIn.
