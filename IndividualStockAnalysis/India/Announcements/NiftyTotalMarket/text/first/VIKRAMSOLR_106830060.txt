Vikram Solar Secures Major 289.87 MWp Order for Grid -Scale Solar  in 
Rajasthan  from NLC India Limited  
Kolkata, India, 27th October 2026:  Vikram Solar, a leading Indian solar PV module manufacturer, has 
bagged an order to supply 289.87 MWp of N -TOPCon modules to  NLC India Limited (NLCIL), on behalf 
of NLC India Renewables Limited (NIRL)  for an 810 MW solar power project at the RUVN Solar Pa rk in 
Pugal, Bikaner, Rajasthan, one of the largest solar parks in the country.  NLC India Renewables Limited 
(NIRL)  is a wholly owned subsidiary of NLC India Limited .  
Built on N -type TOPCon cell technology, the modules are designed to deliver higher energ y yield and 
stable output in high -irradiance, high -temperature conditions such as those of western Rajasthan.  
Spread across the Thar region, the RUVN Solar Park is central to Rajasthan’s effort to harness its vast 
solar resource, as the state continues to lead India’s utility -scale solar build -out. A role in a project of 
this magnitude reinforces Vikram Solar’s standing as a trusted domestic supplier for grid -scale 
installations, where consistent quality, bankability and timely delivery are critical. The or der further 
adds to the company’s order book as demand for high -efficiency, domestically manufactured modules 
continues to rise under India’s drive for self -reliance in solar manufacturing.  
Mr. Sameer Nagpal, Chief Executive Officer, Vikram Solar, said, “As India builds clean energy capacity 
at gigawatt scale, the industry is placing greater value on technology and supply partners it can rely 
on over the long term. Being entrusted with nearly 290 MWp of modules for this landmark project is a 
strong endorsement of our N -TOPCon technology and our ability to deliver at utility scale. As we 
deepen our integrated manufacturing capabilities, our priority is to give customers the performance 
certainty and supply reliability that projects of this size demand .”
