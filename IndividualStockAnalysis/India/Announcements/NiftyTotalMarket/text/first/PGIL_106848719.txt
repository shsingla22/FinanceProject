Pearl Global Industries Reports Record Half -Yearly Revenue of INR 3,330 Crore; 
EBITDA and PAT Grow 47.7% and 63.6%, Respectively  
Gurugram , 05th Nov 2026: 
Pearl Global Industries Limited (PGIL) (BSE: 532808)  (NSE: PGIL) , India’s largest listed garment 
exporter, manufacturing from multiple sourcing regions  in South Asia, South -East Asia and Central 
America has announced its unaudited financial results for the Quarter and Half year E nded 3 0th 
September  2026. 
 
Consolidated Financial Highlights for H1FY27: 
 
• Revenue stood at INR 3,330  crore , marking the highest ever half yearly revenue , and grew 
31.1 % Y-o-Y, led by growth across all locations   
• Adj. EBITDA (excl. ESOP expense) stood at INR 348 crore, up 47.7% Y-o-Y 
• EBITDA margin stood at 10. 5%, the highest ever EBITDA margin in any H1, with an expansion 
of ~120 BPS Y -o-Y  
• PAT stood at INR 225 crore, up 63.6 % Y-o-Y  
•  
Consolidated Financial Highlights for Q2FY27: 
 
• Revenue stood at INR 1, 802 crore , marking the highest ever quarterly revenue , and grew 
37.2 % Y-o-Y, led by growth across all locations   
• Adj. EBITDA (excl. ESOP expense) stood at INR 184 crore, up 51.1% Y-o-Y 
• EBITDA margin for the quarter stood at 10. 2%, the highest ever EBITDA margin in Q2 series , 
with an expansion of ~90 BPS Y -o-Y  
• Highest ever PAT for the quarter stood at INR 126 crore, up 74.7 % Y-o-Y  
 
 
Standalone Financial Highlights for H1FY27: 
 
• Revenue stood at INR 648 crore , grew by 2 2.1% Y-o-Y  
• Adj. EBITDA (excl. ESOP expense)  stood  at INR 32 crore  with EBITDA margin at 4.9% 
• PAT stood at INR 29 crore  
 
Standalone Financial Highlights for Q2FY27:  
 
• Revenue stood at INR 308 crore , grew by 16.8 % Y-o-Y  
• Adj. EBITDA (excl. ESOP expense) stood at INR 9 crore with EBITDA margin at 3.0% 
• PAT stood at INR 1 6 crore  
  
 
  
Balance Sheet Highlights:  
• Networth as on 3 0th September  2026 stood at INR 1,634 crore compared to INR 1,438 crore as on 
31st March 202 6 
• Cash and Bank Balance (excluding cash earmarked for LC payments) stood at INR  534 crore, with an 
additional investments of INR 104 crore in Mutual funds, totaling INR 638 crore , as on 30th September 
2026  
Other Highlights:  
 
• The Company shipped 43.6  million pieces in H1FY27, up from 37.1  million pieces in H1FY26 
• Declared an interim dividend of INR 5.0 per equity share  representing a 20.6% payout ratio 
(wrt. Group PAT) and 1 00% of face value of share  
• PGIL (Holding Company) received a total dividend of ~ INR 22.5 crore in H1FY27 from NorpKnit 
Industries Limited (Bangladesh Subsidiary) and Pearl Global (HK) Limited (Hong Kong 
Subsidiary), in line with fungibility of cash across group entity  
• Inaugurated new manufacturing facility in Bangladesh. This facility will add ~7 Mn pieces of 
annual capacity at optimum utilisation  
 
  
 
  
Commenting on the Results, Mr. Pulkit Seth, Vice -Chairman & Non -Executive Director, said:  
 
“H1FY27 marks another significant milestone in Pearl Global's growth journey, with the 
Company delivering its strongest -ever half -yearly and quarterly performance driven by robust 
revenue growth and improved profitability. For H1FY27, we achieved our highest -ever half -
yearly revenue of INR 3,330 crore , while EBITDA and Profit After Tax grew by 47.7%  and 63.6%  
respectively  on a Y -o-Y basis . 
A key milestone during the period was the inauguration of our new manufacturing facility in 
Bangladesh, which will add ~7 Mn pieces of annual capacity at optimum utilisation. This 
investment further strengthens our global sourcing platform and enhances our ability to offer 
customers greater flexibility, scalability, and supply chain resilience. With manufacturing 
operations across India, Bangladesh, Vietnam, Indonesia, and Guat emala, Pearl Global is 
uniquely positioned to address the evolving sourcing requi rements of global brands.  
To support our long -term vision, the Company plans to invest ~INR 675 -725 crore between 
FY27 and FY30. These investments are expected to strengthen supply chain integration, 
improve operational efficiencies, support sustainable margin expansion, and create a strong 
foundation for achieving our aspirational revenue target of INR 9,000 -10,000 crore by FY30.  
Reflecting our continued commitment to shareholder value, the board has declared an interim 
dividend of INR 5.0 per equity share, representing ~20 .6% payout ratio (wrt. Group PAT) and 
100% of face value of share . 
We enter the second half of FY27 with a strong footing supported by robust order pipeline, an 
expanded manufacturing footprint, and deep partnerships with leading global brands. 
Supported by our strategic investments and strong execution capabilities, we remain confident 
in our ability to deliver sustainable growth and create long -term value for al l stakeholders.”

 About Pearl Global Industries Limited:  
Established in 1987, Pearl Global Industries Limited (PGIL) is one of India’s largest listed garment 
exporters, manufacturing from multiple sourcing regions South Asia (India, Bangladesh), South -East 
Asia (Vietnam, Indonesia) and Central America (Guatemala ). A preferred long -term vendor to most 
leading global brands, company is amongst the leading player in textile Industry. The mainstay 
business is to create value from competitively manufacturing and exporting fashion garments to 
leading global brands. Pro duct range includes knits, woven, denim, outerwear, activewear and 
athleisure. Company has a well -diversified and de -risked manufacturing base with 26 manufacturing  
units spread across India, Bangladesh,  Vietnam , Indonesia,  and Guatemala. PGIL has a total capacity 
to manufacture  ~108 million pieces per year  at optimum utilisation  (including own and outsourced 
facilities). Company revenue structure is primarily export based. PGIL provides total supply chain 
solutions to its customers , including value retaile rs and high -end fashion brand retails in the USA, EU, 
UK, Japan, Australia and Canada . This b usiness model enables them to offer superior quality products 
across various countries, catering to all kinds of consumers.
