  Press Release  
Q2 FY27 | Standalone  Results  
 
 
Neuland Laboratories Reports Q 2 FY27 Standalone Revenue of ₹ 457.2 Cr 
EBITDA at ₹ 104.6 Cr, EBITDA Margin at 22.9% 
 
Hyderabad, India, November  3, 2026: Neuland Laboratories Limited (“Neuland” or “the Company”) (NSE: NEULANDLAB; 
BSE: 524558), a pharmaceutical manufacturer providing active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), complex intermediates 
and custom manufacturing solutions (CMS) to customers in aroun d 80 countries, today announced its unaudited 
Standalone  Financial Results for the quarter  and half year  ended September  30, 2026, as approved by its Board of 
Directors.  
Q2FY27 and H1FY27 Financial Summary  
 (₹ in Crores, except EPS and percentages)  
 
Key Financial Highlights  
● Standalone  revenue decreased 11.4% YoY to ₹ 457.2  Cr; commercial CMS projects contributed the majority share 
of revenue .  
● EBITDA for the quarter stood at ₹ 104.6  Cr, with EBITDA margin reducing  to 22.9%  (down  754 bps YoY)  due to 
operating deleverage.  
● Profit After Tax for the quarter stood at ₹ 51.1  Cr, down  from ₹ 96.5  Cr in Q 2FY26, with PAT margin at 11.2% 
(down  751 bps YoY) . 
● Diluted EPS for the quarter was ₹ 39.9, compared to ₹ 75.2 in Q 2FY26 . Working capital efficiency improved 
meaningfully, with working capital days reducing to 104 days in H1FY27 from 1 55 days at H1FY26, driven largely 
by decrease in inventory days and receivable days.  
● Capex investment cash outflow of Rs. 261 Cr is largely driven by New R&D and Peptide  Block .

 Business Highlights  
● Inauguration of the new peptide manufacturing facility, with encouraging visibility supported by a growing 
pipeline of upcoming projects.  
● Acquisition of approximately 134 acres of land  in Kakinada , AP, providing a strategic base to support Neuland’s 
long -term capacity and growth requirements . 
● 2 DMFs filed in Q2FY27 for Fezolinetant and Ivacaftor.  
 
Management Commentary  
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Saharsh Davuluri, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director, Neuland 
Laboratories , said:  
“While we have experienced a dip in performance this quarter, H1 FY27 was largely in line with plan  and our growth 
expectations remain intact for FY27 and the following year. Commercial CMS molecules continue to drive near -term 
growth, while strong RFP activity along with progress on traction with Big Pharma support customer diversification and 
the long er-term pipeline. The investments in peptides, R&D and land acquisition strengthen our capabilities while enabling 
us to expand with agility.”

About Neuland Laboratories Limited  
For over four decades, Neuland Laboratories has been at the forefront of manufacturing active pharmaceutical 
ingredients (APIs) through its cGMP manufacturing facilities, working with customers in close to 80 countries. Neuland 
has developed more than 300 processes and 100 APIs and  has filed over 1,01 6+ regulatory filings across the US (7 7 active 
US DMFs), the European Union and other geographies. Its record of quality manufacturing and reliability is underpinned 
by cGMP certifications, including from the U.S. FDA, TGA (Australia), EDQM (EU), German Health Authority, ANVISA 
(Brazil), EMA (E U), Cofepris (Mexico), KFDA (Korea), PMDA (Japan), CFDA (China), FSI “SID & GP” (Russia) and Health 
Canada, along with ISO 9001, ISO 14001, OHSAS 18001 and ISO 27001 certifications. For more information, visit 
www.neulandlabs.com .
