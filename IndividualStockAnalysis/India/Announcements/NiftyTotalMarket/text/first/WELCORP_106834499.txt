Welspun Corp Delivers Highest Ever Quarterly EBITDA in Q2FY27   
Record All Time High Order Book | Strengthening Global Footprint | Strong Balance Sheet 
 
October 29, 2026, Mumbai: Welspun Corp Ltd (WCL), the flagship company of Welspun World, announced its consolidated 
financial results for the quarter ended September 30th, 2026. 
 
Q2FY27 Highlights: 
 
 EBITDA  for Q2FY27 stood  at 782 crore,  up 25% YoY,  maintaining strong momentum .  
 EBITDA  for H1FY27 grew by 30% to Rs. 1,538 cr ore 
 PAT without exceptional items for H1FY27 grew 26% YoY to ₹997 crore. 
 ROCE stood strong at 22.7%  
 Net cash position further strengthened to ₹3,887 crore after incurring capex of ₹1,616 crore.  
 Current global order book stands at an all-time record high of ~₹50,000 crore. 
 Expanding Spiral (HSAW) Pipe capacity by 225 KMTPA in USA at a capex of US$115 million  
 Setting up a greenfield Spiral (HSAW) Pipe Plant along with coating facility in Jordan in a  JV with Perma Pipes Inc at 
a total cost of US$125 million  
 Acquiring 49% direct stake in its step down subsidiary Welspun Special Coating Private Ltd. for setting up of an 
Internal FBE (Fusion Bonded Epoxy) coating plant in India in a partnership with Perma-Pipe .

Speaking  about  the results,  Mr. Vipul  Mathur,  MD & CEO,  Welspun  Corp  Ltd. said, 
 
“We have delivered a consistent performance during Q2FY27, with sustained  progress across key financial 
parameters. H1FY27 EBITDA grew by 30% to Rs. 1,538 cr, against our full -year guidance of Rs. 2,850 cr. Our net 
cash position improved further, while ROCE stood at ~ 23%. Our order book reached an all -time record high of 
~50,0 00 cr, providing strong medium to long -term business visibility.  
With strong demand visibility in the USA and the Middle East, we are strengthening our manufacturing footprint by 
adding a new spi ral pipe facility in the USA and setting up a greenfield spiral pipe facility in Jordan through a joint 
venture with Perma -Pipe. Our entry into Jordan marks another step in expanding our reach into new markets and 
reinforcing our position in the global lin e pipe industry.  
As we pursue these opportunities, we remain focused on our core products and geographies, prudent capital 
allocation, maintaining strong supplier - customer relationships, innova tion and building capabilities across our 
organisation.  
Our people remain integral to our growth strategy, while our sustainability agenda continues to guide responsible 
manufacturing and long -term value creation .” 
 
About  Welspun  Corp  Ltd: 
Welspun Corp Limited (WCL) is the flagship company of Welspun World, one of India’s fastest growing 
multinational  groups.  WCL  has a diversified  portfolio  spanning  Line Pipes,  Ductile  Iron (DI) Pipes,  Stainless  Steel 
Bars, Pipes & Tubes, and TMT Rebars. The company also houses Sintex, India’s most trusted brand in water 
storage tanks and advanced plastic piping solutions, including OPVC.  
 
WCL serves customers across six continents and more than fifty countries, supported by world class 
manufacturing facilities in India, the United States of America, and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. With a strong 
focus on responsi ble and sustainable growth, WCL is ranked 5th globally and 2nd in India among steel 
companies  in the S&P Global  Corporate  Sustainability  Assessment  (CSA)  for the Dow Jones  Sustainability  Index 
(DJSI). This  recognition reflects  WCL’s commitment  to deliverin g critical  infrastructure  solutions  that strengthen 
energy security, water accessibility, and sustainable development worldwide.
