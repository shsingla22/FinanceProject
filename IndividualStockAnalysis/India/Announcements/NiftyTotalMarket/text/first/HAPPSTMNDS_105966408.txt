Happiest Minds wins  Gold and Silver at the Brandon Hall Group HCM Excellence 
Awards  2024  
Seattle , London and Bengaluru, August 2 7, 2024:  Happiest Minds Technologies Limited (NSE: 
HAPPSTMNDS), a ‘Born Digital .  Born Agile’, Mindful IT Company, today announced  that it has been 
honored with two prestigious accolades at the 17th Brandon Hall Group HCM Excellence Awards  
2024. Happiest Minds  won a Gold in Talent Acquisition for the ‘Best New Hire Onboarding 
Program ’ - Data Engineering on Microsoft Azure and a Silver in Future of Work for the ‘Best 
Program for Upskilling Employees ’ - Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET ). These 
awards reinforce Happiest Minds' position as a leader in the IT industry, particularly highlighted by 
its partnership with StackRoute  — an NIIT venture renowned for its excellence in tech education.  
The Brandon Hall Group HCM Excellence Awards recognizes organizations that achieve excellence 
in various corporate sectors, including Learning and Development, Talent Management, Leadership 
Development, Talent Acquisition, Workforce Management, HR, and Sales Performance. Happiest 
Minds stole the limelight for its innovative approach to integrating new talents into its ecosystem  
and its forward-thinking approach to  preparing its workforce for the evolving landscape of the IT 
industry through comprehensive upskilling programs.  
                    
Rajesh Chandran Sogasu, Senior Vice President, Head  -Talent Acquisition  and L&D , Happiest 
Minds,  said, “These awards showcase our company’s dedication to building the future of work . We 
have always concentrated on equipping our  people  with the skills needed to  remain at the forefront  
and tackle tomorrow’s challenges. Through ongoing investment in professional development and 
emerging technologies, we ensure that we lead the industry with innovation and expertise ” 
Dr Yogesh Kumar Bhatt,  Executive Vice President, StackRoute, NIIT Limited, said, "Our tailored 
programs, focusing on data engineering with Microsoft Azure and comprehensive SDET training, address Happiest Minds' unique needs with precision. These initiatives have driven innovation, filled 
talent gaps, and furnished new hires with vital skills in cloud engineering and problem -solving. The 
SDET training's emphasis on JavaScript and automation tools has significantly contributed to successful project deployments, highlighting the effectiveness of our approach. I'm grateful for the 
leadershi p at Happiest Minds for the chance to develop talents aligned with their  business goals. ”
