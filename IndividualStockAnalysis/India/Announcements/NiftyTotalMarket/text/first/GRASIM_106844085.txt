Grasim  and Suzlon enter into pact  to Develop, Commercialize and Scale India’s 
first Wind Turbines with Recyclable Blades Powered by Recyclamine® 
Technology.  
4 November 2026 | New Delhi  
Grasim Industries Limited ’s Advanced Materials Division and its renewable arm Aditya 
Birla Renewables Limited (ABRe n) have entered into a Memorandum of 
Understanding (MoU)  with Suzlon Energy Limited, to collaborate on a pilot basis for 
the development, commercialization, and scaling of wind turbines equipped with 
recyclable blade technology. Signed at the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 
2026, the partnership is founded on a shared commitment to advancing circularity in 
the wind energy sector and accelerating the adoption of sustainable end -of-life 
solutions for wind turbine blades.  
Today, conventional wind turbine blades are predominantly manufactured using 
thermoset composite materials that are difficult to recycle at the end of their service 
life, often resulting in landfill disposal or incineration. Blades manufactured using 
Recyc lamine® epoxy technology, supplied by Grasim, can be chemically recycled at 
end-of-life, enabling the recovery of valuable composite constituents for reuse in new 
applications. This breakthrough technology supports circular economy principles, 
minimizes wa ste, reduces environmental impact, and enhances the overall 
sustainability of wind energy projects.  
Under this collaboration, Suzlon, as the wind turbine OEM, will integrate Recyclamine® 
technology into the manufactur ing of recyclable wind turbine blades. Aditya Birla 
Renewables will deploy a wind turbine equipped with recyclable blades at one of its 
selected project sites, demonstrating the commercial viability of circular blade 
technology under real -world operating cond itions. The parties will also jointly explore 
opportunities to drive broader commercialization and market adoption of recyclable 
blade -enabled wind turbines powered by Recyclamine® technology across India and 
other mutually agreed markets.  
“This collaboration marks an important step toward building a truly circular wind energy 
ecosystem. By combining the Recyclamine® technology with Suzlon’s manufacturing 
expertise and ABRe n’s renewable energy ambitions, we are demonstrating that 
sustainability and innovation can go hand in hand. Together, we aim to accelerate the 
adoption of recyclable wind turbine blades and set new benchmarks for resource 
efficiency in the wind industry", said Rajesh Balakrishnan,  Chief Executive Officer, 
Advanced Material s, Grasim Industries Ltd . 

 
                               
 
“The future of renewable energy lies not just in generating clean power, but in building 
truly circular solutions. At Suzlon, we are advancing this vision through our flagship 
S144 platform, which has one of the world's lowest carbon footprints among wind 
turbines, and by working towards making a ll future turbines recyclable within the next 
three to four years. This partnership brings together innovation and scale to accelerate 
material circularity, with the potential to enable significant CO ₂ savings. It is another 
step towards ensuring that ever y turbine we build delivers lasting value for both the 
planet and future generations."  Said Girish Tanti, Co -Founder, Suzlon Group . 
“At ABRen, we believe the energy transition must be accompanied by innovation that 
enhances environmental sustainability. This partnership is an important step toward 
addressing the end -of-life challenges associated with wind turbine blades and 
advancing c ircularity within the renewable energy sector .” said Amit Jain, Chief 
Executive Officer , Aditya Birla Renewables Limited
