33. TDS on Dividend paid to Shareholder:
 For Resident Individual shareholder
 As per section 393(1)[T able Sr. No. 7] of Income T ax 
Act, 2025, any domestic company making payment 
to a resident shareholder shall deduct tax at 10% at 
the time of payment of dividend to shareholder if it 
exceeds ` 10,000 and who has provided valid PAN.
 Further, if the resident individual shareholder 
provides duly filed declaration in Form 121 for 
relevant tax year, no TDS will be deducted.
 In case of invalid or non-availability of PAN, tax will be 
deducted at the rate of 20%.
 For Resident shareholder other than individual 
(HUF/ LLP/AOP/Companies/Firm/Trust):
 At 10% on the entire amount of dividend to be 
received by the shareholder without any threshold.
 In case of invalid or non-availability of PAN, tax will be 
deducted at the rate of 20%.
