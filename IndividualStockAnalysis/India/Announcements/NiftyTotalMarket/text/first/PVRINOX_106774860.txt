PVR INOX STRENGTHENS ITS BENGALURU PRESENCE WITH A NEW 4DX 
DESTINATION AT SOUL SPACE SPIRIT  
~Three new screens, advanced cinema technology and elevated design come together to 
create a larger, more immersive entertainment destination in Bellandur  ~ 
National , September 8, 2026 : PVR INOX Limited, India ’s largest and most premium cinema 
exhibition company, has expanded PVR Soul Space Spirit, Bellandur, with three new auditoriums, 
including Bengaluru ’s eighth 4DX experience and two mainstream screens.  
The addition takes the cinema to seven screens and positions Soul Space Spirit as a more 
expansive entertainment destination for audiences across the Bellandur –Marathahalli –Varthur –
Sarjapur Road corridor. Located on Bengaluru ’s Outer Ring Road, the propert y is surrounded by 
established residential communities and major technology hubs, making it a natural leisure 
destination for audiences who live and work in the area. The location is listed at Soul Spirit Central 
Mall, Bellandur, on Outer Ring Road.   
For o ver 15 years, PVR Soul Space Spirit has served the Bellandur community. This expansion 
builds on that legacy by adding more choice, contemporary design and a more immersive way to 
experience films close to home and work.  
Making its debut at Soul Space Spir it, Bengaluru ’s eighth 4DX auditorium brings a new layer of 
immersion to the destination. Motion -enabled seating and multisensory effects are designed to 
place audiences at the centre of the action, extending the experience beyond the screen.  
The three new  auditoriums have a dedicated entrance on the fifth floor, while the existing four 
screens will continue to operate on the fourth floor. Across the expanded cinema, guests can 
enjoy superior laser projection, Dolby Atmos  Surround Sound and Next Gen 3D. The  property will 
offer a total seating capacity of 1143 . 
Commenting on the occasion, Mr. Ajay Bijli, Managing Director, PVR INOX Limited, said  
“Bengaluru is one of India ’s most dynamic cinema markets, shaped by a discerning audience, a 
vibrant urban culture and a strong affinity for shared entertainment experiences. For us, 
expanding at Soul Space Spirit is about deepening our relationship with a community that has 
been part of our journey for many years. As the city continues to evolve, we remain focused on 
creating cinema destinations that are closer to where people live and work, while offering 
experiences that make going to the movies more meaningful, social and memorable. ” 
 
 
 
Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli, Executive Director, PVR INOX Limited, added , “The expans ion of Soul 
Space Spirit is designed to give audiences greater choice and a more elevated cinema experience. 
With three additional screens, including a new 4DX auditorium, the property now brings together 
enhanced technology, contemporary design and a wide r range of viewing formats under one roof. 
The addition allows us to strengthen an established cinema while ensuring that it continues to 
meet the expectations of today ’s moviegoers. ” 
The expansion is part of PVR INOX ’s continued focus on strengthening its  presence in high -growth 
urban catchments and evolving established properties in line with changing audience 
expectations.
