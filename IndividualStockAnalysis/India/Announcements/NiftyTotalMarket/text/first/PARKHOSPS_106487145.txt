Press Release  
Acquisition of Krishna Super -speciality Hospital , Bathinda , Punjab  
Expanding its footprint in North India , Park Medi World  Limited  (NSE:  PARKHOSPS ; BSE : 544645), 
North India’s 2nd largest chain of hospitals, has acquire d 250 bedded Krishna Super -speciality 
Hospital, Bathinda  (“Hospital” ), transition ing from managing to fully acquiring the 250 -bed Hospital 
in Bathinda . The Park Group has been managing operations of Krishna Super -speciality Hospital  since 
July 2025, and has now acquire d 100% shareholding from the current promoters in an all -cash deal for 
₹40 crore .  
This Hospital  is a comprehensive healthcare hub featuring:  
❖ Infrastructure:  250 beds (including 70 ICU beds), modular OTs, and automated labs.  
❖ Expertise:  Highly -trained experienced medical professionals across multiple super -specialities 
such as  Cardiac Sciences, Endocrinology, Neurosciences , Oncology & Radiation, Orthopaedics & 
Joint Replacement, Urology & Nephrology, Critical Care, Gastroenterology and more . 
❖ Strategy:  This acquisition strengthens Park Group's cluster -based growth model, driving 
operational synergies and clinical excellence across North India.  
❖ Social Impact:  The Hospital aims to bridge the healthcare gap for rural and economically -weaker 
communities, providing metropolitan -grade care locally and affordably.  
 
Speaking about the acquisition, Dr. Ankit Gupta, Managing Director, Park Medi World 
Limited , said:  
“We are proud to announce the acquisition of Krishna Super -speciality Hospital, Bathinda , 
marking a significant milestone in our mission to provide world -class healthcare across North 
India. After managing the hospital's operations for the past several months, we have committed to 
bring this 250 -bed facility fully into the Park Medi World fam ily. Centrally located to serve both 
metropolitan and rural patients, th is hospital  will help us in bridging the gap between urban and 
rural medical access , reflectin g philosophy of our Founder, Dr. Ajit Gupta , 'wellness for all '—
ensuring that premium, lif e-saving care is not a luxury, but a reality for every family in the 
Northern Region. ” 

About Krishna Super -speciality Hospital, Bathinda , Punjab  
Strategically positioned in the heart of Bathinda, Krishna Super -speciality Hospital , 
owned by  Mahip Hospitals Private Limited , is a premier 250 -bed facility designed for 
maximum accessibility. It serves as a vital healthcare hub for both urban and rural 
districts, offering a centralized location for multiple  super -specialties. The Hospital 
integrates advanced care in fields such as Cardiac Sciences, Neurosciences, Oncology, 
Orthopaedics , and more , bringing world -class medical expertise and highly trained 
professionals together under one roof.  
 
About Park Group  
Park Group is North India’s 2nd largest Hospital Chain, currently operating 14 hospitals  
with a combined capacity of 3,250 beds. Park Group is in process of integrating additional 
seven  hospitals which are under various stages of execution and it will add 2,010 beds . 
These expansions will take Park Group’s total bed capacity to 5,260 beds  by March 2028, 
underscoring  its aggressive growth and leadership in healthcare delivery. The Group has 
established a strong presence across key cities including Delhi, Gur gaon, Faridabad, 
Panipat, Karnal, Sonipat, Ambala, Mohali, Patiala, Bathinda, Behror and Jaipur . 
Built on a commitment to accessible, high -quality healthcare, Park Group offers a 
comprehensive range of clinical services —spanning super -specialities such as 
cardiology, neurology, oncology, orthopaedics, gastroenterology, critical care, 
nephrology, and w omen & child health. Each facility is equipped with modern medical 
infrastructure, advanced diagnostics, 24×7 emergency support, and multi -disciplinary 
teams delivering evidence -based care.  
Through continuous expansion and strategic investments, Park Group is strengthening 
its mission to build a dependable, technology -enabled healthcare ecosystem that serves 
both metropolitan and regional communities across North India.
