Thangamayil Jewellery  opened 2 new stores in 
Chennai  
• The brand operated 66+ stores  across Tamil Nadu  
 
Chennai, 24th August 2026:  Leading jewellery retailer  Thangamayil Jewellery 
has expanded its presence in Chennai with the opening of two new stores in 
Mogappair West and Purasaiwakkam on 23rd August 2026. The launch takes 
the brand’s operational network to 6 6+ stores across Tamil Nadu.  
The Mogappair West  showroom and the Purasaiwakkam showroom were  
inaugurated by  the Management of Thangamayil Jewellery  in the presence of 
company officials and customers. A range of special offers has been introduced 
to mark the opening of these new showrooms.  
The newly inaugurated stores are located at Plot No. 4/5, TNHB, 8th Block, 
Mogappair West, Nolambur, Chennai - 600037 and No. 138 ( Old No. 228), 
Purasaiwakkam High Road, Chennai - 600084. The new outlets in Mogappair 
West and Purasaiwakkam span ning 6270  sq.ft and 8746 sq.ft respectively , 
have been designed to deliver a modern and immersive shopping experience.  
The grand opening witnessed an overwhelming response from customers 
across the city, as hundreds of jewellery enthusiasts, families, and well -wishers 
thronged the showroom to celebrate the launch and experience its premium 
collections.  

Speaking on the inauguration, Mr.  Ba. Ramesh, Joint Managing Director, 
Thangamayil Jewellery Limited said, “We are happy to announce the launch of 
our new stores in Chennai , which is a very significant market for us. These 
flagship showrooms reflect our commitment to offering an unparalleled 
jewellery shopping experience with the finest craftsmanship, transparency, and 
exceptional service”.  
The new showrooms offer a wide range of gold, diamond, and silver jewellery 
collections.  The stores have been launched to cater to growing consumer 
demand in Chennai and will serve customers across categories including bridal 
jewellery, festive purchases, everyday wear, gifting, and special occasions.   
About Thangamayil Jewellery Ltd:  
Thangamayil Jewellery Limited (TMJL), is one of the fastest growing 
companies in India, positioned in the market as a chain of retail jewellery stores 
across Tamil Nadu.   Headquartered in Madurai, Thangamayil Jewellery turned  
public limited company in 2007. It released its maiden equity shares in 2010 , 
and the shares were listed in Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) Limited and 
National Stock Exchange (NSE) Limited. The c ompany has its presence in 
Chennai t hrough its flagship store at T.Nagar and stores in s uburbs of Chennai 
and overall presence in Tamil Nadu all comprising 66+ stores, thereby 
increasing the stores to foster future growth and development.  Today, the 
company boasts a total operating area of approx. 1,49,000  square feet  all over 
Tamil Nadu.
