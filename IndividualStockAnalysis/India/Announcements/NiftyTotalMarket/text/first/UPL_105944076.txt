Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 –  Update on Class action suit filed against Advanta Seeds 
Pty Limited  (“Advanta ”) in Australia . 
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements)  Regulations,  2015 (“SEBI LODR Regulations”)  the Company  vide 
communication dated 29
th February, 2024,  had informed the Exchange under  Regulation 30(4)(i)(a) of  
SEBI LODR Regulations that , High Court of Australia had granted  a Special Leave to appeal on the order 
of  Queensland Court of Appeal that  dismissed a class action litigation against Advanta, Company's 
subsidiary in Australia. The matter pertained to certain set of commercial growers commencing a class 
action in the Supreme Court of Queensland at Brisbane in which they alleged that the contaminated grain sorghum seed that was supplied by Advanta had caused them pure economic loss in the form of reduced 
income and increased expenditure.  
 
The Hon’ble  High Court  of Australia  (please refer to the order uploaded on the website 
https://eresources.hcourt.gov.au/showCase/2024/HCA/25 on 7th August, 2024 has dismissed  the appeal 
and has decided the matter in favour of Advanta. The High Court has upheld the judgment of the Court 
of Appeal, that the Advanta was not liable for the economic loss to the growers.  For synopsis of the said 
order and details please refer to the court link https://www.hcourt.gov.au/assets/publications/judgment -
summaries/2024/hca- 25-2024- 08-07.pdf  
 The Company is pleased to inform that the Hon’ble High Court has ruled in its favour in this long and 
complex matter.  Advanta began operations in Central Queensland more than 60 years ago and is proud of the contribution made to agriculture across Australia.  Throughout this case, Advanta has worked 
closely with growers to support improved crop productivity, innovation and profitability.  
 
This marks the end of the Class Action as there are no further avenues of appeal available.  This order 
has no financial or other implications for the Company.
