Commenting on results, Mr. Naresh Patel, Executive Chairman & Managing Director, 
Acutaas Chemicals  Limited,  said: “We delivered strong revenue of ₹4,369 million, 
representing 42.7% year -on-year growth. The performance was led by the continued 
momentum in our Pharmaceutical Intermediates business, which was offset by softer 
performance in the Specialty Chemicals segment .  
A significant milestone during the quarter was the inauguration of our South Korea 
manufacturing facility ahead of schedule. The facility is equipped to produce high -purity 
chemicals used in photoresists, semiconductor packaging materials, etching material s and 
other semiconductor applications, positioning us to capitalize on opportunities in the fast -
growing semiconductor ecosystem.  
Improved revenue visibility and robust order pipeline gives me confidence to revise our FY27 
revenue growth guidance upward from 25% to around 30%. We are also raising our EBITDA 
margin guidance to 38 -40% range.”

ABOUT ACUTAAS CHEMICALS  LIMITED:  
Acutaas Chemicals Limited is a speciality chemicals manufacturer serving a wide range of 
industries, including pharmaceuticals, semiconductors, battery chemicals , personal care, 
agrochemicals, and fine chemicals. Our core strength lies in the research -driven 
development and manufacturing of advanced pharmaceutical intermediates used in both 
regulated and generic APIs, as well as intermediates for New Chemical Enti ties (NCEs).  
We are also expanding our portfolio to include high -value speciality chemicals for emerging 
technologies such as battery materials, semiconductor, and next -generation electronic 
applications.
