Earnings  Release        For Immediate Publication  
Nuvama delivers another quarter of diversified  growth ; Q2 PAT up 26% YoY to ₹319 Cr  
oH1 PAT reaches ₹625 Cr
oClient Assets reach ₹5.5 trillion
Mumbai, 6th November  2026: Nuvama Wealth Management Limited (NSE, BSE: NUVAMA), one 
of India’s leading wealth management companies, announced  its financial results for the 
quarter and half year ending 30th September  2026. 
Nuvama Group continued to deliver strong and diversified growth in Q2 and H1  FY27, 
underpinned by the strength of its integrated wealth management platform. Operating profit 
grew 26% YoY to ₹319 Cr in Q2 FY27 while H1 FY27 rose 21% YoY to ₹625 Cr. 
Wealth Management continued its track record of consistent compounding, with profits 
growing 22% YoY in Q2 and net flows reaching record levels. Asset Services maintained strong 
momentum, while Capital Markets delivered a healthy performance supported by a  diversified 
revenue mix . Reflecting continued client confidence, client assets reached ₹5.5 trillion , up 25% 
YoY, while MPIS and ARR client assets across Nuvama Wealth and Private increased 27% YoY 
to nearly ₹1.1 trillion .

Commenting on the performance  Ashish Kehair, MD & CEO of Nuvama Group  said,  
 
“We delivered another quarter of strong performance, with Q2 operating profits growing       
26% YoY and RoE remaining strong at ~29%. Wealth Management continued its track record 
of consistent compounding with record net flows, Asset Services sustained strong momentum, 
and Capital Markets delivered a healthy performance despite a softer market en vironment.  
 
While we are pleased with our progress,  we remain in the early innings of a much larger 
opportunity. The structural shifts underway are expanding both the size and depth of the 
addressable market  across each of our businesses . 
 
We believe Nuvama is uniquely positioned to benefit from these trends. Over the years, we 
have built scaled and profitable franchises across our business segments . While each segment 
is independently strong, the real advantage lies in how they work together. The synergies  
create a powerful ecosystem that is difficult to replicate. We believe the full value of this 
integrated platform is only beginning to emerge . 
 
At the heart of this franchise are our people. Our ability to attract, develop and retain high -
quality talent  remains one of our most important competitive advantages. As we look ahead, 
we remain focused on building an institution that delivers enduring value for clients, employees 
and shareholders alike."

About Nuvama Group  
 
Nuvama has built a strong foundation of trust and reputation in the Indian market over 30 years.
