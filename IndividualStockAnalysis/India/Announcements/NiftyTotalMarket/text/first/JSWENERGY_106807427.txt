JSW Energy ’s Installed Capacity Reaches 15.1 GW;  Adds ~1.7 G W since April 2026  
 
Mumbai, India — October  5, 2026 – JSW Energy Limited ("the Company") is pleased to announce 
the addition of 1, 694 MW of generation capacity since April 2026 , including ~1.4 GW of 
renewable capacity . With these additions, the Company’s total installed capacity stands at  15,147 
MW.  
The capacity added in FY27 to date comprises 593 MW of solar, 108 MW of wind , 542 MW of 
hybrid and 150 MW of hydro  projects , along with 300 MW of inorganic thermal capacity  of Maruti 
Clean Coal & Power Ltd . 
 
 
 
 
 
Renewable energy now constitutes ~61% (9, 189 MW) of the Company's operational portfolio, 
comprising 3,125 MW of wind, 2,426 MW of solar, 1, 857 MW of hybrid, and 1,781 MW of hydro 
capacity. The thermal portfolio stands at 5,958 MW. The Company remains firmly on track to 
achieve its FY27 RE capacity addition target of 3 GW, having already delivered approximately 46% 
of the targeted capacity.  
JSW Energy has a total locked -in generation capacity of 32.4 GW — comprising 15. 1 GW 
operational and 1 4.5 GW under construction across thermal, hydro, and renewables — along 
with a pipeline of 2.8 GW. Locked -in energy storage capacity stands at 29.6 GWh, comprising 
26.4 GWh of pumped hydro storage and 3.2 GWh of battery energy storage — positioning the 
Company to supply firm, round -the-clock power as the sector shifts from energy to 
dispatchability . 
The Company is targeting 30 GW of generation capacity and 40 GWh of energy storage by 2030, 
and carbon neutrality by 2050.  
 
 
 Mr. Sharad Mahendra, Joint Managing Director and CEO of JSW Energy  
"Having commissioned 1.4 GW of renewable energy capacity in the first half of FY27, we remain 
firmly on track to deliver the balance 1.6 GW during the year. The remaining capacity is fully de -
risked, with land, transmission connectivity, and equipment suppl y already in place. Supported by 
a favourable  construction season in the second half, we are well positioned to achieve our 3 GW 
annual capacity addition target, a significant milestone in our roadmap to 30 GW by FY30. "

 ABOUT JSW ENERGY:  JSW Energy Ltd is one of the leading Private sector power producers in 
India and part of the USD 25 billion JSW Group which has significant presence in sectors such as 
steel, energy, infrastructure, cement, sports among others. JSW Energy Ltd has establish ed its 
presence across the value chains of power sector with diversified assets in power generation, 
and transmission. With strong operations, robust corporate governance and prudent capital 
allocation strategies, JSW Energy continues to deliver sustainabl e growth, and create value for 
all stakeholders. JSW Energy began commercial operations in 2000, with the commissioning of 
its first 2x130 MW thermal power plants at Vijayanagar, Karnataka. Since then, the company has 
steadily enhanced its power generation  capacity from 260 MW to 1 5.1 GW, ensuring diversity in 
geographic presence, fuel sources and power off -take arrangements. The Company is presently 
constructing various power projects to the tune of 1 4.5 GW, with a vision to achieve a total power 
generation capacity of 30 GW by 2030.
