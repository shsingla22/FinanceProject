Mphasis partners with Indian School of Business (ISB) to establish 
an ‘AI Hub’  
~  Mphasis F1 Foundation will invest ~ INR 20 crore over the next four years  
~ The hub to drive various Impactful AI initiatives across healthcare and finance sectors  
   
 
 
Bengaluru , Hyderabad, May  21, 202 6 
 
Mphasis , (BSE: 526299 ; NSE: MPHASIS ), a global AI -led, platform -driven technology solutions provider, and the Indian 
School of Business  (ISB), today announced the launch of the ‘ Mphasis AI Hub ’, a cross -functional collaboration aimed at 
accelerating AI -led research, innovation, and capacity building for societal and sectoral impact in India. The initiative is 
supported by the Mphasis F1 Foundation , which will invest ~ INR 20 crore over four years in Phase 1 to advance research 
and applied AI solutions.  
 
The initiative will focus initially on Healthcare and Finance, with plans to expand into other priority sectors. At the core of 
the partnership is the establishment of a dedicated AI r esearch hub at ISB, designed to serve as a platform for 
interdisciplinary research and multi -stakeholder collaboration. Through this initiative, ISB aims to advance responsible AI 
innovation in the financial and healthcare sectors by driving research that addresses real -world challenges; enabling the 
responsible adoption, eva luation, and impact assessment of AI solutions in live environments; strengthening digital 
literacy and institutional capacity among relevant stakeholders; and fostering wider knowledge -sharing through 
repositories, partnerships, and annual convenings.  
 
The Mphasis AI Hub will also focus on building frameworks to assess tangible business value and long -term sustainability 
of AI deployments, generate policy insights, create AI learning resources, support faculty -led pedagogical innovations, and 
sustain collaboration across academia, industry, and government.  
 
“We are pleased to partner with the Indian School of Business. As AI continues to reshape industries and redefine how 
organisations operate, this research platform, combined with ISB’s academic expertise, will advance responsible, 
informed decision- making. Through this initiative, we look forward to building a meaningful collaboration that brings 
together cross -functional collaboration to advance AI -led knowledge creation and innovation ,” said Deepa Nagraj, Senior 
Vice President & Global Head - ESG, Sparkle  Innovation Ecosystem, and Communications, Mphasis . 
 
“We are thankful for this collaboration with Mphasis, as this reflects our belief that AI, when guided by rigorous research 
and ethical frameworks, can drive meaningful change across sectors and communities. The partnership will help 
strengthen ISB’s commitment to building knowledge that helps shape India’s future through innovation, inclusion, and evidence -based decision- making
,” said Professor Madan Pillutla, Dean, ISB.  
 
The Mphasis AI Hub represents a significant step forward in ISB’s mission to advance evidence -led research and harness 
technology for national development. By combining ISB’s academic expertise with Mphasis’ leadership in technology, the initiative aims to unlock new possibi lities at the intersection of AI, innovation, and societal impact.
