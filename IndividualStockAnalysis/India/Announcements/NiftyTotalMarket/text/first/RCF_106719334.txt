Sub: Intimation pursuant regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
In terms of Regulation 30 read with Schedule III Part B of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that the Department of 
Fertilizers (DoF), Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers, Government of India, has issued Notification 
No. 12012/3/2023-UPP (E.34185) dated 30th July, 2026 regarding the fixation of revised Energy 
Norms applicable under the Urea Pricing Policy. 
As per the aforesaid notification, the revised Energy Norm for the Company's Thai Unit has been 
fixed at 5.984 Gcal per Metric Tonne (PMT) with effect from April 1, 2025, as against the existing 
norm of 6.200 Gcal PMT. The revised Energy Norm shall remain applicable up to March 31,2028. 
Based on the notification, the Company has made a preliminary assessment and estimates an 
adverse financial impact of approximately Rs.171.54 crore on account of revision in the energy norm 
applicable compriSing Rs.132.52 crore pertaining to FY 2025-26 and Rs.39.02 crore pertaining to 
Q1 of FY 2026-27. The impact is subject to detailed evaluation, final computation and accounting 
treatment in accordance with the applicable Accounting Standards and the provisions of the Urea 
SubSidy Scheme. 
Further, there is no change in the Energy Norm applicable to the Company's Trombay Unit. 
The said notice was received by the Company on July 30, 2026 at 17.58 pm
