PRESS RELEASE                                           October  29, 202 6 
 
Chennai, October 29, 2026 : The Board of Directors of Equitas Small Finance Bank Limited at its 
meeting held today, approved the unaudited financial results for quarter and half year ended 
September 30, 2026   
 
Total Business crossed Rs. 1 Lakh Crore milestone  in Q2FY27  
Highest Quarterly PAT of Rs. 218 Cr in Q2FY27, growth of 803% YoY  
ROA and ROE for Q 2FY27 at 1.31% and 13 .50% respectively  
Gross Ad vances grew by 30 % YoY and 6% QoQ  
Deposits grew by 1 9% YoY  & 7% QoQ , CASA at 2 6% 
 
1. Business  Highlights : 
 Gross advances grew by 30% year -on-year and 6% quarter -on-quarter, crossing the 
₹50,000 crore milestone, supported by consistent disbursements across business 
verticals.  
 Total Deposits grew by 19% year -on-year and 7% quarter -on-quarter, also crossing the 
₹50,000 crore milestone.  
 Overall disbursements stood at ₹7,450 crore  in Q2FY27 , up 38% year -on-year and 10% 
quarter -on-quarter.  
 Non -MFI advances grew by 24% year -on-year, led by MSE growth of 38%, Housing 
Finance growth of 27%, and Gold Loan growth of 193%.  
 Used Car and Used Commerci al Vehicle advances grew by 29% and 21% year -on-year, 
respectively.  
 CASA Ratio stands at 26%. Cost of funds increased by 7 bps to 7.12%  in Q2FY27 as 
compared to 7.05% in Q1FY27.  
      
2. Profitability & Key Ratios:  
 Net income for Q2FY27 grew by 32% year -on-year and 3% quarter -on-quarter, while 
total operating expenses grew by 20% year -on-year and 4% quarter -on-quarter.  
 The Bank reported its highest -ever quarterly PAT of ₹218 crore in Q2FY27 , up 803% 
year -on-year and 19% quarter -on-quarter.  
 Net interest margin  (NIM) for Q2FY27 stood at 6.69%, declined by  55 bps quarter -on-
quarter.  

 The cost -to-income ratio stood at 69.36%  in Q2FY27 , as compared to 68.38% in 
Q1FY27 and 75.89% in Q2FY26.  
 The cost -to-assets ratio stood at 5.49%  in Q2FY27 , as compared to 5.61% in Q1FY27  
 
3. Asset Quality  & Provisions : 
 Gross NPA improved by 13 bps quarter -on-quarter to 2.29%  in Q2FY27 as compared 
to 2.42% in Q1FY27.  
 Net NPA improved by 1 bp s quarter -on-quarter to 0.70%  in Q2FY27  as compared to  
from 0.71% in Q1FY27.  
 Provision coverage ratio remained stable at 70.08%, and at 86.73% including 
technical write -offs.  
 Net slippages improved by 4 bps quarter -on-quarter to 1.39% in Q2FY27  as 
compared to  1.43% in Q1FY27.  
 Credit cost declined to 0.94%  in Q2FY27  as compared to  1.37% in Q1FY27. Exc luding 
the ARC transaction, credit cost for Q2FY27 stood at 1.12%.  
 
4. Capital, Treasury & Liquidity:  
 
 The Bank’s net worth stood at ₹6,553 crore.  
 As on  30 September 2026, total CRAR stood at 20.92%, comprising Tier I capital of 
16.35% and Tier II capital of 4.57%.  
 The Bank’s Certificate of Deposit programme carries the highest short -term rating of 
A1+ from India Ratings, CareEdge Ratings and CRISIL.  
 Liquidity Coverage Ratio stood at 173.30% as on 30 September 2026.  
 Income from investments, including dividend income and net of mark -to market 
impact was Rs. 27 crore for the quarter  
 
About Equitas Small Finance Bank Limited [ ESFB ] 
Equitas Small Finance Bank is one of the largest small finance banks in India. As a new -age 
bank, we offer a bouquet of products and services tailored to meet the needs of our 
customers – individuals with limited access to formal financing channels, as well as affluent 
and mass affluent, Small & Medium Enterprises (SMEs) and corporates. Our firmly entrenched 
strategy focuses on providin g credit to the unbanked and underbanked micro and small 
entrepreneurs, developing products to address the growing aspirations at the ‘bottom of the 
pyramid’, fuelled by granular deposits and ‘value for money’ banking relationships.
