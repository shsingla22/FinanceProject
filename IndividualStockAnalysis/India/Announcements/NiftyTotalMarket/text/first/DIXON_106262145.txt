About Dixon Technologies (India) Limited   
 
Dixon Technologies (India) Limited is the largest* home grown design-focused and solutions company engaged in 
manufacturing products in the consumer durables, lighting and mobile phones markets in India. Their diversified 
product portfolio includes (i) consumer electronics like LED TVs; (ii) home appliances like washing machines; (iii) 
lighting products like LED bulbs and tubelights, downlighters; (iv) mobile phones; and (v) CCTV & DVRs (vi) 
Wearables (vii) Refrigerators. Dixon also provides solutions in reverse logistics i.e. repair and refurbishment services 
of LED TV panels.
