Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Commissioning of Hybrid Power Project 
 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that the Company has successfully set up a Hybrid 
Power Project for its captive consumption comprising Wind Turbine Generators with a 
capacity of 6.60 MW (2 WTG of 3.3 MW) along with Solar Power Plant with a capacity of 
3.60 MW AC. The said project is located on Revenue Survey Nos. 689 & 449 at villages of 
Mota Khijadiya & Sal Pipaliya of Taluka Paddhari, District Rajkot, Gujarat (for Wind energy 
based power) and on Survey Nos. 317, 318 & 319 of Village Dangra of Talukas Dhrol, Districts 
Jamnagar, Gujarat (for Solar energy based power). 
 
We are pleased to inform you that the Hybrid Power Plant has been successfully 
commissioned on 31 May 2025.  
 
This intimation is also being made available on the Company’s website at www.rrkabel.com .    
 
We request you to take the above on record.
