Dual Recognition for Jindal Stainless at CII National Energy Management 
Awards 2026  
• Jajpur and Hisar units recognised for excellence in energy efficiency, process innovation, and 
responsible manufacturing . 
National, September 22, 2026 : Jindal Stainless, India’s leading stainless steel manufacturer, has received dual 
recognition at the CII National Award for Excellence in Energy Management 2026 , with its Jajpur  and Hisar 
manufacturing units being honoured for their contributions to energy efficiency and sustainable operations. The 
awards recognise the company’s ongoing efforts to integrate energy -efficient technologies, process innovation, 
and resource optimisati on across its manufacturing operations.  
The Jajpur unit was conferred the ‘Excellent Energy Efficient Organization’  Award, while the Hisar unit received 
the ‘Excellent Energy -Efficient Unit’  Award at the ceremony held in Hyderabad from September 16 –18, 2026. 
Organised by the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII), the awards recognise organisations demonstrating 
excellence in energy management, conservation, innovation, and sustainable indust rial practices.  
The recognition reinforces Jindal Stainless’ commitment to embedding energy efficiency within its manufacturing 
operations and advancing its long -term decarbonisation roadmap.  
Commenting on the achievement Managing Director, Jindal Stainless, Mr Abhyuday Jindal, said,  “At Jindal 
Stainless, sustainability and operational excellence go hand in hand. The recognition of our Jajpur  and Hisar units 
at the CII National Awards reflects the commitment of our teams to responsible manufacturing, continuous 
innovation, and prudent resource utilisation. As we advance our decarbonisation journey, energy efficiency will 
remain a critical pill ar of our efforts to build a more sustainable future.”  
The Jajpur unit’s  recognition acknowledges its sustained efforts to optimise energy consumption, deploy 
resource -efficient technologies, enhance process efficiencies, and strengthen environmentally responsible 
operations. These initiatives support the unit’s efforts to imp rove operational performance while contributing to 
the company’s broader decarbonisation and sustainability objectives.  
At the Hisar unit , a key component of the evaluation was the presentation of its energy management case study 
before a distinguished jury panel. The presentation highlighted the plant’s integrated approach to energy 
efficiency through technology adoption, renewable energy integration, waste heat recovery systems, fuel 
substitution, process optimisation, and employee -led conservation initiatives. Together, these measures support 
energy conservation, greenhouse gas emission reduction, productivity enhancement, and improved re source 
efficiency.  
The dual recognition highlights Jindal Stainless’ continued focus on strengthening its manufacturing capabilities 
through operational excellence and responsible resource management. By investing in clean energy, energy -
efficient technologies, and continuou s process improvement, the company remains committed to advancing 
industrial efficiency and contributing to India’s energy transition and climate goals.

About Jindal Stainless  
India’s leading stainless steel  manufacturer, Jindal Stainless, had an annual turnover of INR 42,955 crore (USD 
4.86 billion) in FY26 and an annual melt capacity of 4.2 million tonnes. It has 16 stainless steel manufacturing 
and processing facilities in India and abroad, including in Sp ain and Indonesia, and a worldwide network in 12 
countries, as of March 2026. In India, there are ten sales offices and six service centres, as of March 2026. The 
company’s product range includes stainless steel slabs, blooms, coils, plates, sheets, precis ion strips, wire rods, 
rebars, blade steel, and coin blanks.  
 
Jindal Stainless relies on its integrated operations to enhance its cost competitiveness and operational efficiency. 
Founded in 1970, Jindal Stainless continues to be inspired by a vision for innovation and enriching lives and is 
committed to social respon sibility.  
 
Jindal Stainless remains focused on a greener and sustainable future. The company manufactures stainless steel 
using electric arc furnace, a process that significantly reduces greenhouse gas emissions and allows for 
recyclability of scrap without compromis ing on quality.
