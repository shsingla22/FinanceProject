Dear Sir/Ma’am, 
With reference to the captioned subject and in compliance with Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the 
Listing Regulations, we hereby inform you that M/s. Walker Chandiok & Co LLP, Chartered Accountants (FRN:001076N/N500013), have resigned as the Statutory Auditors of Nodwin Gaming Private Limited 
(“Nodwin”) and Absolute Sports Private Limited (“Absolute ”), material subsidiaries of the Company, with effect 
from November 14, 2024,  after issuing the Limited Review Report(s) dated November 13, 2024, on the 
unaudited financial results of Nodwin and Absolute for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2024.   The Members of the Company, at the 25
th Annual General Meeting (“AGM”) held on September 30, 2024, 
approved the appointment of M/s. M S K C & Associates, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No. 001595S) as the Statutory Auditors of the Company to hold office for a term of   5 (five) consecutive years, i.e., 
from the conclusion of the 25
th AGM till the conclusion of the 30th AGM of the Company. Accordingly, in line 
with the management’s intent to align the statutory auditors of the material subsidiaries with the Statutory 
Auditors of the Company, to streamline the audit process across the group and enhance efficiency, M/s Walker 
Chandiok & Co. LLP have resigned from their office as Statutory Auditors of Nodwin and Absolute.  
M/s. Walker Chandiok & Co LLP have not raised any concern regarding their resignation from Nodwin and Absolute. The resignation letters dated November 14, 2024, along with Annexure A, in accordance with SEBI
