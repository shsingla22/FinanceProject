The brief profile of Ms. Radhika Shah is as under:  
 Ms. Radhika Shah is a seasoned Governance and Legal Professional with over 20 years of experience. She has a proven track record of managing complex and transformative projects, across governance and legal matters including mergers & acquisitions. Throughout her 
career, she has played a pivotal role in spearheading strategic legal initiatives, ensuring 
regulatory compliance, and strengthening governance frameworks.  
 
Ms. Shah is currently the Legal Head and Company Secretary of Sanofi India Limited (Sanofi) 
where she is a part of the India Leadership Team working closely with Board of Directors and 
Country Chair & Managing Director and is instrumental in co- implementin g the India strategy. 
At Sanofi, she has been instrumental in guiding major projects including leading the demerger 
and subsequent listing of Sanofi’s Consumer Healthcare Business in India, unlocking substantial value for shareholders, and steering the reg ulatory approval process. She has also 
been at the forefront of establishing strategic partnerships with large pharmaceutical companies, including Cipla and Dr. Reddy’s. Prior to Sanofi, Ms. Shah worked with Asian 
Paints Limited where she was instrumental in adoption of best internal Corporate Policies, 
Codes and Practices including the Whistle Blower Policy, Code of Conduct for prevention of 
Insider Trading and Policy on Related Party Transactions.  
 
Ms. Radhika Shah is an Associate Member of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India and 
holds a law degree from University of Mumbai.
