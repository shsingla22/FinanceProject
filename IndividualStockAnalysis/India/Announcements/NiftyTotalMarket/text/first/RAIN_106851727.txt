 Slide 3 – Jagan Reddy Nellore  
 
Thank you, Sarang, and good day to everyone.  
 
As always, we begin with safety  – the foundation of RAIN’s culture of 
operational excellence and the basis of  everything we do.  
 
At the end of the third quarter of 2026, RAIN’s year -to-date Total 
Recordable Incident Rate, or TRIR, stood at 0.22. While this  compares 
well with leading chemical and industrial benchmarks, we hold ourselves 
to a higher standard. Having achieved  year-end TRIRs of  0.13 in 2024 
and 0.11 in 2025, our teams across the Company are fully focused on 
returning to a TRIR at or below 0.15  by the end of this year.  
 
Encouragingly, a number of  RAIN facilities around the world once again 
achieved their highest -ever number of days without a recordable incident  
– a testament to  strong local leadership, disciplined execution and the 
proactive engagement of our employees and contractors. Our leadership 
teams are focused on sustaining this momentum and on sharing these 
sites’ best practices  to drive improvement at locations  that have faced 
greater challenges.  
 
Safety is embedded in our operating philosophy and in the decisions we 
make every day, from the frontline to senior management. This approach 
reinforces a culture of personal accountability and proactive prevention,

 empowering  every employee to identify, report and address unsafe

Slide 4 – Jagan Reddy Nellore  
 
Turning to Slide 4, our results for the third quarter of 2026  mark  another 
quarter of steady progress towards normalised  earnings. Geopolitical  and 
climate -related developments continue to make our operating 
environment dynamic and uncertain , directly affecting  energy  and 
logistics  and, in turn,  our operating costs, raw material availability , and 
inbound  and outbound transportation costs . Against this backdrop, we 
continue to combine  business discipline with measured optimism, and we 
are pleased  with the improved performance RAIN delivered during  the 
quarter.  
 
Since the second quarter, global trade has continued to face  disruption , 
and the conflicts in the Persian Gulf and Ukraine  have persisted.  The third 
quarter also brought  an exceptionally  dry summer in Europe , which  raised  
logistics costs and constrained transport capacity  for RAIN and other 
companies operating  in the region. In addition, elevated  oil prices 
resulting from the Persian Gulf conflict have increased  transportation fuel 
costs  worldwide. For RAIN, the greatest impact has been on movements  
into, out of and within Europe, where fuel costs were already the highest 
across  our business portfolio.
