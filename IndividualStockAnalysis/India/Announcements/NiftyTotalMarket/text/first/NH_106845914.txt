Further to our letter dated December 12, 2025, and pursuant to Regulation 30 and Regulation 51 read 
with Regulations 37 and 59A and Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI Listing Regulations"), we wish to inform you that 
the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal, Bengaluru Bench ("NCLT"), vide its order in C.P.(CAA) 
No. 14/BB/2026 ("Order"), uploaded on the website of the NCLT today, i.e. November 4, 2026, has 
sanctioned the Scheme of Arrangement ("Scheme") between NH Integrated Care Private Limited  
("Demerged Company"), a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, and Narayana Hrudayalaya 
Limited ("Company" or "Resulting Company") and their respective shareholders and creditors under 
Sections 230 to 232 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, with the Appointed 
Date of April 1, 2025.  
 
A copy of the Order, as uploaded on the website of the NCLT, is enclosed herewith for your information 
and records. The certified copy of the Order is awaited.  
 
The Scheme provides for the demerger of the Clinical Services (as defined in the Scheme) undertaking 
of the Demerged Company, comprising 9 clinics located in Bengaluru and 1 clinic in Hosur, into the 
Company on a going-concern basis. As the Demerged Company is a wholly owned subsidiary of the 
Company, no shares shall be issued by the Company as consideration pursuant to the Scheme and, 
accordingly, there will be no change in the share capital or shareholding pattern of the Company. The 
Scheme shall become effective on the last of the dates on which the certified copy of the Order is filed 
by the Demerged Company and the Company with the Registrar of Companies ("Effective Date" as 
defined in the Scheme). The Company will inform the Stock Exchanges upon the Scheme becoming 
effective.  
 
This intimation, along with the Order, will also be available on the Company's website at 
www.narayanahealth.org . 
 
  
This is for your information and records.

submitted the report on 06.04.2026.  As per the report, the concerned 
stakeholders consented with 100% voting in favour, thereby approving 
the Scheme with the requisite majority.
