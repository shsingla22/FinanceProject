Dear Sir  / Madam,  
 
We refer to our intimation letter dated 25th September  2026 in relation to the approval for 
reduction of share capital of C&S Electric Limited, a subsidiary of Siemens Limited.  
 
1. The Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, New Delhi ( “NCLT ”) had, vide its Order 
dated 24th September 2026, approved the reduction of 343,948 equity shares having a 
face value of INR 10/ - each, in aggregate, constituting 0.78% of the total issued, 
subscribed and paid -up equity share capital of C&S Electric Limited ( “C&S”) held by 
Specified Shareholders (excluding Siemens Limited) ( “C&S Capital Reduction ”).  
 
2. Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations  and the SEBI Master Circular 
No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025 -CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026  dated 30th January 2026 (‘SEBI Master 
Circular’) and other  applicable circulars on disclosure of material events and information , 
we wish to inform you that the Registrar of Companies, New Delhi (“ROC”) has issued a 
“Certificate of Registration of Order Confirming Reduction of Share Capital”  dated  
26th October, 2026 confirming the registration of the Order dated 24th September, 2026 
and the minutes approved by the Hon’ble NCLT in accordance with Section 66(5) of the 
Companies Act, 2013.  
 
3. The percentage of shareholding of Siemens Limited prior to the C&S Capital Reduction 
was 99.22% of the paid -up equity share  capital of C&S. Pursuant to Section 66(5) of the 
Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 6(3) of the National Company Law Tribunal 
(Procedure for Reduction of Share Capital of Company) Rules, 2016 and upon issuance 
of the Certificate of Registration of Order Confirming Reduct ion of Share Capital dated  
26th October 2026  by the R OC, C&S has become a wholly owned subsidiary of  
Siemens Limited with effect from such date.   
 
4. The requisite information pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read 
with SEBI Master is enclosed herewith ( Annexure 1 ). 
 
Intimation from C&S about receipt of the said  certificate  from ROC  was received by the 
Company  at around 6.39 p.m. on 26th October  2026.  
 
Kindly take the above information on record.  
 
Yours faithfully,
