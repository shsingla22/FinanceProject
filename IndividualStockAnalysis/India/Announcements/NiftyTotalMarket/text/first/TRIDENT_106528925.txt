PRESS RELEASE  
 
S&P GLOBAL UPDATES CSA AND ESG SCORES OF TRIDENT LIMITED  
 
Ludhiana, India, February 10, 2026:  
Trident Limited is proud to announce a significant milestone in its sustainability journey, achieving an S&P 
Global CSA Score of 72—a remarkable 16-point increase over the previous year. 
Commenting on the same, Mr. Deepak Nanda, Managing Director, remarked, "Achieving this score 
recognizes our holistic commitment to sustainability. By harmonising ESG principles with our core business 
operations, we are not just building a better company, but a more sustainable future for the communities 
we serve." 
The S&P Global ESG Score measures a company’s performance on, and management of, material ESG risks, 
opportunities, and impacts informed by a combination of company disclosures, media and stakeholder 
analyses, modelling approaches, and in-depth company engagement via the S&P Global Corporate 
Sustainability Assessment (CSA).

About Trident Limited 
Trident Limited is the flagship company of Trident Group, an Indian business conglomerate and global 
player. Headquartered in Ludhiana, Punjab, Trident Limited is a vertically integrated textile (Yarn, Bath & 
Bed Linen) Paper (Wheat Straw-based) and Chemical manufacturer.  
Trident’s yarn, bath & bed linen, and paper businesses have earned global recognition and are delighting 
millions of customers across India and the world. Trident is one of the largest players in home textiles in 
India. 
Supplying national, captive, and retailer-owned brands; the organization is highly decorated with awards 
from its customers, vendors, and various government entities in recognition of advancing the highest 
standards in product quality, social responsibility, and environmental stewardship.  
The company operates in three major business segments: Textile (Yarn, Bath & Bed Linen) Paper (Wheat 
Straw-based) and Chemical, with its manufacturing facilities in Punjab and Madhya Pradesh.
