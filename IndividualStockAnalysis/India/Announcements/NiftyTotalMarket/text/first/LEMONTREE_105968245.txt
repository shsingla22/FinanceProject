Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in  
Ujjain, Madhya  Pradesh  
 
New Delhi, August 28th, 2024 : Lemon Tree Hotels is proud to announce the 
signing of its newest property, Lemon Tree Hotel, Ujjain , Madhya Pradesh . The 
property  is expected to open in FY 2027  and shall be managed by Carnation 
Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned  subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
 
Ujjain, a historic city in Madhya Pradesh, India, is celebrated for its deep 
spiritual and cultural signific ance. Known as the "Spiritual Capital of India," it is 
home to the revered Mahakaleshwar Temple, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas 
dedicated to Lord Shiva, attracting millions of pilgrims annually. Ujjain's role as a 
major pilgrimage site extends to Hindus, J ains, and Buddhists, with the city 
playing a central part in the Kumbh Mela, a grand Hindu festival held every 12 
years. Its rich blend of ancient temples, historical monuments, and vibrant 
street markets offers visitors an immersive experience into India' s spiritual and 
cultural tapestry.  
 
Lemon Tree Hotel, Ujjain , Madhya Pradesh  will feature 72 well-appointed roo ms, 
a restaurant, a  bar, a swimming pool and other public areas . The Devi Ahilyabai 
Holkar International Airport, Indore  is approximately 50 kms  away while the 
Ujjain Railway Station is 4  kms away . The hotel is well connected by roadways 
for both public and private transport.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise 
Business, Lemon Tree Hotels, remarked, “Spiritual destinations have seen a 
renewed interest and there has been a marked uptick in the number of pilgrims 
visiting places of religious and spiritual significance. B eing the “Spiritual Capital 
of India” , Ujjain  is on the must -visit list for pilgrims  and w e are thrilled to 
expand our presence in  the city .” This opening will be in addition to our four  
existing and  two upcoming hotels in the state.  
 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of the largest hotel chains in India and  
owns/leases/operates/franchises hotels across the upscale, upper -midscale, 
midscale and economy segments. LTHL delivers differentiated yet superior 
service offerings, with a compelling value proposition. The group offers seven 
brands to meet gues ts’ needs across all levels, viz. Aurika Hotels & Resorts, 
Lemon Tree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox Hotels by Lemon Tree Hotels, 
Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels and Keys 
Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels.  
 

LTHL opened its fi rst hotel with 49 rooms in May 2004. Today, the company has 
a portfolio of 160+ hotels, which includes over 110 + operational hotels, and 
more than 60 hotels set to open in India and internationally. Lemon Tree Hotels 
are located across metro regions, inclu ding the NCR, Mumbai, Kolkata, 
Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Chennai, as well as numerous tier I, II and III cities 
such as Pune, Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, Jaipur, Indore, Aurangabad, Udaipur, 
Visakhapatnam, Kochi, Ludhiana, Thiruvananthapuram, Vijayawada etc. The  
company expanded internationally with hotels opening in Dubai in December 
2019, in Bhutan in February 2020 and in Nepal in April 2024.
