19. c.  A brief on types of customers
   Triveni Turbines caters to diverse base of customers, from more than 80+ countries with wide range of IPG & API 
compliant steam turbine products that are built to suit the customer’s requirement and provide aftersales services 
which includes supply of parts & spares and servicing, overhauling & refurbishment of rotating equipment. Our 
customers base spans over broad spectrum of industries including sugar, distillery, food processing, paper, textiles, 
palm oil, energy, cement, steel, fertilizer, petrochemicals, geothermal and chemicals.
