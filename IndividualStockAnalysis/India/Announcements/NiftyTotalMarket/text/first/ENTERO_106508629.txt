With reference to the captioned subject matter, we would like to inform that Senior Drugs 
Control Officer cum Licensing Authority , Karnal Zone , has temporarily suspended the drug 
license of Atreja Healthcare Solutions  Private Limited (“ Atreja ”), a wholly owned subsidiary of 
the Company, for a period of three days from 30.01.2026 to 01.02.2026, as an administrative 
action for the contraventions of provisions of Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 read with rule 64 
and 65 (5) of Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945, while conducting its business by Atreja . 
 
The details required to be furnished in compliance with Regulation 30 read with Part A of 
Schedule III of SEBI Listing Regulations and SEBI Master Circular No.
