Mastek Appoints Amit Gajwani as Chief Operating Office r 
Mumbai, India, May 18, 2026: Mastek (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted AI -first digital engineering and cloud 
transformation partner, today announced the appointment of Amit Gajwani as Chief Operating Officer (COO), effective 
immediately. Amit succeeds Prameela Kalive, who will continue with Mastek until the end of June 2026 to ensure a 
smooth leadership transition ahead of her superannuation.  
Amit joins Mastek with over 28 years of global leadership experience in driving business growth, strategy, and large -
scale operations across diverse industry verticals. Most recently, he served as Head of Europe, UK and Nordics at LTM Limited, where he suc cessfully built the Europe business unit from the ground up into one of the company’s high -
growth strategic verticals with a strong market presence. He was responsible for end -to-end business growth, 
delivery, and client relationship management across multiple industry sectors, consistently delivering sustained 
double -digit growth through disciplined execution and expansion of key accounts.  
Prior to leading the Europe business at LTM Limited, Amit headed the Media & Entertainment and Travel, Transportation & Hospitality verticals, delivering strong portfolio growth and customer engagement across global 
markets. Earlier, as Executive Vice Pres ident at Cybage, he led global sales and business development along with 
strategic delivery portfolios and key account management, where his leadership integrated sales, delivery, strategy, and marketing functions to drive business expansion and deepen cli ent engagement. Across these leadership roles, 
Amit has built deep expertise in scaling complex businesses, driving revenue growth and operational excellence, and 
strengthening long -term client partnerships.  
Amit Ga jwani, COO, Mastek,  said “I am excited to join Mastek at such a transformative time in its growth journey. 
Mastek has built a strong reputation for delivering customer -centric innovation and business value globally. I look 
forward to working closely with the leadership team and colleagues across the organization to further strengthen 
operational excellence, deepen client partnerships, and drive sustainable growth across markets. ”  
Umang Nahata, CEO, Mastek , said “We are delighted to welcome Amit to Mastek’s leadership team at an exciting 
phase of our growth journey. Amit brings a strong combination of operational leadership, customer -centricity, and 
proven experience in scaling global businesses. His focus on growth, operational efficiency, and client relationships, 
will be instrumental as we continue to accelerate our AI -first transformation strategy. I would also like to sincerely 
thank Prameela Kalive for her outstanding leadershi p, commitment, and contribution to Mastek over the years. We 
wish both the very best for the future. ”  
Prameela Kalive  said, “It has been an incredibly fulfilling journey to be part of Mastek’s growth and transformation 
over the years. I am proud of what we have built together  - a strong culture of collaboration, customer commitment, 
 
and operational excellence that continues to position Mastek for long -term success.  I would like to thank my 
colleagues, customers, partners, and the leadership team for their trust and support throughout this journey. I am 
confident that Amit’s leadership and experience will further strengthen Mastek’s growth momentum, and I wish him 
and the entire team continued success in the years ahead. ”  
Amit and Prameela will work closely over the coming weeks to ensure a seamless transition of responsibilities.
