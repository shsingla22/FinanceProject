Mumbai, February 03  2025:  Thomas Cook (India) L imite d (TCIL), India’s leading o mni-channel travel 
services company, has announced its intention to integrate the Nature Trails brand, operational 
retreats and teams into its domestic operations subject to necessary regulatory ap provals  for a 
consideration not exceeding  Rs 60 0 mn. 
 Nature Trails -  a wholly owned subsidiary of Sterling Holiday Resorts , owns and operates  adventure 
retreats close to Mumbai  in scenic locations amidst natural surroundings ,  offering  a host of curated 
services  across multiple attractive market segments including:  
 
1. Adventure  Holidays  – with experiences such as trekking, kayaking, rafting & zip lining  
2. Educational trips  - including survival skills, self -defence , nature -appreciation  etc. for 
resilience/ad aptabilit y for students and Corpora tes  
3. Corporate  Getaways  – for Team building activities, wellness & leadership development 
programs etc.  
 
Nature Trails is popular for its adventure -driven experiences, attracting younger, activity -seeking 
travelers —an e merging and fast -growing demographic for Thomas Cook India in the domestic 
segment.  TCIL’s data trends reveal that its customers are now much younger than a decade ago and 
increasin gly seeking more curated, local & authentic  travel experiences reflecting t he young, 
aspirational demography of India and its enormous growth potential being fuelled by social media 
and real time experience sharing.  
 This strategic move reinforces Thomas Cook’s commitment to delivering memorable travel 
experiences and strengthens  its presence in India’s domestic tourism sector . Nature Tr ails operates 
on an  asse t-light model and  its resorts along with their experienced operating teams will integrate 
with uninterrupted operations  into TCIL’s services portfolio ; while harnessing the Thomas Cook 
Group’s strengths in retail and online distribution, deep B2C & B2B customer engagement and large 
marketing reach , to drive growth in the near term.  
  
Commenting on the integration , Mr. Mahesh Iyer -  Managing Director and CEO of Thomas Cook 
(India) Limited, said : "We are delighted to be able to integrate Nature Trails into our core travel 
portfolio. Adventure  and educational trave l are growing rapidly  in popularity -  particularly among the 
young and young at heart Indian customer segments.  Nature Trails perfectly aligns with Thomas 
Cook’s  vision of leading the industry in curating differentiat ed travel experiences especially in the 
rapidly growing domestic travel space .” 
 
 
 
Mr. Vikram Lalvani - Managing Director and CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts  Limited , added: "The 
integration of Nature Trails with Thomas Cook India, helps Sterling continue its sharp focus on expanding its portfolio, currently comprising of 57 full -service resorts and hotels in 48 locations  across 
the country ranging from upper -mid to upscale segments. We are confident that under the Thomas 
Cook umbrella, Nature Trails will continue to flourish and grow in the rap idly expanding domestic 
travel opportunity .”
