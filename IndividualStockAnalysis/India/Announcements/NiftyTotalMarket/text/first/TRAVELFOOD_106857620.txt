Operating Environment and Industry Dynamics  
➢ Geopolitical uncertainties, elevated airfares, changes in airline schedules and capacity rationalisation 
weighed on passenger volumes and travel patterns in the first half. This has also led to inflationary pressures 
and hampered consumer confidence. Howev er, the operating environment is expected to improve gradually 
in the second half, supported by the potential easing of geopolitical pressures and seasonally stronger travel 
demand. While the pace of recovery in passenger traffic remains a key monitorable, thes e factors should 
provide a more favourable backdrop for travel volumes.  
➢ TFS continues to navigate the evolving environment through strong execution, an efficient supply  chain,  and 
flexible manpower deployment. The Company remains focused on driving productivity across the existing 
network, ramping up recently opened locations, progressing its development pipeline and scaling new 
growth initiatives, positioning the business well  for continued growth as passenger traffic improves.  
 
Key Operational  Highlights   
➢ TFS expanded its airport presence to 22 airports  as of September 30, 202 6, on a system -wide basis (from 
18 airports as of Sep tember 30, 2025) , with start of operations at Alluri Sitarama Raju International  Airport , 
Bhogapuram.   
➢ The system -wide Travel QSR portfolio expanded to 535 outlets as of September 30, 202 6, from 464 outlets 
a year earlier, with over 70 outlets added across New Delhi, Navi Mumbai, Cochin, Noida and other key 
airports. The lounge portfolio stood at 35 lounges, compared with 37 lounges a year earlier, primarily 
reflecting closures related to terminal transitions and airport infrastructure renovations . 
➢ Our brand portfolio expanded to 156 brands as of September 30, 202 6, on a system -wide basis , with 21 in -
house, regional and international brands added over the last 12 months. The broader portfolio enhances 
our ability to curate the right brand mix across locations and address evolving traveller preferences.  
➢ Under Elite Assist Technology and Services Private Ltd. (EATS), we continue to broaden the EATS ecosystem 
by extending lounge access distribution beyond banking and card -network channels . 
➢ For the second consecuti ve year, Kyra Lounge at Hong Kong International Airport has been named Asia’s 
Leading Airport Lounge at the World Travel Awards  2026 . 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Commenting on the Q2 & H1FY 27 performance , Mr. Varun Kapur, Managing Director and CEO , TFS, said:  
‘It was one of the most challenging quarter s for passenger traffic in recent periods, amid an unusually volatile 
operating environment. Despite these headwinds, TFS' system -wide sales grew 18.8% year -on-year and the business 
continued to deliver healthy underlying profitabilit y, demonstrating the resilience of our portfolio and expanding 
network. This performance reinforces our confidence in the business and its ability to grow through periods of near -
term volatility.  
Our strategy remains focused on strengthening the core while building the next phase of growth for TFS. Over the 
last twelve months, we have mobilised more than 70 units and expanded our presence to 22 airports. Many of these 
are still ramping up, and with  a significant pipeline of outlets yet to be mobilised acros s new and existing airports, 
we have good visibility of continued growth as these operations mature. Alongside this, we continue to develop new 
growth avenues in passenger services, including EATS.  
Looking ahead, our priorities remain clear: deepen our presence at existing airports, scale operations across new and 
greenfield airports, and selectively expand beyond our core, particularly in international lounges and expressways. 
With a strong, debt -free balance sheet and an expanding network, we believe TFS is well positioned to capture the 
long -term growth opportunities across the aviation and broader travel ecosystem .’

3. Net Contract Gains represent revenue in outlets of the Company, and its Subsidiaries/JVs/Associates open for less than 12 mon ths.

4. LFL (Like -for-Like) sales growth refers to growth in revenues generated in the equivalent period of the fiscal year for Travel QSR and 
Lounge outlets opened for at least 12 months. Revenues in respect of closed outlets (other than temporary closures) are excluded 
from the calculation. LFL sales growth is calculated as revenue from Travel QSR and Lounge services in a fiscal year minus re venue from 
Travel QSR and Lounge outlets opened for less than 12 months, divided by the revenue fr om Travel QSR and Lounge services from the 
previous period minus the Revenue from Travel QSR and Lounge outlets that were closed during the equivalent period in the pre vious 
fiscal year. LFL calculations exclude revenues from management and other services.   
 
 
About the  Company:  
Travel Food Services Limited (TFS) is a leading player in the fast-growing Indian airport Travel QS R and Lounge sectors , 
with  a system -wide presence at 2 2 airports across India, Malaysia and Hong Kong. The Travel QSR business comprises 
a range of curated food and beverage concepts adapted to cater to evolving customer  demands. With a portfolio of 
156 partner and in -house brands, TFS operates 5 35 Travel QSR outlets at a system -wide level across India and 
Malaysia as of September 30, 2026. TFS has Travel QSR outlets at select highway sites in India.  
The Lounge business comprises designated areas within air port terminals, accessible primarily by first and business 
class passengers, members of airline loyalty programmes, select credit card and debit card holders and members of 
other loyalty programmes. TFS operates a network of 3 5 lounges  at a system -wide  level across India, Malaysia and 
Hong Kong as of September  30, 2026.  
TFS was founded by one of India’s leading hospitality players, K Hospitality Corp, and has partnered with UK -listed 
SSP Group plc (SSP). TFS benefits from K Hospitality Corp’s 50+ years  of expertise across the F&B industry in the 
Indian subcontinent, and SSP’s 60+ years of rich experience in the global travel retail market. TFS is transforming the 
travel experience for travellers through a variety of cuisines across its diverse formats of restaurants, cafés , bars, 
food courts and lounges.
