Sub.:  Reporting of violation by Designated Person under the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider 
Trading) Regulations, 2015 and the Code of Conduct for Prevention of Insider Trading 
of Bandhan Bank Limited (‘Bank’)  
 
This is to inform you that the Audit Committee of the Board of the Bank , at its meeting held on August 
22, 2024 , has taken a note  of an inadvertent trade by a relative of Designated Person of the Bank 
during the closure of trading window. The Portfolio Management Services availed by  Mr. Sankar 
Krishnan, Immediate Relative of Designated Person (‘ DP’), i.e., spouse of Ms. Divya Krishn an, Non -
Executive Non -Independent Director of the Bank, has traded in the equity shares of the Bank , on behalf 
of Mr. Krishnan , resulting in violation of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Prohibition of 
Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 (‘ PIT Re gulations ’) and the ‘Code of Conduct for Prevention of 
Insider Trading ’ of the Bank (‘ PIT Code ’). 
 
Based on the submission made by Ms. Krishnan, the Audit Committee of the Board of the Bank opined 
that this was an inadvertent Trade made without intent to violate the PIT Regulations and the PIT 
Code. However, considering there is a breach of the PIT Regu lations and the PIT Code, the Audit 
Committee has imposed on Ms. Krishnan, a penalty of Rs. 10,000/ -, which shall be remitted to the 
Investor Protection and Education Fund (‘IPEF’) administ ered by the SEBI  and profit on sale of such 
shares of the referred trade, if any, in future , shall be disgorged and remitted to the IPEF. 
 
You are requested to take note of the above.  
 
Thanking you.
