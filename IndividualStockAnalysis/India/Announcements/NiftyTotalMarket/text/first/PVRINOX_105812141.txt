PVR INOX EXPANDS ITS FOOTPRINT  IN KERALA WITH THE LAUNCH OF KOCHI ’S 
BIGGEST SCREEN FEATURING P[XL]  
~Opens 9-screen multiplex with 2 LUXE , 1 P[XL] and 6 prem iere auditoriums ~ 
National,  April 10, 2024 : PVR INOX, the largest and the most premium cinema exhibitor in India  has today 
announced the opening of its new  9-screen multiplex  in the city of Kochi, Kerala.  Located at Forum Mall,  
the new property sets a new benchmark of entertainment in the city featuring the first P[XL] screen format 
in Kerala  and the largest screen in Kochi City . Additionally, the cinema also features 2 LUXE audis  for a  
luxurious cinematic experience.  
With this laun ch, PVR INOX has augmented its presence in the city of Kochi with 22 screens  across  
3 properties  and consolidated its foothold in  Kerala with 42 screens in 6 properties and 558 screens 
across 99 properties in South.  
Strategically located at the second biggest mall of Kochi at Kundanoor, Maradu the cinema can 
accommodate 1489 guests and introduces PVR's premium large screen cinema format, P[XL]  for an 
enigmatic experience.  The P[XL]  auditorium   is equipped with state of the art 4K laser projection systems , 
Dolby Atmos sound  to make the movie going experience truly extra -large.  The cinema features  laser 
projection , Dolby A tmos and Real D  3D technology . The LUXE format is intended for an audience that 
desires a great and exclusive experience, with a live kitchen serving a wide variety of freshly prepared food 
& beverage for total indulgence apart from service on seat and other amenities for their comfort.  The 6-
premium auditoriums have last -row rec liners for utmost comfort .  
Commenting on the announcement, Mr. Ajay Bijli , Managing Director , PVR INOX Limited, said, “We are 
delighted to launch the first P[XL] screen in Kerala  as we look forward to presenting a  superlative movie 
going experience to our patrons. We are thrilled to announce the opening of our third multiplex  in Kochi 
and the 6th in Kerala  which is the result of the evolving demand from audiences that keeps us assertive for 
bringing innovative globa l standards of cinematic experience to every part of the country. ” 
The multiplex is designed in a contemporary style reverberating luxury, glamour and exuberance. Curated 
artworks of eminent actors and actresses with a “V” theme  raises the foyer to a cinematic gallery. Foyer 
double height area is complimented by ceiling hung artworks and chandelier elements over curvilinear 
concessionaire. Seating space with Gold metal and teal leather add elegance to the foyer area along with 
a Luxe Lounge that add to the opulence of the space . Furthermore, the cinema offers the most extensive 
cafe -style food  options in Kerala, introducing Ko chi's first authentic Italian Neapolitan pizza. This culinary 
delight, rooted in the vibrant streets of Na ples, Italy, is recognized globally as an art form in the world of 
gastronomy.  
 
 “South India holds a lot of promise for our growth plans  given the significantly high movie consumption in 
in this region and relative under penetration of multiplexes, as compared to the other parts of the country. 
With passionate audiences  and increased appetite for movie viewing , we are focus ed on accelerati ng 
growth in underpenetrated S outh  India market.  We are  are thrilled to  introduce Kochi ’s first large screen 
format with laser projection “, said Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli, Executive Director, PVR INOX Ltd.
