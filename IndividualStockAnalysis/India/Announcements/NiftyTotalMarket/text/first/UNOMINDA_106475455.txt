In continuation of our earlier communication dated May 23, 2024, we wish to inform you that Uno Minda 
Limited ("the Company") was holding 47.93% equity stake in Toyoda Gosei Uno Minda India Private 
Limited, Joint Venture Company, while its promoter group entity, Minda Investments Limited, held 1.97%. 
The joint venture partner, Toyoda Gosei Co. Ltd., held the remaining 51.10%. 
Subsequently, the Company had acquired the entire 1.97% equity stake from Minda Investments Limited. 
Pursuant to this consolidation of shareholding, the Company and the joint venture partner has entered 
into a restated Joint Venture Agreement to reflect the consolidated shareholding structure. 
Post consolidation, the Company continues to hold 49.90% equity stake, while the joint venture partner 
continues to hold 51.10% in Toyoda Gosei Uno Minda India Private Limited. Further, there is no change in 
the operations or management or control of the joint venture company pursuant to the aforesaid 
consolidation of its holding. 
The said restated JV Agreement has been executed today, i.e., December 23, 2025. 
The detailed disclosure for the aforesaid transaction, pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of SEBI 
Listing Regulations read with Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/Po02/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024, as
