Crisil Limited: Unaudited financial results 
for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 
Highlights for the quarter and half-year ended September 30, 2026: • Crisil 
a company of S&P Global 
• Income from operations up 20.2% for the quarter, and up 25. 7% for the nine months. 
• Profit before tax (PBT) up 5.3% in the quarter, and up 21.0% for the nine months. 
• Interim dividend of Rs 17 per share declared 
The Board of Directors of Crisil Limited, at its meeting today approved the unaudited financial results for 
the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. 
Crisil's consolidated income from operations for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 (03 2026), was 
up 20.2% to Rs 1,095.2 crore, compared with Rs 911.2 crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous 
year. Consolidated total income for Q32026 rose 18.2% to Rs 1,120.9 crore, compared with Rs 948.2 
crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. 
Profit before tax was up 5.3% to Rs 276.1 crore in Q3 2026, compared with Rs 262.3 crore in the 
corresponding quarter of the previous year. Profit after tax was up 5.9% to Rs 204.5 crore, compared with 
Rs 193.1 crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. 
Crisil's consolidated income from operations for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 , was up 
25.7% to Rs 3,228.2 crore, compared with Rs 2,567.4 crore in the corresponding period of the previous 
year. Consolidated total income for the nine months rose 24.8% to Rs 3,302.9 crore, compared with Rs 
2,646.9 crore in the corresponding period of the previous year. 
Profit before tax was up 21.0% to Rs 864.3 crore in the nine months, compared with Rs 714.5 crore in the 
corresponding period of the previous year. Profit after tax was up 24. 7% to Rs 654.3 crore, compared with 
Rs 524.5 crore in the corresponding period of the previous year. 
03 2026 PST earnings include a foreign exchange gain of Rs 2.3 crore as compared to a gain of Rs 15.5 
crore for the same period in 2025. 
Says Amish Mehta, Managing Director & CEO, Crisil, "Our Q3 2026 performance reflects the strength 
of our diversified portfolio and sustained growth across our domestic and international businesses. The 
resilience of the Indian economy continues to support growth opportunities, while complex global 
environment is increasing the need for our trusted insights, analytics and differentiated solutions. We 
advanced our investments in domain-led Al and the workforce of the future to deliver richer insights, 
enhance client experience and improve efficiency . As Crisil completes four decades of making markets 
function better, we remain anchored in quality, independence and trust, supporting the Viksit Bharat vision 
while serving clients across global markets." 
We raised India's GDP growth forecast for this fiscal to 7.0% from 6.6%, reflecting stronger-than-expected 
first-quarter growth and the healthy growth indications in the second quarter. Growth is expected to 
moderate in the second half as benefits of GST rationalisation fade and risks from a below-normal 
monsoon and a challenging global environment persist. 
CPI inflation rose to 5.5% in September, reflecting increases across food, fuel and core categories. RBl's 
Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) raised the repo rate by 25 bps to 5.50% and adopted a 'calibrated 
tightening' stance, retaining flexibility to either raise rates further or pause, depending on inflation trends.

Crisil Ratings maintained its leadership position backed by investor preference for best-in-class 
assessments and its revenue grew 25.6% on-year in Q3 2026. 
Corporate bond yields remained elevated, resulting in a 6.1 % on-year decline in issuance by value in the 
first nine months of 2026, despite a 35. 7% increase in Q3 2026 on a contracted base following a 31. 7%

Meanwhile, bank credit growth remained robust at 19.1 % as of August 2026 from 10.3% a year earlier, 
with credit to large corporates rising 14.9% and lending to non-banking financial companies driving 
services-sector credit growth. 
Global Analytics Center saw growth in delegation of surveillance support to S&P Global Ratings and 
expanded analytical and operational support to S&P in new areas. 
Overall, the Ratings services segment revenue grew 28.9% on-year in Q3 2026. 
Crisil Integral IQ saw traction and wins in Fundamental Research and Credit Lending Solutions . The 
business added new logos during the quarter. 
Crisil Coalition Greenwich continued to focus on deepening client engagement and saw demand in 
Corporate and Investment Banking. The revenue growth moderated during Q3 2026 following the 
accelerated renewal pipeline conversion in H1 2026. The PriceMetrix acquisition is reflected in Q3 2026 
and 9M 2026 results. 
Crisil Intelligence saw continued traction in data analytics, consulting , and credit and risk solutions. 
Research Analytics and Solutions segment revenue grew 16.5% on-year in Q3 2026. The segment profit 
for Q3 2025, includes a higher FX gain as compared to Q3 2026. 
We continued to scale our domain-led Al capabilities through the development and deployment of 
differentiated products and solutions. Such strategic efforts led to significant industry recognition-a 
analyst recognitions in Q3 2026 and taking YTD recognitions to 18, including being named a 'Category 
Leader' in Model Validation (Complex Assets) for the fifth consecutive year in Chartis RiskTech100 ® 2027 
report and 6 recognitions in Chartis RiskTech Credit Risk Management Solutions 2026. 
Franchise activity remained robust in Q3 2026. Crisil Ratings hosted the inaugural edition of India Inc 
Summit 2026, themed 'Growth and grit amidst geopolitical uncertainties ' and Ratings Round-up H1 FY27 
media webinar, themed 'India Inc buffeted, not bent by geographical crosscurrents'. 
Crisil Integral IQ hosted webinars on 'Redefining Credit Risk in Banking' and 'The Autonomy Paradox: 
How Much Rope Should We Give Al?' and published multiple thought leaderships including US private 
credit trends, FRTB IMA, global economy report and UK PRA CP9/26. 
Crisil Coalition Greenwich hosted a webinar on Al in Corporate Treasury, titled 'Breaking Barriers, Driving 
Transformation ', and published multiple thought leaderships including Corporate Treasury Resilience in 
the Middle East and Investment bank spending.

About Crisil Crisil 
a company of S&P Global 
Crisil is a global, insights-driven analytics company. Our extraordinary domain expertise and analytical rigour help clients make 
mission-critical decisions with confidence. 
Large and highly respected firms partner with us for the most reliable opinions on risk in India, and for uncovering powerful insights 
and turning risks into opportunities globally. We are integral to multiplying their opportunities and success. 
Headquartered in India, Crisil is majority owned by S&P Global. 
Founded in 1987 as India's first credit rating agency, our expertise today extends across businesses: Crisil Ratings, Crisil 
Intelligence, Crisil Coalition Greenwich and Crisil Integral IQ. 
Crisil's global workforce operates in the Americas, Asia-Pacific, Europe, Australia and the Middle East, setting the standards by 
which industries are measured.
