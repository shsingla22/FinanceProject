Pine Labs Appoints Jayaram Karthik as C hief Operations Officer  
 
Veteran business leader to shape technology -led merchant experience across markets.  
 
Mumbai , September  2, 202 6 – Global fintech platform Pine Labs today announced the appointment of 
Jayaram Karthik as its Chief Operations Officer. In this role, Jayaram will lead operations and merchant 
experiences across India and International markets, building technology -enabled op erating models that 
deliver seamless, trusted, and locally relevant journeys across the group. The appointment comes as Pine 
Labs strengthens its operational capabilities to support the next phase of growth.  
 
Jayaram brings over 25 years of experience across operations, risk, analytics and technology, with a 
proven track record of helping businesses scale while maintaining speed, trust and simplicity. He has held 
leadership positions at American Express, Citiba nk, Amazon, Tata and Razorpay , where he led large -scale 
operational transformation and strong mandates to strengthen the operating model and standardize end 
to end merchant journeys. He joins Pine Labs with a strong conviction that sustainable growth is built on 
exceptional human and technology -led experiences.  
 
Commenting on the appointment, B Amrish Rau, CEO, Pine Labs , said:  
 
"The future of fintech will be shaped not just by the products we build, but by how effectively we bring 
together technology  and operations to deliver high -quality customer experience s. Today, Pine Labs 
operate s across more than 20 countries and building a unified operational excellence model is now central 
to how we scale that footprint. Jayaram's deep expertise in streamlining complex operating processes  for 
high -growth businesses will help us strengthen the foundation required for our next phase of global 
growth."  
 
Jayaram Karthik, Chief Operations Officer , Pine Labs , said:  
 
"The next competitive advantage in fintech will come from turning complexity into simplicity for 
customers. That requires a strong combination of technology, operations and experience to work as one. 
Pine Labs has built an impressive global business, and I  look forward to helping scale the operational 
foundation that will enable the company to grow faster, standardization to serve customers better and 
build lasting trust across markets."  
 
The appointment reinforces Pine Labs' commitment to building a globally scalable operating foundation 
that powers superior experience, strengthens trust and supports sustainable growth across markets. As 
the company continues to expand internationally, it remains focused on combining innovation, 
operational excellence and regulatory rigor to serve businesses more effectively.
