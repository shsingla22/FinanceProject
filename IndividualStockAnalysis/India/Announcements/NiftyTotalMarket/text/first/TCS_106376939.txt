TCS Recognised in  Everest Group’s 2025 Elevate Honors for 
Market -Leading Growth  
 
The honour highlights TCS’ industry -leading organic growth, which saw the company mark $39.4 billion in 
Total Contract Value and $30 billion in revenues in FY25  
DALLAS  | MUMBAI , September  18, 2025:  Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS), a global 
leader in IT services, consulting, and business solutions , has been recognised in  Everest Group’s  2025 Elevate 
Honors in the category  Growth Honor of the Year  at the 2025 Everest Group - Elevate Dallas Conference.  
The award reflects TCS’ leadership in market -leading, constant -currency revenue growth among publicly  
listed global service providers with revenues over $5 billion. This recognition is a testament to TCS’ strength 
in generating organic business growth, operational performance, delivery excellence, and consistent market -
leading results, reflecting the company’s performance merit and impact across global markets . 
A partner to some of the world’s leading enterprises, TCS believes in building perpetually adaptive enterprises 
by helping them navigate today’s challenges and prepare for tomorrow’s opportunities. This core philosophy 
is driving the next -gen solutions, se rvices, products and platforms that TCS builds and innovates. The 
company recently enhanced its focus on AI by appointing a Chief AI Officer, to further scale its business, 
capabilities and talent on that front. Additionally, TCS’ comprehensive suite of se rvices in AI, data, enterprise 
solutions, digital engineering, cloud, and cyber security contributed to a company -wide total contract value 
of $39.4 billion in FY25. The company’s robust financial performance and commitment to innovation saw its 
revenues s urpass $30 billion in FY25 highlighting its leadership in technology -driven transformation.  
Ronak Doshi, Partner, Everest Group , said, “We are proud to launch the Everest Group Elevate Honors 
recognition program at Elevate in Dallas, Sept ember  2025. Elevate Honors recognitions are based on Everest 
Group’s independent, fact based, analyst -driven research. Honorees are selected based on objective, data -
driven analysis and serves as third party validation of high performance and excellence. We con gratulate TCS 
for their recognition in the  Growth Honor of the Year (annual revenue > USD 5 billion).”   
Everest Group's latest recognition builds on TCS’ recent achievements in 2025. During the year, TCS became 
the world’s second largest global IT services brand, becoming only the second company in the sector  to reach 
a brand valuation above $20 billion ($21.3 billion) according to Brand Finance. This milestone reflects an 
impressive 826% growth in brand value over the last 15 years, rising from $2.3 billion in 2010.  Fortune 
Magazine listed TCS as one pf the World’s Most Admired Companies for 2025, and Whitel ane Research 
ranked it first for customer satisfaction in Europe for the twelfth year running.  
Nikhil Shahane, Global Head - Analyst Relations, Tata Consultancy Services,  said, “We are pleased to receive 
the Everest Elevate Growth Honor of the Year 2025. This recognition reflects our unwavering commitment to 
growth, driven by our expertise in AI and Digital Transformation, and the trust our clients place in us to deliver 
positive business outcomes. At TCS, we continue to inve st in innovation, talent, and partnerships that drive 
meaningful impact across industries, to enable our customers to be perpetually adaptive enterprises.”  
Everest Group, a global research and consulting firm, is widely recognised for its in -depth analysis and 
coverage of IT Services and BPO sectors. The firm specialises in strategic sourcing, talent management, and 
digital transformation for large enterprise s. The Elevate Honors are determined through Everest Group’s 
independent, analyst -led research, with recipients chosen solely on the basis of performance excellence.

Tata  Consultancy  Services  Ltd (TCS)  
Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS) is a digital transformation and technology partner 
of choice for industry -leading organizations worldwide. Since its inception in 1968, TCS has upheld the highest 
standards of innovation, engineering excellence and customer service.  
 
Rooted in the heritage of the Tata Group, TCS is focused on creating long term value for its clients, its 
investors, its employees, and the community at large. With a highly skilled workforce of over 600,000 
employees  in 55 countries and 202 service delivery centers across the world, the company has been 
recognized as a top employer in six continents. With the ability to rapidly apply and scale new technologies, 
the company has built long term partnerships with its clients – helping them emer ge as perpetually adaptive 
enterprises. Many of these relationships have endured into decades and navigated every technology cycle, 
from mainframes in the 1970s to Artificial Intelligence today.  
 
TCS sponsors 14 of the world’s most prestigious marathons and endurance events, including the TCS New 
York City Marathon, TCS London Marathon and TCS Sydney Marathon with a focus on promoting health, 
sustainability, and community empowerment.
