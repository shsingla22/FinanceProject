Dear Madam /Sir, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (‘Listing Regulations’), we would like to inform you that, based on the recommendation of the Nomination & 
Remuneration Committee , the Board of Directors at its meeting held today  i.e. June 10, 2025 , inter alia, considered, noted and 
approved the following changes in the Key Managerial Personnel viz. Company Secretary and Compliance Officer of the Company: - 
 
i. Relinquishment of Mr. Ritesh Shah (ICSI Membership No. A14037 ), Company Secretary of the Company with effect from 
close of business hours of June 10, 2025 , and his consequent cessation as the Compliance Officer of the Company under 
Listing Regulations and also as the Key Managerial Personnel of the Company .  
 
Notwithstanding the above, Mr. Ritesh Shah will continue to serve the Company in his existing capacity as the Chief Legal 
Officer  and will remain a be part of Senior Management Personnel  of the Company .  
  
ii. Appointment of Mr. Sumeet Maheshwari  (ICSI Membership No. 15145 ), as Company Secretary of the Company and 
Compliance Officer under Listing Regulations  who would also be a Key Managerial Personnel and part of Senior Management 
Personnel with effect from June 11, 2025 .  
 
Further, Mr. Sumeet Maheshwari  (having contact details as sumeetmaheshwari@abdindia.com ) would act as one of the Key 
Managerial Personnel authorised for the purpose of determining Materiality of an Event or Information and for the purpose of making 
Disclosures to the Stock Exchange(s) under Regulation 30 of Listing Regulations in place of Mr . Ritesh Shah  from June 11, 2025 . 
The other Key Managerial Personnel authorised for the purpose of determining materiality and making disclosures under the Lis ting 
Regulations would remain unchanged.  
 
The details as required under Clause 7 of Para A of Part A of Schedule III of Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circu lar No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/ PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 11th November, 2024 are given in Annexure A.
