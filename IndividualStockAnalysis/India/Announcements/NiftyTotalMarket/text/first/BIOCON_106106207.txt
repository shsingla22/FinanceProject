NOTIFICATION TO STOCK EXCHANGE  
COMPANY STATEMENT  
 
 
Japan Health Authority (PMDA ) Approves Biocon Biologics’ 
Ustekinumab BS,  Biosimilar to J&J's Stelara®  
 
  
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, January  07, 202 5 
  
  
Biocon Biologics Ltd (BBL) , a fully integrated global biosimilars company and subsidiary of 
Biocon Ltd (BSE code: 532523, NSE: BIOCON), announced today that the Pharmaceuticals and 
Medical Devices Agency (PMDA) of Japan has approved Ustekinumab BS subcutaneous 
injection [YD], a biosimilar to the reference product, Stelara® ( Ustekinumab ). The biosimilar 
Ustekinumab has been developed and manufactured by the Company and will be 
commercialized and marketed in Japan by the Company’s exclusive commercial partner, 
Yoshindo  Inc. 
  
Ustekinumab, a  monoclonal antibody, is approved for the treatment of  Psoriasis Vulgaris 
and Psoriatic Arthritis (PsA).  
 
Biocon Biologics Ltd previously notified the Stock Exchange on Aug 29, 2024, that the 
Company had entered into a settlement and licensing agreement with Janssen Biotech Inc., 
Janssen Sciences Ireland, and Johnson & Johnson (collectively known as Janssen) to 
commercialize Ustekinumab  in Japan, upon regulatory approval.
