adjusting the calculation of EBITDA & EBITDA MarginsMumbai, November 4, 2026 – Afcons Infrastructure Limited , one of India’s large international 
infrastructure players, today announced its financial results for the quarter ended
30thSeptember 2026.
2Commenting  on the Results,  Mr. Subramanian  Krishnamurthy,  Executive  Chairman  (Whole -time  
Director)  said,  “Our  order  inflow  remained  strong  during  H1 FY27, with  new  orders  of nearly  ₹16,000 
crore  across  roads,  bridges,  metros,  tunnels  and marine  infrastructure,  of which  more  than  45% came  
from  overseas  markets . This took  our pending  order  book  to ₹43,837 crore,  excluding  L1 projects .Our 
year -to-date  order  intake  stands  at nearly  ₹19,500 crore,  providing  healthy  revenue  visibility . Supported  
by a robust  project  pipeline  and bids under  evaluation,  we remain  on track  to achieve  our full-year  order  
inflow  guidance  of ₹30,000 crore .
On the execution  front,  we have  made  progress  in addressing  project -specific  execution  challenges,  with  
land  handovers  advancing  and certain  approvals  and clearances  moving  forward . This has improved  
project  readiness  and growth  visibility  for H2. While  our H1 performance  reflected  these  constraints,  our 
focus  is now  on converting  the improved  visibility  into stronger  execution,  collections  and cash  flows .
During  the quarter,  we completed  the final  segment  of the Thilamalé  bridge  connecting  Vilimalé  Island  
with  Malé  under  the Greater  Malé  Connectivity  Project,  a significant  milestone  for the largest  
infrastructure  project  funded  by India  in an overseas  country . In Bihar,  Phase  II of the Mithapur -Sipara  
Elevated  Corridor  was inaugurated,  reducing  the expected  travel  time  between  Mithapur , Mahuli  and 
Punpun  from  approximately  one hour  to around  15 minutes . These  milestones  reflect  Afcons’  
engineering  capabilities  and its ability  to deliver  complex  infrastructure  projects  with  lasting  economic  
and social  impact .”Afcons Infrastructure Limited

3About Afcons Infrastructure Limited
Afcons  Infrastructure  Limited,  the flagship  infrastructure  engineering  and construction  company  of the 
Shapoorji  Pallonji  Group,  is one of India’s  largest  international  infrastructure  players . The Company  has a 
rich experience  of over  60 years  with  a robust  track  record  of timely  execution  of large -scale,  complex  and 
high -value  projects  in domestic  and overseas  markets . Afcons  is a well-diversified  infrastructure  
construction  company,  and it has delivered  projects  ranging  from  expressways,  underground  and elevated  
metros,  railways,  bridges,  dams,  irrigation  systems,  hydro,  water  supply,  ports,  breakwaters,  and oil & gas 
around  the world .
