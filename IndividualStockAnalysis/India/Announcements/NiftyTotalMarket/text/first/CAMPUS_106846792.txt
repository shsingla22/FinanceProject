Campus Activewear Limited Q2 and H1FY27 performance  
Sustained Double-Digit Volume Growth with Stronger Profit Growth 
New Delhi, November 5, 2026 : Campus Activewear Limited, India’s leading omnichannel sports 
and athleisure footwear brand, has announced its results for the second quarter ended September 30, 
2026.

Business update  
 Consistently delivered double-digit volume growth in Q1 and Q2FY27, despite shift in the festive 
season to Q3 this year. 
 Women’s and Kids’ segments remain key growth drivers, delivering ~29% YoY growth in 
H1FY27. 
 Forayed into performance footwear with the launch of court shoes during the quarter, 
expanding the product portfolio into new consumer use cases. 
 Achieved record production volumes during the quarter, strengthening preparedness to meet 
anticipated festive season demand. 
Commenting on the results and performance, Campus Activewear’s CEO Mr. Nikhil Aggarwal said, 
“We are pleased to report a strong performance in Q2 and H1FY27, delivering consistent double-digit 
volume growth in both Q1 and Q2, despite the festive season shifting to Q3 this year. This reflects the 
strength of the Campus brand and our continued focus on understanding evolving consumer 
preferences.  
 
Our focus on product innovation and portfolio expansion continues to strengthen our proposition 
across consumer segments. We launched 160+ new SKUs during H1FY27, reinforcing our efforts to stay 
relevant to evolving consumer needs. Our Women’s & kids’ portfolio recorded strong growth of ~29% 
YoY in H1FY27, contributing 23% to overall volumes and underscoring strong consumer acceptance. 
Premiumization trends remain encouraging, with ASP growth supported by a higher share of premium 
SKUs and strong market response to refreshed collections. Our entry into performance footwear and 
initiatives such as the Outplay by Campus Kids campaign reflect our commitment to expanding into 
new consumer segments and tapping new growth opportunities. 
 
While navigating a challenging macroeconomic environment, we remained focused on driving 
profitable growth through calibrated pricing actions, prudent sourcing, productivity improvements and 
a focus on product mix. These measures, supported by disciplined execution, remain central to 
managing cost pressures while sustaining growth momentum. 
 
Looking ahead, we remain firmly anchored to our long-term strategy of innovation, agility, and a 
consumer-first approach. As we enter the festive season, we remain focused on serving consumer

Campus Activewear Limited (NSE: CAMPUS, BSE: 543523) 
 
Campus Activewear Limited (CAL) is India’s leading sports and athleisure footwear brand by value and 
volume. Introduced in 2005, Campus offers a diverse portfolio of sports and athleisure footwear for the 
entire family, with a wide range of styles, colours and price points catering to evolving consumer 
preferences. 
The Company’s thrust on in-house manufacturing facilities, integrated supply chain, robust omnichannel 
sales and distribution network with pan-India presence, strong design capabilities accelerating swift 
product commercialization, strong brand-recall, innovative branding and marketing approach, 
digitization focus for effective distributor and retailer relationships has enabled CAL to successfully cater 
diverse consumer needs across different demographics and price points. 
CAL owns and operates 8 manufacturing facilities across India with an installed annual capacity for 
assembly of 31.9 million pairs as on September 30, 2026. The Company has over 250+ distributors 
directly servicing and fulfilling orders of over 31,000+ geographically mapped retailers in more than 850 
districts at a pan-India level as on September 30, 2026. Of these approximately 31,000 retailers, the 
internal sales force of 220+ employees have direct field coverage of approximately 17,000 retailers as 
on September 30, 2026. The remaining approximately 14,000+ retailers are covered through CAL’s 
distributor ‘feet on street’ initiative, which further depends on the Company’s distributor management 
system, sales force activation application and retailers’ engagement initiative. 
Campus has an extensive online presence across third-party marketplaces, including Flipkart, Amazon, 
AJIO and Myntra, alongside its own e-commerce website and prominent quick-commerce platforms. 
In addition, the Company continues to expand its exclusive brand outlet (EBO) network through both 
company-owned and company-operated (COCO) stores and franchise-operated stores. As of September 
30, 2026, Campus has over 315 EBOs across India.
