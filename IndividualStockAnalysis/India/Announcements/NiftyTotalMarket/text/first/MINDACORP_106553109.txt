ANNEXURE -II 
 
Minda Corporation and Turntide to form a Joint Venture to deliver advanced mobility vehicle 
powertrain solutions for the rapidly growing Indian market  
[Collaboration aims to develop & manufacture advanced EV and hybrid power train solution s, supporting India’s 
rapid shift to sustainable mobility]  
  
New Delhi, India  – Mar. 09, 2026:  Minda Corporation Limited ( “Minda Corp ” or the “Company ”; NSE: 
MINDACORP, BSE: 538962), the flagship company of Spark Minda, today announced a strategic joint venture 
agreement with Turntide Technologies  (Turntide) , subject to  the necessary regulatory approvals. Under the joint 
venture, Minda Corp through its wholly owned SPV  will hold 49% stake , and Turntide will hold 51%. This venture 
will bring new generation powertrain  solutions for the electric vehicle industry.  
  
The Joint Venture Company will focus on the development and manufacturing of advanced new ge neration high 
and low -voltage motor controllers, high-voltage and high -performance electric motors , pumps for thermal 
applications, and other customised controllers tailored primarily for India’s growing EV segment.  
  
This partnership aims to bring Turntide’s advanced global technology closer to the Indian market and deliver  
localized, premium products suited to domestic requirements.  
India’s EV market is expanding rapidly, driven by government initiatives and growing sustainable transportation 
solutions demand. The joint venture will leverage the combined strengths of Minda Corp and Turntide to accelerate  
the development cycles and strengthen the local supplier  ecosystem for electric vehicles. The new venture aims to 
meet OEMs stringent performance and quality requirements while delivering cost advantages through local 
manufacturing and cutting -edge  solutions.  
Mr. Ashok Minda, Chairman and Group CEO of Minda Corporation Limited , commented: “This partnership 
with Turntide marks a significant milestone in our journey towards electrification. By combining Turntide’s 
globally proven technology with Minda Corporation Limited ’s strong local presence, we aim to deliver advanced, 
high-quality , and cost-competitive EV powertrain solutions that support India’s transition to sustainable mobility. 
This will result to meet ‘Make in India’ objectives  and enhance the resilience of domestic supply chain of EV 
ecosystem .” 
Mr. Steve Hornyak, CEO of Turntide Technologies,  commented: “India’s electrification growth story is 
central to Turntide’s strategy, and this joint venture is a key step in bringing our powertrain technologies closer 
to customers in the market. We’re proud to partner with Minda Corporation, a fantastic team with de cades of 
experience serving global OEMs, as we deliver localized, high -performance solutions for India’s rapidly evolving 
mobility ecosystem.”  
About Turntide Technologies :  
Turntide Technologies designs and manufactures best -in-class electric motors, power  electronics, energy storage, 
and thermal equipment for anything that moves.  
  
Turntide Technologies operates in North America, the U.K., and India and serves customers in global markets and 
industries including off highway automotive, commercial vehicles,  rail, marine, light vehicles, and premium 
automotive.
