JBM Electric Vehicles launches Next Generation ‘GALAXY’ Electric

o Offers the highest seating capacity in its segment paired with highest luggage space 
e JBM ‘GALAXY’ platform has successfully demonstrated operations of over 900 kms per

e JBM EV continues to hold the highest market share in the Indian electric luxury coach

Ahmedabad/Gandhinagar, July 10, 2026: JBM Electric Vehicles (JBM EV), a global 
leader in e-mobility and EV Ecosystem Solutions launched the next generation 
‘GALAXY’ electric luxury coach at Prawaas 5.0 yesterday. The vehicle was launched 
by Shri Nitin Gadkari, Hon’ble Union Minister of Transport & Highways in the 
august presence of Shri Nishant Arya, Chairman, JBM Electric Vehicles. JBM EV 
is part of JBM Auto, the flagship company of the $3.3 bn global Indian conglomerate, 
JBM Group. 
The new ‘GALAXY’ Coach showcases 22 Maharaja reclining seats + 20 plush sleeper 
berths with an onboard washroom. JBM Galaxy is also available in another seat 
configuration offering 24 Maharaja reclining seats + 20 plush sleeper berths (without 
washroom). It also offers the highest seating capacity in its segment paired with 
highest luggage space, a combination designed to strengthen operator revenue 
potential and passenger convenience on longer routes. With this launch, JBM 
GALAXY coach will be available in 3 variants i.e. seater, sleeper & seater and sleeper. 
Speaking on the occasion, Shri Nishant Arya, Chairman, JBM Electric Vehicles, 
said, “The next generation GALAXY has been developed to meet the evolving 
expectations of premium inter-city travel with a shamer focus on luxury, utility and 
business efficiency. It reflects our commitment to creating electric mobility solutions 
that are sophisticated in design, practical in operation and aligned with the future of 
sustainable transportation.” 
Styled with a European-inspired design language, the coach features class leading 
luxurious interiors, panoramic windows, individual AC louvers and reading lights, USB 
charging sockets, generous leg and head room, etc. setting new benchmarks in 
passenger comfort. The vehicle comes with a robust and comprehensive safety and 
performance package with an ADAS-enabled architecture, EBS and ABS, hill-hold, live 
surveillance cameras, emergency exits, etc. to ensure uncompromised passenger 
safety on long-distance intercity travel. 
JBM GALAXY comes with kneeling and height adjustment feature for easier boarding 
alongwith electronically controlled air suspension. It is powered by high-efficiency 
modular battery packs paired with fast charging and regenerative braking. The overall 
package is aimed at delivering a seamless long-distance travel experience to the 
passengers.
