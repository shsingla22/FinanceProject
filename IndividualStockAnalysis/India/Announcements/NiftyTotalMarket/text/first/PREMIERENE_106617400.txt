        Subject: Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations- Update on – Acquisition 
of 51% equity stake in Ksolare Energy Private Limited in Joint venture with Syrma SGS 
Technology Limited 
 
        This is with reference to our earlier disclosures dated 23rd October, 2025 regarding the proposed joint 
venture between Premier Energies Limited (“Company”) and Syrma SGS Technology Limited 
(“Syrma SGS”) for the purpose of acquiring K-Solare Energy Private Limited (“K-Solare”). 
  
        In this regard, we wish to inform that, after mutual discussions and evaluation of evolving strategic 
considerations, the Company and Syrma SGS have decided not to proceed further with the proposed 
acquisition of K-Solare. The parties have agreed to terminate the discussions with K-solare amicably, 
and there are no material financial implications arising from the same. 
  
        The Company remains committed to solar inverter and renewable energy electronics segment and is 
evaluating alternative approaches to develop this business. 
  
       This disclosure is being made in compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.
