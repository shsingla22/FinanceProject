Sub: Board Comments on fine levied by the Exchanges 
 
Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
In continuation of our reply dated 22nd August, 2024 to the notices dated 21st August, 2024 of 
National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and BSE Limited (BSE) regarding levy of fines due 
to non-compliance with the provisions of Regulation 17(1) of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 
i.e., half of the Board was not independent, for the quarter ended 30th June, 2024, it is to inform that 
such non-compliance, imposition of fines by the Stock Exchanges and replies thereto by the 
Company which is mentioned as below were duly informed & noted by the Board of Directors at its 
meeting held on 7th November, 2024:  
 
“IRCON being a government company as defined under provisions of Section 2(45) of the 
Companies Act 2013, as per the Articles of Association of IRCON, the President of India is vested 
with the power to appoint Directors (including Independent Directors/Women Director) on the Board 
of the Company and accordingly all Directors in IRCON are appointed by the Government of India 
through its Administrative Ministry, Ministry of Railways (MoR); hence, IRCON has no role to play in 
the appointment of any Director (including Independent Director / Woman Director) unless 
nominated by the Government. Further, requests have already been made from time to time to the 
Administrative Ministry i.e. Ministry of Railways for appointment of requisite number of Independent 
Directors on the Board of the Company in order to ensure all compliances pertaining to Composition 
of Board. However, due to not having requisite number of Independent Directors on the Board, half 
of the Board is not Independent and it was requested that levy of fine is not applicable as the 
Company has no role to play in it.”
