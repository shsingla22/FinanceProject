SUB :  SJVN  achieves  Commercial Operation of 45 MW out of 50 MW Sonitpur Solar 
Power Project in Assam  
 
Sir/Madam,  
 
This is to inform that SJVN Green Energy Limited (SGEL), a wholly owned subsidiary of 
SJVN Limited  has declared the Commercial Operation of 45 MW out of the 50 MW Solar 
Power Project situated at Sonitpur District, Assam . 
 
SGEL signed a Power Purchase Agreement with Assam Power Distribution Company Limited 
(APDCL) on 07.02.2024 for the supply of 50 MW of solar power. Power from the Project is 
being supplied to APDCL under this Agreement.  
 
The Trial Run Certificate for the Project was issued by APDCL  vide letter dated 21.11.2026, 
and the Project achieved commercial operation on 21.11.2026.  
 
Kindly take the above information on record and oblige.
