HCLTech launches AI -Powered Operations for mainframe environments  
 
NEW YORK  and  NOIDA, India, Nov . 2, 2026  — HCL Tech , a leading global technology company, 
today announced the launch of its AI -Powered Operations offering, designed to help 
enterprises modernize and optimize their mission -critical mainframe environments  through 
AI-enabled automation, observability and operational intelligence.  
 
The offering brings together specialized mainframe skill agents and conversational AI 
assistants to support system administration, observability, optimization and workflows , under 
human oversight. These capabilities help improve efficiency, accelerate issue resolution and 
enable more proactive, insight -driven operations. HCLTech's dedicated Mainframe Center of 
Excellence underpins the offering with deep domain expertise, indu stry best practices and 
continued innovation.  
 
"Enterprises running mission -critical workloads on mainframes are under increasing pressure 
to modernize operations and drive greater efficiency , without compromising reliability or 
control . Our AI -Powered Operations offering directly addresses this business need, combining 
HCLTech's deep mainframe expertise with AI -driven capabilities to help clients simplify 
operations, reduce risk and unlock greater value from their mainframe investments as  they 
advance their broader transformation agendas," said Rampal Singh, Senior Vice President and 
Global Business Head of Hybrid Cloud at HCLTech.  
 
"AI is fundamentally changing how enterprises manage and extract value from their most 
strategic technology platforms. Through our dedicated Mainframe Center of Excellence and 
AI innovation capabilities, we are embedding intelligent agents and operational intelligence 
directly into mainframe environments, enabling organizations to shift from reactive 
management to proactive, insight -driven operations, while preserving the security, resilience 
and governance these platforms demand," said Raghu Kidambi, Corpo rate Vice President and 
Head of AI Ecosystems  at HCLTech.
