In reference to your email/letter dated 01st July 2024 seeking Clarification/ Confirmation on news

has no role in the decision relating to merger and as of now, Bank is not in receip t of any 
information  from Government of India about merger.  Hence, the abovementioned news item is 
factually incorrect.  We provide our point wise responses to the query raised by you as under  :
