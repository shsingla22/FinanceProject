 In the current quarter, stand -alone underlying volume growth was in the mid -teens at 
15.2% whereas the Consumer and Bazaar businesses had an underlying volume growth 
of 12.7%, and we saw a very strong bounce -back in the B2B underlying volume growth 
at 25.2%.  
 Gross margins in the quarter were higher than the fourth quarter of last year by 690 
basis points, largely led by softening in commodity costs. VAM consumption was at 
$925 a ton versus $1,300 in the fourth quarter last year. We also, as mentioned earlier,

would notice that the fourth quarter revenues were nearly INR 250 crores lower than 
the third quarter, and that had some kind of a negative operating leverage. And as a 
result, our EBITDA margins for the quarter was 20.5%. However, compared to same 
period last year, it was nearly 200 basis points higher.  
 In the current quarter also, we took a onetime impairment charge on account of one of 
our manufacturing facilities at Mahad, and that is why the depreciation and 
amortization charge is higher. This onetime impairment charge is about INR 20 crores.
