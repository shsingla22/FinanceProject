In compliance of Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform the following:  
NLC India Limited (NLCIL) participated in the Tranche–V auction of Critical and Strategic 
Minerals conducted by the Ministry of Mines and emerged as the Preferred Bidder for 
Semhardih Phosphorite & Limestone Block and Raipura Phosphorite & Limestone Block, 
Balod District, Chhattisgarh on 27.05.2025. Subsequently, NLCIL has submitted 
Performance Securities for both the blocks to the Government of Chhattisgarh on 10.06.2025. 
The Letter of Intent (LoI) for Composite Licence from the State Government of Chhattisgarh 
issued on 02.09.2025 for both the blocks. NLCIL has prepared and submitted the Scheme of 
Prospecting to the Director of Geology and Mining, Govt. of Chhattisgarh on 30.03.2026.  
In terms of Regulation 30 & 51 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations,2015 (LODR), a composite licence has been granted for 
Semhardih Phosphorite & Limestone Block and Raipura Phosphorite & Limestone Block vide 
letter Nos. LAND-4401/29/2025-MRD and LAND-4401/30/2025-MRD dated 24.04.2026 
respectively by Mineral Resources Department, Govt. of Chhattisgarh to carry out detailed 
geological exploration  in the blocks.
