Bhoomi Poojan Ceremony at our New and Largest Upcoming  Manufacturing 
Site in Dahej, Gujarat  
 
 
Mumbai , 13th May 2024: Laxmi Organic Industries Limited (LOIL) is pleased to  inform that Bhoomi 
Poojan ceremony was done at our new and largest upcoming manufacturing  site at Dahej, Village Jolve 
and Vadadala, Bharuch, Gujarat  on the auspicious day of Akshaya Tritiya  on 10th May 2024.  The 
expansion at Dahej will add to the capacity of the  existing units and provide business continuity to 
customers.  
 
This state -of-the-art facility will have the best -in-class facilities for E ssential  & Specialty  products and 
will be equivalently equipped with safety measures. With this new upcoming site, we plan to increase 
our capacities and new product offerings going forward.  
 
 
Commenting on the same, Dr Rajan Venkatesh, CEO of Laxmi Organic said,  “We are excited to 
announce that on the auspicious occasion of Akshaya Tritiya, we conducted the Bhoomi Poojan at 
LOIL's new and largest upcoming site in Dahej, Gujarat. This project will offer our customers a wide 
range of product portfolio encompassing E ssentials and Specialties chemicals. Leveraging our robust 
track record, enduring customer relationships, ongoing R&D initiatives for product innovation and 
substantial room for expansi on in Dahej, we are optimistic of outperforming the industry growth.  
 
At this new site Dahej we remain ‘Geared to Win’ and ‘Geared for Growth’ to support our customers 
ambitions.  
 
Lastly, a big shout out to the LOIL projects and Dahej  site team for wonderfully orchestrating the 
same.”  
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
About Laxmi Organic Industries  Limited (L OIL) 
 
Laxmi Organic Industries Limited is a global supplier of Essentials and Specialty chemicals with a 
presence  in over 52 countries and serving the needs of regional and global customers. The Company 
has leading  market share in India and globally across product categories. It has three existing 
manufacturing sites in  Mahad, Maharashtra; an upcoming site in Lote, Maharashtra and an expansion 
is planned at the 84 acres  brownfield site in Dahej, Gujarat.
