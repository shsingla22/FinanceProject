Emami Unveils its New & Refreshed Corporate Identity  
To Reflect Evolving Vision and Global Ambition s 
Group businesses to sport new  distinct  name styles  
 
 
Kolkata, Ju ly 30, 2025  
 
Emami is  excited to announce the launch of its new & refreshed corporate brand identity, 
a significant milestone in its evolution and completion of 50 years.  The new corporate 
brand identity honours  Emami’s rich legacy  deeply committed to the entrepreneurial 
spirit and values of the Founders, Mr RS Agarwal and Mr RS Goenka, and at the same time  
staying firmly attuned to the evolving needs of modern consumers embrac ing a new era 
driven by a clear vision of progression and growth.  
 
"Our rebranding marks a pivotal step in Emami's evolution," said  Mr Harsha Vardhan 
Agarwal, Vice Chairman & Managing Director, Emami Limited. “Our new core  
corporate  identity reflects who we are today — an organization rooted in heritage but 
powered by innovation, diversification and a global outlook. It is a symbol of the journey we have made, and the exciting path ahead.  We believe this refreshed corporate identity 
will strengthen our market position and foster deeper connections with our consumers 
and partners, as we continue to deliver high-quality, value -driven, and innovative 
offerings across our businesses."     The new corporate identity retains strong visual links to its former design — a deliberate 
decision that highlights Emami ’s deep -rooted heritage and values, even as it moves 
forward with confidence.  The  iconic ellipse has gracefully evolved into a sphere , 
representing the brand’s expanding global footprint, adaptability  and future -ready 
mindset. At the heart of the design, the stylized ‘e’  captures the essence of innovation 
and reinvention that has propelled the Organization’s success over five decades , while 
also symbolizing the continuous growth and dynamism that define its forward-looking spirit.  
 
The signature  colour palette remains, ensuring continuity and brand recognition across 
Emami’s diverse businesses, while simultaneously embracing a more modern, 
progressive aesthetic.  A refined typeface in the new wordmark  conveys confidence, 
boldness  and adaptability —  qualities that perfectly mirror Emami’s vision for the future.     
   
 
 
 This rebranding includes each of Emami’s diverse businesses adopting a modern 
typeface and a distinctive colour  derived from the new sphere, with each entity 
establishing a uniquely styled name and visual identity . 
 The rollout of the new corporate identity is expected to be completed a cross  Emami’s 
business entities  over the next couple of month s.
