   Press Release  
 
 
India moving from a luxury -consuming nation to a luxury -creating one: Rampur 
Jugalbandi's Grand Finale Makes Its First India Appearance  
Final two expressions of the eight -part single malt series limited to 240 bottles  
New Delhi, November 25, 2026  – Radico Khaitan Limited, one of India’s largest alcobev players, is 
strengthening its presence in the luxury spirits segment with the launch of Rampur Jugalbandi #7 and 
#8 India Editions, the final two expressions of its acclaimed single malt series. Limite d to just 240 
bottles, priced at ₹50,000 (Telangana) each and bottled at 50% ABV, the exclusive edition marks the 
first and likely last time the Jugalbandi series will be launched in the domestic market. It will be 
available across Karnataka, Haryana, Utta r Pradesh, Maharashtra and Telangana.  
The launch comes as Radico Khaitan steps up its efforts to build a stronger presence in India's luxury 
spirits market. The Company's luxury and semi -luxury portfolio has more than tripled in sales 
value between FY23 and FY26. During FY26, the Company reported sales of ₹475 crore  and is 
targeting around 25% growth in th is segment in FY27.  
The portfolio includes Rampur Indian Single Malt, Jaisalmer Indian Craft Gin, Sangam World Malt, 
Virasat Indian Single Malt and Kohinoor Reserve Indian Dark Rum. At a broader level, the Company's 
Prestige & Above portfolio grew 35.8% year -on-year in volume  to 5.28 million cases in Q2 FY27, 
contributing 77.1% of its total IMFL sales value.  
Commenting on the launch, Abhishek Khaitan, Managing Director, Radico Khaitan , said, “Our 
ambition is to build Indian spirits brands recognised not just for their quality, but also for their 
craftsmanship, provenance and ability to create a distinctive experience for consumers. Luxury is built 
over time through consistency, character and a  clear point of view. With Rampur Jugalbandi, we are 
bringing together rare cask expressions and the craft of Indian single malt -making in an edition created 
for c ollectors. This is an important expression of our belief that Indian luxury spirits can command a 
place among the most admired names in the world.”  
The two expressions, Rampur Jugalbandi #7 (Mizunara Cask) and #8 (PX Sherry Cask), represent the 
culmination of the eight -part Jugalbandi series. Both were first unveiled at TFWA Cannes in 2025 for 
global markets and now make their India debut in this limi ted edition.  
Jugalbandi  #7 brings together ex -Bourbon and rare Japanese Mizunara oak cask maturation, while 
Jugalbandi  #8 combines ex -Bourbon with Pedro Ximénez (PX) sherry cask maturation. The two 
expressions explore contrasting cask influences, bringing together the character of Indian single malt 
with distinctive influences from Japanese oak and Spanish sherry casks.  
With only 240 bottles allocated for the Indian market, the India Edition has been conceived as a 
collector’s release rather than a conventional market launch. The 50% ABV bottling also brings the 
expressions to Indian consumers at a higher strength, offeri ng a distinct presentation of the final two 
chapters of the series.  
Through Rampur Indian Single Malt and its wider luxury portfolio, Radico Khaitan is seeking to 
contribute to the growing recognition of Indian spirits in premium and luxury markets, while building 
brands with a distinctive identity and global relevance.
