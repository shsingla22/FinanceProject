Collaboration framework between  the Petroleum Technology Research Centre  (PTRC), 
Canada and Oil India Limited  (OIL)  
  
 
  
To str engthen the friendly relations between Canada and India and acknowledging the 
importance of cooperation in addressing global challenges related to energy transition and 
sustainability;  a collaboration framework was signed between  the Petroleum Technology 
Research Centre  (PTRC), a not -for-profit energy research company of the Province of 
Saskatchewan of Canada and Oil India Limited  (OIL)  a Maharatna CPSE under the aegis of 
Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas, Govt of India, in the  fields of CO 2 Capture, Utilization and 
Storage (CCUS ), Geothermal, development  of Clean Energy  Technologies  and research 
interests with mc2+ for start ups, a platform created under the aegis of Ministry of Petroleum & 
Natural Gas, Government of India , on the sidelines of the Global Energy Show 2026 on 10th 
June 2026 at Calgary.  
 
The event was attended by The Premier of Saskatchewan Hon’ble Scott Moe, His 
Excellency the High Commissioner of India to Canada,  CEO of PTRC , CMD of OIL , and other 
senior officials. The domains of collaborations include the following:   
 

a. Utilization of captured CO 2, both related to permanent storage  through geologic al 
sequestration and mineralization in geologic al formations .  
b. Innovative enhanced oil recovery (EOR) methods  to help lessen the environmental 
impacts and energy use of oil/gas production . 
c. Working with established networks and partners specific to each of OIL and PTRC to 
identify business opportunities in both countries.  
d. Identification of potential geothermal projects of mutual interest  to study and participate .  
e. Collaborative research by both OIL and PTRC's Energy Innovation Hub lab on projects 
involving subsurface energy production  and sub surface storage . 
f. Collaborative research interests with mc2+ for start ups , a platform created under the 
aegis of Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas, Government of India .
