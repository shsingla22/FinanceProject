1. Investment of up to INR 50,00,00,000 (Indian Rupees Fifty Crores ) in Retail Pulse Labs Private Limited ("RPLPL")  
In continuation to our earlier intimation dated June 12, 2026, regarding the proposed acquisition of Kirana Club Pte. Ltd. 
("Kirana Club ") and its subsidiary RPLPL , approved making an investment of up to INR 50 ,00,00,000 (Indian Rupees 
Fifty Crores ) in RPLPL.  This is subject to completion of the first tranche of the said acquisition, upon which RPLPL will 
become a step -down subsidiary of the Company.  
 
The proposed investment will be made in one or more tranches within a period of 1 (one) year from the date of closing of 
first tranche of the original acquisition  disclosed on June 12, 2026 , by way of subscription to, or purchase of, equity or 
equity linked instruments of RPLPL , or any other permissible securities/instruments, in accordance with applicable laws 
and definitive agreements. The specific instrument(s), price and number of securities will be determined at the time of 
each tranche, and the Company will make appropriate disclosures in accordance with the SEBI Listing Regulations.

alignments  and operational modifications in relation to the transaction.  The overall aggregate consideration amounting to 
INR 2,02,08,52,202.40 (Rupees Two Hundred Two Crores Eight Lakhs Fifty -Two Thousand Two Hundred Two and Forty 
Paise Only) and the scope of the proposed acquisition remain unchanged from the terms previously disclosed.
