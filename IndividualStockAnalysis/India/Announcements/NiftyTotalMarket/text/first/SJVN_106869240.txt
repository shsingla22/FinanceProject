PRESS RELEASE  
 
Hon’ble Prime Ministers of India and Nepal Jointly Lay Foundation Stone of SJVN’s 
669 MW Lower Arun Hydro Electric Project  
 
Landmark ₹5,792 Crore Hydropower Project to Strengthen India –Nepal Energy Partnership 
and Accelerate Clean Energy Development  
 
In a significant milestone towards strengthening bilateral cooperation in energy sector, Hon’ble 
Prime Minister of India , Shri Narendra Modi and Hon’ble Prime Minister of Nepal , Shri 
Balendra Shah jointly  laid the Foundation Stone (Shilanyas) of SJVN's 669 MW Lower Arun 
Hydro Electric Project in Nepal through a virtual ceremony held from New Delhi.  
 
Shri Bhupender Gupta, Chairman & Managing Director, SJVN, expressed his heartfelt 
gratitude to the Hon’ble Prime Minister of India, Shri Narendra Modi, for his continued 
guidance and blessings and to the Hon’ble Prime Minister of Nepal, Shri Balendra Shah,  for 
reposing confidence in SJVN as a development partner. He also extended his sincere thanks  to 
the Ministry of Power, Ministry of External Affairs and other key Ministries of the 
Governments of India and Nepal for their support and cooperation in expediting the requisite 
clearances and approvals for the Project.  
 
On this occasion, an event was organised at Lower Arun Project site attended by project 
employees, Representatives from Ministry of Energy, Govt of Nepal, Investment Board of 
Nepal, District Coordination Committee, Leaders of local government - Mayor and Ga on Palika 
Pramukh , wards, District Forest department, Community Forest User Groups and local 
community. SJVN Management and employees witnessed the occasion virtually through live 
streaming.  
 
The 669 MW Lower Arun Hydro Electric Project  is being developed on Arun River in 
Sankhuwasabha and Bhojpur districts of eastern Nepal by SJVN Lower Arun Power 
Development Company Pvt. Ltd. (SLPDC), a wholly owned subsidiary of SJVN.  The 
estimated cost of the Project is ₹ 5,792.36 crore, with a tentative tariff of ₹ 4.99 per unit. The 
project is envisaged to generate approximately 2,901 million units of energy  annually . 
 
The distinctive salient feature of Lower Arun Project is its integration with SJVN's 900 MW 
Arun -3 Hydro Electric Project, located upstrea m on same river . Both projects are designed to 
operate under Tandem Operation System, wherein water discharged from Arun -3 Hydro 
Electric Project  will be utilised for energy  generation at Lower Arun  Hydro Electric Project . 
This arrangement will ensure optimum utilisation of available water resources, enabling 
additional renewable energy  generation without constructing a separate storage reservoir.  
 
The project comprises four generating units of 167.25 MW each and includes 17.4 km long 
Head Race Tunnel  and Surface Power house. The project is being developed on Build, Own, 
Operate and Transfer (BOOT) basis and is targeted for commissioning during financial year  
2030 -31. Major construction packages have already been awarded and construction activities 
are underway.

The Lower Arun Hydro Electric Project is expected to deliver substantial long -term economic 
and social benefits. Under Project Development Agreement, 21% of the generated energy will 
be provided free of cost to Government of Nepal, strengthening the country’s  energy security. 
The project will also contribute to Nepal's revenue through applicable taxes and royalties, 
besides supporting foreign exchange earnings through cross -border energy trade.  
 
The Project is expected to create significant direct and indirect employment opportunities 
during its construction and operational phases. The development activities will stimulate local 
economic growth by generating business opportunities in Sankhuwasabha and Bhojpur 
districts.  
 
The project will also contribute to the socio -economic development of the region through local 
area development initiatives, including the construction and upgradation of schools, healthcare 
facilities, drinking water supply systems and other essential com munity infrastructure. These 
initiatives are expected to enhance the quality of life of local communities while promoting 
entrepreneurship and regional economic development.  
 
The Government of Nepal awarded the 669 MW Lower Arun Hydro Electric Project  to SJVN 
through international competitive bidding on 29th January 2021. Subsequently, a Memorandum 
of Understanding was signed with Investment Board Nepal on 11th July 2021. The Project 
Development Agreement was signed on 1st June 2023 in the presence of Hon’ble Prime 
Ministers of India and Nepal . 
 
SJVN Limited, a Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise under Ministry of Power, 
Government of India, is a joint venture of Government of India and Government of Himachal 
Pradesh. Beginning its journey with 1500 MW Nathpa Jhakri Hydro Power Station, SJVN  has 
diversified its portfolio across hydro, thermal, solar, wind and power transmission sectors. 
Through its growing presence in Nepal, SJVN continues to play an important role in 
strengthening regional energy cooperation.
