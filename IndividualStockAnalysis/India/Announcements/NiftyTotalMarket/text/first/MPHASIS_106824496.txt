Mphasis ’ Near -Term and Net -Zero Emissions Reduction Targets Receive Validation 
from  Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) Services  
   
 
Bengaluru, October 22, 202 6 
 
Mphasis , (BSE: 526299 ; NSE: MPHASIS ), a global AI -led, platform -driven technology solutions provider , today announced 
the official validation of its near -term and net -zero greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions reduction targets by SBTi Services.  
This marks an important milestone in Mphasis’ commitment to addressing climate change through science -based 
emissions reduction targets.  
Mphasis aims to reduce emissions across its operations and value chain, with defined goals for both near -term and long -
term reductions  with FY 2024 -25 as baseline year . These targets cover Scope 1 and  Scope  2 emissions, as well as Scope 3 
emissions across relevant activities in its value chain, establishing measurable milestones towards the company’s net -zero 
ambition.   
Mphasis’ validated targets include:  
• Net-Zero Target : To achieve net -zero greenhouse gas emissions  across its value chain by FY  2050  
• Near -Term Targets:   
o To reduce absolute Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG emissions  by 66.01% by FY  2036 from FY 2025 base year  
o To reduce Scope 3 GHG  emissions from purchased goods and services, capital goods, fuel - and energy -
related activities, business travel, employee commuting and upstream leased assets by 66.33% per USD 
value added within the same timeframe.  
• Long -Term Targets :  
o To reduce absolute Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions by 90 .0% by FY 2050 from a baseline year FY 2025  
o To reduce Scope 3 GHG emissions by 97 .0% per unit value added (economic intensity)  within the same 
timeframe.  
 
“We believe climate action requires collective commitment from organizations, and it remains an important priority for 
us. The validation of our targets by SBTi Services is a significant milestone in our sustainability journey and strengthens o ur 
resolve to  embed environmental responsibility into the way we operate, grow and build capabilities, while steadily 
advancing our long -term climate commitments ,” said Deepa Nagraj, Senior Vice President & Global Head - ESG, Sparkle 
Innovation Ecosystem, and Communications, Mphasis.  
The validation of Mphasis’ targets supports its ambition to achieve net -zero greenhouse gas emissions across its value 
chain by FY  2050. The company continues to pursue its commitment to achieve carbon neutrality across its facilities and 
operations by FY 2030, with renewable sources already meeting 57.82% of its total energy needs.  
Mphasis’ sustainability efforts have also received external recognition, including a Silver Medal from EcoVadis, placing it 
among the top 15% of assessed companies, and an improved CRISIL Core ESG score of 73 in FY  2026, up from 69 in the 
previous assessment. The company remains focused on strengthening its environmental, social and governance practices 
alongside its business priorities .

About SBTi  
The Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) is a global organization that helps companies set 
greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions -reduction targets aligned with climate science and the Paris Agreement. It provides standards and 
criteria for establishing near -term and long -term net -zero targets. SBTi covers Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 emissions across a  
company’s operations and value chain. It validates corporate emissions -reduction targets against its applicable criteria. SBTi enables 
companies to develop credi ble decarbonization strategies and contribute to global climate change mitigation. 
https://sciencebasedtargets.org/
