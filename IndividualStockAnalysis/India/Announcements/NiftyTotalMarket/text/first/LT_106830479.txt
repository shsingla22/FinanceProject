Infrastructure  & Utilities  Segment  
 
The Infrastructure & Utilities  segment secured order inflow of ₹ 35,509 crore during the 
quarter  ended September  30, 2026, registering a decline of  13% on y-o-y basis , impacted by 
the deferment of select targeted orders . International orders constituted 31% of the total 
order  inflow of the segment during the quarter . 
 
As on September  30, 202 6, the segment order book stood at ₹ 361,368 crore, with 
international orders contributing 34% to the total.  
 
The segment recorded customer revenues of ₹ 25,133 crore for the quarter ended              
September  30, 2026, registering a y -o-y growth  of 6%. The growth is largely attribut ed to 
improv ing execution across the domestic private sector and international projects . 
International revenues constituted 30% of the total customer revenues of the segment during 
the quarter.  
 
The EBITDA margin  of the segment for the quarter  ended September  30, 2026, was at 5.7% 
compared to 6.2% in the corresponding quart er of the previous year. The contraction  in 
margin is primarily on account of cost pressure s and change in sales mix . 
 
Energy  - Conventional  Segment  
 
The Energy – Conventional segment secured orders valued at ₹ 78,413 crore during the

& Onshore and CarbonLite Solutions businesses . International order inflow constituted 73% 
of the total order inflow during the quarter . 
 
The segment order book stood at ₹ 2 86,888 crore  as on September  30, 202 6, with

For the quarter ended September  30, 202 6, the segment recorded customer revenues of      
₹ 17,285 crore, reflecting a y -o-y growth of 29% on the back of improved execution in the

the segment’s total customer revenues for the quarter.  
 
The segment recorded an EBITDA margin of 7.4% for the quarter  ended September  30, 2026, 
compared to 8.2% in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The decline  in margin 
is primarily attributable to a higher proportion of orders in  early stage s of execution within
