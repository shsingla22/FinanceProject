balance funds for the acquisition of securities of Bliss GVS 
Pharma Limited through proceeds of issuance of equity -like 
instruments  to Purebliss  Pharma Solutions Private Limited 
(“Purebliss ”). 
Size of agreement  The issue size is up to INR 300,00,00,000.

dematerialised form on a private placement basis.
