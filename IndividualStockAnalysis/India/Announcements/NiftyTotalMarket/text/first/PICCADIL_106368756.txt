PRESS RELEASE  
 
CASHMIR - INDIA’S 1ST SMALL -BATCH ORGANIC VODKA WINS ‘GOLD ’ 
AT THE VODKA MASTERS 2025  
 
New Delhi, 9th September 2025  — India’s first premium small -batch organic craft vodka, CASHMIR , 
has achieved a landmark victory by winning Gold at The Vodka Masters 2025 by The Spirits Business . 
This historic achievement marks the first-ever international gold medal for a premium Indian organic 
small -batch vodka brand , putting India firmly on the global vodka map.  
 
Launched in May 2025  by Piccadily Distilleries , CASHMIR vodka marks a bold new chapter for India’s 
spirits industry. At its heart lies Sona Moti —a rare, 2,000 -year -old organic heritage winter wheat that 
once thrived in northern India but had nearly vanished into extinction. After six years of dedicated 
revival, this ancient grain was painstakingly brought back to life, with enough cultivated to distill a 
small batch. Non -GMO, non -hybrid, and fully organic Sona Moti, revered as the “Golden Pearl” , forms 
the soul of a vodka unlike any other in the world.  
 
CASHMIR undergoes a 7X distillation for unparalleled purity and a 5X precision filtration through 
activated carbon, mango charcoal and layers of platinum, gold and silver. This refined process results 
in remarkable clarity and an exceptionally smooth, velvety finish. Finally, it is blended with pristine 
glacial water from the Kashmir Valley, celebrated for its natural purity and mineral balance, to create a 
vodka that is crisp, clean and luminous in character. With zero sugar and no artificial flavours, 
CASHMIR vodka is 100% vegan and crafted with complete transparency. A seamless fusion of heritage, 
innovation and authenticity, CASHMIR Vodka elevates Indian craftsmanship onto the world stage.

The Vodka Masters, organized by The Spirits Business, represents one of the most rigorous and

Cashmir's commitment to pure, additive -free craftsmanship.

About Cashmir Vodka  
Launched in May 2025, CASHMIR Vodka is India’s first premium small -batch organic vodka , crafted 
from the rare heritage grain Sona Moti and pristine Himalayan glacial water by Piccadily Agro 
Industries Limited, a public listed company. This spirit celebrates purity, authenticity and 
uncompromising craftsmanship. Inspired by Kashmir Valley's untouched serenity, CASHMIR uses the 
finest Sona Moti winter wheat - a 2000 -year -old organic grain known as the Golden Pearl  - and 
mineral -rich glacial water from Kashmir's pristine springs. Through 7X distillation and 5X precision 
filtration, it delivers a velvety texture and clean finish that redefines sophistication. Every sip captures 
exceptional balance and refinement, embodying the serene essence of a land that has stirred 
imaginations for centuries.

About Piccadily Agro Industries Limited (PAIL)  
 
Piccadily Agro Industries Limited (PAIL) is a publicly listed company and  operates primarily in two 
strategic business segments: Distillery and Sugar. Its manufacturing facility is located in Indri, Haryana, 
covers 168 acres and is equipped with advanced technology for producing a diverse range of products, 
including Malt, Extra Neutral Alcohol (ENA), Ethanol, and White Crystal Sugar.  
 
Piccadily Agro Industries Limited has established itself as a key player in the alcoholic beverages 
industry, particularly renowned for its expertise in malt spirits. The company boasts a robust portfolio 
that includes premium  expressions of Indri single malt whisky, Camikara  – India’s 1st pure s ugarcane 
juice aged rum , Cashmir Vodka and blended malt whisk ies.  
 
In 2022, Piccadily Agro Industries Limited made a significant mark with the launch of ‘Indri’ its flagship 
single malt whisky brand, aimed at catering to discerning consumers who appreciate quality and 
craftsmanship in spirits. By focusing on premiumization strategies and leveraging its technical 
capabilities, the company has successfully positioned itself as a leader in the Indian single malt whisky 
market by becoming the ‘fastest growing single malt whisky brand’ in 2024.
