PVR INOX LIMITED OPENS A 5-SCREEN MULTIPLEX IN COIMBATORE, 
TAMIL NADU AT ALVEAL FUN SAVVY MALL  
 ~ Expands presence in Tamil Nadu with 24th cinema in the state, enhancing the cinematic 
experience in Coimbatore  ~ 
National, 02nd  October  2024 : PVR INOX Limited, India’s largest and most premium cinema exhibitor, 
announced the opening of a new 5 -screen cinema  at Alveal Fun Savvy Mall, Coimbatore. This marks PVR 
INOX’s third cinema in Coimbatore and the 24th cinema in Tamil Nadu, expanding the screen count in the 
state to 144 screens and 20 screens in Coimbatore.  Known as the second largest city in Tamil Nadu and 
often referred to as the "Manchester of South India" due to its thriving textile industry, Coimbat ore remains  
a key market for PVR INOX’s expansion strategy.  
With this cinema opening, the company continues to reaffirm its position in South India , offering world -
class cinematic experiences to audiences in the region.  
Located at Alveal Fun Savvy Mall the  new cinema features 894 seats spread across its 5 auditoriums, offering 
moviegoers an exceptional, immersive experience. With this launch, PVR INOX continues to enhance its 
footprint in Tamil Nadu, offering cutting -edge cinematic experiences to audiences in the region.  
The new PVR INOX cinema at Alveal Fun Savvy Mall has been designed to offer a luxurious movie -watching 
experience, blending modern aesthetics with the latest in cinema technology. The multiplex features a 
striking pre -foyer, welcoming guests  with a captivating entrance lobby that transitions from the outside 
world to a world of entertainment. The spacious layout ensures a seamless flow throughout the area, with 
dynamic screens showcasing the latest movie offerings.  
 
At the heart of the foyer,  the central concessionaire offers a vibrant and bold atmosphere, featuring rich 
materials and a modern aesthetic. The layered ceiling design and crystal embellishments enhance the 
overall grandeur of the space, ensuring a visually appealing environment fo r moviegoers.  
 
The 5 auditoriums at PVR INOX Coimbatore feature comfortable Udine sliders ensuring a comfortable 
viewing experience. Each auditorium boasts a unique colour  theme —ranging from red, golden, violet, and 
teal—creating a vibrant and engaging atmosphere. Four of the auditoriums are equipped with  Next Gen 3D 
system, 2k Next Gen projector  technology , and Dolby 7.1 sound, while one auditorium boasts DTS X sound, 
delivering superior sound quality for an unparalleled auditory experience.  
 
Speaking on the launch, Mr. Ajay Bijli, Managing Director, PVR INOX Limited, said, “We are thrilled to 
expand our presence in Coimbatore and Tamil Nadu with the launch of our third cin ema in the city. Our 
commitment to providing a world -class movie -going experience remains strong, and we are confident that 
this new cinema will enhance the entertainment options available to the people of Coimbatore. The cinema's 
modern design and state -of-the-art technology ensure that audiences can enjoy films in the most immersive 
and luxurious setting possible.”  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The new cinema adds another destination for movie lovers in Coimbatore, offering a premium environment 
for experiencing the latest blockbu sters. From stylish interiors to cutting -edge sound and projection 
technologies, PVR INOX continues to redefine cinema entertainment in India.  
 
Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli, Executive Director, PVR INOX Limited, added, “We are delighted to unveil our 
newest mul tiplex in Coimbatore, a city that has always embraced high -quality cinema experiences. With this 
opening, we further strengthen our foothold in Tamil Nadu, bringing the best cinematic experience to our 
patrons. Our cinemas continue to be a part of the evol ving landscape of entertainment, offering moviegoers 
an experience that goes beyond just watching films.”  
 
With its innovative design, immersive sound, and luxurious ambiance, PVR INOX at Alveal Fun Savvy Mall is 
set to become the premier destination for movie enthusiasts in Coimbatore.
