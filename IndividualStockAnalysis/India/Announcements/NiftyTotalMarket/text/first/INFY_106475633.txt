Sub: Update on McCamish Cyber Incident  
 
In continuation to our statement  dated March 14, 2025, we would like to update that on December 22, 
2025, Infosys McCamish Systems (“ IMS”), a subsidiary of Infosys BPM limited (a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Infosys limited) and the State of South Dakota (United States of America) Division of 
Insurance entered into a Consent Order.  
 
The South Dakota Division of Insurance expressed concerns that IMS may have violated South Dakota Codified Laws related to notification with respect to a 2023 data breach.  
 IMS has entered into a Consent Order to resolve this matter without any further action. The Consent 
Order specifically acknowledges that this is not an admission that IMS violated the Acts as listed above.
