PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Foundation Inaugurates 108 -Bed Hospital in Collaboration with  
St. John’s Medical College Hospital at Brigade Meadows, Kanakapura Road  
 
Bengaluru, September 10, 2024: Brigade Foundation, a Not -for-Profit Trust of the Brigade Group, today 
inaugurated a state -of-the-art, 108 -bed hospital – St. John’s Medical College Hospital at Brigade Meadows, 
in collaboration with St. John’s National Academy of Health Sciences. The new facility is located within 
Brigade Meadows, a 60 -acre development with 3000 completed and occupied apartments on Kanakapura 
Road. Equipped with specialist doctors and modern diagnostic equipment, this hospital will provide 
comprehensive affordable healthca re services to the urban and rural residents of South Bengaluru.  
 
The hospital was blessed  by Most Rev. Mar Andrews Thazhath  - President -CBCI & Archbishop of 
Trichur , and inaugurated by Shri Dinesh Gundu Rao  - Hon’ble Minister for Health & Family Welfar e, 
Govt. of Karnataka , in the august presence of Chief Guests Sri Ramalinga Reddy -Hon’ble Minister for 
Transport & Muzrai Department, Govt. of Karnataka, Sri Sharan Prakash Patil  - Hon’ble Minister for Medical 
Education & Skill s Development, Govt. of Karnataka, Shri S.T. Somashekar – Hon’ble MLA,  Most Rev. Victor 
Henry Thakur  – Chairman , Governin g Board of St. John’s, Most Rev. Dr. Peter Machado  - Archbishop of 
Bengaluru, Most Rev. George Antonysamy – Archbishop of Madras -Mylapore . The inauguration ceremony 
was attended by several dignitaries and government officials .   
 
Speaking at the inauguration ceremony, M R Jaishankar , Lifetime Trustee, Brigade Foundation & Executive 
Chairman, Brigade Enterprises Limited , expressed his satisfaction with the partnership, stating, “ This 
unique collaboration between Brigade Foundation  and the not -for-profit institution, St John’s Medical 
College Hospital of the CBCI Society  started with the setting up of a Health Centre with 10 beds in th e 
Brigade Meadows campus during June 2020, at the peak of Covid . The good response to the health centre 
indicated the need for a full-fledged  hospital  and a  MoU was signed in June 2022 to establish this 108 bed  
hospital, and it has come to fruition within 2 years.  This collaboration involves Brigade Foundation providing 
the land and the air -conditioned  building, at no cost  to St John’s , and for them to equip the hospital with 
medical equipment, staff & doctors in order to give affordable healthcare in this large neighbourhood 
extending up to Kanakapura town. Furthermore, f or the 10 ,000 plus residents of Brigade Meadows, it is 
huge convenience to have a reputed hospital within the campus .” 
 
In addition to the hospital inaugurated today, Brigade has begun construction of another 108-bed, not -for-
profit hospital  in collaboration with St John’s  in Hardware Park, Bengaluru North , marking a significant 
expansion in accessible healthcare for Bengaluru’s underserved populations.  
 
Rev. Fr. Jesudoss R , Director of St. John’s National Academy of Health Sciences , emphasized the 
Institution’s commitment to delivering affordable and accessible healthcare, saying ,“This hospital will be 
a sign of service, where healing meets humanity, where care meets compassion, and where the values 
of both St. John’s and Brigade come together to create a place of well -being for all. In this new 
facility, we will continue to serve o ur community with the same dedication and commitment to excellence 
that has defined St. John’s for over six decades. This new hospital will reiterate  our mission to 
delivering quality healthcare with integrity, compassion and excellence .” 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The hospital's services include all specialties including super specialties such as Cardiology, Endocrinology,  
Gastroenterology, Neonatology, Nephrology, Neurology, Pulmonary Medicine, and Urology. The hospital will 
provide all laboratory  and imaging diagnostics, as well as Blood  Storage, Dietetics, Physiotherapy, Pharmacy, 
and Ambulance  services . 
 
About Brigade Foundation : Brigade Foundation established in 2003, a not -for-profit  trust of Brigade 
Group, with the objective to support Community Development, Education & Healthcare. Brigade Foundation 
has successfully established 3 well known CBSE / ICSE schools in Bengaluru under the name 'The Brigade 
School', at JP Nagar, Malleswaram  and Whitefield. Brigade Foundation in association with the Bangalore 
Chamber of Industry & Commerce (BCIC) is setting up the Brigade -BCIC Skill Development Academy – a 
Centre of Excellence at Aeros pace Park, Bangalore North. Brigade Foundation has also set up The Indian 
Music Experience Museum (IME), India’s first interactive music museum and two Not-for-Profit Hospitals 
of 10 8 beds each at Brigade Meadows, Kanakapura Road and Brigade EL -Dorado, AeroPark in Collaboration 
with St John’s Medical College Hospital.  
 
About Brigade Group:   Established in 1986, Brigade Group has close to four decades of expertise in building 
positive experiences for all their stakeholders and winning their customers’ trust. Brigade has developed
