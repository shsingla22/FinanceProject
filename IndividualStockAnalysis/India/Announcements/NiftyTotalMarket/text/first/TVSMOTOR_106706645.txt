TVS MOTOR LAUNCHES TVS RAIDER IN EGYPT,  
SET TO REDEFINE THE PREMIUM COMMUTER 
SEGMENT FOR YOUNG AND URBAN RIDERS  
Cairo , Egypt,  July 22, 2026: TVS Motor Company (TVSM), part of TVS VENU, is a leading global 
manufacturer of two and three- wheeler s, today announced the launch of TVS Raider in Egypt. The 
launch event at Hilton Pyramids Golf, Cairo , brought together  TVSM’s  distributor partner, dealer 
network, media, influencers , financial partners and key stakeholders  to celebrate the introduction of one 
of TVS Motor's most successful premium commuter motorcycles to the Egyptian market.  
 
The launch marks an important milestone in TVSM’s strategy to strengthen its footprint in North Africa 
and expanding  its premium commuter motorcycle portfolio in Egypt. The TVS Raider , with its sporty 
design, advanced technology and rider -friendly features , redefines the premium commuter segment for 
young,  urban riders.  
 
The TVS Raider is powered by a 
124.76cc air & oil -cooled 3V engine 
producing 12.9 PS at 8000 rpm and 11.5 Nm of torque at 6500 rpm, paired with a 5- speed gearbox. Equipped 
with telescopic front suspension, a 
gas-charged 5- step adjustable 
monoshock, 240 mm front disc brake 
with SBT, and 80/100- 17 front & 
100/90- 17 rear tubeless tyres, it 
delivers a confident blend of 
performance, comfort and agile 
handling.

 Powering the TVS Raider is an advanced 124.76cc air & oil -cooled 3V engine that delivers 12.9 PS of 
power and 11.5 Nm of torque, ensuring responsive performance and an engaging ride. The motorcycle comes equipped with a full digital reverse LCD cluster, gear shift and position indicator, and a top- speed 
recorder for a modern riding experience. Its gas -charged 5- step adjustable monoshock  suspension and
