LT Foods further strengthens U.S. food  portfolio with acquisition of 
the brand Maya Kaimal , a leading brand with mainstream consumers  
 
 The acquisition will further strengthen the LT Foods ’ U.S. portfolio with premium 
Indian -inspired foods, accelerating growth across Ready -to-Eat (RTE) , Ready -to-
Cook (RTC) and R eady -to-Heat (RTH)  segment  
 
Delhi (India), 18th November  2026 : LT Foods Limited, a global FMCG company in the consumer 
food space, today announced that its U.S. subsidiary, LT Foods Americas Inc., has entered into 
an agreement to acquire 100% stake in MK Food s Holdings, Inc. , a U.S. -based branded packaged 
foods compan y, for an enterprise value of USD 5.8 million on a cash -free, debt -free basis. The 
transaction is expected to be completed within three months, subject to the fulfilment of 
customary closing conditions.  
 
MK Food Holdings  Inc., through its wholly owned subsidiary Maya Kaimal Foods LLC, offers a 
portfolio of premium Indian -inspired shelf -stable sauces, ready -to-eat curries and rice products. 
Its brand, Maya Kaimal , is a leading brand among mainstream consumers, with a strong 
presence in the premium  Indian -inspired foods category. It reported revenue in tune of USD 
17.9 million for the 12 months ended September 30, 2026, compare d with USD 15.5 million in 
2025.  
 
The acquisition represents an important strategic step in LT Foods ’ journey to further 
strengthen  its Ready -to-Eat (RTE) , Ready -to-Cook (RTC) and R eady -to-Heat (RTE, RTC and RTH) 
portfolio . Maya Kaimal ’s culinary and product -development capabilities will complement LT 
Foods ’ existing strengths, enabling the company to broaden its product portfolio, deepen its 
engagement with U.S. consumers and unlock new distribution and cross -category growth 
opportunitie s. 
 
Ernst & Young (E&Y) acted as the exclusive Transaction Advisor for LT Foods on this deal.  
 
Speaking on the acquisition, Mr . Ashwani Arora, Managing Director & CEO, LT Foods 
Limited, said,  “This acquisition is a strategic step in further strengthening o ur consumer foods 
platform in the U.S. Maya Kaimal brings an established premium brand, a differentiated Indian -
inspired portfolio and strong capabilities in sauces and convenient meal solutions. These are 
highly complementary to our existing business and give us a compelling opportunity to expand 
our portfolio into high -growth, value -added categories. ”

About LT Foods Limited  
LT Foods Ltd. [NSE: LTFOODS, BSE: 532783] is a leading global FMCG company in the consumer food 
space. It is a leading player globally in the specialty rice and rice -based foods business for more than last 
70 years. The company delivers the finest quality and taste experiences in more than 85 countries across 
India, the U.S., U.K., Europe, the Middle East, the Far East and the Rest of the World. The company ’s 
flagship brands include DAAWAT®, one of India ’s most loved and consumed Basmati brands, Royal®, which 
is one of North America ’s most loved brand and many more. The company is proudly expanding into the 
future food preferences of millennials by offering organic food in global markets and supplying organic 
food -ingredients to leading businesses. The company is committed to nurturing the goodness of food 
for people, the community and the planet.  
 
With a consolidated revenue of around Rs. 11,023 crore as of FY ’26, LT Foods has an integrated “Farm to

automated state -of-the-art and strategically located Processing Units in India, the U.S. and Europe, and 
a robust distribution network with 1400+ distributors across the globe.
