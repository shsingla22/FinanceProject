Sub: Special Window for Re -lodgement of Transfer and Dematerialization of Physical Shares  
 
Dear Sir / Madam,  
 
Securities and Exchange Board of India has  vide Circular No. HO/38/13/11(2)2026 -MIRSD -POD/I/3750/2026 dated 
January 30, 2026, re-opened a special window for re -lodgment  of transfer requests and dematerialization (“demat”) of 
physical shares for a period of one year from February 5, 2026 to February 4, 2027.  
 
This facility is available only for transfer and dematerialization of physical securities which were sold/purchased  prior to 
April 1, 2019. The facility is also available for transfer requests that were submitted earlier and were rejected, or kept 
pending due to deficiencies in documents, process, or otherwise.  
 
The concerned investor (s)/transferee (s) shall mandatorily have a demat account and provide the Client Master List 
(“CML”), along with the transfer documents and share certificate, while re -lodging the request with the Company’s 
Registrar  to an Issue  and Transfer Agent (“RTA”). Due process shall be followed for such requests  as per the applicable 
law. Transfer requests submitted after February 4, 2027, shall not be accepted by the Company/RTA.  
 
Concerned, stakeholders  may contact the Company’s RTA  i.e., Computech Sharecap Limited  within the aforesaid period 
for further assistance.   
 
This communication is being disseminated for the awareness of the concerned stakeholders. The y are requested to take 
note of the aforesaid special window and avail themselves of the facility within the stipulated period.
