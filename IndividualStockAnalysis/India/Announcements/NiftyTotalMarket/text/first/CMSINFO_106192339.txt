Dear Sir/Madam  
 
Further to our above referred letters on the subject and pursuant to the Regulation 30 read 
with regulation 31A of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 as amended  (‘SEBI LODR Regulations’) , we 
hereby inform that the National Stock Exchange of India Limited , vide its letter No. 
NSE/LIST/COMP/CMSINFO/467/2025- 2026,  and BSE Limited , vide its letter No .  
LIST/COMP/SHB/16/2025- 26, both dated April 02, 2025,  have granted its No Objection 
Letter for de-classification of Sion Investment Holdings Pte. Ltd.  (‘Sion’) as the Promoter of 
the Company.  
 
As already informed, Sion sold all the equity shares held by it in the Company on February 
27, 2024. Further, Sion has not been associated with the Company in any capacity. Also , Sion 
and its representatives do not participate in the management of the Company in any manner 
or capacity whatsoever and Sion's nominees do not hold any key managerial positions in the 
Company.  
Accordingly, pursuant to proviso (a) to sub -clause (vi.) of clause (a) o f sub regulation (3) of 
regulation 31A of the SEBI LODR Regulations, the de- classification of Sion as the  Promoter 
of the Company does not require further approval of the shareholders at a General Meeting  
of the Company . Consequently , Sion I nvestment Holdings Pte. Limited has ceased  to be the 
Promoter of the Company with immediate effect.
