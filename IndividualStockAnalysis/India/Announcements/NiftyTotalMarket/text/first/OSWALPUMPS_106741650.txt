Oswal Pumps Secures Additional 20 MW Rooftop Solar Order in Bihar under PM Surya Ghar Yojana  
 
Karnal, Haryana,  13th August  2026:  Oswal Pumps Limited announces that it has received a Letter of Award 
(LoA) from North Bihar Power Distribution Company Limited (NBPDCL) for the design, supply, and 
installation of 20 MW of grid -connected rooftop solar capacity across 18,089 consumer sites of 1.1 kW each 
in the Darbhanga Circle of Bihar, under the PM Surya Ghar – Muft Bijli Yojana (PMSG -MBY). The award 
builds on the Company's earlier order wins in the state and reinforces its expanding presence under the 
programme.  
 
The projects carry an installation order value of approximately INR 7 8 Crore, with an estimated long -term 
revenue potential of approximately INR 67 Crore from energy supply over a 10 -year tenure under the 
RESCO model, translating into a cumulative revenue opportunity of approximately INR 145 Crore. Each 
beneficiary household  is expected to receive approximately 15,000 units of solar power over the contract 
period.  
 
Commenting on the orders, Mr. Vivek Gupta, Chairman and Managing Director, Oswal Pumps Limited,  
said 
 
“We are pleased to have received this additional order from NBPDCL. Bihar continues to be an important 
market for us under the PM Surya Ghar programme, and this award reflects the continued confidence of 
state discoms in our capabilities. We look forward to  commencing execution and delivering on this 
mandate, in line with our broader commitment to India's clean energy transition. ” 
 
About  Oswal  Pumps  Limited  
 
Oswal Pumps is one of India’s fastest -growing, vertically integrated solar pump manufacturer. With a legacy of over 
two decades in pump engineering and manufacturing, the company is a fully integrated provider  of turnkey  solar  
pumping  systems.  It has established  a strong  presence  across  solar -powered  and grid -connected submersible and 
monoblock pumps, electric motors, and solar modules —all marketed under the trusted ‘Oswal’ brand.  The Company  
is accredited  with  ISO 9001:2015,  ISO 45001:2018,  and ISO 14001:2015  certifications  and is listed under the Ministry 
of New and Renewable Energy’s Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM).  
 
With a strong pan -India distribution network, growing international footprint across countries, and a focused  design  
and engineering  team,  Oswal  Pumps  continues  to cater  to the diverse  requirements  of end- users in the agricultural, 
residential and industrial sectors.
