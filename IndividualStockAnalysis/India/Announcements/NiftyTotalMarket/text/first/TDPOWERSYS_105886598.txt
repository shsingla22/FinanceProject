Sub: Update on  the strike announced by  Workers  Union.  
 
In continuation of our letter dated June 4, 2024 informing about the receipt of a strike notice  from 
the Workers Union, we further inform that, the Company  approached the Nelamangala Court with 
an application to obtain an injunction to restrain  the Workers’ U nion from obstructing operations  
& ensu ring smooth passage of  employees and vehicles carrying goods from entering and leaving 
the factory pre mises during a strike or agitation.   
 
The Honorable Additional Civil Judge & JMFC at Nelamangala Court  by its order dated June 18, 
2024  received by the Company on June 19, 2024,  has granted a temporary injunction  order 
restraining the Workers Union, during  a strike,  from obstructing the Company , its directors, 
executives, staffs, workmen, vehicles, vehicles carrying with finished goods and raw materials 
etc., from entering into and leaving  from the factory gates  of the Company and are permitted to 
agitate  beyond 25 meters from the factory gates  for a period of six months from the date of the 
order .  
 
This injunction provides the Company with relief from potential disruptions by the union and 
ensuring the uninterrupted operation of the company's business  during this period.
