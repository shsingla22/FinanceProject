SHRI RAJESH PRATAP SINGH SISODIA ASSUMES CHARGE AS 
DIRECTOR (P & P) OF NLC INDIA LIMITED 
Shri Rajesh Pratap Singh Sisodia assumed charge as Director (Planning & Projects) 
of NLC India Limited. today, 02.12.2025, at Neyveli where he was warmly welcomed 
to the NLCIL family by CMD and Functional Directors. 
A seasoned professional with more than three decades of rich experience across 
various core engineering sectors, Shri Rajesh Pratap Singh Sisodia has led various 
technical divisions before serving as Executive Director at Bharat Heavy Electricals 
Limited. Now, he takes over as Director (Planning & Projects) in NLC India Limited. 
Shrl Sisodia holds a degree in Mechanical Engineering from Regional Engineering 
College, Kurukshetra. He started his career in BHEL 1n 1990 as Engineer Trainee. 
He possesses more than 35 years of experience working in BHEL at manufactur ing 
units, construction sites, project management and business divisions of Power and 
Industry Sectors. 
He brings on-board his vast experience in varied core areas of design and 
engineering , site construction, project management of large EPC projects, 
Marketing and contract management functions coupled with expertise in emerging 
New and Renewable Energy and Coal gasification division. He has also worked at 
BHEL's International Project site for erection and commissioning of large gas 
turbines. During his tenure in BHEL, he was instrumental in introducing State of 
art technology in gas turbine, supercritical boiler and turbine sets and Power plant 
equipment(s) mitigating impact on Environment, Air Cooled Condenser with 
supercritical sets and Flue gas desulphurisation. 
He was also a nominee Director in BHEL's Joint Venture with Karnataka Power 
Corporation Ltd. and NTPC Ltd. 
Congratulatmg Shri Rajesh Pratap Singh Sisodia on his new role, NLCIL CMD Shri 
Prasanna Kumar Motupalli stated -"With his experience in core engineering areas, 
broad vision and strategic acumen, his tenure as Director (Planning & Projects) 
will be pro-active in creating and managing new Projects of NLCIL with focus on 
developing long-term strategic plans, managing project portfolios and securing 
resources, in line with the company's vision." 
NLCIL wishes Shri Rajesh Pratap Singh Sisodia on his new role and looks forward 
to his continued leadership in steering the company 's strategy towards greater 
innovation and growth. ·
