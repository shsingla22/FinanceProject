Revolt Motors Powers Festivities with ‘Revolt Festive Dhamaka’ – 
Benefits up to ₹20,000  
New Delhi, 2 2nd September 2025:  Revolt Motors, India’s No.1 electric motorcycle brand,  has 
announced the launch of its exclusive festive campaign ‘Revolt Festive Dhamaka’  this Navratri, offering 
customers unmatched savings and celebrations on two wheels. Under this special offer, buyers can 
avail benefits worth up to ₹20,000, which include a direct cash benefit  of up to ₹13,000 on select 
models along with free insurance worth up to ₹7,000.  
The ‘Revolt Festive Dhamaka’ is valid from 22nd September to 2nd October 2025 across all authorised 
Revolt Hubs nationwide and is applicable on the entire product range including RV400, RV400 BRZ, 
RV1, RV1+ and the newly launched RV BlazeX.  
 
Speaking on the announcement, Ms. Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises Limited , 
said, “Festivals in India are about new beginnings, and Revolt Motors is proud to be a part of this 
journey with our riders. With ‘Revolt Festive Dhamaka’, we are combining affordability, performance, 

   
and sustainability to ensure more Indians can switch to clean mobility while enjoying significant festive 
savings.”  
Revolt Motors continues to drive EV adoption across India by delivering smarter, greener, and more 
affordable mobility solutions, furthering the Government’s vision for sustainable transportation.  
For festive bookings, test rides, and more information, visit www.revoltmotors.com  or the nearest 
authorised Revolt Hub
