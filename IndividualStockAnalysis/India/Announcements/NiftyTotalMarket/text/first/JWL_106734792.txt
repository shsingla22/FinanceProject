Jupiter Wagons Wins Orders Worth Over Rs. 211 Crore from JSW Port Logistics 
and Orissa Alloy Steel Private Limited  
National: August 10, 2026: Jupiter Wagons Limited, a provider of comprehensive mobility solutions 
encompassing rail, road and marine transportation, has secured two significant orders worth over Rs. 211 Crore from JSW Port Logistics Private Limited and Orissa Alloy Steel Private Limited (OASPL),  reinforcing its 
position as a trusted partner in India's evolving freight logistics sector while strengthening its presence across 
the private wagon ownership and leasing ecosystem. 
 Jupiter Wagons has signed a  Rs. 147.11 crore Letter of Intent (LoI) with JSW Port Logistics Private 
Limited  for the manufacture and supply of 7 BOSM rakes  comprising 329 BOSM wagons.  
 This is Jupiter Wagons' second order from the JSW Group in just six weeks  a clear signal of the confidence 
one of India's largest industrial conglomerates continues to place in the Company's execution capability, following its earlier Rs. 122.88 crore order from JSW (South) Rail Logistics Private Limited  in June 2026. 
With this latest win, Jupiter Wagons' cumulative order intake from the JSW Group alone now stands at approximately Rs. 270 crore  in under two months. 
 In a separate development, Orissa Alloy Steel Private Limited (OASPL)  has placed a Purchase Order on 
Jupiter Wagons for the manufacture and supply of 150 wagons, under the LSFTO Scheme. The order is 
valued at Rs. 64.16 crore.  Together, the two orders have a combined value of about Rs. 211.27 crore  (incl GST)  and further strengthen 
Jupiter Wagons' order book while underscoring the growing demand for modern freight transportation solutions across industrial and logistics sectors.  
 Commenting on the development, Mr. Vivek Lohia, Managing Director, Jupiter Wagons Limited, said:  
"These order wins from JSW Port Logistics and Orissa Alloy Steel Private Limited add further momentum to 
Jupiter Wagons' growth journey and reaffirm the trust private sector players continue to place in our manufacturing strength and execution track record. As India's rail freight ecosystem sees rising private investment and ownership of rolling stock, Jupiter Wagons sees continued opportunities across this evolving landscape. Demand for efficient, specialised and high- capacity rolling stock keeps rising, and we remain 
committed to meeting it through advanced wagon solutions that improve logistics efficiency, reduce turnaround times, and support India's broader infrastructure and economic growth story." 
These orders reflect Jupiter Wagons' growing traction with private sector customers and reinforce the 
Company's role as a preferred manufacturing partner for specialised , high- capacity wagon platforms. Backed 
by its integrated manufacturing footprint and a broad- based product portfolio, Jupiter Wagons remains focused 
on scaling capacity and execution capability to meet the accelerating demand for rolling stock across Indi a's rail 
freight and logistics landscape.
