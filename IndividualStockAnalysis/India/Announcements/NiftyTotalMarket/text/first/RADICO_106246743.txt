 Radico Khaitan Introduces TRIKĀL Indian Single Malt – Eternal Whisky 
A Celebration of the Past, Present, and Future 
New Delhi, India, May 23, 2025 – After the unprecedented success of Rampur Indian Single Malt, 
Radico Khaitan has taken yet another step in its premiumisation journey with the launch of TRIKĀL 
Indian Single Malt – Eternal Whisky . This exceptional expression marks a meaningful addition to the 
Company’s luxury portfolio, reflecting Radico Khaitan’s passion for creating homegrown spirits that 
speak to modern consumers who appreciate both elegance and authenticity. 
Born at the legendary Rampur Distillery, over 80-year-old 
sanctum of spirit alchemy, TRIKĀL is not merely a whisky, it is a 
temporal voyage. Its very name, drawn from ancient Sanskrit 
for ‘the three dimensions of time’ - past, present, and future, 
echoes like a mantra across eras. Forged through generational 
mastery and rooted deep in India’s history, TRIKĀL captures the 
soul of tradition while whispering to the future. It is a 
convergence of heritage and horizon. A whisky crafted not just 
for the moment, but for every moment that ever was, or ever 
will be. 
TRIKĀL opens with notes of warm spice, soft vanilla, and ripe 
stone fruits. The palate is rich and layered, offering toasted oak, 
honeyed peat, and subtle smoke, with a long, evolving finish. 
Each sip reveals a new dimension, making it a whisky meant to 
be savoured slowly and thoughtfully. 
Speaking on the launch, Mr. Abhishek Khaitan, Managing Director, Radico Khaitan , said: "The Indian 
Single Malt segment has witnessed a remarkable transformation over the past decade, evolving from a niche 
category to a beacon of premiumisation in the global alcobev space. With growing global and domestic 
appreciation for Indian malts, we see a huge opportunity to deepen our footprint in this category. The launch 
of TRIKĀL marks yet another milestone in Radico Khaitan’s journey to elevate Indian spirits on the world stage 
and reflects our long-term vision of investing in innovation-led, homegrown luxury."  
Reflecting on the craftsmanship behind the new launch, Mr. Amar Sinha, Chief Operating Officer, 
Radico Khaitan , added: "TRIKĀL is a result of passion and precision. Every element, right from the selection 
of barley to the rare combination of casks used for maturation, to carefully curate a whisky that is layered, 
complex, and uniquely Indian. We have blended tradition and technique to create an indulgent experience 
that connects with the deeper essence of time itself."  
TRIKĀL priced between INR 3500 and 4500 will be immediately available in Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, and 
Maharashtra; and it is poised for a pan India besides global launch in the times ahead. 
With TRIKĀL, Radico Khaitan presents a single malt that is not bound by convention but guided by a 
timeless philosophy, connecting what has been, what is, and what lies ahead.
