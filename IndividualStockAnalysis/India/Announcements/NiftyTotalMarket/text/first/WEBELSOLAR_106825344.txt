Websol Renewables Pvt. Ltd. – A Wholly Owned Subsidiary of Websol Energy System Limited, 
Appoints Vinay Vyas as CEO 
Kolkata, October 22, 2026:  Websol Energy System Limited, one of India's legacy manufacturers of solar 
cells and modules, has announced the appointment of Mr. Vinay Vyas as Chief Executive Officer  of its 
wholly owned subsidiary, Websol Renewables Pvt Ltd. 
Mr. Vyas brings over 18 years of experience in renewable energy and energy storage. He has worked on 
a clean energy portfolio of more than 8 GW, valued at ₹24,000 crore, and led the development of what is 
described as the world's first integrated green hydrogen and ammonia project at ACME Cleantech. His 
roles have included Chief Executive Officer of FusionX Energy, Group Lead (Vice President) for 
renewable power, BESS and green hydrogen at Welspun Group, and Director of the Global Renewable 
Energy Center at Canadian Solar. He also chaired the Energy Storage Committee at the National Solar 
Energy Federation of India (NSEFI) in 2025. 
Websol crossed ₹1,000 crore in revenue in FY26 and plans to take its manufacturing capacity to 5,350 
MW of solar cells and 4,550 MW of modules by 2028. Websol Renewables extends the Company's 
presence further along the solar value chain. Mr. Vyas's experience in solar module manufacturing 
strategy at Canadian Solar, and in energy storage and energy trading at FusionX Energy, where he 
secured a CERC energy trading licence, is relevant to this next phase. 
“We are pleased to welcome Mr. Vyas to Websol Renewables. His track record of building renewable 
businesses at scale, will be valuable as we expand further along the solar value chain. Backed by 
Websol’s three decades in solar manufacturing, we look forward to his contribution in creating long-term 
value for all stakeholders,” said Mr. Sohan Lal Agarwal, Chairman and Managing Director, Websol 
Energy System Limited. 
Sharing his thoughts on the appointment, Mr. Vinay Vyas  said, “It is a great opportunity to join Websol 
Renewables. Websol has built a strong legacy in solar manufacturing, and Mr. Sohan Lal Agarwal’s 
leadership has taken the Company to an important stage of growth. I see a clear opportunity to build the 
business at scale and look forward to working with the entire team to take it to new heights." 
Websol continues to expand its solar cell and module manufacturing capacity while strengthening its 
presence across the renewable energy value chain. The Company remains focused on building scale, 
deepening its capabilities, and supporting India’s transition to clean energy. 
 
 
 
 
About Websol Energy System Limited  
Websol Energy System Limited, BSE (517498) and NSE (WEBELSOLAR), is one of India's earlier 
manufacturers of solar photovoltaic cells, with more than 30 years of industry experience. The Company 
operates an integrated manufacturing facility at the Falta Special Economic Zone in West Bengal, with a 
solar cell capacity of 1,200 MW and a module capacity of 550 MW, using Mono PERC and bifacial 
technology. The facility is designed to process wafers up to 210 mm. Websol supplies solar cells 
primarily within India, supporting module manufacturers in meeting Domestic Content Requirement 
norms, and markets its modules in India and internationally.
