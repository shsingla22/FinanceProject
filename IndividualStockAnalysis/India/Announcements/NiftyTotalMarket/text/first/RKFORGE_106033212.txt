Sub: - Outcome of the Board Meeting October 24, 2024 pursuant to Regulation 30 Regulation 30 of 
the SEBI (Listing Obligation and Disclosure Requirements) Regulation, 2015 (as amended) : 
Investment in share capital of AMP Energy C&I Fifteen Private Limited. 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR Regulations”), we wish to inform you that the Board 
of Directors of the Company at its meeting held on October 24, 2024, has approved investment of Rs. 
12.90 crore in the form of equity and debentures in AMP Energy C&I Fifteen Private Limited and has 
entered into Share Subscription and Shareholders Agreements and other relevant agreements with 
AMP Energy C&I Fifteen Private Limited for procurement of solar power under captive generation 
scheme as per Electricity Act, 2003 read with Electricity Rules, 2005. 
The details as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read with SEBI Circular 
No. CIR/CFD/CMD/4/2015 dated September 9, 2015 in relation to the Proposed Investments are 
enclosed as “Annexure A”. 
Copy of the same is being also made available on the website of the Company at 
www.ramkrishnaforgings.com 
The Board Meeting commenced at 11.00 (LS.T) and concluded at 13.30 (LS.T). 
We request you to kindly take the abovementioned information on record and oblige.
