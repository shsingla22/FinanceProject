Kaynes Technology Accelerates AR/AI Wearables Leadership by absorbing 
the core team from  Mustard Smartglasses Team  
 
Kaynes Technology India Limited today announced the successful integration  of the 
core team behind Mustard Smartglasses, a pioneering force in AI -powered, 
waveguide -based smart wearable technology. This strategic move marks a 
significant leap in Kaynes’ ambition to lead in advanced design and emerging 
technology for consumer and enterprise markets through stron g ODM/OEM 
partnerships.  
 
Integration of Mustard Smartglasses Team – Boosting Next -Gen Design & 
Innovation  
 
By welcoming Mustard’s elite minds and product visionaries, Kaynes gains industry -
leading expertise in the design of lightweight, AI -powered, developer -friendly 
wearables. Mustard’s smart  glasses platform seamlessly integrates AI audio, 
camera systems, and advanced waveguide optics, empowering both real -time AR 
overlays and intuitive, always -on interfaces.  
This integration  enables Kaynes to strengthen its role as a leading ODM/OEM 
partner, designing and manufacturing next -generation AR/VR smart  glasses 
platforms for global brands. Leveraging open SDKs for third -party innovation, 
Kaynes will support a wide spectrum of use case s from real -time translation and 
enhanced accessibility for consumers to AI -driven remote inspection and SOP 
monitoring for enterprises.  
 
Significance of the Integration  
 
The addition of Mustard’s core innovations, including deep experience with Digi -lens 
waveguide optics, further strengthens Kaynes’ ongoing investments in advanced 
manufacturing and technology platforms. It also reinforces Kaynes’ commitment to 
an open, developer -centric ecosystem  creating headroom for India and global 
brands to launch AI -driven, AR -first products at scale.  
Kaynes Technology India Limited continues to focus on driving innovation, reliability, 
and global competitiveness in India’s electronics and semiconductor manufacturing 
landscape, building next -generation capabilities to address surging demand across 
consu mer, industrial  and enterprise verticals.
