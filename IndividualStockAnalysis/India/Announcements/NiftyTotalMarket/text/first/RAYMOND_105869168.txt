A 'specified person' means a person who has:  
i. not filed return of income for one preceding assessment year relevant to the previous 
year immediately prior to the previous year in which tax is required to be deducted, 
for which the time limit of filing return of income under sub-Section (1) of Section 139 
has expired; and  
ii. subjected to tax deduction/collection at source in aggregate amounting to Rs. 50,000 
or more in said immediate previous year.  
 
The non-residents who do not have the permanent establishment and residents who are not 
required to file a return under Section 139 of the Act are excluded from the scope of a 
specified person.
