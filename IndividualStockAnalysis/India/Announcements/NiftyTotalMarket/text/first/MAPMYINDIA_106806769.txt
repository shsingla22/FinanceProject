MathWorks and MapmyIndia  Mappls  Accelerate ADAS Validation for Indian Roads 
Using High -Definition Maps 
MapmyIndia HD maps integrate with MATLAB®, Simulink®, and RoadRunner™ workflows , enabling 
automotive engineers to simulate and validate ADAS solutions for Indian driving conditions  
Bengaluru, [05 Octo ber, 2026] : MathWorks, the leading developer of mathematical computing 
software for designing engineered systems, today announced a collaboration with MapmyIndia  
Mappls,  India’s leading advanced digital maps and deep -tech products and platforms company , to 
support automotive engineers developing and validating Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) 
for Indian road environments.  
By integrating MapmyIndia’s high -definition (HD) maps with MATLAB, Simulink, and RoadRunner, 
engineering teams can simulate realistic road and traffic scenarios and begin system validation earlier 
in the development cycle.  The integration enables engineers to  create digital twins of Indian road 
environments, simulate complex traffic scenarios, and test ADAS controllers within a Model -Based 
Design environment . 
Developing ADAS for Indian roads presents distinct engineering challenges, including dense and 
heterog eneous traffic conditions, variable lane behaviour, diverse road infrastructure, and rapidly 
evolving road layouts. MapmyIndia HD maps address these challenges by providing lane -level road 
geometry, traffic controls, detailed road attributes, and India -specific features, such as speed 
breakers, potholes, and faded lane markings, creating an accurate digital representation of real -world 
road conditions.  
“India's roads present some of the world's most complex and dynamic driving environments. 
Developing ADAS solutions for these conditions requires far more than adapting models built for 
structured traffic ecosystems. MapmyIndia's HD maps capture the scale and complexity of Indian 
roads with accuracy and detail — from lane -level geometry and traffic controls to  speed breakers, 
potholes, faded lane markings and other critical localised road attributes. Our collaboration with 
MathWorks empowers automotive  engineers  to simulate real Indian driving conditions, validate 
systems earlier, and accelerate the development  of ADAS solutions that are truly built for India.” sa id, 
Sapna Ahuja, President Automotive business and Chief Operating Officer, MapmyIndia Mappls.  
MapmyIndia Mappls location technologies and HD maps extend the MathWorks ecosystem for 
automated -driving de velopment by connecting location intelligence with Model -Based Design 
workflows for simulation, algorithm development , and validation. The resulting solution helps 
automotive engineering teams accelerate the deployment of ADAS solutions developed specifically 
for the Indian market.  
“As automotive teams expand ADAS development for India, virtual validation is becoming esse ntial to 
evaluate system performance across diverse driving conditions,” said Vijayalayan R., Senior Manager 
– Automotive, MathWorks. “By integrating MapmyIndia’s HD maps with MATLAB, Simulink, and 
RoadRunner, engineers can test ADAS algorithms earlier in development, reduce reliance on costly 
physical testing , and improve system performance before deployment on Indian roads. ”
