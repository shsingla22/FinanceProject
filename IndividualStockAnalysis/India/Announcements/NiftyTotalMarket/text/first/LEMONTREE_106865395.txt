LEMON TREE HOTELS OPENS LEMON TREE RESORT, CHITTORGARH 
IN RAJASTHAN  
 
The 98 -villa resort in historic Mewar features villas with private pools and an 
indoor venue for destination weddings and corporate retreats  
New Delhi,  18th November , 2026 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited has announced 
the opening of Lemon Tree Resort, Chittorgarh , strengthening its leisure 
presence in Rajasthan. Nestled in the historic Mewar region, the property caters 
to travellers exploring Chittorgarh’s storied past, while offering a dedicated setting 
for destination weddings and corporate retreats. With this l aunch, the Group’s 
Rajasthan portfolio stands at 25 hotels, comprising 15 operational hotels and 10 
in the pipeline.  
 
The resort is managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
 
Designed as a tranquil retreat, the resort features 98 villas across two categories, 
offering guests the choice of a private balcony or a private pool. Recreational 
facilities include a full -service spa, an outdoor swimming pool, a fitness centre, 
and an indoor games  zone. For dining, guests can enjoy global and local favourites 
at Citrus Café, the all -day dining restaurant, or opt for in -room dining. To host 
destination weddings, social celebrations, and MICE events, the property features 
Tangerine, a spacious indoor  banquet venue.  
 
Located approximately 14 km from the iconic Chittorgarh Fort , the resort serves 
as an ideal base for discovering architectural marvels such as Vijay Stambh, 
Maharani Padmini Palace, and Kalika Mata Temple. Nature enthusiasts and 
spiritual travellers also have easy access to Bassi Wildlife Sanctuary and Shri 
Sanwari ya Seth Temple.  
 
Commenting on the opening, Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, 
Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd. , said, “Rajasthan's rich heritage, cultural diversity and 
growing tourism potential make it an important market for us. Beyond its 
established hubs, destinations like Chittorgarh offer promising opportunities to 
extend our presence and cater to travellers seeking  more immersive experiences. 
With Lemon Tree Resort, Chittorgarh, we bring together the region's heritage 
appeal and a differentiated resort offering. Its private -pool villas and dedicated 
banquet facilities allow us to cater to leisure travellers as well as the growing 
demand for destination weddings, celebrations and corporate retreats. As we 
expand across the state, our focus remains on strengthening our presence in high -
potential markets with offerings tailored to their distinct character.”  
 
The property is approximately 95 km from Maharana Pratap Airport, Udaipur, and 
9 km from Chittorgarh Junction. Its location on the Bhilwara –Udaipur Highway 
also makes it a convenient stop for guests exploring Rajasthan by road.
