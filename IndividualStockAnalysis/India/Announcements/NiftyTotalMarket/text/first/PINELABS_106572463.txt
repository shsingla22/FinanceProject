Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that Mopay Services Private Limited 
(“MSPL”) , a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, has decided to initiate the process of voluntary 
strike -off of its name from the register of companies maintained by the Registrar of Companies under 
Section 248 of the Companies Act, 2013 , as approved by its shareholders at their meeting held today i.e. 
on March  27, 2026 . 
 
MSPL is not carrying on any business operations and does not have any activity, and therefore, in order to 
streamline the group structure and reduce administrative and compliance overheads, it has been decided to 
initiate the process for striking -off its name. MSPL shall make an application with the Registrar of 
Companies under Section 248 of the Companies Act, 2013, for removal of its name from the Register of 
Companies.  
 
Upon completion of the necessary formalities and subject to approval of the Registrar of Companies, the 
said entity shall stand dissolved and will consequently cease to be a subsidiary of the Company. The 
proposed strike -off will not have any material impa ct on the financial position or operations of the 
Company.
