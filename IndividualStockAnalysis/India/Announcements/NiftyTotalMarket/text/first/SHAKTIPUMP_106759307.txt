Shakti Pumps’ ESG Impact Rati ng Upgraded to 78 “Good”;  
Marking Continued Progress on  its Sustainability Journey  
Indore, Madhya Pradesh (India), 27th August 2026 – Shakti Pumps (India) Limited (SPIL) [BSE: 531431, NSE: 
SHAKTIPUMP], India’s leading manufacturer of energy-effici ent pumps and motors, is proud to announce that it has 
received its upgraded ESG Rating, [ICRA ESG] Impact Rating 78, Good by ICRA ESG Ratings Limited. The rating 
underscores the company’s strong commitment to environm ental stewardship, social responsibility, and robust 
governance across its operations.  
The ESG Impact Rating is a comprehe nsive assessment that evaluates a comp any’s performance on material ESG 
parameters, including climate risk management, energy efficiency, human capital development, supply chain 
sustainability, and governance transparency. The score improvement to 78 highlights the strengthening of ESG 
profile during FY26, supported by enhanced sustainability targets, improved health & safety performance, broader 
CSR & community engagement, and enhanced environmental disclosures. 
Mr. Dinesh Patidar, Chairman of Shakti Pumps (India) Limited, shared his thoughts on this achievement: “We are proud 
to receive this ESG rating upgrade, which reflects our co ntinued commitment to responsible growth and sustainable 
value creation. At Shakti Pumps, sustainability is deeply integrated into our business st rategy, guiding our decisions 
across renewable energy solutions, reso urce efficiency, governance practices, and social responsibility. This 
upgraded rating is a recognition of the progress we have made in strengthening our ESG performance, enhancing 
transparency, and creating a positive impact for communitie s and the environment. As we move forward, we remain 
committed to advancing our sustainability agenda thro ugh innovation, continuous improvement, and meaningful 
stakeholder engagement. I would like to thank all our stak eholders for their unwavering support in this journey.” 
 About Shakti Pumps (India) Limited  
SPIL founded in 1982 as a partnership firm and later converted to a public limited company in 1995, manufactures solar pumps, 
energy-efficient stainless-steel submersible pumps, pressure booster pumps, pump-motors, and other products. SPIL is the only company that manufactures a wide range of products for solar pu mp installation in-house, including Variable Frequency Drives, 
Structures, Motors, Invertors, and so on. Pithampur, Madhya Pradesh, is home to two manufacturing facilities with a combined 
capacity of 500,000 pumps and motors per year (India). Shakti Pumps is at the forefront of sustainable innovation and reliabili ty 
in solar pumping solutions, while also being environmentally responsible. The company has been at the forefront of transforming the agriculture sector through solar pump technology. All Shakti submersible pumps are based on Stainless Steel (SS), which is a testimony to the latest technology and quality in manufacturing. Notably, Shakti Pumps has the distinction of
