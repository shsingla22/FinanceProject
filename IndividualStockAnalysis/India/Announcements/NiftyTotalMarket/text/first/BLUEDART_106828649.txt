PRESS RELEASE  
 
 
Blue Dart Launches International Parcel Connect to Enable India’s Global E -
commerce Aspirations  
 
Mumbai, 26th October  2026:  Blue Dart Express Limited, South Asia’s premier express air 
and integrated transportation and distribution company, announced the launch of 
International Parcel Connect, its B2C cross -border parcel shipping service. Starting with 
eligible shipments from In dia to the United States, the service is designed for e -commerce 
businesses, D2C brands, marketplace sellers, SMEs and growing exporters seeking 
affordable and reliable access to overseas customers.  
International Parcel Connect offers businesses an alternative between premium -priced 
express services and lower -cost postal options. Powered by DHL eCommerce, it combines 
Blue Dart’s capabilities and customer support in India with an established international 
parcel network, offering Standard and Expedited delivery options and shipment visibility.  
Commenting on the launch, Balfour Manuel, Managing Director, Blue Dart , said, “For 
years, our customers have asked us to extend Blue Dart’s trusted service to their 
international shipping needs. International Parcel Connect answers that call with a world -
class solution, combining our operational strengths in India with the reach of ou r 
international network. As Indian enterprises pursue opportunities beyond domestic 
markets, we are committed to providing the dependable logistics connections that turn their 
global aspirations into growth.”  
Dipanjan Banerjee, Chief Commercial Officer, Blue Dart , said, “ India’s B2C e -commerce 
exports are currently estimated at around US$2 billion, reflecting the opportunity for Indian 
businesses to reach overseas customers. For many sellers, the choice has been between 
premium -priced express delivery and lower -cost postal  shipping. International Parcel 
Connect bridges this gap, allowing customers to choose between two delivery speeds to suit 
their needs. Shipment visibility and Blue Dart customer support in India provide greater 
control and a local point of contact, making  cross -border shipping more accessible and 
easier to manage.”  
The service supports eligible B2C parcels weighing 50 g to 11 kg, across categories 
including apparel and fashion products, automobile spare parts, books, handicrafts, 
imitation jewellery and rugs, alongside other eligible marketplace and e -commerce 
produc ts. Shipments are subject to applicable customs, regulatory, commodity, weight and 
dimensional requirements.  
Beginning with the United States, Blue Dart plans to progressively extend International 
Parcel Connect to other relevant markets, including the United Kingdom, countries in the 
European Union, Canada and Australia, to support Indian businesses’ global e -commerce 
aspirations.
