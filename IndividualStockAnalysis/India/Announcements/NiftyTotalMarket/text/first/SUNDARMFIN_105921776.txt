shall retire at every Annual General Meeting.  Section 152 (6)(d) of the

every Annual General Meeting shall be those who have been longest in office 
since their last appointment.  Further, Reg. 17(1A) of the SEBI (LODR) 
Regulations, 2015 provides that the appointment/continuity of a person,

the passing of a Special Resolution.
