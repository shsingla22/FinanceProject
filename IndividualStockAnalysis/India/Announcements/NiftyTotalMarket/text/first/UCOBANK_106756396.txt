Sub: Extension of Additional Charge of the post of Managing Director & Chief 
Executive Officer of UCO Bank 
 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that the Department of 
Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India, vide its letter 
no.4/1/2025-BO.1 (Part I) dated 25th August, 2026, has conveyed that the 
additional charge of the post of Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer 
(MD & CEO) of UCO Bank assigned to Shri Rajendra Kumar Saboo, Executive 
Director, UCO Bank, is hereby extended for a further period of three months 
beyond 31st August, 2026, i.e. up to 30th November, 2026, or until the 
appointment of a regular incumbent to the post of MD & CEO of the Bank, or 
until further orders, whichever is earlier. 
The Bank had earlier intimated the Stock Exchanges vide its disclosure dated 
19th June, 2026 regarding the assignment of the additional charge of the post 
of MD & CEO to Shri Rajendra Kumar Saboo. 
The aforesaid extension is in continuation of the said disclosure.
