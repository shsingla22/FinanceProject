• Revenue growth in the hospital business for the quarter was led by a ~7% increase in  
ARPP and an increase of ~9% in occupied beds compared to Q 2 FY26.  
• The Company  through its wholly owned subsidiary  Fortis Hospotel Limited (FH TL), 
entered into definitive agreements with Seth Sunder Lal Jain Charitable Eye Hospital 
to provide healthcare services to a 400+ bedded super -specialty hospital in Ashok 
Vihar, New Delhi. The ~3.1 acre facility is ex pected to commence operations in 3 –4 
years, subject to necessary approvals, with operationaliz ation of beds  in phases . 
• The Company through its wholly owned subsidiary  International Hospitals Limited  (IHL) 
recently acquired Suguna Hospital, a 110 bedded facility in Rajajinagar, Bengaluru . 
The transaction includes the acquisition of the hospital’s business operations, building 
and land. The facility has potential to expand to ~150 beds.  
• As part of its inorganic growth strategy, the Company aims to further  strengthen its 
presence across key clusters by evaluating strategic M&A and greenfield opportunities  
including built -to-suit (Sale or Lease) models  in established markets such as Mumbai, 
Kolkata and Hyderabad. These growth initiatives are expected to driv e network 
synergies  and strengthen its competitive positioning in these select micro -markets. 
Additionally, the Company is evaluating similar such growth and expansion 
opportunities in  select  attractive and underserved Tier 2 markets across central India 
and in proximity to its key clusters  in order  to further broaden its geographic al footprint .  
 
DIAGNOSTICS  BUSINESS HIGHLIGHTS  
• Revenue mix of the business continued to improve, with the B2C share increasing to 
54% in the current quarter from 52% in Q2FY26.  
• Continuing with its network expansion strategy, primarily the addition of new customer 
touch points (CTPs); total CTPs of Agilus as on 30th September  2026 stood at 4,551. 
• In Q2 FY27, Agilus conducted ~11.39 Mn tests versus ~ 10.62 Mn tests in Q 2 FY26.  
• The preventive and specialized portfolio revenues in Agilus’ overall revenues grew 
28% and 14% respectively in Q2 FY27.

Commenting on the results for the quarter, Dr Ashutosh Raghuvanshi, MD and CEO, 
Fortis Healthcare stated,  “We have witnessed another quarter of sustained growth, 
maintaining our growth trajectory in Q2 FY27 across our hospital and diagnostics businesses.  
We remain committed to strengthening our clinical capabilities and expanding our presence 
in key markets.  We advanced our network expa nsion strategy recently through the acquisition 
of 110 bedded Suguna Hospital in Bengaluru taking the total number of operational beds  in 
the region  to approx. 1 ,000 beds . We also  entered into a long -term healthcare services 
agreement for a 400+ bedded super -specialty hospital in Ashok Vihar, New Delhi. Both t hese 
growth initiatives will further strengthen our presence in the Bengaluru and Delhi NCR clusters  
respectively and suppor t our long -term growth ambitions .” 
He further added,  “Our diagnostics business continued to demonstrate consistent growth and 
strong operational momentum.  With our ongoing capacity expansion initiatives, focus on 
clinical excellence and disciplined executi on of our growth strategy, we remain well positioned 
to capitalise on the growing demand for quality healthcare services in India and deliver 
sustainable growth over the coming years.”  
About Fortis Healthcare Limited:  Fortis Healthcare Limited is a leading  integrated healthcare delivery 
service provider in India. The healthcare verticals of the company primarily comprise hospitals, 
diagnostics and day care specialty facilities. Currently, the company operates 3 7 healthcare facilities 
(including JVs and O&M facilities) across 12 states. The Company’s network comprises over 6,300 
operational beds (including O&M beds) and 400 + diagnostics labs.
