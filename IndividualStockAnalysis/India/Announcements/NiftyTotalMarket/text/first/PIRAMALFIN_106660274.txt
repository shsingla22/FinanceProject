Shares held in Physical Form  
Consequent to the Merger of Piramal Enterprises Limited (‘PEL’), the Company had allotted one equity 
share of PFL for every one equity share of PEL held as on the record date i.e. September 23, 
2025.Accordingly, for the equity shares of PEL, held in the Un claimed Suspense Account and Suspense 

Escrow Demat Account as on the record date, PFL equity shares were issued and allotted in the above 
mentioned ratio, in the respective accounts.  
 
Further, for the equity shares of PEL held in physical form, the Company  had issued and allotted the 
equity shares in an Escrow Demat Account of the Company. Accordingly, since all the shares of the 
Company are held in dematerialized form, the practice of issuing Letters of Confirmation is no longer 
applicable.  
 
In view of the  aforesaid, Members  holding erstwhile PEL shares in physical form are required to follow 
the procedure and formalities i.e. submission of ISR forms and relevant documents to claim their shares 
from the Escrow Demat Account. On completion of all the fo rmalities, the equity shares of PFL will be 
directly credited to their demat account.  
 
In case of any queries, Members may send an email to the Company’s Registrar and Transfer Agent,
