 International Gemological Institute (IGI) delivers strong  Q2FY27  
performance ; Revenue up 31%, EBITDA up by 39% YoY             
 
PRESS  RELEASE:  Mumbai , October 21 , 2026  
 
International  Gemological  Institute  Limited (IGI),  one of the world’s largest independent grading and 
accreditation services providers, reported strong performance for the quarter  and half -year ended 
September 30, 2026.    
Consolidated revenue from operations  grew 31% YoY  to INR 3,980 Mn in Q2 FY27 , while  EBITDA grew 
39% to INR 2,445 Mn. EBITDA Margin  expanded 340 bps to 61.4%, from  58.0 % in Q 2 FY26 . PAT grew 
43% to INR 1,858 Mn, at margin of 46.7%.  
Growth was broad -based across segments, with LGD loose stones up 3 7% YoY, LGD jewelry up 47%, 
natural diamond loose stones up 8%, natural diamond jewelry up 3% and colored gemstones up 7x, 
including AGL . IGI delivered 4.35 Mn reports in the quarter, up 26% YoY.  
For H1 FY27 , revenue from operations grew 27% to INR 7,688 Mn and EBITDA grew 34% to INR 4,683 
Mn. EBITDA Margins rose  from 57.8 % in H1 FY26 to 60.9% in H1 FY27. PAT grew 37% to INR 3,516 
Mn, at a margin of 45.7%. Report volumes grew 22% to 7.91 million.

Industry  Trends  and Strategic  Outlook  
The global gems and jewelry  industry is witnessing a gradual broadening of growth drivers, with value 
creation increasingly shifting beyond traditional diamond categories. Manufacturers, retailers and 
brands are expanding product offerings across lab -grown diamonds, colo red gemstones and 
customised jewelry , reflecting a more diversified consumer landscape and creating new opportunities 
across the industry ecosystem.  
India continues to strengthen its position as a strategic hub for the global jewelry  industry, supported 
by its manufacturing capabilities, skilled workforce and growing domestic consumption. Industry 
stakeholders are increasingly focused on moving up the value chain through branded products, 
innovation and stronger engagement with end co nsumers across international markets.  
Technology is also becoming a more important differentiator across the sector. The adoption of AI, 
automation and digital workflows is helping improve efficiency, consistency and turnaround times 
across various stages of the jewelry  value chain. As product categories expand and consumer 
expectations evolve, industry participants are investing in capabilities that support scalability, 
accuracy and enhanced customer experience.  
Against this backdrop, IGI remains focused on strengthening its global platform through investments 
in technology, laboratory capabilities and market development initiatives. Our diversified exposure 
across natural diamonds, lab -grown diamonds, jewelry  and colored  gemstones provides multiple 
avenues of growth, while our global footprint positions us to benefit from evolving industry trends 
and increasing participation across the broader jewelry  ecosystem.  
 
 
 
 
 
 About  IGI  
The International Gemological Instit ute Limited  (IGI), a Blackstone -backed company, is India’s largest 
independent accreditation and certification services provider with a 50% market share. With 3 7 
laboratories  and 21 Schools  of Gemology  in 10 countries,  IGI's  global  presence  enables  it to capitalize 
on the ongoing transformation in industry . 
For 50 years,  IGI has provided  the fine jewelry  community  and consumers  with  a broad  range  of 
services, including natural  and laboratory -grown diamond reports,  colored stone  grading and  origin 
reports,  as well as jewelry  identification  and appraisal  reports.  With  the rise of lab-grown  diamonds, 
IGI screens millions of natural and lab -grown diamonds to ensure that the industry and consumers 
receive the product they expect. Regardless of location or marketplace, authentic IGI Laboratory 
Reports empower trust and confidence among buyers an d sellers alike.
