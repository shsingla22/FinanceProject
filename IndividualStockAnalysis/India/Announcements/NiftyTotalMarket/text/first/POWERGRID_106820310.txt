Sub.:  POWERGRID declared as successful bidder under TBCB  
 
Dear Sir,  
 
In terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended, this is to inform that Power Grid 
Corporation of India Limited (“POWERGRID”) has been declared as successful bidder 
under Tariff Based Co mpetitive Bidding (“TBCB”) to establish Intra-State Transmission 
System for the project namely “ Establishment of 765 kV GIS Pune West (District - Pune) ” 
on Build, Own, Operate and Transfer (BOOT) basis, at quoted Annual Transmission 
Charges of Rs. 199.5 5 Crore.  The Letter of Intent (LoI) has been received by 
POWERGRID on  16th October, 2026.  
 
The project comprises establishment of new 765/400 kV GIS Pune West Sub -
station in the state of Maharashtra. Further , the project also involves construction of 
765kV and 400kV transmission lines traversing through the state of Maharashtra along 
with the associated bays.  
 
With this project, POWERGRID has secured ten (10) transmission system 
projects under  TBCB in FY 2026 -27 till date, with cumulative Annual Transmission 
Charges of  ₹7,863.63 Crore .
