PRESS RELEASE 
Hon’ble Prime Minister of India Shri Narendra Modi Inaugurates Advanced Mother 
and Child Centre at PGIMER, Chandigarh, Supported by Infosys Foundation 
• Infosys Foundation commits over INR 147 crore towards critical medical 
equipment for the institute’s Advanced Mother and Child Center  
• Over 25,000 pregnant women and newborns expected to benefit annually  
Bengaluru, India – July 22, 2026: Infosys Foundation, the philanthropic and CSR arm of  Infosys , today 
announced the inauguration of an advanced mother and neonatal care facilities wing at the Postgraduate 
Institute of Medical Education and Research (PGIMER), Chandigarh, which will help strengthen the 
institute’s maternity and neonatal care facilities. Through this collaboration, Infosys Foundation committed 
a grant of over INR 147 crore towards critical medical equipment for the institute’s newly opened Advanced 
Mother and Child Centre (AMCC), which will help reduce maternal and neonatal mortality rates and provide 
low-income families access to high- quality and affordable healthcare.  
  
The Advanced Mother and Child Centre (AMCC) was inaugurated by the Hon'ble Prime Minister of India, 
Shri Narendra Modi , in the presence of; Shri Gulab Chand Kataria, Hon’ble Governor of Punjab & 
Administrator, UT Chandigarh; Shri J.P. Nadda, Hon’ble Union Minister for Health and Family 
Welfare; Shri Ashwini Vaishnaw, Union Minister for Railways ; Shri Manish Tewari , Member of 
Parliament , Chandigarh; Mr. Sunil Kumar Dhareshwar, Trustee, Infosys Foundation ; Prof Vivek Lal, 
Director, P GIMER,  along with other senior government officials and executives.  
  
The AMCC is equipped with triage and emergency services for pregnant women, labor and delivery units, 
resuscitation facilities, obstetric intensive care and high dependency units for specialized care, neonatal 
intensive care units, modular operation theatr es, a blood bank, a human milk bank, and a kangaroo mother 
care ward, among other care facilities. The center will aim to benefit over 25,000 pregnant women and newborns annually.  
 
Prof Vivek Lal, Director, Postgraduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh,  
said, “Strengthening maternal and neonatal healthcare remains a national priority as India works towards 
building a healthier and more inclusive future.   I am deeply indebted to the Hon'ble Prime Minister of India, 
Shri Narendra Modi ji, Hon’ble Union Minister for Health and Family Welfare, Shri J.P. Nadda Ji, and the 
Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, for having dedicated this Advanced Mother and Child Center 
(AMCC), at the Postgraduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh, to the nation.   This 
center will play an important role in expanding access to quality care for mothers and newborns. The Ayushman Scheme launched by the Hon’ble Prime Minister and the Hon’ble Union Minister for Health and 
 
Family Welfare, is providing cutting edge healthcare free of cost to underprivileged patients. I deeply 
appreciate the contribution of Infosys Foundation in their ongoing endeavor in strengthening healthcare 
across India to positively impact the underserved communities.”    
 
Sunil Kumar Dhareshwar, Trustee, Infosys Foundation, said “The great strides that India is making in 
its socio -economic development must be supplemented by advancements in healthcare facilities to build a 
stronger nation. Our support to PGIMER is in line with the Infosys Foundation’s commitment to supporting  
transformative healthcare initiatives that are inclusive and create a lasting impact on society. By providing 
sophisticated critical care medical equipment to AMCC, we aim to provide enhanced support towards 
maternal and neonatal care.”
