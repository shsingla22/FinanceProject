Allied Blenders and Distillers Wins Silver at Global LACP Vision Awards  2023/24  
Annual Report Competition  
Mumbai, 24th March  2025  – Allied Blenders and Distillers Ltd. (ABD), India’s third -largest spirits 
company, has earned global recognition  at the prestigious 2023/24 Vision Awards Annual 
Report Competition , hosted by the League of American Communications Professionals (LACP) . 
ABD has been  honoured with the Silver Award  in Food Beverage category ( upto $1 bn revenues) , 
placing its annual report among the most outstanding corporate communications worldwide.  
ABD’s annual report has been  ranked amongst  Top Annual Reports Globally , a noteworthy  
accomplishment  in a competition that drew nearly 1,000 entries from over 20 countries, 
representing top -tier brands across diverse industries.  
From design and storytelling to accessibility , ABD’s report stood out across every judging 
category. The company received near -perfect scores in First Impression , Creativity , Message 
Clarity , and full marks in Narrative, Financials, Accessibility, and Shareholder Letter , a testament 
to the report’s excellence in both form and substance.  
Speaking about the achievement, Alok Gupta, Managing Director of ABD , said, “At ABD , we have 
always believed that true leadership is measured not just by market share, but by the clarity with 
which a company defines its purpose and communicates it. This recognition from the LACP is a 
powerful affirmation of the standards we hold ourselve s to. Our annual report reflects the story 
of transformation, of a company evolving with intent and responsibility. To be acknowledged on 
a global platform is not only an ho nour but also a reminder of the responsibility we carry as an 
Indian spirits company building its voice on the world stage.”  

ABD’s global recognition highlights its rise as a leader not just in the alco -bev market, but also in 
corporate transparency and communication. As the company scales its premium portfolio, this 
honour reflects its commitment to clarity, credibility, and wo rld-class standards.  
 
 About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited ( www.abdindia.com ) 
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD) is the third largest Spirits company in India, in terms of annual sales 
volumes between FY14 and FY24. ABD has a presence in five main Flavors, i.e., whisky, brandy, rum, vodka, 
and gin, with ‘millionaire’ brand s like Officer’s Choice Whisky, Officer’s Choice Blue Whisky, Sterling Reserve 
Premium Whiskies and ICONiQ White Whisky. Currently, its manufacturing network comprises 36 units, of 
which 9 are owned bottling units, 2 owned distilleries, and 25 non -owned ma nufacturing units.
