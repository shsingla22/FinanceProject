c. A brief on types of customers:
As a global digital retailer, we engage with customers through our 24x7 proprietary television shopping 
channels and multiple digital platforms like proprietary website, mobile applications and marketplaces. Our 
offerings include fashion jewellery, gemstones, and a curated range of lifestyle products comprising home 
décor, hair care, beauty care, apparels and accessories. A key consumer segment for us continues to be 
Gen-X and Baby Boomers, driven by factors such as convenience, highest disposable income mix, and strong 
alignment with our product offering. Additionally, we also serve select B2B clients on an opportunity-driven 
basis. We engage in B2B to stay aligned with evolving jewellery trends and to keep pace with advancements 
in manufacturing technologies. This also allows us to ensure efficient operations and optimum utilisation of our 
manufacturing capacities.
