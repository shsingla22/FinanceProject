Subject: Update- Hon’ble High Court of Delhi sets aside the Debarment imposed by the Ministry of

Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“SEBI LODR Regulations ”), and in continuation of the Company’s earlier intimation dated October 
11, 2025 regarding the order passed by the Ministry of External Affairs (“ MEA”) imposing a Debarment 
on BLS International Services Limited (“ the Company ”) from participation in future tenders of the MEA 
and India Mission abroad for period of two (2) years, we wish to inform you of the following 
development: 
 
The Company had challenged the aforesaid debarment order by filing a writ petition before the Hon’ble 
High Court of Delhi. The Company has received an order from the Hon’ble High Court of Delhi setting 
aside the debarment order passed by the MEA. Consequently, the debarment imposed on the 
Company pursuant to the said order stands quashed.  
 
This intimation is being made in compliance with the applicable provisions of the SEBI LODR Regulations 
and for the information of the Stock Exchanges and stakeholders. 
 
Thanking you, 
 
Kindly take the same on your record and bring notice to all the stakeholders concerned.
