About DOMINO S.r.l : 
Founded in 1951, Domino S.r.l. began its journey by designing and manufacturing mechanical motorcycle levers. Over the decades, the company has continuously evolved  - expanding its 
expertise from mechanical components to advanced electronic systems, including ride  - by - 
wire throttles, control devices, quick shifters, handlebars, and grips.  
Thanks to its internationally recognized technical know- how, Domino holds a strong position 
in the OEM market, supplying leading European and global motorcycle manufacturers.  
Domino products are also deeply rooted in the world of racing  - on both road and off -road 
circuits  - equipping top -tier teams that have gone on to win world championship titles. This 
ongoing presence in motorsport serves as both a proving ground and a constant driver of 
innovation, pushing the company forward.
