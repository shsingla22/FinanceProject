Press Release  
IRCTC Partners with Quality Council of India (QCI) for Digital, Third-Party Food Safety and 
Hygiene Audit 
New Delhi, 15.10.2026:  In a major move to transform the on-board dining experience for 
millions of daily rail passengers, the Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation 
Limited (IRCTC)  has signed a strategic Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Quality 
Council of India (QCI) . This high-stakes partnership establishes a rigorous, technology-driven, 
independent third-party audit mechanism to ensure the highest standards of food safety and 
hygiene across all IRCTC-managed mobile (trains) and static (station) catering units. 
Under this strategic alliance, QCI will deploy certified professionals to conduct intense, 
unannounced quarterly and half-yearly inspection cycles. These audits will enforce strict 
compliance with statutory Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)  regulations 
and international culinary standards. 
The MoU was signed by Sh. Sudhir Warrier, General Manager/Quality & Projects, IRCTC, and Sh. 
Subroto Ghosh, Principal Advisor/Head-PPID, Quality Council of India, in the presence of Sh. 
Ramanand Nagender Shukla, CEO,NABL and Sh. Mohit Padhi, Asst.Secretary General from Quality 
Council of India and Sh. Rahul Himalian, Chairman & Managing Director and Sh. Manoj Kumar 
Sharma, Director/Catering Services from IRCTC. 
To eliminate data tampering and guarantee absolute transparency, the audit process will leverage 
QCI's pioneer digital infrastructure. Inspectors will use a proprietary mobile application featuring 
mandatory geo-tagging, time-stamping, and photographic evidence . Audit reports will be 
submitted within a strict 24-hour turnaround window , enabling real-time detection of 
operational gaps and triggering immediate Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) tracking 
against defaulting vendors. 
Key Public Benefits & Impact Areas: 
 Zero-Compromise Onboard Dining:  Rigorous independent screening across trains ensures 
passengers are protected from subpar onboard meal preparation. 
 Consistent Quality at Stations:  Complete standardization guarantees immaculate cooking 
environments across all major station-based static units. 
 Strict Vendor Accountability:  Digital evidence-backed reports submitted within 24 hours ensure 
rapid penalty or corrective loops for catering licensees. 
 Visible Hygiene & Grooming Protocols:  On-ground checks will strictly enforce staff medical 
fitness, mandatory protective gear, and professional hospitality etiquette. 
 Sanitary Storage Compliance:  Enhanced monitoring ensures the absolute segregation of 
vegetarian and non-vegetarian raw items, temperature-controlled meal holding, and verified pest 
control. 
 Future-Proof Upgrades:  The umbrella agreement establishes an institutional mechanism 
allowing IRCTC to seamlessly introduce future joint programs, including Passenger Customer 
Satisfaction Surveys, infrastructure audits, and specialized staff training. 
 
 
Speaking on the partnership, representatives highlighted QCI’s unmatched credentials in 
executing complex, mega-scale public monitoring projects—such as SwachhSurvekshan  and Pan-
India Milk & Food Surveillance for FSSAI—making them the ideal choice to spearhead this 
nationwide railway hospitality upgrade. 
This partnership marks a definitive shift toward a transparent, passenger-first future, ensuring 
every meal served on Indian Railways aligns with elite safety and health protocols. 
About IRCTC 
Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation Ltd. (IRCTC) is a 'Navratna' listed company 
under Ministry of Railways, Government of India and is the professional hospitality and travel arm 
of Indian Railways incorporate for upgradation of catering services over Indian Railways and 
promote rail-bound domestic and inbound tourism in the country. 
About Quality Council of India (QCI) 
Established in 1996 through a joint Cabinet decision, the Quality Council of India (QCI) is the 
nation's apex autonomous quality body operating under the Department for Promotion of 
Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce & Industry, Government of India. It 
specializes in large-scale nationwide quality benchmarking, third-party assessments, and strategic 
governance audits.
