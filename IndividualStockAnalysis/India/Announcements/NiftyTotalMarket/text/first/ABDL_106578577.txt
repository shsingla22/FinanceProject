Allied Blenders & Distillers Onboards  Industry Stalwart  Amar Sinha  
 ~Three decades of experience to lead next phase of ABD growth ~  
 
Mumbai , 2nd April 2026  – Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD), India’s largest domestic  
spirits company by volume, announced that the Board of Directors approved the appointment 
of Mr. Amar Sinha as Managing Director ( MD)  Designate  effective  2nd April 2026 . 
As Mr. Alok Gupta’s tenure as Managing Director near s completion , Mr. Amar Sinha’s  
appointment a ligns with ABD’s strategy of value creation  and long -term growth and  
underscores  the Company’s commitment to  a seamless leadership transition .  
Mr. Amar Sinha brings over three decades of leadership experience  across the alco beverage  
and consumer sectors . Over the course of his career, he has led corporations  through phases 
of transformation and growth while building strong brands and businesses . His leadership 
journey includes roles as Chief Operating Officer at Radico Khaitan, Managing Director at 
Whyte & Mackay India and executive position s at Herbertsons  (UB Group ) and others . 
He has received several recognitions for leadership excellence, including the Rajeev Gandhi 
Excellence Award – Most Enterprising CEO of the Year (2015), the Make in India Excellence 
Award (2016), and Business Leader of the Year at the IndSpirit Awards in 2023 .  
With t his deep -rooted expertise across  sales, marketing, and business transformation , Mr. 
Amar Sinha  is uniquely positioned to accelerate ABD ’s next chapter of market  leadership and 
growth . 
Commenting on the appointment, Mr. Kishor e Chhabria, Non -Executive Chairman , ABD , said,  
“I would lik e to thank Alok for the leadership and commitment he has brought to ABD over the 
years , including his role in strengthening the governance framework  and the company’s 
presence in the Indian alco -beverage industry. As we look ahead to the next phase of our 
journey, I am pleased to welcome Amar. With his deep industry experience, I am confident that 
he will successfully  build on the momentum we have created.”  
 
 
 
 
 
Mr. Alok Gupta, Managing Director, ABD , added,  "It has been a wonderful journey leading 
ABD , and I take great  pride in the progress and milestones achieved together.  With the 
strength of our brands, dedication of our teams , and the strategic foundation we have built, 
ABD is well positioned for its next phase of growth.”  
Sharing his thoughts on joining the company, Mr. Amar Sinha, Managing Director  - Designate , 
ABD  said, “I am delighted  to be joining ABD  at this important stage of the journey. ABD has 
built a strong presence in the Indian alco -beverage industry over the years, with a solid 
portfolio of brands. I look forward to working with the team and driving strategy for the next 
phase of growth.”   
 
About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited ( www.abdindia.com ) 
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD) is the largest domestic Spirits company in India, in terms of 
annual sales volumes. ABD has a presence in five main flavors, i.e., whisky, brandy, rum, vodka, and gin, 
with ‘millionaire’ brands like Officer’s Ch oice Whisky, Officer’s Choice Blue Whisky, Sterling Reserve 
Premium Whiskies and ICONiQ White Whisky. Currently, its manufacturing network comprises 38 units, of 
which 9 are owned bottling units, 1 is owned PET bottle manufacturing plant, 2 owned distiller ies, and 26 
non-owned manufacturing units.
