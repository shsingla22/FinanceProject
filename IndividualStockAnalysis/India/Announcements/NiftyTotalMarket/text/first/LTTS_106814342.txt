Bengaluru (India), October 12, 2026 : L&T Technology Services (BSE: 540115, NSE: LTTS) , a 
global leader in Engineering Intelligence Solutions and ER&D services, today inaugurated its AI-Native 
EI Lab for Digital Manufacturing in Bengaluru, an experiential environment where customers and 
partners can see AI-Native Manufacturing, digital twins and autonomous systems working together 
across the lifecycles. 
Inauguration of the EI Lab for Manufacturing and Operations at LTTS’ Bengaluru Campus
The EI Lab extends LTTS’ Engineering Intelligence  charter to the manufacturing shop floor. Through 
live and scenario-based demonstrations, customers can move from digital engineering and virtual 
validation to connected production, intelligent operations and autonomous systems, and see the 
integration pathways that make each of them scale. 

REGISTERED OFFICE: L&T House, N.M. Marg, Ballard Estate, Mumbai - 400 001                                                                    IN: L72900MH2012PLC232169 The EI Lab is built around multiple capability pillars:
●Manufacturing Intelligence:  An AI-powered Manufacturing Command Center with executive 
decision-intelligence, manufacturing data fabric with knowledge graph, a supply chain control 
tower supported by AI-powered production planning and multi-plant benchmarking.
●AI-Native Factory:  Here intelligent machines, robots, cobots, drones, autonomous systems 
and digital twins work together to execute manufacturing tasks with minimal human 
intervention. This layer has an NVIDIA accelerated environment combining NVIDIA 
Omniverse , NVIDIA Isaac Sim , NVIDIA PhysX , NVIDIA Cosmos , NVIDIA NeMo , and 
OpenUSD-based digital twin workflows, accelerated visualization and technologies for 
Industrial and Physical AI. 
●Operations AI & Autonomy:  A verification gate that checks actions generated by agent 
swarms before they reach a machine, enabling verifiable, governed execution and safer 
autonomous decision-making across connected manufacturing environments. This layer 
encompasses factory design & simulation, production, process & quality control, 
maintenance and sustainability. 
●LTTS’ Unified Intelligence platform: EIOne , the Engineering Intelligence platform that 
brings together 100+ EI solutions and agentic AI swarms into a single, governed ecosystem. 
It empowers enterprises to rapidly build, deploy, orchestrate, and scale AI-driven engineering 
& manufacturing workflows while ensuring enterprise-grade security, governance, and 
operational control.
“AI is transforming how manufacturers design, simulate and operate the factories of the future ,” said 
Vishal Dhupar, Managing Director for South Asia at NVIDIA. “The EI Lab, combining NVIDIA 
technologies spanning hardware, software and world models and LTTS’ deep engineering expertise, 
gives manufacturers a practical environment to explore and validate AI-driven, simulation-first 
approaches to industrial transformation.”
“The EI Lab for Manufacturing brings Engineering Intelligence into a tangible manufacturing 
environment, allowing customers to experience how engineering, AI and physical systems can come 
together to address real-world challenges. Our collaboration with NVIDIA adds advanced simulation 
and accelerated computing capabilities to this environment, while our engineering & manufacturing 
expertise helps translate these technologies into practical manufacturing solutions for our global 
customers ,” said Amit Chadha, CEO & Managing Director, L&T Technology Services and 
NASSCOM Executive Council Member .
