Firstsource appoints Akash Pugalia as  
President , Global Trust & Safety  Practice  
  
Mumbai, India | May 10, 2024:  Firstsource Solutions Limited (NSE: FSL, BSE:532809), a leading global 
provider of Business Process Management (BPM) services and an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company, has 
announced the appointment of Akash Pugalia as President , Global Trust & Safety . Based in the San 
Francisco Bay Area, he will spearhead the growth and expansion of the Trust & Safety practice on a global 
scale.  
 
With over two decades of experience in the BPM industry,  specifically in the Media, Entertainment, Gaming, 
and Trust & Safety sectors,  Akash brings a transformative leadership style and a proven track record of 
success to  this role.  Akash is known  for prioritizing  client satisfaction, stakeholder trust, and operational 
excellence, fostering enduring relationships and driving significant growth.  Akash has played a pivotal role 
in catapulting a $50 million business into a $1 billion powerhouse within a mere four years. His dedication 
to innovation is evident through his embrace of cutting- edge AI solutions for proactive threat detection, 
showcasing  his forward- looking approach to cybersecurity. He has also spearheaded the creation of a non-
platform- based advisory council focused on tackling online threats.  
 
Ritesh Idnani, Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer at Firstsource Solutions , shares , “With 
every leap of technology come boundless opportunities as well as  significant  compliance and security risks. 
At Firstsource, we grasp this duality, acknowledging the imperative of offering clients the best of talent and 
technology to build brand  trust, while addressing evolving vulnerabilities.  Our Trust & Safety practice  has 
grown steadily,  leverag ing technology to enable best -in-class detection and prevention practices for clients.   
We're thrilled to welcome Akash, whose extensive experience and dedication to excellence positions  him as 
the ideal leader to propel this  practice to new heights. " 
 
Akash Pugalia,  President , Global Trust & Safety at Firstsource Solutions , says,  "A robust Trust & Safety 
practice is paramount in today's digital landscape.  It serves as the bedrock for fostering user confidence and 
maintaining brand integrity. In the digital age of today, trust is the currency that influences every user 
experience. I am excited to join Firstsour ce and work closely with this incredible team to build that currency 
for our clients. Firstsource has the technology and talent to maximize this capability and build it into a top 
suite service, and I look forward to lead that charge. "
