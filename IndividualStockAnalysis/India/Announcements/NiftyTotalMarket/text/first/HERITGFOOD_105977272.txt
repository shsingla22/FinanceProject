PRESS RELEASE  
 
Heritage Foods Limited Contributed ₹1 Crore each to  Andhra 
Pradesh State Disaster Management Authority and to Telangana 
State Disaster Management Authority to Support Flood Relief 
Efforts.  
  
Hyderabad, 4th September,2024 : Heritage Foods Limited, a leading dairy 
company, has announced a contribution of ₹1 crore each to the Andhra 
Pradesh State Disaster Management Authority and Telangana  State Disaster 
Management Authority . This contribution is made in support of the ongoing 
flood relief efforts in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, where recent floods have 
caused widespread devastation, affecting lakhs of fa milies and communities. 
The contribution from Heritage Foods aims to assist in the ongoing relief and 
rehabilitation efforts, providing much -needed aid to those affected by this 
natural disaster.  
  
The donation is part of Heritage Foods Limited's commitment to corporate 
social responsibility and its dedication to supporting communities in times of 
need. The company's contribution will assist the state governments in providing 
essential relief and reh abilitation to those affected by the floods, helping rebuild 
lives and restore normalcy.  
  
 
 
 
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mrs. Nara Bhuvaneswari , Vice Chairperson and 
Managing Director of Heritage Foods Limited, said, "At Heritage Foods, we 
believe in standing by our communities, especially in times of crisis. The recent 
floods in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana have impacted many lives, and we 
are c ommitted to supporting the states’ efforts in providing relief and rebuilding 
the affected areas. We trust our contribution will make a meaningful difference 
in the lives of those who have been affected."  
  
Heritage Foods Limited has a long -standing tradition of contributing to social 
causes and has been actively involved in various community welfare activities. 
This contribution to the Andhra Pradesh State Disaster Management Authority 
and Telangana State Disaster Management Authority  to Support Flood Relief 
Efforts, is a testament to the company's enduring commitment to the welfare of 
society.
