Dear Sir/Madam, 
This is in continuation of our above referred letter on the captioned subject. It may be 
mentioned that as per the requirement of Clause 6.9 of Section VII-A of Chapter VII of SEBI Master 
Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30/01/2026, the matter in relation 
to NSE notice for non-compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI LODR'1 was placed before the Board of Directors of RailTel in their 
meeting held on 02/02/2026 for their comments on the same. 
2. It may be brought out that RailTel's Board has considered the matter and after due deliberations, 
expressed its views that since the power to appoint Directors including Independent Directors on the 
Board of the Company vests with the President of India acting through Ministry of Railways, it was 
beyond the control of the Company to ensure compliance with the provisions of Regulation 17(1) of 
the SEBI LODR, as the Company cannot on its own induct or appoint Independent Directors on the 
Board of the Company. The Board requested to waive off the fine/penalty for the reported non­
compliance. 
3. Accordingly, it is requested to kindly waive-off the fine levied on RailTel for non-compliance of SEBI 
LODR during the Quarter ended 30/09/2025.
