Government Revises ACC PLI Timelines for Ola Electric; 
Company Secures Full Five -Year Incentive Window 
Through CY2031, Unlocking Up to ₹7,240 Crore  
 
* MHI revises timelines of PLI scheme for Ola Electric's battery unit.  
* First milestone of 6 GWh by quarter -end, well ahead of the revised December 2026 timeline.  
* PLI incentives to be disbursed quarterly over the next  five years beginning next quarter.  
* Accelerates India's only operational ACC PLI gigafactory towards 20 GWh and Ola 
Electric's broader  energy roadmap . 
Bengaluru, August 12, 2026: Ola Electric Mobility Limited today announced that the Ministry 
of Heavy Industries (MHI), Government of India, has approved revised timelines under the 
ACC Production Linked Incentive Scheme for Ola Cell Techn ologies Private Limited (OCT), 
its wholly owned subsidiary.  
The MHI revision is much more than a timeline extension. The approval secures a full five -
year PLI window through CY2031 for Ola Electric's 20 GWh allocation and unlocks up to 
₹7,240 crore in cum ulative PLI incentives. Disbursements will be made quarterly, beginning 
next quarter, creating a recurring incentive stream as Ola Electric scales its cell business.  
Ola Electric currently has 2.5 GWh of installed cell -manufacturing capacity, with a furth er 3.5 
GWh under installation. The company will reach 6 GWh by the end of the current quarter, 
achieving the initial installed -capacity milestone well ahead of the revised December 2026 
timeline. The MHI decision has effectively extended the original timel ines by two years.  
Lithium cells are becoming a foundational technology across electric mobility, energy storage, 
drones, robotics and next -generation industrial systems. Building these capabilities in India 
will strengthen the country's energy security a nd technology independence while creating a 
globally competitive domestic battery ecosystem.  
Ola Electric is building a multi -chemistry cell platform spanning NMC and LFP technologies, 
supported by indigenous R&D, increased localisation of battery materia ls, improved 
manufacturing yield, and closed -loop material recovery.  
Bhavish Aggarwal, Chairman and Managing Director, Ola Electric, said:  
"The revised timeline is more than an extension. It transforms the economics of our cell 
business by converting an earlier milestone overhang into a five -year, quarterly PLI 
opportunity of up to ₹7,240 crore.  
We hadn't factored any incentives into our business projections after overshooting the original 
timelines. Ola is now well ahead of the government's revised sch edule, and enables us to access 
the full potential of ₹7240 cr and receive disbursement as soon as next quarter."  
At the company's annual Sankalp event on this Independence Day, it will unveil its  broader 
energy product roadmap -including Shakti, Mahashakt i, and the next phase of its energy 
platform.  
 
 
About Ola Electric:  
Ola Electric Mobility Limited is an electric vehicle and energy technology company with 
vertically integrated capabilities spanning electric vehicles, battery cells and critical vehicle 
components.  
Its manufacturing ecosystem includes the Ola Futurefactory and an operational Gigafactory in 
Tamil Nadu, supported by the Bengaluru -based Battery Innovation Centre, which focuses on 
advancing next -generation cell and battery technologies.
