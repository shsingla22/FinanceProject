 Media Release  
 
Refex eVeelz set to expands its EV Fleet with 1,000 Electric Cars in Strategic Partnership with Leading 
Ride-Hailing Platform  
 
Chennai, March 04, 2025  – In a significant step towards accelerating sustainable urban mobility, Refex 
Green Mobility Limited (“ Refex eVeelz ”), a wholly owned subsidiary of Refex Industries Limited, has 
entered into an agreement with one of India’s largest ride-hailing platforms  to induct and operate 
1,000 electric four-wheelers (e4Ws)  across Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Mumbai  in FY26. 
This expansion is a part of the company’s strategic growth roadmap and reinforces its commitment to 
India’s clean mobility transition. 
  
With a 100% electric fleet , this initiative marks another milestone in Refex eVeelz’s mission to drive 
large-scale EV adoption while reducing urban carbon footprints by minimizing tailpipe emissions. The 
ride-hailing platform will continue to cater to demand and build new mobility categories, while Refex 
eVeelz will focus on seamless service delivery, ensuring reliable and sustainable transportation 
solutions . 
  
"At Refex eVeelz, we are committed to playing a leading role in shaping the future of sustainable 
mobility in India. This strategic expansion not only strengthens our presence across key metropolitan 
cities but also aligns with our long-term vision of enabling a cleaner, smarter, and more efficient 
transportation ecosystem,"  said Mr. Anil Jain, Managing Director of Refex Industries Limited , the 
holding company of Refex eVeelz.  
 
Already a leader in four-wheeler EV-as-a-service, Refex eVeelz operates a fleet of nearly 1,300 electric 
vehicles at present, across multiple cities such as Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai, and Pune. 
The addition of 1,000 new e4Ws, combined with the organic growth of its fleet size in the employee 
transportation segment, will further solidify its role in driving the mainstream adoption of electric 
mobility solutions across India. 
 
This initiative aligns with India’s broader sustainability goals, contributing to the nation’s long-term 
vision of net-zero emissions by 2070. By focusing on scalable, tech-enabled operations and strategic 
partnerships, Refex eVeelz continues to pave the way for a greener, more sustainable urban 
transportation ecosystem.
