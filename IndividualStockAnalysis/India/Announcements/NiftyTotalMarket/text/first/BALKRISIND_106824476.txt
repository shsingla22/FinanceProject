M
ilan/Mumbai, 22 October 2026 - Pirelli and Balkrishna Industries Ltd.  (BKT) have signed a strategic 
agreement aimed at capturing the opportunities offered by the Indian car  tyre market, one of the most 
dynamic and promising at the global level.  
I
ndia is today  one of the most attractive automotive markets  in the world, with car registrations estimated 
to increase by  5-7% annually from 2025 to 2030 and a premium Car  tyre market seen growing almost 
fivefold.  
The partnership - which unites  Pirelli’s  high-end technological leadership with BKT ’s local 
industrial  positioning and strong presence – foresees a first phase based on an off-
take agreement,  through which BKT will produce, in one of its  own newly constructed plants  equipped 
with the most advanced technology,  up to 800,000 Pirelli branded tyres  by 2030 with  Pirelli’s specific 
technological and quality standards.   
T
his agreement paves the way for a second phase which  envisages , subject to  certain conditions, the  
creation of a 50- 50 joint venture and the construction of a production plant dedicated to High 
Value  car tyres, both with the Pirelli brand and the BKT brand to serve India and other international 
markets. 
F
or Pirelli,  with only a commercial presence in India at present, the agreement constitutes a significant 
step towards reinforcing its positioning in a Country characterized by great potential  and growing demand 
for High Value  tyres, the segment where Pirelli is focused.   
F
or BKT, the agreement supports the company’s expansion into the passenger car tyre segment and is 
expected to create new opportunities in both domestic and international markets.

arco Tronchetti Provera, Executive Chairman of Pirelli, said: “India is taking an ever more central 
role in the evolution of the global automotive industry, thanks to the market’s growth, development of 
mobility and progressive strengthening of the Country’s industrial and technological competencies . The 
agreement with BKT, a first-class industrial partner with solid roots in India and a significant international 
presence, represents for P irelli a strategic choice which will allow us to strength en our position in a market 

among those with the great est growth potential and to accom pany its evolution,  drawing on our 
competencies, innovation and positioning in the segments with the greatest value”.  
 
Arvind Poddar, C hairman  and Managing Director of Balkrishna Industries Ltd. (BKT)  said: “This

Andrea Casaluci, CEO of Pirelli,  added : “We are confident that the collaboration between BKT, with its

About Pirelli 
Founded in Milan in 1872, Pirelli is today one of the world's largest tyre manufacturers and the only one fully focused on the Consumer 
market, with products and services for cars, motorbikes, and bicycles. Positioned at the top of the range in the High Value, Premium, and 
Prestige segments, the brand combines deep Italian roots  with international scale, supported by 18 plants in 12 countries, a commercial 
network in over 160 countries, around 31,000 employees, and €6.8 billion in revenues in 2025. Driven by cutting- edge technology and 
sustainability, Pirelli invested 5.6% of its  2025 High Value revenues in R&D. Its flagship technology is Cyber ™ Tyre, the world’s first 
system that collects data from sensors embedded in the tyre and communicates it in real time to the vehicle’s electronics, increasing 
safety, vehicle efficiency and supporting road infrastructure monitoring. Demonstrating its environmental leadership, Pirelli is also the 
world's first company to produce FSC -certified tyres  using sustainable natural rubber and rayon, an achievement reflected in its top 
standings on global sustainability benchmarks like the Dow Jones Best -In-Class World and Europe indices. Pirelli’s legacy also extends 
into sports and culture. Competing in mo torsports since 1907, the company is active in over 350 competitions and has been the exclusive 
Global Tyre  Partner of Formula One™ since 2011, with a contract running through 2028. Beyond racing, Pirelli has published the iconic 
Pirelli Calendar since 1963, fosters design and lifestyle projects through Pirelli Design, manages the Pirelli HangarBicocca contemporary 
art space, and pres erves its heritage and culture via Fondazione Pirelli.  
 
About BKT  
Established in 1987, Balkrishna Industries Ltd. (BKT) is an Indian multinational and a leading Off -Highway tyre manufacturer 
headquartered in Mumbai. With a portfolio of over 3,600 specialized products for agriculture, industrial, construction, mining, earthmoving, 
and material handling applications, the company serves customers in more than 160 countries. The company also manufactures rubber 
tracks for the agricultural and industrial sectors. Recently, BKT announced its entry into the Two- Wheeler, Passenger Car, and Commercial 
Vehicle segments for the Indian market. The company’s vertically integrated manufacturing ecosystem is supported by in- house carbon 
black production and R&D centers focused on product innovation, performance, and application  specific design.  
Driven by a strong commitment to innovation, sustainability, and customer -centric solutions, BKT continues to expand its global footprint 
while investing in advanced technologies, manufacturing capabilities, and next -generation mobility solutions. The company’s focus on 
developing high- performance products tailored to the evolving needs of its customers enables BKT to strengthen its position as a trusted 
partner across diverse markets and applications. Through continuous investment in research and development, operational excellence, 
and sustainable practices, BKT aims to create long- term value while supporting the changing demands of the global mobility industry.
