Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 201 5 
and in furtherance to our intimation dated August 05, 2025, this is to inform you that the Company 
has today i.e. on July 27 , 2026, entered into the following Amendment Agreements:  
 
1) Power Supply and Offtake Amendment Agreement with Pro -Zeal Green Power E leven Private 
Limited (‘SPV’) for supply of 3.30 MW captive hybrid - wind & solar power at the Company’s 
Surat Unit, in addition to the supply of 6.60 MW captive hybrid - wind & solar power, under 
the original Power Supply and Offtake Agreement with SPV;  
 
2) Investment and Shareholders' Amendment Agreement with Prozeal Green Power Private 
Limited (‘PGPPL’) and SPV, for investment by the Company for an amount not exceeding ₹3.63 
crores by way of Compulsory Convertible Debentures in the SPV representing 26%, in  one or 
more tranches (and balance 74% investment will be made by PGPPL ), to secure the additional 
supply as mentioned above. This will be in addition to the investment by the Company for an 
amount upto ₹6.60 crores, under  the original Investment and Shareholders' Agreement.  
 
The above arrangement will facilitate significant savings on power costs of the Company whilst 
progressing the Company’s sustainability initiatives. In this regard, please find enclosed Annexure A .
