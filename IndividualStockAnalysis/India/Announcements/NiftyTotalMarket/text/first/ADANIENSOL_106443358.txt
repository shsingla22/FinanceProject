In this regard, it may be noted that Battery Energy Storage Systems ( “BESS ”) are 
being developed under the Adani Group’s integrated utility platform, which 
includes Adani Green Energy Limited (“AGEL”), Adani Energy Solutions Limited 
(“AESL”) and Adani Power Limited (“APL”).  
 
To our knowledge, currently, AGEL will implement Adani’s first BESS project, 
with a capacity of 1,126 MW / 3,530 MWh. As such, at this stage, there is no 
event which triggers any disclosure requirements for the Company, under the 
applicable regulations.   
 
Given the integration with transmission and distribution assets, and the 
transformative potential of BESS for the grid and power sector, the Adani Group, 
in the future, will evaluate opportunities for the Company and APL  to contribute 
their expertise and add value to this initiative as part of AGEL’s 50 GW 
renewable energy roadmap by 2030.
