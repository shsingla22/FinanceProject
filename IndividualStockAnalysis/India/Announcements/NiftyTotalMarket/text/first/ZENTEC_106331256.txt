Zen Technologies Protects Core IP – A Competing Patent Revoked in Landmark Win
Zen Technologies  Limited  (“Zen”)  has successfully  safeguarded  its pre-existing  intellectual  
property  (IP) by securing  the revocation  of a subsequently  granted  Indian  patent  filed under  
a similar  category  by individuals  associated  with MAG 5 Innovations  Private  Limited . 
The patent,  originally  granted  in April  2023 , was revoked  on August  06, 2025 , following  a 
post-grant  opposition  initiated  by Zen in October  2023 . 
This outcome  reaffirms  Zen’s  strong  intellectual  property  position  and its ongoing  
commitment  to safeguarding  the company’s  innovation -led advancements  in combat  
training  simulation  technology .
Commenting  on the development,  a spokesperson  from  Zen’s  Intellectual  Property  team  
said, “This  decision  is a significant  affirmation  of Zen’s  pioneering  work  in simulation -
based  defence  training . We remain  dedicated  to protecting  our proprietary  technologies  
that are central  to our leadership  in this domain .”
The revocation  reinforces  Zen’s  stature  as an IP-rich defence  technology  company  and 
provides  continued  confidence  to shareholders  and stakeholders  in its long-term innovation  
roadmap  and patent -backed  product  portfolio .PR ES S  & MED IA R ELEAS E
