YES BANK strongly refutes The Banker’s factually incorrect report alleging cyber 
fraud  
Mumbai, November 1 3, 2026: YES BANK, one of India’s leading private sector banks, 
today issued a clarification regarding a report published by The Banker , a banking and finance 
publication of the Financial Times  Group, on November 13, 2026, titled ‘ India on high alert 
after alleged $3.4mn cyber loss at Yes Bank ’. The report alleged that the Bank had incurred a 
loss of approximately USD 3.4 million ( approx.  INR 32 crore) in a cyber fraud incident 
involving government accounts at the end of October 2026.  
Clarifying the facts, YES BANK states that its systems have not been compromised, no 
government accounts have been impacted, and the Bank has not incurred any financial loss 
in connection with the alleged incident. The claims made in the article are factua lly incorrect 
and do not reflect the actual position.  
 
The Bank has taken up the matter with the media house, highlighting the factual inaccuracies 
in the article and seeking its retraction, as the publication of such incorrect information is 
detrimental to the Bank's reputation and may create unwarranted conc erns among its 
stakeholders.  
 
YES BANK remains committed to upholding the highest standards of banking security, 
operational integrity and customer trust.
