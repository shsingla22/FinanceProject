 Brief details of litigation/ dispute viz. name(s) of the opposing party, court/ tribunal/agency where litigation is filed, brief details of dispute/litigation; On 3rd July, 2025 a demand cum show cause notice (SCN) from the office of the Commissioner, CGST & Central Excise, Ujjain Commissionerate, Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh under the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 read with Madhya Pradesh Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and Integrated Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 has been received by VE Commercial Vehicles Limited (VECV), subsidiary of the company, alleging contraventions of the provisions of CGST Act, 2017 pertaining to FY 2017-18.
