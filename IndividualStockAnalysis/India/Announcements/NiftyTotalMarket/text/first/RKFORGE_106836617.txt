Rationale for 
Alteration of Main 
Objects The Company is exploring to foray into the business of designing, developing, 
manufacturing, engineering etc. of products, systems and solutions etc. for semi- 
conductors, aerospace applications, drones defense, and other related technologies 
etc. by itself or through its subsidiaries. 
To diversify into the new business activities, the Company shall be required to 
insert new clause under its objects to the Memorandum of Association (“MOA"). 
Accordingly, it was considered necessary to alter the object clause of the MOA to 
enable the Company to enter into the abovesaid new business activities. The said 
alteration in object clause of the MOA is subject to the approval of the shareholders 
of the Company. 
Brief  Details of 
Amendment to Clause 
11(A) of the MOA Clause IILA. of the Memorandum of Association (MOA) of the Company be altered 
by adding the following new sub-clause 9 after the existing sub-clause 8: 
“9. To promote, undertake, carry on either on its own or through any other entity 
or to enter into agreements, contracts, partnership, alliance or any other 
arrangement for technical, financial and operational assistance or sharing of 
profits / losses with any Person / Body / Bodies Corporate incorporated in India 
or abroad either under a Strategic Alliance or Joint Venture or any other 
arrangement, in India or abroad, the business of designing, developing, 
manufacturing, operating, simulation, engineering, integrating, testing, 
commissioning, validating, fabricating, forging, erecting, installing, 
remodelling, delivering, assembling, repairing, servicing, refurbishing, 
upgrading, overhauling, hiring, renting, distributing, trading, marketing, 
buying, selling, importing, exporting, supplying and dealing in all types and 
varieties, descriptions, specifications, characteristics, and applications of 
engineering and technological products, Systems, equipment, cameras, 
armaments, tools, machines and Components used for various commercial, 
industrial, agricultural, civil, defence and space applications including but not 
limited to Rocket & Missile, Electronic warfare and surveillance, Battle tanks and 
armoured vehicles, other Land systems, Arms & ammunitions, Naval systems, 
Space Research & Exploration, Unmanned Aerial vehicles (UAVs), Unmanned 
Aircraft System (UASs), Small Unmanned Aerial Vehicle(SUAVs), Route 
Patrolling Vehicle (RPV), Remotely Piloted Aircrafts (RPAs), Unmanned Water 
Vehicle(UWV), Unmanned Ground Vehicle (UGV), Counter UAVs, Drones, Drone 
Controllers etc, and commissioning systems, projects comprising of technologies 
involving Mechanical, Optical, Electrical, Electronic, Software & other related 
technologies required for commercial, industrial, agricultural, Semi-Conductor, 
Defence, Space, Aerospace and allied industry, by contact or non-contact 
method, with in-house or outsourced facilities.”
