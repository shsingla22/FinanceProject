TCS’ HyperVault to establish large -scale AI data cent er 
campus in Telangana  
 
The p urpose -built campus will support  frontier AI companies and hyperscalers with high- density, 
liquid -cooled compute infrastructure on  a global scale  
HYDERABAD | MUMBAI , SEPTEMBER 05 , 2026:  HyperVault, a subsi diary of Tata Consultancy 
Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS) has secured  264 acres of land to  develop a large -scale AI data 
cent er campus  of up to 1GW capacity in Hyderabad .  
The Hyderabad AI data center campus  will be purpose -built to serve the needs of frontier AI 
companies and  hyperscalers, enabling high -density GPU deployments for AI training, inference and 
advanced computing workloads. The development of the data center  campus will be executed in a 
phased manner , in line with customer demand and technology requirements.  
At full build -out, the campus is expected to rank among st the most  advanced and largest AI 
infrastructure campuses in India . The campus will also leverage green energy and water -neutral 
design principles as part of the build- out. This reinforc es Telangana’s position as a strategic hub for 
artificial intelligence and advanced digital infrastructure. Beyond the campus, the project is 
expected to catalyze India’s AI infrastructure ecosystem across power, cooling, networking, 
construction, engineering and operations. The project will generate  several thousand direct and 
indirect jobs in the  region . HyperVault and its partners are expect ed to  invest up to Rs 70,000 crore 
to build and manage this infrastructure.  
Shri A. Revanth Reddy, Hon’ble Chief Minister of Telangana , in a meeting with officials from TCS’ 
HyperVault, called the project ‘historic’ not just for Telangana but for India.    
“This establishes that Hyderabad is now at the forefront of the global AI revolution with a strong 
focus on infrastructure. This decade is one of Artificial Intelligence. Access to AI models and compute is fast -becoming public infrastructure and having this  compute reside in Telangana gives us an 
advantage ,” said the Chief Minister.  
IT & Industries Minister D. Sridhar Babu , who led the negotiations to script a record speed 
partnership for a milestone agreement, said, “ We tell the investors and corporations across the 
world, Telangana Means Business. We move at the speed and with clarity of process that matches the expectations of the tech and corporate world.”  
“This agreement germinated from a conversation between the Hon’ble Chief Minister with N . 
Chandrasekaran, Chairman of Tata Sons, in Davos in January this year. Since then, within a few months, officials and our friends at HyperVault and TCS have moved at the speed of thought to make 
this happen,”  he said.

About HyperVault AI Data Center Limited  
HyperVault AI Data Cen ter Limited, a subsidiary of Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), is building 
secure, scalable, AI -ready data cen ter infrastructure for hyperscalers, AI companies, and global 
enterprises. Backed by the wider Tata ecosystem, with TPG as strategic partner, HyperVault is being 
built in phases toward capacity in excess of one gigawatt. Its data centers  are purpose -built and 
liquid -cooled, with direct -to-chip cooling, high rack density, an energy model that brings together 
green power and reliability, and network connectivity. HyperVault provides the foundation to 
develop, deploy, and scale AI training a nd inferencing workloads.
