Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ( “SEBI Listing Regulations ”) we wish to inform you that the Company has 
received an order from the Telangana Pollution Control Board (“TGPCB ”) directing closure of operations at 
the Company ’s manufacturing site at Suryapet, Telangana.  
 
The closure order has been issued following an industrial accident that occurred during the manufacturing 
process , resulting in exposure to one employee  on duty at the facility . The employee was immediately provided 
emergency medical assistance and was subsequently shifted to a specialized hospital in Hyderabad for 
advanced treatment. Despite receiving the best possible medical care and sustained efforts by the attending 
medical  teams, the employee unfortunately succumbed to his injuries.  
 
The Company has immediately initiated  appropriate corrective and preventive measures to address the 
observations stated  in the directions issued by TGPCB and expects to promptly submit the requisite 
representations and compliances to TBPCB, for seeking cancellation  of the closure directions at the earliest  
and allowing the site to resume operations . 
 
The Company will keep the Stock Exchanges informed of any material developments in this regard.  
 
The details required under Regulation 30 read with Clause 20 of Para A of Part A of Schedule III  of the SEBI 
Listing Regulations and the SEBI Master Circular dated 30 January 2026 are enclosed as Annexure I . 
 
The disclosure containing details of the communication received from TGPCB , as required under Regulation 
30(13)  of the SEBI Listing Regulations read with the Industry Standards prescribed by the Industry Standards 
Forum (ISF), is enclosed as Annexure II.  
 This is for your information and record.  
Thank you.
