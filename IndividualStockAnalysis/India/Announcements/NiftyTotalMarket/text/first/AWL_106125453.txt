Food & FMCG 
The Food & FMCG segment recorded revenue of  INR 1,558 crores in Q3, up by 22% YoY. 
The segment continued to experience double-d igit growth in both General Trade and E-
commerce channels. Bundling our low-penetration products with fast-selling items 
continued to drive consumer trials and adop tion. On LTM basis, the segment recorded 
revenue of ~INR 6,150 crores.  In wheat flour, we outpaced industry growth  and gained market share during the current 
financial year. The introduction of small pack sizes (up to 2-KG) continued to play a key 
role in driving strong growth in the South and West regions.  In rice, the Company is working on its supply chain to improve produc t availability in the
