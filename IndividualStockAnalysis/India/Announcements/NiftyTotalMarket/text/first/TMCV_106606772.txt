at 34,833 units, compared to 27,221 units during April 2025 , up 28% YoY .

Part of the USD 180 billion Tata Group, Tata Motors Ltd., (BSE: Scrip code 544569; NSE: Scrip code TMCV) is India’s largest a nd a globally 
renowned manufacturer of utility vehicles, pick -ups, trucks, and buses. With over eight decades of leadership in comm ercial mobility,

intelligent fleet solutions support a wide range of applications —from last -mile delivery  to public transport while seamlessly driving the

customer experience and drive sustainable growth. The company operates in India and South Korea, with a global presence across
