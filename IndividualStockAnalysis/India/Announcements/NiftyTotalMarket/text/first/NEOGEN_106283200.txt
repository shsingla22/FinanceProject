Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
In terms of regulation 30 read with Schedule III of Listing Regulations and with reference to our disclosure 
dated March 5, 2025, March 7, 2025 and April 23, 2025, regarding the fire incidence that took place at our  
Multi-Purpose Plant (MPP3)- Facility, the warehouse and tank farms located at Dahej SEZ on March 5, 2025 at 
around 12.30 a.m. IST., we would like to further inform that the Company has received an amount of                         
Rs. 50 crores towards an on-account payment for loss of property, plant and equipment from the Insurance 
Company today, based on recommendation given by the surveyors in their interim report. The further 
settlement shall be determined in various stages following the completion of the assessment for loss of 
property, plant and equipment including other assets at Dahej SEZ Plant, loss of profit due to business 
interruption and reinstatement value of assets. 
 
Further details as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular No.

Details as per SEBI Circulars 
1 Insurance amount claimed and realized 
by the listed entity for the loss/damage;  An on-account payment of Rs. 50 crore has been released, with 
the further settlement to be determined in various stages 
following the completion of the assessment. 
2 The actual amount of damage caused 
due to the natural calamity or other 
force majeure events. The Company has recognised a loss of Rs 348.16 Crores (Rs. 
362.90 crores on consolidated basis) on account of damage to 
certain property, plant & equipment, inventory and estimated 
cost of incidental charges. The Company has recognised an 
insurance claim receivable of Rs 334.60 Crores (Rs. 348.82 
crores on consolidated basis) to the extent of recovery of loss 
after adjusting applicable deductibility considering its 
assessment of loss and admissibility of claims as per the policy, 
adequacy of coverage and nature of loss. The Company has not 
accounted for a claim for loss of profit due to business 
interruption and excess value of reinstatement of assets over 
written down value as per accounting conservatism. The 
aforementioned losses and corresponding insurance claim have 
been considered on a net basis as Rs 13.56 Crores (Rs. 14.08 
crores on consolidated basis).  
3 Details of steps taken to restore 
normalcy and the impact of the natural 
calamity/other force majeure events on 
production or service, financials of the 
entity. In continuation to our intimation dated April 23, 2025, we 
submit that the Production/Operation of the Multi-Purpose 
Plant (MPP3)- Facility, the warehouse and tank farms which 
were affected in fire is suspended temporarily. It is tentatively 
estimated that the reconstruction of the impacted facilities may 
take around 9- 12 months to reinstate the plant and restart 
operations, for which planning and construction work has 
already been initiated.  
 
In the interim, the Company has shifted production of critical 
select specialty products to other sites based on approval from 
customers. This, combined with the planned expansion in 
Patancheru Plant, will help minimize the impact on earnings 
going ahead to minimize business disruption.  
 
Further there is no change in the revenue guidance provided by 
the Company in its disclosure dated March 7, 2025.
