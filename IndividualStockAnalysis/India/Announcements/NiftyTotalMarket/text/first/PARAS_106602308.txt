We are pleased to inform that Paras Defence and Space Technologies Limited (“ PARAS ”) has signed  a Memorandum 
of Understanding (MoU) with Complus Systems Pvt. Ltd. , Bengaluru  (“Complus Sys tems ”) and JV Micronics , Faridabad  
(“JV Mic ronics ”).  
 
PARAS, Complus Systems, and JV Micronics will integrate their respective expertise to collaboratively promote and 
pursue opportunities both in India and internationally for the design, development, manufacturing, installation, and 
servicing of Anechoic and  Semi -Anechoic Chambers.  
PARAS will serve as the consortium leader, overseeing the design, development, manufacturing, and installation of the 
Chambers, along with the associated Filters and Engineered Points of Entry. In addition, PARAS will coordinate business 
development activi ties, represent the consortium in external engagements, and provide overall strategic direction.  
Complus Systems offers comprehensive technical expertise tailored to project requirements, including instrumentation 
procurement, engineering support, and active participation in project execution.  
JV Micronics contributes manufacturing, integration, and support services for RF absorbers and related products and 
solutions as part of consortium projects.
