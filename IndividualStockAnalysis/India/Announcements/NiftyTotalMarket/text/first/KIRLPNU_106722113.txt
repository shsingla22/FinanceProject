PRESS RELEASE  
 
 
Pune, India –  3rd  August  2026 : Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Ltd. (BSE: 505283, NSE: 
KIRLPNU), a leading player in the air, refrigeration, and gas compressor industry, has 
completed acquisition of 99.49 %  of the total voting power  in M/s. Kirloskar South East 
Asia Co. Ltd. (KSEA), further strengthening its strategic presence in the region.  Now 
Kirloskar South East Asia Co. Ltd is subsidiary of Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Limited.   
 
Kirloskar South East Asia Co. Ltd. (KSEA), incorp orated in 2016, is engaged in the trading of 
compressors across the South -East Asian market. The company has its registered office in 
Bangkok, Thailand.  
 
By establishing operations in Thailand through its subsidiary, the Company aims to 
strengthen its pres ence in the South -East Asian region, enhance customer engagement, 
and provide faster sales and after -sales support. A local presence will enable the Company 
to respond more effectively to customer requirements, improve market coverage, and build stronger r elationships with channel partners and end users.
