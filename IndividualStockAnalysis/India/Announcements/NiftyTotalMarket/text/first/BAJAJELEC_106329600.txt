Bajaj Electricals Expands Portfolio with the Launch of  Switchgear range  - Built 
on Trust, Safety and Precision 
Mumbai, 7 Aug 2025  – Bajaj Electricals, one of India’s most trusted 
names in lighting solutions, proudly announces the launch of a robust 
range of switchgears encompassing of Miniature Circuit Breakers  
(MCB) and Residual Current Circuit Breakers  (RCCB) , Isolators, 
Changeover Switch es and Distribution boards (DBs) designed to 
deliver enhanced safety, precision, and ease of installation. With this 
strategic foray, Bajaj Electricals extends its generational expertise into 
a new category , one that plays a critical role in every Indian household  
and workspace s. 
For over 80 years, Bajaj has lit up lives across generations. With the 
launch of BAJAJ SECURA  (TM) range of switchgear products, the brand 
builds on this legacy to ensure that the homes it illuminates are now 
safer and more secure than ever before. This new offering is 
thoughtfully designed with the electrician’s journey in mind where 
every installation is a testament to their expertise. But even the 
smallest fault can impact years of trust. This new range of switchgear aims to reinforce that trust with dependable protection and performance that professionals can count on, time after time.  
“The move into switchgear is a natural and strategic extension of our lighting solutions business. Consumers trust us 
not just to light up their homes, but to protect them too. BAJAJ SECURA  (TM) range of switchgear  offers just that  - 
peace of mind through superior engineering. It is designed for our electricians who build trust every day  and for the 
families who count on them ,” said Rajesh Naik, COO – Lighting Solutions, Bajaj Electricals Ltd.  
The range stands out with its fast -tripping mechanism, shock -proof terminals that safeguard against accidental 
contact, and silver -copper  contacts that ensure long-term, consistent performance. Every unit is rigorously tested 
and crafted using premium components to meet the highest standards of safety and reliability.  
Bajaj’s deep market understanding revealed a growing demand for reliable switchgear solutions in both residential and commercial segments , especially in tier 2 and tier 3 markets where trust and durability play a crucial role in 
decision -making. With this insight, the range was developed not just as a product, but as a promise of trusted safety . 
From homes to high -rises, and from local electricians to project contractors, BAJAJ SECURA
 (TM) range of switchgear 
is built to protect – your home, your safety , and your peace of mind.
