Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in Rajasthan  
 
 
New Delhi,15th April  2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels announces its latest signing — 
Lemon Tree Resort, Mori Ber a, Rajasthan. The property shall be managed by 
Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels 
Limited, and is expected to start operations in FY27.  
 
Lemon Tree Resort, Mori Ber a, Rajasthan  will feature 17 well-appointed rooms , a 
restaurant, a bar, a swimming pool, a spa , Jacuzzis and other public areas. The 
Udaipur Airport, the nearest airport, is approximately 150 kms from the property. 
Mori Bera railway station is about 2.7 kms away from the property. The resort is 
also well -connected by road, offering easy access to both public and private 
transport.  
 
Mori Bera in Rajasthan  is a part of what is called the ‘Leopard Country’ and 
showcases the unique coexistence of wildlife and local communities. It's especially 
famous for leopard sightings in the wild, making it a popular destination for nature 
lovers and wildlife photographers. The tranquil Jawai Dam , close by , adds to the 
region's charm  offering picturesque views and birdwatching opportunities.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels commented, “We are excited to strengthen our footprint in 
Rajasthan complementing our portfolio of 10 operational and seven upcoming 
hotels. Moreover, this signing further expands our wildlife portfolio – a segment 
that has been growing exponentially over the years.”
