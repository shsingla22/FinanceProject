Revolt Motors’ Premium Electric Bikes Now More 
Affordable Than Ever With New Pricing and Exclusive 
Offers  
New Delhi, 8th May  2024:  Revolt Motors, India’s leading electric motorcycle manufacturer, is 
thrilled to reveal a newly optimized pricing structure for its popular RV400 and RV400 BRZ 
models, now making them more accessible than ever.   
The RV400 now starts at an attractive ex -showroom price of Rs. 1,49,950, offering exceptional 
value to those seeking innovative and sustainable mobility. Similarly, the RV400 BRZ is now 
priced at Rs. 1,47,950, delivering remarkable style and performance at  an unbeatable price 
point.   
To further enhance the appeal, Revolt Motors is also introducing exclusive offers, including a 
flat Rs. 10,000 discount on both models. Additionally, customers can take advantage of a 
special exchange program, offering an extra Rs. 5,000 off when they trad e in their old bikes 
for a brand -new Revolt electric bike.   
These enhancements reflect Revolt Motors’ ongoing commitment to providing superior value 
through cost efficiencies that directly benefit customers. By optimizing production costs for 
spare parts and batteries, Revolt Motors ensures its electric bikes remai n highly attractive in 
terms of price and value.   
Ms. Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of Revolt Motors , expressed her excitement, saying, “We’re 
delighted to unveil this new pricing and exclusive offers to ensure our electric bikes deliver the 
best value possible. Customer satisfaction remains our utmost priority, and we are committed 
to making high -qualit y, environmentally -friendly mobility accessible to all.”  
The RV400 and RV400 BRZ are celebrated for their advanced technology, long -range 
capabilities, and innovative features such as the MyRevolt app. By optimizing prices and 
offering exclusive deals, Revolt Motors continues to lead the way in promoting electri c 
mobility, empowering more people to embrace a sustainable future.  
For more information or to book a test ride, interested customers can visit Revolt Motors’ 
official website at http://www.revoltmotors.com  or reach out to their nearest dealership.
