Mastek Recognized  with the ‘Voice of the Customer Honor –  Value Excellence’ Award 
at Everest Group Elevate Honors™  2025 
Mumbai, India , September 16, 2025 – Mastek  (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704) , a trusted AI-first digital 
engineering and cloud transformation partner, today announced  that it has been honored  with the 
‘Voice of the Customer Honor –  Value Excellence ’ recognition at the Everest Group Elevate Honors ™ 
2025.  Mastek was recognized based on the feedback of 400+ customers for delivering high -impact 
value, with buyers scoring the company in the top percentile for customer service excellence, client 
management, ease of doing business, and proactive engagemen t. Everest Group is a leading global 
research firm .  
Mastek’s recognition highlights its collaborative partnership with customers, consistently delivering 
outstanding impact through innovation, technical expertise, and alignment with strategic business 
priorities. Winning the Voice of the Customer Honor – Value Excellence reflects Mastek’s track record in 
generating strategic results with outcome- driven business impact and accelerating value realization 
across eve ry customer engagement.  
Ronak Doshi, Partner, Everest Group  said “We are proud to launch the Everest Group Elevate Honors 
recognition program at Elevate in Dallas, Sept 2025. Elevate Honors recognitions are based on Everest Group’s independent, fact based, analyst -driven research. Honorees are selected based on objectiv e, 
data- driven analysis and serves as third party validation of high performance and excellence. We 
congratul ate Mastek for winning the award this inaugural year”  
"We are proud to receive this recognition from the esteemed global research and advisory leader, 
Everest Group. This recognition reflects the trust our customers place in us and validates our approach of 
aligning with client’s business in delivering  meaningful business outcomes."  said Umang Nahata, CEO, 
Mastek.  He further added that “Mastek under his leadership is all set to ride the AI wave and ready t o 
Lead with AI with AI for Tech , AI for Business and AI for Data feat well supported by over 3500 trained AI 
professionals. ” 
Mastek is present in 40+ countries and is transforming enterprises to achieve substantive gains across 
their value chains. Leveraging expertise in AI, Data & Analytics, cloud, and digital engineering, Mastek 
enables organizations to streamline revenue stre ams, optimize operational volumes against RoI, and 
unlock new business opportunities. Through its partnerships with Oracle, AWS, Microsoft, Snowflake, 
and Salesforce, Mastek helps customers modernize core systems, enhance talent collaboration, and drive sc alable growth worldwide.  
Everest Group’s Elevate Honors ™ award program is designed to spotlight best -in-class service and 
technology providers.  The program is based on independent, fact -based, analys t-driven research that 
utilizes  proprietary data and performance assessments across multiple recognition categories . 
The inaugural recognition program was celebrated at Everest Group Elevate – Dallas 2025, held 
September 10 –12, in the presence of distinguished industry leaders from diverse domains including 
sales, marketing, revenue, and strategy.
