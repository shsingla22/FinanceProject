Non-Executive Director will be of immense benefit and value to the Company.

to be appointed as a Director of the Company. Further, in accordance with Regulation 17(1C) of the SEBI Listing Regulations,

at the next general meeting or within a time period of three months from the date of appointment, whichever is earlier.

Company, by way of this Postal Ballot.
