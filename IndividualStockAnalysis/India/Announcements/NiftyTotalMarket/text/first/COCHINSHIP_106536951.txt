Subject: Business Updates – CSL declared L1 in the tender  floated  by the Ministry of

1. We would like to inform that at the meeting held today at the Ministry of Defence, New 
Delhi, Cochin Shipyard Limited (CSL) has been declared as L1  in the tender floated by the

the Indian Navy . The estimated total order value is around Rs. 5,000 crore.

of necessary formalities in this regard, which will be updated in due course.   
3. None of the promoter/ promoter group/ group companies have any interest in the entity 
that awarded the order. Further, the said order also does not fa ll under the purview of related 
party transactions .
