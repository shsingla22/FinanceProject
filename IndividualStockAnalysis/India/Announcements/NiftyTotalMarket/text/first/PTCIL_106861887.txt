Lucknow, 16 November 2026: PTC Industries Limited (“PTC”), a leading Indian manufacturer of high-
performance materials and precision-engineered components for critical aerospace and defence
applications, has announced financial results for the quarter and half year ended 30 September 2026
on 14 November 2026.

02 KEY FINANCIAL COMMENTARY
PTC Industries delivered a strong first half of FY27, driven by scaling advanced manufacturing
programmes and execution across aerospace and defence applications. On a consolidated basis, the
Company recorded strong H1FY27 growth, with Total Income rising 77.3% YoY, EBITDA growing 116.9%
YoY and Profit After Tax increasing 168.2% YoY.
During the quarter, Aerolloy Technologies Limited (“ATL”), the wholly owned subsidiary of PTC
Industries, continued to remain a key growth driver. Aerolloy reported Total Income of ₹ 909.0 million in
Q2FY27, a growth of 213.3% YoY over ₹ 290.1 million in Q2FY26, with EBITDA of ₹ 428.3 million at an EBITDA
Margin of 47.1%, and PAT of ₹ 304.4 million, a growth of 213.6% YoY. Trac Precision Solutions (UK), the
group’s precision-machining platform supporting high-value aerospace and industrial applications,
reported Total Income of ₹ 718.9 million and EBITDA of ₹ 26.6 million in Q2FY27.

04 MAJOR STRATEGIC MILESTONES AND RECENT DEVELOPMENTS
Global Commercial Aerospace
Landmark agreement with Airbus for Titanium castings: Aerolloy Technologies Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of
PTC Industries Limited, signed a landmark agreement with Airbus for the development, qualification, industrialisation
and future supply of titanium castings for the A320neo, A330neo and A350 aircraft programmes. The agreement
strengthens Aerolloy’s participation in global commercial aerospace supply chains and reflects the Company’s
integrated capabilities across titanium materials, precision castings, machining and inspection — delivering fully
machined, ready-to-fit titanium castings through one integrated manufacturing route.
Safran Aircraft Engines selection for the LEAP engine programme: Aerolloy Technologies received a development
order from Safran Aircraft Engines for critical titanium cast components, fully machined and ready-to-fit, for the LEAP
engine programme. An accompanying MoU establishes the commercial framework for these components. Together,
these developments provide a foundation for potential long-term serial production following successful development
and qualification.
Space Engagement
Blue Origin engagement: PTC’s Chief Technology Officer and Head of International Sales visited Blue Origin’s facility in
Kent, Washington. The engagement supports PTC and Aerolloy’s long-term positioning in space applications, bringing
together their capabilities in advanced materials, superalloys, precision castings and high-performance components.
Capital Raise and Growth Platform
₹1,800 crore Qualified Institutions Placement: PTC completed a ₹ 1,800 crore Qualified Institutions Placement
through the issuance of 8,54,700 equity shares at ₹ 21,060 per share. The QIP witnessed strong demand from both
international and domestic institutional investors and was oversubscribed over 5 times the issue size. The proceeds
strengthen the capital base and provide added flexibility for organic expansion, strategic investments and inorganic
growth opportunities.
Workforce Development and Industrial Skilling
STrIDE Academy: PTC signed an MoU with the MSME and Export Promotion Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh,
under the Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel Skill Training and Employment Zone Development Scheme. STrIDE Academy at the
Strategic Materials Technology Complex will focus on precision machining, robotics, automation, specialised welding,
advanced metallurgy, industrial maintenance and quality assurance.
05 ABOUT PTC INDUSTRIES
PTC Industries Limited has over six decades of experience in manufacturing precision metal components and strategic materials for critical
applications. Through its wholly owned subsidiary Aerolloy Technologies Limited, the group manufactures Titanium and Superalloy castings and
materials for aerospace and defence applications in India and globally.
PTC is making a multi-million-dollar investment to establish a fully integrated Titanium and Superalloy manufacturing ecosystem at its new
facility in the Lucknow node of the Uttar Pradesh Defence Industrial Corridor.Q2FY27 marked another quarter of strong execution for PTC Industries, with healthy growth across
revenue, proﬁtability and margins. The performance reﬂects the increasing contribution of our
advanced materials and precision manufacturing platform, while the progress at Aerolloy continues to
strengthen our presence across global aerospace and defence programmes.
Over the past several years, we have been building PTC ONE™ - our ‘From Melt to Mission™’
manufacturing system with the belief that India must develop deeper, more integrated capability in
critical aerospace and defence manufacturing. The quarter and the subsequent period have
advanced that journey on three fronts. Our agreement with Airbus and the development order from
Safran Aircraft Engines for the LEAP programme take fully machined, ready-to-ﬁt titanium castings
from Aerolloy into the world’s leading commercial aircraft and engine programmes. The ₹ 1,800 crore
QIP, with signiﬁcant participation from global and domestic institutional investors, gives us the
ﬁnancial strength to pursue the next phase of growth, both organically and through carefully
considered strategic opportunities. And through STrIDE Academy, we have begun to build the skilled
workforce that manufacturing of this kind requires.
These are capabilities that take years to build, qualify and establish. The results of this half year show
them at work, with growth in income carried through to margins and to proﬁt. Our purpose is
unchanged: to strengthen India’s sovereign capability in critical materials and components, to build a
globally relevant enterprise, and to create enduring value for all our stakeholders.”SPEAKING ON THE PERFORMANCE, MR. SACHIN AGARWAL, CHAIRMAN & MANAGING DIRECTOR, SAID
