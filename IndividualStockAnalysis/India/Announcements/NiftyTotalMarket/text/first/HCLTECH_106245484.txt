HCLTech  named  a Leader in ISG's  AWS Ecosystem Partners Quadrant Report 2024 
for the US and APAC regions  
 
NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, May  23, 2025 —HCLTech,  a leading global technology company, 
has been recognized as a Leader  in the 2024 ISG Provider  Lens™ Quadrant Report  for AWS 
Ecosystem Partners  in both the US and APAC regions. The company earned this  distinction 
across all four evaluated categori es: AWS Professional Services, AWS Managed Services, AWS 
Data Analytics, AI and ML  and AWS SAP Workloads . 
 
As per the report,  
• In Professional and Managed Services, HCLTech stands out for guiding clients through 
cloud migrations and operational excellence with a consultative model rooted in 
automation, compliance and continuous innovation.  
• In Data Analytics, AI and ML, HCLTech  has shown leadership in helping enterprises 
realize tangible value through tools and platforms like A mazon SageMaker  and 
HCLTech AI Foundry . 
• HCLTech has also successfully enabled enterprise clients to adopt serverless solutions 
such as AWS Glue , Amazon Kinesis , translating data into real -time insights and 
competitive advantage.  
• HCLTech's leadership in SAP Workloads  shines through its track record of executing 
smooth, scalable cloud migrations while advancing SAP modernization objectives 
through AWS -nativ e capabilities.  
"HCLTech's  holistic approach to AWS engagements balances technical depth with business 
resilience, making it a dependable partner for enterprises navigating complex cloud 
environments ," said Ashwin Gaidhani, Research Partner, ISG.  
 "By harnessing the AWS ecosystem and our advanced AI capabilities, we unlock efficiencies  
and innovation  that empower clients to swiftly adapt to market changes, enhance decision -
making and drive their business forward with resilience and confidence ," said Prabhakar 
Appana, Senior Vice President of AWS Business Unit, HCLTech.  "Our leadership across all key 
segments in ISG's AWS Ecosystem Partners  Quadrant rep ort reaffirms our unwavering 
commitment to shaping the future of cloud transformation."
