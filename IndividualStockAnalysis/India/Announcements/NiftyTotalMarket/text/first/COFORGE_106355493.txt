Coforge Earns Great Place  To Work® Certification  for Fifth 
Year  in a Row  
 
Greater Noida, Fri , August  29, 202 5: Coforge Limited  (NSE: COFORGE),  a global digital 
services and solutions provide  announced that it , has been certified as a Great Place T o Work® 
by the Great Place To Work India for the  fifth consecutive year.  
 
The Great Place To Work Assessment is considered a ‘Gold Standard’ in workplace c ulture 
assessment. Great Place to Work  measured employee perceptions among 5000 Coforge 
employees using its rigorous Trust Index ™ Employee Survey.  Coforge continues to uphold the 
The Great Place To Work Assessment benchmarks for pride, satisfaction, growth, and well -
being.  
 
The achievement reflects Coforge’s belief that great workplaces are built by great people, and its commitment to creating an environment where employees feel valued, supported, and 
motivated. Coforge’s continued recognition as a Great Place To Work stems from its focus on 
people — building policies that put employees first, fostering open and trustworthy leadership, 
and creating a workplace rooted in mutual respect. By investing in well -being, learning, and 
equal opportunities, the company has built a cultu re where people choose to stay and grow. 
This strength is reflected in its high retention, and the strong bond of trust between teams 
and management.  
 Pankaj Khanna, Chief People Officer, Coforge , said, “Achieving the Great Place To Work 
Certification for the fifth consecutive year is a testament to the strength of our culture and the dedication of our people. At Coforge, we live by the motto, ‘Coforge is People, Coforge is Growth,’ creating an environment  where our teams can perform at their highest potential 
while feeling valued, respected, and supported. This positive culture is reflected in our high  
retention  rates, which highlight long -term employee trust and satisfaction.  This recognition 
reaffirms our commitment to fostering trust, inclusivity, and excellence, ensuring Coforge 
remains an employer of choice for top talent across the globe."
