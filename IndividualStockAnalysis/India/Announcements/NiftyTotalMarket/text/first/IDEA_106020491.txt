Ref: “Vodafone Idea Limited” (IDEA/532822) 
 
In continuation to our letter dated 10 September 2024, we wish to inform you that the 
Members of the Company, have passed the following Special Resolutions, as stated in the 
Postal Ballot Notice dated 27 August 2024, through remote e-voting with requisite majority:  
1. Adoption of Vodafone Idea Limited Employee Stock Option and Performance Stock Unit 
Scheme 2024. 
2. Extension of Vodafone Idea Limited Employ ee Stock Option and Performance Stock Unit 
Scheme 2024 to employees of Subsidiary Companies of the Company. 
 
The remote e-voting commenced on Wednesday, 11 September 2024, at 9.00 a.m. (IST) and concluded on Thursday, 10 October 2024, at 5.00 p.m. (IST). 
 
The aforesaid Special Resolutions shall be deemed as passed on Thursday, 10 October 2024, being the last date of e-voting. 
 
The details of the voting results of Postal Ballot  in terms of the provisions of Regulation 44(3) 
of the SEBI (Listing Obligatio ns and Disclosure Requirement s) Regulations, 2015 and the 
Scrutinizer's Report are attached as Annexure 1  and Annexure 2  respectively. 
 
The aforementioned documents have also been  uploaded on the website of the Company at 
www.myvi.in and website of National Securities Depository Limited at www.evoting.nsdl.com .  
 
The above is for your information and dissemination to the members. 
 
Thanking You, 
 
Yours truly, 
For Vodafone Idea Limited
