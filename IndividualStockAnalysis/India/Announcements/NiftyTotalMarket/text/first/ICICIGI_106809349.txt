This is with reference to our letter dated November 22, 2024, wherein the Company had 
informed the stock exchanges regarding receipt of an Order from the Assistant 
Commissioner of Central Tax, Ameerpet GST Division, Hyderabad GST Commissionerate 
at Hyderabad Branch, raising a GST demand of ₹ 2,41,82,080/-, Interest of ₹ 1,47,485/- 
and levying Penalty of ₹ 2,64,01,686/- for the period July 2017 to March 2018 under

Company had filed an appeal against the said Order. 
 
In this regard, please note that the Company has received an Order from the Joint 
Commissioner (Appeals-I), Hyderabad on October 6, 2026 at 11:15 a.m., confirming the 
GST demand of ₹ 2,41,82,080/-, Interest of ₹ 1,47,485/- and levying Penalty of                                       
₹ 2,64,01,686/-. The Company would pursue an appeal / evaluate any other legal options 
including filing of writ petition. The requisite details of the aforesaid Order are enclosed 
herewith as Annexure 1.
