 1 INTRODUCTION  
Pursuant  to the Securities  & Exchange  Board  of India  (Prohibition of Insider  Trading)  
Regulations, 2015 (“Regulations”), the Board of Directors formulated and adopted the Code 
of Internal Procedures  and Conduct for  regulating, monitoring and reporting Trading  by Insiders  
(hereinafter referred to as “the  Code”  or “Code  ”) at its meeting  held on  23rd July 2015 and 
thereafter  amended  the Code  from time to time in compliance with the amendments to the 
Regulations.  
 
2 DEFINITIONS,  APPLICABILITY  AND CLARIFICATIONS  
2.1 Terms used in this Code (that have their first letter capitalized) have the meanings given in Schedule 1, unless the context otherwise requires. Terms that are not defined in this Code have the meanings given in the Regulations.  
 
2.2 This Code applies to all promoters, members of promoter group, directors, Designated 
Persons and their Immediate Relatives who  are in  possession  or access to UPSI  as 
envisaged  by the  Regulations.  This Code  deals  with: 
(a) the handling of Unpublished Price  Sensitive  Information  (‘UPSI’)  (see para 3 below);  
(b) trading in securities by Insiders (see para 4 below); and (c) disclosures required to 
be made by certain persons  with respect  to their holding of and trading in securities  of the 
Company (see para 5 below).  
 
Temporary classification of an employee in the category of two levels below Chief  
Executive Officer merely due to exit/movement of his/her line manager or skip -level 
manager from the Company or within the Company, be excluded from being included 
as ‘Designated P ersons’.  
 
2.3 If any person requires clarifications regarding this Code or the Regulations, e.g., whether 
any information constitutes UPSI, whether a trade requires pre -clearance or triggers 
disclosures, shall  seek immediate clarification from the Secretarial Department or the 
Compliance Officer  appointed under this Code (see para 6 below).  
 
3 HANDLING  OF UNPUBLISHED PRICE  SENSITIVE INFORMATION  
3.1 All Unpublished Price  Sensitive  Information (hereinafter  referred  to as “UPSI) must  be 
handled on a need -to-know  basis only and no UPSI  shall  be communicated  to any person 
except  in furtherance of legitimate purposes, performance of duties or discharge of legal 
obligations. Each Executive Committee (ExCom) Members shall ensure ‘Chinese Walls’ are used to manage and control the dissemination of UPSI by employees within

 their departments, and with employees in other departments, so that all UPSI is 
disseminated or accessible on a need -to-know basis only.  
 
The members  of ExCom  shall  ensure  that no employee  below  such rank as they may 
decide  based  on the need  to know  such information,  shall  be provided  access rights  to 
information  in any other department, without the approval of the department head of 
the concerned department in which access rights are granted.  
 
3.2 Policy  for determination  of legitimate  purposes  
 
Sharing of  Unpublished Price  Sensitive  Information shall  only be for legitimate  purpose  
which  shall include the following - 
 
sharing of unpublished price sensitive information in the ordinary course of business, by 
an insider with partners, collaborators, lenders, customers, suppliers, merchant bankers, 
legal advisors, auditors, insolvency professionals,  rating  agencies,  statutory  authorities,  
regulatory  bodies,  industry associations or other advisors or consultants, parent or holding 
Company(ies ), its affiliates and employees, including, without  limitation,  for the purpose  
of preparation  of consolidated accounts on a group- wide basis, rendering services on a 
shared business services model and such other business activities as may be approved by the Board or Stakeholder Relationship & General Committee of the Board from time to time, provided that such sharing has not been carried out to evade or circumvent the 
prohibitions of these regulations.  
 
3.3 Any person in receipt of UPSI pursuant to a “legitimate purpose” as per 3.2 above shall 
be considered an “insider” for purposes of these regulations and due notice shall be 
given by the insider sharing such information to such persons to maintain confidentiality 
of such UPSI in compliance with this code.  
 
3.4 All employees in possession of UPSI should ensure that relevant files are kept secure and under lock and key. When  transmitting  or sharing  UPSI  through electronic media,  
such information  should be clearly marked ‘Confidential’.  
 
3.5 Process for Investigation in case of leak / suspected  leak of Unpublished Price  Sensitive  
Information  
 
i. The Committee shall comprise of the Chief Financial Officer, Chief Human Resources

 Officer, General  Counsel  and Company  Secretary  and Compliance  Officer  (“CO”)  
(‘Members’).  Any three members of the Committee shall form  quorum.  The members 
shall have the authority to initiate appropriate  inquiries/investigations  on becoming  
aware of leak of UPSI  or suspected  leak of UPSI and  shall  inform  the Board  promptly of

employees  and other stakeholders including cross examination, as may be necessary.  
 
ii.   Speak  up/ whistleblower  policy of the Company shall  also include  such leakage of 
information and employees  can make  use of such policy  to report  instances of  leak  of 
UPSI.   
 
iii. If an inquiry has been initiated in case of leak of UPSI or suspected leak of UPSI. The 
CO along with anyone of the Members shall make a report of the findings of the inquiry 
and investigation and initiate actions to be taken pursuant to the recommendation(s) of 
the Members and also place the summary of the findings before the Audit Committee at 
the next Audit Committee meeting.  
 
3.6 Process for sensitive transactions:  
 
Relevant  ExCom  members  shall  put in place a process of  how and when  any person  who 
is required to be included as an ‘Inside r’ on sensitive transaction(s). Individuals should 
be made aware of the duties and responsibilities attached to the receipt of i nside 
information, and the liability that gets attache d with the  misuse or unwarranted use of 
such information.  
 
4 TRADING  IN SECURITIES  
4.1 All Designated  Persons  and their Immediate  Relatives  shall  only trade  in securities  of the 
Company when  the Trading  Window is open. The  Trading  Window  shall  be closed in  
each calendar  quarter, from the first calendar day of the first month of such quarter, till 
48 hours after the UPSI is made generally  available.  (For example,  if the Board  approves  
the results of the April  to June quarter  on July 23, the Trading  Window  will close  on July 
1 and open 48 hours  after declaration  of results  and the same is generally made 
available.)

 The gap between  meeting of  the Audit  Committee  for recommending the  financial 
statements for approval of the Board and the meeting of the Board of Directors for 
approval of the Financial Statements  every  quarter  and year end shall  be as narrow  as 
possible  preferably  on the same  day to avoid leakage of material information.  
 
4.2 Apart from this, the CO shall also be authorized to close the trading window for some 
of the Designated Persons to the exclusion of others and to include any person having 
access to UPSI.  
 
4.3 The CO shall determine and notify the time of opening and closing of the Trading 
Window in accordance with the Regulations and the Listing Regulations.  
 
The trading  window  shall  be opened 48 (Forty -eight)  hours  after the UPSI  referred  to 
above  is made generally available or immediately after the activity/project is 
abandoned/not being pursued.  
 The trading window may not be closed for UPSI not emanating from within the Company  
4.4 Designated  Persons shall  not enter  into Futures  & Options  transactions in respect  of the 
securities of the Company.  
 
4.5 When  the Trading  Window  is open, all trading  by Designated  Persons  shall  either  be 
(a) subject  to preclearance by the CO, or 
(b) in accordance  with an approved and published  Trading  Plan.  
 
Pre-Clearance  of Trading  
4.6 Designated  Persons  and their Immediate  Relative(s)  who wish  trade  in Company  
securities based  on pre -clearance  by the CO should follow  the procedures  in Schedule  2 
of this Code . It shall  be the responsibility  of the Designated  Persons  to obtain  approvals 
in respect of the trade (s) in Company’s securities proposed to be entered into by their 
Immediate Relative(s). In case the CO or any of their immediate relative(s) wishes to 
trade in USL  shares,  he/she  would have  to make  the application  to the Managing  Director  
of the Company. The remaining provisions of this clause, as applicable to Designated 
Person, would also apply to the CO . 
 
4.7 A Designated Person shall not apply for pre -clearance of any proposed trade if such 
Designated Person is in possession of UPSI at that  time.
