Dear Sir /Madam , 
 
We hereby wish to inform you as under:  
 
The Company's Netherlands based subsidiary, Apollo Tyres (NL) B.V. (“ATNL”) having a manufacturing

to ATNL Works Council’s prior advice  and approval of ATNL Supervisory Board . The submission of the  RfA
