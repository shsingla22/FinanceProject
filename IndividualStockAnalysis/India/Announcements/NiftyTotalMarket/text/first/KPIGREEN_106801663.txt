With reference to the captioned subject, we hereby inform that KPI Green Energy Limited (“the 
Company”) has entered into a binding offer dated September 29, 2026 to acquire 100% of equity 
and all other equity like instruments of Alfanar Energy Private Limi ted (“AEPL”) and Netra Wind 
Private Limited (“NWPL”)  
 
AEPL and NWPL own the wind project having capacity of 301.4 MW and 206.5 MW, respectively, 
aggregating to 507.9 MW of wind power capacity at Bhuj, Gujarat, India.  
 
The acquisition will be completed upon execution of definitive transaction documents and 
fulfilment or waiver, as applicable, of the conditions precedent specified therein, including 
requisite lenders, contractual and regulatory approvals.  
 
The detailed disclosure as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read with
