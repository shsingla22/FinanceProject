Tata Power Advances 2.8 GW+ Data Centre Opportunity Pipeline Across 
18 Leading Blue Chip Data Centre Players, Supporting India's AI and 
Digital Growth 
 
• Engaging across 29 active workstreams spanning Renewable Energy, T&D and Integrated 
Infrastructure solutions for India's fast- growing data centre ecosystem  
• Integrated presence across value chain, spanning Generation, T&D, positions the Company uniquely to participate in the rapidly evolving AI and data centre ecosystem  
 
Mumbai,  October 14, 2026:  Tata Power , one of India’s largest integrated power companies is 
emerging as a preferred partner for India's fast -growing data centre ecosystem, with a diversified 
opportunity pipeline of ov er 2.8 GW  spanning renewable energy, transmiss ion and distribution 
opportunities.  
  
As demand from AI, cloud and digital platforms accelerate, the Company is expanding its role beyond 
power supply to provide a comprehensive suite of solutions, including powered land, transmission 
and grid connectivity , renewable energy, storage, cooling and utility infrastructure, enabling the 
development of hyperscalers and advanced data centre facilities.  
 
Tata Power has built a strong and diversified data centre opportunity pipeline, comprising approximately 430 MW of upcoming load under its distribution network, 1,236 MW of transmission-
linked opportunities, and 1,150 MW of green energy capacity under active engagement. With 29 
active workstreams across 18 leading global and domestic Data Centre Operators, Tata Power is 
playing a key role in powering some of India's largest hyperscalers and AI -ready digital infrastructure 
projects.  
 
Tata Power’s integrated presence across the power value chain, spanning generation, transmission 
and distribution, positions it uniquely  to participate in the rapidly evolving AI and data centre 
ecosystem. This enables Tata Power to offer a comprehensive suite of solutions, including powered land, transmission and grid connectivity, renewable energy, energy storage, cooling systems, and 
utility infrastructure, thereby supporting the development of hyperscalers and next -generation data 
centre facilities.  
 
To meet the rapidly growing power requirements of hyperscale data centres in the Mumbai 
Metropolitan Region (MMR), Tata Power is significantly enhancing its transmission infrastructure 
through the upgradation of its 110 kV and 220 kV network. The company is augmenting both 
transformation and transmission capacities while building robust network redundancy to ensure 
reliable and unin terrupted power supply for large -scale digital infrastructure projects. In parallel, Tata 
Power is developing new receiving stations and advancing a 400 kV transmission ring network, creating a resilient, secure and future -ready grid capable of supporting the next wave of data centre 
growth in the region. This infrastructure enhancement forms part of Tata Power’s ₹15,000 crore 
Mumbai Grid Upgradation and Expansion Plan.  
 
As India strengthens its position as a global AI and digital infrastructure hub, energy  availability will 
be a critical enabler. The Ministry of Power, based on CEA estimates, projects that AI -led data centres 
could add approximately 26.3 GW of electricity demand by FY2031 -32. With the IEA highlighting the 
growing role of emerging economies such as India in driving data centre and electricity demand, Tata 
Power is well positioned to deliver the integrated energy infrastructure required to support the 
country's digital future.  
 
  
About Tata Power:  
The Tata Power Company Limited, a vertically integrated power company and part of the Tata Group, India’s largest 
multinational business conglomerate, has built a diversified portfolio across the entire power value chain, with its total 
operational and under -construction capacity surpassing 26 GW. This includes approximately 17.7 GW of clean and green 
energy capacity (including projects under construction) and around 8.8 GW of thermal generation capacity, ensuring a 
balanced and resilient energy mix. The Company also has a strong transmission and distribution footprint, with over 7,800 
circuit kilometres of transmission lines, including pipeline projects, and serves over 13 million customers across its 
distribution businesses, making it one of  India’s largest private power distribution companies. Further strengthening its 
leadership in future-ready energy solutions, Tata Power has established 4.9 GW of integrated solar cell and module 
manufacturing capacity, signed 2.8 GW of pumped hydro storage projects, and expanded its EV charging network to over 
7,000 public charging points across 717 cities and towns in India. As a pioneer in India’s clean energy transition, Tata 
Power remains committed to achieving Net Zero by 2045 and continues to partner  with public and private stakeholders to 
deliver reliable, sustainable, and technology -led energy solutions across the country.
