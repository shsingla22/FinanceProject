Karur Vysya Bank partners with Tambaram City Municipal 
Corporation to make Mullai Nagar greener; launches 
Sponge Park  
Nature -based urban infrastructure project aims to enhance groundwater 
recharge, mitigate flooding, and create sustainable community spaces  
Chennai, June 18 , 2026:  Karur Vysya Bank (KVB), under its flagship 
environmental sustainability initiative KVB Bhoomi , today launched the 
Sponge Park at Mullai  Nagar, Tambaram , in partnership with the Tambaram 
City Municipal Corporation. The Park has been designed as an integrated 
blue-green infrastructure project that captures, stores, and naturally filters 
rainwater, helping reduce urban flooding while repleni shing groundwater 
resources. Developed in response to the growing challenges of rapid 
urbanisation , declining groundwater levels, and seasonal waterlogging in the 
region, the initiative seeks to transform an urban space into a sustainable and 
multifunction al public asset.  
Mullai Nagar was identified as a location where environmental and community 
needs intersect. The area faces recurring urban flooding, groundwater stress, 
and limited green public infrastructure. The Sponge Park has been designed 
as a natur e-based solution that simultaneously addresses water management, 
climate resilience, biodiversity enhancement, and community well -being, 
creating a model for sustainable urban development  
The inauguration  ceremony was graced by Shri D. Sarathkumar, 
Honoura ble Minister for Human Resource Management, Government of 
Tamil Nadu , in the presence of  Shri S. Balachander, IAS, Commissioner, 
Tamba ram City Munici pal Corporation and Shri B. Sankar, Executive 
Director, Karur Vysya Bank , along with senior officials from the Bank and the 
Municipal Corporation.  
Speaking on the project , MD & CEO Mr. Ramesh Babu , Karur Vysya Bank , 
said, "At Karur Vysya Bank, we believe that sustainability is a very important 
part of our commitment to responsible growth and community development. 
We are focused on creating long -term environmental impact by supporting 
initiatives that address pressing ecological challenges. The Sponge Park at 
Mulla i Nagar  under KVB Bhoomi  is a pioneering example of how nature -based 
infrastructure can improve water security, mitigate urban flooding, enhance 
biodiversity, and create healthier public spaces. We are pleased to partner 
 
 
with the Tambaram City Municipal Co rporation in building a greener and more 
resilient future for the community."  
The project is expected to benefit residents across all age groups by providing 
a safe, accessible, and eco -friendly recreational space while strengthening the 
area's resilience to climate -related challenges. In addition, the park will serve 
as a living demonstration of sustainable urban planning and environmental 
management,  creating  greater awareness of water -sensitive design and 
ecological conservation among visitors and studen ts. 
The initiative aligns with KVB's CSR focus on environmental sustainability and 
contributes towards multiple United Nations Sustainable Development Goals 
(SDGs), including Clean Water and Sanitation (SDG 6), Sustainable Cities 
and Communities (SDG 11), Climate Action (SDG 13), Good Health and Well -
being (SDG 3), and Life on Land (SDG 15).
