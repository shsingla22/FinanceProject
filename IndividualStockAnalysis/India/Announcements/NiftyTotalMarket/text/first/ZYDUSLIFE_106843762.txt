Ref.: Appointment of statutory auditors pursuant to mandatory auditor rotation 
 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Deloitte Haskins and Sells LLP (Firm Registration No. 117366W/W-100018) (“ Deloitte”), the 
existing statutory auditors, will complete its second term of 5 (five) consecutive years at the 
conclusion of 32nd Annual General Meeting (“ AGM”) for the financial year 2026-2027. 
Accordingly, Deloitte shall cease to hold office as the statutory auditors of the Company upon 
completion of its tenure, in accordance with the mandatory auditor rotation requirements 
prescribed under section 139(2) of the Companies Act, 2013. 
 
Based on the recommendation of the Audit Committee and subject to approval of the 
members of the Company, the Board of Directors at their meeting held on November 4, 2026, 
approved the appointment of M/s. Walker Chandiok & Co LLP, Chartered Accountants, (Firm 
Registration No. 001076N/N500013) (a member firm of Grant Thronton International) as the 
statutory auditors of the Company for the first term of 5 (five) years commencing from the 
conclusion of 32nd AGM for the financial year 2026-2027 and continuing until the conclusion 
of 37th AGM for the financial year 2031-2032. 
 
The resolution relating to appointment of M/s. Walker Chandiok & Co LLP, Chartered 
Accountants, as the statutory auditors of the Company will be proposed to the members of 
the Company for approval at the 32nd AGM for financial year 2026-2027.
