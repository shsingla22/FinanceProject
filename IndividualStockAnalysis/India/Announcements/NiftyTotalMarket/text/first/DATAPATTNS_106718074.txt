Vertical Integration  
Data Patterns currently designs and manufactures radar 
electronics but procures radomes and composite structures 
externally. This  results in margin leakage to third -party 
vendors, schedule dependency, and limited scope when 
bidding for integrated subsystem  programmes. Acquiring 
STAC brings this capability in -house.
