Our Founder
Shri Ashok Sarin, the esteemed Founder of Anant Raj 
Group, embodied the highest standards of integrity and 
ethical values. In 1969, Shri Ashok Sarin, an industry 
stalwart, laid the foundation of the Anant Raj Group 
with the blessings of his parents, Lala Anant Ram Sarin 
and Smt. Raj Kumari Sarin. 
He established the Company from scratch, adhering to 
orthodox and clean principles, devoid of any litigation. 
Today, we take pride in being litigation-free, a rarity in 
the real estate sector . Under his visionary leadership, 
Anant Raj has flourished into one of India’s prominent 
real estate developers. 
In tribute to our revered Founder , we pledge to continue 
progressing while upholding the three core principles: 
location, permission, and execution.  These principles 
reflect the values of our Founder and serve as the 
cornerstones of our success. For more details, please visit:
www.anantrajlimited.com
At Anant Raj, we continue to be guided by the enduring vision of our 
founder, Shri Ashok Sarin. We strive to honour his legacy through every 
facet of our business. Shri Ashok Sarin’s foundational principles and 
aspirations form the bedrock of our operations, steering us toward 
excellence and ethical practices in the real estate sector.
Fuelled by our achievements, we are far from complacent. 
Instead, we are more energised than ever, driven by the 
indomitable spirit that has characterised Anant Raj throughout 
its rich history. We are poised to continue our growth trajectory, 
ever inspired to uphold the standards and principles instilled by 
our founder.FY 2023-24 has been a year of unprecedented achievements, marking a significant milestone in 
our legacy of over fifty years of excellence. We surpassed the ` 1,500 Crore revenue mark and 
recorded a Profit after Tax of ` 265.93 Crore – the highest in the past 15 years, demonstrating 
our robust financial health and operational efficiency.
Our performance is not merely a reflection of our financial achievements but validates our 
steadfast commitment to strategic execution and excellence. It embodies the realisation of 
our visionary founder’s dream and our ability to navigate through challenges with a clear and 
effective vision.
