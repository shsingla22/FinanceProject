Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
We wish to inform, that the Company has received two rectification orders from  
Dy. Commissioner of Income Tax, Pune under Section 155(18) of the Income-tax Act, 
1961 with demand of INR 32.98 Crores for financial year 2018-19 and INR 12.84 Crores 
for financial year 2016-17 respectively.

apparent on the face of the record. The Company is in the process of pursuing 
appropriate remedial actions with the relevant authorities to resolve the aforesaid 
issues, pursuant to which the demands are expected to be quashed.
