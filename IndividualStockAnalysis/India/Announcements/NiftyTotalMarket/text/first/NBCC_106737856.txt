Subject ( विषय): General: Communication on Tax Deduction at Source (TDS) on 
1st Interim Dividend (FY 2026 -27) 
 
Sir (महोदय ), 
 
This is in reference to NBCC’s 1st interim dividend for the FY  26-27 declared by the 
Board of Directors in the Board Meeting dated August  11, 202 6. The Company has 
fixed Monday , August  17, 202 6, as the record date for ascertaining the eligibility of 
shareholders for payment of the interim dividend.  
 
All the shareholders are hereby informed that , in terms of the Income -tax Act, 2025 
(the Act), which applies from April 1, 2026, read  with the Finance Act, 2026, the 
Income -tax Rules, 2026 and other applicable law,  dividend paid or distributed by a 
domestic company is generally taxable in the hands of  the shareholder . Therefore, the 
Company be required to deduct tax at  source (TDS) at the time of payment of 
dividend.   
Accordingly, to claim exemption from TDS on dividend, shareholders are requested  to 
submit the requisite documents as prescribed under Income Tax Act, 2025 to the 
company via email at dividend.tax@nbccindia.com  on or before Tuesday , August  
18, 202 6. A detailed communi cation  regarding TDS on 1st interim Dividend (FY  2026-
27) is available on the website of the Company at  
https://www.nbccindia.in/webEnglish/announcementNotices
