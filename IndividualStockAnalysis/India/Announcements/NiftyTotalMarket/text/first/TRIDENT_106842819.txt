PRESS  RELEASE  
 
 
Trident  Limited  announces  Q2FY27  Results  
  
 
Ludhiana , Ind ia, Nov 0 3rd 2026 : Trident Limited has reported financial results for the Second  quart er 
ended  30th Sep 2026 . 
Financial  highlights  for Q2FY27 : 
 
 Consolidated Total Income  is INR 1737 Crore in Q2FY27 in comparison to INR 1803 Crore in 
Q1FY27.  
 Consolidated EBIDTA for the quarter is INR 337 Crore  at 19 %, up 6% QoQ and 45 % YoY . 
 Consolidated Net Profit for the quarter is INR 176  Crore  at 10 %, up 11% QoQ and 9 3% YoY.  
Financial  highlights  for H1FY27 : 
 
 Consolidated Total Income  of INR 3540  Crore, up 0.3 % YoY.  
 Consolidated EBIDTA is INR 653 Crore  as compared to INR 544  Crore in H1FY26 , up 20% 
YoY.  
 Consolidated Net Profit is INR 334 Crore, up 45 % YoY.  
 Net Debt stands at INR 722 Crore on Sep 30, 2026 as compared to INR 1025 Crore as on June 30, 
2026 , a reduction of INR 304 Crore.  
 
 
Commenting on the results, Mr. Deepak Nanda, Managing Director, Trident Limited, said, “During 
Q2FY27, Trident Limited delivered a resilient performance with consolidated revenue of INR 1,737 
crore and EBITDA of INR 337 crore, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 19.4%, an improvement of 653 
bps over the corresponding quarter last year. The quart er witnessed strong growth in profitability, 
with Profit Before Tax increasing by 87.6% to INR 232 crore and Net Profit rising by 93.1% to INR 176 
crore over the corresponding quarter last year, supported by our continued focus on operational 
excellence, c ost optimization, product mix enhancement, and disciplined execution across businesses.  
 
Pleased to update that  the Board has approved INR 4,650 crore expansion and modernization 
programme, underscoring our confidence in the long -term growth potential of the textile and paper 
sectors. The proposed investments include significant capacity additions in yarn, sheeting, and paper 
businesses, establishment of a new yarn manufacturing facility at Dhar, Madhya Pradesh, and 
development of a 60 MW hybrid renewable energy project. These investments will enhance our 
manufacturing capabilities, im prove operational efficiencies,  reinforce our cost competitiveness  and 
make our operations sustainable . 
 
As we continue to focus on profitable growth and value creation, this strategic expansion will position 
Trident to capitalize on future opportunities, serve customers more effectively across global markets, 
and create sustainable long -term value for all stakeholders”.

About Trident Limited  
Trident Limited is the flagship company of Trident Group, an Indian business conglomerate and global 
player. Headquartered in Ludhiana, Punjab, Trident Limited is a vertically integrated textile (Yarn, Bath & 
Bed Linen) Paper (Wheat Straw -based) and Chemical manufacturer.  
Trident’s yarn, bath & bed linen, and paper businesses have earned global recognition and are delighting 
millions of customers across India and the world. Trident is one of the largest players in home textiles in 
India.  
Supplying national, captive, and retailer -owned brands; the organization is highly decorated with awards 
from its customers, vendors, and various government entities in recognition of advancing the highest 
standards in product quality, social responsibility, and environmental stewardship.  
The company operates in three major business segments: Textile (Yarn, Bath & Bed Linen) Paper (Wheat 
Straw -based) and Chemi cal, with its manufacturing facilities in Punjab and Madhya Pradesh.
