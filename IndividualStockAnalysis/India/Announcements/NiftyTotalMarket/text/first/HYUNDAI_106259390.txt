Hyundai Motor India Limited Reports Total Monthly Sales of 
58,701  units in May 2025  
 
▪ Total monthly sales  for Hyundai Motor India Limited  include domestic 
sales of 43,861 units and export sales of 14, 840  units in May 2025  
▪ Availability of few critical models was impacted in May on account of 
scheduled biannual plant maintenance shutdown  
 
Gurugram | June  02, 2025:  Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) recorded total 
monthly sales of 58,701  units in May  2025, comprising domestic sales of 43,861  
units and export sales of 1 4,840  units.  
  
Commenting on HMIL sales, Mr. Tarun Garg, Whole -time Director and Chief 
Operating Officer, HMIL  said,  “HMIL’s  total sales volume for May 2025 stood at 
58,701 units. May is a month of our routine week -long biannual maintenance 
shutdown at our Chennai manufacturing facility  which affect s availability of 
few critical models . We continue to witness consistent growth in our exports 
volume and this is a testament to the 'Make in India, Made for the World' 
philosophy that we passionately uphold . Going forward, w e remain hopeful of  
a steady increase in demand for both domestic as well as international 
shipments  with reduced uncertainty on the geo -political front  and improved 
macro -economic situation .”
