Gurugram , India, November  05, 2026: Bharti Airtel Limited (“Bharti Airtel” or “the Company”) today announced its audited 
consolidated results for the quarter  ended September  30, 202 6. 
 
Q2’27 Performance:  
 
Our total Customer base expanded to  693 Mn across 15 countries supported by strong quarterly net addition s of 12 Mn c ustomers  
across India and Africa .  
 
Consolidated revenues for the quarter grew 16.7%  YoY and  4.0%  QoQ to Rs 60,878 crore, led by strong momentum  in both India and 
Africa. Consolidated EBITDA for the quarter stood at Rs 35,240 crore with EBITDA margin of  57.9% .  
 
India revenues for Q2 ’27 reached Rs  42,308 crore, registering a strong  growth of 9.4%  YoY and 2.7%  QoQ.  
 
India Mobile revenue up 10.1%  YoY, driven by strong customer additions and improved realization  with Average Revenue Per User 
(ARPU)  expanding to Rs 271 in Q2’27 from Rs  256 in Q2’26. 
 
Strategic focus on strengthening our portfolio proposition led to exceptional performance in postpaid segment , resulting in record net 
additions of  1.2 Mn taking total  base to  31.2 Mn. The smartphone data customer base expanded by  18.8 Mn over the past year.  
 
During the quarter, Airtel strengthened  its postpaid p roposition  by adding Apple's newly expanded iCloud+ offerings, which now also 
includes Apple TV and Apple Arcade, offering greater value and another reason for customers to upgrade.  
 
We continue  to strengthen our digital infrastructure and deployed  497 towers and  11,684 mobile broadband base stations during the 
quarter. Over the past year, network expansion included  5,649 towers and  44,718 kilometers of fiber, significantly enhancing 
coverage, capacity, and resilience.  
 
Driven by strong demand and market penetration, the Homes segment revenue grew by 25.3%  YoY. Net customer additions for the 
quarter stood at 676 K, bringing the total Homes customer base to  15.4 Mn. 
 
With a customer base of 15.6 Mn, Digital TV delivered revenues of Rs  771 crore for the quarter , continuing to benefit from strong 
underlying growth in IPTV . 
 
Airtel Business posted  another quarter of double digit revenue growth of 11.4%  YoY, led by strong momentum across the portfolio .  
 
During the quarter, Airtel Business and ITI Limited enter into strategic partnership to accelerate India’s digital transformation . As 
enterprises across the country advance their digitization in line with the nation’s digital growth and development agenda, th e 
collaboration will combine the complementary strengths and engineering excellence of ITI Limited and Airtel Business, to help  
businesses modernize legacy systems, improve operational efficiency, strengthen security, and respond faster to changing busi ness 
demands.  
 
Africa delivered yet another quarter of robust growth of 20.6%  YoY in Constant Currency, with 193.9  Mn customer base. Reported 
Revenue growth also came in strong at 25.7%  YoY.  
 
 
 
 
In a statement, Gopal Vittal,  Executive Vice Chairman , said:  
 
We delivered another quarter of consistent performance with consolidated revenues of Rs 60,878 crore, increasing 4.0%  QoQ, 
underscoring the strength of our diversified portfolio. Africa delivered a standout quarter with 6.7%  QoQ constant currency revenue 
growth. Africa’s improving performance is also benefiting from the rollout of Airtel’s homegrown technology stack across mark ets, 
powering our go -to-market capabilities. Group -wide synergies are also beginning to deliver tang ible operating gains. India business 
recorded strong revenue growth of 2.7%  sequentially.  
India Mobile delivered revenue growth of 3.4%  QoQ, backed by our strategy of portfolio premiumization and winning quality 
customers. Our postpaid momentum remained strong with record high net customers additions of  1.2 million. We added  2.9 million 
smartphone data customers with continued improvement of our industry leading ARPU at Rs 271.  
The Homes business saw 676 K customer additions, driven by disciplined customer acquisition and continued progress on our 
convergence strategy.  
Airtel Business sustained strong momentum with revenue growth of 3.8%  QoQ and 11.4%  YoY, led by strong performance across 
the portfolio.  
Our new growth engines continue to gain momentum. Our investment in a strategic land parcel during the quarter marks another 
important step towards realising our ambition of building 1 GW of data centre capacity. In Financial Services, early traction  following 
the commercial launch has been very encouraging, with our digital platforms, advanced data analytics and disciplined operatin g 
model providing a strong foundation to scale the business.  
We continue to maintain a strong and resilient balance sheet, anchored by robust cash generation and disciplined capital 
allocation.

About Bharti Airtel  
Headquartered in India, Airtel is a global communications solutions provider with over 650 million customers in 15 countries across India and Africa. 
The company also has its presence in Bangladesh and Sri Lanka through its associate entities. The company is ranked second amongst mobile 
operators globally, and its networks cover over two billion people. Airtel is India’s largest integrated communications solut ions provider and the second 
largest mobile operator in Africa. Airtel’s retail portfolio includes high-speed 4G/5G mobile, Wi -Fi (FTTH+ FWA) that promises speeds up to 1 Gbps 
with convergence across linear and on -demand entertainment, video streaming services, digital payments and financial services. For enterprise 
customers, Airtel offers a gamut of s olutions that includes secure connectivity, cloud and data center services, cyber security, IoT, and cloud -based 
communication. Airtel’s digital arm – Xtelify, empowers telcos globally to leverage the power of AI, data and technology to accelerate their di gital 
transformation and drive growth. Xtelify also offers Airtel Cloud in India enabling enterprises with a sovereign, telco -grade cloud platform that 
guarantees secure migration, effortless scaling, lower costs and no vendor lock - ins. Within its diversi fied portfolio, Airtel also offers passive 
infrastructure services through its subsidiary Indus Tower Ltd. For more details visit www.airtel.com
