Yatharth Hospitals Inaugurates its new Super -Speciality Facility in Model Town, New Delhi  
New Delhi, 14th July 2025 : Strengthening its commitment to accessible, high -quality healthcare in the heart 
of the national capital, Yatharth Super Speciality Hospitals has officially inaugurated its new super speciality 
hospital in Model Town, New Delhi today.  
The inauguration ceremony was graced by the esteemed presence of Smt. Rekha Gupta, Honourable Chief 
Minister of Delhi, as the Chief Guest. She was joined by Shri Ashok Goel Devraha, Member of Legislative 
Assembly, Model Town  and Shri Raj Kumar Bhatia, Member of Legislative Assembly, Adarsh Nagar as Guests 
of Honour, along with other eminent dignitaries and luminaries.  
Addressing the gathering, Smt. Gupta emphasized the growing need for reliable healthcare infrastructure in 
Delhi and commended Yatharth Hospitals’ contribution towards bridging this critical gap. With a unique 
blend of compassion and clinical excellence, Yatharth Hospitals is setting new standards in quality healthcare 
delivery.   
 
Expressing optimism over the launch of the new facility, Mr. Yatharth Tyagi, Director, Yatharth Group of 
Hospitals said  “This is not just the inauguration of another hospital, but a reaffirmation of our purpose and 
philosophy. At Yatharth Hospitals, we believe that healthcare is not a privilege, but a fundamental right that 
every individual deserves, irrespective of geography or background. With the launch of our new facility in 
Model Town, we are taking one more step towards bridging the gap between people and access to quality 
medical care.“  
The newly launched Yatharth Hospital in Model Town marks a significant expansion in the group’s bed 
capacity, aimed at strengthening healthcare infrastructure in Delhi. Strategically built on approximately two 
acres of land, the facility houses 300 beds , including 70 critical care beds , to cater to patients requiring 
specialized and intensive treatment.  
  

 
 
 
As part of the rapidly expanding Yatharth Group of Hospitals - which now operates 7 super -speciality 
hospitals with over 2,300 beds  and 900+ specialist doctors across multiple cities. The Model Town facility 
reinforces the group’s commitment to delivering advanced and world -class healthcare closer to the 
community, backed by cutting -edge medical technology and clinical expertise.  
The Model Town facility is equipped with state -of-the-art facility, which includes multiple centres of 
excellence, critical care units, modular operation theatres, advanced diagnostics, and specialized 
departments across cardiology, oncology, orthopaedics, neurology, nephrology , and more. The hospital is 
also fully equipped to offer emergency and trauma care services, making it a vital addition to Delhi’s 
healthcare ecosystem.  
This new facility represents Yatharth Hospital’s vision to expand its legacy of trust and clinical excellence 
beyond Noida, Greater Noida, Noida Extension, Jhansi -Orchha, and Faridabad, where the group has already 
established a strong presence. The Model Town facility is expected to cater to a wide catchment area, 
offering patients expert -led, best -in-class healthcare without the need to travel far.  
About Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Ltd:  
Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Limited (Yatharth Hospitals) is one of North India’s leading healthcare 
providers, known for its commitment to delivering high quality medical care through a network of state -of-the-art 
facilities. It operates five super specialty hospitals located in North India, i.e., at Noida, Greater Noida, Noida 
Extension and Faridabad in Delhi NCR, and Jhansi -Orchha in Madhya Pradesh. Its Noida Extension Hospital and 
Greater Noida is the 8th and 10th largest private hospital in the Delhi NCR, respectively, in terms of number of 
beds in Fiscal 2023. Yatharth Hospitals has recently added  two new hospitals, in Delhi and Faridabad, through 
strategic acquisitions, expanding its total bed capacity to 2,300+ beds.
