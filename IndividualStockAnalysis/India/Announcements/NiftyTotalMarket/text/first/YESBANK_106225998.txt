(i) HDFC Bank Limited (“ HDFC ”), Purchaser and the Bank;

(iii) Kotak Mahindra Bank Limited (“ Kotak Mahindra ”), Purchaser and the Bank;  
(iv) Axis Bank Limited (“ Axis ”), Purchaser and the Bank;

(vi) Federal Bank Limited (“ Federal ”), Purchaser and the Bank; and 
(vii) Bandhan Bank Limited (“ Bandhan”), Purchaser and the Bank

2,136,830,297 equity shares representing 6.81% of the equity share capital of the Bank from the
