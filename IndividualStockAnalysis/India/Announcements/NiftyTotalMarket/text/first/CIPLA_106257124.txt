Sub: USFDA inspection at Company’s manufacturing facility in Bommasandra, Bengaluru, India  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, 
we hereby notify that United States Food and Drugs Administration (‘USFDA’) has conducted a current Good 
Manufacturing Practices (‘cGMP’) inspection at the Company’s manufacturing facility in Bommasandra, 
Bengaluru from 26th to 30th May 2025. 
 
On conclusion of the inspection, the Company received 1 (one) observation in Form 483. The Company will 
work closely with the USFDA and remains committed to address the observation comprehensively within 
stipulated time.
