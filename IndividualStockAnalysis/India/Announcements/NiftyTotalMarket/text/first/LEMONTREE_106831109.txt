LEMON TREE HOTELS SIGN S LEMON TREE HOTEL, SIRHIND               
 
With this addition, the Group will have four operational and 10 upcoming hotels 
in Punjab  
New Delhi, 27th October , 2026 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, one of India’s 
leading hospitality companies, has announced the signing of Lemon Tree Hotel, 
Sirhind . The property will be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a 
wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
Sirhind, situated in the historic district of Fatehgarh  Sahib, is steeped in rich 
cultural and religious heritage. Its connectivity to Chandigarh and nearby 
commercial hubs positions it to cater to a diverse mix of travellers, from business 
and transit guests to visitors seeking to explore the region’s signifi cant historical 
landmarks and cultural attractions.  
The hotel will feature 76 well -appointed rooms, along with a restaurant, banquet  
hall, meeting/ conference room, a swimming pool, spa, fitness centre and other 
public areas. The property will offer a comprehensive hospitality experience for 
pilgrim and leisure travellers, combining comfortable accommodation with dining, 
wellness and event facilities.  
Commenting on the signing, Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, 
Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd.,  said, “Our growing presence in Punjab reflects the 
diverse opportunities the state offers across business, religious tourism and 
leisure. Sirhind’s  rich religious and historical heritage, coupled with its regional 
connectivity, makes it a compelling addition to Lemon Tree Hotels  network. With 
four operational hotels and 10 upcoming properties in the state, we continue to 
build a portfolio that responds to the distinct demand drivers of each destination.”  
The hotel will be conveniently connected to key transport hubs. Sirhind  Junction 
is approximately 3.5 km from the property, while Shaheed Bhagat Singh 
International Airport, Chandigarh, is approximately 45 km away.  
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India's leading hospitality companies,

& Resorts, Lemon T ree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys 
Select, and Keys Lite – the group offers experiences across upper upscale, 
upscale, upper midscale, midscale, leisure, wildlife, and spiritual segments.  
 
 
LTHL operates 130+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing 
pipeline of 140+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, 
Bengaluru, and Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, 
and Indore – and with an international presence in Bhutan and Nepal – Lemon 
Tree Hotels delivers exceptional comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, 
refreshing experience.  
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 270+
