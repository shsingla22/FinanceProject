Information Classiﬁcation: Public  OPSWAT signs distribution deal with Redington to scale critical 
infrastructure protection across the Middle East, Türkiye , Africa  and 
the Commonwealth  of Independent States  
 
The partnership combines OPSWAT's  critical infrastructure security solutions with regional reach, 
financing and 24/7 services to strengthen resilience across government, defence, energy and other 
essential sectors  
 
Dubai, United Arab Emirates – October 01,  2026 – OPSWAT , a global leader in critical infrastructure 
protection (CIP) cybersecurity solutions , and Redington, a leading technology aggregator,  today announced 
a distribution partnership covering the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC), Levant, Türkiye, Africa and the 
Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS). The partnership combi nes OPSWAT’s CIP portfolio with 
Redington’s on- the-ground sales, technical, warehousing, logistics and financing capabilities . Together, 
OPSWAT and Redington will widen access to specialised protection for segmented, air -gapped and 
operational technology (OT) environments across government, defence, banking, telecoms, energy , utilities 
and other regulated sectors.  
 
The agreement will have an initial emphasis on solutions such as MetaDefender Kiosk ™ and MetaDefender 
Optical Diode™ for government, defence and OT environments. Redington’s local stockholding, logistics 
infrastructure and in- country technical resources will support the movement and deployment of these 
solutions across a region in which procurement requirements vary sign ificantly and supply -chain continuity 
remains under pressure. Its financing capabilities will also help partners pursue complex, multi -stage 
projects, particularly in frontier markets.  
 
Critical infrastructure has become an increasingly visible front in geopolitical conflict, with both cyber and 
physical incidents demonstrating how rapidly disruption can spread beyond the initial target  and  particularly 
affecting growth markets. The World Economic Forum’s Global Cybersecurity Outlook 2025 found that 36% 
of organisations in Africa lacked confidence in their country’s preparedness to respond to a major cyber incident affecting critical infrastructure, compared with 15% in Europe and North America.  
 The partnership will enable channel partners to build integrated solutions addressing the security and compliance requirements of critical environments. OPSWAT’s specialised capabilities for protecting files, removable media, segmented networks and OT environments can be combined with infrastructure and 
cybersecurity technologies available through Redington’s wider portfolio. Partners can then add 
assessments, implementation and professional services to deliver complete, outcome- based CIP 
propositions , rather than standalone products.  
 
Managed services will form a central part of this partnership.  Through Redington’s DigiGlass offering, 
partners will be able to extend customer engagements into 24/7 security operations without having to make 
the upfront investment required to build their own security operations capabilities.  
 
Hussam Sidani, Regional Vice President – META, OPSWAT, said , “Critical infrastructure customers cannot 
wait for technology to clear borders, financing gaps or skills shortages. Redington brings resilience on all 
three fronts. Its financial stability gives partners the confidence to pursue complex, multi -stage projects; its 
regional stock and logistics help keep hardware- led deployments moving when supply chains are under 
pressure; and programs like DigiGlass  give partners a path from product resale into recurring, higher -value 
managed services. ” 
 
 
Information Classiﬁcation: Public   
Sayantan Dev, Chief Executive Officer, Software Solutions Group, Redington, said, “Critical infrastructure 
is becoming increasingly connected, making its protection more complex and more critical. As AI 
accelerates both innovation and the sophistication of emerging threats, partners need the right 
technologies, expertise and support to stay ahead. This is where technology ecosystem orchestration 
becomes important – bringing together the right security capabilities, partner expertise and market context. 
Through our partnership with OPSWAT, we aim to enable partners to strengthen cyber resilience, address 
emerging AI -driven risks and build relevant solutions that help customers protect the critical infrastructure 
their businesses and economies depend on.”  
 During the first 12 months, OPSWAT and Redington will prioritise Africa, Türkiye and CIS  through  recruiting 
and certifying partners, building qualified pipeline and pursuing early customer reference wins. OPSWAT 
will lead technical certification and enablement, while Redington drives partner recruitment and channel 
logistics across the region. The same partner, enablement and go- to-market framework will be available 
across the wider METAPCIS footprint from day one, with execution tailored to local regulations, 
procurement requirements and channel readiness .
