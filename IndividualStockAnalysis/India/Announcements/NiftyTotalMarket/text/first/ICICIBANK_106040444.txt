 I(A) Information and Instructions for e-Voting by Individual Shareholders holding shares of the Company in Demat Mode:
 As per the SEBI Master Circular dated July 11, 2023, all “individual shareholders holding shares of the Company

Depositories/Depository Particpant(s). The procedure to login and access e-voting, is given below:

IV. You will able to see Company Name “ICICI Bank Limited” on the next screen. Click
