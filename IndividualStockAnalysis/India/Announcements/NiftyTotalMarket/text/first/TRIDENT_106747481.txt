Sub: Update on the Incorporation of a New Domestic Wholly Owned Subsidiary (DWOS)
Dear Sir/ Madam,
This is in continuation to our letter dated July 21, 2026, wherein the Company had informed the
Stock Exchanges regarding the approval of the Board of Directors to incorporate a New Domestic
Wholly Owned Subsidiary (DWOS) for enhancing brand presence, drive brand-building, sales,
marketing and business development initiatives and marketing for Trident products in overseas
markets.
In this regard, we wish to inform you that Trident Global Industries Limited has been incorporated as
a New Domestic Wholly Owned Subsidiary of Trident Limited on August 17, 2026. The Certiﬁcate of
Incorporation issued by the Ministry of Corporate Aﬀairs has been received by the Company.
The detail(s) as required under Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with SEBI Master Circular No.
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 (as amended) is provided as
Annexure.
