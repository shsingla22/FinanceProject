Mastek  Honored with Prestigious Award at the 19th Annual 2024 
Globee® Awards for Technology  
Mastek Secures Two Globee Awards for CEO Hiral Chandrana and CTIO Ritwik Batabyal  
Mumbai , 22 July, 2024  – Mastek  (NSE: MASTEK; BSE: 523704), a trusted digital engineering and cloud 
transformation partner , is delighted to announce that it has been recognized as a winner at the 19th 
Annual 2024 Globee® Awards for Technology, one of the world’s premier business awards programs. 
Mastek was awarded the Gold Globee for Executive of the Year in the IT Integrated category. This award 
recognizes CEO  of Mastek  Hiral C handrana for his exceptional leadership and relentless pursuit of 
excellence , which has driven the company's growth and empowered employees to exceed client 
expectations  
Additionally, Mastek w as awarded  the Silver Globee for Chief Technology Officer of the Year in the IT 
Integrated category. Mastek’s CTIO Ritwik Batabyal won for his pivotal role in driving technological 
innovation, ensuring efficient technical operations, and aligning technology strategies with Mastek's overall business goals. These accolades highlight Mastek’s commitment to decompleXify tasks for its 
partners and deliver  significant business value.  
The Globee® Awards for Technology honor and celebrate the achievements and positive contributions of organizations and individuals from all over the world. This award reflects Mastek’s commitment to 
innovation, excellence, and the continued development of cutting -edge solutions in the technology 
sector.  
 “Congratulations to all the exceptional winners of the 2024 Globee Awards for Technology,”  said San 
Madan, President of Globee Awards . “Your groundbreaking innovations and unwavering dedication 
have set a new standard of excellence in technologies everywhere. At Globee Awards, we proudly celebrate your remarkable achievements and look forward to witnessing the transformative impact you 
will continue to make in the world. Keep pushing the boundaries of what’s possible and inspiring us all.”  
The Globee® Awards, which highlight achievements in various industries, are known for their rigorous 
judging process. The judging panel includes industry experts from a wide range of fields, ensuring that 
each entry is evaluated fairly and comprehensively.  
Explore the complete list of 2024 winners here:  https://globeeawards.com/technology/winners/  
Over 2,145 judges from around the world, representing a diverse array of industry experts, applied for 
the judging process. The judges are listed here: https://globeeawards.com/technology/judges/
