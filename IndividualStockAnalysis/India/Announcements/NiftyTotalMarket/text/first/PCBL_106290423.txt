Sub: - PCBL Chemical Limited Awarded “A” Rating in CDP’s Supplier Engagement Assessment

Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we are pleased to inform you that PCBL Chemical Limited has been 
awarded an ‘A Rating’ (the highest rating) in the CDP’s Supplier Engagement Assessment (SEA) for the 
reporting year 2024.The CDP SEA evaluates companies on key parameters including governance, targets, 
Scope 3 greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, and value chain engagement as part of its climate change questionnaire.  
 
This recognition reflects our continued commitment to environmental stewardship, transparency, and 
proactive engagement across our supply chain.   
 Achieving the highest rating in this globally recognized assessment reinforces our brand reputation and 
strengthens stakeholder confidence in our sustainability practices.
