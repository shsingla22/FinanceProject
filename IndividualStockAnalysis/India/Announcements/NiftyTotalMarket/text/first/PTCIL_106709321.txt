PTC Industries Receives Development Order from Gun Factory Kanpur for 
Major Artillery Gun Components  
Strengthening PTC's Role in India's Defence Manufacturing and Advanced Land Systems  
Key Highlights  
 Development order from Gun Factory Kanpur.  PTC Industries Limited has received a 
development order from Gun Factory Kanpur for two major artillery gun components.  
 Strengthening role in defence manufacturing.  The order reinforces PTC's growin g position in 
India's defence and aerospace manufacturing ecosystem, particularly in advanced, mission -critical 
components for land defence systems.  
 Expanding defence capability.  The order supports PTC's long -term direction of moving towards 
larger and mor e complex defence components, assemblies and sub -systems.  
 Aligned with PTC ONE™.  This development strengthens PTC ONE™ - From Melt to Mission™, the 
Group's integrated manufacturing doctrine for serving critical aerospace, defence and strategic 
applications . 
Why This Matters  
This development order is another important step in PTC's defence journey. It places the Company 
deeper into the value chain for indigenous artillery gun platforms and builds on its continuing 
experience with demanding artillery applicat ions.  
Artillery systems require high -integrity components capable of performing under severe load, shock, 
vibration and operational conditions. The ability to develop and manufacture such components 
domestically is an important part of India's long -term de fence manufacturing capability.  
The order also supports India's objective of building a stronger indigenous defence industrial base 
under Aatmanirbhar Bharat and Make in India.  
Lucknow, India - 24 July 2026  
PTC Industries Limited, an advanced manufacturing  company serving critical and super -critical 
aerospace, defence and strategic applications, today announced that it has received a development 
order from Gun Factory Kanpur for two major artillery gun components.  
The order marks another significant milesto ne in PTC's growing contribution to India's defence 
manufacturing ecosystem. The Company is currently engaged in several strategic development 
programmes across the defence and aerospace sectors and continues to support projects of the 
Defence Research and  Development Organisation, Defence Public Sector Undertakings and other key 
defence manufacturing organisations.  
This latest development order reflects the growing confidence in PTC's technical expertise, 
manufacturing discipline and ability to support advanced defence systems with high -integrity 
components.

Building on Continuing Artillery Capability  
PTC Industries continues to be engaged in advanced artillery applications through ongoing 
development and supply of critical cast components for the M777 ultra -lightweight towed howitzer 
programme for BAE Systems.  
The M777 programme requires demanding quality, metallurgi cal and performance standards, and 
PTC's ongoing participation continues to strengthen the Company's credibility in high -performance 
defence components for artillery applications.  
The new development order from Gun Factory Kanpur builds on this programme e xperience and 
represents a further step in PTC's engagement with indigenous artillery gun platforms.  
PTC ONE™ - From Melt to Mission™  
This order strengthens PTC ONE™ - From Melt to Mission™, the Group's integrated manufacturing 
doctrine for transforming advanced materials and manufacturing capability into mission -critical 
components, assemblies and systems.  
PTC ONE™ is equally relevant to defence, artillery, land systems, naval platforms, space, energy and 
aerospace applications where material integrity, manufacturing quality, process control and 
execution discipline are essential.  
The present order adds another proof point to this journey. It demonstrates PTC's increasing 
relevance across India's defence manufacturing chain - from critical components toda y towards 
larger and more complex assemblies and sub -systems in the future.  
Chairman's Statement  
Mr. Sachin Agarwal, Chairman & Managing Director, PTC Industries Limited, said:  
"This development order from Gun Factory Kanpur is an important step in PTC's g rowing role in 
India's defence manufacturing ecosystem. Our continuing work on the M777 ultra -lightweight 
howitzer programme has strengthened our experience in demanding artillery applications, and 
this new order further expands our engagement with advance d indigenous artillery platforms. 
We remain committed to supporting India's self -reliance in defence manufacturing."  
Strategic Significance  
This order represents another important step in PTC's evolution from a precision component 
manufacturer into an inte grated advanced manufacturing company serving aerospace, defence, 
space, energy and strategic sectors.  
Artillery gun systems are among the most demanding land defence platforms, requiring components 
that can withstand severe mechanical, structural and oper ational conditions. The development of 
such components requires strong metallurgical understanding, precision manufacturing, quality 
systems and execution discipline.
