Respected Chairman and Board Members,  
 
At the outset, it is my privilege and pleasure to acknowledge the support and learnings that I 
achieved by being a part of the board of Waaree Energies Ltd. It was also my privilege to 
contribute in whatever little way possible during deliberations while learning from the vast 
experience of other august board members. I once again also congratulate the team for a 
successful IPO an d building the runway for strong growth into the future.  
   
While the journey has been personally enriching and professionally rewarding, my personal 
circumstances have changed since the time I became involved with the company. My wife, 
Dr. Indu Dhiman, has secured a professor position at TU Delft, Netherlands, and we would 
be moving to possibly settle there. In view of this, my availability in India will be limited and 
therefore I do not feel that I will be able to do adequate justice to my position on the board at 
a juncture of vibrant growth where Waaree finds  itself.  
 
In view of this, I request that I may please be allowed to resign from the position of Non-
Executive Director w.e.f. 23rd December 2024.
