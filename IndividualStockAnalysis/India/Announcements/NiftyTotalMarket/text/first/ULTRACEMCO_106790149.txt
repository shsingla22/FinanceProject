Sub: Company Update: Commissioning of cement capacity  
 
Dear Sirs,  
 
We are pleased to inform the commissioning of 4.6  mtpa of cement capacity as detailed 
hereunder : 
 
(i) 3.6 mtpa greenfield integrated capacity at Petnikota, Andhra Pradesh . 
(ii) 1.0 mtpa through debottlenecking at the Company’s existing cement plants at 
Visakhapatnam (Andhra Pradesh), Patratu (Jharkhand) and Nathdwara (Rajasthan) . 
 
Consequent to the above, the Company’s total domestic grey cement manufacturing capacity 
now stands augmented to 204.7 mtpa. Alongwith its overseas capacity of 5.4  mtpa, the 
Company’s global capacity stands at  210.1 mtpa.  
 
The additional capacity will strengthen the Company’s presence in the southern, eastern and 
northern markets, extend its market reach and improve logistics efficiency.
