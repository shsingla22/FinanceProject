Redington Gulf Signs Strategic Collaboration Agreement with AWS to 
Accelerate AI Adoption  
  
Dubai, United Arab Emirates, 22nd October  2026:  Redington Gulf, the leading technology aggregator, 
announced today that it has signed a strategic collaboration agreement (SCA) with Amazon Web Services 
(AWS), aimed at accelerating AI adoption in the Middle East, Turkey and Africa (META) region.  
  
Redington Gulf recently achieved the AWS AI Services Competency and AWS Digital Sovereignty Services 
Competency, validating Redington Gulf’s ability to support customers and partners across AI -led 
transformation, sovereign cloud requirements and broader cl oud modernisation  initiatives. Redington 
Gulf now holds six AWS Competencies including the AWS Migration and Modernization Consulting, the 
AWS SAP Consulting Competency, the AWS Small and Medium Business Services Competency and the 
AWS Security Consulting Competency.  
 
Under the agreement, Redington Gulf will help customers adopt and scale cloud and AI solutions with 
AWS. The focus will be on removing complexity across the technology journey by identifying the right 
solutions, accelerating deployment and supporting custo mers as they scale. As part of the agreement, 
Redington Gulf will act as a distributor, working with other AWS Partner Network (APN) members in the 
region. By orchestrating technology, expertise and market access, Redington Gulf will help organisations  
turn cloud and AI investments into faster adoption, stronger business outcomes and long -term growth.  
  
“Our relationship with AWS has continued to evolve alongside changing customer priorities, and this SCA 
marks the next phase of that journey,” said Sayantan Dev, Chief Executive Officer, Software Solutions 
Group, Redington . “It opens new opportunities in areas such as agentic AI, with a strong focus on high -
growth markets such as Saudi Arabia. It also enables us to help our partners in the META region scale 
faster, build stronger capabilities, and expand access to solutions  through AWS Marketplace. This is 
orchestration in action: connecting technology, expertise and market access to create greater choice for 
customers and drive scalable business outcomes.”  
 
"This is a collaboration designed to accelerate cloud and AI adoption across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia 
and the Middle East,” said Afaf Chekkar, Director EMEA Strategic Partners, AWS.  “Redington Gulf’s 
regional expertise can be a key enabler of rapid innovation, providing customers with new routes to 
adopting solutions that have a material impact in growing business value."   
  
Looking ahead, Redington Gulf will work with AWS to develop new pathways for  Redington Gulf’s  partners 
and customers to access emerging technologies, build new solutions leveraging AWS services and address 
evolving market needs.
