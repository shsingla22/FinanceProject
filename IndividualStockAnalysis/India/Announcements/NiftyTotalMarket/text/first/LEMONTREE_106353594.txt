Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in Ma harashtra  
 
New Delhi , 27th August 2025 : Lemon Tree Hotels announces its latest signing – 
Lemon Tree Resort, Pench, Maharashtra . This propert y shall be managed by 
Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels 
Limited.  
 
Lemon Tree Wildlife Resort, Pench, Ma harashtra will feature 60 well-appointed 
rooms . To cater to its guests , the resort will also feature a r estaurant , a banquet, a 
swimming pool , a spa  and other recreational facilities . The Nagpur Airport is 
approximately 85 kms away from the property, while the Nagpur Railway Station is 
about 62 kms away. The hotel is well connected by roadways for both public and 
private transport.  
 
Located in the heart of India, the forest area of Pench is contiguously  shared by two 
Indian states, Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra . Named after the River Pence which 
flows through the park, Pench National Park is one of the most popular wildlife 
reserves in India. Spread over a massive area of 758 sq km , it forms the backdrop 
of ‘The Jungle Book’, the well -known novel by Rudyard Kipling , which has contributed 
much to the park’s popularity . Wildlife enthusiasts can spot the Royal Bengal tiger, 
jackal, peafowl, wild boar, sloth bear, Indian leopard, striped hyena, four -horned 
antelope, Indian wolf, and a host of other animals . In 1975 and 1992, the sanctuary 
got the recognition of national park and tiger reserve, respectively.  
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO - Managed & Franchise Business, 
Lemon Tree Hotels commented , "We are delighted to further enhance our wildlife 
portfolio where we already have five existing properties.  This resort will further 
expand our presence in Maharashtra where we currently operate 14 hotels and have 
10 more upcoming ones ."
