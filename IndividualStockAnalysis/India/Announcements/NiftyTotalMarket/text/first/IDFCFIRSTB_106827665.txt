Sub.: Outcome of Board Meeting  
 
Dear Sir  / Madam,  
 
Pursuant to the applicable provisions of the SEBI (L ODR ) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that the Board 
of Directors of the Bank, at its meeting held today, i.e. October 24, 2026, based on the recommendation of the 
Nomination and Remuneration Committee, has, inter alia , approved the following:  
 
A. Appointment of Part -Time Non -Executive Chairperson:  
The term of Mr. Sanjeeb Chaudhuri (DIN: 03594427) as Part -Time Non -Executive Chairperson (Independent 
Director) of the Bank is due to expire on May 9, 2027.  
 
Accordingly, the Board has approved the appointment of Mr. S. Ganesh Kumar (DIN: 07635860), Independent 
Director, as the Part -Time Non -Executive Chairperson of the Bank, with effect from May 10, 2027 until April 
29, 2029, being the expiry of his existing t erm as an Independent Director, subject to approval of the RBI and 
other regulatory approvals.  
 
B. Re-appointment of Whole -Time Director  
Re-appointment of Mr. Pradeep Natarajan (DIN: 10499651) as Whole -Time Director, designated as Executive 
Director, for a further period of three (3) years upon expiry of his current term on May 31, 2027, subject to 
the approval of the RBI and other requisit e statutory/regulatory approvals.  
 
C. Completion of tenure of Independent Director  
Upon completion of his second term as an Independent Director and the maximum tenure permissible for a 
non-executive director under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, read with the applicable RBI Directions, Mr. 
Aashish Kamat (DIN: 06371682) shall cease to be an Independent Director of the Bank with effect from the 
close of business hours on December 17, 2026.  
 
The Board has placed on record its  deep  appreciation for his valuable guidance and  exceptional contributions  
during his tenure  as an Independent Director on the Board of the Bank . 
 
The details required pursuant to the applicable SEBI Master Circular are enclosed as Annexure s A, B, and C. 
 
The Board Meeting commenced at 10:00 a.m.  and concluded at  02:45 p.m.  
 
Please take the above on record.
