Press Release            
 
Transrail Lighting Secures New Orders Worth ₹ 412 Crore   
Order s come mainly  from reconductoring of Transmission Lines using  
HTLS conductors  manufactured by the Company.  
 
 
Mumbai , October 0 6, 2026  – Transrail Lighting Limited (BSE: 544317, NSE: TRANSRAILL),  one of the leading 
Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) players in the Transmission & Distribution (T&D) sector, 
today announced that it has secured new orders worth ₹ 412 crore in its core Transmission & Distribution 
(T&D) business.  Post these new orders, the order intake for the year has grown to ₹2,021  crore.  The 
Company has further L1 orders totalling to ₹ 409 crore.  
The order wins mainly includes  projects in domestic market for transmission line reconductoring through 
HTLS conductors . These additional reconductoring orders apart from the ones the Company declared few 
days back, will help Transrail to cater to such  new types of projects where the high -performance carbon core 
HTLS conductors which are manufacture d inhouse,  are used to increase the current carrying capacity of the 
Transmission lines.  
With the increased manufacturing capacities (Tower 184,400 MTPA and Conductor 40,800 KMPA) and the tailwinds associated with the T&D sector in both domestic and international markets , the Company is well 
aligned for profitable growth .  
Mr. Randeep Narang, MD & CEO, stated,  
"I am happy to successively announce new order wins worth ₹ 412 crore, which further helps to increase our 
orderbook and reinforce our position as an integrated T&D player. With this we further expand our domestic 
footprint in transmission line reconductoring projects using high- performance carbon- core HTLS conductors 
manufactured by us .  
We are motivated to see the Government’s thrust for the sector by approv ing Phase -III of the Green Energy 
Corridor which will strengthen India’s power infrastructure to support the evacuation of 900 GW of non- fossil 
fuel-based generation. Industry tailwinds like this  coupled with our enhanced manufacturing capacities for 
towers and conductors, makes us well placed to continue our growth and create sustainable value ."
