Dear Madam / Sir, 
In continuation of our intimation dated March 7, 2024, we wish to inform that the Scheme of Merger 
of BSE Tech Infra Services Private limited (“BSE Tech Infra”) (Wholly owned subsidiary of BTPL) with 
BSE Technologies Private Limited (Wholly owned subsidiary of BSE Limited) under Section 233 of the 
Companies Act, 2013 read with Companies (Compromise, Arrangements and Amalgamations) Rules, 
2016, has been approved by the Regional Director, Western Region vide order dated September 24, 
2024. 
BSE Tech Infra and BTPL have filed the aforesaid Order with the Registrar of Companies in the 
prescribed Form INC-28 on October 1, 2024 i.e. today and the Scheme has become effective from 
October 1, 2024 (“Effective Date”). Accordingly, BSE Tech Infra has ceased to exist from effective 
date. 
This intimation is also being made available on the website of the Company at www.bseindia.com
