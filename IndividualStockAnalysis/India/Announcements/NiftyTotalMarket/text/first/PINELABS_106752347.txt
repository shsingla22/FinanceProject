OUR MARKET DEBUT
Our listing at BSE Limited and the NSE Limited marked a watershed moment in 
Pine Labs’ evolution as an institution. The strong response to our public offering, 
reflected in its oversubscription, highlighted investor confidence in the resilience of 
our business model, the scale of our opportunity and our ability to create enduring 
value as a public company.
Our ~H 3900 Crores IPO, structured as a fresh issue 
of H2,080 Crores alongside an Offer for Sale of 
H1,819.9 Crores expanded our shareholder base 
and also drew participation from some of most 
respected institutional investors, demonstrating 
strong conviction in our growth story. The breadth 
of participation spanning domestic mutual funds, 
foreign institutional investors, long-only funds, 
high-net-worth individuals and retail investors reflected the expanded recognition of Pine Labs 
as a category-defining commerce infrastructure 
platform. The listing was a collective endorsement of 
our technology-driven business and our ambition to 
scale across India and beyond. The proceeds from 
the fresh issue would be channelled into deepening 
our technology infrastructure and accelerating 
international expansion, laying a firm foundation for 
the next phase of purposeful, sustainable growth.
A LETTER FROM OUR CEO
B. Amrish Rau  
Chairman, Managing Director,
and Chief Executive Officer, 
Pine LabsDear Members, 
 
Winning transactions sounds simple. But 
it is the hardest thing to do consistently, at 
scale, across merchants, markets, banks, 
and rails that are constantly changing 
underneath. It is also the belief that has 
shaped every decision we have made at Pine 
Labs, and every layer of infrastructure we 
have built beneath it.
Every transaction is a vote of confidence, 
from a consumer, a merchant, a brand, 
or a financial institution. Our journey 
has therefore been about continuously 
increasing the number of transactions we 
win, while expanding the value we create 
around each one.
FY 2025-26 was another important step in that journey — and our first full year of 
profitability. Our platforms processed $194 Billion of GTV, up 50% year on year, across 740 
Crores+ transactions. Revenue from operations grew 19% to ₹2,711 Crores, Adjusted EBITDA 
grew 57% to ₹559 Crores with margins expanding to 21%, and we delivered PAT of ₹113 Crores  
compared with a loss of ₹145 Crores in FY 2024-25. Operating cash flow increased eight-
fold to ₹395 Crores. That swing to profitability demonstrates the operating leverage inherent 
in the platform we have built: as our businesses scale, technology and infrastructure 
investments support significantly greater volumes without a proportionate increase in our 
cost base. 
We now serve more than 11 Lakhs Merchants, with over 20 Lakhs+ digital checkout points  
across our markets. But for us, these numbers are not the destination. They are the foundation 
on which we are building the next phase of Pine Labs.
This is also our first annual report since our IPO, and I want to use it to explain the architecture 
of what we are building, and why it matters more now than at any point since we started.
 
FROM PAYMENTS TO FULL STACK COMMERCE
You may still think of us as an offline payments company. We’ll take that—that’s where you 
first saw us win. But that’s not the whole picture: we don’t see online or offline. We only see the 
transaction. And we’ve built to win it, wherever it happens.

We started with payments acceptance. We then built capabilities around the transaction: 
affordability, EMI, offers, loyalty, issuing, prepaid, online payments, transaction processing, 
and fintech infrastructure. Today, our offerings are increasingly full stack.
Banks want to keep their merchant relationships. Merchants want faster, simpler payment 
systems. Brands want to hold the money that keeps a customer coming back. Pine Labs sits 
inside all three relationships at once, which is why the stack matters more than any single 
layer in it.
