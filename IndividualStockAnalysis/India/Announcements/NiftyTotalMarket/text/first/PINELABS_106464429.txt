Setu launches India's first Agentic Bill Payments Experience on ChatGPT and Claude  
 
Bengaluru, December  9, 2025 – Setu by Pine Labs, today announced the launch of India’s first agentic bill -
payments experience, designed to address two major challenges in bill payments: the fear of incorrect 
charges and the stress of missing due dates.  
While paying a bill takes minutes —keeping track of them takes hours. Most consumers in India spend a 
considerable amount of time tracking, checking, and paying their bills. Setu’s new agentic assistant 
handles this for the consumer by fetching every bill, identifying anomalies by comparing them with past 
patterns, and ensuring timely payments each month.  
Commenting on the launch, B Amrish Rau, CEO, Pine Labs,  said “India’s digital public infrastructure has 
transformed how we pay, but the work behind monthly bills has remained manual.  This agentic 
experience brings automation and trust together, giving people a smarter, safer way to handle their bills.”  
The bill -payment agent operates on simple rules defined by the consumer, ensuring it never initiates an 
incorrect payment or exceeds preset limits.  
Here’s what the agent does: 
● Fetches all bills in real- time  
● Checks bills for unusual charges  
● Pays safely using pre -blocked funds  
● Follows the limits and approvals you set  
● Tracks spending patterns and shares helpful insights  
The experience is designed to be safe and entirely under the consumer’s control. It brings a future -ready 
way of handling bills into the present, with the agent managing routine tasks while the consumer sets the 
boundaries within which it operates, significantly reducing the time and stress associated with monthly 
bill management.  
Now accessible  on Anthropic’s Claude and OpenAI’s ChatGPT, the experience will roll out to additional AI 
platforms in a phased expansion.  
For more details and demo video, please visit: https://blog.setu.co/articles/indias -first-agentic -bill-
payment -experience
