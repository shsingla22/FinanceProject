PRESS RELEASE   
 
Biocon Named Among Science Magazine’s Best Employers in 
Global Biotech and Pharma for 2026  
 
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India: Octo ber 29, 2026 : 
 
Biocon Limited (BSE code: 532523, NSE: BIOCON) , an innovation -led global biopharmaceutical 
company, today announced that it has been ranked No. 13 among the Top 20 Global Employers in 
biotechnology, biopharmaceutical, pharmaceutical, and related industries in the 2026 survey 
conducted by the prestigious U.S. -based Science  magazine . 
 
This recognition reflects Biocon ’s long -standing reputation as an employer of choice in the global life 
sciences industry. Since making its debut on the highly regarded Science Careers Top Employers list in 
2012, Biocon has consistently featured among the industry ’s leading employers.  
 
In the 2026 survey, Biocon was recognized for three key attributes: social responsibility, respect for 
employees, and a culture aligned with employee values.  
 
Shreehas Tambe , CEO and Managing Director , Biocon,  said: “I am proud that Biocon has been 
recognized by Science  magazine as one of the world’s Top 20 biopharmaceutical employers for the 15th 
consecutive year. This recognition reflects the talent, passion and commitment of our people. At Biocon, 
we believe that great science begins with great people. As we advance o ur ambition to become a fully 
integrated global medicines company, we remain committed to fostering a culture of innovation, 
excellence and collaboration enabling us to make a meaningful difference to patients worldwide.”  
 
Biocon's people -centric culture continues to be a key differentiator in strengthening its employer brand 
globally. Today, employees representing 37 nationalities across more than 60 cities worldwide 
contribute diverse perspectives and expertise to Biocon ’s mission of delivering life -changing medicines 
to patients around the world.  
 
Abo ut the Survey  
The Science and Science  Careers’ 2026 Top Employers Survey polled employees in biotechnology, 
biopharmaceutical, pharmaceutical, and related industries to determine the 20 best employers as well 
as their driving characteristics. Respondents to the web -based survey were asked to r ate companies 
based on 24 characteristics that cover categories such as corporate image, financial prowess, 
leadership and direction, and work culture/environment.  
  
The rankings  are determined from a study conducted by an independent research firm commissioned by 
the Science /AAAS Office of Publishing . 
  
The findings are based on approximately 5,100 completed surveys. A vast majority of the survey 
participants came from North America (71%), Europe (19%), and Asia/Pacific Rim (6%).  
  
Survey responses were analyzed by U.S. -based The Brighton Consulting Group, which used a 
mathematical process to determine the driving characteristics of a top employer and to assign a unique 
score to rate each company’s employer reputation.

About Biocon Limited  
Biocon Limited (BSE: 532523, NSE: BIOCON) is a global biopharmaceutical company driven by its purpose to

addresses some of the world’s most pressin g healthcare challenges across chronic and non -communicable

20+ biosimilar assets, as well as GLP -1 peptides and other complex generics. With an integrated lab -to-patient 
model,  Biocon brings together research and development, manufacturing, and commercial capabilities to ensure 
reliable and scalable supply of medicines. The company operates in more than 120 countries, supported by seven 
manufacturing sites, three R&D sites, 18 o ffices worldwide, and a workforce of over 9,500 employees. Biocon has
