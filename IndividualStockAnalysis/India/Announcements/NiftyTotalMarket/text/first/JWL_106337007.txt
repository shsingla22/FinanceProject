EXTRACT OF THE RESOLUTIONS PASSED IN THE MEETING OF THE BOARD OF 
DIRECTORS OF M/S JUPITER WAGONS LIMITED HELD ON TUESDAY, 12TH DAY OF 
AUGUST, 2025 
 
TO CONSIDER AND APPROVE THE REQUEST RECEIVED FROM MS. SAGARIKA 
GUPTA FOR RECLASSIFICATION OF SHAREHOLDING FROM PROMOTER 
CATEGORY TO PUBLIC CATEGORY 
 
It was informed to the Board of Directors that Late Samir Kumar Gupta, one of the Erstwhile Promoter 
of the Company, had passed away on April 9, 2023. His name was accordingly continued to be included 
under the Promoter category in the shareholding pattern filed with both NSE and BSE Limited in 
compliance with  the provisions of Regulation 31A of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”). Consequently, 55,100 equity shares 
of the Company held in his name were transmitted to his legal heir Ms. Sagarika Gupta in accordance 
with applicable laws and regulations. Following such transmission, the name of Ms. Sagarika Gupta  
was included under the Promoter category  in the shareholding pattern submitted to both NSE and 
BSE Limited . 
 
Ms. Sagarika Gupta, Promoter of the Company have expressed her intention not to be classified as 
Promoter or part of the Promoter Group and have accordingly submitted a written request letter dated 
5th August, 2025 to the Company seeking reclassification of her shareholding from “Promoter 
Category” to “Public Category” in accordance with the provisions of Regulation 31A of the SEBI 
Listing Regulations, 2015. 
 
The Board was further informed that the said legal heir, namely Ms. Sagarika Gupta neither exercise 
any control over the affairs of the Company nor hold any key managerial position or directorship in 
the Company and is not involved in the day-to-day management or decision-making of the Company. 
 
The Board, after due consideration and on being satisfied that the conditions as prescribed under 
Regulation 31A of the SEBI Listing Regulations have been duly fulfilled and that such reclassification 
does not violate any provisions of law and is in the best interest of the Company, may consider her 
request for the said reclassification, subject to necessary approvals from the stock exchanges and any 
other regulatory authorities, as may be required. After due discussion, the Board considered the request 
and thereafter passed the following resolutions; 
 
“RESOLVED THAT  the Board of Directors hereby takes note of the transmission of 55,100 equity 
shares of the Company held in the name of erstwhile Promoter, Late  Samir Kumar Gupta, to his legal 
heir, Ms. Sagarika Gupta and in compliance with Regulation 31A of the Securities and Exchange 
Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing 
Regulations”), post transmission of said shares, Ms. Sagarika Gupta has been classified under the 
‘Promoter/Promoter Group’ category in the shareholding pattern of the Company. 
 
RESOLVED FURTHER THAT pursuant to Regulation 31A of the SEBI Listing Regulations, 2015 
and other applicable provisions, including any statutory modifications or re-enactments thereof, and 
subject to necessary approvals from the Stock Exchange(s) and/or other regulatory authorities as may 
 
 
be required, the consent of the Board be and is hereby accorded for the reclassification of the 
shareholding of Ms. Sagarika Gupta, who is part of the Promoter/Promoter Group of the Company, to 
the ‘Public’ category shareholding. 
 
RESOLVED FURTHER THAT  the Board hereby affirm that the said legal heir, Ms. Sagarika Gupta 
do not exercise any control over the Company, is not involved in the management of the Company, 
and does not hold any special rights in the Company through formal or informal arrangements. 
 
RESOLVED FURTHER THAT  pursuant to provisions of Regulation 31A of SEBI Listing 
Regulations, 2015, Ms. Sagarika Gupta, Outgoing Promoter Shareholder has confirmed that she and 
the “persons related to her”:  
 
 do not hold more than 10% of the fully paid up equity share capital and voting rights of the 
Company.;  
 do not exercise control over the affairs of the Company, directly or indirectly;  
 do not have any special rights through formal or informal arrangements including through any 
shareholder agreements, if any, granting special rights to her shall be terminated;  
 shall not be represented on the Board of Directors (including not having a nominee director) 
of the Company;  
 shall not act as a key managerial personnel in the Company;  
 and shall at all times from the date of such reclassification, continue to comply with conditions 
mentioned under sub regulation (4) of Regulation 31A of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, 
post reclassification from “Promoter Group” Shareholder to “Public” Shareholder; 
 
RESOLVED FURTHER THAT  as required under the provisions of Regulation 31A of SEBI Listing 
Regulations, 2015, Ms. Sagarika Gupta, the Outgoing Promoter Shareholder has further confirmed 
that she is neither a ‘wilful defaulter’ as per the Reserve Bank of India Guidelines nor a fugitive 
economic offender. 
 
RESOLVED FURTHER THAT  the Company shall file necessary applications with the Stock 
Exchanges for approval of the said reclassification and shall update the shareholding pattern 
accordingly upon receipt of requisite approvals. 
 
RESOLVED FURTHER THAT any of Mr. Vivek Lohia, Managing Director or Mr. Vikash Lohia, 
Deputy Managing Director or in their absence any other directors of the Company or Mr. Ritesh 
Kumar Singh, Company Secretary of the Company be and are hereby severally authorized to sign and 
submit all necessary documents, declarations, and filings with the Stock Exchanges and other 
regulatory authorities, and to do all such acts, deeds, and things as may be necessary to give effect to 
this resolution.”
