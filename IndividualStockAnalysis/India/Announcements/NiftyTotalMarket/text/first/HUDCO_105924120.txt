Housing and Urban Development Corporation Limited (HUDCO) has signed a non-binding 
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Government of Rajasthan through its Department 
of Finance, on 24th July, 2024 at Jaipur, Rajasthan. The MoU is an in-principle arrangement 
between the parties with the objective to provide financial assistance for housing and urban 
infrastructure projects in the State of Rajasthan to the extent of Rs. 1,00,000.00 Crore (for next 5 
years), subject to mutually agreed terms and conditions. 
 
The MoU was executed by Shri Sanjay Kulshrestha, CMD, HUDCO and Shri Debasish Prusty, 
IAS, Secretary to Government, Finance (Budget) Department, Rajasthan in the presence of      
Shri Bhajan Lal Sharma, Hon’ble Chief Minister, Rajasthan.
