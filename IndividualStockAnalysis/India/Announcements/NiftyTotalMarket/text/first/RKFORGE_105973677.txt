> DIVESTMENT QOF 100% EQUITY STAKE HELD IN ITS WHOLLY QWNED SUBSIDIARY. 
GLOBE ALL INDIA SERVICES LIMITED (“GAISL") 
Divestment of 100% equity stake held in its Wholly-owned Subsidiary, Globe All India Services 
Limited (“GAISL”) to Yatra Online Limited (“Buyer”) for an aggregate consideration of INR 
128,00,00,000 (Indian Rupees One Hundred Twenty Eight Crore only), net of Debt. GAISL is not a 
material subsidiary of the Company. The divestment of 100% equity stake in GAISL will be subject 
to the completion of conditions precedent and closing actions as specified in the share purchase 
agreement being executed by and among the Company, Buyer and GAISL (“SPA”). 
Post the completion of the proposed divestment, the Company will cease to hold any shareholding 
in GAISL, and consequently GAISL will cease to be a subsidiary of the Company.
