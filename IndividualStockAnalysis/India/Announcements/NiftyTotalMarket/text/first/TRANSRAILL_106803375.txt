Press Release            
 
Transrail increases its Tower Manufacturing  capacity  
by another 12,000 MTPA   
 
Revised total capacity stands at 184,400 MTPA  
 
 
Mumbai, September 30 , 202 6: Transrail Lighting Limited (BSE: 544317, NSE: TRANSRAILL), one of the 
leading EPC players in the Transmission & Distribution (T&D) sector, has further increased its Tower 
Manufacturing capacity by 12,000 MTPA (Metric Tonnes Per Annum)  as part of its brownfield expansion at 
Silvassa location .  
With this expansion the company’s Tower manufacturing capacity has increased from 172,400 MTPA  to 
184,400 MTPA . Also important to note is that apart from this current increase, the company is further in the 
process of enhancing its tower capacity as part of its phase 2 expansion , which once achieved w ill take the 
overall capacity of the Company beyond 200,000 MTPA .  
Commenting on the development, Mr. Randeep Narang, MD & CEO, stated,  “This expansion will contribute 
to helping us  further strengthen our manufacturing base which will in turn assist in executing our large order 
book and target growth. We continue to further enhance our tower manufacturing capacity and will take it 
beyond 200,000 MTPA once the planned capex program is fully completed. ” 
  
About Transrail Lighting Limited:  
Transrail is a leading turnkey engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) company with primary focus 
on power transmission and distribution business with 4 decades of experience. With its headquarters in India, 
it is a global enterprise with a footpri nt in 64 countries across 6 continents. It provides turnkey solutions from 
Designing, Engineering, Supply, Manufacture, Construction, Testing  services across all its business verticals 
which include Transmission Lines, Substations, Civil Construction, Railways, Solar EPC and Pole s & Lighting. 
It has more than 2,800 employees. As part of Power T&D business, Transrail has large -scale manufacturing 
facilities in India for Galvanized Lattice Towers, Overhead Conductors and Galvanized Monopoles in addition 
to a well -accredited Tower tes ting facility.
