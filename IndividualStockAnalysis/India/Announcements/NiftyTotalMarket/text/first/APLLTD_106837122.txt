PRESS RELEASE 
30th October, 2026 Vadodara, India 
 
Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited receives USFDA Final Approval for Venetoclax 
Tablets, 10 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg. 
 
Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited (Alembic) today announced that it has received final 
approval from the US Food & Drug Administration (USFDA) for its Abbreviated New Drug 
Application (ANDA) for Venetoclax Tablets, 10 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg. The approved ANDA 
is therapeutically equivalent to the reference listed drug product (RLD), Venclexta Tablets, 
10 mg, 50 mg, and 100 mg, of AbbVie Inc. (AbbVie). Venetoclax tablets are indicated for the 
treatment of adult patients with chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) or small lymphocytic 
lymphoma (SLL). Venetoclax tablets are also indicated, in combination with azacitidine, or 
decitabine, or low-dose cytarabine, for the treatment of newly diagnosed acute myeloid 
leukemia (AML) in adults 75 years or older, or who have comorbidities that preclude use of 
intensive induction chemotherapy. Refer label for a detailed indication and usage. 
 
Alembic was a first ANDA applicant to submit a substantially complete ANDA with a 
paragraph IV certification for Venetoclax Tablets, 10 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg. Therefore, with 
this approval, Alembic may be eligible for 180 days of generic drug exclusivity. 
  
According to IQVIA, Venetoclax tablets, 10 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg, had an estimated market 
size of US $1,584 million in the United States for twelve months ending June 2026. 
   
Alembic has a cumulative total of 248 ANDA approvals (228 final approvals and 20 tentative 
approvals) from the USFDA.
