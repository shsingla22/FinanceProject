not exceeding INR 130,00,00,000 (Indian Rupees One Hundred and Thirty Crores only) ("Buyback Size") from all of the Equity

Companies Act, rules framed thereunder including the Share Capital Rules and Management Rules, to the extent applicable, and 
the Listing Regulations ("Buyback"). The Buyback Size does not include any expenses incurred or to be incurred for the Buyback

("Transaction Costs"). The Buyback Size constitutes 9.68% and 9.66% of the aggregate of the fully paid-up equity share capital

March 31, 2024, respectively (i.e., the last audited financial statements available as on the date of Board Meeting recommending 
the proposal of the Buyback).
