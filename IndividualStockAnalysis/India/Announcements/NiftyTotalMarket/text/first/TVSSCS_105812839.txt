TVS SCS achieves milestone of 500,000 two-wheeler CKD kits  for its customer   
• Demonstrate s a consistent pattern of adding 1 lakh CKD Kits to the milestone every 5 months  
Chennai, April 11, 202 4: TVS Supply Chain Solutions Limited (NSE: TVSSCS, BOM: 543965), a global 
supply chain solutions provider and one of the largest and fastest growing integrated supply chain 
solutions providers in India, today announced that they achieved a milestone of 500,000 CKD 
(Complete Knock -Down) kits for its c ustomer , TVS Motor Company. This was achieved in TVS SCS’ 
dedicated warehouse facility at Hosur. The company’s advanced  warehouse  facility spanning 1,50,000  
sq. ft, ensures defect -free dispatches and prevents parts mismatch.  
At its Hosur facility, TVS SCS provides end -to-end value -added services like, production planning, 
quality management, inbound material activities, kitting, reverse logistics, export, etc. The fully 
automated facility achieved its first milestone of 1 00,000 CKD kits in July 2022 , subsequently, it 
demonstrated  a consistent pattern of adding one lakh  kits to the milestone once in every five months.  
Says  Mr. Ravi Viswanatha n, Managing Director, TVS Supply Chain Solutions , “It is a  remarkable 
milestone that highlights the significance  of our digital capabilities , scalability and adaptab ility that 
ensure d we stay ed in line with our customers' evolving requirements . Through our value -added 
service s we deliver world -class sub -assembly and kitting processes b y utilising AI and visual 
technology .” 
The CKD kits cater to TVS Motor's 26 export markets in Asia and Africa. TVS SCS’s ultra -modern tech -
driven facility  supports in enhancing global export capabilities through CKD assembly with a focus on 
achieving zero defects.  
Currently, around 220 personnel are employed across various production departments  at its Hosur  
facility. This includes 30 women employees involved in various activities  like primary pack ing, 
secondary packing, quality,  and binning processes  of the CKD kits .

development of the supply chain solutions marke t in India.  For the last 15 years, TVS SCS has managed large and

consumer, tech and tech infra, rail and utilities, and healthcare.
