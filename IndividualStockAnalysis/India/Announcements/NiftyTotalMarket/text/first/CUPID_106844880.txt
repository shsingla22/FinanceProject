Cupid Limited Announces Landmark Long-Term Exclusive Retail 
Partnership with V2 Retail Across 425+ Stores; Targets ₹400 Crore of 
Incremental Annual Revenue  
Cupid Secures Dedicated 400 Sq. Ft. Retail Space in Every Existing and Future V2 Retail Sto re, 
Creating One of Its Largest Organised Retail Expansion Platforms Across India  
Mumbai, November 04, 2026: Cupid Limited  (“Cupid” or “the Company”), one of India’s fastest-growing pe rsonal 
care, wellness and consumer healthcare companies, today announced that it has entered into a landmark long-term 
exclusive retail arrangement with V2 Retail Limited , one of India’s leading and fastest-expanding value retail 
chains.  
Under this strategic arrangement, Cupid Limited will receive 400 square feet of dedicated and exclusive retail 
space across V2 Retail’s entire network of over 425 stores , as well as all future stores opened by V2 Retail across 
India.  
The partnership represents a significant milestone in Cupid’s domestic FMCG expansion strategy, providing the 
Company with an immediate platform for large-scale consumer engageme nt, enhanced brand visibility and 
accelerated distribution of its growing personal care and wellness po rtfolio.  
Management expects the V2 Retail partnership to generate approximately ₹400 crore in revenue within the next 
12 months . Supply operations are expected to commence shortly, with Cupid products targeted to be available 
across V2 Retail’s entire store network by mid-December 2026 , enabling the Company to accelerate its nationwide 
retail presence and strengthen its domestic FMCG business.  
Key Highlights of the Landmark Partnership 
• 425+ Stores, Nationwide Reach:  Immediate access to V2 Retail’s extensive and rapidly expanding reta il 
network across India. 
• 400 Sq. Ft. Exclusive Retail Space Per Store:  Dedicated Cupid retail presence representing over 1.7 lakh 
square feet across the current store network upon full rollout. 
• ₹400 Crore Revenue Target: Management expects the partnership to generate approximately ₹400 crore in 
revenue within the next 12 months, supported by nationwide retail distributio n and expanding product 
availability. 
• Nationwide Rollout by December 2026:  Supply operations are expected to commence shortly, with product 
availability targeted across all V2 Retail stores by mid-December 20 26. 
• Long-Term Structural Growth Opportunity:  The arrangement extends to all existing and future V2 Retail 
stores, ensuring that Cupid’s retail footprint expands alongside V2 Retail’s growth.  
• Expanding Product Portfolio:  The dedicated retail space will showcase Cupid’s growing range of pe rsonal 
care, wellness, hygiene and other consumer products, with new categories  and products introduced 
progressively. 
A Transformational Step in Cupid’s Domestic Consumer Business  
The partnership is expected to significantly accelerate Cupid Limited’s  transition into a scaled, consumer-facing 
personal care and wellness brand. By securing a dedicated and excl usive retail footprint within V2 Retail’s nationwide 
ecosystem, Cupid will gain direct access to millions of potential consumers , strengthening brand recall, product 
discovery and repeat purchases.  

 
   The arrangement also provides a highly scalable retail distribution platform, enabling Cupid to introduce new 
products and categories  across an established national retail network without having to indepe ndently develop a 
comparable physical store infrastructure.  
Importantly, Cupid’s presence will expand automatically with V2 Retail’s future store additions , creating a long-
term avenue for sustained business growth and increasing market penetration.  
Strong Visibility for Sustained Growth 
Cupid Limited believes the arrangement will provide multiple long-term growth advantages , including 
accelerated brand penetration, improved visibility  across organised retail, stronger consumer engagement  and 
the ability to scale new product launches  through an established nationwide network.  
The Company expects the partnership to become a major contributor to its domestic FMCG business  as the rollout 
progresses, with the combination of dedicated retail infrastructure, e xpanding product offerings and V2 Retail’s 
future store additions creating substantial growth opportunities.  
The ₹400 crore first-year business expectation reflects management’s  assessment of the partnership’s commercial 
potential and is subject to rollout progress, consumer demand, product availability and operating performance.  
Cupid remains focused on building a diversified consumer and healthcare platform through its domestic FMCG 
operations, international healthcare business, expanding manufacturing  capabilities and strategic distribution 
partnerships.  
The V2 Retail partnership marks another significant step in this strateg y and reinforces Cupid’s ambition to emerge 
as a major force in India’s fast -growing personal care and wellness industry.

About  Cupid Limited 
Established in 1993 , Cupid Limited is one of Bharat's leading healthcare and consumer wellness companies , 
engaged in the manufacturing and marketing of male and female condom s, water-based personal lubricants, IVD 
kits and a growing portfolio of Consumer Healthcare & FMCG produ cts. The Company operates with a strong 
commitment to public health and quality, maintaining ethical business pra ctices aligned with international 
standards. 
Cupid's Consumer Healthcare & FMCG portfolio includes fragrance  products (Eau De Parfums, Deodorants and 
Pocket Perfumes), personal care products (Hair Removal Spray s, Face Wash, Hair & Body Oils, Toilet Sanitizers), 
wellness products, IVD kits, personal lubricants and other consume r healthcare offerings. The Company continues 
to strengthen its domestic presence through an expanding network acros s modern trade, organised retail and 
pharmacy channels , enabling deeper market penetration across India. 
The Company is also expanding its manufacturing capabilities through i ts upcoming Palava manufacturing 
facility , which will significantly enhance production flexibility and support the g rowing demand across both 
international B2B healthcare and domestic Consumer Healthcare & FMCG busi nesses. 
Cupid has a strong international presence with exports to 125+ countries  and is the first company in the world to 
receive WHO/UNFPA prequalification for both male and female condoms . The Company serves a diversified global 
customer base through PFSCM, WHO/UNFPA, institutional procurement programmes, government tenders and 
private export markets , reinforcing its position as a trusted global healthcare supplier . 
Cupid Limited is listed on the BSE (530843)  and NSE (CUPID) .
