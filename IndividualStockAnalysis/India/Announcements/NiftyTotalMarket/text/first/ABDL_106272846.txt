ARTHAUS Blended Malt Scotch , ZOYA  Gin and ICONiQ White Whisky Shines at Monde 
Selection 2025  
Allied Blenders and Distillers  recognised globally for 3 of its newest in brand portfolio  
Mumbai, 1 9th June 2025  - Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD), the largest domestic 
spirits company in India by volume  has earned global acclaim at the esteemed Monde 
Selection 2025 World Selection of Spirits and Liqueurs , with three of its brands  - Arthaus 
Blended Malt Scotch Whisky, Zoya Special Batch Gin, and ICONiQ White Whisky  each 
winning the Gold Quality Award .  
Arthaus and Zoya are key brands in the super -premium and luxury thrust of the company, 
while ICONIQ White Whisky has recently been listed as the fastest growing spirits brand for 
2024 by Drinks International in its highly acclaimed Millionaires Club 2025 report:  the second 
consecutive year running and an unprecedented achievement.  
  
This global honour is a proud moment for ABD, as it focuse s on the journey toward s 
premiumisation driven by innovation, craftsmanship, and a deep understanding of evolving 
consumer trends  

Bikram Basu, Managing Director, ABD Maestro  Private  Limited  added “Recognition from Monde 
Selection Brussels 2025 is a significant testament of the vision and values guiding our portfolio. We 
created ICONiQ three years ago and it has truly been a gamechanger. In the past 18 months, we have 
followed it with Zoya and Arthaus - both super -premium and luxury products. The accolades reflect 
product excellence and highlight the dedication, skill, and passion of the teams who brought these 
brands to life. As an Indian company on a global stage, we remain committed to setting new 
benchmarks in quality and creativity.”  
The Monde Selection Gold Quality Award is a globally recognized seal of quality that assures 
consumers they are choosing a product that meets the highest international standards . 
Receiving this award reflects ABD’s ongoing commitment to maintaining high levels of quality 
and consistency, and to meeting the expectations of its customers  
About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited  
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD) is the largest domestic Spirits company in India, in terms of 
annual sales volumes. ABD has a presence in five main flavors, i.e., whisky, brandy, rum, vodka, and 
gin, with ‘millionaire’ brands like Officer’s Ch oice Whisky, Officer’s Choice Blue Whisky, Sterling Reserve 
Premium Whiskies and ICONiQ White Whisky. Currently, its manufacturing network comprises 36 units, 
of which 9 are owned bottling units, 2 owned distilleries, and 25 non -owned manufacturing units.  
 
About ABD Maestro Private Limited  
ABD Maestro Private Limited is a new super -premium and luxury spirits brand company and a 
subsidiary of ABD. The brands which ABD Maestro is marketing currently  include Arthaus Blended Malt 
Scotch Whisky, Zoya Special Batch Gin and its flavours, Woodburns Contemporary Indian Malt Whisky, 
Pumori Small Batch Gin and Pink Gin, Segredo Aldeia Café and White Rums. Through its partnership 
with Roust Corporation, the company has introduced  Russian Standard Vodka to India in three distinct 
segments: Original, Gold, and Platinum .
