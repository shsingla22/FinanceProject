Blitz 2025: Happiest Minds’ 8th Annual Tech Fest Spotlights 
Disruption, Innovation & Acceleration  
Bengaluru, August 6, 2025: Happiest Minds Technologies Limited (NSE , BSE : HAPPSTMNDS), a ‘Born 
Digital. Born Agile’, Mindful IT Company , hosted the grand finale of its 8th annual technology festival, 
Blitz, on July 24, 2025, at St. John’s Auditorium, Bengaluru. This year’s theme, Disruption. Innovation. 
Acceleration. , shaped the tone of the event, showing up consistently across sessions, showcases,  and 
conversations, reflecting the ongoing transformation within the company and the broader tech 
ecosystem.  
The event was inaugurated with the traditional lighting of the lamp by senior leadership, including Ashok 
Soota ( Chairman & Chief  Mentor ), Joseph Anantharaju (Co -Chairman & CEO), Venkatraman Narayanan 
(Managing Director), Preeti Menon (Chief Delivery Officer, PDES), Priya Prasad (IG Head: Retail, CPG & Logistics), Sridhar Mantha (President & CEO of GBS), Rajiv Shah (Executive Director), Po ornima 
Bethmangalkar (IG Head: Industrial, Manufacturing, Energy & Utilities), and Ritesh Gupta (CTO, PDES).  
 
 
The event opened with a welcome address by Joseph Anantharaju. He spoke about Blitz’s evolution into 
a strategic platform that goes beyond being just a festival. With the rapid pace of GenAI advancements, 
he emphasized how Blitz has become essential for st aying ahead and driving alignment across the 
organization. He also outlined the company’s ongoing transformation journey, including verticalization, the launch of GBS, and the establishment of dedicated sales teams.  
 

 
 
 
 
 
This was followed by the keynote address from Ashok Soota, who highlighted the value of foundational 
thinking in today’s fast -changing tech landscape. He underscored the need to deeply understand 
customer needs, identify gaps early, and focus on building s ustainabl e solutions. Drawing from this year’s 
showcases, he appreciated the clarity and domain expertise demonstrated by various teams across the 
organization.  
The tech note this year was delivered by Ritesh Gupta, who focused on the growing importance of using AI effectively and responsibly in the modern workplace.  
Over the course of the day, Happiest Minds from Bengaluru, Noida , Hyderabad, Pune, Bhubaneswar, 
Madurai, Coimbatore,  and Chennai came together to celebrate ideas in motion through live sessions, 
technology booths, debates, quizzes, and cultural showcases. While Bengaluru teams joined on -ground, 
other campuses participated virtually and celebrated in their own style.  
The competition lineup included One- Minute Video, Tech Debate, Tech Accelerator, Technical 
Whitepapers, Vlog (Women- only), Treasure Hunt, Tech Quiz, Prompt Engineering Contest, and the ever -
popular Tech Booths.  
Team Data Dynamos  emerged as the overall winners of Blitz 2025, with a total of 884 points. Their 
consistent and high -quality performance across categories earned them the top spot. Speaking about 
their journey, the team shared how each round challenged them to push bounda ries, think creatively and 
deepen their collaboration. Blitz helped them learn, adapt and innovate as a team.  
Held on July 23, 2025, Booth Day featured 39 booths  across locations, including 29 in Bengaluru. The 
booths presented practical solutions in AI, analytics, automation, sustainability and security, each built 
around real customer needs.  
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
The top three booths at Blitz 2025 stood out for their innovation, execution, and real -world impact.  
Marketing Analytics Solutions secured the winning spot for its strong application of data -driven insights. 
Electrify AI: Powering the GPU Era followed as the first runner -up, impressing with its relevance to 
emerging computing demands. ScanAssist: Instant  Product Intelligence for Retail was named second 
runner -up for its smart take on enhancing retail experiences. Together, these solutions reflected a clear 
customer focus, practical problem -solving, and sharp technical execution.  
Winners across categories were felicitated by the senior leadership team across the locations.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
  
During the event, Paul Jacob , Happiness Evangelist and Senior Director , People Practice , announced the 
launch of the ‘ Happometer ’ Widget. This mood check -in tool allows team members to select an emoji 
based on how they feel and receive a personalized suggestion, such as a guided meditation, a podcast, 
or relevant content. It’s a simple shift, designed to make emotional wellness more intuitive and accessible 
across teams.  
Blitz stands as a space for focused thinking, real collaboration and purposeful innovation. It's where ideas 
are shaped by real -world challenges and driven by curiosity, clarity and intent.
