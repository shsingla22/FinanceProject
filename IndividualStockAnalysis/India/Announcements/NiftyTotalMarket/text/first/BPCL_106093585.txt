 In terms of Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements) Regulations , 
2015, we wish to inform you that BPCL participated in NTPC’s tender for the selection of Solar 
Power Generators to establish 1200 MW ISTS -connected solar PV power projects across India. 
During the Reverse Auction, BPCL emerged as the lowest bidder for a capacity of 150 MW. Upon 
finalization of the contract, the project will be developed over a two- years timeline at an estimated 
capital outlay of Rs 756.45 crore and is projected to generate annual revenue of approximately Rs 100 crore by producing around 400 million units of clean energy. This milestone highlights B PCLs 
commitment to advancing renewable energy initiatives and contributing to India’s clean energy transition.
