Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
This is in continuation to our earlier intimation dated June 24, 2025, wherein we had informed 
that the Board of Directors of Zydus Wellness Limited (“the Company ”) and Naturell (India) 
Private Limited (“ NIPL”), a wholly owned subsidiary, have accorded their in-principle approval 
for voluntary liquidation of NIPL and expeditious consolidation of NIPL’s business with the 
Company on a going concern basis, subject to approval of Members of NIPL and compliance 
of applicable laws. 
 
In this regard, we wish to now inform that the members of NIPL in their Extra Ordinary 
General Meeting held today i.e. July 1, 2025, have accorded their approval for voluntary 
liquidation of NIPL and expeditious consolidation of NIPL’s business with the Company on a 
going concern basis, subject to consent of creditors of NIPL and compliance of applicable laws. 
Accordingly, subject to approval of the creditors of NIPL, the process shall be deemed to have 
been commenced from July 1, 2025.
