Hyderabad, India, October  19th, 2026 : NATCO  Pharma Limited  (BSE:  524816  and NSE: NATCO PHARM) ("NATCO") today  
announce s launch of Carboplatin Injection , a generic version  of Paraplatin ® in the U.S. market by its U.S. subsidiary             
NATCO Pharma USA LLC.  NATCO launche s this product in manufacturing collaboration with Teyro Labs Pvt Ltd , based in
