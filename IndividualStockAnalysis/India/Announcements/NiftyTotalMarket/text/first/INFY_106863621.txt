PRESS RELEASE 
Infosys Positioned as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant ™ for Public 
Cloud Optimization and Transformation Services for the Fourth Consecutive Year  
Recognition highlights Infosys’ AI-first, platform -led approach to continuous cloud optimization 
and transformation across hybrid multi -cloud environments  
Bengaluru, India –  November 17, 2026: Infosys  (NSE, BSE, NYSE:INFY), a global leader in AI -first 
business consulting and technology services, today announced that it has been positioned as a Leader in 
the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant ™ for Public Cloud Optimization and Transformation Services*, for the 
fourth year in a row.  
  
This recognition reflects Infosys' strength in helping enterprises achieve business outcomes through cloud 
transformation and optimization. It is also a testament of Infosys' ability to execute and completeness of 
vision, reflecting the reliability and scale of Infosys' delivery across enterprises' cloud and AI modernization 
journeys.  
  
Through Infosys Cobalt  and Infosys Topaz , with a productized service delivery approach on the Topaz 
Fabric , Infosys helps enterprises modernize hybrid multi -cloud environments and reimagine business 
processes in an AI -first manner. With AI embedded across the migration, modernization, operations, and 
optimization lifecycles, Infosys accelerates enterprise cloud journeys through zero- touch operations, self -
healing systems, and AIOps -driven resiliency, built on a secure and sovereign cloud and AI foundation with 
intrinsic guardrails. Across engagements spanning industries, Infosys helps clients transform complex legacy landscapes, including mainframe and data centre -based estates, into cloud- first, API -driven, and 
serverless environments, applying cloud -native engineering, DevSecOps, zero- trust security, and FinOps 
to improve agility, efficiency, and time to market for its clients.  
 
 
Anant Adya, EVP and Service Offering Head, Infosys, said "Enterprises today need technology 
programs that deliver measurable business value, not just migration outcomes. Our continued investments in cloud, powered by the integration of Infosys Cobalt and Infosys Topaz, are helping clients modernize 
faster, reduc e risk, and cost, and build leaner, smarter business processes at scale. We're backing this with 
a deliberate talent strategy, upskilling our workforce and building innovation labs across regions to co-
innovate the future with our clients. Our positioning as a Leader for the fourth consecutive year in the 
Gartner Magic Quadrant for Public Cloud Optimization and Transformation Services reflects our ability to 
consistently deliver on client expectations and drive real business outcomes from cloud transformati on." 
 
 
*Gartner® Magic Quadrant ™ for Public Cloud Optimization and Transformation Services was previously 
named as the Gartner® Magic Quadrant ™ for Public Cloud IT Transformation Services.
