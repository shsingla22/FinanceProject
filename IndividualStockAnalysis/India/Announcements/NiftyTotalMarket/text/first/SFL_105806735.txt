Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
We are pleased to inform you that the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, 
(“DCDRC”), East Delhi, announced an order on March 11, 2024, in favor of our company in 
the matter of an insurance claim. A brief Order of DCDRC was uploaded on its website on 
April 3, 2024.  As per the order, the company has been awarded compensation amounting to 
Rs. 21,38,77,055/- along with an interest rate of 10% from the date of rejection, i.e., July 27, 
2020. Additionally, the company has been granted Rs. 7,50,000 for mental anguish and losses, 
and Rs. 2,50,000 for legal expenses. The detailed Order is yet to be uploaded on its website. 
 
In compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as well as the SEBI Circular dated July 13, 2023, we have 
provided the requisite details in Annexure 1 , enclosed herewith. 
 
Furthermore, this information will be available on the company's official website at 
www.sheelafoam.com.
