Airtel offers Free International Roaming on All Postpaid Plans  
 
Announces launch of New Postpaid plans with added benefit of Free International 
Roaming, along with Unlimited Data, Fast Lane tech, safety features and 
entertainment  
  
New Delhi, October 7, 2026:  Bharti Airtel (“Airtel”), one of India’s leading telecommunications service 
providers, today announced the launch of  new postpaid plans , with even more benefits built in. From 
October 8, 2026, c ustomers can enjoy one international trip with free roaming every year with these new 
postpaid plans, along with all existing benefits.  
  
The complimentary International Roaming pack offers 5GB of data, 60 minutes of calls, 25 outgoing SMS, 
and free incoming SMS, all valid for 5 days. Customers have complete flexibility to activate the 5- day pack 
at the exact time of their choice,  making it easier to stay connected wh ile travelling abroad. For 
customers  on Airtel Postpaid family plans,  the free International Roaming option is available for  each and 
every family member who is part of the plan.  
  
Free International Roaming is the latest addition to Airtel Postpaid plans which come with superior 
experience through Fast Lane technology, Unlimited Data,  network -level protection against spam 
and fraudulent links.  Customers can also enjoy a  range of entertainment subscriptions  and access to 
exclusive experiences through the Airtel Postpaid Advantage Club, including comedy shows, movie premieres and concerts.  
  
Commenting on the launch, Amit Tripathi, Director – Market Operations, Bharti Airtel, 
said,  “Postpaid for us  is about delivering the best -in class  experience in the Indian market . We have the 
best network experience in the country, on which we offer our postpaid customers Fast Lane technology 
on 5G, and constant Spam and frauds protection.  What is more, we offer the customers the best value –
through access to entertainment and services that enrich their everyday lives ; and now  Free International 
Roaming for them.  We are determined to constantly raising the game for customers and give 
them more and more reasons to choose Airtel Postpaid. ” 
  
How can customers get the new plans?  
Effective October 8, 2026, these postpaid plans are available for all customers opting for Airtel 
Postpaid.  Existing Airtel customers  (both on old postpaid plans or on prepaid)  can easily  upgrade  to 
these plans  through the Airtel App or by visiting the nearby Airtel Store.  Non-Airtel customers  can switch to 
Airtel Postpaid by  raising a switching request  to Airtel through the Airtel App or by visiting an Airtel store.

About Bharti Airtel:   
Headquartered in India, Airtel is a global communications solutions provider with over 650 million customers in 15 countries across 
India and Africa. The company also has its presence in Bangladesh and Sri Lanka through its associate entities. The company is 
ranked second amongst mobile operators globally, and its networks cover over two billion people. Airtel is India’s largest in tegrated 
communications solutions provider and the second largest mobile operator in Africa. Airtel’s retail portfolio includes high- speed 4G/5G 
mobile, Wi -Fi (FTTH+ FWA) that promises speeds up to 1 Gbps with convergence across linear and on- demand entertainment, video 
streaming services, digital payments and financial services. For enterprise customers, Airtel offers a gamut of s olutions that includes 
secure connectivity, cloud and data center services, cyber security, IoT, and cloud- based communication. Airtel’s digital arm – Xtelify, 
empowers telcos globally to leverage the power of AI, data and technology to accelerate their di gital transformation and drive growth. 
Xtelify also offers Airtel Cloud in India enabling enterprises with a sovereign, telco- grade cloud platform that guarantees secure 
migration, effortless scaling, lower costs and no vendor lock - ins. Within its diversi fied portfolio, Airtel also offers passive infrastructure 
services  through its subsidiary Indus Tower Ltd. For more details visit  www.airtel.com
