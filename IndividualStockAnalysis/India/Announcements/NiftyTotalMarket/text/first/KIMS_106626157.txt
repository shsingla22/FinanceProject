Glimpses of Clinical achievements during the Q4 – 2 026 
KIMS Secunderabad 
 
• In a significant milestone for hearing healthcare i n India, Doctors at KIMS hospitals have 
successfully performed the country’s first smart Co chlear implant surgery on a 3-year-old child 
suffering from severe hearing loss. By receiving su ch implant at such a tender age, the child is 
expected to have better hearing ability and improve d language development. 
 
KIMS Kondapur  
 
• Doctors at KIMS Cuddles, Kondapur achieved a major clinical success by saving the life of a 14-
year-old girl using ultra-rapid veno-venous extraco rporeal membrane oxygenation (VV-ECMO). 
The child was admitted in a critical condition with  severe pneumococcal pneumonia, empyema, 
profound hypoxaemia and septic shock. The patient w as supported on VV-ECMO for eight days.  
 
• A highly complex case of chronic jaundice that was deemed inoperable even at leading 
international centres was successfully treated. The  multidisciplinary team devised a two-stage 
surgical strategy. In the first stage an extremely complex shunt surgery was performed diverting 
the intestinal blood flow to a renal vein. In the s econd stage a marathon surgery lasting 10 hours 
was performed to permanently relieve both the bile duct and intestinal obstructions. 
 
• A challenging laparoscopic Whipple’s surgery was su ccessfully performed on a patient with a 
prior Kidney transplant who was diagnosed with Panc retic cancer. Performing major pancreatic 
surgery on some one on immunosuppressive therapy ca rries high risk with mortality rates up to 
50%. 
 
KIMS Seethammadhara 
 
• A team of doctors at KIMS Hospital at Seethammadhar a, Vizag, successfully treated a 65-year-old 
man who was at high risk of fatal stroke due to cri tical blockages in key arteries supplying blood 
to the brain. Considering the seriousness an emerge ncy endovascular procedure was performed 
following which the patient recovered well. 
 
• A marathon 9 hour-long advanced surgical procedure was successfully performed on a 42-year-
old woman suffering from ovarian cancer. The proced ure was performed using cytoreductive 
surgery combined with Hyperthermic Intraperitoneal Chemotherapy (HIPEC).  
 
KIMS ICON  
 
• In a remarkable medical feat, doctors at KIMS ICON hospital, have successfully treated a 17-year-
old boy who sustained a rare and complex kidney inj ury following a road accident, which also led 
to the discovery of an even rarer congenital anomal y. Doctors diagnosed a pelvi-Ureteric junction 
(PUJ) avulsion a complete and traumatic disconnecti on. Medical literature indicates that PUJ 
avulsion from blunt trauma is extremely uncommon. I ts occurrence in a kidney with a duplex 
system is considered exceptionally rare. 
 
• 37-year-old mam who sustained severe injuries follo wing a blast at his workplace and exposure 
to ammonia gas and steam, was saved by a team of do ctors who immediately initiated VV-ECMO 
to provide temporary support for lung function and maintain oxygenation. 
 
 
 
KIMS Anantapur  
 
• A woman with rare reproductive anomaly diagnosed wi th cervical cancer was successfully 
treated by performing a complex surgery. This condi tion is not the commonly known MRKH 
syndrome, but an exceptionally rare developmental a nomaly of the female reproductive system. 
 
• A successful Cochlear implant surgery was performed  on 57-year-old woman. This is the first 
time such a procedure has been conducted on a patie nt of this age group in Rayalaseema region.  
 
KIMS Kurnool 
 
• A rare ABO -incompatible kidney transplant was perf ormed, giving a 28-year-old patient a new 
lease of life. The patient received kidney from his  father despite their incompatible blood groups. 
• The doctors successfully corrected a severe S- shap ed spinal deformity in an 18-year-old girl 
through a highly complex surgery involving the plac ement of 30 spinal screws. Remarkably, the 
patient was able to walk independently from the fir st day after surgery, and her vital functions 
returned to normal soon after. 
• A rare and highly complex brain vascular procedure using an advanced minimally invasive 
technique was performed saving the life of a young woman who presented with severe 
headache. Diagnosis revealed an arteriovenous malfo rmation (AVM). Such malformations carry a 
significant risk of rupture, leading to life-threat ing haemorrhage. Entire abnormal vessel cluster 
was closed in a single session without the need for  open brain surgery. The procedure lasted 
approximately six hours.
