    Motherson Sumi Wiring India Limited (MSWIL) posted Q1FY27 revenues of Rs. 3,407 crores, up 37% YoY, consistently outpacing industry growth in a challenging cost environment. Noida, Uttar Pradesh – 04th Aug 2026 – Motherson Sumi Wiring India Limited (MSWIL) announced its financial results for the fiscal year 2026-27, first quarter, which ended on 30th June 2026.  Mr Vivek Chaand Sehgal, Chairman, Motherson Sumi Wiring India Ltd., said,  "Our first quarter performance reflects the resilience of the company and the enduring strength of our customer relationships.  We continue to outperform industry growth despite ongoing cost headwinds, demonstrating our ability to execute consistently and create value alongside our customers. Our priority remains clear: supporting our customers, helping them succeed in a dynamic environment, and delivering on our commitments. None of this would be possible without our employees, who are our most valuable asset. Their passion, expertise, and unwavering dedication will continue to drive our success.”     Key Highlights  • MSWIL’s timely expansion into Greenfields projects and successful ramp-up of new customer programs contributed to substantial revenue growth and enhanced customer confidence. • Higher average copper prices compared with the prior year and significant minimum wage revisions in multiple states impacted profitability. • Constructive discussions continue with our customers to recover cost increases and maintain business momentum.  Financial Performance        Motherson Sumi Wiring India Limited (MSWIL)      Motherson Sumi Wiring India Limited (MSWIL) is a leading and fast-growing full-system solutions provider to OEMs in the wiring harness segment in India. Motherson Sumi Wiring India is a joint venture between Samvardhana Motherson International Limited (SAMIL) and Sumitomo Wiring Systems, Ltd. (SWS).  The company has the capabilities to meet customer needs at every stage of the supply chain, from initial product design to final production of integrated, cutting-edge Electrical & Electronic Distribution Systems for power supply or data transfer across vehicles, in support of current and future automotive trends.
