HCLTech introduces 20 Industry AI Solutions to transform vertical value chains 
with AI  
NOIDA, Oct ober  08, 2026 : HCLTech, a leading global technology company, has rolled out 20 
purpose -built Industry AI Solutions (IAIS), continuing its strategy to build differentiated IP in 
Advanced AI. Combining deep domain expertise with AI capabilities, the solutions address 
high -value, industry -specific solutions across banking, healthcare and life sciences, 
aerospace, retail, telecom, mobility, energy and manufacturing.  
Several of these solutions are already deployed at scale across client operations globally, while 
others are progressing through early -stage deployments.  
A dedicated global team of engineers and domain experts have built these solutions in -house.  
"AI has a potential to transform the very heart of business value chains but  realising that 
potential requires deep domain specificity and the ability to scale . Our Industry AI solutions 
bridge this gap by translating domain expertise into scalable AI intelligence, accelerating value 
stream innovation and helping enterprises navigate rapid technological change with greater 
agility, and measurable outcomes." Dr. Gaurav Dhakar, Global Head - Industry AI Solutions, 
HCLTech  
The portfolio is focused on translating AI into measurable business outcomes:  
• Manufacturing and logistics: HCLTech's VisionX , a computer -vision AI platform, has 
improved response times by more than 90% and reduced operational costs by 70% 
across factory and warehouse environments.  
• Manufacturing: SmartTwin enables manufacturers to simulate products and plants 
before physical deployment, helping deliver up to 44% faster time -to-market for new 
products.  
• Life sciences and healthcare: HCLTech's Intelligent Regulatory Platform delivers 
regulatory intelligence, while its Intelligent Safety Platform has reduced 
pharmacovigilance case -processing time by 40 -50%.  
• Procurement and finance operations: Autonomous Accounts Payable, an AI -led invoice 
management platform, is live at a major global motorbike and tools manufacturer, 
cutting processing cycle time by 60%.  
HCLTech plans to continue expanding its portfolio of repeatable Industry AI Solutions as 
enterprises move from AI experimentation towards scaled adoption and explore new solutions 
to unlock productivity, efficiency, innovation and growth.
