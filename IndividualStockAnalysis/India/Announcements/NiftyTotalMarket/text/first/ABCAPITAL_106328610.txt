Sub:  Intimation pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing 
Regulations”) - Update on tax litigation of subsidiary company  
 
This is with reference to the earlier intimation filed by the Company dated 14 August 2023, 
informing inter-alia about the Appeal filed by Aditya Birla Sun Life Insurance Company Limited 
(ABSLI), a material subsidiary of the Company, before the Commissioner of Income-Tax (Appeals) 
[CIT(A)] against the demand of Rs. 2,10,29,99,974  for the Assessment Year (AY) 2016-17 and 
filing of stay application before the Assessing Officer (AO) against the demand of AY 2016-17. 
Accordingly, the Deputy Commissioner of Income Tax (AO) has now issued an Order Giving Effect 
(OGE) dated 04 August 2025 (received by the Company on 05 August 2025) nullifying the entire 
demand of Rs. 2,10,29,99,974, basis the order received from the CIT(A) holding re-assessment 
proceedings invalid. 
 
 
Also, with reference to the earlier intimation filed by the Company for AY 2022-23 dated 04 
February 2025 pertaining to ABSLI, the office of Deputy Commissioner of Income Tax has issued 
a stay order dated 05 August 2025 (received by the Company on 05 August 2025) against the 
demand order pertaining to said AY amounting to Rs. 184,49,93,420 subject to adjustment of Rs. 
15,23,99,276 from the refund determined for AY 2024-25. 
 
We request you to take the aforesaid on records.
