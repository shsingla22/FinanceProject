Executive Summary  
• TBO’s robust performance in a challenging macro environment continues to demonstrate the platform’s 
inherent resilience. The organic business delivered 21% YoY growth in gross profit, translating into 36% YoY 
growth in Adj. EBITDA and underscoring the plat form’s structural operating leverage.  
• The India business continued on the strong trajectory , delivering healthy gro wth across both the Airl ines and 
Hotels + Ancillary businesses.  
• Monthly transacting buyers in the organic international business grew 17.4% YoY to a record 14,449 for the 
quarter. We view this growth as a leading indicator of the continued benefits of last year’s investment in KAM 
hiring.  
• On a constant currency basis, the organic business delivered YoY growth of 16% in GTV, 14% in gross profit 
and 27% in adjusted EBITDA.  
• During the quarter, we saw  a robust recovery in our Middle East Hotels + Ancillary busines s, with regional GTV 
growing 10% YoY on a constant currency basis in Q2 ’FY27. While the underlying market is unlikely to have fully 
recovered, we believe our performance suggests market share gains for TBO.  However , the subsequent 
escalation in Saudi  Arabia  meaningfully amplified the fluidity of the situation in the market.  
• Gross profit margin in the Hotels and Ancillary segment recovered to 5.5% of GTV for the quarter, up from 
5.2% in Q1 ’FY27.  
• Cash and Cash Equivalents (including bank balances, bank deposits and liquid investment s) were ₹ 2,060Cr as 
on 30th September  2026 . During the quarter, the company prepaid $10Mn of its term loan bring down the 
total outstanding term debt to $60Mn.  

Founders’ Message  
Dear Fellow Shareholders,  
This quarter was about  continuity , execution  and delivery . While the macro environment continued to be challenging 
in many ways, the business c ontinued to deliv er – Monthly transacting buyers reached an all-time  high  as we  continue 
to reap the benefits of last year ’s investments , GTV for the organic business was up 22% YoY,  Adj. EBITDA growth 
contin ued to outpace Gross pr ofit growth driven the platform ’s inherent operating le verage, the integration  efforts at 
Classic Vacations remains on track , we continue to build the platform capabilities in line with our thesis on luxury 
complex trips.  
Our investments in strengthening our commercial presence and expanding agent relationships continue to deliver 
results. Our expanded KAM base enables us to engage with more agents. As new agents become more familiar with 
our self-service  platform, they require less support, freeing up capacity for our commercial teams to develop new 
relationships. This ability to support a growing business without a proportionate increase in costs is fundamental to 
TBO’s platform economics  and the inherent operating leverage .  
Our conviction in the opportunity ahead remains strong. The breadth of our global network gives us access to diverse 
travel markets, while our relationships with suppliers provide a foundation for deeper participation in those markets. 
Our strategic priorities centre on expanding this distribution, strengthening differentiated supply and consistently 
improving the value we deliver to our partners . 
Luxury and complex travel are particularly compelling opportunities within this strategy. Personal advice, trusted 
relationships and reliable service play an important role in these journeys. By combining our technology and global 
reach with richer content , curated supply and broader trip capabilities, we aim to help advisors serve more of their 
customers’ travel needs through TBO.  
Classic Vacations, which has now completed its first year with TBO, adds meaningful depth to this proposition. Its 
established advisor relationships, luxury expertise and supplier partnerships complement our wider platform. Our 
focus remains on integrating  these strengths thoughtfully, supporting its commercial capabilities and preserving the 
service standards associated with the Classic Vacations brand.  During the quarter, we meaningfully expanded the sales 
team at Class ic to allow it to  maintain a high touch relationship  with key cust omers while actively expanding the 
customer base. These costs will be partially offset by cost optimization  initia tives . The impact of such init iatives are 
already showing early green shoots in the form of a small yet directionally pertinent  margin growth in the Class ic 
vacations P&L on a trailing 12 month  basis ( 2.5%  Adj. EBITDA conversion from GTV vs 2.4% estimated at the time of 
the acquisition) .  
Technology remains central to how we strengthen our proposition and improve productivity. Our focus on AI, simpler 
workflows and greater operating efficiency supports both better service and a lower cost to serve. Alongside 
disciplined investment in distri bution and supply, these capabilities are important to building a more competitive and 
scalable business.  
We remain focused on creating enduring shareholder value through profitable growth, thoughtful capital allocation 
while continuously  striving to perfect  the underlying consistent  execution capabilit ies.    
Gaurav Bhatnagar  and Ankush Nijhawan
