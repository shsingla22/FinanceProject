Gross Written Premium Income: - 
 In Q3 2025, Gross Written Premium of the company stood at ₹ 2,677 cr compared to ₹ 2,428 cr in Q3 2024, 
achieving a growth of 10.2%.  
 Effective from October 1, 2024, other applicable long-term premium is accounted on 1/n basis, without 1/n 
basis, Gross Written Premium for Q3 2025 is 2,738 cr, achieving a growth of 12.8%.

 Premium Retention Ratio for Q3 2025 is 83.8%, as compared to 87.9% in Q3 2024.

 Without 1/n basis, there is no impact on profit after tax for Q3 2025. 
Assets Under Management: -

About the Company 
 
We aim to make insurance simple. Through innovation and transparency, we believe in delivering a seamless customer 
experience journey in a significant financial product an individual would purchase in their lifetime. We are one of the 
leading digital full stack insurance companies, leveraging our technology to power what we believe to be an innovative 
approach to product design, distribution and customer experience for non-life insurance products. We offer motor 
insurance, health insurance, travel insurance, property insurance, marine insurance, liability insurance and other 
insurance products, which the customers can customize to meet their needs.  
For more details visit https://www.godigit.com/ .
