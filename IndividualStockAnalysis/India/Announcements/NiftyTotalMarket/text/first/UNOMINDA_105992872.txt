NOTICE is hereby given that the Extra Ordinary General Meeting of the
Shareholders of TAKE Solutions Limited will be held on Wednesday, October 9,
2024 at 11.00 a.m. IST. through Video Conferencing (“VC”) /Other Audio-Visual
Means (“OAVM”). The venue of the meeting shall be deemed to be the Registered
Oﬃce of the Company at Oﬃce No. 56, Old No. 116, 4th Floor, Ragas Building,Dr. Radhakrishnan Salai, Mylapore, Chennai – 600 004.
