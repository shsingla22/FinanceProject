ANNEXURE 2(B) 
 
BRIEF PROFILE - RAJESH UMAKANT SHANOY  
 
 
Mr. Rajesh Umakant Shanoy is a qualified Company Secretary, Cost Accountant and 
CIMA (Strategic level) (UK), with overall more than 2 4 years of rich experience in 
corporate secretarial,  legal, financial, and cost and management 
accounting  functions  across sectors, including manufacturing, chemicals, media 
and financial services.   
 
 
Prior to joining Biocon, he was working with Bajaj group since Dec 2011, including  
overseeing compliance of the Companies Act, SEBI and allied laws for Bajaj Finance 
Limited and its two subsidiaries. From April 2017, he headed the Corporate Secretarial function of Bajaj 
Allianz Life Insurance Company Limited. Prior to this, he has worked with leading organizations, including 
Akzo Nobel Chemicals India Limited, Deepak F ertilisers and Indiacom Limited.
