HERO MOTOCORP EXTEND S TITLE SPONSORSHIP OF THE HERO WORLD CHALL ENGE  THROUGH 2030  
 
Tiger Woods to continue as Global Corporate Partner of Hero MotoCorp 
  
ALBANY, BAHAMAS  – TGR Live announced Hero MotoCorp, the world’s largest manufacturer of 
two-wheelers, has extended its title sponsorship of the Hero World Challenge. With Hero MotoCorp 
as the title sponsor, the PGA TOUR -sanctioned tournament will remain at Albany, Bahamas t hough 
the 2030 playing.  
 
“I’d like to thank Dr. Pawan Munjal and Hero MotoCorp for their continued support of the Hero 
World Challenge,” tournament host Tiger Woods said. “Hero MotoCorp has played a vital role in 
the tournament’s evolution , growth and suc cess;  and their commitment is felt by  everyone who 
joins us at  Albany.”  
 
Speaking about the extension of the title sponsorship, Dr. Pawan Munjal, Executive Chairman, Hero 
MotoCorp, said, "We are proud to extend this partnership, which exemplifies our dedication to 
creating meaningful change in communities, much like the transformative innovations we pioneer 
in the automotive industry. Together with Tiger, we are committed to elevating the Hero World 
Challenge as a powerful platform for positive social impact and global inspiration.”  
 
Additionally, Hero MotoCorp announced the renewal of their  association with Woods as a Global 
Corporate Partner.  
 
“Through the years I have come to respect Pawan as a visionary leader and more importantly a 
trusted friend,”  Woods said. “His progressive leadership has made a significant impact not only on 
the future of global mobility but also the game of golf.”  
 
"Our partnership with Tiger Woods is a profound source of pride. Over the past decade, I have 
witnessed firsthand his extraordinary impact  - not only as one of the greatest athletes in history but 
also as a global ambassador of excellence and a beacon of inspiration for millions.  This enduring 
collaboration is more than a testament to shared values; it’s a commitment to redefining 
boundaries, driving innovation, and shaping a better world. Together, we remain resolute in our 
mission to create a meaningful and lasting legacy that transcends sports and inspires generations 
to come. ” Dr. Munjal noted.  
 
Hero MotoCorp first  title sponsored the Hero World Challenge in 2014 at Isleworth Golf & Country 
Club. In 2015, the tournament moved to Albany, Bahamas where it will be played for the ninth 
time in 2024. Under Hero’s title sponsorship, the tournament has grown from 18 player s to 20.  
 
The Hero World Challenge is the longest -running PGA TOUR event managed by TGR Live . The 
event  was started by Woods and his father Earl in January 2000 to raise funds for TGR Foundation. 
Established in 1996 by Woods, TGR Foundation’s mission is to empower students to pursue their 
passions through education.  
 
- more - 

As the founder and host of the Hero World Challenge , Woods’ vision has always been to utilize the 
event to raise  awareness , support  and resources that make s a positive impact in the lives of youth  
around the world . Through TGR Foundation’s signature programs and its TGR Learning Labs , 
students from under -resourced communities  receive learning experiences in a safe and inspiring 
environment . These opportunities provid e students with knowledge, tools and confidence needed 
to prepare for  their future s and unleash their potential . 
 
Proceeds from the 2024 Hero World Challenge benefit TGR Foundation , Tavistock Foundation and 
Bahamas Youth Foundation . 
 
Tournament rounds will be held December 5-8 at Albany, bringing the event back to The Bahamas 
for its ninth  year. Live television coverage of the 202 4 Hero World Challenge will be provided by 
GOLF Channel during all four rounds and by NBC duri ng the third and final rounds.
