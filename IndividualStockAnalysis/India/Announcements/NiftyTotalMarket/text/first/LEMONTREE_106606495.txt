LEMON TREE HOTELS OPEN S ITS THIRD HOTEL  IN NEPAL  WITH  
LEMON TREE PREMIER, BIRATNAGAR  
 
The newly launched hotel features 80 well-appointed rooms and suites, complemented by 
vibrant dining and expansive event facilities  
 
 
New Delhi,  30th April  2026: Lemon Tree Hotels Limited today announced the opening of 
Lemon Tree Premier, Biratnagar, marking a key expansion of its upscale footprint in Nepal. 
Historically known as the country’s industrial powerhouse, Biratnagar is currently 
undergoing a modern trans formation, and Lemon Tree Premier arrives as the city’s first 
major branded hotel designed to meet this new momentum.  
 
This opening represents a major milestone in the company’s international expansion 
strategy, reinforcing its commitment to growing its footprint in high -potential neighbouring 
markets. With Nepal emerging as a key tourism and business destination, this lau nch 
strengthens Lemon Tree Hotels’ presence in South Asia.  
 
Located in Biratnagar, the bustling capital of Nepal’s Koshi Province, the hotel is 
strategically positioned in one of the country’s most important industrial and commercial 
hubs. Often regarded as the gateway to Eastern Nepal, the city offers access to pr ominent 
tourist and pilgrimage destinations such as Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve and Baraha 
Kshetra. The hotel is conveniently located just 3.5 km from Biratnagar Airport, ensuring 
seamless connectivity for both domestic and international travellers.  
 
The hotel brings the brand’s signature upscale standards to the region for the first time 
with 80 well -appointed rooms and suites built for the modern executive. It also introduces 
the city to acclaimed dining destinations including Republic of Noodles (br and’s signature 
Pan-Asian restaurant) along with Citrus Café (all -day dining), Slounge (a contemporary 
recreation bar) and a Tea Lounge, creating a new social center for the local community 
and visitors alike.  
 
For business and social engagements, the property houses expansive banquet and meeting 
facilities, including the Tangerine ballroom with pre -function area and a well -equipped 
boardroom, making it an ideal destination for conferences, events, and celebratio ns. 
Recreational facilities include a rejuvenating spa – Fresco, a well -equipped fitness centre, 
and a swimming pool, offering guests a holistic and relaxing stay experience.  
 
Speaking on th is opening , Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, Lemon Tree 
Hotels Ltd. , said, "Biratnagar has always been the heart of Nepal’s commerce, and its 
transformation into a modern regional gateway is a journey we are proud to be part of. As 
the city’s infrastructure and connectivity modernize , there is a clear need for hospitality 
that matches this ambition. By bringing Lemon Tree Premier to Biratnagar, we aren't just 
opening a hotel; we are making a long -term commitment to the city’s growth. Our goal is 
to provide a trusted, high -quality home  base that offers unparalleled services  to both  global 
travel lers and the local business community in this vital hub."

ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India’s leading hospitality companies, catering 
to a wide range of customers – from value -conscious travellers to premium business and 
leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels & Resorts, Lemon T ree Premier, 
Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys Select, and Keys Lite – the group offers 
experiences across upper upscale, upscale, upper midscale, midscale, leisure, wildlife, and 
spiritual segments.  
 
LTHL operates 1 30+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing pipeline 
of 130+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and 
Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, and Indore – and with an 
international presence in Dubai, Bhutan, and Nepal – Lemon Tree Hotels delivers 
except ional comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, refreshing experience.  
 
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 250+ properties 
(operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for both business and 
leisure travellers.
