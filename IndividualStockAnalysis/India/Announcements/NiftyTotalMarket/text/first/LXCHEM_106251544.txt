In continuation of our earlier disclosure dated October 24, 2025, we wish to inform you of further 
developments in the ongoing litigation. 
 
The Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Company Limited ( MSEDCL ) has filed a Special Leave 
Petition ( SLP) before the Hon’ble Supreme Court challenging the Final Order dated October 24, 2024, 
passed by the Appellate Tribunal for Electricity ( APTEL) in Appeal No. 245 of 2018 and Appeal No. 376 of 
2018. 
 
In the interim, despite the APTEL judgment dated October 23, 2024, MSEDCL has continued to levy 
Wheeling Charges, Wheeling Losses, and Transmission Charges and Losses. As a result, the Company 
was constrained to file a Contempt Petition before APTEL, seeking appropriate directions against MSEDCL. 
However, the Contempt Petition was dismissed by APTEL on the ground that it does not possess civil 
contempt jurisdiction under the Contempt of Courts Act. In the meantime, after the judgment in Contempt 
Petition was reserved by APTEL, the Company filed Writ Petition (L) No. 14504 of 2025 before the Hon’ble 
Bombay High Court, challenging the continued levy of charges by MSEDCL as illegal.  
 
The detailed disclosure as required under the SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 
dated July 13, 2023, is enclosed herewith as ‘Annexure – A’ .  
 
We shall continue to monitor the matter closely and will keep you informed of any significant 
developments in due course.
