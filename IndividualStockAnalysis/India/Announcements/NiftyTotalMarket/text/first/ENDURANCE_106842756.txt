Performance Highlights [Half Year ended September 2026]: 
• Consolidated Total Income including Other Income increased by 31.6% on a YOY basis. 
Standalone Total Income representing the India business grew 37.9%. 
• 76% of Consolidated Total Income including Other Income came from Indian operations 
(including Maxwell) and the balance came from European operations. 
• Consolidated EBITDA margin stood at 12.6%, compared to 14.0% in the corresponding 
period last year. 
• Consolidated PAT at INR 519 crore was 14.4% higher than last year. 
• Aftermarket sales from Indian operations stood at INR 303 crore vs. INR 243 crore in the 
corresponding period of last year. 
• Consolidated Basic and Diluted EPS for the period stood at INR 36.88 per share (not annualised) 
compared to INR 32.25 per share (not annualised) in the corresponding period of last year.

Commenting on the Company's performance and recent developments, Mr. Anurang Jain, Managing 
Director of the Company said: 
"During Q2 FY27, the Indian automotive market recorded healthy growth, with two-wheeler sales 
volumes rising 18% year-on-year, three-wheelers 27% and passenger vehicles 24%. Against this 
backdrop, Endurance recorded 40% growth in standalone topline and 18% growth in absolute EBIDTA. 
With expected softening of aluminium prices, improved pass-through of higher costs, ramping up of 
new plants, and fruits of our efforts on cost management and operational efficiency, we expect an 
increasing trend in profitability. 
In India, commissioning of the AURIC Shendra plant marks progress in converting orders into advanced 
manufacturing operations deploying high-end technology. It will produce aluminium die-casting 
components for Indian and global four-wheeler OEMs across EV and ICE platforms, as well as non­
automotive applications. We also commenced supplies of dual-channel ABS during the quarter, 
expanding our braking systems portfolio. Our battery pack plant, which commenced two-wheeler 
battery pack production in Ql FY27, is fast ramping up. 
In Europe, new car registrations in Q2 grew 5.6% year-on-year, with major share of the growth coming 
from Chinese OEMs. Our revenue in Euro terms also grew 5.6%. In the next quarter, our results in 
Europe will be further strengthened by Fondalpress, which we acquired in October 2026. Fondalpress 
strengthens our European aluminium casting and machining capabilities and offers a track record of 
generating profitable revenue, best-in-class quality and factory management processes with significant 
scope for further expansion. We are strengthening our European operations through organic growth 
and selective acquisitions. During the quarter, we accelerated the acquisition of Stoferle's remaining 
32% stake, taking our ownership to 100%, and acquired Fondalpress in Italy, enhancing our aluminium 
casting and machining capabilities. These developments strengthen our European manufacturing base 
and create opportunities for operational integration." 
About Endurance Technologies Limited: 
Endurance is one of the leading automotive component manufacturers, offering a diverse range of 
technology-driven products with operations in India and Europe (Italy and Germany). In India, the 
Company predominantly caters to two and three-wheeler OEMs, with products including aluminium 
castings, suspensions, transmissions, braking, battery packs and battery management systems. In 
Europe, it supplies aluminium castings to four-wheeler OEMs and also serves the aftermarket for two 
wheeler components. 
As a complete solutions provider, Endurance offers end-to-end services by partnering with its 
customers from concept through to end-user delivery and also caters to the replacement market. Of 
the Company's 37 plants, 22 are in India, five in Germany, and ten in Italy. Endurance is also 
equipped with an in-house tool room, a 29-acre proving ground, five DSIR-approved R&D facilities in 
India, and two technical centres in Italy.
