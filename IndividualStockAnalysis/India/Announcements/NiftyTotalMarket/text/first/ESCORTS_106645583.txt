Escorts Kubota sharpens horticulture play with  
Kubota NeoStar compact and narrow tractor series  
 
• The Kubota NeosStar series now has a new 27 HP category narrow  variant purpose -built 
for sugarcane  and inter -row applications.  
• The NeoStar series is introduced in the 21 -30 HP segment, catering to the 21 -27 HP 
range with compact four -wheel drive tractors. The series is equipped with Power 
Steering and ECO PTO across all models for operator convenience and fuel -efficient 
operations . 
• Builds on Kubota’s legacy and the trust of 8 0,000+  compact and narrow tractor 
customers in India  
 
Faridabad, 28 May 2026: Escorts Kubota Limited, the major agri- machinery and construction 
equipment, today announced the introduction  of Kubota Neostar , a significant upgrade to  its 
compact tractor range  in the 21 -30 HP segment . Catering to the 21 –27 HP range , this series  
is engineered for higher productivity, improved operator comfort and smarter upgrade value, 
and is being introduced with a focus on high- potential sugarcane , vineyard  and horticulture 
clusters across states of Maharashtra, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh and Gujarat . The rollout  
builds on Kubota’s strong legacy and the trust of more than 80,000 compact and narrow 
tractor customers in India.  
Escorts Kubota operates three tractor brands in India -  Farmtrac, Powertrac & Kubota -  
catering to entry -premium, value and premium segments,  respectively. The NeoStar series 
sits within the premium Kubota brand and reinforces the company’s focus on application- led 
products for the 21 –27 HP category  range , one of the fastest- growing parts of the Indian 
tractor market, having grown around 35% over the last three years on rising horticulture mechanization.  
The range is offered in two configurations. NeoStar narrow variant with a true width of 
around 2.98 ft, among the few such offerings in the segment, is designed for  sugarcane &  
narrow inter -row operations, while NeoStar is positioned as a smart, all- round compact 
tractor for vineyard,  orchards & multi -crop usage in confined- space applications.  
The New addition in the NeoStar series is inclusion of  new narrow variants, among them a 27 
HP category  purpose -built for narrow sugarcane fields  and interculture operations. The  power 
steering across all models provide  superior operator comfort  while Eco PTO improves fuel 
efficiency during operations , while the  compact design and enhanced ergonomics make the
