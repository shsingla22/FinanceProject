Kalyan Jewellers India Limited recorded  revenue in excess of 
Rs 20,900 Crore and PAT of Rs 776 crore  for the first half of 
FY27 
Thrissur, 10 Nov 2026: The company  recorded consolidated revenue of Rs 20,912 
crore for the first half of FY27 as against Rs 15,125  crore in the previous financial 
year, a growth of 38%. Consolidated PAT for the first half of FY27 was Rs 776 crore 
as against a PAT of Rs 525 crore  for the same period  in the previous financial year , 
a growth of 48%.   
Consolidated revenue and PAT for Q 2 FY27 was Rs 10,323 Cr and Rs 427 Cr 
respectively , a growth of 31% and 64% respectively.  
 Standalone revenue  (India)  and PAT for Q 2 FY27 was Rs 9,007 Cr and Rs 424 Cr 
respectively , a growth of 32% and 62% respectively . 
Mr Ramesh Kalyanaraman, Executive Director, Kalyan Jewellers India Limited 
said, “The ongoing festive quarter has started off very well and we are very 
encouraged by the growth on t he ground , especially  during Navratri and 
Dhanteras . We are upbeat about the wedding season and look forward to end ing 
the quarter on a strong note .”    
About Kalyan Jewellers  
Headquartered in Thrissur in the state of Kerala, Kalyan Jewellers  is one the 
largest jewellery retailers in India with a presence in the Middle East , US and the 
UK. The company has enjoyed a longstanding presence in the Indian market for 
over three decades and has set industry benchmarks in quality, transparency, 
pricin g and innovation. Kalyan offers an array of traditional and contemporary 
jewellery designs in gold, diamonds and precious stones catering to the distinct 
needs of customers. Kalyan Jewellers has  546 showrooms across India , USA , UK 
and the Middle East , with  a retail area exceeding 12,99,000  sq. ft .
