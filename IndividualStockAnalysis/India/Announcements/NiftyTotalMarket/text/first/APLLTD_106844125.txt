Alembic Pharmaceuticals strengthens strategic collaboration with 
DifGen to expand its complex generics portfolio in the US  
 
Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited ("Alembic") today announced the expansion of its 
strategic collaboration with DifGen Pharmaceuticals ("DifGen") . 
 Building on their existing relationship, including the joint venture in Canada, the expanded collaboration combines DifGen’s development and US manufacturing capabilities with 
Alembic’s established US commercial platform. DifGen will lead development, regulatory, 
manufacturing and supply activities, while Alembic will manufacture select products and commercialise the portfolio in the US.  
 
Key highlights of the collaboration:  
 
• Broad, differentiated complex generics portfolio:  portfolio of 20+ differentiated 
products across injectable, transdermal, ophthalmic, oral and other complex dosage forms addressing an aggregate reference market of ~US$ 5 billion.  
• Higher value opportunities and faster market access: portfolio includes  open FTF 
opportunities, potential Day 1 launches and products with limited expected competition.  
• Near term revenue visibility with potential to scale:  initial launches are expected 
from FY28, providing near -term revenue visibility and a platform for progressively 
increasing contribution to the US business as the broader portfolio is commercialised  
over the next few  years.  
• US manufacturing optionality: reserves capacity for Alembic at DifGen’s US facilities, 
with a framework to progressively build US manufacturing capacity for select dosage forms . 
 
Commenting on the collaboration,  Mr. Pranav Amin, Managing Director, Alembic 
Pharmaceuticals Limited, said,  
“This collaboration further strengthens our complex generics franchise in the US. The portfolio expands Alembic’s exposure to differentiated opportunities , particularly in 
transdermal systems, complex injectables and other specialised dosage forms. Importantly, the collaboration provides  near-term commercial opportunities and access to 
US manufacturing capabilities. We believe this is a capital efficient way to deepen our pipeline,  strengthen the quality, and sustainability of Alembic’s US business growth and 
create long- term value for our stakeholders.”
