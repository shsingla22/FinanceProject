A.Back ground of Parties
Uls
an Aluminum Limited ["UAL"] is a leading supplier of aluminium  flat rolled products, with an
annual capacity of 325 kts, to a variety of market segments comprising automotive, beverage cans and a number of specialty alumin ium applications including industrial and light gauge. With its
predecessor entities, Novelis Korea Limited 
["NKL"] and Aluminium  of Korea Limited, UAL has a
history of over 50 years of operation and employs 600 employees. In September 2017,   NKL
entered into a 50:50 joint venture with Kobe Steel Limited [“Kobe Steel”]  for the joint ownership
and operation of NKL's manufacturing plant in Ulsan, Korea.
NKL sells various raw materials, including sheet ingot, prime ingot and hardeners, to UAL and UAL
sells alumin ium flat rolled products for automotive, beverage cans and specialty alumini um
applications to NKL on an arm’s length basis. Prior to formation of UAL, the facility was amanufacturing plant of NKL and had manufactured alumin ium flat rolled products for NKL’s
customers in the beverage can and specialty alumin ium application markets throughout the
region and for Novelis automotive plant in Changzhou, China. UAL possesses managerial,Special Business
