Press Release  1 
 
 
Hyderabad/Mumbai, 6 November 2026  
Cohance Lifesciences Limited (formerly Suven Pharmaceuticals Limited), a leading global 
contract development and manufacturing organisation (CDMO), today announced its unaudited ﬁnancial results for the second quarter and half year ended 30 September 2026 . 
Q2FY27 Financial Performance  
• Q2 FY27 revenue from operations was ₹4,767 million, increasing 12.9% sequentially and declining 14.2% year -on-year. Gross margin was 70.8%, compared with 74.6% in 
Q2 FY26, reﬂecting an unfavourable business and product mix.  Adjusted EBITDA was 
₹458 million, with an adjusted EBITDA margin of 10%, compared with 2.2% in Q1 FY27.
 
H1FY27 Financial Performance  
• H1 FY27 revenue from operations was ₹8,989 million, down 18.6% year -on-year. 
Gross margin was 71.1%, compared with 73.8% in H1 FY26.  Adjusted EBITDA was 
₹551 million, with an adjusted EBITDA margin of 6.1%.  
 
Cohance Lifesciences Announces Q2 & H1FY27 Results  
Q2 delivered sequential growth ; H2 to drive YoY growth in revenue and EBITDA  
Mr. Umang Vohra, Executive Chairman and Group CEO, said:  “Q2 reﬂects an  improving 
business trajectory and the broader Cohance platform beginning to come together. We 
expect H2 to be materially stronger than H1, with year -on-year growth in both revenue and 
EBITDA, supported by improving visibility across our businesses . At the same time, we 
remain focused on progressing our regulatory commitments and maintaining the highest 
standards of quality and compliance across our facilities. We see signiﬁcant value 
potential as we continue to integrate and scale the Cohance plat form, and we are still at 
the beginning of that journey”

Key Business Highlights of H1FY27  
• Small Molecules: Stronger Q3/Q4 order visibility and multiple commercial reload 
orders received, supporting improved delivery schedules and H2 recovery  
• Secured three signiﬁcant programmes with large global pharma customers, comprising two commercial and one Phase III programme  
• Two molecules moved into commercial supply, with two additional molecules 
expected over the next 12 –15 months, including one with US FDA approval  
• NJ Bio successfully delivered two ADC GMP campaigns, including an end -to-end 
development programme. One customised payload programme is progressing through scale -up 
• Nucleic Acid business is seeing strong demand for specialised nucleosides and  
amidites, with increasing commercial opportunities and larger -scale  
manufacturing programmes across the Cohance network  
• Qualiﬁcation programmes progressing with two Japanese customers, alongside 
opportunities with a European innovator and potential manufacturing transfers  
• NJ Bio, Inc. was named Runner -Up in the Best Contract Research Organization 
(CRO) category at the 2026 World ADC Awards, marking seven consecutive years 
of recognition at the event  
 
Outlook  
H2 FY27 is expected to be materially stronger than H1 FY27, supported by Small Molecule 
deliveries and reloads, continued resilience in API+, nucleic -acid growth and progressive 
improvement across the portfolio. Higher revenue conversion, improved utilisat ion and a 
better business mix are expected to support progressive margin recovery. The strategic direction remains unchanged.
