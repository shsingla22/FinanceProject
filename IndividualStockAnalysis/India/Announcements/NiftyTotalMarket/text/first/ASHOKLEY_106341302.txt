Dear Sir / Madam,  
 
Subject:  Termination of Ashok Leyland Limited - Employee Stock Option Plan 2018  
 
We wish to inform that t he Shareholders of the Company had on November 13, 2018 through Postal Ballot 
approved the Ashok Leyland Limited - Employee Stock Option Plan 2018  (ESOP Plan  2018 ). Subsequently, 
BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited had provided in -principle approval for the same 
on Septemb er 11, 2019 and September 16, 2019 respectively.  Under the ESOP Plan 2018 the Company 
had granted 201,10,000 options to eligible employees. None of these options have vested till date due to 
the vesting criteria  not being fulfilled and hence there has been  no exercise of options till date under this 
plan .  
 
Based on the recommendation of Nomination and Remuneration Committee , the Board of Directors  at 
their meeting held today i.e., August 14, 2025 ha ve accorded approval for termination of the ESOP Plan 
2018  as per the clauses in the said plan.  
 
As a result  of the above , the ESOP Scheme 2018 has been terminated with immediate effect .
