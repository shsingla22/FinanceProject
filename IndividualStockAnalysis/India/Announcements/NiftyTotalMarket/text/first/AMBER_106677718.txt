Sub : Declassification of Ascent Circuits Private Limited and Sidwal Refrigeration Industries Private Limited, 
as Material Subsidiaries of Amber Enterprises India Limited 
 
Ref: Disclosure pursuant to Regulation 30 of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended ("SEBI (LODR) Regulations"), read with the Company's Policy 
for Determination of Material Subsidiary and Governance of Subsidiary ("Policy"), we hereby inform the 
stock exchanges of the following: 
 
Ascent Circuits Private Limited ("Ascent"), a subsidiary of IL JIN Electronics (India) Private Limited ("IL JIN"), 
which is itself a subsidiary of Amber Enterprises India Limited (the "Company" or "Amber"), and accordingly 
a step-down subsidiary of the Company; and Sidwal Refrigeration Industries Private Limited ("Sidwal"), a 
wholly-owned subsidiary of Amber — have ceased to be classified as Material Subsidiaries of the Company, 
with effect from financial year 2026–27. 
 
This determination is based on the audited financial statements of the Company, Ascent and Sidwal for the 
financial year 2025–26. Based on such audited financials, the income and net worth of both Ascent and Sidwal 
did not exceed the threshold prescribed under Regulation 16(1)(c) of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, which 
defines a subsidiary as "material" if its income or net worth exceeds 10% of the consolidated income or net 
worth, respectively, of the listed entity and its subsidiaries in the immediately preceding accounting year.
