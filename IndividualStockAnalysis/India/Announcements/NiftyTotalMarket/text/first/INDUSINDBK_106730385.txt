EazyDiner and IndusInd Bank Launch a Lifetime -Free Credit Card That 
Saves You ₹21,495 a Year to Eat Out  
Extending exceptional savings on eating out along with the convenience of everyday UPI payments, 
the lifetime -free EazyDiner IndusInd Bank Platinum Rupay Credit Card delivers savings of ₹21,495 
every year at zero fees: extra ₹500 off on dining - 3 times a m onth, a free monthly BookMyShow 
ticket, a complimentary 3 months EazyDiner Prime membership and payments on UPI QR code.  
Mumbai, August 07, 2026:  From conversation over a hearty Sunday brunch with old school friends to the 
nervous clatter of soup spoons on a first date, at EazyDiner the goal is for every eating out moment to be 
just as rewarding when the bill arrives. To revolutionise both worlds E azyDiner and IndusInd Bank today 
announced the launch of the EazyDiner IndusInd Bank Platinum Credit Card on the RuPay network. With 
RuPay's UPI capability, cardholders can now link the card to their preferred UPI app and make payme nts 
at any QR code while collecting points for transactions that have become muscle memory.  
Moving away from traditional marketing phrases such as “unlocking a world of privilege” or “deserving 
the extraordinary” this lifetime -free card brings you ₹21,495 every year, including ₹18,000 in annual extra 
dining savings through payments on the EazyDin er app, a complimentary BookMyShow ‘Buy One Get 
One’ movie ticket every month worth ₹2,400 annually, and a three -month complimentary EazyDiner Prime 
membership worth ₹1,095.  
EazyDiner Prime members enjoy guaranteed discounts ranging from 25% to 50% across over 40,000 
premium restaurants in India and Dubai. EazyDiner is India's leading dining platform, helping users 
discover restaurants, reserve tables and seamlessly enjoy excl usive savings across more than 40,000 
restaurants in India. Every reservation comes with an exclusive offer and users collect EazyPoints with 
every transaction that can be redeemed to achieve free dining experiences, at EazyEvents and 
complimentary luxury hotel stays for free. Users can further unlock extra savings while enjoying a fast, 
convenient and rewarding payment experience that brings together restaurant discovery, reservations, 
payments and loyalty on a single platform.  
Commenting on the launch, Mr. Kapil Chopra, Founder, EazyDiner,  said: " We spend a lot of time trying 
to figure out what makes people eat out more often and how to celebrate it. At its heart, dining out is more 
than just breaking bread, it's a bid for connection. Gathering across a table is how we celebrate, reconnect 
or explo re and add novelty into our mundane routines. At EazyDiner, we've always tried to make those 
moments happen more often, whether through meaningful savings or complimentary dessert that comes 
with a side of surprise. We wanted to build a card that rewards and multiplies those moments while fitting 
naturally into the way India pays today. With RuPay, customers can now use the card through any UPI app 
and unlock ₹21,495 in savings every -year value through dining, entertainment and exclusive membership 
benefits. It's a gift that keeps on giving while you dine and even long after you leave the restaurant .” 
Speaking on the launch, Mr. Jagdeep Mallareddy, Executive Director, IndusInd Bank , said: " At 
IndusInd Bank, we are committed to delivering innovative, customer -centric solutions that enhance our 
value proposition and seamlessly integrate into our customers' everyday lives. Our partnership with 
EazyDiner delivers an elevated customer experience by combining the convenience of RuPay Credit Card 
on UPI with exclusive dining privileges ."
