Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
we wish to inform you that, Clean Fino -Chem Limited (“CFCL”), Wholly Owned Subsidiary of the 
Company has commenced commercial production of Hydroquinone ( HQ) and Catechol in a dedicated 
production block today, Thursday 18th December , 2025 . 
 
The Company is pleased to achieve following notable distinctions by commercializing Hydroquinone  
and Catechol : 
• The Company would be key  domestic manufacturer of Hydroquinone and Catechol , leading 
to import substitution led demand traction . 
• With commercialization of Hydroquinone , value proposition of performance  segment 
strengthens further by creating cross -selling opportunity with MEHQ customers . Also, it will 
further enhance TBHQ market share.  
• Catechol will also be captively consumed for production of Guaiacol  and Veratrole . 
Commercialization of Hydroquinone  and Catechol , functionally critical specialty chemical, underscores 
robust in-house R&D capabilities supported by synergistic in -house project engineering capabilities.
