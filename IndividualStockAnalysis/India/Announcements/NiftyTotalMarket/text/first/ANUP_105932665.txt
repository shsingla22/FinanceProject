Ahmedabad, July 31, 2024:  The Anup Engineering Limited (ANUP), announced its unaudited financial 
results for the  quarter ended  on June  30, 2024. 
  
• Revenue from operation of Q1 FY25 at Rs 14 6.0 Crores  with EBIDTA of Rs 33.0 Crores  
(22.6%) and PAT of Rs  24.0 Crores  (16.5%) 
• Order book stood at Rs 8 10 Crores as on June  30, 2024.
