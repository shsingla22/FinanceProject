Tata Power Renewables Commissions 300 MW Project for SECI in Karnataka  
 
 
 
  
 
  
 
 
Tata Power Renewables commissions the 300 MW Koppal Solar Project,  
advancing India's clean energy ambitions  
 
• Project has deployed Solar Trackers to enable the solar modules to track the movement 
of the sun and optimise energy generation.  
• Project to deliver 810 MUs of renewable power annually, supporting India's clean energy transition  
• Expected to mitigate 103,820 tonnes of CO ₂ emissions each year through advanced 
solar tracking technology  
 
National  November  5, 2026: Tata Power Renewable Energy Limited (TPREL), one of India’s 
leading renewable energy companies and a subsidiary of The Tata Power Company Limited, 
has commissioned its 300 MW solar project for Solar Energy Corporation of India (SECI ) in 
Koppal, Karnataka, marking another significant milestone in its journey of developing and executing large- scale renewable energy projects across India. TP Saurya, a wholly owned 
subsi diary of TPREL, is the developing company of this project.  
Upon commissioning, the project is expected to generate 810 MUs of renewable energy annually and help mitigate an estimated 103,820 tonnes of carbon emissions each year, reinforcing the transition towards a cleaner energy future.   
The project will supply renewable power to the SECI. A key technology feature of the project is the deployment of Solar Trackers, enabling the solar modules to track the movement of the sun and optimise energy generation. The project deploys TP580HG10NB bifacial modules. The combination of advanced tracking technology and high- efficiency bifacial modules supports 
efficient utilisation of the project site and enhances renewable power generation.   
The Koppal project also refl ects TPREL’s focus on building a diverse and inclusive workforce 
across its renewable energy operations. Around 16 women professionals, accounting for nearly 40% of the workforce at the project site, have contributed in key roles including Site Managers and Site Engineers, supporting technical execution, engineering and team management. Their 

 
participation highlights the growing role of women in technical and leadership positions within 
India’s renewable energy sector.   
The commissioning of the Koppal project further strengthens TPREL’s portfolio of utility -scale 
renewable energy projects and reinforces its commitment to supporting India’s transition towards a low -carbon energy future through technology -led solutions, strong execution 
capabilities and respon sible project development.   
With the addition of this project, TPREL's total renewable utility capacity has reached 12 GW.   
Out of this around 7.2 GW is operational, including 5.8 GW of solar and 1.3 GW of wind capacity, while close to 4.9 GW is under vari ous stages of implementation.  
 
About Tata Power:  
The Tata Power Company Limited, a vertically integrated power company and part of the Tata Group , India’s largest 
multinational business conglomerate, has built a diversified portfolio across the entire power value chain, with its total 
operational and under -construction  capacity surpassing 26 GW. This includes approximately 17. 7 GW of clean and green 
energy capacity (including projects under constru ction) and around 8.8  GW of thermal generation capacity, ensuring a 
balanced and resilient energy mix. The Company also has a strong transmission and distribution footprint, with over 7,800 
circuit kilometres of transmission lines, including pipeline projects, and serves  over 13 million customers across its distribution 
businesses, making it one of India’s largest private power distribution companies.  Further strengthening its leadership in 
future -ready energy solutions, Tata Power has established 4.9 GW of  integrated solar cell and module manufacturing capacity, 
signed 2.8 GW of pumped hydro storage projects, and expanded its EV charging network to over 7, 000 public charging points 
across 717 cities and towns in India.  As a pioneer in India’s clean energy t ransition, Tata Power remains committed to 
achieving Net Zero by 2045 and continues to partner with public and private stakeholders to deliver reliable, sustainable, and 
technology -led energy solutions across the country.
