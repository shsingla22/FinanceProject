TVS MOTOR COMPANY  JOINS FORCES WITH SEWA, SIDBI AND  
NRDC TO EMPOWER WOMEN THROUGH SUSTAINABLE E -
MOBILITY SOLUTIONS  
 
Udaipur , February 27 , 2025:  TVS Motor Company  (TVSM)  - a leading global automaker in 
the two and three -wheeler segment  - has partnered with the Self -Employed Women’s 
Association (SEWA), Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI), AMU Leasing, and  
Natural Resources Défense Council (NRDC) to launch  the ‘Wheels of Empowerment’  
initiative . The program  aims to empower women in rural areas by fostering financial 
independence through entrepreneurship,  using electric vehicles (EVs) for income generating 
activities . 
 
‘Wheels of Empowerment’  addresses the mobility opportunity in rural India by providing 
affordable, sustainable, and accessible electric two- wheelers, aligning with TVS Motor 
Company's vision of inclusive mobility. Currently, 26 TVS iQube electric scooters  have been 
deployed, with an additional 100 planned for the next quarter. The program aims to provide 
10,000 EVs in the next three years. SEWA and NRDC are working to aggregate demand across 21 Indian states,  by engaging with SEWA members . The program is also seek ing 
government support to facilitate broader adoption and policy backing for rural e- mobility.  
 
Speaking at the initiative,  Manu Saxena, S enior  Vice President , EV Business , TVS Motor 
Company , said,  “TVS iQube  has established itself as one of India's leading electric two -
wheelers, with a community of more than 5,00,000 customers . The 'Wheels of Empowerment,' 
initiative aims to combine the goodness of sustainable mobility  with income generation activity 
to unlock  new possibilities . The partnership with SEWA, SIDBI and NRDC is a pivotal move 
which will give us scale, ins ight and access required to create models of economic 
independence.”  
 
‘Wheels of Empowerment’  brings together TVS M’s prowess in EV technology , SEWA’s 
expansive 3.2-million -member  network, SIDBI’s financial expertise, AMU Leasing’s 
operational capabilities, and NRDC’s innovative approach to drive sustainable mobility 
solutions . To make TVSM EVs more affordable, SIDBI , NRDC and AMU have introduced 
concessional financing and risk -sharing models, bridging the price gap between electric and 
internal combustion engine vehicles.   
 
“Electric  vehicles need to be affordable and accessible to facilitate the transition to clean 
mobility. NRDC brought together grassroot  organizations, development banks, original 
equipment manufacturers and other key stakeholders to partner and develop innovative 
financial models to enable EV ownership by women in rural communities. This initiative is 
more than just about vehicles —it endorses the empowerment of women, strengthening 
livelihoods, and making clean mobility a reality for all,”  said Dipa Singh Bagai, Country 
Head, NRDC India.  
 
TVS Motor Company will continue to conducts product demonstrations and technical training 
camps, equipping SEWA members with essential troubleshooting skills . An extended two -year 
warranty and bi -monthly rural service camps will enhance the ease of ownership. Even though 
rural markets account for 55% of the country’s two- wheeler sales , it remains largely untapped 
for electric mobility . Early adopters have reported significant fuel cost savings, increased 
convenience, and improved livelihood opportunities, becoming endorsers  for e-mobility within 
their communities.
