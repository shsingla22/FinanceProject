AXISCADES Appoints Senior Leaders to Spearhead  Accelerated Growth  
KP Mohanakrishnan  appointed  as Deputy CEO  & President - Aerospace , and  D Murali Krishnan 
named  COO & President - ESAI to drive transformation in the company growth . 
 
11th April 2025, Bengaluru : AXISCADES Technologies Limited (BSE: 532395 | NSE: AXISCADES) , 
a leading end to end technology and engineering solutions provider, is pleased to announce the 
appointment of Mr. KP Mohan akrishnan as  Deputy CEO  & President - Aerospace  and Mr. D Murali 
Krishnan as Chief Operating Officer & President – ESAI , pursuant to a resolution passed by the Board 
of Directors in its meeting held on 09th April 2025 . 
The appointments of Mr. Mohanakrishnan and Mr. Murali Krishnan mark a strategic step forward in 
AXISCADES’ ongoing transformation journey. As the company accelerates efforts to realign its 
organizational structure with the rapidly evolving global aerospace , defence -tech, and semiconductor 
ecosystem, these leadership additions come at a pivotal moment . The recent integration of its 
subsidiaries i.e., AXISCADES Aerospace & Technologies Pvt. Ltd. and Mistral Solutions Pvt. Ltd. was 
significant in unlock ing synergies and reinforcing AXISCADES  as a key player in India’s drive for self -
reliance in aerospace and defence technology. Both leaders are poised  to drive this momentum and play 
an important role in advancing towards the company’s vision  of being a Product -Driven, Non -Linear 
Technology Leader  delivering cutting -edge innovation and creating unparalleled value for all 
stakeholders.   
Welcoming them to the organization, Alfonso Martinez , CEO and MD of  AXISCADES said, “We are 
delighted to welcome Mohanakrishnan and Murali  Krishnan  to AXISCADES at a defining moment in 
our growth journey. Mohanakrishnan’s experience in building long -term partnerships and scaling multi -
million -dollar programs across the global aerospace and defence ecosystem will sharpen our business & 
execution edge , while Murali Krishnan brings a rare blend of digital foresight and operational excellence, 
with a track record that spans aerospace, defence, rail transport, healthcare, and sustainability . As we 
accelerate our transformation  from investing in AI -led aerospace & defence applications , and 
semiconductor innovation to expanding our chip -to-product capabilities and deepening global 
partnerships , leaders like Mohanakrishnan and Murali will play a critical role in driving impact at scale. I 
look forward to collaborating with them as we shape the next phase of our journe y defined by 
innovation, agility, and impact .” 
Mr. Mohan akrishnan, steps into the role of Deputy CEO following a distinguished career of over three 
decades spanning  across Aerospace & Defence, Automotive, and General Engineering . In his previous 
leadership roles at marquee organizations such as Mahindra Aerostructures Pvt. Ltd. and Tata Advanced 
Materials Ltd ., he has been  instrumental in driving strategic manufacturing relationships  and establishing 
long-term strategic value . His work has involved developing  deep engagement across international 
OEMs, Tier suppliers, and Indian defence  /public sector agencies such as MoD, HAL, ISRO, and 
DRDO .  
Commenting on the appointment Mr. Mohan akrishnan said, “I am deeply hono ured to take on the role 
of Deputy CEO and President of Aerospace at such a pivotal  time for AXISCADES as it transitions 
from  a service -based to a technology -driven product company. I would like to extend my thanks to Dr.

Sampath  Ravinarayanan , the Chairman, for his guidance and leadership. His contributions have laid a 
foundation for our success, and I am excited to build upon that as we continue to unlock high -value 
areas. Our goal is to become one of the top global aerospace players by addressing the industry’s needs 
in engineering as well  as rapid and service -based manufacturing  which covers  services like maintenance, 
repair, and outcome -based contracts , to enhance customer value and create recurring revenue streams. ” 
Mr. D Murali Krishnan joins AXISCADES as COO along with his  existing position  as CEO of Mistral 
Solutions , a subsidiary of AXISCADES specializing in Chip-to-product, Defence, Electronics, 
Semiconductor and Artificial Intelligent applications. Previously, he led a $200 million Digital 
Engineering business at Cyient Limited, managing over 4,000 associates and delivering end -to-end 
Product Development Life Cycle services.  He has also served as CEO of Engineering Services at Pennar 
Industries . 
“I am excited to have the opportunity to work closely with the top management to deliver on our shared 
vision of innovation and engineering excellence in my  role as COO  & President of ESAI . Our key 
business sectors are entering a new era defined by AI -led innovation, full -stack product development, 
and increased global collaboration , and w e’re uniquely positioned to lead this shift by using our deep 
domain expertise, technology solutions, engineering talent, and trusted relationships across the 
ecosystem. With our strong foundation in product engineering and a growing capability in chip -to-
product solutions, we have the opportunity to shape next -generation systems that are smarter and 
globa lly competitive,” said Mr. Murali Krishnan . 
These strategic appointments reflect AXISCADES ’ focus on leadership  excellence , digital innovation, 
and AI domain strength as it charts its next phase of growth in aerospace, defence, digital engineering, 
and semiconductor technologies.  
 
About  AXISCADES  Technologies  Ltd. 
 
AXISCADES is a leading, end -to-end technology, product and solutions provider aiding the creation of innovative, 
sustainable, and safer products worldwide in the Aerospace, Defense and ESAI domains. Headquartered in Bangalore with 
subsidiaries and offices worldwide, in USA, UK, France, Germany, Denmark, Canada  and China . AXISCADES has a 
diverse team of over 3 200+ professionals working across 1 7 locations across globe, striving to reduce the program risk and 
time to market. The comprehensive portfolio of sol utions covers the complete product development lifecycle from concept 
evaluation to manufacturing support and certification.
