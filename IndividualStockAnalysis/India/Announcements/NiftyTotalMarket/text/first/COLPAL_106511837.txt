PRABHA NARASIMHANThe Company does not have any subsidiary/associate/joint venture entity for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2025 and for the previous year. On November 21, 2025, the Government of India notified four Labour codes ('The New Labour Code'), consolidating existing labour laws, In accordance with Ind 
AS 19, changes to the employee benefit plans arising from legislative amendments are treated as plan amendments, requiring immediate recognition of past 
service cost in the statement of profit and Loss. The New labour code has resulted in one time increase in employee benefit expenses of Rs. 839 Lakhs which has 
been recognized in the Statement of Profit and Loss under Exceptional Items for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2025. The Government is in 
the process of notifying related rules to the New Labour codes and impact of these will be evaluated and accounted with applicable standards in the period in 
which they are notified.
