Press Release 
 
NALCO Delivers Historic FY26 Performance, Setting New 
Benchmarks in Production and Sales 
 
 
PERFORMANCE HIGHLIGHTS 
Production:  
 
Highest-ever Bauxite Excavation: 77.01 Lakh Tonne  
Highest-ever Bauxite Transportation: 77.07 Lakh Tonne  
Highest-ever Alumina Hydrate: 23.00 Lakh Tonne 
Highest-ever Calcined Alumina: 22.75 Lakh Tonne  
Highest-ever Cast Metal: 4.72 Lakh Tonne  
Highest-ever Net Power Generation: 6,953 MU 
Highest-ever Coal Production: 40 Lakh Tonne 
Sales: 
Highest-ever Total Alumina Sale:14.46 Lakh Tonne  
Highest-ever Domestic Alumina Sale: 1.38 Lakh Tonne 
Highest-ever Aluminium Metal Sale: 4.74 Lakh Tonne 
Highest-ever Domestic Aluminium Metal Sale:4.61 Lakh Tonne 
 
Bhubaneswar, 02.04.2026:  National Aluminium Company Limited (NALCO), the 
flagship Navratna CPSE under the Ministry of Mines, Government of India, and leading 
manufacturer and exporter of alumina and aluminium, has delivered an extraordinary 
performance in FY 2025–26, breaking all previous records in both production and sales 
since its inception over four decades ago. 
 
Marking a historic milestone, NALCO achieved its highest-ever production across 
multiple operational fronts, reinforcing its leadership as an integrated player in the 
alumina and aluminium sector. The company recorded bauxite excavation of 77.01 
lakh tonnes and bauxite transportation of 77.07 lakh tonnes, while alumina hydrate 
production reached 23.00 lakh tonnes and calcined alumina production stood at 22.75 
lakh tonnes. Cast metal production was recorded at 4.72 lakh tonnes, alongside a 
highest-ever net power generation of 6,953 MU and coal production of 40 lakh tonnes. 
 
======================================================= 
 
On the sales front too, the company also recorded unprecedented achievements, 
registering its best-ever performance across key segments, with total alumina sales 
reaching 14.46 lakh tonnes, domestic alumina sales at 1.38 lakh tonnes, and aluminium 
metal sales recorded at 4.74 lakh tonnes including highest-ever domestic metal sale of 
4.61 lakh tonnes. 
 
Worth mentioning that the company recorded a 6.13% growth in bauxite 
transportation over the previous year, while calcined alumina production witnessed a 
significant increase of 11.16% and aluminium cast metal production rose by 2.61% 
year-on-year. On the sales front, total alumina sales registered a robust growth of 
30.74%, with total aluminium sales also increasing by 2.82% compared to the previous 
year. 
Shri Brijendra Pratap Singh, Chairman-cum-Managing Director, NALCO, expressed 
pride in the company’s remarkable accomplishments. While reviewing the company’s 
performance at the Annual Business Plan strategic meet held at Angul, he 
congratulated the entire NALCO team and stakeholders for their unwavering 
commitment and collective efforts. “This achievement reflects the dedication, 
discipline, and collective strength of our employees. We have consistently striven to 
surpass our own benchmarks, and this milestone further strengthens our path towards 
sustained business excellence,” he said. 
Looking ahead, Shri Singh emphasized the company’s roadmap for continued growth 
and innovation. “We must continue improving and expanding our capabilities to meet 
future demands. Our progress will be driven by a renewed focus on the 3Ps - Plant 
Efficiency, People Efficiency, and Process Efficiency. As we move forward, our 
vision is firmly set on embracing digital transformation and advancing our green 
initiatives.” Shri Singh also acknowledged the invaluable guidance and support 
extended by the Ministry of Mines, Government of India, and the continued 
cooperation of the Government of Odisha in enabling NALCO’s journey of 
excellence. 
With record-breaking performance in FY26, NALCO has not only reinforced its 
position as a key player in the global aluminium industry but has also set a new 
benchmark for operational excellence and sustainable growth among public sector 
enterprises.
