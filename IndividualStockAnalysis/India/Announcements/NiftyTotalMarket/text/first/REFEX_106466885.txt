RE:  Disclosures under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and

Subject: Clarification to a reported event or information circulated in the electronic media.  
 
Dear Sir(s)/ Madam, 
 
This is with reference to the news/reports circulated by some print and electronic media, stating that ‘ certain 
premises related to the Refex Group in Chennai  was subjected to Search operation by the Income Tax Department (“ IT 
Dept.”)’. 
 
We hereby inform that the Income Tax Department conducted search operations at the registered office of the 
Company and certain other locations associated with the Refex Group. The search operations commenced on December 
09, 2025. 
 
During the entire process, the Company and its officers extended full cooperation to the authorities and have provided 
all relevant information as required. 
 
The Company shall continue to provide necessary updates to the Stock Exchanges as and when any further reportable 
event arises, in compliance with SEBI Listing Regulations. The Company’s business operations are completely 
unaffected by the search operations and continuing without any interruption. 
 
Meanwhile, it has come to our notice that several media articles and social media posts have been circulating regarding 
the ongoing matter. These articles have appeared even though the search operations were still underway and no official 
communication has been issued either by the authorities or by the Company. 
 
We submit that certain vested-interest individuals / entities appear to have circulated incorrect, misleading and 
baseless information with mala fide intention to take undue advantage of the situation. The Company categorically 
denies and strongly rejects such speculative media reports, which narrate false and misleading stories unrelated to the 
facts. The facts, information and numbers published in the media articles and social media pots are absolutely and 
completely misleading and incorrect without any substance and base.  
 
The process is going on, and the Company shall continue to cooperate with the authorities under the advice of its legal 
experts. The Company remains committed to transparency and shall make disclosures to stakeholders at the 
appropriate time in accordance with applicable laws. 
 
Rest assured, we at Refex, strictly adhere to the highest standards of ethical conduct and legal compliance.  
 
You are requested to take the above information on records and disseminate the same on your website.  
 
Thanking you.
