Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015  (‘Listing Regulations’)  – Demise of Mr. P S 
Raghavan, Independent Director  
 
We are deeply saddened to announce  the sudden untimely demise  of Mr. P. S. Raghavan, 
Independent Director of the Company, today, 24th November 2025.  
 
Mr. Raghavan had been associa ted with the Company as a Non -Executive , Independent 
Director since 9th May 2017, and was serving his second term  of office as an Independent 
Director until 8th May 2027. Mr. Raghavan’s experience & expertise in Foreign Affairs, 
Management & Administration and Business knowledge have been invaluable to the Company  
and instrumental in navigating unprecedented challenges. Mr. Raghavan  will always be 
remembered with great respect.  
 
The Board of Directors  of the Company  and all employees convey their deepest sympathy, 
sorrow, and heartfelt condolences to his bereaved family .

Raghavan under Regulation 30 read with  Para A (7) of Part A of Schedule III of the Listing 
Regulations and SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 11th
