Commenting  on the results,  Mr. Udayant  Malhoutra,  CEO and Managing  Director  said:
The Aerospace  segment  remained  the leading  contributor  to the Company’s  revenue  in H1 FY2027 , recording  year-on-year revenue  
growth  of 18.1%, despite  a modest  sequential  moderation  during  the quarter  compared  with Q1 FY27. Growth  was supported  by a 
steady  revenue  contribution  from DML’s  aerospace  component  manufacturing  business . The Airbus  A220 doors  program   continued  to 
advance,  further  strengthening  the Company’s  capabilities  in complex  aerostructure  manufacturing . The Company  remains  focused  on 
scaling  high-value aerospace  programs,  deepening  relationships  with global  OEMs  and expanding  its capabilities  in defence  and unmanned  
technologies  to support  long-term growth .
