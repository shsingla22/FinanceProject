Zydus and Formycon: FYB206 Becomes First Publicly Announced 
Keytruda® Biosimilar Accepted for Review by USFDA 
 
 The US Food and Drug Administration (USFDA) has accepted the Biologics License 
Application (BLA) for FYB206 for review. To the best of the companies’ knowledge, this is the 
first publicly announced acceptance by the USFDA of a filing for a proposed biosimilar to 
Keytruda®. 
 FYB206 was developed without a comparative Phase III efficacy trial, following a development 
pathway agreed upon with the USFDA in early 2025. 
 Zydus holds exclusive commercialization rights for the United States and Canada and brings 
extensive commercial experience in the North American market for this product. 
Ahmedabad, India and Planegg-Martinsried, Germany, 13 October 2026  
Zydus Lifesciences Limited (Zydus), an innovation-led global life-sciences company, and Formycon AG 
(Formycon), an independent developer of biosimilars, today jointly announced that the US Food and Drug 
Administration (USFDA) has accepted for review the Biologics License Application (“BLA”) for 
FYB206, a proposed biosimilar to Keytruda®1 (pembrolizumab). To the best of the companies’ 
knowledge, this is the first publicly announced acceptance by the USFDA of an application for a 
Keytruda® biosimilar. The BLA was submitted in the third quarter of 2026. Under the Biosimilar User 
Fee Amendments (BsUFA), the USFDA aims to complete its review within twelve months of submission, 
comprising up to 60 days for the filing review and 10 months for the substantive review. Based on this 
timeline, a decision could be expected in the third quarter of 2027. The regulatory dossier for FYB206 
has also been submitted to Health Canada. 
Dr. Sharvil P. Patel, Managing Director of Zydus Lifesciences Limited, said: “Acceptance of the 
application marks a significant step toward expanding access to affordable pembrolizumab therapy in the 
U.S. and Canada. As demand for high-quality, accessible oncology treatments continues to rise, FYB206 
has the potential to improve patient access and outcomes while strengthening our oncology biosimilars 
portfolio. Leveraging the biologics expertise of Formycon and our established oncology platform in North 
America, we remain focused on delivering meaningful value to patients.” 
Dr. Stefan Glombitza, CEO of Formycon AG, said: “With the FDA’s acceptance of the FYB206 
application for review, we are among the very first companies to advance a biosimilar to the world’s best-
selling biologic medicine to this stage. We developed FYB206 on a path agreed with the FDA, without 
conducting a comparative Phase III efficacy trial. This enabled us to accelerate the program and 
 
 
substantially reduce development investment while maintaining the rigorous scientific standards required 
for biosimilar approval. With Zydus, we have a partner with strong commercial capabilities and a clear 
oncology strategy in the US and Canada. Together, we aim to make this important cancer therapy 
accessible to more patients.” 
Pembrolizumab is an immune checkpoint inhibitor that binds to and blocks the PD-1 receptor and is used 
to treat numerous types of cancer. In 2025, worldwide sales of Keytruda®, including the subcutaneous 
formulation Keytruda Qlex™, reached USD 31.7 billion.2 In the first half of 2026, combined sales of 
Keytruda® and Keytruda Qlex™ totaled USD 16.40 billion.3 Keytruda® therefore remains the world’s best-
selling biologic medicine. In February 2025, Formycon agreed with the USFDA that FYB206 does not 
require a comparative Phase III efficacy trial. 
In December 2025, Formycon and Zydus had entered into an exclusive licensing and supply agreement 
for FYB206 in the United States and Canada. Formycon is responsible for completing development, 
preparing and filing the regulatory dossiers, and supplying the product. With the USFDA’s acceptance of 
the BLA for review, Formycon has reached a regulatory milestone under the agreement that entitles it to 
a milestone payment from Zydus. Zydus will commercialize FYB206 in both markets, subject to 
regulatory approval and resolution of any intellectual property issues. Formycon holds the global rights 
to FYB206 and will participate in the gross profits of its commercialization partners.

About Biosimilars:  
Biosimilars are successor products to biologic medicines. They are not copies; each one is developed 
from the ground up and must demonstrate to the health authorities that it has no clinically meaningful 
differences from the reference product in terms of safety and efficacy. According to the IQVIA Institute, 
118 biologic medicines with sales of USD 232 billion will lose their exclusivity in the U.S. alone by 2034, 
and for only twelve of them is a biosimilar in development. In Europe, biosimilars have saved EUR 75 
billion and made more than seven billion days of treatment possible.
