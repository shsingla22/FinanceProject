Update on US FDA Inspection at  Sun Pharma  Halol Facility  
 
Further to our intimation dated 14 June 2025 regarding the subject matter, in which we had informed that 
the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) conducted an inspection at the Company’s Halol Facility 
from 2 June to 13 June 2025, the FDA has now classi fied the facility as “Official Action Indicated” (OAI). 
This means the FDA considers the facility to be not fully compliant with regard to certain current good manufacturing practices (CGMP).  
 
The Halol facility is  under Import Alert , resulting in the ref usal of shipments from the facility into the US, 
barring certain exemptions due to drug shortage, until the facility becomes fully compliant with FDA and 
CGMP standards.  
 Sun Pharma remains committed to working with the FDA and maintaining CGMP standards a t all of its 
facilities to provide high -quality medicines to patients globally.
