 Key Highlights: Quarterly Performance  
 
 
 
 
2 
 Investor Release  
❑ Last year, festive sales began in late September, resulting in part of the demand falling in Q2. This 
year, festive sales began from the second week of October, shifting that demand into Q3. This makes 
the YoY comparison for Q2 less like -for-like. Despite this shift in festive demand, we continued to 
deliver healthy year -on-year growth . 
❑ Gross Profit for Q2 FY27 stood at INR 2,435.6 mn (53.9% margin),  compared with INR 2,174.5 mn 
(57.7% margin) in Q2 FY26, registering a growth of 12.0% YoY. Gross margin% were impacted by 
the sharp increase in Polyol and TDI prices. We made calibrated price increases to protect absolute 
profitability while still keeping  our products affordable.  
❑ ESOP expenses for Q2FY27 stood at  INR 25.5 Mn, compared with INR 3.6 Mn in Q2 FY26.   
❑ During the quarter  and half year ended Sep’26 , Advertisement and marketing spend was 5.2% & 
6.3% respectively, of Revenue from Operations.  
❑ The Company recorded a deferred tax charge of INR 64.3 mn in Q2 FY27, compared with nil in Q2 
FY26 and INR 73.0 mn in Q1 FY27. This is attributable to two factors: (i) normal -course reversal of 
timing differences relating to depreciation and provisions as they unwind through the P&L, and (ii) the 
unwinding of the deferred tax asset on carried -forward losses. This is a non -cash, accounting -
driven movement and does not impact the Company's cash tax outflow or underlying 
operating profitability.  
❑ Excluding deferred tax, PAT for Q2 FY27 stood at INR 223.2 mn (4.9% of Revenue from 
Operations) , compared with INR 159.6 mn (4.2%) in Q2 FY26, registering a growth of 39.9% YoY. 
For H1 FY27, PAT excluding deferred tax stood at INR 530.0 mn (6.2%), compared with INR 355.7 
mn (4.9%) in H1 FY26, up 49.0% YoY.  
 
 
 
Commenting on the results, Mr. Ankit Garg, Chairman, CEO and Executive Director : “We are pleased 
to report our highest -ever quarterly Revenue from Operations of INR 4,519.9 mn in Q2 FY27, registering a 
growth of 19.9% YoY. This takes our H1 FY27 revenue to INR 8,569.0 mn, up 18.4% YoY. Reported EBITDA 
(excluding Other Income) stood at INR 501.5 mn, up 21.5% YoY, with an EBITDA margin of 11.1%. Profit 
Before Tax increased to INR 265.4 mn, up 66.3% YoY, while Profit After Tax, excluding deferred tax, stood 
at INR 223.2 mn, registering a growth of 39.9% YoY.  
We delivered this growth despite the festive sale season commencing in the second week of October this 
year, compared with the last week of September last year, resulting in all the  festive demand shifting to Q3. 
Accordingly, we believe that H1 performance, when viewed together with Q3, will provide a better reflection 
of our underlying business momentum.  Management Commentary

Commenting on the results, Mr. Chaitanya Ramalingegowda , Executive Director: “The operating 
environment remained challenging during the quarter, with crude -linked raw materials, particularly Polyol and 
TDI, continuing  to remain elevated, with further price increases witnessed in recent months. Geopolitical 
disruptions in West Asia also impacted raw material availability and logistics across the industry. Over the 
last two quarters, we made calibrated price increases to  balance customer affordability with absolute 
profitability.  
Our retail expansion continued at an accelerated pace, with 41 COCO stores added in Q2 FY27 and 68 in 
H1 FY27, taking our network to 205 active stores across 137 cities. Our MBO network expanded to 2,589 
stores across 787 cities, compared with 2,250 stores acr oss 701 cities as of Jun'26. Own channels 
contributed 71.1% of H1 FY27 revenue. We are proud to share that we have crossed the milestone of 10 mn 
lifetime orders , reflecting the growing scale of our business and the trust customers place in Wakefit . 
Our planned FY27 capex of around INR 1,000 -1,200 mn remains on track, with approximately 80% allocated 
towards retail expansion, particularly our jumbo store format. Construction of our first jumbo store in 
Bengaluru is progressing, and we are targeting its launch in Q2' FY 28.  
As we continue to scale our operations, our focus will remain on strengthening our retail network, improving 
the productivity of existing stores and expanding our product catalogue. We believe these initiatives will help 
us enhance customer reach, drive be tter store economics and build a stronger foundation for our next phase

About Wakefit Innovations Limited  
Wakefit Innovations Limited (NSE: WAKEFIT | BSE: 544642), headquartered in Bengaluru and incorporated 
in 2016, is the largest D2C home and furnishings company in India in terms of revenue in Fiscal 2024 (Source: 
Redseer  Report). The company offers a wide range of products, including mattresses, furniture, and 
furnishings, through omnichannel presence, ensuring a seamless customer experience across all 
touchpoints, both online and offline. It is a full -stack vertically in tegrated company, enabling it to control every 
aspect of operations, from conceptualizing, designing and engineering the products to manufacturing, 
distributing and providing customer experience and engagement. Over the years, Wakefit has evolved from
