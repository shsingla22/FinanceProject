Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015,  regarding current production 
capacity of the Company’s manufacturing plants 
 
Dear Madam/Sir,  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that the Company's manufacturing plants, RFL- I and RFL- II, located at 326- 327, MIE, Bahadurgarh, 
Haryana, were constructed in 1994 and 1999 respectively and are now over 25 years old. To enhance the infrastructure, ensure safety compliance, and leverage increased Floor Area Ratio (FAR) for future expansion, the management is in the 
process of reconstruction and renovation of these plants.  
Further, in line with evolving market trends, the Company is actively expanding into the fashionable footwear segment. The production of fashion- forward 
footwear requires speci alized production lines and involves more complex 
manufacturing processes. This product -mix transition necessitates modifications 
to conventional assembly layouts and workflows, representing a key structural factor influencing the daily output capacity.  
Consequent to the aforesaid reasons, the total manufacturing capacity across all 
the plants has been reassessed / optimised as 9,10,000 pairs per day from the 
previous capacity of 10,50,000 pairs per day.  
The same is for your information and record please.
