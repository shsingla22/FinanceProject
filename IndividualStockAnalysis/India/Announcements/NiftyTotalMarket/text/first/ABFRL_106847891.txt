Performance Highlights for the Quarter  
1. ABFRL posted  YoY growth of 2% in Q2, with overall revenue at Rs. 2030 Cr.  
a. Quarterly growth moderated due to shift in festive dates to Q3  and subdued wedding season  
b. Revenue in H1 was up 6% YoY to reach Rs. 4056 Cr  
 
2. Emerging businesses anchored growth amid st a challenging quarter  
a. Ethnic portfolio (ex . TCNS) grew 12% YoY despite shift in festive and subdued wedding calendar  
 
 
b. Luxury portfolio grew 11% YoY  
c. TMRW portfolio delivered 10% YoY growth  
d. OWND grew 58% vs LY led by store additions  
 
3. EBITDA  for the quarter stood at Rs. 67 Cr, down 43% vs LY , mainly on account of  
a. Festive shift and the  resultant adverse impact on  operating leverage  
b. Scale up of new businesses – OWND and Galeries Lafayette  
 
4. Brands continued to expand their retail footprint in line with strategic priorities  
a. Added 35 new stores in Q2, taking total store additions to over 80+ in H1  
b. Total area now spanning  7.8 Mn. sq. ft.  
 
5. ABFRL closed its books in Q2 with gross cash of Rs. 1143 Cr. 
a. At standalone level, cash stood at Rs. 628 Cr.

Masstige & Value Retail  
Pantaloons format  demonstrated resilience amid st festive calendar realignment  as the related consumptio n 
shifted into  Q3 this year,  impacting the YoY picture this quarter . Adjusting for the festive calendar shift and 
store closures, the business grew at  ~6% vs LY in Q2.  The brand continued to consistently  pursue its fashion -
forward positioning, with a sharper merchandise proposition, focused brand campaigns and 360° festive 
activations reinforcing consumer engagement. E -commerce also delivered 19% YoY growth  this quarter , led 
by targeted engagement campaigns and an improved online shopping experience.  
In parallel, Pantaloons continued to optimise its retail network through selective store additions and strategic 
closures, sharpening its focus on high -potential markets and larger, upgraded formats. The retail network for 
Pantaloons stood at 387 stores at quarter -end. 
OWND sustained its strong growth trajectory, delivering 58% YoY growth, primarily led by store additions . 
The brand continued to scale its presence, with its retail network reaching 90 stores at quarter -end.

Luxury Retail  
The Luxury portfolio delivered robust revenue growth across the super -premium and luxury segments.  
The Collective and Mono Brands (TCMB) strengthened its luxury proposition through new brand partnerships, 
an expanded omnichannel presence and curated consumer experiences. The retail footprint, including mono -
brand stores, stood at 49 locations.  
Galeries Lafayette reinforced its positioning as a differentiated luxury destination through exclusive launches,

TMRW  
The portfolio delivered 10% YoY revenue growth, driven by category expansion and an enhanced range of 
premium offerings. Including WROGN, growth stood at 19% YoY. Profitability improved for another 
consecutive quarter, driven by increasing scale. The portfolio also strengthened its omnichannel presence,

Way forward   
The quarter was shaped by a shift in the festive calendar and a subdued wedding season, which impacted the 
timing of consumer purchases and moderated reported revenue  & EBITDA  growth. Despite these near -term 
headwinds, the underlying business remained resilient, with newer emerging businesses delivering healthy 
growth and providing strong support to overall performance.  
Looking ahead, despite near -term macroeconomic uncertainties, we remain confident in the significant long -
term opportunities across India's fashion and lifestyle market  and the  strength of o ur diversified portfolio that 
will position us to capitalise it well . We remain focused on expanding through larger, more productive stores, 
scaling emerging businesses, sharpening merchandise assortments to reflect evolving consumer aspirations 
and strengthening brand salience. Driving profitable growth remains central to our strategy, with disciplined 
execution enabling us to  deliver sustainable, long -term value.  
 
 
About Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Limited  
Aditya Birla  Fashion and Retail Limited (ABFRL), part of the Aditya Birla Group, is India’s leading fashion powerhouse, 
offering a distinguished portfolio of renowned brands and retail formats, catering to multiple high -growth segments. As of 
September  30, 2026, the company ha s presence across 1268 stores spanning ~7.8 Mn. sq. ft. retail space.  
ABFRL’s portfolio includes Pantaloons , one of India’s most loved fashion destinations, and OWND, a growing retail 
format for GenZ and the youth. The company also operates The Collective , who commands a dominant position as one 
of the country’s most influential multi -brand luxury retailers, with exclusive long -term tie -ups with global fashion brands 
including Ralph Lauren, Hackett London, Ted Baker and Fred Perry. ABFRL has also partnered with Paris 
based Galeries Lafayette  to introduce a high -end luxury destination in India.  
ABFRL is a market leader in branded ethnic wear, its portfolio includes Jaypore and Tasva  and it has forged strategic 
partnerships with India’s top designers such as Tarun Tahiliani, Sabyasachi, Shantnu & Nikhil and House of 
Masaba.  Recently, the Company further expanded its ethnic wear leadership with integration of  TCNS brands , home to 
leading women’s brands W, Aurelia, Wishful, Elleven and Folk song .  
To address the evolving preference of digitally native consumers, ABFRL is also building a platform of new -age, digital -
first fashion  brands under its tech -led venture TMRW . This platform collaborates with emerging entrepreneurs to co-
create scalable fashion businesses in India’s rapidly expanding fashion ecosystem.
