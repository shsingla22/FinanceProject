Dear Sir, 
Pursuant to Reg. 30 of SEBI LODR, we would like to inform that the Company has received notices from BSE 
Ltd. (BSE) and National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) dt. 21.11.2024 regarding non-compliance with 
the provision of Regulation 17(1) of the SEBI (LODR) relating to composition of Board of Directors of the 
Company during the quarter ended 30 September 2024 and imposition of fine of Rs. 3,65,800/- each by BSE 
& NSE for such non- compliance. 
In response to the notices, IndianOil vide letter dt. 22.11.2024 has represented to the BSE & NSE that being a 
Government Company, the power to appoint Directors (including Independent Directors) vests with the 
MoP&NG, Govt. of India and hence the shortfall in Independent Directors including non-appointment of 
Women Independent Director on the Board of the Company during the quarter ended 30" September 2024 
was not due to any negligence / default by the Company. Accordingly, IndianOil should not be held liable to 
pay the fines and the same should be waived-off. IndianOil regularly takes up with MoP&NG for appointment 
of requisite number of Independent Directors (including Woman Independent Director) to ensure compliance 
with Corporate Governance norms enunciated under SEBI (LODR) as well as the Companies Act. 
We would also like to inform that the Company had received similar notices from the BSE & NSE in the past 
imposing fines and waiver request from the Company was considered favorably by the Exchanges.
