The Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting held today, inter alia, transacted the following business: 
(i)  Change in Nominee Director 
 
(a) Due to change in nomination by M/s DENSO Corporation, Japan; Mr. Naohisa Kuriyama, Nominee Director 
has resigned with effect from 28th March, 2025.  
 
(b) On the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board has approved the 
appointment of Mr. Yusuke Hara as Nominee Director of M/s DENSO Corporation, Japan with effect from 
28th March, 2025 (subject to approval of the Shareholders). 
 
(ii)  Change in Alternate Director 
 
(a) Dr. Yasuhiro Iida, Alternate Director to Mr. Naohisa Kuriyama vacated his office in accordance with the 
provisions of Section 161 of the Companies Act, 2013. 
 
(b) Further, on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board has approved 
the appointment of Dr. Yasuhiro lida as an Alternate Director to Mr. Yusuke Hara (subject to approval of the 
Shareholders). 
 
The details pertaining to the appointment(s) are enclosed as Annexure-A.

Brief Profile  Mr. Yusuke Hara has been associated with M/s DENSO Corporation, 
Japan since 1999 and started his professional journey with Production 
Technology Development Department. Thereafter, in 2014, Mr. Hara 
associated with DENSO International Asia Co. Ltd. (Thailand) and became 
Head of ASEN Regional Production Planning and Production Technology. 
In 2016, Mr. Hara became Deputy General Manager of Corporate Strategy 
Division, Corporate Planning and Global Strategy Department and was 
instrumental in long term business planning and implementation of global 
management system. From 2021 to 2024, Mr. Hara was in control of 
General Affairs and Personnel Division and then was appointed as 
Executive Officer in Air Conditioning System Division. Mr. Hara holds a 
degree in Science and Engineering from Saitama University, Japan. Mr. 
Hara has vast and varied experience in corporate planning and global 
strategy in line with global practices of DENSO Group.

Brief Profile            Dr. Yasuhiro Iida has been associated with M/s DENSO Corporation, 
Japan since 1985 and started his professional journey with Production 
Engineering Division as Director in August 2008, where he led the 
development of production systems for components in six different areas 
such as thermal, diesel, gasoline and ceramic. In 2011, Dr. Iida was 
appointed as President of DENSO Thailand Co. Limited and in 2012,  
Dr. Iida was assigned as Executive Director of M/s DENSO Corporation, 
Japan. In 2015, Dr. Iida was appointed as CEO in the Asia Pacific region 
(President, DENSO International Asia Limited). In 2018, Dr. Iida was 
appointed as Head of Thermal Systems Business Group and in 2021;    
Dr. Iida was named as the Head of Global Production Center. In 2022, as 
a head of Purchasing Group, Dr. Iida led significant business of promoting 
carbon neutrality, digital transformation and business continuity planning. 
Dr. Yasuhiro Iida is presently the Chairman & Managing Director of            
M/s DENSO International India Private Limited.
