This is to inform  you that the Board  of Directors  of Kansai  Nerolac  Paints  Limited  has in the Board  meeting

(i) Retirement  of Mr. Anuj  Jain as the Managing  Director:  
 
The Company and Mr.  Anuj Jain, Managing Director, (holding Director Identification Number: 08091524)

of business on 31st March, 2025.  
 
The Company places on record the contribution of Mr. Jain for 35 long years in various capacities across 
geographies. He has led the Company from the front as the Managing Director for the last three years and 
contributed significantly to the progress of t he Company.

as under:  
Reasons  for change  Mutually  agreed  retirement  
Date  of resignation  Close  of business  on 31st March, 2025

Press  Release              Mumbai,  Feb 5, 2025  
 
Kansai Nerolac  Paints Ltd., India and Managing Director, Mr. Anuj  Jain have mutually agreed 
that Mr. Jain will take early retirement  from the services of the Company  at the end of the 
Financial year ending 31st March 2025.  
 
The Company places on record the contribution of Mr . Anuj Jain for 35 long years in various 
capacities across geographies. He has led the Company from the front as Managing Director & 
CEO for the last three years and contributed significantly to the progress of the Company.  
 
Mr. H Nishibayashi, Board member affirmed the outstanding leadership shown by Mr . Jain during 
a very challenging environment during the last three years.  
 
The entire Board expressed their gratitude towards Mr . Jain.   
 
Mr. Anuj Jain expressed gratitude to the Board for believing in him and giving him the charge of 
the Company and that he is very fortunate to work for a very long period with the Company and 
could contribute to the growth of the Company.   
 
He will be available to the Company for a smooth transition to the new leadership  and for any 
advice when required.  
 
The Board has decided to appoint Mr. Pravin Chaudhari as the Managing Director of the Company 
effective from 1st April 2025.  Mr. Chaudhari is currently a nominee of Kansai Paint Japan on the 
Board of Kansai Nerolac Paints Ltd. He is also designated as Head  of India Operations.  
Mr. Chaudhari had a long stint with Kansai Nerolac Paints Ltd.  since 1993 and held various 
positions, including as Executive Director during the period from 2008 to 2018. He brings vast 
experience in various domains.  
 
The Board wishes him the very best in his new assignment.
