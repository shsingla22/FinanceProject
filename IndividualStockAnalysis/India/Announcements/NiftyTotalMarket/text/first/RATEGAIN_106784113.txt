RateGain Named Fastest Growing SaaS Company at The Economic Times Enterprise AI Awards 2026 
 
Independent jury reinforces RateGain’s leadership at the intersection of AI, data and travel technology 
 
India, 18 September 2026: RateGain Travel Technologies Limited (NSE: RATEGAIN, BSE: 543417), a global 
provider of AI-powered SaaS solutions for the travel and hospitality industry, has been named the Fastest 
Growing SaaS Company at the ET Enterprise AI Awards 2026. The award places RateGain among the fastest-
scaling enterprise AI businesses serving the travel industry globally. 
 
That scale is evident in where the company's technology operates. RateGain works with 33 of the world's top 
40 hotel chains, four of the top five airlines, seven of the top ten car rental companies and 25 Global Fortune 
500 companies, which together account for a substantial share of how travel is priced, distributed and sold 
worldwide. 
 
That reach is rooted in a data position built over more than two decades, rather than a recent product cycle. 
RateGain connects more than 200,000 properties to over 700 demand partners and processes more than 231 
billion availability, rate and inventory each year, giving it a broad view of how travel is priced and distributed. 
In March 2026, the consolidation of its marketing technology capabilities under Sojern added the world's 
largest and most comprehensive source of travel intent data alongside that. Together, these capabilities give 
RateGain’s AI models access to a proprietary dataset that its competitors do not have, allowing customers to 
make pricing, distribution and marketing decisions from a single view of the traveller rather than three 
fragmented ones. 
 
Commenting on the win, Bhanu Chopra, Founder & Managing Director, RateGain Travel Technologies 
Limited, said , “We are proud to be recognized as the Fastest Growing SaaS Company at the ET Enterprise AI 
Awards 2026. The recognition underscores the momentum we are building at the intersection of data, AI and 
travel technology, and validates the strength of the platform we have built over the past two decades. As our 
customer base grows, the data and insights generated across the network further strengthen our models and 
capabilities, creating a compounding advantage. As a global company trusted by some of the world’s largest 
travel brands, this accolade is a meaningful validation of both our growth trajectory and our ability to deliver 
enterprise AI at scale”.  
 
Presented by The Economic Times, the Enterprise AI Awards 2026 recognize organizations, technology 
providers and enterprise leaders driving measurable business transformation through artificial intelligence, 
across categories spanning AI products, enterprise adoption, responsible AI and industry-specific 
innovation. Entries in the category were assessed by an independent jury on growth in annual recurring 
revenue, user growth, customer retention and engagement, and the scale of enterprise and SMB adoption. 
 
About RateGain Travel Technologies Limited 
RateGain Travel Technologies Limited is a global provider of AI-powered SaaS solutions for travel and 
hospitality, working with 14,000+ customers and 700+ partners across 160+ countries. 
RateGain helps travel and hospitality businesses accelerate revenue generation through acquisition, 
retention, and wallet share expansion. 
 
Today, RateGain is one of the world's largest processors of electronic transactions, price points, and travel 
intent data, enabling revenue management, distribution, and marketing teams across hotels, airlines, 
destination marketing organizations, online travel agents, metasearch companies, package providers, car 
rentals, travel management companies, cruises, and ferries to drive better business outcomes. 
Founded in 2004 and headquartered in India, RateGain works with 33 of the Top 40 Hotel Chains, 4 of the 
Top 5 Airlines, 7 of the Top 10 Car Rental companies, and all leading DMOs, OTAs, and metasearch platforms, 
including 25 Global Fortune 500 companies, unlocking new revenue every day.
