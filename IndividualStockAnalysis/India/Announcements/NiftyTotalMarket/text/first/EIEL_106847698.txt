Sub: Resignation as Statutory Auditors of Suyog Urja Limited  
Dear Sir/Madam, 
This is to inform you that we hereby tender our resignation as the Statutory Auditors of Suyog Urja 
Limited with effect from 04 November 2026. 
Our resignation is pursuant to the request received from the Company vide its communication dated 
02 November 2026, consequent to the recent change in management and the Company's decision to 
align its audit and financial reporting framework with that of its parent company and the larger 
group. 
As communicated by the management, the Company proposes to appoint the existing Group Auditor 
of the parent company as its Statutory Auditor to facilitate greater synergy in the audit approach, 
uniformity in accounting and reporting practices, and efficient consolidation of financial statements 
at the parent company level. 
Accordingly, we are tendering our resignation to facilitate the Company's proposed change in audit 
arrangements. We confirm that our resignation is solely on account of the aforesaid management 
request and the proposed alignment of the Company's audit and financial reporting framework with 
that of the parent group, and is not attributable to any concerns regarding our professional conduct 
or performance as Statutory Auditors. 
We would like to place on record our sincere appreciation to the Board of Directors and the 
management for the cooperation, support and professional courtesy extended to us during our tenure 
as Statutory Auditors. 
Thanking you,
