Regulations, 2015 
Pursuant to Regulation 30(2) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we 
would like to inform that the Company has received notices from BSE & NSE vide their notices dated November 
21,2024, in respect of non-compliances of Regulation 17 (1),18(1) and 19 (1) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 regarding non -compliance with the composition of Board 
Directors, Audit and Nomination and Remuneration Committee. A fine of Rs. 9,77,040/- each have been levied 
by both the Exchanges for such non- compliance. 
In this regard, the Company have represented to the BSE & NSE vide its letter dated November 22,2024 
stating, that being a Government Company, the power to appoint Directors including Independent Directors 
vests with Department of Fertilizers (DoF), Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, Government of India. 
The shortfall in number of Independent Directors including non-appointment of Women Independent Director 
on the Board of the Company during the quarter ended September 30,2024 was not due to any negligence / 
default by the Company. Accordingly, the Company should not be held liable to pay the fines and the same 
should be waived-off. The Company regularly takes up with DoF for appointment of requisite number of 
Independent Directors including Woman Independent Director to ensure compliance with Corporate 
Governance norms enunciated under SEBI (LODR) as well as the Companies Act, 2013 
The details as per SEBI Master Circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 
are enclosed as Annexure 1.
