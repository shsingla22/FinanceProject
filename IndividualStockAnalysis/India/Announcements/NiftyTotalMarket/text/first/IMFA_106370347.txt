Dear Sir/Madam, 
This disclosure is in continuat ion of our earlier intimations dated 22nd January 
2025 and 18th March 2025 relating to Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) and 
Share Subscription and Shareholders Agreement (SSHA) entered into by the 
Company with JSW Green Energy One Ltd & JSW Green Energy Seven Ltd 
respectively to supply hybrid renewable power of 70 MW Contracted Demand 
(Solar capacity of 50 MW AC & Wind capacity of 100 MW). 
Due to change in location from where the hybrid renewable energy will be 
sourced, the earlier agreements stand cancelled and the Company has entered 
into fresh PPA and SSHA today with JSW Renew Energy Twelve Limited to 
supply hybrid renewable power of 70 MW Contracted Demand (Solar capacity 
of 55 MW AC & Wind capacity of 108 MW). Consequently, the investment 
amount has increased marginally from Rs 83.26 crore to Rs 85.38 crore.
