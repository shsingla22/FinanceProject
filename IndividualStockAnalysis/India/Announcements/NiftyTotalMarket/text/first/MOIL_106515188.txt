Dear Sir/Madam,   
  
In terms of Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, it is to inform that in line with the business practice of fixing/revising prices of Manganese Ore and 
other produc ts, the Company has fixed/revised prices of different grades of Manganese Ore and other products, effective 
from 01. 02.2026 as under.    
1. The prices of all Ferro grades of manganese ore with Manganese content of Mn - 44% and above have been increased 
by 5% for the month of February 2026 on the prices prevailing since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 
31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  
 
2. The prices of all other Ferro grades of manganese ore with Manganese content below Mn -44% have been increased 
by 5% for the month of February, 2026 on the prices prevailing since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 
31.01.2026/01.02.2026 except BG4584. In case of  BG4584, continued the January 2026 price for month of 
February, 2026 w.e.f. midnight of 31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  
 
3. The prices of SMGR (Mn -30%) and Fines grades have been increased by 5% for the month of February, 2026 on 
the prices prevailing since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  
 
4. The prices of SMGR (Mn 25%) grades have been continued for the month of February 2026 prevailing since 
01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  
 
5. The prices of all Chemical grades have been increased by 5% for the month of February 2026 on the prices 
prevailing since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  
 
6. The prices of SMGR (Mn 20%) grades have been continued for the month of February 2026 prevailing since 
01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  
 
7. The prices of Metal Mandi Fines namely (DBF575 and MSF592 except UKF532) have been continued for the 
month of February 2026 prevailing since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 31.01.2026/01.02.2026. In case of UKF532, 
price have been increased by 10% for the mo nth of February 2026 on the prices prevailing since 01.01.2026 w.e.f. 
midnight of 31.01.2026/01.02.2026.  
 
8. The basic price Rs.1,90,000/ - PMT of EMD have been continued for the month of February 2026 prevailing since 
01.01.2026 w.e.f. midnight of 31.01.2026/01.02.2026.
