PVR INOX Marks Twin Launch Milestone in Gurugram and Raipur; Unveils State -
of-the-Art Multiplexes at ELAN MIRACLE and ZORA The Mall  
~ PVR INOX launches a 6 -screen cinema in Gurugram  and unveils Chhattisgarh ’s first Luxe property in 
Raipur, both opening today ~  
 
National, April 25, 2025 : PVR INOX , India’s largest and most premium cinema exhibitor, proudly 
announces the launch of two landmark properties in Gurugram  and Raipur, underscoring its relentless 
drive to shape the future of cinema across diverse consumer landscapes. These strategic additions reflect 
the brand’s dual focus —fortifying its presence in high -growth urban markets while pioneering innovative 
forma ts in India’s emerging regions.  
In Gurugram , the brand opens its 13th property at ELAN MIRACLE —a vibrant, 7 -acre retail and lifestyle 
destination along the fast -evolving Sector -84, Dwarka Expressway . With this launch, PVR INOX’s screen 
count in the city rises to 68, further cementing its leadership in one of India’s most future -forward urban 
corridors. The 6-screen cinema  offers a seating capacity of 762, including 67 plush recliners , and is 
equipped with state -of-the-art 2K projection , Dolby 7.1 surround sound , and  Next Gen 3D . It also debuts 
the brand’s first -ever ‘Cine Café’ —a chic, lounge -style social space designed to elevate pre - and post -
screening interactions for today’s experience -seeking audiences.  
Simultaneously, in Raipur , PVR INOX introduces Chhattisgarh ’s first PVR INOX Luxe  at the newly launched 
ZORA The Mall . Developed under the innovative FOCO (Franchise Owned, Company Operated)  model, 
this 5 -screen multiplex features 978 seats , including a luxurious Luxe auditorium  and four Mainstream 
screens. With BARCO 4K Laser Projection , Dolby ATMOS , and Next Gen 3D , the property combines top -
tier technology with immersive aesthetics inspired by natural elements —warm lighting, indoor greenery, 
and café -style lounges —offering audiences a sensory journey from foyer to finale.  
Speaking on the opening , Ajay Bijli , Managing Director, PVR INOX Ltd., said:  
“At PVR INOX, we are focused on strengthening our presence  across both metro cities and emerging 
markets with a balanced and future -ready approach. The opening of our 1 3th cinema in Gurugram 
reinforces our commitment to premium experiences in key urban hubs, while the launch of Chhattisgarh’s 
first PVR  INOX  Luxe in Raipur —under the FOCO model —marks a strategic step. Together, these properties 
reflect our vision to scale thoughtfully, partner effectively, and deliver world -class entertainment to 
audiences across the country.”  
Adding to this, Sanjeev Kumar Bijli, Executive Director, PVR INOX Ltd.,  remarked:  
“Our expansion is not just about more screens, but smarter, future -ready formats that reflect evolving 
consumption patterns. With ELAN MIRACLE, we tap into the fast -paced growth of New Gurugram, while 
ZORA Mall in Raipur showcases the power of the FOCO mod el in unlocking Tier 2 potential. Whether it’s 
design innovation or tech -led immersion, we’re building cinema ecosystems that meet audiences where 
they are —and where they’re headed.”  
As India embraces new frontiers of growth, PVR INOX continues to reimagin e what cinema means for 
modern audiences —offering not just entertainment, but thoughtfully designed destinations that combine 
technology, comfort, and community .  
 
With every new opening, the brand brings its vision of cinema as an elevated experience  close r to reality —
one that caters to a generation that values authenticity, immersion, and shared moments.   
Whether in metro cities or rising markets, PVR INOX remains committed to delivering stories not just on 
screen, but through every space it curates.  
With these opening s, PVR strengthens its growth momentum in FY 2025 -26 with 1,734 screens at 351 
properties in 110 cities (India and Sri Lanka).
