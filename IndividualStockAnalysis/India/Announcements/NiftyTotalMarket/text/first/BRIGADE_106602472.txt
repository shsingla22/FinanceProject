 PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Group acquires 5.72 acres land in Hyderabad;  
to develop a residential project  
 
Hyderabad, April 28, 2026  – Brigade Group has announced the acquisition of a prime land parcel in Hyderabad 
through a recent auction conducted by the Telangana Industrial Infrastructure Corporation (TGIIC). The 
outright purchase of Plot 6 at Osman Nagar, Hyderabad comprising 5.72 acres was acquired at a price of INR 
44 Crores per acre, excluding registration costs. The company plans to develop a residential project. The 
acquisition is a pivotal step in Brigade’s broader strategy to deepen its roots in Hyder abad and further portfolio 
expansion.     
 
Commenting on the development, Amar Mysore, Executive Director, Brigade Enterprises Limited said, 
“Hyderabad has been a key focus market for us, having emerged as one of the most dynamic real estate markets 
in the country and this land parcel in Osman Naga r is a strategic fit, meeting our stringent criteria for scale and 
suitability for premium development. We are committed to contributing to Hyderabad’s skyline by integrating 
high -technology developments and world -class construction standards that reflect the city’s status as a global 
enterprise hub. This acquisition is a vital step in our expansion in the city, as we strive to deliver excellence and 
create future -ready spaces that meet the evolving demands of the city”  
 
Osman Nagar, Hyderabad is an ideal location for a residential project that balances accessibility, livability , and 
long -term value. Driven by its strategic proximity to IT hubs like HITEC City and Gachibowli, seamless 
connectivity via the Outer Ring Road, a serene low -density environment, and strong growth momentum.  
 
About Brigade Group:  Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers with 
close to four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and winning their 
customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark bu ildings and transformed skylines across India in 
the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, and GIFT City with 
developments across Residential, Office, Retail, Hospitality and Education Sectors.
