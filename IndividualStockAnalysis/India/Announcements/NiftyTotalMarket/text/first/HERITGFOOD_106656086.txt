Sub:  Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 – Heritage Foods Limited 
receives “Best Environmental Award” for Narketpally Plant  
Dear Sir/Madam,  
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we 
wish to inform that Narketpally Plant of the Company has been conferred with the “Best Environmental Award”  
by the Telangana Pollution Control Board, Government of Telangana , on the occasion of World Environment Day  
on June 5, 2026 . 
The award  was presented  by Mrs. Konda  Surekha,  Hon’ble  Minister  for Forest,  Environment  and Endowments, 
Government  of Telangana,  in recognition  of the plant's  continuous  contribution  towards  environmental 
conservation and sustainability. The award was received on behalf of the Company by Mr. L. Srinivas, Head – 
Operations of the Company.  
The award acknowledges the plant’s sustained efforts in implementing Zero Waste practices, Circular Economy 
procedures, modern energy conservation systems, and various sustainability initiatives aimed at protecting and 
preserving the environment. 
The recognition reflects the organization’s strong commitment towards environmental protection and sustainable 
development.
