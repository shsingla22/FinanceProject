JBM Auto’s Growth Impetus continues in Q3 with 7.80% YoY 
Increase in Net Profit 
 Profit before Tax up by 10.38%, 
  EPS up by 8.18% 
 EBIDTA up by 20.29% 
 
Gurugram, January 29, 2025: JBM Auto Limited, India’s leading automotive and electric 
vehicle company (BSE: 532605 & NSE Code: JBMA) , today announced its consolidated results 
for the quarter ended 31st Dec 2024 and has shown 7.80% increase in net profit to Rs. 52.42 
crore in Q3FY25 as compared to Rs. 48.63 crore in Q3FY24. 
 
Sales including other operating income stood at Rs. 1,396.15 crore compared to Rs. 1,346.17 
crore in Q3FY24, an increase of 3.71%. The company recorded a profit before tax of Rs. 72.75 
crore in Q3FY25 as compared to Rs. 65.91 crore during the third quarter in the previous year, 
an increase of 10.38%.  
 
EBIDTA increased by 20.29% at Rs. 192.83 crore compared to Rs. 160.30 crore in Q3FY24. 
Earnings per share (EPS) for Q3FY25 stood at Rs. 4.45, compared to Rs. 4.12 in Q3FY24.
