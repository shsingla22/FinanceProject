23rd September  2025:  Lemon Tree Hotels Limited announces the opening of  
Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels, Krishna, Vijayawada . This managed hotel 
is the 5th property of the group in  Andhra  Pradesh  and the third property in the 
city.  
 
This smart hotel features 44 well -appointed rooms that delight guests with 
impeccably clean interiors, efficient service  and an affordable value proposition. It 
also has a multi -cuisine restaurant  – Keys Café – along with a boardroom. Our 
open-hearted, cheerful smiles, inclusive culture, and environmental sensitivity are 
perfectly paired with the brand’s signature warm welcome and fond farewell.  
 
Vijayawada is home to several iconic landmarks, including the Kanaka Durga 
Temple, a revered pilgrimage site dedicated to Goddess Durga, situated atop 
Indrakeeladri Hill. The Prakasam Barrage, spanning the Krishna River, is another 
major attraction, offeri ng scenic views and serving as a vital link for irrigation and 
transportation. The city is also known for its thriving educational institutions, 
cultural festivals, and vibrant cuisine, particularly its famous Andhra -style spicy 
delicacies. With its strate gic location, rapid urbanisation , and deep -rooted 
traditions, Vijayawada stands as a dynamic and evolving city in southern India.
