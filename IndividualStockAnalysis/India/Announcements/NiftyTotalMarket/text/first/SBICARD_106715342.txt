SBI Card and Google Pay Launch Co-Branded Google Pay Flex SBI Card 
• Rewards customers on big or small spends, while simplifying everyday transactions by offering 
a unified digital payments experience 
• Avail instant rewards, flexible payment options, and seamless account management through the 
Google Pay app 
New Delhi, July 29, 2026: SBI Card, India's largest pure-play credit card issuer and Google, jointly 
announced the launch of 'Google Pay Flex SBI Card' as part of the Flex by Google Pay offering. The 
Google Pay Flex SBI Card is designed to simplify and elevate the everyday payment experience, 
delivering a convenient blend of rewards, flexibility, and security. This partnership brings together 
SBI Card's trusted credit card expertise and Google Pay's digital ecosystem to deliver a simple yet 
enhanced payments experience. 
The Google Pay Flex SBI Card turns everyday spending into rewarding experiences by enabling 
customers to earn Stars on all value purchases, with instant redemption at the time of payment, 
offering customers immediate value on spends. Customers can earn up to 18,000 Stars as reward 
points yearly on everyday spends, while earning up to 8 Stars for every cumulative ~500 spent. The 
credit card enables customers to earn reward points across a wide range of categories including 
groceries, bill payments, travel bookings, utility payments, shopping, and more, across online and 
in-store outlets. To offer financial flexibility, customers also have the option to convert their latest 
outstanding bill into convenient EMl's. 
Designed to simplify everyday payment experience, the credit card offers customers an integrated 
digital journey, enabling them to conveniently apply for the card, make payments, earn rewards, 
and manage their account within the Google Pay app. 
The contactless credit card is available on the RuPay and VISA payment networks, offering 
cardholders the choice to select their preferred payment network. The RuPay variant can be linked 
to UPI, enabling seamless transactions across millions of UPI merchants nationwide. Both variants 
can be tokenised on Google Pay for the convenience of tap-to-pay at millions of merchant outlets. 
Salila Pande, Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer, S81 Card, said, "At SB/ Card, our 
focus is on creating best-in-class products that make transactions simpler, seamless, and more 
relevant for our customers. Launch of the Google Pay Flex SB! Card is a significant step in that 
direction. By combining convenience, meaningful rewards, flexibility, and security, the distinctive 
card will redefine everyday payment experience in India. As India's digital payment ecosystem 
continues to evolve, we remain committed to building more such innovative and customer-centric 
products that create long-term value."

About SBI Card 
SBI Cards and Payment Services Limited ('SBI Card') is a non-banking financial company that offers 
extensive credit card portfolio to individual cardholders and corporate clients which includes 
lifestyle, rewards, travel & fuel, and banking partnership cards along with corporate cards covering 
all major cardholder' segments in terms of income profile and lifestyle. The brand has a wide base 
of over 22.6 MM+ cards-in-force. SBI Card has a diversified customer acquisition network that 
enables the organisation to engage prospective customers across multiple channels. SBI Card is a 
technology driven company. 
The Company is listed on National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. ("NSE") and BSE Ltd.('BSE").
