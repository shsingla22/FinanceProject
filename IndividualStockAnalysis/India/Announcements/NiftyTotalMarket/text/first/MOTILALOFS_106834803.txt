Motilal Oswal Financial Services Ltd.  |  Q2 & H1FY27 Results  |  Page 1 PRESS RELEASE  
 
Motilal Oswal Financial Services reports 29% YoY growth in Operating 
PAT for Q2FY27, led by 69% growth in Asset Management  
Highest -ever half -yearly Total PAT of ₹2, 583 Cr, up 5 1% YoY | Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) 
share rises to 66%  
Mumbai, October 29, 2026: Motilal Oswal Financial Services Ltd. (MOFSL) today announced its results for 
the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026. Operating PAT grew 29% YoY to ₹7 18 Cr in Q2FY27, 
led by strong growth in the Asset Management busine ss. Total PAT  for H1FY27, including Other 
Comprehensive Income, rose 5 1% YoY to ₹2, 583 Cr, the highest -ever half -yearly profit in the Company's 
history.  
ARR assets grew to ₹3.2 Lakh Cr, up 2 6% YoY, and ARR revenue grew to ₹1,1 82Cr, up 2 7% YoY. Sequentially, 
every business segment delivered higher revenue and profitability, with net operating revenue up 1 4% and 
Operating PAT up 1 8% QoQ.  
During Q2, Motilal Oswal group secures custodian licence from SEBI, completes institutional services val ue 
chain. This approval places the group among a small set of institutions authorised to offer safekeeping of 
securities, trade settlement, corporate action processing, and regulatory reporting of institutional clients, 
including, alternative investment fu nds, portfolio management services, mutual funds, insurance companies 
and pension funds, amongst others. The company will commence operations in Q 4FY27, subject to the 
completion of operational readiness requirements.  
Q2FY27 at a glance  
• Operating PAT ₹7 18 Cr, up 29% YoY and 1 8% QoQ; H1FY27 Operating PAT ₹1,3 27 Cr, up 2 2% YoY  
• Highest -ever half -yearly Total PAT ₹2, 583 Cr, up 5 1% YoY (including OCI)  
• Asset Management PAT ₹30 4 Cr, up 69% YoY  — now the largest contributor at 4 3% of Operating 
PAT 
• ARR share of revenue rises to 66% , improving the quality and predictability of earnings  
• Credit rating AA+ / Stable from all three leading rating agencies  following India Ratings' upgrade  
• Received approval from SEBI to act as a  Custodian of Securities

Built for Compounding  
ARR now contr ibutes 66% of revenue, and the rising share of the Asset and Private Wealth businesses is 
lifting the quality and predictability of earnings.  
Over the last 10 years, MOFSL has delivered 33% Operating PAT CAGR, 2 6% EPS CAGR and an average ROE 
of 23% , funded  entirely through internal accruals with no equity dilution. Net worth compounded at 25% 
over the same period, after three buybacks and consistent dividend payouts.  
Management Commentary  
Commenting on the results, Mr. Motilal Oswal, MD & CEO, MOFSL, said: "Q2FY27 reflects the deliberate 
shift in our earnings mix. Asset Management is now our largest profit pool, and two -thirds of our revenue is 
recurring, which makes our earnings more resilient through market cycles. With AA+ ratings from all three 
agencies and a decade of growth funded entirely from internal accruals, we are well placed to deliver strong 
growth ahead ."
