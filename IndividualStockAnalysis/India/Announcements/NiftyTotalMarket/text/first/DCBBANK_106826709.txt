Annexure I - Brief profile of Mr. Praveen Kutty  
Mr. Praveen Kutty is currently serving as the Managing Director & CEO of DCB Bank Limited since 
April 29, 2024. He brings with him over 35 years of banking experience. He has been associated with 
the Bank in various capacity from the year 2007  - Heading Retail Banking, Agri Banking & SME Banking. 
Before joining the Bank, he has worked with Citibank India and North America, where he successfully 
managed multiple consumer banking businesses including Credit Cards, Personal Loans, Home L oans, 
Branch Banking, Wealth Management and NRI Banking.   
Mr. Kutty holds a B. Com and an MBA degree.  
He does not hold directorship in any other company or body corporate.
