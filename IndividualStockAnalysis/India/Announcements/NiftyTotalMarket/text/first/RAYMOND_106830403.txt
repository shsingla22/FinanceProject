Raymond Limited continued with steady growth  momentum  in Q2 FY27 , with Total Income  of ₹ 683 Cr, 
reflecting a 21% increase over the previous year.  While, the quarterly EBITDA  stood at ₹ 103 Cr with an EBITDA 
margin  of 15.1%, an increase of 30% over the previous year .  
 
This strong performance was anchored by the Aerospace  & Defence  and Precision Technology & Auto 
Components  divisions. In the Aerospace division, we capitalized on the shift toward domestic production of 
sophisticated subsystems, securing a high -value pipeline for global Tier -1 partners. Similarly, the Precision 
Technology & Auto Components division saw healthy  growth in export of critical components for the hybrid 
sector , ensuring healthy operational momentum across the group . 
 
Commenting on the performance, Gautam Hari Singhania , Chairman & Managing Director , Raymond Limited 
said;  "Our strong double -digit growth  across Q2 and H1 FY27 highlights the compounding momentum of our 
pure -play engineering platform. Driven by exceptional execution in our Aerospace & Defence and Precision 
Technology segments, and with our Andhra Pradesh facility progress ing firmly on schedule, we are consistently 
converting our robust order book into sustainable, high -margin value for our shareholders ."

Q2 FY27 Segmental  Performance   
 
Aerospace  & Defence  Business:   
 
During  Q2 FY27 , the company  delivered  a stellar  financial  performance,  achieving  an all-time  high  revenue  of 
₹ 139 crore —a robust  72%  Y-o-Y growth  compared  to ₹ 81 crore  in Q2 FY26.  Operating  profitability  tracked  
this top-line expansion,  with  EBITDA  surging  72%  to ₹ 29 crore  (up from  ₹ 17 crore  in Q2 FY26)  while  
maintaining  strong  operational  efficiency  with  EBITDA  margins  at 21% . 
 
This record  performance  was fueled  by ramped -up production  for leading  global  OEMs  and key additions  to 
our product  portfolio.  With  supply  chain  headwinds  abating  and our expanded  operational  capacity  fully  
online,  we are uniquely  positioned  to seamlessly  execute  against  a rapidly  growing  multi -year  order  book.  
 
We continue  to capitalize  on growing  global  demand  by expanding  our footprint  into the high -precision  
aerospace  sector.  Backed  by our ongoing  ~₹510  crore  greenfield  capacity  expansion  in Andhra  Pradesh  and 
advanced  engineering  infrastructure , we remain  well-positioned  to drive  long -term  growth  in both  aerospace  
and defence  markets.  
 
Precision  Technology  & Auto  Components :  
Generated  ₹ 471 crore  in revenue  in Q2 FY27, representing  a 15%  Y-o-Y growth  over  ₹ 409 crore  in Q2 FY26.  
This performance  was anchored  by steady  domestic  market  traction  and sustained  export  deliveries  against  
long -term  OEM  schedules.  EBITDA  for the quarter  on a like to like basis  grew  by 39%  to ₹ 61 crore  (vs. ₹ 44 
crore  in Q2 FY26 excluding  one-time  gain  of ~₹ 13 crore ) with  an EBITDA  margin  of 13.0% , supported  by volume  
ramp -up and targeted  cost optimization  initiatives.   
 
We continue  to capitalize  on 'China  Plus  One'  tailwinds  by deepening  our global  footprint,  expanding  into 
adjacent  industrial  and defence  sectors,  and entering  the automotive  aftermarket.  Backed  by our ongoing  
~₹430  crore  greenfield  manufacturing  expansion  in Andhra  Pradesh  and brownfield  capacity  acceleration , 
we remain  well-positioned  to drive  long -term  business  momentum  both  domestically  and internationally.  
 
 
Raymond  Limited  continues  to remains  net-debt -free,  with  a net cash  surplus  of ₹ 77 crore  as of September  
’26, providing  the financial  flexibility  required  to fund  future  organic  and inorganic  growth  opportunities.  
 
About  Raymond  Limited  
With  the inception  in 1925,  Raymond  Limited  has been  a pioneer  and leader  in fabric  manufacturing  and then  forayed  in other  sectors  such  as engineering  
and Real  Estate.  With  the acquisition  of Maini  Precision  Products  Limited  (MPPL)  Raymond’s  engineering  business  has forayed  into the sunrise  sector s of 
Aerospace  & Defence  & EV components  and caters  to international  as well as domestic  markets.  After  demerging  its Lifestyle  Business  and Real  Estate  
verticals  into independent  listed  entities,  Raymond  Limited  now  has two core  verticals  within  the Engin eering  business  – Precision  Technology  & Auto  
Components  and Aerospace  & Defence.  It serves  a global  customer  base  of both  B2B and B2C clients  across  more  than  90 countries  in Asia-Pacific,  Africa,  
Latin  America,  Europe,  and North  America,  with  exports  contributing  over  65% to our total  business  due to our widespread  reach  and customer -centric  
approach.  Raymond’s  engineering  business  commands  a leadership  position  in manufacturing  files and hand  tools  and has a significant  presence  in national  
and international  markets.
