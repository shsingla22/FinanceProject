United Breweries Limited Expands Mainstream Beer Innovation  
Kingfisher Smooth to Arunachal Pradesh  
 
November 19, 2026 : United Breweries Limited (UBL), part of the HEINEKEN Company, has 
launched Kingfisher Smooth in Arunachal Pradesh, building on its successful rollout in 
Rajasthan, Karnataka and Maharashtra. The brand addresses growing demand for a smooth yet 
strong beer in India's mainstream strong beer segment.  
Designed for younger legal -age consumers, Kingfisher Smooth combines the strength 
consumers expect with a smoother taste profile. Brewed using imported hops and containing no 
added sugar, it delivers a refreshing and approachable beer while retaining the c haracter of a 
strong brew . It is available at ₹80 for a 500 ml can and ₹70 for a 330 ml pint across retail outlets.   
The strong beer segment continues to account for ~80% of the share of beer consumption  in 
India. As drinking occasions become more diverse, consumers are increasingly looking for beers 
that offer a balance of strength and drinkability. Developed through extensive consumer research 
and testing, Kingfisher Smooth responds to this shift, offer ing a differentiated choice within the 
mainstream strong beer category.  
Speaking on the expansion, Vikram Bahl, Chief Marketing Officer, U BL, part of the  
HEINEKEN Company , said, “Kingfisher has long enjoyed a strong connect with consumers in 
Arunachal Pradesh, and the strong response to Kingfisher Smooth since its launch reflects 
consumers’ willingness to try new offerings and their growing appetite for choice within the strong 
beer category. This expansion is driven by that demand,  and the enduring trust consumers place 
in the Kingfisher brand."  
While Kingfisher Strong continues to set the benchmark for a classic, full -bodied strong beer, 
Kingfisher Smooth expands the flagship brand's portfolio and reinforces UBL's commitment to 
innovation and consumer -centricity.
