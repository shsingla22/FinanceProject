31st December 2025 . 
 
The Government of India has implemented four new Labour Codes ("Codes"), including the Code on Wages, 2019,  
with effect from 21 st November 2025. The Company has carried out actuarial valuation for the quarter and nine  
months ended 31 st December 2025 considering uniform definition of "wages" as per the Codes on Wages and  
recorded additional obligation of Rs. 19.55 crore, which has been disclosed as an exceptional item in the results for  
the quarter and nine months ended 31 st December 2025.

pursuant to assignment of the voting rights on the 24.5% equity holding in ETPL to the promoter of ETPL. The

has been fair valued and income of Rs. 587.0 crore being excess of fair value of holding in ETPL over carrying  
value has been disclosed as an exceptional item in the results  for the quarter ended 30 th June 2025 .
