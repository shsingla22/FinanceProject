PRESS RELEASE  
Brigade Group Reports Revenue of ₹ 1250 Cr in Q2 FY27; Profit Before Tax grows 
47% YoY  
Bengaluru, October 29, 2026:  Brigade Group reported improved performance for Q2 FY27 
on launch momentum picking up with real estate segment sales for the quarter of ₹ 1,827 Crores 
across 1.3 Mn sqft volume.  Total Income for the quarter stood at ₹ 1,250 Crores in Q2 FY27, 
as against ₹ 1,430 Crores in Q2 FY26. The company reported a PBT of ₹ 288 Crores in Q2 
FY27, registering a growth of 47% over Q2 FY26.  
“Q2 FY27 has been a positive quarter for Brigade Group, with healthy momentum across our 
businesses. Residential continues to drive growth, underpinned by sustained demand and a 
strong project pipeline. Customers are increasingly prioritising quality, location, larger homes 
and better amenities, and we continue to see resilient demand across the mid and premium 
segments. The festive period has further strengthened customer engagement across our 
residential and retail businesses. With a robust pipeline, disciplined execution and a diversified 
portfolio spanning residential, commercial, retail, hospitality and warehousing, we are well 
positioned to maintain this momentum and deliver sustainable, long-term growth. 
 
Having recently commemorated Brigade’s 40th anniversary, we look ahead to our next chapter 
with the same commitment to building responsibly and creating enduring value for our 
customers, stakeholders and the communities in which we operate.” said Pavitra Shankar, 
Managing Director, Brigade Enterprises Limited.   
Q2 FY27 CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS  
 Revenue in Q2 FY27 of ₹ 1,250 Crores as against ₹ 1,430 Crores in Q2 FY26 
 EBITDA in Q2 FY27 of ₹ 478 Crores as against ₹ 375 Crores in Q2 FY26 
 PBT in Q2 FY27 of ₹ 288 Crores as against ₹ 196 Crores in Q2 FY26 
 PAT in Q2 FY27 of ₹ 219 Crores as against ₹ 170 Crores in Q2 FY26 
 Collections for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹ 1,633 Crores. 
Q2 FY27 SEGMENT HIGHLIGHTS  
 
REAL ESTATE 
Real estate revenue stood at ₹ 760 Crores. EBITDA stood at ₹ 175 Crores, an increase of 59% 
over Q2 FY26, with a margin of 23%. During the quarter, 2.4Mn sqft of Projects launched with 
a GDV of approximately ₹ 3000 Cr. 
 
 
 
 
  
 
LEASING  
During the quarter, revenue from leasing stood at ₹ 329 Crores. EBITDA stood at ₹ 242 Crores, 
an increase of 9% over Q2 FY26, with a margin of 74%. Occupancy stood at 90% as of 
September 2026. Completed two projects, viz. Brigade Square and Arcadia at Brigade 
Cornerstone Utopia, added 0.27 mn sqft of lease space.  
HOSPITALITY  
Hospitality revenue in Q2 FY27 stood at ₹ 161 Crores. EBITDA stood at ₹ 59 Crores, an 
increase of 41% over Q2 FY26, with a margin of 37%.  
H1 FY27 CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS  
 Revenue in H1 FY27 of ₹ 2,429 Crores as against ₹ 2,763 Crores in H1 FY26 
 EBITDA in H1 FY27 of ₹ 903 Crores as against ₹ 750 Crores in H1 FY26 
 PBT in H1 FY27 of ₹ 572 Crores as against ₹ 389 Crores in H1 FY26 
 PAT in H1 FY27 of ₹ 436 Crores as against ₹ 328 Crores in H1 FY26 
 Collections for H1 FY27 stood at ₹ 3,489 Crores.
