Appeals filed by the Distribution Licensees of Haryana and GRIDCO. Supreme Court upheld 
the Judgment dated 20th December 2019 of the Tribunal and confirmed GKEL’s position that 
linkage coal received is to be allocated on pro -rata basis among the beneficiaries of the 
Project.
