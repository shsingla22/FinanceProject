Abu Dhabi Investment Office partners with Motherson 
to develop large -scale automotive manufacturing 
facility in KEZAD  
Abu Dhabi, 06 May 2026 : The Abu Dhabi Investment Office (ADIO) announced its  support for  
Samvardhana  Motherson International Limited ’s (Motherson ) manufacturing hub in Abu Dhabi , which 
further strengthens the emirate’s position as a global hub for advanced manufacturing and automotive 
supply chains.  
The partnership reflects ADIO’s continued focus on enabling advanced manufacturing and 
strengthening high -value supply chains across priority industrial sectors, reinforcing Abu Dhabi’s 
competitiveness as a regional and global centre for manufacturing and export.  
The partnership supports the development of a large -scale integrated manufacturing facility to produce 
a range of automotive components for  Motherson’s global operations and third -party OEM customers.  
The project is expected to create more than 1, 000 jobs across technical, engineering, manufacturing 
and corporate functions , supporting Emiratisation, enabling greater participation of national talent and 
contributing to knowledge transfer and capability building in advanced industrial sectors.  
Located within Khalifa Economic Zones Abu Dhabi  (KEZAD) , the site is being developed in phases  on a 
single approximately 87,652 square metre  land plot, with c onstruction already underway.  Once fully 
operational, the facility  will serve as a regional manufacturing and export base, leveraging Abu Dhabi’s 
logistics infrastructure, including connectivity through AD Ports Group, to enable efficient access to 
regional and global markets  and strengthen integration across supply chains.  
The partnership reflects Abu Dhabi’s continued focus on enabling high -value industrial investment 
through a coordinated ecosystem that integrates infrastructure, connectivity and business support. It 
will contribute to expanding non -oil exports, strengthening local supply chains, advancing industrial 
capabilities and reinforcing Abu Dhabi’s position as a competitive global manufacturing and export hub.
