Dear Sir/Madam , 
We wish to inform you that Greytip Software Pvt. Ltd. (“GreytHR”) is currently an Associate company of the 
Company, holding 22.7% on a fully diluted and converted basis. 
  
The Board of Directors of the Company has agreed to enter into the revised shareholders’ agreement, whereby 
the new investor, Apax Digital Funds, advised by Apax Partners LLP, will acquire 52.1% stake in GreytHR, 
as result of a primary investment and secondary purchases from existing investors and the promoters of 
GreytHR, subject to agreed pre-closing conditions. 
Following the aforesaid transaction, the control of the management of GreytHR would be with the Apax 
Digital Funds, being the majority shareholder. It is clarified that the Company has neither participated in the 
primary funding or the secondary sale of shares and the number of shares held by the Company remains the 
same. However, post the above transaction, the Company’s shareholding will decrease to 18.71% on a fully 
diluted and converted basis. 
Consequently, following this dilution, GreytHR will no longer qualify as an Associate Company of the 
Company
