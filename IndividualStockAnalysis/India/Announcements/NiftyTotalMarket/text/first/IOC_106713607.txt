IndianOil Paradip Refinery -  
Transforming Odisha by Powering Progress and Fostering Inclusive Growth 
 
Strategic Investments at Paradip Refinery 
IndianOil has made a cumulative investment of approximately ₹43,359 crore at Paradip, 
encompassing its state-of-the- art Refinery Units (₹34,555 crore), Polypropylene Plant (₹3,150 
crore), and Mono Ethylene Glycol (MEG) Plant (₹5,654 crore)  - an investment that today 
sustains the livelihoods of over 12,500 people in and around Paradip.  
In addition, IndianOil is investing ₹13,805 crore in the ongoing Paraxylene -Purified 
Terephthalic Acid (PX-PTA) project at Paradip to strengthen the petrochemical value chain. 
Further, around ₹4,382 crore is planned to be invested in the Bhadrak Textile Pa rk Project 
through Joint venture with MCPI Private Limited to support Odisha’s textile sector and ₹ 1,064 
crore is planned to be invested in Hydroprocessed Esters and Fatty Acids (HEFA) 
based Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) project through a Joint venture with the company - M11 
Energy Transition Pvt. Ltd for producing SAF. 
These investments represent critical milestones in IndianOil’s strategic vision to develop 
Paradip into an integrated energy and petrochemical hub. The investments underscore the 
Corporation’s steadfast commitment to fostering innovation, enhancing self -reliance under the 
Government of India’s Aatmanirbhar Bharat initiative, and advancing the national energy 
transition towards a cleaner and more sustainable future. 
 
Operational Excellence 
IndianOil ’s Paradip Refinery continued its growth trajectory with several significant 
milestones. During FY 2025 –26, Paradip Refinery recorded the highest-ever crude processing 
of 16.35 Million Metric Tonnes and commissioned the Standby Sulphur Recovery Unit-III 
(SRU-III) and the New Hydrogen Generation Unit (NHGU). Building on this momentum, the 
1.2 Million Metric Tonnes Per annum (MMTPA) Purified Terephthalic Acid (PTA) Unit under 
the PX –PTA Project is nearing completion, marking a key milestone in Paradip Refinery’s 
petrochemical expansion. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Awards & Recognitions 
Paradip Refinery has earned several prestigious recognitions during FY 2025 –26, including 
Kalinga Safety Excellence Award, Kalinga Environment Excellence Award, National Safety 
Council of India (NSCI) Safety Award and Green Champions Award. 
 
Employment Generated 
 
The development and operation of Paradip Refinery directly and indirectly support the 
livelihoods of over 12 ,500 individuals, including skilled and unskilled workers, contractors, 
and supply chain partners. IndianOil also facilitates local skill development and capacity- 
building programs, creating meaningful economic opportunities in surrounding communities. 
 
Contribution to the Exchequer 
 
During FY (2025-26), Paradip Refinery contributed over ₹ 30,392 crore to the state and central 
exchequer through various duties, taxes and levies – underlining its role as a responsible 
corporate entity and economic enabler. 
 
Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Initiatives 
Staying true to its core value of Care, IndianOil has implemented impactful CSR projects in 
and around Paradip Refinery. Key initiatives include contribution towards Education, Skill 
Development and Youth Training, Health Care, Nutrition, Rural Development, Self-
Employment, Support to Orphanages, Support to Old Age Home  - ensuring inclusive growth 
and upliftment of underprivileged communities. IndianOil Paradip Refinery has spent ₹56.71 
crore towards impactful initiatives under Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) and Corporate 
Environment Responsibility (CER) from FY 2014-15 to FY 2025-26. 
 
Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Kausik Basu, Executive Director & Refinery Head of Paradip 
Refinery, said, "Paradip Refinery stands as a beacon of industrial excellence, innovation and 
inclusive growth. IndianOil’s continued investments at Paradip reaffirm its commitment to 
developing Paradip into a world-class integrated Refinery and Petrochemical hub that 
strengthens India’s energy security, accelerates sustainable development, and creates enduring 
value for the people of Odisha and the Nation.”  
 
 
 
 
 
IndianOil: Powering India’s Energy Future 
 
IndianOil is a Fortune 500, integrated energy major with presence in almost all the streams of 
oil, gas, petrochemicals, and alternative energy sources. IndianOil is one of the largest and most 
trusted corporates in the country, touching the lives of over a billion Indians. With nearly 
30,000 workforce, the company accounts for one third of the national refining capacity. With 
extensive distribution & marketing infrastructure, and advanced R&D facilities, IndianOil has 
been providing energy access to millions of people across the length and breadth of the country 
through its ever-expanding network of over 65,000 customer touch points. 
 
About Paradip Refinery 
Dedicated to the Nation on February 07, 2016, by Hon’ble Prime Minister of India Sh Narendra 
Modi Ji, Paradip Refinery is the largest refinery on the eastern coast of India and stands as the 
Energy Gateway to Eastern India. From the barren lands towards a sprawling 3300 acres of 
Technological marvel, the Refinery has transformed the skyline of Paradip, Odisha and stands 
proudly as a shining jewel in the crown of Indian Oil.   
Known for its operational excellence and environmental stewardship, Paradip Refinery is a 
benchmark in safety, reliability, and performance.
