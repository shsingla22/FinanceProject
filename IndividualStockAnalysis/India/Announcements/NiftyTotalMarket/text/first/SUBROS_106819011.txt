Sub:  Press Release: Intimation regarding the inauguration of Subros Advanced Technologies & 
Innovation Centre (SATIC) at Noida  
            Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we wish to inform as below: 
New Delhi, October 15, 2026: The Subros Advanced Technologies and Innovation Centre (SATIC) has been 
established at Sector 136, Noida as an extension of the Company's existing Research & Development 
Centre and represents a step towards strengthening its technological capabilities and supporting its long-
term growth and innovation objectives of the Company.  
The centre is intended to provide an enhanced environment for advanced technology development, 
engineering innovation, Digitalization and research activities in the field of automotive thermal 
management systems for PV, CV, Bus, Railways segments etc. It will support the Company's efforts to 
develop technologies aligned with the evolving requirements of the automotive industry, regulations 
including advancements in vehicle electrification, energy efficiency, thermal management and 
sustainable mobility. 
The initiative is aligned with the Company's strategic focus on strengthening its research and development 
capabilities, enhancing engineering competencies and preparing for emerging technology requirements 
in the automotive sector . 
 Company Brief: 
Subros Limited founded in 1985 as a joint venture with Suri family of India, DENSO Corporation & Suzuki Motor 
Corporation, Japan; is the leading manufacturer of thermal products for automotive applications in India, in 
technical collaboration with DENSO Corporation, Japan. The Company is the only integrated manufacturing unit in 
India for Auto Air Conditioning systems and Thermal Products. Subros Limited manufactures compressors, condensers, 
heat exchangers and all connecting elements required to complete AC loop and caters to all segments viz. 
Passenger Vehicles, Buses, Trucks, Refrigeration Transport and Railways.
