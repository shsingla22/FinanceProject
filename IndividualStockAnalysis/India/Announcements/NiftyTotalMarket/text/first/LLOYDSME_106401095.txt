 This disclosure is being made pursuant to Regulation 30(7) of the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“ Listing Regulations ”), 
and further to our earlier disclosure dated 12

approval of the Competition Commission of India (“ CCI”). We are pleased to inform you that the CCI, 
vide its letter dated 7th October, 2025, has approved the acquisition of 49.99% equity share capital of 
TPPL.
