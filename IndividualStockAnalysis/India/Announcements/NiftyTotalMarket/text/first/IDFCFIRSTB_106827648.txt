IDFC FIRST Bank Q2 FY27  Results: PAT up 196.5% YoY  at ₹ 1,045 Crore  
PAT crosses ₹  1,000 crore for the second consecutive quarter  
Mumbai, 24 October 2026:  IDFC FIRST Bank today announced its unaudited financial results for the quarter and half year 
ended 30 September 2026. The Bank reported Profit After Tax of ₹1,045 crore in Q2 FY27, up 196.5% year -on-year. For H1 
FY27, Profit After Tax stood at ₹2,120 crore, with Return on Assets at 0.99%.  
Management Commentary  
1. Strong deposit growth:  Total deposits grew 25.9% YoY and 11.8% QoQ to reach ₹3,48,583 crore.  
2. Healthy CASA:  Average CASA ratio remained strong at 48.4% in Q2 FY27. The slight decline from 50 .1% was 
primarily due to the large mobilisation of FCNR(B) deposits during the quarter.  
3. Loan growth:  Loans and advances, including credit substitutes and FCNR(B) -backed loans, grew 30.1% YoY to 
₹3,46,913 crore. Excluding loans secured by FCNR(B) deposits, loan growth was 22.2%, gross of IBPC.  
4. Operating leverage:  Opex  growth has remained lower than income growth by about 500 bps YoY for consecutive 
quarters.  
5. Improving cost -to-income ratio:  Incremental cost -to-income ratio was 55% in H1 FY27. The core cost -to-income  
ratio  (excluding trading gains) improved to 70.16% in Q2 FY27 from 70.73% in Q1 FY27.  
6. Strong growth in core operating profit:  Core operating profit, excluding trading gains, grew 38.5% YoY to ₹2,527 
crore in Q2 FY27.  
7. Stable asset quality:  GNPA stood at 1.32% and Net NPA at 0.40%. Credit cost, as a percentage of average assets, 
reduced to 1.01%.  
8. PAT grew 196.5% YoY to ₹1,045 crore  in Q2 -FY27 , resulting in an ROA of 0.93%. H1 FY27 ROA stood at 0.99%.  
9. FCNR(B) mobilisation:  During Q2 FY27, the Bank mobilised ₹34,390 crore (approximately US$3.6 billion) of 
FCNR(B) deposits despite having no branch, subsidiary or representative office outside India .  This demonstrates 
the Bank's digital capabilities and the trust it enjoys among customers.   
These deposits are largely of 3 -to-5-year tenor and provide strong structural liquidity for the coming years. The 
fully loaded cost of FCNR(B), including PSL benefits, is approximately 1% lower than the cost of raising domestic 
fixed deposits.  
FCNR mobilisation should benefit NII, PAT, EPS and RoE, although it is optically NIM -dilutive because the balance 
sheet expanded by ₹24, 885 crore as loans were extended by IBU branch against FCNR(B) deposits.  
10. Debut international rating:  S&P Global Ratings assigned the Bank an investment -grade long -term rating of 'BBB -'. 
 
Commenting on the results, Mr. V. Vaidyanathan, MD and CEO, said,  “Our PAT crossed ₹1,000 crore for the second quarter 
in a row, in both Q1 and Q2 FY27. We are also now internationally rated, with an investment -grade rating from S&P Global 
Ratings. Our Opex growth has been lower than income growth by about 500 basis poin ts for the second  consecutive quarter, 
demonstrating strong operating leverage. Consequently, our cost -to-income ratio continues to decline, and our ROA is now 
within touching distance of 1%.  
Further, raising over ₹34,000 crore of FCNR(B) deposits has been a significant positive for the Bank. It provides us with  
structural liquidity  of 3 to 5 years, which is particularly valuable in a rising interest -rate environment. The fully loaded cost 
of these deposits, including PSL benefits, is about 1% lower than the cost of raising comparable deposits in the domestic 
market.  Overall, Q2 FY27 was an excellent quarter, and we hope to sustain this momentum.”  
  
 
 
About the Bank  
1. Vision:  To build a world -class Bank in India, founded with principles of Ethical, Digital, and Social Good Banking.   
2. Scale:  IDFC FIRST Bank is one of India’s fast -growing private banks, building its UI, UX and technology  stack with  
fintech -grade capabilities.  As of September 30, 2026, the Bank serves over 40 million customers, with a customer 
business  at ₹ 6,88,328  crore  ($72.0 b) comprising customer deposits of ₹ 3,41,415  crore ($35.7b) and loans assets 
of ₹ 3,46,913  crore  ($36.3b). Customer deposits grew 26.9% YOY and loan  assets  30.1% YOY. We reach over 60,000 
cities, towns, and villages, operate  through a network of about 1,200  branches  and digital channels .
