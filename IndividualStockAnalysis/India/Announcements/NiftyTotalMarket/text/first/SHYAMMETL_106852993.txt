PRESS RELEASE 
 
Shyam Metalics Reaffirms Credit Ratings of ‘CRISIL AA+/Stable’ and ‘CRISIL A1+’; Bank 
Debt Ratings Enhanced to ₹4,500 Crore 
 
Kolkata, November 09, 2026  — Shyam Metalics and Energy Limited (SMEL), one of India’s leading 
integrated multi-metal producing companies, announced today that CRISIL Ratings Limited has reaffirmed 
the company’s long-term rating at ‘CRISIL AA+/Stable’  and short-term rating at ‘CRISIL A1+’. CRISIL 
has enhanced the rated bank debt amount to ₹4,500 Crore  (up from ₹3,600 Crore), and reaffirmed the rating 
for its ₹50 Crore Commercial Paper programme at ‘CRISIL A1+’.   
 
The reaffirmation underscores the Shyam Metalics Group’s established market position in the steel sector, 
strong financial risk profile, healthy operating performance, and high operational efficiency driven by 
deeply integrated operations.  
 
Key Highlights Driving the Rating Reaffirmation 
 
 Robust Operating Performance & Growth:  Operating profitability and top-line growth continue 
to strengthen, supported by higher sales volumes across core product segments. Profitability 
metrics are projected to expand further in the upcoming fiscal year.  
 
 Capacity Expansion & Diversification:  The group continues to scale its aggregate production 
capacities. Strategic expansion into flat steel, pig iron, stainless steel, and aluminium foils 
reinforces its product diversification and market reach.  
 
 Integrated Value Chain & Efficiency:  Operations cover the complete value chain from raw 
materials to value-added finished products. Operational efficiency is heavily supported by captive 
power generation, waste heat recovery plants, coal washeries, and dedicated railway sidings. 
Planned coal block operationalization will further strengthen long-term energy and raw material 
security.  
 
 Strong Balance Sheet & Conservative Leverage:  The group maintains a conservative financial 
structure with a strong net worth, significant liquidity surplus, and minimal debt reliance, leading 
to low gearing and strong interest coverage.  
 
 Disciplined Funding of Growth Capex:  Multi-year capital expenditure plans for capacity 
expansion will be funded predominantly through strong annual internal accruals and available cash 
reserves, ensuring debt metrics remain at safe levels.  
 
 Commitment to ESG & Sustainability:  SMEL continues to advance its environmental goals by 
increasing its renewable energy consumption, maintaining Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) 
operations for full water reuse, and adhering to international standards for emissions reporting.

About Shyam Metalics and Energy Limited 
 
Shyam Metalics is a leading and fastest-growing integrated metal-producing company based in India 
primarily in the steel industry in West Bengal, Odisha, Jharkhand, and Madhya Pradesh with a focus on 
Long & Flat Steel Products, Ferro Alloys, Aluminium, and Stainless Steel. The company got listed itself 
on the exchanges in 2021 and possesses a market capitalization of more than ₹ 35,000 Cr. Headquartered 
in Kolkata, West Bengal, the company is amongst the largest producers of ferro alloys in terms of installed 
capacity in India. 
The company has an aggregate installed metal capacity of 16.93 MTPA across the steel value chain and is 
supported by a 467 MW aggregated installed capacity of captive power plants. For more information, please 
visit our website at https://www.shyammetalics.com/ .
