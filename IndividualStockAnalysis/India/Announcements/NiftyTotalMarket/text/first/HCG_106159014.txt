the approval of the shareholders of the Company, as specified below:
2. The Company has approved, an amendment to the Scheme to provide,  an option to employees  
to surrender employee stock options held by such employees (Relevant Option Holder) that 
have vested prior to or will vest immediately following the Trade Sale (as defined in the grant 
letters for the aforesaid options), at the request of the Relevant Option Holder and provide them 
cash in accordance with the terms of the employee stock option letters/ agreement to be entered  
into between the Company and the Relevant Option Holder. Provided however that such 
surrender and payment shall be subject to, inter alia, the following conditions: (i) the Relevant  
Option Holder offers to surrender all (but not less than all) the employee stock options  held by 
the Relevant Option Holder on or before 10 March 2025; (ii) the Company shall accept the 
surrender of a maximum of 16,19,741 employee stock options from all the Relevant Option 
Holders in the aggregate; (iii) if employee stock options more than Relevant Options have been 
offered for surrender by the Relevant Option Holders, the number of Relevant Options accepted  
for surrender by the Company from each Relevant Option Holder shall be reduced 
proportionately such that the total number of employee stock options accepted for surrender 
does not exceed the Relevant Options; and (iv) payment of the amounts mentioned above shall 
be made only in respect of the options accepted for surrender.
