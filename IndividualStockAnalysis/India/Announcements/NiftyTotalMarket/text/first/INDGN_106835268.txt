“Extending our strong start to FY27, Q2 revenue grew 5.6% sequentially in INR, our fifth successive quarter 
above 5% . Growth was broad -based, driven by expansion across our top 5 and top 20 accounts and an increase 
in the number of $1 million+ clients to 59. We recorded significant deal wins in GenAI-enabled transformation 
and upstream engagements”, said Manish Gupta, Chairman and CEO, Indegene. “With a healthy pipeline, 
sustained deal momentum and favourable industry tailwinds, we expect growth and margin expansion to come 
together in the second ha lf.” 
 
“Despite the annual wage increase that takes effect in our second quarter, EBITDA margin remaine d stable at 
16.7%, a marked improvement on historical Q2 margin trends . Profit after tax grew 10% YoY to INR 1,123 
million”, said Suhas Prabhu, CFO, Indegene. “With the wage increase now fully reflected in our cost base, we 
expect EBITDA to grow faster than revenue  in the coming quarters. In addition , higher yields on our 
investment s and lower amorti zation should help  PAT margin expan d faster than  EBITDA margin. ”  
 
 About Indegene

global scale. Indegene combines  deep life sciences expertise with AI -embedded platforms and governed 
operating models to improve speed, consistency, and compliance across mission -critical operations. By
