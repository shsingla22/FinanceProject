From Track to Road, Across 7 Million Journeys:  
TVS Apache Launches ‘ Tu Race Laga’  
 
• TVS Apache now has 7 Million Global  Riders  
• The latest campaign film -  ‘Tu Race Laga’ propels TVS Apache’s next chapter, 
deepening its commitment to customer obsession, inspiring a new generation of 
riders, and shaping the future of motorcycling  
 
 
Mumbai, July 06, 2026:  TVS Motor Company, part of TVS V enu, a global leader in the 
two and three -wheeler segments, today marked a landmark milestone for its brand TVS 
Apache by celebrating 7 million customers worldwide and the launch of its new brand campaign film -  ‘Tu Race Laga. ’  
 Since its inception in 2005, every TVS Apache has been built on a 'Track to Road' philosophy , translating TVS Racing's championship- winning expertise into everyday 
performance. Backed by over four decades of racing heritage, TVS Apache combines race-derived technology, precision engineering, and rider -centric innovation to deliver 
an uncompromising performance experience on every ride.  
 Beyond its motorcycles, TVS Apache has cultivated one of the country's most passionate riding communities. With over 500,000 registered members, the community is united by a shared love for performance, adventure, and camaraderie. Through curated rides, track experiences, and immersive brand engagements, TVS Apache continues to create meaningful connections that go far beyond ownership.  
 Over the years, TVS Apache has consistently introduced segment -first innovations like -   
roto-petal disc brakes, f uel injection, d ual-channel ABS, m ultiple ride modes, TVS 
SmartXonnect ™, glide through technology,  factory customi sation through the Built -To-
Order platform,  keyless ride system,   and advanced rider-assist features like traction 
control and dynamic stability systems among others.  With a global community of over 7 
million customers across 90 countries, TVS Apache continues to stand as one of the world’s  fastest -growing performance motorcycle brands.  
 To commemorate and celebrate the milestone of 7 million customers globally, the brand 
has unveiled its new campaign film, “ Tu Race Laga’ , directed by acclaimed Indian 
filmmaker and National Award winner Mr. Ram Madhvani. Rooted in TVS Apache’s ‘Track to Road’ philosophy, the film captures the brand’s belief of  adrenaline, control, and excitement of the racetrack.

About TVS Motor Company
