Hyundai Motor India Limited introduces new variants and exciting 
updates for Hyundai VENUE , VERNA and Grand i10 NIOS  
Gurugram, 0 8 January 202 5:  Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) continues to elevate its 
product lineup by introducing new variants and feature upgrades for three of its popular 
models —VENUE , VERNA  and Grand i10 NIOS. Designed to meet the needs of aspirational 
buyers, these enhancements deliver advanced technology, superior comfort and a refined 
ownership experience.  
Introducing the  new variants  and upgrades, Mr. Tarun Garg, Whole -Time Director and 
Chief Operating Officer, Hyundai Motor India Limited, said, “The latest product updates on 
our 3 key models reflect Hyundai’s commitment to delivering cutting -edge technology, 
premium design and exceptional performance. We are confident  that  these enhancements 
will resonate with our customers and elevate their driving experience .” 
Hyundai V ENUE : To offer exceptional value and meet the growing demand  of customers, 
Hyundai is expanding the range of options with the introduction of new variant  and enhanced 
feature offerings at select variants  of Hyundai VENUE . These updates come loaded with 
innovative and aspirational features, ensuring every customer enjoys a premium and 
enhanced experience on every journey.  
The n ew variant , Hyundai VENUE Kappa 1.2 l MPi Petrol SX Executive MT  comes  with a host 
of features , including : 
• Smart Electric Sunroof  
• 20.32 cm (8”) Touchscreen Infotainment System  
• Wireless Android Auto & Apple Car Play 
• Smart Key with Push Button Start/Stop   
• Fully Automatic Temperature Control  (FATC) and many more  
Updates to the Hyundai VENUE existing variants include : 
• Hyundai VENUE Kappa 1.2 l MPi Petrol  S MT and S+ MT now include a rear camera and 
wireless charger enhancing driving experience with added functionality.  
• Hyundai VENUE Kappa 1.2 l MPi Petrol S(O) MT variant  now features a smart key with 
push  button  start /stop  for added convenience. In addition to smart key, t he Knight 
edition of the variant  comes with wireless  charger, further elevating its premium 
appeal.  
• Hyundai Venue Kappa 1.2 l MPi Petrol S(O)+ Adventure MT variant now includes a 
smart key with push button start/stop and Wireless charger for added convenience 
and functionality.

Hyundai V ERNA : To enhance the segment with aspirational features and thrilling 
performance, Hyundai  is introducing two new variants to the V ERNA  line-up - the 1.5 l Turbo 
GDi petrol S(O) DCT and the 1.5 l MPi petrol S IVT. These additions aim to meet the aspirations 
of premium sedan customers. Hyundai is also bringing a  feature update  to the existing 1.5 l 
MPi petrol S MT variant. These updates align with Hyundai’s commitment to innovation and 
customer satisfaction, raising the bar for the  VERNA ’s performance and style,  making  it a 
more  desirable  sedan.  
• Hyundai VERNA 1.5 l Turbo GDi petrol S(O)DCT  – The new variant comes loaded 
with premium and convenience features like : 
o Smart Electric Sunroof  
o 20.32 cm (8.0") Touchscreen Infotainment System  
o Wireless Android Auto & Apple CarPlay   
o R16 (D=405.6 mm) Black Alloys  
o Fully Automatic Temperature Control (FATC)  
o Smartphone Wireless Charger   
o Red Front Brake Calipers

• The existing Hyundai VERNA 1.5 l MPi petrol S MT variant  of the V ERNA  has been
