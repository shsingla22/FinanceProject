Route Mobile Limited announces Q3 FY26 Results and Leadership Update 
 
Q3FY26 Revenue from operations stands at Rs. 1,107.06 crore, PAT stands at Rs. 102.56 crore 
 
Mumbai, February 09, 2026: Route Mobile Limited (“Route Mobile”), a leading cloud communication 
platform service provider to enterprises, over-the-top (“OTT”) players, and mobile network operators has 
announced its financial results for the quarter ended on December 31, 2025, alongside a leadership 
transition. 
 
Highlights for Q3FY26 Vs Q3FY25 (Y-o-Y) Consolidated Financials 
 
 Revenue from operations stood at Rs. 1,107.06 crore as against Rs. 1,183.79 crore in Q3FY25  
 Profit Before Tax stands at Rs. 135.21 crore as against Rs. 106.95 crore in Q3FY25 
 Profit After Tax reported was at Rs 102.56 crore as against Rs 85.47 crore in Q3FY25 
 EPS stands at Rs. 15.51 (basic) and Rs. 15.51 (diluted) for Q3FY26 
 
Q3FY26 Vs Q2FY26 (Sequential) Consolidated Financials 
 
 Revenue from operations stood at Rs. 1,107.06 crore for Q3FY26 as compared to Rs. 1,119.42 
crore in Q2FY26 
 Profit Before Tax (PBT) stood at Rs. 135.21 crore for Q3FY26 as compared to Rs. 2 crore in 
Q2FY26. PBT margin stood at 12.2% in Q3FY26 
 Profit After Tax (PAT) reported at Rs. 102.56 crore for Q3FY26 as against a loss of Rs. 18.83 
crore in Q2FY26. PAT margin stood at 9.3% in Q3FY26 
 
The Company also announced that its Board of Directors has re-designated Mr. Rajdipkumar Gupta  as 
the Managing Director of the Company, effective February 9, 2026, upon his ceding the role of Chief 
Executive Officer. He shall continue to be considered a Whole-time Key Managerial Personnel (“KMP”) of 
the Company in accordance with the Companies Act, 2013 and the SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”). All other terms of his appointment, 
as earlier approved by the Board and the Shareholders, remain unchanged. 
 
In addition to his role as Managing Director, Mr. Gupta will step into the role of Advisor to Mr. Seckin 
Arikan, CEO of Proximus Global and Chairman of the Route Mobile Group Board. Mr. Gupta also retains 
his equity interest in Proximus Global via Clear Bridge Ventures LLP, underscoring the long-term 
partnership established through Route Mobile’s founders’ reinvestment during Proximus Global’s 
(formerly Proximus Opal) formation and integration. 
 
Further, the Company announced that its Board of Directors has elevated Mr. Tushar Agnihotri  to the 
role of Chief Executive Officer and KMP, effective February 9, 2026. With extensive experience in CPaaS 
and a strong background in sales and operations, Mr. Agnihotri will lead the Company’s overall operations 
and drive strategic initiatives aimed at accelerating growth and profitability. He will continue to work 
closely with Mr. Gupta to translate the Group’s strategy into tangible, sustainable outcomes for Route 
Mobile. This strengthened leadership structure brings added clarity and focus, positioning the Company 
for its next phase of sustainable and profitable growth. 
 
Mr. Seckin Arikan, Chairman of the Board, Route Mobile Limited and CEO, Proximus Global , 
commented, “Our performance this quarter reflects solid profitable growth. Despite a transitional period 
in parts of the portfolio, disciplined execution drove solid margin expansion thanks to strong seasonal 
contributions across regions. I would like to congratulate Mr. Agnihotri on his appointment as CEO and 
am confident his industry experience will drive our continued growth. I also extend sincere thanks to              
 
 Mr. Gupta for his leadership in shaping Route Mobile’s success and look forward to benefiting from his 
continued expertise as Managing Director and Advisor to the Board. As we advance our strategy, we 
remain committed to building a scalable, differentiated business supported by a solid pipeline and the 
strengths of Proximus Global.” 
 
Mr. Rajdipkumar Gupta, Managing Director, Route Mobile Limited , added, “Q3 25-26 saw slightly 
downward revenue as we managed a transitional period in certain segments of our business. More 
importantly, our gross margins have strengthened considerably, driven by new customer additions in 
higher-margin segments, demonstrating the effectiveness of our portfolio optimization strategy. I am 
grateful to our teams across geographies for their dedication and agility in executing this strategic shift 
while delivering consistent service quality to our customers.” 
 
About Route Mobile Limited (www.routemobile.com) (BSE: 543228; NSE: ROUTE) 
 
Established in 2004, Route Mobile Limited ("RML") is a cloud communications platform service provider 
catering to enterprises, over-the-top (OTT) players, and mobile network operators (MNO). RML's portfolio 
comprises solutions in messaging, voice, email, SMS filtering, analytics, and monetization. RML has a 
diverse enterprise client base across various industries, including social media companies, banks and 
financial institutions, e-commerce entities, and travel aggregators. RML is headquartered in Mumbai, India, 
with a global presence in Asia Pacific, the Middle East, Africa, Europe, and the Americas. Route Mobile is 
now a part of Proximus Group, a provider of digital services and communication solutions operating in 
Belgium and international markets.  
 
About Proximus Global  
 
Proximus Global, combining the strengths of Telesign, BICS, and Route Mobile, is transforming the future 
of communications and digital identity. Together, our solutions fuel innovation across the world’s largest 
companies and emerging brands. Our unrivaled global reach empowers businesses to create engaging 
experiences with built-in fraud protection across the entire customer lifecycle. Our comprehensive suite of 
solutions – from our super network for voice, messaging, and data, to 5G and IoT; and from verification and 
intelligence to CPaaS for personalized omnichannel engagement – enables businesses and communities to 
thrive. Reaching over 5 billion people worldwide, securing more than 180 billion transactions annually, 
and connecting 1,000+ destinations, we honor our commitment to connect, protect and engage everyone, 
everywhere.
