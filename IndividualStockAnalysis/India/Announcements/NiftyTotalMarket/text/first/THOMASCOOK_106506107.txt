PRESS RELEASE  
 
Sterling Strengthens  Its Rajasthan Footprint with the Launch of  
Sterling Amargarh Jodhpur  
• A Purpose -Fit Resort Anchoring Sterling’s Travel Circuit in Rajasthan  
Chennai, 23 Jan 2026:  Sterling Holiday Resorts has announced the launch of Sterling 
Amargarh Jodhpur , a grand destination resort that cements  the brand’s presence in 
Rajasthan and reinforces its credentials in large -format, experience -led stays. Set across 
sprawling landscaped greens on Pal Road, the resort captures the regal spirit of Marwar 
while oﬀering the scale, comfort and versatility that today’s leisure, wedding and corporate 
travellers increasingly seek.  
Located within the city oﬀering  easy connectivity to Jodhpur’s iconic forts, palaces and 
heritage precincts, Sterling Amargarh Jodhpur is positioned as a preferred base for family 
holidays, destination weddings and large corporate gatherings —bringing together heritage 
character with contemporary infrastructure.  
Commenting on the launch, Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director & CEO, Sterling Holiday 
Resorts , said,  “Rajasthan remains a cornerstone of Sterling’s destination strategy, and 
Jodhpur is one of its most compelling cultural anchors. With Sterling Amargarh Jodhpur, we 
are introducing a resort that brings scale and versatility into a heritage setting— making it  
equally relevant for leisure travellers, large celebrations and corporate events. This launch reﬂects our focus on building destination- led resorts that co mbine local character with the 
reliability of a national hospitality network.”   
Sterling Amargarh Jodhpur features thoughtfully designed elegant rooms and suites across Classic, Premier and Suite categories, blending heritage -inspired design with modern 
comforts. The resort is purpose -built for celebrations at scale, with two expansive indoor 
banquet halls —'Kesari ’ and ‘Marwar ’—supported by ‘Rajwada’ – The Lawn s, an expansive  
outdoor venue ideal for grand weddings, social celebrations and large -format corporate 
events.  
Dining forms a central part of the resort’s character. ‘Sangri ’ – The Flavours of Rajasthan, the 
all-day live -kitchen restaurant, showcases authentic regional specialities alongside popular 
global favourites, while an al fresco courtyard dining setting creates a distinctive atmosphere 
for intimate dinners and celebrato ry evenings. Guests can also enjoy a range of leisure and 
activity oﬀerings, including an outdoor swimming pool, cultural evenings and family -friendly 
spaces, making the resort well suited to multi -generational stays.  
 
 
  The resort’s location allows guests to explore Jodhpur’s cultural landmarks with ease, 
including Mehrangarh Fort, Jaswant Thada, Umaid Bhawan Palace, Mandore Gardens, 
Kailana Lake and the Clock Tower markets, while returning to the comfort and calm of a landscaped destination resort setting.  
“Amargarh has long been a part of Jodhpur’s hospitality landscape, and this association 
marks an important new chapter for the resort. Partnering with Sterling allows us to 
strengthen the property with wider national distribution, consistent service standards and 
deeper experience in destination -led stays and celebrations, while preserving the authentic 
Rajasthani character that deﬁnes Amargarh. We see this as a meaningful step in positioning 
the resort for the next phase of growth” — Anil Garg,  Managing Di rector Neha Enterprise  
Sterling Amargarh Jodhpur  is also strategically positioned within Sterling’s broader Rajasthan 
destination network, enabling ﬂexible multi -city travel options that appeal to extended 
celebrations and destination wedding groups. The resort is located approximately 250 –280 
km (5 –6 hours by road) from Sterling in Udaipur, about 190 km (4 –4.5 hours) from Sterling 
Pushkar, and roughly 285 km (5 –6 hours) from Sterling Rudraksh Jaisalmer, allowing families 
and guests to combine heritage cities, desert forts and lakesides into well- paced wedding 
travel itineraries. This connectivity allows  pre-wedding events, post -wedding leisure stays 
and cultural exploration across Rajasthan to be woven into a seamless journey, backed by 
the consistency and service assurance of a trusted national hospitality brand.  
With large -format event infrastructure, resort -style accommodation and easy access from 
key guest catchments, Sterling Amargarh Jodhpur  strengthens Sterling’s positioning as a 
preferred partner for extended destination weddings and multi -day celebrations, rather than 
standalone event venues.
