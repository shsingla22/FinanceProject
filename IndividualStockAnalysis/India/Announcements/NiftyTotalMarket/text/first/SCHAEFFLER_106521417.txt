Schaeffler showcases future -ready , comprehensive  aftermarket 
solutions at ACMA Automechanika New Delhi  
 
New Delhi  | February  05, 2026 | Schaeffler  India Limited , a leading motion 
technology company, announces its participation at ACMA Automechanika  2026  
in New Delhi . Schaeffler India will showcase its future -ready aftermarket portfolio  
that supports the complete lifecycle of vehicles ranging from two -wheelers, 
passenger cars  with internal combustion engines (ICE) , commercial vehicles 
(CVs) Vs, tractors, to hybrids and electric vehicles ( EVs).  
A key highlight of this participation is the introduction of Schaeffler’s dedicated 
range of two -wheeler spare parts for the Indian aftermarket, featuring essential 
high -demand components such as bearings, fuel injectors, clutch plates, engine 
oil, and bra ke pads. This expanded offering further strengthens  Schaeffler India’s 
unified aftermarket presence while reinforcing its commitment to delivering 
reliable and accessible motion technology solutions across all vehicle segments.  
The Vehicle Life time Solutions division reinforces Schaeffler India’s commitment 
to simplifying sourcing, and - technical support for its  partners , including  
independent workshops and retailers. It will expand the Motion Technology 
company’s reach in the aftermarket network and position it as the right partner 
for workshops handling mixed vehicle fleets.  
Schaeffler India will also showcase EV -ready repair and maintenance solutions, 
the REPXPERT training ecosystem  that enabl es workshops to service new -age 
mobility with a focus on safety, reliability, and long -term vehicle uptime. Through 
structured training and hands -on technical support, Schaeffler empowers 
workshops to enhance repair quality, improve productivity, and build c ustomer 
trust, positioning repair and maintenance as a key enabler of sustainable mobility.   
Harsha Kadam, Managing Director  and CEO , Schaeffler India  Limited said, 
“Vehicle lifetime services such as repair and maintenance are non -negotiable 
enablers of sustainable mobility. As a Motion Technology leader, we are acutely 
aware of the commitment it takes to offer a universal suite of products and 
services to enable the ecosystem made of distribution networks and workshops to 
carry that promise forward. Our showcase at the ACMA Automechanika 2026  in 
New Delhi  is a comprehensive display of how we are b ridging today’s vehicle 
aftermarket needs  with tomorrow’s technologies . “
