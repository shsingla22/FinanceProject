Zydus receives EIR for SEZ II manufacturing facility, Ahmedabad 
 
 
Ahmedabad, India, November 5, 2025 
 
We wish to inform that the USFDA conducted an inspection at the group’s manufacturing plant 
located SEZ – II, Ahmedabad , from 11th to 14th August 2025. The inspection was a Pre-Approval 
Inspection (PAI). The USFDA has concluded this inspection as closed.  
 
The inspection concluded with NIL observations. The EIR has classified the facility as No Action 
Indicated (NAI).  
 
This may kindly be considered as a disclosure pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.   
 
Please bring the aforesaid news to the notice of the members of the exchange and the investors at large.
