Escorts Kubota Announces Price Revision Across  Kubota,  
Farmtrac  and Powertrac Tractors Amid Rising Input Costs  
Revised prices across the product range will  come into effect in  November 2026, after the festive 
season  
Faridabad, October 30, 2026:  Escorts Kubota Limited (EKL), one of India's leading agri -machinery and 
construction equipment manufacturers, today announced a price increase across its Kubota, Farmtrac 
and Powertrac tractor ranges. The price revision wil l come into effect in November  2026 after the festive 
season.  
The price revision is driven by the continued rise in input costs, including raw materials, energy, logistics and manufacturing expenses , mainly  impacted by inflationary trends and ongoing geopolitical 
uncertainties. 
Over the past several months, EKL has undertaken a range of cost optimization and operational efficiency 
initiatives to mitigate the impact of rising costs. The company has also absorbed a substantial portion of 
these increases. However, with input costs c ontinuing to remain elevated, the price revision has become 
necessary to partially mitigate higher manufacturing and operating expenses. 
The price revision wil l come into  effect in November 2026 after the festive season and will vary across 
models, variants and geographies within the Kubota, Farmtrac  and Powertrac portfolios. Revised prices 
will be available through authorised dealerships.  
Manish Sharma, President –  Agri Business, Escorts Kubota Limited, said,  “We have made every effort 
to minimize the impact of rising input costs on our customers. This measured revision enables us to sustain 
our commitment to quality, innovation and customer value. We remain focused on delivering reliable, 
technology -led soluti ons through our Farmtrac, Powertrac and Kubota brands to support the productivity 
and profitability of Indian farmers.”  
EKL has continued to expand its tractor offerings through launches including the Farmtrac PROMAXX 
series, Powertrac Shaurya series and expanded Digitrac range, alongside Kubota's NeoStar and MU series. These additions, supported by continued dealer network  expansion, reflect the company's focus on 
providing farmers with a wider choice of reliable, application- specific tractors suited to diverse agricultural 
needs.
