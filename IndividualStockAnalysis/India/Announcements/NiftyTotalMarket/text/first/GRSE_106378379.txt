ANNEXURE -I 
Brief Profile  
M/s Guha Nandi & Co, Chartered Accountants, Kolkata  
 
M/s Guha Nandi & Co., Chartered Accountants, is a long -standing audit firm of  Eastern 
India. The firm was established in the year 1954 and completed 71 years in the field 
of profession of chartered accountancy.  
 
At present, the firm is having 11 (eleven) FCA partners with several years of experience of whom 5(five) are DISA qualif ied. The firm has 2 (two) qualified chartered 
accountants and 3 (three) qualified cost & management accountants in its employment. With adequate staff strength, the firm is capable of handling audit assignments including internal audit of large- scale enter prises both in public and private sectors.  
 
The firm has conducted statutory audit of around 76 Public Sector Undertakings in 
various sectors namely mining, power, aluminum, manufacturing, steel, oil & gas, chemicals, construction, engineering, telecommunications, insurance, shipbuilding etc. and also performed as central statutory auditors of various Public Sector Banks for 23 years. The firm has rich experience in conducting internal audit of PSUs and Banks in 
the last several years.
