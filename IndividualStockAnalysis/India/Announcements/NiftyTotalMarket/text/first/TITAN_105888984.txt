Mr. Saumen Bhaumik will take over the position of Managing Director of CaratLane.  The 
current Managing Director Mr. Avnish Anan d, has desired to step down from the position of 
Managing Director of Cara tLane due to personal  reasons. 
Saumen Bhaumik joined  Titan in the early 90's and has been the CEO of Titan's  EyeCare

and bringing it into sustained profitability.  Prior to taking the leadership role in EyeCare , Saumen

being the regional business head for the North region.
