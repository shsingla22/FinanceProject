HCLTech scales  South Africa  operations with  new  headquarters  featuring an AI 
Center of Excellence  
 Doubles down on upskilling of local talent with graduation  ceremony of fresh batch of learners 
from HCLTech’s skills development program  
 
JOHANNESBURG, South Africa, Oct. 7, 2026  – HCLTech, a leading global technology 
company, is scaling its South Africa operations with the launch of its new  regional headquarters  
in Johannesburg . Located in the premium business district of Sandton,  the modern facility will 
further strengthen  HCLTech’s technology  innovation and delivery  to enterprises  in South Africa 
and the region.   
Notably, the new facility  will house an AI Center of Excellence where enterprises  can 
experience the business -value of HCLTech’s  full-stack AI portfolio  of AI -led Service 
Transformation, Industry AI Solutions, AI Foundry, AI Engineering, AI Factory and AI Advisory , 
while  discover ing industry -specific use cases and accelerat ing their AI transformation journeys . 
The facility w ill also  be a training hub focused on developing next -generation technology talent 
in South Africa and strengthening the country's future digital workforce.  
Sandeep Saxena, Chief Growth Officer, Growth Markets 2 (India, Middle East and Africa, 
France, Iberia and Italy), HCLTech, said, “South Africa has been  a strategic market for HCLTech 
for over  15 years,  and we are proud to be  a trusted partner to enterprises in the region.  With our 
differentiated portfolio that includes full -stack AI solutions, we are deepening our commitment 
to South Africa with this new facility . In line with our corporate philosophy of supercharging 
progress for our people and local communities, we continue to have a sharp focus on enabling 
the local talent ecosystem through upskilling programs and university linkages.”  
HCLTech will host a graduation ceremony for learners completing its annual skills development 
programme  later today . Over the last five years (FY21 -FY25), the company has supported nearly 
500 students and learners, with nearly 50 participants joining HCLTech in full -time roles. 
Through partnerships with the University of Johannesburg, the University of Pretoria and North -
West University, HCLTech continues to strengthen South Africa's digital talent pipeline and help 
bridge the gap between education and employment.  
Over the past five years, HCLTech has also invested in ICT and digital literacy programmes that 
have positively impacted more than 20,000 beneficiaries across South Africa. Through 
partnerships with educational institutions, community organizations and industry leaders, 
HCLTech continues to expand access to techno logy, digital skills and employment 
opportunities, particularly for underserved communities, helping build a stronger and more 
inclusive digital future.  
To commemorate the milestone , HCLTech ’s leadership team  joined  the open ing ceremony at 
the Johannesburg Stock Exchange, reinforcing its long -term commitment to South Africa and its 
growing technology ecosystem.
