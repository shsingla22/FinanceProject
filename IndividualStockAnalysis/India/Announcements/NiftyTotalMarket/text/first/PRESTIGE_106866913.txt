 Prestige Estates Launches Prestige Avon on Thanisandra Main Road, North Bengaluru  
 
Bengaluru, November  20, 2026:  Prestige Estates Projects Limited, one of India’s leading real 
estate developers, has announced the launch of Prestige Avon , a premium residential 
development located on Thanisandra Main Road in North Bengaluru.  
Spread across approximately 3 acres , Prestige Avon comprises 260 thoughtfully designed 
apartments  with an overall saleable area of approximately 0.4 million sq. ft.  The project has 
an estimated gross development value (GDV) of approximately ₹5,500 million . 
Offering a selection of 2 and 3 BHK residences , Prestige Avon also features home offices in 
select layouts, catering to the evolving needs of urban homebuyers who increasingly seek 
flexible spaces that can adapt to both work and personal requirements. Apartment sizes range 
from approximately 1,306 sq. ft. to 2,160 sq. ft.  
Strategically located on Thanisandra Main Road, the project benefits from its proximity to 
Bengaluru’s key connectivity corridors, including the Outer Ring Road (ORR) and 
Kempegowda  International Airport. The location also provides excellent access to established 
and emerging retail, commercial and social infrastructure.  
Commenting on the launch, Mr Irfan Razack, Chairman and Managing Director, Prestige 
Group, said “Prestige Avon is designed for homebuyers looking for a well -connected address 
combined with thoughtfully planned homes and contemporary lifestyle amenities. 
Thanisandra  has emerged as one of North Bengaluru’s key residential corridors, benefiting 
from strong connectivity and the continued development of the region. With its carefully 
planned residences, including select layouts with dedicated home -office spaces, Prestige  
Avon responds to the changing expectations of today’s urban homebuyer.”
