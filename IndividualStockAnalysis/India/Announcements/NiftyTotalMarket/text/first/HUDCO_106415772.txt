Sub.:  Signing of Memorandum of Understanding between Housing and Urban Development 
Corporation Limited (HUDCO) and Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority (JNPA) 
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- 
Sir/Madam, 
Housing and Urban Development Corporation Limited (HUDCO) has signed a Memorandum of 
Understanding (MoU) with Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority (JNPA) on 24th October, 2025. The 
purpose of this non-binding MoU is to establish and initiate cooperation for exploring collaboration 
in existing and upcoming infrastructure development and financing & refinancing of port 
infrastructure projects at JN port for an estimated amount of Rs.5,000 Crore.  
 
The MoU was signed by Shri Sanjay Kulshrestha, CMD, HUDCO and Shri Unmesh Sharad Wagh, 
Chairman, JNPA.
