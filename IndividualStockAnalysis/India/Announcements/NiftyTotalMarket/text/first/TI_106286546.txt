NEWS RELEASE 
 
Tilaknagar Industries’ Mansion House Tops Indian Brandy Charts 
Again; Ranks Global Number Two by Volume in 2024 
 
Courrier Napoleon Emerges Third-Fastest Growing Brandy, Worldwide  
  
Mumbai, July 01, 2025:  Tilaknagar Industries Ltd. (TI) (BSE: 507205 | NSE: TI), a 
leading Indian-Made Foreign Liquor (IMFL) manufacturer, has announced that its flagship 
brand, Mansion House Brandy , has  retained its position as India’s largest-selling brandy 
and the second largest-selling brandy in the world, for the second year in a row. The 
rankings have been published in ‘The Millionaires’ Club’ report released by international 
trade journal Drinks International  in June 2025 . 
With 7.8 million cases sold in 2024, Mansion House Brandy  now ranks as the 29th largest 
brand globally, reaffirming its legacy and international appeal.   
Adding to the momentum, Courrier Napoleon Brandy, another millionaire brand from TI, 
has made a powerful mark by becoming the third fastest-growing brandy in the world. 
With 2.0 million cases sold in 2024 and an impressive 25 per cent year-on-year growth, 
Courrier Napoleon Brandy also ranks 13th among the world’s fastest-growing spirits 
brands, across categories.  
Drinks International’s  ‘The Millionaires’ Club’ report is an annual listing that features 
alcoholic beverage brands from around the world that register sales volumes in excess of 
one-million in nine-litre cases. 
Mr Amit Dahanukar, Chairman and Managing Director, Tilaknagar Industries  said, 
“It’s incredibly rewarding to see Mansion House Brandy continue to dominate the brandy 
landscape both in India and globally. This success is a reflection of the trust our consumers 
place in us and the consistent effort we’ve made to elevate Indian spirits. Courrier 
Napoleon’s exceptional growth is equally exciting and speaks to the rising global appetite 
for quality, homegrown brands.” 
As the undisputed leader in India’s brandy space, TI is driving innovation and expanding 
the category’s footprint. The company has introduced contemporary and premium 
offerings such as Mansion House Reserve , Mansion House Chambers , and flavoured brandy 
variants—a first-of-its-kind in the segment. In the luxury space, the company recently 
launched Monarch Legacy Edition , the only 100 per cent pure grape brandy made from 
Indian and French grape spirits. 
Backed by a robust distribution network and a growing footprint in climatically aligned 
regions such as the East and North-East India, TI is reshaping perceptions around brandy 
and taking Indian spirits to the world stage. 
These global rankings are a testament to TI’s vision, deep consumer trust and commitment 
to excellence across generations. 
 
About Tilaknagar Industries: 
Tilaknagar Industries (TI) is one of India’s leading alcoholic beverage companies, with a 
rich legacy spanning over 90 years. Founded in 1933 by Shri Mahadev L. Dahanukar as 
Maharashtra Sugar Mills, TI has, over the years, transformed in to a major player in the 
India Made Foreign Liquor (IMFL) industry having a manufacturing footprint of 19 units 
across 12 states. TI’s brand portfolio straddles multiple categories, featuring two 
‘Millionaire’ brandy brands—Mansion House and Courrier Napoleon—along with a strong 
presence in whisky, rum, and gin through Mansion House Whisky, Madiraa Rum and Blue 
Lagoon Gin. Recently, TI has expanded into the luxury segment with Monarch Legacy 
Edition Brandy.
