Change in Senior Management  
1. Pursuant to the applicable provisions of the SEBI LODR , read with the extant RBI Circular, 
and in continuation of the letter bearing Ref. No.: BBL/471/2024 -25 dated January 26, 2025, 
please be informed that the Board of Directors (the ‘Board’) of Bandhan Bank Limited (‘the 
Bank’), at its meeting held today, i. e., Wednesday, January 29, 2025, has considered and 
approved the appointment of Mr. Sundeep Bhan  as the Chief Risk Officer (‘CRO’) of the 
Bank , in the interim, with effect from  January 2 9, 2025 , for a period of six months or till the 
date the new CRO joins  the Bank, whichever is earlier . The Bank is in the process of 
identifying its new CRO , the details of  which will be shared  in due course.  
 
Brief Profile of Mr. Sundeep Bhan  
Mr. Bhan is a Management Graduate from the University of Kolkata. He has over 29+ years 
of experience in Centralized Banking Operations, Corporate Banking, Credit Operations, 
Credit Appraisals, Loan syndications, Profit Centre Operations, Retail Banking, Client 
Servicing, Operational Risk & Fraud M anagement. Presently associated with the Bank as 
EVP and Head Operational & Fraud Risk, he is responsible for Operational Risk 
Management & Operational Resilience as well as for monitoring and reporting of Frauds.
