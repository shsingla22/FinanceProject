Subject : Outcome of the Board Meeting held on June  09, 2026  
 
Pursuant to  the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), this is to inform you that the Board of 
Directors of Orkla India Limited (the “Company”) at its meeting held today  i.e., June 09, 2026 , have 
inter-alia, considered and approved the following  matters of business :  
 
(i) Ratification of the pre-IPO “Employee Stock Option Plan 2025” and “Management Stock 
Option Plan 2025” of Orkla India Limited  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 and other applicable provisions of the SEBI Listing Regulations, we would 
like to inform you that the Shareholders of the Company have approved the “Employee Stock Option 
Plan 2025” and “Management Stock Option Plan 2025”  at its meeting held on May 16, 2025 . Further, 
pursuant to Regulation 12 of SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits  and Sweat Equity ) Regulations, 
2021, no company shall make -any fresh grant of employee stock options which involves allotment or  
transfer of shares to its employees under any schemes/ plans formulated prior to its Initial Public  
Offering ("IPO") unless such pre-IPO Scheme/ Plan is ratified by its shareholders subsequent to the  
IPO. Accordingly, in terms of Regulation 12(1) of the  SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits  and Sweat 
Equity ) Regulations, 20 21, the Company seeks approval from its shareholders at the ensuing Annual 
General Meetin g to ratify  the “Employee Stock Option Plan 2025” and “Management Stock Option 
Plan 2025”   
 
The Board of Directors at their meeting held today i.e., June 09, 2026 , recommended to shareholders 
for ratification of “Employee Stock Option Plan 2025” and “Management Stock Option Plan 2025”  at 
the ensuing Annual General Meeting.  
 
(ii) The 30th Annual General Meeting of the Company will be held on Wednesday , August 19, 
2026 , through Video Conferencing (VC) / Other Audio -Visual Means (OA VM)  
 
The Notice of the Annual General Meetin g (AGM) and Annual Report  of the Company for the FY 2025 -
26 shall be submitted to the Stock Exchanges in due course of time simultaneously with the dispatch of 
the Notice of AGM and Annual Report to the shareholders of the Company.  
 
The Board Meeting commenced today at 9:30 A.M. (IST) and concluded at 5:00 P.M. (IST).
