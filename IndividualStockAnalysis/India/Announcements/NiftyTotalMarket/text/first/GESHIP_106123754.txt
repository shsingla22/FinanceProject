PRESS RELEASE  
  
G E Shipping contracts to sell its 2011 built Supramax Dry Bulk Carrier  
“Jag Rishi”  
  
The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited (G E Shipping) has contracted to sell its 2011 built 
Supramax Dry Bulk Carrier, Jag Rishi of about 56,719 dwt on January 24, 2025 to an unaffiliated 
third party. The vessel will be delivered to the new buyer by Q4 FY25.  
   
Including Jag Rishi, company’s current fleet stands at 39 vessels, comprising 26 tankers (5 crude carriers, 17 product tankers, 4 LPG carrier) and 13 dry bulk carriers (2 Capesize, 8 Kamsarmax, 3 
Supramax) aggregating 3.10 M n dwt.
