Namita Thapar of Emcure appointed as Non-Executive Independent 
Director  at Delhivery 
Feb 07, 2025 : Delhivery, India's largest fully-integrated logistics service provider, has 
announced the  appointment  of Namita Thapar,  Executive  Director at Emcure Pharmaceuticals 
Limited as a Non-Executive Independent Director to the Board of the Company with effect from 
February 17, 2025. 
Namita is the Executive Director of Emcure Pharmaceuticals , a global pharmaceutica l company 
headquartere d in Pune. It has a presence in 70+ countries and an employee strength of over 
10,000. She is the CEO of their India Business which is ~8000 cr. in annual revenue. Prior to 
joining the family business, Namita worked for 6 years at Guidant Corporation , USA, in their 
business finance team. Namita is an MBA graduate from the Fuqua School of Business at Duke 
Universit y and a Chartere d Accountant from ICAI. She is one of the Sharks in the popular TV 
show “Shark Tank India”. 
Commenting on the appointment, Sahil Barua, Managing Director and CEO said,  "We’re excited 
to bring Namita onboard.  Her extensive experience in  leading a high-growth business  and 
innovating in a sector like healthcare brings a valuable perspective to our industry. "
