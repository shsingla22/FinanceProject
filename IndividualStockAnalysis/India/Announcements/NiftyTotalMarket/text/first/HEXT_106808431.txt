Hexaware as a Best Firm for FDE Talent to Work f or 
Certiﬁed by Analytics India Magazine  
Earns top scores in AIM’s employee -led assessment of the forward -deployed engineering 
experience  
October 6, 2026 : Hexaware Technologies [NSE: HEXT], an AI -ﬁrst digital and IT services 
company, has  been certiﬁed by Analytics India Magazine (AIM) as a Best Firm for Forward -
Deployed Engineering (FDE) talent to work for.  The certiﬁcation follows feedback from 129 
Hexaware AI architects drawn from talent developed through the company ’s AI Academy and 
Architecture Fellowship program.  
AIM’s assessment uses an anonymous employee survey covering 33 statements, rated on a 
ﬁve-point scale and normalized to a 10 -point score. It measures ﬁve areas: customer and 
solution alignment; FDE delivery excellence and ownership; growth and learning; inclusion and 
belonging; and psychological safety. Hexaware recorded an overall score of 8.42/10.  
Among its strongest individual measures were outcome -based success measurement at 8.80, 
production readiness at 8.74, and responsible AI voice and failure recovery at 8.69 each.  
“Forward -deployed engineering is where technology meets the reality of a customer’s 
business,” said Vinod Chandran, Chief Operating Oﬃcer, Hexaware . “Our goal is to give these 
teams the context, autonomy and support to solve the right problem, get solutions into 
production and stay accountable for the outcome. This certiﬁcation is especially meaningful 
because it comes directly from the people doing  that work.”  
Tony Mohanty, Executive Vice President & Global Head – Talent Management, Hexaware , said, 
“Strong FDE talent cannot be built through a one -time training program. It takes deep technical 
learning, exposure to real problems, mentoring and repeated opportunities to apply what 
people learn. Our AI Academy and Architecture Fellowship are bui lt around that idea, and this 
feedback gives us a valuable measure of how that investment is translating into the employee 
experience.”  
The certiﬁcation gives Hexaware an external benchmark for the environment it is creating for 
AI architects working in customer -facing, production -oriented roles.
