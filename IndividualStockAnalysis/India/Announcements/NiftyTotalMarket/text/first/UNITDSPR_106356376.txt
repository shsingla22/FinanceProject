The 26th Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the members of the Company was held today i.e., Friday, 
29th August 2025 at 3:30 pm IST through Video Conferencing  (VC) / Other Audio -Visual Means  
(OAVM) . 
 
Mr. V K Viswanathan , Independent Director and Chair person of the Board chaired the meeting.  
 
The f ollowing directors were present  at the AGM :  
 
1. Mr. Praveen Someshwar , Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer (MD & CEO)  and 
Chairperson of Risk Management Committee – participated  from Bengaluru . 
2. Mr. Pradeep Jain, Executive Director & Chief Financial Officer (ED & CFO)  – participated from 
Bengaluru.  
3. Dr. Indu Bhushan , Independent Director and Chairperson of Corporate Social Responsibility and 
Environmental, Social & Governance  Committee  – participated from Kochi . 
4. Mr. Mukesh Hari Butani , Independent Director – participated from Zurich, Switzerland . 
5. Ms. Amrita Gangotra , Independent Director  and Chairperson of Nomination and Remuneration 
Committee and Stakeholders Relationship and General Committee – participated from Bengaluru . 
 
Ms. Preeti Arora and Mr. Mark Sandys, Non -Executive Director s of the Company expressed their 
inability to participate in the meeting owing to their prior commitment s. 
 
Mr. Mital Sanghvi, Company Secretary participated from Bengaluru.  Representatives from Price 
Waterhouse & Co. Chartered Accountants LLP  (Statutory Auditors ), Mr. Sudhir V. Hulyalkar, 
Secretarial Auditor for financial year 2024 -25 and Scrutiniser for the AGM, and representative  from 
M/s. Makarand M. Joshi & Co., proposed Secretarial Auditor were present  via VC . 
 
With requisite quorum being present, the Chai rperson  declared the meeting to be in order and welcomed 
the members.  
 
Chairperson  mentioned  that  since  the AGM  was  being  held  electronically,  physical  attendance  of 
members was dispensed with and the requirement of appointment of proxy was not applicable. It was 
also informed  that the statutory  registers,  as required  under  the Companies  Act, 2013  were  made 
available  electronically  for inspection  by members  during  the AGM  at Central  Depository  Services 
(India) Limited (CDSL) e-voting platform. 
Since the Notice of the Meeting had already been circulated in advance to the members, it was taken as 
read.  Chairperson  announced  that, as reports  of Statutory  Auditor  and Secretarial  Auditor  did not 
contain any qualifications, the same were also taken as read. 
Chairperson  delivered  his speech and  briefed  on general  instructions  regarding  participation  of members 
in the AGM. Chairperson then invited members who had registered in advance to speak at the AGM. 
Mr. V K Viswanathan, Independent Director and Chairperson of the Board and Mr. Pradeep Jain, ED 
& CFO responded to the queries received from members. 
Chairperson then announced that, in compliance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 read 
with the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the Company had 
provided remote e-voting facility to its members. The remote e-voting commenced at 09:00 hrs IST on 
25th August 2025 and ended at 17:00 hrs IST on 28th August 2025. Mr. Sudhir V Hulyalkar, Company 
Secretary in Practice (CP-6137), was appointed as Scrutinizer for scrutinizing the e-voting process.
