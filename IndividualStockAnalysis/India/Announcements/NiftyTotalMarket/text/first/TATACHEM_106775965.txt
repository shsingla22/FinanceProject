Sub: Intimation  under  Regulation  30 of SEBI (Listing  Obligations  and Disclosure  
Requirements) Regulations, 2015  for  Update on Notice issued to Tata Chemicals Magadi Limited  
 
In continuation to our disclosure dated July 29, 2026, under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (‘SEBI Listing Regulations’) on the Notice from the 
Ministry of Mining, Blue Economy and Maritime Affairs, Kenya dated July 28, 2026 to Tata Chemicals 
Magadi Limited (TCML), the Kenyan subsidiary of Tata Chemicals Limited, the Company is providing  a 
status update on the said matter as mentioned hereunder.  
 
The Cabinet Secretary, Ministry of Mining, Blue Economy and Maritime Affairs,  met the executives  of 
TCML on September 8, 2026,  and had a fruitful engagement regarding the matters  raised by the  
Ministry.  
 
To facilitate a comprehensive and timely resolution of the matter s, it was  agreed to establish a high-level 
technical committee . The Committee will be led by the Principal Secretary for Mining  on behalf of the 
Ministry and the Chief Executive Officer of TCML  on its behalf . 
 
The Committee will undertake a detailed technical review of all matters, prepare and submit a report to 
the office of the Cabinet Secretary  for consideration and further direction.  
 
This is also to establish a sustainable framework that promotes responsible mining, value addition, 
community development and mutually beneficial partnerships.  
 
We request  you to kindly  take the above  information  on record.
