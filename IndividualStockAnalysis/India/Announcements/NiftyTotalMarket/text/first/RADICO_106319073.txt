audited figures in respect of the full financial year and published year to date reviewed 
figures for the nine months period ended December 31, 2024.

Employee Stock Option ('ESOP') by the eligible employees, as per the ESOP Scheme 
2006.

equity share of the Company with a face value of 2/- at an exercise price of 2,081 .48/-
per share.
