Meesho’s Mega Blockbuster Sale Opens with ~75% YoY Growth in Day 1 Orders  
 
● Day 1 orders grew ~75% YoY  as festive demand scaled across Bharat  
● Orders crossed ~75,000 a minute at peak  within 15 minutes of the sale going live  
● Transacting customers jumped ~60% YoY  
● Seller participation grew sharply, with transacting sellers up ~63% YoY  and Tier 4+ transacting 
sellers doubling YoY  
● AI-powered discovery shaped demand, powering ~85% of Day 1 orders  
● Branded shopping gained momentum across Bharat, with Meesho  Mall orders growing over 
~200% YoY  
● Creator -led discovery deepened, with Content Commerce orders up ~3.7X YoY  and ~81% of 
creators from non -metro India  
Bengaluru, October 10, 2026:  The Meesho Mega Blockbuster Sale 2026 opened to strong demand across 
India, with Day 1 orders growing ~75% YoY . As India’s largest e -commerce platform by Placed Orders , 
Meesho saw orders peak at over ~75,000 per minute within just 15 minutes of the sale going live , 
underscoring the scale at which festive demand came onto the platform. Transacting customers grew 
~60% YoY , with strong demand across Fashion, Home & Kitchen, Beauty & Personal Care, Kids and 
Electronic Accessories.  
How Aspiration and AI -led Discovery are Reshaping Festive Shopping  
Meesho Mall saw orders grow over ~200% YoY on Day 1 , as branded shopping reached deeper into the 
country. Tier 3+ Mall customers nearly tripled YoY , while over 1 in 3 shoppers bought a Mall product for 
the first time  across 1,000+ brands . 
The shift was most visible in everyday aspiration -led categories, with over 8 in 10 first -time Mall buyers 
shopping Beauty & Personal Care , including face washes, perfumes, soaps and skincare, followed by 
Health & Wellness and Footwear . The trend points to branded consumption becoming both more 
affordable and more accessible beyond the metros . 
Creator -led commerce saw similarly strong momentum, with Content Commerce orders growing ~3.7X 
YoY. ~90% of active Content Commerce creators are nano creators and 81% come from non -metro India , 
making discovery more local and relatable.  
Personalised recommendations played a central role in festive discovery, powering ~85% of orders.

How India’s Festive Basket is Evolving  
Fashion remained a key part of festive shopping, with sarees, kids’ clothing sets and kurta sets among the 
most -shopped products on Day 1. But the broader pattern was more revealing: festive preparation is 
increasingly extending beyond the outfit to the entire occasion, from grooming and accessorising to 
decorating, cleaning, hosting and puja preparation.  
That showed up in sharp growth across puja articles and wall décor , alongside highly specific festive 
needs: torans and wall hangings grew 8X, earlobe support patches for heavy earrings nearly 7X, and 
rosemary for hair care over 6X . Shoppers also stocked up on perfumes, face washes, rangoli, decorative 
lights and candles, incense and puja essentials, kitchen cleaners and even metal cleaners for polishing 
puja utensils , capturing the many small preparations that go into getting festive -ready.  
Sellers See Strong Festive Acceleration  
The festive surge translated into strong momentum for sellers on Day 1. Seller participation grew sharply, 
with transacting sellers up ~63% YoY , while transacting sellers from Tier 4+ markets doubled YoY .  Sellers 
receiving their first -ever Meesho order on Day 1 grew ~43% YoY . The growth reflects the breadth of 
festive demand on the platform, creating opportunities for more sellers across categories and geographies 
to participate in the festive season.  
As the Mega Blockbuster Sale continues, Meesho remains focused on making festive shopping accessible 
across Bharat while enabling sellers, brands and creators to grow.
