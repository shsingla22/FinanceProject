CarTrade  Tech Launches AI -Powered Transaction Tool: “SuperDost”  
 
Mumbai, India | March 04 , 2026 – CarTrade Tech Limited (NSE: CARTRADE | BSE: 543333), India’s leading 
digital marketplace and parent company of CarWale, BikeWale, OLX India, and Shriram Automall, today 
announced the launch of SuperDost : an AI -powered transaction tool designed to power instant  transactions 
between high -intent buyers and sellers  for Automobiles and Used Products . 
 
SuperDost is a live AI A gent which  operationalizes the ‘Matchmaker’ strategy by leveraging the combined 
scale and proprietary data of over 85 million unique monthly visitors  across OLX India and CarWale to drive 
real-time  and Instant , high -intent transactions  between buyers and sellers  for Automobiles and Used 
Products . 
 
Over the last three years, the Company has delivered consistent growth:  
Revenue CAGR: 32%  |EBITDA CAGR: 112%  |PAT CAGR: 83%  |EPS CAGR: 92%  
This performance has been driven by strong execution across all its businesses, Consumer Group, 
Remarketing Auctions, and OLX India supported by disciplined capital allocation and operational focus.  
 
CarTrade Tech serves over 150 million annual users  across each of its platforms, ~95%  of which is organic . 
This scale reflects the strength of the foundation we have built: Trusted Brands, Loyal Customers, 
Differentiated Data , and our IP and proprietary Technology . These long -term structural advantages position 
the Company uniquely to unlock the massive opportunities that AI can create.  
 
AI is a significant enabler for digital marketplace businesses. The Company is actively building and integrating 
AI-led capabilities across its platforms to develop world -class products, improve customer experience, and 
enhance efficiency and productivity.  
 
Structural tailwinds remain strong and favorable, with GST rationalization and rising digital adoption across 
new vehicle and used product markets expanding the Company’s total addressable opportunity.  
 
SuperDost  is designed for instant, intelligent matchmaking at scale , bringing  together the Company’s 
Consumers, Dealers and Partners  across Automobiles and Used Products within a unified AI -powered 
interface.  It Real -Time  connects the most relevant, high -intent buyers and sellers across the platform s. 
 
  
 
 
 
All you need to do is take a photo and SuperDost  will help you sell or buy instantly.  
 
 
SuperDost  will: 
• Increase conversion and efficiency  
• Reduce time -to-transaction  across buying and selling journeys  
• Deepen engagement  and long -term platform loyalty with all our customers  
• Unlock scalable monetization  opportunities across the ecosystem  
 
In the first phase,  SuperDost will be rolled out for Used Car Dealers giving them an AI -powered transaction 
tool which will help them Instantly  sell their vehicles or buy vehicles for their stocks.

Akshay Shankar , Chief Product Officer , CarTrade Tech Limited, said:  “At CarTrade  Tech, we operate at a
