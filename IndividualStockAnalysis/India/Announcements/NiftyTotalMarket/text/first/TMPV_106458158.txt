Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd. registered total sales of 59,199 units in 
November 2025 
  
Mumbai, December 1, 2025: Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd. sales in the domestic & international 
market for November 2025 stood at 59,199 units, compared to 47,117  units during November 2024.
