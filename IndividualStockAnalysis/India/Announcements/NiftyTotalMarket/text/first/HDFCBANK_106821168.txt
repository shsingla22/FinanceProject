Annexure I – Brief profile  of Mrs. Lily Vadera  
 
Mrs. Lily Vadera, has over 34 years of experience in Central banking. She is a M.A in International 
Relations. She retired as an Executive Director from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) in October 2020. 
As the Executive Director of the  RBI, she was in -charge of the Department of Regulation (DoR) where 
she dealt with the regulatory framework for various entities in financial sector, covering all categories 
of banks and non -banking finance companies.  
During her tenure in RBI, Mrs. Vadera represented the RBI in various international forums such as the 
Policy Development Group of Basel Committee on Banking Supervision of BIS, Switzerland, 
Financial Stability Board and the Sub -groups of the G -20 on SME financing and Green financing. Mrs. 
Vade ra also represented RBI on a committee set up by the Government on SME financing.  
Mrs. Vadera was instrumental in putting in place a framework for a regulatory sandbox to provide an 
enabling environment for fintech players to foster innovation in financial  services and played a 
significant role in the amalgamation of banks in stress. She represented RBI and played an important 
role as a member of the Insolvency Law Committee set up by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA).
