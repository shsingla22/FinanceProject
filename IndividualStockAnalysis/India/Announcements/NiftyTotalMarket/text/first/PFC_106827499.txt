PFC Consulting Limited (a wholly owned subsidiary of Power Finance Corporation Limited) has been 
nominated as the 'Bid Process Coordinator' (BPC) for selection of developer through tariff based 
competitive bidding for the Independent Transmission projects (ITPs) by Ministry of power, Gol.  
 
As per the provisions of tariff based competitive bidding Guidelines issued by the Ministry of Power,  
the BPC has to prepare the project profile and initiate the process of land acquisition and forest clearance, 
if required. For these activities, One Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) is required to be incorporated which 
will take up various preparatory activit ies of the transmission project e.g. survey and preparation of 
report, initialization of the process of acquisition of land and also initiate the process of seeking forest 
clearance, if required.  
 
The SPV has to be transferred to the Successful Bidder selected through International Competitive 
Bidding process under Tariff Based Competitive Bidding Guidelines.  
 
The Rajasthan State Government, in exercise of powers conferred by Sub para 3.3 of para 3 of the 
Guidelines for Tariff Based Competitive Bidding (TBCB) for Transmission Service circulated by 
Ministry of Power (MoP ), Government of India, under Section 63 of Electricity Act, 2003, published in 
the Gazette of India Notification / Resolution dated 10th August, 2021, appointed M/s PFC Consulting 
Limited as Bid Process Coordinator (BPC) for the development of above  mentioned Intra -State 
Transmission Projects vide its notification No. F.12(12) Energy/2021/00865/7791575 dated May 26, 
2026 as published in Rajasthan Extraordinary Gazette on 02.06.2026.
