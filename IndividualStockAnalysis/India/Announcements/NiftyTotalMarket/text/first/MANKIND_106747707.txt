Trust Deed(s) as amended and the applicable provisions of SEBI (Issue and Listing of Non-
Convertible Securities) Regulations, 2021 read with Listing Regulations, as amended, post

Debenture Trustee, viz. Catalyst Trusteeship Limited, over tangible and intangible assets of the 
Company/its subsidiary(ies). Consequent to creation of the revised security cover as stated 
above, the pledge on 28,280 equity shares of Bharat Serums and Vaccines Limited (“BSVL”) 
(constituting 38.68% of the paid-up equity share capital of BSVL), which was subsisting in 
favour of the Debenture Trustee on behalf of the NCD holders, has been released on August 
17, 2026. In compliance with the applicable provisions of the Listing Regulations and the terms 
of the Debenture Trust Deed(s) the revised security cover is 1.26 times the amount of 
outstanding NCDs as on date. No shares of BSV are now under pledge for NCDs.
