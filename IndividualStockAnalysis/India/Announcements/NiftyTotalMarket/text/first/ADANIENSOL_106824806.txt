AESL Delivers Record Q2 Performance, Sustains Strong Growth Momentum  
 
Q2FY27  PAT more than doubles  to Rs 1,364  crores  on YoY basis  
EBITDA rose 67% YoY to a record Rs 3,548 crore  in Q2FY27  
 
EBITDA crossed a record high of  Rs 6,726  crore half yearly mark, ended up 62% higher YoY  
 
 
 
Editor’s Synopsis  
Financial  Highlights:   
• In Q2FY27, EBITDA rose 67% YoY to a record Rs 3,548 crore , supported by growth in transmission, smart 
metering, a significant contribution from energy solutions platform segment and steady performance in the 
distribution business . 
• Total income grew by strong 51% YoY to an all -time high of Rs 10,239  crore  in the quarter , supported by 
stronger operating performance across the portfolio, the energy solutions platform , the ongoing smart meter 
rollout, and higher Service Concession Arrangement (SCA) income reflecting increased capex execution  
• PAT increased  by 145% YoY to Rs 1,364 crore  in Q2FY27 from Rs 557 crore  in Q2FY26  
• Round -the-clock power supply contract of 2,500 MW signed with MSEDC L  
 
Capex Execution Performance:  
• Transmission capex in H1 FY27 increased by 1.7x to Rs 5,542 crore, as against Rs 3,343 crore in H1 FY26 
• AESL completed 1 4.82 million cumulative smart meter installations, demonstrating strong operational 
efficiency and setting new execution benchmarks in the industry  
Energy Solutions Platform:  
• Energy Solutions Platform continues its strong performance with EBITDA of Rs 810 crore in Q2  
Growth Opportunity (Locke d-in Growth):  
• During Q2 FY27, AESL secured two  transmission  projects,  Vizag Power Transmission Limited and Satara  
Power  Transmission Limited , the company’s aggregate transmission under construction pipeline stands at ~ 
Rs 87,000 crore . The company’s smart meter order book remains at 24 .6 million  meters with a revenue 
potential of Rs 29,519 crore  
• The near -term tendering pipeline in the transmission sector remains robust  at ~Rs 1 .4 lakh crore s. Whereas 
the nation wide market opportunity for smart metering continues  at 86 million meters  
Capital Management:  
• Raised Rs 3,500 crore via QIP , secured primary and secondary equity investments from marquee investors  
 
Ahmedabad, 22 October  2026: Adani Energy Solutions Limited (“AESL”), part of the globally diversified Adani portfolio  
and the largest private transmission , distribution,  smart metering and energy solutions platform company in India, today 
announced its financial and operational performance for the quarter  ended September  30, 202 6. 
“AESL closed the first half of the fiscal year with strong financial and operational momentum, laying a robust foundation 
for future growth.  We significantly expanded our growth pipeline by winning the strategic Vizag  and Satara  transmission 
assets, elevating our active order book to ~Rs 8 7,000 crore.  
Operationally, our Mumbai distribution business grew by  ~11% in MUs sold , while our smart metering platform scaled its 
installation base to ~15 million meters.  A significant  2.5 GW RTC power supply contract with MSE DCL reinforced AESL’s 
core competencies in multi -source energy aggregation and power supply optimization  for its customers . 
Backed by robust quarter -on-quarter performance across all business segments  and strict financial prudence, we are 
well-positioned to convert this massive project pipeline into long -term stakeholder value. ” said Kandarp Patel, CEO, 
Adani Energy Solutions

Segment -wise Progress  and Outlook:  
Transmission:  
• Robust project pipeline of 15 projects worth ~ Rs 87,000 crore  to be executed  
• The near -term transmission tendering opportunity at ~Rs 1.4 lakh crore remains solid   
• The company reported strong operational parameters during the quarter, with an average system 
availability of over 99.8%. Robust line availability resulted in an incentive income of Rs 61 crore in H1FY27,  
reflecting the best O&M practices  
Distribution business (AEML Mumbai and MUL):  
• The distribution business recorded a steady business performance . Adani Electricity Mumbai Ltd.’s 
(AEML) Regulated Asset Base ( RAB) stands at Rs 10,456  crores  (Equity of Rs 5,564  crores and Debt of 
Rs 4,892  crores)  as of  H1FY27, recording a growth of 11% YoY  
• 99.99% supply reliability maintained — AEML continues to lead India's distribution sector on operational 
benchmarks  
• Total units sold in the Mumbai circle increased by 11% from 2,650 MUs in Q2FY26 vs 2,930 MUs in 
Q2FY27  
• MPSEZ (Mundra) Utility Ltd.’s (MUL)  units sold grew 26% YoY to 460  MUs vs 364 MUs last year, driven 
by strong industrial and commercial demand  
• The distribution loss in AEML network was at 4.29% in H1 FY27, driven by improved operational efficiency  
Smart Meters:  
• Installed 14.8 million  smart meters cumulatively as of H 1 FY27 
• The under -implementation pipeline stands at 24.6 million smart meters, comprising ten projects with a 
revenue potential of over Rs 29,519 crore  
Energy Solutions Platform:  
• Entered into a Power Purchase Agreement ("PPA") with Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution 
Company Limited ("MSEDCL") for the supply of 2,500 MW round -the-clock ("RTC") power for a period 
of 25 years  
• supply tie -up ~4.6 GW across solar & wind, ~3. 5 GWh of BESS which will cater robust energy portfolio 
across C&I, data centers and utilities, delivering integrated renewable and round -the-clock digital 
energy solutions  
ESG  and Other Updates : 
• AESL achieved the highest ESG score among global utilities in S&P Global’s latest MSA review, 
improving its score to 90/100 from 81, well above the industry average of 41  
• AESL achieved Intertek -verified Zero Waste to Landfill status, diverting over 6 lakh kg of waste and

• AEML retained its position as India’s top -ranked distribution utility, with A+ rating in REC’s Consumer 
Service Rating of DISCOMs (CSRD) for excellence in customer service  
 
 
About Adani Energy Solutions Limited (AESL):  
AESL, part of the Adani portfolio, is a multidimensional organization with presence in various facets of the 
energy domain, namely power transmission, distribution, smart metering, and cooling solutions. AESL is the 
country’s largest private transmission c ompany, with a presence across 1 6 states of India and a cumulative 
transmission network of 29,802  ckm and 1,43,425 MVA transformation capacity. In its distribution business, 
AESL serves more than 1 3 million consumers in metropolitan Mumbai and the industrial hub of Mundra SEZ. 
AESL is ramping up its smart metering business and is on course to become India’s leading smart metering 
integrator with an order book of over 2 4.6 million meters. AESL, with its integrated offering through the 
expansion of its distribution network through parallel licenses and competitive and tailored retail solutions, 
including a significant share of green power, is revolutionizing the way energy i s delivered to the end 
consumer. AESL is a catalyst for transforming the energy landscape in the most reliable, affordable, and 
sustainable way.
