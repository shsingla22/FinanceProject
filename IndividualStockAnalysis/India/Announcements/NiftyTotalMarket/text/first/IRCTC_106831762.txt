Sub: Board Comments on fines levied by the Exchanges for non -compliance s of certain  Regulation s of 
SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 . 
 
Sir / Madam,  
 
This is with reference to NSE’s and BSE’s e- mails dated August 25, 2026 and September 29, 2026 imposing 
fines of ₹10,23,060/ - each on the Company for non- compliances with the provisions of Regulations 17(1), 
18(1),19(1)/( 2), 20(2)/(2A), & 21(2) of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for the Quarter ended June 30, 2026 
and ₹29,500/ - each for non -compliance of the Regulation 44(5) of Listing Regulations for the FY 2025- 26.  
 
As advised by the Stock Exchanges, the matter of the aforesaid non- compliances was placed before the Board of 
Directors of the Company at its meeting held on October 28, 2026. The Board deliberated upon the matter and 
noted that “the non- compliances relating to Regulations 17(1), 18(1), 19(1)/( 2), 20(2)/(2A), and 21(2) of the SEBI 
LODR Regulations were due to reasons beyond the control of the Company. The Company had taken proactive 
steps by informing and requesting its administrative ministry, the Ministry of Railways, well in advance regarding 
the potential non- compliances. The Board expressed its concern regarding the notices received from BSE & NSE 
and advised that the administrative ministry be requested on a regular basis to expedite the appointment of the 
requisite number of Independent Directors, including a Woman Independent Director, on the Board of IRCTC to 
ensure compliance with the SEBI LODR Regulations, the Companies Act, 2013, and other applicable laws.  
   
Further, d ue to the non- receipt of comments from the Comptroller and Auditor General of India (C&AG) within the 
requisite timeframe, the Company was unable to convene its 27th Annual General Meeting (AGM) by August 31, 
2026 in accordance with the timeline prescribed under Regulation 44 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. The 
Board also noted that upon receipt of C&AG comments on September 04, 2026, the Company had dispatched 
the Notice along with full Annual Report on September 07, 2026, ensuring compliance with Regulation 36 of the 
SEBI LODR Regulations, Sections 136 and 143 of the Companies Act, 2013, and the Secretarial Standard on General Meetings (SS -2) issued by ICSI  and successfully conducted its 27
th AGM on September 29, 2026 . The 
Board accordingly authorised the Director (Finance) & Company Secretary to submit a formal request to NSE and BSE for waiver of the fines imposed.”  
 
This is for your kind information and record please.
