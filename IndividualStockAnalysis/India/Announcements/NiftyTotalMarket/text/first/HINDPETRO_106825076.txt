                  Mumbai, 22 October,  2026 
Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) today announced its financial results for the 
quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026. The results reflect resilient and robust 
performance by the Refineries and the Marketing divisions of the company in the midst of the 
ongoing West Asia crisis.  
 
 
Financial Performance 
 
 Revenue from Operations 
o Q2 FY27: ₹  1,39,140  crore (₹ 1,10,323 crore in Q2 FY26) 
o H1 FY27: ₹  2,84,267   crore (₹ 2,30,458 crore in H1 FY26) 
 Gross Refining Margin before Export Cess (GRM) 
o Q2 FY27: US$ 32.15  per barrel (US$ 8.80 per barrel in Q2 FY26) 
o H1 FY27: US$ 27.89 per barrel (US$ 5.95 per barrel in H1 FY26) 
 Standalone Profit/Loss After Tax 
o Q2 FY27: Net Profit ₹ 3,483 crore (PAT ₹ 3,830 crore in Q2 FY26) 
o H1 FY27: Net Loss ₹ (8,044) crore (PAT ₹ 8,201 crore in H1 FY26) 
 Consolidated Profit/Loss After Tax 
o Q2 FY27: Net Profit ₹ 3,625 crore (PAT ₹ 3,859 crore in Q2 FY26) 
o H1 FY27: Net Loss ₹ (8,639) crore (PAT ₹ 7,970 crore in H1 FY26)  
Operational Performance 
 
Refining Performance: 
 Refineries recorded crude throughput of 6.20 MMT during 2QFY27, operating at 100% of 
the capacity. 
o Visakh Refinery registered crude throughput of 3.60 MMT, operating at 95% of its 
capacity 
o Mumbai Refinery registered crude throughput of 2.60 MMT, operating at 109 % of its 
capacity

Strategic Investments and Infrastructure Development 
 Capex in 2QFY27: ₹ 2,085 crore; cumulative for 1HFY27: ₹ 3,819 crore - focused on

refuelling operations.

New Initiatives 
 Samriddhi 2.0 – Accruals under Project Samridhi 2.0 (the EBITDA improvement program of 
the company) during 1HFY27 is ₹ 327 Crore (US$ 0.18/bbl.) 
 As of 30th September 2026, HP Green R&D Centre (HPGRDC) has filed a total number of 800 
patents, out of which 339 patents have been granted. 
 HPCL commissioned India’s first Refinery Blue Hydrogen unit in Visakh Refinery during the 
quarter, which is based on patented Higas Technology and HP Novel Amine. 
 HPCL launched HP Navya, the 10 kg Composite LPG Cylinder, and partnered with Swiggy 
Instamart to enter the Q-Commerce ecosystem; released 0.36 lakh new connections till Sep’26. 
 Strengthened HPCL’s global presence in Lubricants business with sales in Turkey and Sudan, 
thus, expanding its footprint to 35 countries worldwide.
