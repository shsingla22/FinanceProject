Airtel Announces Revised Mobile Tariffs

to be upwards of  INR 300, to  enable a financially healthy business model  for Telcos in India.  We 
believe that this level of ARPU will enable  the substantial investments required in network 
technology and spectrum  and offer a modest return on capital .  
In this light , we welcome the announcements in the industry to repair tariffs. Airtel will also revise 
its mobile tariffs as indicated below, from July 3rd, 2024. We have ensured that there is a very 
modest  price increase (less than 70p per day) on  the entry level plans, in order to eliminate any 
burden on budget challenged consumers.
