Quarter  ended September  30, 2026 – Media  Press  Release  
 
Nykaa delivers strong Q2  FY2027  performance;  consolidated Net revenue up 2 8% YoY , PAT up 207%  YoY 
 
FSN E-Commerce  Ventures  Limited  (referred  to as “Nykaa”  or the “Company”),  today  announced  its financial  results  for the 
quarter  ended September  30, 202 6 
 
Q2 FY2027 FINANCIAL  HIGHLIGHTS  SUMMARY : 
 
• Consolidated  GMV  grew  30% YoY to Rs. 6,151 Cr 
• Revenue  from  Operations grew  28% YoY to Rs. 3,006 Cr  
• Gross  Profit  grew  31% YoY to Rs. 1, 382 Cr 
• EBITDA  grew  65% YoY to Rs. 263 Cr 
• EBITDA  margins  expanded  to 8.7% in Q2 FY2027 vs 6.8% in Q2 FY2026  
• Profit Before Tax  (PBT) was Rs. 149 Cr, up 16 8% YoY  
• Net Profit (PAT) was Rs. 101 Cr, up 2 07% YoY  
 
BUSINESS SUMMARY : 
Beauty saw s trong momentum on the back of accelerated customer acquisition  and premiumization of existing customers , 
and 330+ brands were launched in the past year.  House of Nykaa continues to outperform supported by strong innovation , 
and Nykaa is  excited about welcoming  Simply  Nam , a specialized, premium, professional grade colour cosmetics brand founded

BEAUTY EB2B: SUPERSTORE BY NYKAA : 
 
• Superstore by Nykaa continued to scale , with GMV reaching  Rs. 328 Cr, supported by an expanding 
retailer network and wider  geographic reach. Its registered retailer base  grew 27% YoY to over 550k+, 
making it one of India's fastest -growing beauty retail distribution platforms . This growth was achieved 
while keeping the number of business development executives (BDEs) constant over the past year, 
reflecting gains in productivity.  During the quarter, Superstore further strengthened its portfolio across 
new -age skincare, makeup and wellness brands , with multiple brand launches  across Recode , Lacto 
Calamine , Clay Co.,  BLA BLI BLU, Renee & Love Earth , among others  
 
• This quarter, Superstore also launched ‘Meri Dukan’, a personalised  shopping experience designed to 
help retailers make more informed purchase decisions. Tailored to each retailer’s purchase history, 
geography and store type, it highlights products due for replenishment, identifies demand in local 
markets, surfaces daily deals and price drops, and recommends relevant products and brands.

• Nykaa acquired a 82% majority stake in Simply Nam , India’s pioneering celebrity makeup artist brand . 
Founded in 2020 by celebrated makeup artist Namrata Soni and Bozzil, a venture studio founded and

The acquisition adds a complementary dimension to House of Nykaa’s makeup portfolio, which caters 
to diverse consumer s, from trend -led makeup for younger audiences to celebrity -led beauty brands 
with inclusivity at its core . Among these - Simply Nam allows Nykaa  to play in the specialized, premium, 
professional grade makeup segment . The acquisition is subject to regulatory approvals and closing 
conditions .  
 
• Nykaa strengthened its strategic partnership with L’Oréal through a collaboration with BOLD, L’Oréal’s

The partnership combines Nykaa’s  consumer ecosystem, retail network and distribution capabilities 
with L’Oréal’s global beauty expertise, mentorship and strategic guidance to support emerging brands 
in scaling their businesses, while enabling founders to retain operational and creative c ontrol.
