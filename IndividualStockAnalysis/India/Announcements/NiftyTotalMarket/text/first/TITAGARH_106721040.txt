Titagarh Naval Systems  Limited (a  wholly  owned  subsidiary  of Titagarh  Rail Systems  Limited)  Completes Series 
with Launch of Fifth & Final Diving Support Craft A24 (YARD 329) for the Indian Navy  
 
Kolkata, 1 August 2026: Titagarh Naval Systems Limited (TNSL), a wholly owned subsidiary of Titagarh Rail 
Systems  Limited,  is proud  to achieve  another  milestone  in its shipbuilding  journey  with  the launch  of the fifth 
Diving  Support  Craft  A24 (YARD  329)  for the Indian  Navy.  The vessel  is the fifth  and final  ship  in a series  of five 
Diving Support Craft (DSCs) being constructed under the Government of India's Make in India initiative.  
The launch ceremony was graced by Mrs. Kamal Singh, wife of Vice Admiral Gurcharan Singh,  AVSM, NM  Chief of 
Personnel  who performed the traditional naming and launch of the vessel. The craft was successfully side 
launched into the Hooghly  River  at 1505  Hrs on 1st August  2026.  Around  200 guests  were  present  to witness  this 
important occasion.  
Designed as catamaran -type vessels, these Diving Support Crafts are equipped with advanced, indigenously 
sourced systems to support the Indian Navy’s Command Clearance Diving Teams (CCDTs). They will play a 
critical  role in underwater  operations,  including  inspection,  repair,  maintenance,  and salvage  activities  within 
harbour and near -shore environments.  
Speaking on the occasion, Shri Prithish Chowdhary, Dy . Managing Director, Titagarh Rail Systems Limited, said:  
"This achievement reflects the strength of Indian manufacturing and engineering, and demonstrates how 
indigenous capabilities are steadily evolving to deliver complex  vessels towards important platforms. A t 
Titagarh , we remain committed to expanding our capabilities across  maritime sector, contributing to the 
nation's vision of self -reliance and the Hon'ble Prime Minister's vision of Viksit Bharat through world -class 
manufacturing  from 'Chip Se Ship Tak'."  
Speaking on the occasion, Cmde. Hemant Khatri, Managing Director & CEO, Titagarh Naval Systems Limited, 
said:  "The launch of DSC A24 (YARD 329) completes the launch of all five Diving Support Craft being built for the 
Indian Navy , a proud moment for our team and a significant milestone for naval shipbuilding in Eastern India. 
As the first private shipyard in West Bengal to build and deliver commissioned vessels for the Indian Navy, we 
remain focused on delivering reliable, high -qual ity platforms that strengthen the Navy's operational capabilities 
while preparing ourselves for more advanced defence shipbuilding programmes in the years ahead."  
Speaking  on the occasion,  Vice Admiral  Gurcharan  Singh,  AVSM, NM  COP,  Indian  Navy,  said:  A ship's launch is a 
proud milestone that reflects years of planning, hard work and teamwork. The launch of DS C A24, the fifth vessel 
in this series, is another important step in the project . With nearly 70% indigenous content, it showcases the 
success of the Government of India's Atmanirbhar Bharat and Make in India initiatives. It also highlights the 
strong partnership between the Indian Navy and Indian industry in building world -class ship s in India. I sincerely 
thank Titagarh Naval  Systems Limited, the Indian Navy, the Indian Register of Shipping, and all the engineers, 
technicians and workers whose dedication made this achievement possible.  
In addition to operational deployment , the DSCs will also serve as training platforms for the Indian Navy's diving 
personnel, enhancing operational readiness and capability development.  
The launch marks another significant milestone for Titagarh Naval Systems Limited (TNSL), the dedicated 
shipbuilding and maritime systems company formed following the strategic restructuring of Titagarh Rail Systems 
Limited. Strengthening its focus on mari time growth, TNSL is also developing a state -of-the-art, 50 -acre shipyard 
at Falta, West Bengal, to cater to both national  and international shipbuilding requirements.  
With a proven track record of delivering complex defence and commercial vessels, TNSL continues to advance 
India's self -reliance in shipbuilding and remains committed to supporting the nation's maritime ambitions and the 
vision of Viksit Bharat.  
 
 
 
About  TNSL:  
 
Titagarh  Naval  Systems  Limited  is poised  to mark  a significant  milestone  in India’s  maritime  sector  with  the 
establishment of a state -of-the-art shipyard at Falta, south of Kolkata. To drive this growth, the Board of  Directors 
has, in principle, approved the transfer of the shipbuilding and maritime systems business into a wholly  owned  
subsidiary,  Titagarh  Naval  Systems  Limited,  with  an approximate  order  book  of 500 crores (including taxes).  
Designed  with  advanced  automation  and fully  aligned  with  Industry  4.0 standards,  the new  facility  builds  on TRSL’s  
strong  legacy  of delivering  more  than  35 vessels —including  coastal  research  ships  for NIOT,  the Indian Navy,  Coast  
Guard,  and GRSE —and is engineered  to handle  ships  up to 180 meters  in length,  with  an annual capacity of 12 -16 
vessels.  
 
The establishment  of Titagarh  Naval  Systems  Limited  will enable  TRSL  to concentrate  more  sharply  on its core 
railway  systems  business,  while  empowering  the new  company  to pursue  aggressive  growth  in shipbuilding  by 
securing contracts for specialized vessels that contribute to the nation’s maritime excellence. Titagarh Naval 
Systems Limited will also carry forward existing projects, including the Diving Support Craft project. To drive  
further  expansion,  Titagarh  Naval  Systems  Limited  is actively  exploring  partnerships  with  strategic  collaborators 
and investors to accelerate scaling and enhance its capabilities under various Government of India maritime 
initiatives.
