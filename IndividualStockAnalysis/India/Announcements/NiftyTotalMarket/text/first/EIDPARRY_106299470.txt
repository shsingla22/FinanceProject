Embracing 
Change.
E.I.D.- Parry (India) Limited has been 
in business across five challenging decades.
These decades were marked by 
changes in economic direction, global trade flows, consumer preference and climate.
An overarching reason why the 
Company endured and excelled during these tumultuous times was because it embraced change.
By responding proactively to evolving 
times, the Company remained ahead of the curve, stayed competitive across market cycles and prepared to graduate to the next level.
EID Parry: 
Realigning the business in a rapidly evolving world
At 50, EID Parry is realigning itself with an 
evolving world.
Lifestyles are transforming. Consumer 
demand is rising. There is a growing digitalisation focus. There is a deeper respect for food hygiene.
This transforming reality opens new 
opportunities.
EID Parry has embarked on initiatives to 
deepen its relevance to a transforming landscape with an enhanced focus on value creation, consumer engagement and operational sustainability.
In doing so, the Company is creating the 
foundation of a modern company drawing extensively from its pedigreed past, deepening its relevance to the present and building for an exciting future.
EID Parry remains young at 50.EID Parry: 
Transforming to service the critical needs of the world
For decades, EID Parry was known as a 
sugar company. 
The Company is repositioning its identity 
and deepening its brand as a bio-energy and foods company. 
The ethanol and co-generation businesses 
will service the growing clean energy needs of a modern world. 
The consumer products business will 
address the need for quality packaged and branded food products, addressing the convenience cum hygiene needs of the market under the trusted Parry brand. 
This portfolio broad-basing beyond 
wholesale sugar, by increasing a focus on institutional sugar, distillery operations, power, co-generation and Consumer Products Group, is expected to widen the Company’s revenue streams with a limitless addressable market and enhanced value-addition. 
These profitable verticals – sugar & 
biofuel and consumer products – will empower the Company to capitalise on a modernising and transforming India.

EID Parry:
Unleashing 
the power of the Parry brand 
A decisive initiative by EID Parry during the last 
financial year was its extension into the consumer products business.
This extension is expected to unleash the value of 
the Parry brand, the Company’s principal asset.
The new business will leverage competencies 
developed across the decades — product differentiation, brand building, and extensive distribution — to build the branded consumer products group.
Through this business, the Parry brand is 
positioned to deepen its presence across millions of Indian households and emerge as a prominently consistent feature of every family’s monthly shopping basket.
This business will empower the Company to 
decouple from the cyclical and regulatory influences of the traditional sugar sector.
It will deepen the Company’s positioning as a 
science-driven, consumer-centric enterprise.
