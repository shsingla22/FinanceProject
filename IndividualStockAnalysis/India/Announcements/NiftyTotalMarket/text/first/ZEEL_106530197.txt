Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (‘SEBI Listing Regulations’), we hereby inform you that 
Securities and Exchange Board of India vide its Order dated February 10, 2026 has imposed penalty of 
Rs. 4,00,000/- (Rupees Four Lakhs Only) on Cyquator Media Services Private Limited, a promoter of Zee 
Entertainment Enterprises Limited, for violation of provisions of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares 
and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 in respect of non disclosures and/or incorrect disclosures with respect 
of pledge of shares on December 14, 2016, invocation of pledge of shares on January 28, 2019 and transfer 
of shares on February 02, 2019. 
 
The details required to be disclosed as per Regulation 30 of SEBI Listing Regulations read with SEBI 
Circular HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, is enclosed as
