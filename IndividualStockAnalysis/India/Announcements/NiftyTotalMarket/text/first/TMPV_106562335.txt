Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd. to increase prices of its passenger vehicles from 1st April 2026  
Mumbai, March 20 , 202 6: Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd. (TMPV), India’s leading manufacturer of cars and 
SUVs, today announced a price increase for its passenger vehicle (ICE) portfolio, effective 1st April 2026.   
This revision is being undertaken to partially offset the continued increase in input costs.  
The weighted average price increase will be 0.5 % of ICE portfolio and the extent will vary acr oss models and variants
