accordance with the recognition and measurement principle laid down in Indian Accounting

has been carried out by the statutory auditors.

figures in respect of the full financial year ended 31 March 2025 and the reviewed year to date 
published unaudited figures for the nine months ended 31 December 2024, as approved by the 
board of directors.

standalone financial results which were reviewed by S.R. Batliboi & Associates LLP.

unaudited financial results for the quarter ended 30 June 2025 as per para 4 of Indian Accounting

2013.
