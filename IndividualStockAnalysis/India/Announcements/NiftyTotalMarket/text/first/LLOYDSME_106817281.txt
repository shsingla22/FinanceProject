Dear Sir / Madam,  
 
In compliance with Regulation 30 and 51 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (“ SEBI ”) 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“ Listing Regulations ”), we 
would hereby like to inform that the Board at its meeting held today, i.e. Wednes day, 14th October , 
2026  inter alia, approved the following:  
 
1. Approval for issuance of Non -Convertible Debentures for an amount not exceeding Rs.  3,000 
Crore (Rupees Three Thousand Crore Only) in one or more tranches on private placement 
basis  
 
The Board of Directors have granted an enabling approval for issuance of Non -Convertible 
Debentures for an amount not exceeding Rs. 3,000 Crore (Rupees Three  Thousand Crore Only) in 
one or more tranches, on private placement basis subject to all applicable regulatory / statutory 
approval(s).  
 
The details required pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with Clause 
A(2)(2.1)  of Annexure 18 of SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 11th 
November, 2024 (“ Disclosure Circular ”), are set out under Annexure - A. 
 
2. Approval for Raising of Funds through Issuance of USD -Denominated Notes and/or Other 
Debt Securities  in one or more tranches  
 
The Board of Directors of the Company has approved raising of funds through the issuance of 
United States Dollar (“ USD ”) denominated senior secured floating rate or fixed rate notes  
(“Notes ”) and/or bonds / debentures / other debt securities  (“Securities ”), in one or more tranches 
for an aggregate principal amount of up to USD 130 million  (United States Dollar One Hundred  
and Thirty Million) , with an option to upsize the issuance by an additional USD 50 million  (United 
States Dollar Fifty Million) , under Regulation S under the United States Securities Act of 1933 
and subject to receipt of all applicable regulatory and statutory approvals.  
 
The details required pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with Clause 
A(2)(2.1)  of Annexure 18 of the Disclosure Circular  will be provided upon further approval of the 
pricing, tenure and other terms and conditions of the proposed issuance by the Board or Committee 
of the Board of Directors, as authorised by the Board of Directors.  
 
 
 
 
 
  
3. Approval for Cancellation of Issuance of Non -Convertible Debentures aggregating up to Rs. 
600 Crore  on Private Placement Basis  
 
The Board of Directors of the Company has approved and noted the cancellation of the proposed 
issuance of Non -Convertible Debentures (“ NCDs ”) aggregating up to Rs. 600 Crore (Rupees Six 
Hundred Crore Only), on a private placement basis, which was approved by the Board of Directors 
at their meeting held on 21st September, 2026, subject to applicable regulatory/statutory 
requirements.  
 
The approval for the issuance of NCDs aggregating up to Rs. 950 Crore (Rupees Nine Hundred 
and Fifty Crore Only), on a private placement basis, as approved by the Board of Directors at its 
meeting held on 21st September, 2026, shall remain unaffected.  
 
The Board Meeting commenced at 02:00 P.M. (IST) and concluded at 03:40 P.M. (IST)   
 
The same will also be available on the Company’s Website at www.lloyds.in .  
 
The same may please be taken on record and suitably disseminated to all concerned.  
 
Thanking you.
