Before we start the official business of the AGM, I would like to talk briefly about the progress of 
the Company has made during the year that went by. 
 
As we presented the Integrated Annual Report for FY 2026, Zydus Wellness stands at the 
intersection of evolving lifestyles, changing aspirations, and growing desire to live better and 
healthy life every day. The theme “Choose Wellness, Choose Life” captures a powerful truth. 
Wellness is shaped by the choices we make every single day. Across the world, consumers are 
becoming more conscious of how they live, what they consume and how they care for their 
health and well-being. Wellness is no longer viewed as an occasional choice; it is increasingly 
becoming a way of life. At Zydus Wellness, we believe this shift presents both an opportunity and 
 
 
  
responsibility. An opportunity to serve every consumer’s need and a responsibility to do so with 
trust, science and integrity.  
 
FY26 was a year that tested resilience while creating new avenues for growth. Consumer demand 
patterns continue to evolve, weather conditions impacted certain seasonal categories and 
businesses across industries had to navigate an increasingly dynamic environment. Yet despite 
these challenges, we remained focus on our long-term priorities and continue to strengthen the 
foundation of our business. Our performance during the year reflects the strength of our 
portfolio, the commitment of our teams and the disciplined execution of our strategy. Revenue 
from operations increased by 46.2% to Rs. 36,610 million. While EBITDA grew 34.2% to Rs. 5,097 
million. While certain parts of our portfolio faced temporary challenges, the overall business 
remained resilient. Within our domestic portfolio, seasonal brands were impacted by delayed 
summers and unseasonal rainfall. At the same time, our skincare haircare portfolio delivered 
strong growth supported by innovation, premiumisation and deeper consumer engagements.  
 
Our food and nutrition segment witnessed continued growth reflecting consumers increasing 
focus towards healthier lifestyles and the strength of our offerings in these categories. The 
Wellness landscape continued to expand rapidly. Consumers today seek products that support 
healthier living and preventive care and better nutrition, while expecting greater convenience 
accessibility and authenticity. We are responding by strengthening our portfolio – investing in 
innovation, building deeper consumer connections across both the physical and digital channels.  
 
Our portfolio comprises iconic brands such as Sugar Free, Glucon-D, Complan, Everyuth, Nycil, 
Nutralite, RiteBite Max Protein, and I'm Lite, which continues to enjoy the trust of millions of 
consumers built over years through consistent quality, reliability and science-led innovation, 
these brands continue to evolve through new formats, product innovations and wellness-
oriented positioning. By responding to emerging trends such as functional nutrition, 
convenience-led consumption and healthier-conscious choices, we continue to strengthen their 
relevance while sustaining category leadership.
