Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
Subject: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations 2015 – Disclosure of 
material event  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform you that the petition filed by REC Limited , under  Section 7 
of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 , against the Company , has been dismissed by  the 
Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, New Delhi Branch (Court – II).     
 
The said petition was filed by REC Limited in its capacity as a holder of Redeemable Preference 
Shares (RPS) of the Company. The Hon’ble NCLT has dismissed the petition on the ground that 
RPS does not constitute a “financial debt” under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, and 
hence REC Limited does not qualify as a “financial creditor” eligible to initiate Corporate 
Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) under Section 7.  
 
We are enclosing herewith the details , for the above as prescribed under SEBI Listing Regulations 
read with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024  as an 
Annexure A.   
 
We request you to kindly take the above information on record.
