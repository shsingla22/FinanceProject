BRIEF BACKGROUND 
 
1. The underlying proceedings related to an allegation by the Directorate General of GST Intelligence 
("DGGI"), Raipur Zonal Unit, that the Company had availed Input Tax Credit (" ITC") amounting to 
₹14,65,96,291  on the basis of invoices issued by certain suppliers alleged to be non-existent or 
fictitious, in connection with coal and ash handling. 
 
2. The Order-in-Original dated 27.03.2025 had confirmed the entire demand of ₹14,65,96,291 along with 
applicable interest under Section 50 and penalty of ₹14,65,96,291/- under Section 74 read with Section 
122(1)(vii) and 122(1)(x) of the CGST Act, 2017, and had appropriated ₹5,86,92,809 already 
paid/reversed by the Company under protest towards the said demand. 
 
3. The Company had filed an appeal against the Order-in-Original before the Commissioner (Appeals), 
CGST & Central Excise, Bhopal. 
 
DETAILS OF THE ORDER-IN-APPEAL 
 
4. The Commissioner (Appeals) has, by the Order dated October 13, 2026 (received by the Company on 
October 15, 2026), allowed the Company's appeal and held that the demand, interest and penalty 
confirmed under the Order-in-Original are not sustainable.   
 
 
 
5. Consequently, the aggregate demand of Tax and Penalty of ₹ 29,31,92,582/- (along with the 
associated interest) stood vacated pursuant to the Order.  
 
IMPACT ON THE COMPANY 
 
The Order is favourable to the Company and results in setting aside of the demand that was the subject 
matter of our earlier intimation dated March 29, 2025. The Order is not expected to have any material 
adverse impact on the financials, operations or other activities of the Company. The amount of ₹5,86,92,809 
previously paid/reversed by the Company under protest in relation to this matter would, consequent to 
the Order, become eligible for refund/re-credit in accordance with applicable law and procedure.
