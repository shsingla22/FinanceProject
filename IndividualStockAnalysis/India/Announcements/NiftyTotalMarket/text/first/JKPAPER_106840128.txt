Press Release 
 
Unaudited Results for Quarter Ended September,2026 
 
Consolidated Quarterly Turnover at Rs.2,054 Cr, up by 11%, 
EBITDA & PAT increased by 26 % ,36 % respectively 
JK Paper Ltd., (JKPL)  one of India’s largest Paper & Packaging Solutions Companies, 
recorded a Consolidated Turnover of Rs. 2,053.69 Cr (up 11 %), EBITDA of Rs.300.86 
Cr (up 26%) ,Profit before Tax (PBT) of Rs.143.72 Cr (up 48 %) and Profit after Tax 
(PAT) of Rs.102.10 Cr (up 36%), for Quarter-2 ended September,2026.  
 
For the Half Year ended September 2026,the Consolidated Turnover, EBITDA, PBT 
and PAT stood as  Rs.4,052.36 Cr (up 12 %), Rs.621.76 Cr (up 21 %), Rs.326 Cr (up 
53 %) and Rs.237.76 Cr (up 50 %)  respectively. 
  
 
Commenting on the results, Shri Harsh Pati Singhania said, “Better product mix has 
contributed to improved performance during the quarter compared to the corresponding 
period. The Packaging Conversion businesses continues to show an increasing trend 
both in terms of revenue and profit.” 
 
During the Quarter, JK Paper acquired remaining stake of 20% in Radheshyam Wellpack 
Private Limited (RWPL) . Consequent to that, RWPL has become a Wholly Owned 
Subsidiary Company of JK Paper Ltd.  
 
CRISIL Ratings Limited  has reaffirmed its ratings to  “CRISIL AA/Stable, CRISIL A1+”  on the 
Bank facilities, Non- Convertible Debentures (NCDs) and Commercial Papers of the Company 
respectively. 
 
The Company continued it’s focus on social farm forestry initiatives around all plant 
locations to strengthen availability of adequate raw material besides improving green 
cover in the states of Odisha, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Maharashtra. 
Total Saplings planted so far during the first six months of this year are 6.63 Cr. covering 
47,532 Acres.
