Brief Profile of Mr. Narasinganallore  Venkatesh Srinivasan (“Mr. N S Venkatesh”)  
 
Mr. N S Venkatesh is a Chartered Accountant and a Science graduate from Kerala University. He 
holds Diplomas in Management and Financial Services and is a Certified Associate of the Indian 
Institute of Bankers (CAIIB)  and the Indian Institute of Banking and Finance (IIBF) . 
 
He possesses specialized knowledge and practical expertise in Risk Management , Treasury 
operations , Accountancy, Banking, Business Management, Economics, Finance  and  
International Banking.  
 
With a distinguished career spanning over 4 decades in the banking and financial services sector , 
including more than 20 years in Treasury and International Banking , he has held several pivotal 
leadership positions. His tenure at the Industrial Development Bank of India (IDBI)  extended for 
over 19 years, during which he served in senior leadership roles  such as Chief Financial Officer 
and Executive Director. Additionally, he has held positions as Chief Executive Officer of the 
Association of Mutual Funds in  India (AMFI)  and Executive Director at Lakshmi Vilas Bank.  
  
He has been an active member of various committees and working groups of the Reserve Bank 
of India (RBI) . He has chaired the Fixed Income, Money Markets & Derivatives Association 
(FIMMDA)  and served as a Committee Member of the Foreign Exchange Dealers’ Association of 
India (FEDAI) . He was  also an invitee -ex officio to SEBI’s Mutual Funds Advisory Committee and 
a member of SEBI’s Corporate Bonds and Securitization Advisory Committee (CoBoSAC) . 
Currently, he serves as a Director on the Boards of several repu ted companies.  
  
Brief Profile of Mr. Satyajit Dwivedi  
 
Mr. Satyajit Dwivedi  holds a Bachelor of Science in Physics and a Master of Science with 
specialization in Semiconductor Physics from Utkal University. He is a Certified Associate of the 
Indian Institute of Bankers (CAIIB)  and holds a Diploma in Microfinance from the Indian Institute 
of Banking and Finance.  
 
He is an eminent and highly accomplished development finance professional with an illustrious 
career spanning over 35 years in the domains of Agriculture and Rural Economy, Microfinance, 
Co-operation, Banking, Business Management, Finance, Financial Inclusion and Risk 
Management.   
 
He started his career with Bank of India and then held several senior cadre positions in NABARD . 
He has been an acclaimed faculty member at the prestigious College of Agricultural Banking 
(CAB)  of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) . He has held the position of Managing Director & Chief 
Executive Officer of Nabkisan Finance Ltd. He also served as the Chief Executive Officer of the 
National Centre for Financial Education (NCFE) . 
 
His exceptional leadership and contributions earned him recognition among the “100 Most 
Influential Leaders in Banking and Financial Services Institutions” by The Economic Times  in the 
year 2017.  
  
 
 
 
 
Brief Profile of Ms. Malini Thadani  
 
Ms. Malini Thadani joined the Board of AU Small Finance Bank in November 2022. She has 
completed a Diploma Programme for Non -Executive Directors, conducted by Financial Times in 
Hong Kong, a Programme in Social Entrepreneurship from INSEAD, Paris, France a nd the Cycle 
Special Etranger, at the Ecole Nationale D’Administration in France. She has acquired a 
Certificate of Public Administration and a Master’s Degree from Ohio University, USA, a Master’s 
Degree in History from Hindu College, University of Delhi and a Bachelor’s degree in History from 
Lady Shri Ram College, University of Delhi.  
 
She has special knowledge and practical experience in the fields of Sustainability & Sustainable 
Finance , CSR, ESG,  Banking, Risk Management, Business Management  and  Public Affairs.  
 
Her career spanned over 4 decades, first  with the Indian Revenue Service and thereafter  with 
HSBC across India and Hong Kong.  She was Head of Sustainability, Asia, at the HongKong and 
Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited in Hong Kong and has held leadership positions in the 
areas of Communications, Sustainability, CSR, and Public Affairs at HSBC India. Before that, she 
held senio r positions in the Indian Revenue Service from 1980 to 1995.  
  
She is visiting faculty at the Indian School of Development Management (ISDM)  where she 
teaches a course on CSR, Sustainability and ESG.  
  
As Independent Director on corporate Boards and not -for-profits, she advises organisations to 
future proof themselves by effectively integrating sustainability and ESG into their strategic 
vision. She is skilled in designing, developing and implementing la rge-scale, complex, cross -
border sustainability projects in partnership with global and regional think tanks, not -for-profits, 
and academia, specialising in areas including livelihoods, employability, entrepreneurship, 
sustainable finance, and environmenta l resource security.
