 PRESS RELEASE  
 
SJVN’s Rampur HPS Sets New Benchmark with Fastest -Ever 2000 MU 
Generation  
Shimla: 06.02.2026  
 
Sh. Bhupender Gupta, Chairman & Managing Director, S JVN has i nformed 
that SJVN  has achieved a significant milestone as its  412 MW  Rampur Hydro Power 
Station recorded  fastest -ever generation of 2000 Million Units on February 5, 2026. 
The achievement was accomplished nine days ahead of previous record set on 
February  14, 2020, marking best generation performance of the Power Station in last 
eleven  years of operation.  
On this occasion, SJVN Management c ongratulat ed employees  and everyone 
directly and indirectly involved in achieving this milestone. They expressed gratitude 
for continued guidance and support of  the Government of India  and Himachal 
Pradesh , Ministry of Power, Central Electricity Authority  and District administrations 
of Shimla and Kullu.  
Sh. Bhupender Gupta, Chairman & Managing Director, commended discipline  
and strong work culture of Rampur HPS Team  under Head of Project, Sh. Vikas 
Marwah .  
Sh. Ajay Kumar Sharma, Director (Personnel) attributed the milestone to 
collective competence, effective human resource practices and seamless inter -
departmental coordination.   
Sh. Sipan Kumar Garg, Director (Finance), SJVN, stated  that this  record 
performance demonstrates  the synergy between sound financial management and 
operation al excellence.   
Since its commissioning, Rampur Hydro Power Station has consistently set 
new benchmarks in generation performance, reflecting strength of its engineering 
design, rigorous maintenance regimes and sustained operational excellence.  
This achievement reinforces SJVN’s commitment to operational excellence 
and sustainable power generation for the nation.
