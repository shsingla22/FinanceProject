Pidilite Ventures Invests in Imagimake – A Leading Innovation-Driven 
Educational Toys and Hobby Products Company
Mumbai, 17 October, 2025  – Pidilite Ventures Pvt. Ltd. (Pidilite Ventures), a wholly owned subsidiary 
of Pidilite Industries Limited (Pidilite), announced an investment not exceeding ₹340 Million in 
Imagimake Play Solutions Private Limited (Imagimake), an innovation-led company designing and 
distributing educational toys, games, and art & hobby products. This Series B round comprises a mix 
of primary and secondary capital. 
This marks Pidilite Ventures’ second investment in Imagimake, following their Series A infusion of ₹200 
Million in October 2023. The continued support underscores strong confidence in Imagimake’s vision 
and execution capability. 
Commenting on the investment, Sanket Parekh, Director at Pidilite Ventures , said: 
“We are delighted to deepen our partnership with Imagimake. Over the last two years, the team has 
demonstrated impressive growth by creating innovative product lines, scaling distribution, and 
expanding globally. We believe Imagimake is uniquely positioned to become a category-defining brand 
at the intersection of play and learning, both in India and overseas.”
The investment from this round will enable Imagimake to accelerate capacity expansion, strengthen 
R&D, and scale its global distribution network. 
Disha Katharani, Co-founder of Imagimake , said: “At Imagimake, we believe play has the power to 
shape curiosity, skills, and creativity. This funding allows us to bring more innovative products to life, 
reaching millions of children globally and inspiring them to learn and explore through play.”
Ravi Kumar, Co-founder of Imagimake , added: “Pidilite is an iconic Company we have always admired, 
and their continued backing is a huge vote of confidence in our journey. With this Series B round, we 
will accelerate capacity expansion, invest in new product development, and deepen our footprint in 
international markets.”
About Pidilite Ventures
Pidilite Ventures ( www.pidilite.vc ) is the venture arm of Pidilite. Pidilite Ventures invests in fast-
growing and innovative companies that have synergies with Pidilite’s core and adjacent businesses. 
About Imagimake
Founded by Ravi Kumar and Disha Katharani, Imagimake is an innovation-driven company offering 
nearly 120 SKUs across educational puzzles, STEM-based kits, creative play products, and hobby sets. 
Its products are available in 4,000+ retail outlets in India and exported to 30+ countries, with the U.S. 
being the largest international market. Flagship successes include Mapology , a puzzle series that has 
become a household name, and Mission Chandrayaan , developed to celebrate ISRO’s space 
achievements. The company’s Look What I Made!  range of tactile, exploratory play products 
reinforces its philosophy of making play meaningful and enriching.
