Pursuant to Regulation 30(4) of SEBI (LODR) 2015, we are writing to inform you of a 
significant change in our company's retail strategy at one of our stores situated at Municipal 
No. 5-4-14 & 14/1, Nizamshahi Road, Abids, Hyderabad, Telangana – 500001, measuring an 
area of 2,000 sq. ft., approximately, details of which are elaborated below.  
 
We are transitioning one of our Exclusive brand outlets (EBO) “Samsung Store”, to an 
Exclusive brand outlet of “Haier Store” , while continuing to sell Samsung products in our 
other multi-brand outlets. This strategic move focuses on leveraging Haier’s strong presence 
in the large appliances segment, refrigerators, washing machines, and air conditioners to 
drive growth, strengthen brand positioning, and enhance market share. The dedicated Haier 
store will provide a focused brand experience, superior product availability, expert guidance, 
and specialized after-sales support, ensuring convenience and confidence in their purchase. 
This approach aims to achieve sustainable growth by aligning customer satisfaction with 
business objectives. 
 
Focused Brand 
Experience  Customers get a dedicated Haier store, making it easier to explore 
large appliances like refrigerators, washing machines, and air 
conditioners with expert guidance Enhanced Growth 
Potential  Leveraging Haier’s strong market reputation in large appliances will 
boost sales, market share, and overall revenue. 
Improved 
Customer Support:  Specialized after-sales service and product support increase 
customer satisfaction and trust, strengthening long-term loyalty.  
We believe this change will significantly benefit our customers and stakeholders.
