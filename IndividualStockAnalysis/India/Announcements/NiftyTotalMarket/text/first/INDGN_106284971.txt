Indegene Identified as a Leader in ISG’s Provider Lens ™ Evaluation for Life Sciences 
Digital Services in Commercial Operations - Digital Evolution (Service Providers) 
Quadrant 2025  
 
Princeton , US, June  30, 202 5: Information  Services  Group  (ISG),  a leading  global  technology  research  and advisory  firm,  
has identifi ed Indegene as a  Leader in its Provider Lens ™ for Life Sciences Digital Services in Commercial Operations - 
Digital Evolution (Service Providers) Quadrant  2025 .  
 
Rohan Sinha, Principal Analyst for Healthcare and Life Sciences at ISG,  noted, “ Indegene is a key player in life sciences 
commercial operations. They effectively streamline content processes and integrate seamlessly with major industry 
platforms, ultimately boosting client responsiveness and effectiveness .”  
 
ISG acknowledged Indegene’s strengths in implement ing holistic creative workflows , interoperability via robust 
integration with tech ecosystems and streamlined content creation processes for improved content efficiency , and faster 
and more accurate  review  automation.   
 
“We remain focused on helping our clients modernize and transform their commercial operations using an AI -first, data -
driven approach,” said Vivek Ghai, EVP - Enterprise Commercial Solutions, Indegene. “This recognition reflects our 
commitment and deep cap abilities to delivering purpose -built, insight -driven solutions that accelerate commercial 
outcomes , and help our clients be future -ready.” 
 
 
 
ISG evaluated 32 service providers with strong capabilities in digitally transforming life sciences commercial operations, 
focusing on areas such as data analytics, customer engagement, and personali zed medicine. Indegene is the only life 
sciences specialist named as a leader in the quadrant.  
 
ISG emphasized the critical role of technology in commercial operations, particularly in the context of omnichannel 
engagement, regulatory compliance, and growing GenAI adoption.
