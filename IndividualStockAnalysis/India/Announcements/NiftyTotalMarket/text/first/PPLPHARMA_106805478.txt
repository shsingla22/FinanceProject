Company's Aurora facility from the Government of Ontario under the Life Sciences Scale-Up Fund 
(LSSUF) program, which aims to support innovative Ontario-based life sciences companies in scaling

expected to contribute to employment generation and retention, support innovation and 
commercialization initiatives, and strengthen the broader life sciences and biomanufacturing 
ecosystem in Ontario.

Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as 
it is not expected to have a material impact on the Company's operations, financial position, or 
performance.
