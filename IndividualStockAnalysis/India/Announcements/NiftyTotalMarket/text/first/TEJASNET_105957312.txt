We refer to our letter of intimation dated December 8, 2023 wherein we informed that Saankhya

(MSS) Terminals (Xponders) for vessel communication and support system in marine fishing 
vessels for monitoring, control and surveillance (MCS), and the provisional purchase order would 
be deemed as final / confirmed purchase order only after the delivery and acceptance of proto 
terminals (Xponders) and bulk production clearance by NewSpace India Limited. 
 
In continuation of the above letter of intimation, we now inform / disclose that NewSpace India 
Limited has accepted the proto terminals (Xponders), has issued the bulk production clearance 
to Saankhya, and the provisional purchase order has been converted to final purchase order.
