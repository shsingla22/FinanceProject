Commenting on the performance, Mr. Anuj Kumar, Managing Director said,  
 
“Q2 FY ’27 underscores the strength of CAMS ’s platform -led business model and the 
growing benefits of our diversification strategy. We delivered double -digit revenue growth, 
expanded EBITDA margins to 46% and achieved healthy profit growth despite a volatile 
operating environment.  
 
Our core Mutual Fund franchise continues to demonstrate industry -leading scale and 
resilience, with assets under management reaching a record ₹58.4 lakh crore and with 
continued outperformance across growth in equity assets, SIPs and investor count. The ra pid 
scaling of SIFs, growing adoption of GIFT City Retail Funds and expansion of our client base 
to 32 AMCs reinforces our strategy of building a high -quality, institution -led MF RTA 
franchise.  
 
Equally importantly, our non -mutual fund businesses have emerged as meaningful growth 
engines. With non -MF revenues nearing 16% of total revenues and CAMSPay crossing the 
6% revenue  contribution milestone, we are seeing increasing validation of our strateg y to 
build adjacent platforms serving the broader financial services ecosystem. Strong 
growth  across Payments, Alternatives, KRA, Insurance Repository and our newly 
commercialized ConsenPro  platform provide us with confidence in the sustainability and 
breadth of our future growth trajectory.  
 
Alongside business growth, our technology modernization and AI -led transformation 
programs are now delivering sustained benefits. From the successful deployment of 
FundsNet (the modernized MF transaction -acceptance platform), CAMSlens  and KYCHUB to 
rapidly increasing scale in AI -led transaction processing and enterprise -wide data 
modernization, we are building a future -ready operating platform that will enhance 
productivity, scalability and client experience. We remain confident in our  ability to deepen 
market leadership, accelerate innovation and create long -term value for clients, 
shareholders and all stakeholders.”  
 
About CAMS Limited ( www.camsonline.com ) BSE: 543232; NSE: CAMS  
CAMS is a financial infrastructure and service partner to the asset management industry, providing platform -
based services to the BFSI segment. The company is India's leading registrar and transfer agent for mutual funds, 
serving an aggregate market share of ~6 7% based on mutual fund average assets under management (“AAuM ”). 
CAMS is also the market -leading platform and service partner for alternative investment funds and portfolio 
managers, offering end -to-end digital and fund administration services. Its subsidiary CAMSKRA is a fast -growing 
KYC repository for capital mark ets, leveraging new -age technologies to deliver seamless investor onboarding. 
CAMSPay specializes in secure, seamless and innovative payment solutions for mutual funds, BFSI and EdTech 
sectors. Through CAMSRep, the company provides services to insurance co mpanies and the insurance 
ecosystem. CAMS has also expanded into new -growth areas such as Account Aggregator and Central Record -
Keeping Agency (CRA) services for the National Pension System. The company has further strengthened its digital 
capabilities through its majority stake in Think360.ai, a full -stack Data Science and  AI-focused firm, and Fintuple 
Technologies, a digital transformation partner for capital market entities.
