 Annexure A 
Details with respect to directions received from GPCB as per as per SEBI Circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-
1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023 and SEBI Master Circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/2023/120 dated 
July 11, 2023 
 
1 Name of the authority  Gujarat Pollution Control Board, Bharuch, Gujarat  (“GPCB”) 
2 Nature and details of the action(s) 
taken, initiated or order(s) passed;  
 A Direction is issued under Section 31A of the Air (Prevention 
and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981 (“ Direction ”) by GPCB, 
directing non-utilisation of MPP-3, drum storage shed, the 
warehouse and tank farms which were affected in fire before 
obtaining revocation/ written approval from Department of 
Industrial Safety and Health (“ DISH”) and GPCB and has 
imposed an Environment Damage Compensation (EDC) cost of 
Rs. 34.60 lakhs on the Company and has directed to submit a 
Bank Guarantee of Rs. 7.5 Lakhs for compliance assurance at 
the time of revocation.  
3 Date of receipt of direction or order, 
including any ad-interim or interim 
orders, or any other communication 
from the authority;  April 22, 2025  
4 Details of the violation(s)/ 
contravention(s) committed or alleged 
to be committed;  The Direction issued by the GPCB does not mention any 
violation/contravention committed or alleged to be committed. 
The Direction is issued by GPCB in respect of Fire Accident 
which impacted environment quality of the surrounding. 
5 Impact on financial, operation or other 
activities of the listed entity, 
quantifiable in monetary terms to the 
extent possible  
 Production/Operation of the Multi-Purpose Plant (MPP3)- 
Facility, the warehouse and tank farms which were affected in 
fire is suspended temporarily. It is tentatively estimated that 
the reconstruction of the impacted facilities may take around 9-
12 months to reinstate the plant and restart operations, for 
which planning and construction work has already been 
initiated.  
 
In the interim, the Company has shifted production of critical 
select specialty products to other sites based on approval from 
customers. This, combined with the planned expansion in 
Patancheru Plant, will help minimize the impact on earnings 
going ahead to minimize business disruption. 
 
Further there is no change in the revenue guidance provided by 
the Company in its disclosure dated March 7, 2025.
