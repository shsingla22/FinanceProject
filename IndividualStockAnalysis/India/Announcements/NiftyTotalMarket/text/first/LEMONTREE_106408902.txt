Lemon Tree Hotels launches its 2nd property in  Jharkhand  with  
Lemon Tree Hotel , Ranchi  
 
The hotel features 45 well-appointed rooms  and suites , a restaurant , a bar and 
recreational areas  
 
New Delhi, 15th October 2025:  Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, India’s fastest -
growing mid -scale and premium hotel chain, today announced the launch of its 
latest franchised property  – Lemon Tree Hotel, Ranchi . This is the brand’s second  
property in the state after Jamshedpur , further strengthening its presence in 
Jharkhand . 
 
This franchised  hotel features 45 well-appointed rooms  and suites , complemented 
by Citrus Café – a multi -cuisine  restaurant , Slounge – the hip -recreation bar, a 
fully equipped fitness center and a swimming pool to keep you feeling as -fresh-
as-a-lemon  and value -driven stay experience.  
 
Located in the eastern part of India, Ranchi is the capital of Jharkhand and an 
emerging hub for business, education, and healthcare. Known as the "City of 
Waterfalls," Ranchi combines natural beauty with growing infrastructure, making 
it an attractive des tination for both business and leisure travellers. With its 
expanding industrial base, rich mineral resources, and improving connectivity, the 
city offers a conducive environment for corporate growth.  
 
Ranchi’s cultural vibrancy, scenic surroundings, and developing urban landscape 
further enhance its appeal as a destination for professionals and tourists alike.  
 
Commenting on the opening Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, Lemon Tree 
Hotels said, “ Ranchi is emerg ing as one of India’s preferred destinations, drawing 
both leisure seekers and business travellers alike . With this new opening, our first 
in the city, we are delighted to strengthen our presence in Jharkhand and offer 
guests the brand’s signature combination of comfort, warmth, a nd value in a city 
that epitomis es culture and hospitality .”
