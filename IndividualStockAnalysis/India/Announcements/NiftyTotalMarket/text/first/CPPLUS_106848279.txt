surveillance brand with the most extensive CCTV & Security Products portfolio, announced its unaudited financial results for the quarter & half year ended September 30, 2026.

Financial & Operational Highlights – Q2 FY27 
• Revenue of INR 1,746.0 crore, up 89.9% YoY , driven by strong traction by CP PLUS brand in the 
overall AIL revenue. IP products made up ~79% (vs ~71% in Q2 FY26)  of the CP PLUS portfolio  
• Gross Margin of 30%, up 24 bps YoY, with CP PLUS contribution rising to ~88% (from ~86% in Q2 
FY26) of Revenue  
• EBITDA of INR 307.4 crore, up 176.7% YoY, with margins improving by 552 bps  to 17.6% , 
supported by a favorable product & brand mix and better operational efficiencies  
• PAT before exceptional item of INR 219.9 crore, up 214.2% YoY, attributed to lowering Finance 
cost by 14% YoY , and better cost efficiencies

Financial & Operational Highlights – H1 FY27 
• Revenue of INR 3,148.4 crore, up 89.7% YoY , driven by strong traction by CP PLUS brand in 
the overall AIL revenue. IP products made up ~79%  of the CP PLUS portfolio  
• Gross Margin of 30.4%, up 374 bps YoY, with CP PLUS contribution rising to ~88% of revenue  
• EBITDA of INR 515.2 crore, up 192.7% YoY, with margins improving by 576 bps  to 16.3% , 
supported by a favorable product & brand mix and better operational efficiencies  
• PAT before exceptional item of INR 362.1 crore, up 252.0% YoY, attributed to lowering of 
Finance cost by 41.1% YoY , and better cost efficiencies  
 
Strategic Developments & Business Highlights  
• Market Leadership: AIL commands ~42% share of India’s video -surveillance market in the 
twelve months to September 2026, up from 30% at the IPO (company estimate)  
• Balance Sheet: 
o Net cash of INR 1,071.2 crore as at September 30, 2026, following the INR 1,500 crore 
QIP; all borrowings repaid in October 2026  
• Capacity Expansion:  
o Current capacity stands at 2.5 million units per month; capacity is planned to more than double through Kadapa Phase III and the new facility in Greater Noida, funded

o On November 5, 2026, the Company signed definitive agreements to invest INR 45.4 
crore for a 30% stake (fully diluted) in BLUE (KGraph AI Solutions Pvt Ltd), a video -

• Marketing Initiatives:

campaign across major Indian airports  
The flagship CP PLUS  brand continued its strong momentum, contributing approximately 88% of 
overall Q2 FY27 revenue. IP products accounted for nearly 79% of the CP PLUS portfolio, reflecting 
increasing adoption of higher -value intelligent surveillance solutions

Commenting on the results, Mr. Aditya Khemka, Managing Director, said:  
“We are pleased to report a strong first half, with Q2 building on the momentum of the previous 
quarter. During the quarter Revenue was INR 1,746 crore, representing a growth of 89.9% YoY, while EBITDA increased by 176.7% YoY to INR 307.4 crore. EBITDA margin was 17.6%, supported by a 
favorable product mix, increased localization and operating leverage. Profit After Tax before 
exceptional item was INR 219.9 crore, registering a growth of 214.2% YoY.  
 
We further strengthened our leadership position in India’s video surveillance market, with market share 
reaching ~42% in the twelve months to September 2026, up from 30% at the IPO. CP PLUS continued to 
strengthen its premium brand positioning, supported by capacity expansion, investment in R&D and growing distribution network.  
 During the quarter, we successfully completed the Qualified Institutions Placement (QIP), with participation from leading institutional investors, reflecting their confidence in our strategy and growth 
prospects. We closed the quarter with net cash of INR 1,071 crore and repaid all borrowings in October. 
The proceeds will enhance our financial flexibility and support repayment of borrowings, capital expenditure, inorganic growth through acquisitions, and general corporate purposes, enabling us to strengthen  our market position and drive sustainable long- term growth.  
 On the marketing front, we are pleased to collaborate with Ranveer Singh as a brand ambassador for CP PLUS. This association will support our efforts to expand brand reach and build awareness of the role of intelligent security in everyday life.  
 
Supported by favorable industry dynamics and continued business momentum, we have revised our 
FY27 guidance upwards, with Revenue expected in the range of INR. 6,500 – 6,800 crore, EBITDA 
margin of 14.0 – 15.0% and PAT margin of 9.0 –  10.0%. Our focus remains on disciplined execution, 
technology development and wider market reach to deliver sustainable growth and long- term value for 
all stakeholders.”  
 
About  Aditya  Infotech  Limited:  
Aditya  Infotech  Limited  (BSE:  544466  | NSE:  CPPLUS)  offers  a comprehensive  range  of advanced  video 
security  and surveillance  products,  technologies,  and solutions  for enterprise  and consumer  segments 
under our ‘CP PLUS’ brand, which has strong recall value. In addition, we offer solutions and services 
such as fully integrated  security systems and  Security -as-a-Service directly and  through our distribution 
network that address the requirements of end -customers engaged in a broad range of sectors. Our 
manufacturing  activities  include  the production  and sale of our CP PLUS  products  and the provision  of 
after- sales services in relation to the CP PLUS products sold by us.
