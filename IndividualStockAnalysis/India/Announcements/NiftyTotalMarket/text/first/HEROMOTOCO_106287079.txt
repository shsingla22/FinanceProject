HERO MOTOCORP  DISPATCHES 5.54 LAKH  MOTORCYCLES AND 
SCOOTERS  IN JUNE 2025  
 
MAINT AINS RETAIL MOMENTUM WITH 3.94 LAKH VAHAN 
REGISTRATIONS DURING  THE MONTH  
 
Hero MotoCorp, the world’s largest manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters, dispatched 
553,963  motorcycles and scooters during June 2025 . This translates into a 10% growth over 
June 2024.  
 
The company recorded 3.94 lakh  VAHAN * registrations , reflecting sustained demand across 
urban and rural markets . The onset of  monsoon across  most parts of the country and positive 
economic outlook are expected to accelerate  demand in the lead -up to the festive season.  
*Data from VAHAN except Telangana, as on July 1 , 2025.  
 
VIDA, powered by Hero , continued its growth momentum, dispatching 7,178  units  and 
recording 7, 665  VAHAN regist rations that translates into a steady gain . The company 
continues to see positive growth across its entire scooter portfolio, including both EV and ICE 
models.  
 
Strengthening its portfolio, VIDA has introduced the “EVOOTER” category  with the launch of 
VX2  - Badalte India ka Scooter . The EVOOTER c ombin es the sustainability, intelligence, and 
performance of an EV with the comfort and practicality of a scooter . VIDA’s pioneering 
Battery -As-A-Service (BaaS) model offers “ pay-per-km” subscriptions, eliminat ing high 
upfront costs, making EV ownership truly fle xible and accessible.  
 
Driven by a focused strategy to deepen its presence in key markets while expanding into new 
geographies , the company continued its strong performance in global business, recording 
significant growth in the month and steady growth for  the quarter .  
   
Hero MotoCorp and Harley -Davidson announced the 2025 model year line -up and pricing 
for Harley -Davidson motorcycles in India. The range introduce d the new CVO™ Street Glide® 
and CVO™ Road Glide® —super -premium, limited -edition models showcasing top -tier 
performance, design, and technology. Prices for the available models start at ₹2.39 lakh for 
the H -D X440 and go up to ₹42.30 lakh for the Road Glide ™.
