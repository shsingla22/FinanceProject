Sandeep Poddar, Company Secretary briefed the members on the process of e-voting . He

The Chairman then delivered  his speech and provided insights on various areas,  such as   
macro -economic  environment, GPS strategy, integration of  Citibank consumer business,

thereafter placed on record his gratitude to all the members for having reposed their 
confidence in the Bank and continuing association.

queries  on various subjec t matters such as CASA, dividend, financials , subsidiar y performance ,

Questions  raised  by the members , were appropriately responded to by Amitabh Chaudhry , 
Managing Director & CEO of the Bank . 
 
The e-voting facility was made available for  15 minutes from the conclusion of the meeting . 
 
The Chairman then announced the conclusion of the 30th Annual General Meeting of the Bank 
at 12:15 pm.
