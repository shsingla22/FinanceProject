Bhubaneswar, 27/09/2024:  In the 43rd Annual General Meeting held here today through Virtual 
mode, the shareholders of National Aluminium Company Limited (N ALCO), the Navratna CPSE 
under the Ministry of Mines, Govt. of India, have approved the Annual Accounts for 2023-24 
with a sales turnover of Rs.13,149 crore, net profit of Rs.2,06 0 crore and an export turnover of 
Rs. 4,275.73 crore.  
The shareholders also approved a final dividend of Rs.2 per equ ity share (40% on face value of 
Rs.5/- each) amounting to Rs.367.33 crore for the FY  2023-24. Thus, total dividend paid during 
the year amounts to Rs.918.32 crore, which is 45% of PAT
