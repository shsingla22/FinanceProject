With reference to the captioned subject and in furtherance to the announcement made by Dixon Technologies (India)

inform that the parties have consummated the transaction today i.e. 3rd March, 2026 and upon consummation of the

26,00,000 equity shares of INR 10/- each respectively, of the JV Company.

on a fully diluted basis.
