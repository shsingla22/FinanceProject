 TVS MOTOR COMPANY BRINGS FLAGSHIP 
ADVENTURE TECHNOLOGY TO THE A2 CLASS 
WITH RTX 300  
 Built on the new RT -XD4 platform, the RTX 300 promises a new spirit of 
exploration, infused from decades of race engineering  
 With a strong character of ride experience at its DNA, the RTX 300 is 
loaded with technologies that are reserved for machines twice its 
capacity  
Singapore/ Tuscany, September 7, 2026 - TVS Motor Company, part of TVS VENU, today 
unveiled the RTX 300, its most fully equipped adventure trail motorcycle for Europe. 
Developed on the new RT- XD4 engine platform for adventure riders and A2 licence 
holders, the RTX 300 enters the mid- capacity trail segment with a level of standard 
technology unmatched in its class.  
The RTX 300 is the clearest expression yet of what TVS Motor’s European strategy is built on: engineering developed for some of the world’s most demanding road conditions, brought to European riders without the compromises usually attached to an accessible 
price point. Rally -inspired design and a steel trellis chassis built in harmony with the 
Dynamics of a Discoverist rider make it as capable off the beaten track as it is inviting  on 
the road.  
Thanks to its weight distribution, long- travel suspension and upright riding position, the RTX 
300 offers reassuring handling for riders taking their first steps on a bigger machine, while 
retaining the performance and adjustability that experienced riders  expect. It is a motorcycle 
designed for two groups at once: the youthful and tech savvy A2 licence holders moving up, 
and returning riders coming back to motorcycling who are unwilling to give up the soulful 
rides on two wheels  they have grown used to.  
Speaking at the launch, Peyman Kargar, President, International Business, TVS Motor 
Company said, “Europe is our new frontier, and TVS Motor is bringing RTX 300 for the 
most experienced riders and the most demanding regulation in the world. The RTX 300: a 
motorcycle that carries the technology, the build quality and the warranty of a far more 
expensive machine, and makes it available to riders at the beginning of their journey. It is a 
statement of where we intend to be in this market.”  
“Italy is one of the most knowledgeable motorcycle markets in Europe, and riders here 
judge a machine by how it behaves on the road. That is why we chose to put the RTX 300 
into the hands of the press on Tuscan roads. The ride modes, the suspension travel and 
the connectivity are all there as standard because Italian riders would notice immediately if 
they were missing,”  added Giovanni Notarbartolo di Furnari, General Manager, Italy, 
TVS Motor Company.  
The five key strengths 
 Adventure- inspired design and ergonomics: a  substantial , athletic silhouette, 
monovolume design with a modern dual -LED headlight  that is commanding and 
 assertive . The upright riding position, tall windscreen and weight distribution deliver 
comfort both seated on the road and standing off on a trail . 
 Trellis  chassis and suspension: a tubular  steel  trellis frame with a 41 mm upside-
down fork, 180 mm of suspension travel front and rear, and 200 mm of ground 
clearance, built to take poor surfaces and trails in its stride.  
 Comprehensive standard equipment: cruise control, bi -directional  quickshifter and 
tyre pressure monitoring, depending on version.  
 Rider assistance and safety: an electronic throttle control, combined with 
advanced chassis controls  offers  four ride modes  at the disposal of the rider on the 
fly. Urban, Rain, Tour and Rally  modes , make available calibrated power delivery, 
traction control and ABS  control  as demanded by the surface underneath.  
 Digital cockpit and m ap mirroring: h igh daylight -brightness  5-inch TFT display 
mirrors GPS maps directly through the SmartXonnect app, alongside music and call management  (depending on version) .  
Four modes, four ways to ride  
The four ride modes are four distinct characters, each built around how the motorcycle 
should respond to the control demand of the rider . 
 Urban softens throttle response for stop- start traffic and keeps traction control at its 
most conservative. It is where a rider new to a 300 will spend their first weeks  and 
makes the daily ride a great convenience.  
 Rain retains the softer throttle response and raises ABS protection for slippery wet or 
cold surfaces.  
 Tour opens up the full linear  power  delivery  for motorway s and mountain- pass riding, 
with cruise control and the quickshifter making long rides to travel  distances, explore 
places with no fatigue.  
 Rally gives the throttle to respond with its full potent , cuts traction control intervention 
and allows rear ABS to be switched off for loose surfaces , leaving the full control and 
fun at rider’s desire 
For a returning rider, the mode switch replaces the mechanical symphony  that once had to 
be learnt  by feel. For an A2 rider, it is a safety net that comes off gradually, at their own 
pace.  
Vimal Sumbly, SVP and Business Head, TVS Premium and TVS Racing said, “ The RTX 
300 was built by a team that has spent years making premium motorcycles for riders who 
use them hard and every day. Europe asks different questions of a machine, and answering 
them meant changing the suspension and the electronics. The standard we build to is the 
same in Chennai as it is in Tuscany.”  
Warranty and support  
The RTX 300 is supplied with a five- year manufacturer’s warranty and two years of 
roadside assistance.  
 
 Technical specifications  
Engine and performance. Inclined single cylinder, 4 valves, DOHC, liquid -cooled, with 
electronic throttle. Displacement 299 cc. Maximum power 36  PS (26,5  kW). Maximum 
torque 28.5 Nm. Six -speed gearbox with assist and slipper clutch. Four ride modes: Urban, 
Rain, Tour and Rally.  
Chassis and dimensions. High -strength steel tubular trellis frame with cast aluminium 
swingarm. Suspension by  WP, 41 mm upside- down front fork and rear m onoshock, fully 
adjustable front and rear, with 180 mm of travel at both ends. Braking by Ø 320 mm front 
disc with radial -mount caliper and Bosch dual -channel ABS, and Ø 240 mm rear disc with 
floating caliper. Wheels 19- inch front and 17 -inch rear, alloy rims with mixed- use tyres. Kerb 
weight 180 kg. Ground clearance 200 mm. Seat height 835 mm. Wheelbase 1,428 mm. 
Length, width and height 2,176 by 884  by 1,400 mm. Fuel tank 12.5 litres.  
Two variants 
The RTX 300 is offered in two equipment levels, Essential and Adventurer  - 
RTX 300 Essential. Designed for everyday mobility and spontaneous adventures. The 
Essential captures the core of the RTX experience, smart technology and everyday 
versatility, for riders who want every journey to be more than routine. It comes with four ride modes, a 5 -inch TFT display, cruise control, traction control, switchable ABS and a full LED 
lighting system. Available in Lightning Black and Pearl White.  
Available from November 2026. Price €4,890.  
 RTX 300 Adventurer. For riders who rarely take the shortest route. The Adventurer adds 
equipment developed for longer distances and rougher ground, for riders who treat every 
journey as an invitation to go further. It carries everything in the Essential specification and 
adds  a bidirectional Q uick shifter and tyre pressure monitoring, advanced suspension 
adjustment and premium finishes, a brass -coated chain, enhanced connectivity and 
dedicated touring accessories. Available in Viper Green.  
Available from November 2026. Price €5,290.
