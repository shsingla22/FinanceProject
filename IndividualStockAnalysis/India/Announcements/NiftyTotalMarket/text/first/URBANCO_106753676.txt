Dear Sir/ Ma’am,  
 
This is in reference to a defamation and disparagement suit filed by the Company against Kent RO Systems 
Limited (‘ Kent RO ’) in the Hon’ble Delhi High Court (“ Delhi HC ”) on August 11, 2026.  Kent RO launched a 
concerted advertising campaign featuring several advertisements and social media content through various 
influencers, premised on false and misleading claims pertaining to the 2 -year filter life and 2 -year service  
life feature offered by the Company for its Native M0, M1, M2, M1 Pro and M2 Pro water purifiers (“ NATIVE ”). 
Kent RO’s advertisements falsely stated that the 2 -year filter life and 2 -year service life feature of NATIVE 
water purifiers, amongst other things, is a “marketing gimmick” and that using NATIVE water purifiers is 
“unsafe” and “risky” for consumers.  
 
Accordingly, the Company sued Kent RO before the Delhi HC, on the basis that Kent RO’s advertisements 
disparaged Urban Company and its NATIVE water purifiers and  also defamed them. The case was heard 
at length by the Hon’ble Delhi High Court on August 12, 2026.  
 
After a very detailed hearing in the matter, Kent RO made a statement before the Delhi HC that it would pull 
down the offending advertisements that are subject matter of the suit, and will not run any other 
advertisement or promotional content making the s ame or any other similar claims regarding water purifiers 
offering a 2 -year filter life/2 -year no -servicing feature, that would be disparaging of Urban Company. Kent 
RO has been asked to pull down the advertisements and social media content within 15 days of August 12, 
2026. While the Delhi HC passed an order on the same date of hearing i.e. August 12, 2026 - it was published 
on its website yesterday i.e. August 22, 2026.  
 
There are other pending matters between the Company and Kent RO, including a patent infringement suit 
filed by Kent RO (and a counterclaim filed by the Company against Kent RO’s patent) and a suit for tortious 
interference filed by the Company against Kent  RO, which are still sub -judice .
