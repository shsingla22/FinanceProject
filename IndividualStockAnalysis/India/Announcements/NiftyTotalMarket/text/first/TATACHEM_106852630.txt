7 November 202 6: Tata Chemicals Limited today declared its financial results for the quarter and 
half year ended September  30, 202 6. 
 
Commenting on the results, R. Mukundan, Managing Director & CEO, Tata Chemicals Limited , 
said , “During Q2FY27, Tata Chemicals Limited delivered a resilient operating performance in a 
challenging global environment.  Higher sales , production volumes , improved operating efficiencies 
and disciplined cost management helped mitigate the impact of continued pressure on soda ash 
prices and elevated input , energy and logistics costs.  
 
Market conditions for soda ash remain challenging, with global oversupply, subdued demand and 
evolving trade flows continuing to weigh on prices and realisations. Exports from the US to 
Southeast Asia were particularly impacted by unremunerative pricing. I n this environment, our 
immediate priorities remain operational excellence, structural cost reduction, cash generation and 
disciplined capital allocation.  
 
Despite a resilient standalone operating performance, our consolidated results were impacted by 
a sharp rise in UK natural gas costs . The prolonged Middle East conflict continued to drive inflation 
in key inputs, including limestone and CO₂, as well as energy, freight, and logistics costs across our 
operations. Additionally, the temporary suspension of Kenya operations weighed on conso lidated 
performance.  
 
We continue to advance our LIFE  strategy , progressively shaping the portfolio towards 
sustainability -led, application -focused and less cyclical businesses, while strengthening the 
competitiveness of our core operations.  
 
In Living Essentials , the demand outlook remains positive, supported by premiumisation and 
increasing consumption across food, feed, pharmaceutical and beverage applications. Salt and 
bicarbonates are expected to provide stable growth, while prebiotics offer attractive long -term 
opportunities driven by increasing consumer focus on health and wellness.  
 
In Industrial Essentials , particularly soda ash, the near to medium  term  outlook remains 
challenging due to excess global capacity, weak pricing and geopolitical and cost pressures. We 
will remain focused on improving efficiency, optimising our global manufacturing and supply chain 
footprint, and serving markets where we can generate sustainable value.  
 
In Farm Essentials , uneven rainfall and delayed sowing affected demand during parts of the 
quarter, although Kharif acreage subsequently recovered. The broader agricultural outlook remains 
cautiously positive, supported by healthy crop coverage, improving irrigation, policy  support, 
technology adoption and resilient rural demand. However, weather volatility, crop -price 
movements and elevated input costs remain key near -term risks.
