Sub: Update on new manufacturing facility at Bharuch  
It may please be recalled that the Company vide intimation dated 16 November, 2022 had 
intimated about setting up of new manufacturing facility at Bharuch, Gujarat to manufacture 
Spherical Roller Bearings and Cylindrical Roller Bearings and components thereof (hereinafter 
referred to as ‘Project’). It was also intimated that the Company would invest approx. Rs. 600 
crores in Project and commercial production from Project was expected to commence from 
January, 2025.

Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we are giving following updates: 
 The Company is expecting total investment in Project around Rs. 700 crores.  
 Commercial production from Project would commence in Q4 2024-2025 i.e. January – 
March, 2025.
