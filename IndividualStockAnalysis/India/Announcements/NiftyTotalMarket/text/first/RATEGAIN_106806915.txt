Bhanu Chopra:  Thanks, Mukesh. FY25-26 was the year RateGain became a 
structurally different company. A year ago, I stood before you and 
said we were making a deliberate choice to become AI-first, and that 
FY26 would be a year of investment. Both of those things have 
happened, and the company that emerged at the end of the year is 
larger, more global, and better-positioned than the one that started 
it. 
 The most consequential decision of the year was the acquisition of 
Sojern in November. We paid USD280 million, related to USD30 
million earn-outs, for a business that gave us something no 
competitor has, which is the largest pool of travel intent data in the 
industry. Bringing Sojern together with Adara has created a 
platform that can tell a hotel or an airline not just what to price, but 
who to reach and when.

 The integration has been delivered ahead of plan. We've realized 
USD15 million of annualized cost savings within the first 100 days. 
We completed the customer migration onto a single platform, and 
now we operate as a single company rather than two businesses 
running side by side.  
 Our financial performance reflects that step-change in scale. 
Operating revenue for the year grew 69.4% to INR1,823.6 crores. 
Adjusted EBITDA grew 54.4% to INR358.3 crores, and adjusted 
profit after tax grew 19.6% to INR249.9 crores. Reported profit was 
lower than the previously year because of other income, 
amortization, and one-time costs associated with the acquisition, 
and Ankit will take you through that in detail.  
 What sits underneath those numbers is a business generating 
strong free cash flow, which is why we've been able to repay a 
significant portion of the acquisition debt well ahead of the 
committed schedule. 
 The year also validated the product choices we had made. UNO now 
brings our channel manager, reservation system, booking engine, 
and VIVA voice agent into a single stack for hotels.  
 VIVA handles live reservation calls in more than 50 languages, 
answering guest queries, quoting rates, confirming bookings, and 
managing upsells without adding to front-desk workflow. Sojern's 
AI concierge has gone live across Red Roof's portfolio in the US.  
 In Distribution, we've launched Agentic ARI and RateIQ, industry-
first offerings that solve real problems for our customers. Agentic 
ARI makes UNO the first channel manager built on intelligent 
distribution logic, and hotels are seeing up to 30% to 40% 
optimization in ARI traffic alongside meaningfully better rate 
accuracy. RateIQ uses AI to show large chains where revenue is 
leaking across their channels and quantifies the commercial impact.
