MEDIA RELEASE  
 
Riju Jhunjhunwala appointed as Chairman of Malana Power and AD 
Hydropower  
 
• Mr. Ravi Jhunjhunwala will continue to serve on both Boards, providing continuity and guidance.  
  
• MPCL (Operates 86 MW Malana Hydroelectric Project)  and ADHPL (operates the 192 MW Allain 
Duhangan Hydro Power Project) are part of the LNJ Bhilwara Group through HEG Advanced 
Materials.  
 
October 26, 2026: The Boards of Malana Power Company Limited (MPCL) and AD Hydropower Limited 
(ADHPL),  have appointed Mr . Riju Jhunjhunwala as Chairman  of the Board of each company, subject to 
approval of the Board of HEG Advanced Materials Limited.  Both are LNJ Bhilwara Group companies 
owned through HEG Advanced Materials Limited.  
The appointments were approved at the meetin gs of the respective Boards held on October 26, 2026, 
following the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of MPCL. Mr. Ravi 
Jhunjhunwala will continue to be the MD of MPCL and serve on both Boards, providing continuity and 
guidance.  
Mr. Riju Jhunjhunwala is also the Chairman, Managing Director and CEO of HEG Advanced Materials 
Limited, which houses the LNJ Bhilwara Group’s advanced battery materials, battery energy solutions 
and green power businesses. His appointment brings the leade rship of the Group’s hydropower 
companies in line with HEG Advanced Materials.  
Commenting on this,  Mr. Riju Jhunjhunwala, Chairman, Malana Power Company Limited and AD 
Hydropower Limited, said, “MPCL and ADHPL are among the Group’s longest -standing clean e nergy 
assets, and the speed with which Malana was built remains a benchmark for hydropower in India. 
Hydropower provides the kind of reliable, renewable generation that will become more valuable as 
India adds more solar and wind capacity. I look forward to  working with the Boards and the teams to 
run these assets well and to strengthen their role within HEG Advanced Materials’ green power 
platform.”  
 
 
Mr. Ravi Jhunjhunwala said, “MPCL and ADHPL were built in some of the most challenging terrain in 
the country, and their teams have run them with real commitment, delivering reliable clean power 
for years. With Riju as Chairman, the companies will build further on that strong foundat ion within 
HEG Advanced Materials. I remain fully engaged with both Boards and look forward to supporting their 
next phase of growth.”  
Malana Power Company Limited (MPCL) is a hydropower company of the LNJ Bhilwara  Group, owned 
through HEG Advanced Materials  Limited. It operates the 86 MW Malana Hydroelectric Project, a run -
of-river plant in the Kullu district of Himachal Pradesh. Construction began in January 1999, and the 
project was commissioned in 2001 in a reco rd 30 months. The plant generates an average of 350 GWh 
of electricity a year.  
AD Hydro Power Limited (ADHPL), a subsidiary  of Malana Power Company Limited, also owned through 
HEG Advanced Materials Limited operates the 192 MW Allain Duhangan Hydroelectric  Project located 
near Manali in the Kullu District of Himachal Pradesh, India. Commissioned in September 2010, the run -
of-the-river project harnesses the hydrologic potential of the Allain and Duhangan rivulets.
