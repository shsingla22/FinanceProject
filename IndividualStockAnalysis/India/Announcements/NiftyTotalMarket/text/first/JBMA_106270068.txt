June 16, 2025 | Hamburg, Germany: JBM Electric Vehicles (JBM EV), a global leading electric bus 
manufacturer and EV Ecosystem player launched its all-electric city bus - ECOLIFE today at the ongoing UITP 
Summit 2025 in Hamburg, Germany. This also marks JBM Electric Vehicles’ foray into the European Union 
region with an array of products & solutions specifically tailored towards providing end-to-end e-mobility 
ecosystem solutions. The bus was unveiled by Mr. Nishant Arya, Vice Chairman, JBM Group. JBM Electric 
Vehicles is the flagship company of the $3.0 billion global conglomerate, JBM Group. 
As part of its strategic expansion into the European market, JBM EV has 
partnered with KazenMaier as its preferred German leasing partner. 
This partnership marks a significant milestone in JBM EV’s commitment 
to sustainable mobility solutions, with KazenMaier set to cooperate 
with JBM EV in providing state-of-the-art electric buses and EV 
Ecosystem solutions for the German market. 
Speaking at the occasion, Mr. Nishant Arya, Vice Chairman, JBM Group 
said, " Our mission is to decarbonize public transport by creating new 
and sustainable global benchmarks. At JBM EV, we believe that the 
future of green mobility lies in creating vehicles that seamlessly blend 
innovation, sustainability and user-centric design. Our electric mobility 
solutions are not just about reducing carbon footprints—they are about 
elevating the quality of life for individuals and communities by making 
transportation smarter, safer, and more accessible”. 
“This year we have successfully completed a decade in the public 
mobility space. We envision to serve over 20 billion passengers and 
clock 3 billion e-kilometres in next 3-4 years, delivering safe, reliable 
and unforgettable experiences to the world”, he further added. 
Mr. Max Nastold, CEO, Kazenmaier Leasing said, "As a leasing 
company with a strong focus on green asset financing, we found in JBM 
Electric Vehicles a great partner to help build the ecosystem for the 
future of mobility. Our vision is to finance the mobility of tomorrow — 
and with JBM EV, we’ve found the right partner for this mission. 
Together, we are driving sustainable mobility in public transport. The 
key lies in the turnkey solution that combines the bus, charging 
infrastructure, and leasing. JBM’s technology and our leasing expertise 
ensure easy access to zero-emission bus solutions." JBM EV’s SDG Focus

In a strategic move to strengthen its European operations, JBM EV has established its European headquarters 
in Frankfurt, Germany. This hub will serve as the company’s central base for marketing & sales, aftersales, 
operations and partnerships across the EU. The European business is led by Mr. Daniel Frade, Director - Sales, 
Marketing & After Sales — Europe, a seasoned industry expert who brings deep experience in mobility 
solutions and international market development. 
JBM’s disruptive EV Ecosystem — JBM E-Verse encompasses in-house capabilities in fast charging 
infrastructure, efficient Lithium-ion batteries, power infrastructure and a network that ensures zero 
downtime. The E-Verse demonstrates the company's commitment towards providing end-to-end e-mobility 
solutions, designed in India for the world. 
JBM Electric Vehicles brings to the table an extensive experience of 11000 electric buses deployed and under 
execution in various geographies across the globe. The company has set up the world's largest (outside China) 
dedicated integrated electric bus manufacturing facility, located in India, with an annual production capacity 
of 20,000 electric buses. 
Over the last 5 years, JBM electric buses have clocked 165+ million e-kms having catered to over 1 billion 
passengers having saved over 350+ mn litres diesel and 1+ billion kgs CO; till date. The company has 
pledged to clock over 3 billion e-kms and catering to 10+ billion passengers in next 3-4 years. JBM electric 
buses homogenized to European standards have been designed around the core product ethos of Safety, 
Performance and Passenger Comfort. 
JBM EV’s product and capability showcase at the UITP Summit this year is in true conferment with the 
company’s Net Zero 2040 commitment. Aptly aligned with the United Nation’s Sustainable Development Goal 
#3 - Good Health & Well Being, SDG#9 - Industry innovation & infrastructure, SDG#11 - Sustainable Cities & 
Communities and SDG#13 — Climate Action, the company has also showcased JBM E-Verse, an indigenously 
designed EV ecosystem platform. 
Safety — Our Top Priority: 
JBM ECOLIFE is equipped with the proprietary Advanced Driver-Assistance System (ADAS) technology that 
assists the driver detect obstacles and checks driver errors for utmost safe transit. A host of other additional 
safety features include, Advance Emergency braking system (AEBS), Forward vehicle collision warning 
systems (FVCWS), Lane Departure Warning System, Pedestrian and cyclist collision warning (MOIS), Blind 
spot detection, protection of vehicle against cyber-attack, Intelligent Speed assistance, alcohol interlock 
installation facilitation, driver drowsiness, attention warning and advance driver distraction warning and Fire 
detection & suppression system FDSS, 360 degrees camera, etc. 
The ECOLIFE citybus comes with a light weight monocoque full stainless steel body structure and equipped 
with state-of-the-art features including Intelligent Transport System as per EU norms, advanced telematics 
giving 24x7 access to vehicle’s state of health amongst host of other features. 
JBM has been a pathbreaker in the e-mobility segment and has redefined public transportation through its 
best-in-class, modern and technologically superior electric buses that have established benchmarks in 
passenger comfort, safety, affordability, and innovation, reflecting the key ethos of JBM products. All the 
four types of buses are equipped with a series of customisable options and adaptable features.

About JBM Electric Vehicles (P) Ltd. 
JBM Electric Vehicles (P) Ltd., part of the $3.0 bn Indian conglomerate, JBM Group, is the leading electric bus 
and EV ecosystem player globally. The company specialises in manufacturing ultra sophisticated and 
technologically superior zero emission electric buses that have established class leading benchmarks across 
various geographies globally. JBM EV is spearheading the global shift towards electric mobility and the 
company currently offers an extensive portfolio of e-buses ranging from city, intercity, luxury coach, staff, 
school, tarmac and special purpose vehicles. For more info, please visit www.jbmbuses.com.
