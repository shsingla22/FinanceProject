Sterling  Forays into Jharkhand with B usiness Hotel in Steel City of India – Bokaro  
• First Pan -Indian  hospitality  brand in Bokaro .  
• Featuring 74 upscale and well -appointed rooms and suites . 
• 6000 sq.  ft. indoor Banquet Hall –  The l argest in the c ity. 
• Chic and Stylish Ballroom s – ideal for meetings and social events . 
• 3 diverse and popular dining venues for a range of culinary experiences . 
14/10/2024  Sterling Holiday Resorts  – India’s leading leisure hospit ality brand  has made its debut in 
Jharkhand with its hotel, Sterling City Centre Bokaro . Located within the SAIL city  in the heart of Bokaro, 
within close distance of all important corporates and landmarks of the city , the property c onnect s 
seamlessly with the major business sectors  in Bokaro &  other industrial areas in Jharkhand like Ranchi, 
Dhanbad  and Sindri.  
Sterling is the first pan -India hospitality brand in Bokaro and brings its signature warmth and award -
winning hospitality to the St eel city.  The hotel provides a wide range of rooms and suites ranging from 
200 sq.ft to up to 730 sq.ft., all with upscale and chic décor  and fully equipped and with all modern 
amenities  – making  it the ideal destination for business travelers on short or long stays and for guests 
using Bokaro as a base to explore the region.  
Conveniently located within SAIL city and closest to the airport, the hotel is the ideal venue and epicenter  
for corporates and leading business  houses c onverging for events and conferences . The hotel boasts of 
the largest indoor banqueting venue in the city, ‘Conventia,’ which spans 6,000 square feet and is ideal 
for conferences, exhibitions, and social gatherings. Additional versatile spaces, including t he Alora 
Ballroom, Atrium Lounge, and Outdoor Lawns, cater to a variety of events, from intimate meetings to 
grand celebrations.  
Guests can indulge in a diverse culinary experience across three distinct dining venues. Aurum, a popular 
pub, offers a live DJ  and an extensive selection of premium spirits. The Hub serves as a stylish coffee shop 
featuring fresh bakes and casual fare, while Melange presents an upscale dining experience with a curated 
selection of global cuisines in both indoor and outdoor settin gs. 
"The introduction of Sterling City Centre Bokaro marks our first  in the vibrant  state of Jharkhand. This 
strengthens our presence in the “ Bleisure ” (business -cum -leisure) segment –  following our hotels running 
successfully in Madurai, Dehradun, Haridwar etc. Bokaro is at the core  of Jharkhand's industrial hub , and 
our location here  positions us to cater to the evolving needs of business around the city and the state ." 
said Mr. Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director  & CEO , Sterling Holiday Resorts.  
“We are delighted to collaborate with Sterling Holiday Resorts through our partner, Morpho Hotels. 
Sterling City Centre Bokaro is a testament to Bokaro’s warmth  and traditional hospitality. As a landmark 
property in SAIL City the hotel has commanded respect and admiration from many businesses and locals 
alike, especially for our large banquet venues, trendy restaurants and pubs. With a whole new renewed 
product, upgraded rooms and the association of Sterling, I am confident about the boost to our hotel, it 
will make Sterling City Centre Bokaro a preferred hotel for both business and leisure ,” said Mr. Deepak 
Gupta, Owner Hans Regency.  
Apart from Its proximity to Bokaro Airport and local shopping malls , the hotel ensures ease of access for 
business travelers to major business centers such as ONGC, SAIL, and Siemens Ltd.  and leisure travelers 
 
to explore notable local attractions, including the Baba Baid yanath Jyotirling , the City of Waterfalls – 
Ranchi, and the Holy Parasnath Hills in Giridi h.
