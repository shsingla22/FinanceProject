Dear Sir/Madam,  
We wish  to inform you that Dalmia Cement  (Bharat)  Limited  (‘DCBL ’) wholly  owned subsidiary  of 
the Company,  has received  order  on August 28, 2024 at 3:00  P.M.  under  Section  73 of the Central 
Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and Delhi Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 from  the 
Assistant Commissioner, Central GST, Division - Cannaught Place, New Delhi  confirming  demand 
for allegedly passing duplicate Input Tax C redit  (‘ITC’) and for availing ineligible ITC due to              
non-payment of GST by supplier  for the period 2019 -20. 
 
The authority  has confirmed  demand of- 
1) tax amounting  to Rs. 81.10 Lacs  along  with interest; and 
2) penalty  amount of  Rs. 8.20 Lacs.  
 
The matter is contestable before the Appellate Authorities and DCBL will file an appeal against 
the said order before the Joint Commissioner (Appeals) within prescribed timelines.
