Schaeffler India appoints Amit Bhalerao as Chief 
Operating Officer  
 
 
 
• Amit Bhalerao b rings  over two decades of global manufacturing leadership 
to strengthen Schaeffler India's operations, effective August 10, 2026  
Pune , India  | August  07, 2026 | Schaeffler India Limited (BSE: 505790, NSE: 
SCHAEFFLER), the Motion Technology Company, today announced the 
appointment of Amit Bhalerao as its Chief Operating Officer (COO), effective 
August 10, 2026. In this role, Amit will lead the company's manufacturing 
operations across India and drive operational excellence, manufacturing 
strategy, digital transformat ion and capability development, while 
strengthening Schaeffler India's customer -centric approach and long -term 
growth agenda.  
As a member of Schaeffler India's Executive Leadership Team, Amit will 
work closely with business and functional leaders to shape the company's 
manufacturing footprint, enhance operational performance, accelerate 
localisation initiatives, and build future -ready manufacturing capabilities 
across its plants.  
Welcoming Amit to the leadership team,  Harsha Kadam, Managing 
Director & Chief Executive Officer, Schaeffler India Limited , said:  
"India continues to be a strategic growth market for Schaeffler, and 
strengthening our manufacturing and operations capabilities is central to our 
journey as the leading Motion Technology Company. Amit brings extensive 
experience in leading complex manufacturing operations, driving operational 
excellence and building high -performing teams. His leadership will further 
enhance our manufacturing competitiveness, customer focu s and innovation 
capabilities as we continue delivering greater value to our customers and 
stakeholders.  We wish Amit the very best and many successes in his journey at 
Schaeffler in India. " 
Amit brings over 23 years of global leadership experience across 
manufacturing, operations, business excellence, lean transformation, 
quality and supply chain management.

Schaeffler India brings a n 80-year global legacy of innovation in motion technology to the Indian market,
