 Performance  Highlights  for Q2’FY2 7 
 
▪ Revenue from Operations for Q 2’FY2 7 grew by 17.6 % to ₹ 667.8  Cr as compared to        
₹ 567.9 Cr in Q2’FY2 6.  
 
• Sustained revenue growth was driven by:   
− Volume growth, both in domestic and export markets  
− Capacity addition (Y -o-Y) and successful new product launches  
− Marginally higher ASPs driven by calibrated pricing actions to partially offset raw material inflation  
• Inventory damage due to exceptionally heavy rainfall and flooding, led to a sales growth loss of estimated 30 0–350 bps during Q2’FY27  
 
▪ EBITDA  for Q2’FY2 7 moderated by  16.8% to ₹ 82.8 Cr as compared  to ₹ 99.5 Cr in 
Q2’FY2 6. EBIDTA margin for Q 2’FY2 7 stood a t 12.4% as compared to 17. 5% in  
Q2’FY2 6. EBITDA Margin improved sequentially from 12.3% in Q1 ’FY27 . 
 
• Consumption margins improved sequentially, supported by softer raw material 
prices during the early part of the quarter, partially offset by a sharp increase in input costs during the latter half.  
• Benefits from improved consumption margins were partly offset by higher employee expenses arising from temporary under -absorption of fixed 
overheads of Reynolds Team due to transitory impact in commencement of 
production, marginally affecting EBITDA margin s. 
 
▪ PAT for Q 2’FY2 7 moderated  by 37.0% to ₹ 38 .3 Cr as compared to ₹  60.9 Cr in Q2’FY2 6. 
PAT margin for Q 2’FY2 7 stood at 5.7% as compared to  10.7% in  Q2’FY2 6.  
• Profit After Tax was impacted higher primarily due to exceptional expense  of         
₹ 10.4 Cr arising from damages caused by heavy rainfall and flooding.  
• Relevant insurance claims  have been lodged, and the associated recovery is 
expected to substantially offset the financial impact in due course.  
 
 
Performance  Highlights  for H1’FY27  
 
 Revenue from Operations for H1’FY27 grew by 18.4 % to ₹ 1,338.3  Cr as compared to                         
₹ 1,130.2  Cr in H1 ’FY26 , in line with our yearly guided range . 
 
 EBITDA  for H1’FY27 moderated by  16.6%  to ₹ 165.3  Cr as  compared  to ₹ 198.3 Cr in

H1’FY26.  
 
 PAT for H1’FY27 moderated by 30.3% to ₹ 83.6 Cr as compared to ₹ 120.0 Cr in

Results  Release   
 
Commenting on the results and performance, Mr. Santosh Raveshia, Managing Director, 
DOMS Industries Limited said: 
 
 
"We reported another quarter of sustained growth, with revenue increasing by 17.6% year- on-
year in Q2 ’FY27 and 18.4% in H1 ’FY27. This growth was supported by healthy volume expansion 
across both domestic and export markets, reflecting the strength of our brands, the benefits of ongoing capacity expansion, and a continued positive market response to our new product 
launches.  
 Growth during the quarter was broad- based, with all our key categories delivering positive 
performance. Our Scholastic Stationery, Scholastic Art Material and Office Supplies businesses 
continued to benefit from healthy underlying demand, while Hobby & Cra ft maintained strong 
momentum, supported by portfolio expansion and increased manufacturing capacity. The Back -
to-School category also delivered robust growth.  
 
The quarter also presented certain external challenges. Unusually heavy rainfall and flooding in 
Gujarat affected sales growth by an estimated 300 –350 basis points. In addition, raw material 
prices remained volatile amid ongoing geopolitical uncertainties.  Despite these headwinds, we 
maintained our EBITDA margin at 12.4%, in line with the previous quarter.  
 
I am pleased to share that we have achieved a significant milestone with the commissioning of 
the first building at our new 50 -acre manufacturing facility. Dedicated to Reynolds pen 
production, this building is expected to begin contributing to revenues from Q3 ’FY27 onwards, 
marking an important step in our long -term capacity expansion roadmap.  
 Looking ahead, we remain encouraged by the positive demand environment across our key 
categories and markets and remain confident of delivering our guided consolidated sales growth 
of 18 –20% for FY27. "

About  DOMS  Industries  Limited:  
 
 
DOMS Industries Limited (“DOMS”) is one of India’s largest manufacturers and marketers of Stationery and Art products company. The Company designs, develops, manufactures and sells a broad portfolio of products across eight core categories within the Stati onery and Art products 
- Scholastic Stationery, Scholastic Art Material, Paper Stationery, Kits and Combos, Office 
Supplies, Back to School, Hobby and Craft and Fine Art Products. Recently , DOMS expanded into 
the baby hygiene segment through the acquisition of Uniclan Healthcare Private Limited, advancing its strategy to grow into adjacent categories that serve the evolving needs of children and young consumers.  
 
The Company’s products are primarily sold under the flagship brand ‘DOMS’, as well as through 
other brands/ sub -brands, like Reynolds, C3, Amariz , FixyFix  and Wowper and associate brand 
ClapJoy . The Company’s multi -channel distribution network is spread domestically across 28 
states and 8 UTs of India as well as in 5 5+ countries globally covering US, Middle East & Africa, 
Asia Pacific, Europe  and  Australia .
