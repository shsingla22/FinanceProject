ii. TDS to be deducted at higher rate in case of non-filers of Return of Income (Section 206AB):  
The Finance Act, 2021, has inter alia  introduced special provisions vide Sections 206AB of the Act, which 
is effective from July 1, 2021. Accordingly, tax at higher of the following rates would be deducted from the

a. At twice the rate specified in the relevant provision of the Act; or

c. At the rate of 5%.

functionality for determining whether the shareholder is a 'Specified person' as per Section 206AB 
or not. The Company will be using functionality of the Inco me Tax department to determine the 
applicability of Section 206AB of the Act.
