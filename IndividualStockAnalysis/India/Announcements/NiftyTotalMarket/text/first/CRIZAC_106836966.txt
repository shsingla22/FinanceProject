Commenting on the results, D r. Vikash Agarwal , Managing Director, Crizac Limited , said : 
 
Q2 FY27 was shaped by external disruption to global student mobility, as international travel disruption and flight cancellat ions 
between February and June, along with currency movements in key source markets, led a number of students, mainly postgraduate s, 
to defer their plans this year. We reported Total  Income of ₹1, 112 million, Operating EBITDA of ₹330 million and PAT of ₹369 
million. Even though Total  Income declined by 3 4.5% YoY, the Board has declared an interim dividend of ₹4 per equity share, 
reflecting its confidence in our long- term fundamentals.  
We view this quarter as a one -off rather than a change in underlying demand. We continued to gain market share and grow in 
absolute terms, even as policy headwinds reduced visa volumes in our key markets, demonstrating a model built to perform through 
mark et cycles. Pent -up demand from students who deferred is already coming through in Q3 as they return for the upcoming intakes, 
and we expect this to continue into Q4.  
Our platform remains anchored by long- standing university partnerships, an established counselling partner base and scalable 
technology. Based on our application pipeline, we believe we are on track to maintain our FY26 performance in FY27. With our 
key in takes, including the peak intake quarter, falling in the second half, we expect H2 FY27 to be significantly stronger than the  
first half, and to deliver growth over H2 FY26.  
As highlighted in our Q1 investor call, the travel disruption and flight cancellations were expected to affect our Q2 perform ance. 
Sequentially, Total  Income declined from ₹2,0 84 million in Q1 FY27, reflecting seasonality and these external factors.  
We continue to pursue targeted acquisitions to broaden our destination presence and offset pressure in constrained markets. I nova 
Consultancy Limited, acquired through our wholly owned UK subsidiary, was consolidated in the current quarter. It strengthens  
our UK and European university partnerships and marks our entry into the Netherlands. Integration is progressing as planned.  
The technology investments we began deploying in March 2026, including Edumentor , are making our platform AI -ready, which 
we believe will be a lasting advantage as we grow.  
Operating  EBITDA declined by 44.0% YoY, at an Operating  EBITDA margin of 32.2%, reflecting lower Operating Income  and a 
higher cost base from team build- out, technology and expansion. PAT declined by 23.7% YoY, and by 10.7% to ₹840 million for H1 
FY27.  
Tighter visa and immigration policies are a recurring risk in our industry and have historically been cyclical. We believe we  are 
closer to the end of the current cycle, and we expect to benefit as conditions normalise . Over the longer term, structural demand for 
quality international education remains strong, underpinned by growing aspirations across India, Africa and wider Asia. We be lieve 
we are well -positioned to capture this opportunity through our diversified pres ence across 90+ source countries and 15 destination 
markets.
