Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 -  
Incident involving Data Loss  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we hereby inform you that the Bank has 
experienced an incident involving the loss of certain data.  
On 16 October 2026, the Bank received communications from its Threat Intelligence Service 
Provider, the Defence Cyber Agency, and CERT -In regarding an alert concerning the suspected 
exposure of the Bank’s customer data on the dark web.  During  the preliminary analysis of 
approximately 375,000 records available on the dark web, 3,897 customer records containing 
Personally Identifiable Information (PII) were identified. The exposed information reportedly 
included customers’ registered mobile numb ers, dates of birth, names, gender, email addresses, 
residential addresses, branch and CIF identifiers, lead IDs, and metadata associated with 
loan/lead records and other related files.  
A detailed investigation was required to establish the authenticity and completeness of the 
exposed data, determine the potential source and extent of the exposure, and assess the 
associated risks to customers and the Bank.  
The Bank promptly activated its cyber incident response and containment protocols. 
Additionally, an independent CERT -In-empanelled cybersecurity agency was engaged to 
conduct a comprehensive assessment and investigate the nature and extent of the alleged 
compromise.  
We wish to submit  that this does not have any material impact on Bank’s Core business 
operations, financial performance or business continuity.  The Bank’s core business functions 
continue to operate normally without disruptions. A detailed assessment is currently underway  
and appropriate remedial and preventive measures are being implemented as required.  
We reassure that the Bank continues to monitor the situation closely and take all appropriate 
measures in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.  
We request you to kindly take note of the above pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) 
Regulations, 2015.
