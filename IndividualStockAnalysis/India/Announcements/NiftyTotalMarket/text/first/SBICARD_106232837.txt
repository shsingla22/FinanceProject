SBI Card Partners Apollo HealthCo; Introduces Apollo SBI Card SELECT Card 
~Apollo SB/ Card SELECT card offers up to 25% value back on purchases across categories from 
Apollo 24/7 app and select Apollo Pharmacy retail stores~ 
New Delhi, 15 May 2025: SBI Card, India's largest pure-play credit card issuer and Apollo HealthCo, 
which operates Apollo Pharmacy -India's largest retail pharmacy network and Apollo 2417 -
country's leading omni-channel digital health platform, have extended their strategic partnership to 
introduce a unique, health and wellness focused co-branded credit card -Apollo SBI Card SELECT 
card, today. This one-of-its-kind premium credit card has been thoughtfully designed to address the 
evolving needs of today's health-conscious consumers, offering a compelling blend of healthcare 
savings and financial rewards. Customers can easily apply for this card digitally on the Apollo 2417 
app and opt to apply via SBI Card SPRINT, by visiting the SBI Card website SBI Card.com . Customers 
can also apply for the card at select Apollo Pharmacy stores. 
The Apollo SBI Card SELECT cardholders enjoy a rewarding shopping experience on transactions, 
including pharmacy products, health check-up packages, blood tests, and more through the Apollo 
2417 app and Apollo Pharmacy stores. Cardholders get 10% back as reward points, and additionally 
up to 15% back as health credits, giving them a total value back of up to 25%. Reward points can 
be converted into Health Credits, which are redeemable across the entire product range of Apollo 
2417 App and Apollo pharmacy stores. 
Apollo SBI Card SELECT card customers receive ~1,500 e-gift voucher as welcome benefits, 
redeemable on the Apollo 2417 app and at Apollo pharmacy stores. They also enjoy Apollo Circle 
benefits that provide priority access to services and special discounts on doctor consultations, 
diagnostic tests and pharmacy orders on Apollo platforms. Additionally, Apollo SBI Card SELECT 
card customers also get a complimentary 1-year FITPASS PRO Membership, offering access to a 
wide network of gyms, fitness classes, and wellness programs, across India. 
Ms. Salila Pande, MD & CEO, SBI Card said, "At SB/ Card, we understand the evolving spending 
needs and aspirations of today's conscious consumers who are focused on good health and well­
being. Our collaboration with Apollo HealthCo to introduce Apollo SB/ Card SELECT card is firmly 
aligned with this need. By offering thoughtful healthcare benefits and valuable rewards, we aim to 
empower our customers to stay committed to their health and wellness goals, while enjoying varied 
financial benefits."

Card, we're introducing a revolutionary financial product designed to seamlessly connect health 
and wellness with everyday living. By turning everyday spending into health investments, we're 
making access to care seamless, intuitive, and affordable. It's our way of saying that healthcare

The Apollo SBI Card SELECT card also offers various lifestyle and travel benefits. Customers can 
avail spend-based annual fee reversal upon achieving annual spends of ~ 3 lakhs. Moreover, 
customers get a 2-year Priority Pass Membership for international lounge access and four 
complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per year (maximum one per quarter), adding to the 
premium experience. Additionally, accelerated earnings of 2 reward points per ~ 100 spent on 
dining, movies, entertainment, and travel, elevating the customer's lifestyle experience on each 
transaction. 
As an annual milestone benefit, cardholders can avail free comprehensive health check-up on 
spends of ~ 50,000 within 90 days of joining and annual renewal. Also, on spending ~ 6 lakhs 
annually, customers are entitled to get a free Noise smartwatch worth~ 7,999. 
The joining and renewal fee of the Apollo SBI Card SELECT card is ~1,499 plus applicable taxes. The 
contactless card is available on both Rupay and MasterCard platforms.

About SBI Card 
SBI Cards and Payment Services Limited ("SBI Card") is a non-banking financial company that offers extensive 
credit card portfolio to individual cardholders and corporate clients which includes lifestyle, rewards, travel 
& fuel, and banking partnership cards along with corporate cards covering all major cardholder' segments in 
terms of income profile and lifestyle. The brand has a wide base of over 20 MM+ cards in force as of March 
2025. SBI Card has a diversified customer acquisition network that enables the organisation to engage 
prospective customers across multiple channels. SBI Card is a technology driven company. 
The Company is listed on National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. ("NSE") and BSE Ltd.('BSE"). 
P.S. The brand name of the company is 'SBI Card', and it is registered in the name of 'SBI Cards and Payment 
Services Limited'. The company is trading under the entity name 'SBICARD' on stock exchanges. 
About Apollo HealthCo 
Launched in 2020, Apollo HealthCo operates Apollo 2417 -India's largest digital healthcare platform -
alongside Apollo Pharmacy, the country's largest pharmacy retail network of over 6,500 stores serving users 
across 19,000+ pincodes. Through an omni-channel ecosystem, Apollo HealthCo facilitates ~1 00k daily 
medicine deliveries at home, ~300k monthly doctor consultation bookings and ~150k monthly diagnostic 
bookings to over 37 million registered users. 
In addition to its consumer-facing offerings, Apollo HealthCo also operates in the pharmaceutical distribution 
segment, managing the sourcing and distribution of pharmaceuticals, FMCG, and wellness products at scale. 
Backed by cutting-edge technology, Al-driven tools, and a trusted legacy of clinical excellence, Apollo 
HealthCo is shaping the future of omni-channel, consumer-first, integrated healthcare in India.
