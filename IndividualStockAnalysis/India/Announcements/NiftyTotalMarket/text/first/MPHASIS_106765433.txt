Mphasis and TechnoPro Holdings Sign Strategic agreement  to Accelerate AI -led 
Enterprise Transformation in Japan  
-Partnership to introduce Mphasis’ Modernize ™ and Mphasis Optimize ™ offerings to the 
Japanese market , enabling AI -powered legacy modernization and enterprise performance optimization  
   
 
 
Tokyo, Bengaluru, September  02, 2026  
Mphasis , (BSE: 526299 ; NSE: MPHASIS ), a global AI -led, platform -driven technology solutions provider,  today announced 
that they have signed an agreement  with TechnoPro Holdings , Japan's largest technology -focused engineering 
staffing  and IT solutions provider , to bring M phasis ’ AI-powered enterprise offerings  to the Japanese market. The 
partnership will bring  together TechnoPro Holdings’ extensive engineering ecosystem with Mphasis’ expertise in AI, 
enterprise modernization and digital transformation.  
As part of this collaboration, TechnoPro Holdings will introduce Mphasis Modernize and Mphasis Optimize  product lines, 
built on Mphasis TriaTM (Mphasis’ proprietary Enterprise Agency platform)  offering  to clients across Japan, enabling 
organizations to leverage artificial intelligence  that delivers a governed enterprise agency  to modernize legacy 
environments, optimize business operations, and improve enterprise decision -making. This is expected to significantly 
enhance both the speed and scale of solution delivery for their client s. It also supports the creation of a new value -
generation model, in which challenges identified by TechnoPro engineers at client sites are addressed using AI, with the 
resulting knowledge and best practices subsequently scaled across other clients and industries.  
The partnership will also bring Mphasis’ cybersecurity, compliance , and security solutions to TechnoPro Holdings’ clients, 
while allowing both companies to pursue opportunities across strategic industry verticals, initially focusing  on financial 
services and manufacturing.   
The two companies will jointly drive enterprise transformation, focusing on the following areas:  
• System Modernization - Leveraging Mphasis  Modernize ™, the companies will support legacy system renewal and 
the advancement and streamlining of software development.  
• Advancement of Business Processes and Decision -Making - Leveraging Mphasis Optimize ™, the companies will 
drive AI -based operational efficiency, decision support, and operational transformation.  
• Engineering Advancement Using AI Agents -  Leveraging Mphasis NeoIP ™ and Mphasis TriaTM,” the companies will 
build a development and operating environment in which TechnoPro’s engineers work alongside AI agents.  
These solutions share a common foundation in Mphasis’ proprietary knowledge platform, Mphasis Ontosphere ™. The 
platform is an ever -evolving and dynamic knowledge base which integrates evergreen business intelligence and AI -
assisted implementation. Mphasis NeoIP evolves into an integrated platform spine combining Ontosphere -powered 
knowledge graphs and multi -agent orchestration capabilities.  
In addition to the offerings, to support this rollout, TechnoPro Holdings will also deploy Mphasis Tri a - the company’s 
Enterprise Agency Platform, creating AI -powered software development environments for its workforce. Leveraging 
Mphasis’ specialized AI platform will accelerate software development through autonomous code generation and testing, 
while emb edding AI into core business functions including talent allocation, recruitment, sales matching and corporate 
planning. TechnoPro Holdings will also use its own operations as a large -scale AI deployment environment, with proven 
workflows subsequently packa ged into industry -specific AI solutions for customers across automotive and manufacturing 
sectors.

The collaboration comes at a time when Japanese enterprises are accelerating technology 
modernization initiatives. As organizations navigate an ageing technology landscape, talent 
shortages and increasing business complexity, they are looking to upgrade mission -critical 
systems while adopting AI to improve productivity and business agility. Mphasis, with its deep, long -standing domain 
expertise in enterprise AI, is uniquely positioned to help make that vision a reality.  
“Japan represents a significant growth  opportunity as enterprises accelerate digital transformation and AI adoption. By 
combining our enterprise AI capabilities including Mphasis NeoIP suite and Mphasis Tria Agency platform with TechnoPro 
Holdings' deep relationships across Japan's automotive, manufacturing and technology sectors , as well as targeting the 
Financial Services sector . This partnership enables us to bring our enterprise offerings to one of the world's most 
important industrial markets. Deploying Mphasis NeoIP and Mphasis Tria will give TechnoPro's engineers automated, AI -
driven software -building environments that accelerate delivery at scale ,” said Nitin Rakesh, Chief Executive Officer and 
Managing Director, Mphasis.  
“Our group has set the evolution into “Japan’s largest AI -implementation company” as a key theme of our mid -term 
management plan. TechnoPro’s greatest strength is that approximately 30,000 engineers and researchers work directly 
on-site with a wide range of  clients, giving them a deep understanding of real -world development, manufacturing, and IT 
operations. Through this partnership with Mphasis, we will combine this on -site and domain knowledge with world -class 
AI, automation, and AI -agent technologies, bui lding a framework in which every individual engineer can use AI to 
implement transformation for our clients. Rather than treating AI merely as an efficiency tool, we will use it as a weapon 
that extends our engineers’ capabilities and the value we provide,  contributing to our clients’ development productivity, 
operational advancement, and business growth ,” said Gaku Shimaoka, President, Representative Director, and CEO of 
TechnoPro Holdings .
