Sub: Monetisation of  DUDP Project  
Madam/ Sir,  
 
It is hereby informed that , the Daman Upside Development Project (DUDP), in the western offshore, located about

strong project execution, innovative use of the Drill -Deck and strong performance by drilling and Production teams.

be ramped up in a phased manner.
