Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
1. In terms of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (SEBI, LODR, 2015), we hereby inform you that the  
Company has received notice dated 27 May 2026 from BSE Ltd. (BSE) regarding non-
compliance with the provisions of Reg 17 (1), 18 (1) and 19(1)/19(2) of the SEBI 
LODR, 2015, pertaining to non-availability of required Independent Directors including 
Woman Independent Director on the Board of the Company, non-constitution of Audit 
Committee and Nomination and Remuneration Committee during the quarter ended 
31 Mar 2026. In view of the said non-compliances, a fine of ₹9,55,800/- (incl. GST) 
has been imposed by the BSE (Stock Exchange).  
 
2. In response to the aforesaid notices, the Company has submitted a letter No. 
GRSE/BD-69/CG/04/2025-26 dated 29 May 2026, that Garden Reach Shipbuilders & 
Engineers Limited is a Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSE) under the 
administrative control of Ministry of Defence (MoD), Government of India. The 
President of India acting through MoD is the Promoter of the Company. The Directors 
on to the Board (including Independent Directors & Independent Woman Director) of 
the Company are appointed by the Government of India through Presidential Orders. 
Further, non-compliance has arisen due to non-appointment of Independent Directors 
including Woman Independent Director by the Government of India. The action for 
appointment of Independent Directors including Woman Independent Director on to 
the Board of CPSEs is not in the hands of the Company and also beyond the control 
of the Company. Therefore, GRSE has requested that it should not be held liable for 
the fines imposed and that the fines may be waived-off.  
 
3. The Company continues to actively pursue the matter with the Government of 
India through Ministry of Defence (Promoter of the Company), for the timely 
appointment of the requisite number of Independent Directors to ensure compliance 
with Corporate Governance norms enunciated under the SEBI LODR, 2015.  
 
 
 
 
 
4. This is for your kind information and record.
