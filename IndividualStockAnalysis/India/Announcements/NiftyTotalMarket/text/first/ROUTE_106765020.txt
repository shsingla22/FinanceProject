Marko Erkic appointed as CEO of Masiv  
 
A proven leader in the omnichannel communications space, Marko Erkic 
joins at the helm of Masiv to accelerate CPaaS growth across LATAM and 
support Proximus Global’s mission to evolve enterprise -consumer 
engagement worldwide.  
 
Bogotá, Colombia –  September 1, 2026 –  Masivian S.A.S. (operating as ‘Masiv’), a Colombian 
CPaaS provider and a subsidiary of Route Mobile , a Proximus Global company, has announced 
the appointment of Marko Erkic as its new CEO, effective today, September 1, 2026.   
 
Marko Erkic is an experienced growth and operations leader in the digital communications, telecommunications, and technology sectors, with a strong track record across Latin America. 
He brings commercial leadership experience from the payments and infrastructure industry, 
having served as Chief Revenue Officer at both Fora Solutions and Minka. Earlier in his career, 
Marko spent ten years in senior regional leadership roles at Infobip, spanning business 
development, country management, and regional leadershi p across Latin America. This 
combination of deep industry expertise and cross- sector commercial leadership positions him 
to accelerate Masiv's growth and deepen its omnichannel offering across the region.   
 
Marko Erkic said of his appointment: "I am honored to join Masiv at such a transformative time. 
By combining Masiv's powerful omnichannel capabilities with the immense scale and resources 
of Proximus Global, we have a unique opportunity to redefine how enterprises build relationships 
with the ir audiences. I look forward to working with this talented team to build upon our CPaaS 
momentum and deliver the cutting -edge communications solutions that our customers rely on 
to grow and succeed."  
 Masiv, a leading omnichannel communications platform, forms a key part of Route Mobile's CPaaS portfolio. It empowers businesses to build smarter, data -driven relationships with their 
customers.  
 Tushar Agnihotri, CEO, Route Mobile, said: "We are very pleased to announce the appointment 
of Marko Erkic as the new CEO of Masiv. As Proximus Global continues its mission to seamlessly 
connect, protect, and engage customers worldwide, Masiv will play a pivotal role in that journey, particularly i n driving Route Mobile's strategy and accelerating our growth across the LATAM 
market. Marko's leadership and vision will help us empower more enterprises with world- class 
omnichannel solutions and solidify our position as a global CPaaS leader."  
 
About Route Mobile Limited ( https://routemobile.com/ ) (BSE: 543228; NSE: ROUTE)  
 
Established in 2004, Route Mobile Limited ("RML") is a cloud communications platform service provider catering to enterprises, over- the-top (OTT) players, and mobile network operators 
(MNO). RML's portfolio comprises solutions in messaging, voice, email, S MS filtering, analytics, 
and monetization. RML has a diverse enterprise client base across various industries, including social media companies, banks and financial institutions, e -commerce entities, and travel 
aggregators. RML is headquartered in Mumbai, India, with a global presence in Asia Pacific, the 
Middle East, Africa, Europe, and the Americas. Route Mobile is now a part of Proximus Group, a 
 
provider of digital services and communication solutions operating in Belgium and international
