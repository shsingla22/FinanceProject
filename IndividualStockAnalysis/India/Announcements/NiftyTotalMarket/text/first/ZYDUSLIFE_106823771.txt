Zylidac  Bio and Prolific  Machines  Partner to Scale Prolific’s Next -Generation  
Biologics Manufacturing  Platform to GMP Production  
Zylidac’s Emeryville facility and Prolific Machines  will partner to scale Prolific's light -controlled  
cell line development platform , designed to  deliver higher  yield,  improved  control , and enhanced 
quality for complex biologics , from laboratory development  into GMP manufacturing  
EMERYVILLE, Calif., October 2 1, 2026  – Zylidac Bio, a fully integrated biologics CDMO within 
the Zydus Lifesciences Limited (Zydus) group , and Prolific  Machines , a biotechnology company 
advancing biologics development and manufacturing through integrated optogenetics -enabled 
solutions delivering breakthrough yields, superior on -target quality, and precise process control , 
today announced a partnership to scale Prolific's platform from early development t hrough GMP 
production. Both companies are headquartered in Emeryville  in the  San Francisco Bay Area . 
Prolific ’s technology  uses light to control protein expression in engineered cell lines , enabling 
substantial gains in  productivity and product quality . The platform combines optogenetics -
enabled cell lines, illumination systems  compatible  with standard  bioreactors, AI -driven process 
optimization , and advanced analytics. The technology is particularly useful for difficult -to-
express  proteins including  multi -specific antibodies , fusion proteins, cytokines and other 
complex modalities . Prolific has reported a record -breaking productivity milestone of 21 g/L in a 
15-day intensified fed -batch CHO run for a monoclonal antibody  (mAb)  at small scale , along 
with up to three -fold titer increases for other  complex formats .  
Under the agreement, Zylidac  Bio will first complete a tech transfer of Prolific's process at 50-
liter scale to reproduce Prolific's results at the Zylidac site. The companies plan to subsequently 
scale client programs 250 liters, and into GMP manufacturing scale. Prolific's illumination 
hardware will work seamlessly with Zylidac's existing bioreactors, eliminating the need for 
customers to build new manufacturing infrastructure.  
"After demonstrating success at development scale with industry -leading yields across mAbs 
and difficult -to-express proteins,  the next  step is scaling  the technology to GMP production  for 
clinical and commercial supply ,” said Deniz Kent, Ph .D., Chief Executive Officer and Co -
Founder of Prolific. " We are very pleased to partner with  Zylidac , with a proven team and a 
great new facility in immediate proximity to our labs in Emeryville. Our scien ce and production 
teams can work on the same program in the same week, and our clients get a manufacturing 
partner that already knows the technology."  
The partnership creates an end -to-end development and manufacturing pathway for Prolific’s 
clients, spanning early development through commercial -scale supply. Programs can start with 
cell line development at Prolific and move seamlessly a short distance to Zylidac for scale -up, 
drug substance and drug product manufacturing, fill -finish, and packaging.  
     
 
"Our teams visited each other's labs early on, and it was clear the capabilities were  highly 
complementary ," said Steve Lavezoli, Chief Commercial Officer of Zylidac. "Prolific focuses on 
early -stage cell line development work, while we offer the process development, scale -up and 
GMP capacity required for later stages , including programs based on new expression 
technology like Prolific's . We built our Emeryville site to take a molecule from cell line to finished 
product. We're excited to be the team that takes this technology to production scale."  
About Prolific Machines  
Prolific advances  biologics development and manufacturing through optogenetics -enabled cell 
lines and biomanufacturing solutions. Its platform brings real -time, tunable control of protein 
expression into standard manufacturing equipment, improving yield, product quality an d process 
control for conventional and complex biologics. The platform is portable across pharma and 
CDMO facilities and supports programs from early development to scalable, low -COGS 
manufacturing through flexible service and licensing models. Learn more at prolific -
machines.com.  
About Zylidac  Bio 
Zylidac is a fully integrated biologics manufacturer specializing in monoclonal antibodies, 
bispecific antibodies, fusion proteins and other complex biologics. From its 83,000 -square -foot 
facility in Emeryville, California, Zylidac takes a molecule from ce ll line development through 
drug substance and drug product manufacturing, fill -finish, labeling and secondary packaging. 
Many team members have worked together for more than 30 years, conducting more than 40 
cell bank programs and advancing more than 30 m olecules into the clinic. Each Zylidac program 
runs at one site, under one quality system and one master services agreement, with one team 
responsible from start to finish. Learn more at zylidac bio.com.
