 Dear Sir/Madam,   
 This is further to the disclosures made by the Company in its Prospectus dated November 13, 2025 and the 
subsequent stock exchange disclosure dated December 08, 2025, wherein the Company had, inter alia, 
informed that it had entered into a Share Acquisitio n Terms Agreement ( “SATA” ) with Utkarsh Classes & 
Edutech Private Limited ( “Utkarsh Classes” ) and its respective shareholders on February 18, 2023, as 
amended from time to time  (“Transaction Documents” ), for the acquisition of up to 100% of the equity share 
capital of Utkarsh Classes in five tranches, to be completed by FY 2027 -28. The Company has completed the 
first three tranches of the acquisition and currently holds 75.50% of the equity share capital of Utkarsh 
Classes.  
 
In continuation thereof, and pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI Listing Regulations, 
we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Company, at its meeting held today, i.e., Wednesday, 
November 04, 2026, has, inter alia,  considered and approved:  
 
1. the execution of a third amendment agreement to the SATA to replace the PAT- linked valuation 
mechanism for Tranche IV with a mutually agreed fixed purchase consideration and to dispense with  
the acquisition under Tranche V, including deletion of the corresponding provisions and valuation mechanism from the SATA; and  
2. the acquisition under Tranche IV, pursuant to the Transaction Documents, of 25,599 equity shares of Utkarsh Classes, representing 12.25% of its fully diluted equity share capital, for an aggregate 
purchase consideration of INR 2,55,990 (Indian Rupees Two L akh Fifty -Five Thousand Nine Hundred 
and Ninety only), in accordance with the terms of the aforesaid Amendment Agreement, thereby 
increasing the Company's shareholding in Utkarsh Classes from 75.50% to 87.75%.  
 The meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company commenced at 0 2:45 P.M. (IST)  and concluded at 
03:37 P.M. (IST) .
