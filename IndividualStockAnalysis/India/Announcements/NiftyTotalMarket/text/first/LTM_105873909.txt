LTIMindtree’s Strategic Partnership with SAP 
Combining the collective power of Business Technology Platform, Business AI with SAP RISE and SAP GROW 
to innovate for engineer-to-order and project-centric manufacturing businesses 
Warren, New Jersey, US, Mumbai, India  – June 04, 2024 : LTIMindtree  [NSE: LTIM, BSE: 540005], a global 
technology consulting and digital solutions company , has expanded its partnership with SAP, a leading 
provider of enterprise application software, to deliver innovations for complex manufacturing industries. 
The collaboration aims to tackle the unique challenges of this sector, offering streamlined solutions that 
enhance efficiency, reduce costs, and ensure seamless upgrades. 
Today, manufacturers are building increasingly complex products, often tailored to their customers' unique 
functional requirements, while competing in an increasingly fast-paced economic environment. The alliance 
focuses on utilizing SAP’s advanced technologies, including the Business Technology Platform and Business 
AI, integrated with SAP GROW. These tools are designed to provide a foundation that simplifies upgrades, 
like moving to SAP S/4HANA, while also offering pre-built extensions for complex manufacturing processes. 
  
Vineet Moroney, SVP and Global Head of Enterprise Applications, LTIMindtree  emphasized the strategic 
nature of the partnership, "By focusing on simplifying core and transforming with Business Technology 
Platform (BTP) extensions and AI solutions, we are setting a new standard in the industry. Our extensive 
experience with complex manufacturing processes positions us uniquely to create purpose-built impactful 
solutions for our clients." 
Reflecting on the collaboration, Jan Gilg from SAP  stated, "This partnership illustrates the synergy between 
SAP’s technological prowess and LTIMindtree’s deep industry knowledge. The advantage of LTIMindtree’s 
legacy, rooted in the Larsen & Toubro Group—one of the world’s distinguished engineering, manufacturing, 
and construction firms—accelerates these capabilities, eliminating the need to build solutions from scratch. 
Together, we are crafting solutions that streamline the core experience for customers on SAP Cloud ERP. 
We strive to upgrade not just innovatively but deeply aligned with the real-world needs of the 
manufacturing sector."
