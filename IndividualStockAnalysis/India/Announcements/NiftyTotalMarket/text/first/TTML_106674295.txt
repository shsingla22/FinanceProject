TTBS Launches #RozBanateHainNayaKal, Celebrating the MSMEs Helping 
India Build Its Future Every Day 
A nationwide campaign celebrating the entrepreneurs, manufacturers, service providers, and 
innovators whose everyday efforts power India's growth story 
 
New Delhi, June 25, 2026:  As India celebrates MSME Day 2026, Tata Tele Business Services (TTBS)  has 
launched #RozBanateHainNayaKal , a campaign dedicated to celebrating the millions of micro, small 
and medium enterprises (MSMEs) whose everyday efforts are helping shape India's future.  
Across the country, MSMEs are driving economic activity, creating livelihoods, fostering innovation, 
and strengthening local communities. Their journey is rarely straightforward, as they navigate 
challenges ranging from working capital and compliance to customer acquisition and digital adoption. 
Despite these realities, they continue to move forward with resilience, determination, and an 
unwavering entrepreneurial spirit.  
The #RozBanateHainNayaKal campaign is rooted in a 
simple insight: MSMEs don't wait for the future to arrive. 
They quietly create it every single day. With this campaign, 
TTBS seeks to shine a spotlight on the often-unsung 
enterprises that keep India moving forward, recognizing 
not just their contribution to business and industry, but 
their role in shaping the nation's future.

"Behind India's progress are millions of MSMEs that innovate, unlock opportunities and contribute to 
development across sectors and communities, said Vishal Rally, Chief Revenue Officer at Tata 
Teleservices Limited.  “With #RozBanateHainNayaKal, we celebrate these entrepreneurs and 
enterprises while reaffirming our commitment to enabling their growth and success through trusted 
digital solutions."   
Through a series of digital stories, customer narratives, social media initiatives, and brand 
engagements, the campaign will spotlight the individuals and enterprises that quietly contribute to 
national growth.    
For decades, MSMEs have served as the backbone of India's economy. Today, as businesses navigate 
an increasingly digital and competitive environment, their ability to adapt, innovate, and grow has 
become even more critical to the country's aspirations.  

  
 
 
At TTBS, supporting this journey remains a key priority. Through a portfolio of connectivity, 
collaboration, cloud, security, and communication solutions, TTBS continues to help businesses 
simplify operations, improve productivity, strengthen customer engagement, and scale with 
confidence.
