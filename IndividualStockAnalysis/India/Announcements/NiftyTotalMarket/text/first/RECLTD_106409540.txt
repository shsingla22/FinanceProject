Sub: Board comments on fine levied by the Exchanges -Reg. 
This is with reference to NSE Letter and BSE Email dated August 29, 2025, regarding 
non-compliance by REC Limited ("REC" / "the Company") with the requirements 
pertaining to the composition of Board of Directors during the quarter ended June 30, 
2025 and with the composition of Audit Committee and Nomination & Remuneration 
Committee for certain period during the said quarter, as stipulated in the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations , 2015 ("Listing Regulations"). 
As advised by the Stock Exchanges, the aforesaid matter of non-complian ce was placed 
before the Board of Directors of the Company in their meeting held on September 28, 
2025, wherein the Board noted the position of non-compliances with respect to the 
composition(s) of the Board of Directors , Audit Committee and Nomination & 
Remuneration Committee and the fines imposed by the Stock Exchanges related 
therewith. 
In this regard, the Board desired that regular follow-up be made with the appointing 
authority i.e. Ministry of Power, Government of India, to expedite the process for 
appointment of requisite number of Independent Directors on the Board of REC. The 
Board also noted that the stock exchanges have been requested to waive the fines so 
imposed on the Company and also not impose any further fine/penalty in this regard, as 
the matter of appointment ofIndependent Directors is beyond the control of the Company 
and there is no violation on the part of the Company. 
Further, we would like to submit that, being a Government Company and as per Articles 
of Association of the Company , inter-alia the power to appoint Independent Directors on 
the Board of the Company is vested with President ofIndia acting through administrative 
Ministry i.e. Ministry of Power, Government ofIndia and the Company has no role in the 
appointment of Directors.
