PNG Jewellers Introduces LiteStyle in Nashik, Blending 
Tradition and Everyday Elegance 
 
Nashik, 26 September 2025:  PNG Jewellers unveiled its LiteStyle shop-in-shop in Nashik, a 
significant milestone in the brand’s growing retail network. Positioned within the existing PNG 
Jewellers store, the LiteStyle counter caters to customers seeking contemporary jewellery 
that effortlessly combines sophistication with everyday functionality. 
LiteStyle is designed for the modern woman who wants jewellery that adapts seamlessly from 
office hours to evening gatherings. Crafted with finesse, these lightweight gold and diamond 
pieces carry PNG’s legacy of trust while embracing a younger aesthetic. 
Actress Bhagyashri Limaye , who inaugurated the counter, remarked, “Jewellery is no longer 
limited to weddings and festivals. LiteStyle is about embracing your individuality every day 
with elegance. I believe Nashik’s women will love the practicality and beauty of these designs, 
which bring out quiet sophistication in daily life.”  
Customers visiting the new counter can enjoy up to 50% off on LiteStyle jewellery making 
charges as part of the launch celebrations.
