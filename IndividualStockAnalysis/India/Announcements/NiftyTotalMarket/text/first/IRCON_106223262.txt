Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 [“SEBI (LODR) Regulations”], it is hereby informed that the Ministry of 
Railways vide its order no. 2021/E(O)II/40/23 dated 7th May, 2025 ( Annexure ) has entrusted 
the additional charge of Director (Works), IRCON to Shri N.C. Karmali, IRSE, PED/GS, Railway 
Board, in addition to his own, with effect from the date of assumption of charge and until further 
orders. 
 
The Company is taking necessary action to appoint Shri N.C. Karmali (DIN:09103211) in 
compliance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the SEBI (LODR) Regulations 
and requisite disclosures in this regard will be submitted upon his assumption of charge as 
Director (Works).
