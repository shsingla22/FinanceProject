PRESS RELEASE 
 
Godawari Power & Ispat Ltd.  Unveils New Brand Identity, Heralds a New Chapter 
of Growth 
 
Key Highlights: 
 
A new chapter begins : GPIL reveals its new corporate logo at the Q2 FY2026–27 board meeting, 
marking the major milestone in its brand transformation journey. 
 
ONE Godawari, one shared ambition : The transformation aims to unify the Group's diverse 
businesses guided by the brand promise ‘Powering New Horizons.’ 
 
Raipur, 13 November 2026 : Godawari Power & Ispat Limited (GPIL) (NSE: GPIL | BSE: 532734), the 
flagship company of the Hira Group, Chhattisgarh, announced its refreshed brand identity with the 
unveiling of the new corporate logo at the Company's second-quarter FY2026–27 board meeting, 
marking an important milestone in its journey towards building a unified, future-ready brand. 
 
The logo reveals the first glimpse of Godawari's evolving corporate identity, reflecting the Group's 
journey from its established industrial foundations towards a diversified business portfolio and 
broader growth ambitions. 
 
Built around the vision of ONE Godawari, the brand transformation seeks to bring the Group's 
businesses together under a unified master brand, strengthening recognition, consistency and 
stakeholder confidence across its operations and markets. 
 
The new logo features a distinctive red motif symbolising progress, forward momentum and the 
ambition to explore new horizons. Together with the brand promise “Powering New Horizons,” it 
reflects Godawari's commitment to building on its legacy while embracing emerging opportunities. 
 
The transformation represents a natural progression from the Group's longstanding spirit of 
“Growing Stronger Together” towards a renewed identity that signals its next phase of growth.

Chairman's Statement: 
 
Commenting on this significant milestone, Shri B. L. Agrawal, Chairman & Managing Director, 
Godawari Power & Ispat Limited , said: “Godawari's journey has been built on trust, strong 
relationships and the collective efforts of our people. Over the years, we have strengthened our 
capabilities, expanded our businesses and created new opportunities for growth.” 
 
He added , “ONE Godawari is about moving forward with a shared purpose, while ‘Powering New 
Horizons’ expresses our commitment to creating lasting value for our stakeholders and contributing 
meaningfully to India's progress.” 
  
The new corporate identity will be implemented progressively across Godawari's business verticals 
and Group companies enabling a unified brand presence across its businesses.  
 
The transformation is intended to strengthen the Group's overall brand equity, improve consistency 
and create a more integrated stakeholder experience, while preserving the distinct operational 
strengths of its businesses. 
  
Powering the Next Chapter 
With established capabilities across mining, steel, ferro alloys and power, alongside growing 
interests in energy storage, healthcare, real estate and allied sectors, Godawari continues to evolve 
as a diversified business Group. 
 
The ONE Godawari transformation provides a common platform to communicate this evolution, 
bringing together the Group's industrial legacy, emerging capabilities and long-term aspirations. 
 
About Godawari Power & Ispat Limited 
Godawari Power & Ispat Limited (GPIL) is the flagship company of the Godawari Group, with 
integrated operations spanning mining, iron and steel, ferro alloys and power. The broader Group 
has interests and emerging initiatives across energy storage and other diversified sectors. Built on a 
foundation of industrial expertise, operational excellence and responsible growth, Godawari 
continues to invest in capabilities and opportunities that support its long-term ambitions.
