.
These

having

ECM

are
used
for
surveillance
,
acquisition
,
tracking
of
air
targets
and
control
of
air
defence
guns
for
effective
neutralization
.
The
system
is
modular
by
design
and
is
easy
to
deploy
,
operate
and
maintain
.
