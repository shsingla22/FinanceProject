Re: Update on matter relating to Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Board in respect of land 
held by a subsidiary of the Company  
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
In continuation of our intimation dated March 18, 2026, regarding the order passed by the Chief 
Executive Officer and Executive Member of the Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Board 
(“KIADB”) in relation to land admeasuring approximately 78 acres situated at Kadugodi Industrial Area, 
Bengaluru Urban District (“ Land”), held by Embassy East Business Park Limited, a subsidiary of the 
Company (“ EEBPL”) (hereinafter referred to as the “ KIADB Order ”), and pursuant to Regulation 30 of 
the SEBI LODR Regulations, we wish to further inform that EEBPL, in order to safeguard its interests and 
those of all stakeholders, including public shareholders at large, has filed a writ petition before the 
Hon’ble High Court of Karnataka challenging the said KIADB Order.  
 
The matter was listed before the Hon’ble High Court of Karnataka on March 26, 2026, wherein the 
learned counsel appearing on behalf of the respondents (i.e., KIADB) sought time, however upon 
submissions made by counsel for EEBPL, learned counsel for KIADB submitted that no further steps 
pursuant to the impugned KIADB Order shall be taken by KIADB / the concerned authorities until the 
next date of hearing. The matter has accordingly been adjourned to April 8, 2026.  
 
The formal order passed by Hon’ble High Court  of Karnataka, is awaited, however, in line with the 
Company’s commitment to high standards of governance and timely disclosure, and in the interest of 
all stakeholders, this update is being disseminated.  
 
Please take the same on record.
