Wipro Appoints Angan Guha as President and Chief Executive Officer for 
Americas 2 Strategic Market Unit  
 
EAST BRUNSWICK, N.J. | BENGALURU, India –  Nov. 06, 2026  – Wipro Limited  (NYSE: WIT, 
BSE: 507685, NSE: WIPRO), a leading AI -powered technology services and consulting company,  
today announced the appointment of Angan Guha as President and Chief Executive Officer for 
Americas 2 Strategic Market Unit , effective December 8, 2026 . He will report to the CEO and 
Managing Director, Srini Pallia,  and serve on the Wipro Executive Leadership Team. 
 
In this role, Angan will focus on strengthening the business and market momentum across BFSI, 
Energy & Utilities, and Manufacturing, and to accelerate growth, deepen client relationships, and 
deliver greater value to our clients in the region . 
 “We are delighted to welcome Angan to Wipro’s leadership team,” said Srini Pallia, Chief 
Executive Officer and Managing Director of Wipro Limited.  “His experience in driving 
business growth, combined with his focus on innovation and execution, will be valuable as we 
strengthen our Americas business . I am confident that his leadership will help us accelerate 
growth, deepen client relationships, and deliver greater value to our clients.”  
 
“I am honored and excited to re join Wipro at such a pivotal time for our industry ,” said A ngan 
Guha. “The evolving AI landscape presents unprecedented opportunities for businesses to 
innovate and lead. I look forward to collaborating with the talented team at Wipro and helping  
clients navigate this transformation, drive meaningful business outcomes, and build on Wipro’s 
strong consulting and technology capabilities.”  
 
Angan Guha is an experienced technology and business leader who has spent more than  three 
decades driving  growth, digital transformation and client success across BFSI, EMR and 
Manufacturing. Prior to serving as Global CEO & Managing Director of Birlasoft Ltd., Angan spent 
over 28 years at Wipro,  holding several senior leadership roles, including CEO of the Americas  2 
Business Unit and member of Wipro’s Leadership Team.  
 
Angan holds a degree in Electronics Engineering from the University of Pune and has also 
completed programs  in management from the Booth School of Business, University of Chicago.
