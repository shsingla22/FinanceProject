Vedanta Limited posts  record Q2 & H1 results  
 
Record Quarterly  PAT of ₹6,20 2 crore, up 151% YoY  
 
Record  Quarterly  EBITDA of ₹9,938 crore, up 10 6% YoY  
 
Best in Industry, ND/EBITDA ratio of 0.1x , Net debt reduced by ₹4,624 crore  
 
Mumbai,  Oct 28, 202 6: Vedanta Limited  (VEDL)  today announced its Unaudited Consolidated Results 
for the Second Quarter  and Half Year ended 3 0th Sep 2026. 
 
• Best -ever half-yearly PAT  of ₹11,49 6 crore, up 15 1% YoY  
• Record half-yearly Revenue of ₹49,26 8 crore, up 54% YoY  
• Highest -ever half-yearly EBITDA  of ₹18,407 crore, up 10 3% YoY  
• Net Debt / EBITDA improved to 0.1x, best -in-class in the industry  
• Zinc International  Gamsberg  Phase II  commissioned , total MIC capacity to 500 kt p.a. 
• FACOR : Successful  extraction of  first ton ne of underground ore from Ostapal Mines  
• Portfolio of 11 critical  mineral blocks , 5 under exploration  
• Declared 1st Interim Dividend of ₹5 per share

Business Highlights  Q2 FY27 and H1 FY27 : 
Key businesses continue to deliver strong operating performance : 
▪ Zinc India  
o Record Q2 mined metal production at 271 kt, up 5% YoY; record H1 at 539 kt, up 3% YoY . 
o Silver production at 173 t in Q2, up 20% YoY; H1 at 321 t, up 10% YoY . 
o Lowest Zinc COP post underground transition at 703 $/t, lower 29% YoY and 17% QoQ . 
▪ Zinc International  
o Mined metal production at 53  kt in Q2, down 12% YoY and up 9% QoQ; H1 at 102  kt, down 
13% YoY . 
o Gamsberg Phase II was commissioned during Q2 FY27, adding 200 ktpa of MIC capacity 
and increasing  total VZI capacity to 500 ktpa . 
▪ Copper  
o Copper India sales at 52 kt in Q2, up 16% YoY . H1 sales at 105 kt, up 9% YoY . 
o Copper International sales at 6 kt in Q2, down 74% YoY and 43% QoQ; H1 at 16 kt, down 
64% YoY, impacted by closure of the Strait of Hormuz . 
o Copper International: 1 55 KTPA  rod plant at KSA  progressing well , expected commissioning 
in Q3  FY27.  
▪ FACOR  
o Record Q2 ore production at 50 kt, up 6% YoY; record H1 at 202  kt, up 31% YoY . 
o Ferrochrome production at 24 kt in Q2, up 26% YoY; H1 at 53 kt, up 13% YoY . 
o Successful extraction of underground ore from the Ostapal Mines , to strengthen ore linkage 
security.  
 
▪ Ports (Vizag General Cargo Berth)  
o Record Q2 dispatch  volume at 1.74 MMT, up 26% YoY; H1 at 3.39 MMT, up 18% YoY .  
o Discharge volume at 1.83 MMT in Q2, up 22% YoY; H1 at 4.19 MMT, up 32% YoY .
