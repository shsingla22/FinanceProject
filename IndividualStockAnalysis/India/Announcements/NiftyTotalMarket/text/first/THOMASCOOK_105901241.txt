Thomas Cook India Group wins  CNBC-TV18 I ndia Risk Management 
Awards for the 6th time 
 
 
Mumbai, July 2, 2024 : Thomas Cook (India) Limited - India’s leading omni channel travel services company has 
been honoured with the award for ‘Masters of Risk –  Travel & Hospitality Sector’  at the CNBC -TV18 India Risk 
Management Awards , 2024.  
 
The India Risk Management Awards  acknowledge and recognize companies that have developed best -in-class 
risk management practices and capabilities across business categories . This yea r, the Awards spotlighted India's 
resilience amidst geopolitical uncertainties, highlighting strategies to navigate an evolving landscape of risks effectively . The stringent selection process comprised of analysis of company data, systems and frameworks and 
was judged by an independent panel of industry experts.  
 
Mr. Madhavan Menon, Executive Chairman, Thomas Cook (India) Limited  said, “ At the Thomas Cook India  
Group, our strong enterprise risk management framework is fundamental to our business practices . My Team 
and I , are honoured to accept the India Risk Management Awards , 2024 from CNBC -TV18 and ICICI -Lombard. 
This noteworthy accolade for the sixth time, serves to reiterate our Company’s commitment and delivery in  
building forward- thinking and resilient strategies in risk management .”
