PRESS RELEASE 
Cyient Semiconductors Secures Strategic  
Investment from GlobalFoundries  
Hyderabad, India – November 19, 2026:  Cyient Semiconductors, a global 
semiconductor company specializing in custom  silicon solutions, announced that it has 
approved a USD 10 million strategic equity  investment by GlobalFoundries (GF), a 
leading manufacturer of essential semiconducto rs, enabling AI at scale from the cloud 
to the physical world.   The transaction will be executed at the same pre-money equity 
valuation of USD 490 million as the Cyient Semiconductors' recent financing round led 
by Edelweiss, resulting in a combined post-money equity valuation of USD 510 million. 
The investment marks a significant milestone in Cyient Semiconductors' growth 
journey and reflects growing confidence in  the company's strategy, execution, and 
long-term market opportunity. Through this investment, Cyient Semiconductors aims 
to expand its turnkey custom ASIC capabilities and accelerate custom silicon 
development and strengthen long-term wafer supply assurance and supply chain 
resilience.  
 
The investment comes at a pivotal time for India's semiconductor industry as 
government and industry-led initiatives continue to strengthen the country's position 
within the global semiconductor value chain. Cyient Semiconductors is well-positioned 
to contribute to this momentum throug h advanced semiconductor design, product 
development, and intellectual property cr eation from India for global markets. 
"This investment is a strong validation of our vision and the opportunity ahead for 
semiconductor innovation from India. We  believe India has th e talent, technical 
depth, and ecosystem momentum to become a significant force in the global 
semiconductor industry. Our ambition is to  build a world-class semiconductor company 
from India that develops differentiate d custom silicon for global markets. 
This investment will help us accelerate innovation, strengthen our engineering 
capabilities and scale our global customer engagements as we execute on our long-
term growth strategy. Having a leadin g semiconductor ecosystem partner as a 
strategic investor validates our technology  and business model, accelerates our access 
to advanced manufacturing capabilities, and strengthens our ability to deliver custom 
silicon and power produc ts globally,” said, Suman Narayan, CEO, Cyient 
Semiconductors 
  
 
Cyient Semiconductors is focused on delivering custom silicon solutions to customers 
across automotive, industrial, AI, communicati ons, and data center markets. Through 
its end-to-end capabilities spanning ch ip architecture, design, semiconductor 
manufacturing, packaging, testing, qualification, and supply chain management, the company enables customers to accelerat e innovation and bring differentiated 
semiconductor products to market faster. 
About Cyient Semiconductors 
Cyient Semiconductors is a Hyderabad-headquartered provider of custom ASIC/ASSP 
solutions, with a focus on analog mixed- signal, intelligent power, and advanced 
semiconductor platforms. With design center s in India, Belgium, and the U.S., Cyient 
Semiconductors enables global customers in  data centers, robotics, automotive, and 
industrial automation to achieve higher efficiency and fast er time-to-market.
