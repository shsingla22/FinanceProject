Pursuant to Regulation s 30 and 33 of Listing Regulations, we wish  to inform that  the Board of Directors

1. Unaudited Financial Results (Standalone and Consolidated) for the 3rd quarter and nine months  ended 
31st December, 2024 which are enclosed herewith along with Limited Review Report of the Auditors 
thereon. 
2. Approved modification to ‘ Titagarh Rail Systems Limited – Employees Stock Option Scheme 2023 ’ 
(TRSL ESOP Scheme 2023), for increase of Stock options from 5,00,000 to 15,00,000 stock options, 
subject to approval of the shareholders of the Company  and such other approvals as may be 
applicable including the Stock Exchanges .  
3. Alteration in the Object Clause of the Memorandum of Association of the Company  to inter alia  include 
carrying on the business of Safety and Signaling Systems . The said alteration is subject to the 
approval of the shareholders of the Company and such other approvals as may be applicable . 
4. Approved  Notice of  Postal Ballot for seeking approval of the shareholders of the Company for the 
above said special business items . The said Notice of Postal Ballot will be sent to the shareholders 
and Stock Exchanges in due course.

of equity shares by Titagarh Firema Engineering Services Private Limited (TFESPL), Joint venture company and approved amendment to Joint Venture agreement (JVA). Upon allotment and execution 
of amendment to JVA, TFESPL will become a subsidiary of the Company. Relevant disclosures 
required in terms of Regulation 30 of Listing Regulations read with Schedule III and SEBI Circulars will be given post the aforesaid allotment by TFESPL.
