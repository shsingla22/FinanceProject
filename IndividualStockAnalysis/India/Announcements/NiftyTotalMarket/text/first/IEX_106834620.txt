234.7 Crore in H1FY’26.
Consolidated Profit After Tax in H1FY’27 at Rs 274.0 Crore, increased 12.3% from Rs

Q2FY’27

BUSINESS UPDATE
On the power sector front, the country witnessed sustained demand growth during the first
half of the year, supported by strong industrial activity and higher irrigation demand amid a
weak monsoon. Power demand grew 10.2% year-on-year to 897 BUs in H1 FY’27.
During Q2’FY27, elevated temperatures due to El Niño and deficient monsoon rainfall further
contributed to electricity demand reaching 502 BUs, marking a record growth of 11.5% year-
on-year. As a result of increase in power demand, the average market clearing price in the
Day-Ahead Market at Rs 5.7/unit during Q2 FY’27 increased 46% compared to Q2 FY’26.
Similarly, the average market clearing price in the Real-Time Market at Rs 5.2/unit during Q2
FY’27 increased 49% compared to Q2FY’26.

On the gas market front, the Indian Gas Exchange (IGX), traded gas volumes of 69.5 million
MMBtu in H1FY’27, higher by 71% over H1 FY’26. In Q2FY’27, IGX traded gas volumes of 42
million MMBtu, a growth of 160% over Q2 FY’26 led by volumes from domestic gas producers
and heightened power demand being met by gas power plants during the months of August
and September. In Q2 FY’27 nearly 30% of the traded gas volumes were from the power
sector.
For H1FY’27, IGX recorded a profit after tax of Rs 39.7 Crore, as compared to Rs 23.7 crore in
H1 FY’26, an increase of 67.4% YoY. IGX’s Profit after tax in Q2 FY’27 at Rs.23.4 crore, was
higher by 144% compared with Rs 9.6 Cr in Q2 FY’26.
In H1FY’27, ICX Pvt Ltd (formerly known as International Carbon Exchange) issued 94 lakh I-
RECs, registering a growth of 15% over H1 FY’26. Revenue for ICX Pvt Ltd in H1FY’27 stood at
Rs 4.4 Crore, higher by 18.5% on a YoY basis. For Q2 FY’27 ICX Pvt Ltd. issued nearly 52 lakh I-
RECs, a growth of 38.4% YoY. Revenue for ICX Pvt Ltd in Q2 FY’27 stood at Rs 2.3 crore, higher
by 20.7% on a year-on-year basis. The I-REC is a globally recognized digital certificate that
serves as transferable proof of generation of 1 MWh of energy from renewable sources.
In Q2FY’27, IEX’s wholly owned subsidiary, Indian Coal Exchange Limited, applied for a licence
with the Coal Controller Organisation (CCO), the regulatory authority for coal exchanges.
ABOUT IEX
IEX is India’s premier electricity exchange providing a nationwide, automated trading platform for physical
delivery of electricity, renewable power, renewable energy certificates and energy saving certificates. The
exchange platform enables efficient price discovery and increases the accessibility and transparency of the power
market in India while also enhancing the speed and efficiency of trade execution. The Exchange is ISO Certified
for quality management, Information security management and environment management since August 2016.
The Exchange is a publicly listed company with NSE and BSE since October 2017 and is approved and regulated
by Central Electricity Regulatory Commission since 27 June 2008.
