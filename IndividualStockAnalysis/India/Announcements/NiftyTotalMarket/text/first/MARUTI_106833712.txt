Maruti Suzuki, India’s No.  1 Electric Vehicle exporter, crosses EV exports of 50,000 units 
•Accounts for nearly 93 % of India’s  EV exports in passenger vehicle category during April -September  2026**.
•EVs shipped to about  50 countries  including the U.K.,  Norway, Germany, Japan and Switzerland
Hansalpur/ New Delhi, 29 October  2026:  Maruti Suzuki India Limited (“Maruti Suzuki”)  today announced that 
cumulative exports of its Made -in-India  EV have crossed 50,000 units  since  the commencement of global shipment s 
in August 2025.  
Europe was the first market to receive these vehicles and remains the largest export destination . In FY2025 -26, 
Maruti Suzuki was India's No.  1 exporter of EVs* and maintained its leadership position during April -September 
FY2026 -27**.

Speaking on the milestone, Mr. Hisashi Takeuchi, Managing Director & CEO, Maruti Suzuki India Limited , said, 
“Maruti Suzuki's EV export journey began in August 2025 when the Hon'ble Prime Minister of India, Shri Narendra 
Modi, commemorated the start of production of the e VITARA  at the Hansalpur facility , for sale in over 100 countries. 
At the same event, he hailed Maruti Suzuki as a brand ambassador^ of the 'Make in India' initiative. Today, Maruti 
Suzuki exports EVs to nearly 50  of these  countries, with Europe being the largest destination. In April -September 
2026, Maruti Suzuki accounted for nearly 93%** of India's  electric passenger v ehicle exports. This milestone reflects 
India's advanced manufacturing capabilities and its growing competitiveness .” 
A true testament of ‘Make in India, Make for the World’ , the Company has been India’s No. 1 passenger vehicle 
exporter for the past five consecutive years^^ and command s over 50% share** of passenger vehicle exports from 
India during April -September  2026.  Maruti Suzuki currently exports 1 6 models to nearly 120 countries.  
At the heart of Maruti Suzuki's EV export success is its Hansalpur facility in Gujarat, the exclusive manufacturing 
base for Suzuki's first global Battery Electric Vehicle . The facility recently expanded to an annual production capacity 
of 1 million vehicles, becoming the first Suzuki manufacturing facility globally to reach this capacity at a single 
location. With a cumulative investment of INR 252,887 million (INR 25,288.7 crore) , Hansalpur is also a key export 
hub, accounting for nearly half of Maruti Suzuki’s overall overseas shipments in FY 2025 -26.
