 
     
 
 HUDCO/List. Comp./SE/2024-25                                                                      10th March, 2025 
 
Sub.: Outcome of the Board Meeting – Declaration of Second Interim Dividend and fixation 
of Record date.  
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- 
 Sir/Madam, 
 The Board of Directors, in their meeting held on Monday, 10th March, 2025, inter-alia 
considered and approved declaration of Second Interim Dividend of Rs. 1.05/- per equity 
share, i.e. @ 10.50% (subject to deduction of TDS) on the face value of Rs. 10/- each, for 
the Financial Year 2024-25 and fixed Friday, 14th March, 2025 as Record date for 
determining the eligibility of shareholders for payment of Interim Dividend. 
The process for payment of Interim Dividend will be completed within 30 days of its 
declaration. 
 
The Board Meeting commenced at 11:50 a.m. (IST) and concluded at 02:05 p.m. (IST)
