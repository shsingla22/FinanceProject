1st March 2025:  Lemon Tree Hotels Limited announces the rebranding of Keys Select by 
Lemon Tree Hotels, Katti -Ma, Chennai to Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotels, Katt i-Ma, 
Chennai. This Hotel is managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a subsidiary of the 
Company.  
 
The renovated hotel has several new facilities to improve the guest experience  at various 
touchpoints . This efficient  hotel features 30 w ell-appointed and newly refurbished rooms 
and suites,  Keys Café  — a multi -cuisine coffee shop;  Unlock Bar  — a resto bar  and a fully 
equipped fitness center.  Upgrades have also been made in the public areas including the 
lobby and the reception to create a pleasant first impression upon the guest. The hotel 
also has expansive banquet spaces and conference fa cilities to meet  the discerning guests’ 
business and events requirements along with a kid’s room to keep young guests 
entertained.  
 
Chennai is a vibrant city known for its rich cultural heritage and modern development. 
Renowned for classical music and dance, especially Bharatanatyam, it hosts the annual 
Margazhi Festival, celebrating its artistic traditions. With its blend of modernity and 
tradition, Chennai offers a unique experience, where bustling markets, delicious South 
Indian cuisine, and warm hospitality thrive alongside the growth of a cosmopolitan 
lifestyle.  
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED   
 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of the largest hotel chains in India, and 
owns/leases/operates/franchises hotels across the upscale, upper -midscale, midscale and 
economy segments. It delivers differentiated yet superior service offerings, with a 
compelling value proposition. The group offers seven brands to meet guests’ needs across 
all levels, viz. Aurika Hotels & Resorts, Lemon Tree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox 
Hotels by Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Prima by Lemon Tree Hotels, Keys Select by Lem on 
Tree Hotels and Keys Lite by Lemon Tree Hotels.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
LTH opened its first hotel with 49 rooms in May 2004. Today, the company has a portfolio 
of 200+ hotels, which includes over 110 operational hotels and more than 90 hotels set 
to open in India and internationally. Lemon Tree Hotels are located across India , in key 
metro regions including the NCR, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Chennai, 
as well as numerous tier I, II and III cities such as Pune, Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, Jaipur, 
Indore, Aurangabad, Udaipur, Visakhapatnam, Kochi, Ludhiana, Thiruvanant hapuram, 
Vijayawada, etc. The company has also expanded internationally to Dubai, Bhutan and 
Nepal.
