Sub: Trade by Designated Person  
 
This is to inform you that the Company has noticed a trade during Prohibited Period by Ms. Vrunda Bhaskar, 
spouse of Mr. Bhaskar Bhat, Independent Director, involving purchase of 200 equity shares on                               
November 8, 2026  at a trade value of Rs. 1.79 Lakhs .  
 
The Audit Committee was informed of the aforesaid matter . Based on  the information shared  by Mr. Bhaskar 
Bhat , it was noted that  the lapse was inadvertent and the trade was made without any intent to violate the 
Marico Insider Trading Rules, 2015 (“ MIT Rules ”) or the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 
(“SEBI PIT Regulations ”). Mr. Bhaskar Bhat also informed that his spouse was unaware of the Prohibited Period 
and not in possession of any Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (“ UPSI ”) at the time of trade . Further, 
while the Prohibited Period was in effect until November 11, 2026, the relevant UPSI had been disseminated by 
the Company to the stock exchanges and publicly available on November 6, 2026.  
 
After considering the facts and submissions , the Audit Committee  has levied a penalty of Rs. 3 Lakhs  on                 
Mr. Bhaskar Bhat  towards the aforesaid violation . The penalty amount has been remitted by him to SEBI 
Investor Protection and Education Fund (IPEF ), and r equisite reporting is being made to stock exchanges in 
accordance with the SEBI PIT Regulations .
