Closure of USFDA inspection at Zydus’ formulation manufacturing 
plant at SEZ II, Ahmedabad 
 
Ahmedabad, India, 13 August, 2025 
 
We wish to inform that the USFDA conducted an inspection at the group’s formulation 
manufacturing plant located SEZ – II, Ahmedabad. The inspection was a Pre-Approval 
Inspection (PAI) for 3 products with general cGMP and was conducted from 11th to 13th August, 
2025. 
  
The inspection concluded with NIL observations.  
 
This may kindly be considered as a disclosure pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
