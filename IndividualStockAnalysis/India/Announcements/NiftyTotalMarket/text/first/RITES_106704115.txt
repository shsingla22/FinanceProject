                                                      Press Release  
RITES & MECON sign MoU  
to explore business avenues  
 
Gurugram, July 20, 2026:  RITES Ltd., a premier transport infrastructure consultancy, has signed a 
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with MECON Ltd, an undertaking under the Ministry of Steel, 
to collaborate in identifying business opportunities and jointly pursuing projects. The partnership aims 
to leverage the complementary strengths of both organisations—RITES’ extensive expertise in 
transport infrastructure and engineering consultancy, and MECON’s capabilities in the metal, mining, 
energy, and allied sectors.  
 
 
 
Under the MoU, the organisations will jointly explore consultancy assignments and infrastructure 
opportunities across a wide range of sectors, including railways, metro rail, highways, expressways, 
airports, ports, ropeways, bridges, tunnels, urban transport, energy, water resources, hydropower, 
wastewater management, and industrial infrastructure. The collaboration will focus on opportunities 
in India and international markets, combining the technical expertise and sectoral experience of both 
organisations to deliver integrated infrastructure solutions.  
 
 
About RITES Limited:  
RITES Limited, a Navratna Public Sector Enterprise, is a leading player in the transport consultancy and engineering sector 
in India, having diversified services and geographical reach. The company has an experience spanning 52 years and 
undertaken projects in over 55 countries in Asia, Africa, Latin America, South America, and Middle East region.
