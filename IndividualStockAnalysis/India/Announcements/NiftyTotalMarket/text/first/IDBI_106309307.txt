and Administration) Rules, 2014, IDBI Bank provided the facility of remote e-voting 
to the Members vide AGM Notice dated June 18, 2025 mailed to Members on June 
25, 2025 as per the directions of MCA and SEBI. Members voted electronically from 
Thursday, July 17, 2025 at 9.00 a.m. (IST) to Monday, July 21, 2025 at 5.00 p.m 
(IST), being the last date fixed for remote e-voting. Further, on July 22, 2025, the day

2014.
