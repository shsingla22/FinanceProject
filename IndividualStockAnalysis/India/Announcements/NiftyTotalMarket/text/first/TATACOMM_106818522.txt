(ii) Other Restructuring:  
The Board of Directors has approved the transfer of the entire shareholding held by the 
Company in Kaleyra, Inc., to Tata Communications (Netherlands) B.V. (‘TCNL’), a                    
wholly- owned subsidiary of the Company. As consideration for the aforesaid transfer, the 
Company shall subscribe to and acquire ordinary shares of TCNL at fair market value, as determined pursuant to an independent valuation report. The proposed transaction is 
intended to simplify the Company’s existing group structure by consolidating ownership of 
Kaleyra, Inc. under TCNL, without any change in the ultimate ownership and beneficial 
economic interest of the Company in Kaleyra, Inc. and its subsidiaries.

iii. Notes to Segments:  
The Group’s (the Company and its subsidiaries together referred to as “the Group”) operating segments comprise of Voice 
Solutions, Data Services, Transformation services, Real Es tate & Campaign Registry. The composition of the operating 
segments is as follows:
Voice Solutions includes International and National Long Distance Voice services.
Data Services includes Core connectivity services and D igital platforms & connected  services. (Refer note 5).
Transformation Services includes the business of providing  telecommunication network manage ment and support services. 
These services are carried out by the Company’s wholly owne d subsidiary Tata Communications Transformation Services 
Limited and its subsidiaries. 
Campaign Registry includes the business of collecting robotic ally driven campaign information and processing and sharing 
that information with mobile operators and the messaging ecosy stem to reduce spam. These services are carried out by the 
wholly owned indirect subsidiary of the Company.
Real Estate segment includes lease rentals for premises given on lease .
