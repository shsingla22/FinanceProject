capacity utilisation of Property, Plant, Equipment Block. 
The Depreciation on the unreconciled / pending to be updated values which are being carried 
forward from the NCL T / RP period has been provided only @ 20% of the applicable 
depreciation on such values citing that the manufacturing operations were not operating at 
optimum capacity and the same has been considered based on estimates of capacity 
utilization and normal wear and tear which, in the opinion of the management, is expected to 
fairly represent the depreciation charge for the year. Depreciation on fresh additions which 
were being fully put to used is being provided at appropriate rate.
