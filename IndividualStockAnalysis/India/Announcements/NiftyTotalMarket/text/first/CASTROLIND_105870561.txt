Mumbai, 31 May 2024: Castrol POWER1 in collaboration with Viacom18 has c oncluded the 
thrilling search for India’s top motorcycling talen t with the Grand Finale of Castrol POWER1 
presents India’s Ultimate MotoStar on MTV  at the iconic Kari Motor Speedway Racetrack in 
Coimbatore. This electrifying event, which saw fier ce competition among the nation’s best, 
crowned Pillarisetty Sai Rahil from Hyderabad , Jagadeesh Nagaraj from Bengaluru , and 
Padhmini Mohan Nair Soorya from Chennai  as India’s Ultimate MotoStars. As a reward, these 
three winners will get a once-in-a-lifetime opportu nity of undergoing exclusive training at the 
prestigious Castrol Honda LCR MotoGP™ Team’s racing  facility in Europe. 
 
The power-packed journey of India’s Ultimate MotoSt ar right from auditions, selection, training, 
and final races to determine the winners, hosted by  bike enthusiast, Karan Kundrra is set to air in 
a 1-hour special episode on MTV and a three-episode  series on JioCinema on 1 June 2024 at 
8:30 pm.  
 
Launched in December 2023, this exhilarating initia tive by Castrol POWER1 and Viacom18 aimed 
to uncover and nurture the country’s most promising  motorcycle racers. This first time ever 
initiative attracted over 50,000 aspiring riders fr om not only the metro hubs but also tier II and III  
cities, that included participants from various bac kgrounds such as engineers, businessmen, 
mechanics, students, women riders, including mother s, epitomizing the spirit and passion of 
India's biking community. Out of this, through a me ticulous selection process, 240 contestants 
were shortlisted to come for city auditions that we re held in Chennai, Mumbai, New Delhi, and 
Kolkata.  
 
After intense scrutiny, and a series of exhilaratin g challenges, the ULTIMATE 18 were selected 
to compete for the title of India's Ultimate MotoSt ar. These 18 finalists then received advanced 
training at the iconic Kari Motor Speedway in Coimb atore under the leadership of Indian racing 
legend Rajini Krishnan from India’s esteemed racing  and training academy, RACR. With a 
rigorous professional training, the final rev-up ra ce was conducted as per the standards of 
international motoracing championships under the gu idance of FMSCI, to determine the winners 
of India’s Ultimate MotoStar. 
 
Mr. Rohit Talwar, Vice President and Head of Market ing, Castrol India Limited, said,  “India's 
Ultimate MotoStar is a phenomenal success, offering  bike enthusiasts the opportunity to hone 
their skills under the guidance of expert professio nals and unleash their full potential. We are 
thrilled to provide these young riders with a platf orm to showcase their talents. Congratulations to 
our winners, who now have the incredible opportunit y to train with the Castrol Honda LCR 
MotoGP™ Team in Europe.”
