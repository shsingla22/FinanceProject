Sub.: Capacity Expansion announced by the Company’s associate Company viz. East 
Pipes Integrated Company for Industry, KSA – General Announcement 
 
We are pleased to inform you that our associate Company East Pipes Integrated Company for Industry (EPIC), a listed entity, has announced the signing off contracts with different entities to 
establish a new production line for Helical Submerged Arc Welded (HSAW) pipes in it’s factory 
in Dammam 2
nd Industrial City. The cost of this project is approximately SAR 48 million (approx. 
Rs. 113 crore). The project will be funded from the EPIC’s available cash, bank facilities and 
other financing programs. The expected date of completion of the project is fourth quarter of the fiscal year 2025-26 and the expected date of commercial production is quarter one of the fiscal 
year 2026-27. The new line will add an approx. 100,000 MTs to the existing nominal capacity of 400,000 MTs. It may also be noted that there is no related party involved in this.The above announcement has been made by EPIC to Saudi Stock Exchange, today.  
  
The financial impact of the project will be reflected in the financial year 2025-2026 and 2026-27. 
 EPIC is Saudi Arabia’s leading manufacturer of Helical Submerged Arc Welded (HSAW) pipes. It’s fully integrated manufacturing facilities with impeccable track record of execution of mega 
orders in time & Quality with customer centric approach, positions them as a most preferred 
supplier in the KSA market and continue to pioneer in supporting KSA’s strategic objectives under 
the Vision 2030.
