PVR INOX Opens Sikkim’s First Multiplex at Westpoint Mall, Gangtok – Bringing a World -Class 
Cinema Experience to the Hills  
~ A landmark FOCO model collaboration with Westpoint Infra brings a next -gen multiplex experience 
with 3 high -tech screens, 3D immersion, and laser projection  
National , 24 October 2025: PVR INOX, India’s largest and most premium multiplex chain, proudly 
announces the launch of its first cinema in Sikkim, located a t Westpoint Mall, Gangtok. Developed under 
the Franchise -Owned Company -Operated (FOCO) model in collaboration with Westpoint Infra Pvt. Ltd., 
this 3 -screen multiplex marks a significant milestone for the brand, bringing a world -class cinematic 
experience t o the Northeast.  
The new property features 482 seats across three mainstream auditoriums, each equipped with state -of-
the-art 2K Laser projection  and Dolby 7.1 surround sound  with next -gen 3D offering an immersive viewing 
experience.  
All seats are finished in premium leatherette, with ergonomic Slider models designed for enhanced 
comfort. Aesthetically, each auditorium showcases a unique colour palette —Green (Sy camore) Teal, and 
Gold adding visual distinction to the luxurious interiors . Spanning 12,271 sq. ft., the multiplex also offers 
an extensive food and beverage menu, blending gourmet selections with local favourites . 
Strategically located near MG Marg in the heart of the city, the cinema is housed within Westpoint Mall —
a premium s hopping and lifestyle destination featuring popular brands such as H&M, Pantaloons, Levi’s, 
Vero Moda, Pizza Hut, and KFC. Its central location makes it easily accessible . 
“Our entry into Sikkim is a proud and important milestone for PVR INOX. It reflects our strong commitment 
to transforming the way people experience movies —not just in big cities, but in every part of the country. 
This multiplex is more than just a cinema; it’s a place where the latest technology, comfort, and great 
storytelling come toget her to celebrate the unique spirit of this beautiful region. We are thrilled to bring 
world -class entertainment to the people of Sikkim and set a new standard for premium cinemas in every 
part of the country,”  said Mr. Ajay Bijli , Managing Director, PVR IN OX Limited.  
Speaking on the launch of this new property, Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli, Executive Director, PVR INOX 
Limited , said  “We are truly thrilled to bring the PVR INOX experience to the enchanting state of Sikkim, in 
collaboration with Westpoint Infra Pvt. Ltd. This launch is a landmark moment as we introduce the state’s 
first multiplex where the timeless charm of Gangtok meets world -class entertainment. Known for its 
breathtaking landscapes, rich culture, and warm people, Sikkim deserves a cinematic de stination that 
mirrors its spirit. With cutting -edge 2K Laser projection, Dolby 7.1 sound, and luxurious interiors, our new 
multiplex is designed to offer a grand, immersive experience that celebrates both technology and tradition. 
More than just a theatre , it’s a place where stories come alive and communities come together amidst the 
magic of the Himalayas.”  
 
 
 
 
This opening also follows recent FOCO property launches in Raipur, Jabalpur, and Siliguri, reaffirming the 
company’s strategic focus on growing thr ough the FOCO model across emerging Tier -II and Tier -III 
markets.

About  PVR INOX  Limited  
PVR INOX  Limited  is the largest  film exhibition  company  in India  with  1,764  Screens  across

have created iconic cinema brands with storied histories of achieving significant milestones
