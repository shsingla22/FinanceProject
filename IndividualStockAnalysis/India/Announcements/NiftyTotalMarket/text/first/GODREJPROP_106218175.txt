Sub: Outcome of the Board meeting held on May 02, 2025 
 
Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
Further to our letter dated April 28, 2025, please note that the Board of Directors (“the Board”) at its meeting 
held on May 02, 2025, has, inter alia, considered and approved the following transactions: 
 
1. Raising of Funds by way of issuance of Debt securities upto Rs. 2,000 crore: 
 
The Board has approved raising of funds, by issue of Non-Convertible Debentures, Bonds and/ or other Debt 
Securities on a private placement basis, in one or more tranches, for an amount not exceeding Rs. 2,000 crore 
(Rupees Two Thousand Crore Only). The Board also authorized a Committee of Directors to decide on all 
matters relating to the issuance of Non-Convertible Debentures, Bonds and/or other Debt Securities from time 
to time, including the quantum, timing and terms and conditions thereof.  
 
2. Conducting the 40th Annual General Meeting: 
 
The Board has decided to hold the 40th Annual General Meeting of the Company on Friday, August 01, 2025, 
at 02.30 p.m.  
 
3. Re-appointment of Mr. Gaurav Pandey (DIN: 07229661) as the Managing Director and Chief 
Executive Officer of the Company: 
 
Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of the Board of Directors at its 
meeting held today, the Board have re-appointed Mr. Gaurav Pandey (DIN: 07229661) as the Managing Director 
and Chief Executive Officer of the Company for a further period of 3 (three) years commencing from January 
01, 2026, subject to the approval of the Members of the Company. 
  
 
The relevant details pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with SEBI Master Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 are enclosed as Annexure A.  
 
4. Appointment of M/s BNP and Associates as Secretarial Auditor of the Company: 
 
Based on the recommendation of the Audit Committee, the Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting 
held today has approved, the appointment of  M/s BNP and Associates, a Peer Reviewed Firm of Company 
Secretaries in Practice, having Firm Registration No. P2014MH037400  as Secretarial Auditor of the Company for 
Audit period of five consecutive years commencing from financial year 2025-26  till financial year 2029-30, 
subject to the approval of the shareholders of the Company at the ensuing Annual General Meeting. 
 
Consequent to the above, M/s A.K. Jain & Co., Company Secretaries, existing Secretarial Auditor have ceased to 
be the Secretarial Auditor of the Company with effect from May 02, 2025. 
 
The relevant details pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 are enclosed in Annexure B . 
 
 
The Board meeting commenced at 11:45 a.m. and concluded at 01:05 p.m.
