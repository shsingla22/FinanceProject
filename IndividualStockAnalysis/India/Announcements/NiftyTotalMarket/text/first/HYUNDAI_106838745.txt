Gurugram |  November  01, 2026:  Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) achieved its  
highest -ever total monthly sales of 82,299 units (domestic + exports) in October 2026,  
registering a strong 17.7% YoY growth. The milestone was driven by record domestic  
sales of 62,799 units (+16.7% YoY) and exports of 19,500 units (+21.1% YoY).  
 
Commenting on the monthly sales performance, Mr. Tarun Garg, MD & CEO - HMIL,  
said, “October 2026 has been an exceptional month for Hyundai Motor India, with total  
sales of 82,299 units, our highest -ever monthly sales since inception with a  
robust 17.7% YoY growth.  Strong festive demand, coupled with healthy momentum  
in both domestic and export markets, helped us set this new sales record. Going  
forward, the newly launched Hyundai BAYON - our Intelligent Utility Vehicle, will  
provide further impetus to our growth in India as well as across 50+ export  
markets,  reinforcing India’s role as a strategic manufacturing and export hub for  
Hyundai.”
