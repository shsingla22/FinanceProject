The Board of Directors, in their meeting held today i.e. Monday , 23rd March, 2026, inter -alia

i) Declaration of 4th Interim Dividend for Financial Year 2025- 26 and fixation of Record date:  
 
Declaration of 4th Interim Dividend of Rs. 1.25/- per equity share i.e., @ 12.50%  (subject to 
deduction of TDS) on the face value of Rs.10/ - each, for the Financial Year 2025- 26 and fixation

The process of payment of Interim Dividend will be completed within 30 days of its declaration.

communication uploaded HUDCO’s website under the tab Investors>Dividend>TDS 
Communication.
