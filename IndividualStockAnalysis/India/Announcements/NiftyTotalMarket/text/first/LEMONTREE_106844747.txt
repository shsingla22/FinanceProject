LEMON TREE HOTELS LAUNCHES LEMON TREE RESORT, PAVAGADH  
IN GUJARAT  
 
The 44 -room resort offers a blend of leisure, cultural experiences and 
contemporary hospitality in the historic destination  
New Delhi,  4th November , 2026 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited has announced the 
launch of Lemon Tree Resort, Pavagadh  in Gujarat. Set amidst the region's rich 
cultural heritage and natural landscape, the resort offers an inviting getaway for 
leisure travellers, families and corporate groups, with convenient access to some 
of the area's most significant historical and spiritual attractio ns. 
The hotel is managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
Marking the group's 11th operational resort, the newly launched property  reflects 
the Lemon Tree Resorts philosophy of thoughtfully curated upper -midscale 
retreats that blend modern comfort with the vitality of nature. With fresh, vibrant 
interiors and playful design elements, the resort offers guests an inviting setting 
to unwind and discover the destination.  
It features 44 rooms across five categories – Heritage Tents, Heritage Cottages, 
Bunga Huts, Heritage Suites and Royal Tents. Its dining offering includes Citrus 
Café, a multi -cuisine restaurant with seating for approximately  100 guests.  
For meetings and celebrations, the resort offers a banquet hall, two boardrooms 
and an expansive outdoor lawn. Guests can also enjoy a refreshing swimming 
pool, spa, fitness cent er, kids’ zone and indoor and outdoor recreational activities.  
Commenting on the launch, Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, 
Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd.,  said, “Gujarat offers a diverse hospitality landscape 
with opportunities spanning business and industrial centres, pilgrimage 
destinations and emerging leisure markets. Our growing presence in the state 
reflects our confidence in its potential and our commitment  to building a portfolio 
that responds to the diverse travel needs of the region.  Pavagadh is a destination 
where history, spirituality and nature come together. We are delighted to bring 
Lemon Tree's distinctive hospitality to this beautiful setting with the comfort, 
warmth and thoughtful  service  that define our brand. With 14 operational hotels 
and another 18 in the pipeline, we remain focused on  strengthening our presence 
across the state.”  
Located on Pavagadh -Halol Road in Gujarat’s Panchmahal district, the resort offers 
access to attractions such as Champaner Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and 
Kalika Mata Temple. Hathni Mata Waterfall and Jambughoda Wildlife Sanctuary 
are also within d riving distance. Vadodara Airport is approximately 37 kms away.  

 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India's leading hospitality companies, 
catering to a wide range of customers – from value -conscious travellers to 
premium business and leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels 
& Resorts, Lemon T ree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys 
Select, and Keys Lite – the group offers experiences across upper upscale, 
upscale, upper midscale, midscale, leisure, wildlife, and spiritual segments.  
LTHL operates 130+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing 
pipeline of 140+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, 
Bengaluru, and Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, 
and Indore – and with an international presence in Bhutan  and Nepal – Lemon 
Tree Hotels delivers exceptional comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, 
refreshing experience.  
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 270+ 
properties (operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for 
both business and leisure travellers.
