RESOLVED FURTHER THAT  consent of the members be and is hereby granted to the Board to create, grant, 
offer and issue in one or more tranches under the ABLBL ESOP Scheme 2026, at any time to or for the benefit

exercisable into not more than 1,83,07,800 equity shares of ₹10/- each (“Equity Shares”) being 1.50% of the

terms of the ABLBL ESOP Scheme 2026 as per Applicable Laws), at such price, in one or more tranches and on 
such terms and conditions as may be fixed or determined by the Board in accordance with the SEBI SBEB & SE 
Regulations or other provisions of Applicable Laws, as may be prevailing at that time. 
 
RESOLVED FURTHER THAT  the maximum number of Stock Options to be granted to eligible employees under 
the ABLBL ESOP Scheme 2026 shall not cumulatively exceed 1,83,07,800 Employees Stock Options and 
Performance Stock Units (“Stock Options”) and exercisable into not more than 1,83,07,800 equity shares of face 
value of Rs. 10/- each (or such other number adjusted in terms of ABLBL ESOP Scheme 2026 as per Applicable 
Laws). 
 
RESOLVED FURTHER THAT  the Equity Shares issued and to be allotted on exercise by the Board pursuant to the 
ABLBL ESOP Scheme 2026 in the manner aforesaid shall rank pari passu  in all respects with the existing equity 
shares of the Company.  SPECIAL BUSINESS:
