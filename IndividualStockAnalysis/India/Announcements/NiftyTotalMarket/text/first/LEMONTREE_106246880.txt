Sub: Conversion of Lemon Tree Mountain Resort , Kas auli (Manchised  Hotel ) to

Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
In continuation to our earlier announcement dated 21.08.2023, w e would like to inform that 
the Franchise Agreement dated 2 1.08.2023 executed between M/s. Atithivan Hospitality 
Private Limited  (Franchisee ) & Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (Franchisor ) for Lemon Tree 
Mountain Resort, Kas auli, have been terminated today by the parties with mutual consent.  
 
Simultaneously with the execution of the aforesaid Termination Agreement, the Parties have 
entered into a Hotel Operating Agreement & License Agreement for Lemon Tree Premier, 
Kasauli.  The property shall be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly owned 
subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited and is expected to open in FY 202 7.  
 
This Hotel will now feature 78 well-appointed rooms (an increase from previous disclosure of 
50 rooms), a restaurant, a bar, a banquet hall, meeting /conference  room, a swimming pool , 
a spa , fitness center and other public areas . 
 
Please take the above intimation on record.
