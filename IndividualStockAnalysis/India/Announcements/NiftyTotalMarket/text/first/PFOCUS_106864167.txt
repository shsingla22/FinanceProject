Prime Focus company DNEG Announces Technology Partnership With 
Meta To Deliver 3D Movie Titles for Meta VR Glasses  
 
MUMBAI – November 17, 2026  
 
DNEG , an Academy Award® winning visual effects and animation company and part of the Prime 
Focus group of companies, announced today a technology partnership with Meta to create a 
catalog of much -loved movie titles in stereo 3D ahead of the launch of Meta  VR Glasses in Spring 
2027.  
 
The DNEG Stereo team is currently in production on a number of titles in the catalog, including 
the recently announced conversion of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping  in 
collaboration with Meta, Lionsgate, and director F rancis Lawrence.  
 
Meta VR Glasses feature a 5K Infinite Display built on micro -OLED panels, with 37 pixels per 
degree, delivering stunning clarity and detail. The visual experience delivered by Meta VR 
Glasses allows DNEG Stereo to deliver the highest -quality 3D versions of these beloved movies 
with cinema -grade clarity - and even to break the boundaries of normal cinematic framing to create 
exciting new immersive moments.  
 
DNEG’s Stereo team has been a leader in the field of stereo 3D conversion for over 1 5 years, 
and the team delivered the world’s first feature -length stereo conversion for a Hollywood movie. 
Since then, it has won numerous awards for its stereo conversion work on stand -out 3D shows, 
including Gravity for director Alfonso Cuarón and Mission : Impossible - Fallout  for director 
Christopher McQuarrie. Recent work includes Spider -Man: Brand New Day, Supergirl  and 
Ramayana: Part One . 
 
Speaking about the partnership, Prime Focus founder and DNEG Global CEO Namit Malhotra 
said, “I’m excited and enth used to announce DNEG’s partnership with Meta to deliver never -
before -seen 3D content for Meta VR Glasses. The launch of Meta VR Glasses is set to be a 
game -changer and will give cinema fans a new way to experience their favorite movies in stunning 
3D. The  creative possibilities opened up by this new technology align perfectly with DNEG’s 
stated ambition to ‘Transform the Future of Storytelling’, and we look forward to exploring further 
opportunities with the Meta team to deliver the highest -quality immersi ve experiences for this 
exciting new technology.”
