smelting outside China would expand our addressable market.

Gerard Sweeney  
Thank you, Sarang. On the first part of the question, every tonne of new 
primary aluminium  capacity , wherever  it is built, adds to  global 
demand for CPC and coal tar pitch.  With China operating close to its  
government -imposed production  cap, Chinese -backed producers are,  
 
Page 3 of 26 for the first time , building  smelting capacity outside China , notably in  
Indonesia , Angola, Ka zakh stan, etc., while  smelters in the Middle East  
and South Asia , especially India , have announced further expansions.  
There are anode plants also being built in some of these countries to 
supply anodes for the new smelters. Much of this new  capacity  lies 
within economic shipping distance  of our plants in India, North 
America and Europe. Our long -term investments  in flexible, multi -
modal logistics , together with our global blending model, allow us to 
source raw materials widely and to supply customers from more than 
one plant, which we believe keeps our delivered cost s competitive as 
regional competition intensifies. The third quarter illustrated this 
flexibility: as shipping in the Persian Gulf partially recovered, we 
resumed deliveries to Middle East smelters, having made none in  the 
second quarter. On  the second part, we are  in regular  contact with 
almost every smelter and major carbon user, and our volumes to each 
depend  on market conditions, freight  costs and competitors’  supply 
constraints . While  we would not describe these as entirely new 
customer relationships,  we have increased  sales to certain less -
traditional Carbon and Advanced Materials customers in regions where  
competitors faced  supply constraints , and we see scope to build on 
these relationships as new smelting capacity comes on stream . 
 
Sarang  
Thanks, Gerry. As European regulations such as the  Carbon Border 
Adjustment Mechanism , or CBAM , take firmer shape, how are our 
 
Page 4 of 26 European carbon -distillation and calcination operations positioned 
with respect to  carbon costs ? What  capital expenditure may be required 
over the medium term to maintain  compliance and support the green 
transition?  
 
Gerard Sweeney  
CBAM entered its definitive phase on 1 January 2026. Importers of 
covered goods  into the European Union  now accrue a carbon cost on 
the embedded emissions  of those goods, with certificates priced off the 
EU Emissions Trading System at about 75 euros per tonne  of CO 2 in 
the first half of 2026. CBAM currently covers cement, iron and steel, 
aluminium, fertilisers , electricity and hydrogen, and the European 
Commission’s December 2025 proposal would extend it to downstream 
steel and aluminium products. The vast majority of RAIN’s products , 
including CPC, coal tar pitch and our principal Advanced Materials 
products, fall outside this scope, so  we do not foresee CBAM -driven 
capital expenditure at our European plants . Independently of CBAM, 
we continue to reduce the carbon footprint of these plants by lowering 
natural gas consumption and buying more green electricity , within our 
normal, disciplined capital program.  CBAM may also benefit RAIN 
indirectly . By placing a carbon cost on higher -emission steel and 
aluminium imports, it supports EU steelmakers, which supply the coal 
tar processed at our European  distillation plants , and EU aluminium 
smelters, which buy our carbon products. We will continue to monitor 
 
Page 5 of 26 the legislative process closely, particularly any further extension of its 
scope.
