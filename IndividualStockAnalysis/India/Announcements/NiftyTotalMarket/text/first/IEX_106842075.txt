MEDIA RELEASE: IEX POWER MARKET UPDATE, OCTOBER’26
● IEX ACHIEVES MONTHLY ELECTRICITY TRADED VOLUME OF 13.1 BU IN OCTOBER’26, AN
INCREASE OF 16.5% YOY
● ACHIEVES MONTHLY VOLUME OF 5.7 BU IN RTM, INCREASE OF 23.7% YOY
New Delhi, 03rdNovember 2026: Indian Energy Exchange (IEX), India's premier electricity
exchange, recorded monthly electricity traded volume of 13,092 MUs in October 2026, an
increase of 16.5% year-on-year.
According to government data, India’s energy consumption touched 159.8 BUs in October
2026, registering a year-on-year growth of 21.9%. This rise in power demand drove buy bids
in the Day-Ahead Market up 265% year-on-year, resulting in an increase in prices. The average
market clearing price in the Day-Ahead Market registered a 149% increase year-on-year,
rising from Rs 2.67/unit in October 2025 to Rs 6.66/unit in October 2026. The sharp increase
was also attributable to the low base of the corresponding month last year, when power
demand was relatively lower. Similarly, the average market clearing price in the Real-Time
Market stood at Rs 5.78/unit during October 2026, registering an increase of 112% year-on-
year.
However, demand-supply dynamics remained volatile even during October. Warmer weather
due to El Niño and below-normal monsoon rainfall, resulted in strong power demand in the
first half, while cooler weather, rainfall and the impact of Cyclone Arnab led to an easing of
demand during the latter days of the month. Higher supply liquidity during periods of subdued
demand resulted in near-zero prices in the Real-Time Market (RTM) across multiple time
blocks.
ELECTRICITY MARKET: DAY- AHEAD, REAL-TIME & TERM- AHEAD MARKET
The Day-Ahead Market (DAM) including HP-DAM, achieved 4,508 MU volume in October ’26
as compared to 4,684 MU volume in October’25, decline of 3.8% YoY.
The Real-Time Electricity Market (RTM) volume increased to 5,670 MU in October’26, from
4,583 MU in October’25, registering an increase of 23.7% YoY.
Day Ahead Contingency and Term-Ahead Market (TAM) comprising of HPTAM, intraday,
contingency, daily & weekly and monthly contracts up to 3 months, traded 2,175 MU in
October’26 as compared to 911 MU volume in October’25, increase of 138.8% YoY.
GREEN MARKET: GREEN DAY-AHEAD & GREEN TERM-AHEAD MARKET
IEX Green Market, comprising the Green Day-Ahead and Green Term-Ahead Market
segments, achieved 738 MU volume during October’26 as compared to 1,055 MU in
October’25, decline of 30% YoY. The weighted average price in Green Day-Ahead Market (G-
DAM) for October’26 at Rs 4.13/ unit, increased 67% on a year-on-year basis.

INDIAN ENERGY EXCHANGE LIMITED
IEX is India’s premier energy exchange providing a nationwide, automated trading platform for physical delivery of
electricity, renewable energy and certificates including renewable energy certificates as well as the energy saving
certificates. The exchange platform enables efficient price discovery and increases the accessibility and transparency of
the energy market in India while also enhancing the speed and efficiency of trade execution. The Exchange is ISO Certified
for quality management, Information security management and environment management since August 2016. The
Exchange is a publicly listed company with NSE and BSE since October 2017 and is approved and regulated by Central
