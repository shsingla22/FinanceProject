Pursuant  to Regulation  30 of the SEBI  (Listing  Obligations  and Disclosure  Requirements)  Regulations,  2015,  
we have  issued  communications  dated  20th July,  2023,  21st July,  2023,  26th  July,  2023,  2nd July,  2025  and 
05th August,  2025  to apprise  on the status  of the litigation  proceedings  relating  to the usage  of the 
trademark  “CARLTON ” by the Company  in India  in connection  with  travel  luggage.   
 
Present  Status :  
 
 Matter is Sub -Judice : Proceedings in CS (COMM) No. 730 of 2019 and CS (COMM) No. 52 of 2020 
(“Captioned Matters ”) are pending before the Delhi High Court for determination of the right of 
the Company to use the trademark “CARLTON” for travel luggage in India. The counterparty in the 
matter, Carlton Shoes L imited & Ors, has not yet filed their affidavits of evidence for any witness in 
the matter despite the direction of the Hon’ble Supreme Court for the expeditious disposal of the 
suits in its order dated August 01, 2025.  
 
 Interim Relief for Disposal of Exi sting Inventory : By way of an order dated August 01, 2025, read 
with the order dated January 23, 2026, pending outcome of the Captioned Matters, the Supreme 
Court of India has granted the Company, the liberty to dispose off its existing ‘Carlton’ branded 
inventory before  June 01, 2026 . 
 
Recent  Development:   
 
Safari  Industries  (India)  Limited  (“Safari ”), a competitor  of the Company  has notified  the stock  exchanges  
on February  18, 2026,  that it has acquired  a long -term  license  to use the ‘Carlton ’ trademark  in India  from  
Carlton  Shoes  Limited.  Given  that the Captioned  Matters  are sub-judice,  we are unab le to understand  the 
rationale  or purpose  of the license.  We are, however,  concerned  that this development  is likely  to create  
confusion  in the minds  of our customers,  distributors  and stakeholders  and are therefore  issuing  the 
clarifications  below.
