Cash and Debt  
As of 31st March  2024 
• IndiGo had a total cash balance of INR 347,375 million  comprising  INR 208,230 million  of free 
cash and INR 139,145 million of restricted  cash.  
• The capitalized operating lease liability was INR 434,886 million. The total debt (including 
the capitalized operating lease liability) was INR 512,800 million.
