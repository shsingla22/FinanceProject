The Board of Directors of the Company at its Meeting held on 30th May, 2025, recommended payment of

The Register of Members and Share Transfer Books will remain closed from 9th September 2025 to 15th 
September 2025 (both days inclusive) for determining the eligibility of members for payment of dividend. 
Payment of dividend, if approved at the Annual General Meeting, will be made to those members whose 
names will be on the Company’s Register of Members on 8th September 2025 (‘Record Date ’) and to those 
whose names will appear as Beneficial Owners as at the close of the business hours on 8th September 2025 
as per the details to be furnished by the Depositories, viz. National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL) and 
Central Depository Services (India) Limited (CDSL) for this purpose.  
  
In accordance with the provisions of the Income Tax Act, 1961 as amended by and read with the provisions 
of the Finance Act, 2020, with effect from 1st April 2020, dividend declared and paid by the Company is 
taxable in the hands of its members and the Company is required to deduct tax at source (TDS) from 
dividend paid to the members at the applicable rates.
