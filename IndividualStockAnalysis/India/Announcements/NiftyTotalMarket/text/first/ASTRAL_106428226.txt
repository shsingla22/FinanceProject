Notes: 
1. The group has defined its businesses in two verticals (segments) namely: 
a) Plumbing (Pipes, fittings, water tanks, bathware). 
b) Paints and Adhesives (Erstwhile Resinova + Seal It + Astral Coatings Private Limited (Gem)). 
 
2. Overall Demand scenario in Polymer Industry was weak and the average PVC prices drop was 
10.6% on Y-O-Y basis in Q2 FY-26. Inspite of that we are happy to inform that our Company 
has delivered an Industry Leading volume and value growth of 20.6% and 15.7% respectively 
in Plumbing Business in Q2 FY-26. This clearly suggest that Company was able to grow its share 
in value added products and delivered EBIDTA growth of 20% on Y-O-Y basis in Q2 FY-26. 
 
3. During Q2 FY-26, Bathware Business has achieved a sales revenue of Rs. 329 million compare 
to last year Rs. 289 million which resulted into growth of 13.8% and Half year ended 
September 30, 2025, bathware business registered sales of Rs. 663 million compare to last 
year Rs. 551 million which resulted into growth of 20.3%.  
 
4. Our Adhesive India Business has grown by 15.8% in Sales with EBIDTA margin of 17% and 
Adhesive Overseas Business has grown by 5.2% in Sales with EBIDTA Margin of 7.3% in                    
Q2 FY-26 on Y-O-Y basis. 
 
5. Paint Business has grown by 17.1% in Q2 FY-26 on Y-O-Y basis in Sales. 
 
6. During H1 2025-26, the Company has increased its plumbing production capacity from       
3,81,957 M.T. to 3,89,301 M.T.. 
 
7. Recently in October 2025, Company has started commercial production at Kanpur plant, which 
will gear up in H2 and will improve the sales volume in northern market for the Company. At 
present capacity is 15,224 M.T. and will further increase in coming quarter gradually. 
 
8. Consolidated cash (including cash equivalents) and bank balances as at September 30, 2025 is                   
Rs. 5,552 million.   
 
9. The Board of Directors have approved a payment of interim dividend of Rs. 1.50 per share. 
(Face value of Re.1/- each). 
 
10. The figures for the previous periods have been regrouped / reclassified wherever necessary to 
confirm with the current period’s classification.
