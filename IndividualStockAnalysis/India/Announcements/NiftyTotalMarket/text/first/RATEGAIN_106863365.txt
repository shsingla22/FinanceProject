 Quicklease Car Rental Partners with RateGain to Strengthen Competitive Pricing 
Intelligence Across Its Fleet 
The UAE-based car rental company will leverage Rev-AI’s Rate Insights and Rate Shopping capabilities to 
monitor competitor pricing and enable faster, more informed commercial decisions. 
 
India, 17 November 2026:  RateGain Travel Technologies Limited (NSE: RATEGAIN, BSE: 543417), a global 
provider of AI-powered SaaS solutions for the travel and hospitality industry, today announced that 
Quicklease, a UAE-based car rental company, has partnered with RateGain to strengthen its competitive 
pricing intelligence and enable faster, more informed pricing decisions.  
  
Through this partnership, Quicklease will use Rev-AI, RateGain’s revenue management solution for car 
rentals, including Rate Insights and Rate Shopping , to gain greater visibility into competitor pricing, track 
market movements, and respond more effectively to changing demand and competitive conditions. 
 
The partnership will equip Quicklease’s revenue and commercial teams with a clearer view of the 
competitive landscape, helping them assess their pricing position, identify opportunities and make more 
informed decisions across their fleet. As pricing becomes increasingly dynamic, these capabilities will help 
the company move towards a more agile, intelligence-led approach to revenue management 
 
Speaking on the partnership, Sanchit Garg, Executive Vice President & General Manager (Rev-AI, Car 
& OTA), RateGain Travel Technologies Limited, said,  “Pricing has become a strategic lever for car rental 
operators, particularly in markets where demand and competitive conditions can change quickly. The ability to 
understand the market, identify pricing opportunities, and act with confidence is increasingly important to 
protecting revenue and improving commercial performance. With Rev-AI, Quicklease can bring this intelligence 
into its pricing process, helping its teams make more informed decisions as market conditions evolve. We are 
pleased to partner with Quicklease as it continues to strengthen its revenue management capabilities in the 
UAE”.  
 
Mohammad Daud Ali, Group General Manager, Quicklease, said,  “In a competitive car rental market, 
having the right pricing intelligence at the right time is essential to making smarter commercial decisions. Our 
partnership with RateGain gives us greater visibility into competitor pricing and market movements through 
Rev-AI, Rate Insights and Rate Shopping, helping our teams respond faster and with greater confidence. As we 
continue to strengthen our pricing capabilities, we see this partnership as an important step toward staying 
competitive, improving pricing decisions, and driving stronger revenue performance”.  
 
The partnership further strengthens RateGain’s presence in the car rental industry. Rev-AI helps operators 
connect demand, booking performance, fleet utilisation, competitor pricing and market signals to support 
faster, more informed commercial decisions. Its broader capabilities also enable operators to automate 
pricing with greater control.
