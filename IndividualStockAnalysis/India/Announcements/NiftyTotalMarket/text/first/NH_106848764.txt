Subject: Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 – Conferment of the French National Order of Merit on Dr. Devi 
Prasad Shetty, Founder and Chairman 
 
We wish to inform you, by way of voluntary disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, that Dr. Devi Prasad Shetty, Founder and Chairman 
of Narayana Hrudayalaya Limited (the “Company”), has been conferred the distinction of Knight of 
the French National Order of Merit ( Ordre national du Mérite ) by the French Republic, at a ceremony 
held in Bengaluru on November 5, 2026.  
 
As stated in the citation of the French Republic, the distinction has been conferred in recognition of Dr. 
Shetty’s generosity, availability and commitment to the French community in Bengaluru during the COVID-
19 pandemic. The citation notes that Dr. Shetty and Narayana Health opened their doors to the French 
Consulate and the wider French community, providing support to vaccination programmes and essential 
logistical assistance during an exceptionally challenging period. 
 
The National Order of Merit was established on December 3, 1963 by President Charles de Gaulle. It is the 
second-highest national order of the French Republic, after the Legion of Honour, and is conferred by 
decree of the President of the French Republic. It recognises distinguished service by both French citizens 
and foreign nationals. 
 
The Company is proud of this recognition of its Founder and Chairman. 
 
Kindly take the above information on record.
