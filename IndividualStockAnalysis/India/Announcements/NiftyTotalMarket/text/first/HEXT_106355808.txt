Hexaware Wins Three Stevie® Awards at the 2025 International Business 
Awards® 
Honors spotlight growth in North America and modern marketing excellence  
 
Iselin, New Jersey, 29th August 2025 : Hexaware Technologies [NSE: HEXT], a global 
provider of IT solutions and services, announced it has won three Bronze Stevie® Awards 
at the 22nd Annual International Business Awards® (IBAs): Fastest Growing Company of 
the Year – in Canada and the U.S.A.,  Marketing Department of the Year, and  Marketing 
Executive of the Year  for CMO Nidhi Alexander.   
 
Winners were determined by a panel of 250+ judges, who evaluated more than 3,800 
submissions from organizations in 78 different countries and territories. Hexaware’s wins 
reflect a year of AI-led execution, brand elevation, and measurable business impact.  
 
 Fastest Growing Company of the Year (Canada & U.S.A.)  
Judges praised Hexaware’s  sustained, strategic growth, noting strong year-over-year 
performance,  forward-looking use of generative AI across platforms, and  credible market 
signaling  through analyst recognitions and brand value gains. Reviewers called it 
a metrics-rich  submission that underscored operational excellence and long-term 
resilience.  
 
 Marketing Department of the Year  
The panel highlighted a  top-class marketing organization  that blends  creativity with data 
science to deliver outcomes. Judges cited  AI-driven efficiency,  unified dashboards and 
agile workflows, and  in-house creative production  that maintains quality while 
accelerating speed to market, positioning Hexaware’s team as a  blueprint for modern, 
accountable marketing.  
 
 Marketing Executive of the Year: Nidhi Alexander   
Judges noted Nidhi Alexander’s leadership in tying marketing directly to revenue, brand 
momentum, and community outcomes. Feedback emphasized her  AI-first operating 
model, pipeline influence, and  clarity in linking brand equity to business outcomes, while 
recognizing a people-centered approach that builds durable engagement.  
 
R Srikrishna, CEO & Executive Director, Hexaware , said: “Recognition across growth, 
department performance, and executive leadership speaks to the depth of effort across 
Hexaware. The teams delivered in competitive markets and did it with a clear line of sight 
to client value.”  
 
Nidhi Alexander, Chief Marketing Officer, Hexaware , said: “It is encouraging to see this 
work acknowledged. The focus has been simple: use insight, data, and an AI-first 
operating model to raise the quality of engagement and convert ideas into outcomes the 
business can see.”  
The International Business Awards® honor achievements in management, marketing, 
customer service, new products and services, technology, and more.  A global panel of 
judges determines gold, silver, and bronze winners  each year.  

 
For more details on the International Business Awards, visit 
https://stevieawards.com/iba/ .
