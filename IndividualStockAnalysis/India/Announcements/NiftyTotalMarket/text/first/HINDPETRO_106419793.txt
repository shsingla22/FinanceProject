731% Jump in H1 FY26 Standalone Profit After Tax  
Rs. 5/- per share of Interim Equity Dividend declared  
  
Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) today announced its financial results for the 
quarter and half year ended September  30, 2025 . The period  witnessed a n excellent  financial 
performance duly supported by robust refining and marketing performance .  
 
• 731% increase in H1 Standalone Profit After Tax ( ₹ 8,201  crore ) 
• Strong Refinery performance with 9.7% increase in Throughput  in H1 ( 13.23 MMT ) 
• Steady growth in Marketing with 3.5% increase in Sales volume in H1 ( 25.11 MMT ) 
• Significant progress on reduction of Debt Equity Ratio (Standalone  D/E at 1.07 as compared 
to 1.38 as on 31st March 2025 ) 
• Strong Refinery performance with Gross Refining Margin (GRM)  at US$ 8.80 per barrel  in 
Quarter 2

Financial  Performance  
 
The company registered an YoY increase in Profit After Tax by 731% for H1 FY26, and by 
507% for Q2 FY26 . 
 
• Revenue from Operations  
o H1 FY26 : ₹ 230,458  crore (₹  229,074  crore in H1 FY25) 
o Q2 FY26:  ₹ 110,323  crore (₹  108,196  crore in Q2 FY25)  
• Gross Refining Margin (GRM)  
o H1 FY26: US$ 5.95 per barrel (US$  4.03 per barrel in H1 FY25) 
o Q2 FY26: US$ 8.80 per barrel (US$  3.12 per barrel in Q2 FY25)  
• Standalone Profit After Tax (PAT)  
o H1 FY26: ₹ 8,201  crore (₹  987 crore in H1 FY25) 
o Q2 FY26: ₹ 3,830  crore (₹  631 crore in Q2 FY25)  
• Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT)  
o H1 FY26 : ₹ 7,970  crore (₹  777 crore in H1 FY25) 
o Q2 FY26:  ₹ 3,859  crore (₹  143 crore in Q2 FY25)  
Operational Performance  
Refining Performance:  
• Refineries recorded  highest ever  crude throughput  of 13.23 MMT  during  H1 FY26, up 
9.7% from 12.06 MMT in H1 FY25.  
o Visakh  Refinery registered highest -ever crude throughput of 8.14 MMT operating at 
108% of its enhanced name plate capacity  of 15 MMTPA  
o Mumbai Refinery registered throughput of 5.09 MMT operating at 10 7% of its name 
plate capacity  of 9.50 MMTPA  
 
• Refineries recorded crude throughput  of 6.57 MMT  in Q2 FY26, up 4.3% from 6.30 MMT 
in Q2 FY25. 
o Visakh Refinery registered crude throughput of 3.98 MMT operating at 105% of its 
name plate capacity  
o Mumbai Refinery registered throughput of 2.5 9 MMT operating at 108% of its name 
plate capacity  
• One new grade  of crude oil was  processed during  Q2 FY26, taking the total number of new 
grades processed during H1 FY26 to five.
