ABD Maestro Continues to Expand Its Premium Gin Portfolio with the Launch 
of ZOYA PINK Mix Berries Gin 
 
A vibrant twist with berries that resets the bar for India ’s gin culture  
 
Mumbai, 9th April :  ABD Maestro Pvt. Ltd., the super-premium and luxury spirits subsidiary of 
Allied Blenders & Distillers (ABD), proudly unveils ZOYA Pink Mix Berries Gin , a small batch-
led extension of the celebrated ZOYA portfolio.  
 
The introduction of ZOYA PINK  arrives at a time when India ’s premium spirits landscape is 
witnessing strong momentum; driven by a new generation of consumers embracing c raft-led 
offerings and experimentation with cocktails . Trending as a leading global market for spirits , 
consumer demand in India is increasingly being shaped by flavour-forward p rofiles, visual 
appeal, and elevated drinking experiences .  
 
 
 
 
 

 
ZOYA Pink Mix Berries Gin artfully balances a classic juniper base with a vibrant infusion of 
strawberry, cranberry, blackberry, and raspberry.  The result is a beautifully blushed spirit 
that is both visually striking as well as indulgent, offering a smoo th, refreshingly berrilicious 
finish. 
 
Made in small batches from premium grain neutral spirits, this gin reflect s ZOYA ’s 
commitment to quality and craftsmanship. Ingredients are meticulously sourced fro m across 
the globe to deliver a balanced and refined expression. Versatile in i ts appeal, Zoya Pink lends 
itself seamlessly to a range of occasions from sunlit brunches and relaxed su ndowners to 
intimate evening gatherings. 
 
The launch further strengthens ZOYA ’s positioning as a contemporary gin brand that 
combines refinement with modern flavour exploration. 
Commenting on the launch, Bikram Basu, Managing Director, ABD Maestro , said, “At ABD 
Maestro, we view the expansion of the ZOYA portfolio as an opportuni ty to reimagine the gin 
experience. With ZOYA Pink Mix Berries Gin , we have crafted a vibrant expression that 
balances artisanal botanicals, fabulous choice of berries, wit h a smooth, indulgent profile. ” 
ZOYA Pink Mix Berries Gin is currently available in Maharashtra at a price of INR 2,500 with a 
phased rollout planned across key markets in India. Zoya Gin portfolio wit h its existing 
flavours of Watermelon Gin and Espresso Coffee Gin has a strong established prese nce in key 
markets in India and expanding its presence in travel retail and key i nternational markets.
