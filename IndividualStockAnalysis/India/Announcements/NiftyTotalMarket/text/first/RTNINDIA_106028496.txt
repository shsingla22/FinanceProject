Neobrands Limited Launches Akkord – A New Era in Musical 
Instruments with Unmatched Quality and Affordability  
New Delhi, 21st October  2024 – Neobrands Limited, a fully -owned subsidiary of RattanIndia 
Enterprises Ltd., is thrilled to announce the launch of Akkord, a revolutionary new brand in the musical 
instruments category. Akkord is poised to transform the musical landscape by offering an exceptional 
range of high -performance guitars, ukuleles, keyboards, and accessories that harmonize top -notch 
quality with unbeatable affordability.  
     
Akkord caters to musicians of all levels, from enthusiastic novices and dedicated hobbyists to seasoned 
professionals. Designed to inspire creativit y and elevate musical experiences, Akkord ’s products are 
crafted to meet the diverse needs of today ’s music community. With a starting price of just Rs. 1 ,799, 
Akkord ensures that premium -quality instruments are accessible to everyone.  
 
The 38 -inch cut -away guitar by Akkord delivers a rich, full -bodied sound and comes with essential 
accessories, offering versatility with its 18 frets. The 61 -key keyboard is engineered for comprehensive 
performance, featuring a digital display, a compact design, 55 timbres,  and 255 rhythms. It also 

                                                                                           
includes keyboard percussions, 24 demonstration songs, and tempo control, making it ideal for both 
practice and performance. Our ukuleles, crafted from premium mahogany with rosewood fretboards, 
are available in vibrant colo urs an d some models come with a built -in equalizer for personalized sound 
adjustments.  Each Akkord instrument is meticulously designed for durability and superior 
performance, catering to musicians who seek both quality and value.  
With prices starting at Rs. 1 ,799 for ukuleles, Rs. 1 ,999 for guitars, and Rs. 3 ,799 for keyboards, Akkord 
makes high -quality musical instruments accessible to all. We believe that exceptional music should be 
affordable without compromising on quality.  
Akkord ’s products are available on Amazon.in, offering the convenience of browsing and purchasing 
from home. Discover our full range and find the ideal instrument for your needs at 
www.amazon.in/Akkord . 
Ms. Anjal i Rattan,  Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises,  stated, “We are excited to introduce 
Akkord, a brand that brings together high -quality instruments and affordability. Our mission is to 
support every musician ’s journey, whether they are just starting out o r are seasoned professionals. Our 
forthcoming expansion into new product categories will further our commitment to providing 
exceptional musical gear. ”
