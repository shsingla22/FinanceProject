Sub: Signing of Memorandum of Understanding between Housing and Urban 
Development Corporation Limited (HUDCO) and Yamuna Expressway Industrial 
Development Authority (YEIDA)  
 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
 
Housing and Urban Development Corporation Limited (HUDCO) has signed a Memorandum of 
Understanding (MoU) with Yamuna Expressway Industrial Development Authority (YEIDA), a 
Government of Uttar Pradesh Undertaking on 21st August, 2024 at New Delhi.  
The MoU is a collaboration to extend long-term financial assistance in terms of loan towards 
various projects on both sides of Yamuna Expressway undertaken by YEIDA, to undertake 
comprehensive range of Fee-based Consultancy services from concept to commissioning in 
identified sectors and Project Management Consultancy Services from project conceptualisation 
to completion, at mutually decided terms.  
The MoU was signed by Shri Sanjay Kulshrestha, CMD, HUDCO and Dr. Arun Vir Singh, Chief 
Executive Officer, YEIDA in presence of other senior officials of HUDCO and YEIDA.
