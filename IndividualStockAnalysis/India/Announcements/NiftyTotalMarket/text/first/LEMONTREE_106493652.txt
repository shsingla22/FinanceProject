Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
We wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited at its meeting 
held on January 9, 2026, based on the recommendations of the Reorganization Committee, 
the Audit Committee and the Committee of Independent Directors, has inter -alia, considered 
and approved a Composite Scheme of Arrangement .   
 
The Board has also approved the execution of the Share Purchase Agreement  (“SPA”) by and 
amongst the Company, APG Strategic Real Estate Pool N.V.  (“APG”) , Coastal Cedar 
Investments B.V. (Investor), an affiliate of Warburg Pincus , and Fleur Hotels Limited  (Fleur) , 
for the sale of APG’s entire shareholding of 41.09%  in the Fleur to the Investor.  
 
Simultaneously, the Board has also approved the execution of the Shareholders’ Agreement 
(“SHA”) by and amongst, the Fleur, the Company, Mr.  Patanjali Govind Keswani, Spank 
Management Services Private Limited and the Investor . 
 
A copy of the Investor Presentation providing details on the subject matter is attached  
herewith .
