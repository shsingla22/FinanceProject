PRESS RELEASE  
Ground Breaking Ceremony  for IL JIN Electronics New Facility at Pune  
 
 
 
• IL JIN Electronics , an Amber Group company, performs ground breaking ceremony for 
new state of the art facility in Pune . 
 
• In-line with Electronic s Division growth strategy , the new facility will augment the 
capacity and capabilities  for the PCB -Assembly business  vertical.   
 
 
Gurugram , India; Feb 04th, 2025:  IL JIN Electronics, an Amber Group company, performs ground 
breaking ceremony for new  state of the art facility in Pune , Maharashtra . The new facility will be 
sbuild with 1,41,000 sq feet  area and will enhance capacity & capability through modernisation 
and automation.  
 
Mr. Sanjay Kumar Arora, Whole Time Director  of IL JIN Electronics , commented : “In-line with 
our growth strategy the new modern facility will augment the capacity and create new growth 
avenues in the PCB -Assembly vertical , and strengthen  the offerings of the Amber Group.  
 
We are well poised to be a full stack EMS company with the expansion of business applications 
in PCB-A front coupled with  the expansion of Ascent facility at Hosur, and JV with Korea Circuit  in 
the bare board PCB front ”  
 
 
 

About Amber Group  
Amber Group is a market leader in the HVAC industry, serving customers since 1990, The  
Group (along with its subsidiaries) operat es across three  business  verticals “Consumer Durables  
Division”, “Electronic s Division” and “Railway Subsystems &  Defense Division”.  
 
“Consumer Durables” vertical comprises of RAC finished goods, RAC components and  
Non-RAC components. “ Electronics Division” consisting of EMS and PCB vertical delivers 
solutions to various segments of industries  including  consumer durable, consumer electronics, 
automotive,  industrial , defense, space and others . “Railway Subsystems & Defense  vertical” 
provides integrated solutions to rolling stock customers, including Indian  Railways, Metro, RRTS, 
buses and Defen se, offering customised solutions in HVAC, doors,  gangways, and pantry 
systems.
