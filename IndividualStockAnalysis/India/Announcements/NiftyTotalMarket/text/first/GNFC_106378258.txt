We hereby inform that on September 19, 2025, a sudden leakage occurred at the TDI-II Plant, 
Dahej, necessitating an immediate shutdown of the plant as a precautionary measure. The safe 
shutdown procedures were promptly and effectively executed. 
 
The incident remained confined within the plant premises, with no gas release beyond the plant 
boundary, no damage to property, and no loss of life. 
 
In line with the Company’s strong safety culture, the TDI-II Plant will be restarted after 
completion of the necessary checks and remedial measures.
