With reference to the captioned subject and our intimation dated 14 November 2025, pursuant to Regulation 
30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended (“SEBI (LODR) 
Regulations”), we hereby inform the exchanges that IL JIN Electronics (India) Private Limited (“IL JIN”), a 
material subsidiary of the Company, has acquired 80% stake in the equity share capital of Shogini Technoarts 
Pvt Ltd (“Shogini”) on 1 December 2025, for a total purchase consideration of approximately Rs. 506 Crore. 
 
Consequent to the aforesaid acquisition, Shogini has become a subsidiary of IL JIN and a step-down subsidiary 
of Amber Enterprises India Limited (“the Company”). 
 
The disclosures required under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, in line with SEBI Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 11 November 2024 and other applicable circulars, have already been duly 
submitted as Annexure – A and Annexure – B to our intimation dated 14 November 2025. 
 
This intimation shall also be available on the Company’s website at www.ambergroupindia.com . 
 
We request you to kindly take the above on record and disseminate the same on your website.
