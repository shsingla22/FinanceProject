Sub.:  Disclosure under  the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (‘SEBI LODR ’) – Sale of NPA Portfolio to ARC  
 
Pursuant to the applicable provisions of the SEBI LODR, please be informed that the Board of Directors 
of Bandhan Bank Limited (‘ the Bank ’), on Monday, June 15, 2026 , has approved the proposal for sale 
of identified Non-Performing Assets (' NPA ‘) with more than 180 Days Past Due ( ‘DPD ’), pertaining to 
the Housing Finance Portfolio of the Bank , having  principal outstanding amounting to Rs. 303.74 Crore, 
as on May 31, 2026 , through a bidding process with Asset Reconstruction Companies (‘ ARC’). The Bank 
shall go for bidding as per Swiss Challenge method for sale of the aforesaid NPA Portfolio.  
 
You are requested to take note of the above.  
 
This disclosure is being simultaneously uploaded on the Bank’s website, www.bandhan.bank.in .
