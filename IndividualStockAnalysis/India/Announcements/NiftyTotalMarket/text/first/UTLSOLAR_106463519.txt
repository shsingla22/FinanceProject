Fujiyama Power Systems Limited 
Appoints Surya Kumar Yadav as its Brand Ambassador 
New Delhi, 8" December 2025 — Fujivama Power Systems Limited (“Fujiyama” or the “Company’”) 
(BSE: 544613 | NSE: UTLSOLAR), one of India’s leading providers of rooftop solar solutions, offering 
an extensive portfolio of over 500 SKUs across solar panels, inverters, lithium and tubular batteries, 
chargers and power-electronics systems, announced the appointment of cricketing star Surya Kumar 
Yadav (SKY) as its Brand Ambassador. 
Widely celebrated for his dynamic style of play, consistency and high-energy performance, SKY 
reflects the values that define Fujiyama Solar’s focus on performance, reliability and technology-driven 
solutions. 
With a legacy of over two decades of UTL group of trust built through world-class solar panels, 
inverters and energy-storage solutions, ‘Fujiyama Solar’ further contributes to empowering Indian 
homes and businesses with clean, affordable, and sustainable energy. The association with Surya 
Kumar Yadav marks a significant step in increasing consumer confidence and accelerating India’s 
shift towards green energy. 
Commenting on the agreement, Mr. Yogesh Dua, CEO and Joint Managing Director, Fujiyama 
Power Systems said: 
“Surya Kumar Yadav represents performance, dependability and a fearless approach and qualities 
that resonate strongly with the ‘Fujiyama Solar’ brand. We are delighted to welcome him to the 
Fujiyama family. Together, we aim to inspire millions of Indians to ‘Hit Energy Bills for a Six’ by 
adopting rooftop solar solutions.” 
Surya Kumar Yadav, who is represented by RISE Worldwide, shared his enthusiasm about the 
partnership: 
“lam happy to be associated with Fujiyama Solar, a brand committed to building a greener and more 
energy-efficient India which is a need of the hour. Solar power and sustainable living are the future, 
and Fujiyama’s innovation and trust make it a natural choice for every household.” 
With this collaboration, ‘Fujiyama Solar’ plans to launch nationwide promotional campaigns 
highlighting its on-grid/off-grid rooftop solar solutions, including advanced inverter technologies, 
energy-storage solutions, and long-life solar panels designed for Indian conditions. 
   
  
  About Fujiyama Power Systems Limited: 
Fujiyama Power Systems Limited is one of India’s leading providers of rooftop solar solutions, offering 
an extensive portfolio of over 500 SKUs across solar panels, inverters, lithium and tubular batteries, 
chargers and power-electronics systems. With 29 years of operating experience, the company 
combines strong engineering capabilities with an integrated manufacturing model spanning four 
facilities across Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh and Haryana, and a 2 GW fully integrated SPGS 
(Solar Power Generating System) expansion at Ratlam. Fujiyama’s business is predominantly B2C, 
serving Indian households through a deep distribution and service network of more than 7,500 channel 
partners, including distributors, dealers, exclusive Shoppes and service engineers, enabling seamless 
delivery, installation and after-sales support. Its strong presence in Tier-2 and Tier-3 markets, together 
with backward integration in key components, supports cost efficiency and supply-chain resilience. 
With 1 GW of solar-cell capacity expected to be commissioned and significant expansion underway, 
Fujiyama is well positioned to capture India’s accelerating rooftop solar opportunity. For more details, 
please visit: httos://www.utlsolarfujiyama.com/
