Subject : Clarification on news article appearing in The Economic Times dated  October 12, 
2026   
 
This is with reference to a news article appearing in The Economic Times dated October 12, 2026  
titled “FMCG Majors in Race for Big Bite of Snacks Brand Farmley”, which refers to Orkla India 
Limited as one of the parties purportedly evaluating a transaction involving Farmley.  
 
The Company wishes to clarify that the news article is speculative in nature. As part of its ordinary 
course of business, the Company evaluates various strategic opportunities from time to time. As on 
date, there is no definitive proposal or event that has  occurred in relation to the subject matter of the 
article that requires disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015.  
 
The Company remains committed to making disclosures in accordance with applicable law and will 
inform the stock exchanges of any material development as and when a disclosure obligation arises.  
 
This clarification is being issued to avoid speculation and to ensure dissemination of accurate 
information to investors.  
 
We request you to take this on record and treat the same as compliance.
