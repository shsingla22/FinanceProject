Noida, 16th November , 2026  
 
Dixon enters into an Asset Purchase Agreement with Vimal wherein Dixon will acquire movable assets such as 
Moulds, R esearch & Development Lab equipment , Basket Assembly Korean machines, jigs and fixtures  etc. from 
Vimal for an amount of up to INR 49,75,00,000/ - (Rupees Forty Nine Crores and Seventy Five Lakhs Only) 
excluding applicable taxes and subject to satisfactory completion of conditions precedent as set out in the 
Agreement.  
 
Commenting on this transaction, Mr. Atul B. Lall, Vice Chairman & Managing Director, Dixon 
Technologies (India) Limited  said, “Signing this asset purchase agreement marks an important milestone in 
strengthening our washing machine business and advancing our growth strategy. The acquisition will enhance 
our semi-automatic washing machine - ODM capabilities and enable us to deepen our engagement with marquee 
anchor customers, thereby increasing our wallet share through a broader product portfolio and greater 
manufacturing capabilities. It will also strengthen our position in the outsourced semi -automatic washing 
machine segment and enhance our overall share in the Indian washing machine market.   
 
The integration of these assets with our existing manufacturing ecosystem is expected to improve capacity 
utilisation, drive operational efficiencies and provide greater economies of scale. Additionally, the acquired 
moulds, R&D laboratory equipment, specialised machinery and technical infrastructure will strengthen our 
product development, engineering and design capabilities, enabling us to respond more effectively to evolving 
customer requi rements and accelerating  new product introductions.  We believe this acquisition will further 
consolidate our position as a leading washing machine ODM in India, deepen our strategic customer 
relationships and create a stronger platform for sustainable, pro fitable growth .” 
 
About Dixon Technologies (India) Limited   
 
Dixon Technologies (India) Limited is the largest* home -grown design -focused and solutions company engaged 
in manufacturing products in the consumer durables, lighting and mobile phones markets in India. Its diversified 
product portfolio includes (i) Consumer elect ronics like LED TVs; (ii) Home A ppliances like washing machines; 
(iii) Lighting products like LED bulbs and tubelights, downlighters; (iv) Mobile phones; (v) Wearables and 
Hearables; (v i) Refrigerators; and (vii) Telecom and IT hardware products. Dixon also provides solutions in 
reverse logistics i.e. repair and refurbishment services of LED TV panels.
