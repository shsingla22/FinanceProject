Subject: Clarification on certain news articles pertaining to one “DB Stock Broking” and some 
of those news articles contain the name of “Angel One” as being associated with the said “DB 
Stock Broking”. 
 
With reference to the captioned articles, pertaining to one “DB Stock Broking” which contains 
the name of “Angel One” as being associated with the said “DB Stock Broking”, kindly take note 
of our response/ clarification: 
 
In this regard, we would like to clarify and bring to your notice that the said entity “DB Stock 
Broking” is not our Authorized Person (AP). However, Deepankar Barman (whose name is being 
reported in the news articles as being associated with DB Stock Broking) is registered as an 
Authorized Person (AP) with us in his individual capacity since 2019. 
 
We wish to further inform you that while we are conducting a thorough investigation of this AP, 
we have also initiated the process of termination of this AP on August 21, 2024 and the same has 
been informed to the Exchanges. 
 
Deepankar Barman is registered as an AP with us since 2019 and the total number of customers 
mapped to him are 280. In the past 12 months, only 44 nos. of the said clients have placed trades. 
The total pay-in amount from this AP and all clients mapped to this AP in the last 12 months is INR 
82,77,367. Our data shows that the total trading volume of this AP and the mapped customers 
with Angel One Limited is miniscule. Therefore, we are unable to comprehend and/or comment 
on the figures which are being reported in some news articles. 
 
We would further like to inform you that we follow a robust AP audit process carried out by our 
dedicated Audit team. We carry out detailed audits of our APs annually in terms of Circular No. 
NSE/INSP/42448 dated October 18, 2019 and Circular No. NSE Circular No. NSE/COMP/56947 
dated June 02, 2023. All active APs are inspected once every 3 years. We carry out inspection of 
atleast 30% of our APs in a calendar year and for APs’ meeting the additional criteria mentioned 
in Exchange Circular dated October 18, 2019, the said APs are inspected annually. As per process, 
the Company had conducted annual AP audits for Deepankar Barman and there were no negative 
observations found against him.
