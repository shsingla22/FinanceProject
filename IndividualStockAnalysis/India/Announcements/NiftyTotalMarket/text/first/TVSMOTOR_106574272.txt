Bengaluru,  March  30, 202 6: The TVS Apache RTR 160 4V has redefined the 160cc 
segment  - setting new benchmarks as the most powerful and widely celebrated motorcycle in 
its class.  TVS Motor Company (TVSM), a global leader in the two and three- wheeler 
segments,  announced today  the introduction of two new variants alongside the existing 
top variant , further strengthening the TVS Apache RTR 160 4V’s position in the 
premium performance motorcycle segment. With this update, the Projector headlamp, 
All-LED Lighting and Assist & Slipper Clutch, will now be available across the TVS 
Apache RTR 160 4V range.  
 
With a strong commitment to democratizing racing, for the last two decades, TVS Apache has 
built world -class performance machines that nurtures a thriving and passionate global 
community of riders who share the brand’s racing spirit.  TVS Apache motorcycles are deeply 
rooted in TVS Racing’s “Track to Road” philosophy, bringing race- derived agility, precision 
and high performance to everyday riding.  
 
Built on a foundation of performance excellence, cutting- edge technology, and race -inspired 
design, the latest updates elevate the TVS Apache RTR 160 4V rider experience. The  
introductions  focus on enhancing rider safety, night -time visibility  and control , while 
retaining the motorcycle’s proven performance and racing DNA. T he new variants  of the 2026  
TVS Apache RTR 160 4V are now equipped with best -in-segment Class- D projector 
headlamp with LED DRLs , a comprehensive all -LED lighting setup including LED turn 
signal lamps, and an Assist & Slipper Clutch that enables smoother downshifts and 
improved rider  comfort. The top variant  (USD with TFT  & Traction Control )  already features  
these advanced technologies.
