PRESS RELEASE  
 
M R Jaishankar, Executive Chairman of Brigade Enterprises Limited, Honoured with 
Lifetime Achievement Award at The Economic Times Real Estate Awards 2026  
 
Bengaluru, February  26, 2026 : Brigade Group is proud to announce  that M R Jaishankar,  Executive Chairman  
of Brigade Enterprises Limited,  has been conferred with the prestigious Lifetime Achievement Award  at the 
Economic Times Real Estate Awards 2026. The ceremony, held on December 25 in Mumbai, celebrated 
industry leaders whose work has left a lasting impact on India’s real estate landscap e. 
 
This honour stands as a testament to Mr. Jaishankar’s remarkable four -decade -long journey —one defined 
by vision, resilience, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to shaping cities and communities. Under 
his leadership, Brigade Group has grown into one of India’s most respected real estate developers, creating 
spaces that reflect trust, quality and endless possibilities.  
 
Reflecting on the recognition, M R Jaishankar  said:  
 
“The last 40 years in the real estate industry have been with learning, growth, and truly memorable experiences. I am 
grateful to have been part of an industry that builds not just structures, but aspirations and communities. I would like 
to thank  The Econ omic Times for this honour and for recognising our journey.”  
 
The Brigade family extends its warmest congratulations to Mr. Jaishankar on this well -deserved accolade.
