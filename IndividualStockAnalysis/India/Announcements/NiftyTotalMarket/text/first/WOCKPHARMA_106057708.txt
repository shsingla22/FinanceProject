ZIDEBACTAM ( WCK 6777) :         
The Company’s another breakthrough antibiotic  
once -a-day β - lactam enhancer, WCK 6777 with 
unique out -patient treatment advantage has been 
granted Fast Track designation by US FDA and has 
successfully completed Phase I study conducted by 
National Institutes of Health, US.  
Additionally, recognizing its potential to meet significant unmet medical needs, the US FDA 
has recently granted Fast Track designation to WCK 6777 for the treatment of complicated 
urinary tract infections (c UTI), including pyelonephritis, and complicated intra -abdominal 
infections (cIAI).  
WCK 6777 is the only once -a-day drug in global antibiotic pipeline designed for outpatient -
parenteral antimicrobial therapy (OPAT) in ambulatory settings. WCK 6777 is activ e against 
entire range of meropenem -resistant Gram negative pathogens generally encountered in 
community as well as hospital urinary tract infections (UTI) and intra -abdominal infections 
(IAI). Such a therapeutic option is expected to cut hospital admissio ns, facilitate early patient 
discharge and thus offer patient -centred care for MDR infections.  
The promising safety data from this study paves the way for the advancement of WCK 6777 
into Phase II / III clinical trials.
