PVR INOX BRINGS SMART CINEMAS TO INDIA’S GROWTH 
MARKETS, BEGINNING WITH BIHAR’S MUZAFFARPUR, WITH 
‘MIRZAPUR’ MARKING THE OPENING  
~Bringing world- class cinema, immersive technology and the signature PVR INOX 
experience to audiences beyond the metros  ~ 
National, September 4, 2026:  A new chapter in PVR INOX’s expansion across India’s growth markets 
begins in Muzaffarpur with the opening of its first SMART Cinemas property in Bihar. The cinema opens 
with the highly anticipated Mirzapur , bringing the signature PVR INOX experience to one of North Bihar’s 
prominent commercial and educational centres, known for its entrepreneurial spirit, rich cultural heritage 
and evolving retail landscape.  
The four -screen property adds to PVR INOX’s existing network in Bihar, taking its total screen presence 
in the state to 11 screens. With a seating capacity of 644 , the cinema features Christie’s 4K RGB laser 
projection system, high-fidelity 7.1 surround sound and last-row recliner seating.  
The opening marks the first step in PVR INOX’s strategy to expand its presence across India’s emerging 
growth markets. Designed specifically for Tier III cities, SMART Cinemas combines the PVR INOX 
experience with a capital -efficient, asset-light model. 
As aspirations rise and consumption patterns evolve, cities beyond the metros are becoming an 
increasingly important part of India’s cinema journey. Muzaffarpur reflects this shift, with a growing 
audience seeking contemporary entertainment experiences closer to home. With SMART Cinemas, PVR 
INOX is extending its reach to this new generation of moviegoers while strengthening its presence in the 
city’s evolving entertainment landscape. 
Mr. Ajay Bijli, Managing Director, PVR INOX Limited, said, “For nearly three decades, PVR INOX has 
defined and shaped India's changing cinema landscape. With SMART Cinemas, we are taking the next 
step by bringing our experience to a new generation of audiences in India's growth markets. Muzaffarpur 
is an important beginning for us , a city with a strong commercial and cultural identity and rising 
aspirations. Our vision is to make world-class cinema more accessible across the country, while creating 
local opportunities and contributing to the growth of the ecosystems we enter.”  
Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Bijli, Executive Dir ector, PVR INOX Limited, said, “The opportunity in India's 
growth markets is significant, and SMART Cinemas gives us a new way to participate in this growth. The asset light model  allows us to work closely with local developers and entrepreneurs, combining their 
understanding of the market with PVR INOX’s operating expertise. This gives us the ability to build a wider network in a capital -efficient manner, while maintaining the standards and guest experience that 
are central to our brand.”  
The Muzaffarpur property has been developed in partnership with ICON Plaza Mall, creating a new 
entertainment anchor for the destination and strengthening the organised retail ecosystem in the city.  
 
 
 
 
 Mr. Amar Nath Pandey and Mr. Raghav Pandey, Directors, ICON Plaza Mall,  said, “This is a 
partnership we strongly believe in. We have invested in building this cinema because we are confident 
that PVR INOX's brand, operating discipline and reach will not only bring audiences to these screens but 
also contribute to footfall acr oss ICON Plaza Mall. Muzaffarpur has never had an experience like this, 
and we are proud to be the first to bring it to the city. We are actively working with PVR Inox to identify 
and evaluate more opportunities to expand such cinemas in other cities in the state. ” 
PVR INOX Muzaffarpur is now open to the public, with show timings and ticket bookings available through 
the PVR INOX website and mobile application, as well as traditional online aggregator platforms.

ABOUT PVR INOX LIMITED   
 
PVR INOX Limited is India’s largest film exhibition company, operating 1,785 screens across 356  
properties in 114  cities in India and Sri Lanka. Formed through the merger, in 2023, of two of the country’s 
most established and widely recognized cinema brands, PVR Limited a nd INOX Leisure Limited, the 
company represents the convergence of scale, operational excellence, and long-term creative vision. 
Operating as a unified platform, PVR INOX brings together deep industry expertise and a strong 
consumer -centric approach to del iver world -class cinema experiences.  
 
PVR INOX continues to invest in continuous innovation, next-generation formats, and immersive 
experiences, enabling the seamless introduction of global content and storytelling to audiences across 
the region. Guided by international benchmarks and evolving audience preferences, the company 
remains committed to shaping the future of out-of-home entertainment in South Asia. Its portfolio spans 
thoughtfully designed child-friendly auditoriums, state-of -the-art projection and immersive sound 
technologies, a diverse and curated food and beverage offering, film and non-film programming, and an 
expansive suite of premium large-format screens.
