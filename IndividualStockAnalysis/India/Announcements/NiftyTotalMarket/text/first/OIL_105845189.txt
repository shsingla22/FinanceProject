PRESS RELEASE  
Clarification w.r.t.  the news item s published in local Newspapers  
 
Reference the news items published in local newspapers concerning OIL, the 
response is provided herewith.   
The liquidated damage  (LD) as reported has already been recovered w.r.t. the 
construction of ETP at Tengakhat, Dibrugarh.  
For projects, viz., Baghjan Madhuban Pipeline  was completed on time  and the 
connectivity to the national gas grid will be made at the earliest aligned with North -
East Hydrocarbon Vision 2030 . Efforts are underway to complete Nadua -Khagarijan 
OCS  and Baghjan GGS by Decemer 2024.  The delay against these projects will be 
dealt as per contractual provisions  once commissioned .  
Apropos  queries raised by CAG during their annual evaluation process , responses 
are being submitted  by OIL .  
Pursuant to grant of M AHARATNA  status to OIL, restructuring and automation of 
various activities are underway  in FHQ at Duliajan  to bring in operational efficiency. 
The media reports regarding shifting of offices are ill informed and is being denied 
herewith.     
Further, OIL has decided to develop Duliajan as a SMART city and necessary 
actions have already been initiated.   
The above may be brought to the notice of all the stakeholders for kind information.  It 
is requested to contact OIL spokesperson  at 9435749758 for ensuring v eracity of 
such information prior to publication .
