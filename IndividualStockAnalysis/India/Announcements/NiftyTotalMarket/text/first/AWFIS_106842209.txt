requirements that need to be addressed before completion of the transaction. The revised completion 
timeline has not yet been finalised, and will be determined after taking into account the time required

the Business Transfer Agreement, as amended, from time to time and applicable laws.  
Until completion of the transaction, the Company will continue to operate the Business Undertaking in 
the ordinary course of business. The Company will continue to keep the Stock Exchanges informed of 
material developments in relation to the proposed transaction.
