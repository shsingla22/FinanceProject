Subject: Cancellation and Fresh Grant of Employee Stock Options under Phase –  V of 
the RFL ESOP Plan 2014 –  Disclosure pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended 
 
Dear Madam/ Sir,  
 
Pursuant to the provisions of the Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR Regulations”), as amended from time to time, 
we hereby inform that Nomination and Remuneration Committee of the Company at its meeting 
held today i.e. Friday, November 13, 2026, approved the cancellation of 27,600 Employee 
Stock Options (“Options”) granted under the Phase-V of the RFL Employee Stock Option Plan, 
2014 (“RFL ESOP Plan -2014”)  to one employee, pursuant to his resignation from the services 
of the Company.  
 
Further, pursuant to the provisions of RFL ESOP Plan-2014 the cancelled options were  added 
back to the stock options pool and were made available for future grants, subject to compliance 
with the provisions  of the applicable laws. 
 
Furthermore, the committee has also approved fresh grant of 14,700 ( Fourteen Thousand 
Seven Hundred Only ) Options convertible into equal number of Equity Shares of the Company 
of face value of Re. 1/- each, under the Phase-V of RFL ESOP Plan- 2014, to the eligible 
employees of the Company.  
 
The relevant disclosure, as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR Regulations 
pursuant to Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD- POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 
30, 2026 is annexed herewith.  
 
The same is for your information and record please.  
 Thanking You, 
 
For Relaxo Footwears Limited
