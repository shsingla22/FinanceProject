NEW YORK and NOIDA, India, Aug 26, 202 6 — HCLTech , a leading global technology company, 
reported significant progress in FY2 6 against its sustainability goals and commitment to becoming 
net zero by 2040.   
 
The company has achieved a 55.84% reduction in Scope 1 and 2 emissions from its FY20 baseline, 
surpassing its target of a 50% reduction by 2030 four years ahead of schedule. It has also reduced 
its total emissions footprint (Scope 1, 2 and 3) by 27.7% since FY20.  Driving this progress is 
HCLTech's accelerating shift toward clean energy. Since FY20, the company has reduced energy 
consumption from conventional sources by 31%. Renewable energy  now accounts for 49.79% of 
HCLTech’s total energy consumption and 89.65% of energy use d in its data centers , reflecting 
sustained decarbonization efforts across the comp any’s operations and value chain.   
 
HCLTech has also strengthened its leadership in resource stewardship . The company replenishes 
51 times more water than it consumes across its global operations and has achieved an industry -
leading water consumption rate of 10.68 liters per capita per day. Reinforcing its commitment to 
circularity and sustainable workplaces , all HCLTech campuses in India have maintained Zero Waste 
to Landfill Platinum certification since 2025 . All HCLTech campus es have also achieved Platinum or 
LEED Platinum certification from leading Green Building Councils.   
“Sustainability is becoming a catalyst for innovation and long- term growth. By combining 
technology, AI and human ingenuity, we are reducing our emission footprint while helping clients 
build more resilient and sustainable businesses. As AI reshapes indust ries, we remain committed to 
deploying it responsibly, ensuring that innovation advances not only business outcomes but also environmental stewardship and societal progress,” said Vipul Arora, Global Head of Sustainability at HCLTech.  
 HCLTech invested 2.08% of revenue in employee well -being  in FY26, the highest in the IT industry. 
Women represented over 50% of the Board and 29.6% of the workforce. More than 90% of 
employees in nearshore facilities were hired locally, with women comprising 66% of that workforce. 
The number of employees with d isabilities increased by 27.4%.
