Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we would like to inform you that the 
Board of Directors ("Board") of Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd (“Company’) at its’ meeting held 
today i.e. on 07 November 2026, inter-alia  has considered and approved the following: 
 
1. Issuance of Corporate Guarantee on behalf of Sturdy Niketan Private Limited, step-down 
subsidiary 
The Board has approved issuance of Corporate Guarantee for an amount not exceeding INR 200 Crores 
(Rupees Two Hundred Crore only) in favour of ICICI Bank Ltd for securing the credit facility to be 
availed by Sturdy Niketan Private Limited, a step-down subsidiary of the Company. 
Disclosures required under Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI Listing Regulations and 
SEBI Master Circular No. Ho/49/14/14(7)2025-Cfd-Pod2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, is 
provided in ‘ Annexure-I’ . 
 
2. Change in Corporate Office 
 
The Board has approved shifting of Corporate Office of the Company from its existing premises situated 
at “8, India Exchange Place, Ruby House, 2nd Floor, Kolkata -700001 West Bengal” to the “5th Floor, 
C6/108/ New, Budge Budge Trunk Road,  Kolkata - 700088, West Bengal” with effect from 07 
November 2026.  
 
Upon such shifting, the existing Corporate Office located at 8, India Exchange Place, Ruby House, 2nd 
Floor, Kolkata – 700001, West Bengal shall continue to be maintained as an additional place of office 
of the Company at Kolkata. 
 
We further confirm that there is no change in the registered office address of the Company, which 
remains the same as: 23A, Netaji Subhas Road, 8th floor, Suite no 15, Kolkata-700001, West Bengal.
