Ahmedabad, May  28, 202 6: The Anup Engineering Limited (ANUP), one of India’s leading 
manufacturers  of static process equipment, catering to core industries such as Oil & Gas and 
Petrochemicals, emerging new ‑age energy segments including LNG/LPG and Hydrogen, as well as 
high‑technology sectors such as Nuclear and Aerospace, today announced its financial results for the 
quarter and year ended 31 March 2026.  
 
The results reflect a stable operating performance  including ind ustry leading margin , despite a difficult  
geopolitical and macroeconomic environment.  
 
Consolidated  Financial Highlights : 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
• Phase -2 of the Kheda manufacturing facility was commissioned at the end of January 2026,

segments positions it well to enhance capabilities, diversify revenue streams, and strengthen
