Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015  - Updates  on Prag  Distillery (P) Ltd   
 
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
We refer to our earlier disclosure dated May 30, 2024  on M/s. Prag Distillery (P) Ltd  (Prag), 
wholly owned subsidiary of Tilaknagar Industries Ltd.  We now wish to inform you that the 
Office of the Commissioner of Distilleries & Breweries, Andhra Pradesh, Vijayawada has 
extended the validity of the Letter of Intent (LOI)  issued earlier for Grant of Expansion Licence  
for augmentation of Bottling capacity by 200 lakh proof litres  (approx 30  lac cases per annum) , 
in favour of Prag at its factory in Andhra Pradesh.  
 
The above extension of LOI is subject to compliance with applicable rules and payment of LOI 
fees along with interest  till the date of payment  and obtaining the Expansion Licence up to 
August 31, 2025 . The Board of Directors ( “Board ”) of Prag has considered the above proposal 
and has agreed to pay the licence  fees and the interest till the date of payment  aggregating to   
Rs 34 crores  (approximately) , to carry out the Expansion project , subject to the Holding 
Company’s approval and availability of funds.  Accordingly, t he Board of the Holding Company 
at its meeting held today i.e on July 2 9, 2025 has approved the expansion project of Prag and 
agreed in principle to provide financial assistance  to Prag and has authorized the Finance 
Committee to  take all necessary steps and actions on behalf of the Compan y to effect the said 
assistance . 
 
This approval enables Prag  to proceed with the steps required for commencement and 
expansion of bottling operations at the site and fulfill its growing capacity requirements in 
Andhra Pradesh.
