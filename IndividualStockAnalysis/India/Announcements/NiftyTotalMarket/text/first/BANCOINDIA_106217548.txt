NRF enters into turbocharges
NRF is a leading supplier of engine cooling and air conditioning parts for the automotive
aftermarket. The total offer exists of more than 13,000 references, which is rapidly growing. As an
addition to the existing product portfolio NRF enters into turbochargers. For this purpose NRF
performed an asset deal with Alanko, a German based supplier of turbochargers.
NRF is proud to announce that it performed an asset deal with Alanko, a German based supplier of
turbochargers. This deal follows the decision of NRF to add turbocharges to its existing product
portfolio. NRF is constantly expanding the product portfolio to serve its clients in the best possible
way. This asset deal gives NRF a strong start in the turbocharger business. As part of the deal NRF
also acquired the name Alanko. NRF will employ the current management team of Alanko to secure
knowledge ofAlanko on turbochargers.
