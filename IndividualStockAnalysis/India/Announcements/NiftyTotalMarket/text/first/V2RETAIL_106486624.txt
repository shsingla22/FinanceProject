Building on strong quarterly performance, the 9M FY26 (April –December 2025) results reflect a transformative

Q3 FY26 Highlights – Sustained Momentum in Scale and Store Productivity  
 
1. Robust Top -Line Growth  
 
• Standalone revenue stood at  ₹ 927 Cr  in Q3 FY26, vs. ₹ 591 Cr in Q3 FY25  
• YoY growth of 57%  delivered on a high base  
• Sustained high -double -digit revenue growth delivered on an already high base from FY25, 
demonstrating the scalability of the Company’s value -fashion model  
 
2. Resilient Same Store Sales Growth (SSSG)

cumulativ e sales growth of 68%   
 
• Reported SSSG: 2% YoY
