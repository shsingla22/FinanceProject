1. Securities and Exchange Board of India ("SEBI") has passed a Final Order 
dated August 22, 2024 bearing reference number 
WTM/AN/CFID/CFID 1/30660/2024.;.25 in the matter of Reliance Home 
Finance Limited (hereinafter referred to as "Final Order"). 
2. In the said Final Order, -
(i) at paragraph 54. 7 (xiii) thereof, it has been held that Noticee Nos. 6-28 were 
not in violation of Regulation 4(2) of SEBI (Prohibition of Fraudulent and 
Unfair Trade Practices relating to Securities Market) Regulations, 2003 
("PFUTP Regulations"). In Table -38 of the Final Order, violations by 
Noticees are summarized. However, the violations committed by Noticee 
Nos. 6-25 & 27 are listed to erroneously include Regulation 4(2) (f), (k) and 
(r) of PFUTP Regulations. 
(ii) at paragraph 55.2 (xi) thereof, it has been held that Noticee No. 1 inter alia 
violated Regulation 33(1) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("LODR Regulations"). In Table -38 
of the Final Order, violations by Noticees are summarized. However, the 
violations committed by Noticee No. 1 listed therein erroneously do not 
include Regulation 33(1) of LODR Regulations. 
3. Therefore, in Table -38 of the Final Order, -
(i) with respect to the violations committed by Noticee Nos. 6-25 & 27, the 
reference to "4(2)(f), (k) and (r)" shall stand deleted. 
(ii) with respect to the violations committed by Noticee No. 1, after the numbers 
and symbols "30(7),", the numbers and symbols "33(1 )," shall stand 
inserted. ~
