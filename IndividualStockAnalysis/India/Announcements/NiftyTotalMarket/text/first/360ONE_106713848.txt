relating to the acquisition of 360 ONE Capital Market Private Limited (“360 ONE Capital” and

This refers to our disclosure dated January 27, 2025, regarding the execution of SPSSA, pursuant to 
which the Company acquired 360 ONE Capital. One of the conditions of the SPSSA provided for a 
lock-in of the shares of the Company allotted to Mr. Saahil Murarka and Batlivala & Karani Resources

shares allotted to an extent of 20% of their respective individual shareholding on an annual basis.

individual basis’ by executing an amendment agreement with Allottees dated today. All other terms 
and conditions of the SPSSA, including the aggregate number of shares held by Allottees and the 
lock-in remain unchanged. 
 
We request you to kindly note the above and oblige.
