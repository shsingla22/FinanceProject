Belrise Industries Reports Adj. PAT Growth of 25% YoY in Q2 FY27; 
Manufacturing Revenue Up 26%  
• Adj. PAT up 25% YoY to ₹1,663 million and Manufacturing revenue up 26% YoY in Q2  FY27  
• Diversification gathers pace with entry into large American smartphone player ’s ecosystem , a strategic 
order in Aerospace  and a technology partnership  with Yamato, Japan  for filtration technologies  
Pune, 13th November 2026: Belrise Industries Limited (BIL), one of India’s leading integrated automotive 
component manufacturers with a diverse portfolio of safety -critical systems and engineering solutions 
announced its unaudited financial results for the quarter and half year end ed 30th September 2026.

• Manufacturing Revenue up 26% to ₹23,441 Mn. as compared to ₹18,601 Mn. in Q2 FY26

o Manufacturing EBITDA Margins stood at 13.5%  
• 71% of manufacturing revenue is from powertrain -neutral products  
 
Other Business & Financial Highlights (H1 FY27)  
• Manufacturing Revenue up 23% to ₹45,420 Mn. as compared to ₹36,923 Mn. in H1 FY26

Key Operational Highlights for Q2 FY27  
Diversification Beyond Automotive  
• Entered electronics manufacturing  with a maiden LoI (Letter of Intent)  for the supply of 
CNC fixtures from an ecosystem partner of one of the largest American smartphone 
companies . Discussions are underway for other categories, including Main Logic Board (MLB) 
fixtures and Final Assembly, Testing and Packaging (FATP) fixtures.  
• Chester Hall (UK), UK -based subsidiary,  secured its first incremental order since 
acquisition , an additional order for track beams  on a legacy program for one of the largest 
aircraft platforms of the world’s leading aircraft OEM , with improved commercial terms 
including price increases.  
Technology Partnership  
• Subsequent to the quarter, entered into a License and Technical Assistance Agreement with 
Yamato Co., Ltd., Japan , granting Belrise exclusive rights to manufacture and sell air filters, oil 
filters and resin air intake manifolds  in India for automotive and industrial OEMs.  
 
 
New Order Wins  
• Progressing towards Tier-0.5 in commercial vehicles : won a program from one of India’s 
leading CV OEMs  to supply complete chassis frame assemblies  across its HCV platforms 
from a new facility at Dharwad , expected to ramp up from  Q1 FY28 . The incremental content 
per vehicle is ₹60,000 –1 lakh, with peak annual revenue potential of ₹1,500 –2,000 million . 
• Entered complete 3W body manufacturing : selected as the second source  for complete body 
and chassis assemblies  for one of the highest -selling models of one of India’s largest 2W & 
3W OEMs , adding incremental content per vehicle of ~ ₹9,000. Production from the new facility 
at Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar  is expected to ramp up from Q1 FY28 , with peak annual revenue 
potential of ₹1,000 –1,200 million . 
• Continued to scale the suspension portfolio : onboarded our 6th OEM customer , one of the 
largest premium 2W manufacturers in South India, with supplies commenced in Q3 FY27; and 
secured additional orders from a fast -growing 2W & 3W OEM , with production expected from 
Q4 FY27.  
Commenting on the Q2 & H1 FY27 performance, Mr. Shrikant Badve, Managing Director of 
Belrise Industries Limited said,  
“Q2 FY27 was a quarter of strong execution  supported by a healthy demand environment. Manufacturing 
revenue grew 26% year -on-year to ₹23,441 million, and adjusted  PAT grew 25% to ₹1,663 million.  
This quarter, Belrise stepped into a new industry altogether. We secured our  maiden LoI (Letter of Intent)  
for the supply of CNC fixtures from an ecosystem partner of one of the largest American smartphone 
companies . Electronics manufacturing in India is set to become one of the country’s largest , best-cost 
manufacturing ecosystems, and this order gives us an opportunity to showcase the precision engineering 
capabilities we have built over the years.  
We also s ecured an incremental track beam order for one of the world’s largest aircraft OEMs , marking 
our first order extension  since the acquisition of Chester Hall in the UK. The order strengthens our 
participation in the legacy program of one of the world’s largest aircraft platforms, with an extended long -
term supply commitment.  
After the quarter, our technology partnership  with Yamato of Japan added air filters, oil filters and resin 
air intake manifolds technologies to our R&D repertoire , strengthening our position with Japanese OEMs  
and increasing our lightweighting capabilities . 
We remain confident in diversifying business  beyond automotive , increasing partnership s with global 
OEMs and growing our order book while maintaining a healthy balance sheet . This positions us well for 
the second half of the year and beyond. ” 
 
 
About Belrise Industries Limited (BIL)  
Belrise Industries Limited is a leading automotive component company providing technology led solutions based in 
Pune, India. BIL offers a diverse range of safety critical systems and other engineering solutions for two -wheelers, 
three -wheelers, four -wheel ers, commercial vehicles and agri -vehicles.  
The Company's product portfolio includes metal chassis systems, polymer components, suspension systems, body -in-
white components and exhaust systems, among others. The products are largely agnostic to vehicle powertrain types, 
reflecting their ability in c atering to both electric vehicles and internal combustion engine vehicles. BIL specializes in 
precision sheet metal pressing and fabrication with 24% market share in two -wheelers metal fabrication. BIL has 2 7 
manufacturing facilities across 10 states in India and 2 countries outside India, with long standing customer 
relationships with more than 38 OEMs.  
BIL leverages its expertise in precision engineering by diversifying into non -automotive segments like aerospace & 
defense, renewable energy and electronics.
