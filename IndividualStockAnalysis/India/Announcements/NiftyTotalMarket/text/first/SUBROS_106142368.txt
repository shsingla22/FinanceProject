An Explanatory Statement pursuant to Sections 102, 110 and 
other applicable provisions, if any, of the Act, pertaining to the

meeting held on January 30, 2025  has appointed Mr. Ravi

remote e-voting process in a fair and transparent manner.

Participants. Shareholders are advised to update their mobile number and email Id in their demat accounts in order to access
