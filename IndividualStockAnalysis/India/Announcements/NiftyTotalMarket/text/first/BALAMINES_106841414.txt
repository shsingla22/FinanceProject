Total Revenue for Q2FY27 stood at ₹ 472 crore, as compared to ₹ 348 crore in Q2FY26, 
indicating stable  operational performance. Volumes were maintained at similar levels, supported 
by stable commodity prices and consistent demand across key segments.  
Total volumes stood at 24,491 MT for Q2FY27 as against 26,165 MT in Q2FY26.

Q2FY26.

Growth Drivers & Expansion Pipeline  
The Company continues to strengthen its growth platform through capacity expansion, import 
substitution opportunities, and a sharper focus on value -added specialty chemicals.  
During the period, commercial production  commenced at the expanded Acetonitrile (ACN) facility, 
increasing installed capacity to 1,440 TPM. The upgraded plant incorporates advanced 
technology and enhanced process efficiencies, positioning the Company to improve operational 
performance over the m edium term.  
Following the successful commissioning of its 100,000 TPA Dimethyl Ether (DME) plant, India's 
first commercial -scale DME facility, the Company has established a presence in a high -potential 
import substitution market with applications spanning  healthcare, personal care products as 
Aerosol application apart from usage as a fuel for various applications. Further, the DME Road 
Tanker valves are finally approved after a rigorous testing process of PESO. The next phase of 
work is in the pipeline to establish a robust network of customers as the company has already 
studied the ever expanding Aerosol market. There are opportunities for export of DME which are 
explored on a regular basis.  
The next product planned for commercialization is N -Methyl Morpho line (NMM), further expanding 
the specialty chemicals portfolio.  
In parallel, Balaji Speciality Chemicals is progressing with its expansion program that is aimed at 
increasing the share of high -value specialty chemicals. The Unit -I brownfield expansion, 
comprising EDA derivatives such as DETA, TETA, Piperazine, AEEA, and AEP, remains on track 
for commissioning during the end of  FY27. The Unit -II greenfield project at MIDC, Chincholi, 
which will manufacture HCN, NaCN, EDTA, and EDTA -2Na, is targeted for com missioning in Q4 
FY27.  
These investments are expected to broaden the product portfolio, enhance backward integration, 
increase exposure to higher -margin specialty chemicals, and create a strong foundation for the 
Company's next phase of growth.  
  
On the pe rformance, Mr. D. Ram Reddy, Managing Director, commented ,  
Q2 FY27 reflected resilient execution and steady progress on our strategic priorities. As we enter 
H2 FY27, our focus is firmly on driving higher utilization, improving operating efficiencies and

in value -added and import -substitution products. We are now focu sed on scaling these capacities

Overall, the volumes have dented due to various external factors, howev er the company has

Our growth pipeline remains robust, with N -Methyl Morpholine (NMM) and multiple specialty

With a stronger product mix, a visible expansion roadmap and a disciplined focus on execution,

long-term value for shareholders.  
About Balaji Amines Limited (BAL)  
Balaji Amines Ltd (BAL), set up in 1988, is a leading manufacturer of Aliphatic Amines in India. 
Broadly, the company is specialised in manufacturing Methyl Amines, Ethyl Amines, Derivatives 
of Aliphatic Amines and Specialty Chemicals. Its business is broadly classified into three 
segments - Amines, Specialty  Chemicals and Derivatives. BAL commenced manufacturing of 
Methyl Amines in 1990 and later added facilities of Ethyl Amines and other derivatives of Methyl 
Amines and Ethyl Amines. The company enjoys leadership position in many of its products like 
Monomet hylamine (MMA), Dimethyl amine (DMA), Trimethylamine (TMA), Dimethyl Amino 
Ethanol (DMAE), Mono Methyl Amino Ethanol etc.  
 It caters to a host of industries like Pharma, Agro Chemicals, Paint Stripping & Resins, Rubber 
cleaning chemicals etc. The company h as four state of the art manufacturing sites – three near 
Solapur and one near Hyderabad. In addition, BAL possesses a fully furnished Laboratory which 
helps the company in development of newer products. A majority of its products are being 
exported to maj or customers worldwide and the company has become one of the leaders in 
Specialty Chemicals among the International Specialty Chemical players. The company also 
operates a 5 -star hotel in Solapur – Balaji Sarovar. It has tied up with Sarovar group for 
mana gement of this hotel.
