TVS MOTOR COMPANY IGNITES AFRICA’S PREMIUM 
MOTORCYCLE ARENA WITH THE LAUNCH OF TVS APACHE 
RTR 310 IN MOROCCO  
• 40 Years of Racing Pedigree in one machine  
• Performance engineered with a legacy of speed, style, and pure thrill  
 
Casablanca, March  28, 2026:  TVS Motor Company —a global leader in two and three- wheeler 
manufacturing— today marked a defining moment for performance motorcycling in Africa with the 
official launch of the TVS Apache RTR 310 in Morocco. Born on the racetrack and engineered for the 
road, the TVS Apache RTR 310 distils decades of competition- grade learning into a streetfighter that 
delivers track -bred agility, class -leading power delivery and an unmatched suite of rider -centred 
technologies.  
From dominating Indian roads to winning hearts of over 6.5 million customers across  Columbia, Mexico, 
PAN-Africa, Europe and South East Asia; the legend of the TVS Apache has gone global while proudly 
showcasing its Indian roots.  With Morocco’s premium motorcycle segment growing, this motorcycle  with 
a #RaceTrackToRoad  philosophy  promises an unmatched riding experience, setting new benchmarks 
and offering a gateway into the world of the freestyler.  
"As the first African  market where it is being launched, TVS Apache RTR 310 ’s launch in Morocco 
marks a significant step in the brand’s  journey . Morocco is one of the most strategically important 
markets in Africa, and the launch here first is a testament to our confidence in the Moroccan rider and the region’s appetite for genuine performance motorcycling. Built on our ‘Track to Road’ philosophy, the Apache RTR 310 carries 40 years of racing DNA into a machine that will redefine what premium means in this market. This is not just a product launch — this is TVS planting its performance flag on the African 
continent ,” said Mr. Rahul Nayak, Senior Vice President – International Business, TVS Motor 
Company.  
 Morocco’s two- wheeler industry has grown 42% in the last year . The 201– 350cc premium segment is 
one of the fastest growing categories currently accounting for  6.6% of the total market and is forecast 
to hit 10% by 2030. The launch of the Apache RTR 310 positions TVS at the apex of this rapidly 
maturing performance segment — a machine with the heritage, technology and price- to-performance 
credentials to challenge any rival.
 
Presenting at the launch event,  Mr. Omar Messouidi, Managing Director – Motorsport Maroc (Hindi 
Motors), said, “ With the Apache RTR 310, we are offering Moroccan enthusiasts access to a machine 
that can hold its own on any racetrack in the world, yet is engineered to be a thrill every single day on 
our roads. We have invested heavily in training our service network so that every RTR 310 owner 
receives a world- class ownership experience from day one. This launch marks the beginning of a new 
performance era for TVS in Morocco and across Africa .” 
 Engineered with cutting- edge racing technology and honed by TVS Racing pedigree, the TVS Apache, 
ever since its launch  20 years ago, has fuelled the adrenaline of riders across 60+ countries. This 
blazing legacy of speed, style, and pure thrill is crafted for enthusiasts who live life in the fast lane!

forged across four decades of TVS Racing — from national championships to international endurance 
circuits. Its ‘Track to Road’ philosophy is not a marketing claim; it is an engineering mandate that 
touches every system on the motorcycle.
