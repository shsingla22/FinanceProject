Speaking  on the occasion  Mr. Sanjay  Labroo,  Chairman  & Managing  Director,  Asahi  India  Glass  
Limited  said, 
 
“I am pleased to share that Q2 FY27 has been our best -ever quarter of financial performance. On a 
YoY basis, our net sales grew 36.9% to ₹1,591 crores, and EBITDA  grew 76.4% to ₹351 crores. 
EBITDA and PAT margins for Q2 stood at 22.1% and 10.5%, respectively.  
For H1 FY27, our net sales stood at approximately ₹3,010 crores, and EBITDA  was 682 crores, 
representing YoY growth of 25.3% and 69.5%  respectively. I firmly believe that our financial 
performance remained healthy due to our sustained strategy of value addition and integration.  
Amidst global uncertainties and macro -economic volatility, we continued to face significant 
inflationary pressures on input and energy costs in both our businesses. While several of these 
reasons remain beyond our control, I believe we can improve our financial performance in the 
automotive business as there is some scope to further enhance operational efficiency. I am happy 
to share that this quarter we commissioned our Phase III tempered operations at Patan and 
expanded our laminated capacities at Chennai plant. Besides the above, we supported our OEM 
customers with their 12 new model launches this quarter.  
Our Automotive glass business continues to benefit from sustained demand backed by growth and 
premiumization of automotive industry in India. Our Architectural glass business is supported by 
rising urbanization, infrastructure development, increasing emphasis on green buildings, and 
higher adoption of value -added glass products. I thank the Government of India for continuing to 
recognize the unfair trade environment faced by Indian glass industry from dumped imports and 
supporting the industry with a fair Minimum Import Price (MIP). Our Consumer Glass Solutions 
business continues to expand by offering customized glass, window s and door solutions, along with 
organized automotive glass repair and replacement services.  
AIS shall continue to invest significant resources in expanding its state -of-the-art manufacturing 
capacities, introducing new technology products and processes, strengthening vertical integration, 
enhancing customer proximity, and building indigenous capabilities across all our businesses. 
These investments are beginning to contribute meaningfully to our growth and competitive 
positioning. We continue to re -invest in the future growth of AIS.  
With continued focus on improving asset utilization, driving cost optimization and enhancing 
productivity, AIS is well positioned to capitalize on the structural growth opportunities across all 
the business segments .”~

About  Asahi  India  Glass  Limited  (AIS) 
Asahi India Glass Limited (AIS) was incorporated in 1984. It is India’s leading integrated glass 
solutions provider, with capabilities spanning the entire automotive and architectural glass value 
chain, from float glass manufacturing to processing, fabrication, installation and after -sales 
services. Promoted by the Labroo family, AGC Inc. (Japan) and Maruti Suzuki India Limited, AIS 
commands  ~80% of the  Indian  passenger  vehicle glass  market  and ~26%  of the domestic  value -added 
architectural glass market. Its diversified portfolio encompasses automotive glazing solutions 
(windshields, sidelites, backlites, sunroofs, acoustic, solar -control and head -up display (HUD)  
glass), architectural glass (float, energy -efficient glass, value -added and designer products) and 
consumer solutions (AIS Windows, AIS Glasxperts and AIS Windshield Experts). The company 
operates 15 manufacturing units and 1 1 advanced warehouses cum sub -assembly units  across India, 
supported by an aggregate float glass manufacturing capacity of 2,150 tonnes per day (TPD).  
For more  information  about  the Company  and its businesses,  please  visit www.aisglass .com
