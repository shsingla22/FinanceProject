. The global economic environment remained challenging during the year, marked
by geopolitical tensions, tariff measures and the outbreak of war in West Asia in

the historical average of 3.7 per cent recorded during 2000 to 2019. Global
headline inflation is projected at 4.4 per cent in 2026 and 3.7 per cent in 2027,
reflecting upward revisions and continuing external pressures.
