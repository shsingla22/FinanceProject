As per the terms and conditions of the tender, in respect of the four PPP Railneer plants, the Developer-cum-Operators (DCOs)
are to be reimbursed the GST on the supply of Railneer, net of the Input Tax Credit (ITC) availed by them. However, complete
information regardingtheITCavailedbytheDCOsisnotavailable.Basedon theinformation madeavailablebycertainDCOs,an
amount of ₹ 99.32 lakhs and ₹ 192.00 lakhs has been accounted for as a claim by the Company towards ITC for the quarterand
half year ended 30 September 2026, respectively, and an amount of ₹ 397.86 lakhs was accounted for during FY 2025-26.
Accordingly, the total amount accounted for inthe books of theCompany aggregatesto ₹1847.30 lakhs and ₹ 2039.30 lakhs as
at 31 March 2026 and 30 September 2026, respectively. The DCOs have contested the Company's claim towards ITC. 
The management has issued letters to the DCOs directing them to furnish GSTR data, so as to enable IRCTC to ascertain the
actual ITC availed by them. Based on such data, recovery of ITC shall be affected strictly in accordance with the contract
conditions.
The DCOs of Sankrail, Hapur & Nagpur plants have challenged the same before the Hon’ble Court. In case of Sankrail Plant, the
Hon’ble Delhi High Court has appointed an Arbitrator and restrained the Company from taking any recovery related steps in
respect of any amount pertaining to the alleged input tax credit until the learned Sole Arbitrator takes up the application under
Section 17 for consideration. Similarly, in respect of Hapur and Nagpur plants, the matter are under arbitration before a
common Sole Arbitrator for these DCOs. In case of Parassala Plant, contract has been terminated and a notice of dispute has
already been issued. Necessary action will be taken in accordance with the concession agreement of Parassala Plant.
The National Anti-Profiteering Authority (NAA) had issued a notice dated 25 February, 2022 alleging profiteering of ₹5041.44
lakhunderCGST Act,2017 for on account of non-passing thebenefit of tax reduction to consumers. The show cause notice was
contested by the Company; however, no order was passed by the Authority. Subsequently, Competition Commission of India
(CCI) was empowered to adjudicate the matter. The matter was taken up by the Principal bench of the Hon'ble Goods & Services 
Tax Appellate Tribunal (GSTAT).
The Hon'ble GSTAT, vide its judgment dated 14 October 2026, has passed an order against the Company in relation to
proceedings under Section 171 of the CGST Act, 2017, concerning alleged profiteering in respect of Rail Neer packaged drinking
water.TheHon'ble Tribunalhas acceptedtheDGAPreport dated11June2021anddirectedtheCompany todeposit thealleged
profiteered amount of ₹5,041.44 lakh in the Central and State Consumer Welfare Funds in the prescribed ratio of 50:50, along
withinterest@18%perannumfrom28June2019till thedateofdeposit. Further, theTribunalhasimposeda penalty@10%of
the profiteered amount with the provision that the penalty shall not be payable if the entire profiteered amount along with
applicableinterestisdeposited within30daysfromthedateof theorder.Theappeal tothesaidorder isbeingpreferredbefore
the Competent court of jurisdiction.
