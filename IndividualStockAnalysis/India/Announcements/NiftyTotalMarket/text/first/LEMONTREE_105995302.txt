Subject:  Clarification/Explanation with regard to Item No. 4 to 32nd Annual General Meeting 
(‘AGM’) Notice dated 7th August, 2024 
 
Ref: TO APPROVE GRANT OF STOCK APPRECIATION RIGHTS TO THE EMPLOYEES 
OF GROUP COMPANY(IES) INCLUDING SUBSIDIARY COMPANY, ITS ASSOCIATE 
COMPANY AND/OR HOLDING COMPANY (IES), IN INDIA OR OUTSIDE INDIA 
UNDER LTHL STOCK APPRECIATION RIGHTS SCHEME – 2024. 
 
Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
The Company wish to hereby submit following clarification: 
 
Considering the concerns and recommendations of proxy advisors with regard to granting Stock Appreciation 
rights to the employees of Group Companies including subsidiaries, associate/holding Company, and in order 
to adhere to good governance standards, the management of the Company shall avoid making any 
recommendations/proposals for taking up any grant to the employees of group/ associate/holding company 
with Nomination and Remuneration Committee and thereby make it possible that the grants under the LTHL 
Stock Appreciation Rights Scheme – 2024 (“Scheme”) , subject to shareholder approval, will be made 
available only to employees of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited and its existing subsidiaries.   
 
The above clarification is available at the website of the Company and shared with the respective proxy 
advisors as well.  
  
Please take the above intimation on record.
