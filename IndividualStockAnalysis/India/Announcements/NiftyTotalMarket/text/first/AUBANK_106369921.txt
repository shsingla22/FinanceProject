With deep grief and intense sorrow, we hereby inform about the sad and untimely demise of                    
Mr. Vimal Jain,  Chief Financial Officer of the Bank on September 10, 2025.  
 
Vimal Ji, as he was fondly referred to,  was associated with the Bank since 2010  and  was a pillar 
of strength for the Bank and the Finance and Accounts  function , play ing a crucial leadership role 
throughout his 15 + years of career with the Bank . He was a deeply loved and admired leader 
whose perseverance,  keen learning aptitude, and exemplary leadership capabilities paved the 
way for his emergence as a credible and respected banker .  
 
The Bank immensely benefitted from his vision and leadership during his tenure. The strong team 
created by him in Finance and Accounts will carry forward his vision and legacy with commitment 
as a true tribute to him.   
  
Mr. Vimal Jain’s sudden and unexpected passing away today morning due to a cardiac arrest  will 
be an irreparable loss to the Bank and his presence will be deeply missed. The Bank expresses  
deep sympathy and condolences to his family.  
 
The Bank is initiating necessary steps for interim succession and will update the regulators in due 
course regarding any further developments, as required under applicable RBI and SEBI 
regulations .
