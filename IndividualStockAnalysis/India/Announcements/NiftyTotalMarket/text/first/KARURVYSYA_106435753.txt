Karur Vysya Bank opens new branch in Madurai  
 
Madurai, 1 0th Nov 2025:  Karur Vysya Bank (KVB) has opened a new branch in Kochadai Village of 
Madurai West , taking the total number of branches to 48 across the city. The new  branch was 
inaugurated by Dr. Koothan U.T, MBBS, DGO, DNB(OG), FIMS, FRM , Consultant in Reproductive 
Medicine & Laparoscopy, Sri Subham Hospital, Madurai along with Dr. Hemalekha, DGO., DNB, (OBS 
& Gyne), FMA, Infertility Specialist, Laparoscopic Surgeon, Apollo Hospitals, Madurai. With this 
expansion, Karur Vysya Bank now has 896 branches across India.  
The new branch will offer a comprehensive range of banking services like accounts, deposits and loans , 
including savings and current accounts and it will cater to all basic banking transactions and specific 
needs of customers, offering an entire spectrum of banking products and financial services covering 
retail, institutional and consumer lending.  
Speaking on the branch expansion , Shri Ramesh Babu , Managing Director & CEO, Karur 
Vysya Bank,  said, “The inauguration of the new branch in Madurai is a mark of yet another  significant 
milestone in the growth and expansion journey of Karur Vysya Bank. This new branch strengthens 
our mission to drive financial inclusion by giving the local population access to modern banking facilities. 
We believe this will not only improve the convenienc e and accessibility of service to our clients but 
also generate new employment opportunities in the region. At KVB, we beli eve in developing 
relationships that last through trust, transparency, and high -quality customer service. ” 
KVB offers internet banking and mobile banking facilities.  KVB DLite, the Mobile Banking app of the 
bank, offers convenience of financial and non -financial services through 150+ features.  The app has 
been recently upgraded with several customer friendly features
