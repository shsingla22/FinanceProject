Dr. Lal PathLabs launches SOVAAKA — a next -generation wellness centre 
blending science, technology, and personalized care  
Gurugram, 6th January  2026 : Dr. Lal PathLabs  Limited ( “Dr. Lal PathLabs ”), one of India’s most trusted 
diagnostics brand s, today announced the launch of Sovaaka , its new -age Diagnostic Experience Centre 
in Gurugram.  
Built on the philosophy of “Science Behind Wellness,”  Sovaaka introduces a new standard in 
preventive and lifestyle healthcare. As an advanced wellness initiative from the house of Dr. Lal 
PathLabs, the centre brings together precision diagnostics, cutting -edge technology, and medical 
expertise to help individuals take proactive control of their health.  
Sovaaka  offers a  deeply personalised  diagnostics -led wellness experience that goes beyond routine 
health checks. Its doctor -designed programs — including Eterna (for women) and Vita (for men) — are 
customised to an individual’s age, gender, and lifestyle . It brings together  advanced diagnostics such 
as high -sensitivity blood panels, genetic testing, AI -supported imaging, and seamlessly integrated  
digital health records. With end -to-end clinical report evaluation and tailored nutrition guidance, 
Sovaaka delivers a truly comprehensive, understanding of one’s health and wellness trajectory.  
“Sovaaka marks a pivotal moment in the evolution of diagnostics — from disease detection to disease 
prediction,” said (Hony) Brig . Dr. Arvind Lal, Executive Chairman, Dr. Lal PathLabs . “In a world where 
Non -Communicable Diseases (NCDs) or Lifestyle Diseases are affecting millions  silently, science -led 
wellness and early screening play a transformative role. Testing today can truly save tomorrow.”  
Equipped with low-dose CT, 1.5T MRI, DEXA scanning, genetic analysis, and a wide menu of routine 
and advanced blood tests , the centre ensures fast turnaround, comprehensive insights, and a deeply 
personalised evaluation experience.  
“With Sovaaka, we are reimagining how preventive healthcare is delivered,” said Mr. Shankha 
Banerjee, CEO, Dr. Lal PathLabs . “By bringing diagnostics, advanced imaging, and lifestyle -based 
programmes under one roof, we are creating a destination for individuals committed to investing in 
their long -term health.”  
The launch comes at a time when India is witnessing rapid growth in the preventive health and 
wellness segment, driven by increasing incidence of Non -Communicable Diseases, rising awareness, 
evolving lifestyles, and greater  emphasis on early detection. With Sovaaka, Dr. Lal PathLabs takes a 
significant step forward in strengthening preventive care by offering comprehensive, evidence -backed 
solutions that empower individuals to understand their health better and act early.  
About Sovaaka:  
Sovaaka is the premium wellness vertical from Dr. Lal PathLabs, India’s most trusted diagnostics brand. 
With its tagline “Science Behind Wellness,” Sovaaka merges diagnostic excellence, medical expertise, 
and AI technology to offer personalized, preventive, and pr edictive health experience . 
About Dr . Lal PathLabs:  
Dr. Lal PathLabs  is a provider of diagnostic and related healthcare tests and services in India. Through 
its integrated, nationwide network, the company offer patients and healthcare providers a broad 
range of medical diagnostic tests and services for patient diagnosis , prevention, monitoring and 
treatment.  As on March  31, 2025 the company has over 298 clinical laboratories , including National 
Reference Lab at Delhi, Regional Reference Lab s at Kolkata, Bangalore & Mumbai , over 6,607  Patient 
Service Centres  (PSCs) and more than 12,365  Pick-up Points (PUPs). Its customers include individual 
patients, hospitals , healthcare providers and corporate customers.
