Rangeela Vodka goes desi swag with Jhatpat Jamun and Kala Khatta 
~ ABD Maestro brings India’ s favourite stree t flavours to the super-premi um vodka 
segmen t ~ 
Mumbai,  3rd November  2026:  ABD Maestro  Private  Limited,  the super-premium  and luxury 
spirits  subsidiary  of Allied  Blenders  and Distillers  Limited  (ABD),  has expanded  its Rangeela 
Vodka  portfolio  with  two new  Flavours, Rangeela  Jhatpat  Jamun  Vodka  and  Rangeela  Kala 
Khatta Vodka . Inspired by the flavours of India’s street carts and roadside gola stalls, ‘JJ’ and 
‘KK’ are chatpata,  masaledar  and unmistakably  filmy. The  flavours also reflect  the spirit  of 
Ranveer Singh, Co-Founder and Creative Partner at ABD Maestro, whose energy and love for 
pop culture have shaped Rangeela’s distinctive personalit y. 
Each expression is crafted for  layered complexit y, balancing fruit with savoury spice, cooling 
herbs and a hint of heat. Rangeela Jhatpat Jamun opens with fresh garden mint on the nose, 
followed by the sweet-tart richness of jamun on the palate and a gentle chilli warmth on the 
finish . Rangeela  Kala Khatta  leads  with zesty  lemon  on the nose,  while tangy tamarind  and 
the sweet-sou r, chatpata character of the much-loved gola flavour unfold on the palate. Both 
flavours are bold, refreshing expressions, best enjoyed chilled or in a mix.  

Commenting on the launch,  Bikram Basu, Vice Chairman, ABD Maestro,  said “Rangeela 
Vodka celebrates India ’s vibrant, unapologetic spirit. With Jhatpat Jamun and Kala Khatta, we 
are tapping into tastes that we instantly connect with, translating street -side love for a flavour 
into a world -class, super -premium liquid. Triple distilled and platinum chill filtered, these 
homegrown flavours deliver exceptional craft spirit for modern social occasions. ” 
The launch responds to growing consumer interest in  flavours that feel familiar, local and 
made for sharing, whether at  home gatherings , rooftop parties, casual bars or weekend 
getaways. It also reinforces ABD ’s continued focus on premiumisation through innovation -led 
brands.  Rangeela Jhatpat Jamun and Kala Khatta Contemporary Indian Flavoured Vodka  are 
available in Maharashtra in 750 ml and 180 ml bottles, priced at ₹2,500 and ₹625 respectively, 
with Goa, Haryana and Uttar Pradesh to follow shortly .

About ABD Maestro Private Limited  
ABD Maestro Private Limited is a super -premium and luxury spirits company , and a subsidiary of Allied 
Blenders and Distillers Limited, one of the leading domestic Spirits  companies  in India. Bollywood 
superstar  Ranveer Singh is a co -founder and creative partner at ABD Maestro.  
The ABD Maestro  portfolio includes The Collective Li mited Edition, Arthaus Blended Malt Scotch Whisky, 
Aodh  Irish Whiskey, Woodburns Contemporary Indian Malt Whisky, Yello  Designer Whisky, Zoya  Special 
Batch Gin and its flavours, Rangeela  Contemporary Indian Vodka, Pumori Small Batch Gin and Pink Gin, 
Segre do Aldeia Café and White Rums. Through its partnership with Roust Corporation, the company has 
introduced Russian Standard Vodka to India in three distinct segments : Original, Gold , and Platinum.
