HCLTech selected by  Carrix to modernize  port operations with AI-driven digital 
technologies  
MEXICO CITY, Mexico  and NOIDA, India , Jan 13, 202 5—HCLTech , a leading global technology 
company, today announced an agreement with Carrix , the world’s  largest independent marine  
and rail  terminal operator, to improve  Carrix’s  global port operations  with HCLTech ’s advanced  
suite of  AI of Things  (AIoT)  technology platform.  
  
HCLTech will leverage its expertise in automation, AIoT  and Vision AI  to unify Carrix’s various 
terminal  management tools into one optimized system  to help achieve its goal towards  
impl ementing  a scalable , AI-powered approach to  safe and secur e port operations . The 
integrated  AIoT platform  will enhance decision- making, streamline compliance processes and 
improve overall operational performance at Carrix’s transportation ports.  
 
The VisionX AI Edge advanced AIoT technology platform will integrate with Carrix’s network of 
cameras, in -vehicle fleet management systems and sensors to generate actionable insights 
with real -time dashboards. As a result, t hese AI -powered insights will im prove  worker safety, 
situational awareness, process workflow optimization, productivity enhancement and asset 
health monitoring  enabling increased  operational efficiency and opportunity for seamless 
business growth across its global ports . 
  
“As we continue  our digital  transformation  journey , we are confident that HCLTech ’s AI-led 
capabilities , customer -centric approach  and partner ecosystem will help us achieve the 
desired outcome of Improving safety and  efficiency of our port operations ,” said Jim Downing , 
CIO at Carrix.  
 
“We are proud to partner with Carrix to help modernize its global port network  and achieve  
higher  standards for safety, compliance and efficiency,” said Ajay Bahl, Chief Growth Officer, 
Americas, MEGA Industries, HCLTech. “ We look forward to working alongside Carrix as it 
leverages AI to transform its  international business operations.”  
 
This implementation will initially be deployed in Carrix’s transportation ports in Mexico with 
opportunity to expand to more of its  ports  located in North and South America .
