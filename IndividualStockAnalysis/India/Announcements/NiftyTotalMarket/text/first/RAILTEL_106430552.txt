Dear Sir/Madam, 
This is in continuation of our above referred letter on the captioned subject. It may be 
mentioned that as per the requirement of Clause 6.9 of Section VII-A of Chapter VII of SEBI 
Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated 11/11/2024, the matter in relation to 
NSE notice for non-compliance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 was placed before the Board 
of Directors of RailTel in their meeting held on 29/10/2025 for their comments on the same. 
2. It may be brought out that RailTel's Board has considered the matter and after due 
deliberations, expressed its views that since the power to appoint Directors including Independent 
Directors on the Board of the Company vests with the President of India acting through Ministry 
of Railways, it was beyond the control of the Company to ensure compliance with the provisions 
of Regulation 17, 18, 19, 20 and 21 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, as the Company 
cannot on its own induct or appoint Independent Directors on the Board of the Company. The 
Board requested to waive off the fine/penalty for the reported non-compliance. 
3. Accordingly, it is requested to kindly waive-off the fine levied on RailTel for non-compliance of 
SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 during the Quarter ended 30/06/2025.
