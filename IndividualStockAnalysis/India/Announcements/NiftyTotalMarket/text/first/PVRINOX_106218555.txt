 PVR INOX UNVEILS NOIDA ’S FIRST DIRECTOR ’S CUT AT DLF MALL OF INDIA, REDEFINING LUXURY 
CINEMA.  
~ Five opulent auditoriums, gourmet cuisine, and world -class service redefine luxury cinema in Noida. ~ 
 
National, May 02, 2025:  PVR INOX, India’s largest and most premium cinema exhibition company, proudly 
announces the launch of its first Director’s Cut in Noida at DLF Mall of India, under its luxury arm —The 
Luxury Collection. This milestone, in collaboration with DLF, brings Noi da’s finest luxury cinema to life, 
marking the debut of the fifth Director’s Cut in India and setting a new benchmark in high -end cinematic 
experiences. Designed to elevate movie -going, Director’s Cut seamlessly blends world -class 
entertainment with except ional hospitality, offering unparalleled comfort and luxury. With this launch, 
PVR INOX strengthens its presence in Noida with 27 screens across 2 properties and reinforces its footprint 
in Northern India with 473 screens  across 99 properties . 
PVR INOX Director’s Cut at DLF Mall of India redefines luxury cinema with five meticulously designed 
auditoriums, offering an exclusive experience for 215 guests . Each theatre combines cutting -edge 
technology, including 4K laser projection, 7.1 Dolby surro und sound, and next -gen 3D , with plush leather 
recliners for unmatched comfort . 'The Grove'  (28 seats) offers an intimate, boutique atmosphere, perfect 
for a personal cinematic escape. 'The Venetian'  (87 seats) brings the elegance of Venice with a lavish, 
grand design. 'The Theatre'  (34 seats) and 'The Library'  (34 seats) offer cozy, immersive settings for 
cinephiles, while 'The Gallery'  (32 seats) boasts a sophisticated, refined ambiance, perfect for a truly 
elevated movie experience.  
This is the first mul tiplex in North India to have all the latest Hugo  screens  and the first national multiplex 
chain with Hugo Screens ever . For the first time at PVR INOX, we are introducing Harkness Hugo -SR 
screens , specifically engineered for laser projection . These pioneering 2D and 3D movie screens  come 
with built -in speckle reduction, enhanced contrast, and an optically whiter appearance on axis, ensuring 
a stunningly vivid and immersive visual experience.
