Revolt Motors Expands into Sri Lanka, Marking First 
International Launch of Its Electric Motorcycles  
New Delhi, 16th September 2024:  Revolt Motors, India’s No.1 electric motorcycle company, is 
thrilled to announce its entry into the Sri Lankan market. In partnership with Evolution Auto 
Pvt Ltd, a leading player in Sri Lanka’s automotive industry, Revolt Motors is set to 
revolutionize the country’s mobility landscape by offering advanced  and performance -driven  
electric motorcycles designed to cater to diverse rider needs.  
Following an overwhelmingly positive response at the Colombo EV Expo, Revolt Motors has 
received a warm welcome from Sri Lanka’s growing community of electric vehicle enthusiasts. 
The partnership with Evolution Auto Pvt Ltd marks the beginning of Revolt’s expansion across 
Sri Lanka, starting with Colombo. Over time, Revolt Motors plans to extend its presence to key 
regions across the country, offering Sri Lankans access to sustainable mobility solutions.  
Known for its AI-enabled technology, Revolt’s motorcycles provide a smart and connected 
riding experience. Riders can customize sound profiles, choose riding modes, and monitor 
performance through the Revolt App.  The company is introducing all its models in Sri Lanka, 
ensuring options for thrill -seekers looking for a sporty ride as well as daily commuters seeking 
reliability and eco -friendly transportation. These electric motorcycles blend speed, comfort, 
and techn ology. Available in vibrant colours, they bring an indivi dualized touch to each ride 
while leading the zero -emission movement.  
As part of its ambitious long -term strategy, Revolt Motors plans to establish a robust network 
of dealerships across Sri Lanka. By 2029, the company aims to open 90 dealerships  
nationwide, ensuring easy access to its electric motorcycles and top -tier after -sales service for 
all customers. The first phase of this expansion will see the opening of 15 dealerships  within 
the next four months, reflecting the rising demand for electric mobility solutions.  The Sri 
Lankan customers will receive the same high standa rds of quality and service that have made 
Revolt Motors a trusted name in India.  The company’s commitment to customer satisfaction 
is reflected in its integrated 3S service model, ensuring that all necessary support is provided  
under one roof.  
Speaking on the announcement , Ms. Anjali Rattan, Chairperson of RattanIndia Enterprises 
Limited, the parent company of Revolt Motors , stated, “ Our entry into Sri Lanka is not just a 
business move; it is a commitment to driving the global transition to sustainable mobility. We 
are confident that the people of Sri Lanka will embrace the freedom, innovation, and 
environmental consciousness that Revo lt Motors stands for. This is just the beginning of our 
journey in making clean and efficient transportation acce ssible to everyone .” 
“We are absolutely thrilled to announce our partnership with Revolt Motors to bring their 
revolutionary electric motorcycles to Sri Lanka,” said Zahran Ziyawudeen, CEO of Evolution 
Auto Pvt Ltd . “This collaboration is a key step toward a sustainable future, reducing carbon 
emissions and promoting cleaner transportation in Sri Lanka. Revolt Motors’ innovative 
   
electric bikes meet the rising demand for eco -friendly options, offering advanced technology 
and strong performance for local roads .” 
With a firm focus on innovation, sustainability, and customer satisfaction, Revolt Motors is set 
to leave a lasting impact on Sri Lanka’s automotive landscape, bringing in a new era of electric 
mobility.  
Learn  more about how Revolt Motors is revolutionizing electric mobility , visit  its website at 
www.revoltmotors.com  or visit your nearest dealership.
