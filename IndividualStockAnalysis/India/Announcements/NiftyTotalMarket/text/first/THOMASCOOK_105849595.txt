Sterling Resort in Kanha recognized in Top 1% among 8.6 million listings worldwide  
● Sterling Kanha wins TripAdvisor “Best of the Best” award for 2nd consecutive year.  
● 26 Sterling resorts conferred TripAdvisor Travellers’ Choice Award 2024.  
Chennai, Tamilnadu -  May 16, 2024 -  Sterling Holiday Resorts is thrilled to announce that 
Sterling Kanha has been recognized in TripAdvisor’s Travellers’ Choice Awards for 2024. This 
is the second year in a row that Sterling Kanha is being recognized as the “Best of the Best” 
under the category of Best Boutique Hotels.  
TripAdvisor is the world’s largest travel platform with more than 463 Million travellers per 
month and more than 859 Million reviews. TripAdvisor confers the “Best of the Best” award 
on less than 1% of more than 8.6 million accommodations listed by them across 49 countries 
worldwide. The award adds to the broader recognition for Sterling, which has received 
TripAdvisor Travellers’ Choice Award for 2024, for 26 of its resorts. This award is conferred on 
the top 10% of the 8.6 Million accommodations on TripAdvisor.  
On the occasion, Mr. Vikram Lalvani, MD & CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts said, “At Sterling, 
we are committed to  provide memorable stays filled with unforgettable discoveries and 
unique experiences. Sterling Kanha being honoured with the “Best of the Best” award for the second consecutive year, marks a significant achievement. This accolade not only adds strength to  the stature of the Sterling brand in the hospitality industry but also reinforces out 
team’s commitment to deliver on our brand promise to every guest.”  
Sterling Kanha is a beautiful property set amidst 8.5 acres with a natural pond and situated 
on the ba nks of the Banjar River, close to the Kanha National Park and Tiger Reserve. The 
resort boasts of classic tents and traditionally styled spacious villas of 615 sq. ft. with machans – which are ideal for families. The restaurant serves delectable local dish es along with regular 
favourites and also boasts of a swimming pool with spectacular views.  
Summer is the best season to spot tigers in the wild –  the perfect time to holiday at Sterling 
Kanha to enjoy the hospitality in this award- winning resort.
