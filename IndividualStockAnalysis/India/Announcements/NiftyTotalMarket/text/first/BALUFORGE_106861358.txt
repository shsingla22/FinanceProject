Management’s Message 
“H1 FY27 has been a record half for Balu Forge , reflecting the strength of our business 
and the progress we are making towards the next phase of growth. Revenue grew 17% to 
Rs.625 crore, while PAT increased 17% to Rs.143 crore. EBITDA stood at Rs.179 crore, up 
15%, with a margin of 28.6%.  Q2 EBITDA margin further improved by 130 bps to 28.9% . 
Equally encouraging is the strong cash generation, with operating cash flow reaching 
Rs.245 crore, nearly 7x the previous year. Receivables reduced by Rs.31.2 crore , while 
net debt -to-equity remained low at 0.04x , reflecting our focus on disciplined execution 
and a stronger balance sheet.
Building on our 37-year engineering legacy , we are entering the next phase of growth by 
creating a globally competitive, integrated precision manufacturing platform. Q2 provides 
the base before our new capacities begin contributing to growth.  We are scaling forging 
capacity from 100,000 MTPA to 168,000 MTPA  and precision machining capacity from 
58,000 MTPA to 80,000 MTPA . Investments in advanced machining, automated forging 
and specialised manufacturing will enable us to bring more complex, higher -value 
capabilities in -house and deepen our partnerships with global OEMs.
Our business mix is steadily shifting towards higher -value applications , with Defence, 
Aerospace and Railways contributing nearly 50% of our Rs.2,400 crore order book . The 
new 6.7 -metre ring rolling capability  further expands the range and complexity of 
components we can manufacture. With defence dispatches underway  and expanded 
capacities progressively coming on stream, we remain confident of delivering 25–30% 
revenue growth in FY27.
The promoter group's commitment of up to Rs.162 crore through investments  reflects its 
confidence in our vision and growth plans. As we move ahead, our focus remains on 
technology -led expansion, operational excellence, prudent capital allocation and 
stronger customer relationships . Our ambition is not just to grow larger, but to build a 
globally trusted precision engineering business that creates sustainable, long -term value 
for all stakeholders.” 
                       
                     
                       
                     Mr. Jaspal Singh Chandock
