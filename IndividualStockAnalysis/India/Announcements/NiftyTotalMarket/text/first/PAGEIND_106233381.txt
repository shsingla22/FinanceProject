Inside  Us
We are a team  of committed  Company  Secretaries  & Lawyers,  all of whom  have  relevant  
experience  of working  in different  roles  and across  various  industries .
As a firm,  our focus  is on building  relationship  with  clients  based  on trust  and through  timely  
delivery  of services  that are tuned  to the needs  of the client .
We believe  in delivering  quality,  maintaining  integrity,  being  ethical  in our approach,  ensuring  
commitment  to timelines,  all of these  are further  proved  by the fact that our clients  have  
stood  by us for decades .
Our philosophy  is mainly  focused  on deep  “Client  Centric”  engagements  and this has been  in 
the DNA  of all functions  of “PVA” .
Additionally  our firm is also “Peer  Reviewed”  by the Institute  of Company  Secretaries  of India,  
New  Delhi  (ICSI) .
