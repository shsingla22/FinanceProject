Summary of Proceedings of the 19th Annual General Meeting of  
Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Limited 
 
The 19th Annual General Meeting (“AGM”)  of the Members of Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care 
Services Limited (the “Company” ) held today i.e. Friday, September 25, 2026 at 12:00 Noon 
(IST) through video conferencing /  Other Audio-Visual Means ( “VC/OAVM” ) in accordance with 
the applicable provisions of Companies Act, 2013 read with the Rules framed  thereunder and the 
Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure  Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 and the Circulars issued by Ministry of Corporate Affairs  (“MCA”) and the 
Securities and Exchange Board of India (“SEBI”). 
 
Chairman of the Meeting 
 
Dr. Ajay Kumar Tyagi, Chairman & Whole-time Director of the Company, was unable to attend 
the Meeting, Mr. Yatharth Tyagi, Whole-time Director of the Company, with the consent of the 
Board of Directors, took the Chair and presided over the Meeting.

The number of members as on cut-off date i.e. Friday, September 18, 2026 were 99769.

Dr. Ajay Kumar Tyagi, Chairman & Whole-time Director and Dr. Kapil Kumar, Managing Director 
of the Company were unable to attend the Meeting due to unavoidable circumstances. 
The Chairperson of the Audit Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee and 
Stakeholders Relationship Committee were present at the AGM to answer the queries of 
members, if any. 
Thereafter, the Company Secretary requested the Chairman to address the members and apprise 
them of the Company’s strong operational and financial performance during the Financial Year 
2025–26. After the Chairman’s speech, the Chairman requested Mr. Amit Kumar Singh, Group 
Chief Executive Officer to provide an overview of the Company’s business. Thereupon, he 
apprised the Members of the Company about the key highlights, its progress, recent acquisitions 
and CSR activities undertaken during the year. Thereafter, the Company Secretary requested Mr. 
Yatharth Tyagi, Chairman of the meeting, to deliver his speech. 
 
 
Mr. Yatharth Tyagi apprised the Members about the business operations of the Company, its 
financial position and growth, expansion initiatives, prospects and key achievements. 
The Company Secretary informed the members that since this meeting was convened through 
VC/OAVM and the notice convening 19th AGM, Directors’ Report, Auditors’ Report, Financial 
Statement including Consolidated Financial Statements having already been circulated to the 
members, and the resolutions provided in the notice have already been put to vote through e-
voting, there will be no proposing and seconding for the resolutions. Thereafter, he read in brief 
the resolutions proposed in the AGM notice. 
 The Company Secretary confirmed that the Notice of the 19th AGM, along with the Annual 
Report for FY 2025–26, was sent electronically to all eligible shareholders on Thursday, 
September 03, 2026 . 
 Remote e-voting was conducted from  Tuesday, September 22, 2026 (09:00 AM)  to 
Thursday,  September 24, 2025 (05:00 PM) . 
 E-voting facility was also provided during the AGM through CDSL. 
 It was further informed that members who had not cast their votes through remote e-
voting could vote during the AGM. 
 It was also confirmed that there were no qualifications  in the Statutory Auditor’s Report 
or Secretarial Auditor’s Report. 
 Members who had registered themselves as speakers were invited to raise their queries, 
which were addressed suitably. Some of the members who participated at the AGM 
extended their compliments to the Chairman and the Management for the performance of 
the Company.
