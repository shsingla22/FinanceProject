manufactures, and commercializes a wi de range of generic pharmaceuticals, branded specialty pharmaceuticals and 
active pharmaceutical ingredients globally in over 150 countries.  
The company has 31 manufacturing and packaging facilities that are approved by leading regulatory agencies including 
USFDA, UK MHRA, EDQM, Japan PMDA, WHO, Health Canada, South Africa MCC, Brazil ANVISA. The Company’s robust 
product portfolio is spread over 7 major therapeu tic/product areas encompassing CNS, Anti -Retroviral, CVS, Antibiotics,
