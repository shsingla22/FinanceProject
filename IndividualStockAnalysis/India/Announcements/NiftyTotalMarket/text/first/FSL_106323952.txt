Firstsource reaffirms sustainability governance with FY25 ESG Report  
 
Mumbai, India | August  04, 2025: Firstsource  Solutions Limited  (NSE:FSL, BSE:532809), a leading global provider of 
specialist domain -led Business Process Services (BPS) and an RP -Sanjiv Goenka Group company , released  its 
Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Report  for the fiscal year 202 4-25, reflecting a year of bold action, 
integrated strategy, and measurable impact.  
 
The report underscores how Firstsource is embedding ESG into the fabric of business operations through FirstConscious, 
its ESG framework, and UnBPO ™, the company’s transformation -led operating philosophy. Together, these strategic 
levers are enabling Firstsource to move beyond compliance, to redefine what a responsible  and sustainable  business 
looks like in today’s world.  
 
Dr. Sanjiv Goenka, Chairman of RPSG Group and Firstsource , said, “We are living through a time of extraordinary 
unpredictability, where disruption is structural, not episodic. At Firstsource, we’ve moved from reacting to what’s 
coming to shaping what’s next. This ESG Report reflects that shift. Guided by our FirstConscio us framework and powered 
by UnBPO ™, we are embedding sustainability into the very architecture of how we operate, building a high -
performance, high -trust business that’s built to last. Because real transformation creates real value, for people, for the 
planet, and for the long term. ”
