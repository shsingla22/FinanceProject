Sub:  Change of the Part-time Government Director on the Board of Ircon International

Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 [“SEBI (LODR) Regulations”], it is hereby informed that the Ministry of 
Railways vide its order no. 2022/PL/51/10 dated 28th January, 2025 (copy enclosed as 
Annexure ) has appointed Shri Anand Bhatia, Addl. Member (CE), Railway Board as Part-time 
Government Director on the Board of IRCON with immediate effect, till he holds the post of 
Addl. Member (CE), Railway Board or further orders, whichever is earlier.  
 
The Company is taking necessary action to appoint Shri Anand Bhatia in compliance with 
provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the SEBI (LODR) Regulations and requisite 
disclosures in this regard will be submitted upon his appointment by the Board of Directors.
