Transrail Lighting Continues Order -Winning Momentum  
with ₹408  Crore Orders   
Order mainly comprises of a substation project in MENA region  
 
 
Mumbai , October 14, 2026  – Transrail Lighting Limited (BSE: 544317, NSE: TRANSRAILL),  one of the leading 
Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) players in the Transmission & Distribution (T&D) sector, 
today announced that it has received new orders worth ₹ 408 crore  which has further strengthened the 
orderbook position of the Company.  With this, the year -to-date order intake position stands at ₹ 2,429  crore.  
The order wins mainly consists of a GIS substation (Gas Insulated substation) EPC project in MENA region  
apart from few other domestic orders . This win highlight s the wide technical and operational skill sets the 
Company  possess  and the confidence placed by international clients on it for high technology projects .  
 
Mr. Randeep Narang, MD & CEO, stated,  
"We continue to garner new orders on back of the increasing T&D spends worldwide.  With this, just in last 
fortnight we have accumulated orders worth ₹ 1,394  crore  taking the YTD order inflow to ₹ 2,429  crore . 
Amongst these new order wins of ₹ 40 8 crore, t he major  order is a Greenfield EPC job of a substation project 
in MENA region which is o n advanced gas insulated technology  which is a testimony to our advanced skill 
sets. We continue to grow in a balanced manner both in domestic and International markets across our 
products and services ." 
 
About Transrail Lighting Limited:  
Transrail is a leading turnkey engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) company with primary focus 
on power transmission and distribution business with 4 decades of experience. With its headquarters in India, 
it is a global enterprise with a footpri nt in more than 60 countries across 6 continents. It provides turnkey 
solutions from Designing, Engineering, Supply, Manufacture, Construction, Testing services across all its 
business verticals which include Transmission Lines, Substations, Civil Construction, Railways, Solar and Pole 
& Ligh ting. As part of Power T&D business, Transrail has largescale manufacturing facilities in India for 
Galvanized Lattice Towers, Overhead Conductors including HTLS and Galvanized Monopoles in addition to a 
well-accredited Tower testing facility.
