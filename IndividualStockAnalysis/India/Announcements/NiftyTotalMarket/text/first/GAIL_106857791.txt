Press Release  
 
Signing of MoU by GAIL (India) Limited & Krishak Bharati Cooperative Limited 
with Andhra Pradesh Economic Development Board for exploring the opportunity 
to setup a gas -based fertilizer project in the State of Andhra Pradesh  
 
Visakhapatnam, November 12, 2026:  In a significant step towards strengthening India's fertilizer 
self-sufficiency, GAIL (India) Limited (GAIL) and Krishak Bharati Cooperative Limited 
(KRIBHCO) today signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Andhra Pradesh 
Economic Development Board ( APEDB) in the 31st CII Partnership Summit 2026 to explore the 
opportunity of establishing a gas -based urea plant in Andhra Pradesh.  
 
The MoU was formally signed by Shri Sagili Shan Mohan (IAS), Chief Executive Officer (CEO), 
APEDB, Shri Sanjay Agarwal, ED (BD, Start -up & R&D), GAIL and                         
Shri C. S. Azad, Executive Director (Technical), KRIBHCO in the august presence of                        
Shri N. Chandra Babu Naidu, Hon’ble Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh. During the signing 
ceremony, Shri R. K. Singhal, Director (Business Development), GAIL, Shri V Sudhakar 
Chowdary, Chairman, KRIBHCO & Shri S. S. Yadav, Managing Director, KRIBHCO were al so 
present.  
 
The urea production facility is envisaged to have a capacity of 1.27 Million Metric Tons Per 
Annum (MMTPA) at Mulapeta, Srikakulam district of Andhra Pradesh along GAIL’s Srikakulam 
Angul Natural Gas Pipeline (SAPL).
