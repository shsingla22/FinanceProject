Sub: Annual scheduled maintenance shutdown - Intimation under Regulation 30 of the 
SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
 
In reference to the above captioned subject, we would like to inform you that our Company's 
Soda Ash plant will be temporarily closed for planned maintenance activities. This annual 
maintenance shutdown is scheduled to take place from September 26, 2025 to October 12, 
2025. This is expected to result in a total production shortfall of approximately  22,000-25,000 
tons. The purpose of the activities is to carry out essential maintenance tasks to ensure the 
efficient and safe operations of our Soda Ash plant.  
 
During this period, we want to assure our stakeholders that we have a sufficient reserve stock 
of Soda Ash to meet our customers' requirements. Therefore, there will be no adverse impact 
on our supply commitments during the aforementioned period. 
 
We kindly request you to please take note of this information and take appropriate measures 
to disseminate it to all concerned parties as per the SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
