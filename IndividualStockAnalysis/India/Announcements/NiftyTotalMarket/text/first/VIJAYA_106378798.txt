Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Sub:  Intimation under Regulation 30  of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015  
 
This is to inform you that a regional daily newspaper (“Prajaprashna Epaper”) dated 
September 18, 2025  published an article carrying certain allegations of irregularities, pricing 
issues, and non -compliances  in relation to the operations of our Dilsukhnagar branch  of the 
company . 
 
We wish to clarify that the said allegations are factually incorrect, misleading, and baseless . 
The Company has issued a Press Note dated September 19, 2025 , published in two regional 
newspapers, namely Nava Telangana  and Mana Telangana , refuting such claims and placing 
on record the true facts.  
 
In the interest of transparency and to avoid misinformation among stakeholders, we are 
submitting herewith the following documents for reference:  
 
1. Copy of our Press Note dated September 19 , 2025;  
2. Translated copy of the said  Press Note  in English ; 
3. Copy of the article published in Prajaprashna Epaper  dated September 18, 2025;  and 
4. Translated copy of the said article in English . 
 
The Company reiterates that it continues to operate with the highest standards of integrity, 
medical ethics, and regulatory compliance , and reserves its rights to initiate appropriate 
proceedings against the publication and dissemination of such defamatory content.  
 
Please take the information on record.  
 
Thanking you.

Press Note – Clarification on False Allegations Published in Prajaprashna Epaper  
Hyderabad, 19 September 2025  
 
This is in reference to an article published in the Prajaprashna  Epaper  on 18 September 2025 (Page 
8), which contains several factually incorrect, misleading, and defamatory statements regarding 
Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Limited. We strongly deny all such allegations and issue this clarification 
in the public interest:  
 
1. On Allegations of Irregular “Check Tests”  - All diagnostic tests at Vijaya are conducted 
strictly based on valid referrals and medical necessity. Tests are performed by qualified 
radiologists, pathologists, and certified technologists, in full compliance with applicable 
medical regulations and stand ards. Allegations of unlawful or unauthorized testing are 
entirely false and misleading.  
 
2. On Pricing and Alleged Exploitation  - Vijaya follows a transparent and standardized 
pricing structure, benchmarked against national healthcare guidelines and industry norms. 
All prices are publicly displayed at every centre and are compliant with applicable laws. The 
claim of arbitrary or expl oitative pricing is baseless.  
 
3. On Comparisons to a “Corporate Hospital”  - Vijaya Diagnostic Centre is a diagnostic 
services provider and does not operate as a hospital. We do not offer in -patient or therapeutic 
treatment services and function strictly under applicable diagnostic licensing norms.  
 
4. On Regulatory Oversight and Supervision  - Every Vijaya centre is NABL - and NABH -
accredited, duly licensed, and subject to regular inspections by statutory authorities. The 
suggestion that officials are bribed or deliberately ignore violations is entirely 
unsubstantiated, defamatory, and appears in tended to damage our standing.  
 
5. On Alleged Malpractice at Dilsukhnagar Centre  - These allegations are wholly 
fabricated. All procedures at our Dilsukhnagar branch are conducted under the same 
stringent protocols and by qualified professionals. Claims regarding the involvement of 
“unqualified doctors” are defamatory, false, and unaccep table.  
 
Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Limited operates with the highest standards of integrity, professionalism, 
and patient -centered ethics. With over 150 centres nationwide, we are proud to be India’s leading 
diagnostic network, trusted by millions.  
 
This article appears to be a deliberate attempt to tarnish our reputation, potentially at the behest of 
vested interests. Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Limited expressly reserves its right to initiate appropriate 
civil and criminal proceedings against those inv olved in the publication and dissemination of such 
defamatory content.

Prajaprashna – Telugu Daily  
Scandal at Vijaya Diagnostic Centre  
Check tests of doctors in violation of rules  
• Doctors’ manipulations under the guise of a diagnostic centre  
• Functioning like a corporate hospital  
• Arbitrary pricing to exploit patients  
• No regulation on prices and tests by District Medical Authorities  
• Monthly shares to DMHOs and private doctors?  
Hyderabad (Prajaprashna News):  
The irregularities at Vijaya Diagnostic Centre in the city seem never -ending. Presenting 
itself like a corporate hospital, the centre is allegedly deceiving patients in the name 
of doctors. Unlike any other diagnostic centre in the city, it is accused of c harging 
exorbitant prices and exploiting patients. Owing to the negligence of district medical 
authorities —or due to their acceptance of bribes —there has been no regulation or 
control over the centre’s operations.  
The charges at Vijaya Diagnostic are reportedly so high that they shock patients at 
first glance. Allegations are rife that the centre is looting patients with the highest 
rates in the market, yet the government has so far failed to respond, which has turn ed 
into a farce. A diagnostic centre is meant to be a facility for tests only, but several 
Vijaya Diagnostic branches in the city are accused of exploiting innocent patients in 
the name of doctors, cashing in on their health needs.  
Medical authorities who should be monitoring these activities are being criticized for 
being blinded by bribes. The branch in Dilsukhnagar is cited as an example, where 
DM&HO monitoring is absent. Patients are allegedly looted of lakhs under the pretext 
of tests. Furthermore, doctors are engaged and their prescribed tests are being done 
at exorbitant rates, with no concern about whether such doctors are truly qualified.  
There are also criticisms that proper tests are not even being conducted, and since 
the institution operates like a corporate setup, no one is focusing attention on these 
issues. As monthly "shares" are allegedly distributed to concerned parties, officials  
turn a blind eye. Though aware of these irregularities, authorities ignore them, 
satisfied with their own benefits.  
Many are now demanding that the government and the State Medical and Health 
Department immediately focus their attention on the functioning of Vijaya Diagnostic 
Centres.
