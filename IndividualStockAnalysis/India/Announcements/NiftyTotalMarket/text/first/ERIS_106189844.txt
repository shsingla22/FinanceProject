Rationale for the action : The number of operating entities in the Eris g roup ha s increased  following several  
acquisitions made over the last 3 years. At present, each operating entity has its own set of billing and collection 
transactions with a common  set of Channel Partners. The Board of the listed entity had recently approved the 
integration  of this role into a single entity, namely  EHPL, to drive  operational efficiencies and better regulatory 
compliance as described below : 
 
• Reduction in the number of Channel Partner agreements required, since now every Channel Partner would 
enter into a single agreement with EHPL covering their businesses with all Eris g roup entities  
 
• A consequent reduction (and s treamlining ) of billing and collection operations between the Eris group  and 
its Channel Partners, since now all transactions  would be done by a single entity EHP L 
 
• Reduction in the number of stock and sale licenses that are to be applied, maintained and renewed over 
time 
 
The aforesaid measure would significantly augment the role of EHPL, which had been carrying on the limited 
business of trade generics thus far. The credit facilities that have been availed (as per the table) by EHPL are 
 
 
aimed at enabling it to discharge  its augmented role. The Corporate Guarantee extended by the listed entity to 
Citibank are aimed at smoother extension of the desired working capital limits to EHPL by the lenders.
