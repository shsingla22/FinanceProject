Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
A. Twenty-Seventh (27th) Annual General Meeting of CDSL:  
 
This is to inform you that the Twenty-Seventh (27th) Annual General Meeting (“AGM”)  of 
the Shareholders of the Company will be held on Thursday, August 14, 2025 at 11:00 A.M. 
(IST) through Video Conferencing (“VC”)/Other Audio Visual Means (“OAVM”) in accordance 
with the applicable circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs and the Securities and 
Exchange Board of India from time to time.  
 
The Notice of AGM and Annual Report of the Company for the financial year 2024-25 will be 
sent separately to the Stock Exchange and the Shareholders of the Company in due course. 
 
B. Record Date for Dividend: 
 
The Board of Directors of the Company at its Meeting held on May 03, 2025, had recommended 
a final dividend for the financial year 2024-25 of ₹ 12.50/- per equity share of the face value 
of ₹ 10/-. for approval by the Shareholders at the AGM. 
 
In terms of Regulation 42 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, the Company has fixed Thursday, August 07, 2025 , as the “Record Date ” 
for the purpose of determining the Shareholders eligible to receive dividend for the financial 
year 2024-25. The Dividend, if approved at the AGM, will be paid within a period of 30 days 
from the conclusion of the AGM. 
 
The above information will be made available on the Company’s website www.cdslindia.com
