(A Subsidiary of Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited)

MRPL Results Mangalore: July 18th, 2025 
The Board of Directors of Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL), a subsidiary company of ONGC 
and Schedule "A" Mini Ratna Category I Company during its 270th Meeting held on July 18, 2025, approved it's 
Standalone and Consolidated financial results for the first quarter (Q1 FY 2025-26) ended June 30, 2025, 
Major Highlights for the first quarter (Q1 FY 2025-26): 
• Highest ever Crude processed in any of the April month is during April 25 i.e. 1512 TMT, (Previously 1481 TMT 
in April 22). 
• Completion of shutdown of major units in Phase-2 complex. 
Financial Highlights for the first quarter Q1 FY 2025-26 (Q1 FY 2024- 25): 
• Revenue from operations ₹ 20,988 Crore (Q1 FY 2024-25 ₹ 27,289 Crore). 
• Proft/ (Loss) Before Tax ₹ (403) Crore (Q1 FY 2024-25 Profit / (Loss) Before Tax ₹ 101 Crore). 
• Profit/ (Loss) After Tax of ₹ (272) Crore (Q1 FY 2024-25 Profit / (Loss) After Tax ₹ 66 Crore). 
• GRM of US$ 3.88 per bbl (Q1 FY 2024-25 GRM of US$ 4.70 per bbl).
