Coromandel International Limited Posts Q2 FY27 Results  
 
National , 06th November 2026: Coromandel International Limited (BSE: 506395, NSE: COROMANDEL), 
one of India’s leading agri -solutions providers, announced its financial results for the quarter and half -
year ended September 30, 2026. The Company continues to strengthen its leadership acr oss fertilisers, 
crop protection, bio -products, specialty nutrients, organic fertilisers and agri -retail while making 
notable advances in agri -drone spraying and other digital initiatives that drive sustainability and farm 
productivity.

The Company reported 6% revenue growth during the quarter and 10% in H1; however, EBITD A 
declined,  largely reflecting the  margin pressure in Fertiliser business due to  spurt in  raw material costs 
triggered by ongoing Middle East crisis  not adequately covered by subsidy . 
 
Update on Key Initiatives  
 
Company’s long term strategic initiatives remain on track . The new NPK fertiliser capacity is 
progressing well and is expected to be completed by Q4 FY27. In Crop Protection, additional capacity 
for one of its key molecules  is expected to come on stream  during Q3. The Company also plans to set 
up a launch plant for fluoro intermediates to support phased R&D -to-commercial scale -up and 
strengthen its capabilities in speciality chemistry.  The Retail business continued expan sion of its store 
footprint and opened close to 100 new stores during the quarter, taking the network to over 1300 
stores . 
Commenting on the results , Mr. S. Sankarasubramanian, Managing Director & CEO, Coromandel 
International Limited, said:  
“Coromandel delivered a resilient performance in a challenging business environment experienced 
during Q2, reflecting the strength of its diversified portfolio , customer connect  and market presence 
across Indian and global geographies . The Company sharpened its focus on differentiated offerings, 
launching ten new products across the Nutrients and Crop Protection businesses during the quarter.  
 
 
   
  
   
In the Fertiliser business, deficit rainfall, elevated raw material prices and lower Nutrient Based Subsidy 
rates for the Kharif season impacted product offtake and margins in the NP/NPK segment. Despite 
these headwinds, Coromandel continued to support the  farming community, supplying ~3.3 million 
tons  of fertilisers  during H1, while strengthening its leadership in the phosphatic segment, with market 
share improving to 19% from 17% last year.  
The newly commissioned Phosphoric acid and sulphuric acid plant at Kakinada helped mitigate 
external supply disruptions  and enabled value accretion through timely support by the Government . 
Going forward, we expect the NBS rates for the Rabi season to appropriately reflect movements in raw 
material prices and support the continued promotion of balanced nutrition.  
The Crop Protection business recorded revenue growth of 22% during the quarter, supported by 
healthy performance in export markets. In the domestic market, amid softer consumption conditions, 
we continued to strengthen the portfolio with a sharper focus on differentiated and unique 
combination products.  
The Retail business widened  its reach  across key agricultural markets while broadening its portfolio of 
products and services. Its speciality  nutrients, organic and biological businesses delivered strong 
performance, reflecting increasing farmer adoption of differentiated and sustainable crop solutions.”  
About Coromandel  
Coromandel International Limited is amongst India's pioneers and leading Agri solutions provider, 
offering diverse products and services across the farming value chain. It operates in two major 
segments: Nutrient and other allied businesses and Crop Protec tion. These include Fertilizers, Crop 
Protection, Bio Products, Specialty Nutrients and Organic businesses. The Company is 2nd largest 
manufacturer and marketer of Phosphatic fertilizer in India. The Company's Crop Protection products 
are marketed in India  as well as in international geographies, offering wide range of technical and 
formulation products. The Specialty Nutrients business of the Company focuses on water soluble 
fertilizers, secondary & micronutrients and Nano fertilizers products. The Company  is leading marketer 
of Organic fertilizers in India. The Bio Products business of the company focusses on plant extractions 
for various applications. It also operates a network of more than 1200 rural retail outlets across Andhra 
Pradesh, Telangana, Karna taka, Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra. Through these Retail outlets, the 
Company offers agri inputs and farming services including crop advisory, soil testing and farm 
mechanization to around 3 million farmers. The Company currently has 8 R&D centers and a strong 
Regulatory setup, supporting the businesses in process development and new produ ct introduction. 
The Company currently has 21 manufacturing facilities, spread widely across India, producing wide 
range of Nutrient and Crop Protection products, which are marketed through an extensive network of 
dealers and its own retail centers.  
The Company clocked a turnover of Rs. 31,827 Crores during FY 25 -26. Its efforts towards environment 
have been well recognized by international organizations like UNDP and has also been voted as one of 
the ten greenest companies in India by TERI. Coromande l is a part of the Murugappa Group with a 
turnover of INR 105,582 crore in FY 25 -26.
