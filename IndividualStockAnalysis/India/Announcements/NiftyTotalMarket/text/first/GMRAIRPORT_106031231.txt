It has come to our notice that certain print media outlets have inaccurately reported 
the recent Supreme Court judgement dated 18th October 2024 passed in connection 
with the Appeals filed by the Airports Economic Regulatory Authority of India (AERA) 
against the decisions of the TDSAT w here various private airport operators, including 
Delhi International Airport Limited (DIAL), a subsidiary of GMR Airports Limited  
(formerly GMR Airports Infrastructure Limited)  is a party. In particular, it has been 
misreported/ misinterpreted in some med ia reports that the judgement upholds 
AERA’s decision -making authority over tariffs for Non -Aeronautical Services.  
  
We would like to categorically clarify that the said Judgement has nothing to do with 
decision -making power of AERA over tariffs for Non -Aeronautical Services, but only 
upheld the ability of AERA to file an appeal challenging the TDSAT judgements in 
Supreme Court and participate as a party Appellant/Respondent as the case may be 
when such challenge to the TDSAT judgements is being heard in t he Supreme Court.  
  
This limited finding was pursuant to a legal objection by the private airport operators 
challenging AERA’s right to file an appeal.  
 
As such the said Judgement is limited in its scope and operation only to the above 
and the Appeals filed by AERA remain pending decision on its own merit, including on 
the issue whether the AERA has jurisdiction to determine tariff for non -aeronautical 
services like Cargo and Ground Handling Ser vices at such private airports.
