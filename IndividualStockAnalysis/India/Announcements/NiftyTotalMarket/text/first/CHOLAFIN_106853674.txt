The Board of Directors (“the Board”) at its meeting held on 28 October, 2026 considered a capital raising proposal for the 
Company to support the projected business growth across product segments, strengthen its capital base, meet its long-term 
funding requirements and for general corporate purposes.
Accordingly, the Board considered raising additional capital not exceeding ₹ 5,000 crores, in one or more tranches, through a 
Qualified Institutions Placement (“QIP”) to eligible investors in accordance with Chapter VI of the SEBI ICDR Regulations.
Pursuant to the provisions of sections 23, 42, 62(1)(c), 71 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the 
approval of the Members is required for the proposed issue and allotment of Securities through QIP . Accordingly, the approval 
of the Members is being sought, pursuant to the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 read with Rules made 
thereunder, the SEBI ICDR Regulations and in terms of the provisions of the SEBI Listing Regulations to issue and allot Securities 
through QIP as stated in the resolution. The proposed Special Resolution is in the nature of an enabling authorisation and 
permits the Board to undertake the QIP without the need for any further approval from the Members, subject to applicable 
statutory and regulatory requirements.
The net proceeds of the proposed Issue shall be utilised for any of the aforesaid purposes as may be permitted under applicable 
law. The Securities proposed to be issued shall be listed on the Stock Exchanges where the Equity Shares of the Company are 
presently listed, subject to the receipt of requisite regulatory approvals, as may be applicable.
The proposed issue of Securities may be consummated through single or multiple offer documents, in one or more tranches, at 
such time or times, at such price, at a discount or premium to market price in such manner and on such terms and conditions 
as the Board or its duly constituted committee may in its absolute discretion decide taking into consideration prevailing market 
conditions and other relevant factors and wherever necessary in consultation with the lead manager(s) and other advisors and 
subject to SEBI Regulations and other applicable laws, regulations, rules and guidelines.
Since the pricing and other terms of the QIP will be determined at the time of the Issue, an enabling resolution is being proposed 
to give adequate flexibility and discretion to the Board or its duly authorised committee to finalize the terms of the securities that 
may be issued to the qualified institutional buyers in the QIP . The issue price of the Securities shall be determined in accordance 
with the provisions of Chapter VI of the SEBI ICDR Regulations and shall not be lower than the floor price prescribed thereunder,
