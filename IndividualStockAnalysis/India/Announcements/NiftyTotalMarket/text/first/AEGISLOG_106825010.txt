SUB: Scrutinizer's Report on the E-voting process conducted for the Extra Ordinary 
General Meeting (EGM) held on Wednesday, October 21, 2026 at 04.00 p.m. IST through 
Video Conferencing (“VC”) / Other Audio Visual Means (“OAVM”) pursuant to the 
provisions of Section 108 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with Rule 20 of the Companies 
(Management and Administration) Rules, 2014, as amended from time to time. 
 
I, Mr. Prasen Naithani, of P. Naithani & Associates, Practicing Company Secretaries, 
appointed as the “Scrutinizer” by the Board of Directors of Aegis Logistics Limited (“The 
Company”) pursuant to Section 108 of the Companies Act, 2013 ("the Act") read with Rule 
20 of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014, as amended, for the 
purpose of conducting and scrutinizing the E-voting process in a fair and transparent manner 
in respect of the Special Resolutions, as set out in the Notice dated Monday, 28th  September, 
2026 and to submit a report thereon. In this regard, I hereby submit my report as under: 
 
1. The Company engaged the services of M/s. MUFG Intime India Private Limited. 
(hereinafter referred to as the "Service Provider" or “MUFG Intime”) to offer the e-
voting facility to its shareholders. The Remote e-voting facility was offered and kept 
open by the Company to its Shareholders for the period commencing on Saturday, 
October 17, 2026 at 09.00 A.M. IST and ends on Tuesday, October 20, 2026 at 05.00 
P.M. IST. The shareholders whose names appeared in the Register of Members / List 
of Beneficial Owners as on Wednesday, October 14, 2026 (i.e. cut - off date) were 
allowed to participate and vote electronically during the aforesaid period of Remote e-
voting. The Company had also provided E-voting facility to the shareholders present 
at the EGM through VC / OAVM and who had not cast their vote earlier.
