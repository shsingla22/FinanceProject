Bengaluru, July 15th, 2026 : The Board of Directors of Jana Small Finance Bank Limited at its meeting 
held today,  approved the audited financial results  for the quarter ended 30th June , 2026 .

About  Jana  Small  Finance  Bank  
 
Jana Small Finance Bank, a scheduled commercial bank, is the 4th largest Small Finance Bank in India  
with an experience of over 1 7 years in lending and serving more than 12 million customers. The Bank 
was launched in March 2018, is a solid combination of a digital institution and national presence across  
23 states and 2 union territories, spread across 826 branches.  From its origin of being a NBFC -MFI,  the 
bank has transformed steadily over the years, leading to 70%+ of its lending book being secured , 
mostly backed by mortgages. The Bank has created a fast growing retail deposit franchise with a  
strong brand presence and high standar ds of customer service. The bank's management team has an  
average  experience  of 29 years  with  a reputed  and well-rounded  Board.
