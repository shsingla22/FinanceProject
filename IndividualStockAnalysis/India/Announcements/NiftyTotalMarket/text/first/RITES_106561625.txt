Subject:  Intimation of change of Company Secretary and Compliance Officer of the Company 
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations 2015, this is to inform you that Shri 
Ashok Mishra, has tendered his resignation from the position of Company Secretary and Compliance 
Officer (Key Managerial Personnel) of the Company. 
 
He tendered his resignation through email dated 26.02.2026 and his resignation was accepted by 
Competent Authority on March 19, 2026. The reasons for the resignation were mentioned in his email 
dated 26.02.2026. The email of resignation received from Shri Ashok Mishra and order accepting the 
resignation is enclosed herewith. 
 
The Board of Directors of the Company in their meeting held today i.e. March 19, 2026 has approved 
the appointment of Shri Nikhil Agarwal, an associate member of The Institute of Company Secretaries 
of India (Membership No. A42626), working as Joint General Manager (Finance) in RITES Limited as 
Company Secretary & Compliance Officer of the Company w.e.f. March 19, 2026 till the appointment 
of regular incumbent to the post. 
 
The information in terms of Regulation 30 read with Schedule Ill - Para A of Part A of the Listing 
Regulations (as applicable) and SEBI Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026
