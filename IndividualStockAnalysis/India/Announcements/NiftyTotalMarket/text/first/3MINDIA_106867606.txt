The following Explanatory Statement is furnished in respect of Special Business of the Postal Ballot Notice: 
Item No. 1:
Based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board on November 6, 2026 
appointed Mr. Chun Hao Lok (DIN: 12029181) as an Additional Director of the Company (categorized as Non- Executive 
and Non-Independent Director) with effect from November 7 , 2026. He will hold office until the next General Meeting or 
for a period of three months, whichever is earlier.
The Company has received a notice in writing pursuant to Section 160 of the Companies Act, 2013, from a Member 
proposing his candidature for the office of Director.
Mr. Chun Hao Lok is currently serving as Vice President of Asia Operations and Singapore Country Governance Leader, 
he joined 3M in July 2025. He has over 25 years of working experience in USA and European multi-national companies. In 
his last role as Global VP for Supply Chain and Operations for Huntsman Textile Effects / Archroma, he was managing 23 
factories globally and supported businesses in the chemical and electronics industries. Mr. Chun Hao Lok has held several 
senior leadership roles in AkzoNobel and Flextronics. He has expertise in Supply Chain Management and operations.
Mr. Chun Hao Lok holds a Masters (Hons) in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, has completed INSEAD Supply Chain 
Management Programme and is APICS Certified Supply Chain Professional (CSCP).
The Board believes that Mr. Chun Hao Lok’s extensive experience and skills in supply chain management, manufacturing, 
operational effectiveness and country governance will be a valuable addition to the Board and that his appointment will be 
in the best interest of the Company. As a Non-Executive and Non-Independent Director, he is liable to retire by rotation. 
No sitting fees shall be paid for attending the Meetings of the Board of Directors or its Committees.
Mr. Chun Hao Lok has given his consent to act as a Director of the Company and has confirmed that he is not debarred 
from holding the office of a Director by virtue of any Order passed by SEBI or any such regulatory authority(ies) and he is 
not disqualified from being appointed as a Director in terms of Section 164 of the Act.
Accordingly, an Ordinary Resolution seeking the appointment of Mr. Chun Hao Lok as a Non-Executive and Non-
Independent Director of the Company is included in the Notice at Item No. 1. The Board recommends the Ordinary 
Resolution set forth at Item No. 1 for the approval of the Members of the Company.
None of the Directors and/or Key Managerial Personnel of the Company or their relatives, except Mr. Chun Hao Lok, may 
be deemed to be concerned or interested, directly or indirectly, in the proposed resolution.
