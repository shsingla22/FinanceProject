Star Health Insurance Extends Support Amidst Uttarkashi Flash Flood Crisis  
Uttarakhand, August 07, 2025: As heavy rains and cloudburst -triggered flash floods batter parts of 
Uttarkashi, Star Health and Allied Insurance (Star Health Insurance), India’s leading health insurer, has 
stepped up its emergency response measures to support affected residents and policyholders across the 
region. 
The sudden surge in the Khir Ganga and surrounding rivers has caused widespread damage in Tharali 
village and the Harsil region. Rescue operations are underway, and authorities have issued red alerts for 
nearby areas with ongoing rainfall and landslide risks. In such emergencies, the risk of waterborne 
infections, injuries, and worsening chronic conditions increases significantly.  
Mr. Amitabh Jain, COO, Star Health Insurance , stated, "We are deeply saddened by the loss and 
displacement caused by the flash floods in Uttarkashi . In such difficult moments, our foremost priority is to 
extend timely and compassionate support to those affected. We stand with the people of Uttarakhand and 
are activating all possible resources to ensure uninterrupted healthcare assistance and swift cl aim 
resolution." 
Star Health’s On-Ground Response:  To help those impacted by the Uttarkashi flood, Star Health 
Insurance has rolled out the following emergency initiatives:  
 24x7 Emergency Helpline : The helpline numbers (18001024477 / 18004252255) are activ e to 
assist policyholders with medical guidance and claim support  
 Help Desks & Nodal Officers: Local help desks are being set up in Uttarkashi and nearby districts, 
supported by designated nodal officers to handle flood -related claims and provide policy assistance. 
The branches that are nearest to flood zone are Dehradun & Haridwar branch & can be located with 
www.starhealth.in/lookup/branch . 
 Free Telehealth Services:  Free teleconsultation services are being offered to flood -affected 
residents for any med ical concerns, including infections, injuries, and chronic health management. 
Download the Star Health App to access free consultations.  
 Faster Claim Processing: All claims related to flood -induced illnesses and hospitalisations will 
be fast-tracked and processed on priority.  
 Health Risk Communication:  Star Health is initiating awareness campaigns on flood -related 
hygiene, disease prevention, and mental health via WhatsApp, social media.  
Mr. Jain added, "In times of disaster, healthcare cannot wait. We urge affected individuals to stay safe, 
access medical support promptly, and rely on verified health information. Our commitment goes beyond 
policies, it's about protecting lives and helping communities rebuild."  
Health Precautions for Residents in Affecte d Areas: 
Star Health recommends the following safety steps to protect against common health risks during floods:  
 Avoid contact with contaminated flood water  
 
   
 
 Boil water or use purification tablets before consumption  
 Wash hands regularly and use hand sanitizers 
 Store food safely and discard items exposed to floodwater  
 Keep wounds clean and seek medical help for any infections or fever  
 Use mosquito repellents and nets to prevent vector -borne diseases 
 Avoid walking barefoot, wear protective footwear  
 Access teleconsultation if you experience symptoms like diarrhea, rashes, or breathing issues  
Star Health and Allied Insurance assures its customers that all flood -related health claims, including 
waterborne and vector -borne diseases, will be addressed with ur gency. The insurer is committed to ensuring 
continued access to health care and financial protection during this critical time.
