Global fintech Pine Labs enters the Philippines market with GCash for Business as its first payments 
partner  
 
Manila, Philippines, May  12, 2026 – GCash  for Business, backed by the largest cashless ecosystem in 
Philippines, is partnering with global fintech platform Pine Labs.  
Pine Labs, known for its advanced point -of-sale software capabilities, intelligent platform enhancements 
and integrated Value -Added Services (VAS), will power the next phase of merchant acquisition for GCash 
in the region.  
The partnership between GCash for Business and Pine Labs will integrate Instalment Payment Plan (IPP) 
offers, rewards, loyalty, and cashback features with the existing merchant acquiring solution of GCash. 
This tech -first integration will also support acce ptance of multiple modes of digital payments including 
card and QR payments and is expected to give MSMEs in the region a smarter fintech infrastructure.  
GCash for Business empowers millions of Filipino MSMEs to grow with accessible, digital payment 
solutions. With tools like GCash PocketPay —turning any phone into a POS —and GCash SoundPay for real -
time audio confirmations, merchants can transact with ease and confidence.  
Newly launched devices like GCash SoundPay Plus and EasyPOS further unlock enterprise -grade 
capabilities, giving MSMEs more powerful tools at a lower barrier to entry.  With a growing number of 
Filipino MSME merchants on the GCash platform, these businesses are now being enabled to accept both 
card and QR payments through Pine Labs point -of-sale technology.  
Commenting on the partnership, Paul Albano, General Manager and Head of GCash for Business, said 
“GCash helps Filipino entrepreneurs grow by giving them simple, reliable ways to accept payments and 
manage their daily operations. With our collaboration with Pine Labs, merchants can now offer more 
payment choices and added perks, making it easier for th eir businesses to expand and serve more 
customers.”  
“Through this collaboration  with GCash , we are delivering high -performance software integrations, 
smarter payment flows, and powerful value -added services to help merchants in  the Philippines  scale 
their businesses . Our rapid deployment capabilities will improve merchant onboarding, engagement, and 
retention in the region ,” said Dheeraj Chowdhry, Chief Business Officer - South east Asia, at Pine Labs .  
The partnership reinforces the commitment of both companies to build a fully compliant, robust, scalable, 
secure, and future -ready fintech ecosystem that can enable millions of merchants to adopt digital 
payments at the point of sale.  
 
With a locally incorporated entity, Pine Labs already has a significant presence in the Philippines  and caters 
to leading banks, brands, merchants, and financial institutions globally . Pine Labs  powers one of the 
largest gift card programs in the Philippines  for SM Retail, the largest retail chain in the country.  
 
MSMEs can sign up for these solutions at the GCash for Business Portal at business.gcash.com .
