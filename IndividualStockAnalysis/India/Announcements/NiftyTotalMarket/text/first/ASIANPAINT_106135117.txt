This is in continuation of our letter no. APL/SEC/42/2024 -25/16 dated 3rd December 2024, 
wherein it was informed that, based on the request received from Vijal Holding and Trading 
Company Private Limited (‘Vijal Holding’ ), an entity forming part of the Promoter(s) Group of the 
Company, the Company had applied to the Stock Exchanges, BSE Limited (‘BSE’) and National 
Stock Exchange of India Limited (‘NSE’) , seeking their approval for reclassification of Vijal Holding 
from the ‘Promoter(s) Group’ category to the ‘Public’ category . 
 
Vijal Holding does not hold any shares in the Company.  
 
In continuation to the above and in accordance with Regulation 31A(8) of the SEBI ( Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015,  we would like to inform you that 
the Company has today i.e., 3rd February 2025 received the necessary approvals from BSE and 
NSE, for the reclassification of Vijal Holding from the ‘Promoter(s) Group’ category to the ‘Public’ 
category . Copies of the approval letters from the stock exchanges are enclosed.  
 
Accordingly, Vijal Holding ceases to be a part of the Promoter(s) Group of the Company.  
 
You are requested to take the above information on record.
