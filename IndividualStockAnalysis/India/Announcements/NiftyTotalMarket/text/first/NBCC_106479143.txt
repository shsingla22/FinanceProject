Subject: Settlement of land disputes between NBCC (India) Limited and the 
Government, NCT of Delhi (GNC TD) 
 
Sir, 
 
This is hereby informed that a settlement has been reached between NBCC (India) 
Limited and the Government,  NCT of Delhi  (GNTCD) to resolve the long pending 
litigation regarding a land admeasuring 42 .46 acres in Sultanpur/Ghitorni village in 
Delhi.  
 
As per the settlement, the total 42.46 -acre Ghitorni land will be equally divided 
between NBCC and GNCTD, resulting in a 21.23 -acre share for NBCC.  
 
Further, f or this 21.23 -acre of land, GNCTD will execute a perpetual lease deed in 
favour of NBCC.  The approved terms will grant development rights to NBCC in 
accordance with the Master Plan for Delhi (MPD -2021), permitting sub -leasing and 
mixed -use development under city zoning.  
 
The same is available on the website of the company -   
https://www.nbccindia.in/webEnglish/announcementNotices
