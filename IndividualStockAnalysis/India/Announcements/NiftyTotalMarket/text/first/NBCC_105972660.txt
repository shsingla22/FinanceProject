Sir (महोदय) ,  
This is in reference to the NBCC’s intima tion dated August 27, 2024 and pursuant 
to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing  Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that the Board of Directors in its Meeting 
held on Saturday, August 31, 2024, have inter alia, considered and approved the 
following: 
1. Issuance of Bonus Shares and fixation of record date  
The Board of Directors has recommended the issuance of Bonus Shares to the 
Shareholders of the Company in the ratio of 1:2 i.e. 1 (One) new fully paid-up 
Equity Share of ₹ 1/- (Rupee One Only) each for every 2 (Two) existing fully paid-
up Equity Share of ₹ 1/- (Rupee One Only) each to the eligible members of the 
Company as on Record Date, subject to the approval of the Shareholders in the 
forthcoming Annual General Meeting.  
The Board has fixed Monday, October 07, 2024  as Record date to determine 
the eligibility of members to receive bonus shares. Other details are enclosed at 
Annexure-I  
The Board Meeting commenced at 03:00 p.m. (IST) and concluded at 04:00 p.m. 
(IST). 
 
The aforesaid information is also available on the website of the Company at 
https://www.nbccindia.com/webE nglish/BoardMeetingNotices
