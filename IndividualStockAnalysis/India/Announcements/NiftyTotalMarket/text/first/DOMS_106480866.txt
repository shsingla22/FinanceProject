Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
In continuation to our letters dated March 27, 2025, April 29, 2025 and July 31, 2025 wherein it was, 
inter alia , informed that, the Board of Directors of DOMS Industries Limited (‘ the Company ’), 
approved the acquisition of additional 13.0% stake in Pioneer Stationery Private Limited (‘ Pioneer’), a 
subsidiary of the Company, from certain existing shareholders. 
 
Further, as informed in our letter dated August 30, 2025, the Company completed partial acquisition 
of 6.5% stake in Pioneer from certain existing shareholders and the acquisition of remaining stake was 
proposed to be completed on or before December 31, 2025. 
 
In this regard and as mutually agreed between Gala Group Shareholders and the Company, we would 
like to inform that the date of proposed acquisition has been extended from December 31, 2025 to 
March 31, 2026. Accordingly, the acquisition of the remaining stake in Pioneer shall be completed on 
or before March 31, 2026. 
 
All other details of our letter dated March 27, 2025, vide Ref No. DIL/SE/24-25/96, shall remain 
unchanged.
