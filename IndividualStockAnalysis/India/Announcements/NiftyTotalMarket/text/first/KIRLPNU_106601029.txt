Brief Profile
Mr Rahul Kirloskar, is a Business Executive with over 40 years of experience in
managing various industries including engineering products and solutions. He has
been associated with the Kirloskar Group of Companies at senior levels in different
capacities. He started his career in the then Kirloskar Cummins Ltd., and later joined a

become the market leader for industrial refrigeration compressors in India. The

Company has introduced many new products like centrifugal air compressors as well
as screw compressors for refrigeration.
