Press Release – Financial Results for H1 FY27 & Q2 FY27 
 
Bikaji Foods International Delivers Strong Q2 FY27 Performance with Revenue  growth of 13.0 % to 
INR 9,385  Mn and EBITDA Margins at 12.6%  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Bikaner , November 5 , 2026: Bikaji Foods International, India’s leading ethnic snacks and packaged foods 
company, today announced its Financial Results of the company for the quarter and half year ended on 
September 30, 2026 , reporting a robust operational and financial performance driven by sustained 
momentum across core categories, distribution expansion, premium portfolio growth and continued 
traction across emerging channels.  
  
 
 
H1 - FY27 Financial  Performance  Summary : 
 
• Revenue from operations grew by 12.8 % YoY to INR 16,728  mn, supported by healthy 
volume growth and deeper market penetration  
• Volume growth stood at 6.2% YoY  
• EBITDA stood at INR 2,177  mn with EBITDA margin of 13.0% 
• Profit After Tax (PAT) stood at  INR 1,284 mn 
• Earnings per share (EPS) stood at INR 5.22 
 
H1 - FY27 Business  Segments  Review:  
 
• Ethnic Snacks: Ethnic Snacks revenue increased by 15.6% YoY, continuing to outperform overall 
business growth and reinforcing the category's importance as a key growth driver  
• Packaged Sweets: Revenue witnessed an 8.1% YoY decline, largely attributable to the change in 
the timing of the Diwali festive season.  
• Western Snacks: Revenue grew by 32.7 % YoY  
• Papad: Revenue de-grew by 6.4 % YoY  because of supply chain issues in hand made papad  
 
 
 
 
Q2FY27: Sales of food products grew by 13.0%YoY, driven by sustained demand across Snacks business , continued 
expansion across markets  and improving traction across channels; Gross margin stood at 34.4  %; EBITDA margin at 
12.6  % 
➢Core  markets : Core markets registered robust growth of 17.5% YoY . 
➢Expanding Distribution reach: Direct coverage increased by approximately 19.2k outlets, taking the total 
reach to  3.9 lakh outlets  
➢Sustained Business Momentum:  Growth was supported by continued consumer demand, deeper market 
penetration and improving channel traction

About  Bikaji  Foods  International  Ltd. (https://www.bikaji.com/ ) 
Bikaji Foods International is the third largest ethnic snacks company in India with an international 
footprint, selling Indian snacks and sweets and is the second fastest growing company in the 
Indian organised snacks market. (Source: F&S Report ). Late Shri Shiv Ratan Agarwal launched the 
Bikaji brand in the year 1993  with  an in-depth  understanding  of Indian  tastes  and preferences.  The 
company  as of March  31, 2026, has  operations  in 27 states  and 7 union  territories  in India.  The 
company  also exports  its products  to 47 international  countries,  including North America, Europe, 
the Middle East, Africa and Asia Pacific. Bikaji Foods'  product  range  includes  six principal  
categories:  bhujia,  namkeen,  packaged  sweets,  papad,  western snacks  as well as other  snacks  
which primarily include gift packs (assortment), frozen food, mathri range and cookies.
