Sterling Opens Leopard Haven Jawai, Deepens Rajasthan 
Footprint and Wilderness Portfolio  
New resort adds a distinctive wildlife stop to SHRL’s Rajasthan circuit, combining 
leopard country with locally rooted experiences and intimate stays  
Chennai, 14 October 2026:  Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited (SHRL) has announced the 
opening of Sterling Leopard Haven Jawai  in Rajasthan, adding strength to both its 
presence in the state and its growing wilderness portfolio. Set near Bera amid Jawai’s 
distinctive granite terrain, the resort gives travellers a base to explore one of India’s most 
unusual wildlife landscapes —  where leopards move through rocky outcrops and caves 
rather than dense forests.  
Jawai’s appeal, however, extends well beyond the safari. The Jawai Dam reservoir brings 
rich birdlife into the experience, while the surrounding countryside, tribal villages and 
Rabari communities and regional culinary traditions reveal a slower, more text ured side 
of Rajasthan. The opportunity is therefore not simply to visit Jawai for a sighting, but to spend enough time to understand the place around it.  
Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director & CEO, Sterling Holiday Resorts Limited (SHRL), 
said:  “Jawai is compelling because the reason you arrive and the reason you remember 
the holiday can be very different. A leopard may create the ﬁrst pull, but the real richness 
comes from everything that surrounds that experience.  
For Sterling, this is exactly the kind of destination that strengthens the portfolio in more 
than one way. It deepens our presence in Rajasthan, adds further weight to our national 
wilderness offering and also creates a natural link in an Mount Abu -Udaipur –Jawai –
Jodhpur journey. Our growth is increasingly about building more around why people travel 
— creating richer reasons to stay, explore and discover more of India. ”  
Boasting of 19 indulgent cottages, suites and villas, Sterling Leopard Haven Jawai has 
been designed as an intimate retreat.  The Jawai Villas offer private balconies and 
bathtubs; Pool Villas add plunge pools and secluded outdoor spaces; while the Two -
Bedroom Pool Villa brings adjoining villas together for groups who want to stay together 
while retaining privacy. An inﬁnity poo l, kids’ play area and outdoor spaces provide easy 
downtime between excursions.  
Local cuisine adds another layer to the stay. At Jawai Vista, guests can indulge in 
Rajasthani ﬂavours inspired combinations of regional millets and rice, alongside the familiar Methi Bajra Khichda, Panchkutte ki Subzi, Mathania Chicken, Jawai Laal Maas, 
and Chef’s curated specials bowls, all coupled with views ac ross the valley and pool. 
  
  
 
 
  
Where conditions permit, outdoor breakfasts, safari picnics and locally inspired meals 
take the experience beyond the restaurant and into the landscape itself.  
That connection to the local ecosystem becomes tangible through guided leopard 
safaris, excursions around Jawai Dam, birdwatching and crocodile spotting, 
alongside opportunities to explore the surrounding countryside and communities . 
Together, these experiences reveal more of Jawai’s natural and lived character, moving 
the holiday beyond a single wildlife sighting and giving travellers more reasons to explore 
the region at a slower pace.  
Its location between Udaipur and Jodhpur further strengthens that proposition. Jawai 
introduces a contrasting wilderness chapter into a Rajasthan journey otherwise 
dominated by forts, palaces and heritage cities. For SHRL, that creates strategic value 
beyo nd another resort opening: a stronger Rajasthan presence, greater relevance in 
wilderness travel, and a more compelling regional circuit for guests.  
Speaking on the launch, Manish Gurani, owner BMMR Hospitality LLP said:  “What 
drew us to Jawai was its rarity — a place where wildlife, open landscapes and everyday 
life exist side by side without the destination feeling over -engineered for tourism. We 
wanted the resort to preserve that sense of space and discovery while givi ng travellers 
the comfort to stay longer and see more of the region. Sterling was the right partner 
because it understands how to build demand around destinations, not just properties, 
and has the reach to introduce Jawai to a much wider leisure audience. ”  
Sterling Leopard Haven Jawai also reﬂects the company’s broader approach to 
expansion: building its portfolio around distinct reasons to travel . 
Rather than viewing destinations simply as locations for hotels, Sterling is increasingly 
developing hospitality bases around the motivations that take travellers there — whether 
wildlife, nature, spirituality, culture, wellness or discovery.  
That philosophy is consistent with Sterling’s stated approach of building travel circuits rather than simply “marking dots on a map. ”
