We would like to inform you that, EVEY Trans Private Limited (‘EVEY”) had been 
awarded two Letters of Award (LOAs) from Telangana State Road Transport 
Corporation (TSRTC) for 500 Buses for Intra-city and 50 Buses for Inter-city 
operations, respectively. As per the contractual terms, these buses were required to 
be procured from Olectra Greentech Limited (“Olectra/ Company”) and who were 
additionally responsible for maintenance of these buses. 
In this regard, pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 we 
had submitted the required disclosure vide our letter dated 06.03.2023 about 
bagging of aforesaid orders for 500 Buses for Intra-city and 50 Buses for Inter- 
city operations from TSRTC along with all required details. 
In terms of the tender conditions, EVEY is executing this Contract with TSRTC 
through a Special Purpose Vehicle Company (SPV) namely “EVEY Trans (TEL) 
Private Limited” wherein EVEY is holding 74% stake and Olectra is holding 
remaining 26% stake. 
Out of the above orders, in respect of a lot of SO Buses for Inter-city operations 
TSRTC has reduced the order to 10 buses which have already been delivered and in 
respect of the balance 40 buses the order has been withdrawn by TSRTC.
