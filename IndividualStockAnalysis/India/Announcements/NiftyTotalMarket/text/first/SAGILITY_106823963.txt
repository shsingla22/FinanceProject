Sagility Accelerates Provider Growth Strategy Through 
Acquisition of Chirok Health  
Transaction expands provider  - focused capabilities in Clinical Documentation Improvement, 
Coding Audit and Quality Assurance , Revenue Integrity , Value -Based Care Solutions , HCC 
Coding and Risk Adjustment.  
 
Bangalore , 21 October , 2026  – Sagility, a leading tech nology and AI -led healthcare operations 
company focused on improving payer and provider performance, today announced that it has 
acquired Chirok Health, a provider of Clinical Documentation Improvement (CDI), medical 
coding, coding quality assurance programs and revenue integrity services for healthcare 
organizations.  
The acquisition adds specialized clinical and coding expertise to Sagility’s provider services 
portfolio, complementing its strengths in revenue cycle management, patient access, analytics, 
and technology and AI -led healthcare operations. It broadens Sagility’s ability to address 
evolving provider needs with integrated solutions.  
Founded with a focus on helping healthcare providers improve documentation quality, coding 
accuracy, reimbursement outcomes, and financial performance, Chirok Health has established 
a strong reputation for delivering high -quality clinical and administrativ e solutions to hospitals 
and health systems. Chirok also brings a highly specialized team of clinical documentation 
specialists, coding professionals, auditors, and healthcare subject matter experts further 
enhanc ing Sagility's provider -focused domain expertise  to support healthcare providers across 
a broader spectrum of the revenue cycle and clinical operations value chain.  
"The acquisition of Chirok Health is an important step in Sagility's strategy to build a 
comprehensive provider -focused services platform. Chirok's leadership in clinical 
documentation improvement, medical coding , quality assurance programs , and revenue 
integrity complements our existing capabilities and expands our ability to support healthcare 
providers across the healthcare  value chain  by improving operational, clinical and financial 
outcomes .” said  Ramesh Gopalan, Managing Director and Group CEO of Sagility . 
“Together with our recent acquisition of CareSeed, this transaction enhances our ability to help 
healthcare organizations improve quality outcomes, optimize reimbursement, advance value -
based care initiatives, and drive meaningful operational transformation  through analytics, 
automation, and AI ."  
"Joining Sagility represents an exciting next chapter for Chirok Health," said Kanar Kokoy , 
Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Chirok Health. "Both organizations share a 
commitment to clinical excellence, customer service, innovation, and measurable outcomes. 
Our clients will continue to receive the specialized expertise and high touch  support they value 
today, while gaining access to a broader set of capabilities that can help address evolving 
operational and financial challenges."  
 
As healthcare providers continue to face increasing reimbursement complexity, talent 
challenges, and growing value -based care requirements, the combined organization will be 
positioned to help clients improve financial sustainability, quality outcomes, operational 
performance, and patient experience . The combination will also create opportunities to deepen 
existing client relationships, expand the services footprint across both organizations’ client 
bases, and introduce complementary capabilities that can contribute to the growth of the 
combined busi ness.  
Canaccord Genuity LLC served as the exclusive financial advisor to Chirok Health on the 
transaction.  
About Sagility   
Sagility is a healthcare operations partner that helps payers, providers, and pharmacy benefit 
managers reduce cost, improve quality, and enhance member and provider experiences. With 
more than 25 years of exclusive focus in healthcare, Sagility combines d eep domain expertise 
with technology and AI -led transformation, embedding analytics, automation, and AI into 
operations. The company delivers services across the United States, India, the Philippines, 
Jamaica, and Colombia.  
For more information, visit https://sagility.com/    
About Chirok Health  
Founded in 2020, Chirok Health provides clinical documentation integrity, medical coding, 
audit and quality assurance, and revenue cycle optimization services. The company supports 
healthcare organizations across fee -for-service and value -based care models , combining 
clinical and coding expertise with technology -enabled workflows to strengthen documentation 
accuracy, compliance, and financial performance.  
To learn more, visit chirokhealth.com.

Acquisition Rationale:  
 Acquiring Chirok Health accelerates Sagility's strategy of building a comprehensive provider -
focused services platform by adding specialized clinical documentation, medical coding and 
coding quality assurance to bolster its mid -revenue cycle capabilities. The transaction deepens 
Sagility's clinical domain expertise, expands its addressable market across providers and 
payers, and creates new opportunities to drive revenue growth through integrated clinical and 
revenue cycle solutions.   
1. Strengthens Sagility's Provider end -to-end Revenue Cycle services  
Expands Sagility's capabilities across Clinical Documentation Improvement (CDI), medical 
coding and coding QA enhancing Sagility’s ability to serve health systems, physician groups, 
and value -based care organizations.  Chirok’s mid -revenue cycle solutions a re a natural 
complement to Sagility’s proven front -end and back -end revenue cycle offerings, helping 
Sagility generate improved financial and operational outcomes for healthcare providers from 
patient access through final payment.  
2. Accelerates Cross -Sell and Revenue Growth Opportunities  
The acquisition creates significant cross -selling opportunities across both organizations' client 
bases by introducing Sagility's broader analytics, AI and automation driven RCM  services  to 
Chirok clients while also offering Chirok's solutions to current Sagility provider and payer 
customers. The combined organization is well positioned to deepen client relationships, expand 
services footprint and generate increased operating synergies an d value for current and future 
clients.  
3. Expands Value -Based Care and Payer Opportunities  
Chirok enhances Sagility’s capabilities in risk adjustment  creating a more comprehensive 
offering for risk -bearing providers and health plans. Combining these capabilities with 
Sagility's care management, care coordination, and care -gap closure offerings c reates 
differentiated solutions.  
4. Strategic Clinical Talent and Domain Expertise  
Chirok brings a highly specialized team of clinical documentation specialists, coding 
professionals, auditors, and healthcare subject matter experts enhancing Sagility's clinical 
capabilities and leadership in provider operations.
