Press Release 
 
Jupiter Wagons to manufacture metro trains and propulsion 
systems in India through joint venture with Korea’s Woojin 
Industrial Systems  
 
80:20 joint venture brings complete technology transfer for metro coaches and for propulsion systems 
serving metro, EMU, Vande Bharat, semi-high-speed and high-speed trains, completing JWL’s rail 
portfolio from the wheels to the traction motor 
 
National, November 23rd 2026: Jupiter Wagons Limited (JWL) (NSE: JWL | BSE: 533272), one of India’s leading 
rail and mobility manufacturers, has entered into a joint venture with Woojin Industrial Systems Co., Ltd. (Woojin) of 
the Republic of Korea, a respected name in railway propulsion, train control and metro rolling stock with more than 
five decades of engineering heritage. JWL will hold 80 per cent of the joint venture and Woojin will hold 20 per cent. 
The joint venture marks JWL’s entry into two of the most technology-intensive segments of rolling stock: complete 
metro coaches and propulsion systems. It will manufacture entire metro coaches in India together with all their critical 
components from Woojin’s portfolio, including Propulsion Systems, Train Control and Management Systems 
(TCMS), automatic door systems, bogies and aluminium car body shells. 
 
Propulsion, the electrical system that powers, controls and regeneratively brakes a modern electric train, sits at the 
heart of every electric trainset. Beyond metro, the joint venture’s propulsion systems will serve the wider spectrum of 
India’s electric trains, including Vande Bharat trainsets, Electric Multiple Units (EMUs), and semi-high-speed and 
high-speed trains. 
 
Ready for India’s metro era  
India’s metro story has grown from a handful of cities into a national movement. New lines are opening in state capitals 
and fast-growing cities, while established networks extend their reach to meet rising ridership. Operators increasingly 
seek trains that are lighter, more energy efficient, more automated and built in India. The joint venture is designed for 
exactly this demand: it will manufacture complete metro rakes in India, pairing Woojin’s proven metro and train 
control technology with JWL’s manufacturing scale and deep relationships across India’s rail ecosystem 
 
Exclusive technology, Indian engineering 
Woojin will undertake a complete transfer of technology to the joint venture. Under Make in India, the joint venture 
will design, engineer, manufacture, test, commission and maintain these critical systems in India. A dedicated 
manufacturing facility, including capacity for aluminium car bodies, will support India’s move towards lighter and 
more energy-efficient trains. 
 
A pathway to high-speed rail 
Woojin brings a proven record across the speed spectrum. Since 2019, it has been entrusted with more than 2,200 
metro and commuter cars in South Korea for Seoul Metro, Korea Railroad Corporation (KORAIL) and Busan 
 
Transportation Corporation, and has also supplied rolling stock and rail systems to other countries. Its propulsion 
technology has powered Korea’s KTX high-speed trains and its intercity fleets. This gives JWL a proven foundation 
for Vande Bharat, semi-high-speed and high-speed trains as India builds its first high-speed corridor and prepares a 
new generation of high-speed lines announced in the Union Budget 2026-27. 
 
From the wheel to propulsion to complete metro 
The joint venture completes a rail portfolio that now spans the whole train. JWL manufactures freight wagons, bogies, 
couplers; railway wheels, axles and wheelsets through Jupiter Tatravagonka Railwheel Factory (JTRWF), in which 
Italy’s Lucchini RS and SIMEST are shareholders; brake systems, brake discs; and weldable cast manganese steel 
crossings. It is building passenger rail capability through its collaboration with Poland’s PESA Bydgoszcz. With metro 
coaches, propulsion and train control now added, JWL will deliver complete trainsets, from the wheels and bogies and 
train control systems. This makes JWL one of very few companies in the world with this end-to-end capability, 
delivered from its state-of-the-art facilities across India. 
 
Mr. Vivek Lohia, Managing Director, Jupiter Wagons Limited, said:  “Propulsion is the heart of every modern 
train, and metro is where India’s cities are heading. As India builds metro, regional and high-speed networks at a 
scale the world has rarely seen, building these trains and their core technology at home is a strategic necessity. With 
Woojin, we bring proven metro, propulsion and train control technology to India on an exclusive basis, with Indian 
engineers building on it and Indian ownership of what we create. This joint venture takes JWL from the wheel to the 
propulsion system and makes us a complete rolling stock partner for India’s rail ambitions. It also adds a strong 
industrial chapter to the India and Korea partnership.”
