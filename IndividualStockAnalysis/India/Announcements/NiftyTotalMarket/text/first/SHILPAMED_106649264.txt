USFDA Inspection of Shilpa Medicare Ltd. Unit VI, Dabaspet , Bengaluru  
 
This is to notify that the USFDA conducted Pre-Approval Inspection (PAI)  at Unit VI of Shilpa Medicare Limited, 
located at Plot No.29/A, Sompura, Phase 4, Avarehalli Industrial Area, Dabaspet , Bengaluru, Karnataka from  25 
May 2026 to 29 May 2026 . The inspection ended with the issuance of FDA Form 483 with  5(five)  procedural 
observations  and none of which ha ve been categorized as a repeat observation  and not related to data integrity .  
 
The facility is involved in manufacturing, packaging, labelling and testing of specialized finished dosage forms as 
oral dispersible/dissolving Films and Transdermal Patches.    
 
Unit VI had previously undergone a USFDA GMP inspection and holds a Voluntary Action Indicated (VAI)  status, 
reflecting the facility's strong track record of regulatory compliance . In addition to its USFDA approval, Unit VI 
holds accreditations  from Europe - EMA, Saudi Arabia -SFDA, UK -MHRA , and Australia - TGA.  
 
The Company shall respond comprehensively to the procedural observations within the stipulated time.  
 
We do not expect this development to have any material impact on the current business operations or existing 
supplies from this facility.
