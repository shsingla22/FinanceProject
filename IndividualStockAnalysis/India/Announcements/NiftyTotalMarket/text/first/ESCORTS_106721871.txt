On Consolidated basis, For the quarter ended June 2026, Revenue from Continuing operations came 
at z 3,207.6 crore up 28.3%, as against z 2,500.1 crore in corresponding quarter and up by 8.1 % as 
against z 2,968.2 crore in sequential quarter. Consolidated Net Profit After Tax before exceptional item 
from continuing operatio ns for quarter ended June 2026 came at z 385.9 crore up by 26.8% as against 
z 304.4 crore in corresponding quarter and up by 20.4% as against z 320.5 crore in the Sequentia l 
quarter. Earnings per share (EPS) for the quarter ended June 2026 is at z 35.08 as against z 33.59 in 
corresponding quarter and z 29.13 in sequential quarter. 
The corresponding quarter of the previous year included a one-time gain arising from the divestment 
of the RED business, as well as an exceptional gain from the sale of land and building. Consolidated 
Net Profit after tax including exceptional items and discontinued operations came at z 385.9 crore as 
against z 1,397.1 crore in corresponding quarter and as against z 320.5 crore in sequential quarter. 
Commenting on the results, management said: "We are pleased to deliver our best-ever Q 1 
performance, driven by strong business fundamentals, disciplined execution, and healthy treasury 
income. Despite commodity inflation pressures, our focused cost management and operational 
efficiencies helped sustain growth. We remain confident about the future, supported by a robust 
pipeline of new product launches, continued innovation, and our commitment to creating value for 
customers and stakeholders." 
SEGMENT WISE PERFORMANCE 
Agri Machinery Products 
For the quarter ended June 2026, Tractors volumes came at 36,862 units up by 20.5% as against 
30,581 units in correspond ing quarter and up by 14.3% as against 32,257 units in sequential quarter. 
In domestic tractor market volumes were up by 22.9% against 18.6% in Industry leading to some 
market share gain. Segment revenue came at z 2,766.5 crore in quarter ended June 2026 up by 26.8% 
as against z 2,181.5 crore in corresponding quarter and up by 15.5% as against z 2,395.7 crore in 
sequential quarter. EBIT margin for the quarter ended June 2026 came at 10.8% as against 12.6% in 
corresponding quarter and 11.3% in sequential quarter. 
Construction Equipment 
For the quarter ended June 2026, Construction Equipment's sales volume was at 1,344 units up by 
27.4% as against 1,055 units in corresponding quarter and as against 1,877 units in sequential 
quarter. Segment revenue came at z 419 .6 crore in quarter ended June 2026 up by 39 .2% as against 
z 301.5 crore in corresponding quarter and as against z 556.5 crore in sequentia l quarter. EBIT margin 
for the quarter ended June 2026 were at 5.4% as against 5.8% in correspond ing quarter and 12.7% in 
sequential quarter.
