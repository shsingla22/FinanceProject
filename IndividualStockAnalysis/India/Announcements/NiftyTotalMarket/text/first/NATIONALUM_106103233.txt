PRESS RELEASE  
 
	
Dr.	Tapas	Kumar	Pattanayak 	takes	over	as	
Director 	(HR)	of	NALCO 	
	
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Bhubaneswar, 02/01/2025:  D r .  T a p a s  K u m a r  
Pattanayak has assumed today the charge of Director 
(Human Resources) of National Aluminium Company 
Limited (NALCO), a Navratna company, under Ministry 
of Mines, Govt of India. Prior to the present assignment, 
Dr. Pattanayak was serving in the Indian Oil Corporation 
Ltd as Executive Director (HR) at the Refineries Division 
Head Office at Delhi.  
With a distinguished career spanning over 33 years in the 
field of Human Resources & Industrial Relations, Dr. 
Tapas Kumar Pattanayak brings with him a wealth of 
experience in organizational development, employee 
engagement, talent acquisition, Industrial Relations, 
learning & development and HR strategy. Dr. Pattanayak has rich  experience in mining, 
refinery, oil and gas sectors. An alumnus of Utkal University, Dr. Pattanayak has Masters and 
PhD in the area of Personnel Management & Industrial Relations and also holds a degree in 
Law.   
Dr. Tapas Kumar Pattanayak has been keen in implementing innova tive HR practices that aligns 
with organizational goals. At NALCO Dr. Pattanayak will be over seeing all human resources 
functions, including talent acquisition, employee development, organizational effectiveness, 
and ensuring that NALCO’s HR practices continue to support the company’s vision and long-
term objectives.  
Shri Sanjay Lohiya, IAS, Additional Secretary, Ministry of Mine s and CMD, NALCO, 
congratulated Dr. Pattanayak for assuming the role of Director (HR), and expressed hope that 
his strategic focus on innovation will enhance the organization 's reputation as a great place to 
work, fostering a collaborative, inclusive, and high-performanc e culture across the 
organization. With his joining as Director (HR), NALCO’s Board of Directors has been further 
strengthened, he said.
