Usha Martin unveils New Logo | Embarks on a Transformative Journey
Kolkata, September 16, 2024: Usha Martin Limited (NSE: USHAMART, BSE: 517146, 
Luxembourg SE: US9173002042), a leading specialty wire rope solutions provider globally, 
embarks on a transformative journey with the unveiling of a fresh new identity. Over the past 
few years, Usha Martin has consistently pushed boundaries to become one of the world’s 
leading manufacturers and innovators of high-performance wire ropes used in critical 
applications across various industries. As the Company has expanded its capabilities, the brand 
needed to evolve to better represent who Usha Martin is today. 
The new logo signifies two fundamental strengths of the Usha Martin brand. The engineering 
capabilities are represented through a hexagon, symbolizing the cross section of a wire rope, 
and the endeavour for constant growth, excellence, and innovation is illustrated through an 
upward arrow. The new design is modern, dynamic, and reflects Usha Martin’s commitment to 
engineering excellence and progressive growth. The Company has now established a unified 
identity for Usha Martin and its subsidiaries/JVs, ensuring a consistent and recognisable 
experience. This transformative journey reinforces the Company’s ability to deliver exceptional 
products and services with a stronger and well-integrated global team.
The tagline “Engineered to Transform” speaks to Usha Martin’s role as a catalyst for progress. 
The Company recognises its critical role in the success of the end-industries it serves – including 
oil and gas, renewable energy, construction, elevators, mining, fishing, and ports, among others. 
Usha Martin is transforming how people move, how energy is consumed, and how infrastructure 
is built for tomorrow. The Company is focused on creating  impactful solutions that help 
customers stay ahead of the curve and empower progress for industries worldwide. 
Commenting on the development, Mr. Rajeev Jhawar, Managing Director of the Company said, 
“This is an exciting time for Usha Martin as we unveil a new logo that reflects both our 
transformative journey and our engineering expertise. The new identity symbolizes our 
commitment to innovation and our relentless pursuit of excellence. This evolution will further 
help us strengthen our position as a leading global player in the wire rope industry. We are 
dedicated to driving transformation in the industries we serve and continuing to deliver 
cutting-edge engineering solutions that empower our customers to achieve their goals."

Established in the year 1960, Usha Martin is a leading global and India’s No. 1 specialty steel wire rope 
solutions provider. The Company is also engaged in the manufacturing of high-quality wires, low relaxation prestressed concrete steel strand (LRPC), bespoke end-fitments, accessories and related services. 
Usha Martin’s wire rope manufacturing facilities in Ranchi, Hoshiarpur, Dubai, Bangkok and UK produce 
the widest range of wire ropes that find application in various industries across the world. All of the company’s facilities are equipped with the latest state-of-the-art high-capacity machines to manufacture world-class products.
Usha Martin’s global R&D center located in Italy is actively engaged in designing of wire ropes and uses 
proprietary design software to develop products that are the best in class. The Company also has an extensive and dedicated network of distribution centers located across the globe.
