Ampere powers ahead: FY27 E2W volumes surpass full -year FY26 in just 7 
months; reaches the Top 5 position  
 
- 93% YoY growth reflects accelerating consumer momentum  
- During FY27, 70,333 VAHAN registrations till November 15, 2026, versus 61,842 in 
full-year FY26  
- Secures 5th position with 5.7% market share for Nov 2026 (till November 15, 2026 ) 
 
New Delhi/Bengaluru, 16 November 2026:  Ampere, the electric two -wheeler brand of Greaves 
Electric Mobility Limited (GEML), continues to accelerate its growth momentum, supported by 
increasing consumer traction for its products and a growing presence across the Indian electric two -
wheeler marke t. 
In FY27, until November 15, 2026, Ampere recorded 70,333 VAHAN registrations, surpassing the 
61,842 registrations recorded during the entire FY26. This represents 93% year -on-year growth, 
compared with 36,452 registrations during the corresponding period o f FY26. Ampere reached  5th 
position with a 5.7% share of the electric two -wheeler market as on 15th Nov 2026  
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Vikas Singh, Managing Director, Greaves Electric Mobility , said, 
“India’s transition to electric mobility will be shaped by products that deliver real value without 
compromising aspiration. That is the opportunity we are building Ampere around. Through ‘Bharat Ki 
Smart Ride’, we are bringing together reliability, afford ability and meaningful technology for everyday 
India. The momentum we are seeing strengthens our resolve to scale with discipline, deepen our 
consumer connect and build a sustainable and profitable electric mobility business.”  
Ampere’s multiple award -winning EV portfolio, including the Nexus EX+, Magnus GMax, Magnus Neo, 
and Reo Vyb, is designed around the diverse everyday mobility needs of Indian consumers. The 
portfolio reflects the Ampere’s ‘Bharat Ki Smart Ride’ proposition of combining practical innovation, 
reliability and value for India’s evolving mobility needs.  
Ampere has also demonstrated product capability in demanding real -world conditions. The Magnus 
GMax with Intellipack recently completed the Ultimate Endurance Ride, covering over 4,000 km in just 
15 days, from Dong Valley in Arunachal Pradesh to Guhar Moti  in Gujarat, traversing mountains, plains 
and desert. The Nexus has previously conquered the 70 hairpin bends of Kolli Hills and reached Shipki 
La Pass at 13,200 ft, demonstrating performance and capability across varied Indian terrain.  
Ampere continues to strengthen its product portfolio, retail reach, financing ecosystem and customer 
experience as it builds towards making electric mobility a more accessible and preferred choice for 
consumers across India.

About Greaves Electric Mobility Limited:  
Greaves Electric Mobility Limited (GEML), the electric mobility business of Greaves Cotton Limited 
(GCL), is one of the first companies in India to focus on the evolving market of electric vehicles (“ EV”) 
and has been among the frontrunners at driving EV adoption in the country. With over 17 years  of 
experience designing and manufacturing electric vehicles, GEML serves both the electric 2 -wheeler 
and 3 -wheeler segments. GEML along with its subsidiaries, operates three manufacturing plants 
located in Hyderabad, Noida, and Ranipet. The company supports customers through 600+ Ampere 
touchpoints across India, enabling wider access, dependable service and an enhanced ownership 
experience.  GEML holds 8 India Book of Records* and 1 Asia Book of Records* titles, reflecting its 
focus on technology and design led innovative & durable mobility solutions . Subsidiaries MLR Auto 
Limited and Bestway Agencies Private Limited help extend GEML’s market presence. Committed to 
affordability and sustainability, GEML provides clean mobility solutions contributing to India’s shift 
toward electric transportation.
