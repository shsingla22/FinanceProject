 AIR INDIA AND SBI CARD TO LAUNCH CO-BRANDED  
CREDIT CARD SOON 
Industry leading organisations across airlines, credit cards and payment networks unite to curate new 
co-brand credit card program  
Gurugram, 28 October 2026: Air India, India’s leading global airline, and SBI Card, India’s largest 
pure-play credit card issuer, today announced having entered a partnership to develop a new co-brand 
Visa-powered credit card program that will offer a rewarding suite of travel and lifestyle benefits to 
members of Air India's Maharaja Club loyalty programme.  
The partnership combines the complementary strengths of three leading organisations across airlines, 
credit cards, and payment network, to create a compelling proposition for Air India's fast-growing base 
of frequent flyers and loyalty members.  
As India’s flag carrier, Air India has a long and distinguished history of connecting India to the world. 
SBI Card brings trusted credit card expertise, deep experience of serving Indian customers, and a 
strong understanding of their evolving payment needs. With over four decades of presence in India, 
Visa combines its technology leadership with the scale of its global payments network and an extensive 
experience in building successful co-brand partnerships around the world.  
Nipun Aggarwal, Chief Commercial Officer, Air India,  said, "As we transform every aspect of the 
customer journey with Air India, from a modernising fleet and world-class cabin products to an 
expanding global network, we are also building one of India's most compelling loyalty ecosystems with 
Maharaja Club. We see a tremendous opportunity to create a co-brand credit card that is unmatched in 
its ability to reward customers for both their everyday earning and their travel & lifestyle aspirations. 
Together with SBI Card and Visa, our goal is to build India's most compelling travel credit card 
programme and make Maharaja Club the loyalty currency of choice for millions of Indians." 
Salila Pande, Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer, SBI Card, said, “As travel aspirations 
evolve and customers seek experiences that offer greater value and convenience, we see a significant 
opportunity to redefine the role of credit cards in their travel journeys. At SBI Card, our focus remains 
on developing differentiated credit card propositions that are aligned with evolving customer 
preferences, unique spending needs, and lifestyle aspirations. Through this strategic partnership, we 
aim to create a compelling travel-focussed credit card that delivers rewarding experiences to customers. 
This partnership marks another significant step towards delivering innovative payment solutions for 
customers and expanding customer choice.” 
Suresh Sethi, Group Country Manager, India and South Asia, Visa , said, “Our partnership with Air 
India and SBI Card reflects a shared ambition to shape the next generation of payment experiences - 
where every transaction adds greater value to the journey. By bringing together Visa’s trusted global 
network and decades of co-brand expertise, we aim to create a compelling proposition that elevates 
travel for Air India and SBI Card customers in India and around the world.” 
With a simplified structure, a host of customer-friendly features, renamed tiers, and an updated identity, 
Maharaja Club has added over five million new members since the programme was redesigned in 2024. 
Maharaja Club members are now able to earn and redeem Maharaja Points on flights operated by Air 
India and Air India Express to and from 104 destinations across North America, Europe (including the 
UK), Australia, Middle East, Africa, Southeast Asia, South Asia, in addition 25 other Star Alliance 
member airlines. Maharaja Club’s partnerships with over 100 other brands across categories, including 
hospitality, retail, lifestyle and financial services, enables members to earn Maharaja Points across a 
broad range of travel and everyday spends.

 About Air India Group 
The Air India Group – comprising full-service global airline, Air India, and value carrier, Air India Express 
– is spearheading a new era of Indian aviation. The Air India story began in 1932 when JRD Tata piloted 
the airline’s inaugural flight and opened the skies for aviation in India. Today, Air India Group employs 
more than 30,000 people, operates over 300 aircraft and carries travellers to 60 domestic and 51 
international destinations across five continents. 
Returning to Tata Sons in 2022 following 70 years under Government ownership, Air India Group is in 
the midst of a five-year transformation programme, Vihaan.AI. As part of the transformation, Air India 
has placed orders for 600 new aircraft. In addition to taking new aircraft deliveries, Air India is 
progressively retrofitting all its legacy aircraft. 
The Air India Group operates South Asia’s largest aviation training academy in Gurugram, India. The 
construction of a new flying school and a greenfield maintenance base is in progress. 
With transformation underway across all facets of the business and India’s rich legacy of hospitality, Air 
India is committed to being a world class global airline with an Indian heart.
