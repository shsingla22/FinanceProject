Consolidated Financials for Q 2 FY2 7 Results  
 
• Godrej Properties ’ achieves bookings  of INR 8, 008  crores  in Q2 FY27  and INR 
16,659 crores  in H1 FY27 . 
• Best- ever quarter for s ustenance sales growing  83% YoY and 43 % QoQ in Q2 
FY27 . Sustenance sales  grew 55%  in H1 FY27 . 
• Collections grew by 31 % YoY to INR 5 ,318  crores  in Q2 FY27 and  grew 25% 
YoY to INR 9,666 crores in H1 FY27 . 
• Operating cash flow grew by 297% QoQ and 33% YoY to INR 1,584 crores in 
Q2 FY27 . Operating cash flow in H1 FY27  was INR 1,983 crores.  
• Construction and related outflow grew by 42% in H1 FY27.  
 
 
Mumbai, October  23, 2026: Godrej Properties Limited  (GPL), a lea ding national real estate 
developer, announced its  financial results for the second  quarter ended September  30, 2026. 
 
CORPORATE HIGHLIGHTS :  
 
• Sales highlights  
o Godrej Properties’ achieved a booking value of INR 8,008 crores in Q2 FY27. 
This was achieved through the sale of 3,658 units  with a total area of 6.2 million 
sq. ft.  
o H1 FY27 booking value grew 7% YoY to INR 16,659  crore . This was achieved 
through the sale of 7,396 units with a total area of 12.3 million sq. ft. This is the 
highest ever H1 booking value achieved by Godrej Properties.  
o Best-ever quarter for sustenance sales growing  83% YoY and 43% QoQ in Q2 
FY27. Sustenance sales grew 55%  in H1 FY27.  3 projects contributed more than 
INR 500 crores to sustenance sales in the quarter -  Godrej Trilogy in MMR, 
Godrej Vanantara in Bengaluru and Godrej Samaris in Gurugram.  
o Sales  in Q2 FY27  were also driven by  strong demand in some key new 
project  launches including Godrej Crown Residences, Noida (INR 1,0 36 crores) , 
Godrej Regent Park (INR 463 crores)  and Godrej Florenne (INR 142 crores)  in 
Bengaluru, and 2 plotted projects –  Rivershore Estate, Nagpur (INR 310 crores)  
and Greenvale Estate, Raipur (INR 276 crores) . Godrej Brooklyn Avenue, 
Hyderabad, launched towards the end of first quarter also contributed INR 727 
crores to bookings.  GODREJ PROPERTIES LIMITED

Commenting on the performance of Q 2 FY2 7, Mr. Pirojsha  Godrej, Chair person , Godrej 
Properties Limited, said:  
“While some of the headline earnings and bookings numbers for the quarter look muted, Q2 was a 
very strong operating quarter with sustenance sales growth of 83% YoY to INR 5,054  crore and 
cash collections growth of 31% YoY to INR 5,318 crore.   We only launched one third of our expected 
annual launch pipeline in the first half of the financial year and were still able to deliver 43% of our annual bookings guidance highlighting the continued strength of underlying demand in the secto r.   
  
Our launch pipeline has never been stronger and with launches picking up substantially from this 
month onwards, we remain confident of surpassing our full year guidance across all key operating 
metrics.   We are especially focused on generating strong cash flows and expect  INR  15,000 crore of 
collections and INR 7,000 crore of operating cash flow  in H2 FY27.   This will allow us to both invest
