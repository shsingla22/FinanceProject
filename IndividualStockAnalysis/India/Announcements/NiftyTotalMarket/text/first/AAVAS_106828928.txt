Dear Chairperson & Members of the Board,
Subject: Resignation from the Position of Independent Director of the Company.
Over the past several months I  have been giving considerable thought to my continued 
association as an Independent Director with Aavas, considering the increasing professional 
commitments to my company, Waterfield Advisors.
In this context and after considerable deliberation, I  believe this may be the right time for me 
to step down from the Aavas Board. Aavas is at an inflexion point, with a successful promoter 
and senior leadership transition over the past two years. A new director would bring in fresh 
perspectives and provide the necessary focus to the company's evolving priorities and future 
growth initiatives.
Accordingly, I  hereby resign from the position of Independent Director w.e.f. close of business 
hours on October 26, 2026. Consequently, I  shall also cease to be a Member and Chairperson 
of all Committees of the Board of the Company on which I  am currently serving.
I  confirm that my resignation is due to the reasons set out above, and that there is no other 
material reason for my resignation.
It has been a privilege to serve on the Board, and I  would like to thank each of you for your 
support, guidance, and collaboration throughout my tenure. I  remain confident about the 
Company's future under the leadership of Manu and wish the Board and management 
continued success.
Yours faithfully,
