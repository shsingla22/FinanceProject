GMM Pfaudler appoints Mr. Gregory Gelhaus  
as Chief Transformation Office r of the GMM Pfaudler Group  
 
Mumbai, May  5, 202 5: GMM Pfaudler Ltd, a leading technology, systems, and service provider for the 
chemical and pharmaceutical industries, announced today that Mr. Gregory Gelhaus has been appointed 
as Chief Transformation Officer (CTO)  of the GMM Pfaudler Group effective May 5, 2025 . 
  
Mr. Gelhaus is part of the promoter family and will join the company's senior management team. Mr. 
Gelhaus has a proven track record of driving business growth, executing financial and operational 
turnarounds, and expanding into new markets. As CTO, he wil l lead the group’s transformation efforts 
and key strategic initiatives to drive business expansion, improve operational efficiencies, and enhance 
collaboration and integration across the company's operations and locations.  
  
Mr. Gelhaus has extensive and diverse experience across various industries in North America, Europe, and 
the Asia Pacific. Before joining GMM Pfaudler, he served as the Chief Operating Officer for Asia Pacific and 
Central Europe at Global Blue, a publicly traded company backed by private equity. In that role, he 
managed 17 businesses across 15 countries and was a member of the Executive Committee. Prior to his 
tenure at Global Blue, he held positions at PricewaterhouseCoopers, Alvarez & Marsal, and Kirkham Group 
Pte Ltd.  
 
Mr. Gelhaus holds a BBA in Accounting from the Ross School of Business at the University of Michigan 
and an MBA  with concentrations  in Finance and Marketing from  the Wharton School at the University of 
Pennsylvania.  Mr. Gelhaus  is a Certified Public Accountant (CPA) in the State of New York  in the United 
States.  
 
Mr. Gelhaus will be based in Switzerland and will report to Managing Director Tarak Patel.  
 
“We are delighted to welcome  Greg  as Chief Transformation Officer , I am confident his extensive 
experience across various industries and geographies will be a tremendous asset to the company and the 
management team ,” stated Tarak Patel, Managing Director of GMM Pfaudler.  
 
Commenting on his appointment, Mr. Gregory  Gelhaus  said, “I am  honoured  to assume this role 
and to continue  building on  the company's 60 -year legacy. I look forward to working closely with the 
leadership team to drive growth and create long -term value for  our stakeholders.”  
 
About GMM Pfaudler  Limited 
GMM Pfaudler Limited delivers corrosion -resistant technologies, systems, and services worldwide and 
remains the preferred choice by consistently providing its customers in the chemical and pharmaceutical 
industries with innovative and cost -effective solutions. GMM Pfaudler has 19 manufacturing locations  
with an extensive sales and service network and employs more than 2000  people across 4 continents.
