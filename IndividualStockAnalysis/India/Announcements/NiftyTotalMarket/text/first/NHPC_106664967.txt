In compliance to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, it is to inform that NHPC Limited has signed a Memorandum of Agreement (MoA) with Government of 
Arunachal Pradesh today i.e. 16.06.2026 for implementation of Etalin HEP (3097 MW). The brief of MoA is as under: 
 
i. Grant of permission by the State Government to NHPC to undertake preliminary investigation for 
preparation of the Pre-feasibility Report, detailed investigation for DPR preparation, financing and 
subsequent development, commissioning, implementation, operation and maintenance of Etalin HEP 
(3097 MW) being established on Dri & Tangon rivers in Dibang Basin of the State of Arunachal 
Pradesh. 
 
ii. The project shall be implemented by NHPC on Build, Own, Operate and Transfer (BOOT) basis for 
lease period of 40 (forty) years from the Commercial Operating Date (COD). 
 
iii. The project shall be developed as a run-of the river Scheme in tune with the State of Arunachal 
Pradesh Hydro Power Policy, 2008 and the amendments thereto.
