Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 [“SEBI (LODR) Regulations”], it is hereby informed that the Ministry of Railways vide its order 
no. 2021/E(O)II/40/21 dated 31st December, 2025 ( Annexure ) has entrusted the additional charge 
of Director (Projects), IRCON to Shri Sudhir Singh, IRSE, ED/CE(BS), Railway Board, in addition 
to his own, from the date of assumption of charge of the post on or after 1st January, 2026 and until 
further orders. 
 
The Company is taking necessary action to appoint Shri Sudhir Singh (DIN:11288339) in 
compliance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the SEBI (LODR) Regulations and 
requisite disclosures in this regard will be submitted upon his assumption of charge as Director 
(Projects).
