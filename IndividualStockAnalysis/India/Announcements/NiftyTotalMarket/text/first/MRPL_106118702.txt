The Board of Directors of Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited 
(MRPL), a subsidiary company of ONGC and Schedule "A" Mini Ratna 
Category I Company during its 267th Meeting held on January 20, 2025, 
approved it's limited reviewed Standalone and Consolidated financial 
results for the third quarter (Q3 FY 2024-25) and nine months (IXM FY 
2024-25) ended December 31, 2024. 
Major highlights for the third quarter (Q3 FY 2024-25): 
> Ever Highest Gross Crude & Net Crude processed for Q3 :-
> Gross crude processing of 4,541.5 TMT (Previous best 4,459.1 
TMT in Q3 FY 2022-23) 
> Net crude processing of 4,601.0 TMT (Previous best 4,479.7 TMT 
in Q3 FY 2022-23). 
> Merey-16 Crude from Venezuela (API-15.69) was processed for the 
first time in November 2024. 
> Ever Highest Production of following products during a quarter :-
> ATF for Q3 FY 2024-25 i.e. 763.1 TMT (Previous best was 747.5 
TMT in Q2 FY 2024-25). 
> Benzene for Q3 FY 2024-25 i.e. 60.6 TMT (Previous best was 49.7 
TMT). 
Financial highlights for the third quarter Q3 FY 2024-25 (Q3 FY 2023- 24): 
• Revenue from operations ₹ 25,601 Crore (Q3 FY 2023-24 ₹ 28,364 
Crore). 
• Profit Before Tax ₹ 469 Crore (Q3 FY 2023-24 Profit Before Tax ₹ 591 
Crore). 
• Profit After Tax of ₹ 304 Crore (Q3 FY 2023-24 Profit After Tax ₹ 387 
Crore).
