Summary of the proceedings of the  30th Annual General Meeting of members of the                                            
AU Small Finance Bank Limited (“the Bank”) held on Friday, August 8 , 202 5, at 0 4:00 P .M (IST)  
through Video Conferencing ("VC")/Other Audio -Visual Means ("OAVM")  
 
The  30th Annual General Meeting (“ AGM /Meeting ”) of the members of AU Small Finance Bank 
Limited (“the Bank ”) was held on Friday, August 8, 2025  through Video Conferencing (“VC”)/ 
Other Audio -Visual  Means (“OAVM”)  in compliance with the applicable circulars issued by the 
Ministry of Corporate Affairs  (“MCA”) and the Securities and Exchange Board of India (“ SEBI”) 
and as per the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 read with the rules made 
thereunder  which  commenced  at 4:00 P.M (IST) . As a testament to the Bank’s  unwavering 
commitment to sustainability and responsible leadership, the Bank host ed its second                       
Carbon Neutral Annual General Meeting  
 
The Bank had also provided one -way live webcast of the proceedings of the AGM  through 
National Securities Depositories Limited  (“NSDL”). 
 
The AGM was attended over Video Conferencing by the following  Directors & Key Managerial 
Personnel of the Bank:  
 
1. Mr. Harun Rasid Khan , Part Time Chairman and  Independent Director  
2. Mr. Sanjay Agarwal, Founder, Managing Director & CEO (MD & CEO)  
3. Mr. Uttam Tibrewal, Whole Time Director and Deputy CEO  
4. Prof. M S Sriram, Independent Director and Chairperson of Nomination and Remuneration 
Committee  
5. Mr. Pushpinder Singh, Independent Director  
6. Mr. V G Kannan, Independent Director  
7. Mr. Kamlesh Vikamsey, Independent Director and Chairperson  of Audit Committee  
8. Ms. Malini Thadani, Independent Director  
9. Ms. Kavita Venugopal, Independent Director and Chairperson of Stakeholders 
Relationship Committee  
10. Mr. J  M Prasad , Independent Director  (Additional)  
11. Mr. Divya Sehgal, Non Independent Director  and  Non Executive Director  
12. Mr. Vimal Jain, Chief Financial Officer   
13. Mr. Manmohan Parnami, Company Secretary   
 
As per the attendance records, the meeting was attended by 78 members.  
 
Mr. Manmohan Parnami, Company Secretary welcomed Members, Honorable Chairman , Board 
members , Auditors  and Scrutinizer  at the 30th AGM  of the Bank . 
 
Thereafter,  he briefed the members on the process of remote e -voting  and informed that remote 
e-voting for AGM commenced on Monday , August 4 , 202 5 (9:00 AM IST) and ended on Thursday 
August 7 , 202 5 (5:00 PM IST). The members who have not casted their vote earlier through remote  
e-voting were given an opportunity to cast their vote during the AGM through e -voting facility.  
 
He informed the members that CS Manoj Maheshwari, Practicing Company Secretary  
(Membership FCS - 3355)  has been appointed as  a scrutinizer  by the Board of Directors of the 
Bank for scrutinizing remote e -voting process and e -voting during the AGM in a fair and 
transparent manner.  
 
He then  informed that the statutory registers , certificates,  and other documents as required 
under various laws  were made available for inspection by the members.  
 
Company Secretary  further apprised the members that the notice convening the 30th AGM of the 
Bank along with the Board’s Report & annexures thereto and Auditors’ report thereon w ere 
circulated to all the members and with the permission of members, the same was taken as read. 
He also informed the members that Statutory Auditor’s Report and Secretarial Auditor’s Report 
do not contain any qualification or adverse remarks.  
 
Mr. Harun Rasid Khan , Part Time Chairman and  Independent Director of the Bank, Chaired the 
AGM.  The requisite quorum  being present, the Chairman called the meeting to order . He 
welcomed and extended warm greetings to all the Members, Board of Directors, Key Managerial 
Personnel, Auditors,  and other Invitees who had joined the AGM.  
 
Chairman apprised the members that Mr. Nandkumar Saravade, Independent Director 
(Additional) has expressed his inability to attend the meeting due to other preoccupation.  
 
Chairman  also  informed that the representatives from  M/s. M S K A & Associates and                                      
M/s. Mukund M Chitale & Co.,  Joint Statutory Auditors of the Bank and M/s V.M. & Associates, 
Secretarial Auditor , and Scrutinizer of the Bank has also joined the meeting . 
 
Chairman with immense pleasure and pride informed the shareholders that on August 7, 2025, 
the Bank  has  received an in-principle approval from the RBI to transition from a Small Finance 
Bank to a Universal Bank , a historic milestone marking a new chapter in AU’s journey. 
Acknowledging the trust and support of regulators, shareholders, depositors, and business 
partners, he highlighted  that Board  continues  to rema in committed to strong governance and 
robust  risk management. He assured the shareholders  that the Bank is fully prepared to expand 
its reach and deliver best -in-class financial services across both digital and physical platforms.  
 
Chairman while addressing the shareholders, shar ed valuable insights on several key aspects of 
the Bank’s performance and strategic direction. He highlighted the Bank’s strong financial 
performance , robust growth trajectory , and emphasized on the role of technology in enhancing 
operational excellence,  expanding digital  avenues  for financial inclusion,  Bank’s strong 
governance for sustainable growth, leadership development and employee empowerment.  
 
The address concluded with an optimistic outlook, expressing confidence in the Bank’s 
continued progress and future readiness.  in his address to shareholders, h e affirmed to the 
shareholders  that Bank shall continue to have strong oversight with deeper insights and sharper 
foresight . 
 
Thereafter, Mr. Sanjay Agarwal, MD & CEO welcomed and  thanked all the Members, Directors, 
Auditors and Scrutinizer present at the meeting. He commenced  his address by acknowledging 
that the receipt of the RBI’s  in-principle approval for Bank’s transition into a Universal Bank  is a 
testament to t he deep trust and confidence of stakeholders and years of patient capital, 
purposeful investment and disciplined growth.   
 
He reflect ed upon the  Bank’s  remarkable journey as it completed 30 years of building a strong 
franchise and 8 years as a Bank. He acknowledged the Bank’s stable performance, scalable 
growth, and sustainable impact during FY25, despite facing economic challenges . He 
emphasized the role of technology as a key enabler of growth and highlighted Bank’s  deep -rooted 
connect with Bharat, along with its purpose -driven people -first approach. He also introduced the 
Bank’s eighth Dharma – 'Collaborate'. At the end  of his address, he extended his sincere  gratitude 
to all stakeholders who have been part of the journey , from the founding team to regulators, 
investors, Board of Directors and employees  for their continuous support and trust.

He informed that the Voting results (remote e -voting and e -voting at the AGM) along with the

Thereafter , the Members who have registered themselves as speaker shareholder were  provided 
opportunity to ask questions or express their views . Speaker Shareholders then expressed their 
views / asked questions  on various issues relating to future business plans, financial 
performance, key financial ratios, customer service, current banking products, upcoming digital 
products, etc. MD & CEO , Chairman and Chief Financial Officer  of the Bank replied to their 
queries to their satisfaction .  
 
Thereafter, Mr. Uttam Tibrewal, Whole -Time Director & Deputy CEO  conveyed  a vote  of thanks  to 
the shareholders . He expressed his gratitude to the Chairman and Board for their support and 
guidance, MD & CEO for his dedication and passion in building a sustainable institution  and all 
stakeholders for their  unwavering faith and partnership  that led to the landmark achievement  of 
receiving the RBI’s in-principle for the Bank’s transition to a  Universal Bank . He highlighted the 
Bank’s strengthened foundations , its focus on  inclusive growth, innovation and sustainability 
and readiness to embrace the future.  Concluding his address,  he extended a sincere vote of 
thanks  to the Government, RBI, regulatory bodies, shareholders, customers, employees, and 
partners for their continued trust and belief.  
 
After the discussion on all the agenda items completed successfully, the Chairman thanked the 
members, directors and others present at the AGM  and e-voting period  for 15 minutes  was given 
to sharehold ers. After the end  of said e-voting period, the meeting concluded at 6:10 p.m. (IST) .
