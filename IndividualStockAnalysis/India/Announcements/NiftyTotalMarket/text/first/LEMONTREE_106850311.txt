LEMON TREE HOTELS SIGNS  
KEYS SELECT BY LEMON TREE HOTELS, MATHURA  
 
Making its debut in Mathura, the Group further strengthens its presence in Uttar 
Pradesh, with eight operational hotels and 19 upcoming properties in the stat e 
 
 
New Delhi, 6th November, 2026 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, one of India’s leading 
hospitality companies, has announced the signing of Keys Select by Lemon Tree 
Hotels, Mathura . The property will be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, 
a wholly owned subsidiary of Lemon Tree Hotels Limited.  
 
Mathura, one of India's most significant religious and cultural destinations, attracts 
visitors throughout the year for its spiritual heritage and historical significance. Its 
location within the popular Agra –Mathura –Vrindavan tourism belt makes it an 
important market for hospitality development, catering to both leisure  and business 
needs . 
 
The upcoming hotel will feature 65 well -appointed rooms, offering a comfortable and 
welcoming setting for visitors to the destination . Its facilities will include a restaurant,  
banquet hall and meeting/ conference facilities, swimming pool, spa , fitness centre  
and other public areas . 
 
Commenting on the signing, Mr. Vishvapreet Singh Cheema, President, Lemon 
Tree Hotels Ltd.,  said, “Uttar Pradesh is an important market for us, with strong 
demand across religious tourism, leisure and business travel. The Agra –Mathura –
Vrindavan trail, in particular, attracts visitors throughout the year. With an 
operational Lemon Tree Hotel in Agra and Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotels , 
Vrindavan  in the pipeline , the Mathura signing further builds our presence across this 
important pilgrimage and leisure destination . Together, these properties will enhance  
our ability to serve travellers to the region, offering comfortable stays and warm, 
consistent hospitality that makes Lemon Tree Hotels a preferred choice. ” 
 
The hotel will offer convenient connectivity to key transport hubs, with Mathura 
Railway Station approximately 5 km away. Noida International Airport, Jewar, is 
approximately 95 km from the property.
