Jyothy Labs Limited Is Now Great Place To Work Certiﬁed  
 
Mumbai, February 14, 2025 : Jyothy Labs Limited (Jyothy Labs), one of the leading Indian FMCG 
Companies today proudly announces its Great Place To Work CertiﬁcationTM for the period from 
February 2025 to February 2026.  
 
The highly sought-after certiﬁcation, renowned worldwide, serves as a testament to Jyothy Labs’ 
commitment to fostering an exceptional workplace environment where every voice is heard, 
every contribution is valued, and every individual has the opportunity to grow.  
 
Ms. M. R. Jyothy, Chairperson and Managing Director, Jyothy Labs Limited, expressed her 
gratitude, stating, “This achievement is not just a badge of honour; it reﬂects the dedication, 
collaboration, and unwavering commitment that each of our people brings to the workplace 
every day. Our journey to building a thriving, inclusive, and empowering work culture has 
been shaped by passion of our people, teamwork, and shared values and is a testament to 
the incredible culture we have built together, one that values trust, collaboration, and 
excellence .” 
 
Achieving the Great Place To Work CertiﬁcationTM is a signiﬁcant milestone for Jyothy Labs, which 
empowers its employees and fosters inspiration, innovation, growth, and shared success.  
 
About Jyothy Labs Limited:  Founded in 1983, Jyothy Labs Limited (JLL) has grown from a 
singlebrand company with its ﬂagship brand Ujala to a multi-brand FMCG listed on both BSE and 
NSE. Today, Jyothy Labs is a household name across India, producing and marketing a diverse 
range of products in Fabric Care, Dishwashing, Household Insecticides, and Personal Care 
Segments. The company’s portfolio includes popular brands such as Ujala, Exo, Henko, Pril, 
Margo, Maxo, Mr. White, Morelight, Neem and Maya, each with a strong presence in its respective 
category and recognized for quality and consumer trust.
