TVS STRENGTHENS ITS MODERN-RETRO LINE 
WITH THE LAUNCH OF THE LATEST RONIN 
SERIES, INSPIRED BY AGONDA BEACH  
 
• Born from custom motorcycle culture, the TVS Ronin Agonda sets itself apart from 
previous Ronin variants.  
• In Indonesia, it is priced at IDR 37,0 80,000 (on- the-road price, Greater Jakarta) . 
 
Singapore/Jakarta, October  29, 2026  – TVS Motor Company – a global leader in two and 
three -wheeler manufacturing, has officially introduced the TVS Ronin Agonda, the newest 
series in the TVS Ronin lineup, inspired by the custom -modification style of Agonda Beach. 
The launch was held at Jimbaran Resto, Ancol, Jakarta, and was attended by media, dealers, 
and key opinion leaders. Carrying the spirit of “Where Adventure Meets the Ocean Soul,”  
the Ronin Agonda is a true interpretation of a modern -retro motorcycle that combines the 
toughness of adventure riding with coastal custom aesthetics, making it an exclusive choice 
for riders in Indonesia.  
The name Agonda comes from Agonda Beach in Goa, India, which is known for its white sand, 
warm sunshine, and calm, laid- back atmosphere. This atmosphere is reflected in the 
motorcycle’s color scheme. The fuel tank is white with thin red and blue pinstripes, creating 
the triple -tone harmony that is the hallmark of the Ronin Agonda.  
The motorcycle was first unveiled at Moto Soul 2025, TVS’s annual event held at Vagator 
Beach, Goa, India. Born from custom motorcycle culture, the TVS Ronin Agonda sets itself 
apart from previous Ronin variants. In Indonesia, it is priced at IDR 37,0 80,000 (on -the-road 
price, Greater Jakarta) . 
The TVS Ronin Agonda belongs to the Base Variant and is equipped with four features that support riding safety and comfort:  
• Single Channel ABS (front wheel): helps maintain stability during braking.  
• Rain Mode and Urban Mode: Rain Mode is selected in wet weather, while Urban 
Mode is for daily riding on dry roads.  
• Glide Through Technology (GTT): makes riding in traffic jams easier, as the 
motorcycle can move slowly or crawl in 1st, 2nd, and 3rd gear without the rider needing to pull the clutch lever or twist the throttle, which reduces rider fatigue.  
• Slipper Clutch: makes gear changes, especially downshifts, feel smoother and less 
jerky.  
In Indonesia, the TVS Ronin is available in two variants:  
• Top Variant: available in Nimbus Grey and Midnight Blue.  
• Base Variant: available in Lightning Black and Agonda.  
Both variants come with Glide Through Technology, a Slipper Clutch, and a choice of Rain 
and Urban riding modes. The difference lies in the additional equipment on the Top Variant, 
which includes:  
 
 
   
• Visor  
• USB charger  
• Dual Channel ABS  
• Adjustable clutch and brake levers  
• Digital instrument panel with SmartXonnect, which provides Voice Assist, turn -by-turn 
navigation, and incoming call notifications  
Anthony Ho, Regional Business Head of TVS Motor Company for Indonesia and Singapore, 
stated that the arrival of this model is a strategic step for the company. “The arrival of the TVS 
Ronin Agonda in Indonesia is a strategic step for us to expand the TVS portfolio. Over the 
past three years, the TVS Ronin has been well received by Indonesian consumers, as shown 
by the many awards it has won from 2024 to the present. We hope that the TVS Ronin Agonda  
will meet the enthusiasm of modern- retro motorcycle fans, as well as adventure and 
modification enthusiasts in Indonesia,” he said.  
Meanwhile, Nahason Jurun, Head of Marketing at PT TVS Motor Company Indonesia, 
explained the reason behind the launch theme. “The arrival of the latest TVS Ronin Agonda 
series reflects our commitment to strengthening the modern -retro lineup in Indonesia. Its 
distinctive look is inspired by the coastal custom style of Agonda Beach, blending the free 
spirit of adventure lovers with a relaxed lifestyle. This is why we chose the theme ‘Where 
Adventure Meets the Ocean Soul.’ We have prepared this 225 cc motorcycle as an exclusive 
choice for consumers who love touring and freedom of self -expression,” he said.
