Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
This is to inform you that the Board of Directors in their meeting held on 20th January, 2024 
had approved expansion of 6 MnTPA comprising – (1) brown field Expansion in clinker 
capacity by 3.3 MnTPA (Design - 10,000 TPD, Potential – 12,000 TPD) & cement grinding 
capacity by 3 MnTPA at Panna, Hamirpur and Prayagraj (1 MNTPA each) and (2) 3 MnTPA 
greenfield Cement grinding capacity in Bihar, to be commissioned in phases in 24 months from 
the date of start of work at site.  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we are pleased to inform you that today we have commissioned 1.00 
MnTPA cement grinding facility at JK Cement Works, Prayagraj, Unit: JK Cement Ltd. 
 
With this the installed capacity of JK Cement Works, Prayagraj stands enhanced from 2.00 
MnTPA to 3.00 MnTPA  and the Company’s overall grey cement production capacity stands 
at 26.26 MnTPA (including capacity of its subsidiary company) 
 
We request you to take the same on your records and oblige.
