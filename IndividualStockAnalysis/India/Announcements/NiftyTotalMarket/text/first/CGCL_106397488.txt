Dear  Sir/Madam,
In compliance with  Regulation 30 and Regulation 51 read with  Schedule III of SEBI (Listing  Obligations  and 
Disclosure Requirements)  Regulations,  2015  (“SEBI Listing  Regulations”),  the Board at its meeting  held today,  
October  04, 2025,  has considered  and approved  the appointment  of Mr. Monu Ratra  as the Chief  Executive 
Officer  (CEO)  of the Company,  designated  as a Key Managerial  Personnel  (KMP)  and forming  part of the Senior  
Management,  with  effect  from October  07, 2025.
The appointment  has been  made with  a focus  on driving  the Company’s  medium-term growth plans  and 
strengthening  its leadership bandwidth  to support  the Company’s  growth journey.  Mr. Monu  Ratra,  CEO shall  
report  to Mr.  Rajesh Sharma,  the Managing  Director  and Promoter  of the Company.
The details  of the aforementioned  appointment  are set out in Annexure A.
