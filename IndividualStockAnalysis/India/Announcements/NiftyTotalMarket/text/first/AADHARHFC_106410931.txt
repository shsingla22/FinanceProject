Special Window for Re-lodgment of Transfer Requests of 
Physical Shares
Members may note that SEBI had discontinued the transfer 
of securities in physical mode with effect from April 01, 2019. 
Subsequently, SEBI clarified that transfer deeds lodged prior to 
deadline of April 01, 2019 and rejected/returned due to deficiency 
in the documents may be re-lodged with requisite documents till 
March 31, 2021 as the cut-off date for re-lodgement of transfer deeds.
SEBI has further decided to open a special window only for 
re-lodgment of transfer deeds, which were lodged prior to the 
deadline of April 01, 2019 and rejected/returned/not attended due 
to deficiency in the documents/process/or otherwise, for a period 
of six months from July 07, 2025 till January 06, 2026  in order to 
facilitate ease of investing for investors and to secure the rights of 
investors in the securities which were purchased by them.
Concerned Shareholders may contact Company’s RTA for the 
relodgement of old transfer deeds which were rejected prior to 
April 1, 2019. However, it may be noted that, the securities that will 
be re-lodged for transfer shall be issued only in demat mode.
