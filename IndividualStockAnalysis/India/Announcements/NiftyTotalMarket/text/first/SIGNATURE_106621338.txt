Dear Sir/Madam, 
We would like you to take note of our Key Financial updates for Q4FY26 and FY26: 
a} Revenue from operation: Revenue recognition increased by 113% to INR 11.1 bn in Q4FY26 from INR 5.2 bn in Q4FY25. Further, 
revenue of the Company stood at INR 26.0 bn during FY26 in comparison to INR 25.0 bn during FY25. 
b) Profit after tax (PAT): The Company’s PAT has surged by 1,785% to INR 11.5 bn in Q4FY26 from PAT of INR 0.61 bn in Q4FY25 on 
account of profit earned on strategic joint venture partnership with Millennia Realtors Private Limited of RMZ Group in Gurugram 
Commercity Limited (a wholly owned subsidiary) of the Company. Accordingly, PAT of the Company shot up by 979% to INR 10.9 bn 
in FY26 from PAT of INR 1.01 bn in FY25. 
¢) Adjusted gross profit margin: The Company has reported an adjusted gross profit margin of 30% in FY26 and 28% in Q4FY26. During 
the same period previous year, the company achieved 31% in FY25 and 45% in Q4FY25. 
d) Adjusted EBITDA margin: The Company has reported an adjusted EBITDA margin of 9% in FY26 and 16% in Q4FY26. During the same 
period in previous year the company reported an adjusted EBITDA margin of 14% in FY25 and 25% in Q4FY25. 
Key Operational updates for O4FY26 and FY26: 
a) Pre-Sales: The Company has achieved pre-sales of INR 82.5 bn in FY26 and INR 15.7 bn for Q4FY26 in comparison to INR 102.9 bn in FY25 and 
INR 16.2 bn for Q4FY25. 
b) Sales realization: Average Sales realization stood at INR 15,250 per sq. ft. in FY26 versus INR 12,457 per sq. ft. in FY25. Higher 
realization were driven by increased sales in premium markets and sales price increase across key regions. 
c) Collections: Collections were INR 40.1 bn in FY26 and INR 9.2 bn for Q4FY26 in comparison to INR 43.8 bn in FY25 and INR 11.7 bn for 
Q4FY25. In addition to annual collections, company also received INR 12.37 bn out of the total consideration of INR 12.93 Bn, for the sale 
of 50% stake in a subsidiary company, from the RMZ group. This transaction also marks entry of the company in large scale commercial 
development in the NCR region. 
d) Net Debt: Our net debt stood at a historic low of INR 2.0 bn at the end of FY26 in comparison to INR 8.8 bn at the end of FY25. The 
company has INR 27.70 bn of cash and cash equivalents as of 31‘ Mar 2026, which enables a very strong balance sheet position to 
strategize our foreseeable future.
