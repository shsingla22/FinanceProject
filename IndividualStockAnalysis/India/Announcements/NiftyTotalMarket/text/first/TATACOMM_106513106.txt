PRESS RELEASE  
Tata Communications Launches AI-Ready 
Suite to Empower Enterprises  
 
Tata  Communications IZO+ ™ Multi Cloud Network, Tata Communications Edge 
Distribution Platform and ThreadSpan ™ are designed to connect , protect and 
provide control  across enterprise digital environments  
 
Mumbai, INDIA, January 30, 2026  
 
Tata Communications , the leading global communications technology player, today  unveiled  an 
AI-ready suite  of platforms and solutions designed to help organisations scale the adoption of 
artificial intelligence ( AI) with confidence, control and clarity.  
 
The suite  comprises  three independent , complementary  offerings  — Tata Communications 
IZO+TM Multi Cloud Network , Tata Communications Edge Distribution Platform, and  
ThreadSpan TM — built on the company’s  digital fabric  of solutions, that brings together  
network, cloud , cybersecurity  and other offerings . The offering s aim to remove silos, simplify 
operations and embed trust across the digital continuum.  
  
As AI moves from pilot projects to the core of business decision -making, enterprises are 
discovering a hard truth: yesterday’s infrastructure was never built for today’s AI ambitions.  
 
AI is rapidly  mov ing from experimentation to the mainstream , placing unprecedented pressure  
on digital foundations . But as AI workloads spread across clouds, edges and regions, 
organisations are finding themselves grapp ling with spiralling costs, unpredictable performance, 
security gaps and limited visibility.  
 
Point solutions are proving insufficient. What enterprises need today  is an infrastructure that is 
designed — end to end — for AI: flexible enough to scale, smart enough to optimise itself, and 
secure enough to be trusted with mission -critical data.   
 
Tata Communications ’ AI-ready  suite  directly addresses these challenges  — not in isolation, but 
as a cohesive platform . Together, they form an architectural foundation that will help  
enterprises navigate the complexity, cost and risk that come with deploying A I at scale.  
 
Commenting on the release, A.S. Lakshminarayanan, MD & CEO, Tata Communications , said: 
“Digital infrastructure is becoming increasingly complex, and AI is amplifying that challenge. 
With our new suite of AI -ready offerings and our Digital Fabric, we are bringing together a 
secure, unified and intelligent foundation that simplifies how enterpr ises design and run their 
 
digital environments. This enables our customers to reduce complexity, operate with confidence 
and focus their energy on innovation and scale AI securely.”  
 
These principles are embedded across Tata Communications’ AI -ready suite of platforms  that 
redefine how enterprises build and run distributed AI environments.  
 
Tata Communications IZO+TM Multi Cloud Network  
In the age of distributed AI, connectivity is no longer a utility —it is a strategic advantage. As 
enterprises spread workloads across clouds, complexity and cost quickly become barriers to 
scale . Tata Communications  IZO+TM Multi Cloud Network removes that friction, giving 
organisations control over how data moves, performs and costs. Intelligent policy, built -in 
optimisation and a single view of performance make scaling AI simpler, faster and more 
predictable . 
 
Tata Communications Edge Distribution Platform  
Tata Communications’ Edge Distribution Platform brings intelligence closer to where data is 
created, enabling faster responses and real -time digital experiences. By combining content 
delivery, security and compute at the edge, the platform reduces latency while ensuring 
enterprise applications remain protected and resilient . The integrated approach goes beyond 
faster delivery  — it creates the foundation required to run real -time AI applications at global 
scale. The need is for platforms that deliver millisecond -level performance, protect AI models 
and APIs, and scale efficiently across regions.  
 
ThreadSpan TM 
ThreadSpan ™ brings clarity and control to complex digital environments by providing  a unified, 
single -pane view across hybrid and multi -vendor networks . It combines  visibility, manageability, 
security , and automation  across network, cloud and security domains. Powered by AI -driven 
orchestration , it enables enterprises to enforce consistent poli cies, intelligent resource 
coordination and early identification of potential issues  — shifting operations  from reactive 
firefighting  to proactive autonomy, improving security audit  readiness  and efficiency  at scale.  
 
By bringing connectivity, edge intelligence and predictive observability into a unified AI -ready 
ecosystem, Tata Communications is helping enterprises unlock real business value from AI, with 
infrastructure that is simpler to manage, faster to deploy and built for the demands of 
tomorrow.
