Sub.: 100 Days Campaign – “Saksham Niveshak” – Updating your KYC
Dear Shareholder,
The Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (“IEPFA”), Ministry of Corporate Affairs (“MCA”) has launched a 
100 Days Campaign - “Saksham Niveshak”, to reach out to shareholders whose dividend remain unpaid/unclaimed. 
The purpose of the campaign is to create awareness and encourge shareholders to update their KYC details with the 
Company to prevent the transfer of such dividends to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (“IEPF”).
The Company fully supports this initiative. It has through various communications including social media (linkedin, X 
and Instagram) reached out to its shareholders urging them to update their KYC details. These communications are 
available on the website of the Company at www.ultratechcement.com as well as on the website of Stock Exchanges 
where the shares of the Company are listed i.e. on BSE Limited at www.bseindia.com and the National Stock 
Exchange of India Limited at www.nseindia.com.
Further Securities and Exchange Board of India, vide its various circulars mandated that those shareholders (holding 
shares in physical form), whose folio(s) are not updated with the KYC details (any of the details viz., PAN, contact 
details, mobile number, bank account details and specimen signature, if any) shall be eligible for any payment 
including dividend only through electronic mode with effect from 1st April, 2024.
As per our records, the following dividend(s) declared by the Company remain unclaim ed by you. The said dividend(s) 
shall be released immediately upon completion of the requisite KYC formalities.
Further, all the shares in respect of which the dividend remains unpaid or unclaimed for seven consecutive years or
more are liable to be transferred to IEPF. After such transfer, the dividend can no longer be claimed from theCompany and must be claimed directly from the IEPFA.
In view of the above, you are requested to submit duly filled and signed KYC documents as given below:
• ISR-1 duly filled in along with self-attested supporting documents for updation of KYC details,
• ISR-2 duly filled in with banker attestation of signature along with original cancelled cheque with your name(s)
printed thereon or self-attested copy of bank passbook/statement,
• ISR-3 If you wish to opt out of nomination and
• SH-13 Form for updation of nomination for the aforesaid folio.
Shareholders can download the forms from the website of the Company at www.ultratechcement.com or the websiteof KFin Technologies Limited at https://ris.kfintech.com/clientservices/isc/isrforms.aspx
