TVS MOTOR COMPANY LAUNCHES WORLD’S  FAVOURITE RACING 
MACHINE  TVS APACHE IN KENYA 
 
~ Expands its premium performance motorcycle range in East Africa  
~ Leading the way for urban commuting in the region with premium products  
 
Nairobi, November 17, 2025:  TVS Motor Company, a global leader in two and three -
wheeler manufacturing, today launched the TVS Apache RTR 180 in Kenya. The 
company has  associated with its long -standing distribution partner in Africa -  Car & 
General for this venture. The introduction of TVS Apache in Africa marks a major 
milestone, bringing a legacy of racing excellence, advanced technologies  and two 
decades of global credibility to Kenyan biking culture. The TVS Apache nameplate is 
the touchstone of TVSM ’s “Track to Road” philosophy that guarantees urban 
enthusiasts experience race- bred innovation, setting new benchmarks for technology 
and performance.  
 With over 6.5 million riders in over 60 countries  TVS Apache has evolved into a global 
phenomenon. Its arrival in Kenya is a major moment in the evolution of Kenyan biking 
culture and an opening of doors to a new generation of enthusiasts to experience performance, style, and innovation.  
 The TVS Apache RTR 180 represents the prefect blend of power, precision and performance, forged from 43 years of TVS racing heritage. The product is equipped 
with 177.4cc high-performance engine, delivering ultimate performance of 15.22 PS  
at 8,500 RPM and 14.1 Nm of torque at 6,500 RPM. It features disc brakes, and adjustable race- tuned suspension,  ensuring an exhilarating ride experience. The 
motorcycle features beast-inspired LED headlamp, tubeless tyres  with wider rear tyre , 
5-speed gear box and much more, making it a true benchmark in its category.  
 Close on the heels of the EICMA debut where TVSM wowed visitors with its ambition, versatility and scale, the introduction of Apache in Kenya is a statement of intent to expand its global footprint by entering new markets . It demonstrates the company’s 
focus  to go deeper in existing geographies with the larger aim of deliver ing world -
class, race -proven engineering in every market, fuelling aspirations and transforming 
lives.  
 Commenting on the launch, Mr. Peyman Kargar, President –  International 
Business, TVS Motor Company, said, “ Kenya is central to TVSM’s continental 
ambitions, given its role as a regional commercial hub and its influence on motorcycle 
culture across East Africa. The nation’s growing community of discerning riders and 
evolving urban infrastructure make it the ideal market for the Apache’s debut. This 
move further strengthens TVSM’s commitment to serve Kenyan consumers with 
products that enhance their mobility aspirations and complement their lifestyles.  With 
this launch of new premium motorcycle –  TVS Apache RTR 180,  it enhances our 
commitment to offering performance- led, premium mobility solutions across Africa. 
The product has been designed with a combination of racing- inspired power and
