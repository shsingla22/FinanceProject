We refer to the outcome of Board Meeting dated 2nd February, 2024, wherein it was informed that  the 
Board of Directors had approved disposal of its investments in its non -material subsidiary, Century Ply 
(Singapore) Pte Ltd. (CSPL) due to shut -down of its operation and that of its step-down subsidiaries in 
Laos since more than five years.  
 
In furtherance thereof, this is to inform you that the Company has completed disposal and transfer of its 
entire stake in CSPL today, i.e., 23rd April, 2024. Consequently, CSPL remains a subsidiary of the 
Company till 22nd April, 2024. Further, Century Ply Laos Co. Ltd. and Century Huesoulin Plywood Lao 
Co., Ltd., both subsidiaries of CSPL, also ceases to be step -down subsidiaries of the Company from the 
said date.

a)  the gross total turnover of CSPL  for the year ended 31st March, 2023 was Rs. Nil which constitutes   
Nil % of the consolidated turnover of the Company.  
 
b)  the net  worth of CSPL  as on 31st March, 2023 was Rs. 234.04 lakh which constitutes only 0.12% of 
the consolidated net worth of the Company. 
 
c)  the company has dispose d of its entire shareholding at total consideration of USD  0.92 million to the

promoter group/group companies.
