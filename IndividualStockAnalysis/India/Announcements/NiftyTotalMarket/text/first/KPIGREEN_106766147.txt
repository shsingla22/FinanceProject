Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015  (“SEBI Listing Regulations”)  read with SEBI Master Circular No. 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025 -CFD -POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026  (“SEBI Master Circular”) , we wish 
to inform that  M/s. K A Sanghavi & Co LLP , Chartered Accountant s, (ICAI Firm Registration No. 
0120846W/W100289), have resigned as the Statutory Auditors of Sun Drops Energia Limited  
(“SDEL”) , a material subsidiary of KPI Green Energy Limited (“ KPI Green ”), effective  September 0 2, 
2026, vide its resignation letter of the even date . 
 
There are no concerns raised by the resigning auditor with respect to the management of SDEL which 
may have hampered the audit process. As stated in the resignation letter, the resignation is intended to 
facilitate alignment of the Statutory Auditors of SDEL with the statutory auditors of its Holding 
Company i.e. KPI Green . Accordingly, there was no matter warranting deliberation by the Audit 
Committee as per SEBI master Circular and, consequently, separate disclosure of the Audit 
Committee’s views is not applicable.  
 
A copy of the resignation letter along with the annexure containing the required information from the 
Statutory Auditor in the format as specified in the SEBI Master Circular, as received from M/s. K A 
Sanghavi & Co LLP is attached herewith as Annexure – II. 
 
Pursuant to Section 139 and other applicable provisions, if any, of the Companies Act, 2013  and  based 
on the Audit Committee’s recommendation and subject to members’ approval, the Board of SDEL 
approved appointment of M/s. MSKC & Associates LLP, Chartered Accountants , a member firm of BDO 
International  (Firm Registration No.: 001595S/S000168), as the Statutory Auditors of the SDEL , to fill 
the casual  vacancy arising due to resignation of previous Statutory Auditor i.e. M/s. K A Sanghavi & Co 
LLP , Chartered Accountants . This appointment will be subsequently approved by the shareholders of 
SDEL at its ensuing Annual General Meeting  (AGM) . 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Further, based on the recommendation of Audit Committee, the Board of SDEL has recommended  the 
appointment of M/s . MSKC & Associates LLP, Chartered Accountants , a member firm of BDO 
International  (Firm Registration No.: 001595S/S000168) , as Statutory Auditors , to audit the books of 
accounts of SDEL , for a term of five (5) consecutive years who  shall hold office from the conclusion of 
07th AGM of SDEL  till the conclusion of 12th AGM  to be held in the year 2031 , subject to approval of 
Members of SDEL  at the ensuing AGM.  
 
The details as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master 
Circular dated January 30, 2026, are enclosed herewith as Annexure I. 
 
The aforesaid information was received from SDEL  today at 03:50 P.M.  
 
We request you to take the same on your record.

for re-appointment. Accordingly, in view of the aforesaid alignment, we hereby tender our resignation 
as the Statutory Auditors of the Company with immediate effect. 
There are no other reasons for our resignation other than those stated above. 
We have completed our Statutory Audit till March 31, 2026 and we issued our Audit Report on May 05, 
2026.
