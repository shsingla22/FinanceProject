Remote e-Voting.  
Pursuant to Section 108 of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 20 of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 
2014, as amended, and Regulation 44 of the SEBI LODR, as amended, the Company is providing its Members with the facility to 
exercise their right to vote on the resolutions proposed to be considered at the AGM through remote e-voting. The details of the remote 
e-voting facility are as follows:  
 
Particulars  Details 
Cut-off Date for determining eligibility for e-Voting  Friday, 18th September 2026  
Commencement of Remote e-Voting  Tuesday, 22nd September 2026 at 09:00 a.m. (IST)  
End of Remote e-Voting  Thursday, 24th September 2026 at 05:00 p.m. (IST)  
 
Members may exercise their vote through remote e-voting by following the procedure provided in the Notice of the AGM.  
 
Members who have cast their votes through remote e-voting may attend the AGM but shall not be entitled to cast their vote again at 
the AGM. Members attending the AGM who have not voted through remote e-voting and are otherwise eligible to vote will be provided 
the facility to cast their votes during the AGM.
