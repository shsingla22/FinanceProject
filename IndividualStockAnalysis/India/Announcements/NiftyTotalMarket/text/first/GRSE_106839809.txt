PRESS RELEASE 
From Guided- Missile to Next Generation Corvettes: GRSE Advances Its Warship -
Building Legacy  
N
ew Delhi/Kolkata, 02 November  2026: Marking a significant milestone in India's 
indigenous warship- building programme, Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers Ltd. (GRSE), 
a Navratna Defence Public Sector Undertaking under the Ministry of Defence, Government of 
India, signed a contract with the Indian Navy in New Delhi today  for the construction of five Next 
Generation Corvettes (NGCs).  
The contract, valued at approximately ₹32,000 cror e, was signed by Shri Dinesh Kumar,  
IAS, JS & AM (MS) , Ministry of Defence & Cdr Shantanu Bose, IN (Retd), Director (Shipbuilding), 
GRSE , in the presence of Shri Rajesh Kumar Singh, IAS, Defence Secretary  of India , Cmde PR 
Hari, IN (Retd), Chairman & Managing Director, GRSE, and other senior officials from Ministry 
of Defence, Indian Navy & GRSE . 
G
RSE had emerged as the lowest bidder (L1) for the prestigious project. The signing of 
the contract marks a major step forward in strengthening India's indigenous warship- building 
capabilities and advancing the vision of Aatmanirbhar Bharat in defence manufacturing.  
B
uilding on its extensive experience in the construction of guided- missile and anti -
submarine warfare corvettes, GRSE will now undertake the construction of these next -
generation warships, which will feature enhanced capabilities, advanced sensors and sophisticated weapons systems. The project further reinforces the shipyard's position as a key 
contributor to India's maritime defence preparedness and indigenous warship design and 
construction.

The corvettes will feature an advanced Combined Diesel and Gas Turbine –Cross -
Connect (CODAG -CC) propulsion arrangement, driving two Controllable Pitch Propellers (CPP). 
This propulsion architecture is designed to support the operational requirements of thes e 
versatile frontline combat platforms.  Designed for a wide spectrum of maritime missions, the 
NGCs will undertake multi -role operations, including offensive ship -to-ship and ship- to-surface 
missile attacks, anti -missile defence, anti -submarine warfare, es cort duties, maritime interdiction 
operations (MIO), including Visit, Board, Search and Seizure (VBSS), and local naval defence.  
 
To support these missions, the warships will be equipped with an advanced suite of 
weapons, sensors and navigation systems. Th e proposed systems include Medium Range Anti -
Ship Missiles, Medium Range Surface- to-Air Missiles (MRSAM) and 324 mm torpedo tubes. The 
armament suite will also include an OTO Melara 76 mm Super Rapid Gun Mount (SRGM) with the latest upgrade, an AK -630 Clos e-In Weapon System (CIWS) and 12.7 mm Stabilised 
Remote- Controlled Guns (SRCGs) integrated with an Electro- Optic -based Fire Control System 
(EOFCS).  
 
The sensor suite will include an Electro- Optical Infrared Search and Track (EOIRST) 
system and a Multi -Function Surveillance, Track and Guidance Radar (MFSTAR). The corvettes 
will also have helicopter -operating capabilities, enhancing their operational flexibility across a 
range of maritime scenarios.  
 
The shipyard is currently building nine warships,  including an advanced guided missile 
frigate under the Navy's Project 17A, four ASW -SWCs and four Next Generation Offshore Patrol 
Vessels. Apart from these, GRSE is executing orders for  36 other  platforms, including 13 vessels 
for export. Over the past si x decades, GRSE has established itself as one of India's leading 
warship builders, with a distinguished track record of delivering 118 warships to the Indian Navy, 
Indian Coast Guard and friendly foreign nations.  
 
 
About GRSE:  
The journey of Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers Ltd., a Navratna Public Sector Undertaking, under the 
administrative control of Ministry of Defence, dates back to 1884 when it started its journey as a small workshop to 
repair vessels of River Steam Navigation Company. The Company was taken over by the Government of India in 1960. GRSE has the distinction of becoming the first shipyard of independent India to build a warship for Indian 
Navy, the Seaward Defence Boat (SDB) INS Ajay, way back in 1961. GRS E also built the first ever Indian Export 
Warship “CGS Barracuda” for Govt. of Mauritius, a Fast Patrol Vessel “ SCG PS Zoroaster ” for Seychelles and an 
Ocean- Going Cargo & Passenger Ferry Vessel “MV   Ma Lisha” for Cooperative Republic of Guyana. GRSE has 
07 Operational Units at Kolkata, West Bengal and 01 Operational Unit at Ranchi, Jharkhand. GRSE is the only 
Indian Shipbuilder to have four distinct shipyards as a unique infrastructure advantage. GRSE has built over 800 
platforms, including 118 warships f or Indian Navy, Indian Coast Guard, and friendly foreign countries -  highest 
number of warships built & delivered by any Indian shipyard till date. From Frigates, Corvettes, Fleet Tankers, 
Landing Ship Tank, Landing Craft Utility to Survey Vessels, Offshor e Patrol Vessels and Fast Attack Craft - the 
repertoire is rich and varied. In addition to shipbuilding & ship repair, GRSE has also diversified into engineering business with a product profile of pre- fabricated steel bridges, various deck machinery items and assembly, testing 
& overhauling of marine diesel engines and the manufacture of 30mm Naval Surface Guns. Noteworthy accolades include Defence Minister ’s Award 2025 for Excellence in Financial Performance, Defence Minister ’s Award 2022 in 
Designing the most silent ship for Indian Navy for ASW operation, Defence Minister ’s Award of Excellence for In-
house Design Effort for Offshore Patrol Vessel for Government of Mauritius, CGS Barracuda, Best Performing 
Defence Shipyard of India for Four Years in a row. GRSE embraced infrastructure modernization over past decades 
and can undertake concurrent construction of 28 ships at a time. A strong team of highly skilled design engineers 
and state- of-the-art Next Generation VR Lab & latest software aid in the in- house design capabilities. With proven 
credentials, the shipyard is on a growth path, reaffirming its motto “ Infinite Passion Meets Unwavering 
Commitment ”.
