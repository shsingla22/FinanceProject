on 07.02.2020. As against the said order, NLCIL filed CMA No. 1797 of 2020 
before the Hon’ble Madras High Court.

pronounced Orders to set aside the impugned Award dated 16.12.2016, also the 
order dated 07.02.2020 passed by PDJ, Cuddalore and accordingly allowed the 
CMA No. 1797 of 2020 filed by NLCIL.
