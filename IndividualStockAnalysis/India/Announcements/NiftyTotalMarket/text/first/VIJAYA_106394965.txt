Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30  of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015  – “Resignation of Vice President – Operations ” 
 
With reference to the captioned subject, we wish to inform you that Mr. V. Siva Rama Raju 
Vegesna , Vice President – Operations of the Company, has tendered his resignation on 
account of personal reasons. The resignation has been accepted  by the company , and he 
will be relieved from the services of the Company with effect from November 08, 2025 
(close of business hours).  
 
Mr. Vegesna has been associated with the Company for over six  (6) years as part of the  
Management Team  at the Group Level . During his tenure, he played a pivotal role in 
strengthening the Company’s Investor relations & Strategy and  supporting the Company’s 
expansion into new markets.  
 
The Board and Management place on record their sincere appreciation of his dedicated 
service, professional acumen, and valuable guidance extended to the organization over the 
years.  
 
The Company conveys its best wishes to him for good health, happiness, and continued 
success in all his future endeavours.
