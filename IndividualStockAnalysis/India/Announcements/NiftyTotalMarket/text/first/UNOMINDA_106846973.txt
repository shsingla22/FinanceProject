Uno Minda Delivers Record Q2 FY27 Performance with 38% YoY 
Revenue and PAT Growth 
❖ Q2 FY27 Consolidated Revenue of, 6,655 Cr., Y-o-Y growth 38% 
❖ Q2 FY27 Consolidated EBIDTA of, 733 Cr., Y-o-Y growth 33% 
❖ Q2 FY27 Consolidated PAT (Uno Minda share) at, 420 Cr., Y-o-Y growth 38% 
❖ Board approves additional investment of ,85 crore to expand Sunroof manufacturing 
capacity at Bawal 
Gurugram -November 51 2026 : Uno Minda Limited ("Uno Minda"), a global technology 
leader in automotive components and systems manufacturing, today announced its financial 
results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, delivering another record quarter with its 
highest-ever quarterly revenue and profit. The performance reflects strong business 
momentum, broad-based growth across product segments and continued progress in scaling 
its manufacturing capacities and technology-led businesses. 
For Q2FY27, Uno Minda reported consolidated revenue of, 6,655 crores, registering a strong 
38% year-on-year growth compared to Revenue of, 4,814 crores in Q2FY26. The growth was 
broad-based, driven by volume expansion, increasing value addition and a favourable product 
mix across key business segments, including Switches, Lighting, Alloy Wheels and Seating. The 
rapidly expanding Green Mobility comprising of EV Systems and Alternate Fuel businesses 
also contributed to the overall growth momentum. Additionally, the ramp-up of recently 
commissioned facilities and incremental capacities supported the strong performance. 
The Company reported EBITDA of, 733 crores in Q2FY27 as against EBITDA of ,552 crores in 
Q2FY26, reflecting a growth of 33% YoY. 
PAT attributable to shareholders of Uno Minda stood at ,420 crore in Q2 FY27, registering a 
strong year-on-year growth of 38% compared to ,304 crore in Q2 FY26. The growth in 
profitability reflects the strength of the Company's diversified business portfolio and 
sustained operating performance. 
Financial for Hl FY 27 
We have achieved consolidated revenues of Rs 12,212 Crores for the half year ending at 
September 2026 registering a robust 32% year-on-year growth in Hl FY27. 
EBITDA for Hl FY27 increased by 27% to ,1,305 crore, compared to the normalised EBITDA 
of ,1,026 crore in Hl FY26. PAT attributable to shareholders stood at ,716 crore for Hl FY27, 
registering a healthy year-on-year growth of 32% over the normalised PAT of ,543 crore in 
the corresponding period last year. 
Expansion in Sunroof Manufacturing Facility 
The Board has approved an additional capital investment of approximately ,85 crore towards 
expanding its Sunroof manufacturing capacity at Bawal, Haryana. The proposed expansion is 
aimed at catering to incremental customer orders secured by the Company and the growing

About Uno Minda Limited: 
Uno Minda Limited incepted in 1958, is a global technology leader in Auto component and 
systems manufacturing supplying to leading OE Ms in the world. We design and manufacture 
over 28 categories of components an~ systems for vehicles across all segments (passenger 
cars, commercial vehicles, two-and three-wheelers) catering to both internal combustion 
engines (ICE) and electric/hybrid vehicles. 
We are one of the leading manufacturers of automotive switching systems, automotive 
lighting systems, automotive acoustics systems, automotive seating systems and alloy wheels. 
We have a leadership position in almost all the products it manufactures. Technology and 
innovation are the two strong pillars of our organisation, on the basis of which we have 
continued to lead the emerging trends in the automotive sector, over the past six decades. 
The Group is a global player in the automotive sector with 78 manufacturing facilities in India, 
Indonesia, Vietnam, Germany, Spain, and Mexico, as well as 37 R&D and Engineering Centres 
in India, Germany, Czech Republic, Vietnam, Japan, Taiwan, Korea & Spain. We have 18 
JVs/Technical Agreements with world renowned manufacturers from Japan, Korea, and 
China.
