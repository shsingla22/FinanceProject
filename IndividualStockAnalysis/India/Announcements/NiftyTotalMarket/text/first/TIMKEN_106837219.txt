Timken India Receives Two  BIS licenses for their Bearings  
 
The brand received BIS licenses for Spherical Roller Bearings across two 
manufacturing facilities.  
 
Bangalore, Karnataka, October  30, 2026  —Timken India Limited (the 
“Company”) today announces that it has been awarded two Bureau of Indian 
Standards (BIS) licenses for its bearings.  
 
This achievement confirms Timken India’s leadership in manufacturing 
excellence, quality assurance and regulatory compliance across multiple bearing 
categories and production sites.  
 Key highlights:  
 
• The Company has received two  BIS licenses for bearing products.  
• Timken India has received S RB Roller production licenses at its Bharuch 
(new) plant.  
• Timken India has received Cylindrical Roller Bearing production licenses at 
its Bharuch ( old) plant.  
 
“This is a remarkable milestone which validates the strength of our design, 
testing, and quality control procedures across our manufacturing facilities ,” said 
Sanjay Koul, Managing Director, Timken India Limited. “Securing the first BIS 
license in India for SRB  for the new Bharuch facility and for CRB for the old Bharuch 
plant underscores our commitment to the highest standards of quality, engineering 
and customer service. These licenses enable us to better serve industrial sectors 
that demand certified, high- performance bearings  – from automotive and rail to 
heavy industries and renewables.”  
 
Customer and market impact  
 
• Enhanced confidence: BIS -certified bearings reduce technical qualification barriers 
for OEMs, infrastructure projects and regulated sectors.  
• Stronger supply assurance: Certified manufacturing across three plants shortens lead times and improves regional availability.  
• Tender readiness: Distributors and customers will benefit from ready 
documentation and qualification support for tenders requiring BIS -certified 
components.  
 About the BIS licenses - The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) licenses were issued 
after rigorous evaluation of product design, manufacturing processes, and quality -
control systems. Each license confirms conformity to the relevant Indian national 
standards  for bearing performance and safety.
