e The 22" Annual General Meeting Notice and the Annual Report for the 
financial year 2024-25 were circulated to the Members through e-mail.

as the Statutory and Secretarial Auditors’ Report as read, since there were 
no qualifications in the said Auditors’ Report. 
e The Company has provided the facility of Remote E-voting for its Members. 
e As per various Circulars issued by MCA and SEBI, the AGM was held 
through video conference. 
e As the AGM was being held through video conference, the facility for 
appointment of proxies by the members was not applicable. 
o The Company had provided the facility to cast the votes electronically, on all 
resolutions set forth in the Notice. Members, who have not yet cast their votes 
and were participating in the meeting, were requested to cast their votes 
during the meeting, through e-voting system provided by NSDL. 
e The Members, who have already voted through Remote E-voting and were 
present at the AGM, need not vote again.
