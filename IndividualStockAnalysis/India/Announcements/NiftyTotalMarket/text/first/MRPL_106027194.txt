New Delhi: 18th October, 2024 
The Board of Directors of Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals 
Limited (MRPL), a subsidiary company of ONGC and Schedule "A" Mini 
Ratna Category I Company during its 266th Meeting held on October 18, 
2024, approved it's limited reviewed Standalone and Consolidated 
financial results for the second quarter (Q2 FY 2024-25) and half year 
(H1 FY 2024-25) ended September 30, 2024. 
Major highlights for the second quarter (Q2 FY 2024-25): 
➢ Devangonthi Marketing Terminal Commissioned during Aug 24, HSD 
tanker loading commenced. 
➢ Elathur depot made fully operational dispensing 15TKL product 
Month on Month. 
Capacity Maximization: 
✓ Month on Month highest Crude Processing in Q2. 
✓ Q2 Capacity Utilization -118.3%. 
✓ H1 Capacity Utilization -117.6%. 
Awards:
✓ Water Conservation and Horticulture Development Award. 
✓ 1st Prize in Best Cogen Boiler State-level award. 
✓ Greentech Award for Circular Economy Leadership in Waste 
Management & Recycling. 
✓ Mahatma Award-2024 for Corporate Environment Excellence (CER). 
✓ The Economic Times HR Future Ready Organization Award 2024-25. 
✓ Mahatma Award-2024 for HR Excellence and Social Impact. 
✓ Organization with Innovative HR Practices at the Asia Pacific HRM 
Congress Award-2024. 
✓ Outstanding Award for Official Language Implementation.
