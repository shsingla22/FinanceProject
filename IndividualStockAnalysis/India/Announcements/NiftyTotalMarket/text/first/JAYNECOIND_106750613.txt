SECTION A  General disclosures
This Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (“BRSR”/“this Report”) has been prepared by Jayaswal Neco 
Industries Limited, referred to throughout this Report as “the Company” or “JNIL ”, in accordance with the format prescribed 
by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) under the National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct.
JNIL is a long-established manufacturer of iron and specialty steel products, with integrated operations spanning captive 
mining, steelmaking and precision castings. As industrial growth increasingly depends on how responsibly it is achieved, the 
Company views environmental, social and governance (ESG) considerations as central to its ability to operate sustainably 
and retain the trust of its stakeholders. This BRSR serves as the Company’s primary tool for communicating that approach, 
setting out, in a structured and transparent manner, how JNIL manages its environmental footprint, its responsibilities 
toward employees and communities, and the governance standards that guide its decision-making.
Environment
StewardshipAcross its integrated steel plants, casting divisions and captive mining operations, the Company continues to invest 
in reducing its ecological footprint. Fume extraction and dedusting systems help keep air emissions and ambient 
air quality in check, and the zero liquid discharge approach supports efficient wastewater recycling and rainwater 
harvesting so that fresh-water use stays low. The Company also maintains an ongoing afforestation programme, 
including a dedicated “Oxy-zone”, aimed at supporting biodiversity, improving the local microclimate and building 
long-term carbon sequestration capacity. Together, these initiatives reflect a proactive approach to environmental 
stewardship that keeps pace with the Company’s industrial growth.
Social 
ResponsibilityFor the Company, social responsibility is a core value rather than a one-off commitment, and it shapes how it 
engages with both its workforce and the communities around its operations. The Company’s programmes in 
education, vocational training, healthcare and sanitation are designed to create inclusive, long-term development in 
the regions where it works, with a particular emphasis on skill-building and economic independence for youth and 
women. Internally, the Company works to sustain a supportive and inclusive workplace culture, reflected in strong 
employee retention, in the belief that a motivated workforce is central to sustainable success. Taken together, 
these initiatives are meant to ensure that the Company’s industrial growth translates into lasting benefit for the 
communities it touches.
Governance 
ExcellenceThe Company treats sound governance as the foundation of stakeholder trust and long-term sustainability. Its 
governance framework rests on transparency, accountability and ethical leadership, supported by disciplined 
compliance with statutory and regulatory requirements and by timely, accurate reporting. Internal controls and risk-
management systems are designed to keep operations resilient, while stakeholder engagement is  
approached openly to build trust and collaboration. Ongoing training reinforces ethical conduct at every level,  
so that governance functions as a shared value across the organization rather than a compliance checkbox.
I. Details of the listed entity
Headquartered in Nagpur, Maharashtra, the Company has grown from a modest iron foundry into one of India’s leading 
integrated manufacturers of alloy steel and iron & steel castings. With operations spanning the value chain, from captive 
mining of iron ore and limestone through to advanced steel processing, the Company has built a strong platform for 
producing alloy steel rolled products, billets, sponge iron, pellets, pig iron and skull, as well as precision castings. Iron 
ore, one of the key raw materials, is drawn from captive mines in Chhattisgarh, which supports a consistent supply and 
keeps the integrated steel plant running efficiently.The Company’s operations are certified under the Integrated Management System (IMS) and the International 
Automotive Task Force (IATF) framework, in line with globally recognized quality standards, underscoring its focus on 
optimized processes and continuous improvement. Its diverse product portfolio serves industries such as automotive, 
engineering, power, railways, cement, construction, agricultural equipment, bearings, structural components and 
fastener manufacturing.
Guided by a philosophy of “Sustainable Growth,” the Company pairs decades of manufacturing experience with a 
continued focus on innovation, environmental stewardship and community welfare. Its integrated operations, technical 
capability and customer-centric approach are what allow it to remain a trusted partner in India’s industrial landscape.

  c. A brief on types of customers
JNIL is one of the largest alloy steel manufacturers and ferrous casting producers in India. The Steel Plant Division’s 
product portfolio comprises wire rods, bars, bright bars, steel billets, pig iron, sponge iron, and pellets. These high-
quality alloy steel long products cater to a diverse range of applications across the automotive, auto components, 
engineering, power, oil & gas, fasteners, bearing, railway, and construction sectors.
Over the years, the Company has established itself as an approved supplier to all major automotive Original 
Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), following a rigorous and time-intensive approval process. The Steel Segment 
primarily serves Tier-2 manufacturing companies, including auto component manufacturers and forging 
companies, which in turn supply products and services to leading OEMs.
The Engineering Castings and Automotive Castings Divisions are majorly involved in the production of cylinder 
heads, housing, hubs, carrier housing, backhoe loaders, rib plates for metro rails, and steel valve castings, among 
others. These products are used in automobile and tractor manufacturing, construction applications, petroleum 
refineries, irrigation, railways, and commercial vehicle manufacturing.
The Centricast and Construction Castings Divisions of the Company manufacture pipes, fittings, manhole covers, 
frames, and gratings used in various construction activities. They also cater to the demand for steel plant items 
such as ingot moulds, bottom plates, slag pots, and valve body castings. Additionally, we are developing CI flash 
plates and door frames for Coke Oven Plants.
 Through its foundry products, JNIL caters to prestigious agencies and companies, including government, 
semi-government, and public sector undertakings, municipal corporations, corporate clients, builders and civil 
contractors, and EPC firms.
 Through its expansive product range and commitment to quality, the Company continues to play a pivotal role 
in supporting India’s industrial and infrastructure growth, while maintaining strong relationships with both
