Zen Technologies to acquire majority stake in Cogknit Semantics, an 
indigenous developer of Battlefield Management Systems
Hyderabad, India–18October 2026 :ZenTechnologies Limited(“Zen”) announced
thatitsBoard ofDirectors hasapproved aninvestment of₹135.15crore fora51.98%
stake (fully diluted basis) inCogknit Semantics Private Limited (“Cogknit”),a
Bengaluru -based defence technology company .Cogknit won theiDEX challenge tobuild
aBattlefield Management System (BMS) forMain Battle Tanks andisarecipient ofthe
RakshaMantri’s Award forInnovation (2022 ).
Zen’s investment comprises asubscription toCogknit’s primary funding round andthe
acquisition ofexisting shares .Investor Ashish Kacholia andhisassociates willalsoinvest
intheprimary round .Oncompletion ofthetransaction, which issubject tocustomary
closing conditions, Cogknit willbecome asubsidiary ofZen, anditsfounding team will
continue toleadthecompany .
Recent conflicts, from Ukraine toWest Asia, have shown thatbattles aredecided lessby
thenumber oftanks andguns than byhow quickly every unitcanseethebattlefield,
share what itsees andactonit.Battlefield Management Systems, which give every
fighting unitacommon picture offriendly forces, theenemy andtheplan, arecentral to
themodernisation ofarmoured forces worldwide .
Cogknit’s BMS digitally connects tanks, vehicles andsoldiers, sothatevery commander
works from onereal-time picture ofthebattlefield .Ithascompleted field trials with the
Indian Army, andisdesigned anddeveloped inIndia, adding tothecountry’s indigenous
defence technology base andtotheself-reliance oftheArmed Forces .
ForZen, theacquisition isaligned with Samvid, itsproposed AIplatform forshared
battlefield awareness .Cogknit’s BMS adds thecommand -and-control layer thatconnects
tanks, vehicles andsoldiers, andisintended tocomplement Samvid, aswell asZen’s
