BCML collaborates with CIPET Ahmedabad to bring ‘Bioyug on Wheels’ 
driving  Sustainable Innovation in Ahmedabad, Gujarat  
Ahmedabad, September  1st, 2025  – Balrampur  Chini Mills Ltd successfully conducted the Ahmedabad edition of its 
nationwide outreach initiative, ‘Bioyug on Wheels’.  This dynamic program aimed to bring the vision of a circular 
economy to life through interactive exhibitions, live demonstrations, and insightful discussions that highlighted cutting -
edge advancements in the bioeconomy.  
Organized in collaboration with CIPET Ahmedabad , the event fostered meaningful engagement among students, 
researchers, entrepreneurs, and sustainability advocates from across the region held on 30th August 2025 at CIPET 
Ahmedabad . Attendees experienced a full day of immersive activities, including:  
• A live tour of Bioyug on  Wheels that  showcased innovative bio -based items made from PLA  bioplastics which 
are 100% compostable  
• Live Demonstrations and Expert Q&A sessions with industry leaders  
• Interactive Exhibits and hands -on learning opportunities  
• Networking Sessions with innovators from academia and industry  
•  Seminar & Panel Discussion focused on the future of the bioeconomy and sustainable development  
The event served as a vibrant confluence of ideas and innovation, providing a unique platform for dialogue, inspiration, 
and collaboration in support of a more sustainable future. With Balrampur Bioyug’s visionary leadership and CIPET’s 
technical expertise, the Ahmedabad edition of “BioYug on Wheels” helped further the national conversation around 
bioeconomy and environmental stewardship.  The conference featured esteemed speakers, including Dr. S. K. Nayak, 
former Director General of CIPET, and Shri Paritos h Diwasali, Director of CIPET Ahmedabad. Dr. Nayak shared valuable 
insights on the evolving landscape of bio -based industries in India, emphasizing the critical role of research and policy in 
scaling sustainable solutions. Shri Diwasali highlighted CIPET’s  ongoing efforts to promote innovation and skill 
development in the field of bioplastics. Senior officials from leading companies such as Deltora Biopolymers Pvt. Ltd. 
and Vision Industries , Ahmedabad also participated, offering industry perspectives on the practical applications and 
commercial potential of bio -based technologies.  
 
Ms. Shweta Suryawanshi, Sales Head, BCML PLA Division said , “the  enthusiastic engagement and knowledge exchange 
witnessed in Ahmedabad underscore the growing momentum for sustainable solutions within the region. We remain 
steadfast in our commitment to driving systemic change, supporting India’s environmental objective s, and promoting 
sustainable growth within the bioeconomy.  Since its launch in Mumbai in May 2025, Bioyug on Wheels has successfully 
traversed key regions including Bhopal & Indore in Madhya  Pradesh, before reaching Gujarat. Following its successful 
engagement in Gujarat, Bioyug on Wheels is set to advance its journey southward, beginning with Kochi, Kerala .  Hosting the 
event in Ahmedabad, a vibrant industrial and innovation hub —offered an excellent platform to connect with a broad 
spectrum of stakeholders committed to sustainability and innovation. The strong interest from academia, industry experts, 
entrepreneurs, and policymakers reflect  the growing market demand for viable bio -based alter natives.”  
As Bioyug  on Wheels travels from city to city, it brings people and organizations together to support India’s move toward 
a more sustainable, circular economy. Balrampur Bioyug is dedicated to spread  awareness about PLA innovation and 
creating positive changes  across industries and communities across the country.  
About  Balrampur  Chini  Mills  Limited  (CIN:  L15421WB1975PLC030118):  
Balrampur Chini Mills Limited (BCML) is among India’s largest integrated sugar companies, excelling in distillery
