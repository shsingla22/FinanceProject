Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 - Paytm Singapore Board approves partnership with Funding Societies, including a 
proposed investment of up to INR 200 crore  
 
Dear Sir / Ma’am,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 
(“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform you that Paytm Singapore Pte. Ltd. (“Paytm Singapore”),  a step 
down wholly owned subsid iary of One 97 Communications Limited (“the Company”), has informed us today, 
November 19, 202 6 at 06.22 p.m. (IST) that its Board of Directors at its meeting held today, have approved a 
partnership with Funding Asia Group Pte. Ltd., Singapore (“Funding Societies”).  
 
Under this partnership, Paytm Singapore will make an investment of upto INR 200 crores  (Indian Rupees Two 
Hundred Crores Only) into subsidiaries of Funding Societies, including an initial investment of approximately 
INR 162 Crores (Indian Rupees One Hundred Sixty Two Crores Only) for a 19.1% stake in CardUp Pte. Ltd. 
(CardUp), its payment subsidiary. The remaining investment of approximately INR 38 Crores (Indian Rupees 
Thirty Eight Crores Only) may be made in one of the affiliates of Funding Societies. The proposed investments 
are subject to confirmatory due diligence, customary corporate approvals, execution of definitive documents 
and any regulatory approvals.  
 
In addition to the aforesaid proposed investments, the Company will also enter into business and technology 
partnership with CardUp under which the Company will provide payments and lending technology to CardUp 
for expanding Paytm’s technology led payments  and financial services stack in Singapore. This is in -line with 
the Company’s previously disclosed roadmap to expand its presence in select international markets through 
the “Partner Operated” model, wherein the Company (and / or its subsidiaries) provides its proven acceptance 
platform and innovative devices, while the local partner leads sales, distribution and on -ground operations to 
serve merchants. The Company will monet ize its contributions through licensing fees, revenue -sharing and 
other arrangements, while deploying limited capital.  
 
The relevant details as required under the SEBI Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/49/14/14(7)2025 -CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 are enclosed herewith as 
Annexure - I & II.  
Further, In continuation to our earlier disclosure dated October 26, 2026, we hereby inform that the Company 
has completed an additional investment of INR 500 Crores  (Indian Rupees Five Hundred Crores only) in its 
wholly owned subsidiary, Paytm Cloud Technologies Limited (“PCTL”). Subsequently, PCTL has also completed 
an additional investment of INR 500 Crores (Indian Rupees Five Hundred Crores only) in its wholly own ed 
subsidiary, Paytm Singapore Pte. Ltd. (“Paytm Singapore”), a step -down wholly owned subsidiary of the 
Company.
