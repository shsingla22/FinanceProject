SPECIAL WINDOW FOR TRANSFER AND DEMATERIALISATION 
OF PHYSICAL SECURITIES 
Pursuant to SEBI circular No. HO/38/13/11 (2)2026-MIRSD-POD /1/3750/2026 dated January 
30, 2026, titled "Ease of Doing Investment -Special Window for Transfer and Dematerialisation 
of Physical Securities" shareholders of the Company , holding shares in physical form are 
hereby informed that SEBI has opened a Special window for a period of 1 (one) year 
commencing from February 05, 2026 to February 04, 2027, to apply for transfer and 
dematerialisation ("Demat") of physical securities that were sold/purchased prior to April 01, 
2019, or for the transfer request(s) which were rejected / returned / not attended due to 
deficiency in documents , process or any other reason, may send the documents along with 
original share certificate (s) to the Company for processing. The shareholders are requested to 
refer to the aforesaid Circular available on the website of the Company before submission.
