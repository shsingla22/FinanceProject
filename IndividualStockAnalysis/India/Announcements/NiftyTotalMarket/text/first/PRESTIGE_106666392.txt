PRESTIGE LAUNCHES PHASE 2 OF ITS BEST -SELLING PLOTTED DEVELOPMENT 
IN NORTH BENGALURU - PRESTIGE GARDENIA ESTATES  
 
Prestige Estates is delighted to announce the launch of the next phase of its best-selling  plotted 
development Prestige Gardenia Estates in Devanahalli, North Bengaluru.  
 
Being in the North Bangalore corridor, it is within minutes of Kempegowda International 
Airport, NH 44, the Satellite Town Ring Road, major IT parks, schools, hospitals, and retail 
destinations.  
 
Spread over approx. 21 acres of meticulously planned, infrastructure -ready land, this enclave 
offers wide, tree -lined corridors, modern utilities and curated green zones —creating a 
sustainable, nature -centric community where every plot enjoys an elegant ba lance of open 
space and contemporary comfort. The project comprises of 195 plots and has a total 
development area of just under 0.5 mn sft with a revenue potential of almost Rs. 400 Crores.  
 
The property together with the Phase -1, boasts world -class leisure amenities, including a 
state -of-the-art clubhouse, jogging and walking tracks, multipurpose and pickleball courts, an 
outdoor gym, yoga lawn, reflexology path, flower and Zen gardens, a chi ldren’s play area 
with tree house, skating rink and climbing wall, sunken seating pockets, a dedicated pet park, 
etc., amongst other bespoke facilities.  
 
Launched to immediate acclaim, Prestige Gardenia Estates has been exceptionally well -
received and has sold out at launch, underscoring the strong demand for sustainable, well -
connected living in North Bengaluru.  
 
About Prestige Group:  The Prestige Group is one of India’s most respected and diversified 
real estate developers, with a legacy spanning nearly four decades and a portfolio that 
includes residential, commercial, retail, hospitality, and integrated township projects across 
majo r cities. As of March 2026, the Group has delivered 316 projects spanning 212 million sqft 
and currently has a pipeline of 135 projects across 227 million sqft.
