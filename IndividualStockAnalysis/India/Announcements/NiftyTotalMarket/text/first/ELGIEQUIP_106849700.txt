Press Release -6/11/2026 
Elgi Equipments Limited -Second Quarter 
Elgi Equipments Ltd, manufacturer of air compressors, posted a consolidated sales for 
the quarter of Rs. 1,134 Crores compared to Rs. 968 Crores in the corresponding quarter 
in 2025-26, representing a growth of 17% .. Standalone sales for the quarter was Rs. 708 
Crores compared to Rs. 568 Crores for the same quarter in 2025-26, representing a 
growth of 25%. 
Consolidated PAT for the quarter was Rs. 128 Crores for the quarter, compared to Rs. 
121 Crores in the same period in 2025-26, representing a growth of 6%. The Standalone 
PAT for the quarter Rs. 129 Crores as compared to Rs. 91 Crores in the same period in 
2025-26. 
Growth momentum continued in the Indian and American markets. Performance in 
Europe and Australia remained subdued amid prevailing economic headwinds. 
Our automotive business also achieved its expected growth when compared to the same 
period in 2025-26. 
Outlook for the Third Quarter:- 2025;..27 
Notwithstanding the geo-political tensions and rising cost pressures, the Company 
maintains a positive outlook for Q3 and remains on track to achieve its budgeted 
performance.
