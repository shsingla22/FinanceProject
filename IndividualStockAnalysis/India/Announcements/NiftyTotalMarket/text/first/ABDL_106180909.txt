Allied Blenders and Distillers dominates INDSPIRIT Awards 2025, named 
Company of the Year  
- Arthaus  Blended Malt Scotch wins 3 top awards with New Product of the Year, Best 
Blended Scotch, Best Packaging.  
- Zoya Watermelon wins for Best Premium Flavour Gin.  
Mumbai, March 2 5, 2025  - Allied Blenders and Distillers Ltd (ABD) has achieved exceptional 
recognition  at the prestigious INDSPIRIT Awards 2025 , securing a n unprecedented five awards  
including the coveted Company of the Year , reinforcing  its position to be elite innovators in the 
Indian spirits industry.  
Further highlighting this remarkable achievement, ABD's premium flagship Arthaus Blended 
Malt Scotch Whisky  secured an impressive triple win with New Product of the Year, Best 
Blended Scotch  for its masterful liquid complexity, and Best Packaging  and digital integration for 
its striking visual and storytelling . To top it ABD's Zoya Special Batch Premium Gin - Watermelon  
was hono ured with the Best Flavoured Gin , demonstrating the company's exceptional versatility 
across the product  spectrum.  
The INDSPIRIT Awards stand as the definitive benchmark of excellence in India's competitive 
spirits landscape, featuring a rigorous evaluation process conducted by a distinguished panel of 
master blenders, design visionaries, and industry veterans. ABD's c ommanding performance 
across multiple categories reflects the extraordinary depth and versatility of its meticulously 
crafted portfolio.  
Speaking about this milestone achievement, Alok Gupta, Managing Director,  ABD , said, 
"INDSPIRIT's recognition validates our strategic journey toward s category leadership through 
innovation and uncompromising quality. The Company of the Year honour acknowledges not just 

our current success, but our commitment to shaping the future of Indian spirits. Seeing Arthaus 
and Zoya celebrated as category benchmarks confirms our ability to anticipate evolving tastes 
while honouring our heritage of exceptional craftsmanship."  
Bikram Basu, Managing Director designate for ABD Maestro  Pvt Ltd , the new subsidiary 
company which is  a strategic business venture in collaboration with Powerhouse Ranveer Singh 
said, “ Arthaus and Zoya  will both be with our super -premium and luxury portfolio at ABD 
Maestro . This team set up the brands from scratch and it will be good to move with them to a 
new set -up for improved focus. We are grateful for the recognition from INDSPIRIT, and these 
moments support and give confidence  to build a great margin forward business.”  
These accolades underscore ABD's continued commitment to excellence and innovation. As the 
company advances its premium portfolio strategy, th e recognition at INDSPIRIT 2025 represents 
a significant milestone in the journey toward s establishing top quality and creativ e products . 
About Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited ( www.abdindia.com ) 
Allied Blenders and Distillers Limited (ABD) is the 3rd largest Spirits company in India, in terms of 
annual sales volumes between FY14 and FY24. ABD has a presence in five main Flavors, i.e., whisky, 
brandy, rum, vodka, and gin, with 'millionaire' brands like Officer's Choice Whisky, Officer's Choice 
Blue Whisky, Sterling Reserve Premiu m Whiskies and ICONiQ White Whisky. Currently, its 
manufacturing network comprises 36 units, of which 9 are owned bottling units, 2 owned 
distilleries, and 25 non -owned manufacturing units.
