About ACME Solar Holdings Limited  
ACME Solar Holdings Limited  is a pure play fully integrated renewable energy company  in India 
with a diversified portfolio across solar, wind, storage, hybrid and Firm and Dispatchable 
Renewable Energy (“FDRE”) projects. The company is one of the top 10 renewable energy 
Independent Power Producer in India with an operational contracted capacity of 2,990 MW and 
~2.3 GWh of BESS  capacity and under construction contracted capacity of 5,081 MW including 
~15 GWh of BESS installation .  
The company  generates long term stable cashflows through the sale of electricity to various off -
takers including central and state government -backed entities via long term fixed tariff PPAs. The 
company has in -house EPC and O&M capabilities allowing it to control processes, costs and 
timelines and giving flexibility in the choice of technology and suppliers.
