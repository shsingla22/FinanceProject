PRESS RELEASE  
 
BuzzWorks strengthens presence in Hyderabad by leasing office space to MyComplianceOffice  
 
With this, BuzzWorks Leases a total of 1.1 lakh Sq Ft in Hyderabad  
 
Bengaluru, March 1 3, 2026: Brigade Group’s managed workspace provider BuzzWorks has announced the 
lease of 550 seats in its Mindspace Business Park, Hyderabad premises to Dublin based 
MyComplianceOffice  (MCO) , a globally recognised regulatory technology (RegTech) and compliance 
technology company. This is MCO’s largest office space in India. With this signing, BuzzWorks total leased 
office space in Hyderabad currently stands at 1.1 lakh sq. ft. The new office at BuzzWorks Mindspace 
Business Park will house MCO’s core functions includ ing Product Engineering, Product Management, 
Customer Success and Global Operations, and AI -enabled Compliance Technology initiatives, functions 
that are central to MCO’s global delivery model.  
 
Commenting on the development, Siddharth Verma, Business Head, BuzzWorks India said, “As a 
preferred destination for global technology enterprises seeking to establish and scale their India operations, 
Hyderabad continues to remain a key market for us. Thi s strategic partnership with MCO reflects our 
commitment to provide a combination of future -ready facilities with world -class amenities to enable our 
partners to scale their India operations seamlessly. It is a testament to our continued focus to create st rategic 
growth environments to empower global delivery models through excellence in infrastructure and 
design.”  
 
Speaking about the company’s evolution and strategic expansion in India, Bapiraju Nandury, Managing 
Director & VP, MyComplianceOffice said,  “The establishment of our India entity in 2023 has been a 
significant step in our journey, and in just two years, our Hyderabad office has grown to become our largest 
globally. As we continue to expand and plan to scale our team further over the coming yea r, India will 
remain central to our long -term innovation and growth strategy.  As part of this expansion, MCO has 
partnered with BuzzWorks for its new workspace in Hyderabad, supporting the company’s next phase of 
growth.”  
 
About BuzzWorks: BuzzWorks is a managed workspace provider offering strategic office solutions 
designed for today’s evolving workforce. Part of Brigade Group, BuzzWorks creates environments where 
energy, collaboration, and innovation come together naturally.  
 
About Brigade Group:  Established in 1986, Brigade Group is one of India’s leading property developers 
with close to four decades of expertise in building positive experiences for all their stakeholders and 
winning their customers’ trust. Brigade has developed many landmark bu ildings and transformed skylines 
across India in the cities of Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, and 
GIFT City with developments across Residential, Office, Retail, Hospitality and Education Sectors.  
 
About MyComplianceOffice (MCO): MCO provides integrated compliance management software that 
enables 1500+ global financial services firms across 128 countries to operate efficiently, ethically, and 
compliantly. With 30 products on a single system, the powerful MyComplianceOffice platform  enables 
compliance professionals to demonstrate that they are proactively managing compliance obligations and 
the regulated activities of employees, the company, and third -party relationships. For more information, 
visit  mycomplianceoffice.com
