Review of Operations for the Quarter ended September 30, 2026 
 
For Q2 FY27, Glenmark’s consolidated revenue from operations was at Rs. 42,473 million (USD 444.5 
million) as against Rs. 60,469 million (USD 699.7 million) in the corresponding quarter last year. On a 
like-for-like basis, Glenmark’s consolidated revenue in Q2 FY27 recorded YoY growth of ~20%.  
 
For H1 FY27, Glenmark’s consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 82,658 million (USD 869.6 
million) as against Rs. 93,113 million (USD 1,081.3 million) in the corresponding quarter last year. On 
a like-for-like basis, Glenmark’s consolidated revenue in H1 FY27 recorded YoY growth of ~18%.

3 Management  Discussion  & Analysis:  Q2 FY27  
 
TEVIMBRA® (TISLELIZUMAB) & BRUKINSA® (ZANUBRUTINIB) PARTNERED WITH BEONE  
 Glenmark launched Tislelizumab and Zanubrutinib in India under the respective brand 
names TEVIMBRA® and BRUKINSA® in Q1 FY26. 
 TEVIMBRA® (Tislelizumab) and BRUKINSA® (Zanubrutinib) continued to build strong 
momentum in the Indian Oncology market, reinforcing Glenmark's strategic focus on 
bringing innovative therapies to patients.  
 On the back of these two launches, Glenmark has further strengthened its standing in 
Oncology and is now ranked 4th among Indian pharmaceutical companies in Oncology. 
 
NEBZMART® GFB Smartules® / GLENMARK AIRZ® FB Smartules® 
 In Q3 FY26, Glenmark announced the launch of NEBZMART® GFB Smartules® and 
Glenmark AIRZ® FB Smartules®, the world’s first nebulized, fixed-dose triple therapy for the 
treatment of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) 
 Both products combine three proven medicines - Glycopyrronium, Formoterol, and 
Budesonide - to reduce airway obstruction, inflammation, and improve lung function and 
symptom control. As a single, easy-to-use nebulized therapy, it minimizes the burden of 
multiple medications. 
 NEBZMART® GFB Smartules® and GLENMARK AIRZ® FB Smartules® have demonstrated 
strong market traction since launch, further enhancing Glenmark's leadership position in 
Respiratory care and drug-device innovation. 
 
JABRYUS® PARTNERED WITH PFIZER 
 Glenmark launched JABRYUS® (Abrocitinib), a first of its kind oral advanced systemic 
treatment for the treatment of moderate-to-severe Atopic Dermatitis (AD) in India in 
partnership with Pfizer. 
 JABRYUS® has been well received by dermatologists as a novel treatment for moderate-to-
severe AD, with improved efficacy and oral convenience to patients. 
 
 
INDIA – GLENMARK CONSUMER CARE LIMITED (GCCL)  
Primary sales for GCCL in Q2 FY27 were Rs. 1,252 million with a YoY growth of 46.5%. GCCL 
delivered a strong performance in Q2 FY27, driven by sustained momentum across its key brands. 
 CANDID POWDER™, the flagship brand, reported >40% growth during the quarter and 
further strengthened its leadership position in the category. It expanded its market 
leadership, reaching ~60% market share, as per the IQVIA SEP’ 2026 dataset. 
 LA SHIELD™ portfolio delivered robust growth of ~35% in Q2 FY27 across its key variants. 
 The SCALPE™ portfolio continued its strong growth trajectory, registering ~70% growth 
during the quarter, supported by healthy performance across key variants. 
 Other brands such as BONTRESS™ and EPISOFT™ delivered impressive growth.
