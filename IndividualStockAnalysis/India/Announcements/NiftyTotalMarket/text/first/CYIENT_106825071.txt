PRESS RELEASE 
 
Cyient Reports Strong Q2 Performance, Positioned  
to Sustain Momentum Through the Year 
EBIT Expansion, Order Intake Gr owth & Robust Cash Generation 
Underscore Sustained Business Momentum 
 
Hyderabad, October 22, 2026:  Cyient, a global lifecycle engineering company , announced its 
financial results for the quarter  ended September 30, 2026.  
 
Financial Highlights for Q2 FY27 
 Cyient DET* Revenue at INR 1,664 crores , with growth of 8.1%  QoQ and 15.7%  YoY; 
Organic growth of QoQ  2.1%  and 9.3% YoY. 
 Cyient DET* EBIT stood at INR 237 crores , with a margin of 14.2% and QoQ growth of 
16.8%  
 Cyient DET* PAT at INR 153 crores , with QoQ growth of 8.7%   
 Cyient DET* FCF at INR 214 crores , FCF to PAT conversion 139% 
 Q2FY27 Order Intake grew by 20.8%  YoY  
 Interim dividend of INR 12  per share announced 
 
*Cyient DET (Digital, Engineer ing, and Technology) segment 
 
Commenting on the results, Krishna Bodanapu, Executive Vice Chairman and Managing 
Director, Cyient , said, “ Q2 FY27 reflects sustained momentum across Cyient Group, with record 
order books and continued investment in our next phase of growth. 
 
Cyient Semiconductors delivered 8% revenue growth QoQ, marking its sixth consecutive 
quarter of growth, while securing a signific ant turnkey program and a major engagement 
with a global semiconductor company. Our AI data-center power products remain on track, 
with R&D investments expected to begin transl ating into revenue next  fiscal year. While 
near-term supply constraints may impact margin s and growth, a healthy order book and an 
ASIC pipeline of over $100 million underpin our confidence in the long-term outlook. 
 
Cyient DLM achieved another record or der book, with book-to-bill above 1.6x, 
strengthening revenue visibility. Revenue grew over 26.5% YoY and over 30% in H1, in INR 
terms, with growth across all key segm ents led by Aerospace and Industrial. 
 Cyient DET delivered growth during the qua rter and remained focused on building the 
capabilities that support our long-term st rategy. Reflecting our strong operating 
performance and disciplined capital allocation, the Board has declared an interim dividend 
of ₹12 per share. 
 
 
Looking ahead, the Defense, semiconductors  and AI industries present the highest 
opportunities for growth, and we are pursu ing these together as a Group, bringing 
customers an end-to-end offering spanning engi neering, silicon, manufacturing and lifecycle 
support, reinforcing the compounding value of our complementary capabilities.   Our strong order books, maturing pipeline, and focused investments in high-growth areas 
give me confidence that we can navigate th rough near-term industry headwinds and keep 
creating sustainable and long-t erm value for shareholders.”
