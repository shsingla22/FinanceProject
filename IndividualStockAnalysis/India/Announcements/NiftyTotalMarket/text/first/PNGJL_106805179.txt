Subject: Update on Acquisition of Silvostyle Jewellers Limited  
 
Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
This is in continuation to our earlier intimation dated August 20, 2026, wherein the Company had informed that 
the Board of Directors had approved the acquisition of 100% stake in Silvostyle Jewellers Limited (“SJL”) , by 
way of acquisition of Equity Shares through fresh issue and acquisition from the existing shareholders, in one or 
more tranches.  
 We hereby inform you that the acquisition has been completed pursuant to the terms of the Share Purchase 
Agreement and the applicable transaction documents.  
 
In this regard:  
 
1. The Company has made the requisite payment towards subscription to the fresh E quity Shares of SJL.  
 
2. The Board of Directors of SJL has approved the allotment of 1,48,50,000 (One Crore Forty- Eight Lakh 
Fifty Thousand) E quity Shares  to the Company.  
 
3. Pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement, the existing shareholders of SJL have transferred 1,50,000 
(One Lakh Fifty Thousand) E quity Shares  to the Company.  
 
Accordingly, upon completion of the aforesaid transactions, the Company has acquired an aggregate of 
1,50,00,000 (One Crore Fifty Lakh) E quity Shares , representing 100% of the P aid-up Equity Share capital 
of SJL . 
 
Consequently, Silvostyle Jewellers Limited has become a wholly owned  subsidiary of P N Gadgil Jewellers 
Limited , with effect from the date of completion  i.e. October 01, 2026,  of the aforesaid transactions.  
The details of the acquisition were earlier disclosed by the Company pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, vide its intimation dated August 20, 2026. 
The aforesaid disclosure may be read together with the present intimation.
