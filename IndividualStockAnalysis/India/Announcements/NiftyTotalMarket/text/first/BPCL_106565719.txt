Social Media Post 
 ‘BPCL has successfully commissioned its Second- Generation (2G) Bioethanol Refinery at Bargarh. 
The facility produces 100 KL/day of fuel -grade bioethanol from rice straw through advance 
lignocellulosic technology, pretreatment and fermentation.  Designed as a Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) plant with solid- liquid separation, it ensures efficient 
resource use and environmental stewardship.  It supports India’s E20 Ethanol Blending Programme and National Biofuels Policy, reducing emissions and fostering a circular economy. The project was delivered safely  with ~20 million LTA -free manhours, reflecting outstanding 
teamwork and engineering excellence.’
