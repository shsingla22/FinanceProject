PRESS RELEASE  
 
Brigade Group Signs Leasing of Approximately 1,56,000 sq ft at Brigade Twin Towers  
 
Bengaluru, March 3,  2026:  Brigade Group, one of India’s leading real estate developers, has announced 
significant leasing  of around 1,56,000 square feet of commercial space in Brigade Twin Towers, 
Yeswanthpur,  to two global industry leaders - MANN+HUMMEL Filter Private Ltd, a Germ an filtration 
technology leader and  Labcorp  (NYSE: LH), a global leader of innovative and comprehensive laboratory 
services. On the back of strong market response and sustained demand for premium Grade A office spaces, 
Brigade Twin Towers has established i tself as one of the most sought -after office developments along the 
ORR corridor.  
 
Commenting on the development, Nandakumar O P, Chief Operating Officer -Office Spaces, Brigade 
Enterprises Limited, said, “The steady demand and quick occupancy reflect the growing demand for well -
planned workspaces in this region. At Brigade, we understand  the needs of  global enterprises requirements  
in terms of locations that offer easy accessibility, responsible design, and a supportive business environment. 
These recent leasing agreements reaffirm our focus on building workplaces that enable businesses to grow 
and operate efficiently. It motivates u s to remain committed to delivering quality infrastructure that supports 
the evolving needs of the commercial market.”  
 
 “As a global leader in filtration, our priority was to find a workspace that mirrors our commitment to 
innovation, quality, and sustainability. Brigade Twin Towers provides a modern, well -equipped 
environment that aligns with our operational needs in Indi a and our mission to help to provide cleaner 
mobility, water, industry  and air.  The open workplace design promotes cross -functional collaboration and 
teamwork, strengthening how our teams work together. We are excited to transition into this premium 
campus  which aligns perfectly with our 85 -year legacy of excellence.” Mr. Sudeesh  Karimbingal, Managing 
Director India & VP - OE RoA, MANN+HUMMEL Filter Private Ltd said.  
 
“Brigade Twin Towers offers the ideal setting for Labcorp’s continued growth in India, combining 
world -class facilities with an environment that fosters innovation and teamwork. This new workspace 
enhances our ability to support global research programs wh ile providing our employees with a modern 
and well -connected campus. We look forward to advancing our mission of improving health and improving 
lives in our new space,” said Imran Ali, Senior Vice President, Global Head Early Development Research 
Operation s and Country Head, India, Labcorp.  
 
Located with seamless access to major business districts and public transport, Brigade Twin Towers offer a 
contemporary workplace environment designed for maximum efficiency. Beyond the office floor, the 
campus provides an integrated experience featuring r etail options and thoughtfully designed open spaces, 
ensuring that the modern workforce benefits from a balanced and premium professional lifestyle.
