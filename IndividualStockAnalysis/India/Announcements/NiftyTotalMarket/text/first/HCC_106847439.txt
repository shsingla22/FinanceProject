Press Release 
HCC appoints Vivek Singhal as Chief Executive Officer 
Mumbai: November 5, 2026: Hindustan Construction Company Limited (HCC) today 
announced the appointment of Vivek Singhal as Chief Executive Officer, effective November 
5, 2026. 
Singhal brings over four decades of experience in infrastructure and EPC, spanning project 
execution, business leadership, business development and P&L management. His 
experience includes 16 years in international markets, delivering projects and managing 
assets worth -~28,500 across five countries . He has worked across airports, metro rail, 
urban transport, hydropower, bridges and other infrastructure sectors. 
Mr. Arjun Dhawan, Vice Chairman & Managing Director, HCC said: "Vivek has built and 
run large infrastructure businesses across India and international markets and has delivered 
at scale. That depth will strengthen HCC's leadership as we build on our engineering and 
execution capabilities and enter our next phase of growth." 
Singhal joins HCC from GMR Airports Ltd., Singapore, where he served as Chief Executive 
Officer, with responsibility for global strategy, P&L management, business development and 
airport asset development across international markets. 
Previously, he held senior leadership positions at Tata Projects Ltd.; GMR Group in the 
Maldives; Alpine Bau GmbH in Singapore; and Larsen & Toubro. At Tata Projects, he led the 
Urban Infrastructure business, growing its turnover from inception to over ~3.300 crore, with 
an order backlog exceeding ~38,000 crore. At Alpine Bau, he led major underground metro 
projects in Singapore with a combined contract value exceeding US$1 billion. 
Singhal holds a Bachelor's Degree in Civil Engineering from Motilal Nehru National Institute 
of Technology , Allahabad, and has completed the Advanced Management Programme at 
~~ liM Bangalore . He is a Fellow of the Institution of Civil Engineers, UK.
