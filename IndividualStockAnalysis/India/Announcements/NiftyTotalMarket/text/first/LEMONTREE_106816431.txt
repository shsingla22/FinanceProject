LEMON TREE HOTELS ANNOUNCES THE SIGNING OF  KEYS SELECT BY 
LEMON TREE HOTELS, BAREILLY  
Strengthens  the group’s footprint  in Uttar Pradesh with its 26th property in the state  
New Delhi,  October 13 , 2026 : Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, a leading name in the Indian 
hospitality sector, today announced the strategic addition of Keys Select by Lemon Tree 
Hotels, Bareilly  to its expanding pipeline.  
The property will be operated under a franchise agreement, further expanding the group's 
presence across the region . 
This milestone development marks a deliberate expansion of the Group’s network within 
Uttar Pradesh, strengthening its presence across high -growth, mid -tier commercial hubs 
throughout North India. With this signing, the Group will have 8 operational hotels and 18 
upcoming properties in Uttar Pradesh . 
Bareilly is rapidly advancing as one of Uttar Pradesh’s major urban centers , driven by 
expanding industrial activity, traditional manufacturing sectors, and its vital role as a key 
transport nexus for the Rohilkhand region. Positioned along major arterial highways, the 
city experiences a rising influx of corporate executives, tra nsit operators, and event -driven 
travelers. This consistent regional activity generates strong demand for branded, high -
quality, and reliable accommodations that balance modern corporate utility with 
exceptional value.  
The upcoming hotel will feature 52 well -appointed rooms  tailored to provide a 
comfortable, contemporary stay. To serve both corporate and local social events, the 
property will feature a multi -cuisine restaurant, a versatile banquet and conference hall, a 
modern fitness center  and inviting public spaces.  
Commenting on the signing, Mr. Kapil Sharma , Executive Director  & CFO , Lemon 
Tree Hotels Ltd. , said:  “The signing of Keys Select in Bareilly reflects our strategy of 
expanding into high -potential regional markets where we see sustained demand from 
business and transit travellers. Through an asset -light approach, we are able to scale our 
presence across Utt ar Pradesh in a capital -efficient manner while strengthening our long -
term value creation .” 
The property offers exceptional transit convenience for domestic travelers, located just 4.9 
km from Bareilly Airport (approx. an 11 -minute drive) and roughly 12 km from Bareilly 
Junction Railway Station, ensuring seamless connectivity for arriving guests.  
 
 

 
 
 
ABOUT LEMON TREE HOTELS LIMITED  
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited (LTHL) is one of India's leading hospitality companies, catering 
to a wide range of customers – from value -conscious travellers to premium business and 
leisure seekers. With seven distinct brands – Aurika Hotels & Resorts, Lemon T ree Premier, 
Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox, Keys Prima, Keys Select, and Keys Lite – the group offers 
experiences across upper upscale, upscale, upper midscale, midscale, leisure, wildlife, and 
spiritual segments.  
LTHL operates 130+ hotels across 80+ cities in India and abroad, with a growing pipeline 
of 140+ upcoming properties. From metro hubs like Delhi -NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and 
Hyderabad to tier II & III cities such as Jaipur, Udaipur, Kochi, and Indore – and with an 
international presence in Bhutan and Nepal – Lemon Tree Hotels delivers exceptional 
comfort, consistent quality, and a warm, refreshing experience.  
Since opening its first 49 -room hotel in 2004, the group has grown to 270+ properties 
(operational and upcoming), becoming a trusted name in hospitality for both business and 
leisure travellers.
