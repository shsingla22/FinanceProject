HPCL partners with Delhivery for Pan-India Lubricant Distribution   
India, 27th January 2025: Hindustan Petroleum Co rporation Ltd (HPCL), India’s leading Lube 
Marketer, has partnered with Delhivery, India’s lar gest fully integrated logist ics service provider, to 
manage nationwide distribution of HPCL’s branded l ubricants, known as HP Lubricants, to its 
various stakeholders.   
HPCL aims to achieve a quantum jump in the oper ational excellence of its supply chain, which 
involves the movement of millions of Lubricant SKUs across the country, with Delhivery’s robust 
Part Truck Load (PTL) logistics infrastructu re and cutting-edge technology solutions underpinned 
by centralized tracking. The coll aboration is expected to set a new milestone in the Indian logistics 
landscape. 
Ch Srinivas, Executive Director - Lubes from HPCL , says, “At HPCL, we are driven by excellence. 
This collaboration integrates our track-and-trace  platform with Delhivery’s logistics solution, 
delivering a seamless end-to-end system. Toget her, we’re setting new benchmarks in efficiency 
and product integrity, ensuring best-in-class servic e for our stakeholders. We ’re excited to partner 
with Delhivery, a company that shares our  commitment to innovation and reliability.” 
Suraj Saharan, Cofounder of Delhivery, says, "Securing this mandate highlights Delhivery’s 
capability to deliver efficient enterprise grade outcomes and handle large-volume logistics with 
precision. We are committed to delivering seam less solutions that enhance HPCL’s supply chain 
performance and expand their national reach.” 
About HPCL HPCL is a Central Public Sector Enterprise in Maharatna category with strong presence in refining 
and marketing of petroleum products, with market share of over 20% in the country. HPCL owns 
and operates two refineries at Mumbai and Visakh  and a lube refinery at Mumbai, supported by a 
vast marketing network. The customer touchpoints constitute over ~ 23,000 retail outlets and ~ 
6,400 LPG distributors which services a customer bas e of 9.65 crore LPG customers. HPCL has its 
Research & Development Centre in Bengaluru, wh ich provides advanced technical support to the 
refineries and marketing SBUs for operational im provement and acquiring new technologies. HPCL 
along with its 20 JV and Subsidiary companies oper ates across the oil & gas value chain. HPCL 
has announced its plan to reach Net Zero in Scope 1 & 2 emissions by 2040. More information 
about HPCL is available at www.hindustanpetroleum.com
