Blue Dart Launches India's Largest Integrated Operating Facility at Bijwasan, Delhi 
New Delhi, 14th January 2025: Blue Dart, South Asia's premier express air and integrated 
transportation and distribution company, has launched India's largest Low-Emission Integrated 
Operating Facility in Bijwasan, Delhi. This facility, equipped with solar power installations, 
connects multiple air and ground services between North India and the rest of the country, 
marking a significant milestone in the company's journey toward operational excellence and 
environmental stewardship. 
Strategically situated along the prominent Dwarka Expressway in Bijwasan, Delhi, the facility 
enjoys a prime location and is set to be just 20 minutes from Indira Gandhi International Airport. 
This positions it as a critical logistics hub, enhancing connectivity across the Northern region and 
beyond. With direct access to the IGI Airport, Indian Railways, metro corridors, and key national 
highways, the Bijwasan facility seamlessly integrates with the upcoming Gati Shakti Logistics 
Corridor. 
By fortifying freight movement and providing robust multi modal connectivity, this state-of-the­
art, 2.5 lakh sq. ft. facility aligns with the PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan and exemplifies 
Blue Dart's focus on advancing India's express logistics infrastructure. 
On the launch, Tobias Meyer, CEO, OHL Group, emphasized the importance of low-emission 
operations, stating, "We want to be 'the green logistics provider of choice' as sustainability is an 
increasingly vital differentiator in our industry. Supporting our customers' decarbonization 
journey remains crucial as we continue to lead in low-carbon logistics solutions. This facility 
underscores our global commitment to efficient, more sustainable logistics and reinforces our 
investment in India's economic potential." 
Highlighting the facility's strategic value, Pablo Ciano, CEO, OHL eCommerce, said, "This 
milestone demonstrates our focus on delivering superior infrastructure and innovative solutions 
that empower customers and fuel their growth. Our core principles, sustainability and 
operational excellence, remain integral to our global strategy, and this facility stands as a 
testament to that vision." 
Balfour Manuel, Managing Director, Blue Dart, added, "The Bijwasan facility is more than an 
infrastructure investment-it reflects Blue Dart's commitment to sustainability, scalability , and 
service excellence. As India's largest low-emission integrated logistics hub, it enhances our 
distribution capabilities, reinforcing our promise of efficient, more sustainable cargo movement. 
By strategically expanding our network with state-of-the-art facilities and adopting advanced 
technologies, we continue to drive flexibility, accuracy, and speed across our operations. These 
investments position Blue Dart to seize emerging opportunities and deliver exceptional value to 
our customers and stakeholders."

The facility is fully equipped for palletized cargo handling, with the capacity to handle over 5.5 
lakh shipments per day (inbound and outbound), optimizing cargo throughput for high-volume 
shipments. State-of-the-art automated systems ensure precise and rapid sorting, reducing 
manual handling and improving resource utilization to meet evolving business demands. Solar 
power installations with a 600-kW capacity are pivotal in minimizing carbon emissions and 
supporting more sustainable energy practices. 
With over 250 ground network routes and eight dedicated freighters, Blue Dart continues to 
expand its infrastructure to address India's growing logistics needs. The Bijwasan facility 
strengthens Blue Dart's position as a leading trade facilitator, supporting over 19,000 pin codes 
and more than 56,000 locations nationwide. 
Blue Dart's sustainability initiatives extend beyond operational enhancements. The company 
also offers decarbonisation options to its customers to reduce their carbon footprint via its 
GoGreen Plus service. Additionally, Blue Dart incentivizes subcontractors to adopt more 
sustainable transport solutions by providing education, standards, and financial support. The 
launch of the Bijwasan facility reaffirms Blue Dart's leadership in more sustainable logistics, 
exemplifying its focus on innovation, environmental responsibility, and reliable express logistics 
services across India.
