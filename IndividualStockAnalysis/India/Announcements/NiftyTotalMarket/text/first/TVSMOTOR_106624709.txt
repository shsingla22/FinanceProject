Birtamod , May 15, 2026 : TVS Motor Company  (TVS M) - a leading global manufacturer  in the 
two and three- wheeler segments , today  launch ed its connected passenger electric three-
wheeler, TVS King  EV MAX  in Nepal . The TVS King EV MAX brings together cutting -edge 
technology and eco-friendly innovation that will deliver a smart, sustainable and future- ready 
urban mobility solution.  The product  is designed to dominate the streets with its  unmatched 
performance and durability.  TVSM continues to drive its leadership in last -mile connectivity 
with a diverse portfolio of innovative and future- ready ICE & EV three -wheeler mobility 
solutions  across markets .  
 
With a range of 179 kms on a single charge, fast charging in just 2 hours and 15 minutes for 
0 - 80% charge and 3.5 hours for 100% charge; Best -in-class acceleration of 0- 30 kmph in 
just 3.7 seconds, smart features like TVS SmartXonnect ™, the TVS King EV MAX meet s 
consumer needs in the most economical manner, offers users  real-time navigation, alerts and 
vehicle diagnostics through their smartphones. It combines excellent performance, comfort 
and connectivity, making it a standout choice for modern urban mobility.  
 
The TVS King EV MAX is powered 
by a high- performance 11 kW 
motor,  reliable  51.2V lithium -ion 
LFP battery making it perfect for 
urban commuting. With a top 
speed of 60 km/h (ECO Mode: 40 
kmph; City: 50 kmph; Power: 60 
kmph), the vehicle ensures 
efficient travel while maximizing 
passenger comfort through its 
spacious cabin and ergonomic 
seating design.  
 
Nepal has emerged as one of the fastest -growing EV markets globally , with strong adoption 
of passenger and commercial mobility solutions , including electric three -wheelers. Due to their 
lower operating costs  and higher efficiency , three -wheelers are increasingly becoming a
