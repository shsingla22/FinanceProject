Subject: Notice for Exercise of Call Option of Basel III Compliant Additional Tier I bond of Rs. 1500 Crore 
issued on 2nd December 2021, Coupon@ 8.05% Per Annum (ISIN: INE476A08134)  
It has been our privilege to have you as a valued Investor in Canara Bank (“Bank”) Basel III Compliant 
Additional Tier I Bond (ISIN INE476A08134), issued on 2nd December 2021. As per terms of Information 
Memorandum, the Bond carry a coupon rate of 8.05% p.a. payable annually and Bank may exercise call option 
after the instrument has run for at least 5 years. 
This is to bring your kind notice that our Bank has decided to exercise the call option on 2nd December, 2026. 
Bank has received necessary approvals from the Reserve Bank of India vide their letter dated 27th October 2026. 
Accordingly, Notice is hereby given with the approval of appropriate authority, that the Bank has decided to 
exercise Call Option on the above-mentioned Bond and declared 17th November, 2026 as record date for this 
purpose.  
In this regard we wish to inform you the following: 
 The principal amount together with interest accrued for the period from 02/12/2025 to 01/12/2026 will be 
paid on 02.12.2026, and the bond will not carry any obligation from the said call option date. 
 After verification and completion of necessary formalities, the amount payable on 02/12/2026 will be 
remitted through RTGS/NEFT/Direct Credit in favor of the bond holders in the list of beneficiaries appearing 
in the records of NSDL/CDSL in case of Bonds held in demat form as at the closure of Record Date which is 
17th November 2026. 
 The Bond shall be taken as discharged on payment of interest cum principal amount by the Bank as aforesaid. 
Such payment will be a legal discharge of the liability of the Bank towards the holders of the subject bond. 
 On such payment being made, the Bank will inform NSDL/CDSL and accordingly the Bond in the account of 
the Bondholders with NSDL/CDSL will be extinguished. 
 Bank’s liability to the Bondholders towards all their rights including for the payment or otherwise shall cease 
and stand extinguished from the call option date. 
 Payment will be subject to terms contained in the Placement Memorandum.
