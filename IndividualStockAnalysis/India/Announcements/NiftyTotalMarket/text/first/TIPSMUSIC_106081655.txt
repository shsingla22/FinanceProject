Tips Music and Sterling Reserve Music Project Set to 
Revolutionize Bollywood Classics with 'Tips Take 2' 
Link- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ts1nGcsA0b0  
Mumbai, 10th December, 2024 : Tips Music Limited and Sterling Reserve 
Music Project  are set to take the music industry by storm with their musical 
revival project, Tips Take 2 . This innovative collaboration promises to breathe 
new life into Bollywood's most beloved classics, offering music lovers a fresh and 
electrifying musical experience. 
Mr Hari Nair, CEO of Tips Music Ltd ., said, "We are delighted to partner with 
Sterling Reserve to launch our first music IP "Tips Take 2", which reimagines iconic 
90s hits and creates a new take on our catalogue. The recently launched Brand & 
Partnerships division has ideated and worked on this exclusive IP. They also work 
with brands for bespoke content, in-film placements, music video brand 
integrations, and licensing our musical hits for their advertisement campaigns. We 
look forward to collaborating with brands, to redefine audience engagement 
through innovative, tailored entertainment solutions across both music and films. 
Tips Take 2 is just the beginning of our journey into branded IPs, with many more 
exciting projects on the horizon." 
The project launches with the first song, "Sambhala Hai Maine,"  on 9th 
December. At the heart of this musical reinvention is  Dev Negi the powerhouse 
voice, who takes the center stage. Joining him, on screen, are the stunning Heli 
Daruwala  and the charismatic  Parth Samthaan , who bring some serious heat 
with their electrifying chemistry. Together, they put a bold, modern spin on the 
90s classic “Sambhala Hai Maine”  – originally sung by Kumar Sanu. 
Bikram Basu, Chief Innovation and Strategy Officer, Allied Blenders and 
Distillers, shared, “Launched as a digital first property with a mix of emerging 
and established artists, and now in its sixth year, the Sterling Reserve Music 
Project is collaborating with Tips Music to reimagine timeless Bollywood hits from 
the ‘90’s in a new way. The Sterling Reserve Music Project ‘Take 2’ with Tips will 
have people cheering for sure”  
The project stands as a testament to the evolving landscape of Indian music, 
offering three carefully selected song recreations that promise to captivate 
audiences. Tips Music and Sterling Reserve Music Project continue to push creative 
boundaries, providing a platform that celebrates established artists while 
simultaneously nurturing emerging musical talents. 
                                                                                                                
The first track, " Sambhala Hai Maine,"  will be available across all major music 
platforms, promising to transport listeners to a new dimension of musical 
nostalgia.  
About TIPS Music Ltd.: 
 
TIPS is a public-listed music label in India, founded in 1988 by Mr. Kumar Taurani 
and Mr. Ramesh Taurani. Right from acquiring/ producing superhits in the 90s like 
Khalnayak, Phool Aur Kaante, Soldier, Coolie No.1, Raja Hindustani, Rangeela, 
Gupt, Pardes, Taal, Raaz, etc. to musical blockbusters films like Ajab Premi ki 
Gazab Kahani, Prince, Ramaiya Vastavaiya, Race (Franchise) and acquiring 
regional Tamil blockbusters film music like Ponniyin Selvan1 & 2. Over the 
decades, Tips Music has been home to all popular artists, including Alka Yagnik, 
Udit Narayan, Kumar Sanu, Sonu Nigam, Atif Aslam, Badshah, Diljit Dosanjh, Arijit 
Singh, A.R. Rehman, B Praak, to name a few. The label has also launched many 
new talents and played a pivotal role in shaping their musical careers. The 
company boasts an extensive catalogue of 31,000 “THE MUST HAVE HITS” songs 
and is considered to be a prominent music creator across major languages and 
genres of music from India
