In terms of Regulation 30 and 51 of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we write to inform the following: 
 
Neyveli Uttar Pradesh Power Limited (NUPPL), a Joint Venture Company of NLC India Limited 
and U.P. Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Limited, with equity shareholding in the ratio of 51:49 
respectively, is establishing a 3 x 660 MW Ghatampur Thermal Power Plant at Ghatampur, Uttar 
Pradesh. 
 
To meet the fuel requirement of the said power plant, Pachwara South Coal Block located in 
Dumka District, Jharkhand, was allotted to the Company by the Ministry of Coal, Government of 
India, vide allotment order dated 03rd October, 2016. The coal block, which was previously 
unexplored, has been fully explored by NUPPL through detailed drilling across the entire block. 
 
The Pachwara South Coal Block has an extractable coal reserve of 264.84 million tonnes, with a 
normative mining capacity of 9 million tonnes per annum and an average coal grade of G10. 
 
We are pleased to inform that, subsequent to commencement of overburden removal on 19th 
December, 2025, the Pachwara South Open Cast Project has successfully commenced coal 
production on 29th March, 2026.
