Milestone alert! HomeFirst achieves ₹10,000 Crore AUM  
Mumbai, India – May 15, 2024 – Home First Finance Company, a leading housing finance 
company in India, is pleased to share that it has crossed the milestone of Assets Under 
Management (AUM) of ₹10,000 Crore.  
Over 14 years, Home First Finance Company has dedicated itself to empowering 
individuals and families to realize their dream of owning a home. The company's 
customer-centric approach has been pivotal in attaining a robust growth trajectory 
leading to this achievement!   
"We are immensely proud to reach this significant milestone of ₹10,000 Crore AUM," said 
Manoj Viswanathan, CEO of Home First Finance Company. "This accomplishment is a 
testament to our team's hard work, dedication, and innovative spirit. It reflects our 
unwavering commitment to making homeownership more accessible to millions of 
Indians. We believe, this is just the beginning for us, and we will continue to strive towards 
excellence, ensuring that our customers receive the best possible home financing 
solutions and services." 
 
For more information about Home First Finance Company and its services, please visit 
www.homefirstindia.com .
