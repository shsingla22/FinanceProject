Pursuant to Regulation 30, 42 and other applicable Regulations of SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), we wish to inform you that the 
Board of Directors of the Bank in their meeting held today i.e., Friday, June 05, 2026 , has inter-alia, 
considered and approved the following: 
 
1. Fixation of date for 104th Annual General Meeting of the Bank  
 
The 104th Annual General Meeting (“AGM”) of the members of the Tamilnad Mercantile Bank 
Limited (the “Bank”) will be held on Tuesday, July 14, 2026, at 11:00 A.M. IST  through Video 
Conferencing (“VC”) / Other Audio-Visual Means (“OVAM”). In accordance with circulars issued 
by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and Securities Exchange Board of India (SEBI), 
shareholders will be able to attend and participate in the AGM only through VC / OAVM.  
 
The Bank will despatch/disseminate the Annual Report for the FY 2025-26 and also Notice 
convening the 104th AGM, in accordance with the provisions of the applicable laws. 
 
2. Record Date for the proposed Dividend for the FY 2025-26 
 
The proposed Dividend, if approved by the members at the AGM, will be paid on or before August 
13, 2026, to those shareholders, whose name appears on the Register of Members / Beneficial 
Owners as furnished by NSDL / CDSL as on the Record date i.e., Friday, June 12, 2026 .
