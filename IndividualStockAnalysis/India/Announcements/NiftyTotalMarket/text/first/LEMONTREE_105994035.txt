Lemon Tree Hotels signs a new property in  
Srinagar  
 
New Delhi, 20th September  2024: Lemon Tree Hotels announced its latest 
signing – Keys Select by Lemon Tree Hotel s, Srinagar . The property, which shall 
be managed by Carnation Hotels Private Limited, a wholly -owned subsidiary of 
Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, is expected to open in FY 2026.  
 
Srinagar , the summer capital of the Indian union territory of Jammu and 
Kashmir, is known for its stunning natural beauty. Situated in the Kashmir 
Valley, along the banks of the Jhelum River , Srinagar is famous for its 
picturesque landscapes, including the iconic Dal Lake and Nigeen Lake, which 
feature traditional houseboats and Shikara rides. The cit y is also known for its 
Mughal g ardens, such as Shalimar Bagh and Nishat Bagh, showcasing beautiful 
terraced lawns and fountains. With a rich cultural heritage and a blend of 
Kashmiri, Mughal, and Persian influences, Srinagar is a popular tourist 
destination.  
 
Keys Select by Lemon Tree H otels, Srinagar  will feature 40 well-appointed 
rooms, two restaurant s, a meeting room, and a fitness center . The Sheikh Ul 
Alam International Airport  is about 9 kms from the property . The hotel is also 
well connected by roadways for both public and private transport.  
 
Mr. Vilas Pawar, CEO — Managed & Franchise Business, Lemon Tree Hotels said 
on the occasion , “We are excited to share the expansion of our portfolio in 
Srinagar , where we currently have one  existing propert y. This addition will 
complement our existing four hotels and one upcoming propert y in the state .”
