LatentView Analytics Invests $3m in Healtheon AI  
  
Strategic investment in an Agentic AI start -up targeting the  
healthcare Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) sector  
  
Chennai, April 2, 2026  –LatentView Analytics  (BSE: 543398, NSE: LATENTVIEW),  an AI -driven 
analytics, data engineering, and consulting firm, has completed a $3M investment in Healtheon 
AI, a company at the forefront of the Agentic AI revolution in healthcare Revenue Cycle 
Management (RCM) and a leader in building collaborative healthcare specific Agentic AI 
teammates that automate complex tasks and improve efficiency.  
 
“This investment is a strategic step forward in line with our AI vision of prioritizing advanced 
technology and proven expertise to deliver maximum value to our clients,” said Rajan 
Sethuraman, CEO, LatentView Analytics. “With this investment, we are not o nly investing in 
healthcare; we are investing in an Agentic AI solution that solves a healthcare problem. 
LatentView will now be a preferred partner for the deployment of Healtheon AI  with its 
customers.”  
 
“We are thrilled to welcome LatentView as an investor and strategic partner in Healtheon AI”, 
said Nick van Terheyden, MD, CEO of Healtheon AI. “LatentView is a leader in the data 
analytics and AI space, and they have a clear, strategic vision to bring the  latest in AI to their 
clients. Healtheon AI will build on LatentView’s global data analytics capability to deliver a 
unique cognitive multi -agent platform built specifically for healthcare Revenue Cycle 
Management (RCM). We look forward to growing with th is partnership.”  
 
Unlike traditional automation tools, Healtheon AI’s architecture deploys specialized, role -based 
AI agents working together in a decentralized, fault -tolerant system, capable of handling the 
complexity and high -stakes nature of healthcare finance. From eli gibility verification and prior 
authorization to medical coding, claims processing, and denial remediation, Healtheon AI’s 
agents integrate seamlessly with human teams to navigate clinical, financial, and regulatory 
workflows with precision and resilience.  The system is  built to deploy at scale for real -time 
processing, with performance contracts anchored in quantifiable outcomes and guaranteed 
reduction in RCM labor costs and denial leakage. The partnership will deploy "AI workers" that 
predict, prescribe, and act in an  explainable and compliant manner, reducing administrative 
waste.  
 
Rajan Venkatesan, CFO, LatentView Analytics, said, “We are happy to successfully complete 
this strategic $3M single tranche investment via a SAFE note. This investment is in line with 
our strategy of a build -invest -partner approach to become an AI -first an d AI-led organization. 
We see significant synergies from this partnership.”  
 
Sridhar Obilisetty, who serves on the Board of Directors at Healtheon AI, added, “The strategic 
synergy between LatentView and Healtheon AI is unique in the industry. By combining 
Healtheon AI’s autonomous, agentic AI innovations purpose built for healthca re with 
LatentView’s leading horizontal analytics expertise, we are creating a highly differentiated 
 
 
partnership. Together, we are positioned to deliver value across the entire healthcare 
ecosystem, including providers, patients, health systems, and payors. We are excited to partner 
with LatentView as we expand our footprint and accelerate impact.”
