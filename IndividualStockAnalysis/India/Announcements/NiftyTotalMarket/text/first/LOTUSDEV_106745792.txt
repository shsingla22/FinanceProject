Press Release 
Estimated GDV of INR 550 Crs. for the redevelopment project at Shivaji Park 
Beach Front
Mumbai,  14th August  2026
Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Limited,  a developer  of luxury  and  ultra  luxury  residential  
and  commercial  properties  in Mumbai,  with  a focus  in redevelopment  & joint  
development  , today  announced  its appointment  as the preferred  developer  for a 
prominent  residential  redevelopment  project  at Shivaji  Park  Beach  Front , Dadar .
The Company  has  been  appointed  as the preferred  developer  by a housing  society  
for the redevelopment  of its residential  property,  located  on the Shivaji  Park  beach  
front . The project  enjoys  an unobstructed,  sought -after  address  along  one  of 
Mumbai's  most  iconic  stretches,  offering  residents  uninterrupted  sea  views  of the 
Arabian  Sea .
The project  has  an estimated  GDV  of INR 550  Crs., with  a saleable  area  of 
approximately  44,000  sq. ft. The Company  expects  the project  to commence  by the 
end  of FY28 or Q1 FY29, with  completion  targeted  within  approximately  3 to 3.5 years  
from  commencement .
This  appointment  reflects  the growing  strength  and  recognition  of the Lotus  
Developers  brand,  and  the Company's  increasing  acceptance  among  housing  
societies  in newer,  high -value  micro -markets  across  Mumbai .
Mr. Anand  K Pandit,  Chairman  & Managing  Director,  Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  
Limited,  said,  "We  are delighted  to be entrusted  with  this  redevelopment  
opportunity  at Shivaji  Park  Beachfront,  one  of Mumbai's  most  sought -after  
residential  addresses . Being  chosen  by the society  reaffirms  the trust  that  
homeowners  continue  to place  in the Lotus  brand  when  it comes  to reimagining  
where  they  live.
Redevelopment  is fast  becoming  the preferred  path  for ageing  housing  societies  in 
the city's  finest  neighbourhoods,  as residents  look  to modernise  their  homes  without  
compromising  on location . Shivaji  Park  Beachfront's  uninterrupted  sea  view  makes  
this  a particularly  special  project  for us, and  one  that  fits naturally  into  our growing  
portfolio  of Coastline  Luxury  Collection  across  Mumbai .
As part  of our broader  growth  strategy,  we are consciously  expanding  into  newer  
micro -markets,  but always  with  a discerning  eye — seeking  out addresses  that  offer  
something  truly  distinctive,  be it an unobstructed  sea  view  or a lush,  green  outlook . 
It is this  scarcity  and  setting  that  allow  us to create  homes  our residents  will 
genuinely  value,  and  that  command  a rightful  premium  in the market .” Sri Lotus Developers Appointed as the Developer for Residential

2About  Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Limited
Incorporated  in 2015  (formerly  AKP Holdings  Limited),  Sri Lotus  Developers  & Realty  Ltd. is a 
Mumbai -based  real estate  developer  specializing  in luxury  and ultra -luxury  residential  and 
premium  commercial  projects,  with  a strong  focus  on redevelopment  in the western  suburbs  
of Mumbai . The company  has completed  4 projects  aggregating  over  4.2 lakh  sq. ft. of 
carpet  area,  with  8 ongoing  and 15 upcoming  developments  covering  ~49.6 lakh  sq. ft. of 
carpet  area  and ~39.6 lakh  sq. ft. of saleable  area .
With  a strong  presence  in Juhu  and Andheri  West . the company  has also  expanded  into 
other  micro  markets  of Mumbai  like South  and Central  Mumbai,  Bandra,  and Versova . Lotus  
Developers  manages  end-to-end execution —from  design  and construction  to sales  and 
customer  engagement —ensuring  consistent  quality,  superior  materials,  and customer  
satisfaction . Guided  by its philosophy  of creating  inclusive  and high -quality  living  spaces,  
the company  is shaping  some  of the most  coveted  addresses  in Mumbai’s  real estate  
landscape .
