Tata Consultancy Services  has been recognised for its AI -First delivery, Proprietary IP, Investment Velocity, 
Outcome -Aligned Commercials, Observability, and Production Scalability.  
MUMBAI, November 17, 2026: Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS), a global leader in IT 
services, consulting, and business solutions, has been recogni sed as a Leader in the HFS Services -as-Software 
(SaS) Star Ratings 2026 for Agentic Application Management Services (AMS). TCS was positioned highest 
among the 33 agentic AMS vendors  evaluated in this report.  
The HFS Research report  drew on more than 400 buyer evaluations that assessed  AI-First delivery, 
Proprietary IP, Investment Velocity, Outcome -Aligned Commercials, Observability, and Production 
Scalabi lity. It examined  how service providers combined  AI agents with  human expertise  to manage 
enterprise applications.  
The report highlight ed TCS’ AI -powered automation, strong governance, and its ecosystem of strategic 
partnerships spanning hyperscalers, enterprise platform providers, and leading AI innovators , as key 
strengths. Enterprise buyers participating in the assessment recogni sed TCS for its delivery discipline, 
scalability, and proactive problem management.  
Additionally, t he report showcased TCS’  five-level Human+AI Service Autonomy Model ™ that is underpinned 
by an extensive portfolio of proprietary offerings  and capabilities . HFS Research also cited TCS ’ Agentic AMS  
vision focused on reducing run costs, improving operational efficiency, and accelerating issue resolution,  as 
a key differentiator.  
Phil Fersht, CEO and Chief Analyst, HFS Research , said, “TCS  rates among the SaS Star Leaders on the 
fundamentals enterprises rely on for agentic AMS at scale, agents that integrate into existing ITSM without 
forcing new ways of working, and a delivery model that extends from a single queue to a full estate with 
outcomes baselined and tracked. Buyers credit its depth of contextual knowledge, disciplined governance and 
production -grade scale, underpinned by a mature au tomation stack spanning  ignio , TCS Cogni x and the newer 
TCS AI WisdomNext  platform. TCS is rated highly  by enterprise customers for its capabilities that they trust to 
run and scale the estate dependably .” 
“AI agents are becoming an integral part of enterprise technology operations, working alongside people to 
make services more proactive, resilient and reliable,” said K Krithivasan, Chief Executive Officer and 
Managing Director, TCS . “Our Human+AI  Service Autonomy Model for AMS brings together agentic AI with 
our deep understanding of clients’ technology landscapes and existing investments to deliver greater 
autonomy and better business outcomes. This recognition reflects our continued focus on AI -powered services 
transformation, an integral part of TCS’ five -pillar AI strategy.”  
As enterprise AI moves beyond experimentation to large -scale adoption across business operations and 
transformation programs, including agentic AI initiatives, TCS is helping customers  navigate this shift through 
its Infrastructure -to-Intelligence strategy. This full -stack AI approach is supported by a portfolio of proprietary 
offerings, including:  
• ignio™ AIOps:  It helps enterprises streamline and optimize IT operations by combining unified 
observability, AI -powered insights, and closed -loop automation across hybrid and multi -cloud 
environments.

About Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)  
Tata Consultancy Services (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS) is the technology partner of choice for industry -leading 
organizations worldwide. Since its inception in 1968, TCS has upheld the highest standards of innovation, 
engineering excellence and customer service . 
It has set an aspiration to become the world's largest AI -led technology services company and is enabling its 
clients to transform themselves across the full AI stack, from infrastructure to intelligence.  
Rooted in the heritage of the Tata Group, TCS is focused on creating long term value for its clients, its 
investors, its employees, and the community at large. With a highly skilled workforce spread across 56 
countries and 194 service delivery centers  across the world, the company has been recognized as a top 
employer in six continents. With the ability to rapidly apply and scale new technologies, the company has 
built long term partnerships with its clients. Many of these relationships have endured in to decades and 
navigated every technology cycle, from mainframes in the 1970s to artificial intelligence today.  
TCS sponsors 14 of the world’s most prestigious marathons and endurance events, including the TCS New 
York City Marathon, TCS London Marathon, Tata Mumbai Marathon, and TCS Sydney Marathon with a focus
