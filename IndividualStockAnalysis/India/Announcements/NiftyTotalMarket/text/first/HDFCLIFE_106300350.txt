based on  the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board
noted and approved the following changes in the Key Managerial Personnel viz. Company
Secretary and Compliance Officer of the Company :

Authority of India (IRDAI), and in line with the principles outlined in the Master Circular  
on Corporate Governance for Insurers, issued by the IRDAI, which inter alia, mandates

General Counsel, Chief Compliance Officer and Company Secretary, will cease to hold  
the role of Company Secretary upon end of business hours on July 16, 2025. Mr Gangan 
shall continue to serve as General Counsel, and Chief Compliance Officer under IRDAI  
Regulations.

Company Secretary  (Key Managerial Personnel)  and Compliance Officer, in terms of  SEBI
Listing Regulations and SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, with
effect from July 17, 2025.
