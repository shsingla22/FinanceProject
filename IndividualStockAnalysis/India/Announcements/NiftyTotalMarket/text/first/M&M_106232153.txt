External Investors Show Conviction in M&M's Growth Gem, CLPL
Mumbai, December 13, 2023: Classic Legends Private Limited (CLPL), an identified growth gem 
for Mahindra &Mahindra (M&M), owns iconic brands including Jawa, Yezdiand BSA, and operates 
in the fast-growing premium motorcycle segment in India. External investors, along with M&M will 
invest Rs. 875 Crore (investors Rs. 350 Crore andM&M Rs. 525 Crore) over the next 2-3 years, to 
build a strong business that leverages the strengths of M&M and its partners.
