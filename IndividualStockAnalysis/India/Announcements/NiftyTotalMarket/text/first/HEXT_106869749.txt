Hexaware Recognized as a Challenger in the 2026 Gartner® 
Magic Quadrant ™ for Service Integration and Management 
Services  
November 24, 2026:  Hexaware Technologies (NSE: HEXT), a global provider of IT solutions and 
services, today announced it has been recognized as a Challenger in the 2026 Gartner® Magic 
Quadrant ™ for Service Integration and Management Services.   
  
Hexaware believes this recognition reﬂects its focus on end -to-end visibility across the supplier 
ecosystem and outcomes tied to user experience and business goals, supported by AI -led operations 
that enable enterprises to move from managing incidents to eliminating them.   
 
The company’s proprietary AI -driven Tensai® platform uniﬁes multi -vendor telemetry and automates 
workﬂows to rapidly stabilize supplier estates.   
 
 Gartner, Magic Quadrant for Service Integration and Management Services, by Andrea Lanzavecchia, Joe Trejo, Jess Aggio, 
21 October 2026  
 
 

 
 
"We see service integration as a path to Zero Friction, not merely a coordination function, " said Girish 
Ravindran, Executive Vice President & Global Head –  Digital IT Operations, Hexaware. "We believe 
being recognized as a Challenger validates that vision. Our experience -led governance and AI -driven 
operations give clients end -to-end visibility and clear accountability across every supplier, along with 
a measurable route from managing incide nts to eliminating them. "
