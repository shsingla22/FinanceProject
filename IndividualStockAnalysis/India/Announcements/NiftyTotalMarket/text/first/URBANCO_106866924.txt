PRESS RELEASE
Urban Company raises 2,500 crore through ₹  
Qualified Institutions Placement
Issue oversubscribed over 6x; over 15,000 crore of demand from ₹ marquee domestic and 
global institutional investors
Gurugram, November 20, 2026  – Urban Company Limited (NSE: URBANCO), India’s  
leading  home  services  platform,  yesterday  announced  the  successful  completion  of  its  
Qualified Institutions Placement (“QIP”) of equity shares, raising 2,500 crore. The QIP drew ₹  
strong, diversified participation from marquee domestic and global institutional investors.
The issue opened on November 16, 2026 and closed on November 19, 2026. On November  
19, 2026, the Fund Raise Committee of the Board approved the allotment of 16,02,56,410  
equity shares of face value 1 each at an issue price of 156 per share. This is at 3.1% ₹ ₹  
discount to the floor price of 161.03 per share, calculated under Chapter VI of the SEBI (Issue ₹  
of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018.
Key highlights
●Total demand of over 15,000 crore, about 6x the issue size. ₹
●Bids from more than 60 investors; shares allotted to 33 investors, 13 of them did not  
hold Urban Company shares as on the date of the issue.
●Mutual funds were allotted over 50% of the issue. Allottees include 13 mutual  
funds, among them SBI MF, ICICI Prudential MF, Mirae Asset MF, Invesco MF,  
WhiteOak Capital MF, Motilal Oswal MF, Aditya Birla Sun Life MF, Kotak MF,  
Nippon India MF, UTI MF, Edelweiss MF, Bandhan MF and JM Financial MF.
●Domestic  insurers including HDFC Life, Bajaj Life Insurance and Edelweiss Life.
●Global institutional investors include Goldman Sachs Asset Management, Pictet,  
Amundi, Norges Bank Investment Management, Arohi Asset Management and  
Manulife, Dymon Asia and Millennium.
●Domestic institutional investors like Premji Invest participated.

●Building the supply ecosystem for ‘InstaHelp by UC’;
●Brand marketing and business promotion to increase awareness and visibility of  
the platform across segments;

About Urban Company:
Urban Company Limited (NSE: URBANCO) is a technology-driven platform that connects  
customers with trusted professionals for home and beauty needs - from cleaning and repairs to  
salon and spa services. As per Urban Company’s H1 FY27 Earnings Index, average monthly  
net earnings in-hand reached 30,708 for all active service professionals (Ex InstaHelp by ₹  
UC), while the top 5% of service professionals earned 56,731. In addition, all active service ₹  
professionals  are  covered  under  group  life  and  accidental  insurance,  which  includes  
accidental life and disability cover of up to 10 lakh, along with free accidental hospitalisation ₹  
and OPD treatment coverage that scales with their reward tier, among other benefits.
