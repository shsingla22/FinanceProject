HCLTech featured among  the top 50  global companies  in Forbes  World’s Top 
Companies for Women 2026   
 
NOIDA, India, Nov . 12, 2026  — HCLTech , a leading global technology company, has been 
featured among the top 50  companies in the  Forbes' list of World's Top Companies for 
Women  2026 .  
 
HCLTech is the only India -headquartered company among the top 50 in the ranking of over 
400 global companies based on a survey of 140,000 women professionals on three principal 
themes : employer brand, public opinion and leadership. This is the second consecutive year 
that HCLTech has been featured in Forbes' list of World's Top Companies for Women .  
 
“This recognition for the second year in a row reflects HCLTech’s commitment to  career 
advancement for women and building a workplace anchored in  our core value of inclusion, 
which is focused on  creating an environment where everyone can succeed and do their best 
work . We are combining an AI -driven talent strategy with continued investments in learning, 
career development and mentorship to create more equitable opportunities for growth  and 
enabling women to advance their careers ,” said Ramachandran  Sundararajan, Chief People 
Officer, HCLTech.  
 
With women representing 50% of its board and initiatives such as Allyship and the Women 
Connect Network Employee Resource Group (ERG) , HCLTech remains committed to  helping 
foster an inclusive culture . The company’s  Women Career Development Programs  support 
women employees at  every stage of their career , while its Second Career Return Program 
enables women from diverse backgrounds  to re‑enter the workforce. Programs like these 
ensured that HCLTech had a 99.88% return -to-work rate  for women employees who  took 
parental leave in FY26 .
