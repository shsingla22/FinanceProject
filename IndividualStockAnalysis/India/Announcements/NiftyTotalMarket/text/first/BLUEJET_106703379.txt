Blue Jet Healthcare Awarded EcoVadis Silver Medal  
for Sustainability Excellence,  
Ranks Among the Top 15% Globally  
 
Mumbai, July 20, 202 6: Blue Jet Healthcare Limited  today announced that it has  been 
awarded “ Silver Medal” by EcoVadis for its outstanding sustainability management practices 
in areas such as Environment, Labor and Human Rights, Ethics and Sustainable Procurement. 
This prestigious achievement places blue Jet  amongst the top 15% of companies with a 90th 
percentile ranking  in sustainability performance  evaluated by EcoVadis.  
EcoVadis evaluates companies on four key areas  viz... environment , labor  and human rights, 
ethics, and sustainable procurement.  Blue Jet Health achieved an EcoVadis Sustainability Score 
of 77 out of 100  and earning  the EcoVadis Silver Medal.  
 
Commenting on the achievement , Mr. Shiven Akshay Arora , Managing Director said -             
“This achievement of the EcoVadis Silver Medal marks a significant milestone in our 
sustainability journey. Having previously earned the EcoVadis Committed Badge, this 
recognition reflects the meaningful progress , we have made in strengthening our 
environmental, social, ethical, and governance practices across  our operations.  
This recognition is a testament to the collective efforts of our employees, business partners, 
and stakeholders who continuously strive to embed environmental, social, and governance 
(ESG) principles into every aspect of our operations. ” 
 
About Blue Jet Healthcare  
Established in 1968, Blue Jet Healthcare is a manufacturer of specialty chemicals and 
pharmaceutical intermediates serving global pharmaceutical companies. The Company 
operates with a focus on quality, compliance, and governance -led growth.
