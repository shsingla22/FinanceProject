RediffPay Initiates CUG Testing Ahead of UPI Launch, 
Post Securing TPAP License from NPCI  
 
Set to Launch India’s First Financial Wellness–Focused UPI App Soon 
 
Gandhinagar/Mumbai, December 29, 2025  — Rediff.com India Ltd. announced that it has received final 
approval from the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) for its Third-Party Application Provider 
(TPAP) license, paving the way for the rollout of its digital payments platform, RediffPay. With this 
approval, the company has commenced Closed User Group (CUG) testing—an essential step before entering 
the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) ecosystem—and is preparing to launch India’s first UPI app built 
around financial-wellness-customer-centric UPI payment app designed to encourage saving, investing and 
responsible access to credit. 
 
On Monday, the company commenced Closed User Group (CUG) testing as it prepares to enter the Unified 
Payments Interface (UPI) ecosystem. “This approval is a significant milestone for Rediff,” said Mr Vishal 
Mehta, Chairman and Managing Director, Rediff.com India Ltd. “It enables us to extend secure, 
interoperable UPI services through RediffPay and advances our mission to broaden financial inclusion while 
delivering simple, intuitive digital payment experiences for users across India.” 
 
RediffPay aims to blend traditional UPI capabilities with a broader financial wellness and customer centric 
proposition. Beyond standard bill payments, mobile recharges, and cross-app UPI interoperability payments, 
the app will encourage better financial habits through curated savings and investment products—such as 
equities, mutual funds (MFs), fixed deposits (FDs), recurring deposits (RDs) and similar other products—
alongside a pathway to quick credit, for individuals and merchants seeking convenience of UPI payments, 
credit access and savings at their fingertips. 
 
The RediffPay platform will also support Credit Line on UPI, expanding access to formal credit—
particularly in Tier-2, Tier-3 and smaller towns and cities. Under NPCI’s Credit Line on UPI framework, 
users can make merchant payments of up to INR 1,00,000 per day and withdraw up to INR 10,000 in cash 
via pre-approved credit lines, helping bring new-to-credit consumers into the formal economy through 
micro-credit for everyday purchases. 
 
To further deepen inclusion, RediffPay plans to onboard small, micro, mid and large merchants across 
Indian geography by offering RediffPay UPI QR-code-enabled point-of-sale (POS) solutions, integrating 
merchant credit demand into its UPI ecosystem. 
 
“We are going beyond a traditional UPI payments app,” Mr Mehta  said. “RediffPay is designed to make 
saving and accessing credit as seamless as making a payment. Financial wellness is as important as health 
wellness, and we aim to reshape how people perceive UPI—moving from spending alone to saving, 
investing and inclusive growth.” 
 
RediffPay is positioned as a wealth-multiplier and a financial-wellness-customer-centric UPI payment app 
by encouraging saving and investment promoting messaging within the app. The TPAP license marks 
Rediff’s strategic entry into the consumer-facing digital payments market. Following the TPAP approval, the 
company plans to launch India’s first purely Indian-owned UPI payment app. Axis Bank will act as the 
Payment System Provider (PSP) bank for RediffPay. 
 
Rediff.com India Ltd. is a subsidiary of Infibeam Avenues Ltd (proposed to be renamed-- AvenuesAI Ltd.), 
which acquired a controlling stake in Rediff in 2024 and has since increased its holding to 82%. The 
company aims to serve first its existing base of 60 million unique Rediff visitors and email users. It will 
offer localized email services compliant with the DPDP Act and provide multilingual UPI to reach the next

About Rediff.com: 
    
Founded in 1996, Rediff.com is one of India’s most iconic digital brands, with a long-standing presence across online content, communication, and 
enterprise services. Headquartered in Mumbai, Rediff is reimagining its platform to meet the needs of a rapidly digitizing, AI-driven economy. 
 
Rediff is transforming into an AI-first digital platform company, focused on building scalable, intelligent services across content, payments, 
enterprise-grade email communications, and business productivity, to serve consumers, businesses, and institutions. 
 
Rediff’s digital content platforms deliver timely news, business and market insights, interviews, opinion, sports, entertainment, and real-time data 
experiences across devices. In addition to its content portfolio, Rediff offers enterprise-grade email and communication services to businesses, 
institutions, and government organizations. 
 
As part of its next growth strategy, the company is scaling new product verticals including RediffPay, a UPI-based digital payments platform; 
RediffOne, an integrated AI-powered business suite offering enterprise applications such as email, ERP, HRMS, CRM, productivity tools, and e-
commerce capabilities; and Rediffmail Enterprise, a secure and scalable enterprise-grade email solution. The company has also introduced an 
innovative platform — the Global Agentic AI Marketplace — allowing users (companies, individuals, or developers) to build, buy, and operate AI 
agents. 
 
With a renewed focus on artificial intelligence, cloud-native architecture, and platform innovation, Rediff is building the foundation for the next 
generation of digital services in India.

payments and platform businesses, catering to merchants, enterprises, corporations, governments, and financial institutions in both domestic and 
international markets.  Infibeam Avenues’ international operations span the United Arab Emirates, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Australia, and the 
United States of America, with additional presence in Oman, where it partners with three of the country’s largest banks.
