LiteStyle by PNG Jewellers Debuts in Chhatrapati 
Sambhajinagar with a Fresh Retail Experience 
 
Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, 23rd September 2025:  PNG Jewellers proudly announced the 
launch of its LiteStyle shop-in-shop in their Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar store, bringing a 
refreshing dimension of lightweight, stylish fine jewellery to customers in the city. The new 
counter, introduced within an existing PNG Jewellers store, showcases the brand’s versatile 
designs aimed at modern, independent women who prefer everyday elegance. 
The launch marks a new chapter in PNG’s expansion strategy of combining tradition with 
contemporary retail formats. With LiteStyle, customers can explore jewellery that is 
lightweight, versatile, and perfect for work, casual outings, or festive occasions. 
At the inauguration, actress Sonalee Kulkarni  expressed her delight, saying, “LiteStyle is such 
a thoughtful concept. As women, we often look for jewellery that is practical, elegant, and 
comfortable to wear daily. This launch will surely empower women in Chhatrapati 
Sambhajinagar to align their aspirations in jewellery with Litestyle’s.”  
The launch offer includes up to 50% off on LiteStyle jewellery making charges , making this 
festive season the perfect time to own these trend-forward designs.
