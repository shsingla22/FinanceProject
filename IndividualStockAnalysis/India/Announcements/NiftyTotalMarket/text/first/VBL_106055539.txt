1. Transaction Overview - Tanzania
➢SBC Beverages  Tanzania  Limited , Tanzania  (SBCT)  is engaged  in the 
business  of manufacturing  and distribution  of PepsiCo  branded  non-
alcoholic  beverages  in the territory  of Tanzania .
➢The proposed  transaction  is for acquisition  of 100% stake  in the business  
conducted  by SBCT , subject  to regulatory  and other  approvals  (if any)  
including  but not limited  to PepsiCo  Inc., Fair Competition  Commission  
(FCC)  Tanzania,  Tanzania  Revenue  Authority  (TRA) .
➢Proposed  transaction  is at an Equity  value  of ~ USD 154.50 mn  (~INR  
13,040 mn); 1 USD = 84.40 INR. 
➢SBCT  achieved  net revenue  of TZS 474,939 mn (~ INR 15,053 mn) and 
sales  volume  of 74mn 8oz cases  in FY ending  Mar’ 24; 1 INR = 31.55 TZS.
➢SBCT  manufactures  and sells the PepsiCo  franchised  brands : Pepsi,  Pepsi  
Max,  7UP, 7UP FREE,  Mirinda , Mountain  Dew  and Evervess . 
➢SBCT  also manufactures  and sells energy  drink  under  its own  brand  Supa  
Komando .
➢SBCT  has five manufacturing  facilities  located  at one each  in Dar-es-
Salaam,  Mbeya,  Arusha  and two in Mwanza .
➢There  are total  12 beverage  manufacturing  lines  - 9 for RGB and 3 for 
PET.
