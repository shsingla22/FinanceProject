Pursuant to Regulation 30  of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”), we 
wish to inform you that the Company has come to know about  action initiated by the Food 
and Drug Administration (FDA), Maharashtra, concerning certain stock of Patanjali Cow 
Ghee held by one of its super distributor/Food Business Operators (FBO) in Maharashtra.  
 
 
In this regard, we wish to place the following facts on record:  
 
1. FDA communication not addressed to the Company  
 
The communication/order in relation to the identified stock of Patanjali Cow Ghee was 
issued to and received by the concerned super distributor/FBO. The said communication 
has not been addressed or issued directly to the Company. The Company has been 
infor med of the matter by the concerned FBO and has obtained a copy of the 
communication for its examination.  
 
2. Sampling of Products and Status of Laboratory Reports  
 
As per the information and documents made available to the Company, FDA officials 
collected samples of the concerned batches of Patanjali Cow Ghee for laboratory 
testing.  
 
However, as on the date of this disclosure, neither the Company nor the concerned FBO 
has received any authenticated laboratory test report establishing that the samples have 
failed the prescribed quality parameters.  
 
 
 
It is important to clarify that the collection of samples for testing, by itself, does not 
establish adulteration, substandard quality or any violation of applicable food safety 
standards.  
 
 
3. Direction regarding the Identified Stock  
 
As per the communication received by the concerned FBO, directions have been issued 
regarding the identified stock, including restriction on its sale pending further action.  
 
As per the communication available with the Company , the sampling of the product and 
directions concerning the stock does not, by itself, contain any confirmed laboratory 
  
 
 
  
 
 
 
 
finding establishing adulteration or failure of prescribed quality standards.  
 
4. Company's commitment to quality and regulatory compliance  
 
The Company reiterates its commitment to maintaining the highest standards of product 
quality, consumer safety and regulatory compliance.  
 
The Company is taking cognizance of the matter and is seeking relevant details and 
supporting documents from the concerned FBO and the regulatory authorities. The 
Company is fully willing to cooperate with the authorities and facilitate independent 
verific ation of the concerned batches, as may be required.  
 
In the event that any authenticated laboratory report establishes a deviation from the 
prescribed quality standards, the Company shall examine the findings and take 
appropriate corrective action in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
 
5. Company's position and further developments  
 
The Company believes that any conclusion regarding the quality or conformity of the 
product should be based on authenticated laboratory findings and established 
regulatory procedures.  
 
At present, in the absence of any laboratory report establishing failure of the prescribed 
quality parameters, it would be premature to conclude that the concerned product is 
adulterated or substandard.  
 
The Company is closely monitoring the matter and shall make further disclosures to the 
Stock Exchanges, as may be required under applicable SEBI regulations, upon receipt of 
any material developments or further directions from the concerned regulatory 
auth orities.  
 
The Company remains committed to transparency, consumer confidence and 
compliance with all applicable statutory and regulatory requirements .
