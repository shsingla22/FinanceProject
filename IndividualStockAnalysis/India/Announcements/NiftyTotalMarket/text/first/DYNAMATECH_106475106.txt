Profile of Air Chief Marshal V. R. Chaudhari (Retd.) 
Air Chief Marshal V. R. Chaudhari (Retd.) is an alumnus of the National Defence 
Academy (61st Course), the Air Force Academy, and the Defence Services Staff 
College, Wellington. He was commissioned into the Flying Branch of the Indian Air 
Force as a fighter pilot on 29 December 1982 and is a Qualified Flying Instructor with 
over 3,650 hours of accident-free flying. During his early and mid-career, he served in 
several key instructional and operational roles, including as a member of the first 
formation aerobatics team on the Kiran Mk-II, Flight Commander of a fighter squadron, 
and Air Force Examiner on all MiG variants and trainer aircraft. 
He took command of a fighter squadron on 17 September 2001 during a period of 
heightened operational alert and successfully led the unit through Operation Parakram. 
Despite limited availability of pilots and aircraft, he deployed the squadron across three 
bases and achieved full operational readiness in record time. Over the course of his 
operational duties, he flew more than 150 sorties, including day and night ORP 
missions, demonstrating exceptional leadership and operational competence. 
In addition to his command responsibilities, he made significant contributions to the 
development of tactics for Beyond Visual Range (BVR) Vs BVR missile engagements 
and UAV interception procedures in coordination with Tactics and Air Combat 
Development and Training Establishment (TACDE) and enabled Dissimilar Air Combat 
Training for MiG-21 squadrons, strengthening overall combat effectiveness . 
In recognition of his distinguished and exceptional service to the nation, he was 
awarded the Vayu Sena Medal in 2004, the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal in 2015, and the 
Param Vishisht Seva Medal in 2021.
