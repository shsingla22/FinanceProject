     MOIL Registers Best ever September Production  
Achieves 10.3 % production growth in Q2 FY’26  
 
MOIL delivered an impressive operational performance in September 2025 and Q2 
FY’26, driven by strong growth across key parameters.  
Perfor mance Highlights: September 2025 vs September 2024  
 Best ever September Production  of 1.52 lakh tonnes , registering a growth of 
3.8%  over corresponding period last year (CPLY).  
 Exploratory Core Drilling surged  significantly,  reaching 5,314 meters , a 
remarkable growth of 46%, demonstrating MOIL’s strong focus on expanding its 
resource base.  
Quarterly Performance: July –Septemb er 2025 vs July –September 2024  
 Best ever Q2 Production  of 4.42 lakh tonnes , higher by 10.3%  over CPLY . 
 Sales  increased to 3.53 lakh tonnes , achieving an impressive growth of 18.6% .  
 Best ever Q2 Exploratory Core Drilling of 21,035  meters , showing a growth of 
4.1%  over CPLY.  
Commenting on the performance, Shri Ajit Kumar Saxena, CMD, MOIL, said:  
“The growth achiev ed across key parameters  underscores MOIL’s commitment to 
strengthening its operational excellence and ensuring long -term sustainability.  With continued 
emphasis on exploration  in all its operating mines , MOIL is well positioned to enhance its 
reserves /resource to main tain lead ership in the manganese sector. ”
