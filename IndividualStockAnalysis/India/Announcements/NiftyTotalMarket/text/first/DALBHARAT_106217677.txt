Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
This is in continuation of our earlier disclosure dated August 11, 2024, made pursuant to Regulation 
30 of the Listing Regulations, regarding an Order received by Dalmia Cement (Bharat) Limited (“ DCBL”), 
wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, under Section 74 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 
2017 and Bihar Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 from the Deputy Commissioner State Tax, Special 
Circle, Patna, Bihar, determining tax of Rs. 66,96,277/- along with interest and penalty amount of  
Rs. 66,96,277/- due to ineligible claim of Input Tax Credit on tax invoices issued by supplier, who has 
allegedly not paid outward tax liability from October 2019 to January 2020. 
 
In this regard, DCBL had preferred an appeal against the aforesaid Order and the Additional 
Commissioner State Tax (Appeal), Central Division, Patna, Bihar, has now passed an Order confirming 
the aforesaid demand of tax along with interest and penalty. 
 
The Order was received on April 30, 2025 at 07:04 P.M.  
 
DCBL has good grounds to contest and will file 2nd appeal against the said Order before the Appellate 
Authorities within prescribed timelines.
