Grasim Industries Recognised as a Sustainable Organisation 2024 by 
Times Now - Global Sustainability Alliance 
 
Grasim Industries Ltd., a flagship company of the Aditya Birla Group was honoured at the  
3rd edition of the Times Now Global Sustainability Alliance - Sustainable Organisation 2024 
event. Pavan Jain, Chief Financial Officer of Grasim Industries, received the Sustainable 
Organisation 2024 trophy and Surya Valluri, Chief Sustainability Officer, accepted the 
certificate. 
The Global Sustainability Alliance is a platform for global change-makers, individuals, and 
organizations who have championed sustainability. The 3rd edition of Times Now Sustainable 
Organisations 2024 aims to highlight and promote futuristic actions by sustainability leaders, 
celebrating their success stories, inspirations, and achievements in fostering responsible 
businesses and a greener future. 
Ipsos, one of the largest market research companies operating in 90 markets with more than 
20,000 professionals, served as the research partner for identifying the Sustainable 
Organisations of India. Following strict criteria, from 300 identified sustainable organisations, 
40 companies were recognised at the 3rd edition of the Times Now Global Sustainability 
Alliance - Sustainable Organisations 2024. 
    
 
 

About Grasim Industries Limited 
Grasim Industries Limited, a flagship company of the Aditya Birla Group, ranks amongst the top publicly listed 
companies in India. Incorporated in 1947, it started as a textiles manufacturer in India. Today, it has evolved into a 
leading diversified player with leadership presence across many sectors. It is a leading global producer of Cellulosic 
Fibres, Diversified Chemicals, Fashion Yarn and Fabrics producer in India. Implementing next phase of transformational 
growth journey, the company has entered paints business under the brand name ‘Birla Opus’. Out of the six plants to 
be set-up for manufacturing decorative paints across pan India locations, three plants commenced operations in 
Apr’24. Leveraging the Group synergies, Grasim has launched ‘Birla Pivot’, the B2B online marketplace for building 
materials. Through its subsidiaries, UltraTech Cement, Aditya Birla Capital and Aditya Birla Renewables, it is also India’s 
prominent cement producer, leading diversified financial services player and clean energy solutions player. At Grasim, 
there is an endeavour to create sustainable value for 45,000+ employees, 2,52,000+ shareholders, society, and 
customers. The company reported consolidated net revenue of ₹1,30,978 Cr. and EBITDA of ₹20,837 Cr. in FY 2024.
