Lupin Launch es Dapagliflozin  and Metformin Hydrochloride  
 Extended -Release Tablets in the United States  
 
 
Mumbai, India, Naples,  Fla, APRIL 22, 2026 : Global pharma major Lupin Limited (Lupin) today 
announced the launch of Dapagliflozin and Metformin Hydrochloride Extended- Release Tablets, 5 
mg/500 mg, 5 mg/1,000 mg, 10 mg/500 mg, 10 mg/1,000 mg  in the United States following the approval 
for its Abbreviated New Drug Application from the U.S. FDA as bioequivalent to Xigduo® XR for the 
indications in the approved labeling.  
 
About Lupin  
Lupin Limited is a global pharmaceutical leader headquartered in Mumbai, India, with products distributed in over
