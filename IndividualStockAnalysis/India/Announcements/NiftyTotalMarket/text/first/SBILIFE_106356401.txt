Summary  of proceedings  of the 25th Annual  General  Meeting  (‘AGM’)  of SBI Life Insurance  
Company  Limited  (“the  Company”)  
 
The 2 5th Annual General Meeting (“AGM”) of the Company was duly convened on Friday , 
August 2 9, 202 5 at 11:00 A.M. through Video Conference ( “VC”) or Other Audio -Visual Means 
(“OAVM ”) facility. The Meeting was conducted in compliance with the relevant provisions of 
the Companies Act, 2013,  Secretarial Standards,  SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), general circulars issued by the 
Ministry of Corporate Affairs and other circulars issued by Securities & Exchange Board of India  
(“SEBI ”).    
 
Mr. Challa Sreenivasulu Setty , Chairman and Non -Executive Director,  chaired the meeting. 
 
The meeting was attended by 90 members  through electronic  means i.e. VC/O AVM facility 
provided by KFin Technologies Limited.

shareholders, Board membe rs and all others prese nt.

President & Chief Financial Officer , informed that the members were provided with the facility 
to exercise  their right to vote by electronic means, through remote e -voting and  e-voting at the 
AGM in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and SEBI Listing 
Regulations.  
 
The remote e -voting platform was kept open for voting from Monday , August 2 5, 202 5 up to 
Thursday , August 2 8, 202 5. The Members who had joined the meeting through VC and who 
had not cast their vote through remote e -voting, were provided the option to vote through  
e-voting facility made available during the AGM.  
 
Managing Director & CEO  highlighted the Company’s continued strong performance across key 
financial and non -financial metrics during the financial year 2024 -25. It was noted with 
satisfaction that this progress was documented in the Company’s 7th Integrated Annual Report 
and 3rd ESG and BRSR Report . 
 
He informed the members that over the past 24 years, the Company has grown into one of 
India’s most trusted life insurance providers, driven by a customer -first approach and 
adherence to strong values . In FY 2024 -25, the Company achie ved growth in Individual New 
Business Premium  and launched  new products  specially in non -linked product segment. In 
addition, the Company made significant strides in enhancing digital capabilities, leading to 
improved operational efficiency and an enriched  customer experience.  Further, h e highlighted 
the role of life insurance in financial resilience,  however  the penetration levels in India remain 
relatively low which  presents both a challenge and an opportunity for future growth. He also 
expressed confidence that increasing consumer awareness, supportive regulatory 
developments and ongoing digital transformation will act as key enabler . 
 
The Chairman  thereafter  announced that the quorum for the meeting was present and called 
the meeting to order.  
 
With the permission of the members, the notice convening the meeting was taken as read. The 
Chairman informed that there were no qualifications, observations or comments on Financial 
statements or matters which may have any adverse effect on the functionin g of the Company  
in the Auditor’s Report and the Secretarial Auditor in their Report for the financial year ended 
March 31, 202 5. 
 
The Chairman then commenced his speech and gave an overview of the operations and the 
financial performance of the Company du ring FY 202 4-25. Thereafter , shareholders  were 
invited , who had done prior registrations, to speak and ask questions, if any.  
 
Response s to the questions raised by the Members was provided by the Chairman . Thereafter, 
the Chairman concluded his speech by placing on record his appreciation and gratitude to all 
stakeholders for having reposed their trust and confidence in the Company.
