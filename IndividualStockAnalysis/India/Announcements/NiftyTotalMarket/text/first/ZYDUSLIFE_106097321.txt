Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
In our earlier intimation dated September 17, 2024, Zydus Lifesciences Limited (“the Company ”) 
intimated about execution of Business Transfer Agreement (“ BTA”) to purchase the API business 
(“the Target Business ”) of Sterling Biotech Limited (“ SBL”), subject to certain conditions 
precedent and closing date adjustments as provided in the BTA, with effect from such date, and 
in such manner and on the terms and conditions as mentioned in the BTA. 
 
In the said intimation, it was mentioned that the said transaction is expected to be completed 
by December 31, 2024.  
 
We hereby now inform that SBL is still in the process of completing certain conditions precedents 
of the BTA and is expected to take some more time to complete the same. In view of the same, 
and as per the terms and conditions of the BTA, the Company and SBL have mutually decided to 
extend the date of closing upto March 31, 2025.  
 
The Company and SBL have today, i.e. December 27, 2024, executed a letter to extend the date 
of completion as above. In view of the same, the transaction is expected to be completed on or 
before March 31, 2025.
