TCS Conducts  the World’s  Largest AI Hackathon  
 
The Hackathon is part of TCS’ strategy to augment the  AI-first culture in the company . It is a key  enabler to  
the tcsAI initiative  which aims to accelerate  AI adoption  and give employees the right  AI tools and 
experience s to reimagine their work  & develop innovative solutions for our customers  
 
MUMBAI, Octo ber 16, 2025:  Tata Consultancy Services  (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS), a global leader in IT 
services, consulting, and business solutions, has concluded the first edition of  the tcsAI Hackathon  2025 
globally. With over  281,000 employees participating from across 58 countries , the hackathon has made 
history by becoming the world’s largest AI Hackathon . Anchored on 21 industry -relevant AI themes,  the event 
set a new benchmark in diversity and reach for the industry  with  33% of participa tion from  women , 53% 
local employee s taking part in overseas geographies, 36% mid -level and senior participants, 38% employees 
in the Gen Z  age group taking part , and 29% non -technical participants .  
 
Part of TCS’ initiatives to accelerate AI adoption among employees under the unified banner tcsAI (to be read 
as 'TCS to the power of AI' ), the Hackathon is a key enabler to driving AI-first culture  at TCS . tcsAI also provides 
TCS employees access to GenAI -powered platforms, tools, and environments  to explore, learn and 
meaningfully integrate AI into their everyday work .  
 
The Hackathon brought together  People, Platforms and Partners in a unique  approach  to deliver exciting  
and gamified experiences to employees.  AI solutions from t op hyperscaler and niche partners  and TCS’ own 
solutions  like TCS AI WisdomNextTM were integrated seamlessly to cater to each participant person a and 
solution needs.  Participants accessed unlimited on -demand online AI labs and physical AI Friday labs  and 
mentors , demonstrating what the world’s largest phygital AI foundries look like . 
 
True to its theme, Generative AI is playing  a significant role in the comprehensive evaluation of the Hackathon 
submissions with TCS’  AI experts in the loop  – setting an example of  how AI augments humans to achieve 
scale and effectiveness . 
 
Aarthi Subramanian, Executive Director – President and Chief Operating Officer , Tata Consultancy Services, 
said,  “Attracting over 281, 000 participant s is a powerful signal of how AI has captured the imagination of all 
TCSers . The Hackathon was not just a competition, but a way to co -create the future of work, democrati sing 
AI, upskilling our people, and delivering solutions with real impact for our clients and communities. Whether 
employees are accessing AI in the flow of work, participating in hackathons, or experimenting with new tools, 
the overall tcsAI initiative is designed to multiply their individual impact and amplify our collective 
capabilities.”  
 
The tcsAI Hackathon 2025 continues  in its weekly AI Fridays in-person format  at major TCS campuses in India. 
TCS customers have provided feedback indicating interest in participating in TCS' work on AI innovation.  
Several TCS customers  and TCS groups are assessing the solutions developed during these Hackathons for 
potential integration into their products and services, both for clients and for internal use.
