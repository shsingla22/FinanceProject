Proceedings of the 26th Annual General Meeting of “Aditya  Vision  Limited”  held on 
Tuesday, July 15, 2025: - 
 
The 26th (Twenty Sixth ) Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the shareholders of Aditya  Vision  
Limited (“ the C ompany”) was held on Tuesday, July 15, 2025  at 0 4:30 PM through Video 
Conferencing (VC) / Other Audio -Visual Means (OAVM).  
 
Mr. Yashovardhan  Sinha, Chairman and Managing Director of the Company welcomed all the 
members present in the 26th Annual General Meeting of the Company.  
 
The requisite quorum was being present, the Chairman called the Meeting to order.  
 
The Company Secretary informed the Shareholders that the Company had provided to the 
Shareholders, the facility to cast their vote electronically through remote e -voting facility 
provided by Central Depository Services Limited (CDSL) which had commenced on  Saturday, 
 
set forth in the Notice.  
 
The chairman delivered his speech highlighting the performance of the Company and his vision 
for the future growth of the company. He sincerely thanked the shareholders for their support, 
co-operation, blessings, trust and wishes for the company.  
 
The Company Secretary  informed that the Board of Directors had appointed M/s Deepak Dhir 
& Associates,  Practicing Company Secretary as the Scrutinizer to scrutinize the remote e -voting 
and e -voting process in a fair and transparent manner.  
 
The Company Secretary stated the resolutions set out in the notice .

as follows;
