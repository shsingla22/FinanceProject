MOIL Achieves Highest -Ever July Production in FY’2 6 
 
 
 
MOIL  achieved manganese ore production of 1.45 lakh tonnes in July 2025, marking a n 
impressive growth of 1 1.4% over the corresponding period last year (CPLY) , despite adverse  
weather conditions . 
 
Despite heavy rainfall , MOIL demonstrated strong operational momentum duri ng April –July 
2025, with production at 6.47 lakh tonnes (7.8% growth y -o-y and exploratory drilling of 43,215 
meters (up 11.4% over CPLY).  Sales during the period stood at 4.53 lakh tonnes, compared to 
5.02 lakh tonnes during April –July 2024 . 
 
Shri Ajit Ku mar Saxena, CMD, congratulated the MOIL team for record production 
performance and appreciated the continued efforts to enhance production & sales even under 
challenging weather conditions.
