Sterling Launches Valley of Pine, Lansdowne  
An off -the-grid Uttarakhand retreat for drive -to travellers  
 
• Strengthens Sterling’s presence in Uttarakhand  with its ninth resort in the state . 
• Pins Gumkhal  on the travel map for  scenic unhurried getaways, workcations and 
intimate celebrations . 
 
 
Chennai,  04 Feb 2026:  Sterling Holiday Resorts  today announced the launch of Sterling Valley 
of Pine, Lansdowne, a boutique mountain retreat nestled amid dense pine forests near 
Gumkhal— a view -led retreat with warm, pinewood interiors and open terraces, created for 
unhurried ge taways. The opening marks Sterling’s ninth resort in Uttarakhand —reinforcing the 
brand’s circuit strength in one of India’s most loved hill -and-forest holiday regions.  
 Conceived for purposeful mountain travel, Sterling Valley of Pine, Lansdowne introduces a 
quieter, forest- driven escape within Sterling’s hill portfolio —designed for travellers seeking 
space, privacy and meaningful outdoor time. Well -suited for leisure stays, intimate celebrations 
and leadership offsites, the retreat also works as a natural connector within Uttarakhand’s circuit around mountain s, spiritual havens  and other drive -to destinations —especially for 
travellers from Delhi NCR  and Western UP . 
 Designed to frame the valley from multiple vantage points, all rooms feature private balconies with far -reaching views across the pine valleys, while select indulgent havens include access to 
open terraces overlooking the pool and the surrounding landscape . The guest experience is 
anchored around food, togetherness and meaningful moments —supported by three signature 
venues:  
 
• Lans Café  – all-day dining in a warm pinewood setting with panoramic valley and garden 
views  
• Sky Social  – an open- to-sky terrace for sunset gatherings, bonfire evenings and small 
celebrations  
• Sky Vista – a glass- walled meeting venue overlooking the valley and forests, designed 
for leadership offsites and private workshops  
 Commenting on the launch,  Vikram Lalvani, Managing Director & CEO, Sterling Holiday 
Resorts, said:  “Uttarakhand continues to be one of India’s most enduring year -round holiday 
regions, and Sterling has built deep familiarity with how travellers engage with the state across seasons. Valley of Pine, Lansdowne  adds a calmer, more design- forward chapter to our  
  
  
 
 mountain portfolio —created for guests who want meaningful time outdoors and destinations 
that feel restorative yet accessible. It also strengthens our presence across Mussoorie, Nainital, 
Dehradun, Rishikesh and Jim Corbett National Park —helping us curate connected, experience -
led journeys across the region.”  
 
 
Also s peaking on the association, Ms. Meghna Bhatnagar, from  Dev Bhoomi,  said: “We are 
thrilled to partner with Sterling for our flagship retreat in Lansdowne. Our vision was to create a 
retreat that brings a different guest from across India, while we offer them refined hospitality. Sterling is natural choice to bring this vision to  life — their deep understanding of India’s leisure 
segment, regional presence, and ability to blend local character with national scale make them 
an ideal collaborator."  
 
Among the other compelling reasons to stay are curated regional delicacies, a swimming pool, steam and sauna facilities, guided nature walks, bonfire evenings and pet -friendly vacations —
making it a well- rounded, off -the-grid retreat.  
 Strategically located near Gumkhal,  Lansdowne  is just 136 km (3– 3.5 hrs) from Rishikesh, 175 km 
(3.5–4 hrs) from Dehradun, 200 km (4 –4.5 hrs) from Mussoorie, 219 –252 km (5.5 –6 hrs) from 
Nainital, and 118 km (3 –3.5 hrs) from Jim Corbett National Park —making it viable to combine 
mountain, spiritual, urban and w ildlife destinations into extended itineraries.
