PRESS  RELEASE  
 
Websol Energy Q2FY27 PAT Jumps  73% YoY to ₹80 Crore  
Revenue More Than Doubles to ₹361 Crore  (YoY)  
 
Kolkata, 4 November,  2026: Websol Energy System Limited, (“Websol” or the “Company”) (BSE: 517498; 
NSE: WEBELSOLAR), one of the leading manufacturers of high -efficiency solar cells and solar modules in 
India, announced its unaudited financial results for the  second quarter  and half year  ended 30th September, 
2026.  
 
Websol reported revenue from operations of ₹361.02 crore in Q2FY27, up 114.6% from ₹168.22 crore in 
Q2FY26. EBITDA increased 77.3% year -on-year to ₹128.14 crore from ₹72.29 crore, while profit after tax (PAT) 
grew 73.2% to ₹80.21 crore, compared with ₹46.3 2 crore in the corresponding quarter last year.  
 
During the quarter, Websol also made progress on two important strategic priorities: strengthening its 
balance sheet through the repayment of its outstanding IREDA term loan and securing land for its next phase 
of manufacturing expansion in West Bengal.

Business  Highlights  for Q2FY27  
• Cell  production  for the quarter  was  255 MW during Q2FY27, with capacity utili zation of 91%.  
• Module  production was  88 MW during Q2FY27, with capacity utili zation of 68%.  
• The outstanding order book stood at ₹1,280 crore as of 30 September 2026 . 
 
Strategic Capacity Expansion and Land Allotment  
During the quarter, the Group secured a 54.20 -acre land allotment from the Government of West Bengal at 
Falta Industrial Park for a greenfield solar cell manufacturing facility. The planned complex will add 4 GW of 
cell capacity, executed in two equal 2 GW phases.  
 
 
 
   
 
Situated near the Company’s existing Falta SEZ operations, this expansion leverages over 30 years of 
operational history, a skilled regional workforce, and an established supply chain to accelerate project 
timelines. The initiative reinforces Websol’s posi tion as a major integrated solar manufacturing hub in 
Eastern India, directly aligning with national clean energy targets.  
 
Commenting  on the performance,  Mr. Sohan  Lal Agarwal,  Managing  Director , said:  
 
“Q2FY27 has been a quarter we are particularly proud of. Our revenue more than doubled  since last year 
same period , while our cell lines operated at 91% utili zation.  We also repaid our IREDA term  loan and 
received the land allotment at Falta for our next phase of expansion.  These achievements reflect years of 
hard work, investment and the trust of our customers.  
 
Having spent over thirty years building Websol, I have learnt that building capacity and making it successful 
are two very different things. What gives me confidence today is seeing our existing plants perform 
consistently, even as we prepare for the next phase of growth. Our TOPCon upgrade is progressing, with trial 
runs targeted by the end of FY27.  
 
As we look ahead, Websol is evolving from a solar cell manufacturer into an integrated solar energy solutions 
provider. The Board has approved entry into EPC, Solar IPP, including solar parks, and energy trading, 
marking our expansion across the broader so lar energy value chain. We believe these initiatives will open 
new growth opportunities and strengthen Websol's position in India's renewable energy sector. ” 
 
 
About Websol Energy System Limited: Founded among the pioneers of solar photovoltaic manufacturing 
in India, Websol specializes in producing high -efficiency solar cells and modules incorporating advanced 
Mono PERC technology. The Company supplies solar cells primarily within India, supporting module 
manufacturers to comply with Domestic Content Requirement norms, while its modules are marketed both 
in India and internationally. Its fully modernized manufacturing facility, located at Falta Special Economic 
Zone in West Bengal, operate s with a current solar cell capacity of 1,200 MW and module capacity of 550 
MW. The facility is designed to process wafers up to 210 mm, offering higher energy output and optimized 
land use for rooftop installations.  
 
Websol’s integrated production model, manufacturing both cells and modules in -house, enhances supply 
chain control and flexibility to address market dynamics. The Company maintains established partnerships 
with reputed clients and benefits from supportive policy frameworks that encourage local production and 
technology advancement, underpinning its long -term strategic positioning in the solar industry. For more 
details, please visit: https://www.websolenergy.com
