I am pleased to present the Report on the Postal Ballot conducted by Solar Industries India 
Limited seeking consent of its Members through Special Resolution as contained in the 
Notice of Postal Ballot dated May 16, 2024 . 
 
1. In terms of provisions of Section 110 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with Companies 
(Management and Administration) Rules, 2014, I was appointed as the Scrutinizer by 
the Company to conduct the Postal Ballot as contained in the Notice dated May 16, 
2024. 
 
In terms of Sections 110 and 108 of the Companies Act, 2013, and all other applicable 
provisions, if any, of the Act read with Rules 20 and 22 of the Companies 
(Management and Administration) Rules, 2014, and extant Circulars issued by Ministry 
of Corporate   Affairs in this regard, approval by way of Special Resolution were sought 
for: 
 
- Item no. 1 : Appointment of Shri Dinesh Kumar Batra (DIN: 08773363)  as an 
Independent Director  of the Company. 
 
2. Notice of Postal Ballot: 
2.1. The Company had hosted the Notice of Postal ballot on its website on  
May 24, 2024 . 
2.2. Notice regarding dispatch of Postal Ballot Notice was published on May 25, 2024 
in Loksatta (Nagpur Edition) and Business Standard (All India Edition). 
2.3. The Company has informed that, Notice of Postal Ballot is sent to the Members of 
the Company whose names appear on the Register of Members/List of Beneficial 
Owners as on Friday, May 17, 2024  ("the cut-off date" ) and who have registered 
their e-mail addresses with the Company or with the depository(ies)/ depository 
participants.  
2.4. The Company has completed sending of Postal Ballot Notice through E-Mail on May 
24, 2024  to 66,336 Members who had registered their email-ids with the Company 
or with the depository(ies) / depository participants. 
2.5. The Notice has also been uploaded on the Website of the Company at 
www.solargroup.com  The Notice can also be accessed from the website of the 
www.bseindia.com  and www.nseindia.com  respectively and 
the Notice is also available on the website of National Securities Depository 
-voting Facility) i.e. 
www.evoting.nsdl.com . 
 
 
 
 
3. In terms of the aforesaid Notice, Members were required to convey their assent or 
dissent, only through remote e-voting system, on e-voting platform provided by NSDL 
from Monday, May 27, 2024 at 9.00 a.m. (IST) to Tuesday, June 25, 2024 at 
5.00 p.m. (IST) . 
 
4. 624 Members had cast their votes on the e-voting platform till 5:00 p.m. (IST) on 
Tuesday, June 25, 2024. 
 
5. Members' demographic details and their voting rights were provided by NSDL. 
 
6. The Management of the Company is responsible to ensure compliance with the 
requirements of the Act and Rules relating to the remote e-voting on resolution 
contained in the Postal Ballot Notice.  
 
7. My responsibility as scrutinizer for the remote e-voting is restricted to making a 
Scrutinizer's Report of the votes cast for assent or dissent of the resolution. 
 
8. After the scrutiny of the remote e-voting results, I report that the Special Resolution 
as contained i n  the Notice of Postal Ballot dated May 16, 2024 has failed to get the 
requisite majority.  
Further, pursuant to sub-regulation 2A of Regulation 25 of SEBI Listing Regulations 
(Sixth amendment) 2022, where a special resolution for the appointment of an 
independent director fails to get the requisite majority of votes but the votes cast in 
favour of the resolution exceed the votes cast against the resolution and the votes cast 
by the public shareholders in favour of the resolution exceed the votes cast against the 
resolution, then the appointment of such an independent director shall be deemed to 
have been made under sub-regulation (2A). 
In light of the above amendment, Shri. Dinesh Kumar Batra (DIN: 08773363) shall be 
deemed to be appointed as an Independent Director of the Company under Regulation 
25(2A) of SEBI Listing Regulations. 
 
9. The electronic data and all other relevant records relating to e-voting were handed 
over to CS Khushboo Pasari, Company Secretary & Compliance officer of Solar 
Industries India Limited.
