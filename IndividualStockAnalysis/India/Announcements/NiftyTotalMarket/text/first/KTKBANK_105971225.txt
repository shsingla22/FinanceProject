PRESS RELEASE 
Karnataka Bank offers unique facility of Personal Loans on ONDC (Open Network 
for Digital Commerce ) Network. 
Karnataka Bank, headquartered in Mangaluru and a leading Private sector Bank, is 
proud to announce its pioneering move of being an early adopter and first Bank to offer 
loans on Open Network, a revolutionary move by ONDC (Open Network for Digital 
Commerce) in democratizing credit at scale. 
The Bank will be offering Personal loans through the Buyer APPs on the ONDC 
Network, which will be available for Bank's existing customers initially and will be 
extended to 'New to Bank' customers. 
Shri Srikrishnan H, Managing Director & CEO of Karnataka Bank, stated "this is yet 
another big step in democratizing credit. Karnataka Bank is proud to be the first Bank 
to integrate into ONDC' s rails and is excited to innovate and work closely with their 
ecosystem, evolve products and services & contribute to build India's Digital Public 
Infrastructure." 
Shri Sekhar Rao, Executive Director of Karnataka Bank, added, "we are excited to be an 
early adopter of ONDC for financial services and believe that the standardized 
protocols of ONDC enables great convenience in connecting lenders to multiple buyer 
APP' s, thus unlocking huge potential. Customers will now have more choices with 
better price discovery." 
Shri T Koshy, MD & CEO, ONDC, added that, "ONDC's mission is to democratize 
financial services in India by leveraging technology to create networks that enable the 
low-cost distribution of financial products. Moreover, ONDC is building a 
technological environment that encourages innovation in financial products but in a

controlled and responsible manner. Karnataka Bank's initiative is a perfect example of 
how ONDC Network can empower both financial institutions and consumers, marking 
a significant milestone in our journey towards a more accessible and equitable financial 
ecosystem in India."
