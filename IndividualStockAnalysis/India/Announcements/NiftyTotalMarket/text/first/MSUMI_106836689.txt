    Motherson Sumi Wiring India Ltd. reports strong Q2FY27 growth, supported by higher customer volumes and increasing content per vehicle. Quarterly revenue rises 47% YoY to ₹4,054 crore. Noida, Uttar Pradesh – 30th Oct 2026 – Motherson Sumi Wiring India Limited (MSWIL) announced its financial results for the fiscal year 2026-27, second quarter, which ended on 30th Sep 2026.  Mr Vivek Chaand Sehgal, Chairman, Motherson Sumi Wiring India Ltd., said,  “Our resilient performance reflects the continued trust of our customers and strength of our executional capabilities. The growth in the business has been supported by higher customer volumes, increasing content per vehicle and our participation in new model launches across both EV and ICE platforms. At the same time, our teams continue to focus on operational efficiencies and financial discipline while navigating elevated commodity costs. We remain grateful to our customers for their continued support and to our people for their dedication. We will continue to focus on creating sustainable, long-term value for all our stakeholders.”     Key Highlights  • Robust growth of  ~47% YoY and 19% QoQ reflected continued customer trust. • Premiumization-led higher content per vehicle and new EV/ICE model wins continues to be the key  growth driver.  • Timely expansion and ramp-up of Greenfields projects helped to support higher customer volumes.  • Performance remained resilient amidst headwinds from higher copper prices  and other cost inflationary pressure. • Consistent focus on financial prudence, with disciplined working capital management.  Financial Performance               Motherson Sumi Wiring India Limited (MSWIL)      Motherson Sumi Wiring India Limited (MSWIL) is a leading and fast-growing full-system solutions provider to OEMs in the wiring harness segment in India. Motherson Sumi Wiring India is a joint venture between Samvardhana Motherson International Limited (SAMIL) and Sumitomo Wiring Systems, Ltd. (SWS).  The company has the capabilities to meet customer needs at every stage of the supply chain, from initial product design to final production of integrated, cutting-edge Electrical & Electronic Distribution Systems for power supply or data transfer across vehicles, in support of current and future automotive trends.
