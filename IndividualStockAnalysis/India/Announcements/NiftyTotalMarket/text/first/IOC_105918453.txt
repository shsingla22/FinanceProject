NOTICE
NOTICE  is hereby given that the 65th Annual General Meeting 
(“AGM ”) of the members of Indian Oil Corporation Limited 
(“IndianOil” / “Company”) will be held on Friday, August 09, 2024

Means (“VC/OAVM”) to transact the business mentioned below.
