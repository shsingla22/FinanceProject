Withdrawal of rating(s) assigned to the Bank facilities of JK Files and Engineering Limited  
We have noted that the engineering business of JK Files and Engineering Limited has been amalgamated with JK Maini 
Precision Technology Limited under the composite scheme of restructuring approved and sanctioned by the Hon'ble 
National Company Law Tribunal  (NCLT), Mumbai Bench vide no. C.P.(CAA)/03/MB -III/2025 in C.A.(CAA)/152/MB -
III/2024 pronounced on July 04, 2025. Basis the scheme of restructuring the bank facilities of JK Files and Engineering 
has been transferred and /or are in the process of being tra nsferred to JK Maini Precision Technology Limited. However, 
the Bankers have confirmed that there is no outstanding amount against the rated facilities in the name of the rated 
entity. Taking note of the same, we hereby withdraw our outstanding ratings of 'CARE AA - (Rating Watch with 
Developing Implications) and CARE A1+ (Rating Watch with Developing Implications)' assigned to bank facilities of JK 
Files and Engineering Limited with immediate effect.   
2. As per our normal procedure, we will be announcing the withdrawal of the rating through a Press Release, a copy of 
which is enclosed.  Meanwhile, please ensure that the ratings are not used hereafter, for any purpose 
whatsoever.  
3.  In case of any future rating requirements, we will be happy to offer our services.  
If you need any clarification, you are welcome to approach us in this regard.
