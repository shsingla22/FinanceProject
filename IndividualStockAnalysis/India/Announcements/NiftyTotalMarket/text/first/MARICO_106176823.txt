International Day of Forests – Marico Limited Highlights 2 .25 Lakh Saplings 
Planted till date across Maharashtra, Gujarat and Tamil Nadu  
 
Marico has covered 200 acres of land  through its Afforestation programme.  
 
Mumbai , March  20, 2025 : On the occasion of International Day of Forests , Marico Limited, one of 
India's leading FMCG companies, has made significant strides in its afforestation initiatives, reinforcing 
its commitment to wards agrarian  community development . The company has successfully planted   
2.25 lakh s of saplings  till date across key regions, including Jalgaon, Sanand, and Perundurai , 
underscoring the company’s dedication to afforestation as a key pillar of its CSR objects . Marico has 
covered  approximately  200 acres of land  through its afforestation programme.  
 
Recognizing the importance of biodiversity conservation, community need, afforestation programme 
focus on increasing green cover, improving soil health, and enhancing the local ecosystem . 
The initiative  has resulted in a noticeable increase in native bird species and small mammals , 
contributing to ecological balance . The company has also implemented innovative planting 
techniques, such as drip irrigation, using green energy by installing solar panels , agroforestry models, 
central plantations of fruit trees to enhance afforestation outcomes. These methods not only enhance 
forest cover but also create  income -generation  opportunities  for farmers , ensuring  long -term 
economic returns.  
The overall objective of the programme is to provide livelihood opportunities to farmers, SHGs and 
also enhancing the quality of land for the benefit of the local community.   
Marico’s Afforestation programme is a combination of Miyawaki, cropping of climate -resilient plant -
Moringa and Community based programme. Projects sites are provided by the Gram panchayat and 
municipal corporation.  
There is active participation of the community, and their involvement ensure the monitoring of the 
project site. Employee volunteering and community engagement programs conducted to raise 
awareness and encourage participation in forest conservation efforts . 
Sharing his thoughts on Marico’s afforestation effort s, Amit Bhasin, Chief Legal Officer, Group 
General Counsel, and Secretary of the CSR Committee, Marico Limited  said, “Our afforestation 
initiatives demonstrate our deep -rooted commitment to creating a positive impact on both the 
environment and the communities we serve.  By integrating innovative afforestation techniques, water 
management, and community participation, we are ensuring that our green initiatives leave a lasting 
legacy for future generations . 
We have undertaken  mix of  afforestation  techniques in our project location  across  Tamil Nadu, 
Gujarat and Maharashtra .  With a continued focus on  promoting eco-friendly agriculture practices, 
and enhancing livelihoods of local communities , we remai n committed in our mission to build a more 
resilient  and equitable future .”
