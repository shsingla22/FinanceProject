Dear Sirs,  
 
Pursuant to the requirements of Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure  
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (SEBI LODR), we wish to inform that Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) and Oil India Limited (OIL ) have signed a Memorandum of 
Understanding (MoU) at India Energy Week (IEW) 2026 to collaborate on the development of a 
Compressed Bio- Gas (CBG) project. The MoU provides a framework for joint efforts aimed at 
promoting cleaner and more sustainable energy solutions.  
 
As part of the collaboration, OIL will implement a CBG plant using HPCL’s indigenously developed 
HP RAMP technology. HPCL will provide the requisite technology to enable efficient conversion of 
waste into compressed bio- gas, while OIL will leverage its operational capabilities for project 
implementation.  
  
The MoU highlights the commitment of both public sector undertakings to clean energy and waste -
to-energy initiatives. It aligns with India’s energy transition objectives by supporting a circular 
economy,  reducing carbon emissions, and strengthening the domestic bio- energy ecosystem through 
indigenous technological solutions.
