Summary of proceedings of the 66th Annual General Meeting (AGM) :  
 
The 66th Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the Members of Elecon Engineering Company 
Limited (“the Company”) was held on Thursday, 25th June, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. (IST) through 
Video Conferencing (“VC”)/Other Audio Visual Means (“OVAM”) at the venue deemed to 
be the Registered Office of the Company at Anand -Sojitra Road, Vallabh Vidyanagar – 388 
120, Gujarat. The meeting was held in compliance with the circulars issued by the Ministry of 
Corporate Affairs (“MCA”) and the Securities and Exchange Board of India (“SEBI”) and as 
per the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Rules made thereunder.
