SRF Limited Reports Strong Growth in Q2 & H1 FY27  
Gurugram, October 2 1, 2026: SRF Limited, an Innovation led Materials and Chemicals Company , engaged in 
the manufacturing of industrial and specialty intermediates today announced its consolidated financial results 
for the second quarter and half year ended September 30, 202 6. The Company’s unaudited results were 
approved by the Board of Directors in a meeting held earlier today.  
Consolidated Q2FY2 7 Financials   
The consolidated revenue of the Company increased 35% from ₹3, 640 crore to ₹ 4,904 crore in Q2FY2 7 when 
compared with Corresponding Period Last Year (CPLY). The Company’s Operational Earnings before Interest 
and Tax ( Operational EBIT) increased  26% from ₹ 650 crore to ₹ 816 crore  in Q2FY2 7 when compared with 
CPLY. The Company’s Profit after Tax (PAT) increased  31% from ₹ 388 crore to ₹ 509 crore in Q2FY2 7 when 
compared with CPLY.  
Commenting on the results, Chairman and Managing Director, Ashish Bharat Ram said , “Our Businesses have 
delivered a strong performance, despite an uncertain and volatile global environment. Most importantly, our 
Capex investments targeting opportunities in sunrise sectors remain extremely robust, positioning us well for 
the future ”.  
Consolidated Q2FY2 7 Segment Results  
The Chemicals Business  reported a 2 5% increase in segment revenue, from ₹1, 667 crore to ₹ 2,079 crore 
during Q2FY2 7 compared to CPLY. Operating profit grew by 10%, from ₹ 481 crore to ₹ 528 crore in the same 
period.  The Fluorochemicals Business delivered robust refrigerant sales volumes, supported by positive pricing 
momentum across key products, while maintaining its leadership position across primary  markets . The 
Specialty Chemicals Business navigated a dynamic operating environment shaped by evolving global pricing 
trends, industry overcapacity in China and ongoing geopolitical developments . During the quarter, both the 
Specialty Chemicals and Fluorochemicals Businesses undertook initiatives to drive operational efficiencies.  
The Performance Films & Foil Business  reported a 51% increase  in segment revenue, from ₹1,4 08 crore 
to ₹2,122 crore during Q2FY2 7 compared to CPLY. The o perating profit  increase d 73%, from ₹ 119 crore to 
₹206 crore in Q2FY2 7 over CPLY. During Q2FY2 7, the Business achieved its highest -ever packed production of 
films. However, Q2 demand was moderated by inventory build -up following heightened buying activity amid 
the volatility witnessed in Q1. Demand for Aluminium  foil, meanwhile, remained healthy during the quarter.  
The Technical Textiles Business  reported a 25% increase in segment revenue , from ₹ 474 crore to ₹ 595 
crore during Q2FY2 7 over CPLY. The operating profit increased  71%, from ₹ 42 crore to ₹ 72 crore in Q2FY2 7 
over CPLY. The Business delivered a steady performance during the quarter, supported by healthy demand 
and improved margins .

Capex  
Performance Films & Foil Business  
The Board has approved an investment of approximately ₹600 crore to establish a battery -grade Aluminium  
foil manufacturing facility in India. The proposed facility, with a total capacity of approximately 12,000 metric 
tons per annum, will cater to both lithium -ion (Li -ion) and sodium -ion (Na -ion) battery applications, supporting 
the growing requirements of the  energy storage  and electric mobility  sectors. The investment is proposed to 
be undertaken in two phases, with an estimated project cost of ₹300 crore for each phase. Phase I is expected 
to be completed by September 2028, followed by Phase II by September 2030 .

About SRF Limited  
With an annual turnover of ₹15,416 crore (US$ 1.6 billion), the Company’s diversified business portfolio covers Fluorochemica ls, 
Specialty Chemicals, Performance Films & Foil, Technical Textiles and Coated and Laminated Fabrics.  The Company has 16 
manufacturing facilities —13 in India and one each in Thailand, South Africa, and Hungary —and has recently expanded with an office 
in Dubai, further strengthening its global presence.  With a workforce of over 9,500 employees representing diverse nationalities, SRF 
exports to more than 90 countries.  
Equipped with state -of-the-art R&D facilities, SRF has filed 539 patents for R&D and technology so far, of which 160 have been granted. 
A winner of the prestigious Deming Prize for two of its businesses, namely Tyre Cord and Chemicals, SRF continues to redefine  its work 
and corporate culture with TQM as its management way.
