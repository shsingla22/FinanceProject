FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE  
 
Equitas  Small  Finance  Bank  Limited  announces  Mr. Bala ji Nuthalapadi  as Interim  CEO,  subject  to 
RBI Approval  
 
Board initiates the process to identify a successor to Mr. P. N. Vasudevan  
 
India, November 4, 2026:  Equitas Small Finance Bank Limited announced on 3 November 2026 that 
its Board of Directors, based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration 
Committee, has approved the appointment of Mr. Balaji Nuthalapadi, currently serving as a Whole -
Time  Director (Executive Director) of the Bank, as its Interim Chief Executive Officer (CEO), subject 
to the approval of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).   
 
As Executive Director at Equitas Small Finance Bank  Limited , Mr. Balaji Nuthalapadi has played a 
pivotal  role in enhancing digital capabilities, upgrading technology and improving operational 
efficiency. Prior to joining Equitas  Small Finance Bank Limited , he was with Citi for 28 years and 
served as Managing Director and Head of India Citi Solutions Centers and Head of India Operations 
and Technology. He worked in multiple geographies across Asia & EMEA in roles spanning asset 
sales, wealth management, re -engineering, consumer operations and technology and Global 
Capability Center leadership where he led the scale -up of Citi’s global hubs in India.  
 
The appointment follows the resignation of Mr. P. N. Vasudevan as Managing Director and CEO of 
the Bank. Mr. Balaji Nuthalapadi's appointment will take effect from the date of RBI approval or such 
other date as may  be stipulated by the RBI. Equitas Small Finance Bank Limited  shall ensure  to its 
stakeholders and investors that smooth and seamless transition  which connote stability.  
 
The Board has placed on record its deep appreciation and sincere gratitude to Mr. P. N. Vasudevan 
for his invaluable contributions, exemplary leadership and dedicated service to the Bank during his 
tenure. The Bank will determine his relieving date following the receipt of RBI approval for the interim 
CEO appointment and in consultation wi th Mr.  P. N.  Vasudevan.  
 
The Board has also initiated the process of  identifying a successor to Mr. P. N. Vasudevan and will 
submit its recommendation to the RBI in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.  
 
Equitas Small Finance Bank Limited remains committed to its strategic priorities, business objectives 
and its mission of delivering inclusive, responsible and customer -centric banking services. The Bank 
will continue to focus on strengthening its customer relationships, expanding its reach and  creating 
sustainable value for all its stakeholders.  
 
About Equitas Small Finance Bank Limited (ESFB)  
Equitas Small Finance Bank Limited is one of the largest small finance banks in India. As a new -age 
bank, we offer a bouquet of products and services tailored to meet the needs of our customers – 
individuals with limited access to formal financing channels , as well as affluent and mass affluent, 
Small & Medium Enterprises (SMEs) and corporates. Our firmly entrenched strategy focuses on 
providing credit to the unbanked and underbanked micro and small entrepreneurs, developing 
products to address the growing aspirations at the ‘bottom of the pyramid’, fuelled by granular 
deposits and ‘value for money’ banking relationships.
