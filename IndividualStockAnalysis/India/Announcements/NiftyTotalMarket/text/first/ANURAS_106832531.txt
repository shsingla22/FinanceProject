MEMBERS PRESENT THROUGH VC/ OA VM: 
In aggregate, 38 (Thirty-Eight) Members holding 58.56% of the paid-up share capital, 
were present (including the Directors who are holding shares and representing the 
corporate Members) who joined the live web-cast facility available through VC. Since 
the Meeting was held through VC/OAVM, physical attendance of Members was 
dispensed with and accordingly, no Member was considered to be physically present 
in person/through the proxy. 
The Meeting was scheduled to commence at 09:30 a.m. IST and commenced at 09:39 
a.m. IST. 
Dr. Kiran Patel, the Chairperson, occupied the Chair and welcomed all the Members . 
Thereafter, the Chairperson informed the Members about the AGM being conducted 
through VC/ OA VM in accordance with the Act, the relevant circulars issued by MCA 
and SEBI. He informed that the Annual Report, along with the Notice of 23rd AGM, 
was sent to all the eligible Members electronically on September 08, 2026, in 
compliance with the issued circulars. He also informed that Members attending the 
Meeting through VC are counted for the purpose of quorum under Section 103 of the 
Act. 
Further, Mr. Ashish Gupta, Company Secretary & Compliance Officer, confirmed that 
the requisite quorum was present to commence the proceedings of the Meeting. He

participation in the Meeting. The Members were also informed that the remote e­
voting facility on the resolutions to be passed in the AGM was provided to all the

voting may cast their votes after the conclusion of the AGM and the Members who had 
already cast their votes can not cast the same again.

Personnel and their Shareholding, the Register of Contracts or Arrangements in which

Association of the Company are available electronically for inspection by the Members

Company's website www.anupamrasayan .com and also apprised that the e-voting 
results along with the Scrutinizer's Report would be uploaded on the Stock Exchanges 
viz. BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited, where the shares of 
the Company are listed as well as uploaded on the website of the Company and on the 
website of the NSDL. 
Thereafter, the Chairperson gave a brief speech and briefed the Members on the 
Company's progress during financial year 2025-26, and informed that the Company 
is committed to creating long-term value by expanding its capabilities, deepening the 
technology base and strengthening its position as a globally integrated specialty 
chemicals company. 
He highlighted the structural changes in the specialty chemicals industry, including 
the reconfiguration of global supply chains and increasing demand for differentiated 
capabilities, reliable manufacturing partners, and greater participation across their 
value chains. He states about the acquisition of Jayhawk Fine Chemicals Corporation 
in the United States, which establishes the Company's manufacturing presence in one 
. • 
of the world's largest chemical markets, and the acquisition of a controlling stake in 
Bliss GVS Pharma Ltd., which would further expand its presence in pharmaceuticals. 
He also highlighted Tanfac Industries Limited's fluorochemical expertise and the 
Company's capabilities in complex syntheses and process development. 
He emphasized that the innovation remains central to the Company's growth strategy, 
with more than 50 products commercialized over the last five years and a strong 
pipeline across pharmaceutical and polymer applications. He also highlighted the 
Company's sustainability initiatives, noting that nearly 60.35% of its electricity is 
sourced from renewable energy, supporting its efforts towards energy net zero by 
2027. 
Looking ahead, he expressed confidence in the meaningful opportunities across 
performance materials, pharmaceuticals, and specialized chemistries, supported by 
the Company's broadening portfolio, growing global footprint, and strong customer 
relationships. 
Thereafter , the Chairperson concluded his speech by expressing gratitude to 
employees, customers, partners and Members for their trust and support. Further, the 
Chairperson invited Mr. Anand Desai, Managing Director, to address the Members . 
Thereafter, Mr. Anand Desai greeted the Members and provided an overview of the 
Company's performance during the financial year 2025-26, describing it as a landmark 
year for the Company, with strategic initiatives undertaken over the past several years 
translating into tangible business outcomes. He stated that the Company achieved its 
highest-ever consolidated revenue of z2,384 crore and net operating cash flow of z334 
crore, supported by improved capacity utilisation, disciplined working capital 
management and healthy traction across business verticals. 
He highlighted the evolving business mix, with greater participation in higher-value , 
technology-intensive applications . Performance Materials revenue increasing from z97 
crore in FY 2022 to z305 crore in FY 2026 and pharmaceutical revenue increasing from 
z21 crore to z339 crore, resulting in a more diversified portfolio. He apprised the 
Members that the Company has more than doubled its manufacturing capacity over 
the last three years and expanded its portfolio to over 125 products, with more than 90 
molecules in its development pipeline. 
He further highlighted the strategic acquisitions of Jayhawk Fine Chemicals 
Corporation in the United States and a controlling stake in Bliss GVS Pharma Ltd., 
strengthening the Company's capabilities, geographic reach and participation across 
specialty chemicals and pharmaceutical value chains. He stated that the sustainability 
and operational excellence also remain key priorities of the Company . Concluding the 
address, he emphasized the Company's focus on growth, strengthening customer 
relationships and increasing participation in global value chains, and thanked the 
employees, customers, partners and Members for their continued trust and support. 
Thereafter, the Chairperson invited Mr. Gopal Agrawal, CEO, to address the Members. 
Thereafte r, Mr. Gopal Agrawal greeted and apprised the Members that FY26 was a 
year of continued progress for the Company , supported by improving market 
conditions, higher asset utilisation and healthy traction across the business verticals. 
He further apprised the Members about financial and operational highlights of the 
Company as mentioned hereunder:
