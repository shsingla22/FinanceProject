Shareholders are requested to note that as per the revised provisions of the Income Tax Act, 1961 ("the Act"), dividend paid or 
distributed by a company are t axable in the hands of the shareholders. The Company shall therefore be required to deduct tax 
at source at the applicable rates ("TDS") at the time of making the payment of dividend as and when declared by the Company. 
The Company shall consider the reque sts received by it from its shareholders as on the Record Date fixed by the Company in 
relation to its proposed dividend(s). However, no withholding of tax is applicable if the dividend payable to resident indivi dual 
shareholders having valid Permanent Acc ount Number ("PAN") in aggregate during F.Y 202 5-26 is less than INR 10,000 per 
annum. Tax at source will not be deducted in cases where a shareholder provides Form 15G (applicable to individual) / Form 
15H (applicable to an individual above the age of 60 years), provided that the eligibility conditions are being met. Blank Form 
15G and 15H can be downloaded from the link given at the end of this communication or from the website of KFin  Technologies 
Limited, (Registrar and Transfer Agents/RTA) viz. https://ris.kfintech.com/form15 . Please note that all fields are 
mandatory, and Company may at its sole discretion reject form if it does not fulfil the requirement of law. Fresh forms will be 
required to be submitted and forms submitted earlier will not be considered.
