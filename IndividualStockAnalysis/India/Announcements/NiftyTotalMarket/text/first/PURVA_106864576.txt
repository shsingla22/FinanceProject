Puravankara launches Purva Cityspire in Kochi with ₹2,000 
crore GDV  
Kochi: Puravankara Limited (NSE: PURVA | BSE: 532891), one of India’s most trusted and admired 
real estate developers, has launched Purva Cityspire, part of its ~ 18-acre integrated township, Purva 
City, in Edappally, Kochi. The estimated gross development value (GDV) of the project is ₹2,000 crore .  
Purva Cityspire combines a well -connected location, thoughtfully designed homes, and extensive 
landscap ing. Over 50% of the development is planned as open spaces, including ground -level and 
podium -level landscapes for recreation, relaxation and community activities.  
The launch comes amid steady appreciation in Kochi’s residential property values, supported by 
expanding employment opportunities, improving infrastructure and growing demand for quality 
housing.  
The development comprises 1,056 premium 2 and 3 -bedroom residences across six towers rising up to 
33 floors. Homes range from 1,2 71 sq. ft. to 1,895 sq. ft., with prices starting at ₹1.44 crore.   
Commenting on the launch, Mallanna Sasalu, CEO – South, Puravankara Limited, said, “Kochi 
remains a key market for Puravankara, driven by its expanding economic base, improving infrastructure 
and growing demand for quality homes. Edappally has emerged as a preferred residential destination, 
with excellent connectivity to major employment hubs, including Kakkanad. Its appeal is further 
strengthened by Metro connectivity, major highways and access to established retail, educational a nd 
healthcare facilities.  According to a recent Cushman & Wakefield report, residential property values in 
off-CBD locations such as Edappally have appreciated by approximately 11% year -on-year, reflecting 
market strength . NRIs, who account for an estimated 20 –30% of Kochi’s residential demand, continue 
to be an important buyer segment, further underscoring the city’s appeal as a long -term residential 
investment destination.”  
With Purva Cityspire, we are creating an integrated residential community that responds  to evolving 
buyer preferences. Our focus is on three aspects of everyday living: comfort at home, generous open 
spaces and lifestyle amenities that serve different generations. We believe the combination of a well -
connected location, quality development and thoughtful desig n will create lasting value for residents. ” 
Purva Cityspire offers access to the Edappally Metro Station, NH -66 and NH -544, as well as Lulu Mall 
and the Infopark and SmartCity employment hubs in Kakkanad . Educational and healthcare institutions 
in the wider area include Cochin University of Science and Technology, Aster Medcity and Amrita 
Hospital.  
The project offers more than 40 lifestyle amenities catering to all age groups. Twin clubhouse s spanning 
over 42,700 sq ft  feature wellness, recreation and entertainment facilities. Distinctive amenities include 
a private bowling alley, a golf simulator, a n observation  cum stargazing  deck and an elevated work -
from-home zone with an outdoor deck.  
The residences are designed to be open on three sides, maximising natural light and cross -ventilation . 
With just six residences per floor, the design combines high -rise living with privacy, comfort and a 
greater sense of space.  
 
Purva Cityspire is registered in three phases with the Kerala Real Estate Regulatory Authority. Phase 1 
(K-RERA/PRJ/ERN/173/2026) has a proposed completion date of 31 July 2031; Phase 2 (K -
RERA/PRJ/ERN/175/2026), 31 July 2032; and Phase 3 (K -RERA/PRJ/ERN/1 74/2026), 30 June 2033.  
 
About Puravankara Limited   
The Puravankara Group is one of India's most trusted realty majors, headquartered in Bengaluru with 
a pan -India presence. Over the past five decades, the company has catered to the full spectrum of 
housing and plotted development needs. The group has also forayed into developing Grade A 
commercial real estate and is rapidly expanding its footprint. Additionally, its interior design arm, Purva 
Streaks, serves customers seeking an integrated interior design solution.  
As of September 30, 2026, Puravankara has completed 98 projects totalling approximately 60 msft 
across nine cities: Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Coimbatore, Mangaluru, Kochi, Mumbai, Pune, and 
Goa. The company's total land bank is approximately 46 msft, and ongoing projects total 33.61 msft.
