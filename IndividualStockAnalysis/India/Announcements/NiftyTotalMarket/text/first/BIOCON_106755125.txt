COMPANY STATEMENT  
 
Biocon Announce s Pegfilgrastim Biosimilar  Marketing Approval by 
Japan Ministry of Health , Labour and Welfare ( MHLW)  
 
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, August  25, 202 6 
  
Biocon Biologics  Ltd. , a wholly -owned subsidiary of Biocon Ltd. (BSE code: 532523, NSE: 
BIOCON) , received marketing approval  for its pegfilgrastim biosimilar  (“Product”)  from 
Japan’s Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) . The product , a biosimilar to 
Neulasta®,  is developed and manufactured by Biocon Biologics  Ltd, parent entity of 
Biocon Biologics UK PLC (Biocon Biologics Ltd and Biocon Biologics UK PLC shall be 
collectively referred to as “Biocon”) , with Global Regulatory Partners Japan serv ing as 
the Marketing Authorization Holder  on behalf of Biocon.  Sandoz K.K. shall  exclusively 
promot e, sell, and distribut e the Product  in Japan.  
 
This approval further strengthens Biocon’s portfolio of biosimilar products in Japan  and 
reaffirms the company’s commitment to increasing patient access to high -quality and 
affordable medications globally . 
 
– Company Spokesperson
