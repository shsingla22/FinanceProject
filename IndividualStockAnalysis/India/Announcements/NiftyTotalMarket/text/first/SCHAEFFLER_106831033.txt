Schaeffler India Limited announces results for the third  
quarter and nine months  ended September  30, 2026  
 
 
 
• Double -digit growth momentum across both domestic and export 
businesses  
• Earnings quality continued to be managed amid volatile macro -economic 
landscape  
Pune , India  | October 27 , 2026  | Schaeffler India Limited (BSE: 505790, 
NSE: SCHAEFFLER), the Motion Technology Company, today announced 
that the Board of Directors approved the results for the third  quarter, 
ended September  30, 2026 . 
July– September  2026 ( Third  Quarter)  
• Revenue from operations (net) for the quarter (Q 3’26) was INR 30,058  
million, 27.4% higher than the corresponding quarter of 2025 and 
12.1 % higher than preceding quarter (Q 2’26) 
• PBT (before exceptional items) for the quarter (Q 3’26) was INR 4,970 
million , 20.4 % higher  than the corresponding quarter of 2025. PBT 
margin for the quarter stood at 16.5 %, compared to 17.5% during the 
corresponding quarter of 2025 and 16.9%  during the preceding 
quarter (Q 2’26) 
• Net Profit for the quarter was INR 3,70 4 million and net profit margin 
stood at 12.3% 
 
January – September  2026 ( Nine  Months)  
• Revenue from operations (net) for the nine  months period was INR 
81,942 million, higher by 21.4 %  than the corresponding period of 2025  
• PBT (before exceptional items) for the nine  months period was INR 
13,752  million, higher by 17.9% than the corresponding period of 2025. 
PBT margin for the nine  months stood at 16.8%, compared to 17.3% 
during the corresponding period of 202 5 
• Net profit for the nine  months period was INR 10,268 million and net 
profit margin stood at 12.5% compared to 12.9% during the 
corresponding period of 2025

Commenting on the results, Mr. Harsha Kadam, Managing Director and 
Chief Executive Officer, said,  “ I am pleased to share that Schaeffler India

About Schaeffler India Limited  
Schaeffler India brings a 80-year global legacy of innovation in motion technology to the Indian market, 
offering precision engineering solutions. As a trusted partner for making motion more efficient, intelligent, 
and sustainable – over the entire life cycle, Schaeffler offers innov ative technologies, products, and solutions 
for electric mobility, CO₂ -efficient drives, chassis solutions and renewable energies. With five manufacturing 
plants in India (Talegaon, Savli, Maneja, Hosur and Shoolagiri), three R&D centers, five sales office s, and a 
strong industrial and automotive aftermarket network, Schaeffler India ensures nationwide accessibility of 
products and solutions to its customers.  
With renowned product brands and a comprehensive range of products and services in the mobility 
ecosystem by means of eight motion families: from bearing solutions and linear guidance systems through 
to repair and monitoring services, Schaeffler is committ ed to partnering with its customers across segments 
for their engineering and motion technology needs. With its presence in India for over 60 years and with a 
dedicated workforce of over 3,800 employees Schaeffler is a leading motion technology company in India.
