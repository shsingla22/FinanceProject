Sub:  Intimation regarding receipt of approval from the Stock Exchanges for 
reclassification of an entity forming part of the ‘Promoter(s) Group’  category to the 
‘Public’ category  
 
This is in continuation of our letter no. APL/SEC/26/2023 -24/89 dated  15th February 2024 , wherein 
it was informed that , based on the request received from Hitech Insurance Broking Services 
Limited (‘HIBSL’), an entity forming part of the Promoter(s) Group of the Company,  the Company 
had applied to the Stock Exchanges, BSE Limited (‘BSE’) and National Stock Exchange of India 
Limited  (‘NSE’) , seeking their approval for reclassification  of HIBSL  from the ‘Promoter(s) Group’ 
category to the ‘Public’ category . 
 
HIBSL does not hold any shares in the Company . 
 
In continuation to the above and in accordance with Regulation 31A(8) of the SEBI  (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations , 2015 , we would like to inform you that 
the Company has today i.e., 11th September 2024 received the necessary approvals from BSE 
and NSE, for the reclassification  of HIBSL from the ‘Promoter(s) Group’ category to the ‘Public’ 
category . Copies  of the  approval letters from the stock exchanges are enclosed . 
 
Accordingly , HIBSL ceases to be a part of the Promoter(s) Group of the Company.  
 
You are requested to take the above information on record.  
  
Thanking you,  
 
Yours truly,
