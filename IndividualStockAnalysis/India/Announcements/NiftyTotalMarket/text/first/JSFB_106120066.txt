Bengaluru, January  21st, 2025 : The Board of Directors of Jana Small Finance Bank Limited at 
its meeting today, approved the unaudited financial results for the quarter and nine months 
period ending December  31, 2024.

About  Jana  Small  Finance  Bank  
 
Jana Small Finance Bank, a scheduled commercial bank, is the 4th largest Small Finance Bank in India  
with an experience of over 16 years in lending and serving 12 million customers. The Bank was  
launched in March 2018, is a solid combination of a digital institution and national presence across  22 
states and 2 union territories, spread across 778 branches.  From its origin of being a NBFC -MFI,  the 
bank has transformed steadily  over the years, leading to 68% of its lending book being secured,  mostly 
backed by mortgages. The Bank has created a fast growing retail deposit franchise with a  strong brand 
presence and high standards of customer service . The bank's management team has an  average  
experience  of 24  years  with  a reputed  and well-rounded  Board.
