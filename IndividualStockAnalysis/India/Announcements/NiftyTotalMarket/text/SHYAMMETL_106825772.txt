PRESS RELEASE  
Shyam Metalics’ Subsidiary Shyam Sel and Power Limited Secures NCLT Sanction for 
Merger of Shree Venkateshwara Electrocast Private Limited; Effective Retroactively from 
April 01, 2024  
Strategic amalgamation consolidates aluminium foil operations under a single umbrella while 
unlocking significant financial synergies through substantial tax efficiency.  
Kolkata, October 23, 2026  — Shyam Metalics and Energy Limited (BSE: 543299, NSE: 
SHYAMMETL), a leading integrated metal producing company in India, today announced that the 
Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Kolkata Bench, vide its order dated October 
16, 2026, has approved the Scheme of Amalgamation of Shree Venkateshwara Electrocast Private 
Limited (SVEPL) into and with Shyam Sel and Power Limited (SSPL), a flagship wholly-owned 
subsidiary of the Company.  
The merger takes effect retroactively from the Appointed Date of April 01, 2024 . Upon filing the 
certified copy of the NCLT order with the Registrar of Companies (ROC), West Bengal, SVEPL 
will be dissolved without winding up.  
Key Highlights & Strategic Rationale  
1. Product Portfolio Integration & Centralization:  The amalgamation brings 
complementary product specializations under a single corporate umbrella. It rationalizes 
and centralizes the Group’s growing aluminium foil product portfolio, enabling operational 
efficiencies, enhanced supply chain coordination, and seamless market reach.  
2. Capital Efficiency & Simplified Structure:  Since SVEPL was already a 100% wholly-
owned subsidiary of SSPL, no fresh equity shares will be issued or allotted, and the existing 
share capital of SVEPL will stand cancelled upon implementation.  
3. Strengthened Balance Sheet & Continuity:  The merged entity (SSPL) maintains a robust 
balance sheet. All assets, liabilities, contracts, and workforce of SVEPL vest smoothly into 
SSPL. 
Significant Financial Synergies & Tax Benefits  
Under Section 72A of the Income-tax Act, 1961, the merger allows SSPL to absorb and set off the 
accumulated carried-forward business losses and unabsorbed depreciation of SVEPL, creating a 
comprehensive eligible tax shield for the combined entity.  
At the effective corporate tax rate applicable under Section 115BAA, this tax set-off yields a 
substantial direct net tax benefit and immediate cash-flow savings for the combined entity.  
The Income Tax Department has considered the modified Appointed Date of April 1, 2024, and 
issued its No Objection Certificate (NOC) for the Scheme on May 15, 2026.

About Shyam Metalics and Energy Limited  
Shyam Metalics is a leading and fastest-growing integrated metal-producing company based in 
India primarily in the steel industry in West Bengal, Odisha, Jharkhand, and Madhya Pradesh with 
a focus on Long & Flat Steel Products, Ferro Alloys, Aluminium, and Stainless Steel. The company 
got listed itself on the exchanges in 2021 and possesses a market capitalization of more than ₹ 
33,000 Cr. Headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal, the company is amongst the largest producers 
of ferro alloys in terms of installed capacity in India. The company has an aggregate installed metal 
capacity of 16.93 MTPA across the steel value chain and is supported by a 467 MW aggregated 
installed capacity of captive power plants. For more information, please visit our website at 
https://www.shyammetalics.com/ .
