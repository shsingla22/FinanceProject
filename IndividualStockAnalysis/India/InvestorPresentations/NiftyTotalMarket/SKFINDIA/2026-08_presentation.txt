Future Focus: Developing next -generation cage technology capable of ~30,000 rpm for 
future EV platforms.The ChallengeEV and hybrid powertrains demand high -speed, 
low-noise (NVH) and thermally stable bearings in compact designs. 
The Solution
Developed a high -speed deep groove ball bearing capable of 
~20,000 rpm. 
Optimised  cage design and internal geometry for low friction, 
reduced heat generation and enhanced durability. 
The Impact
Delivered quieter operation, higher energy efficiency and 
improved drivetrain reliability.
