Message from Managing Director
/4
Akey milestone inQ3has been securing orders from BSNL forBharatNet Phase III,both directly forthe
Punjab Telecom Circle and through consortium arrangements with Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd.(RVNL) forthe
Uttar Pradesh (East) and Uttar Pradesh (West) circles .
With acomprehensive product portfolio encompassing routers, optical fiber cables, fiber connectivity
solutions, transport solutions, and power management systems, along with extensive experience in
deploying over 200,000fiber kilometers ontheground, HFCL iswell-positioned tomeet therequirements of
theBharatNet Phase IIIprogram .Asatrusted partner, weeagerly anticipate continued collaboration and
meaningful contributions totheprogram's success .Our proven expertise inexecuting strategically vital
projects further reinforces ourreputation asareliable partner forboth private and government enterprises
worldwide .
During the quarter, we also inaugrated a state -of-the-art defense equipment manufacturing facility in 
Hosur, underscoring our commitment to innovation, excellence, and national advancement. This facility 
will empower us to provide cutting -edge defense technologies to the armed forces, enhancing their 
operational efficiency and confidence in critical missions. This facility will produce advanced Thermal 
Weapon Sights, Electronic Fuzes , High Capacity Radio Relay (HCRR) and Surveillance Radars, designed to 
meet the evolving needs of defence forces across the Globe.
