international markets .

routing and digital engagement capabilities, while engaging with financial -services leaders and technology partners, on

servicing, payments and support .

messaging application onSalesforce AgentExchange inAugust 2026 ,enabling enterprises tosend SMS and WhatsApp messages

customer data remaining within theorganization’s Salesforce environment .

capabilities across thecommunications ecosystem .
