Sustainability strategy
25
Trent has maintained "B-" in the CDP (Carbon Disclosure Program) - Climate Change 2024 evaluation.
The rating signifies that Trent is conscious about the environmental impacts and is taking appropriate actions.Three Pillars
•Resource Efficient:
─Energy conservation, renewable sources of 
energy, packaging waste reduction and 
management practices 
•Responsible by Design: 
─Selection of materials, development of 
suppliers, design of supply chain networks 
and processes
•People Conscious: 
─Prioritizing the well -being and growth of our 
own employees  and communities
