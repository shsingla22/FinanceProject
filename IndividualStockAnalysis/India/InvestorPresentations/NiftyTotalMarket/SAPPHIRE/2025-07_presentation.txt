FY25 Highlights
•In a difficult year Sapphire has delivered double 
digit Rest. count & Revenue growth. EBITDA 
grew by 4% at 17.1% and Adj. EBITDA declined 
by 4%. We added 91 restaurants during the 
year.

with double digit revenue growth of 11% and 
Rest. EBITDA margin of 17.3%. We achieved  a 
significant milestone of 500 KFC restaurants 
during the year and thereby doubled the count 
over last ~3 years.
•Sri Lanka business had a very strong 
turnaround with 14% LKR revenue growth 
(double digit SSSG & SSTG) & 24% in ₹ terms
