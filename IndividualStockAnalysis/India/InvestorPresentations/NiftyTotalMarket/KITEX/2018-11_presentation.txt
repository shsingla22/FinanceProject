Kerala floods
Kerala witnessed one of it’s worst flood floods during the month of August 2018. Though the area of Kitex 
factory was not affected, transportation via road, rail and air was affected for almost 2 weeks. 
Transportation of raw materials and employees to our factory was mildly affected.
Kitex Garments had overcome challenges posed by the floods without any impact in production, operations, 
turnover and  profits of the company.
