Wind World India O&M Portfolio Acquisition | A Landmark Transaction
This is a marquee transaction in the renewable O&M sector and a milestone in the journey of 
Inox Green
Inox Green has completed the payment for the transfer of Wind World India’s 4.5 GW Wind O&M 
business through an NCLT process
The payment has been made through Inox Green’s subsidiary Vibhav Energy Private Limited (VEPL) 
pursuant to the Business Transfer Agreement (BTA) executed between VEPL and WWIL
Line-by-line financial consolidation will take place from Q3 FY27 
The acquired O&M portfolio services a marquee client base that includes the Tata Group, ReNew , 
Greenko Group, Apraava Energy and Hindustan Zinc, amongst others
The assets are spread across key wind -rich States including Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, 
Rajasthan, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh and Andhra Pradesh
The portfolio generated revenue of Rs. 580 crore in FY26 and benefits from contracted annual price 
escalation of approximately 5%
The transaction is value accretive for shareholders
There is significant scope to bring in operational efficiencies through enhanced service offerings, price 
and cost optimisation; integration initiatives under way
