Q4FY26 Pax Traffic and YoY Growth FY26 Pax Traffic and YoY Growth

•Domestic pax traffic up 1.5% YoY (FY26 up 0.5% YoY) and International pax traffic down 1.6% YoY  (FY26 up 2.0% YoY)
