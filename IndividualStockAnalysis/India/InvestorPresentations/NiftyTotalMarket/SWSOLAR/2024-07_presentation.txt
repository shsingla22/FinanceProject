| CONSOLIDATED PROFIT & LOSS – 1Q FY25
Revenue has grown  78%  YoY in Q1 aided by higher  execution in Domestic  
EPC projects . Sequentially,  top-line declined  by 22%  due to tight  liquidity  
conditions,  which  are expected  to ease  with  improvement in credit  ratings
Gross  margins  came  in at 11.1% in Q1 compared  to FY24 gross  margin  of 
10.3%. With  legacy  international  projects  behind  us, gross  margins  are likely  
to continue to trend  at ~10%
O&M  gross  margins  are enhanced  due to one-off income  of ~INR  3 crore  due

margin  was 23% in 1QFY 24, and has begun  to trend  towards  more  steady -
state margins  compared  to FY24 margin  of 16.7%
