| CONSOLIDATED PROFIT & LOSS – 2Q FY25
Revenue  has grown  36% YoY in Q2 aided  by higher  execution  in Domestic  
EPC projects . Sequentially,  top-line grew  by 13%.
Gross  margins  came  in at 10.1% in Q2 compared  to FY24 gross  margin  of

to continue  to trend  at ~10%
O&M  gross  margins  at 28% are higher  than  average  due to lower  expenditure

towards  more  steady -state  margins  (~25%)
