ABB in India at a glance
ABB Group
ABB is a technology leader in electrification and automation, enabling a more 
sustainable and resource -efficient future. By connecting its engineering and 
digitalization expertise, ABB helps industries run at high performance, while becoming 
more efficient, productive and sustainable so they outperform. At ABB, we call this 
‘Engineered to Outrun’. The company has over 140 years of history and around 110,000 
employees worldwide. ABB’s shares are listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange (ABBN) and 
Nasdaq Stockholm (ABB).
ABB in India
ABB is  present in India for over a century and manufacturing for more than 75 years. The 
manufacturing unit is a listed entity. It operates with the entire eco -system of ABB R&D, 
Engineering, and Services.
