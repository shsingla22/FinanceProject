Vedanta Limited posts  record Q2 & H1 results  
 
Record Quarterly  PAT of ₹6,20 2 crore, up 151% YoY  
 
Record  Quarterly  EBITDA of ₹9,938 crore, up 10 6% YoY  
 
Best in Industry, ND/EBITDA ratio of 0.1x , Net debt reduced by ₹4,624 crore  
 
Mumbai,  Oct 28, 202 6: Vedanta Limited  (VEDL)  today announced its Unaudited Consolidated Results 
for the Second Quarter  and Half Year ended 3 0th Sep 2026. 
 
• Best -ever half-yearly PAT  of ₹11,49 6 crore, up 15 1% YoY  
• Record half-yearly Revenue of ₹49,26 8 crore, up 54% YoY  
• Highest -ever half-yearly EBITDA  of ₹18,407 crore, up 10 3% YoY  
• Net Debt / EBITDA improved to 0.1x, best -in-class in the industry  
• Zinc International  Gamsberg  Phase II  commissioned , total MIC capacity to 500 kt p.a. 
• FACOR : Successful  extraction of  first ton ne of underground ore from Ostapal Mines  
• Portfolio of 11 critical  mineral blocks , 5 under exploration  
• Declared 1st Interim Dividend of ₹5 per share  
 
Financial Highlights  Q2 and H1 FY27 : 
o Achieved highest -ever quarterly  revenue of ₹25,812 crore, up 57% YoY and 10% QoQ, taking 
half-yearly  revenue to a record ₹49,26 8 crore, up 54% YoY.  
o Delivered best -ever quarterly  EBITDA of ₹ 9,938 crore, up 10 6% YoY and 1 7% QoQ, taking 
half-yearly  EBITDA to a record ₹18, 407 crore, up 10 3% YoY.  
o Achieved highest -ever quarterly PAT* of ₹6,20 2 crore, up 151% YoY and 17% QoQ, with half-
yearly PAT reaching a record ₹11,49 6 crore, up 15 1% YoY.  
o Quarterly EBITDA margin† of 60%, up by 1055 bps YoY and 293 bps QoQ . 
o Net Debt/EBITDA improved to 0.1x from 0.3x in Q1 FY27 . 
o Net Debt reduced by ₹4,6 24 crore QoQ to ₹3,6 75 crore, from ₹8,299 crore in June 2026, 
majorly driven by Cash Flow from Operations . 
o Generated strong FCF (pre -capex) of ₹5, 803 crore in Q2 FY27, compared with ₹4,412 crore 
in Q1 FY27, with cumulative H1 FCF (pre -capex) of ₹10, 215 crore . 
o Invested Growth Capex of ₹1,1 35 crore in Q2 FY27, with cumulative H1 FY27 Growth Capex 
of ₹2,283 crore.  
o Strong liquidity with Cash & Cash Equivalents at ₹26,6 22 crore compared to ₹19,992 crore at 
the end of Q1 FY27.
