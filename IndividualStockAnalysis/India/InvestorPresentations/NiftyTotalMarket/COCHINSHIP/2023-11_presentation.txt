IMPORTANT OPERATIONAL 
EVENTS FOR H1 FY 2024
=== page 5 ===
Highlights –Shipbuilding 
-Delivered four Electric Hybrid 100 Pax Water Metro boat to Kochi Metro .
-Completed the steel cutting of 2 nos. of Commissioning Service Operation Vessels (CSOV) 
being built for Pelagic Wind Services, Cyprus.
-Received order for 8 nos. of Hybrid Electric Catamaran Passenger Vessels for IWAI 
-Ministry of Defence, inked a deal with CSL for the Mid Life Upgrade and Re -Powering of 
'INS Beas' at a cost of Rs. 313.42 Crs. 
–The estimated duration is around 24 months.
–The project involves modernization and upgradation of various systems and equipment 
on board the ship to enhance its operational capabilities.
=== page 6 ===
Highlights –Shipbuilding 
-Naval Ship Repair Yard ( Karwar ), Indian Navy, signed Multiple Refit Contract (MRC) with 
Cochin Shipyard Limited for Water Jet Fast Attack Crafts refits.
-M/s. Syncrolift , Norway, delivered ship lift platform having size 130 x 27 Mtr. and 2000 T 
approx., at ISRF site of CSL. Commissioning activities are in progress .
-CSL was honoured with meritorious PMA National Award 2023 in the category of ‘Project 
Excellence’ for the remarkable feat of construction of the Indigenous Aircraft Carrier (INS 
Vikrant), India’s largest warship.
-Cochin Shipyard Limited upgraded to ‘Schedule A’ Company .
-CSL awarded seed funding of Rs. 1.70 crores to 5 start-ups through IIMK (LIVE) backed 
USHUS Marine Start Up Support Program. 
=== page 7 ===
Highlights –Shipbuilding 
-UCSL bagged an international order from M/s. Wilson Shipowning AS, Norway, for Design 
and Construction of 6 Nos. new generation diesel electric 3800 DWT general cargo vessel 
with an option for 8 more vessels
–The estimated contract value is Rs.580 crores (Approx.) and will be completed within 
March 2026.
–These vessels are intended for the transport of general cargo at inland as well as 
coastal waters of Europe.
