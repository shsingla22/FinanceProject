Management Commentary
"The Company reported sequential growth in Q2 FY27, with Revenue from Operations increasing by 6.9% quarter -on-quarter to ₹5,063  million, supported by steady 
execution of its solar pump order book despite a competitive pricing environment. Operating EBITDA stood at ₹574 million, wit h a margin of 11.3%, while PAT* stood at 
₹396 million, translating into a margin of 7.7%.
Operating EBITDA margin reduced by 437 basis points quarter -on-quarter, primarily due to higher raw material costs and increased  operating expenses. Higher raw 
material costs accounted for approximately 210 basis points of the decline, driven by an increase in aluminium frame, glass a nd copper prices, as well as higher zinc and 
steel costs at the Company’s subsidiary, Walso  Solar Solutions Private Limited, particularly in relation to orders secured at earlier input cost levels. The remaining 227 b asis 
points were attributable to higher employee costs associated with strengthening the Company's workforce to support the scale -up of its Solar EPC business, increased 
project related operating expenses, and higher debtors provisions resulting from the applicable accounting standard. Conseque ntly, PAT* margin declined by 343 basis 
points quarter -on-quarter to 7.7%, largely reflecting the decline in operating profitability.
The Company's solar pump order book currently stands at 9,487 pumps, complemented by a near -term pipeline of approximately 10,00 0 pumps across direct PM -KUSUM, 
Magel Tyala , indirect PM -KUSUM and export opportunities, providing visibility on future execution.
Against the backdrop of delays in the rollout of PM -KUSUM 2.0, the Company continues to strategically diversify its revenue stre ams beyond its core government -led solar 
irrigation business. It is actively expanding its presence across Rooftop Solar, Utility -Scale Solar and Commercial & Industrial  (C&I) Solar EPC segments. The combined order 
book across these segments currently stands at approximately 143 MW, and a robust pipeline of 168 MW. This diversification is  expected to broaden the Company's 
addressable market, strengthen its revenue base and create additional avenues for long -term growth.
Further strengthening its backward integration capabilities, the Company, through its wholly owned subsidiary, Oswal Solar En ergy Private Limited, has announced plans 
to establish a 1.2 GW TOPCon  solar cell manufacturing facility, subject to shareholders' approval. This strategic initiative is aimed at securing a reliab le supply of Domestic 
Content Requirement (DCR) -compliant solar cells, enhancing supply chain integration and improving cost competitiveness over the medium to long term. Solar cell 
manufacturing will not only improve its supply chain and market competitiveness, but will also improve the margin profile of the company.
Looking ahead, the Company remains focused on disciplined execution of its order book, restoring operating margins through co st optimisation and operational 
efficiencies, and building a more diversified and integrated business platform to drive sustainable growth and long -term value c reation for all stakeholders."
-Vivek Gupta 
Chairman and Managing Director, Oswal Pumps Limited*Attributable to the Owners of the parent
