–Addition ofmore retail outlets including Kiranas thus expanding itsreach and increase inthe number ofproducts being sold ateach counter,
enhancing itsrange
–Eradication ofthe wholesale and weighted distribution, resulting inexpansion ofitsreach and presence inover 600 districts across India
–Price stability across geographies which assured channel partners with more security, confidence and better returns ontheir investments
