Refex Industries: Spearheading Exponential Growth in Sustainable Solutions

Company),  headquartered  in Chennai,  is a pioneer  in 
sustainability -driven  solutions  in India . With  over  22 years

wheeler  EVs. Leveraging  technology -driven  offerings,  Refex

lead  in advancing  solutions  that  tackle  environmental

•With  constraints  on hydroelectric  projects  and nuclear  power  concerns,  coal  is
