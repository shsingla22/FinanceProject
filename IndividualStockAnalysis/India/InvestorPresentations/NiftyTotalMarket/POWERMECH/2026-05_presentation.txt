4Leadership Commentary
Commenting  on the performance  Mr. Sajja  Kishore  Babu,  Chairman  and Managing  Director  said:
“As we conclude  FY2026, the year reflects  meaningful  progress  in strengthening  our position  across  the power  and infrastructure  sector  
through  disciplined  project  execution,  expansion  into larger  EPC opportunities  and diversification  across  new business  verticals . During  the 
year,  we successfully  scaled  our core Power  ETC business,  secured  a high profile  Balance  of Plant  EPC contact  and strengthened  the O&M 
portfolio . Looking  ahead,  we are optimistic  about  opportunities  in areas  such as energy  storage  and urban  mobility .
From  a financial  perspective,  a strong  performance  was delivered  during  the quarter  reflecting  steady  execution  across  business  verticals . 
Revenue  from Operations  was Rs. 2,111 crore  registering  a 14% YoY increase,  supported  by sustained  execution  in core segments  and ramp -up 
of operations  on newly  secured  orders,  except  for administrative  delays  in the water  division . EBITDA  for the quarter  was Rs. 237 crore,  
reflecting  a YoY growth  of 2% with EBITDA  margins  at 11.2% which  were lower  compared  to Q4FY25, primarily  due to increased  operating  
expenses  and reduction  in other  income . For FY2026, Revenue  from Operations  reached  Rs. 6,062 crore,  marking  a 16% YoY increase  and 
EBITDA  was Rs. 750 crore  increasing  16% YoY, with a margin  of 12.3%. 
 
Order  inflows  during  FY2026 remained  broad  based  across  thermal  power,  industrial  infrastructure,  O&M,  railways  and energy  transition  projects . The Company  secured  total order  
inflow  of Rs. 7,210 crore  and was lower  than expectations  primarily  due to the unexpected  cancellation  of the BESS order  of Rs. 1,563 crore  by WBSEDCL . However,  the total order  
backlog  (including  MDO)  as on 31st March  2026 was Rs. 55,151 crore,  providing  strong  execution  visibility  over the medium  term.
A major  milestone  during  the year was the award  of the Balance  of Plant  EPC package  for the 1 × 800 MW Singareni  thermal  power  project  from BHEL,  which  marked  our entry  into high 
value,  integrated  Balance  of Plant  EPC projects . This order  represents  a strategic  inflection  point  as the Company  focuses  on larger  industrial  EPC led opportunities  and strengthens  its 
positioning  in thermal  power  infrastructure . The Company  also secured  several  other  strategic  orders  during  the year. In particular,  towards  the end of FY26, we entered  metro  O&M 
which  expands  our service  portfolio  beyond  the core O&M business . The MDOs  also progressed  during  FY2026, with both contracts  now contributing  meaningfully  to the cash flow from 
operations .
As we enter  FY2027, we remain  focused  on disciplined  execution  and improving  our profitability . With leading  delivery  capabilities,  long standing  customer  relationships  and a 
strategically  diversified  order  book,  we are confident  of our market  position  in the near term. For FY2027, the Company  is cautiously  optimistic  and focused  on targeting  order  inflows  
through  increased  participation  in BOP EPC packages,  O&M contracts  and energy  infrastructure  projects .”
