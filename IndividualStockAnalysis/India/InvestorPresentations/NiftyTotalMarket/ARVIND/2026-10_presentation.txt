Macro Economic conditions
1.Defense, aerospace and renewable energy spending remains strong, supporting demand for composites, protective materials and 
technical textiles.
2.Early festive-season trends are encouraging, continues to provide support for Arvind’s domestic Textile business.
3.US market presents mixed signals; strong GDP growth, improving consumer confidence and rising disposable incomes are positive
indicators, although higher interest rates and inflation could weigh in on demand in the coming quarters.
4.EU apparel market is stabilizing, with improving conditions for major retailers and brands. The India-EU FTA is expected to provide 
an additional tailwind for exporters such as Arvind.
Business
1.Advanced Materials India business is strong, higher demand is driven by industrial capex, infrastructure, mobility and specialty
applications. Expected to achieve ~18% revenue and EBITDA growth with a stable margin of 14-15%
2.Dalco integration assumed full steam, capex programs are preponed to foster higher growth, margin to remain stable at 15-16%.
3.Textile segment is expected to achieve early double digit revenue growth, while margin may remain at current levels due to 
elevated cost scenario. 
4.The operating environment continues to be challenged by persistent raw material cost escalation, nevertheless the Company 
remains focused on executing its mitigation measures effectively, which are expected to partly offset these pressures and support 
margin resilience.
