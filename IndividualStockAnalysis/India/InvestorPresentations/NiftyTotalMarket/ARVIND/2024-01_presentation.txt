technologies

technologies

opportunities for the industry

events, and hosting technology visits

Outlook for Q4 and FY2024
Demand
Market demand showing signs of coming off the bottom, though caution prevails
Costs
Cotton and other input costs likely to remain soft
Container shipping costs likely to remain elevated
Revenue & Margin
We expect a stronger Q4 leading to a healthy H2 as guided earlier
•Based on the current trends, Denim & Garments volumes likely to show improvements.
•Textile and AMD margins expected to remain healthy at around Q3 levels
Capex & Debt
Long Term debt expected to settle around Rs 400 crores by end of March 2024
Expect to incur about Rs. 220 crores in capex during the year
