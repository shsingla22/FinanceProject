Amber Strategy
With Government’s encouraging policy framework for domestic manufacturing, Amber plans to expand its portfolio in the electro nics (EMS) space by further strengthening 
its R&D and PCBA manufacturing capabilities through backward integration of Bare PCB Boards and adding new industries in its EMS  portfolio. This strategic move positions 
Amber as a key player in the electronics EMS space, aligning well with the sector's anticipated double -digit growth in FY 25 and  beyond.
