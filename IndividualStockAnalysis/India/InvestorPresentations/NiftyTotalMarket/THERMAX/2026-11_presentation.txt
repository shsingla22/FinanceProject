Confidential and Internal Purpose only Conserving Resources, Preserving the FutureConserving Resources, Preserving the Future. 16Highlights at Thermax
•During the quarter, Thermax launched the Thermax -i series of intelligent boilers and heaters. The series 
continuously optimises performance as operating conditions change. Its first product, UltraPac -i, is a 
biomass -fired boiler that anticipates variations in fuel characteristics. Pilot installations have 
demonstrated fuel savings of up to 10%, offering the potential to lower operating costs and associated 
carbon emissions.
•Thermax has introduced REDD, its new digital mascot and customer companion, as part of the ‘One in 
Red’ initiative. REDD represents a new, recognisable  face for Thermax - bringing greater consistency, 
accessibility and warmth to the way customers engage with the brand.
•The recently concluded WETEX 2026 in Dubai gave Thermax a strong platform to showcase its 
solutions across clean water, clean air, clean energy and digital technologies. Water solutions took 
centre  stage, with advanced desalination, water recovery and wastewater treatment technologies 
drawing strong interest. The event also enabled meaningful customer and partner engagement across 
the MENA region, strengthening relationships and opening new opportunities for collaboration.
