MDs Comments
Mr. Pradeep Ghisulal Rathod
Chairman & Managing DirectorCommenting  on the Result,  Mr. Pradeep  Rathod,  Chairman  & Managing  Director,  Cello  World  Limited  
said
“Cello  World  has shown  growth  in both revenue  and profitability  compared  to Q4 FY23. In the quarter  gone by, 
revenues  increased  by 5%, while  EBITDA  rose by 20%. We experienced  good growth  in our writing  instruments  
and furniture  businesses  during  this period .
For the full year,  our Revenues  have grown  by 11% with profitability  growing  even faster  at 24% despite  the well-
recognized  sluggish  demand  within  the industry  throughout  the year. This is a testament  to our widespread  market  
presence,  well-established  brand,  scale of operations,  and PAN India distribution  network,  which  help us identify  
the changing  needs  and preferences  of our customers  and, hence,  innovate  our products  accordingly  to cater to 
those  requirements  and stay relevant  in the market,  as well as having  an edge over competitors .
Looking  forward  to FY25, we anticipate  a growth  rate of 15%-17%, with demand  expected  to rise in the second  
half of the year. Our new glassware  facility  in Rajasthan  will commence  operations  in Q2 FY25, with a ramp -up by 
year end. This will substantially  enhance  our glassware  business,  driving  rapid  growth  in this vertical  over the next 
two years . Additionally,  on the e-commerce  front,  new avenues  have emerged,  presenting  excellent  opportunities  
for future  growth .
At Cello,  we always  focus  on outperforming  industry  growth  and continuously  improving  our position  in the 
consumer  market . We remain  confident  in our business's  growth  potential  and its diverse  portfolio,  which  also 
benefits  from the trend  of premiumization .”
