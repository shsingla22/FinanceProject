MD’s Insights andReflections
“Against  the backdrop  of continued  pressure  in the global  economic  environment,  we are pleased  to report  a solid  performance  this 
quarter . We have  reported  consolidated  revenues  of Rs. 1,405 crore  in Q2 FY27, an increase  of 55% YoY,  accompanied  by improved  
profitability  and a stronger  balance  sheet . New  contracts  worth  Rs.673 crore  in Q2 FY27 across  the various  segments  have  further  
strengthened  our order  book  and growth  visibility .
We are encouraged  by the progress  made  during  the first half of FY27, building  on the recovery  seen  over recent  quarters . Strong  
domestic  demand,  improving  business  in international  markets  and a focus  on operational  efficiency  have  supported  this 
performance . Our investments  in capacity  expansion,  new capabilities  and a wider  product  portfolio  are contributing  to more  
sustainable  and balanced  growth . While  global  trade  policies,  geopolitical  developments  and supply  chain  uncertainties  continue  to 
create  challenges,  our diversified  operations  and longstanding  customer  relationships  help us navigate  this environment . We remain  
excited  by the opportunities  across  commercial  vehicles,  passenger  vehicles,  railways  and industrial  applications,  both  in India  and 
overseas .
Our priorities  are to achieve  higher  capacity  utilisation , improve  efficiencies  and increase  profitability  from  investments  made  over 
the past few years . We are making  progress  in scaling  up casting  and aluminium  forging  operations,  while  our Mexico  facility  
strengthens  our presence  closer  to international  customers . Expansion  in Railways,  passenger  vehicles  and higher  value -added  
products  will further  support  growth . Looking  ahead,  we remain  confident  about  our long-term  prospects . With  a strong  order  
pipeline  and a more  diversified  business,  we remain  focused  on sustaining  growth  momentum,  improving  cash  flows,  strengthening  
our balance  sheet  and enhancing  return  on capital,  while  creating  long-term  value  for all stakeholders .”MR. NARESH JALAN
