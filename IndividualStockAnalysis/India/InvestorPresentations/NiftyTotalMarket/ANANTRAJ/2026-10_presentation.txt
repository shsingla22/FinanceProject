Key Highlights of H1 FY27
9Data Center & Cloud Services
•Anant  Raj Cloud  Private  Limited  continues  to expand  its cloud  services  and data  Centre  infrastructure . The Company  has operationalized  two data  Centre  
campuses  with  a combined  capacity  of 28 MW  and is targeting  an aggregate  IT load  capacity  of 357 MW  by FY 2031 –32 in North  and South  India .
•The Company  expects  to achieve  an aggregate  IT load  capacity  of 63 MW  by the end of FY 2026 –27 along  with  AI enabled  Infrastructure/  Workload . The 
expansion  of additional  cloud  services  (including  AI enabled  Infrastructure)  under  the brand  name  “Ashok  Cloud”  at Manesar  and Panchkula  is progressing  
as scheduled .
•Further,  the Company  is on track  to commence  Artificial  Intelligence  (AI) enabled  services  during  the current  financial  year,  in line with  its planned  
expansion  into AI-led digital  infrastructure  and services .
•Anant  Raj Cloud  Singapore  Pte. Ltd. (“AR  Cloud  Singapore”),  a wholly  owned  subsidiary  of Anant  Raj Limited,  is established  in Singapore  with  the objective  
of sourcing  clients  from  Singapore,  Southeast  Asia and other  global  markets  for the data  centres  established  by Anant  Raj Cloud  Private  Limited  at three  
locations  in Haryana . Furthermore,  Anant  Raj Cloud  Singapore  Pte. Ltd. and Orange  Business  Services  Singapore  Pte. Ltd, a Singapore -based  digital  
technology  and network  services  company,  have  signed  a Memorandum  of Understanding  (MoU)  with  the Government  of Haryana,  through  the 
Department  of Industries  & Commerce . The collaboration  aims  to promote  Haryana -based  cloud  and data  centre  services  to global  markets  and facilitate  
the hosting  of overseas  data  in the state . 
•The Company  has filed  the Composite  Scheme  of Arrangement  with  the National  Stock  Exchange  of India  Limited  and BSE Limited  for obtaining  their  
respective  No Objection  Letters/Observation  Letters  (NOCs) . The requisite  formalities  have  been  completed,  including  the receipt  of the necessary  in-
principle  no-objection  certificates/no -objection  certificates  from  all the Banks  and submission  to the Stock  Exchanges/  the Securities  and Exchange  Board  of 
India  (“SEBI”) . The Company  expects  to receive  the requisite  NOCs  from  the Stock  Exchanges/SEBI  shortly .
