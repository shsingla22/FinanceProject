Services,  out of which  117 MW  IT Load  will commence  by FY 2028 . 
• ARCPL  to invest  ~₹4,500 Crore  in Andhra  Pradesh  for development  of Data  
Center,  IT Park  and Cloud  Services . Total  planned  capacity  of 50 MW  IT load  
capacity .
