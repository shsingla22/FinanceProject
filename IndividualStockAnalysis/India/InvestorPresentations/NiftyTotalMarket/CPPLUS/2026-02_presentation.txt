Management Commentary
Commenting on the Results, Mr. Aditya Khemka, Managing Director said:
”Q3FY26marked another quarter ofprofitable growth and operational excellence forAditya Infotech Limited .During
thequarter, ourrevenue grew 37.3%toINR1,139.1crore, supported byscaled execution androbust demand forCPPLUS -
branded IPcameras and ourextensive STQC -certified portfolio .EBITDA surged 98.7%YoY toINR 144.6crore with
margins accretion by391bpsYoY to12.7%,driven byafavourable product mix, higher localization, and favourable
operating leverage .Adjusted Profit After Taxincreased 138.8%YoYtoINR96crores .
For 9MFY26,revenue grew 31.1%YoY toINR 2,798.8crores, while EBITDA rose 100.5%YoY toINR 320.6crores, with
margins expansion by395 bps YoY to11.4%.Adjusted Profit After Tax for9MFY26stood atINR 198.9crores, representing
anincrease of138.6%YoY.
Onthetechnology front, weannounced alandmark collaboration with Qualcomm Technologies todevelop AI-
enabled, insight -driven video security solutions forIndia’s industrial, enterprise, and public safety applications .The
partnership combines CPPLUS’ product ecosystem and distribution strength withQualcomm’s edge AIhardware, on-
device processing capabilities, and AIsoftware platforms, with commercial availability isexpected inthefirstquarter of
2026 .
Ourmulti -brand strategy allows ustoserve customers across multiple price points .With ournewly introduced product
‘Nexivue’,weareexpanding ourpresence inrural and mass -market segments, including theunorganized sector, with
products expected toreach themarket bytheend ofFebruary 2026 .The exit ofnon-STQC brands has created a
structural vacuum, positioning thismulti -brand approach asastrategic advantage forustomaximize market share
while preserving thepremium positioning ofCPPLUS .
On the Marketing front, we are collaborating with acclaimed actor Vijay Sethupathi asour Brand Ambassador to
strengthen our consumer presence inTamil Nadu bymaking advanced security solutions more approachable, relevant,
and culturally resonant forhouseholds and businesses alike .
Further, wecontinued tostrengthen ourleadership inIndia’s video surveillance market, supported byfavorable
industry tailwinds and theshift toward STQC -certified solutions, which isdriving consolidation infavor ofhome -grown
players .With one ofthemost comprehensive STQC -certified product portfolios and strong local manufacturing
capabilities, wearewell positioned toconsolidate market share .Our market share stood at~38.9%,inQ2FY26
highlighting asignificant market share gain of~7.5%QoQ .ForQ3FY26,contribution from CPPLUS stood at~87%of
revenue with IPcameras forming nearly ~75%ofouroverall portfolio .
Tosupport thisgrowing demand, wecontinue toscale manufacturing capacity, currently at1.8million units permonth,
with plans toscale to2.1million units permonth byQ4FY26.
Backed bystrong industry tailwind and disciplined execution, wearerevising ourFY26outlook toreflect revenue
growth attheupper end oftheprojected range and EBITDA margins of11–12%.Wearealso increasing ourPAT
guidance to7–7.5%,reinforcing ourcommitment tolong-term value creation forallstakeholders .Aditya Khemka
