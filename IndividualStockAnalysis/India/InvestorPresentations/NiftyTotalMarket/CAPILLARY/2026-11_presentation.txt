Our Growth levers
1. NRR Expansion
FY27 Q2 Organic NRR at 116%(CC 109%) from our three levers of expanding existing customer revenue, 
viz.,: 
●platform usage overages and inflationary increases,
●product upgrades and service upsells, and
●new brands, geographies and business units
NRR expansion is further supported by customers from our earlier acquisitions that have now fully 
migrated to our platform, opening up expansion opportunities across our product suite.
2. New Customer wins
Accelerating momentum with targeted enterprise wins and larger deal sizes across global markets.
FY 27 Q2 TTM New ACV grew by 79% YoY (excl. 1 large healthcare customer*) largely driven by new wins, the 
majority of which are anchored on our AI stack - aiRA.
3. M&A
The SessionMacquisition, completed on May 1st, is performing as planned and has achieved break-even for Q2/H1 . 
Integration remains on track, and we continue to build on SessionMenterprise relationships across North America, 
LATAM and APAC.
* The TTM Q2 FY26 base excludes an exceptional new contract value from our first entry into the healthcare vertical.
=== page 10 ===
Our Profitability levers
1. NRR-linked margin expansion
Incremental revenue from existing customers comes at higher gross margin, improving profitability. Expansions 
leverage hosting and customer support capacity already in place, with these costs not scaling in proportion to 
incremental revenue. 
Subscription GM% is at a stable 67% in FY27 H1 (67% in FY27 Q2) led by NRR-linked expansion at higher margins;this 
is despiteSessionMrevenues addedat lower GMs.
2. Leverage on non-COGS
~60% of our costs are linked to non-COGS - technology, S&M, Corporate functions, which do not grow linearly with 
revenues.
YoY Non-COGS costs increased in FY27 H1 by only 34% compared to the 47% increase in revenue from operations.. 
Further a  large majority of the Non-COGS cost growth came from the SessionMacquisition.
3. Upgrade of Customers from M&A
Post upgrade to our platform customers move from a ~30% gross margin to a ~65% margin, leading to better 
profitability and cash flow generation, making our M&A a 4-5 yearcash pay back engine.
In FY27 Q2 we completed our first customer migration leveraging our AI-powered migration capabilities, laying the 
groundwork for a growing number of migrations in the quarters ahead.

Performance Divergence
Organic NRR of 116% (CC 109%) remains strong, reflecting healthy expansion 
within our existing customer base and deepening adoption of the core Capillary 
product suite.
Customer cohorts from our most recent acquisitions (Kognitiv, SessionM) can be 
expected to deliver retention metrics comparable to the organic business only

Inorganic NRR (94%) reflects expected migration dynamics from inorganically 
acquired customers which result in NRR below 100%.
Across past acquisitions, ~70% of revenue has been retained post-migration, with
