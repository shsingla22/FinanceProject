Message from the Management
KRISHNA BODANAPU
Executive Vice -Chairman & 
Managing DirectorCommenting  on the results,  Mr. Krishna  Bodanapu,  Executive  Vice  Chairman  and  Managing  Director,  said :“Q2 
FY27 reflects  sustained  momentum  across  Cyient  Group,  with  record  order  books  and  continued  investment  in our 
next  phase  of growth .
Cyient  Semiconductors  grew  revenue  8% QoQ,  its sixth  consecutive  quarter  of sequential  growth,  securing  a large  
turnkey  program  and  a major  global  semiconductor  engagement . AI data -center  power  products  remain  on track,  
with  R&D  investments  expected  to begin  generating  revenue  next  fiscal  year . Excluding  product  R&D,  which  fully  
accounts  for the EBIT  loss,  the core  business  is EBIT -neutral  and  self -sustaining . Supply  constraints  may  weigh  on 
near -term  margins  and  growth,  but a healthy  order  book  and  ASIC  pipeline  above  $100  million  support  our long -
term  outlook .
Cyient  DLM  achieved  another  record  order  book,  with  book -to-bill above  1.6x, strengthening  revenue  visibility . 
Revenue  grew  over  26.5% YoY  and  over  30% in H1, in INR  terms,  with  growth  across  all key segments  led by 
Aerospace  and  Industrial .
Cyient  DET  delivered  growth  during  the quarter,  recorded  our highest -ever  Q2 order  intake,  highest  EBIT  in ten 
quarters,  and  remained  focused  on building  the capabilities  that  support  our long -term  strategy . TAO  Digital’s  
acquisition  strengthens  our platform  software  and  data  engineering  capabilities,  supporting  customers’  enterprise  
AI adoption  and  positioning  us for longer -term  engagements . Reflecting  our strong  operating  performance  and  
disciplined  capital  allocation,  the Board  has  declared  an interim  dividend  of ₹12 per share .
Looking  ahead,  the Defense,  semiconductors  and  AI industries  present  the highest  opportunities  for growth,  and  
we are pursuing  these  together  as a Group,  bringing  customers  an end -to-end  offering  spanning  engineering,  
silicon,  manufacturing  and  lifecycle  support,  reinforcing  the compounding  value  of our complementary  
capabilities . Our  strong  order  books,  maturing  pipeline,  and  focused  investments  in high -growth  areas  give  me 
confidence  that  we can  navigate  through  near -term  industry  headwinds  and  keep  creating  sustainable  and  long -
term  value  for shareholders
