infrastructure, cement, sports among others. JSW Energy Ltd has established its presence across the value

MW ensuring diversity in geographic presence, fuel sources and power off-take arrangements . The 
Company is presently constructing various power projects to the tune of 2.1 GW, with a vision to achieve
