Success Story
KSB Shirwal Plant (Energy Pumps Division) was credited with the distinguished ISO 19443:2018 Certificate
KSB Limited has achieved ISO 19443:2018 certification, 
becoming the first company in the Indian pump industry 
to earn this accreditation. 
This certification encompasses the development, 
production, marketing, and servicing of pumps, auxiliary 
components, and systems for nuclear applications, specifically for products and systems classified as ITNS (Important to Nuclear Safety).
