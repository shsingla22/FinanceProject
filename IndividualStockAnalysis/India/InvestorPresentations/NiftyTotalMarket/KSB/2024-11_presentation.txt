Solar Project Inauguration:
On September 3, we celebrated a significant milestone of successful virtual inauguration of our open access solar power project.
The event, held at the IPD, and inaugurated by Dr. Stephan Bross (CTO KSB SE & Co), was a memorable occasion for our 
company. 
This remarkable project boosts a capacity of 6.65 Megawatts Peak ( MWp ) and is set to 
generate approximately 10 million kilowatt-hours (kWh) of power annually. This output is equivalent to meeting nearly 50% of KSB India's current total power requirements of 
manufacturing plant and substantially 
reduces our carbon footprint. As a result, we are advancing towards our goal of making KSB more greener effectively elevating our share of renewable energy to 71%.
