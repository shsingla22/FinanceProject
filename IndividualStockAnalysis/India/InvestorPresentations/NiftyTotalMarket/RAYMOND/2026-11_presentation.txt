Economic Expansion & Industrial Growth
Defying deceleration forecasts, India's real GDP expansion was driven by resilient 
domestic consumption and front -loaded capital expenditure.
Manufacturing output serves as a key engine, expanding by 9.0% YoY across 18 of 
23 tracked industrial sectors.
Private Sector & Output Momentum
Private sector activity remains firmly expansionary, marked by steady factory 
output and export order gains.
Robust domestic demand continues to cushion business activity against broader 
global economic deceleration risks.OPERATIONAL HEADWINDS & MARGIN PRESSURES

Cost Pressures & Supply Chain Risks
Heightened security risks and shipping disruptions along Middle East routes 
sustain elevated maritime freight rates and insurance surcharges.
Volatile Brent Crude benchmarks expand the merchandise import bill, directly 
squeezing manufacturing input margins.
INFLATIONARY TRENDS & MONETARY POLICY
Headline retail inflation remains above the RBI's 4.0% medium -term target, 
predominantly driven by elevated food and beverage inflation at 5.95% .
Strategic input management is essential to buffer operational margins against 
persistent cost -push headwinds.
