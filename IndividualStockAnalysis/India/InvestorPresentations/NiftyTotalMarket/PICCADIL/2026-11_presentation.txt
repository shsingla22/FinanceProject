Management’s Message
Q2 FY2026-27 delivered strong growth, led by the Distillery business. 
Revenue from Operations of the Distillery division grew 30.5% YoY to 
H271 crore, while the Company’s Revenue from Operations increased 
19.4% YoY to H278 crore. The Distillery division contributed 97.6% of the 
Company’s Revenue from Operations, compared with 89.3% in Q2 last year. 
Our branded alcobev business continued to perform well, with volumes growing 38% 
YoY in Q2 and 45% YoY in H1, supported by sustained demand across markets. We are 
also progressing with new product launches from both the Indri and Chhattisgarh 
facilities, which will further broaden our portfolio and support growth
. 
Capacity utilisation at both the Indri and Chhattisgarh distilleries 
continues to improve following the recent expansions. With H2 typically 
contributing a higher share of our branded alcobev business, and with 
improving utilisation and new launches, we expect the business mix and 
operating performance to strengthen through the second half of the year. 
Harvinder Chopra 
Managing Director

Introduction
Piccadily Agro Industries Ltd. (PAIL) is a name 
synonymous with heritage, craftsmanship and 
innovation. Established in 1994, it has grown to 
become India’s largest independent producer of malt 
spirits. Our portfolio includes single malt whiskies, 
cane juice rum and a range of IMFL brands, setting 
new benchmarks in quality and innovation. Beyond 
spirits, we manufacture Ethanol, Extra Neutral Alcohol 
(ENA), DDGS and white crystal sugar.
With a diverse portfolio of premium alco-bev brands 
and more products in the offing we endeavour to define 
the future of niche and premium Indian alco-bev spirits 
in India and overseas. We plan to capitalise organic and
