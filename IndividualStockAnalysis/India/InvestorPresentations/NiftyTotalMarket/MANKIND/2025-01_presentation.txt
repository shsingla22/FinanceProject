oEBIT excludes other income
oCapital employed isthesum oftotal equity, total borrowings, total lease liabilities and deferred taxliabilities (net)
lessdeferred taxassets andCash .
•Adjusted ROCE =(Adjusted EBIT fortheyear )/(Adjusted Capital employed lesscash)

deferred taxliabilities (net), M&A related impact (net ofTax) less deferred taxassets ,Cash and cash used for
acquisitions .
•Cash =(Cash andcash equivalents +Other bank balances +investment inMutual funds)
•NetCash =(Cash -Current borrowings -Non Current borrowings attheendoftheyear)

Operating working capital isthesum ofInventories andTrade receivables lessTrade payables
