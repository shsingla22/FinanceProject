➢Pronounced an Arbitration Award for EPC Project

section of NH 29E, executed for UP PWD.

o On September 11, 2026, NHAI extended the three -year debarment imposed on the concessionaire, Awadh Expressway Private Limited, t o PNC Infratech, restricting 
the Company from participating in NHAI and MoRTH projects during the debarment period.
o Subsequently, on October 12, 2026, the Hon’ble Delhi High Court granted a stay on the effect of the debarment orders issued b y the Authority against both the 
Company and the concessionaire.

o The Company has incorporated a subsidiary company in the name ‘ PNC lnfratech  (Kazakhstan) LLP ' in the Republic of Kazakhstan with the primary objective of 
providing infrastructure services in the country, particularly in the Roads and Highways sector.
