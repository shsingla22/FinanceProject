About Prime Focus Limited
Prime Focus Limited (PFL), the world’s largest independent integrated media services
powerhouse, employs over 9,000 professionals in 18 cities across 5 continents. We
provide end-to-end creative services (visual effects, stereo 3D conversion and
animation), technology products & services (CLEAR™ Media ERP Suite and Cloud-
enabled media services), production services (equipment rental) and post-production
services (Digital Intermediate and picture post) to the Media & Entertainment industry.
Listed on the BSE and NSE of India and recognized on the Fortune India ‘Next 500’ list,
Prime Focus has operations in Bangalore, Chandigarh, New Delhi, Goa, Hyderabad,
Kolkata, London, Los Angeles, Mumbai, New York, Johannesburg, Capetown, Abu
Dhabi, Toronto,Montreal, Chennai, Sydney and Vancouver.
