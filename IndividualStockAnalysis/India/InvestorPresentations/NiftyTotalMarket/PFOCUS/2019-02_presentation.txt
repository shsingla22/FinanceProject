About Prime Focus Limited 
  
Prime Focus Limited (PFL), the world’s  largest independent integrated media services 
powerhouse, employs over 9,000 professionals in 18 cities across 5 continents and 7 
time zones. We provide end-to-end creative services (visual effects, stereo 3D 
conversion and animation), technology products & se rvices (CLEAR™  Media ERP Suite 
and Cloud-enabled media services), production services  (equipment rental) and post-
production services (Digital Intermediate and pictu re post) to the Media & Entertainment 
industry. 
 
Listed on the BSE and NSE of India and recognized on the Fortune India ‘Next  500’ list, 
Prime Focus has operations in Bangalore, Chandigarh, New Delhi, Goa, Hyderabad, 
Kolkata, London,  Los Angeles, Mumbai, New York, Johannesburg, Capetown,  Abu 
Dhabi, Toronto, Montreal, Chennai,   Sydney and Vancouver.
