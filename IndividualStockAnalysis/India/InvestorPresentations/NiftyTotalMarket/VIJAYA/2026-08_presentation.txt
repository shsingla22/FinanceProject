Management Commentary
Commenting on the Q1 FY27 
results and future business 
outlook, Ms. Suprita Reddy, 
MD & CEO at Vijaya 
Diagnostic Centre Ltd. said:I’m pleased  to start  on a positive  note,  as we delivered  robust  year -on-year  revenue  growth  of approximately  
22.8%, supported  by volume  growth  of nearly  16.5% in Q1 FY 27. 
Coming  to PH, we delivered  year -on-year  growth  of approximately  18%, primarily  driven  by network  expansion . 
During  the quarter,  we further  expanded  our network  with  the successful  commissioning  of 4 spokes  across  
Hyderabad,  AP and Pune .
In April,  we commissioned  a hub centre  in Gachibowli,  Hyderabad  with  a 160-slice  cardiac  CT.
I am pleased  to announce  that  in July,  we have  also commissioned  our flagship  hub in Bengaluru  located  at JP 
Nagar,  where  we have  introduced  advanced  imaging  technologies,  including  a digital  PET-CT with  an in-built  
cardiac  CT and a 75cm wide  bore  3T Omega  MRI. It also houses  full-fledged  lab which  will serve  as the central  
lab for our entire  Bengaluru  network .
Our expansion  plan  for FY27 reflects  the next  stage  of our growth  story . We plan  to commission  9 hub centres
