Commenting on the 
business outlook , Ms. 
Suprita Reddy, MD & CEO 
at Vijaya Diagnostic Centre 
Ltd. said:Vijaya  reported  another  strong  financial  year  with  YOY growth  of 24.4% and an organic

2 each  in Pune,  West  Bengal  and Bengaluru . Additionally,  the execution  of 3 more  hubs  in

notable  increase  in both  footfalls  and revenue  across  our network .
Moving  forward,  our key areas  of focus  would  be stabilisation  of the newly  launched  hubs  and 
building  strong  brand  equities  in these  new  geographies,  commissioning  additional  new  hubs
