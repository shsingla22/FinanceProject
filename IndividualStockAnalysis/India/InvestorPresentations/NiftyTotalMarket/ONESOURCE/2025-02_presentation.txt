     Press Release January 29, 2025 OneSource announces strong financial results in its first quarter post-listing; Q3FY25 revenues at ₹3,926 million with an EBITDA of ₹1,432 million, demonstrating a 36.5% EBITDA margin  Q3FY25 Performance Highlights • Revenues of ₹3,926 million grew 18% QoQ. • EBITDA grew 85% QoQ to ₹1,432 million. • EBITDA margin of 36.5% grew by 1,334 basis points QoQ. • Q3 FY25 normalized PAT was ₹898 million, resulting in an EPS of ₹7.8 for the quarter. • Reported PAT was impacted by one-time OneSource setup and transaction costs. Bangalore, India, January 29, 2025 - OneSource Specialty Pharma Limited (BSE:544292, NSE: ONESOURCE) today announced its consolidated financial results for the quarter (Q3FY25) ended December 31, 2024.
