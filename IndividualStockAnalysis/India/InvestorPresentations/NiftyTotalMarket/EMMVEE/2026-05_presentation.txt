•ALMM for Cells (Proposed): Initiative to extend domestic preference to solar cells to support upstream manufacturing. Proposed start: 1 Jun 2026.
•DCR Requirements (Select Schemes): Initiative to ensure domestic content in selected government programs and procurement structures through DCR conditions ,
