Dear  Shareholders,
Q2 FY27 was  the strongest  quarter  in Embassy  Developments'  history . Pre-sales  reached  approximately  INR  3,225  crore,  nearly  eight  times  the  same  quarter  last  year  and well ahead  of our previous  record  set 
in Q4 FY26. For the first half of FY27, pre-sales  stood  at approximately  INR 4,093 crore . That  is close  to 90% of our  entire  FY26 pre -sales , which  were  themselves  more  than  double  FY25. The investments  of the 
past  few years  in land,  product  and people  are now  clearly  visible  in our sales .
An important  moment  of the quarter  came  in Juhu . At Embassy  Terazza , launched  in Q2, we signed  a landmark  ~INR  711 crore  transaction  for an entire  residence,  the  largest  single  residential  transaction  in 
India  to date . A buyer  committing  at that  scale  in Mumbai's  most  discerning  market,  within  about  18 months  of our entry  into the city, says  more  about  the Embassy  brand  than  anything  we could  say ourselves . 
It reflects  trust  in our design,  our execution  and our name . In Worli,  Embassy  Citadel,  one  of India's  tallest  residential  developments  at over  300  meters,  has  sold  more  than  INR  1,300  crore  of inventory  within  
six months  of launch .
Bengaluru  continues  to anchor  the platform . Embassy  Origins , comprising  Riverine  and South  Reserve,  generated  over  INR  1,800  crore  of pre -sales  within  two  weeks  of launch . Our growth  is now  broad -based  
across  projects,  price  points  and geographies .
Collections  for Q2 FY27 were  approximately  INR 546 crore,  taking  H1 FY27 collections  to approximately  INR 1,041 crore . Our large  and growing  base  of sold  receivables  gives  us strong  visibility  into future  cash  flows .
I also  want  to be clear  about  how  this growth  shows  up on our balance  sheet . Net institutional  debt  stood  at approximately  INR 3,982 crore  as of September  30, 2026 , with  cash  and cash  equivalents  of 
approximately  INR 1,258 crore . We are investing  in construction  and new  launches  ahead  of the collections  those  projects  will generate . Most  of our recent  launches  are in their  early  stages,  and customer  
payments  are linked  to construction  milestones . As these  projects  rise above  ground,  collections  should  accelerate  meaningfully,  with  the inflection  point  expected  in FY28, and debt  should  then  come  down . 
Leverage  remains  well within  comfortable  levels .
In October  2026 , all of ~3.25 crore  warrants  issued  to promoter  group  were  converted  into  equity  at INR  111.51 per  warrant  well  ahead  of time  and the entire  proceeds  of INR 363 crore  were  utilized  to repay  
shareholder  debt  owed  to the promoter  group . This transaction  reflects  the promoter  group’s  continued  confidence  and financial  commitment  to the Company  while  further  strengthening  its capital  structure .
Execution  remains  central  to everything  we do. During  H1 FY27, we received  Occupancy  Certificates  for One  09, Gurugram,  and all remaining  towers  at Golf  City,  Savroli , bringing  us closer  to handing  over  homes  
to our customers .
With  three  of our 11 planned  FY27 launches  complete  and the rest scheduled  for H2, we remain  on track  to deliver  our guidance  of ~INR  6,000 crore  in owned  pre-sales  and ~INR  2,000 crore  from  development  
management  projects . Just  as importantly,  we remain  on track  for ~INR  3,000 crore  in collections .
I would  also  like to thank  Mr. Sachin  Shah  for his leadership  and  his contribution  to the  evolution  of Embassy  Developments . As part  of a planned  transition,  I will take  on the role of Managing  Director  & CEO  
effective  January  1, 2027 . Sachin  will continue  with  the Company  and Embassy  Group  as Senior  Advisor  to the Chairman  and me, ensuring  continuity  as we enter  our next  phase  of growth .
Thank you to our customers, partners, investors and employees for your continued confidence and support.
Best  Wishes,
Aditya  Virwani

entity  vested  with  the incoming  Promoter/Promoter  Group  upon  the Scheme  becoming  effective . As required  under  Ind AS 103, a fair value  assessment  was  carried  out for the inventory  and investment  property  of the combined  entity .

P&L are reported  lesser  to the extent  of difference  between  cost  incurred  and fair value . However,  the cashflow  on aforesaid  assets  are not being  negatively  impacted . 
•The Consolidated  unaudited  financial  results  issued  under  the name  of EDL (legal  acquirer)  represents  the continuation  of the financial  results  of NAM  (accounting  acquirer)  except  for capital  structure  and reflects  the assets  and
