About Religare Enterprises Limited (Religare) – http://www.religare. com  
 
Religare Enterprises Limited (REL) is the holding company for one of India’s leading 
diversified financial services groups.  REL offers an integrated suite of financial services 
through its underlying subsidiaries and operating entities, including loans to  SME s, 
Capital Markets, Wealth Management, Health Insurance and Asset Management. REL is 
listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and National Stock Exchange ( NSE) in India.   
As a group , Religare caters to almost every segment of the market fr om mass retail to 
affluent, HNIs, UHNIs, mid -size corporates, SME s to large corporates and institutions. 
The group has a presence in over 1,500  locations in India and also has an international 
footprint beyond India through its Capital Markets & Global Asset Management 
businesses.
