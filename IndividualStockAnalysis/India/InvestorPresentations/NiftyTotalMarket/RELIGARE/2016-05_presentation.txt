About Religare Enterprises Limited (Religare)  –http://www.religare. com  
 
Religare Enterprises Limited (REL) has built a diversified financial services platform 
operating across three verticals, viz. Lending, Health Insurance and Capita l Markets. 
Through its operating entities, REL’s financial services offerings include loans to 
SMEs, Capital Markets, Wealth Management, and Health Insurance. REL is listed on 
the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and National Stock Exchange (NSE) in India.   As 
a group, Religare caters to almost every segment of the market from mass retail to 
affluent, HNIs, UHNIs, mid -size corporates, SMEs to large corporates and institutions 
with presence in over 1,400 locations in India.
