^^ Not annualised  
 
 
Reporting basis: From this quarter, the Company reports its financial results under Ind AS, as prescribed by IRDAI, with 1 April 
2025 as the transition date. Comparative figures are presented on the same basis.  
KPIs:  
 
Premium Income: - 
• In Q2 2027, Insurance Revenue of the Company stood at ₹ 2,720 cr compared to ₹ 2,675 cr in Q2 2026  
• In Q2 2027, Gross Direct Premium of the Company stood at ₹ 2,351 cr compared to ₹ 2,379 cr in Q2 2026, a de -growth 
of 1.2%.  
• In Q2 2027, Gross Written Premium of the Company stood at ₹ 2,571 cr  compared to ₹ 2,667 cr in Q2 2026, a de -growth 
of 3.6%. Excluding health reinsurance inward and government health business, which we chose not to renew as pricing 
was unattractive, Gross Written Premium was flat.  
 
Profitability:  
• Ind AS Profit before tax (Ex MTM) for the Q2 2027 stood at ₹ 27 8 cr, compared to ₹ 370 cr in Q2 2026.  
• Ind AS Profit after tax (Ex MTM) for the Q2 2027 stood at ₹ 20 8 cr, compared to ₹ 2 77 cr in Q2 2026.  
Profitability without Flood Loss:  
• Ind AS Profit before tax (Ex MTM) for the Q2 2027 stood at ₹ 34 1 cr, compared to ₹ 380 cr in Q2 2026.  
• Ind AS Profit after tax (Ex MTM) for the Q2 2027 stood at ₹ 25 5 cr, compared to ₹ 2 85 cr in Q2 2026.  
Combined Ratio: - 
• Combined Insurance Service Ratio (CISR) for Q2 2027 is 99.8%, as compared to 94.9% in Q2 2026 . 
• Combined Insurance Service Ratio ( CISR ) Ex Flood Loss for Q2 2027 is 97. 4%, as compared to 94.5% in Q2 2026 . 
 
Assets Under Management: - 
• As on September 30, 2026, assets under management stood at ₹ 24,3 71 cr at book value, compared to ₹ 21,019 cr as 
at September 30, 2025, increased by ₹ 3, 352 cr, having a growth of 15. 9%. 
 
Solvency: - 
• Solvency ratio  (basis IGAAP)  was at 2.40 x as at September 30, 2026 as against 2.42x as at March 31, 2026, higher than 
the minimum regulatory requirement of 1.50x.

About the Company  
 
We aim to make insurance simple. Through innovation and transparency, we believe in delivering a seamless customer experience  
journey in a significant financial product an individual would purchase in their lifetime. We are one of the leading digital full stack 
insurance companies, leveraging our technology to power what we believe to be an innovative approach to product design, 
distribution and customer experience for non -life insurance products. We offer motor insurance, health insurance, travel 
insurance , property insurance, marine insurance, liability insurance and other insurance products, which the customers can 
customize to meet their needs.
