HCG Key Highlights: Q4 and FY26
HCG Key Highlights: Q4 and FY26
HCG delivered revenue of INR 6,523 Mn in Q4FY26, reflecting ~11% YoY growth; FY26 revenue stood at INR 25,454 Mn, up ~15% YoY , supported by sustained 
volume growth and steady ARPP improvement
•Broad -based growth continued across clusters in Q4
oWest remained the key growth driver, growing ~13% YoY , supported by strong volume momentum across hospitals and improved trac tion 
oSouth delivered ~9% YoY growth, supported by healthy performance across tier -2 centres , partially offset by softer medical value tourism
oEast grew ~8% YoY , driven by higher patient volumes, with ARPP impacted by lower case -mix in Kolkata
•International business continued to scale meaningfully, growing ~39% YoY in Q4 FY26 and ~71% YoY in FY26, supported by ramp -up in radiation oncology, PET and stronger patient 
inflows
•FY26 performance remained strong across the network, with South, West and East clusters delivering ~13%, ~14% and ~11% YoY re venue growth, respectively. Excluding Milann , FY26 
revenue grew ~15% YoY , driven by ~12% volume growth and ~3% ARPP improvement. 
•Modality mix1 (FY26):  Medical oncology (~38% of revenue); Radiation (~14% of revenue); Surgical oncology (~21% of revenue); OP Onco  services (~16% of revenue); Non -oncology 
specialties (~11% of revenue)
•Payor mix2 (FY26):  Cash + TPA (~56% of revenue); Govt (~33% of revenue); MVT(Medical Value Travel)  (~2% of revenue) and others (~9% of revenue)
HCG delivered Adjusted EBITDA of INR 1,252 Mn in Q4FY26, up ~17% YoY; FY26 Adjusted EBITDA stood at INR 4,711 Mn, up ~19% YoY
•Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 19.2% in Q4 FY26 versus 18.3% in Q4 FY25, supported by operating leverage and improved net work productivity
•FY26 Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded by ~68 bps YoY to 18.5%, reinforcing HCG’s trajectory of sustainable margin improvement
•ROCE stood at 14.0% in FY26, supported by higher utilization, centre  maturity and disciplined capital allocation
Key Business Developments: Strengthening platform for the next phase of growth
•North Bangalore facility commenced operations, bringing MR -LINAC technology to Bangalore and strengthening HCG’s advanced oncolo gy capabilities
•Rights issue completed to support growth investments and strengthen balance sheet
•Theranostics  launch, robotic surgery expansion and addition of day care centre  in Ahmedabad to further strengthen clinical capabilities, access and throughput
•Key roles hiring including CFO and investor relations have also been completed with leaders joining in May’26
