HCG Key Highlights: Q2 FY27
HCG Key Highlights: Q2 FY27
HCG delivered revenue of INR 7,318 Mn in Q2FY27, reflecting ~16% YoY growth
•Broad -based growth continued across clusters in Q2FY27. Revenue growth was led by volume growth of 9% YoY . ARPP grew by 7%, improvement driven by a favourable 
payor mix and increased contribution from high -end therapies, including robotic surgeries (up 37% YoY), and other advanced treat ments
oSouth:  Revenue of INR 2,941 Mn (contributing 40% of revenue) delivered ~18% YoY growth
▪Growth was driven by a 14% increase in ARPP and 4% growth in patient volumes. Performance led by Bangalore CoE and ramp -up of North Bangalore facility. 
ARPP improvement led by payor mix improvement and contribution of high -end therapies
oWest:   Revenue of INR 3,1 47 Mn (contributing 43% of revenue) delivered ~10% YoY growth
▪Growth led by 7% volume growth and 3% ARPP improvement. Growth led by Maharashtra, was partly offset by moderated growth in G ujarat region due to 
rationalization of institutional mix
oEast:  Revenue of INR 928 Mn (contributing 13% of revenue) delivered ~23% YoY growth
▪Growth led by volume growth of 18% YoY  while ARPP grew by 5% YoY . Growth was broad -based across the region, led by strong performance at Ranchi
•International business revenue was INR 302 Mn, grew 54% YoY in Q2FY27, driven by stronger patient inflows for radiation and medical oncology
HCG delivered Adjusted EBITDA2 of INR 1,425 Mn in Q2FY27, up ~17% YoY
•Adjusted EBITDA2 margin improved to 19.7% in Q2 FY27 from 19.2% in Q2 FY26 , driven by continued improvement in quality of revenue. Margin expansion partially 
impacted due to provision towards the revision of wages under the Karnataka Wage Act
•The North Bangalore facility continues to ramp up;  after accounting for start -up losses of the facility, Operational EBITDA stood at INR 1,364 Mn
Key Business Developments: 
1.Capacity Expansion: Added 25 operational beds in Q2 FY27, comprising 13 beds in Kenya, 7 in Kolkata and 5 in Nagpur, taking total bed additions t o 146 in H1 FY27
2.Advanced Technology Deployment: Operationalised the state -of-the-art adaptive radiation therapy system MR -LINAC at North Bengaluru, a LINAC in Africa and a card iac 
robotic surgery system at HMS Ahmedabad
3.Key Clinical Program Additions: 
•Advanced Cancer Therapies: Performed first CAR -T cell therapy at HCC Ahmedabad, strengthening advanced oncology treatment capabi lities
•Robotic & Minimally Invasive Surgery: Introduced robotic breast surgery at KR, robotic spine surgery at Rajkot and Transoral Robotic Surgery (TORS) at Baroda
•Precision Diagnostics: Launched a 1,212 -gene liquid biopsy panel and introduced two new nuclear medicine tracers at KR, enhancin g precision cancer diagnostics
