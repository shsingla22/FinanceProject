7
LiPF₆sales ramping upwell, with sales tofive major global electrolyte players .Production isbeing progressively ramped up
along with new plants being added tomeet strong customer demand, backed bymulti -year order visibility .
LFP plant samples are progressing through customer qualification programs globally including India. We are now progressing 
towards development of higher end LFP grades. In view of strong emerging global market demand, further substantial 
capacity expansions underway.
Multiple PVDF and PTFE grades are at advanced stages of customer qualification across both wet and next -generation dry

priming us for their upcoming cell manufacturing programs. We expect domestic electrolyte demand to pickup towards the 
end of next financial year.
