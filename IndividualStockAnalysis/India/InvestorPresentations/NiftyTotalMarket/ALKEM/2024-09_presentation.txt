3While pharma will still be <20% of the overall healthcare spend, it will grow to be as large as US Gx market (in net

2030, from ~$150 -160 Bn today (~4% of GDP today) on back of rising income levels, consciousness towards health, 
and enhanced government outlay
2Growth will be broad -based, with several sub-segments beyond pharma emerging as attractive opportunities

leaders in India
6Channel power will continue to rise (aggregated distributors will have 15 -20% share by 2030 and Organized pharma
