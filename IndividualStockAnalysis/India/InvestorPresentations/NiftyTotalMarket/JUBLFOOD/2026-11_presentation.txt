 Dear Shareholders,  
Q2FY27 marked further progress across our businesses, with improving operating momentum and tangible 
outcomes from our strategic priorities. Key highlights included sequential and year -on-year expansion in 
standalone EBITDA margins, a sustained recovery in  Domino’s India LFL growth, over 50% LFL growth at 
Popeyes, and stronger  than expected cash generation from operations in Turkey. Together, these achievements 
reflect the resilience of our businesses and the growing traction of our strategic initiatives.  
 
Consolidated revenue from operations grew 11.8% YoY to ₹2,608 crore, while EBITDA increased 7. 3% to 
₹513 crore. Standalone revenue from operations grew 11.5% YoY to ₹1,885 crore, with EBITDA rising 12.4% 
to ₹372 crore. Domino’s India recorded LFL growth of 4.1%, up from 2.5% in Q1FY27 and 0.2% in Q4FY26.  
Popeyes  delivered its fourth consecutive quarter of 40 –50% LFL growth. Across the group, JFL added 108 
stores.  
 
At Domino’s India, growth was supported by sustained momentum in Delivery and early signs of recovery in 
Dine -In and Takeaway (DITA). Product innovation across the Maxxx range, Double Cheese Burst, Cold 
Coffee and Mousse has broadened our proposition and improved  ticket -size. We remain focused on 
introducing products and formats that expand the occasions in which customers engage with the brand . 
 
Programme to turnaround the performance in DITA is gaining traction. Infrastructure upgrades have been 
completed in approximately 20% of identified DITA -heavy stores, while My Meal @ ₹119 has been rolled 
out nationally. These initiatives are laying the foundation for a more sustainable recovery in the chan nel. 
 
Popeyes is emerging as a meaningful second growth engine for JFL. With a presence across 25 cities and 
system Average Daily Sales (ADS) broadly comparable with established players in most markets, the brand 
is gaining scale and customer love . Gross margin expanded by 150 bps YoY. Our priority is to translate this 
momentum into a profitable national network, with expansion guided by store-level returns.  
 
DP Eurasia delivered healthy growth across revenue, operating profitability and PAT, with reported EBITDA 
margins above 20% and PAT margins consistently in the 8 –10% range. The business remains accretive to 
JFL’s consolidated financials.  
 
We have navigated elevated input costs through procurement efficiencies, calibrated pricing, menu -mix 
optimisation and tighter cost controls, supporting YoY gross margin expansion. Investments in supply -chain 
capacity and technology are also improving prod uctivity across stores, delivery and inventory management, 
creating the foundation for greater operating leverage . 
 
Our priorities remain clear: sustain disciplined execution in Domino’s India, scale Popeyes while improving 
unit economics, develop our international businesses and embed technology across operations. We will 
continue to allocate capital towards growth opp ortunities and revenue -generating assets, while maintaining a 
sharp focus on returns and long -term value creation.  
 
I wish all of you a very happy Diwali . 
 
Sameer Khetarpal  
CEO  & MD

 Key questions on performance and outlook : 
Domino 's India:  
 
1. Domino’s India LFL improved to 4.1% in Q2FY27. What drove this, and how do you see the 
path back to your targeted  range of 5–7% in the  medium -term ? 
Domino’s India’s like -for-like (LFL) growth improved sequentially to 4.1% in Q2FY27, from 2.5% 
in Q1FY27 and 0.2% in Q4FY26. This improvement was driven by sustained growth in the Delivery 
channel and early signs of recovery in Dine -In and Takeaway (DITA).  
 
Late-night, particularly, emerged as a strong -performing day -part. Over the past 6 –9 months, we have 
scaled our late -night offerings, with over 90% of our network now serviceable until 3 AM. This has 
helped us tap into an additional consumption occasion an d drive incremental orders. On the BPO front, 
new product launches in the beverage and dessert categories during Q1FY27 helped improve 
attachment rates. Along with a favourable shift in menu mix, this contributed to higher BPO during 
the quarter.  
 
Going forward, we will continue to prioritise driving customer frequency and creating new 
consumption occasions through targeted, day -part-specific and channel -specific offerings, while 
continuing to strengthen our product portfolio and improve menu mix . 
 
2. Can you provide more colour on the progress  in the Dine -in and Take -away ( DITA ) channel ? 
We are beginning to make progress in the DITA -channel. The channel revenue grew 2.4% YoY. The 
turnaround programme is built on four levers:  
1) Improving focus on stores with higher DITA potential by improving experience   
2) Upgrading infra in 100+ stores  
3) Incentivising customers to take -away their order by giving superior value vs delivery as a 
chan nel 
4) Targeting dine -in customers through CRM and local store marketing  
 
Infrastructure upgrades have been completed in 20% of stores and we are expanding our AI -enabled 
surveillance to strengthen compliance across key areas such as guest service, cleanliness, footfall 
tracking and bill compliance.  
 
On the customer proposition front, My Meal at Rs. 119 and best -deals ever available only in store for 
take-away. The proposition is contributing to the recovery on two fronts: driving incremental store 
footfalls and improving ticket -size. 
 
As outlined in our earlier communication, DITA represents a significant opportunity for us. However, 
rebuilding customer habits and strengthening the in -store experience takes time. Our focus for FY27 
is on establishing a stronger foundation through infras tructure, customer propositions and operational 
excellence, positioning the channel for a return to sustainable growth .

 3. How have recent product launches performed, and how is your approach to innovation 
evolving ? 
Domino’s continues to be the most innovative brand in the country targeting GenZ, new users and its 
loyal base of customers. Recent launches, including the Maxxx range, Double Cheese Burst, Mousse 
and Cold Coffee, have ramped up quickly and received an enc ouraging customer response. Chicken 
Maxxx has strengthened our position in the chicken category, especially in South and East. The Maxxx 
range confirms our platform -led approach to innovation: it adds a new platform built around 
indulgence / generous toppi ngs to our existing Chicken platforms. Cold Coffee and Mousse (both 
accretive on margins) have raised attachment rates of sides, which improves the overall value 
proposition for customers and contributes to higher average ticket size.  
 
We will keep building on our established platforms while testing new formats, flavours and 
propositions. Our aim is to make innovation a sustained driver of engagement, frequency, and growth.  
 
Popeyes : 
 
4. Popeyes has delivered 40%+ LFL for four consecutive quarters. How is it performing against 
established competitors, and how is it tracking towards the ₹1,000 crore ambition ? 
Popeyes is now a meaningful second growth engine for JFL. LFL growth was 53% in Q2FY27, the 
fourth consecutive quarter above 40%. Revenue grew 122% YoY, and we added 11 stores during the 
quarter. The brand is now present in 25 cities, and Average Daily Sal es (ADS) are broadly comparable 
with established players in most of its markets. This reflects strong consumer acceptance in a relatively 
short time.  
 
Innovation is central to building the brand. During Q2 we expanded the vegetarian range with five 
Paneer Burger flavours and launched Choco Bomb, a chocolate dessert. This growth is coming with 
better unit economics, not at the expense of profitability. Gr oss margin expanded 150 bps YoY. We 
are making steady progress towards our ambition of building Popeyes into a ₹1,000 crore brand over 
the next three to four years . 
 
5. ADS for Popeyes is no w broadly at par with competitors, why not accelerate store openings ? 
Our test for accelerating is whether Popeyes has reached competitive benchmarks on customer 
experience, gross margins, store productivity and unit economics in the markets where it operates. We 
are exceeding or close on most of these:  
 
Customer experience:  Popeyes has the highest Google customer ratings among key competitors  
across markets that Popeyes operates in  
Gross margins:  GM% is  coming in line with where the industry operates at but has another 200 -
300bps opportunity  
Store productivity:  system ADS is broadly at par with competitors in most markets but still has 
headroom to grow  
Unit economics:  we have optimised capex, staffing model and other operating costs and rest of the 
improvements will come with scale and increase in store -density (e.g., cost per delivery)

 Brand -level profitability is now within sight, but we are not there yet . We are following the playbook 
that has worked for Domino’s. That means building a high -quality, profitable network: entering the 
right micro -markets, maintaining attractive store -level economics and ensuring that every new store 
meets our return thresholds . Once the margin gap is closed, that foundation will allow us to expand 
faster and with greater confidence . 
 
Margins:  
 
6. With input cost inflation, particularly in dairy, rising further during the quarter, how are you 
protecting margins ? 
Impact of inflation is to the tune of approx. 200bps in the P&L due to  increase in LPG  cost, hike in 
minimum wages  and raw material  cost. Commodities like oil and cheese are now on the rise. We are 
managing inflation through several levers rather than relying mainly on price increases:  
 
Procurement:  we have used our scale to strengthen vendor partnerships and improve sourcing 
efficiency  
Pricing:  we are taking targeted, data -led pricing actions where our value proposition allows, while 
protecting affordability  
Menu mix and wastage:  we are managing menu mix to balance customer preferences with product 
economics and continuing to reduce wastage  
Discounts:  using personalisation and data models, we have been able to target discounts  
Technology:  tech and software cost is one o f the other  areas we ar e driving operating leverage  through . 
We have integrated AI into  software development , bring down develo pment time and cost  
simultaneously  
 
Together, these measures expanded standalone gross margin by 110 bps YoY to 75.5%. Standalone 
EBITDA grew 12.4% and EBITDA margin expanded 16 bps, despite the input cost environment  
remaining challenging . 
 
Our earlier investments in supply -chain capacity are now starting to generate efficiencies. The backend 
is designed to support a network of 4,500 –5,000 stores, so operating leverage will grow as the network 
scales and utilisation improves . At the store level, technology is improving manpower scheduling, 
demand forecasting and inventory management, which reduces food wastage and energy use. We 
believe this combination will help us manage the current inflation while strengthening the long -term 
resilience of our margins . 
 
International : 
 
7. DP Eurasia’s reported EBITDA declined YoY and inflation -adjusted LFL was negative. How 
should investors read its underlying performance ? 
DP Eurasia continued to deliver a robust operating and financial performance during the quarter, with 
healthy growth across key financial metrics, including revenue, operating profitability and PAT. The 
business continues to generate reported EBITDA margin s in excess of 20% and PAT margins in the

 range of 8 –10%, reflecting the strength of its underlying economics. Importantly, DP Eurasia has 
consistently remained accretive to JFL's consolidated financials across all key profitability metrics.  
 
However, comparisons of reported EBITDA and PAT margins across periods are influenced by the 
accounting treatment prescribed under Ind AS 29 for hyperinflationary economies. Reported EBITDA 
includes the impact of Monetary Gain or Loss (MGL), which captures  the effect of inflation on 
monetary assets and liabilities. While the impact of MGL on reported EBITDA has been reducing 
sequentially, it may continue to influence year -on-year comparisons over the next two to three quarters.  
 
8. Coffy has reported negative LFL growth for past few quarters. What are the underlying reasons 
and what initiatives are being taken to drive growth?  
Coffy’s LFL performance has remained under pressure over the past few quarters, primarily due to 
lower operating capacity at university stores and reduced visibility on key delivery platforms. We have 
responded with targeted initiatives to improve customer  acquisition, including student -focused offers 
and a refreshed marketplace strategy. Beyond addressing these near -term challenges, we are 
undertaking a broader brand reset, with a sharper positioning centred on emotional connect, product 
quality and greate r consumer relevance.  
 
In parallel, we are upgrading the in -store experience through a refreshed store format and improving 
customer engagement through more targeted, technology -led marketing. New app features, loyalty 
initiatives, partnerships and localised marketing are aimed at building customer frequency and 
strengthening digital engagement. These initiatives are beginning to show traction, and our focus is to 
translate the improved brand proposition and customer experience into higher store productivity and 
a sustainable rec overy in growth . 
 
Capex : 
 
9. Is there any change to your FY27 Capex guidance ? 
No. Our Capex guidance for FY27 and the next few years remains unchanged. We expect annual 
Capex to remain broadly in the range of ₹750 –900 crore as the business continues to scale, with 
investments focused on network expansion and in technology . 
 
Technology:  
 
10. How are technology investments translating into tangible business outcomes ? 
Our technology investments are delivering measurable gains in customer experience, conversion, store 
productivity and financial controls.  
 
Customer experience:  as noted last quarter, more accurate rider tracking and delivery -time 
predictions are improving the delivery experience and lifting conversion. We have also upgraded the 
search  feature  on the Domino’s app so it understands what customers mean, not just exact keywords. 
This makes products easier to find and has further improved conversion.
