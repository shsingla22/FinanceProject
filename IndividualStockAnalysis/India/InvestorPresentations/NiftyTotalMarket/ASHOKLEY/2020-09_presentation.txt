Customer Solutions Business
eN-Dhan is a fuel loyalty program aimed at reducing the fuel spends of the customer and facilitates cashless 
transactions and better handling of operations of the fleet. 
SKS is the largest Commercial Vehicle breakdown assistance program powered by ServiceMandi . It aims at 
providing skilled and qualified technical support at the break down site with a 4 hour TAT. 
ServiceMandi, with a network of 20,000+ Technical Associates, is the largest aggregated digital service 
platform for Commercial Vehicles in India. It helps connect qualified and trained technical associates with 
fleet owners.
Market Place is set of specialized products aimed at improving the performance, ergonomics and safety of

Giving back to society(CSR)
The Road to School is an educational initiative that addresses some of the critical issues related to 
primary and upper primary education. The vision of the Road to School Program is to “Provide holistic 
development opportunities focused on quality and inclusion leading to education as a Social Leveler” . 
Road to School is a Global Program aligned with United Nations Global Sustainable Development 
Goals and to the National agenda of improving quality of education, improving health, nutrition, 
social and civic awareness amongst underprivileged children from Government Schools.
The initiative touches the lives of 56512 children across 714 schools in 5 districts -Hosur, Chennai, 
Namakkal, Salem and Sankagiri .
