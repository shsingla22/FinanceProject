Financials: Significant Updates for FY25
➢Record  Order  Inflow : Achieved  the highest -ever  annual  order  inflow  of ₹32,888 crore  in 
FY25, surpassing  guidance,  with  strong  wins  across  buildings,  transmission  & distribution,  
and irrigation  segments .
➢All-Time  High  Order  Book : Consolidated  order  book  reached  a record  ₹ 71,568 crore  as of 
March  31, 2025 , ensuring  robust  revenue  visibility  for the coming  years .
➢Sustained  Revenue  Growth : Consolidated  revenue  for FY25 stood  at ₹ 22,355 crore,  
reflecting  strong  execution  momentum  across  major  business  verticals  and geographies .
➢Profitability  : Reported  a consolidated  PAT of ₹ 820 crore,  up from  ₹ 711 crore  in FY24, 
driven  by operational  excellence  and disciplined  margin  management .
