PERFORMANCE OUTLOOK
We sustained the growth momentum in Q2 FY 27 with broad-based, double-digit UVG leading to 
20% plus revenue growth. 
Despite very high material inflation, amidst unprecedented volatility and uncertainty we delivered 
steady margins in H1 FY27 . We remain confident to continue the first half business momentum 
into the second half of the year.
We will continue to invest behind our brands, innovation and distribution. 
Our resilient business model and our disciplined execution will help us deliver profitable growth in 
the current environment.
