“The  second  quarter  of FY27 was a significant  step  forward  for Saatvik . Revenue  from  
operations  grew  77%  sequentially  to ₹9,026 million,  EBITDA  rose  to ₹696 million,  and 
profit after  tax increased  to ₹202 million  from ₹54 million  in the previous  quarter . We 
closed  the first half with  a confirmed  order  book  of 6.68 GW, with  1.76 GW of new  
orders  added  during the half,  which gives  us strong  visibility  on execution  in the 
coming  quarters .
The commencement  of commercial  production at our Odisha facility  on 2 November  
2026  marks  an important  milestone  in our journey . With about  1.3 GW of module  
capacity now  operational  and the full 4 GW expected  by December  2026.  
We have  also initiated  cell line trial runs,  with  production  activities  currently  in 
progress . The ALMM -2 inspection  is expected  in November,  while  groundwork  has 
commenced  for a new  3.6 GW solar  cell line,  further strengthening our integrated  and 
scalable  manufacturing platform  in line with  India’s  domestic  manufacturing  
priorities .
India’s  solar  sector  continues  to offer  a large  and sustained  opportunity . We are well 
positioned  to participate  in it, and we remain  focused  on executing  our plans  with  
discipline  and delivering  long- term  value  to all our stakeholders .”
Mr. Neelesh Garg
