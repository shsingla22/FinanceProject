Key Highlights – Operational & Financial Performance (Q3 FY25)
Clinical & 
Operational• Narayana  Institute  of Cardiac  Sciences  (NICS),  Bengaluru  has successfully  performed  the following  procedures  : 
• 93 Robotic  Cardiac  Surgeries
• 34 Transcatheter  Aortic  Valve  Implantation  (TAVI)
• Mazumdar  Shaw  Medical  Centre  (MSMC),  Bengaluru  has performed  following  procedures  during  the quarter :
• First  case  of Total  Shoulder  Replacement . 
• Performed  15 Renal  Transplants .
• Rabindranath  Tagore  International  Institute  of Cardiac  Science  (RTIICS),  Kolkata  has performed Mechanical  
Thrombectomy and clot retrieval  from  occluded  vessel  in Cath  Lab on a 33-year -old onECMO  support .There  are 
only  5 published  case  reports  of ECMO  patients  undergoing  mechanical  thrombectomy​ .
• MMI  Narayana  Super  Speciality  Hospital,  Raipur  hasintroduced  self-service  kiosks  for registration,  payments,  and 
appointments  for the patients . Achieved  85% adaptation  for consultation  booking  via kiosks  & NH CARE  App and a 
5% increase  in payment  via online  transfers .
Financial 
Performance• Consolidated  operating  revenues  of INR 13,667 Mn in Q3 FY25, an increase  of 13.5% YoY and -2.4% QoQ .
• Consolidated  EBITDA  of INR 3,254 Mns  in Q3 FY25 at margin  of 23.8%, and consolidated  PAT of INR 1,931 Mns  for Q3 
FY25 at a margin  of 14.1%.
