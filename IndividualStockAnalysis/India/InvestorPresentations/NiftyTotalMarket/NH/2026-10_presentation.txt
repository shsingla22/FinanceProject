17Digital Hospitals – Way ForwardBANASHANKARI · DIGITAL HOSPITAL
OP Journey   Outpatient 5 steps · all digital
Paperless Registration
Kiosk or counter registration creates the patient ID and books the appointment or 
walk -in — no paper form at any step.
Touch -Free Consultation Invoicing
Consultation bill raised and paid at the kiosk or counter; receipt digital, refund to 
source or hospital wallet.
Counter -Free Service Invoicing
Doctors' investigation and procedure orders post to the same visit and are billed 
digitally — no separate cash counter.
Live Queue Tracking
Token is shared with the patient on NHCare or WhatsApp; QMS runs the ward 
TVs, doctor -wise tokens and wait -time tracking.
Green -Channel Pharmacy
Prescription lands on Pharmacy Cart in NHCare and the kiosk; patient pays digitally 
and collects at the green channel.
IP Journey   Inpatient 6 steps · all digital
Upfront Cost Estimate
Estimate built in ATHMA from package and per -day rates, shared with the family 
before admission.
One -Tap Digital Consent
Consent captured and signed digitally on WhatsApp or NHCare — no paper 
consent form at the counter.
Paperless Admission
WhatsApp/NHCare -based admission bot — documents, advance payment, bed 
allotment and IP number.
Always -On IP Companion
Every in -stay request or complaint ticketed by the companion bot, escalated L1 –L3 
on a 30 -minute SLA.
Discharge Without Delay
Mark for discharge, summary, final bill and payment link all on WhatsApp; the file 
never moves on paper.
Digital Gate Pass
Visitor, attendant and discharge passes issued as a QR on WhatsApp, validated by 
security at the exit gate.
Eleven steps, one platform — zero paper handoffs.
