18CSR initiatives
Restored lifeline for community in Karamta , Kutch
❖Karamta , located  130 kms from  Bhuj,  is a small  hamlet,  home  to 60 families . The 
region  faces  an arid,  dry climate,  and water  scarcity  has been  a pressing  challenge .
❖The families  relied  on a 40-foot-deep  well,  located  outside  the vicinity  of hamlet . 
The well had deteriorated  with  cracked  walls,  overgrown  paths,  and the absence  of 
a protective  structure  made  access  difficult  and dangerous . 
❖UltraTech  rebuilt  and reinforced  the well’s  structure,  sanitized  the water,  installed  a 
secure  cover  to prevent  any accidents,  paved  surrounding  areas  for safe access  and 
educated  locals  on groundwater  harvesting  techniques .
