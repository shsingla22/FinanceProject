Myriad of Initiatives 
IRDAI has also undertaken various initiatives towards boosting the insurance 
penetration, such as permitting insurers to conduct video-based KYC, launching 
standardized insurance products and allowing insurers to offer rewards for low- 
risk behaviour 
Mission Insurance 
Mission of ‘Insurance for All’ by 2047 which is expected to lead to a significant 
increase in insurance penetration and would help in bolstering the ease of doing 
business and will aid in making the sector more investment-friendly 
Commission Regulations 2023 
Payment of Commission Regulations 2023, the insurance regulator has replaced 
the earlier individual cap on commission payments on insurance products with 
an overall cap on expenses of management of insurers leading to higher 
Insurance Payouts to brokers.
