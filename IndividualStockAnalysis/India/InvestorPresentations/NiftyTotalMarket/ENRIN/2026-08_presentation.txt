performance underscores the resilience of our business model and the disciplined execution of our strategy.

sustained value cre ation. With India adding more than 30 GW of renewable energy capacity in the first half of 
CY2026 and power demand rising globally  – driven by electrification, industrial growth, and AI -enabled data 
centers – the need for stronger, smarter, and more flexible energy infrastructure has never been greater. As 
a company committed to helping realize the Viksit Bharat vision, Siemens Energy India Limited is uniquely 
positioned to support this transformation through its innovative grid technologies, grid flexibilization solutions, 
and advanced power generation portfolio. Backed by technology leadership, local manufacturing capab ilities, 
and execution excellence, we remain committed to enabling our customers' energy transition  journeys,  while 
delivering profitable and sustainable growth. ”
