Management 
Commentary We delivered  another  strong  quarter  in 2QFY27, with  revenue  and EBITDA  reaching  their  highest -
ever  levels . Revenue  grew  ~45% YoY and EBITDA  grew  ~37% YoY , with  margins  remaining  healthy  
at 28%. All three  verticals  contributed  meaningfully  to this performance,  with  our API segment  
delivering  its highest -ever  quarterly  revenue,  underscoring  the breadth  and resilience  of our 
business .
Our novel  FDF portfolio  continues  to make  steady  progress,  with  niche  launches  lined  up through  
FY27 and beyond . We expect  these  launches  to contribute  meaningfully  to our growth  and to 
further  strengthen  the quality  of our business  over  time .
At the core  of our strategy  is a deliberate  focus  on innovation -led differentiation,  and it is 
increasingly  translating  into high -value  partnerships  with  global  companies . Our expanded  
partnership  with  Orion  Corporation  is a strong  example,  extending  our relationship  into immuno -
oncology  through  an exclusive  arrangement  for our Nivolumab  biosimilar  in Europe,  with  Shilpa  as 
the development  and supply  partner . This partnership  reinforces  our position  as a partner  of choice  
for complex  therapies .
These  strategic  choices  are also reflected  in our capital  efficiency,  with  ROCE  continuing  its 
improving  trend . Our progress  on sustainability  has also been  recognized  externally,  with  an 
improvement  in our CRISIL  ESG score,  reflecting  our commitment  to responsible  and well-governed  
growth . With  strong  first-half momentum,  a maturing  pipeline,  and a sharpened  strategic  focus,  we 
are well positioned  to deliver  a stronger  FY27.
5— Mr. Vishnukant  Bhutada
