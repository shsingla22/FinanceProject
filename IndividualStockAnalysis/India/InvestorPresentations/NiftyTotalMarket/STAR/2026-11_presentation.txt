“Badree Komandur  MD and Group CEO
Strides  delivered  a strong  second  quarter,  with  revenue  of ₹14,297 m growing  
~17% YoY ,  driven  by Ex-US Markets . Our  diversification  strategy  continues  to play  
out,  with  Ex-US Markets  growing  31% YoY  to ₹6,963 m and,  for the first  time,  
surpassing  our US business  in quarterly  revenue . Our  US business  delivered  9% 
YoY  growth . 
EBITDA  grew  to ₹2,509 m, crossing  the ₹2,500 m mark  in a quarter  for the first  time . 
This  was  despite  higher  raw  material,  freight  and  operating  costs  arising  from  
geopolitical  disruptions . We remain  focused  on mitigating  these  pressures  
through  continuous  cost  optimization  initiatives  during  the year .
Net  Debt  increased  by ₹2,114 m during  H1 due  to an elongated  cash -to-cash  cycle  
and  higher  inventory  holding  to navigate  current  market  conditions . We expect  the 
Net  Debt  to EBITDA  ratio  to stabilize  by the end  of the year .
We are also  pleased  to welcome  Mr. Biren  Gabhawala  to our Board  as an 
Independent  Director . His deep  expertise  in financial  reporting,  audit  and  
corporate  governance  will further  strengthen  the Board’s  oversight .
While  the external  environment  remains  dynamic,  we are committed  to delivering  
sustainable  and  profitable  growth .

Health where it matters 19Strengthening Board Governance: Mr. Biren Gabhawala  Joins the Board as 
Independent Director
With his appointment*, Independent Directors will make up 6 of the 10 Board members
Mr. Biren Gabhawala , Chartered Accountant (FCA)
Biren, aged 60 years, is a Chartered Accountant with more than three decades of experience in 
accounting, audit, taxation, financial reporting, corporate finance, M&A, FEMA, business consulting, 
valuations and insolvency -related matters.
He currently serves as a Senior Partner at C.M. Gabhawala  & Co., a Mumbai -based chartered 
accountancy firm. He has advised organisations  across diverse industries and stages of business 
development on financial reporting, taxation, audit, cross -border transactions, deal structuring, 
corporate finance, valuations and regulatory matters.
Biren also has extensive experience on the boards and committees of listed companies. He served as 
an Independent Director of eClerx  Services (2011 –2025) and 3M India (2014 –2024) including as 
Chairperson of their respective Audit Committees. He also served on the Stakeholders’ 
Grievance/Relationship, Corporate Social Responsibility and Nomination and Remuneration 
Committees, as applicable.
His extensive experience is expected to strengthen the Board's financial oversight, internal controls, 
risk management and corporate governance capabilities.
