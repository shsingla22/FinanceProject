Adcock Ingram - Profile 
Established in 1890 as a pharmacy in Krugersdorp, South Africa and evolved to be one of the biggest pharmaceutical and 
healthcare companies on the African continent
Second -largest South African based pharmaceutical company in terms of private market share 
Engages in the manufacturing, selling & marketing, and distribution of healthcare products to private and public sectors in 
Southern Africa
Southern Africa: Conducts business through four commercial divisions - Prescription, OTC, Hospital & Consumer 
Owns 3 manufacturing facilities near Johannesburg, South Africa and 49% of an Indian JV that runs 2 manufacturing facilities in 
India.
Source: Adcock Ingram Annual Report FY 24 and Adcock Ingram Website
