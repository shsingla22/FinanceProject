The highlights of the Company are as follows:  
 
1. Partnerships (excluding API & B2G) revenue growth of 33% Y-o-Y 
2. Franchise revenue growth of 11% Y-o-Y 
3. Pathology (excluding materials & others) revenue grew by 8% Y-o-Y 
4. Radiology revenue grew by 9% Y-o-Y 
5. Execution of share purchase agreement for acquisition of 100% stake in Think Health 
Diagnostic Private Limited to foray into ECG at home services 
6. Entered into partnership with TestEasy to introduce genome sequencing
