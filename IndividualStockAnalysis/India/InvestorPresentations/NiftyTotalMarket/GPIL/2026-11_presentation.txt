5 https://www.godawaripowerispat.com/Management Commentary
GPIL  delivered  a resilient  performance  during  H1FY27, with  revenue  growing  29% 
YoY, driven  by improved  realizations  across  key product  categories  and healthy  
sales  volumes . However,  profitability  was impacted  by elevated  input  costs,  
primarily  due to higher  iron ore procurement  costs  stemming  from  mining -related  
constraints  and higher  coal prices  amid  the ongoing  West  Asia  crisis . The 
permission  for overburden  dumping  and the commissioning  of the beneficiation  
plant  are expected  to strengthen  captive  iron ore utilization,  improve  cost 
efficiencies,  and support  margin  recovery  from  Q4FY27.
GPIL  continues  to execute  its growth  roadmap  through  strategic  investments  in 
backward  integration,  value -added  steel,  renewable  energy  and energy  storage . 
The 5.4 MnT  Beneficiation  Plant  remains  on track  for commissioning  in Q3FY27, 
with  the full benefits  of expanded  pellet  capacity  and improved  profitability  
expected  from  Q4FY27. The 20 GWh  BESS  project  is also progressing  well and is 
slated  for commissioning  in Q1FY28. The proposed  0.7 MnT  CRM  complex  is also 
on track , with  plans  to set up a Container  Manufacturing  Line, further  
strengthening  GPIL's  value -added  steel  portfolio .
Backed  by its integrated  operations,  captive  resources , and strategic  growth  
initiatives,  GPIL  remains  firmly  focused  on its Vision  2030  targets  of achieving  4x 
growth  in revenue  and 3x growth  in EBITDA  and PAT, while  building  a stronger,  
more  competitive  business  for the future .
- Mr. B.L. Agrawal, Chairman and Managing Director

INTERNATIONAL MARKET DOMESTIC MARKET
Iron ore prices remain under pressure
Iron ore prices  have  softened,  with  benchmark  prices  trading  
around  US $90 - 92/t in early  November . Weak  Chinese  steel  
demand,  subdued  mill profitability  and elevated  port  inventories  
continue  to weigh  on prices,  with  mills  adopting  a cautious,  need -
based  procurement  approach .
China's steel demand continues to decline
China's  steel  demand  is projected  to decline  by 2.0% in CY26 and 
1.0% in CY27, amid  continued  weakness  in the property  sector  and 
subdued  infrastructure  activity,  despite  policy  support  to stimulate  
domestic  demand .
Global Supply on the Rise
Global  iron ore supply  is expected  to rise with  the ramp -up of 
Guinea's  Simandou  project,  adding  to downside  pressure  on prices . 
Iron ore prices  are expected  to average  around  US$90–100/t over  
the near  term , with  further  downside  risks  from  incremental  low-
cost supply .Iron ore prices moderated
NMDC's  64% Fe fines  prices  moderated  from  Rs. 4,700/t to Rs. 
4,500/t during  Q2FY27, reflecting  a modest  correction  in domestic  
iron ore prices .
Pellet prices remain stable
Iron ore pellet  prices  traded  in the range  of Rs. 8,900 - 11,000/t 
during  Q2FY27, with  current  prices  around  Rs. 11,100/t. Higher  
input  costs  and supply  constraints  supported  prices  during  the 
quarter,  while  recent  demand  softness  has led to some  correction .
Strong steel demand outlook
India's  crude  steel  production  grew  2.9% YoY to 85.1 MnT  during  H1 
FY27, while  finished  steel  consumption  increased  7.6% YoY to 85.0 
MnT . Infrastructure,  housing,  railways  and manufacturing  investments  
continue  to support  the domestic  steel  demand  outlook .
