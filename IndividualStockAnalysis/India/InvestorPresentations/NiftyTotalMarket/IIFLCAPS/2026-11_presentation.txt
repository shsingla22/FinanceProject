23Fairfax Investment Update  
Update on Fairfax 
InvestmentThe  Company  has  received  all requisite  regulatory  and  shareholder  approvals  for the proposed  change  in control  
pursuant  to FIH Mauritius  Investments  Ltd and  its affiliate  (“Fairfax”)  acquiring  up to 51% stake  in the Company  
through  a preferential  allotment,  open  offer  and  arrangements  with  the existing  promoters . The  preferential  
allotment  and  open  offer  are under  implementation  and  will be completed  within  the prescribed  statutory  
timelines . The  promoter  arrangements  will be subject  to the outcome  of shares  tendered  in the open  offer .
Leadership  ContinuityUpon completion of the transaction, Fairfax  will join the Promoter Group alongside the existing promoters.             
FIH Mauritius Investments Ltd will also have the right to nominate up to two directors to the Board, for which 
shareholder approval has been received.
Capital  for GrowthThe  capital  infusion  of ~ ₹ 20 billion  is expected  to support  growth  across  broking,  wealth  and  asset  management,  
institutional  equities,  investment  banking  and  related  financial  services .
Long -term  PartnershipFairfax  India’s  long -term  capital  and  global  network  are expected  to strengthen  institutional  confidence  and  
expand  IIFL  Capital’s  international  relationships .
