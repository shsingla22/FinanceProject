Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director“We are pleased to report Q2 FY27 revenue of USD 487.6M, marking our 26th sequential 
quarter of growth. This reflects a 7.8% increase quarter -over-quarter and 20.0% year -
over-year, with an EBIT margin of 15.0%.
We made substantial progress towards the Nagarro acquisition, securing approximately 
94.04% of its share capital and voting rights, excluding treasury shares. We received 
shareholder approval for an equity raise of up to USD 450M, with combined equity and 
borrowings capped at USD 1,250M at the Extraordinary General Meeting. The proposed 
combination aligns with our long -term M&A strategy to strengthen our engineering 
capabilities, diversify our geographic presence and expand our global delivery 
capabilities. We value the confidence shown by our shareholders and financing partners 
as we work towards closing.
In parallel, we continue to strengthen our AI capabilities through our 3C framework and 
AI-driven platforms. Our focus is on developing Enterprise Context within our clients' 
environments that evolves with successive engagements and continues to grow in value 
as models, platforms and agents change.
Our sincere thanks to our clients, partners, employees and shareholders for their 
continued trust and support. We look forward to building on this momentum as Persistent 
prepares for an important new chapter.”
