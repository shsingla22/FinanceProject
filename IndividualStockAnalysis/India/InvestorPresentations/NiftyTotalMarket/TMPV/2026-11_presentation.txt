8Q2 FY27 performance 
V O L U M E  &  R E V E N U E
-Volumes reflect improvements in the UK and Overseas markets while conditions remain challenging in 
China. In the Middle East, ongoing disruption impacted wholesales quarter -on-quarter (QoQ) but retail 
volumes in the region indicate underlying demand is still strong. Volumes in the US remain steady and 
continue to represent about a third of total wholesales.
-Q2 wholesales of 82.5k, up 24.7% YoY and up 4.0% QoQ
-Q2 retails of 79.0k, down 7.5% YoY and down 1.1% QoQ
-Q2 revenue of £5.9bn, up 20.3% YoY and down 1.3% QoQ
P R O F I T A B I L I T Y
-Q2 adjusted EBIT margin of 1.1% and 2.0% for H1 reflects adverse market and model mix QoQ
-Profit before tax and exceptional items of £6m for Q2 and £115m for H1, up from a loss of £(485)m and 
£(134)m respectively a year ago, following recovery from the cyber incident in Q2 FY26
C A S H  F L O W
-Negative free cashflow of £540m for the quarter was largely due to the impact of reduced profitability 
together with negative working capital typical of the second quarter of the fiscal year
-Q2 cash balance £1.8bn; total liquidity £6.0bn including £1.7bn undrawn RCF, £1.5bn undrawn UKEF 
backed facility and a new £1.0bn syndicated term loan signed on 29 September 2026
