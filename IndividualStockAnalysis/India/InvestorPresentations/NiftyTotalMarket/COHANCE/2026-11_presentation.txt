Q2FY27: Sequential  recovery  gaining  traction ; integrated  capabilities strengthening  the growth  platform
 Q2 delivered  a clear  sequential  recovery , with  revenue up ~13% QoQ and  adjusted  EBITDA  margin  improving  to 10%  from  2% in Q1, 
demonstrating  progress  from  the Q1
 Strategic  Progress : One  Cohance  integration  is driving  operational  and  commercial  progress  across  businesses  
o Pharma  CDMO : 
• In Small  Molecules, the order  and  reload  position  for Q3 and  Q4 has  improved materially . Secured  two  new  commercial  and  one  late -
stage  programme  with  large  global  pharma customers,  strengthening  relationships  and  medium -term  growth  opportunities  
• In nucleic  acid  business,  Sapala’s  strong  performance  is supported  by increasing  integration  of its specialised  chemistry  and  
customer  relationships  with  Cohance’s  development  and  manufacturing  network .
o API+ : API+ revenue grew  Yo Y,  supported  by selective  pricing  actions,  while  lower  API volumes,  shipment  phasing  and  higher  raw -material  
costs  impacted margins .
o Specialty  Chemicals : Specialty  Chemicals  declined  YoY  due  to lower  AgChem  volumes,  pricing  pressure  and  programme  phasing, while  
Performance  Materials  remained relatively  stable .
 Focused  on expanding  customer  value -chain participation  and  improving  programme  conversion  through  integrated  development -to-
manufacturing  capabilities,  including  bioconjugation,  ADC  payload -linkers  and  R&D infrastructure .
 Will maintain  disciplined  capital  allocation, with  investments  phased based on customer  visibility,  project  readiness and  expected returns .
FY27 Outlook : Stronger  H2 expected
 H2FY27 revenue and  EBITDA  expected to grow  Yo Y,  supported by continued  recovery  and  progressive integration  of Cohance’s  capabilities to 
unlock  platform  potential . H2 focus  is on improving  revenue mix  and  utilisation  to support  progressive margin  recovery .
 H2 is expected  to be stronger  than H1, driven  by Small Molecule  deliveries,  API+ resilience  and  Sapala growth,  with  improved revenue mix  and  
utilisation  supporting  EBITDA  recovery .
