unvested ESOP recorded so far is reversed in that 
quarter 
•For new ESOP grants, the total estimated charge would 
be the number of options granted times the fair value 
per share computed basis the fair value of the option, 
as per the valuation report .The charge is front -ended 
with approximately 52% in Year 1, 27% Year 2, 15% in 
Year 3 and 6% in Year 4 
•Movements of share price after the date of the grant 
do not affect the ESOP charge for already granted 
ESOPs
