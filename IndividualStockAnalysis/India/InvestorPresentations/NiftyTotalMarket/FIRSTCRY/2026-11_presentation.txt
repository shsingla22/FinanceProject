Message from Managing Director & CEO  
 
Dear Shareholders, 
FirstCry was built to give every parent and every child a trusted one-stop destination with safe, high-
quality products and a seamless experience, whether they find us in a maternity ward, on our app, in 
any of our 1,188 stores or at their door two hours after ordering. From day zero, our goal has been to earn 
parents’ trust and continue to raise the bar. Sixteen years later, that goal has not changed. Over the last 
twelve months, 12.1 million parents placed their trust in FirstCry, and we keep building to stay worthy of 
it. 
Our first two years as a listed company have strengthened our strategic focus and reinforced our 
commitment to serving parents and children. Expectations for faster delivery have evolved, competition 
in diapers has intensified, and our India margins have come under pressure. We have responded with 
focused initiatives, faster execution of new ideas through Rocketbees & FirstCry Qwik and clear 
communication on our progress and outcomes. We remain committed to being transparent about our 
strategic choices, including the near-term margin impact of investing in our own delivery network to 
support long-term growth and enhance the customer experience.  
In H1 FY27, our investments in our own delivery network, quick commerce and changes in offline 
assortment have started to show in the results of our India Multi-channel business. India Multi-channel 
revenue grew 18.6% year on year in Q2 FY’27, our strongest growth in eight quarters. Further our 
consolidated revenue grew 14.1% in Q2 FY’27, our strongest growth in six quarters. Below, we share the 
key initiative and their early results.

Emerging Trends: Building for How Parents Will Shop Next 
Parenting is changing: parents are busier than ever; families are choosing fewer, higher-quality products 
over fast fashion. At FirstCry, we are building products and customer experiences not just for how parents 
shop today, but for what they are telling us about how they will shop tomorrow. 
Bonfino: Broadening our premium offering.  As Indian families increasingly trade up, they are looking for 
products that combine thoughtful design and quality with global standards. This year, we expanded the 
Bonfino range in India across children’s fashion, gear, toys and school supplies. Bonfino builds on more 
than a decade of experience developing our own brands, which now account for over 58% of India 
Multi-channel GMV. By giving families more reasons to choose one of our own brands when they trade 
up, Bonfino supports our premiumisation strategy and gives us room to further grow the home brand 
share of GMV.  
FirstCry International store. This quarter, we launched the International Online Store on FirstCry, giving 
parents across India one place to discover and shop for global parenting brands. The store brings 
together names such as Carter’s, Joie, Dr. Brown’s, Nuna and Silver Cross across categories including 
clothing, feeding and baby gear. By bringing these brands together on our platform, we’re making it 
easier for families to find authentic products, with the convenience of nationwide delivery and FirstCry 
customer support.  
AI: a smarter shopping experience for busy parents.  Indian consumers have been quick to adopt AI 
powered search, and AI is now transforming how they shop. At FirstCry, we have drawn on more than 15 
years of purchase data to refine our personalised recommendation engine and our Smart Styler, which 
builds a complete look around a single item. These AI-enabled tools now drive a meaningful and growing 
share of our revenue. Behind the scenes, AI is speeding up how quickly new products go live and 
powering customer support across voice, email and chat, so parents get faster resolution to their queries. 
FirstCry World: India’s grandest kids’ carnival. Moms and Dads across India are increasingly involving their 
children in choosing the toys they play with, the clothes they wear, and the activities they do. We want 
to be in on the secret! On 5th & 6th December in Pune and 9th & 10th January in Bengaluru, we will host 
FirstCry World, our weekend-long carnival for kids and parents. Kids can enjoy character meet-and-
greets, storytelling, theatre, play areas and a grand parade, while parents can compete in stroller races, 
hear from paediatricians and educators, and indulge in some retail therapy with leading kids’ and 
women’s brands and a FirstCry pop-up store. I look forward to seeing you there.

Looking Ahead 
The past year tested our operating model in a fast-changing competitive environment. We learned from 
it. We invested through it. And we changed much of what needed changing. 
The progress in Q2 FY27 is encouraging, particularly the growth momentum in India Multi-channel, 
improvements in delivery capabilities and stronger operating performance across our businesses. 
Our priorities remain clear - higher sustained growth, continue improving the customer experience, 
increase purchase frequency and strengthen profitability. The investments we have made in our delivery 
network, stores and product offering are beginning to show results, and our focus now is on building on 
this progress consistently. We know that shareholder trust, like customer trust, must be earned 
continuously. We are committed to doing exactly that. 
We are sure that with all the above initiatives are setting up FirstCry for a great parenting experience and 
outstanding trajectory for next decade.

FAQs 
Figures are for Q2 FY27 (quarter ended 30 September 2026) compared with Q2 FY26, or for H1 FY27 where 
stated, in INR Mn unless stated. Adjusted EBITDA excludes ESOP cost and other income.  
Q1. How did the business perform in Q2 FY27? 
● Consolidated:  Revenue grew 14.1% year on year to INR 23,958 Mn, our strongest growth in last six 
quarters, with adjusted EBITDA margin of 4.9% (5.8% in Q2 FY26). While the reduction in the Gross 
Margin of India Multi-channel business brought down the adjusted EBITDA, it got largely positively 
offset by cost optimisation below the gross magin in India Multi-channel business, narrower losses in 
International and higher profitability at GlobalBees. 
● India Multi-channel:  India Multi-channel revenue grew 18.6% year on year in Q2, our strongest growth 
in eight quarters. India Multi-channel adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 6.9% from 5.7% in Q1, while 
remaining below 9.1% a year ago. 
● International:  Revenue grew about 6% amid difficult macro conditions. Losses continued to narrow, 
to 6.4% of revenue compared to 8% an year earlier. 
● GlobalBees:  Revenue grew about 8%, held back by a planned warehouse transition at one core 
brand. Adjusted EBITDA margin rose to 3.6% of revenue compared to 2.1% an year earlier. 
Q2. How is India Multi-channel growth trending?  
 
India Multi-channel revenue grew 18.6% year on year in Q2 FY27, our strongest growth in eight quarters. 
Growth has now improved for six consecutive quarters, led primarily by volume growth, with online, offline

Q3. What is the outlook for India Multi-channel gross margin and EBITDA margin? 
Gross margin.  In Q3 FY26, India Multi-channel gross margin fell 140 bps year on year because of 
heightened competition in diapers and additional drop in gross margin by 140 Bps in Q4 FY 26 driven by 
higher crude-linked raw material costs & rupee depreciation in our manufacturing business. The 
competitive intensity in the diaper business has started softening in the online and offline business and 
has lead to a 100 bps gross margin improvement in Q2 FY 27 over Q3 FY 26. We believe that this gross 
margin will further improve in Q3 FY 27 as well, as evident from the improved gross margin in October, 
2026.  
Manufacturing margins, however, remain under pressure from rising crude-linked raw material costs and 
the lag in passing these increases on to customers. While the cost increases experienced in Q4 FY 26 
have been majorly passed through, subsequent increases in input prices have extended this pass 
through lag and continued to weigh on gross margins. 
Stabilisation in crude-linked input prices would allow customer pricing to catch up, supporting a recovery 
in manufacturing gross margins. A decline in input prices could provide a further margin benefit, 
depending on the timing of corresponding adjustments to customer pricing. While the crude prices from 
October, 2025 to October, 2026 has almost doubled, we have seen the crude prices continuously 
coming down starting 1st week of November, 2026. If the same momentum continues, we will see a 
recovery of gross margin in our manufacturing business starting Q4 FY 27. 
EBITDA margin.  India Multi-channel adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 6.9% in Q2 FY27 from 5.7% in Q1 
FY27, while remaining below 9.1% a year ago. Crude-linked inputs and currency movements continued 
to put pressure on manufacturing margins. We however, remain focused on improving EBITDA margins 
through cost optimisation below the gross margin line. These efficiencies have positively offset 
approximately 70 basis points of the gross margin pressure at the EBITDA level. Further, we also see a 
reduction in our logistic cost with the reverse logistics by Rocketbees (pick up of return from customers) 
has also scaled up.

Q4. Discuss the performance of the International business. 
 
International revenue growth slowed to 6% year on year, on account of the geopolitical uncertainty and 
disruption witnessed in the Middle East during the quarter. Profitability continued to improve. Gross 
margin rose from 26.3% to 28.5% year on year in Q2 FY 27, and adjusted EBITDA losses fell to INR 159 Mn,

A particularly encouraging aspect of this journey has been the pace of gross-margin improvement in our 
Middle East business. The business has achieved, in approximately five years, a gross-margin profile that 
took our India business nearly eight years to build. We believe this is a strong validation of the FirstCry 
India playbook that we have successfully taken to the Middle East—leveraging the experience, 
capabilities and learnings accumulated over many years in India to accelerate the development of our 
business in a newer market. The improvement has been driven by many of the same structural levers that 
have underpinned our India business, including increasing contribution from home brands, improving 
sourcing and procurement economics, better commercial terms with third-party brands, optimisation of 
product and category mix, and increasing scale.  
While the Middle East has its own distinct consumer preferences and market dynamics, our ability to 
adapt and apply the core elements of the India playbook has enabled us to progress faster along the 
margin curve. We believe these levers have further room to develop as the business scales, providing an 
opportunity to progressively strengthen gross margins and, together with operating leverage, continue 
reducing losses over time.

Q5. Discuss the performance of the GlobalBees business. 
GlobalBees has evolved considerably since we started this journey. The initial phase focused on 
acquiring promising digital-first consumer brands and building common capabilities around them. Over 
the last few years, our focus has increasingly shifted from acquiring brands to strengthening the 
businesses we already own—building scale, improving profitability and creating enduring consumer 
brands. As part of this process, we rationalized brands that did not meet our expectations on growth or 
profitability. With the rationalization largely complete, management can now focus its resources and 
capital on our core categories (Home Improvement & Utilities, Home Appliances, Health & Personal 
Care, and Active Lifestyle & Accessories), where we see the highest potential for growth. 
In Q2 FY27, GlobalBees revenue grew about 8% year on year. Growth was slower than last year because 
one of our core brands moved its warehouse and inventory. This was a planned transition we flagged 
on our previous earnings call and completed in Q2, and it does not, in our view, alter the underlying 
growth potential of the portfolio. Profitability improved sharply: adjusted EBITDA rose from INR 104 Mn to 
INR 187 Mn year on year, and adjusted EBITDA margin from 2.1% to 3.6%. Our Q2 growth would have 
been stronger had the annual sales by 3P Platforms would not have advanced to Q3 and had festivals 
like Dussehra not fallen 3 weeks later this year, shifting some festive sales from Sep 26 to Oct 26. 
GlobalBees is now entering its next phase, from portfolio creation and rationalisation to sustainable 
organic growth and profitability. Our focus is on scaling the strongest brands, improving operating 
leverage and generating better returns on the capital invested. As these businesses gain scale and 
maturity, this also gives us strategic flexibility around monetisation over time, from bringing in strategic or 
financial partners, pursuing other value unlocking opportunities or enabling certain businesses to access 
the capital markets independently when they are ready. Any such decision would be guided by what 
we believe maximises long-term value for GlobalBees and Brainbees shareholders.
