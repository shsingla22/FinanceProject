The Scope and quality of online services as online Service Index (OSI) 
The Status of the development of telecommunication infrastructure or the Telecommunication Infrastructure Index (TII)

Key Trends in E -Governance:
In India, the national level e -governance programme called National e -Governance Plan ( NeGP ) was initiated in 2006 with 31 Mission Mode Projects (MMP)
Due to the shortcomings in NeGP , the Government of India conceptualized ‘ eKranti ’ or NeGP  2.0
New and revamped projects are in accordance with the key principles of e -Kranti namely ‘Transformation and not Translation’, ‘In tegrated Services and not Individual 
Services’, ‘Government Process Reengineering (GPR), among others
The portfolio of Mission Mode Projects has increased from 31 to 44 MMPs
