increased requirement being metfrom non-OPEC countries such asUSA, Norway &Canada .Global oildemand

Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR) inUSA remains atmulti decade lows onaccount offrequent draws tocontain
inflation .Current level of357 mnbarrels issignificantly lower than lastdecade average of650 mnbarrels .
Refilling theSPR hascommenced anditislikely tocreate anartificial floor inthecrude oilmarket .
