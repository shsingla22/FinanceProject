and customer anxiety about EVs leading to OEMs 
scaling down production

remain steady between 16 -17mn units (C24: 16mn)

China

•EV penetration : C24 : 13% (same as C23); C29 : 43%

•Protect/ Optimise  Margin

•Gears plant developing electric vehicle 
transmission parts
