 STRATEGY OVERVIEW

STRATEGYShort Term
Medium term
Electric 
Vehicles•CY26 is expected to be good for all segments post the GST 
cut :  IHS forecasts production of Light Vehicles  to grow at 
7.4% & of MHCVs at 5.3%; CRISIL expects 2W production to 
grow 8 -10%
•Healthy growth in all segments; MHCVs may lag
•CAGR Next 5 years for Light Vehicles : 5.3% (IHS), 2W: 7 -9% 
(CRISIL), Tractors : 6 -7% (CRISIL), MHCVs : 1.8% (IHS)
•Steady expansion; good models being launched
•Penetration Increase in next 5 years - e4W: 3% to 15%; e2W 
: 6% to 20 -25%; e3W & eBus  to penetrate faster
•Across verticals - expand capacities, develop higher value -
added products and improve technological capabilities to 
cater to a growing but highly competitive market
•Identified product categories that will drive growth in each 
vertical based on high volumes or EVs and the level of 
product complexity.  Examples –  
•Bengaluru Forgings : fully machined  precision forgings for 
driveline; 
•Aluminum: housings for e2W & e4W, high tonnage machined 
castings; 
•Stampings :  high tonnage press panels; Composites & Gears : 
Add EV parts to existing portfolio •Light vehicles: Negative growth in CY24 & CY25 as well as 
CY26 forecasts
•MHCVs: CY25 saw drop of -1.6% vs -24.7% in CY24. CY26, 
CY27 & CY28 to grow on small base
•In the next 3 -5 years, light vehicles demand to remain 
steady between 15 -17mn units (CY25: 16mn); Rising costs, 
delayed EVs & competition from China
•MHCVs to grow 5 -8% p.a. in next 3 years
•After initial surge, growth in EV penetration slowing down 
(CY25: 16%)
•Most EV projects delayed
•Protect profitability;
•Adapt plants to lower volume requirements,
•Seek additional business opportunities as supply 
chain consolidates. 
•Dependency on crankshafts to be redundant in EVs; 
•Plan to offset by steel & aluminum parts not affected by 
EV transition, like battery plates, aluminum knuckles and 
differential crowns;  
•Develop EV  transmission parts at Gears plant.
