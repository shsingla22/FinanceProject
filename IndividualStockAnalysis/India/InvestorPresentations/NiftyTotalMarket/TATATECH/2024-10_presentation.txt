Shift in Vehicle Cybersecurity

Software Defined Vehicles (SDVs) 
driven by safety and sustainability, 
facing cybersecurity challenges. The 
market for SDVs is expected to reach 
USD 210.88 billion by 2032, shaping 
the future of the automotive industry.

Automobile Companies Build AI -
Powered Cars

in the last few years  and we have seen 
a software -oriented approach 
replacing the traditional hardware -
centric approach in automobiles. Tata

of Understanding (MoU) with TiHAN  IIT 
Hyderabad to develop Software 
Defined Vehicles (SDV) and ADAS.

industry is undergoing a 
transformative shift towards 
Software -Defined Vehicles (SDVs), 
signalling  a new era where software 
takes precedence over hardware.
