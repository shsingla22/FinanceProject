Balance sheet deleveraging is a key part of our strategy going forward
•The Company intends to become Net Debtzero company (corporate level)
•Currently net debt is at INR 2,13,770.45 lakhs as on nine month period ended 31stDecember 2023

•Will further reduce our debt as well as expand our business
•However, Company would continue to need Patient Money for Aggregating or Cleaning Land Banks
•Projects in pipeline are located in Mumbai city including South Central Mumbai
