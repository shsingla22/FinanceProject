Message from the Chairman
Dr. Sampath Ravi Narayanan
Founder, Chairman & Managing DirectorTwo years ago, AXISCADES was a collection of businesses: 
some core, some sub -scale, and some simply not ours to 
run. Today, we are becoming a focused, technology -led 
company built on four pillars: Defence , XIDA, Aerospace and 
Space.
Getting here required hard decisions. This half, we 
completed Phase 1 of our strategic divestment on time, 
giving us the balance sheet to invest in what matters. We 
also chose to confront the past rather than carry it. Two 
legacy, non -core acquisitions made before the current 
leadership, Add Solutions and Epcogen , have been 
addressed decisively and in one go. These are one -time, 
non-cash charges that do not touch our customers, orders or 
cash, and they will not recur. We would rather show a clean 
picture than a comfortable one.
Our core is growing fast. In the first half, Defence  grew 
46% and XIDA grew 55%. Aerospace Manufacturing 
delivered an impressive ₹23.1 Cr of revenue in H1, with 
Cloud Wave earning an EBITDA margin above 20%. Our 
retained business also grew 55% and, for the first time, is the 
majority of Group revenue. The company we are building is 
already larger than the company we are leaving behind.
India’s defence  moment is real, and we are at its heart.  
Since our last results, we have won 16 new defence  orders. 
We have the projects won, the people to execute them and 
the platforms to deliver.Our infrastructure is ready for this growth. DAL is fully in 
place. At DAC, Phase 1, which houses our defence  units and 
the XIDA expansion, will be completed by Q2 FY28. In 
Aerospace, Cloud Wave was earnings -accretive from its first 
quarter. In Space, our partnerships are in place and we 
remain on track for first revenue in FY28.
We recognize the questions on investors’ minds. Q2 
operating margins were lower because of non-cash  ESOP 
charge  booked in one quarter. We also incurred 
transformation -related legal and advisory costs, and carried a 
corporate structure ahead of revenue. Each eases from Q3, 
and the underlying business remains healthy and profitable. 
On execution, most of our commitments are delivered or on 
course. Where a timeline has moved, such as the divestment 
Phase 2 closing, we are keeping you informed. With our cost 
base largely in place, growth now flows through. Our order 
momentum supports a stronger second half, and Power 930 
remains on course.
The hardest part is behind us. Our business is simpler, our 
finances are stronger and our focus is clearer. I invite you to 
stay invested and to stay with us on this journey. Trust our 
direction and hold us to our promises. Together, we will build 
an AXISCADES that India and the world see as a leader in 
defence , aerospace and advanced technology.
My sincere thanks to our employees, customers, Board 
colleagues and shareholders for their commitment, 
confidence, counsel and belief.

Biting the Bullet: Legacy Non -Core Assets Written Off 
A bold, one -time decision —two legacy acquisitions cleaned out in a single quarter, so the Company moves forward on a clean bal ance sheet
Add Solutions
A legacy, non -core acquisition. Voluntary dissolution has been initiated 
and the investment has been written off.
Impairment: ₹38.4 Cr (Add Solutions and legacy holdings)Epcogen Private Limited
Acquired on 1 December 2023, providing engineering design and 
solutions for the energy sector. The business transferred with the 
divested engineering services business on 30 September 2026, at the 
Phase 1 closing. Goodwill of ₹7.98 Cr was impaired in FY26; the 
remaining goodwill of ₹19.9 Cr has been written off in Q2 FY27.
Why we took the hit now
Both were legacy, non -core acquisitions. Rather than carry them forward, we chose to take the full hit now, decisively and in a single quarter. The 
write -offs are exceptional items —non-cash and below EBITDA; operating revenue and EBITDA are unaffected —and are absorbed by the 
exceptional gain recognised in Q2 FY27. Total exceptional write -offs in Q2 FY27: ~₹58.3 Cr (₹38.4 Cr impairment in continuing op erations plus ₹19.9 
Cr Epcogen in discontinued operations). Exceptional gain in Q2 FY27: ₹140.84 Cr on the Phase 1 disposal, including ₹20.94 Cr FCTR recycled and 
after the Epcogen write -off, less ₹11.54 Cr of transaction costs; net discontinued exceptional gain ₹129.30 Cr.
One clean break: the past is written off once, and the balance sheet and management attention are now fully on Defence, XIDA,
Aerospace and Space.
