19FY 26 -27 
OutlookClosing the year with strong performance  momentum 
while laying the groundwork for future growth acceleration
Customer trust is rebuilt and the destocking situation on 
legacy Heubach products is easing - we see especially 
global accounts buying again more in Jan and early Feb
Cautiously navigating a challenging market environment  
driven by geopolitical uncertainty resulting in logistics 
challenges, rising RM cost, and customer’s cautionary 
purchasing behavior
Integration is well progressing (SAP, GCC, One Culture) and 
One Sudarshan is further solidifying
Our work on value capture will help in profit and working 
capital improvements
