DisclaimerAbout The Company
Founded in October 2017 and headquartered in Neemrana, Rajasthan, KRN Heat Exchanger and Refrigeration Limited is one of Indi a's leading manufacturers of heat 
exchangers and HVAC&R solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial applications. The Company manufactures heat excha ngers, cooling coils, and integrated 
HVAC&R solutions for domestic and global markets, with a diversified portfolio comprising Fin & Tube Coils, Bar & Plate, Comp lete HVAC Systems, Bus AC Systems, 
Refrigeration Components, Technical Tubes, Sheet Metal Parts, and Tubings . KRN serves diverse end -use industries including HVAC, refrigeration, data centers , automotive, 
railways, industrial cooling, process applications, power, and marine.
Its state -of-the-art manufacturing facilities in Neemrana, Rajasthan are equipped with integrated production, testing, and quali ty-control infrastructure, enabling efficient 
manufacturing and ensuring high standards of quality and precision. Focused on customized product development, precision engi neering, operational excellence, and 
stringent quality standards, KRN delivers reliable, application -specific solutions. Supported by a skilled workforce and a custo mer -centric approach, the Company 
continues to strengthen its domestic and international presence, with established exports across key geographies including No rth America, Europe, and the UAE.
The Company  got listed  in NSE  and BSE in October,  2024

continued strength of our business model and the growing demand for efficient heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refr igeration

international markets.

the trust that customers are placing in our manufacturing capabilities, product quality and execution. At the same time, stro ng domestic
