Implementation of Green Initiatives
Bharti Infratel has institutionalized ‘GreenTowers P7 ’ programme, aimed at minimizing dependency on diesel consumption 
and thereby, reducing the carbon footprint
The ‘GreenTowers P7’ programme is based on seven innovative ideas deploying cleaner energy technologies
We have adopted a three -pronged strategy to run this programme:
Solar Installations and Diesel Free Towers 1
Close to ~3,000 solar powered towers
Over 43,000 (~ 47% of the Portfolio) towers across the network are Green towers
Improving Energy Efficiency of Towers 2
Implemented hybrid battery bank solutions in towers across the country
Reduction of Power Consumption  via Free Cooling Units (FCU) 3
FCUs utilize the outside ambient air for cooling the shelter
