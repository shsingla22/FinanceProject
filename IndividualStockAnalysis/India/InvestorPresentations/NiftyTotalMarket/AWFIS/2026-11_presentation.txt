with more than 12months ofvintage reached 84.1%.

design further enhances workspace quality, space utilisation and operational efficiency, supporting higher realisations .

rental revenue .Our integrated offerings across co-working, managed offices, design and build, and allied workplace services position
usasalong -term partner forGCCs, from their initial entry into India toexpansion across multiple locations .

strengthening client renewals and expanding through capital -efficient supply models isexpected todrive higher realisations and
profitability .
Aswemove ahead, weare confident that our strategy isdelivering thedesired results, with Gold and Elite centres gaining traction
