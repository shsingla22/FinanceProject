KEY EVENTS TILL DATE IN FY27
6Supplies of dual channel ABS commenced in Oct 2026 . Setting up additional capacities for ABS.
Also setting up additional Disc Brake capacities in Chennai, SOP expected in Q4 FY27.
Second SMT line for electronics installed to cater to in -house ABS ECU and higher BMS volumes.
New infrastructure setup at Sanand, Gujarat to cater to large volume orders for Solar Dampers 
and Actuators. SOP for Solar Damper took place in Q2 FY27, Actuators expected in Q4 FY27. 
Production commenced at the AURIC Shendra  plant in Chh. Sambhajinagar,
 in Sept 2026. Facility to cater to 4Ws EV OEMs & exports .
Production commenced at the Battery Pack plant in Pune in Q1FY27, full ramp up of
2W business in Q3 FY27. Expect SOP for newly nominated 4W Battery Pack business
in H1 FY28.
New plant being constructed to meet growing Aluminium Forging production. SOP expected in 
Q4FY27. 
Expanding 4W machined aluminium casting capacity across Chennai, 
Vallam  and Chakan. Facilities to increase penetration in Indian and Korean OEMs.
