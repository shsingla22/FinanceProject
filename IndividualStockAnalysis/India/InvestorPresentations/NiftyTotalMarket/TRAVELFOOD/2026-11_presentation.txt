It was one of the most challenging quarters for passenger traffic in recent periods, amid an unusually volatile 
operating environment. Despite these headwinds, TFS' system -wide sales grew 18.8% year -on-year and the 
business continued to deliver healthy underlying profitability, demonstrating the resilience of our portfolio and 
expanding network. This performance reinforces our confidence in the business and its ability to grow through 
periods of near -term volatility .
Our strategy remains focused on strengthening the core while building the next phase of growth for TFS. Over the 
last twelve months, we have mobilised more than 70 units and expanded our presence to 22 airports. Many of 
these are still ramping up, and with a significant pipeline of outlets yet to be mobilised across new and existing 
airports, we have good visibility of continued growth as these operations mature. Alongside this, we continue to 
develop new growth avenues in passenger services, including EATS .
Looking ahead, our priorities remain clear: deepen our presence at existing airports, scale operations across new 
and greenfield airports, and selectively expand beyond our core, particularly in international lounges and 
expressways. With a strong, debt -free balance sheet and an expanding network, we believe TFS is well positioned 
to capture the long -term growth opportunities across the aviation and broader travel ecosystem.
