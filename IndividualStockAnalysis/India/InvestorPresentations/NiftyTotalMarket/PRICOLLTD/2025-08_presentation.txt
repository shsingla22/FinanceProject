Pricol Partners with Italy's Domino S.r.l for Two -Wheeler Control Systems
Pricol Limited, has entered into a Technology License Agreement with Italy -based DOMINO S.R.L., a 
global leader in motorcycle control systems. This partnership aims to expand advanced two -wheeler 
handlebar control technologies such as throttles, switches, and allied products across India and 
Southeast Asia, marking a pivotal step in Pricol’s innovation -driven growth strategy.
