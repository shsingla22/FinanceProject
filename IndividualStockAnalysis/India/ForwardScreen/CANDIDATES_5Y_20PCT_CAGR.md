## How to read this list

These names are *statistical candidates* — companies whose
current fundamentals match historical patterns that delivered
>=20% 5-year CAGR with high probability. They are NOT individual
buy recommendations. In particular:

1. **Valuation overlay matters.** The single biggest historical
   failure in PRIMARY-screen matches (AFFLE) was a P/E >100x
   company where multiple compression dragged 5-yr returns to
   +5.8%, far below the +42% peer average. PRIMARY candidates
   trading above ~75x P/E deserve extra scrutiny.

2. **Diversify across sectors.** The historical winners cluster
   in capex-heavy domestic-consumption businesses. Concentration
   in a single sector raises factor risk.

3. **Re-screen annually.** A company that satisfies the screen
   today may not next year as its capex cycle matures.

4. **Cross-check with cash flow.** The screen trusts reported P&L
   and BS numbers. For any candidate, verify operating cash flow
   tracks reported net profit (no receivables/inventory bloat).
