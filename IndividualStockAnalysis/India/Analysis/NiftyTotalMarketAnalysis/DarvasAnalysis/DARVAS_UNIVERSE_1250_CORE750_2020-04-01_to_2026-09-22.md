# Screen 1,250, fund the top 750 first — April 2020 → September 2026

The rule you asked for, replayed: the universe stays at the top 1,250 NSE
companies by market cap (the backtest stand-in for NiftyTotalMarket ∪
top 1,250), but each Friday's signals are funded in size order — names
inside the top 750 by market cap first, then within a size tier fresh
full qualifiers before ladder-watch promotions, then the loudest volume
reaction. Everything else is the current skill: three gates, the ladder
watch with breakout-only promotion, ten equal slices, daily stops, weekly
ratchets, Angel One charges on every order and capital-gains tax settled
every 1 April. Same archive, same window, same frictions as the earlier
notes; "net" is after costs and tax, the Nifty figure is pre-cost, pre-tax.

Funding the large names first recovers almost the whole gap: ₹442 against
₹461 on the narrow universe, from ₹181 when small caps were allowed to
take the slots. Gross return is identical to the narrow run (₹609 vs
₹608), so the small difference net is costs and the timing of tax on a
slightly different trade path.

## What is live

The live skill now runs exactly this rule: universe NiftyTotalMarket ∪
top 1,250, funding order large names (index constituents or top 750 by
market cap) → within a tier fresh before promoted → loudest reaction.
Smaller names are marked † in the closing section and take a slice only
if one is left. The constituents file carries each name's market-cap
rank so the report can tell the tiers apart.
