### 4. Track record — the incoming management

**Vivek Pabari** — Chief Financial Officer and Key Managerial Personnel. from: internal; experience: more than 17 years; qualifications: Post Graduate Diploma in Management from IIM Bangalore, specialising in finance, and is a Bachelor of Technology in Information & Communication Technology.

The filing's own profile: “Mr. Vivek Pabari has been one of the finance leaders in the Company, in charge of critical portfolios of Corporate Finance, Investor Relations and Treasury. He was part of the core team that led the Initial Public Offering of the Company and acquisitions of Ecom Express and Spoton Logistics. Vivek has been with Delhivery Limited since June 2021. He has more than 17 years of experience. Prior to Delhivery, he was an investment banker with Deutsche Bank, where he advised corporate clients on several capital raises and M&As.”
