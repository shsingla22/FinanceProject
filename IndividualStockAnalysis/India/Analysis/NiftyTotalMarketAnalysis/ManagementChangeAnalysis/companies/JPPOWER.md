## JPPOWER — Jaiprakash Power Ventures Ltd.

**Change of control — completed · CEO / MD change · CFO change**

### 1. What is changing

**Ownership — Change of control — completed.** Promoter Jaiprakash Associates Ltd went through insolvency; NCLT approved Adani Enterprises' resolution plan. Under it JAL signed a share sale agreement on 20-05-2026 transferring its entire 24% stake in JPPOWER to Adani Power Ltd for Rs. 2,993.59 crore. Implemented 21-05-2026; JAL's voting rights passed to APL and the Jaypee executive directors resigned.
