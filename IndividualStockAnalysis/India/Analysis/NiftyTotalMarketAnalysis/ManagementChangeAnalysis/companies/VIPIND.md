### 1. What is changing

**Ownership — Change of control — completed.** Under a 13 July 2025 share purchase agreement, Piramal-family promoter entities sold out to Multiples Private Equity funds, which acquired control and were classified as promoters. The transaction completed in December 2025: promoter sellers offloaded 26.07% via open market and Multiples' funds bought 18.68%, holding 22.92%.
