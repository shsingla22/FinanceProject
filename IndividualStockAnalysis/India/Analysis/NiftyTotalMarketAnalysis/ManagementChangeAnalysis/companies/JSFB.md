## JSFB — Jana Small Finance Bank Ltd.

### 1. What is changing

**Ownership — Large stake change, same controller.** Jana Holdings Limited, a promoter of Jana Small Finance Bank, signed a Share Purchase Agreement on 18 May 2026 to sell 5,160,903 shares (~4.9% of paid-up capital) to TVS Motor Company Limited for INR 193.31 crore.
