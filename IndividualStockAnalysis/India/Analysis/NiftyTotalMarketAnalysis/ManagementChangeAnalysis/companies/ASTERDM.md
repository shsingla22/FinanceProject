### 1. What is changing

**Ownership — Change of control — completed.** The scheme of amalgamation of Quality Care India Limited into Aster DM Healthcare became effective 1 July 2026. Shares issued under the scheme made BCP Asia II Topco IV Pte. Ltd. a promoter holding 29.71% — larger than founder Azad Moopen's 24.01%.
