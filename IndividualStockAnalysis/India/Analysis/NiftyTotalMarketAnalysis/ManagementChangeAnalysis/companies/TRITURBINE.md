## TRITURBINE — Triveni Turbine Ltd.

**Within the promoter family / group**

### 1. What is changing

**Ownership — Within the promoter family / group.** Rati Sawhney transferred her 26.60% stake in Subhadra Trade and Finance (promoter holding 27.28% of Triveni Turbine) to the Nikhil Sawhney Trust without consideration, lifting the Trust to 63.30% of Subhadra. The Trust, part of the promoter group, thereby indirectly acquired control; promoter holding stayed 55.84%. Nikhil Sawhney Trust now leads.
