## AADHARHFC — Aadhar Housing Finance Ltd.

**Change of control — completed**

### 1. What is changing

**Ownership — Change of control — completed.** Blackstone's BCP Topco VII sold its entire 75.19% promoter stake in Aadhar Housing Finance: 28,20,52,121 shares to BCP Asia II Holdco VII (a different Blackstone fund) at ₹425 and 4,41,39,236 shares to AXDI LDII SPV 1. Completed 25–26 February 2026 after a mandatory open offer; the acquirer became promoter and the old promoter group was reclassified as public.
