### 5. Why the demerger — point by point

No earnings-call transcripts were supplied ("no transcripts on file"), so this reconstruction rests solely on Raymond Limited's filings. Management's own framing, set out in the EGM explanatory statements of May 25, 2026 and September 8, 2026, is that the separation is already executed — "the demerger of its lifestyle and real estate business" — leaving Raymond Limited as "a conglomerate which is in engineering businesses through its subsidiaries," namely JK Maini Precision Technology Limited (precision engineering and auto components) and JK Maini Global Aerospace Limited (aerospace / new-age industrial and technology-led businesses), while Raymond Lifestyle Limited (branded textile, branded apparel, garmenting, high-value shirting, emerging) and Raymond Realty Limited (100-acre Thane land ~₹25,000 cr revenue potential plus asset-light Mumbai JDAs ~₹27,000 cr potential) trade separately. The problem the structure is said to solve is focus and clean capital allocation: the Q2/H1 FY2027 group update (filed November 2, 2026) titles the result "Raymond Portfolio: Pure-Play Platforms" across three listed entities, each with its own ~75%-independent board (vs a ~50% top-100 Indian average), its own ring-fenced audit/pay/risk/related-party committees, its own CARE/CRISIL ratings (RLL AA/A1+, Raymond Realty and TenX A+/A-, JKMPTL A+/A1+, JKMGAL A/A1 within ₹9,476 cr rated debt) and its own balance sheet — group net debt/EBITDA 0.43x on TTM EBITDA of ₹1,738 cr and revenue of ₹13,115 cr, liquidity up from ₹571 cr in FY20 to ₹2,791 cr, and short-term debt cut from 74% of gross debt in 2020 to 38% in 2026 with 4.24-year average maturity. It also de-concentrates earnings: non-lifestyle verticals now generate 49% of YTD EBITDA (388.16 of 792), with engineering Raymond Ltd. posting H1FY27 income of ₹1,311 cr (+17%), EBITDA ₹203 cr (+22%), PBT ₹84 cr (+69%), aerospace & defence revenue ₹262 cr (+56%) and a ₹77 cr net cash surplus, against FY21 engineering revenue of just ₹141 cr and EBITDA of ₹22 cr. The separated, residual listed company is then being funded as an acquisition vehicle described as being "at a pivotal inflection point": ₹330.88 cr of 18-month warrants at ₹497 to promoter-group JK Investors (Bombay) Limited (29.83% to 35.91% diluted; ultimate beneficial owner Gautam Hari Singhania), then ₹214.71 cr at ₹645 to Minerva Ventures Fund (0% to ~4.31%, ultimate beneficial owner Ghanshyam Hurry), with 75% earmarked for Indian and international acquisitions in aerospace, automotive and defence including acquisition-debt repayment or bridge funding, and 25% for general corporate purposes, CARE Ratings appointed monitoring agency. Nobody outside is named as having demanded the separation: no investor campaign, lender covenant or JV partner appears in the texts; the visible external pressure is from the stock exchanges, whose queries forced a June 10, 2026 corrigendum and an October 22, 2026 restatement of the allottee's diluted stake. Running the other way, the January 27, 2026 Scheme folds wholly-owned Everblue Apparel Limited (denim garmenting, ₹10,858 lakh turnover) back into Raymond Limited "to enable simplified structure and greater management focus," so the pattern management describes is separation of the big consumer and property platforms combined with re-absorption of small subsidiaries into the listed engineering holdco.

**Point by point, with the source:**

1. The May 27, 2026 EGM explanatory statement states the separation is complete and defines what the residual listed company now is — an engineering holdco operating through JKMPTL and JKMGAL. “Raymond Limited ("Company") is a conglomerate which is in engineering businesses through its subsidiaries and has consistently invested in building world-class capabilities across its business verticals.” — *Scheme*
2. The same statement (repeated in the September 11, 2026 notice) gives the explicit cause-and-effect: because lifestyle and real estate were demerged, the remaining entity can pursue a focused multi-sector engineering strategy. “Having undertaken significant restructuring and demerger activities in recent years — including the demerger of its lifestyle and real estate business — Raymond is now positioned to pursue an accelerated, multi-sector growth strategy through its subsidiary companies, including:” — *Scheme*
3. Management frames the moment created by the separation as requiring fresh growth capital, which is what both warrant issues (₹330.88 cr to promoter group JK Investors (Bombay) Limited at ₹497, ₹214.71 cr to Minerva Ventures Fund at ₹645) are for. “The Company is at a pivotal inflection point in its growth trajectory.” — *Scheme*
4. The November 2, 2026 Q2/H1 FY2027 group update brands the outcome of the demergers as three single-focus listed platforms: Raymond Lifestyle Limited, Raymond Realty Limited and Raymond Limited (Aerospace & Defence plus Precision Technology and Auto Components). “Raymond Portfolio: Pure-Play Platforms” — *Scheme*
5. The same update argues the separation de-risks group earnings by removing reliance on the lifestyle business, quantifying the shift precisely for H1FY27. “Non-lifestyle verticals (Realty + Raymond Ltd.) now generate 49% of YTD EBITDA (388.16 of 792), signalling an increasingly balanced, multi-engine growth profile.” — *Scheme*
6. Governance is presented as a deliberate consequence of splitting into three listed vehicles — independent boards and independently chaired audit, pay, risk and related-party committees at each entity, above what LODR requires. “This is not a statutory response to LODR, LODR would allow far less. It is a deliberate architecture.” — *Scheme*
7. Each separated entity carries its own leverage, liquidity and rating profile: group net debt/EBITDA 0.43x, ₹1,738 cr TTM EBITDA, ₹3,382 cr cash, with Raymond Limited at -0.34x and Raymond Realty at 1.70x; the engineering arm reports a ₹77 cr net cash surplus. “Net cash surplus of Rs77 Cr as of September 2026, backing disciplined growth investments.” — *Scheme*
8. Who actually put up money: the promoter group, not an outside activist or lender — the May 2026 issue is justified on promoter conviction and on speed versus a QIP or rights issue, given time-sensitive acquisition targets. “Promoter participation in the fund-raise demonstrates a strong personal commitment and conviction in the Company's strategic direction.” — *Scheme*
9. The stock exchanges, not investors, forced disclosure changes: NSE (June 3, 2026) and BSE (June 8, 2026) queries produced a corrigendum that dropped subsidiary capex/capacity expansion and re-labelled 75% of proceeds as acquisition funding including acquisition-debt repayment. “Funding acquisitions of businesses in India and internationally across targeted growth sectors either directly or through subsidiary entities for organic and inorganic growth including repayment of acquisition debt upto 75% of the aggregate proceeds of the issue; and” — *Scheme*
10. Public institutions were not uniformly convinced by the promoter-funded step: 31.72% of institutional votes polled went against the June 18, 2026 resolution (overall 96.36% in favour), whereas the October 3, 2026 Minerva resolution passed with 99.9978% in favour and institutions 100% in favour. “Whether promoter/promoter group are interested in the agenda/resolution? Yes” — *Scheme*
11. Asked on June 24-25, 2026 about a reported German engineering acquisition (Deharde), the company neither confirmed a target nor denied the programme, pointing back to the stated objects of the warrant issue. “The Company evaluates various strategic opportunities in the ordinary course, for growth and expansion of its business.” — *Scheme*
12. The January 27, 2026 Scheme of Amalgamation runs opposite to the demergers — wholly-owned Everblue Apparel Limited (denim garmenting, ₹10,858 lakh turnover, ₹376 lakh net worth) is absorbed into Raymond Limited with no shares issued, purely for structural simplicity and focus. “To enable simplified structure and greater management focus on the business, it is imperative for the Transferor Company to be merged into the Transferee Company.” — *Scheme*
13. The amalgamation rationale lists the specific benefits claimed from collapsing subsidiaries into the listed holdco, the same vocabulary used for the larger separation. “Simplification of overall group structure by fully integrating EBAL's garmenting operations directly into RL and creating efficiencies through amalgamation;” — *Scheme*

**How it evolved:** The idea is presented as already settled rather than emerging: by the January 27, 2026 Everblue Apparel amalgamation filing, Raymond Limited is described as holding "investment in Engineering and Aerospace business through its subsidiaries" with standalone turnover of only ₹289 lakh, and the scheme's stated logic is simplification and management focus. The May 25, 2026 board outcome and May 27, 2026 EGM notice first spell out the post-separation rationale — demerger done, engineering holdco now raising ₹330.88 cr of promoter warrants for a multi-sector growth strategy through JKMPTL and JKMGAL — and that framing is repeated verbatim in the September 8/11, 2026 notice for the ₹214.71 cr Minerva Ventures Fund issue. The plan changed under exchange pressure: the June 10, 2026 corrigendum deleted "Capital expenditure and capacity expansion at subsidiary companies" and recast 75% of proceeds as acquisitions "including repayment of acquisition debt," the June 18, 2026 EGM passed with 31.72% of public-institution votes against, and the June 24-25, 2026 rumour verification on a German target (Deharde) was answered as speculative. By the November 2, 2026 Q2/H1 FY2027 group update the separation is marketed as three "Pure-Play Platforms" with per-entity boards, ratings and balance sheets, and the second raise (approved October 3, 2026 with 99.998% in favour) was granted exchange in-principle approval on November 6, 2026.

**What management expects from it:** Three focused listed platforms, each with its own board, ratings and balance sheet. Lifestyle: premiumisation, net cash of ₹69 cr. Realty: ~20% pre-sales growth, 17-19% EBITDA margin, ~20% ROCE, 9-10% PAT margin, debt/equity below 1.0x. Engineering Raymond Ltd.: aerospace scale-up at ~21% segment margins, operating leverage (PBT margin 6.4% vs 4.4%), and warrant capital for acquisitions without pre-funding dilution.

*Not explained on the calls:* No earnings-call transcripts were provided; filings never explain demerger mechanics, ratios, record dates, costs, who requested it, or why garmenting sits under the engineering holdco.
