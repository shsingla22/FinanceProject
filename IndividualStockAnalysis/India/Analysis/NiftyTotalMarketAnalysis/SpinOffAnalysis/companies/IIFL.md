### 5. Why the demerger — point by point

No rationale on file: no call passages and no scheme rationale text.

- *Stated reason, in one line:* Private equity investor ADIA in IIFL Home Finance needs an exit, and demerger is 'the most elegant way to get it listed separately'; demerger also releases parent capital adequacy, since subsidiaries are valued at cost.
- *What it reveals:* Subsidiaries are 'valued at cost' in the parent, a disadvantage for parent capital adequacy. IIFL Home Finance is over-capitalised and under-levered (CAR 41.7%, net gearing ~2.6-2.7x, single-digit ROE) while parent NBFC CAR is 17.1% and consolidated gearing 4.0x; Samasta CAR 24.9%.
- *Parent or spin:* unclear · *Read next:* Restated Articles clause 2.1(d) in the Dec 4, 2026 EGM notice - Blackstone's consent right over any demerger of material subsidiaries
- *In their words:* “No, even Samasta can get separated, so there will be demerger, there will be three entities.”
