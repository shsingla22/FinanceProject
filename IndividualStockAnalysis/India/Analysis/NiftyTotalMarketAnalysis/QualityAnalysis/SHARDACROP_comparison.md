### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 0 eased, 3 worsened, 2 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Every call frames results as an industry cycle turn — a destocking bust two years ago followed by 'revival in demand' and price recovery — with gross margins swinging from 29.9% (FY25) to 35.9% (FY26) and PAT up 124%, while the company simultaneously lifts registration capex to INR505 crores at the cycle peak; demand is also explicitly weather-dependent. Consumable crop-protection demand is a partial offset, but the price/inventory cycle clearly drives earnings. (The long view had said: Across all five calls the business is described in classic commodity-cycle terms: a 2023-24 glut of Chinese production crushed prices (some products trading at 25% of prior levels) and caused the FY24 collapse (PAT INR32cr) and a 2022 inventory write-off, followed by a violent recovery (FY25 PAT +854%, FY26 PAT +124%, Q4FY26 EBITDA margin 24.8% vs 15.8% FY25). Realisation, not volume, drives the swing — FY26 full-year price/mix was -1.8% with FX +10.3% — so current peak-looking margins are cycle- and currency-flattered rather than structurally earned.)
- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Sharda sells off-patent generic molecules through third-party distributors, and the numbers show it is a price-taker: price/product mix was -1.8% for FY26 and -3.0% in Q4, with the full-year revenue gain coming from volume (13.4%) and FX (10.3%) rather than pricing — analysts pressed on negative realization in both Q1 and Q2 and management deflected rather than defending pricing power. Margin expansion is input-cost and currency driven, not premium-driven. (The long view had said: Sharda sells off-patent molecules that are chemically identical to rivals' — it is itself the good-enough challenger to innovators, and is symmetrically exposed to cheaper Chinese generics, as the price collapse to a quarter of prior levels and the 2022 inventory write-off demonstrated; FY26 price/mix was still negative (-1.8%) even in a recovery year, and the Q2FY26 exchange where management deflected repeated questions on negative realisation was unconvincing. The protection is regulatory rather than brand-based, and end-farmers have no awareness of the Sharda name.)
- **Fashion Risk**: NO SIGNAL → LOW. Why now: Demand is described purely in functional, repeat-purchase terms — crop consumption and weather — across all four calls, with no growth attributed to a trending product, brand or moment. (The long view had said: No taste- or trend-driven demand element appears anywhere in the transcripts; the portfolio is functional herbicides, insecticides and fungicides bought on a seasonal repeat cycle.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (LOW); Technological Innovation (NO SIGNAL); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 8 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
