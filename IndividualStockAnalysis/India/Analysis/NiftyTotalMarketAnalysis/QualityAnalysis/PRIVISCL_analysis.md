# Privi Speciality Che (PRIVISCL) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Privi Speciality Chemicals is an Indian maker of aroma chemicals — the fragrance and flavour ingredients that go into perfumes, soaps, personal care and other everyday consumer products, which management describes as 'an integral part of human life.' It sells these ingredients in bulk to global fragrance and consumer-goods companies (Givaudan is both a customer-type partner and a joint-venture partner), earning money on the spread between raw material/energy costs and the price of the chemicals it manufactures, so profits swing with input costs, product mix and plant yields. Manufacturing is in India (plants at Mahad, head office in Navi Mumbai), with installed capacity moving from 48,000 to 54,000 tonnes a year, and roughly 65–69% of revenue comes from exports. FY26 revenue was about INR 2,583 crore with EBITDA margin around 25.8% and profit after tax of about INR 328 crore, on sales volume of roughly 42,400 tonnes.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Core aroma chemicals / flagship products** — The bread-and-butter bulk aroma chemical portfolio sold to global fragrance customers — this is the bulk of volumes (about 42,400 tonnes sold in FY26) and the products whose capacity is being expanded from 48,000 to 54,000 tonnes.
- **Speciality and value-added / downstream products** — Higher-priced, higher-margin specialty molecules and downstream derivatives that management is deliberately growing as a share of the mix; the calls credit this shift as a main reason EBITDA margins rose from around 20% to over 25%.
- **Galaxmusk, Prionyl and Camphor** — Three named newer products that absorbed significant capex over roughly three years and were ramped up from FY24 onward — Prionyl uses a proprietary in-house process and had its capacity raised again in early 2025, while the Camphor plant received FDA approval, opening it to pharmaceutical uses.
- **Prigiv — joint venture with Givaudan** — A 51:49 JV with the Swiss fragrance major Givaudan, set up on a greenfield site to make complex ingredients; it turned EBITDA-positive in Q3 FY26 and made its first net profit in Q4 FY26, with a further INR 50 crore of capex funded by both partners in the same 51:49 ratio.
- **Biotechnology / renewable feedstock R&D** — An early-stage effort, still at kilogram lab scale, to turn biomass and biowaste into value-added chemicals and to build new molecules from renewable feedstock — management presents it as future intellectual property rather than current revenue.
- **Group subsidiaries being merged in (Privi Fine Sciences, Privi Biotechnologies)** — Two group companies being amalgamated into the listed parent to simplify the structure; stock exchange no-objection was received and NCLT approval is expected in FY27.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
