### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 4 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 3. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 2 weakened, 6 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: Nothing in the last four calls addresses heritage, A&P spend, distributor leverage or brand stretch — the customer is a government procurement agency and the discussion is entirely product capability and order flow. (The long view had said: Reputation is real and cited — 30+ years, 'battle proven' after Op Sindoor leading to ~Rs.800cr of anti-drone orders, world-class claims validated at ITSEC — but this is institutional credibility with a single buyer class, with no A&P spend, distributor leverage or consumer heritage story of the kind the pattern describes.)

**That weakened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The Jul 2026 call splits the order book into equipment and AMC, showing a genuine post-sale annuity stream (~26% of the Rs.1,239 Cr book) on top of a differentiated front-end product; but no attach rates, renewal rates or installed-base density are quantified, and working capital is a use rather than a source of cash (257-day cycle, advances paid to suppliers), so customers are not funding the business. (The long view had said: Across the full arc management quantifies AMC as a distinct, compounding stream tied to installed base — Rs.35.9cr of FY23 standalone revenue, guided to grow as equipment leaves warranty at 8-11% of contract value, and by Q1 FY2027 disclosed as a separate Rs.318.4cr AMC order book alongside Rs.920.6cr equipment; customer advances are also discussed as cash funding (Rs.70cr unrecognised advances in FY23).)
- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Directional evidence of share capture exists — roughly Rs.800 Cr of post-Operation Sindoor anti-drone orders, Rs.931 Cr booked in four months to Jan 2026, and export enquiries from friendly countries — but management never cites a share number or names a competitor's position, so persistence cannot be verified from these calls. (The long view had said: Management repeatedly asserts leadership — 'number one in India' in anti-drone, dominance in army and naval simulation, expansion into Navy/Air Force (C295) and North America/EU — but never gives a share number rising over time, and FY2026 revenue actually fell 29.4% to Rs.687.7cr on delayed order inflow, so the claim is qualitative rather than demonstrated.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Innovation Dominance (STRONG FIT); Pricing Power (LIKELY FIT); Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Friendly Middleman (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
