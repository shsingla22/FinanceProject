### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 3 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Technological Innovation**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Across all four calls, the core categories — transformers, motors, switchgear, railway propulsion — are mature, standards-driven formats with no defensive discussion of a competing technology platform, and no analyst raised disruption; innovation is described as incremental cost/value engineering rather than a format shift the installed base must chase. (The long view had said: Core transformer/switchgear/motor technology evolves incrementally (400 kV GIS type-testing, AMX drives, IE3/IE4 motors, EV motors still in development), which is value-adding small-scale innovation; the real exposure is the company's own large capital bet into semiconductors — a INR7,584 crore OSAT project plus the Axiro RF design business — an industry with far higher innovation and obsolescence rates than its installed base of electrical equipment.)

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: The last four calls show a business riding a customer-capex upcycle: Power Systems sales rose 48%/44%/50% year-on-year through FY26 (full-year +46%) with PBIT margin expanding ~281bps to 21.9%, backlog +91%, driven by transmission/PSU and hyperscale data-centre orders — i.e. revenue tied to customers' capacity expansion, not their operations. Management is simultaneously adding capacity at the peak (INR748 crore greenfield switchgear expansion, INR7,584 crore CG Semi project, INR3,000 crore QIP), and commodity inflation that 'could not be fully passed on' has already compressed Industrial margins from 12.1% to 9.9% for FY26. (The long view had said: Revenue is anchored to customers' capex programmes — utility/PSU transmission capex, data-centre build-outs, railway rolling-stock tenders — and management is adding transformer capacity from ~17-20,000 MVA to ~110,000 MVA within roughly two years while peers do the same, with Power Systems margins running at cycle-peak levels (19% FY25 → 21.9% FY26, 23.8% in Q4) on 'improved price realization'; the Industrial side simultaneously shows the other leg of the cycle, with LT motor demand flat-to-negative for 4-5 quarters and repeated commodity-inflation margin hits (Industrial PBIT 12.1% → 9.9% FY26).)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 8 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
