### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 3 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 1. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 3 weakened, 5 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The four calls discuss only system/order revenue (HPC, Private Cloud, AI systems) with no attach rates, AMC/service revenue share, renewals or installed-base monetisation; customer advances on strategic orders are mentioned only as a receivables-accounting note, not as a recurring funding model. (The long view had said: Revenue is one-off hardware/system sales won on tenders; software and services was guided at only 2-4% of revenue (Aug 2023) and no attach rate, AMC share, renewal or deferred-revenue discussion appears in any of the 12 calls. Working capital is the opposite of customer-funded — cash conversion cycle of 140 days (Q1FY24), 88 days (Dec 2024), 84 days (Mar 2026) with receivable days of 86-114 and rising inventory.)

**That weakened:**

- **Innovation Dominance**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Management credits growth to sustained in-house R&D and named a new product (Skylus.ai, a composable GPU orchestration platform launched FY25) and commits to further R&D investment, but no R&D spend figure, pipeline detail or share-of-industry-R&D is disclosed, and gross/EBITDA margins (~14%) are too thin to fund outsized innovation spend. (The long view had said: Repeated, delivered product-led innovation across all 12 calls: Intel next-generation architecture licence and ARM/RISC systems plus an SMT line (Aug 2023), NVIDIA Blackwell GB200 roadmap (Jan 2025), and FY26 AI segment growth of 459.6% attributed explicitly to years of in-house R&D, with commitment to keep investing in R&D and manufacturing depth. The gap versus the pattern is that R&D spend is never quantified or disclosed as a rising line item.)
- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: FY26 revenue grew 90% with the AI segment up 459.6% and management repeatedly claiming the No.1 domestic position, which implies share gain in a fast-growing market — but no explicit share percentages, competitor comparisons or trend data are given, and the gains are order-driven and government-concentrated rather than demonstrably structural. (The long view had said: Growth has massively outpaced the market — 60% in 9M FY25 and 90% in FY26 to Rs 21,836 million, with AI rising from ~15% to 43.4% of revenue and management claiming largest-domestic-OEM status — and exports were built from zero to ~8-9% of revenue as guided. But no explicit market-share percentages or share-versus-named-competitor data are given in any call, so the share gain is inferred from growth rather than disclosed.)
- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: No heritage narrative, A&P spend, distributor leverage or brand-adjacency launches are discussed; the 'largest OEM'/'Make in India' framing is positioning and policy alignment rather than evidence of brand-driven premium pricing. (The long view had said: There is real institutional reputation — 'India's largest OEM in high-end computing solutions', 'India's only full-stack domestic provider', and a personal endorsement of its AI GPU systems by NVIDIA's CEO at the 2024 AI Summit — but this is B2B credentialing, not heritage brand power: no A&P spend, no premium pricing, no distributor leverage or adjacency launches under the name are discussed in any call.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Friendly Middleman (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Corporate Culture (STRONG FIT); Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 0 eased, 1 worsened, 4 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Revenue is capex-linked equipment (AI/HPC compute systems bought in customers' and government's infrastructure build-out), and the last four calls show violent lumpiness: Q3 FY26 revenue +165% Q-o-Q on a single Rs. 4,504mn strategic order, with EBITDA margin dropping to 12.2% from 15.0% the prior quarter, and management itself conceding quarterly results are unmeasurable. FY26 revenue +90% and AI segment +459.6% are boom-period numbers whose durability the calls cannot establish. (The long view had said: Revenue is explicitly tied to customer/government capex programmes — high-end compute is bought as infrastructure, not consumed in operations — and the resulting swings are extreme: -13% in Q1 FY24, then +141% year-on-year and +165% quarter-on-quarter in Q3 FY26 on one strategic order, with EBITDA margin sliding from ~15% to 12.2% as mix shifted. Management itself rides an AI capex wave it dates ('at least the next 18 to 24 months') and is already exposed to commodity memory/flash price spikes, the classic peak-cycle setup where neither the height nor duration is knowable.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Stakeholder Concentration (HIGH RISK); Technological Innovation (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
