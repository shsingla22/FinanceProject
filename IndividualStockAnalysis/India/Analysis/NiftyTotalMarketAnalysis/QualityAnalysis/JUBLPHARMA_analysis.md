# Jubilant Pharmo (JUBLPHARMA) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Jubilant Pharmova is an India-listed, diversified specialty pharmaceutical company that mostly makes its money in the United States and in US dollars, with roughly 5,000 people across India and North America. It sells radioactive imaging drugs and runs a network of 46 radiopharmacies that deliver them to hospitals, sells allergy treatment extracts to US allergy clinics, manufactures sterile injectable medicines under contract for large innovator drug companies, does contract drug-discovery research and makes active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) for other drug firms, and sells its own generic medicines in the US and other regulated markets. Revenue therefore comes from a mix of selling its own specialty products and being paid a fee to research or manufacture for other pharma companies. In FY2026 revenue was about Rs. 8,280 crore with EBITDA of Rs. 1,326 crore (a 15.9% margin), and it operates 6 FDA-regulated manufacturing plants (2 each in Canada, the US and India), research centres in Bengaluru, Noida and France, and the 46-site radiopharmacy network; it is also spending money to expand injectable capacity in Spokane (US) and Montreal (Canada).

**The parts of the business:**

- **Radiopharmaceuticals / Radiopharma (part of Specialty Pharmaceuticals)** — Makes and distributes radioactive drugs used to image the body — SPECT products (like MAA, DTPA, Sulfur Colloid, Mertiatide), PET products, the Ruby-Fill heart-imaging system and MIBG — and delivers them through the second-largest US network of 46 radiopharmacies; management calls it the largest segment at over 45% of company revenue, with target margins of 38–40%, though FY2026 was hurt by a shortage of SPECT cold-kit supply.
- **Allergy Immunotherapy (part of Specialty Pharmaceuticals)** — Sells allergenic extracts used to treat allergies in the US, where it is the second-largest player and the sole supplier of venom immunotherapy extracts; it has grown steadily and operates above pre-COVID volumes, and management wants to add adjacent new products to regain outright leadership.
- **CDMO Sterile Injectables (contract manufacturing)** — Fills and finishes injectable medicines to order for many of the world's largest innovator pharma companies from plants in Spokane (US) and Montreal (Canada); it was the biggest driver of FY2026 revenue growth, is doubling capacity with new isolator fill-finish lines, and has 10-plus products (about 80% complex biologics, including one of the world's largest oncology products) in tech transfer on Line 3, expected to reach $80–90 million of peak revenue.
- **CRDMO — Drug Discovery Services** — Provides end-to-end contract drug-discovery and development research to biotech and large pharma customers from research centres in Bengaluru, Noida and France, covering chemistry, DMPK, discovery biology and clinical trial data management; it grew 15% in FY2026 to more than Rs. 650 crore and management says demand is supported by the industry patent cliff.
- **CRDMO — CDMO-API / custom manufacturing** — Manufactures active pharmaceutical ingredients, increasingly as custom manufacturing for innovator clients rather than commodity supply; it runs at about a 15% EBITDA margin today with spare capacity, and management expects innovator custom-manufacturing revenue to start showing up in FY2027 and lift margins.
- **Generics** — Sells its own off-patent medicines across many therapy areas into the US and other regulated markets, plus a branded generics push in India; it was badly hit in FY2023 by US price erosion (notably sartans) and a US FDA import alert on the Roorkee plant, but management says the business has since been turned around and grew in FY2026.
- **Jubilant Therapeutics (Proprietary Novel Drugs)** — An in-house research effort developing its own new cancer and auto-immune drugs — such as JBI-802, a dual LSD1/HDAC6 inhibitor in Phase I/II for advanced solid tumours, and a brain-penetrant PRMT5 inhibitor — with two molecules in clinical trials; it is a long-term bet rather than a revenue contributor today.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
