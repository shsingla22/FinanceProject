### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 3 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 2 weakened, 8 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Friendly Middleman**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Uptake is prescriber- and formulary-mediated rather than end-consumer marketed — Q4 FY26 credits Yesintek's growth to 'expanding formulary coverage and increasing physician confidence' — but the pattern's low price sensitivity is absent: awards come via NHS regional tenders, the Spanish national tender and affordability programmes like CalRx, which are explicitly price-competitive. (The long view had said: Prescribers do matter ('increased pull-through at the physician level', ophthalmologists' comfort with a biosimilar), but the defining economics are absent — the paying middleman is the PBM/payer/tender authority and price sensitivity is extreme, with Viatris losing and regaining a customer purely on contracting.)

**That weakened:**

- **Brand Strength**: LIKELY FIT → NO FIT. Why now: Product brands (Yesintek, Ogivri, Fulphila, Yesafili, Bosaya) are regulatory/commercial labels for interchangeable biosimilars sold on affordability and formulary access; there is no A&P spend, heritage-premium or premium-pricing evidence in the FY26 calls. (The long view had said: There is heritage language ('extensive heritage and expertise in insulins', a track record of high quality since 2018 on adalimumab) and named product brands stretched across geographies, but no A&P spend, no premium pricing and no consumer pull — brand supports credibility with regulators and physicians, not price.)
- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The four FY26 calls discuss product launches, tenders and formulary wins but never attach rates, AMC/service revenue share, renewal rates or deferred revenue/customer advances as a funding source; the evidence is silent on a recurring post-sale stream. (The long view had said: the calls are silent and the numbers are mixed (1 of 3 checks supporting).)

*Unchanged: Market Share Gainers (STRONG FIT); Innovation Dominance (LIKELY FIT); Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 1 worsened, 3 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Strained on the long view, Strained on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: Several material revenue streams sit on single named counterparties — an Amgen licence is what permits US denosumab commercialisation, Civica is the sole route to the California insulin supply, Ajanta is the out-licensee for semaglutide across 26 countries, and much of the biosimilars franchise came bundled from the Viatris transaction — though no one relationship drives group results. (The long view had said: Concentration has shown up repeatedly and materially across the five years: FY23 advanced-market biosimilar economics ran entirely through Viatris ('Viatris did lose a customer' cut Trastuzumab share from 10-11% to 7-8%), US access hinges on three large PBMs, Hulio depended on a licence from FKB Japan, and Syngene's FY26 degrowth is attributed to one client.)
- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Biocon competes chiefly on affordability, and the buying decision sits with intermediaries — payers, PBMs, tender committees, formulary boards — not the patient, which is precisely the structure that erodes premiums; the CalRx arrangement supplying 'affordable insulin glargine' to a state government is a low-margin-by-design contract. (The long view had said: This is the structural risk: patients have no brand awareness and intermediaries — PBMs, formularies, tender committees and prescribers — pick the molecule, so management concedes continuous price erosion in biosimilars and repeatedly flags 'price erosion' and 'pricing pressure' in generics/API; the FY25 generics segment saw PBT margins collapse to 3% on exactly this.)

**That worsened:**

- **Government Dependency**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: Across all four calls, revenue is repeatedly won or lost through government-set channels — NHS regional awards, the Spanish national tender, Ontario public formulary funding, emerging-market tenders — and the company has entered a multi-year contract to supply insulin directly to the government of California under CalRx, explicitly on an affordability basis, while also invoking the Finance Minister's Biopharma Shakti initiative. (The long view had said: A recurring share of revenue is set by public buyers and policy — national/hospital tenders in the UK, Italy, APAC, MENA and LatAm, a multi-year contract with the government of California via Civica under CalRx, and Indian policy scaffolding (PLI, 'Biopharma Shakti') repeatedly invoked — while FDA/EMA/MHRA inspection outcomes gate supply; it is bounded by breadth rather than absent.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (WATCH); Technological Innovation (WATCH); New Entrants (WATCH).*

**Financial resilience:** Strained on the long view (1 of the 3 stress checks is flagged — one warning light, not yet a pattern.) vs Strained on the one-year view (1 of the 3 stress checks is flagged — one warning light, not yet a pattern.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
