# Sumitomo Chemi. (SUMICHEM) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Sumitomo Chemical India Limited (SCIL) makes and sells crop protection chemicals — insecticides, herbicides, fungicides and plant growth regulators — that Indian farmers spray on crops like rice, cotton, soybean, groundnut and chilli. It sells mostly in India (about 83% of revenue) through a network of distributors and dealers, backed by heavy grassroots demand-generation work with farmers, such as its 100-day 'Every Day Farmers' Day' campaign that reached over 5.5 lakh farmers via ~17,000 meetings; the rest of sales are exports to Japan, South America, the US, Europe, Africa, Southeast Asia and the Middle East, including shipments to affiliate Sumitomo companies. It makes money by selling branded formulations at a markup over input costs — branded formulations were 81% of domestic sales in FY26 — with gross margins around 42-43% and EBITDA margins of roughly 21-25%. Business is highly seasonal and weather-driven, tied to the Indian kharif and rabi cropping seasons, and revenue was around INR 1,987 crores in the first half of FY26 with FY26 profit after tax of INR 543 crores.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Insecticides** — Chemicals that kill crop-damaging pests; historically the single largest category at about 39-41% of total revenue, though management says consumption was hurt by the heavy monsoon rains in FY26.
- **Herbicides** — Weed-killers, the fastest-growing category at roughly 26-27% of revenue, driven by the soybean herbicide flumioxazin, the newly launched rice herbicide Lentigo and the flagship glyphosate brand Mera 71 — up 19% for FY26 and 87% in Q4.
- **Plant growth regulators (PGR)** — Products that influence how crops grow, around 10-11% of revenue; this category plus biologicals was held back for much of FY26 by regulatory constraints on biostimulants.
- **Fungicides** — Products that control crop diseases, about 9% of revenue, including Excalia Max for rice which management says kept growing strongly in speciality segments.
- **Biostimulants** — A smaller product line whose sales were blocked by regulatory approval delays through most of FY26; clearances came through in November-December 2025 and a new biostimulant called Top Gr[een] has since been registered.
- **Exports** — Sales outside India, which rose from about 11% to 17% of revenue in H1 FY25 and were roughly 17-19% in the periods discussed, going to Japan, South America (Brazil), the US, Europe, Africa (Kenya, Ethiopia) and new markets in Asia and the Middle East.
- **Environmental Health division** — Branded pest-control and urban vector-control solutions rather than farm chemicals — a small division that management says posted healthy growth off a low base in H1 FY26.
- **Barrix Agro Sciences (subsidiary)** — A subsidiary whose top line was broadly flat in H1 FY26, with profits squeezed by higher input costs and by the regulatory restrictions on biostimulant sales.
- **Animal nutrition distribution** — A distribution business mentioned only in passing; management says an interim reduction in its revenues was one reason headline FY26 growth was held to 3%.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
