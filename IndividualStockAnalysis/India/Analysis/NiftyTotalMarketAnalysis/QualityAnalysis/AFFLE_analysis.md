# Affle 3i (AFFLE) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Affle 3i (formerly Affle (India) Limited) is a listed Indian ad-tech company that runs an AI-powered "Consumer Platform Stack" used by advertisers to acquire and re-engage customers on mobile phones — mainly through on-device and in-app placements rather than browser ads. Its customers are advertisers and brands across verticals such as gaming, fintech, quick commerce, edtech, hospitality and travel, OTT and FMCG, with about 75% of revenue coming from direct customers. It earns money almost entirely on a results-linked basis: it charges per "converted user" (CPCU), so revenue equals the number of consumer conversions delivered multiplied by a rate per conversion — 120.3 million conversions at INR 60.0 each in Q4 FY2026. It operates across India and global emerging markets (Indonesia, Africa, LATAM), which contributed roughly 72-74% of revenue, and developed markets including the US and Europe at roughly 26-28%; full-year FY2026 revenue was INR 27.1 billion with EBITDA of INR 6.10 billion and PAT of INR 4.55 billion.

**The parts of the business:**

- **CPCU business (Cost Per Converted User)** — The core ROI-linked model where advertisers pay only for a completed consumer conversion — this is essentially the whole company, at INR 7.21 billion of the INR 7.24 billion Q4 FY2026 revenue (120.3 million conversions at an INR 60.0 rate), up from 61.9 million conversions at INR 52.1 in Q1 FY2023.
- **Non-CPCU business** — A small residual line management describes as the entry point for certain customers and partners for "top-of-the-funnel tech enablement" on the platform, explicitly designed to funnel those customers into the deeper CPCU model later.
- **India & Global Emerging Markets** — The company's anchor geography — India plus markets like Indonesia, Africa and LATAM — contributing 71.6-73.9% of revenue in recent quarters and growing about 19-21% y-o-y, where management says digital's share of total ad spend is still under-penetrated.
- **Developed Markets** — The US and other developed-market billing entities, contributing roughly 26-28% of revenue and growing 17-18% y-o-y recently (36.9% in Q1 FY2025); exposure to Europe was described as negligible in 2022, and a North America CEO was hired in FY2026 to accelerate this.
- **Consumer Platform Stack (Newton, Niko, OpticksAI)** — The underlying technology rather than a separate revenue line — Newton is the campaign platform, Niko is an agentic AI engine automating bidding, targeting and budget allocation in a self-service mode, and OpticksAI adds creative optimization; backed by an IP portfolio of 18 patents granted and 21 filed/pending covering fraud intelligence, precision targeting and AI-native ads.
- **Acquisitions / inorganic growth** — A recurring driver alongside organic growth — Jampp materially boosted FY2023 revenue and all acquired businesses were integrated into one cash-generating unit by Q1 FY2025; in May 2026 the board approved a ~INR 11 billion preferential warrant issue to promoter Affle Holdings to fund further deals.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*

**Capital Allocation — Good (+0.67)** · 6 of 6 checks had evidence
