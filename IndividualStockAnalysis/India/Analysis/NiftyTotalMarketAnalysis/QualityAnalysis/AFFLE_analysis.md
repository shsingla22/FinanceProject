# Affle 3i (AFFLE) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Affle 3i (formerly Affle (India) Limited) is a digital advertising technology company that runs what management calls an AI-powered "Consumer Platform Stack" — software that places ads inside mobile apps and on devices (as opposed to browser/cookie-based web ads) and uses its own data, algorithms and patented targeting to turn those ad impressions into actual users who install, sign up or buy. Its customers are advertisers and brands across verticals such as gaming, fintech, quick commerce, edtech, hospitality and travel, OTT, FMCG and D2C retail, with about 75% of revenue coming from direct customers. It is paid almost entirely on an ROI-linked "CPCU" (cost per converted user) basis — the advertiser pays a fixed rate per consumer conversion delivered, so revenue is essentially conversions multiplied by the CPCU rate (Q4 FY2026: 120.3 million conversions at INR 60.0 each), and its main cost is buying ad inventory and data, at roughly 61-63% of revenue. It is anchored in India and other global emerging markets (~72-74% of revenue) with the rest from developed markets like the US, and delivered FY2026 revenue of INR 27.1 billion, EBITDA of INR 6.1 billion and PAT of INR 4.55 billion.

**The parts of the business:**

- **CPCU business (Cost Per Converted User)** — The core, ROI-linked model where advertisers pay per consumer conversion delivered rather than per impression — it is effectively the whole company, with CPCU revenue of INR 7.21 billion out of INR 7.24 billion total revenue in Q4 FY2026, growing at ~20% y-o-y.
- **Non-CPCU business** — A small remainder that management describes as an entry point for certain customers and partners doing "top-of-the-funnel" tech enablement on the platform, intended to pull them into the deeper-funnel CPCU model later.
- **India & Global Emerging Markets** — The company's anchor geography — India, Indonesia, Africa, LATAM and similar markets where digital's share of total ad spend is still low — contributing 71.6%-73.9% of revenue in recent quarters and growing around 20% y-o-y.
- **Developed Markets** — The US and other developed-market entities, contributing roughly 26-28% of revenue and growing 17-37% y-o-y in the quarters discussed, with a dedicated North America CEO hired in FY2026 to accelerate it.
- **Consumer Platform Stack (incl. Newton, Niko, OpticksAI)** — The unified technology layer behind the revenue — Newton is the campaign platform, Niko is an agentic AI engine that automates bidding, targeting and budget decisions in a self-service mode, and OpticksAI adds creative optimization; the stack is backed by an IP portfolio of 18 granted and 21 pending patents covering fraud detection, precision targeting and AI-native ads.
- **Inorganic growth / acquisitions** — An ongoing strategy of buying and integrating ad-tech businesses (e.g. Jampp, consolidated into a single cash-generating unit by FY2025), with the Board approving an ~INR 11 billion warrant issue to promoter Affle Holdings in FY2026 to fund further deals.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
