**Checks that improved:**

- **Network effects** (Competitive Advantage): Poor → Weak. Now: Nothing in the business model — commodity metals sold to buyers and power sold to exchanges and DISCOMs — creates any self-reinforcing network. | The long view had said: The business is a vertically integrated commodity producer selling coal, steel, ferro alloys and electricity into open markets and PPAs — there is no two-sided platform or any effect that strengthens with user scale.
- **Profit margin** (Return on Capital): Good → Excellent. Now: Out of every ₹100 of sales, ₹31 is operating profit. Margins have moved about ±2.8 points a year — very steady, a sign costs are under control. | The long view had said: Out of every ₹100 of sales, ₹31 is operating profit. Margins have moved about ±6.4 points a year — sizeable swings, suggesting key costs are outside management's control.

**Checks that regressed:**

- **Share donators** (Industry Structure): Good → Weak. Now: Rather than weak rivals to take share from, competition is strengthening — large primary steel makers adding capacity and Chinese exports rising 7.5% to ~119 million tons. | The long view had said: The company has repeatedly acquired assets from failed or weak operators — SKS Power out of insolvency, a JV to reopen SECL's Bartunga Hill mine — and continues to shop the NCLT pipeline.
- **Distribution advantage** (Competitive Advantage): Neutral → Weak. Now: The advantage described is vertical integration into captive coal, iron ore and power, not a distribution or service network rivals cannot match. | The long view had said: Logistics investment is real but defensive rather than rival-beating — a railway siding at the coal mine, a jetty in Indonesia, grid connectivity for solar — serving own cost efficiency rather than an unmatched service reach.
- **Independent, long-term and tenacious** (Management): Excellent → Good. Now: Long-gestation commitments — a 40-year PPA for Rehar, coal blocks with production deadlines out to 2033, and the independent decision to terminate a 113MW long-term PPA to sell merchant — show long-horizon, independent execution. | The long view had said: Multi-year tenacity is well evidenced: the SKS resolution was pursued through NCLT, NCLAT and the Supreme Court from 2023 to 2026, while Rehar hydro was delivered ahead of its March 2025 schedule and coal-mine approvals have been ground out block by block over five years.
- **Cyclical market growth** (Growth): Excellent → Good. Now: Steel prices hit multi-year lows before recovering 10-15% in December and India turned net exporter after safeguard duties, with power prices also turning up — a visible cycle bottom with an identifiable upturn. | The long view had said: Management identified the cycle bottom in steel and power and the subsequent turn is now visible — steel prices up 10-15% off multi-year lows, safeguard duties turning India a net exporter, and power tariffs expected to rise into summer.

*Unchanged: Cost of working capital; Return on capital (headline); Customer types (retail vs corporate); Barriers to entry and rationality; Halo effect (caution); Merger and acquisition; Disciplined stewards; Intangible benefits; Asset turn; Mini-monopolies; Pricing, Mix and Volume; Out of limelight; Candor; Oligopoly (vs duopoly); Structural end-market growth; Capex for growth (not maintenance); Assurance benefits; Technology advantage; Gain in market share; Geographical expansion; Partial monopoly / broken competition / switching costs; Security by obscurity.*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 8 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
