# FSN E-Commerce (NYKAA) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

FSN E-Commerce Ventures, which trades under the brand name Nykaa, is an Indian retailer that sells beauty and personal care products and fashion/apparel to Indian consumers, mainly through its own websites and apps (Nykaa.com, Nykaa Fashion, Nykaa Man) and through its own chain of physical stores — 276 stores across 94 cities as of the December 2025 quarter. It makes money chiefly by buying stock from more than 6,000 global and domestic brand partners and reselling it at a mark-up, plus selling its own in-house brands at higher margins, distributing products wholesale to small retailers, and running e-commerce/store operations on behalf of outside brands such as Nike and Kiehl's. It operates almost entirely in India — 44+ warehouses reaching 98% of pincodes — with a smaller Gulf (GCC) platform called Nysaa and some export of its own brands. In FY26 it crossed INR 10,000 crores (about $1 billion) of net revenue, up 26%, with an EBITDA margin of 7.5% and a small net profit of INR 204 crores (2% of revenue).

**The parts of the business:**

- **Beauty (Beauty Multi Brand Retail / omni-channel beauty)** — The core business: reselling makeup, skincare, haircare and fragrance from thousands of third-party brands online and in Nykaa's own stores — roughly INR 15,000 crores of GMV in FY26, about 27% growth, and the only clearly profitable vertical at a 9.6% EBITDA margin for the year.
- **Nykaa Fashion (Nykaafashion.com)** — The clothing and accessories platform, with about 6,000 brands including Nike and H&M serving roughly 11 million customers; it is growing fast (GMV up ~31% in the December 2025 quarter) but still loses money, though losses narrowed to about -2.0% of net revenue that quarter from -5.4% a year earlier.
- **House of Nykaa Brands (own brands / House of Brands)** — Nykaa's own-label products across beauty and fashion — more than 12 significant brands led by Dot & Key, Kay Beauty, Nykaa Cosmetics, Nykd and Twenty Dresses — together about $400 million of annualised GMV and 16 million customers served, and management calls out both strong growth and margin expansion here.
- **Superstore by Nykaa (eB2B distribution)** — A wholesale app that supplies beauty products from 200+ brands to small shops — general trade stores, beauty outlets, pharmacies and salons — reaching about 3.8 lakh (380,000) transacting retailers across roughly 1,000-1,100 cities, and now contributing positively to beauty's profitability after earlier losses.
- **Nykaa Man** — A separate platform selling men's grooming and personal care products; described as one of Nykaa's four consumer platforms but never sized separately in these calls.
- **Nysaa (GCC platform)** — The company's Gulf-region business, added around FY24, described only as a growing presence in the GCC and as the fourth of Nykaa's consumer platforms.
- **B2B2C brand partnerships and import/distribution** — Running other companies' India businesses end-to-end — Nike's and Foot Locker's direct-to-consumer operations and Kiehl's entire India business (website, stores and marketplace selling) — plus acting as exclusive import and distribution partner for more than 40 international brands.
- **Physical retail store formats** — Company-run store chains under formats called Nykaa Luxe, Nykaa On Trend, Nykaa Kiosk (for own labels) and the newer Nykaa Perfumery, totalling 276 stores in 94 cities and described as one of the largest beauty store networks in India.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
