*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That weakened:**

- **Pricing Power**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Revenue grew 11-14% while the paying supplier base fell from 2.22 lakh to 2.18 lakh across the window, so realisation per customer is clearly rising in the sticky Gold/Platinum tiers; but the one explicit price increase disclosed (Silver tier, end of Q2 FY26) cost volume for at least three consecutive quarters, which is the opposite of pricing power without volume loss. Why now: No market-share figures or named competitors appear in any of the four calls; BUSY is gaining scale fast (45,000 new licenses in FY26, normalised billing growth 24-57%), while the core marketplace shed paying suppliers in three of the four quarters and enquiries declined from 31mn to 26mn. (The long view had said: IndiaMART is the clear category leader — ~50 lakh GST-registered businesses on the platform out of 1.6 crore, roughly 50% of its serviceable market, and management argues it out-discovers Moglix/Udaan/ONDC-type rivals — but explicit share numbers are absent and the paying-supplier base has now been shrinking, so recent evidence is of consolidation, not gain.)

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 3 eased, 2 worsened, 1 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Core revenue is an annuity-like subscription (deferred revenue Rs. 2,014 crores, +16% y-o-y) tied to SME operating spend rather than customer capex, but the last 12 months show real macro sensitivity: management blamed muted Q4 gross adds on war-driven market pain, and treasury mark-to-market swings whipsawed other income from +Rs. 135 crores (Q3) to -Rs. 34 crores (Q4) to +Rs. 107 crores (Q1). (The long view had said: Revenue is annuity-like subscription with deferred revenue of ~Rs 2,014 crores providing visibility, but the calls repeatedly tie SME demand and gross additions to macro conditions — commodity price spikes in FY23, COVID-category demand 'weaning away', and the war in Feb-Mar 2026 costing an estimated 1,000-1,500 customers in one quarter.)
- **Technological Innovation**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Management devoted opening remarks in all four calls to AI adoption — agentic call handling, standardised cataloguing, intelligent matchmaking — while the platform's core asset, buyer enquiry volume, fell every quarter of the window (31mn → 28mn → 27mn → 26mn), the pattern you would expect if AI-native discovery starts substituting for a directory marketplace. (The long view had said: The entire buyer funnel rests on search-engine discovery, and by FY26-FY27 management concedes Google is 'under pressure from ChatGPT', that agentic/bot traffic has made traffic unreportable, and that AI parsing is forcing OTP walls; unique business enquiries have been stuck at 26-27 million for roughly three years while analysts repeatedly ask about LLM disruption of discovery.)
- **Stakeholder Concentration**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Revenue is spread across roughly 2 lakh 18 thousand paying suppliers with no named customer, licensor or supplier discussed in any of the four calls; the only concentration disclosed is tier-level, with Platinum and Gold at ~50% of the base and >75% of revenue, and management reports those tiers retaining well. (The long view had said: The 2.2 lakh paying supplier base is highly fragmented with no named customer, but revenue is skewed — Gold/Platinum are ~50% of customers and over 75% of revenue, with the top 10% doing ~50% of revenue — and buyer acquisition has been effectively single-channel dependent on Google, which management admits is now cannibalising its own results.)

**That worsened:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: Across three consecutive calls management attributed falling paying-supplier counts to a Silver-tier price increase, the classic good-enough signature — the price-sensitive tail of SMEs walks when the premium is raised, presumably toward free or cheaper lead sources — with Silver monthly churn at ~7% and the paying base declining from 2.22 lakh to 2.18 lakh over the window even as margins were flattered by not spending to acquire. (The long view had said: The low end of the product is clearly commoditised and price-elastic — Silver monthly churn runs ~7% a month versus <1% for Platinum, an FY26 Silver price increase directly moderated gross adds by an estimated 1,000-1,500 customers, and after four years management still cannot explain the structural silver churn; the value-added tiers, however, remain defensible.)
- **Fashion Risk**: NO SIGNAL → LOW. Why now: Demand is functional and repeat-purchase: suppliers renew annual subscriptions to obtain convertible leads, with Platinum monthly churn well under 1% and Gold at 1-1.5%; nothing in the four calls attributes growth to a trending product, brand moment or taste-driven demand. (The long view had said: the judge read the calls and found nothing, and all 2 computed fingerprints are clean.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH); Government Dependency (LOW).*

*Unchanged: New Entrants (WATCH).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 2 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 2 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 8 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
