### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 0 worsened, 4 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Technological Innovation**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Gen-AI is raised in every call from Apr 2024 onward and directly touches the core product — human analyst hours turned into research, credit assessment and benchmarking output — so the installed delivery model is exposed even though management frames AI as an adopted tool rather than a threat. Nothing in the recent calls shows a competitor's platform displacing them, and the innovation cadence described is incremental and value-adding. (The long view had said: Management itself elevated Gen-AI to a front-and-centre risk in Apr 2026 ('emergence of enhanced Gen-AI models'), and the first analyst question asked explicitly about AI threats to each business — a research/analytics/offshoring franchise where the product is human analyst output sits directly in the path of a large-scale technology shift, not a small-scale one.)
- **Government Dependency**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Government and regulation appear in the recent calls only as demand drivers (client regulatory-compliance spend, RBI's supportive measures for bank credit) and CSR work, never as the setter of Crisil's own pricing or revenue; the business is asset-light analytics sold to a globally diversified private client base rather than anchored in fixed infrastructure or state tenders. (The long view had said: Ratings is a SEBI-licensed activity whose volumes swing on regulatory action — RBI's NBFC risk-weight hike cut bank credit growth from 16.6% to 11%, and the April 2025 rollback was cited as the reason for the expected recovery — while PLI schemes, government capex and government/multilateral consulting are recurring demand drivers, and SEBI mandated ESG ratings through licensed CRAs. Pricing, however, is not regulator-set and the business is not anchored in fixed infrastructure.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Fashion Risk (LOW); New Entrants (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Stakeholder Concentration (HIGH RISK); Cyclicality (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 6 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
