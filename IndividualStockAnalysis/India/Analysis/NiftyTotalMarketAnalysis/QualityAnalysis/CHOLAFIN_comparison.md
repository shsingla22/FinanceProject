### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 2 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 1. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 0 strengthened, 2 weakened, 4 unchanged; 4 carried forward unchanged from the long view; 1 could not be compared.

*Foundation test: FAIL on the long view, FAIL on the one-year view.*

**That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: AUM compounded 20–21% and Q4 disbursements grew 25% year-on-year, well above system credit growth, with broad-based gains across vehicle, LAP, SME and SBPL — but management never cites explicit market-share numbers or comparisons against named competitors, and part of the growth is offset by deliberate exits (fintech partnerships, supply-chain finance). (The long view had said: AUM compounds from INR86,703 crores (Q1 FY23) to INR2,42,630 crores (Q4 FY26) with explicit share gains named against industry growth, and — importantly — share is given up where underwriting deteriorates (SCV cut in FY24, fintech and supply-chain finance exits), which is the quality version of this pattern.)

Why now: Every margin gain in the window is attributed to falling cost of funds after RBI rate cuts, not to raising prices; management is a rate-taker whose lending yields follow the cycle, and growth in Q3/Q4 was credited to GST-driven vehicle price cuts stimulating demand. (The long view had said: Chola defends yield in niches banks avoid (SRTO, small commercial vehicle, gold loan at ~15%), but margin movement across FY26 is explained by falling cost of funds rather than price increases, and in FY23 vehicle yields fell ~50bps on competition — pricing is segment-selection, not demonstrated pricing power.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (NO FIT); Toll Roads (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Corporate Culture (STRONG FIT); Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 0 worsened, 3 unchanged; 4 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Unknown on the long view, Unknown on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: Roughly half the book is vehicle finance whose credit costs and demand management repeatedly ties to seasonality, monsoon, freight capacity utilisation and CV/PV OEM volumes; the Q4 FY26 call added a INR200 crore precautionary overlay explicitly for crude, refined fuel, LPG and global shipping/commodity volatility, showing earnings sensitivity to external cycles. Bounded rather than high because the lending is multi-product (vehicle, LAP, home, SME, CSEL, gold) and largely funds operating assets of small road transport operators rather than a single capex programme. (The long view had said: Across all 14 calls, demand and credit cost are explained in cyclical terms — freight availability, capacity utilisation, IIP, monsoon, crude prices and vehicle replacement cycles — and vehicle finance funds the customer's own capex (trucks, tractors, construction equipment), so NCL swung from ~1.2% pre-pandemic to 2.2% in FY26 Q1 before easing, with ROA compressing from 3.7% (Aug 2022) to 3.2% (FY25/FY26 Q3) and only recovering to 4.1% once the cycle turned; the May 2026 INR200 crore overlay for crude/LPG/shipping volatility confirms management itself sees macro-cycle second-order risk.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW); Stakeholder Concentration (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 11 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
