### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 9 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 5. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 0 strengthened, 4 weakened, 5 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That weakened:**

- **Toll Roads**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Structurally a rational duopoly supplying a mission-critical, low-cost-share service — registrar/transfer agency, and management describes orchestrating client transitions in and rarely out — but the pattern's 'customers pay without negotiating' test is contradicted by the April 2025 pricing discounts and the telescopic yield compression discussed in Q4 FY26. (The long view had said: KFin is one of only two meaningful mutual-fund RTAs and one of three licensed CRAs for NPS — a regulator-anchored oligopoly whose fee is a few basis points of an AMC's cost base yet is mission-critical record-keeping, so transitions take 'two to three quarters' and client losses are rare enough to be named individually (Navi, Feb-2023). Management explicitly frames wins as licence/mandate-based and regulatory-anchored across all 17 calls.)
- **Innovation Dominance**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Management claims a quarterly cadence of new product deployment and credits technology for pension share gains, but no R&D spend, share of industry R&D, or revenue attribution to named launches is disclosed on any of the four calls — and the innovation has not translated into pricing power. (The long view had said: Named proprietary platforms arrive nearly every year with attributable revenue — Hexagram/mPower fund accounting, XAlt for alternatives, data lakes (eight contracts by Jan-2025), mPower Wealth (five mandates within three months of October-2024 launch), India's first gig-economy pension platform — and management ties win rates directly to them. Absolute R&D spend and share-of-industry R&D are never disclosed, which is the only gap.)
- **Corporate Culture**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Leadership is stable (Nadella/Mathur across all four calls) and the diversification promise made four years ago has visibly been delivered — non-domestic-MF revenue now near 40% — and management discloses bad news plainly (flat PAT, yield compression, Issuer Solutions base effect, margin dilution from Ascent). Against that, EBITDA/PAT growth of 5-7% has lagged the repeated 20%+ top-line framing, and the one-year lens does not let me test through-cycle capital allocation. (The long view had said: Same CEO (Sreekanth Nadella) and CFO (Vivek Mathur) through all 17 calls; the FY2021-stated strategy of getting non-MF revenue toward 50% was tracked publicly and reached ~40% by Jul-2026, and the NPS breakeven promised for years was delivered and then sustained at ~15% EBITDA. Bad news is disclosed promptly and specifically — equity AUM share erosion, margin compression from Ascent, tepid Issuer Solutions, folio erosion — alongside a net-cash balance sheet.)
- **Brand Strength**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: There is clear institutional reputation — 60% win rate on new AMC mandates, ~80% by market cap of the quarter's IPOs, and reputation-adjacent proof points like the ESG score rise to 63 and Bitsight 810 — but no heritage story, A&P spend or premium pricing, and the brand demonstrably does not command price. (The long view had said: Heritage since 1985 and reputation clearly win mandates — 80% of Q1 FY27 main-board IPOs by market cap, admission to BlackRock's nine-firm global Aladdin administrator list, and 'right to win' language in international bids — but this is B2B institutional credibility with no A&P spend, distributor leverage, or consumer brand stretch.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (QUANT SIGNAL); Forward Integrators (QUANT SIGNAL).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 8 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
