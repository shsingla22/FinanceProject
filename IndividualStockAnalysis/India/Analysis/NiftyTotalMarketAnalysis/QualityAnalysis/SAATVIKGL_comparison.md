### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 4 unchanged; 2 could not be compared. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Technological Innovation**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: Management explicitly flags an industry-wide shift toward high-efficiency technologies while the installed base still spans Mono PERC and N-TOPCon, and the company is now sinking capital into a fixed cell/ingot/wafer technology choice (6 GW cell, 6 GW ingot-wafer) whose payback runs years beyond the current cell generation. The calls contain no discussion of next-generation formats or how the new lines would be re-tooled, so the exposure is real but not shown to be acute within the window. (The long view had said: Management itself describes a 'tectonic technology change' in which Multi/Mono/Mono PERC lines go obsolete within a year — including ~12GW of India's 26GW installed cell base — and notes generations turn over even within TOPCon (M10 to M10R to G12R), so the INR1,850cr greenfield cell/module plant is being sunk into a format with a short shelf life.)

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Modules are sold into customers' capex programmes (IPPs, EPC players, utility-scale projects), and the last four calls show exactly the volatility that implies: EBITDA margin fell from 19.8% (Q1FY26) to 16.08% (Q2) to 13.11% (Q3) on monsoon execution delays, customer project-schedule slippage and GST-driven deferment, while the company is simultaneously committing large capital to Odisha modules, 6 GW cells, ingot/wafer and 5 GW encapsulant. Management itself frames pricing in commodity terms (silver, aluminium, copper) and pre-emptively denies cyclicality, which is a tell rather than a mitigant. (The long view had said: Revenue is entirely tied to solar developers' capex programmes and to commodity-driven module pricing; across the four calls EBITDA margin fell from 19.8% (Q1FY26) to 13.1% (Q3) to ~7% in Q4FY26 as silver, aluminium, copper, oil and the rupee moved, while the company simultaneously commits INR1,850cr (Odisha) plus ~INR1,700cr FY27 and ~INR2,000cr FY28 capex into the upswing — the classic expansionary over-investment pattern.)

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); New Entrants (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH).*

*Not comparable: Shifting Consumer Preferences (NOT ASSESSED → NOT ASSESSED — could not be assessed on either view); Fashion Risk (NOT ASSESSED → NOT ASSESSED — could not be assessed on either view).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 27 items carried from the full-history analysis, judge_failed: claude CLI failed: , with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
