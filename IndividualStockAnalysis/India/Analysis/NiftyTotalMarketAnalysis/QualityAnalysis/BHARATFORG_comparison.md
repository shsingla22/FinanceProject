### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 0 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 4. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 5 strengthened, 1 weakened, 3 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: FAIL on the long view, FAIL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Pricing Power**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Tariff costs are contractually recovered in price ('compensated in price') and standalone EBITDA margin held at ~27-28% through a year when North American CV revenue fell ~51% year-on-year, but this is cost pass-through on locked-in programmes rather than above-inflation price increases, and the company still absorbed INR 14-31 crores of tariff per quarter. (The long view had said: Standalone margins are high and were defended through steel, freight and energy inflation (27-28% held FY25-FY26), but pricing is cost-pass-through negotiated with OEMs — years of 'price repair' in Europe and absorbed tariff costs show no ability to raise price above inflation at will.)
- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: No heritage/A&P/consumer-brand discussion in these calls; reputation is referenced only implicitly through OEM approvals. (The long view had said: the calls are silent and the numbers are mixed (1 of 2 checks supporting).)
- **Innovation Dominance**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Repeated references to new products and programmes — aerospace jet engine structures and landing systems, drones/unmanned sea and air platforms with in-house payload capability — but no R&D spend disclosure, no share-of-industry-R&D, and gross margins are not innovation-funded at high-margin levels. (The long view had said: Consistent new-product record — nine artillery platforms, ATAGS with DRDO, CQB carbine, naval/unmanned systems, aluminium light-weighting, aerospace jet-engine structures and landing systems — but R&D spend is never disclosed or quantified, and the e-mobility/Tork bet was written off (Aug 2024 impairment, FY26 write-off).)
- **Corporate Culture**: PARTIAL → STRONG FIT. Why now: Across the four calls management flagged bad news early and repeatedly (European steel restructuring, EV/e-mobility write-off, 'weak spots in our overall business portfolio'), guided honestly to a weak Q2/Q3 in Aug/Nov and then delivered the promised Q4 recovery, kept net debt-to-equity at 0.18x standalone, and the JSA acquisition made three years ago was marked at 3.5-4x cost via the Premji Invest stake. (The long view had said: Long-tenured family leadership (Amit Kalyani and Subodh Tandale on all 16 calls), a conservative balance sheet through the cycle (net debt/equity 0.2 in 2022, 0.15-0.18 in FY26, first equity raise since 2010-11), ROCE explicitly targeted, and bad news disclosed promptly (Tork impairment, EV write-off, CDP liquidation); offset by promises that slipped badly — the US plant was to be profitable by FY24 and Europe 'breakeven by Q4' FY25, yet overseas losses persisted into FY26 and the German steel unit is now being wound up.)
- **Toll Roads**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Management stresses the components are critical to customers and that requalification takes years, which is why customers absorbed tariff costs rather than re-sourcing; but there is no explicit 'tiny share of customer cost' or certification-standard framing, and pricing is clearly cyclical rather than toll-like. (The long view had said: Forgings are a material, negotiated share of OEM cost: the Aug 2024 and FY26 calls describe having to seek 'price repair' from European customers over years and absorbing tariff (Rs.31cr in Q3FY26, Rs.12cr in Q4) and energy cost hits rather than passing them on unnegotiated.)

**That weakened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: No discussion of attach rates, AMC/service revenue, renewals, installed base or customer advances anywhere in the four FY26 calls; revenue is discussed purely as component/defence order execution. (The long view had said: Defence spares/consumables (Rs.300-400cr/yr in Aug 2022) and MRO were repeatedly cited as an annuity tail behind capital equipment sales, and by Feb 2026 management framed MRO as a structural ROCE booster; but there is no discussion of attach rates, renewal rates, deferred revenue or customer advances funding the business.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (NO FIT); Forward Integrators (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
