### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 0 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 0. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 1 weakened, 2 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view; 5 could not be compared.

*Foundation test: FAIL on the long view, FAIL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Cost to Replicate**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The one hard-to-replicate asset visible in these calls is the low-cost deposit franchise — CASA at 56% with cost of deposits of 3.44% is a decades-built branch and customer base a funded entrant could not assemble quickly. But management never articulates a replication barrier itself, and the asset side (legacy NPAs, PCA exit only in Mar 2021) offers no such defence. (The long view had said: Nothing described is un-replicable — the digital footprint (UPI, mobile app) is industry table-stakes, the corporate relationships were lost during PCA and are being rebuilt, and the equity story hinges on a government/LIC disinvestment process management cannot even comment on. No articulable reason a funded competitor could not build the same franchise.)

**That weakened:**

- **Pricing Power**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Pricing in these two calls is explicitly market- and policy-driven, not company-driven: management attributes the 80bps yield fall in Q1 to repo/policy rates, and in Q2 says it deliberately follows rather than leads on deposit pricing. NIM expansion comes from repricing lag, not from raising prices above cost inflation. (The long view had said: NIM expanded from 3.26% to 4.22% (ex-IT refund) as loan repricing ran ahead of deposit repricing, and management deliberately lagged the market on deposit rates — a form of conditional pricing power from the CASA base. But this is rate-cycle transmission, not price rises above cost inflation; in the Jul 2022 call yield on advances had actually fallen 80bps with the policy cycle.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (NO FIT); Market Share Gainers (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL).*

*Not comparable: Friendly Middleman (NOT ASSESSED → NOT ASSESSED — could not be assessed on either view); Toll Roads (NOT ASSESSED → NOT ASSESSED — could not be assessed on either view); Brand Strength (NOT ASSESSED → NOT ASSESSED — could not be assessed on either view); Innovation Dominance (NOT ASSESSED → NO FIT — could not be assessed on the long view); Forward Integrators (NOT ASSESSED → NOT ASSESSED — could not be assessed on either view).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
