### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 6 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 4. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 3 weakened, 6 unchanged; 1 could not be compared.

*Foundation test: UNKNOWN on the long view, UNKNOWN on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The four calls describe one-off B2B shipments of specialty fats and butters; there is no discussion of licences, AMCs, subscriptions, renewal rates, attach rates or deferred revenue/customer advances as a funding source. (The long view had said: Across all six calls the model is spot/contract B2B sales of fats — no licences, AMCs, subscriptions, attach rates or renewal metrics — and the business consumes working capital rather than being funded by customers (5-6 month cycle, 151→125 days, INR 710 crore inventory), the opposite of billed-in-advance negative working capital.)

**That weakened:**

- **Toll Roads**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Manorama supplies a niche, customised specialty ingredient into a market it calls structurally undersupplied, backed by a long list of qualification certifications (FSSC 22000, RSPO, Kosher, Halal, EcoVadis, Sedex) that create switching friction — but no call quantifies the ingredient as a tiny share of customer cost, and CBE is a material input cost for a chocolate maker, so the classic toll-road economics are only partly evidenced. (The long view had said: CBE/specialty fats are a small but functionally critical ingredient for Mondelez, Ferrero, Nestle and Hershey — management repeatedly stresses the structural/functional properties customers need, a 2-3 year customer qualification cycle, an undersupplied niche with little new global capacity, and a wall of certifications (FSSC 22000, RSPO, Kosher, Halal, Fairtrade, EcoVadis, Sedex) that anchor supplier status.)
- **Cost to Replicate**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Management articulates a concrete replication barrier — a wild-seed sourcing network built on rural/tribal collection plus a Burkina Faso processing plant, supercritical fractionation technology and multi-year customer qualification — but the calls assert the moat more than they evidence why a well-funded incumbent like the large global CBE players could not rebuild it. (The long view had said: The articulable barrier is concrete and time-based: a multi-decade collection network of tribal gatherers across thousands of forest collection centres in India and West Africa, integrated extraction-refining-fractionation at one site, a 2-3 year customer qualification cycle with Fortune 500 chocolate makers, and being the only player carrying all these exotic fats in one basket — a well-funded copycat cannot assemble the seed supply chain and customer approvals quickly.)
- **Brand Strength**: LIKELY FIT → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: As a B2B ingredient maker the calls contain no heritage story, A&P spend, distributor leverage or consumer-brand adjacency discussion; the trust language is customer-relationship and certification based rather than brand based. (The long view had said: There is B2B reputational capital — 100+ awards, a full certification stack, two decades of supply to the Fortune 500 chocolate names — but no consumer brand, no A&P spend, no distributor leverage or adjacency launches under the name; trust is technical/audit-based, not brand-based.)

*Unchanged: Corporate Culture (STRONG FIT); Pricing Power (LIKELY FIT); Innovation Dominance (LIKELY FIT); Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (NO FIT); Forward Integrators (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
