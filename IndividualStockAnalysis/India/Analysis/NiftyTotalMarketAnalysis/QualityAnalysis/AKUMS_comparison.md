### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 2 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 1 weakened, 8 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: No licence/AMC/subscription or installed-base language anywhere in the four calls; the only customer-funding evidence is the €100 million part consideration received upfront for the multi-year European CDMO contract (Q1FY26), which lifted net cash to ~INR1,518 crore. CDMO is repeat-order manufacturing rather than a front-end sale with an attached recurring stream. (The long view had said: Across all eight calls revenue is pure make-to-order manufacturing income with no licence fees, AMC, subscription or renewal streams; management explicitly confirmed there is no milestone or licensing income in CDMO revenue (Feb 2026). Working capital is positive and rising (91 to 105 days in FY26), the opposite of customer-funded negative working capital, though the €100mn EU part-consideration is a one-off advance.)
- **Brand Strength**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Brand language appears only for the small Akumentis domestic formulations arm (INR446 crore FY26, +2.9%), where management cites new launches and brand building; there is no heritage story, no disclosed A&P investment, and no evidence of premium pricing or share gain from brand in the core B2B CDMO business. (The long view had said: The owned-brand businesses underperform rather than demonstrate brand power — domestic branded grew 2.9% in FY26 (below IPM) with margin gains explicitly from overhead and debtor rationalisation, not premium pricing, and international branded was flat at INR143 crore; no heritage, A&P or premiumisation narrative appears in any call.)

**That weakened:**

- **Toll Roads**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Contract manufacturing is the bulk of the customer's product cost, not a 1-2% critical input, and the calls show pricing passed straight through — API price declines of 10-12% and then 8% flowed directly into flat CDMO revenue despite 7%+ volume growth, the opposite of a toll-road's negotiation-free pricing. (The long view had said: Strong certification/regulatory anchoring (1,000 DCGI approvals, EU GMP, ANVISA, WHO-GMP, 24-month customer audit cycles) creates real conversion stickiness, and management attributes FY26 volume gains to preference for compliant manufacturers — but Akums is the customer's entire manufacturing cost, not a 1-2% ingredient, and the cost-plus model shows it cannot price like a toll.)

*Unchanged: Market Share Gainers (STRONG FIT); Innovation Dominance (LIKELY FIT); Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 4 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Fashion Risk**: QUANT FLAG → LOW. Why now: Demand is functional and repeat-purchase — chronic therapies in gynaecology, cardio-diabetics, paediatrics, antibacterials and essential medicines — with growth attributed to portfolio depth, field-force productivity and regulatory approvals, never to a trending product or brand moment. (The long view had said: 1 numeric fingerprint is present but the calls offer nothing either way — numbers alone are only ever a flag to investigate, never a verdict.)

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: Across all four FY26 calls management framed results primarily in terms of commodity API price movement — a 10-12% decline over 12 months (Q1), a broad-based 8% YoY drop across the top 200 APIs (Q2), continued cephalosporin softness (Q3/Q4) — which swung group EBITDA margin from 14.8% (Q1) to 9.3% (Q2) and back to 13.1% (Q4) on nearly flat revenue, and drove the API segment into sustained losses; the offsetting flow-product nature of formulations (medicines consumed, not customer capex) and continued countercyclical investment through the weak H1 keep it from being a pure commodity cycle. (The long view had said: Revenue is mechanically tied to the API price cycle under a cost-plus model — API prices fell 7-12% in FY25, ~10-12% in FY26 Q1 and ~20-25% for the full year, holding CDMO revenue growth to 4-5% despite volume growth, then reversed upward on geopolitics in FY26 Q4; the API segment itself is a pure commodity (cephalosporin erosion >30%) and has run EBITDA-negative for eight straight quarters. Offsetting this, the core CDMO product is a flow/consumable (make-to-order medicines consumed in patient demand, not customer capex), percentage margins are contractually preserved through the cycle, and management kept investing (Baddi, Penem, injectables, >INR100 cr/yr R&D) through the weak H1 FY26.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (QUANT FLAG); Shifting Consumer Preferences (QUANT FLAG).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 3 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
