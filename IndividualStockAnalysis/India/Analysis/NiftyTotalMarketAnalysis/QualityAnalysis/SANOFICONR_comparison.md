### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 5 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: The molecules are off-patent and cheap substitutes are ubiquitous in Indian pharmacies, where the pharmacist frequently makes the call — management's own remedy is to educate pharmacists on 'superiority versus competitors,' which is the classic defence of a price premium through an intermediary that captures the extra margin. (The long view had said: The portfolio is built on long-off-patent molecules (fexofenadine, ibuprofen-paracetamol, pheniramine, vitamin D) where cheaper equivalents are freely available and the intermediary — doctor or pharmacist — controls the choice; management's repeated emphasis on educating HCPs and pharmacists about 'superiority versus competitors' is precisely the Nobel Biocare-style defence of a premium, and the recall period proved the substitution risk is live when rivals took the shelf space within a year.)

**That worsened:**

- **Shifting Consumer Preferences**: LOW → WATCH. Why now: The demand base is migrating from doctor-prescribed to self-choice, with online search, AI tools and social media now the dominant influencers — a real shift away from the prescription franchise this portfolio was historically built on, and one the company is only now retooling for after years of low-single-digit brand spend. (The long view had said: The consumer shift discussed — away from doctor visits toward self-medication and online health search — runs in the company's favour, and the whole demerger thesis is a pivot from a prescription-led pharma model to an FMCH self-choice model to capture it.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Cyclicality (LOW); Technological Innovation (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 3 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
