## Step 2 — Where it improved and where it regressed

### Bucket 1 — Business quality (34-check framework)

Of the 34 quality checks, 28 could be genuinely re-tested on both views: 6 improved, 2 regressed, 20 unchanged. 2 checks carry the long view's verdict forward unchanged (the window could not re-test them). 1 was newly assessable in the recent view.

| Area | Long view | Last one year | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capital Allocation | Neutral (-0.25) | Neutral (-0.50) | -0.25 |
| Return on Capital | Weak (-1.33) | Weak (-1.00) | +0.33 |
| Growth | Neutral (+0.50) | Good (+0.67) | +0.17 |
| Management | Good (+1.00) | Good (+1.00) | +0.00 |
| Industry Structure | Neutral (-0.50) | Neutral (+0.00) | +0.50 |
| Customer Benefits | Good (+1.00) | Good (+1.25) | +0.25 |
| Competitive Advantage | Neutral (+0.50) | Neutral (+0.00) | -0.50 |

**Checks that improved:**

- **Barriers to entry and rationality** (Industry Structure): Neutral → Excellent. Now: Profit margins have barely moved over the years (±0.7 points) — consistent with rational pricing and few new competitors. And from the calls: Approval and land barriers are visible (launches gated by RERA and OC timelines) and pricing behaviour is rational, with realizations up ~9% year-on-year rather than discounting. | The long view had said: Profit margins swing by ±8.6 points — a hint of price wars, cost shocks, or new entrants disturbing the industry. And from the calls: Approval complexity is a genuine barrier and pricing behaviour is rational with no discount wars, but the same approval friction hits SOBHA as hard as anyone and does not deter branded entrants.
- **Share donators** (Industry Structure): Neutral → Good. Now: Weaker developers are ceding ground — SOBHA grew NCR sales to a record while peers in the same Gurgaon market flagged softness, letting it take the INR4-5 crore sweet spot. | The long view had said: Consolidation toward branded developers is implicit in the sales gains, but the calls never identify weak competitors ceding share — peers are described as facing the same conditions and Gurgaon absorption was slow for SOBHA too.
- **Geographical expansion** (Growth): Good → Excellent. Now: Expansion is credible and delivering: presence extended to 13 cities with a first Mumbai project, Greater Noida entry, and NCR posting record annual sales of INR2,450 crores and record quarterly sales of INR1,384 crores on the Gurgaon Crescent launch. | The long view had said: Expansion beyond Bangalore has genuinely worked — NCR now ~30% of sales with Greater Noida and Gurgaon launches selling 50-60% at launch — with deliberately small, learning-first entries into Mumbai and Hyderabad.
- **Gain in market share** (Growth): Good → Excellent. Now: Clear share capture in a fragmented market with plenty of runway — FY26 sales of INR8,136 crores were an all-time high and Q1 FY27 sales rose 76% year-on-year, with individual launches selling 40-80% at launch. | The long view had said: Clear share capture with plenty of runway — Bangalore record Rs 4,500cr, NCR record Rs 2,450cr, FY26 presales +30% and Q1FY27 +76% — though FY25 was a stumble where the Rs 8,500cr guidance was missed.
- **Profit margin** (Return on Capital): Weak → Neutral. Now: Out of every ₹100 of sales, ₹6 is operating profit. Margins have moved about ±0.7 points a year — very steady, a sign costs are under control. | The long view had said: Out of every ₹100 of sales, ₹6 is operating profit. Margins have moved about ±8.6 points a year — sizeable swings, suggesting key costs are outside management's control.
- **Assurance benefits** (Customer Benefits): Good → Excellent. Now: The buyer is explicitly purchasing delivery certainty and quality, which SOBHA backs with in-house backward integration and rising completions — 5.4 million sq ft delivered in FY26, up 19%, guided to 6-6.5 million sq ft in FY27. | The long view had said: The proposition is certainty of quality and delivery, backed by backward integration and accelerating handovers (3,188 homes / 5.4mn sqft in FY26, +19%, targeting 6-6.5mn in FY27).

**Checks that regressed:**

- **Pricing, Mix and Volume** (Growth): Good → Neutral. Now: Realization did rise from INR13,412 to INR14,675 (+9.4%) and to INR15,655 in Q1 FY27, but the step-change in sales value is driven overwhelmingly by launch volume (6.89 million sq ft launched in one quarter), not price/mix. | The long view had said: Realization roughly doubled over the period on price hikes and premium mix (Gurgaon-led +32% in FY25, +9.4% in FY26), but management now explicitly guides that FY27 growth shifts to volume rather than price.
- **Advertisement and promotion** (Capital Allocation): Good → Neutral. Now: S&M spend is disclosed only as rising in line with sales and launches, with no evidence given on efficiency or durable brand-equity build. | The long view had said: A&P is disclosed only generically and tied to launches, though brand equity demonstrably supports realization (Rs 8,431/sqft in Q1FY23 to Rs 15,655/sqft in Q1FY27).

*Unchanged: Mini-monopolies; Oligopoly (vs duopoly); Partial monopoly / broken competition / switching costs; Cost of working capital; Customer types (retail vs corporate); Independent, long-term and tenacious; Halo effect (caution); Return on capital (headline); Security by obscurity; Distribution advantage; Cyclical market growth; Structural end-market growth; Candor; Convenience benefits; Out of limelight; Intangible benefits; Capex for growth (not maintenance); Disciplined stewards; Technology advantage; Asset turn.*

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these — identical by construction): Distribution to shareholders (Weak); Persistence of growth (Neutral).*

**Newly assessable in the recent view:**

- **Network effects** — Weak: Project-by-project residential sales carry no self-reinforcing two-sided network; each launch must be re-sold from scratch, as the per-project absorption commentary shows.

### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 1 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 3. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 0 weakened, 8 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Pricing Power**: PARTIAL → STRONG FIT. Why now: Realisation rose from INR13,412 (FY25) to INR14,675 (FY26, +9.4%) and to INR15,655 in Q1 FY27, while volumes hit records — FY26 sales INR8,136 crores and Q1 FY27 up 76% year-on-year — so price increases came with no volume loss, though part of the lift is mix from Gurgaon/Mumbai entries. (The long view had said: FY23–FY26 shows genuine pricing power — 4–6% Bangalore hikes absorbed without volume loss, realisation up 11% year-on-year in Aug 2022, 32% in Nov 2024 and 9.4% in FY26 — but by May 2026 management says pricing has stabilised and growth must come from volume, so the pricing lever is presented as spent, and mix/geography drives much of the reported increase.)
- **Innovation Dominance**: PARTIAL → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: No R&D spend, pipeline of products, or innovation-led premiumisation is disclosed in the last-12-month calls; project launches are not discussed in R&D terms. (The long view had said: the calls are silent and the numbers are mixed (1 of 2 checks supporting).)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Brand Strength (STRONG FIT); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (NO FIT); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Low Price Plus (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 0 worsened, 2 unchanged; 4 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Government Dependency**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: Earnings are gated by government approvals rather than government money: non-receipt of occupancy certificates in three projects deferred close to INR500 crores of revenue in Q3 FY26 and crushed that quarter's profit, while launch timing (and hence the FY26 sales plan) hung on RERA and other approvals still pending. No subsidy, tariff or PSU-order dependence is discussed, so the exposure is procedural, not policy-price. (The long view had said: Fortunes are repeatedly gated by government action rather than by demand: launches wait on RERA and design approvals, revenue recognition swings on occupancy/completion certificates ('significant improvement in the fourth quarter, primarily driven by receipt of occupancy certificates, which got delayed in the third quarter'), the Hosur land bank sits idle awaiting conversion approvals, Pune sales benefited from an FSI rule change, and Greater Noida entry came via a won authority land parcel. Management concedes the approval risk is permanent and outside its control.)
- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → LOW. Why now: The calls affirmatively show the premium is being paid rather than defended: average realisation rose from INR13,412 to INR14,675 psf in FY26 and to INR15,655 psf in Q1 FY27, with SOBHA Magnus selling ~80% and SOBHA Crescent ~60% at launch — no discounting or price-defence discussion appears. Homebuyers choose the brand directly, so there is no intermediary capturing the margin as in the Nobel Biocare case. (The long view had said: Sobha explicitly sells a price premium (realization up from INR8,431/sq ft in FY23 to INR15,655/sq ft in FY27) in a market with many local developers offering cheaper equivalents, and the FY25 episode where premium-heavy inventory sold slowly — forcing a shift back to mainstream ticket sizes — shows buyers will trade down when the premium stretches. Pricing power has since flattened, with management saying increases must now come from volume, not price.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (QUANT FLAG); Technological Innovation (LOW); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (HIGH RISK); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
