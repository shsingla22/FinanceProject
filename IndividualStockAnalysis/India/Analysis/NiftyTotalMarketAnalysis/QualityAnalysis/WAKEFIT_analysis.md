# Wakefit Innovations (WAKEFIT) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Wakefit Innovations is an Indian home and sleep solutions company, started in 2016 as a mattress ("sleep solutions") player and now selling mattresses, furniture (beds, sofas, wardrobes, dining tables, chairs) and home furnishings (pillows, cushions, towels, mats, rugs, carpets, comforters, curtains) to ordinary household consumers across the mass, masstige and premium price segments. It makes money by manufacturing most of what it sells in its own five factories under a vertically integrated, direct-to-consumer model, and selling it under a single Wakefit brand — about two-thirds of sales (67.2% in FY26) come through its own website and its own company-owned company-operated (COCO) stores, with the rest through e-commerce and quick-commerce marketplaces and roughly 1,948 multi-brand outlet (MBO) stores on a cash-and-carry basis. Operations are entirely within India — 139 own stores across 76 cities, MBOs in 536 towns and cities, and delivery reach into more than 700 districts across 28 states and 6 union territories. Revenue was INR 1,489 crore in FY26, up about 17%, with reported EBITDA of INR 182 crore (12.2% margin), and management says it still holds well under 1% of the combined mattress, furniture and home accessories market.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Mattresses** — Its original and largest business, sold pan-India, at 61.4% of FY26 revenue with 17% annual growth, and management calls it the most profitable of the three categories.
- **Furniture** — Beds, sofas, wardrobes, dining tables, chairs and seating made to order on a catalogue basis after a confirmed order, at 29.3% of FY26 revenue growing ~24% for the year off a smaller base — sold mainly in cities where Wakefit has a physical store, and seen by management as the biggest source of future margin gains since the furniture market is roughly 10x the mattress market.
- **Furnishings** — Pillows, cushions, towels, mats, rugs, carpets, comforters, runners and curtains plus newer home decor items such as live plants, the smallest category at 9.3% of FY26 revenue.
- **Own channels (website + COCO stores)** — Wakefit's own website and 139 company-owned company-operated stores across 76 cities (about 4 lakh sq ft, asset-light because they mostly hold display stock), contributing 67.2% of FY26 sales and over 74% in Q4, at higher margins because no third party takes a cut; retail revenue grew 49% in FY26 and a 100,000+ sq ft "Jumbo" store is planned for next year.
- **External channels (marketplaces + MBOs)** — Sales through major e-commerce and quick-commerce platforms and 1,948 multi-brand outlets across 536 towns and cities on a cash-and-carry (no credit) basis, making up the remaining roughly one-third of sales and used to test demand in new geographies cheaply.
- **In-house manufacturing and supply chain** — Five automated factories plus a central mother warehouse feeding inventory-holding and delivery points, run on just-in-time, low-inventory lines — the cost engine of the business, exposed to crude-linked input costs (polyol, TDI, PU foam, spring coils, fabric, MDF board) that spiked 30–160% in early 2026 and forced two price increases of 7–8% each in March and April.
