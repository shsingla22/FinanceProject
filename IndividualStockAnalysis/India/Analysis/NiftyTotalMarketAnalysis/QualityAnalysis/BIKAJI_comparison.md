### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 0 eased, 1 worsened, 7 unchanged. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: LOW → WATCH. Why now: Demand is a flow/consumable staple (bhujia, namkeen, sweets bought for daily consumption, not customer capex), and volumes grew every quarter of FY26; but margins swing with agri-commodity inputs — pulses and edible oil softening lifted gross margin to ~35% in Q1FY26, while by Q4FY26 edible oil rose 12-14% and packaging 25-30% on the war, forcing an April price rise. The big INR 500 crore capex cycle is behind them with utilisation only ~52%, so the over-investment risk is bounded but the fixed-cost base is now carried at half load. (The long view had said: Demand is consumption-linked packaged snacks (bhujia, namkeen, papad, sweets) bought in customer operations, not capex — volumes grew every year across the 14 calls with only Diwali-timing shifts between Q2/Q3; the fluctuation is in input costs (palm oil, moong dal, packaging), which management repeatedly passed on via 2.5–3% price hikes and grammage cuts. The one caution is heavy FY22–FY24 plant building now running at 50–52% utilisation, though management has since paused major capex.)

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (WATCH); New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW); Stakeholder Concentration (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 1 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
