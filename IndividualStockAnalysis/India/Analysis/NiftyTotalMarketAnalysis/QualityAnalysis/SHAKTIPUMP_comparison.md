### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 1 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 0. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 1 weakened, 8 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Friendly Middleman**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The new rooftop business is explicitly built through trained trade professionals — 57 exclusive channel partners and 400 installers trained in Nov 2025, extended to distributors by May 2026 — but the core solar-pump business is won through state government tenders where price, not an expert recommender, decides. (The long view had said: The buying decision runs through government rate contracts and L1 tenders where price is the binding criterion, not through an expert recommender insulating the company from price sensitivity; the only channel-building (rooftop distributors, export dealers) is recent and unaccompanied by any trade-training or professional lock-in narrative.)
- **Cost to Replicate**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The articulable barrier is integrated manufacturing depth — pumps, motors, VFDs, structures plus DCR cell/module capacity, which management claims will make it 'the only fully integrated rooftop service provider' — but this is capital a well-funded entrant can buy, and the collapse in tender realizations shows the advantage is not currently protecting price. (The long view had said: The four-year record answers the replication test negatively: new entrants arrived, tender prices fell and margins more than halved, showing 40 years of brand and 14 patents did not prevent a well-funded copycat from competing away the economics; the integration case (cells/modules by 2027-28) is a promise, not yet a demonstrated barrier.)

**That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Volume growth is strong and geographically widening — 86,086 installations in FY26 (+20%), +57.6% in Q1 FY27, first large Karnataka order plus Haryana and Punjab — and management claims leadership, but no explicit market-share percentages or comparisons to named competitors are given, and part of the growth is bought with a stretched receivable book. (The long view had said: Volume leadership is persistent — 46% of KUSUM-2 installs in 2022, 86,086 pumps in FY26 (+20%) and +57.6% year-on-year in Q1 FY27 — but reported share fell to roughly 20% by Jul 2024, and recent share is being defended in an environment of new entrants and falling realizations, which is share held at the cost of profitability.)

*Unchanged: Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Brand Strength (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (NO FIT); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 5 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Strained on the long view, Strained on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Technological Innovation**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Across all four calls no competing technology platform is discussed defensively and no analyst raises disruption; the innovation cadence described is incremental and value-adding — an in-house inverter claimed to generate ~10% more than competitors, VFDs, and backward integration into DCR cells and modules. (The long view had said: Core motor/pump/VFD technology is stable and innovation is incremental (14 patents granted of 29 filed, grid-export pump patent), but management concedes solar panel technology moves much faster than its own domain, and it is now committing heavy capital to DCR cell/module lines whose technology vintage could date.)

**That worsened:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Solar pumps are sold into L1 tenders where the government intermediary chooses and the farmer has no brand preference, so price is set competitively — recent Magel Tyala tenders came in with 3%–4% lower realisation, an analyst pressed management directly on why tender prices fell, and margins have compressed to single digits despite record volumes. (The long view had said: Tenders are awarded on L1 lowest-price matching, so a cheaper 'good enough' pump directly resets Shakti's realization — analysts pressed exactly this point as margins halved — and the product is not so complex that challengers cannot meet spec. Protection rests on brand and after-sales rather than on structural niche.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
