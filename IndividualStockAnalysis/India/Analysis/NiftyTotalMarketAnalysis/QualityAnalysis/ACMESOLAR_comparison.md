### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 2 worsened, 4 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Strained on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Electrons are a fully undifferentiated product and there is no premium to defend — winning depends purely on being the lowest bid, with tariffs discovered at INR2.764/unit for the RUMSL solar park and returns defended through capex savings (GST cut, non-applicability of ALCM, free night charging by discom, >10% saving versus budgeted capex) rather than through price. Any competitor with equal access to cheap Chinese modules and batteries can match the proposition, so margins rest on execution and cost, not protection. (The long view had said: The output is undifferentiated electrons sold to an intermediary (SECI/NHPC) that selects purely on lowest discovered tariff — there is no brand, no end-customer awareness, and no price premium ACME can defend, so any challenger with cheaper capital or cheaper Chinese kit resets the price. Management's defence is cost position and a return floor, not product differentiation.)

**That worsened:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: Off-taker concentration is explicit — 86% of the portfolio sits with central counterparties and the same few names (SECI, NHPC, SJVN) recur every quarter — while operations are geographically concentrated with 2,250 MW of the 2,890 MW operating fleet in Rajasthan. Supply is equally concentrated on Chinese modules and cells and a short list of battery vendors (Narada, Trina), with management having to address China's VAT export rebate withdrawal directly. (The long view had said: Off-take is concentrated in a handful of central agencies — 86% central by Q1 FY26, rising to '100% central' for the under-construction book, with SECI now notified as the single REIA — while assets are geographically concentrated (Rajasthan ~2,250 MW of 2,890 MW operational, prompting analyst questions on curtailment) and battery/cell supply is concentrated on a few Chinese vendors after management explicitly ruled out domestic assembly.)
- **Cyclicality**: LOW → WATCH. Why now: Revenue is overwhelmingly contracted under 25-year PPAs and management explicitly de-links results from power-demand swings, but the company is in a heavy expansionary capex phase (INR12,475 crores committed, 5.1 GW under construction) and is deliberately adding uncontracted merchant BESS arbitrage exposure (2.3 GWh running merchant at ~INR2.2 crores/day realisation), which is a price-spread-driven, cycle-sensitive earnings stream. (The long view had said: Revenue is contracted under 25-year fixed-tariff PPAs, and management demonstrated the insulation directly when Q1 FY26 national power demand fell 1% year-on-year with no revenue effect; EBITDA margin has been stable at 87-91.5% across all seven calls despite heavy capex. The residual cyclicality is input-side (module/cell and battery pricing, China VAT rebate withdrawal) and the growing merchant BESS arbitrage book, which is uncontracted by design.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (ELEVATED).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Technological Innovation (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Strained on the one-year view (1 of the 3 stress checks is flagged — one warning light, not yet a pattern.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
