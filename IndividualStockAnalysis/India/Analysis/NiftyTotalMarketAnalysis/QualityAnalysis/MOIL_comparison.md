### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 1 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 1. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 2 weakened, 6 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Innovation Dominance**: PARTIAL → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: No R&D spend, pipeline or named new products are disclosed in any of the four meets; exploration drilling metres (87,661 m in FY24) are resource replacement, not innovation, and cannot be read as R&D-led dominance. (The long view had said: Some process/product initiatives recur — the only EMD plant in India, and by Mar 2026 beneficiation, briquetting/agglomeration of low grades and high-speed shaft technology — but no R&D spend is disclosed, no named new products with revenue impact, and capex is conventional mine development rather than innovation-led.)

**That weakened:**

- **Cost to Replicate**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The asset barrier is genuine and concrete — 10 mines over 100 years old, roughly 20% of India's manganese resources, leases already extended to 2032 and 2042, both host state governments as promoters, and scarce underground-mining manpower; no funded newcomer can conjure these. But the barrier protects the ore body, not the earnings: 5.5 mn t of annual imports bypass it entirely and set the price. (The long view had said: The advantage is finite-location and un-replicable: 10 mines over 100 years old with leases extended to 2032–2042, ~20% of India's manganese resources yielding ~50% of national production, the country's only EMD plant, and a central location no importer can match on freight — a well-funded entrant cannot acquire equivalent leases or orebodies in a few years.)
- **Low Price Plus**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: None of the four meets discusses cost culture, itemised savings, no-frills choices or passing savings to price; the only cost-side references are capex and renewable energy sourcing (43% of energy in Mar 2026), not a replicable-proof cost advantage. (The long view had said: There is real cost consciousness — manpower cost per tonne targeted down INR300–400 by redeployment (Jun 2023), 43–60% of energy self-supplied from windmills/solar, and 'we are working constantly on our cost' as the answer to the royalty-tax risk (Aug 2024) — but as a PSU it shows no itemised cost culture and margins fell with prices in FY26 rather than volume/turns offsetting.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (NO FIT); Forward Integrators (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Toll Roads (PARTIAL); Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT); Brand Strength (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
