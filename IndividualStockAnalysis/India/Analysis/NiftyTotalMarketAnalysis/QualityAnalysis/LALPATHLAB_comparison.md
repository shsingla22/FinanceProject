Now: Management volunteers the unflattering detail — weak seasonal-fever volumes, the Rs. 30 crore labour-code hit, the prior-year Rs. 41 crore Suburban benefit, the exact 2–3% CGHS price tailwind — and refuses to over-guide on one good quarter. | The long view had said: Consistently candid on unfavourable points — flagged that COVID margins were unsustainable, separately disclosed the Rs 30 cr Labour Code and Rs 41 cr Suburban one-offs, and repeatedly cautioned against extrapolating a single strong quarter.

Now: Organized chains are still under one-fifth of the market and DLPL is growing well ahead of it — 12.2% FY26 revenue and 19.1% in Q1 FY27, the highest in four years — with runway in West India, Tier-3/4 and rural where it is barely present. | The long view had said: Long runway in a market where organized players hold a minority share, and delivery has improved from ~11% growth in FY25 to 12.2% in FY26 and 19.1% in Q1 FY27, the highest in four years, with Tier 3+ now 39% of revenue.

Now: Profit margins have barely moved over the years (±0.0 points) — consistent with rational pricing and few new competitors. (The long view had said: The trajectory is the opposite of low-price-plus: LPL explicitly refused to match the discounting wave, runs 27-31% EBITDA margins on premium realizations (RPP up from Rs. 707 to Rs. 968), and reinvests surplus rather than passing it to price; cost obsession is absent from the culture described on calls.)

**That weakened:**

(The long view had said: Growth has re-accelerated from ~11% (FY25) to 12.2% (FY26) to 19.1% in Q1FY27 with guidance raised toward mid-teens, and management frames the unorganized-to-organized shift as share capture, plus early international steps (Nepal, Bangladesh, Dubai, Ghana) — but no explicit market-share percentages or share-versus-named-competitor data are ever given.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 2 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
