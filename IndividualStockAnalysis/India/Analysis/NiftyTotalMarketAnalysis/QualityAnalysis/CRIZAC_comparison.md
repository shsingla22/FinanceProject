### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 4 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 2 weakened, 7 unchanged; 1 could not be compared.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → STRONG FIT. Why now: Every operating metric rose across the four calls — university partners 170 (FY25) to 250 (Q2) to 400+ (Q4), applications +43% year-on-year to 3.94 lakh, active agents +36% — off a self-declared sub-1% share, and expansion into LATAM, UAE, New Zealand and Ireland is being funded from internal cash with no credit or margin sacrifice; the one caution is enrolments grew only 14% in Q4 versus 43% application growth. (The long view had said: Applications grew 43% and active agents 36% in FY26, with Ireland share near 10% and global share under 1% leaving runway, but enrollment growth was only 14% and FY26 revenue growth of 22.7% undershot the 30% guided in Aug 2025 — application growth is not converting into proportionate share of enrollments.)

**That weakened:**

- **Toll Roads**: LIKELY FIT → NO FIT. Why now: Crizac's take is a commission on tuition — a material, negotiated cost line for the university, not a sub-1% critical input; across the four calls there is no certification, standards, accreditation or industry-body anchoring language, and the top 10 universities at 66% of revenue implies concentrated counterparties with bargaining power. (The long view had said: There is regulatory/standards anchoring — the UK Agent Quality Framework is described as raising barriers and entrenching compliant platforms — but the commission is a material recruitment cost to universities, not a sub-1% ingredient, and universities openly multi-home across competing platforms.)
- **Brand Strength**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Trust and tenure with institutions is the stated asset — 400+ university partnerships and 5+ years continuous relationship across the top 30 — and management positions Crizac as the sector's trusted infrastructure; but this is B2B reputation, with no A&P spend, heritage narrative or consumer pull disclosed on any of the four calls. (The long view had said: There is no consumer brand or A&P spend at all — this is a B2B intermediary — but management leans on institutional trust and tenure (5+ years with top-30 university partners) as the reason universities allocate recruitment to them.)

*Unchanged: Friendly Middleman (STRONG FIT); Pricing Power (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (NO FIT).*

*Not comparable: Low Price Plus (NOT ASSESSED → NOT ASSESSED — could not be assessed on either view).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 2 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
