### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 3 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 1 weakened, 5 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Low Price Plus**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Repeated itemised cost levers across Q2–Q4 FY26 (offshoring, pyramid and sub-con rationalisation, utilisation, SG&A, ESOP) show real cost discipline, but these are deployed to defend margin rather than to pass price savings to customers, and management explicitly positions itself as a premium technology-services firm, not a low-cost general IT vendor. (The long view had said: Management explicitly positions away from commodity/low-cost IT services and toward premium technology services; the cost programmes discussed in FY25 are about internal margin (utilisation 78%→88%, employee benefit rationalisation), not about passing savings to customers as price.)
- **Pricing Power**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Q3 FY26 disclosed a shift to tool-driven, non-linear commercials worth 150bps of margin — pricing-for-value rather than headcount — and FY26 EBIT rose to 15.6% (+31% year-on-year) alongside 17.4% growth; but there are no explicit price increases without volume loss, and AI-led delivery carries deflation risk management has not quantified. (The long view had said: Growth is volume/headcount-led, not price-led: Q1FY23 disclosed offshore billing rate up just 1.6% and onsite 0.2% against 9-17% volume growth, and margin expansion through FY25-FY26 is attributed to utilisation, subcon, currency and cost optimisation — never to price increases; no premiumisation or elasticity language anywhere in 17 calls.)

**That weakened:**

- **Brand Strength**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Management asserts brand strength in the Nagarro call and cites awards/recognitions each quarter, but there is no heritage-and-premium-pricing evidence, no A&P disclosure, and B2B brand here is really delivery reputation. (The long view had said: There is a real B2B brand build — Brand Finance Top-100 India brands and 'third-fastest growing Indian brand' (Jul 2022), sustained analyst/ISG recognition, and by Jun 2026 the merger rationale rests on 'two companies have very strong brand name in respective markets' — but no heritage-premium pricing, no A&P spend disclosure, and no evidence brand alone wins deals.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL).*

*Unchanged: Market Share Gainers (STRONG FIT); Corporate Culture (STRONG FIT); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 3 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Across all four FY26 calls the business shows no cycle-like swing: 22nd, 23rd and 24th consecutive quarters of sequential growth, FY26 revenue +17.4% year-on-year with EBIT margin expanding to 15.6%, and demand described in terms of client operating/engineering programmes and multi-year renewals rather than any commodity or capex cycle. Bookings are annuity-shaped — ACV of $1.8bn on TCV of $2.4bn for FY26, plus a 6.5-year, $650mn+ contract signed in June 2026. (The long view had said: Revenue is discretionary enterprise tech spend that management repeatedly frames against the macro cycle (Jul 2022 'headwinds being experienced by the global economy', Jul 2024 'challenging business environment'), but the base is operations-linked annuity work — renewals, multi-year managed-services and engineering contracts — not customer capex equipment, and the company posted 24 consecutive quarters of sequential growth through the FY24-25 IT downturn.)

**That worsened:**

- **Technological Innovation**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: The single dominant theme across all four calls is a platform-level shift, not incremental innovation: management explicitly says commercials are moving away from people-based pricing ("a combination of people and tool-driven pricing models"), which attacks the headcount-linked revenue model the whole delivery infrastructure is built around, and the Q4 call catalogues AI roadmap announcements from Anthropic, OpenAI, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Google, Snowflake and Databricks with an expectation that adoption "accelerate[s] significantly." The response — a 1,700-person AI huddle, a CTO for 'Engineering Hyper-Productivity', an AI Investor Day, and a $2.9bn merger to gain scale — is the scale of reaction that signals a large-scale, not small-scale, technology transition. (The long view had said: The whole 17-call arc shows an industry mid-platform-shift: by FY25-26 nearly every call is dominated by AI, and management is explicitly rebuilding the delivery model away from billable headcount toward platforms (SASVA, iAURA, GenAI Hub, 500+ agents, 120 patents) while stating the goal is higher revenue and profit per employee — an admission that the installed labour-pyramid economics are the thing at risk. Persistent is investing hard, but the risk is the industry, not the management.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Government Dependency (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 9 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
