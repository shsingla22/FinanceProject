# Bayer Crop Sci. (BAYERCROP) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Bayer CropScience Limited is the India-listed arm of the global Bayer CropScience business, selling farm inputs — crop protection chemicals (insecticides, herbicides, fungicides) and seeds, especially hybrid corn seed — to Indian farmers, the large majority of them smallholders (management says it reaches about 20 million of the 50 million smallholders Bayer touches worldwide). It makes money by selling these products through a farm-input distribution channel each cropping season, so revenue swings with the monsoon, sowing acreage and how much stock the channel can 'liquidate'; margins depend on input/raw-material costs (much of its production base is in Europe, and Chinese agrochemical supply and prices set the price ceiling in the market). Named brands include Dekalb, Roundup, Nativo, Laudis and Council Activ. It operates in India (registered office in Thane, Maharashtra; present in the country over 125 years, merged with Monsanto India in 2019), is among Bayer CropScience's top 5 countries globally, and has a small export component; it pays out most of its cash as dividends (cumulatively over 80% of cash flow, Rs 90 per share at the last half year).

**The parts of the business:**

- **Crop Protection (CP) portfolio** — Agrochemicals — insecticides, herbicides and fungicides sold to farmers to protect crops; management calls it the 'dominant portfolio' by revenue, but the slower grower at just under 6% annual growth over five years, and the part hardest hit by high input prices in FY2024-25 and by the washed-out second quarter of FY2025-26.
- **Corn seeds portfolio** — Hybrid corn (maize) seed, the company's clear growth engine — nearly doubled over five years at roughly 15-16% annual growth, grew double-digit in the first half of FY2025-26 on both volume and price, and is riding corn becoming India's third field crop on ethanol and animal-feed/silage demand.
- **Others (exports, Roundup, traded seeds)** — A residual bucket management describes as exports, the Roundup herbicide line and seeds it buys in and resells; Roundup pricing was called out as a drag on revenue in FY2023-24, but no size is given for the bucket.
- **Transformation / partnerships (not a revenue line)** — Management's label for its ecosystem-building work — digitisation of farming, working with farmer producer organisations (FPOs), mechanisation, agronomy advice, financing and market linkage — framed as investment in future growth drivers rather than a segment that reports sales today.
- **Environmental Science (divested)** — A business the company sold, generating one-off exceptional income in FY2022-23; an earlier seeds divestment to Crystal did the same in FY2021-22 — both are gone from the ongoing business.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
