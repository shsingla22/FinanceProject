# Action Const.Eq. (ACE) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Action Construction Equipment (ACE) is an Indian manufacturer that builds and sells heavy machinery — cranes, construction equipment, material-handling equipment like forklifts, and farm tractors and implements — from its plants at Dudhola, Palwal in Haryana. It makes money by selling these machines in volume (2,863 units in one quarter of FY25, for example), mostly to Indian buyers in infrastructure, construction, manufacturing, logistics and farming, with government infrastructure spending on roads and railways cited as the main demand driver. Revenue is overwhelmingly domestic: in the June 2026 quarter exports were about 3% of sales (Middle East, notably Saudi Arabia, plus African markets) and defence supplies to the Indian armed forces about 5%. Quarterly standalone revenue has grown from roughly INR 501 crore (Jun 2022) to INR 836 crore (Jun 2026), with EBITDA margins rising from about 9% to about 20%, and the business is seasonal — 40-45% of the year's revenue in the first half, 55-60% in the second.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Cranes** — The core and largest business, making pick-and-carry cranes and tower cranes where management claims market leadership; it is being extended into heavier truck, crawler and rough-terrain cranes through a joint venture with Japan's KATO Works starting end-July 2026.
- **Construction Equipment** — Backhoe loaders and similar site machinery — a smaller line (about INR 52 crore in the Jun 2022 quarter at roughly 4.5% margins) that management has since grouped with cranes and material handling and targeted for 15%+ annual growth.
- **Material Handling (later called 'Metal Handling')** — Forklifts and related lifting/handling equipment for factories and logistics (about INR 38 crore in the Jun 2022 quarter at ~11% margins), and the line behind the custom-built forklift orders for the Indian armed forces.
- **Agri Equipment** — Tractors and farm implements, the smallest and lowest-margin division — roughly 8% of revenue (INR 61 crore in Q2 FY25, INR 42.67 crore in Q1 FY27) with margins in the low single digits, and demand tied to the monsoon and crop cycle.
- **Defence business** — Sales of backhoe loaders, 4x4 multipurpose tractors, forklifts and rough-terrain forklifts to India's Ministry of Defence; around 5% of revenue in the June 2026 quarter, in line with the stated goal of 5-10% of overall revenue, and boosted by a single order of over INR 400 crore.
- **Exports and overseas projects** — About 3% of revenue in the June 2026 quarter (Middle East and Africa), and including a roughly INR 200 crore (US$24.98 million) contract to design and set up a tractor, backhoe and farm-implement assembly plant for the Government of Ghana, funded by an India Exim Bank credit line and intended to open West African markets.

### Government Dependency — HIGH RISK
*"Fortunes decided in Delhi"*

**Why this verdict:** the calls show clear, repeated exposure; this risk has no numeric fingerprint to cross-check, so it is judged from the calls alone.

**What the calls show:** Management anchors the demand outlook in government budgets nearly every call (INR11.11 lakh crore capex, road/rail allocations, 'Viksit Bharat'), while a growing revenue slice is directly government-sourced — a INR400cr+ Ministry of Defence order, defence at 5-6% of revenue, and a US$25mn Ghana government plant financed by India EXIM Bank; policy also sets product economics via emission norms and even steel prices ('softening of commodity especially steel due to policy decisions'). ### Cyclicality — ELEVATED
*"Boom-and-bust earnings"*

**Why this verdict:** the calls show clear exposure, but none of the 3 computed fingerprints confirms it in the numbers yet — held one step below the top severity.

**What the calls show:** ACE sells capital equipment bought out of customers' capex — management says ~50% of sales go indirectly to crane rental companies whose buying swings with sentiment, and the record shows violent swings: backhoe industry down 30-40%, EBITDA margin moving from 9.3% (Q1FY23) to 20.4% (Q1FY27), FY26 with no growth at all, and every call framing demand and margins around steel/commodity prices and infra capex cycles. Guidance is repeatedly withheld or hedged because the cycle is unreadable.

> "50% of our business is indirectly to rental company... enquiries and order conversion did dip" — *management, on an earnings call*

*Silver lining: Countercyclical capacity investment (INR4,500cr to INR5,100cr capacity, 82 acres of land bought ahead of demand) and diversification into agri, material handling, defence and exports*

- ✅ Sales fell in only 1 of the last 11 years with swings of ±18 points — no pronounced boom-and-bust signature in revenue.
- ✅ Operating margin stayed between 3% and 15% (±4.0 points a year) — swings within the normal range, no extreme boom-and-bust profile.
