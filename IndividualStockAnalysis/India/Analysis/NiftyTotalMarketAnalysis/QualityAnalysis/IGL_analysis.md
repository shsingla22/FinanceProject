# Indraprastha Gas Ltd. (IGL) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

*Industry: ENERGY*

Indraprastha Gas Limited (IGL) is one of India's leading city gas distribution (CGD) companies: it buys natural gas, pipes it through its own network, and sells it in two forms — CNG as vehicle fuel sold through its own filling stations, and PNG (piped natural gas) delivered to homes, shops/restaurants and factories. Its customers are motorists (about 20 lakh vehicles as of 2024), roughly 32.75 lakh households, ~5,400 industrial and ~7,400 commercial customers, plus bus fleets such as Delhi's DTC and DIMTS. It makes money on the spread between the cost of the gas it procures and the price it charges, which management tracks as EBITDA per standard cubic metre (guided at Rs. 7–8 per SCM; Rs. 5.4 in Q3 FY26), on volumes of about 9.4 million standard cubic metres a day and quarterly revenue of roughly Rs. 4,465 crores. It operates 12 licensed 'geographical areas' across 4 states — Delhi (still ~70% of volumes but shrinking as a share), Uttar Pradesh, Haryana and Rajasthan — running over 2,500 km of steel pipeline, ~29,200 km of MDPE distribution pipe and 955 CNG stations.

**The parts of the business:**

- **CNG (compressed natural gas for vehicles)** — Sells compressed gas as motor fuel at its own stations to cars, autos, taxis, commercial vehicles and city buses; it is the biggest part of the business at roughly 6.9–7.0 million SCM a day (over 50 lakh kg/day) out of ~9.4 million total, growing ~3% headline but ~10% once the shrinking DTC bus volumes are excluded.
- **PNG – domestic (household piped gas)** — Piped gas supplied to more than 32.75 lakh homes for cooking and heating, growing around 8% year-on-year and part of the ~2.5 million SCM/day total PNG segment.
- **PNG – industrial and commercial** — Piped gas sold to about 5,400 factories and 7,400 shops, hotels and other commercial establishments as a substitute for liquid fuels; commercial demand grew ~8% recently while industrial demand was described as subdued.
- **LNG stations / green logistics** — A newer, small business of LNG refuelling stations for long-haul trucking — one station was doing 4,000–5,000 kg/day in 2024 with 5–6 more planned and a Board-approved target of around 100 stations over 5–6 years; management calls the sector 'at the nascent stage' but expects it to contribute meaningfully.
- **Joint ventures / associate gas companies (CUGL, MNGL)** — Minority stakes in other city gas distributors whose profits add to IGL's earnings — MNGL was selling about 1.1 million SCM/day and growing ~9%, while CUGL was around 0.33 million SCM/day with flat volumes and lower profits (as of the Jan 2023 call).
- **Saudi Arabia joint venture (new)** — A planned 40%-equity JV with a local partner to build gas distribution in Saudi industrial cities that currently run on liquid fuels — IGL contributes technical, operational and safety expertise, with each industrial city said to hold potential of 1–1.5 million SCM of gas; it is an early-stage investment, not yet contributing revenue.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*

## Section 2 — Does it look like a long-term winner? (11 multibagger patterns)

### Cost to Replicate — STRONG FIT
*"Could a newcomer copy this?"*

**Why this verdict:** the calls confirm the pattern with clear, repeated evidence (no computable check applies — the calls decide).

**What the calls show:** The replication test is passed on physical and regulatory grounds: 2,500 km of steel plus ~29,200 km of buried MDPE, 975 CNG stations on finite prime Delhi-NCR sites, and PNGRB-authorised exclusivity that even a state-backed rival (Haryana City Gas in Gurgaon) has had to litigate for years to challenge — no well-funded entrant rebuilds that in a few years. The real threat is substitution (EV, propane), not replication.

> "steel pipeline network of over 2,500 kilometers and an MDPE network of approximately 29,000 kilometers" — *management, on an earnings call*

### Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) — PARTIAL
*"Sells once, earns forever"*

**Why this verdict:** the calls show some of the traits, with mixed numbers (2 of 3 checks supporting).

**What the calls show:** The installed base does generate an annuity — 32.75 lakh piped households, ~5,400 industrial and ~7,400 commercial customers whose gas offtake recurs and whose density management tracks call after call (27 lakh households in Jul-2024 → 31.75 lakh in Nov-2025) — but across all 13 calls there is no discussion of attach rates, AMC/service revenue share, renewals or deferred revenue/customer advances as a funding source; the recurring stream is metered commodity volume, not contracted fees.

> "we supply natural gas to more than 31.75 lakh households, nearly 5,300 industrial units and around 7,200 commercial establishments" — *management, on an earnings call*
