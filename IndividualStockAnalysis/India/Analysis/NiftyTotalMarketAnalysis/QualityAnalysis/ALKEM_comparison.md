### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 3 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → LOW. Why now: Across all four FY26 calls demand is described in prescription/therapy-volume terms (IQVIA IPM outperformance, chronic and acute scripts), never in commodity-price or customer-capex terms; revenue and EBITDA margin moved in a narrow band (21.9% → 23% → 22.2%) with 11–17% top-line growth each quarter, and the company invested into new areas (MedTech, US CDMO) while absorbing costs rather than scaling back. (The long view had said: Revenue is flow/consumption-linked (prescription pharma, no customer-capex exposure), but the calls show margins swinging hard on API commodity cycles and US price erosion — EBITDA fell to 7.9% in Q1FY23 on cephalosporin/PenG raw-material spikes, recovered to ~20% by FY26 largely on 'favorable API prices', and FY27 guidance was already being walked back on renewed API/packaging inflation.)

**That worsened:**

- **Government Dependency**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: Government action hit results in three of the four calls: the GST revision cost INR50–60 crores of Sikkim fiscal benefit in H2, Labour Code notifications produced exceptional charges of INR528 million (Q3) and INR602.7 million (Q4), and the MD stated outright that drug pricing is regulator-set and unmitigable; the FY27 outlook adds duty/geopolitical supply-chain pressure on APIs. (The long view had said: Pricing on roughly 30% of the India portfolio is set by NLEM/DPCO with no price lever, the threatened trade-margin cap has hung over the largest trade-generic franchise since FY23, US revenue depends on USFDA inspection/approval outcomes (St. Louis, Indore observations; Prolia/Xgeva awaiting approval), and the Labour Codes forced Rs.528mn and Rs.603mn exceptional charges in FY26 — but demand itself is private retail, not government orders or subsidies.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (LOW); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Technological Innovation (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 8 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
