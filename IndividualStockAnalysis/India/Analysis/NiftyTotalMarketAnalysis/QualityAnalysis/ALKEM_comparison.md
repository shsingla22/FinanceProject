### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 4 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → LOW. Why now: Across all four FY26 calls demand is prescription/consumption-led (IQVIA volume and therapy-share data), not tied to any customer's capex or a commodity price; revenue grew 11.2%, 17.2%, 10.7% and 14.6% Y-o-Y with EBITDA margin steadily improving to 20.4% for FY26 from 19.4%, i.e. no cycle-peak distortion in either sales or margins. (The long view had said: Demand is prescription/consumption-linked rather than customer-capex-linked, but reported margins swung violently across the five years — EBITDA of 7.9% in Q1FY23 versus 22.2% in Q3FY26 — driven largely by commodity API price cycles (cephalosporins, PenG), US price erosion and flu-season base effects; the Jun 2026 call again flags API and packaging inflation forcing management to withdraw its 100bps-a-year margin improvement guidance.)

**That worsened:**

- **Government Dependency**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: Government action hit reported numbers in three of the four calls: the GST revision cost INR50-60 crores of Sikkim fiscal benefit in H2 FY26, the Labour Codes notification produced exceptional charges of INR528 million (Q3) and INR602.7 million (Q4), and the MD stated flatly that drug pricing is set by regulation and cannot be mitigated. Indian pharma pricing (DPCO/NLEM) and fiscal incentives are structurally policy-determined. (The long view had said: Roughly 30% of the domestic portfolio is NLEM price-controlled with no price lever, the trade-margin-cap threat recurs across calls, USFDA inspection outcomes gate the US business, and the FY26 labour-code notification forced INR528mn and INR603mn exceptional charges; the MD openly concedes pricing risk is unmanageable.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Technological Innovation (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 10 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
