### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 3 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view; 2 could not be compared. Financial resilience: Unknown on the long view, Unknown on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: Lending is a credit-cycle business and the company is still working off the damage of the last cycle: a ~INR23,000 crore legacy book being run down (~INR5,000-6,000 crore collections a year, IBC recoveries) after a INR2,761 crore quarterly loss in the year-ago period, with NIM now compressing as the mix retailizes. The retail mortgage book is a flow-type, operations-linked asset with stable 1.2%/0.7% gross/net NPAs, which bounds the exposure. (The long view had said: The lender's fortunes track the property and rate cycle: management frames the FY23 opportunity on a housing-sales upcycle and 'increasing interest rate cycle', while the legacy wholesale/CRE developer book built in the prior up-cycle forced a multi-year run-down, ~INR1.3 lakh crore of deleveraging, a consolidated loss in the Sept-2024 quarter and a 1.9% terminal credit cost on INR3.6 lakh crore of past disbursals; CRE project and developer loans are funding customers' capex, the dangerous form.)

**That worsened:**

- **Technological Innovation**: LOW → WATCH. Why now: The asset-light model depends on staying technically integrated with partner banks fast enough to clear RBI's 15-day co-lending timeline, and management repeatedly frames tech/AI as the thing that must be rebuilt ("significant transformation on the tech side" over 4-6 months, IHC's AI ecosystem). This is incremental, value-adding innovation rather than a platform shift threatening their format — no competitor technology is discussed defensively. (The long view had said: Across the 16 calls technology appears only as a steady, value-adding cadence — co-lending tech integration with banks, the 2017-18 e-mortgage platform progressively improved into digital KYC, banking analysis, e-signing and e-disbursal, and now IHC's AI in credit decisioning — with no analyst question or defensive discussion about a platform shift making the franchise legacy.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
