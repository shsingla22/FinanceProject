### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 1 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 0. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 0 strengthened, 5 weakened, 4 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That weakened:**

- **Corporate Culture**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Within the window management owns bad news promptly (the Q1 FCI-rice tender misjudgement explained in full, the West UP cane diversion called 'a surprise, very honestly speaking'), discloses the ₹22.4 crore labour-code provision as an exceptional item, and delivered the distillery turnaround it flagged in Q1 by Q3–Q4; leadership (Sawhney, Taneja, Sinha, Rajpal) is unchanged across all four calls and the balance sheet regained ICRA AA+ Stable. Only one year of evidence and no management history on file, so the multi-year promise-versus-delivery record cannot be judged. (The long view had said: The same leadership team (Sawhney, Taneja, Sinha) runs all 16 calls, targets set in earlier calls are delivered and reconciled later (660 KLPD distillery capacity, Co0238 variety reduction 77%→50%, ₹340 crore capex tracked tranche by tranche), misses are owned plainly, and the balance sheet stayed conservative through the cycle to an ICRA AA+ reaffirmation.)
- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Management quotes ex-mill commodity prices per kilo for refined versus sulphitation sugar and sells ethanol into OMC tenders; there is no A&P spend, heritage positioning or adjacency launch discussed in any of the four calls. (The long view had said: A brand build has started — IMFL launches in July 2024 (The Crafters Stamp, Matsya) with IMIL now among the top five players in Uttar Pradesh, and brand-presence exhibitions in the Middle East for gearboxes — but there is no heritage story, no disclosed A&P spend, and it is only two years old.)
- **Innovation Dominance**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Across all four FY26 calls there is no R&D spend, product pipeline or named new-product revenue; performance is attributed to feedstock procurement, recovery rates and cost optimisation. The only 'innovation' analogue is agronomic cane development lifting recovery 26bps — useful, but not innovation-funded pricing power. (The long view had said: Power Transmission shows a real, repeated R&D thread that converts into cost reduction and new addressable markets (compressor gearboxes, small hydro, API, and the Q4 FY26 first-in-Asia axial compressor test gearbox), but R&D spend is never quantified and the sugar/ethanol majority of the business is agronomy, not innovation-led.)
- **Forward Integrators**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The business sells upstream-to-downstream through government-mediated channels — OMC ethanol allocations, DFPD monthly sugar quotas, institutional bulk buyers — with no own stores, franchising, D2C or brand-owned retail mentioned in the window. (The long view had said: Triveni has moved forward from bulk alcohol into its own branded IMFL/IMIL spirits, and in sugar toward premium refined and pharma-grade product (70% of output by FY25), but there are no own stores, franchising, royalties or D2C economics discussed.)
- **Cost to Replicate**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Nothing in the FY26 calls articulates why a funded newcomer could not replicate the asset base; earnings swing on maize prices, cane SAP, monsoon recovery, DFPD release quotas and MoPNG ethanol policy, and management repeatedly petitions government for MSP revision, subsidies and export windows rather than pointing to any structural barrier. (The long view had said: Some barriers are real but unarticulated by management: regulated UP cane command areas and licensed mill capacity cannot be replicated by a funded entrant, and the defence/test-rig gearbox technology is described as first-of-a-kind in Asia; against that, distillery KLPD capacity and water EPC bidding are straightforwardly buyable with capital.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Toll Roads (PARTIAL); Friendly Middleman (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 0 worsened, 2 unchanged; 4 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Ethanol — the segment that drove the FY26 turnaround (₹1,550 crore revenue) — is sold almost entirely to a single buyer group, the OMCs, under allocation cycles and tenders; Q4 revenue fell because of 'a calibrated scale-down of some ethanol dispatches aligning with the OMC's delivery timelines', and a court case in the south 'played havoc with the ethanol blending programme'. Sugar and engineering sales, by contrast, are spread across institutional buyers, traders and industrial customers. (The long view had said: Ethanol, which is 92% of alcohol sales and the group's growth engine, is sold almost entirely to three state Oil Marketing Companies whose tender cycles, allocations and delivery schedules dictate volumes — Q4 FY26 revenue fell on a 'calibrated scale-down of some ethanol dispatches aligning with the OMC's delivery timelines,' and a single court case froze the Cycle-2 tender. Feedstock is similarly concentrated on FCI rice allocation and maize procurement, and water revenues depend on a handful of government awards.)
- **New Entrants**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: The Q4 FY26 call describes the informal Gur/Khandsari sector — structurally different, largely unregulated competitors — aggressively outbidding mills for cane, driving Triveni's crush down 8.8% and Western UP crush down 17% where its largest capacities sit; management's response is to seek regulatory containment rather than commercial defence. (The long view had said: Two structurally different entrants have reset economics: unregulated gur/khandsari units outbid mills for cane, cutting Triveni's West UP crush 17% and group crush 8.8% in SS2025-26; and grain-based distilleries — a different feedstock class — overtook sugarcane ethanol nationally for the first time in FY24, shifting the OMC allocation mix against cane-based capacity.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Good Enough Goods (ELEVATED); Shifting Consumer Preferences (QUANT FLAG); Technological Innovation (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (HIGH RISK); Government Dependency (HIGH RISK).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 9 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
