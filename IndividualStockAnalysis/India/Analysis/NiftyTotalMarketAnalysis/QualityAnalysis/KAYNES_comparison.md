### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 0 eased, 1 worsened, 3 unchanged; 4 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Strained on the long view, Strained on the one-year view.

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Revenue is explicitly gated on customer project readiness and approval cycles rather than steady operational consumption — management attributed a ~20% shortfall to customers holding inventory until their projects align (Feb 2026) and blamed Q4 FY26 misses on 'last-minute customer deferment' (May 2026) — while the company simultaneously commits very large expansionary capex (INR1,500 crores into HDI PCB, two OSAT units) into the notoriously cyclical semiconductor chain. (The long view had said: Revenue is tied to customer project readiness rather than customer operations — management repeatedly explains shortfalls as 'customers don't take our products unless their entire project is aligned' (Feb 2026, ~20% shortfall to plan) and West Asia/Russia-Ukraine driven deferments (May 2026) — while simultaneously committing heavy countercyclical capex (INR1,500cr HDI PCB, two OSAT units), so the capital is going out while the top line slips. Margins have nonetheless been stable at ~14-16% EBITDA across all 17 calls, so this is not a commodity cycle.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Good Enough Goods (ELEVATED); New Entrants (NO SIGNAL); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
