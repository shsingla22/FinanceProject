### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 3 eased, 0 worsened, 4 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Technological Innovation**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Affle operates in a high-innovation ad-tech industry where platform-level shifts (iOS privacy/SKAN, agentic AI, CTV) can reset economics, and management devotes large parts of every call to keeping pace — Niko, OpticksAI, self-service automation, and continual patent filing 'to ring-fence our technology moat'. The calls show adaptation and small-scale, continuous innovation rather than a defensive posture about a competitor's platform, which bounds the exposure. (The long view had said: Adtech is a high-innovation industry where platform owners repeatedly reset the rules: Google's cookie deprecation reversal, Apple's iOS privacy changes, Google's in-app ad format changes, and now GenAI/agentic AI, with analysts asking directly about competitive dynamics 'in the GenAI era, especially from walled gardens as well as the Gen AI natives'. Management answers confidently and has adapted so far, but the recurrence of these externally-imposed platform shifts across all 17 calls is the risk itself.)
- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Affle is an intermediary layer in a crowded performance-marketing market where inventory and data cost consumes ~61% of revenue, and cheaper or self-serve alternatives (including the walled gardens' own tools) are a persistent structural threat — management's answer is to keep asserting a patent/IP moat and 'premium inventories' positioning. The calls nonetheless show pricing holding rather than eroding, so the exposure is real but bounded. (The long view had said: Gross margin has eroded for seven-plus consecutive quarters — inventory and data cost drifted from ~60-61% to 63.3% — and an analyst asked outright whether this is 'because we are responding to the competition'. Management denies price competition ('we are not fighting the competition on pricing. We are fighting them on value') and attributes it to forward investment, but CPCU rate growth of only ~3% y-o-y against rising input cost is consistent with a commoditising ad-delivery layer where agencies sit between Affle and the advertiser.)
- **Stakeholder Concentration**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Across all four calls growth is described as broad-based with no named customer, licensor or supplier driving results, and management repeatedly cites new logo additions and new account additions in both developed and emerging markets. No analyst question in the excerpts turns on a single relationship's renewal. (The long view had said: Customer base is broad (direct customers 70-75%, top verticals broad-based, 'no material changes in the collection risk'), but the supply side runs through a few gatekeepers — Google's Android/Play policy changes, Apple's iOS, and named OEM/CTV partners (TCL, Oppo, Honor, app stores) that management calls central to its premium-inventory strategy. Agency intermediaries also caused a working-capital wobble in Q3 FY2026 when collection days stretched on 'periodic client audits'.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (ELEVATED).*

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (WATCH); Government Dependency (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 3 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
