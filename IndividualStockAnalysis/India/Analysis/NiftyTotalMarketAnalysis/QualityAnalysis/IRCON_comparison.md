### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 4 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Stressed on the long view, Stressed on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: L1 lowest-price tendering means there is no brand premium to defend — the buyer selects on price and IRCON has visibly conceded margin rather than hold price, with new wins explicitly booked at lower margins. The bound is technical prequalification: management leans on credentials for complex work (DFC execution history, Kavach, signalling diagnostics) that thins the bidder field on larger, more specialised packages. (The long view had said: In L1 tendering the buyer's choice is price, not brand — management repeatedly defends its premium on 'credentials' and in-house execution while cheaper contractors win at or below estimate, and the erosion is quantified: PAT margin guidance drifted from 7-7.5% (Aug-2022) to 6.1-6.3% and standalone core EBITDA to ~4%.)

**That worsened:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Indian Railways and its arms are effectively the single counterparty — domain expertise is self-described as 'mainly in railways', 90-92% of the order book is domestic, and nearly half of it is nomination work that exists only because of the parent relationship. A single client decision also moved results directly: a Rs.108 crore liquidated-damages provision on the DFCC job in Q4 FY25. (The long view had said: Indian Railways is effectively the single customer — management cannot even quote total industry awards because 'that would be the domain of Indian Railways' — with NHAI/MoRTH as the only other meaningful client and 92% of the order book domestic; the coal JVs are all Coal India subsidiaries.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
