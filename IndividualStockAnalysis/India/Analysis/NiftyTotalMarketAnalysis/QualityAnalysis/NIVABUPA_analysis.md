# Niva Bupa Health (NIVABUPA) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Niva Bupa is an Indian health insurance company (IRDAI Registration No. 145), majority-owned by Bupa, which management describes as bringing over eight decades of global health insurance expertise. It sells medical insurance policies in India — mostly to individual customers and families, plus group cover sold to companies and some personal accident and travel cover — and collects premiums in exchange for paying customers' hospitalisation and treatment bills. It makes money the way any insurer does: premiums collected (gross written premium of INR 9,433 crore in FY26, up 27.4%) less claims paid and less the cost of selling and running the business — a combined ratio of 101.4% in FY26 — topped up by investment income on the float, which was an AUM of about INR 9,670 crore earning a 7.2% yield. It sells entirely through others' hands and its own — agents, banks, brokers including digital ones like Policy Bazaar, and direct-to-consumer — and reported FY26 profit after tax of INR 366 crore and a 10.1% share of India's retail health market.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Retail health** — Health policies sold to individual people and families, priced by health status, age and income, and the core of the company at roughly 67-68% of business, growing 35% in FY26 with a retail market share of 10.1% and a retail loss ratio around 67%.
- **Group health** — Health cover sold to employers and other groups for their people, the second-largest line at roughly 30-31% of the product mix.
- **Personal accident and travel** — Small add-on covers for accidents and overseas/domestic travel, described consistently as only about 2-2.4% of business.
- **Investments (float)** — The pool of premium money held before claims are paid, invested to earn a return — about INR 9,670 crore of assets at a 7.2% annualised yield in FY26 — which supplements underwriting results.
- **Ecosystem health services / customer app** — Free-to-use health services bundled with policies — health check-ups, doctor consults and a new chronic condition management programme delivered through the Niva Bupa app (12.3 million downloads, 5.7 lakh monthly active users, 50,000+ check-ups and 6,000+ consults a month) — positioned not as a separate revenue line but as a way to keep customers healthy and hold down future claims.
- **Preferred provider network (PPN) and claims management** — A curated hospital network — 49 cities and more than 1,000 hospitals by FY26, carrying about 20% of claims in those cities — used together with auto-adjudication, fraud analytics and billing review to keep treatment costs and claim payouts down.
- **Multi-channel distribution** — The sales machine itself, which management calls a deliberate strategic choice: agency (~30%), bank corporate agents (~21%), other corporate agents (~9%), brokers including digital players like Policy Bazaar (~27%) and direct-to-consumer (~13%), expanded in FY26 by a net 58,000 agents, 50-plus brokers and 23 financial institutions.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
