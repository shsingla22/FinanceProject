*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That weakened:**

- **Toll Roads**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Distribution take is a small slice (88 bps gross yield) of a customer's asset base and sticky once an SIP is set, but the last 12 months show pricing set by SEBI/GST rather than by Prudent — TER-to-BER reset, removal of 5 bps exit load, back-book repricing — which is the opposite of a supplier that customers pay without negotiating. (The long view had said: Prudent's share of the investor's cost is embedded in a regulated TER and customers rarely switch platforms once assets are mobilised, but the take is ~88-91bps of AUM — not a trivial 1-2% of the customer's cost base — and pricing is set by AMCs/SEBI rather than by oligopoly discipline.)

(The long view had said: Prudent/Fundzbazar has 10-year platform heritage and strong pull with distributors (5,100 partners added in FY26, 600/month in Q1FY27), but this is trade reputation rather than consumer brand — there is no A&P spend, premium pricing or category stretch under a consumer name.)

Why now: Fundzbazar is a direct-to-retail channel alongside the distributor base, and 30 new branches are being added in FY27, but no own-channel share of AUM or revenue is disclosed in these four calls, so the extent of forward integration cannot be sized. (The long view had said: Prudent owns the direct-to-investor Fundzbazar channel (~11% of AUM) and keeps expanding its physical branch network (16 branches in Q1FY25, ~30 planned in FY27), and is now weighing its own MF-only PMS licence — but it explicitly rules out becoming a manufacturer.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
