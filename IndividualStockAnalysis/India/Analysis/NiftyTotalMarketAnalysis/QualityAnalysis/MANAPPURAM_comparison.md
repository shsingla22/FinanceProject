### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 2 worsened, 5 unchanged. Financial resilience: Unknown on the long view, Unknown on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: A gold loan is a largely undifferentiated product where the borrower can compare rate and LTV directly, and management's response to competition is explicitly price-led — introducing schemes at 'comprehensive prices' in Q1 and running repeated price sensitivity tests in Q3 to set next year's scheme mix — which is the classic signature of a premium being competed away, though margins have so far held. (The long view had said: A gold loan is an undifferentiated commodity product and the calls show the premium eroding steadily: yields fell from 25-26% (Q1 FY22) to 21.7% (2022) to 18.3% (FY26), teaser schemes were reintroduced by the industry, and management ultimately conceded it could no longer price above peers, benchmarking instead to 'whatever is the yield in the market.' Borrowers show little brand loyalty and switch on rate, exactly the Nobel Biocare dynamic of a simple product losing its price premium to adequate cheaper rivals.)

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: Across all four FY26 calls management explicitly ties AUM growth and demand to the gold price — consolidated gold AUM rose 99.1% year-on-year in Q4 FY26 while gold tonnage (the real volume measure) rose only 2.8% year-on-year in Q3, so the book is being inflated by a commodity price the company does not control; simultaneously the microfinance cycle drove a consolidated loss in Q4 FY25 and Rs.156-168 crore quarterly losses at Asirvad through FY26, and FY26 consolidated PAT still fell 17.5% year-on-year despite the gold boom. (The long view had said: AUM is heavily levered to the gold price rather than volume — Q4 FY26 gold AUM rose 99.1% Y-o-Y while tonnage grew only ~3-4% Q-o-Q and 2.8% Y-o-Y in Q3, so the balance sheet inflates and deflates with a commodity; separately the microfinance credit cycle swung Asirvad from ~4% ROA to INR156 crore quarterly losses and drove consolidated PAT down 17.5% in FY26. But the demand base is operations-linked working capital for farmers, small businesses and households, and management argues it is countercyclical.)
- **New Entrants**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: Competitive pressure is flagged defensively in every call of the window — growth reported 'in spite of heightened competition' (Q1), 'the lending environment remains competitive' (Q3) and 'competition remained elevated' (Q4) — and the RBI's harmonised gold loan rules are themselves described as creating a 'level playing field for all regulated entities', i.e. banks and other regulated lenders moving into what was an NBFC niche, with management responding through pricing schemes. (The long view had said: Competitive intensity is repeatedly discussed defensively — banks lending at 90% LTV, fintechs, IIFL's return in 2024, and 'the lending environment remains competitive' in FY26 — and the response was an explicit price cut: Manappuram abandoned premium pricing and repriced to market, with gold yields falling from ~21-25% in FY22-23 to 18.3%. That is margin investment as a competitive response, though the entrants are conventional lenders rather than a structurally new format.)

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Technological Innovation (LOW); Stakeholder Concentration (LOW); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Unknown on the long view (none of the stress checks could be computed.) vs Unknown on the one-year view (none of the stress checks could be computed.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 1 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
