### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 5 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 4. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 4 weakened, 4 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → STRONG FIT. Why now: Above-industry domestic growth claimed and delivered every quarter of FY26 (9.4%, 10.6%, 15%, 10% FY) and Canada growth attributed to share gains in all four calls, with international up 22% for FY26 on new registrations rather than acquisitions alone. (The long view had said: Share gain is an explicit, repeatedly-tracked target — 'grow ahead of industry by about 200 basis points' from Call 1, delivered as 15% domestic growth in Q3 FY26 vs IPM, with Canada growth attributed to market share gains in every call and international expansion via locally-acquired platforms (Canada, UK/Europe, emerging markets).)

**That weakened:**

- **Innovation Dominance**: STRONG FIT → LIKELY FIT. Why now: A named, dated pipeline with visible revenue impact recurs each call — Amphotericin B ramping Europe (+22.7% Q2, +29.6% Q3), Bevacizumab biosimilar, Gilead Lenacapavir licence, ADCs, 7 commercialised biotherapeutics — but R&D spend is never quantified and 61% gross margins are ordinary for the sector, so the 'high margins fund outsized R&D' loop isn't evidenced. (The long view had said: R&D is disclosed and rising (INR383.5 crore, 4.2% of revenue, +50bps in FY26) alongside a named product cadence every year — chirality in cardiac, seven commercialised biotherapeutics, Amphotericin B in Europe, Bevacizumab for wet AMD, Asparaginase, Lenacapavir — plus a Scientific Advisory Committee steering complex injectables, biosimilars and ADCs.)
- **Corporate Culture**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Founder/family-led with candid disclosure of bad news (Zuventus attrition causing a soft Q4, rising debt, Labour Code hit) and FY26 revenue guidance beaten at 16.6%; against that, the 150bps base-margin guidance came in at 'over 100 basis points' and the earlier debt-free-by-FY26 aim was missed. (The long view had said: Founder-family led with long tenure (Satish Mehta 30 years, Samit/Namita Mehta as directors), promises made on early calls were later delivered — FY26 revenue and the committed 75–100bps margin expansion both exceeded — and bad news is disclosed promptly and quantified, e.g. the Zuventus restructuring attrition that cut Q4 domestic growth to 5% and the multi-quarter FCM drag.)
- **Low Price Plus**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: the calls are silent and no computable check supports the pattern. (The long view had said: There are cost/turns traits — India-led manufacturing, operating leverage, MR productivity rising from INR5.4 lakh to INR7 lakh in two years, and 80–110bps of margin expansion — but no low-price positioning, itemised savings culture, or passing savings to customers; Emcure competes on differentiation, not price.)
- **Pricing Power**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: No price-led growth is described in the last 12 months; margin gains are attributed to mix, operating leverage and productivity, and the flagship FY26 launch needed a price cut to get traction (Q4 FY26 call). (The long view had said: India growth is split roughly 4% price / 4% volume, which is steady branded-generic pricing, but the record also contains genuine deflation — FCM going off-patent dragged domestic growth for a year-plus, institutional pricing was lost to competitors, and the flagship Poviztra needed an April 2026 price cut to lift uptake.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Friendly Middleman (STRONG FIT); Brand Strength (LIKELY FIT); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

**That worsened:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: This is the structural exposure: Emcure is largely a branded-generic house where the prescribing doctor, not the patient, controls choice and brand awareness at the end-customer is low — and the Poviztra episode is the mechanism in live action, with cheaper synthetic generics arriving and management both defending the premium on origin/data/device and cutting price within months of launch. ## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
