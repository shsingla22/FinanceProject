# Welspun Corp Ltd. (WELCORP) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

*Industry: METALS*

Welspun Corp makes large steel pipes and sells them mainly to oil & gas companies and government water agencies, which use them to move natural gas, crude oil and drinking or irrigation water over long distances. It earns money by winning bulk supply contracts (an order book of roughly INR 25,750 crore, about $2.7 billion, as of Jul 2026) and manufacturing the pipe in its own mills, so revenue follows project awards rather than steady retail sales; FY26 revenue was about INR 17,000 crore with EBITDA of about INR 2,371 crore and PAT of INR 1,613 crore. It operates from three main geographies — India, the United States (Little Rock plant, where management claims 33-35% market share) and Saudi Arabia through its associate company East Pipes (EPIC) — plus smaller Indian businesses in specialty steel, building materials and plastic products. Demand drivers it cites are US LNG exports and gas-fired power for AI data centres, Saudi oil, gas and desalination infrastructure under Vision 2030, and Indian government water schemes like Jal Jeevan Mission, which had turned weak by FY27.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Line pipes (Oil & Gas) - India, USA and Saudi Arabia** — Large-diameter welded and coated steel pipes (HSAW, ERW, including a first Indian API X65 hydrogen-grade pipe) sold to pipeline and energy companies; this is the core and biggest business, with the US described as the pivot for growth and plants booked through FY28, and single orders as large as INR 5,000 crore.
- **Ductile Iron (DI) pipes** — Iron pipes for municipal and irrigation water supply in India, sold largely off government schemes; capacity was being raised from 500,000 to 600,000 tonnes with an order book above 300,000 tonnes (~INR 2,500 crore) in 2024, but by Jul 2026 management scaled it down on weak funding and overcapacity and switched the furnace output toward exporting pig iron.
- **Welspun Specialty Steel (WSSL) - stainless steel bars and seamless pipes** — A small but higher-value unit making stainless bars and seamless/specialty pipes for thermal and nuclear power, defence, aerospace and petrochemicals; volumes are in the low thousands of tonnes with an order book around INR 300 crore, and management stresses it is the only fully integrated player (own steelmaking, rolling and pipe making).
- **Sintex (plastic products and pipes)** — Consumer-facing plastic water tanks, pipes, fittings and OPVC sold through a dealer and distribution network in India; revenue grew 14% in Q1 FY25 but by FY27 it was hurt by the same weak Indian spending, with the company still investing in capacity and dealers ahead of a rebound.
- **Building materials / Long steel products (TMT bars, blast furnace and pig iron)** — TMT reinforcement bars and pig iron made from its own blast furnace and sinter plant commissioned in 2022, aimed at Indian infrastructure demand; TMT volumes grew about 175% in Q1 FY25, and by Jul 2026 the unit had pivoted to being one of India's largest pig iron exporters.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
