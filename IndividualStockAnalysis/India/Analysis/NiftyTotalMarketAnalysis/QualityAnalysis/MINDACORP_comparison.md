### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 2 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 1. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 2 weakened, 5 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → STRONG FIT. Why now: Persistent, quantified outgrowth in all four calls — revenue +16% vs industry +1.9% (Q1), +19% vs +10.1% (Q2), +25% vs +16.8% (Q3), +29% vs +20% (Q4) — driven by explicitly higher share of business with existing customers, new customer additions and content per vehicle, and won without buying it: EBITDA margin expanded 29bps and FY26 PAT rose 40% on 22% revenue growth. (The long view had said: Persistent, repeated outperformance of the industry across the whole period: Q1 FY23 outperformed industry production, Q3 FY26 revenue +25% vs industry +17%, Q4 FY26 +29% vs +20%, FY26 +22.3%, with management attributing it to increased share of business, content per vehicle and new customers, and naming 2W/3W/CV wiring harness as the share-gain segments. Critically, the share was not bought — EBITDA margin rose from 10.6% (Q1 FY23) to 11.9% and PAT margin from 5.2% to 7.3% over the same span.)
- **Pricing Power**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Premiumisation is a stated growth pillar in every call and mix/content-per-vehicle lifted margins each quarter (11.3% → 11.6% → 11.8% → 11.9%), but management frames pricing as commodity pass-through rather than price increases above cost inflation; no volume-inelastic price rises, scarcity or waiting lists are cited. (The long view had said: Management states explicitly that commodity escalation (copper, aluminium, zinc up 30-40% in FY26) is passed through with no profit element and a quarter's lag; margin gains came from mix and operating efficiency, not price rises above cost inflation. Gross margin has been roughly flat (~37%) since FY25.)

**That weakened:**

- **Corporate Culture**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Family-promoter led (Ashok Minda chairman, Aakash Minda ED) with promoters injecting Rs.105 crores of warrant money used to cut debt, a long-term Vision 2030, an employee ESOP scheme, rising dividend, and a promise visibly delivered within the window — the long-awaited sunroof order flagged in Q2 after repeated waiting; disclosure of the Rs.4 crore labour-law exceptional item is a small mark of candour, but within a one-year lens there is no instance of management owning a miss or a shortfall. (The long view had said: Promoter-family led with long-tenured leadership (Ashok Minda then Aakash Minda through the whole period), promoter infusing INR 104 crores via warrants used to repay debt, ESOP 2025 for employees, and an explicit record of promises kept across calls: the ~10% to a sustained 11.5% EBITDA commitment was delivered, the HCMF LOI (Q3 FY24) became a signed JV and then sunroof orders, R&D and patent targets were met, and ROCE is the stated yardstick (22% today, 25% by 2030). Caveats: the FY23 decision to stop disclosing total order book reduced transparency, and the sunroof plant slipped from a Q1 FY26 commissioning to Q1 FY27 SOP.)
- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The four calls discuss OEM order books, content per vehicle and an 'aftermarket' vertical, but never attach rates, renewal rates, AMC/licence streams, deferred revenue or customer advances as a funding source — the evidence is silent on recurring-revenue mechanics. (The long view had said: There is a genuine replacement stream — aftermarket is a steady 10-12% of revenue and management splits order wins as "50% of new orders and 50% of replacement orders" on 4-5 year platform lifetimes — but across all 16 calls there is no license/subscription/AMC revenue, no attach or renewal rates, and no deferred revenue or customer advances funding the business (it is a working-capital-consuming Tier-1 with ~INR 1,100 crores gross debt).)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (NO FIT); Forward Integrators (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Brand Strength (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
