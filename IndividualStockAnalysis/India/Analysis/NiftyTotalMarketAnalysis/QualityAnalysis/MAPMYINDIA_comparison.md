### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 3 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 4. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 3 weakened, 6 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: The Mappls app reached 45mn+ downloads (10mn in FY26 alone) and management treats it as proof of technology leadership that pulls B2B customers, but there is no sustained A&P spend disclosure or evidence of premium pricing accompanying share gain. (The long view had said: There is genuine heritage (map-building from 1995) and a consumer brand emerging — Mappls app at 45m downloads and ~100m MAU by FY26 — but A&P spend is small and lumpy (advertisement fell from INR3.87cr to INR1.57cr in a single quarter in FY23) and the consumer app remains unmonetised, with management deflecting the monetisation question in Feb-2026.)
- **Cost to Replicate**: PARTIAL → STRONG FIT. Why now: The barrier is articulable and concrete: decades of proprietary Indian ground-truth map data now embedded as the platform for the government's own national geospatial system with Survey of India, reinforced by defense/PSU trust and the 'Owned in India' indigenisation preference — a well-funded copycat cannot rebuild the data or that sovereign positioning in a few years. (The long view had said: There is a concrete replication barrier articulated repeatedly — 30 years of proprietary field data collection, the only Indian firm doing both data acquisition and the platform, plus geospatial regulation favouring Indian ownership and 'Owned in India' government preference — but the moat has not converted into pricing or growth outperformance, and well-funded entrants (Ola Maps, Google) appeared within a single year.)

**That weakened:**

- **Innovation Dominance**: STRONG FIT → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Innovation is clearly regular and self-funded at 44-46% EBITDA margins — HD maps for autonomous driving, digital twin via Mappls DT, the mGIS platform, embedded AI — but no R&D spend is disclosed and FY26 revenue was near-flat (INR463cr to INR474cr), so innovation did not translate into volume or pricing gains this year. (The long view had said: Across all four sampled years management names a rolling pipeline of self-funded products with revenue attached — RealView/3D metaverse maps and drone digital twins (2022), e-horizon/ADAS/EV range prediction and AI analytics-consulting (2024), AI-infused products and GIS-led platform (2026) — funded off 40%+ EBITDA margins and INR120cr of FY26 organic capital allocation including IP creation.)
- **Pricing Power**: LIKELY FIT → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: No price increases, premiumisation or elasticity are discussed in any of the four calls; margin commentary is about mix and operating leverage, not price. (The long view had said: Pricing-for-value is described consistently across calls and they refused to follow Google/Ola price cuts while holding 35-45% EBITDA margins, but across 18 calls there is no instance of explicit price increases driving revenue, and FY26 revenue grew ~1% (INR463cr to INR474cr) with A&M growth (15%) trailing auto production (18%).)
- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Order-book wins expanded into new arenas (Zepto/quick commerce, Survey of India, IOCL, DMRC MoU) with INR780cr of new orders booked, yet no explicit market-share figures versus named competitors are given and FY26 revenue grew only ~2%, so share gain is asserted through wins rather than demonstrated. (The long view had said: Management asserts it will be the 'disproportionate winner' and is the only surviving Indian map player, but never discloses share numbers, and the later record cuts against it — FY26 A&M grew 15% against 18% industry auto production, C&E declined 3%, and full-year revenue grew ~1%.)

*Unchanged: Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 3 eased, 0 worsened, 1 unchanged; 4 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Revenue is tied to customers' project and programme cycles rather than a commodity cycle, but it swings hard: FY26 grew only ~1-2% (INR463 crores to INR474 crores) against a 24% five-year CAGR, Q3 was weak on customer-requested delivery deferrals, then Q4 rose 54.8% sequentially with EBITDA up 141%. Management itself insists the business must be judged yearly, not quarterly, which concedes the lumpiness. (The long view had said: A&M revenue tracks auto OEM production and new model go-lives (Q3 FY26: industry production +18% vs A&M +15%), and B2B/government order execution makes quarters lumpy — FY26 revenue was near-flat (INR463cr to INR474cr) with EBITDA margin swinging from 46% (Q1 FY23) to a weak Q3 FY26 and back to 44.6% in Q4. But the underlying demand is operations-linked (map licences per vehicle, APIs consumed in customers' daily transactions, IoT SaaS annuities), not customer capex equipment.)
- **Technological Innovation**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: An analyst explicitly asked whether recent Anthropic AI launches hurt or help the business, and management answered defensively that they 'embraced AI much earlier'; the company is simultaneously having to re-platform from 2D/3D SD maps to live HD maps for autonomous driving. Mapping is a high-innovation field where an incumbent's data format and installed platform can become legacy, but the calls show a steady, incremental innovation cadence rather than a disrupted franchise. (The long view had said: The company sits in a high-innovation industry where the platform can shift under it: analysts pressed on Google's street-view entry (Aug 2022), Ola Maps and Google Maps pricing/product moves (Aug 2024), and by Feb 2026 an analyst asked directly whether Anthropic/AI launches threaten the business — management answered defensively and reframed AI as something they 'embraced much earlier'. Its own installed formats (2D maps, embedded head units, GPS trackers) must be continuously re-platformed to 3D/HD/4D/e-horizon/ADAS, which is capital- and time-intensive.)
- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Results in this window are visibly driven by a handful of named large contracts — the INR233 crores e-commerce order, IOCL's INR110 crores 23,000-truck deployment, Survey of India, Zepto (backed by a INR25 crores equity investment to cement the relationship) — and analysts questioned each individually. Order-book conversion of only ~18% and the admission that a few very large opportunities could swing the outcome show lumpy, relationship-dependent revenue, though the base is spread across automotive OEMs, government and corporates. (The long view had said: A small number of named relationships repeatedly move the numbers — the Hyundai-Kia programme (an INR400+ crore contract with an old-programme ramp-down visibly denting a quarter), and the Ola relationship which deteriorated into a legal notice for breach of contract in 2024. In Q4 FY26 the ~INR45-50cr revenue shortfall was attributed to 'maybe 5 accounts or 6 accounts', which is a candid admission of how few names drive a quarter.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 6 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
