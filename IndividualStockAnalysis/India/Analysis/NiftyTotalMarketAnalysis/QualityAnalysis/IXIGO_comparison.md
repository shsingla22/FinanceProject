Now: Profit margins have barely moved over the years (±2.1 points) — consistent with rational pricing and few new competitors. And from the calls: Pricing behaviour is turning rational — ixigo moderated discounting and improved contribution margin while publicly disowning a price war — but the category still requires marketing intensity and capital-rich entrants like Prosus-backed platforms can fund aggression. | The long view had said: Profit margins swing by ±48.5 points — a hint of price wars, cost shocks, or new entrants disturbing the industry. And from the calls: Barriers are low and getting lower — take rates are competed down, growth in buses is explicitly chosen over margin, and management itself warns AI platforms could de-distribute incumbent travel apps.
- **Profit margin** (Return on Capital): Poor → Neutral. Now: Out of every ₹100 of sales, ₹5 is operating profit. Margins have moved about ±2.1 points a year — very steady, a sign costs are under control. | The long view had said: Out of every ₹100 of sales, ₹5 is operating profit. - **Partial monopoly / broken competition / switching costs** (Industry Structure): Neutral → Good. ### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 3 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 5. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 3 strengthened, 2 weakened, 6 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: There is no licence, AMC or subscription stream, but ixigo does monetise an ancillary 'peace of mind' layer attached to each booking, with a disclosed attach rate and rising take rates (Q4 FY26, Q2 FY26); it is attach-at-point-of-sale rather than a recurring post-sale annuity, and no deferred revenue / customer-float funding is discussed. (The long view had said: There is attach-rate language and a large repeat base — Q4 FY26 value-added-services attach rate over 31%, 85.8% of FY25 transactions from repeat bookers, and paid VAS like ixigo Assured, Travel Guarantee and Price Lock — but these are per-transaction fees, not renewing licences/AMCs, and no deferred revenue or customer-advance funding is ever discussed.)
- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → STRONG FIT. Why now: Deliberate brand building since 2023 with sustained A&P (Rohit Sharma, RCB, CSK/TNPL, multi-year NDLS metro station branding), India's highest-rated travel apps with 5mn+ ratings, and rising 'ixigo' brand searches on Google Trends — brand is being converted into share gain plus better conversion, exactly the pattern's signature. (The long view had said: Sustained and rising A&P (brand marketing INR21.4cr FY23 to INR55cr FY24, Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli, Mahesh Babu, IPL with RCB/CSK, multi-year NDLS metro station branding), a three-brand portfolio at 4.8 Play Store ratings, and adjacency launches under the same names; but the brands are ~18 years old at most and confer share gain without any premium pricing.)
- **Pricing Power**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Q2 FY26 flight revenue grew 60% on 29% GTV growth 'despite some moderation in our discounting', and Q4 FY26 showed sequentially better contribution margin — take rates rising via ancillaries rather than headline price increases; no explicit price-rise-without-volume-loss or scarcity language, and the category remains discount-competitive. (The long view had said: The trajectory is margin dilution, not pricing power: flight take rates were guided down to 7%–7.5% (Jul 2024), Travel Guarantee was explicitly run at lower margin, trains contribution margin fell from 34% to 31% (Q4 FY25), and group contribution margin percentage slid to ~36% by FY26 — premiumisation described is customer mix/wallet shift, not price increases ixigo takes.)

**That weakened:**

- **Friendly Middleman**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Sales are direct B2C through ixigo/ConfirmTkt/AbhiBus apps, driven by organic product-led growth, app ratings and consumer brand campaigns — no prescriber, installer or trade professional stands between the company and the paying traveller. (The long view had said: The July 2024 call launched travelsupermall.com, a B2B2C platform distributing inventory through offline travel agents, and the group leans on partner funnels (PhonePe, Meesho, Rapido, Uber, ChatGPT); but there is no trade-training programme and the later calls show demand driven by ixigo's own consumer brands and apps, not by recommending professionals.)
- **Forward Integrators**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: ixigo already owns the customer relationship; the integration described runs the other way — toward supply (direct hotel onboarding via HELLO, 26 airlines on NDC pipes, GDS tie-ups, 17 SRTCs on AbhiBus) — so the forward-integrating-manufacturer pattern does not apply. (The long view had said: The excerpts are silent on this pattern in its manufacturer sense; if anything the movement is backward toward supply (NDC pipes with 26 airlines, GDS deals, the HELLO hotels extranet and direct hotel onboarding) while distribution increasingly runs through third-party apps, so there is no own-store/franchise/D2C-share evidence to judge.)

*Unchanged: Innovation Dominance (STRONG FIT); Market Share Gainers (STRONG FIT); Corporate Culture (STRONG FIT); Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 0 worsened, 6 unchanged. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Every one of the four calls opens with an exogenous demand shock — Operation Sindoor airspace closure and the AI 171 crash in Q1, a domestic market that contracted 2% Y-o-Y in Q2, the DGCA FDTL-driven cancellations in Q3, and the Middle East air corridor plus the Maha Kumbh base effect in Q4 — so volumes and mix swing hard even though the company is not commodity- or customer-capex-linked. (The long view had said: Management itself frames flights in cycle language — airline capacity constraints, groundings and high fares compressing take rates and market growth (domestic market grew only 1.2% in Q4 FY26) — and results swing with one-off events (Maha Kumbh created a high base, Pahalgam/Middle East conflicts hit bookings). But the demand is consumer operating spend, not customer capex, with 85.8% repeat bookers and a multimodal mix that cushions each shock.)
- **Technological Innovation**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Management itself frames the moment as an 'AI inflection point' and raised INR1,296 crores partly to fund AI-led growth, and the group's apps are now distributed as native apps inside ChatGPT — an acknowledgment that discovery may migrate to assistant platforms the company does not own; equally, airline NDC pipes shift direct-connect economics. (The long view had said: Across FY26 calls management repeatedly frames AI as an existential industry reset — 'the slow death of software', 'most existing software cannot survive this inversion', and explicit worry about 'de-distribution' as users take screenshots to external AI platforms — and has rebuilt the company (NewCo, ixigo NEXT) around it. That is a high-innovation-rate industry where the incumbent's app-and-funnel infrastructure is the thing at risk.)

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); New Entrants (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 1 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
