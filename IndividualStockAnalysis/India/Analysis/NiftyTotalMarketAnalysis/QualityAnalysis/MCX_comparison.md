### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 4 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 4. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 1 weakened, 4 unchanged; 4 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Toll Roads**: LIKELY FIT → STRONG FIT. Why now: Exchange fees are a negligible fraction of the notional value hedged yet the platform is essential infrastructure for price discovery and risk management, anchored in SEBI regulation and warehouse/delivery rules; management repeatedly frames MCX as the benchmark venue for Indian commodity risk transfer (Q1 FY26, Q4 FY26 'Price in India: Hedge in India'). (The long view had said: The transaction fee is a trivial fraction of the notional traded yet the exchange is indispensable infrastructure for hedging and price discovery, anchored by SEBI regulation, warehouse/delivery standards and RBI's domestic-gold-hedging mandate; the structure is a rational near-duopoly where MCX's bullion franchise held share even after a large competitor attacked it (Q4 FY26).)
- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: The last four calls discuss transaction-linked revenue (ADT, turnover, traded UCCs) but never attach rates, AMC/licence renewals, deferred revenue or customer advances as a funding source, so the evidence is silent on a recurring post-sale stream. (The long view had said: the calls are silent and the numbers are mixed (2 of 3 checks supporting).)

**That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Participation is clearly widening — ADT up from ~INR2 lakh crore to INR7.5 lakh crore, new members, rising traded UCCs, FPIs at 2-3% of ADT and 'double-digit' of energy — and management sees 'headroom', but no explicit market-share figures versus named competitors are given in these calls. (The long view had said: MCX has defended rather than visibly gained share — bullion share 'untouched' against a large competitor's product blitz — while growth came from turnover (ADT up ~2.5x in FY26) and participant expansion (UCCs +64%, FPIs 2-3% of ADT); explicit rising share numbers versus named rivals are not presented.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Pricing Power (QUANT SIGNAL); Brand Strength (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (NO FIT); Forward Integrators (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Cost to Replicate (STRONG FIT); Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 8 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
