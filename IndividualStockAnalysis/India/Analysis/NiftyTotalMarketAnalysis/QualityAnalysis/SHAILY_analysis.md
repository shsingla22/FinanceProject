# Shaily (SHAILY) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Shaily Engineering Plastics is an India-based contract manufacturer that moulds precision plastic components and assembles finished products for global customers, processing roughly 5,500-6,500 tonnes of polymer a quarter across its plants. It works largely on an outsourced-manufacturing basis for big overseas brands — home-furnishings and FMCG companies, pharmaceutical firms, and industrial/automotive customers — with about 68-80% of revenue coming from exports, and it bills on a formula that passes raw-material price moves through to customers plus a manufacturing margin. Increasingly it also sells its own patented drug-delivery devices (pen and auto-injectors, including the spring-driven Shaily Neo and Shaily Harmony pens) for insulin, GLP-1 drugs like Semaglutide and Tirzepatide, and other therapies, which earns much higher margins than plain moulding. Group revenue was about INR 754 crores in the first nine months of FY26 (up 32%) with EBITDA margins near 29%, and it is building capacity in India plus a new AED 130-150 million (about INR 300-350 crore) pen-injector plant in Abu Dhabi due by Q4 FY28.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Consumer** — Makes plastic parts and assembled products for home-furnishings and FMCG brands, plus toys — historically the biggest segment (INR 511 crores, roughly half of FY26 revenue) but shrinking, down 9% in FY26 on weak European and US furniture demand, and now being extended into consumer-electronics precision components and semiconductor trays for a Korean customer.
- **Healthcare** — Designs, gets approved and manufactures drug-delivery devices — mainly pen injectors and auto-injectors, plus contract manufacturing of medical devices such as dry powder inhalers — for pharma companies, generics makers and contract manufacturers; it grew 139% to INR 393 crores in FY26 (40% of revenue, up from 21%) and is the company's main growth engine, with capacity going from 80 million to about 150 million pens a year.
- **Industrial** — Supplies engineered plastic components to automotive customers and, more recently, power-tool and LED-lighting makers; the smallest segment at INR 87 crores in FY26 but growing fast (up 41% for the year, 60% in Q4).
- **Shaily UK (IP / device development arm)** — A UK-based development operation set up around 2021 that creates the company's own intellectual property in drug-delivery devices — including human-factors engineering capability to serve large pharma — and feeds the patented device designs that the Healthcare segment then manufactures.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
