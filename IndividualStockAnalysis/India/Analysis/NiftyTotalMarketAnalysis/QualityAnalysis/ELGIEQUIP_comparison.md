### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 5 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 3. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 3 weakened, 7 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Management asserts a quality/performance reputation built over decades and talks about continuing to 'grow the brand', but the last-12-month calls show no sustained A&P disclosure, no premium pricing realisation (pricing contribution was negligible) and no brand-led adjacency stretch. (The long view had said: There is a consistent brand promise ('ELGi always better'), a 1960 heritage story and reliability/efficiency positioning, but management is candid that outside India the brand is a weakness needing funding — the Mar 2026 call attributes below-target US distribution sales to 'our brand being less well known requires a lot more investment at the top of the funnel'.)

**That weakened:**

- **Forward Integrators**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: ELGi owns two acquired US distribution businesses (Patton's, Michigan Air) plus Patton's Medical, and pivoted the UAE from a channel-only model to direct sales — genuine movement toward the customer — though distribution operations were the weakest US vertical through FY26 and no store/franchise/D2C economics are disclosed. (The long view had said: A consistent multi-year move down the channel: acquisition and ownership of distribution businesses (Patton's, Michigan Air in the US; Pulford in Australia) covering direct equipment sales plus multi-brand service, and an explicit pivot from channel-only to direct sales in the UAE and in key European markets, with the Mar 2026 call stating the strategic logic outright.)
- **Pricing Power**: LIKELY FIT → NO FIT. Why now: FY26 growth came from ~3-4% volume plus forex with pricing contributing almost nothing, and the FY27 price action is explicitly a 2.5-3% cost-recovery correction whose demand impact management admits it cannot predict — cost pass-through, not pricing above inflation; the Middle East also required price corrections after 'pricing challenges'. (The long view had said: Price increases are taken regularly and passed through — 12% of the 42% Q1FY23 growth was price, tariffs were 'successfully navigated' via price increases in FY26, and 2.5-3% correction was already in the market by the Jun 2026 call — but management repeatedly admits under-recovery of commodity inflation ('we have recovered only 40% of that'), FY26 pricing was 'hardly much' with volume growth only 3-4%, and Chinese price deflation is pressuring the low-kW segment.)
- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Repeated evidence of share capture — converting US distributors from competition, record North American revenue, Patton's Medical expanding into California and the Northeast, Demand=Match traction, and India go-to-market winning new accounts — but no explicit share percentages versus named competitors, and Europe, Australia and Southeast Asia went the other way. (The long view had said: Share gain is claimed and repeated across regions and years — improved share across all Indian product segments with 17% ISAAME CAGR and 22% Europe CAGR over five years (Feb 2025), share grown in de-growing European markets and distributors converted from competitors in the US (Feb 2026) — and it is gained through product and reach investment rather than price, though Europe and US Portables show the gains are uneven.)

*Unchanged: Corporate Culture (STRONG FIT); Innovation Dominance (LIKELY FIT); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
