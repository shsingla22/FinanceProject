### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 2 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 0. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 0 strengthened, 3 weakened, 6 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Revenue growth ran well ahead of industry volumes in Q1 FY26 (16% vs 2% industry production growth) and stayed at 16–18% through the year with new export wins, implying content and share gain, but management never cites explicit market-share numbers or named-competitor comparisons. (The long view had said: Persistent, quantified outperformance of industry volumes in every year of the record — 59% vs 37% industry in Q1 FY23, 20% and 18% revenue growth against a strong FY26 industry, 2-wheeler switch growth of 26-30% on rising share of business, lighting share gains, and a new 2-wheeler lighting order worth ~INR 450 crores (25% of current 2W lighting revenue) explicitly to raise share of business.)
- **Corporate Culture**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Disclosure quality is good — Q1 FY26 stripped out Rs 69 crores of prior-period incentive income to give a like-for-like normalized number rather than flattering the headline — and the promoter family plus a long-serving CFO front every call; but the FY26 excerpts show no owned mistakes, no return-on-capital metric, and rising borrowings to fund capex and working capital. (The long view had said: Exceptionally stable leadership (Nirmal Minda as Chairman/CMD and Sunil Bohra as Group CFO presenting all 16 calls), guidance delivered as promised (11% ± 50bps margin held from FY23 through FY26; Bawal, Supa, Khed, Kharkhoda and Indonesia capacities commissioned on the timelines announced years earlier), and candour about bad news — the FY26 call openly flagged alloy wheel penetration softness and customers switching back to steel wheels, and the INR 28 crore labour-code hit was disclosed separately rather than buried.)
- **Toll Roads**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: No certification, standards or 'critical tiny cost' language appears; instead management describes absorbing annual cost escalations and volatile input costs at a roughly flat ~10.7–11.3% EBITDA margin, which is the opposite of toll-road price indifference. (The long view had said: Products are designed into a model and stay for its life, and regulatory anchoring repeatedly creates captive demand (AVAS mandate from Oct 2026, side/curtain airbags via TG, LED transition), but the components are a meaningful share of vehicle cost (INR 90,000-95,000 kit value) and pricing is actively negotiated with OEMs rather than accepted without question.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL).*

*Unchanged: Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Brand Strength (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 14 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
