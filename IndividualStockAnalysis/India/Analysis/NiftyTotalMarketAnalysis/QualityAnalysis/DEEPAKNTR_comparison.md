### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 1 worsened, 3 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: The products are chemical intermediates bought on price by industrial customers, and cheaper underpriced imports have compressed realisations all year — management's defence is cost and integration, not brand or a defensible premium, which is the classic good-enough dynamic. Exposure is bounded rather than high because the Phenolics franchise held and expanded share on scale and integration economics (EBIT margin 20% in Q4 FY26), suggesting genuine scale protection rather than a premium being competed away. (The long view had said: A meaningful part of the portfolio is standardised, substitutable chemistry (phenol, acetone, IPA, sodium nitrite, intermediates) where buyers switch on price — management repeatedly cites 'persistently underpriced product availability from China' and 'aggressive import competition' compressing AI realizations, and defends position through cost and integration rather than any brand or specification premium. Bounded because Phenolics held EBIT margins of 15-20% on domestic logistics advantage and scale.)
- **Stakeholder Concentration**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: No single customer, licensor or supplier is described as driving results in any of the four calls; management repeatedly describes broadening the base — 'adding new customers through a diversification effort and into new geographies' and 'pivoting to non-traditional geographies'. On the supply side, the FY26 commissioning of nitric acid, nitration and hydrogenation assets is explicitly framed as cutting external dependency. (The long view had said: Single-customer dependence surfaced concretely in Q2 FY25 when one key customer could not absorb committed volumes, and European agrochemical customers as a bloc drove an AI-segment revenue decline; ~46% of standalone exports go to Europe. The polycarbonate project also concentrates on one licensor/asset seller (Trinseo) and one on-site gas partner (Praxair). Bounded because volumes were successfully redirected.)

**That worsened:**

- **New Entrants**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: Chinese producers operating with structurally different cost economics are named as the dominant competitive pressure in three of the four calls, and the Advanced Intermediates response — market-share defence, geographic pivots and 'aggressive optimization actions' — is exactly the margin investment of an incumbent under attack. The damage shows: AI EBIT fell to INR 15 crore on INR 652 crore of revenue in Q3 FY26 despite 18% volume-led revenue growth. (The long view had said: Chinese producers with structurally lower-cost, oversupplied capacity are discussed defensively call after call and have visibly reset Advanced Intermediates economics — Q3 FY26 AI revenue grew 18% while EBIT fell to INR15 crore (~2% margin). The response is cost leadership and integration rather than a new competitive format, and the pressure is import-based rather than a new business model.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (HIGH RISK); Government Dependency (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
