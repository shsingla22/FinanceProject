# Datamatic Global Ser (DATAMATICS) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Datamatics is a Mumbai-headquartered IT and outsourcing company that sells technology services, back-office processing and customer-service work to large enterprises, billing them under service contracts and, increasingly, licences for its own software products. Its work spans building and running digital systems (cloud, automation, analytics, AI, Salesforce), taking over clients' routine paperwork and data processing, and handling their customer support and experience programmes. It also builds and runs automated fare collection systems for metro rail projects in India, such as the Kolkata and NCRTC deals it says are worth about Rs. 350 crores. The United States is by far its biggest market at roughly 52-56% of revenue, followed by the UK and Europe at around 21-22%, India at 15-17% and the rest of the world at about 8-9%; quarterly revenue has grown from about Rs. 327 crores in mid-2022 to about Rs. 510 crores by the December 2025 quarter, and the company reports zero net debt with net cash and investments of around Rs. 540 crores.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Digital Operations** — Runs clients' back-office and data-processing work for them, delivered on Datamatics' own automation platforms; it is the largest and steadiest segment at roughly half of revenue (Rs. 273.8 crores of Rs. 510.1 crores in the December 2025 quarter, Rs. 694 crores for FY24) with the best profitability, EBIT margins of about 18-24%.
- **Digital Technologies** — Software and IT services work — cloud, DevOps, low-code/no-code, SaaS platforms, analytics and AI, plus the company's own 'Tru' product suite (TruBot robotic automation, TruCap+ document processing, TruAI, TruBI) and metro fare-collection platforms; it is about a third of revenue (Rs. 169.6 crores in the December 2025 quarter) and was the problem child for years, with margins that went negative in FY23 and only 0.7-4.6% in FY24 before recovering to around 10.8% by FY26.
- **Digital Experiences** — Manages customer contact and engagement across channels on clients' behalf, covering the whole customer lifecycle; it is the smallest segment at roughly 13-15% of revenue (Rs. 66.7 crores in the December 2025 quarter) and has been soft recently because two clients moved the work to their own in-house centres.
- **Dextara Digital (acquisition)** — A platinum-level Salesforce services provider bought for roughly Rs. 110 crores in FY24 to push further into the US market and 'hyperscaler' partnerships, aimed at the roughly 25% of Datamatics customers that already use Salesforce.
- **AI and product investment** — Not a reported segment but a stated spending line of about Rs. 40-50 crores a year on new technology, including partnerships with Microsoft and Google, a small language model and copilot of its own, Gen AI built into the automation products for over 30 customers, and a Google Gemini Enterprise rollout with 200 employees certified in one month.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
