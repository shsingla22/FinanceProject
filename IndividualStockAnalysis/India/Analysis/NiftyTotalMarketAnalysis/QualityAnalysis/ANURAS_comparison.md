### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 3 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 1. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 0 strengthened, 4 weakened, 6 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That weakened:**

- **Forward Integrators**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The Bliss GVS Pharma deal (May 2026 call) and Jayhawk both move the company downstream — from key starting materials toward finished dosage formulations and closer to end applications — but this is B2B vertical integration, with no own stores, franchising, royalties or D2C economics discussed. (The long view had said: No own stores, franchising or D2C; the forward move is industrial — the Jayhawk acquisition for onshore US manufacturing and the Bliss GVS stake to extend 'from key starting materials to finished dosage formulations' (May 2026), which gains customer proximity but is unproven, debt-funded (INR300cr NCDs) and not the shelf-space/mini-monopoly economics of the pattern.)
- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Evidence of geographic expansion with local presence (US onshore via Jayhawk, Japan at ~17% of Q3 revenue, multiple MNC customers added in FY26) and 84% 9M revenue growth, but no explicit market-share figures versus named competitors, and the top 10 customers still make up 81% of revenue. (The long view had said: Wallet-share and geographic gains are documented over the whole period — Japan from 1% of revenue in 2018 to 13% in Q1FY23 and ~17% by Q3FY26, plus new MNC customer additions — but management never cites market-share numbers versus named competitors, and the FY25 collapse (Q1FY25 revenue -34% YoY on agrochem destocking) shows growth remained macro-dependent.)
- **Low Price Plus**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Cost competitiveness is mentioned only as a backward-integration benefit from Tanfac, not as a cost culture with itemised savings; the economics are the opposite of the pattern — capital-heavy (INR315 crore capex in FY26 alone), ~250-day working capital, i.e. low asset turns rather than high. (The long view had said: There is a genuine, itemised cost programme — 12.5MW solar saving ~Rs 10cr/yr rising to Rs 14cr (Jul 2022), a Rs 59cr hybrid plant saving ~Rs 15cr (Aug 2024), and repeated 'cost optimization/process optimization down the cost curve' pitches to customers (Feb 2026) — but the economics are the opposite of the pattern: low asset turns (INR2,500cr gross block for ~INR1,676cr standalone revenue) and 250-day working capital, not high turns.)
- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: No heritage story, A&P spend, consumer brand or distributor leverage is discussed anywhere in the last-12-month calls; the business is contract manufacturing under customers' end products. (The long view had said: the calls are silent and the numbers are mixed (1 of 2 checks supporting).)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (QUANT SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Toll Roads (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 3 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Nothing in the calls shows management defending a price premium against cheaper substitutes or customers trading down; the products are custom-synthesised intermediates sold under multi-year LOIs to the innovator who specifies them, with backward integration into HF/KF via Tanfac supporting cost position rather than premium defence. (The long view had said: In the more commoditising lines the pitch is explicitly cost curve rather than differentiation — management defends its position against Chinese pricing dominance by citing cost and process optimisation as it scales, and blended EBITDA margins have drifted from 28% (Q1 FY23) to 22-23% (FY26) even as revenue reached a record, consistent with cheaper alternatives capping price.)

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: The last four calls are explicitly framed around a chemical/agrochemical cycle turning: management attributes the FY26 surge to industry recovery, destocking normalisation and agro offtake revival, and the swing itself is extreme (Q2 FY26 revenue +148.8% YoY, H1 +122%, then Q3 +31%, Q4 +26%), with EBITDA margin oscillating between ~19.5% and ~25.5% quarter to quarter. Agrochemicals were still 55% of standalone revenue in FY26, and FY26 followed several weak quarters — the classic peak/trough pattern where current growth and margins cannot be separated from cycle position. (The long view had said: The agrochem end-market cycle drove violent swings across the period: Q1 FY23 revenue +31% with 28% EBITDA margins, then Q1 FY25 revenue -34% YoY with margins down ~5 points to 23% on channel destocking, then 9M FY26 revenue +84% as agro recovered — sales and margins fluctuating exactly as the cyclicality channel describes, alongside a Rs. 670 crore capex programme committed at the top of the prior cycle and completed into the trough.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Fashion Risk (LOW); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Stakeholder Concentration (HIGH RISK); Government Dependency (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
