### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 0 worsened, 5 unchanged; 1 could not be compared. Financial resilience: Unknown on the long view, Unknown on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: Bank earnings visibly swing with the RBI rate cycle — domestic NIM slid 2.72% (Q2FY26) to 2.65% to 2.61% as the December rate cut repriced advances while deposit rates stayed sticky — and credit cost is at a cycle trough (negative in Q2FY26, GNPA 4.48%→2.78%, recovery 2.4x slippages), a level that flatters current profit and cannot persist indefinitely. Exposure is bounded because the book is operations-linked retail/agri/MSME lending rather than customer-capex equipment, and management is provisioning countercyclically. (The long view had said: Earnings swing violently with the credit and interest-rate cycle: Q1FY23 showed an operating-profit collapse from Rs.6,400cr to Rs.5,379cr driven by a Rs.1,409cr MTM loss on G-Secs and halved trading profit, while credit cost fell from 2.46% (Jun-22) to 0.33% (Jun-24) and GNPA from 14.33% (Jun-21) to 2.78% (Jun-26) — the FY24-FY27 record profits are the benign phase of a provisioning cycle, not a step-change in earning power. Q4FY26 again showed treasury income 'very subdued' as bond yields hardened, offset only by an actuarial AS-15 write-back.)
- **Shifting Consumer Preferences**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: The relevant preference migration in banking — customers moving from branch to digital — is one the bank has affirmatively pivoted into rather than been eroded by: 95%+ of transactions are digital, WhatsApp banking users nearly doubled, UPI transactions on PNB One up 53%, and management explicitly targets the under-30 cohort that is 35% of its customer base with digital-first products. (The long view had said: Customer behaviour has shifted structurally on two fronts the calls document: savings migrating out of low-cost CASA into term/market products (46.3%→37% over four years, the one guidance line management repeatedly missed), and servicing moving wholly to digital channels, forcing continued branch and workforce reinvestment.)

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Technological Innovation (WATCH); New Entrants (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (LOW).*

*Not comparable: Fashion Risk (NOT ASSESSED → LOW — could not be assessed on the long view).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
