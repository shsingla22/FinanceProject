**Checks that regressed:**

Now: Distribution reach is being actively built out with new sales, solution and large-deal coverage, but as a mid-tier vendor Mphasis shows no reach that larger rivals cannot match. | The long view had said: A strong go-to-market reach — client-mining pyramid, proactive deal engine and hyperscaler channel partnerships with AWS and Microsoft — supports consistent large-deal capture, though rivals of similar scale have comparable reach.

### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 5 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 3. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 3 weakened, 6 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Innovation Dominance**: LIKELY FIT → STRONG FIT. Why now: Every call in the window names newly launched proprietary platforms with revenue traction — NeoIP (Oct 2025), the NeoZeta/NeoSaBa/NeoRaina/NeoCrux suite (Jan 2026), Continuum AI via the Theory and Practice acquisition (Apr 2026), Tria (Jul 2026) — alongside a long-standing R&D hub and 250+ AI/ML models, with AI-led deals rising from 12% to 70% of pipeline. (The long view had said: Named platform launches recur every year with disclosed commercial impact — NextLabs/Mphasis.ai, NeoZeta and NeoCrux (Jul 2024), NeoIP (FY26), Tria (FY27) — feeding pipeline from 12% to 70% AI-led and record $2.12bn FY26 TCV; but R&D spend is never disclosed, gross margin is a low ~28% and the margin band has been cut, so this is not high-GM-funded R&D dominance.)

**That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Deal-making metrics point to gains — LTM TCV doubled over four quarters, five consecutive quarters of net-new TCV above $400mn, and wallet-share expansion at existing clients — but no call gives explicit market-share figures or share versus named competitors, and FY27 revenue guidance is only high-single to low-double-digit. (The long view had said: Share gain is claimed and quantified consistently across all four years — 8 straight quarters of 20%+ BFS growth and client-bucket migration in FY23, wallet-share consolidation through the FY25 downturn, and FY26 delivering above the 'better-than-industry' promise with a client pyramid adding $20M/$50M/$75M/$100M+ logos; the FY25 top-10 decline of 10.1% was disclosed and subsequently reversed to 13.7% year-on-year growth by Q4FY26.)
- **Friendly Middleman**: QUANT SIGNAL → NO FIT. Why now: Across all four calls the sale is direct to the enterprise buyer and is moving further up the same customer's org chart (CIO/CTO to CFO/COO), with no prescriber, installer or third-party expert channel anywhere in the model. (The long view had said: all 2 computable checks point this way but the calls offer no evidence — numbers alone are only ever a screening hint, never a confirmed fit.)
- **Low Price Plus**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The calls discuss cost reduction as a client outcome and an EBIT margin band of 14.75%-15.75%, but say nothing about Mphasis's own cost culture, itemised savings or a low-price/high-turns strategy. (The long view had said: Cost-arbitrage positioning is a repeated theme across the five years — offshore-led delivery, 'zero cost transformation' (FY23), 'Savings Led Transformation' (FY25), 'structural efficiency in a self-funded way' (FY26) — but this is a cost proposition sold to clients rather than an internal cost obsession with itemised savings, and returns come from margin band defence, not high asset turns.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Forward Integrators (QUANT SIGNAL).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 4 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view; 1 could not be compared. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Management reports the business through a BFS / non-BFS split every quarter, confirming banking and financial services remains the anchor vertical, and repeatedly points to penetration of 'some of our top clients' and clients representing '>50% of company revenue' on one platform — concentration in both vertical and account terms. Bounded, because the calls show deliberate broadening rather than a single named relationship whose renewal is in question. (The long view had said: The book is deliberately built on a few very large relationships — top 5 averaging ~$150M each, all top 6 clients above $75M, one client above $150M — and that concentration bit visibly when top-10 accounts declined 10.1% year-on-year in Q1FY25 and when a single client's revenue restructuring dragged EMEA in FY26; vertical concentration in BFS is similarly heavy. Bounded because the base has broadened materially over the period.)

**That worsened:**

- **Technological Innovation**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: All four calls are dominated by AI as a platform-level shift that attacks the economics of the company's own installed base — the people-based, effort-priced services model — with management conceding outright deflation in that model and that scale advantages are being reset. This is large-scale, not incremental, innovation and Mphasis is re-platforming its entire delivery stack (NeoIP, then Tria) inside twelve months in response. (The long view had said: This is the dominant risk across the whole 17-call arc: management moved from 'AI is a tailwind' (2022) to explicitly conceding volume destruction in people-based service lines (Jul 2024) to admitting in Jul 2026 that annuity run deals are 'highly susceptible to disruption' and that they face the same AI disruption fears as peers; the installed delivery model (headcount-linked, price-per-widget, legacy ITO — which fell 21.6% year-on-year) is precisely the infrastructure a platform shift makes legacy. The heavy platform rebuild (Mphasis.ai, NeoZeta, NeoCrux, NeoIP, Tria, TAP acquisition) is a response to, not evidence against, the disruption.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 6 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
