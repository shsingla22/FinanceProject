# Meesho (MEESHO) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Meesho runs India's largest online shopping app by number of shoppers and orders, where mostly small and mid-sized sellers list low-priced everyday goods (clothing and similar mass-market items) and sell them directly to price-conscious consumers, including deep in small towns and rural India. In the December 2025 quarter it had 251 million shoppers who bought at least once in the past year (up 34%) and 846,000 sellers (up 81%), with goods worth Rs 10,995 crore sold on the platform in that quarter alone. It earns money off the sellers who use the platform — the fastest-growing piece being advertising that sellers buy to promote their products, which management wants to reach about 5.5%-6% of the value of goods sold, versus a total contribution margin of 2.3% in Q3 FY26. It also runs its own delivery network, Valmo, alongside outside courier partners, so cutting delivery cost is the main lever for keeping prices low; the business operates only in India, listed on the NSE/BSE, and was still loss-making at the EBITDA level as of these calls.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Core marketplace (value commerce platform)** — The main shopping app where lakhs of small, mostly unbranded sellers list cheap everyday products for mass-market Indian buyers — this is the bulk of the business, carrying essentially all of the 251 million annual shoppers and Rs 10,995 crore of quarterly goods value.
- **Ads** — Sellers pay to have their products shown more prominently, using an AI-driven system built for small sellers with tiny budgets who don't know which keywords to bid on; management won't disclose the numbers yet but says returns to sellers rose about 50% in a year and targets ads reaching 5.5%-6% of goods sold, versus current total contribution margin of 2.3%.
- **Meesho Mall** — A brands section selling affordable/value packs of national, regional and newer direct-to-consumer brands to the same mass-market buyers; management calls it a substantial, fast-growing part of the business that earns a higher ad take than the rest of the platform, but does not disclose its size.
- **Valmo (logistics network)** — Meesho's own national delivery network, used alongside third-party couriers — roughly 50-55% of orders were handled in-house as of May 2026 — with no fixed target for the split; a rushed capacity build-up after a partner shut down hurt margins in FY26, and cost per delivered order was falling again (down about Re 1 sequentially) by Q1 FY27.
- **Payments (prepaid and BNPL)** — Efforts to shift buyers from cash-on-delivery to paying before delivery, which is cheaper to service, plus a buy-now-pay-later product management calls still very early in its life cycle.
- **New initiatives — Kirana Club and low-cost local logistics / grocery** — A recently acquired B2B business serving small neighbourhood shops (kiranas) across small towns and rural India, plus a separate local delivery network being built for perishables and fast-moving goods; management says both are very small, at 'early product-market fit' or still experimental, and will sit in the disclosed 'new initiatives' bucket with no near-term profit contribution.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
