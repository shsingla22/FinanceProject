### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 3 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: The Executive Chairperson's FY27 framing implicitly concedes that a plain outsourced-services offering is becoming commoditised and must be upgraded to defend value, and the CRDMO market has many lower-cost providers of routine chemistry and biology work; margin defence in FY27 rests on 'disciplined cost management' rather than pricing power. (The long view had said: Discovery/FTE research services are substitutable and clients explicitly shop on value — Hunt framed Syngene's appeal as helping cash-constrained biotechs stretch funding through 'very good value for money' — and margins have compressed from ~30% to 23-25%; management's answer is to move up the value chain rather than defend price.)

**That worsened:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: A single commercial product (Librela) at the largest large-molecule biologics client single-handedly turned FY26 from guided mid-single-digit growth into a 3-5% revenue decline and is guided to depress FY27 too, while BMS — one customer, 700 scientists, 25 years — is repeatedly named as the largest relationship; results are visibly hostage to two named counterparties. (The long view had said: A single commercial product (Librela) from the largest large-molecule biologics customer single-handedly turned FY26 guidance from mid-single-digit growth into a 3-5% revenue decline and cut EBITDA margin from 27% to 23%, and management says the impact continues into FY27; separately BMS is the largest client with 700+ scientists dedicated to it, so both the CDMO and research sides rest on a few named relationships.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (QUANT FLAG); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (WATCH); Technological Innovation (WATCH); Government Dependency (WATCH).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
