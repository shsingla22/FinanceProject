### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 5 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 6. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 1 weakened, 9 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Pricing Power**: NO FIT → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Premiumisation is real (Owndays lenses 38% of India revenue, Meller sunglasses, B by Lenskart smart eyewear) but the 7% ASP lift is explicitly mix-led rather than price-led, and Q4 product margin was flat at 64% as rupee depreciation absorbed the benefit — while management simultaneously launches at the low end for mass adoption. (The long view had said: Management explicitly and repeatedly denies taking price increases — the 7% ASP move in Q3 was mix (progressives, Owndays lenses) and Q4 margin was held at 64% only because vertical integration absorbed rupee depreciation, while GST cuts and opening-price-point campaigns pushed price down; premiumisation happens on the customer's terms, not through price hikes.)

**That weakened:**

- **Friendly Middleman**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The optometrist is Lenskart's own employee (increasingly a remote in-house optom serving multiple stores), and demand is pulled by brand and eye-test acquisition direct from the paying consumer — there is no independent prescriber or trade channel being cultivated in any of the three calls. (The long view had said: The expert-prescriber dynamic is central — ~6mn eye tests a quarter in India with ~half first-time exams drive the sale, and remote optometry scaled from 168 to 623 stores in a year — but the optometrist is Lenskart's own employee, not an independent professional channel, so there is no third-party trade to train or court; demand is pulled by the Lenskart brand direct to consumer.)

*Unchanged: Brand Strength (STRONG FIT); Innovation Dominance (STRONG FIT); Forward Integrators (STRONG FIT); Market Share Gainers (STRONG FIT); Low Price Plus (LIKELY FIT); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
