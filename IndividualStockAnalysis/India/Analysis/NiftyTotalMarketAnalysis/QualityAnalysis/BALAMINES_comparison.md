### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 0 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 0. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 3 weakened, 4 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Toll Roads**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: There are certification/standards traits — BIS certification at Units I and III making it 'the only BIS certified Morpholine manufacturer in India', plus PESO/MPCB/pharma-grade approvals gating products — but the toll-road economics are absent: management repeatedly flags that customers are pushing back on price ('continued pressure across key product lines', acetonitrile merely 'stable around INR140 to INR150'). (The long view had said: Evidence contradicts toll-road economics: realisations track ammonia/methanol/acetic-acid swings, DMF is fought over with Chinese imports year after year, EDA prices slid from ~300 to 270-280 within a quarter, and a large metformin customer backward-integrated into methylamines — customers clearly negotiate and can switch or self-supply.)

**That weakened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The four calls are entirely about bulk chemical volumes (MT), plant commissioning and realizations; there is no discussion of licences, AMCs, subscriptions, attach rates, installed base or customer advances funding the business. (The long view had said: Nine calls of bulk chemical tonnage discussion contain no mention of licences, AMCs, subscriptions, attach rates, renewals or deferred revenue as a funding source; the evidence is silent rather than contradictory.)
- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: No consumer or trade brand discussion at all — no A&P spend, heritage story, distributor leverage or brand-led adjacency launches appear in the last four calls. (The long view had said: No heritage story, A&P spend, distributor leverage or consumer-facing brand is discussed anywhere in the nine calls — the product is sold on specification and availability.)
- **Cost to Replicate**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The only articulable barriers offered — BIS certification, an integrated site and internally-developed technology — have not stopped sustained price deflation, and the company's own behaviour shows the barrier is buyable: it is spending INR750 crores plus multiple plants to enter products others already make. Running at 35–40% utilisation while still adding capacity indicates the industry's constraint is demand, not any replication difficulty. (The long view had said: Some articulable barriers exist — indigenous process technology, first-and-only domestic capacity in certain molecules (n-butylamines, TETA), BIS certification for morpholine, and an integrated amines chain (methylamine feeding DMF) that showed its worth in the March 2026 supply shock — but these are engineering plants a well-funded entrant can rebuild, as a single customer's backward integration into methylamines demonstrates.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Market Share Gainers (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 10 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
