### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 2 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 2 weakened, 6 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → STRONG FIT. Why now: Share gains are quantified and repeated across all four calls — FY26 replacement share up ~0.5% in PCUV and TBR and ~1% in motorcycle, EV OEM share 25-30%, international saliency 19%→20.4% standalone (23% with CAMSO) — alongside 15.5% FY26 and 18.3% Q1 FY27 growth well ahead of the industry's single-digit outlook. (The long view had said: Share numbers are quantified and rising across the record: ~50% of 2-wheeler OEM business (FY23), 25% EV share of business (FY25) rising to 29% in passenger EV (FY26), FY26 replacement share up 0.5-1% across PCUV/motorcycle/TBR, and international saliency 19% to 20.4% standalone / 23%+ with CAMSO, with localised entities in Europe and new markets (US, Vietnam, Peru).)
- **Pricing Power**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Management does take explicit, sizeable price increases (7-8% replacement, 3-4% OE, 4-5% international in Q1 FY27) and premium mix is growing fast, but these are reactive cost pass-throughs that lag input costs — gross margin contracted 575bps Q-o-Q and EBITDA fell to 9.1% (Jul 2026), which is cost recovery, not pricing power above inflation. (The long view had said: Pricing is pure cost pass-through with a lag, not pricing above cost inflation: gross margin swung 40% (FY23) to 36.9% (Q3 FY25) to 39.7% (Q4 FY26) to 33.9% (Q1 FY27), and in Q1 FY27 a 16-18% RM surge left only 7-11% of price taken, collapsing EBITDA margin to 9.1%. Management concedes hikes 'come with a lag' and are 'insufficient' to cover RM.)

**That weakened:**

- **Low Price Plus**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Cost work is mentioned only in passing (cost optimisation against RM inflation, SAP RISE cost efficiency) with no itemised savings, no no-frills positioning and no pass-savings-to-price strategy — the stated direction is premiumisation, not low price. Not enough either way to judge. (The long view had said: CEAT self-describes as a 'quality value player' and shows a real cost culture — WEF Lighthouse plants at Chennai and Halol, itemised discretionary cuts that held other expenses flat at ~18.6% in Q1 FY27, other expenses down INR34 crore sequentially in Q4 FY26 — but it is a capital-heavy #3/#4 player, not the scale cost leader, and returns are only 'double-digit post-tax ROCE'.)
- **Cost to Replicate**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Nothing in the window articulates why a funded entrant could not catch up: economics swing with rubber and crude prices, Asian competitors are pressuring European pricing, Sri Lankan share is under attack from local competition, and the CAMSO value chain itself is being rebuilt over 4-6 quarters — i.e. the capability is buyable and buildable rather than un-replicable. (The long view had said: Some genuine time-and-capital barriers — INR1,205 crore of 2-wheeler capacity phased to FY31, multi-year OEM homologation and EV nominations, deep rural distribution ('growth rate in rural market was almost 40%-50% higher than urban'), WEF Lighthouse plants — but the industry has several equally-capitalised incumbents and the FY27 margin swing shows no advantage that stops a funded rival; nothing here is un-replicable in a few years.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL).*

*Unchanged: Corporate Culture (STRONG FIT); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Brand Strength (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 6 unchanged. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Volume/revenue growth was steady all four quarters (12-20% year-on-year), but margins swing hard with the commodity input basket: standalone EBITDA margin moved 13.7% (Q2 FY26) to 14.1% to 14.6% and then collapsed to 9.1% in Q1 FY27 on a 575bps gross-margin contraction from a 15-16% rubber/crude-derivative cost jump that price hikes lag by a quarter. Demand is also framed around monsoon, rural cash flows, fuel prices and the auto OEM cycle, though the bulk of sales is replacement (a flow/consumable product), not customer capex equipment. (The long view had said: Across all 14 calls margins swing violently with the natural rubber/crude commodity cycle rather than with volumes — EBITDA went 13.1% (Q4FY23) → 10.5% (Q3FY25) → 14.6% (Q4FY26) → 9.1% (Q1FY27) — and management frames every quarter's outlook around rubber, crude and rupee, while capex is being stepped up to INR1,300-1,400cr plus an INR1,205cr 2-wheeler board approval at the same time input costs spike.)

**That worsened:**

- **New Entrants**: NO SIGNAL → WATCH. Why now: Management flagged low-cost Asian competitors pressuring pricing in Europe in Q3 FY26 and intensified local competition in Sri Lanka in 2-wheeler and PCR, where the stated goal shifted from growth to defending existing share. The response is premiumisation and OEM fitment rather than discounting, and it has not visibly reset domestic economics. (The long view had said: the judge read the calls and found nothing, and all 2 computed fingerprints are clean.)

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 3 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
