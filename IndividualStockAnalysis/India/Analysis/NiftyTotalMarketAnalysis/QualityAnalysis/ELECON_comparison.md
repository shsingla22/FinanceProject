**Checks that improved:**

- **Barriers to entry and rationality** (Industry Structure): Neutral → Good. Now: Profit margins have barely moved over the years (±2.1 points) — consistent with rational pricing and few new competitors. And from the calls: Integrated in-house manufacturing and 75 years of engineering are cited as entry barriers with no new entrants mentioned, and margins held near 21% EBITDA through a weak year, implying no discount war. | The long view had said: Profit margins swing by ±5.2 points — a hint of price wars, cost shocks, or new entrants disturbing the industry. And from the calls: Rather than new entrants, players have been exiting segments such as power gearing, ownership is stable under the promoter family, and there is no mention of discount wars — margins held at ~21% EBITDA even through the FY26 downturn.
- **Profit margin** (Return on Capital): Neutral → Good. Now: Out of every ₹100 of sales, ₹22 is operating profit. Margins have moved about ±2.1 points a year — very steady, a sign costs are under control. | The long view had said: Out of every ₹100 of sales, ₹22 is operating profit. Margins have moved about ±5.2 points a year — sizeable swings, suggesting key costs are outside management's control.
- **Assurance benefits** (Customer Benefits): Good → Excellent. Now: The whole pitch is reliability for mission-critical infrastructure — customized high-value solutions, lifecycle support and trusted relationships in power, steel, cement and ports, where equipment failure is far costlier than price. | The long view had said: The pitch is reliability, shortest lead times and end-to-end lifecycle support on mission-critical drives, and OEM approvals plus repeat preference show customers buy certainty rather than the lowest price.
- **Capex for growth (not maintenance)** (Capital Allocation): Neutral → Good. Now: Management repeatedly references recently commissioned capacity and 'strategic capital expenditure' supporting growth, but no capex quantum, split of growth vs maintenance, or incremental ROIC is disclosed in these calls. | The long view had said: A INR400 crore FY26-FY28 growth capex program exists but only ~INR95 crore was spent in FY26 while capacity utilisation sat at 56-60% and ROCE fell from 29% in FY24 to 20.4% in FY26, so growth capex is neither high nor generating rising incremental returns yet.

**Checks that regressed:**

- **Gain in market share** (Growth): Good → Neutral. Now: Management asserts leadership in India's organized industrial gear market and 'growing acceptance' overseas, but shows no share-gain evidence — gear revenue fell 21% in Q4 FY26 and FY26 growth was 6%. | The long view had said: Elecon leads India's organized gear market yet has a 'minuscule' global share, and it has visibly converted that runway — overseas revenue share rose 27%→34% by FY25 with overseas order book up 73% year-on-year in Q1 FY27.
- **Pricing, Mix and Volume** (Growth): Neutral → Weak. Now: Growth is volume/order-execution driven while mix has repeatedly hurt margins — gear EBIT margin fell from 27.8% to 18.2% in Q3 FY26 on 'higher employee cost and product mix', with no evidence of price-led growth. | The long view had said: Mix is improving toward higher-margin engineered products (45%) and exports, but the company could not pass through raw-material inflation in FY27 — gear margins fell despite 16% revenue growth — so pricing power is not the growth engine.
- **Candor** (Management): Good → Neutral. Now: Disclosure is clean on adjusting one-offs (arbitration, MTM gain), but weak quarters are repeatedly attributed to 'timing-related' and 'transient' external factors across four consecutive calls without owning the miss, and the INR102 crore goodwill impairment is stated without explanation. | The long view had said: Management quantifies margin bridges precisely, restates adjusted numbers to strip one-offs, admits the lower-margin first defence order, and openly concedes the year missed its own expectations.

### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 1 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 1 weakened, 5 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Genuine heritage is repeatedly invoked — 75 years, leadership in India's organized industrial gear market, presence in ~95 countries — but there is no A&P spend, no premium-pricing evidence, and FY26 revenue grew only 6% with gear down 21% in Q4. (The long view had said: Genuine heritage and sustained brand investment — '75 years of engineering excellence' (Apr 2026), 'over seven decades of industry experience', and repeated brand-building spend that management says drove overseas acceptance — but the brand delivers reference credibility rather than demonstrable premium pricing, and there is no A&P quantification or category stretch.)
- **Innovation Dominance**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: R&D is cited as a differentiator in every call, but no R&D spend figure, no named new products with revenue impact, and no pipeline quantification appears anywhere in the FY26–Q1FY27 calls. (The long view had said: R&D and new-product development are described as 'the only constant in the company' (Jul 2024), the engineered-vs-catalogue mix has risen to 45:55 by FY26, and patents have been filed — but R&D spend is never disclosed or quantified, no named products with revenue impact are given, and management declined to discuss patent commercialisation (Jul 2026).)

**That weakened:**

- **Pricing Power**: LIKELY FIT → NO FIT. Why now: No price increases are discussed in any of the four calls; instead gear EBIT margin fell from 27.8% to 18.2% year-on-year in Q3 FY26 on employee cost and mix, and Q1 FY27 MHE EBIT fell 25.3% on input-cost spikes — cost inflation was absorbed, not passed through. (The long view had said: Early in the period management did pass through inflation aggressively (Jul 2022: short offer validity, went back to customers for increases, gross margins expanded), but by FY26-FY27 pass-through failed — Q1 FY27 gear margins fell to 17.9% from 18.8% and MHE lost ~2.5-3% margin purely on input cost, and management said high input costs were lengthening enquiry-to-order conversion. Conditional pricing power exists only in replacement/engineered/defence repeat orders.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL); Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (NO FIT).*

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 0 worsened, 4 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 9 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
