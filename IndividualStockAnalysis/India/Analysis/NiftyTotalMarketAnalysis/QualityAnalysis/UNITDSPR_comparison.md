### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 0 worsened, 6 unchanged. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Spirits demand here is consumption-linked and repeat-purchase, not tied to any customer's capex or commodity price cycle; the only softness described is a discretionary demand moderation (luxury/premium growth easing from ~25-30% to 11%), which management attributes to post-COVID normalisation while EPS crossed INR20 and ROCE crossed 25% for the first time. (The long view had said: Spirits is a repeat-purchase flow product, not customer-capex equipment, but the premium end has visibly cycled: Luxury & Premium growth fell from 25-30% two to three years ago to 11%, attributed to post-COVID revenge consumption and duty-free-to-duty-paid tailwinds unwinding, with headline pricing swinging from 0.2-0.3% (FY17-22) to ~2% (last 3 years) and expected to moderate again.)
- **Stakeholder Concentration**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: No single customer, supplier or licensor is presented as driving results; the portfolio spans three trademarks above INR1,000cr NSV, three above INR500cr and four above INR100cr, and management explicitly runs an 'and' strategy across both Diageo global and USL-owned trademarks rather than depending on either. (The long view had said: Route to market runs through state corporations and state-controlled retail, so a handful of state administrations effectively are the customers — Andhra alone swung 3.1pp of FY25 NSV growth after a five-year absence, and a INR37 crore receivable impairment was traced to 'one particular customer'. Offsetting this, the brand portfolio itself is broad.)

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH); Fashion Risk (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW); Good Enough Goods (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
