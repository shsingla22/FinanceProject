### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 0 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 3. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 3 strengthened, 1 weakened, 6 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Afcons has a genuine reputational asset in complex engineering — Chenab bridge, ENR's 8th-ranked international marine and 12th bridge contractor, MIKE award eight years running — but the calls show no pricing premium or share gain from it, with FY26 revenue down 5.4% and order intake far below guidance. (The long view had said: A 65-year heritage, five-star export house status and ENR global rankings (8th in marine, 12th in bridges) plus repeat orders from satisfied overseas clients give genuine reputational standing, but it is a B2G credential rather than a consumer brand and it delivers no pricing premium — FY26 order inflow still fell far short.)
- **Innovation Dominance**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Innovation is repeatedly evidenced — national TBM tunnelling records of 740m and 777m per month, CII Grand Award as top innovative company across all sectors, MIKE award for the 8th consecutive year — but no R&D spend, pipeline economics or margin uplift from innovation is disclosed on any FY26 call. (The long view had said: Sustained engineering/knowledge-management leadership — MIKE award eight consecutive years, CII Grand Award for most innovative company, national TBM tunneling records of 740m then 777m per month — but no R&D spend is ever disclosed or quantified, and innovation has not translated into margin or revenue growth.)
- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: NO FIT → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: The four FY26 calls discuss only lump-sum EPC contract execution, order book and receivables; there is no mention of licences, AMC/service contracts, attach rates, renewals or deferred revenue as a funding source. (The long view had said: There is no licence, AMC or subscription stream — revenue is lumpy project execution off a tendered order book, and customer advances have shifted from a free funding source to an interest-bearing cost (75% interest-free falling to ~60% by FY26), with working capital and debt rising rather than customers funding the business.)

**That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The last twelve months show the opposite of persistent share gain: FY26 revenue declined 5.4%, order inflow was INR4,125 crores against INR20,000 crores guided, and the Q1 promise of 20–25% turnover growth was missed entirely amid rising competition. (The long view had said: International expansion with local adaptation is real and improving — ENR marine rank moved from 10th (2023) to 8th (2025), entry into Croatia and a EUR100m Uganda road win, ~30% of turnover from overseas — but domestic order inflow collapsed to INR4,125 crores in FY26 against a INR20,000 crore guidance, so gains are not persistent across the whole business.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Forward Integrators (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Friendly Middleman (NO FIT); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Low Price Plus (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
