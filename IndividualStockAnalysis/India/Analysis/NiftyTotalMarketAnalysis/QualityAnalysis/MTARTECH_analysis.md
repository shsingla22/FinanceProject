# MTAR Technologies (MTARTECH) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

MTAR Technologies is a Hyderabad-based precision engineering and manufacturing company that builds highly-engineered, technology-intensive components, sub-systems and assemblies to its customers' designs — it is a specialist supplier, not a brand-name product company. Its biggest business by far is making 'hot boxes' and related units for solid oxide fuel cells for a large overseas clean-energy customer (Bloom Energy), alongside parts for India's civil nuclear reactor programme (NPCIL projects such as Kaiga 5 & 6 and reactor refurbishments), space and aerospace work for ISRO and multinationals like IAI, Thales and GKN, Indian defence programmes, and an in-house 'products' line of items such as ball screws and roller screws that substitute for imports. It earns money order-by-order against a disclosed order book — INR2,580 crores at the end of FY26 — converting those orders into shipments from its own plants, so revenue tracks order inflow and factory capacity. It manufactures entirely in and around Hyderabad, India, but management says exports now make up the majority of revenue; FY26 revenue was INR876 crores with EBITDA of INR171 crores and profit after tax of INR94 crores.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Clean Energy — Fuel Cells** — Makes hot boxes (the core power-generating module of a solid oxide fuel cell) and electrolyzer units for a single large US customer, Bloom Energy — this is the company's dominant segment, contributing INR387 crores in the first nine months of FY26 with INR1,080 crores of fresh orders in that period, and capacity is being expanded from roughly 12,000 boxes a year toward 20,000 and then 30,000 as AI data-centre power demand grows.
- **Civil Nuclear Power** — Supplies critical sub-systems and components for Indian nuclear reactors (items like fuel machining heads and nuclear-island sub-assemblies) as a pre-qualified NPCIL vendor; management says it has been in this field 35+ years, and it holds an order book of over INR650 crores from Kaiga units 5 & 6 and reactor refurbishment work to be executed over three to three-and-a-half years.
- **Space** — Builds launch-vehicle and engine hardware for ISRO — most notably the semi-cryogenic engine — plus first articles and batch production for multinational aerospace customers; it was a small line (INR8.5 crores in Q1 FY25, about 7% of revenue) that management repeatedly flags as a fast-growing one, and is now largely reported together with defence.
- **Aerospace and Defence** — Machines precision parts and is moving up to full structural assemblies for multinational customers (IAI, Thales, GKN) and Indian defence programmes including AMCA landing gear and actuator assemblies; revenue was about INR72 crores in the first nine months of FY26 against an order book of over INR360 crores, with volume production just beginning.
- **Products and Others** — Sells its own catalogue-type precision products — ball screws, roller screws, electromechanical actuators, bellows and similar items positioned as import substitutes — plus small amounts of hydro, wind and other work; it generated about INR84 crores in the first nine months of FY26 and management expects it to cross INR200 crores in FY27.
- **Oil and Gas (new vertical)** — A newly built business where the company is starting with first-article parts before moving to volume production; the dedicated plant was still being set up as of the May 2026 call, expected to be commissioned by end-September and fully operational thereafter, so it contributes essentially nothing yet.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
