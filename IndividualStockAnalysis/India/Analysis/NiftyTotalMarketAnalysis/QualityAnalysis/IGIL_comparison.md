### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 0 eased, 2 worsened, 6 unchanged. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That worsened:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: This is the clearest Nobel Biocare setup: ~60% EBITDA margins on a service where the jeweller, not the end consumer, picks the lab and cheaper labs can issue a 'good enough' certificate — which is precisely why IGI spent the whole window buying consumer awareness (Women's World Cup, IPL/Gujarat Titans, 'Heera Ki Asli Pehchan'); realisations corroborate the pressure, with a ~5% ASP decline in Q3 CY25 and base-business ASP up only 2% in Q1 FY27. (The long view had said: This is the live structural question in the calls: as LGD volumes explode and stone prices collapse, analysts ask repeatedly whether price per report must be given up ('is there a pressure that with higher volume we may need to forgo some pricing?'), consol ASP fell ~5% on smaller-carat mix in Q3 CY25, and one analyst's channel check found jewelers 'not so keen on getting certification for the LGD diamond' — the intermediary, not the consumer, controls whether and from whom a report is bought, the Nobel Biocare setup. Against that, realised pricing has held stable for four-plus quarters, India ASP is up 2%, and management is directly attacking the low end-consumer brand awareness with its first consumer campaigns.)
- **New Entrants**: LOW → WATCH. Why now: The certification industry is contested by other labs whose grading-standard changes management discusses as an opportunity rather than a threat; entry barriers for lower-cost local labs are low even though no specific new-format entrant is named in these four calls. (The long view had said: No structurally different competitor is discussed defensively in any of the seven calls, and there is no discounting or margin investment framed as a competitive response — pricing is described as stable across four-plus quarters and ASP is rising. Management affirmatively describes the industry as having very high entry barriers with only a few players at scale, and IGI is itself the consolidator (AGL acquisition, Italy launch).)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 1 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
