- **Advertisement and promotion** (Capital Allocation): Neutral → Good. Now: A&P shows up as clinical outreach rather than advertising — a six-month large-scale breast/prostate cancer awareness program that builds brand equity and feeds the oncology funnel, but no spend disclosure or measured payback. | The long view had said: A&P is modest and episodic — sales and marketing spend only resumed post-COVID and campaigns are clinical awareness drives (breast/prostate cancer) rather than sustained brand-equity investment; brand is built by outcomes and rankings instead.

### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 6 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 4. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 0 strengthened, 3 weakened, 8 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That weakened:**

- **Cost to Replicate**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Hard-to-replicate elements are visible — a flagship reputation compounded over years (seventh consecutive Newsweek ranking), a deep senior-clinician bench, JCI/NABH accreditation and scarce urban land parcels (Guwahati, Mumbai FSI, South Delhi) — but the calls also show a well-capitalised entrant can buy the same equipment and hire clinicians, as Medanta itself did in building Noida to full accreditation in six months. (The long view had said: Replication requires a 43-acre 1,400-bed campus plus ~Rs.45,000mn of greenfield capex over five years, 1,400+ employed super-specialists poached from AIIMS-calibre institutions, and a multi-year ramp (Lucknow took five years to shift from developing to mature) before any JCI/Newsweek reputation accrues — capital alone cannot compress the clinical-reputation timeline.)
- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Capacity and volumes are compounding well above market — beds +20.5% (623 added in FY26), IP +16%, OP +19%, international +33% with mix rising 6%→7% — and the gains come with rising margins ex-Noida rather than bought growth, but management never cites market-share numbers or names competitors. (The long view had said: Capacity and volumes compound persistently (beds +20.5% in FY26, IP volumes +16%, OP +19%, international revenue +33% to 7% of mix) with new-geography entry into Noida, Guwahati, Varanasi and Delhi, but management never quotes market-share numbers or names competitor share, so gain is inferred from growth rather than disclosed.)
- **Low Price Plus**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Positioning across all four calls is explicitly premium — 'world-class', quaternary care, Da Vinci Xi robots, CAR T-cell, 3 Tesla MRI, JCI/NABH accreditation — with no cost-obsession, itemised savings or no-frills language anywhere; costs are rising (manpower, repairs) and compressing mature margins. (The long view had said: The founding premise is world-class outcomes 'at a fraction of the cost' and management deliberately restrains tariffs while attacking costs (material-cost optimisation assignment in FY23, material cost improvement offsetting employee costs in FY26), but this is premium quaternary care with 25%+ EBITDA margins, not a no-frills high-turns model.)

*Unchanged: Brand Strength (STRONG FIT); Corporate Culture (STRONG FIT); Pricing Power (LIKELY FIT); Innovation Dominance (LIKELY FIT); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 2 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
