# Saatvik Green Energy (SAATVIKGL) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Saatvik Green Energy is an Indian company that manufactures solar panels (photovoltaic modules) — the flat panels that turn sunlight into electricity — and sells them to large power-plant developers, commercial and industrial customers, EPC contractors, and a wide base of retail dealers and distributors, mostly within India. Around 95% of its revenue comes from selling modules; it also builds solar projects (EPC) and maintains them (O&M), and is adding smaller product lines such as solar water pumps, inverters, transformers and battery storage. Its factories are in Ambala, Haryana (about 3.8 GW of annual module capacity, later expanded with another 1 GW, plus a 2 GW encapsulant-film plant), with a large new integrated cell-and-module plant being built in Odisha. FY26 revenue was about INR 4,548 crore (INR 45,484 million) with a confirmed order book of roughly 5.89 GW as of March 2026.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Solar module manufacturing** — Makes and sells high-efficiency solar panels (Mono PERC and N-TOPCon, in monofacial and bifacial versions) for rooftop, commercial and large utility projects — this is the core business at roughly 95% of sales, with production of 3,162 MW in FY26 at over 84% capacity utilisation.
- **EPC and O&M services** — Designs and builds solar projects end-to-end for customers and then operates and maintains them; management describes it as part of the same value chain as modules, with the panels used in these jobs counted inside module sales.
- **Solar cell manufacturing (Odisha, upcoming)** — A greenfield integrated plant in Odisha for solar cells — the component inside a module — originally planned at 4.8 GW and later raised to 6 GW, alongside 4 GW of module capacity; management calls it the key backward-integration step for better cost control and margins, with cell production expected from around October of FY27.
- **EPE encapsulant film manufacturing** — An in-house 2 GW plant at Ambala commissioned in Q3 FY26 that makes the protective film used inside modules, with plans to expand it to 5 GW, aimed at securing supply and steadying margins rather than being a large revenue line on its own.
- **Solar pumps** — Sells solar-powered water pumps, mainly riding government rural schemes like PM-KUSUM; it was only about 0.35% of revenue in Q3 FY26 but management is making it a standalone business unit and calls it a high-growth vertical.
- **Inverters and power electronics** — Assembles and sells inverters, including the UDAY Series on-grid inverters launched in FY26, with hybrid/off-grid inverters and B2C solar kits planned — a new and still small downstream line.
- **Transformers (Melcon)** — Entered power transmission equipment by acquiring an 80% stake in Melcon Transformers and Electricals during FY26; described as a strategic entry into energy infrastructure rather than a material revenue contributor yet.
- **Battery energy storage (Saatvik Power Storage Solutions)** — A newly launched subsidiary focused on battery energy storage systems, including BESS for commercial and industrial customers; at this stage it is an early-stage growth initiative.
- **Ancillary components and planned ingot/wafer** — Expanding into solar components such as aluminium frames, ribbons and junction boxes, and planning 6 GW of ingot and wafer manufacturing, all aimed at owning more of the solar supply chain in future years.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
