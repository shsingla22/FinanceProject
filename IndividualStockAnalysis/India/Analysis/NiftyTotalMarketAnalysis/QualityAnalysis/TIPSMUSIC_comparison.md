### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 5 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 5. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 2 weakened, 6 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Heritage is real and un-copyable at the catalogue level — 90s titles like 'Deewana Mujhe Kar Gaya' and 'Soldier Soldier' doing 2-3 billion Instagram views, 158.3mn cumulative YouTube subscribers — but there is no A&P spend, premium pricing or label-brand-led share gain discussed; consumer affinity attaches to the songs, not the Tips name. (The long view had said: There is a genuine heritage story — a repertoire built from 1988 that spans two generations and is now marketed as 'new retro', plus a 158mn-subscriber YouTube identity that gives distribution leverage — but there is no sustained A&P spend, no premium pricing and adjacency stretch is limited to a related-party film arm.)

**That weakened:**

- **Pricing Power**: LIKELY FIT → NO FIT. Why now: Realisations are under pressure, not rising: the Oct 2025 call describes yield pressure across the industry as free-tier platforms shut or paywall, and the Jul 2026 call confirms that price hikes are taken by Spotify/YouTube, not by Tips — the company is a royalty-rate taker awaiting a subscription-mix shift it does not control. (The long view had said: Tips does not set the price of its own output — per-stream realisation is fixed by platforms (India ₹0.04-0.10 vs ₹0.50-0.90 globally) and it suffered YouTube CPM deflation in FY23 — but it does negotiate hard at contract renewals (the 5-year YouTube fight, Warner, the pending Shorts deal) and benefits passively from Spotify/YouTube subscription price hikes.)
- **Toll Roads**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Music content is a large, negotiated share of a streaming platform's cost base rather than a sub-2% incidental input, and the Q1 FY27 call shows Indian labels realising INR0.04-0.10 per stream against INR0.50-0.90 globally — the platforms hold the pricing pen, not Tips. (The long view had said: Tips sits in a rational oligopoly of Indian labels whose catalogue platforms must license, and it claims unique structural positioning ('the only ones' with Warner and Sony Publishing distribution tie-ups); but content royalties are a very large, not tiny, share of a streaming platform's cost base, and there is no certification/standards or regulatory anchoring, so the core toll-road test fails.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Market Share Gainers (LIKELY FIT); Friendly Middleman (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Corporate Culture (STRONG FIT); Cost to Replicate (STRONG FIT); Innovation Dominance (LIKELY FIT); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 5 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Fashion Risk**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Demand rests on taste and virality: management credits a '100X spike' in creations from a few catalogue tracks going viral on Instagram, and the CFO concedes that the peak ~650 million Shorts view run-rate was driven by a few Reels going viral and is not the normal level, while forward growth depends on an unconfirmed slate of 4-5 Hindi films whose release dates slip. The offset is a deep, diversified library whose 1990s catalogue is still the largest single contributor. (The long view had said: Music demand rests on taste and the acquisition model is hit-rate dependent — quarters are narrated through specific films (Crew, Ishq Vishk Rebound, Hai Jawani Toh Ishq Hona Hai) and viral moments, management admits picking hits 'comes with experience', and payback on new content takes 4-5 years with no exclusivity over any artist; but the exposure is bounded because the profit engine is the back catalogue, not this year's fashion.)

**That worsened:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Roughly 70% of FY26 revenue is digital, funnelled through a handful of named platforms (Spotify, YouTube, JioSaavn, Meta), and the calls repeatedly show that leverage sitting on the other side: a couple of platforms shut down and directly dented digital growth, per-stream yields are set by the platforms at INR0.04-0.10, and the YouTube Shorts contract came up for renewal in June 2026 with the executive who negotiated it (CEO Hari Nair, absent from the Q4 and Q1 calls) departing and no replacement identified. (The long view had said: YouTube alone is 45-50% of revenue on an 'industry pool' payout the company does not set, its Shorts deal renewal has been an open analyst question for several quarters and negotiations remain unresolved into Q1 FY27, international distribution runs through a single partner (Warner), a Wynk receivable had to be settled for Rs.12 crores, and the executive who negotiated the Warner and YouTube deals (Hari Nair, CEO FY24-FY26) has exited with no replacement found.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Government Dependency (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Technological Innovation (WATCH); New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH); Cyclicality (LOW); Good Enough Goods (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 9 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
