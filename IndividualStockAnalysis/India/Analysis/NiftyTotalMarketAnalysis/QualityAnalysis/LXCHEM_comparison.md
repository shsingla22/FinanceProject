### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 2 worsened, 3 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Strained on the long view, Strained on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Even in Specialties — the supposedly differentiated half — Laxmi held market share (~8-9% of diketene derivatives) only by conceding price: management attributes 12% of the Q3 specialty revenue decline to 'market price moderation' and frames the Essentials strategy as 'volume-driven profitable growth' rather than premium defence, indicating these are substitutable intermediates sold to industrial buyers on price. (The long view had said: The Essentials book (ethyl acetate) is an undifferentiated commodity priced off spreads with no brand protection, and even the Specialties book proved unable to hold price — management concedes there are no cost-plus formulas and that roughly 12% of the Specialties decline was pure 'market price moderation' passed through from cheaper feedstocks, with the defence framed as cost position rather than premium.)

**That worsened:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Individual products and customers repeatedly explain the whole result: Q3's 30% specialty decline decomposes into one agro intermediate (~10%), one non-repeating campaign product (~5-6%) and price moderation (12%), while 'deferred deliveries to select global customers' shifted an entire half-year of revenue and management refused to quantify it on competitive grounds. The largest new growth leg is anchored on a single named counterparty, Hitachi Energy. (The long view had said: A single agro intermediate for one customer represented roughly 10% of top line and its unanticipated phase-out drove the 30% Specialties revenue decline in Q3 FY26 and a full-year FY26 drag; results are also repeatedly swung by deferred shipments from 'certain key customers', an SI plant whose capacity was contracted to one agrochemical major, and a multiyear Hitachi contract.)
- **Technological Innovation**: LOW → WATCH. Why now: The single largest specialty revenue hit of FY26 was product obsolescence — an agrochemical active ingredient being phased out took ~10% of specialty revenue, a substitution the company could see coming but not prevent. Offsetting this, the Hitachi Energy EconiQ contract puts Laxmi on the winning side of the SF6-to-eco-efficient-gas transition, and management frames growth around a new-product-development cycle rather than defending a legacy format. (The long view had said: Over five years no call raises platform obsolescence, competing technology formats or analyst questions on disruption; innovation is incremental and additive — moving from acetyl bulk into diketene specialties and then into fluoro intermediates via proven Miteni technology, with continuous new-product development and customer qualification cycles.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Fashion Risk (QUANT FLAG); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (HIGH RISK); Government Dependency (WATCH); New Entrants (WATCH).*

**Financial resilience:** Strained on the long view (1 of the 3 stress checks is flagged — one warning light, not yet a pattern.) vs Strained on the one-year view (1 of the 3 stress checks is flagged — one warning light, not yet a pattern.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 6 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
