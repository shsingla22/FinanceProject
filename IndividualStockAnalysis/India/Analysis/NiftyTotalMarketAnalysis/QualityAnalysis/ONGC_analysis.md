Where the two halves do touch, they tell one story. The lumpy growth and the thin gross retention point back to the same place: a business whose selling prices and input costs are largely set outside its own doors, which is also why no pricing-power or brand pattern could be established. Underlying resilience is judged SOUND, and the combined rating lands at 46 out of 100 — 45% weight on business quality at 52, 30% on multibagger fit at 10, 25% on risk safety at 80 — a Mixed, two-star verdict.
