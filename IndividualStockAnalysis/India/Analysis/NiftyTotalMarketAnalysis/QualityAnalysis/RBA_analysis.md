# Restaurant Brands (RBA) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Restaurant Brands Asia (RBA), formerly Burger King India, runs fast-food restaurants under franchise rights to global brands — mainly Burger King, plus Popeyes. It sells burgers, chicken, coffee and other quick-service food directly to walk-in and delivery customers, so revenue comes from the food sold at its own company-operated outlets rather than from franchise fees. It operates in two countries: India, where it had 577 restaurants across 100+ cities as of December 2025 (revenue about INR 577 crore in that quarter), and Indonesia, which it acquired in March 2022. Profit comes from growing same-store sales and traffic while squeezing costs — better supply chain, lower delivery discounts, cheaper utilities — with India profitable and growing and Indonesia a turnaround story.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Burger King India** — The core and by far the largest business — burger restaurants built on a value menu (2-for-INR79/INR99 deals) plus core and premium items, grown from the first store in 2014 to about 580 outlets and targeting close to 600 by March 2026, delivering the group's positive same-store sales and rising restaurant- and company-level EBITDA.
- **BK Café** — Coffee and beverage counters built inside existing Burger King India restaurants to add a new day-part; rolled out very fast from FY22 (86 by June 2022) to 351 cafés by mid-2024, with all new restaurants now opening with one.
- **Delivery business (India)** — Orders fulfilled off-premise, run as a separate profit focus from dine-in; management has raised its margin by roughly 2 percentage points by cutting discounts and shifting product mix, while dine-in traffic grew 18% over three years.
- **Digital / app and self-ordering kiosks** — The ordering and customer-relationship layer — app, delivery and self-ordering kiosks now account for about 91-92% of all India orders, with monthly active users up about 51% year over year; it is a channel and cost-control tool rather than a separate revenue segment.
- **Burger King Indonesia** — Acquired in March 2022 and much smaller than India (about INR 152 crore of quarterly revenue in mid-2022); after years of losses it has turned around, with four consecutive positive same-store-sales quarters, positive EBITDA and heavily cut corporate overhead by FY26.
- **Popeyes Indonesia** — A fried-chicken brand RBA got master franchise rights for in 2022 (a plan of 300 restaurants over 10 years, with USD 5 million deposited), but it stalled at only 24-25 outlets, is described by management as struggling, and they now say it should sit in someone else's portfolio rather than theirs.
