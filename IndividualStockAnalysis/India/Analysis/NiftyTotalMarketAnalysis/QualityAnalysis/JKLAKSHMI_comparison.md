### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 0 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 2 weakened, 5 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: There is an active brand portfolio and premiumisation push — Green Plus/Pro Plus rejuvenated, Platinum Heavy Duty and Platinum Supremo absorbed from the UCWL merger, premium mix up from 23% to 26% of volume in Q2 FY26. But no heritage/A&P/distributor-leverage narrative, and the premium mix does not translate into price realisation, which fell. (The long view had said: A real premiumisation effort exists — premium products rose from 14% (FY22) to 21% (Nov-22) to ~30% (Aug-24) of trade volume, with blended-cement and trade-mix targets — but the brand does not command premium pricing versus leaders (it sells at a persistent Rs.20/bag-plus discount) and there is no heritage, A&P or adjacency narrative on any call.)
- **Innovation Dominance**: NO FIT → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: No R&D spend, pipeline or named product innovation is disclosed on any of the four calls; digital/AI is discussed only as an internal efficiency lever, not as innovation-driven pricing power. (The long view had said: Across 16 calls there is no R&D spend, pipeline or named-product-launch disclosure; 'innovation' is confined to process efficiency — AI/ML on pyro-process and grinding, digital supply chain — which management concedes it cannot even quantify.)

**That weakened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: No discussion of licences, AMCs, subscriptions, attach rates, renewals or customer advances in any of the four calls. (The long view had said: the calls are silent and the numbers are mixed (1 of 3 checks supporting).)
- **Cost to Replicate**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Nothing described would stop a funded entrant — the industry added a record 64 million tons of capacity in FY26 taking national capacity to 712mt at 69% utilisation, and JK Lakshmi's own advantages (efficiency programmes, brands, grinding units) are exactly what rivals are building. Limestone leases are the only scarce asset and the company is itself acquiring them. (The long view had said: There are genuine finite-resource barriers — limestone leases at Sirohi, Udaipur (40-50 years), Nagaur (45-50 years), Kutch and 250mt of auctioned Assam reserves, plus captive railway sidings — which a well-funded newcomer cannot simply rebuild. But these are industry-wide rather than JKLC-specific, and the AMDCL Assam contract cancellation (INR130cr stuck in litigation) plus Aravalli/Supreme Court delays at Nagaur show the barrier cuts against the company too.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL).*

*Unchanged: Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 3 worsened, 3 unchanged. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Government Dependency**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: Policy directly moved the company's realisations within the window: the 22nd September 2025 GST reduction caused prices to fall 'beyond GST pass-through level' and confused trade channel pricing, and management repeatedly attributes demand swings to infrastructure spending and to elections (Bihar, and again in May 2026) pulling labour off sites. Pricing is not regulator-set and there is no PSU-tender dependence, which bounds the exposure. (The long view had said: Fortunes repeatedly hinge on state action: limestone leases (Sirohi renewal due 2030, Nagaur and Kutch leases, mines won 'under the auction route'), the AMDCL mining development agreement cancellation forcing a Rs.130 cr receivable now sitting in a High Court hearing, Nagaur delayed pending a Supreme Court Aravalli ruling, the Sept-2025 GST rate cut that broke non-trade pricing, and demand swings driven by elections and government infrastructure spend.)
- **Stakeholder Concentration**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Results are discussed entirely at geography and channel level — trade roughly 49-53% of volume against non-trade/institutional, spread across North, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Haryana, West UP, Mumbai and a small East presence — with no named customer, licensor or supplier driving outcomes and no analyst question about a single relationship's renewal. (The long view had said: No single customer, licensor or supplier is named as driving results, but management concedes an unusually narrow geographic footprint that behaves like concentration — Gujarat/Mumbai, Rajasthan-Haryana-west UP and Chhattisgarh only — so one state's non-trade pricing swung group realisation by ~9% in Q3 FY26; the sales base itself is a wide dealer/institutional network.)

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Cement is a price-taking commodity tied to construction activity, and across all four calls management frames results in terms of industry demand growth, regional price swings and input-cost cycles — prices slipped in Q3, pet coke jumped ~40% quarter-on-quarter and coal ~30% in Q4, while record 64 mt industry capacity addition pushed pan-India utilisation to ~69%. Simultaneously the company is committing ~Rs. 3,000 crore to Durg plus three greenfields to reach 30 mt by FY30, i.e. heavy capex into a period management itself calls slowing with weak pricing power. (The long view had said: Cement demand and price swing hard across the four years: Nov-2022 prices under pressure and fuel at Rs.12,000-13,000/t, Aug-2024 'price level which is the lowest in the last three years', Feb-2026 realisation down ~9-10% quarter-on-quarter post-GST, and May-2026 record 64 mt industry capacity addition with utilisation at 69% and pet coke up 40% quarter-on-quarter; EBITDA/t fell from ~Rs.1,000 to ~Rs.730 while the company commits Rs.3,000 cr to Durg and Rs.1,500-2,000 cr annual capex into a softening price cycle.)
- **Shifting Consumer Preferences**: NO SIGNAL → LOW. Why now: Demand is functional construction consumption — housing and infrastructure — and the only portfolio movement described is upward, with premium share rising from 23% to 26% of volume and rejuvenated Green Plus / Pro Plus brands; no volume decline in any quarter is attributed to changing end-user habits. (The long view had said: the judge read the calls and found nothing, and the only computed fingerprint is clean.)
- **Fashion Risk**: NO SIGNAL → LOW. Why now: Nothing in the four calls ties growth to a trend, celebrity or style cycle; volumes are explained by grinding-unit commissioning, geography mix, labour availability and price, i.e. a utilitarian building material bought on availability and price. (The long view had said: the judge read the calls and found nothing, and all 2 computed fingerprints are clean.)

*Unchanged: New Entrants (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
