*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

Why now: Explicit, rising share numbers across every segment in the window: ~29% of transmission projects bid in FY26 with the pipeline rising from INR60,000cr (Oct) to ~INR78,000cr (Jan); India's largest smart-metering platform at 1.34 crore installed, going to ~4.7 crore with the IntelliSmart acquisition; C&I load roughly doubling within two quarters. Share is being taken on cost of capital and execution rather than by underwriting deterioration. (The long view had said: Persistent, quantified share gain across all three years: transmission network grew 4% vs a 1% market in Q1 FY24, share of projects bid rose from ~20% (Jan 2025) to ~29% (Apr 2026), and in smart meters AESL installed 83 lakh against a 70 lakh target — reportedly matching all other players combined — then bought IntelliSmart to reach a 47 million-meter book. Crucially, management repeatedly caps bidding to protect returns, so the share is not bought.)

There is no franchising, own-store or D2C economics of the classic pattern, and the C&I sell-side is still being tied up. (The long view had said: the calls are silent and no computable check supports the pattern.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Brand Strength (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 9 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
