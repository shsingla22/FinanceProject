**Checks that improved:**

- **Partial monopoly / broken competition / switching costs** (Industry Structure): Weak → Good. Now: Lock-in is brand-habit rather than contractual switching cost, but the evidenced loyalty through extreme price moves plus deepening direct rural reach functions as a partial regional moat. | The long view had said: Consumer FMCG here has no switching costs or captive aftermarket — the 2022 collapse in Saffola showed customers downtrading away instantly on price.
- **Barriers to entry and rationality** (Industry Structure): Neutral → Good. Now: Profit margins have barely moved over the years (±2.1 points) — consistent with rational pricing and few new competitors. And from the calls: Pricing conduct is rational — costs passed through in calibrated steps, benefits passed back on the way down, and unprofitable volume declined rather than defended — but entry barriers in foods and D2C are low, as Marico's own acquisition of small challenger brands demonstrates. | The long view had said: Profit margins have barely moved over the years (±1.7 points) — consistent with rational pricing and few new competitors. And from the calls: Entry barriers are low and pricing behaviour is periodically irrational, with competitors selling below cost and national players running BTL-driven discount strategies.
- **Gain in market share** (Growth): Good → Excellent. Now: Consistent, quantified share capture across all four calls — VAHO value share to an all-time-high ~30% (+150bps MAT), Saffola Oats consolidating leadership, and >95% of the business gaining or sustaining share — with meaningful runway left in foods and premium personal care. | The long view had said: Persistent, verified share capture across the years (2022: highest gains among organized CNO players; 2026: >95% of portfolio gaining/sustaining share), but the core coconut oil franchise at ~64% is already dominant, limiting runway there.
- **Technology advantage** (Competitive Advantage): Weak → Neutral. Now: The claimed edge is digital go-to-market, media buying and first-party data rather than product technology; no R&D intensity or proprietary technical benefit is evidenced. | The long view had said: No proprietary technology edge is claimed; the advantages cited are brand, procurement, supply chain and media, with AI/analytics mentioned only as an operating investment.

**Checks that regressed:**

Now: Management leans promotional — a standalone 'Transforming into a Digital-first Powerhouse' investor event, superlatives about the pricing action, and self-positioning as shaping the category rather than participating in it. | The long view had said: Commentary is operationally dense and explicitly prefers delivery over announcement, though the company is a widely covered large-cap that stages standalone strategy calls with promotional framing.
- **Advertisement and promotion** (Capital Allocation): Excellent → Good. Now: A&P was sustained through an unprecedented input-cost squeeze and is being re-pointed to digital (55% of core ad spend, 100% in digital brands), with brand equity evidenced by >95% of the portfolio gaining/sustaining share, though absolute A&P-to-sales ratios are not given. | The long view had said: A&P has been protected even through margin pressure, shifted to efficient master-brand and digital media (55% of core ad spend), and translates into >95% of the portfolio gaining or sustaining share.

Margins have moved about ±2.1 points a year — very steady, a sign costs are under control. | The long view had said: Out of every ₹100 of sales, ₹17 is operating profit. Now: Recent deals (4700BC ~INR140cr ARR, Cosmix ~INR100cr ARR at high-teen EBITDA, Candid/Skinetiq at mid-20s EBITDA) are small, already-profitable, complementary bolt-ons bought without a FOMO premium, with Beardo (5x) and Plix (6x) as post-deal proof points; marked down from +2 because four deals landed in quick succession and Plix's margin still needs repair. | The long view had said: A string of small, complementary bolt-ons (Beardo, True Elements, Plix, Just Herbs, Kaya, 4700BC, Cosmix, Skinetiq) bought at disciplined valuations with a stated screen for existing unit economics, and post-deal scale-up plus margin improvement is demonstrated (Beardo 5x, Plix 6x).

Now: Headline 'multi-year high' revenue was substantially a commodity pass-through artefact rather than operating skill, so the strong optics need discounting even though share gains and Foods margin repair are genuinely skill-driven. | The long view had said: The assessment survives halo-stripping because management itself separates skill from windfall — distinguishing offtake from primary sales, pricing from volume, and flagging copra deflation as a cyclical tailwind — though FY26's record growth is partly commodity-price driven.

### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 6 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 6. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 1 weakened, 8 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → STRONG FIT. Why now: More than 95% of the portfolio gained or sustained market share in every quarter of the window with penetration gains rising from 75% to over 90%, VAHO value share hit an all-time high of ~30%, and international share gains came through localised franchises in Bangladesh, Vietnam, MENA and South Africa — and the share was not bought, since they explicitly sacrificed sub-threshold-margin Saffola volumes. (The long view had said: Share gains are quantified and rising across the whole period — 96% of portfolio gaining share in FY23, >90% in FY25, >95% in FY26 — including record 64% coconut-oil share and VAHO value-share turnaround, and gains were explicitly not bought with margin (declined bottom-of-pyramid discounting).)
- **Low Price Plus**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: There is a genuine, repeatedly cited cost culture ('institutionalized resilience in cost management', supply-chain and back-end advantage over smaller players, price-point packs and grammage management), but the stated strategic direction is premiumisation and mix upgrade rather than low price plus scale. (The long view had said: Across the period management repeatedly refused to chase volume at low price — declining to fight deep-discounters in coconut oil (Aug 2022) and refusing 'BTL driven' price war at the bottom of the pyramid in hair oils (Aug 2024); the stated model is premium positioning plus cost discipline, not low price plus scale.)

**That weakened:**

- **Friendly Middleman**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Demand is driven by consumer brand pull, A&P and retail distribution (Project SETU, quick commerce, general trade), not by professionals prescribing to a paying end customer; the only professional touchpoint is Kaya's 'dermatologist-backed solutions' and a planned chemist/cosmetic outlet expansion, which is channel reach rather than expert recommendation. (The long view had said: the calls are silent and the numbers are mixed (1 of 2 checks supporting).)

*Unchanged: Pricing Power (STRONG FIT); Brand Strength (STRONG FIT); Corporate Culture (STRONG FIT); Innovation Dominance (LIKELY FIT); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (QUANT SIGNAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 6 unchanged. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Shifting Consumer Preferences**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: The calls show Marico moving with the health/nutrition and online-shopping shifts rather than being eroded by them — a foods portfolio explicitly built around 'better-for-you' positioning, protein deficiency as the stated Cosmix thesis, and new-business share of India revenue rising to 23% en route to a third by FY30. Volume softness in the last year is attributed to pricing, not changing habits. (The long view had said: The portfolio is being actively re-pointed at shifting preferences — health/nutrition (protein deficiency, vegan/vegetarian, clean label, 'free-from'), premiumisation, and the channel migration to e-commerce/quick commerce — while legacy mass segments show the strain, with VAHO volumes declining and down-trading from premium to base edible oils in FY23. The pivot is working rather than failing, but the demand base of the mass core is genuinely migrating.)

**That worsened:**

- **Government Dependency**: LOW → WATCH. Why now: No government contracts or tariffs set Marico's revenue, but policy visibly moves the P&L in every recent call: the September GST rate change caused transitionary trade-channel disruption, ~30% of the India business had to pass benefits through as price cuts or grammage, and management cites GST, MSPs and monsoon policy as demand drivers. (The long view had said: Revenue is consumer-brand sales with company-set MRPs and calibrated pricing actions; government appears only as a demand-side macro factor (GST rate rationalisation aiding affordability, budgetary allocation to the rural economy), never as a regulator of price, subsidy or tender, and there is no fixed-infrastructure or PSU-order dependence anywhere in the calls.)

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); New Entrants (WATCH); Fashion Risk (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 3 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
