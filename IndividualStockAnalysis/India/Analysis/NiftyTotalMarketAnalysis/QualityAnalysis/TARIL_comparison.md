### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 1 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 0. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 2 weakened, 8 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Low Price Plus**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: There is a genuine, itemised cost programme — six backward-integration plants (CTC, pressboard, RIP bushings, fabrication, CRGO already commissioned) targeting 80-85% of raw material in-house and a claimed 150-200bps margin uplift — but management explicitly keeps the savings as margin and screens orders for profitability rather than competing on price at scale. (The long view had said: The strategy is the opposite of low-price-plus: from Jan 2025 through Jul 2026 management repeatedly turned away volume to chase margin and payment terms, and backward-integration savings (150-300bps) are explicitly retained as margin rather than passed to price; there is no no-frills cost culture with high asset turns.)

**That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Volume and order-book expansion are evidenced (record 33,763 MVA produced in FY26, order book +26% year-on-year, Q1 FY27 inflow of INR2,114 crores including a >INR1,000 crore PGCIL ultra-mega order) but management gives no market-share numbers or comparison against named competitors, and FY26 inflow of INR2,374 crores was deliberately moderated. (The long view had said: Capacity-based position (second largest in India), 75,000+ MVA capacity, 5-year revenue CAGR of 27%, an unprecedented INR1,000+ crore single PGCIL order and first US/Australia export wins all point to gain — but management never quotes a market-share number or compares against named peers, and it deliberately capped order intake in FY26, letting the order book stall at INR5,000 crore versus the INR8,000 crore promised.)
- **Pricing Power**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Realisations are set by tender, not by the company: Q2 FY26 margins were hit by 'delivery of the last batch of low-margin orders', and the stated route to better pricing is order selection and cost-out, not price increases without volume loss. (The long view had said: EBITDA margin rose from single digits (Q1 FY24) to ~15.5% and the global 2-3 year transformer shortage lets TARIL refuse orders beyond 24 months and cherry-pick price — but this is cycle scarcity plus mix/backward integration, not structural pricing power, since domestic orders are awarded on L1 lowest-bid basis and renewables/distribution transformers are admittedly low margin.)

*Unchanged: Brand Strength (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (NO FIT); Friendly Middleman (NO FIT); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Forward Integrators (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 0 eased, 1 worsened, 4 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Strained on the long view, Strained on the one-year view.

**That worsened:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: On the customer side PGCIL alone accounted for roughly half of the INR2,114cr Q1 FY27 inflow and also gates TARIL's growth through facility approvals (radiator approved, tank approval 'initiated'), so one relationship drives both orders and qualification. On the supply side, concentration is already proven damaging: a shortage of CTC materially cut Q2 FY26 production and revenue, and management concedes 'component availability remains a key bottleneck globally'. (The long view had said: Results are repeatedly driven by named counterparties: the GETCO stop-deal and unpaid INR183 crores single-handedly caused the FY24 Q1 collapse and forced deliberate slowdown of dispatches, and by Q1FY27 a single PGCIL 'ultra mega' order above INR1,000 crores accounted for roughly half the quarter's inflow, with PGCIL also controlling the approvals TARIL needs (radiator, tank, HVDC empanelment). Receivables of INR1,057 crores at 130 days concentrate this exposure on the balance sheet.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (NO SIGNAL); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (HIGH RISK); Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
