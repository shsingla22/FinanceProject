### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 2 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 1. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 0 strengthened, 2 weakened, 8 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That weakened:**

- **Corporate Culture**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Several promises were delivered on time — the speciality carbon black doubling to 130,000 MTPA (guided Oct 2025, in trial production by Jan 2026), the Mangalore liquid-pitch terminal, and the FY24 PAT-doubling goal reached ahead of the FY27 target — alongside long-tenured family leadership and R&D/ROCE-anchored language. Against that, the LFP Phase 1 goalpost quietly moved (Oct 2025/Jan 2026: 40,000 MTPA on track for Q3 FY27; April 2026: only a 2,000 MTPA first milestone by Q3 FY27 with full Phase 1 pushed to FY29) without the calls owning it as a delay, and CFO/MD churn sits in the recent record. (The long view had said: Promises made on earlier calls were delivered and checked back against: the FY25 guidance to double PAT in three years was achieved in two, FY26 commissioned the carbon black expansion and 600k debottlenecking as promised, balance sheet stayed net-cash with ROCE 32-34%, and promoters raised their stake to 52.5% via warrant conversion at INR316; the tone is promotional and bad news is deflected (the volume decline was explained away), which is the main qualifier.)
- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The four calls (Oct 2025–Jul 2026) contain no discussion of AMC/service contracts, subscriptions, attach or renewal rates, installed base monetisation, or customer advances/deferred revenue as a funding source. Himadri sells product volumes (pitch, carbon black, tyres, battery materials) with no recurring stream described. (The long view had said: No discussion of service/AMC contracts, subscriptions, attach rates, renewals, deferred revenue or customer advances anywhere across the six calls — the model is pure product tonnage sales.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
