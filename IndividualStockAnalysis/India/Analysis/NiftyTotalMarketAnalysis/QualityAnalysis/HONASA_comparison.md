### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 3 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 4. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 2 weakened, 7 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Real evidence of brand-building — Kantar Brand Power ranked Mamaearth #1 in face cleanser and shampoo among online buyers, Derma Co the #1 sunscreen brand per Euromonitor, brand health at multi-quarter highs, and successful stretch into new categories (hair care, moisturizers) — but the brands are 8-9 years old with no heritage moat, and Mamaearth needed a costly reset before returning to growth in FY26. (The long view had said: Real brand assets — Mamaearth in the top-15 BPC brands, most-searched BPC brand, Kantar Brand Power Index up three straight years, six brands past Rs.100cr ARR and stretch into new categories — but heritage is <10 years and the FY25 Mamaearth decline showed the brand's durability failing its first real test before recovering to mid-teens growth by FY26.)
- **Friendly Middleman**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Almost all demand is pulled directly by the consumer through e-commerce, influencers and Kantar brand-power surveys rather than by a prescriber; the only middleman-flavoured element is the chemist-channel expansion for The Derma Co, with no professional training or endorsement programme described in any of the four calls. (The long view had said: Across all calls the model is consumer-pull, not expert-recommended: management explicitly states brands are built in consumers' minds and bought on platforms, and dismisses the beauty-advisor counter channel as sub-scale (<500 counters, <20% of GT sales). BBlunt salons and hairstyling certification exist but are described as brand-equity/CSR activity, not a prescriber channel.)

**That weakened:**

- **Innovation Dominance**: STRONG FIT → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Innovation is a stated pillar with named launches each year and blind-test-winning reformulations (Tinosorb M filters, peptide-stem cell hair care), and a dedicated Chief Innovation Officer — but no R&D spend figure or share-of-industry-R&D is disclosed, and the 'innovation' is formulation/marketing-led rather than a defensible technology lead. (The long view had said: High gross margins fund a repeatable, named innovation cadence every year — Multani Mitti/Rosemary/sunscreen stick in FY24, Tinosorb M filters and peptide-stem-cell haircare in FY26 — with quantified revenue impact (13% of H1 FY24; 7-8% of FY26 growth from one-year-old launches) and a dedicated Chief Innovation Officer plus blind-test-winning formulation discipline; R&D spend itself is never disclosed.)
- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Honasa is a one-shot consumable BPC seller — the calls describe repeat purchase via hero SKUs and distribution, never license fees, AMCs, subscriptions, renewal or attach rates. Negative working capital is present but comes from trade/distributor terms and marketplace settlement, not billed-in-advance recurring contracts. (The long view had said: the calls are silent and the numbers are mixed (2 of 3 checks supporting).)

*Unchanged: Market Share Gainers (STRONG FIT); Pricing Power (LIKELY FIT); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Low Price Plus (NO FIT); Cost to Replicate (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
