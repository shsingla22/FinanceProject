### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 4 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Electricity is a flow product sold into operations, and ~80% is under long-term PPAs, but the calls repeatedly show earnings swinging on merchant power prices, wind seasonality and grid curtailment — Q3 FY26 revenue per MW fell despite capacity growth, and the company runs a very large, continuous capex programme (INR8,800 crore in one quarter, 4.5-5 GW p.a.) that must be funded through the cycle. (The long view had said: Electricity is a flow product with 25-year PPAs covering the large majority of capacity, but the calls repeatedly show earnings swinging on merchant prices, weather and curtailment — solar merchant realisation fell to INR2.20 while energy sales grew 42% against only 31% revenue growth in Q1FY26, and FY26 lost INR1,300-1,500 crores of EBITDA to curtailment and weak merchant pricing; growth is also funded by very heavy capex (INR42,000 crores guided for FY27).)

**That worsened:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Offtake is concentrated in a small set of state DISCOM counterparties, and in Q1 FY27 management disclosed that essentially all previously-merchant capacity, including the new battery portfolio, has been contracted to a single related party, Adani Energy Solutions (~4 GW) — one relationship now underpins a large share of non-DISCOM revenue. (The long view had said: Offtake is concentrated in government counterparties and, from FY26-27, increasingly in a single related party — all previously merchant solar, wind and battery capacity has been contracted to sister company Adani Energy Solutions at board-approved 'arm's length' rates for 15-25 years, and ~4-5GW already sits there; generation is also physically concentrated at one site (Khavda, ~10GW of 20GW) and evacuation depends on a handful of transmission lines.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Fashion Risk (LOW); New Entrants (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Technological Innovation (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
