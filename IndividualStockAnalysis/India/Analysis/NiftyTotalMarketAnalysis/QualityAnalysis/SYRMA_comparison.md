### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 1 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 1. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 1 weakened, 7 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Across all four FY26 calls there is no discussion of AMC/service revenue, attach rates, renewals, subscriptions or customer advances funding the business; revenue is discussed purely as build-to-print/ODM manufacturing volumes and an open order book. (The long view had said: Across all 15 calls the revenue model is order-book based EMS execution (INR6,400 crore open order book, Q3 FY26) with no license, AMC, subscription or attach-rate discussion; the business consumes working capital rather than being funded by customer advances — 80 days in Nov 2022, 62–76 days through FY25–FY26.)
- **Cost to Replicate**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: There are real but rebuildable barriers: ECMS-approved PCB capacity with anti-dumping protection (first of three approved), a 30-year EU export record with a German plant, an EcoVadis gold rating and long customer qualification cycles — yet EMS capacity is fundamentally capital-and-time replicable by a well-funded entrant, and Syrma is itself the new entrant in PCBs. (The long view had said: Nothing in the calls articulates why a well-funded entrant could not replicate the model: SMT lines are bought and commissioned in months, cost and quality are described as industry hygiene, contracts carry pass-through pricing, and growth depends on PLI incentives, tariffs and FTAs rather than a durable structural barrier.)

**That weakened:**

- **Low Price Plus**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: There is a genuine efficiency/working-capital discipline (69 to 63 days, ROCE 12.4% to 16.9%, net cash of INR467 crores), but the strategy in FY26 was explicitly to exit low-price volume rather than to pass scale savings into price — consumer was deliberately capped below 35% to lift gross margin ~500bps. (The long view had said: Classic low-margin/high-turns EMS economics with a visible cost and working-capital discipline — EBITDA margin 4.6% (Q1 FY25) rising to 11.3% (FY26), working capital cut 80→63 days and ROCE 12.4%→16.9% (20.1% goodwill-adjusted) — but the cost culture is presented as table stakes for the industry, not an un-copyable advantage.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Corporate Culture (STRONG FIT); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT); Brand Strength (NO FIT); Forward Integrators (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 1 worsened, 3 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view; 1 could not be compared. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Customer concentration remains material — top 5 at 34% and top 10 at 47% of FY26 revenue — and management highlighted a single framework contract with 'one of our major customers' worth $250 million over two to three years, so one relationship carries visible weight; the base is broadening though, with top 10 down from 56-57% in H1 FY26 to 47% for the year and eight new customers onboarded. (The long view had said: FY26 disclosure puts the top 5 customers at 34% and top 10 at 47% of revenue, and single-relationship events move the numbers — export weakness in FY25 was attributed to a planned slowdown at one existing customer, while the consumer vertical (up to 53% of revenue in Q1 FY25) rests on a narrow high-volume telecom set. Trajectory is toward broadening rather than tightening.)
- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Management repeatedly describes the consumer vertical as structurally low-margin and deliberately caps it below 35% of revenue, which is an admission that a large slice of EMS work is price-commoditised and cannot be defended on value; the defence is mix shift into auto, industrial, healthcare, ODM and exports rather than pricing power in the commodity work itself. (The long view had said: The high-volume consumer/telecom work is explicitly commodity-grade EMS — gross margin fell to 16% when it hit 53% of mix, and management calls it low-margin and consciously caps it, which is an admission that a large slice of the business competes on price rather than differentiation. The defence is a deliberate shift toward design-led, harder-to-substitute work.)

**That worsened:**

- **Fashion Risk**: NO SIGNAL → LOW. Why now: Demand is functional, contracted B2B manufacturing for auto, industrial, healthcare, IT/railways and defence customers, with growth attributed to order books, framework contracts and vertical mix rather than any trending product or brand moment; management points to a INR5,800 crore order book and multi-year contracts as the visibility driver. (The long view had said: the judge read the calls and found nothing, and the only computed fingerprint is clean.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (QUANT FLAG).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Cyclicality (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW).*

*Not comparable: Shifting Consumer Preferences (NOT ASSESSED → NOT ASSESSED — could not be assessed on either view).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
