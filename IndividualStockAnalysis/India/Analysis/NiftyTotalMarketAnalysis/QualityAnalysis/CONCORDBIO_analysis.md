# Concord Biotech (CONCORDBIO) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Concord Biotech is an Ahmedabad-based drug ingredient maker that grows active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) using fermentation — brewing microbes and purifying the output — rather than ordinary chemical synthesis. Its main products are immunosuppressant APIs (drugs used after organ transplants), where management says it is the world's only supplier with the complete fermentation-based portfolio, plus a growing range of oncology, anti-infective and antifungal APIs. It sells these bulk ingredients to other pharmaceutical companies in India and abroad (roughly half domestic, half export in FY26, with the US, Europe and the Middle East as key markets), and since 2016 it has also made finished medicines itself — tablets/capsules, and more recently injectables and soft gels — sold under its own brand in India and to customers overseas. Revenue is about 80% APIs and 20% finished formulations; FY26 revenue fell about 12% on tariff uncertainty, a regulatory approval delay that blocked European supplies for about three months, and a stalled Middle East tender, with reported EBITDA margin around 35%.

**The parts of the business:**

- **API business (fermentation-based active pharmaceutical ingredients)** — Makes and sells bulk drug ingredients — immunosuppressants (its leadership area), oncology, anti-infectives and antifungals — to pharma manufacturers worldwide; it is the core of the company at roughly 80% of revenue, INR 829 crores in FY26.
- **Formulations (oral solid dosage)** — The Valthera facility, started in 2016, makes finished tablets and capsules (capacity around 802 million units) sold in India — mainly critical care, nephrology and rheumatology through a field force of 175+ people — as well as in emerging and regulated markets; it is the smaller leg at roughly 20-26% of revenue depending on the quarter.
- **Injectables facility** — A newer plant that started production in FY26 and has WHO-GMP certification, aimed at own-brand domestic sales, contract manufacturing and government supply contracts; management puts its peak revenue potential at about INR 600 crores, though start-up costs dented margins in FY26.
- **CDMO / second-source supply** — Contract development and manufacturing for other drug companies, including acting as a backup ('second source') supplier — described as small today but in advanced discussions, with supplies begun to two innovator companies in FY26.
- **Stellon Biotech (US subsidiary)** — A newly set-up American arm that gives Concord its own marketing, distribution and commercialisation presence in the US; still an early-stage cost rather than a revenue contributor.
- **Concord Lifegen (India subsidiary)** — A wholly owned company incorporated to strengthen marketing, sales and distribution of Concord's products within India.
- **Cell and gene therapy investment (Celliimune Biotech)** — An investment into a cell-and-gene-therapy venture described as a long-term growth platform; no revenue attributed to it in the calls.
- **Soft gel facility** — A newly commercialised soft gelatin capsule line described as an additional avenue for revenue; no size disclosed.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*

### Government Dependency — HIGH RISK
*"Fortunes decided in Delhi"*

**Why this verdict:** the calls show clear, repeated exposure; this risk has no numeric fingerprint to cross-check, so it is judged from the calls alone.
