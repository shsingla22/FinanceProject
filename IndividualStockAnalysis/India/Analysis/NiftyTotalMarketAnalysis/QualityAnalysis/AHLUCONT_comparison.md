### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 0 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 1. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 1 weakened, 8 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Reputation in complex buildings wins marquee, prequalification-heavy work (CSMT, Central Vista INR2,600 crores) and repeat blue-chip private clients like DLF, SmartWorld and Emaar — but this is contractor credentialling, not heritage brand power, and it delivers no price premium. (The long view had said: A real reputation asset in building EPC — repeat marquee clients (DLF, Adani, Google, Tata Memorial, Central Vista, CSMT) and prequalification credibility that management says limits competition in complex private work — but there is no premium pricing (margins compressed), no A&P, and no consumer/heritage brand economics.)

**That weakened:**

- **Cost to Replicate**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Nothing in the last-12-months evidence explains why a well-funded entrant could not replicate the position: work is won on lowest bid in a crowded field, execution is exposed to labour supply, NGT bans and client clearances, and roughly 40-44% order-book concentration in NCR is a vulnerability rather than a barrier. (The long view had said: There is a time-based barrier a funded newcomer cannot buy quickly — decades of prequalification track record, in-house multi-discipline EPC design capability built over 4-5 years, and a 17-state site network — which is why only a handful of contractors qualify for complex private high-rise work. But the economic proof fails: management says margin compression is industry-wide and structural, indicating the barrier does not translate into excess returns.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (NO FIT); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT); Innovation Dominance (NO FIT); Forward Integrators (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 0 eased, 1 worsened, 4 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Revenue is entirely a function of customers' capital projects — real-estate developer launches and government building capex — with no flow/consumable component; FY26 saw guidance cut mid-year from 15-20% to 10-15%, Q4 PAT de-grew 3.6% YoY, and EBITDA margin swung from 6.58% (1QFY25) to 10.92% (2QFY26) back to 9.05-9.35%, showing how much execution and margin fluctuate. Management also concedes structural margin de-rating from 13-14% to ~9.5% while capex intensity rises, so peak-cycle economics are not recoverable.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
