### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 1 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 1 weakened, 5 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view; 2 could not be compared.

*Foundation test: FAIL on the long view, FAIL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → STRONG FIT. Why now: The March 2026 governance call and Q1 FY27 call both lean on the HDFC brand as the institutional asset — 30 years of stakeholder delivery, trust and integrity as the 'tone at the top' — and it was stress-tested in this very window: after the chairman's abrupt resignation the bank reported stable and strong deposit flows. (The long view had said: Thirty years of consistent delivery and the deposit inflows that held through the March 2026 chairman-resignation and Dubai-branch shock demonstrate real trust equity, and market leadership across cards, wheels, salary and mortgages shows brand-led distribution. But the brand does not translate into premium pricing, and the FY26 events are a live durability test rather than a passed one.)
- **Market Share Gainers**: PARTIAL → STRONG FIT. Why now: Share gain is quantified and repeated across the window: deposit share up 30-50bp a year for five years, CASA share gained in FY26 on 9.5-10% growth, incremental and stock share gains again in Q1 FY27, plus 21-22% of system card issuance and 26-28% of card spends — and it is paired with, not bought at the expense of, 1.15% GNPA and a 125bp provisioning buffer. (The long view had said: Deposit share gain is genuine and repeated (30-50bps a year over five years, gains again in Q1 FY27) and the bank is #1 or #2 in cards, auto, mortgages and MSME. But on advances it deliberately ceded share for three years to fix the post-merger CD ratio — 5.4% loan growth in FY25 and 12% in FY26 against system 13.5-14% — so share gain is not persistent across the franchise, and relative branch/SA productivity versus system has narrowed.)

**That weakened:**

- **Cost to Replicate**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Concrete replication barriers are articulated — 9,700 branches, 100 million customers, a granular sub-₹3cr retail deposit base now at 47% of net accretion, ~$1bn of cumulative tech build and dominant settlement/acquiring plumbing built over three decades — which a funded entrant could not assemble quickly; but the same calls show the advantage is contestable at the margin, with 'intense' competition, thin corporate spreads and a cost-of-funds disadvantage to historical norms. (The long view had said: The replication test is concretely articulable and repeatedly evidenced: 9,700 branches (40% under five years of vintage) at INR330 crores per branch, 100 million customers, three decades of cycle-tested underwriting at 1.15% GNPA, a licence-gated deposit franchise, and ~$1bn of accumulated tech stack — none buyable in a few years by a funded entrant.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL).*

*Unchanged: Toll Roads (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

*Not comparable: Friendly Middleman (NOT ASSESSED → NOT ASSESSED — could not be assessed on either view); Forward Integrators (NOT ASSESSED → PARTIAL — could not be assessed on the long view).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 1 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
