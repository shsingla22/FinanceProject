### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 0 eased, 1 worsened, 5 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Across all four FY26 calls management frames results in terms of an industry pricing cycle and input-cost swings — 'several quarters of pricing pressure', a negative 5% rate variance in Q3, and a recovery explicitly linked to crude prices — while EBITDA margin swung from 12.6% (Q1) to 12.9% (Q2) to 9.3% (Q3) back to 13.4% (Q4) even as revenue grew steadily, exactly the volatile-margin signature of a commodity-priced business; this ran alongside heavy expansion capex (Rs.150-200 crore guided for FY26) into Sayakha and Tarapur that then sat under-utilised through the soft patch. (The long view had said: Across all four years management explains results almost entirely through input-cost and realization cycles — crude, coal, solvents, forex in FY23; a multi-year price collapse (negative rate variance of ~10-11% in FY25, ~5% in FY26); and now the West Asia war lifting API prices again. Consolidated EBITDA margin has swung from >20% in COVID to 11.9% (Q1 FY25) to 9.3% (Q3 FY26) to 13.4% (Q4 FY26), while a ~INR600cr capex programme was being funded through the trough.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (ELEVATED); Shifting Consumer Preferences (QUANT FLAG).*

*Unchanged: Good Enough Goods (HIGH RISK); Government Dependency (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
