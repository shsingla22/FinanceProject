# Choice Intl. (CHOICEIN) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Choice International is an India-based financial services group that sells investing, lending and advisory services to ordinary retail savers, small businesses (MSMEs) and government bodies, with a deliberate push into Tier-2/Tier-3, semi-urban and rural markets. It makes money three ways: fees and commissions from stock broking, mutual-fund/wealth distribution and insurance sales; interest on small secured loans it lends off its own book through its NBFC arm; and fee income from consulting mandates for state and central government projects plus investment-banking fees for managing IPOs. Distribution runs through a network of over 67,000 self-employed 'Choice Business Associates', roughly 200+ branches, and in-house apps (Choice FinX, Choice Money, Choice Connect), with a new tie-up giving it access to India Post Payments Bank's postal network. It operates only in India; FY26 consolidated revenue was Rs 1,145 Cr with profit after tax of Rs 238 Cr, up 24% and 46% respectively.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Broking and Distribution** — The biggest piece at roughly 58-62% of revenue (Rs 179 Cr in Q4 FY26 with Rs 55 Cr pre-tax profit), it runs the retail stock-broking business — about 13 lakh demat accounts and Rs 52,482 Cr of client assets — and earns commissions on top by distributing mutual funds and wealth products to those same clients.
- **Wealth Product Distribution (within Broking and Distribution)** — Sells mutual funds and other investment products to retail clients, with AUM of about Rs 4,268 Cr in Q4 FY26; it grew largely through acquisitions (Arete Capital, and the wealth-distribution businesses of Fintoo and Glory), and the recent India Post Payments Bank mandate is meant to scale it further.
- **Insurance Distribution** — Sells life, health and motor policies for 40+ insurance partners through a 'POSP' agent model, collecting around Rs 84 Cr of premium and about 50,000 policies in Q4 FY26 — a small but steady cross-selling business rather than a main profit driver.
- **Asset Management (Choice AMC / Choice Mutual Fund)** — A newly built, wholly owned fund house that got final SEBI approval in FY26 and launched its first products — a Gold ETF, then the Nifty 50 and Nifty Next 50 index funds — so it is still at start-up scale with index and commodity products planned next.
- **NBFC (Choice Finserv)** — The lending arm, contributing about 13-15% of revenue (Rs 43 Cr revenue and only Rs 2 Cr pre-tax profit in Q4 FY26), which lends roughly Rs 800 Cr to MSMEs and individuals in semi-urban and rural areas — mostly secured loans such as micro-LAP, vehicle and rooftop-solar financing — at healthy margins (11-12% NIM) with net bad loans at 1.86%.
- **Advisory — Government Consulting (Choice Consultancy Services)** — Consults for state and central governments on infrastructure and development programmes (Jal Jeevan Mission, MSME awareness drives, digital PACS computerisation, World Bank-backed governance work), and is the most profitable part per rupee of revenue — with the whole advisory segment at 28% of revenue, Rs 330 Cr FY26 revenue, Rs 120 Cr pre-tax profit, and a Rs 698 Cr order book giving two-to-three years of visibility.
- **Advisory — Investment Banking** — Advises companies on raising money, having completed 15 IPOs so far with 37 live mandates and a tentative pipeline of about Rs 9,700 Cr; it is reported inside the advisory segment and is fee-driven and deal-dependent.
- **Choice Business Associate (CBA) network and technology platforms** — Not a revenue segment itself but the group's delivery engine — 67,000-plus associates plus in-house apps (FinX, Money, Connect), 200-plus branches and 49 project offices — described as what lets every other vertical reach and cross-sell to small-town customers.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
