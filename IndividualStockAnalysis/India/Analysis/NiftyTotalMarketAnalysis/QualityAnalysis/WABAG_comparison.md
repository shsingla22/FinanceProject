### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 2 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 3. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 3 strengthened, 2 weakened, 5 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → STRONG FIT. Why now: Every call in the window frames the EPC plant sale as the front end and long-term O&M as the annuity behind it — O&M share of revenue moved past 20% (Q1) and 18% for 9M, with O&M at 36-38% of the Rs.163bn order book, plus a repeat 5-year O&M renewal in Bahrain; management calls this stable, predictable cash flow, though no attach/renewal rates or deferred-revenue funding are quantified. (The long view had said: The EPC plant sale is repeatedly followed by long-dated O&M annuities (30%+ of backlog in FY23 rising to 36% of a Rs.163bn book by Feb 2026, 18% of 9M FY26 revenue, contracts of 10-20 years), which management calls cash-accretive and margin-stabilising; but there is no evidence of advance billing funding the business — working capital is a positive 101 days, not negative.)
- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → STRONG FIT. Why now: Heritage is central and un-replicable — founded in Germany in 1924, 100-year centenary, 1,500+ projects in 25+ countries, top-three global private operator — and it converts into repeat business from marquee clients (repeat Miahona STP, repeat Reliance orders, four decades in Saudi), which is how B2B brand shows up. (The long view had said: A genuine 100-year heritage (founded 1924, centenary celebrated in Vienna in the Aug 2024 call) plus marquee references and a stated ambition to be the most trusted name in water; but this is B2B reputation used for prequalification — no A&P spend, distributor leverage or premium pricing is ever evidenced.)
- **Innovation Dominance**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Named new capability launches each quarter with revenue attached — ultra-pure water/ZLD for RenewSys solar cells, biogas upgradation for CBG, robotic sewage cleaning in Ghaziabad, energy-efficient desalination — but no R&D spend is disclosed or discussed, and gross margins are EPC-level, so the high-margin-funds-R&D loop is not evidenced. (The long view had said: Consistent technology positioning across the years — own R&D centres in Europe and India, 125+ patents, and new verticals named and then converted (ultra-pure water for semiconductors/PV cells, CBG with Peak Ventures in FY25, the RenewSys UPW/ZLD order executing by FY26) — but R&D spend is never quantified and gross margins are EPC-level, not innovation-funded.)

**That weakened:**

- **Corporate Culture**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Delivery matches guidance across the window — 12 consecutive net-cash-positive quarters, Rs.1,000 crore cash, debt reduction, 18% revenue growth 'fully in line with our medium-term guidance', and the labour-code hit disclosed rather than buried — but the 5-year record shows unusual churn at the top (two chairmen, an MD and a CFO exited FY22-FY25), so long-tenured stability holds only for Rajiv Mittal. (The long view had said: The FY26 investor meet explicitly scores management against targets given three years earlier (15-20% growth, 20% ROCE, 13-15% EBITDA) and shows delivery; the balance sheet stayed conservative through the cycle — net cash for 12 consecutive quarters, Rs.1,000+ cr cash, AA-/Stable — with a board where all committees are chaired by independent directors, though there has been churn in the chairman/MD seats over FY22-FY25.)
- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Leadership claims and a rising international mix (over 40% of revenue in Q1 to 50% for FY26 YTD, ~50% of the order book) plus a 'Glocal' local-depth strategy point to gains won with margin intact and net cash, but no explicit market-share percentages or comparisons against named competitors are given in any of the four calls. (The long view had said: Order backlog compounds from Rs.10,000 cr (Aug 2022) to Rs.11,000 cr (Aug 2024) to Rs.17,200 cr (FY26) with ~20% revenue growth, and management claims clear leadership positions — top three globally in desalination and private operations, largest in India — won through international expansion (Senegal, Saudi, Bangladesh, Zambia); crucially the share is not bought badly, as they refuse state/municipal-funded work without payment security.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
