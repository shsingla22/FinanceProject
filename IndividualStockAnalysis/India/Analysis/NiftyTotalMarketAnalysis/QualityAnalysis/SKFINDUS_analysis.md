# SKF India (Indust.) (SKFINDUS) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

SKF India (Industrial) Limited makes and sells bearings and related industrial products — tapered roller bearings, cylindrical roller bearings, deep groove ball bearings, plus seals and lubrication products, and some higher-end items like hybrid (ceramic) bearings that it still imports from group factories. It sells partly direct to large manufacturers (OEMs) in wind, railways, metals/steel, general machinery, heavy industry, defence, tractors/agriculture and off-highway equipment, and partly through a network of distributors that serves replacement/aftermarket demand; it also exports, mainly to Europe and Southeast Asia. Money comes from manufacturing and selling these components, with distribution/aftermarket sales carrying higher margins than OEM sales; management said Q4 FY26 sales were about INR 9.5 billion, up 9.8% quarter-on-quarter, with pre-tax margin of 11.5% excluding one-off demerger costs. It operates out of India with plants in Pune and Ahmedabad (a new Pune plant is being built, part of INR 800 crore-plus capex to FY30), was recently demerged from SKF India Limited (which kept the automotive business), and still does some contract manufacturing for that sister company.

**The parts of the business:**

- **OEM business** — Direct sales of bearings to large equipment makers — wind and defence, railways, metals, general machinery and heavy industry — which management said was about 51% of FY26 sales (railway ~11%, general machinery ~7%, wind and defence ~7%, heavy ~7%, metals ~6%) and was the main growth driver in Q4, though at lower margins than distribution.
- **Distribution business** — Sales through distributors, largely serving aftermarket/replacement demand, at around 35% of FY26 sales; it dipped to 32% of the quarter's mix because the company held back shipments to distributors with overdue payments, but management expects it to come back and it carries the better margins.
- **Exports** — Roughly 6% of FY26 sales, going mainly to Europe and some Southeast Asian countries, with potential also flagged for Middle East and Africa on tapered roller bearings.
- **Sales to SKF India Limited** — About 4% of FY26 sales — manufacturing the industrial company does for its sister automotive company, SKF India Limited, which grew in Q4 on the back of that company's automotive business.
- **ACES strategy** — Management's plan through 2028 built on four pillars — accelerate localization (make more in India and buy more components locally), commercial excellence (key account management for OEMs, sales coverage for aftermarket), disciplined execution, and working as one SKF across internal departments — aimed at getting margins back to about 13% near term and around 15% by FY30.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
