### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 2 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 0. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 0 strengthened, 3 weakened, 6 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That weakened:**

- **Corporate Culture**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Disclosure is candid and prompt across all four calls — the West Bengal BESS cancellation and the missed order target were volunteered in the opening remarks, and the water-division certification problem was flagged repeatedly rather than buried; the balance sheet is conservative (D/E 0.32x, net debt INR 163 crore) and operating cash flow was fixed from ~nil in FY25 to INR 430 crore. Against that, the FY26 revenue guidance of INR 6,500 crore and 25% growth given in Q1 and reaffirmed in Q2 landed at INR 6,107 crore and 16%. (The long view had said: Founder-promoter Sajja Kishore Babu has led throughout with family succession (Rohit Sajja) and promoter equity infusion to support lender confidence; the balance sheet stayed conservative through the build-out (D/E 0.21x FY24, 0.32x FY26; net debt INR163cr on INR6,107cr revenue), and bad news is disclosed rather than buried — missed order targets quantified, non-moving FGD orders removed from backlog, and the BESS cancellation named as the reason for the FY26 shortfall.)
- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Evidence of expansion — FY26 revenue INR 6,107 crore (+16%), executable order book up 10.5% to INR 15,899 crore, entry into monorail O&M, BOP EPC and storage — but no explicit market-share figures or comparison versus named competitors, and FY26 order intake reached only 72% of the INR 10,000 crore target. (The long view had said: Persistent expansion is documented — O&M installed base from ~67,000MW (2022) to ~75,000MW (Feb 2026), headcount 32,000 to 48,000, revenue INR3,618cr FY23 to INR6,107cr FY26 — and they claim O&M leadership, but no explicit share percentages versus named competitors, and order intake missed the INR10,000cr target twice (INR8,759cr FY24; INR7,210cr FY26).)
- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: the calls are silent and no computable check supports the pattern. (The long view had said: There is durable B2B reputational equity — 25+ years, contracts awarded without performance guarantees, credit rating upgraded A to A+, and a 'long association' with BHEL that repeatedly converts to orders — but no consumer brand, A&P spend, or pricing premium from the name.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (NO FIT); Toll Roads (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT); Innovation Dominance (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 0 eased, 1 worsened, 3 unchanged; 4 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Revenue is anchored to customers' capacity-expansion capex — thermal BOP/EPC packages, new coal plants and private capex from Adani/JSW — which management itself frames as a bullish investment cycle rather than steady operating demand; quarterly margins already swing (13.95% in Q1 on an exceptional Riverbed Mineral contribution, 12.08% in Q3, 11.17% in Q4) and civil revenue fell 22% year-on-year in Q2 on rains and certification. The recurring O&M and long-dated MDO books bound the risk but do not remove the capex linkage. (The long view had said: Revenue is almost entirely tied to customers' capex programmes — thermal plant erection, FGD EPC, BOP EPC, mine development, water and railway projects — so the order book swings with India's power capex cycle; the calls track demand as megawatts ordered by Adani/NTPC/BHEL rather than as recurring operating spend, and the FY26 order intake missed the INR 10,000 cr target at INR 7,210 cr. The thermal capex upswing described from FY24-FY26 is itself an expansionary phase whose duration is unknowable.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Good Enough Goods (ELEVATED); Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH); New Entrants (NO SIGNAL); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 10 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
