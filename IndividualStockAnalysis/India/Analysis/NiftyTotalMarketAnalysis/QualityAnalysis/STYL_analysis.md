# Seshaasai Technologi (STYL) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Seshaasai Technologies (STYL) is a Mumbai-headquartered Indian company, founded over three decades ago as a secure printing and fulfilment business, that now describes itself as a technology-led solutions provider to India's banking and financial services (BFSI) ecosystem, plus government, enterprise, retail, logistics and telecom customers. It makes and delivers the physical and digital plumbing behind everyday finance and commerce — payment cards and cheque books, printed customer communication like insurance kits and statements, citizen ID cards, and RFID tags, SIM/eSIM cards and tracking devices — manufactured in its own certified secure facilities (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Navi Mumbai, Kundli, with Nagpur and Bengaluru expansions under construction) and dispatched through its own fulfilment and logistics network. It earns money largely by the piece — cards issued, documents printed and mailed, tags supplied — under long-running programs and multi-year bank tenders, supported by its own software platforms (RUBIC, eTaTrak, IOMS, izeIoT) that add a recurring, technology-linked layer; management says roughly 97–98% of revenue is recurring in nature and over 95% comes from existing clients. Operations are essentially India-centric with early international traction in Europe and Africa; FY26 revenue was INR 1,441 crore with a 27.4% EBITDA margin, and Q1 FY27 revenue was INR 377 crore, up 21.1% year-on-year.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Payment Solutions** — Manufactures, personalises and fulfils payment cards (including metal, biometric, eco-friendly and wearable variants), cheque books and merchant QR kits for almost every major private, public sector, small finance, cooperative and payments bank plus fintechs — the largest vertical at about 50% of FY26 revenue (42% in Q1 FY27), where management claims a top-two position in Indian card manufacturing and where FY26 volumes dipped on industry-wide factors like lower Jan-Dhan account openings and softer card renewals.
- **Communication and Fulfilment Solutions** — Designs, prints and dispatches customer communication such as insurance policy kits, portfolio statements, regulatory notices and mailers, plus identity work like citizen and tax ID cards and branch print-management supply for BFSI, government and enterprise clients — about 39–40% of revenue and the year's growth engine, up 29% in FY26 and 13% year-on-year in Q1 FY27, run on its RUBIC, eTaTrak and IOMS platforms.
- **IoT Solutions** — Makes RFID inlays, tags and reading hardware (tunnels, gates, kiosks, point-of-sale units) with in-house antenna design and chip bonding at Bengaluru, plus newer SIM and eSIM manufacturing and personalisation, sold into retail, logistics, renewable energy, pharma, manufacturing and telecom — the smallest but fastest-growing vertical, at about 11% of FY26 revenue (up 45%) and 18% in Q1 FY27 (up 145%).
- **Proprietary technology platforms (RUBIC, eTaTrak, IOMS, izeIoT)** — Software that runs card personalisation, order management, dispatch tracking across courier partners, and IoT device management for clients — not reported as a separate revenue line, but management presents it as what separates the company from pure manufacturers and as a source of steady recurring revenue and customer stickiness.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
