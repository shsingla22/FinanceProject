### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 0 worsened, 5 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Unknown on the long view, Unknown on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: Earnings swing with the rate and credit cycle rather than a commodity cycle: NIM fell 2.55%→2.41% in Q2 FY26 on RBI rate cuts and full-year FY26 NIM dropped 30bps, and FY26 operating profit grew only 4% while net profit grew 14% on falling credit costs (0.76%→0.48%) — a benign-cycle tailwind that reverses. Exposure is bounded by the granular, operations-linked loan mix. (The long view had said: Banking earnings here swing with the credit and rate cycle: GNPA fell from 9.98% (Mar'22) to 1.81% (Jun'26) with credit cost dropping 0.76%→0.15%, while NIM went 2.55%→3.07%→2.52% and treasury swung from Rs.295 crore MTM losses in FY23 to 'record breaking' revaluation gains in Q1FY27; management repeatedly frames outcomes in terms of crude at $102, rate cycles and GDP downgrades, and corporate slippages clustered in road/manufacturing capex assets.)
- **Good Enough Goods**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: Loans and deposits are undifferentiated products where the customer chooses on rate: global NIM of ~2.5% is thin, and management's stated strategy in every call is to chase 'low-cost deposits' and 'high-yielding advances' — i.e. it is a price-taker on both sides, with no brand premium to defend. Evidence is inferential from the margin profile rather than an explicit competitive discussion. (The long view had said: Credit is an undifferentiated product and the bank has no pricing power: it concedes home-loan rates 'to keep pace with the market competition' without passing through EBLR hikes, and describes peers undercutting to 7.60-7.80% on AAA corporate advances where it cannot compete, while paying up to 7.50% for bulk deposits. Borrowers show no brand attachment, and the CD ratio at 82-83% versus an ideal 78-79% shows it stretching to hold share.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Fashion Risk (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Technological Innovation (WATCH); New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
