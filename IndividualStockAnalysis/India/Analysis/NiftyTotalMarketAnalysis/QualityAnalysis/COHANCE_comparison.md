### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 4 eased, 2 worsened, 1 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Strained on the long view, Strained on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: FY26 revenue fell ~13% almost entirely on customer inventory destocking, delayed reloads and biotech funding winter rather than a commodity price cycle — an inventory/order-phasing cycle that swung reported CDMO revenue by 27% year-on-year in Q3 while underlying demand was stable. Demand is operations-linked (commercial supply into customers' marketed drugs), not tied to customer capex programmes. (The long view had said: Across the 21 calls the revenue line swings violently on factors outside the company's control: COVID one-off molecules (~INR120cr) that vanished in FY23, Ag-chem destocking and 'commodity pricing' in FY24, then pharma customer destocking of two large commercial molecules worth ~INR260cr plus biotech funding-cycle slowdowns in FY26, taking FY26 revenue down ~13% and adjusted EBITDA margin from ~41% (FY24) to 21%. Demand is operations-linked (commercial API/intermediate supply, >70% of Pharma CDMO revenue is commercial), not customer capex, but customer inventory and launch-sequencing cycles have repeatedly whipsawed both sales and margins.)
- **Technological Innovation**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: The business is exposed to modality shifts — value is migrating toward ADCs, oligonucleotides and payload-linker chemistry, forcing continuous capability investment (conjugation suite, OEB6 block, cGMP oligo building-block facility), and in specialty chemicals the OLED line is explicitly in a product-cycle transition. The exposure is bounded because the company is on the adopting side and its innovation is incremental rather than platform-replacing. (The long view had said: The business is chemistry-manufacturing rather than a proprietary format, and management is investing on the right side of the modality shift (ADC payload-linkers, oligonucleotides, exatecan-linked payloads, semiconductor-linked chemistries) — this is the small-scale, value-adding innovation cadence that is a mitigant. But the installed small-molecule/API base does face obsolescence at the product level (a large commercial molecule at patent expiry, OLED 'going through a product cycle transition'), and the platform pivot itself signals that legacy small-molecule capacity is not where growth sits.)
- **New Entrants**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: The specialty chemicals segment is being reset by structurally cheaper Chinese generic supply, and management flags it twice as a cause of the FY26 decline; in CDMO the pressure is the reverse — Indian peers collectively benefit from innovators de-risking away from China. (The long view had said: Structurally cheaper Chinese competition is explicitly named as depressing the agrochemical business, and the API segment has absorbed price resets from generic competition. Offsetting this, the same geopolitical shift (China Plus One / Biosecure) is bringing Western CDMOs and innovators to Cohance as a substitute source, so the entrant dynamic cuts both ways.)
- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Cheaper substitutes are visibly attacking the commoditising end of the portfolio — mature API molecules saw pricing pressure and agrochemicals faced Chinese generic competition — though the CDMO core sells complexity (high-potent, OEB6, payload-linker, complex oligos) that management says is hard to replicate, supported by a 70.8% gross margin. (The long view had said: The generic API and formulation half of the business is genuinely exposed to cheaper substitutes — management conceded a 4-5% price correction across products in FY24 and 'select mature molecules experienced pricing pressure' in FY26 — and here the customer, not the end patient, controls the choice. The ADC/oligonucleotide and complex-chemistry platforms are the niche, scale-protected part that a good-enough challenger cannot easily attack, and gross margin held at 70.8% in FY26.)

**That worsened:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Results are repeatedly driven by a handful of named relationships and molecules — destocking in 'two large commercial molecules' plus one large molecule nearing patent expiry drove the entire FY26 decline, and in API Plus the top 15 products are around half of revenues. Management was questioned on and had to defend single-relationship dependency during the MD's exit. (The long view had said: Repeatedly, a handful of relationships and molecules decide the year: two large commercial products drove ~INR260cr of the FY26 shortfall, three API products 'accounted for most of the impact', top 15 products are ~half of API revenue, and the company is 'the exclusive payload intermediate supplier to a major innovator'. The incoming Group CEO explicitly named the problem in May 2026, and management has historically stated its customer strategy is to farm a small set of large innovators rather than broaden.)
- **Shifting Consumer Preferences**: NO SIGNAL → LOW. Why now: The B2B demand base is not taste-driven, and the one structural shift in customer preference — innovators wanting supply chains outside China and integrated ADC/oligo partners — runs in the company's favour, with management describing this as the source of RFQ growth. (The long view had said: The calls contain no discussion of end-consumer attitude or channel shifts; Cohance is a B2B contract manufacturer whose demand base is innovator pipelines, not consumer taste.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Fashion Risk (QUANT FLAG).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (WATCH).*

**Financial resilience:** Strained on the long view (1 of the 3 stress checks is flagged — one warning light, not yet a pattern.) vs Strained on the one-year view (1 of the 3 stress checks is flagged — one warning light, not yet a pattern.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
