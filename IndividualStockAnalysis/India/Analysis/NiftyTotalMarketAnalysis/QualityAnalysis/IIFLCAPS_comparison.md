### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 1 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 1. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 1 weakened, 9 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: There is a genuine recurring/annuity layer — cross-sell (FPD) assets grew from ~Rs.44,000cr (Nov 2025) to ~Rs.52,000cr (May 2026) with a stated 60/40 ARR:TBR mix in mutual funds, PMS and AIF, and management explicitly manages that mix. But the front-end 'product sale' advantage is absent (broking revenue is regulated and falling), there is no attach/renewal-rate or deferred-revenue-as-funding discussion, and working capital is negative in the wrong direction — finance cost rose 17% to Rs.210cr on MTF funding needs. (The long view had said: Management explicitly pivots from transactional to annuity income — distribution AUM grew from ~Rs.31,300cr (FY25) to ~Rs.52,000cr (FY26) at a blended 75-90bps trail yield, and by Jul 2026 they distinguish ARR from non-ARR income. But there is no front-end product advantage, no attach/renewal rates, no deferred-revenue-as-funding discussion, and the recurring stream is bought with RM hiring and 75-90% sub-broker payouts rather than installed-base density.)

**That weakened:**

- **Brand Strength**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: IIFL is an established capital-markets name and admin spend is rising on branding and marketing, but the brand is not shown converting into premium pricing or share gain; tellingly, management frames the incoming Fairfax stake — not its own heritage — as the source of credibility. (The long view had said: Management repeatedly asserts brand equity as one of its 'pillars' for the wealth transformation, and expects Fairfax's 51% stake to 'enhance our credibility and brand' — but there is no premium pricing, no heritage narrative, no sustained A&P disclosure beyond generic 'branding and marketing' cost lines, and brand is invoked as an input to a pivot rather than evidenced by results.)

**That eased:**

Why now: The wealth business — the segment management is investing behind as retail broking declines — is described as facing intensifying competition, and the firm is losing RMs from its existing PCG channel while adding only 2-3 wealth RMs a quarter; the business remains loss-making with break-even pushed to next year. Cash market share slipped from 2.9% to ~2.6% and F&O share sits below 1%.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Technological Innovation (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 6 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
