### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 3 eased, 1 worsened, 2 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **New Entrants**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Parallel licensing is opening distribution areas to structurally new competitors — analysts questioned the Nagpur parallel-licence opportunity and reported loss-making first four years — and management noted peers are bidding 'aggressively' on projects, which pressures tender-based returns. (The long view had said: Renewable and C&I bidding is crowded enough that management concedes peers are 'going aggressively' on leverage and that it must walk away on threshold IRR, and it describes itself as 'already aggressive... in all the bids' for C&I consumers — the classic sign of returns being competed down by better-capitalised entrants rather than an incumbent setting price.)
- **Technological Innovation**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: No defensive discussion of disruption in any of the four calls; instead the company is adding the newer technologies itself — 112MW/450MWh battery in the FDRE award, 3GW pumped storage, and India's largest green hydrogen-natural gas blending project in its CGD business — a steady small-scale innovation cadence rather than an installed base being obsoleted. (The long view had said: The 2.7 GW gas fleet — a third of installed capacity — already runs at only 30-35% PLF and is being displaced economically by cheaper renewables plus storage, and the company is having to re-platform toward FDRE/battery hybrids (350 MW solar + 150 MW wind + 450 MWh battery), 3 GW pumped storage and green-hydrogen blending. This is transition rather than a Nokia-style reset, and the incumbent is funding the new platform itself.)
- **Shifting Consumer Preferences**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: The shift toward green/renewable energy is being met head-on rather than eroding the franchise: renewables reached ~2GW installed with a 4GW pipeline and INR6,500 crore of FY26 renewable capex out of roughly INR9,350 crore total, plus rooftop solar rollouts in Ahmedabad and Surat; underlying electricity demand in the distribution business grew 4-5%. (The long view had said: Customers are migrating behind the meter: Q3 FY26 saw Y-o-Y unit declines in Ahmedabad and Surat with the analyst explicitly asking whether rooftop self-generation was the cause, and the company itself books rooftop-solar installation incentives, confirming the channel shift is real and underway in its own licence areas.)

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: Quarterly results swing violently on merchant power and LNG spreads: Q1 FY26 PBT fell 25% on a INR333 crore drop in merchant/LNG gains with thermal PLF collapsing from 60% to 39%, then Q2 FY26 PBT rose 42% on a INR304 crore merchant swing the other way; management itself frames the business as navigating 'cyclical variations', and gas price/monsoon demand drive the gas fleet's economics. (The long view had said: The thermal/merchant segment is openly commodity-cycle driven — LNG at $12-17 suppressed merchant sales in Q4 FY25, a favourable window swung thermal contribution up INR304 crores in Q2 FY26, and by May 2026 management said $16-20 gas makes merchant sales 'very difficult'; management itself says no medium/long-term gas PPA is possible because 'you cannot have control on the gas prices.' Capex is also expanding hard into the upcycle (renewable capex INR6,500 crores in FY26, FY27 'much, much higher'). Bounded because ~ the majority of earnings sit in regulated distribution.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Good Enough Goods (QUANT FLAG); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
