### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 2 worsened, 2 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Revenue is throughput/operations-linked (a flow business — cargo moved, not customer capex), which showed up as FY26 revenue +17% and EBIT +27% even with containers down 4%; but the calls are dominated by exogenous swings — six-plus quarters of container decline on US tariffs, Red Sea/Suez routing pulling tonnage off GPPL services, Middle East disruption forcing management to withdraw FY27 guidance, and dry bulk swinging on fertiliser tender timing. Management is also investing counter-cyclically ($90m liquid jetty) without waiting for concession clarity. (The long view had said: Volumes and margins swing hard on exogenous trade and geopolitical cycles — revenue -10% in Q2FY25 on the Red Sea crisis, six straight quarters of container decline on US tariffs, then +32% revenue in Q2FY26 on a fertiliser-tender surge, and management refused full-year FY27 guidance owing to the Middle East; but the underlying demand is throughput from customers' operations, not their capex.)

**That worsened:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Container performance is visibly a function of one relationship — Maersk (also the promoter): management attributes the Q3 green shoots to 'structural initiatives we have done with Maersk', quantifies Maersk's own 15% volume growth, pins recovery hopes on Maersk's MECL and the new FI2 service, and the Q4 shortfall is one disrupted Maersk Middle East feeder; the MOU signing was staged with Robert Maersk Uggla. Liquid growth likewise rides on a single terminal operator's (AVTL) LPG/ammonia expansion, and ONGC is a named single contract. (The long view had said: Container performance is repeatedly explained by the actions of a single anchor line — Maersk service withdrawals, the disrupted Middle East feeder, the new FI2 service — with an analyst noting Maersk-related RPT revenue has gone nowhere for five to six years; RoRo and the new offshore-supply stream likewise hinge on named counterparties (NYK, a five-year ONGC contract).)
- **New Entrants**: NO SIGNAL → WATCH. Why now: Analysts pressed on the Dedicated Freight Corridor reaching JNPT by Dec-26/Jun-26 resetting the cost of serving the northern hinterland; management's rebuttal was a rail-tariff/distance argument rather than evidence, and it did not have the share-of-services-also-calling-JNPT number to hand. This is a structural change in a competitor's economics rather than a discounter entering, so exposure is real but bounded. (The long view had said: the judge read the calls and found nothing, and all 2 computed fingerprints are clean.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Technological Innovation (LOW); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Good Enough Goods (WATCH).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 10 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
