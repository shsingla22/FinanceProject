### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 0 eased, 1 worsened, 6 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: FY26 was explicitly described as 'a year of 2 distinct halves' — industry -0.4% in H1 and +16.7% in H2, with Maruti's own domestic volumes swinging from -5.6% to +12.3% — and margins moved with commodity, energy, FX and discount swings (EBIT 8.1% to 8.8% sequentially, 80bps adverse commodity). (The long view had said: FY26 was explicitly 'a year of 2 distinct halves' — industry PV down 0.4% in H1 on affordability pressure then +16.7% in H2, with Maruti swinging 17.9% between halves; margins move with commodity/energy/freight prices (80bps adverse in Q4 alone) and management is adding ~0.5m units of capacity plus INR14,000 crore capex into a policy-boosted upcycle, the classic over-investment setup.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
