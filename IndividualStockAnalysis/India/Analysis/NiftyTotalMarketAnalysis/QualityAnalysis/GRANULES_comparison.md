### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 4 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 4. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 3 weakened, 4 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Pricing Power**: NO FIT → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Gross margin is stable-high and rising (65.6%, +74bps year-on-year in Q1 FY27) but the calls attribute this to mix — complex generics rising from ~39% to 50% of finished dosages — with no instance of raising prices without volume loss; in a generic business this reads as premiumisation-by-portfolio rather than demonstrated pricing power. (The long view had said: The record is the opposite of pricing power: US price erosion is discussed in almost every year, European paracetamol collapsed on oversupply (Aug 2024), and as late as Apr 2026 management confirmed no price increases despite rising raw material costs — margin gains came from mix shift to complex generics, not price.)
- **Brand Strength**: NO FIT → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: Granules sells generics and CDMO services B2B; the last-12-month calls contain no heritage story, A&P spend, distributor leverage or brand-led adjacency launches. (The long view had said: Granules sells APIs/PFIs into other companies' brands and OTC private label through Granules Consumer Health for chains like Walmart and Target; there is no heritage story, A&P spend or consumer-brand premium anywhere in the calls — its customers own the brands.)

**That weakened:**

- **Forward Integrators**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Granules has moved from API supplier toward owning the customer-facing end in the US — its own manufacturing (GPI Chantilly), packaging (GCH) and a new distribution centre — plus full molecule-to-tablet integration; but there is no franchising, own-store or D2C economics disclosed, and no revenue share attributed to the forward-integrated channel. (The long view had said: A sustained, delivered move down the chain toward the customer: finished dosages went from 50% of sales (FY23) to 76% (Q1FY25) with own US manufacturing (GPI), packaging (GPAK), distribution centre and the Granules Consumer Health commercial arm selling private label direct to US chains — control that lifted GPI from 74th to 27th among US generic companies.)
- **Low Price Plus**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Cost discipline is claimed as a core strength alongside quality and scale — 'Swiss innovation Indian scale' and 'Indian efficiency' pitched to CDMO customers — but management is explicitly steering away from this model rather than doubling down, and no itemised cost savings or cost-culture specifics are given in the last four calls. (The long view had said: Cost leadership is the stated strategic anchor from the first call ('global cost leadership position') through FY26, delivered via backward-integrated chemistry, biocatalysis/enzyme platforms and asset turns of ~2x; low unit economics are offset by scale and rising ROCE (9.4% Q1FY24 → 19.6% Q1FY25 → 18% Q1FY27).)
- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The calls describe product sales (finished dosages, APIs) and project-based CDMO work; there is no discussion of attach rates, AMC/service revenue, renewals, installed base or customer advances funding the business. (The long view had said: Across the 17 calls there is no discussion of licence fees, AMC/service contracts, renewal or attach rates, or deferred revenue/customer advances as a funding source; the only repeat-revenue-like vertical (peptide CDMO) is explicitly described as lumpy and project-driven.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Market Share Gainers (STRONG FIT); Innovation Dominance (LIKELY FIT); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 3 worsened, 2 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Demand across the last four calls is operations/consumption-linked prescription volume (finished dosages 74% of revenue, ADHD/controlled substances, Europe +81%), not customer capex; revenue rose sequentially every quarter of FY26 into a record Q1 FY27 with gross margin steady at 65.6%. The only cyclical element flagged is the small project-driven peptide CDMO, which management itself calls lumpy quarter to quarter. (The long view had said: The legacy API/PFI franchise (paracetamol, metformin) behaves like a commodity cycle — input PAP shortages then price falls, then a customer inventory glut plus industry capacity built for demand that never came, killing European sales for several quarters; but the underlying demand is flow-type (medicines consumed in customer operations, not customer capex), and by FY26–FY27 the mix shift to complex generics delivered six straight quarters of sequential growth and steadier 22–23% EBITDA margins.)

**That worsened:**

- **Government Dependency**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: A single regulator determines the earnings path: the US FDA warning letter caused a voluntary production halt at Gagillapur, kept the India formulation business 'delivery constrained' through FY26, and as of Jul 2026 nine ready applications are still waiting behind FDA clearance the company admits it cannot schedule. Every one of the four calls opens with regulatory status rather than commercial performance. (The long view had said: Fortunes are visibly gated by state actors: a PLI sanction underwrote the DCDA project, the largest growth engine is DEA-quota-governed controlled substances, and an FDA warning letter at Gagillapur held back nine ready-to-launch applications and forced multi-year remediation spend — though pricing and revenue are set by markets, not a regulator or tariff.)
- **Technological Innovation**: LOW → WATCH. Why now: The legacy franchise is high-volume oral solid dosage chemistry, and the industry's growth is migrating to peptides/injectables — a modality shift management is responding to by buying Senn and building Ascelis rather than one it is being disrupted by. Innovation described is incremental and value-adding (TFA-free chemistry, LPPS hybrid, TAG-assisted synthesis, 505(b)(2), digitalised quality systems); no analyst on these four calls raised a disruption threat to the existing base. (The long view had said: Across the whole period innovation is incremental and self-directed — biocatalysis/enzyme platforms, continuous manufacturing, TFA-free and TAG-assisted peptide synthesis, 505(b)(2) and first-to-file filings — rather than a competitor platform threatening an installed base; no call shows analysts or management defending a legacy format against disruption.)
- **Fashion Risk**: NO SIGNAL → LOW. Why now: Demand is functional and repeat-purchase — chronic ADHD and controlled-substance therapy, oncology, and API supply into regulated markets — with no growth attributed to a trending product, brand moment or taste-driven category across any of the four calls. (The long view had said: the judge read the calls and found nothing, and all 2 computed fingerprints are clean.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
