**That eased:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: No single customer, licensor or supplier is named as driving results in any call; management consistently describes a broad base across retail, institutional, export and Tier 2/3 distribution channels, and no analyst raised a renewal or relationship risk. **That worsened:**

Why now: Across all four FY26 calls demand is framed in terms of infrastructure expansion, housing/construction activity and customer capex cycles, and realization is explicitly tied to copper/aluminium/PVC price movements — Q3 FY26's 42.3% revenue growth was attributed substantially to higher commodity prices, and the company is adding INR1,200 crores of capacity into the upcycle; margins swung from 4.7% (Q2 FY25) to 8.1% (Q2 FY26), showing how much operating leverage rides on the cycle. (The long view had said: Across the 11 calls, revenue and margins swing with copper/aluminium: FY25 saw wire volumes fall (domestic wire flat to -9%, 9M volume growth only 6%) and W&C segment margin drop to ~6.4% on 'volatile metal prices', while FY26 Q3 revenue jumped 42.3% and EBITDA 86% 'mainly due to higher commodity prices' after a ~20-25% copper move in one quarter. Demand is also tied to construction/infrastructure and to channel stocking-destocking, and the company is committing INR1,200cr of capex into a period every player is expanding capacity.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
