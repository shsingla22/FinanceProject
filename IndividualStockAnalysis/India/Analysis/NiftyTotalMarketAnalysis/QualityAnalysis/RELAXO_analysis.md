# Relaxo Footwears (RELAXO) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Relaxo Footwears is an Indian footwear maker that designs, manufactures and sells low-priced everyday shoes, slippers and sandals under its own brands, mainly to India's mass-market and value-conscious consumers. It sells almost entirely through a wholesale distribution network — distributors (about 630 as of Q4 FY26) who supply roughly 70,000 retail shops — plus its own retail stores, e-commerce platforms like Amazon and Flipkart, large-format retail, and some exports. Money is made on volume: it sold pairs at an average realisation of roughly INR 156 a pair in H1 FY25, so profit depends heavily on the cost of imported plastic raw materials (EVA, PVC, polyethylene) and factory labour, which management repeatedly flags as the swing factor on margins. Full-year FY26 revenue was INR 2,702 crores with EBITDA of INR 374 crores and profit after tax of INR 179 crores; the company is net debt free, spends about 4% of revenue on advertising, and manufactures in its own plants across states including Haryana.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Open footwear (Hawaii / EVA slippers and sandals)** — Cheap slippers and sandals — the mass-market core of the business at roughly 80% of volume — and the part hit hardest when raw-material prices swing or poor consumers down-trade to cheap unorganized-sector products.
- **Closed footwear (shoes, mainly Sparx)** — Sports shoes and other closed footwear, roughly 20% of the business; management calls it the more resilient, premium-leaning part they want to grow (targeting 8-10% growth in Sparx) and where capacity has already been added, so no further expansion is needed.
- **Bahamas brand** — One of the three main consumer brands, an EVA-based open-footwear line at the value end, which suffered along with the rest of the EVA category during the 2022 raw-material volatility.
- **Flite brand** — Another EVA/open-footwear brand in the value segment; management grouped it with Bahamas as the weaker performers versus Sparx in FY25.
- **Relaxo brand** — The namesake brand, also in the EVA/Hawaii mass segment, named alongside Bahamas and Flite as affected by cheap local competition in FY23.
- **Sales channels — general trade, retail, e-commerce and large-format retail** — General trade (distributors to small shops) is the biggest contributor to sales and drove the Q4 FY26 recovery, while company retail stores, e-commerce (Amazon, Flipkart) and large-format retail are smaller but growing channels management is pushing to expand.
- **Exports** — An international sales stream mentioned only in passing as an area management wants to grow steadily alongside the domestic business; no size given in these calls.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
