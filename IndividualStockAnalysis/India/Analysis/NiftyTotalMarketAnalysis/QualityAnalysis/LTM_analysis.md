# LTM (LTM) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

LTM (formerly LTIMindtree, and before that Larsen & Toubro Infotech) is an India-headquartered IT services company that builds, modernizes and then runs the technology used by large corporations — application development, cloud migration, data platforms, infrastructure and security operations, ERP and, increasingly, AI systems. Its customers are big global enterprises, including 77 Global Fortune 500 companies as of 2022, in banking and financial services, insurance, technology/media/telecom, manufacturing and energy, consumer/retail and utilities. It earns money by charging for the people, projects and managed-service contracts it delivers, winning multi-year 'total contract value' deals (order inflow of about US$1.3 billion in one quarter of FY25, US$1.7 billion in Q1 FY2027) and increasingly through outcome-based pricing on its AI products; quarterly revenue was about US$1.13 billion in Q2 FY25 rising to US$1.22 billion in Q1 FY2027, at roughly 15.5% operating (EBIT) margin. Work is delivered from India and other offshore/near-shore centres (Poland, and Romania and Portugal being added), with North America its anchor market at about 73% of revenue, Europe about 15% and emerging markets about 12%.

**The parts of the business:**

- **iRun** — Runs and secures clients' existing technology estates — infrastructure operations, application support and cybersecurity — with AI built into the delivery model; it is one of the three lines of business the company reorganized into and produced deal wins this quarter with a US insurer and a global business travel company.
- **iTransform** — Does the large-scale build-and-change work — software engineering, modernization, data platforms, digital/interactive experiences and enterprise (ERP-type) platforms; the calls cite it in the biggest vendor-consolidation deals, such as a US multinational consolidating its whole IT services landscape.
- **Business AI** — Reimagines clients' actual business processes and business models using domain knowledge, data, AI agents and small language models — for example an AI pricing/promotions platform for a European consumer company; together with Creative and Industrial AI it accounts for roughly US$150 million of revenue on a quarterly run-rate basis.
- **BlueVerse™ (AI ecosystem)** — The company's underlying AI platform and product library — prebuilt agents, industry-specific small language models and offerings such as BlueVerse Voicing (17 implementations in 12 months) and BlueVerse Currency, an outcome-based pricing model — described as foundational to all three lines of business rather than a separate business.
- **Financial Services segment** — Banking, financial services and insurance clients; historically the largest slice (BFSI was 47% of the portfolio in 2022 and led growth again in FY25), it grew 3.2% sequentially but declined 2.5% year-over-year in Q1 FY2027.
- **Technology and Services segment** — Technology, media, telecom and services clients; one of the faster-growing segments, up 3.4% sequentially and 10% year-over-year in Q1 FY2027.
- **Production segment** — Manufacturing, energy and utilities-type clients (with ERP and industrial work); it fell 5.7% sequentially in Q1 FY2027 mainly because of seasonal pass-through revenue dropping out, but grew 5.3% year-over-year.
- **Consumer segment** — Consumer packaged goods, retail and similar clients; smallest-sounding but fastest-growing on a yearly basis, up 18.2% year-over-year in Q1 FY2027 though down 0.7% sequentially on delayed ramp-ups in India and the Middle East.
- **Randstad Technology and Consulting Services acquisition (announced May 2026)** — A proposed purchase of Randstad's technology and consulting arm in Europe and Australia — about €469 million (roughly US$500 million) of annual revenue for an enterprise value of €160 million — bringing new geographies, aerospace/defence, automotive and utilities clients, cybersecurity talent and near-shore centres, plus a five-year deal to serve Randstad's own India centre.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
