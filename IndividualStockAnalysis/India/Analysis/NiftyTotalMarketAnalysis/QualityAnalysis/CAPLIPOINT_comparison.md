### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 6 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 6. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 2 weakened, 7 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Caplin Steriles earns an annuity-like profit-share and milestone stream on top of front-end product sales (25% of CSL revenue in Q3 FY26), but the calls describe no installed base, attach or renewal rates, and the model is working-capital heavy — six months of stock held in subsidiary warehouses — rather than customer-funded. (The long view had said: Across all 16 calls the model is product sales plus one-off tenders and partner milestone/profit-share; there is no licence, AMC, subscription or attach-rate language, and working capital is heavily positive rather than customer-funded — receivables 121–136 days and a deliberate six-month inventory held near customers.)
- **Pricing Power**: NO FIT → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: the only computable check points this way but the calls offer no evidence — numbers alone are only ever a screening hint, never a confirmed fit. (The long view had said: No call in the series describes a price increase; management instead talks about defending stable pricing in a deflationary generic market by picking smaller wholesalers, and gross-margin gains (55.3%→59.6%) are attributed to mix (Caplin Steriles, onco) and COGS, not price.)

**That weakened:**

- **Innovation Dominance**: STRONG FIT → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Regular, profitable incremental innovation is visible — first-cycle US approvals in 14-15 months vs a 20-month industry norm, 21-24 own-label launches in year one, BFS/prefilled-syringe/cartridge/GLP-1/oncology/hormone pipelines and a Vizag API backward-integration push — but R&D spend is never disclosed or benchmarked as a share of industry, and generic development is not R&D-led category dominance. (The long view had said: R&D is disclosed and rising to ~5% of turnover and above INR 100 crores for the first time in FY26, with a named cadence of new dosage platforms each year (PFS, cartridges, IV bags, ophthalmics, BFS via Rommelag, lyo, onco, hormones/inhalers) and first-cycle approvals in 14–15 months versus a ~20-month industry norm; the 2022 promise of Phase II/III lines and complex products has been visibly delivered by 2026.)
- **Friendly Middleman**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Latin American sales run through independent pharmacies and the 'last mile' private market where the pharmacist, not the patient, effectively selects the product — Caplin protected these pharmacies through COVID and now holds 41% of their shelf space — but the calls describe no professional training programmes or prescriber lock-in. (The long view had said: LatAm sales run through independent pharmacies and a branded-generic/CNS push in Guatemala, El Salvador, Honduras and Dominican Republic where the pharmacy shelf is the point of choice (41% shelf share), but the calls describe no professional training programmes or prescriber lock-in, and the US business is explicitly distribution-only.)

*Unchanged: Forward Integrators (STRONG FIT); Market Share Gainers (STRONG FIT); Corporate Culture (STRONG FIT); Brand Strength (LIKELY FIT); Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 2 worsened, 4 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Supplier-side dependence is material and rising by design — roughly 50% of products outsourced, about 30% of outsourcing from China, plus Chinese partners supplying the Mexico filing pipeline and the oncology tender products, and CSL now outsourcing production to a third-party South Indian manufacturer. Customer side is affirmatively broad: thousands of independent LatAm pharmacies with 41% Caplin shelf space, and owned/acquired channel partners with distribution buys underway in Chile. (The long view had said: Caplin Steriles' US revenue has run at a persistent 75:25 product/milestone-and-profit-share split, meaning a quarter of that stream depends on a small number of named licensing partners, and single tenders visibly move the books (the El Salvador tender pushing receivables to 136 days in Q4 FY26); the risk is bounded and falling as the base broadens.)

**That worsened:**

- **Technological Innovation**: LOW → WATCH. Why now: The Chairman repeatedly frames biosimilars, peptides, GLP-1s, robotics and AI as forces reshaping generics — visiting China three weeks to study it, noting a peptide's price fell from $500/gram to $100 — and defends the base business as un-disruptable while simultaneously buying into the new platforms (GLP-1 cartridges filed in four LatAm countries, blow-fill-seal, biosimilars via Chinese associates). Exposure is real but management's cadence is incremental capability addition, not a bet-the-company format change. (The long view had said: No call across 2022-2026 shows a competitor platform discussed defensively or the installed base becoming legacy; instead there is a steady cadence of incremental capability additions (new vial/PFS lines, lyophilisation, blow-fill-seal, ophthalmic ointments, digital logbooks, video SOPs) that add value rather than reset industry economics.)
- **Shifting Consumer Preferences**: NO SIGNAL → LOW. Why now: The demand base is non-discretionary essential medicines and hospital injectables, and the only preference trend discussed — the shift toward nutraceuticals/dietary supplements and TCM-style functional foods — is being approached as an incremental new opportunity, not as erosion of the existing book. No volume declines anywhere in the four calls are attributed to changing habits. (The long view had said: the judge read the calls and found nothing, and the only computed fingerprint is clean.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Cyclicality (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
