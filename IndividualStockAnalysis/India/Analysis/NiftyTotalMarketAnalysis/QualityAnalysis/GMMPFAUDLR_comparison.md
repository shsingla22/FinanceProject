### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 2 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 2 weakened, 5 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Brand is invoked as an asset — SEMCO's 'large and established brand name' in Brazil and the plan to consolidate the global mixing platform 'under one brand' — but there is no heritage story, A&P spend, or premium-pricing-plus-share-gain evidence in these calls. (The long view had said: The Pfaudler name carries real heritage and technology leadership in glass-lined equipment — management says it is the first choice and market leader, and the brand has been stretched into mixing, systems and services — but there is no A&P spend, no premium pricing evidence, and the flagship glass-lined market is described as mature and price-pressured.)
- **Toll Roads**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Management describes a concentrated supply structure ('one of the two other suppliers who have local manufacturing in the US') and stresses in-house process technology for critical duty equipment, but glass-lined reactors and systems are customer capex, not a sub-1% consumable, and no certification/standards or 'don't switch once converted' language appears. (The long view had said: Glass-lined reactors and heavy engineering vessels are large negotiated capex items, and management explicitly reports competitive intensity and pricing pressure rather than price-insensitive customers; margins swing with steel and gas prices (Aug 2022, Aug 2024).)

**That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: FY26 order intake rose 20% to INR 3,714 crore and backlog hit an all-time high of INR 2,205 crore (+27% y/y) while the core chemical end market shrank, and geographic expansion continues via SEMCO/Poland — but no explicit market-share numbers or comparisons to named competitors are given, so share gain is inferred rather than stated. (The long view had said: Management claims leadership and 'first choice' status in glass-lined but explicitly says that market is mature and the play there is not share growth; real gains are in adjacent verticals and geographies — non-traditional industries rose to ~50% of order intake by FY26, with Brazil (SEMCO), Australia and Poland expansion — though hard share numbers are never given.)
- **Low Price Plus**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: There is real cost action in the window — Hyderabad and UK closures, a 30-person German downsizing costing INR 44 crore plus INR 9 crore, China cost-saving measures, Brazil as a low-cost source — but it is footprint rationalisation to protect margin, and no call describes passing savings into price or a scale-driven low-price proposition, so the pattern itself is untested by the evidence. (The long view had said: There is a genuine, itemised cost programme — India/Poland low-cost sourcing, shutdown of Hyderabad and UK plants, 30 headcount out of Germany with a INR 44 crore provision (Feb 2026) — and an explicit ambition to add cost leadership to technology leadership, but this is restructuring inherited costs, not a scale-driven low-price model with high asset turns.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (NO FIT); Forward Integrators (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Innovation Dominance (LIKELY FIT); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 10 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
