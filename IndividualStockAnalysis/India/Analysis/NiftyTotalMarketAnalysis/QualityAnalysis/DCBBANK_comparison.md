### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 0 worsened, 4 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view; 1 could not be compared. Financial resilience: Unknown on the long view, Unknown on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Government Dependency**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: As a regulated bank, pricing and capital are materially set by policy rather than the company: repo cuts drive lending yields instantly while deposit repricing lags, CRR changes swing margins, RBI approval gated the promoter capital infusion, RBI rules cap gold LTV, and the new labour code imposed a one-off INR 26.87 crores hit. This is regulatory-systemic exposure, not dependence on government contracts, subsidies or tenders, which are absent from the calls. (The long view had said: Bank economics are repeatedly reset by the regulator rather than the market: the Nov-2021 RBI 'out of order' daily-90-day circular drove the FY23 gold/SME slippage spike, the penal-interest-to-penal-charges circular structurally moved income from NIM to fees in FY25, co-lending regulation is flagged as a growth constraint ('provided there is no change in regulations on co-lending'), repo cuts of 100bps drove a 56bps fall in yield on advances in FY26, RBI LTV caps define the gold product, and PSL obligations dictate the agri/KCC/tractor mix.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
