### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 3 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 3. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 2 weakened, 7 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 0 eased, 1 worsened, 4 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Every revenue line is customer capex: T&D tied to PGCIL/utility capex programmes (management cites a 'multi-decade super global capex cycle' and a ~INR90,000cr/annum PGCIL pipeline), B&F to developer and data-centre capex, Urban Infra to metro capex — none of it is flow/operations-linked, and the Q2 FY26 call already flagged 'temporary headwinds due to lower capex, geopolitical issues'. The 22-35% revenue growth and 62% PBT jump in FY26 are being delivered at what management itself describes as a capex upcycle, so current margins cannot be assumed to be mid-cycle. (The long view had said: Every revenue line is customer-capex driven — PGCIL/PFC/REC transmission tenders, Aramco oil & gas, metro rail, data centres, residential B&F — and management explicitly frames the T&D outlook as a capex cycle, while input commodities (steel, aluminium, zinc, copper, diesel) drive margin discussion in FY26. Order books swing with client capex programmes, not client operations, which is the dangerous form; the FY24→FY26 run has been the up-leg of that cycle (revenue INR 19,626cr → 27,143cr, consol PBT margin 4.1%→4.9%), so current margins are cycle-elevated by an unknowable amount.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
