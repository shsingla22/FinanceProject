# Honeywell Automation India Ltd. (HONAUT) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

*Industry: INDUSTRIAL MANUFACTURING*

Honeywell Automation India Limited (HAIL) is the Indian listed arm of the global Honeywell group, and it sells industrial and building automation — the control systems, software and equipment that run and monitor large facilities. Its customers are industrial and infrastructure owners: refineries and petrochemical complexes, thermal power plants, pulp and paper mills, airports, metro stations, commercial buildings, data centres, pharma/life-sciences plants and newer areas like green hydrogen and lithium-ion battery gigafactories, plus other Honeywell companies abroad that buy its manufacturing and engineering work. It makes money by integrating and installing these systems as projects (often large, multi-year domestic contracts that tie up working capital), by distributing specific Honeywell building-management and sensing products, and then by earning steady lifecycle service and annuity revenue off the installed base — management's stated strategy is to build install base in an asset class and harvest that annuity. It operates in India as its home geography plus exports to Honeywell subsidiaries worldwide; FY2023-24 revenue was ₹4,582 crore, up 18% (FY2022-23 was ₹3,448 crore), with exports growing 16% and domestic 10%, and the company aims to grow at 2x India's GDP versus a historical ~11.5%, or 1.5x GDP.

**The parts of the business:**

- **System Integration – Process Solutions** — Designs, installs and upgrades control and safety systems for heavy process industries such as refineries, petrochemical complexes, pulp and paper machines and thermal power plants (a named FY24 win was upgrading the distributed control system at a thermal plant); the calls describe Oil & Gas as historically a major end market and this as a 'core business platform and offering.'
- **System Integration – Building Solutions** — Supplies integrated building management, safety, security and access-control systems for infrastructure and commercial sites — airports (including an advanced visual guidance docking system), metro stations, commercial buildings, data centres, a lithium-ion battery gigafactory and a leading life-sciences/pharma manufacturer; the calls list several of these as FY24 key wins but give no separate revenue figure.
- **Distribution of Building Management Systems and Sensing Solutions** — Sells specified Honeywell building-management and sensing product lines into the Indian market as a distributor; management names it as one of the three legs of the HAIL portfolio but does not size it in the calls.
- **Global manufacturing and Engineering Services (exports)** — Manufactures and does engineering work in India for Honeywell subsidiaries worldwide; the calls say exports grew 16% year-on-year versus 10% domestic, so it is a meaningfully faster-growing part of the mix, though the exact share is not disclosed.
- **Services / lifecycle annuity on installed base** — Ongoing service, digital and performance-management work (process control, cyber security, remote visibility, managed performance) on systems already installed — management calls this the point of building install base and notes service growth is 'linear', i.e. steadier than lumpy project revenue.
- **Energy transition and sustainability projects (within automation)** — Automation for newer energy-related assets — an indigenously built green hydrogen plant and a battery energy storage/load management project in Lakshadweep — described as an emerging-industry pivot rather than a separately reported segment; note management explicitly says Honeywell's Aerospace and Energy & Sustainability Solutions businesses sit with Honeywell Inc, not with HAIL.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
