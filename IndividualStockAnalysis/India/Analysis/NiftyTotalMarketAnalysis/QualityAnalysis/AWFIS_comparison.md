**That strengthened:**

Why now: Founder-led with a stable core team (Ramani, Lakhani) and a genuinely disciplined financial posture — gross debt of ~INR7 crores in Q1, net-cash through FY26, ROCE sustained above 60% — and they disclosed the Transform shortfall openly with its causes. Against that, the Transform full-year guidance reaffirmed in both Q1 and Q2 was not met (Q3 revenue declined), and the CFO changed mid-year, so the promise-to-delivery record inside this 12-month window is mixed. (The long view had said: Guidance set on early calls was met or beaten (FY25 guided 30% revenue, delivered 42%; margin guided +150bps, delivered +440bps), the balance sheet stayed net cash throughout with ROCE explicitly used as the yardstick, leadership is founder-led with internal succession (Lakhani, then Rochlani), and bad news is disclosed plainly — the FY26 D&B shortfall and the GRAP-IV delays were volunteered, and a mis-stated FY24 cohort figure was corrected in the transcript.)

(The long view had said: Seats and revenue have compounded hard across the nine calls (FY24 INR849cr → FY25 INR1,208cr → FY26 INR1,493cr; 110,540 → 184,000 signed seats) and the share gain is profitable, with EBITDA margin 31%→36.8% and ROCE 43%→60%+; but management asserts leadership without ever citing an explicit market-share number versus named competitors.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
