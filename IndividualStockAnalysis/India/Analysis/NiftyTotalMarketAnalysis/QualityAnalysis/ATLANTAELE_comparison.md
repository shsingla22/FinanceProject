### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 2 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 1. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 2 weakened, 8 unchanged.

But there is no A&P spend, no premium pricing, and no adjacency stretch under the name. **That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Growth is far above any plausible industry rate — FY26 revenue +48.8% to INR1,851.5cr, Q3 +80% YoY, order book to a record INR3,116cr on record Q1 FY27 inflow of INR972cr — and capacity went 16,000 to 63,000 MVA, but management never cites a market-share number or a share position against named competitors, so persistent share gain is inferred, not evidenced. ### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

**That worsened:**

Why now: Revenue is capital-goods revenue tied directly to utility and renewable-developer capex programmes — management itself flags year-end budget-driven procurement and heavy H2 skew, and the company has just completed a ~4x capacity build (16,000 to 63,000 MVA) whose returns depend on the transmission cycle holding; Q1 FY27 revenue fell 37.6% QoQ with EBITDA margin normalising from ~20% to 16.5%, showing how much the reported margin is cycle/mix dependent. ## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 2 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
