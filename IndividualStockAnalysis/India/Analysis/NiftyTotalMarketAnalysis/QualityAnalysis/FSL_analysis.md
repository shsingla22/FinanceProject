# Firstsource Solutions Ltd. (FSL) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

*Industry: IT*

Firstsource Solutions is an Indian-listed outsourcing company (Mumbai head office, listed on NSE/BSE as FSL) that runs customer-facing and back-office business processes on behalf of large companies — answering calls and chats, processing claims and paperwork, chasing overdue payments, and increasingly redesigning those processes with automation and AI. Its customers are mostly big US and UK firms in banking and lending, health insurance and hospital systems, telecom/media/technology, and utilities and retail. It makes money mainly from ongoing multi-year service contracts priced per transaction, per interaction or per seat/outcome (management calls a contract worth over $5 million of annual value a 'large deal'), plus newer platform-fee-plus-per-interaction pricing on its AI collections platform. Work is delivered from the US, UK, India and other offshore/nearshore sites (including Mexico, plus Australia and Canada more recently) by about 36,000 staff; FY26 revenue was about INR 95.6 billion (roughly $1 billion, up ~19.7% in rupees) at an EBIT margin of 11.7%.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Banking and Financial Services (BFS)** — Handles lending and banking processes for US and UK banks, mortgage lenders, fintechs and card issuers — historically the company's most cyclical and heavily discussed vertical, and in FY26 still growing about 9% year-on-year with the strongest exit pipeline of recent quarters.
- **Mortgage (within BFS)** — Loan origination and servicing support for US mortgage lenders — about $32 million of revenue in one quarter of FY23 when it collapsed (down 48% year-on-year) and dragged down group guidance; by FY26 it has been repositioned as consulting-led 'mortgage transformation' work aimed at cutting clients' costs while volumes stay weak.
- **Collections (within BFS)** — Recovering overdue debts for card issuers, banks, fintechs and utilities across first-party, third-party and legal collections, increasingly through a digital/AI platform; management calls it 25-plus years of domain expertise and it is now being extended into the UK (a large regulated UK collections deal went live in FY26) and reinforced by the Pastdue Credit acquisition.
- **Healthcare** — Serves both health insurers ('payers') — member and provider services, claims processing, digital intake — and hospitals/health systems ('providers') via revenue cycle management; described as a core growth pillar and a key differentiator because few rivals cover both sides, with 16% year-on-year growth in Q4 FY26 and bolstered by the QBSS and TeleMedik acquisitions.
- **Communications, Media and Technology (CMT)** — Customer service and back-office work for telecom, media, education-technology and consumer-technology firms — including newer work like training large consumer tech companies' AI models — and one of the fastest-growing verticals, up 14% year-on-year in Q3 FY26 with three of that quarter's five large deals.
- **Diverse portfolio (Utilities & Energy, Retail)** — Mainly UK utilities, energy and retail clients — the smallest but fastest-growing bucket, up 21% year-on-year and 37% sequentially in Q3 FY26, with the Pastdue Credit deal adding several large new utility logos.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
