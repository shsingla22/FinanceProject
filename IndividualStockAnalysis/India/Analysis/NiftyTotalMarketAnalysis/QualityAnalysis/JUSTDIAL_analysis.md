# Just Dial (JUSTDIAL) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Just Dial runs India's largest local search platform: consumers use its website and app (85-87% of traffic is mobile) to find local businesses and services near them — restaurants, hardware shops, AC repair, tutorials and so on — searching a free database that grew from about 32.8 million listings in mid-2022 to 48.8 million by March 2025, drawing roughly 148 to 191 million unique visitors a quarter. It makes money not from users but from the small and medium businesses in that database, which pay for 'paid campaigns' (advertising subscriptions that give them visibility and leads), sold by a large in-house sales and telesales force of roughly 13,000 people; about 613,000 campaigns were active at March 2025, at around INR 2,200-2,300 per campaign per month in the big cities and about INR 1,100 in smaller ones. Subscriptions are mostly annual and paid upfront (with some monthly bank-debit plans), so cash is collected first and booked as revenue over the following 12 months — quarterly revenue rose from INR 186 crores in Q1 FY23 to INR 289 crores in Q4 FY25 at a roughly 29% EBITDA margin. It operates only in India, and separately earns very large interest income — INR 87-109 crores a quarter, often more than the operating profit — on a treasury portfolio of cash and bonds that had grown to INR 5,279 crores by March 2025.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Core local search business (listings and paid campaigns)** — The whole operating business: a free searchable directory of local businesses that attracts consumer traffic, monetised by charging those businesses for paid advertising campaigns — this is essentially 100% of the INR 289 crores of quarterly operating revenue management discusses.
- **Active paid campaigns** — The count of merchant advertising subscriptions running at any time — the key volume driver, growing from about 484,000 in June 2022 to 613,290 in March 2025, with growth in revenue coming from a mix of more campaigns plus price/realisation increases.
- **Top 11 cities vs rest of India** — Management splits the business geographically: the 11 largest cities are about 40% of campaigns but roughly 56-64% of revenue, while smaller towns are cheaper (realisations were about 48-49% of big-city levels in 2024, up from 40-42% a few years earlier) and are the stated catch-up opportunity.
- **Traffic / listings database** — The free consumer side — unique visitors (191.3 million in the March 2025 quarter, 87% mobile) and total listings (48.8 million) — which management treats as the raw material that 'leads monetisation' rather than a revenue line of its own.
- **Collections, deferred revenue and realizable value** — Because customers pay upfront for a year, management tracks cash collected (INR 340 crores in the March 2025 quarter), deferred revenue on the balance sheet (INR 558 crores) and 'realizable value' of new sign-ups as the leading indicators of future reported revenue.
- **Treasury / other income** — A very large pile of cash and bonds — INR 3,740 crores in mid-2022 rising to INR 5,279 crores by March 2025, partly from a INR 2,000 crore promoter infusion in 2021 — yielding about 7.2% and producing INR 87-109 crores a quarter of other income, an amount comparable to or larger than the operating profit; it has sat undeployed for years, with a dividend policy only 'expected' as of the April 2025 call.
- **New initiatives** — Referred to in the July 2022 call as new products being rolled out to users 'in a phase-wise manner' with focus on user experience and broader product, service and geographic coverage; no revenue is attributed to them and they drop out of the later calls, where management says the focus is entirely on the core business.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*

### Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) — STRONG FIT
*"Sells once, earns forever"*

**Why this verdict:** the calls confirm the pattern with clear, repeated evidence and the numbers agree (3 of 3 checks computable supporting).

**What the calls show:** Every call is built around annual/monthly subscription listings billed in advance: deferred revenue (explicitly 'advances received from customers') compounds from INR353cr (Jul 2022) to INR468cr (Oct 2023) to INR558cr (Apr 2025), collections routinely exceed accrued revenue, and management stresses renewals, active paid campaign base (484k to 613k) and realizations per campaign. The business is customer-funded with negligible capital intensity, though the 'product upfront' leg is absent — there is no hardware/product sale, only the subscription itself.

> "deferred revenue, which is advances received from customers has grown to INR468 crores now" — *management, on an earnings call*

- ✅ Customers effectively fund the business — cash arrives 0 days before suppliers are paid.
- ✅ Capital spending is about 1% of sales — growth needs little capital.
- ✅ Operating cash was positive in 12 of 12 years — the reliable cash engine recurring-revenue businesses show.
