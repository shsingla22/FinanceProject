### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 2 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 4 weakened, 5 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Track record on signature jobs (Mumbai/Bangalore/Kolkata metros, Udangudi, Vizhinjam, High Court circuit bench) plus an A to A+ rating upgrade supports prequalification credibility, but this is B2B reputation, not heritage brand with premium pricing — there is no A&P spend, no pricing premium and no consumer-brand adjacency in any FY26 call. (The long view had said: The 'brand' is a technical/prequalification reputation in marine and underground work — the 9km Udangudi breakwater described as a first in Asia, Vizhinjam completed where the client had struggled for years, Kolkata/Mumbai/Bangalore metro underground delivered — which wins invitations to bid. But there is no consumer heritage, no A&P spend, no distributor leverage, and no premium pricing derived from the name.)
- **Innovation Dominance**: NO FIT → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: The FY26 calls disclose no R&D spend, pipeline or named product launches; adoption of precast and large-diameter tunnelling is capability extension, not disclosed innovation economics, so the evidence is silent on this pattern. (The long view had said: No R&D spend is ever disclosed across 16 calls; capability comes from buying equipment (TBMs, trench cutters) and hiring experienced engineers for the new data-centre division. Gross margins are thin and set by tender, so there is no high-margin flywheel funding innovation.)

**That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Order intake stepped up sharply in FY26 — roughly INR19,000 crores secured versus a historic ~INR7,000 crores, order book to INR29,000 crores and first-ever INR10,000 crores revenue, with overseas wins in Abu Dhabi, Bangladesh and Colombo — but no explicit market-share figures versus named competitors are given, and a material part of the inflow is captive group work won under promoter push rather than open-market share capture. (The long view had said: Persistent, profitable scale gain over the full period: quarterly revenue Rs 826cr (Q1 FY22) to Rs 2,973cr (Q4 FY26), first-ever Rs 10,061cr year, order inflow of Rs 19,000cr against a historic ~Rs 7,000cr run-rate, order book Rs 21,000cr to Rs 29,000cr — achieved while EBITDA margin rose from ~8% to 11.9% and net D/E fell to 0.18x, plus international expansion (Colombo, Bangladesh, Abu Dhabi/ADNOC). Share is not quantified against named competitors, which is the one gap.)
- **Corporate Culture**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Across FY26 the MD disclosed bad news promptly (Vadhvan Port stalled, revenue shortfall owned in Q3), guided conservatively against extrapolating the 15.1% Q4 margin, delivered the Q3 promise of a 15–20% sequentially better Q4, and ran the balance sheet down to 0.18x net debt/equity — but the record is short, with chairman churn through FY2023–FY2026 and the CFO absent from the Q4 call. (The long view had said: Bad news is disclosed early and owned (Bangalore/Delhi elevated metro losses flagged repeatedly until 'no legacy job left out'; Bangladesh political risk and Vadhavan land trouble stated plainly rather than spun), and the Aug-2022 promise of double-digit EBITDA was ultimately delivered (10.2% FY25, 11.9% FY26) alongside a conservative balance sheet through the whole cycle and a long-tenured MD and operating team. Caveat: chairman and CFO seats have churned and the promoter changed from ITD Thailand to Adani.)
- **Friendly Middleman**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Work is won directly from asset owners through competitive tenders and L1 positions (metros, ports, airports, Petronet, ADNOC, group entities) — there is no professional intermediary specifying the product, and no trade-training or channel-relationship investment appears anywhere in FY26 calls. (The long view had said: The evidence is silent — clients (NHAI/metro corporations, Adani group, ADNOC, Navy) are both the specifier and the payer; no professionals, installers or prescribers intermediate the sale.)
- **Forward Integrators**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The company remains a pure contractor handing assets over to owners on completion — no concessions, owned assets, franchising or direct-to-customer channel is discussed in any FY26 call; it hands projects over rather than moving toward the end user. (The long view had said: Silent — no own stores, franchising, licensing or direct-to-customer channel is relevant to or mentioned by an EPC contractor.)

*Unchanged: Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (NO FIT); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

**That eased:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Awards are decided by lowest-price L1 tendering, so there is no structural price premium to defend, and management is candid that commoditised segments carry thin economics ('margin in the road sector, you know what is the margin'). (The long view had said: The L1 lowest-price tender model is structurally a good-enough market — the client controls the choice and pays for compliance, not brand, which is why margins have been pinned near 10-11% through four years of record growth. **That worsened:**

Why now: Since the promoter change, group-linked work is repeatedly named as a core driver — Vizhinjam breakwater, a group power plant, data centres, group airports — and management frames future growth as promoter-driven, while an analyst questioned the company specifically on its readiness and visibility for Adani's data centre pipeline. A single promoter-customer relationship materially shapes the order book. Single projects also swing results materially (Ganga Expressway at INR600 crores in one quarter; Vadhavan's stall cutting FY26 revenue guidance), and the Mor Sagar EPC award is not even assured.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL); Fashion Risk (NO SIGNAL).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
