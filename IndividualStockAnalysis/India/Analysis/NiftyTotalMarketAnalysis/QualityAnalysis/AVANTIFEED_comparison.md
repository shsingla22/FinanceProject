### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 1 unchanged; 5 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Government Dependency**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: The US 50% reciprocal tariff was flagged in Q1 and Q2 FY26 as a policy event with 'significant impact' on the company, and by Q4 FY26 US export volumes for India had fallen 17.9% YoY — an outcome set by a foreign government's duty decision, not by the company. Exposure is bounded because the feed division (roughly INR 4,538 crore of INR 6,279 crore gross income and the bulk of PBT) is domestic and unregulated. (The long view had said: Government action determines both price and profit at nearly every link: feed selling prices require Andhra Pradesh government permission (a Rs 2.56/kg hike was rolled back), wheat/soya availability hinges on export bans and MSP, the import duty on shrimp feed was cut to 5% then reinstated at 15%, US CVD/IEEPA/Section 122 tariffs drive the export book, and profits include PLI, RODTEP, Operation Green grants and PMMSY schemes.)

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Every one of the four FY26 calls frames performance around fluctuating commodity inputs and the aquaculture cycle: fish meal moved from INR 91/kg (Q4 FY25) to INR 130/kg consumption price in Q4 FY26 with a spot price of INR 230/kg, driving feed PBT down 28% YoY in Q4 and prompting guidance that FY27 will be 'challenging'. Feed PBT margin swung between roughly 13% and 16% on essentially flat revenue, and volumes depend on shrimp prices, disease and climate. (The long view had said: Across all 17 calls performance is dictated by commodity input prices and farm-crop cycles outside management's control — fish meal moved Rs 94→111→102→93→117→130 and 'doubled from INR 100 to INR 240 in a span of two months', soya and wheat swing with crops/El Nino/export bans, and feed PBT margins oscillate quarter to quarter (Q4 FY26 feed PBT fell 28% YoY on raw material costs; PBT% guided down from 16% to 14.5-15%). Volumes swing further with weather, white-spot disease, forced harvesting and farm-gate shrimp prices.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Good Enough Goods (ELEVATED); New Entrants (WATCH); Technological Innovation (QUANT FLAG); Fashion Risk (LOW); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 12 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
