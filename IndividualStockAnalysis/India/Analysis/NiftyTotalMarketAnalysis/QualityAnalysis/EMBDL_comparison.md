### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 0 worsened, 3 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Strained on the long view, Strained on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Government Dependency**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: Revenue timing is gated by state approvals — RERA registrations, OCs, IOD for Juhu — and the FY26 presales miss was attributed directly to an approval delay; separately a government land agency (KIADB) issued a resumption order over 78 acres at Kadugodi that had to be set aside in the Karnataka High Court. Pricing and demand, however, are set by the market rather than by tariff, subsidy or a government customer. (The long view had said: Nearly every value driver in these calls runs through a government or judicial decision: RERA approvals gate launches (a Bangalore approval delay caused the FY26 presales shortfall to 93% of guidance), the 1,500-acre Nasik land bank is under an MIDC eviction/termination notice requiring SEZ de-bonding, 78 acres at Kadugodi were subject to a KIADB resumption order, Juhu awaits IOD, and an IBC Section 7 admission briefly pushed the stock into the ASM framework. Management explicitly needs to 'settle with the government' before the Nasik asset can be developed.)
- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → LOW. Why now: The calls affirmatively show pricing power rather than premium erosion: Embassy Paradiso sold out at ~INR200 crore against an INR175 crore GDV estimate, Citadel absorbed ~INR800 crore in 45 days, and management argues buyers are consolidating toward trusted branded developers — end-buyers are highly brand-aware in a post-RERA market where delivery credibility (restoring 6 delayed legacy Indiabulls projects, 3,300 handovers) is the differentiator, and no intermediary controls the choice. (The long view had said: In Mumbai, management concedes it cannot yet defend a brand premium and is pricing below established developers and offering discounts — the classic good-enough dynamic where a less-established brand competes on price. Location scarcity and high buyer brand awareness in Indian residential limit how far this goes, and in Bengaluru the premium is clearly holding.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Technological Innovation (ELEVATED); Fashion Risk (ELEVATED); New Entrants (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (HIGH RISK); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH).*

**Financial resilience:** Strained on the long view (1 of the 3 stress checks is flagged — one warning light, not yet a pattern.) vs Strained on the one-year view (1 of the 3 stress checks is flagged — one warning light, not yet a pattern.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
