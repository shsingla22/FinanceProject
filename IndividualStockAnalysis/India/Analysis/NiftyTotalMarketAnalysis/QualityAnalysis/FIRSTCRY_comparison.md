- **Profit margin** (Return on Capital): Poor → Neutral. Now: Out of every ₹100 of sales, ₹3 is operating profit. ### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 2 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 2 weakened, 7 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

**That strengthened:**

- **Corporate Culture**: PARTIAL → STRONG FIT. Why now: Over the four calls management promised sequential growth improvement and delivered it quarter by quarter, promised ~50% of online volumes on RocketBees by mid-2026 and beat the schedule, and disclosed bad news promptly and quantified (140bps diaper margin hit, 200bps supply-chain drag, GST-driven deferral), alongside a founder-led, stable leadership team and a shift to free-cash-flow and PAT positivity. (The long view had said: Founder-led with a stable team, candid ownership of mistakes ('we were late' on logistics), no acquisitions since Sep 2022, and delivered promises — sequential India growth improved 7.5%/7.9%/8.9%/11.4% through FY26 and RocketBees beat its own city/volume target — but the headline 'structurally superior growth' promise has been rolled forward from FY26 to FY27, and the brand-partner decline question was waved away rather than engaged.)

**That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: AUTC grew 10-14% and consol revenue 12% in FY26 with sequential quarterly acceleration (7.5%→7.9%→8.9%→11.4%), but no share figures are given and management describes defending — not gaining — in diapering against horizontals and quick-commerce entrants in both India and the Middle East. (The long view had said: AUTC compounds ~10-14% and offline GMV re-accelerated to mid-teens in Q4 FY26 with GlobalBees core categories at 28-30%, but management never cites a market-share number and concedes ground in diapering to horizontals and quick commerce in both India and the Middle East.)
- **Innovation Dominance**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: No R&D spend, product pipeline or new-product revenue contribution is disclosed; the named initiatives (RocketBees, FC Qwik) are logistics/operating build-outs rather than an innovation engine funded by high gross margins. (The long view had said: Innovation is operational rather than R&D-led: named initiatives (RocketBees own logistics, FirstCry Qwik 3-hour delivery, width-to-depth assortment) were launched, scaled and quantified across FY26 calls, but no R&D spend, pipeline or share-of-industry-R&D is disclosed and gross margins are falling, not rising.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Forward Integrators (STRONG FIT); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Brand Strength (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 4 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view; 1 could not be compared. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Growth over the last four calls was repeatedly moderated by consumer-cycle factors — a 'broad-based consumer slowdown', GST-driven deferral of purchases, 'muted consumer sentiment' in Q3, an unusually soft summer, and in Q4 input-cost swings from rupee depreciation and crude-linked raw material prices in the manufacturing arm. But demand is baby/child consumables bought in operation of the household, not customer capex, and the company stayed PAT and free-cash-flow positive throughout the downturn. (The long view had said: Across FY26 calls management repeatedly attributes growth misses to a broad-based consumption slowdown, weather (unusually soft summer, early monsoon), geopolitical conflict and, in Q4 FY26, crude-linked raw material prices and rupee depreciation hitting manufacturing gross margin — but demand is replenishment-led (diapering, baby care) rather than tied to any customer's capex cycle, and margins moved in a narrow band while EBITDA kept compounding.)

**That worsened:**

- **New Entrants**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Structurally different competitors are resetting economics in both geographies: 'heightened competitive intensity in the diapering category' cost ~140 bps of India gross margin in Q3 which 'has continued' into Q4, and in international, two horizontal e-commerce players that entered in late Q3 FY25 have driven 'elevated promotional activities' that management has now discussed defensively for four straight quarters. Quick-commerce speed expectations forced an entirely new channel build. (The long view had said: Structurally different competitors are hitting both geographies: quick commerce and heightened competitive intensity in diapering pressured India growth and gross margins for two consecutive quarters (~140 bps), and two horizontal e-commerce entrants ran elevated promotions in UAE/KSA from late Q3 FY25 through FY26, which management chose to sit out at the cost of top-line growth.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Fashion Risk (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Technological Innovation (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH).*

*Not comparable: Government Dependency (NOT ASSESSED → LOW — could not be assessed on the long view).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
