### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 3 eased, 0 worsened, 5 unchanged. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Revenue is a fee on market-linked AUM, so earnings track equity levels and flows: the Apr-2026 call describes markets 'correcting from near record highs' on FII outflows and crude, and Q1 FY27 shows MF AUM growth decelerating to 6% YoY with a 'marginal reduction in the SIP book'. This is market cyclicality rather than commodity or customer-capex cyclicality, and the recurring SIP base cushions it. (The long view had said: Revenue and profit ride equity market levels: Q1 FY23 PAT was dented by treasury mark-to-market, Q4 FY26 PAT fell to Rs.187cr from Rs.228cr purely on MTM in other income, and the SIP AUM dropped from Rs.87,000cr to Rs.76,000cr on market movement alone. But the underlying demand is flow-like — monthly SIP contributions, 40 lakh SIP accounts and continuous industry inflows even through the FII-outflow correction — rather than tied to any customer's capex cycle.)
- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Active management faces the classic good-enough attack — index funds at a fraction of the fee now lead industry equity NFO collections — and the intermediary controls the choice, with management stating flows depend on getting onto large banks' and distributors' recommendation lists rather than on end-investor brand pull. Management's defence is investment-performance improvement rather than a structural moat, and the calls contain no discussion of a defended price premium. (The long view had said: This is the sharpest risk: an index or ETF is the good-enough substitute for active management at 8bps versus 63-64bps, direct plans undercut regular ones, and the intermediary — not the end investor — controls the choice, with distributors taking ~65% of TER and bank recommendation lists gating flows. Fee capture is visibly eroding: equity yield slipped from ~68bps in FY25 to 63-64bps, a further 5bps TER cut landed in April 2026, and telescoping pricing compresses yields automatically as AUM grows. The product is not complex and end-investor brand awareness is low.)
- **Fashion Risk**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Demand is functional and repeat-purchase rather than taste-driven: management anchors the franchise on a recurring SIP book of 40 lakh accounts representing ~44% of equity AUM, plus a broad multi-product suite (Flexi Cap, BAF, Multi Asset, Mid/Small Cap, Index) rather than one trend bet. Thematic funds like the Conglomerate and Consumption funds are cited as flow drivers, but they are a bounded part of a diversified shelf. (The long view had said: Flows visibly chase whatever category is in vogue — industry NFO money 'majorly coming from sectoral/thematic funds', the company's own best NFO was a Quant Fund, and management admits market share suffers because 'a certain segment in the market has been giving a significant outperformance, in that segment we as a fund house do not have a dominant presence.' Fund fashions rotate quickly, though the core demand is repeat-purchase.)

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Technological Innovation (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
