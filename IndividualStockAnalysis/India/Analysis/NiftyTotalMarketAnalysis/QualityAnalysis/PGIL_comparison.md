### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 3 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 0 strengthened, 2 weakened, 8 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Repeated evidence of wallet-share and customer gains — Vietnam grew strongly while the country's garment exports de-grew, two major customers added in Dhaka, Vietnam utilisation 63%→80%+ and Indonesia 39%→47% — but management never cites explicit market-share numbers or share versus named competitors. (The long view had said: Persistent, profitable share gain is the clearest thread across the record: revenue INR3,158cr (FY23) to INR3,436cr (FY24) to INR5,025cr (FY26), capacity 82m to 101m pieces ahead of the H1 FY27 target, customer mix widened from ~80-90% US in FY23 to five major markets by FY26, all while ROCE held at 28% and net debt/EBITDA stayed at 0.25x — share bought with capability, not credit or margin.)

Why now: No R&D spend, named new products or pipeline is disclosed in any of the four calls; references to 'high value-added products' and sustainable laundry capacity are capability/mix comments, too thin to judge innovation dominance either way. (The long view had said: There is design and process capability — 75+ designers across eight locations using CLO/Browzwear/Optitex 3D tools (Aug 2022), continuous automation and digitisation capex, solar and sustainable-laundry projects (FY26) — but no R&D line item, no named new products with revenue impact, and gross margin is described as 'largely a function of the product mix', not innovation.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 0 worsened, 4 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Across all four FY26 calls revenue and margins were remarkably stable — five-plus consecutive INR1,000cr+ quarters, FY26 revenue up 11.5% to INR5,025cr and adjusted EBITDA margin holding 9.3% (10.3% ex-tariff/new-plant losses) — and demand is driven by retailers' seasonal replenishment orders (spring/summer 2026 placements), i.e. a flow product tied to customers' operations, not their capex. Management also kept expanding capex (Bangladesh 6m pieces, Bihar, Vietnam) straight through the worst of the tariff shock rather than pulling back. (The long view had said: Demand is consumer-apparel (a flow/replenishment product, not customer capex), but the calls repeatedly show the retail inventory and macro cycle swinging orders — the FY23 US destocking, the FY24 'relatively flat' market, and FY26 retailers cutting piece counts as tariffs raised tickets. Group revenue and adjusted EBITDA margin nonetheless compounded steadily (7.9% Q1FY23 to 10.3% FY26) and the company kept investing (Bihar, Bangladesh, Guatemala, Vietnam land) through the tariff shock.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Technological Innovation (LOW); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); New Entrants (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Fashion Risk (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 8 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
