*Foundation test: FAIL on the long view, FAIL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Low Price Plus**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The high-quality-loans leg of the bank variant is clearly met — GNPA down to 1.57%, net NPA 0.36%, PCR 94.76%, credit cost 0.49% and slippage described as industry best — but the cheap-deposit leg fails: CASA is admitted to be structurally low (29.70% vs a 30-32% target) and management concedes margins are below peers, and no cost-culture or cost-to-income discipline is discussed. (The long view had said: The bank variant fails on its key leg — funding is expensive, not cheap: cost of deposits rose through FY25 on 7.5%+ incremental deposits, CASA is under 30%, and by Jul 2026 the MD concedes Canara has one of the lowest NIMs in the PSU pack because of bulk-deposit dependence. Cost-to-income did improve (50%+ to 47% in FY25) and asset quality is now industry-best, but there is no evidence of a replicable low-cost funding culture.)

**That weakened:**

- **Cost to Replicate**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Nothing in the calls articulates why a funded competitor could not replicate the franchise; the evidence points the other way — a commoditised, repo-linked loan book that reprices instantly, a structurally weak CASA franchise and NIM below peers, with the strongest recent growth driver being a government scheme (ECLGS 5.0) available to every bank. (The long view had said: Two articulable barriers exist — government ownership/sovereign deposit backing, and a South India branch density that anchors a Rs 2.45 lakh crore gold-loan book customers prefer to transact in person for. But neither yields pricing power (lowest-in-peer-group NIM, price-taker on loan yields), and nothing in the calls describes an advantage a well-funded entrant or a larger PSU could not rebuild.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
