Now: The bank called the NIM trough at 2.8% in Oct 2025 and then delivered the recovery as high-cost deposits repriced 40-60bps down, i.e. it sits just past the bottom of the rate cycle with the tailwind ahead. | The long view had said: Mixed cycle position: the rate cycle appears to be turning favourably for NIM, but credit metrics (12bps slippage, 9bps credit cost) are at cyclical peaks, meaning provisioning can only normalise upward.

Now: It is a small, unglamorous regional bank, but it is a listed, brokerage-covered name with individual and institutional investors on every call, so it is hardly ignored by capital. | The long view had said: A 96-year-old Thrissur-based bank with a deliberately modest, safety-first ambition operating in a regional NRI/gold niche that attracts little capital or attention.

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
