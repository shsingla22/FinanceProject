# Le Travenues Techno (IXIGO) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Le Travenues Technology Limited, which trades as ixigo, is an Indian online travel agency (OTA) that sells train, bus and flight tickets to everyday Indian consumers through a family of apps and websites — ixigo, ConfirmTkt and AbhiBus — with hotels and metro tickets being added more recently. Its core customer is the mass-market Indian traveller, including what management calls the 'Next Billion Users' in Tier 2, 3 and 4 towns, many of whom are booking a flight or a ticket online for the first time in their lives. It makes money by taking a cut on the tickets it sells (convenience fees and commissions on gross booking value) plus growing fees from add-on 'peace of mind' services such as ixigo Assured, Assured Flex and Travel Guarantee — in Q4 FY26 over 31% of transactions carried such an add-on. The business is almost entirely India-focused, though it also sells international flights out of India (over 20% of flights GTV by Q3 FY26); for FY26 it reported gross transaction value of about INR 18,693 crores (~USD 2 billion), revenue from operations of INR 1,228 crores and profit after tax of INR 71 crores.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Trains** — Train ticket booking through the ixigo and ConfirmTkt apps (sold via IRCTC), historically the company's anchor business and still the OTA market leader with a 62% OTA share; it was 43% of GTV and 35% of contribution margin as of Q3 FY26, though its share of the mix is shrinking as flights and buses grow faster, and it faced volume pressure in Q4 FY26 from railway policy changes and tight seat supply.
- **Flights** — Domestic and international air ticket booking, the fastest-scaling large vertical and by Q4 FY26 the biggest business by GTV at INR 2,018 crores in the quarter, supported on the supply side by GDS tie-ups with Amadeus and Travelport and 26 airlines connected via NDC.
- **Buses (AbhiBus)** — Intercity bus ticket booking, acquired in 2021 when it did roughly INR 400–500 crores of annual GTV and scaled to over INR 2,400 crores in the twelve months to Q3 FY26 (about 6x), still compounding 40–50% a year and now the largest contributor of contribution margin, with management deliberately favouring growth over margin here.
- **Hotels** — A new and still small line, with a hotel supply platform called HELLO for onboarding properties directly and a small on-ground team; management says it will not report hotels separately until it reaches product-market fit and meaningful scale.
- **Value-added services / 'peace of mind' products** — Paid add-ons layered on top of tickets — ixigo Assured and Assured Flex, AbhiAssured free cancellation, Travel Guarantee (discounted alternate travel if a waitlisted train ticket doesn't confirm) and Flight Tracker Pro — which management now describes as the real business ('we are in the Peace of Mind business'), attached to over 31% of Q4 FY26 transactions, generally at slightly lower margin than the core ticket business in their early phase.
- **AI customer-experience stack (TARA)** — In-house AI agents and chat/voice bots that handle customer support and disruption management — 4.35 million queries handled end-to-end in Q4 FY26, with 81.5% of voice and 91.1% of chat conversations resolved without a human — which management frames as the main source of operating leverage rather than a separate revenue line.
- **Distribution partnerships and B2B2C** — Selling through other people's platforms and through offline agents — an exclusive tie-up powering flights, buses and trains inside PhonePe, plus Meesho, Uber (intercity buses), native apps on ChatGPT, and travelsupermall.com (TSM), a B2B2C platform that pushes travel inventory out to offline travel agents; these are described as reach and market-share levers rather than separately sized segments.
- **Adjacent and ancillary offerings** — Smaller add-on businesses around the core ticket: food delivery on trains with Swiggy (40,000+ restaurants, 160+ stations), metro ticketing in Delhi, Mumbai and Bengaluru, and the AU-ixigo co-branded credit card — all described as early-traction or supporting initiatives, with no separate size disclosed.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
