Now: Profit margins have barely moved over the years (±2.1 points) — consistent with rational pricing and few new competitors. And from the calls: The chosen niches — complex injectables, transdermal patches, ADCs, non-infringing routes — carry real regulatory and technical entry barriers that limit new entrants, and pricing discussion shows no discount war. | The long view had said: Profit margins swing by ±4.6 points — a hint of price wars, cost shocks, or new entrants disturbing the industry. ### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 3 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 3. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 3 weakened, 7 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Pricing Power**: NO FIT → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Gross margin held at a high 68–72% through FY26 with EBITDA margin up ~3% year-on-year, and management leans on limited-competition positioning (Nilotinib facing little generic competition), but no call cites an actual price increase taken without volume loss. (The long view had said: Evidence points to price deflation, not pricing power — FY23 gross margin fell 68%→61% on API/formulation price erosion, Europe is admitted to be tender-driven, and management expects Nilotinib generic entry to hit FY28; margin recovery by FY26 comes from mix and operating leverage, never from disclosed price increases.)

**That weakened:**

- **Friendly Middleman**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Prescription pharma economics show through — the Q3 FY26 call credits NorUDCA's launch success to physician response rather than end-consumer demand — but no call describes trade training programmes or systematic channel/prescriber relationship investment. (The long view had said: The FY26 NorUDCA launch is explicitly prescriber-led — physicians choose, patients pay — and distribution runs through three large pharma partners' detailing forces rather than end-customer marketing; but no trade training programmes or channel-relationship investment is described across the 18 calls.)
- **Brand Strength**: LIKELY FIT → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: Nothing in the FY26 calls addresses heritage, A&P spend, distributor leverage or brand-led adjacency launches — the business is described as B2B/partner-led science, not brand. (The long view had said: There is a reputational (B2B) brand — a stated global reputation for compliant, non-infringing chemistry that helps win CDMO mandates, and the FY26 own-label NorUDCA launch — but no heritage story, A&P spend, or distributor leverage is discussed, and consumer brand economics are absent.)
- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The four FY26 calls describe product supply, CDMO development revenues and profit shares, but never attach rates, AMC/service revenue share, renewal rates or deferred revenue/customer advances as a funding source. (The long view had said: CDMO/licensing structure gives multi-year annuity-like streams — the Unicycive deal is a 4+4 year exclusive supply agreement with $10m staged milestones and customer-funded capex, and later calls show recurring licensing income (~INR200 crores) plus quarterly partner profit-shares — but there is no installed base, attach rate, renewal rate or deferred-revenue funding discussion in the classic sense.)

*Unchanged: Innovation Dominance (STRONG FIT); Toll Roads (PARTIAL); Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (NO FIT); Forward Integrators (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
