### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 4 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **New Entrants**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Nitric acid realisations were explicitly hurt by imported product and downstream dumping, and IPA suffered because global propylene was cheap abroad but not available in India at those prices — i.e. offshore low-cost supply, not domestic rivals, is resetting price. Management's answer is freight proximity, the East-coast Gopalpur plant and specialty/pharma-grade mix rather than price matching. (The long view had said: Analysts pressed management on Chinese ammonium nitrate exports rising from 7% to 30% share and on Chambal/GNFC adding ~500 KTPA domestic ammonia/AN capacity into a market management concedes is flat, while nitric acid pricing is 'under pressure from excess imports and downstream dumping from abroad' and new capacity; management's answer was to reject the 'one quarter lens' rather than rebut the supply math.)

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: All four calls attribute swings to commodity inputs and end-market cycles: ammonia is 75-80% of chemical variable cost and moved '10% to 200%' across five Q1s, IPA tracked propylene/acetone, and Q3 FY26 EBITDA fell 27% Y-o-Y on raw-material inflation plus slower mining; the company is also mid-way through a large capex cycle (Gopalpur TAN, Dahej acid) that pushed net debt/EBITDA from 1.5x to 2.27x within the year. (The long view had said: Across all 16 quarters results swing on commodity spreads — ammonia, phos acid, propylene/acetone, FGAN prices — and on coal/mining activity and monsoon; Q1FY23 EBITDA margin 24.3% vs 12% a year later, then FY26 EBITDA down with Q3 EBITDA -27% Y-o-Y on raw-material inflation, and TAN demand is explicitly seasonal/cyclical and tied to mining output targets.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Technological Innovation (LOW); Shifting Consumer Preferences (NO SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
