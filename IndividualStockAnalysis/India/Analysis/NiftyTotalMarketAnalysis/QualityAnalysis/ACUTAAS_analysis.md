# Acutaas Chemical (ACUTAAS) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Acutaas Chemicals (formerly Ami Organics) is an Indian specialty chemicals maker based in Surat, Gujarat, with plants at Sachin, Jhagadia and Ankleshwar. Its main business is making advanced pharmaceutical intermediates — the chemical building blocks that drug companies use to make finished medicines, mostly for chronic (long-term) conditions — sold to both innovator and generic drugmakers in India and overseas, increasingly on a contract-development-and-manufacturing (CDMO) basis where it develops and makes molecules to a customer's specification. A smaller specialty chemicals arm sells commodity/agrochemical-type chemicals, materials for semiconductor and memory-chip makers (through its Baba Fine Chemicals unit and a South Korean JV, Indichem), and electrolyte additives for lithium-ion batteries. It earns money by selling these chemicals in bulk, mainly for export to customers in Europe, Japan, Korea, Taiwan, China and the US, and reported FY26 revenue of about INR 1,339 crore with PAT of INR 356 crore, targeting 25% revenue growth in FY27.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Advanced Pharmaceutical Intermediates** — Makes over 480 chemical intermediates that go into medicines (90%+ into chronic-illness drugs), and is by far the biggest part of the company — roughly 78% of sales back in FY23 and INR 292.7 crore of INR 329.7 crore total revenue in Q1 FY27, growing 76.5% year-on-year.
- **CDMO (within Advanced Pharmaceutical Intermediates)** — Contract development and manufacturing of molecules for pharma customers, described as the main growth driver of the pharma segment in recent quarters, with a pipeline of new products and NCE work that management says takes time to commercialize but then ramps steeply.
- **Specialty Chemicals** — The smaller second segment — about INR 37-42 crore per quarter versus INR 292 crore for pharma — covering commodity chemicals, semiconductor materials and battery chemicals.
- **Commodity Chemicals (within Specialty Chemicals)** — Lower-margin bulk chemicals including agrochemical import-substitute products; management is deliberately phasing these out from FY27 and replacing them with higher-margin products using the same plants.
- **Baba Fine Chemicals (BFC) / semiconductor chemicals** — An acquired business selling chemicals used in making semiconductors and memory chips to customers in Korea, Japan and Taiwan; it was weak at around INR 5 crore a quarter in FY25 but has recovered strongly and is expected to become a sizeable, self-sustaining business by FY28.
- **Battery Chemicals** — Electrolyte additives for lithium-ion batteries, made at a new Jhagadia plant that started commercial supply in Q1 FY27, with two products commercialized, two more due in FY27, long-term customer contracts and what management calls unprecedented demand — still small today but one of the two 'investment phase' verticals meant to stand on its own by FY28.
- **Indichem Inc (South Korea joint venture)** — A JV in South Korea for semiconductor/electronics materials into which the company put INR 190 crore in FY26; its R&D centre is running and sending customer samples, with manufacturing planned.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*

### Cyclicality — ELEVATED
*"Boom-and-bust earnings"*
