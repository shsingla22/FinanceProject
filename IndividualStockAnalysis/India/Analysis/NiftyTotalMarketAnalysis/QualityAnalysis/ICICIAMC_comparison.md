### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 0 worsened, 5 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Unknown on the long view, Unknown on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: The classic good-enough attack is live in the disclosed margin table: passive earns 10-12bps and arbitrage 30bps against 67bps for active equity, and the cheap products are growing roughly twice as fast as the premium ones, while direct plans (no distributor trail) rose to 29.5% of equity AUM. Management never has to defend the premium in these calls because the economics have so far held. (The long view had said: Cheap substitutes are structurally taking share of incremental money: passives at 9-12bps and direct plans at zero commission grow far faster than the 66-67bps active equity book (own passives +48.3% year-on-year vs active +21.9%), while the regulator cut TER slabs and the intermediary — MFD, bank or national distributor controlling ~63% of equity AUM — decides where flows go. Active equity margin has already drifted from 67bps to 66bps.)
- **Fashion Risk**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Category fashion exists at the industry level — mid-caps and small-caps attracted the highest flows after outperforming 24%/17.2%, while 'thematic and hybrids... faced reduction in net flows' — but the company's demand base is repeat-purchase systematic investing spread across a diversified product suite (equity 14% share, equity-hybrid 26.6% share, debt, passive, alternates, SIF), not a single trending product. (The long view had said: Flows chase whichever style is currently in favour: in Q1 FY27 mid-cap and small-cap categories attracted the highest net flows after small-caps returned 24%, while thematic and multi-asset hybrid funds saw flows fall sequentially — the firm's largest relative overweight (26.6% share of equity-oriented hybrids) sits in the category losing favour. Management explicitly positions the house as 'a relatively defensive AMC', a style that is rewarded or ignored depending on sentiment.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
