**That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The calls discuss margin expansion and operating efficiency but never a low-price proposition, itemised cost savings, no-frills choices, or savings passed to customers — the evidence is silent on the pattern's core. (The long view had said: There is a real cost culture — digital journeys keeping opex low, branches costing only Rs 3-5 lakh each, stable CAC, and operating leverage that took Q4 FY26 EBITDA margin to 39% on 23% revenue growth — but Choice positions itself as a full-service phygital broker against discount players, not as a low-price offer, so the 'low price' half of the pattern is missing.)

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 3 eased, 0 worsened, 4 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Strained on the long view, Strained on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Broking and distribution is ~58-59% of revenue and management explicitly attributes conditions to RBI rate cuts, returning FPI flows and market sentiment; the Q4 FY26 call showed wealth AUM falling 23% and stock broking AUM slipping from Rs. 60,500 Cr to Rs. 52,482 Cr on shifting market sentiment. Bounded because the revenue base is operations-linked (brokerage, AUM fees, interest income, insurance renewals) rather than customer capex, and the advisory order book of Rs. 698 Cr gives stated two-to-three-year visibility. (The long view had said: Broking revenue is a direct function of market activity — management concedes derivative regulatory changes and volatility create headwinds, and the Q4 FY26 wealth AUM fell 23% on debt-fund redemptions — while advisory revenue rides government capex mission programmes rather than customer operations. Diversification across four segments and a 24–36 month advisory order book (Rs. 698 Cr) materially damp the swings.)
- **Technological Innovation**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Across all four calls the only technology discussion is the company's own incremental platform build-out (FinX, Choice Money, Choice Connect, digital onboarding, digital investment platform for IPPB); no competitor platform is discussed defensively and no analyst raised disruption or AI. (The long view had said: The business is built around 211+ physical branches and a 67,000-strong associate network in an industry where discount/digital-first brokers reset economics; analysts repeatedly probe the moat versus Angel One and Motilal Oswal, and management answers by defending the physical model. Offsetting this, 70% of broking revenue already executes digitally and the company runs in-house platforms (FinX, Choice Money, Choice Connect) with a dedicated CPTO building lower-CAC digital acquisition.)
- **Shifting Consumer Preferences**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: The savings-behaviour shift management describes runs in the company's favour and it has already repositioned the portfolio toward it: the Q4 debt-MF redemptions that cut wealth AUM 23% were offset by 35% growth in equity MF AUM, which management says carries higher margins, and the AMC was launched with gold ETF and Nifty index funds to capture the same flow. (The long view had said: Two live preference migrations touch the business — the structural shift of household savings from physical to financial assets (a tailwind management is positioned for) and the shift toward self-directed online investing (a headwind to a branch-led model); the 23% wealth AUM fall as clients rotated out of debt funds shows allocation preference moving quickly against reported AUM. The demand base itself is not eroding, so exposure is bounded.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 2 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
