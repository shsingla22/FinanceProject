### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 0 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 4. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 4 strengthened, 1 weakened, 5 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Pricing Power**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: The Jul 2026 call attributes margin expansion partly to 'pricing discipline' and 'judicious pricing actions' plus premiumisation in consumer healthcare, but no explicit price increases with retained volumes, elasticity or scarcity language are given, and margins (12.5% EBITDA) are far from stable-high. (The long view had said: By Jul 2026 management credits 'pricing discipline' and premiumisation for a 400bps EBITDA margin expansion, and in Nov 2022 spoke of 'judicious price increases' to offset inflation — but this is inflation pass-through and mix, not repeated above-cost price rises; the Chinese competitive attack on the CHG portfolio over five to six muted quarters is the opposite signal.)
- **Brand Strength**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Real brand evidence sits in the India Consumer Healthcare segment only — Power Brands at 53% of consumer sales growing 23%, ~12% of sales on media and trade promotion, and the new 'i-choose' master brand as an adjacency stretch — but this is the smallest of the three businesses and CDMO/CHG carry no brand premium story. (The long view had said: Only the smallest of three segments shows brand economics: India consumer Power Brands rose from 42% of segment sales (H1 FY23) to 53% (Q1 FY27) on ~12-15% of revenue spent on media and trade promotion, with the 'i-choose' master brand as an adjacency stretch. The CDMO and CHG businesses, which are the bulk of revenue, are B2B and show no brand premium.)
- **Innovation Dominance**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Named capability investments recur — the USD90 million ADC expansion at Riverview/Lexington, the Ajinomoto AJICAP collaboration, a 27–28 product injectable pipeline against a ~$2bn addressable market, 100+ consumer launches in three years — but R&D spend is never disclosed or shown growing, and gross margins are not high enough to fund outsized R&D. (The long view had said: There is a regular launch cadence (10 new products and 11 SKUs in one quarter, ~15% of consumer sales from launches since Apr'20; 27-37 injectable SKUs in pipeline; ADC payload-linker site at Riverview and the Ajinomoto AJICAP collaboration), but R&D spend is never disclosed or discussed as a share of industry — growth is capex-led, and blended gross margin of ~64-65% does not fund outsized R&D.)
- **Market Share Gainers**: PARTIAL → STRONG FIT. Why now: Share gains are explicit and repeated across the window: US Sevoflurane share moved 30% (three years prior) → 44% (Oct 2023) → 48% (Jul 2026), alongside sustained ~76-77% leadership in Baclofen prefilled syringes and #1 Fentanyl positions in Japan, South Africa and Indonesia, with ex-US inhalation anesthesia expansion added. (The long view had said: Genuine leadership positions are disclosed and held — Gablofen at 77% of the US baclofen prefilled-syringe market (Aug 2023), 48% US Sevoflurane share and #1 Fentanyl brands in Japan/South Africa/Indonesia — but these are defended rather than persistently gained; CHG growth was muted for five to six quarters under Chinese competition before recovering.)

**That weakened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The calls discuss order inflows, RFPs and new commercial orders, but never attach rates, renewal rates, AMC/service revenue share, or deferred revenue/customer advances as a funding source. (The long view had said: the calls are silent and the numbers are mixed (1 of 3 checks supporting).)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Friendly Middleman (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Toll Roads (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 0 worsened, 5 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: A meaningful slice of revenue sits in categories where cheaper equivalents are the competitive reality — generic APIs (soft demand in FY23), and hospital generics such as sevoflurane and baclofen bought by procurement intermediaries with no end-patient brand awareness; management leans on 'pricing discipline' and 'judicious pricing actions' to hold margin against input inflation, which is the classic price-defence posture. (The long view had said: Cheaper Chinese entrants attacking inhalation anesthesia — a product whose end choice sits with hospitals and procurement rather than a brand-aware consumer — stalled CHG growth for five to six quarters, and management repeatedly leans on 'pricing discipline' and 'judicious price increases' to hold margin; the generic API and unpatented-molecule CDMO work is similarly price-contested.)
- **Government Dependency**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Nothing in the four calls shows pricing, tariffs or subsidies setting revenue; demand comes from innovator-pharma customers and retail consumers, and the only policy reference — geopolitical and trade uncertainty — is described as pushing work toward multi-geography CDMOs like PPL rather than threatening it. Regulatory exposure is compliance-shaped (USFDA inspections), and the record is clean: zero OAI, five facilities cleared in twelve months in FY24 and a Sellersville EIR again in Q1 FY27. (The long view had said: Pricing is not regulator-set and the customer base is private, but the entire franchise is gated by regulatory inspection outcomes (USFDA inspections and zero-OAI status are repeatedly presented as the core differentiator), and Jul 2026 flags geopolitical and trade/tariff uncertainty reshaping supply chains; legacy government tender sales were also carried over from Piramal Enterprises at demerger.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH).*

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
