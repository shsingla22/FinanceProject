# Grasim Industries Ltd. (GRASIM) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

*Industry: CEMENT & CEMENT PRODUCTS*

Grasim Industries is a large Indian manufacturing group (part of the Aditya Birla group, incorporated in 1947) that makes and sells industrial and consumer products, and it also owns controlling stakes in listed companies in cement and financial services, so most of its reported revenue comes from those subsidiaries. Its own factories make man-made cellulosic fibre and yarn sold to textile makers, and chlor-alkali/specialty chemicals (caustic soda, chlorine derivatives, epoxy) sold to industrial customers; since 2021-22 it has added two new consumer- and trade-facing businesses, decorative paints under the Birla Opus brand and a B2B e-commerce platform called Birla Pivot. It makes money mainly by selling tonnes of fibre and chemicals at prevailing global prices (caustic soda and viscose prices, cotton prices and the rupee all swing its earnings), and increasingly by selling branded paint through dealers, contractors and institutional projects. It operates chiefly in India, with plants at sites such as Vilayat, Nagda and Kharagpur, while selling into and competing against global fibre and chemical markets (China, Indonesia, US, EU). Consolidated revenue reached a record INR 1,75,431 crore (over $18 billion) in FY26, versus INR 41,039 crore on a standalone (own-operations) basis.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Cellulosic Fibre (VSF / CSF)** — Makes viscose staple fibre, a wood-pulp-based fibre used by textile mills as an alternative to cotton and polyester; it is one of the company's two traditional core businesses, with quarterly volumes around 200 kilotonnes and quarterly revenue of roughly INR 3,700 crore in the earlier calls.
- **Cellulosic Fashion Yarn (VFY / CFY) and Insulators** — A smaller sister business making filament yarn for fashion textiles plus electrical insulators, running at roughly INR 583 crore of revenue in a quarter (about a sixth the size of the fibre business).
- **Chemicals (Chlor-Alkali and Specialty Chemicals)** — Produces caustic soda and chlorine plus value-added chlorine products, chloromethanes, water-treatment and sanitation chemicals, epoxy and ECH; earnings hinge on the 'ECU realization' per tonne, and the specialty chemicals piece had grown to about 30% of chemical business revenue by mid-2024.
- **Paints – Birla Opus** — A decorative paints business launched commercially in April 2024 across six new plants, selling emulsions, enamels, waterproofing, wood finish, wallpaper and tools through 50,000+ dealers, ~1,000 exclusive galleries and institutional projects; it doubled revenue in FY26 and is the number three player in organized decorative paints, closing in on number two when combined with Birla White putty.
- **B2B E-commerce – Birla Pivot** — An online marketplace selling building and construction materials to businesses, including its own private-label plywood, doors and tiles; described as high-growth and less capital-hungry, running at over INR 550 crore of revenue per quarter as of mid-2024.
- **Building Materials – UltraTech Cement** — The group's cement arm, consolidated into Grasim's results, which crossed 150-155 million tonnes of annual capacity and has been buying stakes in rivals such as Kesoram and India Cements; it is the single largest contributor to consolidated revenue.
- **Financial Services – Aditya Birla Capital** — The group's lending, insurance and financial-products arm, described as growing through an omnichannel distribution network with a focus on risk management and return on capital.
- **Textiles / Birla White putty** — Legacy fabric and wall-putty lines mentioned alongside the newer businesses; textiles delivered a record quarterly EBITDA in Q1 FY23, and Birla White putty revenue is counted together with Birla Opus when management measures paints market share.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
