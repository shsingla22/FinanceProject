### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 6 unchanged. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: No named customer, licensor or supplier drives results in any of the three calls; the business runs through 679,000 retail touchpoints, 765 rural distributors, 8 owned plus 22 outsourced plants and 45 export markets, with 95% of raw material sourced locally from a fragmented farmer base. The visible dependency is geographic (70% South, and Kerala's distribution restructuring cut Q4 group growth from 7.8% to 6.2%) rather than stakeholder concentration. (The long view had said: Concentration is geographic and channel-level rather than single-customer: 70% of revenue from four southern states, 70% of the 21% international business from the GCC, and Kerala alone dragged Q4 group growth from 7.8% to 6.2% via one distribution restructuring plus an LPG-driven HoReCa slump. Management also attributed a flat breakfast quarter to just two quick-commerce platforms restructuring internally.)

**That worsened:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: Management explicitly and repeatedly frames the core spices business as a premium defended against a 60%-unorganized market and local brands: MRPs were cut in step with raw material deflation specifically to hold the price gap, and discounts are run in blended spices to manage value perception. Pure spices are low-complexity products where a cheaper local grinder can be adequate, which is precisely the Nobel Biocare structure. (The long view had said: In pure spices — the larger part of a segment that is 67% of revenue — 60% of the market is unorganized and management states the brand premium is capped at 10-15% before consumers defect to buying and grinding chilli themselves, which forced MRP cuts mirroring the full ~25-30% commodity deflation and roughly 6-7% lower realisation across FY26. This is repeated in all three calls and is the structural reason value growth lagged volume growth by 5-7 points all year.)

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (WATCH); Government Dependency (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW); New Entrants (LOW); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 3 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
