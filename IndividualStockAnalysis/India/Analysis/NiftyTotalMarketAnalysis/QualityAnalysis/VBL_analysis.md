# Varun Beverages Ltd. (VBL) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

*Industry: CONSUMER GOODS*

Varun Beverages Limited (VBL) is a franchise bottler for PepsiCo: it manufactures, bottles, packages and distributes PepsiCo-brand soft drinks, juice-based and energy drinks (such as Sting) and packaged drinking water, and in some markets also makes and distributes PepsiCo snacks such as Cheetos. It sells to retailers rather than consumers directly, having built a distribution and cold-chain network reaching about 3 million retail outlets (per the Aug 2022 call, including ~250,000 overseas), supported by visi-coolers and chilling equipment. It makes money on the volume of 'cases' sold multiplied by a net realization per case (about Rs. 178 per case in CY2025), with profitability driven by operating leverage across its plants and by backward integration into packaging (preform/PET) that lowers input costs. Its core market is India, where it runs a large network of greenfield and brownfield plants across states, plus a growing international business concentrated in Africa (South Africa and neighbouring territories, DRC, Morocco, Zimbabwe, Zambia) — CY2025 consolidated volumes were 1,213.1 million cases on revenue of Rs. 216,853.8 million, EBITDA of Rs. 50,493.7 million and PAT of Rs. 30,620.4 million.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Carbonated soft drinks (CSD)** — Fizzy PepsiCo-brand soft drinks, including the energy drink Sting, and by far the largest part of the business at roughly 71%-74% of total sales volume across the calls (73.6% in Q1 CY2026).
- **Juices / non-carbonated beverages (NCB)** — Juice-based and other non-fizzy drinks, the smallest volume category at roughly 6%-9% of total volume (5.9% in CY2025, 7.5% in Q1 CY2026), with new launches in the juice-based drink segment mentioned in 2026.
- **Packaged drinking water** — Bottled drinking water, the second-largest category at roughly 18%-22% of total sales volume (20.2% in CY2025).
- **India business** — The core market, run through a growing set of greenfield and brownfield plants (Bihar, Jammu, Sandila, Supa, Gorakhpur, Khordha, Kota, Jabalpur, Prayagraj, Buxar, Damtal, Meghalaya, Sri City), which drove 14.4% volume growth in Q1 CY2026 and was net debt-free with ~Rs. 12,250 million of free cash at end-CY2025.
- **International territories** — Overseas franchise markets, led by Africa — South Africa (via the BevCo acquisition, plus Lesotho, Eswatini, Namibia, Botswana, Mozambique, Madagascar), DRC, Morocco, Zimbabwe and Zambia — growing faster than India (21.4% volume growth in Q1 CY2026) and earning a higher realization per case; expanded further by the Twizza acquisition in South Africa (ZAR 2,053 million enterprise value) and an agreed acquisition of Crickley Dairy (~ZAR 238 million).
- **Snacks business** — Manufacturing and packaging PepsiCo snacks (Cheetos) in Morocco from May 2025 plus snack distribution in Zimbabwe and Zambia — a newer, smaller 'growth engine' that management says has ramped up well but is not separately quantified in the calls.
- **Backward integration / packaging** — In-house production of packaging inputs such as PET preform at its plants (13 integrated plants as of the May 2024 call, with more added since, including in DRC), which management credits — along with lightweighting and sugar reduction — for supporting gross margins of roughly 55%.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
