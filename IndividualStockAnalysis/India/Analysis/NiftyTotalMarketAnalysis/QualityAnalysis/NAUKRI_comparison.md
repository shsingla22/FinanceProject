### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 1 worsened, 5 unchanged. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Recruitment billings (~75% of the business) track corporate hiring appetite: FY26 growth halved from 18% in Q4FY25 to 9-11% every quarter, with management attributing it to tariffs, geopolitics and cautious hiring, and 99acres' primary (developer) segment stayed weak all year. Bounded rather than severe because it is renewal/subscription-based flow spend rather than customer capex, growth stayed positive in double digits on revenue, and margins expanded through the slowdown (53%→58-62%). (The long view had said: Billings swing hard with the corporate hiring cycle — recruitment billings grew 64.6% year-on-year in Q1FY23, decelerated to 7-9% through FY24-FY26 on 'geopolitical headwinds, tariffs related uncertainty and a generally cautious hiring stance', with management itself framing these as periodic cycles; but demand is subscription/database spend out of customer opex rather than customer capex, and recruitment operating margins held in a narrow 54-59% band throughout, so the damage shows in growth, not in margins or capital destruction.)
- **Stakeholder Concentration**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Billings are spread across sectors (Tech/IT/BPM, GCCs, recruitment consultants, other sectors each disclosed separately) and growth is described as driven by customer-count expansion; the only concentration flag is management noting quarterly renewal timing of large accounts, which they explicitly say washes out over the full year. (The long view had said: Customer concentration is genuinely low — Naukri serves 120,000+ customers with no single account named as a driver — but the calls reveal a real channel/supplier concentration on Google search traffic, whose withdrawal directly cut Shiksha's client deliveries and billings, and in real estate Meta and Google capture a meaningful share of the new-project ad market the company is trying to win.)

**That worsened:**

- **Technological Innovation**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: AI search is already destroying one of the four businesses: management moved from 'facing some headwinds from the new AI bots and the new AI mode introduced by Google' (Aug 2025) to 'the AI related impact is now very visible in the Shiksha domestic business and has led to a sharp drop in traffic' and a forced business-model pivot (Feb 2026). The same intermediation risk sits over every traffic-dependent classifieds platform the group owns. (The long view had said: AI-driven change in search behaviour has already destroyed part of a segment: Shiksha's traffic dropped sharply as Google referral traffic fell, translating into a direct billing impact by Q4FY26 (billings -13% year-on-year) and forcing a pivot of the business model into counselling and voicebots. Management devotes large blocks of the Feb-26 and May-26 calls to defending Naukri against horizontal AI platforms — the classic defensive posture the risk describes — while acknowledging AI's economic viability in Indian hiring 'is still unknown'.)

*Unchanged: New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Government Dependency (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 3 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
