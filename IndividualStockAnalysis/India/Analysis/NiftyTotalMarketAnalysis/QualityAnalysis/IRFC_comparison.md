**That strengthened:**

- **Brand Strength**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: There is a real reputational asset — sovereign linkage, Navratna status and an unblemished zero-NPA record that translates into cheaper borrowing (first ECB in 3 years at 'perhaps best in the market' yen rates, a rare zero-coupon bond) — but it buys cheap funding, not premium pricing or consumer brand extension. (The long view had said: There is no consumer or trade brand, no A&P spend, and no premium pricing; IRFC's reputational asset is its sovereign linkage and zero-NPA credit standing, which is a rating/ownership attribute rather than a replicable-by-heritage brand, and it competes purely on rate.)

**That weakened:**

- **Toll Roads**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Financing cost is a large, actively negotiated line for borrowers, and IRFC wins business through open RFPs against banks rather than being paid without negotiation — the opposite of toll-road behaviour. (The long view had said: Until FY25 IRFC was the statutory sole borrowing arm of the Ministry of Railways — a captive, non-negotiated channel with a fixed 40/35 bps spread and sovereign anchoring, which is toll-road-like; but the 2025-26 pivot to competitive RFP bidding against banks and NBFCs ('we are winning more than 60% of the bids', 'we are losing few also') is the opposite of a non-negotiated toll.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Low Price Plus (STRONG FIT); Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
