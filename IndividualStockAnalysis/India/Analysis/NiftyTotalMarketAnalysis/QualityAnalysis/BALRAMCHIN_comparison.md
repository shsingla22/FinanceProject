### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 1 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 0. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 2 strengthened, 2 weakened, 7 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Friendly Middleman**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The only channel-expert dynamic disclosed is on the not-yet-commissioned PLA business, where demand is being built through compounders and converters who qualify the resin technically before brand owners buy — 50 projects, 20 completed. No such mechanism exists in sugar or ethanol, where the buyer is OMCs and bulk traders. (The long view had said: the calls are silent and no computable check supports the pattern.)
- **Forward Integrators**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Management explicitly frames PLA and the gypsum-board plant as forward integration and value extraction from each unit of cane, but this is downstream industrial integration, not the own-store/franchise/D2C channel ownership the pattern describes — no store counts, royalties or D2C revenue share exist. (The long view had said: The downstream moves (ethanol, PLA, gypsum boards) are vertical processing of by-products, not integration toward the end customer; as of Apr 2026 there are no own stores, franchises or D2C channels and even the gypsum board sales route is undecided.)

**That weakened:**

- **Corporate Culture**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Promoter family participated proportionately in the Rs.450 crore preferential issue to hold 43%, and management convened an unscheduled call to disclose a Rs.230 crore PLA cost overrun, candidly including that suppliers 'take advantage'. Against that, the overrun itself and an equity raise months before commissioning are delivery misses, and management declined detail on PLA offtake agreements. (The long view had said: Long-tenured family leadership (Vivek Saraogi with Avantika Saraogi succeeding into cane/PLA), promoters subscribing Rs.193 crore pro-rata to keep the 43% stake, a voluntary call to pre-empt information asymmetry, and repeated public ownership of misses — the withdrawn crushing guidance in Aug 2024 and the candid PLA cost overrun in Apr 2026.)
- **Low Price Plus**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Management cites operational efficiency and higher crushing as partial offsets to cane cost inflation, but there is no low-price-plus-differentiation proposition or itemised cost culture — realisations are administered/market-set (Rs.41–42/kg UP), not chosen. (The long view had said: The integrated model is consistently framed as a structural cost advantage — captive cane, bagasse-based power, zero inbound logistics — and in Jun 2026 management claims this makes them a prospective lowest-cost PLA producer; but this is asset integration rather than a documented no-frills cost culture with itemised savings passed to price.)

*Unchanged: Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (NO FIT); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT); Brand Strength (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 0 worsened, 4 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Technological Innovation**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: The distillery asset base was built around sugar-derived feedstock and is now partly stranded by the shift to grain, and the company is simultaneously betting INR3,080 crore on PLA — a first-of-its-kind-in-India technology with a INR230 crore cost overrun, offtake agreements still unsigned and downstream applications (e.g. gutka barrier packaging) still in validation. (The long view had said: Core sugar milling is a mature, non-disruptable process, but the company has staked Rs.3,080 crore — a bet larger than its historic asset base — on licensed PLA bioplastic technology from Sulzer, a first-of-its-kind plant in India whose global peers have seen economics collapse (TotalEnergies Corbion 35% to 12% margins, asset up for sale). Ethanol volumes also depend on an ICE vehicle fleet whose long-run displacement is not addressed.)
- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: PLA's competitiveness is openly framed as a function of the fossil-plastic price rather than any protected premium — management calls the crude-driven rise in conventional plastic prices 'very helpful' — and imported neat PLA already runs ~10,000 tonnes a year, so a cheaper substitute exists on both sides of the product. (The long view had said: The new businesses face cheaper incumbent substitutes: PLA's competitiveness rests explicitly on fossil plastic staying expensive ('crude price rise... that is good for us'), and the gypsum board is being launched with no premium positioning and no distribution portfolio versus branded players like Gyproc. The peer margin path (35% to 12%) is exactly the good-enough compression pattern.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (HIGH RISK); Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); New Entrants (WATCH).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
