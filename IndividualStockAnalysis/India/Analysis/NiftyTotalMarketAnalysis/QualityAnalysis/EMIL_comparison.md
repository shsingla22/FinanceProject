### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 1 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 0. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 3 strengthened, 1 weakened, 7 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PARTIAL on the long view, PARTIAL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: None of the four FY26 calls mention extended warranties, AMC/service contracts, subscriptions, attach rates, renewals or deferred revenue as a funding source; the evidence is silent on any recurring stream. (The long view had said: Across all 15 calls there is no attach-rate, AMC, service-contract, subscription or renewal revenue discussed; the only 'other operating income' is OEM incentives/commissions, and working capital is positive and inventory-heavy (50 days in Q1FY25, 73 inventory days at Mar-26, 60 WC days at Dec-25) rather than customer-funded.)
- **Low Price Plus**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The model is structurally low-margin/scale retail (FY26 EBITDA margin 6.1%, pre-Ind AS ~4%, revenue INR7,183 crores) with supplier sellout incentives booked against purchases, but across the last 12 months management shows no itemised cost obsession or no-frills culture — discounting in Q2 FY26 was a reactive margin-dilutive response, not a structural cost advantage. (The long view had said: Management explicitly disavows price as its competitive weapon, positioning instead on premium display, locations and experience; no itemised cost-savings culture is described, and store operating costs in new clusters run 10.5-11% vs 6-7% at maturity.)
- **Cost to Replicate**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The only articulated barrier is cluster density and a decades-old regional reputation — the 83 stores over four years old earn 7.3% EBITDA versus 3.1% for the 140 newer ones, showing maturity takes years to build; but management itself confirms well-funded competitors are entering Telangana/AP, and a multi-brand electronics store network is replicable with capital, so this does not pass the cost-to-replicate test. (The long view had said: The advantage is cluster density and store locations that a funded competitor can rebuild — and demonstrably is: management concedes new players entered AP/Telangana and that competition is 'very stiff', while margins depend on a 4-year store maturation curve (3.1% for new stores vs 7.3% mature) rather than any un-replicable asset.)

**That weakened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Over the last 12 months the network grew from 208 to 223 stores with NCR revenue up 30-38% and AP up 14-18%, and management claims share gains in AP and share retention in the South against new entrants — but no explicit share percentages are given, FY26 SSSG was only 5.3% (negative 4.8% in H1) and annualised ROCE fell to 9.8-13.4%, so the expansion has so far come at the cost of returns. (The long view had said: Management repeatedly claims share gains (AP revenue +18.2% in Q3FY26, NCR +30%, 'sustained our market share' despite new entrants) and grew from ~100 stores in 2022 to 223 by Mar-2026, but no explicit share percentages are given and growth is store-additions-led — 9MFY26 SSSG was just 0.19% and FY26 revenue grew only 7%.)

*Unchanged: Brand Strength (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Friendly Middleman (NO FIT); Toll Roads (NO FIT); Pricing Power (NO FIT); Innovation Dominance (NO FIT); Forward Integrators (NO FIT).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 2 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
