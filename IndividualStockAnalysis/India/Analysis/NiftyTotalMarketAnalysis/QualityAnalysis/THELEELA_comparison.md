### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 0 eased, 1 worsened, 5 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That worsened:**

- **Cyclicality**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: Luxury hotel demand is discretionary and travel-dependent, and all four FY26 calls flag external shocks (Operation Sindoor, aviation disruption, Middle East conflict) with March occupancy actually taking a hit; meanwhile the company is investing hard into the up-cycle — BKC (~INR 800 crores of its share), Dubai (USD 70mn), Coorg, 23% key growth — with net debt/EBITDA rising from 0.3x post-IPO to 1.6x, the classic expansion-at-peak pattern, on a high-operating-leverage P&L that swung from a Q1 FY25 loss of INR 75 crores to 52% EBITDA margins. (The long view had said: Luxury hospitality here is a high-operating-leverage, asset-heavy business whose results swing with travel cycles and shocks: Q1 FY26 was hit by Operation Sindoor, Q4 FY26 RevPAR growth collapsed from ~20% to 6% on the West Asia conflict, and MICE bookings were cancelled — meanwhile the company is expanding at its fastest-ever pace (23% key growth, nine greenfield projects, ~Rs 1,650 crore outlay) at a point where margins are at record highs (49% FY26, 55% in Q4), the classic set-up for over-investment at a peak. Demand is nonetheless consumer/operations-linked rather than tied to any customer's capex, and leverage remains low at 1.6x net debt/EBITDA.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Government Dependency (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH); Fashion Risk (WATCH); New Entrants (LOW); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Good Enough Goods (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 4 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
