# Astral Poly Technik Ltd. (ASTRAL) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

*Industry: INDUSTRIAL MANUFACTURING*

Astral is an India-based maker of plastic pipes and fittings for plumbing, drainage, fire-sprinkler and infrastructure use, sold mainly through a dealer/distributor channel and specified by consultants, plumbers and project builders. Around that core piping business it has added related building-products lines — adhesives and construction chemicals, bathware (faucets, sanitaryware, PTMT fittings), water tanks, valves and paints — largely so the same channel and the same brand can carry more products. It makes money by manufacturing at its own plants across India (Santej, Hyderabad, Ghiloth, Kanpur, Rajasthan, Guwahati, Dahej, Jamnagar and others) and selling on volume at managed margins; management repeatedly cites roughly 15%-18% EBITDA margins and, in the more recent calls, double-digit volume growth. Operations are overwhelmingly Indian, plus an adhesives business in the UK (a 100%-owned subsidiary since FY26) with a US manufacturing and sales arm, and some export enquiries for valves.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Pipes / Plumbing (piping business)** — The original and by far the biggest business — CPVC and PVC pipes and fittings for plumbing, plus drainage (Drain Pro, Silencio low-noise), fire-sprinkler CPVC, OPVC, composite pipe and water tanks; it is the anchor of the company (Rs 1,400 crore of capex over ~4 years went across verticals with piping plants central), and grew 15.75% in Q2 FY26 with 20% volume growth company-wide.
- **Infra pipes** — Large-diameter and specialised pipes for government and infrastructure projects, including double-wall corrugated pipe (a corrugator being added at almost every plant), desalination products and pipe used in projects like the bullet train; described as running at or beyond capacity in 2023 but as a weak, low-government-spending area in FY25-FY26.
- **Valves (ball valve division)** — A newer in-house line of industrial ball valves, thick ball and non-return valves in PVC and CPVC from 0.5 inch to 4 inch, built with moulds and parts sourced from Europe; still small but expanding, with about 50 SKUs going into commercial production from Q4 FY25.
- **Adhesives India** — Adhesives, sealants and construction chemicals (from the Resinova acquisition about 13 years ago), run in four sub-segments — maintenance, industrial chemicals, industrial adhesives and wood — plus construction chemicals; it grows steadily at roughly a 15% run rate with 15%-16% EBITDA margins, crossed Rs 100 crore in construction chemicals in FY26, and management's stated ambition is Rs 2,000 crore of Indian revenue within 3-4 years.
- **Adhesives UK (and US)** — The overseas adhesives arm — a UK business (made a 100% subsidiary, with a new India-based CEO installed after mismanagement) plus a US plant making waterproofing products, adhesives, sealants and silicones that were previously exported from the UK; it was the problem child, swinging from -2% EBITDA in Q2 FY25 to 7.33% in Q2 FY26, with management guiding to 8-10%+ EBITDA and double-digit growth.
- **Bathware (faucets and sanitaryware)** — Faucets, ceramic sanitaryware and a 150-SKU PTMT taps and bathroom-fittings range, made largely in-house at an expanded Jamnagar plant and sold through the same plumbers, consultants and dealers as pipes; it is a small but fast-growing line off a low base — about Rs 29 crore of net sales in Q2 FY25 (up 63%), 20% growth in H1 FY26, and 25-30% growth in faucets with a large project order book by May 2026.
- **Paints** — Decorative and industrial paints built on the acquired Gem Paints business, relaunched under the Astral brand and rolled out state by state (Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Rajasthan) through newly opened depots; it is the smallest and least mature line — growth was only 4.5% in Q2 FY25 before reaching about 19-20% in FY26 — and margins are still squeezed by the cost of building distribution and manpower.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
