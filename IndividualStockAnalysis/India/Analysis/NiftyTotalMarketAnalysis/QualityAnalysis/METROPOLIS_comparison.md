### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 6 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 5. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 3 weakened, 7 unchanged.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Pricing Power**: PARTIAL → LIKELY FIT. Why now: Realisations and revenue per patient/per test rose through FY26 and mix shifted to specialty and genomics, but growth is described as largely volume-led and management concedes 'pricing gaps are narrowing' — there is no evidence of regular above-inflation list-price increases. (The long view had said: There is a genuine price lever — a Jan-2024 B2C price increase, ~5-6% realisation improvement per patient, premium specialty pricing and FY29 guidance naming 'price increases' as a growth source — but the Aug 2024 call concedes volume dips for one or two quarters after a hike, and no Q4 FY26 increase was taken at all, so the increases are not frictionless or fully regular.)

**That weakened:**

- **Forward Integrators**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: There is a genuine push toward the end customer — B2C at ~60% of revenue, self-referrals at 40%, a new consumer app and partner app, and collection-centre expansion across 750+ towns — but no franchise royalty economics, own-store profitability or D2C logistics investment is disclosed. (The long view had said: The multi-year strategy is explicit forward integration to the consumer: B2C lifted from 60% of focus-city revenue (2022) to 60% of group revenue (FY26), a franchise ARC/retail model funding 5,000+ collection centres across 750 towns, home visits, digital channels built from 0% to 25% of revenue, and a planned tech-enabled D2C vertical plus 100 mini hubs.)
- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: LIKELY FIT → QUANT SIGNAL. Why now: The four calls contain no discussion of licence fees, AMC/service contracts, subscriptions, attach rates, renewal rates or deferred revenue as a funding source; diagnostics revenue is described per-test and per-patient. Evidence is silent rather than contradictory. (The long view had said: There is repeat-customer stickiness language (self-referrals 40%, B2C 60%, chronic/longitudinal monitoring, digital customers with higher lifetime value in the May 2026 call), but across all 17 calls there is no license/AMC/subscription stream, no attach or renewal rates, and no deferred revenue or customer advances discussed as a funding source.)
- **Toll Roads**: LIKELY FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Diagnostics is a small share of a patient's total treatment cost yet drives clinical decisions, and NABL/CAP accreditation plus internal quality protocols give some standards anchoring — but management explicitly says pricing gaps with competitors are narrowing, which is not toll-road behaviour. (The long view had said: Some gold-standard traits are present — CAP-accredited genomics labs, an Internal Quality Control Index, and specialty tests that are critical to a treatment decision at small cost relative to the episode of care — but management repeatedly describes B2B discount structures, government tenders and an era of 'aggressive pricing', which is not oligopoly toll-collection.)

*Unchanged: Friendly Middleman (STRONG FIT); Brand Strength (STRONG FIT); Innovation Dominance (LIKELY FIT); Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 1 worsened, 6 unchanged. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **New Entrants**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: Management states affirmatively and repeatedly that no structurally different competitor is entering and that the earlier discount-funded, digital-first entrant wave has burnt out, leaving consolidation rather than price war. No discounting or margin investment is framed as a competitive response — margins expanded through the year. (The long view had said: Venture-funded health-tech/online diagnostics entrants with large marketing budgets and discount-led pricing were an explicit competitive theme in FY23-FY24 ('a year ago, the industry was characterized by aggressive pricing and intense competition'), and a January-2024 price increase visibly dented volumes for a quarter; by FY25-FY26 management reports those entrants stagnating and pricing stabilising, so exposure is real but receding.)

**That worsened:**

- **Good Enough Goods**: WATCH → HIGH RISK. Why now: Routine pathology is a low-complexity, price-transparent product where cheaper regional and unorganized labs sell an outcome most patients cannot distinguish, and the prescribing doctor — not the patient — controls the choice; management concedes the premium is under pressure and spends every call justifying it on quality, interpretation and institutional knowledge rather than on a structural barrier. The Nobel Biocare pattern is visible: management's own answer to price convergence is to build scientific depth and move mix toward specialty and genomics. (The long view had said: Routine pathology is exactly the good-enough setup — a non-complex product where the prescribing doctor, not the patient, controls the choice and unorganised standalone labs and discounters undercut on price; management spends five years defending its premium ('premium pricing', 'quality over low prices') and a modest B2C price rise cost it a quarter of volume growth, showing real elasticity in the routine book.)

*Unchanged: Technological Innovation (WATCH); Cyclicality (LOW); Government Dependency (LOW); Stakeholder Concentration (LOW); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 2 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
