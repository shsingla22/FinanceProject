### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 2 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 0 strengthened, 1 weakened, 8 unchanged; 2 could not be compared.

*Foundation test: FAIL on the long view, FAIL on the one-year view.*

**That weakened:**

- **Pricing Power**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: Across all four calls NIM is described as flat or steady while cost of funds fell 27bp, 7bp and 4bp sequentially to 7.41% — i.e. the funding benefit was given away in asset yields rather than retained, and the MSME book was shrunk rather than repriced. There is no mention of price increases, premiumisation or inelastic demand anywhere in the window. (The long view had said: Management consistently prioritises margin over growth and has held NIM steady through a 45bps cost-of-funds rise (FY23) and through FY26, which is margin defence rather than pricing power; against that, they repeatedly flag intensifying price competition and now guide to NIM moderation, so there is no evidence of raising price above cost inflation without volume loss.)

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 1 eased, 0 worsened, 3 unchanged; 4 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Unknown on the long view, Unknown on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: The franchise is about as unconcentrated as a lender can be — 119.3 million customers with 3.9-4.8 million added per quarter, a 74 million cross-sell base, 241,000 active distribution points and 32 lines of business, with growth described as 'secular across all businesses'. ## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 8 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
