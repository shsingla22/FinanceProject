### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 0 worsened, 3 unchanged; 3 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Government Dependency**: HIGH RISK → WATCH. Why now: Meaningful slices of the business run through policy: CNG station and booster demand depends on city-gas policy and gas availability, the process-gas segment turns on government/PSU project finalisation, and entry into commercial air conditioning with Zephyros is explicitly gated on approval under the PLI scheme. Management also flags gas shortages as a monitored risk and notes capex is moving only 'in few government-led sectors'. (The long view had said: A large share of demand sits on policy-determined infrastructure: CNG city-gas distribution, gas availability and pricing, biogas and hydrogen programmes whose scale-up management repeatedly defers, and a PLI application that gates the Zephyros ramp; management itself says capex is hesitant 'except in few government-led sectors' and flags 'uncertainty around several green initiatives'. Biogas is the textbook case — orders won, then 'a period of quiet' pending unresolved generation economics.)
- **Good Enough Goods**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: In the commoditised parts of the range — standard screw compressors and boosters — the company faces cheaper competition it will not match on price, and screw compressors were explicitly a laggard for the year with muted offtake in general engineering. Management's response is to concede volume rather than defend share, which shows the premium is not protectable in those lines. (The long view had said: The clearest realised risk in the record: management admits customers in gas distribution down-traded to cheaper products they found acceptable, costing market share plus a INR2 crores bad-debt write-off, and Calana boosters were shipped in record numbers 'at a near zero margin'. Screw compressors and boosters are now explicitly ceded rather than defended on price, and FY26's elevated 24.9% segment margin is management's own flagged 'one-timer' against a sustainable 18-20%.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Cyclicality (HIGH RISK); Technological Innovation (WATCH); Stakeholder Concentration (WATCH).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 6 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
