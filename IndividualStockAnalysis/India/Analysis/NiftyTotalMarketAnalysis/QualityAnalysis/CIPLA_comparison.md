### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 6 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 4. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 3 weakened, 5 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → STRONG FIT. Why now: Share gains are quantified and persistent across all four calls: #1 in the US albuterol MDI market (22%, 19.6%, 21% through the year), South Africa private-market secondary growth beating the market every single quarter (6.2 vs 4.7, 6.3 vs 5.7, 6.6 vs 4.8, 6.5 vs 5.7), and India respiratory outperforming therapy IPM by 400bps — and it is being won with launches and volume rather than discounting. (The long view had said: Share gains are quantified and persistent across markets and years: US albuterol 17% (Jun 2024) to 19.6% (Mar 2026) to 21% and #1 in MDI; South Africa private secondary growth beating the market in every period cited (10.6% vs 7%, 7.8% vs 1.7%, 6.6% vs 4.8%, 6.5% vs 5.7%) while holding the #1 Rx rank; India chronic mix and IPM brand count rising.)

**That weakened:**

- **Friendly Middleman**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: The India branded-prescription franchise is the classic prescriber-chooses/patient-pays model — growth is tracked in IQVIA prescription share and driven by field execution with doctors — but the calls show no trade-training programmes, and large parts of the business (trade generics, US generics) are price-negotiated channel sales rather than expert-recommended. (The long view had said: The core India and South Africa engines are branded prescription businesses where doctors choose and patients pay, with management repeatedly tracking IQVIA Rx growth, chronic mix (61.5% FY25 to 60.4% FY27), 'in-clinic excellence' and a retail task force touching 1.5 lakh chemists; on the Jul-2024 call management explicitly said the prescriber is price-insensitive.)
- **Pricing Power**: LIKELY FIT → NO FIT. Why now: Across all four calls growth is attributed to volume, new launches and mix — never to price increases; management welcomed GST cuts and committed to passing benefits through, while the US business showed 'increased competition in new launches' and Revlimid revenue decay, i.e. the deflation the pattern says must be avoided. (The long view had said: Management repeatedly cites 'calibrated price hikes' and 'calibrated pricing actions' to offset freight/forex inflation in branded markets (Aug 2022, Jul 2024), but the US generic business runs the opposite way with acknowledged price erosion, so pricing power is confined to the India/SA branded book.)
- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The four calls discuss product sales, launches and market share but never license fees, AMC/service contracts, renewal or attach rates, deferred revenue or customer advances as a funding source. Chronic-therapy repeat prescriptions are not described in recurring-contract terms. (The long view had said: Across all excerpts there is no discussion of licence fees, AMC/service contracts, subscriptions, attach rates, renewals or deferred revenue as a funding source — the model is straightforward product sales.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Forward Integrators (LIKELY FIT); Toll Roads (PARTIAL).*

*Unchanged: Brand Strength (STRONG FIT); Innovation Dominance (LIKELY FIT); Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
