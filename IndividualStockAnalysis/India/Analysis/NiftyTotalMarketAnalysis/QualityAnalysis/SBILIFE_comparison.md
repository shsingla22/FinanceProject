### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 3 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 2. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 1 strengthened, 1 weakened, 8 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: PASS on the long view, PASS on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Friendly Middleman**: LIKELY FIT → STRONG FIT. Why now: Sales run almost entirely through intermediaries — 58,000-60,000 SBI/RRB certified insurance facilitators plus a rapidly expanded agency force (1,20,000 gross agent additions and 120 new branches in FY26) — with productivity per branch/agent, not end-customer marketing, as the disclosed growth lever. (The long view had said: Sales are almost entirely intermediated — 59,000-60,000 certified SBI/RRB bank facilitators contributing 47-63% of APE plus ~2.6 lakh agents — and management's growth levers are channel investment and training (Agency 2.0, then 'agency next', 120 new branches in FY26, branch/agent productivity metrics) rather than end-customer marketing. Commission structures for corporate and individual agents are discussed as the key channel lever post-regulation.)

**That weakened:**

- **Low Price Plus**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Cost ratios are disclosed as a headline advantage — opex ratio 6.1% and total cost ratio 10.6% for FY26 — supporting high-volume, low-margin scale economics, but the calls show no itemised cost-savings culture and Q1 FY27 ratios deteriorated to 7.7%/12.0%. (The long view had said: Cost leadership is the explicit self-description and is evidenced quarter after quarter: opex ratio 7.2%→6.6%→6.1%→5.3% and total cost ratio ~10.5-10.8% on a INR1trn premium base, with management arguing regulatory surrender-value changes hurt them least because of it, and in Jul 2026 refusing to cut costs further because they are already lowest.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Toll Roads (NO FIT).*

*Unchanged: Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront) (STRONG FIT); Brand Strength (PARTIAL); Innovation Dominance (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Market Share Gainers (PARTIAL); Corporate Culture (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL); Pricing Power (NO FIT).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 1 worsened, 3 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Individual APE is still ULIP-dominated (68% in 9M FY26, 65% FY26, 61% in Q1 FY27) so new business volumes and investment income track equity-market sentiment — Q1 FY27 PAT growth of 22% was explicitly credited to market conditions, and AUM slipped from INR5.1tn at Dec-25 to INR4.9tn at Mar-26 before recovering to INR5.2tn. This is market cyclicality rather than a capex/commodity cycle, and renewal premium at 57-58% of gross written premium plus 87.7% 13th-month persistency gives a large recurring, operations-linked base. (The long view had said: Not a commodity or customer-capex cycle, but the mix is heavily ULIP (65% of individual APE in FY26, 61% in Q1FY27) so volumes and mix track equity markets — management attributes ULIP growth to 'positive movement in equity market' (Jul 2024) and a sluggish Q4 FY26 to market/geopolitical events; renewal premium at 58% of GWP is a genuine flow-like base that damps this.)
- **Shifting Consumer Preferences**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Management explicitly attributes mix change to shifting customer demand away from unit-linked savings toward protection and guaranteed products — ULIP share of individual APE fell from 72% to 61% over the window while par APE grew 133% and pure protection 122% in FY26 — so the composition of the demand base is genuinely moving, though the company is capturing rather than losing that shift. (The long view had said: Demand rotates between product formats on rates and market sentiment and management concedes it does not control this — non-par guaranteed grew 621% in Q1FY23, then ULIP took over (58% of individual NBP by Q1FY25), then par grew 133% in FY26 and non-par share fell to 19%; savings also compete with mutual funds for the same customer wallet.)

**That worsened:**

- **Stakeholder Concentration**: ELEVATED → HIGH RISK. Why now: The SBI/RRB bancassurance relationship dominates distribution — 57% of total APE in H1 FY26, 62% at 9M and 60% for FY26, sourced through ~58,000-60,000 SBI-employed CIFs whose productivity management reports as a headline metric; the franchise depends on continued access to a parent bank's branch network it does not control. (The long view had said: SBI bancassurance is the dominant distributor throughout — 55% of individual NBP in 2022, 63% in 2024, still 60% of total APE in FY26 — and credit life rides SBI's home-loan book, with analysts explicitly questioning what happens if open architecture is mandated; management's answer is channel diversification rather than a denial of dependence.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (QUANT FLAG); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Technological Innovation (LOW).*

**Financial resilience:** Sound on the long view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.) vs Sound on the one-year view (none of the 3 stress checks (leverage, cash conversion, returns on capital) is flagged.)

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 5 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
