# Gabriel India (GABRIEL) — The Analyst's Report

## About the business

Gabriel India makes ride-control parts for vehicles — shock absorbers, struts, front forks (including newer inverted/upside-down forks) and dampers — and, through a subsidiary, car sunroofs. It sells mainly to Indian vehicle manufacturers (OEMs are about 86% of revenue) across two-wheelers/three-wheelers, passenger cars, commercial vehicles and Indian Railways coaches and locomotives, with named customers including Bajaj, TVS, Atul Auto, Mahindra, Ola, Ather, Ampere, Tata, Volkswagen, Stellantis, Hyundai, Kia, Piaggio and Hero MotoCorp; the rest comes from the replacement/aftermarket channel (roughly 11-13%) and a small but growing export business (e.g. cabin and axle dampers for DAF). It earns money by winning per-model supply programs and then selling parts by volume for the life of that model, so revenue tracks Indian auto production, with margins driven by cost programs such as "CORE 90" (EBITDA around 9%). Operations are in India (plants include the sunroof facility at Oragadam near Chennai), and it is part of the ANAND Group, which treats Gabriel as its automotive growth engine toward a Rs 50,000 crore 2030 group target; FY26 consolidated revenue was Rs 4,667 crores, up 15%.

**The parts of the business:**

- **Two-wheelers and three-wheelers** — Shock absorbers and front forks for motorcycles, scooters and three-wheelers — the largest business at roughly 61-65% of revenue, with about 31-32% market share and near-100% presence on leading electric two-wheeler makers (Ola, Ather, TVS, Ampere).
- **Passenger cars** — Shock absorbers and struts for cars and SUVs, about 22-25% of revenue, with roughly 35% share in the fast-growing utility-vehicle segment and program wins on platforms from Tata, Volkswagen, Stellantis, Citroen and Maruti's Jimny.
- **Commercial vehicles (and railways)** — Dampers for trucks and buses at about 12% of revenue, plus supply to Indian Railways coaches, electric locomotives and the Siemens loco program; exports are being built here, notably cabin dampers and newly won axle dampers for DAF.
- **Aftermarket (replacement market)** — Selling the same ride-control parts as replacements through the spares channel — about 11-13% of revenue, described as a consistent focus area, crossing Rs 400 crores for the first time in FY24.
- **Sunroofs — Inalfa Gabriel Sunroof Systems (IGSS)** — A subsidiary making car sunroofs at the Oragadam (Chennai) plant, ramped from zero to about 10,000 units a month and reaching Rs 434 crores of revenue in FY26 at roughly 15% EBITDA margin — the highest-margin part of the group, with new wins at Hyundai and Kia.
- **New/emerging lines** — Early-stage diversification management flags each call — solar dampers (samples under validation in Europe, North America and India), e-bike components (first European development order, plus an award-winning patented integrated dropper post with Supernova), and JVs in fasteners (Jinos) and lubricants; small today, positioned as future growth.
- **Group restructuring / ANAND Group consolidation** — Not a product line but a stated strategy: automotive businesses of the parent group are being folded into Gabriel — the Anchemco/Asia Investment scheme took effect 22 May 2026 after NCLT approval, with more group businesses intended to follow.

*Described strictly from management's own words on the earnings calls.*
