### Bucket 2 — Multibagger patterns (11 patterns)

The long view matches 4 of the 11 winning patterns; the one-year view matches 4. Genuinely re-tested pattern verdicts: 4 strengthened, 2 weakened, 4 unchanged; 1 carried forward unchanged from the long view.

*Foundation test: FAIL on the long view, FAIL on the one-year view.*

**That strengthened:**

- **Recurring Revenue (Product Upfront)**: LIKELY FIT → STRONG FIT. Why now: Across all four FY26 calls management describes subscription revenue collected upfront (Q1FY26 operating cash flow of ~INR63cr vs INR40cr EBITDA because cash is billed in advance and revenue spread over the year), a growing installed base of ~800k monthly transacting truck operators, and 'power users' using 2+ services (~50% of base, +20-22% y/y) with near-zero churn. (The long view had said: Across all four calls management describes subscription-led, prepaid revenue with ~93% contribution margin, minimal churn and customer advances funding the business — Q1FY26 operating cash of INR63cr exceeded INR40cr EBITDA precisely because subscriptions are collected upfront and amortised.)
- **Market Share Gainers**: LIKELY FIT → STRONG FIT. Why now: Explicit, repeated share numbers rising: ~25-30% of India's truck operators transacting monthly, and tolling GTV growing 24-27% against NETC CV industry growth of 15-16%, an 11-point delta management attributes to product and distribution rather than price. (The long view had said: Share gain is quantified and persistent across the record: tolling GTV grew 27% in FY26 against NETC CV industry growth of 16%, share is disclosed at 'late 40s' with acquisition share running higher still, and monthly transacting truckers rose from 5.7 lakh (Q2FY24) to 8.5 lakh — 25-30% of India's truckers — while profitability improved rather than being sacrificed.)
- **Innovation Dominance**: PARTIAL → STRONG FIT. Why now: Very high contribution margins (93-94% in Q3FY26) fund a continuous, named product cadence — fuel sensor (up 55% q/q in Q2FY26), fuel payments, fleet docs, superloads (4 cities to 9 to 10 new cities), vehicle finance — with growth businesses up ~271% y/y in Q3FY26 and ~300% in Q4FY26; R&D is not separately disclosed, but the innovation-funded-by-core-profit loop is explicit and repeated every quarter. (The long view had said: Named new offerings arrive each year with visible revenue impact (loads marketplace and vehicle finance in FY25, SuperLoads in FY26 growing ~4x, fuel sensor, AIS GPS), and the built-out platform lets them launch at near-zero incremental cost — but no R&D spend or share-of-industry-R&D is ever disclosed, and the new verticals are still loss-making.)
- **Brand Strength**: NO FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: There is trust language ('their trust with digital is not that high... We basically build trust with our offline network') and clear adjacency stretch under one name — tolling, telematics, fuel sensor, fleet docs, superloads, vehicle finance all on the BlackBuck app — but no heritage story, A&P spend or brand-led premium pricing is disclosed. (The long view had said: the calls are silent and no computable check supports the pattern.)

**That weakened:**

- **Corporate Culture**: STRONG FIT → PARTIAL. Why now: Founder-CEO Rajesh Yabaji has led an unchanged strategy for 7-8 years and the last-12-months calls show promises converting to delivery — FY26 was the first full year of PAT (INR160cr) with EBITDA +84% while still funding new verticals, and management flags seasonal weakness and macro headwinds openly; but within this one-year window there is no instance of owning a mistake, and the management-history record supplied is too thin to judge tenure or capital allocation across cycles. (The long view had said: Founder-led with an unchanged strategy repeated verbatim across five years and delivered on: the operating-leverage promise made in Dec 2024 produced FY26 EBITDA of INR190cr and first full-year PAT of INR160cr, and bad news is volunteered — SuperLoads and vehicle finance called unprofitable, 'we're still figuring out' on the SuperLoads playbook, and the OMC loyalty suspension and West Asia trade headwind flagged before they hit.)
- **Friendly Middleman**: PARTIAL → NO FIT. Why now: The buyer and the user are the same person — the truck operator; the 10,000-person feet-on-street, technician and channel-partner network is BlackBuck's own distribution and servicing arm, not an independent professional prescribing to a separate paying customer. (The long view had said: There is a real trade channel — 10,000 touchpoints of technicians, channel partners, lubricant shops and garages that stock and install the products — but the truck operator both chooses and pays, so the defining 'expert prescribes, someone else pays' economics are absent.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): Pricing Power (QUANT SIGNAL).*

*Unchanged: Toll Roads (PARTIAL); Low Price Plus (PARTIAL); Forward Integrators (PARTIAL); Cost to Replicate (PARTIAL).*

### Bucket 3 — Risks (8 channels)

Genuinely re-tested risk verdicts: 2 eased, 0 worsened, 4 unchanged; 2 carried forward unchanged from the long view. Financial resilience: Sound on the long view, Sound on the one-year view.

**That eased:**

- **Cyclicality**: ELEVATED → WATCH. Why now: Management repeatedly frames demand against the CV/trucking cycle and season — a Q2 FY26 trough in the rainy quarter, H2 described as 'the positive season for the CV industry', and 'unfavourable macro headwinds' in Q4 FY26 — but revenue is subscription/usage-linked (tolling GTV, telematics subscriptions, fuel payments) tied to trucks operating rather than to customers' capex, and core growth held at ~30-37% y/y through all four quarters. (The long view had said: Revenue is flow-linked (tolling GTV per trip, fuel payments, telematics subscriptions collected upfront, ~90% recurring), but management repeatedly indexes to the CV/trucking cycle — calling H2 'the positive season for the CV industry', flagging seasonally strong vehicle-finance disbursals, and warning in May 2026 that the West Asia conflict will drag trade movement and hit near-term growth because heavy intercity trucking feels macro headwinds early.)
- **Stakeholder Concentration**: WATCH → LOW. Why now: The customer base is about as fragmented as it gets — ~8.2-8.5 lakh monthly transacting truck operators averaging three to five trucks each, served through a 10,000-touchpoint network across ~90% of districts; no single customer, licensor or supplier is named as driving results in any of the four calls. (The long view had said: No customer concentration whatsoever — ~800,000 fragmented monthly transacting truck operators — but the supply/partner side is concentrated: the fuel business rides entirely on OMC loyalty programmes (suspension already dented Q4 FY26 profitability), and vehicle finance is asset-light with ~90% of the ~INR600 crore book on lending partners' balance sheets.)

*Carried forward from the long view unchanged (the window could not re-test these): New Entrants (WATCH); Shifting Consumer Preferences (LOW).*

*Unchanged: Government Dependency (HIGH RISK); Technological Innovation (WATCH); Good Enough Goods (WATCH); Fashion Risk (LOW).*

## How this comparison was built

Both sides ran the same three analysis engines (BusinessAnalysis, MultibaggerPattern, QualityRisks) through their respective orchestrators:

- **Long view (full AnalystSkill):** statements over ~a decade, the whole call timeline — engines ran with_calls.
- **Last one year (RecentAnalystSkill):** latest fiscal year (prior year as baseline only), last 12 months of calls — engines ran 7 items carried from the full-history analysis, with_calls.

Comparison rules: only verdicts assessed on both views are counted; one-year-lens silences are listed but never scored as regressions; every movement line carries both sides' evidence so the WHY of the change is visible, not just the direction. Research tooling; not investment advice.
