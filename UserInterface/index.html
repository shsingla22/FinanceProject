Every company gets a full workup from three analysis engines — the 34-check quality framework, the 11 multibagger patterns, and the 8-channel risk check — combined into one explainable rating out of 100. 742 Nifty Total Market companies: balance sheets, P&L, cash flow, working capital, management & concall data.

How to use

  • Analyse / rate a company — “analyse DIXON”, “rate CRISIL”, or just type a company name: you get the rating, the quality scorecard, the multibagger patterns it fits, and the risk check, in one view.
  • Select / rank companies — “best 10 companies”, “top 5 by growth”, “select good businesses in PHARMA”.
  • Compare — “compare TITAN and DMART”.
  • Drill into a topic — “working capital of IXIGO”, “margins of ASIANPAINT”, “risks of TATASTEEL”.
  • Understand the method — “explain the framework”, “explain return on capital”.

Every company analysis now follows the analyst's flow: first what the business is and its parts (in management's own words), then the verdict with its plain-English explanation, then all three analyses in full — the 34-check quality framework, the 11 multibagger patterns and the 8 risk channels — each verdict with its why, run live via Claude (Opus 5). The written Markdown report is generated together with the page (download button under the verdict), and the “Ask about this analysis” box answers follow-ups strictly from the same records. Anything the evidence can't answer stays not assessed — never guessed.
